A museum makes two promises. “If room A opens, room B opens. If room A closes, all tours are refunded.” Each room is either open or closed. Room B is closed. What can someone with a booking be told for certain?

- No tours are refunded
- Only tours of B are refunded
- All tours are refunded
- Room A is open
- The first promise has been broken

## Explanation

With B closed, A cannot be open without contradicting the first promise. A is therefore closed, activating the second promise, which covers every tour.
