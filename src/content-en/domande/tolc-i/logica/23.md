“The visit takes place unless the museum is closed” means that an open museum guarantees the visit. Which situation violates this guarantee?

- Museum closed and visit canceled
- Museum closed and visit held elsewhere
- Museum open and visit canceled
- Museum open and visit held
- Visit canceled, with no information about whether the museum is open

## Explanation

The text gives the sentence the meaning of the implication “museum open $\Rightarrow$ visit held”. An implication is false only when the antecedent is true and the consequent is false, so the guarantee is violated when the museum is open and the visit is canceled. Cases with the museum closed are not governed by the implication, so a visit canceled or held elsewhere is compatible with the guarantee. If we know only that the visit is canceled, we cannot establish a violation, because the information about whether the museum is open is missing. The situation that violates the guarantee is therefore **museum open and visit canceled**.
