All engineers in the group know Python. Some people in the group know Python. Which conclusion is justified?

- At least one engineer belongs to the group
- Everyone in the group is an engineer
- Whether any engineers belong to the group is undetermined
- Nobody is an engineer
- Anyone who knows Python is necessarily an engineer

## Explanation

The people who know Python might all lie outside the engineers’ set. A group containing at least one engineer who knows Python is also compatible. Since both situations satisfy the premises, the existence of engineers remains undetermined. The first sentence states an inclusion without asserting that the included set is nonempty.
