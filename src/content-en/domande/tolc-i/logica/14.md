A check states: “There are at most two errors.” Which count makes that statement false?

- At least two errors
- Exactly two errors
- At most one error
- At least three errors
- No errors

## Explanation

“At most two” corresponds to $n\le2$, where $n$ is the integer number of errors. Its negation is $n>2$, namely $n\ge3$. Two errors still satisfy the statement. Thus “at least two” would include both a case satisfying it and cases contradicting it.
