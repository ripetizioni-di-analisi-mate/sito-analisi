“All large boxes are numbered” is false, and “no large box is numbered” is also false. What follows?

- There are numbered large boxes and unnumbered large boxes
- All large boxes are numbered
- No box is large
- There is exactly one large box
- Every numbered box is large

## Explanation

The first universal statement being false provides a large box without a number. The second being false provides a numbered large box. These boxes are distinct, because the same box cannot simultaneously be numbered and unnumbered. Both categories must therefore exist, although their sizes are not determined.
