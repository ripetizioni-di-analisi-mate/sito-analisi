A group follows the rule “choose A or B”, with inclusive “or”. Which choice is ruled out?

- Choosing neither A nor B
- Choosing A only
- Choosing B only
- Choosing both A and B
- Choosing A, B and also C

## Explanation

Inclusive disjunction requires at least one of its two members. A alone, B alone and both are therefore allowed. The rule says nothing about C and does not forbid choosing it additionally. Only the case in which both A and B are absent is ruled out.
