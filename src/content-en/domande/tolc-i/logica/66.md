Anna and Bruno own $18$ books altogether. Anna owns more than Bruno. Which statement is justified, assuming nonnegative integer counts?

- Anna owns at least 10 books, but the exact number is undetermined
- Anna owns exactly 10 books
- Bruno owns exactly 8 books
- Anna owns 9 books
- Bruno owns at least 10 books

## Explanation

From $a+b=18$ and $a>b$, we obtain $a>9$. Since $a$ is an integer, $a\ge10$. However, both $(a,b)=(10,8)$ and $(11,7)$ satisfy the data, so the exact value is not unique. The correct conclusion states the bound common to all solutions without arbitrarily choosing one pair.
