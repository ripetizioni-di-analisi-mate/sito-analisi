The birth month of each of $28$ people is recorded. What is the largest same-month group size whose existence is always guaranteed?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $12$
- $14$

## Explanation

At most two people per month would accommodate only $12\cdot2=24$ people. Twenty-eight therefore force at least one month to contain three. Four are not guaranteed: four months with three people and eight months with two total $4\cdot3+8\cdot2=28$, with no group of four.
