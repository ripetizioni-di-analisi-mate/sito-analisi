Four cards have a letter on one side and a number on the other. The visible faces are A, D, 4, 7. To test “if there is a vowel, the other side has an even number”, which cards must be turned over?

- A and 4
- D and 4
- Only A
- A and 7
- All four are necessary

## Explanation

The rule is violated by a vowel paired with an odd number. Thus A must be checked for an odd number on its reverse, and 7 for a vowel. D is not a vowel and 4 is even, so neither card can hide a forbidden case, regardless of its reverse face.
