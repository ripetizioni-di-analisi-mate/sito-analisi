A key is in exactly one of rooms A, B and C. Ada says “It is in A”, Bea says “It is not in A”, and Ciro says “It is in C”. Exactly two statements are true. Where is the key?

- In C
- In A
- In B
- In A or B
- The data are incompatible

## Explanation

Ada’s and Bea’s statements are negations of one another, so exactly one is true regardless of the key’s location. To have two true statements, Ciro’s must also be true, placing the key in C. In C, Bea and Ciro are truthful and Ada is not, confirming the count.
