Three activities must satisfy A before B, B before C and C before A. They take place one at a time. What is the situation?

- The only order is ABC
- The only order is CAB
- No order satisfies all three constraints
- Three orders are possible
- Every order is allowed

## Explanation

The first two constraints imply A before C. The third instead requires C before A, contradicting a time order without simultaneity. The chain forms a cycle, and no activity can start while respecting every precedence. Merely changing where the cycle is read from does not resolve the conflict.
