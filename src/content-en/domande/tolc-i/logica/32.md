Three groups contain $10$, $9$ and $8$ members. Their pairwise intersections contain $4$, $3$ and $2$, including one person common to all three. How many people belong to at least one group?

- $18$
- $19$
- $20$
- $27$
- $9$

## Explanation

Apply inclusion and exclusion for three sets: $10+9+8-4-3-2+1=19$. The person common to all three groups is initially added three times and then subtracted three times. The final addition counts that person exactly once. Omitting it would give $18$, leaving that person out of the total.
