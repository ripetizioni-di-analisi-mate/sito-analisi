An object is in one of eight boxes. Each question has only a yes-or-no answer and may ask whether the object belongs to a chosen group of boxes. How many questions suffice in the worst case with an optimal strategy?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $7$
- $8$

## Explanation

Two binary answers distinguish at most $2^2=4$ outcomes, so they cannot identify eight boxes. Three answers distinguish up to $2^3=8$ outcomes. This bound is attained by first splitting the boxes into two groups of four, then the selected group into two pairs, and finally distinguishing the last two boxes.
