Which reduced fraction represents $0.\overline{36}$?

- $\dfrac9{25}$
- $\dfrac3{11}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac4{11}$
- $\dfrac6{11}$

## Explanation

Setting $z=0.\overline{36}$, multiplication by $100$ shifts one full repeating block and gives $100z=36+z$. Subtracting $z$ gives $99z=36$, hence $z=\dfrac{36}{99}=\dfrac4{11}$. The fraction $\dfrac9{25}$ instead represents the terminating decimal $0.36$, which does not contain the repeated block.
