A plant produces $m$ parts per hour for $t$ hours. A stoppage reduces the time by $2$ hours, while an adjustment increases the rate by $3$ parts per hour. Which expression is the new total minus the old total?

- $3m-2t-6$
- $mt+3t-2m-6$
- $3t+2m-6$
- $3t-2m-6$
- $3t-2m$

## Explanation

The new total is $(m+3)(t-2)$ and the old total is $mt$. The $mt$ terms cancel on subtraction, and the product of the two changes contributes $3\cdot(-2)=-6$.
