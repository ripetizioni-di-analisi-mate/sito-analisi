Which value of $k$ makes the equation $x^2-8x+k=0$ have a double root?

- $32$
- $4$
- $8$
- $16$
- $64$

## Explanation

A double root occurs when the discriminant is zero. Here $a=1$, $b=-8$ and $c=k$, so $\Delta=(-8)^2-4k=64-4k$. The condition $\Delta=0$ gives $k=16$. With this value, the polynomial becomes $(x-4)^2$, whose only real zero is $4$, with multiplicity two.
