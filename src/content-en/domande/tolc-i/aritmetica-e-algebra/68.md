A beam's maximum load $C$ is between $980$ and $1020$ kg inclusive. A planned load can exceed its estimate $p$ by up to $5\%$. What is the greatest estimate that guarantees compliance for every beam?

- $\dfrac{1020}{1.05}$ kg
- $\dfrac{1000}{1.05}$ kg
- $\dfrac{980}{1.05}$ kg
- $980$ kg
- $980\cdot0.95$ kg

## Explanation

The worst combination is the largest actual load $1.05p$ and the weakest beam, whose limit is $980$. The requirement is $1.05p\le980$, making $\dfrac{980}{1.05}$ kg the greatest acceptable estimate.
