Two real numbers satisfy $(a+b)^2=289$ and $(a-b)^2=225$. What value of the product $ab$ is determined by these data?

- $-16$
- $32$
- $8$
- $64$
- $16$

## Explanation

Subtracting the squares cancels $a^2$ and $b^2$. The identity $(a+b)^2-(a-b)^2=4ab$ then gives $4ab=289-225=64$, so $ab=16$.
