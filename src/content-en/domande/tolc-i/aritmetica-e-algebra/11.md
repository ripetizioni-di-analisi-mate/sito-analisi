A vehicle covers two equal distances, the first at $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the second at $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is its average speed over the whole trip?

- $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $50\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

Take each leg to be $60\,\mathrm{km}$ long. The first takes two hours and the second takes one. Average speed is total distance divided by total time, so it is $\dfrac{120}{3}=40\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
