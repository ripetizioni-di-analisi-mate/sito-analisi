For which value of $k$ does $(k-2)x=3$ have no real solutions?

- $2$
- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $-2$

## Explanation

If $k\ne2$, the coefficient of $x$ is nonzero and division gives the unique solution $x=\dfrac3{k-2}$. If $k=2$, the equation becomes $0=3$, which is impossible. Thus the only parameter value that removes all solutions is $2$.
