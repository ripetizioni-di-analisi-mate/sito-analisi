Which polynomial equals $(3x-2)^2$?

- $9x^2+4$
- $9x^2+12x+4$
- $9x^2-12x-4$
- $9x^2-12x+4$
- $9x^2-6x+4$

## Explanation

The square of a binomial contains the square of each term and twice their product. The three contributions are $(3x)^2=9x^2$, $2(3x)(-2)=-12x$ and $(-2)^2=4$. Their sum is $9x^2-12x+4$. The positive sign of the constant term comes from squaring the negative number.
