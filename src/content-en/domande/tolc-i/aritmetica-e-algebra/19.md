The actual side of a square plate lies between $4.95$ and $5.05\,\mathrm{cm}$, inclusive. By how much can its area exceed the nominal $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ at most?

- $0.0025\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $0.4975\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $0.5025\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

For positive side lengths the area increases with the side. The largest excess is $(5+0.05)^2-25=2\cdot5\cdot0.05+0.05^2=0.5025\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The quadratic term contributes even when the tolerance is small.
