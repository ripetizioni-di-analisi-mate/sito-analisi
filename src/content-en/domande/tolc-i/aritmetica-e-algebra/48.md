Let $Q(t)$ be a polynomial of degree two with $Q(0)=2$, $Q(1)=5$ and $Q(-1)=1$. What is the coefficient of $t$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Write $Q(t)=at^2+bt+c$, so $Q(1)=a+b+c$ and $Q(-1)=a-b+c$. In the difference $Q(1)-Q(-1)$ the quadratic term and the constant cancel, therefore $2b=5-1=4$. It follows that the coefficient of $t$ is $b=2$. The value $4$ is $2b$, while $3$ is the difference $Q(1)-Q(0)$, which also contains $a$.
