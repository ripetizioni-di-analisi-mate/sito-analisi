A program evaluates a quadratic score $Q(t)$. You know that $Q(0)=2$, $Q(1)=5$ and $Q(-1)=1$. What is the coefficient of $t$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Writing $Q(t)=at^2+bt+c$, the quadratic term and the constant cancel in $Q(1)-Q(-1)$. This leaves $2b=5-1=4$, so $b=2$.
