A courier charges $4$ euros for each block of $3$ kg or part thereof, with no fixed fee. A delivery costs $12$ euros. What is the exact range of possible masses $m$ in kg?

- $9\le m<12$
- $m=9$
- $6<m\le9$
- $6\le m<9$
- $3<m\le6$

## Explanation

The fee represents three blocks. Exactly $6$ kg still costs just two blocks, while $9$ kg uses exactly three. Every mass above $6$ and up to and including $9$ uses three blocks.
