A register keeps only negative integers $x$ for which $x^2+2x-3>0$. Starting at $-1$ and moving left through the negative integers, which is the first to be kept?

- $-2$
- $-1$
- $-4$
- $-3$
- $-5$

## Explanation

Factor the quadratic as $(x+3)(x-1)$. For $x<0$ the second factor is negative, so $x+3<0$ is also necessary. The value $-3$ gives zero, making $-4$ the first eligible integer.
