What is the complete solution set of $x^2+2x-3>0$?

- $(-3,1)$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $[-3,1]$

## Explanation

The polynomial factors as $(x+3)(x-1)$. For $x<-3$, both factors are negative and their product is positive; between $-3$ and $1$, their signs are opposite; for $x>1$, both are positive. The points $-3$ and $1$ make the product zero and are excluded by the strict inequality.
