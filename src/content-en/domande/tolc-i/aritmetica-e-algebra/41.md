A sum of $96$ euros is divided in the ratio $5$ to $3$. What is the larger share?

- 60 euros
- 48 euros
- 54 euros
- 64 euros
- 36 euros

## Explanation

The ratio assigns five parts to the larger share and three to the smaller. The total therefore contains $5+3=8$ equal parts, each worth $96\div8=12$ euros. The larger share is $5\cdot12=60$ euros. The smaller is $36$ euros, and their sum recovers the given total.
