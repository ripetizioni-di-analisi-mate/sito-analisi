A decimal display increases by $1$ per reading. Between $3998$ and $4002$ inclusive, during which step do the most digits change at once?

- From $3999$ to $4000$
- From $4000$ to $4001$
- From $3998$ to $3999$
- From $4001$ to $4002$
- The same number of digits changes at every step

## Explanation

Adding one to a final digit of $9$ produces a carry. In $3999$ the carry passes through three places and changes the thousands as well: four digits change. In each other step only the units digit changes.
