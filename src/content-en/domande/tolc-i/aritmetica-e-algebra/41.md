The integers from $3998$ to $4002$ are written in order. In which step from one number to the next do the most digits change?

- From $3999$ to $4000$
- From $4000$ to $4001$
- From $3998$ to $3999$
- From $4001$ to $4002$
- The same number of digits changes at every step

## Explanation

Adding $1$ to a number ending in the digit $9$ produces a carry. In $3999$ the carry passes through the units, tens and hundreds and reaches the thousands, so in the step to $4000$ all four digits change. In the other steps the last digit is not $9$, therefore only the units digit changes.
