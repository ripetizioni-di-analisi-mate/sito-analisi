Which set satisfies both $x+4>2$ and $3x\le9$?

- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $(-2,3]$
- $(-3,2]$
- $(-2,3)$
- $[-2,3]$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $x>-2$ after subtracting $4$ from both sides. The second gives $x\le3$ after dividing by the positive number $3$. Both must hold, so their intersection is taken, giving $(-2,3]$. The different endpoint treatment comes from the strict and non-strict inequalities.
