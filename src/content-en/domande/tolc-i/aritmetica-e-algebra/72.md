Can a positive perfect square have exactly four positive divisors?

- Yes, for example $36$
- No, every positive perfect square has an odd number of positive divisors
- Yes, for example $64$
- Yes, for example $16$
- Yes, for example $9$

## Explanation

The divisors of a positive square $N$ form pairs $d,\dfrac{N}{d}$, apart from $\sqrt N$, which stands alone. Their number is therefore odd. The four examples have $3,5,9,7$ positive divisors respectively.
