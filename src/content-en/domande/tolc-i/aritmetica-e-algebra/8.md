What is the complete solution set of $|2x-1|=5$?

- $\{-3,3\}$
- $\{0,5\}$
- $\{-2,3\}$
- $\{2,-3\}$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

A number has absolute value $5$ when it equals $5$ or $-5$. Thus one solves $2x-1=5$ and $2x-1=-5$. The first gives $2x=6$, hence $x=3$; the second gives $2x=-4$, hence $x=-2$. The two values produce opposite arguments with the same distance from zero.
