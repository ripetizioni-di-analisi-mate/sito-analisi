A switch trips at the two positions satisfying $|2x-1|=5$. Moving right from the first position to the second, by how much does $x$ increase?

- $\dfrac52$
- $10$
- $5$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

At the two trip points $2x-1$ goes from $-5$ to $5$, an increase of $10$. Since the coefficient of $x$ is $2$, the corresponding change in position is $\dfrac{10}{2}=5$. The two positions are indeed $-2$ and $3$.
