What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in $(x+2)^3$?

- $2$
- $12$
- $8$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The cube is a product of three factors equal to $x+2$. A degree-two term is obtained by choosing $x$ from two factors and $2$ from the third. There are three possible positions for the factor contributing $2$, and each produces $2x^2$. Their sum is $6x^2$, so the required coefficient is $6$.
