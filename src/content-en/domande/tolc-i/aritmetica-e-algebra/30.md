If $A=2x^2-x+1$ and $B=x^2+3x-2$, what is the constant term of $A-2B$?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The constant term is the value of the polynomial at $x=0$, because all positive powers of $x$ vanish. Thus $A(0)-2B(0)=1-2(-2)=1+4=5$. The factor $-2$ also multiplies the negative constant term of $B$, producing the positive contribution $4$.
