A machine's check uses the remainder of $2^n$ modulo $5$. What is the smallest positive integer $p$ for which the check returns the same result at $n$ and at $n+p$ for every integer $n\ge0$?

- $1$
- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

Starting at $n=0$ the remainders are $1,2,4,3$, then $1$ returns because $2^4=16$ leaves remainder $1$. Multiplication by $2^4$ therefore preserves the remainder for every $n$. No step from $1$ through $3$ works even at $n=0$.
