What is the complete solution set of $x^2-5x+6=0$?

- $\{1,6\}$
- $\{-1,-6\}$
- $\{2\}$
- $\{2,3\}$
- $\{-2,-3\}$

## Explanation

To factor the trinomial, one seeks two numbers with sum $-5$ and product $6$: they are $-2$ and $-3$. This gives $(x-2)(x-3)=0$. A product of real numbers is zero if at least one factor is zero, so the solutions are $2$ and $3$. Both satisfy the original equation.
