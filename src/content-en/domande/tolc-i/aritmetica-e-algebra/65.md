A gear turns through $-\dfrac{3}{8}$ of a revolution, then $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of a revolution. What signed rotation of smallest absolute value will restore its initial orientation, taking the direction of the second movement as positive?

- $-\dfrac{29}{24}$
- $\dfrac{13}{24}$
- $\dfrac{11}{24}$
- $-\dfrac{13}{24}$
- $-\dfrac{11}{24}$

## Explanation

The net turn is $-\dfrac{9}{24}+\dfrac{20}{24}=\dfrac{11}{24}$ of a revolution. To recover the initial orientation one can turn back $\dfrac{11}{24}$ or forward $\dfrac{13}{24}$, apart from full revolutions. The shortest correction is $-\dfrac{11}{24}$.
