How many real solutions does $4(x-1)=4x+1$ have?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- Infinitely many
- $0$

## Explanation

Expanding the left-hand side gives $4x-4=4x+1$. Subtracting $4x$ from both sides leaves $-4=1$, which is false for every choice of $x$. The equation therefore has no solutions. Equal coefficients of the unknown do not suffice for infinitely many solutions: the constant terms would also have to match.
