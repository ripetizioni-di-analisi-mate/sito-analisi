Plan A costs a fixed $6$ euros plus $2$ euros per hour; plan B costs $3.50$ euros per hour. What is the smallest positive integer number of hours for which A costs less than B?

- $4$
- $5$
- $3$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

Writing the hours as $h$, the required comparison is $6+2h<3.5h$. Subtracting $2h$ gives $6<1.5h$, hence $h>4$. The value $4$ gives equal costs and does not satisfy the strict comparison. The smallest integer greater than $4$ is $5$, giving $16$ euros versus $17.50$ euros.
