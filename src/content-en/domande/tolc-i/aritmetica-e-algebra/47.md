Six identical pumps empty a basin in $10$ hours. How long do four pumps take at the same constant individual flow rate?

- 12 hours
- 15 hours
- 8 hours
- 20 hours
- 40 hours

## Explanation

The volume to be emptied is fixed. With identical pumps, the product of pump count and time is constant: the work requires $6\cdot10=60$ pump-hours. With four pumps, the time is $60\div4=15$ hours. Reducing the number of pumps increases the time; this is inverse proportionality.
