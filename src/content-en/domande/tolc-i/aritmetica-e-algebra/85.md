What is the complete solution set of $(x-2)^2\ge0$?

- $\{2\}$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,2]$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The square of any real number is nonnegative. At $x=2$, it is zero and satisfies the non-strict inequality; at every other value, it is positive and still satisfies it. Therefore every real number is a solution. The set $\{2\}$ would solve only the equation $(x-2)^2=0$.
