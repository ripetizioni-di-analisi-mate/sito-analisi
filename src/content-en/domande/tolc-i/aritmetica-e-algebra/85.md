To keep a rectangular bracket in proportion, a drawing requires width $2k$ and height $3k$ with $k>0$. Which single piece of information fails to determine its dimensions?

- The diagonal length
- The perimeter
- The difference between height and width
- The area
- The height-to-width ratio

## Explanation

The ratio is always $\dfrac{3}{2}$ regardless of $k$, so it does not determine the scale. The other data equal $6k^2$, $10k$, $k$ and $\sqrt{13}\,k$ respectively, and each determines exactly one positive $k$.
