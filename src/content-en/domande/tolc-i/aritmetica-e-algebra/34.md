A printer rounds a measurement to the nearest tenth, breaking ties towards the larger number. Which measurements can produce the printed value $2.4$?

- $(2.35,2.45]$
- $(2.39,2.41)$
- $[2.30,2.40)$
- $[2.35,2.45)$
- $[2.35,2.45]$

## Explanation

The boundaries are the midpoints between $2.4$ and the adjacent tenths. A value of $2.35$ rounds to $2.4$, whereas $2.45$ rounds to $2.5$, so only the left endpoint is included.
