An item drops from $60$ to $45$ euros. What percentage increase on the discounted price restores it to $60$ euros?

- $15\%$
- $25\%$
- $20\%$
- $\dfrac{100}{3}\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

The required increase is $60-45=15$ euros. The reference value for this change is the discounted price, $45$ euros. The ratio is $\dfrac{15}{45}=\dfrac13$, so the percentage is $\dfrac{100}{3}\%$. The value $25\%$ instead describes the discount relative to the original $60$ euros and uses a different denominator.
