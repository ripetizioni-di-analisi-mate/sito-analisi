A workshop records two nonnegative consumption figures $u$ and $v$. The smaller is $7$ and their product is $84$. Which extra information is needed to determine the sum?

- Which of the figures is labelled $u$
- The value of $u-v$
- None: the sum is already determined
- The ratio of the figures
- The mean of the two figures

## Explanation

One factor is $7$, so the other must be $\dfrac{84}{7}=12$. Swapping the labels does not affect their sum, which is already known to be $7+12=19$.
