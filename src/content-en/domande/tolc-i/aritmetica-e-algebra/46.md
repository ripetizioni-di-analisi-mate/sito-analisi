Two parts must have lengths $x$ and $10-x$ centimetres. The shorter part must be at least $4$ centimetres long. How many distinct integer values can $x$ take?

- $3$
- $5$
- $6$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The smaller length is at least $4$ when both lengths are at least $4$. Thus $x\ge4$ and $10-x\ge4$, giving $4\le x\le6$. The integers are $4,5,6$.
