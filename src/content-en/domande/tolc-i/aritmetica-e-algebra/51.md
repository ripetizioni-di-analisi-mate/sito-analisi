Between which consecutive integers does $\sqrt{41}$ lie?

- $6$ and $7$
- $20$ and $21$
- $5$ and $6$
- $8$ and $9$
- $7$ and $8$

## Explanation

The radicand is compared with the squares of nearby integers. Since $6^2=36<41<49=7^2$, taking square roots gives $6<\sqrt{41}<7$. This preserves order because the square root is increasing on nonnegative numbers. The comparisons are strict because $41$ is not a perfect square.
