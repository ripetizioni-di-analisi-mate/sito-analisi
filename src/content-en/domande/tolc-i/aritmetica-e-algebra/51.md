A code consists of an integer $n$ and its remainder modulo $9$ as a check value. Which change to $n$ is guaranteed to preserve the check value for every code?

- Switching two decimal digits
- Doubling $n$
- Adding $1$ to each digit
- Changing the last digit from $2$ to $3$ whenever it is $2$
- Adding $10$ to $n$

## Explanation

Every power of $10$ has remainder $1$ modulo $9$, so the number and its digit sum have the same remainder. Switching two digits preserves their sum. Adding $10$, for example, changes the remainder by $1$.
