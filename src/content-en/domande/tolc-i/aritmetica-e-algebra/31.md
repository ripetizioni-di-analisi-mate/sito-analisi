A machine accepts precisely those integers $n$ for which $\dfrac{n-2}{5}$ is an integer. Which pair of inputs will both be accepted?

- $-3,\ 12$
- $-5,\ 5$
- $2,\ 10$
- $7,\ 13$
- $-2,\ 12$

## Explanation

An accepted input has the form $n=2+5k$, where $k$ may be negative. Subtracting $2$ from $-3$ and $12$ gives $-5$ and $10$, both multiples of $5$. In each other pair at least one difference is not a multiple of $5$.
