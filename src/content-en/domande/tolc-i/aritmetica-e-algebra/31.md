Which value solves $\dfrac{x-1}{3}+\dfrac{x+1}{2}=4$?

- $\dfrac{23}{5}$
- $\dfrac{23}{6}$
- $\dfrac{19}{5}$
- $\dfrac{25}{6}$
- $5$

## Explanation

The denominators are nonzero constants, so they impose no exclusions. Multiplying both sides by $6$ gives $2(x-1)+3(x+1)=24$. Expanding gives $2x-2+3x+3=24$, hence $5x=23$ and $x=\dfrac{23}{5}$. The factor $6$ must multiply every term on both sides.
