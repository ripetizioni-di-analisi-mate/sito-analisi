A $3\,\mathrm m$ cable is bent into a square without overlap. A second cable forms a square in the same way with twice the area. How long is the second cable?

- $3\sqrt2\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $\sqrt6\,\mathrm m$
- $9\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac32\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The area of a square is proportional to the square of its side. Doubling the area multiplies the side by $\sqrt2$. Its perimeter, equal to the cable length, also increases by a factor of $\sqrt2$.
