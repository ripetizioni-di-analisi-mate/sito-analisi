An item costs $122$ euros including a tax equal to $22\%$ of its pre-tax price. What is the pre-tax price?

- 100 euros
- $95.16$ euros
- 105 euros
- 110 euros
- 144 euros

## Explanation

If $P$ is the pre-tax price, the final price is $P+0.22P=1.22P$. Solving $1.22P=122$ gives $P=100$ euros. Subtracting $22\%$ of $122$ would use the wrong base, because the tax is defined as a percentage of the pre-tax price.
