A copier can only enlarge all lengths by the same factor. A $12\times18$ cm rectangle must fit completely, with its sides parallel, on a $20\times27$ cm page. What is the largest possible scale factor?

- $\dfrac{47}{30}$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac53$
- $\dfrac{10}{9}$
- $\dfrac94$

## Explanation

In the more favourable orientation, the factor $k$ must satisfy $12k\le20$ and $18k\le27$. The smaller of $\dfrac{5}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is $\dfrac{3}{2}$. Rotating the figure would fit the $18$ cm side into $20$ cm, limiting $k$ to $\dfrac{10}{9}$.
