What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x+1}=x-1$?

- $\{3\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{0,3\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The square root is nonnegative, so the right-hand side requires $x\ge1$. Squaring gives $x+1=x^2-2x+1$, or $x(x-3)=0$. The candidates are $0$ and $3$, but only $3$ satisfies the restriction. Substituting it makes both sides equal to $2$, confirming the solution.
