What is the sum of the odd numbers strictly between $8$ and $20$?

- $70$
- $99$
- $78$
- $65$
- $84$

## Explanation

The required numbers are $9,11,13,15,17,19$. Pairing the first with the last, the second with the next-to-last, and the two middle terms gives $9+19=28$, $11+17=28$ and $13+15=28$. The three pairs have the same sum, so the total is $3\cdot28=84$.
