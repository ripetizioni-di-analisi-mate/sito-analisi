A miscalibrated scale shows a mass proportional to the true one: for an object of $100$ g it shows $96$ g. If the scale shows $L$ grams, which expression gives the true mass of the object?

- $\dfrac{L}{1.04}$
- $L+4$
- $0.96L$
- $\dfrac{L}{0.96}$
- $1.04L$

## Explanation

Since the reading is proportional to the true mass, their ratio is constant and equals $\dfrac{96}{100}=0.96$. If the true mass is $m$, then $L=0.96\,m$. Dividing by $0.96$ gives $m=\dfrac{L}{0.96}$, valid for every reading. The expressions $L+4$ and $1.04L$ correct by $4$ g or by $4\%$ of the reading, and so they do not recover the true mass.
