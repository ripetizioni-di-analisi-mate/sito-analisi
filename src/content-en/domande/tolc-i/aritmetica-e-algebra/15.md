A tank holds $24$ litres of a mixture containing $15\%$ additive by volume. How many litres should be withdrawn and replaced with the same volume of a $20\%$ mixture to reach $16\%$? Volumes are additive.

- $19.2$
- $2.4$
- $6$
- $1.2$
- $4.8$

## Explanation

The amount of additive must increase by $24\cdot0.01=0.24$ litres. Each litre replaced adds a net $0.20-0.15=0.05$ litres of additive, so $\dfrac{0.24}{0.05}=4.8$ litres must be replaced.
