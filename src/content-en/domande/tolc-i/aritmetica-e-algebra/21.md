Let $u$ and $v$ be two real numbers such that $u^2<v^2$. Which relation holds whatever the signs of $u$ and $v$?

- $|u|<|v|$
- $uv>0$
- $u>-v$
- $u+v>0$
- $u<v$

## Explanation

Since $u^2=|u|^2$ and $v^2=|v|^2$, the given inequality reads $|u|^2<|v|^2$. The square root is increasing on nonnegative numbers, so $|u|^2<|v|^2$ gives $|u|<|v|$. The other relations can be false. For $u=1$ and $v=-2$ we have $u^2<v^2$, but $uv=-2<0$, $u+v=-1<0$, $u>v$ and $u<-v$. Therefore only $|u|<|v|$ holds in every case.
