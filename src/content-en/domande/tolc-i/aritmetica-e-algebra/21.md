A sensor reports only the square of a real voltage. For two voltages $u,v$ it reports $u^2<v^2$. Which relation is guaranteed even if their signs are unknown?

- $|u|<|v|$
- $uv>0$
- $u>-v$
- $u+v>0$
- $u<v$

## Explanation

The square root is increasing on nonnegative numbers, so $u^2<v^2$ gives $\sqrt{u^2}<\sqrt{v^2}$, or $|u|<|v|$. Reversing the sign of a voltage leaves the sensor reading unchanged.
