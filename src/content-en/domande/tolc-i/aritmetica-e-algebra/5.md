What is the complete solution set of $\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}>0$?

- $(-2,1)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[1,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2\}$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator requires $x\ne-2$. The ratio is positive when numerator and denominator have the same sign. For $x<-2$, both are negative; for $-2<x<1$, their signs are opposite; for $x>1$, both are positive. The value $x=1$ is also excluded, because the ratio is zero there and the inequality is strict.
