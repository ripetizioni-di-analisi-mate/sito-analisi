A valve is tested for real settings $x$ between $-3$ and $2$. It opens when $\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}>0$. What is the total length of the portions of the test interval on which it opens?

- $3$
- $1$
- $5$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The ratio is positive when its numerator and denominator have the same sign. Within the test interval this happens on $[-3,-2)$ and $(1,2]$. Each part has length $1$, even with one endpoint excluded, giving total length $2$.
