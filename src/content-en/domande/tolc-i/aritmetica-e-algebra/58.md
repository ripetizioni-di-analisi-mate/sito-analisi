Software uses $(x+1)^2-x^2$ for the area gained when the side of a square increases by one unit. If the same increase is applied to a square whose original side is $5$ units longer, how much larger is the area gain?

- $36$
- $25$
- $10$
- $5$
- $11$

## Explanation

The area gain is $2x+1$. Starting from side $x+5$ gives $2(x+5)+1$. Subtracting the two gains gives $10$, independently of the original side.
