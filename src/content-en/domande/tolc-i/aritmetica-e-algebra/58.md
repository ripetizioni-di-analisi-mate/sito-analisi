When the side of a square goes from $x$ to $x+1$, the area increases by $(x+1)^2-x^2$. How much larger is this increase if one starts from a square whose side is $5$ units longer?

- $36$
- $25$
- $10$
- $5$
- $11$

## Explanation

Expanding the square gives $(x+1)^2-x^2=2x+1$. Starting from side $x+5$, the area increase is $2(x+5)+1=2x+11$. The difference between the two increases is $(2x+11)-(2x+1)=10$, so the increase is larger by $10$ square units, whatever the initial side.
