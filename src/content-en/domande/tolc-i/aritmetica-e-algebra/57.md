What is the minimum value of $|x-2|+|x+1|$ for real $x$?

- $0$
- $3$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The expression adds the distances of $x$ from $2$ and from $-1$. For $-1\le x\le2$, it equals $(2-x)+(x+1)=3$. If $x>2$, the sum is $2x-1>3$; if $x<-1$, it is $1-2x>3$. The minimum is therefore $3$, attained at every point in the closed interval $[-1,2]$.
