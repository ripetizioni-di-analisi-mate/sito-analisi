How many distinct real solutions does $x^3-9x=0$ have?

- $0$
- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ gives $x(x^2-9)=0$. The difference of squares factors further, giving $x(x-3)(x+3)=0$. The three factors vanish at $0$, $3$ and $-3$, respectively. These values are distinct, so there are three real solutions. Dividing immediately by $x$ would lose zero.
