Take the reciprocals of the nonzero real roots of $x^3-9x=0$. What is the product of the two resulting numbers?

- $-9$
- $-\dfrac19$
- $0$
- $\dfrac19$
- $-\dfrac13$

## Explanation

Factoring as $x(x-3)(x+3)$ gives roots $0,3,-3$. Zero has no reciprocal. The remaining reciprocals are $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{3}$, whose product is $-\dfrac{1}{9}$.
