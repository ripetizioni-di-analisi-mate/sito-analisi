For a positive integer $n$, consider the greatest common divisor of $2n+1$ and $4n+5$. Which set contains exactly its possible values?

- $\{3\}$
- $\{1,3\}$
- $\{1,3,5\}$
- $\{1,2,3\}$
- $\{1\}$

## Explanation

A common divisor also divides $(4n+5)-2(2n+1)=3$, so it can only be $1$ or $3$. At $n=1$ the numbers are $3$ and $9$, with greatest common divisor $3$. At $n=2$ they are $5$ and $13$, which are coprime.
