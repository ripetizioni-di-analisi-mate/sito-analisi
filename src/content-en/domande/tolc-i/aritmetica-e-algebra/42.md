Two lights flash every $14$ and $21$ seconds, starting together. After how many seconds does the first new simultaneous flash occur?

- $63\,\mathrm s$
- $42\,\mathrm s$
- $84\,\mathrm s$
- $35\,\mathrm s$
- $28\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The elapsed time must be a positive multiple of both periods. The first common instant is therefore their least common multiple. Since $14=2\cdot7$ and $21=3\cdot7$, $\operatorname{mcm}(14,21)=2\cdot3\cdot7=42$. In that time, the first light completes three periods and the second two.
