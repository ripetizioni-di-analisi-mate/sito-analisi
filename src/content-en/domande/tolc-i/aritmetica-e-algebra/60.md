For the positive integer $n=100a+10b+c$, where $a,b,c$ are digits, which expression returns the tens digit for every three-digit number? The symbol $\lfloor t\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $t$.

- $n-10\lfloor \dfrac{n}{10}\rfloor$
- $n-100\lfloor \dfrac{n}{100}\rfloor$
- $\lfloor \dfrac{n}{10}\rfloor-\lfloor \dfrac{n}{100}\rfloor$
- $\lfloor \dfrac{n}{100}\rfloor$
- $\lfloor \dfrac{n}{10}\rfloor-10\lfloor \dfrac{n}{100}\rfloor$

## Explanation

Integer division by $10$ gives $10a+b$, while division by $100$ gives $a$. Subtracting $10a$ from $10a+b$ leaves just the digit $b$.
