Two algorithms require $n^2$ and $3n+4$ operations respectively on an input of positive integer size $n$. For how many sizes does the first require strictly fewer operations?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

We need $n^2-3n-4=(n-4)(n+1)<0$. For $n>0$ the second factor is positive, so $n<4$. The possible sizes are $1,2,3$.
