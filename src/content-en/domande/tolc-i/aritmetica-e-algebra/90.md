Two positive resistances $R_1$ and $R_2$ are combined according to $\dfrac{1}{R}=\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_2}$. With $R_1$ fixed, how does $R$ compare with $R_1$ for every finite $R_2>0$?

- $R>R_1$
- $R<0$
- $R=R_1+R_2$
- $R=R_1$
- $0<R<R_1$

## Explanation

The sum of reciprocals is positive and strictly greater than $\dfrac{1}{R_1}$. Taking reciprocals of positive numbers reverses their order, so $R$ is positive and less than $R_1$.
