Which fraction has a terminating decimal representation?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac5{12}$
- $\dfrac2{15}$
- $\dfrac4{21}$
- $\dfrac7{40}$

## Explanation

A reduced fraction has a terminating decimal if its denominator contains only the prime factors $2$ and $5$, because it can then be converted to a power of ten. Here $40=2^3\cdot5$ and $\dfrac7{40}=\dfrac{175}{1000}=0.175$. The other reduced denominators contain the factor $3$ or $7$, preventing a terminating representation.
