How is the system $\begin{cases}x+2y=3,\\2x+4y=7\end{cases}$ classified?

- It has infinitely many solutions
- It has two solutions
- It has no solutions
- It has one solution
- It has four solutions

## Explanation

Doubling the first equation gives $2x+4y=6$. The second requires the same expression to equal $7$. The conditions are incompatible because $6\ne7$, so no pair satisfies both. Geometrically, the equations represent two distinct parallel lines.
