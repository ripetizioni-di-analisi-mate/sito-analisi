A test bench records $x+2y=3$ in one measurement and $2x+4y=7$ in a second. By how much must only the right-hand constant of the second reading be reduced to make the records consistent?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $3$
- $7$

## Explanation

The second expression is twice the first, so it should equal $2\cdot3=6$. Subtract one from $7$. With that correction every pair satisfying the first reading also satisfies the second.
