What is the complete real solution set of $x^4-5x^2+4=0$?

- $\{1,2\}$
- $\{-2,2\}$
- $\{-4,-1,1,4\}$
- $\{-2,-1,1,2\}$
- $\{-\sqrt5,\sqrt5\}$

## Explanation

Setting $u=x^2$ gives $u^2-5u+4=(u-1)(u-4)=0$. Both $u=1$ and $u=4$ are nonnegative, so they produce real solutions. From $x^2=1$, one obtains $x=\pm1$; from $x^2=4$, one obtains $x=\pm2$. All four distinct values are included.
