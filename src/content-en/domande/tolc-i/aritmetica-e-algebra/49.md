To solve $x^4-5x^2+4=0$, a technician sets $u=x^2$ and obtains $u=1$ or $u=4$. Only principal square roots are recorded. Which real readings have been lost in this last step?

- $1$ and $2$
- $-4$ and $-1$
- $-2$ and $0$
- $-2$ and $-1$
- Only $-2$

## Explanation

Each positive $u$ has two preimages under $x\mapsto x^2$. The recorded principal roots are $1$ and $2$. The values $-1$ and $-2$ have the same squares and also satisfy the original equation.
