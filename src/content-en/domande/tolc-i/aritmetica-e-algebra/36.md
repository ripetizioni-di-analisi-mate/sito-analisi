Two files are compressed: the first shrinks from $30$ to $15$ MB, the second from $10$ to $9$ MB. By what percentage does their combined size decrease?

- $40\%$
- $16\%$
- $50\%$
- $30\%$
- $60\%$

## Explanation

The initial total is $40$ MB, and the final total is $24$ MB. Saving $16$ MB corresponds to $\dfrac{16}{40}=40\%$. An unweighted average of the two percentages would give equal weight to files of different sizes.
