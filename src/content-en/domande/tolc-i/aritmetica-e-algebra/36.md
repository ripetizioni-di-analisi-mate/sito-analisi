What is $2^0+2^{-1}+2^{-2}$?

- $\dfrac74$
- $\dfrac94$
- $\dfrac54$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac72$

## Explanation

The zero exponent gives $1$; the negative exponents give $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac14$, respectively. Their sum is therefore $1+\dfrac12+\dfrac14=\dfrac{4+2+1}{4}=\dfrac74$. Adding exponents is a rule for a product of powers, so it does not apply to the sum here.
