Two files are compressed: the first shrinks from $30$ to $15$ MB, the second from $10$ to $9$ MB. By what percentage does their total size decrease?

- $40\%$
- $16\%$
- $50\%$
- $30\%$
- $60\%$

## Explanation

The initial total size is $30+10=40$ MB and the final one is $15+9=24$ MB, so the decrease is $16$ MB. As a percentage of the initial size the decrease is $\dfrac{16}{40}=0.4$, that is $40\%$. The mean of the two percentages, $50\%$ and $10\%$, would give $30\%$, but it would give the same weight to two files of different sizes.
