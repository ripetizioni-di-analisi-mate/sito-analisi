Two sensors give readings $u$ and $v$. Only their sum $s=u+v$ and difference $d=u-v$ are recorded. Which formula recovers $v$ from these two values?

- $s-d$
- $\dfrac{s}{2}-d$
- $\dfrac{s+d}{2}$
- $d-s$
- $\dfrac{s-d}{2}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the recorded values eliminates $u$: $s-d=u+v-(u-v)=2v$. Division by two recovers $v$ even when one reading is negative.
