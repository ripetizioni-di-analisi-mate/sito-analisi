Of two real numbers $u$ and $v$ only the sum $s=u+v$ and the difference $d=u-v$ are known. Which expression gives $v$?

- $s-d$
- $\dfrac{s}{2}-d$
- $\dfrac{s+d}{2}$
- $d-s$
- $\dfrac{s-d}{2}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the difference from the sum eliminates $u$, because $s-d=(u+v)-(u-v)=2v$. Dividing by $2$ gives $v=\dfrac{s-d}{2}$. The expression $\dfrac{s+d}{2}$ gives $u$ instead, because $v$ cancels in the sum $s+d$.
