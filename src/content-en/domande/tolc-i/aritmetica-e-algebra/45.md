A force $F$ is distributed uniformly over a square of side $a$. Pressure is force divided by area. If the force triples and the side doubles, by what factor is the pressure multiplied?

- $\dfrac16$
- $6$
- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The original area is $a^2$ and the new one is $(2a)^2=4a^2$. The ratio of new to original pressure is $\dfrac{3F}{4a^2}\div\dfrac{F}{a^2}=\dfrac{3}{4}$.
