What is the solution set of $\dfrac{x+2}{x-1}=1$?

- $\{1\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{-2\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

The domain excludes $x=1$. For any other value, multiplication by $x-1$ gives $x+2=x-1$. Subtracting $x$ from both sides produces the false equality $2=-1$. Thus no admissible value solves the equation. The point excluded from the domain cannot be recovered as a solution.
