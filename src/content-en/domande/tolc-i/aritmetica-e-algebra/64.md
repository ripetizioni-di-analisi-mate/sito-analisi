Which expression equals $\dfrac1{\sqrt3-1}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{2}$
- $\sqrt3+1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{2}$

## Explanation

Multiply numerator and denominator by the nonzero conjugate $\sqrt3+1$. The denominator becomes $(\sqrt3-1)(\sqrt3+1)=3-1=2$. The numerator becomes $\sqrt3+1$, so the ratio is $\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{2}$. This transformation preserves the value because it amounts to multiplying by one.
