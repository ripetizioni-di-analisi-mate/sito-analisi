What is the Euclidean remainder when $-26$ is divided by $7$?

- $2$
- $6$
- $3$
- $-5$
- $5$

## Explanation

With positive divisor $7$, the remainder must be an integer from $0$ to $6$. The multiple of $7$ immediately below or equal to $-26$ is $-28=7(-4)$. The difference $-26-(-28)=2$ gives the remainder, so $-26=7(-4)+2$. The negative value $-5$ does not meet the definition of a remainder.
