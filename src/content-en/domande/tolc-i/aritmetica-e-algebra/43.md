Two motors together consume $18$ units per hour. Switching off the first and running the second at half its consumption for one hour uses $5$ units. How many units does the first motor save by operating for one hour instead of three?

- $8$
- $10$
- $16$
- $26$
- $20$

## Explanation

At full consumption the second motor uses $2\cdot5=10$ units per hour, so the first uses $18-10=8$. Removing two hours of operation for the first saves $2\cdot8=16$ units.
