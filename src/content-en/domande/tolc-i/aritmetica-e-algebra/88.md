A control system applies the commands “double” and “add $3$” in that order. Starting from the result, which sequence will undo both commands?

- Add $3$, then divide by $2$
- Divide by $3$, then subtract $2$
- Subtract $3$, then divide by $2$
- Subtract $2$, then divide by $3$
- Divide by $2$, then subtract $3$

## Explanation

Starting with $x$ gives $y=2x+3$. Undo the most recent command first: $y-3=2x$, and dividing by two then gives $\dfrac{y-3}{2}=x$.
