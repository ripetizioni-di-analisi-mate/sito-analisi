A balance has two linearly related scales. Readings $10$ and $30$ on the first correspond to $4$ and $14$, respectively, on the second. Which first-scale reading corresponds to zero on the second?

- $2$
- $8$
- $6$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

An increase of $20$ on the first scale produces an increase of $10$ on the second. To move from $4$ down to $0$ on the second, the first must decrease by $8$, giving $10-8=2$.
