Which is the largest of $7\cdot10^{-3}$, $9\cdot10^{-4}$, $2\cdot10^{-2}$, $8\cdot10^{-3}$ and $1\cdot10^{-2}$?

- $7\cdot10^{-3}$
- $9\cdot10^{-4}$
- $8\cdot10^{-3}$
- $2\cdot10^{-2}$
- $1\cdot10^{-2}$

## Explanation

Expressing every number as a multiple of $10^{-4}$ gives coefficients $70$, $9$, $200$, $80$ and $100$, respectively. The largest is $200$, corresponding to $2\cdot10^{-2}$. Comparing only the original coefficients would be incorrect because the powers of ten differ.
