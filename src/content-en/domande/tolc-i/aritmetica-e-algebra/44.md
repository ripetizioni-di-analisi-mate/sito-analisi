What is $\sqrt[3]{-216}+\sqrt{81}$?

- $15$
- $9$
- $-15$
- $3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The cube root preserves the sign of the radicand: since $(-6)^3=-216$, the first term is $-6$. The principal square root of $81$ is the nonnegative number $9$. Their sum is therefore $-6+9=3$. The signs of the two terms follow from the different root definitions.
