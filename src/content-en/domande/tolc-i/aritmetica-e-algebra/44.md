A calculation must give zero when $\sqrt[3]{-216}$ is added to the principal square root of a positive integer $m$. What value should be recorded for $m$?

- $6$
- $81$
- $216$
- $36$
- $12$

## Explanation

The cube of $-6$ is $-216$. The other radical must therefore equal $+6$ to cancel the first term. Thus $m=6^2=36$, which does give a zero sum.
