A bar expands by $0.004$ m per metre of original length. Which expression gives the same coefficient in millimetres per metre?

- $4$
- $40$
- $0.4$
- $0.004$
- $4000$

## Explanation

The denominator remains one metre of original length. Only the unit of the numerator changes: $0.004\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm{mm}$. The coefficient is therefore $4$ millimetres per metre.
