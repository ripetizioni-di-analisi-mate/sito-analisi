For which real values is $\dfrac1{\sqrt{(x-1)(x+2)}}$ defined?

- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $(-2,1)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,+\infty)$
- $[-2,1]$
- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The square root must exist and be nonzero because it is in the denominator. Thus $(x-1)(x+2)>0$ is required. The product is positive outside the zeros $-2$ and $1$, where the factors have the same sign. Both zeros are excluded because they would make the denominator zero.
