Seven pieces, each $420\,\mathrm{mm}$ long, are cut from a $3{,}000\,\mathrm{mm}$ bar, leaving an offcut. Detaching each piece removes an additional $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ of material. How long is the offcut?

- $21\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $39\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $57\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $42\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Each detached piece uses $420+3=423\,\mathrm{mm}$ of bar. All seven use $7\cdot423=2961\,\mathrm{mm}$, leaving $3000-2961=39\,\mathrm{mm}$.
