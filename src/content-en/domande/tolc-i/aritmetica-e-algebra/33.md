Each factor in $(x+1)(x+2)\cdots(x+8)$ occurs once. What sign does the product have at $x=-\dfrac{13}{2}$?

- Negative, with six negative factors
- Positive, with two negative factors
- Positive, with six negative factors
- Negative, with seven negative factors
- Zero, because one factor vanishes

## Explanation

At $x=-6.5$, the factors from $x+1$ to $x+6$ are negative, while $x+7$ and $x+8$ are positive. The six minus signs cancel in pairs and no factor is zero.
