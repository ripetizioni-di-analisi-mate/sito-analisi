A designer proposes replacing $ab$ with $\dfrac{(a+b)^2}{4}$ for every pair of real numbers. When is the replacement exact?

- For every pair with $a+b=1$
- For all positive $a,b$
- Exactly when $a=-b$
- Exactly when $ab=0$
- Exactly when $a=b$

## Explanation

Multiplying the equality by $4$ gives $(a+b)^2-4ab=0$. The left side is $(a-b)^2$, which vanishes exactly when $a=b$.
