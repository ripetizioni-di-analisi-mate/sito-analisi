What is the minimum value of $x^2-8x+21$ for real $x$?

- $21$
- $-5$
- $4$
- $0$
- $5$

## Explanation

Expanding $(x-4)^2$ gives $x^2-8x+16$. Adding $5$ gives the original polynomial, so $x^2-8x+21=(x-4)^2+5$. The square is always nonnegative and vanishes at $x=4$. Therefore the minimum is $5$, and it is attained at that point.
