A setting must satisfy $-3x+6\le0$. After recalibration, the constant term changes from $6$ to $9$ while the coefficient of $x$ stays the same. How far to the right does the smallest allowed value of $x$ move?

- $\dfrac13$
- $9$
- $3$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

For constant term $c$, the inequality becomes $x\ge \dfrac{c}{3}$. The minimum therefore changes from $\dfrac{6}{3}=2$ to $\dfrac{9}{3}=3$. The difference between the two thresholds is one unit.
