What is the complete solution set of $-3x+6\le0$?

- $(-\infty,2]$
- $(2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,2)$
- $[-2,+\infty)$
- $[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Subtracting $6$ from both sides gives $-3x\le-6$. Dividing by the negative number $-3$ reverses the inequality and gives $x\ge2$. The value $2$ is included because it makes the left-hand side zero. For example, $x=3$ gives $-3\le0$, consistently with the interval found.
