If $2<x<4$, which interval contains exactly all possible values of $\dfrac1x$?

- $\left(\dfrac12,\dfrac14\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac14,\dfrac12\right]$
- $(2,4)$
- $\left(0,\dfrac14\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac14,\dfrac12\right)$

## Explanation

The numbers involved are positive. As the denominator increases, its reciprocal decreases, so $x>2$ implies $\dfrac1x<\dfrac12$ and $x<4$ implies $\dfrac1x>\dfrac14$. The endpoints are excluded, as in the original interval. Every intermediate value is obtained by taking its reciprocal as $x$.
