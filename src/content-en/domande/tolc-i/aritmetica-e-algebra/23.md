In a batch, $\dfrac38$ of the parts pass the first test. Exactly $\dfrac27$ of the remaining parts are discarded. What is the smallest possible positive batch size?

- $14$
- $28$
- $56$
- $8$
- $112$

## Explanation

The total must be $8k$, so $3k$ parts pass and $5k$ remain. For exactly $\dfrac27$ of these to be discarded, $7$ must divide $5k$. Since $5$ and $7$ are coprime, the smallest choice is $k=7$, giving $56$ parts.
