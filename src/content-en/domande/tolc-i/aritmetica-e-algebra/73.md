Exactly $11$ containers are bought, each costing either $3$ or $5$ euros. Which total is possible?

- 42 euros
- 32 euros
- 39 euros
- 38 euros
- 56 euros

## Explanation

Eleven containers costing $3$ euros would total $33$ euros. Replacing one with a $5$ container adds $2$ euros, so the totals are $33+2k$, where $k$ is an integer from $0$ to $11$. They are all odd numbers from $33$ to $55$. The total $39$ is admissible and corresponds to $k=3$.
