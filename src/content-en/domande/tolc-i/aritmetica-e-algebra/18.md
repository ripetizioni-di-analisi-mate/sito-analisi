What is $\sqrt{10}(\sqrt{10}-\sqrt5)$?

- $5$
- $5\sqrt2$
- $10-5\sqrt2$
- $10-10\sqrt2$
- $10-\sqrt5$

## Explanation

The distributive property gives $\sqrt{10}\sqrt{10}-\sqrt{10}\sqrt5=10-\sqrt{50}$. Since $50=25\cdot2$, the second root is $5\sqrt2$. The result is therefore $10-5\sqrt2$. The original product is positive because $\sqrt{10}>\sqrt5$, and the result found is also positive.
