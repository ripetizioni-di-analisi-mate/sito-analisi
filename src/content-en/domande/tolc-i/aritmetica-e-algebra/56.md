A machine packs only boxes of $6$ or $10$ screws. Which positive even quantity is impossible to ship using only full boxes?

- $14$
- $18$
- $26$
- $16$
- $22$

## Explanation

With no boxes of $10$, the total must be a multiple of $6$. With one box of $10$, another $4$ screws would be needed to reach $14$. Two boxes of $10$ already exceed it. The other totals are $10+6$, $3\cdot6$, $10+2\cdot6$ and $2\cdot10+6$.
