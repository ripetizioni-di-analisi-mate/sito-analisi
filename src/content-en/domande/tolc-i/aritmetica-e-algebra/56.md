$\dfrac25$ of a tank’s contents are removed, followed by one third of what remains. What fraction of the initial contents is left?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac4{15}$
- $\dfrac8{15}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

After the first removal, $1-\dfrac25=\dfrac35$ of the original contents remains. The second removal leaves two thirds of this quantity, so the final fraction is $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac25$. The third removed in the second step refers to the remaining contents, so one third of the initial total is not subtracted directly.
