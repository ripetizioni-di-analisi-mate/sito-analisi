Two elevation differences vary independently, with $-3\le a\le-1$ and $2\le b\le4$. What is the smallest possible product $ab$?

- $-6$
- $12$
- $-2$
- $-12$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The product is negative. To make it as small as possible, maximize its magnitude by choosing $|a|=3$ and $b=4$. Both endpoints are allowed and the product is $(-3)\cdot4=-12$.
