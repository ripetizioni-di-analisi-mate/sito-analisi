A batch of $120$ items includes $15$ rejects. Only good items are added. What is the smallest number that brings the proportion of rejects down to at most $10\%$?

- $45$
- $15$
- $12$
- $30$
- $3$

## Explanation

The number of rejects stays at $15$ while the total becomes $120+x$. The condition $\dfrac{15}{120+x}\le\dfrac{1}{10}$ requires $120+x\ge150$, hence $x\ge30$. Adding $30$ reaches the threshold exactly.
