Three kilograms of a product cost $12$ euros. How many grams can be bought for $7$ euros at the same unit price?

- $1800\,\mathrm g$
- $1750\,\mathrm g$
- $2100\,\mathrm g$
- $2000\,\mathrm g$
- $1600\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The price per kilogram is $12\div3=4$ euros. Thus $7$ euros buys $\dfrac74\,\mathrm{kg}=1.75\,\mathrm{kg}$. Since one kilogram equals one thousand grams, the required mass is $1.75\cdot1000=1750\,\mathrm g$. The result remains below the original three kilograms.
