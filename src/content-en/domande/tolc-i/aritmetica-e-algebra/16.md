How many real solutions does $(x-2)^2+3=0$ have?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

For every real $x$, the square $(x-2)^2$ is nonnegative. Adding $3$ makes the left-hand side at least $3$, so it cannot equal zero. The minimum is attained at $x=2$ and equals $3$. The quadratic formula is not needed.
