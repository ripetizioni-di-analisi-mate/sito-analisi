What is the normalized scientific notation of $0.04\cdot0.003$?

- $1.2\cdot10^{-3}$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-5}$
- $7\cdot10^{-3}$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-4}$
- $12\cdot10^{-3}$

## Explanation

The factors are $4\cdot10^{-2}$ and $3\cdot10^{-3}$. Their product is $12\cdot10^{-5}$. To obtain a coefficient at least one and less than ten, write $12=1.2\cdot10$, giving $1.2\cdot10^{-4}$. The corresponding decimal value is $0.00012$.
