A test reading is $\dfrac{99}{101}$. A technician wants to measure how far below $1$ it is, without rounding the reading first. Which fraction is its exact relative shortfall from the reference $1$?

- $\dfrac{101}{99}$
- $\dfrac{2}{101}$
- $\dfrac{1}{101}$
- $\dfrac{2}{99}$
- $\dfrac{99}{100}$

## Explanation

The absolute shortfall is $1-\dfrac{99}{101}=\dfrac{101-99}{101}=\dfrac{2}{101}$. Dividing by the reference gives the relative shortfall. Since the reference is $1$, its numerical value is unchanged.
