On a plan at a scale of $1$ to $250$, a wall measures $7.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its actual length?

- $1.8\,\mathrm m$
- $18\,\mathrm m$
- $180\,\mathrm m$
- $7.2\,\mathrm m$
- $28.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The scale compares lengths in the same unit. The actual length is therefore $7.2\cdot250=1800\,\mathrm{cm}$. Since $100\,\mathrm{cm}=1\,\mathrm m$, dividing by $100$ gives $18\,\mathrm m$. The scale factor can be applied before conversion, or after the drawn length has also been converted to meters.
