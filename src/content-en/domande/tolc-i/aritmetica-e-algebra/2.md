A price of $80$ euros increases by $20\%$ and then decreases by $20\%$. What is the final price?

- 72 euros
- $76.80$ euros
- 80 euros
- 64 euros
- 96 euros

## Explanation

The first change multiplies the price by $1.20$, giving $80\cdot1.20=96$ euros. The second multiplies this new price by $0.80$, giving $96\cdot0.80=76.80$ euros. The percentages apply to different bases: the discount is $19.20$ euros, whereas the initial increase was $16$ euros.
