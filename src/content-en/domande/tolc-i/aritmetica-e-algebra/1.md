A student writes $\dfrac34+\dfrac56=\dfrac9{12}+\dfrac{10}{12}=\dfrac{19}{24}$. Which step needs correcting, and why?

- Only the last: nine twelfths plus ten twelfths is nineteen twelfths
- Both conversions: the common denominator must be $24$
- No step: both numerators and denominators should be added
- The conversion of $\dfrac56$: the numerator must remain $5$
- The conversion of $\dfrac34$: the numerator must remain $3$

## Explanation

Both conversions preserve the value of the fractions because each numerator and denominator are multiplied by the same factor, $3$ and $2$ respectively. The unit being counted is now a twelfth, and there are $9+10=19$ of them. The result is therefore $\dfrac{19}{12}$.
