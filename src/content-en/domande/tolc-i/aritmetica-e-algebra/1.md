What is $\dfrac34+\dfrac56$?

- $\dfrac{19}{12}$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac{15}{24}$
- $\dfrac{17}{12}$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

To add the fractions, the parts are expressed with a common denominator. The least common multiple of $4$ and $6$ is $12$, so $\dfrac34=\dfrac9{12}$ and $\dfrac56=\dfrac{10}{12}$. The sum is $\dfrac{9+10}{12}=\dfrac{19}{12}$. The result exceeds one, consistently with two terms that are both greater than one half.
