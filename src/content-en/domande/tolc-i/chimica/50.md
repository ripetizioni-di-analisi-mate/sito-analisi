What fraction of water's mass comes from oxygen? Use $M(\mathrm H)=1\,\mathrm{g/mol}$ and $M(\mathrm O)=16\,\mathrm{g/mol}$.

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac1{18}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac89$

## Explanation

One mole of water has mass $2\cdot1+16=18\,\mathrm g$, of which sixteen come from oxygen. The fraction is $\dfrac{16}{18}=\dfrac89$. The ratio $\dfrac13$ would count oxygen atoms among all atoms, but atoms of different elements have different masses. Atomic and mass fractions do not coincide.
