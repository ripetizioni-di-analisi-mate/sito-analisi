A compound has empirical formula $\mathrm{CH_2O}$, with formula molar mass $30\,\mathrm{g/mol}$. Its molecular molar mass is $90\,\mathrm{g/mol}$. What is its molecular formula?

- $\mathrm{CH_2O}$
- $\mathrm{C_3H_2O}$
- $\mathrm{C_3H_6O_3}$
- $\mathrm{C_2H_4O_2}$
- $\mathrm{C_3H_3O_3}$

## Explanation

The molecular formula contains an integer multiple of the empirical unit. The factor is $\dfrac{90}{30}=3$, so every subscript is multiplied by three: carbon $3$, hydrogen $6$, oxygen $3$. Changing only one subscript would alter the atomic ratio determined by the empirical formula and conflict with the data.
