A neutral atom has electron distribution $2,8,1$ across its first three shells. How many valence electrons does it have in the main-group model?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $8$
- $11$

## Explanation

The outermost occupied shell is the third and contains one electron. This is the valence electron in the stated model. The sum $2+8+1=11$ counts all electrons and determines atomic number for the neutral atom. The number of occupied shells, three, instead describes the period.
