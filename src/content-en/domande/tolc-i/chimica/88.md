Which name correctly distinguishes $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ from $\mathrm{FeCl_2}$, taking each chloride as charge $-1$?

- Iron(II) chloride
- Iron(I) chloride
- Iron(III) chloride
- Iron(III) oxide
- Iron(III) chlorate

## Explanation

Three chloride ions supply total charge $-3$, so iron must have oxidation state $+3$ to make the formula neutral. Roman numeral III communicates this state. In $\mathrm{FeCl_2}$, iron would instead be $+2$. Chloride and chlorate name different anions; the latter also contains oxygen.
