For $2\mathrm{CO}+\mathrm{O_2}\to2\mathrm{CO_2}$, what oxygen volume reacts with $6\,\mathrm L$ of CO if all gas volumes are measured at the same temperature and pressure and the gases are ideal?

- $6\,\mathrm L$
- $12\,\mathrm L$
- $2\,\mathrm L$
- $3\,\mathrm L$
- $1\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

For ideal gases at the same temperature and pressure, volume is proportional to amount. The stoichiometric oxygen/CO ratio is $\dfrac12$, so the required volume is $6\cdot\dfrac12=3\,\mathrm L$. This equivalence between volume ratios and coefficients requires the stated conditions and does not automatically extend to solids or liquids.
