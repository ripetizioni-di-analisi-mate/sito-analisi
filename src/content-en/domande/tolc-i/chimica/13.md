What is the oxidation number of oxygen in $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$, taking each hydrogen as $+1$?

- $-2$
- $0$
- $-1$
- $+1$
- $+2$

## Explanation

The molecule is neutral, so oxidation numbers sum to zero. If $x$ is the oxygen value, $2(+1)+2x=0$, hence $x=-1$. A peroxide is an exception to the common assignment of $-2$ to oxygen. Using $-2$ would give a sum of $-2$, inconsistent with a neutral molecule.
