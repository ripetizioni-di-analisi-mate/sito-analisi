A homogeneous water–ethanol mixture is distilled to enrich the distillate in ethanol. Which property is used?

- Ethanol's inability to pass through a filter
- Conversion of water into carbon
- The components' different volatilities
- Different charges of water's protons
- Spontaneous separation into two immiscible layers

## Explanation

A mixture's vapor can have a different composition from its liquid because components differ in their tendency to vaporize. Condensing the vapor produces distillate enriched in the more volatile component under the stated conditions. An ordinary filter does not separate two molecularly mixed miscible liquids. Enrichment does not necessarily imply absolute purity.
