A container holds only ice and liquid water at equilibrium. How many chemical substances and phases are present?

- Two substances and one phase
- Two substances and two phases
- One substance and two phases
- One substance and one phase
- Three substances and two phases

## Explanation

Ice and liquid water have the same composition, $\mathrm{H_2O}$, so they constitute one chemical substance. They are nevertheless portions with different physical properties and organization, separated by a surface, so there are two phases. A change of state does not introduce a second compound or change molecular identity.
