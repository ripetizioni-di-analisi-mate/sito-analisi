One species loses two electrons per particle and another gains three. What are the smallest particle counts, respectively, that balance the electrons?

- $2$ and $3$
- $1$ and $1$
- $3$ and $3$
- $2$ and $2$
- $3$ and $2$

## Explanation

The total number of electrons lost must equal the number gained. The least common multiple of two and three is six. Therefore three particles lose $3\cdot2=6$ electrons and two particles gain $2\cdot3=6$ electrons. The multipliers apply to entire half-reactions, not to individual atoms selected within them.
