A hydrate $\mathrm{MX\cdot xH_2O}$ loses all and only its water when heated. A $5.4\,\mathrm g$ sample yields $3.6\,\mathrm g$ of MX, with $M(\mathrm{MX})=72\,\mathrm{g/mol}$ and $M(\mathrm{H_2O})=18\,\mathrm{g/mol}$. What is $x$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

Lost water has mass $5.4-3.6=1.8\,\mathrm g$, or $0.10\,\mathrm{mol}$. The anhydrous compound is $\dfrac{3.6}{72}=0.050\,\mathrm{mol}$. The water-to-MX mole ratio is therefore $\dfrac{0.10}{0.050}=2$. The hydrate index comes from the mole ratio, not the direct mass ratio.
