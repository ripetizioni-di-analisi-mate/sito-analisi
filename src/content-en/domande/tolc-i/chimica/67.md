A pure substance chemically decomposes into two different elemental substances. What can be concluded?

- The initial substance is necessarily a mixture
- The initial substance is a compound
- It is an element because it is pure
- It is an isotope of both product elements
- Decomposition always violates conservation of mass

## Explanation

A pure compound contains chemically combined elements and can produce simpler substances through suitable transformations. An element does not chemically decompose into other elements. Purity describes a single composition, not the absence of different elements from the formula. Total product mass equals initial mass in a closed system.
