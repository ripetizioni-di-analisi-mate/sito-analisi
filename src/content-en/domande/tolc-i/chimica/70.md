Diamond and graphite are elemental substances made of carbon, but have very different properties. Which explanation is correct?

- Graphite contains only neutrons
- Diamond is a mixture of carbon and water
- The carbon atoms change atomic number
- Graphite is always liquid at room temperature
- The atoms are bonded and arranged in different structures

## Explanation

Elemental composition alone does not determine every property. In diamond, each carbon belongs to a three-dimensional bonding network; graphite forms layers with a different electronic organization. The two structures are allotropes of carbon. Atomic number remains unchanged: changing the bonding structure does not turn one element into another.
