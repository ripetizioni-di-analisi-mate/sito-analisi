What does $\mathrm{Ag^++Cl^-\to AgCl(s)}$ represent in aqueous solution?

- Reduction of silver to metal
- Evaporation of water without changes to solutes
- Decomposition of chloride into elements
- Formation of a sparingly soluble solid from dissolved ions
- Melting of an existing crystal

## Explanation

The symbol $(s)$ indicates a solid phase formed from ions in solution. This is therefore precipitation. Silver keeps oxidation number $+1$ and chlorine keeps $-1$, so the reaction is not reduction of silver to metal. Total charge cancels in the precipitate's one-to-one ratio.
