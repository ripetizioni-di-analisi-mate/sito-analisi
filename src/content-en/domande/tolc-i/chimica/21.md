Which equation represents complete dissociation of calcium chloride in water?

- $\mathrm{CaCl_2\to Ca^{2+}+2Cl^-}$
- $\mathrm{CaCl_2\to Ca^++Cl_2^-}$
- $\mathrm{CaCl_2\to Ca^{2+}+Cl^-}$
- $\mathrm{CaCl_2\to Ca+Cl_2}$
- $\mathrm{CaCl_2\to2Ca^++Cl^{2-}}$

## Explanation

The formula unit contains one calcium and two chlorine atoms. In solution it separates into one calcium cation with charge $+2$ and two chloride anions with charge $-1$. The charge sum is $+2+2(-1)=0$, consistent with the neutral salt. Three ions are released per formula unit, with atoms also conserved.
