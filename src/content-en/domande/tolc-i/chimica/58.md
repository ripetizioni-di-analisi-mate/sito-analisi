Which particle has zero charge and a mass of the same order as the proton?

- Electron
- Proton
- Neutron
- Hydrogen cation
- Positron

## Explanation

The neutron is electrically neutral and has a mass close to the proton's. The electron and positron have much smaller masses and negative and positive charges, respectively. The proton and the most common hydrogen cation are positively charged. Both requested properties, charge and mass order, must be satisfied.
