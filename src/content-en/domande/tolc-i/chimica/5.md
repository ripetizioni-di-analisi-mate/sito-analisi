A solution contains $0.50\,\mathrm{mol}$ of solute and has a final volume of $2.0\,\mathrm L$. What is its molar concentration?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{mol/L}$

## Explanation

Molar concentration is the amount of solute per liter of solution. Since volume is already expressed in liters, $c=\dfrac nV=\dfrac{0.50}{2.0}=0.25\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. Each liter therefore contains one quarter of a mole. Multiplying amount and volume would not produce the unit of concentration.
