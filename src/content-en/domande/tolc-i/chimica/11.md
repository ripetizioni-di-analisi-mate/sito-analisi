Which substance is a hydrocarbon?

- $\mathrm{C_3H_8}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3OH}$
- $\mathrm{CO_2}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COOH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3Cl}$

## Explanation

A hydrocarbon contains only carbon and hydrogen. Propane, $\mathrm{C_3H_8}$, meets both conditions. The other formulas contain oxygen or chlorine and belong to other classes. The presence of carbon alone is insufficient to classify a substance as a hydrocarbon.
