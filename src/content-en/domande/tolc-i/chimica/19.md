A $4\,\mathrm g$ quantity of solute is dissolved in $96\,\mathrm g$ of water without losses. What is the solute mass percentage?

- $\dfrac{25}{6}\%$
- $96\%$
- $25\%$
- $4\%$
- $0.04\%$

## Explanation

The solution mass is the sum $4+96=100\,\mathrm g$. The solute mass fraction is therefore $\dfrac{4}{100}=0.04$, equivalent to $4\%$. The denominator includes the solute. Dividing by $96$ instead compares solute mass with solvent mass alone and defines a different ratio.
