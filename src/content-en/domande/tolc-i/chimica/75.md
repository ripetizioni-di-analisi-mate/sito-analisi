Which equation conserves atoms and charge when zinc metal reacts with hydrogen ions to form $\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}$ and molecular hydrogen?

- $\mathrm{Zn+H^+\to Zn^{2+}+H_2}$
- $\mathrm{2Zn+2H^+\to Zn^{2+}+H_2}$
- $\mathrm{Zn+2H^+\to Zn^++H_2}$
- $\mathrm{Zn+H_2\to Zn^{2+}+2H^+}$
- $\mathrm{Zn+2H^+\to Zn^{2+}+H_2}$

## Explanation

One hydrogen molecule requires two hydrogen atoms, supplied by two protons. The initial charge is thus $+2$, matching the final zinc ion. Zinc loses two electrons and the two protons gain them. Both atoms and charge must be checked simultaneously for an ionic reaction.
