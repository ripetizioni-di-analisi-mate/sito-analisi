What is the oxidation number of oxygen in $\mathrm{O_2}$?

- $-2$
- $0$
- $-1$
- $+2$
- $+4$

## Explanation

In an elemental substance, each atom has oxidation number zero. The two atoms in $\mathrm{O_2}$ share electrons between nuclei of the same element, so no formal charge separation is assigned. The value $-2$ is common in oxygen compounds but does not apply to the free element.
