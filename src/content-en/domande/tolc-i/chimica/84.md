Which description of benzene, $\mathrm{C_6H_6}$, is consistent with the elementary aromatic model?

- An acyclic alkane with single bonds only
- A salt made of six carbon cations
- A mixture of unbonded carbon and hydrogen
- A carbon ring with delocalized electrons
- A molecule containing six hydroxyl groups

## Explanation

Benzene is an aromatic ring compound with a system of delocalized electrons. Its formula $\mathrm{C_6H_6}$ is incompatible with a saturated acyclic alkane, which would have fourteen hydrogens for six carbons. The aromatic molecular description concerns bonding and electron distribution, not an elemental mixture or ionic lattice.
