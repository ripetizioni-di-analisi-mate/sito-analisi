At $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, a dilute solution has $[\mathrm{OH^-}]=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. Taking the ion product as $10^{-14}\,(\mathrm{mol/L})^2$, what is its pH?

- $3$
- $11$
- $7$
- $-3$
- $14$

## Explanation

The ion product gives $[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]=\dfrac{10^{-14}}{10^{-3}}=10^{-11}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. Therefore pH is $11$. Three is the pOH, the negative logarithm of hydroxide concentration. The solution is basic because hydroxide exceeds hydronium under the stated conditions.
