Which formula contains a carboxyl group?

- $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3CHO}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COOH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3OCH_3}$

## Explanation

A carboxyl group combines a carbonyl and hydroxyl attached to the same carbon, summarized as COOH. It occurs in ethanoic acid, $\mathrm{CH_3COOH}$. An alcohol has OH on a saturated carbon; aldehydes and ketones have carbonyls with different connections. The group's structure matters, not merely the oxygen count.
