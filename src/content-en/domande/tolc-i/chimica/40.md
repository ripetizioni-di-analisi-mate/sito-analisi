In the neutral structure $\mathrm{H_2C=CH{-}CH_3}$, how many bonds does the central carbon form in total, counting a double bond twice?

- $2$
- $3$
- $5$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

The central carbon is connected to the first carbon by a double bond, to the third by a single bond and to one hydrogen by another single bond. The count is therefore $2+1+1=4$. There are only three neighboring atoms, but the bond count, including bond order, is four.
