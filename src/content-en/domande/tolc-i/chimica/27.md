What is the oxidation number of sulfur in $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$, taking each oxygen as $-2$?

- $+8$
- $+6$
- $+4$
- $+2$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The sum must equal the ion charge, namely $-2$. Writing $x$ for sulfur's value, $x+4(-2)=-2$, hence $x=+6$. Setting the sum to zero would give $+8$ but would incorrectly treat the ion as a neutral molecule. The superscript charge applies to the entire group.
