Glucose and NaCl both dissolve in water. Which difference explains the greater conductivity of a NaCl solution at comparable concentrations under usual conditions?

- Glucose contains no electrons
- Water always becomes a metal with NaCl
- NaCl releases mobile ions, whereas glucose remains mainly molecular
- All glucose turns into oxygen
- Conductivity depends only on color

## Explanation

Electric current requires mobile charge carriers. Dissolved NaCl supplies sodium cations and chloride anions. Glucose molecules instead remain predominantly neutral, and dissolution alone does not produce a comparable amount of ions. Solubility and electrolyte behavior are therefore distinct properties.
