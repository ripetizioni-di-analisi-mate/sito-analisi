Carbon is assigned $+4$ in carbon dioxide. Which interpretation is correct?

- Each molecule contains a separate free carbon ion
- The carbon nucleus loses four protons
- The molecule has total charge $+4$
- Carbon becomes an element with atomic number four
- It is a formal oxidation number, not proof of a free C4+ ion inside the molecule

## Explanation

Oxidation number comes from a convention for assigning electrons and helps track redox reactions. In a covalent compound it does not necessarily identify the actual charge of a separate ion. $\mathrm{CO_2}$ is neutral and contains polar covalent bonds; the carbon nucleus keeps its proton number.
