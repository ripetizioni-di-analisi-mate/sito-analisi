Which condensed formula represents an alcohol?

- $\mathrm{CH_3CHO}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COOH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3OCH_3}$

## Explanation

In ethanol, the OH group is bonded to a saturated carbon and identifies the alcohol function. In a carboxylic acid, OH instead belongs to the COOH group, which has different properties. The other formulas represent an aldehyde, a ketone and an ether. Oxygen's presence alone cannot distinguish these classes.
