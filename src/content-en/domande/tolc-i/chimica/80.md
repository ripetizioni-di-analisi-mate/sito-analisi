In a model of complete hydrogenation, one $\mathrm{H_2}$ molecule adds across the double bond of ethene, $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$. What is the product formula, with no atom loss?

- $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$
- $\mathrm{CH_4}$
- $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$
- $\mathrm{C_4H_6}$
- $\mathrm{C_2H_6}$

## Explanation

The two carbons remain in the molecule, and the two added hydrogens join the original four. The result is $\mathrm{C_2H_6}$, ethane. The double bond becomes single and each carbon completes its valence by bonding one new hydrogen. This is addition: no carbon is added or removed.
