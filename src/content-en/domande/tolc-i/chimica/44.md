Two dilute aqueous solutions have equal analytical concentrations of monoprotic acids. One acid is strong and the other weak. Neglecting hydronium from water, which statement is correct?

- The weak acid gives more hydronium and lower pH
- The pH values are equal because analytical concentrations match
- Molar mass alone determines pH
- The strong acid gives more hydronium and lower pH
- A weak acid never produces hydronium

## Explanation

The strong acid transfers essentially all available protons per molecule to water, whereas the weak acid reacts only partially. At equal initial concentration, the strong acid therefore produces more hydronium. Since pH decreases as hydronium increases, its solution has lower pH. The comparison requires the stated concentration and proticity conditions.
