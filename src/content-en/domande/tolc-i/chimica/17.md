How many oxygen atoms are represented in total by $3\mathrm{Ca(NO_3)_2}$?

- $6$
- $18$
- $9$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

Each nitrate group contains three oxygen atoms. The outer subscript two means two groups per formula unit, so each unit contains $3\cdot2=6$ oxygen atoms. The coefficient three multiplies the entire formula, giving $3\cdot6=18$. Using only one of the two multipliers would omit part of the represented composition.
