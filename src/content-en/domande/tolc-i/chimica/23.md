What is the conjugate base of $\mathrm{HCO_3^-}$ when the ion donates a proton?

- $\mathrm{H_2CO_3}$
- $\mathrm{CO_2}$
- $\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}$
- $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$
- $\mathrm{CO_3^-}$

## Explanation

Removing a proton removes a hydrogen atom with charge $+1$. The remaining charge therefore changes from $-1$ to $-2$, while carbon and oxygen remain unchanged, giving $\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}$. $\mathrm{H_2CO_3}$ would instead be the conjugate acid obtained by gaining a proton. The charge change is part of the transformation.
