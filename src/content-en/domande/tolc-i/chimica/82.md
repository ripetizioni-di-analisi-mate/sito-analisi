Which feature allows the dipoles of water's two O–H bonds to give a nonzero overall contribution?

- The molecule is linear and perfectly symmetric
- The molecule has a bent geometry
- Hydrogen is identical to oxygen
- Water is necessarily a positive ion
- Both O–H bonds are nonpolar

## Explanation

O–H bonds are polar and the molecule is bent. Their contributions therefore do not point in opposite directions and do not cancel completely. The molecule remains electrically neutral but has an asymmetric internal charge distribution. Electrical neutrality and absence of a dipole are different properties.
