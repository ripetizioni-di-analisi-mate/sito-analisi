A reaction can theoretically produce $20\,\mathrm g$ of pure substance. After isolation, $15\,\mathrm g$ are obtained. What is the percentage yield?

- $25\%$
- $5\%$
- $75\%$
- $\dfrac{400}{3}\%$
- $35\%$

## Explanation

Yield compares the amount actually obtained with the maximum theoretical amount. Thus $\dfrac{15}{20}\cdot100\%=75\%$. The value $25\%$ is the fraction not recovered relative to the theoretical amount. Reversing the ratio would give more than one hundred percent and would not represent the requested yield.
