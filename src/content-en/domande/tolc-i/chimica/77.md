A salt's solubility changes from $40$ to $15\,\mathrm g$ per $100\,\mathrm g$ of water when a solution is cooled. Initially it is saturated and contains $100\,\mathrm g$ of water. Without evaporation, how much salt crystallizes at final equilibrium?

- $15\,\mathrm g$
- $25\,\mathrm g$
- $40\,\mathrm g$
- $55\,\mathrm g$
- $0\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Initially forty grams are dissolved. At the new temperature, only fifteen can remain dissolved in the same water amount. The difference $40-15=25\,\mathrm g$ enters the solid phase. Total solute remains forty grams: its distribution between solution and crystals changes, not the overall salt mass.
