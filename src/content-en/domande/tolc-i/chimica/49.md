An ionic salt conducts current when molten, whereas its solid crystal conducts very poorly. Which change explains the difference?

- All ions become neutral atoms
- Melting creates new protons
- Only electrons form, with no nuclei
- The ions become mobile in the liquid
- The salt necessarily becomes a metal

## Explanation

Ions are present both before and after melting. In the crystal they occupy constrained positions; in the liquid they can move under an electric field and carry charge. The difference therefore concerns carrier mobility, not creation of carriers or conversion of the salt into another element.
