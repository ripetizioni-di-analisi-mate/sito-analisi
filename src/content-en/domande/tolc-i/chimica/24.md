Which description of bonding in the $\mathrm{H_2}$ molecule is correct?

- A proton is permanently transferred between nuclei
- Two positive ions attract without electrons
- A neutron forms the bond between the atoms
- One electron pair is shared between two nuclei
- Electrons remain excluded from the nuclear region

## Explanation

A covalent bond involves sharing an electron pair attracted by both nuclei. In $\mathrm{H_2}$ the atoms are identical, so the bond is nonpolar. The molecule retains two distinct nuclei; a chemical bond does not consist of nuclear fusion or proton transfer between them.
