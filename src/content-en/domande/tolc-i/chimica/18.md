A sample of $\mathrm{O_2}$ contains $1.2\cdot10^{24}$ oxygen atoms. Using $N_A=6.0\cdot10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}$, how many moles of molecules does it contain?

- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

Each $\mathrm{O_2}$ molecule contains two atoms, so the number of molecules is $\dfrac{1.2\cdot10^{24}}{2}=6.0\cdot10^{23}$. Dividing by the Avogadro constant gives one mole of molecules. Two moles would instead refer to the atoms: the counted entity must remain explicit.
