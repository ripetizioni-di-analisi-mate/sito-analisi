Which change preserves the chemical identity of water molecules?

- Electrolysis into hydrogen and oxygen
- Reaction of water with sodium metal
- Evaporation of water
- Chemical decomposition of water
- Formation of water from hydrogen and oxygen

## Explanation

During evaporation, molecules pass from liquid to gas while remaining $\mathrm{H_2O}$ molecules. Distances and interactions between molecules change. The other changes produce or consume water through a reaction, changing the identities of the substances present. An observable change does not necessarily imply a chemical transformation.
