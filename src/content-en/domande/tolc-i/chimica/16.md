Which equation correctly describes complete combustion of methane?

- $\mathrm{CH_4+2O_2\to CO_2+2H_2O}$
- $\mathrm{CH_4+O_2\to CO_2+2H_2O}$
- $\mathrm{CH_4+2O_2\to CO+2H_2O}$
- $\mathrm{CH_4+2O_2\to2CO_2+H_2O}$
- $\mathrm{CH_4+O_2\to CO_2+H_2O}$

## Explanation

One carbon atom requires one $\mathrm{CO_2}$ molecule, and four hydrogen atoms require two water molecules. The products then contain four oxygen atoms, supplied by two $\mathrm{O_2}$ molecules. Carbon, hydrogen and oxygen are thus conserved. CO would also represent a product of incomplete combustion.
