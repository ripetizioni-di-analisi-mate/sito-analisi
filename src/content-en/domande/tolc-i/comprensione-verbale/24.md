A customer describes a chair as “antique”. The joiner’s estimate says “received as antique”. The estimate covers only repairing one leg and includes no appraisal.

To whom does the wording of the estimate attribute the description?

- To an appraiser who dated the wood
- To whoever has already completed the repair
- To the joiner as issuer of a certificate
- To the customer who presented the chair in those terms
- To the original maker of the chair

## Explanation

“Received as” reports the description under which the item arrived. The quoted work concerns a leg and does not include establishing age.
