A bulletin presents reports received by three service desks during one week. Zero means that a desk submitted its return with no reports; a dash means that its return has not arrived. Each report is assigned to only one desk.

| Desk | Reports |
| --- | ---: |
| North | 12 |
| Central | 0 |
| South | — |

The methodological note specifies that the published total includes only available returns. It must therefore not be used as the certain total for the entire network. The previous week, every desk submitted data, giving a total of fifteen reports. The editor advises against describing the difference between the two published totals as an established decrease in overall reports: the comparison is affected by the different completeness of the data.

Which piece of information would complete the current total?

- South’s report count from the previous week
- The current total for North and Central alone
- South’s historical average used instead of the current figure
- The difference between the two already published totals
- The number of reports South received in the current week

## Explanation

The only absent return is South’s for the relevant week. Recovering it would complete coverage of the total. South’s previous count and historical average do not replace its current count; North and Central are already included, and the difference between totals does not reveal the missing value.
