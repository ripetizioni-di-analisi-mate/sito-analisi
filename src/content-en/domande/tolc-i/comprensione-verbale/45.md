On a workshop map, the number beside each town indicates how many workshops joined the catalogue. Participation is optional. Two towns show the same number, four.

Which comparison can be made between the two towns?

- Their industrial output is equal
- Four workshops in each town are absent
- The same proportion of workshops joined in each
- They have the same total number of workshops
- They have the same number of workshops in the catalogue

## Explanation

Four counts participating workshops. Comparing participation rates would require the total workshops in each town, which optional participation leaves unknown.
