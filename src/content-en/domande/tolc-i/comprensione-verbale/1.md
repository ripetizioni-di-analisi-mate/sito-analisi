In its quarterly report the library distinguishes two time bands. Entries before noon were steady, and entries after dinner rose. The counter clicks at every entry, including when someone returns the same evening.

An editor must complete “After dinner there were more…”. Which subject is documented?

- recorded entries
- individual loans
- minutes of reading per person
- first-time readers
- distinct visitors

## Explanation

The same person can trigger the counter more than once. The rise concerns the events counted in the evening time band.
