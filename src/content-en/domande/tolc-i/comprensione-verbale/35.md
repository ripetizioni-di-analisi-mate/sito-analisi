A call for proposals ranks two criteria: technical quality first, then cost only when technical ratings are tied. One bid is cheaper than every other bid.

What information is missing before one can conclude that the cheapest bid is chosen?

- That the bid received a passing technical score even if a rival scored higher
- That the price gap exceed the difference in technical scores
- That the other bids are priced above the minimum permitted
- That another bid has the same price
- That no bid has a higher technical rating than this one

## Explanation

Cost decides only among bids at the best technical level achieved. If a rival had a higher technical rating, the lower price would not suffice.
