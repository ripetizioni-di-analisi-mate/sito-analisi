The harbour log gives two times for the same signal. The keeper records when he pulls the bell rope. The sentry at the far pier records when she hears the sound. Both use synchronised clocks.

What should be distinguished before attributing the difference to a recording error?

- The discrepancy between two unsynchronised clocks and the gap between two signals on the same pier
- Emission of the signal and its reception
- The sound of two bells under the same name
- The order of two successive signals
- The scheduled test time and the time at which the keeper decided to cancel it

## Explanation

Pulling the rope and hearing the sound are distinct events. Synchronisation makes the clocks comparable, but does not turn the events into a single instant.
