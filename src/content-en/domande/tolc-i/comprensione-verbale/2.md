The evening library log contains only the time of each entry. A volunteer recalls that some tables were used for group study. The report proposes measuring individual reading time in future.

Why would matching each entry to an exit time still leave the report’s question open?

- The report deals exclusively with loans
- Time on the premises includes activities other than reading
- An exit time would not allow length of stay to be calculated
- The activity could be identified solely from the time of day
- The counter would still confuse arrivals with departures even if they were matched

## Explanation

The difference between the times measures the length of a visit. The volunteer’s account shows why this can include time spent talking to other people.
