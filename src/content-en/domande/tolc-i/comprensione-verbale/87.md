A spending estimate gives two scenarios: with the yard available, direct delivery. With the yard closed, temporary storage. The higher total includes storage. Before ordering, access to the yard is confirmed.

How should this confirmation be used when reading the estimate?

- Treat actual expenditure as certain to the last cent
- Treat the direct delivery scenario as the relevant one
- Use the higher total anyway because it was printed
- Halve only the storage item
- Add the two totals because both had been forecast

## Explanation

The two totals depend on alternative states of the yard. Confirmation identifies the applicable scenario without turning the estimate into final accounts.
