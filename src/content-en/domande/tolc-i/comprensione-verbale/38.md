The office promises to answer “questions received before the meeting, except those already covered in the memo”. Nora’s question arrived on Monday, the meeting is on Tuesday, and the memo answers her question.

What does the promise imply for Nora?

- Her question was submitted after the deadline
- She is entitled to a further reply solely because she wrote on Monday
- Her question is excluded from the commitment to give a further reply
- The memo loses effect when a question arrives
- The office must reopen the memo on Tuesday

## Explanation

Nora meets the timing requirement but falls under the exception introduced by “except”. The wording allows a voluntary reply without promising one.
