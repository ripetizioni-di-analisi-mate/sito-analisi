At the close of its evening trial the library records more entries after dinner. Management has asked for a comparison of costs, visits and activities before deciding whether to retain the new hours.

Which headline gives due weight both to the result and to the next step?

- Library to close in the evening to contain costs
- Costs rise faster than visits
- Evening opening confirmed thanks to new loans
- Evening entries rise, decision on hours to follow review
- Review deferred until entries rise

## Explanation

The report presents the increase as observed and the decision as future. The economic comparison has been requested, but its outcome is still open.
