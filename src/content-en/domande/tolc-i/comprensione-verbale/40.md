A rehearsal-room sheet says, “Take a break between reading and recording.” The afternoon log lists, in order, reading, recording, break. The technician objects only to the placement of the break.

Which minimal change implements the instruction?

- Remove the break and retain reading and recording
- Start with the break and then read and record
- Add a second reading after the break
- Swap reading and recording while leaving the break last
- Move the break before recording and after reading

## Explanation

“Between” requires the two activities to fall on opposite sides of the break. In the log both precede it. Moving the break forward one position restores the required order.
