A list of corrections says, “On page 8, for ‘from north to south’ read ‘from south to north’.” The arrow on the accompanying map already pointed north. The editor changed only the sentence.

Which account describes the relationship between map and sentence after the change?

- The arrow corrects only the former direction on the map
- The two indications now point opposite ways
- The new sentence no longer specifies a direction
- The sentence and the arrow were both reversed
- The sentence now indicates the direction the arrow already showed

## Explanation

Travelling from south to north means heading north. The correction brings the words into line with the arrow without requiring a map change.
