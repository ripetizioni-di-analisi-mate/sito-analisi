A contract states “average wait of two days”. During the quarter some cases finished the same day and others took five days. A user takes the wording as a promise that their case will finish within two days.

Which distinction resolves the misunderstanding?

- A mean across quarters and a mean of same-day cases alone
- The maximum time that quarter and the slowest case observed in that quarter
- Elapsed time and the number of cases still open
- The typical opening day and the typical completion day of a case
- An average across cases and an upper limit for each case

## Explanation

A five-day case can coexist with faster ones and contribute to a two-day average. A two-day upper limit would rule it out.
