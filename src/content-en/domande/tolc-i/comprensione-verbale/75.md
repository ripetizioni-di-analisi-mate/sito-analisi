“Everyone understood the new sign,” writes a manager after a trial. The minutes report that everyone present chose the correct exit and that, moments before, a guide had pointed it out aloud.

Which conclusion distinguishes the observed outcome from the sign’s contribution?

- The guide certainly had to correct a wrong direction on the sign
- Without the guide no one present would have chosen correctly
- Correct choices unambiguously measure how understandable the sign was
- The sign was necessarily ignored by everyone
- The exit was chosen correctly, but the spoken instruction could also have guided the choice

## Explanation

Both cues were available before the choice. The observed behaviour fits reading the sign, following the spoken instruction, or both.
