An author delivers two files: a “commented draft”, with alternatives in the margin, and a “print copy”, without comments. A reader of the draft claims both proposed endings will be printed.

Which document should be checked directly against the claim?

- A list of recipients of the draft
- Only the note confirming the existence of alternatives
- The date when the reader made the remark
- The print copy, checking whether it contains both endings
- The receipt for delivery of both files, without opening them

## Explanation

Alternatives in the margin document a stage of choice. The file called “print copy” is where to verify whether both endings entered the text intended for readers.
