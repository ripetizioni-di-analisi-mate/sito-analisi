A schedule estimates twelve days for a job “from receipt of all drawings”. The order is signed on the first of the month. The last drawing arrives on the fifth. The estimate distinguishes order date from the start of the count.

Which event starts the stated estimate?

- Receipt of the first drawing, because the count starts only once
- Signature of the order, even without drawings
- Receipt of the final drawing, completing the required material
- The day the expected duration was announced
- The day when the client began drawing

## Explanation

“All” makes the completion of the set relevant. The first of the month identifies signing, an event the estimate keeps separate.
