To compare two alarms a technician listens in the same room. One rings while the fan is on, the other when it is off. The report attributes the second alarm’s greater audibility to its different signal.

Which new test would better separate the two explanations?

- Raise fan speed and the volume of the first alarm together
- Repeat only the second alarm in silence
- Listen to each alarm both with the fan on and with it off
- Ask which alarm is preferred after a single trial in the original conditions
- Change room and listener for every trial

## Explanation

The original comparison changes alarm and background noise together. Testing each under both conditions allows signal comparisons at the same fan setting.
