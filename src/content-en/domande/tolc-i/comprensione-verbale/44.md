The festival table lists “Saturday 40 workshops, Sunday 35”. A note says, “A workshop lasting two days appears in both columns.” The organiser wants to know how many distinct workshops were offered.

Which information would make the sum of the columns usable for this purpose?

- The number of two-day workshops, to add a second time
- The number of Sunday-only workshops, to remove from the total
- The number of workshops starting Saturday, to subtract in full
- The number of workshops appearing in both, to subtract once
- The number of places offered across the two days, to subtract once

## Explanation

Each two-day workshop enters the sum twice but should enter the distinct list once. Subtracting one copy for each overlap corrects the double count.
