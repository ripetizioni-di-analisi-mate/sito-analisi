In a project report two estimates differ because one also includes testing. To compare them the client requests both “up to delivery of the part, excluding testing”.

What is the effect of the client’s request?

- It guarantees both suppliers use the same production times
- It turns the suppliers’ estimates into recorded actual times
- It makes it certain that testing will never take place
- It shows the broader initial estimate was an arithmetic mistake
- It aligns the scope of the estimates without requiring their values to match

## Explanation

Excluding testing from both removes a difference in scope. Estimates of the remaining work may still differ even though they now refer to the same set of activities.
