The survey invitation was addressed to adult commuters. Participation was voluntary, and nobody changed means of transport at the researchers’ request.

Which sentence in a methods note would need a correction?

- “Answers came from those who opted in”
- “The invitation could be declined”
- “The research team assigned transport to participants”
- “The invitation specified an adult population”
- “Transport had been chosen by the commuters”

## Explanation

Attributing the assignment to the team turns a choice already made by the commuters into an experimental intervention. The passage explicitly allows freedom both of transport choice and of participation.
