A report says, “The box is dry. Yet the sheet inside has a stain.” An earlier draft used “therefore” in place of “yet”.

What relationship does the final wording suggest?

- The sheet dried after the box
- The dry box produced the stain
- The second observation cancels the first
- The stain proves that the box is now wet
- The stain runs counter to the expectation created by the dry box

## Explanation

“Yet” places together two observations that are surprising in combination. “Therefore” would present the second as a consequence of the first without further evidence.
