A museum projects a reconstruction of a drawing invisible to the naked eye. The caption distinguishes solid lines detected on the panel from dashed lines joining missing parts to make the figure readable.

What information would visitors lose if everything were displayed with solid lines?

- The overall shape proposed by the makers of the projection
- The boundary between detected marks and proposed joins
- The graphic position of the parts brought together on the panel
- The number of figures visible in the projection
- The ability to follow the completed figure with the eye

## Explanation

Line style in the key declares the source of the information. Making it uniform would make reconstructed segments look equally well documented.
