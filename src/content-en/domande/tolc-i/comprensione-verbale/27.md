In a story the caretaker closes the door, then the account shifts from the corridor to her thoughts: “Now they will think I have left.” The reader finds her at the desk behind the door.

What does the story reveal by shifting to the caretaker’s thoughts and then to the desk?

- The door can be seen from the corridor
- The caretaker is still inside and anticipates a mistaken interpretation
- The door has been closed
- The room opens onto the corridor
- A caretaker works in the building

## Explanation

The closed door is visible from the corridor. The shift to the thought and then to the desk reveals what the gesture could conceal from others.
