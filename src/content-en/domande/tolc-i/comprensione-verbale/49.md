A questionnaire asked, “Which modes of transport did you use yesterday? Tick all that apply.” Sixty per cent ticked bus and 55 per cent train. Both percentages use all respondents as the denominator.

What conclusion is forced by the sum exceeding 100%?

- All train users also took a bus
- The two questions were asked of separate groups
- Fifteen per cent used only the train
- Some respondents selected both modes
- Some must have left the form blank

## Explanation

If the groups were disjoint, their combined size could not exceed all respondents. Multiple selections let one person appear in both.
