At a meeting the minutes read, “Three people present abstained. Among those casting a vote, supporters outnumbered opponents.” The minutes do not give the vote totals.

Which result follows even without knowing total attendance?

- The gap between supporters and opponents is three
- Everyone present cast a vote for or against
- Supporters account for over half of everyone present
- Supporters account for over half the votes cast for or against
- Opponents number fewer than the three abstainers

## Explanation

Of two groups of votes, the larger exceeds half their combined total. Adding abstainers changes the denominator, and the broader claim would need the counts.
