A designer predicts, “If light enters only through the top slot, covering it will leave the base dark.” He covers it and the base remains lit. The log confirms the cover was opaque and complete.

Which hypothesis needs reviewing in light of the test outcome?

- A conclusion that light enters specifically from the right
- The belief that the base’s brightness, observed only as still lit, has been precisely measured
- The statement that the cover was in place, without further evidence
- The record that the base was lit, to bring it into line with the prediction
- The hypothesis that the top slot is the only light entrance

## Explanation

With the slot actually covered, the predicted consequence, darkness, fails. The single-entrance hypothesis falls, but the test alone does not locate another opening.
