The report specifies that well-being is reported by adult commuters who replied. The comparison concerns group averages based on usual means of transport.

Which three qualifications belong in a faithful summary?

- All adults, medical diagnosis, comparison of averages
- Respondents, clinically verified health, comparison of averages
- Respondents, identical journeys, individual comparison
- All adult commuters, self-reported well-being, comparison of averages
- Respondents, self-reported data, comparison of averages

## Explanation

The opening sentence limits both the population and the type of data. “Group averages” supplies the third qualification and does not imply that every cyclist outscores every other commuter.
