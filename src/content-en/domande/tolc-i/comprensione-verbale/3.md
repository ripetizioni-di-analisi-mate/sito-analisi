“Evening entries increased, while those in the morning remained steady.” The previous line of the library report mentioned the counters installed at the two doors.

Which rewriting resolves “those” while preserving the comparison?

- No counters were replaced in the morning
- The same people came in each morning
- The number of morning entries stayed steady
- Each morning visit lasted the same time
- The same number of counters was used in the morning

## Explanation

“Evening” and “in the morning” qualify the same noun, “entries”. Substituting “counters” for the pronoun would change the quantity compared across the two time bands.
