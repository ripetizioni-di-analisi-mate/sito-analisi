“How punctual!” says the supervisor, looking at the clock as the technician enters. The appointment slip on the table says nine. The clock shows ten and the technician mutters, “I missed the train.”

Which clue supports an ironic reading of the exclamation?

- The supervisor looks at a clock, even without comparing it with the agreed time
- The technician has come for an appointment
- The technician mentions the train, irrespective of the scheduled time
- There is an appointment slip on the table, even if its time were unknown
- The actual time contradicts the praise for punctuality

## Explanation

Ten is an hour after the appointment. In that setting the praise communicates reproach, reinforced by the technician’s attempt to explain.
