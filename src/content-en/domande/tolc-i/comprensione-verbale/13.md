In a textile guide “compact” described both a tightly woven fabric and a sample book that was easy to carry. The editor replaced only the second occurrence with “taking up little space”.

Which property does “compact” still describe in the revised version?

- The weight of the entire volume
- The stiffness of a fabric that will not bend
- The closeness of the threads in the fabric
- How easily the sample book can be stored
- The small thickness of the closed sample book

## Explanation

The replacement concerns the sample book, where size mattered. The remaining occurrence goes with “tightly woven”, which describes the weave.
