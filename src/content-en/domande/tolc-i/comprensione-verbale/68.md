In the maintenance log “resolved” may be entered only after a full test. A technician writes, “Component replaced. Test postponed.”

Which heading accurately summarises the entry?

- Replacement postponed until after testing
- Fault verified as resolved
- Work performed, verification still pending
- Component to be ordered
- Test completed with no intervention

## Explanation

“Replaced” places the work in the past; “postponed” places the test in the future. The log’s requirement for writing “resolved” has not yet been met.
