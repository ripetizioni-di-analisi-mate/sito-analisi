On a tram dashboard a bar represents passengers boarding during the hour. A dot represents passengers on board at the start of the following hour. The nine o’clock bar reaches 30 and the ten o’clock dot reaches 12.

How can both values be correct?

- The bar includes everyone present at nine, even those who boarded earlier
- The bar counts events over time, the dot shows who is still on board at one instant
- The gap between 30 and 12 must give everyone who alighted, whatever the initial load
- The dot counts only how many of the 30 boarders remained, excluding everyone already aboard
- The bar represents the maximum and the dot the minimum load for the hour

## Explanation

Some passengers may have alighted during the hour. The initial number on board is also unknown, so simply subtracting 12 from 30 would not generally give the number who got off.
