A workshop statement distinguishes a “deposit refunded when the key is returned” from the “participation fee”. The organiser wants to compare the ultimate cost for two students who both return the key.

How should the deposit be treated in this comparison?

- As a second fee always retained
- As the only expense, ignoring the fee
- As an advance that is refunded and does not affect ultimate cost
- As a fine on every return
- As a prize deducted even if never paid

## Explanation

Both meet the refund condition. The deposit affects the cash needed upfront, but payment and return offset each other in the final balance.
