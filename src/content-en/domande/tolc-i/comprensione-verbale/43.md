In an order summary “total” includes orders cancelled after issue. The “shipped” row excludes cancellations and orders still awaiting preparation. A reader subtracts shipped from total and labels the result “cancelled”.

Which component has the reader overlooked?

- Shipments already delivered but not yet paid for
- Shipments recorded but not yet delivered to the customer
- Orders still awaiting preparation
- Orders cancelled after issue
- Orders shipped and delivered

## Explanation

Removing shipped orders leaves two categories identified by the summary, cancelled and awaiting preparation. The difference equals cancellations only if nothing remains to prepare.
