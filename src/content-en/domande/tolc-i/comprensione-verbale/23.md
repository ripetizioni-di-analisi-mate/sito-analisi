A photographer publishes two images of the same sill. In the second she moves only the curtain. She writes, “I did not retouch the colour of the wall. The reflection from the fabric is enough to change its appearance.”

What changes according to the photographer?

- The fabric’s own colour between the first shot and the second
- The wall’s position in relation to the sill
- The way the wall appears in the photograph
- The paint applied to the wall
- Colour added by digital retouching

## Explanation

“Change its appearance” refers to the effect of reflected light. The wall remains the subject, and the stated intervention is moving the curtain.
