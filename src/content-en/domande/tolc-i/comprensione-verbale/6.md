Commuters who answered the questionnaire and cycle report greater well-being on average. The authors note that prior health could have influenced the choice of transport.

Which news brief preserves the strength of the original conclusion?

- Among respondents cycling is associated with greater reported well-being
- Prior health explains the entire observed difference
- Among respondents cycling improved each individual’s well-being
- Distance explains the cyclists’ advantage
- The effect of transport was measured before the survey

## Explanation

“Report … on average” describes the observed comparison. The prior-health explanation remains possible and prevents the whole difference from being attributed to cycling.
