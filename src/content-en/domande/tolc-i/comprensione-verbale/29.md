In the first paragraph Sara picks up a key from the floor. Only in the last line do we read, “Two hours earlier Leo had dropped it while looking for his phone.”

What is the chronological sequence of events?

- Leo drops the key after seeing Sara holding it
- Sara picks up the key and then Leo loses it while looking for his phone
- The finding and the dropping are simultaneous
- Leo looks for the phone and drops the key, then Sara picks it up
- Sara takes the phone and Leo picks up the key

## Explanation

“Two hours earlier” places the last information narrated before the opening scene. “It” refers back to the key Sara picked up.
