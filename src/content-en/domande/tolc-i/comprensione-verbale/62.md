A hire service states, “The first hour is included in the opening charge.” Each subsequent hour started is billed in full. A customer keeps the tool for one hour and five minutes.

What is added to the opening charge?

- Only five minutes on a prorated basis
- One full additional hour
- Five sixtieths of the opening charge
- Nothing until the end of the second hour
- Two full additional hours

## Explanation

The first hour is already included. Five minutes start another hour, and “started” requires that hour to be billed in full.
