A newspaper headline says, “The neighbourhood calls for a track.” The article specifies that only members of the group behind the proposal attended the design workshop, and that everyone present supported it.

Which subject would make the headline faithful to the vote described?

- “All future track users”
- “The members attending the workshop”
- “All neighbourhood residents”
- “Most of those absent”
- “The municipal office that will decide”

## Explanation

Unanimity concerns those present. The article identifies them as members of the sponsoring group and records no views from other residents.
