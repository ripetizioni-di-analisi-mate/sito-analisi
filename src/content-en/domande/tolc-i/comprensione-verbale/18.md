In the catalogue a clock appears under “Pini gift, 1962”. The introduction says dates beside donors’ names record when objects entered the museum. The dial bears the date 1911.

Which note can the cataloguer write without claiming more for the dates than they show?

- The clock entered the museum in 1911
- Pini built the mechanism in 1962
- The museum acquired the clock in 1962. Its dial bears 1911
- The dial was donated in 1911 and replaced in 1962
- All the clock’s parts certainly date to 1911

## Explanation

The introduction defines the year of the gift. The inscription establishes what is written on the dial, but does not automatically date the mechanism or case.
