A circular offers course places “to the first twenty applicants in order of registration”. A poster instead says, “twenty places for the fastest to complete the course”.

Which basis of comparison has shifted in the poster?

- From the first lesson time to the date when a certificate is issued
- From the number of applications received to the number of correctly completed forms
- From cost to participant age
- From the registration date to the date the first twenty names are published
- From registration time to speed of completion

## Explanation

“In order of registration” selects applications before the course begins. The poster uses a performance that becomes known only after attendance.
