To forecast hut supplies, planners assume how many people will arrive and how many meals each will have. Actual arrivals match the forecast, but more meals are served than expected.

What can be inferred about meals per person on average, counting the same guests and period?

- Every guest necessarily ate more meals than predicted for that guest
- The forecast concerned only arrivals and never meals
- They matched the assumption exactly because arrivals matched
- They were more numerous than the assumed average
- The hut necessarily received uncounted visitors

## Explanation

The number of people, the denominator, matched the forecast while total meals increased. Meals per person therefore rose on average, without revealing how each individual stay differed.
