A radio station reports, “The trains are deserted.” Its correspondent had written, “In the rear carriage of the first Sunday train I counted three passengers. I did not go through the others.”

Which two extensions does the radio station make beyond the testimony?

- From a count of people present to a calculation of seats sold and then refunds
- From the number of empty seats in the carriage to punctuality and then fares
- From one carriage to the whole train and from one service to several
- From three passengers to three carriages and from Sunday to Saturday
- From a morning service to afternoon services only, and from the rear to first class

## Explanation

The correspondent counted the passengers in a single carriage, the rear one, and states that he did not go through the others. His observation also concerns a single service, the first Sunday train. The headline “The trains are deserted” instead speaks of trains in the plural, with no limit on carriages or services. Therefore the radio station extends the testimony from one carriage to the whole train and from one service to several. The other options attribute to the radio station claims about seats sold, punctuality, days or times of day, which the headline does not mention.
