A presenter introduces a radio piece. “First you will hear today’s train, then the stationmaster from those days. The whistle joining the two was added in the studio.” A listener cites the whistle as evidence that the old train sounded like that too. Which detail determines whether the quotation is valid evidence?

- The broadcast order proves the whistle was recorded first
- The stationmaster’s presence authenticates every sound in the piece
- The whistle is a production transition with no historical origin documented in the piece
- Two consecutive sounds necessarily belong to the same recording
- The presenter describes the old whistle as louder than the new one

## Explanation

The presenter attributes the link to the studio. The whistle can smooth the transition between recordings, but the piece supplies no evidence that it came from the old train.
