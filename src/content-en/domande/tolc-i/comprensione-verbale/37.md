A garage sign separates two entitlements. An hourly ticket permits car entry. Bicycle storage is reserved for subscribers. An hourly ticket does not count as a subscription. Paolo has only an hourly ticket.

Which request is covered by Paolo’s ticket?

- Storing his bicycle for a week
- Entering by car while the ticket is valid
- Automatically converting the ticket into a subscription
- Reserving bicycle storage for a friend
- Using bicycle storage provided the car stays outside

## Explanation

The first sentence assigns car entry to the ticket. Bicycle storage requires a subscription, and the sign excludes treating the two as equivalent.
