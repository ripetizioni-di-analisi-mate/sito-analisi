A display in front of a counter is readable to anyone walking straight in from the door. During tests all staff approach from that side. Two customers explain that from the queue they see it at an angle and cannot distinguish the last digit.

Which test would examine the difficulty the customers describe?

- Check from behind the counter which number is sent to the display
- Have people read the whole number from several actual queue positions
- Repeat the reading from the front entrance several times
- Count customers who entered through the main door
- Ask testers to recall the last digit they saw head-on

## Explanation

The clue is the oblique viewing angle, absent from the initial tests. Reading the whole number from the queue checks whether the last digit disappears under actual use conditions.
