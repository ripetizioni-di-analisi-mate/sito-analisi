A contract defines a “local” supplier as one based within twenty kilometres of the depot. Parts may be made elsewhere. A sheet says that a local workshop supplied the panels.

Which rewriting follows the stipulated definition?

- The workshop itself manufactured every panel
- Every panel component comes from the workshop’s premises
- The panels were made within twenty kilometres of the depot
- The supplying workshop is based within twenty kilometres of the depot
- The actual delivery route was shorter than twenty kilometres

## Explanation

“Local” qualifies the supplier’s place of business. The contract expressly permits manufacturing elsewhere and gives no delivery route.
