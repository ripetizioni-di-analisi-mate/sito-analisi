The printing instructions say, “Keep the transparency until the office has compared the copy with the original.” The copy has come out of the printer, but the office has not received it yet.

Which event ends the stated duty to retain the transparency?

- The start of printing
- Delivery of the original to the printer alone
- Completion of the comparison by the office
- The copy emerging from the printer
- Sending the copy to the office

## Explanation

“Until” sets the endpoint at completion of the comparison. Printing and dispatch may precede it, but neither substitutes for it.
