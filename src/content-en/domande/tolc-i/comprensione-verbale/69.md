A support form has two fields, “What did you observe?” and “What cause do you suspect?” A customer writes, “The picture disappears after a minute. I think the cable may be faulty.”

How should the report be distributed between the fields?

- Both statements under operating times
- Faulty cable under observations, disappearing picture under hypotheses
- One minute under proven faults and the rest removed
- Disappearing picture under observations, faulty cable under hypotheses
- Both statements under established causes

## Explanation

The customer directly reports the effect on the screen. “I think” signals that she attributes it to the cable without having established a defect.
