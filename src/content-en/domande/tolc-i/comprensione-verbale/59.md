A publisher writes, “The most viewed guide on our website in March.” Its distributor proposes printing “The year’s bestselling guide” on the cover. The site counts page views and records no shop sales.

Which evidence would be needed to support the proposed cover wording?

- The names of shops displaying the guide without sales quantities
- Additional March views of the same page
- The full-year number of page views for every guide
- Comparable guide sales for the full year in the market covered by the claim
- The page count of the guide

## Explanation

The cover changes measure, period and scope. Establishing a sales lead also requires figures for other guides in the same year and market.
