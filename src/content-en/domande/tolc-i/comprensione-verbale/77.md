Tuesday’s bulletin announced maintenance for Thursday. A Wednesday update postpones the work until Friday and says all other times remain valid. The site retains both dated bulletins.

How should the current schedule be reconstructed?

- Move all other Thursday activities to Friday too
- Replace the work date as updated and retain the other times
- Use Thursday because it was published first
- Discard every other time because it appeared in the older bulletin
- Record two jobs, one on Thursday and one on Friday

## Explanation

“Postpones” identifies one job with a new date. The update expressly limits the change to that job and confirms the other times.
