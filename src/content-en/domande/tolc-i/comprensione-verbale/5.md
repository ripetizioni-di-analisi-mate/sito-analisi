During evening opening some library users read alone at the tables and others work in groups. The library wants to estimate how long each visitor spends on the first activity, even when someone alternates between the two.

Which field in a new visit diary supplies the necessary information?

- Visitor’s favourite activity
- Total number of empty tables
- Time the visitor sat down
- Number of books placed on the table
- Minutes spent reading alone during the visit

## Explanation

Someone who sits down at eight may change activities several times. Recording the duration of reading alone separates those intervals from group work.
