A workshop accepts proposals “by Friday”. Anyone who submits a proposal on time but forgets the attachment has two extra days to supply it. Lea submitted no proposal by Friday.

Why can Lea not invoke the stated extension?

- The rule concerns only those who already submitted the attachment
- The extension presupposes timely submission of the proposal
- The attachment must precede the proposal
- The two days run from the first request for information
- The text forbids any addition after Friday

## Explanation

The clause grants time to complete an existing submission. Lea lacks precisely what it requires, a proposal submitted by the deadline.
