Instructions define a “removable” module as one that can be detached without breaking the casing. A review says, “Being removable, the module was replaced during servicing.” No service sheet is attached.

Which document would most directly fill the gap in the review?

- A price list quoting the cost of replacement
- A manual repeating the definition of “removable”
- A drawing of the fitting confirming removability
- A catalogue listing compatible spare parts
- A service record documenting removal and installation of the replacement

## Explanation

The review claims that work actually took place. Drawings, manuals and prices describe what can be done, while the service record would document the work performed.
