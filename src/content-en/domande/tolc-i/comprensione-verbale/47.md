A paper publishes the time of the “middle runner” in a race with five finishes ordered fastest to slowest. A timing error changes only the last time, which remains the slowest.

Which part of the account can stay unchanged after correction?

- The time gap between first and last
- The third finisher’s time
- The time assigned to the last finisher
- The combined time of all five
- The arithmetic mean of the five times

## Explanation

The middle runner occupies third place. Fifth remains fifth, and that time does not enter the selection of the middle value, though it does enter the sum and mean.
