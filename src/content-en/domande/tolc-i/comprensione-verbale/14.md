A director’s notebook has two lines crossed out in pencil. The margin reads, “Cut them only for school performances, so we can reach the discussion on time.” Both lines are audible in the evening premiere recording.

How can the notebook and the recording be reconciled?

- The cuts had been proposed for all performances but not yet approved
- The lines were added after the premiere
- Pencil always indicates a note made after recording
- The marginal note limits the cut to a different type of performance
- The lines were retained only in the recording, regardless of performance type

## Explanation

“Only for school performances” specifies where the crossing-out applies. The evening premiere falls outside that case, so it may include both lines.
