A monthly magazine costs four euros per issue. Its publisher writes, “With the annual subscription you receive twelve issues for the price of ten.” An advertisement adds “two extra copies”.

Which reading of “extra” fits the offer?

- Each issue contains two additional booklets
- Fourteen issues arrive instead of twelve
- The price of two issues is added to the price of ten
- Two of the twelve issues are beyond the ten whose price is paid
- The subscription year lasts fourteen months

## Explanation

The publisher sets the number delivered at twelve. The comparison behind “extra” is with the ten paid for, not with the twelve already promised.
