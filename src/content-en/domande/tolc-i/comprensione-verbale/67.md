A card states, “If the light is red, open the log. If the log reports no paper, refill the tray.” The light is red, but the log reports only “cover open”.

Which action is warranted by the first signal, independently of what it reveals?

- Refill the tray regardless
- Consult the log
- Switch the machine off permanently
- Record that paper had run out
- Declare the cover repaired

## Explanation

The light triggers consultation. Refilling depends on a second, specific message, which “cover open” does not satisfy.
