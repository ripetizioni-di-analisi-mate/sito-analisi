A workshop announces, “If a key has been returned, its outline on the board is covered.” An inspection finds one outline uncovered. The announcement does not say whether other objects can also cover an outline.

Which inference is guaranteed by the rule?

- Someone took the key during the inspection
- Uncovered outlines indicate lost keys only
- No key at all has been returned
- The key corresponding to the uncovered outline has not been returned
- Every covered outline must have its own key on it

## Explanation

If the key had been returned, the outline would have to be covered. Finding it uncovered rules out return under the rule, without explaining where the key is.
