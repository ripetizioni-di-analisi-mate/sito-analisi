The initial design had two isolated seats. After visits to a senior centre the designers placed them together and left one side clear, so someone arriving with a walker can stop beside a seated person. The seat count remains two.

Which effect might disappear from a report recording seat numbers alone?

- A demonstrated reduction in waiting time
- The removal of one of the two seats
- The ability of someone using a walker to stop beside a seated person
- Replacement of the seats with one walker space
- An increase in how many people can sit

## Explanation

The count stays the same, but the layout and side clearance change. Those features enable the proximity described.
