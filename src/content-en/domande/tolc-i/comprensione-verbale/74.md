To test two sound signals, a museum asks entrants which they prefer. Some choose the more pleasant sound and then fail to recognise it when they need to turn on a simulated visit. The signal’s purpose is to indicate that turn.

Which distinction separates the criteria in the two test stages?

- Liking expressed at entry and memory of the name given to the sound
- Visitor opinion and exclusively designer opinion
- Recognition before the visit and enjoyment after turning
- Stated preference for the sound and ability to recognise it along the route
- A choice made alone and one made as a group

## Explanation

Liking a sound is what is asked at the entrance. Failure to recognise it when turning concerns its effectiveness in the intended task.
