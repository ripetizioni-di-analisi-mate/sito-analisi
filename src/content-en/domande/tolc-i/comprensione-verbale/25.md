To describe the noise around a machine, a transcriber uses square brackets for background sounds. The transcript reads, “Move [whistle] slowly.” Nowhere does the transcriber identify who produced the whistle.

Which reading aloud correctly distinguishes speech from annotation?

- The brackets indicate that “slowly” was inaudible
- The person said “Move, whistle slowly”
- “Whistle” names the speaker
- The transcriber instructs someone to whistle softly
- The spoken phrase is “Move slowly”, interrupted by a marked sound

## Explanation

The stated convention assigns brackets to background sounds. The whistle falls between the words, and the notation does not identify its source.
