Minutes state, “Six members present. The proposal received three votes for, two against and one abstention.” The newsletter says, “The proposal was approved unanimously by voters.” Even excluding the abstainer, the editor must change the description.

Which detail alone makes “unanimously” incorrect?

- The fact that one attendee did not vote for or against
- The fact that there were three supporting votes
- The existence of the two opposing votes
- The absence of the total club membership
- The presence of six members in the room

## Explanation

Among those voting there are both supporters and opponents. Even one opposing vote would rule out unanimity regardless of how the abstention is treated.
