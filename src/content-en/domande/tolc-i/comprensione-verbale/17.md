An undated letter gives thanks for “the newly reopened bridge” and asks about a festival still to come. A register dates the reopening to 3 May and the festival to 12 May of the same month. Both references concern that year.

What is the narrowest time interval that the two clues justify?

- Necessarily on the very day of reopening
- From the reopening on 3 May until before the festival
- Only after the end of the festival
- Only on the eve of the festival
- On any day before 3 May

## Explanation

“Reopened” places writing after the bridge event, while the festival is still ahead. Intersecting the two constraints gives an interval without fixing an exact day.
