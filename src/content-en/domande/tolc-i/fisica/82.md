Which mechanism lets the Sun transfer energy to Earth through nearly empty space?

- Conduction through air
- Radiation
- Convection of vacuum
- Transport of hot water
- Friction between planets

## Explanation

Radiation transfers energy through electromagnetic waves, which can travel in vacuum. Conduction and convection instead require matter: the former through local interactions, the latter through fluid motion. Electromagnetic radiation crosses the distance between Sun and Earth.
