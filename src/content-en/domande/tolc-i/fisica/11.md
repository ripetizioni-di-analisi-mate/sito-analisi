Which quantity remains described by a single value even when the direction of motion changes?

- Kinetic energy
- Velocity vector
- Acceleration
- Displacement
- Momentum

## Explanation

Kinetic energy $K=\dfrac12mv^2$ depends on mass and speed. It is therefore a scalar quantity. The other quantities are vectors and also require a direction and sense; knowing only their magnitude is insufficient to determine them.
