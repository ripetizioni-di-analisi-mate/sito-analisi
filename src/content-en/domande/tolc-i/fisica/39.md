An echo returns after $0.6\,\mathrm{s}$. Sound travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far away is the wall?

- $204\,\mathrm{m}$
- $51\,\mathrm{m}$
- $340\,\mathrm{m}$
- $102\,\mathrm{m}$
- $170\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The delay includes travel from the source to the wall and back. Total path length is $v\Delta t=340\cdot0.6=204\,\mathrm{m}$. Distance to the wall is half the path, or $102\,\mathrm{m}$. Using the total path would overestimate the distance by a factor of two.
