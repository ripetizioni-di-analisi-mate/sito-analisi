A fixed amount of ideal gas in a rigid container goes from $300\,\mathrm{K}$ to $450\,\mathrm{K}$. How does pressure change?

- It halves
- It stays the same
- It is multiplied by 3
- It is multiplied by 0.67
- It is multiplied by 1.5

## Explanation

The rigid container keeps volume constant. The ideal-gas equation gives $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1}=\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}=\dfrac{450}{300}=1.5$. Temperatures are absolute, so the ratio can be used directly. Pressure rises because temperature rises at unchanged volume.
