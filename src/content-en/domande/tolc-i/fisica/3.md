A net force of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a body of mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the acceleration magnitude?

- $36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Newton’s second law connects net force and acceleration, $F_{\mathrm{ris}}=ma$. Since the statement already gives the sum of forces, $a=\dfrac{12}{3}=4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ follows directly. Acceleration has the direction and sense of the net force; mass measures resistance to a change in velocity.
