A beam parallel to the axis passes through a thin diverging lens. Which description of emerging rays is correct?

- They converge at the focal point on the outgoing side
- They stay parallel to the axis
- They all return toward the source
- They converge at the lens center
- They diverge as if from the focal point on the incoming side

## Explanation

A diverging lens bends rays away from the axis. Their backward extensions meet at the focal point on the incoming side. That point is virtual: emerging rays do not actually pass through it, but appear to originate there.
