A person pushes a wall with $70\,\mathrm{N}$. Which force is the reaction specified by Newton’s third law?

- The person’s weight
- The floor’s force on the wall
- The wall’s opposite 70 N force on the person
- A 140 N force on the person
- The person’s friction force on the floor

## Explanation

The interaction pair involves the same two bodies in reverse order. The person’s force on the wall is paired with the wall’s force on the person, equal in magnitude and opposite in direction. They act on different bodies and therefore do not cancel in the force balance of the person alone.
