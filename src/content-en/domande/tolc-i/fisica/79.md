To melt $0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice already at melting temperature, use $L=3.3\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How much heat is required without losses?

- $1.65\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.3\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $660\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6.6\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The ice is already at melting temperature, so heat is needed only for the phase change. Use $Q=mL=0.2\cdot3.3\cdot10^5=6.6\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{J}$. Temperature stays constant during melting, but energy transfer is positive.
