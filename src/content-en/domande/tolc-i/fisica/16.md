A ray passes from a medium of index $2$ into air of index $1$. What is the critical angle?

- $30^\circ$
- $15^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

At the critical angle, the refracted ray runs along the surface. Snell’s law gives $2\sin\theta_c=1$, so $\sin\theta_c=\dfrac12$ and $\theta_c=30^\circ$. Total internal reflection occurs for incident angles greater than this value, measured from the normal.
