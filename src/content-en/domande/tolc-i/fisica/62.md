Two balls start simultaneously from the same height. One is dropped and the other launched horizontally. Neglecting air resistance and assuming level ground, which lands first?

- The dropped ball
- They arrive together
- The horizontally launched ball
- It depends on their masses
- The ball with greater horizontal speed

## Explanation

Both have zero initial vertical velocity and the same vertical acceleration $g$. Their vertical equation is therefore identical, $h=\dfrac12gt^2$, giving the same fall time. Horizontal velocity changes the distance reached, but not vertical motion in the model without air resistance.
