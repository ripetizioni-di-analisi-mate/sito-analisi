A crate stays at rest under a horizontal push of $8\,\mathrm{N}$. The maximum available static friction is $15\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the actual friction magnitude?

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $23\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The body is in equilibrium, so horizontal net force is zero. Static friction takes the value needed to balance the push, namely $8\,\mathrm{N}$ in the opposite direction. The value $15\,\mathrm{N}$ is an upper limit, reached only at the threshold of sliding.
