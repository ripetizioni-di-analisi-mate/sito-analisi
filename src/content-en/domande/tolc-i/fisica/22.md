An electromagnetic wave in vacuum has frequency $150\,\mathrm{MHz}$. What is its wavelength if $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $200\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The prefix mega means $10^6$, so $f=1.5\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{Hz}$. From $c=\lambda f$, $\lambda=\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{1.5\cdot10^8}=2\,\mathrm{m}$. The powers of ten cancel; ignoring the prefix would change the result by a factor of one million.
