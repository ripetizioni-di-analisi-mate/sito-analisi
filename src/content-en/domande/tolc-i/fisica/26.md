Two bodies have masses $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $2\,\mathrm{kg}$. The first moves right at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the second left at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is total momentum?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ leftward
- $2\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ rightward
- $14\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ leftward
- $14\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ rightward
- Zero

## Explanation

Take rightward as positive. The momenta are $p_1=3\cdot2=6$ and $p_2=2\cdot(-4)=-8\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$. Their sum is $-2\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$, so the magnitude is $2$ and direction is leftward. Adding magnitudes would lose the information about opposite directions.
