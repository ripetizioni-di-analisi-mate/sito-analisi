The separation of two stationary point charges doubles while the charges remain unchanged. How does the electric-force magnitude change?

- It halves
- It doubles
- It quadruples
- It becomes one quarter
- It stays unchanged

## Explanation

Coulomb’s law contains the square of separation in the denominator. Replacing $r$ by $2r$ gives $\dfrac{F_2}{F_1}=\dfrac{r^2}{(2r)^2}=\dfrac14$. Charge signs determine attraction or repulsion, but do not change this ratio of magnitudes.
