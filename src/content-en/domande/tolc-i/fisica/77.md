A crate has kinetic energy $60\,\mathrm{J}$ and stops under constant friction of $15\,\mathrm{N}$, the only force doing work. What distance does it travel?

- $900\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Stopping brings kinetic energy to zero, so net work is $-60\,\mathrm{J}$. Friction does $W=-F_ds$, hence $-15s=-60$ and $s=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Initial energy determines the required resisting work; mass is unnecessary when that energy is already given.
