What is the kinetic energy of a mass of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy is calculated with $K=\dfrac12mv^2$. The speed is squared, so $K=\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot3^2=9\,\mathrm{J}$. The product $mv=6\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ would describe momentum and have different units; the factor of one half is necessary in the energy formula.
