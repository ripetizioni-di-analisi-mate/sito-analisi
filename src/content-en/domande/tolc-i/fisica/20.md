A container holds $0.5\,\mathrm{L}$ of liquid with mass $400\,\mathrm{g}$. What is its density in SI units?

- $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Mass is $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and volume is $5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Density is $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}=\dfrac{0.4}{5\cdot10^{-4}}=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Using liters and grams directly would give a numerical value in units different from those requested.
