A magnet is held stationary relative to a stationary loop and magnetic flux stays constant. What is the induced electromotive force?

- It increases with time
- It is always one volt
- It is zero
- It depends only on the magnet’s color
- It is maximum because a field is present

## Explanation

Induction depends on the time variation of flux, not merely on the presence of a magnetic field. Since flux is constant, its change is zero and induced electromotive force is zero. Moving the magnet or changing loop orientation could instead change the flux.
