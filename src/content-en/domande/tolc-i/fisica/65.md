A simple pendulum oscillates at small amplitude with period $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{g}}$. Its length quadruples with unchanged gravity. What is the new period?

- $4T$
- $\dfrac T2$
- $\dfrac T4$
- $T$
- $2T$

## Explanation

Period depends on length through a square root. Substituting $4\ell$ gives $T_2=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{4\ell}{g}}=2T$. The small-oscillation condition justifies the stated formula; direct proportionality to length would incorrectly give $4T$.
