A ray makes $20^\circ$ with a plane mirror’s surface. What is the reflection angle measured from the normal?

- $20^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $140^\circ$
- $70^\circ$

## Explanation

The normal is perpendicular to the surface. The incident angle from the normal is therefore $90^\circ-20^\circ=70^\circ$. By the law of reflection, the reflected angle has the same value. The initial datum referred to the surface, whereas the law uses the normal.
