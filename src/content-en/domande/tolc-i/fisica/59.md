An ideal hydraulic press has piston areas $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ at the same height. Applying $30\,\mathrm{N}$ to the first, what force is obtained at the second?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pressure increase is transmitted through the liquid, so $\dfrac{F_1}{A_1}=\dfrac{F_2}{A_2}$. The area ratio is ten and $F_2=30\cdot10=300\,\mathrm{N}$. Increased force comes with a smaller displacement of the larger piston, consistent with energy conservation.
