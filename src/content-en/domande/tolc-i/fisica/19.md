A body travels around a circle at constant speed. Which description of acceleration is correct?

- It is zero
- It is tangential along the motion
- It is tangential opposite the motion
- It points toward the center
- It points outward

## Explanation

Speed stays constant, but the direction of velocity changes. Changing the velocity vector requires acceleration toward the center, with magnitude $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. Tangential acceleration would instead change speed and would therefore not describe the stated uniform motion.
