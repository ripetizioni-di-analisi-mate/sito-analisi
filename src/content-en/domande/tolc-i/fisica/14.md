A real object is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a thin converging lens of focal length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. At what distance does the real image form?

- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Using positive distances for a real object and real image, the lens equation gives $\dfrac1q=\dfrac1f-\dfrac1p=\dfrac1{10}-\dfrac1{30}=\dfrac1{15}$. Taking the reciprocal gives $q=15\,\mathrm{cm}$. The positive result confirms a real image forming on the opposite side from the object.
