Two bulbs are in parallel across an ideal source. One burns out and its branch opens. How does the other bulb’s power change, assuming its resistance is unchanged?

- It stays unchanged
- It doubles
- It halves
- It becomes zero
- It quadruples

## Explanation

The ideal source maintains the same voltage across every connected branch. For the intact bulb, $V$ and $R$ stay unchanged, and so does $P=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$. Total source current decreases because the open branch no longer contributes.
