A block slides without friction on a plane inclined at $30^\circ$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12$, what is acceleration along the plane?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The weight component parallel to the plane is $mg\sin30^\circ$. The normal force balances the perpendicular component. Along the plane, $ma=mg\sin30^\circ$, so $a=g\sin30^\circ=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Mass cancels and acceleration points downhill along the plane; the instantaneous velocity direction is not specified.
