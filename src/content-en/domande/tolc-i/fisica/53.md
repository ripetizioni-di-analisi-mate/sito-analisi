An ideal spring of constant $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed by $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$. How much elastic energy does it store?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Elastic energy is $U_e=\dfrac12kx^2$. The squared compression is $0.04\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so $U_e=\dfrac12\cdot100\cdot0.04=2\,\mathrm{J}$. Multiplying final force by compression would give twice as much, because force rises from zero during compression.
