A charge moves between two points at the same electric potential. What work does the electrostatic field do?

- Always positive
- Zero
- Always negative
- Equal to the initial potential
- It depends on path length

## Explanation

The field’s work is $W=-q\Delta V$. Since initial and final potentials are equal, $\Delta V=0$ and work is zero. The electrostatic field is conservative, so the potential difference between endpoints matters, not the length of the path followed.
