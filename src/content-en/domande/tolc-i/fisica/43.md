Velocity is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the first $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the next $2\,\mathrm{s}$. What is total displacement?

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Displacement is the signed area under the velocity-time graph. The first segment contributes $4\cdot3=12\,\mathrm{m}$ and the second $(-2)\cdot2=-4\,\mathrm{m}$. The sum is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. Adding magnitudes would instead give the distance traveled, $16\,\mathrm{m}$.
