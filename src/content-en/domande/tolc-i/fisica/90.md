A $5\,\Omega$ resistor carries a constant $2\,\mathrm{A}$ for $10\,\mathrm{s}$. How much energy does it dissipate?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Joule-heating power is $P=I^2R=2^2\cdot5=20\,\mathrm{W}$. Current is constant, so dissipated energy is $E=P\Delta t=20\cdot10=200\,\mathrm{J}$. Time multiplies power; the value $20$ would be only the power in watts.
