A lens forms an image that can be projected onto a screen. Which property is necessary for that image?

- The image is always upright
- The image is always larger than the object
- The image is on the same side as the real object
- Light rays actually converge at the image points
- Rays meet only in backward extensions

## Explanation

A screen intercepts light rays where they actually meet, forming a real image. Virtual images instead arise from intersections of ray extensions and cannot be collected directly on a screen. Projectability alone does not determine image size.
