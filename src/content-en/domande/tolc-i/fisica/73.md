A positive charge of $3\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ is moved to a potential $12\,\mathrm{V}$ higher. By how much does its potential energy increase?

- $4\,\mu\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mu\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mu\mathrm{J}$
- $-36\,\mu\mathrm{J}$
- $0.25\,\mu\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Potential-energy change is $\Delta U=q\Delta V$. Charge and potential change are both positive, so $\Delta U=3\cdot12=36\,\mu\mathrm{J}$. The field does the opposite work, $-36\,\mu\mathrm{J}$; the sign distinguishes gained potential energy from electric work.
