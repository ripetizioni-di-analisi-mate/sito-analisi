Which property allows a prism to separate white light into colors?

- Colors have different speeds in vacuum
- Index depends on wavelength
- Frequency changes at each interface
- Reflection follows different laws for each color
- The prism emits new colors

## Explanation

In the material, index varies with wavelength; this property is called dispersion. Snell’s law then produces different refraction angles for the beam’s components. Their frequencies remain those of the incident light; the deflection experienced by each component differs.
