A gas expands against constant external pressure $2\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$, increasing volume by $1\,\mathrm{L}$. How much work does it do?

- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

With constant external pressure, work is $W=p_{\mathrm{est}}\Delta V$. One liter is $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, so $W=2\cdot10^5\cdot10^{-3}=200\,\mathrm{J}$. The sign is positive because the gas expands and transfers mechanical energy to the surroundings.
