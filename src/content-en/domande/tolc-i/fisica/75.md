A source has electromotive force $12\,\mathrm{V}$ and internal resistance $1\,\Omega$. It supplies a $5\,\Omega$ external resistor. What is terminal voltage while delivering current?

- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $14\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Current passes through the internal and external resistances in series, so $I=\dfrac{12}{1+5}=2\,\mathrm{A}$. Internal voltage drop is $Ir=2\,\mathrm{V}$; terminal voltage is $12-2=10\,\mathrm{V}$. The same value appears across the load, $IR=2\cdot5=10\,\mathrm{V}$.
