A $2\,\mathrm{m}$ bar has expansion coefficient $2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. How much does it lengthen for a $50\,\mathrm{K}$ increase?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For small thermal intervals, use $\Delta\ell=\alpha\ell_0\Delta T$. The product is $2\cdot10^{-5}\cdot2\cdot50=0.002\,\mathrm{m}$, or $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. This is the length change; final length would be $2.002\,\mathrm{m}$.
