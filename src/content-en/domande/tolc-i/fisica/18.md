A person travels $100\,\mathrm{m}$ along a straight line and returns to the start. Total time is $50\,\mathrm{s}$. What are the magnitude of average velocity and average speed?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The final position equals the initial one, so displacement is zero and average velocity is zero. Distance traveled includes the outward and return trips, totaling $200\,\mathrm{m}$. Average speed is therefore $\dfrac{200}{50}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
