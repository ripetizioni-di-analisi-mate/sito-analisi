A liquid of mass $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and specific heat capacity $4000\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ warms by $3\,\mathrm{K}$ without phase changes. How much heat does it absorb?

- $2000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Without phase changes, $Q=mc\Delta T$. The product is $0.5\cdot4000\cdot3=6000\,\mathrm{J}$. The value of $c$ refers to one kilogram and one kelvin: both mass and temperature increase determine the required energy.
