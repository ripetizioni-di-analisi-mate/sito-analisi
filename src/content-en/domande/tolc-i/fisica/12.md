A $200\,\mathrm{W}$ appliance runs for half an hour at constant power. How much energy does it consume in kilowatt-hours?

- $100\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

Power in kilowatts is $0.2\,\mathrm{kW}$ and time is $0.5\,\mathrm{h}$. Energy is $E=P\Delta t=0.2\cdot0.5=0.10\,\mathrm{kWh}$. The kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy: the hour factor multiplies power, so it does not mean power delivered in an hour.
