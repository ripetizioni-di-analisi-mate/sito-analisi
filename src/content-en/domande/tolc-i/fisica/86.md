A load weighing $100\,\mathrm{N}$ is held in equilibrium by two symmetrical ideal strings, each at $60^\circ$ to the vertical. What is the tension in each string? Use $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac12$.

- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal tension components cancel by symmetry. The vertical components support the weight: $2T\cos60^\circ=100\,\mathrm{N}$. Since $2\cos60^\circ=1$, $T=100\,\mathrm{N}$. Simply dividing weight by two would ignore the strings’ inclination.
