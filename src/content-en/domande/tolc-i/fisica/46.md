An ideal spring with constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the elastic-force magnitude?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $203\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Hooke’s law gives magnitude $F=k|x|$. Extension in meters is $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$, so $F=200\cdot0.03=6\,\mathrm{N}$. The spring force points toward the unstretched configuration and therefore opposes the extension.
