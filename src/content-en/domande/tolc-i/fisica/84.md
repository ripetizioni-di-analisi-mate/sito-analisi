A real object is placed between a converging lens and its focal point. What image is obtained in the thin-lens model?

- Real, inverted and reduced
- Real, upright and enlarged
- Virtual, inverted and reduced
- Virtual, upright and enlarged
- No image in every case

## Explanation

With $0<p<f$, the equation $\dfrac1q=\dfrac1f-\dfrac1p$ gives $q<0$. The image is therefore virtual on the object’s side. Magnification $M=-\dfrac qp$ is positive and greater than one, so the image is upright and enlarged, as in a magnifying glass.
