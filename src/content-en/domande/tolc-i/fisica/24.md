A length measurement is $(50.0\pm1.0)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the percentage relative uncertainty?

- $1\%$
- $50\%$
- $0.02\%$
- $2\%$
- $5\%$

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of absolute uncertainty to the measured value, $\dfrac{1.0}{50.0}=0.02$. Multiplying by one hundred gives $2\%$. Units cancel because numerator and denominator are both lengths expressed in centimeters.
