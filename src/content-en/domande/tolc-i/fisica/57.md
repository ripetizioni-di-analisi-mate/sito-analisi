Two equal positive charges are fixed at points $A$ and $B$. What is the electric field at the segment’s midpoint?

- It points from A to B
- It is zero
- It points from B to A
- It is perpendicular to AB
- Its magnitude is twice either contribution

## Explanation

The midpoint is equally distant from the two charges, so their fields have equal magnitude. Each field points away from its charge; at the midpoint the directions are opposite. Their vector sum is therefore zero, even though each contribution is nonzero.
