A cyclic engine absorbs $500\,\mathrm{J}$ and releases $350\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle. What is its efficiency?

- $70\%$
- $43\%$
- $150\%$
- $85\%$
- $30\%$

## Explanation

Over a cycle, internal energy returns to its initial value, so net work is $500-350=150\,\mathrm{J}$. Efficiency compares this work with absorbed heat, $\eta=\dfrac{150}{500}=0.30=30\%$. The ratio $\dfrac{350}{500}$ instead describes the fraction released.
