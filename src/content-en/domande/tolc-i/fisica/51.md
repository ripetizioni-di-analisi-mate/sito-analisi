A gas has temperature $27\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is its absolute temperature rounded to the nearest kelvin?

- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $27\,\mathrm{K}$
- $246\,\mathrm{K}$
- $273\,\mathrm{K}$
- $310\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The conversion is $T=t_C+273.15$. This gives $27+273.15=300.15\,\mathrm{K}$, which rounds to $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Gas laws use this absolute temperature; the Celsius value cannot replace it in a ratio of temperatures.
