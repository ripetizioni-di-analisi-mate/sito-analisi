A body floats in water with $60\%$ of its volume immersed. What is the ratio of its average density to water density?

- $1.67$
- $0.40$
- $0.60$
- $1$
- $0.06$

## Explanation

In equilibrium, weight equals buoyancy, $\rho_cVg=\rho_fV_{\mathrm{imm}}g$. Canceling $V$ and $g$ gives $\dfrac{\rho_c}{\rho_f}=\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{imm}}}{V}=0.60$. The exposed fraction, $0.40$, describes a different part of the volume and does not enter buoyancy.
