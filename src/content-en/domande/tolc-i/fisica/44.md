A charged particle moves parallel to a nonzero uniform magnetic field. What is its magnetic force?

- The force has magnitude $\lvert q\rvert vB$ and is parallel to velocity
- The force has magnitude $\lvert q\rvert vB$ and is perpendicular to velocity
- The force opposes velocity and slows the particle
- It is zero
- The force has magnitude $2\lvert q\rvert vB$

## Explanation

Magnetic-force magnitude is $|q|vB\sin\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between velocity and field. For parallel or antiparallel vectors, sine is zero. The particle therefore experiences no magnetic force, even though it is charged and moving.
