What shape do magnetic-field lines have near a very long straight current-carrying wire?

- Straight lines parallel to the wire
- Radial lines pointing away from the wire
- Parabolas in the wire’s plane
- Circles around the wire in perpendicular planes
- Segments ending at the wire

## Explanation

The wire’s cylindrical symmetry produces a field tangent to circles centered on the wire. Their planes are perpendicular to the current. Direction follows the right-hand rule: thumb along conventional current and curled fingers along the field.
