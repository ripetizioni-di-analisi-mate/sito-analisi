A fully immersed body displaces $0.004\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water. What is the buoyant force if $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Buoyancy equals the weight of displaced fluid. Displaced water mass is $\rho V=1000\cdot0.004=4\,\mathrm{kg}$; its weight is $4\cdot10=40\,\mathrm{N}$. The body’s mass is not needed to calculate buoyancy when immersed volume is already known.
