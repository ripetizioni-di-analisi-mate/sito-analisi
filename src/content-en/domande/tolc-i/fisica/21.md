Currents of $5\,\mathrm{A}$ and $2\,\mathrm{A}$ enter a junction. A current of $3\,\mathrm{A}$ leaves through one branch. What current leaves through the only other branch in steady state?

- $4\,\mathrm{A}$
- $10\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $7\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

In steady state, charge does not accumulate at the junction, so incoming currents sum to outgoing currents. Thus $5+2=3+I$, giving $I=4\,\mathrm{A}$. The positive result agrees with the assumed outward direction of the unknown branch.
