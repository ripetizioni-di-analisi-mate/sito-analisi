A horizontal force increases linearly from zero to $8\,\mathrm{N}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What impulse does it deliver during this interval?

- $12\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N\,s}$

## Explanation

Impulse is the area under the force-time graph. Linear variation forms a triangle with base $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and height $8\,\mathrm{N}$, so $J=\dfrac12\cdot3\cdot8=12\,\mathrm{N\,s}$. Multiplying maximum force by total time would incorrectly assume constant force.
