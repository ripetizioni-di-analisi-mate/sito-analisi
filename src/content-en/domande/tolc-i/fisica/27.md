A constant $10\,\mathrm{N}$ force makes $60^\circ$ with a displacement of $4\,\mathrm{m}$. What work does it do? Use $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac12$.

- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Only the force component parallel to displacement contributes to work. It is $F\cos60^\circ=5\,\mathrm{N}$, so $W=5\cdot4=20\,\mathrm{J}$. The angle is acute and work is positive; the perpendicular component transfers no energy through this displacement.
