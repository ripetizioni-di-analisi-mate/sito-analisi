How is an ideal voltmeter connected to measure the potential difference across a resistor?

- In series, with zero resistance
- In parallel, with infinite resistance
- In parallel, with zero resistance
- In series, with infinite resistance
- Only to the source’s positive terminal

## Explanation

The voltmeter must compare the potentials at both ends, so it connects to the same nodes as the resistor. Ideally infinite resistance makes instrument current zero and leaves the circuit unchanged. An ideal ammeter instead connects in series and has zero resistance.
