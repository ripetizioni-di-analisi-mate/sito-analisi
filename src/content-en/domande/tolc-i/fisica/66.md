A swing receives small periodic pushes. Why can its amplitude grow strongly when the push period is close to its natural period?

- Resonance favors energy transfer
- Mass decreases with every push
- Gravity becomes zero
- The natural period becomes zero
- The rope always loses tension

## Explanation

Near the natural frequency, a periodic driving force can supply energy in step with the oscillation. Resonance occurs, increasing amplitude until dissipation and other effects limit growth. The phenomenon concerns push frequency and does not require a change in mass.
