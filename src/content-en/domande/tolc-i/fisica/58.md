A body starts from rest and descends through a vertical drop of $5\,\mathrm{m}$ without friction. What speed does it reach with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The loss of potential energy becomes kinetic energy, $mgh=\dfrac12mv^2$. Mass cancels and $v=\sqrt{2gh}=\sqrt{100}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The vertical drop matters: guide length or shape does not change the result when friction is absent.
