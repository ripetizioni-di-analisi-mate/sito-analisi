A wave passes between two stationary media with an unchanged source. Its speed is lower in the second medium. Which pair describes frequency and wavelength?

- Lower frequency and same wavelength
- Both greater
- Both unchanged
- Same frequency and shorter wavelength
- Higher frequency and shorter wavelength

## Explanation

The source sets the temporal rhythm, so frequency remains unchanged at the boundary. Since $\lambda=\dfrac vf$, lower speed at constant frequency requires a shorter wavelength. Crest spacing changes while the number of oscillations per second stays the same.
