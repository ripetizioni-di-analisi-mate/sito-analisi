Which function on $\mathbb R$ has a graph symmetric about the vertical axis?

- $f(x)=x^3-2x$
- $f(x)=x^2+x$
- $f(x)=(x-1)^2$
- $f(x)=x^4-2x^2$
- $f(x)=x^3+1$

## Explanation

The required symmetry is equivalent to $f(-x)=f(x)$ for every real number. Even powers satisfy $(-x)^4=x^4$ and $(-x)^2=x^2$, so $(-x)^4-2(-x)^2=x^4-2x^2$. After expansion, the other expressions contain a nonzero odd term and do not satisfy this identity.
