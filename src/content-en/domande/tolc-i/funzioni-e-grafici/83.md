What is the complete solution set of $\sqrt{x+1}\le2$?

- $(-\infty,3]$
- $[0,3]$
- $[-1,3]$
- $[-1,4]$
- $[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x+1\ge0$, so $x\ge-1$. Both sides of the inequality are nonnegative, so squaring preserves the comparison and gives $x+1\le4$, namely $x\le3$. Intersecting the conditions gives $[-1,3]$, with both endpoints allowed.
