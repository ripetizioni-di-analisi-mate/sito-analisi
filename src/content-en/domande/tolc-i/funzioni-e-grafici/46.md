The function $f$ is strictly decreasing on $\mathbb R$ and $f(1)=8$. Setting $g(x)=-f(x)$, which relation is guaranteed?

- $g(3)>-8$
- $g(3)<-8$
- $g(3)=-8$
- $g(3)>8$
- $g(1)=8$

## Explanation

Since $3>1$ and $f$ is strictly decreasing, $f(3)<f(1)=8$. Multiplying by $-1$ reverses the direction, giving $-f(3)>-8$, namely $g(3)>-8$. The monotonicity information alone does not determine an exact value of $g(3)$.
