Service A costs $4+n$ euros for $n$ uses; service B costs $1+2n$ euros. What is the smallest integer number of uses for which A is strictly cheaper?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $5$
- $1$

## Explanation

Compare costs using $4+n<1+2n$. Subtracting $n+1$ gives $3<n$. Since $n$ counts uses, it is an integer, and the smallest allowed value is $4$. With three uses both costs are $7$ euros, so there is not yet a strict saving.
