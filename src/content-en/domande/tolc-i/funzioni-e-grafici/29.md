What is $\log_2 3\cdot\log_3 2$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $6$
- $1$
- $\log_6 5$

## Explanation

The change-of-base formula gives $\log_2 3=\dfrac{\ln3}{\ln2}$ and $\log_3 2=\dfrac{\ln2}{\ln3}$. Multiplication cancels both numerator factors with denominator factors, leaving $1$. The logarithms of $2$ and $3$ are nonzero, so these cancellations are valid.
