The function $f$ is continuous and strictly increasing on the interval $[1,5]$, with $f(1)=-2$ and $f(5)=3$. How many solutions does the equation $f(x)=0$ have in the interval $(1,5)$?

- Exactly one
- None
- Exactly two
- At least two
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

The graph of a function that is continuous on an interval has no breaks, so $f$ takes every value between $f(1)=-2$ and $f(5)=3$. Since $0$ lies between $-2$ and $3$, there is at least one point of $(1,5)$ where $f$ equals $0$. A strictly increasing function takes different values at different points, so $f$ cannot equal $0$ at two distinct points. Consequently the equation $f(x)=0$ has **exactly one** solution in $(1,5)$.
