A continuous, strictly increasing function on $[1,5]$ has endpoint values $-2$ and $3$. How many distinct solutions of $f(x)=0$ are guaranteed in $(1,5)$?

- Exactly one
- None
- Exactly two
- At least two
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

Continuity ensures that every intermediate value, including zero, occurs. Strict increase prevents two inputs from producing the same value.
