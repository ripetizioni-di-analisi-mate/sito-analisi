What is the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{x-2}}{x-5}$?

- $[2,5)\cup(5,+\infty)$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,5)$
- $[5,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The square root requires $x-2\ge0$, so $x\ge2$. The denominator instead requires $x-5\ne0$, so $x\ne5$. Both conditions must hold. We include $2$, where the square root is zero, and exclude $5$, where division is undefined. The domain is therefore $[2,5)\cup(5,+\infty)$.
