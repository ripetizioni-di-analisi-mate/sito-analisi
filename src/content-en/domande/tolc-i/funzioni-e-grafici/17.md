What is the set of zeros of $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-4x}{x-4}$?

- $\{0,4\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{4\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{4\}$

## Explanation

The denominator excludes $x=4$. For other inputs, factor the numerator as $x(x-4)$ and simplify to obtain $f(x)=x$. The only allowed input producing zero is therefore $0$. The value $4$ also makes the numerator zero, but it is outside the domain and cannot be a function zero.
