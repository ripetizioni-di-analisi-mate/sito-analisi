What is the complete solution set of $|x-2|=x-2$?

- $(-\infty,2]$
- $\{2\}$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-2,2)$

## Explanation

A number equals its absolute value exactly when it is nonnegative. Applying this property to $u=x-2$ gives $x-2\ge0$, namely $x\ge2$. For $x<2$, the absolute value would be $2-x$, opposite to and therefore different from $x-2$.
