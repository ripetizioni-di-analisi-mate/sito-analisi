What is $10^{\log_{10}7}$?

- $7$
- $70$
- $\log_{10}70$
- $10$
- $\dfrac7{10}$

## Explanation

By definition, $\log_{10}7$ is the exponent that must be applied to base $10$ to obtain $7$. Raising $10$ to that exponent therefore returns $7$. The identity $a^{\log_a b}=b$ requires $a>0$, $a\ne1$ and $b>0$, all of which hold here.
