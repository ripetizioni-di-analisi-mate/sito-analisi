A tariff is $C(n)=5$ for $0\le n\le3$ and $C(n)=5+2(n-3)$ for $n>3$. How much extra does going from $3$ to $5$ uses cost?

- $9$
- $10$
- $2$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

For three uses, the first piece applies and the cost is $C(3)=5$. For five uses, use the second piece: $C(5)=5+2(5-3)=9$. The requested increase is $C(5)-C(3)=9-5=4$. The value $9$ is the final total cost, distinct from the requested increase.
