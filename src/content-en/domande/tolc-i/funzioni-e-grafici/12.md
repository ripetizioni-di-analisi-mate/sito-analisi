The domain of $f$ is $[-1,4]$. What is the domain of $g(x)=f(x+2)$?

- $[1,6]$
- $[-3,2]$
- $[-1,4]$
- $[-2,8]$
- $(-3,2)$

## Explanation

The input entering $f$ is $x+2$, so it must belong to $[-1,4]$. Impose $-1\le x+2\le4$ and subtract $2$ throughout, obtaining $-3\le x\le2$. The endpoints remain included because the original domain included its endpoints.
