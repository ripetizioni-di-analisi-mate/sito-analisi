If $f(u)=\dfrac1u$ and $g(x)=x^2-16$, what is the domain of $f\circ g$?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{16\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-4,4\}$
- $(-4,4)$
- $[0,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The inner function $g$ allows every real number. Its output must nevertheless be nonzero because it becomes the denominator in $f$. Thus $x^2-16\ne0$, namely $x\ne-4$ and $x\ne4$. The excluded inputs are the preimages of zero under $g$.
