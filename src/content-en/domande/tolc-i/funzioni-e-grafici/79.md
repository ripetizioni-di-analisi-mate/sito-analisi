What is the inverse of $f(x)=\dfrac{x+2}{3}$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac3{x+2}$
- $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-2}{3}$
- $f^{-1}(x)=3x+2$
- $f^{-1}(x)=3x-2$
- $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac3x-2$

## Explanation

From $y=\dfrac{x+2}{3}$, first obtain $3y=x+2$ and then $x=3y-2$. The inverse therefore assigns input $3y-2$ to each value $y$. Renaming the variable gives $f^{-1}(x)=3x-2$. The reciprocal of the original formula does not perform this operation.
