Which relation fails to define a unique real function $y$ of $x$ on its full set of horizontal coordinates?

- $y=x^2$
- $y=|x|$
- $x=y^2$
- $y=\sqrt x$
- $y=\dfrac1x$

## Explanation

In the relation $x=y^2$, the horizontal coordinate $x=4$ corresponds to two vertical coordinates, $y=2$ and $y=-2$. A single input thus has two images, contradicting the definition of a function. The other relations assign exactly one output to every input in their natural domains.
