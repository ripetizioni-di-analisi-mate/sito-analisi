What is the complete solution set of $\log_5(2x+3)<\log_5(7-x)$?

- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac43\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac43,7\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac32,7\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac32,\dfrac43\right)$
- $\left[-\dfrac32,\dfrac43\right]$

## Explanation

Positivity of both arguments requires $x>-\dfrac32$ and $x<7$. Since base $5$ is greater than one, comparing the logarithms is equivalent to $2x+3<7-x$, namely $x<\dfrac43$. Intersecting with the domain gives $-\dfrac32<x<\dfrac43$, with both endpoints excluded.
