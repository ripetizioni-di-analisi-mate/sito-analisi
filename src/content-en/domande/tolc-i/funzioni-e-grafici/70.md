For which values of $k$ does the line $y=k$ meet $y=(x+2)^2-1$ at two distinct points? Give the complete set.

::grafico{f="(x+2)^2-1" xmin=-6 xmax=2 ymin=-2 ymax=6 punti="-2,-1"}

- $k\ge-1$
- $k<-1$
- $k>2$
- $k=-1$
- $k>-1$

## Explanation

The common horizontal coordinates satisfy $(x+2)^2=k+1$. There are two distinct roots when the right side is strictly positive, namely $k>-1$. For $k=-1$, the roots coincide at the vertex, while for $k<-1$ there are no real roots. A subset such as $k>2$ does not include all required values.
