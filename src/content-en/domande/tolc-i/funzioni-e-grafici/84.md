Which identity is valid for every $x\ne0$?

- $\ln(x^2)=2\ln x$
- $\ln(x^2)=(\ln|x|)^2$
- $\ln(x^2)=\ln|x|+2$
- $\ln(x^2)=2\ln|x|$
- $\ln(x^2)=\ln(2|x|)$

## Explanation

For $x\ne0$, the number $|x|$ is positive and satisfies $x^2=|x|^2$. The power property can therefore be applied to $|x|$, giving $\ln(x^2)=\ln(|x|^2)=2\ln|x|$. The formula with $\ln x$ excludes negative values and is not valid on the entire requested domain.
