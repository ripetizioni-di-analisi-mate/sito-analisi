What is $\log_4 8$?

- $2$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$
- $3$

## Explanation

We seek $c$ such that $4^c=8$. Writing both bases as powers of $2$ gives $2^{2c}=2^3$. Equality of exponents gives $2c=3$ and $c=\dfrac32$. The value lies between $1$ and $2$, as expected from $4<8<16$.
