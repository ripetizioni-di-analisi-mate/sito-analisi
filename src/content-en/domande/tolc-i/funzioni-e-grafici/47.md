A device compares two positive values by storing only the integer part of their base-$2$ logarithms. For which pair would the comparison show a tie even though the values differ?

- $7,\ 8$
- $9,\ 15$
- $15,\ 16$
- $3,\ 5$
- $4,\ 9$

## Explanation

Every number from $2^3=8$ inclusive to $2^4=16$ exclusive has a logarithm with integer part $3$. The other pairs span a power of two, with the power itself at the start of the new band.
