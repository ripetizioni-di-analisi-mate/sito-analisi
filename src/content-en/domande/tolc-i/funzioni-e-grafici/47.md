What is $\log_4 8$?

- $2$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$
- $3$

## Explanation

The number $\log_4 8$ is the exponent $c$ such that $4^c=8$. Writing $4$ and $8$ as powers of $2$, the equation becomes $2^{2c}=2^3$. Two powers of $2$ are equal only if they have the same exponent, so $2c=3$ and $\log_4 8=\dfrac32$. The value lies between $1$ and $2$, as it must since $4<8<16$. The value $\dfrac23$ comes from swapping base and argument, because $\log_8 4=\dfrac23$.
