It is known that $f(4)=-2$. Which point must belong to the graph of $g(x)=f(x+3)-1$?

- $(7,-3)$
- $(4,-3)$
- $(1,-1)$
- $(7,-1)$
- $(1,-3)$

## Explanation

To use the known value $f(4)$, the inner argument must be $4$, so $x+3=4$ and $x=1$. The new vertical coordinate is $f(4)-1=-2-1=-3$. The corresponding point is $(1,-3)$. The horizontal translation is determined from the inner argument, not from its sign in isolation.
