The function is $f(x)=x+1$ for $x<2$ and $f(x)=k$ for $x\ge2$. Which value of $k$ makes the graph continuous at the join?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $3$
- $-1$

## Explanation

On the left-hand piece, as $x$ approaches $2$, the value $x+1$ approaches $3$. On the right-hand piece and at $2$, the function equals $k$. Joining the pieces without a jump therefore requires $k=3$. The value depends on the ordinates of both pieces at the join.
