A mass follows exponential decay and halves every $3$ hours and initially equals $160\,\mathrm g$. Which formula describes the mass after $t$ hours?

- $M(t)=160\left(\dfrac12\right)^{3t}$
- $M(t)=160-\dfrac t3$
- $M(t)=160\left(\dfrac12\right)^{\frac t3}$
- $M(t)=160\cdot2^{\frac t3}$
- $M(t)=\dfrac{160}{3t}$

## Explanation

The number of halving intervals elapsed in $t$ hours is $\dfrac t3$. Each interval multiplies the mass by $\dfrac12$, so the formula is $160\left(\dfrac12\right)^{\frac t3}$. At $t=0$ it returns $160$, and at $t=3$ it returns $80$. These checks verify the initial value and halving time.
