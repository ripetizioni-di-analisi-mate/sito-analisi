Let $a=\log_2 3$. Which expression is equal to $\log_2 18$?

- $2a$
- $1+a^2$
- $1+2a$
- $2+2a$
- $3a$

## Explanation

Since $18=2\cdot3^2$ and the logarithm of a product is the sum of the logarithms of the factors, we have $\log_2 18=\log_2 2+\log_2 3^2$. The first term equals $1$, because $2^1=2$. The logarithm of a power is the exponent times the logarithm of the base, so $\log_2 3^2=2\log_2 3=2a$. Therefore $\log_2 18=1+2a$. The expression $1+a^2$ comes from writing $\log_2 3^2$ as $\left(\log_2 3\right)^2$, while $2a$ is obtained by ignoring the factor $2$ in $18=2\cdot9$.
