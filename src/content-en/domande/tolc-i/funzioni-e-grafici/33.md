A scale uses the coordinate $s=\log_{1/2}x$. Points A and B have coordinates $1$ and $3$ respectively. How are their original values $x_A$ and $x_B$ related?

- $x_B=4x_A$
- $x_B=x_A/2$
- $x_B=x_A/4$
- $x_B=x_A+2$
- $x_B=x_A-2$

## Explanation

The inverse scale is $x=(1/2)^s$. Moving two units up the scale multiplies the original value by $(1/2)^2=1/4$.
