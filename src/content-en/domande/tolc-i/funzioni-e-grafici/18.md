In the graph of $f(x)=2^x-3$, which description holds on $[-2,2]$?

::grafico{f="2^x-3" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-4 ymax=6 punti="-2,-2.75;0,-2;2,1"}

- It is strictly increasing and always positive.
- It is strictly decreasing and always negative.
- It is strictly increasing and takes both negative and positive values.
- It is constant up to zero and then increasing.
- It is always negative.

## Explanation

The base-$2$ exponential is strictly increasing, and subtracting $3$ does not change monotonicity. At the endpoints, $f(-2)=\dfrac14-3=-\dfrac{11}{4}<0$ and $f(2)=4-3=1>0$. Thus the function takes values of both signs. Increasing behavior and positivity describe different properties.
