Which value solves $4^x+2^x-6=0$?

- $1$
- $-3$
- $2$
- $-1$
- $\log_2 3$

## Explanation

Set $u=2^x$, so $u>0$ and $4^x=u^2$. The equation becomes $u^2+u-6=(u+3)(u-2)=0$. The root $u=-3$ is incompatible with a positive power. Hence $u=2$, so $2^x=2$ and $x=1$. The substitution requires preserving the constraint on $u$.
