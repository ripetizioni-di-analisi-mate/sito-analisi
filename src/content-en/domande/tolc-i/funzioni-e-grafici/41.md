The graph of $f(x)=a^x$, with $a>0$, passes through $(2,25)$. What is $a$?

- $5$
- $-5$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}$
- $25$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

Belonging to the graph means that the coordinates satisfy the formula. Thus $a^2=25$, which algebraically allows $a=5$ and $a=-5$. The assumption $a>0$ excludes the second possibility. The real exponential therefore has base $5$, also consistent with $f(0)=1$.
