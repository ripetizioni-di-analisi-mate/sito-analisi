A real function has domain exactly $[1,5]$. Which quantity is certainly defined?

- $f(0)$
- $f(6)-f(2)$
- $f(-1)$
- $f(f(1))$
- $f(1)+f(5)$

## Explanation

The inputs $1$ and $5$ both belong to the domain, so $f(1)$ and $f(5)$ exist and can be added. The inputs $0$, $6$ and $-1$ are excluded. The composition $f(f(1))$ is not guaranteed either: the value $f(1)$ might lie outside $[1,5]$.
