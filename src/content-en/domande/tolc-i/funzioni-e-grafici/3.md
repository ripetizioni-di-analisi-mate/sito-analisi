What is $\log_2 32$?

- $4$
- $16$
- $5$
- $2$
- $\dfrac15$

## Explanation

The logarithm asks for the exponent applied to base $2$ to obtain $32$. The successive powers are $2,4,8,16,32$, so $2^5=32$. Hence $\log_2 32=5$. The division $32\div2$ calculates a quotient rather than the exponent required by the definition.
