On $[-1,1]$, a function equals $x^2$ for $x<0$ and $x+1$ for $x\ge0$. Which statement is correct?

- It is injective, because the formulas differ.
- It is even on its whole domain.
- It is not a function, because it has two formulas.
- It is not injective, because $f(-1)=f(0)=1$.
- Its range is $[-1,2]$.

## Explanation

The definition assigns exactly one formula to each input, so it describes a function. However, $-1$ uses the first piece and gives $1$, while $0$ uses the second and also gives $1$. Distinct inputs share an image, so injectivity fails. Having two formulas does not prevent a relation from being a function.
