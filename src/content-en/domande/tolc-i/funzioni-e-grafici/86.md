Which value solves $\log_3(x+1)-\log_3(x-1)=1$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The domain requires $x>1$. The logarithm difference becomes $\log_3\dfrac{x+1}{x-1}=1$, hence $\dfrac{x+1}{x-1}=3$. Multiplying by the nonzero denominator gives $x+1=3x-3$, so $x=2$. This value satisfies the domain, and the ratio equals $3$.
