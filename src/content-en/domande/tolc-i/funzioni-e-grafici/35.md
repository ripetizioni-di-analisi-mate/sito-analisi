A function graph is the piecewise-linear curve joining, in order, $(-3,5)$, $(-1,3)$, $(0,4)$, $(2,1)$ and $(4,2)$. Which point in the interior of the domain is a relative but not an absolute maximum?

::grafico{linee="-3,5 -1,3 0,4 2,1 4,2" punti="-3,5;-1,3;0,4;2,1;4,2" xmin=-4 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=6}

- $(-1,3)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(-3,5)$
- $(4,2)$
- $(0,4)$

## Explanation

The graph rises from $(-1,3)$ to $(0,4)$ and immediately afterward falls toward $(2,1)$. The value $4$ is therefore greater than the values at nearby inputs on both sides: there is a relative maximum at $x=0$. It is not absolute because $f(-3)=5>4$. The points with horizontal coordinates $-1$ and $2$ are relative minima; the domain endpoints are excluded by the requirement of an interior point.
