For $f(u)=\sqrt u$ and $g(x)=x+7$, which complete description of $f\circ g$ is correct?

- $\sqrt{x+7}$, $x\ge-7$
- $\sqrt x+7$, $x\ge0$
- $\sqrt{x+7}$, $x\ge0$
- $\sqrt{x+7}$, $x>-7$
- $x+\sqrt7$, $x\in\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The inner function produces $g(x)=x+7$, and the outer function takes the square root of that result. The formula is therefore $\sqrt{x+7}$. Its domain requires $x+7\ge0$, giving $x\ge-7$. The endpoint is allowed because a square root may have a zero radicand.
