An affine function satisfies $f(1)=4$ and $f(4)=10$. What is $f(6)$?

- $14$
- $12$
- $16$
- $20$
- $24$

## Explanation

For an affine function, the ratio of the change in output to the change in input is constant. Here $m=\dfrac{10-4}{4-1}=2$. From $x=4$ to $x=6$, the input increases by $2$, so the output increases by $2\cdot2=4$. Hence $f(6)=10+4=14$.
