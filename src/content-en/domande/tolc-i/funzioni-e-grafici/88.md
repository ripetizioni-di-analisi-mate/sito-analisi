Which equality holds for every $x>0$?

- $\ln\sqrt x=\sqrt{\ln x}$
- $\ln\sqrt x=2\ln x$
- $\ln\sqrt x=\dfrac12\ln x$
- $\ln\sqrt x=\ln\dfrac x2$
- $\ln\sqrt x=\ln x-1$

## Explanation

For $x>0$, write $\sqrt x=x^{\frac12}$. The logarithm power rule brings the exponent in front, so $\ln(x^{\frac12})=\dfrac12\ln x$. Positivity of $x$ makes both sides defined. Taking a square root of the logarithm’s value would be a different operation.
