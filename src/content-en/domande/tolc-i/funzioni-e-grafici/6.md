What is the vertex of the parabola $y=(x-3)^2+2$?

- $(3,2)$
- $(-3,2)$
- $(3,-2)$
- $(-3,-2)$
- $(0,11)$

## Explanation

The square is nonnegative and vanishes when $x-3=0$, namely at $x=3$. At that point the function takes its minimum value $2$. The vertex is therefore $(3,2)$. The point $(0,11)$ lies on the parabola but is the vertical intercept rather than the vertex.
