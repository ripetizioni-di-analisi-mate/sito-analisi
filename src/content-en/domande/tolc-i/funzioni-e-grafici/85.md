The point $(9,3)$ lies on $y=\sqrt x$. Which point corresponds to it under the transformation $y=\sqrt{2x}$?

- $\left(9,\dfrac32\right)$
- $(18,3)$
- $(9,6)$
- $\left(3,\dfrac92\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac92,3\right)$

## Explanation

To preserve the same square-root value, its argument must remain $9$. The new formula therefore requires $2x=9$, namely $x=\dfrac92$. The vertical coordinate stays $3$. Thus $f(2x)$ compresses horizontal coordinates by a factor of $2$, without dividing vertical coordinates.
