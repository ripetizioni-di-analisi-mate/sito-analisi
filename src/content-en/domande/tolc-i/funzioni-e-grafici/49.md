Which value solves $\log_2x+\log_2(x-2)=3$?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $8$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The conditions $x>0$ and $x-2>0$ reduce to $x>2$. Combining the logarithms gives $\log_2[x(x-2)]=3$, hence $x^2-2x=8$. Factoring as $(x-4)(x+2)=0$ yields $4$ and $-2$. Only $4$ satisfies the original domain, so it is the sole solution.
