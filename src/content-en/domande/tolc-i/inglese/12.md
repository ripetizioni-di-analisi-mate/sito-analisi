Complete: “The exam starts ___ nine o'clock ___ Monday ___ June.”

- on / at / in
- at / on / in
- in / on / at
- at / in / on
- in / at / on

## Explanation

An exact time takes *at*, a day of the week takes *on*, and a month takes *in*. This gives *at nine o'clock on Monday in June*. The three positions do not describe the same kind of time: using one preposition throughout would lose the distinction.
