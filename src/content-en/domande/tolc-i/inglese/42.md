Complete: “The instructions are clear. Please read them ___ before starting.”

- careful
- carefully
- care
- carefulness
- more careful

## Explanation

The gap describes how to read, so it requires the adverb *carefully*. *Careful* and *more careful* are adjective forms; *care* and *carefulness* are nouns in the proposed uses. The sentence is not describing the noun *instructions*, already characterized by *clear*.
