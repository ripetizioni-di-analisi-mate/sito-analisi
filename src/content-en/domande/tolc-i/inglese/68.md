Transform the teacher's request to us, “Please wait here”: “The teacher asked us ___ here.”

- wait
- waiting
- to wait
- waited
- that wait

## Explanation

This request is reported using *ask + person + to + verb*. *Asked us* therefore requires *to wait*. The addressee remains *us*; the infinitive expresses the requested action without turning it into a claim that the waiting has already occurred.
