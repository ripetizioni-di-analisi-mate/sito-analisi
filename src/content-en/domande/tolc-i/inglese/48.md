Complete: “When I was a child, I ___ live near the station, but now I live in the countryside.”

- am used to
- use
- used to
- have used
- using to

## Explanation

*Used to + base verb* describes a past situation that has ended, confirmed by the contrast with the current home. *Am used to* means being accustomed and would require a noun or *living*. It is not the construction for describing the former place of residence.
