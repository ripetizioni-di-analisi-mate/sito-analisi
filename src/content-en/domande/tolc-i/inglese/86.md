You want a passer-by to use your camera. Complete: “Could you ___ a photograph of us in front of the museum?”

- take
- keep
- return
- leave
- show

## Explanation

The collocation for creating a photo is *take a photograph*. The context asks the passer-by to use the camera, so it is not about showing, keeping or returning an existing photograph. The lexical combination specifies the desired action.
