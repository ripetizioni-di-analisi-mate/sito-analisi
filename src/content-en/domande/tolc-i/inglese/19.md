Complete: “She waited for ___ hour outside ___ university.”

- a / an
- an / an
- a / a
- an / a
- many / a

## Explanation

*Hour* begins with a vowel sound because its *h* is silent, so it requires *an*. *University* begins with the consonant sound /j/, so it requires *a*. The choice depends on pronunciation, not the first written letter.
