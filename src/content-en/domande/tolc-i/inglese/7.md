Complete: “He doesn't ___ meat.”

- eats
- eat
- ate
- eating
- eaten

## Explanation

The auxiliary *doesn't* already carries third-person singular marking and negation. The main verb therefore remains *eat*, without *-s*. *Ate* is past, *eaten* is a participle and *eating* is an *-ing* form: none is required after *doesn't*.
