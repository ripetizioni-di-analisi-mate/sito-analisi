Read: “Leave the small bottles on the shelf. Take the larger ones downstairs.” Which noun does *ones* refer to?

- shelf
- stairs
- bottles
- labels
- rooms

## Explanation

*Ones* replaces the plural noun *bottles*, avoiding repetition. *Larger* selects the bigger bottles and distinguishes them from the small ones. It does not refer to singular *shelf* or to nouns such as *labels* and *rooms*, which the text never introduces.
