Complete: “We have very ___ information about the new course.”

- few
- a few
- little
- many
- several

## Explanation

*Information* is normally uncountable in English. *Very little information* expresses a very small amount. *Few*, *many* and *several* require a plural countable noun; adding *a* to *few* does not change this incompatibility with the given noun.
