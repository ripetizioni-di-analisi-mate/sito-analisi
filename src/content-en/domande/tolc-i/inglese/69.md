Complete with the expression meaning “on condition that”: “You can borrow the book ___ you return it by Friday.”

- even though
- because
- before
- provided that
- after

## Explanation

*Provided that* introduces the condition for permission: returning the book by Friday. *Because* would indicate a cause, *even though* a concession, and *before/after* a temporal order. Only the selected answer expresses the explicitly requested relationship.
