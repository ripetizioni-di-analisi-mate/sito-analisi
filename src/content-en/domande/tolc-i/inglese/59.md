Which sentence uses *must* to express a strong deduction rather than an obligation?

- You must submit the form by Friday; it is required.
- Visitors must wear a badge under the entry rules.
- We must pay the fee before enrolment; it is compulsory.
- His coat is here, so he must be nearby.
- Drivers must stop when this signal is red.

## Explanation

In the coat example, evidence leads to a conclusion about the person's location: *must be nearby*. The other sentences require actions through a rule, deadline or condition. The modal form is the same, but its function changes with context.
