Which expression is equivalent to *eventually* in “After several attempts, we eventually opened the door”?

- possibly
- by chance
- at the same time
- for a short time
- in the end

## Explanation

The attempts precede a result finally achieved. *Eventually* therefore means *in the end*. It does not express merely the possibility of opening, because the text states that the door was opened; nor does it attribute the result to chance.
