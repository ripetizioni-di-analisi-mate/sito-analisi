You want to receive the other person's ruler temporarily. Complete: “Could you ___ me your ruler for a moment?”

- borrow
- owe
- keep
- own
- lend

## Explanation

The subject *you* is the person providing the object, so the verb is *lend*. *Borrow* instead describes the recipient: one could say *Could I borrow your ruler?*. The choice depends on subject and recipient roles, not merely the general idea of a loan.
