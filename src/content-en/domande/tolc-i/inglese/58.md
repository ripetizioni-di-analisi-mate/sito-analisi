Complete: “Room A has twelve seats and Room B has twenty. Room A has ___ seats than Room B.”

- more
- fewest
- fewer
- most
- much

## Explanation

Twelve is less than twenty, so the comparison expresses a smaller number. *Seats* is plural countable and takes *fewer* in this construction. *Fewest* would be a superlative, while *more* would describe the opposite of the supplied data.
