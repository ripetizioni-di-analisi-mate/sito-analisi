Complete to express inability: “Without my glasses I am unable to see the small letters. I ___ read this notice.”

- can
- can't
- should
- must
- may

## Explanation

The first sentence establishes a lack of visual ability in the current situation. *Can't read* expresses this inability. *Can* would assert ability; *should* and *must* would suggest advice or obligation; *may* does not communicate the required inability.
