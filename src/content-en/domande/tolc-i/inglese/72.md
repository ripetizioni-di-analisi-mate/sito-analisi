Read: “Tomorrow's class is going ahead, but it will be online because the heating at the college is broken.” What changes compared with the on-site class?

- The date of the class.
- How students attend the class.
- The teacher's identity.
- The subject of the class.
- The class is cancelled entirely.

## Explanation

*Is going ahead* confirms that the class will take place. *Will be online* changes the way of attending, and the heating failure explains why. The message announces no new teacher, new topic or change of date.
