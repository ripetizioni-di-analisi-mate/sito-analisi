Complete to express purpose: “We left early ___ catch the last train.”

- so that
- because of
- instead of
- in order to
- despite

## Explanation

The purpose of leaving early is catching the train. *In order to* precedes base-form *catch*. *So that* would require a clause with subject and verb, while *because of*, *instead of* and *despite* do not take the isolated base verb here.
