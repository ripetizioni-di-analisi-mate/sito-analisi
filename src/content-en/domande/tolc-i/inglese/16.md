Complete the conditional prediction: “If it rains tomorrow, we ___ at home.”

- will stay
- would stayed
- staying
- have stay
- will stayed

## Explanation

The sentence presents a future possibility using present-tense *if*. The predicted consequence can be expressed by *will stay*: *will* requires the base form. The other options do not form a grammatical main clause with subject *we* in this context.
