Which sentence preserves the meaning of “You cannot enter unless you have a valid pass”?

- If you have a valid pass, you must enter.
- A valid pass is never needed for entry.
- Everyone without a pass may enter.
- If you do not have a valid pass, you cannot enter.
- Having a pass guarantees entry regardless of all other rules.

## Explanation

*Unless* is equivalent here to *if not*: entry is excluded without a valid pass. The sentence therefore presents the pass as necessary, without requiring holders to enter or guaranteeing entry regardless of any other rules.
