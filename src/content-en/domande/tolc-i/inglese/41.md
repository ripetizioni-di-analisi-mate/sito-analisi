Choose the standard position of the frequency adverb in the sentence with *be*.

- Mia is usually on time.
- Mia is on usually time.
- Mia usually on time is.
- Mia on is usually time.
- Mia is on time usually always.

## Explanation

With *be*, a frequency adverb normally follows the verb and precedes the complement: *is usually on time*. The phrase *on time* is not split by the adverb. The other proposed sequences disrupt the structure or accumulate incompatible adverbs in this context.
