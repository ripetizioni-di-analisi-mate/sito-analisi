Complete: “Although the task was difficult, Mia ___ to finish it before lunch.”

- enjoyed
- avoided
- suggested
- prevented
- managed

## Explanation

*Manage to do something* means succeed in doing it, here despite difficulty. *Managed* takes the infinitive *to finish*. *Enjoy*, *avoid* and *suggest* require other patterns; *prevent* does not combine this way with an infinitive or with the intended meaning of success.
