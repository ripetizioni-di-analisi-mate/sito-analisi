Complete: “Look at the desk: there ___ two books on it.”

- is
- was
- has
- are
- be

## Explanation

The sentence presents what is on the desk now. *Two books* is plural, so the existential construction requires *there are*. *Is* is singular, *was* is past singular, and *has* does not express existence in this structure.
