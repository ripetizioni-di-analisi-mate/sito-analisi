Complete with the article identifying the previously mentioned book: “I bought a book yesterday. ___ book is on my desk.”

- An
- A
- Some
- Many
- The

## Explanation

The second sentence refers back to the same book, now identifiable to the reader. The definite article *the* expresses that reference. *A* would introduce an unidentified book, while *an* does not match the initial sound of *book*; the other options are not the required article.
