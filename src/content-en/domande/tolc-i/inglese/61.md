Complete: “We moved to this town two months ___.”

- ago
- for
- during
- yet
- already

## Explanation

*Ago* follows the duration and locates the event that far in the past relative to the present. *For* would precede a duration rather than follow it; *during* would need a following complement. Neither *yet* nor *already* completes the time expression *two months ___*. Past simple *moved* matches the completed event.
