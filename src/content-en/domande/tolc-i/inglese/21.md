Complete: “Can you help ___ with this problem?”

- me
- I
- my
- mine
- myself's

## Explanation

The pronoun identifies the person receiving help and is the object of *help*. It must therefore be *me*. *I* is a subject, *my* requires a noun, and *mine* expresses possession; *myself's* is not a personal-pronoun form.
