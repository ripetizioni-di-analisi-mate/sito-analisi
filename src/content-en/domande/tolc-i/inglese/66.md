Read: “Your first appointment was moved from Tuesday to Wednesday. The follow-up is still on Friday.” Which appointment changed day?

- The first appointment.
- The Friday follow-up.
- Both appointments.
- Neither appointment.
- An appointment originally on Thursday.

## Explanation

*Was moved from Tuesday to Wednesday* describes the first appointment's change. *Still on Friday* confirms that the follow-up remains unchanged. The move therefore cannot be extended to both visits, nor can a Thursday appointment be introduced.
