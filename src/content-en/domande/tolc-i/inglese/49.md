Read: “At weekends this bus stops at the museum on request. Tell the driver when you get on.” What must a passenger do to get off at the museum on Saturday?

- Wait for an automatic museum stop.
- Show a museum ticket after getting off.
- Ask another passenger to stop the bus.
- Ask the driver when boarding.
- Board the bus only at the museum.

## Explanation

Saturday falls within *at weekends*. The stop is *on request*, and the following instruction specifies whom to ask and when: the driver, when boarding. Waiting for an automatic stop would ignore the condition stated in the message.
