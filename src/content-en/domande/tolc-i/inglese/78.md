Complete in the present perfect continuous: “I started waiting an hour ago and I am still here. I ___ for an hour.”

- has been waiting
- have being waiting
- have been waiting
- am been waiting
- have been waited

## Explanation

The requested form is *have/has + been + -ing verb*. With *I*, use *have been waiting*, consistent with an activity begun an hour earlier and still continuing. *Has* does not agree; *being* does not replace *been*; *waited* is not the progressive form.
