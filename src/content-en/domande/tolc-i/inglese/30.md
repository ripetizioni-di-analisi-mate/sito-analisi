Complete: “We stayed quiet ___ the lecture and took notes ___ the teacher was speaking.”

- while / during
- during / during
- while / while
- since / for
- during / while

## Explanation

*The lecture* is a noun phrase, introduced by *during*. *The teacher was speaking* is a complete clause, introduced by *while*. The two words can connect similar times, but require different grammatical structures after them.
