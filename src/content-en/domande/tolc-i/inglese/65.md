Complete: “We have ___ time, but the room is not warm ___.”

- enough / very
- much / too
- too / enough
- enough of / enough
- enough / enough

## Explanation

*Enough* precedes noun *time* and follows adjective *warm*. The two positions depend on the category of the modified word. *Enough of* would require a defined phrase such as *the time available*; *very* cannot occupy the proposed final position.
