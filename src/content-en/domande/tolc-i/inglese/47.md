Complete the offer using *tea* as an uncountable noun: “Would you like ___ tea before you leave?”

- many
- some
- a few
- each
- an

## Explanation

An offer naturally uses *some* even though it is phrased as a question. *Tea* here denotes an uncountable amount of the drink. *Many* and *a few* would require plural units such as *cups*, while *each* and *an* do not complete this use of the noun.
