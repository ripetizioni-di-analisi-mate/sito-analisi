Complete: “Both bags weigh five kilograms. The blue bag is as heavy ___ the black one.”

- than
- like
- to
- that
- as

## Explanation

The weights are equal, and the structure is *as + adjective + as*. The second *as* introduces the comparison target. *Than* follows a comparative such as *heavier*, not the equality construction already begun with *as heavy*.
