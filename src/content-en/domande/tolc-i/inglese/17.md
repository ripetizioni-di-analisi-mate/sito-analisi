Complete the hypothetical statement: “I do not have enough free time. If I had more time, I ___ more books.”

- am read
- would read
- will reading
- have readed
- reads

## Explanation

The first sentence establishes that available time is insufficient. *If I had* therefore presents an imagined situation, with the consequence *would read*. *Would* takes the base form; *read* has the same spelling as the participle here but a different function.
