You want to know who called Nora yesterday. Which question asks about the subject of the action?

- Who did Nora call yesterday?
- Who did called Nora yesterday?
- Who Nora called yesterday?
- Who called Nora yesterday?
- Who does Nora called yesterday?

## Explanation

In the intended answer, the unknown person makes the call. *Who* therefore replaces the subject and the verb remains *called*, without *did*. *Who did Nora call?* is grammatical but asks whom Nora called, reversing the roles.
