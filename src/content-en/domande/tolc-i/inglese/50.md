Read: “The rear entrance is for staff only. Visitors should use the main entrance.” Which statement matches the notice?

- Visitors must use the rear entrance.
- Both entrances are closed to staff.
- The rear entrance is closed to everyone.
- Visitors may use either entrance without restriction.
- Visitors should enter through the main entrance.

## Explanation

*Staff only* reserves the rear entrance for employees. The second sentence explicitly directs visitors to the main entrance. The text does not close the rear entrance to everyone or authorize visitors to choose freely between the two entrances.
