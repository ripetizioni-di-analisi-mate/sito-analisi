Complete the offer made at that moment: “This box is heavy.” “I ___ you carry it.”

- helped
- will help
- was helped
- have help
- am help

## Explanation

The second speaker offers help as the problem arises. *Will help* can express this immediate willingness. Past forms would describe another time reference; *have help* and *am help* do not construct the required verb.
