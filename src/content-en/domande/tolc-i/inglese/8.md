Complete: “There isn't ___ milk in the fridge.”

- many
- a few
- any
- an
- every

## Explanation

*Milk* is uncountable here and the sentence is negative: *any milk* indicates the absence of the quantity sought. *Many* and *a few* require plural countable nouns; *an* and *every* require singular countable nouns. The noun type excludes these distractors.
