What does “The meeting has been put off until Friday” mean?

- The meeting has been cancelled permanently.
- The meeting has been postponed to Friday.
- The meeting now takes place every Friday.
- The meeting has been moved to an earlier day.
- The meeting has already finished.

## Explanation

The phrasal verb *put off* means postpone. *Until Friday* specifies the intended new date without making the meeting recurring. A postponement is not permanent cancellation and does not state that the meeting has already ended.
