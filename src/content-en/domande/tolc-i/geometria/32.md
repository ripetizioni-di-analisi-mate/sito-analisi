Among all rectangles with perimeter $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the greatest area?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

If one side is $x$, the other is $10-x$ because their sum is half the perimeter. The area is $x(10-x)=25-(x-5)^2$. The square is nonnegative, so the area is at most $25$, attained when $x=5$. Thus the maximum-area rectangle is a square with side $5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
