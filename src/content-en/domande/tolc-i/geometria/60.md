On the surface of a cube with edge $2\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the shortest path between two opposite space vertices?

- $2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\sqrt2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\sqrt5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Unfolding two adjacent faces gives a rectangle with sides $4$ and $2$, whose opposite corners correspond to the given vertices. The shortest planar segment measures $\sqrt{4^2+2^2}=2\sqrt5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The three symmetric unfolding choices give the same value. The diagonal $2\sqrt3$ would pass through the interior rather than stay on the surface.
