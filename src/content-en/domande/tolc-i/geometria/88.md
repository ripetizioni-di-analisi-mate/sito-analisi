Which curve is the locus of points equidistant from $F=(0,1)$ and the line $y=-1$?

- $y=x^2$
- $x^2+y^2=1$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{4}$
- $y=\dfrac{x^2}{2}$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

The squared distance from $F$ is $x^2+(y-1)^2$, while that from the line is $(y+1)^2$. Equating them gives $x^2+y^2-2y+1=y^2+2y+1$, hence $x^2=4y$. The locus is the parabola $y=\dfrac{x^2}{4}$, with focus $F$ and the given directrix.
