Two triangle sides are $4$ and $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. If the third side has integer length in centimeters, what is the greatest possible perimeter?

- $26\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $22\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The third side $c$ must satisfy $|9-4|<c<9+4$, or $5<c<13$. The largest allowed integer is $12$, so the maximum perimeter is $4+9+12=25\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $c=13$ is excluded because the segments would be collinear and would not form a nondegenerate triangle.
