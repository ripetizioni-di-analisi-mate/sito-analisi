A chord of a circle with radius $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center. What is the chord length?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $26\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\sqrt{194}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord. The triangle formed by the center, chord midpoint, and one endpoint is right-angled, with hypotenuse $13$ and leg $5$. Half the chord is $\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=12\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the full chord is $2\cdot12=24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
