What is the shortest distance from $P=(5,3)$ to a point of the segment with endpoints $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(2,0)$?

- $3$
- $\sqrt{34}$
- $\sqrt{10}$
- $6$
- $3\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The perpendicular projection of $P$ onto the $x$-axis is $(5,0)$, outside the segment. For a point $(x,0)$ with $0\le x\le2$, the squared distance is $(5-x)^2+9$, minimized when $x$ is as close to $5$ as possible, namely $x=2$. The required distance is therefore $PB=\sqrt{3^2+3^2}=3\sqrt2$.
