What is the radius of the circle $x^2+y^2-4x+6y-12=0$?

- $25$
- $\sqrt{12}$
- $\sqrt{13}$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $x^2-4x=(x-2)^2-4$ and $y^2+6y=(y+3)^2-9$. The equation becomes $(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2=12+4+9=25$. The right side is $r^2$, so the positive radius is $r=5$. Terms introduced by completing the square must also be accounted for in the equation.
