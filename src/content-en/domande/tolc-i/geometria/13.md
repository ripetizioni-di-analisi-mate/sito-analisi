An exterior angle of a triangle measures $128^\circ$. One nonadjacent interior angle measures $53^\circ$. What is the other?

- $52^\circ$
- $105^\circ$
- $75^\circ$
- $127^\circ$
- $181^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angle adjacent to the exterior one is $180^\circ-128^\circ=52^\circ$. The three interior angles sum to $180^\circ$, so the third is $180^\circ-52^\circ-53^\circ=75^\circ$. Equivalently, an exterior angle equals the sum of the two nonadjacent interior angles: $128^\circ-53^\circ=75^\circ$.
