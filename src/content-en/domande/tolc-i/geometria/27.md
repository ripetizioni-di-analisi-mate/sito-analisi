A convex polygon has interior angle sum $1260^\circ$. How many vertices does it have?

- $7$
- $9$
- $8$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

Drawing diagonals from one vertex divides an $n$-sided polygon into $n-2$ triangles. Thus $(n-2)180^\circ=1260^\circ$. Dividing by $180^\circ$ gives $n-2=7$, hence $n=9$. The value $7$ counts the subdivision’s triangles, rather than the polygon’s vertices.
