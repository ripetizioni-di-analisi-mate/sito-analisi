What are the asymptotes of the hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{9}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}=1$?

- $y=\pm\dfrac32x$
- $y=\pm2x$
- $y=\pm3x$
- $y=\pm\dfrac23x$
- $x=\pm3$

## Explanation

For a hyperbola in the form $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$, the asymptotes are $y=\pm\dfrac ba x$. Here $a=3$ and $b=2$, giving slopes $\pm\dfrac23$. The ratio uses semiaxes, rather than their squares. The vertical lines $x=\pm3$ pass through the vertices but are not asymptotes.
