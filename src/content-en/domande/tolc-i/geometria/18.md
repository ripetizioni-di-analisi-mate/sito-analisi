In triangle $ABC$, $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of $AB$ and $AC$. If $BC=14\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is $MN$?

- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $28\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac72\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The ratios $AM:AB$ and $AN:AC$ are both $1:2$, and the angle at $A$ is shared. Triangles $AMN$ and $ABC$ are therefore similar with scale factor $\dfrac12$. The side corresponding to $BC$ is $MN$, so $MN=\dfrac12\cdot14=7\,\mathrm{cm}$; also, $MN$ is parallel to $BC$.
