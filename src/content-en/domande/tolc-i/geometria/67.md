Which line is tangent to the circle $x^2+y^2=25$ at $(3,4)$?

- $4x-3y=0$
- $3x+4y=25$
- $3x+4y=5$
- $4x+3y=25$
- $y=4$

## Explanation

The radius from $(0,0)$ to $(3,4)$ has direction $(3,4)$. The tangent is perpendicular to this radius, so its equation is $3(x-3)+4(y-4)=0$. Expanding gives $3x+4y=25$. It passes through the given point and is $\dfrac{25}{5}=5$ from the origin, equal to the radius.
