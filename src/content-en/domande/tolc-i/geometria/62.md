What is the area of the triangle bounded by the coordinate axes and the line $2x+y=8$?

- $32$
- $16$
- $8$
- $12$
- $64$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis, $y=0$, so the intercept is $(4,0)$. On the $y$-axis, $x=0$, so the other intercept is $(0,8)$. The triangle with the origin is right-angled with legs $4$ and $8$, hence its area is $\dfrac{4\cdot8}{2}=16$.
