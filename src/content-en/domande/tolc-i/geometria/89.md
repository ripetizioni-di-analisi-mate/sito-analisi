Point $P$ lies on the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$. If its distance to one focus is $3$, how far is it from the other focus?

- $2$
- $5$
- $8$
- $7$
- $10$

## Explanation

For every point on an ellipse, the sum of the distances to the foci equals the full major axis, $2a$. Here $a=5$, so the sum is $10$. If one distance is $3$, the other is $10-3=7$. The semiaxis $5$ alone is not the sum of the two distances.
