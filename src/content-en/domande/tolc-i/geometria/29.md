A rhombus has diagonals of $10$ and $24\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its perimeter?

- $68\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $26\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $52\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular and bisect one another. One side is therefore the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $5$ and $12$, so it measures $\sqrt{25+144}=13\,\mathrm{cm}$. All four sides are equal, and the perimeter is $4\cdot13=52\,\mathrm{cm}$. The product of the half-diagonals instead belongs to an area calculation.
