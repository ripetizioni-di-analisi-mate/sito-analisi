In a regular polygon, each adjacent exterior angle is $24^\circ$. How many sides does it have?

- $15$
- $12$
- $24$
- $8$
- $30$

## Explanation

Following the boundary of a convex polygon, the exterior turns sum to a full turn, or $360^\circ$. Since the polygon is regular, every turn is $24^\circ$. The number of sides therefore satisfies $24n=360$, giving $n=15$. The corresponding interior angle would be $156^\circ$.
