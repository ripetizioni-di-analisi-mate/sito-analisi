How many points do the parabola $y=x^2$ and line $y=2x-1$ have in common?

- $0$
- $2$
- $3$
- Infinitely many
- $1$

## Explanation

A common point satisfies $x^2=2x-1$, or $x^2-2x+1=(x-1)^2=0$. The only horizontal coordinate is $x=1$, giving $y=1$. Thus there is one contact point. The double root indicates tangency but represents a single point in the plane, not two distinct intersections.
