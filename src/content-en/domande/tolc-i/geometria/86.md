Point $(3,2)$ lies on the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{18}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$, with $b>0$. What is $b$?

- $2\sqrt2$
- $8$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

Substitution gives $\dfrac9{18}+\dfrac4{b^2}=1$. The first term is $\dfrac12$, so $\dfrac4{b^2}=\dfrac12$ and $b^2=8$. Since $b>0$, it follows that $b=\sqrt8=2\sqrt2$. The value $8$ is the denominator $b^2$, not the semiaxis.
