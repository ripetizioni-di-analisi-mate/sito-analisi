An inscribed angle subtends the same arc as a central angle of $114^\circ$. What is its measure?

- $114^\circ$
- $228^\circ$
- $66^\circ$
- $57^\circ$
- $33^\circ$

## Explanation

For the same arc, the inscribed angle is half the central angle. Hence the required measure is $\dfrac{114^\circ}{2}=57^\circ$. Specifying the same arc determines which pair of angles to compare. Subtracting from $180^\circ$ would give a supplementary angle, which is not the relationship in this problem.
