For which value of $k$ are the points $(0,1)$, $(2,5)$, and $(3,k)$ collinear?

- $6$
- $8$
- $5$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

The line determined by the first two points has slope $\dfrac{5-1}{2-0}=2$ and intercept $1$, so its equation is $y=2x+1$. The third point is collinear if it lies on the same line. Substituting its horizontal coordinate $3$ gives $k=2\cdot3+1=7$.
