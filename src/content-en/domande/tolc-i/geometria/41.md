From an exterior point $P$, a tangent is drawn to a circle centered at $O$, touching at $T$. If $OP=10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the radius is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is $PT$?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{136}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Radius $OT$ is perpendicular to the tangent at the contact point. In right triangle $OTP$, the hypotenuse is therefore $OP=10$, while $OT=6$. Thus $PT=\sqrt{10^2-6^2}=\sqrt{64}=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The difference $10-6$ would describe the shortest distance from $P$ to the circle, not the tangent segment.
