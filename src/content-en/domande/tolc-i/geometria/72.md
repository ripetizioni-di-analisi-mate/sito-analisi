What is the reflection of point $(-2,5)$ in the line $y=x$?

- $(2,5)$
- $(5,-2)$
- $(-2,-5)$
- $(-5,2)$
- $(2,-5)$

## Explanation

Reflection in $y=x$ exchanges the coordinates. Thus $(-2,5)$ becomes $(5,-2)$. Their midpoint is $\left(\dfrac32,\dfrac32\right)$, on the reflection line, and the segment joining them has slope $-1$, perpendicular to the line with slope $1$.
