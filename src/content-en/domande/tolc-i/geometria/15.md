A triangle has side lengths $4,5,7$. How is it classified by its angles?

- Right-angled
- Acute
- Equilateral
- Degenerate
- Obtuse

## Explanation

The sides form a triangle because $4+5>7$. Compare the square of the longest side with the sum of the other two squares: $7^2=49>4^2+5^2=41$. The angle opposite side $7$ is therefore greater than $90^\circ$. The triangle is obtuse; equality between the squared quantities would indicate a right triangle.
