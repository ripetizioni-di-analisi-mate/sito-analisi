Two sides of a triangle measure $4$ and $7$. Which interval contains exactly all possible lengths of the third side $c$?

- $3<c<11$
- $0<c<11$
- $3\le c\le11$
- $4<c<7$
- $c>3$

## Explanation

The triangle inequality requires both $c<4+7=11$ and $c>|7-4|=3$. Both endpoints are excluded: with $c=3$ or $c=11$, the three segments would be collinear. The correct interval therefore contains all and only the lengths strictly between $3$ and $11$.
