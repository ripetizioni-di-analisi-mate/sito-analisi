Two disks of radii $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ are externally tangent. What is the area of their union?

- $13\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $25\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $5\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $6\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $10\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

External tangency means the disks share only one point, so they have no overlapping region of positive area. The union area is the sum $\pi\cdot2^2+\pi\cdot3^2=13\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Adding the radii before squaring would produce the area of a different disk with radius $5$.
