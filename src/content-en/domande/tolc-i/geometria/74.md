A triangle has vertices $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$ and $C=(1,1)$. What are the coordinates of its orthocenter, the intersection of the altitude lines?

- $(1,1)$
- $(2,0)$
- $\left(\dfrac53,\dfrac13\right)$
- $(1,3)$
- $(2,-1)$

## Explanation

Side $AB$ is horizontal, so the altitude line from $C$ is $x=1$. Side $AC$ has slope $1$, so the altitude from $B$ has slope $-1$ and equation $y=-x+4$. The two lines intersect at $(1,3)$. The orthocenter lies outside the triangle, consistent with the obtuse angle at $C$; it must not be confused with the centroid $\left(\dfrac53,\dfrac13\right)$ or the circumcenter $(2,-1)$.
