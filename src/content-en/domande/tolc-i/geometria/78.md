What is the area of a regular hexagon with side $4\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $12\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $48\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $24\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $16\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Joining the center to the vertices gives six equilateral triangles of side $4$. Each has altitude $2\sqrt3$ and area $\dfrac{4\cdot2\sqrt3}{2}=4\sqrt3$. Adding the six areas gives $24\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. This decomposition applies because all central angles are $60^\circ$.
