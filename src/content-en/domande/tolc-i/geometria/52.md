A pyramid of volume $120\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is cut by a plane parallel to its base halfway up its height. What is the volume of the frustum containing the original base?

- $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $105\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $90\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The small pyramid near the apex is similar to the full pyramid and has half its height, so every length has ratio $\dfrac12$. Its volume is $120\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=15\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The frustum is the remainder, $120-15=105\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Cutting halfway up does not halve a pyramid’s volume.
