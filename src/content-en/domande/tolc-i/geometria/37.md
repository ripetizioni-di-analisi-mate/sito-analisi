A circular sector has radius $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and central angle $90^\circ$. What is its complete perimeter?

- $2\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(8+2\pi)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(4+2\pi)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(8+4\pi)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The angle is a quarter of a full turn, so the arc length is $\dfrac14\cdot2\pi\cdot4=2\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$. The sector boundary also includes two radii with combined length $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its perimeter is therefore $(8+2\pi)\,\mathrm{cm}$. Counting only the arc would omit the two straight boundary segments.
