What is the center of symmetry of the hyperbola $\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{9}-\dfrac{(y+1)^2}{4}=1$?

- $(-2,1)$
- $(2,-1)$
- $(3,2)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(0,0)$

## Explanation

The new coordinates $X=x-2$ and $Y=y+1$ turn the equation into $\dfrac{X^2}{9}-\dfrac{Y^2}{4}=1$, symmetric about the new origin. This origin corresponds to $x=2$, $y=-1$. The denominators determine the curve’s dimensions, while the coordinate shifts locate its center.
