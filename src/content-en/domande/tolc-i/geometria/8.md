What is the vertex of the parabola $y=x^2-4x+3$?

- $(-2,-1)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(2,-1)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(1,0)$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $y=(x-2)^2-1$. The squared term is nonnegative and vanishes at $x=2$, so the minimum value is $-1$ and the vertex is $(2,-1)$. The point $(1,0)$ lies on the parabola, but it is a root, rather than the point where the curve reaches its minimum.
