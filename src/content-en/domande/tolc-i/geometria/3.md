A right cone has base radius $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its volume?

- $36\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $6\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $12\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $18\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The base is a disk of area $A_b=\pi\cdot2^2=4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. A cone has one third of the product of base area and height, so $V=\dfrac{4\pi\cdot9}{3}=12\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Omitting the divisor $3$ would give the volume of a cylinder with the same base and height.
