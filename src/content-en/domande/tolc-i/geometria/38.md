A wheel of diameter $70\,\mathrm{cm}$ makes ten revolutions without slipping. How many meters does it travel?

- $14\pi\,\mathrm{m}$
- $70\pi\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\pi\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac7{10}\pi\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac72\pi\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

One revolution without slipping covers one circumference, namely $\pi d=70\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$. Ten revolutions cover $700\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$. Dividing by $100$ to convert to meters gives $7\pi\,\mathrm{m}$. The given $70$ is already the diameter and must not be doubled.
