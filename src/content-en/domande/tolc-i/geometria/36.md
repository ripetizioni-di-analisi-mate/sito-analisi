An annulus has outer radius $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ and inner radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area?

- $24\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $12\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $74\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $49\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The annulus is the larger disk with the smaller disk removed, so its area is $\pi\cdot7^2-\pi\cdot5^2=\pi(49-25)=24\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The difference of squares differs from the square of the difference: $(7-5)^2=4$ would describe a disk of radius $2$, not the given region.
