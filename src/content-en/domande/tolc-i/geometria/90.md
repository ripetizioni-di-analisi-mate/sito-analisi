Which parabola has roots $-2$ and $4$ and passes through $(0,-16)$?

- $y=(x+2)(x-4)$
- $y=2(x-2)(x+4)$
- $y=-2(x+2)(x-4)$
- $y=2(x+2)(x+4)$
- $y=2(x+2)(x-4)$

## Explanation

The roots impose the form $y=a(x+2)(x-4)$. Substituting $(0,-16)$ gives $-16=a\cdot2\cdot(-4)=-8a$, so $a=2$. The required parabola is $y=2(x+2)(x-4)$. The factors determine the roots, while the extra point fixes the opening scale and direction of concavity.
