A triangle has vertices $(0,0)$, $(6,0)$, and $(0,3)$. What are its centroid coordinates?

- $(3,\dfrac32)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(2,\dfrac32)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(1,2)$

## Explanation

The midpoint of the side joining $(6,0)$ and $(0,3)$ is $M=\left(3,\dfrac32\right)$. The centroid lies two thirds of the way along the median from the origin, so $G=\dfrac23M=(2,1)$. Equivalently, each centroid coordinate is the mean of the corresponding coordinates of the three vertices.
