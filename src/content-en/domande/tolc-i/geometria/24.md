What is the area of the triangle with vertices $A=(-2,1)$, $B=(4,1)$, and $C=(1,5)$?

- $24$
- $15$
- $6$
- $12$
- $18$

## Explanation

Base $AB$ is horizontal and has length $4-(-2)=6$. The altitude from $C$ is its distance from $y=1$, namely $5-1=4$. Hence $A=\dfrac{6\cdot4}{2}=12$. The horizontal coordinate of $C$ does not change the area as long as the base and the vertex’s height remain the same.
