In an equilateral triangle with side $8\,\mathrm{cm}$, how far is a vertex from the opposite side?

- $8\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The distance is the perpendicular altitude, which in an equilateral triangle passes through the midpoint of the opposite side. Half the base is $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the hypotenuse of the resulting right triangle is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Thus $h=\sqrt{8^2-4^2}=\sqrt{48}=4\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
