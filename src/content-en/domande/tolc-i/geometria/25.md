How many diagonals does a convex polygon with $9$ sides have?

- $54$
- $36$
- $18$
- $9$
- $27$

## Explanation

From each of the $9$ vertices, diagonals reach $9-3=6$ vertices: the vertex itself and its two neighbors are excluded. The product $9\cdot6=54$ counts every diagonal twice, once from each endpoint. Dividing by two therefore gives $27$ distinct diagonals.
