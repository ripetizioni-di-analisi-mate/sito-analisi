An isosceles triangle has equal sides of $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ and base $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area?

- $60\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $65\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The altitude to the base divides it into two equal $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ parts. In each right triangle, $h=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore $A=\dfrac{10\cdot12}{2}=60\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The sloping $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ side is not perpendicular to the base and cannot replace the altitude in the product.
