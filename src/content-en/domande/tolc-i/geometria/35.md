From a square sheet with side $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, four $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ squares are cut at the corners. Folding the flaps makes an open box. What is its internal volume?

- $128\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $144\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $72\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The flaps are $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ high. Two $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ segments are removed along each sheet side, so the box base is a square of side $10-2-2=6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its volume is $6\cdot6\cdot2=72\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The remaining sheet area is not directly the base area.
