What is the distance between $A=(1,2)$ and $B=(4,6)$ in the Cartesian plane?

- $7$
- $25$
- $\sqrt7$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The horizontal displacement is $4-1=3$ and the vertical displacement is $6-2=4$. These displacements are perpendicular, so $AB=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{25}=5$. The sum $3+4=7$ measures a broken path parallel to the axes; the required distance is the length of the direct segment between the points.
