Two similar solids have surface areas in the ratio $9:25$. What is their volume ratio in the same order?

- $9:25$
- $3:5$
- $27:125$
- $81:625$
- $81:125$

## Explanation

The surface area ratio is the square of the length ratio, so $k=\sqrt{\dfrac9{25}}=\dfrac35$. The volume ratio is the cube of $k$, namely $\left(\dfrac35\right)^3=\dfrac{27}{125}$. Thus convert surface areas to lengths before calculating volumes; cubing $\dfrac9{25}$ directly would give an incorrect factor.
