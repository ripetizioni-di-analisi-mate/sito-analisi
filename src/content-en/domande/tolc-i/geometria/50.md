A rectangular box has edge lengths $3,4,12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its space diagonal?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $19\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{153}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The diagonal of the base with sides $3$ and $4$ is $5$. This diagonal is perpendicular to the vertical edge of length $12$, so a second Pythagorean calculation gives $d=\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=13\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $5$ describes only one face and does not join opposite space vertices.
