A right trapezoid has bases of $8$ and $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its perimeter?

- $38\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $44\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The side perpendicular to the bases is the height and measures $8$. The sloping side is the hypotenuse of a triangle with legs $14-8=6$ and $8$, so it is $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$. Adding all four sides gives $8+14+8+10=40\,\mathrm{cm}$. The base difference is the horizontal projection of the sloping side.
