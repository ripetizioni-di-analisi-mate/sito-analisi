A sphere of radius $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ is inscribed in a cube. What volume of the cube lies outside the sphere?

- $(27-36\pi)\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $(216-108\pi)\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $(216-18\pi)\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $(216-36\pi)\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $180\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The sphere touches the cube’s six faces, so the cube edge equals the diameter, $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The cube volume is $6^3=216$, while the sphere volume is $\dfrac43\pi\cdot3^3=36\pi$. The part outside the sphere but inside the cube therefore has volume $(216-36\pi)\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
