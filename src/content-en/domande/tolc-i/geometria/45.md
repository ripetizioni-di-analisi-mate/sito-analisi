Two circles have radii $2$ and $4$, and their centers are $6$ apart. What is their relative position?

- Intersecting at two points
- Internally tangent
- Concentric
- Exterior with no common points
- Externally tangent

## Explanation

The center distance equals the sum of the radii, $6=2+4$. The two disks therefore touch at one point along the line joining the centers, and the circles are externally tangent. Internal tangency would instead require a center distance equal to the radius difference, namely $2$.
