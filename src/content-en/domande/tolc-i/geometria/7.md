What are the center and radius of the circle $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=9$?

- Center $(-2,1)$, radius $3$
- Center $(2,-1)$, radius $3$
- Center $(2,1)$, radius $9$
- Center $(2,-1)$, radius $9$
- Center $(-2,-1)$, radius $3$

## Explanation

Compare the equation with $(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$. The term $x-2$ gives $a=2$, while $y+1=y-(-1)$ gives $b=-1$. Also $r^2=9$, and because the radius is positive, $r=3$. The right side represents the square of the radius, rather than the radius itself.
