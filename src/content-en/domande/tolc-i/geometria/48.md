A right cone has radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its lateral surface area?

- $60\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $25\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $65\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $90\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $130\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The cone’s lateral area is $\pi rs$, where $s$ is its slant height. In an axial cross section, radius and height are perpendicular legs, so $s=\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=13\,\mathrm{cm}$. Thus $S_l=\pi\cdot5\cdot13=65\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Using the vertical height $12$ instead of the slant height would underestimate the area.
