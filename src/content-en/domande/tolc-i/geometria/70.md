A quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle. One interior angle measures $112^\circ$. What is the opposite angle?

- $112^\circ$
- $56^\circ$
- $248^\circ$
- $78^\circ$
- $68^\circ$

## Explanation

The opposite angles subtend arcs that together make the complete circle. Each inscribed angle is half the central angle over its arc, so the opposite angles sum to $\dfrac{360^\circ}{2}=180^\circ$. The required angle is therefore $180^\circ-112^\circ=68^\circ$.
