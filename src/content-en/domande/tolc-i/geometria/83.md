What is the distance between the foci of the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$?

- $3$
- $9$
- $6$
- $10$
- $2\sqrt{41}$

## Explanation

The major semiaxis is $a=5$ and the minor one is $b=4$. Each focus is $c=\sqrt{a^2-b^2}=\sqrt{25-16}=3$ from the center. The foci lie symmetrically on opposite sides of the center, so their separation is $2c=6$. The value $3$ is only the half-separation.
