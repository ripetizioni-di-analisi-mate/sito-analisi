Which line passes through $(0,2)$ and $(2,6)$?

- $y=3x+2$
- $y=2x-2$
- $y=x+2$
- $y=4x+2$
- $y=2x+2$

## Explanation

The first point gives the intercept $q=2$. The slope comes from the coordinate changes, $m=\dfrac{6-2}{2-0}=2$. Hence the equation is $y=2x+2$. Substituting $x=2$ gives $y=6$, so the second point also satisfies the equation; passing through the first point alone would not be sufficient.
