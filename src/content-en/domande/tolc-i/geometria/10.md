What is the major semiaxis length of the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$?

- $25$
- $10$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The denominators are the squares of the semiaxes. On the $x$-axis, setting $y=0$ gives $x^2=25$, so the endpoints have coordinates $\pm5$. On the $y$-axis, the endpoints have coordinates $\pm3$. The major semiaxis is therefore $5$; the full major axis joining its two endpoints would measure $10$.
