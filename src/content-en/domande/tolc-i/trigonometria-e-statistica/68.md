Which identity holds for every real $x$?

- $\cos(\pi-x)=\cos x$
- $\cos(\pi-x)=\sin x$
- $\cos(\pi-x)=-\cos x$
- $\cos(\pi-x)=-\sin x$
- $\cos(\pi-x)=1-\cos x$

## Explanation

The unit-circle points corresponding to $x$ and $\pi-x$ are reflections across the vertical axis. Their horizontal coordinates are opposite and their vertical coordinates equal. Since cosine is the horizontal coordinate, $\cos(\pi-x)=-\cos x$.
