One group contains five observations all equal to $2$, and another contains five all equal to $8$. Each group has zero variance. What is the descriptive variance of the ten combined observations?

- $0$
- $3$
- $9$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

The combined mean is $\dfrac{5\cdot2+5\cdot8}{10}=5$. Every observation lies at distance $3$ from the new mean, so the deviations are all $-3$ or $3$ and their squares all equal $9$. The variance of the ten observations is the mean of these squares, so it equals $9$. The value $0$ is obtained as the mean of the two within-group variances, which do not account for the distance between the group means $2$ and $8$.
