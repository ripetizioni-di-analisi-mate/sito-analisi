A file contains three measurements with mean $5$ and population variance zero. A fourth value is added by mistake and the new mean becomes $6$. What is the new variance using divisor $4$?

- $4$
- $1$
- $3$
- $9$
- $12$

## Explanation

Zero variance and mean $5$ require three values equal to $5$. The new total is $24$, so the added value is $9$. The deviations from $6$ are $-1,-1,-1,3$ and their mean square is $12/4=3$.
