One group contains five observations all equal to $2$, and another contains five all equal to $8$. Each group has zero variance. What is the descriptive variance of the ten combined observations?

- $0$
- $3$
- $9$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

The combined mean is $\dfrac{5\cdot2+5\cdot8}{10}=5$. Deviations from the new mean are all $-3$ or $3$, so every squared deviation is $9$ and their mean is $9$. Zero within-group variances do not remove the spread caused by different group means.
