What is the solution set of $\tan x=1$ in $[0,2\pi)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac\pi4,\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac\pi4,\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac\pi4\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac\pi2,\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{0,\pi\right\}$

## Explanation

Tangent equals $1$ at $\dfrac\pi4$ and has period $\pi$. Thus its solutions are $x=\dfrac\pi4+k\pi$. Only $k=0$ and $k=1$ lie in the specified interval, giving $\dfrac\pi4$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$. Points where cosine is zero are outside the domain.
