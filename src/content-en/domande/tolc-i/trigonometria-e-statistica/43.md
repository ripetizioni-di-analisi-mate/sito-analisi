It is known only that four observations belong to $[0,10)$ and another six to $[10,20)$. Which quantity can be determined exactly from this information?

- The mean of the ten observations
- The median of the ten observations
- The number of observations below 10
- The variance of the ten observations
- The minimum of the ten observations

## Explanation

Exactly the four observations in the first class are below $10$, because its right endpoint is excluded. This count is therefore exactly four. The positions of the observations within each class are unknown: they can change without changing the two frequencies. Consequently, the mean, median, variance, and minimum are not determined exactly. Using class midpoints would produce an estimate of the mean, not its certain value.
