A menu consists of one of $3$ starters, followed by exactly one dish chosen from $4$ main courses or $2$ side dishes. How many menus are possible?

- $18$
- $24$
- $9$
- $14$
- $12$

## Explanation

After the starter, exactly one dish is chosen from two alternative categories, giving $4+2=6$ choices. All six are available for each of the three starters, so there are $3\cdot6=18$ menus. Multiplying $4$ by $2$ would instead require choosing both dishes.
