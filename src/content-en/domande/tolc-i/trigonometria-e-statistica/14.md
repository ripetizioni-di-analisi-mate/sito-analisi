The classes $[0,10)$, $[10,20)$, $[20,30)$ and $[30,40)$ have frequencies $3,8,10,4$. How many observations are strictly below $30$?

- $10$
- $25$
- $18$
- $21$
- $22$

## Explanation

The first three classes contain exactly the values below $30$. Their frequencies sum to $3+8+10=21$. The value $30$, if present, belongs to the fourth class and is excluded from the requested count. Individual values within the classes are not needed.
