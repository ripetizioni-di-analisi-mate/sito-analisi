How many four-symbol sequences, each symbol being T or C, contain exactly two T symbols?

- $6$
- $8$
- $4$
- $12$
- $16$

## Explanation

A sequence is determined by the two positions occupied by T; the other two must be C. Choose the positions in $\binom42=\dfrac{4\cdot3}{2}=6$ ways. The two T symbols are not distinguished, and no additional order is assigned to the selected pair of positions.
