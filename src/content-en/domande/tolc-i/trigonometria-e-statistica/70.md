Three nonoverlapping categories have absolute frequencies $8,12,n$ and cover all $32$ observations. What is the relative frequency of the third?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac58$
- $\dfrac3{20}$
- $12$
- $\dfrac38$

## Explanation

Absolute frequencies must sum to the total, so $n=32-8-12=12$. The third category’s relative frequency is $\dfrac{12}{32}=\dfrac38$. The value $12$ is the absolute count, whereas relative frequency is its share of the total.
