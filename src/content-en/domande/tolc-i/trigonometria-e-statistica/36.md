What is the range of the data $-4,-1,3,7,11$?

- $15$
- $7$
- $11$
- $4$
- $26$

## Explanation

The range is the maximum minus the minimum. Here the maximum is $11$ and the minimum is $-4$, so $11-(-4)=15$. The intermediate observations do not affect this measure. Subtracting the negative minimum increases the distance between the extremes.
