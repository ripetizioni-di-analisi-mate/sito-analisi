What is the solution set of $\sin(2x)=0$ in $[0,\pi)$?

- $\{0,\pi\}$
- $\left\{0,\dfrac\pi2,\pi\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac\pi4,\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\left\{0,\dfrac\pi2\right\}$

## Explanation

Sine is zero when its argument is a multiple of $\pi$, so $2x=k\pi$ and $x=\dfrac{k\pi}{2}$. The specified interval allows $k=0,1$. The value for $k=2$ is $\pi$, excluded by the open right endpoint.
