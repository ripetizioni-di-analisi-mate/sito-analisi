For three increasing values and a total of $10$ observations, which sequence can represent the cumulative frequencies?

- $5,4,10$
- $2,7,10$
- $2,7,9$
- $2,11,10$
- $-1,7,10$

## Explanation

Cumulative frequencies are nonnegative, nondecreasing and end at the total $10$. The sequence $2,7,10$ meets all conditions and corresponds to absolute frequencies $2,5,3$. The other sequences violate at least one condition: they decrease, exceed the total, start negative or fail to end at $10$.
