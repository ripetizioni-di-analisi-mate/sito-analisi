What is the fundamental period of $f(x)=\sin(4x)$?

- $2\pi$
- $4\pi$
- $\dfrac\pi4$
- $8\pi$
- $\dfrac\pi2$

## Explanation

Sine repeats its values when its argument increases by $2\pi$. Increasing the input by $T$ increases the argument $4x$ by $4T$. The smallest positive condition is $4T=2\pi$, giving $T=\dfrac\pi2$. The factor $4$ shortens the period without changing the amplitude.
