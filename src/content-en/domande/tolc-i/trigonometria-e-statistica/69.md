In the dataset $1,3,5,7,30$, the value $30$ is replaced by $50$. How do the mean and median change?

- Both increase by $4$
- The mean increases by $20$ and the median is unchanged
- The mean is unchanged and the median increases by $4$
- The mean increases by $4$ and the median is unchanged
- Both are unchanged

## Explanation

The sum increases by $50-30=20$, while there are still $5$ observations, so the mean increases by $\dfrac{20}{5}=4$. The first four sorted values keep their positions, and the third value is still $5$. The median is therefore unchanged.
