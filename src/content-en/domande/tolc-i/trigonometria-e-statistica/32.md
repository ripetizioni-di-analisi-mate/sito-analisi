In a pie chart representing $200$ responses, a sector measures $72^\circ$. How many responses does it represent?

- $72$
- $40$
- $36$
- $144$
- $20$

## Explanation

The sector occupies $\dfrac{72}{360}=\dfrac15$ of the circle. The same fraction of the total responses is $\dfrac15\cdot200=40$. The angle measures a share of a full turn; it is not directly the number of responses.
