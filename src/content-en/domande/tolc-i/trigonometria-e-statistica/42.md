Which expression equals $1-\cos^2x$ for every real $x$?

- $\sin x$
- $\sin^2x$
- $-\sin^2x$
- $(1-\cos x)^2$
- $\cos(2x)$

## Explanation

Subtracting $\cos^2x$ from the identity $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$ gives $1-\cos^2x=\sin^2x$. The square remains necessary: $\sin x$ can be negative, whereas the original expression is always nonnegative.
