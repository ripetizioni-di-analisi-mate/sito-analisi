Five distinct people stand in a row. Two specified people must be next to each other. How many rows are possible?

- $24$
- $60$
- $48$
- $120$
- $12$

## Explanation

Initially treat the two neighboring people as a single block. Together with the other three people, there are four objects to arrange in $4!=24$ ways. The two people within the block may swap, giving two internal orders. The total is $24\cdot2=48$.
