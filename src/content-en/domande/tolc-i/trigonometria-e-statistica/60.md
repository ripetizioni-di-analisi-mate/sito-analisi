$4$ is added to every observation in a numerical dataset. How do the mean and descriptive variance change?

- Both increase by $4$
- The mean is unchanged and the variance increases by $4$
- The mean increases by $4$ and the variance increases by $16$
- Both are multiplied by $4$
- The mean increases by $4$ and the variance is unchanged

## Explanation

The new sum increases by $4N$, so the mean increases by $4$. Every new deviation is $(x_i+4)-(\bar x+4)=x_i-\bar x$, identical to the original. Thus the squared deviations and their mean, the variance, also remain unchanged.
