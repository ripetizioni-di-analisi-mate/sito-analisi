A wheel’s position is represented by $(\cos x,\sin x)$. Two photographs show the same cosine and opposite, nonzero sines. Which transformation takes the first position to the second?

- Reflection in the vertical axis
- Reflection in the horizontal axis
- Rotation through a full turn
- Horizontal translation by one unit
- Projection onto the centre

## Explanation

Cosine is the horizontal coordinate and stays fixed. Sine is the vertical coordinate and changes sign, as in the reflection $(u,v)\mapsto(u,-v)$.
