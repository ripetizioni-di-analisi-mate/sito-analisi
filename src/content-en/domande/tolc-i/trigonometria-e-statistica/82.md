From two points aligned with a tower base, on the same side and level ground, the elevation angles are $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. The points are $20\,\mathrm m$ apart, and measurements start at ground level. How tall is the tower?

- $20\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$
- $10\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$
- $10\,\mathrm m$
- $20\,\mathrm m$
- $30\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Let $d$ be the distance from the nearer point to the base and $h$ the height. Then $h=d\sqrt3$ and $h=(d+20)\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$. Equating gives $3d=d+20$, so $d=10$ and $h=10\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$. The larger elevation angle belongs to the nearer point.
