Which option describes all and only the $x\in[0,2\pi)$ for which sine is strictly positive?

- $x\in[0,\pi]$
- $x\in(\pi,2\pi)$
- $x\in\left(0,\dfrac\pi2\right)$
- $x\in[0,2\pi)$
- $x\in(0,\pi)$

## Explanation

Sine is the vertical coordinate of the point on the unit circle. It is positive in the upper half-plane, corresponding to angles between $0$ and $\pi$. At both endpoints the height is zero, so both are excluded by the strict inequality. In the lower half-plane sine is negative.
