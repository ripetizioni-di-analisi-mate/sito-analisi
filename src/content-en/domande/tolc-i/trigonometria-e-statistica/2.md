How many radians correspond to $150^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac\pi6$
- $\dfrac{6\pi}{5}$

## Explanation

Since $180^\circ$ equals $\pi$ radians, multiply the degree measure by $\dfrac\pi{180}$. This gives $150\cdot\dfrac\pi{180}=\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$. The result is less than $\pi$, as expected for an angle smaller than a straight angle.
