How many solutions does $\sin x\cos x=0$ have in $[0,2\pi)$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

The product is zero when at least one factor is zero. Sine vanishes at $0$ and $\pi$, and cosine at $\dfrac\pi2$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. These four distinct values lie in the interval. The point $2\pi$ is excluded.
