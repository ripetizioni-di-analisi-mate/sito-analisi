How many subsets of $\{A,B,C,D\}$ contain A and do not contain D?

- $8$
- $4$
- $2$
- $6$
- $16$

## Explanation

Including A and excluding D are already fixed. For each of B and C, two independent choices remain: include or exclude. The total is $2^2=4$: the subsets are $\{A\}$, $\{A,B\}$, $\{A,C\}$ and $\{A,B,C\}$.
