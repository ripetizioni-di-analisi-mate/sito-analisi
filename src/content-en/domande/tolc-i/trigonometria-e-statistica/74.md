A circular sector has radius $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and central angle $\dfrac\pi3$ radians. What is its area?

- $12\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $2\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $36\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $6\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $18\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The angle occupies $\dfrac{\frac\pi3}{2\pi}=\dfrac16$ of a full turn. The sector occupies the same fraction of the circle’s area, which is $\pi\cdot6^2=36\pi$. Thus its area is $\dfrac16\cdot36\pi=6\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
