In a triangle, the side opposite $30^\circ$ is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. How long is the side opposite $45^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{9\sqrt2}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\sqrt2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The sine rule connects each side with its opposite angle: $\dfrac b{\sin45^\circ}=\dfrac9{\sin30^\circ}$. Thus $b=9\cdot\dfrac{\frac{\sqrt2}{2}}{\frac12}=9\sqrt2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The larger angle is opposite the longer side, consistent with this result.
