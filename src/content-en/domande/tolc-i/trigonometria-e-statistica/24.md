In the histogram, the heights are absolute-frequency densities. Which class contains the most observations?

::grafico{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,8 0,8;2,0 7,0 7,4 2,4;7,0 11,0 11,3 7,3" xmin=-1 xmax=12 ymin=-1 ymax=9 nomi="x,d" titolo="Three classes with widths 2, 5 and 4 and densities 8, 4 and 3."}

- $[0,2)$
- $[7,11)$
- All three classes have equal frequency
- $[2,7)$
- The areas cannot determine it

## Explanation

A class frequency equals its rectangle area. The three areas are $2\cdot8=16$, $5\cdot4=20$ and $4\cdot3=12$. Thus the second class contains the most observations despite being lower than the first. With unequal widths, comparing heights alone is insufficient.
