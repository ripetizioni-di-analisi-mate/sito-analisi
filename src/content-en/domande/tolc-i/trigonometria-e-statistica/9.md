How many distinct pairs can be chosen from five people if the two people have no different roles?

- $20$
- $25$
- $5$
- $10$
- $15$

## Explanation

Choosing a first and second person gives $5\cdot4=20$ ordered choices. Each pair appears twice, once in each order. Dividing by $2$ gives $\binom52=10$. There is no further division by the number of people, because duplication comes only from exchanging the two members.
