A nonempty dataset of real numbers has zero descriptive variance. Which conclusion is necessary?

- Every observation is zero
- Every observation equals the mean
- The mean is zero
- There are exactly two observations
- The observations sum to one

## Explanation

Variance is the mean of nonnegative squares. It can be zero only if every square is zero, so every observation equals the mean. The common value need not be zero: for example, $5,5,5$ has zero variance and mean $5$.
