In a triangle $ABC$, let $a=BC=5$, $b=AC=8$ and $\alpha=\angle A=30^\circ$. How many noncongruent triangles satisfy these data?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- Infinitely many
- $2$

## Explanation

The sine rule gives $\sin\beta=\dfrac{8\sin30^\circ}{5}=\dfrac45$. There is an acute angle $\beta_1$ between $30^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ and its supplement $\beta_2$, between $90^\circ$ and $150^\circ$. Both leave a positive third angle after adding $30^\circ$, so they produce two different triangles.
