For the ordered values $1,2,3,4$, the cumulative frequencies are $5,13,18,22$. How many observations equal exactly $3$?

- $18$
- $5$
- $13$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The cumulative frequency $18$ counts all observations no greater than $3$. Subtract the $13$ observations no greater than $2$ to leave exactly those equal to $3$. Thus the required frequency is $18-13=5$.
