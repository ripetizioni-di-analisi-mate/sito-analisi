Which angle in $[0,2\pi)$ has the same terminal side as $-\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$?

- $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac\pi4$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac\pi2$

## Explanation

Angles with the same terminal side differ by a multiple of $2\pi$. Adding one full turn gives $-\dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2\pi=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, which lies in the required interval. Simply changing the sign reflects the rotation and generally changes the terminal side.
