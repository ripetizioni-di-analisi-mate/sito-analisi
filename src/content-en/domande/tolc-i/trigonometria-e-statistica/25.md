What is the exact value of $\cos15^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}$

## Explanation

Write $15^\circ=45^\circ-30^\circ$. The formula $\cos(a-b)=\cos a\cos b+\sin a\sin b$ gives $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\dfrac12=\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}$. The sign between the products is positive because this is the cosine of a difference.
