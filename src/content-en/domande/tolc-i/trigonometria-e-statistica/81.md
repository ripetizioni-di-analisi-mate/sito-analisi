A circular arc has radius $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and central angle $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ radians. How long is it?

- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac\pi9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The radian definition is $\theta=\dfrac sr$, so arc length is $s=r\theta$. Substitution gives $6\cdot\dfrac{2\pi}{3}=4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$. The angle is already in radians, so no conversion is needed.
