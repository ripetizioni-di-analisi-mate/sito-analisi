How many three-digit numbers with distinct digits can be formed from $0,1,2,3,4$?

- $60$
- $36$
- $64$
- $48$
- $100$

## Explanation

The first digit cannot be zero, so it has $4$ choices. The second may be zero but must differ from the first, so it still has $4$ choices. The third has $3$ remaining digits. The total is $4\cdot4\cdot3=48$.
