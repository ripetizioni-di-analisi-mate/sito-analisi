In a race with $7$ participants and no ties, how many podiums with first, second and third places are possible?

- $35$
- $210$
- $343$
- $42$
- $21$

## Explanation

First place has $7$ choices, leaving $6$ for second and $5$ for third. The product is $7\cdot6\cdot5=210$. There is no division by $3!$, because exchanging two people changes the finishing order and therefore the podium.
