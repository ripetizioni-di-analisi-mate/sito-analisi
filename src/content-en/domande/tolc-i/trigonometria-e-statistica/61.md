A dataset has descriptive variance $5$. Every observation is multiplied by $3$. What is the new variance?

- $45$
- $15$
- $8$
- $\sqrt{15}$
- $5$

## Explanation

Multiplying all observations by $3$ also multiplies the mean by $3$, so each deviation is tripled. The squared deviations are multiplied by $9$, and so is their mean. The new variance is $3^2\cdot5=45$.
