Two sides of a triangle have lengths $5$ and $8$, with included angle $60^\circ$. What is the third side length?

- $\sqrt{89}$
- $\sqrt{129}$
- $3$
- $13$
- $7$

## Explanation

The cosine rule gives $c^2=5^2+8^2-2\cdot5\cdot8\cos60^\circ=25+64-40=49$. A length is positive, so $c=7$. Summing the squares without the mixed term would apply only to an included right angle.
