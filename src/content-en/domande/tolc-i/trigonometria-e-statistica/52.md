Measurements in centimetres have a standard deviation of $3$ cm. The same data are converted to millimetres. What are the numerical value and units of the new population variance?

- $30\,\mathrm{mm}^2$
- $900\,\mathrm{mm}^2$
- $90\,\mathrm{mm}^2$
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}^2$
- $900\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The standard deviation changes from $3$ cm to $30$ mm. Variance is the square of the standard deviation, so it is $(30\ \mathrm{mm})^2$.
