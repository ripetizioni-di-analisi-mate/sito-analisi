What is the descriptive standard deviation of $0,0,6,6$, using divisor $N=4$ in the variance?

- $9$
- $3$
- $6$
- $18$
- $\sqrt3$

## Explanation

The mean is $3$, so the deviations are $-3,-3,3,3$. All their squares equal $9$, whose mean, the variance, is $9$. Standard deviation is the square root of the variance: $\sqrt9=3$.
