On a grid, a path goes from $(0,0)$ to $(3,2)$ using unit steps only east or north. How many paths avoid the point $(1,1)$?

- $6$
- $10$
- $2$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

There are $\binom52=10$ paths in total. Those passing through $(1,1)$ have an initial segment with one east and one north step, in $2$ orders, and a final segment with two east steps and one north step, in $3$ orders. Thus there are $2\cdot3=6$. Each forbidden path splits uniquely into these segments. By complement, there are $10-6=4$ allowed paths.
