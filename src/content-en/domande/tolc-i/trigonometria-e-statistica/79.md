What is $\sin(30^\circ+60^\circ)-\sin30^\circ-\sin60^\circ$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1+\sqrt3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The first term is $\sin90^\circ=1$. Subtracting the other two values gives $1-\dfrac12-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{2}$. The result is not zero: the sine of a sum is generally not the sum of the sines.
