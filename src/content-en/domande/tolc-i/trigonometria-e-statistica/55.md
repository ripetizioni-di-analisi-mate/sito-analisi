A triangle has sides of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ with included angle $30^\circ$. What is its area?

- $40\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $80\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $20\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $40\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The altitude relative to the base of length $8$ is $10\sin30^\circ=5$. Thus the area is $\dfrac12\cdot8\cdot5=20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Equivalently, use $\dfrac12ab\sin\gamma$, where the angle is included between the two given sides.
