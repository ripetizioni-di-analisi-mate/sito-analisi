How many real solutions does $\cos x=\dfrac65$ have?

- One
- Two
- None
- Four
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

For every real angle, cosine is a horizontal coordinate on the unit circle and belongs to $[-1,1]$. The value $\dfrac65$ exceeds $1$ and cannot be attained. Thus the equation has no real solutions, regardless of periodicity.
