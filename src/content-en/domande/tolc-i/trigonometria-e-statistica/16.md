An acute angle $\alpha$ satisfies $\cos(2\alpha)=-\dfrac12$. What is $\sin\alpha$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$

## Explanation

Using $\cos(2\alpha)=1-2\sin^2\alpha$ gives $-\dfrac12=1-2\sin^2\alpha$, hence $\sin^2\alpha=\dfrac34$. Since the angle is acute, its sine is positive. The allowed root is therefore $\sin\alpha=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
