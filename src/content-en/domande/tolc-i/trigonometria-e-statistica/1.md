What is $\sin\dfrac\pi6$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

An angle of $\dfrac\pi6$ radians equals $30^\circ$. Halving an equilateral triangle of side $2$ gives a leg of length $1$ opposite $30^\circ$ and a hypotenuse of length $2$. Sine is their ratio, so its value is $\dfrac12$.
