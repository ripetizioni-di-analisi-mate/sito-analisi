What is the complete solution set of $\cos x\ge\dfrac12$ in $[0,\pi]$?

- $\left[\dfrac\pi3,\pi\right]$
- $\left(0,\dfrac\pi3\right)$
- $\left[0,\dfrac\pi6\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{2\pi}{3},\pi\right]$
- $\left[0,\dfrac\pi3\right]$

## Explanation

On $[0,\pi]$, cosine decreases from $1$ to $-1$ and reaches $\dfrac12$ at $x=\dfrac\pi3$. The inequality therefore holds before that point, including equality. The endpoint $x=0$ is also allowed because its cosine is $1$.
