If $\alpha$ is acute and $\sin\alpha=\dfrac5{13}$, what is $\sin(2\alpha)$?

- $\dfrac{10}{13}$
- $\dfrac{120}{169}$
- $\dfrac{25}{169}$
- $\dfrac{60}{169}$
- $\dfrac{119}{169}$

## Explanation

Cosine is positive and equals $\sqrt{1-\dfrac{25}{169}}=\dfrac{12}{13}$. The sine double-angle formula therefore gives $\sin(2\alpha)=2\cdot\dfrac5{13}\cdot\dfrac{12}{13}=\dfrac{120}{169}$. The value $\dfrac{119}{169}$ would instead be the double-angle cosine.
