A right triangle has hypotenuse $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an angle of $30^\circ$. How long is the leg opposite that angle?

- $5\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt3}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The opposite leg divided by the hypotenuse equals the sine of the angle. Writing the leg as $c$ gives $\dfrac c{10}=\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12$. Multiplying by $10$ yields $c=5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Cosine would instead give the adjacent leg.
