Dataset A is $2,4,6$ and dataset B is $0,4,8$. Which comparison is correct, using descriptive variances?

- They have the same mean and variance
- B has twice A’s mean and four times its variance
- They have the same mean and B has twice A’s variance
- A has greater variance than B
- They have the same mean and B has four times A’s variance

## Explanation

Both datasets have mean $4$. The deviations in A are $-2,0,2$ and those in B are $-4,0,4$, exactly twice as large. Squared deviations and hence variance are four times as large: $\dfrac{32}{3}$ for B versus $\dfrac83$ for A.
