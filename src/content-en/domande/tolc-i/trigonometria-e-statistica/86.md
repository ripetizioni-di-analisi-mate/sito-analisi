$3$ people are chosen from $3$ boys and $2$ girls. How many groups contain at least one girl?

- $9$
- $10$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

There are $\binom53=10$ groups in total. Exactly one contains no girls: the group of all three boys. Subtracting it gives $10-1=9$. The condition permits either one or two girls, so counting only exactly one would miss allowed groups.
