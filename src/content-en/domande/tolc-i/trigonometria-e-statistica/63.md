A motor completes one turn per second. A sensor outputs $\cos(2\pi t)$ at time $t$ in seconds, but records only at integer times $t=0,1,2,\ldots$. Which other signal produces exactly the same samples?

- $g(t)=0$
- $g(t)=\cos(\pi t)$
- $g(t)=1$
- $g(t)=\sin(2\pi t)$
- $g(t)=-1$

## Explanation

At each sample the motor has completed a whole number of turns, so the cosine is $1$. The constant signal $1$ matches all samples even though the signals differ in between.
