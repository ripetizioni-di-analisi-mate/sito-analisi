What is the descriptive variance of $1,3$, using divisor $N=2$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $0$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The mean is $\dfrac{1+3}{2}=2$. The deviations from the mean are $-1$ and $1$, whose squares are both $1$. The requested variance is therefore $\dfrac{1+1}{2}=1$. Using divisor $N-1$ would produce a different quantity from the one specified.
