The charge in an RC circuit is given by $q(t)=9(1-e^{-t/4})$ in coulombs, with $t$ in seconds. What is the initial current in amperes?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $0$
- $36$
- $9$

## Explanation

Current is the derivative of charge. At zero the exponential equals one, and differentiation introduces the reciprocal of the time constant. This gives $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
