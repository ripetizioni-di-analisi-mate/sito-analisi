A fund receives continuous deposits of $6$ units per year and earns instantaneous interest at $2\%$ of its balance $C$. Which equation describes the balance before any withdrawals?

- $C'=0.02(C+6)$
- $C'=0.02C+6$
- $C'=6-0.02C$
- $C'=6C+0.02$

## Explanation

Interest and deposits are both inflows. The former is proportional to the balance and the latter is constant, so add $0.02C$ and $6$.
