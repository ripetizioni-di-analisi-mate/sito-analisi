A velocity satisfies $v'=4-v^2$ and $v(0)=3$. Without solving, how does the velocity initially change, and at what positive value is its acceleration zero?

- It decreases; acceleration is zero at $v=4$.
- It decreases; acceleration is zero at $v=2$.
- It stays constant at $3$.
- It increases; acceleration is zero at $v=2$.

## Explanation

The initial derivative is $4-9=-5$, so the velocity decreases. The right-hand side has zeros $-2$ and $2$; only the latter is positive.
