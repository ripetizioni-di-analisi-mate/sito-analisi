Which function solves $y''+16y=0$ with $y(0)=0$ and $y'(0)=9$?

- $- \dfrac{9 \sin{\left(4 x \right)}}{4}$
- $9 \sin{\left(4 x \right)}$
- $\dfrac{9 \sin{\left(4 x \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9 \cos{\left(4 x \right)}}{4}$

## Explanation

The first condition makes the cosine term vanish. The derivative of the sine term at zero equals its coefficient times $4$.
