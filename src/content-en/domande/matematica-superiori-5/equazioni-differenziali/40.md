For $y'=-2y+6$ and $y(0)=3$, what is $y(5)$?

- $0$
- $-3$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The initial value makes the right-hand side zero, so the constant function equal to three satisfies both the equation and the initial condition. Uniqueness for the initial value problem rules out any other solution. This gives $3$.
