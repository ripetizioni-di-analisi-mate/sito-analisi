An oscillator follows $s(t)=7\cos(3t)$, with position in meters and time in seconds. What is its initial acceleration in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $0$
- $-63$
- $63$
- $-21$

## Explanation

The second derivative of the cosine introduces the square of the angular frequency with a minus sign. At time zero the cosine equals one. This gives $-63$.
