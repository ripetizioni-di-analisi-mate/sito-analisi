Which function solves $y\prime\prime+y=0$, $y(0)=8$, and $y\prime(0)=0$?

- $8 \sin{(x )}$
- $8 \cos{(x )}$
- $8 e^{- x}$
- $\cos{(8 x )}$

## Explanation

The general solution is $y=A\cos x+B\sin x$. The initial value gives $A=8$, while the initial derivative gives $B=0$. Therefore the solution is $y=8\cos x$.
