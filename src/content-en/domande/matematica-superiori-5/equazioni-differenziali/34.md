For a solution of $y''+4y=0$, which quantity remains constant, as can be checked by differentiating it?

- $y''+4y'$
- $y'+4y$
- $(y')^2+4y^2$
- $(y')^2-4y^2$

## Explanation

The derivative of $(y')^2+4y^2$ is $2y'(y''+4y)$, which vanishes by the given equation. Its value is therefore fixed by the initial data.
