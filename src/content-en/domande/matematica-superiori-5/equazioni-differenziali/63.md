In $dy/dt=-ky$, with $t$ in seconds, introduce dimensionless time $s=kt$ and set $Y(s)=y(t)$. Which equation does $Y$ satisfy for $k>0$?

- $\dfrac{dY}{ds}=-k^2Y$
- $\dfrac{dY}{ds}=-Y$
- $\dfrac{dY}{ds}=-Y/k^2$
- $\dfrac{dY}{ds}=Y$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $dy/dt=k\,dY/ds$. The equation becomes $k\,dY/ds=-kY$, and the factor $k$ cancels.
