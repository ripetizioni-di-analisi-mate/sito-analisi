The charge in an RC circuit is given by $q(t)=5(1-e^{-t/2})$ in coulombs, with $t$ in seconds. What is the initial current in amperes?

- $10$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$

## Explanation

Current is the derivative of charge. At zero the exponential equals one, and differentiation introduces the reciprocal of the time constant. This gives $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
