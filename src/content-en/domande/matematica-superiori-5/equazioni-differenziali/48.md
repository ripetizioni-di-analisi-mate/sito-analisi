An oscillator satisfies $y''+y=0$. Only $y(0)=0$ is known. Which additional measurement determines the trajectory completely?

- The fact that $y(2\pi)=0$.
- The velocity $y'(0)$.
- The acceleration $y''(0)$.
- The fact that $y(\pi)=0$.

## Explanation

The initial condition removes the cosine term, leaving $y=B\sin x$. Every $B$ gives zero initial acceleration and zero displacement at $\pi$ and $2\pi$, but $y'(0)=B$ distinguishes the trajectories.
