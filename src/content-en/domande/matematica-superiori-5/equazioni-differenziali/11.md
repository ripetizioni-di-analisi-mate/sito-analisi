For $y''-3y'+2y=e^x$, the trial $Ae^x$ fails for every $A$. Which lowest-degree modification can produce a particular solution?

- $A+Be^x$
- $Axe^x$
- $Ae^{2x}$
- $A\cos x+B\sin x$

## Explanation

The number $1$ is a simple root of $(r-1)(r-2)=0$. Multiply the trial by $x$ exactly once to remove its overlap with the homogeneous solution.
