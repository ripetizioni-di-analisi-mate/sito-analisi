The equation $y'' + 3y' + 2y = 6$ has a constant right-hand side. Which particular solution does the method of undetermined coefficients give?

- $y_p = 3x$
- $y_p = 6$
- $y_p = 3$
- $y_p = 12$

## Explanation

A constant right-hand side is a polynomial of degree $0$. The characteristic equation $r^2 + 3r + 2 = 0$ has the roots $-1$ and $-2$, so $0$ is not a root and we look for a constant $y_p = c$.

Since $y_p' = y_p'' = 0$, substituting gives $2c = 6$, therefore $c = 3$ and the particular solution is $y_p = 3$.
