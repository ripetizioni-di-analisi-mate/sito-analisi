A population follows $N(t)=5e^{2t}$. How long does it take to reach three times its initial size?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 \right)}}{3}$
- $2 \ln{\left(3 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Divide by the initial value $5$. Taking the natural logarithm turns $e^{2t}=3$ into $2t=\ln 3$. This gives $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}{2}$.
