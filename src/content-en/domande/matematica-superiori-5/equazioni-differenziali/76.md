An initially uncharged capacitor follows $q(t)=Q(1-e^{-t/\tau})$. For known $Q$ and $0\le q<Q$, which quantity plotted against $t$ gives a straight line?

- $\ln(1-q/Q)$
- $e^{q/Q}$
- $\ln(q/Q)$
- $q^2$

## Explanation

Divide by $Q$ and subtract from one to get $1-q/Q=e^{-t/\tau}$. Taking logarithms gives the line $-t/\tau$, whose slope determines the time constant.
