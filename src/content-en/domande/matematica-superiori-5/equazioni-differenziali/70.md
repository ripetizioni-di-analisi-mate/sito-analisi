A resistor $R$ and a capacitor $C$ are in series across constant voltage $V$. The charge satisfies $Rq'+q/C=V$. What is the equilibrium charge?

- $RCV$
- $V/C$
- $V/R$
- $CV$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the charge is constant, so $q'=0$. This leaves $q/C=V$, giving $q=CV$, independently of resistance.
