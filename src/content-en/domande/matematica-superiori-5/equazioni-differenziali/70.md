Which function solves $y''+4y=0$ with $y(0)=0$ and $y'(0)=5$?

- $5 \sin{\left(2 x \right)}$
- $\dfrac{5 \sin{\left(2 x \right)}}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{5 \sin{\left(2 x \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5 \cos{\left(2 x \right)}}{2}$

## Explanation

The first condition makes the cosine term vanish. The derivative of the sine term at zero equals its coefficient times $2$.
