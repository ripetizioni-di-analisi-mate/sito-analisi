The solution of $y'=y^2\cos x$ with $y(0)=1$ is $y=1/(1-\sin x)$. What is the largest interval containing zero on which it is defined?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\left(-\pi,\pi\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{3\pi}{2},\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

The denominator vanishes where $\sin x=1$, at $x=\pi/2+2k\pi$. The two successive such points surrounding zero are $-3\pi/2$ and $\pi/2$.
