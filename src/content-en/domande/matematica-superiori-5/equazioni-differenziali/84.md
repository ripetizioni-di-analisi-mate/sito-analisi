A quantity changes at constant rate $c$ until $t=1$ and at rate $2c$ after $t=1$, with $c>0$. Which property does the solution have when the two pieces are joined without a jump?

- It has a jump of size $c$.
- It is periodic.
- It is continuous with unequal one-sided derivatives at $1$.
- It is differentiable at $1$ with derivative $3c$.

## Explanation

Integration gives two linear pieces. Matching fixes the second constant to preserve the value, while the slopes on either side remain $c$ and $2c$.
