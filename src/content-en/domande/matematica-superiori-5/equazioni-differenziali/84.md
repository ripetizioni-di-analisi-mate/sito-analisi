A substance decays according to $N(t)=N_0e^{-4t}$. How long does it take for only one quarter of $N_0$ to remain?

- $4 \ln{\left(4 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 \right)}}{4}$

## Explanation

One quarter is the square of one half. Solving $e^{-4t}=1/4$ gives $4t=\ln 4$. This yields $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 \right)}}{4}$.
