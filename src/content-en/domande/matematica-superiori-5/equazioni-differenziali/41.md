In the decay law $N'=-kN$, time is measured in days. What units must $k$ have?

- It is dimensionless.
- $\mathrm{days}$
- $\mathrm{days}^{2}$
- $\mathrm{day}^{-1}$

## Explanation

The derivative has units of amount per day. For $kN$ to have these same units, $k$ must have units of inverse time.
