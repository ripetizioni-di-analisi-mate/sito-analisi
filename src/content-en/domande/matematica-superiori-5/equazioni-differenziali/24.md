A substance decays according to $N(t)=N_0e^{-2t}$. How long does it take for only one quarter of $N_0$ to remain?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 \right)}}{2}$
- $2 \ln{\left(4 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 \right)}}{2}$

## Explanation

One quarter is the square of one half. Solving $e^{-2t}=1/4$ gives $2t=\ln 4$. This yields $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 \right)}}{2}$.
