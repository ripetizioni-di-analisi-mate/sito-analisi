The temperature of an object is given by $T(t)=40+90e^{-5t}$ in degrees Celsius. What temperature does it approach?

- $90$
- $40$
- $0$
- $130$

## Explanation

The exponential with a negative exponent approaches zero. After a long time only the constant term remains, which is the temperature of the environment. This gives $40$.
