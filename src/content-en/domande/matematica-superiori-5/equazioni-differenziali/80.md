The temperature of an object is given by $T(t)=30+70e^{-4t}$ in degrees Celsius. What temperature does it approach?

- $70$
- $0$
- $100$
- $30$

## Explanation

The exponential with a negative exponent approaches zero. After a long time only the constant term remains, which is the temperature of the environment. This gives $30$.
