What is the solution of the initial value problem $y' = (x + 1)\,y$, $y(0) = 1$?

- $y=e^{x+1}$
- $y=e^{x^2+x}$
- $y=1+x+\dfrac{x^2}{2}$
- $y=e^{\frac{x^2}{2}+x}$

## Explanation

The equation is separable and, since $y(0)=1>0$, a positive solution is sought near $x=0$. Separating the variables gives $\dfrac{dy}{y}=(x+1)\,dx$, and integrating gives $\ln y=\dfrac{x^2}{2}+x+c$. The condition $y(0)=1$ gives $\ln 1=c$, so $c=0$ and therefore $y=e^{\frac{x^2}{2}+x}$.
