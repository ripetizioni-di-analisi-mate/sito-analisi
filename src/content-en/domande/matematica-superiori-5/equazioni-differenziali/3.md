Solve the initial value problem $y' = (x + 1)\,y$, $y(0) = 1$.

- $y=e^{x+1}$
- $y=e^{x^2+x}$
- $y=1+x+x^2/2$
- $y=e^{x^2/2+x}$

## Explanation

Near the positive initial value, separate variables to obtain $dy/y=(x+1)\,dx$. This gives $\ln y=x^2/2+x+c$; the initial condition $y(0)=1$ sets $c=0$.
