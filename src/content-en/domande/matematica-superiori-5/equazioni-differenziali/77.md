A population follows $N(t)=7e^{3t}$. How long does it take to reach three times its initial size?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}{3}$
- $3 \ln{\left(3 \right)}$

## Explanation

Divide by the initial value $7$. Taking the natural logarithm turns $e^{3t}=3$ into $3t=\ln 3$. This gives $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}{3}$.
