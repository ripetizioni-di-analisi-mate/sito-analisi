A substance decays according to $N(t)=N_0e^{-3t}$. How long does it take for only one quarter of $N_0$ to remain?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $3 \ln{\left(4 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 \right)}}{3}$

## Explanation

One quarter is the square of one half. Solving $e^{-3t}=1/4$ gives $3t=\ln 4$. This yields $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 \right)}}{3}$.
