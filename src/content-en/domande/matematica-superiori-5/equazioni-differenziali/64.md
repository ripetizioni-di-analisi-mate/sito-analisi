A damped oscillator satisfies $y''+ay'+by=0$. At an equilibrium crossing it has $y=0$, $y'=2$ and $y''=-6$. Which coefficient is determined by this single measurement?

- $a=-3$
- $b=-3$
- $a=3$
- $b=3$

## Explanation

Substituting the data eliminates the term $by$. The remaining equation is $-6+2a=0$, fixing $a=3$ and leaving $b$ undetermined.
