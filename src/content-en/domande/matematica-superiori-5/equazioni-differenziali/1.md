When checking $y=e^{-x}+x-1$ in the equation $y'+y=x$, what is the sum of the derivative and the function?

- $x+2e^{-x}$
- $x+1$
- $x-1$
- $x$

## Explanation

The derivative is $-e^{-x}+1$. Adding $e^{-x}+x-1$ cancels both the exponential terms and the constants, leaving $x$.
