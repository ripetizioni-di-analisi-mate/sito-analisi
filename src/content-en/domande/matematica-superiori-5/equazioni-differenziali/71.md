Which function solves $y''+9y=0$ with $y(0)=0$ and $y'(0)=7$?

- $\dfrac{7 \sin{\left(3 x \right)}}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{7 \sin{\left(3 x \right)}}{3}$
- $7 \sin{\left(3 x \right)}$
- $\dfrac{7 \cos{\left(3 x \right)}}{3}$

## Explanation

The first condition makes the cosine term vanish. The derivative of the sine term at zero equals its coefficient times $3$.
