Which function solves $y'=-4y^2$ with $y(0)=\dfrac{1}{9}$ in a neighborhood of zero?

- $\dfrac{1}{9 - 4 x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9 x + 4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4 x + 1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4 x + 9}$

## Explanation

Separating the variables gives $(1/y)'=4$. The initial condition determines $1/y(0)=9$.
