A population starts at $500$, satisfies $P'=kP$, and doubles every two hours. When does it reach $8000$?

- $8\,\mathrm h$
- $16\,\mathrm h$
- $32\,\mathrm h$
- $4\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

The ratio $8000/500$ is $16=2^4$. Four doublings are needed, each taking two hours, so the time is eight hours.
