Solve the initial value problem $y'' - y' - 6y = 0$, $y(0) = 0$, $y'(0) = 5$.

- $y=e^{2x}-e^{-3x}$
- $y=e^{3x}+e^{-2x}$
- $y=e^{3x}-e^{-2x}$
- $y=5e^{3x}-5e^{-2x}$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation is $(r-3)(r+2)=0$. For $y=Ae^{3x}+Be^{-2x}$ the data give $A+B=0$ and $3A-2B=5$. Hence $A=1$ and $B=-1$.
