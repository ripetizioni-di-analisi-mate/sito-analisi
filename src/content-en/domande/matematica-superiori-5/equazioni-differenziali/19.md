Which function solves $y\prime\prime+y=0$, $y(0)=5$, and $y\prime(0)=0$?

- $5 \sin{(x )}$
- $5 e^{- x}$
- $5 \cos{(x )}$
- $\cos{(5 x )}$

## Explanation

The general solution is $y=A\cos x+B\sin x$. The initial value gives $A=5$, while the initial derivative gives $B=0$. Therefore the solution is $y=5\cos x$.
