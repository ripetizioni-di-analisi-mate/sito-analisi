The temperature of an object is given by $T(t)=20+50e^{-3t}$ in degrees Celsius. What temperature does it approach?

- $70$
- $0$
- $50$
- $20$

## Explanation

The exponential with a negative exponent approaches zero. After a long time only the constant term remains, which is the temperature of the environment. This gives $20$.
