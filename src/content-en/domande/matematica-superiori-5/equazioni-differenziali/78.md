A population follows $N(t)=9e^{4t}$. How long does it take to reach three times its initial size?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 \right)}}{3}$
- $4 \ln{\left(3 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}{4}$

## Explanation

Divide by the initial value $9$. Taking the natural logarithm turns $e^{4t}=3$ into $4t=\ln 3$. This gives $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}{4}$.
