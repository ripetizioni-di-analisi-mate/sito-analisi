The equation $y'=y/x$ is considered on $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$. Given $y(-1)=2$, what can we conclude about $y(1)$ if the function must satisfy the equation on each component of the domain?

- It can be any real number.
- It must be $-2$.
- It must be $2$.
- It must be zero.

## Explanation

Every solution has the form $Cx$ on each half-line, since the derivative of $y/x$ is zero. The left-hand datum gives $C=-2$ there. On the right the constant can be chosen independently.
