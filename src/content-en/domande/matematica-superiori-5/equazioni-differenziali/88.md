An oscillator follows $s(t)=5\cos(2t)$, with position in meters and time in seconds. What is its initial acceleration in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $-20$
- $-10$
- $0$
- $20$

## Explanation

The second derivative of the cosine introduces the square of the angular frequency with a minus sign. At time zero the cosine equals one. This gives $-20$.
