For $y''+2y'+ky=0$, when do sinusoidal factors occur in the nonzero real solutions?

- $k>1$
- $k<1$
- $k\le0$
- $k=1$

## Explanation

The roots of $r^2+2r+k=0$ are a nonreal conjugate pair when the discriminant $4-4k$ is negative. Thus sine and cosine appear exactly when $k>1$.
