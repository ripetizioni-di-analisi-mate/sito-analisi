A mass of $0.5$ kg oscillates on a spring of stiffness $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$. It starts at rest $0.1$ m from equilibrium. What is its maximum speed?

- $0.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}=\sqrt{100}=10$ radians per second. Starting at rest at an extreme gives an amplitude of $0.1$ m. The maximum speed is $\omega A=1$ m/s.
