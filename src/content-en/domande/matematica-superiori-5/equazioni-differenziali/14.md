Which function solves $y\prime\prime+y=0$, $y(0)=4$, and $y\prime(0)=0$?

- $4 \sin{(x )}$
- $4 \cos{(x )}$
- $4 e^{- x}$
- $\cos{(4 x )}$

## Explanation

The general solution is $y=A\cos x+B\sin x$. The initial value gives $A=4$, while the initial derivative gives $B=0$. Therefore the solution is $y=4\cos x$.
