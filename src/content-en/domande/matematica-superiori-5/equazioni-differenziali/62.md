Two solutions of $y''+y=0$ have initial data $(y(0),y'(0))$ equal to $(1,0)$ and $(0,2)$, respectively. Which combination of the solutions $u,v$ has initial data $(3,-4)$?

- $3u-4v$
- $3u-2v$
- $3u+2v$
- $-2u+3v$

## Explanation

By linearity, $au+bv$ is a solution. At zero its value is $a$ and its derivative is $2b$, giving $a=3$ and $b=-2$.
