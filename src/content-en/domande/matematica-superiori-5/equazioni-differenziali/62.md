Let $u$ and $v$ be the solutions of the equation $y'' + y = 0$ satisfying $u(0) = 1$, $u'(0) = 0$ and $v(0) = 0$, $v'(0) = 2$ respectively. Which linear combination of $u$ and $v$ is the solution with $y(0) = 3$ and $y'(0) = -4$?

- $3u-4v$
- $3u-2v$
- $3u+2v$
- $-2u+3v$

## Explanation

Since the equation $y'' + y = 0$ is linear and homogeneous, every combination $y = a\,u + b\,v$ with $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ is one of its solutions. At $x = 0$ we have $y(0) = a\,u(0) + b\,v(0) = a$ and $y'(0) = a\,u'(0) + b\,v'(0) = 2b$. The required conditions therefore give $a = 3$ and $2b = -4$, that is $b = -2$. Since the initial value problem has exactly one solution, the solution sought is $3u - 2v$. The combination $3u - 4v$ comes from setting $b$ equal to $y'(0)$ without dividing by $v'(0) = 2$.
