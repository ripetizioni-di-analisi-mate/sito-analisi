For $yy'=x$ with $y(0)=2$, which branch of the implicit relation represents the solution?

- $y=\sqrt{x^2+2}$
- $y=\sqrt{x^2+4}$
- $y=x+2$
- $y=-\sqrt{x^2+4}$

## Explanation

Integrating $y\,dy=x\,dx$ gives $y^2=x^2+c$. The initial value sets $c=4$ and selects the positive square root.
