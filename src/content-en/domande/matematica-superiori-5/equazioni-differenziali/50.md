Which function solves $y'=-3y^2$ with $y(0)=\dfrac{1}{7}$ in a neighborhood of zero?

- $\dfrac{1}{3 x + 1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3 x + 7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7 x + 3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7 - 3 x}$

## Explanation

Separating the variables gives $(1/y)'=3$. The initial condition determines $1/y(0)=7$.
