For $y''+y=0$ impose the boundary values $y(0)=0$ and $y(\pi)=1$. How many solutions meet both?

- None.
- One.
- Two.
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

The general solution is $A\cos x+B\sin x$. The first datum forces $A=0$, so all solutions satisfying the first condition also vanish at $\pi$, contrary to the second datum.
