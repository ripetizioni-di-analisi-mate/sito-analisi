Along which line does the direction field of $y'=x-y$ have horizontal line elements?

- $y=x+1$
- $y=-x$
- $y=x$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

A line element is horizontal when its slope is zero. Setting $x-y=0$ gives the diagonal $y=x$. This describes slopes at those points for any solution.
