In $y'=y(1-y)$, a solution starts with $0<y(0)<1$. Which way does the field point, and which equilibrium lies ahead?

- It increases towards $1$.
- It stays at its initial value.
- It increases without bound.
- It decreases towards $0$.

## Explanation

The product is positive between $0$ and $1$. The value $1$ is an equilibrium and uniqueness prevents crossing it. The limit must be a zero of the right-hand side, hence it is $1$.
