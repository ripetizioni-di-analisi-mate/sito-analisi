Which function solves $y'=-2y^2$ with $y(0)=\dfrac{1}{5}$ in a neighborhood of zero?

- $\dfrac{1}{2 x + 1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5 - 2 x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 x + 5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5 x + 2}$

## Explanation

Separating the variables gives $(1/y)'=2$. The initial condition determines $1/y(0)=5$.
