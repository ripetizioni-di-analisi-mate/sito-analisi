The charge in an RC circuit is given by $q(t)=7(1-e^{-t/3})$ in coulombs, with $t$ in seconds. What is the initial current in amperes?

- $7$
- $0$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

Current is the derivative of charge. At zero the exponential equals one, and differentiation introduces the reciprocal of the time constant. This gives $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
