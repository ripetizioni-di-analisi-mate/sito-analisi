Which of the following differential equations is separable?

- $y' = x^2 + y^2$
- $y' = x + y$
- $y' = e^{x + y}$
- $y' = \sin(xy)$

## Explanation

A first-order equation is separable if its right-hand side is the product of a function of $x$ alone and a function of $y$ alone.

By the properties of powers, $e^{x + y} = e^{x}\, e^{y}$, so the equation $y' = e^{x + y}$ is **separable** with $g(x) = e^{x}$ and $h(y) = e^{y}$.

The right-hand sides $x + y$, $\sin(xy)$ and $x^2 + y^2$ cannot be written as a product $g(x)\, h(y)$, therefore the other three equations are not separable.
