An oscillator follows $s(t)=9\cos(4t)$, with position in meters and time in seconds. What is its initial acceleration in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $144$
- $-36$
- $-144$
- $0$

## Explanation

The second derivative of the cosine introduces the square of the angular frequency with a minus sign. At time zero the cosine equals one. This gives $-144$.
