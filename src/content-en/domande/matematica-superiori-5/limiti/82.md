What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to -\infty} x + \sqrt{x^{2} + 2 x}$?

- $-2$
- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

For $x<0$ we have $\sqrt{x^2}=-x$. Using the conjugate gives the quotient $\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt{x^2+2x}-x}$, and the denominator divided by $-x$ approaches two. This gives $-1$.
