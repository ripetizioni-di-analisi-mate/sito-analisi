The sequence $a_n$ equals $1$ at even indices and $-1$ at odd indices. Which property proves that it does not converge?

- Every term is nonzero.
- Its absolute value is constant.
- It is bounded above.
- Its even and odd subsequences have different limits.

## Explanation

If a sequence converges, every subsequence has the same limit. Here the even indices give the limit $1$ and the odd indices give $-1$.
