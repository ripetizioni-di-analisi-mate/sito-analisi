Let $a_n$ be the decimal number with $n$ nines after the decimal point. What is the limit of $n(1-a_n)$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $+\infty$
- $9$

## Explanation

Each new digit divides the error by ten, so $1-a_n=10^{-n}$. The exponential $10^n$ grows faster than $n$, and $n/10^n$ tends to zero.
