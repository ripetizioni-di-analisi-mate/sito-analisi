For $f(x)=5-2x$, the condition $|x-1|<\delta$ must guarantee $|f(x)-3|<0.02$. What is the largest possible $\delta$?

- $0.01$
- $0.04$
- $0.02$
- $0.1$

## Explanation

The distance from the limit is $|f(x)-3|=2|x-1|$. Keeping $|x-1|<0.01$ keeps this below $0.02$. Any larger radius includes points outside the tolerance.
