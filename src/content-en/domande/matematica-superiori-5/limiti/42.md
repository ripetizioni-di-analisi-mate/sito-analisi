For $x\ne0$ we have $f(x)=\dfrac{|x|}{x}$. What happens to the limit at zero?

- The limit equals one.
- The one-sided limits exist and are different.
- Both one-sided limits equal negative one.
- The limit equals zero.

## Explanation

To the left of zero the fraction equals negative one. To the right it equals one, so the two one-sided limits differ.
