What is the order of the infinitesimal $f(x) = \sin\left(x^{3}\right) \cdot \left(e^{x} - 1\right)$ as $x \to 0$, with respect to the reference infinitesimal $x$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

By the standard limit of the sine with argument $x^3 \to 0$ we have $\sin\left(x^{3}\right) \sim x^{3}$. By the standard limit of the exponential we have $e^{x} - 1 \sim x$.

In a product each factor can be replaced by an asymptotic function, so $f(x) \sim x^{3} \cdot x = x^{4}$. Therefore $\dfrac{f(x)}{x^4} \to 1$ and the order of the infinitesimal is $4$.
