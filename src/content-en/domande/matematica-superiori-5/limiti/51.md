What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to \infty} x \left(\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{9}{x}} - 1\right)$?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the conjugate turns the product into $\dfrac{9}{\sqrt{1+9/x}+1}$. The denominator approaches two. This gives $\dfrac{9}{2}$.
