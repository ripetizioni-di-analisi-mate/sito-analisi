Near $2$, suppose $g(x)\le f(x)\le h(x)$, with $\lim_{x\to2}g(x)=1$ and $\lim_{x\to2}h(x)=3$. If the limit of $f$ exists, what follows?

- It belongs to $[1,3]$.
- It must be $2$.
- It must differ from $1$.
- It must be strictly less than $3$.

## Explanation

Order is preserved upon taking limits, giving two non-strict inequalities. Either endpoint is possible, for example if $f=g$ or $f=h$.
