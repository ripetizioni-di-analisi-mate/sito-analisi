What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to -\infty} x + \sqrt{x^{2} + 3 x}$?

- $-3$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $x<0$ we have $\sqrt{x^2}=-x$. Using the conjugate gives the quotient $\dfrac{3x}{\sqrt{x^2+3x}-x}$, and the denominator divided by $-x$ approaches two. This gives $- \dfrac{3}{2}$.
