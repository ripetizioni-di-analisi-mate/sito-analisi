An increasing sequence satisfies $a_n<5$ for every $n$. Which additional fact guarantees that its limit is exactly $5$?

- Its first term is positive.
- Consecutive terms differ by less than $1$.
- For every $\varepsilon>0$ some term exceeds $5-\varepsilon$.
- All its terms are rational.

## Explanation

Once a term exceeds $5-\varepsilon$, monotonicity keeps all later terms between that level and $5$. This is the condition for convergence to $5$.
