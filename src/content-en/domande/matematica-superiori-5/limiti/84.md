What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to -\infty} x + \sqrt{x^{2} + 4 x}$?

- $-4$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

For $x<0$ we have $\sqrt{x^2}=-x$. Using the conjugate gives the quotient $\dfrac{4x}{\sqrt{x^2+4x}-x}$, and the denominator divided by $-x$ approaches two. This gives $-2$.
