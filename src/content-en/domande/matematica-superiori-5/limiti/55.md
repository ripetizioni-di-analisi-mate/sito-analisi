For $x>0$ let $f(x)=x\lfloor1/x\rfloor$, where $\lfloor t\rfloor$ is the greatest integer no larger than $t$. What is its limit as $x\to0^+$?

- $0$
- $+\infty$
- $1$
- It does not exist

## Explanation

The inequalities $\dfrac1x-1<\lfloor1/x\rfloor\le\dfrac1x$ give $1-x<f(x)\le1$. Both bounds approach one.
