Calculate $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{x^2 + 3x} - \sqrt{x^2 - x} \right)$.

- $0$
- $+\infty$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Multiplication by the conjugate reduces the numerator to $4x$. Factor $x>0$ from the denominator. The remaining sum $\sqrt{1+3/x}+\sqrt{1-1/x}$ tends to $2$.
