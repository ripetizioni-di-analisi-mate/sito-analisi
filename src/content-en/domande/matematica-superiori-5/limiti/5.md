For $f(x)=\dfrac{2x-1}{x^2-x}$, what are the right-hand and left-hand limits at zero, in that order?

- $(+\infty,+\infty)$
- $(-1,-1)$
- $(+\infty,-\infty)$
- $(-\infty,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The numerator tends to $-1$, and the denominator is $x(x-1)$. Near zero the denominator is negative on the right and positive on the left, while the quotient grows in magnitude.
