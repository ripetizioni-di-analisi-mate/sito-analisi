What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to \infty} x \left(\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{7}{x}} - 1\right)$?

- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the conjugate turns the product into $\dfrac{7}{\sqrt{1+7/x}+1}$. The denominator approaches two. This gives $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
