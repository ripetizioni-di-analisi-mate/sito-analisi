What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x^5 + e^{x}}{2e^{x} + \ln x}$?

- $2$
- $+\infty$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The limit has the form $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$. Dividing the numerator and the denominator by $e^{x}$ gives
$$
\frac{x^5 + e^{x}}{2e^{x} + \ln x} = \frac{\frac{x^5}{e^{x}} + 1}{2 + \frac{\ln x}{e^{x}}} .
$$
By the hierarchy of infinities, $e^{x}$ is an infinity of higher order than $x^5$ and $\ln x$, so $\dfrac{x^5}{e^{x}} \to 0$ and $\dfrac{\ln x}{e^{x}} \to 0$. Therefore the limit equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
