Consider $\ln(\sin x)$ near $0$. From which side of its real domain can zero be approached, and with what limit?

- From both sides, with limit $0$.
- From the left, with limit $-\infty$.
- From the right, with limit $+\infty$.
- From the right, with limit $-\infty$.

## Explanation

The logarithm requires $\sin x>0$, which near zero occurs only on the right. The argument tends to zero through positive values, so its logarithm tends to $-\infty$.
