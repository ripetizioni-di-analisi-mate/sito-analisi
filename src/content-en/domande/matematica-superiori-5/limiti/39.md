What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to+\infty}\dfrac{9x^2+1}{2x^2+3}$?

- $18$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Divide the numerator and denominator by $x^2$. The terms $\dfrac{1}{x^2}$ and $\dfrac{3}{x^2}$ approach zero. This leaves the ratio of the leading coefficients, namely $\dfrac{9}{2}$.
