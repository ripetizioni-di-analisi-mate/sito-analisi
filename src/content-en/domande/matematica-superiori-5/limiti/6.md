Find the value of the real parameter $k$ for which $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{k x^3 - x + 2}{5 - 2x^3} = 3$.

- $k=6$
- $k=-\dfrac32$
- $k=-6$
- $k=3$

## Explanation

Dividing the numerator and denominator by $x^3$ gives the limit $k/(-2)$. Setting it equal to $3$ yields $k=-6$.
