What value does $\dfrac{2^{x} + 3^{x}}{3^{x + 1}}$ tend to as $x \to +\infty$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The numerator and the denominator tend to $+\infty$, so the limit is in the form $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$.

We have $3^{x + 1} = 3 \cdot 3^{x}$. Dividing the numerator and the denominator by $3^x$ we obtain
$$
\frac{2^{x} + 3^{x}}{3 \cdot 3^{x}} = \frac{\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{x} + 1}{3} .
$$
Since the base $\dfrac{2}{3}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, we have $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{x} \to 0$, so the limit equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
