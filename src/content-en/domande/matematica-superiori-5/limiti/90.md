The measure $h$ of an angle is given in degrees. What is the limit of the ratio $\dfrac{\sin h^\circ}{h}$ as $h\to0$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{180}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$

## Explanation

An angle of $h$ degrees measures $u=\dfrac{\pi h}{180}$ radians, so $\sin h^\circ=\sin u$. The ratio can therefore be written as
$$
\dfrac{\sin h^\circ}{h}=\dfrac{\sin u}{u}\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{180} .
$$
As $h\to0$ also $u\to0$, so the first factor tends to $1$ by the standard limit of the sine. Consequently the ratio tends to $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$.
