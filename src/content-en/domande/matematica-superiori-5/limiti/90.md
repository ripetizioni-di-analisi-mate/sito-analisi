A calculator interprets its numeric input $h$ in degrees. What is the limit of the displayed sine divided by $h$ as $h\to0$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{180}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$

## Explanation

The angle in radians is $u=\pi h/180$. The ratio is therefore $(\sin u/u)(\pi/180)$, whose first factor tends to one. The angular unit sets the scale factor.
