Find the order of the infinitesimal $f(x) = \sqrt{1 + x^2} - 1$ relative to $x$ as $x \to 0$.

- $4$
- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The conjugate gives $\sqrt{1+x^2}-1=x^2/(\sqrt{1+x^2}+1)$. Dividing by $x^2$ gives a finite nonzero limit of $1/2$, so the order is $2$.
