What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to \infty} x \left(\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{5}{x}} - 1\right)$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the conjugate turns the product into $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{1+5/x}+1}$. The denominator approaches two. This gives $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
