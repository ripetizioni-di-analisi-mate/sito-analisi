As $x \to +\infty$ the root $\sqrt{x}$ and the variable $x$ both tend to $+\infty$. What is the limit of the difference $\sqrt{x} - x$?

- $-1$
- $0$
- $+\infty$
- $-\infty$

## Explanation

Both terms tend to $+\infty$, so the difference is in the indeterminate form $+\infty - \infty$.

We factor out the term of highest degree, that is $x$:
$$
\sqrt{x} - x = x \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} - 1 \right) .
$$
The second factor tends to $-1$ and the first to $+\infty$, therefore the product tends to $-\infty$.
