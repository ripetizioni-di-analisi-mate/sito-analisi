What is the limit of the sequence $a_n=\dfrac{7 n^{2} + n}{3 n^{2} + 4}$?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $3$
- $7$

## Explanation

Divide both numerator and denominator by $n^2$. The coefficients $7$ and $3$ remain because the other terms approach zero. This gives $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
