What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{1 - \cos{\left(3 x \right)}}{x \sin{\left(7 x \right)}}$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{9}{7}$
- $\dfrac{9}{14}$
- $\dfrac{3}{14}$

## Explanation

Factor $x^2$ out of both numerator and denominator. The two quotients approach $9/2$ and $7$. This gives $\dfrac{9}{14}$.
