What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{e^{7 x} - 1}{\sin{\left(4 x \right)}}$?

- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

Divide the numerator and denominator by $x$. The first quotient approaches $7$ and the second approaches $4$. This gives $\dfrac{7}{4}$.
