A thermometer reading satisfies $T(t)=20+4e^{-t}\cos t$ for $t\ge0$. Which bound guarantees that the reading eventually stays close to $20$?

- $|T(t)-20|\le4e^{-t}$
- $T(t)=20+4e^{-t}$ for every $t$
- $T(t)\ge20$ for every $t$
- $|T(t)-20|\ge4e^{-t}$

## Explanation

Since $|\cos t|\le1$, the deviation is at most $4e^{-t}$ in magnitude. This upper bound tends to zero even though the deviation changes sign.
