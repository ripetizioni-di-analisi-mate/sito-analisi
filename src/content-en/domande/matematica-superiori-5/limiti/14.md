What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to+\infty}\dfrac{4x^2+1}{2x^2+3}$?

- $8$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Divide the numerator and denominator by $x^2$. The terms $\dfrac{1}{x^2}$ and $\dfrac{3}{x^2}$ approach zero. This leaves the ratio of the leading coefficients, namely $\dfrac{4}{2}$.
