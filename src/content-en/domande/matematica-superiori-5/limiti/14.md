For a geometric sequence with first term $6$ and ratio $-1/3$, what are the limit of the terms and the limit of the partial sums, in that order?

- $(6,0)$
- $\left(-\dfrac13,\dfrac92\right)$
- $(0,9)$
- $\left(0,\dfrac92\right)$

## Explanation

The ratio has modulus less than one, so its powers tend to zero despite their alternating signs. The infinite sum is $6/(1+1/3)=9/2$.
