What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{1 - \cos{\left(3 x \right)}}{1 - \cos{\left(7 x \right)}}$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{9}{49}$
- $\dfrac{49}{9}$

## Explanation

Apply the limit of $(1-\cos u)/u^2$, which equals one half, twice. This leaves the squares of the coefficients of $x$. This gives $\dfrac{9}{49}$.
