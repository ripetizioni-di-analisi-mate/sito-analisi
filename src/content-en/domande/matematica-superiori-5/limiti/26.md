A student claims that if $a_{n+1}-a_n\to0$, the sequence $a_n$ converges to a real number. Which sequence disproves the conclusion?

- $a_n=n$
- $a_n=1/n$
- $a_n=(-1)^n$
- $a_n=\sqrt n$

## Explanation

For $a_n=\sqrt n$ the difference between consecutive terms is $1/(\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt n)$ and tends to zero. The terms themselves grow without bound.
