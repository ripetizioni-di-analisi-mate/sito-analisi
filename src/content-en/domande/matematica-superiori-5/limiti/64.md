What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{1 - \cos{\left(2 x \right)}}{x \sin{\left(5 x \right)}}$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Factor $x^2$ out of both numerator and denominator. The two quotients approach $2$ and $5$. This gives $\dfrac{2}{5}$.
