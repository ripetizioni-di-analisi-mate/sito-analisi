Which point is isolated in the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{x^2(x-3)}$?

- $3$
- $0$
- $4$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The radicand vanishes at zero. For $x\ne0$, its sign is that of $x-3$, so the domain is $\{0\}\cup[3,+\infty)$. Only zero has a neighbourhood containing no other domain point.
