About a function $f$ it is known that $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x} = 3$. What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} f(x)$?

- $3$
- $0$
- It cannot be determined from the data
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

For $x \neq 0$ we can write $f(x) = \dfrac{f(x)}{x} \cdot x$. The first factor tends to $3$ and the second tends to $0$, so by the product law for limits $f(x) \to 3 \cdot 0 = 0$. Therefore the limit is $0$, whatever the function $f$ satisfying the hypothesis, as for example $f(x) = 3x$ or $f(x) = 3x + x^2$.
