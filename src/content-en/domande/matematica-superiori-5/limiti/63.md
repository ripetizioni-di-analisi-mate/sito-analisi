Which observation would suffice to disprove $\lim_{x\to0}f(x)=2$?

- $f(1)=3$.
- For every $\delta>0$ there is an $x$ with $0<|x|<\delta$ and $|f(x)-2|\ge1$.
- $f(0)=3$.
- $f(x)\ne2$ for every $x\ne0$.

## Explanation

For the fixed tolerance $\varepsilon=1$, every neighbourhood contains a point outside the allowed band. Thus the definition of the limit cannot hold.
