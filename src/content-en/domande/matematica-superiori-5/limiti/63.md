What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{e^{9 x} - 1}{\sin{\left(5 x \right)}}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{5}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $5$

## Explanation

Divide the numerator and denominator by $x$. The first quotient approaches $9$ and the second approaches $5$. This gives $\dfrac{9}{5}$.
