What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to+\infty}\dfrac{7x^2+1}{2x^2+3}$?

- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Divide the numerator and denominator by $x^2$. The terms $\dfrac{1}{x^2}$ and $\dfrac{3}{x^2}$ approach zero. This leaves the ratio of the leading coefficients, namely $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
