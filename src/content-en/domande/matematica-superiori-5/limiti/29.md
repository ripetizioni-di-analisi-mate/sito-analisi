A tank contains $V(t)=60-\dfrac{60}{t+1}$ liters at time $t\geq0$. How many liters short of the limiting volume is it after five time units?

- $50$
- $60$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

The fractional term approaches zero, so the limiting volume is sixty liters. The shortfall at the given time equals the value of the fractional term. This gives $10$.
