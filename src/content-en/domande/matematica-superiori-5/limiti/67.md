What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{1 - \cos{\left(2 x \right)}}{1 - \cos{\left(5 x \right)}}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{25}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Apply the limit of $(1-\cos u)/u^2$, which equals one half, twice. This leaves the squares of the coefficients of $x$. This gives $\dfrac{4}{25}$.
