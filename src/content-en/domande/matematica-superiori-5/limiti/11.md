Calculate $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left( 1 - \frac{3}{x} \right)^{2x}$.

- $e^{-3}$
- $1$
- $e^{-6}$
- $e^{6}$

## Explanation

The base is positive for large $x$. The logarithm is $2x\ln(1-3/x)$. Since $\ln(1-3/x)$ is asymptotic to $-3/x$, this tends to $-6$. Exponentiating gives $e^{-6}$.
