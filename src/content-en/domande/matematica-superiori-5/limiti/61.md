What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{e^{5 x} - 1}{\sin{\left(3 x \right)}}$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Divide the numerator and denominator by $x$. The first quotient approaches $5$ and the second approaches $3$. This gives $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
