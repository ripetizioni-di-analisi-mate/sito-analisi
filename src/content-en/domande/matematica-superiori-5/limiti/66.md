What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{1 - \cos{\left(4 x \right)}}{x \sin{\left(9 x \right)}}$?

- $\dfrac{16}{9}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Factor $x^2$ out of both numerator and denominator. The two quotients approach $8$ and $9$. This gives $\dfrac{8}{9}$.
