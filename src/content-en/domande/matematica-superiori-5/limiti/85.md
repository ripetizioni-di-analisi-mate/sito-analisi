What is the limit of the sequence $a_n=\dfrac{5 n^{2} + n}{2 n^{2} + 3}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

Divide both numerator and denominator by $n^2$. The coefficients $5$ and $2$ remain because the other terms approach zero. This gives $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
