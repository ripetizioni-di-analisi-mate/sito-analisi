For positive $x$, what is the smallest threshold $M$ beyond which $\dfrac{x+3}{2x}$ differs from $\dfrac12$ by less than $0.01$?

- $100$
- $50$
- $150$
- $300$

## Explanation

The difference is $\dfrac3{2x}$. The inequality $\dfrac3{2x}<0.01$ is equivalent to $x>150$, so no smaller threshold suffices.
