What is the limit of the sequence $a_n=\dfrac{9 n^{2} + n}{4 n^{2} + 5}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

Divide both numerator and denominator by $n^2$. The coefficients $9$ and $4$ remain because the other terms approach zero. This gives $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
