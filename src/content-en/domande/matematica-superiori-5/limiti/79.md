What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0^+} \dfrac{x^{2} \sin{\left(\dfrac{1}{x} \right)}}{x + 2}$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The sine stays between $-1$ and $1$. By the squeeze theorem the numerator approaches zero, and the denominator approaches $2$. This gives $0$.
