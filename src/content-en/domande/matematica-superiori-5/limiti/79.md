The total cost of producing $q$ units is $C(q) = q^2 + 5q + 200$ euros, where $200$ euros are the fixed costs. What value does the average cost $\bar{C}(q) = \dfrac{C(q)}{q}$ tend to as $q \to 0^{+}$?

- $0$
- $5$
- $+\infty$
- $200$

## Explanation

For $q > 0$ we have
$$
\bar{C}(q) = \frac{q^2 + 5q + 200}{q} = q + 5 + \frac{200}{q} .
$$
As $q \to 0^{+}$ the first two terms tend to $0$ and to $5$, while $\dfrac{200}{q}$ tends to $+\infty$, because the numerator is positive and the denominator tends to $0$ through positive values. Therefore the average cost tends to $+\infty$, because the fixed costs of $200$ euros are spread over an ever smaller number of units.
