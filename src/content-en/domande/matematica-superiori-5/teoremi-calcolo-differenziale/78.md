If $0<u<v<\pi/2$, which estimate uses the mean value theorem correctly for $\sin v-\sin u$?

- $\sin v-\sin u>v-u$
- $(v-u)\cos u<\sin v-\sin u<(v-u)\cos v$
- $\sin v-\sin u=v-u$
- $(v-u)\cos v<\sin v-\sin u<(v-u)\cos u$

## Explanation

The increment is $(v-u)\cos c$ for some $u<c<v$. Cosine decreases strictly on this interval and $v-u$ is positive, giving both strict inequalities.
