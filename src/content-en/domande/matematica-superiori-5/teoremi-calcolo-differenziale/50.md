For $f(x)=x^3$ on $[0,3]$, at which point is the tangent parallel to the secant line through the endpoints of the graph?

- $3 \sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The secant line has slope $9$. By the mean value theorem, set $3x^2$ equal to this value and take the positive solution inside the interval. This gives $\sqrt{3}$.
