Apply Rolle's theorem to $f(x)=\left(x - 5\right) \left(x - 2\right) + 3$ on $[2,5]$. Which horizontal coordinate satisfies its conclusion?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The product is zero at both endpoints, so the function values agree. The derivative is $2x-7$ and is zero only once. This gives $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
