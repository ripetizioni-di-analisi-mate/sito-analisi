A function $f$ is differentiable on $\mathbb R$ and $f'(x)\to3$ as $x\to+\infty$. What does $f(x+2)-f(x)$ approach?

- $3$
- $6$
- $0$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

The mean value theorem writes the increment as $2f'(c_x)$, where $x<c_x<x+2$. Since $c_x$ also tends to infinity, $2f'(c_x)\to6$.
