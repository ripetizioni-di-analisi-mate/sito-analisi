The function $f(x)=\dfrac{\ln x}{x}$ changes monotonicity where the numerator of its derivative changes sign. How does it change and at which input?

- From decreasing to increasing at $x=0$.
- From decreasing to increasing at $x=e$.
- From increasing to decreasing at $x=1$.
- From increasing to decreasing at $x=e$.

## Explanation

The derivative is $(1-\ln x)/x^2$ for $x>0$. Its numerator is positive before $e$ and negative afterwards.
