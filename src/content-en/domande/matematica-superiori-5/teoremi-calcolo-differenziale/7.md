For $f(x)=x(x-6)$ on the interval $[0,6]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Rolle's theorem?

- $0$
- $6$
- $3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial and equals zero at both endpoints. The hypotheses of Rolle's theorem therefore hold. The derivative $2x-6$ is zero at $c=3$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
