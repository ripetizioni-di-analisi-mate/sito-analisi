For $f(x)=x(x-20)$ on the interval $[0,20]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Rolle's theorem?

- $0$
- $10$
- $20$
- $-10$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial and equals zero at both endpoints. The hypotheses of Rolle's theorem therefore hold. The derivative $2x-20$ is zero at $c=10$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
