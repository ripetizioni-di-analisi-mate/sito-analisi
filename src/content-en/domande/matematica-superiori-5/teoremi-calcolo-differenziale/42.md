On an interval, differentiable functions $f$ and $g$ have the same derivative. Their graphs share the point at $x=2$. What follows?

- They coincide only at $2$.
- They are both constant.
- They coincide throughout the interval.
- Their derivative must vanish at $2$.

## Explanation

The difference has zero derivative, so the mean value theorem makes it constant on the interval. As the difference at $2$ is zero, that constant is zero.
