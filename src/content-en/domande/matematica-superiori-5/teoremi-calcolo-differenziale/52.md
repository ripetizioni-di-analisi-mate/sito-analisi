A differentiable function $f$ satisfies $|f(x)-f(y)|\le3|x-y|$ for all $x,y$. What pointwise information follows for $f'$?

- $|f'(a)|\le3$ for every $a$.
- $f'(a)=3$ for every $a$.
- $f'(a)\ge0$ for every $a$.
- $f'$ must be constant.

## Explanation

Set $x=a+h$, $y=a$ and divide by $|h|$ for $h\ne0$. The absolute difference quotients are at most $3$, and the same bound holds in the limit.
