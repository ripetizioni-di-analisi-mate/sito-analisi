A function is twice differentiable on $[0,1]$, with $f(0)=f(1)$ and $f''(x)>0$ throughout. How many interior points satisfy $f'(x)=0$?

- None.
- Exactly two.
- Any positive number.
- Exactly one.

## Explanation

Rolle's theorem guarantees at least one zero of $f'$. Since $f''>0$, the derivative is strictly increasing and cannot vanish at two different points.
