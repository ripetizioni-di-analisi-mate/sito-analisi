The function $f$ is differentiable on the real line and $f'(x)=2$ for every $x$. If $f(0)=5$, what is $f(3)$?

- $10$
- $11$
- $6$
- $-1$

## Explanation

By the mean value theorem, the difference $f(3)-f(0)$ is the product of the constant derivative and the interval length $3$. This gives $11$.
