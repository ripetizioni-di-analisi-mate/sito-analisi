For $f(x)=x^2$ on the interval $[9,11]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem?

- $9$
- $11$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous on the closed interval and differentiable in its interior. The slope of the secant line is $\dfrac{(11)^2-9^2}{2}=20$. Since $f^\prime(c)=2c$, we obtain $c=10$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
