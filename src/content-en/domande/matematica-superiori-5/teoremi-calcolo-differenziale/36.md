A differentiable function is periodic with period $4$. Which statement holds for every real $a$?

- There is $c\in(a,a+4)$ with $f'(c)=0$.
- $f'(a)=0$ for every $a$.
- $f(a)=0$ for every $a$.
- The only derivative zero is $a+2$.

## Explanation

Periodicity gives $f(a)=f(a+4)$. Differentiability implies continuity, so Rolle's hypotheses hold on each interval of one period.
