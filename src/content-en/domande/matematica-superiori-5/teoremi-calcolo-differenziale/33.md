Let $f(x)=x$ for $0<x\le1$, and set $f(0)=1$. Which hypothesis of Rolle's theorem on $[0,1]$ fails?

- The equality $f(0)=f(1)$.
- Differentiability on $(0,1)$.
- Continuity at $0$.
- Continuity at $1$.

## Explanation

The endpoint values agree, but the right-hand limit at zero is $0$ whereas the assigned value is $1$. On the open interval the derivative is always $1$.
