What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0} \left(7 x + 1\right)^{\dfrac{4}{x}}$?

- $e^{4}$
- $e^{7}$
- $28$
- $e^{28}$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the power is the ratio of a logarithm tending to zero to the variable. L'Hôpital's rule gives the product of the two coefficients. The result is $e^{28}$.
