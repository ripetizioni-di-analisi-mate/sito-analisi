A differentiable function on $\mathbb R$ satisfies $f'(x)>1$ everywhere and $f(0)=0$. What is the sign of $f(x)-x$ for $x<0$?

- Always zero.
- Positive.
- Negative.
- Alternating on every interval.

## Explanation

The function $h(x)=f(x)-x$ has positive derivative and is strictly increasing. To the left of zero its values are therefore below $h(0)=0$.
