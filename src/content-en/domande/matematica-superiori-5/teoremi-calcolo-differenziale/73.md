The parametric curve $(x(t),y(t))=(t^3,t^2)$ runs from $t=-1$ to $t=1$. Where does Cauchy's equality $\Delta x\,y'(t)=\Delta y\,x'(t)$ hold, and what happens to $y'/x'$ there?

- At $t=1/2$ the ratio vanishes.
- At $t=0$ the equality holds and the ratio is undefined.
- The ratio is zero at all interior points.
- The undivided form holds at no interior point.

## Explanation

The increments are $\Delta x=2$ and $\Delta y=0$, so $2(2t)=0$ is required. This gives $t=0$, where $x'=3t^2$ also vanishes. The undivided equation is valid, while the ratio is $0/0$.
