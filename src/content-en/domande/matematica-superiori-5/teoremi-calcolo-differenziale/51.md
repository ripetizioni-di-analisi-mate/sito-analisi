A differentiable function satisfies $f(0)=0$ and $f'(x)\ge2$ for $x>0$. What behaviour is forced as $x\to+\infty$?

- $f(x)\to2$
- $f(x)\to+\infty$
- $f(x)/x\to2$ necessarily.
- $f(x)-2x\to0$ necessarily.

## Explanation

On $[0,x]$ the mean value theorem gives $f(x)=xf'(c)\ge2x$. This forces divergence, while faster growth remains possible, as for $f(x)=2x+x^2$.
