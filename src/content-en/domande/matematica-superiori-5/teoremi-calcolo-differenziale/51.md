For $f(x)=x^3$ on $[0,4]$, at which point is the tangent parallel to the secant line through the endpoints of the graph?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{4 \sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $4 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The secant line has slope $16$. By the mean value theorem, set $3x^2$ equal to this value and take the positive solution inside the interval. This gives $\dfrac{4 \sqrt{3}}{3}$.
