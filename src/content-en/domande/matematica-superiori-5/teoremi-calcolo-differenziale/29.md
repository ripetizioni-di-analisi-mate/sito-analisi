A function has a local minimum at an interior point $a$. Near zero, what are the signs of $[f(a+h)-f(a)]/h$ for $h<0$ and $h>0$, respectively?

- Both strictly positive.
- Nonpositive and nonnegative.
- Nonnegative and nonpositive.
- Both strictly negative.

## Explanation

The numerator is nonnegative on both sides, while the denominator changes sign. If the one-sided derivatives agree, zero is their only possible common limit.
