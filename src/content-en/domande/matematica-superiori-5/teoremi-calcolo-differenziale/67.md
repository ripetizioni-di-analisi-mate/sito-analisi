What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{- 2 x + e^{2 x} - 1}{x^{2}}$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator are still zero after one differentiation. After two differentiations the numerator tends to the square of the coefficient in the exponent and the denominator equals two. This gives $2$.
