What is $\lim_{x\to0^+}x^x$, even though both base and exponent tend to zero?

- $0$
- $1$
- $e$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the expression is $x\ln x$. Written as $(\ln x)/(1/x)$, the ratio of derivatives is $-x\to0$. Continuity of the exponential gives $e^0=1$.
