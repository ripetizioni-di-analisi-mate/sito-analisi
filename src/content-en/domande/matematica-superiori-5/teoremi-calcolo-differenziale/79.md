A differentiable graph starts at $A$, ends at $B$ and meets the nonvertical line joining them at an additional interior point $P$. How many tangents parallel to that line are guaranteed at distinct interior points? The three horizontal coordinates are distinct.

- Exactly three.
- Exactly one.
- At least two.
- None.

## Explanation

Subtract the equation of the line from the function. The difference vanishes at all three inputs and satisfies Rolle's hypotheses on both subintervals separated by $P$. This gives one tangent of the required slope inside each subinterval.
