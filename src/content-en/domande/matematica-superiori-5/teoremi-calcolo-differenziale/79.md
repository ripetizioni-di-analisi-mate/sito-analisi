What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0} \left(5 x + 1\right)^{\dfrac{3}{x}}$?

- $e^{5}$
- $15$
- $e^{15}$
- $e^{3}$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the power is the ratio of a logarithm tending to zero to the variable. L'Hôpital's rule gives the product of the two coefficients. The result is $e^{15}$.
