A student concludes from $\lim_{x\to+\infty}f'(x)=0$ that $f$ has a horizontal asymptote. Which function on $x>1$ disproves the conclusion?

- $f(x)=1/x$
- $f(x)=\ln x$
- $f(x)=e^{-x}$
- $f(x)=4$

## Explanation

The derivative of $\ln x$ is $1/x$ and tends to zero, while the logarithm grows without bound. Slopes approaching zero can still produce unbounded total change.
