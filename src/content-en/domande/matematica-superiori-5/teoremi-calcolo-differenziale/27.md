For $f(x)=x(x-14)$ on the interval $[0,14]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Rolle's theorem?

- $0$
- $14$
- $7$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial and equals zero at both endpoints. The hypotheses of Rolle's theorem therefore hold. The derivative $2x-14$ is zero at $c=7$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
