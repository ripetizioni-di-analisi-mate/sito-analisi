A twice differentiable function satisfies $|f''|\le4$ on $[0,1]$. We approximate $f(1/2)$ by the tangent at input zero, using $f(0)+f'(0)/2$. What is the smallest upper bound on the absolute error guaranteed by these data?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Set $R(x)=f(x)-f(0)-xf'(0)$. Both $2x^2+R(x)$ and $2x^2-R(x)$ have zero value and derivative at zero, and nonnegative second derivative. They are therefore nonnegative for $x\ge0$, giving $|R(x)|\le2x^2$. At $1/2$ this bounds the error by $1/2$, which is attained by $f(x)=2x^2$.
