The limit of $(\sin x-ax)/x^3$ as $x\to0$ is to be finite. What is the only admissible value of $a$?

- $a=-1$
- $a=0$
- $a=1$
- $a=3$

## Explanation

For $a\ne1$, dividing the numerator by $x$ gives a limit of $1-a\ne0$, while dividing the denominator by $x$ gives zero. With $a=1$ the linear term cancels and three successive differentiations give the finite limit $-1/6$.
