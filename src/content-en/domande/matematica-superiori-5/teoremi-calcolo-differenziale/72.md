What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{- 9 x + \ln{\left(9 x + 1 \right)}}{x^{2}}$?

- $-81$
- $\dfrac{81}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{81}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Apply l'Hôpital's rule twice. The second derivative of the logarithm is negative, and the second derivative of the denominator is two. This gives $- \dfrac{81}{2}$.
