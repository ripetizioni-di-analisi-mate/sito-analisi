Consider applying l'Hôpital's rule to $\dfrac{4+\sin x}{9+x}$ as $x\to0$. Which statement is correct?

- The quotient diverges because the denominator tends to zero.
- The quotient has the indeterminate form zero over zero.
- The quotient has the indeterminate form infinity over infinity.
- The quotient can be evaluated by direct substitution.

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator approach finite, nonzero values. By continuity, their quotient can be evaluated directly.
