What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to \infty} x^{\dfrac{3}{x}}$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $0$
- $e^{3}$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the power is proportional to $\ln(x)/x$. By l'Hôpital's rule this ratio tends to zero, so exponentiating gives the limit of the power. The result is $1$.
