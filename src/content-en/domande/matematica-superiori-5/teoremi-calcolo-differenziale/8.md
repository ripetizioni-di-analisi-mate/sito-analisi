For which values of $k$ is $e^x-kx$ strictly increasing on $[0,+\infty)$?

- $k<1$
- $k\le1$
- $k\ge1$
- $k\le0$

## Explanation

The derivative is $e^x-k$ and its minimum $1-k$ is attained at the origin. For $k=1$ it is positive at every interior point, so increase is still strict. For $k>1$ it is negative near zero.
