What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{e^{3x}-1}{x}$, which can be evaluated using l'Hôpital's rule?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

Both numerator and denominator tend to zero and are differentiable in a neighborhood of $0$. The derivative of the denominator is $1$, so it never vanishes. The quotient of the derivatives is $3e^{3x}$ and tends to $3$.
