Temperature $T$ is continuous for three hours. In the first hour $|T'|\le2$, and in the next two hours $|T'|\le0.5$, with derivatives in degrees per hour inside each segment. What is the sharpest bound these data give on $|T(3)-T(0)|$?

- $3$ degrees
- $6$ degrees
- $2$ degrees
- $1$ degree

## Explanation

The mean value theorem bounds the first change by $2\cdot1=2$ and the second by $0.5\cdot2=1$. The triangle inequality gives $3$, attained by steady increases at the two maximum rates.
