Apply Rolle's theorem to $f(x)=\left(x - 7\right) \left(x - 3\right) + 4$ on $[3,7]$. Which horizontal coordinate satisfies its conclusion?

- $3$
- $7$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

The product is zero at both endpoints, so the function values agree. The derivative is $2x-10$ and is zero only once. This gives $5$.
