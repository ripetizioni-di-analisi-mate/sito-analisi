The same curve is traversed using a new parameter $u$, where $t=2u+1$. Why does $(dy/du)/(dx/du)$ still equal $(dy/dt)/(dx/dt)$ wherever the denominator is nonzero?

- One derivative doubles and the other halves.
- Both derivatives stay individually unchanged.
- Both derivatives acquire the same factor $2$.
- Both parameters always have the same value.

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $dy/du=(dy/dt)\,2$ and $dx/du=(dx/dt)\,2$. The common factor cancels, leaving the tangent slope unchanged.
