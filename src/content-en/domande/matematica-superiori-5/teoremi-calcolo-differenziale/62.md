Apply Cauchy's mean value theorem to $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x$ on $[0,7]$. Which horizontal coordinate satisfies its conclusion?

- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$

## Explanation

The ratio of the changes in function values is $7$. Since $f'(x)/g'(x)=2x$, set the two quantities equal. This gives $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
