For $L=\lim_{x\to0}(\cos(2x))^{1/x^2}$, what is the limit of the logarithm of the expression, before returning to the power?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $e^{-2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Near zero the cosine is positive. The logarithm is $\ln(\cos(2x))/x^2$. L'Hôpital's rule turns it into $-\tan(2x)/x$, which tends to $-2$. Thus the power tends to $e^{-2}$.
