The function $f$ is differentiable on the real line and $f'(x)=3$ for every $x$. If $f(0)=7$, what is $f(4)$?

- $-5$
- $14$
- $19$
- $12$

## Explanation

By the mean value theorem, the difference $f(4)-f(0)$ is the product of the constant derivative and the interval length $4$. This gives $19$.
