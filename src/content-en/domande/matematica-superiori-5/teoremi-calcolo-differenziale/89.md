On an interval $f'(x)/g'(x)=2$, with $g'$ nowhere zero, and $f(1)=3$, $g(1)=4$. Which relation must the whole parametric graph $(g(x),f(x))$ satisfy?

- $f(x)=2g(x)-5$
- $f(x)=2g(x)$
- $f(x)=g(x)+2$
- $g(x)=2f(x)-5$

## Explanation

The function $f-2g$ has zero derivative and is therefore constant on the interval. At $1$ it equals $3-2\cdot4=-5$, giving the line containing the graph.
