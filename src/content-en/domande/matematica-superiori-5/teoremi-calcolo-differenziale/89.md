A differentiable function satisfies $|f'(x)|\le3$ for $1\le x\le4$ and $f(1)=5$. Which proposed reading for $f(4)$ would prove that at least one datum is wrong?

- $15$
- $14$
- $5$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The mean value theorem gives $|f(4)-5|\le3(4-1)=9$. The value $15$ is ten from five, whereas the other readings can occur on lines with allowed slopes.
