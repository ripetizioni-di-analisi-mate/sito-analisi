For $f(x)=x(x-8)$ on the interval $[0,8]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Rolle's theorem?

- $0$
- $8$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial and equals zero at both endpoints. The hypotheses of Rolle's theorem therefore hold. The derivative $2x-8$ is zero at $c=4$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
