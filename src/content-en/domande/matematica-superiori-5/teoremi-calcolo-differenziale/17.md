For $f(x)=x(x-10)$ on the interval $[0,10]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Rolle's theorem?

- $5$
- $0$
- $10$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial and equals zero at both endpoints. The hypotheses of Rolle's theorem therefore hold. The derivative $2x-10$ is zero at $c=5$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
