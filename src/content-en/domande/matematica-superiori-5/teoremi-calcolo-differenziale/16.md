For $f(x)=x^2$ on the interval $[5,7]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem?

- $5$
- $7$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous on the closed interval and differentiable in its interior. The slope of the secant line is $\dfrac{(7)^2-5^2}{2}=12$. Since $f^\prime(c)=2c$, we obtain $c=6$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
