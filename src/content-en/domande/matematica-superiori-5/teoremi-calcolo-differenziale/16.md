An algorithm computes $x_{n+1}=f(x_n)$ within an interval where $f$ is differentiable and $|f'|\le1/2$. A fixed point $p$ lies in the same interval, and $|x_0-p|\le8$. What is the smallest guaranteed upper bound on $|x_3-p|$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The mean value theorem gives $|f(x)-f(p)|\le|x-p|/2$. Since $f(p)=p$, the error bound halves at each step, giving $8,4,2,1$. Equality is attained, for example, by $f(x)=x/2$, $p=0$ and $x_0=8$.
