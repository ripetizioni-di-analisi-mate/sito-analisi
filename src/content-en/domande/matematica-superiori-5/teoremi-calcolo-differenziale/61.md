Apply Cauchy's mean value theorem to $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x$ on $[0,5]$. Which horizontal coordinate satisfies its conclusion?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

The ratio of the changes in function values is $5$. Since $f'(x)/g'(x)=2x$, set the two quantities equal. This gives $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
