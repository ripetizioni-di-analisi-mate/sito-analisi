As $x\to+\infty$, differentiable functions $f,g$ tend to zero. Eventually $g<0$, $g'\ne0$ and $f'/g'\to-2$. What sign must $f$ have for sufficiently large $x$?

- Negative.
- Positive.
- It must be zero.
- It must change sign infinitely often.

## Explanation

The quotient has the form $0/0$, so by l'Hôpital's rule $f/g\to-2$. The quotient is eventually negative, as is $g$. Their product $(f/g)g=f$ is therefore positive.
