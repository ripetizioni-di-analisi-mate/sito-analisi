A differentiable function is constant and equal to $7$ for $1<x<3$. How is $x=2$ classified when local extrema are understood in the non-strict sense?

- It is both a local maximum and a local minimum.
- It is only a strict local maximum.
- It is only a strict local minimum.
- It is not a local extremum.

## Explanation

All values in a neighbourhood of $2$ equal $f(2)$. Both the inequality defining a local maximum and the one defining a local minimum therefore hold.
