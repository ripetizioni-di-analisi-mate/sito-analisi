For $f(x)=x(x-12)$ on the interval $[0,12]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Rolle's theorem?

- $0$
- $6$
- $12$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial and equals zero at both endpoints. The hypotheses of Rolle's theorem therefore hold. The derivative $2x-12$ is zero at $c=6$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
