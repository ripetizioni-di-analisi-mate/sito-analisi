Let $f(x)=(x-4)^3+9$. What happens at the stationary point with horizontal coordinate $4$?

- It is an inflection point with a horizontal tangent.
- It is a local maximum.
- It is a local minimum.
- It is a corner.

## Explanation

The derivative $3(x-4)^2$ is positive on both sides. The function increases through the stationary point, so it has no extremum there.
