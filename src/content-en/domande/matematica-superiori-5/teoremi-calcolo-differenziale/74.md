What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to \infty} \dfrac{\ln{\left(x^{4} + 1 \right)}}{\ln{\left(x \right)}}$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The quotient of the derivatives simplifies to a constant times the ratio of the power to one plus the same power. The second factor tends to one. This gives $4$.
