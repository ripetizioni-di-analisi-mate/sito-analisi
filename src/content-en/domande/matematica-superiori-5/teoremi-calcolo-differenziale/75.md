What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to \infty} \dfrac{\ln{\left(x^{5} + 1 \right)}}{\ln{\left(x \right)}}$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

The quotient of the derivatives simplifies to a constant times the ratio of the power to one plus the same power. The second factor tends to one. This gives $5$.
