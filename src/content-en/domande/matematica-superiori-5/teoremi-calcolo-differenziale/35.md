A fourth-degree polynomial has two distinct roots $a<b$, both of multiplicity two. How many distinct real roots does its derivative have?

- Four.
- One.
- Two.
- Three.

## Explanation

The double roots already give two derivative zeros, at $a$ and $b$. Rolle's theorem supplies a third strictly between them. A cubic derivative cannot have another.
