Apply Rolle's theorem to $f(x)=\sqrt{x(1-x)}$ on $[0,1]$. Do the vertical endpoint tangents prevent its application?

- Yes, because differentiability is required at both endpoints.
- No. The guaranteed point is $c=1/2$.
- Yes, because the function is zero at the endpoints.
- No. The only guaranteed point is $c=0$.

## Explanation

The function is continuous on the closed interval and differentiable throughout its interior. Inside, $f'(x)=(1-2x)/(2\sqrt{x(1-x)})$, which vanishes only at the midpoint.
