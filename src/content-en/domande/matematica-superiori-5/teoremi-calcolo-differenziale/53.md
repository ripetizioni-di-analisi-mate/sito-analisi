A differentiable function $f$ has at most one solution of $f'(x)=2$. How many distinct points can its graph share with the line $y=2x+7$?

- Exactly three.
- At most one in every case.
- At most two.
- Infinitely many in every case.

## Explanation

Three intersections would give three zeros of $f(x)-2x-7$. Rolle's theorem on the two intervening intervals would yield two distinct points where $f'=2$. Two intersections are possible, as with $f(x)=2x+7+x^2-1$.
