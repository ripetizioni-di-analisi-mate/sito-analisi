Consider $f(x)=|x-3|$ on $[-1,7]$. Which hypothesis of Rolle's theorem fails?

- Differentiability on the open interval.
- Continuity on the closed interval.
- Equality of the endpoint values.
- Continuity at the right endpoint.

## Explanation

Both endpoints are a distance $4$ from the vertex along the horizontal axis. At $x=3$ the one-sided derivatives are $-1$ and $1$, so the graph has a corner.
