Let $F(x)=\ln x$ for $x>0$. If $c$ is its mean value point on $[a,b]$, what expression for $c$ follows when $0<a<b$?

- $c=\dfrac{a+b}{2}$ for every choice of endpoints.
- $c=\dfrac{b-a}{\ln b-\ln a}$
- $c=\dfrac{\ln b-\ln a}{b-a}$
- $c=ab$

## Explanation

The tangent slope at the required point is $1/c$. Equating this with the secant slope and taking reciprocals gives the logarithmic mean of the endpoints, strictly between them.
