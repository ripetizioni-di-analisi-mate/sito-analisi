To prove the mean value theorem from Rolle's theorem, set $h(x)=f(x)-mx$. Which choice guarantees $h(a)=h(b)$ for $a<b$?

- $m=\dfrac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$
- $m=\dfrac{f(a)+f(b)}{2}$
- $m=f'(a)$ always.
- $m=b-a$

## Explanation

The endpoint condition is equivalent to $f(b)-f(a)=m(b-a)$. With this choice the zero of $h'=f'-m$ given by Rolle's theorem is the mean value point.
