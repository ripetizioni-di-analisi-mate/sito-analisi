For $f(x)=x^2$ on the interval $[10,12]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem?

- $11$
- $10$
- $12$
- $22$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous on the closed interval and differentiable in its interior. The slope of the secant line is $\dfrac{(12)^2-10^2}{2}=22$. Since $f^\prime(c)=2c$, we obtain $c=11$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
