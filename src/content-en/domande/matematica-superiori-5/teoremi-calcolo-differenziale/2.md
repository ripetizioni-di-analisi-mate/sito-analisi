What are the vertical coordinates of the two points with horizontal tangents found by applying Rolle's theorem to $f(x)=\sin(2x)$ on $[0,\pi]$?

- $0,0$
- $1,-1$
- $1,1$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The endpoints have the same height. The condition $2\cos(2x)=0$ yields $\pi/4$ and $3\pi/4$ as the horizontal coordinates. Evaluating the sine at those points gives opposite vertical coordinates.
