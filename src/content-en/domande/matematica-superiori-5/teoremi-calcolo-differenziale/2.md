For $f(x)=x(x-4)$ on the interval $[0,4]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Rolle's theorem?

- $0$
- $2$
- $4$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial and equals zero at both endpoints. The hypotheses of Rolle's theorem therefore hold. The derivative $2x-4$ is zero at $c=2$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
