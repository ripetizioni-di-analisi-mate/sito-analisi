What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0} - \dfrac{2}{e^{2 x} - 1} + \dfrac{1}{x}$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $2$

## Explanation

After the fractions are put over a common denominator, the numerator and the denominator both have zeros of order two. Their second derivatives at zero are respectively the square of the coefficient in the exponent and twice that coefficient. This gives $1$.
