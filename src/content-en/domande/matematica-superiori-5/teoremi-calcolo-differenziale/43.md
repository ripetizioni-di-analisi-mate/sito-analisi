A differentiable function has a root $r$ in $[1,3]$ and satisfies $f'(x)\ge4$ throughout that interval. If $f(2)=0.12$, how far from $r$ can $2$ be at most?

- $4$ as the sharpest bound.
- $0.12$
- $0.48$
- $0.03$

## Explanation

By the mean value theorem, $|f(2)-f(r)|=|f'(c)|\,|2-r|\ge4|2-r|$. Dividing the residual $0.12$ by four gives $0.03$.
