A function $f$ has a continuous derivative on $[0,1]$, and every value of $f'$ is an integer. If $f(1)-f(0)=2$, what is $f'(1/3)$?

- $2$
- $\dfrac23$
- $0$
- Any integer.

## Explanation

The mean value theorem gives a point where $f'$ equals the mean slope $2$. A continuous derivative taking only integer values is constant: between two different integers it would also take noninteger values.
