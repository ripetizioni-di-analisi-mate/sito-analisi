Consider $f(x)=x^3$ and $g(x)=x$ on $[-1,1]$. How many interior points actually satisfy Cauchy's equation $f'(c)/g'(c)=\Delta f/\Delta g$?

- No point.
- Exactly one, $c=0$.
- Every point in the interval.
- Two, $c=\pm1/\sqrt3$.

## Explanation

The ratio of increments is $2/2=1$. The equation $3c^2=1$ gives two admissible solutions. The theorem's existence statement allows more than one point.
