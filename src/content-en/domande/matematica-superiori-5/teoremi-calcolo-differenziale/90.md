The function $f$ is differentiable on the real line and $f'(x)=4$ for every $x$. If $f(0)=9$, what is $f(5)$?

- $29$
- $-11$
- $20$
- $18$

## Explanation

By the mean value theorem, the difference $f(5)-f(0)$ is the product of the constant derivative and the interval length $5$. This gives $29$.
