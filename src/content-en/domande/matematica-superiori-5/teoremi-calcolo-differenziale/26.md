For $f(x)=x^2$ on the interval $[7,9]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem?

- $7$
- $8$
- $9$
- $16$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous on the closed interval and differentiable in its interior. The slope of the secant line is $\dfrac{(9)^2-7^2}{2}=16$. Since $f^\prime(c)=2c$, we obtain $c=8$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
