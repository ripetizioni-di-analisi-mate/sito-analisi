A differentiable function on $(0,1]$ satisfies $|f'(x)|\le5$ and $f(1)=0$. Can it have a vertical asymptote at zero?

- No, because $|f(x)|\le5(1-x)$ on the interval.
- Yes, because zero is outside the domain.
- Yes, because $f'$ might have no limit.
- No, because $f$ must be constant.

## Explanation

For $0<x<1$, the mean value theorem on $[x,1]$ gives $|f(x)-f(1)|\le5(1-x)$. Thus $f$ stays bounded in magnitude as $x$ approaches zero.
