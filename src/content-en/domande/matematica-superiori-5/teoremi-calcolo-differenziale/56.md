On $[0,7]$ we have $|f'(x)|\leq 3$, and $f$ is continuous on the closed interval and differentiable in its interior. What upper bound for $|f(7)-f(0)|$ follows from the mean value theorem?

- $10$
- $4$
- $21$
- $147$

## Explanation

The mean value theorem expresses the change in the function as the interval length times the derivative at an interior point. Applying the given bound on the absolute value of the derivative gives $21$.
