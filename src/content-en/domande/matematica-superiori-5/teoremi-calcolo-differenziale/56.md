A curve is given by $(x(t),y(t))=(\cos t,\sin t)$ for $0\le t\le\pi/2$. At what parameter value is the tangent parallel to the chord joining the endpoints?

- $t=\pi/4$
- $t=0$
- $t=\pi/6$
- $t=\pi/2$

## Explanation

The chord from $(1,0)$ to $(0,1)$ has slope $-1$. The instantaneous slope is $y'/x'=-\cos t/\sin t$, equal to $-1$ where sine and cosine agree.
