Two differentiable graphs $y=f(x)$ and $y=g(x)$ meet at $x=1$ and $x=5$. What property must they have at some intermediate input?

- Both tangents are horizontal.
- Both outputs are zero.
- Their tangents are parallel.
- The two curves meet a third time.

## Explanation

The difference $h=f-g$ vanishes at both endpoints. Rolle's theorem yields an interior point where $h'=f'-g'=0$, so the two slopes agree.
