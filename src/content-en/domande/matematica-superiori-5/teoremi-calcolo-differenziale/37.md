For $f(x)=x(x-18)$ on the interval $[0,18]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of Rolle's theorem?

- $9$
- $0$
- $18$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The function is a polynomial and equals zero at both endpoints. The hypotheses of Rolle's theorem therefore hold. The derivative $2x-18$ is zero at $c=9$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
