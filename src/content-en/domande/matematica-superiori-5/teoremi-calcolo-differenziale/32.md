For $x\ne0$, let $f(x)=x^2(2+\sin(1/x))$ and $f(0)=0$. Which observation directly verifies Fermat's conclusion at zero?

- The quotient $f(h)/h$ is positive for either sign of $h$.
- $\sin(1/h)$ tends to zero.
- $f$ is constant near zero.
- $|f(h)/h|\le3|h|$ for $h\ne0$.

## Explanation

The factor $2+\sin(1/h)$ lies between $1$ and $3$, so zero is a minimum and the absolute difference quotient is at most $3|h|$. This upper bound tends to zero.
