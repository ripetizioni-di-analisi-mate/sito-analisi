For $f(x)=x^2$ on the interval $[6,8]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem?

- $7$
- $6$
- $8$
- $14$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous on the closed interval and differentiable in its interior. The slope of the secant line is $\dfrac{(8)^2-6^2}{2}=14$. Since $f^\prime(c)=2c$, we obtain $c=7$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
