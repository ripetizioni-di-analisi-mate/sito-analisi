Apply Rolle's theorem to $f(x)=\left(x - 9\right) \left(x - 4\right) + 5$ on $[4,9]$. Which horizontal coordinate satisfies its conclusion?

- $9$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$

## Explanation

The product is zero at both endpoints, so the function values agree. The derivative is $2x-13$ and is zero only once. This gives $\dfrac{13}{2}$.
