The paths $f(t)=t^2$ and $g(t)=t^3$ both have mean velocity $1$ on $[0,1]$. At what time do they both simultaneously have instantaneous velocity $1$?

- $t=1$
- $t=1/2$
- $t=1/\sqrt3$
- At no instant.

## Explanation

For the first path $2t=1$ requires $t=1/2$, while for the second $3t^2=1$ requires $t=1/\sqrt3$. The mean value theorem supplies a time for each path but does not require the two times to coincide.
