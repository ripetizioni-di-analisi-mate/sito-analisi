Let $f(x)=(x-3)^3+7$. What happens at the stationary point with horizontal coordinate $3$?

- It is a local minimum.
- It is a corner.
- It is a local maximum.
- It is an inflection point with a horizontal tangent.

## Explanation

The derivative $3(x-3)^2$ is positive on both sides. The function increases through the stationary point, so it has no extremum there.
