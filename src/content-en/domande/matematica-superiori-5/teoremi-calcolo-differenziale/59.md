Two differentiable quantities $f(t)$ and $g(t)$ satisfy $g'(t)<0$ and $2\le f'(t)/g'(t)\le3$ throughout an interval. If $g$ changes from $9$ to $5$, what are the bounds on the change $\Delta f$?

- $8\le\Delta f\le12$
- $-12\le\Delta f\le-8$
- $-3\le\Delta f\le-2$
- $-8\le\Delta f\le0$

## Explanation

Cauchy's theorem puts $\Delta f/\Delta g$ between $2$ and $3$. As $\Delta g=-4$, multiplying by $-4$ reverses the inequalities.
