Suppose $f''(x)=2$ on $[0,2]$, and let $L$ be the line joining the graph's endpoints. What is the maximum of $L(x)-f(x)$ there?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The second derivative of $f-x^2$ vanishes, so $f(x)=x^2+ax+b$. The chord is $L(x)=(2+a)x+b$. The difference is $2x-x^2=1-(x-1)^2$, with maximum $1$.
