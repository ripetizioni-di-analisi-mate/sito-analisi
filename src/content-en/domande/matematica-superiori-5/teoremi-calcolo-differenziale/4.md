Consider the function $f(x)=|x-2|$ on $[1,3]$. Why does Rolle's theorem not apply?

- The function is not continuous on the interval
- The endpoint values are different
- The interval is open
- The function is not differentiable at $x=2$

## Explanation

The function is continuous and equals $1$ at both endpoints. At $x=2$ the one-sided derivatives are $-1$ and $1$, so the derivative does not exist. The hypothesis of differentiability in the interior therefore fails.
