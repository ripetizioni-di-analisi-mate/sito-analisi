For $f(x)=x^3-x$ on $[0,2]$, which equation sets an interior tangent's slope equal to the slope of the endpoint secant?

- $3c^2-1=0$
- $3c^2-1=6$
- $c^3-c=3$
- $3c^2-1=3$

## Explanation

The secant's slope is $(f(2)-f(0))/(2-0)=6/2=3$. The mean value theorem equates it to $f'(c)=3c^2-1$. The interior point is $c=2/\sqrt3$.
