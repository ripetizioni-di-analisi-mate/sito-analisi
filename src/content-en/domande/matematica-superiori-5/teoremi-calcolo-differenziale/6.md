A function equals $x^2+a$ on $[0,1]$ and $bx-1$ on $(1,3]$. We choose $a=0$ and $b=2$ to obtain a differentiable join. Which branch contains the mean value point for $[0,3]$?

- The linear branch, at $c=5/2$.
- The quadratic branch, at $c=1/3$.
- The quadratic branch, at $c=5/6$.
- The joining point, $c=1$.

## Explanation

The average slope is $(5-0)/3=5/3$. The linear branch has constant slope two. In the quadratic branch $2c=5/3$ gives the required interior point.
