For $f(x)=x^2$ on the interval $[3,5]$, which point $c$ satisfies the conclusion of the mean value theorem?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous on the closed interval and differentiable in its interior. The slope of the secant line is $\dfrac{(5)^2-3^2}{2}=8$. Since $f^\prime(c)=2c$, we obtain $c=4$, which lies in the interior of the interval.
