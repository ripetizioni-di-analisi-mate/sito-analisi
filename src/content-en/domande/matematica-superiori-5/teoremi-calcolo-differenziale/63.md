Apply Cauchy's mean value theorem to $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x$ on $[0,9]$. Which horizontal coordinate satisfies its conclusion?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $18$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

The ratio of the changes in function values is $9$. Since $f'(x)/g'(x)=2x$, set the two quantities equal. This gives $\dfrac{9}{2}$.
