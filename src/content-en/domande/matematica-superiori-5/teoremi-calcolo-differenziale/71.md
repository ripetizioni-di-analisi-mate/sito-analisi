What is the value of $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{- 7 x + \ln{\left(7 x + 1 \right)}}{x^{2}}$?

- $-49$
- $- \dfrac{49}{2}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{49}{2}$

## Explanation

Apply l'Hôpital's rule twice. The second derivative of the logarithm is negative, and the second derivative of the denominator is two. This gives $- \dfrac{49}{2}$.
