Differentiable functions $f$ and $g$ satisfy $f(2)=g(2)=0$, $f'(2)=6$ and $g'(2)=-3$. What is $\lim_{x\to2} f(x)/g(x)$ even without knowing whether $f'$ and $g'$ are continuous near $2$?

- The limit must be infinite.
- $-1/2$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Divide each function by $x-2$. By the definition of the derivative the two quotients approach $6$ and $-3$. The second limit is nonzero, so their ratio has the stated limit.
