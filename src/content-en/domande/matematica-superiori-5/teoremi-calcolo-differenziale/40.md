A vehicle travels $90$ km in $2$ hours. Its position is continuous and differentiable throughout the trip. What does the mean value theorem imply about its speed along the route?

- Its speed is $45$ km/h at every instant.
- Its speed is zero at least once between the start and finish.
- Its speed is $90$ km/h at least once between the start and finish.
- Its speed is $45$ km/h at least once between the start and finish.

## Explanation

The slope of the secant line is the distance traveled divided by the elapsed time. The mean value theorem guarantees an instant between the start and finish at which the speed equals the average speed.
