A function is continuous on $[-1,1]$ and has positive derivative on both $(-1,0)$ and $(0,1)$. What can be said about $f(-1)$ and $f(1)$ without knowing $f'(0)$?

- $f(-1)<f(1)$
- $f(-1)=f(1)$
- $f(-1)>f(1)$
- The order could be reversed by a jump at $0$.

## Explanation

Applying the mean value theorem on $[-1,0]$ gives $f(-1)<f(0)$, and applying it on $[0,1]$ gives $f(0)<f(1)$. Continuity at the junction suffices for this comparison.
