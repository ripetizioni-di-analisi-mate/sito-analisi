The cubic $f(x)=x^3-4x$ satisfies Rolle's hypotheses on $[-2,2]$. How many interior points satisfy the conclusion and what are they?

- Two, $c=\pm\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}$.
- One, $c=0$.
- Two, $c=\pm2$.
- One, $c=\sqrt3$.

## Explanation

The polynomial is zero at both endpoints. Solving $f'(c)=3c^2-4=0$ gives two interior points. Rolle's theorem guarantees at least one.
