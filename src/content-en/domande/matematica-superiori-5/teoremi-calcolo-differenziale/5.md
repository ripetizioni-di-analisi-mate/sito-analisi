On the graph of $f(x)=1/x$, which input in $(1,3)$ gives a tangent parallel to the chord between the endpoints?

- $\sqrt3$
- $2$
- $\dfrac13$
- $3$

## Explanation

The chord has slope $(1/3-1)/(3-1)=-1/3$. Setting $-1/c^2=-1/3$ gives the unique positive interior root $c=\sqrt3$.
