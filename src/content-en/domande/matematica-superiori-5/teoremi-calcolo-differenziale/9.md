On $x<1$ the derivative of $F(x)=\arctan\!\left(\dfrac{1+x}{1-x}\right)-\arctan x$ is zero. What is $F(-20)$ without evaluating two arctangents?

- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

A zero derivative makes the function constant on this interval. Evaluation at the origin gives $F(0)=\arctan 1-\arctan 0=\pi/4$.
