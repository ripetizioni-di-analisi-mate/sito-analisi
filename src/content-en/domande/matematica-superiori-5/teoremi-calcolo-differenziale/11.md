For $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x^3$ on $[0,1]$, Cauchy's mean value theorem equates the ratio of increments to the ratio of derivatives. Which interior point gives equality?

- $c=\dfrac32$
- $c=\dfrac12$
- $c=\dfrac23$
- $c=0$

## Explanation

The ratio of increments is one. In the open interval $f'(c)/g'(c)=2/(3c)$ and setting this equal to one gives $c=2/3$.
