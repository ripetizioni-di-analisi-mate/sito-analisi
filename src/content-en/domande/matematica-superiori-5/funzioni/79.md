Two functions $f$ and $g$ take only positive values. Each has range $[1,2]$. If they have the same domain, which conclusion about $h(x)=f(x)+g(x)$ always holds?

- The range of $h$ is contained in $[2,4]$.
- $h$ definitely takes the value $2$.
- $h$ definitely takes the value $4$.
- $h$ definitely takes every value between $2$ and $4$.

## Explanation

Adding the bounds on the two terms gives $2\le h(x)\le4$. Their minima need not occur at the same point. For example, on $[1,2]$ the functions $f(x)=x$ and $g(x)=3-x$ give $h(x)=3$ everywhere.
