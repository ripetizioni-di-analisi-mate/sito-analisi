On which of the following intervals is the function $f(x)=|x-3|$ injective?

- $[2,\infty)$
- $[3,\infty)$
- $(1,4)$
- $(2,5)$

## Explanation

On the right-hand branch we have $f(x)=x-3$, which is strictly increasing. Each of the other intervals contains a pair of points symmetric about $3$.
