The graph of $f$ intersects the line $y=3$ at two points and the line $y=-3$ at three points. How many solutions does $|f(x)|=3$ have?

- $1$
- $6$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The equation includes the solutions of both $f(x)=3$ and $f(x)=-3$. The same input cannot produce both outputs, so the two counts add.
