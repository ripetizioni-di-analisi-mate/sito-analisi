Consider $f(x)=\dfrac{x^3-4x}{x^2+9}$. Which property does it have?

- Its least positive period is $2\pi$.
- Its domain is $(0,+\infty)$.
- It is odd.
- It is even.

## Explanation

The denominator is positive on the real numbers and is even. The numerator is odd, so substituting $-x$ changes the sign of the fraction.
