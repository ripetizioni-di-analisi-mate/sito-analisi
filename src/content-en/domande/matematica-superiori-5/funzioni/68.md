For $f(x)=2x+1$ and $g(x)=4-x$, which expression gives the larger of the two values for every $x$, without splitting the answer into intervals?

- $\dfrac{x+5-|3x-3|}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3x-3+|x+5|}{2}$
- $\dfrac{|3x-3|}{2}$
- $\dfrac{x+5+|3x-3|}{2}$

## Explanation

For two numbers $a,b$, their average plus half their distance is the larger number, so $\max(a,b)=(a+b+|a-b|)/2$. Here their sum is $x+5$ and their difference is $3x-3$. A minus sign before the absolute value would give the smaller number.
