What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{\dfrac{x - 4}{x + 9}}$?

- $(-9,4]$
- $[4,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-9)$ ∪ $[4,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-9]$ ∪ $[4,\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative when the numerator and denominator have the same sign or when the numerator is zero. The value $-9$ makes the denominator zero.
