The demand and supply of a good are $q_d(p) = 60 - 3p$ and $q_s(p) = 2p - 8$. For which prices are the quantity demanded and the quantity supplied both nonnegative?

- $[4, 20]$
- $(4, 20)$
- $[0, 20]$
- $[4, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The two quantities must be nonnegative together, so the price must satisfy the system
$$
\begin{cases}
60 - 3p \geq 0 \\[4pt]
2p - 8 \geq 0
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
p \leq 20 \\[4pt]
p \geq 4
\end{cases}
$$
Therefore the required prices form the **intersection** $[4, 20]$. The endpoints are included, because $q_s(4) = 0$ and $q_d(20) = 0$, and the value $0$ is nonnegative.
