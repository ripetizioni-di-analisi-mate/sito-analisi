The floor $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. On which interval does $\lfloor 2x\rfloor=-1$?

- $(-\tfrac12,0]$
- $(-1,0]$
- $[-\tfrac12,0)$
- $[-1,-\tfrac12)$

## Explanation

The definition gives $-1\le2x<0$. Dividing by two keeps the left endpoint included and gives $-\tfrac12\le x<0$.
