What is the maximum of $f(x)=|x-1|$ on $[-1,4]$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

Absolute value measures distance from $1$. To the left, the farthest point is $-1$ at distance $2$. To the right, the farthest point is $4$ at distance $3$, so the maximum is $3$. The point $1$ is inside the interval and gives distance zero, so it identifies the minimum. The maximum comes from the farther endpoint. Both are allowed, and comparing $3>2$ selects the input $4$.
