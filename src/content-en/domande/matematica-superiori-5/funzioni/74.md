A stone is thrown upwards from the ground. Its height in metres is $h(t)=20t-5t^2$, with $t$ in seconds, from the instant of the throw until the stone falls back to the ground. What is the domain of the function $h$ as a model of this motion?

- $[0,4]$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $[0,2]$
- $[0,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The formula $20t-5t^2$ can be evaluated for every real number, whereas the model describes the motion only from the throw until the return to the ground. The stone is on the ground when $h(t)=0$, that is, $5t(4-t)=0$, so at the instants $t=0$ and $t=4$. Therefore the domain of the model is the interval $[0,4]$, in seconds. The instant $t=2$ is that of maximum height, so the interval $[0,2]$ describes only the ascent.
