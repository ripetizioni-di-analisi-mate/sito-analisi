For $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ and $g(x)=x^2-9$, the composition $g(f(x))$ simplifies to $x-9$. What is its actual domain?

- $\mathbb R$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-3]\cup[3,+\infty)$
- $[9,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Before simplifying, we must evaluate $\sqrt{x}$, which requires $x\ge0$. The outer polynomial accepts every resulting value.
