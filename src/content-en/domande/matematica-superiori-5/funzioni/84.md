The graph of $f$ contains $P=(4,9)$. What is the vertical coordinate of the corresponding point on $g(x)=-5f(x-1)$?

- $-45$
- $-20$
- $4$
- $45$

## Explanation

The point on the new graph has horizontal coordinate $5$. Its vertical coordinate is the value of $f$ at $4$ multiplied by $-5$. This gives $-45$.
