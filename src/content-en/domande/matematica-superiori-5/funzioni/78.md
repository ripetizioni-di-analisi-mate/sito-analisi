What is the range of the function $f(x)=9 - 4 e^{x}$ on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $(-\infty,9)$
- $(9,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,9]$
- $(-4,9)$

## Explanation

The exponential function takes all positive values and only positive values. Multiplying by $-4$ and adding $9$ gives all values less than $9$.
