The graph of $f(x)=\dfrac{x - 3}{x + 7}$ meets the $y$-axis. What is the vertical coordinate of the point of intersection?

- $- \dfrac{7}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{7}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

On the $y$-axis, $x=0$. Substituting zero into the fraction gives $- \dfrac{3}{7}$.
