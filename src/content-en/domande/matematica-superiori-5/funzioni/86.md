On $\mathbb R$, let $f(x)=\min(x^2,4)$. What is the preimage of the single value $4$?

- $(-\infty,-2]\cup[2,+\infty)$
- $\{-2,2\}$
- $[-2,2]$
- $(-2,2)$

## Explanation

The smaller of $x^2$ and $4$ equals $4$ whenever $x^2\ge4$. Thus the graph has two horizontal portions, one for $x\le-2$ and one for $x\ge2$.
