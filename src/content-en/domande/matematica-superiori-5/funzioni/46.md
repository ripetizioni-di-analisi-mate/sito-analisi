What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 5}}$?

- $(-\infty,-5]$ ∪ $[2,\infty)$
- $(-5,2]$
- $[2,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5)$ ∪ $[2,\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative when the numerator and denominator have the same sign or when the numerator is zero. The value $-5$ makes the denominator zero.
