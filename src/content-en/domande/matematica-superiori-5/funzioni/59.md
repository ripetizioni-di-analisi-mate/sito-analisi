A car can be rented with two daily rates. Rate A costs $40$ euros plus $0.25$ euros per kilometer, rate B costs $20$ euros plus $0.50$ euros per kilometer. For which daily distances does rate A cost less than rate B?

- More than $80$ km
- More than $40$ km
- More than $160$ km
- Less than $80$ km

## Explanation

With $x$ kilometers driven, the two rates are the linear functions $A(x) = 40 + 0.25\,x$ and $B(x) = 20 + 0.50\,x$. Rate A costs less when $40 + 0.25\,x < 20 + 0.50\,x$, that is, when $20 < 0.25\,x$. Dividing by $0.25$ gives $x > 80$. Hence rate A is cheaper beyond $80$ km, because its slope is smaller and the $20$-euro difference in the fixed fee is recovered after $80$ km. For $x = 80$ both rates cost $60$ euros.
