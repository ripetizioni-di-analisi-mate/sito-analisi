The graph of $f(x)=e^{x - 3} - 7$ meets the $x$-axis at one point. What is its horizontal coordinate?

- $\ln{\left(7 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(3 \right)} + 7$
- $3 - \ln{\left(7 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(7 \right)} + 3$

## Explanation

Setting $f(x)=0$ gives $e^{x-3}=7$. Taking the natural logarithm gives $x-3=\ln 7$. This yields $\ln{\left(7 \right)} + 3$.
