The graph of $f$ has range $[-2,4)$. What is the range of $g(x)=-f(x)$?

- $(-2,4]$
- $[-2,4)$
- $[-4,2)$
- $(-4,2]$

## Explanation

Multiplying $-2\leq f(x)<4$ by $-1$ reverses the inequalities and gives $2\geq -f(x)>-4$. The included endpoint becomes the right endpoint. The original output $-2$ is attained and becomes $2$. The original output $4$ is excluded and its opposite $-4$ stays excluded. Every value between these endpoints belongs to the range because its opposite belongs to the original interval.
