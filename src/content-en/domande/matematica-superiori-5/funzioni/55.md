The graph of $f(x)=\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 5}$ meets the $y$-axis. What is the vertical coordinate of the point of intersection?

- $- \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $2$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

On the $y$-axis, $x=0$. Substituting zero into the fraction gives $- \dfrac{2}{5}$.
