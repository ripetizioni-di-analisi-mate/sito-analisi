What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{x-10}$?

- $(10,+\infty)$
- $[10,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,10]$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

For a real square root, the radicand must be nonnegative. We therefore require $x-10\geq0$. The domain is $[10,+\infty)$ and includes the endpoint $10$.
