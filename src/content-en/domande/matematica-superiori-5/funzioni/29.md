What is the range of the graph shown?

::grafico{curve="t,t,0,2;t,3,2,4" legenda="y=x;y=3" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=4 punti="0,0;2,2,vuoto;2,3;4,3" titolo="Open points do not belong to the graph."}

- $[0,3]$
- $[0,2]\cup\{3\}$
- $[0,2)\cup\{3\}$
- $[0,2)$

## Explanation

The increasing piece takes every output from $0$ up to but excluding $2$. The horizontal piece gives only $3$, so the range is $[0,2)\cup\{3\}$. The open circle at height $2$ shows that this output is excluded from the sloping piece. The output $3$ is attained at several inputs. There is no point with an output strictly between $2$ and $3$, so the two sets do not form one interval.
