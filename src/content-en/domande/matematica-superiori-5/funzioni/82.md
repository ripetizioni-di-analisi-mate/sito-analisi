The graph of $f$ contains $P=(2,5)$. What is the vertical coordinate of the corresponding point on $g(x)=-3f(x-1)$?

- $15$
- $-6$
- $2$
- $-15$

## Explanation

The point on the new graph has horizontal coordinate $3$. Its vertical coordinate is the value of $f$ at $2$ multiplied by $-3$. This gives $-15$.
