What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=5x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{5} + \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $5 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{1}{5 x + 1}$
- $\dfrac{x}{5} - \dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Set $y=5x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{5}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{5}$.
