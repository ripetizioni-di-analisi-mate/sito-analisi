Let $f(x)=x+4$ and let $g$ be defined only on $\{1,3\}$. What is the domain of $g\circ f$?

- $\{1,3\}$
- $[-3,-1]$
- $\{-3,-1\}$
- $\{5,7\}$

## Explanation

To apply $g$, the value $x+4$ must equal $1$ or $3$. The two equations give the only allowed inputs, $-3$ and $-1$. The set $\{1,3\}$ contains only two numbers. For instance, $-2$ lies between $-3$ and $-1$ but would give the intermediate value $2$, for which $g$ is undefined. The composition therefore has a two-point domain.
