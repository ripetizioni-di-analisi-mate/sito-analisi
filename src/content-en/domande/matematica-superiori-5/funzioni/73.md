A table gives $f(0)=1$ and $f(2)=5$. All we know is that $f$ is continuous on $[0,2]$. Which claim about the missing value $f(1)$ is justified?

- It could be $2$ or $3$, depending on the function.
- It must be $3$, the average of the data.
- It must be $2$ because the inputs are equally spaced.
- It must be $4$, the difference between the endpoint values.

## Explanation

Both the line $2x+1$ and the parabola $x^2+1$ are continuous and pass through the stated endpoints. At the middle input they give $3$ and $2$, respectively. Taking the average would require an additional assumption, such as a straight-line graph.
