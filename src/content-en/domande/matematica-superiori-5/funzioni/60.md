A linear function $f$ satisfies $f(x + 2) - f(x) = 6$ for every $x$, and $f(0) = -1$. What is $f(4)$?

- $13$
- $23$
- $5$
- $11$

## Explanation

Let $f(x) = m x + k$. Then $f(x + 2) - f(x) = 2m$, so the condition gives $2m = 6$, that is, $m = 3$. The intercept is $k = f(0) = -1$, therefore $f(x) = 3x - 1$. Hence $f(4) = 12 - 1 = 11$. The value $23$ would come from taking $6$ as the slope, whereas $6$ is the change of $f$ for an increase of $2$ units in the variable.
