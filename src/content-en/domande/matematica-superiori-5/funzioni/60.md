The graph of $f(x)=e^{x - 4} - 9$ meets the $x$-axis at one point. What is its horizontal coordinate?

- $4 - \ln{\left(9 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(9 \right)} + 4$
- $\ln{\left(9 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(4 \right)} + 9$

## Explanation

Setting $f(x)=0$ gives $e^{x-4}=9$. Taking the natural logarithm gives $x-4=\ln 9$. This yields $\ln{\left(9 \right)} + 4$.
