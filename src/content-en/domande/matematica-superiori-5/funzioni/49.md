For which values of $x$ is $f(x)=\ln{\left(\left(x - 2\right) \left(x + 5\right) \right)}$ defined?

- $(-\infty,-5]$ ∪ $[2,\infty)$
- $(-5,2)$
- $(2,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-5)$ ∪ $(2,\infty)$

## Explanation

The argument of the logarithm must be strictly positive. The product of the two factors is positive to the left of $-5$ and to the right of $2$.
