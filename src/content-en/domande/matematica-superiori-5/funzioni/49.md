Define $f(x)=1$ for rational inputs and $f(x)=0$ for irrational inputs. What is the image of the interval $(0,1)$?

- $\{0,1\}$
- $(0,1)$
- $[0,1]$
- $\{1\}$

## Explanation

The interval contains both the rational number $1/2$ and the irrational number $\sqrt{2}/2$. Both defined output values occur, with no intermediate values.
