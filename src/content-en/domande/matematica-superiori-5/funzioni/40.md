What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=9x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{9} + \dfrac{1}{9}$
- $9 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{1}{9 x + 1}$
- $\dfrac{x}{9} - \dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

Set $y=9x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{9}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{9}$.
