Suppose $f(1)=2$, $f(2)=3$ and $f(3)=1$. What is $f(f(f(1)))$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

The three applications take $1$ to $2$, then $2$ to $3$, and finally $3$ to $1$. In symbols, $f(f(f(1)))=f(f(2))=f(3)=1$. Use the given assignments one at a time. The second application reaches $3$, which is still a known input, so there is one last value to read.
