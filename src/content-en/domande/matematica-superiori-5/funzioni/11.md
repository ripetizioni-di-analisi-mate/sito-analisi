What is the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-4}$?

- $(4,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-4\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{4\}$
- $[4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator must be nonzero. The only value that makes $x-4$ zero is $4$. Therefore the domain is $\mathbb R\setminus\{4\}$.
