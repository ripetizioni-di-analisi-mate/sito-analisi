A nondegenerate rectangle has perimeter $20$ cm. What areas, in square centimetres, can it have?

- $[1,25)$
- $[0,25]$
- $(0,100]$
- $(0,25]$

## Explanation

For base $x$ the other side is $10-x$, with $0<x<10$. The area $x(10-x)=25-(x-5)^2$ attains 25 and can approach zero without vanishing.
