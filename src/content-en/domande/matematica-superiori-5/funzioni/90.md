On which of the following intervals is the function $f(x)=|x-4|$ injective?

- $(3,6)$
- $(2,5)$
- $[4,\infty)$
- $[3,\infty)$

## Explanation

On the right-hand branch we have $f(x)=x-4$, which is strictly increasing. Each of the other intervals contains a pair of points symmetric about $4$.
