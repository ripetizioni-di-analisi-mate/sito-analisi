For every real $x$, $f(x+1)=f(x)+2$ and $f(0)=1$. Which value is determined without any additional information?

- $f(\sqrt2)=1+2\sqrt2$
- $f(\tfrac12)=2$
- $f(\tfrac14)=\tfrac32$
- $f(-2)=-3$

## Explanation

Taking two steps backwards gives $f(-2)=f(0)-4=-3$. The relation connects inputs differing by an integer and leaves the values strictly between $0$ and $1$ unspecified.
