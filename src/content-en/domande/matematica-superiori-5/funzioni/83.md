The graph of $f$ contains $P=(3,7)$. What is the vertical coordinate of the corresponding point on $g(x)=-4f(x-1)$?

- $28$
- $-28$
- $3$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The point on the new graph has horizontal coordinate $4$. Its vertical coordinate is the value of $f$ at $3$ multiplied by $-4$. This gives $-28$.
