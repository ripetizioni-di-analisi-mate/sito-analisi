What is the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-5}$?

- $(5,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-5\}$
- $[5,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{5\}$

## Explanation

The denominator must be nonzero. The only value that makes $x-5$ zero is $5$. Therefore the domain is $\mathbb R\setminus\{5\}$.
