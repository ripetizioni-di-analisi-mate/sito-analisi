What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=8x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{8} + \dfrac{1}{8}$
- $8 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{x}{8} - \dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8 x + 1}$

## Explanation

Set $y=8x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{8}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{8}$.
