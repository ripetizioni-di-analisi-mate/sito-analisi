A function on $\mathbb R$ satisfies $f(x+3)=-f(x)$ for every $x$ and $f(0)=2$. What can we conclude about its period?

- $6$ is a period and $3$ is not.
- The least positive period must be $6$.
- $3$ is a period.
- Every period must be an integer multiple of $6$.

## Explanation

Applying the identity twice gives $f(x+6)=f(x)$. Also, $f(3)=-2\ne f(0)$. The least positive period can be smaller than $6$, as in $f(x)=2\cos(\pi x)$, which satisfies the assumptions and has least positive period $2$.
