What is the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-2}$?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$
- $(2,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2\}$
- $[2,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator must be nonzero. The only value that makes $x-2$ zero is $2$. Therefore the domain is $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$.
