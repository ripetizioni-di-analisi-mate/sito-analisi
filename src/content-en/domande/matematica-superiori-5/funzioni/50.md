For which values of $x$ is $f(x)=\ln{\left(\left(x - 3\right) \left(x + 7\right) \right)}$ defined?

- $(3,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-7]$ ∪ $[3,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-7)$ ∪ $(3,\infty)$
- $(-7,3)$

## Explanation

The argument of the logarithm must be strictly positive. The product of the two factors is positive to the left of $-7$ and to the right of $3$.
