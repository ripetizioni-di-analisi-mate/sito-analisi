What are the least positive periods of $2\sin(\pi x/3)-1$ and $\tan(4x)$, respectively?

- $3,\ \pi/2$
- $6,\ \pi/4$
- $6,\ \pi/2$
- $2\pi/3,\ 4\pi$

## Explanation

The sine argument must increase by $2\pi$ and the tangent argument by $\pi$. Divide those increments by $\pi/3$ and $4$, respectively.
