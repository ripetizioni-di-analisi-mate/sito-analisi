What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=4x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{x}{4} - \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4 x + 1}$

## Explanation

Set $y=4x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{4}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{4}$.
