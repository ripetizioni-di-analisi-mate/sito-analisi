What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=3x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{x}{3} - \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{1}{3 x + 1}$

## Explanation

Set $y=3x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{3}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{3}$.
