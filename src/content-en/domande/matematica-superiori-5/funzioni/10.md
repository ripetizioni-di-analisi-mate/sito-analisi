For $f(x)=(3x-1)/(x+2)$, which value is excluded from the inverse's domain because it has no preimages?

- $0$
- $-2$
- $1/3$
- $3$

## Explanation

Setting $f(x)=3$ gives $3x-1=3x+6$, which is impossible. For every $y\ne3$ the equation instead has the solution $x=(2y+1)/(3-y)$.
