On which of the following intervals is the function $f(x)=|x-2|$ injective?

- $[1,\infty)$
- $(1,4)$
- $[2,\infty)$
- $(0,3)$

## Explanation

On the right-hand branch we have $f(x)=x-2$, which is strictly increasing. Each of the other intervals contains a pair of points symmetric about $2$.
