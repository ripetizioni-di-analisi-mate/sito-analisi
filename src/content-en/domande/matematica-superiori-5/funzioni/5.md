What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=2x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 x + 1}$

## Explanation

Set $y=2x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{2}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{2}$.
