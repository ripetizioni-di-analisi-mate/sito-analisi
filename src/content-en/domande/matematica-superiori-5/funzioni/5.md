Where is $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-2}$ positive?

- $(-1,1)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-1)\cup(1,2)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(-1,2)$

## Explanation

The factors are $x+1$, $x-1$ and $x-2$ in the denominator. Ordering the critical points gives a positive sign between $-1$ and $1$ and beyond $2$.
