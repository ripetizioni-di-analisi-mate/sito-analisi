A surjective function maps five elements onto three elements. Two of the three preimages have one element each. How many elements are in the third?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

The three preimages are disjoint and cover the domain. Two elements are already accounted for, so the remaining $5-2=3$ belong to the third preimage.
