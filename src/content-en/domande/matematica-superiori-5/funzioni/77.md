What is the range of the function $f(x)=7 - 3 e^{x}$ on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $(-\infty,7]$
- $(-\infty,7)$
- $(7,\infty)$
- $(-3,7)$

## Explanation

The exponential function takes all positive values and only positive values. Multiplying by $-3$ and adding $7$ gives all values less than $7$.
