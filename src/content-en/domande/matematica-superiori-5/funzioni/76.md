What is the range of the function $f(x)=5 - 2 e^{x}$ on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $(-\infty,5]$
- $(-\infty,5)$
- $(5,\infty)$
- $(-2,5)$

## Explanation

The exponential function takes all positive values and only positive values. Multiplying by $-2$ and adding $5$ gives all values less than $5$.
