The graph of $f(x)=\dfrac{x - 4}{x + 9}$ meets the $y$-axis. What is the vertical coordinate of the point of intersection?

- $- \dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $4$
- $- \dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

On the $y$-axis, $x=0$. Substituting zero into the fraction gives $- \dfrac{4}{9}$.
