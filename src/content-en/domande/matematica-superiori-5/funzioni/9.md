The function $f(x)=x^2+2x$ maps $\mathbb R$ to $\mathbb R$. What is its range, and why is it not injective?

- $[0,+\infty)$ and $f(-1)=f(1)$.
- $[-1,+\infty)$ and $f(-2)=f(0)$.
- $\mathbb R$ and $f(-2)=f(0)$.
- $(-\infty,-1]$ and $f(0)=f(2)$.

## Explanation

The form $(x+1)^2-1$ gives the minimum and shows that every higher value occurs. Moreover the distinct inputs $-2$ and $0$ both give zero.
