The graph of $f(x)=e^{x - 2} - 5$ meets the $x$-axis at one point. What is its horizontal coordinate?

- $\ln{\left(5 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(2 \right)} + 5$
- $\ln{\left(5 \right)} + 2$
- $2 - \ln{\left(5 \right)}$

## Explanation

Setting $f(x)=0$ gives $e^{x-2}=5$. Taking the natural logarithm gives $x-2=\ln 5$. This yields $\ln{\left(5 \right)} + 2$.
