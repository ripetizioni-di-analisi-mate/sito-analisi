What is the real domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{\dfrac{x - 3}{x + 7}}$?

- $(-7,3]$
- $[3,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-7]$ ∪ $[3,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-7)$ ∪ $[3,\infty)$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative when the numerator and denominator have the same sign or when the numerator is zero. The value $-7$ makes the denominator zero.
