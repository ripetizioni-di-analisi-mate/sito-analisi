The function $f$ is defined on $[-1,1]$ by $f(x)=x^3+2x^2-1$. What is its even part $\dfrac{f(x)+f(-x)}{2}$?

- $2x^2-1$
- $x^3$
- $2x^2$
- $x^3-1$

## Explanation

Replacing $x$ by $-x$ changes the sign of the cubic term. The sum reduces to $4x^2-2$, and dividing by two gives $2x^2-1$.
