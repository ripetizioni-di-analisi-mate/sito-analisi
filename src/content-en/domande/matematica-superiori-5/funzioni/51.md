At how many points does the graph of $f(x) = x^3 - 4x$ meet the $x$-axis?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The points of the $x$-axis have $y$-coordinate $0$, so the required points have the $x$-coordinates that solve $x^3 - 4x = 0$. Factoring out $x$ gives $x\,(x^2 - 4) = x\,(x - 2)(x + 2)$. A product is zero if and only if one of its factors is zero, therefore the solutions are $x = -2$, $x = 0$ and $x = 2$. Hence the graph meets the $x$-axis at $3$ points. Dividing the equation by $x$ would lose the solution $x = 0$ and give only $2$ points.
