For which values of $x$ is $f(x)=\ln{\left(\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 9\right) \right)}$ defined?

- $(-9,4)$
- $(-\infty,-9]$ ∪ $[4,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-9)$ ∪ $(4,\infty)$
- $(4,\infty)$

## Explanation

The argument of the logarithm must be strictly positive. The product of the two factors is positive to the left of $-9$ and to the right of $4$.
