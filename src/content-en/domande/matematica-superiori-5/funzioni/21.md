What is the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-6}$?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{6\}$
- $(6,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-6\}$
- $[6,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator must be nonzero. The only value that makes $x-6$ zero is $6$. Therefore the domain is $\mathbb R\setminus\{6\}$.
