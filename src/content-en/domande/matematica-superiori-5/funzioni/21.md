Two functions $f$ and $g$ are odd and defined on $\mathbb R$. Which property must hold for their pointwise product $h(x)=f(x)g(x)$?

- $h$ is always constant.
- $h$ is always odd.
- $h$ is even.
- $h$ is always strictly positive.

## Explanation

For all $x$, $h(-x)=f(-x)g(-x)=(-f(x))(-g(x))=f(x)g(x)=h(x)$. The two sign changes cancel. A concrete check is $f(x)=g(x)=x$. Their product is $x^2$, which is even and equals zero at $x=0$. Thus oddness of the factors does not imply that their product is strictly positive.
