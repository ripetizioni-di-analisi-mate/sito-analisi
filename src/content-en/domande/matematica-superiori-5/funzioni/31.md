What is the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-8}$?

- $(8,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-8\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{8\}$
- $[8,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator must be nonzero. The only value that makes $x-8$ zero is $8$. Therefore the domain is $\mathbb R\setminus\{8\}$.
