What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=6x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{6} - \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{x}{6} + \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $6 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{1}{6 x + 1}$

## Explanation

Set $y=6x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{6}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{6}$.
