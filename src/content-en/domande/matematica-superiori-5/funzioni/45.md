What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=10x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{10} - \dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{x}{10} + \dfrac{1}{10}$
- $10 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{1}{10 x + 1}$

## Explanation

Set $y=10x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{10}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{10}$.
