What is the inverse of the function $f(x)=7x+1$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{x}{7} + \dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{x}{7} - \dfrac{1}{7}$
- $7 x - 1$
- $\dfrac{1}{7 x + 1}$

## Explanation

Set $y=7x+1$ and isolate $x$. This gives $x=\dfrac{y-1}{7}$. Interchanging the variables gives the inverse $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{7}$.
