What is the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-7}$?

- $(7,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{7\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-7\}$
- $[7,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator must be nonzero. The only value that makes $x-7$ zero is $7$. Therefore the domain is $\mathbb R\setminus\{7\}$.
