What is the real domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-3}$?

- $(3,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{3\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3\}$
- $[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator must be nonzero. The only value that makes $x-3$ zero is $3$. Therefore the domain is $\mathbb R\setminus\{3\}$.
