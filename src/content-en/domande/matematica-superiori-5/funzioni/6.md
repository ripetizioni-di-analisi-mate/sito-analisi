Which of $x^4-3x^2+1$, $x^3/(x^2-1)$ and $e^x+x$ is odd on its domain?

- $x^4-3x^2+1$
- $\dfrac{x^3}{x^2-1}$
- $e^x+x$
- All three.

## Explanation

The quotient has an odd numerator, an even denominator and a symmetric domain. Replacing $x$ by $-x$ therefore reverses the sign of its value.
