Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^{2} + 3}}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $\sqrt{x^{2} + 3}$
- $2 \sqrt{x^{2} + 3}$
- $x \sqrt{x^{2} + 3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{x^{2} + 3}}{2}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the square root is the derivative of the radicand divided by twice the square root. The factor of two cancels. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
