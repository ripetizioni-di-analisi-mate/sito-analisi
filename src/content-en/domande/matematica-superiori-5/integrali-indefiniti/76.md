Suppose $G$ is an antiderivative of $x^2f(x)$. In $\int x^3f(x)\,dx$, integrate $x^2f(x)$ by parts. Which expression results?

- $xG(x)-\int G(x)\,dx$
- $3x^2G(x)-\int G(x)\,dx$
- $xG(x)+\int G(x)\,dx$
- $xG(x)-\int xG(x)\,dx$

## Explanation

Write the integrand as $xG'(x)$. The derivative of the factor $x$ is $1$, so integration by parts gives $xG-\int G\,dx$.
