Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{2 x + 5}{x + 3}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $2 x + 5 \ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}$
- $2 x$
- $2 x - \ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}$
- $2 x + 11 \ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}$

## Explanation

Write the numerator as $a(x+c)+b-ac$. Integrate the constant term and the remaining fraction separately. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
