Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=5x$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $5 x^{2}$
- $5 x$
- $\dfrac{5 x}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5 x^{2}}{2}$

## Explanation

The power rule gives $\int x\,dx=\dfrac{x^2}{2}+C$. Multiply by the coefficient $5$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{5}{2}x^2$ and its derivative is $5x$.
