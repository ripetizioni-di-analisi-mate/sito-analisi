For $n\ge1$, let $I_n$ be an antiderivative of $x^n e^x$. Which formula expresses it in terms of $I_{n-1}$ up to a constant?

- $I_n=n x^ne^x-I_{n-1}$
- $I_n=x^{n-1}e^x-I_{n-1}$
- $I_n=x^ne^x+nI_{n-1}$
- $I_n=x^ne^x-nI_{n-1}$

## Explanation

Integration by parts differentiates $x^n$ to $nx^{n-1}$. The exponential is unchanged by integration, so the remaining term to subtract is $nI_{n-1}$.
