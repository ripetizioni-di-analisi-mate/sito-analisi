Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{6}{x}$ for $x>0$?

- $\dfrac{\log{(x )}}{6}$
- $\dfrac{6}{x}$
- $\dfrac{6}{x + 1}$
- $6 \log{(x )}$

## Explanation

For $x>0$, the derivative of $\ln x$ is $\dfrac{1}{x}$. Multiplying by $6$ gives the integrand. Thus, an antiderivative is $6\ln x$.
