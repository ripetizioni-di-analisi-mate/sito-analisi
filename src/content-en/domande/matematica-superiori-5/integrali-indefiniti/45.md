A device records $dQ/dt$ in coulombs per second. What additional information determines the charge $Q(t)$ itself in coulombs, as well as its changes?

- The second derivative at one time.
- The charge at one known time.
- The maximum of $dQ/dt$.
- The total recording time.

## Explanation

The integral of the current determines changes in charge. One initial value fixes the integration constant and therefore the charge at every time.
