The current through a conductor is known at every instant and equals $i(t)=\dfrac{dQ}{dt}$, in coulombs per second. What additional piece of information determines the value of the charge $Q(t)$ at every instant?

- The second derivative of $Q$ at one instant.
- The value of the charge at one known instant.
- The maximum value of the current.
- The length of the time interval considered.

## Explanation

The charge is an antiderivative of the current, so $Q(t)=\int i(t)\,dt$ is determined up to an additive constant. The current alone therefore gives only the changes in charge. If the charge $Q(t_0)$ at some instant $t_0$ is known, this condition fixes the constant. Consequently the charge is determined at every instant.
