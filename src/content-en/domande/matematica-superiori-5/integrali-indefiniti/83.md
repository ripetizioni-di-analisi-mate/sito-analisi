Which antiderivative of $\sin(3x)\cos x$ follows from the product-to-sum identity?

- $-\dfrac{\cos(4x)}4-\dfrac{\cos(2x)}2$
- $\dfrac{\sin(4x)}8+\dfrac{\sin(2x)}4$
- $-\dfrac{\cos(4x)}8-\dfrac{\cos(2x)}4$
- $-\dfrac{\cos(4x)}8+\dfrac{\cos(2x)}4$

## Explanation

The product equals $\tfrac12[\sin(4x)+\sin(2x)]$. Integrating divides each term by its angular frequency and introduces a minus sign.
