Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 3\right) \left(x + 7\right)}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}}{4} - \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 7 \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}}{4} + \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 7 \right)}}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}}{4} + \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 7 \right)}}{4}$

## Explanation

The difference between the reciprocals of $x+a$ and $x+b$ has numerator $b-a$. Divide by that difference and integrate the two terms. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
