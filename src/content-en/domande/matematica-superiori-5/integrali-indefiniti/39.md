An increasing function $f$ has a differentiable inverse $g$ and an antiderivative $F$. Which formula gives an antiderivative of $g$ for every function satisfying these assumptions?

- $yg(y)-F(g(y))$
- $yg(y)+F(g(y))$
- $F(g(y))$
- $g(y)-yF(y)$

## Explanation

Differentiating the stated expression gives $g(y)+yg'(y)-f(g(y))g'(y)$. Since $f(g(y))=y$, the terms containing the derivative of the inverse cancel.
