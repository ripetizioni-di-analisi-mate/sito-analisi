Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{6x + 2}{3x^2 + 2x + 5}\,dx$.

- $(6x+2)\ln(3x^2+2x+5)+c$
- $\dfrac{1}{3x^2+2x+5}+c$
- $\ln(6x+2)+c$
- $\ln(3x^2+2x+5)+c$

## Explanation

The numerator is the derivative of the denominator. Setting $u=3x^2+2x+5$ gives $\int du/u$. The quadratic is positive for all $x$, so the result is $\ln u+c$.
