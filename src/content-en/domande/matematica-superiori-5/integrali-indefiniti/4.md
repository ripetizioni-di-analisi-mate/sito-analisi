Which family of functions is equal to $\displaystyle\int \frac{6x + 2}{3x^2 + 2x + 5}\,dx$?

- $(6x+2)\ln(3x^2+2x+5)+c$
- $\dfrac{1}{3x^2+2x+5}+c$
- $\ln(6x+2)+c$
- $\ln(3x^2+2x+5)+c$

## Explanation

With $g(x) = 3x^2 + 2x + 5$ we have $g'(x) = 6x + 2$, so the integral reduces to the standard integral $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{g'(x)}{g(x)}\,dx = \ln \lvert g(x) \rvert + c$. The discriminant of the trinomial is $4 - 60 < 0$, therefore $g(x) > 0$ for every $x$ and the absolute value can be omitted. The result is $\ln\left( 3x^2 + 2x + 5 \right) + c$.
