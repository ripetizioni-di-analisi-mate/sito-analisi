Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(5x)$?

- $5 \sin{(5 x )}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{(5 x )}}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(5 x )}}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(5 x )}}{5}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $5\cos(5x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $5$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(5x)}{5}$ equals the integrand.
