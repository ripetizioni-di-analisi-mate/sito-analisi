Which family of functions is obtained by integrating $\dfrac{4x - 2}{x^2 + 2x + 2}$?

- $2\ln(x^2+2x+2)-6\arctan x+c$
- $2\ln(x^2+2x+2)-6\arctan(x+1)+c$
- $2\ln(x^2+2x+2)+6\arctan(x+1)+c$
- $4\ln(x^2+2x+2)-2\arctan(x+1)+c$

## Explanation

The derivative of the denominator is $2x + 2$, and the numerator can be written as $4x - 2 = 2(2x + 2) - 6$. So
$$
\int \frac{4x - 2}{x^2 + 2x + 2}\,dx = 2 \int \frac{2x + 2}{x^2 + 2x + 2}\,dx - 6 \int \frac{dx}{x^2 + 2x + 2} .
$$
The first integral equals $\ln\left( x^2 + 2x + 2 \right)$, without absolute value because the trinomial has negative discriminant and is positive for every $x$. Since $x^2 + 2x + 2 = (x + 1)^2 + 1$, the second integral equals $\arctan(x + 1)$. Therefore the result is $2\ln\left( x^2 + 2x + 2 \right) - 6\arctan(x + 1) + c$.
