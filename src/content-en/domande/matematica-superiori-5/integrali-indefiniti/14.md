Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{4}{x}$ for $x>0$?

- $\dfrac{\log{(x )}}{4}$
- $4 \log{(x )}$
- $\dfrac{4}{x}$
- $\dfrac{4}{x + 1}$

## Explanation

For $x>0$, the derivative of $\ln x$ is $\dfrac{1}{x}$. Multiplying by $4$ gives the integrand. Thus, an antiderivative is $4\ln x$.
