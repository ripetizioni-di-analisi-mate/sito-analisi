Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{4x - 2}{x^2 + 2x + 2}\,dx$.

- $2\ln(x^2+2x+2)-6\arctan x+c$
- $2\ln(x^2+2x+2)-6\arctan(x+1)+c$
- $2\ln(x^2+2x+2)+6\arctan(x+1)+c$
- $4\ln(x^2+2x+2)-2\arctan(x+1)+c$

## Explanation

The numerator is $2(2x+2)-6$. Its first term is twice the derivative of the denominator and gives the logarithm. In the second term the denominator is $(x+1)^2+1$, which gives $\arctan(x+1)$.
