Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{4 x + 9}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{16 x + 36}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(4 x + 9 \right)}}{4}$
- $\ln{\left(4 x + 9 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 x + 9 \right)}}{4}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the logarithm of the argument is the reciprocal of the argument times the coefficient of $x$. To obtain the integrand, divide by that coefficient. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
