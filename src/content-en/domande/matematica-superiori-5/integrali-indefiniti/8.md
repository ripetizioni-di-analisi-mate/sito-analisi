Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{x}{\sqrt{2x + 1}}\,dx$ for $x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$ using the substitution $t = \sqrt{2x + 1}$.

- $\dfrac{x-1}{3}\sqrt{2x+1}+c$
- $x\sqrt{2x+1}+c$
- $\dfrac{x+1}{3}\sqrt{2x+1}+c$
- $\dfrac{(2x+1)^{3/2}}3+c$

## Explanation

With $t=\sqrt{2x+1}$, we have $dx=t\,dt$ and $x=(t^2-1)/2$. The integral becomes $\int(t^2-1)\,dt/2=t^3/6-t/2+c$. Factor out $t$ and use $t^2=2x+1$ to obtain the result.
