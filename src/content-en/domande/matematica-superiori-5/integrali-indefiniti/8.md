Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(3x)$?

- $3 \sin{(3 x )}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(3 x )}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(3 x )}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{(3 x )}}{3}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $3\cos(3x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $3$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(3x)}{3}$ equals the integrand.
