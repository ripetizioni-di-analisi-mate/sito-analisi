An antiderivative $F$ of $f(x)=x^{2} + 4 x$ satisfies $F(9)=0$. What is $F(5)$?

- $\dfrac{940}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{604}{3}$
- $\dfrac{275}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{940}{3}$

## Explanation

The general antiderivative is $\dfrac{x^3}{3}+\dfrac{4x^2}{2}+C$. The condition tells us to subtract the value of the nonconstant part at $9$. This gives $- \dfrac{940}{3}$.
