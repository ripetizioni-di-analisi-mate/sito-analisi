Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\sin{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $- \cos{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}$
- $- \dfrac{\cos{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{2}$

## Explanation

The derivative of cosine carries a minus sign. Differentiating the argument also introduces a factor equal to the coefficient of $x$. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
