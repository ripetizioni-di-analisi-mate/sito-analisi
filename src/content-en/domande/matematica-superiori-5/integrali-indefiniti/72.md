Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=x^{2} e^{x^{3} + 9}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $e^{x^{3} + 9}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x^{3} + 9}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{x^{3} e^{x^{3} + 9}}{3}$
- $3 e^{x^{3} + 9}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $x^3+b$ is $3x^2$. To account for the factor of three, divide the exponential by three. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
