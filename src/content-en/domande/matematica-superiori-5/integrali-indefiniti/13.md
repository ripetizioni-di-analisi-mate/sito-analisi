Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(4x)$?

- $\dfrac{\sin{(4 x )}}{4}$
- $4 \sin{(4 x )}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(4 x )}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(4 x )}}{4}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $4\cos(4x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $4$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(4x)}{4}$ equals the integrand.
