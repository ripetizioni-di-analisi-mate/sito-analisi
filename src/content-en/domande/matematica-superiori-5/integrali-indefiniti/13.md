Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{x + 3}{x^2 - 6x + 9}\,dx$ for $x \neq 3$.

- $\ln|x-3|-\dfrac6{x-3}+c$
- $\ln|x-3|+\dfrac6{x-3}+c$
- $-\dfrac{x+3}{x-3}+c$
- $\dfrac{\ln|x-3|}{x-3}+c$

## Explanation

Write $x+3=(x-3)+6$. The fraction becomes $\dfrac1{x-3}+\dfrac6{(x-3)^2}$, whose integrals are a logarithm and $-\dfrac6{x-3}$, respectively.
