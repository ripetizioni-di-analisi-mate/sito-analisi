For $x \neq 3$, what is $\displaystyle\int \frac{x + 3}{x^2 - 6x + 9}\,dx$?

- $\ln|x-3|-\dfrac{6}{x-3}+c$
- $\ln|x-3|+\dfrac{6}{x-3}+c$
- $-\dfrac{x+3}{x-3}+c$
- $\dfrac{\ln|x-3|}{x-3}+c$

## Explanation

The denominator has zero discriminant and can be written as $(x - 3)^2$. Since $x + 3 = (x - 3) + 6$, for $x \neq 3$ we have
$$
\frac{x + 3}{(x - 3)^2} = \frac{1}{x - 3} + \frac{6}{(x - 3)^2} .
$$
An antiderivative of $\dfrac{1}{x - 3}$ is $\ln\lvert x - 3 \rvert$ and an antiderivative of $\dfrac{6}{(x - 3)^2}$ is $-\dfrac{6}{x - 3}$, so the result is $\ln\lvert x - 3 \rvert - \dfrac{6}{x - 3} + c$.
