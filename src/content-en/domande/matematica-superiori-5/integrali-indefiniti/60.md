To integrate $\dfrac{1}{x^2-4}$ for $x>2$, write it as $\dfrac{A}{x-2}+\dfrac{B}{x+2}$. Which relation eliminates the term in $x$ from the combined numerator?

- $2A+2B=1$
- $AB=1$
- $A-B=0$
- $A+B=0$

## Explanation

Combining the fractions gives $(A+B)x+2(A-B)$. The required numerator is the constant $1$, so the coefficient of $x$ must vanish.
