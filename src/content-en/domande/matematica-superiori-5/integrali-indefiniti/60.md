Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\ln{\left(4 x \right)}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(4 x \right)}}{x}$
- $x \ln{\left(4 x \right)} - x$
- $x \ln{\left(4 x \right)} + x$
- $x \ln{\left(4 x \right)}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts using the factors $1$ and $\ln(ax)$. In the second integral, the product of $x$ and the derivative of the logarithm is one. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
