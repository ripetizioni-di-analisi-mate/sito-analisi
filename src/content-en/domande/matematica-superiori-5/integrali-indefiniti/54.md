Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{x^{2} + 9}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $\ln{\left(x^{2} + 9 \right)}$
- $2 \ln{\left(x^{2} + 9 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x^{2} + 9 \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 x^{2} + 18}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the denominator is $2x$. Take the logarithm of the denominator and account for the factor of two. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
