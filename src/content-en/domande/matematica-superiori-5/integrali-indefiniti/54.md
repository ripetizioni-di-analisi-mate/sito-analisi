For $x > 0$, which function has derivative equal to $\dfrac{\left( 1 + \sqrt{x} \right)^2}{x}$?

- $\ln x + 2 \sqrt{x} + x$
- $\ln x + \sqrt{x} + x$
- $\ln x + 4 \sqrt{x} + x$
- $\ln x + \dfrac{4}{3} x \sqrt{x} + x$

## Explanation

Expand the square and divide each term by $x$:
$$
\frac{\left( 1 + \sqrt{x} \right)^2}{x} = \frac{1 + 2\sqrt{x} + x}{x} = \frac{1}{x} + 2 x^{-\frac{1}{2}} + 1 .
$$
By **linearity** each term is integrated with the table. For $\alpha = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ the power formula gives $\displaystyle\int 2 x^{-\frac{1}{2}}\, dx = 2 \cdot \frac{x^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\frac{1}{2}} = 4 \sqrt{x}$. Therefore an antiderivative is $\ln x + 4 \sqrt{x} + x$.
