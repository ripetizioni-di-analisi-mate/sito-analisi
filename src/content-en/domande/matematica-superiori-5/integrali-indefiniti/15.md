On $x>0$, the function $F(x)=2\sqrt{x}$ is an antiderivative of $1/\sqrt{x}$. Extend it by setting $F(0)=0$. Which property does this extension have at the origin?

- It is continuous but its right derivative is not finite.
- Its right derivative is zero.
- Its right derivative equals two.
- It has a jump discontinuity.

## Explanation

The function tends to zero from the right. The difference quotient is $\dfrac{F(h)-F(0)}{h}=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt h}$ for $h>0$ and tends to $+\infty$.
