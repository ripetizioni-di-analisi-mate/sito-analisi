Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\sin{\left(4 x + 9 \right)}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $- \cos{\left(4 x + 9 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{\left(4 x + 9 \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{\left(4 x + 9 \right)}}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{\cos{\left(4 x + 9 \right)}}{4}$

## Explanation

The derivative of cosine carries a minus sign. Differentiating the argument also introduces a factor equal to the coefficient of $x$. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
