Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 5\right)}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 2 \right)}}{3} + \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 5 \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 2 \right)}}{3} + \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 5 \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 2 \right)}}{3} - \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 5 \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 2 \right)}}{3}$

## Explanation

The difference between the reciprocals of $x+a$ and $x+b$ has numerator $b-a$. Divide by that difference and integrate the two terms. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
