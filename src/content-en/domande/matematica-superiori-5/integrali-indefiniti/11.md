Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{x^2 + x + 1}{x + 2}\,dx$ for $x \neq -2$.

- $x-1+3\ln|x+2|+c$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{2}+x+3\ln|x+2|+c$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{2}-x+\ln|x+2|+c$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{2}-x+3\ln|x+2|+c$

## Explanation

Polynomial division gives $x^2+x+1=(x+2)(x-1)+3$. Integrate the quotient $x-1$ and the term $\dfrac3{x+2}$ separately on each interval of the domain.
