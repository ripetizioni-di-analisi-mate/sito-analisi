A curve has slope $2x$ at every point. Its tangent at $x=1$ passes through $(0,3)$. What is the curve's height at $x=0$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The tangent has slope $2$, so it is $y=2x+3$ and touches the curve at $(1,5)$. For the curve $F(x)=x^2+c$, the value $F(1)=5$ gives $c=4$.
