Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{10}{x}$ for $x>0$?

- $\dfrac{\log{(x )}}{10}$
- $\dfrac{10}{x}$
- $\dfrac{10}{x + 1}$
- $10 \log{(x )}$

## Explanation

For $x>0$, the derivative of $\ln x$ is $\dfrac{1}{x}$. Multiplying by $10$ gives the integrand. Thus, an antiderivative is $10\ln x$.
