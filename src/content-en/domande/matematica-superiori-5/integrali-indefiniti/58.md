Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\ln{\left(2 x \right)}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $x \ln{\left(2 x \right)} + x$
- $x \ln{\left(2 x \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 x \right)}}{x}$
- $x \ln{\left(2 x \right)} - x$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts using the factors $1$ and $\ln(ax)$. In the second integral, the product of $x$ and the derivative of the logarithm is one. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
