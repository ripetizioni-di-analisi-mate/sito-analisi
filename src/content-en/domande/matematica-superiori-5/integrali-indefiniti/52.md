Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{x^{2} + 5}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $2 \ln{\left(x^{2} + 5 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(x^{2} + 5 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 x^{2} + 10}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x^{2} + 5 \right)}}{2}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the denominator is $2x$. Take the logarithm of the denominator and account for the factor of two. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
