Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\sin{\left(3 x + 7 \right)}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\cos{\left(3 x + 7 \right)}}{3}$
- $- \cos{\left(3 x + 7 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{\left(3 x + 7 \right)}}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{\cos{\left(3 x + 7 \right)}}{3}$

## Explanation

The derivative of cosine carries a minus sign. Differentiating the argument also introduces a factor equal to the coefficient of $x$. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
