Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(7x)$?

- $7 \sin{(7 x )}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(7 x )}}{7}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(7 x )}}{7}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{(7 x )}}{7}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $7\cos(7x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $7$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(7x)}{7}$ equals the integrand.
