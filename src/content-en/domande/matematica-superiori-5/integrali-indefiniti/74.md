Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=x \cos{\left(3 x \right)}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\sin{\left(3 x \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{x \sin{\left(3 x \right)}}{3} + \dfrac{\cos{\left(3 x \right)}}{9}$
- $\dfrac{x \sin{\left(3 x \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{x \sin{\left(3 x \right)}}{3} - \dfrac{\cos{\left(3 x \right)}}{9}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, integrating the cosine and differentiating $x$. The remaining term is the integral of the sine with a minus sign in front. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
