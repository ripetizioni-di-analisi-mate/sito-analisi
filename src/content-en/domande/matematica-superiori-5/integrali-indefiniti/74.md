When integrating $\cos^3 x$, which factorisation turns the problem into a polynomial integral in $u=\sin x$?

- $\cos x(1-\sin^2x)$
- $\cos x(1-\sin x)^2$
- $\sin x(1-\cos^2x)$
- $\cos x(1+\sin^2x)$

## Explanation

The substitution requires the factor $\cos x\,dx$. The remaining two factors give $\cos^2x=1-\sin^2x$, so the integral becomes $\int(1-u^2)\,du$.
