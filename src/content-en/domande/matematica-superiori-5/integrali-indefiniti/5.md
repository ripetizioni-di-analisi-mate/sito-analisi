What is the set of antiderivatives of $x^2 e^{x^3}$?

- $e^{x^3}+c$
- $\dfrac{1}{3} e^{x^3}+c$
- $3e^{x^3}+c$
- $\dfrac{x^3}{3}e^{x^3}+c$

## Explanation

The integral reduces to the standard integral $\displaystyle\int e^{g(x)}\,g'(x)\,dx = e^{g(x)} + c$ with $g(x) = x^3$, whose derivative is $3x^2$. We multiply and divide by $3$, so
$$
\int x^2 e^{x^3}\,dx = \frac{1}{3} \int 3x^2 e^{x^3}\,dx = \frac{1}{3}\,e^{x^3} + c .
$$
