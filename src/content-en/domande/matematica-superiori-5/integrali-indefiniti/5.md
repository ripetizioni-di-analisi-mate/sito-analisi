Evaluate $\displaystyle\int x^2 e^{x^3}\,dx$.

- $e^{x^3}+c$
- $\dfrac13 e^{x^3}+c$
- $3e^{x^3}+c$
- $\dfrac{x^3}{3}e^{x^3}+c$

## Explanation

With $u=x^3$, we have $du=3x^2\,dx$. The factor $x^2\,dx$ is therefore one third of $du$, and an antiderivative of the exponential in $u$ is $e^u$.
