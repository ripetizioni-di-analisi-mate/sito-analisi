Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(2x)$?

- $2 \sin{(2 x )}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(2 x )}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{(2 x )}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(2 x )}}{2}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $2\cos(2x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $2$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(2x)}{2}$ equals the integrand.
