Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \left( 4\cos x - 3\sin x + \frac{1}{1 + x^2} \right) dx$.

- $4\cos x+3\sin x+\arctan x+c$
- $4\sin x-3\cos x+\arctan x+c$
- $4\sin x+3\cos x+\arctan x+c$
- $4\sin x+3\cos x+\ln(1+x^2)+c$

## Explanation

Add the three antiderivatives. Differentiating the cosine accounts for the minus sign in the second term, and the derivative of the inverse tangent is $\dfrac1{1+x^2}$.
