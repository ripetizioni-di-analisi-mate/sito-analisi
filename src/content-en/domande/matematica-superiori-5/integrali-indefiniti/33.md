Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(8x)$?

- $\dfrac{\sin{(8 x )}}{8}$
- $8 \sin{(8 x )}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(8 x )}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(8 x )}}{8}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $8\cos(8x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $8$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(8x)}{8}$ equals the integrand.
