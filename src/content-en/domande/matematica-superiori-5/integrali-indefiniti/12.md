What are the antiderivatives of $\dfrac{3x + 4}{x^2 + 3x + 2}$ for $x \neq -1$ and $x \neq -2$?

- $2\ln|x+1|+\ln|x+2|+c$
- $3\ln|x^2+3x+2|+c$
- $\ln|x+1|+2\ln|x+2|+c$
- $\ln|x+1|-2\ln|x+2|+c$

## Explanation

The denominator has positive discriminant and factors as $(x + 1)(x + 2)$. We look for two constants $A$ and $B$ such that $3x + 4 = A(x + 2) + B(x + 1)$ for every $x$. For $x = -1$ we get $A = 1$, and for $x = -2$ we get $-2 = -B$, so $B = 2$. Therefore
$$
\int \frac{3x + 4}{x^2 + 3x + 2}\,dx = \int \frac{1}{x + 1}\,dx + \int \frac{2}{x + 2}\,dx = \ln\lvert x + 1 \rvert + 2\ln\lvert x + 2 \rvert + c .
$$
