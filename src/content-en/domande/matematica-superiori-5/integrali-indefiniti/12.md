Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{4x}$?

- $4 e^{4 x}$
- $e^{4 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{e^{4 x}}{4}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{4x}$ contains the factor $4$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $4$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{4x}}{4}$.
