Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{3x + 4}{x^2 + 3x + 2}\,dx$ for $x \neq -1$ and $x \neq -2$.

- $2\ln|x+1|+\ln|x+2|+c$
- $3\ln|x^2+3x+2|+c$
- $\ln|x+1|+2\ln|x+2|+c$
- $\ln|x+1|-2\ln|x+2|+c$

## Explanation

The denominator factors as $(x+1)(x+2)$. Since $3x+4=(x+2)+2(x+1)$, the partial fractions are $\dfrac1{x+1}$ and $\dfrac2{x+2}$. Each contributes a logarithm.
