Under $x=2\sin t$, with $-\pi/2<t<\pi/2$, what does $\int\sqrt{4-x^2}\,dx$ become?

- $4\int\cos^2t\,dt$
- $4\int\sin^2t\,dt$
- $4\int\sin t\cos t\,dt$
- $2\int\cos t\,dt$

## Explanation

On this interval $\cos t>0$, so the square root equals $2\cos t$. The differential $dx=2\cos t\,dt$ provides the second factor.
