Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(9x)$?

- $9 \sin{(9 x )}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{(9 x )}}{9}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(9 x )}}{9}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(9 x )}}{9}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $9\cos(9x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $9$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(9x)}{9}$ equals the integrand.
