Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{7x}$?

- $7 e^{7 x}$
- $e^{7 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{7 x}}{7}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{7}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{7x}$ contains the factor $7$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $7$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{7x}}{7}$.
