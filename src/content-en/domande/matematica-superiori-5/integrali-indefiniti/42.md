Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{10x}$?

- $10 e^{10 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{10 x}}{10}$
- $e^{10 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{10}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{10x}$ contains the factor $10$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $10$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{10x}}{10}$.
