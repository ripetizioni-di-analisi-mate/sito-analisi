Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(10x)$?

- $10 \sin{(10 x )}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(10 x )}}{10}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{(10 x )}}{10}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(10 x )}}{10}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $10\cos(10x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $10$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(10x)}{10}$ equals the integrand.
