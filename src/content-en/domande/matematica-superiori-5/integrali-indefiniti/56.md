Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=x e^{3 x}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\left(3 x + 1\right) e^{3 x}}{9}$
- $\dfrac{x e^{3 x}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\left(3 x - 1\right) e^{3 x}}{9}$
- $\dfrac{e^{3 x}}{9}$

## Explanation

In integration by parts, differentiate $x$ and integrate the exponential. From the first product, subtract the remaining integral of an exponential function. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
