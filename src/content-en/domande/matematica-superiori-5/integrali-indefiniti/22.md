Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{6x}$?

- $6 e^{6 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{6 x}}{6}$
- $e^{6 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{6}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{6x}$ contains the factor $6$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $6$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{6x}}{6}$.
