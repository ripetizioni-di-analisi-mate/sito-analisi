On $x>0$, simplify $\dfrac{e^{2x}-1}{e^x-1}$ before integrating it. Which function remains?

- $2e^x$
- $e^x+1$
- $e^x$
- $e^x-1$

## Explanation

With $z=e^x$, the numerator factors as $(z-1)(z+1)$. For $x>0$ the first factor is nonzero and cancels, leaving $e^x+1$ to integrate.
