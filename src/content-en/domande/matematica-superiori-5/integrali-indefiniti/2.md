Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{2x}$?

- $2 e^{2 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{2 x}}{2}$
- $e^{2 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{2}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{2x}$ contains the factor $2$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $2$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{2x}}{2}$.
