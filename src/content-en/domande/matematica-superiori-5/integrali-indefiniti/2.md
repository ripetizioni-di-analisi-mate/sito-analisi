Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \left( \frac{3}{x} - 4\sqrt[3]{x} \right) dx$ for $x > 0$.

- $-\dfrac{3}{x^2}-3x^{4/3}+c$
- $3\ln x-3x^{4/3}+c$
- $3\ln x-3x^{1/3}+c$
- $3\ln x-4x^{4/3}+c$

## Explanation

For $x>0$, the first term gives $3\ln x$. In the second term the cube root is $x^{1/3}$, so the coefficient of $x^{4/3}$ is $-4\cdot\dfrac34=-3$.
