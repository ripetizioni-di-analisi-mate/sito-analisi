Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 16}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{4} \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(4 x \right)}}{4}$
- $4 \operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{4} \right)}$
- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{4} \right)}$

## Explanation

Factor the square of $a$ out of the denominator. The substitution $u=x/a$ reduces the problem to finding an antiderivative of $1/(1+u^2)$. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
