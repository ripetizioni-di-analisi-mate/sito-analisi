On $x>0$, simplify $(\sqrt{x}-1)(\sqrt{x}+1)$ before integrating. Which antiderivative results?

- $\dfrac{x^2}{2}+x$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{2}-x$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}-x$
- $x^2-x$

## Explanation

The factors are conjugates, so their product is $x-1$. Integrate the resulting two monomials separately.
