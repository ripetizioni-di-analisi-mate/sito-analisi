An antiderivative $F$ of $f(x)=x^{2} + 2 x$ satisfies $F(5)=0$. What is $F(3)$?

- $\dfrac{146}{3}$
- $18$
- $- \dfrac{98}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{146}{3}$

## Explanation

The general antiderivative is $\dfrac{x^3}{3}+\dfrac{2x^2}{2}+C$. The condition tells us to subtract the value of the nonconstant part at $5$. This gives $- \dfrac{146}{3}$.
