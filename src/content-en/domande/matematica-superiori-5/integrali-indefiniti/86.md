Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{x - 2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2 \left(x - 2\right)^{2}}$
- $\ln{\left(x - 2 \right)}$

## Explanation

Integrate the power with exponent $-2$. Increase the exponent by one and divide by the new exponent, which is $-1$.
