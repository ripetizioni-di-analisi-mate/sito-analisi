An antiderivative $F$ of $f(x)=x^{2} + 3 x$ satisfies $F(7)=0$. What is $F(4)$?

- $- \dfrac{285}{2}$
- $\dfrac{285}{2}$
- $\dfrac{136}{3}$
- $-93$

## Explanation

The general antiderivative is $\dfrac{x^3}{3}+\dfrac{3x^2}{2}+C$. The condition tells us to subtract the value of the nonconstant part at $7$. This gives $- \dfrac{285}{2}$.
