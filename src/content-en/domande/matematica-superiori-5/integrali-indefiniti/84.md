Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 4\right) \left(x + 9\right)}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}}{5} - \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 9 \right)}}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}}{5} + \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 9 \right)}}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}}{5} + \dfrac{\ln{\left(x + 9 \right)}}{5}$

## Explanation

The difference between the reciprocals of $x+a$ and $x+b$ has numerator $b-a$. Divide by that difference and integrate the two terms. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
