A particle has velocity $v(t)=3t^2+2t$ in metres per second and position $s(1)=4$ metres. What is its position at $t=3$ seconds?

- $40\,\mathrm m$
- $38\,\mathrm m$
- $34\,\mathrm m$
- $36\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Integrating the velocity gives $s(t)=t^3+t^2+c$. At $t=1$, $2+c=4$, so $c=2$ and $s(3)=27+9+2=38$ metres.
