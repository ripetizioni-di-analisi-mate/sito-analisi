Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{3x}$?

- $3 e^{3 x}$
- $e^{3 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{3 x}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{3}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{3x}$ contains the factor $3$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $3$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{3x}}{3}$.
