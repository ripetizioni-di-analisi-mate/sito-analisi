Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\left(4 x + 9\right)^{5}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\left(4 x + 9\right)^{6}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\left(4 x + 9\right)^{5}}{20}$
- $\dfrac{\left(4 x + 9\right)^{6}}{24}$
- $\dfrac{\left(4 x + 9\right)^{6}}{6}$

## Explanation

The linear argument has a constant derivative. Integrate the power and also divide by the coefficient of $x$. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
