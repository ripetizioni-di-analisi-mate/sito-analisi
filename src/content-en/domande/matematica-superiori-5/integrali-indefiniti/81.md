Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{4 x + 9}{x + 5}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $4 x - 11 \ln{\left(x + 5 \right)}$
- $4 x$
- $4 x + 9 \ln{\left(x + 5 \right)}$
- $4 x + 29 \ln{\left(x + 5 \right)}$

## Explanation

Write the numerator as $a(x+c)+b-ac$. Integrate the constant term and the remaining fraction separately. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
