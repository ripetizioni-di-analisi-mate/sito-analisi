Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{7}{x}$ for $x>0$?

- $7 \log{(x )}$
- $\dfrac{\log{(x )}}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{x}$
- $\dfrac{7}{x + 1}$

## Explanation

For $x>0$, the derivative of $\ln x$ is $\dfrac{1}{x}$. Multiplying by $7$ gives the integrand. Thus, an antiderivative is $7\ln x$.
