Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 4}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(2 x \right)}}{2}$
- $2 \operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{2} \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{2} \right)}}{2}$
- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{2} \right)}$

## Explanation

Factor the square of $a$ out of the denominator. The substitution $u=x/a$ reduces the problem to finding an antiderivative of $1/(1+u^2)$. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
