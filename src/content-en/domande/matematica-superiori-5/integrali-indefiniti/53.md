A pump supplies $2$ litres per minute until $t=3$, then $5$ litres per minute. The tank is initially empty. What formula gives its volume $V(t)$ after the rate change?

- $V(t)=6+5(t-3)$
- $V(t)=6+5t$
- $V(t)=6+2(t-3)$
- $V(t)=5t$

## Explanation

The first three minutes add $2\cdot3=6$ litres. Integrate the new rate only over the time elapsed since the change, which is $t-3$.
