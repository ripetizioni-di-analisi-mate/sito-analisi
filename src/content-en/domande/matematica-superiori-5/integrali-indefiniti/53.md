Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{x^{2} + 7}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(x^{2} + 7 \right)}}{2}$
- $2 \ln{\left(x^{2} + 7 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 x^{2} + 14}$
- $\ln{\left(x^{2} + 7 \right)}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the denominator is $2x$. Take the logarithm of the denominator and account for the factor of two. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
