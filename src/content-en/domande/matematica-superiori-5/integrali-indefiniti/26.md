A student integrates $p(x)=2f(x)-3g(x)$ term by term on an interval. How many independent real constants does the resulting family need?

- $1$
- $0$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The separate constants occur only through $2c_1-3c_2$. This combination can take any real value, so a single arbitrary constant suffices.
