Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=7x$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $7 x^{2}$
- $\dfrac{7 x^{2}}{2}$
- $7 x$
- $\dfrac{7 x}{2}$

## Explanation

The power rule gives $\int x\,dx=\dfrac{x^2}{2}+C$. Multiply by the coefficient $7$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{7}{2}x^2$ and its derivative is $7x$.
