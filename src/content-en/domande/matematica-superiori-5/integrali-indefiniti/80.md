Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{3 x + 7}{x + 4}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $3 x$
- $3 x + 19 \ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}$
- $3 x + 7 \ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}$
- $3 x - 5 \ln{\left(x + 4 \right)}$

## Explanation

Write the numerator as $a(x+c)+b-ac$. Integrate the constant term and the remaining fraction separately. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
