On $(0,\pi/2)$, the tangent is positive. Using the derivative of $\ln(\tan x)$, which logarithmic expression is an antiderivative of $\dfrac{1}{\sin x\cos x}$?

- $\ln(\sin x)+\ln(\cos x)$
- $-\ln(\tan x)$
- $\ln(\sin x)-\ln(\cos x)$
- $\ln(1+\tan x)$

## Explanation

The logarithm of $\sin x/\cos x$ is the difference of the two logarithms. Its derivative is $\cos x/\sin x+\sin x/\cos x$, which simplifies to $1/(\sin x\cos x)$.
