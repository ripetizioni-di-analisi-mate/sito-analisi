Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=x e^{4 x}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\left(4 x - 1\right) e^{4 x}}{16}$
- $\dfrac{\left(4 x + 1\right) e^{4 x}}{16}$
- $\dfrac{e^{4 x}}{16}$
- $\dfrac{x e^{4 x}}{4}$

## Explanation

In integration by parts, differentiate $x$ and integrate the exponential. From the first product, subtract the remaining integral of an exponential function. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
