Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=6x$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $3 x^{2}$
- $6 x^{2}$
- $6 x$
- $3 x$

## Explanation

The power rule gives $\int x\,dx=\dfrac{x^2}{2}+C$. Multiply by the coefficient $6$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{6}{2}x^2$ and its derivative is $6x$.
