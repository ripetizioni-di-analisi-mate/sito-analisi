A continuous function $f$ is odd. If $F'=f$ and $F(0)=0$, how are the values of $F$ at $x$ and $-x$ related?

- $F(-x)=F(x)+1$
- $F(-x)=-F(x)$ for every such $f$
- $F(-x)=F(x)$
- $F(-x)=0$ for every $x$

## Explanation

The derivative of $F(-x)-F(x)$ is $-f(-x)-f(x)=0$. The difference is constant and is zero at $x=0$, so $F$ is even.
