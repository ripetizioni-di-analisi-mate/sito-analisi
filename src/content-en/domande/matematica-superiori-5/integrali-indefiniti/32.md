Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{8x}$?

- $8 e^{8 x}$
- $e^{8 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{e^{8 x}}{8}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{8x}$ contains the factor $8$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $8$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{8x}}{8}$.
