Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{2 x + 5}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{2}$
- $\ln{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4 x + 10}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the logarithm of the argument is the reciprocal of the argument times the coefficient of $x$. To obtain the integrand, divide by that coefficient. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
