In a partial fraction decomposition of a proper rational function, what numerator form is needed for the irreducible factor $x^2+1$ occurring once?

- $\dfrac{Ax^2+B}{x^2+1}$
- $\dfrac{Ax+B}{x^2+1}$
- $\dfrac{A}{(x+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac{A}{x}+\dfrac{B}{x+1}$

## Explanation

A quadratic factor takes a numerator of degree less than two. Its linear term can yield a logarithm and its constant term an inverse tangent.
