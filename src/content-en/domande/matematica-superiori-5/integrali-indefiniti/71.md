Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=x^{2} e^{x^{3} + 7}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $3 e^{x^{3} + 7}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x^{3} + 7}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{x^{3} e^{x^{3} + 7}}{3}$
- $e^{x^{3} + 7}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $x^3+b$ is $3x^2$. To account for the factor of three, divide the exponential by three. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
