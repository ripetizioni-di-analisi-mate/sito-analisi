A table states that $F'(x)>0$ throughout $[1,4]$. Which pair of prescribed values is inconsistent with this information?

- $F(1)=2,\ F(4)=9$
- $F(1)=-4,\ F(4)=-1$
- $F(1)=0,\ F(4)=1$
- $F(1)=3,\ F(4)=2$

## Explanation

A positive derivative makes $F$ strictly increasing. Its value at the right endpoint must therefore exceed its value at the left endpoint.
