Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\left(3 x + 7\right)^{4}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\left(3 x + 7\right)^{5}}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\left(3 x + 7\right)^{4}}{12}$
- $\dfrac{\left(3 x + 7\right)^{5}}{15}$
- $\dfrac{\left(3 x + 7\right)^{5}}{3}$

## Explanation

The linear argument has a constant derivative. Integrate the power and also divide by the coefficient of $x$. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
