For $f(x)=1+\cos x$, by how much does an antiderivative increase as $x$ goes from $a$ to $a+2\pi$?

- $2\pi$
- $\sin a$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Every antiderivative is $x+\sin x+c$. The sine values at points $2\pi$ apart are equal, while the linear term increases by $2\pi$.
