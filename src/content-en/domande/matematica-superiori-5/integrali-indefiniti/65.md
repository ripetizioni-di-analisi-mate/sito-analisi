Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 9}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(3 x \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{3} \right)}}{3}$
- $3 \operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{3} \right)}$
- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(\dfrac{x}{3} \right)}$

## Explanation

Factor the square of $a$ out of the denominator. The substitution $u=x/a$ reduces the problem to finding an antiderivative of $1/(1+u^2)$. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
