An antiderivative $F$ of $x^2-1$ passes through $(1,0)$. At which other input does this **same** antiderivative vanish?

- $-1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Integration gives $F(x)=\dfrac{x^3}{3}-x+C$. The given point sets $C=\dfrac23$. Thus $3F(x)=x^3-3x+2=(x-1)^2(x+2)$, giving the other zero at $-2$.
