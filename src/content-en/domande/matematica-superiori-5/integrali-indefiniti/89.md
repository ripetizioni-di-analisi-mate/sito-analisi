The functions $F(x)=\ln x+3$ and $G(x)=\ln x-7$ are defined on $(0,+\infty)$. What can we say about their derivatives?

- They are equal at every point in the domain.
- They have opposite signs at every point in the domain.
- They differ by the constant $10$.
- They are equal only at $x=1$.

## Explanation

The difference between the two functions is the constant $10$. Its derivative is zero, so both functions have derivative $\dfrac{1}{x}$.
