For $x<0$, what family results from evaluating $\int\sqrt{x^2}\,dx$ on this interval?

- $\dfrac{x^2}{2}+c$
- $-x+c$
- $-\dfrac{x^2}{2}+c$
- $-\dfrac{x^3}{3}+c$

## Explanation

A square root is nonnegative, so $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|=-x$ on the negative half-line. Integrating $-x$ gives $-x^2/2+c$.
