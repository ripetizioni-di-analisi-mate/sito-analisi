Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{3 x + 7}$ on $(3,+\infty)$?

- $- \dfrac{\ln{\left(3 x + 7 \right)}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9 x + 21}$
- $\ln{\left(3 x + 7 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 x + 7 \right)}}{3}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the logarithm of the argument is the reciprocal of the argument times the coefficient of $x$. To obtain the integrand, divide by that coefficient. Here $a=3$, $b=7$, and $c=4$.
