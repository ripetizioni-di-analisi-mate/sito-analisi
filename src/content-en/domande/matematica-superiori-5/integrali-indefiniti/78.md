Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^{2} + 5}}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $2 \sqrt{x^{2} + 5}$
- $\sqrt{x^{2} + 5}$
- $x \sqrt{x^{2} + 5}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{x^{2} + 5}}{2}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the square root is the derivative of the radicand divided by twice the square root. The factor of two cancels. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
