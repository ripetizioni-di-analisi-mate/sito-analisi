Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}$ with value zero at $x=1$?

- $\log{\left(x^{2} + 1 \right)}$
- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(x \right)} + \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(x \right)}$
- $\operatorname{atan}{\left(x \right)} - \dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The general antiderivative is $\arctan x+C$. Since $\arctan1=\pi/4$, the constant must be the negative of that value.
