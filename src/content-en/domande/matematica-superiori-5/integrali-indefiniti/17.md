Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{5x}$?

- $\dfrac{e^{5 x}}{5}$
- $5 e^{5 x}$
- $e^{5 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{5}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{5x}$ contains the factor $5$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $5$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{5x}}{5}$.
