Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=x \cos{\left(2 x \right)}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\sin{\left(2 x \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{x \sin{\left(2 x \right)}}{2} - \dfrac{\cos{\left(2 x \right)}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{x \sin{\left(2 x \right)}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{x \sin{\left(2 x \right)}}{2} + \dfrac{\cos{\left(2 x \right)}}{4}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, integrating the cosine and differentiating $x$. The remaining term is the integral of the sine with a minus sign in front. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
