Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=x e^{2 x}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\left(2 x - 1\right) e^{2 x}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{e^{2 x}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{x e^{2 x}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\left(2 x + 1\right) e^{2 x}}{4}$

## Explanation

In integration by parts, differentiate $x$ and integrate the exponential. From the first product, subtract the remaining integral of an exponential function. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
