Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\left(2 x + 5\right)^{3}$ on $(2,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{\left(2 x + 5\right)^{4}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\left(2 x + 5\right)^{4}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\left(2 x + 5\right)^{3}}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\left(2 x + 5\right)^{4}}{2}$

## Explanation

The linear argument has a constant derivative. Integrate the power and also divide by the coefficient of $x$. Here $a=2$, $b=5$, and $c=3$.
