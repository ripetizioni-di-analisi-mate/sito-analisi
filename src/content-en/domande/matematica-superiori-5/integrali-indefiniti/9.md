Evaluate $\displaystyle\int x\,e^{-x}\,dx$.

- $-\dfrac{x^2}{2}e^{-x}+c$
- $-(x+1)e^{-x}+c$
- $-(x-1)e^{-x}+c$
- $(x+1)e^{-x}+c$

## Explanation

For integration by parts, differentiate $x$ and integrate $e^{-x}$. This gives $-xe^{-x}+\int e^{-x}\,dx$. The last integral also contributes a minus sign.
