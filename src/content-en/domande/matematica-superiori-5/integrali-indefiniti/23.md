What is the integral of $\dfrac{\cos x}{1 + \sin^2 x}$ with respect to $x$?

- $\cos x \arctan(\sin x) + c$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} \ln\left( 1 + \sin^2 x \right) + c$
- $\arctan(\sin x) + c$
- $\ln\left( 1 + \sin^2 x \right) + c$

## Explanation

The integral is reducible to the standard integral
$$
\int \frac{f'(x)}{1 + [f(x)]^2}\, dx = \arctan f(x) + c
$$
with $f(x) = \sin x$, hence the result is $\arctan(\sin x) + c$.
