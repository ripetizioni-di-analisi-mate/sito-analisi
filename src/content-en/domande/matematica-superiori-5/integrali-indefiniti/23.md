Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\cos(6x)$?

- $6 \sin{(6 x )}$
- $- \dfrac{\sin{(6 x )}}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{(6 x )}}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\cos{(6 x )}}{6}$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $6\cos(6x)$ as the derivative of the sine of the inner function. Therefore, divide by $6$. The derivative of $\dfrac{\sin(6x)}{6}$ equals the integrand.
