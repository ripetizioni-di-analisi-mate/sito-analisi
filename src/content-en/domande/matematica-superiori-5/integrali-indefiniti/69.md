Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}$ on $(4,+\infty)$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{2 \left(x - 4\right)^{2}}$
- $\ln{\left(x - 4 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 4}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{x - 4}$

## Explanation

Integrate the power with exponent $-2$. Increase the exponent by one and divide by the new exponent, which is $-1$. Here $a=4$, $b=9$, and $c=5$.
