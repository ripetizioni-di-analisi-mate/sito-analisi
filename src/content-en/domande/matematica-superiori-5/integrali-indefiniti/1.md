Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \left( 5x^4 - 3x^2 + 2x - 7 \right) dx$.

- $5x^5-3x^3+2x^2-7x+c$
- $x^5-x^3+x^2-7+c$
- $20x^3-6x+2+c$
- $x^5-x^3+x^2-7x+c$

## Explanation

Integrate each monomial by increasing its exponent by one and dividing by the new exponent. The constant contributes too, since an antiderivative of $-7$ is $-7x$.
