What is $\displaystyle\int \left( 5x^4 - 3x^2 + 2x - 7 \right) dx$?

- $5x^5-3x^3+2x^2-7x+c$
- $x^5-x^3+x^2-7+c$
- $20x^3-6x+2+c$
- $x^5-x^3+x^2-7x+c$

## Explanation

By linearity of the integral, each monomial is integrated separately. Since $\displaystyle\int x^n\,dx = \dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + c$ for $n \neq -1$, the first three monomials give $x^5$, $-x^3$ and $x^2$. An antiderivative of the constant $-7$ is $-7x$, so the result is $x^5 - x^3 + x^2 - 7x + c$.
