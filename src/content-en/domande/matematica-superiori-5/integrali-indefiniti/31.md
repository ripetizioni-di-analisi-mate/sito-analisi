Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=8x$ on $\mathbb R$?

- $8 x^{2}$
- $8 x$
- $4 x^{2}$
- $4 x$

## Explanation

The power rule gives $\int x\,dx=\dfrac{x^2}{2}+C$. Multiply by the coefficient $8$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{8}{2}x^2$ and its derivative is $8x$.
