The functions $F(x)=\ln x+4$ and $G(x)=\ln x-9$ are defined on $(0,+\infty)$. What can we say about their derivatives?

- They are equal only at $x=1$.
- They differ by the constant $13$.
- They are equal at every point in the domain.
- They have opposite signs at every point in the domain.

## Explanation

The difference between the two functions is the constant $13$. Its derivative is zero, so both functions have derivative $\dfrac{1}{x}$.
