Evaluate $\displaystyle\int x^2 \ln x\,dx$ for $x > 0$.

- $\dfrac{x^3}{3}\ln x+\dfrac{x^3}{9}+c$
- $\dfrac{x^3}{3}\ln x-\dfrac{x^2}{6}+c$
- $\dfrac{x^3}{3}\ln x-\dfrac{x^3}{9}+c$
- $x^3\ln x-\dfrac{x^3}{3}+c$

## Explanation

Differentiate $\ln x$ and integrate $x^2$ in the integration-by-parts formula. From $\dfrac{x^3}{3}\ln x$, subtract $\int\dfrac{x^2}{3}\,dx=\dfrac{x^3}{9}$.
