Suppose $F'(x)=f(x)$. For $a\ne0$, which expression has derivative $x f(ax^2+b)$?

- $\dfrac{F(x)}{2a}$
- $\dfrac{F(ax^2+b)}{2a}$
- $2aF(ax^2+b)$
- $\dfrac{F(ax^2+b)}{2ax}$

## Explanation

The inner function has derivative $2ax$. Dividing by $2a$ leaves the required factor $x$.
