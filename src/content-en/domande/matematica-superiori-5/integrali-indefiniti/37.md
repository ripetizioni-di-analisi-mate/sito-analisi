Which function is an antiderivative of $f(x)=e^{9x}$?

- $\dfrac{e^{9 x}}{9}$
- $9 e^{9 x}$
- $e^{9 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{x}}{9}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $e^{9x}$ contains the factor $9$. To account for it, divide the antiderivative by $9$. Thus, an antiderivative is $\dfrac{e^{9x}}{9}$.
