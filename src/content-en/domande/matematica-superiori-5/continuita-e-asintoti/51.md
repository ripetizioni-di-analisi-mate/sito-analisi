Consider $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-4)(x+5)}{(x-4)(x+9)}$ on its domain. What type of discontinuity does it have at $x=4$?

- A discontinuity with just one infinite one-sided limit.
- A discontinuity with two infinite one-sided limits.
- Removable.
- A jump discontinuity.

## Explanation

Canceling the common factor gives a finite limit of $\dfrac{9}{13}$. Since $f(4)$ is undefined, assigning it this value makes the function continuous there.
