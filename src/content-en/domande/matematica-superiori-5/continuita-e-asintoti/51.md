As $x \to -\infty$ the function $f(x)=\dfrac{2 x}{\sqrt{x^{2} + 5}}$ has a horizontal asymptote. What is its $y$-coordinate?

- $-2$
- $0$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

For $x<0$ one has $\sqrt{x^2+5}=-x\sqrt{1+\dfrac{5}{x^2}}$, so $f(x)=-\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1+\dfrac{5}{x^2}}}$. Since $\sqrt{1+\dfrac{5}{x^2}}\to 1$ as $x\to-\infty$, the function tends to $-2$, and the horizontal asymptote on the left is $y=-2$.
