The graph of $f$ has asymptotes $x=2$ and $y=-1$. What are the corresponding asymptotes of $g(x)=f(x-3)+4$?

- $x=5,\ y=-5$
- $x=-1,\ y=-5$
- $x=5,\ y=3$
- $x=-1,\ y=3$

## Explanation

Solving $x-3=2$ moves the vertical asymptote to $x=5$. Adding $4$ shifts the horizontal level from $-1$ to $3$.
