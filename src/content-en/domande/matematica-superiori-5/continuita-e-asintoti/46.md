For $x<2$ we have $f(x)=(x+5)^2+3$. For $x\geq 2$ we have $f(x)=kx$. What value of $k$ makes $f$ continuous at $x=2$?

- $104$
- $26$
- $52$
- $\dfrac{52}{5}$

## Explanation

The left-hand limit is $52$. The value of the right-hand branch is $2k$, so we set these equal. This gives $26$.
