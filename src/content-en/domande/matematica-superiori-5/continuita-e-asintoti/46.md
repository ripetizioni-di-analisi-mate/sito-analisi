For $x>0$, let $f(x)=2+\sin x/x$. How many times does the graph meet its horizontal asymptote $y=2$?

- Infinitely many times.
- Never.
- Once.
- Exactly twice.

## Explanation

The difference vanishes wherever $\sin x=0$, at every positive multiple of $\pi$. Also $|\sin x/x|\le1/x\to0$, confirming the asymptote.
