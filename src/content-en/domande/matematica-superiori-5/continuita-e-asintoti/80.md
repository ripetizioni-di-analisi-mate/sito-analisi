On which set is the function $f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x - 1}}{x - 3}$ continuous?

- $[1, +\infty)$
- $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{3\}$
- $[1, 3) \cup (3, +\infty)$
- $(1, 3) \cup (3, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The square root $\sqrt{x - 1}$ is defined and continuous for $x \geq 1$, and the denominator $x - 3$ is a polynomial, so it is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$. A quotient of continuous functions is continuous where the denominator does not vanish. The domain is therefore given by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 1 \geq 0 \\
x - 3 \neq 0
\end{cases}
$$
that is $[1, 3) \cup (3, +\infty)$. Elementary functions are continuous on their domain, therefore $f$ is continuous on all of $[1, 3) \cup (3, +\infty)$, including the point $x = 1$, where the right-hand limit is used.
