Find the value of the real parameter $k$ for which the function
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
2kx + 1 & \text{if } x < 1 \\
k x^2 - 3 & \text{if } x \geq 1
\end{cases}
$$
is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$.

- $k=4$
- $k=-2$
- $k=2$
- $k=-4$

## Explanation

Each formula is continuous on its side. At $1$ equate the left limit $2k+1$ to the right-hand value $k-3$. The equation gives $k=-4$.
