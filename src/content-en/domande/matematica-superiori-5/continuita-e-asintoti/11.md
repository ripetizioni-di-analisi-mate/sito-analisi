For which value of the real parameter $k$ is the function
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
2kx + 1 & \text{if } x < 1 \\
k x^2 - 3 & \text{if } x \geq 1
\end{cases}
$$
continuous on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $k=4$
- $k=-2$
- $k=2$
- $k=-4$

## Explanation

Each formula is continuous on its side. Therefore $f$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$ if at $x=1$ the left limit $2k+1$ equals the value $f(1)=k-3$. The equation $2k+1=k-3$ gives $k=-4$.
