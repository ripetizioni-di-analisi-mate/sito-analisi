What is the x-coordinate of the vertical asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-4}$?

- $-4$
- $0$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The denominator is zero at $x=4$, and the numerator remains nonzero. As the input approaches that point, the absolute value of the function grows without bound. Therefore, the vertical asymptote has equation $x=4$.
