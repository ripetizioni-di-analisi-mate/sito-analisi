What is the x-coordinate of the vertical asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-5}$?

- $-5$
- $0$
- $1$
- $5$

## Explanation

The denominator is zero at $x=5$, and the numerator remains nonzero. As the input approaches that point, the absolute value of the function grows without bound. Therefore, the vertical asymptote has equation $x=5$.
