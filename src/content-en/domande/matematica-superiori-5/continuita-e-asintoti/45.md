During bisection of a continuous function, the value at the midpoint is exactly zero. What follows?

- The left half must be kept.
- An exact root has been found.
- The midpoint is a vertical asymptote.
- The function has two distinct roots.

## Explanation

A point with $f(m)=0$ already satisfies the equation that bisection is solving. No more interval halvings are needed to locate that root.
