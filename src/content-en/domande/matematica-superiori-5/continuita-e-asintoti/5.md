The argument of $\arctan(1/x)$ diverges on either side of zero. What happens to the values of the function itself?

- They oscillate without a limit on either side.
- Both tend to zero.
- Both diverge to $+\infty$.
- They have opposite finite limits $-\pi/2$ and $\pi/2$.

## Explanation

The inverse tangent approaches $-\pi/2$ as its argument tends to $-\infty$ and $\pi/2$ as the argument tends to $+\infty$. The result is a finite jump.
