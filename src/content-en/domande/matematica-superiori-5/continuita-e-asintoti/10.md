Which pair gives the oblique asymptotes of $\sqrt{x^2-2x+5}$, first on the right and then on the left?

- $y=x,\ y=-x$
- $y=x-1,\ y=x-1$
- $y=x-1,\ y=-x+1$
- $y=x+1,\ y=-x-1$

## Explanation

The radicand is $(x-1)^2+4$. The difference between the square root and $|x-1|$ is $4/(\sqrt{(x-1)^2+4}+|x-1|)$ and tends to zero at either end.
