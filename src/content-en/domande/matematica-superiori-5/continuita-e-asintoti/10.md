The function equals $1$ for $x<3$ and $3$ for $x\geq3$. What type of discontinuity does it have at $x=3$?

- A removable discontinuity
- A jump discontinuity
- A discontinuity with an infinite limit
- No discontinuity

## Explanation

The left-hand limit is $1$ and the right-hand limit is $3$. The two limits are finite but unequal. Therefore, at $x=3$ there is a jump discontinuity.
