What value must be assigned to $f(4)$ for continuity at $x=4$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4}$ for $x\ne4$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $16$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $x\ne4$ the function equals $x+4$. The limit as $x\to4$ is therefore $8$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
