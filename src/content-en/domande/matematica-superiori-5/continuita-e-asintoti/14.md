After bracketing $\sqrt7$ in $[2.625,2.75]$, try $r=2.65$. Given $r^2-7=0.0225$, why is the error $|r-\sqrt7|$ less than $0.005$?

- The error is the squared residual for every $r$.
- The residual must be multiplied by $r+\sqrt7$.
- Divide the residual by $r+\sqrt7$, which exceeds $5$.
- The residual equals the error in $r$.

## Explanation

The difference of squares is $(r-\sqrt7)(r+\sqrt7)$. Hence $|r-\sqrt7|=0.0225/(r+\sqrt7)<0.0225/5=0.0045$.
