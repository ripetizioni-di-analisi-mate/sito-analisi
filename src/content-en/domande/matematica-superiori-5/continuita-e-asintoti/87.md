For $x\ne 4$ we are given $f(x)=\dfrac{\sin(9(x-4))}{x-4}$. What value should be assigned to $f(4)$ to obtain a continuous extension?

- $1$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $0$

## Explanation

With $u=9(x-4)$ the quotient becomes $9\sin(u)/u$. The second factor approaches one. This gives $9$.
