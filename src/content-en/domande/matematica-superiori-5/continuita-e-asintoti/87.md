A continuous function is positive at both endpoints of $[-1,1]$. Which example shows that it can still vanish without a sign change?

- $f(x)=x^2+1$
- $f(x)=x^2$
- $f(x)=e^x$
- $f(x)=2-x$

## Explanation

The square is positive at the endpoints and zero at the origin, where the graph touches the axis. Opposite endpoint signs are a sufficient condition for an interior zero.
