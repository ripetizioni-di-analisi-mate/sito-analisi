For $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x^2-3x}$, at which excluded point can a finite value be assigned to restore continuity?

- $x=3$
- $x=0$
- At neither point.
- At both $0$ and $3$.

## Explanation

Cancel $x-3$ to obtain $(x+3)/x$. This tends to $2$ as $x\to3$, whereas at zero the denominator tends to zero and the numerator tends to $3$.
