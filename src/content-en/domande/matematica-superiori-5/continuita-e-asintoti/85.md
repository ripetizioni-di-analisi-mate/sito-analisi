For $x\ne 2$ we are given $f(x)=\dfrac{\sin(5(x-2))}{x-2}$. What value should be assigned to $f(2)$ to obtain a continuous extension?

- $1$
- $5$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

With $u=5(x-2)$ the quotient becomes $5\sin(u)/u$. The second factor approaches one. This gives $5$.
