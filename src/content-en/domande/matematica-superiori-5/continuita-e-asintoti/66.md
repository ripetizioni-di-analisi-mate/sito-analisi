Near zero let $f(x)=\sin(1/x)$. A densely oscillating plot can look like a vertical line. Which fact rules out a vertical asymptote at zero?

- The function is odd.
- The height always stays between $-1$ and $1$.
- The domain excludes zero.
- The function is defined for every $x\ne0$.

## Explanation

A vertical asymptote requires at least one infinite one-sided limit. A height bounded in magnitude by $1$ cannot tend to either infinity.
