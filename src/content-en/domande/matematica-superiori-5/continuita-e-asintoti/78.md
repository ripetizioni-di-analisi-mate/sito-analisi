A zero is located by bisection starting from an interval of length $72$. How long is the interval after it has been halved three times?

- $9$
- $18$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

At each step only one half is kept. After three steps the original length has been divided by $2^3$. This gives $9$.
