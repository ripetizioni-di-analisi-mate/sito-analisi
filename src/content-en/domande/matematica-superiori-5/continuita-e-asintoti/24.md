What value must be assigned to $f(6)$ for continuity at $x=6$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-36}{x-6}$ for $x\ne6$?

- $6$
- $36$
- $0$
- $12$

## Explanation

For $x\ne6$ the function equals $x+6$. The limit as $x\to6$ is therefore $12$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
