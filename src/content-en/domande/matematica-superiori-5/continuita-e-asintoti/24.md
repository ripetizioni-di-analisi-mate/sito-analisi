How many vertical asymptotes does $\tan x$ have on the entire real line?

- Exactly two, at $x=\pm\pi/2$.
- One at every zero of the sine.
- None, because the function is periodic.
- Infinitely many, at $x=\pi/2+k\pi$ for integer $k$.

## Explanation

Tangent is the quotient of sine by cosine. Every zero of cosine is simple, and sine equals $1$ or $-1$ there, giving a pole.
