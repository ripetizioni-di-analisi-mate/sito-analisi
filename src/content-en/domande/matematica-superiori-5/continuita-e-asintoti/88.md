A function $g$ has left limit $-2$ and right limit $2$ at zero, with $g(0)=2$. Which composition must be continuous at zero?

- $g(x)+1$
- $e^{g(x)}$
- $g(x)^2$
- $g(x)^3$

## Explanation

Squaring sends both one-sided limits to $4$, which also equals $g(0)^2$. The other transformations distinguish the two values.
