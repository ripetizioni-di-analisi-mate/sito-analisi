The floor function $f(x)=\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the greatest integer at most $x$. Where in $(0,3)$ is it continuous?

- Nowhere in $(0,3)$.
- Only at $1$ and $2$.
- Throughout $(0,3)$.
- At every point except $1$ and $2$.

## Explanation

Between consecutive integers the function is constant. Crossing an integer raises the value to the next integer, giving a unit jump.
