What is the y-coordinate of the asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{3 x^{3} + 7 x + 4}{x^{2} + 1}$ at the point with x-coordinate $-1$?

- $3$
- $1$
- $4$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Polynomial division gives the quotient $3x$ and a remainder that approaches zero when divided by $x^2+1$. Evaluate the line at $-1$. This gives $-3$.
