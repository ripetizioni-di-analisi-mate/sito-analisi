What value must be assigned to $f(7)$ for continuity at $x=7$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-49}{x-7}$ for $x\ne7$?

- $14$
- $7$
- $49$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $x\ne7$ the function equals $x+7$. The limit as $x\to7$ is therefore $14$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
