A thermostat reading jumps from $19$ to $21$. To replace the jump, use a line segment between $(0,19)$ and $(2,21)$. Which formula gives that continuous transition?

- $19+x$
- $21-x$
- $19+2x$
- $18+x$

## Explanation

The slope is $(21-19)/(2-0)=1$. The starting height is $19$, so $19+x$ matches the prescribed values at both ends.
