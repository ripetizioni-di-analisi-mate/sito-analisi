Consider $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-2)(x+3)}{(x-2)(x+5)}$ on its domain. What type of discontinuity does it have at $x=2$?

- Removable.
- A jump discontinuity.
- A discontinuity with just one infinite one-sided limit.
- A discontinuity with two infinite one-sided limits.

## Explanation

Canceling the common factor gives a finite limit of $\dfrac{5}{7}$. Since $f(2)$ is undefined, assigning it this value makes the function continuous there.
