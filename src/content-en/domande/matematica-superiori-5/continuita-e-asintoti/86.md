For $x\ne 3$ we are given $f(x)=\dfrac{\sin(7(x-3))}{x-3}$. What value should be assigned to $f(3)$ to obtain a continuous extension?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $1$
- $7$

## Explanation

With $u=7(x-3)$ the quotient becomes $7\sin(u)/u$. The second factor approaches one. This gives $7$.
