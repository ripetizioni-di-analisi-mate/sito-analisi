A continuous function has horizontal asymptote $y=3$ as $x\to+\infty$ and $f(0)=5$. What must follow?

- There is some $x>0$ with $f(x)=3$
- There is some $x>0$ with $f(x)=4$
- The function decreases throughout $[0,+\infty)$
- The graph cannot intersect $y=3$

## Explanation

For sufficiently large $x$, $f(x)$ is within $\dfrac12$ of $3$ and hence below $4$. Continuity then guarantees the intermediate value $4$ between zero and such a point.
