As $x \to \pm\infty$ the graph of $f(x) = x \, e^{\frac{1}{x}}$ approaches a line. Which one?

- $y = x - 1$
- $y = x + 1$
- $y = x$
- $y = x + e$

## Explanation

The slope is $m = \displaystyle\lim_{x \to \pm\infty} \frac{f(x)}{x} = \lim_{x \to \pm\infty} e^{\frac{1}{x}} = e^{0} = 1$.

The intercept is the limit of $f(x) - x = x \left( e^{\frac{1}{x}} - 1 \right)$. With $t = \dfrac{1}{x} \to 0$ we obtain
$$
q = \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{e^{t} - 1}{t} = 1
$$
by the **standard limit** of the exponential. Therefore $y = x + 1$ is an oblique asymptote both as $x \to +\infty$ and as $x \to -\infty$.
