What is the x-coordinate of the only vertical asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-4)(x+9)}{(x-4)(x-5)^2}$?

- $-5$
- $-9$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

At $4$ the factor in the numerator cancels the one in the denominator. At $5$, however, the denominator still approaches zero while the numerator is nonzero. This gives $5$.
