The bisection method is applied to a continuous function starting from the interval $[0, 1]$. After how many steps, at least, does the resulting interval have length less than $0.01$?

- $100$
- $10$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

At each step the interval is halved, so after $n$ steps its length is $\dfrac{1}{2^{n}}$. The condition $\dfrac{1}{2^{n}} < 0.01$ is equivalent to $2^{n} > 100$. Since $2^{6} = 64$ and $2^{7} = 128$, the smallest $n$ satisfying it is $7$, and after $7$ steps the length is $\dfrac{1}{128}$.
