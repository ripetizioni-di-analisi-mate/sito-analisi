What value must be assigned to $f(10)$ for continuity at $x=10$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-100}{x-10}$ for $x\ne10$?

- $10$
- $100$
- $0$
- $20$

## Explanation

For $x\ne10$ the function equals $x+10$. The limit as $x\to10$ is therefore $20$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
