What is the oblique asymptote as $x\to+\infty$ for the function $f(x)=x+9+\dfrac{1}{x}$?

- $y=x - 9$
- $y=x + 9$
- $y=9 x$
- $y=x + 10$

## Explanation

Compare $f(x)$ with the line $y=x+9$. Their difference is $\dfrac{1}{x}$, which approaches zero. Therefore, the oblique asymptote is $y=x+9$.
