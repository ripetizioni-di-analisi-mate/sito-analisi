A continuous reading $T(t)$ on $0\le t\le10$ starts at $18$ and ends at $25$ degrees. Once we know it reaches $20$, why is there a *first* time at which it reaches that value?

- The reading must be linear.
- The first time must be the midpoint of the interval.
- Every continuous reading is strictly increasing.
- The set of times with $T(t)=20$ is closed, bounded and nonempty.

## Explanation

If times with reading $20$ converge to $t_*$, continuity gives $T(t_*)=20$ as well. The set therefore contains its infimum in $[0,10]$, which is the first time required.
