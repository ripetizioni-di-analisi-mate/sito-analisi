What is the y-coordinate of the asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{4 x^{3} + 9 x + 5}{x^{2} + 1}$ at the point with x-coordinate $-1$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $1$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Polynomial division gives the quotient $4x$ and a remainder that approaches zero when divided by $x^2+1$. Evaluate the line at $-1$. This gives $-4$.
