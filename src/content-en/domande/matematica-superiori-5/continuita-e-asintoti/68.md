What is the x-coordinate of the only vertical asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-3)(x+7)}{(x-3)(x-4)^2}$?

- $-7$
- $3$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

At $3$ the factor in the numerator cancels the one in the denominator. At $4$, however, the denominator still approaches zero while the numerator is nonzero. This gives $4$.
