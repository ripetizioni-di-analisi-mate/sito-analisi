A continuous periodic function on $\mathbb R$ has horizontal asymptote $y=L$ as $x\to+\infty$. What follows?

- Its least period is $L$.
- It has exactly one zero.
- It is identically equal to $L$.
- It must be strictly increasing.

## Explanation

For a period $T>0$ and any $a$, all values $f(a+nT)$ equal $f(a)$. Since they must tend to $L$, this forces $f(a)=L$ for every $a$.
