The function equals $1$ for $x<8$ and $3$ for $x\geq8$. What type of discontinuity does it have at $x=8$?

- A removable discontinuity
- A discontinuity with an infinite limit
- A jump discontinuity
- No discontinuity

## Explanation

The left-hand limit is $1$ and the right-hand limit is $3$. The two limits are finite but unequal. Therefore, at $x=8$ there is a jump discontinuity.
