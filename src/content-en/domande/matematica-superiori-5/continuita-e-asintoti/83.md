How many zeros does $f(x)=\ln(x)+3x-7$ have on $(0,+\infty)$?

- $0$
- $3$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Near zero the logarithm approaches $-\infty$, while as $x\to+\infty$ the function approaches $+\infty$. Continuity guarantees at least one zero, and the positive derivative makes it unique. This gives $1$.
