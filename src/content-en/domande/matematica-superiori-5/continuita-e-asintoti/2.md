What is the y-coordinate of the horizontal asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{2x+1}{2x-3}$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator have the same degree. The limit at infinity is the ratio of the coefficients of $x$. Therefore, the horizontal asymptote has equation $y=\dfrac{2}{2}$.
