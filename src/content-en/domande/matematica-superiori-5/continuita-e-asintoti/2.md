A function equals $(e^{2x}-1)/x$ for $x\ne0$ and $2$ for $x=0$. Which fact establishes continuity at zero?

- The function is bounded far from zero.
- The denominator vanishes at zero.
- The numerator alone tends to zero.
- The ratio $(e^{2x}-1)/(2x)$ tends to $1$.

## Explanation

Write the ratio as $2(e^{2x}-1)/(2x)$ to get the limit $2$. The assigned function value equals this limit.
