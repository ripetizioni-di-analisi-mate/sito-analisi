For $x<3$ we have $f(x)=7-x$, and for $x\geq 3$ we have $f(x)=4+2x$. What is the right-hand limit minus the left-hand limit at $3$?

- $10$
- $-6$
- $6$
- $14$

## Explanation

The right-hand limit is $10$ and the left-hand limit is $4$. Subtract them in the specified order. This gives $6$.
