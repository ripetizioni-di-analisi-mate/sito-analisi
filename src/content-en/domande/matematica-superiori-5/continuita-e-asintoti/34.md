What value must be assigned to $f(8)$ for continuity at $x=8$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-64}{x-8}$ for $x\ne8$?

- $8$
- $16$
- $64$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $x\ne8$ the function equals $x+8$. The limit as $x\to8$ is therefore $16$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
