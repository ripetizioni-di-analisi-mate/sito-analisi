What is the x-coordinate of the only vertical asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-2)(x+5)}{(x-2)(x-3)^2}$?

- $3$
- $-5$
- $2$
- $-3$

## Explanation

At $2$ the factor in the numerator cancels the one in the denominator. At $3$, however, the denominator still approaches zero while the numerator is nonzero. This gives $3$.
