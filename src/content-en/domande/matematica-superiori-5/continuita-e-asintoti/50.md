Consider $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-3)(x+4)}{(x-3)(x+7)}$ on its domain. What type of discontinuity does it have at $x=3$?

- A discontinuity with just one infinite one-sided limit.
- Removable.
- A jump discontinuity.
- A discontinuity with two infinite one-sided limits.

## Explanation

Canceling the common factor gives a finite limit of $\dfrac{7}{10}$. Since $f(3)$ is undefined, assigning it this value makes the function continuous there.
