A program looks for the asymptote of $f(x)=x^2/(x+1)$ using only $f(x)/x$. What further information identifies the line?

- The number of zeros of the denominator.
- The value $f(0)$, which always gives the intercept.
- The maximum on $[0,1]$.
- The limit of $f(x)-x$, giving intercept $-1$.

## Explanation

The ratio tends to $1$, giving the slope. The difference $f(x)-x=-x/(x+1)$ tends to $-1$, so the line is $y=x-1$.
