A zero is located by bisection starting from an interval of length $56$. How long is the interval after it has been halved three times?

- $14$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $28$

## Explanation

At each step only one half is kept. After three steps the original length has been divided by $2^3$. This gives $7$.
