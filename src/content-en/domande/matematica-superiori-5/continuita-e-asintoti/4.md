What value must be assigned to $f(2)$ for continuity at $x=2$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}$ for $x\ne2$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

For $x\ne2$ the function equals $x+2$. The limit as $x\to2$ is therefore $4$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
