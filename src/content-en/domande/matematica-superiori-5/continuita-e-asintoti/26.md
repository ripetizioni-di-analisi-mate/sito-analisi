We want to repeat, with period $2$, the piece $f(x)=x^2+ax$ defined on $[0,2]$. Which value of $a$ makes the join between adjacent copies continuous?

- $a=2$
- $a=0$
- $a=-2$
- $a=-4$

## Explanation

The endpoints of adjacent copies meet. We need $f(2)=f(0)$, that is, $4+2a=0$. The polynomial is continuous inside each copy.
