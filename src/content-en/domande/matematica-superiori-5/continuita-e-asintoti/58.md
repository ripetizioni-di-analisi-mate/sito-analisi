A graph stays at vertical distance $1$ from the line $y=x$ for every $x>0$. Can $y=x$ be its asymptote as $x\to+\infty$?

- Yes, because the ratio of the heights tends to $1$.
- Yes, provided that the graph is continuous.
- Yes, because the distance is bounded.
- No, because the absolute difference does not tend to zero.

## Explanation

An oblique asymptote requires the difference between the function and the line to tend to zero. A constant distance of one rules this out even if the ratio of their heights tends to one.
