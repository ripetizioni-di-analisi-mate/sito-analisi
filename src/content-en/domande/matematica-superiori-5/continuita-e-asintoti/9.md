What value must be assigned to $f(3)$ for continuity at $x=3$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}$ for $x\ne3$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $9$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $x\ne3$ the function equals $x+3$. The limit as $x\to3$ is therefore $6$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
