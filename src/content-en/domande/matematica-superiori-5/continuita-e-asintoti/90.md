A positive continuous function on $x>0$ tends to $+\infty$ as $x\to0^+$. What value should its reciprocal $g=1/f$ be given at zero for right continuity?

- $g(0)=-1$
- Any positive value.
- $g(0)=0$
- $g(0)=1$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of a positive quantity growing without bound tends to zero. The added value must equal that limit.
