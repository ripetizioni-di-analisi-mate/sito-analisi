Does $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4}$ have a vertical asymptote at $x=4$?

- Yes, because the function is undefined at that point.
- Yes, because the right-hand limit is infinite.
- No, because the function equals zero at that point.
- No, because both one-sided limits are finite.

## Explanation

For $x\ne 4$ the function equals $x+4$, and its limit at $4$ is $8$. Therefore, the excluded point is a removable discontinuity.
