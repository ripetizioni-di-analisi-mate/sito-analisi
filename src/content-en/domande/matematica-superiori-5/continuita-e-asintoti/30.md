Bisection has bracketed a root in $[1.32,1.38]$. If the midpoint is used as the estimate, what is the sharpest error bound from these endpoints alone?

- $0.015$
- $0.03$
- $0.12$
- $0.06$

## Explanation

The midpoint is $1.35$ and is $0.03$ from either endpoint. The root lies within the interval and cannot be farther away.
