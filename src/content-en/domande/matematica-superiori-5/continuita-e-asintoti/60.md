Which lines are vertical asymptotes of the graph of the function $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{\ln x}$?

- $x = 0$ and $x = 1$
- Only $x = 0$
- Only $x = 1$
- No line

## Explanation

The function is defined for $x > 0$ and $\ln x \neq 0$, that is for $x > 0$ with $x \neq 1$. As $x \to 1^+$ we have $\ln x \to 0^+$, so $f(x) \to +\infty$, and as $x \to 1^-$ we have $f(x) \to -\infty$; therefore $x = 1$ is a **vertical asymptote**. As $x \to 0^+$ we have $\ln x \to -\infty$, so $f(x) \to 0$. The limit at $0^+$ is finite, therefore the line $x = 0$ is not a vertical asymptote.
