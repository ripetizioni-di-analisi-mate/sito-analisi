What is the y-coordinate of the horizontal asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{3 x + 7}{\left(x + 4\right)^{2}}$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{7}{16}$

## Explanation

The denominator has degree two and the numerator has degree one. The quotient approaches zero both to the right and to the left. This gives $0$.
