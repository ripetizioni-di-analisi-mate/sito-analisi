The graph of $f(x)=|x-a|$ has a corner at $a$. Which conclusion is correct?

- It has a jump of size $2$ at $a$.
- It is continuous at $a$ and the one-sided slopes differ.
- It has vertical asymptote $x=a$.
- The limit at $a$ does not exist.

## Explanation

The limit of $|x-a|$ is $0=f(a)$ on both sides. The slope changes from $-1$ to $1$, while the limiting function value stays the same.
