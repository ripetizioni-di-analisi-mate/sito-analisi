For $x<4$ we have $f(x)=(x+9)^2+5$. For $x\geq 4$ we have $f(x)=kx$. What value of $k$ makes $f$ continuous at $x=4$?

- $\dfrac{87}{2}$
- $\dfrac{58}{3}$
- $174$
- $696$

## Explanation

The left-hand limit is $174$. The value of the right-hand branch is $4k$, so we set these equal. This gives $\dfrac{87}{2}$.
