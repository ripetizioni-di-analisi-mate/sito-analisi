What is the y-coordinate of the asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{2 x^{3} + 5 x + 3}{x^{2} + 1}$ at the point with x-coordinate $-1$?

- $-2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Polynomial division gives the quotient $2x$ and a remainder that approaches zero when divided by $x^2+1$. Evaluate the line at $-1$. This gives $-2$.
