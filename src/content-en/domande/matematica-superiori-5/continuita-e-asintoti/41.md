As $x\to+\infty$, $f(x)-(2x-1)\to0$. What is the right-hand asymptote of $g(x)=3f(2x)$?

- $y=4x-3$
- $y=12x-1$
- $y=12x-3$
- $y=6x-1$

## Explanation

Substitute $2x$ in the asymptote of $f$ to get $4x-1$. Multiplication by $3$ gives $12x-3$, and three times the original difference still tends to zero.
