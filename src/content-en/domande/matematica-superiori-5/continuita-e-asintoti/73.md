Suppose $f$ is continuous in a neighbourhood of $a$ and $f(a)=4$. Which function must be defined and continuous throughout some neighbourhood of $a$?

- $\dfrac1{f(x)-4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{f(x)}}$
- $\ln(4-f(x))$
- $\dfrac1{x-a}$

## Explanation

Continuity allows the neighbourhood to be restricted so that $f(x)>2$. The square root is then positive, and its reciprocal is a composition of continuous functions.
