A function is defined on $\{0\}\cup[2,3]$. What condition must the assigned value $f(0)$ satisfy for continuity relative to its domain at $0$?

- It must be $f(2)$.
- It can be any real number.
- It must lie between $f(2)$ and $f(3)$.
- It must be zero.

## Explanation

In a neighbourhood of radius $1$ around zero, the only domain point is zero itself. The difference $|f(x)-f(0)|$ is then zero for any choice of $f(0)$.
