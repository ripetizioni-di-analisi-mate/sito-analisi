What are the left- and right-hand limits, in that order, of $f(x)=\dfrac{5}{(x-2)^2}$ at $2$?

- $(+\infty,-\infty)$
- $(+\infty,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator is a square that is positive on both sides and approaches zero. The numerator is a positive constant.
