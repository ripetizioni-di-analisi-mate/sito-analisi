For $x<3$ we have $f(x)=(x+7)^2+4$. For $x\geq 3$ we have $f(x)=kx$. What value of $k$ makes $f$ continuous at $x=3$?

- $\dfrac{104}{3}$
- $312$
- $\dfrac{104}{7}$
- $104$

## Explanation

The left-hand limit is $104$. The value of the right-hand branch is $3k$, so we set these equal. This gives $\dfrac{104}{3}$.
