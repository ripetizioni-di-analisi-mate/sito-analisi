What is the oblique asymptote as $x\to+\infty$ for the function $f(x)=x+10+\dfrac{1}{x}$?

- $y=x - 10$
- $y=10 x$
- $y=x + 10$
- $y=x + 11$

## Explanation

Compare $f(x)$ with the line $y=x+10$. Their difference is $\dfrac{1}{x}$, which approaches zero. Therefore, the oblique asymptote is $y=x+10$.
