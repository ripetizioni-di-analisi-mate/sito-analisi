In a bisection step a computer reports $f(m)=0.002$ at the midpoint with absolute error at most $0.005$. The endpoints have certified opposite signs. Which decision preserves the guarantee that a zero lies in the bracket?

- Declare that $m$ is an exact root.
- Keep both endpoints for now and refine the estimate of $f(m)$.
- Use a negative sign because the error exceeds the reported value.
- Immediately use the positive sign of the displayed value.

## Explanation

The possible values form the interval $[-0.003,0.007]$. It includes both signs and zero, so this measurement cannot safely rule out either half.
