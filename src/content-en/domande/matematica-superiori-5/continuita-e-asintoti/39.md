What value must be assigned to $f(9)$ for continuity at $x=9$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-81}{x-9}$ for $x\ne9$?

- $9$
- $81$
- $18$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $x\ne9$ the function equals $x+9$. The limit as $x\to9$ is therefore $18$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
