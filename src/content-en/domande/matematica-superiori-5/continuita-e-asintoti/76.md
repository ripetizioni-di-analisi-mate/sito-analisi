A zero is located by bisection starting from an interval of length $40$. How long is the interval after it has been halved three times?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$
- $20$
- $5$

## Explanation

At each step only one half is kept. After three steps the original length has been divided by $2^3$. This gives $5$.
