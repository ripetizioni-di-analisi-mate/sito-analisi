The function $f(x) = \ln\left(4 - x^{2}\right)$ is defined on the interval $(-2, 2)$. Which vertical lines are asymptotes of its graph?

- Only $x = 2$
- $x = -4$ and $x = 4$
- No vertical asymptote
- $x = -2$ and $x = 2$

## Explanation

The function is defined for $4 - x^{2} > 0$, that is for $-2 < x < 2$, so the endpoints of the domain are $-2$ and $2$.

As $x \to 2^{-}$ and as $x \to -2^{+}$ the argument $4 - x^{2}$ tends to $0$ while staying positive, therefore the logarithm tends to $-\infty$. So the lines $x = -2$ and $x = 2$ are both **vertical asymptotes**.
