For $f(x)=1/(x-2)$ on $[0,1]$, which pair gives its minimum and maximum, in that order?

- $\left(-\dfrac12,-1\right)$
- $\left(-1,-\dfrac12\right)$
- $\left(0,\dfrac12\right)$
- $(-1,0)$

## Explanation

The pole at $2$ lies outside the interval, on which $f$ is continuous and decreasing. Its minimum is therefore $f(1)=-1$, and its maximum is $f(0)=-1/2$.
