An even function has oblique asymptote $y=2x+3$ as $x\to+\infty$. What is its asymptote as $x\to-\infty$?

- $y=-2x-3$
- $y=-2x+3$
- $y=2x+3$
- $y=2x-3$

## Explanation

For negative $x$, $-x$ is positive and $f(x)=f(-x)$. The line describing $f(-x)$ becomes $2(-x)+3$, with a difference tending to zero.
