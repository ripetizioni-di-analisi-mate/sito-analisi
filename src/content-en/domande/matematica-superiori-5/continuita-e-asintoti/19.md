What value must be assigned to $f(5)$ for continuity at $x=5$ if the function is given by $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-25}{x-5}$ for $x\ne5$?

- $5$
- $25$
- $10$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $x\ne5$ the function equals $x+5$. The limit as $x\to5$ is therefore $10$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal this limit.
