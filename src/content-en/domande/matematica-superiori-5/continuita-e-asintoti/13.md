To locate the zero of $f(x)=x^5+x-1$, start with $[0,1]$. Which interval still brackets the sign change after two bisections?

- $\left[\dfrac14,\dfrac12\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac12,\dfrac34\right]$
- $\left[0,\dfrac14\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac34,1\right]$

## Explanation

The function is negative at $1/2$ and positive at $1$. At the next midpoint $3/4$ its value is $243/1024-1/4=-13/1024<0$, so keep the right half.
