A function equals $x^2-1$ for $x\le2$ and $2x-1$ for $x>2$. Which triple gives its left limit, value at $2$, and right limit, in that order?

- $(4,3,3)$
- $(3,3,3)$
- $(3,4,3)$
- $(3,3,4)$

## Explanation

Both formulas are continuous on their respective pieces. On the left and at the point use $2^2-1=3$. On the right the limit is $2\cdot2-1=3$.
