Remove only $0$ from $[-1,1]$. Which property does $f(x)=1-x^2$ lose on this new domain?

- Continuity at every domain point.
- Attainment of its supremum.
- The existence of a minimum.
- Boundedness above.

## Explanation

Only zero attained the value $1$. This is still the supremum because $f(x)$ approaches $1$ from either side, but no remaining point attains it.
