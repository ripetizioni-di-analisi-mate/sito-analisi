What is the y-coordinate of the horizontal asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{10x+1}{2x-3}$?

- $10$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator have the same degree. The limit at infinity is the ratio of the coefficients of $x$. Therefore, the horizontal asymptote has equation $y=\dfrac{10}{2}$.
