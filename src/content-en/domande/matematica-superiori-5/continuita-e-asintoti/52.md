A function is continuous on $[0,1]$ and vanishes at every rational point there. What values can it take at an irrational point $a$ of the interval?

- Any positive value.
- Only $0$.
- Any integer.
- Only $1$ or $-1$.

## Explanation

Choose a sequence of rational numbers converging to $a$. All their function values are zero, and continuity requires them to converge to $f(a)$.
