For $x<2$ we have $f(x)=5-x$, and for $x\geq 2$ we have $f(x)=3+2x$. What is the right-hand limit minus the left-hand limit at $2$?

- $7$
- $4$
- $-4$
- $10$

## Explanation

The right-hand limit is $7$ and the left-hand limit is $3$. Subtract them in the specified order. This gives $4$.
