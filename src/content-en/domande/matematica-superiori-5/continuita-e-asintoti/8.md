What is the oblique asymptote as $x\to+\infty$ for the function $f(x)=x+3+\dfrac{1}{x}$?

- $y=x - 3$
- $y=3 x$
- $y=x + 4$
- $y=x + 3$

## Explanation

Compare $f(x)$ with the line $y=x+3$. Their difference is $\dfrac{1}{x}$, which approaches zero. Therefore, the oblique asymptote is $y=x+3$.
