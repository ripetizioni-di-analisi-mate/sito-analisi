The function equals $1$ for $x<5$ and $3$ for $x\geq5$. What type of discontinuity does it have at $x=5$?

- A removable discontinuity
- A discontinuity with an infinite limit
- No discontinuity
- A jump discontinuity

## Explanation

The left-hand limit is $1$ and the right-hand limit is $3$. The two limits are finite but unequal. Therefore, at $x=5$ there is a jump discontinuity.
