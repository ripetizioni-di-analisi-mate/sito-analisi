A strictly increasing function has horizontal asymptote $y=3$ as $x\to+\infty$. What happens to the graph of its inverse near $3$ along its domain?

- It has a vertical asymptote $x=3$.
- It has a maximum of height $3$.
- It necessarily passes through $(3,3)$.
- It has a horizontal asymptote $y=3$.

## Explanation

An inverse swaps coordinates. Function values approaching $3$ correspond to original inputs growing arbitrarily large, so the inverse tends to $+\infty$.
