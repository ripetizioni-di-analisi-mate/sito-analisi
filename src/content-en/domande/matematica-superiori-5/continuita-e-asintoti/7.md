Which pair gives the vertical and horizontal asymptotes of $f(x)=(3x-2)/(x+1)$?

- $x=-2,\ y=1$
- $x=1,\ y=3$
- $x=-1,\ y=3$
- $x=-1,\ y=-2$

## Explanation

At $-1$ the denominator vanishes and the numerator equals $-5$. At infinity the degrees match, and the ratio of the leading coefficients is $3$.
