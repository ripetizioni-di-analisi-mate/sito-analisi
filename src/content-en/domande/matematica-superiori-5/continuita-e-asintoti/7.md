What is the y-coordinate of the horizontal asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{3x+1}{2x-3}$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator have the same degree. The limit at infinity is the ratio of the coefficients of $x$. Therefore, the horizontal asymptote has equation $y=\dfrac{3}{2}$.
