For which values of the real parameters $a$ and $b$ is the function
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
x + a & \text{if } x < 0 \\
b \cos x & \text{if } 0 \leq x \leq \pi \\
x - \pi + 2 & \text{if } x > \pi
\end{cases}
$$
continuous on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $a=-2,\ b=-2$
- $a=2,\ b=2$
- $a=-2,\ b=2$
- $a=0,\ b=-2$

## Explanation

The three formulas are continuous on their intervals. At $x=0$ continuity requires $a=b\cos 0=b$. At $x=\pi$ it requires $b\cos\pi=\pi-\pi+2=2$, that is $-b=2$, so $b=-2$ and therefore $a=-2$ as well.
