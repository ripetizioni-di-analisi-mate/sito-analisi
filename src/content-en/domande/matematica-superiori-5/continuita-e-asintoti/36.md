What is the x-coordinate of the vertical asymptote of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x-9}$?

- $-9$
- $0$
- $1$
- $9$

## Explanation

The denominator is zero at $x=9$, and the numerator remains nonzero. As the input approaches that point, the absolute value of the function grows without bound. Therefore, the vertical asymptote has equation $x=9$.
