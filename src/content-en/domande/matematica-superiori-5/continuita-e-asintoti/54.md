For $x<4$ we have $f(x)=9-x$, and for $x\geq 4$ we have $f(x)=5+2x$. What is the right-hand limit minus the left-hand limit at $4$?

- $18$
- $13$
- $8$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The right-hand limit is $13$ and the left-hand limit is $5$. Subtract them in the specified order. This gives $8$.
