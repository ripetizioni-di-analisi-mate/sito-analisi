The function $g(x)=\sqrt{1-x^2}$ is defined on $[-1,1]$. Which limit is needed to check continuity at $1$?

- The left-hand limit, equal to $0=g(1)$.
- Both one-sided limits in $\mathbb R$.
- The right-hand limit, equal to $1$.
- The limit as $x\to+\infty$.

## Explanation

Continuity is tested along the domain. Near its right endpoint the domain approaches only from the left, and the radicand tends to zero.
