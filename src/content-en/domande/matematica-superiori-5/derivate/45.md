At a given point, $f(2)=4$ and $f'(2)=-3$. What is the derivative of $1/f(x)$ at that point?

- $\dfrac{3}{16}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{16}$

## Explanation

The reciprocal rule gives the negative of the derivative of $f$ divided by the square of the function's value. The numerator becomes positive. This gives $\dfrac{3}{16}$.
