We approximate $f(2+h)$ by $f(2)+f'(2)h$ for $f(x)=x^2$ and $h=\dfrac{1}{5}$. What is the approximate value?

- $\dfrac{22}{5}$
- $\dfrac{16}{5}$
- $\dfrac{24}{5}$
- $\dfrac{121}{25}$

## Explanation

Use $f(2)=4$ and $f'(2)=4$. The linear term is $4h$. This gives $\dfrac{24}{5}$.
