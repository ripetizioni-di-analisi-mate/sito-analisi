Two ordinates read at inputs $a-h$ and $a+h$, with $h>0$, each have an absolute error of at most $\varepsilon$. What is the largest error in the secant slope due to the readings?

- $\dfrac{\varepsilon}{h}$
- $\dfrac{\varepsilon}{2h}$
- $2h\varepsilon$
- $\dfrac{2\varepsilon}{h}$

## Explanation

In the extreme case one reading is $\varepsilon$ too high and the other is $\varepsilon$ too low, so their difference can change by $2\varepsilon$. The inputs are $2h$ apart, giving the bound $\varepsilon/h$.
