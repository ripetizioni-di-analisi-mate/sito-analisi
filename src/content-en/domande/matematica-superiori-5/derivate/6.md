When differentiating $e^x(\sin x-\cos x)$, which terms cancel and what remains?

- The sines cancel, leaving $-2e^x\cos x$.
- The cosines cancel, leaving $2e^x\sin x$.
- All terms cancel.
- The exponentials cancel, leaving $2\sin x$.

## Explanation

Factoring out the exponential, the product rule gives $e^x[(\sin x-\cos x)+(\cos x+\sin x)]$. The two cosine terms have opposite signs.
