The water level in a cylinder of constant cross-sectional area $3\,\mathrm{m^2}$ rises at $0.02\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the instantaneous flow rate in litres per second?

- $600\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The volume law $V=3h$ gives $V'=3h'=0.06\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. A cubic metre contains a thousand litres, giving a flow rate of $60\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
