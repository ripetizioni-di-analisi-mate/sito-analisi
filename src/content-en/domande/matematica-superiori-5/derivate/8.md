For $f(x)=e^{-x}\cos(2x)$, which factor fully accounts for the graph's slope at $x=0$?

- The cosine factor, whose derivative is $-2$.
- The exponential, whose derivative is $-1$.
- Both contribute equally.
- Neither, the tangent is horizontal.

## Explanation

At zero both factors equal one. The term containing $-2\sin(2x)$ vanishes, while the one containing $-e^{-x}$ equals $-1$.
