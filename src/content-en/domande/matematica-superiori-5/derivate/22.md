What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{6x}$?

- $e^{6 x}$
- $6 e^{6 x}$
- $6 e^{x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{6 x}}{6}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $6$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=6e^{6x}$.
