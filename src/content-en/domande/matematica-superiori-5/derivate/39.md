What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(9x)$?

- $\cos{(9 x )}$
- $- 9 \cos{(9 x )}$
- $9 \cos{(9 x )}$
- $9 \sin{(9 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $9x$, which is $9$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=9\cos(9x)$.
