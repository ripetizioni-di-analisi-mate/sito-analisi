What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\sqrt{x^{2} + 4}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $\dfrac{2 x}{\sqrt{x^{2} + 4}}$
- $\dfrac{x}{x^{2} + 4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 \sqrt{x^{2} + 4}}$
- $\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^{2} + 4}}$

## Explanation

Divide the derivative of the radicand by twice the square root. The factor of two cancels.
