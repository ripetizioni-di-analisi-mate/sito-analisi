What is the first derivative of $f(x)=x \ln{\left(2 x \right)}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $\dfrac{1}{x}$
- $\ln{\left(2 x \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(2 x \right)} + 2$
- $\ln{\left(2 x \right)} + 1$

## Explanation

In the product rule, the term containing the derivative of the logarithm equals one. The other term is the logarithm itself.
