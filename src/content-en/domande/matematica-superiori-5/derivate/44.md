What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(10x)$?

- $\cos{(10 x )}$
- $- 10 \cos{(10 x )}$
- $10 \sin{(10 x )}$
- $10 \cos{(10 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $10x$, which is $10$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=10\cos(10x)$.
