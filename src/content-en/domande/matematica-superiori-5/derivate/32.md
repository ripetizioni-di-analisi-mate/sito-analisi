What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{8x}$?

- $e^{8 x}$
- $8 e^{x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{8 x}}{8}$
- $8 e^{8 x}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $8$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=8e^{8x}$.
