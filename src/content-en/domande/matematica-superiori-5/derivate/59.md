For $x>0$, $x\ne1$, let $g(x)=\log_x 2$. Which derivative accounts for the variable logarithm base?

- $g'(x)=-\dfrac{\ln2}{x(\ln x)^2}$
- $g'(x)=\dfrac{1}{2\ln x}$
- $g'(x)=\dfrac1{x\ln2}$
- $g'(x)=\dfrac{\ln2}{x\ln x}$

## Explanation

Changing base gives $g(x)=\ln2/\ln x$. The numerator is constant. Differentiating the reciprocal of the denominator gives $-(1/x)/(\ln x)^2$.
