The tangent to $f(x)=x^{3} + 9 x$ at the point with horizontal coordinate $4$ has equation $y=mx+q$. What is $q$?

- $-64$
- $-128$
- $128$
- $100$

## Explanation

The tangent passes through $(4,100)$ and has slope $57$. The equation $f(4)=m\cdot 4+q$ gives the intercept. This yields $-128$.
