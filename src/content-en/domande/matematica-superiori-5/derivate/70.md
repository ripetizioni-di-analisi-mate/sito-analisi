The tangent to $f(x)=x^{3} + 5 x$ at the point with horizontal coordinate $2$ has equation $y=mx+q$. What is $q$?

- $18$
- $-8$
- $-16$
- $16$

## Explanation

The tangent passes through $(2,18)$ and has slope $17$. The equation $f(2)=m\cdot 2+q$ gives the intercept. This yields $-16$.
