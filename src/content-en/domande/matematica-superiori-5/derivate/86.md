Two instruments use different scales related by $Y=aX+b$ with $a>0$. Which expression stays unchanged if only the offset $b$ is varied?

- The derivative of $Y$ with respect to time.
- The initial value of $Y$.
- The sum $Y(t_1)+Y(t_2)$.
- The value of $Y$ when $X=0$.

## Explanation

The time derivative is $Y'=aX'$ because $b$ is constant. Each of the other three quantities contains either $b$ or $2b$.
