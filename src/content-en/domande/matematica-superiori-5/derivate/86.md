We approximate $f(3+h)$ by $f(3)+f'(3)h$ for $f(x)=x^2$ and $h=\dfrac{1}{7}$. What is the approximate value?

- $\dfrac{57}{7}$
- $\dfrac{484}{49}$
- $\dfrac{66}{7}$
- $\dfrac{69}{7}$

## Explanation

Use $f(3)=9$ and $f'(3)=6$. The linear term is $6h$. This gives $\dfrac{69}{7}$.
