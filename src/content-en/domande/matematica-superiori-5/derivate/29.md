The base-$2$ logarithm is defined for $x > 0$. Which function is obtained by differentiating $f(x) = \log_2 x$?

- $\dfrac{\ln 2}{x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x \ln 2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2x}$

## Explanation

By the change-of-base formula $\log_2 x = \dfrac{\ln x}{\ln 2}$, and $\dfrac{1}{\ln 2}$ is a constant. The derivative of a constant times a function is the constant times the derivative of the function, so
$$
f'(x) = \frac{1}{\ln 2} \cdot \frac{1}{x} = \frac{1}{x \ln 2} .
$$
