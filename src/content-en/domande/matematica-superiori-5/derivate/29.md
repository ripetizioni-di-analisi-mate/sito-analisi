What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(7x)$?

- $7 \cos{(7 x )}$
- $\cos{(7 x )}$
- $- 7 \cos{(7 x )}$
- $7 \sin{(7 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $7x$, which is $7$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=7\cos(7x)$.
