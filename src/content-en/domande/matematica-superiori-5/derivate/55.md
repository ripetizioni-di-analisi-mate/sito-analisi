The graphs $y=x^2$ and $y=4-x^2$ intersect. How are their tangents related at each intersection point?

- They are perpendicular.
- They are parallel and distinct.
- The tangents coincide.
- Their slopes are opposite, and the tangents are not perpendicular.

## Explanation

At an intersection $x^2=2$. The slopes are $2x$ and $-2x$. They are both nonzero and their product is $-4x^2=-8$. Perpendicular lines with finite slopes would have product $-1$.
