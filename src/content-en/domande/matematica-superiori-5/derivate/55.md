What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{2 x + 5}{x + 3}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $- \dfrac{1}{\left(x + 3\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{x + 3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x + 3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 3\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

The numerator of the derivative is the difference of the two products in the quotient rule. The denominator is the square of $x+3$.
