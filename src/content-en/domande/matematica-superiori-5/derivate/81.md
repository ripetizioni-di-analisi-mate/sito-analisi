The position is $s(t)=4 t^{3} - 9 t$ in meters, with $t$ in seconds. What is the acceleration at $t=5$, expressed in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $291$
- $24$
- $120$
- $30$

## Explanation

Acceleration is the second derivative of position. The linear term vanishes after the second differentiation. This gives $120$.
