A sensor measures distance $s$ in centimetres and time $t$ in seconds. It records $ds/dt=12$. What is the derivative when distance is expressed in metres and time in minutes?

- $720\,\mathrm{m/min}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{m/min}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{m/min}$
- $0.002\,\mathrm{m/min}$

## Explanation

Twelve centimetres per second is $0.12$ metres per second. There are sixty seconds per minute, so the same slope is $0.12\cdot60=7.2$ metres per minute.
