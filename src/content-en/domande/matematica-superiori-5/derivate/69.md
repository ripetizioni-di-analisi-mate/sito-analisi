Two sensors measure the same position $s(t)$. The second uses a clock $\tau=60t$, where $t$ is in minutes and $\tau$ in seconds. If $\dfrac{ds}{dt}=90$ metres per minute, what speed does $\dfrac{ds}{d\tau}$ give?

- $90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5400\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac23\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

By the chain rule, multiply the derivative with respect to $t$ by $\dfrac{dt}{d\tau}=\dfrac1{60}$. This gives $\dfrac{90}{60}=1.5$ metres per second.
