What is the first derivative of $f(x)=x^{2} e^{- 3 x}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $x \left(2 - 3 x\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $x \left(3 x + 2\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $- 3 x^{2} e^{- 3 x}$
- $2 x e^{- 3 x}$

## Explanation

Differentiate each factor in turn and add the two contributions. The derivative of the exponential includes the negative coefficient in the exponent.
