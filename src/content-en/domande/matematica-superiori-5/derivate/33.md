What is the derivative of $f(x)=\ln(x+8)$ for $x>-8$?

- $\dfrac{1}{x + 8}$
- $\log{(x + 8 )}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x}$
- $\dfrac{8}{x + 8}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the logarithm of $u(x)>0$ is $\dfrac{u^\prime(x)}{u(x)}$. Here $u(x)=x+8$ and $u^\prime(x)=1$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=\dfrac{1}{x+8}$.
