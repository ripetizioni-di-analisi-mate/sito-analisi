What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{7x}$?

- $e^{7 x}$
- $7 e^{x}$
- $7 e^{7 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{7 x}}{7}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $7$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=7e^{7x}$.
