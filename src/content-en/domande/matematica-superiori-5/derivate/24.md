What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(6x)$?

- $\cos{(6 x )}$
- $- 6 \cos{(6 x )}$
- $6 \sin{(6 x )}$
- $6 \cos{(6 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $6x$, which is $6$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=6\cos(6x)$.
