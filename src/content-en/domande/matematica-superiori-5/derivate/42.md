What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{10x}$?

- $e^{10 x}$
- $10 e^{10 x}$
- $10 e^{x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{10 x}}{10}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $10$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=10e^{10x}$.
