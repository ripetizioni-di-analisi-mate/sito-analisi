What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{9x}$?

- $9 e^{9 x}$
- $e^{9 x}$
- $9 e^{x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{9 x}}{9}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $9$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=9e^{9x}$.
