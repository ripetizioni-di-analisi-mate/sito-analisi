The total cost in euros of producing $q$ units is $C(q)=2000+15q+0.05q^2$. What is the marginal cost $C'(50)$ under this continuous model?

- $17.5$ euros per unit.
- $57.5$ euros per unit.
- $20.05$ euros per unit.
- $20$ euros per unit.

## Explanation

The rate of change of cost is $C'(q)=15+0.1q$ euros per unit. At $q=50$ it equals $20$ euros per unit. Producing one whole additional unit changes the cost by $C(51)-C(50)=20.05$ euros, slightly more because the marginal cost increases with $q$.
