What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{5x}$?

- $5 e^{5 x}$
- $e^{5 x}$
- $5 e^{x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{5 x}}{5}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $5$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=5e^{5x}$.
