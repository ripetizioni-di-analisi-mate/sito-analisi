The illuminance at distance $r>0$ from a source is $I(r)=k/r^2$, where $k>0$. If the distance doubles, what fraction of the original sensitivity $|I'(r)|$ remains?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

The derivative is $I'(r)=-2k/r^3$. Comparing $2k/(2r)^3$ with $2k/r^3$ gives $1/8$. The illuminance falls to a quarter, while the sensitivity is governed by a different power of distance.
