What is the first derivative of $f(x)=x^{4} e^{- 5 x}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $x^{3} \left(5 x + 4\right) e^{- 5 x}$
- $x^{3} \left(4 - 5 x\right) e^{- 5 x}$
- $- 5 x^{4} e^{- 5 x}$
- $4 x^{3} e^{- 5 x}$

## Explanation

Differentiate each factor in turn and add the two contributions. The derivative of the exponential includes the negative coefficient in the exponent.
