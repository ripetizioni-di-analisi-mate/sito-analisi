What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(8x)$?

- $\cos{(8 x )}$
- $8 \cos{(8 x )}$
- $- 8 \cos{(8 x )}$
- $8 \sin{(8 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $8x$, which is $8$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=8\cos(8x)$.
