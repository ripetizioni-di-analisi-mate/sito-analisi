What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\left(x^{2} + 5\right)^{2}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $2 x \left(x^{2} + 5\right)$
- $4 x \left(x^{2} + 5\right)$
- $2 x^{2} + 10$
- $4 x \left(x^{2} + 5\right)^{2}$

## Explanation

Multiply the derivative of the power function by the derivative of its argument $x^2+5$.
