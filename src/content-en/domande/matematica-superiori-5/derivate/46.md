A function $f$ is differentiable near $2$ and $f'(2)<0$. Which conclusion follows for sufficiently small positive increments $h$?

- $f(2+h)<f(2)$
- $f(2+h)>f(2)$
- $f(2+h)=f(2)$
- $f(2+h)=f(2)+hf'(2)$ exactly.

## Explanation

The quotient $[f(2+h)-f(2)]/h$ tends to a negative number and therefore stays negative for sufficiently small $h$. Since $h>0$, the change in the output is also negative.
