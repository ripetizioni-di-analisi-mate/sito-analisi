What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{3x}$?

- $e^{3 x}$
- $3 e^{x}$
- $3 e^{3 x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{3 x}}{3}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $3$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=3e^{3x}$.
