We approximate $f(4+h)$ by $f(4)+f'(4)h$ for $f(x)=x^2$ and $h=\dfrac{1}{9}$. What is the approximate value?

- $\dfrac{148}{9}$
- $\dfrac{152}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1369}{81}$
- $\dfrac{136}{9}$

## Explanation

Use $f(4)=16$ and $f'(4)=8$. The linear term is $8h$. This gives $\dfrac{152}{9}$.
