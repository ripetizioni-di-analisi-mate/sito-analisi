The volume differential of a sphere is $dV=4\pi r^2\,dr$. For a relative error of 1% in the radius, what is the first-order relative error in the volume?

- $\dfrac13\%$
- $1\%$
- $3\%$
- $9\%$

## Explanation

Dividing by $V=\dfrac43\pi r^3$ gives $dV/V=3\,dr/r$. The exponent three multiplies the small relative radius error by three.
