What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\ln{\left(x^{2} + 5 \right)}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $\dfrac{2 x}{x^{2} + 5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x^{2} + 5}$
- $2 x \ln{\left(x^{2} + 5 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{\left(x^{2} + 5\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

Divide the derivative of the logarithm's argument by the argument itself. The derivative of $x^2$ appears in the numerator.
