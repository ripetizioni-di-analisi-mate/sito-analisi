For two differentiable functions the values at $1$ are known: $f(1) = 2$, $f'(1) = 3$, $g(1) = 4$, $g'(1) = -1$. What is the value at $1$ of the derivative of the quotient $\dfrac{f}{g}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $-3$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

By the **quotient rule**
$$
h'(1) = \frac{f'(1)\,g(1) - f(1)\,g'(1)}{g(1)^{2}} = \frac{3 \cdot 4 - 2 \cdot (-1)}{16} = \frac{14}{16} = \frac{7}{8} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{5}{8}$ comes from a sign error in the term $f(1)\,g'(1)$. The value $-3$ is the quotient $\dfrac{f'(1)}{g'(1)}$, and the value $\dfrac{7}{2}$ comes from dividing by $g(1)$ instead of $g(1)^{2}$.
