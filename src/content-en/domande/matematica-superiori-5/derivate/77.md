For $f(x)=|x|$, a sensor computes $\dfrac{f(h)-f(-h)}{2h}$ for $h>0$. Why does the result zero fail to measure $f'(0)$?

- The one-sided slopes are $-1$ and $1$.
- The symmetric quotient is undefined for every $h>0$.
- The function is discontinuous at zero.
- The result depends on the value of $h$.

## Explanation

Symmetry gives equal heights at the two endpoints, so the secant joining them is horizontal. Secants through the origin have different one-sided slopes, which prevents the derivative from existing.
