What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\left(x^{2} + 9\right)^{4}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $4 \left(x^{2} + 9\right)^{3}$
- $8 x \left(x^{2} + 9\right)^{3}$
- $8 x \left(x^{2} + 9\right)^{4}$
- $2 x \left(x^{2} + 9\right)^{3}$

## Explanation

Multiply the derivative of the power function by the derivative of its argument $x^2+9$.
