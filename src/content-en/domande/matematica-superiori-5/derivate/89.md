A differentiable function satisfies $f(2+h)=3-4h+h\varepsilon(h)$ with $\varepsilon(h)\to0$ as $h\to0$. What is the tangent at $x=2$?

- $y=-4+3(x-2)$
- $y=3-4x$
- $y=3-4(x-2)$
- $y=3+4(x-2)$

## Explanation

Continuity gives $f(2)=3$. Subtracting $3$ and dividing by $h$ leaves $-4+\varepsilon(h)$, which tends to $-4$. The point of tangency is $(2,3)$.
