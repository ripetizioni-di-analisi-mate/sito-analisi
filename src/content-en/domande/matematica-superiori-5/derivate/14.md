What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(4x)$?

- $\cos{(4 x )}$
- $4 \cos{(4 x )}$
- $- 4 \cos{(4 x )}$
- $4 \sin{(4 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $4x$, which is $4$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=4\cos(4x)$.
