A stone follows $h(t)=20t-5t^2$, in metres and seconds. Which pair gives its velocity and acceleration at the top?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s},\ 0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s},\ -10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s},\ -10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s},\ 10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The velocity $20-10t$ vanishes at $t=2$. Its derivative is constantly $-10$, including the instant when the direction of motion reverses.
