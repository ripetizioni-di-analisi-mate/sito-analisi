What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{4x}$?

- $e^{4 x}$
- $4 e^{x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{4 x}}{4}$
- $4 e^{4 x}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $4$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=4e^{4x}$.
