A function equals $ax^2+bx$ for $x\le1$ and $\ln x$ for $x>1$. Which coefficients give a differentiable join?

- $a=-1,\ b=1$
- $a=1,\ b=-1$
- $a=0,\ b=1$
- $a=1,\ b=0$

## Explanation

Continuity requires $a+b=0$. The one-sided derivatives agree if $2a+b=1$. Solving the equations together gives the stated pair.
