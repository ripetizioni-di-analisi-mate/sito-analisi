The tangent to $f(x)=x^{3} + 7 x$ at the point with horizontal coordinate $3$ has equation $y=mx+q$. What is $q$?

- $-27$
- $-54$
- $54$
- $48$

## Explanation

The tangent passes through $(3,48)$ and has slope $34$. The equation $f(3)=m\cdot 3+q$ gives the intercept. This yields $-54$.
