How do the singularities of $f(x)=|x-1|+\sqrt[3]{x}$ at $0$ and $1$ differ?

- Two corners.
- A corner at $0$, a vertical tangent at $1$.
- A vertical tangent at $0$, a corner at $1$.
- Two cusps.

## Explanation

At zero the difference quotient of the cube root tends to $+\infty$ on both sides. At one its derivative is finite and the absolute value has different one-sided slopes.
