A sensor cannot distinguish speeds above a threshold and reports $F(v)=\min(v,10)$ for $v>0$. How does its local sensitivity $F'(v)$ change across the threshold?

- It equals $1$ below $10$ and $0$ above $10$.
- It equals $0$ below $10$ and $1$ above $10$.
- It equals $1$ on both sides.
- It equals $10$ on both sides.

## Explanation

Below the threshold the output reproduces each input change by the same amount. Above the threshold it is constant. The one-sided derivatives at $10$ differ, so the derivative there does not exist.
