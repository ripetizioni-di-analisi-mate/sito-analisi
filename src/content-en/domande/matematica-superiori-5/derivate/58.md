Let $F(v)=\min(v,10)$ for $v>0$. What is the derivative $F'(v)$ for $0<v<10$ and for $v>10$?

- $F'(v)=1$ for $0<v<10$ and $F'(v)=0$ for $v>10$
- $F'(v)=0$ for $0<v<10$ and $F'(v)=1$ for $v>10$
- $F'(v)=1$ for every $v\neq10$
- $F'(v)=10$ for every $v\neq10$

## Explanation

For $0<v<10$ the minimum of $v$ and $10$ is $v$, so $F(v)=v$ and $F'(v)=1$. For $v>10$ the minimum is $10$, so $F$ is constant and $F'(v)=0$. At $v=10$ the left derivative equals $1$ and the right derivative equals $0$. Since the two derivatives are different, $F$ is not differentiable at $10$.
