What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\left(x^{2} + 7\right)^{3}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $2 x \left(x^{2} + 7\right)^{2}$
- $6 x \left(x^{2} + 7\right)^{2}$
- $3 \left(x^{2} + 7\right)^{2}$
- $6 x \left(x^{2} + 7\right)^{3}$

## Explanation

Multiply the derivative of the power function by the derivative of its argument $x^2+7$.
