For $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x^2}$, the difference quotient at $1$ is $-\dfrac{2+h}{(1+h)^2}$. What is the slope of the tangent there?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The slope is the limit of the quotient as $h\to0$. The denominator approaches one and the signed numerator approaches minus two.
