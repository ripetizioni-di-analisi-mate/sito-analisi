What is the derivative of $f(x)=e^{2x}$?

- $e^{2 x}$
- $2 e^{2 x}$
- $2 e^{x}$
- $\dfrac{e^{2 x}}{2}$

## Explanation

Apply the chain rule. The exponential function is its own derivative, and we multiply it by the derivative of the exponent, which is $2$. Thus $f^\prime(x)=2e^{2x}$.
