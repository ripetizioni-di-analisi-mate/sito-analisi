The position is $s(t)=2 t^{3} - 5 t$ in meters, with $t$ in seconds. What is the acceleration at $t=3$, expressed in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $18$
- $49$
- $36$
- $12$

## Explanation

Acceleration is the second derivative of position. The linear term vanishes after the second differentiation. This gives $36$.
