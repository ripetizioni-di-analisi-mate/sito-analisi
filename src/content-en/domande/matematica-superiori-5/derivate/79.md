A point moves in the plane with position $r(t)=(\cos t,\sin t)$. Its speed is constant. What is its acceleration at $t=0$?

- $(0,1)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(-1,0)$
- $(1,0)$

## Explanation

The velocity is $(-\sin t,\cos t)$ and changes direction although its norm is one. Differentiating componentwise gives $(-\cos t,-\sin t)$, initially pointing towards the centre of the circle.
