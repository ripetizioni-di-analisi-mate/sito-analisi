A road profile is represented by $h(x)=\sqrt{1+x^2}$ for $x\in\mathbb R$. Which of these slopes is **never** attained by the tangent?

- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

We have $h'(x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}$, whose absolute value is always less than $1$. The slope is zero at $0$ and equals $\pm\dfrac12$ at $x=\pm\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$ respectively.
