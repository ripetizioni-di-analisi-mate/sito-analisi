What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{4 x + 9}{x + 5}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $\dfrac{11}{\left(x + 5\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{11}{x + 5}$
- $- \dfrac{11}{\left(x + 5\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{4}{x + 5}$

## Explanation

The numerator of the derivative is the difference of the two products in the quotient rule. The denominator is the square of $x+5$.
