The tangent to $y=x^2$ at the point with horizontal coordinate $3$ meets the $y$-axis. What is the vertical coordinate of that point?

- $9$
- $18$
- $-18$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The tangent passes through $(3,9)$ and has slope six. Moving three units to the left decreases the vertical coordinate by eighteen. This gives $-9$.
