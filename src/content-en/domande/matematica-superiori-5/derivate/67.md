What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{\sin{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{x}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $\dfrac{2 x \cos{\left(2 x + 5 \right)} + \sin{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{x^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{2 \cos{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{x}$
- $\dfrac{x \cos{\left(2 x + 5 \right)} - \sin{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{x^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{2 x \cos{\left(2 x + 5 \right)} - \sin{\left(2 x + 5 \right)}}{x^{2}}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the sine includes the factor from the inner function. In the quotient rule, subtract the term arising from the derivative of the denominator.
