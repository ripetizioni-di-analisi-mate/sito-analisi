The tangent to a differentiable function $f$ at input $u=1/2$ passes through the origin and has slope $6$. Set $F(x)=x f(1/x)$ for $x>0$. What is $F'(2)$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $9$
- $0$

## Explanation

The tangent has equation $y=6u$, so $f(1/2)=3$ and $f'(1/2)=6$. The product and chain rules give $F'(x)=f(1/x)-f'(1/x)/x$. At $x=2$ the two contributions are $3$ and $-3$.
