What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\dfrac{3 x + 7}{x + 4}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $\dfrac{3}{x + 4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{\left(x + 4\right)^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{\left(x + 4\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{5}{x + 4}$

## Explanation

The numerator of the derivative is the difference of the two products in the quotient rule. The denominator is the square of $x+4$.
