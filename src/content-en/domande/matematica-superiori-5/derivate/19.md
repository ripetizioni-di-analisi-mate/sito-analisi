What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(5x)$?

- $\cos{(5 x )}$
- $- 5 \cos{(5 x )}$
- $5 \cos{(5 x )}$
- $5 \sin{(5 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $5x$, which is $5$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=5\cos(5x)$.
