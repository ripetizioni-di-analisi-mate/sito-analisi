What is the first derivative of $f(x)=x^{3} e^{- 4 x}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $x^{2} \left(3 - 4 x\right) e^{- 4 x}$
- $- 4 x^{3} e^{- 4 x}$
- $x^{2} \left(4 x + 3\right) e^{- 4 x}$
- $3 x^{2} e^{- 4 x}$

## Explanation

Differentiate each factor in turn and add the two contributions. The derivative of the exponential includes the negative coefficient in the exponent.
