What are the vertical intercepts of the tangents to $y=x^3-3x$ that are parallel to $y=9x$?

- $0,0$
- $-2,2$
- $-9,9$
- $-16,16$

## Explanation

From $3x^2-3=9$ we find $x=\pm2$. The points of tangency are $(2,2)$ and $(-2,-2)$. Subtracting nine times the horizontal coordinate from each vertical coordinate gives the two intercepts.
