What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(2x)$?

- $\cos{(2 x )}$
- $- 2 \cos{(2 x )}$
- $2 \sin{(2 x )}$
- $2 \cos{(2 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $2x$, which is $2$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=2\cos(2x)$.
