For a positive quantity $M(t)$, the measured value of $\dfrac{d}{dt}\ln M(t)$ is $0.04$ per minute. If $M=150\,\mathrm{g}$ at that instant, what is $M'$?

- $150\,\mathrm{g/min}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{g/min}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{g/min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g/min}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the logarithm is $M'/M$, the relative rate. Multiplying by the current mass gives $M'=0.04\cdot150=6$ grams per minute.
