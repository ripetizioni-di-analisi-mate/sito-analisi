The position is $s(t)=3 t^{3} - 7 t$ in meters, with $t$ in seconds. What is the acceleration at $t=4$, expressed in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $18$
- $24$
- $137$
- $72$

## Explanation

Acceleration is the second derivative of position. The linear term vanishes after the second differentiation. This gives $72$.
