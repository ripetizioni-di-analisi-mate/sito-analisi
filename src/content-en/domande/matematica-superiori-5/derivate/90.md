The signal $u(t)=\sin(2t)$ is differentiated four times. What constant ratio does its fourth derivative bear to the original signal wherever $u(t)\ne0$?

- $-16$
- $4$
- $-4$
- $16$

## Explanation

After two derivatives, $u''=-4u$. Differentiating twice more gives $u^{(4)}=-4u''=16u$, so the ratio is $16$.
