A cart is at $0$, $4$ and $36$ metres at times $0$, $1$ and $3$ seconds. A student takes the arithmetic mean of the average velocities on the two legs. How does this compare with the average velocity over the whole time?

- It is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ lower.
- It is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ higher.
- The two results are equal.
- It is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ lower.

## Explanation

The two partial average velocities are $4/1=4$ and $32/2=16$ metres per second. Their arithmetic mean is $10$, while total displacement divided by total time is $36/3=12$. The legs have different durations, so the second carries twice the weight in the overall average.
