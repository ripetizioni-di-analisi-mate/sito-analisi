What is the first derivative of $f(x)=\cos^{2}{\left(5 x \right)}$ on the set where it is differentiable?

- $10 \sin{\left(5 x \right)} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$
- $- 2 \sin{\left(5 x \right)} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$
- $- 10 \sin{\left(5 x \right)} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}$
- $- 5 \sin{\left(5 x \right)}$

## Explanation

Differentiating the outer power introduces the factor $2\cos(cx)$ with $c=5$. Differentiating the cosine then introduces a minus sign.
