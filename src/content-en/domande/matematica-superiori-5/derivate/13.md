A sphere's radius increases from $5$ to $5.1$ cm. What is the estimated increase in volume in cubic centimetres, using the differential?

- $100\pi$
- $\pi$
- $10\pi$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}(5.1)^3$

## Explanation

The formula $dV=4\pi r^2\,dr$ uses the initial radius and its increment. The product $4\pi\cdot25\cdot0.1$ equals $10\pi$.
