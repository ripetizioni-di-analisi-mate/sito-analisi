Let $f(x)=(x-1)^4g(x)$, where $g$ is a polynomial with $g(1)=2$. Which is the first derivative of $f$ with a nonzero value at $1$?

- The first, with value $8$.
- The fourth, with value $48$.
- The third, with value $12$.
- The fourth, with value $8$.

## Explanation

Writing $g(x)=2+(x-1)q(x)$ makes the leading term of $f$ equal to $2(x-1)^4$. The first three derivatives vanish at $1$, while the fourth equals $2\cdot4!=48$.
