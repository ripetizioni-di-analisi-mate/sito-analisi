Suppose $f'(x+1)=f'(x)$ for every real $x$. Which example shows that this need not imply $f(x+1)=f(x)$?

- $f(x)=x$
- $f(x)=0$
- $f(x)=\cos(2\pi x)$
- $f(x)=\sin(2\pi x)$

## Explanation

For $f(x)=x$ the derivative is constantly $1$ and satisfies the required equality. The function itself increases by $1$ when its input increases by $1$.
