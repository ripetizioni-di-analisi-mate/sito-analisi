What is the derivative of $f(x)=\sin(3x)$?

- $3 \cos{(3 x )}$
- $\cos{(3 x )}$
- $- 3 \cos{(3 x )}$
- $3 \sin{(3 x )}$

## Explanation

The derivative of sine is cosine. The chain rule also requires the derivative of the argument $3x$, which is $3$. Therefore $f^\prime(x)=3\cos(3x)$.
