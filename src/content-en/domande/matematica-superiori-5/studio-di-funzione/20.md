What is the absolute maximum value of $f(x)=5x$ on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $0$
- $5$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

The coefficient $5$ is positive, so the function is strictly increasing. The maximum on the closed interval occurs at the right endpoint $x=2$. The maximum value is $10$.
