At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=- \dfrac{x}{2} + \ln{\left(x \right)}$ attain its maximum on $(0,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$
- $\ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The derivative is $\dfrac{1}{x}-\dfrac{1}{2}$. It is positive to the left of $2$ and negative to the right. This gives $2$.
