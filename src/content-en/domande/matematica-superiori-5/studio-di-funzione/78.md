On $[0,3]$, $f(0)=2$. The derivative is $2$ on $(0,1)$ and $-1$ on $(1,3)$, and $f$ is continuous throughout. Which description of the absolute extrema is correct?

- The maximum is $4$ and both endpoints attain the minimum.
- The maximum is $2$ and the minimum is $-1$.
- Only $1$ attains the minimum.
- Only $3$ attains the minimum.

## Explanation

On the first segment the value rises from $2$ to $4$. On the second it falls by $1\cdot2=2$ and returns to $2$. Continuity also fixes the value at the junction.
