A fence $20$ m long encloses three sides of a rectangle along a wall. What width in meters, measured perpendicular to the wall, maximizes the area?

- $5$
- $20$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$

## Explanation

For a width of $x$, the side parallel to the wall has length $20-2x$. The area is $x(20-2x)$. Its derivative vanishes at $x=5$ and changes from positive to negative. This gives $5$.
