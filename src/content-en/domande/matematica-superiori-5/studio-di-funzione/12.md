For which levels $k$ does the curve $y=x^4-2x^2$ meet the line $y=k$ at four distinct points?

- $-1<k<0$
- $-1\le k\le0$
- $k>0$
- $k<-1$

## Explanation

Setting $u=x^2$ gives $u=1\pm\sqrt{1+k}$. We need two distinct, strictly positive values of $u$, each yielding two opposite inputs.
