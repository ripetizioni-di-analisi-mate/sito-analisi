What is the absolute minimum value of $f(x)=5 - \left(x - 4\right)^{2}$ on $[3,6]$?

- $1$
- $5$
- $9$
- $4$

## Explanation

The parabola has a maximum at the vertex. Of the two endpoints of the interval, take the one farther from $4$, where the squared term equals four. This gives $1$.
