What is the absolute maximum value of $f(x)=6x$ on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $12$
- $0$
- $6$
- $24$

## Explanation

The coefficient $6$ is positive, so the function is strictly increasing. The maximum on the closed interval occurs at the right endpoint $x=2$. The maximum value is $12$.
