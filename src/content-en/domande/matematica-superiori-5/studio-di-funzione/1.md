At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=(x-2)^2+1$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and equals zero only for $x=2$. Therefore, the minimum value of the function is $1$. It is attained at the horizontal coordinate $2$.
