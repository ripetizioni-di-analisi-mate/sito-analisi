For $f(x)=x^3-12x+1$, which sequence of extrema occurs as the graph is read from left to right?

- A maximum at $(-2,17)$ and a minimum at $(2,-15)$.
- A minimum at $(-2,17)$ and a maximum at $(2,-15)$.
- Two minima, both at height $1$.
- Just one maximum, at the origin.

## Explanation

The derivative $3(x-2)(x+2)$ is positive, then negative, then positive. The sign changes classify the extrema, and substitution into the original function gives their heights.
