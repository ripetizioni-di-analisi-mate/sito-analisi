To compare the absolute extrema of $x-2\sin x$ on $[0,\pi]$, which inputs must be evaluated?

- $\pi/3$ only
- $0,\ 2\pi/3,\ \pi$
- $0,\ \pi/3,\ \pi$
- $0,\ \pi/2,\ \pi$

## Explanation

The only interior zero of the derivative $1-2\cos x$ is $\pi/3$. Both interval endpoints must also be included. The function values are $0$, $\pi/3-\sqrt3$ and $\pi$.
