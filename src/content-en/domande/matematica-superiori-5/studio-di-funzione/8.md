What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-3)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $[3,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,-3]$
- $(-\infty,3]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-3)$. It is negative for $x<3$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,3]$.
