What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-6)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $[6,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,6]$
- $(-\infty,-6]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-6)$. It is negative for $x<6$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,6]$.
