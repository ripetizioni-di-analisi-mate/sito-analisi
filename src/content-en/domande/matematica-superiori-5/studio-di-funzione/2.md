Which rewrite of $x^4-8x^2+3$ immediately gives both the absolute minimum and the two inputs where it occurs?

- $(x^2+4)^2-13$
- $(x^2-4)^2-13$
- $(x^2-4)^2+3$
- $(x-2)^4-13$

## Explanation

The square is nonnegative. It vanishes at $x=\pm2$, giving the minimum value $-13$ at both points.
