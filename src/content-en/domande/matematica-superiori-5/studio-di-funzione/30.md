What is the absolute maximum value of $f(x)=7x$ on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $0$
- $14$
- $7$
- $28$

## Explanation

The coefficient $7$ is positive, so the function is strictly increasing. The maximum on the closed interval occurs at the right endpoint $x=2$. The maximum value is $14$.
