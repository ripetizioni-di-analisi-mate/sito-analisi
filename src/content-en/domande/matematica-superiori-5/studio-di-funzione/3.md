What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-2)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $[2,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,2]$
- $(-\infty,-2]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-2)$. It is negative for $x<2$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,2]$.
