For $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2}{x+2}$ a student lists $x=-2$ as a possible maximum because the derivative is undefined there. Which check settles the issue?

- The derivative is negative throughout the domain.
- The numerator vanishes at $-2$.
- The function itself is undefined at $-2$, so the graph has no point there.
- The denominator of the derivative is always negative.

## Explanation

The function excludes $-2$. The inputs giving local extrema are the zeros of the numerator of $f'(x)=x(x+4)/(x+2)^2$, namely $-4$ and $0$, where the sign changes.
