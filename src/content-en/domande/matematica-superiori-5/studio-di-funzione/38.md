What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-9)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $[9,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,9]$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,-9]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-9)$. It is negative for $x<9$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,9]$.
