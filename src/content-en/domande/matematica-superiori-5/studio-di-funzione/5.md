For $f(x)=x^3-6x^2+5x$, the tangent at the inflection point is $y=-7x+8$. Which difference shows that the curve crosses this line?

- $f(x)-(-7x+8)=(x-2)^3$
- $f(x)-(-7x+8)=(x-2)^2$
- $f(x)-(-7x+8)=(x+2)^3$
- $f(x)-(-7x+8)=x-2$

## Explanation

Subtracting the tangent gives $x^3-6x^2+12x-8$. The cube changes sign on passing through $x=2$, the input of the inflection point.
