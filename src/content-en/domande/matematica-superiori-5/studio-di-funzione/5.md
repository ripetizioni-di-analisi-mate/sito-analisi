What is the absolute maximum value of $f(x)=2x$ on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

The coefficient $2$ is positive, so the function is strictly increasing. The maximum on the closed interval occurs at the right endpoint $x=2$. The maximum value is $4$.
