A function has second derivative $f''(x)=x^2-16$. Where is the function concave down?

- $(-\infty,4)$
- $(-\infty,-4)$ ∪ $(4,\infty)$
- $(4,\infty)$
- $(-4,4)$

## Explanation

The second derivative is negative when $x^2<16$. This occurs between the two roots, which are opposites.
