The cost $C(n)=(n-4.4)^2+3$ is defined only for integer quantities $n=0,1,\ldots,8$. Which quantity minimises cost?

- $8$
- $5$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The continuous model has its vertex at $4.4$. Cost increases with the distance from the vertex, so of the two adjacent integers choose $4$, at distance $0.4$ compared with $0.6$ for $5$.
