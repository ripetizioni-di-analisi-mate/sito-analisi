A function has second derivative $f''(x)=x^2-4$. Where is the function concave down?

- $(-\infty,-2)$ ∪ $(2,\infty)$
- $(-\infty,2)$
- $(-2,2)$
- $(2,\infty)$

## Explanation

The second derivative is negative when $x^2<4$. This occurs between the two roots, which are opposites.
