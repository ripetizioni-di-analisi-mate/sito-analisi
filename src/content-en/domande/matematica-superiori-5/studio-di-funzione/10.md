An open box with a square base has volume $32$ cubic decimetres. If $x$ is the base side length, which function gives the surface area to minimize?

- $S(x)=x^2+\dfrac{128}{x}$
- $S(x)=2x^2+\dfrac{128}{x}$
- $S(x)=x^2+\dfrac{32}{x^2}$
- $S(x)=x^2+128x$

## Explanation

The height is $32/x^2$. Add one base and four side faces, each of area $x\cdot32/x^2$. The derivative vanishes at $x=4$ and the minimum area is $48$ square decimetres.
