What is the absolute maximum value of $f(x)=3x$ on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $0$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

The coefficient $3$ is positive, so the function is strictly increasing. The maximum on the closed interval occurs at the right endpoint $x=2$. The maximum value is $6$.
