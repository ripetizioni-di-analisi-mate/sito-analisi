For $f(x)=x^3-2x$, consider starting Newton's method at $x_0=\sqrt{2/3}$. What prevents the first step from being computed?

- The tangent is horizontal and the point is not a root.
- The function is not differentiable at the point.
- The point lies outside the domain.
- The function must vanish wherever the tangent is horizontal.

## Explanation

The derivative is $3x^2-2$, so $f'(x_0)=0$. However, $f(x_0)=x_0(2/3-2)\ne0$. The quotient $f(x_0)/f'(x_0)$ in Newton's formula is therefore undefined.
