A fence $16$ m long encloses three sides of a rectangle along a wall. What width in meters, measured perpendicular to the wall, maximizes the area?

- $4$
- $16$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

For a width of $x$, the side parallel to the wall has length $16-2x$. The area is $x(16-2x)$. Its derivative vanishes at $x=4$ and changes from positive to negative. This gives $4$.
