A variation table gives $f'>0$ just before and just after $x=2$, with $f'(2)=0$. Which graph is possible near $2$?

- A curve with a strict local maximum at $2$.
- A curve with a strict local minimum at $2$.
- A vertical line at $2$.
- A curve that keeps increasing and has a horizontal tangent at $2$.

## Explanation

The positive derivative keeps the graph increasing on both sides. The zero derivative gives a horizontal tangent; for instance, $f(x)=(x-2)^3$ behaves this way.
