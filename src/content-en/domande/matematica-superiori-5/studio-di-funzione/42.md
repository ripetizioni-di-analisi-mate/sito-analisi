A function has derivative $f'(x)=6x(2-x)$. At what horizontal coordinate does it have a local maximum?

- $1$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The derivative is positive between zero and two and negative immediately to the right of two. At zero, its sign changes from negative to positive. Thus the local maximum occurs at $2$.
