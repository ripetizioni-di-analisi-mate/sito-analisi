To the right of $x=0$ a graph follows $y=x^2$, and to the left it follows $y=-x^2$; its value at zero is zero. How is the origin classified?

- A local maximum.
- A local minimum.
- An inflection point with horizontal tangent.
- A corner.

## Explanation

Both one-sided slopes at zero vanish. The graph is concave down on the left and concave up on the right. This change produces an inflection point even though the second derivative at zero does not exist.
