What is the horizontal coordinate of the inflection point of $f(x)=(x-2)^3$?

- $-2$
- $0$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

The second derivative is $f^{\prime\prime}(x)=6(x-2)$. It changes sign from negative to positive as the input passes through $x=2$. The function is differentiable at that point, so it is an inflection point.
