The graph of $f(x)=xe^{2x}$ approaches the horizontal axis as $x\to-\infty$. Why is the height of its absolute minimum strictly negative?

- The minimum is the value of the limit at the left.
- The exponential is negative for $x<0$.
- The asymptote prevents the function from taking the value zero.
- The function takes negative values and its derivative changes from negative to positive at $-1/2$.

## Explanation

The derivative $e^{2x}(1+2x)$ changes sign once. The value at that point is $-1/(2e)$. On the left the zero limit is approached through negative values.
