At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=- \dfrac{x}{3} + \ln{\left(x \right)}$ attain its maximum on $(0,+\infty)$?

- $9$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\ln{\left(3 \right)}$

## Explanation

The derivative is $\dfrac{1}{x}-\dfrac{1}{3}$. It is positive to the left of $3$ and negative to the right. This gives $3$.
