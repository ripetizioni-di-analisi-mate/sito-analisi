At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=(x-3)^2+1$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and equals zero only for $x=3$. Therefore, the minimum value of the function is $1$. It is attained at the horizontal coordinate $3$.
