The inflection points of $f(x)=\ln(x^2+4)$ occur at $x=\pm2$. Which geometric feature do they share?

- The vertical coordinate $\ln4$.
- The same tangent line.
- Zero slope.
- The vertical coordinate $\ln 8$.

## Explanation

The second derivative is $(8-2x^2)/(x^2+4)^2$ and changes sign at both inputs. Since the function is even, the heights agree and equal $\ln(4+4)$.
