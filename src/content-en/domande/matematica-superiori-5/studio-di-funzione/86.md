A path has elevation $h(x)=x^3-3x$ for $-2\le x\le2$. It is traversed from $x=-2$ to $x=2$. What is the total ascent, counting only uphill portions?

- $4$
- $8$
- $0$
- $12$

## Explanation

The derivative $3(x^2-1)$ is positive before $-1$ and after $1$. The elevations at $-2,-1,1,2$ are $-2,2,-2,2$, so each of the two ascents contributes $4$.
