Starting at $x_0=2$, apply one step of Newton's method to $f(x)=x^2-5$. What is $x_1$?

- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The method starts with $x_0$ and subtracts the ratio of the function value to the derivative at the same point. Therefore, subtract $\dfrac{-1}{4}$. This gives $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
