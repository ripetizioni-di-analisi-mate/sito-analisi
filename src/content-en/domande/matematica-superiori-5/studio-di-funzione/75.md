When Newton's method is applied to $f(x)=(x-2)^2$ from $x_n\ne2$, how does the error $e_n=x_n-2$ change at each step?

- $e_{n+1}=e_n^2$
- $e_{n+1}=2e_n$
- $e_{n+1}=e_n/2$
- $e_{n+1}=-e_n$

## Explanation

Here $f'(x_n)=2(x_n-2)$, so Newton subtracts $(x_n-2)/2$ from $x_n$. Subtracting $2$ from the result shows that the error is halved at this double root.
