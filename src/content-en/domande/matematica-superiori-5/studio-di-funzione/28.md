What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-7)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $[7,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,-7]$
- $(-\infty,7]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-7)$. It is negative for $x<7$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,7]$.
