Which conditions must $f(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx$ satisfy to have a stationary inflection point at $x=1$?

- $3+2a+b=0$ and $6+2a=0$.
- $1+a+b=0$ and $3+2a+b=0$.
- $3+2a+b=1$ and $6+2a=1$.
- $a+b=0$ and $a=b$.

## Explanation

We need $f'(1)=0$ and $f''(1)=0$. The second derivative is a straight line of slope six, so it changes sign at its root. This gives $a=-3$ and $b=3$.
