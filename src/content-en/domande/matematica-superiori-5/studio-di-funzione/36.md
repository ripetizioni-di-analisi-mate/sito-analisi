At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=(x-9)^2+1$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $-9$
- $0$
- $10$
- $9$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and equals zero only for $x=9$. Therefore, the minimum value of the function is $1$. It is attained at the horizontal coordinate $9$.
