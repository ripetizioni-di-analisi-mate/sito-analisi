For which values of the real parameter $a$ does the function $f(x) = x^4 + ax^3 + 6x^2$ have no points of inflection?

- $-4 < a < 4$
- $-16 \leq a \leq 16$
- $-4 \leq a \leq 4$
- $-2 \leq a \leq 2$

## Explanation

The second derivative is
$$
f''(x) = 12x^2 + 6ax + 12 = 6\left(2x^2 + ax + 2\right) ,
$$
a trinomial with discriminant $\Delta = a^2 - 16$. If $\Delta < 0$, that is, $-4 < a < 4$, then $f''(x) > 0$ for every $x$ and there are no points of inflection. If $a = \pm 4$ then $f''(x) = 12(x \pm 1)^2$, which vanishes at one point without changing sign, so in this case too **there are no points of inflection**. If $|a| > 4$ the trinomial has two distinct roots and changes sign at each of them, therefore $f$ has two points of inflection. The required condition is therefore $-4 \leq a \leq 4$.
