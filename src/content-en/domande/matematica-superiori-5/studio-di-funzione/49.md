What is the absolute minimum value of $f(x)=3 - \left(x - 2\right)^{2}$ on $[1,4]$?

- $7$
- $2$
- $3$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The parabola has a maximum at the vertex. Of the two endpoints of the interval, take the one farther from $2$, where the squared term equals four. This gives $-1$.
