Given $f'(x)=(x-3)^2(x-7)$, on which open interval is $f$ strictly increasing?

- $(3,\infty)$
- $(7,\infty)$
- $(3,7)$
- $(-\infty,3)$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and vanishes only at $3$. The derivative is positive for $x>7$ and negative to the left of $7$ except at the isolated zero.
