For which $x>0$ does the function $f(x)=x e^{- 4 x}$ attain its maximum?

- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The derivative is the product of a positive exponential and $1-4x$. This second factor changes from positive to negative at its zero. This gives $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
