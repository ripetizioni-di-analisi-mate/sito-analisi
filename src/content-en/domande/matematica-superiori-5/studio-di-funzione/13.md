What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-4)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $(-\infty,4]$
- $[4,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,-4]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-4)$. It is negative for $x<4$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,4]$.
