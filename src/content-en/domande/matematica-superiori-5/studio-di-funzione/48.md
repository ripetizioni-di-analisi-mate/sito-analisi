For $f(x)=\left(x - 4\right)^{4} - 9 \left(x - 4\right)^{2}$, what type of extremum occurs at the point with horizontal coordinate $4$?

- An absolute minimum.
- An inflection point with a horizontal tangent.
- A strict local minimum.
- A strict local maximum.

## Explanation

The first derivative vanishes at $4$. The second derivative at that point is $-18$, so the point is a strict local maximum.
