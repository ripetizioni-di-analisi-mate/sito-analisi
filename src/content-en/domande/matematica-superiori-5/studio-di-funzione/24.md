On which subset of its domain is the function $f(x) = \left(x^2 - 4\right)\ln x$ negative?

- $(1, 2)$
- $(0, 1) \cup (2, +\infty)$
- $(-2, 2)$
- $(0, 2)$

## Explanation

The domain is $(0, +\infty)$, by the condition for the logarithm to exist. On this set the factor $x^2 - 4$ is negative for $0 < x < 2$ and positive for $x > 2$, while $\ln x$ is negative for $0 < x < 1$ and positive for $x > 1$. The product is negative where the two factors have opposite signs, that is, only for $1 < x < 2$. For $0 < x < 1$ both factors are negative, so $f$ is positive there.
