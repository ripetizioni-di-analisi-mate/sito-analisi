What is the horizontal coordinate of the inflection point of $f(x)=(x-6)^3$?

- $-6$
- $0$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

The second derivative is $f^{\prime\prime}(x)=6(x-6)$. It changes sign from negative to positive as the input passes through $x=6$. The function is differentiable at that point, so it is an inflection point.
