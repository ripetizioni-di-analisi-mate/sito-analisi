A function has second derivative $f''(x)=x^2-9$. Where is the function concave down?

- $(-3,3)$
- $(-\infty,3)$
- $(-\infty,-3)$ ∪ $(3,\infty)$
- $(3,\infty)$

## Explanation

The second derivative is negative when $x^2<9$. This occurs between the two roots, which are opposites.
