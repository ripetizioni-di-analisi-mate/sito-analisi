A function $f$ is strictly increasing on $[-2,2]$, with $f(0)=0$. Where does $g(x)=f(x)^2$ attain its minimum?

- Only at $x=2$.
- Only at $x=-2$.
- Only at $x=0$.
- At every point where $f(x)<0$.

## Explanation

A square is always nonnegative and equals zero only where $f(x)=0$. Strict increase makes the input giving that output unique.
