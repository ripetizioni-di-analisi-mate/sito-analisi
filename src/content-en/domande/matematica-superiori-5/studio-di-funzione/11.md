At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=(x-4)^2+1$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $-4$
- $0$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and equals zero only for $x=4$. Therefore, the minimum value of the function is $1$. It is attained at the horizontal coordinate $4$.
