A point moves according to $s(t)=t^3-9t^2+24t$ for $0\le t\le5$. When is its signed velocity smallest, and what is its value?

- $t=5$, $v=-3$.
- $t=3$, $v=0$.
- $t=0$, $v=0$.
- $t=3$, $v=-3$.

## Explanation

The velocity can be written as $v(t)=3(t-3)^2-3$. Its vertex lies in the interval and gives the minimum of this parabola. The negative sign indicates motion opposite to the chosen positive direction.
