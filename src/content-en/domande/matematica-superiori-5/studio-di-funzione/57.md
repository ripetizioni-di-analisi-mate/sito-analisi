For $f(x)=x^{4} + 9$, we have $f''(0)=0$. What can be concluded about the point with horizontal coordinate zero?

- It is an absolute maximum.
- It is an inflection point with a horizontal tangent.
- It is an absolute minimum with no change in concavity.
- It is a corner.

## Explanation

The second derivative equals $12x^2$ and is positive for $x\ne0$. The concavity does not change, and the function attains its absolute minimum at zero.
