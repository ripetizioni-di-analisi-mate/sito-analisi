For $a>0$ let $f_a(x)=x^4-2ax^2$. What is the vertical distance from the local maximum to either minimum?

- $a^2$
- $a$
- $2a^2$
- $\sqrt a$

## Explanation

Writing $f_a=(x^2-a)^2-a^2$ identifies minima of height $-a^2$ at $x=\pm\sqrt a$. Near zero the function is negative while $f_a(0)=0$, giving vertical distance $a^2$.
