What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-5)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $[5,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,5]$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,-5]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-5)$. It is negative for $x<5$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,5]$.
