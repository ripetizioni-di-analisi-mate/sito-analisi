A function $f$ is twice differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, with $f'(2) = 0$ and $f''(2) = -3$. What can be concluded about the point $x = 2$?

- It is a relative minimum point
- It is a point of inflection with horizontal tangent
- It is a relative maximum point
- Nothing can be concluded without the sign of $f'$ near $2$

## Explanation

Since $f'(2) = 0$, the point $x = 2$ is stationary. The second derivative at $2$ exists and equals $-3 < 0$, so the hypotheses of the **second derivative test** are satisfied. The test states that a stationary point with $f''(x_0) < 0$ is a **relative maximum point**. The sign of $f'$ near $2$ is not needed, because the second derivative test already decides.
