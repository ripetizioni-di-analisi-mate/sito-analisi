At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=(x-8)^2+1$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $-8$
- $0$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and equals zero only for $x=8$. Therefore, the minimum value of the function is $1$. It is attained at the horizontal coordinate $8$.
