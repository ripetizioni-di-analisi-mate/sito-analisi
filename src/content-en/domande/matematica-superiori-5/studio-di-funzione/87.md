A function $f$ defined on $[0,+\infty)$ has a unique absolute minimum at $x=3$. How many absolute minima does $h(x)=f(|x|)$ have on $\mathbb R$?

- Two, at $-3$ and $3$.
- One, at $3$ only.
- One, at $0$ only.
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

The minimum occurs precisely when the input to $f$ is $3$. The equation $|x|=3$ has two solutions, giving the same absolute minimum value.
