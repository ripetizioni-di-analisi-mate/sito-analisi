For $f(x)=\left(x - 9\right) \left(x - 4\right)^{2}$, what stationary point is there besides $x=4$?

- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{17}{3}$
- $\dfrac{22}{3}$

## Explanation

The derivative factors as $(x-4)(3x-22)$. Set the second factor equal to zero. This gives $\dfrac{22}{3}$.
