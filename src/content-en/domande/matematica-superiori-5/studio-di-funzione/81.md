A function $f$ is increasing and concave down on an interval. Which behaviour can its derivative have, assuming it is positive throughout?

- Values $1,3,5$ at three increasing inputs.
- Values $5,3,1$ at three increasing inputs.
- Values $5,-1,3$ at three increasing inputs.
- Values $1,5,2$ at three increasing inputs.

## Explanation

Increasing behaviour is consistent with positive slopes. Concavity down requires those slopes to be nonincreasing from left to right. The sequence $5,3,1$ satisfies both conditions.
