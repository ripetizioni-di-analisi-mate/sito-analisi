Starting at $x_0=4$, apply one step of Newton's method to $f(x)=x^2-9$. What is $x_1$?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{25}{8}$
- $\dfrac{39}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$

## Explanation

The method starts with $x_0$ and subtracts the ratio of the function value to the derivative at the same point. Therefore, subtract $\dfrac{7}{8}$. This gives $\dfrac{25}{8}$.
