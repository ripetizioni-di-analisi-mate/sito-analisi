What is the absolute maximum value of $f(x)=8x$ on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $0$
- $8$
- $16$
- $32$

## Explanation

The coefficient $8$ is positive, so the function is strictly increasing. The maximum on the closed interval occurs at the right endpoint $x=2$. The maximum value is $16$.
