What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-8)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $(-\infty,8]$
- $[8,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,-8]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-8)$. It is negative for $x<8$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,8]$.
