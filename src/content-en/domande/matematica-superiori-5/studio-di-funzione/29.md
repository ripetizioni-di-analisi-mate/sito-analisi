The power $x^{x}$, with $x > 0$, first decreases and then increases. On which maximal interval is it strictly increasing?

- $\left(0, \dfrac{1}{e}\right]$
- $[1, +\infty)$
- $[e, +\infty)$
- $\left[\dfrac{1}{e}, +\infty\right)$

## Explanation

We write $f(x) = e^{x \ln x}$, so by the chain rule
$$
f'(x) = e^{x \ln x}\,(\ln x + 1) = x^{x}(\ln x + 1) .
$$
The factor $x^{x}$ is positive, therefore the sign of $f'$ is that of $\ln x + 1$, positive if and only if $\ln x > -1$, that is, $x > \dfrac{1}{e}$. By the monotonicity test $f$ is strictly increasing on $\left[\dfrac{1}{e}, +\infty\right)$ and strictly decreasing on $\left(0, \dfrac{1}{e}\right]$.
