What is the horizontal coordinate of the inflection point of $f(x)=(x-5)^3$?

- $-5$
- $0$
- $5$
- $15$

## Explanation

The second derivative is $f^{\prime\prime}(x)=6(x-5)$. It changes sign from negative to positive as the input passes through $x=5$. The function is differentiable at that point, so it is an inflection point.
