To plot a function on $[0,2]$, a program checks only the inputs $0,1,2$. Which polynomial vanishes at all three sampled inputs and attains a strictly positive maximum between two of them?

- $-[x(x-1)(x-2)]^2$
- $[x(x-1)(x-2)]^2$
- $x(x-1)$
- $0$

## Explanation

The product vanishes at every sampled input. Its square is positive, for instance, at $x=1/2$. By continuity it attains a positive maximum on $[0,1]$, and this must occur inside since both endpoint values are zero. A line segment between the samples would miss it.
