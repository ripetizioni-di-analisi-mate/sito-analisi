Which of the following polynomials vanishes at $x=0$, at $x=1$ and at $x=2$ and has a strictly positive maximum on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $-[x(x-1)(x-2)]^2$
- $[x(x-1)(x-2)]^2$
- $x(x-1)$
- $0$

## Explanation

The product $x(x-1)(x-2)$ vanishes at the three points, so its square and the opposite of its square vanish there too. The square is positive at every point other than $0$, $1$ and $2$, for instance at $x=\dfrac{1}{2}$, whereas the opposite of the square never takes positive values. The square is continuous, so by Weierstrass's theorem it has a maximum on $[0,2]$, and this maximum is positive. The polynomial $x(x-1)$ equals $2$ at $x=2$, so it does not vanish at all three points.
