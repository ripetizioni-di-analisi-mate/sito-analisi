At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=- \dfrac{x}{4} + \ln{\left(x \right)}$ attain its maximum on $(0,+\infty)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\ln{\left(4 \right)}$
- $4$
- $16$

## Explanation

The derivative is $\dfrac{1}{x}-\dfrac{1}{4}$. It is positive to the left of $4$ and negative to the right. This gives $4$.
