Given $f'(x)=(x-4)^2(x-9)$, on which open interval is $f$ strictly increasing?

- $(9,\infty)$
- $(4,\infty)$
- $(4,9)$
- $(-\infty,4)$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and vanishes only at $4$. The derivative is positive for $x>9$ and negative to the left of $9$ except at the isolated zero.
