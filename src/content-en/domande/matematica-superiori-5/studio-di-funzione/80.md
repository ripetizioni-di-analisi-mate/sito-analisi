Starting at $x_0=3$, apply one step of Newton's method to $f(x)=x^2-7$. What is $x_1$?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$

## Explanation

The method starts with $x_0$ and subtracts the ratio of the function value to the derivative at the same point. Therefore, subtract $\dfrac{2}{6}$. This gives $\dfrac{8}{3}$.
