Near $x=1$, the slopes of $f$ change from positive to negative. Let $g(x)=4-f(x)$. What happens on the graph of $g$?

- The graph necessarily passes through $(1,0)$.
- A local maximum occurs at $1$.
- The function is strictly increasing on both sides.
- A local minimum occurs at $1$.

## Explanation

The transformation reverses the signs of the slopes. The graph of $g$ falls to the left of $1$ and rises to the right, so the maximum of $f$ becomes a minimum of $g$.
