What is the absolute maximum value of $f(x)=10x$ on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $20$
- $0$
- $10$
- $40$

## Explanation

The coefficient $10$ is positive, so the function is strictly increasing. The maximum on the closed interval occurs at the right endpoint $x=2$. The maximum value is $20$.
