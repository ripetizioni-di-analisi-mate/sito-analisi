What is the largest interval on which $f(x)=(x-10)^2$ is strictly decreasing?

- $[10,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$
- $(-\infty,10]$
- $(-\infty,-10]$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f^\prime(x)=2(x-10)$. It is negative for $x<10$. Therefore, the function is strictly decreasing up to the vertex, on the interval $(-\infty,10]$.
