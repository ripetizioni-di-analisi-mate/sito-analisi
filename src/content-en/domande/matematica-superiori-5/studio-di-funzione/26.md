At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=(x-7)^2+1$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $-7$
- $7$
- $0$
- $8$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and equals zero only for $x=7$. Therefore, the minimum value of the function is $1$. It is attained at the horizontal coordinate $7$.
