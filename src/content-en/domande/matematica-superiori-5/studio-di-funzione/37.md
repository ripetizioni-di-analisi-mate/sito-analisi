For $0\leq x\leq4$, which horizontal coordinate minimizes the distance between $P=(x,0)$ and $Q=(2,3)$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The square of the distance is $(x-2)^2+9$. Since the square root function is increasing, the distance is smallest where its square is smallest. This gives $2$.
