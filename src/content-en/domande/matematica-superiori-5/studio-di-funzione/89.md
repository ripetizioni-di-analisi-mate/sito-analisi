A convex function on $[0,2]$ has $f(0)=4$ and $f(2)=8$. What follows about $f(1)$ from the graph's position relative to its chord?

- $f(1)=6$ necessarily.
- $f(1)\ge8$
- $f(1)\le6$
- $f(1)\ge7$

## Explanation

At the midpoint the chord has height $(4+8)/2=6$. The graph of a convex function lies on or below its chord, so its height cannot exceed $6$.
