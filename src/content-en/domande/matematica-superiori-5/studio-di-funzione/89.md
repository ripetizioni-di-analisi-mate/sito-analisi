A continuous function takes values of opposite signs at the endpoints of $[3,7]$. It is also strictly decreasing on the interval. How many zeros does the interval contain?

- $3$
- $1$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The intermediate value theorem guarantees at least one zero in the interior. Since the function is strictly decreasing, it cannot be zero at two distinct points. This gives $1$.
