At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=(x-6)^2+1$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $6$
- $-6$
- $0$
- $7$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and equals zero only for $x=6$. Therefore, the minimum value of the function is $1$. It is attained at the horizontal coordinate $6$.
