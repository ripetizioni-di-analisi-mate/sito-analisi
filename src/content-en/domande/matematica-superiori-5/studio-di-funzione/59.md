For which $x>0$ does the function $f(x)=x e^{- 3 x}$ attain its maximum?

- $0$
- $3$
- $- \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The derivative is the product of a positive exponential and $1-3x$. This second factor changes from positive to negative at its zero. This gives $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
