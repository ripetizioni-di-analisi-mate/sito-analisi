Given $f'(x)=(x-2)^2(x-5)$, on which open interval is $f$ strictly increasing?

- $(2,5)$
- $(-\infty,2)$
- $(5,\infty)$
- $(2,\infty)$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and vanishes only at $2$. The derivative is positive for $x>5$ and negative to the left of $5$ except at the isolated zero.
