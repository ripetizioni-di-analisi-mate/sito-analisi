For $f(x)=\left(x - 3\right)^{4} - 7 \left(x - 3\right)^{2}$, what type of extremum occurs at the point with horizontal coordinate $3$?

- An inflection point with a horizontal tangent.
- A strict local maximum.
- An absolute minimum.
- A strict local minimum.

## Explanation

The first derivative vanishes at $3$. The second derivative at that point is $-14$, so the point is a strict local maximum.
