A rectangle with sides $a,b$ is inscribed in a circle of radius $5$ cm. Which constraint proves that its maximum area is $50$ square centimetres?

- $ab=25\pi$
- $a+b=10$
- $a^2+b^2=25$
- $a^2+b^2=100$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the diameter of length ten. Pythagoras gives the stated constraint, and $(a-b)^2\ge0$ implies $2ab\le100$. The square attains equality.
