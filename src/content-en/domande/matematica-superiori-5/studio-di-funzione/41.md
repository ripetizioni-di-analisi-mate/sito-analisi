At what horizontal coordinate does $f(x)=(x-10)^2+1$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $10$
- $-10$
- $0$
- $11$

## Explanation

The square is always nonnegative and equals zero only for $x=10$. Therefore, the minimum value of the function is $1$. It is attained at the horizontal coordinate $10$.
