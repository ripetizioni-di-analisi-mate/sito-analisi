A fence $12$ m long encloses three sides of a rectangle along a wall. What width in meters, measured perpendicular to the wall, maximizes the area?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

For a width of $x$, the side parallel to the wall has length $12-2x$. The area is $x(12-2x)$. Its derivative vanishes at $x=3$ and changes from positive to negative. This gives $3$.
