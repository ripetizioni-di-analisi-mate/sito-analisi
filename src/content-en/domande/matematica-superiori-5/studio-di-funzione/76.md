Newton's method is applied to $f(x)=x^3-2x+2$ starting from $x_0=0$. What happens in the first two iterations?

- The iteration reaches a root at $1$.
- The iteration goes to $1$ and then returns to $0$.
- The first step is undefined.
- The second step gives $-1$.

## Explanation

We have $f(0)=2$ and $f'(0)=-2$, giving $x_1=1$. At $1$, $f(1)=1$ and $f'(1)=1$, so $x_2=0$. The iteration has entered a cycle.
