What is the absolute minimum value of $f(x)=4 - \left(x - 3\right)^{2}$ on $[2,5]$?

- $0$
- $8$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The parabola has a maximum at the vertex. Of the two endpoints of the interval, take the one farther from $3$, where the squared term equals four. This gives $0$.
