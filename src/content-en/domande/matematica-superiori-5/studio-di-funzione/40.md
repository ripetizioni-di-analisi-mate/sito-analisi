What is the absolute maximum value of $f(x)=9x$ on the interval $[0,2]$?

- $0$
- $9$
- $36$
- $18$

## Explanation

The coefficient $9$ is positive, so the function is strictly increasing. The maximum on the closed interval occurs at the right endpoint $x=2$. The maximum value is $18$.
