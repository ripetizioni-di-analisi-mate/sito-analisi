Given $f'(x)=(x-1)^2(x+2)$, which input gives an inflection point with a nonhorizontal tangent?

- $x=-2$
- $x=1$
- $x=-1$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

The second derivative is $3(x-1)(x+1)$ and changes sign at $-1$ and $1$. Only at the first point is the first derivative nonzero, since $f'(-1)=4$.
