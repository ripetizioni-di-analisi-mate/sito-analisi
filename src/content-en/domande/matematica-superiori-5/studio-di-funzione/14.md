What is the horizontal coordinate of the inflection point of $f(x)=(x-4)^3$?

- $-4$
- $4$
- $0$
- $12$

## Explanation

The second derivative is $f^{\prime\prime}(x)=6(x-4)$. It changes sign from negative to positive as the input passes through $x=4$. The function is differentiable at that point, so it is an inflection point.
