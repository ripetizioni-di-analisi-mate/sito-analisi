Given $\int_0^3 f(x)\,dx=9$, what is $\int_0^3(2f(x)-1)\,dx$?

- $18$
- $17$
- $15$
- $6$

## Explanation

By linearity, the first contribution is twice the given integral. The second is the integral of the constant negative one over an interval of length three. This gives $15$.
