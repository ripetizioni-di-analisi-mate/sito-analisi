If $\int_0^{3} f(x)\,dx=7$ and $\int_0^{4} f(x)\,dx=10$, what is $\int_{4}^{3} f(x)\,dx$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $10$
- $17$

## Explanation

Add the integrals along the path from $4$ to zero and from zero to $3$. Reversing the limits of the first integral changes its sign. This gives $-3$.
