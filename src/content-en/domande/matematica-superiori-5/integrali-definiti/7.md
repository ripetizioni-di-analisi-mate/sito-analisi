Find the area of the region bounded by the parabola $y = x^2$ and the line $y = x + 2$.

- $3$
- $9$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac92$

## Explanation

Solving $x^2=x+2$ gives $-1$ and $2$. The line is above the parabola between these points, so the area is $\int_{-1}^2(x+2-x^2)\,dx=9/2$.
