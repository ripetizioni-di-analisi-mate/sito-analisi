What is the area of the region bounded by the parabola $y = x^2$ and the line $y = x + 2$?

- $3$
- $9$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac92$

## Explanation

The $x$-coordinates of the intersection points solve $x^2 = x + 2$, that is $x^2 - x - 2 = 0$, so they are $x = -1$ and $x = 2$. At $x = 0$ the line equals $2$ and the parabola equals $0$, therefore between $-1$ and $2$ the line lies above the parabola. Then the area is the integral of the difference between the line and the parabola,
$$
\int_{-1}^{2} \left( x + 2 - x^2 \right) dx = \left[ \frac{x^2}{2} + 2x - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_{-1}^{2} = \frac{10}{3} - \left( -\frac{7}{6} \right) = \frac{9}{2} .
$$
