Slice the region between $y=x^2$ and $y=x+2$ horizontally. At what height does the **left** end of a slice change from the left branch of the parabola to the line?

- $y=0$
- $y=2$
- $y=4$
- $y=1$

## Explanation

At height $y$ the region requires $-\sqrt y\le x\le\sqrt y$ and $x\ge y-2$. The left end changes when $-\sqrt y=y-2$. Setting $u=\sqrt y\ge0$ gives $(u+2)(u-1)=0$, hence $y=1$.
