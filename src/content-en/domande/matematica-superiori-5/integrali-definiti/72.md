What is the area of the region bounded by the parabola $y = x^2$, its tangent line at the point $(1, 1)$ and the $y$-axis?

::grafico{f="x^2;2*x-1" area="x^2;2*x-1;0;1" punti="1,1,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=3 ymin=-2 ymax=5 legenda="y = x^2;\text{tangent at } (1, 1)" titolo="The parabola $y = x^2$, the tangent line at the point $(1, 1)$ and the $y$-axis"}

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The derivative of $x^2$ at $x = 1$ equals $2$, so the tangent line is $y = 1 + 2\,(x - 1) = 2x - 1$. The parabola is convex, therefore it lies above the tangent line, and the region goes from the $y$-axis, that is, $x = 0$, to the point of tangency $x = 1$. By the theorem on the **area between two curves**
$$
A = \int_0^{1} \left( x^2 - 2x + 1 \right) dx = \int_0^{1} (x - 1)^2\,dx = \left[ \frac{(x - 1)^3}{3} \right]_0^{1} = 0 - \left( -\frac{1}{3} \right) = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
