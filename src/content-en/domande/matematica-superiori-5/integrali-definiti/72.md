What is the average value of $f(x)=x^{2} + 9$ on $[0,4]$?

- $17$
- $\dfrac{172}{3}$
- $\dfrac{43}{3}$
- $9$

## Explanation

The average value is the integral divided by the length $4$. The two contributions to the average are $\dfrac{16}{3}$ and $9$. This gives $\dfrac{43}{3}$.
