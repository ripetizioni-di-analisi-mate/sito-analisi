What is the area between the graph of $f(x)=x-4$ and the $x$-axis on the interval $[0,13]$?

- $\dfrac{81}{2}$
- $97$
- $\dfrac{65}{2}$
- $\dfrac{97}{2}$

## Explanation

The graph crosses the axis at $x=4$. The area is the sum of the areas of two right triangles. The legs of the first each have length $4$, and those of the second each have length $9$. This gives $\dfrac{97}{2}$.
