We want to approximate $\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\,dx$ by the weighted sum $a[f(-1)+f(1)]+b f(0)$. Which weights make it exact for both $f(x)=1$ and $f(x)=x^2$?

- $a=\dfrac13,\quad b=\dfrac43$
- $a=\dfrac23,\quad b=\dfrac23$
- $a=\dfrac12,\quad b=1$
- $a=\dfrac16,\quad b=\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The integral of the constant is $2$, giving $2a+b=2$. The integral of the square is $2/3$, while the weighted sum is $2a$ because the middle value vanishes. Solving yields $a=1/3$ and $b=4/3$.
