What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{5} \dfrac{2}{x + 5}\,dx$?

- $\ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $2 \ln{\left(5 \right)}$
- $2 \ln{\left(10 \right)}$
- $2 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$

## Explanation

Evaluate the logarithm of $x+b$ at the endpoints. The difference of the logarithms is the logarithm of the ratio, and the ratio equals two. This gives $2 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$.
