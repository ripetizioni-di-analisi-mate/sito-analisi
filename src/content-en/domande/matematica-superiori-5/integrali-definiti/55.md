A region is defined by $0\le y\le1$ and $y^2\le x\le\sqrt y$. Which integral gives its area using horizontal slices?

- $\int_0^1(y^2+\sqrt y)\,dy$
- $\int_0^1(\sqrt y-y^2)\,dy$
- $\pi\int_0^1(y-y^4)\,dy$
- $\int_0^1(y^2-\sqrt y)\,dy$

## Explanation

At height $y$, the slice runs from $x=y^2$ to $x=\sqrt y$. Its length is the right endpoint minus the left, and its thickness is $dy$.
