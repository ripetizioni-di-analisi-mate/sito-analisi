What value does the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \frac{2x}{x^2 + 1}\,dx$ have?

- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $2\ln2$
- $\ln 2$

## Explanation

Set $u=1+x^2$. Then $du=2x\,dx$ and the endpoints become $1$ and $2$. Therefore the integral becomes $\displaystyle\int_1^2 \frac{du}{u}=\ln 2-\ln 1=\ln 2$.
