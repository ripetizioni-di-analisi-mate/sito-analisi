What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{9} \dfrac{4}{x + 9}\,dx$?

- $4 \ln{\left(18 \right)}$
- $4 \ln{\left(9 \right)}$
- $4 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(2 \right)}$

## Explanation

Evaluate the logarithm of $x+b$ at the endpoints. The difference of the logarithms is the logarithm of the ratio, and the ratio equals two. This gives $4 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$.
