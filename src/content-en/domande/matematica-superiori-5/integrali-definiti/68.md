What is the area of the region bounded by $y=3x$ and $y=x^2$?

- $\dfrac{27}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$

## Explanation

The curves intersect at zero and at $3$. The line lies above the parabola on that interval, so integrate $3x-x^2$. This gives $\dfrac{9}{2}$.
