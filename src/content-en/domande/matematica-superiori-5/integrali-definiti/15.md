The region under $y=\sqrt{4x}$ for $0\leq x\leq1$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. What is the volume of the solid?

- $4 \pi$
- $8 \pi$
- $2 \pi$
- $2$

## Explanation

The disk method gives $V=\pi\int_0^1 y^2\,dx$. Since $y^2=4x$, integrate $\pi\int_0^1 4x\,dx$. Thus the volume is $\dfrac{4\pi}{2}$.
