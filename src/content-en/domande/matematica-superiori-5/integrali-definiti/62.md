The rectangle $0\le x\le1$, $1\le y\le2$ rotates about the line $y=-1$. What is the area of a cross-section perpendicular to the axis?

- $5\pi$
- $9\pi$
- $\pi$
- $3\pi$

## Explanation

Measure the radii from $y=-1$: they are $3$ and $2$. The washer area is $\pi(3^2-2^2)=5\pi$.
