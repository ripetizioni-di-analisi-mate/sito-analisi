What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{7}^{\infty} \dfrac{1}{x^{3}}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{1}{98}$
- $\dfrac{1}{147}$
- $\dfrac{1}{343}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{98}$

## Explanation

The antiderivative is a power with negative exponent divided by that exponent. Its limit at infinity is zero, so only the negative of its value at the lower limit remains. This gives $\dfrac{1}{98}$.
