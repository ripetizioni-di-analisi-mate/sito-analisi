The region under $y=\sqrt{6x}$ for $0\leq x\leq1$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. What is the volume of the solid?

- $3 \pi$
- $6 \pi$
- $18 \pi$
- $3$

## Explanation

The disk method gives $V=\pi\int_0^1 y^2\,dx$. Since $y^2=6x$, integrate $\pi\int_0^1 6x\,dx$. Thus the volume is $\dfrac{6\pi}{2}$.
