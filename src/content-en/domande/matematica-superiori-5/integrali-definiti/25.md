A thin rod spans $0\le x\le2$ with linear density $\rho(x)=x$ in consistent units. Its centre of mass is $\bar x=\dfrac{\int x\rho(x)\,dx}{\int\rho(x)\,dx}$ over the rod. What is $\bar x$?

- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac83$
- $\dfrac23$
- $1$

## Explanation

The mass is $\int_0^2 x\,dx=2$. The moment is $\int_0^2x^2\,dx=8/3$. Their ratio is $4/3$, towards the denser end.
