The velocity along a straight line is $v(t)=3t-12$, measured in $\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the displacement in meters from $t=0$ to $t=8$ s?

- $0$
- $96$
- $48$
- $-48$

## Explanation

The velocity changes sign at $t=4$. In the signed integral, the two triangular contributions of equal area cancel. This gives $0$.
