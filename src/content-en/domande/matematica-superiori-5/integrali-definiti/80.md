A solid of revolution about the $x$-axis is generated by the region between $y=7$ and $y=4$ for $0\leq x\leq 3$. What is its volume?

- $147 \pi$
- $99 \pi$
- $33 \pi$
- $27 \pi$

## Explanation

The cross sections perpendicular to the axis are washers. Multiply the area of a washer, $\pi(7^2-4^2)$, by the length $3$. This gives $99 \pi$.
