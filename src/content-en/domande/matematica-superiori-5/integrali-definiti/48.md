What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{5} 4 x^{2} + 9 x\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{1675}{6}$
- $\dfrac{500}{3}$
- $145$
- $\dfrac{225}{2}$

## Explanation

Integrate each power separately. The antiderivative with zero constant term vanishes at the lower limit, so only its value at the upper limit is needed. This gives $\dfrac{1675}{6}$.
