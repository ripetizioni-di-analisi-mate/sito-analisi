What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{7} \dfrac{3}{x + 7}\,dx$?

- $3 \ln{\left(7 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $3 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $3 \ln{\left(14 \right)}$

## Explanation

Evaluate the logarithm of $x+b$ at the endpoints. The difference of the logarithms is the logarithm of the ratio, and the ratio equals two. This gives $3 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$.
