Let $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_1^x \sqrt{t^2 + 3}\, dt$. What is the equation of the tangent line to the graph of $F$ at the point with abscissa $x = 1$?

- $y = 2x$
- $y = x - 1$
- $y = \dfrac{x - 1}{2}$
- $y = 2x - 2$

## Explanation

The value of the function is $F(1) = \displaystyle\int_1^1 \sqrt{t^2 + 3}\, dt = 0$. The function $\sqrt{t^2 + 3}$ is continuous, so by the **fundamental theorem of calculus** $F'(x) = \sqrt{x^2 + 3}$ and $F'(1) = 2$. Therefore the tangent line is
$$
y = F(1) + F'(1)\,(x - 1) = 2x - 2 .
$$
