Let $I = \displaystyle\int_1^{2} \frac{1}{x}\, dx$ and $J = \displaystyle\int_1^{2} \frac{1}{x^2}\, dx$. Which relation holds?

- $I < J$
- $I = J$
- $I = 2J$
- $J < I$

## Explanation

For $1 \leq x \leq 2$ one has $x^2 \geq x$, so $\dfrac{1}{x^2} \leq \dfrac{1}{x}$, and by the **monotonicity** of the integral $J \leq I$. The values confirm the strict inequality: $I = \ln 2$ and $J = \left[ -\dfrac{1}{x} \right]_1^{2} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and $\ln 2 > \dfrac{1}{2}$ because $e < 4$, that is $e^{\frac{1}{2}} < 2$.
