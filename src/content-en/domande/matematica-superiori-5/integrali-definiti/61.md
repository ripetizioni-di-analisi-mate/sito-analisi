What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{5}^{\infty} \dfrac{1}{x^{2}}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The antiderivative is a power with negative exponent divided by that exponent. Its limit at infinity is zero, so only the negative of its value at the lower limit remains. This gives $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
