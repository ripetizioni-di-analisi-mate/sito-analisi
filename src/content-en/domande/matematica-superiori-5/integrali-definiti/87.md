A force along the $x$-axis is given by $F(x)=4x+9$ in newtons. How much work does it do, in joules, from $x=0$ to $x=5$ m?

- $95$
- $145$
- $29$
- $50$

## Explanation

Work is the integral of the component of force in the direction of displacement. Integrate the linear term and add the contribution from the constant part. This gives $95$.
