The cross-sections of a solid perpendicular to the $x$-axis are squares of side $1-x$ for $0\le x\le1$. What is the volume?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac13$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Each cross-section is a square of area $(1-x)^2$. Integrating from zero to one gives $[-(1-x)^3/3]_0^1=1/3$.
