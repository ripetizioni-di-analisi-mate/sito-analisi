What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1} e^{4 x}\,dx$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{e^{4}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{e^{4}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{e^{4}}{4}$
- $-1 + e^{4}$

## Explanation

The antiderivative contains a factor equal to the reciprocal of the coefficient of $x$. Evaluate it at the endpoints and subtract the contribution at zero. This gives $- \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{e^{4}}{4}$.
