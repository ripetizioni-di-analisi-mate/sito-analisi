For which value of $\alpha$ does
$$
\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}} = 2 ?
$$

- $\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\alpha = 2$
- $\alpha = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\alpha = 3$

## Explanation

The comparison integral on $[1, +\infty)$ converges if and only if $\alpha > 1$, and in that case it equals $\dfrac{1}{\alpha - 1}$. The condition $\dfrac{1}{\alpha - 1} = 2$ gives $\alpha - 1 = \dfrac{1}{2}$, that is $\alpha = \dfrac{3}{2}$, which satisfies $\alpha > 1$. For $\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$ the integral diverges, while for $\alpha = 2$ and $\alpha = 3$ it equals $1$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ respectively.
