For which value of $a > 1$ does the region between the graph of $y = \dfrac{1}{x}$, the $x$-axis and the lines $x = 1$ and $x = a$ have area $2$?

- $a = e$
- $a = e^2$
- $a = \sqrt{e}$
- $a = 2e$

## Explanation

For $x \geq 1$ the function $\dfrac{1}{x}$ is positive, so the area is the definite integral
$$
\int_1^{a} \frac{1}{x}\,dx = \Big[ \ln x \Big]_1^{a} = \ln a - \ln 1 = \ln a .
$$
The required condition is therefore $\ln a = 2$, that is, $a = e^2$. The value $a = e$ gives area $1$, and the value $a = \sqrt{e}$ gives area $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
