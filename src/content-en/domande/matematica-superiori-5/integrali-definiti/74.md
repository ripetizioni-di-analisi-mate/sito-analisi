For $F(x)=\int_0^{x^2}(t+7)\,dt$, what is $F'(3)$?

- $96$
- $16$
- $60$
- $42$

## Explanation

By the fundamental theorem, the derivative with respect to the upper limit is the integrand evaluated at $x^2$. Multiply by $2x$, the derivative of the upper limit. This gives $96$.
