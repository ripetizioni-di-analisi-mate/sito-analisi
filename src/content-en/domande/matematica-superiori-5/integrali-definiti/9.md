At how many distinct points of $[0,\pi]$ does $f(x)=\sin x$ attain its integral mean value?

- $3$
- $1$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The mean value is $\frac1\pi[-\cos x]_0^\pi=2/\pi$, strictly between $0$ and $1$. Sine reaches it once while increasing and once while decreasing.
