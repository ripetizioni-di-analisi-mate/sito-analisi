A spring has spring constant $k = 400\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The applied force is $F(x) = kx$. How much work is required to increase the extension of the spring from $0.1\,\mathrm{m}$ to $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $16\,\mathrm J$
- $24\,\mathrm J$
- $32\,\mathrm J$
- $8\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Work is the integral of force over displacement. An antiderivative of $400x$ is $200x^2$, so the difference is $200(0.3^2-0.1^2)=16$ joules.
