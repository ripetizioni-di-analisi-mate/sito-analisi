The function $g(x) = x^3 \cos x$ is odd. What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} \left( x^3 \cos x + 1 \right) dx$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $0$
- $8$

## Explanation

Since $g(-x) = (-x)^3 \cos(-x) = -x^3 \cos x = -g(x)$, the function $g$ is odd. In the integral over $[-2, 0]$ the **substitution** $x = -t$ is applied, so $dx = -dt$ and the limits $-2$ and $0$ become $2$ and $0$:
$$
\int_{-2}^{0} g(x)\, dx = \int_{2}^{0} g(-t)\, (-dt) = \int_{2}^{0} g(t)\, dt = -\int_0^{2} g(t)\, dt .
$$
By **additivity** one therefore has $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} g(x)\, dx = 0$. By **linearity** the required integral equals $0 + \displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} 1\, dx = 4$.
