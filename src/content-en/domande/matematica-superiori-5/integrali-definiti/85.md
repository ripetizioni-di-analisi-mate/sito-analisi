A signal takes the values $+2$ and $-2$ for equal lengths of time. What is its root mean square value over one period?

- $\sqrt2$
- $4$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The squared signal is always $4$. Its mean square is therefore $4$ and its square root is $2$, even though the signal itself has zero mean.
