For $F(x)=\int_0^{x^2}(t+9)\,dt$, what is $F'(4)$?

- $104$
- $25$
- $72$
- $200$

## Explanation

By the fundamental theorem, the derivative with respect to the upper limit is the integrand evaluated at $x^2$. Multiply by $2x$, the derivative of the upper limit. This gives $200$.
