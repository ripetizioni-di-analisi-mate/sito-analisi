What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{4} 3 x^{2} + 7 x\,dx$?

- $76$
- $120$
- $64$
- $56$

## Explanation

Integrate each power separately. The antiderivative with zero constant term vanishes at the lower limit, so only its value at the upper limit is needed. This gives $120$.
