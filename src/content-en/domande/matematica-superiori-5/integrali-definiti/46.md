What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{3} 2 x^{2} + 5 x\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{81}{2}$
- $\dfrac{45}{2}$
- $33$
- $18$

## Explanation

Integrate each power separately. The antiderivative with zero constant term vanishes at the lower limit, so only its value at the upper limit is needed. This gives $\dfrac{81}{2}$.
