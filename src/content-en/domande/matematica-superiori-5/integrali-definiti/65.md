What is the area between the graph of $f(x)=x-3$ and the $x$-axis on the interval $[0,10]$?

- $20$
- $\dfrac{49}{2}$
- $58$
- $29$

## Explanation

The graph crosses the axis at $x=3$. The area is the sum of the areas of two right triangles. The legs of the first each have length $3$, and those of the second each have length $7$. This gives $29$.
