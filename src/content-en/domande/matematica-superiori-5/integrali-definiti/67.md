In economics, income distribution is described by the Lorenz curve $L(x)$: the poorest $100x\,\%$ of the population owns the fraction $L(x)$ of total income. The Gini index is $G = 2 \displaystyle\int_0^{1} \big( x - L(x) \big)\,dx$. What is $G$ for $L(x) = x^2$?

::grafico{f="x+0*sqrt(x*(1-x));x^2+0*sqrt(x*(1-x))" area="x;x^2;0;1" xmin=-1 xmax=2 ymin=-1 ymax=2 legenda="y = x;y = x^2" titolo="The line $y = x$ and the Lorenz curve $y = x^2$ on $[0, 1]$"}

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The integral $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \big( x - L(x) \big)\,dx$ is the area between the line $y = x$ and the Lorenz curve, because $x \geq x^2$ on $[0, 1]$. By the definite integral evaluation formula
$$
\int_0^{1} \left( x - x^2 \right) dx = \left[ \frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^{1} = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6} .
$$
Hence $G = 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{6}$ is the area, before multiplying by $2$.
