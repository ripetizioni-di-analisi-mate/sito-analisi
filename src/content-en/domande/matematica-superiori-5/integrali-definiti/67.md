What is the area of the region bounded by $y=2x$ and $y=x^2$?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $4$
- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The curves intersect at zero and at $2$. The line lies above the parabola on that interval, so integrate $2x-x^2$. This gives $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
