If $\int_0^{4} f(x)\,dx=9$ and $\int_0^{5} f(x)\,dx=13$, what is $\int_{5}^{4} f(x)\,dx$?

- $22$
- $13$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

Add the integrals along the path from $5$ to zero and from zero to $4$. Reversing the limits of the first integral changes its sign. This gives $-4$.
