A solid of revolution about the $x$-axis is generated by the region between $y=9$ and $y=5$ for $0\leq x\leq 4$. What is its volume?

- $64 \pi$
- $224 \pi$
- $56 \pi$
- $324 \pi$

## Explanation

The cross sections perpendicular to the axis are washers. Multiply the area of a washer, $\pi(9^2-5^2)$, by the length $4$. This gives $224 \pi$.
