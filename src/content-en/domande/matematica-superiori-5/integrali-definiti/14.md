A body starts from rest with acceleration $a(t)=6-2t$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $0\le t\le6$. How many metres does it travel?

- $0$
- $18$
- $36$
- $72$

## Explanation

Integrating the acceleration gives $v(t)=6t-t^2$. The velocity is nonnegative throughout the interval, so the distance is $\int_0^6(6t-t^2)\,dt=108-72=36$ metres.
