What is the integral $\displaystyle\int_1^e x \ln x\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{e^2+1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{e^2-1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{e^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{e^2+1}{2}$

## Explanation

Integration by parts, with $\ln x$ as the factor to differentiate, gives the antiderivative $F(x)=\dfrac{x^2\ln x}{2}-\dfrac{x^2}{4}$. Since $F(e)=\dfrac{e^2}{2}-\dfrac{e^2}{4}=\dfrac{e^2}{4}$ and $F(1)=-\dfrac{1}{4}$, the integral equals $\dfrac{e^2}{4}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{e^2+1}{4}$.
