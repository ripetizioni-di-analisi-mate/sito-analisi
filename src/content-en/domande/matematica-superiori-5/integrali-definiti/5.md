Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_1^e x \ln x\,dx$.

- $\dfrac{e^2+1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{e^2-1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{e^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{e^2+1}{2}$

## Explanation

Integration by parts gives $x^2\ln(x)/2-x^2/4$. Its upper endpoint value is $e^2/4$. Subtract the lower endpoint value, which is $-1/4$.
