The region under $y=\sqrt{2x}$ for $0\leq x\leq1$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. What is the volume of the solid?

- $\pi$
- $2 \pi$
- $1$
- $1 + \pi$

## Explanation

The disk method gives $V=\pi\int_0^1 y^2\,dx$. Since $y^2=2x$, integrate $\pi\int_0^1 2x\,dx$. Thus the volume is $\dfrac{2\pi}{2}$.
