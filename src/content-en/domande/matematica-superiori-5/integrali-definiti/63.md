A graph uses time in minutes horizontally and flow in litres per second vertically. The numerical area under it is $8$. What is the volume in litres?

- $\dfrac{8}{60}$
- $480$
- $8$
- $68$

## Explanation

Each minute on the horizontal axis is $60$ seconds. To obtain litres, multiply the numerical area by $60$, giving $480$.
