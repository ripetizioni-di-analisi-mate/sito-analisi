What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{9}^{\infty} \dfrac{1}{x^{4}}\,dx$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{2187}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6561}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2916}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2187}$

## Explanation

The antiderivative is a power with negative exponent divided by that exponent. Its limit at infinity is zero, so only the negative of its value at the lower limit remains. This gives $\dfrac{1}{2187}$.
