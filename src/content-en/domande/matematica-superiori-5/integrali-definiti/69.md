What is the area of the region bounded by $y=4x$ and $y=x^2$?

- $\dfrac{64}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{32}{3}$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$
- $32$

## Explanation

The curves intersect at zero and at $4$. The line lies above the parabola on that interval, so integrate $4x-x^2$. This gives $\dfrac{32}{3}$.
