Two continuous flow rates $f(t)$ and $g(t)$ are recorded for $0\le t\le3$. Which information guarantees that the rates were equal at every instant of the interval?

- Only the total volumes at the end of the three hours are known to agree.
- The volumes accumulated from zero to $b$ agree for every $b\in[0,3]$.
- The two rates have the same maximum.
- The two initial rates agree.

## Explanation

If the two accumulation functions agree throughout the interval, their interior derivatives agree. The fundamental theorem identifies those derivatives with the flow rates. Continuity extends the equality to both endpoints.
