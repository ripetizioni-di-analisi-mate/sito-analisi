The region under $y=\sqrt{8x}$ for $0\leq x\leq1$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. What is the volume of the solid?

- $8 \pi$
- $32 \pi$
- $4 \pi$
- $4$

## Explanation

The disk method gives $V=\pi\int_0^1 y^2\,dx$. Since $y^2=8x$, integrate $\pi\int_0^1 8x\,dx$. Thus the volume is $\dfrac{8\pi}{2}$.
