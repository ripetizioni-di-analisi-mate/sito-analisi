A continuous signal $v(t)$ on $[0,1]$ has mean $m=\int_0^1v(t)\,dt$. For every such signal, which inequality follows from $\int_0^1(v(t)-m)^2\,dt\ge0$?

- $\int_0^1 v(t)^2\,dt\ge 2m^2$
- $\int_0^1 v(t)^2\,dt\le m^2$
- $\int_0^1 v(t)^2\,dt\ge m^2$
- $\int_0^1 v(t)^2\,dt\le |m|$

## Explanation

Expanding the square gives the cross term $-2m\int_0^1v(t)\,dt=-2m^2$ and constant term $m^2$. What remains is the integral of the squared signal minus the square of its mean.
