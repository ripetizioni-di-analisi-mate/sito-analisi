The region under $y=\sin x$ on $[0,\pi]$ rotates about the $x$-axis. Which identity lets you integrate the area of a circular cross-section?

- $\pi\sin^2x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}(1+\cos2x)$
- $\pi\sin^2x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}(1-\cos 2x)$
- $\pi\sin^2x=2\pi\sin x\cos x$
- $\pi\sin^2x=\pi(1-\cos x)$

## Explanation

The cross-sectional area is $\pi\sin^2x$. The cosine double-angle formula gives $\cos 2x=1-2\sin^2x$, yielding the identity and the volume $\pi^2/2$.
