A container has horizontal cross-sectional area $A(h)=1+h$ in $\mathrm{m^2}$ at height $h$ metres. At what height does it contain $4\,\mathrm{m^3}$, measured from the bottom?

- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Area varies with height, so volume is $\int_0^h(1+s)\,ds=h+h^2/2$. Setting this equal to $4$ yields $(h-2)(h+4)=0$; only the positive root is a height.
