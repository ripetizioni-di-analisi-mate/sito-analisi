The region under $y=\sqrt{10x}$ for $0\leq x\leq1$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. What is the volume of the solid?

- $5 \pi$
- $10 \pi$
- $50 \pi$
- $5$

## Explanation

The disk method gives $V=\pi\int_0^1 y^2\,dx$. Since $y^2=10x$, integrate $\pi\int_0^1 10x\,dx$. Thus the volume is $\dfrac{10\pi}{2}$.
