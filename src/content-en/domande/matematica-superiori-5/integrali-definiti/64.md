A convex function is sampled at the endpoints of $[a,b]$. How does the single trapezoidal estimate using $f(a)$ and $f(b)$ compare with the integral?

- It is at least as large as the integral.
- It equals the integral of the derivative.
- It always equals the integral.
- It is always strictly smaller.

## Explanation

By convexity, the chord joining the endpoints is on or above the graph. Integrating this inequality gives the trapezoidal upper bound.
