What is the area between the graph of $f(x)=x-2$ and the $x$-axis on the interval $[0,7]$?

- $\dfrac{29}{2}$
- $29$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$

## Explanation

The graph crosses the axis at $x=2$. The area is the sum of the areas of two right triangles. The legs of the first each have length $2$, and those of the second each have length $5$. This gives $\dfrac{29}{2}$.
