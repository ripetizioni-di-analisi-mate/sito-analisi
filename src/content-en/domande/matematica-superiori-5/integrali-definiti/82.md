A force $F(t)=3t^2$ newtons acts for $0\le t\le2$ seconds. For a $2$ kg mass, what velocity change follows from the impulse relation $\int F\,dt=m\Delta v$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The impulse is $\int_0^2 3t^2\,dt=8\,\mathrm{N\,s}$. Divide by the mass to obtain a velocity change of $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
