The velocity along a straight line is $v(t)=2t-6$, measured in $\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the displacement in meters from $t=0$ to $t=6$ s?

- $18$
- $0$
- $-18$
- $36$

## Explanation

The velocity changes sign at $t=3$. In the signed integral, the two triangular contributions of equal area cancel. This gives $0$.
