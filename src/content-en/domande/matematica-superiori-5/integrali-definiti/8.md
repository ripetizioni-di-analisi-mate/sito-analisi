Find the area of the region bounded by the graph of $y = e^{x}$ and the lines $y = 1$ and $x = 2$.

- $2e^2-2$
- $e^2+1$
- $e^2-1$
- $e^2-3$

## Explanation

The curve meets the line $y=1$ at $x=0$. From $0$ to $2$ the vertical height is $e^x-1$, so the area is $[e^x-x]_0^2=e^2-3$.
