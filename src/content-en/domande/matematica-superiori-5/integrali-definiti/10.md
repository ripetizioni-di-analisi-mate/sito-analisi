Let $F(x)=\int_2^x\dfrac{(t-1)(t-3)}{t^2+1}\,dt$. At which value of $x$ does $F$ have a local minimum?

- $x=3$
- $x=1$
- $x=0$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

By the fundamental theorem, $F'(x)$ equals the integrand evaluated at $x$. Its sign changes from positive to negative at $1$ and from negative to positive at $3$, giving a minimum there.
