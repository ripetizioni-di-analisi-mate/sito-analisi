The region under $y=\sqrt{3x}$ for $0\leq x\leq1$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. What is the volume of the solid?

- $3 \pi$
- $\dfrac{3 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The disk method gives $V=\pi\int_0^1 y^2\,dx$. Since $y^2=3x$, integrate $\pi\int_0^1 3x\,dx$. Thus the volume is $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$.
