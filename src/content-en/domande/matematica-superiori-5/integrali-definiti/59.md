Let $u(t)=t$ and $v(t)=1-t$ on $[0,1]$. What is $\displaystyle\int_0^1u(t)\,v(t)\,dt$ minus the product of the mean values of $u$ and $v$ on $[0,1]$?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

Since the interval $[0,1]$ has length $1$, the mean value of a function on $[0,1]$ equals its integral. The mean value of $u$ is $\displaystyle\int_0^1t\,dt=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and that of $v$ is also $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the product of the mean values is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The integral of the product is $\displaystyle\int_0^1(t-t^2)\,dt=\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Therefore the difference equals $\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{4}=-\dfrac{1}{12}$.
