Two signals on $[0,1]$ are $u(t)=t$ and $v(t)=1-t$. A program estimates $\int_0^1u(t)v(t)\,dt$ by multiplying their means. What is the exact value minus this estimate?

- $\dfrac1{12}$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac16$
- $-\dfrac1{12}$

## Explanation

Both means are $1/2$, so the program outputs $1/4$. Integrating the product gives $\int_0^1(t-t^2)\,dt=1/2-1/3=1/6$. The difference is $1/6-1/4=-1/12$. The two signals reach their highest values at opposite ends of the interval.
