What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{-4}^{4} x^{3} + 3 x\,dx$?

- $128.0$
- $0$
- $48$
- $64.0$

## Explanation

The integrand is odd and the interval is symmetric. The contributions to the left and right of zero have equal absolute values and opposite signs. This gives $0$.
