What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\dfrac{\sqrt{3} \sqrt{\pi}}{6}} x \cos{\left(3 x^{2} \right)}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{12}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$
- $0.166666666666667$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{6}$

## Explanation

With $u=ax^2$ we have $du=2ax\,dx$. The limits in $u$ are zero and $\pi/4$, so evaluate the sine at these points. This gives $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{12}$.
