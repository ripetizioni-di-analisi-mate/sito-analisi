For $0\leq t\leq2$ the velocity is $v(t)=2-t$ m/s. How far does the body travel, in meters?

- $0$
- $2$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The velocity is nonnegative on the interval. The area under the graph is the area of a triangle with base two seconds and height two meters per second. This gives $2$.
