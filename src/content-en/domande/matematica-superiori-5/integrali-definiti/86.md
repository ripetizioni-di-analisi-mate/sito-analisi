A force along the $x$-axis is given by $F(x)=3x+7$ in newtons. How much work does it do, in joules, from $x=0$ to $x=4$ m?

- $76$
- $24$
- $19$
- $52$

## Explanation

Work is the integral of the component of force in the direction of displacement. Integrate the linear term and add the contribution from the constant part. This gives $52$.
