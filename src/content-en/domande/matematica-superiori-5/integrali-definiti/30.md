A machine uses $3$ litres per hour for one hour and $1$ litre per hour for the next two hours. What is its average hourly consumption over the three hours?

- $\dfrac43\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $2\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $\dfrac53\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $5\,\mathrm{L/h}$

## Explanation

The integral of the consumption rate is $3\cdot1+1\cdot2=5$ litres. Divide this total by the three hours of operation to obtain the mean rate.
