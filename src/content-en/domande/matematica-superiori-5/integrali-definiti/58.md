For which value of $a > 0$ does the region between the parabola $y = x^2$ and the line $y = a x$ have area $36$?

- $a = 3 \sqrt[3]{4}$
- $a = 6$
- $a = 6 \sqrt[3]{2}$
- $a = 2 \sqrt[3]{9}$

## Explanation

The equation $x^2 = a x$ gives the intersections $x = 0$ and $x = a$, and on $[0, a]$ the line lies above the parabola. So the area is
$$
\mathcal{A} = \int_0^{a} \left( a x - x^2 \right) dx = \frac{a^3}{2} - \frac{a^3}{3} = \frac{a^3}{6} .
$$
The condition $\dfrac{a^3}{6} = 36$ gives $a^3 = 216$, therefore $a = 6$.
