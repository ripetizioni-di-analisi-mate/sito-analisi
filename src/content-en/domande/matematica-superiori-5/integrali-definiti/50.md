What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1} e^{3 x}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{e^{3}}{3}$
- $-1 + e^{3}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{e^{3}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{e^{3}}{3}$

## Explanation

The antiderivative contains a factor equal to the reciprocal of the coefficient of $x$. Evaluate it at the endpoints and subtract the contribution at zero. This gives $- \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{e^{3}}{3}$.
