The region under $y=\sqrt{5x}$ for $0\leq x\leq1$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. What is the volume of the solid?

- $5 \pi$
- $\dfrac{25 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5 \pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The disk method gives $V=\pi\int_0^1 y^2\,dx$. Since $y^2=5x$, integrate $\pi\int_0^1 5x\,dx$. Thus the volume is $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$.
