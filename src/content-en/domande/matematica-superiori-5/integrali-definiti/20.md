Let $f$ be integrable on $[0, 1]$, and let $P$ be a partition of $[0, 1]$ for which the lower sum is $s(P) = 2.1$ and the upper sum is $S(P) = 2.4$. Which of the following numbers can be the value of $\displaystyle\int_0^1 f(x)\, dx$?

- $2$
- $2.3$
- $2.6$
- $4.5$

## Explanation

By the **definition of the Riemann integral**, the integral of $f$ is the supremum of the lower sums and also the infimum of the upper sums. Therefore every partition $P$ satisfies
$$
s(P) \leq \int_0^1 f(x)\, dx \leq S(P) ,
$$
so the integral belongs to the interval $[2.1, 2.4]$. Among the proposed numbers only $2.3$ belongs to this interval.
