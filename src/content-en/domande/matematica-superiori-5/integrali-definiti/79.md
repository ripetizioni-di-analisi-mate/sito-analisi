A solid of revolution about the $x$-axis is generated by the region between $y=5$ and $y=3$ for $0\leq x\leq 2$. What is its volume?

- $8 \pi$
- $16 \pi$
- $32 \pi$
- $50 \pi$

## Explanation

The cross sections perpendicular to the axis are washers. Multiply the area of a washer, $\pi(5^2-3^2)$, by the length $2$. This gives $32 \pi$.
