What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\dfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}{4}} x \cos{\left(4 x^{2} \right)}\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{16}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$
- $0.125$

## Explanation

With $u=ax^2$ we have $du=2ax\,dx$. The limits in $u$ are zero and $\pi/4$, so evaluate the sine at these points. This gives $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{16}$.
