The velocity along a straight line is $v(t)=4t-20$, measured in $\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the displacement in meters from $t=0$ to $t=10$ s?

- $-100$
- $0$
- $100$
- $200$

## Explanation

The velocity changes sign at $t=5$. In the signed integral, the two triangular contributions of equal area cancel. This gives $0$.
