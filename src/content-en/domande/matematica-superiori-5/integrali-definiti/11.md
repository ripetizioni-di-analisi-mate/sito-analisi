What is the volume of the solid generated by a full rotation about the $x$-axis of the curvilinear trapezoid under $f(x) = e^{x}$ on the interval $[0, 1]$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}(e^2-1)$
- $\pi(e-1)$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}e^2$
- $\pi(e^2-1)$

## Explanation

Each cross-section is a disk of radius $e^x$ and area $\pi e^{2x}$. Thus the volume is $\pi\displaystyle\int_0^1e^{2x}\,dx=\pi\left[\dfrac{e^{2x}}{2}\right]_0^1=\dfrac{\pi}{2}(e^2-1)$.
