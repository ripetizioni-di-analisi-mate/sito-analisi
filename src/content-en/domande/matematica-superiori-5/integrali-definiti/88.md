If $\int_0^{2} f(x)\,dx=5$ and $\int_0^{3} f(x)\,dx=7$, what is $\int_{3}^{2} f(x)\,dx$?

- $12$
- $2$
- $7$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Add the integrals along the path from $3$ to zero and from zero to $2$. Reversing the limits of the first integral changes its sign. This gives $-2$.
