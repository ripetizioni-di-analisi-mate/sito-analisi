What is the area of the region bounded by the parabola $y = -x^2 + 2x + 3$ and the $x$-axis?

- $4$
- $16$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$

## Explanation

Since $-x^2+2x+3=4-(x-1)^2$, the parabola crosses the $x$-axis at $x=-1$ and $x=3$ and lies above the axis between the two roots. With the substitution $u=x-1$ the region is symmetric about $u=0$, so the area is $2\displaystyle\int_0^2(4-u^2)\,du=2\left(8-\dfrac{8}{3}\right)=\dfrac{32}{3}$.
