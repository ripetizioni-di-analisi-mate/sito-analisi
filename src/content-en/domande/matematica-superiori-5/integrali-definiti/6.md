Find the area of the region bounded by the parabola $y = -x^2 + 2x + 3$ and the $x$-axis.

- $4$
- $16$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$

## Explanation

The parabola is $4-(x-1)^2$ and crosses the axis at $-1$ and $3$. With $u=x-1$, its area is $2\int_0^2(4-u^2)\,du=2(8-8/3)$.
