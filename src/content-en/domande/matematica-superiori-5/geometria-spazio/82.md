What is the tangent plane to the sphere with center $C=(2,5,0)$ at the point $P=(2,5,3)$?

- $x=2$
- $z=0$
- $z=3$
- $y=5$

## Explanation

The radius $CP$ is parallel to the $z$-axis and gives a normal vector to the tangent plane. The plane passes through a point of height $3$.
