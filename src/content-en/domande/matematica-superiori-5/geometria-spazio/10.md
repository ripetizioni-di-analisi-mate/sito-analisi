To check whether lines $(t,1,2t)$ and $(1,s,3+s)$ intersect, equate the first two coordinates. What follows from the third one?

- The heights are $2$ and $4$ and the nonparallel lines are skew.
- Both heights equal $2$ and the lines intersect.
- The heights differ and the lines are parallel.
- The parameter is unrestricted and the lines coincide.

## Explanation

The first two coordinates force $t=s=1$. The candidates have different third coordinates. Moreover the directions $(1,0,2)$ and $(0,1,1)$ are not proportional.
