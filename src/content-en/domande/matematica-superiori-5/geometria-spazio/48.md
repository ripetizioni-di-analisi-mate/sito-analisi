How far apart are the points $P=\left( 4, \  9, \  5\right)$ and $Q=\left( -4, \  0, \  6\right)$ in space?

- $\sqrt{146}$
- $146$
- $7 \sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{145}$

## Explanation

The absolute differences between corresponding coordinates are $8$, $9$ and $1$. The distance is the square root of the sum of their squares. This gives $\sqrt{146}$.
