The line $(x,y,z)=(3,7,0)+t(1,-1,4)$ meets the plane $z=16$. What is $t$ at the intersection?

- $-4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $16$

## Explanation

The $z$-coordinate on the line equals $4t$. Set it equal to the height of the plane and divide by the coefficient of $t$. This gives $4$.
