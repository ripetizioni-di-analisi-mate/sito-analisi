Let $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ be four non-coplanar points of space and let $M$, $N$, $P$, $Q$ be the midpoints of the segments $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$. Which statement about the quadrilateral $MNPQ$ is true for every choice of the four points?

- It is a parallelogram
- Its four vertices are not coplanar
- It is a rectangle
- It is a trapezoid with only one pair of parallel sides

## Explanation

Since $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of $AB$ and $BC$, one has $\overrightarrow{MN} = \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} + \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BC} = \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AC}$. With the same computation $\overrightarrow{QP} = \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AD} + \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{DC} = \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AC}$. Therefore $\overrightarrow{MN} = \overrightarrow{QP}$, that is, the sides $MN$ and $QP$ are parallel and of the same length, so $MNPQ$ is a **parallelogram**, in particular a planar one. It is not always a rectangle: for $A = (0, 0, 0)$, $B = (1, 0, 0)$, $C = (1, 1, 0)$, $D = (0, 0, 1)$ one has $\overrightarrow{MN} = \left( \dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2}, 0 \right)$ and $\overrightarrow{NP} = \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BD} = \left( -\dfrac{1}{2}, 0, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$, whose dot product equals $-\dfrac{1}{4} \neq 0$.
