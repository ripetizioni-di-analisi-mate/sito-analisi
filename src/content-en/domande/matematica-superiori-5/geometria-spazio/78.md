A sphere passes through $O=(0,0,0)$, $A=(2,0,0)$, $B=(0,4,0)$ and $C=(0,0,6)$. What are the coordinates of its centre?

- $(2,4,6)$
- $(1,2,3)$
- $(1,1,1)$
- $(0,0,0)$

## Explanation

Equal distance from $O$ and $A$ gives $x=1$. The other two pairs give $y=2$ and $z=3$. The three perpendicular bisector planes meet at the required centre.
