What is the midpoint of the segment joining $A=(4,0,9)$ and $B=(0,5,-9)$?

- $\left( 4, \  5, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  \dfrac{5}{2}, \  9\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  \dfrac{5}{2}, \  0\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5}{2}, \  2, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

Average the two coordinates on each axis. The coordinates along $z$ are opposites, so their average is zero.
