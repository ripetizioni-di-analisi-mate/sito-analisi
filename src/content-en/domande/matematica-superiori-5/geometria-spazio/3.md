In the triangle with $A=(1,0,0)$, $B=(0,2,0)$ and $C=(0,0,2)$, the vector $\overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AC}$ equals $(4,2,2)$. What is the area?

- $6$
- $2\sqrt6$
- $\sqrt6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The norm of the cross product is $\sqrt{16+4+4}=2\sqrt6$. It measures the area of the parallelogram on the two sides, which is twice the triangle's area.
