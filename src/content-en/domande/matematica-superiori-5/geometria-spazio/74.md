What are the relative positions of the planes $3x+y=1$ and $6x+2y=7$?

- Intersecting along a line without being perpendicular.
- Perpendicular.
- Parallel and distinct.
- Coincident.

## Explanation

The normal vectors are proportional. Doubling the first equation would give a constant term of two, different from that of the second plane.
