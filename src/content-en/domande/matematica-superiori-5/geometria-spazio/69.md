What is the distance from $P=(4,9,5)$ to the plane $3x+4y=0$?

- $\dfrac{48}{5}$
- $\dfrac{48}{7}$
- $48$
- $\dfrac{53}{5}$

## Explanation

Evaluate $3x+4y$ at the point and divide the absolute value of the result by the magnitude of the normal vector, which is five. This gives $\dfrac{48}{5}$.
