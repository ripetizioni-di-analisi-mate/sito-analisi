The line $(x,y,z)=(2,5,0)+t(1,-1,3)$ meets the plane $z=9$. What is $t$ at the intersection?

- $-3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

The $z$-coordinate on the line equals $3t$. Set it equal to the height of the plane and divide by the coefficient of $t$. This gives $3$.
