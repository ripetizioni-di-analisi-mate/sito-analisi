A line is parametrised by $P(t)=(t,2t,1-t)$ for $t\in\mathbb R$. For which values of $t$ does the point lie in the closed cube $0\leq x,y,z\leq1$?

- $0\le t\le 1$
- $-\dfrac12\le t\le \dfrac12$
- $\dfrac12\le t\le1$
- $0\le t\le \dfrac12$

## Explanation

The first and third coordinates both require $0\le t\le1$. The second imposes the tighter condition $0\le t\le1/2$. The intersection of these conditions describes the entire part of the line inside the cube, including its two boundary points.
