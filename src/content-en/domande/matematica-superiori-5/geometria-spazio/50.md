What is the midpoint of the segment joining $A=(3,0,7)$ and $B=(0,4,-7)$?

- $\left( 3, \  4, \  0\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, \  2, \  7\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, \  2, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  \dfrac{3}{2}, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

Average the two coordinates on each axis. The coordinates along $z$ are opposites, so their average is zero.
