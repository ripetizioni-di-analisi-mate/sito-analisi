What is the distance between the point $P(0,0,3)$ and the plane $z=0$?

- $0$
- $9$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The plane $z=0$ is the plane of the first two coordinate axes. The segment perpendicular to the plane has length equal to the absolute value of the $z$-coordinate. Since $3>0$, the distance is $3$.
