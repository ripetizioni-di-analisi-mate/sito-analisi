The line $(2-t,1+2t,3t)$ crosses the plane $x+y+z=7$. Which first calculation uniquely determines the intersection?

- Set $t=0$ regardless of the plane.
- Solve $3+6t=7$.
- Set $x=y=z=7/3$.
- Solve $3+4t=7$.

## Explanation

Substitution of the three coordinates into the plane gives $2-t+1+2t+3t=7$. From $t=1$ we get the unique common point $(1,3,3)$.
