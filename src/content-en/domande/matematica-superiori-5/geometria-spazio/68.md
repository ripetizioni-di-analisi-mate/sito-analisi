What is the distance from $P=(3,7,4)$ to the plane $3x+4y=0$?

- $37$
- $\dfrac{41}{5}$
- $\dfrac{37}{7}$
- $\dfrac{37}{5}$

## Explanation

Evaluate $3x+4y$ at the point and divide the absolute value of the result by the magnitude of the normal vector, which is five. This gives $\dfrac{37}{5}$.
