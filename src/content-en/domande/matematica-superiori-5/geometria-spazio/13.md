A sphere has centre $C=(1,-2,2)$ and is tangent to the plane $x+2y-2z-2=0$. Which is its equation?

- $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2+(z-2)^2=81$
- $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2+(z-2)^2=3$
- $(x+1)^2+(y-2)^2+(z+2)^2=9$
- $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2+(z-2)^2=9$

## Explanation

The centre's distance from the plane is $|1-4-4-2|/\sqrt{1+4+4}=3$. This is the radius, so its square nine appears in the sphere's equation.
