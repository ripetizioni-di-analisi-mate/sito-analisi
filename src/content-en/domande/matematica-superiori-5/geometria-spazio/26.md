What is the distance between $O(0,0,0)$ and $P(7,14,14)$?

- $35$
- $21$
- $63$
- $7$

## Explanation

The distance in space is the square root of the sum of the squared differences between corresponding coordinates. Here the sum is $\sqrt{49+196+196}$. Its square root is $21$.
