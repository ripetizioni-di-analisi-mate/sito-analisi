The line $(x,y,z)=(4,9,0)+t(1,-1,5)$ meets the plane $z=25$. What is $t$ at the intersection?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $25$
- $5$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The $z$-coordinate on the line equals $5t$. Set it equal to the height of the plane and divide by the coefficient of $t$. This gives $5$.
