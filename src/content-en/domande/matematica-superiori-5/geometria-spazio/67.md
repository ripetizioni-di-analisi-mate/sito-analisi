What is the distance from $P=(2,5,3)$ to the plane $3x+4y=0$?

- $\dfrac{29}{5}$
- $\dfrac{26}{5}$
- $26$
- $\dfrac{26}{7}$

## Explanation

Evaluate $3x+4y$ at the point and divide the absolute value of the result by the magnitude of the normal vector, which is five. This gives $\dfrac{26}{5}$.
