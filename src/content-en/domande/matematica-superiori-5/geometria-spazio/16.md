What is the distance between $O(0,0,0)$ and $P(5,10,10)$?

- $25$
- $45$
- $5$
- $15$

## Explanation

The distance in space is the square root of the sum of the squared differences between corresponding coordinates. Here the sum is $\sqrt{25+100+100}$. Its square root is $15$.
