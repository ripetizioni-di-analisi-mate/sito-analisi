For the triangle with vertices $A=(1,0,1)$, $B=(3,2,1)$, $C=(4,0,3)$, take the plane parallel to $AB$ through $C$ with normal $(1,-1,0)$. How far is $A$ from this plane?

- $3$
- $\dfrac3{\sqrt2}$
- $\sqrt2$
- $\dfrac5{\sqrt2}$

## Explanation

The plane through $C$ has equation $x-y-4=0$. The vector $AB=(2,2,0)$ is orthogonal to the normal. Applying the point-to-plane distance formula at $A$ gives $\dfrac{|1-4|}{\sqrt2}$.
