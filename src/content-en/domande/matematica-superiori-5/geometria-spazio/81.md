One endpoint of a segment lies inside the sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2=4$, and the other lies outside. How many times does it cross the spherical surface?

- Zero times.
- Twice.
- Exactly once.
- At infinitely many points.

## Explanation

A line meets the solid ball in an interval. Starting inside the ball, the segment leaves that interval exactly once before reaching its outside endpoint.
