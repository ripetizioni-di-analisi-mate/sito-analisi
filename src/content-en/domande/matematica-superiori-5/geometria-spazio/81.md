What is the center of the sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2-8x+18y-10z=0$?

- $\left( 8, \  -18, \  10\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  -9, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  9, \  5\right)$
- $\left( -4, \  9, \  -5\right)$

## Explanation

Complete the square in each of the three coordinates. The center makes each of the three resulting binomials zero before it is squared.
