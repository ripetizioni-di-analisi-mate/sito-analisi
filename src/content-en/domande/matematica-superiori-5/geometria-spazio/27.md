What is the distance of the point $P = (3, -4, 12)$ from the $z$-axis?

- $13$
- $7$
- $5$
- $25$

## Explanation

Let $H = (0, 0, 12)$ be the point of the $z$-axis at the same height as $P$. The vector $\overrightarrow{HP} = (3, -4, 0)$ is orthogonal to $\vec{k} = (0, 0, 1)$, so $H$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to the axis. Therefore the distance is
$$
\|\overrightarrow{HP}\| = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5 .
$$
The value $13$ is the distance of $P$ from the origin, $25$ is the square of the required distance and $7 = 3 + 4$ adds the absolute values of the two coordinates instead of their squares.
