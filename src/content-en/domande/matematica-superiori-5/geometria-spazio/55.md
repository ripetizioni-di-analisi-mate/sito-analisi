In a tetrahedron $OABC$, the midpoints of $OA,OB,OC$ determine a plane that cuts off a smaller tetrahedron at the vertex $O$. What fraction of the total volume does the smaller tetrahedron occupy?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac38$

## Explanation

A dilation with centre $O$ and scale factor $1/2$ sends the original tetrahedron to the smaller one. The base area is multiplied by $1/4$ and the height by $1/2$. Together these factors multiply the volume by $1/8$.
