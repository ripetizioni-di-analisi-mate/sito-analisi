How far apart are the points $P=\left( 2, \  5, \  3\right)$ and $Q=\left( -2, \  0, \  4\right)$ in space?

- $\sqrt{30}$
- $42$
- $\sqrt{41}$
- $\sqrt{42}$

## Explanation

The absolute differences between corresponding coordinates are $4$, $5$ and $1$. The distance is the square root of the sum of their squares. This gives $\sqrt{42}$.
