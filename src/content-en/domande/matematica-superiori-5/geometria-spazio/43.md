What is the distance between the point $P(0,0,10)$ and the plane $z=0$?

- $0$
- $100$
- $10$
- $20$

## Explanation

The plane $z=0$ is the plane of the first two coordinate axes. The segment perpendicular to the plane has length equal to the absolute value of the $z$-coordinate. Since $10>0$, the distance is $10$.
