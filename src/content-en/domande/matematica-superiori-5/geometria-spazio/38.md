What is the distance between the point $P(0,0,9)$ and the plane $z=0$?

- $0$
- $9$
- $81$
- $18$

## Explanation

The plane $z=0$ is the plane of the first two coordinate axes. The segment perpendicular to the plane has length equal to the absolute value of the $z$-coordinate. Since $9>0$, the distance is $9$.
