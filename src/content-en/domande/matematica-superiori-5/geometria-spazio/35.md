Opposite vertices of a rectangular box with edge lengths $1$, $2$ and $4$ cm are to be joined by a taut thread on the surface, without entering the box. What is the length of the shortest path?

- $\sqrt{21}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{29}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Unfolding two adjacent faces turns the thread into the diagonal of a rectangle. The three candidate lengths are $\sqrt{(1+2)^2+4^2}=5$, $\sqrt{(1+4)^2+2^2}=\sqrt{29}$ and $\sqrt{(2+4)^2+1^2}=\sqrt{37}$. The space diagonal $\sqrt{21}$ passes through the interior.
