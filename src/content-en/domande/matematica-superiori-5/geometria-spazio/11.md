The perpendicular projection of $P=(3,1,0)$ onto the plane $2x-2y+z+5=0$ is $H=(1,3,-1)$. What is the reflection of $P$ in the plane?

- $(-1,5,-2)$
- $(2,-2,1)$
- $(1,3,-1)$
- $(5,-1,1)$

## Explanation

The foot of the perpendicular is the midpoint between the point and its reflection. The required point is therefore $2H-P=(-1,5,-2)$.
