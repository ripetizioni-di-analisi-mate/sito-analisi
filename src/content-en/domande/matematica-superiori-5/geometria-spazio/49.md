What is the midpoint of the segment joining $A=(2,0,5)$ and $B=(0,3,-5)$?

- $\left( 1, \  \dfrac{3}{2}, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 1, \  \dfrac{3}{2}, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3, \  0\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, \  1, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

Average the two coordinates on each axis. The coordinates along $z$ are opposites, so their average is zero.
