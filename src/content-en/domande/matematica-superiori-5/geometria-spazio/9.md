The lines $(1+t,t,2-t)$ and $(1+s,s,-1+2s)$ both pass through $(2,1,1)$. At what angle do they meet?

- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $0^\circ$

## Explanation

The direction vectors $(1,1,-1)$ and $(1,1,2)$ have dot product $1+1-2=0$. Since they share a point, the lines are perpendicular.
