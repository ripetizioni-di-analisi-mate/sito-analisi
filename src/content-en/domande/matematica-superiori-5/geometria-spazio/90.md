What are the relative positions of the line $(x,y,z)=(4,9,0)+t(1,0,5)$ and the plane $y=10$?

- The line is parallel to the plane and lies outside it.
- The line intersects the plane at an oblique angle.
- The line is perpendicular to the plane.
- The line lies in the plane.

## Explanation

Along the line, the $y$-coordinate is always $9$. It differs from the height required by the plane, while the direction vector of the line is orthogonal to the normal vector of the plane.
