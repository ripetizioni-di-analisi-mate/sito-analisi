The plane $2x + 3y + z = 6$ and the three coordinate planes bound a tetrahedron. What is the volume of the tetrahedron?

- $36$
- $6$
- $18$
- $1$

## Explanation

The plane meets the $x$-axis where $y = z = 0$, that is, at $(3, 0, 0)$; in the same way it meets the $y$-axis at $(0, 2, 0)$ and the $z$-axis at $(0, 0, 6)$. The tetrahedron therefore has a vertex at the origin and three edges along the axes, of lengths $3$, $2$ and $6$ and pairwise orthogonal. The base in the plane $z = 0$ is a right triangle of area $\dfrac{3 \cdot 2}{2} = 3$ and the corresponding height is $6$, therefore the volume is
$$
V = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 3 \cdot 6 = 6 .
$$
The value $36$ is the product of the three edges without the factor $\dfrac{1}{6}$, and the value $1$ is obtained by using the coefficients $2$, $3$, $1$ instead of the intercepts with the axes.
