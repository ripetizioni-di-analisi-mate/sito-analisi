A camera at the origin projects $(4,6,2)$ onto the plane $z=1$ along the line through the origin and the point. What image does it produce?

- $(4,3,1)$
- $(4,6,1)$
- $(2,3,1)$
- $(8,12,1)$

## Explanation

Points on the ray have coordinates $(4t,6t,2t)$. The image plane imposes $2t=1$, so both of the first two coordinates are halved.
