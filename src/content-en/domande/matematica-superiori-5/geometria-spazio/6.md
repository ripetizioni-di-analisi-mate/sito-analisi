A plane passes through $P=(2,-1,0)$ and is perpendicular to the line $(1+3t,2t,-t)$. Which vector can be used as the plane's normal?

- $(1,0,0)$
- $(3,2,-1)$
- $(2,-1,0)$
- $(3,-2,1)$

## Explanation

The direction of a perpendicular line is normal to the plane. Read the entries from the coefficients of $t$. The equation through $P$ is $3(x-2)+2(y+1)-z=0$.
