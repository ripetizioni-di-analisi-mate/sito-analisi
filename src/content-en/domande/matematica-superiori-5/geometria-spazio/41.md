What is the distance between $O(0,0,0)$ and $P(10,20,20)$?

- $30$
- $50$
- $90$
- $10$

## Explanation

The distance in space is the square root of the sum of the squared differences between corresponding coordinates. Here the sum is $\sqrt{100+400+400}$. Its square root is $30$.
