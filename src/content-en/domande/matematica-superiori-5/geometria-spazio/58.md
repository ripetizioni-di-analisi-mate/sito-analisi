The adjacent sides of a parallelogram are $u=(2,0,0)$ and $v=(3,5,0)$. What is its area?

- $5$
- $2 \sqrt{34}$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

With $u$ as the base, the height is the magnitude of the component of $v$ perpendicular to $u$. Multiply the base by that component. This gives $10$.
