Which vector is normal to the plane $4x-9y+z=5$?

- $\left( 4, \  -9, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  -9, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 9, \  4, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  9, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

A normal vector has as its components the coefficients of $x$, $y$ and $z$ on the left-hand side. The term on the right determines the position of the plane.
