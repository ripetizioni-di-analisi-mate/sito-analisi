What is the distance between the point $P(0,0,8)$ and the plane $z=0$?

- $8$
- $0$
- $64$
- $16$

## Explanation

The plane $z=0$ is the plane of the first two coordinate axes. The segment perpendicular to the plane has length equal to the absolute value of the $z$-coordinate. Since $8>0$, the distance is $8$.
