Which plane contains all three points $A=(1,1,1)$, $B=(2,0,1)$ and $C=(1,2,3)$?

- $2x+2y-z=3$
- $2x+2y+z=5$
- $x+y=2$
- $2x-2y+z=1$

## Explanation

Two directions in the plane are $(1,-1,0)$ and $(0,1,2)$. The normal $(2,2,-1)$ is perpendicular to both. Substitution of one point gives the constant three.
