The adjacent sides of a parallelogram are $u=(3,0,0)$ and $v=(4,7,0)$. What is its area?

- $12$
- $21$
- $3 \sqrt{65}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$

## Explanation

With $u$ as the base, the height is the magnitude of the component of $v$ perpendicular to $u$. Multiply the base by that component. This gives $21$.
