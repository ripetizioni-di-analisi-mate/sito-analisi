Let $r$ be the line $P = (1, 2, 0) + t\,(2, -1, 2)$, with $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Which point of $r$ with $t > 0$ is at distance $6$ from the point $P_0 = (1, 2, 0)$?

- $(5, 0, 4)$
- $(3, 1, 2)$
- $(13, -4, 12)$
- $(7, -1, 6)$

## Explanation

The point of $r$ with parameter $t$ is $P_0 + t\,\vec{v}$, with $\vec{v} = (2, -1, 2)$, so its distance from $P_0$ is $\|t\,\vec{v}\| = |t|\,\|\vec{v}\|$. Since $\|\vec{v}\| = \sqrt{4 + 1 + 4} = 3$, the distance equals $3|t|$, and the condition $3t = 6$ with $t > 0$ gives $t = 2$. The required point is therefore $(1 + 4,\ 2 - 2,\ 0 + 4) = (5, 0, 4)$. The point $(13, -4, 12)$ corresponds to $t = 6$, that is, to treating the parameter as a length, and it is at distance $18$ from $P_0$.
