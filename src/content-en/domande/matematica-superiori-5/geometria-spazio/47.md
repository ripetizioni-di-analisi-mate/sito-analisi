How far apart are the points $P=\left( 3, \  7, \  4\right)$ and $Q=\left( -3, \  0, \  5\right)$ in space?

- $\sqrt{86}$
- $\sqrt{85}$
- $\sqrt{59}$
- $86$

## Explanation

The absolute differences between corresponding coordinates are $6$, $7$ and $1$. The distance is the square root of the sum of their squares. This gives $\sqrt{86}$.
