A coordinate system moves its origin from $O$ to $O'=(2,-1,4)$ without changing the axes or units. What is the equation of the original plane $x+z=8$ in the new coordinates $(x',y',z')$?

- $x'+z'=14$
- $x'+z'=2$
- $x'+z'=8$
- $x'-z'=2$

## Explanation

The original coordinates satisfy $x=x'+2$ and $z=z'+4$. Substituting gives $x'+2+z'+4=8$, so the new constant is $2$.
