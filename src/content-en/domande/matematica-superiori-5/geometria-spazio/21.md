What is the distance between $O(0,0,0)$ and $P(6,12,12)$?

- $18$
- $30$
- $54$
- $6$

## Explanation

The distance in space is the square root of the sum of the squared differences between corresponding coordinates. Here the sum is $\sqrt{36+144+144}$. Its square root is $18$.
