The sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2-2x-4z-20=0$ meets the plane $z=6$. Which equation in that plane describes the intersection circle?

- $(x-1)^2+y^2=16$
- $(x-1)^2+y^2=25$
- $(x+1)^2+y^2=9$
- $(x-1)^2+y^2=9$

## Explanation

The sphere can be written as $(x-1)^2+y^2+(z-2)^2=25$. On setting $z=6$ its last square is $16$, leaving radius $3$ for the section.
