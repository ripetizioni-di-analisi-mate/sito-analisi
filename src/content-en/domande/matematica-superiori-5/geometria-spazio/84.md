What is the tangent plane to the sphere with center $C=(4,9,0)$ at the point $P=(4,9,5)$?

- $z=5$
- $x=4$
- $z=0$
- $y=9$

## Explanation

The radius $CP$ is parallel to the $z$-axis and gives a normal vector to the tangent plane. The plane passes through a point of height $5$.
