The sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2=25$ has a chord with endpoints $(-4,3,0)$ and $(4,3,0)$. How far from the centre is the closest point on this chord?

- $8$
- $4$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The chord consists of points $(x,3,0)$ with $-4\le x\le4$. The squared distance is $x^2+9$, which is smallest at $x=0$, giving distance $3$.
