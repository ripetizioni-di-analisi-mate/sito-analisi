What is the distance between $O(0,0,0)$ and $P(9,18,18)$?

- $45$
- $81$
- $9$
- $27$

## Explanation

The distance in space is the square root of the sum of the squared differences between corresponding coordinates. Here the sum is $\sqrt{81+324+324}$. Its square root is $27$.
