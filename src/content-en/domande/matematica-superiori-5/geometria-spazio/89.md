A solid is described by $|x|+|y|+|z|\le2$. What shape is its cross-section in the plane $z=1$?

- A filled square with side length $2$.
- A disk of radius $1$.
- A filled square with diagonals of length $2$.
- A right triangle.

## Explanation

In the assigned plane the condition reduces to $|x|+|y|\le1$. The vertices have plane coordinates $(1,0),(0,1),(-1,0),(0,-1)$, so each diagonal has length $2$.
