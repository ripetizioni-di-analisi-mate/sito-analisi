Let $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ be any four points in space. Which vector is $\overrightarrow{AB} - \overrightarrow{CB} + \overrightarrow{CD}$ equal to?

- $\overrightarrow{DA}$
- $\overrightarrow{AC}$
- $\vec{0}$
- $\overrightarrow{AD}$

## Explanation

Since $-\overrightarrow{CB} = \overrightarrow{BC}$, the expression is $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{CD}$. By the head-to-tail rule each vector starts at the head of the previous one, therefore $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AC}$ and then $\overrightarrow{AC} + \overrightarrow{CD} = \overrightarrow{AD}$. The vector $\overrightarrow{DA}$ is the opposite of the result, and $\vec{0}$ would be obtained by also adding $\overrightarrow{DA}$, closing the path.
