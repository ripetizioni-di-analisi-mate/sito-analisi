Two probes follow $P(t)=(t,0,t)$ and $Q(t)=(1,t-2,1)$ using the same clock. Which statement correctly distinguishes the paths from the motion of the probes?

- The paths meet at $(1,0,1)$, reached at different times.
- The probes collide at $t=1$.
- The probes collide at $t=2$.
- The lines are skew.

## Explanation

The first probe reaches $(1,0,1)$ at $t=1$ and the second at $t=2$. A collision requires all three coordinates to agree at the same instant.
