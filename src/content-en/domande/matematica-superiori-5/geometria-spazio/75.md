The surface of a sphere of radius $2$ centred at the origin is projected orthogonally onto the plane $z=0$. Which figures are its projection and its intersection with the plane, respectively?

- The same boundary circle in both cases.
- The boundary circle and the disk it encloses.
- A closed disk and its boundary circle.
- The same closed disk in both cases.

## Explanation

A point $(x,y,0)$ belongs to the projection when some real height $z$ satisfies $x^2+y^2+z^2=4$. This requires $x^2+y^2\leq4$, the disk. For the intersection we set $z=0$ in the sphere equation, leaving $x^2+y^2=4$, its boundary circle.
