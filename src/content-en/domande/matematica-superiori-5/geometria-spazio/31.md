What is the distance between $O(0,0,0)$ and $P(8,16,16)$?

- $40$
- $72$
- $24$
- $8$

## Explanation

The distance in space is the square root of the sum of the squared differences between corresponding coordinates. Here the sum is $\sqrt{64+256+256}$. Its square root is $24$.
