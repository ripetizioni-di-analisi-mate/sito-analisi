A point $P$ lies outside a sphere. How many distinct lines in space pass through $P$ and are tangent to the sphere?

- Infinitely many.
- Two.
- One.
- None.

## Explanation

In each plane through the centre and $P$, the sphere has a circular section with two tangents from $P$. Rotating the plane about the line joining $P$ to the centre produces infinitely many distinct lines.
