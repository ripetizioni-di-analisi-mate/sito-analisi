What is the tangent plane to the sphere with center $C=(3,7,0)$ at the point $P=(3,7,4)$?

- $x=3$
- $y=7$
- $z=0$
- $z=4$

## Explanation

The radius $CP$ is parallel to the $z$-axis and gives a normal vector to the tangent plane. The plane passes through a point of height $4$.
