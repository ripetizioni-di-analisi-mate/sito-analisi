Two concentric spheres have equations $x^2+y^2+z^2=4$ and $x^2+y^2+z^2=25$. How far apart are their surfaces along a common radius?

- $7$
- $5$
- $21$
- $3$

## Explanation

The radii are the positive square roots of the two right-hand sides. Along a ray from the common center, subtract the smaller radius from the larger one. This gives $3$.
