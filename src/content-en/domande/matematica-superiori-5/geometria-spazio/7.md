Two planes are given by $x+y+z=3$ and $x-y=1$. Which parametrization describes their entire intersection line?

- $(2+t,1+t,-2t)$
- $(2+t,1-t,0)$
- $(2-t,1+t,-2t)$
- $(1+t,2+t,-2t)$

## Explanation

The point $(2,1,0)$ belongs to both. Changes in the first two coordinates must be equal, and the third coordinate must change by twice as much in the opposite direction to preserve both left-hand sides.
