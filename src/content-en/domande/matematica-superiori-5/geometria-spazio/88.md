What are the relative positions of the line $(x,y,z)=(2,5,0)+t(1,0,3)$ and the plane $y=6$?

- The line is perpendicular to the plane.
- The line is parallel to the plane and lies outside it.
- The line lies in the plane.
- The line intersects the plane at an oblique angle.

## Explanation

Along the line, the $y$-coordinate is always $5$. It differs from the height required by the plane, while the direction vector of the line is orthogonal to the normal vector of the plane.
