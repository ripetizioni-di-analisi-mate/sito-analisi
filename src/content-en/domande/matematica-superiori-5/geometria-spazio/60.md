The adjacent sides of a parallelogram are $u=(4,0,0)$ and $v=(5,9,0)$. What is its area?

- $36$
- $18$
- $4 \sqrt{106}$
- $20$

## Explanation

With $u$ as the base, the height is the magnitude of the component of $v$ perpendicular to $u$. Multiply the base by that component. This gives $36$.
