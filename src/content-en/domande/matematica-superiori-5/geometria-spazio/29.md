What is the maximum value of $2x + y - 2z$ as the point $(x, y, z)$ varies on the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4$?

- $3$
- $12$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

The expression is the dot product $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{p}$ with $\vec{a} = (2, 1, -2)$ and $\vec{p} = (x, y, z)$. One has $\|\vec{a}\| = 3$ and, on the sphere, $\|\vec{p}\| = 2$, so, with $\theta$ the angle between the two vectors,
$$
2x + y - 2z = \|\vec{a}\|\,\|\vec{p}\|\cos\theta = 6\cos\theta \leq 6 .
$$
The value $6$ is attained for $\theta = 0$, that is for $\vec{p} = 2 \cdot \dfrac{\vec{a}}{3} = \left( \dfrac{4}{3}, \dfrac{2}{3}, -\dfrac{4}{3} \right)$, therefore the maximum is $6$. The value $3$ ignores the magnitude of $\vec{p}$, and $12$ uses the square of the radius instead of the radius.
