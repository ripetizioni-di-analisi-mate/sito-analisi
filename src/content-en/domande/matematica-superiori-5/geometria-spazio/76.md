A sphere with centre $C=(1,-1,2)$ and radius $3$ undergoes the dilation $P\mapsto 2P$ about the origin. What are the centre and radius of its image?

- Centre $(1,-1,2)$ and radius $6$.
- Centre $(2,-2,4)$ and radius $6$.
- Centre $(2,-2,4)$ and radius $3$.
- Centre $(2,-2,4)$ and radius $12$.

## Explanation

The centre is sent to $2C=(2,-2,4)$. For each point $P$ on the sphere the distance between the images is $|2P-2C|=2|P-C|=6$, so the image has radius $6$. The dilation factor applies to lengths.
