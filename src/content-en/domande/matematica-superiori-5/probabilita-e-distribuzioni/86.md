A density equals $f(x)=kx$ for $0\leq x\leq 3$ and zero elsewhere. What must $k$ be?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The integral of the density over the entire real line must equal one. The integral of $kx$ over the interval is $\dfrac{k3^2}{2}$. This gives $\dfrac{2}{9}$.
