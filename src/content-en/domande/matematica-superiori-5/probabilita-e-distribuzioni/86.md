A Poisson counter with mean $2$ per interval displays “empty” for a zero count and “occupied” otherwise. What is the probability of an “occupied” display in two consecutive independent intervals?

- $(1-e^{-2})^2$
- $1-e^{-4}$
- $e^{-4}$
- $2(1-e^{-2})$

## Explanation

The probability of zero events in one interval is $e^{-2}$. Both intervals must be occupied, so independence gives the product of the two complementary probabilities.
