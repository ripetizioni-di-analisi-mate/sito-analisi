The number of cars arriving at a petrol station in ten minutes has a Poisson distribution with mean $2$, and the numbers of arrivals in disjoint time intervals are independent. What is the probability that at least one car arrives in each of two consecutive ten-minute intervals?

- $(1-e^{-2})^2$
- $1-e^{-4}$
- $e^{-4}$
- $2(1-e^{-2})$

## Explanation

In a ten-minute interval the probability that no car arrives is $P(X=0)=e^{-2}$. Hence the probability that at least one arrives is $1-e^{-2}$. The two intervals are independent, so the probability that at least one car arrives in both is the product $(1-e^{-2})^2\approx0.748$.
