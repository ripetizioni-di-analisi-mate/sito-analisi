An urn contains $4$ red balls and $6$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $4$ red balls and $10$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{4}{10}$.
