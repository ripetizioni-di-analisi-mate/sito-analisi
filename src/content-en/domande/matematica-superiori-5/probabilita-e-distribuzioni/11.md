The number of misprints per page has a Poisson distribution with mean $0.5$. Which expression gives the probability of at least two misprints?

- $1-\dfrac32e^{-1/2}$
- $1-e^{-1/2}$
- $\dfrac32e^{-1/2}$
- $\dfrac18e^{-1/2}$

## Explanation

Subtract the probabilities of zero and one misprint from one. They equal $e^{-1/2}$ and $\tfrac12 e^{-1/2}$, respectively. The probability of exactly two would count only one outcome in the tail.
