There are $3$ independent trials with success probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$. What is the probability of exactly one success?

- $\dfrac{48}{125}$
- $\dfrac{1}{125}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{16}{125}$

## Explanation

Choose which of the $3$ trials is the success. For each possible position, multiply the probability of success by the probabilities of the other outcomes. This gives $\dfrac{48}{125}$.
