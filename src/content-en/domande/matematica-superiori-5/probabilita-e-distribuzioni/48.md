Which Poisson distribution approximates a binomial distribution with $200$ independent trials and success probability $0.015$?

- $\operatorname{Pois}(0.015)$
- $\operatorname{Pois}(200)$
- $\operatorname{Pois}(3)$
- $\operatorname{Pois}(197)$

## Explanation

The Poisson approximation applies to a binomial count with many trials and a small success probability. Choose the Poisson parameter to equal the binomial mean so the two models have the same expected number of successes. Here $np=200\cdot0.015=3$, giving the approximation $\operatorname{Pois}(3)$. The parameter $0.015$ concerns one trial.
