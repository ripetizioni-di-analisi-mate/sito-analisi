Of 20 components, 4 are defective. One is drawn, found to be free of defects, and not returned to the batch. What is the probability that the next component is defective?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{19}$
- $\dfrac{3}{19}$

## Explanation

The first draw reduces the batch to nineteen components. Since the component removed was free of defects, four defective ones remain. This gives $\dfrac{4}{19}$.
