A normally distributed variable has mean $40$ and standard deviation $5$. What standardized value corresponds to $x=31$?

- $- \dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}$
- $-9$
- $- \dfrac{9}{5}$

## Explanation

Subtract the mean from the observed value and divide by the standard deviation. The result is negative because the value is below the mean. This gives $- \dfrac{9}{5}$.
