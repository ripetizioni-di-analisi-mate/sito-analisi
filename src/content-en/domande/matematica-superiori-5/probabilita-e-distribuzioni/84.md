A box contains four tickets worth $0,0,2,6$ euros. One is chosen at random. A player may read its value and then decide whether to pay one euro to claim it. What is the expected net gain under the best choice?

- $1$ euro
- $1.50$ euros
- $2$ euros
- $3$ euros

## Explanation

The player declines both zero-value tickets and pays nothing. The four equally likely gains are therefore $0,0,1,5$ euros, with mean $6/4=1.50$ euros.
