A homogeneous Poisson process records exactly one arrival in a ten-minute interval. Given that this is the only arrival, what is the probability that it occurred in the first three minutes?

- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac7{10}$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

With rate $\lambda$ per minute, the probability of exactly one arrival in the first three minutes and none in the next seven is $(3\lambda e^{-3\lambda})e^{-7\lambda}$. The probability of exactly one arrival in ten minutes is $10\lambda e^{-10\lambda}$. Their ratio is $3/10$.
