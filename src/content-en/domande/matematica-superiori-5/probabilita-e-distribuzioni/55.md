Among $50$ students, $20$ study English, $25$ study French, and $3$ study both. What is the probability that a randomly selected student studies at least one of the two languages?

- $\dfrac{3}{50}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{39}{50}$
- $\dfrac{21}{25}$

## Explanation

Adding the sizes of the groups counts students who study both languages twice. Subtract the size of the intersection once and divide by the total. This gives $\dfrac{21}{25}$.
