Both students in group A pass a test. In group B, four out of eight pass. The first sampling method chooses a group with probability $1/2$ each and then chooses a student uniformly within it. The second selects a student uniformly from all ten. What are the respective probabilities of selecting a student who passed?

- $\dfrac35,\ \dfrac34$
- $\dfrac35,\ \dfrac35$
- $\dfrac34,\ \dfrac34$
- $\dfrac34,\ \dfrac35$

## Explanation

With equally likely groups, average their pass rates to get $(1+1/2)/2=3/4$. With equally likely students, directly count the six who passed out of ten, giving $3/5$. The first design gives each student in the smaller group greater weight.
