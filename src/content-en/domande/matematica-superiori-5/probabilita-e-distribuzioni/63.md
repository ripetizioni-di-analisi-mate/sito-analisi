There are $5$ independent trials with success probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. What is the probability of at least one success?

- $\dfrac{1}{1024}$
- $\dfrac{243}{1024}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{781}{1024}$

## Explanation

The complement is the event that all trials fail. By independence, raise the probability of failure to the number of trials and subtract the result from one. This gives $\dfrac{781}{1024}$.
