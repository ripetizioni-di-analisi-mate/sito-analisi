Let $X$ be normally distributed with mean $60$ and variance $25$. Find the quantile of order $0.05$ using $z_{0.95}\approx 1.645$.

- Approximately $51.775$
- Approximately $58.355$
- Approximately $68.225$
- Approximately $18.875$

## Explanation

A quantile of order $0.05$ leaves five percent of the probability on the left. By symmetry of the standard normal, its threshold is the negative of the quantile of order $0.95$. The original variable has standard deviation $\sqrt{25}=5$. To return to the scale of $X$, multiply the standard threshold by $5$ and add the mean.

$$
q_{0.05}\approx 60+5(-1.645)=51.775.
$$

The negative sign places the result in the lower tail, below the mean.
