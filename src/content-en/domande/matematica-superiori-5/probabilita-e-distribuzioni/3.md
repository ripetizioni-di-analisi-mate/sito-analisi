Five people's birth months are independent and uniform over twelve months. Which expression gives the probability that at least two share a month?

- $1-\left(\dfrac{11}{12}\right)^4$
- $\dfrac{12\cdot11\cdot10\cdot9\cdot8}{12^5}$
- $1-\dfrac{12\cdot11\cdot10\cdot9\cdot8}{12^5}$
- $\dfrac{\binom52}{12}$

## Explanation

To avoid every match, each additional person must have a previously unused month. There are twelve, eleven, ten, nine and eight permitted choices in sequence. Subtract the resulting probability from one.
