A warehouse has $50$ parts. Department A produced $20$ of them, of which $3$ are defective. There are $7$ defective parts in all. Given that a randomly chosen part is defective, what is the probability that it came from A?

- $\dfrac{3}{50}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Among the defective parts, only $3$ came from A. The denominator is the total number of defective parts because the part is known to be defective. This gives $\dfrac{3}{7}$.
