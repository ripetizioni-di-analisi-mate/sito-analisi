There are $4$ independent trials with success probability $\dfrac{1}{7}$. What is the probability of exactly one success?

- $\dfrac{1}{2401}$
- $\dfrac{216}{2401}$
- $\dfrac{864}{2401}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

Choose which of the $4$ trials is the success. For each possible position, multiply the probability of success by the probabilities of the other outcomes. This gives $\dfrac{864}{2401}$.
