A random variable is uniformly distributed on the interval $[0,4]$. What is the probability that it lies between $0$ and $2$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution, probability is proportional to interval length. The specified interval has length $2$ and the full interval has length $4$. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
