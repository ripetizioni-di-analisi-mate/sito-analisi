A deck of $40$ cards has four suits, with ten cards and three face cards per suit. How many distinct cards are face cards or belong to the suit of cups?

- $9$
- $22$
- $15$
- $19$

## Explanation

There are twelve face cards and ten cups. The three face cards of cups were counted twice, so subtract them once. The count is $12+10-3=19$.
