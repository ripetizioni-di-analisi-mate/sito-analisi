How many shortest paths lead from $(0,0)$ to $(3,2)$ using only unit steps to the right or upward?

- $6$
- $5$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

Increasing the horizontal coordinate from $0$ to $3$ requires three steps to the right. Increasing the vertical coordinate from $0$ to $2$ requires two upward steps. Each path is a sequence of five steps determined by the positions of the two upward steps. Once those positions are chosen, the other three are fixed. This gives $\binom52=\dfrac{5\cdot4}{2}=10$ paths.
