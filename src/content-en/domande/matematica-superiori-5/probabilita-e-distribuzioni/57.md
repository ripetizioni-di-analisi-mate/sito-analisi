Among $90$ students, $40$ study English, $45$ study French, and $5$ study both. What is the probability that a randomly selected student studies at least one of the two languages?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{17}{18}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$

## Explanation

Adding the sizes of the groups counts students who study both languages twice. Subtract the size of the intersection once and divide by the total. This gives $\dfrac{8}{9}$.
