Let $X\sim U(-2,2)$ and $Y=|X|$. What is $P(Y\le \dfrac12)$?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Condition $|X|\le\dfrac12$ includes all values at distance at most $\dfrac12$ from zero, on both its negative and positive sides. The interval from $-\dfrac12$ to $\dfrac12$ lies entirely in the support and has length $1$. The uniform density is constant on a support of length $4$, so

$$
P\!\left(Y\le\frac12\right)=\frac{1}{2-(-2)}=\frac14.
$$
