A random variable is uniformly distributed on $[0,7]$. What is the probability that it exceeds $3$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

The favorable interval extends from $3$ to $7$. For the uniform distribution, divide its length by the total length. This gives $\dfrac{4}{7}$.
