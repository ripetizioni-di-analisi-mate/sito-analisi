A normally distributed variable has mean $30$ and standard deviation $4$. What standardized value corresponds to $x=23$?

- $- \dfrac{7}{16}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{4}$
- $-7$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$

## Explanation

Subtract the mean from the observed value and divide by the standard deviation. The result is negative because the value is below the mean. This gives $- \dfrac{7}{4}$.
