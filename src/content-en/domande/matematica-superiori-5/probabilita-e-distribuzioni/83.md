A laboratory records lengths rounded to the nearest centimetre. If the true length $X$ has a continuous density, which probability is the theoretical frequency of readings of “10 cm”?

- $P(9.5\le X<10.5)$
- $P(X=10)$
- $P(10\le X<11)$
- $P(X\le10)$

## Explanation

Rounding groups a full centimetre-wide interval of true lengths into one recorded value. For a variable with a density the boundary values have probability zero, so the rule used for ties does not affect the result.
