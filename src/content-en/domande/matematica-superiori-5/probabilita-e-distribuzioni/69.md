A random variable equals $4$ with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $9$ otherwise. What is its mean?

- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $\dfrac{13}{5}$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

Add the two values weighted by their respective probabilities. The second weight is $\dfrac{4}{5}$. This gives $8$.
