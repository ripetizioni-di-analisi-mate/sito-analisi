A uniform distribution on $(2,6)$ is replaced by a uniform distribution on $(12,16)$. Which characteristic stays the same?

- The mean.
- The median.
- The quantile of order $0.25$.
- The variance.

## Explanation

Adding $10$ to each endpoint gives the new interval, so the second variable can be written as $Y=X+10$. Its mean rises from $4$ to $14$ and every observation also increases by $10$. Each value therefore has the same deviation from its mean as before. The variance depends on the squared deviations and retains its value $\dfrac{4^2}{12}=\dfrac43$. The two supports also have equal length.
