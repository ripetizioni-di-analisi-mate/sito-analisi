The number of arrivals in an hour has a Poisson distribution with parameter $3$. What is the probability of exactly two arrivals?

- $\dfrac{9}{e^{3}}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2 e^{3}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 e^{3}}$
- $e^{-3}$

## Explanation

In the Poisson formula, multiply $e^{-3}$ by $3^2$ and divide by $2!$. This gives $\dfrac{9}{2 e^{3}}$.
