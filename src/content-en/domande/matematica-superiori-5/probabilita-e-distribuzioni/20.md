In a model with $500$ parts, $30$ are defective. An inspection flags $24$ defective parts and $14$ nondefective parts. Calculate the negative predictive value of the inspection.

- $\dfrac{1}{77}$
- $\dfrac{228}{235}$
- $\dfrac{76}{77}$
- $\dfrac{114}{125}$

## Explanation

Negative predictive value is the proportion of nondefective parts **among unflagged parts**. Of the $470$ nondefective parts, $470-14=456$ are unflagged. Another $30-24=6$ defective parts are also unflagged. The denominator includes both groups, so the answer is $\dfrac{456}{456+6}=\dfrac{76}{77}$. Dividing $456$ by $470$ would give specificity, whose reference group is all nondefective parts.
