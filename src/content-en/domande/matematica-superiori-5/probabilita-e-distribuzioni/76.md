A random variable is uniformly distributed on $[0,5]$. What is the probability that it exceeds $2$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The favorable interval extends from $2$ to $5$. For the uniform distribution, divide its length by the total length. This gives $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
