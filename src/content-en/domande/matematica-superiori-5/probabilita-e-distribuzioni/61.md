There are $3$ independent trials with success probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is the probability of at least one success?

- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The complement is the event that all trials fail. By independence, raise the probability of failure to the number of trials and subtract the result from one. This gives $\dfrac{7}{8}$.
