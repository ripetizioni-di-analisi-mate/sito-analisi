A microphone detects a signal on each attempt with probability $1/2$, independently across attempts. What is the smallest number of attempts for which the chance of at least one detection exceeds $90\%$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

Use the complementary event. After $n$ attempts the probability of no detection is $2^{-n}$. For $n=3$ this is $1/8>0.10$, whereas for $n=4$ it is $1/16<0.10$.
