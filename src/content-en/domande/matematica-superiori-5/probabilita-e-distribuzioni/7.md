In a model $2\%$ of the population has a certain condition. A test is positive for $95\%$ of people with the condition and $10\%$ of the others. Among $10\,000$ people, what fraction of the positive results is expected to come from people with the condition?

- $\dfrac{980}{1170}$
- $\dfrac{190}{200}$
- $\dfrac{190}{10000}$
- $\dfrac{190}{1170}$

## Explanation

We expect $200$ people with the condition and $190$ positive results among them. Among the remaining $9800$ people a further $980$ positives are expected. The denominator is the sum of both positive groups.
