An urn contains $6$ red balls and $8$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $6$ red balls and $14$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{6}{14}$.
