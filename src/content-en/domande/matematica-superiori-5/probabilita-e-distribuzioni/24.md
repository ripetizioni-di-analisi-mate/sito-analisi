A random variable is uniformly distributed on the interval $[0,12]$. What is the probability that it lies between $0$ and $6$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution, probability is proportional to interval length. The specified interval has length $6$ and the full interval has length $12$. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{6}{12}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
