A multiple-choice item has four answers, one correct. A correct guess earns $3$ points and an incorrect guess earns $-1$. Leaving it blank earns zero. After eliminating one definitely incorrect answer, what is the expected score for guessing among the three remaining answers?

- $0$ points
- $\dfrac13$ point
- $1$ point
- $\dfrac23$ point

## Explanation

The probability of a correct guess is now $1/3$, and the probability of an error is $2/3$. The weighted mean is $3\cdot(1/3)-1\cdot(2/3)=1/3$.
