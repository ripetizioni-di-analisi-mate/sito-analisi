Height in centimetres follows a normal distribution with mean $175$ and standard deviation $6$. Given $\Phi(1.5)=0.9332$, what percentage exceeds $184$ cm?

- $93.32\%$
- $6.68\%$
- $86.64\%$
- $3.34\%$

## Explanation

The standardized height is $(184-175)/6=1.5$. The table gives the probability on the left, so the right tail is $1-0.9332=0.0668$.
