The waiting time $T$ has an exponential distribution with rate $3$ per minute. What is $P(T>4)$, with $T$ measured in minutes?

- $1 - e^{-12}$
- $e^{- \dfrac{4}{3}}$
- $e^{-12}$
- $e^{- \dfrac{3}{4}}$

## Explanation

The probability of exceeding time $t$ is $e^{-\lambda t}$. Substitute the rate and the threshold in minutes. This gives $e^{-12}$.
