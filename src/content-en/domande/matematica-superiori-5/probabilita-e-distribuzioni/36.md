An urn contains $9$ red balls and $11$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{9}{11}$
- $\dfrac{11}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $9$ red balls and $20$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{9}{20}$.
