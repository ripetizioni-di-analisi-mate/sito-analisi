A warehouse has $70$ parts. Department A produced $30$ of them, of which $4$ are defective. There are $9$ defective parts in all. Given that a randomly chosen part is defective, what is the probability that it came from A?

- $\dfrac{2}{35}$
- $\dfrac{2}{15}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

Among the defective parts, only $4$ came from A. The denominator is the total number of defective parts because the part is known to be defective. This gives $\dfrac{4}{9}$.
