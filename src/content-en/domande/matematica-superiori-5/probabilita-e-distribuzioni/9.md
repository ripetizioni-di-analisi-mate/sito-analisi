In a game you gain $v$ euros for drawing an ace from a forty-card deck with four aces, and lose three euros otherwise. What net prize makes the game fair?

- $12$ euros
- $30$ euros
- $3$ euros
- $27$ euros

## Explanation

The expected gain must be zero. The prize is received with probability $1/10$ and the loss incurred with probability $9/10$, so $v/10-27/10=0$.
