A random variable is uniformly distributed on the interval $[0,6]$. What is the probability that it lies between $0$ and $3$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution, probability is proportional to interval length. The specified interval has length $3$ and the full interval has length $6$. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{3}{6}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
