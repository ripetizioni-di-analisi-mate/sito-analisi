The number of arrivals in an hour has a Poisson distribution with parameter $4$. What is the probability of exactly two arrivals?

- $e^{-4}$
- $\dfrac{8}{e^{4}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 e^{4}}$
- $\dfrac{16}{e^{4}}$

## Explanation

In the Poisson formula, multiply $e^{-4}$ by $4^2$ and divide by $2!$. This gives $\dfrac{8}{e^{4}}$.
