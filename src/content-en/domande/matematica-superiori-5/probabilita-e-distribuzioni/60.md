A warehouse has $90$ parts. Department A produced $40$ of them, of which $5$ are defective. There are $11$ defective parts in all. Given that a randomly chosen part is defective, what is the probability that it came from A?

- $\dfrac{5}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

Among the defective parts, only $5$ came from A. The denominator is the total number of defective parts because the part is known to be defective. This gives $\dfrac{5}{11}$.
