A train's arrival time, in hours, is uniformly distributed between $9$ and $15$. Calculate the probability that it arrives after $13$ and before $16$.

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac27$

## Explanation

A uniform density assigns equal probabilities to intervals of the same duration within its support. The latest possible arrival is $15$, so only the two hours from $13$ to $15$ contribute within the window from $13$ to $16$.

$$
P(13<X<16)=\frac{15-13}{15-9}=\frac26=\frac13.
$$

The denominator is the six hours allowed by the model. The density is zero between $15$ and $16$.
