Among $70$ students, $30$ study English, $35$ study French, and $4$ study both. What is the probability that a randomly selected student studies at least one of the two languages?

- $\dfrac{13}{14}$
- $\dfrac{2}{35}$
- $\dfrac{57}{70}$
- $\dfrac{61}{70}$

## Explanation

Adding the sizes of the groups counts students who study both languages twice. Subtract the size of the intersection once and divide by the total. This gives $\dfrac{61}{70}$.
