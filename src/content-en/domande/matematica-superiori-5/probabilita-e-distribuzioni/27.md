A binomial random variable has $n=7$ and success probability $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its expected value?

- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}$

## Explanation

The expected value of a binomial random variable is $E[X]=np$. Multiply $7$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $E[X]=\dfrac{7}{2}$.
