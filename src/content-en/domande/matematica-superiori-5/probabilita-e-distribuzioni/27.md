Two components each have reliability $0.9$. They always fail together or work together. What is the reliability of their parallel configuration?

- $\dfrac{99}{100}$
- $\dfrac{81}{100}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

A parallel system needs only one working component. Here the two always have the same state, so only “both working” and “both failed” are possible. The system has the same failure probability as one component, $0.1$. Its reliability is therefore $1-0.1=0.9$. The value $0.99$ would require the joint failure probability to equal $0.1^2$, as it would under independence.
