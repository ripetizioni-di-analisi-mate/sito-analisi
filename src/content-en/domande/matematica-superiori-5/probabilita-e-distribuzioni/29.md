A random variable is uniformly distributed on the interval $[0,14]$. What is the probability that it lies between $0$ and $7$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution, probability is proportional to interval length. The specified interval has length $7$ and the full interval has length $14$. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{7}{14}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
