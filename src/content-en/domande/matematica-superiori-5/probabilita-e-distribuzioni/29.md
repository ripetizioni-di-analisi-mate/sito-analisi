All applicants come from A or B, with admission rates $30\%$ and $40\%$, respectively. Which overall admission rate is inconsistent with the data?

- $39\%$
- $32\%$
- $45\%$
- $35\%$

## Explanation

Let $p$ denote the share of applicants from A. The overall admission probability is $0.30p+0.40(1-p)=0.40-0.10p$. As $p$ ranges from zero to one, the rate must lie between $0.30$ and $0.40$, so $45\%$ is incompatible. The group rates have nonnegative weights that sum to one, so their weighted average lies between the two rates.
