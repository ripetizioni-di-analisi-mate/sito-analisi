A variable takes the values $-1,0,2,5$ with probabilities $0.2$, $0.3$, $0.4$, $0.1$, respectively. Given $E[X]=1.1$, what is its variance?

- $3.20$
- $4.30$
- $1.21$
- $3.09$

## Explanation

The second moment is $E[X^2]=0.2+4\cdot0.4+25\cdot0.1=4.3$. Subtracting the square of the mean gives $4.3-1.21=3.09$.
