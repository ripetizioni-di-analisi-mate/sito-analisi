A reader accepts a code when the **first** of its three independent uniform digits from $0$ to $9$ is the **unique** largest. Given that the first digit is $4$, what is the acceptance probability?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac4{25}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac1{10}$

## Explanation

Both remaining digits must belong to $\{0,1,2,3\}$. Each has probability $\dfrac4{10}$ and the draws are independent, giving $\dfrac4{25}$.
