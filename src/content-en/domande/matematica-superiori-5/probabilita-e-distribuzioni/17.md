A binomial random variable has $n=5$ and success probability $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its expected value?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

The expected value of a binomial random variable is $E[X]=np$. Multiply $5$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $E[X]=\dfrac{5}{2}$.
