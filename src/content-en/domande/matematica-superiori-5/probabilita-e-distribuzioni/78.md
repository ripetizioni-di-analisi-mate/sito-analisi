Let $X$ be a variable with a density and suppose $P(X\le4)=0.7$. What is $P(X<4)$?

- $0.3$
- $0$
- $1$
- $0.7$

## Explanation

Event $X\le4$ is the disjoint union of $X<4$ and $X=4$. Since $X$ has a density, its integral over a single point is zero, so $P(X=4)=0$. Subtracting this contribution gives

$$
P(X<4)=P(X\le4)-P(X=4)=0.7-0=0.7.
$$

The choice of endpoint would matter if there were a point mass at $4$.
