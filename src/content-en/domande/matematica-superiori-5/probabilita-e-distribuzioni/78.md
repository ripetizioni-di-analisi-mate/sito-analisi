A random variable is uniformly distributed on $[0,9]$. What is the probability that it exceeds $4$?

- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The favorable interval extends from $4$ to $9$. For the uniform distribution, divide its length by the total length. This gives $\dfrac{5}{9}$.
