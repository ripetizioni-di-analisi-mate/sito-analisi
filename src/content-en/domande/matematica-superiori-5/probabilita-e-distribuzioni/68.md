Let $X\sim U(1,9)$. What is $P(X<5\mid X>3)$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Knowing $X>3$ narrows the range of possible values from $(1,9)$ to $(3,9)$. Within that range the favorable condition $X<5$ leaves the segment $(3,5)$. The constant density cancels in the conditional probability, so the probability is the ratio of the lengths of the two segments.

$$
P(X<5\mid X>3)=\frac{5-3}{9-3}=\frac26=\frac13.
$$

The denominator is the length remaining after the condition is imposed.
