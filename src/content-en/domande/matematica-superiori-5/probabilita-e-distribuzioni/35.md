A fair coin gives $X=1$ for heads and $X=-1$ for tails. Compare two rules. In the first case $Y=X$, and in the second $Y=-X$. The distribution of $Y$ is the same in both cases. What are the two values of $E[XY]$, in that order?

- $0,\ 0$
- $1,\ -1$
- $1,\ 1$
- $-1,\ 1$

## Explanation

Under the first rule the product is always $X^2=1$. Under the second it is always $-X^2=-1$. Knowing the distribution of each individual variable can therefore leave the mean of their product undetermined.
