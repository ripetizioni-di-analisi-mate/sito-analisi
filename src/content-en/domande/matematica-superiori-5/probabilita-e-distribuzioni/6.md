An urn contains $3$ red balls and $5$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $3$ red balls and $8$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{3}{8}$.
