Two cards are drawn without replacement from a deck of $40$ cards containing four aces. Which pair distinguishes the conditional chance of a second ace given a first ace from the chance of drawing two aces?

- $\dfrac3{40},\ \dfrac3{400}$
- $\dfrac1{10},\ \dfrac1{100}$
- $\dfrac1{130},\ \dfrac1{13}$
- $\dfrac1{13},\ \dfrac1{130}$

## Explanation

After observing the first ace, three aces remain among thirty-nine cards. For the joint probability multiply $3/39$ by the initial probability $4/40$.
