An urn contains $8$ red balls and $10$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $8$ red balls and $18$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{8}{18}$.
