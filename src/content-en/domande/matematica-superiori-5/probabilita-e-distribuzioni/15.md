Eight students are divided into two unlabeled groups of four, using every student. How many divisions are possible?

- $140$
- $16$
- $70$
- $35$

## Explanation

Choose a set of four students in $\binom84=70$ ways. The other four then form the complementary group. Because the groups are unlabeled, choosing either one first gives the same division. Each division therefore appears exactly twice among the seventy choices, giving $\dfrac{70}{2}=35$.
