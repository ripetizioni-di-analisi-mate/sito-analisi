A density equals $k(2x-x^2)$ on $[0,2]$ and zero elsewhere, with $k$ chosen to give total area one. What is its mean, by symmetry?

- $\dfrac34$
- $0$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

The polynomial $2x-x^2=1-(x-1)^2$ is symmetric about $x=1$. Contributions from deviations to the right and left of the centre cancel in computing the mean.
