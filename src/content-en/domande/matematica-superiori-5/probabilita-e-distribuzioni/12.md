A binomial random variable has $n=4$ and success probability $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its expected value?

- $4$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The expected value of a binomial random variable is $E[X]=np$. Multiply $4$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $E[X]=\dfrac{4}{2}$.
