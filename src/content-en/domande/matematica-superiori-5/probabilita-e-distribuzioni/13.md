An exponential waiting time has a mean of five minutes. Which rate and probability should a spreadsheet use for a wait longer than ten minutes?

- Rate $1/5$ per minute and probability $1-e^{-2}$.
- Rate $5$ per minute and probability $e^{-50}$.
- Rate $1/5$ per minute and probability $e^{-2}$.
- Rate $1/10$ per minute and probability $e^{-1/2}$.

## Explanation

The rate is the reciprocal of the mean time. In the survival probability $P(T>t)=e^{-\lambda t}$ the product of rate and time is $(1/5)\cdot10=2$.
