The waiting time $X$ at a counter, in minutes, has an exponential distribution with mean $5$ minutes. What are the parameter $\lambda$ and the probability that the wait exceeds $10$ minutes?

- $\lambda=\dfrac{1}{5}$ per minute and probability $1-e^{-2}$.
- $\lambda=5$ per minute and probability $e^{-50}$.
- $\lambda=\dfrac{1}{5}$ per minute and probability $e^{-2}$.
- $\lambda=\dfrac{1}{10}$ per minute and probability $e^{-\frac{1}{2}}$.

## Explanation

For the exponential distribution the mean is $\dfrac{1}{\lambda}$, so $\dfrac{1}{\lambda}=5$ gives $\lambda=\dfrac{1}{5}$ per minute. The probability that the wait exceeds $t$ minutes is $P(X>t)=e^{-\lambda t}$. For $t=10$ the exponent is $-\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot10=-2$, so the probability is $e^{-2}$.
