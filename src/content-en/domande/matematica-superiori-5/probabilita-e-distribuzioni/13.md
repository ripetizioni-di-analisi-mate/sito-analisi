A binomial random variable has $n=4$ and $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its variance?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

The variance of a binomial random variable is $np(1-p)$. Here the factors $p$ and $1-p$ both equal $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the variance is $\dfrac{4}{4}$.
