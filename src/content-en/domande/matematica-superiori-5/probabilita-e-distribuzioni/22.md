A binomial random variable has $n=6$ and success probability $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its expected value?

- $6$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

The expected value of a binomial random variable is $E[X]=np$. Multiply $6$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $E[X]=\dfrac{6}{2}$.
