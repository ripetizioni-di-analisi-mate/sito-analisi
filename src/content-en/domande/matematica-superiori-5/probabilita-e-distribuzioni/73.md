The number of arrivals in an hour has a Poisson distribution with parameter $2$. What is the probability of exactly two arrivals?

- $\dfrac{4}{e^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{e^{2}}$
- $e^{-2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 e^{2}}$

## Explanation

In the Poisson formula, multiply $e^{-2}$ by $2^2$ and divide by $2!$. This gives $\dfrac{2}{e^{2}}$.
