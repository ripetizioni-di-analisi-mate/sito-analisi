A bus passes at 8:00, 8:15 and 8:30. A student arrives at the stop at a time uniformly distributed between 8:00 and 8:30. What is the probability that the student waits less than $5$ minutes?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the arrival minute after 8:00, with $X\sim U(0,30)$. The wait is less than $5$ minutes when the student arrives in the $5$ minutes before a bus, that is, for $10<X<15$ or for $25<X<30$. The two intervals are disjoint and each has length $5$, so the favorable length is $10$.

$$
P(\text{wait}<5)=\frac{5+5}{30}=\frac13.
$$

The value $\dfrac16$ counts only the interval before 8:15 and leaves out the one before 8:30.
