There are $5$ independent trials with success probability $\dfrac{1}{9}$. What is the probability of exactly one success?

- $\dfrac{20480}{59049}$
- $\dfrac{4096}{59049}$
- $\dfrac{1}{59049}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$

## Explanation

Choose which of the $5$ trials is the success. For each possible position, multiply the probability of success by the probabilities of the other outcomes. This gives $\dfrac{20480}{59049}$.
