An urn contains $4$ red balls and $9$ blue balls. What is the probability of drawing two red balls without replacement?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{13}$
- $\dfrac{16}{169}$
- $\dfrac{1}{13}$

## Explanation

After drawing the first red ball, $3$ red balls remain out of $12$ balls. Multiply the probability of the first red ball by the conditional probability of the second. This gives $\dfrac{1}{13}$.
