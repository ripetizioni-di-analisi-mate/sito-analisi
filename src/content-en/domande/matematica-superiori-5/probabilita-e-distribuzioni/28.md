A binomial random variable has $n=7$ and $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its variance?

- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{1}{28}$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$

## Explanation

The variance of a binomial random variable is $np(1-p)$. Here the factors $p$ and $1-p$ both equal $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the variance is $\dfrac{7}{4}$.
