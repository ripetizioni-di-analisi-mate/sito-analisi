One die is chosen at random from a fair die and a die with a six on every face. The first roll is a six. What is the probability that a second roll of the same die is also a six?

- $\dfrac{6}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{37}{42}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

Given the first six, Bayes' rule gives probability $1/7$ for the fair die and $6/7$ for the loaded one. The chance of another six is therefore $(1/7)(1/6)+(6/7)\cdot1=37/42$.
