The waiting time $T$ has an exponential distribution with rate $4$ per minute. What is $P(T>5)$, with $T$ measured in minutes?

- $e^{- \dfrac{5}{4}}$
- $e^{-20}$
- $1 - e^{-20}$
- $e^{- \dfrac{4}{5}}$

## Explanation

The probability of exceeding time $t$ is $e^{-\lambda t}$. Substitute the rate and the threshold in minutes. This gives $e^{-20}$.
