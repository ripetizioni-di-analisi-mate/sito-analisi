A binomial random variable has $n=10$ and success probability $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its expected value?

- $10$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$

## Explanation

The expected value of a binomial random variable is $E[X]=np$. Multiply $10$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $E[X]=\dfrac{10}{2}$.
