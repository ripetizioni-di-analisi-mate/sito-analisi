A waiting time $T$ has an exponential distribution with mean $4\,\mathrm{min}$. Calculate $P(4\,\mathrm{min}<T<12\,\mathrm{min})$.

- $1-e^{-2}$
- $e^{-2}$
- $e^{-1}-e^{-3}$
- $1-e^{-3}$

## Explanation

For an exponential waiting time, the rate is the reciprocal of the mean. With $T$ measured in minutes, the tail probability at threshold $t$ is therefore $e^{-\frac{t}{4}}$. The tail beyond four minutes includes waits longer than twelve minutes. Subtract the latter to isolate the required interval.

$$
P(4<T<12)=e^{-\frac44}-e^{-\frac{12}{4}}=e^{-1}-e^{-3}.
$$

Including the endpoints leaves the area unchanged because each endpoint has probability zero.
