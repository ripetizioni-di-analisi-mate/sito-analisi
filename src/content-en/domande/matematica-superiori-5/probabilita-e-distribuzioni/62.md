There are $4$ independent trials with success probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. What is the probability of at least one success?

- $\dfrac{65}{81}$
- $\dfrac{16}{81}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{81}$

## Explanation

The complement is the event that all trials fail. By independence, raise the probability of failure to the number of trials and subtract the result from one. This gives $\dfrac{65}{81}$.
