A binomial random variable has $n=10$ and $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its variance?

- $5$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{40}$

## Explanation

The variance of a binomial random variable is $np(1-p)$. Here the factors $p$ and $1-p$ both equal $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the variance is $\dfrac{10}{4}$.
