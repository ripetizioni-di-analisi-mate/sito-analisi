Let $X$ be uniform on $[-1,1]$. A chosen point earns a prize if $X^2>X$. What is the probability of winning?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

The product $X(X-1)$ is positive for $X<0$ or $X>1$. Within the support this leaves the portion from $-1$ to $0$, of length one out of a total length of two.
