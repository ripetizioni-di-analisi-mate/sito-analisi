A density equals $f(x)=kx$ for $0\leq x\leq 4$ and zero elsewhere. What must $k$ be?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The integral of the density over the entire real line must equal one. The integral of $kx$ over the interval is $\dfrac{k4^2}{2}$. This gives $\dfrac{1}{8}$.
