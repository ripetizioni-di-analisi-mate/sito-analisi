An urn contains $5$ red balls and $7$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $5$ red balls and $12$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{5}{12}$.
