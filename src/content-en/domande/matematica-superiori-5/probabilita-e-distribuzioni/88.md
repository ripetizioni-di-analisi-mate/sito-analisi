Scores on a test have a normal distribution with mean $70$. The only reported datum is that 16% of scores are below 60. What percentage lies between $60$ and $80$?

- $16\%$
- $84\%$
- $32\%$
- $68\%$

## Explanation

The cutoffs are equally far from the mean. Normal symmetry puts another $16\%$ above $80$, leaving $100\%-2\cdot16\%=68\%$ in the middle.
