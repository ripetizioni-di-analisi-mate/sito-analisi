A binomial random variable counts the successes in $36$ independent trials, each with success probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. What is its variance?

- $36$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{27}{4}$

## Explanation

The variance of a binomial random variable is $np(1-p)$. Here $np=9$, so multiply by the probability of failure. This gives $\dfrac{27}{4}$.
