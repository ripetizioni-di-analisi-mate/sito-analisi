Two fair dice are rolled independently. Let $X$ denote the larger of the two results. What is $p_X(2)$?

- $\dfrac1{12}$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac1{36}$
- $\dfrac19$

## Explanation

The maximum is $2$ when both dice show at most $2$ and at least one shows $2$. The qualifying ordered pairs are $(1,2)$, $(2,1)$ and $(2,2)$. Since the dice are fair and independent, all $6\cdot6=36$ pairs are equally likely. This gives $p_X(2)=\dfrac3{36}=\dfrac1{12}$. The pair $(1,1)$ would have maximum $1$.
