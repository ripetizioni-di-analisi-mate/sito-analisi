A binomial random variable has $n=8$ and $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its variance?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{32}$

## Explanation

The variance of a binomial random variable is $np(1-p)$. Here the factors $p$ and $1-p$ both equal $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the variance is $\dfrac{8}{4}$.
