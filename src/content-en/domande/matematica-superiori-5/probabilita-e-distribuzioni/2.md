Three distinct balls are drawn together at random from those numbered $1$ to $10$. How do we calculate the probability that all are even?

- $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3$
- $\dfrac{\binom53}{\binom{10}3}$
- $\dfrac{\binom52}{\binom{10}3}$
- $\dfrac{5^3}{\binom{10}3}$

## Explanation

Every set of three balls has equal probability. Five balls are even, so there are $\binom53=10$ favourable sets among $\binom{10}3=120$ possibilities. The probability is $1/12$.
