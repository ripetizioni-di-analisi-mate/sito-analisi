A binomial random variable has $n=2$ and success probability $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its expected value?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The expected value of a binomial random variable is $E[X]=np$. Multiply $2$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $E[X]=\dfrac{2}{2}$.
