An urn contains $2$ red balls and $5$ blue balls. What is the probability of drawing two red balls without replacement?

- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{21}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{4}{49}$

## Explanation

After drawing the first red ball, $1$ red balls remain out of $6$ balls. Multiply the probability of the first red ball by the conditional probability of the second. This gives $\dfrac{1}{21}$.
