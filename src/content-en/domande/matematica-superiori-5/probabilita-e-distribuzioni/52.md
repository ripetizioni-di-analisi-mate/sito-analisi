Four different textbooks $A,B,C,D$ are placed on a shelf in random order. What is the probability that $A$ and $B$ are next to each other?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Treating $A$ and $B$ as a single block leaves three objects, which can be arranged in $3!$ ways. The block can be $AB$ or $BA$, giving $2\cdot3!$ favourable orders among the $4!$ equally likely ones.
