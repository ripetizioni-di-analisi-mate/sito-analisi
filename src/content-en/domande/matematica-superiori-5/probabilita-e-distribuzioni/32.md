A binomial random variable has $n=8$ and success probability $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its expected value?

- $8$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The expected value of a binomial random variable is $E[X]=np$. Multiply $8$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $E[X]=\dfrac{8}{2}$.
