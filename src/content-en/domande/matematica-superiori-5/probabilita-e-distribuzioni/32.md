A random variable $X$ takes the values $0$, $2$ and $5$ with masses $c$, $2c$ and $5c$. What is the value of $c$?

- $\dfrac17$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The three listed values exhaust the possibilities for $X$, so their masses must sum to $1$. The coefficients of $c$ sum to $1+2+5=8$, giving $8c=1$ and $c=\dfrac18$. The resulting individual masses are $\dfrac18$, $\dfrac28$ and $\dfrac58$. All are nonnegative, and their sum satisfies the normalization condition.
