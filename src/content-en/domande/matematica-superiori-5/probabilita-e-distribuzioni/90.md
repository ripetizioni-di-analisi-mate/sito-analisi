Roll two independent fair dice. What is the probability that the first shows $4$ or the second shows $5$, including when both occur?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$
- $\dfrac{11}{36}$

## Explanation

Each event has probability one sixth. The pair in which both occur has probability one thirty-sixth and must be subtracted once. This gives $\dfrac{11}{36}$.
