A system chooses an unordered pair uniformly from three women and two men. Given only that the pair includes at least one woman, what is the probability that both are women?

- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

Only the pair of men is excluded from the ten original pairs. There are three pairs of women among the nine equally likely remaining pairs, giving conditional probability $3/9=1/3$.
