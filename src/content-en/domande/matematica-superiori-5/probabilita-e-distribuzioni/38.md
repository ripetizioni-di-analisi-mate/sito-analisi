A binomial random variable has $n=9$ and $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its variance?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$

## Explanation

The variance of a binomial random variable is $np(1-p)$. Here the factors $p$ and $1-p$ both equal $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the variance is $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
