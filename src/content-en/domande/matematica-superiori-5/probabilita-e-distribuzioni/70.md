A binomial random variable counts the successes in $10$ independent trials, each with success probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its variance?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

The variance of a binomial random variable is $np(1-p)$. Here $np=5$, so multiply by the probability of failure. This gives $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
