A duration has an exponential distribution, and its probability of exceeding $2$ hours is $\dfrac14$. What is the median in hours?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $4$

## Explanation

The median is the duration whose exceedance probability is $\dfrac12$. For an exponential distribution, the tail probability at twice the time is the square of the original tail probability. Since $2$ hours is twice $1$ hour,

$$
P(T>1)=\sqrt{P(T>2)}=\sqrt{\frac14}=\frac12.
$$

Choose the positive root because probabilities are nonnegative. The median is therefore $1$ hour.
