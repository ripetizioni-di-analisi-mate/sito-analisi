An urn contains $3$ red balls and $7$ blue balls. What is the probability of drawing two red balls without replacement?

- $\dfrac{9}{100}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$

## Explanation

After drawing the first red ball, $2$ red balls remain out of $9$ balls. Multiply the probability of the first red ball by the conditional probability of the second. This gives $\dfrac{1}{15}$.
