A normally distributed variable has mean $20$ and standard deviation $3$. What standardized value corresponds to $x=15$?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{9}$
- $-5$

## Explanation

Subtract the mean from the observed value and divide by the standard deviation. The result is negative because the value is below the mean. This gives $- \dfrac{5}{3}$.
