A variable $X$ has standard deviation $4$. Set $Y=10-3X$. What is the standard deviation of $Y$?

- $12$
- $36$
- $144$
- $2$

## Explanation

If $\mu=E[X]$, the mean of $Y$ is $10-3\mu$. Subtracting this mean gives $Y-E[Y]=-3(X-\mu)$, so the squared deviation is multiplied by $9$. Standard deviation is the square root of variance, so it is multiplied by $|-3|=3$ and equals $3\cdot4=12$. The constant term cancels in the deviation.
