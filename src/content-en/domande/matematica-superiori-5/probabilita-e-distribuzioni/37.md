A binomial random variable has $n=9$ and success probability $p=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is its expected value?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{18}$

## Explanation

The expected value of a binomial random variable is $E[X]=np$. Multiply $9$ by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $E[X]=\dfrac{9}{2}$.
