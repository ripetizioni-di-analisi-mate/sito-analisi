An urn contains $7$ red balls and $9$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $\dfrac{7}{16}$
- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $7$ red balls and $16$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{7}{16}$.
