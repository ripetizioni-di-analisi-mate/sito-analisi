Two modules have independent exponential lifetimes with rates $\lambda_1,\lambda_2>0$. A device shuts down as soon as the first module fails. What is the rate of the device's exponential lifetime?

- $\dfrac{\lambda_1+\lambda_2}{2}$
- $\min(\lambda_1,\lambda_2)$
- $\lambda_1+\lambda_2$
- $\dfrac{\lambda_1\lambda_2}{\lambda_1+\lambda_2}$

## Explanation

For the device to last beyond $t$, both modules must still be working. Independence gives $e^{-\lambda_1t}e^{-\lambda_2t}=e^{-(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)t}$. Comparing this with the exponential survival function gives the new rate.
