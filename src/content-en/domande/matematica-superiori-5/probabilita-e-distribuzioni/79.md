The waiting time $T$ has an exponential distribution with rate $2$ per minute. What is $P(T>3)$, with $T$ measured in minutes?

- $e^{- \dfrac{2}{3}}$
- $e^{- \dfrac{3}{2}}$
- $e^{-6}$
- $1 - e^{-6}$

## Explanation

The probability of exceeding time $t$ is $e^{-\lambda t}$. Substitute the rate and the threshold in minutes. This gives $e^{-6}$.
