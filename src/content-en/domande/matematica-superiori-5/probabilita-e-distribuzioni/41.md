An urn contains $10$ red balls and $12$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{5}{11}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{6}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $10$ red balls and $22$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{10}{22}$.
