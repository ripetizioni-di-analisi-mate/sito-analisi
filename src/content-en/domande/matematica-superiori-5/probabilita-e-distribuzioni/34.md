A random variable is uniformly distributed on the interval $[0,16]$. What is the probability that it lies between $0$ and $8$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution, probability is proportional to interval length. The specified interval has length $8$ and the full interval has length $16$. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{8}{16}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
