A duration is exponential with rate $\lambda>0$ per hour and has already exceeded $s>0$ hours. Which expression gives, for every $t\ge0$, the probability of failure within the next $t$ hours?

- $1-e^{-\lambda t}$
- $1-e^{-\lambda(s+t)}$
- $e^{-\lambda(s+t)}$
- $e^{-\lambda t}$

## Explanation

Survival through $s$ is given. Within that condition, failure within the next $t$ hours is the complement of survival beyond $s+t$. The conditional survival probability is the ratio of the tail probabilities at the two thresholds.

$$
P(T>s+t\mid T>s)=\frac{e^{-\lambda(s+t)}}{e^{-\lambda s}}=e^{-\lambda t}.
$$

The failure probability is therefore $1-e^{-\lambda t}$. The elapsed time cancels because the lifetime is exponential.
