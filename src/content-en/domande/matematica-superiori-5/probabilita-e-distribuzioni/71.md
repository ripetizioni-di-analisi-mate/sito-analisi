A binomial random variable counts the successes in $21$ independent trials, each with success probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. What is its variance?

- $\dfrac{14}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $21$
- $7$

## Explanation

The variance of a binomial random variable is $np(1-p)$. Here $np=7$, so multiply by the probability of failure. This gives $\dfrac{14}{3}$.
