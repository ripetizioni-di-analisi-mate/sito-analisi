Two fair independent dice are rolled. Which complementary event gives the probability that their product is even?

- Both dice show an odd number.
- Both dice show an even number.
- At least one die shows an odd number.
- The sum is odd.

## Explanation

The product is odd only when both factors are odd. This has probability $(1/2)^2=1/4$, so the desired probability is $3/4$.
