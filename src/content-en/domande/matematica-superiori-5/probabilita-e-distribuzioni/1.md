An urn contains $2$ red balls and $4$ blue balls, all equally likely to be drawn. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

Classical probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to possible outcomes. There are $2$ red balls and $6$ balls in total. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{2}{6}$.
