A density equals $f(x)=kx$ for $0\leq x\leq 2$ and zero elsewhere. What must $k$ be?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The integral of the density over the entire real line must equal one. The integral of $kx$ over the interval is $\dfrac{k2^2}{2}$. This gives $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
