A continuous random variable has a uniform density on $[2,8]$. What is the probability that its value lies between $3$ and $5$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The favorable interval has length two. Since the density is constant, divide two by the total length, which is six. This gives $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
