One class has $8$ workshop participants out of $20$ students. Another has $15$ out of $30$. What is the relative frequency across the two classes combined?

- $\dfrac9{20}$
- $\dfrac{23}{50}$
- $\dfrac{23}{30}$
- $\dfrac{15}{50}$

## Explanation

The number of participants is $8+15=23$. The number of people surveyed is $20+30=50$. Dividing the two totals gives $\dfrac{23}{50}$. The two classes have different sizes, so their percentages carry different weights.
