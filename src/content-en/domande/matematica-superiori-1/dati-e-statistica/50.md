For the values $1,2,3$, the cumulative frequencies are $5,12,20$, respectively. How many observations equal $2$ exactly?

- $12$
- $7$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The twelve observations with value at most $2$ include the five with value $1$. Subtracting those with value $1$ gives $12-5=7$ observations with value exactly $2$.

The final cumulative count also includes $3$, which is irrelevant here. To isolate one value, compare its cumulative count with the immediately preceding one.
