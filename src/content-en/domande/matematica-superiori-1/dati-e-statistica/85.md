Two classes in a relative density histogram have widths $2$ and $5$ and heights $0.20$ and $0.12$. Which class contains more observations and what is its share?

- The second, $60\%$.
- The first, $60\%$.
- The first, $20\%$.
- The second, $12\%$.

## Explanation

The first rectangle has area $2\cdot0.20=0.40$. The second has area $5\cdot0.12=0.60$. The second height is smaller, but the larger width gives it a larger area. In a relative density histogram, the second area means that $60\%$ of the observations belong to that class.
