The cumulative counts at $0,2,4,8$ are $2,4,8,10$. What is the median?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The last cumulative count gives a total of $10$, so find the fifth and sixth values. At value $2$ only four positions have been reached. The block at $4$ extends through the eighth position, so it contains both the fifth and sixth. The mean of these equal values is $4$.
