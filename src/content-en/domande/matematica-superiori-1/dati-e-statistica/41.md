A table shows three categories with frequencies $3$, $6$, and $9$. What is the total number of observations?

- $18$
- $9$
- $6$
- $54$

## Explanation

The categories represent disjoint parts of the group observed. Adding all the absolute frequencies gives the total. The number of categories and the number of observations count different things.

$$
N=3+6+9=18
$$

The result is $18$.
