A list has at least two values and mean $10$. One value below $10$ is removed. What must happen to the mean of the remaining values?

- It becomes less than $10$.
- It stays exactly $10$.
- It becomes greater than $10$.
- It can rise or fall.

## Explanation

With $N$ values the initial sum is $10N$. Removing a value $x<10$ leaves $10N-x$, which exceeds $10(N-1)$. Dividing by the new number of values, $N-1$, gives a mean greater than $10$. The calculation takes the smaller denominator into account as well.
