The mean of 4 observations is $7$. Three observations are $4$, $6$, and $8$. What is the fourth?

- $7$
- $28$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

Use the mean and the number of observations to find the total sum. Then subtract the sum of the three known values. The difference is the missing value.

$$
x=4\cdot7-(4+6+8)=10
$$

The result is $10$.
