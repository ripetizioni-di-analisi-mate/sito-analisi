Three disjoint categories cover all responses. The published percentages, each rounded to the nearest whole percent, are $33\%$, $33\%$, and $33\%$. What can be concluded?

- Exactly $1\%$ of the responses must be missing.
- The percentages could be consistent with all responses.
- At least one category must have absolute frequency zero.
- The three exact relative frequencies sum to $0.99$.

## Explanation

Three exact frequencies of $\dfrac13$ add to one. Each corresponds to $33.\overline{3}\%$, which rounds to $33\%$. The rounded values therefore sum to $99\%$ even though the distribution is complete. Rounding can account for the entire difference from one hundred.
