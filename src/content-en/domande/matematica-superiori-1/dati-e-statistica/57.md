A series of measurements has mean $12$, median $10$, and range $8$. Add $4$ to every measurement. What are the three new values?

- $16,14,8$
- $16,14,12$
- $16,10,8$
- $12,14,8$

## Explanation

Adding $4$ to every measurement raises the mean by $4$. The ordering is unchanged, and the middle value or the mean of the two middle values also rises by $4$. The extremes both move by $4$, so the distance between them remains $8$. The new values are $16$, $14$, and $8$.
