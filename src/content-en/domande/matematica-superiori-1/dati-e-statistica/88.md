Five deliveries take $9,10,10,11,40$ minutes. Which pair of statistics best answers, in order, “typical duration without the exceptional delay” and “equal duration that preserves the total of all five journeys”?

- Mean $16$ and median $10$.
- Median $10$ and median $10$.
- Range $31$ and mean $16$.
- Median $10$ and mean $16$.

## Explanation

The middle value is $10$, close to the four durations between $9$ and $11$. The mean includes the $40$ minutes too, because the second question preserves the total. The five durations add to $80$ minutes, so splitting them into five equal parts gives $16$ minutes per delivery.
