The value $3$ occurs 2 times and the value $5$ occurs 4 times. What is the mean of the six observations?

- $4$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{13}{3}$

## Explanation

Weight each value by its own frequency. Divide the weighted sum by the total frequency. The mean is closer to the value that occurs more often.

$$
\bar x=\frac{2\cdot3+4\cdot5}{2+4}=\frac{13}{3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{13}{3}$.
