Which list has the same range as $2,5,5,10$ but a different median?

- $2,4,6,10$
- $2,3,4,9$
- $2,3,4,10$
- $1,5,5,9$

## Explanation

The original list has extremes $2$ and $10$, so its range is $8$, and both middle values equal $5$. The list $2,3,4,10$ keeps those extremes but has median $\dfrac{3+4}{2}=3.5$. The other candidates change the range or keep the median at $5$.
