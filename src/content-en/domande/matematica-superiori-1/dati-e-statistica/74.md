An enrolment form asks for the highest qualification obtained, to be chosen among lower secondary certificate, upper secondary diploma and degree. What type of variable is “qualification”?

- Nominal categorical variable
- Ordinal categorical variable
- Discrete quantitative variable
- Continuous quantitative variable

## Explanation

The categories lower secondary certificate, upper secondary diploma and degree are expressed in words, so the variable is categorical. The categories have a natural order, because a degree requires the diploma and the diploma requires the lower secondary certificate.

Therefore the variable is **ordinal categorical**. Thanks to the order one can count, for example, how many people have at least the diploma, whereas a mean of the qualifications has no meaning.
