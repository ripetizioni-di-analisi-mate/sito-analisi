A survey records satisfaction as low, medium or high. Which statistic uses only the order of these categories?

- The mean difference in euros
- The median category
- The ratio of high to low
- The sum of the categories

## Explanation

The responses can be ordered and the middle position identified. The labels specify no numerical distances between adjacent levels.
