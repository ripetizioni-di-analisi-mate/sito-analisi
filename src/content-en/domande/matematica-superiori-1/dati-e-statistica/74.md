A survey records the operating system of a smartphone. What type of variable is this?

- Continuous quantitative variable
- Nominal categorical variable
- Discrete quantitative variable
- Ordinal categorical variable with a fixed natural order

## Explanation

The responses are categories. They are not measurable quantities and have no single natural order. The variable is therefore nominal categorical.

The variable is nominal categorical.
