A school wants to estimate the proportion of enrolled students who use the library. Which survey design reduces the risk of hearing only from library users?

- Collect more responses at the library exit.
- Invite only students with library cards.
- Give the questionnaire only to students borrowing a book.
- Select students from the school roll and contact them outside the library as well.

## Explanation

The population includes every enrolled student, including students who never use the library. Selecting students from the school roll gives them a chance to respond. The other designs start with people already connected to the service. More answers at the same entrance would increase the sample without changing the selection rule.
