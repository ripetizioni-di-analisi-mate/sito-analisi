A bar chart compares $56$ and $66$ with a vertical axis starting at $55$. Which statement is correct?

- The second value is at least ten times the first
- The difference is 1 because the axis starts close to the values
- The two values must be equal
- The numerical difference is 10, but the truncated axis can make the gap look larger

## Explanation

The numerical labels show a gap of ten units. If the bar chart axis starts above zero, the ratio of the visible heights can differ from the ratio of the values. Read the scale to make the comparison.

The numerical difference is 10, and the truncated axis can make the gap look larger.
