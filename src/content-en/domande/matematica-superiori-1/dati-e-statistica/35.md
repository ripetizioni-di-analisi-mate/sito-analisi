A category contains $18$ people and represents $30\%$ of the sample. How many people are in the sample?

- $54$
- $24$
- $60$
- $6$

## Explanation

If $N$ is the sample size, the category count is $\dfrac{30}{100}N=18$. Therefore $10\%$ corresponds to $18:3=6$ people. The total, $100\%$, represents $60$ people.

Check by applying the percentage to the recovered total. The category’s share then gives eighteen again, the count stated in the question.
