A histogram uses $[0,5)$ and $[5,10)$, with two observations in each. Which pair of lists produces this same histogram but two different means?

- $1,2,7,8$ and $2,3,6,7$.
- $1,1,6,6$ and $4,4,9,9$.
- $1,2,3,8$ and $3,4,7,9$.
- $0,1,2,3$ and $5,6,7,8$.

## Explanation

The lists $1,1,6,6$ and $4,4,9,9$ put two values in each class. Their sums are $14$ and $26$, so with four observations their means differ. The lists $1,2,7,8$ and $2,3,6,7$ instead share the sum $18$. The histogram fixes class counts while leaving the values within each class unspecified.
