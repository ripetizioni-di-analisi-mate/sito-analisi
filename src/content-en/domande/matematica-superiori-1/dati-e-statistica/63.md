A set of measurements has a mean of $8$ cm. If each measurement is doubled, what is the new mean?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $64\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Doubling each measurement also doubles their sum. The number of observations stays the same. The new mean is therefore twice the original mean.

$$
\overline{2x}=2\bar x=2\cdot8=16
$$

The result is $16\,\mathrm{cm}$.
