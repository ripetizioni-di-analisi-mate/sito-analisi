What is the median of the data $7, 3, 5, 4, 11$?

- $4$
- $5$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

Order the data to find the median. With five observations, take the third in increasing order. The middle entry of the original list is reliable only if the list is already ordered.

$$
3<4<5<7<11,\qquad\operatorname{median}=5
$$

The result is $5$.

Count positions from the first sorted value. With five values, exactly two positions remain on each side.
