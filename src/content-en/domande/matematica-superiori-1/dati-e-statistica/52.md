Two sectors measuring $84^\circ$ and $126^\circ$ represent categories A and B in a pie chart. What is the ratio of their counts, in that order?

- $3:2$
- $7:30$
- $5:6$
- $2:3$

## Explanation

Each angle is proportional to its category’s count by the same scale factor. The factor therefore cancels in the ratio. Simplifying $\dfrac{84}{126}$ by dividing by $42$ gives $\dfrac23$. The ratio $\dfrac{84}{360}$ would describe the share of A among all observations.
