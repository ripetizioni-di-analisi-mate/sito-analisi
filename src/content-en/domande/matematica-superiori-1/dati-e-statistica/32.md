Three mutually exclusive responses have relative frequencies of $30\%$, $25\%$, and the remaining share. The last response is chosen by $18$ people. How many people responded?

- $60$
- $72$
- $80$
- $40$

## Explanation

The third response makes up $45\%$ because the first two shares add to $55\%$. If the total is $N$, then $\dfrac{45}{100}N=18$, giving $N=40$. To check, $30\%$ and $25\%$ of $40$ are $12$ and $10$, and $12+10+18=40$.
