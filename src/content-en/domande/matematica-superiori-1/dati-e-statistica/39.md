The value $6$ occurs 2 times and the value $8$ occurs 4 times. What is the mean of the six observations?

- $7$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{22}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

Weight each value by its own frequency. Divide the weighted sum by the total frequency. The mean is closer to the value that occurs more often.

$$
\bar x=\frac{2\cdot6+4\cdot8}{2+4}=\frac{22}{3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{22}{3}$.
