Eight measurements have mean $12$. One measurement recorded as $9$ was actually $15$. What is the corrected mean?

- $18$
- $12.82$
- $12.75$
- $11.25$

## Explanation

The initial sum is $8\cdot12=96$. Subtract $9$ and add $15$, increasing the sum by $6$ to $102$. There are still eight measurements. The correction to the mean is therefore $\dfrac68=0.75$, giving $12.75$.
