What is the median of the ordered data $3, 5, 8, 12$?

- $5$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $7$

## Explanation

An even number of observations has two middle positions. The median is the arithmetic mean of the two middle values. This calculation uses only those two values.

$$
\operatorname{median}=\frac{5+8}2=\frac{13}{2}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{13}{2}$.
