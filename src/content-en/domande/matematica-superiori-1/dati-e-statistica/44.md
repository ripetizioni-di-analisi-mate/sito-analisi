In a table, one category appears $12$ times with relative frequency $\dfrac38$. Another category has relative frequency $\dfrac14$. How many observations belong to the second category?

- $3$
- $12$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

The first count gives the total because $\dfrac38 N=12$. Dividing by $\dfrac38$ gives $N=32$. The second frequency represents a quarter of all observations, so its count is $32/4=8$. Taking a quarter of $12$ would use only the observations in the first category.
