In a list of $9$ measurements, $5$ are strictly less than $12$. What must be true of the median?

- $\operatorname{Med}<12$
- $\operatorname{Med}=12$
- $\operatorname{Med}>12$
- It can be any real number.

## Explanation

The median of nine measurements is the fifth value after sorting. The five values below $12$ occupy the first five positions, even if some are equal. So the fifth observation is still in that group. Its exact value is unnecessary to conclude that it is below $12$.
