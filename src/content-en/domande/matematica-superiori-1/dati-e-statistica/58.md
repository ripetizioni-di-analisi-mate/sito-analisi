Of $20$ measurements, $5$ must each be increased by $4$ units and the others stay the same. How does the mean change?

- It increases by $4$ units.
- It increases by $1$ unit.
- It increases by $20$ units.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

Only some measurements increase by $4$. The sum increases by $5\cdot4=20$ units and the denominator stays at $20$. The mean therefore rises by $20/20=1$ unit. Equivalently, the correction applies to a quarter of the data, so the increase in the mean is a quarter of $4$.
