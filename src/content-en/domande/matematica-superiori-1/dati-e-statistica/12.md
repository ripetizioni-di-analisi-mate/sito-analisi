Four values are in sorted order as $2,5,k,12$. The median is $7$. What is $k$?

- $7$
- $12$
- $5$
- $9$

## Explanation

With four values, the median is the mean of the second and third. Thus $\dfrac{5+k}{2}=7$. The sum of the two middle values must be $14$, and subtracting $5$ gives $k=9$. This respects the stated ordering because $5\le9\le12$.
