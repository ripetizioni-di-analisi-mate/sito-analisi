The value $2$ occurs three times and the value $6$ occurs $n$ times. The mean is $4$. What is $n$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The three observations equal to $2$ each contribute two units below the mean. The observations equal to $6$ each contribute two units above it. Balancing the contributions requires $3\cdot2=n\cdot2$, so $n=3$. Substitution in the weighted mean gives $\dfrac{2\cdot3+6\cdot3}{3+3}=4$.
