The value $4$ occurs 2 times and the value $6$ occurs 4 times. What is the mean of the six observations?

- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $5$
- $10$
- $2$

## Explanation

Weight each value by its own frequency. Divide the weighted sum by the total frequency. The mean is closer to the value that occurs more often.

$$
\bar x=\frac{2\cdot4+4\cdot6}{2+4}=\frac{16}{3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{16}{3}$.
