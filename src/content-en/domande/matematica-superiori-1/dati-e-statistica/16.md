What is the mode of the data $3, 4, 4, 4, 6, 6$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The mode is the value with the greatest frequency. The value $3$ plus one occurs three times, while the other values occur less often. The mode names the observed value, while its frequency counts how often it occurs.

The result is $4$.
