The four ordered observations are $2,5,7,x$, where $x\ge7$. What is the median for every allowed $x$?

- $5$
- $7$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

For four observations, the middle positions are the second and third. They contain $5$ and $7$, even if the last value changes. The median is therefore $\dfrac{5+7}{2}=6$.
