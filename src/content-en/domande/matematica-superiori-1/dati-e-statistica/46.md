The values $1,2,3$ have frequencies $3$, $6$, and $4$, respectively. How many observations are at most $2$?

- $6$
- $9$
- $3$
- $13$

## Explanation

“At most two” includes the values one and two. Add the frequencies of both values. The frequency of three is outside the requested count.

$$
N_{\le2}=3+6=9
$$

The result is $9$.
