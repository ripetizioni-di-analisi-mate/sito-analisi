Two disjoint groups have means $10$ and $16$, and their combined mean is $14$. What is the ratio of the second group’s size to the first’s?

- $1:2$
- $1:1$
- $2:1$
- $7:8$

## Explanation

The first group is on average $4$ units below $14$, and the second is $2$ units above. The total deviations must balance. If their sizes are $n_1$ and $n_2$, then $4n_1=2n_2$. So the second group, whose average deviation is smaller, needs twice as many observations.
