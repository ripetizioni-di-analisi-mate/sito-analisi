What is the arithmetic mean of $5, 6, 7, 8$?

- $26$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $7$

## Explanation

The arithmetic mean is the sum of the observations divided by their number. There are four observations. The mean can be a noninteger even when all the values are integers.

$$
\bar x=\frac{5+6+7+8}4=\frac{13}{2}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{13}{2}$.
