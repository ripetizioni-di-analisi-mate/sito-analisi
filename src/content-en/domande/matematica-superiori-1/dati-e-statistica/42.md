Five vehicles in a fleet are observed for eight days each. How many units and how many daily observations are there if the unit is the vehicle?

- $40$ units and $40$ observations.
- $5$ units and $40$ observations.
- $8$ units and $5$ observations.
- $5$ units and $8$ observations.

## Explanation

The same unit can supply several measurements. Here each of the five vehicles provides eight daily observations, giving $5\cdot8=40$. To find a mean per vehicle we would group the eight observations for each one, since a new day does not add a new vehicle to the fleet.
