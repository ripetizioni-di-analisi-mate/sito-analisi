Five numbers have mean $8$. Three of them are $4$, $7$, and $9$. What can be found about the two remaining numbers?

- They must both equal $10$.
- Their sum is $40$.
- Their difference is $10$.
- Their sum is $20$.

## Explanation

The sum of all observations is $5\cdot8=40$. The three known values contribute $4+7+9=20$, so the other two sum to $20$. They could be $10$ and $10$, or $8$ and $12$. The sum is determined, while finding the individual values would require more information.
