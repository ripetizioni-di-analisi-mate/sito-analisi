A table has four disjoint and exhaustive categories. The relative frequencies of the first three are $0.2$, $0.35$, and $0.1$. What is the relative frequency of the fourth category?

- $0.35$
- $0.65$
- $0.55$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

Relative frequencies sum to $1$. The first three sum to $0.2+0.35+0.1=0.65$. The fourth is therefore $1-0.65=0.35$.
