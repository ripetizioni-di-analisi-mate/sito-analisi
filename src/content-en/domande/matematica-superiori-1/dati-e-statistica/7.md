The sorted data are $3,5,7,9,10$. Two observations below $3$ are added. What is the new median?

- $7$
- $3$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

With seven values, the new median is the fourth value. The two added observations take the first two positions, shifting $3$ into third place and $5$ into fourth. The median becomes $5$, whatever the two new values are, provided both are below $3$.
