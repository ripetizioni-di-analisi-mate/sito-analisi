A histogram uses classes $[0,4)$ and $[4,10)$ with counts $8$ and $12$. Which class includes an observation equal to $4$?

- $[0,4)$
- Both classes.
- Neither class.
- $[4,10)$

## Explanation

The round bracket excludes the right endpoint of $[0,4)$. The square bracket includes the left endpoint of $[4,10)$. The value $4$ is therefore counted once, in the second class. This convention avoids leaving a boundary value out of the table or counting it twice.
