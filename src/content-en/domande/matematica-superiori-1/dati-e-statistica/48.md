The values $1,2,3$ have frequencies $5$, $10$, and $6$, respectively. How many observations are at most $2$?

- $10$
- $5$
- $21$
- $15$

## Explanation

“At most two” includes the values one and two. Add the frequencies of both values. The frequency of three is outside the requested count.

$$
N_{\le2}=5+10=15
$$

The result is $15$.
