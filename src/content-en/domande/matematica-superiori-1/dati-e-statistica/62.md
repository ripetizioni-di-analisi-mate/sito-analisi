The values $1,4,5,10$ are replaced by their negatives. What is the median of the new values?

- $4.5$
- $-4.5$
- $-5$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Taking negatives reverses the order. The sorted list is $-10,-5,-4,-1$. With four observations use both middle positions, giving $\dfrac{-5-4}{2}=-4.5$. The median changes sign because both values that determine it were replaced by their negatives.
