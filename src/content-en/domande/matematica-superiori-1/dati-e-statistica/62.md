A set of measurements has a mean of $7$ cm. If each measurement is doubled, what is the new mean?

- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $49\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Doubling each measurement also doubles their sum. The number of observations stays the same. The new mean is therefore twice the original mean.

$$
\overline{2x}=2\bar x=2\cdot7=14
$$

The result is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
