A data set has a mean of $10$. If 3 is added to every value, what is the new mean?

- $28$
- $10$
- $13$
- $30$

## Explanation

Adding three to every value increases the sum by three times the number of observations. Dividing by the same number of observations makes the mean increase by three. The size of the group does not affect this result.

$$
\overline{x+3}=\bar x+3=10+3=13
$$

The result is $13$.
