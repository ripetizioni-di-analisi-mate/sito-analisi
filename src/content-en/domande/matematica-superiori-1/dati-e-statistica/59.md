Replace each value $x$ with its deviation $x-\bar x$, where $\bar x$ is the original mean. What is the mean of the new values?

- $\bar x$
- $-\bar x$
- $0$
- It depends on the maximum.

## Explanation

If there are $N$ observations, the original sum is $N\bar x$. Subtracting $\bar x$ from each of the $N$ values removes exactly $N\bar x$ from the sum. The new sum is zero and the number of observations is unchanged. So the mean deviation is always zero.
