Five measurements have minimum $6$ and maximum $10$. Which of these means is possible?

- $6$
- $10$
- $10.5$
- $8$

## Explanation

A mean lies between the minimum and maximum. Here it lies strictly between them because the list includes a $6$ and a larger value, and a $10$ and a smaller value. The value $8$ is attainable, for example with the five measurements $6,7,8,9,10$.
