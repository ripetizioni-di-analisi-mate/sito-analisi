The observations are $2,4,4,5,20$. The last observation is corrected from $20$ to $9$. What is the new median?

- $5$
- $4$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

After the correction, the ordered data are $2,4,4,5,9$. With five observations, the median is the third value. That value is $4$ because the correction leaves the first four values unchanged.
