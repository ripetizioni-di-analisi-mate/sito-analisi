The values $1,2,3$ have frequencies $4$, $8$, and $5$, respectively. How many observations are at most $2$?

- $8$
- $4$
- $12$
- $17$

## Explanation

“At most two” includes the values one and two. Add the frequencies of both values. The frequency of three is outside the requested count.

$$
N_{\le2}=4+8=12
$$

The result is $12$.
