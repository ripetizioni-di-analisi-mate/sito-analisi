The cumulative counts at $0,1,2,3$ are $4,10,17,22$. How many observations are strictly greater than $1$ and at most $2$?

- $10$
- $13$
- $7$
- $17$

## Explanation

Start from the $17$ observations at most $2$. These include the $10$ observations at most $1$, which the question excludes. This leaves $17-10=7$ values. Here $2$ is the only value meeting both inequalities, so the difference is exactly its absolute frequency.
