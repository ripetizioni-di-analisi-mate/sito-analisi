What is the mode of the data $5, 6, 6, 6, 8, 8$?

- $5$
- $6$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$

## Explanation

The mode is the value with the greatest frequency. The value $5$ plus one occurs three times, while the other values occur less often. The mode names the observed value, while its frequency counts how often it occurs.

The result is $6$.
