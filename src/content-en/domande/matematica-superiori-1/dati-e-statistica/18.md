Responses “low, medium, high” are coded as $1,2,3$ or $1,2,8$. Which result is guaranteed to be the same under both encodings?

- The mean of the codes.
- The percentage of ratings of at least medium.
- The difference between the codes for high and medium.
- The range of the codes when all three ratings occur.

## Explanation

Both encodings place medium and high above low. The count of ratings at least medium uses the same responses and denominator. The numerical code for high changes, which can change the mean and distances even though the students’ ratings are exactly the same.
