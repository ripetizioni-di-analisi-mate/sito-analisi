What is the mode of the data $4, 5, 5, 5, 7, 7$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}$

## Explanation

The mode is the value with the greatest frequency. The value $4$ plus one occurs three times, while the other values occur less often. The mode names the observed value, while its frequency counts how often it occurs.

The result is $5$.
