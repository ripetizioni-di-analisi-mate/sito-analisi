The values $1$, $2$, $4$, and $5$ have frequencies $5$, $5$, $4$, and $2$. One more observation equal to $4$ is recorded. What are the modes now?

- $1,2,4$
- $1,2$
- $4$
- $1,2,4,5$

## Explanation

The new observation raises the frequency of $4$ from four to five. The frequencies of $1$ and $2$ are already five and stay there. The value $5$ still occurs only twice. The maximum frequency is therefore five, attained by three distinct values.
