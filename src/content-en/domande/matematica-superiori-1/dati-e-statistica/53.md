A pie chart is meant to split one total into exclusive categories. Which data set meets this condition directly?

- One main means of transport recorded for each traveller.
- All sports each student plays, with several allowed.
- The number of students with at least one absence and the number with at least three.
- The attendance percentage in each of three different classes.

## Explanation

Recording one mode of transport per traveller assigns each person to one sector. The sector counts then reconstruct the total number of travellers. In the sports data, a person may be counted several times. The two absence counts overlap. The class percentages refer to different groups.
