An absent student's missing grade is mistakenly entered as $0$. The four present students have mean grade $7$. What mean would the register display?

- $5.6$
- $7$
- $6.75$
- $4$

## Explanation

The recorded grades sum to $28$. Entering a zero leaves the sum unchanged but increases the divisor to five, giving $\dfrac{28}{5}=5.6$.
