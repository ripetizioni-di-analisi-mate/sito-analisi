The four students present at a test have mean $7$. For a fifth student, who was absent, the mark $0$ is entered by mistake in place of the missing value. What mean appears in the register?

- $5.6$
- $7$
- $1.4$
- $3.5$

## Explanation

The four students present have mean $7$, so the sum of their marks is $4\cdot7=28$. The zero entered for the absent student does not change the sum, while it raises the number of values from four to five.

Therefore the mean that appears in the register is $\dfrac{28}{5}=5.6$. The mean $3.5$ comes from averaging $7$ and $0$, as if the four students present and the absent one had the same weight.
