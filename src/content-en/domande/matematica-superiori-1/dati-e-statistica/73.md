In a team each player wears a shirt number from $1$ to $23$, assigned at the start of the season. What type of variable is the shirt number?

- Nominal categorical variable
- Discrete quantitative variable
- Ordinal categorical variable
- Continuous quantitative variable

## Explanation

The shirt number serves to tell the players apart, so it is a label and not the result of a count or a measurement. The sum or the mean of the shirt numbers describes no property of the players, therefore the variable is not quantitative.

The number $10$ does not indicate a player who comes after the one with number $5$ in any ranking, so the categories have no natural order. The variable is therefore **nominal categorical**, even though its categories are written with digits.
