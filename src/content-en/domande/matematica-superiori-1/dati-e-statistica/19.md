A sixth observation is added to the data $1,2,2,4,4$. Which value makes $2$ the only mode?

- $1$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Initially $2$ and $4$ each occur twice. Adding $2$ raises its frequency to three while the frequency of $4$ stays at two. Adding $1$ creates another tie, and adding $4$ makes it the only mode. The new value $3$ would leave the two highest frequencies unchanged.
