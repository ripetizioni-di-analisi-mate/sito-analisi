What is the mode of the data $6, 7, 7, 7, 9, 9$?

- $6$
- $9$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$

## Explanation

The mode is the value with the greatest frequency. The value $6$ plus one occurs three times, while the other values occur less often. The mode names the observed value, while its frequency counts how often it occurs.

The result is $7$.
