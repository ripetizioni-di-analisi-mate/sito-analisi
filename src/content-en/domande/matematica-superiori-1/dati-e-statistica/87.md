What is the median of the ordered data $6, 6, 7, 7, 26$, which include a value much larger than the others?

- $\dfrac{52}{5}$
- $6$
- $7$
- $26$

## Explanation

The median of five ordered observations is the third value. The extreme value greatly increases the sum and consequently the mean. It leaves the middle position unchanged in this list. The third observation is still the required value.

$$
\operatorname{median}=7
$$

The result is $7$.
