Only a median of $6$ and an odd sample size of $7$ are known. What is the greatest possible number of observations strictly below $6$?

- $4$
- $5$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The fourth ordered value is $6$. Only the first three positions can contain values strictly below it, and all three could do so.
