A list of $7$ observations has median $6$. What is the largest possible number of observations strictly less than $6$?

- $4$
- $5$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

Seven ordered values have the central position in fourth place, so $x_{(4)}=6$. The values in positions five to seven are greater than or equal to $x_{(4)}$, therefore none of them is strictly less than $6$.

That leaves the first three positions, which can contain values less than $6$, as in the list $1,2,3,6,7,8,9$. The largest number of observations strictly less than $6$ is therefore $3$. The answer $4$ also counts the central position, which contains the value $6$.
