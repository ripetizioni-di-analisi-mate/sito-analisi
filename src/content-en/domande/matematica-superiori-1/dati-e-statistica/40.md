The value $3$ occurs four times and the value $x$ occurs six times. The mean of the ten observations is $6$. What is $x$?

- $5$
- $6$
- $12$
- $8$

## Explanation

The mean gives the total sum $10\cdot6=60$. The four observations equal to $3$ contribute $12$. This leaves $60-12=48$ for the six observations equal to $x$, so $x=48:6=8$.

Substituting the result back into the sum, the six equal terms give exactly the remaining forty-eight points. This recovers the total of sixty and confirms the required mean.
