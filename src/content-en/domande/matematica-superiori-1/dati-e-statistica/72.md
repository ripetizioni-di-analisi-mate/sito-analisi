A survey allows several answers to a question about languages spoken. The percentages for the languages add to $140\%$. What feature of the data can explain this?

- The sample must contain $140$ people
- Each category has frequency zero
- Languages are quantitative data
- A person may belong to several categories

## Explanation

Each percentage uses the same total number of people. A participant speaking two languages contributes to two numerators, so the total can exceed $100\%$.
