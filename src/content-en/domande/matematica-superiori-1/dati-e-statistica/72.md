A questionnaire allows several answers to the question on languages spoken. The percentages of the individual languages, computed on the number of people, add up to $140\%$. Which feature of the data explains the result?

- The sample consists of $140$ people
- The percentages were rounded to whole numbers
- Language spoken is a quantitative variable
- One person can fall into several categories

## Explanation

Each percentage has as its denominator the total number of people who answered. The percentages of disjoint and exhaustive categories add up to $100\%$, because each person is counted in exactly one category.

When several answers are allowed the categories are not disjoint, because a person who speaks two languages appears in the numerator of two percentages. Therefore the sum can exceed $100\%$. Rounding to whole numbers shifts the sum by only a few percentage points, so it does not explain an excess of $40\%$. The feature that explains the result is that one person can fall into several categories.
