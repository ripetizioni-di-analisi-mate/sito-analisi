A sector of a pie chart has an angle of $72^\circ$. The sample contains $45$ observations. How many are in the category represented by that sector?

- $12$
- $18$
- $9$
- $20$

## Explanation

The sector occupies the fraction $\dfrac{72}{360}=\dfrac15$ of the circle. It represents the same fifth of the sample, so the count equals $45:5=9$.
