Four marks out of ten have an average of $7$. A fifth mark is added, at most $10$. What is the highest possible average?

- $8$
- $7.6$
- $8.5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The four marks total $4\cdot7=28$. The final denominator is always $5$, so the mean is largest when the new mark is $10$. This gives $\dfrac{28+10}{5}=7.6$. Reaching a mean of $8$ would require a total of $40$ and a fifth mark of $12$, above the limit.
