A group of $10$ students has a mean of $6$. Another group of $20$ students has a mean of $9$. What is the mean of the $30$ students altogether?

- $8$
- $7$
- $7.5$
- $9$

## Explanation

The groups contribute sums $10\cdot6=60$ and $20\cdot9=180$, respectively. Divide $60+180$ by the total number, $30$. The result is $8$.

Each group is weighted by its number of students. That is why the result is closer to the mean of the group with twenty students. A simple mean of the two group means would assign each group the same weight.
