A category represents $25\%$ of $24$ observations. What is the angle of its sector in a pie chart?

- $25^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $100^\circ$
- $180^\circ$

## Explanation

The whole pie chart spans three hundred sixty degrees. The sector occupies the same fraction of the circle as the relative frequency. A quarter of the total corresponds to a quarter turn.

$$
\alpha=\frac{25}{100}\cdot360^\circ=90^\circ
$$

The result is $90^\circ$.
