A $120^\circ$ sector represents $18$ observations. Another sector measures $80^\circ$. How many observations does the second sector represent?

- $27$
- $12$
- $8$
- $36$

## Explanation

The $120^\circ$ sector is a third of the circle, so the total is $3\cdot18=54$. The $80^\circ$ sector occupies $\dfrac{80}{360}=\dfrac29$ of the circle, giving $\dfrac29\cdot54=12$ observations. As a check, compare the angles directly. Since $\dfrac{80}{120}=\dfrac23$, the count is two thirds of $18$, which is $12$.
