The median of $2,4,7,9,11$ is $7$. Which value can replace $11$ while keeping the same median?

- $6$
- $4$
- $1$
- $8$

## Explanation

After replacement there are still five values, so the median is the third. To leave $7$ in the middle, the new value must be at least $7$. Replacing $11$ with $8$ gives $2,4,7,8,9$. Each other choice would place at least three observations before $7$.
