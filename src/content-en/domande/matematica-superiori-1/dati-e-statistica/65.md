Eight lengths have range $4$ centimetres. What happens when the lengths are written in millimetres?

- The range is $40$ millimetres and there are still $8$ observations.
- The range is $4$ millimetres and there are still $8$ observations.
- The range is $4$ millimetres and there are now $80$ observations.
- The range is $40$ millimetres and there are now $80$ observations.

## Explanation

Converting centimetres to millimetres multiplies the numerical values of both extremes by $10$. Their difference is also multiplied by $10$. This gives $40$ millimetres, the same length as $4$ centimetres. The number of observations is a count and is unaffected by the conversion.
