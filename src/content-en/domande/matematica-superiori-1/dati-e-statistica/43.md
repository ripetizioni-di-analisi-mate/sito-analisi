A waiting time is measured to the nearest second. The recorded values are whole numbers. How is the duration before rounding classified?

- Discrete quantitative.
- Ordinal categorical.
- Continuous quantitative.
- Nominal categorical.

## Explanation

Recording to the nearest second chooses a precision for writing down the data. The measured duration can take values between consecutive seconds. For example, $4.2$ seconds is recorded as $4$. The question classifies the variable before rounding, which makes it continuous quantitative.
