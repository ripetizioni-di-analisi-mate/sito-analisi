A list of $8$ durations, allowing repeats, has a median of $9$ minutes. Which statement about the data must hold?

- One duration is exactly $9$ minutes.
- At least four durations are at most $9$ minutes.
- Exactly four durations are less than $9$ minutes.
- The sum of the durations is $72$ minutes.

## Explanation

The median lies between the fourth and fifth values in order. The fourth is therefore at most $9$, and so are the first four durations. The two middle values could be $8$ and $10$, with no observed $9$. The sum instead depends on all observations.
