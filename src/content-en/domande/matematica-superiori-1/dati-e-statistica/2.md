Three durations are $5$, $8$, and $11$ minutes. The value $5$ is corrected to $2$, and $11$ to $14$. What happens to the mean?

- It rises by $3$ minutes.
- It stays at $8$ minutes.
- It falls by $3$ minutes.
- It rises by $1$ minute.

## Explanation

The first correction subtracts $3$ minutes from the total, and the second adds $3$. The sum remains $24$ and there are still $3$ observations. So the mean stays at $8$ minutes. Here the corrections change how spread out the values are, but they balance exactly in the mean.
