A series of temperatures in degrees Celsius has range $8$. Each value is increased by $10$ degrees to correct the instrument’s zero. What is the new range?

- $18$
- $8$
- $10$
- $2$

## Explanation

The maximum and minimum both increase by $10$ degrees. The two increases cancel in their difference. If the extremes were $M$ and $m$, the new range is $(M+10)-(m+10)=M-m$. The range stays at $8$ even though the individual temperatures have changed.
