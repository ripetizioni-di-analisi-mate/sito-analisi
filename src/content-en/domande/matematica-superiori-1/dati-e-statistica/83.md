A bar chart starts at $82$ and compares counts of $85$ and $95$. What is the difference between the counts, and what is the ratio of the second visible height to the first?

- Difference $10$ and ratio $\dfrac{19}{17}$.
- Difference $13$ and ratio $\dfrac{13}{3}$.
- Difference $10$ and ratio $\dfrac{13}{3}$.
- Difference $3$ and ratio $\dfrac{19}{17}$.

## Explanation

The counts differ by $95-85=10$. Starting at the chosen baseline gives visible heights of $85-82=3$ and $95-82=13$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{13}{3}$. The ratio of the actual counts is $\dfrac{95}{85}=\dfrac{19}{17}$ because it uses the full values.
