Error counts are grouped as $0$, $1$, and “at least $2$”, with frequencies $3$, $4$, and $2$. What is the smallest possible total number of errors?

- $6$
- $9$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The four one-error cases contribute four. The two cases in the last class contribute at least four more, giving a minimum of eight.
