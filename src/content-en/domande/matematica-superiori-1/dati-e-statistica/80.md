The number of mistakes in a group of dictations is reported in a table with the classes $0$, $1$ and “at least $2$”, with frequencies $3$, $4$ and $2$. What is the smallest total number of mistakes compatible with the table?

- $6$
- $9$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The three dictations in class $0$ contain no mistakes. The four dictations in class $1$ contain one mistake each, so they contribute $4$ mistakes.

The two dictations in the class “at least $2$” each contain at least two mistakes, therefore they contribute at least $2\cdot2=4$ mistakes. The total is thus at least $0+4+4=8$, and it equals exactly $8$ when the two dictations in the last class have two mistakes each. The answer $9$ is the number of dictations, while the answer $6$ counts only one mistake for each dictation in the last class. The smallest total number of mistakes is therefore $8$.
