To find out how much time the students enrolled at a school spend on homework each day, $120$ students chosen from the class lists are interviewed. What is the population of the survey?

- The $120$ students interviewed
- The time spent on homework
- All the students enrolled at the school
- The classes of the school

## Explanation

The population is the set of all the units to which the survey refers. The question concerns the students enrolled at the school, so the statistical unit is the student and the population consists of all the enrolled students.

The $120$ students interviewed are only part of the population, therefore they form the sample. The time spent on homework is the property recorded for each student, that is, the variable. The population of the survey therefore consists of all the students enrolled at the school.
