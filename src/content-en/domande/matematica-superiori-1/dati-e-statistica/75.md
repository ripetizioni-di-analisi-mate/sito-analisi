Two volunteers classify ten photographs and agree on seven. Three photographs are assessed a second time, each with agreement. What is the agreement percentage across the thirteen paired judgements?

- $70\%$
- $100\%$
- $\dfrac{10}{13}\cdot100\%$
- $\dfrac7{13}\cdot100\%$

## Explanation

The agreements increase from seven to ten. The denominator is the number of comparisons made, thirteen.
