A survey invites $40$ people. Of the $30$ respondents, $12$ are in favour. Which interval contains all possible proportions in favour among those invited?

- From $30\%$ to $55\%$.
- From $40\%$ to $55\%$.
- From $30\%$ to $40\%$.
- From $12\%$ to $22\%$.

## Explanation

If none of the ten non-respondents is in favour, the count is $12$ out of $40$, or $30\%$. If all are in favour, it reaches $12+10=22$ out of $40$, or $55\%$. The value $40\%=12/30$ refers only to people who responded.
