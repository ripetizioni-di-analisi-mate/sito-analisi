A stop records $0$, $6$ and $3$ buses in three successive hours. Which information is lost if only the hourly mean and the three-hour duration are retained?

- The total count given the three hours
- The mean count per minute across all three hours
- The hour of the maximum and the maximum itself
- The total number of hours stated in the register

## Explanation

The mean is $3$ for any order and also for the counts $3,3,3$. It recovers the total but leaves the maximum and its hour undetermined.
