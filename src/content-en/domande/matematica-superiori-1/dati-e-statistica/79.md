In three consecutive hours $0$, $6$ and $3$ buses pass a stop. Only the hourly mean and the number of hours are kept. Which information can no longer be recovered?

- The total number of buses that passed
- The mean number of buses per minute
- The hour in which the largest number of buses passed
- The mean number of buses per half hour

## Explanation

The hourly mean is $\dfrac{0+6+3}{3}=3$ buses. From the mean and the number of hours one recovers the total $3\cdot3=9$ buses, and dividing the total by $180$ minutes or by six half hours gives the means per minute and per half hour.

The counts $0$, $6$, $3$ and $6$, $3$, $0$ have the same mean $3$, but the maximum falls in the second hour in the first case and in the first hour in the second. Therefore the hour in which the largest number of buses passed cannot be recovered from the mean.
