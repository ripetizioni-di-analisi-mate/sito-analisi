A group of $7$ measurements has mean $12$. Which single measurement can be added while keeping the same mean?

- $12$
- $0$
- $7$
- $24$

## Explanation

The initial sum is $7\cdot12=84$. With eight measurements a total of $8\cdot12=96$ is needed to keep the mean. So the added measurement must be $96-84=12$. In general a new value equal to the old mean increases the sum by exactly what the new denominator requires.
