Among $15$ observations, one category occurs $6$ times. What is its relative frequency?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $6$

## Explanation

Relative frequency is the absolute frequency divided by the total number of observations. The common factor $3$ cancels. The result is between zero and one.

$$
f=\frac{6}{15}=\frac25
$$

The result is $\dfrac{2}{5}$.
