An outcome occurs $18$ times out of $30$ in one group and $20$ times out of $50$ in another. Which group has the higher absolute frequency and which has the higher relative frequency?

- Both in the second.
- Both in the first.
- Absolute in the first, relative in the second.
- Absolute in the second, relative in the first.

## Explanation

For absolute frequency, compare $18$ and $20$ directly. For relative frequency, divide each count by its own group size. The ratios are $\dfrac{18}{30}=60\%$ and $\dfrac{20}{50}=40\%$. The second group has more cases, but the first has the higher proportion.
