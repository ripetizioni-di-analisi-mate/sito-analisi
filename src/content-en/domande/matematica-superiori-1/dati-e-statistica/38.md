Three disjoint classes have combined mean $13$. The first has mean $10$ and the second has mean $13$. The first and third have the same number of students. What is the third class’s mean?

- $13$
- $16$
- $19$
- $10$

## Explanation

The second class is already centred at $13$, so adding up its observations does not move the mean away from that value. The first class contributes an average deviation of $3$ units below $13$. The third is the same size, so it must contribute an average deviation of $3$ units above. Its mean is $16$.
