The value $5$ occurs 2 times and the value $7$ occurs 4 times. What is the mean of the six observations?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{19}{3}$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

Weight each value by its own frequency. Divide the weighted sum by the total frequency. The mean is closer to the value that occurs more often.

$$
\bar x=\frac{2\cdot5+4\cdot7}{2+4}=\frac{19}{3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{19}{3}$.
