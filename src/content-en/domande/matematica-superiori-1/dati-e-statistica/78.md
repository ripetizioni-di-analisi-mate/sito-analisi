A museum records $30$ visitors on Monday and $40$ on Tuesday, and each person is counted only once per day. What are the possible numbers of different people who visited the museum over the two days?

- The integers from $30$ to $40$
- The integers from $40$ to $70$
- The integers from $10$ to $70$
- The integers from $35$ to $70$

## Explanation

The counts for the two days refer to visits, and the same person can appear in both. The number of different people is largest when no Monday visitor comes back on Tuesday, and then it equals $30+40=70$.

The number is smallest when all $30$ Monday visitors come back on Tuesday, because then the different people are only the $40$ Tuesday visitors. Letting anywhere from $0$ to $30$ people come back gives every intermediate value. The possible numbers of different people are therefore the integers from $40$ to $70$.
