Only the counts on two consecutive days are known: $30$ and $40$ visitors. Each person is counted once per day. Which interval of integers gives exactly the possible totals of distinct visitors across the two days?

- From $30$ to $40$
- From $40$ to $70$
- From $10$ to $70$
- From $35$ to $70$

## Explanation

The minimum occurs if all first-day visitors return. The maximum occurs with disjoint groups and is $30+40=70$.
