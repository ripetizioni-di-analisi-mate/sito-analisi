What are the modes of $2,2,3,4,4,7$?

- $3$ e $4$
- $2$ e $7$
- $4$ e $7$
- $2$ e $4$

## Explanation

The values $2$ and $4$ each occur twice. The values $3$ and $7$ each occur once. Two values have the highest frequency, so the modes are $2$ and $4$.
