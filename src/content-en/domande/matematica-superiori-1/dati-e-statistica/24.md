What is the range of the data $7, 14, 8, 10$?

- $14$
- $9$
- $21$
- $7$

## Explanation

The range is the difference between the maximum and the minimum. Find those two extremes in the data set. Their positions in the original list do not matter.

$$
R=14-7=7
$$

The result is $7$.
