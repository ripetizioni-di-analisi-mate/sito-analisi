The data are $5,8,13,k$ and have range $10$. What are all possible values of $k$?

- $3$
- $15$
- $5$ or $13$
- $3$ or $15$

## Explanation

If $k$ is between $5$ and $13$, the range stays at $8$. To reach $10$, it must be the new minimum or new maximum. In the first case $13-k=10$ gives $k=3$. In the second, $k-5=10$ gives $k=15$. Both satisfy the condition for their respective extreme.
