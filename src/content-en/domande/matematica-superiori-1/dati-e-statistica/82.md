Two bar charts have a vertical axis starting at $0$ and the same height on the page. The axis of the first goes up to $100$, that of the second up to $200$. In each chart one bar is half as tall as the axis. What is the ratio of the value of the bar in the first chart to that of the bar in the second?

- $1:1$
- $1:2$
- $2:1$
- $1:4$

## Explanation

With the axis starting at $0$, the height of a bar is proportional to the value, according to the scale of its own axis. In the first chart half the axis corresponds to the value $\dfrac{100}{2}=50$, in the second to the value $\dfrac{200}{2}=100$.

The ratio of the two values is therefore $50:100=1:2$. The answer $1:1$ comes from comparing the heights on the page without reading the scales of the two axes.
