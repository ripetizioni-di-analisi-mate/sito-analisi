Two bar charts have a zero baseline and the same physical height. The first vertical scale ends at $100$ and the second at $200$. If a bar fills half of each frame, what is the ratio of the first represented value to the second?

- $1:1$
- $1:2$
- $2:1$
- $1:4$

## Explanation

The two half heights stand for $50$ and $100$. Their ratio is $50:100=1:2$.
