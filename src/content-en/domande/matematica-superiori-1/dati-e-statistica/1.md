What is the arithmetic mean of $3, 4, 5, 6$?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $18$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The arithmetic mean is the sum of the observations divided by their number. There are four observations. The mean can be a noninteger even when all the values are integers.

$$
\bar x=\frac{3+4+5+6}4=\frac{9}{2}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{9}{2}$.

The total is preserved if we imagine sharing it into four equal parts. The mean is the value of each part.
