A set of measurements has a mean of $9$ cm. If each measurement is doubled, what is the new mean?

- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $81\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Doubling each measurement also doubles their sum. The number of observations stays the same. The new mean is therefore twice the original mean.

$$
\overline{2x}=2\bar x=2\cdot9=18
$$

The result is $18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
