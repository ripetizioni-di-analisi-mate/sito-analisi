A calibration transforms every value $x$ into $y=3x-4$. The mean of the new values is $38$. What was the original mean?

- $\dfrac{34}{3}$
- $110$
- $\dfrac{38}{3}$
- $14$

## Explanation

Multiplying all measurements by $3$ multiplies their mean by $3$. Subtracting $4$ from each measurement then subtracts $4$ from the mean. Thus $3\bar x-4=38$. To recover $\bar x$, first add $4$ and then divide by $3$, giving $14$.
