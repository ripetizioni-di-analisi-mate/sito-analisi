In a survey of $30$ people, $30\%$ choose one answer. How many choose it?

- $90$
- $1$
- $9$
- $21$

## Explanation

The percentage corresponds to three tenths of the total. Multiplying the number of people by three tenths gives the absolute frequency. The result is an integer because it counts people.

$$
n_i=\frac{30}{100}\cdot30=9
$$

The result is $9$.
