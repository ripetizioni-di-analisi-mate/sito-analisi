The mean of five measurements is $12$. One measurement is corrected from $10$ to $15$. What is the new mean?

- $12$
- $15$
- $17$
- $13$

## Explanation

The initial sum is $5\cdot12=60$. The correction increases it by $15-10=5$. There are still five measurements, so the new mean is $\dfrac{60+5}{5}=13$.
