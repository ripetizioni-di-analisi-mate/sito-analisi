Six judges each award a whole-number score from $0$ to $10$. Which exact mean could appear on the score sheet?

- $7.2$
- $7.4$
- $7.6$
- $7.5$

## Explanation

The total must be an integer. Multiplying each candidate by $6$, only $7.5$ gives an integer total, $45$. Three scores of $7$ and three of $8$ achieve it.
