Six judges each give an integer score from $0$ to $10$. Which of these values can be the exact mean of the six scores?

- $7.2$
- $7.4$
- $7.6$
- $7.5$

## Explanation

The sum of six integer scores is an integer, and the mean is this sum divided by $6$. Therefore a possible mean, multiplied by $6$, must give an integer.

We have $6\cdot7.2=43.2$, $6\cdot7.4=44.4$ and $6\cdot7.6=45.6$, so these three means are ruled out. On the other hand $6\cdot7.5=45$, and the sum $45$ is obtained, for example, with three scores of $7$ and three scores of $8$. The possible mean is therefore $7.5$.
