In two disjoint groups, response A occurs $9$ times out of $15$ and $8$ times out of $25$. What is its relative frequency in the combined group?

- $\dfrac{23}{50}$
- $\dfrac{17}{25}$
- $\dfrac{17}{40}$
- $\dfrac{8}{25}$

## Explanation

Add the counts for A and, separately, the group sizes. A occurs $17$ times out of $40$, so its relative frequency is $\dfrac{17}{40}$. The simple mean of the two group frequencies would give $\dfrac{23}{50}$, but it would assign equal weight to groups of different sizes.
