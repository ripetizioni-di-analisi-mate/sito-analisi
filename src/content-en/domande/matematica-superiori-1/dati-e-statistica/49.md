Four ordered values are possible, some with frequency zero. Which row could give the cumulative relative frequencies at all four values?

- $20\%,20\%,60\%,100\%$
- $20\%,50\%,40\%,100\%$
- $20\%,50\%,80\%,90\%$
- $50\%,50\%,100\%,110\%$

## Explanation

The cumulative frequency increases or stays the same because each step adds a nonnegative frequency. It must reach $100\%$ when every value has been counted. The row $20\%,20\%,60\%,100\%$ corresponds to frequencies $20\%,0\%,40\%,40\%$. The two equal entries show there were no observations at the second value.
