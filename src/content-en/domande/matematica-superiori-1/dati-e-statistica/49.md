The values $1,2,3$ have frequencies $6$, $12$, and $7$, respectively. How many observations are at most $2$?

- $18$
- $12$
- $6$
- $25$

## Explanation

“At most two” includes the values one and two. Add the frequencies of both values. The frequency of three is outside the requested count.

$$
N_{\le2}=6+12=18
$$

The result is $18$.
