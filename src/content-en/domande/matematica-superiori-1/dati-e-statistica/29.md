In a group of $18$ people, which exact percentage can correspond to a whole number of people?

- $50\%$
- $25\%$
- $10\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the group size must give a whole count. Half of $18$ is $9$. A quarter is $4.5$, a tenth is $1.8$, and two fifths are $7.2$. These last three results are fractional, so none can be a count of people for the stated percentage.
