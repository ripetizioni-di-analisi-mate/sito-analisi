What is the coordinate of the point $\dfrac35$ of a unit to the left of $-2$?

- $-\dfrac75$
- $\dfrac{13}5$
- $-\dfrac{13}5$
- $-\dfrac85$

## Explanation

A move to the left decreases the coordinate. Starting from $-2=-\dfrac{10}5$, subtract three fifths to reach $-\dfrac{13}5$. As a check, the point is between $-3$ and $-2$ and is $\left|-\dfrac{13}5+2\right|=\dfrac35$ from $-2$. The point $-\dfrac75$ is the same distance away, but lies to the right because it is reached by adding instead of subtracting.
