A tap delivers $\dfrac34$ L per minute. Which calculation gives the number of seconds needed to fill half a litre?

- $\left(\dfrac34:\dfrac12\right)\cdot60$
- $\left(\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac34\right):60$
- $\left(\dfrac12:\dfrac34\right):60$
- $\left(\dfrac12:\dfrac34\right)\cdot60$

## Explanation

Dividing the required litres by litres per minute gives the time in minutes, namely $\dfrac23$. Multiplying by $60$ gives $40$ seconds.
