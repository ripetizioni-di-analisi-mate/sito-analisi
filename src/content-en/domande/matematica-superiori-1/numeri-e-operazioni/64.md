You need to carry $\dfrac{17}{2}$ litres. Each container holds at most $\dfrac32$ litres and may be partly filled. What is the smallest number of containers you need?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{17}{3}$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{17}{2}:\dfrac32=\dfrac{17}{3}=5+\dfrac23$ measures the volume in full containers. Five containers hold only $\dfrac{15}{2}$ litres, leaving one litre. A sixth can hold it because its capacity is $\dfrac32$ litres. The question asks for a whole number of containers sufficient to carry everything, so round the fractional quotient up to the next integer.
