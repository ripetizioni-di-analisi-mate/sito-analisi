Calculate $\dfrac1{4}-\dfrac1{5}$.

- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

A common denominator is $20$. The numerators become $5$ and $4$, respectively. Their difference is one.

$$
\frac1{4}-\frac1{5}=\frac{5-4}{20}=\frac{1}{20}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{20}$.
