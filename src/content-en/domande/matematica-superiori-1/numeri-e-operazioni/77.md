A strip of length $1$ is folded in half three times, always folding all layers together. What fraction of the original length is each segment between successive creases?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Each fold doubles the number of equal sections, from $1$ to $2$, then $4$ and finally $8$. A single section is therefore one eighth of the original length.
