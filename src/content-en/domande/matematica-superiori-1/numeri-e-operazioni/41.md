Consider $A=0:(-7)$ and $B=(-7):0$. Which statement is correct?

- $A=0$ and $B$ is undefined
- $A$ is undefined and $B=0$
- $A=B=0$
- Both $A$ and $B$ are undefined

## Explanation

The quotient must give back the dividend when multiplied by the divisor. For $A$, the only solution to $(-7)\cdot A=0$ is $A=0$. For $B$, we would need a value such that $0\cdot B=-7$, but any product with zero equals zero. What matters here is which term is the divisor.
