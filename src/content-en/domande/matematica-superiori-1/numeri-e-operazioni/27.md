Calculate $\dfrac{5}{4}:\dfrac23$.

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{15}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$

## Explanation

The divisor is a nonzero fraction. Dividing by two thirds is equivalent to multiplying by three halves. Multiply and reduce the resulting fraction.

$$
\frac{5}{4}:\frac23=\frac{5}{4}\cdot\frac32=\frac{15}{8}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{15}{8}$.
