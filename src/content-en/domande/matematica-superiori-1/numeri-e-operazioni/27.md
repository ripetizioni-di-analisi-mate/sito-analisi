Nenad wrote $\dfrac7{10}:\left(-\dfrac15\right)=\dfrac{10}7\cdot\left(-\dfrac15\right)$. Which correction makes the step valid?

- $\dfrac{10}7\cdot(-5)$
- $\dfrac7{10}\cdot5$
- $\dfrac7{10}\cdot(-5)$
- $\dfrac7{10}\cdot\left(-\dfrac15\right)$

## Explanation

In the multiplication that replaces division, the dividend stays $\dfrac7{10}$. The divisor $-\dfrac15$ is replaced by its reciprocal $-5$. The quotient is $-\dfrac72$. Check by multiplying by the original divisor: $\left(-\dfrac72\right)\left(-\dfrac15\right)=\dfrac7{10}$. Inverting the dividend, as in the original step, changes the value this check should reproduce.
