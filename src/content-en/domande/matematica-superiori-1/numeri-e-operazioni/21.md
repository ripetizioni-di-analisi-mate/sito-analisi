Calculate $\dfrac{9}{6}\cdot\dfrac13$.

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators. The common factor of three can be cancelled before multiplying. The final fraction has exactly the same value.

$$
\frac{9}{6}\cdot\frac13=\frac{9}{18}=\frac{1}{2}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
