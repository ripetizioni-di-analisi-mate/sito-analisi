Express $3+\dfrac2{4}$ as a single fraction.

- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

To add the integer to the fraction, express the integer with the same denominator. Multiply the numerator of the integer by that denominator. Then add the numerators.

$$
3+\frac2{4}=\frac{12+2}{4}=\frac{7}{2}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
