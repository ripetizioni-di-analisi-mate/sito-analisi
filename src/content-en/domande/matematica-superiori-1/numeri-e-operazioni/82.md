Which number belongs to $\mathbb Q$ but not to $\mathbb Z$ and has absolute value less than $1$?

- $-1$
- $-\dfrac34$
- $0$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The number $-\dfrac34$ is a ratio of integers lying strictly between $-1$ and $0$. Both zero and $-1$ are integers, while $\dfrac32$ has absolute value greater than $1$.
