What is the greatest common divisor of $24$ and $36$?

- $4$
- $24$
- $72$
- $12$

## Explanation

Write the numbers as $24=3\cdot 4\cdot2$ and $36=3\cdot 4\cdot3$. The remaining factors, two and three, are relatively prime. The greatest common divisor is therefore $12$.

$$
\gcd(24,36)=12
$$

The result is $12$.
