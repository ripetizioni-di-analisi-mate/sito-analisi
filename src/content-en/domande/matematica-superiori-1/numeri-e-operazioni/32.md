To calculate $\gcd(84,30)$, start with $84=2\cdot30+24$. Which step comes next in the Euclidean algorithm?

- $84=3\cdot24+12$
- $24=0\cdot30+24$
- $24=4\cdot6+0$
- $30=1\cdot24+6$

## Explanation

In the first step, the common divisors of $84$ and $30$ are the same as those of $30$ and remainder $24$. The next division is therefore $30:24$, with quotient $1$ and remainder $6$. Then dividing $24$ by $6$ gives remainder zero. The last nonzero remainder, $6$, is the greatest common divisor.
