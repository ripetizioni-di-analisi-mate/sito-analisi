Which interval contains $x=\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac78$?

- $0<x<\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac12<x<\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac34<x<1$
- $1<x<\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The product is $\dfrac{28}{40}=\dfrac7{10}$. To compare it with the endpoints, use twentieths: $\dfrac12=\dfrac{10}{20}$, $\dfrac7{10}=\dfrac{14}{20}$ and $\dfrac34=\dfrac{15}{20}$. The value falls between $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac34$. As a check, it is smaller than both factors, since multiplying a positive number by a positive fraction less than one makes it smaller.
