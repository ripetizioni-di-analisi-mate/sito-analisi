A pupil removes the parentheses in $11-(-33+3)$ in a single step. Which line preserves its value?

- $11-33+3$
- $11+33-3$
- $11+33+3$
- $11-33-3$

## Explanation

You are subtracting the entire sum, so both terms change sign. The new line gives $41$, agreeing with the direct calculation $11-(-30)$.
