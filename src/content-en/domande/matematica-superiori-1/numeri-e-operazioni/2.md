Calculate $-4-4+8$.

- $-16$
- $0$
- $8$
- $7$

## Explanation

First add the two negative terms to obtain $-8$. The positive term is $8$. Their sum is therefore $0$.

$$
-4-4+8=-8+8=0
$$

The result is $0$.
