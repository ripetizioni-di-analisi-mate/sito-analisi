In which division is the quotient strictly greater than both the dividend and the divisor?

- $\dfrac34:\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac12:\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac54:\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac53:\dfrac53$

## Explanation

Dividing $\dfrac34$ by $\dfrac12$ doubles it, giving $\dfrac32$, greater than both numbers. Reversing the terms gives $\dfrac23$, less than $\dfrac34$. In the divisions by $\dfrac32$ and $\dfrac53$, the divisor is greater than $1$. Since the dividend is positive, dividing by that number gives a quotient smaller than the dividend, which fails the required comparison.
