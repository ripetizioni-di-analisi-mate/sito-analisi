Make packs of 4 red pens and 3 blue pens using $16$ red pens and $12$ blue pens. After giving away 2 packs, how many pens are left?

- $26$
- $14$
- $28$
- $2$

## Explanation

Each pack contains seven pens and the available supplies make the same number of packs for both colors. Giving away two packs removes fourteen pens. The remaining total is therefore seven times the number of packs left.

$$
\frac{16}4=4,\qquad(4-2)\cdot7=14
$$

The result is $14$.
