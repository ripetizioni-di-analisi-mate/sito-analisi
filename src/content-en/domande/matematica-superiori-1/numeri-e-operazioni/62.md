A shopping list has $6$ packs at $3$ euros and a $2$ discount on the whole purchase. The till prints $6(3-2)$. By how much is that total too low?

- $6$ euros
- $10$ euros
- $2$ euros
- $12$ euros

## Explanation

The correct total is $6\cdot3-2=16$ euros, whereas the till shows $6$ euros. The discount was applied five extra times, a difference of $10$ euros.
