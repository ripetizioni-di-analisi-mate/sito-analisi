Which pair of recurring decimals sums to $1$?

- $0.\overline{27}+0.\overline{72}$
- $0.\overline{27}+0.\overline{27}$
- $0.\overline7+0.\overline1$
- $0.\overline3+0.\overline7$

## Explanation

The two-digit repeating blocks $27$ and $72$ represent $\dfrac{27}{99}$ and $\dfrac{72}{99}$. Their numerators add to exactly $99$.
