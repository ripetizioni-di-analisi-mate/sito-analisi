Which fraction in lowest terms equals $1.2\overline4$?

- $\dfrac{56}{45}$
- $\dfrac{124}{99}$
- $\dfrac{31}{25}$
- $\dfrac{11}{9}$

## Explanation

If $x=1.2\overline4$, then $10x=12.\overline4$ and $100x=124.\overline4$. Subtract these multiples because their digits after the decimal point match. This leaves $90x=124-12=112$, so $x=\dfrac{112}{90}=\dfrac{56}{45}$. Using $100x-x$ would leave a decimal part because the digit $2$ in $x$ comes before the repeating block. The result lies between $1.24$ and $1.25$.
