Calculate $\dfrac{6}{15}+\dfrac{1}{3}$.

- $\dfrac{7}{18}$
- $\dfrac{11}{15}$
- $\dfrac{7}{15}$
- $\dfrac{11}{30}$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $15$. The second fraction becomes $\dfrac{5}{15}$. Add the numerators and keep the common denominator.

$$
\frac{6}{15}+\frac13=\frac{6+5}{15}=\frac{11}{15}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{11}{15}$.
