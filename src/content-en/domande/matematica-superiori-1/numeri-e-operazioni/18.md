Equally spaced marks $A,B,C,D$ have increasing coordinates. If $A=-2$ and $D=1$, what is the coordinate of $B$?

- $-\dfrac12$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac54$
- $0$

## Explanation

Four marks enclose three intervals. The total distance is $3$, so each step is $1$ and $B=-2+1=-1$.
