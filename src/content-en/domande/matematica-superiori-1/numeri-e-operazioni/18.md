What is the correct number-line order for $-\dfrac58$, $-\dfrac12$ and $-\dfrac7{12}$?

- $-\dfrac12<-\dfrac7{12}<-\dfrac58$
- $-\dfrac58<-\dfrac7{12}<-\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac7{12}<-\dfrac58<-\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac58<-\dfrac12<-\dfrac7{12}$

## Explanation

In twenty-fourths, the three numerators, in the order given, are $-15$, $-12$ and $-14$. With the same positive denominator, the number with the smallest numerator is the smallest fraction. This gives $-15<-14<-12$. Among these negative fractions, the one furthest from zero lies furthest left, even though it has the largest absolute value.
