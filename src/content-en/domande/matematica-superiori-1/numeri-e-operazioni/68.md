Two rational numbers $a$ and $b$ satisfy $ab<0$. What can you conclude about the sign of $(-a)b$?

- It is negative
- It is zero
- It depends on which original factor is negative
- It is positive

## Explanation

By associativity, $(-a)b=(-1)(ab)=-(ab)$. The original product is negative, so its opposite is positive. For example, if $ab=-6$, the new product is $6$. It does not matter which factor was negative: changing the sign of exactly one turns two different signs into two matching signs. The product cannot be zero because $ab<0$.
