Which number lies exactly halfway between $\dfrac{5}{6}$ and $1$?

- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{11}{6}$
- $\dfrac{6}{11}$

## Explanation

The midpoint of two numbers is their arithmetic mean. Add the two values using a common denominator. Then divide the sum by two.

$$
\frac12\left(\frac{5}{6}+1\right)=\frac{11}{12}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{11}{12}$.
