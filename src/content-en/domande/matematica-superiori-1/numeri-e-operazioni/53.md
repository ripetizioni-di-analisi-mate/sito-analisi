A segment has midpoint coordinate $\dfrac56$ and left endpoint coordinate $-\dfrac13$. What is the coordinate of the right endpoint?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac53$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The distance from the left endpoint to the midpoint is $\dfrac56-\left(-\dfrac13\right)=\dfrac76$. Travel the same distance to the right of the midpoint to reach the right endpoint. Its coordinate is therefore $\dfrac56+\dfrac76=\dfrac{12}{6}=2$. Check using the mean of the endpoints: $\left(2-\dfrac13\right):2=\dfrac56$, which gives back the stated midpoint.
