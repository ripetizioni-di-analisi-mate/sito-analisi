A container is filled to $\dfrac23$ of its capacity. Then $\dfrac14$ of the liquid is poured out. What fraction of the capacity remains filled?

- $\dfrac5{12}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The fraction $\dfrac14$ refers to the liquid already in the container. Three quarters of the initial contents therefore remain. Multiplying $\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac23$ gives $\dfrac12$ of the capacity.
