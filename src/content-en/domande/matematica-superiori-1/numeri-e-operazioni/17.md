Calculate $\dfrac{5}{12}+\dfrac{1}{3}$.

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $12$. The second fraction becomes $\dfrac{4}{12}$. Add the numerators and keep the common denominator.

$$
\frac{5}{12}+\frac13=\frac{5+4}{12}=\frac{3}{4}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
