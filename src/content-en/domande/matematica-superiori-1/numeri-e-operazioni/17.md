Which common denominator lets you rewrite both $\dfrac56$ and $\dfrac38$ with integer numerators?

- $24$
- $12$
- $18$
- $32$

## Explanation

The fractions are already in lowest terms. To keep each value with an integer numerator, the new denominator must be a multiple of $6$ and of $8$ respectively. Of the choices, only $24$ meets both conditions. The rewritten fractions are $\dfrac{20}{24}$ and $\dfrac9{24}$. The denominator $32$ works for $\dfrac38$, but it is not divisible by $6$.
