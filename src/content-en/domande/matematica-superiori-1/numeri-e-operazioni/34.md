What can be concluded about the greatest common divisor of two consecutive positive integers?

- It is always $2$
- It is always $1$
- It is always the smaller number
- It is always greater than $1$ when both numbers exceed $10$

## Explanation

If $d$ divides both $n$ and $n+1$, it also divides their difference $(n+1)-n=1$. The only positive divisor of $1$ is $1$ itself, so there can be no greater common divisor. For example, if an integer divides both $35$ and $36$, it must also divide $36-35=1$. The same subtraction works for every pair of consecutive integers.
