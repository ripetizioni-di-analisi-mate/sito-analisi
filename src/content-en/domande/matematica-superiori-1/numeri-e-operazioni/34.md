What is the greatest common divisor of $36$ and $54$?

- $6$
- $18$
- $36$
- $108$

## Explanation

Write the numbers as $36=3\cdot 6\cdot2$ and $54=3\cdot 6\cdot3$. The remaining factors, two and three, are relatively prime. The greatest common divisor is therefore $18$.

$$
\gcd(36,54)=18
$$

The result is $18$.
