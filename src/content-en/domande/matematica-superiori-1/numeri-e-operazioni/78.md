Calculate $\dfrac1{5}-\dfrac1{6}$.

- $-1$
- $\dfrac{1}{30}$
- $\dfrac{2}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

A common denominator is $30$. The numerators become $6$ and $5$, respectively. Their difference is one.

$$
\frac1{5}-\frac1{6}=\frac{6-5}{30}=\frac{1}{30}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{30}$.
