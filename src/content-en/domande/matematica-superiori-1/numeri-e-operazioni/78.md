Before calculating the exact value of $x=\dfrac58-\dfrac23$, what can we say about it?

- $x<-\dfrac1{12}$
- $-\dfrac1{12}<x<0$
- $0<x<\dfrac1{12}$
- $x>\dfrac1{12}$

## Explanation

The order $\dfrac7{12}<\dfrac58<\dfrac23$ follows by writing the fractions as $\dfrac{14}{24}$, $\dfrac{15}{24}$ and $\dfrac{16}{24}$. Subtracting $\dfrac23$ from all three terms makes the first endpoint $-\dfrac1{12}$ and the last one zero. The desired result is negative but closer to zero than $-\dfrac1{12}$. Finishing the exact calculation gives $-\dfrac1{24}$, which is inside the predicted interval.
