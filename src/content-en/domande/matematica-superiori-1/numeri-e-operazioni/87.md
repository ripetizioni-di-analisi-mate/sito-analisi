Which integer makes the equation $-4+z=5$ true?

- $1$
- $-9$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

Add $4$ to both sides of the equation. Only $z$ remains on the left. This gives the number that produces the required value when added to the initial negative value.

$$
z=5+4=9
$$

The result is $9$.
