An integer leaves remainder $2$ on division by $5$. What remainder does its triple leave on division by $5$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Write the integer as $5k+2$. Its triple is $15k+6=5(3k+1)+1$, and a remainder must be smaller than $5$.
