What is the distance on the number line between $-4$ and $9$?

- $13$
- $5$
- $-13$
- $9$

## Explanation

The distance is the absolute value of the difference between the coordinates. The positive and negative points lie on opposite sides of the origin. Therefore their distances from the origin are added.

$$
|9-(-4)|=13
$$

The result is $13$.
