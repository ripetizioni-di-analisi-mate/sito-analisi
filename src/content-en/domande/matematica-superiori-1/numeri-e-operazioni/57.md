The points $A=-4$, $B=2$ and $C=5$ lie on one line. Which distance equality verifies that $B$ is between the other two?

- $AB+BC=AC$
- $AB+AC=BC$
- $AC+BC=AB$
- $AB=BC$

## Explanation

It takes $6$ units to reach $B$ from $A$ and another $3$ to reach $C$. Their sum is $9$, the direct distance from $A$ to $C$.
