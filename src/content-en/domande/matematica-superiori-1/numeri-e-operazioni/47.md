In the expansion of $0.\overline{572}$, what is the hundredth digit after the decimal point?

- $2$
- $0$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The repeating block has three digits. After $99$ digits exactly $33$ blocks are complete, so digit $100$ starts the next block.
