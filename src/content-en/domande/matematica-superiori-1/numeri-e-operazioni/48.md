Where does the repeating bar go in the exact decimal representation of $\dfrac7{12}$?

- $0.\overline{583}$
- $0.5\overline{83}$
- $0.583$
- $0.58\overline3$

## Explanation

Dividing $70$ by $12$ gives digit $5$ and remainder $10$. The next step uses $100$ and gives $8$ with remainder $4$. Then $40$ gives $3$ and remainder $4$ again. Only at this last step does the same remainder recur, so only $3$ repeats. The digits $58$ come before the repeating block.
