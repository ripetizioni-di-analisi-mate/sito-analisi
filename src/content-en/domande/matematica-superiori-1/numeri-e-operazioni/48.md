What fraction equals $0.\overline{4}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{99}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

Use a letter to denote the repeating decimal and multiply it by ten. Subtracting the original number eliminates the repeating part. The difference between the left-hand sides equals nine times the required number.

$$
z=0.\overline{4},\quad10z-z=4,\quad z=\frac{4}{9}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{4}{9}$.
