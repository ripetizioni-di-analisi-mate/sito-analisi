Which is the smallest of $\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$ and $\mathbb Q$ that contains both $\dfrac{-18}{-6}$ and $\dfrac{21}{-7}$?

- $\mathbb N$
- $\mathbb Q$
- None of the three
- $\mathbb Z$

## Explanation

Both divisions are exact. The first quotient is $3$ because its numerator and denominator are both negative. The second is $-3$ because the signs are opposite. Both are integers, but $-3$ rules out the natural numbers. The rational numbers also contain both values, since any integer can be written with denominator $1$.
