Calculate $\dfrac{4}{9}+\dfrac{1}{3}$.

- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{7}{18}$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $9$. The second fraction becomes $\dfrac{3}{9}$. Add the numerators and keep the common denominator.

$$
\frac{4}{9}+\frac13=\frac{4+3}{9}=\frac{7}{9}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{7}{9}$.
