There are $8$ identical packs with $56$ labels in total, $24$ of which are green. How many labels that are not green are in each pack?

- $4$
- $3$
- $7$
- $32$

## Explanation

There are $56-24=32$ labels that are not green in all. Since the packs are identical, each gets $32:8=4$ of them. A second calculation starts with one pack: it contains $56:8=7$ labels in total, of which $24:8=3$ are green. The difference is $7-3=4$. The value $32$ counts all the packs together.
