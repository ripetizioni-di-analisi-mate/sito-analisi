Calculate $\dfrac1{7}-\dfrac1{8}$.

- $-1$
- $\dfrac{2}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{56}$

## Explanation

A common denominator is $56$. The numerators become $8$ and $7$, respectively. Their difference is one.

$$
\frac1{7}-\frac1{8}=\frac{8-7}{56}=\frac{1}{56}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{56}$.
