You take $\dfrac14$ of a kilogram of flour from $\dfrac56$ of a kilogram. What fraction of the **initial** amount remains?

- $\dfrac7{12}$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac7{10}$

## Explanation

The remaining flour weighs $\dfrac56-\dfrac14=\dfrac7{12}$ of a kilogram. The question asks what part of the original amount that represents, so divide by $\dfrac56$. This gives $\dfrac7{12}\cdot\dfrac65=\dfrac7{10}$. A check starts with the part taken, which is $\dfrac14:\dfrac56=\dfrac3{10}$ of the original. The two fractions of the initial amount add to $1$.
