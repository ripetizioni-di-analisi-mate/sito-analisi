A bottle contains $\dfrac{7}3$ liters. If the same amount is added, how many liters does it contain?

- $\dfrac{7}{6}\,\mathrm{L}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{L}$
- $\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{L}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

Add the two quantities, which use the same unit. The denominators are equal, so only the numerators are added. The total is twice the initial quantity.

$$
\frac{7}3+\frac{7}3=\frac{14}3
$$

The result is $\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{L}$.
