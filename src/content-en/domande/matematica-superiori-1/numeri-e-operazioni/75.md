A log uses decimal hours. How much longer is an activity of $1$ hour and $30$ minutes than one of $1.30$ hours?

- $0\,\mathrm{min}$
- $18\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12\,\mathrm{min}$
- $30\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

In $1.30$ hours, the decimal part is $\dfrac{30}{100}$ of an hour. Since an hour contains $60$ minutes, that part is $\dfrac{30}{100}\cdot60=18$ minutes. The durations are therefore $90$ and $78$ minutes, a difference of $12$ minutes. As a check, $30$ minutes is half an hour, so the first duration can also be written as $1.50$ hours.
