You want to mark the midpoint between $\dfrac{11}{12}$ and $1$. What is the distance from either endpoint to that point?

- $\dfrac{11}{24}$
- $\dfrac{23}{24}$
- $\dfrac1{24}$
- $\dfrac1{12}$

## Explanation

The interval has length $1-\dfrac{11}{12}=\dfrac1{12}$. Halving it gives the step $\dfrac1{24}$, and the point has coordinate $\dfrac{23}{24}$.
