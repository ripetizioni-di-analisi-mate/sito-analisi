Which expression differs from the other three because of the grouping?

- $-3^2$
- $(-1)\cdot3^2$
- $(-3)^2$
- $-(3^2)$

## Explanation

In $(-3)^2$ the base of the power is $-3$, so its square is $9$. In the others the square of $3$ is taken first and then negated.
