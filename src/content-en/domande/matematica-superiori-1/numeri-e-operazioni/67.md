Calculate $(-4)\cdot(5)$.

- $20$
- $-9$
- $-20$
- $-16$

## Explanation

The factors have opposite signs, so their product is negative. Multiplying their two positive magnitudes gives the absolute value. Apply the sign after finding that magnitude.

$$
(-4)\cdot5=-(4\cdot5)=-20
$$

The result is $-20$.
