Which of the following numbers becomes $2.4$ when rounded to the nearest tenth?

- $2.49$
- $2.36$
- $2.31$
- $2.46$

## Explanation

To round to the nearest tenth, one looks at the hundredths digit. If this digit is $5$ or greater, the tenths digit increases by one; otherwise it stays the same. In $2.36$ the hundredths digit is $6$, so $2.36$ rounds to $2.4$. The number $2.31$ instead rounds to $2.3$, while $2.46$ and $2.49$ round to $2.5$.
