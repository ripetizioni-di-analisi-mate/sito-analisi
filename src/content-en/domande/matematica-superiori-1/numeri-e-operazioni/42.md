A display rounds to the nearest tenth. Which unrounded reading could have produced $2.4$?

- $2.49$
- $2.36$
- $2.31$
- $2.46$

## Explanation

The hundredths digit of $2.36$ rounds the tenths digit up by one. The number $2.31$ rounds to $2.3$, while $2.46$ and $2.49$ round to $2.5$.
