What fraction in lowest terms represents the decimal $0.5$?

- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$

## Explanation

A terminating decimal represents a fraction whose denominator is a power of ten. The numerator and denominator can then be divided by a common factor. Dividing the reduced numerator by its denominator gives the original decimal.

$$
0.5=\frac{1}{2}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
