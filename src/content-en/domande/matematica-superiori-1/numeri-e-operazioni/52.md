Which number lies exactly halfway between $\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $1$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$

## Explanation

The midpoint of two numbers is their arithmetic mean. Add the two values using a common denominator. Then divide the sum by two.

$$
\frac12\left(\frac{4}{5}+1\right)=\frac{9}{10}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{9}{10}$.
