Two cards show $\dfrac25$ and $\dfrac37$. Add the numerators together and the denominators together to make a new fraction $m$. Where does $m$ lie on the number line?

- $m<\dfrac25<\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac25<\dfrac37<m$
- $m=\dfrac25+\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac25<m<\dfrac37$

## Explanation

The new fraction is $m=\dfrac5{12}$. Cross multiplication gives $2\cdot12<5\cdot5$ and $5\cdot7<3\cdot12$, placing it strictly between the starting fractions.
