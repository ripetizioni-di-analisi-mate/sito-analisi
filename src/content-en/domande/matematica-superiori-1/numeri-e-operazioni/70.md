Two integers $a$ and $b$ satisfy $a<0<b$ and $|a|<b$. Which conclusion always holds?

- $a+b<0$
- $0<a+b<b$
- $a+b=b$
- $a+b>b$

## Explanation

Since $a$ is negative, adding it to $b$ amounts to subtracting $|a|$ from $b$. We subtract a positive amount smaller than $b$, so the result is positive and less than $b$. For instance, with $a=-4$ and $b=7$, we get $7-4=3$. Comparing the distances from zero works even without knowing the two numbers.
