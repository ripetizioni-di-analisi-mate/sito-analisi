The sum $\dfrac12+\dfrac16+\dfrac1{12}+\dfrac1{20}$ can be found without choosing a common denominator by writing each term as the difference of two successive unit fractions. Which difference remains?

- $1-\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12-\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac12-\dfrac1{20}$
- $1-\dfrac15$

## Explanation

The terms are $1-\dfrac12$, $\dfrac12-\dfrac13$, $\dfrac13-\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac14-\dfrac15$. Each intermediate fraction occurs with both signs and cancels.
