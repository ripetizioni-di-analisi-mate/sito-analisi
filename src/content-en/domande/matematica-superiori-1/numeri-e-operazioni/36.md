Which expression without brackets is equal to $-4-(9-12)+2$?

- $-4-9-12+2$
- $-4+9+12+2$
- $-4-9+12-2$
- $-4-9+12+2$

## Explanation

Subtracting the difference $9-12$ is the same as adding its opposite, and by the distributive property the factor $-1$ changes the sign of each of its two terms. So $9$ becomes $-9$ and $-12$ becomes $+12$. The addend $+2$ is not part of the subtracted difference and keeps its sign. This gives $-4-9+12+2$, which equals $1$ like the original expression, since $-4-(-3)+2=1$.

The required expression is therefore $-4-9+12+2$.
