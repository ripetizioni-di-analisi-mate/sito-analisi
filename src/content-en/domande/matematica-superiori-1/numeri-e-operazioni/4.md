Two equal strips are divided into $33$ and $3$ pieces respectively. To add $\dfrac{12}{33}+\dfrac13$, how many small pieces represent the second quantity?

- $3$
- $12$
- $33$
- $11$

## Explanation

One third covers $33:3=11$ of the small pieces. The sum therefore covers $12+11=23$ thirty-thirds.
