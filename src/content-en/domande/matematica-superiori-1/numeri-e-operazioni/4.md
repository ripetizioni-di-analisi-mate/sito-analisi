On the number line, $P$ has coordinate $-7$. Point $Q$ is the same distance from zero as $P$ and has a greater coordinate than $P$. Where is $Q$?

- $-7$
- $0$
- $14$
- $7$

## Explanation

The distance from $P$ to the origin is $|-7|=7$. The only points seven units from zero are $-7$ on the left and $7$ on the right. The requirement for a greater coordinate rules out $P$ itself and selects $7$. The two coordinates have opposite signs, and the distance between the points is $7-(-7)=14$.
