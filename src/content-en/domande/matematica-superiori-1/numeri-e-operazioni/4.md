Calculate $-6-4+12$.

- $-22$
- $14$
- $10$
- $2$

## Explanation

First add the two negative terms to obtain $-10$. The positive term is $12$. Their sum is therefore $2$.

$$
-6-4+12=-10+12=2
$$

The result is $2$.
