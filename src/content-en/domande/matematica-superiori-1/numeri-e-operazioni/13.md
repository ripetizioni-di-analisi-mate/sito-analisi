There are $44$ red pens and $33$ blue ones. Each case holds $4$ red and $3$ blue pens. After two full cases are given away, which calculation counts all the remaining pens?

- $(44+33)-2(4+3)$
- $(44+33)-(4+3)-2$
- $(44-2)+(33-2)$
- $(44+33):2-(4+3)$

## Explanation

One case holds seven pens, so giving away two takes $14$ pens out of the starting $77$. That leaves $63$ pens.
