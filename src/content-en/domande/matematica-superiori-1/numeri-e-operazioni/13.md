Calculate $3\cdot 5-2\cdot(5+1)$.

- $3$
- $6$
- $78$
- $4$

## Explanation

Add the grouped numbers before multiplying by the outer factor. Perform the multiplications before the subtraction. Only the two resulting products need to be compared.

$$
3\cdot 5-2\cdot 6=15-12=3
$$

The result is $3$.
