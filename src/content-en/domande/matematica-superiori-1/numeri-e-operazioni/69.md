For which integers $z$ is the equation $0\cdot z=0$ true?

- For all integers
- Only for $z=0$
- For exactly the nonzero integers
- For no integer

## Explanation

The product of zero and any integer is zero. This follows from $(0+0)z=0z+0z$. Since $0+0=0$, subtracting $0z$ from both sides leaves $0=0z$. The equation therefore also holds when $z=0$. Because every integer satisfies it, the equation cannot assign a unique quotient to $0:0$.
