Calculate $-5-4+10$.

- $-19$
- $11$
- $1$
- $9$

## Explanation

First add the two negative terms to obtain $-9$. The positive term is $10$. Their sum is therefore $1$.

$$
-5-4+10=-9+10=1
$$

The result is $1$.
