How can you reduce $3\cdot11-2(11+1)$ to one subtraction by collecting the factor $11$?

- $(3-2)(11+1)$
- $3(11-2)-1$
- $(3-2)\cdot11-2$
- $(3-2)\cdot11-1$

## Explanation

Distributing $-2$ gives $-2\cdot11$ and $-2$. Only the first combines with $3\cdot11$, leaving $11-2=9$.
