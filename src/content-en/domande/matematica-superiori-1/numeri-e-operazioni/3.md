Which calculation shows that dividing two integers can give a rational number that is not an integer, even when the division is defined?

- $20:(-5)$
- $0:9$
- $7:4$
- $12:0$

## Explanation

The quotient $7:4$ is $\dfrac74$. This is rational because its numerator and denominator are integers and its denominator is nonzero. Since $7=4\cdot1+3$, the quotient is not an integer. The divisions $20:(-5)$ and $0:9$ are also defined but equal $-4$ and $0$ respectively. Finally, $12:0$ is undefined because no number times zero gives $12$.
