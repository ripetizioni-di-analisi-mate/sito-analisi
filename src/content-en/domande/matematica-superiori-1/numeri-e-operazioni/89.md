Given that $a-b=-7$, what is the value of $(b+5)-(a+5)$?

- $7$
- $-7$
- $12$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Removing the parentheses gives $b+5-a-5=b-a$. The two terms of $5$ cancel because the same amount is added to both quantities in the subtraction. Reversing the order of the subtraction changes the sign, so $b-a=-(a-b)=-(-7)=7$. Check with $a=1$ and $b=8$, which satisfy the premise: the new difference is $13-6=7$.
