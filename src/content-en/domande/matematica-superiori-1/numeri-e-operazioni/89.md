Which integer makes the equation $-6+z=7$ true?

- $13$
- $1$
- $-13$
- $11$

## Explanation

Add $6$ to both sides of the equation. Only $z$ remains on the left. This gives the number that produces the required value when added to the initial negative value.

$$
z=7+6=13
$$

The result is $13$.
