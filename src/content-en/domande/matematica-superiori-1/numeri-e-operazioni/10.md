What is the value of $18:3\cdot(2+1)$?

- $2$
- $18$
- $13$
- $54$

## Explanation

The grouped sum equals $3$. Division and multiplication remain and have the same priority. Working from left to right gives $18:3=6$ and then $6\cdot3=18$.
