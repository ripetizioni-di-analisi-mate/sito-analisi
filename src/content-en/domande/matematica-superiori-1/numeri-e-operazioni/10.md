Let $u=0.\overline2$. Which subtraction cancels the entire repeating tail and lets you write $u$ as a fraction?

- $u-\dfrac{u}{10}=2$
- $10u-u=2$
- $10u-u=20$
- $100u-10u=2$

## Explanation

Multiplying by ten gives $2.\overline2$. The decimal parts match, so $9u=2$ and $u=\dfrac29$.
