On the number line, a point moves from $-5$ to $3$ and then back to $-2$. What are the total distance travelled and the net change from the starting point?

- Distance $3$, net change $+13$
- Distance $13$, net change $-3$
- Distance $13$, net change $+3$
- Distance $8$, net change $+3$

## Explanation

The first leg has length $|3-(-5)|=8$, and the second has length $|-2-3|=5$. The total distance is their sum, $13$. The net change is measured from the starting point to the end and equals $-2-(-5)=+3$. The positive sign means the final point lies to the right of the start even after the move back to the left.
