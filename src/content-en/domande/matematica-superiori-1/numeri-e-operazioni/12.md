Two probes are at elevations $-11$ m and $+16$ m. Which measurement stays the same if the first probe becomes the new zero level?

- The lower elevation of $-11$ m
- The upper elevation of $16$ m
- The sum of the elevations, $5$ m
- The vertical distance of $27$ m

## Explanation

Adding $11$ to both elevations gives $0$ and $27$. Their difference stays $27$ because the translation moves both points by the same amount.
