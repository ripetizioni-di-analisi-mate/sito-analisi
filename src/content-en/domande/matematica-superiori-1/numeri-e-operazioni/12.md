In which expression do the brackets make the **entire** difference between $18$ and $6$ get divided by $3$?

- $18-(6:3)$
- $(18:3)-6$
- $(18+6):3$
- $(18-6):3$

## Explanation

To divide the entire difference, first find $18-6=12$ and then divide that value by $3$ to get $4$. In $18-(6:3)$, division acts on one term only and gives $18-2=16$. The expression $(18+6):3$ divides the sum, giving $24:3=8$.
