A bottle contains $\dfrac34$ of a litre. Each glass takes $\dfrac18$ of a litre. How many glasses can be filled?

- $6$
- $4$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

Find how many times $\dfrac18$ fits into $\dfrac34$. Three quarters equal $\dfrac68$, so six portions of one eighth of a litre can be poured.
