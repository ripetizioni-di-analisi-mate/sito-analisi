Which fraction represents the number $0.1\overline2$?

- $\dfrac{11}{90}$
- $\dfrac{12}{99}$
- $\dfrac3{25}$
- $\dfrac29$

## Explanation

For $z=0.1\overline2$, we have $100z=12.\overline2$ and $10z=1.\overline2$. Subtracting the equations removes the repeating parts. This gives $90z=11$, so $z=\dfrac{11}{90}$.
