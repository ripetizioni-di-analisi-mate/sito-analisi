Calculate $\dfrac{4}{3}:\dfrac23$.

- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{9}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The divisor is a nonzero fraction. Dividing by two thirds is equivalent to multiplying by three halves. Multiply and reduce the resulting fraction.

$$
\frac{4}{3}:\frac23=\frac{4}{3}\cdot\frac32=2
$$

The result is $2$.
