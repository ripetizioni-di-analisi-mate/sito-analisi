Which expression places $-\dfrac34$, $-0.7$, and $-\dfrac23$ in increasing order?

- $-\dfrac23<-0.7<-\dfrac34$
- $-0.7<-\dfrac34<-\dfrac23$
- $-\dfrac34<-0.7<-\dfrac23$
- $-\dfrac34<-\dfrac23<-0.7$

## Explanation

In sixtieths, the three values are $-\dfrac{45}{60}$, $-\dfrac{42}{60}$, and $-\dfrac{40}{60}$, respectively. Their common denominator is positive, so it is enough to compare $-45$, $-42$, and $-40$.
