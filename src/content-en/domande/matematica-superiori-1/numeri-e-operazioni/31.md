What is the greatest common divisor of $18$ and $27$?

- $3$
- $18$
- $9$
- $54$

## Explanation

Write the numbers as $18=3\cdot 3\cdot2$ and $27=3\cdot 3\cdot3$. The remaining factors, two and three, are relatively prime. The greatest common divisor is therefore $9$.

$$
\gcd(18,27)=9
$$

The result is $9$.
