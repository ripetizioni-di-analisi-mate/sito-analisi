The temperature at $7$ a.m. is $-6\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. It rises by $9\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and then falls by $5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is the final temperature?

- $-10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $8\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

First add $9$ to the initial temperature to obtain $-6+9=3$. The next change subtracts $5$ from the updated value. The thermometer therefore reads $3-5=-2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
