Which integer makes the equation $-3+z=4$ true?

- $1$
- $7$
- $-7$
- $5$

## Explanation

Add $3$ to both sides of the equation. Only $z$ remains on the left. This gives the number that produces the required value when added to the initial negative value.

$$
z=4+3=7
$$

The result is $7$.
