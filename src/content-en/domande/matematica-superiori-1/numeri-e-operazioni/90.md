Which integer makes the equation $-7+z=8$ true?

- $1$
- $15$
- $-15$
- $13$

## Explanation

Add $7$ to both sides of the equation. Only $z$ remains on the left. This gives the number that produces the required value when added to the initial negative value.

$$
z=8+7=15
$$

The result is $15$.
