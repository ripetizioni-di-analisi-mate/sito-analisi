How does $0.\overline9$ compare with $1$?

- $0.\overline9<1$
- $0.\overline9=1$
- $0.\overline9>1$
- It depends on how many digits $9$ are written

## Explanation

Let $x=0.\overline9$. Then $10x=9.\overline9$. The repeating tails are identical, so $10x-x=9$ and $x=\dfrac99=1$. A decimal such as $0.999$ has only three digits $9$ and equals $\dfrac{999}{1000}<1$. The bar indicates that $9$ repeats indefinitely. The calculation with $10x-x$ uses the full repeating expansion.
