Light A flashes every $6$ seconds starting at $t=0$. Light B flashes every $10$ seconds starting at $t=4$. At what time do they first flash together?

- $24\,\mathrm{s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Light B flashes at $4$, $14$, $24$ seconds and so on. The first of these that is also a multiple of $6$ is $24=4\cdot6$. Light B reaches that time because $24=4+2\cdot10$. The signals start at different times, so the least common multiple of their periods does not directly give their first meeting.
