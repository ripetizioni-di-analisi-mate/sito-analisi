Two signals repeat every $16$ and $24$ seconds, respectively, and start together. How many seconds pass before they next occur together?

- $48\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $96\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The required time must be a multiple of both periods. The first positive time with this property is the least common multiple. The periods are four and six times the same quantity, so twelve times that quantity is enough.

$$
\operatorname{lcm}(16,24)=48
$$

The result is $48\,\mathrm{s}$.
