You need a two-digit number divisible by $9$ and $5$, with an odd tens digit. Which number fits all three conditions?

- $75$
- $95$
- $90$
- $45$

## Explanation

The two-digit multiples of $45$ are $45$ and $90$, since $5$ and $9$ are coprime. Only $90$ has an odd tens digit.
