A sum of two positive fractions exceeds $1$, even though both addends are less than $1$. Which pair demonstrates this?

- $\dfrac38+\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13+\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac15+\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac58+\dfrac12$

## Explanation

One half is four eighths, so $\dfrac58+\dfrac12$ is $\dfrac98$. The parts together cover more than one whole even though each is only a fraction of it.
