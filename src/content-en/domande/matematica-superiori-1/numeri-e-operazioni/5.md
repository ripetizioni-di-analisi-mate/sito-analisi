Leo gets one third of a portion equal to $\dfrac9{22}$ of a cake. What fraction of the whole cake does he get?

- $\dfrac3{22}$
- $\dfrac9{19}$
- $\dfrac{22}{27}$
- $\dfrac{10}{25}$

## Explanation

The portion contains nine twenty-seconds. Dividing it into three equal groups gives each group three twenty-seconds.
