For which pair is the least common multiple equal to the product of the two numbers?

- $(14,21)$
- $(14,15)$
- $(15,25)$
- $(12,18)$

## Explanation

The numbers $14=2\cdot7$ and $15=3\cdot5$ share no prime factor. Their least common multiple must include all four factors and equals $210=14\cdot15$. Each remaining pair shares at least one prime factor. The product would count that factor in both numbers, while the least common multiple uses only the larger of its two exponents.
