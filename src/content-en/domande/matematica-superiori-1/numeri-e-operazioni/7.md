To find the GCD of $66$ and $99$, which smaller pair has precisely the same common divisors?

- $(33,11)$
- $(9,6)$
- $(66,33)$
- $(66,3)$

## Explanation

Each common divisor also divides $99-66=33$. Conversely, a divisor of $66$ and $33$ divides their sum, $99$. The greatest one is $33$.
