Which expression results when you remove the brackets in $-4-(9-12)+2$?

- $-4-9-12+2$
- $-4+9+12+2$
- $-4-9+12+2$
- $-4-9+12-2$

## Explanation

The sign before the brackets applies a factor of $-1$ to both terms inside. Thus $9$ becomes $-9$ and $-12$ becomes $+12$. The $+2$ is outside the brackets and keeps its sign. To check, evaluate $9-12=-3$ first, then $-4-(-3)+2=1$, which agrees with the expression without brackets.
