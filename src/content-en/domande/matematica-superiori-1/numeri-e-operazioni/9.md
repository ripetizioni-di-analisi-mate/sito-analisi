Without evaluating the entire product, how does $(-2)\cdot(-5)\cdot(-8)\cdot\dfrac12$ compare with $-8$?

- It is less than $-8$
- It is greater than $-8$ but negative
- It equals $-8$
- It is positive

## Explanation

By rearranging and regrouping, we can combine $-2$, $-5$ and $\dfrac12$ into one factor. The first two negatives give a positive, and $2\cdot\dfrac12=1$, so the combined factor is $5$. Multiplying $-8$ by $5$ preserves its sign and increases its distance from zero. The result therefore lies to the left of $-8$.
