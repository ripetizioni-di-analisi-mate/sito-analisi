A scale reads $1.375$ kg. If the unit is changed to one eighth of a kilogram, which whole number gives the same mass?

- $11$
- $8$
- $13$
- $14$

## Explanation

One kilogram is eight eighths and $0.375=\dfrac38$. The reading therefore represents eleven eighths of a kilogram.
