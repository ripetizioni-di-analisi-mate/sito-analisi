How many fractions of the form $\dfrac n7$, with integer $n$, are strictly between $4$ and $5$?

- $7$
- $8$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The endpoints can be written as $4=\dfrac{28}7$ and $5=\dfrac{35}7$. With the same positive denominator, only the numerators need to be compared. The allowed ones are $29$, $30$, $31$, $32$, $33$ and $34$: six in all. The step between successive fractions is one seventh. The interval has seven such steps, but the endpoints do not count. Only interior points count.
