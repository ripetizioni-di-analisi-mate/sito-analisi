Two lights flash together and then repeat every $44$ and $66$ seconds. At the next shared flash, how many periods has the faster light completed?

- $2$
- $6$
- $22$
- $3$

## Explanation

The first common positive time is $132$ seconds. The faster light completes $132:44=3$ periods while the other completes two.
