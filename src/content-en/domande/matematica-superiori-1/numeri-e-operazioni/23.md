What should you multiply $-\dfrac34$ by to get $1$?

- $\dfrac43$
- $-\dfrac34$
- $-\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The product must be positive, so the second factor must be negative like the first. To get absolute value $1$, the numerators and denominators in the product must cancel. Using $-\dfrac43$ gives $\dfrac{(-3)(-4)}{4\cdot3}=\dfrac{12}{12}=1$. The opposite $\dfrac34$ would cancel the value in a sum, whereas this product calls for the reciprocal.
