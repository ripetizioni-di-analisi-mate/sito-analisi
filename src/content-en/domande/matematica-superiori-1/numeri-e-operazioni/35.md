Use all $42$ pencils and $56$ markers to make identical packs. What is the greatest number of packs that can be made?

- $7$
- $28$
- $14$
- $98$

## Explanation

The number of packs must divide both $42$ and $56$. Their prime factorisations are $42=2\cdot3\cdot7$ and $56=2^3\cdot7$, so the greatest common divisor is $14$. With this choice, each pack contains three pencils and four markers.
