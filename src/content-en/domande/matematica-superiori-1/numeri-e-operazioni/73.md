A $\dfrac32$-litre container is $\dfrac13$ full. A $\dfrac34$-litre container is $\dfrac23$ full. How do the amounts of water compare?

- The containers hold the same amount of water
- The second holds twice as much as the first
- The first holds twice as much as the second
- The second holds $\dfrac13$ of a litre more

## Explanation

The fractions $\dfrac13$ and $\dfrac23$ refer to different capacities. To find the amounts in litres, multiply each fraction by its container’s capacity. The first holds $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac32=\dfrac12$ a litre and the second holds $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac12$ a litre. The second fraction is twice the first, but it applies to half the capacity.
