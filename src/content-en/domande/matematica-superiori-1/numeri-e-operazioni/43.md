All of these fractions have a denominator containing a prime other than $2$ or $5$. Which one still represents a terminating decimal?

- $\dfrac{14}{90}$
- $\dfrac{18}{84}$
- $\dfrac{21}{84}$
- $\dfrac{24}{70}$

## Explanation

The criterion applies to a fraction in lowest terms. Dividing by $21$ gives $\dfrac{21}{84}=\dfrac14=0.25$, since the reduced denominator is $2^2$. The other fractions reduce to $\dfrac7{45}$, $\dfrac3{14}$ and $\dfrac{12}{35}$ respectively. In the first of those denominators a factor of $3$ remains, and in the others a factor of $7$ remains. All three decimals repeat.
