How many digits are necessary after the decimal point to write $\dfrac{12}{160}$ as a terminating decimal?

- $2$
- $4$
- No finite number of digits is enough
- $3$

## Explanation

Dividing the numerator and denominator by $4$ gives $\dfrac3{40}$. The denominator $40=2^3\cdot5$ divides $1000$ but not $100$, so three decimal places are needed. Multiplying both terms by $25$ gives $\dfrac{75}{1000}=0.075$. The zero in the tenths place is necessary: $0.75$ would give seventy-five hundredths, a number ten times as large.
