Calculate $\dfrac1{3}-\dfrac1{4}$.

- $-1$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

A common denominator is $12$. The numerators become $4$ and $3$, respectively. Their difference is one.

$$
\frac1{3}-\frac1{4}=\frac{4-3}{12}=\frac{1}{12}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{12}$.
