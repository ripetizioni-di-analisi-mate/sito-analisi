To check the result $q$ of $\dfrac{12}{11}:\dfrac23$, which equality must hold?

- $q-\dfrac23=\dfrac{12}{11}$
- $q\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac{12}{11}$
- $q\cdot\dfrac{12}{11}=\dfrac23$
- $q+\dfrac23=\dfrac{12}{11}$

## Explanation

The quotient times the divisor gives the dividend. This check gives $q=\dfrac{12}{11}\cdot\dfrac32=\dfrac{18}{11}$.
