Calculate $3-(-9+3)$.

- $-3$
- $9$
- $15$
- $-9$

## Explanation

Subtracting a sum is equivalent to adding the opposites of its terms. Both signs in the subtracted expression therefore change. Finally, combine the numerical terms.

$$
3-(-9+3)=3+9-3=9
$$

The result is $9$.
