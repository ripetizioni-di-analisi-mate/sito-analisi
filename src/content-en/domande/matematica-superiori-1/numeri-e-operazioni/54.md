In which pair of coordinates, one negative and one positive, is the midpoint to the left of zero?

- $\left(-\dfrac54,\dfrac32\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{11}6,\dfrac53\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac34,\dfrac34\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac23,1\right)$

## Explanation

The midpoint has the same sign as the sum of the coordinates because that sum is divided by $2$. For the correct pair, $-\dfrac{11}6+\dfrac53=-\dfrac16$, so the midpoint is $-\dfrac1{12}$. This is the only pair whose negative endpoint is farther from zero than its positive endpoint. In the symmetric pair the sum is zero.
