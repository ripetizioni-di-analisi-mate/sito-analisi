Which number lies exactly halfway between $\dfrac{6}{7}$ and $1$?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{13}{14}$
- $\dfrac{13}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{13}$

## Explanation

The midpoint of two numbers is their arithmetic mean. Add the two values using a common denominator. Then divide the sum by two.

$$
\frac12\left(\frac{6}{7}+1\right)=\frac{13}{14}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{13}{14}$.
