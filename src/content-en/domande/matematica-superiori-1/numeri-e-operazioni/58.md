What are all the coordinates of points $7$ units away from the point at coordinate $4$?

- Only $11$
- $-3$ and $11$
- $3$ and $11$
- $-7$ and $7$

## Explanation

Starting from $4$, we can move seven units in either direction. To the left we reach $4-7=-3$, and to the right we reach $4+7=11$. The distances are $|(-3)-4|=7$ and $|11-4|=7$. The value $3$ cannot replace $-3$: it is only one unit from the starting point.
