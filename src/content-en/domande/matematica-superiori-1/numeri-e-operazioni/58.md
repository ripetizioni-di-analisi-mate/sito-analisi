A reflection swaps the points $-2$ and $6$. Which coordinate is fixed by that reflection?

- $0$
- $2$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The reflection centre is equally far from the two points. They are $8$ units apart, and four steps from $-2$ towards $6$ reach $2$.
