Which number lies exactly halfway between $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $1$?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

The midpoint of two numbers is their arithmetic mean. Add the two values using a common denominator. Then divide the sum by two.

$$
\frac12\left(\frac{3}{4}+1\right)=\frac{7}{8}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{7}{8}$.
