Calculate $-3-4+6$.

- $-1$
- $-13$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

First add the two negative terms to obtain $-7$. The positive term is $6$. Their sum is therefore $-1$.

$$
-3-4+6=-7+6=-1
$$

The result is $-1$.
