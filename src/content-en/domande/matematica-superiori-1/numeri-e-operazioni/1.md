On the number line, start at $-11$, subtract $4$ and add $22$. On which move and after how many units do you cross zero?

- On the second move, after $15$ units
- On the first move, after $4$ units
- On the second move, after $11$ units
- Upon arriving at $7$

## Explanation

The first move ends at $-15$. Of the $22$ steps to the right, the first $15$ take you back to zero and the remaining $7$ take you onto the positive half-line.
