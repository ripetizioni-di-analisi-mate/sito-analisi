What is the greatest common divisor of $30$ and $45$?

- $15$
- $5$
- $30$
- $90$

## Explanation

Write the numbers as $30=3\cdot 5\cdot2$ and $45=3\cdot 5\cdot3$. The remaining factors, two and three, are relatively prime. The greatest common divisor is therefore $15$.

$$
\gcd(30,45)=15
$$

The result is $15$.
