Let $a=2^2\cdot3^3$ and $b=2^3\cdot3\cdot5$. Which number listed divides both $a$ and $b$?

- $12$
- $24$
- $18$
- $10$

## Explanation

To divide both numbers, a prime factor must appear in both. Its exponent can be no larger than the smaller available exponent. The number $12=2^2\cdot3$ meets both limits. The number $24$ needs three factors of $2$, $18$ needs two factors of $3$, and $10$ needs a factor of $5$. In each case at least one of the two numbers lacks the required factors.
