Write $-\dfrac94=n+r$, with integer $n$ and $0\le r<1$. What are $n$ and $r$?

- $n=-3,\quad r=\dfrac34$
- $n=-2,\quad r=-\dfrac14$
- $n=-2,\quad r=\dfrac14$
- $n=-3,\quad r=\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The number $-\dfrac94$ lies between $-3=-\dfrac{12}4$ and $-2=-\dfrac84$. To add a nonnegative part smaller than one, start at the integer to the left, namely $-3$. The distance from there is $-\dfrac94+\dfrac{12}4=\dfrac34$. Starting at $-2$ would require subtracting one quarter. This reaches the given number, but the remainder $r$ would be negative and violate $0\le r$.
