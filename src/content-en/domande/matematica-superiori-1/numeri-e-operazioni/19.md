Which integer $n$ makes $\dfrac n{21}$ equivalent to $-\dfrac47$?

- $-4$
- $-7$
- $-12$
- $12$

## Explanation

The fraction on the right has denominator $7$. To reach denominator $21$, multiply by $3$. Preserving the value also requires multiplying numerator $-4$ by $3$, giving $-12$. The value remains negative. A check uses cross-products: $(-12)\cdot7=-84$ and $(-4)\cdot21=-84$, so the fractions are equal.
