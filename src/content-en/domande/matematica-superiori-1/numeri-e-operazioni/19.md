Calculate $\dfrac{7}{18}+\dfrac{1}{3}$.

- $\dfrac{8}{21}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{13}{18}$
- $\dfrac{13}{36}$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $18$. The second fraction becomes $\dfrac{6}{18}$. Add the numerators and keep the common denominator.

$$
\frac{7}{18}+\frac13=\frac{7+6}{18}=\frac{13}{18}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{13}{18}$.
