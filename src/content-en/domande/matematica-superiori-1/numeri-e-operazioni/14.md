Which step uses the associative property of addition while keeping the three terms in the same order?

- $(2+5)+8=(5+2)+8$
- $(2+5)+8=2+(5+8)$
- $(2+5)+8=2+(8+5)$
- $(2+5)+8=(8+5)+2$

## Explanation

The associative property moves the parentheses and leaves the sequence $2$, $5$, $8$ in place. The left side gives $7+8=15$, and the right side gives $2+13=15$. The other equations also give $15$, but they change the order of the terms. Justifying those swaps requires the commutative property, with regrouping where needed.
