You know that $(-11)\cdot11=-121$. Using the distributive law, what do you do to $-121$ to obtain $(-11)\cdot12$?

- Reverse its sign
- Add $-11$
- Add $11$
- Multiply by $-11$

## Explanation

The second factor goes from $11$ to $11+1$. The new product is therefore the previous one plus one copy of $-11$, giving $-132$.
