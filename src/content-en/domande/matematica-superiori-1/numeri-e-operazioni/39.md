What is the first common multiple of $8$ and $12$ strictly greater than $50$?

- $60$
- $64$
- $72$
- $96$

## Explanation

The least common multiple is $2^3\cdot3=24$, so the positive common multiples are $24$, $48$, $72$, $96$ and so on. The number $48$ does not exceed $50$, and the next one is $72$. To check that it is a common multiple, use $72=8\cdot9=12\cdot6$. The number $96$ is also divisible by both and exceeds the threshold, but comes after $72$.
