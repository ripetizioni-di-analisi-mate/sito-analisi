Which interval contains exactly the points at distance strictly less than $2$ from $-1$?

- $(-1,2)$
- $(-2,2)$
- $(-3,1)$
- $[-3,1]$

## Explanation

Starting at $-1$, two units left gives $-3$ and two units right gives $1$. Both endpoints are exactly $2$ units away and are excluded.
