The midpoint of two numbers on the number line is $-\dfrac12$. One endpoint is $\dfrac32$. What is the other endpoint?

- $\dfrac12$
- $-2$
- $-\dfrac52$
- $-\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The sum of the endpoints is twice the midpoint, so $2\cdot\left(-\dfrac12\right)=-1$. Subtracting the known endpoint $\dfrac32$ gives $-1-\dfrac32=-\dfrac52$.
