What is $\dfrac{5}{12}+\dfrac{7}{18}$ as a fraction in lowest terms?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac{19}{36}$
- $\dfrac{29}{36}$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of $12$ and $18$ is $36$. In thirty-sixths, the terms are $\dfrac{15}{36}$ and $\dfrac{14}{36}$. Their sum is $\dfrac{29}{36}$. The numerator $29$ is relatively prime to $36$.
