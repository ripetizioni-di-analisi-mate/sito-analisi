During the night the temperature drops by $9\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$, then rises by $4\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. If it ends at $-6\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$, what was the starting temperature?

- $-11\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $1\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-5\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-1\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The overall change is $-9+4=-5$ degrees, so the final temperature is five degrees below the starting temperature. To go back to the start, add five degrees to $-6$ to get $-1$. Check by following the changes in their original order: the temperature falls from $-1$ to $-10$ and then rises to $-6$.
