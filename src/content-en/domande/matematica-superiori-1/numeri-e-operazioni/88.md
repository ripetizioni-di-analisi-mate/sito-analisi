Which integer makes the equation $-5+z=6$ true?

- $1$
- $-11$
- $9$
- $11$

## Explanation

Add $5$ to both sides of the equation. Only $z$ remains on the left. This gives the number that produces the required value when added to the initial negative value.

$$
z=6+5=11
$$

The result is $11$.
