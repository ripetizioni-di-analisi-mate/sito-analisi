Two lights flash together now. They then flash every $12$ and $15$ seconds, respectively. How many times do they flash together in the next $130$ seconds, excluding the initial flash?

- $1$
- $3$
- $10$
- $2$

## Explanation

The lights flash together at each multiple of the least common multiple of their periods. Since $\operatorname{lcm}(12,15)=60$, the positive times within $130$ seconds are $60$ and $120$. The following coincidence is at $180$ seconds.
