Pack $51$ notebooks into boxes that each hold at most $8$. What is the smallest number of boxes needed?

- $6$
- $8$
- $3$
- $7$

## Explanation

Six boxes hold $6\cdot8=48$ notebooks. The remaining three require one more box, so seven boxes are needed. Six boxes hold too few notebooks even when each is full.
