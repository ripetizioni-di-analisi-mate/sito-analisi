A recipe needs $\dfrac35$ of a kilogram of flour for $8$ portions. How much is needed for $6$ portions of the same size?

- $\dfrac35\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac9{20}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac45\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac3{40}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Six portions are $\dfrac68=\dfrac34$ of the original eight. Take $\dfrac34$ of the original amount of flour, giving $\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac9{20}$ of a kilogram. To check, divide by eight first to get $\dfrac3{40}$ of a kilogram per portion. Multiplying by six gives $\dfrac{18}{40}=\dfrac9{20}$ of a kilogram. The amount is smaller than the original, as it should be for fewer portions.
