Calculate $\dfrac1{6}-\dfrac1{7}$.

- $-1$
- $\dfrac{2}{13}$
- $\dfrac{1}{42}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$

## Explanation

A common denominator is $42$. The numerators become $7$ and $6$, respectively. Their difference is one.

$$
\frac1{6}-\frac1{7}=\frac{7-6}{42}=\frac{1}{42}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{42}$.
