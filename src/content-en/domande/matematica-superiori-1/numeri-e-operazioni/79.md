Which change leaves the value of $\dfrac79-\dfrac25$ unchanged?

- Subtract $\dfrac17$ from the first term only
- Add $\dfrac17$ to the first term only
- Subtract $\dfrac17$ from both terms
- Swap the two terms

## Explanation

Subtracting $c$ from both terms gives $(a-c)-(b-c)=a-c-b+c=a-b$. The two contributions of $c$ cancel because the second is inside the quantity being subtracted. The original difference equals $\dfrac{35-18}{45}=\dfrac{17}{45}$. Swapping the terms would give $-\dfrac{17}{45}$, while changing just one term would change the difference by one seventh.
