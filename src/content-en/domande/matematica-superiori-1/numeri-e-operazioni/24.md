Taking $\dfrac45$ of a positive quantity gives $\dfrac6{25}$. What was the original quantity?

- $\dfrac{24}{125}$
- $\dfrac6{25}$
- $\dfrac56$
- $\dfrac3{10}$

## Explanation

The given value $\dfrac6{25}$ represents four fifths. One fifth is therefore $\dfrac6{25}:4=\dfrac3{50}$. The original quantity contains five such parts and equals $5\cdot\dfrac3{50}=\dfrac3{10}$. The product $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac3{10}=\dfrac6{25}$ confirms the calculation. The whole must be larger than its part, so any answer smaller than $\dfrac6{25}$ can be ruled out before calculating.
