Calculate $\dfrac{9}{12}\cdot\dfrac13$.

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators. The common factor of three can be canceled before multiplying. The final fraction has exactly the same value.

$$
\frac{9}{12}\cdot\frac13=\frac{9}{36}=\frac{1}{4}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
