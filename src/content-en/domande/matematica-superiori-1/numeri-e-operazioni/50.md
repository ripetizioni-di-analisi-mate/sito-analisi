A point on the number line has coordinate $-3$. Which two coordinates lie $5$ units away from it?

- $-2$ e $8$
- $-8$ e $2$
- $-8$ e $8$
- $-5$ e $5$

## Explanation

Start at $-3$ and move five units in each direction. The move to the left gives $-3-5=-8$, and the move to the right gives $-3+5=2$.
