Factor $x^4-625$ completely.

- $\left(x - 5\right)^{2} \left(x^{2} + 25\right)$
- $\left(x^{2} - 25\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 5\right) \left(x^{2} + 25\right)$
- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 5\right)$

## Explanation

First apply the difference of squares with bases x squared and $5$ squared. The factor containing the minus sign is itself a difference of squares. Factor it further.

$$
x^4-625=(x^2-25)(x^2+25)=(x-5)(x+5)(x^2+25)
$$

The result is $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 5\right) \left(x^{2} + 25\right)$.
