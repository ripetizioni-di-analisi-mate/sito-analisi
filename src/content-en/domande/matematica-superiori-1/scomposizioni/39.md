How many distinct real zeros does $6x^4-54x^2$ have?

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

First factor out $6x^2$, and then factor $x^2-9$ as $(x-3)(x+3)$. The product vanishes at $0$, $3$, and $-3$. The factor $x$ appears twice, but vanishes at the same value in both cases. There are three distinct values even though the polynomial has degree four.
