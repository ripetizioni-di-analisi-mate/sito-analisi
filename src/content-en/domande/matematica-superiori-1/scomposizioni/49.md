In $-21x^3y+14x^2y^2=mx^2y(3x-2y)$, which integer $m$ makes the identity true?

- $-7$
- $7$
- $-3$
- $-14$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x^3y$ on the right is $3m$, so $3m=-21$ gives $m=-7$. The second term has coefficient $-2m$, which at $m=-7$ is $14$, as required. Checking both coefficients also verifies that the chosen outer sign works for the entire factor in brackets.
