Factor $-6x^2-12x$.

- $- 6 x \left(x + 2\right)$
- $6 x \left(x + 2\right)$
- $- 6 x \left(x - 2\right)$
- $- 6 x - 12$

## Explanation

The common factor may be chosen negative. Dividing both terms by minus $6 x$ gives x and two. Applying the distributive property gives two negative terms again.

$$
-6x^2-12x=-6x(x+2)
$$

The result is $- 6 x \left(x + 2\right)$.
