If $-6x$ is factored out of $-6x^3+18x^2-12x$, which polynomial remains?

- $x^{2} + 3 x + 2$
- $x^{2} - 3 x + 2$
- $x^{2} - 3 x - 2$
- $- x^{2} - 3 x + 2$

## Explanation

The negative factor multiplies every remaining term. To obtain $18x^2$ in the product, the term inside must therefore be $-3x$. The other terms are $x^2$ and $2$.

$$
-6x^3+18x^2-12x=-6x(x^2-3x+2)
$$

Consider the coefficient quotients in order: $(-6)/(-6)=1$, $18/(-6)=-3$, and $(-12)/(-6)=2$. The exponent on $x$ drops by one in each term. The final term therefore gives the constant $2$, and multiplying by $-6x$ recovers the original linear term.
