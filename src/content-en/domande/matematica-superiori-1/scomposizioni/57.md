The products $(3x+1)(4x+2)$ and $(3x+2)(4x+1)$ have the same quadratic and constant terms. What is the first coefficient of $x$ minus the second?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

In the first product the cross-term coefficients are $3\cdot2=6$ and $4\cdot1=4$, which add to $10$. In the second they are $3\cdot1=3$ and $4\cdot2=8$, which add to $11$. The requested difference is $10-11=-1$. Swapping the constants preserves their product but changes how they pair with the coefficients of $x$.
