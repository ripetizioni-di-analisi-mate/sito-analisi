Which product equals $x^2+7x+10$?

- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x - 2\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(x + 10\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 5\right)$
- $x \left(x + 7\right)$

## Explanation

Find two numbers whose sum is the coefficient of x and whose product is the constant term. The two numbers are the given number and two. Multiplying the two binomials recovers the trinomial.

$$
5+2=7,\quad 5\cdot2=10,\quad x^2+7x+10=(x+5)(x+2)
$$

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 5\right)$.
