Which of the following trinomials cannot be written as the product of two monic binomials with integer coefficients?

- $x^2+7x+12$
- $x^2-x-12$
- $x^2+5x+12$
- $x^2-8x+12$

## Explanation

For a positive product $12$ and a positive sum, the possible pairs are $1,12$, $2,6$, and $3,4$, with sums $13,8,7$. None has sum $5$. The other three expressions use the pairs $3,4$, $3,-4$, and $-2,-6$, respectively. Their products and sums recover the required coefficients.
