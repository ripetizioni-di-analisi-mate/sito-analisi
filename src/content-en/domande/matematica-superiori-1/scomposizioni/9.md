Which bases belong in the difference-of-squares identity for $4x^2-121y^2$?

- $2x,\ 11y$
- $4x,\ 11y$
- $2x,\ 121y$
- $4x,\ 121y$

## Explanation

Squaring $2x$ and $11y$ gives the two original monomials. The factorisation is therefore $(2x-11y)(2x+11y)$.
