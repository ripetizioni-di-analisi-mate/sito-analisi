For which positive value of $k$ does the identity $81x^2-k=(9x-4)(9x+4)$ hold?

- $16$
- $4$
- $8$
- $36$

## Explanation

The binomials have opposite constant terms. Expanding them makes the two cross products cancel, while the product of the constants is $(-4)\cdot4=-16$. The quadratic term is $(9x)^2=81x^2$. The left side subtracts $k$, so the positive value required is $16$. The constant term can also be read by setting $x=0$.
