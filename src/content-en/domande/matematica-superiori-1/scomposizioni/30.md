Which integers $r,s$ satisfy both $r+s=-9$ and $rs=18$?

- $-2$ and $-9$
- $-3$ and $-6$
- $3$ and $6$
- $-1$ and $-18$

## Explanation

A positive product requires matching signs. A negative sum requires both signs to be negative. Among pairs of factors of $18$, $3$ and $6$ add to $9$.
