Find the greatest common monomial divisor of $66x^3y^2$ and $99x^2y^4$, with positive integer coefficient. Check that it divides both and gives monomial quotients.

- $33x^2y^2$
- $33x^3y^4$
- $11x^2y^2$
- $33x^2y^4$

## Explanation

The greatest common divisor of $66$ and $99$ is $33$. Dividing both monomials requires the smaller exponent of each variable, which is $2$ for both $x$ and $y$.
