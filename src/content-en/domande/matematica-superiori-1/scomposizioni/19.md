A strip of width $x$ is removed along each of two adjacent edges of a square sheet of side $5$, with $0<x<5$. Which polynomial gives the remaining area?

- $25-10x$
- $25-5x+x^2$
- $25-10x+x^2$
- $25-10x-x^2$

## Explanation

The remaining shape is a square of side $5-x$, so its area is $(5-x)^2=25-10x+x^2$. Subtracting the two rectangles of area $5x$ would remove the square of area $x^2$ at the common corner twice. To count it only once, add its area back to $25-5x-5x$.
