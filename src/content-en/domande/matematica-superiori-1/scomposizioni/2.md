Factor $x^2-121$ into two monic binomials. Choose the signs of their constants so that the terms in $x$ cancel.

- $(x-121)(x+1)$
- $(x-11)(x+11)$
- $(x-11)^2$
- $(x+11)^2$

## Explanation

Since $121=11^2$, use opposite constants in the two factors. The cross terms then cancel and the constants multiply to $-121$.
