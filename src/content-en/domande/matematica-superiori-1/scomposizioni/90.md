What value of $x$ makes the factor $x+7$ zero in the factorization $(x+7)(x+2)$?

- $7$
- $-7$
- $-14$
- $9$

## Explanation

To make the given factor zero, the sum of x and the number must be zero. The value sought is the opposite of that number. Substitute it in the factor to check that it vanishes.

$$
x+7=0,\qquad x=-7
$$

The result is $-7$.
