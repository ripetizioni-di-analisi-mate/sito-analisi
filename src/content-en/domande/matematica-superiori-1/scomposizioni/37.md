Factor $4x^3-4x$.

- $4 x \left(x - 1\right) \left(x + 1\right)$
- $4 x \left(x - 1\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(4 x - 4\right)$
- $4 x \left(x^{2} + 1\right)$

## Explanation

First factor out the common monomial. The remaining factor is a difference of squares. Factor it too to complete the calculation.

$$
4x^3-4x=4x(x^2-1)=4x(x-1)(x+1)
$$

The result is $4 x \left(x - 1\right) \left(x + 1\right)$.
