In $12x^3-27x=3x(2x-3)Q(x)$, which linear factor is missing for the equality to be an identity?

- $2x+3$
- $2x-3$
- $4x+9$
- $x+3$

## Explanation

The greatest common factor is $3x$, leaving $4x^2-9=(2x)^2-3^2$. This difference produces two binomials with the same term $2x$ and opposite constants, $(2x-3)(2x+3)$. The first already appears in the formula. With the second, the cross terms cancel and the product of the constants is $-9$.
