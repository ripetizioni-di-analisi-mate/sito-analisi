Which product is a factorization of $4x^2-9y^2$?

- $\left(2 x - 3 y\right) \left(2 x + 3 y\right)$
- $\left(2 x - 3 y\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x + 3 y\right) \left(4 x - 3 y\right)$
- $\left(2 x - 9 y\right) \left(2 x + y\right)$

## Explanation

The bases of the two squares are twice x and $3 y$. Factor the difference of squares into the product of the sum and the difference of those bases. Take the square root of the coefficient four too.

$$
4x^2-9y^2=(2x-3y)(2x+3y)
$$

The result is $\left(2 x - 3 y\right) \left(2 x + 3 y\right)$.

With two variables, a cross term would contain $xy$. The conjugate binomials produce the opposite terms $-6xy$ and $6xy$, which cancel.
