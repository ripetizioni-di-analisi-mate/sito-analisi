To factor $x^6-1$, start from $(x^3-1)(x^3+1)$. Which linear factor divides the second factor but not the first?

- $x+2$
- $x+1$
- $x-1$
- $x$

## Explanation

At $-1$ the second cubic evaluates to $0$, while the first evaluates to $-2$. Therefore $x+1$ divides only the second factor.
