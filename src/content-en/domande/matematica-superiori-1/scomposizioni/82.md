Factor $x^4-256$ completely.

- $\left(x - 4\right)^{2} \left(x^{2} + 16\right)$
- $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 4\right) \left(x^{2} + 16\right)$
- $\left(x^{2} - 16\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 4\right)$

## Explanation

First apply the difference of squares with bases x squared and $4$ squared. The factor containing the minus sign is itself a difference of squares. Factor it further.

$$
x^4-256=(x^2-16)(x^2+16)=(x-4)(x+4)(x^2+16)
$$

The result is $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 4\right) \left(x^{2} + 16\right)$.
