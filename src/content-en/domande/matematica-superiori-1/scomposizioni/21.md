What is the greatest common factor with positive coefficient of $12x^2yz+18xy^3$?

- $6xy$
- $6xyz$
- $6x^2y^3$
- $3xy$

## Explanation

The greatest common divisor of the coefficients is $6$. The minimum exponent for each of $x$ and $y$ is $1$, while for $z$ it is zero because the second term has no $z$. The result is $6xy(2xz+3y^2)$. Inside the brackets, $2$ and $3$ are coprime and the terms share no variable.
