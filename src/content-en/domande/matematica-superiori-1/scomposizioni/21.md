Which product equals $x^2+5x+6$?

- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 3\right)$
- $\left(x - 3\right) \left(x - 2\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(x + 6\right)$
- $x \left(x + 5\right)$

## Explanation

Find two numbers whose sum is the coefficient of x and whose product is the constant term. The two numbers are the given number and two. Multiplying the two binomials recovers the trinomial.

$$
3+2=5,\quad 3\cdot2=6,\quad x^2+5x+6=(x+3)(x+2)
$$

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 3\right)$.

The cross terms give $2x+3x=5x$. This is why reversing the order of the two factors preserves every coefficient.
