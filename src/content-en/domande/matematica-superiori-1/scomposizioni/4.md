Which product equals $6x^2+12x$?

- $6 x + 12$
- $6 x \left(x - 2\right)$
- $x \left(x + 12\right)$
- $6 x \left(x + 2\right)$

## Explanation

Both terms contain the given numerical factor $6$ and an x. Dividing each term by the common factor gives x and two. The distributive property recovers the original polynomial.

$$
6x^2+12x=6x(x+2)
$$

The result is $6 x \left(x + 2\right)$.
