The factorisation of $x^2-22x+121$ contains two copies of the same factor. At which input do both copies vanish?

- $-11$
- $22$
- $121$
- $11$

## Explanation

The sign of the cross term identifies $(x-11)^2$. Both copies of $x-11$ vanish at $11$, making this a double zero.
