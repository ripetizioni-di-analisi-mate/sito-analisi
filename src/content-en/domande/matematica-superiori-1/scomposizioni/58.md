For what value of $c$ does the identity $x^2+8x+c=(x+5)(x+3)$ hold?

- $8$
- $15$
- $2$
- $16$

## Explanation

Expand the product of the two binomials. The constant term comes from multiplying their constant terms. The identity requires c to equal that product.

$$
(x+5)(x+3)=x^2+8x+15,\quad c=15
$$

The result is $15$.
