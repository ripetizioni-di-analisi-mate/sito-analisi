In the product $(2x+5)(3x-a)$, the coefficient of $x$ must be $7$. What is $a$?

- $-4$
- $4$
- $8$
- $11$

## Explanation

The linear term comes only from the two cross products, $-2ax$ and $15x$. Its coefficient is therefore $15-2a$, and $15-2a=7$ gives $a=4$. Expanding to check gives $(2x+5)(3x-4)=6x^2+7x-20$. The constant term $-20$ is separate from the condition on the middle coefficient.
