The polynomial $P(x)=(x+11)(x-1)$ is already factored. Find $P(-11)$ directly from the two factors.

- $11$
- $132$
- $0$
- $-12$

## Explanation

Substituting $-11$ makes the first factor $x+11$ zero. By the zero-product property you do not need the other factor's value to conclude that $P(-11)=0$.
