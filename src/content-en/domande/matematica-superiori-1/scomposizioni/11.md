Factor $x^2+6x+9$.

- $\left(x - 3\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(x + 3\right)$
- $\left(x + 3\right)^{2}$
- $x \left(x + 6\right)$

## Explanation

The first and third terms are squares. The middle term is twice the product of the bases with a positive sign. The polynomial is therefore the square of their sum.

$$
x^2+6x+9=(x+3)^2
$$

The result is $\left(x + 3\right)^{2}$.

Conjugate factors would cancel the cross terms. Here the two contributions $3x$ instead have the same sign and add.
