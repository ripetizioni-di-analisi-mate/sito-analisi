You add $xy$ to the list $x^2y,xy^3$ when finding its least common multiple. How does the result change?

- It becomes $xy$
- It stays $x^2y$
- It stays $x^2y^3$
- It becomes $x^3y^4$

## Explanation

The original common multiple already contains enough powers of $x$ and $y$ to be divisible by $xy$. The third monomial increases none of the maximum exponents.
