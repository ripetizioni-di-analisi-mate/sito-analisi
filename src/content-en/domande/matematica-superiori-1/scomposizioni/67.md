What is the least common monomial multiple of $8x^2y$ and $12xy^3$ with a positive integer coefficient?

- $4 x y$
- $24 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $24 x^{2} y^{3}$
- $20 x^{2} y^{3}$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the least common multiple of the numerical coefficients. For each variable that occurs, take its greatest exponent. The result contains the factors needed to be a multiple of both.

$$
\operatorname{lcm}=24x^2y^3
$$

The result is $24 x^{2} y^{3}$.

Taking $y^3$ allows the second monomial to divide the result. Checking directly gives the two multipliers $3y^2$ and $2x$.
