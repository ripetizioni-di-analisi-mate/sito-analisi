Factor $-5x^2-10x$.

- $5 x \left(x + 2\right)$
- $- 5 x \left(x - 2\right)$
- $- 5 x - 10$
- $- 5 x \left(x + 2\right)$

## Explanation

The common factor may be chosen negative. Dividing both terms by minus $5 x$ gives x and two. Applying the distributive property gives two negative terms again.

$$
-5x^2-10x=-5x(x+2)
$$

The result is $- 5 x \left(x + 2\right)$.
