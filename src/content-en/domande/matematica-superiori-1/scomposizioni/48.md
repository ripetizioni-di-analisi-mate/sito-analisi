A factorization is $-6x(2x-5)$. To write it in the form $6x\,R(x)$, what must $R(x)$ be?

- $-2x-5$
- $2x+5$
- $2x-5$
- $-2x+5$

## Explanation

Write the outer coefficient as $-6x=6x(-1)$. The factor $-1$ must multiply both terms, giving $R(x)=-(2x-5)=-2x+5$. As a check, $6x(-2x+5)=-12x^2+30x$, the same polynomial obtained from the original factorization. Changing only the first sign would produce the term $-30x$ instead.
