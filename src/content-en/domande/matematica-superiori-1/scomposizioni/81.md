Factor $x^4-81$ completely.

- $\left(x - 3\right) \left(x + 3\right) \left(x^{2} + 9\right)$
- $\left(x - 3\right)^{2} \left(x^{2} + 9\right)$
- $\left(x^{2} - 9\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x - 3\right) \left(x + 3\right)$

## Explanation

First apply the difference of squares with bases x squared and $3$ squared. The factor containing the minus sign is itself a difference of squares. Factor it further.

$$
x^4-81=(x^2-9)(x^2+9)=(x-3)(x+3)(x^2+9)
$$

The result is $\left(x - 3\right) \left(x + 3\right) \left(x^{2} + 9\right)$.
