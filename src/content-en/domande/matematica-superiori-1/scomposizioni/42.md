With $y=2$ fixed, which values of $x$ make $9x^2-4y^2$ zero?

- $x=\pm\frac23$
- $x=\pm\frac43$
- $x=\pm\frac{16}{9}$
- $x=\pm4$

## Explanation

The bases of the squares are $3x$ and $2y$, so the two factors are $3x-2y$ and $3x+2y$. At $y=2$ they become $3x-4$ and $3x+4$. Each vanishes when $3x$ is the opposite of its constant term. Dividing by $3$ gives $4/3$ and $-4/3$, symmetric about zero.
