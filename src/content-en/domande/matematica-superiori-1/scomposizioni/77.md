Factor $x^2y+4xy^2$.

- $x y \left(x + 4 y\right)$
- $x y \left(x + 4\right)$
- $x \left(x + 4 y\right)$
- $x y \left(x - 4 y\right)$

## Explanation

Both terms contain an x and a y. After taking out their product, the first term leaves x and the second leaves $4 y$. Multiply the common factor by the result to verify the factorization.

$$
x^2y+4xy^2=xy(x+4y)
$$

The result is $x y \left(x + 4 y\right)$.

The second quotient is $4y$ because $y^2$ loses just one factor of $y$. Distributing $xy$ therefore recovers $4xy^2$.
