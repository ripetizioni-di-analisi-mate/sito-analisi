What is the largest integer exponent $k$ for which $x^k$ divides the entire polynomial $x^5-2x^3+x^4$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $1$

## Explanation

Every term contains at least three factors of $x$. The term $-2x^3$ contains exactly three and prevents a higher power from being taken out.
