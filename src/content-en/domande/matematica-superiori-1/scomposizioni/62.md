Dividing both monomials $12x^2y$ and $18xy^2$ by their GCD gives which pair of quotients?

- $2xy,\ 3xy$
- $2x,\ 3y$
- $2y,\ 3x$
- $6x,\ 6y$

## Explanation

The GCD is $6xy$. Dividing leaves $2x$ and $3y$, which no longer share a nontrivial monomial factor.
