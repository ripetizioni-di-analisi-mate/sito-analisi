What is the greatest common monomial divisor of $18x^3y^2$ and $27x^2y^4$ with a positive integer coefficient?

- $9 x^{2} y^{2}$
- $54 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $9 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $3 x y$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the greatest common divisor of the numerical coefficients. For each shared variable, take the smaller of the exponents. The resulting monomial divides both given monomials.

$$
\gcd=9x^2y^2
$$

The result is $9 x^{2} y^{2}$.

Division gives the quotients $2x$ and $3y^2$. They share no variables, and $2$ and $3$ have no common divisor greater than $1$.
