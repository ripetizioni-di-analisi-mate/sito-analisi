A classmate writes $9x^2+24x+16=(9x+4)^2$. Which correction makes the identity true?

- $(3x+4)^2$
- $(3x-4)^2$
- $(9x+2)^2$
- $(3x+8)^2$

## Explanation

The leading term $9x^2$ comes from squaring $3x$, since $(9x)^2=81x^2$. The constant term $16$ gives the other base $4$. Adding the two bases gives a positive cross term, $2\cdot3x\cdot4=24x$. The doubled product distinguishes this correction from $(3x-4)^2$, which has the same square terms.
