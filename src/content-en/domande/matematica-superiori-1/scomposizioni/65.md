Two monomials with positive integer coefficients have GCD $1$. Which statement must be true?

- The coefficients are coprime and no variable occurs in both
- Both coefficients are $1$
- The monomials have the same degree
- Neither monomial contains a variable

## Explanation

A prime dividing both coefficients would make the GCD greater than $1$. Any variable occurring in both would also appear in the GCD to at least the first power.
