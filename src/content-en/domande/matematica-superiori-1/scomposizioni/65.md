What is the greatest common divisor of $8x^2$ and $12y^3$ among monomials with a positive integer coefficient?

- $4$
- $4 x y$
- $2$
- $24$

## Explanation

No variable occurs in both monomials. The only factor to consider is the greatest common divisor of $8$ and $12$, which is $4$.

The exponents of $x$ are $2$ and $0$, while those of $y$ are $0$ and $3$. Both minima are zero. The quotients by $4$ are $2x^2$ and $3y^3$, which have no further numerical or variable factor in common.
