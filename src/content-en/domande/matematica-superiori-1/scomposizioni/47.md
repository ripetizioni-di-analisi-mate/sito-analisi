Factor $-4x^2-8x$.

- $4 x \left(x + 2\right)$
- $- 4 x \left(x - 2\right)$
- $- 4 x \left(x + 2\right)$
- $- 4 x - 8$

## Explanation

The common factor may be chosen negative. Dividing both terms by minus $4 x$ gives x and two. Applying the distributive property gives two negative terms again.

$$
-4x^2-8x=-4x(x+2)
$$

The result is $- 4 x \left(x + 2\right)$.
