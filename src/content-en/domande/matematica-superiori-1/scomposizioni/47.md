Factor the greatest common factor with negative sign out of $-20x^3+12x^2-8x$. What remains in the brackets?

- $5x^2+3x+2$
- $-5x^2+3x-2$
- $5x^2-3x+2$
- $5x^2-3x-2$

## Explanation

The coefficients have greatest common divisor $4$, and the minimum exponent of $x$ is $1$. Factoring out $-4x$ gives quotients with signs opposite to the original coefficients. The result is $-4x(5x^2-3x+2)$. In particular, the middle coefficient becomes positive again because $(-4x)(-3x)=12x^2$.
