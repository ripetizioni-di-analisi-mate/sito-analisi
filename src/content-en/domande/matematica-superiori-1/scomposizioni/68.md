What monomial must multiply $12x^2y$ to give its LCM with $18xy^3z$?

- $3yz$
- $6y^2z$
- $3xy^2$
- $3y^2z$

## Explanation

The LCM of the coefficients is $36$. Taking the largest variable exponents gives $L=36x^2y^3z$. The first monomial already has $x^2$ and one $y$, so the multiplier adds one $z$, two factors of $y$, and $36/12=3$. The check is $(12x^2y)(3y^2z)=L$. The second monomial also divides $L$, with quotient $2x$.
