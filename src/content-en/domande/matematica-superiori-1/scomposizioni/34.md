The two binomials in $3(x-2)+x(2-x)$ are opposites. What product results from factoring out $x-2$?

- $(x-2)(3+x)$
- $(x-2)(3-x)$
- $(x-2)(x-3)$
- $(x+2)(3-x)$

## Explanation

Since $2-x=-(x-2)$, the second term is $-x(x-2)$. The coefficients remaining after factoring are therefore $3$ and $-x$. Substituting $x=0$ gives $-6$, as does $(x-2)(3-x)$. To check the identity for every $x$, distribute the factor $x-2$ to recover both terms with their signs.
