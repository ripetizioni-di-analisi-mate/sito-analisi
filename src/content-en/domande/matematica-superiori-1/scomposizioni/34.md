Factor $xy+6x+2y+12$.

- $\left(x + 6\right) \left(y + 2\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 6\right)$
- $\left(x - 2\right) \left(y + 6\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y - 6\right)$

## Explanation

Group the first two terms and the last two terms. Factor each group to leave the same binomial. Then factor out the common binomial.

$$
xy+6x+2y+12=x(y+6)+2(y+6)=(x+2)(y+6)
$$

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 6\right)$.
