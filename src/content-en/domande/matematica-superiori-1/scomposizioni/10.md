Write $-11x^2-22x$ in the form $-11x(x+k)$. Which $k$ gives the correct sign for the second term?

- $-22$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $22$

## Explanation

The reconstructed second term is $-11kx$. Comparing coefficients gives $-11k=-22$, hence $k=2$.
