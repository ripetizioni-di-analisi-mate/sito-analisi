Factor $(x+1)^2-9$.

- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(x + 3\right)$
- $\left(x - 2\right) \left(x + 4\right)$
- $\left(x - 9\right) \left(x + 1\right)$
- $\left(x + 4\right)^{2}$

## Explanation

The bases of the two squares are $x+1$ and $3$. Their difference is $x-2$ and their sum is $x+4$.

$$
(x+1)^2-9=(x+1-3)(x+1+3)
$$

The bases are the entire binomial $x+1$ and the number $3$. Their difference gives $(x+1)-3=x-2$, and their sum gives $(x+1)+3=x+4$. To check the constant term, the factors give $(-2)\cdot4=-8$ at $x=0$, the same result as $1-9=-8$ in the original form.
