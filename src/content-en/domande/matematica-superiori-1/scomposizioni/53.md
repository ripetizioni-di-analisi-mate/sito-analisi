A quadratic of the form $P(x)=a(x-2)(x+3)$ passes through $(0,18)$. What is its leading coefficient?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-6$
- $6$

## Explanation

The point $(0,18)$ gives the equation $P(0)=18$. Substitution yields $18=a(-2)(3)=-6a$, so $a=-3$. Because the product of the two monic factors has leading coefficient $1$, the leading coefficient of $P$ is $a$. The negative sign also explains why the graph opens downward even though its vertical intercept is positive.
