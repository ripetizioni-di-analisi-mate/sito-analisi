Let $x>0$ and $y>0$. Which condition describes exactly when $2x^2y-xy^2$ is strictly positive?

- $y>2x$
- $y=2x$
- $y<2x$
- $y<x/2$

## Explanation

Factoring gives $xy(2x-y)$. Since $x$ and $y$ are positive, $xy$ is positive as well and the sign depends entirely on $2x-y$. The product is positive exactly when $y<2x$, zero when $y=2x$, and negative when $y>2x$. The stricter condition $y<x/2$ covers only some of the possibilities.
