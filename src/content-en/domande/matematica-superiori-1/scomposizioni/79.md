Factor $x^2y+6xy^2$.

- $x y \left(x + 6\right)$
- $x \left(x + 6 y\right)$
- $x y \left(x + 6 y\right)$
- $x y \left(x - 6 y\right)$

## Explanation

Both terms contain an x and a y. After taking out their product, the first term leaves x and the second leaves $6 y$. Multiply the common factor by the result to verify the factorization.

$$
x^2y+6xy^2=xy(x+6y)
$$

The result is $x y \left(x + 6 y\right)$.
