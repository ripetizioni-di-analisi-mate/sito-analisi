The polynomial $x^2(x-1)-2x(x-1)+(x-1)$ hides three copies of the same factor. What is the result when all three are identified?

- $(x-1)(x+1)^2$
- $(x-1)^2$
- $(x+1)^3$
- $(x-1)^3$

## Explanation

First factor $x-1$ out of all three terms. What remains is $x^2-2x+1=(x-1)^2$, which combines with the outside factor to give the cube.
