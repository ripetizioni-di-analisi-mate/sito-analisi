Factor $2x^3-8x$ completely.

- $2 x \left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 4\right)$
- $2 x \left(x - 2\right)^{2}$
- $x \left(x - 2\right) \left(x + 2\right)$
- $2 x \left(x - 2\right) \left(x + 2\right)$

## Explanation

Factor out the monomial $2x$. What remains is $x^2-4$, so use the bases $x$ and $2$ to factor it.

$$
2x^3-8x=2x(x^2-4)=2x(x-2)(x+2)
$$
