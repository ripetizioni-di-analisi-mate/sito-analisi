The expression $18x^3+12x^2$ has been rewritten as $3x(6x^2+4x)$. What greatest common factor with positive coefficient can still be extracted from the brackets?

- $2$
- $2x$
- $6x^2$
- $x^2$

## Explanation

Inside the brackets, both terms still have a common numerical divisor $2$ and a factor of $x$. Thus $6x^2+4x=2x(3x+2)$ and the extracted factors multiply to $(3x)(2x)=6x^2$. The constant term $2$ rules out any further common variable, and the coefficients $3,2$ rule out any further common integer divisor greater than one.
