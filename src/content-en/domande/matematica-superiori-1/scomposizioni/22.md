Which product equals $x^2+6x+8$?

- $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x - 2\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 4\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(x + 8\right)$
- $x \left(x + 6\right)$

## Explanation

Find two numbers whose sum is the coefficient of x and whose product is the constant term. The two numbers are the given number and two. Multiplying the two binomials recovers the trinomial.

$$
4+2=6,\quad 4\cdot2=8,\quad x^2+6x+8=(x+4)(x+2)
$$

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 4\right)$.
