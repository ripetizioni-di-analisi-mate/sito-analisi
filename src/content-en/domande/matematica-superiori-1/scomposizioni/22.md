In the trinomial $x^2+bx+20$, the coefficient $b$ is positive and the constants in the two integer binomial factors differ by $1$. What is $b$?

- $12$
- $9$
- $21$
- $1$

## Explanation

Because both the product and sum are positive, both constants must be positive. The factor pairs of $20$ are $(1,20)$, $(2,10)$, and $(4,5)$, up to order. Only $4$ and $5$ are consecutive. Their difference supplies the given condition, and their sum $4+5=9$ is the coefficient to find.
