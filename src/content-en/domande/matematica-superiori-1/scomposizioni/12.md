For which value of $c$ does the identity $x^2+14x+c=(x+11)(x+3)$ hold? At $x=0$, only the constant terms remain.

- $14$
- $11$
- $8$
- $33$

## Explanation

Putting $x=0$ in the identity gives $c=(0+11)(0+3)=33$ directly. The sum $11+3=14$ also verifies the linear coefficient.
