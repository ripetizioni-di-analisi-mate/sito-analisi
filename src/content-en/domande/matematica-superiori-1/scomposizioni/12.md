Factor $x^2+8x+16$.

- $\left(x - 4\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(x + 4\right)$
- $x \left(x + 8\right)$
- $\left(x + 4\right)^{2}$

## Explanation

The first and third terms are squares. The middle term is twice the product of the bases with a positive sign. The polynomial is therefore the square of their sum.

$$
x^2+8x+16=(x+4)^2
$$

The result is $\left(x + 4\right)^{2}$.
