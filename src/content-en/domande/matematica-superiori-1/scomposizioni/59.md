A quadratic $P(x)$ has leading coefficient $3$ and satisfies $P(0)=-12$. Which product could represent it?

- $(3x-4)(x-3)$
- $(3x+4)(2x-3)$
- $(3x+4)(x-3)$
- $(x+4)(x-3)$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x^2$ comes from multiplying the coefficients of the two $x$ terms, and the value at zero comes from multiplying the constants. Only $(3x+4)(x-3)$ gives both $3\cdot1=3$ and $4\cdot(-3)=-12$. The data do not determine the middle term, so other polynomials outside the choices could also fit.
