What value of $x$ makes the factor $x+4$ zero in the factorization $(x+4)(x+2)$?

- $4$
- $-8$
- $-4$
- $6$

## Explanation

To make the given factor zero, the sum of x and the number must be zero. The value sought is the opposite of that number. Substitute it in the factor to check that it vanishes.

$$
x+4=0,\qquad x=-4
$$

The result is $-4$.

At $-4$ the second factor equals $-2$. Its own zero is $-2$, a value that makes the product vanish through a different factor.
