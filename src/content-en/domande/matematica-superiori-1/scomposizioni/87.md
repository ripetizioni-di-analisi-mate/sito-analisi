A monic quadratic has opposite integer roots and constant term $-25$. Which factor has positive constant term?

- $x-5$
- $x+1$
- $x+5$
- $x+25$

## Explanation

With roots $r$ and $-r$ the product is $(x-r)(x+r)=x^2-r^2$. The constant gives $r^2=25$, so the factors are $x-5$ and $x+5$.
