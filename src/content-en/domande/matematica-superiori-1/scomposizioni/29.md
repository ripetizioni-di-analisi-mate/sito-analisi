To factor $8x^2-2x-3$ by grouping, split $-2x$ into $rx+sx$. Which pair $(r,s)$ makes the same binomial appear in both groups?

- $(4,-6)$
- $(1,-3)$
- $(6,-8)$
- $(-4,2)$

## Explanation

All four choices have the correct sum. To get two groups with a common factor, the product of the coefficients must also be $8\cdot(-3)=-24$. Only $4$ and $-6$ satisfy that condition. Indeed, $8x^2+4x-6x-3=4x(2x+1)-3(2x+1)=(4x-3)(2x+1)$.
