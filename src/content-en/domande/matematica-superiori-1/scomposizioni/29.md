Which product equals $x^2-7x+6$?

- $\left(x - 6\right) \left(x - 1\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(x + 6\right)$
- $\left(x - 6\right) \left(x + 1\right)$
- $x \left(x - 7\right)$

## Explanation

The positive constant term and negative linear coefficient indicate two negative numbers. Minus $6$ and minus one have the required product and sum. These two numbers give the linear factors.

$$
x^2-7x+6=(x-6)(x-1)
$$

The result is $\left(x - 6\right) \left(x - 1\right)$.
