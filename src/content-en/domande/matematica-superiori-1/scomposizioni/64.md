What is the greatest common monomial divisor of $36x^3y^2$ and $54x^2y^4$ with a positive integer coefficient?

- $108 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $18 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $6 x y$
- $18 x^{2} y^{2}$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the greatest common divisor of the numerical coefficients. For each shared variable, take the smaller of the exponents. The resulting monomial divides both given monomials.

$$
\gcd=18x^2y^2
$$

The result is $18 x^{2} y^{2}$.
