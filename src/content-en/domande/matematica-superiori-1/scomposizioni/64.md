A classmate proposes $6x^2y$ as the GCD of $18x^3y$ and $30xy^4$. Which check rules it out?

- $6$ does not divide $30$.
- $y$ does not appear in the second monomial.
- $x^2$ does not divide $x^3$.
- $x^2$ does not divide the variable part of $30xy^4$.

## Explanation

A common divisor must leave nonnegative exponents in both monomial quotients. In the second monomial, $x$ has exponent $1$, so dividing by $x^2$ would leave exponent $-1$. The minimum exponents are instead $1$ for $x$ and $1$ for $y$. Together with the greatest common numerical divisor $6$, these give $6xy$.
