Factor $16x^2-25(x-1)^2$.

- $- \left(x - 5\right) \left(9 x - 5\right)$
- $\left(x + 5\right) \left(9 x - 5\right)$
- $\left(5 - x\right) \left(x - 5\right)$
- $\left(4 x - 5\right) \left(4 x + 5\right)$

## Explanation

The bases are $4x$ and $5(x-1)$. Their difference is $5-x$ and their sum is $9x-5$.

$$
16x^2-25(x-1)^2=(5-x)(9x-5)
$$

The two bases are $4x$ and $5(x-1)$. Subtracting the entire second base gives $4x-5(x-1)=5-x$, while the sum is $4x+5(x-1)=9x-5$. Rewriting $5-x$ as $-(x-5)$ moves the minus sign in front of the entire product.
