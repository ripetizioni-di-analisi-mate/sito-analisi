For which $k$ does grouping $x(y+2)+3(y+k)$ produce a common factor $y+2$ as an identity in $x,y$?

- $k=2$
- $k=-2$
- $k=3$
- $k=0$

## Explanation

The first group already has the required factor. Setting $y=-2$ must also make the second group vanish, giving $3(k-2)=0$ and $k=2$.
