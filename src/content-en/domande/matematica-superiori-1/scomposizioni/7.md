A square of side $7$ is removed from a square of side $13$. Which product of two lengths gives the remaining area through the difference of squares?

- $6\cdot6$
- $7\cdot13$
- $6\cdot20$
- $7\cdot20$

## Explanation

The area is $13^2-7^2$. The difference of the sides is $6$ and their sum is $20$, so $(13-7)(13+7)=120$. If the smaller square is placed in one corner, the remaining area splits into two rectangles with width $6$ and lengths $13$ and $7$. Joining them gives a combined length of $20$.
