Which product is a factorization of $x^2-16$?

- $\left(x - 4\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x + 4\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 4\right)$
- $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 1\right)$

## Explanation

The polynomial is a difference of squares. Its factorization uses the sum and the difference of the two bases. When the factors are multiplied, the mixed products cancel.

$$
x^2-16=(x-4)(x+4)
$$

The result is $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 4\right)$.
