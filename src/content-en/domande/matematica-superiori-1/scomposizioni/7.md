After grouping $xy+11x+2y+22$ as $x(y+11)+2(y+11)$, what operation remains?

- Substitute $y=11$
- Square $y+11$
- Factor out $y+11$
- Factor $x$ out of both groups

## Explanation

The two groups share the whole bracket $y+11$. The remaining factors, $x$ and $2$, add to give $(y+11)(x+2)$.
