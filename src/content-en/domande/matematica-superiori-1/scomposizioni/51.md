The polynomial $P(x)=(x+3)(x-1)$ is already factored. What is $P(-3)$?

- $3$
- $1$
- $0$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Substituting the opposite of the number into the first factor makes it zero. A product is zero if one factor is zero. The value can be found directly from the factored polynomial.

$$
P(-3)=(-3+3)(-3-1)=0
$$

The result is $0$.

The second factor equals $-4$. For the product to vanish, it is enough that the first factor is zero even though the second is nonzero.
