Which product is a factorization of $x^2-25$?

- $\left(x - 5\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x + 5\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 1\right)$
- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 5\right)$

## Explanation

The polynomial is a difference of squares. Its factorization uses the sum and the difference of the two bases. When the factors are multiplied, the mixed products cancel.

$$
x^2-25=(x-5)(x+5)
$$

The result is $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 5\right)$.
