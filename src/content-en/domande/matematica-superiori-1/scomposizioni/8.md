What is $102^2-98^2$? Use the difference of squares to avoid calculating the squares separately.

- $16$
- $400$
- $200$
- $800$

## Explanation

A difference of squares is the product of the difference of the bases and their sum. Here the bases are $4$ apart and symmetric about $100$, so their sum is $200$. This gives $(102-98)(102+98)=4\cdot200=800$. Squaring the difference alone would give $16$ and miss the factor $200$.
