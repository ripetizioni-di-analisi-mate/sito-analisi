Factor $12x^2y-18xy^2$ by taking out the greatest positive common factor.

- $6 x y \left(2 x + 3 y\right)$
- $6 x y \left(2 x - 2 y\right)$
- $3 y \left(2 x - 3 y\right)$
- $6 x y \left(2 x - 3 y\right)$

## Explanation

The GCD of the coefficients is $6$, and each variable occurs to at least the first power. After division, the first term leaves $2x$ and the second leaves $-3y$.

$$
12x^2y-18xy^2=6xy(2x-3y)
$$

Dividing the first term by $6xy$ leaves $2x$, and dividing the second leaves $-3y$. The remaining terms use different variables, and $2,3$ are coprime. This confirms that the greatest common factor was extracted.
