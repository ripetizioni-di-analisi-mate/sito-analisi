A proposed equality is $P(x)=(x-2)(x+1)$. Which single fact about $P$ is enough to reject it?

- $P(-1)=0$
- $P(0)=-2$
- $P(1)=-2$
- $P(2)=1$

## Explanation

The proposed product must vanish at $2$ because its first factor is zero. The other three evaluations are consistent with the product.
