After dividing every term of $P(x)$ by $4x$, the result is $3x^2-5x+2$. What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in $P$?

- $-20$
- $12$
- $-5$
- $8$

## Explanation

To recover the polynomial, multiply every quotient by $4x$. The exponent of each term increases by one. The quadratic term therefore comes from the linear term in the parentheses, $(4x)(-5x)=-20x^2$. The complete polynomial is $12x^3-20x^2+8x$. The coefficients $12$ and $8$ belong to terms of other degrees.
