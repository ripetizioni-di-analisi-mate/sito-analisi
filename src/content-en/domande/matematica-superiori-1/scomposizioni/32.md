Which quadratic factor comes from grouping consecutive pairs in $x^3-6x^2+7x-42$?

- $x^2-7$
- $x^2-6$
- $x^2+42$
- $x^2+7$

## Explanation

The first group gives $x^2(x-6)$ and the second gives $7(x-6)$. The same binomial $x-6$ appears in both groups, so factoring it out leaves the sum $x^2+7$ of the two coefficients. As a check, its two terms recover $x^3-6x^2$ and $7x-42$, respectively, when each is multiplied by the full binomial $x-6$.
