Factor $xy+4x+2y+8$.

- $\left(x + 4\right) \left(y + 2\right)$
- $\left(x - 2\right) \left(y + 4\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y - 4\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 4\right)$

## Explanation

Group the first two terms and the last two terms. Factor each group to leave the same binomial. Then factor out the common binomial.

$$
xy+4x+2y+8=x(y+4)+2(y+4)=(x+2)(y+4)
$$

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 4\right)$.
