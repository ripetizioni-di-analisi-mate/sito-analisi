Without testing every pair, what signs must $r,s$ have in $x^2-12x+11=(x+r)(x+s)$?

- At least one zero
- Both negative
- Both positive
- One positive and one negative

## Explanation

The product $rs=11$ requires equal signs. The sum $r+s=-12$ rules out positive values and identifies the pair $-1,-11$.
