Factor $x^2y+3xy^2$.

- $x y \left(x + 3\right)$
- $x \left(x + 3 y\right)$
- $x y \left(x - 3 y\right)$
- $x y \left(x + 3 y\right)$

## Explanation

Both terms contain an x and a y. After taking out their product, the first term leaves x and the second leaves $3 y$. Multiply the common factor by the result to verify the factorization.

$$
x^2y+3xy^2=xy(x+3y)
$$

The result is $x y \left(x + 3 y\right)$.

Both variables appear in both terms, but each has exponent $1$ in at least one term. That makes the common factor exactly $xy$.
