Why does taking out $11x$ from $11x^2+22x$ leave precisely the binomial $x+2$?

- The two quotients are $x$ and $2$
- The two quotients are $x^2$ and $2x$
- You subtract $11$ from the coefficients
- You discard the second term

## Explanation

The outside factor multiplies every term in the bracket. The products $11x\cdot x$ and $11x\cdot2$ exactly reconstruct the original terms.
