Which product equals $3x^2+6x$?

- $3 x \left(x + 2\right)$
- $3 x + 6$
- $3 x \left(x - 2\right)$
- $x \left(x + 6\right)$

## Explanation

Both terms contain the given numerical factor $3$ and an x. Dividing each term by the common factor gives x and two. The distributive property recovers the original polynomial.

$$
3x^2+6x=3x(x+2)
$$

The result is $3 x \left(x + 2\right)$.

The factor $3x$ is the greatest one because the second quotient is the constant $2$ and the coefficients $1,2$ are coprime.
