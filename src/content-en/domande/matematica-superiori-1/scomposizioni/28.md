Which product equals $x^2-6x+5$?

- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(x + 5\right)$
- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 1\right)$
- $x \left(x - 6\right)$
- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x - 1\right)$

## Explanation

The positive constant term and negative linear coefficient indicate two negative numbers. Minus $5$ and minus one have the required product and sum. These two numbers give the linear factors.

$$
x^2-6x+5=(x-5)(x-1)
$$

The result is $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x - 1\right)$.
