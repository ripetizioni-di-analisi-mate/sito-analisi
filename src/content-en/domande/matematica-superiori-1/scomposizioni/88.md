For which values is $P(x)=(x+2)^2(x-5)$ negative?

- $(-\infty,5)$
- $(-2,5)$
- $(5,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(-2,5)$

## Explanation

The square is strictly positive for $x\ne-2$ and zero at $-2$. The factor $x-5$ is negative for $x<5$ and zero at $5$. For a negative product, both $x<5$ and $x\ne-2$ are required. This gives two intervals because the double zero $-2$ must be excluded even though the sign does not change there.
