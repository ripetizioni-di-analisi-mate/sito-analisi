What is the least common monomial multiple of $12x^2y$ and $18xy^3$ with a positive integer coefficient?

- $36 x^{2} y^{3}$
- $6 x y$
- $36 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $30 x^{2} y^{3}$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the least common multiple of the numerical coefficients. For each variable that occurs, take its greatest exponent. The result contains the factors needed to be a multiple of both.

$$
\operatorname{lcm}=36x^2y^3
$$

The result is $36 x^{2} y^{3}$.
