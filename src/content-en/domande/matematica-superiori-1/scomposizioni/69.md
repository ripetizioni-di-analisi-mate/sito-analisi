The product of two monomials is $280x^5y^4$, and their GCD is $2x^2y$. What is their LCM?

- $140x^3y^3$
- $140x^2y^3$
- $280x^3y^3$
- $140x^5y^4$

## Explanation

For every prime and every variable, the minimum and maximum exponents add to the sum of the two original exponents. Thus the GCD times the LCM equals the product of the two monomials. Dividing by the GCD leaves coefficient $140$ and exponents $5-2=3$ and $4-1=3$.
