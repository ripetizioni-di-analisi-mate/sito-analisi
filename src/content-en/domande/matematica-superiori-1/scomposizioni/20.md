Which trinomial is the square of a difference?

- $9x^2-6x+4$
- $9x^2-12x-4$
- $9x^2-18x+4$
- $9x^2-12x+4$

## Explanation

The first and last terms have bases $3x$ and $2$. Twice their product with a negative sign is $-12x$. The three terms therefore match the square of $3x-2$.

$$
(3x-2)^2=9x^2-12x+4
$$
