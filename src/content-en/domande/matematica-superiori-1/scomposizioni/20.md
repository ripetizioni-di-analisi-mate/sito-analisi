Which trinomial has the form $(ax-b)^2$, with $a$ and $b$ positive?

- $9x^2-6x+4$
- $9x^2-12x-4$
- $9x^2-18x+4$
- $9x^2-12x+4$

## Explanation

The first and last terms have bases $3x$ and $2$. Twice their product with a negative sign is $-12x$. The three terms therefore match the square of $3x-2$.

$$
(3x-2)^2=9x^2-12x+4
$$

With bases $3x$ and $2$, the square of their difference has cross term $-2\cdot3x\cdot2=-12x$. Check all three coefficients separately: the product of the terms in $x$ gives $9x^2$, and multiplying the negative constants gives $4$.
