Which polynomial does $(t+3)^2-(t-3)^2$ give when the entire binomials are used as bases?

- $36$
- $6t$
- $12t$
- $4t^2$

## Explanation

The difference of the two bases is $(t+3)-(t-3)=6$. Their sum is $(t+3)+(t-3)=2t$. The product of the two factors is therefore $6\cdot2t=12t$. Expanding the squares separately shows that the terms $t^2$ and $9$ cancel, while the cross terms are subtracted as $6t-(-6t)$.
