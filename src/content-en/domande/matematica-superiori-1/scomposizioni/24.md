Which product equals $x^2+8x+12$?

- $\left(x - 6\right) \left(x - 2\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(x + 12\right)$
- $x \left(x + 8\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 6\right)$

## Explanation

Find two numbers whose sum is the coefficient of x and whose product is the constant term. The two numbers are the given number and two. Multiplying the two binomials recovers the trinomial.

$$
6+2=8,\quad 6\cdot2=12,\quad x^2+8x+12=(x+6)(x+2)
$$

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 6\right)$.
