A parabola with equation $y=x^2+bx+c$ crosses the horizontal axis at $-5$ and $2$. Which expanded form corresponds to it?

- $x^2-3x-10$
- $x^2+3x+10$
- $x^2+7x+10$
- $x^2+3x-10$

## Explanation

The monic binomial that vanishes at $-5$ is $x+5$, and the one that vanishes at $2$ is $x-2$. Their product $(x+5)(x-2)$ has leading coefficient one as required. The cross products add to $3x$, and the constants multiply to $-10$. The zeros have the opposite signs to the constants in the factors.
