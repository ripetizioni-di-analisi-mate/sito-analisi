How can $x^4+4$ be factored by adding and subtracting the same monomial?

- $(x^2-2)(x^2+2)$
- $(x^2+2)^2$
- $(x^2-2x+2)(x^2+2x+2)$
- $(x^2-2x)(x^2+2x)$

## Explanation

Adding and subtracting $4x^2$ produces $(x^2+2)^2-(2x)^2$. The difference of squares gives the two factors.
