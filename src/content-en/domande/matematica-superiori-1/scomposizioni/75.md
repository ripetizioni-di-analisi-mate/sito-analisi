A rectangle has area $12t^2+18t$ and height $6t$, with $t>0$. Which expression gives its width?

- $2t+3t$
- $6t+9$
- $2t+3$
- $2t^2+3t$

## Explanation

The area is the product of height and width. So factor out the known height $6t$ from both area terms. The first gives $2t$ and the second $3$, hence $12t^2+18t=6t(2t+3)$. Geometrically, the two terms are the areas of adjacent rectangles with equal heights and widths $2t$ and $3$.
