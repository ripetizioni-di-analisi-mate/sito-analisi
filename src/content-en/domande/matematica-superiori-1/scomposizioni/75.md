A rectangle has area $12t^2+18t$ and height $6t$, with $t>0$. Which expression gives its base?

- $2t+3t$
- $6t+9$
- $2t+3$
- $2t^2+3t$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the height and the base, therefore the known height $6t$ is factored out of the two terms of the area. The first term divided by $6t$ gives $2t$ and the second gives $3$, so $12t^2+18t=6t(2t+3)$. The base of the rectangle is therefore $2t+3$. Geometrically, the two terms are the areas of two adjacent rectangles with the same height $6t$ and bases $2t$ and $3$.
