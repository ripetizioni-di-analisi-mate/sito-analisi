Factor $xy+3x+2y+6$.

- $\left(x + 3\right) \left(y + 2\right)$
- $\left(x - 2\right) \left(y + 3\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 3\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y - 3\right)$

## Explanation

Group the first two terms and the last two terms. Factor each group to leave the same binomial. Then factor out the common binomial.

$$
xy+3x+2y+6=x(y+3)+2(y+3)=(x+2)(y+3)
$$

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 3\right)$.

Grouping by $y$ would give $y(x+2)+3(x+2)$. The final product is the same because the two binomials can be multiplied in either order.
