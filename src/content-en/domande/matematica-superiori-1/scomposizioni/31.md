Which pair of binomials with positive integer coefficients passes the middle-term check when factoring $6x^2+7x+2$?

- $(6x+1)(x+2)$
- $(6x+2)(x+1)$
- $(3x+2)(2x+1)$
- $(3x+1)(2x+3)$

## Explanation

In $(3x+2)(2x+1)$ the cross terms are $3x$ and $4x$. Their sum is $7x$, while the outer terms already give $6x^2$ and $2$.
