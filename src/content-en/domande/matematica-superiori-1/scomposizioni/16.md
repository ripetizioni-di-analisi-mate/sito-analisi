After factoring $6x^2$ out of $18x^3+ax^2$, the remaining factor is $3x-5$. What is $a$?

- $-5$
- $5$
- $30$
- $-30$

## Explanation

The distributive property multiplies the outer factor by both terms. Here $6x^2\cdot3x=18x^3$ gives the cubic term, while $6x^2\cdot(-5)=-30x^2$ gives the second term. The required coefficient is therefore $-30$. The number $-5$ is the coefficient inside the brackets, and still needs to be multiplied by the numerical part of the outer factor.
