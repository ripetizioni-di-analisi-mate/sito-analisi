For what value of $c$ does the identity $x^2+6x+c=(x+3)(x+3)$ hold?

- $6$
- $0$
- $10$
- $9$

## Explanation

Expand the product of the two binomials. The constant term comes from multiplying their constant terms. The identity requires c to equal that product.

$$
(x+3)(x+3)=x^2+6x+9,\quad c=9
$$

The result is $9$.

Setting $x=0$ isolates the constant term and immediately gives $c=3\cdot3=9$. The two cross products then check the stated linear coefficient.
