What term should replace $\square$ to give $25a^2+\square+4b^2=(5a+2b)^2$?

- $10ab$
- $20a^2b^2$
- $-20ab$
- $20ab$

## Explanation

In the square $(5a+2b)(5a+2b)$, the cross terms are $(5a)(2b)$ and $(2b)(5a)$. Each is $10ab$, so their sum is $20ab$. When $a$ and $b$ are positive, the same calculation gives the combined area of two rectangles with sides $5a$ and $2b$.
