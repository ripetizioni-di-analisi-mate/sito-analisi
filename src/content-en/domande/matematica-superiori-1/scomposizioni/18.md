Complete the square in $x^2-8x+20$. What can you conclude about its real zeros?

- One real zero, $4$.
- No real zeros: the trinomial is always at least $4$.
- Two real zeros, $2$ and $6$.
- One real zero, $-4$.

## Explanation

The middle term $-8x$ corresponds to the base $x-4$. Squaring it produces the constant term $16$, so $x^2-8x+20=(x-4)^2+4$. The square is always nonnegative, and when it vanishes the trinomial still equals $4$. No real number can therefore make the whole trinomial equal zero.
