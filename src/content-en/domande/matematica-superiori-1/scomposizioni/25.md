Factor $x^2+x-20$.

- $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 5\right)$
- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 4\right)$
- $\left(x - 2\right) \left(x + 10\right)$
- $\left(x + 4\right) \left(x + 5\right)$

## Explanation

The product $-20$ requires opposite signs. The numbers $5$ and $-4$ have that product and the required sum, $1$.

$$
(x+5)(x-4)=x^2+x-20
$$

A negative product requires opposite signs. Among positive factor pairs of $20$, only $4$ and $5$ differ by one. The larger gets the positive sign to give $-4+5=1$, and $(-4)\cdot5$ confirms the constant term.
