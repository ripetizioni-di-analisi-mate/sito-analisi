A classmate proposes $(x-5)(x+4)$ for $x^2+x-20$. Which change corrects the linear term while keeping the constant term?

- Reverse the signs of both constants
- Swap the order of the factors
- Reverse only the sign of $5$
- Reverse only the sign of $4$

## Explanation

The proposed constants sum to $-1$ and multiply to $-20$. Using $5,-4$ keeps the product and gives the required sum $1$.
