Given $P(x,y)=x^2y+3xy^2$, which relationship makes $P$ zero for every real $y$?

- $x=3y$
- $x=-3y$
- $x=-y$
- $x=-3$

## Explanation

Factoring out $xy$ gives $P(x,y)=xy(x+3y)$. The relationship $x=-3y$ makes the final factor zero regardless of $y$. One successful case cannot establish a relationship for every $y$. For example, with $x=-3$ the product is zero at $y=1$, while at $y=2$ it is $(-3)\cdot2\cdot3=-18$.
