A monic cubic has a double zero at $2$ and also vanishes at $-3$. Which product represents it?

- $(x-2)^2(x+3)$
- $(x+2)^2(x-3)$
- $(x-2)(x+3)^2$
- $2(x-2)^2(x+3)$

## Explanation

The zero $2$ gives the factor $x-2$, and its being double requires two copies of that factor. The zero $-3$ gives $x+3$. The product $(x-2)^2(x+3)$ has degree three and leading coefficient one, as required. Adding an outer factor of $2$ would preserve the zeros but change the leading coefficient.
