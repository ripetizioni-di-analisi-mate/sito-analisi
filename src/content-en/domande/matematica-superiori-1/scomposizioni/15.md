What value of $c$ lets $4x^2+cx+25$ be written as $(2x+5)^2$?

- $10$
- $25$
- $20$
- $-20$

## Explanation

The squares of the two terms are already present. The middle coefficient is twice the product of the coefficients $2$ and $5$.

$$
2\cdot(2x)\cdot5=20x
$$
