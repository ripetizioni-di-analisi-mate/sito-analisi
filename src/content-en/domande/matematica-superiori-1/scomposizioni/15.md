What value of $c$ lets $4x^2+cx+25$ be written as $(2x+5)^2$?

- $10$
- $25$
- $20$
- $-20$

## Explanation

The squares of the two terms are already present. The middle coefficient is twice the product of the coefficients $2$ and $5$.

$$
2\cdot(2x)\cdot5=20x
$$

The product has two contributions of $10x$, one for each choice of which factor provides $2x$. Reversing the sign of the middle term would instead give the square of $2x-5$.
