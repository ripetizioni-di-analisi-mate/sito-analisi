Given $u+v=12$ and $u^2-v^2=48$, what is $u-v$?

- $6$
- $12$
- $36$
- $4$

## Explanation

Factoring the difference of squares gives $(u-v)(u+v)=48$. The second factor is already known to be $12$, which is nonzero. It follows that $u-v=48/12=4$. The same information would give $u=8$ and $v=4$, which check the difference through $64-16=48$.
