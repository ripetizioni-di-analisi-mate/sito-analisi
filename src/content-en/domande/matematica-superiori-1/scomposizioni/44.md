Which product is a factorization of $4x^2-36y^2$?

- $\left(2 x - 6 y\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x + 6 y\right) \left(4 x - 6 y\right)$
- $\left(2 x - 36 y\right) \left(2 x + y\right)$
- $\left(2 x - 6 y\right) \left(2 x + 6 y\right)$

## Explanation

The bases of the two squares are twice x and $6 y$. Factor the difference of squares into the product of the sum and the difference of those bases. Take the square root of the coefficient four too.

$$
4x^2-36y^2=(2x-6y)(2x+6y)
$$

The result is $\left(2 x - 6 y\right) \left(2 x + 6 y\right)$.
