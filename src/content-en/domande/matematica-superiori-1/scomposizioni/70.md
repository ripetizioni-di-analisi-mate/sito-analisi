What is the least common multiple of $4x^2$ and $6xy^2$ among monomials with a positive integer coefficient?

- $24 x^{3} y^{2}$
- $12 x^{2} y^{2}$
- $12 x y^{2}$
- $2 x^{2} y^{2}$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the LCM of $4$ and $6$, namely $12$. The greatest exponents are $2$ for $x$ and $2$ for $y$. The result contains the powers of each monomial.

The first monomial has no $y$, but the second has two factors of $y$, which a common multiple must therefore include. The coefficient $12$ is the smallest positive common multiple, and dividing by the given monomials yields $3y^2$ and $2x$.
