What is the least common monomial multiple of $6x^2y$ and $9xy^3$ with a positive integer coefficient?

- $3 x y$
- $18 x^{2} y^{3}$
- $18 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $15 x^{2} y^{3}$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the least common multiple of the numerical coefficients. For each variable that occurs, take its greatest exponent. The result contains the factors needed to be a multiple of both.

$$
\operatorname{lcm}=18x^2y^3
$$

The result is $18 x^{2} y^{3}$.

To reach the result from the first monomial, multiply by $3y^2$, and from the second multiply by $2x$. This checks divisibility by both.
