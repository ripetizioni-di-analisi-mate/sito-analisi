For which real values of $k$ is $x^2+kx+49$ the square of a binomial with real coefficients?

- $k=\pm49$
- $k=\pm14$
- $k=\pm7$
- $k=\pm28$

## Explanation

The leading coefficient and constant term fix the linear part of the base at $x$ or $-x$, and its constant term at $7$ or $-7$. Reversing the sign of the whole binomial leaves its square unchanged, so it is enough to consider $(x+7)^2$ and $(x-7)^2$. Their middle terms are $14x$ and $-14x$, giving the only two possible values of $k$.
