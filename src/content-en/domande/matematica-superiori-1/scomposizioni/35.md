Factor $x(x-3)+2(3-x)$.

- $\left(x - 3\right) \left(x + 2\right)$
- $\left(x - 2\right) \left(x + 3\right)$
- $\left(x - 3\right) \left(x - 2\right)$
- $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x - 3\right)$

## Explanation

The binomial $3-x$ is the additive inverse of $x-3$. Rewrite the second term as $-2(x-3)$ and factor out the common binomial.

$$
x(x-3)-2(x-3)=(x-3)(x-2)
$$
