The four terms of $ax+ay+bx+by$ are areas of rectangles arranged in two rows and two columns. What are the dimensions of the entire rectangle?

- $a+y,\ b+x$
- $ab,\ xy$
- $a+b,\ x+y$
- $a+x,\ b+y$

## Explanation

Use $a,b$ as row heights and $x,y$ as column widths. Adding each pair of dimensions gives the product $(a+b)(x+y)$.
