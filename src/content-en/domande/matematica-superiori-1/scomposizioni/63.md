The GCD of $12x^5y$ and $18x^ay^3$ is $6x^2y$. What is the nonnegative integer exponent $a$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $2$
- $7$

## Explanation

The common divisor uses the smaller exponent of each variable. For $x$ this gives $\min(5,a)=2$. Since $5$ is greater than $2$, the minimum comes from the second monomial and requires $a=2$. The other data are consistent as well, since the GCD of $12,18$ is $6$ and $\min(1,3)=1$.
