What is the greatest common monomial divisor of $30x^3y^2$ and $45x^2y^4$ with a positive integer coefficient?

- $90 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $15 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $15 x^{2} y^{2}$
- $5 x y$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the greatest common divisor of the numerical coefficients. For each shared variable, take the smaller of the exponents. The resulting monomial divides both given monomials.

$$
\gcd=15x^2y^2
$$

The result is $15 x^{2} y^{2}$.
