Someone proposes $66x^2y^2$ as the least common multiple of $22x^2y$ and $33xy^3$. Which change is essential?

- Lower the exponent of $y$ to $1$
- Raise the exponent of $y$ to $3$
- Lower the exponent of $x$ to $1$
- Replace $66$ by $11$

## Explanation

The second monomial contains $y^3$ and cannot divide a monomial containing only $y^2$. The coefficient $66$ and factor $x^2$ are already sufficient, giving $66x^2y^3$.
