You know that $x^2-11x+24$ factors into two monic binomials with integer coefficients. Which pair of constant terms must appear?

- $(-4,-6)$
- $(3,8)$
- $(-3,-8)$
- $(-3,8)$

## Explanation

The product $24$ forces the two constants to have the same sign. Their negative sum $-11$ makes both constants negative. Among the pairs of absolute values with product $24$, only $3$ and $8$ add to $11$. Checking gives $(-3)+(-8)=-11$ and $(-3)(-8)=24$, so the required product is $(x-3)(x-8)$.
