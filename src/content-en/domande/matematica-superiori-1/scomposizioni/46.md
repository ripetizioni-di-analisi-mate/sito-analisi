Factor $-3x^2-6x$.

- $3 x \left(x + 2\right)$
- $- 3 x \left(x + 2\right)$
- $- 3 x \left(x - 2\right)$
- $- 3 x - 6$

## Explanation

The common factor may be chosen negative. Dividing both terms by minus $3 x$ gives x and two. Applying the distributive property gives two negative terms again.

$$
-3x^2-6x=-3x(x+2)
$$

The result is $- 3 x \left(x + 2\right)$.
