Which factor of $x^4-81$ is positive for every real $x$?

- $x^2+9$
- $x^2-9$
- $x-3$
- $x+3$

## Explanation

The first difference of squares gives $x^2-9$ and $x^2+9$. The second factor is at least $9$. All the other proposed factors vanish at some real input.

$$
x^4-81=(x-3)(x+3)(x^2+9)
$$

The square $x^2$ reaches its minimum at $0$, so $x^2+9$ has minimum $9$. At the same input $x^2-9$ equals $-9$, and the two linear factors change sign across their respective zeros.
