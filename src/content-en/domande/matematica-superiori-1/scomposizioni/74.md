For $x\ne 0$ and $y\ne 0$, which expression equals $\dfrac{14x^2y-21xy^2+7xy}{7xy}$?

- $2x^2-3y^2+1$
- $2x-3y+1$
- $2x-3y$
- $2x+3y+1$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator share the factor $7xy$. To write the numerator as a product, divide each of its terms by that factor. The first gives $2x$ and the second $-3y$. The last term is the divisor itself, so it gives $1$, which stays in the brackets. Since $x$ and $y$ are nonzero, the shared factor cancels in the quotient.
