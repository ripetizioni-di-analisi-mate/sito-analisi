Which statement describes the graph of $P(x)=-3(x-4)^2$ near $x=4$?

- It touches the axis at $4$ and lies above it on both sides.
- It touches the axis at $4$ and lies below it on both sides.
- It crosses the axis at $4$, from below to above.
- It crosses the axis at $4$, from above to below.

## Explanation

The factor $(x-4)^2$ vanishes at $4$ and is positive at every other real input. Multiplying by $-3$ makes the product negative on both sides of $4$. For example, $P(3)=P(5)=-3$. The sign stays the same across the zero, so the parabola touches the axis at its highest point and otherwise lies below it.
