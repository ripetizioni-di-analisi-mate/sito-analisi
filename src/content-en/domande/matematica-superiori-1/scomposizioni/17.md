At what point does the graph of $P(x)=16x^2-40x+25$ touch the horizontal axis?

- $\left(\frac54,0\right)$
- $\left(-\frac54,0\right)$
- $\left(\frac45,0\right)$
- $\left(0,\frac54\right)$

## Explanation

The outside terms are $(4x)^2$ and $5^2$, and the middle term is $-2\cdot4x\cdot5$. Thus $P(x)=(4x-5)^2$. The base vanishes at $x=5/4$, while the square is positive to the left and right of that value. The parabola therefore touches the axis at $(5/4,0)$ and stays on the same side.
