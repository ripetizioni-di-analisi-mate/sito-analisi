Factor $x^4-1296$ completely.

- $\left(x - 6\right)^{2} \left(x^{2} + 36\right)$
- $\left(x^{2} - 36\right)^{2}$
- $\left(x - 6\right) \left(x + 6\right)$
- $\left(x - 6\right) \left(x + 6\right) \left(x^{2} + 36\right)$

## Explanation

First apply the difference of squares with bases x squared and $6$ squared. The factor containing the minus sign is itself a difference of squares. Factor it further.

$$
x^4-1296=(x^2-36)(x^2+36)=(x-6)(x+6)(x^2+36)
$$

The result is $\left(x - 6\right) \left(x + 6\right) \left(x^{2} + 36\right)$.
