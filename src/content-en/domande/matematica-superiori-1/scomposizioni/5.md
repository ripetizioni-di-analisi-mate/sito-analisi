The constant in $x^2+13x+22$ allows the positive pairs $(1,22)$ and $(2,11)$. Which piece of information decides between them?

- Their sum must be $13$
- Their difference must be $13$
- Their ratio must be $13$
- Their sum must be $22$

## Explanation

In $(x+r)(x+s)$ the linear coefficient is $r+s$. Of the two pairs, only $2+11$ equals $13$, giving factors $x+2$ and $x+11$.
