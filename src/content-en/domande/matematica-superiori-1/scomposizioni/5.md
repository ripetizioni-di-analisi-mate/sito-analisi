What is the highest-degree monomial common to $18x^3y^2$ and $24xy^4$ with the greatest positive integer coefficient?

- $6 x y^{2}$
- $6 x^{3} y^{4}$
- $12 x y^{2}$
- $6 x y$

## Explanation

The greatest common coefficient is $6$, the GCD of $18$ and $24$. The common power of $x$ is $x$, and the common power of $y$ is $y^2$, because each uses the smaller exponent.

Dividing by $6xy^2$ gives $3x^2$ and $4y^2$. They have coprime coefficients and no variable in common, so there is no further factor left to extract from both.
