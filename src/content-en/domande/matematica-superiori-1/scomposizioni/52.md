On which interval is the graph of $P(x)=(x+5)(x-2)$ below the horizontal axis?

- $x<-5$
- $x>2$
- $-5\le x\le2$
- $-5<x<2$

## Explanation

To the left of $-5$ both factors are negative, making the product positive. Between $-5$ and $2$, $x+5$ is positive while $x-2$ is negative. To the right of $2$ both are positive. At the endpoints the product is zero, so the part below the axis is the open interval $(-5,2)$.
