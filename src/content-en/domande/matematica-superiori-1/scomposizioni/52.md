The polynomial $P(x)=(x+4)(x-1)$ is already factored. What is $P(-4)$?

- $4$
- $1$
- $-4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Substituting the opposite of the number into the first factor makes it zero. A product is zero if one factor is zero. The value can be found directly from the factored polynomial.

$$
P(-4)=(-4+4)(-4-1)=0
$$

The result is $0$.
