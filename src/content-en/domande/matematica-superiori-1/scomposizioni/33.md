In $xy+5x-2y-10$, the two terms containing $y$ are placed in one group. Which line correctly factors each group?

- $y(x-2)+5(x-2)$
- $y(x-2)+5(x+2)$
- $y(x+2)+5(x-2)$
- $y(x+5)-2(x+5)$

## Explanation

The terms containing $y$ give $xy-2y=y(x-2)$. The others give $5x-10=5(x-2)$. After checking the signs in both groups, factor out $x-2$ to get $(x-2)(y+5)$. Grouping the terms containing $x$ would give $x(y+5)-2(y+5)$ and lead to the same final product.
