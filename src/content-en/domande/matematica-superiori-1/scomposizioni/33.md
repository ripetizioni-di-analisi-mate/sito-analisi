Factor $xy+5x+2y+10$.

- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 5\right)$
- $\left(x + 5\right) \left(y + 2\right)$
- $\left(x - 2\right) \left(y + 5\right)$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y - 5\right)$

## Explanation

Group the first two terms and the last two terms. Factor each group to leave the same binomial. Then factor out the common binomial.

$$
xy+5x+2y+10=x(y+5)+2(y+5)=(x+2)(y+5)
$$

The result is $\left(x + 2\right) \left(y + 5\right)$.
