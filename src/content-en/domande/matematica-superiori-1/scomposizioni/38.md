Factor $5x^3-5x$.

- $5 x \left(x - 1\right)^{2}$
- $5 x \left(x - 1\right) \left(x + 1\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(5 x - 5\right)$
- $5 x \left(x^{2} + 1\right)$

## Explanation

First factor out the common monomial. The remaining factor is a difference of squares. Factor it too to complete the calculation.

$$
5x^3-5x=5x(x^2-1)=5x(x-1)(x+1)
$$

The result is $5 x \left(x - 1\right) \left(x + 1\right)$.
