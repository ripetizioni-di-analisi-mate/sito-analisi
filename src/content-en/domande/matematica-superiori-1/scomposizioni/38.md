The product $5x(x^2-16)$ equals $5x^3-80x$. For which values of $x$ is the quotient $\dfrac{5x^3-80x}{5x}$ defined and equal to $x^2-16$?

- For all real numbers.
- For all real numbers except $0$.
- For all real numbers except $-4$ and $4$.
- Only for $-4$ and $4$.

## Explanation

The distributive property verifies the polynomial identity for every $x$. In the quotient, however, the denominator $5x$ vanishes at $0$, where division is undefined. At every other value you can cancel the factor $5x$. Both $x=-4$ and $x=4$ are allowed, since at those values the numerator equals zero.
