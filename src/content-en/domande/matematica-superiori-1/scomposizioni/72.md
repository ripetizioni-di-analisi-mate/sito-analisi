For $x\ne0$, what quotient results when $4x$ is factored out of $4x^2+12x$?

- $x + 12$
- $4 x + 3$
- $x - 3$
- $x + 3$

## Explanation

Factoring out a term means dividing each term by that factor. The first term gives x and the second gives three. The remaining polynomial is their sum.

$$
\frac{4x^2+12x}{4x}=x+3
$$

The result is $x + 3$.

The term $12x$ leaves the constant $3$ because the factor $x$ has already been extracted. The quotient is defined when the divisor is nonzero.
