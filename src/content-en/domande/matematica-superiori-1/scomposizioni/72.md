You need the monic polynomial of least degree divisible by both $(x-1)^2$ and $x+1$. Which do you choose?

- $(x-1)(x+1)$
- $(x-1)^2(x+1)^2$
- $x^2-1+x$
- $(x-1)^2(x+1)$

## Explanation

The first divisor requires two copies of $x-1$, and the second requires at least one copy of the distinct factor $x+1$. Taking exactly those three gives degree $3$, the smallest possible.
