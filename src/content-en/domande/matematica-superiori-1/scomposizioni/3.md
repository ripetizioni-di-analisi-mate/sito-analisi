After spotting $x^2$ and $11^2$ in $x^2+22x+121$, what must still be checked to establish that it is a square?

- $22x=x^2+11^2$
- $22x=(x+11)^2$
- $22x=2\cdot x\cdot11$
- $22x=x\cdot11$

## Explanation

The outer terms suggest bases $x$ and $11$. Checking twice their product, including its sign, confirms that the trinomial is $(x+11)^2$.
