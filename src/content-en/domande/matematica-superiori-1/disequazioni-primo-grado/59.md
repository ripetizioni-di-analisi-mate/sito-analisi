Solve $6-2x>18$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(-6, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right]$

## Explanation

Subtract the initial constant term from both sides. The coefficient of the remaining variable term is negative. Dividing by that coefficient reverses the inequality sign.

$$
-2x>12,\qquad x<-6
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, -6\right)$.
