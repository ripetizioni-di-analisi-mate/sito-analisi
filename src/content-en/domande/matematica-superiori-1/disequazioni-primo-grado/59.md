For which nonzero constants $a$ is the implication “if $ax>ay$, then $x>y$” true for every pair of real numbers $x,y$?

- $a<0$
- $a\ne0$
- $a>0$
- $a=1$

## Explanation

When $a$ is positive, division by $a$ preserves the inequality. When $a$ is negative, division gives $x<y$. For any $a<0$, choose $x=1$ and $y=2$. Then $a>2a$ is true but $1>2$ is false. The condition therefore covers all positive constants, including values other than $1$. The condition must hold for every pair, so this counterexample rules out every negative constant.
