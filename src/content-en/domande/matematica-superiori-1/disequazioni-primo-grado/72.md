Two parallel lines have heights $A=3x+5$ and $B=3x+2$. How much must be added to the constant term of $B$ for $A<B$ to hold for every $x$?

- Exactly $3$
- Any positive amount
- An amount less than $3$
- Any amount greater than $3$

## Explanation

The original gap $A-B$ is constantly $3$. Adding exactly $3$ makes the lines coincide, whereas adding more puts $B$ above $A$ everywhere.
