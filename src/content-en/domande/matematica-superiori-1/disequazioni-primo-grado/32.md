Which real numbers satisfy both $2x-1\ge5$ and $\dfrac{x+1}{2}\le2$?

- $\varnothing$
- $[3,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,3]$
- $\{3\}$

## Explanation

The first condition becomes $2x\ge6$, so $x\ge3$. The second becomes $x+1\le4$, so $x\le3$. The only value that is both at least and at most $3$ is exactly $3$. Substitution gives two equalities, both allowed. Every smaller value violates the first comparison, while every larger value violates the second.
