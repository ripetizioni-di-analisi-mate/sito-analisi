How many integers satisfy $6<x\le 12$?

- $5$
- $7$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

The first admissible integer follows the lower bound. The integer equal to the upper bound is included. There are six consecutive integers in the list.

$$
x\in\{7,8,9,10,11,12\}
$$

The result is $6$.
