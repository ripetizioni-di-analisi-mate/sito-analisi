On the grid $x=\dfrac n2$ with integer $n$, what is the first point strictly greater than $1.6$?

- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac74$
- $\dfrac52$
- $2$

## Explanation

The condition is equivalent to $n>3.2$, so the first integer index is $4$. Although $\dfrac74$ exceeds $1.6$, it does not belong to the grid.
