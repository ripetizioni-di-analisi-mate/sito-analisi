Solve $2x+4>12$. What is the solution set?

- $\left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right]$

## Explanation

Subtract $4$ from both sides. Then divide by two, which is positive, so the sign is preserved. The inequality is strict and excludes the boundary value.

$$
2x>8,\qquad x>4
$$

The result is $\left(4, \infty\right)$.
