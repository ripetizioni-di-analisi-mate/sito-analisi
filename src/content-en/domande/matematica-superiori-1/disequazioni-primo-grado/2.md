Solving $-3x\le33$ gives $x\ge-11$. Which comparison demonstrates why multiplying by $-3$ reverses order?

- $-10=-11,\quad 30=33$
- $-10>-11,\quad 30<33$
- $-10>-11,\quad 30>33$
- $-10<-11,\quad 30<33$

## Explanation

The larger input, $-10$, gives the smaller product, $30$. Equality still holds at $x=-11$.
