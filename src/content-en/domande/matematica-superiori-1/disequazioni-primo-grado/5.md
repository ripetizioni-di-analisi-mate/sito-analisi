For which value of $a$ is $(3,+\infty)$ the solution set of $2x+a>11$?

- $5$
- $8$
- $6$
- $17$

## Explanation

The boundary occurs where the two sides are equal. Thus $2\cdot3+a=11$ and $a=5$. The coefficient $2$ is positive, so values to the right of $3$ make the left-hand side greater than $11$.
