Which integer is the greatest one allowed by $\dfrac{x+1}{2}\le11$?

- $21$
- $22$
- $20$
- $23$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $2$ gives $x+1\le22$. The value $21$ gives equality, whereas the next integer already gives $\dfrac{23}{2}>11$.
