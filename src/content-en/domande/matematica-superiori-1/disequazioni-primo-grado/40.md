What is the union of $[-3,1)$ and $[1,4]$?

- $[-3,4)\setminus\{1\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $[-3,4]\setminus\{1\}$
- $[-3,4]$

## Explanation

The first interval reaches $1$ without including it, and the second interval contains $1$. Therefore there is no gap between the intervals. Both outer endpoints belong to the union.
