Solve $5x-6\ge2x+12$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 6\right)$
- $\left[6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 6\right]$

## Explanation

Collect the variable terms on the left and the constant terms on the right. The coefficient of the variable becomes three. Dividing by three preserves the inequality sign.

$$
3x\ge 18,\qquad x\ge6
$$

The result is $\left[6, \infty\right)$.
