Which of these numbers satisfies $2x+6>18$?

- $7$
- $6$
- $5$
- $-6$

## Explanation

Subtract $6$ and divide by two to simplify the inequality. The equivalent condition requires x to be strictly greater than $6$. Only the next number satisfies this condition among the listed choices.

$$
2x>12,\qquad x>6
$$

The result is $7$.
