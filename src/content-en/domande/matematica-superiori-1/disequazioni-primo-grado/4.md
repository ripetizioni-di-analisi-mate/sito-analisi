Solve $2x+6>18$. What is the solution set?

- $\left[6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 6\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 6\right]$
- $\left(6, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Subtract $6$ from both sides. Then divide by two, which is positive, so the sign is preserved. The inequality is strict and excludes the boundary value.

$$
2x>12,\qquad x>6
$$

The result is $\left(6, \infty\right)$.
