Which inequality results when $x-4$ is subtracted from both sides of $5x+2\le3x+10$?

- $4x-2\le2x+14$
- $4x+6\ge2x+14$
- $4x+6\le2x+10$
- $4x+6\le2x+14$

## Explanation

Subtracting $x-4$ means adding $-x+4$ to each side. The first side becomes $5x+2-x+4=4x+6$ and the second becomes $3x+10-x+4=2x+14$. The sign remains $\le$ because the same expression has been added to each side. At $x=4$ both forms give equality, so the boundary is still included. The addition of $4$ comes from the minus sign in front of the entire difference.
