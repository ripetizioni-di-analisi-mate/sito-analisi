Let $L=5x-11$ and $R=2x+22$. Their values coincide at $x=11$. Which expression for $L-R$ tells you on which side $L\ge R$?

- $3(11-x)$
- $7(x-11)$
- $3(x+11)$
- $3(x-11)$

## Explanation

The difference is $5x-11-(2x+22)=3x-33$. The factor $3$ is positive, so the difference is nonnegative when $x\ge11$.
