What is the solution set of $2x+3>2x+4$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $x>3$
- $\varnothing$
- $x>4$

## Explanation

Subtracting twice x from both sides makes the variable disappear. The remaining comparison between two consecutive numbers is false. No value of the variable can make it true.

The result is $\varnothing$.
