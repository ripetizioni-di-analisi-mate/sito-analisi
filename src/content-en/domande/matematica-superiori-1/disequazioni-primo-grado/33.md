Which interval represents the conditions $x>5$ and $x\le 9$ holding simultaneously?

- $\left(5, 9\right]$
- $\left[5, 9\right]$
- $\left(5, 9\right)$
- $\left[5, 9\right)$

## Explanation

Both conditions must hold at the same time, so take their intersection. The lower value is excluded and the upper value is included. The points between the endpoints satisfy both conditions.

The result is $\left(5, 9\right]$.
