Solve $\dfrac{x+1}2\le 3$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$
- $\left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right)$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the positive denominator two preserves the sign. Then subtract one to isolate the variable. The endpoint is included because the inequality allows equality.

$$
x+1\le 6,\qquad x\le5
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$.
