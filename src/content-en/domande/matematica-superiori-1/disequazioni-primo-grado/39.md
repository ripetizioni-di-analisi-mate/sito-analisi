The half-lines $(-\infty,1)$ and $(1,+\infty)$ share a boundary. What is their union?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{1\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

Every number below $1$ belongs to the first half-line, and every number above $1$ belongs to the second. The round brackets exclude $1$ in both cases. The union therefore contains every real number except $1$. Sharing a boundary does not include it when both intervals leave it open.
