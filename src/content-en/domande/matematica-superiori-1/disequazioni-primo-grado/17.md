Solve $5x-4\ge2x+8$. What is the solution set?

- $\left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right]$

## Explanation

Collect the variable terms on the left and the constant terms on the right. The coefficient of the variable becomes three. Dividing by three preserves the inequality sign.

$$
3x\ge 12,\qquad x\ge4
$$

The result is $\left[4, \infty\right)$.
