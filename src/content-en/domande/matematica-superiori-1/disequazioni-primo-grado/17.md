Which inequality has solution set $[2,+\infty)$?

- $3x-1\ge x+3$
- $3x-1>x+3$
- $3x+1\ge x+3$
- $x-1\ge3x+3$

## Explanation

In $3x-1\ge x+3$, subtract $x$ and add $1$ to get $2x\ge4$. The coefficient $2$ is positive, so the result is $x\ge2$. A strict comparison would exclude $2$. Changing $-1$ to $+1$ on the left would move the boundary to $1$. The direction matters as well, since the interval extends to the right.
