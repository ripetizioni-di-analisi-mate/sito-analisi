The solution set of $ax+b>0$ is precisely $x>2$. Which conditions must $a,b$ satisfy?

- $a=0,\ b=2$
- $a>0,\ b=-2a$
- $a<0,\ b=-2a$
- $a>0,\ b=2a$

## Explanation

The expression vanishes at the boundary, so $2a+b=0$. It must turn positive to the right, so the slope $a$ is positive.
