Which of these values of $a$ makes $2x+a>2x+5$ an inequality with no solutions?

- $6$
- $7$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

Subtracting $2x$ from both sides leaves $a>5$. For $a=5$, this is false because the comparison is strict. The other three values are greater than $5$.
