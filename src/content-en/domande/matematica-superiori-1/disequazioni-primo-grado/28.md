Solve $\dfrac x2-\dfrac x3>5$. What is the solution set?

- $\left[30, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 30\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 30\right]$
- $\left(30, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The common denominator is six, which is positive. The left-hand side simplifies to x divided by six. Multiplying by six gives the final boundary.

$$
\frac x6>5,\qquad x>30
$$

The result is $\left(30, \infty\right)$.
