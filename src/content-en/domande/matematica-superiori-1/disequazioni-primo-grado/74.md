What is the solution set of $5n+9<5n+12$ in the domain $\{0,1,2\}$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{0,1,2\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{1,2\}$

## Explanation

Subtracting $5n$ from both sides leaves $9<12$, which is true. Each of the three domain values is therefore a solution. All real numbers would be the answer if the original domain were $\mathbb R$. Here the equivalence applies to the values allowed in the question, and the comparison includes $0$ since it gives $9<12$.
