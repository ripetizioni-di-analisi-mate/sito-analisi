Solve $\dfrac{x+1}{2}-\dfrac{x-1}{6}\ge2$.

- $[3,+\infty)$
- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,4]$
- $(4,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $6$ gives $3(x+1)-(x-1)\ge12$. Subtracting the binomial also changes the sign of its term $-1$. The result is $2x+4\ge12$, so $x\ge4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x+3-x+1&\ge12 \\[6pt]
2x&\ge8 \\[6pt]
x&\ge4
\end{aligned}
$$
