You have $32$ euros, with an entrance fee of $8$ euros and a price of $4$ euros per ticket. If at least $6$ euros must remain, what is the maximum number of tickets you can buy?

- $5$
- $6$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Set aside $6$ euros, so total spending can reach $26$ euros. After the entrance fee, at most $18$ euros remain for tickets. The condition is $4n\le18$, or $n\le4.5$. Four tickets leave $8$ euros, while five would leave only $4$. Four is therefore the largest allowed integer count.
