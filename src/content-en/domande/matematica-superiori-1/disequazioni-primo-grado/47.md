A buyer has $14$ euros. Each ticket costs 3 euros. What is the maximum number of tickets the buyer can purchase?

- $5$
- $3$
- $4$
- $14$

## Explanation

The cost of x tickets must be at most the amount available. Dividing by the price gives a value between $4$ and $4$ plus one. Since tickets come in whole numbers, the maximum is $4$.

$$
3x\le 14,\qquad x\le \frac{14}{3},\qquad x_{\max}=4
$$

The result is $4$.
