A copy shop charges $4$ euros per copy for orders of $1$ to $5$. For orders of at least $6$ it charges $3$ euros for **every** copy. With $18$ euros, which quantities can you order?

- $\{1,2,3,4\}$
- $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$
- $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$
- $\{1,2,3,4,6\}$

## Explanation

In the first price band $4n\le18$ allows one to four copies. In the second, $3n\le18$ together with $n\ge6$ allows exactly six. An order of five costs $20$ euros.
