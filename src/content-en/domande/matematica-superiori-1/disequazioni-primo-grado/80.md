For which values of $a$ does $3x-a\le3x-2$ hold for every $x$?

- $a>2$
- $a\le2$
- $a\le-2$
- $a\ge2$

## Explanation

Subtracting $3x$ leaves $-a\le-2$. Multiplying by $-1$ reverses the sign and gives $a\ge2$. Equality is allowed when $a=2$.
