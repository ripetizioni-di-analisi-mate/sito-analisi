Solve $\dfrac{x+1}2\le 6$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(11, \infty\right)$
- $\left[11, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 11\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 11\right]$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the positive denominator two preserves the sign. Then subtract one to isolate the variable. The endpoint is included because the inequality allows equality.

$$
x+1\le 12,\qquad x\le11
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, 11\right]$.
