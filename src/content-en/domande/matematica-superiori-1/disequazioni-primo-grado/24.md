Which value shows that $\dfrac{x+4}{3}>2$ and $x+4>2$ have different solution sets?

- $6$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

In the first comparison, multiplying by $3$ gives $x+4>6$, or $x>2$. The second comparison gives $x>-2$. Zero is above $-2$ and below $2$, so it satisfies only the second comparison. The values $3$ and $6$ exceed both thresholds. The number $-3$ falls below both and does not distinguish the inequalities.
