For which real values of $m$ is the solution set of $(m-4)x\le0$ exactly $[0,+\infty)$?

- $m\le4$
- $m=4$
- $m<4$
- $m>4$

## Explanation

To obtain $x\ge0$ by dividing the product by its coefficient, the sign must reverse. That happens when $m-4<0$, or $m<4$. At $m=4$ the inequality becomes $0\le0$, true for negative numbers as well. For $m>4$ the coefficient is positive and the result is $x\le0$. The specified set therefore requires a strictly negative coefficient.
