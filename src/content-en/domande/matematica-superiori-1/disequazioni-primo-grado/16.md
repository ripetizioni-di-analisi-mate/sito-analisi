For what value of $k$ does the inequality $2x+k\ge11$ have solution set $[4,+\infty)$?

- $7$
- $-3$
- $15$
- $3$

## Explanation

The boundary $4$ must make the two sides equal because the coefficient of $x$ is nonzero. Substituting gives $2\cdot4+k=11$, so $k=3$. With this parameter the inequality becomes $2x\ge8$. Division by the positive constant $2$ gives exactly $x\ge4$, including the required boundary. A value just below $4$ makes the left-hand side smaller than $11$.
