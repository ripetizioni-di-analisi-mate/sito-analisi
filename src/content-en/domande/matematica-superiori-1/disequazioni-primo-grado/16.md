Solve $5x-3\ge2x+6$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Collect the variable terms on the left and the constant terms on the right. The coefficient of the variable becomes three. Dividing by three preserves the inequality sign.

$$
3x\ge 9,\qquad x\ge3
$$

The result is $\left[3, \infty\right)$.
