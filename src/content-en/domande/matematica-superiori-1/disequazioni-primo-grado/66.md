For which real numbers is $\dfrac{2x-1}{3}$ smaller than $\dfrac{x+5}{6}$?

- $x<2$
- $x<\dfrac73$
- $x\le\dfrac73$
- $x>\dfrac73$

## Explanation

To clear the denominators, multiply by $6$, which is positive and preserves the sign. This gives $2(2x-1)<x+5$. Expanding and subtracting $x$ gives $3x<7$. The boundary $\dfrac73$ makes the two fractions equal. Since the first fraction must be strictly smaller, this boundary stays outside the solution set.
