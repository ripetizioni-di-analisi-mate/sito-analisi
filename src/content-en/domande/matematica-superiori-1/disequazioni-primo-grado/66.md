Which of these numbers satisfies $2x+3>9$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Subtract $3$ and divide by two to simplify the inequality. The equivalent condition requires x to be strictly greater than $3$. Only the next number satisfies this condition among the listed choices.

$$
2x>6,\qquad x>3
$$

The result is $4$.
