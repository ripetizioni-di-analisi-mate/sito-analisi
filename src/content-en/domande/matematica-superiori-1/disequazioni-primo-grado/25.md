Which inequality results from clearing the denominator in $\dfrac{5-x}{3}<2$?

- $5-x<6$
- $5-x>6$
- $5-x<2$
- $15-3x<6$

## Explanation

The denominator $3$ is positive. Multiplying both sides by $3$ preserves the inequality sign. The whole numerator $5-x$ remains on the left, and the right-hand side becomes $6$.
