For which real values of $b$ does $(b+1)x\le-2$ hold for every $x\in\mathbb R$?

- $b=-1$
- No value of $b$
- $b<0$
- Every real $b$

## Explanation

Zero belongs to the domain of $x$. Substituting $x=0$ makes the term $(b+1)x$ zero for every parameter $b$. The resulting statement $0\le-2$ is false. Since the requirement covers every real number, this one choice of $x$ that violates the inequality is enough to rule out every value of $b$. This check works without knowing the sign of the coefficient of the variable term.
