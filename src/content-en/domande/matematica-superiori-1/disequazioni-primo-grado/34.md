For which values of $c$ is there at least one real $x$ that satisfies both $x\ge c$ and $x<4$?

- $c\le4$
- $c<4$
- $c>4$
- $c\ge4$

## Explanation

If $c<4$, the value $x=c$ satisfies both conditions and shows that the solution set has at least one element. If $c\ge4$, every $x\ge c$ is also at least $4$, violating $x<4$. Even when $c=4$, the single possible shared endpoint is excluded by the strict bound. Thus $c<4$. Once the lower bound reaches four, moving farther to its right cannot satisfy the strict upper bound.
