Which condition is equivalent to $4(2-x)\le12$?

- $x\le -1$
- $x\ge -1$
- $x\ge 1$
- $x>-1$

## Explanation

Dividing by $4$ preserves the sign and gives $2-x\le3$. Subtract $2$, then multiply by $-1$ and reverse the sign. The endpoint $-1$ gives exactly $12$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2-x&\le 3 \\[6pt]
-x&\le 1 \\[6pt]
x&\ge-1
\end{aligned}
$$
