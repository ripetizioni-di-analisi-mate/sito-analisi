Tickets cost $3$ euros and the budget is $35$ euros. Which pair of comparisons certifies that $11$ is the maximum number you can buy?

- $3\cdot11\le3\cdot12<35$
- $3\cdot11\le35<3\cdot12$
- $3\cdot11<3\cdot12\le35$
- $35\le3\cdot11<3\cdot12$

## Explanation

Eleven tickets cost $33$ euros, which is affordable. A total of twelve would cost $36$ euros, exceeding the budget.
