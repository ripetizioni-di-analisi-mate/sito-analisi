What is the set of real numbers that satisfy both $x>6$ and $x\le4$?

- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\left[4, 6\right]$
- $\emptyset$
- $\left(6, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The first condition requires values above the higher boundary. The second requires values at most the lower boundary. The two rays have no points in common, so the intersection is empty.

The result is $\emptyset$.
