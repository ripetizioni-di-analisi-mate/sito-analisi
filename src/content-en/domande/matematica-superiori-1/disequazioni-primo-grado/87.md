An interval $I$ satisfies $I\cap[0,4]=\{4\}$ and contains $6$. Which number must belong to $I$?

- $0$
- $7$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The intersection tells us that $4$ belongs to $I$. An interval containing $4$ and $6$ contains all points between them, including $5$.
