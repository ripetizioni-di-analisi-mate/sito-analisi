A buyer has $20$ euros. Each ticket costs 3 euros. What is the maximum number of tickets the buyer can purchase?

- $6$
- $7$
- $5$
- $20$

## Explanation

The cost of x tickets must be at most the amount available. Dividing by the price gives a value between $6$ and $6$ plus one. Since tickets come in whole numbers, the maximum is $6$.

$$
3x\le 20,\qquad x\le \frac{20}{3},\qquad x_{\max}=6
$$

The result is $6$.
