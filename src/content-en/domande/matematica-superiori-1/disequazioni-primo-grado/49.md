An activity costs a fixed $8$ euros plus $3$ euros per person. The budget is $47$ euros and there are only $11$ places. What is the maximum number of participants?

- $11$
- $13$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

The budget constraint $8+3n\le47$ gives $3n\le39$, so $n\le13$. Capacity also requires $n\le11$. Satisfying both means using the tighter limit of eleven places. Eleven people cost $41$ euros and fit the budget. Twelve would still fit the budget at $44$ euros, but would exceed the available capacity.
