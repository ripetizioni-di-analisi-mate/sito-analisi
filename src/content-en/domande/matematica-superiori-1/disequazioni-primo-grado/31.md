Suppose $a<b$. Which statement still follows when $a$ and $b$ are both negative?

- $\dfrac1a<\dfrac1b$
- $ab<0$
- $a-b<0$
- $a^2<b^2$

## Explanation

Subtracting $b$ from both sides always preserves order. Two negative numbers have positive product and their squares are in reverse order, as $-3<-2$ shows.
