Which even integers belong to $[-3,6]$?

- $\{-2,2,4,6\}$
- $\{-3,0,2,4,6\}$
- $\{-2,0,2,4,6\}$
- $\{-2,0,2,4\}$

## Explanation

An integer is even if it is twice another integer. The first even integer in the interval is $-2$, since the preceding one is $-4$. Moving in steps of two gives $0,2,4,6$. The number $0=2\cdot0$ is even, and $6$ belongs because the square bracket includes it.
