A classmate claims that $11-2x>33$ implies $x>-11$. Which number on the proposed ray disproves the answer?

- $-11$
- $-12$
- $-20$
- $0$

## Explanation

Zero lies on the proposed ray but gives the false statement $11>33$. Dividing $-2x>22$ by $-2$ actually gives $x<-11$.
