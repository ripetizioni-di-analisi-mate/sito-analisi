What is the minimum of $-4x+1$ over all real numbers $x\le-2$?

- There is no minimum
- $9$
- $8$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x$ is negative, so the value of $-4x+1$ decreases as $x$ increases. The minimum occurs at the largest allowed $x$, namely $-2$. Substituting gives $-4(-2)+1=9$. For every $x<-2$ we have $-4x>8$, so the value of the expression is strictly greater than $9$. The minimum of the expression is therefore at the right-hand end of the allowed interval.
