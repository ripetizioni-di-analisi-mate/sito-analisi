A buyer has $11$ euros. Each ticket costs 3 euros. What is the maximum number of tickets the buyer can purchase?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $11$

## Explanation

The cost of x tickets must be at most the amount available. Dividing by the price gives a value between $3$ and $3$ plus one. Since tickets come in whole numbers, the maximum is $3$.

$$
3x\le 11,\qquad x\le \frac{11}{3},\qquad x_{\max}=3
$$

The result is $3$.
