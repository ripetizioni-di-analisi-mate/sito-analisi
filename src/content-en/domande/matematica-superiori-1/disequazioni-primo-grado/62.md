Sensor A is active for $t\in[1,5]$ and sensor B for $t\in[3,7]$. For how many time units are both active?

- $6$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The overlap extends from $3$ to $5$. Duration is the difference $5-3=2$, even when both endpoint instants are included.
