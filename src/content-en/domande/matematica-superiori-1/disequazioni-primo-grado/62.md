Consider the system
$$
\begin{cases}
1\le x\le 5\\
3\le x\le 7
\end{cases}
$$
How long is the interval of its solutions?

- $6$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The solutions of the system are the numbers that satisfy both double inequalities, so they form the intersection $[1,5]\cap[3,7]=[3,5]$. The length of an interval is the difference between the right endpoint and the left endpoint, hence it equals $5-3=2$. The value $3$ is the number of integers $3$, $4$ and $5$ contained in the interval, which differs from its length. The value $6$ is the length of the union $[1,7]$.
