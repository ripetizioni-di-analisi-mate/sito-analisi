Which of these numbers satisfies $2x+11>33$?

- $-11$
- $12$
- $11$
- $10$

## Explanation

In $2x+11>33$, using $12$ gives $35>33$. The candidate $11$ gives only equality, and the two smaller inputs give an even smaller left-hand side.
