A bus has $27$ seats and leaves only if there are more than $18$ passengers and at least $4$ seats remain free. How many different numbers of passengers allow the bus to leave?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the number of passengers, which is an integer. The free seats are $27-n$, and the two conditions must hold together:
$$
\begin{cases}
n>18\\
27-n\ge4
\end{cases}
$$
The second inequality is equivalent to $n\le23$, so the system gives $18<n\le23$. The admissible integers are $19$, $20$, $21$, $22$ and $23$, therefore the numbers of passengers that allow the bus to leave are $5$. The answer $9$ ignores the condition on the free seats, while the answer $6$ also includes $n=18$.
