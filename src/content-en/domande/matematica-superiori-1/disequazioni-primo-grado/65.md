Solve $-1\le5-2x<7$.

- $(-1,3]$
- $[-1,3)$
- $(-3,1]$
- $[-1,3]$

## Explanation

Subtract $5$ from all three expressions and divide by $-2$. Both inequality signs reverse. In increasing order, the values satisfy $-1<x\le3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-6&\le-2x<2 \\[6pt]
3&\ge x>-1
\end{aligned}
$$
