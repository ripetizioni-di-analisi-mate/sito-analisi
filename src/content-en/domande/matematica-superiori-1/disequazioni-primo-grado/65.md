A bus leaves when its whole number of passengers exceeds $18$ and at least $4$ of its $27$ seats are empty. How many different passenger counts allow it to leave?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

The two conditions give $18<n\le23$. The possible integers are $19,20,21,22,23$, so there are five counts.
