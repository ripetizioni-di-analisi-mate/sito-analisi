What is the intersection of $(-\infty,2]$ and $[2,5)$?

- $\varnothing$
- $[2,5)$
- $\{2\}$
- $(-\infty,5)$

## Explanation

The first set contains only values at most $2$. The second contains only values at least $2$. The value $2$ belongs to both and satisfies both conditions.
