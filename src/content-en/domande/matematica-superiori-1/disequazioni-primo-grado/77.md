Which choice of $c$ makes the implication “for every real $x$, if $x\le c$ then $x<5$” true?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

The entire interval $(-\infty,c]$ must fit inside $(-\infty,5)$. This requires $c<5$, since even $c=5$ would allow the counterexample $x=5$.
