Solve $5x-5\ge2x+10$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$

## Explanation

Collect the variable terms on the left and the constant terms on the right. The coefficient of the variable becomes three. Dividing by three preserves the inequality sign.

$$
3x\ge 15,\qquad x\ge5
$$

The result is $\left[5, \infty\right)$.
