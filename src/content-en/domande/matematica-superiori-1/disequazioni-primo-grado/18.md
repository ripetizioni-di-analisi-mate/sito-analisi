For which values of $x$ is $x+4$ at least $3$ more than $2x+1$?

- $x<0$
- $x\le0$
- $x\le3$
- $x\ge0$

## Explanation

Compare the difference $(x+4)-(2x+1)$ with $3$. Simplifying gives $3-x\ge3$, or $-x\ge0$. Dividing by $-1$ reverses the sign, so $x\le0$. Zero is included because it gives a difference of exactly three units. At $x=1$ the difference drops to two, below the required amount. Zero is therefore the largest value that makes the first expression at least three units larger.
