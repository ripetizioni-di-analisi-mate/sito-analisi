A real number must satisfy $x\le-1$ or $x\ge3$. It must also satisfy $-3<x\le4$. What is the set of allowed values?

- $[-3,-1]\cup[3,4]$
- $(-3,4]$
- $(-3,-1)\cup(3,4]$
- $(-3,-1]\cup[3,4]$

## Explanation

Intersect each half-line with $(-3,4]$. The branch $x\le-1$ leaves $(-3,-1]$. The branch $x\ge3$ leaves $[3,4]$. Take the union of the two results because satisfying either branch is enough. Zero meets the outer bounds but neither branch, so the middle stretch between the two intervals is absent from the solution.
