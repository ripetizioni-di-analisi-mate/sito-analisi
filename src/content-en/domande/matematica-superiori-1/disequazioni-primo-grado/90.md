What is the smallest integer that satisfies both $3x>8$ and $x+1\le5$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The first inequality requires $x>\dfrac83$ and the second requires $x\le4$. The intersection contains the integers $3$ and $4$. The smallest is $3$.

$$
\frac83<x\le4
$$
