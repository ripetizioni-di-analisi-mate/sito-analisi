What is the set of real numbers that satisfy at least one of $x<-3$ and $x>3$?

- $\left(-3, 3\right)$
- $\emptyset$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Requiring at least one condition gives a union. Its two parts are the outer rays. Their endpoints are excluded because both inequalities are strict.

The result is $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(3, \infty\right)$.
