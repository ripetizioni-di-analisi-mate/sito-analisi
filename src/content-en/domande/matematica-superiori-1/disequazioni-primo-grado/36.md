Let $a$ be a negative real number. Which comparison in $x$ is equivalent to $ax<5$?

- $x<\dfrac5a$
- $x\ge\dfrac5a$
- $x>-\dfrac5a$
- $x>\dfrac5a$

## Explanation

The parameter $a$ is negative, so it is nonzero and division by $a$ reverses the inequality. The boundary is $\dfrac5a$, which is excluded because multiplying it by $a$ gives exactly $5$. For example, with $a=-1$ the comparison is $-x<5$ and the allowed values are those greater than $-5$.
