A length in centimetres is $L=10+2x$. It may differ from $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ by at most $1\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which values of $x$ are allowed?

- $\left[\dfrac32,\dfrac52\right]$
- $\left(\dfrac32,\dfrac52\right)$
- $[3,5]$
- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac52\right]$

## Explanation

The length is allowed from one centimetre below $14$ to one centimetre above it. Write $13\le L\le15$, or $13\le10+2x\le15$. Subtracting $10$ from all three parts and dividing by $2$ gives $\dfrac32\le x\le\dfrac52$. The endpoints give a difference of exactly one centimetre, which the phrase “at most” allows. All intermediate differences are allowed because length increases linearly with the unknown.
