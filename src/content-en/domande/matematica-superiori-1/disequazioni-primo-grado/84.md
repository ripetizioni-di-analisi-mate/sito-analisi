Solve $-6(x-2)\ge0$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right]$

## Explanation

The factor outside the binomial is negative. Dividing by that factor reverses the inequality sign and gives a condition on the binomial. Finally, add two to both sides.

$$
x-2\le0,\qquad x\le2
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, 2\right]$.
