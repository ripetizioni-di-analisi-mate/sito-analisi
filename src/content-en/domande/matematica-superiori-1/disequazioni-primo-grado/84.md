A rectangle has side lengths $x$ and $x+3$ metres and a perimeter under $26$ metres. What is the interval of allowable values of $x$?

- $[0,5)$
- $(0,5]$
- $(-\infty,5)$
- $(0,5)$

## Explanation

A rectangle has positive side lengths, so require $x>0$. Its perimeter is $2x+2(x+3)=4x+6$. The constraint $4x+6<26$ gives $4x<20$, so $x<5$. Both conditions are required. Zero would make one side vanish, and $5$ would give a perimeter equal to $26$ metres, so both boundary values are excluded.
