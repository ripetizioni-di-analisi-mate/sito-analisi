Solve $2(x-7)<6$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(10, \infty\right)$
- $\left[10, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 10\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 10\right]$

## Explanation

Divide by two, a positive number. Then add $7$ to both sides without reversing the sign. The boundary value does not satisfy the strict inequality.

$$
x-7<3,\qquad x<10
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, 10\right)$.
