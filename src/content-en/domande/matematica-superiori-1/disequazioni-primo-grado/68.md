Which of these numbers satisfies $2x+5>15$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $-5$
- $6$

## Explanation

Subtract $5$ and divide by two to simplify the inequality. The equivalent condition requires x to be strictly greater than $5$. Only the next number satisfies this condition among the listed choices.

$$
2x>10,\qquad x>5
$$

The result is $6$.
