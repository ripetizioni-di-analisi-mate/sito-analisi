How many of $-2$, $0$, $1$, and $3$ satisfy $\dfrac{2-x}{3}>\dfrac12$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Multiplication by $6$ gives $4-2x>3$. After subtracting $4$, divide by $-2$ to get $x<\dfrac12$. Of the four values, only $-2$ and $0$ are to the left of $\dfrac12$, so two are allowed. The first positive value, $1$, already gives $\dfrac13$, which is below $\dfrac12$. The value $3$ is also excluded and gives a negative numerator in the original fraction.
