The first term of a sequence is $8$ and each following term increases by $5$. What is the first position whose term exceeds $38$?

- $7$
- $8$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

At position $n$, there have been $n-1$ increases, giving the term $8+5(n-1)$. The constraint $8+5(n-1)>38$ implies $n-1>6$, so $n>7$. The seventh term equals $38$ exactly and is excluded by the strict comparison. The eighth is $43$ and is the first allowed term because the terms increase by five each time. Checking the preceding index distinguishes the first solution from the later permitted positions.
