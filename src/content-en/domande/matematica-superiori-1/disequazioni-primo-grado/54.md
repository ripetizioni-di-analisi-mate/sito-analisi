A pack contains 4 pencils. How many packs are needed to have at least $25$ pencils?

- $6$
- $7$
- $25$
- $8$

## Explanation

The number of pencils must reach the target. With $6$ packs there is still one pencil missing. One more pack meets the requirement and is the first admissible integer.

$$
4x\ge25,\qquad x\ge\frac{25}{4},\qquad x_{\min}=7
$$

The result is $7$.
