An empty box weighs $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and each kit weighs $7\,\mathrm{kg}$. The shipment must weigh at least $31\,\mathrm{kg}$ and under $45\,\mathrm{kg}$ in total. Which numbers of kits are allowed?

- $\{4,5\}$
- $\{4,5,6\}$
- $\{5,6\}$
- $\{3,4,5\}$

## Explanation

With $n$ kits the total weight is $3+7n$, so write $31\le3+7n<45$. Subtracting $3$ and dividing by $7$ gives $4\le n<6$. Since the number of kits is an integer, only four and five remain. Four kits reach the lower limit of $31$ kilograms exactly, while six reach the excluded limit of $45$.
