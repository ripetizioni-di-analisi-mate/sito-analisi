A pack contains 4 pencils. How many packs are needed to have at least $21$ pencils?

- $6$
- $5$
- $21$
- $7$

## Explanation

The number of pencils must reach the target. With $5$ packs there is still one pencil missing. One more pack meets the requirement and is the first admissible integer.

$$
4x\ge21,\qquad x\ge\frac{21}{4},\qquad x_{\min}=6
$$

The result is $6$.
