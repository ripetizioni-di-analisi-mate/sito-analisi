A lift test requires the load $p$ to exceed $80$ kg by at least $10\%$ of $80$ kg. What is the smallest permitted load?

- $80\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $72\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $88\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The amount above the reference load must be at least $0.10\cdot80=8$ kg. The condition becomes $p-80\ge8$ and includes the boundary of $88$ kg.
