What is the solution set of $3x-3\le3x-2$?

- $\varnothing$
- $x\le3$
- $x\ge-3$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The variable terms cancel. The number on the left is less than the number on the right, so the numerical comparison is true. The inequality holds for every real number.

The result is $\mathbb R$.
