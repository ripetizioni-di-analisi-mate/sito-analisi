The machine $y=2x+1$ accepts outputs with $23\le y<27$. What is the length of the allowed input interval?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $3$

## Explanation

Subtracting $1$ translates the interval without changing its length. Dividing by $2$ halves the length from $4$ to $2$, with inputs in $[11,13)$.
