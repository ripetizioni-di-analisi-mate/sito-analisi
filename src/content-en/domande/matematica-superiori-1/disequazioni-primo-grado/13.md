Solve $2(x-5)<6$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(-\infty, 8\right)$
- $\left(8, \infty\right)$
- $\left[8, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 8\right]$

## Explanation

Divide by two, a positive number. Then add $5$ to both sides without reversing the sign. The boundary value does not satisfy the strict inequality.

$$
x-5<3,\qquad x<8
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, 8\right)$.
