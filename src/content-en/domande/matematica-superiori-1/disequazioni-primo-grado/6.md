Solve $-3x\le 9$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(-3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right]$

## Explanation

Divide by negative three to isolate x. Division by a negative number reverses the inequality sign. The boundary value is included because equality is allowed.

$$
-3x\le 9,\qquad x\ge -3
$$

The result is $\left[-3, \infty\right)$.
