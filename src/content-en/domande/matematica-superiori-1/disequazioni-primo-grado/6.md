To clear the denominators in $\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{x}{3}>11$, which comparison do you get by multiplying every term by $6$?

- $3x-2x<66$
- $3x-2x>66$
- $2x-3x>66$
- $3x-2x>11$

## Explanation

The multiplier $6$ is positive, so it preserves the inequality sign. Dividing $6$ by $2$ and by $3$ gives $3$ and $2$ respectively, and $11$ becomes $66$.
