What are the solution sets, in order, of $\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{x}{2}>0$ and $\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{x}{2}\ge0$?

- $\varnothing,\ \mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R,\ \varnothing$
- $\{0\},\ \mathbb R$
- $\varnothing,\ \{0\}$

## Explanation

The same expression is subtracted from itself, so the difference is zero for every real number. The first comparison reduces to $0>0$, false for every $x$. The second reduces to $0\ge0$, true for every $x$. Allowing equality does not single out the number zero because the expression vanishes for every value of the unknown.
