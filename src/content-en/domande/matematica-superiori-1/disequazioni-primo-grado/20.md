For which real values of $x$ does $7-x\ge3x-9$ hold?

- $[4,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,4)$
- $(-\infty,-4]$
- $(-\infty,4]$

## Explanation

Add $x$ to both sides, then add $9$. This gives $16\ge4x$, so $x\le4$. At the boundary value $4$, both sides equal $3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
7&\ge4x-9 \\[6pt]
16&\ge4x \\[6pt]
x&\le4
\end{aligned}
$$
