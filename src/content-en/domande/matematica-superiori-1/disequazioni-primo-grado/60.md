Solve $7-2x>21$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(-7, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right)$

## Explanation

Subtract the initial constant term from both sides. The coefficient of the remaining variable term is negative. Dividing by that coefficient reverses the inequality sign.

$$
-2x>14,\qquad x<-7
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, -7\right)$.
