Which value of $x$ makes $x+1<3$ true and $x(x+1)<3x$ false?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $-1$

## Explanation

At $x=-1$ the original comparison is $0<3$, which is true. After multiplication by $x$ it becomes $0<-3$, which is false, because the negative multiplier requires reversing the sign. Both inequalities hold for $x=1$. At $x=3$ or $x=4$, the initial comparison is already false, so those values cannot meet the requirement.
