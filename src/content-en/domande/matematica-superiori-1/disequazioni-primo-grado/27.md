For which values of $x$ does $1\le\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{x}{3}<2$ hold?

- $(6,12]$
- $[3,6)$
- $[6,12)$
- $(-12,-6]$

## Explanation

The two fractions have common denominator $6$, so their difference is $\dfrac{3x-2x}{6}=\dfrac{x}{6}$. Multiplying all three parts by $6$ gives $6\le x<12$, with both signs unchanged. At $x=6$ the difference is $1$ and meets the lower bound. At $x=12$ the difference is $2$, excluded by the upper bound.
