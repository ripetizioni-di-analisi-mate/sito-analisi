Solve $\dfrac x2-\dfrac x3>4$. What is the solution set?

- $\left[24, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 24\right)$
- $\left(24, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 24\right]$

## Explanation

The common denominator is six, which is positive. The left-hand side simplifies to x divided by six. Multiplying by six gives the final boundary.

$$
\frac x6>4,\qquad x>24
$$

The result is $\left(24, \infty\right)$.
