A membership card costs $7$ euros and each visit costs $4$ euros. With $38$ euros, what is the greatest number of visits one can buy after paying for the card?

- $8$
- $7$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

Paying for the card leaves $31$ euros. Seven visits cost $28$ euros, while eight cost $32$ euros. The maximum is therefore $7$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
7+4x&\le38 \\[6pt]
x&\le\frac{31}{4}=7+\frac34
\end{aligned}
$$
