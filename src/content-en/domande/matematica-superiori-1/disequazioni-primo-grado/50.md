The spending limit is $B$ euros. Which budget condition is necessary and sufficient for the existence of at least one integer $n\ge1$ satisfying $7+4n\le B$?

- $B\le11$
- $B\ge11$
- $B>11$
- $B\ge7$

## Explanation

The cost increases with $n$ and the smallest allowed value is $1$. The minimum cost is $11$, and a budget of exactly $11$ covers it.
