Pencils come in packs of $4$. How many pencils are left over the requested $45$ when just enough packs are bought?

- $4$
- $0$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

Eleven packs provide only $44$ pencils, so a twelfth is needed. The $48$ pencils bought exceed the requested number by three.
