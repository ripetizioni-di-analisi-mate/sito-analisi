A filter accepts $x<-11$ or $x>11$. Which set of inputs is rejected?

- $(-11,11)$
- $(-\infty,-11]\cup[11,\infty)$
- $\{-11,11\}$
- $[-11,11]$

## Explanation

A rejected number must fail both tests. It must therefore be at least $-11$ and at most $11$, with both endpoints included.
