Solve $-3x\le 15$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(-5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right]$
- $\left[-5, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Divide by negative three to isolate x. Division by a negative number reverses the inequality sign. The boundary value is included because equality is allowed.

$$
-3x\le 15,\qquad x\ge -5
$$

The result is $\left[-5, \infty\right)$.
