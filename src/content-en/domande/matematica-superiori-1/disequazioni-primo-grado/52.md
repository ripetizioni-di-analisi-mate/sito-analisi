A scale reading $m$ differs from the true mass $x$ by at most $2$ g. Which reading guarantees $x\ge100$ g for every allowed error?

- $101\,\mathrm g$
- $100\,\mathrm g$
- $99\,\mathrm g$
- $102\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The smallest true mass consistent with the reading is $m-2$. Guaranteeing the target requires $m-2\ge100$, or $m\ge102$.
