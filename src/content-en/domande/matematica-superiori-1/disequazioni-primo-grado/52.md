A pack contains 4 pencils. How many packs are needed to have at least $17$ pencils?

- $4$
- $17$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

The number of pencils must reach the target. With $4$ packs there is still one pencil missing. One more pack meets the requirement and is the first admissible integer.

$$
4x\ge17,\qquad x\ge\frac{17}{4},\qquad x_{\min}=5
$$

The result is $5$.
