How many of $-3$, $-2$, $0$, and $1$ satisfy $5-2x>9$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The equivalent inequality is $x<-2$. The value $-2$ makes the sides equal, so it is excluded. Only $-3$ is less than $-2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-2x&>4 \\[6pt]
x&<-2
\end{aligned}
$$
