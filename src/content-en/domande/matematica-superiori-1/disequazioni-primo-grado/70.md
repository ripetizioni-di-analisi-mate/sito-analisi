Which integers $x$ satisfy $x<\dfrac{x+1}{2}<x+1$?

- $\mathbb Z$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{-1,0\}$
- $\{0,1\}$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $x<1$ and the second gives $x>-1$. The only integer strictly between $-1$ and $1$ is $0$.
