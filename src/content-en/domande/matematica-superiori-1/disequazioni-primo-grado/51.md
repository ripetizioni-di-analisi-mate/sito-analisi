A pack contains 4 pencils. How many packs are needed to have at least $13$ pencils?

- $3$
- $13$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The number of pencils must reach the target. With $3$ packs there is still one pencil missing. One more pack meets the requirement and is the first admissible integer.

$$
4x\ge13,\qquad x\ge\frac{13}{4},\qquad x_{\min}=4
$$

The result is $4$.
