A tank starts with $40$ litres and loses $3$ litres per hour. At what times $t$ measured from the start does it contain more than $22$ litres?

- $[0,6]$
- $(-\infty,6)$
- $[0,6)$
- $(6,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Use $t\ge0$ because time is measured from the start of the loss. The constraint $40-3t>22$ gives $-3t>-18$, so $t<6$ after division by $-3$. At time zero the tank contains $40$ litres and meets the requirement. At time six it reaches exactly $22$ litres, excluded by the strict comparison. The interval is $[0,6)$.
