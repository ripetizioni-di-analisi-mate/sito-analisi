In $2(x-11)<6$, set $u=x-11$. If $u<3$, how do you move the boundary back onto the $x$ scale?

- $x=11-u>8$
- $x=11u<33$
- $x=u+11<14$
- $x=u-11<-8$

## Explanation

The inverse relation is $x=u+11$. Adding the same constant preserves order and moves the boundary to $14$.
