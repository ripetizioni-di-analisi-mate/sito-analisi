How many integers are added to the solutions of $11<x\le17$ if only the first sign $<$ changes to $\le$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

The initial interval contains the integers from $12$ to $17$. The only point whose membership changes is the left endpoint $11$.
