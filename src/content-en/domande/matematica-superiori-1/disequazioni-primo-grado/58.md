Solve $5-2x>15$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(-5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right)$
- $\left[-5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right]$

## Explanation

Subtract the initial constant term from both sides. The coefficient of the remaining variable term is negative. Dividing by that coefficient reverses the inequality sign.

$$
-2x>10,\qquad x<-5
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, -5\right)$.
