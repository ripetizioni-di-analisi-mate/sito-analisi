For which real numbers $x$ is $7-2x$ negative and at least $-5$?

- $\left[\dfrac72,6\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac72,6\right]$
- $\left(-\infty,\dfrac72\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac72,6\right]$

## Explanation

The two requirements give $-5\le7-2x<0$. Subtracting $7$ gives $-12\le-2x<-7$. Division by $-2$ reverses both signs, giving $6\ge x>\dfrac72$. Include $6$ because it gives $-5$. The boundary $\dfrac72$ gives zero, so exclude it. Read the interval in increasing order, from the open endpoint to the closed endpoint.
