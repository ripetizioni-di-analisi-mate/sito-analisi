The value $u=4-3x$ must lie in $(-2,7]$. Which interval must $x$ lie in?

- $(-1,2]$
- $[-1,2]$
- $[-1,2)$
- $[-2,7)$

## Explanation

Solving for $x$ gives $x=\dfrac{4-u}{3}$. The value of $x$ decreases as $u$ increases. The upper endpoint $u=7$ therefore gives the lower bound $x=-1$, which remains included. The lower endpoint $u=-2$ gives the upper bound $x=2$, which remains excluded. The boundaries switch order but keep the inclusion status set by the original constraint on $u$.
