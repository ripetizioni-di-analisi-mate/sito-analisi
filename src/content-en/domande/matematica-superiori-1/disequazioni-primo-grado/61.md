Let $u=2x-5$. For which values of $x$ is $u\in[1,5]$?

- $[3,5]$
- $[1,5]$
- $[3,5)$
- $[6,10]$

## Explanation

The condition is $1\le2x-5\le5$. Adding $5$ to all three parts gives $6\le2x\le10$. Division by $2$ preserves both inequality signs and gives $3\le x\le5$. Both boundaries are included. Substituting the first gives $u=1$, and substituting the second gives $u=5$, matching the original endpoints. The inverse formula $x=\dfrac{u+5}{2}$ gives a value in the resulting interval for every intermediate $u$.
