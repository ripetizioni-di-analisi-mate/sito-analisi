Solve the compound inequality $7\le2x+1<11$.

- $\left[3, 5\right)$
- $\left[3, 5\right]$
- $\left(3, 5\right)$
- $\left(3, 5\right]$

## Explanation

Subtract one from all three expressions. Then divide each expression by two, a positive number. The left endpoint remains included and the right endpoint remains excluded.

$$
6\le2x<10,\qquad 3\le x<5
$$

The result is $\left[3, 5\right)$.
