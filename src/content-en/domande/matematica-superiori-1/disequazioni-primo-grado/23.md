The values of $t$ fill $(-\infty,3]$. Which interval is filled by the corresponding values $x=t-4$?

- $(-\infty,7]$
- $[-1,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-1]$
- $(-\infty,-1)$

## Explanation

From $t\le3$, subtract $4$ to get $t-4\le-1$, so $x\le-1$. The translation moves every point four units to the left and preserves the order. The allowed value $t=3$ maps to $x=-1$, so the new boundary is included. Conversely, every $x\le-1$ gives $t=x+4\le3$. The implication in both directions shows that every value in the resulting interval is reached.
