A filter must accept every real number except $3$. Which pair of comparisons joined by “or” does this?

- $x<3\ \lor\ x>4$
- $x<2\ \lor\ x>3$
- $x<3\ \lor\ x>3$
- $x\le3\ \lor\ x>3$

## Explanation

Every number other than $3$ lies on one side of it. Allowing equality would also accept $3$. Moving a boundary to $2$ or $4$ would reject a whole interval.
