Which of these numbers satisfies $2x+4>12$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Subtract $4$ and divide by two to simplify the inequality. The equivalent condition requires x to be strictly greater than $4$. Only the next number satisfies this condition among the listed choices.

$$
2x>8,\qquad x>4
$$

The result is $5$.
