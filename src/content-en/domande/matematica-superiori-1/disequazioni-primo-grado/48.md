Two shipping plans cost $A=9+4n$ and $B=18+2n$ euros for $n$ parcels. Starting at what positive whole-number parcel count does B cost no more than A?

- $4$
- $6$
- $9$
- $5$

## Explanation

The requirement is $18+2n\le9+4n$. Subtracting $9+2n$ gives $9\le2n$, so $n\ge4.5$. The price boundary falls between four and five parcels, making five the first permitted count. With four parcels, B costs $26$ euros versus $25$. With five, B costs $28$ euros versus $29$, so the inequality holds.
