A buyer has $17$ euros. Each ticket costs 3 euros. What is the maximum number of tickets the buyer can purchase?

- $6$
- $4$
- $17$
- $5$

## Explanation

The cost of x tickets must be at most the amount available. Dividing by the price gives a value between $5$ and $5$ plus one. Since tickets come in whole numbers, the maximum is $5$.

$$
3x\le 17,\qquad x\le \frac{17}{3},\qquad x_{\max}=5
$$

The result is $5$.
