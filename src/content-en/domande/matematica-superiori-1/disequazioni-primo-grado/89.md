An activity needs at least six participants, costs a fixed $10$ euros plus $5$ euros per person, and has a budget of $50$ euros. There are five places. Which single change makes the activity possible?

- Add one place
- Increase the budget by $10$ euros
- Reduce the fixed fee by $5$ euros
- Raise the minimum number of participants to seven

## Explanation

Five places cannot accommodate the minimum of six participants. Six people already fit the budget, since $10+5\cdot6=40\le50$. Adding one place makes a group of exactly six possible. Changing only the budget or the fixed fee leaves the five-person limit in place, and raising the minimum makes the conflict larger.
