Solve $2x+3>9$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$

## Explanation

Subtract $3$ from both sides. Then divide by two, which is positive, so the sign is preserved. The inequality is strict and excludes the boundary value.

$$
2x>6,\qquad x>3
$$

The result is $\left(3, \infty\right)$.
