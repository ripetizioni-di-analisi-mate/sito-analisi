Where should the open circle marking the boundary of $2x+11>33$ go on a number line?

- $11$
- $22$
- $33$
- $0$

## Explanation

Set $2x+11=33$ to find the boundary, obtaining $x=11$. The strict sign excludes it, and the positive coefficient selects the points to its right.
