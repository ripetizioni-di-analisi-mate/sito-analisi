What is the set of real numbers that satisfy at least one of $x<-5$ and $x>5$?

- $\left(-5, 5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\emptyset$
- $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

Requiring at least one condition gives a union. Its two parts are the outer rays. Their endpoints are excluded because both inequalities are strict.

The result is $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$.
