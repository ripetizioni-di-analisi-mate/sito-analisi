What set of real numbers satisfies $x\le3$ or $x>1$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $(1,3]$
- $[1,3]$
- $(-\infty,1]\cup(3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The two conditions are alternatives. Every real number at most $3$ satisfies the first one. Every real number above $3$ is also above $1$ and satisfies the second. The union therefore contains all real numbers. The interval $(1,3]$ contains exactly the numbers satisfying both conditions, which instead form the intersection.
