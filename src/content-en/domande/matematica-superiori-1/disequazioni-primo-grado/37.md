What is the set of real numbers that satisfy at least one of $x<-4$ and $x>4$?

- $\left(-\infty, -4\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-4, 4\right)$
- $\emptyset$
- $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

Requiring at least one condition gives a union. Its two parts are the outer rays. Their endpoints are excluded because both inequalities are strict.

The result is $\left(-\infty, -4\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$.
