What is the solution set of $\dfrac{4-2x}{-3}\le2$?

- $(-\infty,5]$
- $[5,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,5)$
- $(-\infty,1]$

## Explanation

Move the minus sign from the denominator to the numerator to get $\dfrac{2x-4}{3}\le2$. Multiplication by the positive $3$ gives $2x-4\le6$, so $x\le5$. Multiplying by $-3$ before moving the sign would reverse the inequality in that step and reverse it again when dividing by $-2$. Equality at $5$ is allowed.
