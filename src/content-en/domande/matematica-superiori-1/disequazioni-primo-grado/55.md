A library already has $12$ copies. Each box adds $6$ copies. How many boxes are needed to have at least $47$ copies?

- $5$
- $7$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

The number $x$ of boxes must make $12+6x$ at least $47$. Five boxes give $42$ copies, which is still short. Six boxes give $48$ copies, so six is the minimum.

$$
\begin{aligned}
12+6x&\ge47 \\[6pt]
x&\ge\frac{35}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$
