You are marking the simultaneous conditions $x>11$ and $x\le15$ on a number line. Which endpoints get a filled dot?

- Both $11$ and $15$
- Neither endpoint
- Only $15$
- Only $11$

## Explanation

A filled dot means the endpoint belongs to the solution set. The value $15$ meets both conditions, whereas $11$ fails the first one.
