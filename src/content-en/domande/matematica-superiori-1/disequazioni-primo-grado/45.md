How many integers satisfy $-\dfrac52<x\le\dfrac52$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The boundaries are $-2.5$ and $2.5$. The first admissible integer is $-2$ and the last is $2$. The count includes zero.

$$
\{-2,-1,0,1,2\}
$$
