A real interval contains exactly the integers $2,3,4$. Which right endpoint is impossible, regardless of whether that endpoint is open or closed?

- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac92$
- $5$

## Explanation

If the endpoint were $\dfrac{11}{2}$, the interval would also contain $5$, strictly between $4$ and the endpoint. With endpoint $5$, an open bracket can exclude it.
