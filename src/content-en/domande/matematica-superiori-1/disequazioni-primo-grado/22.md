Starting from $x=7$, what is the largest increase in $x$ allowed by $\dfrac{x+5}{2}\le8$?

- $11$
- $4$
- $8$
- $1$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $2$ gives $x+5\le16$, so the largest value of $x$ is $11$. The question asks by how much to increase the starting value $7$. The difference is $11-7=4$. At that increase the fraction is exactly $8$. Any larger increase would raise the numerator above $16$ and violate the bound.
