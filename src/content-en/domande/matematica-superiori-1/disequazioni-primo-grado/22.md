Solve $\dfrac{x+1}2\le 4$. What is the solution set?

- $\left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right]$
- $\left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right)$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the positive denominator two preserves the sign. Then subtract one to isolate the variable. The endpoint is included because the inequality allows equality.

$$
x+1\le 8,\qquad x\le7
$$

The result is $\left(-\infty, 7\right]$.
