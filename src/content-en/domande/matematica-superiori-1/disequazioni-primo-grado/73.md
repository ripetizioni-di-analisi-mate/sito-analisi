For which value of $a$ does $(a-2)x>0$ have no real solutions?

- $2$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $3$

## Explanation

When $a=2$, the coefficient of $x$ is zero, leaving $0>0$, false for every $x$. If $a>2$, then $x=1$ gives a positive product. If $a<2$, then $x=-1$ gives a positive product. When the coefficient is nonzero there is therefore at least one solution. The only required parameter is $2$.
