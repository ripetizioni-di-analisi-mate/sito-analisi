In triangle $ABC$ the bisector of the angle at $A$ meets side $BC$ at point $D$. It is known that $\widehat{ADB}=110^\circ$ and $\widehat B=40^\circ$. What is the measure of $\widehat C$?

- $70^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $100^\circ$

## Explanation

In triangle $ABD$ the angles at $B$ and $D$ measure $40^\circ$ and $110^\circ$. By the angle sum we therefore have $\widehat{BAD}=180^\circ-40^\circ-110^\circ=30^\circ$.

Since $AD$ bisects the angle at $A$, the whole angle measures $\widehat A=2\cdot30^\circ=60^\circ$. In triangle $ABC$ we therefore have $\widehat C=180^\circ-60^\circ-40^\circ=80^\circ$. The answer $60^\circ$ is the measure of $\widehat A$, while the answer $70^\circ$ is the supplement of $\widehat{ADB}$.
