In triangle $ABC$ the angle bisector at $A$ meets $BC$ at $D$. If $\angle ADB=110^\circ$ and $\angle B=40^\circ$, what is $\angle C$?

- $70^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $100^\circ$

## Explanation

Triangle $ABD$ has a $30^\circ$ angle at $A$. The full angle at $A$ is $60^\circ$, so $\angle C=180^\circ-60^\circ-40^\circ=80^\circ$.
