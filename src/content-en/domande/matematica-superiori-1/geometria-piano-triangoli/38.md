An isosceles triangle has vertex angle $52^\circ$. What is the measure of each base angle?

- $128^\circ$
- $64^\circ$
- $26^\circ$
- $38^\circ$

## Explanation

The base angles of an isosceles triangle are congruent. Their sum is one hundred eighty degrees minus the vertex angle. Divide this sum by two.

$$
\beta=\frac{180^\circ-52^\circ}2=64^\circ
$$

The result is $64^\circ$.
