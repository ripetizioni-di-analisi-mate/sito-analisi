An isosceles triangle has a $40^\circ$ angle, which could be at the base or at the vertex. Which triples of measures in degrees are possible?

- Only $(40,70,70)$
- $(40,70,70)$ and $(40,40,100)$
- Only $(40,40,100)$
- $(40,70,70)$ and $(40,60,80)$

## Explanation

If $40^\circ$ is the vertex angle, the two base angles share the remaining $140^\circ$ equally. If $40^\circ$ is a base angle, subtract it twice and the vertex angle is $100^\circ$. Both triples have two congruent angles and add to $180^\circ$. The triple with three different measures would give a scalene triangle.
