An isosceles triangle has perimeter $34\,\mathrm{cm}$, and each equal side is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ longer than the base. What is the base length?

- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

If the base measures $b$ centimetres, the perimeter is $b+2(b+5)=3b+10$. Solving $3b+10=34$ gives $b=8$. The other two sides measure $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ and satisfy the triangle inequality.
