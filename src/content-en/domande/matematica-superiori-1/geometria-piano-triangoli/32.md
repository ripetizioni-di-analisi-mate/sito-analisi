Two angles of a triangle measure $34^\circ$ and $47^\circ$. What is the measure of the third angle?

- $81^\circ$
- $146^\circ$
- $133^\circ$
- $99^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angles of a triangle sum to one hundred eighty degrees. First add the two known measures. Subtract this sum from the measure of a straight angle to find the remaining angle.

$$
\gamma=180^\circ-(34^\circ+47^\circ)=99^\circ
$$

The result is $99^\circ$.
