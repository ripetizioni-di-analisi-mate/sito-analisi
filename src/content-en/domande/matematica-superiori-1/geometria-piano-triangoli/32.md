Which triple of measures in degrees cannot be the interior angles of a triangle?

- $(10,80,90)$
- $(20,30,130)$
- $(40,60,80)$
- $(0,90,90)$

## Explanation

Interior angle measures must be positive and add to $180^\circ$. Every listed triple passes the sum check. The zero angle in $(0,90,90)$ would make the two rays at that vertex coincide. The other triples have positive measures and can be constructed by drawing two angles at the ends of a base.
