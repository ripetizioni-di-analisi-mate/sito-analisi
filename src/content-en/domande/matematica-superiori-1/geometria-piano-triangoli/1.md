The points $A,B,C$ lie on a line in the stated order. Suppose that $AC=15$ cm and $AB=6$ cm. What is the length of $BC$?

- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $21\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Since B lies inside segment AC, the length of AC is the sum of AB and BC. Subtract AB from the total length. The order of the points is needed to choose this relation.

$$
BC=AC-AB=15-6=9
$$

The result is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
