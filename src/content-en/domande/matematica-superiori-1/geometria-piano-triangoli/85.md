Two triangles have all corresponding angles congruent. Which further observation rules out congruence?

- Their perimeters differ
- Both have a right angle
- The two marked vertices have different names
- They are drawn in different orientations

## Explanation

Congruence requires equality of every pair of corresponding sides. Adding those three equalities would give the same perimeter. Different perimeters therefore rule out congruence even when the angle measures match. Reflecting or rotating a drawing can change how it is placed on the page while leaving every side length unchanged.
