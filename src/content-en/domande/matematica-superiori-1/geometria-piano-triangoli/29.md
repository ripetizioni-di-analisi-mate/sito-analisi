$OC$ bisects $\widehat{AOB}=120^\circ$. Then $OD$ bisects $\widehat{AOC}$. What is $\widehat{DOB}$?

- $90^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$

## Explanation

The first bisector gives two parts of $60^\circ$. The second cuts only the part between $OA$ and $OC$, giving $\widehat{DOC}=30^\circ$. Between $OD$ and $OB$ lie this sector and the whole sector $\widehat{COB}=60^\circ$, for a total of $90^\circ$. Check by subtracting the part between $OA$ and $OD$ from $120^\circ$.
