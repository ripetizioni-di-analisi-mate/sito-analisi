Two complementary angles have measures in the ratio $2:3$. What is the measure of the larger angle?

- $36^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $54^\circ$
- $45^\circ$

## Explanation

The ratio allows the degree measures to be written as $2x$ and $3x$. The total is $90$, so $5x=90$ and $x=18$. The larger angle measures $3\cdot18=54$ degrees.
