A student sees four distinct rays $OA,OB,OC,OD$ and claims $\widehat{AOC}=\widehat{BOD}$ because they are vertical angles. What assumption justifies that reason?

- All four start at $O$
- Only $OA$ and $OB$ are opposite
- $OA$ and $OB$ are opposite, and $OC$ and $OD$ are opposite
- $OA$ and $OC$ form an acute angle

## Explanation

For angles to be vertical, each side of the first must continue past the vertex along one side of the second. The pairs of opposite rays form two intersecting lines. With just the same vertex, the four rays could point in arbitrary directions, and the two angle measures would not necessarily agree.
