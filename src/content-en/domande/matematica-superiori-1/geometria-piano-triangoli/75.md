Two triangles each have perimeter $22\,\mathrm{cm}$, and each has sides of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $7\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which conclusion follows from comparing the side lengths directly?

- They are congruent by ASA.
- They must be equilateral.
- They are congruent by SSS.
- The third sides may have different lengths.

## Explanation

In each triangle, the third side measures $22-6-7=9$ centimetres. The three pairs of sides have equal lengths, so SSS applies.
