$A,B,C$ are collinear, with $AB=9$ cm and $AC=4$ cm. What values can $BC$ have when the point order is unknown?

- Only $5$ cm
- Only $13$ cm
- $5$ cm or $13$ cm
- Any value between $5$ and $13$ cm

## Explanation

If $B$ and $C$ lie on the same ray from $A$, then $C$ is closer to $A$, and the distance is $9-4=5$ cm. If they lie on opposite rays, the two pieces add to $9+4=13$ cm. On the line there are only those two directions from $A$.
