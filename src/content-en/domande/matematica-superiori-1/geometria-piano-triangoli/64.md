In the figure, $AO=OB$ and $CO=OD$. Which relation lets you apply SAS directly to triangles $AOC$ and $BOD$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-3.5 ymax=3.5 linee="-2,-1 2,1;1,-2.7 -1,2.7;-2,-1 1,-2.7;2,1 -1,2.7" punti="-2,-1;2,1;1,-2.7;-1,2.7;0,0" etichette="-2.35,-0.95,A;2.28,1.06,B;1.16,-3.13,C;-1.14,3.1,D;0.36,-0.2,O" titolo="$AB$ and $CD$ meet at $O$"}

- $\widehat{AOD}=\widehat{BOC}$
- $AB=CD$
- $AC=BD$
- $\widehat{AOC}=\widehat{BOD}$

## Explanation

The needed angles must lie between the sides already matched. The shared vertex of those sides is $O$ in $AOC$ and likewise in $BOD$. Both ray pairs are opposite, so the indicated angles are congruent. The other pair of vertical angles also matches, but it lies in the two sectors outside the chosen triangles.
