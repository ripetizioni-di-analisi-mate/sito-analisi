The direct theorem says that $AB=AC$ implies $\widehat B=\widehat C$. What is its converse?

- $\widehat B=\widehat C$ implies $AB=BC$
- $AB=AC$ implies $\widehat A=\widehat B$
- $\widehat A=\widehat B$ implies $AB=AC$
- $\widehat B=\widehat C$ implies $AB=AC$

## Explanation

To form the converse, exchange the hypothesis and conclusion of the given statement. Assume that the angles at $B$ and $C$ are equal and conclude that the opposite sides, $AC$ and $AB$, are equal. In this case the converse is proved by using congruence after swapping the two endpoints of the base in the triangle's order.
