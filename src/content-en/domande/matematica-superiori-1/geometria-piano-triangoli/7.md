$M$ is the midpoint of $AB$, and $N$ is the midpoint of $AM$. What fraction of $AB$ is $NB$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The order is $A,N,M,B$. The segment $NM$ is half of $AM$, hence a quarter of $AB$. The segment $MB$ is half of $AB$. The required length combines both parts and is three quarters of the whole. The quarter between $A$ and $N$ completes the entire segment.
