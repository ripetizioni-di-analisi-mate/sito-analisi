In triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$, $AB=DE$ and $BC=EF$. Which angle equality allows the use of SAS?

- $\widehat B=\widehat E$
- $\widehat A=\widehat D$
- $\widehat C=\widehat F$
- $\widehat B=\widehat D$

## Explanation

Sides $AB$ and $BC$ meet at $B$. Sides $DE$ and $EF$ meet at $E$. The included angles are therefore the angles at $B$ and $E$.
