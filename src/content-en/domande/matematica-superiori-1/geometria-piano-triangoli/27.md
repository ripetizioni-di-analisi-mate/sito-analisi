$OC$ bisects $\widehat{AOB}$. Given $\widehat{AOC}=(4x-3)^\circ$ and $\widehat{COB}=(2x+17)^\circ$, what is the measure of the full angle?

- $37^\circ$
- $20^\circ$
- $74^\circ$
- $106^\circ$

## Explanation

The two parts have equal measure by the definition of a bisector. The equality $4x-3=2x+17$ gives $2x=20$, so $x=10$. Each expression then gives $37^\circ$. The question asks for the whole angle, so add the two halves. The value of $x$ serves only to calculate the angle measures.
