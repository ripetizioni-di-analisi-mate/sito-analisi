An angle bisector divides an angle of $44^\circ$ into two parts. How large is each part?

- $44^\circ$
- $22^\circ$
- $88^\circ$
- $136^\circ$

## Explanation

An angle bisector is the ray that divides an angle into two congruent angles. The two parts add to the original angle. Each part is therefore half the given angle.

$$
\beta=\frac{44^\circ}2=22^\circ
$$

The result is $22^\circ$.
