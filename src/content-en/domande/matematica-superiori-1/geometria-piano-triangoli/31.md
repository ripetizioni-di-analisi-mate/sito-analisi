Two angles of a triangle measure $32^\circ$ and $46^\circ$. What is the measure of the third angle?

- $78^\circ$
- $148^\circ$
- $102^\circ$
- $134^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angles of a triangle sum to one hundred eighty degrees. First add the two known measures. Subtract this sum from the measure of a straight angle to find the remaining angle.

$$
\gamma=180^\circ-(32^\circ+46^\circ)=102^\circ
$$

The result is $102^\circ$.
