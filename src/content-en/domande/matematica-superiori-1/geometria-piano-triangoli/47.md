Compare a triangle's exterior angle with each of the two non-adjacent interior angles. Which relation always holds?

- The exterior angle is less than each one
- The exterior angle is their difference
- The exterior angle is greater than each one
- The exterior angle equals at least one

## Explanation

The exterior angle is the sum of the two non-adjacent interior angles. Each addend is positive, so the total exceeds each one. The comparison concerns those two angles. The adjacent interior angle adds to $180^\circ$ with the exterior angle and can itself be larger, as with an exterior angle of $70^\circ$.
