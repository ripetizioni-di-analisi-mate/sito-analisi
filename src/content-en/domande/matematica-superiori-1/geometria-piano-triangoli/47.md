An exterior angle of a triangle measures $116^\circ$. One of its two nonadjacent interior angles measures $37^\circ$. What is the measure of the other one?

- $64^\circ$
- $153^\circ$
- $79^\circ$
- $143^\circ$

## Explanation

An exterior angle equals the sum of the two nonadjacent interior angles. Subtract the known interior angle from the exterior angle. The result is the measure of the other interior angle.

$$
\beta=116^\circ-37^\circ=79^\circ
$$

The result is $79^\circ$.
