Two triangles are congruent with correspondence $A\leftrightarrow D$, $B\leftrightarrow F$, $C\leftrightarrow E$. What matches $\widehat{BAC}$ and side $AB$?

- $\widehat{FDE}$ and $DE$
- $\widehat{DFE}$ and $DF$
- $\widehat{FDE}$ and $DF$
- $\widehat{DEF}$ and $DE$

## Explanation

The middle letter of the angle is vertex $A$, which matches $D$. The rays of the angle pass through $B$ and $C$, matching $F$ and $E$. This gives $\widehat{FDE}$. Side $AB$ joins the vertex to $B$, so in the second triangle join their matches, $D$ and $F$. Both elements must respect the same correspondence.
