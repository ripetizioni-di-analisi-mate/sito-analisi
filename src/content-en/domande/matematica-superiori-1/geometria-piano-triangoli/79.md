Triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ are congruent with correspondence $A\leftrightarrow D$, $B\leftrightarrow E$, and $C\leftrightarrow F$. If $BC=11$ cm and $AB=8$ cm, what is the length of $EF$?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $19\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The vertex correspondence determines the side correspondence. Endpoints B and C match E and F, respectively. Side EF is therefore congruent to BC.

$$
EF=BC=11
$$

The result is $11\,\mathrm{cm}$.
