The collinear points are in the order $C,A,B$. Given $AC=6$ cm and $AB=11$ cm, which equality respects that order?

- $CB=11-6$
- $CB=6+11$
- $AB=CB+6$
- $AC=CB+11$

## Explanation

The interior point of the longest segment is $A$. To travel all the way from $C$ to $B$, first go along $CA$ and then $AB$. The two pieces add to $17$ cm. A difference would make sense in a different order, with one known segment containing the other.
