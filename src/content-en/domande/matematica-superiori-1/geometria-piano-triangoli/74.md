Triangles $ABC$ and $ADC$ have $AB=AD$ and $BC=DC$. Which third equality is already available for SSS?

- $AB=BC$
- $AC=AC$
- $AD=DC$
- $BD=AC$

## Explanation

The remaining side in the comparison is the same segment $AC$, which belongs to both triangles. Its length is already equal to that of its counterpart. The other choices require new information, and one involves a segment that is not a side of either triangle. The shared side completes the SSS criterion.
