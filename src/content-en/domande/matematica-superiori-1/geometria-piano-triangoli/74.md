Two triangles each have side lengths $7$, $8$, and $9$ cm. Which criterion proves congruence?

- Angle-side-angle, because two angles are given
- Side-side-side
- Side-angle-side, because an angle is given
- Equal perimeters alone, without using the side lengths

## Explanation

The three lengths satisfy the triangle inequality. All three pairs of corresponding sides have equal lengths. The side-side-side criterion therefore guarantees congruence.

The criterion is side-side-side.
