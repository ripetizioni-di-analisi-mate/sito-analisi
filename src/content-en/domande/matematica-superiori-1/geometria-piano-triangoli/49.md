In a triangle, the two interior angles not adjacent to a $110^\circ$ exterior angle are congruent. What triple of interior angles in degrees must it have?

- $(55,55,70)$
- $(35,35,110)$
- $(70,70,40)$
- $(55,70,55+70)$

## Explanation

The two non-adjacent interior angles add to the exterior angle, and since they are equal, each is half of it. This gives two angles of $55^\circ$. The interior angle at the exterior angle's vertex forms a linear pair with it and equals $180^\circ-110^\circ=70^\circ$. The three positive angles make a straight angle and describe an acute isosceles triangle.
