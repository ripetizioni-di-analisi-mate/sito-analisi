The bisector of an angle makes a $24^\circ$ angle with each side. What is the supplement of the whole angle?

- $156^\circ$
- $132^\circ$
- $48^\circ$
- $66^\circ$

## Explanation

The bisector divides the angle into two congruent parts. The whole angle therefore measures $48^\circ$. Its supplement measures $180^\circ-48^\circ=132^\circ$.
