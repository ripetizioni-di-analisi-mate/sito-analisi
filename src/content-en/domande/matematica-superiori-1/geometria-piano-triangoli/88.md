Which pair of side-length triples is a counterexample to “triangles with equal perimeters are congruent”?

- $(5,5,6)$ and $(6,5,5)$
- $(4,6,6)$ and $(3,6,6)$
- $(5,5,6)$ and $(2,6,8)$
- $(5,5,6)$ and $(4,6,6)$

## Explanation

The triples $(5,5,6)$ and $(4,6,6)$ form triangles because the longest side is shorter than the sum of the others. Both perimeters are $16$, but some pair of sides differs in length under any matching. Merely reordering the lengths would give congruent triangles. The triple $(2,6,8)$ is excluded because it would give three collinear points.
