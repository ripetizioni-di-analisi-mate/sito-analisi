The complement of $\alpha$ is half of $\alpha$. What is the measure of $\alpha$?

- $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

A right angle is made up of three halves of $\alpha$. The equation $\alpha+\dfrac{\alpha}{2}=90^\circ$ therefore gives $\alpha=60^\circ$. Its complement measures $30^\circ$, which satisfies both the required ratio and the right-angle sum. Swapping the two values would make the complement twice the angle rather than half of it.
