An angle measures $32^\circ$. What is the measure of its complement?

- $148^\circ$
- $58^\circ$
- $32^\circ$
- $122^\circ$

## Explanation

Complementary angles sum to a right angle. Subtract the given measure from ninety degrees. The result is positive because the given angle is acute.

$$
\beta=90^\circ-32^\circ=58^\circ
$$

The result is $58^\circ$.
