The angles of a triangle have measures proportional to $2$, $3$, and $4$. What is the largest angle?

- $90^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $100^\circ$

## Explanation

The degree measures can be written as $2x$, $3x$, and $4x$. They sum to $180$, so $9x=180$. This gives $x=20$, and the largest angle measures $80^\circ$.
