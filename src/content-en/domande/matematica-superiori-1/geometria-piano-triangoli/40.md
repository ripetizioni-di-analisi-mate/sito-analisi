Two angles of a triangle are $x$ and $2x$. For what range of $x$ is the triangle acute?

- $0^\circ<x<45^\circ$
- $45^\circ<x<60^\circ$
- $30^\circ\le x\le45^\circ$
- $30^\circ<x<45^\circ$

## Explanation

The third angle $180^\circ-3x$ must also be less than $90^\circ$, giving $x>30^\circ$. The angle $2x$ is acute only for $x<45^\circ$.
