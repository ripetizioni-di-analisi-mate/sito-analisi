Two interior angles of a triangle measure $x$ and $2x$. For which values of $x$ is the triangle acute?

- $0^\circ<x<45^\circ$
- $45^\circ<x<60^\circ$
- $30^\circ\le x\le45^\circ$
- $30^\circ<x<45^\circ$

## Explanation

By the angle sum, the third angle measures $180^\circ-3x$. The triangle is acute when all three angles are less than $90^\circ$. Since $x<2x$, the condition $x<90^\circ$ follows from $2x<90^\circ$, so the conditions that must hold together are
$$
\begin{cases}
2x<90^\circ\\
180^\circ-3x<90^\circ
\end{cases}
$$
The first gives $x<45^\circ$, the second gives $3x>90^\circ$, that is $x>30^\circ$. Therefore the triangle is acute for $30^\circ<x<45^\circ$. At the endpoints $x=30^\circ$ and $x=45^\circ$ one angle is a right angle and the triangle is right-angled.
