In an isosceles triangle, the vertex angle is twice each base angle. What is the measure of the vertex angle?

- $45^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

Each base angle measures $x$ degrees, and the vertex angle measures $2x$ degrees. The sum gives $x+x+2x=180$, so $x=45$. The vertex angle therefore measures $90^\circ$.
