In $ABC$, $AB=AC$. Which segment necessarily divides both the base into equal lengths and the vertex angle into equal angles?

- The median from $A$ to $BC$
- The median from $B$ to $AC$
- The bisector of the angle at $C$
- The altitude from $B$ to line $AC$

## Explanation

The base is opposite $A$, where the equal sides meet. The isosceles property concerns the segment from that vertex to the base. Its median coincides with the bisector of the angle at $A$. Segments from the other two vertices concern a different angle or a different side.
