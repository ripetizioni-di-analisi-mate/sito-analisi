In $ABC$, $AB=4$, $BC=7$, and $AC=9$. In $XYZ$, $XY=9$, $YZ=4$, and $ZX=7$. Which vertex matches $B$?

- $Z$
- $X$
- $Y$
- The data do not determine the vertex

## Explanation

The three side lengths allow SSS. Vertex $B$ is where the sides of lengths $4$ and $7$ meet. In the other triangle these sides are $YZ$ and $ZX$, which meet at $Z$. Using the shared vertex avoids matching letters merely by their positions in the names as written.
