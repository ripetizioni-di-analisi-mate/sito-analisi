Two congruent triangles are placed side by side along two corresponding sides of $5$ cm, one on each side of the join. The boundary of the resulting figure measures $26$ cm. What is the perimeter of each triangle?

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $21\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two triangles are congruent, so they have the same perimeter, denoted by $p$. The boundary of the figure includes all the sides of the two triangles except the two $5$ cm sides placed in contact, which remain inside. Therefore the boundary measures $2p-2\cdot5=2p-10$.

From the equation $2p-10=26$ we get $2p=36$, so $p=18$ cm. The answer $13$ cm is half the boundary, while the answer $15.5$ cm adds only one $5$ cm side to the boundary before dividing by two.
