Two congruent triangles are joined along corresponding sides of length $5$ cm on opposite sides of the join. The boundary of the combined figure measures $26$ cm. What is the perimeter of one triangle?

- $18$ cm
- $13$ cm
- $15.5$ cm
- $21$ cm

## Explanation

If one triangle has perimeter $p$, the outer boundary measures $2p-10$. Solving $2p-10=26$ gives $p=18$ cm.
