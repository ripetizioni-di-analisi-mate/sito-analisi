Two angles form a linear pair, and the larger is $46^\circ$ greater than the smaller. What is the larger measure?

- $67^\circ$
- $136^\circ$
- $113^\circ$
- $92^\circ$

## Explanation

Let $\beta$ be the smaller angle. The larger is $\beta+46^\circ$. Their sum is $180^\circ$, so $2\beta=134^\circ$ and $\beta=67^\circ$. Adding the difference gives the larger angle of $113^\circ$. You can also start from half a straight angle and add half the difference, because the two measures are equally far from $90^\circ$.
