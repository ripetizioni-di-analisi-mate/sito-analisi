Two supplementary angles differ by $36^\circ$. What is the measure of the smaller angle?

- $108^\circ$
- $54^\circ$
- $36^\circ$
- $72^\circ$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the measure of the smaller angle in degrees. The larger one measures $x+36$. Since their sum is $180$, the equation is $2x+36=180$, giving $x=72$.
