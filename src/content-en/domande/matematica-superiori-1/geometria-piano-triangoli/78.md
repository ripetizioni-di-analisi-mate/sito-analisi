Suppose $ABC\cong QPR$. Which side of the second triangle corresponds to the side opposite $B$?

- $QP$
- $QR$
- $PR$
- It depends on the side lengths

## Explanation

The side opposite $B$ is $AC$, because it joins the other two vertices. In the congruence statement $A$ matches $Q$ and $C$ matches $R$. So the required side is $QR$. Equivalently, read that $B$ matches $P$ and find the side opposite that vertex in the second triangle.
