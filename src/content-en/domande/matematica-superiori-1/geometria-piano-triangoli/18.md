Two angles measure $112^\circ$ and $68^\circ$. Which relationship is guaranteed by those measures alone?

- They form a linear pair
- They are supplementary
- They are vertical angles
- They are complementary

## Explanation

The sum is $112^\circ+68^\circ=180^\circ$, which defines supplementary angles. To form a linear pair they also need a shared vertex and side, with the other sides opposite. For example, angles drawn around different points keep that sum without forming a linear pair.
