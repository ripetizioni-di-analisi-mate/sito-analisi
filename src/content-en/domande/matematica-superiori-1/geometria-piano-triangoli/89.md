Two triangles have corresponding angles of $40^\circ$, $60^\circ$, and $80^\circ$. Which additional fact guarantees congruence?

- The sides opposite the $80^\circ$ angles have equal length
- One side is twice its counterpart
- The sides opposite $40^\circ$ are the shortest in each triangle
- The sides opposite $60^\circ$ both exceed $1$ cm

## Explanation

The sides opposite the $80^\circ$ angles have the $40^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ vertices as endpoints. Their equality therefore gives the included side required for ASA. The angles alone fix the shape and leave the scale free. Identifying the shortest side or giving a lower bound also leaves the side lengths undetermined.
