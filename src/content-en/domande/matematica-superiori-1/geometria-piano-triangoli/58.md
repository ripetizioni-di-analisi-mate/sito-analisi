An isosceles triangle has perimeter $42$ cm and a side of $12$ cm. It is unknown which side this is. Which triples of lengths in centimetres are possible?

- Only $(12,12,18)$
- $(12,12,18)$ and $(12,15,15)$
- Only $(12,15,15)$
- $(12,12,18)$ and $(10,12,20)$

## Explanation

If the known length is one of the two equal legs, the pair contributes $24$ cm and the base measures $18$ cm. If the known length belongs to the base, $30$ cm remains to share between the legs. The longest side is shorter than the sum of the other two in both cases. Assigning the given length different roles therefore gives two distinct triples.
