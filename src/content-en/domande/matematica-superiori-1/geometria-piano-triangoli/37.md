Which measure can be a base angle of an isosceles triangle?

- $89^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $91^\circ$
- $120^\circ$

## Explanation

A base angle occurs twice. If it measured at least $90^\circ$, the two equal angles would add to at least $180^\circ$, leaving no positive vertex angle. With $89^\circ$, the vertex angle is $2^\circ$. This is a very narrow triangle, but the three measures satisfy the required conditions.
