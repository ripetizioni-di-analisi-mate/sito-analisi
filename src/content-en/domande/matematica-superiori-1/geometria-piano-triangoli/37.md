An isosceles triangle has vertex angle $48^\circ$. What is the measure of each base angle?

- $66^\circ$
- $132^\circ$
- $24^\circ$
- $42^\circ$

## Explanation

The base angles of an isosceles triangle are congruent. Their sum is one hundred eighty degrees minus the vertex angle. Divide this sum by two.

$$
\beta=\frac{180^\circ-48^\circ}2=66^\circ
$$

The result is $66^\circ$.
