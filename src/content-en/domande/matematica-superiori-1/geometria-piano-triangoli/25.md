Two intersecting lines form vertical angles with degree measures $4x+6$ and $7x-27$. What is the measure of either of these angles?

- $50^\circ$
- $130^\circ$
- $11^\circ$
- $44^\circ$

## Explanation

Vertical angles are congruent. Setting $4x+6=7x-27$ gives $3x=33$ and $x=11$. The required measure is $4\cdot11+6=50$ degrees.
