Two angles of a triangle measure $36^\circ$ and $48^\circ$. What is the measure of the third angle?

- $96^\circ$
- $84^\circ$
- $144^\circ$
- $132^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angles of a triangle sum to one hundred eighty degrees. First add the two known measures. Subtract this sum from the measure of a straight angle to find the remaining angle.

$$
\gamma=180^\circ-(36^\circ+48^\circ)=96^\circ
$$

The result is $96^\circ$.
