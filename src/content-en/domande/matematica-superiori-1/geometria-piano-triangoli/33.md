Which pair of acute interior angles forces the third angle to be obtuse?

- $38^\circ$ and $44^\circ$
- $44^\circ$ and $46^\circ$
- $46^\circ$ and $49^\circ$
- $50^\circ$ and $58^\circ$

## Explanation

If the given acute angles add to less than $90^\circ$, a third angle above $90^\circ$ is needed to reach $180^\circ$. The required pair adds to $82^\circ$, leaving $98^\circ$. A sum of exactly $90^\circ$ gives a right angle, as happens with $44^\circ$ and $46^\circ$.
