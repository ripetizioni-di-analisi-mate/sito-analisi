An exterior angle at the base of an isosceles triangle measures $110^\circ$. What is the measure of the vertex angle?

- $70^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- $110^\circ$
- $55^\circ$

## Explanation

The adjacent interior angle measures $70^\circ$. It is a base angle, so the other base angle also measures $70^\circ$. The vertex angle measures $180^\circ-2\cdot70^\circ=40^\circ$.
