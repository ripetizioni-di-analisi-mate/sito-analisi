Two triangles have $\widehat A=\widehat P$ and $\widehat C=\widehat R$. Which side equality lets you apply ASA directly using just the given angles?

- $AB=PQ$
- $BC=QR$
- $AC=PQ$
- $AC=PR$

## Explanation

To apply ASA immediately, use the side joining the vertices of the two given angles. They are $A$ and $C$ in the first triangle and $P$ and $R$ in the second. Other corresponding side pairs could work after deriving equality of the third angles. With the angle data already given, compare the two sides joining both vertices.
