Each of two triangles has a side of length $8$ cm, with adjacent angles of measures $33^\circ$ and $53^\circ$. Which criterion applies?

- Side-side-side
- Side-angle-side
- The given information does not guarantee congruence
- Angle-side-angle

## Explanation

The known side lies between the two given angles. Both angle measures and the side length match in the two triangles. These data satisfy the angle-side-angle criterion.

The criterion is angle-side-angle.
