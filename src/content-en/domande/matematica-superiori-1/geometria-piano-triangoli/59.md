An isosceles triangle has perimeter $40$ cm. Which interval describes all possible base lengths $b$, measured in centimetres?

- $0<b<40$
- $0<b\le20$
- $0<b<20$
- $10<b<20$

## Explanation

The equal legs add to $40-b$. The triangle inequality for the base requires $b<40-b$, so $b<20$. The base must also be positive. Within that interval both legs are positive, and either leg is shorter than the other plus the base. Thus all lengths within the interval are possible.
