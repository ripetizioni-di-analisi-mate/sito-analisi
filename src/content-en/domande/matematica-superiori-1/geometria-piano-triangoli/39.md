In an isosceles triangle, the vertex angle is twice each base angle. What classification follows?

- Equilateral
- Obtuse isosceles
- Right isosceles
- Acute isosceles with unequal side lengths

## Explanation

Let $\beta$ be a base angle. The sum is $\beta+\beta+2\beta=180^\circ$. Thus $\beta=45^\circ$, so the vertex angle is $90^\circ$. The triangle retains its two congruent sides and is right. Three $60^\circ$ angles would conflict with the given two-to-one ratio.
