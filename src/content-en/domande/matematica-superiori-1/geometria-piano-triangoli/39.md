An isosceles triangle has vertex angle $56^\circ$. What is the measure of each base angle?

- $124^\circ$
- $28^\circ$
- $62^\circ$
- $34^\circ$

## Explanation

The base angles of an isosceles triangle are congruent. Their sum is one hundred eighty degrees minus the vertex angle. Divide this sum by two.

$$
\beta=\frac{180^\circ-56^\circ}2=62^\circ
$$

The result is $62^\circ$.
