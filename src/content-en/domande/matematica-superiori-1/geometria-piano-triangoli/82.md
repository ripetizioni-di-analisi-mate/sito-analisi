Drop a perpendicular from $A$ to the line containing side $BC$ of a triangle. Its foot lies beyond $C$ on the side away from $B$. Which angle of the triangle is obtuse?

- $\angle A$
- $\angle C$
- $\angle B$
- No angle

## Explanation

Let the foot be $H$. Angle $ACH$ is acute in right triangle $ACH$. Rays $CB$ and $CH$ are opposite, so $\angle ACB$ is supplementary to an acute angle.
