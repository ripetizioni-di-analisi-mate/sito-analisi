From $A$ the perpendicular is drawn to the line containing side $BC$ of triangle $ABC$. The foot $H$ falls on the extension of the side beyond $C$. Which angle of the triangle is obtuse?

- $\widehat A$
- $\widehat C$
- $\widehat B$
- No angle

## Explanation

Triangle $ACH$ has a right angle at $H$, so its other two angles are acute and in particular $\widehat{ACH}<90^\circ$.

The point $H$ lies beyond $C$, therefore the rays $CB$ and $CH$ are opposite and the angles $\widehat{ACB}$ and $\widehat{ACH}$ are adjacent. Two adjacent angles are supplementary, so $\widehat{ACB}=180^\circ-\widehat{ACH}>90^\circ$. The obtuse angle of the triangle is therefore $\widehat C$, and the other two angles are acute, because a triangle has at most one obtuse angle.
