An angle measures $23^\circ$. What is the measure of its complement?

- $157^\circ$
- $23^\circ$
- $67^\circ$
- $113^\circ$

## Explanation

Complementary angles sum to a right angle. Subtract the given measure from ninety degrees. The result is positive because the given angle is acute.

$$
\beta=90^\circ-23^\circ=67^\circ
$$

The result is $67^\circ$.
