Suppose $AB=DE$, $\widehat A=\widehat D$, and $\widehat C=\widehat F$. Which equality between the remaining adjacent angles completes the ASA criterion for sides $AB$ and $DE$?

- $\widehat B=\widehat F$
- $\widehat B=\widehat E$
- $AB=EF$
- $AC=EF$

## Explanation

The angles adjacent to $AB$ are at $A$ and $B$. Those adjacent to $DE$ are at $D$ and $E$. The angle sum $180^\circ$ together with the two given equalities already implies $\widehat B=\widehat E$.
