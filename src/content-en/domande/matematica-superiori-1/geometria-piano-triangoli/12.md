Which angle has a complement of strictly positive measure?

- $90^\circ$
- $99^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $81^\circ$

## Explanation

To find a complement, subtract the given angle from a right angle. The result is positive only when the given angle is below $90^\circ$. Of the four measures, $81^\circ$ leaves $9^\circ$. The value $90^\circ$ would leave zero, and any larger measure would require a negative value, which is impossible for the requested measure.
