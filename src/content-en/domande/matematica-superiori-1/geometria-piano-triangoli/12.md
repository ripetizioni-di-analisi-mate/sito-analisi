An angle measures $26^\circ$. What is the measure of its complement?

- $154^\circ$
- $26^\circ$
- $116^\circ$
- $64^\circ$

## Explanation

Complementary angles sum to a right angle. Subtract the given measure from ninety degrees. The result is positive because the given angle is acute.

$$
\beta=90^\circ-26^\circ=64^\circ
$$

The result is $64^\circ$.
