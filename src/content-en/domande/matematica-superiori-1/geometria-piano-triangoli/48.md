Choose one exterior angle at each vertex of a triangle. Two measure $115^\circ$ and $135^\circ$. What is the third measure?

- $70^\circ$
- $65^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $110^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angles adjacent to the two given exterior angles are $65^\circ$ and $45^\circ$. The third exterior angle is their sum, hence $110^\circ$. Alternatively, adding the three pairs of interior and exterior angles gives three straight angles. Removing the sum of the interiors leaves a total of $360^\circ$ for the exterior angles.
