The interior bisectors of the two angles in a linear pair enclose an angle. What is its measure?

- $45^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- It depends on the angle measures

## Explanation

The angle between the bisectors consists of two sectors, each between a bisector and the shared side. Each sector is half of one of the given angles. Together the halves add to half of $180^\circ$, namely $90^\circ$. The individual angle measures can vary while their original total remains a straight angle.
