By how much does the supplement of an acute angle exceed its complement?

- $90^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- It depends on the angle

## Explanation

The two measures start from a straight angle and a right angle, respectively. The same angle is subtracted in both calculations, so its contribution cancels when comparing the results. This leaves the fixed difference $180^\circ-90^\circ=90^\circ$. The acute-angle assumption ensures that the complement has positive measure.
