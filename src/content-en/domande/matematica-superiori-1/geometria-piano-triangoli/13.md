An angle measures $29^\circ$. What is the measure of its complement?

- $61^\circ$
- $151^\circ$
- $29^\circ$
- $119^\circ$

## Explanation

Complementary angles sum to a right angle. Subtract the given measure from ninety degrees. The result is positive because the given angle is acute.

$$
\beta=90^\circ-29^\circ=61^\circ
$$

The result is $61^\circ$.
