In triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$, $AB=PQ$ and $BC=QR$. Which equality concerns the angle included between the given sides in both triangles?

- $\widehat A=\widehat P$
- $\widehat C=\widehat R$
- $\widehat B=\widehat Q$
- $\widehat B=\widehat R$

## Explanation

The given sides of the first triangle share endpoint $B$, while those of the second meet at $Q$. The included angle has its vertex at the shared endpoint of the two given segments. With two pairs of congruent sides already given, equality at $B$ and $Q$ completes the SAS hypotheses.
