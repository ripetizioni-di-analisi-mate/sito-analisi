Suppose $ABC\cong FDE$, with corresponding vertices in order. Which side corresponds to $AC$?

- $FD$
- $DE$
- $BC$
- $FE$

## Explanation

The first position matches $A$ with $F$, and the third matches $C$ with $E$. The side with these endpoints is $FE$.
