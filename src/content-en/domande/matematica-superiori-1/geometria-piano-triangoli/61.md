Each of two triangles has sides of length $5$ cm and $7$ cm with included angle $41^\circ$. Which criterion guarantees congruence?

- Side-angle-side, with the included angle between the given sides
- Side-side-side, because three sides are known
- Angle-side-angle, because two angles are known
- No criterion, even though the stated angle is included

## Explanation

The two pairs of corresponding sides have equal lengths. The angle between those two sides is also equal in each triangle. These are exactly the hypotheses of the side-angle-side criterion.

The criterion is side-angle-side, with the included angle between the given sides.
