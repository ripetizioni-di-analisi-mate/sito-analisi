In triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$, $\widehat A=\widehat R$, $\widehat B=\widehat Q$, and $AC=RP$. Which step lets you use ASA on the given side?

- Derive $\widehat C=\widehat P$ from the interior angle sum
- Use $\widehat B=\widehat Q$ directly at the ends of $AC$ and $RP$
- Assume $AB=PQ$ because the given angles are equal
- Compare only the two given angle measures

## Explanation

The third angles are found by subtracting equal sums from $180^\circ$, so $\widehat C$ and $\widehat P$ also agree. Now the angles at $A,C$ and $R,P$ are at the ends of the congruent sides. Applying ASA in that order gives $ABC\cong RQP$. Before that calculation, the angles at $B$ and $Q$ were not endpoints of the given side.
