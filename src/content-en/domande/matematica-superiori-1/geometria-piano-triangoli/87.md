Equilateral triangle $ABC$ is rotated through $120^\circ$ about its centre, sending $A$ to $B$. Where does side $BC$ go?

- To side $AB$
- It stays on side $BC$
- To side $CA$
- To a median

## Explanation

The rotation cycles the vertices: $A$ goes to $B$, $B$ to $C$, and $C$ to $A$. The segment from $B$ to $C$ becomes the segment from $C$ to $A$.
