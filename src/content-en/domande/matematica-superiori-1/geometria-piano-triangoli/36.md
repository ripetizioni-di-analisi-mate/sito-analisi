An isosceles triangle has vertex angle $44^\circ$. What is the measure of each base angle?

- $136^\circ$
- $22^\circ$
- $46^\circ$
- $68^\circ$

## Explanation

The base angles of an isosceles triangle are congruent. Their sum is one hundred eighty degrees minus the vertex angle. Divide this sum by two.

$$
\beta=\frac{180^\circ-44^\circ}2=68^\circ
$$

The result is $68^\circ$.
