$M$ is the midpoint of $AB$ and $AM=9$ cm. What is the length of $AB$?

- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The midpoint divides the segment into two congruent parts. The length of MB therefore equals the length of AM. The whole segment is twice either half.

$$
AB=AM+MB=9+9=18
$$

The result is $18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
