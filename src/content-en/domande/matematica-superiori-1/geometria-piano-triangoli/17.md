Two supplementary angles measure $(2x+15)^\circ$ and $(5x-10)^\circ$. Which value of $x$ is compatible?

- $25$
- $35$
- $15$
- $20$

## Explanation

The two angles add to a straight angle. Combining the variable terms and the constants gives $7x+5=180$. Subtracting gives $7x=175$, so $x=25$. The resulting measures are $65^\circ$ and $115^\circ$, both positive. Substituting both expressions into the original requirement checks the very sum used to set up the equation.
