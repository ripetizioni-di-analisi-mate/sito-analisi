An angle measures $78^\circ$. What is the measure of its supplement?

- $102^\circ$
- $12^\circ$
- $78^\circ$
- $258^\circ$

## Explanation

Supplementary angles sum to a straight angle. The unknown and given measures add to one hundred eighty degrees. Their difference from the total gives the required measure.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-78^\circ=102^\circ
$$

The result is $102^\circ$.
