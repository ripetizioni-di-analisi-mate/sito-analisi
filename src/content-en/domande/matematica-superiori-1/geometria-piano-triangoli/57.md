Mark an interior point on each side of a triangle and join the three marks. Counting only the smallest regions, how many positive-area triangles form the subdivision?

- $4$
- $3$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

The joining segments enclose one central triangle. One further triangle remains at each of the three original vertices, for a total of four.
