An isosceles triangle has perimeter $32$ cm and base $8$ cm. What is the length of each of the equal sides?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the base from the perimeter leaves the sum of the two equal sides. Since the lengths are equal, each is half that sum. The result also satisfies the triangle inequality.

$$
2l=32-8=24,\qquad l=12
$$

The result is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
