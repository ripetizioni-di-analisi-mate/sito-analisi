In $ABC$, point $M$ lies on side $BC$ and satisfies $BM=MC$. Which property of $AM$ follows immediately?

- It is the altitude to $BC$
- It bisects the angle at $A$
- It splits the triangle into two congruent triangles
- It is the median to $BC$

## Explanation

Lying on the side and being equally distant from its endpoints makes $M$ the midpoint of $BC$. The segment joining it to the opposite vertex is the median. An altitude would require a right angle, while a bisector would require two equal angles at $A$. Equality of the side's two pieces concerns only their lengths.
