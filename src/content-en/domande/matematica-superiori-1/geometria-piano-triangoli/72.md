In a triangle the side opposite $\angle A$ is the longest and has length $9$. The other sides have lengths $6$ and $8$. Which angular comparison is certain without measuring any angle?

- $\angle A$ equals the sum of the other two
- The other two angles are equal
- $\angle A$ is twice the smallest angle
- $\angle A$ exceeds both other angles

## Explanation

Side lengths and their opposite angles have the same order. The side of length $9$ exceeds both others, so its opposite angle is the largest.
