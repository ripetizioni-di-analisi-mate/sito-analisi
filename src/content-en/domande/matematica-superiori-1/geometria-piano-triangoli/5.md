The points $A,B,C$ lie on a line in that order. If $AC=35\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $BC=2AB+5\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the length of $AB$?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The point $B$ lies on segment $AC$, so $AB+BC=AC$. Let $x$ be the length of $AB$ in centimeters. Substitution gives $x+(2x+5)=35$, so $x=10$.
