Two triangles have equal corresponding angles. Which additional fact is sufficient to conclude they are congruent?

- Both triangles are acute.
- One side and its corresponding side have the same length.
- Both largest angles measure $70^\circ$.
- The angle sum is $180^\circ$ in both.

## Explanation

If a side equals its corresponding side, consider the angles at its two endpoints. These angles already equal their counterparts by hypothesis, so ASA applies.
