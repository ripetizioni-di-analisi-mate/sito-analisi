Points $A$, $B$, $C$ are collinear in this order, with $AB=10$ cm and $BC=6$ cm. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AC$. How long is $BM$?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Since $B$ lies between $A$ and $C$, we have $AC=AB+BC=10+6=16$ cm. The midpoint $M$ divides $AC$ into two congruent segments, so $AM=\dfrac{16}{2}=8$ cm.

We have $AM=8<10=AB$, therefore $M$ lies between $A$ and $B$. Then $BM=AB-AM=10-8=2$ cm. The answer $8$ cm is the length of $AM$, while the answer $4$ cm is the difference $AB-BC$ without dividing by two.
