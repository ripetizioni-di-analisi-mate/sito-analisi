$M$ is the midpoint of $AB$, and $N$ is the midpoint of $MB$. If $AN=27\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the length of $AB$?

- $54\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

If $AB$ measures $x$ centimetres, $AM$ measures $\dfrac x2$ and $MN$ measures $\dfrac x4$. Therefore $AN$ measures $\dfrac{3x}{4}$. Solving $\dfrac{3x}{4}=27$ gives $x=36$.
