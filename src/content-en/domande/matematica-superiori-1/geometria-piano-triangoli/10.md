In order along a line lie $A,M,N,B$. Point $M$ bisects $AB$, $N$ bisects $MB$, and $AN=27$ cm. Reflect $N$ across $M$. How far is its image from $A$?

- $18$ cm
- $9$ cm
- $27$ cm
- $6$ cm

## Explanation

The three equal parts between $A$ and $N$ are $9$ cm long. Point $M$ is $18$ cm from $A$, and $N$ is $9$ cm from $M$. The reflected point is therefore $18-9=9$ cm from $A$.
