Three interior angles measure $x^\circ$, $(x+20)^\circ$, and $(2x-8)^\circ$. How is the triangle classified by its angles?

- Right
- Acute
- Obtuse
- The data are impossible

## Explanation

The sum gives $4x+12=180$. This yields $x=42$, so the angles are $42^\circ$, $62^\circ$, and $76^\circ$. All are positive, below $90^\circ$, and add to $180^\circ$. The classification follows from the measures after substitution. In particular, the coefficient $2$ in the last formula multiplies the variable and does not itself determine the angle type.
