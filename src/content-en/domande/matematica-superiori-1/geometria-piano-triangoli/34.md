Two angles of a triangle measure $38^\circ$ and $49^\circ$. What is the measure of the third angle?

- $87^\circ$
- $93^\circ$
- $142^\circ$
- $131^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angles of a triangle sum to one hundred eighty degrees. First add the two known measures. Subtract this sum from the measure of a straight angle to find the remaining angle.

$$
\gamma=180^\circ-(38^\circ+49^\circ)=93^\circ
$$

The result is $93^\circ$.
