When do the lengths $8$, $x$, and $x+2$, with $x>0$, form a triangle?

- $x\ge3$
- $x>3$
- $3<x<8$
- $x>6$

## Explanation

The two lengths depending on $x$ always differ by $2$ and add to $2x+2$. The side of length $8$ must lie between these values. The condition $2<8$ is automatic, and $8<2x+2$ gives $x>3$. At $x=3$ the two shorter lengths would exactly add to the longest one, so the triangle would be degenerate.
