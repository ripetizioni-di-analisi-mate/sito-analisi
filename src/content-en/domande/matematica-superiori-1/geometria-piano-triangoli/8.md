$B$ is inside segment $AC$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$ and $N$ the midpoint of $BC$. What length does $MN$ have for any position of $B$?

- $\dfrac{AB}{2}$
- $\dfrac{BC}{2}$
- $\dfrac{AC}{4}$
- $\dfrac{AC}{2}$

## Explanation

$B$ lies between $M$ and $N$. Hence add $MB=\dfrac{AB}{2}$ and $BN=\dfrac{BC}{2}$. The whole parts $AB$ and $BC$ add to $AC$, so their halves add to half of $AC$. Moving $B$ repartitions the segment and leaves the total length unchanged.
