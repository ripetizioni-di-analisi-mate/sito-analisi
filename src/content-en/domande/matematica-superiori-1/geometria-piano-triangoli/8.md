$M$ is the midpoint of $AB$ and $AM=15$ cm. What is the length of $AB$?

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The midpoint divides the segment into two congruent parts. The length of MB therefore equals the length of AM. The whole segment is twice either half.

$$
AB=AM+MB=15+15=30
$$

The result is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
