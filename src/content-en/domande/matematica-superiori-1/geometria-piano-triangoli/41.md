One base angle of an isosceles triangle measures $32^\circ$. What is the measure of the vertex angle?

- $116^\circ$
- $148^\circ$
- $58^\circ$
- $64^\circ$

## Explanation

The other base angle has the same measure as the known one. The two base angles therefore use twice the given measure. The vertex angle brings the total to one hundred eighty degrees.

$$
\alpha=180^\circ-2\cdot32^\circ=116^\circ
$$

The result is $116^\circ$.
