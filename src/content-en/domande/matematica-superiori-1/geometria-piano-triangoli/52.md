A triangle has two sides of $4$ and $9$ cm. How many integer lengths in centimetres are possible for the third side?

- $8$
- $9$
- $13$
- $7$

## Explanation

The third side must exceed the difference between the given lengths and be below their sum. It therefore lies in the open interval $5<c<13$. The candidates run from $6$ through $12$, for a total of $12-6+1=7$ integers. The endpoints would make the vertices collinear, so exclude them before counting.
