Two sides of a triangle measure $4$ cm and $6$ cm. Which interval gives the allowed lengths in centimeters for the third side?

- $\left[2, 10\right]$
- $\left(0, 10\right)$
- $\left(10, \infty\right)$
- $\left(2, 10\right)$

## Explanation

A side is shorter than the sum of the other two sides and longer than the absolute value of their difference. Equality would place the vertices on a line, so both endpoints are excluded. Apply both inequalities to the unknown length.

$$
|6-4|<c<4+6,\qquad2<c<10
$$

The result is $\left(2, 10\right)$.
