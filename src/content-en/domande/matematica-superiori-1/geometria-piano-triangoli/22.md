Two lines intersect. One angle measures $39^\circ$. What is the measure of its vertical angle?

- $141^\circ$
- $39^\circ$
- $51^\circ$
- $78^\circ$

## Explanation

The sides of two vertical angles lie on the same lines and point in opposite directions. Each angle is supplementary to the same adjacent angle. They therefore have the same measure.

$$
\beta=\alpha=39^\circ
$$

The result is $39^\circ$.
