In the figure, which angle is vertical to $\widehat{AOC}$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-3.2 xmax=3.2 ymin=-2.3 ymax=2.3 linee="-2.75,0 2.75,0;-2.05,-1.68 2.05,1.68" punti="-2.6,0;2.6,0;-1.9,-1.56;1.9,1.56;0,0" etichette="-2.65,-0.38,A;2.63,-0.38,B;2.01,1.98,C;-2.01,-1.9,D;0.2,-0.42,O" titolo="Lines $AB$ and $CD$ meet at $O$"}

- $\widehat{COB}$
- $\widehat{BOD}$
- $\widehat{DOA}$
- $\widehat{AOB}$

## Explanation

On line $AB$, the ray opposite $OA$ is $OB$. On line $CD$, the ray opposite $OC$ is $OD$. Reversing both sides gives $\widehat{BOD}$. Reversing just one side gives an adjacent angle. The middle letter stays $O$ because the intersection is the common vertex.
