Two sides of a triangle measure $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which of the following lengths can be the third side?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The third length is greater than $8-3=5$ centimeters and less than $8+3=11$ centimeters. Of the listed choices, only $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ lies inside the interval. The lengths $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ would make the vertices collinear.
