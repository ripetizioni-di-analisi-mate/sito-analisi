A segment $AB$ is fixed and a point $C$ of the plane is sought such that $AC=BC=AB$. How many positions can $C$ have?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

The point $C$ is at distance $AB$ from both $A$ and $B$. Therefore $C$ belongs to the circle with centre $A$ and radius $AB$ and to the circle with centre $B$ and radius $AB$.

The two radii are equal and the distance between the centres is $AB$, so the double inequality $0<AB<AB+AB$ holds. The two circles therefore meet in two points, one in each half-plane with respect to line $AB$. There are thus $2$ positions for $C$, and the two equilateral triangles obtained are congruent by SSS.
