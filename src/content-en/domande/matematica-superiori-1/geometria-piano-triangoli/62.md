Fix a segment $AB$ and seek a point $C$ with $AC=BC=AB$. How many positions can $C$ occupy in the plane, counting opposite sides of $AB$ separately?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

Point $C$ must lie on both circles of radius $AB$ centred at $A$ and $B$. They meet at two points symmetric about $AB$.
