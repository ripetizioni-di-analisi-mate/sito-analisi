Two intersecting lines form a $64^\circ$ angle. How many of the four sectors have measure greater than $90^\circ$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The vertical angle also measures $64^\circ$. Each of the other two sectors forms a linear pair with an acute angle and measures $180^\circ-64^\circ=116^\circ$. The two obtuse angles are vertical to one another. As a check, one angle of each type makes a straight angle and two such pairs make a full turn.
