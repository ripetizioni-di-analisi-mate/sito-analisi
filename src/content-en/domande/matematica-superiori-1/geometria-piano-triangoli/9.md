$M$ is the midpoint of $AB$ and $AM=18$ cm. What is the length of $AB$?

- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $54\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The midpoint divides the segment into two congruent parts. The length of MB therefore equals the length of AM. The whole segment is twice either half.

$$
AB=AM+MB=18+18=36
$$

The result is $36\,\mathrm{cm}$.
