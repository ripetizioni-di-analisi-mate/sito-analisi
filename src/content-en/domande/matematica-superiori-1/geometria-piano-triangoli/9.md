$M$ lies inside $AB$, and the lengths $AM$ and $MB$ are in the ratio $2:3$. If $N$ is the midpoint of $AB$, where is it?

- Between $M$ and $B$
- Between $A$ and $M$
- At $M$
- On the extension past $B$

## Explanation

The ratio divides the segment conceptually into five equal parts. Starting at $A$, you reach $M$ after two parts, while the midpoint is after two and a half. So you pass $M$ before reaching $N$. Whether $AB$ or its parts have integer lengths plays no role.
