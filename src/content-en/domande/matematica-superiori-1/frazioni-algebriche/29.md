For $x\ne6$, calculate $\dfrac{x+2}{x-6}-\dfrac{x-1}{x-6}$.

- $\dfrac{3}{x - 6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 6}$
- $\dfrac{2 x + 1}{x - 6}$
- $\dfrac{3}{x + 6}$

## Explanation

The common denominator stays the same. The minus sign in front of the second numerator reverses the sign of each of its terms. The terms in x cancel and three remains.

$$
\frac{x+2-(x-1)}{x-6}=\frac3{x-6}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{3}{x - 6}$.
