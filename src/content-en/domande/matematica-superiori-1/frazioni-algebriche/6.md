Complete the decomposition $\dfrac{2x-19}{x-11}=2+\dfrac{r}{x-11}$ for $x\ne11$. What is the remainder $r$?

- $22$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $19$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $x-11$ gives $2x-19=2(x-11)+r$. The constant must go from $-22$ to $-19$, so $r=3$.
