Which values are excluded from $\dfrac{x+2}{x^2-9}$?

- $x=-2\;\text{or}\;x=3$
- $x=-3\;\text{or}\;x=3$
- $x=3$
- $x=-3$

## Explanation

The denominator factors into two linear terms. A fraction is undefined if either factor in its denominator vanishes. Both opposite values are therefore excluded.

The result is $x=-3\;\text{or}\;x=3$.

The factorization is $(x-3)(x+3)$. At $-3$ and at $3$ a different factor vanishes. The numerator is zero at $-2$, which is allowed.
