A table only accepts integer inputs. What is the domain of $\dfrac1{2x-1}$ under this condition?

- $\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb Z$
- $\mathbb Z\setminus\{1\}$

## Explanation

The denominator's only real zero is $\dfrac12$. Since this is not an integer, it removes no input from the table.
