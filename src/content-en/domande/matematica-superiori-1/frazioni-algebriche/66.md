For $x\ne3$, rewrite $1+\dfrac{3}{x-3}$ as a single fraction.

- $\dfrac{x + 3}{x - 3}$
- $\dfrac{x}{x - 3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{x - 3}$

## Explanation

Write one as the denominator divided by itself. Add the two numerators. Minus $3$ and plus $3$ cancel.

$$
1+\frac{3}{x-3}=\frac{x-3+3}{x-3}=\frac x{x-3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{x}{x - 3}$.

The final denominator retains $x\ne3$. At $x=0$ the second original term equals $-1$, cancels the first addend, and explains the zero in the numerator of the combined form.
