Two quotes for $n>0$ participants give average costs of $\dfrac{60+4n}{n}$ and $\dfrac{30+6n}{n}$, in euros. For how many participants do they agree?

- $10$
- $15$
- $30$
- $45$

## Explanation

The number $n$ is positive, so you can multiply both averages by the same denominator $n$. The comparison reduces to $60+4n=30+6n$. From $30=2n$ you get $n=15$. At fifteen participants each quote totals $120$ euros, or $8$ euros per person.
