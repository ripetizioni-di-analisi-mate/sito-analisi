For $x\ne-2$, calculate $\dfrac{2x+5}{x+2}-\dfrac{x+3}{x+2}$.

- $\dfrac{x + 8}{x + 2}$
- $1$
- $0$
- $x + 2$

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses both signs in the second numerator. The difference is $2x+5-x-3=x+2$, equal to the denominator on the allowed domain.

$$
\frac{2x+5-(x+3)}{x+2}=\frac{x+2}{x+2}=1
$$
