Suppose $\dfrac1u+\dfrac1v=0$ with nonzero real $u$ and $v$. Which relationship is necessary?

- $u-v=1$
- $u=-v$
- $u=v$
- $uv=1$

## Explanation

The common denominator $uv$ is nonzero. The numerator of the sum is $v+u$, so $u+v=0$ must hold.
