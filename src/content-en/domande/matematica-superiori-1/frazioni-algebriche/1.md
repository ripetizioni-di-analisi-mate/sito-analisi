What is the condition for $\dfrac{x+1}{x-3}$ to be defined over the real numbers?

- $x\ne 3$
- $x\ne -1$
- $x>3$
- $x\ne -3$

## Explanation

An algebraic fraction is defined whenever its denominator is nonzero. Its numerator can be zero. Only the zero of the denominator is excluded.

The result is $x\ne 3$.

On the graph this exclusion applies to the whole vertical line at $x=3$. The input $x=-1$ belongs to the domain and gives a value of zero.
