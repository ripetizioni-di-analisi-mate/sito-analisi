Find the conditions under which $\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{(x-11)^2}$ is defined. Check whether the multiplicity of a factor changes the inputs that make the denominator zero.

- $x\ne11$
- $x\ne-2$
- $x>11$
- $x\ne-11$

## Explanation

The squared denominator vanishes exactly when $x-11=0$. The numerator's zero, $-2$, is allowed because the denominator is nonzero there.
