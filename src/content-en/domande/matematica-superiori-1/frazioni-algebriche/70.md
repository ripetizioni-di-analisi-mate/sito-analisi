Can the ratio $\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}$ ever take the value $1$ for $x\ne2$?

- For every allowed $x$
- For no allowed $x$
- Only for $x=0$
- Only for $x=1$

## Explanation

A ratio of $1$ would require $x+1=x-2$. Subtracting $x$ would give the impossible equality $1=-2$.
