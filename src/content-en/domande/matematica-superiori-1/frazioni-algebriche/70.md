For $x\ne3$, write $2-\dfrac{x+1}{x-3}$ as a single fraction.

- $\dfrac{x + 7}{x - 3}$
- $\dfrac{x - 7}{x - 3}$
- $\dfrac{x - 5}{x - 3}$
- $\dfrac{3 x - 5}{x - 3}$

## Explanation

Write $2$ with denominator $x-3$ and numerator $2(x-3)$. Subtract the entire numerator $x+1$, so the constant term becomes $-6-1=-7$.

$$
\frac{2(x-3)-(x+1)}{x-3}=\frac{x-7}{x-3}
$$
