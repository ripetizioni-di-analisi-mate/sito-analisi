To express $\dfrac2{x-11}-\dfrac{22}{x^2-121}$ over $x^2-121$, which numerator replaces the first $2$?

- $2(x+11)$
- $2(x-11)$
- $2x+11$
- $2(x^2-121)$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $(x-11)(x+11)$. Multiplying top and bottom by $x+11$ gives the required numerator, and subtracting $22$ then leaves $2x$.
