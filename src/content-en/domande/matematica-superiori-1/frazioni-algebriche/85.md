For $x\ne7$, calculate $\dfrac{2x}{x-7}-2$.

- $\dfrac{14}{x - 7}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{2}{x - 7}$
- $- \dfrac{14}{x - 7}$

## Explanation

Rewrite two using the denominator of the first fraction. Then subtract the entire new numerator. The terms containing x cancel.

$$
\frac{2x-2(x-7)}{x-7}=\frac{14}{x-7}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{14}{x - 7}$.
