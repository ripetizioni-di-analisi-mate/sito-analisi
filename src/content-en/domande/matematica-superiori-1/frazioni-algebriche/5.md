In $\dfrac2{(x-11)(x+1)}+\dfrac{5x+3}{(x-11)(x+1)}$, which factor can be cancelled only after the numerators are added?

- $x+1$
- $x-11$
- $5x+3$
- $x-1$

## Explanation

The numerator sum is $2+5x+3=5(x+1)$. The common factor is $x+1$, leaving $\dfrac5{x-11}$ with $x\ne-1,11$.
