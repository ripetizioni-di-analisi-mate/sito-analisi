For how many real values is $\dfrac{x}{x^2+4}$ undefined?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The square of $x$ is at least zero, so $x^2+4$ is at least $4$. The denominator is never zero for a real input.

The fraction is defined even at the point where the denominator reaches its minimum, $x=0$. At that point the denominator is $4$ and the numerator is zero.
