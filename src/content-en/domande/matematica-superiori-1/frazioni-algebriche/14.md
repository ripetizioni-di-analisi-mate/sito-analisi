For $x\ne1$, what is the simplified form of $\dfrac{1-x}{x-1}$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $-x$

## Explanation

The expression $1-x$ has its terms in the reverse order of $x-1$. Write it as $-(x-1)$ and divide by the nonzero factor $x-1$. A quick check uses $x=0$, where the ratio becomes $\dfrac{1}{-1}$ and confirms the negative sign.
