For $x\ne11$, which numerical factor clears the inner fraction bars in $\dfrac{1+\dfrac{x-11}{11}}{\dfrac{x-11}{11}}$ when applied to numerator and denominator?

- $1$
- $11$
- $\dfrac1{11}$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Multiplying both parts by $11$ gives $11+x-11=x$ on top and $x-11$ below. The restriction $x\ne11$ still guarantees a nonzero denominator.
