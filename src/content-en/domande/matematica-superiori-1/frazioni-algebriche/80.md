A total of $360$ euros is split into equal shares. The group grows from $9$ to $12$ people. By what percentage does the share decrease relative to its original value?

- $33\tfrac13\%$
- $75\%$
- $3\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The original share is $\dfrac{360}{9}=40$ euros and the new one is $\dfrac{360}{12}=30$ euros. The decrease of $10$ euros is measured against the original $40$, giving $\dfrac{10}{40}=25\%$. The increase in the number of people is calculated using a different base and does not equal the decrease in the share.
