Find the zero of $\dfrac{(x-11)(x+3)}{(x+3)^2}$. Both factors in the numerator vanish at a real input. Check which candidates are allowed.

- $11$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $-11$

## Explanation

A zero must make the numerator zero while leaving the denominator nonzero. At $-3$ both vanish, whereas at $11$ only the numerator vanishes.
