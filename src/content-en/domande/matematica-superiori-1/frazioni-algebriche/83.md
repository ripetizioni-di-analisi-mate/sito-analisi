A formula indexed by whole days since an observation is $M(t)=\dfrac{t^2-t}{t-1}$, with $t\ge0$. On which days does it produce a value?

- $\{0,1,2,3,\ldots\}$
- $\{2,3,4,\ldots\}$
- $\{0,2,3,4,\ldots\}$
- $[0,+\infty)\setminus\{1\}$

## Explanation

The days allowed by the model are the nonnegative integers. The denominator excludes $1$, even though the numerator can be factored as $t(t-1)$ and $M(t)=t$ works on every other day. In particular $t=0$ is allowed and gives a value of zero, while day $1$ has no value.
