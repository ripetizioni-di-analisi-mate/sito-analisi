For $x\ne5$, calculate $\dfrac{2x}{x-5}-2$.

- $0$
- $\dfrac{2}{x - 5}$
- $\dfrac{10}{x - 5}$
- $- \dfrac{10}{x - 5}$

## Explanation

Rewrite two using the denominator of the first fraction. Then subtract the entire new numerator. The terms containing x cancel.

$$
\frac{2x-2(x-5)}{x-5}=\frac{10}{x-5}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{10}{x - 5}$.
