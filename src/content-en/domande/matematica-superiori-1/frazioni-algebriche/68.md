For $x\ne5$, rewrite $1+\dfrac{5}{x-5}$ as a single fraction.

- $\dfrac{x + 5}{x - 5}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{x - 5}$
- $\dfrac{x}{x - 5}$

## Explanation

Write one as the denominator divided by itself. Add the two numerators. Minus $5$ and plus $5$ cancel.

$$
1+\frac{5}{x-5}=\frac{x-5+5}{x-5}=\frac x{x-5}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{x}{x - 5}$.
