For $f(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}$, calculate $f(-3)$, $f(0)$ and $f(1)$ in that order.

- $(-4,-\tfrac12,0)$
- $(4,\tfrac12,0)$
- $(4,-\tfrac12,\tfrac13)$
- $(4,-\tfrac12,0)$

## Explanation

The only excluded input is $-2$, so all three substitutions are allowed. At $-3$ divide $-4$ by $-1$ to get $4$. At $0$ divide $-1$ by $2$. At $1$ the numerator is zero and the denominator is $3$, giving the zero of the fraction.
