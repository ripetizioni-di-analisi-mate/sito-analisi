A spreadsheet evaluates $\dfrac{A}{B}\div\dfrac{C}{D}$. Which cells must contain nonzero numbers, while the remaining one may be zero?

- $A,B,C$
- $A,C,D$
- $A,B,D$
- $B,C,D$

## Explanation

The denominators $B,D$ must be nonzero to define the fractions. The divisor must also be nonzero, requiring $C\ne0$, while $A=0$ is allowed.
