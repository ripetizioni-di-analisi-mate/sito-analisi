For $x\ne4$, calculate $\dfrac{2x}{x-4}-2$.

- $0$
- $\dfrac{8}{x - 4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{x - 4}$
- $- \dfrac{8}{x - 4}$

## Explanation

Rewrite two using the denominator of the first fraction. Then subtract the entire new numerator. The terms containing x cancel.

$$
\frac{2x-2(x-4)}{x-4}=\frac{8}{x-4}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{8}{x - 4}$.
