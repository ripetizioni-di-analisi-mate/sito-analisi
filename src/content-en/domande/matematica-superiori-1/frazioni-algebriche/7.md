For $x\ne-11,11$, calculate $\dfrac{x^2-121}{x-11}\cdot\dfrac1{2(x+11)}$. After factoring the numerator, decide which factors can cancel.

- $\dfrac{x+11}2$
- $\dfrac1{2(x-11)}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$

## Explanation

The difference of squares factors as $(x-11)(x+11)$. Both linear factors cancel in the product because they are known to be nonzero, leaving the coefficient $\dfrac12$.
