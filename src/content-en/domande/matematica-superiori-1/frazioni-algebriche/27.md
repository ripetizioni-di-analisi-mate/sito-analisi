For $x\ne-2$, which equation without fractions is equivalent to $\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}=\dfrac34$?

- $3(x-1)=4(x+2)$
- $4x-1=3x+2$
- $4(x-1)=3(x+2)$
- $4(x-1)=3$

## Explanation

The product of the denominators is $4(x+2)$ and is nonzero on the domain. Multiplying the left side by that product leaves $4(x-1)$; on the right it leaves $3(x+2)$. As a check, this equation gives $x=10$, and the original fraction at $10$ is $\dfrac{9}{12}=\dfrac{3}{4}$.
