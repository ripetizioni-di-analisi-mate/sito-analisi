What is the set of excluded values for $\dfrac{x-1}{x(x-2)^2}$?

- $\{1,2\}$
- $\{0,2\}$
- $\{2\}$
- $\{0,-2,2\}$

## Explanation

The denominator is the product of $x$ and the square of $x-2$. It vanishes at zero or at $2$. The square of the second factor contributes just one excluded value.

At $x=1$ both factors in the denominator equal $1$ and the numerator is zero. This allowed input shows the difference between a zero and an excluded value.
