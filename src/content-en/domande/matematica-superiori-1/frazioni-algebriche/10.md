Reducing $\dfrac2{x-11}-\dfrac{2x}{x^2-121}$ gives $\dfrac{22}{x^2-121}$. At which allowed inputs is it negative?

- $x\ne-11,11$
- $-11<x<11$
- $x<-11$
- $x>11$

## Explanation

The numerator is constant and positive. The denominator is negative when $x^2<121$, strictly between $-11$ and $11$.
