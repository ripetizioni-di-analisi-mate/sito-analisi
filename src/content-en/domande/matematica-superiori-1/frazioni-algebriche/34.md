How can you describe the graph of $y=\dfrac{x}{x-1}-\dfrac1{x-1}$?

- The entire line $y=1$.
- The line $y=1$ with the point $(1,1)$ missing.
- The line $y=0$ with the point $(1,0)$ missing.
- The curve $y=\dfrac{x}{x-1}$.

## Explanation

Subtracting the numerators over the common denominator gives $\dfrac{x-1}{x-1}$. The quotient is $1$ throughout the domain, but at $x=1$ both original fractions are undefined. Show this with an open point at $(1,1)$ on the graph.
