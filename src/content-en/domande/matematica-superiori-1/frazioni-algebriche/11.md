For $x\ne 3$, simplify $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}$.

- $x - 3$
- $x^{2} + 9$
- $x + 3$
- $1$

## Explanation

Factor the numerator as a difference of squares. The common factor can be canceled because it is nonzero on the stated domain. The original condition still applies.

$$
\frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{x-3}=x+3
$$

The result is $x + 3$.

At $x=4$ the original formula gives $\dfrac{7}{1}=7$. The line $y=x+3$ also contains the point $(3,6)$, which remains outside the graph of the fraction.
