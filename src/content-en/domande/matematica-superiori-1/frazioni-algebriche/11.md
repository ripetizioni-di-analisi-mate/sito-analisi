Let $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-121}{3(x-11)}$. Find $f(10)$ by deciding whether to substitute first or simplify first. Check that $10$ is in the domain.

- $-\dfrac13$
- $10$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

The input $10$ is allowed because it differs from $11$. Factoring the numerator and cancelling $x-11$ leaves $\dfrac{x+11}{3}$, which evaluates to $7$.
