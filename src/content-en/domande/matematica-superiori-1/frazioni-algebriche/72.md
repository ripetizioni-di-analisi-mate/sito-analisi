For $x\ne-4$, simplify $\dfrac{-x-4}{x+4}$.

- $1$
- $0$
- $- x$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The numerator is the additive inverse of the entire denominator. Factor minus one out of the numerator. The common nonzero factor cancels, leaving a negative sign.

$$
\frac{-(x+4)}{x+4}=-1
$$

The result is $-1$.
