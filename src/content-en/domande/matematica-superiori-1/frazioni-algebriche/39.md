Reduce $\dfrac{12x^2}{5(x-1)}\cdot\dfrac{15(x-1)^2}{8x^3}$ to lowest terms on the domain $x\ne0,1$.

- $\dfrac{3(x-1)}{2x}$
- $\dfrac{9(x-1)^2}{2x}$
- $\dfrac{9(x-1)}{2x}$
- $\dfrac{9x}{2(x-1)}$

## Explanation

Reduce the coefficients separately, giving $\dfrac{12\cdot15}{5\cdot8}=\dfrac{9}{2}$. The powers of $x$ leave a single $x$ below the fraction bar. The powers of $x-1$ leave one copy above the bar. The cancellations are valid since both factors are nonzero on the domain.
