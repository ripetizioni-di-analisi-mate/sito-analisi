Which final form retains every restriction of $\dfrac{x+5}{x-1}\div\dfrac{x+5}{x-2}$?

- $\dfrac{x-2}{x-1}$ with $x\ne1,2$
- $\dfrac{x-1}{x-2}$ with $x\ne-5,1,2$
- $\dfrac{x-2}{x-1}$ with $x\ne-5,1$
- $\dfrac{x-2}{x-1}$ with $x\ne-5,1,2$

## Explanation

At $1$ the dividend is undefined, at $2$ the divisor is undefined, and at $-5$ the divisor is zero. At every other value you may multiply the first fraction by $\dfrac{x-2}{x+5}$. Cancelling the factor $x+5$ simplifies the calculation with all three exclusions still in force.
