What is the domain of $\dfrac{x+6}{x-1}\div\dfrac0{x+4}$?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-4,1\}$
- $\{-6\}$
- $\{-4,1\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

Exclude $1$ because it makes the dividend undefined and $-4$ because it makes the divisor undefined. Throughout the remaining domain, the divisor is $\dfrac{0}{x+4}=0$. Thus no input meets the requirement that the right-hand side be defined and nonzero. The domain is empty.
