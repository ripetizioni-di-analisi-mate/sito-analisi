For $x\ne 5$ and $x\ne -5$, calculate $\dfrac1{x-5}-\dfrac1{x+5}$.

- $\dfrac{2 x}{x^{2} - 25}$
- $- \dfrac{10}{x^{2} - 25}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{10}{x^{2} - 25}$

## Explanation

Use the product of the two denominators as a common denominator. Subtract the entire second numerator. The terms in x cancel and the two numerical terms add.

$$
\frac{(x+5)-(x-5)}{x^2-25}=\frac{10}{x^2-25}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{10}{x^{2} - 25}$.
