In $\dfrac{(x-11)^2}{(x+11)^2}\div\dfrac{2(x-11)^2}{(x+11)^2}$, which input would make the divisor zero while leaving both fractions defined?

- $-11$
- $0$
- $2$
- $11$

## Explanation

At $x=11$ the divisor's numerator is zero but its denominator is $22^2$. At $x=-11$ the fractions themselves are undefined.
