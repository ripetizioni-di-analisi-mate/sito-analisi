A sensor manual states that $\dfrac1{x^2+bx+c}$ allows every real input except $2$ and $-3$. Which denominator reconstructs that specification?

- $x^2+x+6$
- $x^2-5x+6$
- $x^2+x-6$
- $x^2-x-6$

## Explanation

The excluded inputs must be the zeros of the denominator. The required monic quadratic is $(x-2)(x+3)$, which expands to $x^2+x-6$.
