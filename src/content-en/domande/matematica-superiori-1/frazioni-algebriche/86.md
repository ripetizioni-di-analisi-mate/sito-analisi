Suppose $0<x<y$. Which comparison between fractions always holds?

- $\dfrac{x+y}{y}<1$
- $\dfrac1x>\dfrac1y$
- $\dfrac1x<\dfrac1y$
- $\dfrac xy>1$

## Explanation

The difference between the reciprocals is $\dfrac{y-x}{xy}$. Both numerator and denominator are positive, so the first reciprocal is greater.
