Which point is missing from the graph of $\dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4}$ compared with the line $y=x+4$?

- $(-4,0)$
- $(4,0)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(4,8)$

## Explanation

The fraction simplifies to $x+4$ on the domain $x\ne4$. The line has ordinate $8$ at $x=4$, so $(4,8)$ is excluded from the graph of the fraction.

Trying to obtain the same point from the fraction gives $\dfrac{0}{0}$. For all the remaining points, the horizontal coordinate ranges freely along the line.
