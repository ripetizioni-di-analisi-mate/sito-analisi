You know only that $x>0$. What single exclusion completes the domain for cancelling $x^2-4$ in $\dfrac{(x^2-4)(x+1)}{x^2-4}$?

- $x\ne2$
- $x\ne-2$
- $x\ne1$
- $x>4$

## Explanation

The quadratic factor vanishes at $-2$ and $2$, as the factorisation $(x-2)(x+2)$ shows. The condition $x>0$ already excludes the negative root, leaving the positive root to exclude. The condition $x>4$ would be sufficient but would discard allowed values, for example $x=1$, where the cancelled factor equals $-3$.
