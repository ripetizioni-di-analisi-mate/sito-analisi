The formula $R(x)=\dfrac{x^2-1}{x^2+1}$ is evaluated at $x=t$ and $x=-t$. Which relationship holds for every real $t$?

- $R(-t)R(t)=1$
- $R(-t)=R(t)$
- $R(-t)=-R(t)$
- $R(-t)=R(t)+1$

## Explanation

Substituting $-t$ leaves each square equal to $t^2$. Both numerator and denominator are unchanged, and the denominator never vanishes over the reals.
