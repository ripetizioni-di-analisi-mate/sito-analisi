For $x\ne-2,2$, simplify $\dfrac{(x^2-4)^2}{(x-2)(x+2)^2}$.

- $x + 2$
- $x - 2$
- $1$
- $\left(x - 2\right)^{2}$

## Explanation

The numerator contains two copies of each factor $x-2$ and $x+2$. The denominator cancels both copies of $x+2$ and only one copy of $x-2$.

$$
\frac{(x-2)^2(x+2)^2}{(x-2)(x+2)^2}=x-2
$$

The new polynomial can be evaluated at any real input, but the original fraction excludes both $-2$ and $2$. At $2$ its graph has a hole right on the horizontal axis.
