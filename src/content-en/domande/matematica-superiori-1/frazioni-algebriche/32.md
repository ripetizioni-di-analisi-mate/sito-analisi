Compute $\dfrac1{x-2}-\dfrac1{2-x}$, with $x\ne2$.

- $0$
- $\dfrac2{2-x}$
- $\dfrac1{x-2}$
- $\dfrac2{x-2}$

## Explanation

The denominator $2-x$ equals $-(x-2)$, so the second fraction is the negative of the first. Subtracting its negative adds two copies of $\dfrac{1}{x-2}$. At $x=3$ the two reciprocals are $1$ and $-1$, and their difference is $2$.
