What is the domain of $\dfrac1{(x^2+2x+5)(x-5)}$?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-1,5\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-5,5\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{5\}$

## Explanation

Complete the square in the first factor to obtain $x^2+2x+5=(x+1)^2+4$, which is positive even when $x=-1$. The first factor therefore contributes no exclusions. The linear factor $x-5$ vanishes at $5$, the sole value at which the division is undefined.
