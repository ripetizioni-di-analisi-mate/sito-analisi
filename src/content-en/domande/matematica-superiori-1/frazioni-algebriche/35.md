For $x\ne0$ and $y\ne0$, you multiply $\dfrac xy$ by a second factor. Which factor guarantees a product of $x^2$ for every allowed pair?

- $\dfrac xy$
- $x+y$
- $xy$
- $\dfrac yx$

## Explanation

The missing factor is $x^2:\dfrac xy$. Multiplying by the reciprocal gives $xy$, which cancels the denominator $y$ in the original product.
