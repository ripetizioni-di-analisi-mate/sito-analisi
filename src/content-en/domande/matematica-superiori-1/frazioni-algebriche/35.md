For $x\ne -2,2$, simplify $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x+2}\cdot\dfrac{x-1}{x-2}$.

- $x - 2$
- $1$
- $x - 1$
- $x + 1$

## Explanation

Factoring the difference of squares gives the numerator factors $(x-2)(x+2)$. Both are nonzero on the specified domain, so they cancel the two denominators.

$$
\frac{(x-2)(x+2)(x-1)}{(x+2)(x-2)}=x-1
$$
