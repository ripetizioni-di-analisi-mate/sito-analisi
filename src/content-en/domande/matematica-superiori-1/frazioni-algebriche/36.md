For which real value of $k$ is the fraction $\dfrac1{(k-2)x+5}$ defined for every real $x$?

- $k=0$
- $k=-5$
- $k=5$
- $k=2$

## Explanation

If $k-2\ne0$, the linear denominator vanishes at $x=-\dfrac{5}{k-2}$, so there is always one excluded value. When $k=2$, the term in $x$ disappears and the number $5$ remains. The fraction becomes $\dfrac{1}{5}$ over all the real numbers.
