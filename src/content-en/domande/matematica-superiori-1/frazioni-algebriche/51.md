Add $\dfrac1{n-1}-\dfrac1n$ for the integers $n=2,3,4,5$. What remains after cancellation?

- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac54$

## Explanation

Each negative term through $-\dfrac14$ cancels with the next positive term. This leaves $1-\dfrac15=\dfrac45$.
