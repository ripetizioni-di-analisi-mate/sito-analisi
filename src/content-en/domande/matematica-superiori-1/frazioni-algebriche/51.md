Which values of $x$ must be excluded from the domain of $\dfrac{3}{x^2-x}-\dfrac{1}{x^2-1}$?

- $\{0,1\}$
- $\{1\}$
- $\{-1,0,1\}$
- $\{-1,1\}$

## Explanation

A difference of algebraic fractions is defined when both fractions are defined. The first denominator factors as $x^2-x=x(x-1)$, so it vanishes for $x=0$ and for $x=1$.

The second denominator factors as $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$, so it vanishes for $x=1$ and for $x=-1$. Therefore all the values that make at least one of the two denominators zero must be excluded, that is $\{-1,0,1\}$. The answer $\{1\}$ keeps only the value common to the two denominators.
