For $x\ne6$, rewrite $1+\dfrac{6}{x-6}$ as a single fraction.

- $\dfrac{x}{x - 6}$
- $\dfrac{x + 6}{x - 6}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{x - 6}$

## Explanation

Write one as the denominator divided by itself. Add the two numerators. Minus $6$ and plus $6$ cancel.

$$
1+\frac{6}{x-6}=\frac{x-6+6}{x-6}=\frac x{x-6}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{x}{x - 6}$.
