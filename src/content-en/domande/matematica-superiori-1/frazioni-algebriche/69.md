A simplified formula agrees with $y=x+4$ except at $x=1$. Which numerator over $x-1$ reconstructs exactly that graph?

- $x^2+3x-4$
- $x^2+5x+4$
- $x^2-3x-4$
- $x+4$

## Explanation

The numerator must be $(x-1)(x+4)$ to recover $x+4$ wherever the fraction is defined. Expansion gives $x^2+3x-4$, and the denominator removes exactly input $1$.
