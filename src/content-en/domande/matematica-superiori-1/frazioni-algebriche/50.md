A total dose $D>0$ is shared equally. With $n\ge1$, what is the decrease per container when the number of containers increases from $n$ to $n+1$?

- $\dfrac{2D}{n(n+1)}$
- $\dfrac D{n(n+1)}$
- $\dfrac D{n+1}$
- $D$

## Explanation

The first dose is $\dfrac Dn$, and the second is $\dfrac D{n+1}$. Subtracting over the common denominator leaves numerator $D(n+1)-Dn=D$.
