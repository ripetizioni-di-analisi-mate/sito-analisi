For $x\ne1$, calculate $\dfrac1{x-1}-\dfrac1{1-x}$.

- $0$
- $\dfrac{2}{x - 1}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{x - 1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 1}$

## Explanation

The denominators are opposites, so the second fraction is $-\dfrac1{x-1}$. Subtracting it adds two copies of the first fraction.

$$
\frac1{x-1}-\left(-\frac1{x-1}\right)=\frac2{x-1}
$$

The final numerator is always $2$. The sign of the difference thus depends entirely on the denominator and changes when you move from one side of $1$ to the other.
