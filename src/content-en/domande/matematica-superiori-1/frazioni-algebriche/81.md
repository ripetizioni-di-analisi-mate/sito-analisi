One tap fills a tank in $a$ hours and another in $b$ hours, each at a constant positive rate. Which formula gives their filling time together?

- $\dfrac{ab}{a+b}$
- $a+b$
- $\dfrac{a+b}{2}$
- $\dfrac1{a+b}$

## Explanation

In tanks per hour, the rates add to $\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b=\dfrac{a+b}{ab}$. The time for one tank is the reciprocal of this combined rate.
