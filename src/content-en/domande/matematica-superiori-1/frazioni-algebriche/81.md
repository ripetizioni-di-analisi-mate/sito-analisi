For $x\ne3$, calculate $\dfrac{2x}{x-3}-2$.

- $\dfrac{6}{x - 3}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{2}{x - 3}$
- $- \dfrac{6}{x - 3}$

## Explanation

Rewrite two using the denominator of the first fraction. Then subtract the entire new numerator. The terms containing x cancel.

$$
\frac{2x-2(x-3)}{x-3}=\frac{6}{x-3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{6}{x - 3}$.
