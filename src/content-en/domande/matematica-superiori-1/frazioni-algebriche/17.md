Which step is valid for $x\ne0$ when simplifying $\dfrac{x^2+4x}{2x}$?

- $\dfrac{x(x+4)}{2x}=\dfrac{x+4}{2}$
- $\dfrac{x^2+4x}{2x}=\dfrac{x^2+4}{2}$
- $\dfrac{x^2+4x}{2x}=\dfrac{x+4x}{2}$
- $\dfrac{x^2+4x}{2x}=\dfrac{x+4}{2x}$

## Explanation

The factor $x$ must be factored out of both terms in the sum $x^2+4x$. It then divides the entire numerator and the denominator, so one copy can cancel. At $x=2$ the original value is $\dfrac{12}{4}=3$, which agrees with $\dfrac{2+4}{2}$.
