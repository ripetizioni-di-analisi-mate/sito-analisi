For $x\ne0$, simplify $\dfrac{4x^2+8x}{4x}$.

- $x + 2$
- $x + 8$
- $4 x + 2$
- $x^{2} + 2$

## Explanation

Factor out the common factor $4 x$ from the numerator. The denominator equals that common factor. After cancellation, the polynomial in the second factor remains.

$$
\frac{4x(x+2)}{4x}=x+2
$$

The result is $x + 2$.
