For $x\ne 6$ and $x\ne -6$, calculate $\dfrac1{x-6}-\dfrac1{x+6}$.

- $\dfrac{12}{x^{2} - 36}$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{x^{2} - 36}$
- $- \dfrac{12}{x^{2} - 36}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Use the product of the two denominators as a common denominator. Subtract the entire second numerator. The terms in x cancel and the two numerical terms add.

$$
\frac{(x+6)-(x-6)}{x^2-36}=\frac{12}{x^2-36}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{12}{x^{2} - 36}$.
