With $x\ne0,-1$, you multiply both parts above and below the main bar of $\dfrac{\dfrac1{x+1}+\dfrac2x}{\dfrac1x}$ by $x$. What expression do you get?

- $\dfrac{3x+2}{x+1}$
- $\dfrac{x+2}{x+1}$
- $\dfrac{3x+2}{x(x+1)}$
- $\dfrac{x}{x+1}+\dfrac2x$

## Explanation

Above the bar, the factor $x$ multiplies both terms, giving $\dfrac{x}{x+1}+2$. Below it, $x$ multiplies $\dfrac{1}{x}$ and leaves $1$. Combine the sum to get $\dfrac{x+2(x+1)}{x+1}$. The result is $\dfrac{3x+2}{x+1}$, with $0$ still excluded by the original formula.
