Which point on the graph of $y=2+\dfrac1{x-3}$ has vertical coordinate $4$?

- $(5,4)$
- $(\tfrac52,4)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(\tfrac72,4)$

## Explanation

Set the requested vertical coordinate and subtract the constant term from both sides. This leaves $2=\dfrac{1}{x-3}$. The denominator must be nonzero, so you can multiply and solve $2(x-3)=1$. You find $x=\dfrac{7}{2}$, which is allowed and lies $\dfrac{1}{2}$ unit to the right of the excluded value. There the fraction contributes exactly $2$.
