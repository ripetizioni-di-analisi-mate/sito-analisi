For which inputs does $\dfrac{x^2-22x+121}{(x-11)^2}=1$ hold?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{11\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-11\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{11\}$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator are identical because the trinomial is $(x-11)^2$. Their ratio is $1$ wherever they are nonzero, while input $11$ would give $0/0$.
