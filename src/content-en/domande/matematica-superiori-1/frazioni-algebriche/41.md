Let $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4}$. What happens at $x=-4$ and at $x=4$?

- $f(-4)=0$ and $f(4)$ is undefined
- $f(4)=0$ and $f(-4)$ is undefined
- $f(-4)=0$ and $f(4)=8$
- Both values are excluded

## Explanation

At $-4$ the numerator is zero and the denominator is $-8$, so the value is zero. At $4$ the denominator vanishes. Reducing to $x+4$ is valid only for $x\ne4$, and at that point the value $8$ belongs only to the line used for comparison.
