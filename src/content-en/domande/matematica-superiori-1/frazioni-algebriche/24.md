For $x\ne6$, calculate $\dfrac2{x-6}+\dfrac3{x-6}$.

- $\dfrac{5}{2 \left(x - 6\right)}$
- $\dfrac{6}{x - 6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{x - 6}$

## Explanation

The fractions already have the same denominator. Add their numerators and retain that denominator. The same domain condition applies to the sum.

$$
\frac2{x-6}+\frac3{x-6}=\frac5{x-6}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{5}{x - 6}$.
