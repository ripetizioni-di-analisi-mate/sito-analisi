For which allowed values of $x$ does $\dfrac{x+2}{x+5}=\dfrac25$ actually hold?

- $\{-5\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-5\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

Impose $x\ne-5$ and multiply both sides by the common denominator $5(x+5)$. This gives $5x+10=2x+10$, equivalent to $3x=0$. The solution $x=0$ belongs to the domain and does give $\dfrac{2}{5}$. The equality therefore holds at a single input.
