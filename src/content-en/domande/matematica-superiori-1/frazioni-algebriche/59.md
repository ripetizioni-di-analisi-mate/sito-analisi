For which $x$ is $\dfrac{(x+1)^2}{x-3}$ greater than or equal to zero?

- $[3,+\infty)$
- $(-1,3)$
- $\{-1\}\cup(3,+\infty)$
- $\{-1\}\cup[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The numerator is positive except at $-1$, where it is zero and the denominator is $-4$. Whenever the numerator is positive, the fraction has the sign of $x-3$. Hence the fraction is positive for $x>3$ and zero at $-1$. Leave out $3$, where the fraction is undefined.
