How does $y=\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{(x-1)^2}$ behave at allowed inputs on either side of its zero $x=2$?

- It changes from negative to positive.
- It is positive on both sides.
- It changes from positive to negative.
- It is negative on both sides.

## Explanation

The denominator is a strictly positive square at every allowed input. The numerator is also strictly positive to the left and right of $2$, so the ratio is positive there. At $2$ the vertical coordinate is zero and the denominator equals $1$. The graph reaches the axis there without crossing it.
