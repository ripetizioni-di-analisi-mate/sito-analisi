At what value of $x$ is the fraction $\dfrac{x-4}{x+1}$ equal to zero?

- $-1$
- $4$
- $-4$
- $0$

## Explanation

A fraction equals zero when its numerator is zero and its denominator is nonzero. The numerator vanishes at the given value. The denominator is $4$ plus one there, which is nonzero.

$$
x-4=0\Rightarrow x=4,\qquad 4+1\ne0
$$

The result is $4$.
