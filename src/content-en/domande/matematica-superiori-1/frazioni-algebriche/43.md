Which values are excluded from $\dfrac{x-2}{x+1}\div\dfrac{x-5}{x+3}$?

- $-3,-1$
- $-3,-1,2,5$
- $-3,-1,5$
- $-1,5$

## Explanation

The two original denominators give $x\ne-1$ and $x\ne-3$. The fraction acting as divisor must also be nonzero, so exclude $5$ as well. At $x=2$ the dividend is zero. Since the divisor is defined and equal to $-\dfrac{3}{5}$, the quotient is zero at that point.
