For $x\ne 5$ and $x\ne -5$, calculate $\dfrac1{x-5}+\dfrac1{x+5}$.

- $\dfrac{2}{x^{2} - 25}$
- $\dfrac{10}{x^{2} - 25}$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{x^{2} - 25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x}$

## Explanation

The product of the two linear factors is a common denominator. The new numerators are x plus $5$ and x minus $5$, respectively. Their constant terms cancel when added.

$$
\frac{x+5+x-5}{(x-5)(x+5)}=\frac{2x}{x^2-25}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{2 x}{x^{2} - 25}$.
