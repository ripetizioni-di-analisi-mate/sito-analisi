The numerator of $\dfrac{x-4}{x^2+4}$ is zero at $4$. What is the domain of the fraction?

- $\mathbb R\setminus\{4\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2,2\}$
- $[0,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The square $x^2$ is at least zero for every real number, so $x^2+4\ge4$ and the denominator never vanishes. At $x=4$ the fraction equals $\dfrac{0}{20}$, which is defined. The set of all real numbers contains this zero of the fraction as well as every other value of $x$.
