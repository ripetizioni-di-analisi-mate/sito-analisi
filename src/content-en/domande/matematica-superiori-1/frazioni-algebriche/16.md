For $x\ne0$, simplify $\dfrac{3x^2+6x}{3x}$.

- $x + 6$
- $3 x + 2$
- $x^{2} + 2$
- $x + 2$

## Explanation

Factor out the common factor $3 x$ from the numerator. The denominator equals that common factor. After cancellation, the polynomial in the second factor remains.

$$
\frac{3x(x+2)}{3x}=x+2
$$

The result is $x + 2$.

At $x=-2$ the remaining factor vanishes. The original denominator is $-6$, so the zero occurs at a point where the function is defined.
