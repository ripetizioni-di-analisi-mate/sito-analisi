Two formulas agree at $x=0$. Which allowed input proves that $\dfrac{x}{x+1}$ and $\dfrac{x^2}{x+1}$ are not equivalent?

- $2$
- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

At $x=2$ the values are $\dfrac23$ and $\dfrac43$, which differ. At $0$ and $1$ the numerators agree, while $-1$ is outside the domain.
