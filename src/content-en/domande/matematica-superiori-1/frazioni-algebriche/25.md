For $x\ne1$, simplify $\dfrac{x+2}{x-1}+\dfrac{2x-5}{x-1}$.

- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{x - 1}$
- $3 x - 3$
- $0$

## Explanation

Adding the numerators gives $3x-3=3(x-1)$. The factor $x-1$ can be canceled on the specified domain, leaving a constant result.

$$
\frac{x+2+2x-5}{x-1}=\frac{3(x-1)}{x-1}=3
$$
