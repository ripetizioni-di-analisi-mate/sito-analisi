Evaluate $-\left(\dfrac{x-2}{x+2}\div\dfrac{2-x}{x+2}\right)$ for $x\ne\pm2$.

- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{(x+2)^2}$

## Explanation

The dividend is the negative of the divisor because $2-x=-(x-2)$. The common denominator is nonzero, and $x-2$ is nonzero as well. Dividing a number by its negative gives $-1$. The minus sign outside the brackets changes the sign again, so the final value is $1$.
