For $x\ne 5$ and $x\ne -5$, calculate $\dfrac{x-5}{x+5}\cdot\dfrac{x+5}{2(x-5)}$.

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators. The two common linear factors are nonzero under the given conditions. They cancel, leaving only the numerical factor.

$$
\frac{(x-5)(x+5)}{2(x+5)(x-5)}=\frac12
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
