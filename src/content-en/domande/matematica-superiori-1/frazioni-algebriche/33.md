For $x\ne0,-1$, suppose $\dfrac{A(x)}{x(x+1)}+\dfrac1{x+1}=\dfrac4x$. What must the polynomial $A(x)$ be for this to hold at every allowed input?

- $3x+4$
- $4x+4$
- $4x+3$
- $3x$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $x(x+1)$. After you multiply by that product, the left side becomes $A(x)+x$ and the right side $4(x+1)$. From $A(x)+x=4x+4$ you get $A(x)=3x+4$. At $x=1$ the numerator you found gives $\dfrac{7}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}=4$.
