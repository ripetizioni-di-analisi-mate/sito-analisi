For $x\ne5$, calculate $\dfrac2{x-5}+\dfrac3{x-5}$.

- $\dfrac{5}{2 \left(x - 5\right)}$
- $\dfrac{6}{x - 5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{x - 5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 5}$

## Explanation

The fractions already have the same denominator. Add their numerators and retain that denominator. The same domain condition applies to the sum.

$$
\frac2{x-5}+\frac3{x-5}=\frac5{x-5}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{5}{x - 5}$.
