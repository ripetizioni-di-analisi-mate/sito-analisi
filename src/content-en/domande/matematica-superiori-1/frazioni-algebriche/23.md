The graph of $\dfrac{x^2+3x+2}{x^2-1}$ has a hole at $x=-1$. What are the coordinates of the missing point?

- $(-1,0)$
- $(-1,\tfrac12)$
- $(-1,-\tfrac12)$
- $(-1,-1)$

## Explanation

Factoring gives $(x+1)(x+2)$ and $(x+1)(x-1)$. The fraction agrees with $\dfrac{x+2}{x-1}$ for $x\ne\pm1$. Substituting $-1$ into the reduced formula gives $\dfrac{1}{-2}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. This is the vertical coordinate of the hole, while $x=-1$ is excluded from the original formula.
