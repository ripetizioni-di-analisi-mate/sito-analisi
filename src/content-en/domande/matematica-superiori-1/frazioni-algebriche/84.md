For $x\ne6$, calculate $\dfrac{2x}{x-6}-2$.

- $0$
- $\dfrac{2}{x - 6}$
- $- \dfrac{12}{x - 6}$
- $\dfrac{12}{x - 6}$

## Explanation

Rewrite two using the denominator of the first fraction. Then subtract the entire new numerator. The terms containing x cancel.

$$
\frac{2x-2(x-6)}{x-6}=\frac{12}{x-6}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{12}{x - 6}$.
