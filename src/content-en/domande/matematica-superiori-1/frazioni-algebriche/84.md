For which real constant $k$ does $\dfrac{x}{x-2}+\dfrac{k}{x-2}$ equal $1$ for every $x\ne2$?

- $2$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Combine the terms over $x-2$ and use $1=\dfrac{x-2}{x-2}$ on the domain. Equating the numerators gives $x+k=x-2$, so $k=-2$. With that value, the numerator and denominator agree at every allowed input. At $2$ the original sum is still undefined even with this choice.
