When is $\dfrac{x-4}{x+1}$ strictly negative?

- $x<-1$
- $x>4$
- $-1\le x\le4$
- $-1<x<4$

## Explanation

Between $-1$ and $4$, the denominator is positive and the numerator is negative. Before $-1$ both are negative, and after $4$ both are positive. The endpoints stay out for two different reasons. At $-1$ the quotient is undefined, while at $4$ it is zero.
