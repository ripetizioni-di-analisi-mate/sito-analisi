At what value of $x$ is the fraction $\dfrac{x-6}{x+1}$ equal to zero?

- $-1$
- $-6$
- $0$
- $6$

## Explanation

A fraction equals zero when its numerator is zero and its denominator is nonzero. The numerator vanishes at the given value. The denominator is $6$ plus one there, which is nonzero.

$$
x-6=0\Rightarrow x=6,\qquad 6+1\ne0
$$

The result is $6$.
