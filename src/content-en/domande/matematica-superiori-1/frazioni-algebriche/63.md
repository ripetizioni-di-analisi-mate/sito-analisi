The numerator of a fraction is always positive. The denominator changes from positive to negative across $x=a$. What happens to the sign of the quotient?

- It stays positive
- It stays negative
- It goes from positive to negative
- It goes from negative to positive

## Explanation

With positive numerator the quotient has the sign of its denominator. It is therefore positive on the first side and negative on the other.
