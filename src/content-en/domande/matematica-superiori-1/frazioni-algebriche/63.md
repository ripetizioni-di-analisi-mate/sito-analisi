At what value of $x$ is the fraction $\dfrac{x-5}{x+1}$ equal to zero?

- $-1$
- $-5$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

A fraction equals zero when its numerator is zero and its denominator is nonzero. The numerator vanishes at the given value. The denominator is $5$ plus one there, which is nonzero.

$$
x-5=0\Rightarrow x=5,\qquad 5+1\ne0
$$

The result is $5$.
