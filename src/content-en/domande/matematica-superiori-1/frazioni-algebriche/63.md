At which point does $-\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{(x+2)^2}$ change sign?

- Only at $1$.
- Only at $-2$.
- At no point.
- At both $-2$ and $1$.

## Explanation

The denominator is positive throughout the domain, which excludes $-2$. The square in the numerator is positive on both sides of $1$ and zero only at $1$. The minus sign makes the fraction negative at every allowed input other than $1$. Thus there is no transition from one sign to the other.
