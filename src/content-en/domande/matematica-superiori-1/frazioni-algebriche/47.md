At which allowed input is $\dfrac2{x^2-9}-\dfrac1{x-3}=0$?

- $1$
- $-3$
- $-1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The common denominator factors as $(x-3)(x+3)$, which excludes the two opposite roots. Multiply the second numerator by its missing factor $x+3$, then subtract the entire product. Over the common denominator the numerator is $2-(x+3)=-x-1$. It vanishes at $-1$, which is allowed. The two original fractions both have value $-\dfrac{1}{4}$ there.
