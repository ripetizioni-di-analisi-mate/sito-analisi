For $x\ne 4$ and $x\ne -4$, calculate $\dfrac1{x-4}-\dfrac1{x+4}$.

- $\dfrac{2 x}{x^{2} - 16}$
- $- \dfrac{8}{x^{2} - 16}$
- $\dfrac{8}{x^{2} - 16}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Use the product of the two denominators as a common denominator. Subtract the entire second numerator. The terms in x cancel and the two numerical terms add.

$$
\frac{(x+4)-(x-4)}{x^2-16}=\frac{8}{x^2-16}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{8}{x^{2} - 16}$.
