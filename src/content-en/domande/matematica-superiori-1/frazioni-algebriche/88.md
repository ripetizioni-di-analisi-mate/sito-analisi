Compare $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}$ and $g(x)=\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}-1$. At which inputs are both defined and their values equal?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{1,2\}$

## Explanation

The first fraction reduces to $x+1$ for $x\ne1$. The second fraction is $x+2$ for $x\ne2$, so subtracting $1$ gives $x+1$ again. The values agree on the intersection of the domains. At $1$ only $g(1)$ exists, while at $2$ only $f(2)$ exists.
