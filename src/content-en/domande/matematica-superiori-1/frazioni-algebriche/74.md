An order for $n$ people costs $18$ euros for delivery plus $3$ euros per person. What is the average cost per person, together with the appropriate domain?

- $18+\dfrac3n$ with $n\in\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$
- $3+\dfrac{18}{n}$ with $n\in\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$
- $3+\dfrac{18}{n}$ with $n\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$
- $3+\dfrac{18}{n}$ with $n\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$

## Explanation

The group pays $18+3n$ euros. Dividing the full amount by $n$, each person pays their own $3$ euros and a delivery share of $\dfrac{18}{n}$. The number of participants must be a positive integer. With $n=6$ each person pays $6$ euros, and the six shares add up to the total of $36$.
