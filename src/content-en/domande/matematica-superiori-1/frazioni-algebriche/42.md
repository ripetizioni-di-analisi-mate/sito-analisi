At which allowed input is $\dfrac{1-x}{x+2}\cdot\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-1}$ zero?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The initial exclusions are $-2$ and $1$. Rewriting $1-x=-(x-1)$ and $x^2-4=(x-2)(x+2)$ reduces the product to $-(x-2)=2-x$. The value $x=2$ makes this form zero and is in the domain. The candidate $x=-2$ makes one numerator zero, but also makes an original denominator zero.
