Which formula and conditions describe $\left(\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}\right)^{-2}$?

- $\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{(x-1)^2}$ with $x\ne-2,1$
- $\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{(x-1)^2}$ with $x\ne1$
- $\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{(x+2)^2}$ with $x\ne-2,1$
- $-\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{(x-1)^2}$ with $x\ne-2,1$

## Explanation

The base is defined for $x\ne-2$ and is zero at $x=1$. A negative exponent requires a nonzero base, so exclude $1$ as well. Take the reciprocal of the base and then square it. On the domain the power is strictly positive.
