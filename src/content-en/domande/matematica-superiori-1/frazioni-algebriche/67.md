For $x\ne4$, rewrite $1+\dfrac{4}{x-4}$ as a single fraction.

- $\dfrac{x + 4}{x - 4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{x}{x - 4}$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{x - 4}$

## Explanation

Write one as the denominator divided by itself. Add the two numerators. Minus $4$ and plus $4$ cancel.

$$
1+\frac{4}{x-4}=\frac{x-4+4}{x-4}=\frac x{x-4}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{x}{x - 4}$.
