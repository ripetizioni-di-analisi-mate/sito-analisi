For $h(x)=\dfrac{x^2-4}{(x-2)^2}$, compare $h(\tfrac{21}{10})$ and $h(\tfrac{201}{100})$. Which pair, in that order, is correct?

- $(41,41)$
- $(401,41)$
- $(41,401)$
- $(\tfrac{41}{10},\tfrac{401}{100})$

## Explanation

After one copy of $x-2$ cancels, $\dfrac{x+2}{x-2}=1+\dfrac{4}{x-2}$ remains. The positive distances from $2$ are $\dfrac{1}{10}$ and $\dfrac{1}{100}$. This gives $1+40=41$ and $1+400=401$. One copy of $x-2$ remains in the denominator, so approaching $2$ from the right makes the values grow sharply.
