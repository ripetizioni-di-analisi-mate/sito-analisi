Find constants $A,B$ such that $\dfrac1{x(x+1)}=\dfrac Ax+\dfrac B{x+1}$ for $x\ne0,-1$. Which pair $(A,B)$ works?

- $(1,-1)$
- $(-1,1)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(0,1)$

## Explanation

Bringing the sum over a common denominator gives numerator $(A+B)x+A$. To make it identically $1$, the coefficients must satisfy $A=1$ and $A+B=0$.
