For $x\ne0,2$, calculate $\dfrac1{x(x-2)}+\dfrac1x$.

- $\dfrac{x - 1}{x^{2} - 2 x}$
- $\dfrac{2}{x \left(x - 2\right)}$
- $\dfrac{x + 1}{x \left(x - 2\right)}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 2}$

## Explanation

The denominator of the first fraction already contains $x$. To match it, multiply the numerator and denominator of the second fraction by $x-2$. The combined numerator is $1+x-2$.

$$
\frac1{x(x-2)}+\frac{x-2}{x(x-2)}=\frac{x-1}{x(x-2)}
$$
