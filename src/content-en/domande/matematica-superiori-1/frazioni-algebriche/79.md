A cyclist rides $60$ km at speed $v>0$ and another $60$ km at speed $2v$. What is the average speed over the whole trip?

- $\dfrac{3v}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2v}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4v}{3}$
- $2v$

## Explanation

Add the times for the two legs. You get $\dfrac{60}{v}+\dfrac{60}{2v}=\dfrac{90}{v}$ hours if $v$ is measured in kilometres per hour. Divide the total distance $120$ by this time to get $\dfrac{120}{\dfrac{90}{v}}=\dfrac{4v}{3}$. The arithmetic mean of the two speeds would weight them equally in time, while the first leg takes twice as long.
