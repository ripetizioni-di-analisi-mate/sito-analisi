A rectangle has area $x^2-9$ and one side of length $x-3$. With all lengths measured in the same unit, which expression describes the other side and its domain in this model?

- $x-3$ with $x>3$
- $x+3$ with $x>3$
- $x+3$ with $x\ne3$
- $x+3$ with $x\ge3$

## Explanation

A rectangle requires the given side to have strictly positive length, so $x-3>0$. Dividing the area by the given side and using $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$ gives the other length. For $x>3$ the second side is positive as well. The value $x=3$ would give a side of zero length.
