Which values must be excluded for $\dfrac1{1+\dfrac1{x-3}}$ to be defined?

- $2$ and $3$
- $3$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The inner fraction requires $x\ne3$. On the remaining domain, the denominator of the outer fraction is $1+\dfrac{1}{x-3}=\dfrac{x-2}{x-3}$, so it vanishes at $x=2$. Keep both conditions when you simplify the reciprocal to $\dfrac{x-3}{x-2}$. The reduced formula alone would also allow evaluation at $x=3$.
