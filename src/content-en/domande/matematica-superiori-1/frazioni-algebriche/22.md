For $x\ne4$, calculate $\dfrac2{x-4}+\dfrac3{x-4}$.

- $\dfrac{5}{2 \left(x - 4\right)}$
- $\dfrac{5}{x - 4}$
- $\dfrac{6}{x - 4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 4}$

## Explanation

The fractions already have the same denominator. Add their numerators and retain that denominator. The same domain condition applies to the sum.

$$
\frac2{x-4}+\frac3{x-4}=\frac5{x-4}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{5}{x - 4}$.
