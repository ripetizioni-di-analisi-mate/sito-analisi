The denominator $(x-11)^2(x+11)$ contains three linear factors when repeats are counted. How many distinct real inputs must be excluded?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The repeated factor excludes $11$ just once. The other factor excludes $-11$, giving two distinct exclusions.
