Which substitution turns $\dfrac{x^2-2x+1}{x-1}$ into the simpler form $\dfrac{u^2}{u}$, with $u\ne0$?

- $u=x^2-1$
- $u=1-x^2$
- $u=x-1$
- $u=x+1$

## Explanation

The numerator is the square of the denominator. Calling $x-1$ by the name $u$ gives $u^2$ and $u$ and turns the exclusion into $u\ne0$.
