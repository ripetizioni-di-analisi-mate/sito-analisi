For $x\ne5$, simplify $\dfrac{5-x}{(x-5)^2}$.

- $\dfrac{1}{x - 5}$
- $-1$
- $- \dfrac{1}{x - 5}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{\left(x - 5\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

The numerator is $-(x-5)$. Cancel one copy of the factor $x-5$ and retain the negative sign.

$$
\frac{-(x-5)}{(x-5)^2}=-\frac1{x-5}
$$
