What is the set of zeros of $\dfrac{(x-2)(x+3)}{x-2}$?

- $\{-3\}$
- $\{2\}$
- $\{-3,2\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The numerator vanishes at $2$ and at $-3$. The denominator excludes $2$. It equals $-5$ at $-3$, so that is the only zero.

The reduced form is $x+3$ with $x\ne2$. On the graph, the point where this line crosses the horizontal axis is included. The open circle has vertical coordinate $5$.
