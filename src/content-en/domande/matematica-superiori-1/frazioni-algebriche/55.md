For $R(x)=\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}$, what is $R(-1)+R(2)$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

Both $-1$ and $2$ are in the domain. Simplification gives $R(x)=x+1$ for $x\ne1$. The two inputs give $0$ and $3$.
