When you write $\dfrac{3x+2}{x+1}-\dfrac{x-5}{x+1}$ as a single fraction with denominator $x+1$, what numerator do you get?

- $2x-3$
- $4x-3$
- $4x+7$
- $2x+7$

## Explanation

The denominators already match, so for $x\ne-1$ subtract the numerators. The minus sign before the second fraction acts on the entire bracket $(x-5)$. This gives $3x+2-x+5=2x+7$. Substituting $x=0$ in the original expression indeed gives $2-(-5)=7$.
