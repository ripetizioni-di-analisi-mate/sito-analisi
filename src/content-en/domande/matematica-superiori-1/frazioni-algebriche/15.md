For $x\ne 2$ and $x\ne-1$, simplify $\dfrac{x^2-x-2}{(x-2)^2(x+1)}$.

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x + 1}$

## Explanation

Factor the numerator as $(x-2)(x+1)$. Cancel the only copy of $x+1$ and one of the two copies of $x-2$. The original excluded values still apply.

$$
\frac{(x-2)(x+1)}{(x-2)^2(x+1)}=\frac1{x-2}
$$
