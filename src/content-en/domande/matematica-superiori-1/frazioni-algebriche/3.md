After reducing $\dfrac{(x^2-121)(x+2)}{(x-11)(x+2)}$ to $x+11$, which pair gives the horizontal coordinates missing from the full line?

- $\{-11,11\}$
- $\{2,11\}$
- $\{-2,11\}$
- $\{-11,2\}$

## Explanation

Before cancellation, the denominator excludes $11$ and $-2$. The simplified line would give a value at both inputs, but the original fraction is undefined there.
