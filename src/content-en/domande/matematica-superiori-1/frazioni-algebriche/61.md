At what value of $x$ is the fraction $\dfrac{x-3}{x+1}$ equal to zero?

- $3$
- $-1$
- $-3$
- $0$

## Explanation

A fraction equals zero when its numerator is zero and its denominator is nonzero. The numerator vanishes at the given value. The denominator is $3$ plus one there, which is nonzero.

$$
x-3=0\Rightarrow x=3,\qquad 3+1\ne0
$$

The result is $3$.
