Which factored numerator lets you reduce $\dfrac{2x(x+3)-2x}{2x}$ in one step, for $x\ne0$?

- $2x[(x+3)-2x]$
- $2x[(x+3)-0]$
- $2x[x-3]$
- $2x[(x+3)-1]$

## Explanation

The second term is $-2x\cdot1$. Collecting the common factor leaves $x+3-1=x+2$, and $2x$ cancels on the stated domain.
