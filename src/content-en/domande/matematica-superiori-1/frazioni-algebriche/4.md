Where is the fraction $\dfrac{0}{x^2-9}$ defined?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{-3,3\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-3,3\}$

## Explanation

The zero numerator gives a value of zero at every point where the quotient exists. Factoring $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$ reveals the denominator’s two zeros. At both of them the expression would become $\dfrac{0}{0}$, so they must be excluded even though the expression is zero everywhere else.
