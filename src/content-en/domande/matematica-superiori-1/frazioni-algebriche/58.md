You know that $x>2$. What is the sign of $\dfrac{x-2}{1-x}$?

- Strictly positive.
- Strictly negative.
- Zero.
- Variable even with this condition.

## Explanation

From $x>2$ we get $x-2>0$. Also $x>2$ implies $x>1$, so $1-x<0$. A strictly positive numerator divided by a strictly negative denominator gives a strictly negative result. For instance, at $3$ the fraction is $\dfrac{1}{-2}$, with the predicted sign.
