For $x\ne0$, rewrite $\dfrac{6x^2-4x+2}{2x}$ by dividing each term of the numerator.

- $3 x - 1$
- $3 x - 4 + \dfrac{1}{x}$
- $3 x - 2 + \dfrac{2}{x}$
- $3 x - 2 + \dfrac{1}{x}$

## Explanation

The first two terms are multiples of $x$, so they simplify to $3x$ and $-2$. The last contribution is $\dfrac1x$ because the term $2$ is constant.

$$
\frac{6x^2}{2x}-\frac{4x}{2x}+\frac{2}{2x}=3x-2+\frac1x
$$
