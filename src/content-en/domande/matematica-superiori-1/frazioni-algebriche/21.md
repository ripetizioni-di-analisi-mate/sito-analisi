For $x\ne3$, calculate $\dfrac2{x-3}+\dfrac3{x-3}$.

- $\dfrac{5}{x - 3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2 \left(x - 3\right)}$
- $\dfrac{6}{x - 3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x - 3}$

## Explanation

The fractions already have the same denominator. Add their numerators and retain that denominator. The same domain condition applies to the sum.

$$
\frac2{x-3}+\frac3{x-3}=\frac5{x-3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{5}{x - 3}$.

To check the steps, use $x=2$. The original fractions are $-2$ and $-3$. Their sum, $-5$, agrees with evaluating the final fraction directly.
