In a box, the ratio of red to blue marbles is $2:3$. There are $8$ red marbles, and $4$ more red marbles are added. What is the new ratio of red to blue?

- $2:3$
- $3:2$
- $1:1$
- $3:4$

## Explanation

The two red shares total $8$ marbles, so one share is worth $4$. The three blue shares contain $3\cdot4=12$ marbles. Adding four marbles to the red group gives $12$ red marbles, with $12$ blue marbles still present. The new ratio is $12:12=1:1$ because the two counts agree.
