Two people split $30$ euros in the ratio $2:3$. How many euros does the second person receive?

- $12\,\text{euros}$
- $30\,\text{euros}$
- $18\,\text{euros}$
- $10\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

There are five shares in total, two for the first person and three for the second. Each share is the total divided by five. The second person receives three shares.

$$
\frac{30}{2+3}=6,\qquad3\cdot6=18
$$

The result is $18\,\text{euros}$.
