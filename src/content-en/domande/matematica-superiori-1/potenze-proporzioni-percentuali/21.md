Calculate $-3^2$, where the minus sign is outside the base of the power.

- $-9$
- $9$
- $-6$
- $6$

## Explanation

The power takes precedence over the minus sign in front of the base. First square the positive number. The sign in front gives the opposite of that square.

$$
-3^2=-(3\cdot3)=-9
$$

The result is $-9$.
