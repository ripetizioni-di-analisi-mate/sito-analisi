A tournament has $24$ sign-ups out of $60$ students in group A and $36$ out of $120$ in group B. Which group has more sign-ups, and which has the higher signup percentage?

- More sign-ups in B, higher percentage in A
- More sign-ups in A, higher percentage in B
- More sign-ups and a higher percentage in B
- More sign-ups and a higher percentage in A

## Explanation

B has more sign-ups because $36>24$. Comparing the rates uses the sizes of the two groups. In A, $\dfrac{24}{60}=40\%$, and in B, $\dfrac{36}{120}=30\%$. B has the lower percentage because its group size is twice that of A, while it has only one and a half times as many sign-ups.
