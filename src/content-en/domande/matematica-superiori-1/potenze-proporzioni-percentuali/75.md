A surface covers $12\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ on a plan at scale $1:200$. What is its real area in square metres?

- $24\,\mathrm{m}^2$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}^2$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}^2$
- $4800\,\mathrm{m}^2$

## Explanation

One centimetre on the plan represents $200$ centimetres, or $2$ metres in reality. A square with side length $1$ centimetre therefore represents a square with side length $2$ metres and area $4$ square metres. The twelve square centimetres correspond to $12\cdot4=48$ square metres. The length factor applies in both directions.
