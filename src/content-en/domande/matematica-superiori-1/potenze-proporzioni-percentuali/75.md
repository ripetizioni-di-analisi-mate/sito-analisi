On a map with scale $1:50000$, a distance measures $21$ cm. What distance does this represent in kilometers?

- $1050\,\mathrm{km}$
- $105\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\dfrac{21}{20}\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Each centimeter on the map represents fifty thousand centimeters on the ground. This is five hundred meters, or half a kilometer. Multiply the map distance in centimeters by half a kilometer.

$$
21\cdot50000\,\mathrm{cm}=\frac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{km}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{km}$.
