What is the difference $4.02\cdot10^5-3.98\cdot10^5$ in scientific notation?

- $4\cdot10^4$
- $4\cdot10^5$
- $4\cdot10^2$
- $4\cdot10^3$

## Explanation

Both coefficients count units of $10^5$, so factor out that power and subtract $4.02-3.98=0.04$. Since $0.04=4\cdot10^{-2}$, the result becomes $4\cdot10^3$. A check using ordinary decimal notation is $402000-398000=4000$. In the final form the coefficient $4$ is in the required interval. The result is four thousand. The expression $4\cdot10^4$ would be forty thousand, ten times the actual difference.
