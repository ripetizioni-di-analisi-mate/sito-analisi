Two groups have no members in common. In the first group $8$ out of $20$ people take part in a race, and in the second group $18$ out of $30$ take part. What percentage takes part across both groups together?

- $50\%$
- $58\%$
- $52\%$
- $26\%$

## Explanation

Across both groups, $8+18=26$ people take part out of $20+30=50$. The fraction $\dfrac{26}{50}$ equals $\dfrac{52}{100}$, or $52\%$. Taking the simple average of $40\%$ and $60\%$ would give $50\%$. That would give equal weight to the two percentages, even though the second describes a larger group. The overall figure is closer to $60\%$, since the second group makes up three fifths of the total.
