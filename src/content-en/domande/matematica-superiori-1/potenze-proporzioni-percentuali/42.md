A machine completes a job in $12$ hours, made up of one hour of set-up and $11$ hours of processing. The set-up lasts one hour whatever the number of machines, while the processing can be shared among identical machines. How long does the whole job take with two machines?

- $6\,\mathrm h$
- $6.5\,\mathrm h$
- $5.5\,\mathrm h$
- $7\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

The $11$ hours of processing amount to $11$ machine-hours, since a single machine completes them in $11$ hours. With two identical machines there are still $11$ machine-hours, so the processing lasts $\dfrac{11}{2}=5.5$ hours. The set-up is not shared among the machines, therefore a whole hour is added to the processing. The total duration is then

$$
1\,\mathrm h+5.5\,\mathrm h=6.5\,\mathrm h.
$$

The answer $6\,\mathrm h$ comes from halving the set-up hour as well, while $7\,\mathrm h$ comes from adding one hour to half of $12$ hours.
