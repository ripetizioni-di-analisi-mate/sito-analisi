It takes 3 identical machines $16$ hours to finish a job. How many hours will 6 machines take to finish the same job at the same rate per machine?

- $32\,\mathrm{h}$
- $8\,\mathrm{h}$
- $16\,\mathrm{h}$
- $24\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

For a fixed job, the product of the number of machines and the time stays constant. Doubling the number of machines halves the time, so divide the original duration by two.

$$
3\cdot16=6t,\qquad t=8
$$

The result is $8\,\mathrm{h}$.
