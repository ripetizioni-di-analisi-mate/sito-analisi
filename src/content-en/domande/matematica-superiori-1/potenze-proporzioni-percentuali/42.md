A job takes $12$ hours with one machine. The first hour is spent on setup, which always takes one hour even with several machines. If two identical machines share the remaining work, how long does the whole job take?

- $6$ h
- $6.5$ h
- $5.5$ h
- $7$ h

## Explanation

The machines can share the $11$ hours of production. The total time is $1+\dfrac{11}{2}$ hours.
