A printing plan for $n$ pages charges a fixed fee plus a constant price per page. It costs $11$ euros for $3$ pages and $19$ euros for $7$ pages. Which formula gives the cost $C(n)$ in euros?

- $C(n)=5n+2$
- $C(n)=2n$
- $C(n)=2n+5$
- $C(n)=2n+11$

## Explanation

Subtracting the two bills removes the fixed fee. The four extra pages cost $19-11=8$ euros, so each page costs $8:4=2$ euros. With three pages the printing costs $6$ euros, leaving $11-6=5$ euros as the fixed fee. The formula $2n+5$ also gives $19$ euros for seven pages. The formula distinguishes the cost of starting an order from the cost of adding a page.
