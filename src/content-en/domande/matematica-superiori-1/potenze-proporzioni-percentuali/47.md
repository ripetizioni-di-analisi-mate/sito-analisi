An item costs €$150$ before tax, and VAT at $22\%$ is added to the price. How many euros of VAT are paid?

- €$183$
- €$3.3$
- €$33$
- €$117$

## Explanation

The VAT is $22\%$ of the price before tax, so $150$ is multiplied by $\dfrac{22}{100}$.

$$
150\cdot\frac{22}{100}=\frac{3300}{100}=33
$$

Therefore the VAT is €$33$. The value €$183$ is the price including VAT, that is $150+33$. The value €$117$ is obtained by subtracting the tax from the price, while €$3.3$ comes from dividing by $1000$ instead of $100$.
