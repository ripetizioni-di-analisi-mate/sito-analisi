A table rounds each percentage to the nearest whole number. Three equally frequent categories cover every observation. What total is displayed?

- $100\%$
- $101\%$
- $99\%$
- $90\%$

## Explanation

Each category accounts for one third, or $33.333\ldots\%$. Each entry displays as $33\%$, giving a displayed total of $99\%$.
