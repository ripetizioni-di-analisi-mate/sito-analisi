A service costs a fixed $7$ euros plus $2$ euros per kilometer. What is the average cost per kilometer for a trip of $7$ kilometers?

- $3\,\text{euros per kilometer}$
- $2\,\text{euros per kilometer}$
- $7\,\text{euros per kilometer}$
- $9\,\text{euros per kilometer}$

## Explanation

The total cost is $7+2\cdot7=21$ euros. Divide that total by the distance to get the average cost per kilometer, which is $21:7=3$ euros per kilometer.
