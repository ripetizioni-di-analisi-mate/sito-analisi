A drink contains concentrate and water in the ratio $1:4$. An amount of water equal to the water already present is then added. What is the new ratio of concentrate to water?

- $1:8$
- $1:5$
- $2:8$
- $1:9$

## Explanation

The concentrate is one part and the water is four parts. Add another four parts of water, so the concentrate stays at one part and the water reaches eight parts. The ratio becomes $1:8$.
