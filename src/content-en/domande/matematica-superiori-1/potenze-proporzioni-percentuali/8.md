By which power of $5$ must $\dfrac{5^3}{5^7}$ be multiplied to give a product of $1$?

- $5^{-4}$
- $5^7$
- $5^{10}$
- $5^4$

## Explanation

Cancelling three factors of $5$ leaves four factors in the denominator. The quotient is therefore $\dfrac{1}{5^4}=5^{-4}$. The number that brings it back to $1$ by multiplication is its reciprocal, $5^4$. The sum of the exponents also checks the result because $-4+4=0$. Factoring $5^7=5^3\cdot5^4$ lets us do the calculation without evaluating large powers.
