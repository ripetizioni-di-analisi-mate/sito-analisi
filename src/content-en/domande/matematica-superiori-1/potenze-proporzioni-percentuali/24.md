Which statement correctly compares $(-2)^4$ and $(-4)^2$?

- Both equal $-16$
- The first is $16$ and the second is $-16$
- The first is $-16$ and the second is $16$
- Both equal $16$

## Explanation

Both exponents are even, so both results are positive. The first power groups four factors of $-2$ into two pairs. Each pair gives $4$, so its value is $4^2=16$. The second power is the product $(-4)(-4)$, which gives the same value. The identity $2^4=(2^2)^2=4^2$ confirms the comparison using the absolute values of the two bases.
