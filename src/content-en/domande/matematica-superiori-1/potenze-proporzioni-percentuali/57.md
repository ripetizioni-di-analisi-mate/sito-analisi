A museum sells $100$ tickets at €$8$ each. It raises the price by $25\%$ and sells $85$ tickets. By how many euros does its revenue change?

- It increases by $50$
- It falls by $120$
- It increases by $80$
- It stays the same

## Explanation

The new price is €$10$. The two revenues are €$800$ and €$850$, so the difference is €$50$.
