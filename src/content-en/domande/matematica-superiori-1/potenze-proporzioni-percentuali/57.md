A museum sells $100$ tickets at €$8$ each. After a $25\%$ price increase it sells $85$ tickets. How does the revenue change?

- It increases by €$50$
- It falls by €$120$
- It increases by €$80$
- It stays the same

## Explanation

The price rises by $25\%$, so the new price is $8\cdot\dfrac{125}{100}=10$ euros. The initial revenue is $100\cdot8=800$ euros and the new one is $85\cdot10=850$ euros. Therefore the revenue increases by $850-800=50$ euros.

Adding the two changes, $+25\%$ on the price and $-15\%$ on the tickets, would give an increase of $10\%$, that is of €$80$. The two changes combine instead through the product of the factors, $1.25\cdot0.85=1.0625$, and indeed $800\cdot1.0625=850$.
