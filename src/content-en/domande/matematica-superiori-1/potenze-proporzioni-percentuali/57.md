An initial quantity of $40$ increases by $10\%$. What is the new value?

- $44$
- $4$
- $36$
- $50$

## Explanation

The percentage increase uses the initial value as its base. Ten percent of that value is added to the original total. The new value is therefore one hundred ten percent of the initial value.

$$
40+\frac{10}{100}\cdot40=44
$$

The result is $44$.
