An initial value of $300$ increases by $10\%$ and then decreases by $10\%$ of the new value. What is the final value?

- $300$
- $297$
- $270$
- $330$

## Explanation

The two percentages apply to different base values. The first factor is eleven tenths and the second is nine tenths. Their product is ninety-nine hundredths.

$$
300\cdot\frac{11}{10}\cdot\frac9{10}=297
$$

The result is $297$.
