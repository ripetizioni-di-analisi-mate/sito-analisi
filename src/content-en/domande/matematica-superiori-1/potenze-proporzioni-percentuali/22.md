For which positive integers $n$ does $(-6)^n=-6^n$ hold?

- For all and only the positive even integers
- For all and only the positive odd integers
- For every positive integer
- Only for the integer $n=1$

## Explanation

Both expressions have absolute value $6^n$. The term $-6^n$ is always negative because the sign is outside the power. The term $(-6)^n$ is negative exactly when an odd number of negative factors leaves one minus sign after pairing. The values therefore agree for every odd $n$.
