A recipe for $4$ people requires $6$ hectograms of flour. How many hectograms are needed for $7$ people if the proportions stay the same?

- $\dfrac{24}{7}\,\mathrm{hg}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{hg}$
- $42\,\mathrm{hg}$
- $\dfrac{21}{4}\,\mathrm{hg}$

## Explanation

The amount of flour is directly proportional to the number of people, so the amount for one person is $\dfrac64=\dfrac32$ hectograms. For $7$ people this amount is multiplied by $7$, therefore the recipe needs

$$
7\cdot\frac32=\frac{21}{2}\ \mathrm{hg}.
$$

The result is $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{hg}$. The value $\dfrac{24}{7}\,\mathrm{hg}$ comes from treating the amount and the number of people as inversely proportional, that is from the calculation $\dfrac{6\cdot4}{7}$, while $42\,\mathrm{hg}$ is obtained by multiplying $6$ by $7$ without dividing by $4$.
