A €$42$ prize must be divided in the ratio $2:3$, with both shares in whole euros. What is the least increase that makes this possible?

- €$1$
- €$3$
- €$2$
- €$5$

## Explanation

Since $2$ and $3$ are coprime, integer shares in this ratio have the form $2k$ and $3k$ for integer $k$. The total must be a multiple of $5$. The first such multiple above $42$ is $45$.
