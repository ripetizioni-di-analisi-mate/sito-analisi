On the same route the average speed rises from $60$ to $75\,\mathrm{km/h}$. By what percentage does travel time fall compared with the original time?

- $25\%$
- $15\%$
- $80\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The distance is the same, so the product of average speed and time stays constant. The new time is $\dfrac{60}{75}=\dfrac45$ of the original time. It therefore retains $80\%$ of the original duration, for a reduction of $20\%$. The ratio $\dfrac{75-60}{60}=25\%$ measures the increase in speed.
