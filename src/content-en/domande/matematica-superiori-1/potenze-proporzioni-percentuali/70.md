An initial value of $700$ increases by $10\%$ and then decreases by $10\%$ of the new value. What is the final value?

- $700$
- $693$
- $630$
- $770$

## Explanation

The two percentages apply to different base values. The first factor is eleven tenths and the second is nine tenths. Their product is ninety-nine hundredths.

$$
700\cdot\frac{11}{10}\cdot\frac9{10}=693
$$

The result is $693$.
