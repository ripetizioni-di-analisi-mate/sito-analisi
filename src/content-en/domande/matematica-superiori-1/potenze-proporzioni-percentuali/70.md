An amount of $150$ euros is subject to a $10\%$ increase and a fixed reduction of $10$ euros. What is the result if the increase comes first, and what is it if the reduction comes first?

- $154$ euros and $155$ euros
- $155$ euros and $154$ euros
- $155$ euros and $155$ euros
- $150$ euros and $150$ euros

## Explanation

In the first order, calculate $150\cdot1.10-10=155$. In the second order, calculate $(150-10)\cdot1.10=154$. When the reduction comes first, the amount used to calculate the increase is $10$ euros lower. The increase is therefore smaller by $10\%$ of $10$ euros, or one euro. The fixed reduction is $10$ euros in both cases.
