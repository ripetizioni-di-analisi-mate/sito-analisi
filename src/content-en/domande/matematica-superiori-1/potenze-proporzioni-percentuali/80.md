On a shelf the ratio of novels to essays is $3:7$. These are the only two kinds of books. What fraction of the books are novels?

- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac7{10}$
- $\dfrac7{3}$
- $\dfrac3{10}$

## Explanation

The ratio $3:7$ gives three shares of novels for every seven shares of essays. The total therefore contains $3+7=10$ shares. The required fraction compares the three shares of novels with all ten shares, giving $\dfrac3{10}$. A denominator of $7$ would compare the novels with the essays alone.
