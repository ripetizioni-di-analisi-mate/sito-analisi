A sticker album already contains $70\%$ of its stickers. There are $24$ missing. How many stickers does the completed album contain?

- $56$
- $72$
- $104$
- $80$

## Explanation

The $24$ missing stickers make up the complement of $70\%$, or $30\%$ of the total. One tenth of the album is $24:3=8$ stickers, so the full album holds $10\cdot8=80$. As a check, $70\%$ of $80$ is $56$, and $56$ and $24$ add up to the total. The value $56$ counts only the stickers already collected, a quantity different from the capacity of the album.
