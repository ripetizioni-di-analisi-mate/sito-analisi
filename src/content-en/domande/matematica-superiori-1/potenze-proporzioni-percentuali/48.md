Calculate $25\%$ of $100$.

- $125$
- $75$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $25$

## Explanation

Twenty-five percent is equivalent to one quarter. The required quantity is therefore one quarter of the total. Dividing the total by four gives the result.

$$
\frac{25}{100}\cdot100=25
$$

The result is $25$.
