A culture doubles every hour. By which factor does its size multiply over each two-hour interval?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

Two hours contain two consecutive doublings. After the first hour the amount is $2Q$, where $Q$ is the starting amount for that interval. The next hour gives $2(2Q)=4Q$. The same factor applies over any two-hour interval. For example, three intervals give $4^3=2^6$, the same as six hourly doublings.
