On a map with scale $1:50000$, a distance measures $18$ cm. What distance does this represent in kilometers?

- $900\,\mathrm{km}$
- $9\,\mathrm{km}$
- $90\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Each centimeter on the map represents fifty thousand centimeters on the ground. This is five hundred meters, or half a kilometer. Multiply the map distance in centimeters by half a kilometer.

$$
18\cdot50000\,\mathrm{cm}=9\,\mathrm{km}
$$

The result is $9\,\mathrm{km}$.
