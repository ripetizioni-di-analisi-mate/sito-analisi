Equal masses of two solutions containing $12\%$ and $28\%$ salt by mass are mixed without loss. What is the resulting salt percentage?

- $40\%$
- $16\%$
- $20\%$
- $8\%$

## Explanation

With $100$ g of each solution there are $12+28=40$ g of salt in $200$ g of mixture. The mass fraction is therefore $\dfrac{40}{200}=20\%$.
