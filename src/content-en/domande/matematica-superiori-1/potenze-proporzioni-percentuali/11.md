Calculate $(2^2)^2$.

- $8$
- $32$
- $16$
- $2$

## Explanation

The outer power indicates that the inner factor is multiplied by itself. To raise a power to a power, multiply the exponents. The overall exponent is therefore twice the inner exponent.

$$
(2^2)^2=2^{2\cdot2}=2^4=16
$$

The result is $16$.
