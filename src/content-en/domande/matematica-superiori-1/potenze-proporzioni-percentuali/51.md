An item costs $60$ euros and is discounted by $20\%$. What is the final price?

- $12\,\text{euros}$
- $72\,\text{euros}$
- $48\,\text{euros}$
- $40\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The discount is twenty percent of the original price. The final price is eighty percent of the original price. Therefore multiply the price by eighty hundredths.

$$
60\left(1-\frac{20}{100}\right)=48
$$

The result is $48\,\text{euros}$.
