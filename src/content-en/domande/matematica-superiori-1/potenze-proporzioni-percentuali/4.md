Which power do you get by rearranging all the factors in $3^2\cdot2^2$ into two identical pairs?

- $5^2$
- $6^4$
- $12^2$
- $6^2$

## Explanation

There are two factors of $3$ and two factors of $2$. Commutativity allows us to pair one $3$ with one $2$ in each of two groups. Each pair has product $6$, leaving $6^2$. Evaluating $3^2=9$ and $2^2=4$ separately confirms that the product is $36$. The value $6^4$ would require four pairs, or twice as many factors as we have.
