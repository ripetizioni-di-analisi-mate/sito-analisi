A price is discounted by $20\%$ and then by $25\%$ of the reduced price. What single discount is equivalent to the two?

- $45\%$
- $60\%$
- $5\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

The first discount leaves $80\%$ of the original price. The second leaves $75\%$ of the remaining amount, so the overall factor is $0.80\cdot0.75=0.60$. The customer pays $60\%$ of the initial price, for a discount of $40\%$. Starting from $100$ euros, the reductions are both $20$ euros because the second uses $80$ as its base.
