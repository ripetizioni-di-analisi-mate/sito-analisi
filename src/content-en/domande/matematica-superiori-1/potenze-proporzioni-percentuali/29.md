What is the ratio of $6\cdot10^5$ to $6$?

- $100000$
- $5$
- $40$
- $10000$

## Explanation

The comparison factor is the ratio of the two quantities. The common numerical factor cancels because it is nonzero. The indicated power of ten remains.

$$
\frac{6\cdot10^5}{6}=10^5=100000
$$

The result is $100000$.
