Between which consecutive powers of ten does $Q=\dfrac{7\cdot10^4}{2\cdot10^7}$ lie?

- $10^{-3}<Q<10^{-2}$
- $10^{-4}<Q<10^{-3}$
- $10^{-2}<Q<10^{-1}$
- $10^3<Q<10^4$

## Explanation

Dividing the coefficients gives $7:2=3.5$, and dividing the powers gives $10^{4-7}=10^{-3}$. The number is therefore $3.5\cdot10^{-3}$. Since $1<3.5<10$, it is greater than $1\cdot10^{-3}$ and less than $10\cdot10^{-3}=10^{-2}$. Multiplication preserves the inequalities because the common power of ten is positive. The lower boundary is one thousandth and the upper boundary is one hundredth.
