After a discount of $20\%$, the price is $48$ euros. What was the original price?

- $\dfrac{192}{5}\,\text{euros}$
- $\dfrac{288}{5}\,\text{euros}$
- $68\,\text{euros}$
- $60\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The amount paid is eighty percent of the original price. To recover the original price, divide by eighty hundredths. Adding twenty percent to the reduced price would use a different base.

$$
P\cdot\frac45=48,\qquad P=48\cdot\frac54=60
$$

The result is $60\,\text{euros}$.
