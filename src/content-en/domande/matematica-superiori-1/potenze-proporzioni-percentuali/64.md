After a $100\%$ discount the price is zero. Which positive starting prices are consistent with that information?

- No positive prices
- Only $100$ euros
- Only $1$ euro
- All positive prices

## Explanation

The factor from original price to final price is $1-\dfrac{100}{100}=0$. Multiplying any positive price by this factor gives zero. For example, both $1$ euro and $100$ euros satisfy the condition. Dividing the final price by the factor would require division by zero, so the original amount cannot be determined.
