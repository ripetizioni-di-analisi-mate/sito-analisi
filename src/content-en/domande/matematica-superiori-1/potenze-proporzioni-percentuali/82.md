In a class of $20$ students, $4$ take part in a workshop. What percentage of the class takes part?

- $5\%$
- $20\%$
- $4\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

The relative frequency is the number of participants divided by the number of students in the class. The ratio simplifies to one fifth. Multiplying by one hundred gives the percentage.

$$
\frac{4}{20}\cdot100=20
$$

The result is $20\%$.
