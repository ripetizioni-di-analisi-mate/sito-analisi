In a $40$-question test a student gets $6$ answers wrong and leaves $4$ questions unanswered. What percentage of the questions received a correct answer?

- $85\%$
- $75\%$
- $25\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

The correct answers are the questions left after removing the wrong ones and the unanswered ones, so there are $40-6-4=30$. The percentage is the ratio of the part to the whole multiplied by $100$, therefore

$$
100\cdot\frac{30}{40}=100\cdot\frac34=75.
$$

The correct answers are therefore $75\%$. The value $85\%$ is obtained by removing only the $6$ wrong answers, while $25\%$ is the complementary percentage, that is the share of questions answered wrongly or left unanswered.
