A price is rounded to the nearest cent. When finding $12\%$ of €$19.99$, how large is the error from replacing $19.99$ by $20$ before rounding the result?

- €$0.012$
- €$0.0012$
- €$0.12$
- €$0.01$

## Explanation

The original difference is one cent. The error in the result is $12\%$ of that difference, or $0.12\cdot0.01=0.0012$ euros.
