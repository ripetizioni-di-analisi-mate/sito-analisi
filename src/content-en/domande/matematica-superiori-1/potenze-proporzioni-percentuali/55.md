A drawing measuring $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ represents an object measuring $6\,\mathrm m$. What is the scale?

- $1:15$
- $1:240$
- $1:150$
- $1:24$

## Explanation

Express both measurements in centimetres. The actual object measures $600\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the ratio is $4:600$. Dividing both terms by $4$ gives $1:150$.
