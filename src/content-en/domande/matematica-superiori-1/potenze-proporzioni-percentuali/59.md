A participation rate rises from $24\%$ to $30\%$. How many percentage points does it rise, and what is the relative increase?

- $6$ percentage points and $20\%$
- $25$ percentage points and $6\%$
- $6$ percentage points and $25\%$
- $30$ percentage points and $25\%$

## Explanation

To find the difference in percentage points, subtract the two rates, so $30-24=6$. For the relative increase, compare those six points with the original $24$. This gives $\dfrac6{24}=\dfrac14=25\%$. Using $30$ as the denominator would answer the reverse question of what decrease takes the rate from $30\%$ back to $24\%$.
