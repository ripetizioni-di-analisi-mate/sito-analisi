A rectangular plaza measures $40\,\mathrm m$ by $20\,\mathrm m$. What area does it take up on a map with scale $1:1000$?

- $80\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $800\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Each centimeter on the map represents $1000\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the ground, or $10\,\mathrm m$. The sides on the map therefore measure $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Their product is $8\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
