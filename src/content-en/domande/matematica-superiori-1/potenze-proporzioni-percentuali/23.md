Without expanding the powers, determine the sign of $\dfrac{(-3)^7(-2)^4}{(-5)^3}$.

- Negative
- Zero
- Positive
- Undefined

## Explanation

The numerator’s first factor is negative because the exponent is odd. The second is positive because the exponent is even. Their product is therefore negative. The denominator is nonzero and negative because $3$ is odd. The quotient of the two negative quantities is positive.
