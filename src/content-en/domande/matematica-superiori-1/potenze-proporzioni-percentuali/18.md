For $a,b\ne0$, which expression always gives the value of $\left(\dfrac ab\right)^{-2}$?

- $-\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}$
- $\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}$
- $\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}$
- $\dfrac b{a^2}$

## Explanation

The fraction is nonzero, so its reciprocal $\dfrac ba$ exists. The exponent $-2$ asks for that reciprocal to be squared. Multiplying the fraction $\dfrac ba$ by itself gives two factors of $b$ in the numerator and two factors of $a$ in the denominator. For example, if the starting ratio is $\dfrac21$, the result is $\dfrac14$.
