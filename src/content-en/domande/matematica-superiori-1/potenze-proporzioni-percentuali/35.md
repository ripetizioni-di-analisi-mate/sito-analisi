Participation rises from $20\%$ to $25\%$ of the group. What is the relative increase in the participation rate?

- $5\%$
- $20\%$
- $25\%$
- $125\%$

## Explanation

The share rises by five percentage points. To find the relative increase, divide that difference by the initial share. This gives $\dfrac{25-20}{20}=\dfrac14$, which equals $25\%$.
