Which expression is the normalised scientific notation for $38\cdot10^{-7}$?

- $3.8\cdot10^{-8}$
- $3.8\cdot10^{-7}$
- $3.8\cdot10^{-6}$
- $3.8\cdot10^{-5}$

## Explanation

To get a coefficient from $1$ inclusive to $10$ exclusive, write $38$ as $3.8\cdot10$. That factor of $10$ increases the exponent $-7$ by one, giving $3.8\cdot10^{-6}$. Keeping the exponent unchanged after dividing the coefficient by ten would give a number one tenth as large. As a check on the location of the decimal point, the number is $0.0000038$ in ordinary decimal notation.
