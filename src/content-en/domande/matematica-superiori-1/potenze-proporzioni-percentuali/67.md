A group of $30$ needs at least $75\%$ attendance. What is the greatest permitted number of absences?

- $8$
- $22$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

The threshold is $22.5$ people present. The count must be a whole number, so at least $23$ must attend and at most $7$ may be absent.
