An initial value of $400$ increases by $10\%$ and then decreases by $10\%$ of the new value. What is the final value?

- $400$
- $360$
- $396$
- $440$

## Explanation

The two percentages apply to different base values. The first factor is eleven tenths and the second is nine tenths. Their product is ninety-nine hundredths.

$$
400\cdot\frac{11}{10}\cdot\frac9{10}=396
$$

The result is $396$.
