A course consists of $30$ lessons, and admission to the exam requires attendance at no less than $75\%$ of the lessons. What is the maximum number of absences allowed?

- $8$
- $23$
- $7$
- $22$

## Explanation

$75\%$ of $30$ is $30\cdot\dfrac{75}{100}=22.5$, so the lessons attended must be at least $22.5$. The number of lessons attended is a whole number, therefore at least $23$ are needed. The absences are then at most $30-23=7$.

The answer $8$ comes from computing $25\%$ of $30$, that is $7.5$, and rounding it up. With $8$ absences only $22$ lessons would be attended, that is fewer than $22.5$.
