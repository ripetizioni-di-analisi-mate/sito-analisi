Two prizes are in the ratio $4:7$, and the second exceeds the first by $18$ euros. How much are the two prizes worth in total?

- $42$ euros
- $66$ euros
- $54$ euros
- $198$ euros

## Explanation

The first prize is worth four shares and the second seven shares of the same value. The difference is three shares, so each share is worth $18:3=6$ euros. Together the prizes contain eleven shares and total $11\cdot6=66$ euros. The individual values are $24$ and $42$ euros, and their difference confirms the given information.
