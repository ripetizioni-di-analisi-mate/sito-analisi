If $A:B=2:3$ and $B:C=3:7$, what is $A:C$?

- $2:3$
- $3:7$
- $5:10$
- $2:7$

## Explanation

In both ratios, $B$ corresponds to the same number of parts, namely three. If $u$ is the value of one part, then $A=2u$, $B=3u$, and $C=7u$. The ratio of $A$ to $C$ is therefore $2:7$.
