How many times as large is $2^{3^2}$ as $(2^3)^2$?

- $1$
- $4$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

In the numerator, $3^2$ is the exponent of $2$, giving $2^9$. The denominator squares a power, so $(2^3)^2=2^{3\cdot2}=2^6$. The comparison the question asks for is the quotient of the two amounts. Three factors of $2$ remain, so the quotient is $2^{9-6}=8$. The explicit values also confirm this, since the quotient of $512$ and $64$ is exactly $8$.
