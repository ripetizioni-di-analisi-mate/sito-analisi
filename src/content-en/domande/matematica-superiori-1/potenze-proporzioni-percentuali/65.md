A $30\%$ discount on a bag saves $18$ euros. What was the price before applying the discount?

- $60$ euros
- $42$ euros
- $54$ euros
- $78$ euros

## Explanation

The $18$ euros is the reduction, or $30\%$ of the original price. If three tenths are worth $18$ euros, one tenth is worth $18:3=6$ euros and ten tenths are worth $60$ euros. The calculation $60-18=42$ gives the amount paid, corresponding to the $70\%$ that remains after the discount. Using $70\%$ to work backwards would treat the $18$ euros as the sale price.
