After a discount of $20\%$, the price is $56$ euros. What was the original price?

- $70\,\text{euros}$
- $\dfrac{224}{5}\,\text{euros}$
- $\dfrac{336}{5}\,\text{euros}$
- $76\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The amount paid is eighty percent of the original price. To recover the original price, divide by eighty hundredths. Adding twenty percent to the reduced price would use a different base.

$$
P\cdot\frac45=56,\qquad P=56\cdot\frac54=70
$$

The result is $70\,\text{euros}$.
