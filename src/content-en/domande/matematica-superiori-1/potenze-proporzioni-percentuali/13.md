Which expression equals $(5^n)^2$ for every positive integer $n$?

- $5^{2n}$
- $5^{n+2}$
- $5^{n^2}$
- $2\cdot5^n$

## Explanation

The square of $5^n$ multiplies two groups that each contain $n$ factors of $5$. There are $2n$ factors in all, giving $5^{2n}$. The equality must hold for every $n$. At $n=1$ the other expressions give $125$, $5$, and $10$, whereas $(5^1)^2=25$. Two of the alternatives agree with the desired value at $n=2$. The identity must hold over the whole set of allowed exponents.
