Calculate $4^{-2}$.

- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $-16$
- $- \dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

A negative exponent gives the reciprocal of the power with the positive exponent. The base is nonzero. Square the base and then take the reciprocal.

$$
4^{-2}=\frac1{4^2}=\frac{1}{16}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{1}{16}$.
