What are the values, in the given order, of $(-4)^{-2}$ and $-(4^{-2})$?

- $\dfrac1{16},\ -\dfrac1{16}$
- $-\dfrac1{16},\ -\dfrac1{16}$
- $-\dfrac1{16},\ \dfrac1{16}$
- $16,\ -16$

## Explanation

The first power has base $-4$. Squaring that base gives $16$ and the exponent $-2$ calls for taking the reciprocal, which is $\dfrac1{16}$. In the second expression, evaluate $4^{-2}=\dfrac1{16}$ first, then apply the minus sign outside the power to get its negative. Both calculations have the same absolute value because both use the reciprocal of $4^2$.
