An item costs $90$ euros. A voucher reduces the item price by $15\%$, and delivery costs a separate $6$ euros. What is the total charge?

- $81.60$ euros
- $82.50$ euros
- $76.50$ euros
- $90$ euros

## Explanation

The voucher applies to the $90$-euro item, so the discount is $90\cdot0.15=13.50$ euros. The reduced price is $90-13.50=76.50$ euros. Then add the $6$ euros for delivery, giving $82.50$ euros. Applying the voucher to $96$ euros would also discount delivery and lower the total to $81.60$ euros.
