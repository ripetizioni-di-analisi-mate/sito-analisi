An item costs $120$ euros and is discounted by $20\%$. What is the final price?

- $24\,\text{euros}$
- $96\,\text{euros}$
- $144\,\text{euros}$
- $100\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The discount is twenty percent of the original price. The final price is eighty percent of the original price. Therefore multiply the price by eighty hundredths.

$$
120\left(1-\frac{20}{100}\right)=96
$$

The result is $96\,\text{euros}$.
