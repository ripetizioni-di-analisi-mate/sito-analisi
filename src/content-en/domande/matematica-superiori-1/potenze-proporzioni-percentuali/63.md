A positive quantity falls by $25\%$. By what fraction of the reduced value must it then rise to return to its starting value?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac43$

## Explanation

After the decrease, $\dfrac34$ of the original value remains. The missing quarter must be recovered, measured against the reduced value. The ratio of the missing quarter to the three remaining quarters is $\dfrac{1}{4}:\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac13$. The factor that restores the starting value is $1+\dfrac13=\dfrac43$, and the question asks for the increase alone.
