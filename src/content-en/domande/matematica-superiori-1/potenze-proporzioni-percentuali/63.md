After a discount of $20\%$, the price is $40$ euros. What was the original price?

- $32\,\text{euros}$
- $48\,\text{euros}$
- $50\,\text{euros}$
- $60\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The amount paid is eighty percent of the original price. To recover the original price, divide by eighty hundredths. Adding twenty percent to the reduced price would use a different base.

$$
P\cdot\frac45=40,\qquad P=40\cdot\frac54=50
$$

The result is $50\,\text{euros}$.
