Let $A=-2^2$, $B=(-2)^0$, and $C=-(2^{-2})$. Which ascending order is correct?

- $C<A<B$
- $A<B<C$
- $A<C<B$
- $B<C<A$

## Explanation

The negative of the square of $2$ gives $A=-4$. The base of $B$ is nonzero, so the zero power gives $1$. Finally, the minus sign in front of the reciprocal of the square of $2$ gives $C=-\dfrac14$. On the number line, $-4<-\dfrac14<1$. The two negative numbers can be compared by their distances from zero. Moving left along the line decreases the value.
