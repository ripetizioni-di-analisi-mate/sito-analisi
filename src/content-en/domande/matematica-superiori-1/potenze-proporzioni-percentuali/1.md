Calculate $2^2\cdot2^2$.

- $16$
- $8$
- $4$
- $32$

## Explanation

Both factors have the same base. For a product of powers with the same base, add the exponents. Then evaluate the resulting power.

$$
2^2\cdot2^2=2^{2+2}=2^4=16
$$

The result is $16$.
