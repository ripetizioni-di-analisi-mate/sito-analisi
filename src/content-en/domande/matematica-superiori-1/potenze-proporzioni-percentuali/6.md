Calculate $3^3:3^2$.

- $243$
- $3$
- $729$
- $1$

## Explanation

The base is nonzero, so the quotient is defined. For a quotient of powers with the same base, subtract the exponent in the denominator. Then evaluate the power with the remaining exponent.

$$
3^3:3^2=3^{3-2}=3^1=3
$$

The result is $3$.
