On a map, $4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ corresponds to a real distance of $1.8\,\mathrm{km}$. What is the scale of the map?

- $1:40000$
- $1:4000$
- $1:400000$
- $1:25000$

## Explanation

Convert the real distance into centimetres, giving $1.8\,\mathrm{km}=180000\,\mathrm{cm}$. Scale is the ratio of the map distance to the real distance. Dividing both terms of $4.5:180000$ by $4.5$ gives $1:40000$. At this scale one centimetre corresponds to $400$ metres, and $4.5$ centimetres represents $1800$ metres. Using kilometres without converting first would compare numerical readings in different units.
