Compare $A$, which is $15\%$ of $80$, with $B$, which is $80\%$ of $15$. Which statement is true?

- $A=B=12$
- $A>B$
- $A<B$
- $A=B=0.12$

## Explanation

For $A$ calculate $\dfrac{15\cdot80}{100}$, and for $B$ calculate $\dfrac{80\cdot15}{100}$. Multiplication is commutative, so the two numerators agree. Both equal $1200$, which gives a quotient of $12$. In this type of product, swapping the numerical percentage and the amount always gives the same numerical result. The symmetry comes from the factor $\dfrac1{100}$, which is common to both calculations.
