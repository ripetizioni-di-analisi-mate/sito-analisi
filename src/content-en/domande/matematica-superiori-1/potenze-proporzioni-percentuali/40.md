A price rises by $25\%$. By what percentage must the new price fall to return to the original price?

- $25\%$
- $15\%$
- $75\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The increase multiplies the price by $\dfrac54$. To return to the original price, multiply by the reciprocal $\dfrac45$, which is $80\%$ of the new price. The required decrease is therefore $20\%$.
