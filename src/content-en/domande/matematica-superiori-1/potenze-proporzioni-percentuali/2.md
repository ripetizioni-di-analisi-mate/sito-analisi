Which positive integer $n$ makes the equation $3^4\cdot3^n=3^{10}$ true?

- $14$
- $6$
- $10$
- $40$

## Explanation

The product on the left contains four factors of $3$ from the first term and another $n$ from the second. The right has ten factors. Since integer powers of $3$ increase with the exponent, we need $4+n=10$, giving $n=6$. Substituting gives $3^4\cdot3^6=3^{4+6}$. The comparison is therefore between exponents once their common base has been fixed.
