A tap has dispensed $6$ litres after $2$ minutes and $12$ litres after $4$ minutes. Assuming the flow is steady, which time and volume pair fits the data?

- $8\,\mathrm{min},\ 22\,\mathrm L$
- $8\,\mathrm{min},\ 24\,\mathrm L$
- $8\,\mathrm{min},\ 20\,\mathrm L$
- $9\,\mathrm{min},\ 18\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Volume divided by time gives the flow rate. The two starting measurements give $6:2=12:4=3$ litres per minute. At a steady flow rate, multiply time by $3$ to get the volume, so $8$ minutes gives $24$ litres. At $9$ minutes the model predicts $27$ litres, since the ratio stays the same.
