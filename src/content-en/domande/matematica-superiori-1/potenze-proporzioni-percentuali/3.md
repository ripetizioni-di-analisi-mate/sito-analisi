Which power of $2$ represents the sum $2^4+2^4$?

- $2^8$
- $2^4$
- $2^5$
- $2^{16}$

## Explanation

The two terms are equal, so the sum is twice $2^4$. This gives $2\cdot2^4=2^{1+4}=2^5$. The exponent $8$ would come from multiplying two powers with exponent $4$. A numerical check distinguishes the two operations, since in this case the calculation is $16+16=32$. The same reasoning applies to the sum of any two identical powers of $2$.
