On a map with scale $1:50000$, a distance measures $9$ cm. What distance does this represent in kilometres?

- $450\,\mathrm{km}$
- $45\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Each centimetre on the map represents fifty thousand centimetres on the ground. This is five hundred metres, or half a kilometre. Multiply the map distance in centimetres by half a kilometre.

$$
9\cdot50000\,\mathrm{cm}=\frac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{km}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{km}$.
