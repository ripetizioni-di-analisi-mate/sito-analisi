Three workers take $10$ hours to do a job. After $4$ hours, three more workers join them at the same rate per person. How many hours does the job take in total?

- $5$
- $7$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

The job takes $3\cdot10=30$ hours for one worker. After four hours the group has done $3\cdot4=12$ hours of that work, so $18$ hours for a single worker remain. Six workers finish it in $18:6=3$ hours, giving a total of $4+3=7$ hours.
