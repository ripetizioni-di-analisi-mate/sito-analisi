Starting from $4^5$, the current value is divided by $4$ at every step. What is the result after six divisions?

- $\dfrac14$
- $4$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Each division by $4$ decreases the exponent by one. After five steps the value is $4^{5-5}=4^0=1$. The sixth step divides by $4$ again, giving $\dfrac14$. The full sequence can also be written as $\dfrac{4^5}{4^6}=4^{-1}$ because the six divisors multiply to $4^6$. The result after the sixth step is positive and below one, as expected when dividing $1$ by $4$.
