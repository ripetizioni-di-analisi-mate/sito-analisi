A calculation produces $7^{30}+7^{30}+7^{30}$. Which of the following divides the result?

- $14$
- $21$
- $49\cdot5$
- $9$

## Explanation

The sum is $3\cdot7^{30}$. It has one factor of $3$ and thirty factors of $7$, so it is divisible by $3\cdot7$.
