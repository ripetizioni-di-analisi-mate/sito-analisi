Which of the following numbers divides $7^{30}+7^{30}+7^{30}$?

- $14$
- $21$
- $245$
- $9$

## Explanation

The three terms are equal, so the sum is $3\cdot7^{30}$. The product $3\cdot7^{30}$ contains the factor $3$ and thirty factors $7$, therefore it is divisible by $3\cdot7=21$.

The number $14=2\cdot7$ does not divide the sum, because $2$ does not appear among the factors of $3\cdot7^{30}$. For the same reason it is not divisible by $245=5\cdot7^2$, which contains the factor $5$, nor by $9=3^2$, which requires two factors $3$. The required divisor is therefore $21$.
