A warehouse inventory rises from $120$ to $150$ boxes. What is the percentage increase relative to the original inventory?

- $20\%$
- $25\%$
- $30\%$
- $125\%$

## Explanation

The increase is $150-120=30$ boxes. The baseline is the original inventory, so divide $30$ by $120$ to get $\dfrac14=25\%$. The quotient $\dfrac{30}{150}=20\%$ would measure the proportion of added boxes in the final total. The new inventory is $125\%$ of its original size, and the increase accounts for the $25$ points above $100\%$.
