A poster states that enrolment has grown by $250\%$ from last year’s $40$ students. How many students are enrolled now?

- $100$
- $140$
- $90$
- $250$

## Explanation

The increase is $2.5\cdot40=100$ students. Adding this to the original $40$ gives $140$.
