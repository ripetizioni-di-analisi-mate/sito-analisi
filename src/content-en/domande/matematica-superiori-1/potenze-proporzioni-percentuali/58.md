Membership of a sports club has increased by $250\%$ compared with the $40$ members of the previous year. How many members are there now?

- $100$
- $140$
- $290$
- $50$

## Explanation

The increase is $250\%$ of $40$, that is $40\cdot\dfrac{250}{100}=100$ members. The increase is added to the starting value, so there are $40+100=140$ members. The same result is reached with the multiplier $1+\dfrac{250}{100}=3.5$, because $40\cdot3.5=140$.

The value $100$ is only the increase, $290$ is obtained by adding $250$ to $40$ as if it were a number of members, and $50$ comes from confusing $250\%$ with $25\%$.
