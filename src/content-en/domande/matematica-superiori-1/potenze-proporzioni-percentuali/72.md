A plan is drawn to a scale of $1:300$. A photocopier enlarges it to $150\%$, that is every length on the sheet becomes $1.5$ times the original one. What is the scale of the copy?

- $1:450$
- $1:150$
- $1:300$
- $1:200$

## Explanation

On the original plan $1$ unit on the sheet corresponds to $300$ real units. On the copy the same real stretch of $300$ units measures $1.5$ units, so the scale of the copy is $1.5:300$. Dividing both terms of the ratio by $1.5$ gives

$$
\frac{1.5}{300}=\frac{1}{200},
$$

therefore the scale of the copy is $1:200$. The scale $1:450$ comes from multiplying $300$ by $1.5$, which corresponds to a reduction of the drawing, while $1:300$ is the scale of the original.
