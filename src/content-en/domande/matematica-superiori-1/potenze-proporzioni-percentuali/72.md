On a map with scale $1:50000$, a distance measures $12$ cm. What distance does this represent in kilometers?

- $600\,\mathrm{km}$
- $60\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $6\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Each centimeter on the map represents fifty thousand centimeters on the ground. This is five hundred meters, or half a kilometer. Multiply the map distance in centimeters by half a kilometer.

$$
12\cdot50000\,\mathrm{cm}=6\,\mathrm{km}
$$

The result is $6\,\mathrm{km}$.
