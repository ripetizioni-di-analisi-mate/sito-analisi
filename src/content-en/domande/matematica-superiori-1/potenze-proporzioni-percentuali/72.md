A plan has scale $1:300$. A photocopier enlarges all lengths on the page to $150\%$ of their originals. What is the scale of the copy?

- $1:450$
- $1:150$
- $1:500$
- $1:200$

## Explanation

The same real length of $300$ units now takes up $1.5$ units on the copy. Dividing both quantities by $1.5$ gives $1:200$.
