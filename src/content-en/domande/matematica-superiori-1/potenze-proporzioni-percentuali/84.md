Fiction makes up $60\%$ of the books in a library. Of the fiction books, $25\%$ are on loan. What percentage of all the books are fiction books on loan?

- $25\%$
- $35\%$
- $45\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

Out of a hundred books, sixty are fiction. Only those sixty books are the baseline for the second calculation. Taking $25\%$ of $60$ gives $\dfrac{60}{4}=15$, so fiction books on loan make up $15\%$ of the total. The product $0.60\cdot0.25=0.15$ expresses the same reasoning for any total. The first factor selects a subgroup, and the second takes a portion of that subgroup.
