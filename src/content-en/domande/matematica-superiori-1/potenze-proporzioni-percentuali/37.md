For a bicycle rental, plan A costs $4$ euros per hour and plan B costs a fixed $12$ euros plus $2$ euros per hour. At what duration do the plans cost the same?

- $6\,\mathrm h$
- $2\,\mathrm h$
- $3\,\mathrm h$
- $12\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

For $t$ hours the prices are $4t$ and $12+2t$. To find when they agree, set $4t=12+2t$, giving $2t=12$ and $t=6$. The fixed charge of $12$ euros is offset once six hours of savings at $2$ euros per hour add up to $12$ euros. Both prices are then $24$ euros. At that duration the average cost for plan B is also four euros an hour.
