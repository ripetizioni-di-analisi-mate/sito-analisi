What is the ratio of a length of $1.2\,\mathrm m$ to a length of $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, taken in that order?

- $4$
- $0.04$
- $\dfrac14$
- $40$

## Explanation

The two lengths must be expressed in the same unit. Converting the first to centimetres gives $1.2\,\mathrm m=120\,\mathrm{cm}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{120}{30}=4$ because the first length is four times the second. Dividing $1.2$ by $30$ directly would compare numbers in different units and give $0.04$. The result has no unit because the centimetres cancel.
