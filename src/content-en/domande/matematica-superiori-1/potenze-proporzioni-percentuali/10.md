What is the value of $(-2)^3+(-2)^4$?

- $-24$
- $8$
- $24$
- $-128$

## Explanation

With a negative base, an odd exponent gives a negative result and an even exponent gives a positive result. We obtain $(-2)^3=-8$ and $(-2)^4=16$. Their sum is $-8+16=8$.
