Which power equals $\dfrac{3^2}{3^5}$?

- $3^{-3}$
- $3^3$
- $3^{-7}$
- $3^{7}$

## Explanation

The numerator contains two factors of $3$ and the denominator contains five. Cancelling leaves $\dfrac{1}{3^3}$, which equals $3^{-3}$.
