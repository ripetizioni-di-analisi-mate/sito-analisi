A shop offers two alternative discounts, $18$ euros or $15\%$ of the price. For which initial prices $P\ge18$ is the first discount strictly better?

- $18\le P<120$
- $P>120$
- $P=120$
- $18\le P\le120$

## Explanation

The percentage discount is $0.15P$ euros. The discounts agree when $0.15P=18$, which gives $P=120$. The value of $0.15P$ increases with $P$. Below $120$ it is under $18$ euros, and above $120$ it is over $18$. The lower bound $18$ is included. Equality at $120$ is excluded by the requirement for the first discount to be strictly better.
