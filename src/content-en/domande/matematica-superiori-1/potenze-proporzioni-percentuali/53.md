An item costs $100$ euros and is discounted by $20\%$. What is the final price?

- $80\,\text{euros}$
- $20\,\text{euros}$
- $120\,\text{euros}$
- $90\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The discount is twenty percent of the original price. The final price is eighty percent of the original price. Therefore multiply the price by eighty hundredths.

$$
100\left(1-\frac{20}{100}\right)=80
$$

The result is $80\,\text{euros}$.
