Four servings require $240\,\mathrm g$ of flour and $160\,\mathrm g$ of sugar. With $900\,\mathrm g$ of flour and $450\,\mathrm g$ of sugar, what is the maximum number of whole servings at the specified amounts per serving?

- $12$
- $15$
- $10$
- $11$

## Explanation

One serving needs $60$ grams of flour and $40$ grams of sugar. The flour would be enough for $900:60=15$ servings. Eleven servings require $11\cdot40=440$ grams of sugar and $660$ grams of flour, so they are possible. Twelve servings would require $480$ grams of sugar, above the available $450$. Sugar therefore limits the number of servings.
