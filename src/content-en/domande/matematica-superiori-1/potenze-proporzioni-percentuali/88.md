A recipe for 4 people requires $10$ hectograms of flour. How many hectograms are needed for 7 people if the proportions stay the same?

- $\dfrac{40}{7}\,\mathrm{hg}$
- $70\,\mathrm{hg}$
- $\dfrac{35}{4}\,\mathrm{hg}$
- $\dfrac{35}{2}\,\mathrm{hg}$

## Explanation

The amount per person is one quarter of the given amount. Multiplying that by seven gives the required amount. The ratio of the new amount to the original amount is seven fourths.

$$
10\cdot\frac74=\frac{35}{2}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{35}{2}\,\mathrm{hg}$.
