A $120\,\mathrm{km}$ journey is split into two legs of equal distance. The average speed is $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$ on the first leg and $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$ on the second. What is the average speed over the whole journey?

- $48\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $50\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

The first leg takes $60:40=1.5$ hours, and the second takes $60:60=1$ hour. Overall average speed is total distance divided by total time, so $120:2.5=48$ kilometres per hour. The simple average of the speeds gives $50$, but the legs have different durations and the slower leg takes more time.
