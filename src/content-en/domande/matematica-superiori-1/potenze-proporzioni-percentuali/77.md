On a tray the ratio of chocolate biscuits to vanilla biscuits is $4:7$. Which of the following numbers can be the total number of biscuits?

- $77$
- $84$
- $70$
- $80$

## Explanation

The ratio $4:7$ means that the biscuits split into equal shares, $4$ chocolate and $7$ vanilla, so $4+7=11$ shares in all. Each share contains the same whole number of biscuits, therefore the total is a multiple of $11$. Among the numbers given the only multiple of $11$ is $77=11\cdot7$, and in that case there are $4\cdot7=28$ chocolate biscuits and $7\cdot7=49$ vanilla biscuits.

The number $84$ is a multiple of both $4$ and $7$, but $84$ divided by $11$ is not a whole number, so $84$ biscuits cannot be split in the ratio $4:7$.
