On a tray the exact ratio of cocoa biscuits to vanilla biscuits is $4:7$. Which total number of biscuits is possible?

- $77$
- $74$
- $78$
- $80$

## Explanation

Since $4$ and $7$ are coprime, the counts are $4k$ and $7k$ for an integer $k$. Their total must be a multiple of $11$.
