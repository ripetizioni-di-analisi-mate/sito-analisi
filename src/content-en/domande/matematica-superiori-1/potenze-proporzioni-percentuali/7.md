For which values of $a$ in $\{-2,0,2\}$ is the quotient $\dfrac{a^7}{a^7}$ defined and equal to $1$?

- $2$
- $0,\ 2$
- $-2,\ 2$
- $-2,\ 0,\ 2$

## Explanation

The denominator $a^7$ is nonzero when $a$ equals $-2$ or $2$. In both cases a nonzero number is divided by itself, giving $1$. The same cancellation requires excluding $a=0$, since that value would turn the fraction into the undefined quotient $\dfrac00$. For $a=-2$, the same minus sign appears in both the numerator and the denominator.
