A stack of $500$ identical sheets is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall. What is the thickness of one sheet in metres, expressed in scientific notation?

- $1.2\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The total height is $6\cdot10^{-2}$ metres. To find the thickness per sheet, divide by the number of sheets, which can be written as $5\cdot10^2$. The quotient of the coefficients is $\dfrac65=1.2$. Subtracting exponents gives $-2-2=-4$, so the thickness is $1.2\cdot10^{-4}$ metres. Multiplying by $500$ recovers the original height.
