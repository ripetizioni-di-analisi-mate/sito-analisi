In the proportion $2:3=12:x$, what is $x$?

- $8$
- $18$
- $36$
- $6$

## Explanation

In a proportion, the product of the inner terms equals the product of the outer terms. This gives an equation with a factor of two multiplying the unknown. Dividing both sides by two gives the missing term.

$$
2x=3\cdot12,\qquad x=18
$$

The result is $18$.
