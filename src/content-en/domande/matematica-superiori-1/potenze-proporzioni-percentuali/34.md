Which operation on the two terms preserves the ratio $3:5$?

- Add $3$ to both
- Multiply both by $3$
- Subtract $2$ from both
- Multiply only the first by $3$

## Explanation

Multiplying both terms by $3$ gives $9:15$, with $\dfrac9{15}=\dfrac35$. The common factor cancels. Adding $3$ would give $\dfrac68=\dfrac34$ and subtracting $2$ would give $\dfrac13$. Multiplying only the numerator produces $\dfrac95$ and triples the quotient. Preserving the fraction requires the same nonzero factor for both terms. This is the property that allows a ratio to be reduced to its simplest whole-number terms.
