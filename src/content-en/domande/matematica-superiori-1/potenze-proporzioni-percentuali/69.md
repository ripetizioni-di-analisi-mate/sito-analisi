A positive quantity first rises by $25\%$. After a second change it is $10\%$ higher than its original value. What was the second change?

- A decrease of $12\%$
- A decrease of $15\%$
- A decrease of $10\%$
- An increase of $12\%$

## Explanation

Taking an initial baseline of $100$ units, the first increase brings the amount to $125$. The overall increase of $10\%$ gives a final amount of $110$. The second stage subtracts $125-110=15$ units from $125$, so the decrease is $\dfrac{15}{125}=0.12=12\%$. Multiplying the factors checks that $1.25\cdot0.88=1.10$. The choice of one hundred units is possible because the changes are ratios that apply to any positive starting amount.
