An initial value of $600$ increases by $10\%$ and then decreases by $10\%$ of the new value. What is the final value?

- $594$
- $600$
- $540$
- $660$

## Explanation

The two percentages apply to different base values. The first factor is eleven tenths and the second is nine tenths. Their product is ninety-nine hundredths.

$$
600\cdot\frac{11}{10}\cdot\frac9{10}=594
$$

The result is $594$.
