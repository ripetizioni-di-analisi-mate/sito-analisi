A voucher gives a $30\%$ discount, with a maximum saving of €$12$. How much is paid for an item costing €$50$ when the voucher is used?

- €$35$
- €$15$
- €$23$
- €$38$

## Explanation

Without the limit, the discount would be $30\%$ of €$50$, that is $50\cdot\dfrac{30}{100}=15$ euros. Since €$15$ exceeds the maximum saving of €$12$, the actual discount is €$12$. The price to pay is therefore

$$
50-12=38\ \text{euros}.
$$

The answer €$35$ comes from applying the $30\%$ without taking the limit into account, while €$23$ comes from subtracting both €$15$ and €$12$.
