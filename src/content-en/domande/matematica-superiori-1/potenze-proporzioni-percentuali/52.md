A shop offers a $30\%$ discount capped at €$12$. How much does a €$50$ item cost after the discount?

- €$35$
- €$12$
- €$42$
- €$38$

## Explanation

Thirty per cent of $50$ is $15$, above the cap. Subtract the maximum allowed discount to get $50-12=38$ euros.
