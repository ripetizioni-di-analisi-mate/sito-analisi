An item costs $80$ euros and is discounted by $20\%$. What is the final price?

- $16\,\text{euros}$
- $96\,\text{euros}$
- $60\,\text{euros}$
- $64\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The discount is twenty percent of the original price. The final price is eighty percent of the original price. Therefore multiply the price by eighty hundredths.

$$
80\left(1-\frac{20}{100}\right)=64
$$

The result is $64\,\text{euros}$.
