$3$ notebooks cost $9$ euros. What is the cost of $15$ notebooks at the same unit price?

- $15\,\text{euros}$
- $9\,\text{euros}$
- $48\,\text{euros}$
- $45\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The price of one notebook is the total cost divided by the number of notebooks. The unit price is three euros. The new cost is that price times the new quantity.

$$
\frac{9}{3}=3,\qquad 3\cdot15=45
$$

The result is $45\,\text{euros}$.
