Four identical machines would take $9$ hours to finish a job on their own. After $3$ hours, two more machines join at the same rate. How many more hours are needed?

- $3\,\mathrm h$
- $6\,\mathrm h$
- $4\,\mathrm h$
- $7\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

The full job requires $4\cdot9=36$ machine-hours. In the first three hours the machines carry out $4\cdot3=12$, leaving $24$. There are now six machines, so the remaining time is $24:6=4$ hours. Seven hours includes the first phase too, whereas the question asks for time remaining when the machines are added.
