After a discount of $20\%$, the price is $24$ euros. What was the original price?

- $30\,\text{euros}$
- $\dfrac{96}{5}\,\text{euros}$
- $\dfrac{144}{5}\,\text{euros}$
- $44\,\text{euros}$

## Explanation

The amount paid is eighty percent of the original price. To recover the original price, divide by eighty hundredths. Adding twenty percent to the reduced price would use a different base.

$$
P\cdot\frac45=24,\qquad P=24\cdot\frac54=30
$$

The result is $30\,\text{euros}$.
