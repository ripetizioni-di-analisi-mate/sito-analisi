Which expression gives the product $(3\cdot10^{-4})(5\cdot10^6)$ in scientific notation?

- $1.5\cdot10^{2}$
- $1.5\cdot10^{-3}$
- $1.5\cdot10^{3}$
- $1.5\cdot10^{10}$

## Explanation

The product of the coefficients is $15$ and the sum of the exponents is $2$. This gives $15\cdot10^2$. To put the coefficient between $1$ and $10$, write $15=1.5\cdot10$, giving $1.5\cdot10^3$.
