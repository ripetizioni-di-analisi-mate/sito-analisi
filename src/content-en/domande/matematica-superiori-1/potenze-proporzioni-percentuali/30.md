A price goes from $120$ to $150$ euros. What is the percentage increase?

- $20\%$
- $25\%$
- $30\%$
- $125\%$

## Explanation

The difference is $150-120=30$ euros. The original price is the base, so the relative change is $\dfrac{30}{120}=\dfrac14$. This equals $25\%$ as a percentage.
