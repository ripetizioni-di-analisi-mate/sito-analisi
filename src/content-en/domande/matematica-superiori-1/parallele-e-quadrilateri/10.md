A transversal crosses two parallel lines. One interior angle measures $47^\circ$. At the other intersection, what is the measure of an angle adjacent to its alternate interior angle?

- $47^\circ$
- $133^\circ$
- $43^\circ$
- $94^\circ$

## Explanation

The alternate interior angle also measures $47^\circ$ because the lines are parallel. An adjacent angle is supplementary, so it measures $180^\circ-47^\circ=133^\circ$.
