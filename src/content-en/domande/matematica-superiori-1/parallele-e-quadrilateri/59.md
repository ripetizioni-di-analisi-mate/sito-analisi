A rhombus has a side of $10$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A rhombus has four congruent sides. To find the perimeter, add four copies of the same length. The angle measures are unnecessary for this computation.

$$
P=4\cdot10=40
$$

The result is $40\,\mathrm{cm}$.
