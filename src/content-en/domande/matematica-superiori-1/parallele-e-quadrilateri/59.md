The diagonals of a rhombus measure $12$ and $16\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its perimeter?

- $56$
- $20$
- $40$
- $48$

## Explanation

The sides are hypotenuses of the right triangles formed by the diagonals. The halves have lengths $6$ and $8$, so the Pythagorean theorem gives $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$. Each side measures $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the perimeter is $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. To check, the legs and hypotenuse form a $3,4,5$ triple with all lengths doubled.
