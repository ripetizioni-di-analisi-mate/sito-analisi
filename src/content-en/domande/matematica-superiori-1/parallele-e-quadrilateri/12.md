Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An interior angle measures $71^\circ$. What is the measure of its same-side interior angle?

- $71^\circ$
- $19^\circ$
- $251^\circ$
- $109^\circ$

## Explanation

Same-side interior angles are supplementary when the lines are parallel. Their measures sum to one hundred eighty degrees. Subtract the known angle from this total.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-71^\circ=109^\circ
$$

The result is $109^\circ$.
