Same-side interior angles on two parallel lines measure $(4x+4)^\circ$ and $(2x+20)^\circ$. What is $x$?

- $8$
- $30$
- $24$
- $26$

## Explanation

The angles are interior and on the same side of the transversal. Their measures therefore add to a straight angle: $(4x+4)+(2x+20)=180$. This simplifies to $6x=156$, so $x=26$. Substitution gives $108^\circ$ and $72^\circ$, obtuse and acute angles that make a straight angle. Equating the two expressions would instead give measures that fail to make a straight angle.
