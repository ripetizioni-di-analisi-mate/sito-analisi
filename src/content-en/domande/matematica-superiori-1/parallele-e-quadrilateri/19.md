Which fact is sufficient to prove that two lines cut by a transversal are parallel?

- Two same-side interior angles both measure $68^\circ$.
- Two alternate interior angles measure $68^\circ$ and $112^\circ$.
- Two same-side interior angles measure $68^\circ$ and $112^\circ$.
- Two corresponding angles measure $68^\circ$ and $112^\circ$.

## Explanation

The only supplementary pair of same-side interior angles uses $68^\circ+112^\circ=180^\circ$. The sufficient condition therefore applies. Alternate interior angles and corresponding angles instead require equal measures, and $68^\circ$ differs from $112^\circ$. Two same-side interior angles of $68^\circ$ would total $136^\circ$, less than a straight angle. The positions determine which calculation to use.
