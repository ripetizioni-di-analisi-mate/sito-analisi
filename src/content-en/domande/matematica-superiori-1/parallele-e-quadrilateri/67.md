An isosceles trapezoid has bases $12$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $90$
- $30$
- $45$
- $60$

## Explanation

Joining a rotated copy gives a parallelogram with base $12+6=18$ and the same height $5$. The combined figure has area $90\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and contains two congruent trapezoids. One has area $45\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The same calculation can be read as the product of the height and the average of the two base lengths, which is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
