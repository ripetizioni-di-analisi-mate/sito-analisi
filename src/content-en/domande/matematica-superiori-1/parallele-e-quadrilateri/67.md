In an isosceles trapezoid, one angle adjacent to the longer base measures $56^\circ$. What is the measure of the other angle adjacent to the same base?

- $124^\circ$
- $34^\circ$
- $56^\circ$
- $112^\circ$

## Explanation

In an isosceles trapezoid, angles adjacent to the same base are congruent. Both angles are on the stated base. Their measures are therefore equal.

$$
\beta=56^\circ
$$

The result is $56^\circ$.
