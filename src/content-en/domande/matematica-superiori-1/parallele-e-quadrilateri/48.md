The diagonals of a parallelogram meet at $O$. If $AC=30$ cm, what is the length of $AO$?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other. O is therefore the midpoint of AC. The length AO is half the length of the diagonal.

$$
AO=\frac{AC}2=\frac{30}2=15
$$

The result is $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
