A parallelogram has diagonals $AC=20$ and $BD=28$ that meet at $O$. Which equality is false?

- $AO=OC$
- $BO=OD$
- $AC+BD=48$
- $AO=BO$

## Explanation

Point $O$ bisects each segment separately. Thus $AO=OC=10$ and $BO=OD=14$. Both equalities between halves of the same diagonal are true. The diagonals also sum to $48$. The only false equality compares halves of different lengths: $AO=10$ is less than $BO=14$. Halving preserves the ratio of the diagonal lengths.
