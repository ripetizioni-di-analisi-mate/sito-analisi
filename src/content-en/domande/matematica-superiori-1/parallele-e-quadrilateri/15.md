Two same-side interior angles between parallel lines have measures in the ratio $4:5$. What is the measure of the smaller angle?

- $100^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles are supplementary. Writing their degree measures as $4x$ and $5x$ gives $9x=180$ and $x=20$. The smaller angle measures $4\cdot20=80$ degrees.
