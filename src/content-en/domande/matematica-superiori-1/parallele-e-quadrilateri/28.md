The four angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio $1:2:3:4$. What is the largest measure?

- $108^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $160^\circ$
- $144^\circ$

## Explanation

The four measures are $k,2k,3k,4k$ in a common unit. Adding gives $10k=360^\circ$, hence $k=36^\circ$. The largest measure is $4k=144^\circ$. The others are $36^\circ$, $72^\circ$, and $108^\circ$. All are positive and below $180^\circ$, and the coefficients in the ratio count the ten units that make a full turn.
