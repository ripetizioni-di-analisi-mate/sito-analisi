Three angles of a convex quadrilateral measure $73^\circ$, $83^\circ$, and $90^\circ$. What is the fourth angle?

- $24^\circ$
- $204^\circ$
- $156^\circ$
- $114^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal divides a convex quadrilateral into two triangles. Its four interior angles therefore sum to two straight angles. Subtract the sum of the known angles from three hundred sixty degrees.

$$
\delta=360^\circ-73^\circ-83^\circ-90^\circ=114^\circ
$$

The result is $114^\circ$.
