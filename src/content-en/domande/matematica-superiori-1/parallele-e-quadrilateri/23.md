Inside right angle $AOB$, ray $OC$ makes an angle of $(2x+8)^\circ$ with $OA$ and $(x+7)^\circ$ with $OB$. What is $x$?

- $15$
- $55$
- $25$
- $30$

## Explanation

The pieces together fill the right angle, so $(2x+8)+(x+7)=90$. This gives $3x+15=90$ and $x=25$. At that value the two angles measure $58^\circ$ and $32^\circ$, both interior as required. The total comes from the whole angle: it is a right angle here, so it is half a straight angle.
