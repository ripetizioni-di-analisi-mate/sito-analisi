The diagonals of rectangle $ABCD$ meet at $O$. The sum $AO+BO+CO+DO$ is $36$. What is the length of $AC$?

- $9$
- $36$
- $12$
- $18$

## Explanation

Group the two halves on each diagonal, making the given sum $AC+BD=36$. A rectangle has congruent diagonals, so the sum consists of two equal lengths, each $18$. You can also note that all four halves are $9$. The question asks for a whole diagonal, made up of two such halves.
