An interior angle of a rhombus measures $54^\circ$. The diagonal from that vertex bisects the angle. What is the measure of each half?

- $27^\circ$
- $54^\circ$
- $126^\circ$
- $36^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal of a rhombus divides the angle at its endpoint into two congruent parts. These parts sum to the original angle. Each is therefore half the given measure.

$$
\beta=\frac{54^\circ}2=27^\circ
$$

The result is $27^\circ$.
