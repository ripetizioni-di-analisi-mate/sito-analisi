Two opposite angles of a parallelogram have degree measures $2x+14$ and $4x-28$. What is the measure of either angle?

- $56^\circ$
- $124^\circ$
- $42^\circ$
- $21^\circ$

## Explanation

Opposite angles are congruent. Equating their measures gives $2x=42$, so $x=21$. Each angle measures $2\cdot21+14=56$ degrees.
