Which condition on the diagonals suffices to show that a parallelogram is a rectangle?

- They bisect each other.
- They are perpendicular.
- They are congruent.
- They have different lengths.

## Explanation

The congruent-diagonals criterion applies to a parallelogram. A common midpoint is a property of all parallelograms. Perpendicular diagonals identify a rhombus, which may have an acute angle.
