A parallelogram has consecutive sides of lengths $10$ cm and $15$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $150\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Opposite sides of a parallelogram are congruent. Each given length appears twice on the boundary. The perimeter is therefore twice the sum of the consecutive side lengths.

$$
P=2(10+15)=50
$$

The result is $50\,\mathrm{cm}$.
