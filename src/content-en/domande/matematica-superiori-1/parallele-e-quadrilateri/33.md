A rectangle has sides $12$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. A parallelogram has the same side lengths and height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the base of length $12$. How many square centimetres do their areas differ by?

- $24$
- $16$
- $48$
- $0$

## Explanation

The two perimeters both equal $2(12+8)=40\,\mathrm{cm}$. In the rectangle the side of length $8$ is also the height, while the given height of the parallelogram is $6$. Their areas therefore differ by $12\cdot8-12\cdot6=24\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Side lengths stay the same when the shape slants. The perpendicular separation of the bases changes, and it controls the enclosed area.
