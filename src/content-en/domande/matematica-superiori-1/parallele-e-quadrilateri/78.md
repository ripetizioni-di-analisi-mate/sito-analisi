Which chain of implications always holds for convex quadrilaterals?

- Rectangle implies square, and square implies rhombus.
- Square implies rhombus, and rhombus implies parallelogram.
- Parallelogram implies rectangle, and rectangle implies square.
- Rhombus implies rectangle, and rectangle implies parallelogram.

## Explanation

A square has four congruent sides and therefore meets the definition of a rhombus. Every rhombus has two pairs of parallel opposite sides, so it is a parallelogram. The direction matters: a rhombus can have a $60^\circ$ angle, and a rectangle can have consecutive sides of lengths $3$ and $4$. These examples disprove chains that assert extra conditions.
