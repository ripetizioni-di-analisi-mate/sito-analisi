Which additional condition on the diagonals guarantees that a rectangle is a square?

- They are perpendicular.
- They are congruent.
- They bisect each other.
- They meet at an interior point.

## Explanation

A rectangle is already a parallelogram, so its diagonals are congruent and bisect each other at an interior point. If they are also perpendicular, the right triangles beside the intersection have matching legs. Hence two consecutive sides are congruent, and all four sides are equal. Combined with the right angles, this identifies a square.
