One angle of a parallelogram measures $63^\circ$. What is the measure of the opposite angle?

- $117^\circ$
- $27^\circ$
- $126^\circ$
- $63^\circ$

## Explanation

Each of the two opposite angles is supplementary to the same adjacent angle. They are therefore congruent. The opposite angle has the given measure.

$$
\gamma=63^\circ
$$

The result is $63^\circ$.
