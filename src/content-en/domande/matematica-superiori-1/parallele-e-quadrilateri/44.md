Which cyclic sequence of interior angles identifies a parallelogram among these four convex quadrilaterals?

- $70^\circ,70^\circ,110^\circ,110^\circ$
- $80^\circ,100^\circ,95^\circ,85^\circ$
- $60^\circ,100^\circ,60^\circ,140^\circ$
- $70^\circ,110^\circ,70^\circ,110^\circ$

## Explanation

In the alternating sequence of $70^\circ$ and $110^\circ$, both pairs of opposite angles are congruent. Every consecutive pair also sums to $180^\circ$, so the same-side interior angle test makes the opposite sides parallel. Four angles can total $360^\circ$ even when their order fails these properties. In the sequence $70^\circ,70^\circ,110^\circ,110^\circ$, the two angles of $70^\circ$ are consecutive and add to $140^\circ$.
