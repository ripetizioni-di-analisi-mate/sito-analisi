In a quadrilateral the diagonals bisect each other, are perpendicular, and have lengths $8$ and $14$. What is the most specific classification guaranteed?

- Rectangle.
- Rhombus.
- Square.
- Trapezoid with exactly one pair of parallel sides.

## Explanation

The shared midpoint satisfies the parallelogram criterion. In a parallelogram, perpendicular diagonals guarantee that two consecutive sides are congruent, and then all four are congruent. The quadrilateral is therefore a rhombus. The lengths $8$ and $14$ differ, while a rectangle would have equal diagonals. This rules out a rectangle and thus a square as well.
