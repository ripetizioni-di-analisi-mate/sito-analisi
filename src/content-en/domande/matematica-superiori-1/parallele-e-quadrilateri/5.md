A transversal crosses two parallel lines, giving corresponding angles with degree measures $3x+15$ and $5x-9$. What is $x$?

- $12$
- $24$
- $6$
- $18$

## Explanation

Corresponding angles are congruent. Equating the expressions gives $2x=24$. At $x=12$, both measures are $51^\circ$.
