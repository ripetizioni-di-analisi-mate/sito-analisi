A transversal crosses two distinct lines. The interior angles on the same side are $88^\circ$ and $94^\circ$. By how much must **only the second** decrease for the data to be consistent with parallel lines?

- $2^\circ$
- $6^\circ$
- $4^\circ$
- $8^\circ$

## Explanation

For parallel lines the interior angles on the same side add to $180^\circ$. The second must be $92^\circ$, two degrees below $94^\circ$.
