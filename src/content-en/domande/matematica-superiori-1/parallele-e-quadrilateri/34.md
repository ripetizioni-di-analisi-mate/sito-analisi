A parallelogram has consecutive sides of lengths $12$ cm and $18$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $216\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Opposite sides of a parallelogram are congruent. Each given length appears twice on the boundary. The perimeter is therefore twice the sum of the consecutive side lengths.

$$
P=2(12+18)=60
$$

The result is $60\,\mathrm{cm}$.
