A parallelogram has area $54\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The side not parallel to the corresponding base is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. What is the perimeter?

- $28$
- $34$
- $30$
- $17$

## Explanation

Recover the base from the area formula by dividing $54$ by the height $6$. The base is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. To find the perimeter, use the consecutive side lengths $9$ and $8$, so $2(9+8)=34\,\mathrm{cm}$. The height has already supplied the missing base length and is not a length to add to the boundary.
