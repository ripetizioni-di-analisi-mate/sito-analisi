Two vertical angles at the first intersection of a transversal both measure $61^\circ$. What does this tell us about the two lines being cut?

- The lines are parallel.
- This information alone does not decide whether the lines are parallel.
- The lines are perpendicular.
- The transversal is parallel to the second line.

## Explanation

Equality of vertical angles is a property of the first intersection. Keep that intersection fixed and rotate the second line around its point on the transversal. The angles at the first intersection remain unchanged as the direction of the second line changes. Only one direction of the second line is parallel to the first. A test requires comparing the two intersections.
