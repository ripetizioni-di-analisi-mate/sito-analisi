In a rhombus, one diagonal makes a $34^\circ$ angle with a side from the same vertex. What is the measure of an interior angle adjacent to the bisected angle?

- $112^\circ$
- $68^\circ$
- $146^\circ$
- $34^\circ$

## Explanation

The diagonal bisects the angle at that vertex, so the angle measures $2\cdot34^\circ=68^\circ$. A rhombus is a parallelogram, so the adjacent angle measures $180^\circ-68^\circ=112^\circ$.
