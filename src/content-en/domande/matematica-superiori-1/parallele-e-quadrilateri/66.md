In an isosceles trapezoid, one angle adjacent to the longer base measures $53^\circ$. What is the measure of the other angle adjacent to the same base?

- $127^\circ$
- $53^\circ$
- $37^\circ$
- $106^\circ$

## Explanation

In an isosceles trapezoid, angles adjacent to the same base are congruent. Both angles are on the stated base. Their measures are therefore equal.

$$
\beta=53^\circ
$$

The result is $53^\circ$.
