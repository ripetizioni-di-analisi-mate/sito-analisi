A parallelogram has consecutive sides of lengths $8$ cm and $12$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $96\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Opposite sides of a parallelogram are congruent. Each given length appears twice on the boundary. The perimeter is therefore twice the sum of the consecutive side lengths.

$$
P=2(8+12)=40
$$

The result is $40\,\mathrm{cm}$.
