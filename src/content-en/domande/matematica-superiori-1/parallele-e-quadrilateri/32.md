In a parallelogram with perimeter $54\,\mathrm{cm}$, one side is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ longer than the other. What is the shorter length?

- $16$
- $22$
- $13.5$
- $11$

## Explanation

Opposite sides have equal lengths, so two consecutive sides total $54/2=27$. If the shorter is $a$, the longer is $a+5$, giving $2a+5=27$. Thus $a=11$, and the other side is $16$. These lengths satisfy the stated difference and give the required perimeter when each is counted twice.
