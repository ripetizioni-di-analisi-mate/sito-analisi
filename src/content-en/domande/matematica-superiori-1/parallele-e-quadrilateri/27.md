Three angles of a convex quadrilateral measure $72^\circ$, $82^\circ$, and $90^\circ$. What is the fourth angle?

- $26^\circ$
- $206^\circ$
- $116^\circ$
- $154^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal divides a convex quadrilateral into two triangles. Its four interior angles therefore sum to two straight angles. Subtract the sum of the known angles from three hundred sixty degrees.

$$
\delta=360^\circ-72^\circ-82^\circ-90^\circ=116^\circ
$$

The result is $116^\circ$.
