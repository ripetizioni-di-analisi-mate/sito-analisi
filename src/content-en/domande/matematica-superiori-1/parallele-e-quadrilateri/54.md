Which figure can have congruent diagonals while having exactly one pair of parallel sides?

- A rectangle with unequal consecutive sides.
- An isosceles trapezoid.
- A rhombus with perpendicular diagonals.
- A rotated square.

## Explanation

In an isosceles trapezoid, the bases form the only parallel pair. The legs and the angles at a common base are congruent. Together with the common base, this gives SAS congruence for the two triangles built on that base, so the diagonals are equal. The other figures are parallelograms and have two pairs of parallel sides even after rotation.
