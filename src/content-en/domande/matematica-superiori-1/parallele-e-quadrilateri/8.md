Where is the alternate interior angle paired with an interior angle $\alpha$?

- At the other intersection, between the lines and on the same side of the transversal.
- At the same intersection, in the vertically opposite sector.
- At the other intersection, outside the lines and on the opposite side of the transversal.
- At the other intersection, between the lines and on the opposite side of the transversal.

## Explanation

The word “interior” places the sector between the two lines. “Alternate” means moving to the opposite side of the transversal and to the other intersection. Moving to the other intersection on the same side would identify a same-side interior angle. Relationships between measures apply once the two positions have been identified.
