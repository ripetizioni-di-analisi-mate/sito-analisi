Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle measures $43^\circ$. What is the measure of its corresponding angle on the other line?

- $43^\circ$
- $137^\circ$
- $47^\circ$
- $86^\circ$

## Explanation

Corresponding angles are congruent when a transversal crosses parallel lines. The angles have the same relative position at the two intersections. Their measures therefore agree.

$$
\beta=43^\circ
$$

The result is $43^\circ$.
