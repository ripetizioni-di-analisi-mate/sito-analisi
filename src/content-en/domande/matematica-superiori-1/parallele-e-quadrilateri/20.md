Which condition on two distinct lines cut by a transversal guarantees that the lines are parallel?

- A congruent pair of vertical angles.
- Two supplementary adjacent angles at one intersection.
- A pair of same-side interior angles that sum to $150^\circ$.
- A congruent pair of corresponding angles.

## Explanation

The corresponding-angle criterion compares angles in the same position at the two intersections. The vertical angles and the adjacent angles mentioned depend on just one intersection. Same-side interior angles on parallel lines sum to $180^\circ$.
