Rays $OA,OC,OD,OB$ occur in that order within a straight angle. Suppose $\angle AOC=25^\circ$, $\angle COD=65^\circ$, and $\angle DOB=90^\circ$. Which ray is perpendicular to $OA$?

- $OC$
- $OB$
- $OA$
- $OD$

## Explanation

Following the order of the rays, the angle from $OA$ to $OD$ includes the $25^\circ$ sector and the $65^\circ$ sector. They add up to $90^\circ$. The final right-angle sector gives a check: from $OA$ to $OB$ the total is $180^\circ$, so $OB$ is opposite $OA$ and $OD$ splits the straight angle in half.
