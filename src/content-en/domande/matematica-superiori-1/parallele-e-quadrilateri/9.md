An angle $\alpha$ and its alternate interior angle measure $(x+12)^\circ$ and $(2x-24)^\circ$. For which $x$ are the lines parallel?

- $36$
- $24$
- $12$
- $64$

## Explanation

The alternate interior angle test requires equal measures. Set $x+12=2x-24$ and subtract $x$ to obtain $x=36$. Both expressions give $48^\circ$, a valid angle measure. The alternate positions make this equality sufficient for parallelism. A total of $180^\circ$ would apply to a pair of same-side interior angles instead.
