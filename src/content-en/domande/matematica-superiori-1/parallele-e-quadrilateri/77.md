A convex quadrilateral has four congruent sides and one angle of $70^\circ$. What is the most specific classification that follows?

- Rhombus.
- Square.
- Rectangle.
- Isosceles trapezoid with exactly one pair of parallel sides.

## Explanation

Both pairs of opposite sides are congruent, satisfying the side test for a parallelogram. With all four sides equal, the more specific family here is the rhombus. The angle of $70^\circ$ is not right, ruling out a rectangle and therefore a square. Two pairs of parallel sides rule out a trapezoid in the stated sense.
