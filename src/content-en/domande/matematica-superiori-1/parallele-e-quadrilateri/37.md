Two consecutive angles of a parallelogram differ by $32^\circ$. What is the smaller measure?

- $74^\circ$
- $58^\circ$
- $106^\circ$
- $148^\circ$

## Explanation

Consecutive angles are supplementary because the side joining their vertices cuts two parallel opposite sides. If the smaller measure is $a$, the larger is $a+32^\circ$, so $2a+32^\circ=180^\circ$. Thus $a=74^\circ$. Check that the larger measure, $106^\circ$, differs by $32^\circ$ and that the two measures together make a straight angle.
