In an isosceles trapezoid, one angle adjacent to the longer base measures $59^\circ$. What is the measure of the other angle adjacent to the same base?

- $121^\circ$
- $31^\circ$
- $118^\circ$
- $59^\circ$

## Explanation

In an isosceles trapezoid, angles adjacent to the same base are congruent. Both angles are on the stated base. Their measures are therefore equal.

$$
\beta=59^\circ
$$

The result is $59^\circ$.
