A diagonal splits angle $A$ of a parallelogram $ABCD$ into parts of $25^\circ$ and $37^\circ$. What is the angle at $B$?

- $62^\circ$
- $143^\circ$
- $118^\circ$
- $130^\circ$

## Explanation

The whole angle at $A$ measures $25^\circ+37^\circ=62^\circ$. Apply the consecutive-angle property to that whole angle, since side $AB$ crosses the two opposite parallel sides. The angle at $B$ is therefore $180^\circ-62^\circ=118^\circ$. Using just one piece of angle $A$ would give the supplement of a different angle.
