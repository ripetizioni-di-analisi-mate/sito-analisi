Three angles of a convex quadrilateral measure $71^\circ$, $81^\circ$, and $90^\circ$. What is the fourth angle?

- $28^\circ$
- $118^\circ$
- $208^\circ$
- $152^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal divides a convex quadrilateral into two triangles. Its four interior angles therefore sum to two straight angles. Subtract the sum of the known angles from three hundred sixty degrees.

$$
\delta=360^\circ-71^\circ-81^\circ-90^\circ=118^\circ
$$

The result is $118^\circ$.
