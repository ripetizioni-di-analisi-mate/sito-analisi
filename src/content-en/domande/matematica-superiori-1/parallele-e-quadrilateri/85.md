A convex quadrilateral has congruent diagonals. What additional fact guarantees that it is a rectangle?

- The diagonals meet at the midpoint of both.
- Only one diagonal is bisected at the intersection.
- A pair of adjacent sides has equal lengths.
- The perimeter is $40\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

If the diagonals bisect each other, the quadrilateral is a parallelogram. It is then a rectangle because the diagonals are congruent.
