Which diagonal property holds for every rectangle and every rhombus?

- The diagonals are congruent.
- The diagonals are perpendicular.
- The diagonals bisect each other.
- Both diagonals bisect the angles at the vertices.

## Explanation

Rectangles and rhombi both belong to the parallelogram family. They inherit the shared midpoint of both diagonals. A rhombus with different diagonal lengths rules out congruent diagonals as a common property. In a rectangle with unequal side lengths the diagonals are not perpendicular and do not split the right angles into equal halves.
