One angle of a parallelogram measures $61^\circ$. What is the measure of the opposite angle?

- $119^\circ$
- $29^\circ$
- $61^\circ$
- $122^\circ$

## Explanation

Each of the two opposite angles is supplementary to the same adjacent angle. They are therefore congruent. The opposite angle has the given measure.

$$
\gamma=61^\circ
$$

The result is $61^\circ$.
