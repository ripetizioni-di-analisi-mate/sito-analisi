The consecutive angles of a quadrilateral are $80^\circ$, $110^\circ$, $80^\circ$, and $90^\circ$. What prevents it from being a parallelogram?

- Neither pair of opposite angles is congruent.
- The angle total exceeds $360^\circ$.
- One pair of opposite angles has different measures.
- Every angle in every parallelogram is right.

## Explanation

In a cyclic list of four angles, the first and third are opposite, and so are the second and fourth. The first pair is congruent. The second measures $110^\circ$ and $90^\circ$. In a parallelogram both opposite pairs must have equal measures. The sum $80^\circ+110^\circ=190^\circ$ also confirms that the first consecutive pair is not supplementary.
