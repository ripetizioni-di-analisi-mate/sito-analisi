Two consecutive sides of a parallelogram differ by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The perimeter is $46\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the length of the longer side?

- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $23\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two consecutive sides sum to $23\,\mathrm{cm}$. If the shorter side measures $x$ centimeters, the longer one measures $x+5$. Solving $2x+5=23$ gives $x=9$, so the longer side is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
