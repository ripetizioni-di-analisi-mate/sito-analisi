The sides of a right angle are $OA$ and $OB$. An interior ray $OC$ forms two angles in the ratio $1:2$. What is the measure of the larger angle?

- $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

The degree measures can be written as $x$ and $2x$. Since $OC$ lies inside the angle, their sum is the right angle and $3x=90$. The larger angle measures $2\cdot30=60$ degrees.
