Three angles of a convex quadrilateral measure $74^\circ$, $84^\circ$, and $90^\circ$. What is the fourth angle?

- $112^\circ$
- $22^\circ$
- $202^\circ$
- $158^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal divides a convex quadrilateral into two triangles. Its four interior angles therefore sum to two straight angles. Subtract the sum of the known angles from three hundred sixty degrees.

$$
\delta=360^\circ-74^\circ-84^\circ-90^\circ=112^\circ
$$

The result is $112^\circ$.
