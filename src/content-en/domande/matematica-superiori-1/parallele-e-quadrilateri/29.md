A diagonal splits angle $A$ of a quadrilateral into $31^\circ$ and $54^\circ$ sectors. The angles at $B$ and $C$ are $75^\circ$ and $106^\circ$. What is the angle at $D$?

- $94^\circ$
- $85^\circ$
- $106^\circ$
- $148^\circ$

## Explanation

The angle at $A$ is the sum of its two sectors, or $85^\circ$. In the quadrilateral total, count the whole measure at each vertex once. The three known measures add to $85^\circ+75^\circ+106^\circ=266^\circ$, leaving $94^\circ$ at $D$. The diagonal supplies the pieces of angle $A$ without changing the total of the four angles.
