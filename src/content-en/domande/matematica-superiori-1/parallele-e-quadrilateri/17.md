Two corresponding angles measure $72^\circ$ and $78^\circ$. What follows?

- The two lines are not parallel.
- The two lines are parallel because both angles are acute.
- The transversal is perpendicular to both.
- The two lines must be perpendicular to each other.

## Explanation

The direct property says that parallelism would make corresponding angles equal. Their measures differ by $6^\circ$, so that necessary condition fails. The fact that both are acute only compares each with $90^\circ$. You can also picture the two line directions in the same position relative to the transversal: they make two distinct, nearby inclinations.
