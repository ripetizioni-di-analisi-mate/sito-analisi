Which description identifies corresponding angles formed by two lines and a transversal?

- They are interior and on opposite sides of the transversal.
- They are interior and on the same side of the transversal.
- They are opposite at the same intersection.
- They are at different intersections and occupy matching positions.

## Explanation

Imagine following the transversal and stopping at each intersection. Selecting a sector in the same position relative to the rays at each stop identifies corresponding angles. The descriptions with two interior sectors specify alternate or same-side angles, depending on the sides of the transversal. Vertical angles belong to one intersection.
