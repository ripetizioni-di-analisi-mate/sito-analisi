Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle measures $52^\circ$. What is the measure of its corresponding angle on the other line?

- $128^\circ$
- $38^\circ$
- $104^\circ$
- $52^\circ$

## Explanation

Corresponding angles are congruent when a transversal crosses parallel lines. The angles have the same relative position at the two intersections. Their measures therefore agree.

$$
\beta=52^\circ
$$

The result is $52^\circ$.
