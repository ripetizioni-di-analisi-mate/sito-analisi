A rhombus has a side of $9$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $81\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A rhombus has four congruent sides. To find the perimeter, add four copies of the same length. The angle measures are unnecessary for this computation.

$$
P=4\cdot9=36
$$

The result is $36\,\mathrm{cm}$.
