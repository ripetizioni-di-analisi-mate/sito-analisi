A rhombus has perimeter $36\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $81$
- $54$
- $108$
- $216$

## Explanation

All sides are congruent, so the base has length $36/4=9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Use the parallelogram area formula, base times its corresponding height, to get $9\cdot6=54\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The value $81$ would be the area of a square with the same perimeter and height $9$. In the stated rhombus, the slant makes the perpendicular distance smaller than the side length.
