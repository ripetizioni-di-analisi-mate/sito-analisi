Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle measures $46^\circ$. What is the measure of its corresponding angle on the other line?

- $134^\circ$
- $46^\circ$
- $44^\circ$
- $92^\circ$

## Explanation

Corresponding angles are congruent when a transversal crosses parallel lines. The angles have the same relative position at the two intersections. Their measures therefore agree.

$$
\beta=46^\circ
$$

The result is $46^\circ$.
