Two corresponding angles on parallel lines measure $(5k-16)^\circ$ and $(3k+20)^\circ$. What is $k$?

- $22$
- $18$
- $9$
- $36$

## Explanation

Because the lines are parallel, the angles in corresponding positions have equal measures. Set $5k-16=3k+20$, so $2k=36$ and $k=18$. Substitution gives $74^\circ$ at each intersection. This checks that the measures agree and that both are positive and less than a straight angle, as the configuration requires.
