A parallelogram has perimeter $70\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its consecutive sides are in the ratio $2:3$. What is the shorter length?

- $21$
- $28$
- $14$
- $35$

## Explanation

Write the consecutive side lengths as $2k$ and $3k$. Each appears twice in the perimeter. Half the perimeter is therefore $2k+3k=35$, giving $k=7$. The shorter side is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the other is $21\,\mathrm{cm}$. Reducing $14:21$ checks the ratio, and adding two copies of each side gives $70\,\mathrm{cm}$.
