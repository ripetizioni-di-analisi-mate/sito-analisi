In trapezoid $ABCD$, $AB\parallel CD$, $\angle A=65^\circ$, and $\angle C=102^\circ$. What are $\angle B$ and $\angle D$, in that order?

- $115^\circ,78^\circ$
- $65^\circ,102^\circ$
- $102^\circ,65^\circ$
- $78^\circ,115^\circ$

## Explanation

Leg $BC$ crosses the parallel bases, so angles $B$ and $C$ are supplementary. Thus $B=180^\circ-102^\circ=78^\circ$. On the other leg, $AD$, the supplement of $65^\circ$ is $115^\circ$ and belongs to $D$. The pairs follow the legs. Checking only the total of $360^\circ$ would fail to detect the measures assigned in the wrong order.
