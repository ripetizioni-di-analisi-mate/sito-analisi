In a trapezoid, one angle on the longer base measures $64^\circ$. What is the angle on the shorter base adjacent to the same leg?

- $64^\circ$
- $26^\circ$
- $244^\circ$
- $116^\circ$

## Explanation

The bases are parallel and the leg is a transversal. The specified angles are same-side interior angles. They are supplementary even when the trapezoid is not isosceles.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-64^\circ=116^\circ
$$

The result is $116^\circ$.
