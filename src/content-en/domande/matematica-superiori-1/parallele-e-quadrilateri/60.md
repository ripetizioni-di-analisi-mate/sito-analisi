A rhombus and a rectangle have the same perimeter. The rectangle has side lengths $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the side length of the rhombus?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The perimeter of the rectangle is $2(8+12)=40$ centimeters. The rhombus has four congruent sides, so each side measures $40$ divided by $4$ centimeters.
