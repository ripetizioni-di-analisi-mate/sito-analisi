Which additional property is enough to identify a square among rhombi?

- The diagonals are perpendicular.
- The diagonals bisect the vertex angles.
- The diagonals share the same midpoint.
- The diagonals are congruent.

## Explanation

A rhombus is already a parallelogram with all sides congruent. Congruent diagonals add the rectangle criterion, implying four right angles. Together with congruent sides, this identifies a square. The other properties hold in every rhombus. For example, a rhombus with perpendicular diagonals of lengths $6$ and $8$ has those properties but does not have congruent diagonals.
