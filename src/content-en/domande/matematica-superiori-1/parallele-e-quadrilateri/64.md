An interior angle of a rhombus measures $66^\circ$. The diagonal from that vertex bisects the angle. What is the measure of each half?

- $66^\circ$
- $114^\circ$
- $24^\circ$
- $33^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal of a rhombus divides the angle at its endpoint into two congruent parts. These parts sum to the original angle. Each is therefore half the given measure.

$$
\beta=\frac{66^\circ}2=33^\circ
$$

The result is $33^\circ$.
