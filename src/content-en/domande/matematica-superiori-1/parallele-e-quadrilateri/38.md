A parallelogram has one right angle. What is the most specific classification this guarantees?

- Square.
- Rectangle.
- Rhombus.
- Trapezoid with exactly one pair of parallel sides.

## Explanation

The angles next to the given one supplement $90^\circ$, so both are right angles too. The opposite angle has the same measure as the given angle. The parallelogram therefore has four right angles and meets the definition of a rectangle. Side lengths remain free: a rectangle with sides $4$ and $7$ meets the angle condition without having four congruent sides.
