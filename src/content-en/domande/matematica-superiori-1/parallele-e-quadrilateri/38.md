One interior angle of a parallelogram measures $59^\circ$. What is the measure of the adjacent interior angle?

- $59^\circ$
- $121^\circ$
- $31^\circ$
- $118^\circ$

## Explanation

The opposite sides of a parallelogram are parallel. The two consecutive angles are therefore same-side interior angles with respect to a side acting as a transversal. Their sum is one hundred eighty degrees.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-59^\circ=121^\circ
$$

The result is $121^\circ$.
