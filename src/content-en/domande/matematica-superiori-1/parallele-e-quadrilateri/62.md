In rhombus $ABCD$ the diagonals meet at $O$, and $\angle A=46^\circ$. What is $\angle ABO$?

- $23^\circ$
- $67^\circ$
- $134^\circ$
- $44^\circ$

## Explanation

First find the whole angle at $B$. It is consecutive to $A$, so it measures $180^\circ-46^\circ=134^\circ$. Diagonal $BD$ bisects the angle at $B$, and ray $BO$ runs along that diagonal. The half requested is $67^\circ$. Halving $46^\circ$ would instead give an angle at $A$, where $AC$ is the angle bisector.
