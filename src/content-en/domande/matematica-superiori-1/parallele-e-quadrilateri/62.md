An interior angle of a rhombus measures $58^\circ$. The diagonal from that vertex bisects the angle. What is the measure of each half?

- $58^\circ$
- $29^\circ$
- $122^\circ$
- $32^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal of a rhombus divides the angle at its endpoint into two congruent parts. These parts sum to the original angle. Each is therefore half the given measure.

$$
\beta=\frac{58^\circ}2=29^\circ
$$

The result is $29^\circ$.
