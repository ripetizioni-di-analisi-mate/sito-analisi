A parallelogram has a side of $9$ cm and perimeter $46$ cm. What is the length of the consecutive side?

- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $37\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $23\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Half the perimeter is the sum of two consecutive side lengths. Divide the perimeter by two, then subtract the known side length. The side found and its opposite complete the boundary.

$$
b=\frac{46}2-9=14
$$

The result is $14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
