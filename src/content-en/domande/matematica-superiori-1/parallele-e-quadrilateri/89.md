Two opposite sides of a parallelogram measure $x+10$ and $3x-4$ centimetres. A consecutive side measures $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the perimeter?

- $58$
- $29$
- $48$
- $46$

## Explanation

Opposite sides are congruent, so $x+10=3x-4$. This gives $2x=14$ and $x=7$. Both expressions give the length $17\,\mathrm{cm}$. The side of length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ also occurs twice, so the perimeter is $2(17+12)=58\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $29$ counts only two consecutive sides and corresponds to half the perimeter.
