A parallelogram has one side twice the length of the next and perimeter $54\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the shorter side length?

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

If the shorter side measures $x$ centimeters, the longer one measures $2x$. Half the perimeter is $27\,\mathrm{cm}$, so $x+2x=27$. The shorter side is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the longer side is $18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
