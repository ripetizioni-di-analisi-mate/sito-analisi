In a trapezoid, one angle on the longer base measures $62^\circ$. What is the angle on the shorter base adjacent to the same leg?

- $62^\circ$
- $28^\circ$
- $118^\circ$
- $242^\circ$

## Explanation

The bases are parallel and the leg is a transversal. The specified angles are same-side interior angles. They are supplementary even when the trapezoid is not isosceles.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-62^\circ=118^\circ
$$

The result is $118^\circ$.
