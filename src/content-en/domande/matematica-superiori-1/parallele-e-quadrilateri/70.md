In an isosceles trapezoid, an angle at the shorter base measures $116^\circ$. What is the measure of each angle at the longer base?

- $116^\circ$
- $64^\circ$
- $58^\circ$
- $32^\circ$

## Explanation

Each leg is a transversal of the bases. The angles along the same leg are supplementary, so the angle at the longer base measures $64^\circ$. The isosceles trapezoid property makes both angles on that base equal.
