In a parallelogram two opposite angles add to $136^\circ$. What is the measure of each of the other two angles?

- $68^\circ$
- $112^\circ$
- $44^\circ$
- $136^\circ$

## Explanation

Opposite angles are congruent, so the first two each measure $136^\circ/2=68^\circ$. Each of the other two is consecutive to one of those and supplements it. The required measure is $180^\circ-68^\circ=112^\circ$. The quadrilateral total provides a check as well: subtracting $136^\circ$ leaves $224^\circ$ to split evenly.
