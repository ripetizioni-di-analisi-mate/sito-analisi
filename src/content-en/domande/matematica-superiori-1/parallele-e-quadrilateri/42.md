One angle of a parallelogram measures $59^\circ$. What is the measure of the opposite angle?

- $121^\circ$
- $59^\circ$
- $31^\circ$
- $118^\circ$

## Explanation

Each of the two opposite angles is supplementary to the same adjacent angle. They are therefore congruent. The opposite angle has the given measure.

$$
\gamma=59^\circ
$$

The result is $59^\circ$.
