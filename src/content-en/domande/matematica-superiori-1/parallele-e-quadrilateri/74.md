A trapezoid has area $42\,\mathrm{cm}^2$, height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, and shorter base $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its longer base length?

- $14$
- $9$
- $7$
- $19$

## Explanation

Doubling the area gives the sum of the bases times the height. Dividing $2\cdot42$ by $6$ gives the base sum, $14$. The longer base is therefore $14-5=9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Dividing the area by the height gives $7$, but that represents the mean of the two base lengths rather than either individual length.
