In a trapezoid, one angle on the longer base measures $68^\circ$. What is the angle on the shorter base adjacent to the same leg?

- $68^\circ$
- $112^\circ$
- $22^\circ$
- $248^\circ$

## Explanation

The bases are parallel and the leg is a transversal. The specified angles are same-side interior angles. They are supplementary even when the trapezoid is not isosceles.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-68^\circ=112^\circ
$$

The result is $112^\circ$.
