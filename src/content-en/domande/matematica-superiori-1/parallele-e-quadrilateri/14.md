Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An interior angle measures $77^\circ$. What is the measure of its same-side interior angle?

- $77^\circ$
- $103^\circ$
- $13^\circ$
- $257^\circ$

## Explanation

Same-side interior angles are supplementary when the lines are parallel. Their measures sum to one hundred eighty degrees. Subtract the known angle from this total.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-77^\circ=103^\circ
$$

The result is $103^\circ$.
