Two same-side interior angles formed by a transversal have equal measures. Which common measure makes the lines parallel?

- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$

## Explanation

For the lines to be parallel, the two same-side interior angles must be supplementary. Since they have the same measure $m$, this requires $2m=180^\circ$, so $m=90^\circ$. Two right angles in those positions also satisfy the converse test. Equality alone needs a further check here because the defining relationship for this pair is a sum.
