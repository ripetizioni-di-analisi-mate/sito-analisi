The diagonals of a parallelogram meet at $O$. Given $AO+BO=13$ and $AC=16$, what is the length of $BD$?

- $10$
- $5$
- $26$
- $18$

## Explanation

The midpoint of $AC$ gives $AO=8$. This leaves $BO=13-8=5$ in the stated sum. Since $O$ also bisects the second diagonal, $BD=2\cdot5=10$. Alternatively, double the given sum to check: the diagonals add to $2(AO+BO)=26$, and subtracting $AC=16$ gives the same length.
