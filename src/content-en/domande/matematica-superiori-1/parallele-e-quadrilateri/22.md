Ray $OC$ splits the convex angle $AOB$ into two angles. Angle $AOC$ is twice angle $COB$, and $\angle AOB=105^\circ$. What is $\angle AOC$?

- $35^\circ$
- $70^\circ$
- $52.5^\circ$
- $75^\circ$

## Explanation

The whole angle contains both pieces because the ray lies inside it. If the smaller piece is $m$, the larger is $2m$, so $3m=105^\circ$ and $m=35^\circ$. The question asks for the doubled piece, $70^\circ$. Check both the ratio $70:35=2:1$ and the sum of the pieces, which gives the stated convex angle.
