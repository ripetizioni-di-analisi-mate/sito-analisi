A parallelogram has consecutive sides of lengths $6$ cm and $9$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $54\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Opposite sides of a parallelogram are congruent. Each given length appears twice on the boundary. The perimeter is therefore twice the sum of the consecutive side lengths.

$$
P=2(6+9)=30
$$

The result is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
