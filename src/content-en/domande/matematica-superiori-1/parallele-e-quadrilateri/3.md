Two parallel lines meet a transversal. Of the eight angles at the intersections, how many measure $126^\circ$ if one has that measure?

- $2$
- $6$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

At the first intersection, the vertical angle also measures $126^\circ$. The other two are adjacent and therefore measure $180^\circ-126^\circ=54^\circ$. At the second intersection the same measures occur again: the lines are parallel, so each angle has a congruent corresponding angle. There are two obtuse angles of $126^\circ$ per intersection, or four altogether.
