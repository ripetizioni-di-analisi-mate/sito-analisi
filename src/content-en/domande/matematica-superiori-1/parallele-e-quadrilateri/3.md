Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle measures $49^\circ$. What is the measure of its corresponding angle on the other line?

- $131^\circ$
- $41^\circ$
- $49^\circ$
- $98^\circ$

## Explanation

Corresponding angles are congruent when a transversal crosses parallel lines. The angles have the same relative position at the two intersections. Their measures therefore agree.

$$
\beta=49^\circ
$$

The result is $49^\circ$.
