Two same-side interior angles on parallel lines differ by $28^\circ$. What is the larger measure?

- $104^\circ$
- $76^\circ$
- $118^\circ$
- $152^\circ$

## Explanation

If the smaller measure is $m$, the larger is $m+28^\circ$. Parallelism makes their sum $180^\circ$, so $2m+28^\circ=180^\circ$. Thus $m=76^\circ$ and the larger measure is $104^\circ$. The two measures are equally far from $90^\circ$, one below and one above, because their mean is half of a straight angle.
