Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An interior angle measures $74^\circ$. What is the measure of its same-side interior angle?

- $106^\circ$
- $74^\circ$
- $16^\circ$
- $254^\circ$

## Explanation

Same-side interior angles are supplementary when the lines are parallel. Their measures sum to one hundred eighty degrees. Subtract the known angle from this total.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-74^\circ=106^\circ
$$

The result is $106^\circ$.
