Given $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(6,2)$, and $D=(2,2)$, which point shows that the diagonals bisect each other?

- $(2,0)$
- $(4,2)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(3,2)$

## Explanation

Find the midpoint of $AC$ by averaging its two horizontal coordinates and its two vertical coordinates. This gives $(3,1)$. For $BD$ the averages are $(4+2)/2=3$ and $(0+2)/2=1$. The diagonals therefore share a midpoint. The points $(2,0)$ and $(4,2)$ are the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $CD$ instead.
