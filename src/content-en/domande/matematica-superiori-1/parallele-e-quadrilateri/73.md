In trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB\parallel CD$, side $AD$ is perpendicular to $AB$ and $\angle B=65^\circ$. What is $\angle C$?

- $115^\circ$
- $65^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $25^\circ$

## Explanation

Side $AD$ is perpendicular to $CD$ as well because the bases are parallel. Angles $A$ and $D$ are therefore right. The same-side interior angles on $BC$ sum to $180^\circ$, giving $C=115^\circ$. Alternatively, removing the two right angles from the total of $360^\circ$ leaves exactly $180^\circ$ to split between $B$ and $C$.
