The diagonals of a parallelogram meet at $O$. If $AC=24$ cm, what is the length of $AO$?

- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other. O is therefore the midpoint of AC. The length AO is half the length of the diagonal.

$$
AO=\frac{AC}2=\frac{24}2=12
$$

The result is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
