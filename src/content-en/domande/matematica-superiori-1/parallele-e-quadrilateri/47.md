The diagonals of a parallelogram meet at $O$. Given $AC=14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $BO=2AO$, what is the length of $BD$?

- $14$
- $21$
- $28$
- $7$

## Explanation

The midpoint on the first diagonal gives $AO=14/2=7\,\mathrm{cm}$. The given relationship doubles this to yield $BO=14\,\mathrm{cm}$. On the second diagonal the midpoint gives $OD=BO$, so $BD=28\,\mathrm{cm}$. The two doublings have different reasons: one comes from the stated relationship between the halves, the other from the midpoint property on one diagonal.
