Which property is sufficient to identify a square among rhombuses?

- Having perpendicular diagonals.
- Having parallel opposite sides.
- Having all sides congruent.
- Having one right angle.

## Explanation

A rhombus is a parallelogram. If it has one right angle, the opposite angle is right and the adjacent angles are supplementary to $90^\circ$, so they are right too. Together with four congruent sides, this gives a square.
