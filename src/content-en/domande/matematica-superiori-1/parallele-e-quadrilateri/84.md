Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ meet at $O$. If $AO=OC$ and $BO=OD$, which angle equality completes the SAS test for triangles $AOB$ and $COD$?

- $\angle AOB=\angle BOC$
- $\angle OAB=\angle ODC$
- $\angle ABO=\angle COD$
- $\angle AOB=\angle COD$

## Explanation

SAS requires the included angle between the two given sides. In the first triangle it is the angle at $O$ between $OA$ and $OB$. In the second it is the one between $OC$ and $OD$. These are vertical angles and are therefore congruent. A pair at different vertices would not be the angles included by the sides that are given.
