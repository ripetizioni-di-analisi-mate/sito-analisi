Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An interior angle measures $68^\circ$. What is the measure of its same-side interior angle?

- $68^\circ$
- $22^\circ$
- $112^\circ$
- $248^\circ$

## Explanation

Same-side interior angles are supplementary when the lines are parallel. Their measures sum to one hundred eighty degrees. Subtract the known angle from this total.

$$
\beta=180^\circ-68^\circ=112^\circ
$$

The result is $112^\circ$.
