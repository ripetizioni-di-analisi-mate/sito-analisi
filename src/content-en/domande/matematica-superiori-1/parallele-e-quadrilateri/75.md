The angles at the longer base of a trapezoid measure $60^\circ$ and $80^\circ$. What are the angles at the shorter base?

- $120^\circ$ and $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$ and $80^\circ$
- $120^\circ$ and $100^\circ$
- $100^\circ$ and $80^\circ$

## Explanation

Each angle at the shorter base is supplementary to the angle at the other end of the same leg. The measures are $180^\circ-60^\circ=120^\circ$ and $180^\circ-80^\circ=100^\circ$.
