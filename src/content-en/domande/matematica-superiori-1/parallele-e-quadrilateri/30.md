The angles of a convex quadrilateral have degree measures $x$, $x+20$, $2x$, and $2x-20$. What is the largest angle?

- $100^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $60^\circ$

## Explanation

The sum simplifies to $6x$. A diagonal divides the quadrilateral into two triangles, so the total is $360^\circ$ and $x=60$. The measures are $60^\circ$, $80^\circ$, $120^\circ$, and $100^\circ$.
