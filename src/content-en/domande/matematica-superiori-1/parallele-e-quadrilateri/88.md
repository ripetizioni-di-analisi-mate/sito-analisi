A diagonal of length $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ divides a parallelogram into two triangles, each with perimeter $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the parallelogram’s perimeter?

- $40$
- $33$
- $13$
- $26$

## Explanation

The two triangle perimeters count the four outer sides and two copies of the interior diagonal. Their sum is $40\,\mathrm{cm}$, so subtract $2\cdot7$ to obtain $26\,\mathrm{cm}$. Another approach is to subtract $7$ from one triangle perimeter, giving $13$ for the sum of two consecutive sides, then double it because opposite sides are congruent.
