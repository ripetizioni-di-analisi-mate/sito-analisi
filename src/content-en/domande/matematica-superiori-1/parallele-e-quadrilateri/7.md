The alternate interior angle of an angle $\alpha$ forms a linear pair with a $124^\circ$ angle. The lines are parallel. What is $\alpha$?

- $124^\circ$
- $62^\circ$
- $56^\circ$
- $112^\circ$

## Explanation

First recover the alternate angle at the intersection where the given measure appears. The two linear-pair angles span a straight line and add to $180^\circ$, so the alternate angle measures $56^\circ$. Since the lines are parallel, this angle and the requested angle are congruent. The given measure belongs to the adjacent sector and must be converted before transferring it.
