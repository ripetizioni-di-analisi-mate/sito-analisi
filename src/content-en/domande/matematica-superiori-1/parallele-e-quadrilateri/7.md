Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An interior angle measures $54^\circ$. What is the measure of its alternate interior angle?

- $126^\circ$
- $36^\circ$
- $54^\circ$
- $108^\circ$

## Explanation

Alternate interior angles lie between the lines and on opposite sides of the transversal. When the lines are parallel, these angles are congruent. The unknown measure therefore equals the known measure.

$$
\beta=54^\circ
$$

The result is $54^\circ$.
