A convex quadrilateral has one pair of opposite sides both parallel and equal. Which step can establish that the remaining two sides are parallel as well?

- Assume both diagonals have the same length
- Treat all interior angles as equal
- Assume a right angle between the given sides
- Draw a diagonal and compare two triangles by side-angle-side

## Explanation

The diagonal is a common side. It forms equal alternate interior angles with the parallel sides. Congruence supplies another equal pair of alternate interior angles, proving the second pair of sides parallel.
