In a parallelogram, one angle is $36^\circ$ greater than an adjacent angle. What is the measure of the larger angle?

- $72^\circ$
- $144^\circ$
- $126^\circ$
- $108^\circ$

## Explanation

Adjacent angles in a parallelogram are supplementary. Writing their degree measures as $x$ and $x+36$ gives $2x+36=180$ and $x=72$. The other angle is $108^\circ$.
