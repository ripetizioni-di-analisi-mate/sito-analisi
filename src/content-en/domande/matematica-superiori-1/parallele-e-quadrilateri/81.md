The diagonals of convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ meet at $O$, where $AO=OC=3$ cm and $BO=OD=5$ cm. Which conclusion is always justified?

- The quadrilateral is a parallelogram
- The quadrilateral must be a rectangle
- The quadrilateral must be a rhombus
- The diagonals must be perpendicular

## Explanation

The equalities show that the diagonals bisect each other. This property is a criterion for recognising a parallelogram. Alone it does not imply either right angles or four congruent sides.

The quadrilateral is a parallelogram.
