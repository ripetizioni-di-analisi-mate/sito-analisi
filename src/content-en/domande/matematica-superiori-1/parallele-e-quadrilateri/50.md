The diagonals of parallelogram $ABCD$ meet at $O$. The lengths of $AO$ and $OC$ in centimeters are $x+2$ and $3x-10$. What is the length of $AC$?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The point $O$ is the midpoint of $AC$, so $x+2=3x-10$. This gives $x=6$. Each half measures $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the entire diagonal measures $16\,\mathrm{cm}$.
