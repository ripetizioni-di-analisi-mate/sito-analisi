The diagonals of a parallelogram meet at $O$. If $AC=18$ cm, what is the length of $AO$?

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other. O is therefore the midpoint of AC. The length AO is half the length of the diagonal.

$$
AO=\frac{AC}2=\frac{18}2=9
$$

The result is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
