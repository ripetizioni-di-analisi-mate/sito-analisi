One angle of a parallelogram measures $57^\circ$. What is the measure of the opposite angle?

- $57^\circ$
- $123^\circ$
- $33^\circ$
- $114^\circ$

## Explanation

Each of the two opposite angles is supplementary to the same adjacent angle. They are therefore congruent. The opposite angle has the given measure.

$$
\gamma=57^\circ
$$

The result is $57^\circ$.
