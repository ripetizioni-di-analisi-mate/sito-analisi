A rhombus has diagonals of lengths $10$ and $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $60$
- $120$
- $30$
- $15$

## Explanation

A rhombus has perpendicular diagonals that bisect each other. Each of the four resulting triangles has legs of lengths $5$ and $6$ and area $5\cdot6/2=15\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The rhombus includes all four triangles, giving total area $60\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. An equivalent calculation uses the entire diagonal as the base of the two larger triangles.
