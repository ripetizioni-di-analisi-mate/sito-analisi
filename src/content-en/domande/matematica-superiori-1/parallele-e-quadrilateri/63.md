An interior angle of a rhombus measures $62^\circ$. The diagonal from that vertex bisects the angle. What is the measure of each half?

- $62^\circ$
- $118^\circ$
- $31^\circ$
- $28^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal of a rhombus divides the angle at its endpoint into two congruent parts. These parts sum to the original angle. Each is therefore half the given measure.

$$
\beta=\frac{62^\circ}2=31^\circ
$$

The result is $31^\circ$.
