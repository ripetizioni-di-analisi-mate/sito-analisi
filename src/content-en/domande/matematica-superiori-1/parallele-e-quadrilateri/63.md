In a convex quadrilateral both diagonals bisect the angles at their endpoints. After drawing one diagonal, which pair of properties proves the triangles on its sides congruent?

- Only three equal pairs of corresponding angles
- Two equal altitudes
- Two equal pairs of corresponding angles and the included common side
- Just the sum of the interior angles

## Explanation

The diagonal bisects the angles at both endpoints and is the side between them in each triangle. The angle-side-angle criterion applies.
