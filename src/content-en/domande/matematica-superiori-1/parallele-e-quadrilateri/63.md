In the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ the diagonal $AC$ bisects both the angle at $A$ and the angle at $C$. Which congruence criterion applies to triangles $ABC$ and $ADC$?

- SAS (side, angle, side)
- SSS (side, side, side)
- ASA (angle, side, angle)
- No criterion, because the data concern only angles

## Explanation

Since $AC$ bisects the angle at $A$, we have $\angle BAC=\angle DAC$. In the same way $AC$ bisects the angle at $C$, so $\angle BCA=\angle DCA$. The side $AC$ is common to the two triangles, and its endpoints are the vertices of these angles. Therefore the triangles have one side and the two angles at its endpoints congruent, and ASA applies. SAS and SSS would require congruent sides other than $AC$, which are not given.

Hence the criterion that applies is ASA.
