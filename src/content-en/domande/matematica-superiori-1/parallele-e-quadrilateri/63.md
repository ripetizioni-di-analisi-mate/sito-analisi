Rhombus $ABCD$ has diagonals meeting at $O$ and $\angle ABO=32^\circ$. What is the interior angle at $C$?

- $64^\circ$
- $148^\circ$
- $116^\circ$
- $58^\circ$

## Explanation

The diagonal through $B$ bisects angle $ABC$. Hence $\angle B=2\cdot32^\circ=64^\circ$. Vertex $C$ is consecutive to $B$, so $\angle C=180^\circ-64^\circ=116^\circ$. Angle $A$ has the same measure, since it is opposite $C$. The location of $O$ tells us that the given angle is only one half of the whole angle at $B$.
