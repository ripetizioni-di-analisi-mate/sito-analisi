What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in the expansion of $(x+x^2)^2$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The first square is $x^2$. Twice the product has degree three and the second square has degree four, so only the first contribution determines the required coefficient.

$$
(x+x^2)^2=x^2+2x^3+x^4
$$
