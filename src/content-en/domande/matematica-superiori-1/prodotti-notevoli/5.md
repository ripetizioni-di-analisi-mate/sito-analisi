To form the linear term of $(x+11)^3$, you take $x$ from exactly one of the three factors. What is its coefficient?

- $363$
- $33$
- $121$
- $1331$

## Explanation

There are three choices for the factor supplying $x$. Each of the other two supplies $11$, giving coefficient $3\cdot11^2=363$.
