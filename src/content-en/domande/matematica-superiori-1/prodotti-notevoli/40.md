Which constant term completes $4x^2+20x+\square$ as the square of a binomial?

- $5$
- $10$
- $100$
- $25$

## Explanation

The first term of the binomial is $2x$. If the second is $b$, the doubled product is $4bx$. Matching it to $20x$ gives $b=5$, so the constant term is $5^2$.

$$
(2x+5)^2=4x^2+20x+25
$$
