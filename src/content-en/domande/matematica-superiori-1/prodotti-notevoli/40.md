For which $c$ does $x^2+4x+c$ have minimum value $3$ over the reals?

- $4$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $7$

## Explanation

Rewrite $x^2+4x+c=(x+2)^2+c-4$. The square reaches zero at $-2$, so $c-4$ must equal $3$.
