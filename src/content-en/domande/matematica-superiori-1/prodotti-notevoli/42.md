Without long multiplication, by how much is $98^2$ smaller than $100^2$?

- $400$
- $396$
- $404$
- $4$

## Explanation

The difference is $(100-98)(100+98)=2\cdot198=396$. Check using the square of a difference: $(100-2)^2=10000-400+4$. Twice the product subtracts $400$, while the square of the offset adds $4$ back. Subtracting that $4$ as well would give a gap of $404$, which conflicts with the first calculation. Both methods also give the check $98^2=9604$.
