Simplify $(x-2)^3+(x+2)^3$.

- $2 x^{3}$
- $2 x^{3} + 24 x$
- $2 x^{3} + 12 x^{2}$
- $24 x$

## Explanation

The two expansions have opposite quadratic terms and opposite constant terms. The cubic and linear terms have the same signs and add.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x-2)^3+(x+2)^3&=2x^3+(-6+6)x^2 \\[6pt]&\quad+(12+12)x+(-8+8) \\[6pt]&=2x^3+24x
\end{aligned}
$$
