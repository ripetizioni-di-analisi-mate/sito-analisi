For $x<0$, which comparison between $(x+7)^2$ and $(x-7)^2$ is correct?

- $(x+7)^2>(x-7)^2$
- $(x+7)^2<(x-7)^2$
- $(x+7)^2=(x-7)^2$
- It depends on how far $x$ is from $-7$

## Explanation

The difference of the squares is $[(x+7)-(x-7)][(x+7)+(x-7)]=28x$. It is negative whenever $x<0$, even at the point where one binomial vanishes. At $x=-7$, for example, the squares equal $0$ and $196$. At $x=0$ the squares agree. This comparison also holds when both binomials are negative.
