The product of two consecutive odd integers is always one less than which square?

- The square of the larger integer
- The square of their sum
- The square of the integer between them
- The square of the smaller integer

## Explanation

If $m$ is the integer between them, the odd integers are $m-1$ and $m+1$. Their product is exactly $m^2-1$.
