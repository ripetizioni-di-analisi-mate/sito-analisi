For which real values of $m$ does $(mx+3)(mx-3)=16x^2-9$ hold for every $x$?

- $m=4$ or $m=-4$
- Only $m=4$
- Only $m=-4$
- $m=16$ or $m=-16$

## Explanation

The product is $m^2x^2-9$, because the mixed terms cancel. Equality for every $x$ requires $m^2=16$, the same constraint obtained by substituting $x=1$. Both $4$ and $-4$ satisfy it. The sign of $m$ disappears when it is squared. Changing the sign of $m$ swaps the factors after reversing the sign of each one.
