For integer $k$, which rewriting of $(2k+1)^2$ directly shows that the square of every odd number leaves remainder $1$ on division by $4$?

- $4k(k+1)+1$
- $4k^2+1$
- $4(k+1)^2+1$
- $4k(k-1)+1$

## Explanation

Expanding gives $4k^2+4k+1$. The first two terms together form a multiple of $4$, leaving exactly one unit.
