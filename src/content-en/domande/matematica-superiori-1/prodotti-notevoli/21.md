What is the expansion of $(x+3)^3$?

- $x^{3} + 9 x^{2} + 27 x + 27$
- $x^{3} + 27$
- $x^{3} + 3 x^{2} + 9 x + 27$
- $x^{3} + 9 x^{2} + 27 x - 27$

## Explanation

To cube a binomial, multiply its square by the binomial itself. The quadratic and linear terms each receive three equal contributions. The coefficients follow the pattern one, three, three, and one.

$$
(x+3)^3=x^3+9x^2+27x+27
$$

The result is $x^{3} + 9 x^{2} + 27 x + 27$.
