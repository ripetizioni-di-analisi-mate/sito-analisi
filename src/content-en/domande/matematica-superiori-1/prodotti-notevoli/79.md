In $(t+a)(t+b)$ the linear coefficient vanishes and the constant is $-9$. Which unordered pair can be $\{a,b\}$?

- $\{-1,9\}$
- $\{0,-9\}$
- $\{-3,3\}$
- $\{-9,1\}$

## Explanation

The linear coefficient is $a+b$, so $b=-a$. The constant then becomes $-a^2=-9$, requiring $a=\pm3$.
