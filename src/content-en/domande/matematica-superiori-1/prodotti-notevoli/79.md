What is the coefficient of $xy$ in $(x+2y+3)(x+2y-3)$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The two brackets are conjugates in $3$, with common part $x+2y$. Their product reduces to $(x+2y)^2-9$. The square of that common part contains the mixed term $2\cdot x\cdot2y=4xy$. Products using just one factor of $3$ cancel, while terms already inside the square remain. The full expansion is $x^2+4xy+4y^2-9$.
