A spreadsheet uses $P=(u+v)^2-u^2-v^2$ to estimate the product $uv$. Which factor should be applied to $P$ to obtain $uv$ exactly?

- $-1$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$

## Explanation

Cancelling the two squares leaves the cross term $2uv$. Multiplying that result by $\dfrac12$ recovers the ordinary product for every pair of inputs.
