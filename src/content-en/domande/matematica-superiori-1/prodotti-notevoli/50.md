Which expression has the same value as $999\cdot1001$?

- $1000^2+1$
- $1000^2-1$
- $1000^2-2$
- $999^2+1$

## Explanation

Both factors are one away from $1000$. Writing them as a difference and a sum gives the identity with $a=1000$ and $b=1$.

$$
(1000-1)(1000+1)=1000^2-1
$$
