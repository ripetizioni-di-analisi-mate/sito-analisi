The graph of $y=-(2x+1)^2$ meets the vertical axis at $y=-1$. What other property follows directly from the formula?

- The maximum is $0$ at $x=-\dfrac12$
- The minimum is $0$ at $x=-\dfrac12$
- The maximum is $0$ at $x=\dfrac12$
- The maximum is $-1$ at $x=0$

## Explanation

A square is nonnegative, and the outside minus makes it nonpositive. The highest possible value is therefore $0$, reached when $2x+1=0$ at $x=-\dfrac12$. The vertical intercept $-1$ is the value at $x=0$, but the graph rises to reach the horizontal axis. At every other point the square is strictly positive and the vertical coordinate is negative.
