The side length of a square increases from $x$ to $x+3$, where $x>0$. How much does its area increase?

- $9$
- $6 x + 9$
- $6 x$
- $x^{2} + 9$

## Explanation

The increase in area is the final area minus the original area. Expand the square of the new side length. The x-squared term cancels in the difference.

$$
(x+3)^2-x^2=6x+9
$$

The result is $6 x + 9$.
