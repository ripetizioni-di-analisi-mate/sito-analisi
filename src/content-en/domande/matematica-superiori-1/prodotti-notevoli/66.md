For which real $z$ is the product $(z+6)(z-6)$ strictly negative?

- $0<z<6$
- $-6<z<6$
- $z<-6$ or $z>6$
- $-6\le z\le6$

## Explanation

Inside $(-6,6)$ the first factor is positive and the second is negative. In the two outer intervals the factors have the same sign, so their product is positive. One factor is zero at each endpoint. The form $z^2-36$ also gives the condition $z^2<36$. The square shows that the interval is symmetric about zero.
