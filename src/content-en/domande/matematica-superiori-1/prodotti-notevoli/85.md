What is the linear coefficient in the expansion of $\left(\dfrac{x}{3}-6\right)^2$?

- $-4$
- $-2$
- $4$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The linear term is twice the product. The factor $-6$ is divided by $3$ in the product with the first term and then doubled.

$$
2\cdot\frac{x}{3}\cdot(-6)=-4x
$$
