If $a,b$ are real and $(a+b)^2=(a-b)^2$, what must be true?

- $ab=0$
- $a=b$
- $a=-b$
- $a^2+b^2=0$

## Explanation

The difference of the squares is $4ab$. It vanishes exactly when at least one factor is zero.
