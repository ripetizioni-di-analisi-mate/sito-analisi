The leading coefficient in $(2x+11)^2$ is not $2$. Which pair gives the coefficients of $x^2$ and $x$, in that order?

- $(2,44)$
- $(4,22)$
- $(2,22)$
- $(4,44)$

## Explanation

Squaring $2x$ gives $4x^2$. The cross term is $2(2x)(11)=44x$, so the coefficient $2$ is used differently in the two terms.
