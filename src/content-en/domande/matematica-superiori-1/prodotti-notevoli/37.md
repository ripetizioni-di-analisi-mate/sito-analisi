In the ordered expansion of $(2x-5)^3$, what are the signs of the coefficients of $x^3,x^2,x$, and the constant?

- $+,-,+,-$
- $+,-,-,+$
- $+,+,-,-$
- $-,-,+,+$

## Explanation

In the expansion the number of factors of $-5$ increases from zero to three. Products with zero or two negative factors have positive coefficients. Those with one or three have negative coefficients. The four terms are $8x^3$, $-60x^2$, $150x$, and $-125$, in that order. The coefficient of $x$ stays positive because its term contains the square of the offset.
