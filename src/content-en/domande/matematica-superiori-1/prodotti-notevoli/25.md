What is the coefficient of $x$ in $(2x+3)^3$?

- $54$
- $18$
- $27$
- $36$

## Explanation

To get a linear term, take $2x$ from one factor and $3$ from each of the other two. There are three choices for the position of $2x$.

$$
3\cdot(2x)\cdot 3^2=54x
$$
