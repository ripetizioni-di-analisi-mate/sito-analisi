Two nonzero real numbers $u,v$ satisfy $(u+v)^3=u^3+v^3$. Which relation between them makes the equality possible?

- $u+v=0$
- $u=v$
- $uv=1$
- $u-v=1$

## Explanation

Expanding and subtracting the two pure cubes leaves $3u^2v+3uv^2=3uv(u+v)$. Since $uv$ is nonzero, the sum $u+v$ must vanish.
