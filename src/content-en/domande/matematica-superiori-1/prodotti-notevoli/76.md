Expand $(3x+3y)(3x-3y)$.

- $9 x^{2} + 9 y^{2}$
- $3 x^{2} - 3 y^{2}$
- $9 x^{2} - 18 x y + 9 y^{2}$
- $9 x^{2} - 9 y^{2}$

## Explanation

Identify a sum and the corresponding difference. Square the entire first term, three times x, and the entire second term. The identity takes the difference of these two squares.

$$
(3x)^2-(3y)^2=9x^2-9y^2
$$

The result is $9 x^{2} - 9 y^{2}$.
