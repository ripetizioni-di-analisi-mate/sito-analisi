Which expression is independent of $x$?

- $(x+8)^2-(x-8)^2-16x$
- $(x+8)^2+(x-8)^2-128$
- $(x+8)^2+(x-8)^2-2x^2$
- $(x+8)^2-(x-8)^2-128$

## Explanation

Adding the squares of $x+8$ and $x-8$ cancels the mixed terms and gives $2x^2+128$. Subtracting $2x^2$ leaves the constant $128$. The difference of the squares gives $32x$. Subtracting either $16x$ or $128$ from that quantity still leaves a variable term. The constant can also be read off by evaluating the chosen expression at $x=0$.
