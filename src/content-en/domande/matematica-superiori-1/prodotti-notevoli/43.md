Which result for $89^2$ passes both checks, $89^2<8100$ and the same last digit as $9^2$?

- $7919$
- $8181$
- $7921$
- $8089$

## Explanation

The square of $90$ is $8100$. Since $89$ is positive and smaller than $90$, its square lies below that bound. The units digit comes from squaring its last digit, $9^2=81$. Among the candidates this leaves $7921$. The expansion $(90-1)^2=8100-180+1$ confirms the full value. The last-digit check alone would still leave two candidates.
