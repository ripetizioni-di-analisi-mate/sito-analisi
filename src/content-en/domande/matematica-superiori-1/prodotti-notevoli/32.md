Two polynomials of degree at most $2$ agree at $x=0,1,-1$. What follows?

- Only their constant terms agree
- Only their linear coefficients agree
- Only their quadratic coefficients agree
- All their coefficients agree

## Explanation

Write the difference as $ax^2+bx+c$. Its zero value at $x=0$ gives $c=0$. The other inputs give $a+b=0$ and $a-b=0$. Adding gives $2a=0$, and subtracting gives $2b=0$. Thus the difference is identically zero, even when both polynomials have degree less than two. Using only the points $1$ and $-1$ could leave a multiple of $x^2-1$.
