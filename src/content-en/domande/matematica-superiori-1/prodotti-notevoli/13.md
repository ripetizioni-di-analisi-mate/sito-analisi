What remains in the arithmetic mean of $(x+11)^2$ and $(x-11)^2$?

- $x^2+121$
- $2x^2+242$
- $x^2-121$
- $22x$

## Explanation

The cross terms cancel in the sum, leaving two copies each of $x^2$ and $121$. Dividing by two leaves $x^2+121$.
