In $(x+2x+1)^2$, two of the three terms contain the same variable. What is the most direct way to find the leading coefficient?

- Add $1^2+2^2$
- Use only the product $1\cdot2$
- Add all three terms at $x=1$
- Add $1+2$ and square $3$

## Explanation

Collecting like terms gives $(3x+1)^2$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is $9$ and includes the cross product between $x$ and $2x$.
