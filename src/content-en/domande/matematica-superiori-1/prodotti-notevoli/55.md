What is the coefficient of $xy$ in $(x-y+2)^2$?

- $2$
- $-1$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The term containing both variables comes from the pair $x,-y$. The pairs containing $2$ produce terms with only one variable.

$$
2x(-y)=-2xy
$$
