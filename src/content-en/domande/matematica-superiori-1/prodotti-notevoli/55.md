In $(a+b+c)^2$, substitute $c=-a-b$. What must $ab+ac+bc$ equal?

- $a^2+b^2+c^2$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{2}$

## Explanation

The bracket is zero, so its square is zero too. Expanding gives $a^2+b^2+c^2+2(ab+ac+bc)=0$, which gives the result.
