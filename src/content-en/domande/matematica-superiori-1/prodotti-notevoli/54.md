For which $k$ is $x^2+y^2+4+2xy-4x+ky$ the square of a trinomial of the form $ax+by+c$?

- $4$
- $-4$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

We can choose $a=1$ because the opposite trinomial has the same square. The term $2xy$ forces $b=1$ and the term $-4x$ forces $c=-2$. The term in $y$ must therefore be $2\cdot y\cdot(-2)=-4y$. A positive coefficient would conflict with one of the choices already fixed. Check the coefficient's magnitude by multiplying $2$ by $1$ and by $2$.
