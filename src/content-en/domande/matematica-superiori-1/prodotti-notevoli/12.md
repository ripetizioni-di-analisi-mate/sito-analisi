To calculate $62\cdot57$, a student uses $(60+2)(60-3)$. What is the total contribution of the mixed products?

- $0$
- $60$
- $-6$
- $-60$

## Explanation

The mixed products are $60\cdot(-3)=-180$ and $2\cdot60=120$. Their sum is $-60$ because the offsets from the chosen base are different. The contribution $-6$ comes from multiplying the offsets. Combining all terms gives the product $3600-60-6=3534$. The actual midpoint would be $\dfrac{119}{2}$, with offsets of magnitude $\dfrac52$.
