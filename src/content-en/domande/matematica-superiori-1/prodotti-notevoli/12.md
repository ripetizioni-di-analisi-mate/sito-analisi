To reduce $(x+11)^2-(x-11)^2$ without expanding, which two factors come from the difference of squares?

- $22,\ 2x+22$
- $2x,\ 2x-22$
- $11,\ x$
- $22,\ 2x$

## Explanation

The difference of the bases is $(x+11)-(x-11)=22$. Their sum is $2x$, so their product is $44x$.
