Two positive integers have mean $30$ and product $891$. How far is each from the mean?

- $3$
- $6$
- $9$
- $27$

## Explanation

Write the two integers as $30-d$ and $30+d$ with $d\ge0$. Their product is $900-d^2$. The given value implies $d^2=9$, so the distance is $3$. The difference between the integers is twice that, $6$, and the reconstructed pair is $27$ and $33$. Positivity also requires $d<30$, which the result satisfies.
