Simplify $(2x+1)^2-(2x-3)^2$.

- $16 x + 8$
- $8 x - 8$
- $-8$
- $16 x - 8$

## Explanation

The difference of the binomials is $4$, while their sum is $4x-2$. The difference of the squares is the product of those two expressions.

$$
((2x+1)-(2x-3))((2x+1)+(2x-3))=4(4x-2)
$$
