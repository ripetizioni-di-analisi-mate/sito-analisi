At which real values do $(t+5)^2$ and $t^2+25$ agree?

- For every $t$
- $t=0$ only
- $t=5$ only
- $t=0$ and $t=-5$

## Explanation

Expanding the first square gives $t^2+10t+25$. Its difference from the second expression is therefore $10t$, which is zero only at $t=0$. This point verifies one equality. For example, at $t=-5$ the first expression is zero and the second is $50$. In general, find the points of agreement by setting the difference equal to zero.
