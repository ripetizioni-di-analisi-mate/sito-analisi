For which $p$ is the polynomial $121x^2+px$ the square of $ax+b$ with real $a,b$?

- $22$
- $0$
- $-22$
- $121$

## Explanation

At $x=0$ the polynomial equals zero, so the square must have $b^2=0$. Hence $b=0$, which makes the mixed term $2abx$ zero regardless of the sign of $a$. The only choice is $p=0$, and the binomial reduces to the monomial $11x$ or its opposite. The value at zero fixes the constant before the mixed term is calculated.
