For which pairs $(x,y)$ is $(x+2y)(x-2y)$ equal to zero?

- Only $x=2y$
- $x=2y$ or $x=-2y$
- Only $x=-2y$
- $x=0$ or $y=0$

## Explanation

The product is zero when at least one factor is zero. This gives conditions $x+2y=0$ and $x-2y=0$, each sufficient. In the expanded form $x^2-4y^2$, they correspond to the bases being equal or opposite. For example, both $(2,1)$ and $(-2,1)$ give a product of zero. At $y=0$ both conditions give the same point $(0,0)$.
