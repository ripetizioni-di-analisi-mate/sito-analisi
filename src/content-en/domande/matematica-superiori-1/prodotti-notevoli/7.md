Which value of $c$ makes $x^2+cx+121=(x+11)^2$ an identity? Compare the coefficients of $x$.

- $121$
- $-22$
- $22$
- $11$

## Explanation

Twice the product of $x$ and $11$ is $22x$. In an identity the coefficients of like powers must match, so $c=22$.
