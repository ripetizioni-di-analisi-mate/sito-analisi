Without calculating each square separately, find $51^2-49^2$.

- $200$
- $4$
- $100$
- $102$

## Explanation

A difference of squares is the difference times the sum. The two numbers differ by $2$ and add to $100$.

$$
(51-49)(51+49)=2\cdot100=200
$$
