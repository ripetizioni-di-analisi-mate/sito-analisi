The squares of two consecutive positive integers differ by $31$. Which is the smaller integer?

- $15$
- $16$
- $30$
- $14$

## Explanation

If the smaller integer is $n$, the difference is $(n+1)^2-n^2=2n+1$. Setting this equal to $31$ gives $n=15$.
