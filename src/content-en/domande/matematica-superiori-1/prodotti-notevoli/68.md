The side length of a square increases from $x$ to $x+5$, where $x>0$. How much does its area increase?

- $25$
- $10 x$
- $x^{2} + 25$
- $10 x + 25$

## Explanation

The increase in area is the final area minus the original area. Expand the square of the new side length. The x-squared term cancels in the difference.

$$
(x+5)^2-x^2=10x+25
$$

The result is $10 x + 25$.
