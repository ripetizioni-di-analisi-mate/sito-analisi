Increasing a square's side length by $2$ cm adds $28$ cm$^2$ of area. What was its original perimeter?

- $6$ cm
- $32$ cm
- $28$ cm
- $24$ cm

## Explanation

If the original side length is $x$, the increase in area is $(x+2)^2-x^2=4x+4$. Setting it equal to $28$ gives $4x=24$, which is already the required perimeter. The original side is $6$ cm and the new one $8$ cm. Area and perimeter increases measure different quantities. The two squares have areas of $36$ and $64$ cm$^2$, respectively.
