Which expression shows that the product of two real numbers $a,b$ cannot exceed the mean of $a^2$ and $b^2$?

- $\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{2}-ab=0$
- $\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{2}-ab=\dfrac{(a-b)^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{2}-ab=-\dfrac{(a-b)^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{2}-ab=\dfrac{(a+b)^2}{2}$

## Explanation

Over denominator $2$ the numerator is $a^2-2ab+b^2$. It is a nonnegative square, vanishing exactly when $a=b$.
