A polynomial $P(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ gives $P(0)=9$, $P(1)=16$, and $P(-1)=4$. Which form matches all three values?

- $(x-3)^2$
- $(x+3)^2$
- $(2x+3)^2$
- $9+7x$

## Explanation

The difference $P(1)-P(-1)=12$ equals $2b$, so $b=6$. The mean of those two values is $10=a+c$, and the value at $0$ gives $c=9$. Thus $a=1$, so $P(x)=x^2+6x+9=(x+3)^2$. The line $9+7x$ matches the values at $0$ and $1$ but gives $2$ at $-1$. The inputs to the right and left of zero separate the linear contribution from the other two.
