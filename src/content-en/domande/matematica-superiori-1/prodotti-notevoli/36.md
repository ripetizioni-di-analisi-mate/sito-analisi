When do $-(x-5)^2$ and $(5-x)^2$ take the same value?

- $x=-5$ only
- $x=5$ and $x=-5$
- For every $x$
- $x=5$ only

## Explanation

Since $5-x=-(x-5)$, the second square is $(x-5)^2$. The outside minus in the first expression gives its opposite. Two opposite numbers are equal only when they are both zero, so $x-5=0$ is required. At $x=0$, for example, the two values are $-25$ and $25$. Each coefficient changes sign between the two polynomials, including the coefficient of $x^2$.
