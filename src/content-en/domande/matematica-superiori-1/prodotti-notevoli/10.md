Calculate $111\cdot89$ using the mean of the two factors as their midpoint. Use their common distance from it to rewrite the product as a difference of squares.

- $9900$
- $9879$
- $10121$
- $9978$

## Explanation

Both factors are $11$ from their mean of $100$. Thus $(100+11)(100-11)=100^2-11^2=10000-121=9879$.
