Doubling both variables of $(a+b)^3$ multiplies the result by $8$. Which proposed term on its own fails this scaling test?

- $a^3$
- $3ab$
- $3a^2b$
- $3ab^2$

## Explanation

In a term of total degree three, three variable factors double, giving an overall factor of $2^3=8$. The term $3ab$ has degree two and is multiplied by only $4$.
