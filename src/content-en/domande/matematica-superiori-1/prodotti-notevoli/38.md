How is the coefficient of $x$ formed in the expansion of $(3x+4)^3$?

- $3\cdot 3^2\cdot 4=108$
- $3\cdot 3\cdot 4^2=144$
- $3\cdot 4^2=48$
- $3^3=27$

## Explanation

To form a term in $x$, take $3x$ from just one bracket and $4$ from each of the other two. The three possible positions of $3x$ give three products equal to $48x$. Choosing two factors of $3x$ instead gives degree two and contributes to the $x^2$ term. The outer factor of $3$ counts the choices and the inner one is the coefficient of the chosen term.
