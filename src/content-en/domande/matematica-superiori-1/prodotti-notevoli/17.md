Which bound on $(3x-4)^2$ is always true for $x\ge0$?

- $(3x-4)^2\le9x^2+16$
- $(3x-4)^2<9x^2+16$
- $(3x-4)^2\ge9x^2+16$
- $(3x-4)^2=9x^2+16$

## Explanation

The expansion is $9x^2-24x+16$. For $x\ge0$ the mixed term $-24x$ is nonpositive, so the square never exceeds $9x^2+16$. The expressions agree at $x=0$, so the bound has to allow equality. At every $x>0$ the mixed term is strictly negative. For $x<0$, the situation changes because $-24x$ becomes positive.
