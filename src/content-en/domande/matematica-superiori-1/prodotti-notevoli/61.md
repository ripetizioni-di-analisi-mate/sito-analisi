What midpoint of the two factors lets you write $7.5\cdot12.5$ as a difference of squares?

- $10$
- $2.5$
- $7.5$
- $12.5$

## Explanation

The mean is $\dfrac{7.5+12.5}{2}=10$. The common distance from that midpoint is $2.5$, half the difference between the factors. The product therefore becomes $10^2-2.5^2=93.75$. Choosing the midpoint is what makes the two mixed terms opposites in the expansion. Check the choice by reconstructing the factors, $10-2.5$ and $10+2.5$.
