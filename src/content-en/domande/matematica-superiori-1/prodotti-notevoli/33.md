For $P(x)=ax^2+bx+c$, you measure $P(1)=12$ and $P(-1)=4$. Which coefficient can you determine from those two values alone?

- $b=4$
- $a=4$
- $c=8$
- $b=8$

## Explanation

In the two evaluations $a$ and $c$ enter with the same sign, while the contribution of $b$ changes sign. Subtracting gives $2b=12-4=8$, so $b=4$. Adding gives only $a+c=8$. These two values leave the individual values of $a$ and $c$ undetermined. An additional value at $x=0$ would determine the constant and then the quadratic coefficient.
