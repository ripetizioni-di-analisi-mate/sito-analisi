What coefficient replaces $c$ so that $x^2+cx+25$ equals $(x+5)^2$ for every $x$?

- $10$
- $5$
- $25$
- $50$

## Explanation

Expand the square of the binomial. The linear coefficient is twice the number added to x. For an identity, the corresponding coefficients in the two polynomials must agree.

$$
(x+5)^2=x^2+10x+25,\qquad c=10
$$

The result is $10$.
