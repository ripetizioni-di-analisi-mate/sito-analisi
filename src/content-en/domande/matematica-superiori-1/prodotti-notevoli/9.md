To compute $21^2$ as $(10+11)^2$, a diagram shows two squares of areas $100$ and $121$. What is the combined area of the two missing pieces?

- $220$
- $110$
- $21$
- $441$

## Explanation

The missing pieces are two rectangles with sides $10$ and $11$. Together they cover $220$ square units and complete the sum $100+220+121=441$.
