Expand $(x-6)^2$.

- $x^{2} - 12 x + 36$
- $x^{2} - 36$
- $x^{2} + 12 x + 36$
- $x^{2} - 6 x + 36$

## Explanation

In the square of a difference, the doubled product is negative. The squares of the two terms remain positive. The constant term is therefore the square of the number subtracted.

$$
(x-6)^2=x^2-12x+36
$$

The result is $x^{2} - 12 x + 36$.
