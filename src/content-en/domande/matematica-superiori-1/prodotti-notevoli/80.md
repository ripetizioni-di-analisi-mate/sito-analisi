Without expanding $(2x-3)^2(x+1)$, what is the **sum of all its coefficients**?

- $0$
- $9$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

In the expanded polynomial, substituting $x=1$ makes every power equal to $1$. Substituting directly in the product gives $(2-3)^2(1+1)=2$.
