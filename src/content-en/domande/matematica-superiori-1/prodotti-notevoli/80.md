What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in $(5x-1)(5x+1)-(3x)^2$?

- $25$
- $34$
- $22$
- $16$

## Explanation

The product of the conjugate binomials gives $25x^2-1$. Subtract the square $9x^2$ and combine the two quadratic contributions.

$$
25x^2-1-9x^2=16x^2-1
$$
