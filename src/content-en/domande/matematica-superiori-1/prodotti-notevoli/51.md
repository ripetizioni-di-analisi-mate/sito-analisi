For every $x$, what is $(3-x)^2-(x-3)^2$?

- $12 x$
- $x^{2} - 9$
- $0$
- $18 - 6 x$

## Explanation

The two binomials are additive inverses. A number and its opposite have the same square, so the difference is zero.

$$
(3-x)^2=\bigl(-(x-3)\bigr)^2=(x-3)^2
$$
