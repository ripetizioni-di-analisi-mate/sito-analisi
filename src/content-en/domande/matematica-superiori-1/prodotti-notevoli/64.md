A square with side length $x>0$ has its side length increased by $4$ units. Which increase in area is possible?

- $16$
- $15$
- $8$
- $24$

## Explanation

The increase is $(x+4)^2-x^2=8x+16$. Since $x>0$, it is strictly greater than $16$. The value $16$ would occur only at $x=0$, outside the assumptions on lengths. The increase $24$ corresponds to $x=1$, where the new square has area $25$ and the original area was $1$. Increasing the original side length by one raises the area gain by another eight square units.
