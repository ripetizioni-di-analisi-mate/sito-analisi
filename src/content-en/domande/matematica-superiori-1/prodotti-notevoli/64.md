A square of side $s$ is measured with a positive error $e$. If the area increase is estimated using only $2se$, how much area is missing?

- $se$
- $2e^2$
- $s^2$
- $e^2$

## Explanation

The exact increase is $(s+e)^2-s^2=2se+e^2$. The estimate counts both strips but misses the corner square with side $e$.
