Which value of $x$ checks the sign of the coefficient of $x$ in $(2x-7)^2$ most directly by making the binomial zero?

- $-\dfrac72$
- $0$
- $7$
- $\dfrac72$

## Explanation

The binomial is zero when $2x=7$, so take $x=\dfrac72$. At that point the two separate squares both equal $49$. Twice the product must therefore equal $-98$, as it does with $-28x$. Taking $x=0$ would check only the constant, because it makes the mixed term zero. The check uses a zero of the factored form.
