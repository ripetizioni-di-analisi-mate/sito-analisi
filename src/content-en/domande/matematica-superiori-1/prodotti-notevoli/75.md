A rectangle has sides $x+5$ and $x-5$. Which value of $x$ makes $(x+5)(x-5)=x^2-25$ the area of a rectangle with both sides positive?

- $-8$
- $5$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

The two side lengths must satisfy $x+5>0$ and $x-5>0$. The second condition requires $x>5$ and also ensures the first. At $x=-8$ the product is positive, but both factors are negative and so cannot represent lengths. At $x=5$ one side has length zero. At $x=8$ the side lengths are $13$ and $3$, giving area $39$.
