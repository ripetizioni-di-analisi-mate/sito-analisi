Calculate $(x+3)(x-3)$.

- $x^{2} + 9$
- $x^{2} - 6 x + 9$
- $x^{2} - 9$
- $x^{2} + 6 x - 9$

## Explanation

Multiplying a sum by the corresponding difference gives a difference of squares. The two mixed products have opposite signs and cancel. The square of the variable and the negative of the square of the number remain.

$$
(x+3)(x-3)=x^2-9
$$

The result is $x^{2} - 9$.
