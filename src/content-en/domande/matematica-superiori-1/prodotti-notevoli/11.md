Which cross terms occur in $(x+y+11)^2$ in addition to the three individual squares?

- $xy+22x+22y$
- $22xy$
- $2xy+22x+22y$
- $2xy+11x+11y$

## Explanation

The three distinct pairs are $(x,y)$, $(x,11)$ and $(y,11)$. Each pair appears twice in distribution and supplies one of the three terms.
