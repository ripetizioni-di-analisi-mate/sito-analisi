Which number completes $x^2-5x+c$ as the square of a binomial?

- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $\dfrac52$
- $25$

## Explanation

Writing the square as $(x+b)^2$ gives the term $2bx$ in $x$. Therefore $b=-\dfrac52$. The required constant is the square of the entire fraction, $\dfrac{25}{4}$. The negative sign of $b$ appears in the mixed term, while its square is positive. Adding just $\dfrac52$ misses the step of squaring the term you found.
