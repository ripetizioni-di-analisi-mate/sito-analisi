What is the smallest positive integer by which to multiply the entire square $\left(\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac13\right)^2$ to obtain integer coefficients?

- $18$
- $36$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The coefficients are $\dfrac14,\dfrac13,\dfrac19$. Their denominators have least common multiple $36$, which transforms the square into $(3x+2)^2$.
