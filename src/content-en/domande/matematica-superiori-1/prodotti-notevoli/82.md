Expand $\left(\dfrac x2+4\right)^2$.

- $\dfrac{x^{2}}{2} + 4 x + 16$
- $\dfrac{x^{2}}{4} + 4 x + 16$
- $\dfrac{x^{2}}{4} + 8 x + 16$
- $\dfrac{x^{2}}{4} + 16$

## Explanation

The square of x divided by two has coefficient one quarter. In the doubled product the factor of two cancels its denominator. The linear coefficient is the second term of the binomial.

$$
\left(\frac x2+4\right)^2=\frac{x^2}4+4x+16
$$

The result is $\dfrac{x^{2}}{4} + 4 x + 16$.
