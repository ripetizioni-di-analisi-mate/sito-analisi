A square's side length increases from $x$ to $x+11$, with $x>0$. Find the area increase by counting the two added strips and the new corner square.

- $121$
- $22x+121$
- $11x+121$
- $22x$

## Explanation

The strips along two sides of the old square have combined area $2\cdot11x$. The corner square adds $11^2=121$ square units.
