Which condition on $u,v\in\mathbb R$ is equivalent to $u^2=v^2$?

- Only $u=v$
- Only $u=-v$
- $u=v$ or $u=-v$
- $u,v\ge0$

## Explanation

Moving the squares to one side gives $(u-v)(u+v)=0$. A product of real numbers is zero when at least one factor is zero, so $u=v$ or $u=-v$. Each condition implies equal squares. The pair $u=2$, $v=-2$ illustrates the case with different signs. Both possibilities occur at once only when $u=v=0$.
