A student writes $(x+6)^2=x^2+36$. Which term is missing on the right-hand side?

- $12 x$
- $6 x$
- $36$
- $x^{2}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the binomial by itself gives two equal mixed products. The proposed expression contains only the squares, so the sum of the mixed products is missing.

$$
x\cdot6+6\cdot x=12x
$$

The result is $12 x$.
