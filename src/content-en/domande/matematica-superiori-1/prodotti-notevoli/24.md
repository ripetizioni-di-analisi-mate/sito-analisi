For which $b$ can the polynomial $x^2-14x+51$ be written as $(x-b)^2+2$?

- $-7$
- $14$
- $49$
- $7$

## Explanation

The expanded square has mixed term $-2bx$. Matching $-14x$ forces $b=7$. The square then contributes $49$ to the constant, and adding $2$ gives the required $51$. With $b=-7$ the constant would still be correct, but the linear coefficient would become positive. The polynomial's smallest value is $2$, reached at $x=b$.
