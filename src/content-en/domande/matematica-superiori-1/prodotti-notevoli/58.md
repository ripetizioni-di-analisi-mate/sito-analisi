Let $A=(x+b)^2$ and $B=(x-b)^2$. If $A+B=20$ and $A-B=-12$, what is the ordered pair $(A,B)$?

- $(16,4)$
- $(4,16)$
- $(8,32)$
- $(10,10)$

## Explanation

Adding the two equalities gives $2A=20-12=8$, hence $A=4$. The sum then gives $B=16$. The negative sign of $A-B$ tells you that $A$ must be the smaller of the two squares. For example, $x=3$ and $b=-1$ produce $2^2$ and $4^2$. The value $16$ is the square of the second base. In this example that base is $4$.
