A square with side length $10$ cm has an outer border $2$ cm wide along each of its four sides. What is the area of the border?

- $96$ cm$^2$
- $44$ cm$^2$
- $80$ cm$^2$
- $84$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

A horizontal measurement crosses the border at both the left and the right, giving an outer side length of $10+2+2=14$ cm. The area between the squares is $14^2-10^2=(14-10)(14+10)=96$ cm$^2$. Counting four strips of $20$ cm$^2$ and four corners of $4$ cm$^2$ gives the same result. This decomposition includes the corners, where the strips along the sides end.
