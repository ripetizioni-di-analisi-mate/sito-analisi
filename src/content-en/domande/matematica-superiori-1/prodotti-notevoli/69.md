The side length of a square increases from $x$ to $x+6$, where $x>0$. How much does its area increase?

- $12 x + 36$
- $36$
- $12 x$
- $x^{2} + 36$

## Explanation

The increase in area is the final area minus the original area. Expand the square of the new side length. The x-squared term cancels in the difference.

$$
(x+6)^2-x^2=12x+36
$$

The result is $12 x + 36$.
