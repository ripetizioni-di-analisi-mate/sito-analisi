To calculate $1001^2-999^2$ without expanding the two squares, which product gives the result directly?

- $1\cdot1000$
- $2\cdot1000$
- $2\cdot2$
- $2\cdot2000$

## Explanation

The two bases add to $2000$ and differ by $2$. The difference of squares is the product of these two quantities, so it is $2\cdot2000=4000$. Their common midpoint $1000$ is half their sum, while each number's offset from the midpoint is half of $2$.
