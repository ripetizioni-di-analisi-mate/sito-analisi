Let $N$ be an odd integer. What remainder does $(N+2)^2-N^2$ always leave on division by $8$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $0$
- $6$

## Explanation

The difference of squares is $(2)(2N+2)=4(N+1)$. Since $N+1$ is even, the product is a multiple of $8$.
