For what value of $c$ is $9x^2+cx+4=(3x-2)^2$ true for every $x$?

- $6$
- $12$
- $-12$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The quadratic and constant terms already match. The doubled product gives the linear coefficient, which must equal $c$.

$$
2(3x)(-2)=-12x
$$
