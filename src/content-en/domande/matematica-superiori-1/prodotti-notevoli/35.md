Let $N$ be an odd integer. What remainder is obtained when $(N+2)^2-N^2$ is divided by $8$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $0$
- $6$

## Explanation

The expression is a difference of squares, so it factorises as the product of the difference and the sum of the bases.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(N+2)^2-N^2&=\bigl((N+2)-N\bigr)\bigl((N+2)+N\bigr)\\[6pt]
&=2(2N+2)=4(N+1)
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $N$ is odd, the number $N+1$ is even, that is $N+1=2k$ with $k$ an integer. Therefore $(N+2)^2-N^2=4\cdot2k=8k$ is a multiple of $8$, and the remainder on division by $8$ is $0$. The answer $4$ comes from stopping at $4(N+1)$ without using the hypothesis that $N$ is odd.
