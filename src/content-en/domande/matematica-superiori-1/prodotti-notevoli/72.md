Without fully expanding, what is the highest-degree term of $(1-2t^3)^2$?

- $-4t^6$
- $2t^6$
- $4t^3$
- $4t^6$

## Explanation

The highest-degree term comes from taking $-2t^3$ from both factors. Its coefficient is squared and its exponents are added.
