Which terms in the expansion of $(x-11)^3$ have negative coefficients?

- Only the constant term
- The quadratic and constant terms
- The linear and constant terms
- Every term except $x^3$

## Explanation

The sign is negative when an odd number of factors $-11$ is chosen. One such factor leaves $x^2$, and three leave no factor of $x$.
