The polynomial $25x^2+40x+c$ is the square of a binomial. What is $c$?

- $4$
- $1600$
- $16$
- $400$

## Explanation

The first term lets us take $5x$ as the variable part of the binomial. If its constant is $b$, twice the product is $2\cdot5xb=10bx$. Matching $40x$ gives $b=4$. The constant of the square is therefore $b^2=16$. Choosing $-5x$ instead reverses both signs in the binomial. The square is thus $(5x+4)^2$, and its mixed term can be reconstructed immediately.
