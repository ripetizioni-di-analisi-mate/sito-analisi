Why does $(x+11)(x-11)$ have only a quadratic term and a constant term?

- Both factors are positive
- The squares have opposite signs for every $x$
- The cross products are $11x$ and $-11x$
- The two constant terms are equal

## Explanation

The linear terms cancel because they are opposites. This leaves $x\cdot x$ and $11\cdot(-11)$, giving $x^2-121$.
