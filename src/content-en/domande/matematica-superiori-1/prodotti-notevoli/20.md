Expand $(2x-3y)^2$ and find the coefficient of $xy$.

- $12$
- $-6$
- $-9$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The first and last terms contain only $x^2$ and $y^2$. The mixed term comes from multiplying the first term by the second and doubling the product.

$$
2(2x)(-3y)=-12xy
$$
