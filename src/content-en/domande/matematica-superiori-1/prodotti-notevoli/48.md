Four rectangles have a perimeter of $48$ cm. Which pair of sides, in cm, gives the largest area?

- $10$ and $14$
- $9$ and $15$
- $8$ and $16$
- $11$ and $13$

## Explanation

The two sides always add to $24$ and have midpoint $12$. Their area is $144-d^2$, where $d$ is the distance from the midpoint. The smallest offset among the choices is $1$, giving $143$ cm$^2$. An offset of $2$ already drops the area to $140$ cm$^2$. If the square is also allowed, a zero offset gives an even larger area.
