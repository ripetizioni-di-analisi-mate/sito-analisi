A cube of side $a>0$ is enlarged to side $a+h$, where $h>0$. Which part of the volume increase comes from the three pieces with dimensions $a,h,h$?

- $h^3$
- $3a^2h^2$
- $3ah^2$
- $3a^2h$

## Explanation

Each specified piece has volume $ah^2$, and there are three of them. In $(a+h)^3-a^3$, the other contributions are $3a^2h$ and $h^3$.
