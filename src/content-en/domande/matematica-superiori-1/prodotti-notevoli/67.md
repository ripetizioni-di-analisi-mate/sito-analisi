The side length of a square increases from $x$ to $x+4$, where $x>0$. How much does its area increase?

- $16$
- $8 x$
- $8 x + 16$
- $x^{2} + 16$

## Explanation

The increase in area is the final area minus the original area. Expand the square of the new side length. The x-squared term cancels in the difference.

$$
(x+4)^2-x^2=8x+16
$$

The result is $8 x + 16$.
