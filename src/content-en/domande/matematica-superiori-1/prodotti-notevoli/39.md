Which constant term completes $x^2-12x+\square=(x-6)^2$?

- $-36$
- $12$
- $36$
- $6$

## Explanation

The linear term already equals the negative doubled product. The square of the second term of the binomial is missing. That square is always nonnegative.

$$
(x-6)^2=x^2-12x+36
$$

The result is $36$.
