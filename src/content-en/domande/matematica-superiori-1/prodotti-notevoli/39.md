Which terms remain after simplifying $(x+2)^3-(x-2)^3$?

- $x^3$ and $x$
- Only $x^2$
- $x^2$ and the constant
- Only the constant

## Explanation

The terms $x^3$ and $12x$ have the same coefficients in both expansions and cancel in the subtraction. The $x^2$ terms are $6x^2$ and $-6x^2$, and the constants are $8$ and $-8$. Subtracting gives $12x^2+16$, which equals $16$ at $x=0$. The result is an even function because it depends on $x$ only through its square.
