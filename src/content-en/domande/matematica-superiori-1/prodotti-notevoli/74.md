In the expansion of $(x+2y-3)^2$, set $y=3$. What is the resulting coefficient of $x$?

- $12$
- $6$
- $-6$
- $3$

## Explanation

Before substitution, the terms with a single $x$ are $4xy$ and $-6x$. At $y=3$ their sum is $12x-6x=6x$. The same coefficient comes from substituting $y=3$ into the trinomial first, reducing it to $x+3$. The mixed term in that binomial's square is $6x$. The coefficient depends on $y$ until the second variable is fixed.
