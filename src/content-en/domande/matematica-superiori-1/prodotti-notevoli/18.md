Which $p$ makes $9x^2+px+16$ a square of a binomial that vanishes at a positive $x$?

- $24$
- $-24$
- $-12$
- $0$

## Explanation

The outside terms require a binomial $3x+4$ or $3x-4$, up to reversing both signs. The first vanishes at $-\dfrac43$ and the second at $\dfrac43$. The condition therefore selects the second binomial. Its mixed term is $-24x$, which fixes $p=-24$. The constant term alone allows both signs for the term inside the brackets.
