Two squares have areas $(x+5)^2$ and $(x-5)^2$. For which $x$ is the first area $160$ larger than the second?

- $-8$
- $8$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

Apply the difference of squares to the two binomials. Their difference is $10$ and their sum is $2x$, so the gap is $20x$. The equality $20x=160$ gives $x=8$. The areas then equal $169$ and $9$. At $x=-8$ the areas trade places. Since $20$ is positive, the gap increases as $x$ increases.
