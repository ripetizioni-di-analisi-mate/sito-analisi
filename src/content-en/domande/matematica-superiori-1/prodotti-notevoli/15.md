The product $(39+u)(39-u)$ is $1505$. What is the value of $u>0$?

- $8$
- $16$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Both factors share the term $39$ and contain opposite terms $u$ and $-u$. The difference of squares therefore gives $u^2$. Take the positive root.

$$
39^2-u^2=1505\quad\Longrightarrow\quad u^2=1521-1505=16
$$
