Which polynomial can be the expansion of $(x-11)^2$, given that it must vanish at $x=11$?

- $x^2+121$
- $x^2-22x+121$
- $x^2+22x+121$
- $x^2-11x+121$

## Explanation

At $11$ the polynomial $x^2-22x+121$ gives $121-242+121=0$. The coefficient $-22$ comes from twice the product of $x$ and $-11$.
