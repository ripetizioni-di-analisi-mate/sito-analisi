Simplify $(u+v)^2+(u-v)^2-2u^2$.

- $2v^2$
- $4uv$
- $0$
- $2u^2$

## Explanation

The doubled products in the two squares are opposites. They add to zero, leaving $2u^2$ and $2v^2$ from the two squares. Finally subtract $2u^2$.

$$
(u+v)^2+(u-v)^2-2u^2=2u^2+2v^2-2u^2
$$
