If $a$ and $b$ are nonzero reciprocals, what is $(a+b)^2-a^2-b^2$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $a+b$

## Explanation

The expansion of $(a+b)^2$ contains both squares and the mixed term $2ab$. Subtracting the squares leaves just that term. Since $ab=1$, its value is $2$ even when both numbers are negative. For example, $a=2$ and $b=\dfrac12$ give $2\cdot2\cdot\dfrac12=2$. The result is constant because the product of the two numbers is fixed.
