For which $x$ does $\left(\dfrac{x}{2}+3\right)^2<\left(\dfrac{x}{2}-1\right)^2$ hold?

- $x<0$
- $x>-2$
- $x<-6$
- $x<-2$

## Explanation

The binomials differ by $4$ and add to $x+2$. The difference of their squares is therefore $4(x+2)$, negative for $x<-2$. The offsets from $\dfrac{x}{2}$ are not opposites, so the squares agree at $x=-2$, where the bases are $2$ and $-2$. The equality case is excluded because the comparison is strict.
