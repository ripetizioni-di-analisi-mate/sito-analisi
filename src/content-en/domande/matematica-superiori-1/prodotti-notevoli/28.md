Which coefficient does substituting $x=0$ check in the proposed identity $(5x-2)^2=25x^2-20x-4$?

- The coefficient of $x$
- The coefficient of $x^2$
- All the coefficients
- The constant

## Explanation

At $x=0$ the terms in $x$ and $x^2$ both vanish. The square on the left gives $(-2)^2=4$, while the right side gives $-4$. The substitution identifies an error in the constant term. Checking the variable terms requires other inputs or a direct comparison with the expansion. The same check gives the point where the graph meets the vertical axis.
