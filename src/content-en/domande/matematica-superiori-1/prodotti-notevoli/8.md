Suppose $(x-4)^2=x^2-8x+k$ for every real $x$. Which substitution isolates $k$ immediately?

- $x=4$
- $x=-4$
- $x=1$
- $x=0$

## Explanation

At $x=0$ both terms containing $x$ vanish, so only $k$ remains on the right. The left side gives $(-4)^2=16$. Other inputs would recover the same result after calculating both variable terms. For example, $x=4$ gives $0=-16+k$. The method applies to the constant term of any polynomial in one variable.
