The trinomial $x^2-22x+c$ must have a double zero at $11$. Which constant is needed?

- $-121$
- $22$
- $44$
- $121$

## Explanation

The square $(x-11)^2$ gives the two terms already present. Its constant term is $(-11)^2=121$, and the squared form confirms the double zero.
