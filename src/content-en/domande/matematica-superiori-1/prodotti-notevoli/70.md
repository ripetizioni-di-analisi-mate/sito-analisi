A square of side length $x>2$ is reduced to side length $x-2$. By how much does its area decrease?

- $4 x + 4$
- $4 x - 4$
- $2 x - 4$
- $4$

## Explanation

The decrease is the original area minus the final area. Subtract the entire expansion of the second square, including its constant term $4$.

$$
x^2-(x-2)^2=x^2-(x^2-4x+4)=4x-4
$$
