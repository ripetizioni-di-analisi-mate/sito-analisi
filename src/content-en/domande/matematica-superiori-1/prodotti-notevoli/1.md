When expanding $(x+11)^2$, which two terms from distribution combine to give the linear term?

- $11x+11x$
- $x^2+11x$
- $11x+121$
- $11+11$

## Explanation

Write the square as $(x+11)(x+11)$. A linear term comes from choosing $x$ in the first factor and $11$ in the second or vice versa, giving a total of $22x$.
