What is the expansion of $(x+3)^2$?

- $x^{2} + 6 x + 9$
- $x^{2} + 9$
- $x^{2} + 3 x + 9$
- $x^{2} + 6 x - 9$

## Explanation

The square of a sum contains the square of each term and twice their product. The doubled product is positive here. Include all three terms.

$$
(x+3)^2=x^2+2\cdot3x+9=x^{2} + 6 x + 9
$$

The result is $x^{2} + 6 x + 9$.
