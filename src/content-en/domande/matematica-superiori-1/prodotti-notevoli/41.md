Calculate $13^2$ using $13=10+3$.

- $169$
- $109$
- $139$
- $115$

## Explanation

Write the number as the sum of ten and the other addend. The square also includes twice the product of the two addends. Add the three numerical contributions.

$$
(10+3)^2=100+60+9=169
$$

The result is $169$.
