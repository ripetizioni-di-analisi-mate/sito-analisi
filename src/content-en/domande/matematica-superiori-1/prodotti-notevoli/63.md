What is the minimum of $(x+6)^2+(x-6)^2$ over all real $x$?

- $0$
- $36$
- $72$
- $144$

## Explanation

The sum of the squares is $2x^2+72$, since the mixed terms have opposite signs. The first term is always nonnegative and becomes zero at $x=0$, where both squares equal $36$. Making one of the squares zero, for example at $x=6$, leaves the other square equal to $144$. The minimum is therefore where the two squares are equal.
