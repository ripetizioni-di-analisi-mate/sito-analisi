In $(4x-3y)^2$ the mixed term is $-24xy$. When is it strictly positive?

- $x$ and $y$ have the same sign and are nonzero
- It is enough that $x<0$
- $x$ and $y$ have opposite signs
- For every $x,y$

## Explanation

The coefficient of the mixed term is fixed and negative. The value of the term depends on the product $xy$. Multiplying $-24xy>0$ by $-\dfrac1{24}$ gives $xy<0$, possible only with opposite signs. If either variable is zero, the mixed term vanishes. For instance, $x=-1$ and $y=2$ give a positive contribution of $48$.
