In the expansion of $(a-2b+3)^2$, which pair of terms produces the term in $b$ without $a$?

- $-2b$ and $3$
- $a$ and $-2b$
- $a$ and $3$
- $-2b$ and $-2b$

## Explanation

The linear term in $b$ comes from pairing the part in $b$ with the constant. Twice the product is $2\cdot(-2b)\cdot 3=-12b$. Pairing with $a$ would yield a term in $ab$, while multiplying two factors of $-2b$ would give the square $4b^2$. The constant $3$ also contributes to the term in $a$ because it pairs with both variables.
