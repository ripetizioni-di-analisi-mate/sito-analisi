To calculate $34^2$, write $34=3\cdot10+4$. After carrying from the units, how many **hundreds** are carried from the tens column?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The units give $4^2=16$, so one ten is carried. The tens column now has $2\cdot3\cdot4+1=25$ tens. Five stay and two hundreds are added to the original nine hundreds, giving $1156$.
