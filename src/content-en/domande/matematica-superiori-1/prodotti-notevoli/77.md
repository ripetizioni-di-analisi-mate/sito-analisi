Which expression remains after the intermediate terms cancel in $(x-1)(x+1)(x^2+1)$?

- $x^4-1$
- $x^4+1$
- $x^3-1$
- $(x^2-1)^2$

## Explanation

The first two factors give $x^2-1$. Multiplying by $x^2+1$ applies the difference-of-squares identity a second time, with base $x^2$.
