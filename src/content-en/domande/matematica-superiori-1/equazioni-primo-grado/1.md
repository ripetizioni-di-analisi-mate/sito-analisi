On balanced scales, three identical packets plus $11$ g weigh $44$ g. What mass does $3x+11=44$ assign to one packet?

- $11\,\mathrm g$
- $33\,\mathrm g$
- $\dfrac{44}{3}\,\mathrm g$
- $55\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

After removing $11$ g, the three packets together weigh $33$ g. Dividing by three gives $11$ g per packet and reproduces the initial total.
