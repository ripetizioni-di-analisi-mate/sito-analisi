Solve the proportion $x:4=3:2$.

- $6$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $24$

## Explanation

Set the product of the inner terms equal to the product of the outer terms. The resulting coefficient of the unknown is two. Divide by two and check the required ratio.

$$
2x=4\cdot3,\qquad x=6
$$

The result is $6$.
