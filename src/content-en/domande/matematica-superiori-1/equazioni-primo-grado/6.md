What do you get after subtracting $2x$ from both sides of $2(x+11)=2x+23$?

- $22=22$
- $22=23$
- $11=23$
- $22x=23x$

## Explanation

After distribution the left-hand side is $2x+22$. Removing the identical terms leaves a false statement, so no real number satisfies the equation.
