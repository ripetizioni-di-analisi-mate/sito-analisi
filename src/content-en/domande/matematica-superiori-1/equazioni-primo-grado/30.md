For which value of $a$ does $a(x-2)=3x-5$ have no solution?

- $0$
- $3$
- $2$
- $\dfrac52$

## Explanation

The simplified form is $(a-3)x=2a-5$. The equation can have no solution only when the coefficient of the unknown is zero. For $a=3$, the right-hand side is $1$, so the equation becomes $0x=1$.
