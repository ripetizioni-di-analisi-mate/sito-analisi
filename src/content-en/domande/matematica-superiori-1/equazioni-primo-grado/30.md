The equation $F(t)=G(t)$ has the unique solution $t=5$. Which number solves $F(x+2)=G(x+2)$ without knowing either formula?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The two sides receive the same input $t=x+2$. To give matching outputs that input must be $5$, so $x+2=5$ and $x=3$.
