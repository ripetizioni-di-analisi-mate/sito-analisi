The equation $2x=1$ is solved first in $\mathbb Z$ and then in $\mathbb Q$. How many solutions does it have in those sets, in that order?

- $(1,0)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(0,1)$

## Explanation

The only possible solution is $\dfrac12$. It belongs to the rationals but not to the integers, so the domain changes the count.
