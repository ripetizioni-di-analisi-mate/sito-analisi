Which value makes the numerator of $\dfrac{x-4}{x-4}$ zero without solving $\dfrac{x-4}{x-4}=0$?

- $0$
- $-4$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The numerator is zero at $x=4$. The denominator has the same factor and is zero at $4$ as well, so the candidate is outside the domain. At every other value, the ratio equals $1$.
