Plan A costs $4$ euros plus $3$ euros per activity. Plan B costs $10$ euros plus $1$ euro per activity. For how many activities do they cost the same?

- $2$
- $5$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

For $n$ activities the costs are $4+3n$ and $10+n$. Equating them gives $2n=6$, hence $n=3$. The common cost is $4+3\cdot3=10+3=13$ euros. Alternatively, A starts six euros cheaper and uses up that advantage by costing two euros more per activity. So it takes three activities for the gap to disappear.
