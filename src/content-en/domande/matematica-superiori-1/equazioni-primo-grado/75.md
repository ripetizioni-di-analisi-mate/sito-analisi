A tank contains two litres of water for each litre of syrup. Adding $3$ litres of water alone changes the water-to-syrup ratio to $3:1$. How much syrup was in the tank?

- $2\,\mathrm L$
- $6\,\mathrm L$
- $3\,\mathrm L$
- $1\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

If there are $s$ litres of syrup, the water increases from $2s$ to $2s+3$ litres. The new ratio requires $2s+3=3s$, giving $s=3$.
