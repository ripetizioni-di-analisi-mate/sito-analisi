The proposed answer to $\dfrac{x+1}{6}-\dfrac{x-2}{4}=3$ is $x=-28$. Without solving again, which values of the two terms on the left verify it?

- $-\dfrac92,\ -\dfrac{15}2$
- $\dfrac92,\ \dfrac{15}2$
- $-\dfrac92,\ \dfrac{15}2$
- $\dfrac92,\ -\dfrac{15}2$

## Explanation

At $x=-28$ the numerators are $-27$ and $-30$. Subtracting gives $-\dfrac92-(-\dfrac{15}2)=3$, matching the right-hand side.
