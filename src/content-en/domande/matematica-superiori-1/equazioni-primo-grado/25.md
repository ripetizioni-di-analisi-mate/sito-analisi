Which equation results from multiplying both sides of $\dfrac{x+1}{6}-\dfrac{x-2}{4}=3$ by $12$?

- $2(x+1)-3(x-2)=36$
- $2(x+1)-3(x-2)=3$
- $3(x+1)-2(x-2)=36$
- $2(x+1)+3(x-2)=36$

## Explanation

Dividing $12$ by $6$ gives $2$, while dividing $12$ by $4$ gives $3$. The minus sign still applies to the whole second term. The right-hand side is also multiplied by $12$, giving $36$.
