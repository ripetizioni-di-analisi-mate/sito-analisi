Today Alice is $10$ and Bruno is $18$. In how many years will their age difference be $10$ years?

- It will not happen
- In $2$ years
- In $8$ years
- In $10$ years

## Explanation

After $t$ years the ages will be $10+t$ and $18+t$. Subtracting gives $(18+t)-(10+t)=8$ for every $t$. The required equation would therefore give $8=10$, which is impossible. The passage of time adds the same amount to both ages. Their ratio can change, while the difference in years stays constant.
