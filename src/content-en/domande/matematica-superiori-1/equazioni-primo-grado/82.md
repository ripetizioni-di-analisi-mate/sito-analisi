The linear expression $F(x)=2x-5$ goes from $-1$ to $3$ as $x$ goes from $2$ to $4$. At which intermediate input is $F(x)=1$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac72$

## Explanation

The value $1$ is halfway between $-1$ and $3$. With a constant rate of change, the input is halfway as well, namely $x=3$, and $2\cdot3-5=1$.
