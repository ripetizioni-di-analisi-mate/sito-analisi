Solve $-2x+4=12$.

- $4$
- $-4$
- $-8$
- $8$

## Explanation

Subtract $4$ from both sides. The coefficient of the variable is negative, and both sides are divided by that coefficient. Substituting the result gives the required value on the right-hand side.

$$
-2x=8,\qquad x=-4
$$

The result is $-4$.
