The mean of three numbers is $5$. The first two are $3$ and $4$. What is the third?

- $5$
- $15$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

The sum of the three numbers is three times their mean. Subtracting the two known numbers from that sum leaves the third number.

$$
3+4+x=3\cdot5,\qquad x=8
$$

The result is $8$.
