Two linear sides $F(x)$ and $G(x)$ satisfy $F(x)-G(x)=4(x-3)$. How do their values compare at $x=4$?

- $G(4)$ is $4$ greater than $F(4)$
- The sides have equal values
- $F(4)$ is $16$ greater than $G(4)$
- $F(4)$ is $4$ greater than $G(4)$

## Explanation

The difference is enough to compare the values. Substituting $4$ gives $F(4)-G(4)=4(4-3)=4$. The positive sign makes the first value greater, and $4$ measures the gap. The sides are equal where their difference vanishes, which occurs at $x=3$.
