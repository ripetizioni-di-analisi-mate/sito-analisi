Three marks average $13$. Relative to $13$, the first two marks of $11$ and $12$ have deviations $-2$ and $-1$. What deviation must the third mark have?

- $-3$
- $+1$
- $+2$
- $+3$

## Explanation

The deviations from the mean sum to zero. The third must offset $-3$ with $+3$, making the mark $16$.
