Solve $5x-4=2x+8$.

- $-4$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{12}{7}$
- $4$

## Explanation

Subtract twice x from both sides. Then add $4$ to both sides. Both the coefficient of the unknown and the constant term are multiples of three.

$$
5x-2x=8+4,\qquad3x=12,\qquad x=4
$$

The result is $4$.
