Solve $-2x+3=9$.

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-6$
- $6$

## Explanation

Subtract $3$ from both sides. The coefficient of the variable is negative, and both sides are divided by that coefficient. Substituting the result gives the required value on the right-hand side.

$$
-2x=6,\qquad x=-3
$$

The result is $-3$.
