What is the real solution set of $3(x-6)=3x-18$?

- $\varnothing$
- $\mathbb R$
- $6$
- $0$

## Explanation

The distributive property makes the two sides identical. Simplifying gives zero equals zero. The equality therefore holds for every real number.

The result is $\mathbb R$.
