In three years Luca will be twice as old as he was five years ago. How old is he today?

- $8$
- $11$
- $16$
- $13$

## Explanation

If $x$ is his age today, his future age is $x+3$ and his past age was $x-5$. The equation $x+3=2(x-5)$ gives $x=13$. Doubling applies to his whole past age: five years ago he was $8$, and in three years he will be $16$. Both ages make sense and the latter is twice the former.
