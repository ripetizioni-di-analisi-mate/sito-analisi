A $36$ cm wire forms a triangle with sides $x,x+2,2x-2$ cm. Which length is greatest after imposing the perimeter?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The side lengths sum to $4x$, so $x=9$. They are $9,11,16$ cm and also satisfy the triangle inequality.
