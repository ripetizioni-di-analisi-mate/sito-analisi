A rectangle has perimeter $34\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its base is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ longer than its height. What is the base length?

- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Half the perimeter is $17\,\mathrm{cm}$. If $h$ is the numerical value of the height in centimeters, the base is $h+5$. Half the perimeter is therefore $2h+5$, giving $h=6$. The base is $11\,\mathrm{cm}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2h+5&=17 \\[6pt]
h&=6 \\[6pt]
h+5&=11
\end{aligned}
$$
