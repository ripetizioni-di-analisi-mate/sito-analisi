What is the solution set of $\dfrac{x-3}{x-3}=0$?

- $3$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{3\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

The domain excludes the value that makes the denominator zero. At every admissible value, the fraction is one. The equation one equals zero has no solutions.

The result is $\varnothing$.
