A calculator prints $x\approx0.33$ for $3x=1$. Using $0.33$ in place of the solution, what exact error do you get on the left-hand side?

- It exceeds the target by $0.01$
- It falls short by $0.03$
- The error is zero
- It falls short by $0.01$

## Explanation

The product $3\cdot0.33$ equals $0.99$. It falls one hundredth short of the required value $1$, whereas the exact solution is $\dfrac13$.
