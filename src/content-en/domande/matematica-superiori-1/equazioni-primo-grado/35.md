Find the real solution set of $\dfrac{x+5}{x+5}=1$.

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{-5\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-5\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{5\}$

## Explanation

The denominator is zero only at $x=-5$. Every other value makes the numerator and denominator equal and nonzero, so the quotient is $1$. The identity holds on the entire domain of the fraction.
