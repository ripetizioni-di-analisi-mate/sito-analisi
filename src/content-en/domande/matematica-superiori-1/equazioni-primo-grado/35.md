A quiz gives the identity $2x+6=2(x+3)$. If the stated domain is $\{0,1,2\}$, how many solutions should be recorded?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

An identity holds at every allowed input. Here the domain contains exactly three elements, so the solution set has three members.
