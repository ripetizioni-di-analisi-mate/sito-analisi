Which form correctly expands the parentheses in $-3(x-2)=x+2$?

- $-3x-6=x+2$
- $-3x-2=x+2$
- $-3x+6=x+2$
- $3x+6=x+2$

## Explanation

The factor $-3$ multiplies both $x$ and $-2$. The first product is $-3x$, and the second is $(-3)(-2)=6$. Thus the constant must be positive. One check sets $x=0$: the original left side equals $-3(0-2)=6$, as in the correct form. The expanded equation then gives $x=1$. The option with $3x$ would also change the sign of the coefficient of the unknown.
