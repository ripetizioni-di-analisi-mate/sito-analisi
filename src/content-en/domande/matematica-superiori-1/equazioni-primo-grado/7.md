Which argument is sufficient to show that $3(x-11)=3x-33$ holds for all real numbers?

- Both sides are positive
- Both sides have zero constant term
- The distributive law makes the two sides identical
- It works at $x=11$

## Explanation

Distribution turns the left-hand side into the right-hand side for every $x$. A single substitution, even a correct one, would only check that input.
