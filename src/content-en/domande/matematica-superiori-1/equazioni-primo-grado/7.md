Solve $2(x-4)=7$.

- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $15$

## Explanation

First divide both sides by two, keeping the binomial intact. Then add back the number subtracted from the variable. Combine the sum into a single fraction.

$$
x-4=\frac{7}2,\qquad x=\frac{15}2
$$

The result is $\dfrac{15}{2}$.
