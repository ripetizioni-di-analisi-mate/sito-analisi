Consider $x+a=2x-a$. If $a$ increases by $3$, by how much does the solution increase?

- $3$
- $\dfrac32$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

Collecting the variable terms gives $x=2a$. A change of $3$ in $a$ therefore produces twice that change, namely $6$, in the solution.
