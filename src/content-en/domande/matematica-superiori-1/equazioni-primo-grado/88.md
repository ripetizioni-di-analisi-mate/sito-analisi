For what value of $c$ is the equation $cx+5=2x+5$ an identity?

- $5$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

An identity must hold for every value of the unknown. The constant terms already match, so the coefficients of the variable must also agree.

$$
(c-2)x=0\;\text{for each }x,\qquad c=2
$$

The result is $2$.
