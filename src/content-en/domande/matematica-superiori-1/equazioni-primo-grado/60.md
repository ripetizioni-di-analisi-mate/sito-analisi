Two numbers have mean $10$. After adding a third number, the mean becomes $14$. What is the third number?

- $4$
- $18$
- $24$
- $22$

## Explanation

The first two numbers sum to $2\cdot10=20$. The three numbers must sum to $3\cdot14=42$. The third number makes up the difference between $42$ and $20$.

$$
20+x=42\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=22
$$
