Two groups of $3$ and $2$ measurements have respective means of $8$ and $13$. Which equation determines the mean $m$ of the combined group?

- $2m=8+13$
- $5m=2\cdot8+3\cdot13$
- $m=3\cdot8+2\cdot13$
- $5m=3\cdot8+2\cdot13$

## Explanation

Each mean must be multiplied by the number of measurements it represents. The overall sum is $50$ across $5$ measurements, giving $m=10$.
