You want a rectangle with perimeter $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ and width $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which conclusion is correct?

- The height is $1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- The height is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- There is no such rectangle
- The height is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The half-perimeter is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and must equal the sum of the width and height. A width of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives $h=9-10=-1$, which cannot be a length. We can also see why it is impossible without solving for $h$: the two horizontal sides already total $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, exceeding the given perimeter before the other two sides are added.
