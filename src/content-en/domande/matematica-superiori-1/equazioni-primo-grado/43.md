A rectangle has perimeter $50$ cm and base $10$ cm. What is its height?

- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Half the perimeter equals the sum of the base and height. Divide the perimeter by two, then subtract the base to find the height.

$$
b+h=25,\qquad h=25-10=15
$$

The result is $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
