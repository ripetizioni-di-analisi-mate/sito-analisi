Which equation has a real solution but none in the domain $x\ge0$?

- $-2x+3=-7$
- $2x=0$
- $-2x+3=9$
- $-2x-3=-9$

## Explanation

In $-2x+3=9$, reducing gives $-2x=6$, so $x=-3$. This is a real solution, but the required domain excludes it. The solution of $-2x+3=-7$ is $5$. The other two are $x=0$ for $2x=0$ and $x=3$ for $-2x-3=-9$. Those three results are nonnegative. In particular, zero is allowed because the inequality includes the endpoint.
