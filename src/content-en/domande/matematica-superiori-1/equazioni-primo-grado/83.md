Solve $-2x+5=15$.

- $5$
- $-10$
- $-5$
- $10$

## Explanation

Subtract $5$ from both sides. The coefficient of the variable is negative, and both sides are divided by that coefficient. Substituting the result gives the required value on the right-hand side.

$$
-2x=10,\qquad x=-5
$$

The result is $-5$.
