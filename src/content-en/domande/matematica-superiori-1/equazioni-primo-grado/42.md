A rectangle has perimeter $40$ cm and base $8$ cm. What is its height?

- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Half the perimeter equals the sum of the base and height. Divide the perimeter by two, then subtract the base to find the height.

$$
b+h=20,\qquad h=20-8=12
$$

The result is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
