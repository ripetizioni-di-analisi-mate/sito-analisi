A rectangle has perimeter $54\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its width is $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ more than twice its height. What is the height?

- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In centimetres, if the height is $h$, the width is $2h+3$. The sum of these two sides is half the perimeter, or $27$. Thus $h+(2h+3)=27$, giving $3h=24$ and $h=8$. The width is $19$, and $2(8+19)=54$ checks the perimeter. The three extra centimetres are part of the width before we count two copies of each side.
