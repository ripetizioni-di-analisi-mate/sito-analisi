Solve $T=F+rq$ for $r$, where $q\ne0$.

- $\dfrac{T}{q}-F$
- $\dfrac{T-F}{q}$
- $\dfrac{F-T}{q}$
- $q(T-F)$

## Explanation

Subtract $F$ from both sides to isolate the product $rq$. Since $q$ is nonzero, divide the entire difference $T-F$ by $q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T-F&=rq \\[6pt]
r&=\frac{T-F}{q}
\end{aligned}
$$
