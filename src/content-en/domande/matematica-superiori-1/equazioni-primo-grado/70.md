The area of a trapezium is $A=\dfrac{(B+b)h}{2}$ with $h>0$. Which expression isolates the base $b$?

- $b=\dfrac{2A}{h+B}$
- $b=\dfrac{2A}{h}-B$
- $b=\dfrac{2A-B}{h}$
- $b=\dfrac{A}{2h}-B$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $2$ and dividing by $h$ isolates the sum of the bases. Only then is $B$ subtracted to obtain $b$.
