In the relation $q=ar+4$, a measurement gives $q=4$ when $r=5$. What can you conclude about coefficient $a$?

- $a$ must be $0$
- $a$ must be $\dfrac45$
- $a$ must be $5$
- The measurement is consistent with every $a$

## Explanation

With $r=5$ and $q=4$, the relation becomes $4=5a+4$. Subtracting $4$ from both sides leaves $5a=0$, so $a=0$. The fact that $r$ is nonzero makes this measurement decisive. If $q=4$ had been measured at $r=0$, we would get the identity $4=4$, true for every coefficient. With the coefficient found, the contribution from $ar$ is always zero and the relation gives a constant value.
