A quantity satisfies $s=3t+6$. If $s=18$, what is $t$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $12$
- $8$

## Explanation

Substitute the known value of s into the formula. Subtract $6$ and divide by the coefficient three. This rearranges the formula to isolate the requested variable.

$$
18=3t+6,\qquad3t=12,\qquad t=4
$$

The result is $4$.
