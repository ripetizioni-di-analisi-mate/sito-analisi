A rectangle has perimeter $30$ cm and base $6$ cm. What is its height?

- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Half the perimeter equals the sum of the base and height. Divide the perimeter by two, then subtract the base to find the height.

$$
b+h=15,\qquad h=15-6=9
$$

The result is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
