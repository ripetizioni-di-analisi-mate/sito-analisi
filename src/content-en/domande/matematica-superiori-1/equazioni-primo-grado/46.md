Which equation without brackets is equivalent to $-[3-2(x+4)]=6$?

- $2x+4=6$
- $2x+5=6$
- $-2x-11=6$
- $2x-11=6$

## Explanation

The outside minus sign changes $3$ to $-3$ and $-2(x+4)$ to $+2(x+4)$. This leaves $-3+2x+8=2x+5$. In this form the solution is visibly positive, since $2x=1$. To check the expression, set $x=0$: the original group is $3-8=-5$. Its opposite is $5$, the constant in the reduced equation. At $x=\dfrac12$ the group equals $3-9=-6$, so its opposite is exactly the right side.
