Which value solves $\dfrac{(x-2)(x-5)}{x-2}=0$?

- $5$
- $2$
- $-5$
- $0$

## Explanation

The denominator excludes $x=2$. The numerator vanishes at $x=2$ or $x=5$ because it is a product. Only $5$ remains, giving $\dfrac{3\cdot0}{3}=0$ in the fraction. We can also cancel $x-2$ within the domain and solve $x-5=0$. A value that makes a common factor zero is excluded if it also makes the denominator zero.
