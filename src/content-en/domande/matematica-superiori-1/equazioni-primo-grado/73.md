Solve $\dfrac{x-5}{x+1}=\dfrac2{8}$ over the real numbers, given $x\ne-1$.

- $7$
- $-1$
- $5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The denominators are nonzero under the stated conditions. Cross-multiply and collect the terms in x. The solution obtained differs from the excluded value.

$$
8(x-5)=2(x+1),\qquad 6x=42,\qquad x=7
$$

The result is $7$.
