The mean of three numbers is $7$. The first two are $5$ and $6$. What is the third?

- $7$
- $10$
- $21$
- $8$

## Explanation

The sum of the three numbers is three times their mean. Subtracting the two known numbers from that sum leaves the third number.

$$
5+6+x=3\cdot7,\qquad x=10
$$

The result is $10$.
