A machine follows $s=3t+9$ for times $t\ge0$. Which values of $s$ can occur?

- $s>9$
- $s\ge0$
- $s\le9$
- $s\ge9$

## Explanation

The rearranged formula is $t=\dfrac{s-9}{3}$. To have $t\ge0$ we need $s-9\ge0$, so $s\ge9$. The endpoint $s=9$ is allowed and corresponds to $t=0$. Any $s<9$ would give a negative time outside the model's domain. For instance, $s=12$ occurs at $t=1$, while $s=6$ would require $t=-1$. The constant is the initial value, and the positive coefficient makes the measurement increase from there.
