A quantity satisfies $s=3t+5$. If $s=15$, what is $t$?

- $5$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$

## Explanation

Substitute the known value of s into the formula. Subtract $5$ and divide by the coefficient three. This rearranges the formula to isolate the requested variable.

$$
15=3t+5,\qquad3t=10,\qquad t=\frac{10}{3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{10}{3}$.
