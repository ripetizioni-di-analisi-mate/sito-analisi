The mean of three numbers is $6$. The first two are $4$ and $5$. What is the third?

- $9$
- $6$
- $18$
- $7$

## Explanation

The sum of the three numbers is three times their mean. Subtracting the two known numbers from that sum leaves the third number.

$$
4+5+x=3\cdot6,\qquad x=9
$$

The result is $9$.
