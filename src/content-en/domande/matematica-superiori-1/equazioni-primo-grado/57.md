Four measurements have mean $8$. After adding a fifth measurement, the mean is $9$. What is the new measurement?

- $13$
- $9$
- $5$
- $17$

## Explanation

The first four measurements total $4\cdot8=32$. With five measurements, a mean of $9$ requires total $5\cdot9=45$. The equation $32+x=45$ gives $x=13$. The new measurement must exceed $9$: the first four together fall short of what they would have totalled with mean $9$. Substitution gives $\dfrac{32+13}{5}=9$.
