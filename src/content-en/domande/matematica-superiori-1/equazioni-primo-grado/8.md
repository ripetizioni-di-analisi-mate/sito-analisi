Two consecutive integers add to $47$. If $m$ denotes the larger one, which equation lets you recover the smaller?

- $m+(m+1)=47$
- $2(m-1)=47$
- $m-(m-1)=47$
- $m+(m-1)=47$

## Explanation

The integer before $m$ is $m-1$. Solving gives $m=24$, so the smaller number is $23$.
