If $5(x+1)=2(x+1)+9$, what is the value of **the group** $x+1$?

- $2$
- $9$
- $\dfrac95$
- $3$

## Explanation

The groups in parentheses are identical. Subtracting $2(x+1)$ from each side leaves $3(x+1)=9$, then $x+1=3$. The value $2$ is the unknown itself, since $x=3-1=2$. The question asks for the whole group. Substituting $3$ into that group gives $5\cdot3=15$ on the left and $2\cdot3+9=15$ on the right. In the key step, removing two copies of the group from five leaves three. A single division then gives the value of the group.
