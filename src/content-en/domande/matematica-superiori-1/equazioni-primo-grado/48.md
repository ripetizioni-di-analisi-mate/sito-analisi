Admission costs 5 euros plus 3 euros per activity. The total paid is $35$ euros. How many activities were purchased?

- $30$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{35}{3}$
- $10$

## Explanation

Separate the fixed fee from the amount that depends on the number of activities. Subtracting five euros leaves the total cost of the activities. Dividing by three gives their number.

$$
5+3x=35,\qquad3x=30,\qquad x=10
$$

The result is $10$.
