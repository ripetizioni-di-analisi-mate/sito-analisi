For a nonnegative whole number $n$ of activities, A costs $5+2n$ euros and B costs $8+4n$ euros. Which statement is true?

- B always costs less than A
- The costs are equal at $n=0$
- The costs are equal at $n=2$
- A always costs less than B

## Explanation

The cost of B minus the cost of A is $8+4n-(5+2n)=3+2n$. For every $n\ge0$ the difference is at least three euros, so B always costs more. Equating the formulas would give $n=-\dfrac32$. A negative number of activities is outside the domain, so no allowed purchase gives equal costs.
