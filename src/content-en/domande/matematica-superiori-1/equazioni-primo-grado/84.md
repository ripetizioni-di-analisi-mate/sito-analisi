Solve $-2x+6=18$.

- $6$
- $-12$
- $12$
- $-6$

## Explanation

Subtract $6$ from both sides. The coefficient of the variable is negative, and both sides are divided by that coefficient. Substituting the result gives the required value on the right-hand side.

$$
-2x=12,\qquad x=-6
$$

The result is $-6$.
