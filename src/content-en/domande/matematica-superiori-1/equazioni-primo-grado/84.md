The equation $-3x+6=12$ came from multiplying both sides of another equation by $-3$. What was the original equation?

- $x+2=-4$
- $x-2=4$
- $3x-6=-12$
- $x-2=-4$

## Explanation

Reverse the step by dividing both sides by $-3$. On the left, divide both terms: $(-3x):(-3)=x$ and $6:(-3)=-2$. On the right, $12:(-3)=-4$. Multiplying $x-2=-4$ by $-3$ again recovers the stated equation. Because the factor is nonzero, the solution $x=-2$ is the same in both forms.
