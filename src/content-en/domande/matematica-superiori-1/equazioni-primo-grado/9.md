A rectangle has perimeter $110$ cm and base $22$ cm. What do the $55$ cm obtained by halving the perimeter represent?

- The sum of the base and height
- The height
- Twice the base
- The difference between height and base

## Explanation

The perimeter counts both the base and the height twice. The semiperimeter counts each once, so the height is $55-22=33$ cm.
