Starting with $x=3$, squaring both sides gives $x^2=9$. What extra information recovers precisely the original equation?

- $x\le0$
- $x\ne3$
- $x\ge0$
- $x\ne0$

## Explanation

The squared equation admits both $3$ and $-3$. Requiring a nonnegative value removes the extra solution and keeps $3$.
