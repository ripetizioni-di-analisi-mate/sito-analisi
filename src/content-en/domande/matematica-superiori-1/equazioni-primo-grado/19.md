Solve $\dfrac{x+2}3=6$.

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $20$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

The denominator is the nonzero number three. Multiplying both sides by three clears the denominator. Then subtract two to isolate the variable.

$$
x+2=18,\qquad x=16
$$

The result is $16$.
