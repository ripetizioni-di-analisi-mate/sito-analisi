Which of the numbers shown can multiply both sides of $\dfrac{x+4}6=\dfrac{x-2}3$ to clear both denominators?

- $9$
- $4$
- $12$
- $1$

## Explanation

The multiplier must be a multiple of $6$ and $3$. The number $12$ works because $12:6=2$ and $12:3=4$. This gives $2(x+4)=4(x-2)$. The least common multiple would be $6$, but a larger common multiple is allowed. The number $9$ is a multiple of $3$ but leaves a denominator on the left.
