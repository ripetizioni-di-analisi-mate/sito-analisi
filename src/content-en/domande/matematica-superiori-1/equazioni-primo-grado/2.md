To solve $2(x-11)=14$ without expanding, what must the whole block $x-11$ equal?

- $3$
- $7$
- $28$
- $18$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides by $2$ isolates the bracket and gives $7$. Adding $11$ then recovers $x=18$.
