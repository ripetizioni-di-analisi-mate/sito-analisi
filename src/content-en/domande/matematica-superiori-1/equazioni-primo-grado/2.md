Which transformation takes $4x+7=2x-1$ to $2x=-8$ in a single step?

- Subtract $2x$ from both sides
- Subtract $2x+7$ from both sides
- Add $2x-7$ to both sides
- Subtract $2x+7$ from the left side only

## Explanation

Subtracting $2x+7$ on the left gives $4x+7-2x-7=2x$. Doing the same on the right gives $2x-1-2x-7=-8$. Subtracting only $2x$ would leave the constant on the left. The chosen transformation is reversible: add $2x+7$ back to both sides to recover the original equation. Substituting $-4$ gives $-9$ on both sides of the original equation.
