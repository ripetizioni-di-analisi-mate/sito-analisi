The mean of three numbers is $8$. The first two are $6$ and $7$. What is the third?

- $8$
- $24$
- $11$
- $9$

## Explanation

The sum of the three numbers is three times their mean. Subtracting the two known numbers from that sum leaves the third number.

$$
6+7+x=3\cdot8,\qquad x=11
$$

The result is $11$.
