What value of $x$ makes the means of $(5,7,x)$ and $(8,10,x)$ equal?

- Any real number
- Only $6$
- No value
- Only $0$

## Explanation

The means are $\dfrac{12+x}{3}$ and $\dfrac{18+x}{3}$. Multiplying by $3$ and subtracting $x$ would require $12=18$. The second mean is always $\dfrac{18-12}{3}=2$ greater than the first. The added value contributes in the same way to both means, so it cannot remove the difference already present between the two totals.
