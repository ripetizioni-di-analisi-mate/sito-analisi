Entry costs $7$ euros and each activity costs $4$ euros. With a budget of $22$ euros, what is the greatest number of activities you can buy?

- $3$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{15}4$
- $5$

## Explanation

After entry there are $22-7=15$ euros left. The quotient $15:4=\dfrac{15}4$ tells us that three activities can be paid for with three euros to spare. A fourth would cost $7+4\cdot4=23$ euros in total. The budget cannot be exceeded, so take the integer below the quotient. Three activities cost $19$ euros altogether.
