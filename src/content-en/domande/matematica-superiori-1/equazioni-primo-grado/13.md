Solve $5x-5=2x+10$.

- $5$
- $-5$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{15}{7}$

## Explanation

Subtract twice x from both sides. Then add $5$ to both sides. Both the coefficient of the unknown and the constant term are multiples of three.

$$
5x-2x=10+5,\qquad3x=15,\qquad x=5
$$

The result is $5$.
