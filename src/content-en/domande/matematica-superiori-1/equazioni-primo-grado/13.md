In $x:4=11:2$, the denominator of the first ratio is twice that of the second. How must the corresponding numerator $11$ change?

- Double it to get $22$
- Halve it to get $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- Add $2$ to get $13$
- Keep it at $11$

## Explanation

Equivalent fractions result from multiplying numerator and denominator by the same number. Here the move from $2$ to $4$ requires factor $2$ in the numerator as well.
