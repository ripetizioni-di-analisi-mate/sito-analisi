Substituting $x=0$ into $4(x+1)=ax+4$ gives $4=4$. What does **that check alone** establish?

- $0$ solves the equation for every $a$
- The equation is an identity for every $a$
- The parameter must be $a=4$
- $0$ is always the only solution

## Explanation

At $x=0$ the product $ax$ is zero for every $a$, so the check establishes a solution shared by all parameters. To distinguish the remaining cases, reduce the whole equation to $(4-a)x=0$. When $a=4$ every real number is a solution, and when $a\ne4$ zero is the only solution. The substitution alone does not determine uniqueness.
