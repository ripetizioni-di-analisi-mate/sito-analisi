On the domain $x\ne3$, why cannot $x=3$ return to the solution set after denominators are cleared?

- Integers cannot solve fractional equations
- Multiplication always changes every solution
- Every equation has at most one solution
- The transformation holds only where the original equation is defined

## Explanation

Multiplying by a denominator compares values at points of the original domain. A candidate outside that domain cannot be tested in the original equality.
