In $3$ years a person will be $12$ years old. How old is the person now?

- $15$
- $12$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

Add the years that will pass to the current age to get the future age. Subtract that time interval from the future age to find the current age. The result is positive and fits the situation.

$$
x+3=12,\qquad x=9
$$

The result is $9$.
