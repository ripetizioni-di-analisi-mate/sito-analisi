A ticket costs $15$ euros plus $3$ euros per activity. How many activities give a total of $72$ euros?

- $57$
- $19$
- $24$
- $29$

## Explanation

Writing $n$ for the number of activities, the total price is the fixed fee plus $3$ euros for each activity, so we have the equation $15+3n=72$.

Subtracting $15$ from both sides gives $3n=57$. Dividing both sides by $3$ gives $n=\dfrac{57}{3}=19$. The number of activities is therefore $19$. The answer $57$ is the variable part of the price, in euros, while the answer $24$ comes from dividing the whole total by $3$ without subtracting the fixed fee.
