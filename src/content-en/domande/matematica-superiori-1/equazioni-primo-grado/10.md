A ticket costs $5$ euros plus $3$ euros per activity. How many activities give a total of $71$ euros?

- $66$
- $22$
- $23$
- $24$

## Explanation

The variable part of the bill is $71-5=66$ euros. At $3$ euros per activity, the number bought is $66:3=22$.
