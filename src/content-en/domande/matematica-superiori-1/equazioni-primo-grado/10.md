Which equation results from expanding $-3(2x-5)+4=2(x+1)$ and combining the constant terms?

- $-6x-11=2x+2$
- $-6x+19=2x+2$
- $-6x+15=2x+5$
- $-6x+19=2x+1$

## Explanation

The factor $-3$ multiplies both terms of $2x-5$, so its expansion has constant term $15$. Adding $4$ gives $19$ on the left. On the right, the factor $2$ multiplies both $x$ and $1$.

$$
-3(2x-5)+4=-6x+15+4
$$
