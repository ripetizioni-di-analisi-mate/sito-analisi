A ticket costs €$5$ plus €$3$ per activity. The cashier swaps the fixed fee and the charge per activity. For what number of activities will the total still be correct?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The two totals are $5+3n$ and $3+5n$. Equating them gives $2n=2$, so they agree only for one activity.
