Which formula converts any reading $s$ of the machine $s=3t+11$ back into the corresponding input $t$?

- $t=3s-11$
- $t=\dfrac{s-11}{3}$
- $t=\dfrac s3-11$
- $t=\dfrac{s+11}{3}$

## Explanation

First remove the offset of $11$ to obtain $s-11=3t$. Dividing by $3$ gives the inverse formula, which at $s=33$ returns $t=\dfrac{22}{3}$.
