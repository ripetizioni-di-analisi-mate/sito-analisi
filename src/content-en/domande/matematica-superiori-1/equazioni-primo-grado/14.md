Which equation has the same solution as $2x-7=8x+5$?

- $3x=6$
- $3x=-6$
- $6x=-2$
- $x-2=0$

## Explanation

Subtracting $8x$ and adding $7$ on both sides gives $-6x=12$, so the only solution is $x=-2$. The same solution follows from $3x=-6$. We can compare the reduced equations directly: dividing $-6x=12$ by $-2$ gives exactly $3x=-6$. The quotient remains negative when both signs are reversed.
