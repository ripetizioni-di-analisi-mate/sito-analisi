Solve $5x-6=2x+12$.

- $-6$
- $6$
- $18$
- $\dfrac{18}{7}$

## Explanation

Subtract twice x from both sides. Then add $6$ to both sides. Both the coefficient of the unknown and the constant term are multiples of three.

$$
5x-2x=12+6,\qquad3x=18,\qquad x=6
$$

The result is $6$.
