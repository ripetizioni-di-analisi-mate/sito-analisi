Which value of $x$ makes the proportion $(x-1):6=(x+2):9$ true?

- $7$
- $1$
- $5$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The denominators $6$ and $9$ are nonzero. Multiplying both sides by $18$ gives $3(x-1)=2(x+2)$. At $x=7$, both original ratios equal $1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x-3&=2x+4 \\[6pt]
x&=7
\end{aligned}
$$
