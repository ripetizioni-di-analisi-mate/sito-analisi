Which of these equations has exactly one real solution?

- $3(x+2)=3x+6$
- $3(x+2)=3x+5$
- $0(x+2)=5$
- $3(x+2)=2x+6$

## Explanation

In the correct form, the coefficients of $x$ differ. Expanding and reducing leaves $x=0$. The equation $3(x+2)=3x+6$ is an identity because the expansion agrees with the right side. The equation with $3x+5$ would require $6=5$, and the one with a zero factor would require $0=5$. Neither of those has a solution.
