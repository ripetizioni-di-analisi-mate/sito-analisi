One tank holds $36$ litres and another is empty. Transfer $q$ litres from the first to the second until the first holds twice as much as the second. Which equation tracks both amounts after the transfer?

- $36+q=2q$
- $36-q=2(36-q)$
- $36=2q$
- $36-q=2q$

## Explanation

After the transfer the amounts are $36-q$ and $q$, still totalling $36$. Imposing the required ratio gives $q=12$, leaving $24$ litres in the first tank and $12$ in the second.
