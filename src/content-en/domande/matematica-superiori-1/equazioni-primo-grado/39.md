Two consecutive integers have sum $27$. What is the smaller integer?

- $14$
- $26$
- $13$
- $12$

## Explanation

If the smaller integer is x, the next is x plus one. Their sum is twice x plus one. Subtract one from the given sum and divide by two.

$$
x+(x+1)=27,\qquad2x=26,\qquad x=13
$$

The result is $13$.
