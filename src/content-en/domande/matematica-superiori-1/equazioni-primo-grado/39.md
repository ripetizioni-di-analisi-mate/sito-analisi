Which number can be the sum of two consecutive **even** integers?

- $44$
- $45$
- $46$
- $48$

## Explanation

Write two consecutive even integers as $2n$ and $2n+2$, where $n$ is an integer. Their sum is $4n+2$, which leaves remainder $2$ upon division by $4$. Only $46$ has that remainder, since $46=4\cdot11+2$. The two numbers are $22$ and $24$. A sum of $44$ or $48$ would be even but would leave remainder zero upon division by $4$.
