If $n$ is the smallest of three consecutive **even** integers, which equation says that their sum is $60$?

- $n+(n+1)+(n+2)=60$
- $n+(n+2)+(n+4)=60$
- $n+(n+2)+(n+3)=60$
- $n+2n+4n=60$

## Explanation

Add $2$ to reach the next even integer, then add $2$ again. The sum is $3n+6$, so $3n=54$ and $n=18$. The value found is even, and $18$, $20$, $22$ do sum to $60$. Using steps of one would model consecutive integers that include an odd number.
