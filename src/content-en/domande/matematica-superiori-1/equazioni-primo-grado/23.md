After multiplying $\dfrac x3+\dfrac12=\dfrac56$ by $6$, someone writes $2x+1=5$. Which term needs correction?

- Replace the term $2x$ by $6x$
- Replace the term $5$ by $30$
- Replace the term $1$ by $3$
- No term needs correction

## Explanation

Apply the factor $6$ to both terms. The product $6\cdot\dfrac x3$ gives $2x$, but $6\cdot\dfrac12$ is $3$. The right side $6\cdot\dfrac56=5$ is correct. The new equation is therefore $2x+3=5$ and its solution is $x=1$, which gives $\dfrac13+\dfrac12=\dfrac56$ in the original form. Leaving the term as $1$ would amount to calculating $6\cdot\dfrac16$ instead of multiplying by one half.
