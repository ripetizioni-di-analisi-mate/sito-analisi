A rectangle has height $x$, width $x+2$, and perimeter $36$, with all lengths in centimetres. Which equation models the given information?

- $x(x+2)=36$
- $x+(x+2)=36$
- $2x+(x+2)=36$
- $2x+2(x+2)=36$

## Explanation

The perimeter counts two heights and two widths, so the sum is $2x+2(x+2)=4x+4$. The product $x(x+2)$ would give the area in square centimetres. The correct equation gives $4x=32$ and $x=8$. The four lengths are $8$, $10$, $8$, and $10$ centimetres, which add to the required perimeter. Using half the perimeter gives the same height after counting just one side of each length.
