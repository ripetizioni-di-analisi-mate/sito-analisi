A rectangle has perimeter $60$ cm and base $12$ cm. What is its height?

- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Half the perimeter equals the sum of the base and height. Divide the perimeter by two, then subtract the base to find the height.

$$
b+h=30,\qquad h=30-12=18
$$

The result is $18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
