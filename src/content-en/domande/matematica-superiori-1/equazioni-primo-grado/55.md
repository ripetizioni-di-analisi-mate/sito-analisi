Anna is $4$ years older than Luca. In $3$ years, the sum of their ages will be $28$ years. How old is Luca now?

- $12$
- $11$
- $9$
- $13$

## Explanation

If Luca is $x$ years old now, Anna is $x+4$. In $3$ years, each will be $3$ years older. Their future ages therefore sum to $2x+10$. The value $x=9$ gives future ages of $12$ and $16$, which sum to $28$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x+3)+(x+4+3)&=28 \\[6pt]
2x&=18 \\[6pt]
x&=9
\end{aligned}
$$
