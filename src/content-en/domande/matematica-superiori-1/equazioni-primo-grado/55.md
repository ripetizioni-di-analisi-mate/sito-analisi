Four years from now two twins' ages will total $34$. What mistake is made by subtracting only $4$ from $34$ before dividing by two?

- It uses a future total instead of a future mean
- It subtracts an amount that should be added
- It counts the increase of only one twin
- It assumes they have the same age

## Explanation

Each twin gains four years, so the combined age increases by eight. Their present total is $34-8=26$ years, making each twin $13$.
