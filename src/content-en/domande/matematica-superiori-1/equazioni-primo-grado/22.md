Solve $\dfrac x2-\dfrac x3=4$.

- $4$
- $24$
- $20$
- $\dfrac{24}{5}$

## Explanation

Rewrite the left-hand side with common denominator six. Its numerator is three times x minus twice x, which simplifies to x. Multiplying both sides by six gives the solution.

$$
\frac{3x-2x}6=4,\qquad x=24
$$

The result is $24$.
