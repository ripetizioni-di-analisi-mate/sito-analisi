What equality results from adding $\dfrac14$ to both sides of $\dfrac{x-1}4=\dfrac35$?

- $\dfrac x4=\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac x4=\dfrac{17}{20}$
- $\dfrac{x-1}4=\dfrac{17}{20}$
- $\dfrac{x+1}4=\dfrac35$

## Explanation

On the left, adding fractions with the same denominator gives $\dfrac{x-1+1}{4}=\dfrac x4$. On the right, use twentieths: $\dfrac35+\dfrac14=\dfrac{12+5}{20}=\dfrac{17}{20}$. The same addition was applied to both sides, even though the left-hand calculation is shorter. The new equation gives $x=\dfrac{17}5$. The right side increases, as it must when a positive fraction is added.
