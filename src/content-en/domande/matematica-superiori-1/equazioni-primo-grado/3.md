Solve $3x+5=20$.

- $15$
- $25$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{25}{3}$

## Explanation

Subtract the same constant term from both sides. The remaining equation has the unknown multiplied by three. Divide both sides by that nonzero coefficient.

$$
3x=20-5=15,\qquad x=5
$$

The result is $5$.
