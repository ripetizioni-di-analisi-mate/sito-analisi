In $5x-11=2x+22$ you want to eliminate the variable on the right and the constant on the left in one operation. What do you add to both sides?

- $11+2x$
- $-11-2x$
- $11-2x$
- $2x-11$

## Explanation

The term $+11$ cancels $-11$ on the left and $-2x$ cancels $2x$ on the right. This leaves $3x=33$, giving $x=11$.
