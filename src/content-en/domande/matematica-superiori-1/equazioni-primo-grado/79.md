Which of the values shown solves $\dfrac{x-4}{x-4}=1$ but does not solve $\dfrac{x-2}{x-2}=1$?

- $4$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The first equation holds for every real number except $4$, and the second for every real number except $2$. At $x=2$, the first fraction is $(-2):(-2)=1$ and the second is undefined. At $x=4$ the roles reverse. Both equations hold at $1$ and $3$. Simplifying both equations to $1=1$ preserves their different original exclusions.
