What value of $b$ makes $5(x-1)+b=5x+9$ an identity over the reals?

- $4$
- $9$
- $-14$
- $14$

## Explanation

Expanding the left side gives $5x-5+b$. The terms in $x$ already agree. For equality at every point we therefore need $-5+b=9$, so $b=14$. Substituting checks the result: $5(x-1)+14=5x-5+14=5x+9$. Any other $b$ would leave a nonzero constant gap. Another way is to impose equality at $x=0$, where the two constants can be read off directly.
