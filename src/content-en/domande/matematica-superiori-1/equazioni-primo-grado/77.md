A fraction $\dfrac{p}{x+1}$ must equal $2$ at $x=3$ and $1$ at $x=7$. Which constant numerator fits both data points?

- $8$
- $4$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

The first datum requires $p=2(3+1)=8$. The second confirms $p=1(7+1)=8$, making the conditions consistent.
