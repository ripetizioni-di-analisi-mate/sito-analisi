A quantity satisfies $s=3t+4$. If $s=12$, what is $t$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$

## Explanation

Substitute the known value of s into the formula. Subtract $4$ and divide by the coefficient three. This rearranges the formula to isolate the requested variable.

$$
12=3t+4,\qquad3t=8,\qquad t=\frac{8}{3}
$$

The result is $\dfrac{8}{3}$.
