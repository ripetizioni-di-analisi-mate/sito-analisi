With $v\ne0$, how do you rearrange $s=s_0+vt$ to find $t$?

- $t=\dfrac{s}{v}-s_0$
- $t=\dfrac{s+s_0}{v}$
- $t=\dfrac{s-s_0}{v}$
- $t=\dfrac{v}{s-s_0}$

## Explanation

First subtract the initial value $s_0$ to leave $s-s_0=vt$. Then divide the entire difference by $v$, which is allowed because $v\ne0$. As a check, substituting $\dfrac{s-s_0}{v}$ for $t$ gives $s_0+(s-s_0)=s$. The form $\dfrac{s}{v}-s_0$ would leave the second term of the numerator outside the quotient. If $v=0$, the right side does not change with $t$, so the measurement does not determine the time.
