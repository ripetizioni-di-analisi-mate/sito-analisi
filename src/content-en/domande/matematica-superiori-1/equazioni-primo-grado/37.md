Two consecutive integers are claimed to have sum $24$. What can you conclude from the equation?

- No such pair exists
- The pair is $11$ and $13$
- The pair is $12$ and $13$
- The pair is $\dfrac{23}{2}$ and $\dfrac{25}{2}$

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the smaller integer. The equation $n+(n+1)=24$ gives $n=\dfrac{23}{2}$, outside the integer domain. The numbers $11$ and $13$ sum to $24$ but differ by two. Also, $2n+1$ is always odd for integer $n$. This explains why no pair of consecutive integers can yield the required sum.
