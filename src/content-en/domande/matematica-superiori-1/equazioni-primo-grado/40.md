Two numbers sum to $50$. Can the smaller one be determined uniquely if all you know is that they are distinct positive integers?

- Yes, it is $24$
- Yes, it is $1$
- No such pair exists
- No: both $(1,49)$ and $(2,48)$ are possible

## Explanation

If the smaller number is $m$, the other is $50-m$. Without a further condition there are many positive integer choices with $m<50-m$, including $1$ and $2$.
