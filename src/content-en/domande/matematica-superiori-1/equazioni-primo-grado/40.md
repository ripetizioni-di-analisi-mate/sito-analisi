Can two consecutive integers sum to $24$?

- Yes, the integers are $11$ and $13$.
- Yes, the integers are $12$ and $12$.
- Yes, the integers are $12$ and $13$.
- No, because the sum of two consecutive integers is odd.

## Explanation

If $x$ is the smaller integer, the larger is $x+1$ and their sum is $2x+1$. This sum is odd for every integer $x$. Indeed, $2x+1=24$ would give $x=\dfrac{23}{2}$, which is not an integer.
