For which value of $a$ is $4$ a solution of $3x+a=19$?

- $12$
- $31$
- $7$
- $15$

## Explanation

Here the value of the unknown is given. Substituting $x=4$ gives an equation in $a$. The term $3x$ contributes $12$, so $a$ makes up the difference to $19$.

$$
a=19-3\cdot4=7
$$
