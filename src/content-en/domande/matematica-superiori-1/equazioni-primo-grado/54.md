Today a sister is $x$ years old and her brother is $3$ years younger. Two years ago their ages totalled $17$. Which equation models this information?

- $x+(x-3)=17$
- $(x-2)+(x-5)=17$
- $(x-2)+(x-3)=17$
- $(x+2)+(x-1)=17$

## Explanation

The brother's present age is $x-3$. Two years ago each was two years younger: the sister was $x-2$, and the brother was $x-3-2=x-5$. Adding gives $2x-7=17$ and $x=12$. Their ages today are $12$ and $9$. The earlier ages were $10$ and $7$, which add to $17$. Going back two years for each person reduces their combined age by four years.
