A quantity satisfies $s=3t+3$. If $s=9$, what is $t$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Substitute the known value of s into the formula. Subtract $3$ and divide by the coefficient three. This rearranges the formula to isolate the requested variable.

$$
9=3t+3,\qquad3t=6,\qquad t=2
$$

The result is $2$.
