If $-4x+6=-2x+14$, what is $x$?

- $-4$
- $4$
- $-2$
- $-10$

## Explanation

Adding $2x$ to both sides makes the coefficient on the left $-2$. Subtracting $6$ leaves a product equal to $8$. Its coefficient is negative and the product is positive, so the solution is negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-2x&=14-6=8 \\[6pt]
x&=\frac{8}{-2}=-4
\end{aligned}
$$
