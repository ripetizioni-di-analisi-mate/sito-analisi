What is the real solution set of $2(x+4)=2x+9$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $4$
- $\varnothing$
- $1$

## Explanation

Expanding the left-hand side gives variable terms with the same coefficient on both sides. Subtracting them leaves an equality between two different numbers. No value of the unknown makes it true.

The result is $\varnothing$.
