For which real value of $r$ does $(r-5)x=7$ have no solutions?

- $0$
- $-5$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The equation has no solution when the coefficient of $x$ is zero and the right side stays nonzero. Here that occurs at $r=5$, giving $0x=7$. For every other $r$ we can divide by $r-5$ to obtain a solution. For example, at $r=7$ we have $2x=7$, with solution $\dfrac72$.
