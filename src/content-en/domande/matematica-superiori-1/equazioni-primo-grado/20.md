Solve $\dfrac{2x+1}{4}=x-2$.

- $\dfrac32$
- $-\dfrac92$
- $9$
- $\dfrac92$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $4$ preserves the solution. The factor $4$ multiplies the entire right-hand side, so $4(x-2)=4x-8$. When the terms in $x$ are collected on the right, the $1$ on the left is added to $8$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x+1&=4x-8 \\[6pt]
9&=2x \\[6pt]
x&=\frac92
\end{aligned}
$$
