The sides $F$ and $G$ are linear and defined on all real numbers. Suppose $F(1)=G(1)$ and $F(4)=G(4)$. What is the solution set of $F(x)=G(x)$?

- $\mathbb R$
- $\{1,4\}$
- $[1,4]$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

Write the difference $F-G$ as $ax+b$. The two substitutions give $a+b=0$ and $4a+b=0$. Subtracting gives $3a=0$, hence $a=0$ and then $b=0$. The difference is zero for every real $x$. Linearity is what makes two distinct checks sufficient: a difference with nonzero coefficient $a$ could have at most one zero.
