Someone divides $x(2x+6)=0$ by $x$ and obtains $x=-3$. Which solution of the original equation was lost?

- None
- $0$
- $3$
- $-6$

## Explanation

Division by $x$ is allowed only if $x\ne0$. In that domain, $2x+6=0$ indeed gives $x=-3$. In the excluded case, the product equals $0\cdot6=0$. The full solution set is therefore $\{0,-3\}$. We can also find it directly: a product is zero exactly when at least one factor is zero.
