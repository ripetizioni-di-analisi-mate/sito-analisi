Someone will be $44$ years old in $11$ years. If $a$ denotes today's age, which equality puts the two ages in the correct places on the timeline?

- $44a=11$
- $a+44=11$
- $a+11=44$
- $a-11=44$

## Explanation

Moving from today to eleven years in the future increases the age by $11$. Reversing that move gives the present age, $44-11=33$.
