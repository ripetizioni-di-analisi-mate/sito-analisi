Solve $5x-3=2x+6$.

- $-3$
- $9$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{9}{7}$

## Explanation

Subtract twice x from both sides. Then add $3$ to both sides. Both the coefficient of the unknown and the constant term are multiples of three.

$$
5x-2x=6+3,\qquad3x=9,\qquad x=3
$$

The result is $3$.
