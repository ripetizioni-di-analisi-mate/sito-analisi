For which values of $m$ does $(m+2)x=5$ have exactly one real solution?

- $\{-2\}$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2\}$
- $\mathbb R$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{2\}$

## Explanation

When $m+2\ne0$, division gives exactly one value of $x$. The coefficient is zero at $m=-2$, making the equality $0x=5$. Therefore the only excluded value of the parameter is $-2$.
