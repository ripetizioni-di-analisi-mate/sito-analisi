One rental costs $8$ euros plus $3$ euros per hour. Another costs $14$ euros plus $2$ euros per hour. After how many hours are the costs equal?

- $3$
- $6$
- $2$
- $22$

## Explanation

Let $h$ be the number of hours. The costs are $8+3h$ and $14+2h$. The first starts $6$ euros lower and adds one extra euro per hour. The gap closes after $6$ hours, when both cost $26$ euros.

$$
\begin{aligned}
8+3h&=14+2h \\[6pt]
h&=14-8=6
\end{aligned}
$$
