Three hours of hire cost $19$ euros and five hours cost $27$ euros under the same fixed fee plus a constant hourly rate. What is the fixed fee?

- $12$ euros
- $7$ euros
- $4$ euros
- $8$ euros

## Explanation

The extra two hours cost $8$ euros, making the hourly rate $4$. Subtracting $3\cdot4$ from $19$ leaves a fixed fee of $7$.
