Starting from the result $11$ of $\dfrac{x+2}{3}$, in what order must you undo the operations to recover $x$?

- Subtract $2$, then multiply by $3$
- Divide by $3$, then add $2$
- Multiply by $3$, then add $2$
- Multiply by $3$, then subtract $2$

## Explanation

The last operation on the input was division by $3$, so it is undone first. This gives $33-2=31$, which reproduces $11$ in the formula.
