Without calculating its exact value, what can you predict about the solution of $\dfrac x6-\dfrac x4=3$?

- It is positive
- It is zero
- There is no solution
- It is negative

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ gives coefficient $\dfrac16-\dfrac14=-\dfrac1{12}$. A solution exists because the coefficient is nonzero. Its sign must be negative: multiplying $x$ by the negative coefficient must give the positive number $3$. If $x=0$, the left side would be zero, and if $x>0$ it would be negative.
