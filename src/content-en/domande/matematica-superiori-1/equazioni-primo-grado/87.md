The family $(m+1)x=m+1$ has a solution shared by every real value of $m$. Which one?

- $-1$
- $m$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=1$ gives the same quantity on both sides for every $m$. In the special case $m=-1$ all inputs satisfy the equation, including $1$.
