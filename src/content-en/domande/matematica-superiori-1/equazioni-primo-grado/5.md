What fraction of $x$ is the left-hand side of $\dfrac x2-\dfrac x3=11$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac56$
- $-\dfrac16$

## Explanation

One half minus one third is three sixths minus two sixths. One sixth of $x$ remains, and if it equals $11$ the whole of $x$ equals $66$.
