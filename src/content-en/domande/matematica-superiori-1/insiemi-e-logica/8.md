Let $A=\{x\in\mathbb Z\mid -2\le x\le2\}$. Which change to $B=\{x\in\mathbb Z\mid 2<x\le4\}$ makes $A\cap B$ a one-element set?

- Replace $2<x$ with $1\le x$.
- Replace $x\le4$ with $x<4$.
- Replace $2<x$ with $3<x$.
- Replace $2<x$ with $2\le x$.

## Explanation

The largest element of $A$ is $2$, and the smallest integer originally allowed in $B$ is $3$. Allowing equality at the lower bound of $B$ adds just $2$, so the intersection becomes $\{2\}$. Including $1$ as well gives two common elements. Restricting $B$ while still excluding $2$ leaves the intersection empty.
