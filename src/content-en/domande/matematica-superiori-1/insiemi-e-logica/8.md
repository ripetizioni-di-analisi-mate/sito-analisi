Let $A=\left\{5, 6, 7\right\}$ and $B=\left\{7, 8\right\}$. What is $A\cap B$?

- $\left\{5, 6, 7, 8\right\}$
- $\left\{5, 6\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$

## Explanation

The intersection contains the elements that appear in both sets. Compare the elements in the two lists. Only their common element satisfies both membership conditions.

The result is $\left\{7\right\}$.
