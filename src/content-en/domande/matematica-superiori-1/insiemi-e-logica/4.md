For $A=\{x\in\mathbb Z\mid -2<x\le1\}$ and $B=\{x\in\mathbb Z\mid 1\le x<3\}$, which list represents $A\cup B$?

- $\{-2;-1;0;1;2\}$
- $\{-1;0;1;2;3\}$
- $\{-1;0;2\}$
- $\{-1;0;1;2\}$

## Explanation

The strict inequalities exclude the endpoints $-2$ and $3$. This leaves $A=\{-1;0;1\}$ and $B=\{1;2\}$. The endpoint $1$ is included in both because of $\le$. It therefore belongs to the union and is listed once, along with $-1$, $0$, and $2$.
