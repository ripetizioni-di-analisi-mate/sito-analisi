In a group of $20$ students, $12$ study English, $8$ study French, and $4$ study both languages. How many study at least one of the two languages?

- $20$
- $4$
- $24$
- $16$

## Explanation

Adding the two group sizes counts each student who studies both languages twice. Subtract the size of the intersection once. The result counts each student once.

$$
12+8-4=16
$$

The result is $16$.
