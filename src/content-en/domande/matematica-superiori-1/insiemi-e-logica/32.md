In a class of $22$, $14$ students choose science and $11$ choose computing. Which of the following counts is possible for the overlap?

- $2$
- $12$
- $0$
- $7$

## Explanation

The overlap needs at least $14+11-22=3$ students, otherwise the union would exceed $22$. It can contain at most $11$, because it is part of the computing group. The value $7$ is within the bounds. It is attained with $7$ in science only, $4$ in computing only, and $4$ in neither.
