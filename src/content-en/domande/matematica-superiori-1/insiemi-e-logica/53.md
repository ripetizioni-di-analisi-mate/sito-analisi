Two finite sets satisfy $A\subseteq B$ and $|A|=|B|=8$. Which conclusion must hold?

- $A=B$
- $A\subsetneq B$
- $A\cap B=\varnothing$
- $|B\setminus A|=8$

## Explanation

The inclusion tells us that all eight elements of $A$ are already in $B$. Since $B$ also has eight, it cannot contain an extra element. This gives $B\subseteq A$, and inclusion in both directions gives $A=B$. The argument uses the fact that the sets are finite.
