Suppose $A=\{-2;0;2\}$ and $A\cup B=\{-2;-1;0;2;3\}$. Which part of $B$ is determined by this information?

- $B\cap A=\{0;2\}$
- $B\setminus A=\{-1;3\}$
- $B\setminus A=\{0;2\}$
- $B\cap A=\{-1;3\}$

## Explanation

To form $B\setminus A$, take the elements of the union outside $A$. These are $-1$ and $3$, and both must come from $B$. Every element of $A$ already belongs to the union, so its presence there gives no information about whether it also belongs to $B$.
