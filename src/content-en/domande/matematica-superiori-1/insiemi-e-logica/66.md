What is the negation of “All $3$ students in the group submitted the assignment”?

- No student in the group submitted the assignment
- At least one student in the group did not submit the assignment
- All the students submitted the assignment late
- Exactly one student in the group submitted the assignment

## Explanation

One counterexample is enough to contradict a universal statement. Some students may still have submitted the assignment. The negation therefore states that at least one student failed to submit it.

At least one student in the group did not submit the assignment.
