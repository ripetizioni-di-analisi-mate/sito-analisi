Which condition is necessary and sufficient for $A\setminus B$ and $B\setminus A$ to be the same set?

- $A=B$
- $A\cap B=\varnothing$
- $A\subsetneq B$
- $B\subsetneq A$

## Explanation

An element of $A\setminus B$ belongs to $A$, while any element of $B\setminus A$ lies outside it. The two differences are disjoint. They can be equal only if both are empty. This means every element of $A$ is in $B$ and vice versa, so $A=B$.
