Let $A=\left\{5, 6, 7\right\}$ and $B=\left\{7, 8\right\}$. What is $A\setminus B$?

- $\left\{5, 6\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{5, 6, 7, 8\right\}$

## Explanation

The difference contains the elements of A that do not belong to B. Start with the list for A. Remove the element that also appears in B.

The result is $\left\{5, 6\right\}$.
