For which of the following values of $x$ is the statement “$x\ge-2$ and $x<3$” false?

- $-2$
- $0$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The inequality $x<3$ excludes $3$. The values $-2$, $0$, and $2$ satisfy both conditions. Therefore $3$ is the only proposed value that makes the conjunction false.
