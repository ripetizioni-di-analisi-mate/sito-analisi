Two nonempty finite sets satisfy $A\subseteq B$. Which equality always holds?

- $A\cup B=A$
- $A\setminus B=B$
- $A\cap B=A$
- $B\setminus A=\varnothing$

## Explanation

Every element of $A$ also belongs to $B$, so each element of $A$ is common to both sets. A shared element must belong to $A$. Therefore $A\cap B=A$.
