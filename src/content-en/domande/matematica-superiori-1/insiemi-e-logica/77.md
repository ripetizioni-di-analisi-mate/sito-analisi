The function $f$ sends $a$ and $b$ to $1$, and $c$ to $2$. How many ordered domain pairs $(u,v)$ have $u\ne v$ and $f(u)=f(v)$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

Only $a$ and $b$ are different inputs with the same image. The two orders $(a,b)$ and $(b,a)$ give two pairs.
