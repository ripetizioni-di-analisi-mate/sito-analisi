Each student borrowed two books. What is the negation of “every student returned at least one of their two books”?

- At least one student returned neither of their two books.
- Every student returned neither of their two books.
- At least one student returned exactly one of their two books.
- At least one student returned both of their books.

## Explanation

We need one student who fails the property “returned at least one book.” For that student the number of books returned must be zero. A student who returned one and kept the other would still meet the property. The negation requires one counterexample and leaves the other students’ actions open.
