Let $A=\left\{3, 4, 5\right\}$. Which statement is true?

- $6$ $\in A$
- $4$ $\in A$
- $3$ $\notin A$
- $5$ $\notin A$

## Explanation

The membership symbol relates an element to a set. The element identified by the correct statement appears in the list for A. The other statements assert or deny membership in ways that conflict with the list.

The result is $4$ $\in A$.
