Two database filters select $A$ and $B$. To recover $A$, the region $A\setminus B$ is joined to a disjoint region. Which region always completes the filter exactly?

- $B\setminus A$
- $A\cup B$
- $A\cap B$
- $B$

## Explanation

Elements of $A$ are either in $B$ or outside it. The first region contains those outside, so the missing region is exactly the intersection.
