Let $A=\{1;2\}$. Its complement in $U$ is $\{3;4\}$. What is the set $U$?

- $\{3;4\}$
- $\{1;2\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{1;2;3;4\}$

## Explanation

The universal set is the disjoint union of $A$ and its complement. Here the elements $1$ and $2$ of $A$ are joined with the elements $3$ and $4$ of the complement.
