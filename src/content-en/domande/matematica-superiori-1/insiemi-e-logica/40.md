Let $f$ be a function from $A=\{1;2;3\}$ to $B=\{4;5\}$ with $f(1)=4$, $f(2)=4$, and $f(3)=5$. How many domain elements have image $4$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

Count the domain elements mapped to $4$. Both $1$ and $2$ have that image. The third element maps to $5$, so the count is $2$.
