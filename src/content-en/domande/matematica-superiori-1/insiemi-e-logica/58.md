Which expression is false in every row of its truth table?

- $(p\land q)\lor(\neg p\land\neg q)$
- $(p\lor q)\land(\neg p\land\neg q)$
- $(p\lor q)\land\neg p$
- $(p\land q)\land p$

## Explanation

The first part of the conjunction requires at least one of $p$ and $q$ to be true. The second requires both to be false. No row meets both requirements. The expressions using $p\land q$ are true when both $p$ and $q$ are true, and $(p\lor q)\land\neg p$ is true with $p$ false and $q$ true.
