Let $A$ be the set of students with a ticket and $B$ the set of students with a reservation. Which set represents students with a ticket and no reservation?

- $A\setminus B$
- $A\cap B$
- $B\setminus A$
- $A\cup B$

## Explanation

The first condition requires membership in $A$. The second keeps that student outside $B$. Together these conditions define the difference $A\setminus B$.
