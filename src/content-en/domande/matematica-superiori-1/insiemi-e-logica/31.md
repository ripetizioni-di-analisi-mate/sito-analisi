In a group of $15$ students, $9$ study English, $6$ study French, and $3$ study both languages. How many study at least one of the two languages?

- $15$
- $3$
- $12$
- $18$

## Explanation

Adding the two group sizes counts each student who studies both languages twice. Subtract the size of the intersection once. The result counts each student once.

$$
9+6-3=12
$$

The result is $12$.
