A rule says that a card is required to use the laboratory. If $L$ denotes laboratory use and $T$ having a card, which formula is equivalent to the rule?

- $\neg T\Rightarrow\neg L$
- $T\Rightarrow L$
- $L\Rightarrow\neg T$
- $L\lor T$

## Explanation

A card is necessary for use, so the original direction is $L\Rightarrow T$. The rule rules out the case in which someone uses the laboratory without a card. The contrapositive rules out the same case by saying that someone without a card does not use the laboratory. Having a card alone does not imply laboratory use.
