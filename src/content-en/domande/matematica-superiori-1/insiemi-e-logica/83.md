With $U=\{1;2;3;4\}$, $A=\{1;2\}$, and $B=\{2;3\}$, a classmate claims that $(A\cup B)^c=A^c\cup B^c$. Which listed element suffices to disprove the claim?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $5$

## Explanation

The number $1$ belongs to $A$, so it is in the union and outside its complement. But $1$ lies outside $B$ and therefore in $B^c$, so it belongs to $A^c\cup B^c$. Its presence on only one side disproves the equality. The number $4$ lies outside both sets and belongs to both sides.
