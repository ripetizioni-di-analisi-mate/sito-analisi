Which number is a counterexample to the statement "every prime number is odd"?

- $9$
- $3$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

A counterexample to a universal statement satisfies the hypothesis and does not satisfy the conclusion, so here a prime number that is not odd is needed. The number $2$ is prime, because its only positive divisors are $1$ and $2$, and it is even. The number $3$ is prime and odd, so it agrees with the statement. The numbers $9$ and $1$ are not prime, so they do not satisfy the hypothesis and cannot disprove the statement.

The counterexample is therefore $2$.
