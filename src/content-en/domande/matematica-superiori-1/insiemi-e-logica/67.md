Which number disproves the statement “every multiple of $4$ greater than $2$ has exactly three positive divisors”?

- $4$
- $6$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

The number $8$ is a multiple of four and has four positive divisors, $1,2,4,8$. It meets the hypothesis and contradicts the conclusion.
