Let $A=\{-2;0;2\}$ and $B=\{x\in\mathbb{Z}\mid -2<x\le2\}$. Which element suffices to disprove $A\subseteq B$?

- $-1$
- $-2$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

To disprove the inclusion, start with an element of $A$ and verify that it lies outside $B$. The number $-2$ is in $A$, but the strict lower bound for $B$ excludes it. The number $-1$ lies in $B$ and outside $A$, so it would concern the reverse inclusion.
