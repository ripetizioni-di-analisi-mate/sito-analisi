The rule $f(x)=x^2$ must define a function with domain $A=\{-2;0;2\}$. Which of the following sets can be its codomain?

- $\{-2;0;2\}$
- $\{1;4;9\}$
- $\{0;1;4\}$
- $\{0;2\}$

## Explanation

Calculate the three outputs. Both $-2$ and $2$ give $4$, while $0$ gives $0$. The codomain must therefore contain $0$ and $4$. It may include extra values even if the rule never yields them. Of the listed sets, only $\{0;1;4\}$ contains both.
