Two doors accept even integer codes and multiples of $3$, respectively. How many codes from $1$ to $12$ open **exactly one** door?

- $8$
- $6$
- $10$
- $4$

## Explanation

There are six even numbers and four multiples of three. The common multiples $6$ and $12$ were counted twice and must be excluded both times, giving $6+4-4=6$.
