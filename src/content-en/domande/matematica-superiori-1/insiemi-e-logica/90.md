Consider $A=\{0;1;2\}$, $B=\{1;3\}$, $C=\{1;2;4\}$, and $D=\{0;3\}$. What is the intersection $(A\times B)\cap(C\times D)$?

- $\{(1;1);(2;1)\}$
- $\{(1;3);(2;3)\}$
- $\{(3;1);(3;2)\}$
- $\{(1;3)\}$

## Explanation

A pair lies in both products if its first coordinate belongs to both $A$ and $C$ and its second to both $B$ and $D$. The possible first coordinates are therefore $1$ and $2$, while the only possible second coordinate is $3$. These choices give two pairs.
