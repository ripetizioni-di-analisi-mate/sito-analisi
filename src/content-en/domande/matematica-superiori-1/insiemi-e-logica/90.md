Let $A=\left\{7, 8\right\}$ and $B=\left\{9, 10\right\}$. Which pair belongs to $A\times B$?

- $(9;7)$
- $(7;9)$
- $(7;8)$
- $(9;10)$

## Explanation

In an ordered pair in the Cartesian product, the first element must belong to the first set. The second must belong to the second set. Reversing the order can therefore change whether the pair belongs to the product.

The result is $(7;9)$.
