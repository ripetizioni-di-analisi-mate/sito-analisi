Let $A=\left\{4, 5, 6\right\}$ and $B=\left\{6, 7\right\}$. What is $A\setminus B$?

- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{6\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 5, 6, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 5\right\}$

## Explanation

The difference contains the elements of A that do not belong to B. Start with the list for A. Remove the element that also appears in B.

The result is $\left\{4, 5\right\}$.
