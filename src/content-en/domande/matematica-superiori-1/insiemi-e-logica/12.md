Let $A=\{1;2;3;4;5\}$ and $A\setminus B=\{1;4\}$. What must $A\cap B$ be?

- $\{1;4\}$
- $\{1;2;3;4;5\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{2;3;5\}$

## Explanation

Every element of $A$ is in exactly one of the regions $A\setminus B$ and $A\cap B$. The numbers $1$ and $4$ are the ones outside $B$. Every other element of $A$ must therefore belong to $B$ as well, forming the intersection $\{2;3;5\}$.
