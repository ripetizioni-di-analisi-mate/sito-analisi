Let $U=\left\{3, 4, 5, 6\right\}$, $A=\left\{3, 5\right\}$, and $B=\left\{4, 5\right\}$. What is $U\setminus(A\cup B)$?

- $\left\{6\right\}$
- $\left\{3, 4, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{3, 4, 6\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$

## Explanation

First find the union of the two sets inside the grouping. Then remove every element of that union from the universal set. The result contains the elements that belong to neither A nor B.

The result is $\left\{6\right\}$.
