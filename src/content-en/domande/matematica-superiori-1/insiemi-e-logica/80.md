On domain $D=\{0;1;2\}$ with codomain $D$, define $f(x)=x$ and $g(x)=2-x$. Which statement is true?

- $f$ and $g$ have the same image set and agree at every $x\in D$.
- $f$ and $g$ have different image sets and agree only at $x=1$.
- $f$ and $g$ have different image sets and agree at no $x\in D$.
- $f$ and $g$ have the same image set and agree only at $x=1$.

## Explanation

The outputs at $0$, $1$, and $2$, in that order, are $0,1,2$ for $f$ and $2,1,0$ for $g$. Their sets of attained values are equal. To compare assignments, look at the same input in both functions. At $0$ and $2$ the outputs differ, while at $1$ both give $1$.
