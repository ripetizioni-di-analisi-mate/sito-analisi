Let $A=\{1;2;4\}$ and $B=\{1;3\}$. Which statement is true?

- $\forall a\in A,\ \exists b\in B,\ a\le b$
- $\exists a\in A,\ \forall b\in B,\ a\le b$
- $\exists b\in B,\ \forall a\in A,\ a\le b$
- $\forall b\in B,\ \forall a\in A,\ a\le b$

## Explanation

With $\exists a\in A$ before $\forall b\in B$, we need a value of $a$ that works with both $b=1$ and $b=3$. The choice $a=1$ meets both inequalities. The other statements require $a\le b$ even when $a=4$, but no element of $B$ is large enough.
