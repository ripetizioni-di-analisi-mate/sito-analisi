An element must belong to both $A\cup B$ and $A\cup C$. Which equivalent condition follows by considering $x\in A$ and $x\notin A$?

- $x\in A\cup(B\cap C)$
- $x\in A\cap(B\cup C)$
- $x\in(A\cap B)\cup C$
- $x\in A\cup B\cup C$

## Explanation

If $x$ belongs to $A$, it already satisfies both requirements. If $x$ is outside $A$, it must lie in $B$ to belong to the first union and in $C$ to belong to the second. Taking the union of these two cases gives exactly $A\cup(B\cap C)$.
