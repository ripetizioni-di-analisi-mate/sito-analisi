Let $A=\left\{3, 4, 5\right\}$ and $B=\left\{5, 6\right\}$. What is $A\cup B$?

- $\left\{3, 4, 5, 6\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{3, 4\right\}$
- $\left\{6\right\}$

## Explanation

The union contains the elements that belong to at least one of the two sets. Write each shared element once. List every element that is present in either set.

The result is $\left\{3, 4, 5, 6\right\}$.
