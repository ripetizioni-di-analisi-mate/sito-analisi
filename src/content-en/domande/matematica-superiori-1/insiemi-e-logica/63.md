For which real numbers is “$x<-1$ or $x>3$” false?

- $-1<x<3$
- $x\le-1$ or $x\ge3$
- $-1\le x\le3$
- $x\ge-1$

## Explanation

For a disjunction to be false, both of its parts must be false. The first fails when $x\ge-1$ and the second when $x\le3$. Both conditions must apply at the same time. The endpoints $-1$ and $3$ are included because the original statement uses strict inequalities.
