In a universal set of $22$ elements, disjoint sets $A$ and $B$ have $7$ and $12$ elements. A third set $C$ has $6$. At least how many elements of $C$ must lie in $A\cup B$?

- $0$
- $6$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

Disjointness lets us add $7+12=19$ for the union. Only $22-19=3$ elements lie outside it. At most three of the six elements of $C$ can be there, so at least three lie in $A\cup B$. This bound is attained by choosing all three outside elements and three from the union.
