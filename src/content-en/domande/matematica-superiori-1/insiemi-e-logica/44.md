Two disjoint sets have $12$ and $18$ elements, respectively. How many elements are in their union?

- $6$
- $216$
- $0$
- $30$

## Explanation

Disjoint sets have no elements in common. Their union involves no double counting, so its cardinality is the sum of their cardinalities.

$$
|A\cup B|=12+18=30
$$

The result is $30$.
