In the universal set $U=\left\{6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11\right\}$, let $A=\left\{6, 7, 8\right\}$. What is the complement of $A$ relative to $U$?

- $\left\{6, 7, 8\right\}$
- $\left\{6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11\right\}$
- $\left\{9, 10, 11\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$

## Explanation

The complement depends on the universal set. Take the elements of U and remove those that belong to A. The remaining elements form the complement.

The result is $\left\{9, 10, 11\right\}$.
