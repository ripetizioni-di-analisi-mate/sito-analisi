The statement “if a number is divisible by $6$, it is divisible by $3$” is true. Which number shows that its converse is false?

- $12$
- $18$
- $10$
- $9$

## Explanation

The converse starts with divisibility by three. The number $9$ is divisible by three but not by six, so it is a counterexample.
