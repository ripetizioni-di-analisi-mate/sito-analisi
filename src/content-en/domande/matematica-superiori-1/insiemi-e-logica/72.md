The implication "if an integer is divisible by $6$, then it is divisible by $3$" is true. Which number shows that its converse is false?

- $12$
- $18$
- $10$
- $9$

## Explanation

The converse swaps hypothesis and conclusion and states that every integer divisible by $3$ is divisible by $6$. A counterexample to the converse must therefore be divisible by $3$ and not by $6$. The numbers $12$ and $18$ are divisible by $6$, so they also satisfy the conclusion of the converse, while $10$ is not divisible by $3$ and does not satisfy its hypothesis. The number $9=3\cdot3$ is divisible by $3$ and is odd, so it is not divisible by $6$.

Therefore the required number is $9$.
