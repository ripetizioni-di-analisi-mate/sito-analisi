Which statement is the contrapositive of “If an integer is a multiple of $10$, then it is even”?

- If an integer is even, then it is a multiple of $10$.
- If an integer is odd, then it is not a multiple of $10$.
- If an integer is not a multiple of $10$, then it is odd.
- If an integer is a multiple of $10$, then it is odd.

## Explanation

The contrapositive assumes the negation of the conclusion and deduces the negation of the hypothesis. The negation of being even is being odd. Thus an odd integer is not a multiple of $10$.
