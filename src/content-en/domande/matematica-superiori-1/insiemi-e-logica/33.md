In a group of $25$ students, $15$ study English, $10$ study French, and $5$ study both languages. How many study at least one of the two languages?

- $20$
- $25$
- $5$
- $30$

## Explanation

Adding the two group sizes counts each student who studies both languages twice. Subtract the size of the intersection once. The result counts each student once.

$$
15+10-5=20
$$

The result is $20$.
