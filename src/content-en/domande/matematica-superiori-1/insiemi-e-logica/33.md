In a group, $11$ people use only the bus, $6$ only a bicycle, and $4$ use both. What is the sum of the total number of bus users and the total number of bicycle users?

- $25$
- $21$
- $17$
- $29$

## Explanation

The totals are $11+4=15$ for the bus and $6+4=10$ for bicycles. Their sum is $25$ because the four people using both types of transport appear in each total. To count distinct users, subtract the overlap once, which would give $21$.
