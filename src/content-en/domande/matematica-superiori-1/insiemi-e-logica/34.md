Let $|A|=16$ and $|B|=12$. Exactly $18$ elements belong to only one of $A$ and $B$. How many elements do the sets share?

- $10$
- $5$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

The sum $16+12=28$ counts the $18$ exclusive elements once and every shared element twice. The overlap therefore contributes $28-18=10$ to the sum. Since each shared element contributes twice to that part, there are $10/2=5$ elements in $A\cap B$.
