In a group of $30$ students, $18$ study English, $12$ study French, and $6$ study both languages. How many study at least one of the two languages?

- $30$
- $24$
- $6$
- $36$

## Explanation

Adding the two group sizes counts each student who studies both languages twice. Subtract the size of the intersection once. The result counts each student once.

$$
18+12-6=24
$$

The result is $24$.
