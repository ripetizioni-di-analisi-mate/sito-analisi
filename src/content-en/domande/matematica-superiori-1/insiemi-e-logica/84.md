We want to select the elements that belong to at least one of $A$ and $B$ and lie outside $C$. Which expression describes the selection?

- $A\cup(B\setminus C)$
- $(A\setminus C)\cap(B\setminus C)$
- $(A\cup B)\cap C$
- $(A\setminus C)\cup(B\setminus C)$

## Explanation

First form $A\cup B$ and remove $C$, or remove $C$ from each set separately and take the union of the results. In the second approach every retained element belongs to at least one set and lies outside $C$. The expression $A\cup(B\setminus C)$ would also admit elements shared by $A$ and $C$.
