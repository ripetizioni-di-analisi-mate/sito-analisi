The function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is given by $f(x)=3x+k$. The image of $-2$ is $1$. What is $k$?

- $-5$
- $7$
- $5$
- $-\dfrac53$

## Explanation

The information means $f(-2)=1$. Substitute $-2$ for $x$, giving $3(-2)+k=1$, hence $-6+k=1$. Adding $6$ to both sides gives $k=7$. To check, substitute the result back into the function to obtain $f(-2)=-6+7=1$, as required.
