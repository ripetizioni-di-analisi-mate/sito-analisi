In the domain $D=\{-2;-1;0;1;2\}$, consider “for every $x\in D$, $x^2\ge x+1$.” Which listed value is a counterexample?

- $-2$
- $-1$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

A counterexample must belong to $D$ and make the inequality false. With $x=0$ the claim would require $0\ge1$, which is false. For $-2$, $-1$, and $2$ we get $4\ge-1$, $1\ge0$, and $4\ge3$, respectively. The single test with $0$ is enough to disprove the universal statement.
