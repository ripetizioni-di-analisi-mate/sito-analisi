Suppose $A\times B=\{(1;4);(1;5);(3;4);(3;5)\}$. What are $A$ and $B$?

- $A=\{1;3\}$ and $B=\{4;5\}$.
- $A=\{4;5\}$ and $B=\{1;3\}$.
- $A=\{1;4\}$ and $B=\{3;5\}$.
- $A=\{1;3;4;5\}$ and $B=\{4;5\}$.

## Explanation

Since the product is nonempty, every element of $A$ occurs as a first coordinate and every element of $B$ as a second. Reading the first coordinates yields $\{1;3\}$ and reading the second yields $\{4;5\}$. Form the product again to check. All four possible pairings are indeed present.
