Let $A=\{1;2\}$ and $B=\{2;3\}$. Which pair belongs to both $A\times B$ and $B\times A$?

- $(1;3)$
- $(2;2)$
- $(2;3)$
- $(3;1)$

## Explanation

The first component must belong to both $A$ and $B$. The same goes for the second component. Since the intersection contains only $2$, the only possible pair is $(2;2)$.
