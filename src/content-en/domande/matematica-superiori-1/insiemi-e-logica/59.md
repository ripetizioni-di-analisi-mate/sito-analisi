A rule admits anyone who meets exactly one of conditions $p$ and $q$. Which formula expresses the rule?

- $p\lor q$
- $\neg(p\land q)$
- $(p\lor q)\land\neg(p\land q)$
- $(p\land q)\lor(\neg p\land\neg q)$

## Explanation

The disjunction $p\lor q$ allows the rows with at least one true condition, including the row where both hold. The negation $\neg(p\land q)$ removes the row where both are true. Requiring both expressions together therefore keeps only the rows with one true condition and the other false.
