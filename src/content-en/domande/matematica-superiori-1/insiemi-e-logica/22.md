The domain is $A=\{0;1;2\}$ and the codomain is $B=\{3;4\}$. The pairs $(0;3)$ and $(2;4)$ are already assigned. Which single pair can complete a function from $A$ to $B$?

- $(2;3)$
- $(1;3)$
- $(1;5)$
- $(3;4)$

## Explanation

The first coordinates $0$ and $2$ already have outputs, while $1$ still needs one. The new pair must therefore have first coordinate $1$ and second coordinate in $B$. The pair $(1;3)$ meets both requirements. It is permitted for $0$ to have the same output $3$.
