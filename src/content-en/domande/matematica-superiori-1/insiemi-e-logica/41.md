Two disjoint sets have $6$ and $9$ elements, respectively. How many elements are in their union?

- $15$
- $3$
- $54$
- $0$

## Explanation

Disjoint sets have no elements in common. Their union involves no double counting, so its cardinality is the sum of their cardinalities.

$$
|A\cup B|=6+9=15
$$

The result is $15$.
