Which statement is true for $A=\{\{2\};4\}$?

- $2\in A$
- $\{2\}\subseteq A$
- $\{4\}\in A$
- $\{2\}\in A$

## Explanation

The elements listed in $A$ are the set $\{2\}$ and the number $4$. This makes $\{2\}\in A$ true. The inclusion $\{2\}\subseteq A$ would require $2$ to be one of the listed elements, and that condition fails here. The number $4$ belongs to $A$, while the singleton $\{4\}$ is a different object.
