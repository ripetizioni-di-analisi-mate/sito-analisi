Of $60$ students, $35$ attend at least one of two courses and $9$ attend both. How many fail to meet the condition “attends exactly one course”?

- $25$
- $34$
- $26$
- $44$

## Explanation

A student who fails to attend exactly one course attends either zero or two. The first group contains $60-35=25$ students and the second contains $9$. The groups are disjoint, so the total is $25+9=34$. The value $26=35-9$ would count students who attend exactly one course.
