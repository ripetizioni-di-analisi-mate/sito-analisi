Suppose $B=\{5\}$ and $A\times B=\varnothing$. What follows?

- $A=\{5\}$
- $A\cap B=\{5\}$
- $A=\varnothing$
- $A$ can contain an element different from $5$.

## Explanation

If $A$ contained even one element $a$, the pair $(a;5)$ would belong to the product. This contradicts the given empty product, so $A=\varnothing$. What matters is whether one coordinate is available from each set. The sets $A$ and $B$ do not need to share an element.
