What is the negation of “Every box contains at least one blue pen”?

- Every box has exactly one blue pen.
- No box contains blue pens.
- There is a box with no blue pens.
- There is a box with at least two blue pens.

## Explanation

To contradict the statement, find a box that violates the condition. The condition “at least one blue pen” fails when the number of blue pens is zero. One box with no blue pens is therefore enough.
