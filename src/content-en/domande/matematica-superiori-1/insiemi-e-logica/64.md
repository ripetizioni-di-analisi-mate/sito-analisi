For a real number $x$, what is the negation of $x>6$?

- $x<6$
- $x\ge 6$
- $x\ne 6$
- $x\le 6$

## Explanation

The negation is true in exactly the cases where the original proposition is false. A number that is at most the threshold may equal it or be smaller. Equality must therefore be included.

The result is $x\le 6$.
