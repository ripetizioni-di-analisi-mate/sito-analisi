To take part in an activity, a person must be in $A$ and outside both $B$ and $C$. Which set describes the eligible participants?

- $A\setminus(B\cap C)$
- $A\setminus(B\cup C)$
- $(A\setminus B)\cup C$
- $(A\cap B)\setminus C$

## Explanation

Belonging to $B$ or to $C$ is enough to be excluded. So remove the entire union $B\cup C$ from the starting set $A$. Removing only the intersection would retain someone in $A$ and $B$ but outside $C$, even though that person fails the requirement.
