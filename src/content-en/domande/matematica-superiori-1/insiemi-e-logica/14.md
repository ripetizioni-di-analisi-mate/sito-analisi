Let $A=\left\{6, 7, 8\right\}$ and $B=\left\{8, 9\right\}$. What is $A\setminus B$?

- $\left\{9\right\}$
- $\left\{6, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$
- $\left\{6, 7, 8, 9\right\}$

## Explanation

The difference contains the elements of A that do not belong to B. Start with the list for A. Remove the element that also appears in B.

The result is $\left\{6, 7\right\}$.
