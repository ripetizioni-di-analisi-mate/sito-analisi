Let $A=\{1;2;3\}$ and $B=\{3;4;5\}$. What is the set of elements that belong to exactly one of the two sets?

- $\{1;2;4;5\}$
- $\{1;2;3;4;5\}$
- $\{3\}$
- $\{1;2\}$

## Explanation

Consider the two exclusive parts. Set $A\setminus B$ contains $1$ and $2$, while $B\setminus A$ contains $4$ and $5$. The number $3$ belongs to both sets and is outside the result.
