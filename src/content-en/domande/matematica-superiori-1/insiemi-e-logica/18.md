In the universal set $U=\left\{5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10\right\}$, let $A=\left\{5, 6, 7\right\}$. What is the complement of $A$ relative to $U$?

- $\left\{5, 6, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{8, 9, 10\right\}$
- $\left\{5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$

## Explanation

The complement depends on the universal set. Take the elements of U and remove those that belong to A. The remaining elements form the complement.

The result is $\left\{8, 9, 10\right\}$.
