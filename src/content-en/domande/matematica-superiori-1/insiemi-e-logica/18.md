Two sets in the same universal set $U$ satisfy $A\subsetneq B$. Which inclusion relates their complements?

- $A^c\subsetneq B^c$
- $B^c\subsetneq A^c$
- $A^c=B^c$
- $B^c\subsetneq A$

## Explanation

An element outside $B$ must also be outside $A$ because every element of $A$ is in $B$. This gives $B^c\subseteq A^c$. Since the original inclusion is proper, some element of $B$ lies outside $A$. It belongs to $A^c$ and is absent from $B^c$, so the inclusion between complements is proper too.
