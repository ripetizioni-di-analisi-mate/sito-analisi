Two disjoint sets have $8$ and $12$ elements, respectively. How many elements are in their union?

- $4$
- $20$
- $96$
- $0$

## Explanation

Disjoint sets have no elements in common. Their union involves no double counting, so its cardinality is the sum of their cardinalities.

$$
|A\cup B|=8+12=20
$$

The result is $20$.
