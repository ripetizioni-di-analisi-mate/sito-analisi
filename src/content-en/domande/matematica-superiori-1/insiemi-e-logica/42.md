In a universe $U$ with $40$ elements the complement $A^c$ has $15$ elements, while $|B|=18$ and $|A\cap B|=10$. How many elements does $A\cup B$ have?

- $43$
- $33$
- $7$
- $23$

## Explanation

The complement $A^c$ contains the elements of $U$ that do not belong to $A$, so $|A|=|U|-|A^c|=40-15=25$. By the union formula, $|A\cup B|=|A|+|B|-|A\cap B|$, because the common elements are counted both in $|A|$ and in $|B|$. Substituting the values gives

$$
|A\cup B|=25+18-10=33 .
$$

The value $23$ comes from using $15$ as the number of elements of $A$, while $43$ is obtained by ignoring the common elements. The value $7$ is instead the number of elements of $U$ outside the union.

Therefore $A\cup B$ has $33$ elements.
