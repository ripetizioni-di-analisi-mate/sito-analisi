There are $32$ students. Of these, $18$ take drama, $15$ take music, and $5$ take neither. How many take both?

- $1$
- $6$
- $10$
- $15$

## Explanation

The number of students taking at least one activity is $32-5=27$. The sum $18+15=33$ counts those who do both activities twice. The difference $33-27=6$ is therefore the number of students who do both.
