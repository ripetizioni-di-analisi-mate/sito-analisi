The set $A$ consists of exactly the positive divisors of $18$, and the set $B$ consists of exactly the positive divisors of $24$. Which set is $A\cap B$?

- The positive divisors of $12$
- The positive multiples of $6$
- The positive divisors of $72$
- The positive divisors of $6$

## Explanation

Listing the divisors gives $A=\{1;2;3;6;9;18\}$ and $B=\{1;2;3;4;6;8;12;24\}$. The intersection collects the elements that appear in both lists, so $A\cap B=\{1;2;3;6\}$. These numbers are exactly the positive divisors of $6$, which is the greatest common divisor of $18$ and $24$. The divisors of $12$ include $4$, which does not divide $18$, and the divisors of $72$, the least common multiple, include $9$ and $24$, which do not belong to both sets.

Therefore $A\cap B$ is the set of positive divisors of $6$.
