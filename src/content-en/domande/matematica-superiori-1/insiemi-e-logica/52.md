Set $A$ consists of the positive divisors of $18$, and $B$ consists of those of $24$. Which set is exactly $A\cap B$?

- The positive divisors of $12$
- The positive multiples of $6$
- The positive divisors of $72$
- The positive divisors of $6$

## Explanation

A common divisor also divides the greatest common divisor. Since $\gcd(18,24)=6$, the intersection is $\{1,2,3,6\}$.
