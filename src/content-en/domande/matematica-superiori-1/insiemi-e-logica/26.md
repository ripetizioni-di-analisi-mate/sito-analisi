Set $A$ has $3$ elements and set $B$ has $4$ elements. How many ordered pairs are in $A\times B$?

- $7$
- $12$
- $9$
- $4$

## Explanation

The Cartesian product contains every pair with its first element in A and its second in B. Pair each element of A with every element of B. Multiply the two cardinalities.

$$
|A\times B|=3\cdot4=12
$$

The result is $12$.
