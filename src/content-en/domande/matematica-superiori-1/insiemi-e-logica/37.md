In a universal set of $34$ elements, $7$ lie outside $A\cup B$ and $18$ belong to $B$. How many belong only to $A$?

- $9$
- $16$
- $11$
- $27$

## Explanation

Removing the $7$ outside elements gives $|A\cup B|=27$. The union is made up of all of $B$ and the part of $A$ outside $B$. These parts are disjoint. The requested count is therefore $27-18=9$, even though the size of the intersection is unknown.
