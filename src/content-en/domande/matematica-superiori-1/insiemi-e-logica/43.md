Two disjoint sets have $10$ and $15$ elements, respectively. How many elements are in their union?

- $5$
- $150$
- $25$
- $0$

## Explanation

Disjoint sets have no elements in common. Their union involves no double counting, so its cardinality is the sum of their cardinalities.

$$
|A\cup B|=10+15=25
$$

The result is $25$.
