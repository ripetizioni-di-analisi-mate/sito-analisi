The sets $A$, $B$, and $C$ are pairwise disjoint. The unions $A\cup B$, $B\cup C$, and $C\cup A$ have $18$, $21$, and $17$ elements, respectively. How many elements are in $A\cup B\cup C$?

- $56$
- $21$
- $28$
- $38$

## Explanation

Each element lies in just one of the three starting sets, and its set appears in two of the three stated unions. Adding $18+21+17=56$ therefore counts every element twice. The requested size is $56/2=28$. Disjointness ensures that every element occurs the same number of times.
