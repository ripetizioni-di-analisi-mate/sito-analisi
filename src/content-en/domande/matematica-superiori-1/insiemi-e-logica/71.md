What is the negation of “There is an integer greater than $3$ with property P”?

- Every integer greater than 3 has property P
- Exactly one integer greater than 3 has property P
- No integer greater than 3 has property P
- There is an integer less than 3 with property P

## Explanation

The original proposition requires at least one example in the stated domain. Negating it rules out every example in that domain. Replacing the domain with smaller integers fails to do this.

No integer greater than 3 has property P.
