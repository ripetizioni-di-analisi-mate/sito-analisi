Let $A=\{-1;1;3\}$ and $B=\{0;1;3;5\}$. Which relation contains all pairs of $A\times B$ satisfying $|a-b|=2$?

- $\{(-1;1);(1;3);(3;5)\}$
- $\{(-1;1);(1;-1);(1;3);(3;1);(3;5)\}$
- $\{(1;-1);(1;3);(3;1);(5;3)\}$
- $\{(-1;1);(1;3);(3;1);(3;5)\}$

## Explanation

Distance $2$ allows $b=a-2$ or $b=a+2$, provided $b$ belongs to $B$. For $a=-1$ and $a=1$ the first candidate lies outside $B$, so only the second is retained. For $a=3$ both $b=1$ and $b=5$ are retained, giving four pairs in total.
