Keep $A=\{1;3\}$ fixed and replace $U=\{1;2;3;4\}$ with $V=\{0;1;2;3;4;5\}$. Which elements are added to the complement of $A$?

- $\{0;5\}$
- $\{1;3\}$
- $\{2;4\}$
- $\varnothing$

## Explanation

In the first universal set the complement contains $2$ and $4$. These remain in the second because they still belong to the universal set and lie outside $A$. Passing from $U$ to $V$ introduces $0$ and $5$, also outside $A$. These are exactly the elements added to the complement.
