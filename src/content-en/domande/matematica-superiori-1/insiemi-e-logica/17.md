In the universal set $U=\left\{4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9\right\}$, let $A=\left\{4, 5, 6\right\}$. What is the complement of $A$ relative to $U$?

- $\left\{7, 8, 9\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 5, 6\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9\right\}$
- $\left\{6\right\}$

## Explanation

The complement depends on the universal set. Take the elements of U and remove those that belong to A. The remaining elements form the complement.

The result is $\left\{7, 8, 9\right\}$.
