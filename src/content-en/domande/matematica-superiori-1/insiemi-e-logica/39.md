In a class of $36$ students, $24$ swim, play tennis, or do both. How many do neither?

- $24$
- $36$
- $12$
- $60$

## Explanation

The group that takes part in at least one sport and its complement split the class into two disjoint groups. The size of the complement is the class total minus the number already counted. Subtract the participants from the total.

$$
36-24=12
$$

The result is $12$.
