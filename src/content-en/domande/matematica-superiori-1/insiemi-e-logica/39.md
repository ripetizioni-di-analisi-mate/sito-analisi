Sets $A$ and $B$ have $12$ and $9$ elements, respectively. If $a=|A\setminus B|$ and $b=|B\setminus A|$, which quantity is already determined?

- $a+b=21$
- $|A\cap B|=3$
- $a-b=3$
- $|A\cup B|=21$

## Explanation

Let $t=|A\cap B|$. Then $a=12-t$ and $b=9-t$. The same count $t$ cancels in the difference, leaving $a-b=3$. Finding the sum of the two side regions or the size of the union would require more information, since both vary with the size of the overlap.
