A rectangle has width $3x$ and height $2x+3$, where $x>0$. What is its area?

- $6 x^{2} + 3$
- $6 x^{2} + 9 x$
- $6 x + 9$
- $10 x + 6$

## Explanation

The area of the rectangle is its width times its height. Apply the distributive property to the product of the monomial and the binomial. Each term contributes to the same area.

$$
A=3x(2x+3)=6 x^{2} + 9 x
$$

The result is $6 x^{2} + 9 x$.
