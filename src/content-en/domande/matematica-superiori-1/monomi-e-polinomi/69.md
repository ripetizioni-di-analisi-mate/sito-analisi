A square of side length $x$ is cut from a corner of a square of side length $x+3$, with $x>0$. Which pair gives the area and perimeter of the remaining shape?

- $(6x+9,\,4x+12)$
- $(6x+9,\,2x+12)$
- $(9,\,4x+12)$
- $(6x+9,\,12)$

## Explanation

The remaining area is $(x+3)^2-x^2=6x+9$. The boundary loses two segments of length $x$, but the cut creates two new interior segments, each of length $x$. These changes cancel in the perimeter, which stays at the original square's perimeter, $4(x+3)=4x+12$. The cut removes only the corner because $0<x<x+3$.
