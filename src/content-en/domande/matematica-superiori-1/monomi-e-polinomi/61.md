A rectangle has sides $3x$ and $3y$, where $x,y>0$. Which expression gives its perimeter?

- $6 x + 6 y$
- $3 x + 3 y$
- $9 x y$
- $6 x + 3 y$

## Explanation

The perimeter is the sum of the four side lengths. Opposite sides of a rectangle have equal length. Each of the two lengths therefore occurs twice.

$$
P=3x+3y+3x+3y=6 x + 6 y
$$

The result is $6 x + 6 y$.
