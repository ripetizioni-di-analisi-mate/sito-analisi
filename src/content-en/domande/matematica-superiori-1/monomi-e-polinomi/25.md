What is the total degree of $x^3y^2+2x^4-y^6+y^6$ after simplifying?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The terms in $y^6$ cancel. The remaining monomials have degrees $3+2=5$ and $4$. The larger degree is $5$.
