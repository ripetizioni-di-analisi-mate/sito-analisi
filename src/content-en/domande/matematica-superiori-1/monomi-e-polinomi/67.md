A grid has $n$ rows and $n+1$ columns. Only its outer frame, one cell wide, is coloured, with $n\ge3$. How many cells remain white?

- $n(n-1)$
- $(n-2)^2$
- $(n-2)(n-1)$
- $(n-1)(n+1)$

## Explanation

Remove two rows and two columns. This leaves $n-2$ rows and $n-1$ columns of white cells.
