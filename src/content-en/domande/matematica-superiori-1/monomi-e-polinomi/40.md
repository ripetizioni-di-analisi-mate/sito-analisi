What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in the expansion of $3x(2x^2-x+5)-x(2x+1)$?

- $-1$
- $-3$
- $-2$
- $-5$

## Explanation

Each outside factor contains $x$. The only products in the expansion that contain $x^2$ are $3x(-x)$ and $(-x)(2x)$. Their coefficients $-3$ and $-2$ are added.

$$
3x(-x)+(-x)(2x)=-3x^2-2x^2=-5x^2
$$
