Let $P(x)=x^3-x+2$. Which pair of distinct inputs gives the same value of $P$?

- $(-1,0)$
- $(-2,0)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(-2,2)$

## Explanation

At $x=-1$ we have $x^3=x$, so the first two terms cancel. The same happens at $x=0$, and both values are $2$. The other options compare $P(-2)=-8+2+2=-4$ and $P(2)=8-2+2=8$ with $P(0)=2$ or with each other. The two outputs differ in each of those pairs. The input $x=1$ would also give $2$, because the cube and the first power agree there.
