Expand $(2x+1)(x^2-3x+4)$. What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in the simplified result?

- $-6$
- $-5$
- $1$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Degree two comes from pairing degrees $1$ and $1$, or degrees $0$ and $2$. The contributions are $(2x)(-3x)=-6x^2$ and $1\cdot x^2=x^2$. They add to $-5x^2$. The other products give degrees other than two. These two pairs of factors therefore suffice to find the requested coefficient. In particular, choosing $-6$ would omit the contribution from the constant term in the first factor.
