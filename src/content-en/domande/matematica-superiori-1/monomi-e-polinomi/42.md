Expand $(x+4)(x+2)$.

- $x^{2} + 8$
- $x^{2} + 6 x + 8$
- $\left(x + 2\right) \left(2 x + 4\right)$
- $x^{2} + 2 x + 8$

## Explanation

Each term of the first factor multiplies both terms of the second. This gives four products. The two linear terms are like terms and can be added.

$$
(x+4)(x+2)=x^2+2x+4x+8=x^{2} + 6 x + 8
$$

The result is $x^{2} + 6 x + 8$.
