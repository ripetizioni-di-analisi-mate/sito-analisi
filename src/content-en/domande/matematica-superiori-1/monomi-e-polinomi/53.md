Let $P(x,y)=3x^2+4xy-5y+7$. What is the constant term of $P$ with respect to both variables?

- $7$
- $-5y+7$
- $3x^2+7$
- $-5$

## Explanation

A constant term with respect to both variables is independent of both $x$ and $y$. Setting $x=0$ still leaves $-5y+7$, which depends on $y$. To find the constant term, also set $y=0$. This gives $P(0,0)=7$. The term $-5y$ has degree zero in $x$ and degree one in $y$. For a polynomial in two variables, evaluating at the origin removes every term of positive total degree.
