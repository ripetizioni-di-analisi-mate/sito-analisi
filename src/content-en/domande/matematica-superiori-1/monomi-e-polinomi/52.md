For which real value of $c$ is the constant term of $(x+c)(2x-3)+6$ zero?

- $-2$
- $3$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

The constant term is found by setting $x=0$, even before expanding the polynomial. The value is $c(-3)+6=-3c+6$. Making it zero requires $c=2$. As a check, the constants in $(x+2)(2x-3)$ multiply to $-6$, which cancels with the external $+6$. All the other contributions contain $x$. The condition constrains only the coefficient of degree zero.
