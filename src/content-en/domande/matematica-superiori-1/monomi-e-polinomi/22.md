Simplify $P=3x^2y-4xy^2-3x^2y+4xy^2$. What is the result and what is its degree under the convention used in this chapter?

- The result is zero and has degree $0$.
- The result is zero and is not assigned a degree.
- The result is a polynomial of degree $3$.
- The result is a polynomial of degree $2$.

## Explanation

The terms in $x^2y$ give coefficient $3-3=0$, while those in $xy^2$ give $-4+4=0$. The simplified result is the zero polynomial. Its degree would need to be the maximum of the degrees of its nonzero terms, but there are none. Nonzero constants are the polynomials of degree zero.
