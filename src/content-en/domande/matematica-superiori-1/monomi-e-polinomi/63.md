A rectangle has sides $5x$ and $5y$, where $x,y>0$. Which expression gives its perimeter?

- $5 x + 5 y$
- $25 x y$
- $10 x + 10 y$
- $10 x + 5 y$

## Explanation

The perimeter is the sum of the four side lengths. Opposite sides of a rectangle have equal length. Each of the two lengths therefore occurs twice.

$$
P=5x+5y+5x+5y=10 x + 10 y
$$

The result is $10 x + 10 y$.
