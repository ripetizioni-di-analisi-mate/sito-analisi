Two rectangles have side lengths $x+1$ and $2x+3$, and $x+4$ and $x+2$, respectively. For which $x>0$ do their perimeters coincide?

- $1$
- $4$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The perimeters coincide exactly when the semiperimeters, which count each side length once, coincide. The first is $3x+4$ and the second is $2x+6$. Equating them gives $x=2$, which is allowed because all side lengths are positive. The side lengths are then $3$ and $7$, and $6$ and $4$, giving perimeter $20$ in each case.
