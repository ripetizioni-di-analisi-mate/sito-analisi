In a product of two polynomials, one factor has only even powers of $x$ and the other has only odd powers. Which powers may appear after simplification?

- Only even powers
- Only powers divisible by $4$
- A constant term must also appear
- Only odd powers

## Explanation

Every resulting exponent is the sum of an even and an odd number, so it is odd. Combining like terms can cancel terms without introducing new exponents.
