Given $P(x,y)=3x^2y-4xy+2y^2$, what is the pair $(P(2,0),P(0,2))$?

- $(8,0)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(0,8)$
- $(8,8)$

## Explanation

Every term contains a factor of $y$, so setting $y=0$ gives $P(2,0)=0$. Setting $x=0$ makes just the first two terms vanish, leaving $2y^2$. When $y=2$ this contribution is $2\cdot2^2=8$. The order of the pair records which variable was set to zero in each substitution. A zero in the first coordinate makes factors containing $x$ vanish.
