The monomial $M$ has a negative coefficient and total degree $2$. Which pair gives the sign of the coefficient and the degree of $M^3$?

- Positive, degree $6$.
- Negative, degree $5$.
- Negative, degree $6$.
- Positive, degree $8$.

## Explanation

Cubing $M$ is the same as multiplying three copies of $M$. The product of three negative coefficients is negative. The exponent of every variable is tripled, so the sum of the exponents is also tripled. The total degree of $M^3$ is $3\cdot2=6$. For example, $(-x^2)^3=-x^6$. The variable part may contain several variables, because multiplying by three distributes over the sum of all the exponents.
