Which monomial gives $12x^4y^3$ when multiplied by $-3x^2y$?

- $4 x^{2} y^{2}$
- $- 4 x^{2} y^{2}$
- $- 4 x^{2} y$
- $- 4 x^{6} y^{4}$

## Explanation

The coefficient must give $12$ when multiplied by $-3$, so it is $-4$. In a product, exponents on the same variable are added. The required exponents are therefore $4-2=2$ and $3-1=2$.

$$
(-3x^2y)(-4x^2y^2)=12x^4y^3
$$
