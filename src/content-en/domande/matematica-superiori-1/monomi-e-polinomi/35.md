Let $P(x)=2x^2-x+3$ and $Q(x)=x^2+3x-2$. Calculate $P(x)-2Q(x)$.

- $- 7 x - 1$
- $2 x^{2} - 7 x + 7$
- $7 - 7 x$
- $5 x + 7$

## Explanation

The factor $-2$ multiplies all three terms of $Q$. After expanding, the quadratic terms cancel, the linear terms give $-7x$, and the constant terms give $7$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(x)-2Q(x)&=2x^2-x+3-2x^2-6x+4 \\[6pt]&=-7x+7
\end{aligned}
$$
