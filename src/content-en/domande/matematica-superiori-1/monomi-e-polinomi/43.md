For which real value of $a$ does the product $(ax+2)(x-3)$ have a zero linear coefficient?

- $-\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$
- $0$

## Explanation

The linear term comes from two products. The first is $(ax)(-3)=-3ax$ and the second is $2\cdot x=2x$. They cancel in the sum when $2-3a=0$, that is, at $a=\dfrac23$. The simplified polynomial at that value is $\dfrac23x^2-6$, with a nonzero quadratic term and a constant term. At $a=0$ the quadratic term disappears, while the linear term remains $2x$.
