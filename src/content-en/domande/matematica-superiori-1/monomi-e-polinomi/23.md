Which polynomial has degree $2$ in $x$, degree $3$ in $y$ and total degree $4$?

- $x^2y^3+5y$
- $x^2+y^3$
- $x^2y^2+xy^3$
- $xy^3+x^3y$

## Explanation

Check the exponents of each variable separately and the sum of the exponents in each term. In $x^2y^2+xy^3$ the maximum for $x$ is $2$, the maximum for $y$ is $3$, and each term has degree $4$. Adding the two individual degrees gives $5$, but different terms attain those maxima.
