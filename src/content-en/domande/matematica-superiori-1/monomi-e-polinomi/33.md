Let $A$ and $B$ be two polynomials. Which expression equals $(A-B)-(A+B)$ for every choice of $A$ and $B$?

- $-2B$
- $2B$
- $0$
- $2A-2B$

## Explanation

The subtraction sign applied to the sum $A+B$ changes it into $-A-B$. The formula becomes $A-B-A-B$. The copies of $A$ cancel, and the two copies of $-B$ add to $-2B$. For example, with $A=x^2$ and $B=x$, the difference becomes $(x^2-x)-(x^2+x)=-2x$. When $B$ is zero, the formula gives zero for every choice of $A$.
