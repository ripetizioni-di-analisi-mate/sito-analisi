A rectangle has perimeter $14x+8$ and one side of length $4x+1$, where $x>0$. What is the length of the adjacent side?

- $3 x + 3$
- $10 x + 7$
- $3 x + 4$
- $7 x + 4$

## Explanation

The semiperimeter $7x+4$ is the sum of two adjacent sides. Subtracting the known side from this sum gives the other side.

$$
(7x+4)-(4x+1)=3x+3
$$
