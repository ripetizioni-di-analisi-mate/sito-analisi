Multiply $M=2x^2y$ by a monomial with coefficient $-3$. The product must have equal exponents of $x$ and $y$ and total degree $6$. What is the second monomial?

- $-3x^2y$
- $-3xy$
- $-3xy^2$
- $-3y^3$

## Explanation

In the product both exponents are $3$. The first monomial needs one more factor of $x$ and two of $y$, so its multiplier is $-3xy^2$.
