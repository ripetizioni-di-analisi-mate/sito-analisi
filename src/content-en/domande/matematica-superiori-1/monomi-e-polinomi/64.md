A rectangle has sides $6x$ and $6y$, where $x,y>0$. Which expression gives its perimeter?

- $6 x + 6 y$
- $36 x y$
- $12 x + 6 y$
- $12 x + 12 y$

## Explanation

The perimeter is the sum of the four side lengths. Opposite sides of a rectangle have equal length. Each of the two lengths therefore occurs twice.

$$
P=6x+6y+6x+6y=12 x + 12 y
$$

The result is $12 x + 12 y$.
