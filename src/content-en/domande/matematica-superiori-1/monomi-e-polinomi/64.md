A rectangle has side lengths $2x-6$ and $x+1$. Which condition identifies the real values of $x$ for which both sides are positive?

- $x>-1$
- $x>0$
- $x\ge3$
- $x>3$

## Explanation

The first side is positive when $2x-6>0$, or $x>3$. The second side is positive when $x>-1$. Both conditions must hold. Every $x>3$ is also greater than $-1$, so their intersection is $x>3$. At $x=3$ the first side is zero, so the two sides are not both positive.
