Calculate $(-2x^2y)^3+8x^6y^2$.

- $0$
- $8 x^{6} y^{2}$
- $8 x^{6} y^{3} + 8 x^{6} y^{2}$
- $- 8 x^{6} y^{3} + 8 x^{6} y^{2}$

## Explanation

The power has coefficient $(-2)^3=-8$ and variable part $x^6y^3$. The second term contains $y^2$. Because the exponents on $y$ differ, the two monomials remain separate in simplified form.

$$
(-2x^2y)^3+8x^6y^2=-8x^6y^3+8x^6y^2
$$
