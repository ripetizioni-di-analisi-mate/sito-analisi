A rectangle has area $A=(x+2)(2x+1)$ and perimeter $P=2(3x+3)$, with $x>0$. Both dimensions are tripled. What are the new area and perimeter?

- $(3A,\,3P)$
- $(9A,\,9P)$
- $(6A,\,3P)$
- $(9A,\,3P)$

## Explanation

The sides are $x+2$ and $2x+1$. The area multiplies the lengths, so it acquires two factors of $3$, giving $9A$. The perimeter adds the side lengths, so the entire sum is multiplied by $3$, giving $3P$. At $x=1$ the side lengths increase from $3$ to $9$, and the area from $9$ to $81$.
