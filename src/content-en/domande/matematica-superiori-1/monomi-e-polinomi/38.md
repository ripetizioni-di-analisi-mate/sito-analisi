How many terms with nonzero coefficients remain after expanding and simplifying $2x(x^2-3x+1)-2x^3+6x^2-5$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first product gives $2x^3-6x^2+2x$. In the full sum, the cubic term cancels with $-2x^3$ and the quadratic term with $6x^2$. This leaves $2x-5$, in which both the coefficient of $x$ and the constant term are nonzero. There are therefore two terms to count after simplification.
