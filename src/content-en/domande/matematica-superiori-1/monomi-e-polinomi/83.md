Starting with $P=x^2+y$, a new polynomial is formed by adding a single monomial. Which result is impossible?

- $x^2+3y$
- $x^2$
- $5x^2+3y$
- $5x^2+y$

## Explanation

The monomial to add would be the difference between the desired result and $P$. To obtain $5x^2+3y$, one would need to add $4x^2+2y$, which has two different variable parts. Adding $2y$ gives $x^2+3y$, adding $-y$ gives $x^2$, and adding $4x^2$ gives $5x^2+y$. Each of those three additions changes only one group of like terms.
