Which monomial must be added to $2x^2y$ to obtain $7x^2y$?

- $5 x y^{2}$
- $9 x^{2} y$
- $5 x^{2} y$
- $7 x^{2} y$

## Explanation

The missing monomial must have the same variable part as the required sum. Its coefficient is the final coefficient minus the one already present. To check the result, add the two coefficients again.

$$
(7-2)x^2y=5 x^{2} y
$$

The result is $5 x^{2} y$.
