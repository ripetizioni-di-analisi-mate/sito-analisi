Subtracting $Q=2x^2+bx-1$ from $P=5x^2-3x+4$ gives a polynomial whose coefficient of $x$ is $5$. What is $b$?

- $8$
- $-8$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Only the linear terms contribute to the requested coefficient. The term $-3x$ from $P$ stays as it is, and $Q$ contributes $-bx$ because the subtraction applies to all of $Q$. Set $-3-b=5$, giving $b=-8$. Substituting that value makes the coefficient $-3-(-8)=5$. The quadratic coefficient of the difference remains $5-2=3$, independently of the parameter value.
