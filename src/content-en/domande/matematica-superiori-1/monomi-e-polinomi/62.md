A rectangle has sides $4x$ and $4y$, where $x,y>0$. Which expression gives its perimeter?

- $4 x + 4 y$
- $8 x + 8 y$
- $16 x y$
- $8 x + 4 y$

## Explanation

The perimeter is the sum of the four side lengths. Opposite sides of a rectangle have equal length. Each of the two lengths therefore occurs twice.

$$
P=4x+4y+4x+4y=8 x + 8 y
$$

The result is $8 x + 8 y$.
