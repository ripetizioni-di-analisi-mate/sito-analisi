Two rectangles share a whole side of length $y$. Their widths are $x$ and $2x$. By how much does the sum of the two perimeters exceed the perimeter of their union?

- $y$
- $2y$
- $3x$
- $2x+y$

## Explanation

The shared side is counted once in each original perimeter. It becomes internal to the outer boundary, removing a total length of $2y$.
