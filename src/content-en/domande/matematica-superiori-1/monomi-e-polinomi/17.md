A monomial with coefficient $1$ stays the same when $x$ is replaced by $-x$ and changes sign when $y$ is replaced by $-y$. Which one can it be?

- $x^2y^3$
- $x^3y^2$
- $x^2y^2$
- $x^3y^3$

## Explanation

A power of $-x$ equals the same power of $x$ when the exponent is even, and it is its opposite when the exponent is odd. The monomial stays the same when $x$ is replaced by $-x$, so the exponent of $x$ is even. The monomial changes sign when $y$ is replaced by $-y$, so the exponent of $y$ is odd. Among the given monomials only $x^2y^3$ has an even exponent in $x$ and an odd exponent in $y$, and indeed $(-x)^2y^3=x^2y^3$ and $x^2(-y)^3=-x^2y^3$.

Therefore the monomial is $x^2y^3$.
