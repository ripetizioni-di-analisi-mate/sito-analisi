A monomial with coefficient $1$ stays unchanged under $x\mapsto -x$ but changes sign under $y\mapsto -y$. Which could it be?

- $x^2y^3$
- $x^3y^2$
- $x^2y^2$
- $x^3y^3$

## Explanation

The exponent of $x$ must be even and that of $y$ odd. Only $x^2y^3$ meets both conditions.
