What is the degree of $P(x)=4x^4+3x^2-3x^4+x$ after simplifying?

- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $8$

## Explanation

Determine the degree after combining like terms. The difference between the two fourth-degree coefficients is one, so the fourth-degree term remains. The highest remaining exponent is four.

$$
P(x)=x^4+3x^2+x
$$

The result is $4$.
