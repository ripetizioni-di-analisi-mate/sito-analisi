A student assigns degree $3$ to $P=4x^3-2x+7-4x^3+x^2$ because the first term is cubic. What is the correct degree of $P$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Degree is read from the simplified form, where each power appears with its final coefficient. The two cubic terms add to zero, so $P=x^2-2x+7$. The quadratic term remains with coefficient $1$. Its position at the end of the original expression is irrelevant because addition allows the terms to be reordered.
