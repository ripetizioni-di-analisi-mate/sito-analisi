For the polynomial $P(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ it is known that $P(0)=5$ and $P(1)+P(-1)=18$. Which coefficient can be found, besides $c$?

- $a=9$
- $a=4$
- $b=4$
- $b=8$

## Explanation

For $x=0$ only the constant term remains, so $c=P(0)=5$. We have $P(1)=a+b+c$ and $P(-1)=a-b+c$, so in the sum the terms with $b$ cancel and $P(1)+P(-1)=2a+2c$. With $c=5$ this gives $2a+10=18$, so $2a=8$ and $a=4$. The value $a=9$ comes from forgetting the contribution $2c$, while $b$ cannot be found, because it does not appear in the sum.

Therefore the coefficient that can be found is $a=4$.
