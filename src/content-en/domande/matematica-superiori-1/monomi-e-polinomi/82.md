For $P(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ we know $P(0)=5$ and $P(1)+P(-1)=18$. Which coefficient can be determined besides $c$?

- $a=9$
- $a=4$
- $b=4$
- $b=8$

## Explanation

The sum is $2a+2c$ because the terms involving $b$ cancel. With $c=5$, solving $2a+10=18$ gives $a=4$.
