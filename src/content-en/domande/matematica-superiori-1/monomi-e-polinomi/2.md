Which expression is a monomial in the displayed variables when the variables are free to take any real value?

- $\dfrac2x$
- $-\dfrac32 x^2$
- $\sqrt{x}$
- $x+y$

## Explanation

The coefficient may be any real number, including the negative fraction $-\dfrac32$. The exponent $2$ is a nonnegative integer, so $-\dfrac32x^2$ is a monomial. A denominator containing $x$ corresponds to the exponent $-1$, while the square root has exponent $\dfrac12$ on its domain. The sum $x+y$ has two different variable parts. For example, the candidate with a variable denominator is undefined at zero.
