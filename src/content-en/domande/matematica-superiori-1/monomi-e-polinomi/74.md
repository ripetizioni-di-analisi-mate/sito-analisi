Which proposed value is enough to disprove the claim that $x^3=x$ for every real number $x$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

To disprove an equality claimed to hold for all values, one input with different outputs suffices. At $x=2$, the left-hand side is $2^3=8$ and the right-hand side is $2$. The two sides agree at $x=-1$, $x=0$ and $x=1$. Those three checks confirm individual cases but do not prove the identity.
