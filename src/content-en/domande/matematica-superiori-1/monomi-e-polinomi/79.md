What is the simplified form of $P-\dfrac12(Q-P)$ when $P=x^2+2x$ and $Q=3x^2-2x+4$?

- $4x+2$
- $-2$
- $4x-2$
- $2x^2+2$

## Explanation

First calculate the inner difference $Q-P=2x^2-4x+4$. Half of that is $x^2-2x+2$. Subtracting that half from $P$ cancels the $x^2$ term, and the $-2x$ term contributes $+2x$. This leaves $4x-2$. As a check, at $x=0$ the original expression gives $0-\dfrac12(4-0)=-2$. The final coefficient of $x$ is the sum of two positive contributions, each equal to $2$.
