Let $P=2x^2-5x$ and $Q=-2x^2+3x+6$. For which real values of $x$ is the sum $P+Q$ strictly negative?

- $x<3$
- $x\ge3$
- $x\le3$
- $x>3$

## Explanation

The quadratic terms have coefficients $2$ and $-2$, so they cancel. The linear terms give $-5x+3x=-2x$, while the constant term $6$ remains. The sum is $-2x+6=2(3-x)$, which is negative exactly when $x>3$. At $x=3$ the sum equals zero. For example, at $x=4$ the linear term is $-8$ and outweighs the constant term.
