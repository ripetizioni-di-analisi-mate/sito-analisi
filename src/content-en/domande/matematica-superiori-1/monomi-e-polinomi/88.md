Which of these polynomials satisfies the identity $P(-x)=-P(x)$ for every $x$?

- $2x^3-x+1$
- $2x^2-x$
- $2x^4-x^2$
- $2x^3-x$

## Explanation

Odd powers change sign when $-x$ replaces $x$, while even powers keep their sign. In the polynomial $2x^3-x$, both degrees are odd. This gives $P(-x)=-2x^3+x=-(2x^3-x)$. Each of the other polynomials contains a nonzero constant or at least one nonzero term of even degree.
