Given $P=\dfrac34x^2-x$ and $Q=-\dfrac34x^2-x$, what is the pair of coefficients of $x^2$ in $P-Q$ and $Q-P$, in that order?

- $\left(-\dfrac32,\dfrac32\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac32,-\dfrac32\right)$
- $(0,0)$
- $\left(\dfrac34,-\dfrac34\right)$

## Explanation

In $P-Q$ the coefficient $-\dfrac34$ is subtracted, giving $\dfrac34+\dfrac34=\dfrac32$. In $Q-P$ the first coefficient is $-\dfrac34$ and $\dfrac34$ is subtracted, giving $-\dfrac32$. Reversing the order of subtraction changes the sign of the whole result. The linear coefficient is zero in both differences because the original linear terms are equal.
