For which nonnegative integer $k$ does the product $(5x^ky^2)(-2xy^3)$ have total degree $10$?

- $5$
- $6$
- $7$
- $4$

## Explanation

The coefficients multiply to $-10$, so the resulting monomial is nonzero. Its exponent of $x$ is $k+1$ and its exponent of $y$ is $2+3=5$. Its total degree is $(k+1)+5=k+6$. Setting that equal to $10$ gives $k=4$. The resulting variable part is $x^5y^5$. The choice $k=5$ would give degree eleven, because it contributes one more factor of $x$.
