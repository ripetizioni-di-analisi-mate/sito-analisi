Which polynomial must be added to $3x^2-2x+5$ to obtain $x^2+1$?

- $- 2 x^{2} + 2 x - 4$
- $- 2 x^{2} - 2 x - 4$
- $2 x^{2} - 2 x + 4$
- $- 2 x^{2} + 2 x + 4$

## Explanation

The missing addend equals the required sum minus the known addend. The linear term becomes $+2x$ because $-2x$ is subtracted.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M(x)&=(x^2+1)-(3x^2-2x+5) \\[6pt]&=-2x^2+2x-4
\end{aligned}
$$
