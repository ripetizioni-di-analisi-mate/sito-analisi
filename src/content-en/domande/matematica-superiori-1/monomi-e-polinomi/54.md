Someone claims that two polynomials with the same constant term are always identical. Which pair of polynomials is a counterexample?

- $x^2+1,\quad 1+x^2$
- $x^2+1,\quad x+1$
- $x+1,\quad x+2$
- $x+1,\quad 2x-x+1$

## Explanation

A counterexample must meet the hypothesis and make the conclusion fail. The polynomials $x^2+1$ and $x+1$ both have constant term $1$, but at $x=2$ their values are $5$ and $3$. The pair with constants $1$ and $2$ fails the hypothesis. In the other two pairs, the polynomials are identical after simplification.
