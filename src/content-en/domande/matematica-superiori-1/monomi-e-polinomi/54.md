What is the constant term of the polynomial $P(x)=6 x^{3} - 2 x^{2} + 7 x - 7$?

- $6$
- $-7$
- $7$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The constant term is the term without the variable. It is also the value of the polynomial when the variable is zero. All other terms vanish at that value.

$$
P(0)=-7
$$

The result is $-7$.
