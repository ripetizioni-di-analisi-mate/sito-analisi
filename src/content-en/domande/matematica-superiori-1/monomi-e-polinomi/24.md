The simplified polynomial $P(x,y)$ has degree $3$ in $x$ and degree $2$ in $y$. Which term, when present with a nonzero coefficient, guarantees a total degree of $5$?

- $x^3y$
- $x^2y^2$
- $x^3$
- $x^3y^2$

## Explanation

Every term has exponent at most $3$ in $x$ and at most $2$ in $y$, so its total degree is at most $5$. The term $x^3y^2$ attains both maxima and has degree $3+2=5$. The other proposed terms have degree $3$ or $4$, so none of them alone guarantees degree $5$.
