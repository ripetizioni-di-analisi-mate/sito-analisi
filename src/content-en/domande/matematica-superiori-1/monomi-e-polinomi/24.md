What is the total degree of the monomial $-6x^5y^3$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $7$
- $8$

## Explanation

The total degree of a nonzero monomial is the sum of the exponents of its variables. The numerical coefficient does not contribute to the degree. Add the two exponents.

$$
(5)+3=8
$$

The result is $8$.
