Given $U=2x^2-4x+1$ and $V=-x^2+x-3$, which polynomial results from subtracting the opposite of $V$ from $U$?

- $3x^2-5x+4$
- $x^2-5x-2$
- $x^2-3x+4$
- $x^2-3x-2$

## Explanation

Subtracting the opposite means writing $U-(-V)$. The subtraction changes the sign of each term of $-V$ again, so it gives the sum $U+V$. The result is $(2-1)x^2+(-4+1)x+(1-3)=x^2-3x-2$. The constant term can be checked in the original expression at $x=0$, which gives $1-3=-2$. The linear contributions from the original polynomials are added directly in the same way.
