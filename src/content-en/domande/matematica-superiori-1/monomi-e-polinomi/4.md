Set $y=-2$ and write $M=-xy^2$ and $N=-x^2y$ as monomials in $x$ alone. What is the pair $(M,N)$?

- $(4x,\,2x^2)$
- $(-4x,\,-2x^2)$
- $(2x,\,-4x^2)$
- $(-4x,\,2x^2)$

## Explanation

Substituting $y=-2$ in the first monomial gives $-x(-2)^2=-4x$. The square applies to the substituted value of $y$, and the minus sign in front of the product remains. The second monomial uses the first power of $y$, so $-x^2(-2)=2x^2$. The variable $x$ retains its original exponent in each monomial. The two substitutions give coefficients with opposite signs because the exponents of $y$ have different parity.
