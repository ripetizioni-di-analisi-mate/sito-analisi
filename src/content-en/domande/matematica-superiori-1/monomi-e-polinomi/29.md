Let $P=ax^2+3x-5$ and $Q=-ax^2+bx+5$. What condition on the parameters makes $P+Q$ the zero polynomial?

- $b=-3$ for every $a$.
- $a=0$ for every $b$.
- $b=3$ for every $a$.
- $a=-b$ for every $b$.

## Explanation

The quadratic terms have opposite coefficients for every choice of $a$, and the constants $-5$ and $5$ cancel. Only $(3+b)x$ remains. For this monomial to be identically zero, its coefficient $3+b$ must vanish. Thus $b=-3$, while $a$ can take any value because it does not appear in the result. When $b=-3$, every term of $Q$ is indeed the opposite of the corresponding term of $P$.
