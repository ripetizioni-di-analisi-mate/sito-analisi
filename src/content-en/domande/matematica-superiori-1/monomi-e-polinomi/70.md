Two rectangles have width $3x$, where $x>0$. Their heights are $x+4$ and $x+1$. How much do their areas differ by?

- $3 x$
- $9 x$
- $9 x^{2}$
- $3 x \left(2 x + 5\right)$

## Explanation

Both areas have the factor $3x$. Multiply the common width by the difference in heights, which is $3$.

$$
3x(x+4)-3x(x+1)=3x(4-1)=9x
$$
