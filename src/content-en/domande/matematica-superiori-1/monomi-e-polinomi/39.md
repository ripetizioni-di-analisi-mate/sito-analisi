Which of the following polynomials can be written as $x^2Q(x)$ with $Q$ a polynomial?

- $x^4-3x$
- $x^3-3$
- $x^4-3x^2$
- $x^5+x^2+x$

## Explanation

Multiplying a polynomial by $x^2$ increases the exponent of each term by two. Thus, in the expansion, every nonzero term must contain at least $x^2$. The polynomial $x^4-3x^2$ meets this condition and equals $x^2(x^2-3)$. The others have a linear or constant term, which would require a negative exponent in $Q$.
