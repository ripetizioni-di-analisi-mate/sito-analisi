A sensor computes $P(x)=x^3-2x+4$, using $P(0)$ as its reference reading. Which expression gives the change at input $t$ from this reference?

- $t^3-2t$
- $t^3-2t+4$
- $4-t^3+2t$
- $(t-4)^3-2t$

## Explanation

The reference is $4$. Subtracting it from $P(t)$ removes the constant term, giving a change of $t^3-2t$.
