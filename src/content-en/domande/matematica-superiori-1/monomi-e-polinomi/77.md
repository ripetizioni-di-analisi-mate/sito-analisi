The expression $(2x^2-3x+1)-(x^2+2x-4)+(-x^2+5x)$ is simplified. What is the result?

- $5$
- $2x^2+4x-3$
- $4x-3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The minus sign in front of $(x^2+2x-4)$ changes the sign of all three of its terms, while the plus sign in front of $(-x^2+5x)$ leaves them unchanged. This gives

$$
2x^2-3x+1-x^2-2x+4-x^2+5x .
$$

The second-degree terms add up to $(2-1-1)x^2=0$ and the first-degree terms add up to $(-3-2+5)x=0$, so only the constant terms remain, with sum $1+4=5$. The value $-3$ comes from leaving the term $-4$ of the subtracted polynomial unchanged.

Therefore the simplified form is $5$.
