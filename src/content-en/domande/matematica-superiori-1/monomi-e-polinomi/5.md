For what value of $k$ are the monomials $kx^2y$ and $-4x^2y$ additive inverses?

- $4$
- $0$
- $-4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Two monomials are additive inverses if their sum is the zero monomial. Since their variable parts agree, the coefficient of their sum is $k-4$.

$$
(k-4)x^2y=0\text{ for every }x,y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=4
$$
