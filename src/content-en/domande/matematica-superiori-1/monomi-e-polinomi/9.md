Let $M=2x$ and $N=-3x^2$. For which real values of $x$ is the product $MN$ positive?

- $x<0$
- $x>0$
- $x\ne0$
- $x\le0$

## Explanation

The product is $-6x^3$. The power $x^3$ has the same sign as $x$, because $x^2>0$ for $x\ne0$. The coefficient $-6$ reverses that sign, so the product is positive when $x<0$. At $x=0$ the product is zero, and for $x>0$ it is strictly negative. For example, $M(-1)=-2$ and $N(-1)=-3$, so their product at that point is $6$.
