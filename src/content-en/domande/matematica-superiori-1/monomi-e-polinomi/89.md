What is the set of all inputs at which the graphs of $P(x)=x^2-4$ and $-P(x)$ meet?

- $x=-2$ and $x=2$
- $x=0$
- $x=2$
- $x=-4$ and $x=4$

## Explanation

At an intersection we have $P(x)=-P(x)$, so $2P(x)=0$ and hence $P(x)=0$. With the given polynomial this means solving $x^2=4$. Both $-2$ and $2$ have square $4$. The shared vertical coordinate is zero at both points, while at $x=0$ the values are $-4$ and $4$. Both intersections therefore lie on the horizontal axis at the two zeros of the polynomial.
