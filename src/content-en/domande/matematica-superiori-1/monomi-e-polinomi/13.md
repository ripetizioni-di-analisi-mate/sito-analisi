As the real parameter $a$ varies, for which value of $a$ is the quotient $\dfrac{6x^3}{(a-2)x}$ undefined for every real $x$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $6$

## Explanation

For the denominator to vanish at every value of $x$, the factor $a-2$ must be zero. This gives $a=2$. For every other value of the parameter, one can choose $x=1$, for example. At that point the denominator is $a-2\ne0$, so the quotient is defined. The fact that $x=0$ is always excluded rules out a single value of $x$.
