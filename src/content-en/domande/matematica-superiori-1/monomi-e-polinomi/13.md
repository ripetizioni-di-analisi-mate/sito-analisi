For $x\ne0$ and $y\ne0$, simplify $\dfrac{15x^5y^3}{5x^2y}$.

- $3 x^{3} y^{2}$
- $3 x^{7} y^{4}$
- $3 x^{3} y^{3}$
- $5 x^{3} y^{2}$

## Explanation

The stated conditions make the denominator nonzero. Divide the coefficients and subtract the exponents of matching variables. The original conditions still apply after simplification.

$$
\frac{15}{5}=3,\qquad x^{5-2}=x^3,\qquad y^{3-1}=y^2
$$

The result is $3 x^{3} y^{2}$.
