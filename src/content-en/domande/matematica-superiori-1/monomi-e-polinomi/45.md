A rectangle has width $2x+3$ and height $x-1$, where $x>1$. Which polynomial gives its area?

- $2 x^{2} + x - 3$
- $2 x^{2} + 5 x - 3$
- $2 x^{2} + x + 3$
- $6 x + 4$

## Explanation

Multiply the two dimensions. The two linear products are $-2x$ and $3x$, so the coefficient of $x$ is one. The constant term is $3(-1)=-3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=(2x+3)(x-1) \\[6pt]&=2x^2-2x+3x-3 \\[6pt]&=2x^2+x-3
\end{aligned}
$$
