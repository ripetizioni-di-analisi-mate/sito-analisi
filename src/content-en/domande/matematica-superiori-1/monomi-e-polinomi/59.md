Which pair $(P,Q)$ consists of two degree-$4$ polynomials whose sum has degree $1$?

- $(x^4+2x,\;x^4+1)$
- $(x^4+x^2,\;-x^4+1)$
- $(x^4+2x,\;-x^4+1)$
- $(x^4+2x,\;-x^4-2x+1)$

## Explanation

Each polynomial must retain degree $4$, while the fourth-degree terms must cancel in the sum. This requires opposite coefficients of $x^4$. The pair $(x^4+2x,-x^4+1)$ gives $2x+1$, of degree $1$. The other pairs reduce to a sum of degree $4$, a sum of degree $2$, or a constant. Even after the fourth-degree terms cancel, the remaining terms must still be checked.
