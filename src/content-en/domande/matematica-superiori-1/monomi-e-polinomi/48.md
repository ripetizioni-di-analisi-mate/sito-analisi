Given $P(x)=2 x^{2} - 3 x + 5$, calculate $P(-3)$.

- $14$
- $-4$
- $20$
- $32$

## Explanation

Substitute the same value for every occurrence of the variable. The square of a negative number is positive. The linear term with a negative coefficient becomes positive at the negative input.

$$
P(-3)=2\cdot(-3)^2-3\cdot(-3)+5=32
$$

The result is $32$.
