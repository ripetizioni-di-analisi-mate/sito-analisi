For what value of $c$ does $c(x+1)+2(x-1)=5x+1$ hold for every $x$?

- $-3$
- $2$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

On the left-hand side, the coefficient of $x$ is $c+2$ and the constant term is $c-2$. When $c=3$, these equal $5$ and $1$, so the identity holds for every real number.

$$
c(x+1)+2(x-1)=(c+2)x+(c-2)
$$
