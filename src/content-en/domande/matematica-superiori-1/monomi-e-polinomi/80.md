Find the coefficient of $x^2$ in the difference $\left(\dfrac{x^2}{3}+2x\right)-\left(\dfrac{5x^2}{6}-x\right)$.

- $\dfrac{7}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The subtraction applies to the entire second polynomial. The linear terms are collected separately. For the quadratic coefficient, express both fractions with the common denominator $6$.

$$
\frac{1}{3}-\frac{5}{6}=\frac{2-5}{6}=-\frac12
$$
