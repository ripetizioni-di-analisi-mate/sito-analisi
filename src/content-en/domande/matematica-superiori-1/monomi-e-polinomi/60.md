What is the degree of $P(x)=(x+2)(x-2)-x^2+3x$ in simplified form?

- $0$
- $2$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $x^2-4$. The two quadratic terms cancel. The term $3x$ remains and its degree exceeds the degree of the constant term.

$$
P(x)=x^2-4-x^2+3x=3x-4
$$
