For $P(x)=x^2-3x+2$, calculate $P(2)-P(-2)$.

- $12$
- $-12$
- $0$
- $-8$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=2$ and $x=-2$ gives the same value for $x^2$ and the constant term. Therefore, the only differing contributions are $-6$ and $6$ in the linear term.

$$
P(2)-P(-2)=(4-6+2)-(4+6+2)=-12
$$
