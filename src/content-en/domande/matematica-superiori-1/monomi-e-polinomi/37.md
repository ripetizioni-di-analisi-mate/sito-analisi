What is the sum of the coefficients of the simplified polynomial $P(x)=-2x(3x^2-x+4)$?

- $-12$
- $-16$
- $6$
- $0$

## Explanation

At $x=1$ every power of $x$ equals one. The value $P(1)$ therefore equals the sum of the coefficients even when $P$ is still written as a product. Evaluating $-2(3-1+4)$ gives $-12$. Expanding confirms the result because the coefficients of $-6x^3+2x^2-8x$ add to $-12$. The same substitution works for any polynomial in one variable.
