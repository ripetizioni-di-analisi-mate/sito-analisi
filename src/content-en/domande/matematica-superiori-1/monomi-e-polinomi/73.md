For which value of $c$ are the monomials $(7-c)x^2$ and $2x^2$ equal for every $x$?

- $5$
- $9$
- $-3$
- $7$

## Explanation

Two monomials with the same variable part are identical when their coefficients match. Set the unknown coefficient equal to two. The resulting equation determines c.

$$
7-c=2,\qquad c=5
$$

The result is $5$.
