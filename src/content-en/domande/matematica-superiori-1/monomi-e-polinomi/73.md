For which real pair $(m,n)$ does the identity $(mx+n)-(x-2)=2x-5$ hold for every $x$?

- $(3,-7)$
- $(1,-3)$
- $(3,-3)$
- $(2,-5)$

## Explanation

On the left, subtraction changes $x-2$ into $-x+2$, giving $(m-1)x+(n+2)$. For an identity, both the linear coefficient and the constant term must match. Solving $m-1=2$ and $n+2=-5$ gives $m=3$ and $n=-7$. Substituting those values gives $2x-5$ for every $x$. Matching only the constant term would leave the linear coefficient unrestricted.
