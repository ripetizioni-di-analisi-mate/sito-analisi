For $x\ne0$, which value of $n\in\mathbb{N}$ makes the quotient $\dfrac{10x^5}{2x^n}$ a monomial of degree $2$?

- $2$
- $5$
- $3$
- $7$

## Explanation

The coefficient of the quotient is $5$. The remaining power is $x^{5-n}$, so a degree of $2$ requires $5-n=2$.

$$
5-n=2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=3
$$
