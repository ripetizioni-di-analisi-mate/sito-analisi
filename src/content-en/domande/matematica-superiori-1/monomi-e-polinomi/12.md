Simplifying $Q(x,y)=\dfrac{12x^2y^3}{4y^3}$ gives $3x^2$. Which pair $(x,y)$ makes $Q$ defined and equal to $0$?

- $(0,0)$
- $(2,0)$
- $(2,-1)$
- $(0,2)$

## Explanation

The denominator $4y^3$ is nonzero when $y\ne0$ and puts no restriction on $x$. For those values the quotient equals $3x^2$, so it vanishes when $x=0$. The pair $(0,2)$ meets both conditions. The original denominator is zero at $(0,0)$, even though the simplified expression gives $0$.
