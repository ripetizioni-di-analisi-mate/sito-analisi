Let $A=(-2xy)^2$ and $B=-(2xy)^2$. Which relation holds for all real values of the variables?

- $B=A$
- $B=-A$
- $B=2A$
- $B=-\dfrac A2$

## Explanation

In $A$ the whole monomial $-2xy$ is squared, giving $A=4x^2y^2$. In $B$ the minus sign is outside the power, so $B=-4x^2y^2$. The two expressions are opposites. In particular, at $x=y=1$ they give $4$ and $-4$, so their values differ at that point. Setting either variable to zero makes both $A$ and $B$ zero.
