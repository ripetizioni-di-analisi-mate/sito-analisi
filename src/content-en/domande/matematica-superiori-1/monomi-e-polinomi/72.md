Which values of $p,q$ make $px^2+qx+1$ equal to $1$ at both $x=1$ and $x=-1$?

- $p=1,\ q=-1$
- $p=-1,\ q=1$
- $p=q=1$
- $p=q=0$

## Explanation

The two substitutions give $p+q=0$ and $p-q=0$. Adding them gives $p=0$, and hence $q=0$.
