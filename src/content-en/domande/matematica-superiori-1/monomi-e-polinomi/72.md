For which values of $p$ and $q$ does the polynomial $px^2+qx+1$ equal $1$ both for $x=1$ and for $x=-1$?

- $p=1,\ q=-1$
- $p=-1,\ q=1$
- $p=q=1$
- $p=q=0$

## Explanation

For $x=1$ the polynomial equals $p+q+1$, while for $x=-1$ the term $qx$ changes sign and the polynomial equals $p-q+1$. Both values must equal $1$ at the same time, so, subtracting $1$ from both sides, we obtain the system

$$
\begin{cases}
p+q=0\\
p-q=0
\end{cases}
$$

Adding the two equations gives $2p=0$, that is, $p=0$, and the first equation then gives $q=0$. The pairs $p=1,\ q=-1$ and $p=-1,\ q=1$ satisfy only the first equation.

Therefore $p=q=0$.
