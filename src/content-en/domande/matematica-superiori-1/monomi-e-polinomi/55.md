Let $P(x)=(x-3)(x+5)+2$. What is its constant term in expanded form?

- $-15$
- $2$
- $-13$
- $17$

## Explanation

The constant term is the value at $x=0$, because each term containing $x$ vanishes there. Substitution gives it directly from the expression with the product.

$$
P(0)=(-3)\cdot5+2=-13
$$
