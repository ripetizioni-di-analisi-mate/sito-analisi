For which value of $k$ does the polynomial $(k-2)x^3+4x^2+1$ have degree exactly $2$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $4$

## Explanation

For the degree to be $2$, the coefficient of $x^3$ must vanish. The term $4x^2$ remains present for every value of $k$.

$$
k-2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=2
$$
