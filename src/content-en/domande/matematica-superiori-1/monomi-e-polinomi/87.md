Reading upwards from the constant term, what is the first exponent at which $x^4+x^2+1$ and $x^4-x+1$ have different coefficients?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The constant terms agree. The coefficient of $x$ is zero in the first polynomial and $-1$ in the second, so the first difference occurs at degree one.
