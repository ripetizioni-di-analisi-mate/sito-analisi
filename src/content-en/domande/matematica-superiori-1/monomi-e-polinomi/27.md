Which simplification correctly combines the like terms in $\dfrac12xy^2-\dfrac34x^2y-xy^2+\dfrac14x^2y$?

- $-xy^2$
- $-\dfrac12xy^2+\dfrac12x^2y$
- $-\dfrac12xy^2-\dfrac12x^2y$
- $\dfrac12xy^2-\dfrac12x^2y$

## Explanation

The terms containing $xy^2$ have coefficients $\dfrac12$ and $-1$, which sum to $-\dfrac12$. The $x^2y$ group gives $-\dfrac34+\dfrac14=-\dfrac12$. The two groups stay separate because combining them would require matching exponents for each variable. At $(2,1)$ the final contributions are $-1$ and $-2$. That substitution also shows the two variable parts producing different values.
