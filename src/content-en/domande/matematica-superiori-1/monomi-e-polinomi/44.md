Let $u$ and $v$ be real parameters. Which formula equals $(x+u)(x+v)-x(x+u+v)$ after simplification in $x$, for every choice of $u$ and $v$?

- $u+v$
- $x(u+v)$
- $0$
- $uv$

## Explanation

The first product contributes $x^2$, $ux$, $vx$ and $uv$. The second product contains the first three contributions, which cancel in the difference. This leaves $uv$, which is constant with respect to $x$. It can also be recovered by setting $x=0$, once all terms involving $x$ have been shown to cancel. The formula gives zero whenever at least one of the two parameters is zero.
