Let $Q(x)=-P(x)$ and $P(-2)=7$. What is $(P-2Q)(-2)$?

- $-7$
- $21$
- $7$
- $-21$

## Explanation

Evaluating the additive inverse at $-2$ gives $Q(-2)=-7$. Subtracting twice that value adds $14$ to $P(-2)$.

$$
(P-2Q)(-2)=7-2(-7)=21
$$
