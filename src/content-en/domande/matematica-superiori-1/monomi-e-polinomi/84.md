The monomial $M(x,y)$ has coefficient $-\dfrac12$, total degree $4$ and value $-27$ at $(x,y)=(2,3)$. What is $M$?

- $-\dfrac12x^3y$
- $-\dfrac12x^2y^2$
- $-\dfrac12y^4$
- $-\dfrac12xy^3$

## Explanation

The variable part must equal $54$ at the given point, because the coefficient multiplies it by $-\dfrac12$. Of the candidates, $xy^3$ gives $2\cdot27=54$. The variable parts $x^3y$, $x^2y^2$ and $y^4$ give $24$, $36$ and $81$. All of the proposed monomials have degree $4$, so evaluation is needed to distinguish them. The condition on the coefficient determines the sign of all four values at the specified point.
