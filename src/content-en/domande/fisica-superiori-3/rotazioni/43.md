A brake applies a torque of $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ over a positive angular displacement of $5\,\mathrm{rad}$. Find its work.

- $15\,\mathrm J$
- $-0.6\,\mathrm J$
- $-15\,\mathrm J$
- $-8\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The angular displacement is positive and the braking torque is negative. Their product is therefore negative. Its magnitude gives the energy the brake removes from the motion in this interval. The total change in kinetic energy would also include the work of any other torques.

$$
W_{\mathrm f}=M_{\mathrm f}\Delta\theta=(-3)\cdot5=-15\,\mathrm J.
$$
