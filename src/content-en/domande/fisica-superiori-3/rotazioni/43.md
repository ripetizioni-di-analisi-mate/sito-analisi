A brake applies a torque of $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ over a positive angular displacement of $5\,\mathrm{rad}$. Find its work.

- $-30\,\mathrm J$
- $-7.5\,\mathrm J$
- $-15\,\mathrm J$
- $15\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The opposing signs make the energy transfer negative. Braking removes mechanical energy from the rotor.

$$
W=M\Delta\theta,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=\left(-3\right) \cdot 5\\[4pt]&=-15\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
