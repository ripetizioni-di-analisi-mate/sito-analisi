What condition ensures that the angular momentum component along a fixed axis has the same value at the beginning and end of an interval?

- Moment of inertia constant by definition
- The same mass at all points
- Zero total external torque impulse about that axis
- Zero angular velocity

## Explanation

The change in the axial component of angular momentum equals the impulse of the external torques along the axis. If the total impulse is zero, the two endpoint readings agree. Intermediate changes are possible when opposing torques act at different times and their impulses cancel.
