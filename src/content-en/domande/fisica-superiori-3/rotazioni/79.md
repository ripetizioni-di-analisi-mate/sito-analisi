Which condition conserves the angular momentum component along an axis?

- Moment of inertia constant by definition
- The same mass at all points
- Zero total external torque impulse about that axis
- Zero angular velocity

## Explanation

Angular momentum changes by the external torque impulse about the axis. It has equal endpoint values when that impulse is zero.
