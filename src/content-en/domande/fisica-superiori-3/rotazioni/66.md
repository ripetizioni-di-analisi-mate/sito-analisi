What does crossing from positive to negative $\omega$ indicate?

- A mass change
- Reversal of the rotation
- Reversal of the chosen positive sense
- Increased inertia

## Explanation

With a fixed axis and sign convention, angular velocity's sign gives the actual sense of rotation.
