With the same axis and sign convention, $\omega$ goes from positive to negative. What has happened?

- A mass change
- Reversal of the rotation
- Reversal of the chosen positive sense
- Increased inertia

## Explanation

The sign of omega gives the direction of rotation relative to the fixed convention. The first value describes rotation in the direction chosen as positive. The second describes the opposite direction. If the angular velocity changes continuously, it reaches zero at least once between the two readings.
