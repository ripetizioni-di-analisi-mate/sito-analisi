A rolling disc does not reach the same centre speed as a frictionless sliding block starting at the same vertical drop. Why?

- The disc loses all energy to friction
- Some of the disc's energy goes into rotation
- The radius cancels gravity
- The block gains no kinetic energy

## Explanation

Gravity converts the same potential energy per unit mass. In the disc the kinetic energy is divided between translation and rotation.
