A wheel of inertia $0.80\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ slows with $\alpha=-2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Find the signed net torque.

- $-1.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-3.2\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-0.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The wheel has positive, constant inertia. The net torque is the inertia times the angular acceleration, so it inherits the negative sign. Here the slowing down implies that the wheel was rotating in the positive direction. The required sign follows directly from the given acceleration.

$$
M_z=I\alpha=0.80\cdot(-2)=-1.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}.
$$
