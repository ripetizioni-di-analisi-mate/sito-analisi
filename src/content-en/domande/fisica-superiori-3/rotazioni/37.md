A wheel of inertia $0.80\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ slows with $\alpha=-2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Find the signed net torque.

- $-1.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-3.2\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-0.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

Inertia is positive, so torque has the sign of angular acceleration. A negative torque acts against the chosen positive sense.

$$
M=I\alpha,\qquad
\begin{aligned}M&=- \frac{4}{5} \cdot 2\\[4pt]&=-1.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}.\end{aligned}
$$
