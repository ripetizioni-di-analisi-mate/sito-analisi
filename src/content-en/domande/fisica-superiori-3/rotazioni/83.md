A tyre rolls forward without slipping on a stationary road. What is the instantaneous velocity of the contact point relative to the road?

- Twice the centre velocity
- Equal to the centre velocity
- Zero
- Vertically upwards

## Explanation

At the bottom of the wheel, rotation about the centre gives a backward velocity. The centre moves forwards at the same speed because the wheel rolls without slipping. The contributions cancel relative to the road, so the contact point is instantaneously at rest.
