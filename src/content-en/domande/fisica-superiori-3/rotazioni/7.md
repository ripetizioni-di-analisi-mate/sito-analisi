A wheel of radius $R=0.26\,\mathrm m$ rolls without slipping on a stationary surface with $|\omega|=6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the speed of its centre?

- $156\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.56\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.78\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the bottom of the wheel, rotation moves the rim backwards relative to the centre. The surface is stationary, so the forward speed of the centre must exactly cancel that contribution. The radius times the angular speed gives the speed of the centre in metres per second.

$$
v_{CM}=|\omega|R=6\cdot0.26=1.56\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
