A wheel of radius $R=0.26\,\mathrm m$ rolls without slipping on a stationary surface with $\omega=6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find the speed of its centre.

- $3.12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.78\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.56\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1.56\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The contact point is momentarily at rest relative to the surface. Translation and rotation cancel there, so centre speed is the given product.

$$
v_{CM}=|\omega|R,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{CM}&=13 \cdot \frac{1}{50} \cdot 6\\[4pt]&=1.56\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
