A rotor with movable masses initially has $I_i=3\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and $\omega_i=2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. After the masses shift, measurements give $I_f=1.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and $\omega_f=3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What signed angular impulse did the external torques supply along the axis?

- $-1.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m\cdot s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m\cdot s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m\cdot s}$
- $10.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The two measurements allow the angular momentum before and after the shift of the masses to be compared. It falls from six to four point five in angular momentum units. The external impulse is the final value minus the initial one, giving a negative result.

$$
\begin{aligned}J_M&=L_f-L_i=I_f\omega_f-I_i\omega_i\\[6pt]&=1.5\cdot3-3\cdot2=-1.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m\cdot s}.\end{aligned}
$$
