Why can the tensions differ for a real accelerating pulley?

- Their difference supplies the net torque
- Its radius changes at every instant
- The pulley cancels weight
- The rope is always heavier than the pulley

## Explanation

Finite inertia and nonzero acceleration require a nonzero torque. The opposing tensions provide the required difference.
