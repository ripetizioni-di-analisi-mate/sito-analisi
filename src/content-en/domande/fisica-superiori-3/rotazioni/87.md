Which reaches higher speed down the same vertical drop, a solid disc or a ring, if both roll without losses?

- The ring
- Equal speeds
- The solid disc
- It depends on the absolute radius

## Explanation

The disc has a lower relative inertia ratio. A smaller ratio leaves more of the energy in centre translation.
