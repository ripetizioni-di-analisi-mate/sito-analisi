A uniform solid disc and a thin ring start from rest on a $45^\circ$ incline. The coefficient of static friction is $0.40$ for both. Which body can maintain rolling without slipping from the start?

- The ring only
- Both
- The solid disc only
- Neither

## Explanation

On an incline the static friction threshold is the tangent of the slope angle times the inertia coefficient divided by one plus that coefficient. The tangent of forty-five degrees is one. Available friction exceeds the disc’s threshold of one third and falls below the ring’s threshold of one half.

$$
\mu_{s,\min}=\frac{b}{1+b}\tan45^\circ,\qquad\frac13<0.40<\frac12.
$$
