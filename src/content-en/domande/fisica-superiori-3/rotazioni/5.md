A body has moment of inertia $I=0.35\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and rotates at $\omega=6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find its rotational kinetic energy.

- $6.3\,\mathrm J$
- $12.6\,\mathrm J$
- $1.05\,\mathrm J$
- $-6.3\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Rotational energy uses the square of the angular velocity. Here the square is thirty-six and it is multiplied by half the moment of inertia. Reversing the rotation while keeping its magnitude would give the same energy, just as it does for translational kinetic energy.

$$
K_{\mathrm{rot}}=\frac12I\omega^2=\frac12\cdot0.35\cdot6^2=6.3\,\mathrm J.
$$
