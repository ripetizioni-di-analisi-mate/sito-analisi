A body has moment of inertia $I=0.35\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and rotates at $\omega=6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find its rotational kinetic energy.

- $6.3\,\mathrm J$
- $12.6\,\mathrm J$
- $3.15\,\mathrm J$
- $-6.3\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Square the angular speed and multiply it by half the moment of inertia. The result is in joules.

$$
K_{\mathrm{rot}}=\frac12I\omega^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}K_{\mathrm{rot}}&=\frac{7 \cdot 6^{2}}{2 \cdot 20}\\[4pt]&=6.3\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
