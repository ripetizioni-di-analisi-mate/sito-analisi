A constant $5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ torque produces $\alpha=20\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Find the rotor's inertia.

- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $-0.25\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

Inertia is torque divided by angular acceleration about the same axis. At fixed torque, high acceleration indicates low inertia.

$$
I=\frac M\alpha,\qquad
\begin{aligned}I&=\frac{5}{20}\\[4pt]&=0.25\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
