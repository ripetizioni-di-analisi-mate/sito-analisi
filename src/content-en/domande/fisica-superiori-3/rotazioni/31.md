Point masses of $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ are $0.30$ and $0.20\,\mathrm m$ from the axis. Find total inertia.

- $0.18\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

The distances are measured from the same axis. Each mass contributes its own mass times its distance squared. The first mass is smaller, but it is farther away and gives the greater contribution. Add the two contributions once they have been calculated separately.

$$
I=2\cdot(0.30)^2+3\cdot(0.20)^2=0.30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}.
$$
