A wheel centre moves forward at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the rim moves tangentially at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the centre in the sense of forward rolling. Find the bottom-point velocity, forward positive.

- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the bottom the rotational contribution points backwards. The negative result shows that the contact point slips backwards against the ground.

$$
v_c=v_{CM}-\left\lvert\omega\right\rvert{}R,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_c&=2 - 3\\[4pt]&=-1\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
