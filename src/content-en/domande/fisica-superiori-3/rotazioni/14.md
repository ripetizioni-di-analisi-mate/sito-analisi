A system changes from moment of inertia $I_i=3.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ to $I_f=1.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ with no external axial torque. It initially rotates at $\omega_i=2.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find $\omega_f$.

- $12.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-6.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Angular momentum stays constant without external torque. Divide its initial value by the final moment of inertia to obtain angular velocity.

$$
\omega_f=\frac{I_i\omega_i}{I_f},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\omega_f&=16 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot 14 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{1}{7 \cdot \frac{1}{5}}\\[4pt]&=6.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
