Two flywheels on the same axis rotate at $-4$ and $+22\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and have moments of inertia of $1.2$ and $0.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ respectively. An internal clutch engages until they rotate together. The angular impulse from external torques along the axis is negligible. What is the final signed angular velocity?

- $9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $11.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-6.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The clutch acts within the two-flywheel system and total angular momentum along the axis is conserved during engagement. Add the signed initial contributions. After engagement the angular velocity is shared, so the relevant inertia is the sum of the two moments of inertia.

$$
\begin{aligned}L_i&=1.2(-4)+0.8(22)=12.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}\\[6pt]\omega_f&=\frac{12.8}{1.2+0.8}=6.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
