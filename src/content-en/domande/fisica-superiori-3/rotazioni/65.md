A disc turns at constant nonzero angular velocity. What acceleration do points on its rim have?

- Radial towards the axis
- Tangential in the direction of motion
- Zero acceleration
- Radial away from the axis

## Explanation

The speed at the rim is constant, so there is no tangential acceleration. The velocity direction changes at every instant. The acceleration magnitude is proportional to the radius and the square of the angular velocity. Its direction is radial and points towards the centre.
