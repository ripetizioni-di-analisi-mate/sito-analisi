A disc rotates at constant angular velocity. What acceleration does a rim point have?

- Radial towards the axis
- Tangential in the direction of motion
- Zero acceleration
- Radial away from the axis

## Explanation

Velocity changes direction at constant speed. The acceleration associated with that change is radially inward.
