Which measurement indicates slipping on a stationary floor?

- The contact point has nonzero velocity relative to the floor
- The centre moves
- The top point moves forward
- The measured angle grows

## Explanation

A surface imposes zero relative contact velocity only during pure rolling. Motion at the other points is compatible with that constraint.
