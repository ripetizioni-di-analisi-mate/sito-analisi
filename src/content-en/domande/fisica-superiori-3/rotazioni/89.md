What measurement indicates slipping for a wheel in contact with a stationary floor?

- The contact point has nonzero velocity relative to the floor
- The centre moves
- The top point moves forward
- The measured angle grows

## Explanation

Contact without slipping requires zero relative velocity between the point on the wheel and the floor. Measuring a nonzero velocity at the contact point detects slipping. The centre and the upper point can have nonzero velocities even while the constraint holds.
