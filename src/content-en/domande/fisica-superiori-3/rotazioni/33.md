A motor applies a constant torque of $+3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ to a rotor with moment of inertia $0.50\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. The angular velocity of the rotor increases uniformly from $3$ to $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in $2\,\mathrm s$. Apart from the motor torque, only a resistive torque acts. What is the resistive torque, with its sign?

- $-2.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity increases uniformly, so the angular acceleration is the ratio of the change in angular velocity to the time. The product of moment of inertia and angular acceleration gives the net torque. This is smaller than the motor torque, so the resistive torque is negative and equals the difference between the two values.

$$
\begin{aligned}\alpha&=\frac{5-3}{2}=1\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}\\[6pt]M_{\mathrm{r}}&=I\alpha-M_{\mathrm{m}}\\[6pt]&=0.50\cdot1-3=-2.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}.\end{aligned}
$$
