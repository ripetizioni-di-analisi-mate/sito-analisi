How does the kinetic energy of a rotor of constant inertia change if $\omega$ changes sign and keeps the same magnitude?

- It changes sign
- It doubles
- It remains unchanged
- It decreases to zero

## Explanation

Energy uses the square of the angular velocity. The two velocities of opposite sign have the same square and the same energy at equal inertia. A measurement of angular momentum along the axis would distinguish the two directions because it depends linearly on velocity.
