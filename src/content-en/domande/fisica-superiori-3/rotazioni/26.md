A motor turns a quarter revolution. What arc length does a point $0.20\,\mathrm m$ from the axis travel?

- $\dfrac{2\pi}{5}\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{10}\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{5}\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{20}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

A quarter turn is one quarter of a full angle, so it equals half of pi radians. The arc length is the radius multiplied by this angle. The final unit is metres. The same calculation takes one quarter of the circumference.

$$
s=R\Delta\theta=0.20\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{\pi}{10}\,\mathrm m.
$$
