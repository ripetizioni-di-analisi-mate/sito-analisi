A motor turns a quarter revolution. What arc length does a point $0.20\,\mathrm m$ from the axis travel?

- $0.628319\,\mathrm m$
- $0.314159\,\mathrm m$
- $0.15708\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.314159\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

A quarter turn is half pi radians. Multiply that angle by the radius to obtain arc length.

$$
s=R\Delta\theta,\qquad
\begin{aligned}s&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot \pi \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&\simeq0.314159\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
