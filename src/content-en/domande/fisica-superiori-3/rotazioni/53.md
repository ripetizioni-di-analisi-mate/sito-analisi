A wheel rolls rightwards without slipping on a fixed surface at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The radius to point $P$ is $30^\circ$ above the forward horizontal. What is the vertical component of the velocity of $P$, positive upwards?

- $-2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The centre velocity is horizontal, so only rotation contributes to the vertical component. The tangent velocity points downwards and to the right. Its magnitude is four by the rolling constraint and it makes thirty degrees with the vertical, so the vertical projection uses the cosine.

$$
v_{P,y}=-|\omega|R\cos30^\circ=-4\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=-2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
