A flywheel of inertia $3\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ rotates in the positive direction at $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. For $2\,\mathrm s$, it receives constant net mechanical power of $9\,\mathrm W$. What angular velocity does it reach?

- $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Constant power supplies eighteen joules in two seconds. Add it to the six joules already stored and find the angular velocity from the new energy. The direction remains positive because energy increases from a positive initial value throughout the interval.

$$
\begin{aligned}K_f&=\frac12\cdot3\cdot2^2+9\cdot2=24\,\mathrm J\\[6pt]\omega_f&=+\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot24}{3}}=4\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
