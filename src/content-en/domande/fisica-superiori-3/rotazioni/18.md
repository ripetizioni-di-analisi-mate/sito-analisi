A disc rotates steadily at $120$ revolutions per minute. Find its angular speed in radians per second.

- $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $240\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

One hundred and twenty revolutions per minute equal two revolutions per second, because a minute contains sixty seconds. Each revolution is two pi radians. Multiplying by two gives the angular velocity in radians per second. The radius would be needed to convert it to tangential speed.

$$
\omega=\frac{120}{60}\cdot2\pi=4\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}.
$$
