A disc rotates steadily at $120$ revolutions per minute. Find its angular speed in radians per second.

- $25.1327\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12.5664\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6.28319\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-12.5664\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Convert revolutions to radians and minutes to seconds. The disc makes two revolutions per second.

$$
\omega=2\pi f,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\omega&=120 \cdot 2 \cdot \pi \cdot \frac{1}{60}\\[4pt]&\simeq12.5664\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
