A rigid body rotates about a fixed axis with moment of inertia $I=0.35\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. The net axial torque is $M=2.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. Find the angular acceleration.

- $14.2857\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $3.57143\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $-7.14286\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $7.14\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

Net torque equals $I\alpha$. Divide by the moment of inertia about the same axis.

$$
\alpha=\frac MI,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\alpha&=\frac{5}{2 \cdot \frac{7}{20}}\\[4pt]&\simeq7.14\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
