A rigid body rotates about a fixed axis with moment of inertia $I=0.35\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. The net axial torque is $M=2.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. Find the angular acceleration. Round to two decimal places.

- $0.14\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $0.88\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $-7.14\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $7.14\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The relation between net torque and acceleration applies about the same fixed axis. Divide the axial torque by the inertia, which is positive. The acceleration is positive too. Its sign gives the direction of the change, while the initial angular velocity would determine whether the body slows down.

$$
\alpha=\frac{M}{I}=\frac{2.5}{0.35}\simeq7.14\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}.
$$
