A disc starts from rest at $\alpha=2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. How far does it turn from $t=2\,\mathrm s$ to $t=4\,\mathrm s$?

- $24\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $12\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $6\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The wheel is already moving at two seconds. Subtracting its angle at two seconds from its angle at four isolates the requested interval.

$$
\Delta\theta=\frac12\alpha(t_f^2-t_i^2),\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta\theta&=\frac{2 \cdot \left(- 2^{2} + 4^{2}\right)}{2}\\[4pt]&=12\,\mathrm{rad}.\end{aligned}
$$
