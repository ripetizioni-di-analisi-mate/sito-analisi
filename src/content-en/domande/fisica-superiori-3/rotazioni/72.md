The only two torques on a rotor already in motion have zero sum. The axis is fixed and the inertia stays constant. How does the rotation evolve?

- It stops immediately
- It speeds up in its initial sense
- It reverses
- It continues at constant angular velocity

## Explanation

The two opposing torques sum to zero about the axis, so the angular acceleration is zero. The angular velocity keeps the value it had before the cancellation. Since the rotor was already moving, it continues to rotate with the same direction and magnitude.
