Two opposite torques cancel on an already spinning rotor of constant inertia. What happens?

- It stops immediately
- It speeds up in its initial sense
- It reverses
- It continues at constant angular velocity

## Explanation

Zero net torque gives zero angular acceleration. Initial angular velocity stays unchanged.
