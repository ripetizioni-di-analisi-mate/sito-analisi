Two bodies start from rest and descend through the same vertical drop while rolling without slipping or dissipation. What quantity lets us compare their final speeds without knowing each mass?

- Weight alone
- The ratio $\dfrac{I_{CM}}{mR^2}$
- Radius alone
- The initial angular velocity

## Explanation

The rolling constraint lets us express rotational kinetic energy in terms of the speed of the centre. In the energy balance per unit mass, the inertia enters as a fraction of mass times radius squared. At the same drop, a larger ratio leaves less energy for translation.
