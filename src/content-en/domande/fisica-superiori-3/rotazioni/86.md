Two bodies roll without losses from rest through the same vertical drop. Which quantity compares their final speeds without requiring the individual masses?

- Weight alone
- The ratio $I_{CM}/(mR^2)$
- Radius alone
- Initial angular velocity

## Explanation

Mass cancels in the energy balance. Inertia enters through its ratio to mass times radius squared.
