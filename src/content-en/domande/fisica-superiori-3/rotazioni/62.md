A uniform solid disc rolls uphill without slipping, with an initial centre speed of $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its maximum rise without dissipation at $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $5.4\,\mathrm m$
- $2.7\,\mathrm m$
- $1.35\,\mathrm m$
- $-2.7\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

At the turning point linear and angular velocities are both zero, so all initial kinetic energy becomes potential energy.

$$
h=\frac{3v_i^2}{4g},\qquad
\begin{aligned}h&=\frac{3 \cdot 6^{2}}{4 \cdot 10}\\[4pt]&=2.7\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
