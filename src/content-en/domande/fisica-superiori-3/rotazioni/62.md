A uniform solid disc rolls uphill without slipping, with an initial centre speed of $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Without dissipation and with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its maximum rise in height?

- $1.8\,\mathrm m$
- $2.7\,\mathrm m$
- $0.9\,\mathrm m$
- $3.6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

As the disc climbs, kinetic energy decreases because potential energy rises. At the highest point the centre is momentarily still and the constraint makes the angular velocity zero too. The height gained depends on all the initial kinetic energy, including the rotational contribution.

$$
h_{\max}=\frac{3v_i^2}{4g}=\frac{3\cdot6^2}{4\cdot10}=2.7\,\mathrm m.
$$
