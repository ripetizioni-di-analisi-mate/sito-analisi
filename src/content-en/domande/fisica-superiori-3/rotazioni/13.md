A body has moment of inertia $I=0.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and rotates at $\omega=7\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find its rotational kinetic energy.

- $9.8\,\mathrm J$
- $19.6\,\mathrm J$
- $4.9\,\mathrm J$
- $-9.8\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Square the angular speed and multiply it by half the moment of inertia. The result is in joules.

$$
K_{\mathrm{rot}}=\frac12I\omega^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}K_{\mathrm{rot}}&=\frac{2 \cdot 7^{2}}{2 \cdot 5}\\[4pt]&=9.8\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
