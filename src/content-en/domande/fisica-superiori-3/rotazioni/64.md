On an angular velocity–time graph, what does the local slope represent?

- Angle swept
- Kinetic energy
- Moment of inertia
- Angular acceleration

## Explanation

The local slope is the ratio of a small change in angular velocity to the corresponding duration. This defines instantaneous angular acceleration. Its units are radians per second squared. The signed area relative to the time axis instead gives a change in angle.
