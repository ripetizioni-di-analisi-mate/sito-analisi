A wheel has initial angular velocity $\omega_0=5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and constant angular acceleration $\alpha=0.8\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ for $t=4\,\mathrm s$. What is the final angular velocity?

- $8.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The acceleration stays constant for four seconds. The change in angular velocity is therefore the rate of increase multiplied by the duration. The radians per second gained are added to the initial five. The positive final value means that the wheel keeps its initial direction.

$$
\begin{aligned}\Delta\omega&=0.8\cdot4=3.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}\\[6pt]\omega_f&=5+3.2=8.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
