A wheel has initial angular velocity $\omega_0=5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and constant angular acceleration $\alpha=0.8\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ for $t=4\,\mathrm s$. Find the final angular velocity.

- $8.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $16.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4.1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-8.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

At constant angular acceleration, multiply acceleration by duration to find the change in angular velocity. Add it to the initial value.

$$
\omega=\omega_0+\alpha t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\omega&=4 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot 4 + 5\\[4pt]&=8.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
