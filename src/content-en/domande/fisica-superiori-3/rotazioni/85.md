Why can static friction give torque during pure rolling on a fixed ramp without dissipating energy?

- Its point of application is momentarily stationary
- The friction coefficient is always zero
- The force always passes through the centre
- Mass cancels the friction work

## Explanation

Torque depends on the moment arm to the centre, while power depends on the velocity of the point of application. That point has zero velocity.
