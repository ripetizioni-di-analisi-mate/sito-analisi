A rotor takes two revolutions to speed up from $4$ to $8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ at constant angular acceleration. How long does the interval last?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}\,\mathrm s$
- $\pi\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\,\mathrm s$
- $2\pi\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Two revolutions equal four pi radians. During a linear change, the average angular velocity is half the sum of the endpoint velocities, here six radians per second. The angle swept is that average multiplied by the duration. Dividing gives the time.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{4\pi}{\frac12(4+8)}=\frac{2\pi}{3}\,\mathrm s.
$$
