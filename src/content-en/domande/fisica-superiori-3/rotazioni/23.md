A blade starts at rest and turns through $18\,\mathrm{rad}$ in $3\,\mathrm s$ at constant angular acceleration. Find that acceleration.

- $8\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

Rearrange the angular law to divide twice the displacement by squared time. The result is positive because the angle is positive.

$$
\alpha=\frac{2\Delta\theta}{t^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\alpha&=2 \cdot 18 \cdot \frac{1}{3^{2}}\\[4pt]&=4\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
