Two discs are observed with the same positive direction. A starts at $+8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and B at $+2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Their respective accelerations are constant at $-3$ and $+1\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. After how much time are their angular velocities equal?

- $2\,\mathrm s$
- $6\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac83\,\mathrm s$
- $1.50\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Set the two angular velocities equal at the same instant. A loses three radians per second each second while B gains one. The initial gap of six therefore closes by four per second. When the gap reaches zero, both velocities are three and a half radians per second.

$$
8-3t=2+t,\qquad t=\frac{8-2}{3+1}=1.50\,\mathrm s.
$$
