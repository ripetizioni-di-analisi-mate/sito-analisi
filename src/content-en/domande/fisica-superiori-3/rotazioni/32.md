Two uniform discs have equal masses and a radius ratio of $3$ to $2$. What is the ratio of their moments of inertia about their perpendicular central axes?

- $4.5$
- $1.125$
- $-2.25$
- $2.25$

## Explanation

Equal masses and shape coefficients cancel in the ratio. Radius enters squared.

$$
I_1/I_2=(R_1/R_2)^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}I_1/I_2&=\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{2}\\[4pt]&=2.25.\end{aligned}
$$
