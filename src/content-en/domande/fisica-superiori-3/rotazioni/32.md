Two uniform discs have equal masses and a radius ratio of $3$ to $2$. What is the ratio of their moments of inertia about their perpendicular central axes?

- $1.5$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac23$
- $2.25$

## Explanation

The discs have the same relative mass distribution and the axes have the same position relative to each disc. The factor of one half and the common mass cancel in the ratio. The square of the radius ratio remains, so three halves is squared.

$$
\frac{I_1}{I_2}=\left(\frac{R_1}{R_2}\right)^2=\left(\frac32\right)^2=\frac94=2.25.
$$
