A uniform solid disc has mass $m=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R=0.28\,\mathrm m$. Find its moment of inertia about the central axis perpendicular to the disc.

- $0.4704\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.1176\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.235\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $-0.2352\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

For a uniform disc the coefficient is one half. Multiply that coefficient by mass and radius squared about the axis.

$$
I=\frac12mR^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}I&=\frac{6 \cdot \left(\frac{7}{25}\right)^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&\simeq0.235\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
