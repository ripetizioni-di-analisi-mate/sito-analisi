A disc rotates at $9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ from $t=0$ to $t=2\,\mathrm s$. Between $t=2\,\mathrm s$ and $t=4\,\mathrm s$ its angular velocity varies linearly and reaches $-4.9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is its net angular displacement from $0$ to $4\,\mathrm s$?

::grafico{linee="0,9 2,9 4,-4.9" punti="2,9,pieno;4,-4.9,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=4.65 ymin=-6 ymax=11 nomi="t (s),ω (rad/s)" titolo="Measured angular velocity across the two intervals"}

- $18\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $22.1\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $25.55\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $36\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The first interval gives a rectangle. In the next interval the velocity varies linearly, so the signed area is the average of the endpoint velocities times its duration. The negative final velocity reduces the area of the second interval. Adding magnitudes would give the total angle swept.

$$
\begin{aligned}\Delta\theta_1&=9\cdot2=18\,\mathrm{rad}\\[6pt]\Delta\theta_2&=\frac{9-4.9}{2}\cdot2=4.1\,\mathrm{rad}\\[6pt]\Delta\theta&=(18+4.1)\,\mathrm{rad}=22.1\,\mathrm{rad}.\end{aligned}
$$
