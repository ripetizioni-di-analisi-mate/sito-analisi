A disc starts from rest with constant angular acceleration $\alpha=0.9\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Find the angle swept in $t=7\,\mathrm s$.

- $44.1\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $22.1\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $11.025\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-22.05\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The area under the angular velocity–time graph is a triangle because the disc starts from rest with constant acceleration.

$$
\Delta\theta=\frac12\alpha t^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta\theta&=\frac{9 \cdot 7^{2}}{2 \cdot 10}\\[4pt]&\simeq22.1\,\mathrm{rad}.\end{aligned}
$$
