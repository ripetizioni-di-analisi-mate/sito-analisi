In planar rigid-body motion, about which axis is rotational inertia taken when adding the centre-of-mass translation term?

- An axis through the centre of mass
- Any parallel axis
- An axis one radius away
- Only the axis at the contact surface

## Explanation

The translation follows the centre of mass. For the rotational part, the relative velocities are measured from that centre. With this choice the cross terms in the energy sum cancel. The inertia must therefore be calculated about the axis through the centre of mass, perpendicular to the plane of motion.
