In planar rigid-body motion, about which axis is rotational inertia taken when adding the centre-of-mass translation term?

- An axis through the centre of mass
- Any parallel axis
- An axis one radius away
- Only the axis at the contact surface

## Explanation

Splitting centre motion from motion relative to it puts all translation in the first term. Inertia refers to the axis of the relative motion.
