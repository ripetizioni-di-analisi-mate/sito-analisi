A uniform solid disc has mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R=0.26\,\mathrm m$. Find its moment of inertia about the central axis perpendicular to the disc.

- $0.338\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.0845\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.169\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $-0.169\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

For a uniform disc the coefficient is one half. Multiply that coefficient by mass and radius squared about the axis.

$$
I=\frac12mR^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}I&=\frac{5 \cdot \left(\frac{13}{50}\right)^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=0.169\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
