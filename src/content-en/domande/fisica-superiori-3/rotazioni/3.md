A uniform solid disc has mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R=0.26\,\mathrm m$. What is its moment of inertia about the central axis perpendicular to the disc?

- $0.338\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.507\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.169\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.676\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

The axis passes through the centre and is perpendicular to the face. For a uniform solid disc in this configuration the coefficient is one half. A ring with the same mass and radius would have twice the inertia because all its mass would be at the greatest distance.

$$
I=\frac12mR^2=\frac{5\cdot(0.26)^2}{2}=0.169\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}.
$$
