Two rotors have the same nonzero kinetic energy and different moments of inertia about their fixed axes. Which has the greater magnitude of angular momentum along its axis?

- The lower-inertia rotor
- The higher-inertia rotor
- They are equal
- It depends only on rotation sense

## Explanation

The energy and angular momentum equations give the square of angular momentum as twice the product of inertia and energy. At fixed energy, greater inertia increases that square and therefore its magnitude. Reversing the direction of rotation would change only the sign.
