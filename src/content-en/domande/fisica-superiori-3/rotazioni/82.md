Two rotors have equal kinetic energy and different inertias. Which has the greater angular momentum magnitude?

- The lower-inertia rotor
- The higher-inertia rotor
- They are equal
- It depends only on rotation sense

## Explanation

The relation $L^2=2IK$ gives a magnitude increasing with the square root of inertia at fixed energy.
