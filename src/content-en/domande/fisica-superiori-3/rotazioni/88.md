When the surface no longer exerts a normal force on a wheel, which constraint can fail during free flight?

- The translational kinetic energy expression
- The definition of angular velocity
- The arc–angle relation for a rim point
- Centre speed constrained to radius times angular speed

## Explanation

Without contact the surface no longer imposes its constraint. Translation and rotation can evolve independently.
