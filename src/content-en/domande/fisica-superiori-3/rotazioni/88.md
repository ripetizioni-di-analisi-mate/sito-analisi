When the surface no longer exerts a normal force on a wheel, which constraint can fail during free flight?

- The translational kinetic energy expression
- The definition of angular velocity
- The arc–angle relation for a rim point
- Centre speed constrained to radius times angular speed

## Explanation

The rolling relation follows from contact with a surface and the absence of slipping. During a fall there is no contact, so the speed of the centre and the angular velocity can evolve separately. For example, weight changes the centre’s velocity and exerts no torque about the centre of mass.
