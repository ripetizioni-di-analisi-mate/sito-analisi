A wheel of radius $0.40\,\mathrm m$ rolls without slipping on a moving horizontal belt. In the laboratory its centre moves rightwards at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and it rotates clockwise at an angular speed of $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the belt velocity $u$, taking rightwards as positive?

- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In the laboratory the bottom point has the centre’s motion plus the velocity from the clockwise rotation. At the belt the first points right and the second points left. Since there is no slip, their sum must equal the velocity of the belt.

$$
\begin{aligned}u&=v_{CM}-|\omega|R\\[6pt]&=4-5\cdot0.40=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
