A point $0.25\,\mathrm m$ from the axis rotates at $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find its instantaneous speed.

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For the same angular interval, arc length is proportional to distance from the axis. Dividing the arc by its duration gives the radius times the angular velocity. Here the distance is one quarter of a metre, so the speed is one quarter of twelve.

$$
v=R|\omega|=0.25\cdot12=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
