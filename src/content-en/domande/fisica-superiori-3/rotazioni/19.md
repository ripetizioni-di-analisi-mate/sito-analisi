A point $0.25\,\mathrm m$ from the axis rotates at $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find its instantaneous speed.

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Arc length is angle times radius. The tangential speed is the rate of change of that length.

$$
v=\omega R,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\frac{12}{4}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
