At distance $0.40\,\mathrm m$ from a point, a particle of momentum $5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ moves perpendicular to the radius. Find the magnitude of its angular momentum about the point.

- $2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

The cross product has its maximum magnitude for perpendicular vectors. Multiply their magnitudes directly.

$$
L=rp\sin90^\circ,\qquad
\begin{aligned}L&=2 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot 5\\[4pt]&=2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
