At distance $0.40\,\mathrm m$ from a point, a particle of momentum $5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ moves perpendicular to the radius. What is the magnitude of its angular momentum about the point?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

Momentum is perpendicular to the segment from the reference point to the particle. The sine of the angle is therefore one and the magnitude of the cross product is the product of the magnitudes. Its direction would require knowing the orientation of both vectors in the plane.

$$
|\vec L|=rp\sin90^\circ=0.40\cdot5=2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}.
$$
