A disc starts from rest at $\alpha=4\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. How far does it turn in its first $3\,\mathrm s$?

::grafico{linee="0,0 3,12" area="4*x;0;0;3" xmin=0 xmax=3.5 ymin=0 ymax=15 nomi="t (s),ω (rad/s)" titolo="The triangular area gives the angle swept"}

- $18\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $36\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $9\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Starting from zero gives a triangular graph. Its final height is twelve radians per second and the base is three seconds.

$$
\Delta\theta=\frac12\alpha t^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta\theta&=\frac{4 \cdot 3^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=18\,\mathrm{rad}.\end{aligned}
$$
