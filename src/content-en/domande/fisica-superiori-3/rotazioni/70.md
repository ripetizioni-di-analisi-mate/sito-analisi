A machine has high inertia about its axis. What follows at the same net torque?

- Greater angular acceleration
- Smaller angular acceleration magnitude
- Zero angular momentum at all times
- Higher angular velocity at every instant

## Explanation

The equation of motion gives acceleration inversely proportional to inertia at fixed torque.
