Two rotors with different inertias experience the same constant nonzero net torque about their own fixed axes. What is the effect of the greater inertia?

- Greater angular acceleration
- Smaller angular acceleration magnitude
- Zero angular momentum at all times
- Higher angular velocity at every instant

## Explanation

For a fixed axis, divide the net torque magnitude by the moment of inertia to obtain the angular acceleration magnitude. A larger denominator makes the result smaller at the same torque. Doubling the inertia halves the acceleration and doubles the time needed for the same velocity change.
