A uniform disc of $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ has radius $0.50\,\mathrm m$. Find its moment of inertia about the axis through its centre perpendicular to the disc.

- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

Inertia reflects radial mass distribution. The solid-disc coefficient is half that of a ring with the same mass and radius.

$$
I=\frac12mR^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}I&=\frac{4 \cdot \left(1 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=0.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
