A wheel of radius $0.50\,\mathrm m$ makes three full revolutions without slipping. What distance does the centre travel on a plane?

- $6\pi\,\mathrm m$
- $3\pi\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In rolling without slipping each revolution advances the centre by one circumference, because the translation is the radius times the angle in radians. Three revolutions here equal six pi radians. Multiplying by half a metre gives the distance travelled by the centre.

$$
s=R(3\cdot2\pi)=3\pi\,\mathrm m.
$$
