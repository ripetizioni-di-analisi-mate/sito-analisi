A wheel of radius $0.50\,\mathrm m$ makes three full revolutions without slipping. Find the centre's distance travelled on a plane.

- $18.8496\,\mathrm m$
- $9.42478\,\mathrm m$
- $4.71239\,\mathrm m$
- $-9.42478\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Each turn lays down one circumference of length on the ground. Multiply by the number of complete turns.

$$
s=2\pi Rn,\qquad
\begin{aligned}s&=3 \cdot 2 \cdot \pi \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&\simeq9.42478\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
