A system changes from moment of inertia $I_i=2.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ to $I_f=1.3\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ with no external axial torque. It initially rotates at $\omega_i=2.6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the final angular velocity $\omega_f$?

- $7.54\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $5.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2.6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.17\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

With no external torque along the axis, the product of inertia and angular velocity is conserved. The initial product is seven point five four. It is divided by the new inertia. Angular velocity rises because a larger factor is now needed to give the same angular momentum.

$$
\omega_f=\frac{I_i\omega_i}{I_f}=\frac{2.9\cdot2.6}{1.3}=5.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}.
$$
