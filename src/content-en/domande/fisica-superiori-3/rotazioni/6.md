A system changes from moment of inertia $I_i=2.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ to $I_f=1.3\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ with no external axial torque. It initially rotates at $\omega_i=2.6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find $\omega_f$.

- $11.6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $5.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2.9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-5.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Angular momentum stays constant without external torque. Divide its initial value by the final moment of inertia to obtain angular velocity.

$$
\omega_f=\frac{I_i\omega_i}{I_f},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\omega_f&=29 \cdot \frac{1}{10} \cdot 13 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{1}{13 \cdot \frac{1}{10}}\\[4pt]&=5.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
