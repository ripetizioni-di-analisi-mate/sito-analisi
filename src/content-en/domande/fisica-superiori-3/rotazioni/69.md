Among axes parallel to a given one, which minimises the body's moment of inertia?

- The one through the centre of mass
- The one furthest from the centre
- A rim axis for every shape
- The one where speed is greatest

## Explanation

For parallel axes, the centre-of-mass inertia gains a term equal to the mass times the squared separation of the axes. The added term is positive for every distinct axis. It is zero for the axis through the centre of mass, which therefore has the smallest inertia in the family.
