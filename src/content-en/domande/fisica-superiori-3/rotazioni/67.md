A uniform solid disc and a thin ring have the same mass and radius. Which has the greater moment of inertia about its central axis perpendicular to the plane?

- The disc
- They are equal
- The ring
- It depends on which spins faster

## Explanation

The contribution to inertia grows with the square of the distance from the axis. In a thin ring all the mass is at the outer radius. In the disc some mass is closer in. At equal mass and radius, the ring’s central moment of inertia is twice the disc’s.
