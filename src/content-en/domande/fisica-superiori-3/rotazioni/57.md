An axisymmetric body rolls without slipping. Its rotational kinetic energy about the centre is one quarter of its total kinetic energy. What is the ratio of its central moment of inertia $I$ to $mR^2$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $3$

## Explanation

Three quarters of the energy is translational, so rotational energy is one third of translational energy. Substituting $v=R\omega$ in $\dfrac{I\omega^2}{mv^2}$ gives $\dfrac{I}{mR^2}=\dfrac13$.
