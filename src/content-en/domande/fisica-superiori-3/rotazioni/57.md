A uniform solid disc rolls without slipping with an initial centre speed of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and drops through $0.90\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no dissipation, what is its final centre speed?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\sqrt{12}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\sqrt{22}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The disc is already rolling at the start and has translational and rotational kinetic energy. Gravity increases the sum of the two terms. For the disc the coefficient of mass times speed squared is three quarters in both states, so the contributions add in speed squared.

$$
\begin{aligned}mgh&=\frac34m\left(v_f^2-v_i^2\right)\\[6pt]v_f&=\sqrt{2^2+\frac{4\cdot10\cdot0.90}{3}}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
