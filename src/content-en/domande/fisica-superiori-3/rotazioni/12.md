A rigid body rotates about a fixed axis with moment of inertia $I=0.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. The net axial torque is $M=3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. Find the angular acceleration.

- $15\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $-7.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

Net torque equals $I\alpha$. Divide by the moment of inertia about the same axis.

$$
\alpha=\frac MI,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\alpha&=\frac{3}{2 \cdot \frac{1}{5}}\\[4pt]&=7.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
