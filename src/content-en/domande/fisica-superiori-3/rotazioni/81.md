For a particle whose momentum lies on the line between the particle and the origin, what is angular momentum about that origin?

- Zero
- Maximum for those magnitudes
- Always negative
- Equal to the momentum magnitude alone

## Explanation

The cross product vanishes for collinear vectors. The line of motion goes through the origin.
