For a particle whose momentum lies on the line between the particle and the origin, what is angular momentum about that origin?

- Zero
- Maximum for those magnitudes
- Always negative
- Equal to the momentum magnitude alone

## Explanation

The particle’s angular momentum is the cross product of its position and momentum vectors. If the vectors lie on the same line, the angle between them is zero or one hundred and eighty degrees. The sine is zero in either case, so the cross product is zero.
