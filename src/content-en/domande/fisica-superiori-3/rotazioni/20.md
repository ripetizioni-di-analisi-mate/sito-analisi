A cutter spins at a constant $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. At what radius is centripetal acceleration $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m$
- $0.4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The milling cutter has constant angular velocity, so its tangential acceleration is zero. Radial acceleration grows with the radius and with the square of the angular velocity. The radius is ten divided by the square of five. Dividing only by five would use the relation for tangential speed.

$$
R=\frac{a_r}{\omega^2}=\frac{10}{5^2}=0.40\,\mathrm m.
$$
