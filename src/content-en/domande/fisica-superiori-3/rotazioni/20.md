A cutter spins at a constant $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. At what radius is centripetal acceleration $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $0.8\,\mathrm m$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.4\,\mathrm m$
- $0.4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Use the radial acceleration because velocity turns at fixed speed. That acceleration is linear in distance from the axis.

$$
R=\frac{a_c}{\omega^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}R&=\frac{10}{5^{2}}\\[4pt]&=0.4\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
