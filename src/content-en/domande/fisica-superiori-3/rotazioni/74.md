The net torque and initial angular velocity are both negative. How does angular speed initially change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It is unchanged
- It vanishes immediately

## Explanation

The negative acceleration points in the same sense as the initial velocity. The signed value decreases and its magnitude increases.
