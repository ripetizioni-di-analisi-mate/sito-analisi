A rotor with a fixed axis and constant inertia has negative initial angular velocity and experiences a negative net torque. How does the angular speed initially change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It is unchanged
- It vanishes immediately

## Explanation

With positive inertia, a negative net torque gives negative angular acceleration. The initial angular velocity is already negative, so its initial change moves it away from zero. The rotor speeds up in the negative direction. The acceleration sign gives the direction of the change.
