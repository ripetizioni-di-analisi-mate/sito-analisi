A rotor of inertia $0.50\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ changes from $4$ to $8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find the work done by net torque.

::grafico{f=".25*x^2" punti="4,4,pieno;8,16,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=9 ymin=0 ymax=21 nomi="ω (rad/s),K (J)" titolo="Net work is the difference between the two ordinates"}

- $24\,\mathrm J$
- $6\,\mathrm J$
- $-12\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Subtract the initial energy. Doubling speed makes the final energy four times larger, and work equals the gain.

$$
W=\frac12I(\omega_f^2-\omega_i^2),\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \left(- 4^{2} + 8^{2}\right) \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=12\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
