The graph shows a wheel’s angular velocity during a three-second measurement. What angle does it sweep over the interval shown?

::grafico{linee="0,4 3,10" punti="0,4,pieno;3,10,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=3.8 ymin=0 ymax=12 nomi="t (s),ω (rad/s)" titolo="Angular velocity measured between t = 0 s and t = 3 s"}

- $18\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $30\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $21\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $12\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The angle equals the area under the angular velocity line. Here that area is a trapezium entirely on the positive side. The endpoints on the graph give a mean velocity of seven radians per second. Multiplying by the three-second measurement duration recovers the full angle.

$$
\begin{aligned}\overline\omega&=\frac{4+10}{2}=7\,\mathrm{rad/s}\\[6pt]\Delta\theta&=7\cdot3=21\,\mathrm{rad}.\end{aligned}
$$
