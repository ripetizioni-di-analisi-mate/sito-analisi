A wheel starts at $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and accelerates at $2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ for $3\,\mathrm s$. Find its final angular velocity.

::grafico{linee="0,4 3,10" punti="0,4,pieno;3,10,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=3.8 ymin=0 ymax=12 nomi="t (s),ω (rad/s)" titolo="The segment slope is two radians per second squared"}

- $20\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The gain over three seconds is six radians per second. Add it to the initial velocity because the acceleration is in the same sense.

$$
\omega=\omega_0+\alpha t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\omega&=4 + 2 \cdot 3\\[4pt]&=10\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
