A rigid disc rotates with nonzero angular velocity. What quantity is the same for two points at different distances from the axis?

- Tangential speed
- Radial acceleration
- Angular velocity
- Arc length per turn

## Explanation

The disc is rigid and all its points sweep through the same angle in the same time. They therefore share the angular velocity. A point farther from the axis travels along a longer arc, so it has a greater tangential speed at the same nonzero angular velocity.
