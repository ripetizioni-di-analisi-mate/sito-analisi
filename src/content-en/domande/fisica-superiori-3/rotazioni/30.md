A uniform disc of mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ has $I=0.36\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about its perpendicular central axis. Find its radius.

- $1.2\,\mathrm m$
- $0.6\,\mathrm m$
- $0.3\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Isolate the squared characteristic distance from the central measurement. The radius is the positive root.

$$
R=\sqrt{2I/m},\qquad
\begin{aligned}R&=\sqrt{2 \cdot 36 \cdot \frac{1}{100} \cdot \frac{1}{2}}\\[4pt]&=0.6\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
