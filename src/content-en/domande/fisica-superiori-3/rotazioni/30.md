A uniform disc of mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ has $I=0.36\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about its perpendicular central axis. What is its radius?

- $1.20\,\mathrm m$
- $0.60\,\mathrm m$
- $0.36\,\mathrm m$
- $\sqrt{0.18}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

For the stated axis, the disc’s moment of inertia is proportional to the square of the radius. The inertia is doubled and divided by the mass. This gives the square of the distance in square metres. Its positive square root is the radius sought.

$$
R=\sqrt{\frac{2I}{m}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot0.36}{2}}=0.60\,\mathrm m.
$$
