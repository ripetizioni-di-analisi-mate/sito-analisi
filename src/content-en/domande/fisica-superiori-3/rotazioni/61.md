A cylinder with a symmetrically distributed mass starts from rest, rolls without slipping and drops through $2.7\,\mathrm m$. It reaches $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ without dissipation. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is $\dfrac{I_{CM}}{mR^2}$?

- $0.5$
- $1.5$
- $0.75$
- $2$

## Explanation

The measured speed and the drop determine the inertia coefficient without knowing the mass or radius. In the energy balance, kinetic energy per unit mass is half the speed squared multiplied by one plus the coefficient. Dividing first gives this sum, from which the coefficient follows.

$$
\begin{aligned}1+b&=\frac{2gh}{v_{CM}^2}\\[6pt]b&=\frac{2\cdot10\cdot2.7}{6^2}-1=0.5.\end{aligned}
$$
