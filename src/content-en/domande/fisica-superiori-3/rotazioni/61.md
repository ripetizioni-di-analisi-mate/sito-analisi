A cylinder with $I_{CM}=\dfrac34mR^2$ rolls from rest through a vertical drop of $3.5\,\mathrm m$ without losses. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find its final speed.

- $6.32456\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12.6491\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.16228\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6.32456\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The shape-dependent inertia ratio multiplies the translational energy to give the rotational energy. Divide by the sum of the coefficients before taking the square root.

$$
v_{CM}=\sqrt{\frac{2gh}{1+I_{CM}/(mR^2)}},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{CM}&=\sqrt{2 \cdot 10 \cdot 7 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{\frac{3}{4} + 1}}\\[4pt]&\simeq6.32456\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
