A wheel with initial angular momentum $8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ receives an external torque of $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ for $2\,\mathrm s$. Find its final angular momentum.

- $14\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

The change in angular momentum is the impulse of the external torque. The rectangle on the torque-time graph has a negative height and a width of two seconds. Add the impulse of minus six to the initial eight, leaving positive angular momentum with a magnitude of two.

$$
L_f=L_i+M\Delta t=8+(-3)\cdot2=2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}.
$$
