A wheel with initial angular momentum $8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ receives an external torque of $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ for $2\,\mathrm s$. Find its final angular momentum.

- $4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

Torque acting for a finite time adds angular impulse. The negative sign reduces the initial value without reversing rotation.

$$
L_f=L_i+M_{\mathrm{ext}}\Delta t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}L_f&=8 - 3 \cdot 2\\[4pt]&=2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
