Positive torque acts while $\omega$ is negative. What sign is the power?

- Positive
- Negative
- Zero
- It depends on mass

## Explanation

Axial power is the product of torque and angular velocity. A positive torque and a negative velocity give a negative product. At that instant the torque takes energy from the motion because it acts in the opposing direction. Mass does not enter the calculation of the sign.
