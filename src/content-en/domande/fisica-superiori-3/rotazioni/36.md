A radial $50\,\mathrm N$ force is applied at the rim $0.40\,\mathrm m$ from the axis. What is the torque about that axis?

- $1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The force's line of action passes through the axis. The perpendicular moment arm is therefore zero.

$$
M=rF\sin 0,\qquad
M=0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}.
$$
