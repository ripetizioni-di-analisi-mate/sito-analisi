A radial $50\,\mathrm N$ force is applied at the rim $0.40\,\mathrm m$ from the axis. What is the torque about that axis?

- $20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

A radial force acts along a line through the axis. The perpendicular distance from the axis to the line of action is therefore zero. Even though its point of application is forty centimetres away, the torque is zero. The point’s distance matters together with the direction.

$$
|M|=F d_\perp=50\cdot0=0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}.
$$
