A wheel rotates at $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ for $2\,\mathrm s$, then at $-2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ for $3\,\mathrm s$. Ignore the transition and add the magnitudes of the two angular displacements.

- $16\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $32\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $8\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Total swept angle counts both motions positively. The net angular displacement would use a signed sum.

$$
\theta_{\mathrm{tot}}=\sum_j|\omega_j|\Delta t_j,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\theta_{\mathrm{tot}}&=2 \cdot 3 + 5 \cdot 2\\[4pt]&=16\,\mathrm{rad}.\end{aligned}
$$
