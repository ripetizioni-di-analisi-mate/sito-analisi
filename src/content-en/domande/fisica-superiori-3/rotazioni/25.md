A wheel rotates at $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ for $2\,\mathrm s$, then at $-2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ for $3\,\mathrm s$. Ignore the transition and add the magnitudes of the two angular displacements.

- $16\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $4\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $10\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $6\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The wheel accumulates ten radians in the positive direction during the first interval and covers six radians in the negative direction during the second. The question asks for the total angle swept, so both magnitudes contribute positively. The net angular displacement would be the difference.

$$
|\Delta\theta_1|+|\Delta\theta_2|=|5\cdot2|+|-2\cdot3|=16\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
