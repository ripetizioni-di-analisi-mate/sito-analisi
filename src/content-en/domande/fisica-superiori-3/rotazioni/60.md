A uniform solid disc starts at rest and rolls downhill without slipping or dissipation. If it gains $30\,\mathrm J$ of kinetic energy, how much is rotational?

- $20\,\mathrm J$
- $5\,\mathrm J$
- $-10\,\mathrm J$
- $10\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

For a disc the rotational-to-translational energy ratio is one to two. Rotation is therefore one of the three parts of the total energy.

$$
K_{\mathrm{rot}}=\frac{K}{3},\qquad
\begin{aligned}K_{\mathrm{rot}}&=\frac{30}{3}\\[4pt]&=10\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
