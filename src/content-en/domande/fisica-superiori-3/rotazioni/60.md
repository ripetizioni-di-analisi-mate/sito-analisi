A uniform solid disc starts from rest and rolls downhill without slipping. With no dissipation it gains $30\,\mathrm J$ of total kinetic energy. How much of that energy is rotational?

- $20\,\mathrm J$
- $15\,\mathrm J$
- $5\,\mathrm J$
- $10\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

A solid disc has a central inertia of half its mass times the radius squared. The rolling constraint makes the rotational energy half the translational energy. The total contains three equal shares, one of which belongs to rotation. One third of thirty joules is ten joules.

$$
K_{\mathrm{rot}}=\frac13K_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac{30}{3}=10\,\mathrm J.
$$
