A planar body of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $v_{CM}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and rotates with $I_{CM}=0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ at $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find its total kinetic energy.

- $9\,\mathrm J$
- $5\,\mathrm J$
- $18\,\mathrm J$
- $14\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The body combines translation of the centre of mass with rotation about the centre. Translation contributes nine joules. For rotation use the centre-of-mass moment of inertia, giving five joules. Their sum is the total kinetic energy in the frame used by the question.

$$
\begin{aligned}K&=\frac12mv_{CM}^2+\frac12I_{CM}\omega^2\\[6pt]&=\frac12\cdot2\cdot3^2+\frac12\cdot0.40\cdot5^2=14\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
