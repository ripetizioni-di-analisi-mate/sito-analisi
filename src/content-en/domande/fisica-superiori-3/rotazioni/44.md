A planar body of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $v_{CM}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and rotates with $I_{CM}=0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ at $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find its total kinetic energy.

- $28\,\mathrm J$
- $7\,\mathrm J$
- $-14\,\mathrm J$
- $14\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Count translation of the centre of mass and rotation about it. The contributions are nine and five joules.

$$
K=\frac12mv_{CM}^2+\frac12I_{CM}\omega^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}K&=\frac{2 \cdot 5^{2}}{2 \cdot 5} + \frac{2 \cdot 3^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=14\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
