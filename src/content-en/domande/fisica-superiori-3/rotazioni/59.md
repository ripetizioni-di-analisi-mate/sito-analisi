A uniform solid disc and a thin ring start from rest on two ramps. Both roll without slipping or dissipating energy. The disc drops through $0.90\,\mathrm m$. What vertical drop allows the ring to reach the same centre speed?

- $0.90\,\mathrm m$
- $0.675\,\mathrm m$
- $1.20\,\mathrm m$
- $1.80\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

At equal final speed the vertical drop is proportional to one plus the inertia coefficient. This factor is two for the ring and three halves for the disc. The ring therefore needs a larger drop. The ratio of the two drops is four thirds.

$$
h_{\mathrm A}=\frac{1+1}{1+\frac12}h_{\mathrm D}=\frac43\cdot0.90=1.20\,\mathrm m.
$$
