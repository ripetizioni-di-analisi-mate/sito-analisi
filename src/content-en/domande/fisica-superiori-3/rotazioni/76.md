A torque varies with angle. Which feature of the torque–angle graph gives its work?

- The average slope
- The final torque
- The final angle alone
- The signed area

## Explanation

Each small angular interval contributes the torque multiplied by the angular displacement in that interval. On a torque–angle graph those products are signed areas. Their sum gives the work in joules. The area below the axis subtracts when the angle increases.
