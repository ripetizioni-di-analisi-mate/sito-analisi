A uniform solid disc starts from rest and rolls without slipping through a vertical drop $h=1.1\,\mathrm m$. With no dissipation, find the final speed of its centre. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $7.5863\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.89658\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.79315\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.79\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Lost potential energy becomes translational and rotational energy. For a solid disc $I_{CM}=mR^2/2$, and the rolling constraint eliminates $R$.

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{4gh}{3}},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\sqrt{4 \cdot 11 \cdot \frac{1}{10} \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100} \cdot \frac{1}{3}}\\[4pt]&\simeq3.79\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
