A uniform solid disc starts from rest and rolls without slipping through a vertical drop $h=1.1\,\mathrm m$. With no dissipation, find the final speed of its centre. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Round to two decimal places.

- $4.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.29\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.68\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.79\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The drop supplies both translational and rotational kinetic energy. For a uniform solid disc, the rolling constraint makes the rotational term half the translational term. This gives a total coefficient of three quarters. The mass cancels, so the height lost is enough to find the result.

$$
\begin{aligned}mgh&=\frac34mv_{CM}^2\\[6pt]v_{CM}&=\sqrt{\frac{4\cdot9.81\cdot1.1}{3}}\simeq3.79\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
