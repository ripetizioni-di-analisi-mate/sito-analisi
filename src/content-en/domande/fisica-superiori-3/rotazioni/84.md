A wheel rolls without slipping on the straight section of a horizontal belt moving at constant velocity. In which frame is the wheel’s contact point instantaneously at rest?

- The ground frame in every case
- The wheel-centre frame
- The frame of the top point
- The belt frame

## Explanation

Slip depends on the velocity of the point on the wheel relative to the surface it touches. The surface is stationary in the belt frame, so the wheel’s contact point is instantaneously stationary there. In the laboratory that point instead has the belt’s velocity.
