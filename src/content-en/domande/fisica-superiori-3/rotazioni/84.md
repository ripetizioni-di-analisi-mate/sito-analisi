A wheel rolls without slipping on a moving belt. In which frame is the stationary contact-point constraint easiest to apply?

- The ground frame in every case
- The wheel-centre frame
- The frame of the top point
- The belt frame

## Explanation

The contact point is momentarily stationary relative to the rolling surface. The ground and belt have different reference frames.
