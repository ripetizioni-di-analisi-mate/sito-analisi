A wheel rolls without slip at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with angular speed $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the diameter?

- $0.4\,\mathrm m$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m$
- $0.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The rolling constraint makes the speed of the centre equal to the angular speed times the radius. The radius is six divided by fifteen. The question asks for the diameter, so the calculated radius is doubled, which gives eighty centimetres.

$$
D=2R=\frac{2v_{CM}}{|\omega|}=\frac{2\cdot6}{15}=0.80\,\mathrm m.
$$
