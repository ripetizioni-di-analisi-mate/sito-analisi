A wheel rolls without slip at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with angular speed $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find the diameter.

- $0.4\,\mathrm m$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m$
- $0.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The rolling constraint makes the speed of the centre equal to the angular speed times the radius. Isolate the radius by dividing six by fifteen. The question asks for the diameter, so double the calculated radius to obtain eighty centimetres.

$$
D=2R=\frac{2v_{CM}}{|\omega|}=\frac{2\cdot6}{15}=0.80\,\mathrm m.
$$
