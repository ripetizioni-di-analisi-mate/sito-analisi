A wheel rolls without slip at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with angular speed $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find the diameter.

- $1.6\,\mathrm m$
- $0.4\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.8\,\mathrm m$
- $0.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The ratio of linear to angular speed gives the radius. Double that ratio to obtain the diameter.

$$
d=2v_{CM}/\left\lvert\omega\right\rvert,\qquad
\begin{aligned}d&=2 \cdot 6 \cdot \frac{1}{15}\\[4pt]&=0.8\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
