How does a point mass's inertia contribution change when the mass slides along the rotation axis?

- It grows linearly
- It grows quadratically
- It becomes negative
- It stays zero

## Explanation

The formula for a point mass uses the perpendicular distance to the axis. Moving along the axis keeps that distance at zero, even when its height changes. A one-centimetre shift perpendicular to the axis would make the contribution positive because the distance would become nonzero.
