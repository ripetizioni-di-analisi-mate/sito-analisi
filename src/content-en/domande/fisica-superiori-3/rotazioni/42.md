A machine applies $4\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ while a shaft turns at $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in the same sense. Find its power.

- $120\,\mathrm W$
- $60\,\mathrm W$
- $30\,\mathrm W$
- $-60\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Power is torque times angle per second. Aligned rotation and torque give positive power.

$$
P=M\omega,\qquad
\begin{aligned}P&=4 \cdot 15\\[4pt]&=60\,\mathrm W.\end{aligned}
$$
