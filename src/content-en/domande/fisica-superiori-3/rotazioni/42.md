A machine applies $4\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ while a shaft turns at $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in the same sense. What power does it deliver?

- $19\,\mathrm W$
- $60\,\mathrm W$
- $3.75\,\mathrm W$
- $-60\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Power measures work done each second. The shaft turns through fifteen radians per second and the torque does four joules of work per radian along the motion. Their product gives sixty joules per second. Torque and angular velocity point the same way, so the power is positive.

$$
P=M\omega=4\cdot15=60\,\mathrm W.
$$
