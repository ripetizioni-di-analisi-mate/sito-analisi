Rotor A has $I_A=0.25\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and $\omega_A=-12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Rotor B has $I_B=0.75\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and $\omega_B=+6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the ratio $K_A/K_B$ of their rotational kinetic energies?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Rotor A has one third of B’s inertia and twice its angular speed. The velocity sign disappears when squared and the speed factor becomes four. Multiplying by the inertia ratio gives a greater rotational energy for A, even though its moment of inertia is smaller.

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{K_A}{K_B}&=\frac{I_A}{I_B}\left(\frac{\omega_A}{\omega_B}\right)^2\\[6pt]&=\frac13(-2)^2=\frac43.\end{aligned}
$$
