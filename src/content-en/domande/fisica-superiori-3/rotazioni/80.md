How can an isolated skater gain kinetic energy by pulling their arms in?

- Their mass increases
- An external impulse must act
- The arm radius increases
- Muscular work reduces the inertia

## Explanation

Pulling the arms in reduces the distances of the masses from the axis. Angular momentum stays constant and the smaller inertia corresponds to greater kinetic energy. Muscular work transfers internal energy to rotation even while the external angular impulse along the axis remains zero.
