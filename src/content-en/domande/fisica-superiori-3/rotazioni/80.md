How can an isolated skater gain kinetic energy by pulling their arms in?

- Their mass increases
- An external impulse must act
- The arm radius increases
- Muscular work reduces the inertia

## Explanation

At constant angular momentum energy is inversely proportional to inertia. Internal work supplies the increase.
