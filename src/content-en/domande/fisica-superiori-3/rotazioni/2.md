A disc starts from rest with constant angular acceleration $\alpha=0.8\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. What angle does it sweep in $t=6\,\mathrm s$?

- $28.8\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The disc starts from rest and its angular velocity increases linearly. At the end it is four point eight radians per second. The angle is the area of a triangle with a six-second base. A rectangle with the final velocity as its height would give twice the angle.

$$
\Delta\theta=\frac12\alpha t^2=\frac{0.8\cdot6^2}{2}=14.4\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
