A disc starts from rest with constant angular acceleration $\alpha=0.8\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Find the angle swept in $t=6\,\mathrm s$.

- $28.8\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-14.4\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The area under the angular velocity–time graph is a triangle because the disc starts from rest with constant acceleration.

$$
\Delta\theta=\frac12\alpha t^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta\theta&=\frac{4 \cdot 6^{2}}{2 \cdot 5}\\[4pt]&=14.4\,\mathrm{rad}.\end{aligned}
$$
