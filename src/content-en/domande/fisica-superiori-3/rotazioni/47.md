Two flywheels on the same axis each have inertia $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and turn at $+3$ and $-3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. An internal clutch couples them with no external angular impulse. How much kinetic energy becomes internal energy?

- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $4.5\,\mathrm J$
- $9\,\mathrm J$
- $18\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The initial angular momenta cancel, so the final shared angular velocity is zero. Both initial kinetic energies are positive because they depend on the square of velocity. The clutch converts their entire sum to internal energy while total angular momentum remains zero.

$$
\Delta E_{\mathrm{int}}=\frac12\cdot1\cdot3^2+\frac12\cdot1\cdot(-3)^2=9\,\mathrm J.
$$
