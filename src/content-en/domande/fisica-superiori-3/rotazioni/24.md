A shaft starts at $\omega_0=6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $\alpha=-4\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ acts for $3\,\mathrm s$. What is the net angular displacement?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $9\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The velocity falls from plus six to minus six radians per second and reaches zero halfway through the three seconds. The positive and negative triangular areas have equal bases and heights. The net angle is zero because the backward interval cancels the forward interval.

$$
\Delta\theta=\frac{6+(-6)}{2}\cdot3=0\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
