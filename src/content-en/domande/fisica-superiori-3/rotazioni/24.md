A shaft starts at $\omega_0=6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $\alpha=-4\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ acts for $3\,\mathrm s$. Find the net angular displacement.

- $1\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Angular velocity changes sign halfway through. The two opposite areas have equal magnitudes.

$$
\Delta\theta=\omega_0t+\frac12\alpha t^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta\theta&=- \frac{4 \cdot 3^{2}}{2} + 6 \cdot 3\\[4pt]&=0\,\mathrm{rad}.\end{aligned}
$$
