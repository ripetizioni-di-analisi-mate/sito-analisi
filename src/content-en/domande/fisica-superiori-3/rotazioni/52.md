A roller of radius $0.25\,\mathrm m$ advances at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ without slipping. Find the speed of its top point.

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the top, both contributions point forward. Their magnitudes agree, giving twice the centre speed.

$$
v_{\mathrm{top}}=v_{CM}+\left\lvert\omega\right\rvert{}R,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{\mathrm{top}}&=2 \cdot 3\\[4pt]&=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
