A recording simultaneously measures a wheel’s top velocity as $+6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and its bottom velocity as $+1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Both are measured relative to the ground and positive to the right. What is the velocity of the centre?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The centre contributes the same velocity at the top and bottom. The rotation velocities have equal magnitudes and opposite signs, so they cancel in the average. The bottom point moves relative to the ground, so assuming it is stationary would contradict the measurement.

$$
v_{CM}=\frac{v_{\mathrm{top}}+v_{\mathrm{bottom}}}{2}=\frac{6+1}{2}=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
