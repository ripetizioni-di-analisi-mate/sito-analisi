A student on a rotating platform extends their arms, increasing inertia by $50\%$. Find the final-to-initial angular speed ratio in an isolated system.

- $1.5$
- $\dfrac23$
- $0.5$
- $1$

## Explanation

The fifty per cent increase makes the final inertia one and a half times its initial value. Since angular momentum is conserved, angular velocity falls by the reciprocal factor. It is therefore two thirds of the initial angular velocity and retains the same direction of rotation.

$$
\frac{\omega_f}{\omega_i}=\frac{I_i}{I_f}=\frac{1}{1.50}=\frac23.
$$
