A student on a rotating platform extends their arms, increasing inertia by $50\%$. Find the final-to-initial angular speed ratio in an isolated system.

- $1.33333$
- $0.666667$
- $0.333333$
- $-0.666667$

## Explanation

The new product must match the initial one. An increase in inertia to one and a half times the original gives the reciprocal speed ratio.

$$
\omega_f/\omega_i=I_i/I_f,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\omega_f/\omega_i&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{3 \cdot \frac{1}{2}}\\[4pt]&\simeq0.666667.\end{aligned}
$$
