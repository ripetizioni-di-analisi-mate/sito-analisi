A lever of length $0.30\,\mathrm m$ receives a $20\,\mathrm N$ force at $30^\circ$ to the arm. Find the torque magnitude.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 3,0" punti="0,0,pieno,O" vettori="3,0,5.598,1.5,F" xmin=-1 xmax=6.5 ymin=-1 ymax=3 titolo="The force makes thirty degrees with the arm r"}

- $6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

Only the component perpendicular to the lever contributes. The sine of thirty degrees is one half, so the effective moment arm is half the length.

$$
M=rF\sin\phi,\qquad
\begin{aligned}M&=3 \cdot \frac{1}{10} \cdot 20 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}.\end{aligned}
$$
