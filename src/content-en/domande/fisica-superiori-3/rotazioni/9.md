A controller measures an initial angular velocity of $+6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. For the first $2\,\mathrm s$ it applies $\alpha=+3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Over the next $1\,\mathrm s$ it applies $\alpha=-1.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. What is the final signed angular velocity?

- $10.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Each stage contributes its own acceleration multiplied by the time it acts. The first change is positive and brings the reading to twelve radians per second. The second stage starts from that value and removes one and a half radians per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}\omega_1 &= 6+3\cdot2 = 12\,\mathrm{rad/s}\\[6pt]\omega_f &= 12-1.5\cdot1 = 10.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
