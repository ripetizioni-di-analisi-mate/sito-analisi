A wheel has initial angular velocity $\omega_0=6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and constant angular acceleration $\alpha=0.9\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ for $t=5\,\mathrm s$. Find the final angular velocity.

- $10.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $21\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $5.25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-10.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

At constant angular acceleration, multiply acceleration by duration to find the change in angular velocity. Add it to the initial value.

$$
\omega=\omega_0+\alpha t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\omega&=9 \cdot \frac{1}{10} \cdot 5 + 6\\[4pt]&=10.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
