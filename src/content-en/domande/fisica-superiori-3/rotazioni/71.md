A force of fixed magnitude is applied to a lever at a fixed distance from the pivot. Which direction maximises the magnitude of torque along the rotation axis?

- Radially outwards
- Parallel to the rotation axis
- Tangent to the path in the plane of rotation
- At thirty degrees to the radius

## Explanation

The force magnitude and point of application fix the force and radius. To maximise the torque, maximise the force component tangent to the point’s path. A force tangent in the plane of rotation puts all of its magnitude in this component. A component along the axis produces zero axial torque.
