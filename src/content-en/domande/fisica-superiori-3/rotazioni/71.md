Which force direction most effectively turns a lever at fixed location and force magnitude?

- Along the lever
- Along the axis
- Perpendicular to the lever
- At exactly thirty degrees

## Explanation

Torque uses the component perpendicular to the arm, which is greatest when the whole force is perpendicular.
