To measure the mass of a plate, its moments of inertia are compared about two parallel axes $0.40$ and $0.20\,\mathrm m$ from the centre-of-mass axis. The more distant axis gives an inertia greater by $0.36\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. What is the mass?

- $2.25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Both values have the same central term, which cancels in the difference. What remains is the mass times the difference of the squared distances from the central axis. Divide the difference of the inertias by that coefficient. Using the squared separation would give a different quantity.

$$
m=\frac{\Delta I}{d_1^2-d_2^2}=\frac{0.36}{0.40^2-0.20^2}=3\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
