In an ideal fall from rest, the available drop quadruples. How does the arrival speed change?

- Quadruples
- Doubles
- Halves
- Stays the same

## Explanation

Energy per unit mass grows in proportion to the drop. Speed is proportional to the square root of the drop.

At fixed mass, doubling speed gives four times the kinetic energy. That is exactly what quadrupling the drop supplies when the body starts at rest and losses are negligible.
