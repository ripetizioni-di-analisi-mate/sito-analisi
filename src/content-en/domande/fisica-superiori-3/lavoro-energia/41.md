A device delivers $180\,\mathrm J$ to a cart's motion. The target requires a kinetic energy increase of $140\,\mathrm J$. What is the work done by the other forces?

- $-40\,\mathrm J$
- $-80\,\mathrm J$
- $-20\,\mathrm J$
- $40\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The device's work is one term in the net work. The remaining contribution is negative because the kinetic gain is smaller than the input.

$$
W_{\rm rest}=\Delta K-W_{\rm app}=(140-180)\,\mathrm J=-40\,\mathrm J.
$$

The other actions account for the forty joules missing from the kinetic gain. This is a net contribution. Assigning it to one force would require information about the others.
