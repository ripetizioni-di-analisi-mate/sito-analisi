Two hanging masses of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ are connected by an inextensible string over an ideal pulley, with negligible string and pulley mass. Starting from rest, the heavier mass drops by $2.0\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find their common speed, rounded to two decimal places.

- $6.32\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The heavier mass loses $60\,\mathrm J$ of potential energy as it falls, while the other gains $40\,\mathrm J$. The remaining $20\,\mathrm J$ becomes combined kinetic energy. The inextensible string requires equal speeds, so $\dfrac12(3+2)v^2=20$ gives $v\simeq2.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Tension does equal and opposite work on the two bodies in the combined balance.
