A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ block slides $5\,\mathrm m$ on a stationary horizontal floor with $\mu_d=0.20$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the work done by friction.

::grafico{vettori="0,0,5,0,s;0,0,-1.5,0,f" assi=0 xmin=-2 xmax=6 ymin=-1 ymax=2 titolo="Kinetic friction points opposite to displacement"}

- $-80\,\mathrm J$
- $-20\,\mathrm J$
- $-40\,\mathrm J$
- $40\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The forty-newton normal force follows from vertical equilibrium. Friction has magnitude eight newtons and opposes displacement.

$$
W_f=-\mu_d mgs,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W_f&=- \frac{1}{5} \cdot 4 \cdot 10 \cdot 5\\[4pt]&=-40\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
