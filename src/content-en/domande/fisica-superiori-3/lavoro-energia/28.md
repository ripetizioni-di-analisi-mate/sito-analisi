Two objects have equal momentum magnitudes and different masses. Which has greater kinetic energy?

- The more massive body
- Their energies are always equal
- It depends on their directions of motion
- The less massive body

## Explanation

Substituting $v=\dfrac p m$ gives $K=\dfrac{p^2}{2m}$. At fixed momentum, energy is inversely proportional to mass.
