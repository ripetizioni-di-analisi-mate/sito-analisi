A constant force $F=16\,\mathrm N$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with a straight displacement of magnitude $s=6\,\mathrm m$. What is the work done by the force?

- $48\,\mathrm J$
- $96\,\mathrm J$
- $24\,\mathrm J$
- $-48\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Project the force along the displacement. The cosine of sixty degrees is one half, so the component along the motion is half the force magnitude.

$$
W=Fs\cos60^\circ=16\cdot6\cdot0.5\,\mathrm J=48\,\mathrm J.
$$

The working component is $8\,\mathrm N$. The result is half the $96\,\mathrm J$ that the same force would do if aligned with the displacement.
