A constant force $F=16\,\mathrm N$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with a straight displacement of magnitude $s=6\,\mathrm m$. Calculate its work.

- $48\,\mathrm J$
- $96\,\mathrm J$
- $24\,\mathrm J$
- $-48\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Project the force along the displacement. The cosine of sixty degrees is one half, so the component along the motion is half the force magnitude.

$$
W=F s\cos 60^\circ,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=16 \cdot 6 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=48\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
