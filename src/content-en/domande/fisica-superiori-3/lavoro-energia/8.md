A motor raises a mass $m=35\,\mathrm{kg}$ through $h=6\,\mathrm m$ at constant speed in $t=4\,\mathrm s$. Calculate the useful average mechanical power. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $1030.05\,\mathrm W$
- $257.512\,\mathrm W$
- $-515.025\,\mathrm W$
- $515\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

At steady speed the kinetic energy is unchanged. Useful power is the potential energy gain $mgh$ divided by the duration.

$$
P=\frac{mgh}{t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}P&=35 \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100} \cdot 6 \cdot \frac{1}{4}\\[4pt]&\simeq515\,\mathrm W.\end{aligned}
$$
