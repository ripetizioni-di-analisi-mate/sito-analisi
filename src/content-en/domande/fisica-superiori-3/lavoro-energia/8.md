A motor raises a mass $m=35\,\mathrm{kg}$ through $h=6\,\mathrm m$ at constant speed in $t=4\,\mathrm s$. What is the useful average mechanical power, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $1030.05\,\mathrm W$
- $257.512\,\mathrm W$
- $-515.025\,\mathrm W$
- $515\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

At steady speed the kinetic energy is unchanged. Useful power is the potential energy gain $mgh$ divided by the duration.

$$
P=\dfrac{mgh}{t}=\dfrac{35\cdot9.81\cdot6}{4}\,\mathrm W\simeq515\,\mathrm W.
$$

The product $mgh$ gives about $2060\,\mathrm J$. The result gives the energy reaching the load per second. Finding the power drawn by the motor would also require the efficiency.
