A resisting force of $25\,\mathrm N$ makes an angle of $120^\circ$ with a velocity of magnitude $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the instantaneous power of the resisting force?

- $-100\,\mathrm W$
- $-200\,\mathrm W$
- $-50\,\mathrm W$
- $100\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Power is the dot product of force and velocity. The cosine of one hundred and twenty degrees is minus one half, giving negative power.

$$
P=Fv\cos\theta=25\cdot8\left(-\dfrac12\right)\,\mathrm W=-100\,\mathrm W.
$$

The resistance component along the velocity is $-12.5\,\mathrm N$. At that instant it removes mechanical energy at one hundred joules per second. Another force may supply positive power at the same time.
