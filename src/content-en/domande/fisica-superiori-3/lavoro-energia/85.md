A resisting force of $25\,\mathrm N$ makes an angle of $120^\circ$ with a velocity of magnitude $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find instantaneous power.

- $-100\,\mathrm W$
- $-200\,\mathrm W$
- $-50\,\mathrm W$
- $100\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Power is the dot product of force and velocity. The cosine of one hundred and twenty degrees is minus one half, giving negative power.

$$
P=Fv\cos\theta,\qquad
\begin{aligned}P&=\left(-25\right) \cdot 8 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=-100\,\mathrm W.\end{aligned}
$$
