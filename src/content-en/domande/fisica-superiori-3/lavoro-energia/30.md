A light body travels at twice the speed of a heavy body. What is the ratio of the lighter mass to the heavier mass at equal kinetic energy?

- One half
- One quarter
- Two
- Four

## Explanation

The squared speed is four times greater for the faster body. For the products to be equal, its mass is one quarter of the other.

The two energies compare mass times squared speed. For example, a one-kilogram object at twice the speed has the same kinetic energy as a four-kilogram object.
