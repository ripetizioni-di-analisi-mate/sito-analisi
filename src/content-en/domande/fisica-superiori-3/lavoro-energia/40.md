A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ body accelerates from rest over $10\,\mathrm m$ to $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the constant component of the net force along the path.

- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $2.5\,\mathrm N$
- $-5\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Net work equals the energy gain. With a constant force component, dividing by displacement gives newtons.

$$
F_s=\dfrac{\Delta K}{s}=\dfrac{4\cdot5^2}{2\cdot10}\,\mathrm N=5\,\mathrm N.
$$

The work is $50\,\mathrm J$ over ten metres. The quotient measures the component along the path. Any transverse component would require further information.
