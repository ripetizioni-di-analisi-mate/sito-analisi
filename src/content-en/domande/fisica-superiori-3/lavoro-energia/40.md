A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ body starts from rest and reaches $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over $10\,\mathrm m$. Find the constant component of the net force along the path.

- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $2.5\,\mathrm N$
- $-5\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Net work equals the energy gain. With a constant force component, dividing by displacement gives newtons.

$$
F=\frac{\Delta K}{s},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F&=\frac{4 \cdot 5^{2}}{2 \cdot 10}\\[4pt]&=5\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
