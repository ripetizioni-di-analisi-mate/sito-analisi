A body starts from rest at a height of $12\,\mathrm m$, with zero potential energy at ground level. With no friction or other forces doing work, at what height is kinetic energy half the mechanical energy?

- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $-6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Half the energy remains as potential energy. Since potential energy is proportional to height, half the initial height is required.

$$
h=\dfrac{h_i}{2}=\dfrac{12}{2}\,\mathrm m=6\,\mathrm m.
$$

Setting zero at the ground matters when describing both forms as half the total. Moving the zero would change the height at which that particular proportion holds.
