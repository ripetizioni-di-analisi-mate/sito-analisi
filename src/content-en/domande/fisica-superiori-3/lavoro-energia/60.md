A spring moves from $x=-a$ to $x=+a$ with $a>0$. What is its total work?

- Positive
- Negative
- Twice the initial elastic energy
- Zero

## Explanation

The endpoints have equal elastic energy because their opposite deformations have equal squares. The spring's work is minus the difference of those energies.
