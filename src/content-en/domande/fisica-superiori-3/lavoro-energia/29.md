A sled has kinetic energy $180\,\mathrm J$ and speed $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its mass?

- $10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearrange the energy definition for mass. Joules divided by speed squared leave a unit of mass.

$$
m=\dfrac{2K}{v^2}=\dfrac{2\cdot180}{6^2}\,\mathrm{kg}=10\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$

Substituting the mass back into the formula recovers $180\,\mathrm J$. The factor of two in the numerator compensates for the one-half in the kinetic energy definition.
