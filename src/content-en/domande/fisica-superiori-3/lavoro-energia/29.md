A sled has kinetic energy $180\,\mathrm J$ and speed $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its mass.

- $10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearrange the energy definition for mass. Joules divided by speed squared leave a unit of mass.

$$
m=\frac{2K}{v^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}m&=2 \cdot 180 \cdot \frac{1}{6^{2}}\\[4pt]&=10\,\mathrm{kg}.\end{aligned}
$$
