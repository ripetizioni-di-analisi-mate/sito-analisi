A constant force has horizontal and vertical components $(6,-8)\,\mathrm N$. Its point of application moves $3\,\mathrm m$ right, then $4\,\mathrm m$ up, and finally $2\,\mathrm m$ left. Find the total work done by this force.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 vettori="0,0,3,0;3,0,3,4;3,4,1,4" segmenti="0,0,1,4" punti="0,0,pieno,A;1,4,pieno,B" xmin=-.38 xmax=3.38 ymin=-.40 ymax=4.42 titolo="The dashed line joins start A and end B."}

- $-26\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $26\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-32\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

For a constant force use the total displacement. Its components are $(1,4)\,\mathrm m$, so $W=(6\cdot1-8\cdot4)\,\mathrm J=-26\,\mathrm J$. The final leftward leg cancels some of the rightward displacement. The negative vertical contribution is larger in magnitude than the positive horizontal contribution.
