A constant force $F=18\,\mathrm N$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with a straight displacement of magnitude $s=7\,\mathrm m$. Calculate its work.

- $63\,\mathrm J$
- $126\,\mathrm J$
- $31.5\,\mathrm J$
- $-63\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Project the force along the displacement. The cosine of sixty degrees is one half, so the component along the motion is half the force magnitude.

$$
W=F s\cos 60^\circ,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=18 \cdot 7 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=63\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
