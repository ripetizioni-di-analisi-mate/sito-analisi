When the external force–deformation graph of an ideal spring is a straight line from zero, how do you find the stored energy?

- The triangular area below the line
- The area of the rectangle at the final force
- The greatest height of the line
- The slope of the line

## Explanation

At low speed the external force has magnitude $kx$, and the stored work is the area below the graph. The force starts at zero.

At deformation $x$, the triangle has base $x$ and height $kx$. Its area measures work in joules. The spring force has the opposite sign to the applied force that deforms it.
