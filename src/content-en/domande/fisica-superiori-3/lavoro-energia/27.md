Two trolleys of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ each move forward in the laboratory at $6$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. By how much does their total kinetic energy decrease in a frame moving forwards at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction?

- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the laboratory the contributions are $36$ and $4\,\mathrm J$, totalling $40\,\mathrm J$. In the new frame the velocities are $+2$ and $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and each trolley has $4\,\mathrm J$. Their total falls to $8\,\mathrm J$. The $32\,\mathrm J$ difference is the energy associated with their common motion.
