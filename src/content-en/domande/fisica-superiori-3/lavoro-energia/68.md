Two smooth tracks lead from the same start point to endpoints at the same height. The body starts from rest on both. How do the arrival speeds compare?

- The longer track gives higher speed
- The shorter track gives higher speed
- They agree only if the travel times agree
- They have equal speeds

## Explanation

Both paths lose the same potential energy. When only conservative forces do work, both paths gain the same kinetic energy.

The two tracks can give different arrival directions and times. If the object reaches both endpoints, the initial and final heights fix the same speed in each case.
