A machine works at $200\,\mathrm W$ for $4\,\mathrm s$ and is off for $6\,\mathrm s$. Find its average power over the ten seconds.

- $160\,\mathrm W$
- $40\,\mathrm W$
- $80\,\mathrm W$
- $-80\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

No energy is transferred during the pause. Total work comes from the four active seconds and is divided by the full duration.

$$
P_m=\frac{P_1t_1}{t_1+t_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}P_m&=200 \cdot 4 \cdot \frac{1}{4 + 6}\\[4pt]&=80\,\mathrm W.\end{aligned}
$$
