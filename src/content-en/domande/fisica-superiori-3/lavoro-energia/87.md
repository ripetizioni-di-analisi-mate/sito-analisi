A machine works at $200\,\mathrm W$ for $4\,\mathrm s$ and is off for $6\,\mathrm s$. What is its average power over the ten seconds?

- $160\,\mathrm W$
- $40\,\mathrm W$
- $80\,\mathrm W$
- $-80\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

No energy is transferred during the pause. Total work comes from the four active seconds and is divided by the full duration.

$$
P_m=\dfrac{P_1t_1}{t_1+t_2}=\dfrac{200\cdot4}{4+6}\,\mathrm W=80\,\mathrm W.
$$

The cycle transfers $800\,\mathrm J$. The machine is active for four tenths of the time, so the average is four tenths of its operating power.
