A motor draws $800\,\mathrm W$ and is $80\%$ efficient. All its mechanical output feeds a gearbox with efficiency $75\%$. In steady operation, what useful power leaves the gearbox?

- $640\,\mathrm{W}$
- $480\,\mathrm{W}$
- $600\,\mathrm{W}$
- $160\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The motor supplies $0.80\cdot800\,\mathrm W=640\,\mathrm W$ to the gearbox. The gearbox keeps seventy-five per cent, leaving $0.75\cdot640\,\mathrm W=480\,\mathrm W$ at the output. The efficiencies multiply because each is a fraction of its own stage's input. Losses in the second stage apply to the reduced power.
