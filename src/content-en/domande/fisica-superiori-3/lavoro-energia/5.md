An ideal spring with stiffness $k=130\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed by $x=0.08\,\mathrm m$. Calculate its elastic energy with zero at the natural length.

- $0.416\,\mathrm J$
- $0.832\,\mathrm J$
- $0.208\,\mathrm J$
- $-0.416\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The force magnitude increases linearly with deformation. The stored energy is the triangular area under the force-magnitude graph.

$$
U_e=\dfrac12 kx^2=\dfrac12\cdot130\cdot0.08^2\,\mathrm J=0.416\,\mathrm J.
$$

The value $0.08\,\mathrm m$ is the magnitude of the compression. Squaring a negative deformation gives the same result. The spring force, in turn, reverses direction between compression and extension.
