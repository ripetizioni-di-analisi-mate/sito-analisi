On a graph of kinetic energy versus speed, how can the more massive body be identified?

- It has a positive intercept
- Its curve meets the speed axis away from zero
- At each positive speed its point is higher
- Its curve is lower

## Explanation

At a given speed, energy is proportional to mass. Both curves start at the origin because both energies are zero at zero speed.
