The force has components $(7,-4)\,\mathrm N$ and the displacement has components $(2,3)\,\mathrm m$. Find the work.

- $26\,\mathrm J$
- $14\,\mathrm J$
- $2\,\mathrm J$
- $-2\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Add the products of matching components. The contribution along $y$ is negative and almost cancels the contribution along $x$.

$$
W=F_x\Delta x+F_y\Delta y,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=- 3 \cdot 4 + 7 \cdot 2\\[4pt]&=2\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
