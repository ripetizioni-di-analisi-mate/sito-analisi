The force has components $(7,-4)\,\mathrm N$ and the displacement has components $(2,3)\,\mathrm m$. What is the work done by the force?

- $26\,\mathrm J$
- $14\,\mathrm J$
- $2\,\mathrm J$
- $-2\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Add the products of matching components. The contribution along $y$ is negative and almost cancels the contribution along $x$.

$$
W=F_x\Delta x+F_y\Delta y=(7\cdot2-4\cdot3)\,\mathrm J=2\,\mathrm J.
$$

The contributions are $14\,\mathrm J$ and $-12\,\mathrm J$. Their signs leave a small sum. Using only the vector magnitudes would lose the information about their relative directions.
