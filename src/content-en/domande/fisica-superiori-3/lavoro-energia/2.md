An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ has speed $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $180\,\mathrm J$
- $90\,\mathrm J$
- $45\,\mathrm J$
- $-90\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The speed is already in metres per second. Square it and multiply by half the mass.

$$
K=\dfrac12mv^2=\dfrac12\cdot5\cdot6^2\,\mathrm J=90\,\mathrm J.
$$

The squared speed is $36\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$ and half the mass is $2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$. Their product has units of joules. Reversing the direction of motion would leave the value unchanged.
