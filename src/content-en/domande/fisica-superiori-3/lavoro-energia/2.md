An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ has speed $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate its kinetic energy.

- $180\,\mathrm J$
- $90\,\mathrm J$
- $45\,\mathrm J$
- $-90\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The speed is already in metres per second. Square it and multiply by half the mass.

$$
K=\frac12 mv^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}K&=\frac{5 \cdot 6^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=90\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
