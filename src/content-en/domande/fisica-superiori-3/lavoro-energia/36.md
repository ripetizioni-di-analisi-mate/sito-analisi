A $600\,\mathrm{kg}$ vehicle travels at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The brake exerts a constant resisting force of $2400\,\mathrm N$. What distance is needed to stop it on level ground?

- $36\,\mathrm m$
- $9\,\mathrm m$
- $-18\,\mathrm m$
- $18\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

At rest, kinetic energy is zero. Resisting work equals $-fd$, so equating it to minus the initial energy determines the distance.

$$
d=\frac{mv_i^2}{2f},\qquad
\begin{aligned}d&=\frac{600 \cdot 12^{2}}{2 \cdot 2400}\\[4pt]&=18\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
