A $600\,\mathrm{kg}$ vehicle travels at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and must stop within $30\,\mathrm m$ on level ground. Braking starts after $1.0\,\mathrm s$ at unchanged speed. Once braking starts, the brake provides the only horizontal force and its magnitude stays constant. What minimum braking force is needed?

- $1440\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

During the reaction time the vehicle travels $12\,\mathrm m$ without slowing. Only $18\,\mathrm m$ remains to remove its initial $43200\,\mathrm J$ of kinetic energy. The minimum constant force is $\dfrac{43200}{18}\,\mathrm N=2400\,\mathrm N$. Using the full distance would omit the reaction distance.
