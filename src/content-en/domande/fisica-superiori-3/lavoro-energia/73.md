A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ skate glides at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ into a level section $3.0\,\mathrm m$ long, with constant friction of $6\,\mathrm N$. A second section has constant friction of $11\,\mathrm N$. How far does it travel in total from entry before it stops?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Entry kinetic energy is $40\,\mathrm J$. The first section removes $18\,\mathrm J$, leaving $22\,\mathrm J$ at the transition. The second section takes $\dfrac{22}{11}\,\mathrm m=2.0\,\mathrm m$ to remove the rest. The total distance is $5.0\,\mathrm m$. The positive intermediate energy confirms that the skate reaches the second section.
