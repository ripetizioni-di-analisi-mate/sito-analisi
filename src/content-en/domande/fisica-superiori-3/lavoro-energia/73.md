A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ sled enters a rough level section at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $\mu_d=0.20$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find its stopping distance.

- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $-4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Friction takes kinetic energy from forty joules to zero. The resisting force is ten newtons, so distance is the ratio of initial energy to resistance.

$$
d=\frac{v_i^2}{2\mu_d g},\qquad
\begin{aligned}d&=\frac{4^{2}}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 10 \cdot \frac{1}{5}}\\[4pt]&=4\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
