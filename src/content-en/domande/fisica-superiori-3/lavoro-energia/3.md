An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ changes speed from $v_i=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $v_f=8.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the total work done by the forces?

- $266.8\,\mathrm J$
- $66.7\,\mathrm J$
- $133\,\mathrm J$
- $-133.4\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The work–kinetic energy theorem subtracts the initial energy from the final energy. Thus take the difference of the squared speeds.

$$
W=\dfrac12 m(v_f^2-v_i^2)=\dfrac12\cdot5(8.1^2-3.5^2)\,\mathrm J\simeq133\,\mathrm J.
$$

The speed increased, so the total work is positive. Squaring just the difference between speeds would give too small a result.
