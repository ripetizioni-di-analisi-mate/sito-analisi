An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ changes speed from $v_i=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $v_f=8.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate the total work done by the forces.

- $266.8\,\mathrm J$
- $66.7\,\mathrm J$
- $133\,\mathrm J$
- $-133.4\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The work–kinetic energy theorem subtracts the initial energy from the final energy. Thus take the difference of the squared speeds.

$$
W=\frac12 m(v_f^2-v_i^2),\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=\frac{5 \cdot \left(- \left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^{2} + \left(\frac{81}{10}\right)^{2}\right)}{2}\\[4pt]&\simeq133\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
