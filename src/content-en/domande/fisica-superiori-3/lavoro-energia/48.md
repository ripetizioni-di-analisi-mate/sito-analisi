A lift rises $3\,\mathrm m$ while a box stays on its floor. Gravitational potential energy is measured relative to the ground floor. Which statement distinguishes its change from the change in height read on a ruler fixed inside the lift?

- Both changes are zero.
- The energy decreases and the inside reading increases.
- The energy increases only if the lift speeds up.
- The energy increases by $mg\cdot3\,\mathrm m$, while the inside reading stays the same.

## Explanation

Energy measured from the ground floor depends on the box's height relative to the building. The inside ruler moves with the box. It measures a different relative position and keeps the same reading.
