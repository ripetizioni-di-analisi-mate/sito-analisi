A person carries an $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ bucket horizontally for $10\,\mathrm m$ and then raises it by $3\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find the potential energy change.

- $480\,\mathrm J$
- $120\,\mathrm J$
- $-240\,\mathrm J$
- $240\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The horizontal segment leaves the height unchanged. Only the vertical segment contributes to the potential energy difference.

$$
\Delta U=mg\Delta h=8\cdot10\cdot3\,\mathrm J=240\,\mathrm J.
$$

The eight-kilogram mass rises three metres with a weight of $80\,\mathrm N$. Increasing only the horizontal distance would leave this potential energy gain unchanged.
