A body's kinetic energy decreases between two points. What can be deduced about the net force?

- The net force is zero at every point
- The net force always points in the direction of motion
- Its overall work is negative
- The force magnitude always decreases

## Explanation

The work–kinetic energy theorem constrains the work summed along the path. More data are needed to recover individual values.

The path can include regions where the body speeds up and others where it slows down. The decrease from entry to exit says the negative work contributions dominate overall.
