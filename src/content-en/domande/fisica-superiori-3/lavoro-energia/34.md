A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley goes from $8$ to $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the laboratory without changing direction. Another observer travels at a constant $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. How much work does the net force do in this observer's frame?

- $-70\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $70\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the observer's frame the velocities are $-2$ and $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The speeds increase, so the net force does $\dfrac12\cdot5(4^2-2^2)\,\mathrm J=30\,\mathrm J$ of work. The same net force can do work of the opposite sign when the displacement is measured in a different frame.
