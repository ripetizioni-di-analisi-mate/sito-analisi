An object of mass $m=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ changes speed from $v_i=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $v_f=8.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate the total work done by the forces.

- $368.64\,\mathrm J$
- $92.16\,\mathrm J$
- $184\,\mathrm J$
- $-184.32\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The work–kinetic energy theorem subtracts the initial energy from the final energy. Thus take the difference of the squared speeds.

$$
W=\frac12 m(v_f^2-v_i^2),\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=\frac{6 \cdot \left(- 4^{2} + \left(\frac{44}{5}\right)^{2}\right)}{2}\\[4pt]&\simeq184\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
