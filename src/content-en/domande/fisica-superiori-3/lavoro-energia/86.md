A $1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$ device operates for $30$ minutes. What energy does it take in, in joules?

- $4.32\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J$
- $2.16\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J$
- $1.08\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J$
- $-2.16\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Watts are joules per second. Convert kilowatts to watts and minutes to seconds before multiplying.

$$
E=P\Delta t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}E&=1200 \cdot 30 \cdot 60\\[4pt]&=2.16\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
