A device draws a constant power of $1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$ for $30$ minutes. What energy does it take in, in joules?

- $4.32\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J$
- $2.16\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J$
- $1.08\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J$
- $-2.16\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Watts are joules per second. Convert kilowatts to watts and minutes to seconds before multiplying.

$$
E=P\Delta t=1200\cdot30\cdot60\,\mathrm J=2.16\cdot10^6\,\mathrm J.
$$

The same interval is half an hour. In billing units the product is therefore $0.60\,\mathrm{kWh}$, the same energy obtained with seconds and watts.
