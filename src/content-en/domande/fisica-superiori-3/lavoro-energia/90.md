During an ideal cycle a motor transfers positive power and then negative power. How do you obtain total work from the power–time graph?

- From peak power
- From the signed area between the graph and the time axis
- From the mean slope of the graph
- From the area counted with positive sign everywhere

## Explanation

Over a short interval work is approximately instantaneous power times duration. Summing the signed contributions gives the signed area.
