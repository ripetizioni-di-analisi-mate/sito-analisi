A cart enters a frictionless descent at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and drops $2\,\mathrm m$. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and find the speed at the bottom.

::grafico{linee="0,2 3,0" punti="0,2,pieno,A;3,0,pieno,B" xmin=-.5 xmax=4 ymin=-.5 ymax=3 nomi="s,h (m)" titolo="Schematic descent with a vertical drop of two metres"}

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

There is kinetic energy at entry as well. After cancelling the mass in the balance, add the descent term to the initial squared speed.

$$
v_f=\sqrt{v_i^2+2gh},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=\sqrt{3^{2} + 2 \cdot 10 \cdot 2}\\[4pt]&=7\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
