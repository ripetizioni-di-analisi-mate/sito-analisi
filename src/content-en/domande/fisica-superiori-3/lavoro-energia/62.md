A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley moves along a fixed track, descending through a height of $2\,\mathrm m$. Its speed is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the start and $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the end. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much total work was done by forces other than weight and the normal force?

- $-15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $55\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy increases by $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot(8^2-3^2)\,\mathrm J=55\,\mathrm J$. Weight does work $mgh=2\cdot10\cdot2\,\mathrm J=40\,\mathrm J$, while the normal force is perpendicular to the motion and does no work. By the work-energy theorem, the other forces therefore do work $(55-40)\,\mathrm J=15\,\mathrm J$. The work is positive, so at least one of the other forces supplies energy to the trolley.
