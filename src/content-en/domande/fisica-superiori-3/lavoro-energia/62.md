A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley drops by $2\,\mathrm m$ on a fixed track. A sensor measures $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the start and $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the end. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much total work was done by forces other than weight and the normal force?

- $-15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $55\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy rises by $(8^2-3^2)\,\mathrm J=55\,\mathrm J$. Gravity provides $2\cdot10\cdot2\,\mathrm J=40\,\mathrm J$ and the normal force stays perpendicular to the motion. The other forces therefore do $15\,\mathrm J$ of positive work. A resisting friction force alone could not explain the readings, since it would remove energy.
