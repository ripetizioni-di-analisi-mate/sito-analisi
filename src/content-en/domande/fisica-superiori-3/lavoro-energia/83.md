A winch lifts a $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ load. At one instant, its velocity and acceleration are $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ upwards. Weight and cable tension are the only forces on the load. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the instantaneous power of the tension?

- $400\,\mathrm{W}$
- $60\,\mathrm{W}$
- $460\,\mathrm{W}$
- $230\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Tension must balance the weight and supply the force needed to accelerate. Newton's second law gives $T=m(g+a)=230\,\mathrm N$. Tension is parallel to the velocity in the same direction, so $P=230\cdot2.0\,\mathrm W=460\,\mathrm W$. Weight times speed alone is the power needed for uniform motion at that speed.
