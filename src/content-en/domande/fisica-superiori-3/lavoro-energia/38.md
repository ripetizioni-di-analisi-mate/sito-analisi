A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ body starts from rest. The two segments contribute work of $40\,\mathrm J$ and $-15\,\mathrm J$. Find its speed after both.

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The energy changes on the two segments add. The body arrives with twenty-five joules of kinetic energy.

$$
v=\sqrt{\dfrac{2(W_1+W_2)}{m}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2(40-15)}{2}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

At the second section the object has $40\,\mathrm J$. Work in that section removes part of the original gain, so the final speed is below the speed at the transition.
