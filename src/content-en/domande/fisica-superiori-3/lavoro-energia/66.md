A cart starts from rest and reaches $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a smooth descent. How much height has it lost, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $3.6\,\mathrm m$
- $1.8\,\mathrm m$
- $0.9\,\mathrm m$
- $-1.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

All the lost potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Cancel mass and solve for the height drop.

$$
h=\dfrac{v_f^2}{2g}=\dfrac{6^2}{2\cdot10}\,\mathrm m=1.8\,\mathrm m.
$$

Each kilogram needs $18\,\mathrm J$ of kinetic energy. Gravity supplies ten joules per kilogram per metre of drop. The required height difference is therefore just under two metres.
