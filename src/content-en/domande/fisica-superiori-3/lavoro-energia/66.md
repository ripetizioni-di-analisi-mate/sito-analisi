A cart starts from rest and reaches $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a smooth descent with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the drop in height.

- $3.6\,\mathrm m$
- $1.8\,\mathrm m$
- $0.9\,\mathrm m$
- $-1.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

All the lost potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Cancel mass and solve for the height drop.

$$
h=\frac{v_f^2}{2g},\qquad
\begin{aligned}h&=\frac{6^{2}}{2 \cdot 10}\\[4pt]&=1.8\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
