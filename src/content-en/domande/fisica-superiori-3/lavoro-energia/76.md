A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart starts from rest and drops $3\,\mathrm m$, while friction takes away $24\,\mathrm J$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find the final speed.

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Gravity releases sixty joules of potential energy. After dissipation, thirty-six remain as kinetic energy.

$$
v_f=\sqrt{\dfrac{2(mgh+W_f)}{m}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2(2\cdot10\cdot3-24)}{2}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The remaining $36\,\mathrm J$ is positive, so arrival is compatible with the balance. Ignoring friction would predict a greater speed because all the potential energy loss would become kinetic energy.
