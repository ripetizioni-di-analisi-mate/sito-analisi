An ideal spring of stiffness $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ launches a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley initially at rest on a smooth track. At a point $0.20\,\mathrm m$ above the starting position, after leaving the spring, the trolley must have speed $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what initial compression is needed?

- $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.28\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The target takes an extra $mgh=4\,\mathrm J$ of gravitational potential energy and $\dfrac12mv^2=4\,\mathrm J$ of kinetic energy. The spring must supply their sum, $8\,\mathrm J$. Solving $\dfrac12kx^2=8\,\mathrm J$ gives $x=0.20\,\mathrm m$. Using only the height gain would underestimate the energy required.
