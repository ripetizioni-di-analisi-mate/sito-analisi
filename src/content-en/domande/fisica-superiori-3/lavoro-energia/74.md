Friction has magnitude $6\,\mathrm N$ over the first $3\,\mathrm m$ and $9\,\mathrm N$ over the next $4\,\mathrm m$. Find its total work along the floor.

- $-108\,\mathrm J$
- $-54\,\mathrm J$
- $-27\,\mathrm J$
- $54\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Multiply each resisting force by its own displacement. Both work terms are negative.

$$
W_f=-f_1 s_1-f_2 s_2=-(6\cdot3+9\cdot4)\,\mathrm J=-54\,\mathrm J.
$$

The first section gives $-18\,\mathrm J$ and the second $-36\,\mathrm J$. The larger force also acts over a longer distance, so the second contribution has twice the magnitude.
