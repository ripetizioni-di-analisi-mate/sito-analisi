Friction has magnitude $6\,\mathrm N$ over the first $3\,\mathrm m$ and $9\,\mathrm N$ over the next $4\,\mathrm m$. Find its total work along the floor.

- $-108\,\mathrm J$
- $-54\,\mathrm J$
- $-27\,\mathrm J$
- $54\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Multiply each resisting force by its own displacement. Both work terms are negative.

$$
W_f=-f_1s_1-f_2s_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W_f&=- 4 \cdot 9 - 18\\[4pt]&=-54\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
