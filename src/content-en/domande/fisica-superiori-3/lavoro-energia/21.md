From $x=0$ to $x=4\,\mathrm m$, $F_x$ increases linearly from $2$ to $8\,\mathrm N$. What is the work in the direction of increasing $x$?

- $20\,\mathrm J$
- $32\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $8\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Find the area of the trapezium. For a straight segment the mean of the endpoints is the average force with respect to position.

$$
W=\dfrac{F_i+F_f}{2}\Delta x=\dfrac{2+8}{2}\cdot4\,\mathrm J=20\,\mathrm J.
$$

The trapezoid contains a rectangle worth $8\,\mathrm J$ and a triangle worth $12\,\mathrm J$. Both lie above the axis, so their contributions add.
