From $x=0$ to $x=4\,\mathrm m$, $F_x$ increases linearly from $2$ to $8\,\mathrm N$. Find the work in the direction of increasing $x$.

- $20\,\mathrm J$
- $32\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $8\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Find the area of the trapezium. For a straight segment the mean of the endpoints is the average force with respect to position.

$$
W=\frac{F_i+F_f}{2}\Delta x,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=\left(2 + 8\right) \cdot 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=20\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
