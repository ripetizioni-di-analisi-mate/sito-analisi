A spring stores $5\,\mathrm J$ when deformed by $0.20\,\mathrm m$. Find the spring constant.

- $500\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $125\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $-250\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Energy is half the spring constant times the squared deformation. Isolating the constant gives newtons per metre.

$$
k=\frac{2U_e}{x^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}k&=2 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{\left(1 \cdot \frac{1}{5}\right)^{2}}\\[4pt]&=250\,\mathrm{N/m}.\end{aligned}
$$
