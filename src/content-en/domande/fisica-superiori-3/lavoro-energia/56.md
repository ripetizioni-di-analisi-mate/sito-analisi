A spring stores $5\,\mathrm J$ when deformed by $0.20\,\mathrm m$. Find the spring constant.

- $500\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $125\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $-250\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Energy is half the spring constant times the squared deformation. Isolating the constant gives newtons per metre.

$$
k=\dfrac{2U_e}{x^2}=\dfrac{2\cdot5}{0.20^2}\,\mathrm{N/m}=250\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$

Using the stiffness found in the energy formula recovers $5\,\mathrm J$. The squared dependence means halving the deformation would leave one quarter of that energy.
