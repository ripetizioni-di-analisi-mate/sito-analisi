An object descends from rest along a fixed smooth guide. Why does the guide's normal force not appear among the work terms?

- It is perpendicular to instantaneous displacement
- It has constant magnitude
- It equals weight at every height
- It acts only at the endpoint

## Explanation

Velocity is along the local tangent. The normal force is perpendicular to the tangent, so its work over each small segment is zero.
