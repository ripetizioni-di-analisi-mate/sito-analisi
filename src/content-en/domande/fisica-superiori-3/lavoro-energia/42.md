A particle travels around a circle at constant speed. What is the net work over one quarter of a turn?

- Positive
- Zero
- Negative
- Centripetal force times radius

## Explanation

The path endpoints have equal speed. It follows that kinetic energy does not change.

At each point the net force is radial and the infinitesimal displacement is tangent to the circle. Each work contribution is zero. The velocity vector changes direction during the quarter-turn.
