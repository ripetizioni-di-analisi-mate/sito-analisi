An object is taken very far from Earth. Why does the product $mgh$ cease to be a good approximation?

- Mass depends on velocity
- Height is measured only beyond Earth's radius
- Weight becomes dissipative
- Gravity changes appreciably with altitude

## Explanation

The near-surface model takes $g$ as nearly constant. Over distances comparable to Earth's radius its change must be included.

When weight decreases during ascent, later metres contribute less to the potential energy change than the first ones. Add the contributions with the different local force values.
