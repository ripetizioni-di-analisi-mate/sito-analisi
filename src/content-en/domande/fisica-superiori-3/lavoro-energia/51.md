Two cables lift the same load from A to B along different paths, one longer. How does work done by gravity compare?

- Its magnitude is greater on the longer path
- It is proportional to the duration
- It is the same on both
- It is positive only on the shorter path

## Explanation

Gravity is conservative. The same initial and final configurations give an identical potential energy change.
