Near a small planet, raising $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ by $5\,\mathrm m$ increases its potential energy by $16\,\mathrm J$. Find the local gravitational acceleration.

- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Assume a nearly uniform field over the short distance. Dividing the potential energy change by mass and height gives the acceleration.

$$
g=\frac{\Delta U}{mh},\qquad
\begin{aligned}g&=\frac{16}{2 \cdot 5}\\[4pt]&=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
