Near a small planet, raising $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ by $5\,\mathrm m$ increases its potential energy by $16\,\mathrm J$. What is the local gravitational acceleration?

- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Assume a nearly uniform field over the short distance. Dividing the potential energy change by mass and height gives the acceleration.

$$
g=\dfrac{\Delta U}{mh}=\dfrac{16}{2\cdot5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$

The potential energy gain per metre is $3.2\,\mathrm{J/m}$, the probe's weight on the planet. Dividing that weight by its two-kilogram mass gives the gravitational acceleration.
