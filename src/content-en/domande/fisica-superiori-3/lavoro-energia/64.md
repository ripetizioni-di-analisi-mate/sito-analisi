A wheel starts from rest and rolls without slipping down a fixed ramp, dropping by $1.2\,\mathrm m$. Its rotational kinetic energy is half the translational kinetic energy of the centre of mass. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible losses, what speed does the centre reach?

- $4.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The potential energy loss $mgh$ supplies both translation and rotation. Their sum is $\dfrac32\cdot\dfrac12 mv^2=\dfrac34mv^2$. Cancelling mass gives $v^2=\dfrac43gh=16\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, so $v=4.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A body with translation alone would reach a greater speed at the same final height.
