Two motors lift the same load to the same height from rest to rest, one taking half the time. How do their useful average powers compare?

- The faster motor has twice the power
- The faster motor does twice the work
- The powers are equal
- The faster motor has half the power

## Explanation

Net energy gain is the same. Halving the time for that work doubles useful average power.
