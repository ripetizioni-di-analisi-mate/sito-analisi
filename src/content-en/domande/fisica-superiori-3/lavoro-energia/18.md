A guide exerts a horizontal reaction of $300\,\mathrm N$ while a lift rises by $9\,\mathrm m$. Calculate the work done by the guide.

- $2700\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $300\,\mathrm J$
- $-2700\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The dot product is zero for perpendicular vectors.

$$
W=Fs\cos90^\circ=0\,\mathrm J.
$$

The guide pushes horizontally while the point of application moves upwards. Its $300\,\mathrm N$ can matter to the horizontal force balance despite transferring no energy to the lift.
