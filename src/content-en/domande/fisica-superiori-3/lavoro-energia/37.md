Two braking forces of equal magnitude stop the same vehicle, one at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the other at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the longer-to-shorter distance ratio.

- $2.25$
- $4.5$
- $1.125$
- $-2.25$

## Explanation

With the same force and mass, stopping distance is proportional to the initial energy and thus to speed squared.

$$
d_2/d_1=(v_2/v_1)^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}d_2/d_1&=\left(\frac{15}{10}\right)^{2}\\[4pt]&=2.25.\end{aligned}
$$
