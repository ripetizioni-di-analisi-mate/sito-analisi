Three spheres each have mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and move at $1$, $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the sum of their kinetic energies.

- $36\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $28\,\mathrm J$
- $14\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Calculate each sphere's energy at its own speed. The squares add because the speeds belong to separate bodies.

$$
K=\sum_j\frac12 mv_j^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}K&=\frac{2 \cdot \left(1 + 4 + 9\right)}{2}\\[4pt]&=14\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
