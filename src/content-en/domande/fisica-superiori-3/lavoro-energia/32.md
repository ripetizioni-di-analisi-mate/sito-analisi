Three spheres each have mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and move at $1$, $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the sum of their kinetic energies?

- $36\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $28\,\mathrm J$
- $14\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Calculate each sphere's energy at its own speed. The squares add because the speeds belong to separate bodies.

$$
K=\sum_j\dfrac12 mv_j^2=\dfrac{2}{2}(1^2+2^2+3^2)\,\mathrm J=14\,\mathrm J.
$$

The three contributions are $1$, $4$ and $9\,\mathrm J$. If one sphere reversed direction, its contribution would still be determined by the magnitude of its velocity.
