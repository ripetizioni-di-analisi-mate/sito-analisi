The zero level is shifted from the ground to a floor $4\,\mathrm m$ higher. A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass is $10\,\mathrm m$ above the ground. Find its new potential energy with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $120\,\mathrm J$
- $240\,\mathrm J$
- $60\,\mathrm J$
- $-120\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The height above the new level is six metres. All potential energies shift by the same constant, and their differences stay the same.

$$
U=mg(h-h_0)=2\cdot10(10-4)\,\mathrm J=120\,\mathrm J.
$$

With zero at ground level the potential energy was $200\,\mathrm J$. The new zero level subtracts $80\,\mathrm J$ from every value. A displacement between any two heights therefore retains the same potential energy change.
