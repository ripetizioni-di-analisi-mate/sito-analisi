The zero level is shifted from the ground to a floor $4\,\mathrm m$ higher. A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass is $10\,\mathrm m$ above the ground. Find its new potential energy with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $120\,\mathrm J$
- $240\,\mathrm J$
- $60\,\mathrm J$
- $-120\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The height above the new level is six metres. All potential energies shift by the same constant, and their differences stay the same.

$$
U=mg(h-h_0),\qquad
\begin{aligned}U&=2 \cdot 10 \cdot \left(10 - 4\right)\\[4pt]&=120\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
