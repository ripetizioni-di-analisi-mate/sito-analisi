An object with weight $18\,\mathrm N$ descends until it loses $54\,\mathrm J$ of potential energy. What is the vertical drop?

- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$
- $-3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The loss magnitude is weight times vertical drop. Dividing by newtons gives metres.

$$
h=\dfrac{-\Delta U}{F_g}=\dfrac{54}{18}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm m.
$$

Here $18\,\mathrm N$ already represents the weight. Every metre of descent loses eighteen joules of potential energy. The requested quantity is the positive vertical distance.
