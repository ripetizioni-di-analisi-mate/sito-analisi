An object with weight $18\,\mathrm N$ descends until it loses $54\,\mathrm J$ of potential energy. Find the vertical drop.

- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$
- $-3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The loss magnitude is weight times vertical drop. Dividing by newtons gives metres.

$$
h=\frac{-\Delta U}{F_g},\qquad
\begin{aligned}h&=\frac{54}{18}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
