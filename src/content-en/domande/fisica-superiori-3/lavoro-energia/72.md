A crate slides on a horizontal belt moving faster than the crate, in the same direction, throughout the segment considered. Kinetic friction on the crate has magnitude $8\,\mathrm N$. As the crate moves forward by $4\,\mathrm m$ relative to the floor, how much work does friction do on it in that frame?

- $-32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The crate slides backwards relative to the belt, so friction from the belt points forwards. The crate's displacement relative to the floor also points forwards. The work is $8\cdot4\,\mathrm J=32\,\mathrm J$. Relative motion at the surfaces determines the direction of friction, while work uses displacement in the chosen frame.
