A force does positive work on a curved path. Which conclusion is certain?

- The force magnitude always grows
- The force is always parallel to the motion
- The body's speed is necessarily constant
- The path-averaged tangential component is positive

## Explanation

Work sums products of tangential force and path length. A positive sum gives a positive distance-averaged component, while its value at each instant requires more data.
