A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ parcel is $2\,\mathrm m$ below the chosen zero level, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the potential energy of the parcel–Earth system.

- $-60\,\mathrm J$
- $-120\,\mathrm J$
- $-30\,\mathrm J$
- $60\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The height is negative relative to the chosen level. The negative potential energy is consistent with that choice of zero.

$$
U=mgh,\qquad
\begin{aligned}U&=\left(-3\right) \cdot 10 \cdot 2\\[4pt]&=-60\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
