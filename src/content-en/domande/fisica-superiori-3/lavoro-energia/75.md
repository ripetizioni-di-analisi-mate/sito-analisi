On a stationary level table, a $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass loses $60\,\mathrm J$ of kinetics to friction alone over $4\,\mathrm m$. Taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find $\mu_d$.

- $0.6$
- $0.15$
- $0.3$
- $-0.3$

## Explanation

The work magnitude equals the kinetic energy loss. Solving for the coefficient divides by weight times distance, which has units of energy.

$$
\mu_d=\frac{-\Delta K}{mgs},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\mu_d&=\frac{60}{4 \cdot 5 \cdot 10}\\[4pt]&=0.3.\end{aligned}
$$
