A sled moves $4\,\mathrm m$ up a ramp inclined at $60^\circ$. Its mass is $8\,\mathrm{kg}$, $\mu_d=0.25$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Apart from the normal force, the only perpendicular component is from weight. Find the work done by friction.

- $-40\,\mathrm J$
- $-80\,\mathrm J$
- $-20\,\mathrm J$
- $40\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The component of weight perpendicular to the ramp is half the weight. Kinetic friction is the coefficient times that normal force and does negative work.

$$
W_f=-\mu_d mg\cos\alpha\,s,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W_f&=- \frac{1}{4} \cdot 8 \cdot 10 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4\\[4pt]&=-40\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
