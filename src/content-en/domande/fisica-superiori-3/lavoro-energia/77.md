A sled moves $4\,\mathrm m$ up a ramp inclined at $60^\circ$. Its mass is $8\,\mathrm{kg}$, $\mu_d=0.25$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Apart from the normal force, the only perpendicular component is from weight. What is the work done by friction?

- $-40\,\mathrm J$
- $-80\,\mathrm J$
- $-20\,\mathrm J$
- $40\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The component of weight perpendicular to the ramp is half the weight. Kinetic friction is the coefficient times that normal force and does negative work.

$$
W_f=-\mu_d mg\cos\alpha\,s=-0.25\cdot8\cdot10\cdot0.5\cdot4\,\mathrm J=-40\,\mathrm J.
$$

The normal force is $40\,\mathrm N$ and friction is $10\,\mathrm N$. Multiply by the length along the ramp. Vertical height would instead enter the work done by gravity.
