A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate is pulled up a straight ramp. The horizontal component of its displacement is $2\,\mathrm m$. Gravity does $-100\,\mathrm J$ of work with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What angle does the ramp make with the horizontal?

- $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $0^\circ$
- $45^\circ$

## Explanation

Work done by gravity is the negative of the potential energy gain. The height increase is $\dfrac{100}{5\cdot10}\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm m$. This equals the horizontal distance, so the two legs are equal and the angle is $45^\circ$. The stated horizontal distance is a leg, rather than the ramp length.
