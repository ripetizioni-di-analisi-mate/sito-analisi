Two springs of stiffnesses $80$ and $120\,\mathrm{N/m}$ are each compressed by $0.10\,\mathrm m$. Find their total elastic energy.

- $2\,\mathrm J$
- $1\,\mathrm J$
- $0.5\,\mathrm J$
- $-1\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The springs store energy separately. Their deformations agree, so factor out the common squared deformation and add the spring constants.

$$
U_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac12(k_1+k_2)x^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}U_{\mathrm{tot}}&=\frac{\left(1 \cdot \frac{1}{10}\right)^{2} \cdot \left(80 + 120\right)}{2}\\[4pt]&=1\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
