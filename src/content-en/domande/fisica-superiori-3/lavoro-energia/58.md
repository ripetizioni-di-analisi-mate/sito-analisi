Two springs of stiffnesses $80$ and $120\,\mathrm{N/m}$ are each compressed by $0.10\,\mathrm m$. What is their total elastic energy?

- $2\,\mathrm J$
- $1\,\mathrm J$
- $0.5\,\mathrm J$
- $-1\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The springs store energy separately. Their deformations agree, so factor out the common squared deformation and add the spring constants.

$$
U_{\rm tot}=\dfrac12(k_1+k_2)x^2=\dfrac12(80+120)\cdot0.10^2\,\mathrm J=1.0\,\mathrm J.
$$

The separate contributions are $0.4\,\mathrm J$ and $0.6\,\mathrm J$. The stiffer spring stores more energy at the same compression, in proportion to the stiffness ratio.
