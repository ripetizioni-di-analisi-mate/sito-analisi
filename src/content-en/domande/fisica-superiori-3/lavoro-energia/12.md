A mass $m=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ is raised by $h=2.2\,\mathrm m$ in uniform gravity. Calculate the increase in gravitational potential energy. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $258.984\,\mathrm J$
- $64.746\,\mathrm J$
- $-129.492\,\mathrm J$
- $129\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The final height exceeds the initial height. In uniform gravity the potential energy difference is weight times the positive change in height.

$$
\Delta U=mgh,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta U&=6 \cdot 11 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&\simeq129\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
