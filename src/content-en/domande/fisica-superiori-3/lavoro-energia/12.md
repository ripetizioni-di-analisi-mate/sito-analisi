A $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ load rises by $2.2\,\mathrm m$ while its $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ counterweight falls by the same distance. How much does gravitational potential energy change for the system of both bodies and the Earth? Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $132\,\mathrm{J}$
- $220\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-44\,\mathrm{J}$
- $44\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The load gains $6\cdot10\cdot2.2\,\mathrm J=132\,\mathrm J$ of potential energy. The counterweight loses $4\cdot10\cdot2.2\,\mathrm J=88\,\mathrm J$. The contributions have opposite signs because the heights change in opposite directions. The total change is an increase of $(132-88)\,\mathrm J=44\,\mathrm J$.
