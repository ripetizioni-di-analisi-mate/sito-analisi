A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ object moves in a plane with kinetic energy $37.5\,\mathrm J$. A sensor measures the component $v_x=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the component $v_y$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The energy gives $v^2=\dfrac{2K}{m}=25\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. The motion is planar, so $v_y^2=v^2-v_x^2=9\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Its magnitude is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The squared component allows either sign. Determining the direction would require another measurement.
