A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ object moves in a plane with kinetic energy $37.5\,\mathrm J$. The component of its velocity along the $x$-axis is $v_x=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the component $v_y$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy gives $v^2=\dfrac{2K}{m}=25\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Since the motion is planar, $v^2=v_x^2+v_y^2$, so $v_y^2=v^2-v_x^2=9\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. The magnitude of $v_y$ is therefore $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value of $v_y^2$ allows either sign, so determining the direction of $v_y$ would require further information.
