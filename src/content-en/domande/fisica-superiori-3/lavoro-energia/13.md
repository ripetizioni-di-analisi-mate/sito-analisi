An ideal spring with stiffness $k=140\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed by $x=0.09\,\mathrm m$. Calculate its elastic energy with zero at the natural length.

- $0.567\,\mathrm J$
- $1.134\,\mathrm J$
- $0.2835\,\mathrm J$
- $-0.567\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The force magnitude increases linearly with deformation. The stored energy is the triangular area under the force-magnitude graph.

$$
U_e=\frac12 kx^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}U_e&=\frac{140 \cdot \left(\frac{9}{100}\right)^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=0.567\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
