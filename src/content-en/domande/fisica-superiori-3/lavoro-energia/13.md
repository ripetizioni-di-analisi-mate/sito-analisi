A mass of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from a vertical spring of stiffness $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The extension changes from $0.10$ to $0.26\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find the change in the sum of elastic and gravitational potential energy.

- $0.96\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5.76\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-0.96\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The spring gains $100(0.26^2-0.10^2)\,\mathrm J=5.76\,\mathrm J$. The mass drops $0.16\,\mathrm m$, so gravitational potential energy falls by $4.80\,\mathrm J$. The net change is $(5.76-4.80)\,\mathrm J=0.96\,\mathrm J$. The final configuration is farther from the minimum of total potential energy.
