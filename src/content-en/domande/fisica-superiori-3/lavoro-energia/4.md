A mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is raised by $h=1.9\,\mathrm m$ in uniform gravity. By how much does its gravitational potential energy increase, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $186\,\mathrm J$
- $46.6\,\mathrm J$
- $9.50\,\mathrm J$
- $93.2\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The final height exceeds the initial height. In uniform gravity the potential energy difference is weight times the positive change in height.

$$
\Delta U=mgh=5\cdot9.81\cdot1.9\,\mathrm J\simeq93.2\,\mathrm J.
$$

The increase belongs to the mass–Earth system. Weight acts downwards on the mass and during the ascent does work of equal magnitude and opposite sign.
