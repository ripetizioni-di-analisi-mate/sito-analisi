A constant $10\,\mathrm N$ force points right while the body returns from $x=7\,\mathrm m$ to $x=2\,\mathrm m$. Find its work.

- $50\,\mathrm J$
- $90\,\mathrm J$
- $-50\,\mathrm J$
- $-70\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Position changes negatively in the chosen frame. Multiplying by a positive force component gives negative work.

$$
W=F_x(x_f-x_i),\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=10 \cdot \left(2 - 7\right)\\[4pt]&=-50\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
