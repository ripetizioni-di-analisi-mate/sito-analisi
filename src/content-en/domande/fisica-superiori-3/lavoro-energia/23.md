A constant $10\,\mathrm N$ force points right while the body returns from $x=7\,\mathrm m$ to $x=2\,\mathrm m$. What is the work done by the force?

- $50\,\mathrm J$
- $90\,\mathrm J$
- $-50\,\mathrm J$
- $-70\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Position changes negatively in the chosen frame. Multiplying by a positive force component gives negative work.

$$
W=F_x(x_f-x_i)=10(2-7)\,\mathrm J=-50\,\mathrm J.
$$

The path has length $5\,\mathrm m$ but is followed towards the left. The same force vector would do positive work on the outward journey to the right.
