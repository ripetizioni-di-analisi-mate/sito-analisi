A $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ sled moves at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A push does $70\,\mathrm J$ of work and resistance does $-25\,\mathrm J$. Find its final speed.

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The algebraic sum of work is forty-five joules. Add it to the initial kinetic energy and take the positive root for speed.

$$
v_f=\sqrt{v_i^2+2W_{\mathrm{tot}}/m},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=\sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot \left(70 - 25\right)}{10} + 4^{2}}\\[4pt]&=5\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
