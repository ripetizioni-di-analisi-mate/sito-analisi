A block enters two consecutive sections with $30\,\mathrm J$ of kinetic energy. One section adds $40\,\mathrm J$ uniformly along the path and the other removes $40\,\mathrm J$ uniformly. In what order can the block cross both sections without further assistance?

- In either order
- The section that removes energy first
- The section that adds energy first
- In neither order

## Explanation

If the resisting section comes first, the block stops once it loses its $30\,\mathrm J$, before the end of that section. Taking the assisting section first raises the energy to $70\,\mathrm J$, which then falls to $30\,\mathrm J$. The overall balance is the same, while access to the second section differs.
