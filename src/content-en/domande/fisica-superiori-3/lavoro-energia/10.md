An object of mass $m=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ has speed $v=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate its kinetic energy.

- $294\,\mathrm J$
- $147\,\mathrm J$
- $73.5\,\mathrm J$
- $-147\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The speed is already in metres per second. Square it and multiply by half the mass.

$$
K=\frac12 mv^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}K&=\frac{6 \cdot 7^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=147\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
