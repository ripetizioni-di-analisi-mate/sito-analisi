An observer on the ground measures $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for a $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley. A train moves in a straight line at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. What is the trolley's kinetic energy in the train's frame?

- $147\,\mathrm{J}$
- $48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the train frame, subtract the train velocity from the trolley's ground velocity. The trolley moves forward at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so $K=\dfrac12\cdot6\cdot4^2\,\mathrm J=48\,\mathrm J$. Its velocity and kinetic energy depend on the frame, while its mass stays the same.
