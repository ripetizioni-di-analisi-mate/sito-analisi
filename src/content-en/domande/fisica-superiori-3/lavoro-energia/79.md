An energy balance at a specified endpoint would give negative kinetic energy. Which conclusion follows?

- The body arrives with negative velocity
- Total energy is negative
- The point is unreachable under the assumptions
- The units of energy need changing

## Explanation

Kinetic energy cannot be negative. When braking on a level segment, the body comes to rest before that point.

The required energy loss would exceed the energy available. To locate the stop, find the first point where the energy balance gives zero kinetic energy.
