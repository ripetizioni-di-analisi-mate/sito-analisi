A $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate travels $8\,\mathrm m$ on a level floor with $\mu_d=0.25$. In one test it is pulled forwards with $50\,\mathrm N$ at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. In the other it is pushed forwards with the same magnitude at $30^\circ$ below the horizontal. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much greater is the net work in the first test?

- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is the same in both tests. The vertical component is $25\,\mathrm N$ upwards in the first and downwards in the second. The normal forces are therefore $75$ and $125\,\mathrm N$. The difference between friction forces is $0.25\cdot50\,\mathrm N=12.5\,\mathrm N$, which over $8\,\mathrm m$ gives $100\,\mathrm J$.
