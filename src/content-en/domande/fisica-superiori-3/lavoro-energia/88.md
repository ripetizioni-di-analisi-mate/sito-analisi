A device takes in $0.75\,\mathrm{kWh}$ of energy at a steady $250\,\mathrm W$. Find the operating time in seconds.

- $21600\,\mathrm s$
- $5400\,\mathrm s$
- $-10800\,\mathrm s$
- $10800\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Convert the energy to joules, using three million six hundred thousand joules per kilowatt-hour. Dividing by watts gives seconds.

$$
t=\dfrac{E}{P}=\dfrac{0.75\cdot3600000}{250}\,\mathrm s=10800\,\mathrm s.
$$

Dividing $0.75\,\mathrm{kWh}$ by $0.250\,\mathrm{kW}$ gives the same result in three hours. Converting those hours to seconds gives the unit requested.
