An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides through $s=4\,\mathrm m$ on a stationary horizontal surface with kinetic friction coefficient $\mu_d=0.13$. The only vertical forces are weight and the normal force. What is the work done by friction, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $-51.012\,\mathrm J$
- $-12.753\,\mathrm J$
- $-25.5\,\mathrm J$
- $25.506\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The vertical forces balance, so the normal force is $mg$. Friction opposes displacement and does negative work.

$$
W=-\mu_d mgs=-0.13\cdot5\cdot9.81\cdot4\,\mathrm J\simeq-25.5\,\mathrm J.
$$

The friction magnitude is about $6.38\,\mathrm N$. Over four metres this interaction removes about twenty-five joules. Any work done by a driving force would be counted separately.
