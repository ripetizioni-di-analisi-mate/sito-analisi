An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides through $s=4\,\mathrm m$ on a stationary horizontal surface with kinetic friction coefficient $\mu_d=0.13$. The only vertical forces are weight and the normal force. Find the work done by friction. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $-51.012\,\mathrm J$
- $-12.753\,\mathrm J$
- $-25.5\,\mathrm J$
- $25.506\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The vertical forces balance, so the normal force is $mg$. Friction opposes displacement and does negative work.

$$
W=-\mu_d mgs,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=- \frac{13}{100} \cdot 5 \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100} \cdot 4\\[4pt]&\simeq-25.5\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
