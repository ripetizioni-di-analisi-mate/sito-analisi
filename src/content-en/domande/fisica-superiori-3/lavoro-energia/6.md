An object starts from rest and travels down a frictionless track with vertical drop $h=2.5\,\mathrm m$. Ignoring air resistance, find its final speed. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $14.0071\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.50179\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-7.00357\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The lost potential energy equals the gain in kinetic energy. Since the object starts from rest, $mgh=\dfrac12mv^2$, and take the positive root.

$$
v=\sqrt{2gh},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\sqrt{2 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100}}\\[4pt]&\simeq7\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
