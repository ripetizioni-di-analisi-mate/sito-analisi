An object starts from rest and travels down a frictionless track with vertical drop $h=2.5\,\mathrm m$. Ignoring air resistance, what is its final speed, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $4.95\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $49.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The lost potential energy equals the gain in kinetic energy. Since the object starts from rest, $mgh=\dfrac12mv^2$, and take the positive root.

$$
v=\sqrt{2gh}=\sqrt{2\cdot9.81\cdot2.5}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq7.00\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

Use the vertical drop of $2.5\,\mathrm m$ even if the track is longer. The mass cancels because it appears both in the potential energy lost and the kinetic energy gained.
