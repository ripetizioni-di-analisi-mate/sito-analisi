For straight-line motion with increasing position, the graph of $F_x$ against time and the graph of $F_x$ against position have numerically equal signed areas. What do they represent?

- Both work
- Impulse and work, respectively
- Both power
- Work and impulse, respectively

## Explanation

The area of $F_x$ against time sums force times duration and gives the impulse along $x$, in $\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. The area of the same $F_x$ against position sums force times displacement and gives work in $\mathrm{N\cdot m}=\mathrm J$. Equal numerical values do not make these the same physical quantity.
