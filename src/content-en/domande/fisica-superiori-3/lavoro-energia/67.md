A mass descends without friction from $5$ to $3\,\mathrm m$ while its speed changes from $2$ to $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which value of $g$ fits the model?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Compare the kinetic energy gain with the loss in potential energy. The ratio keeps the difference of squared speeds and the vertical drop.

$$
g=\frac{v_f^2-v_i^2}{2(h_i-h_f)},\qquad
\begin{aligned}g&=\frac{- 2^{2} + 4^{2}}{2 \cdot \left(5 - 3\right)}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
