A constant force of magnitude $30\,\mathrm N$ does $60\,\mathrm J$ of work while its point of application moves $4\,\mathrm m$ in a straight line. What is the angle between force and displacement?

- $30^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $0^\circ$

## Explanation

The product $Fs$ is $120\,\mathrm J$. The given work is half that, so $\cos\theta=\dfrac{60}{120}=0.50$ and $\theta=60^\circ$. The angle must be acute because the work is positive. An angle of $120^\circ$ would reverse the sign of the cosine and of the work.
