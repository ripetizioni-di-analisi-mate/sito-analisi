A program reads a speed of $21.6\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and uses the number $21.6$ as though it were already in metres per second in the kinetic energy formula. The mass is entered correctly in kilograms. By what factor is the energy overestimated?

- $12.96$
- $3.60$
- $1.80$
- $0.077$

## Explanation

The correct conversion is $21.6\,\mathrm{km/h}=6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The number entered is $3.6$ times the correct speed. Energy depends on speed squared, so the result is too large by a factor $3.6^2=12.96$. Mass cancels because its value is correct in both calculations.
