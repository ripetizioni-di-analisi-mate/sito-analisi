Above a small planet, the same probe gains $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$ of potential energy over the first $4\,\mathrm{km}$ of ascent and $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$ over the next $6\,\mathrm{km}$. What is the ratio of the average weight magnitudes in the two sections, first divided by second?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

On each section, the mean magnitude of weight is the potential energy change divided by the height gained. The values are $40/4\,\mathrm N=10\,\mathrm N$ and $30/6\,\mathrm N=5\,\mathrm N$ as the kilo factors cancel. The ratio is two, consistent with weaker gravity in the upper section.
