The same spring's elastic energy rises from $3\,\mathrm J$ to $27\,\mathrm J$. What is the ratio of the deformation magnitudes, final to initial?

- $3$
- $6$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Energy increases by a factor of nine. The deformation magnitude increases by its square root because the relation is quadratic.

$$
\dfrac{|x_f|}{|x_i|}=\sqrt{\dfrac{U_f}{U_i}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{27}{3}}=3.
$$

The factor of three describes the magnitude change. The energy measurements alone can correspond to a stretched or compressed spring and do not determine the sign of deformation.
