The same spring's elastic energy rises from $3\,\mathrm J$ to $27\,\mathrm J$. Find the final-to-initial magnitude ratio of deformations.

- $3$
- $6$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Energy increases by a factor of nine. The deformation magnitude increases by its square root because the relation is quadratic.

$$
x_f/x_i=\sqrt{U_f/U_i},\qquad
\begin{aligned}x_f/x_i&=\sqrt{\frac{27}{3}}\\[4pt]&=3.\end{aligned}
$$
