A bumper spring stores $18\,\mathrm J$ when compressed by $0.30\,\mathrm m$. Find the magnitude of the elastic force in that configuration.

- $240\,\mathrm N$
- $60\,\mathrm N$
- $120\,\mathrm N$
- $-120\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The energy is the area of a triangle with base $x$ and height $F$. Divide twice the area by the base to get the final force.

$$
F=\dfrac{2U_e}{x}=\dfrac{2\cdot18}{0.30}\,\mathrm N=120\,\mathrm N.
$$

The force rises from zero to its final value. Dividing energy by deformation gives the mean force, $60\,\mathrm N$. The requested final magnitude is twice as large.
