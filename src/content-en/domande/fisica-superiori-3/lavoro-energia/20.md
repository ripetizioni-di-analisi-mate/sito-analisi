A body returns to its starting point while a constant force vector acts. What is the overall work done by that vector?

- Always positive
- Always negative
- The force magnitude times the path length
- Zero

## Explanation

Factor the force out in the dot product with the sum of displacements. Returning to the starting point gives zero total displacement.
