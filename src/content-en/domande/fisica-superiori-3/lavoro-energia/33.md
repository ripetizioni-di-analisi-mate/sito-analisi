A cart rebounds from a wall and reverses direction at the same speed. What changes?

- Momentum reverses and kinetic energy is unchanged
- Both change sign
- Kinetic energy doubles and momentum stays the same
- Both remain identical

## Explanation

Energy depends on squared speed. Momentum is proportional to the velocity vector, so it reverses with it.

The trolley therefore receives an impulse that changes its motion. Equal energies at the endpoints still allow a substantial change in the momentum vector.
