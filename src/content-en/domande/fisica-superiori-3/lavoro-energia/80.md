An applied force and friction do equal and opposite work. These are the only two work contributions besides gravity, which is included in the potential. What conclusion follows?

- Friction dissipates no energy
- Speed is constant at every instant
- Potential energy is constant at every instant
- Mechanical energy agrees at the endpoints

## Explanation

The nonconservative work contributions cancel between the endpoints. Friction still dissipates energy even when applied work replaces exactly the loss.

The forces may act on different sections. The balance gives equal mechanical energy at the two endpoints. Energy may change in between, and kinetic energy can also change with height.
