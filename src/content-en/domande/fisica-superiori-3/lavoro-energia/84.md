A motor takes in $400\,\mathrm W$ for $15\,\mathrm s$ and transfers $4200\,\mathrm J$ usefully. Find the efficiency as a percentage.

- $140\,\%$
- $35\,\%$
- $-70\,\%$
- $70\,\%$

## Explanation

Input energy is input power times time. Efficiency is useful energy divided by input energy over the same interval.

$$
\eta_{\%}=100\frac{E_u}{P_{\mathrm{in}}\Delta t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\eta_{\%}&=100 \cdot 4200 \cdot \frac{1}{15 \cdot 400}\\[4pt]&=70\,\%.\end{aligned}
$$
