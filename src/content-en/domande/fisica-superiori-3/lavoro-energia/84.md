A motor takes in $400\,\mathrm W$ for $15\,\mathrm s$ and transfers $4200\,\mathrm J$ usefully. Find the efficiency as a percentage.

- $140\,\%$
- $35\,\%$
- $-70\,\%$
- $70\,\%$

## Explanation

Input energy is input power times time. Efficiency is useful energy divided by input energy over the same interval.

$$
\eta=\dfrac{E_u}{P_{\rm in}\Delta t}=\dfrac{4200}{400\cdot15}=0.70=70\%.
$$

The input supplies $6000\,\mathrm J$, with $1800\,\mathrm J$ outside the useful output. The ratio is $0.70$, which becomes seventy per cent when expressed as a percentage.
