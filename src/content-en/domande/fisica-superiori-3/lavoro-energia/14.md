A point-like ball starts at rest on a fixed, smooth track that leads into the inside of a vertical circular loop of radius $0.80\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the minimum release height above the bottom of the loop for the ball to complete the loop while maintaining contact?

- $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the top at the contact limit, weight alone produces the radial acceleration, so $v^2=gR$. The release height must supply the top height $2R$ plus the height equivalent of kinetic energy, $v^2/(2g)=R/2$. The sum is $\dfrac{5R}{2}=2.00\,\mathrm m$. Using only $2R$ would give zero speed at the top.
