A circular satellite experiences a field of $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ at radius $10^6\,\mathrm m$. What is its orbital speed?

- $4000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The field points towards the centre and supplies the centripetal acceleration. In a circular orbit the field is therefore the speed squared divided by radius. Multiplying by the radius gives four million square metres per square second. The square root gives the speed.

$$
v=\sqrt{gr}=\sqrt{4\cdot10^6}=2000\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
