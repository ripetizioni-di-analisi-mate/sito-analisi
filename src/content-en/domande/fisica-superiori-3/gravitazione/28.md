A circular satellite experiences a field of $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ at radius $10^6\,\mathrm m$. Find orbital speed.

- $4000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The field already evaluated at the orbital radius is centripetal acceleration. Multiplying by the radius gives squared speed.

$$
v=\sqrt{g(r)r},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\sqrt{4 \cdot 10^{6}}\\[4pt]&=2000\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
