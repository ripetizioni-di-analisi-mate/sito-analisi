What distance enters the force between two nonoverlapping spherically symmetric planets?

- Separation between surfaces
- Sum of atmospheric heights
- Centre separation
- The larger planet's radius

## Explanation

Outside a spherically symmetric distribution, its field is that of its total mass concentrated at the centre. Since the planets do not overlap, the total force uses their two centres. The separation between the surfaces is smaller than the distance that enters the law.
