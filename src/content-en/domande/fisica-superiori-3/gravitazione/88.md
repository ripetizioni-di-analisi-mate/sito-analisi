A probe with positive total energy travels radially outward under gravity alone. What happens at great distance?

- It always returns
- It remains stationary at a finite radius
- It loses all speed exactly at twice the radius
- It approaches a nonzero limiting speed

## Explanation

Gravity slows the outgoing probe, converting part of its kinetic energy into potential energy. As distance increases without limit, the potential approaches zero. The remaining kinetic energy therefore equals the initial total, which is positive. The resulting limiting speed is nonzero.
