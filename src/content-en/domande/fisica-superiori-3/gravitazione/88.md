A probe with positive total energy travels radially outward under gravity alone. What happens at great distance?

- It always returns
- It remains stationary at a finite radius
- It loses all speed exactly at twice the radius
- It approaches a nonzero limiting speed

## Explanation

At infinity potential tends to zero. Kinetic energy retains the positive total energy.
