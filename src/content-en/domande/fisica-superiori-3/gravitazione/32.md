Two satellites follow circular orbits about the same planet. The second period is eight times the first. What is the gravitational field magnitude ratio $\dfrac{g_2}{g_1}$ along the two orbits?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

For the same source, the square of the period is proportional to the cube of the radius. A period eight times as long means a radius four times as large. The field falls with the square of the radius, so divide the original field by sixteen.

$$
\frac{r_2}{r_1}=8^{\frac23}=4,\qquad \frac{g_2}{g_1}=\left(\frac14\right)^2=\frac1{16}.
$$
