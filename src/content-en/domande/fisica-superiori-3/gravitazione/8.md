A satellite of mass $m=160\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels in a circular orbit of radius $r=7600000\,\mathrm m$ around a planet with $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Find the total orbital mechanical energy, with zero potential at infinity.

- $-8.27368\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-2.06842\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $4.13684\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-4.14\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Circular kinetic energy is positive and equals half the potential energy magnitude. Their sum is negative and is half the potential energy.

$$
E=-\frac{\mu m}{2r},\qquad
\begin{aligned}E&=\left(-393000000000000\right) \cdot 160 \cdot \frac{1}{2 \cdot 7600000}\\[4pt]&\simeq-4.14\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
