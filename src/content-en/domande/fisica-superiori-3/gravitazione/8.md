A satellite of mass $m=160\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels in a circular orbit of radius $r=7600000\,\mathrm m$ around a planet with $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Find the total orbital mechanical energy, with zero potential at infinity.

- $-8.27368\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-2.06842\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $4.13684\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-4.14\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

For a circular orbit, the speed squared is the gravitational parameter divided by the radius. The kinetic energy is therefore half the magnitude of potential energy, which is negative with zero at infinity. Their sum remains negative and has half the magnitude of the potential energy alone.

$$
E=\frac{\mu m}{2r}-\frac{\mu m}{r}=-\frac{\mu m}{2r}\simeq-4.14\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J.
$$
