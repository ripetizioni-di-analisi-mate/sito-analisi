At a probe’s position, two sources produce perpendicular fields of magnitudes $3g_*$ and $4g_*$, with $g_*=0.256\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of the total field?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 vettori="0,0,3,0,3g_*;0,0,0,4,4g_*" punti="0,0,pieno,P" xmin=-.72 xmax=4 ymin=-.6 ymax=4.5 titolo="The two fields at P point in perpendicular directions"}

- $1.792\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.28\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.256\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.024\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Fields are vectors. Since they are perpendicular, they form the legs of a right triangle. Use the Pythagorean theorem to find the magnitude of the resultant. The ratio of three to four makes the hypotenuse five times the stated reference value.

$$
|\vec g|=g_*\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\cdot0.256=1.28\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
