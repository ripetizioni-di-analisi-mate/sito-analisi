A spherical planet has surface field $g_0=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the field magnitude at distance $r=2.8R$ from its centre, where $R$ is its radius.

- $2.55102\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.28\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.637755\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-1.27551\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The planet's mass is the same at both radii. The ratio of local to surface field is the square of the inverse ratio of centre distances.

$$
g_r=\frac{g_0}{(r/R)^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}g_r&=\frac{10}{\left(\frac{14}{5}\right)^{2}}\\[4pt]&\simeq1.28\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
