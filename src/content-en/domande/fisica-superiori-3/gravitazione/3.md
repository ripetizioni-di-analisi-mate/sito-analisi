A planet has gravitational parameter $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. A satellite of negligible mass follows a circular orbit of radius $r=7600000\,\mathrm m$ from the centre. Find the orbital speed.

- $14382\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3595.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.19\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-7191\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In a circular orbit the planet’s field provides the centripetal acceleration. Equate the speed squared divided by radius to the gravitational parameter divided by radius squared. A square root remains. The radius is measured from the centre, so the stated value is used directly.

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}}=\sqrt{\frac{3.93\cdot10^{14}}{7.6\cdot10^6}}\simeq7.19\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
