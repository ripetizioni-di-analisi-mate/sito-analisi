Two objects of negligible mass compared with the same source start at the same point. How do their ideal minimum escape speeds compare?

- The heavier object's is greater
- The lighter object's is greater
- They agree
- They depend on volume

## Explanation

The kinetic energy needed to escape and the initial potential energy magnitude both grow in proportion to the body’s mass. Imposing the energy threshold cancels that mass. For two bodies of negligible mass compared with the source, starting at the same radius, the minimum speeds agree.
