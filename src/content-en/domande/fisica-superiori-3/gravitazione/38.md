Escape speed is $8000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at radius $r$. Find it at $9r$.

- $5333.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2666.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1333.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2666.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The planetary parameter stays unchanged. Required speed varies inversely with the square root of radius.

$$
v_e=v_{e,1}\sqrt{r_1/r_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_e&=\frac{8000}{\sqrt{9}}\\[4pt]&\simeq2666.67\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
