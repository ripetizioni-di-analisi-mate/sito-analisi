Escape speed is $8000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at radius $r$. Find it at $9r$.

- $5333.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2666.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1333.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2666.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For a fixed source the escape speed is proportional to the inverse square root of distance from the centre. The new distance is nine times larger, so the square root of the factor is three. The required speed is one third of the initial speed. The local field follows an inverse-square law.

$$
v_{\mathrm{fuga},2}=\frac{8000}{\sqrt9}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq2666.67\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
