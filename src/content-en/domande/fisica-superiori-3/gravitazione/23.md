At distance $2R$ the field is $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the surface value.

- $32\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The surface value is larger because the centre is nearer. Multiplying by four reverses attenuation from ground level to $2R$.

$$
g_0=g(r)(r/R)^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}g_0&=4 \cdot 2^{2}\\[4pt]&=16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
