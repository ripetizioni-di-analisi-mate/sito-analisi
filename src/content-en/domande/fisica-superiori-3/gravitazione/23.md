The period and orbital radius of a negligible-mass satellite in a circular orbit are measured. The planet is spherical. Which quantity requires a further measurement of the planet's radius even when its gravitational parameter $GM$ is known?

- The speed of the satellite in its orbit.
- The centripetal acceleration of the satellite.
- Gravitational acceleration at the surface.
- Potential energy per unit mass on the orbit, relative to infinity.

## Explanation

At the surface $g=\dfrac{GM}{R^2}$, where $R$ is the planet's radius. The other quantities use the measured orbital radius. Planets with the same $GM$ can have different radii and hence different surface gravities.
