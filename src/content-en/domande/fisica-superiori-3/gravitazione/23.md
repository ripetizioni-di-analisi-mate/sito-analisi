At distance $2R$ from the centre of a planet of radius $R$, the field is $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the surface value.

- $32\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The starting distance from the centre is twice the surface radius. Returning to the surface halves the distance and quadruples the field magnitude. Multiply the measured value aloft by four. The units remain those of gravitational acceleration, since the field ratio is dimensionless.

$$
g_0=4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\left(\frac{2R}{R}\right)^2=16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
