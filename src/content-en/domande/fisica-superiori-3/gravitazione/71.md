In which limit can the potential energy difference be approximated by $m g_0h$ above a surface?

- Altitude much greater than the radius
- Altitude equal to two radii
- Altitude small compared with the radius
- Negative test mass

## Explanation

Over a small interval of radii the field varies little and can be approximated by its surface value.
