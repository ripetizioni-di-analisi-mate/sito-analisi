In which limit can the potential energy difference be approximated by $m g_0h$ above a surface?

- Altitude much greater than the radius
- Altitude equal to two radii
- Altitude small compared with the radius
- Negative test mass

## Explanation

The formula using surface gravity treats the field as constant during the climb. In the exact model the field changes with distance from the centre. The relative error in the energy increase is the ratio of altitude to radius. The estimate is therefore accurate when that ratio is small.
