What is the direction of a point source's gravity on a test mass?

- Tangential
- Along the connecting line away from the source
- Along the connecting line towards the source
- Towards geographic north

## Explanation

A positive mass attracts the test mass towards itself. Symmetry about the line joining them requires the force to point along that line. Increasing the separation changes the magnitude, while the direction still points towards the source as long as the body remains on the same side.
