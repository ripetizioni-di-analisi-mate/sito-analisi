At which apsis is the speed greater on a noncircular elliptical orbit?

- At the larger distance
- Equal by definition
- At the smaller distance
- At the geometric centre

## Explanation

At both apsides, velocity is perpendicular to the radius. For the same orbiting mass, the product of distance and speed is proportional to the conserved angular momentum. The shorter distance requires the greater speed to preserve the product, so the faster of the two points is periapsis.
