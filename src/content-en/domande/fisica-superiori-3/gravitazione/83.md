At which of the two apses of an elliptical orbit is speed greater?

- At the larger distance
- Equal by definition
- At the smaller distance
- At the geometric centre

## Explanation

Velocity is transverse to radius at the apses. Conserving radius times speed requires greater speed at smaller radius.
