Two satellites orbit at the same circular radius. The second has three times the mass. Find the ratio of total energy magnitudes, second to first.

- $6$
- $3$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Speed and radius agree, but both kinetic and potential energies scale with mass. Their sum scales the same way.

$$
E_2/E_1=m_2/m_1,\qquad
E_2/E_1=3.
$$
