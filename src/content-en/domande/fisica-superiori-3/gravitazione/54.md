Two satellites follow circular orbits of the same radius around the same planet. The second has three times the mass. Find the ratio of the magnitudes of their total energies, second over first.

- $6$
- $3$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

At the same radius about the same source, the circular speeds coincide. Both kinetic and potential energies are proportional to the satellite’s mass. The magnitude of their sum has the same dependence. Tripling the mass therefore triples the magnitude of the total orbital energy.

$$
\frac{|E_2|}{|E_1|}=\frac{\frac{\mu m_2}{2r}}{\frac{\mu m_1}{2r}}=\frac{m_2}{m_1}=3.
$$
