In a circular orbit why is the field nonzero even though the astronaut feels weightless?

- Gravity stops inside the vehicle
- The satellite and astronaut fall with the same acceleration
- Satellite mass disappears
- Velocity is zero

## Explanation

The field keeps the vehicle and astronaut in free fall around the planet, with nearly equal accelerations. Under these conditions the cabin exerts negligible support force. A scale would read that force and give nearly zero even though gravitational acceleration in orbit is nonzero.
