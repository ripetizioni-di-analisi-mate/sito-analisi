In a circular orbit why is the field nonzero even though the astronaut feels weightless?

- Gravity stops inside the vehicle
- The satellite and astronaut fall with the same acceleration
- Satellite mass disappears
- Velocity is zero

## Explanation

Gravity accelerates both towards the centre. Apparent weight vanishes because there is no need for a support force between astronaut and vehicle.
