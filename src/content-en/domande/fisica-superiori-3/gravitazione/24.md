Two planets have a mass ratio of $4$ to $1$ and a radius ratio of $2$ to $1$. Find their surface field ratio.

- $2$
- $0.5$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

The larger radius exactly balances the larger mass. Different planets can therefore share a surface acceleration.

$$
g_1/g_2=\frac{M_1}{M_2}\left(\frac{R_2}{R_1}\right)^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}g_1/g_2&=\frac{4}{2^{2}}\\[4pt]&=1.\end{aligned}
$$
