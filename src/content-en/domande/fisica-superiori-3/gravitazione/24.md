Two planets have a mass ratio of $4$ to $1$ and a radius ratio of $2$ to $1$. Find their surface field ratio.

- $2$
- $0.5$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

Surface field grows in proportion to the mass and decreases with the square of the radius. For the first planet, four times the mass increases the numerator by four. Twice the radius increases the denominator by the same factor. The effects cancel and the fields are equal.

$$
\frac{g_1}{g_2}=\frac{M_1/M_2}{(R_1/R_2)^2}=\frac{4}{2^2}=1.
$$
