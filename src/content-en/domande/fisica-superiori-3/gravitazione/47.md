Two satellites orbit the same point source. The first follows a circle of radius $R$. The second follows an ellipse with minimum distance $R$ and maximum distance $7R$. What is the ratio of the period of the ellipse to that of the circle?

- $4$
- $7$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

The ellipse’s period uses its semimajor axis. This is the average of the two extreme distances from the focus and equals four circle radii. For the same source, the ratio of periods is that factor raised to the power of three halves, giving eight.

$$
\frac{T_{\mathrm{ell}}}{T_{\mathrm{circ}}}=\left(\frac{\frac{R+7R}{2}}{R}\right)^{\frac32}=4^{\frac32}=8.
$$
