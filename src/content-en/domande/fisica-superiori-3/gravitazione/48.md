At the two apses, the distances are $3R$ and $9R$. What is the periapsis speed divided by the apoapsis speed?

- $6$
- $1.5$
- $-3$
- $3$

## Explanation

At the two extreme distances the velocity is perpendicular to the radius. Angular momentum is therefore the mass times the radius times the speed. Because gravity is central, this product stays constant. At the smaller distance the speed has to be three times that at the larger distance.

$$
\frac{v_p}{v_a}=\frac{r_a}{r_p}=\frac{9R}{3R}=3.
$$
