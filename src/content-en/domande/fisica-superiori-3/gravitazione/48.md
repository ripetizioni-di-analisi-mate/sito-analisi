At the two apses, the distances are $3R$ and $9R$. Find periapsis speed divided by apoapsis speed.

- $6$
- $1.5$
- $-3$
- $3$

## Explanation

Gravitational torque about the focus is zero. At the apses velocity is tangential and radius times speed is conserved.

$$
v_p/v_a=r_a/r_p,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_p/v_a&=\frac{9}{3}\\[4pt]&=3.\end{aligned}
$$
