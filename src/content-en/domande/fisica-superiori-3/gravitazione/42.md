A motor moves a body away from a planet. The body’s potential energy rises from $-28$ to $-20\,\mathrm{MJ}$ and its kinetic energy increases by $3\,\mathrm{MJ}$. Only the motor and gravity do work. How much work does the motor supply?

- $8\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $11\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $5\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-11\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

The motor must supply both the increase in potential energy and the increase in kinetic energy. Gravity does negative work while the body moves outwards. The change in mechanical energy is the sum of eight and three megajoules. Keeping only the first term would omit the greater final speed.

$$
W_{\mathrm{ext}}=\Delta K+\Delta U=3+[-20-(-28)]=11\,\mathrm{MJ}.
$$
