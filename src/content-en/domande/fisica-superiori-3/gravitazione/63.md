Two equal gravitational field magnitudes point in opposite directions at one point. What is the net field?

- Twice one contribution
- Equal to one contribution
- Zero
- The product of magnitudes

## Explanation

The fields are added as vectors at the same point. When their magnitudes are equal and directions opposite, one contribution exactly cancels the other. A test mass would have zero net force at that instant. Each source would still exert its own force on the mass.
