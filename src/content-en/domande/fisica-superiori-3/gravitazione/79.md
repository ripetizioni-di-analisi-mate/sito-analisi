Where is the dominant source in a Kepler ellipse?

- At the centre of every ellipse
- On the minor axis away from the centre
- At one focus
- At a vertex of the ellipse

## Explanation

In the Kepler solution the central force points towards one focus of the ellipse. The dominant source occupies that focus. With positive eccentricity, the ellipse’s centre is displaced from the source. In a circle the two foci coincide with each other and with the centre.
