With zero at infinity, a body’s potential energy at a planet’s surface is $-2.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm J$. Zero is now chosen at the surface. What is the potential energy at twice the distance from the centre in the new reference?

- $-1.0\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $2.0\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm J$
- $1.0\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

With the original reference, doubling the distance halves the magnitude of potential energy. The change from the surface to the final point is positive. The new reference sets the initial value to zero and keeps that change. The new final value therefore equals the increase in potential energy.

$$
U'(2R)=U(2R)-U(R)=(-1.0+2.0)\cdot10^8\,\mathrm J=1.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm J.
$$
