What geometric condition must velocity satisfy in a circular orbit?

- It always points towards the centre
- It always points along the outward radius
- It is tangential and perpendicular to the radius
- It is vertical relative to Earth everywhere

## Explanation

A circle’s radius is perpendicular to the tangent at the point considered. Since the satellite stays at the same distance from the centre, its radial velocity component is zero. The tangential speed must also be the value at which gravity supplies the required centripetal acceleration.
