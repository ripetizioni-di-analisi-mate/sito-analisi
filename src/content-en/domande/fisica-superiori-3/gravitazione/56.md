A very large mass attracts a small one. How do their forces on each other compare?

- The force on the small mass is larger
- Equal magnitudes, same direction
- Zero force on the large mass
- Equal magnitudes, opposite directions

## Explanation

Each mass supplies the field acting on the other. The forces form an action–reaction pair and have the same magnitude. Acceleration can differ because each force is divided by the mass of the body on which it acts. The smaller mass responds with a larger acceleration.
