Two orbits have equal semimajor axes. The second central parameter is nine times the first. Find the period ratio, second to first.

- $0.333333$
- $0.666667$
- $0.166667$
- $-0.333333$

## Explanation

The common semimajor axis cancels in Kepler's third law. Period scales with the inverse square root of the parameter.

$$
T_2/T_1=\sqrt{\mu_1/\mu_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}T_2/T_1&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{9}}\\[4pt]&\simeq0.333333.\end{aligned}
$$
