Two orbits have equal semimajor axes. The second central parameter is nine times the first. Find the period ratio, second to first.

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The same orbital size makes the cubes of the semimajor axes equal. The period is then inversely proportional to the square root of the gravitational parameter. The second source has a parameter nine times as large, so its period is a third of the first. The ratio is below one.

$$
\frac{T_2}{T_1}=\sqrt{\frac{\mu_1}{\mu_2}}=\frac13.
$$
