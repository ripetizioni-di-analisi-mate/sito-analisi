From centre distance $r=7000000\,\mathrm m$ for a planet with $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$, find the minimum escape speed, with no atmosphere or later thrust.

- $1.06\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $21193\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5298.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-10596.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The escape threshold has zero total energy relative to infinity. Initial kinetic energy must equal the magnitude of potential energy.

$$
v_f=\sqrt{\frac{2\mu}r},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=\sqrt{2 \cdot 393000000000000 \cdot \frac{1}{7000000}}\\[4pt]&\simeq1.06\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
