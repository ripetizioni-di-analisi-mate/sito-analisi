From centre distance $r=7000000\,\mathrm m$ for a planet with $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$, find the minimum escape speed, with no atmosphere or later thrust.

- $1.06\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $21193\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5298.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-10596.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the escape threshold the body can travel arbitrarily far with a speed tending to zero. Its initial energy is therefore zero when potential energy is zero at infinity. Initially kinetic energy equals the magnitude of potential energy. The mass cancels, leaving the indicated square root.

$$
v_{\mathrm f}=\sqrt{\frac{2\mu}{r}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot3.93\cdot10^{14}}{7\cdot10^6}}\simeq1.06\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
