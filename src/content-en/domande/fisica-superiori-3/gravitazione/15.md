For two point masses of fixed mass the same sensor reads $72$, $18$ and $12\,\mu\mathrm N$ at separations $r$, $2r$ and $3r$. Treat the first reading as exact and the uncertainties as negligible. Which later reading is inconsistent with Newton’s law?

- Both
- Only the one at $2r$
- Only the one at $3r$
- Neither

## Explanation

With fixed masses, force times the squared separation must retain the same value. At twice the distance the prediction is a quarter of seventy-two, in agreement with the reading. At three times the distance the prediction is a ninth, or eight micronewtons. The third reading is inconsistent.

$$
F(2r)=\frac{72}{4}\,\mu\mathrm N=18\,\mu\mathrm N,\qquad F(3r)=\frac{72}{9}\,\mu\mathrm N=8\,\mu\mathrm N.
$$
