Why is knowing that potential energy is negative insufficient to decide whether a body can escape?

- Only the initial angular momentum is needed
- Kinetic energy is needed to evaluate the total
- Negative potential always makes escape impossible
- Gravity always does zero work

## Explanation

With zero at infinity, potential energy near the source is negative for both a bound body and an escaping body. Adding kinetic energy gives the total, which may be negative, zero or positive. That total determines whether an arbitrarily large distance is accessible.
