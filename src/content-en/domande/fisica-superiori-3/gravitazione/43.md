A body is raised from the surface of a planet of radius $R$ to height $h=R$. What is the ratio of the estimate $mg_0h$ to the exact potential energy gain?

- $4$
- $1$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

A local estimate ignores the decreasing field along the ascent. The exact denominator has $R+h$ in place of a second factor $R$.

$$
\Delta U_{\mathrm{est}}/\Delta U=(R+h)/R,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta U_{\mathrm{est}}/\Delta U&=1 + 1\\[4pt]&=2.\end{aligned}
$$
