A body is raised from the surface of a planet of radius $R$ to height $h=R$. What is the ratio of the estimate $mg_0h$ to the exact potential energy gain?

- $4$
- $1$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

At a height equal to the radius, the distance from the centre doubles. The exact potential energy change is half the product of mass, surface field and radius. Using surface gravity throughout assigns a constant force greater than the average force. The ratio is two.

$$
\frac{mg_0h}{\Delta U}=1+\frac hR=2.
$$
