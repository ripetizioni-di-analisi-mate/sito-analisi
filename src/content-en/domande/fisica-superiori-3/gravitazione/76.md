How much work does gravity do on a satellite over an arc of an ideal circular orbit?

- Constant positive
- Constant negative
- Proportional to the period squared
- Zero

## Explanation

At every point velocity is tangent to the circle while gravity is radial. Their dot product is zero, so the field’s power is zero throughout the arc. Potential energy also has the same value at both ends because the radius is constant.
