A satellite's circular kinetic energy is $9\,\mathrm{MJ}$. What is its potential energy with zero at infinity?

- $-18\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $18\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

The centripetal constraint gives kinetic energy equal to half the potential magnitude. Double kinetic energy and give it a negative sign.

$$
U=-2K,\qquad
\begin{aligned}U&=\left(-2\right) \cdot 9\\[4pt]&=-18\,\mathrm{MJ}.\end{aligned}
$$
