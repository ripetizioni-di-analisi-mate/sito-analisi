A satellite's circular kinetic energy is $9\,\mathrm{MJ}$. What is its potential energy with zero at infinity?

- $-18\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $18\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

On a circular orbit the gravity that sets the potential energy also provides the centripetal acceleration. Speed squared equals the gravitational parameter divided by radius. Kinetic energy is therefore half the magnitude of potential energy. With zero at infinity, potential energy is minus twice the kinetic energy.

$$
U=-2K=-2\cdot9=-18\,\mathrm{MJ}.
$$
