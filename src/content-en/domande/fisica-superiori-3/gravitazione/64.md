On the source line to the left of two positive masses, which way do their fields point?

- Both left
- One right and one left
- Always vertically
- Both right

## Explanation

Both positive sources attract towards their own positions. At a point to the left of both, each source lies to the right of the field point. The two contributions therefore point in the same direction, and the total magnitude is the sum of their magnitudes.
