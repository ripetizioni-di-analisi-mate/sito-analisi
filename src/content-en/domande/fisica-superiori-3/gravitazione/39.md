At a planet's surface, radius is $4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$ and field is $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the escape speed from there.

- $16000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-8000\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The surface field gives the gravitational parameter when multiplied by the square of the radius. For escape at the surface, one factor of radius cancels in the expression for the squared speed. Twice the product of field and radius remains. Take its square root for the speed.

$$
v_{\mathrm{fuga}}=\sqrt{2g_0R}=\sqrt{2\cdot8\cdot4\cdot10^6}=8000\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
