At a planet's surface, radius is $4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$ and field is $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the escape speed from there.

- $16000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-8000\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Substitute $\mu=g_0R^2$ from the field. One radius cancels, leaving the product of field and radius.

$$
v_e=\sqrt{2g_0R},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_e&=\sqrt{2 \cdot 8 \cdot 4 \cdot 10^{6}}\\[4pt]&=8000\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
