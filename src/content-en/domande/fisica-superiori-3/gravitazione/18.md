An attraction of $36\,\mathrm N$ decreases to $4\,\mathrm N$ when only separation changes. By what factor does distance increase?

- $6$
- $3$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The force ratio is inverse squared radius ratio. Take the square root of the larger force divided by the smaller.

$$
r_2/r_1=\sqrt{F_1/F_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}r_2/r_1&=\sqrt{\frac{36}{4}}\\[4pt]&=3.\end{aligned}
$$
