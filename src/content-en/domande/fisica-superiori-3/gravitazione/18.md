An attraction of $36\,\mathrm N$ decreases to $4\,\mathrm N$ when only separation changes. By what factor does distance increase?

- $6$
- $3$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The final force is one ninth of the initial force. With both masses fixed, the change comes entirely from the squared distance. That square must become nine times larger. The distance therefore grows by a factor of three, the square root of the ratio of force magnitudes.

$$
\frac{r_f}{r_i}=\sqrt{\frac{F_i}{F_f}}=\sqrt{\frac{36}{4}}=3.
$$
