A satellite follows an ellipse about a dominant source. At periapsis it is $4.50\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$ away and its speed is $1.422\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At apoapsis its distance is $9.00\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$. Find the apoapsis speed.

- $1.422\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.844\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.11\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.555\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The central force has zero torque about the focus. Angular momentum is conserved and the velocity at each apsis is perpendicular to the radius. The product of distance and speed is therefore conserved. At apoapsis the distance doubles and the speed halves.

$$
v_a=\frac{r_p v_p}{r_a}=\frac{4.50}{9.00}\cdot1.422\cdot10^4=7.11\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
