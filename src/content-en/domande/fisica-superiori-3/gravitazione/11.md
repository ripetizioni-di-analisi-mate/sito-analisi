A planet has gravitational parameter $\mu=GM=3.94\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. A satellite of negligible mass follows a circular orbit of radius $r=7800000\,\mathrm m$ from the centre. Find the orbital speed.

- $14214.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3553.62\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.11\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-7107.24\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Centripetal acceleration equals the field at the satellite. Solve for the speed using the radius measured from the centre.

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac\mu r},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\sqrt{\frac{394000000000000}{7800000}}\\[4pt]&\simeq7.11\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
