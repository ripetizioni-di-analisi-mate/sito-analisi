For the same body at the same point, one source contributes $-18\,\mathrm{MJ}$ of potential energy and a second contributes $-7\,\mathrm{MJ}$. Using the same zero for both contributions, what is the total potential energy?

- $-50\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-12.5\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

For a fixed test mass, add the potential energy contributions of the two sources with the same zero. Energy is a scalar, so add the signed values directly. The two negative contributions give a total more negative than the contribution of either source on its own.

$$
U_{\mathrm{tot}}=(-18-7)\,\mathrm{MJ}=-25\,\mathrm{MJ}.
$$
