One source gives a body potential energy $-18\,\mathrm{MJ}$ at a point. A second source gives $-7\,\mathrm{MJ}$ to that body at the same point. Find the total potential energy due to both sources.

- $-50\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-12.5\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

Potential energies are scalars and add with their signs when they share zero at infinity.

$$
U=U_1+U_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}U&=-18 - 7\\[4pt]&=-25\,\mathrm{MJ}.\end{aligned}
$$
