Why is gravitational torque about the focus zero?

- Force lies along the radius vector
- The satellite is stationary
- The central mass stays still
- Force is always tangential

## Explanation

Torque depends on the perpendicular distance from the reference point to the force’s line of action. Gravity points towards the focus, so its line passes through that point and the moment arm is zero. Angular momentum about the source is conserved for this reason.
