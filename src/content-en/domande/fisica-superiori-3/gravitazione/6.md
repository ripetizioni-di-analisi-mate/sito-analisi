A body of mass $m=160\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at distance $r=7600000\,\mathrm m$ from a planet with $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Find the potential energy with zero at infinity.

- $-1.65474\cdot10^{10}\,\mathrm J$
- $-8.27\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-4.13684\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $8.27368\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Moving the body to infinity requires energy, so the potential at this radius is negative. Divide the gravitational parameter times the mass by the radius.

$$
U=-\frac{\mu m}r,\qquad
\begin{aligned}U&=\left(-393000000000000\right) \cdot 160 \cdot \frac{1}{7600000}\\[4pt]&\simeq-8.27\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
