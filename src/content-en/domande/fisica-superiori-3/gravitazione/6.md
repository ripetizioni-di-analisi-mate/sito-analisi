A body of mass $m=160\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at distance $r=7600000\,\mathrm m$ from a planet with $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Find the potential energy with zero at infinity.

- $-1.65474\cdot10^{10}\,\mathrm J$
- $-8.27\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-4.13684\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $8.27368\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

With zero at infinity, bringing the masses closer lowers their potential energy. The value at finite separation is therefore negative. Multiplying the gravitational parameter by the body’s mass and dividing by the distance gives the magnitude. The body’s speed would determine only the kinetic part.

$$
U=-\frac{\mu m}{r}=-\frac{(3.93\cdot10^{14})\cdot160}{7.6\cdot10^6}\simeq-8.27\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J.
$$
