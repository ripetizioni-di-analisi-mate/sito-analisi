A spherical planet has surface field $g_0=9.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the field magnitude at distance $r=2.6R$ from its centre, where $R$ is its radius.

- $2.81065\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.41\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.702663\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-1.40533\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The planet's mass is the same at both radii. The ratio of local to surface field is the square of the inverse ratio of centre distances.

$$
g_r=\frac{g_0}{(r/R)^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}g_r&=\frac{19}{2 \cdot \left(\frac{13}{5}\right)^{2}}\\[4pt]&\simeq1.41\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
