A spherical planet has surface field $g_0=9.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the field magnitude at distance $r=2.6R$ from its centre, where $R$ is its radius?

- $3.65\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.41\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.733\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.541\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The source is spherical and the point is outside it. The field is reduced by the square of the ratio of surface radius to distance from the centre. The factor of two point six already gives that distance in planetary radii, so divide the surface field by its square.

$$
g=g_0\left(\frac Rr\right)^2=\frac{9.5}{2.6^2}\simeq1.41\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
