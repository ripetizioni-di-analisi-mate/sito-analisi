A circular satellite has mechanical energy $-10\,\mathrm{MJ}$. How much work must be supplied ideally to reach the escape threshold?

- $20\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $5\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $10\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

At threshold the total energy is zero relative to infinity. Positive work supplies the difference from the initial orbit.

$$
W=E_f-E_i,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=0 - -10\\[4pt]&=10\,\mathrm{MJ}.\end{aligned}
$$
