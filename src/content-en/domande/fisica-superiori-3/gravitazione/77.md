A satellite's mass doubles on the same circular orbit around a much more massive source. How does its period change?

- It stays the same
- It doubles
- It halves
- It increases by a factor of four

## Explanation

The orbiting mass cancels in the gravitational and centripetal balance. Period depends on the source and radius.
