A satellite's mass doubles on the same circular orbit around a much more massive source. How does its period change?

- It stays the same
- It doubles
- It halves
- It increases by a factor of four

## Explanation

The gravitational force doubles if the satellite’s mass doubles. Calculating acceleration divides the new force by the new mass, so the acceleration is unchanged. At fixed radius the speed and period stay the same. This comparison assumes the satellite remains much lighter than the source.
