A circular probe travels at a steady speed of $2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and takes $8000\,\mathrm s$ for one turn. Find the radius.

- $2.55\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm m$
- $5.09\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm m$
- $1.60\cdot10^{7}\,\mathrm m$
- $1.01\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The speed is constant, so multiplying it by the period gives the distance travelled in one revolution. In a circular orbit that distance is the circumference. Divide it by two pi to find the radius. The result is the distance from the centre of the planet.

$$
r=\frac{vT}{2\pi}=\frac{2000\cdot8000}{2\pi}\simeq2.55\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m.
$$
