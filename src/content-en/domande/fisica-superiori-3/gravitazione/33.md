A circular probe travels at a steady speed of $2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and takes $8000\,\mathrm s$ for one turn. Find the radius.

- $2.54648\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm m$
- $5.09296\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm m$
- $1.27324\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm m$
- $-2.54648\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Speed times period gives circumference. Divide by twice pi to find the radius.

$$
r=\frac{vT}{2\pi},\qquad
\begin{aligned}r&=2000 \cdot 8000 \cdot \frac{1}{2 \cdot \pi}\\[4pt]&\simeq2.54648\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
