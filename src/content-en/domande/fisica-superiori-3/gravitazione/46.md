An elliptical orbit has semimajor axis $5R$ and a minimum distance of $2R$ from the focus. What is its eccentricity?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="5*cos(t),4*sin(t),0,6.283185" linee="0,0 5,0" punti="0,0,pieno,C;3,0,pieno,F;5,0,pieno,P" xmin=-6 xmax=6 ymin=-4.7 ymax=4.7 titolo="C is the centre, F the focus and P the closest point"}

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The nearest point is one semimajor axis from the ellipse’s centre. Its distance from the focus is smaller because the focus is shifted towards it. The difference between the semimajor axis and minimum distance gives the centre-to-focus distance. Dividing by the semimajor axis gives the eccentricity.

$$
e=\frac{a-r_p}{a}=\frac{5R-2R}{5R}=\frac35.
$$
