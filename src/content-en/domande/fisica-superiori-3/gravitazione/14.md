A body of mass $m=180\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at distance $r=7800000\,\mathrm m$ from a planet with $\mu=GM=3.94\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Find the potential energy with zero at infinity.

- $-1.81846\cdot10^{10}\,\mathrm J$
- $-9.09\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-4.54615\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $9.09231\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Moving the body to infinity requires energy, so the potential at this radius is negative. Divide the gravitational parameter times the mass by the radius.

$$
U=-\frac{\mu m}r,\qquad
\begin{aligned}U&=\left(-394000000000000\right) \cdot 180 \cdot \frac{1}{7800000}\\[4pt]&\simeq-9.09\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
