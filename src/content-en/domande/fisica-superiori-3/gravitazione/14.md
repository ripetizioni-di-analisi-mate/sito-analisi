A probe initially has $K_i=2.00\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J$ and $U_i=-6.00\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J$, with zero at infinity. Only gravity acts. When kinetic energy reaches $5.09\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J$, what is the potential energy?

- $-4.00\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-9.09\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $-6.00\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$
- $1.09\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Mechanical energy stays constant because only gravity acts. The initial sum is negative. The new kinetic energy is greater than the initial value, so potential energy must fall by the same amount. Subtracting the final kinetic energy from the total gives the required value.

$$
U_f=E-K_f=(2.00-6.00-5.09)\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J=-9.09\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J.
$$
