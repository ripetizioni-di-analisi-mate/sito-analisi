A planet's parameter is $5\cdot10^{12}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Find circular speed $5\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$ from its centre.

- $1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $500\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The parameter divided by radius gives one million square metres per second squared. Take its square root for speed.

$$
v=\sqrt{\mu/r},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\sqrt{\frac{5 \cdot 10^{12}}{5 \cdot 10^{6}}}\\[4pt]&=1000\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
