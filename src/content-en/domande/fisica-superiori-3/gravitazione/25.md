At a point where $\dfrac{\mu}{r}=1.0\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, a probe’s tangential velocity component is $1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and its outward radial component is $500\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which description fits these data in the Kepler model?

- Elliptical orbit with distance increasing at that instant
- Circular orbit
- Escape with positive energy
- Straight radial motion

## Explanation

The distance increases at that instant because the radial component is outward. The specific energy uses the squares of both velocity components and is negative. The tangential component gives nonzero angular momentum, so the ideal trajectory is an ellipse.

$$
\varepsilon=\frac{1000^2+500^2}{2}-1.0\cdot10^6=-3.75\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}.
$$
