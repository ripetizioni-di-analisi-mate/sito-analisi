Two satellites have circular orbits about the same planet. The field along the second orbit is one sixteenth of that along the first. What is the circular speed ratio $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1}$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The field decreases with the square of the radius. Reducing it to one sixteenth requires quadrupling the distance. Circular speed instead varies as the inverse square root of radius, so it halves when the radius quadruples. Applying the field law directly to the speed would give a different factor.

$$
\frac{r_2}{r_1}=\sqrt{16}=4,\qquad \frac{v_2}{v_1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt4}=\frac12.
$$
