Is the constant in $T^2/a^3$ the same for satellites around planets of different masses?

- Yes in every system
- It depends on the individual satellites' masses
- It depends only on the unit of T
- No, it depends on the central parameter

## Explanation

The gravitational parameter is in the denominator. Different central masses give different parameters.
