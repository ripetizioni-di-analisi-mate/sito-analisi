Is the constant in $T^2/a^3$ the same for satellites around planets of different masses?

- Yes in every system
- It depends on the individual satellites' masses
- It depends only on the unit of T
- No, it depends on the central parameter

## Explanation

The ratio of squared period to cubed semimajor axis is inversely proportional to the source’s gravitational parameter. Since the universal constant G is the same, a more massive source gives a smaller ratio. It can be treated as constant only when the central source is the same.
