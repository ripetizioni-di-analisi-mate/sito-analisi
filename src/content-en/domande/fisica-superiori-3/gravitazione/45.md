Two probes around different planets have the same period. The second planet’s gravitational parameter is eight times the first. What is the semimajor axis ratio $\dfrac{a_2}{a_1}$?

- $2$
- $8$
- $\dfrac12$
- $4$

## Explanation

Equal periods require the cubed semimajor axis divided by the gravitational parameter to be the same. The second planet’s parameter is eight times larger, so the cubed semimajor axis must also increase by eight. Taking the cube root gives the ratio.

$$
\frac{a_2}{a_1}=\sqrt[3]{\frac{\mu_2}{\mu_1}}=\sqrt[3]{8}=2.
$$
