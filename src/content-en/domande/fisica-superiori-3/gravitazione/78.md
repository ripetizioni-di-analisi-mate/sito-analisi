Around the same source, how do speed and period change for a wider circular orbit?

- Both increase
- Lower speed and longer period
- Both decrease
- Higher speed and shorter period

## Explanation

For a given planet, circular speed falls as the inverse square root of radius. The period is the circumference divided by speed. A wider orbit requires the satellite to travel farther at a lower speed, so the time needed for a revolution increases.
