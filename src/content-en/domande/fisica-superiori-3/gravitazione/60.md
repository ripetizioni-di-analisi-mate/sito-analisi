Two masses remain at rest due to external supports. What follows about their mutual gravitational force?

- It is always zero
- It grows without bound in time
- It always points along the support normal
- It can be nonzero and balanced

## Explanation

Each body is stationary when the forces acting on it sum to zero. The support can exert a force opposing the gravitational attraction. The two mutual attractions act on different bodies, so balancing either body requires a force applied to that same body.
