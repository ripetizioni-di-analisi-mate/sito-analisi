Two masses remain at rest due to external supports. What follows about their mutual gravitational force?

- It is always zero
- It grows without bound in time
- It always points along the support normal
- It can be nonzero and balanced

## Explanation

Rest implies zero net force. Existing gravity can be balanced by the supports.
