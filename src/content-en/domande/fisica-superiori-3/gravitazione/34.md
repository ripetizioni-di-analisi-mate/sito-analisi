How many complete revolutions does a satellite of period $90$ minutes make in $24$ hours?

- $32$
- $16$
- $8$
- $-16$

## Explanation

Use the same units before dividing the total interval by the period. Twenty-four hours are one thousand four hundred and forty minutes. Dividing by ninety gives a whole number, so the satellite completes sixteen orbits and returns to its starting position at the end of the interval.

$$
N=\frac{24\cdot60}{90}=16.
$$
