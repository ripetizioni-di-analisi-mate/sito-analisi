How many complete revolutions does a satellite of period $90$ minutes make in $24$ hours?

- $32$
- $16$
- $8$
- $-16$

## Explanation

Convert hours to minutes before dividing. The ratio counts revolutions.

$$
n=\frac{\Delta t}{T},\qquad
\begin{aligned}n&=24 \cdot 60 \cdot \frac{1}{90}\\[4pt]&=16.\end{aligned}
$$
