Which other unit equals $\mathrm{N/kg}$?

- $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg/s^2}$

## Explanation

A newton is a kilogram metre per second squared. Dividing by kilograms leaves acceleration units.
