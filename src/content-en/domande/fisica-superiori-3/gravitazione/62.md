Which other unit equals $\mathrm{N/kg}$?

- $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg/s^2}$

## Explanation

One newton is one kilogram metre per second squared. Dividing by kilograms cancels the mass unit and leaves metres per second squared. For a mass falling under gravity alone, the field expressed in newtons per kilogram gives the value of its acceleration.
