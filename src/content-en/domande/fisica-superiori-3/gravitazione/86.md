At minimum escape speed, what does speed tend to after unlimited outward travel?

- Its initial value
- Zero
- Twice its initial value
- Infinity

## Explanation

Minimum escape speed corresponds to zero total energy when potential energy is zero at infinity. As the body travels outwards, potential energy rises towards zero and kinetic energy decreases. At any finite distance kinetic energy is positive, while at arbitrarily large distances it tends to zero.
