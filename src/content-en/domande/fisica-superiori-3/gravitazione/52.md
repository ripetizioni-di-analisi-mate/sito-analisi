A circular satellite with energy $-12\,\mathrm{MJ}$ changes to a circular orbit of twice the radius with the same mass. Find the energy change.

::grafico{f="-12/x" punti="1,-12,pieno;2,-6,pieno" xmin=.8 xmax=3.5 ymin=-17 ymax=1 nomi="r/r_1,E (MJ)" titolo="Circular orbital energy rises when the radius doubles"}

- $12\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $3\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $6\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

Energy is negative and inversely proportional to radius. It becomes less negative at twice the radius, giving a positive change.

$$
\Delta E=E_f-E_i,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta E&=- \frac{12}{2} - -12\\[4pt]&=6\,\mathrm{MJ}.\end{aligned}
$$
