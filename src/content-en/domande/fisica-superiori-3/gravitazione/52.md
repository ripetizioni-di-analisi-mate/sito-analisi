A satellite of unchanged mass transfers from a circular orbit of energy $-12\,\mathrm{MJ}$ to another circular orbit around the same source. During the transfer it loses $4\,\mathrm{MJ}$ of mechanical energy. What is the ratio $\dfrac{r_f}{r_i}$ of the radii?

- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The final total falls to minus sixteen megajoules. On a circular orbit, energy magnitude is inversely proportional to radius at fixed satellite mass and source. The radius ratio is therefore the inverse ratio of the energy magnitudes. The final orbit lies closer to the source.

$$
E_f=(-12-4)\,\mathrm{MJ}=-16\,\mathrm{MJ},\qquad \frac{r_f}{r_i}=\frac{12}{16}=\frac34.
$$
