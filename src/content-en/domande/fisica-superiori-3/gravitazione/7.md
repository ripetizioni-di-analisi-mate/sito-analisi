Two satellites orbit the same central mass. The first has period $T_1=2.6\,\mathrm h$. The second semimajor axis is $a_2=2.6a_1$. What is the period $T_2$?

- $6.76\,\mathrm h$
- $17.6\,\mathrm h$
- $10.9\,\mathrm h$
- $4.92\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

Because both satellites orbit the same source, the gravitational parameter cancels from the period ratio. The period grows as the semimajor axis to the power of three halves. Multiply the initial two point six hours by that factor, so the result remains in hours.

$$
T_2=T_1\left(\frac{a_2}{a_1}\right)^{\frac32}=2.6\cdot2.6^{\frac32}\,\mathrm h\simeq10.9\,\mathrm h.
$$
