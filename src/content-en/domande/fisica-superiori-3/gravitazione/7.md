Two satellites orbit the same central mass. The first has period $T_1=2.6\,\mathrm h$. The second semimajor axis is $a_2=2.6a_1$. Find period $T_2$.

- $21.8003\,\mathrm h$
- $5.45009\,\mathrm h$
- $10.9\,\mathrm h$
- $-10.9002\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

Both orbits have the same constant in Kepler's third law. Multiply the first period by the semimajor axis ratio to the three-halves power.

$$
T_2=T_1(a_2/a_1)^{3/2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}T_2&=\frac{13 \cdot \left(\frac{13}{5}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}}{5}\\[4pt]&\simeq10.9\,\mathrm h.\end{aligned}
$$
