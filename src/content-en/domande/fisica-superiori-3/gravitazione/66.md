An astronaut weighs less at another planet's surface. What happens to their inertial mass?

- It decreases in the same ratio
- It stays the same
- It vanishes
- It increases in inverse proportion

## Explanation

Inertial mass is the factor relating net force to acceleration. Changing planets alters the gravitational field and hence the weight at fixed mass. The same applied force would give the same contribution to the astronaut’s acceleration at either location.
