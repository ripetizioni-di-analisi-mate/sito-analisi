Assuming both orbits are circular, A is observed at radius $4.0\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$ with speed $3000\,\mathrm{m/s}$. B is observed at radius $6.0\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$ with speed $2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{\mu_B}{\mu_A}$ of the central parameters inferred from the two readings?

- $1$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac49$

## Explanation

For each orbit the estimated parameter is the radius times the square of the speed. In the ratio the distance factor is three halves and the squared speed factor is four ninths. Their product is two thirds, so those exact readings would be inconsistent with a shared source.

$$
\frac{\mu_B}{\mu_A}=\frac{6.0}{4.0}\left(\frac{2000}{3000}\right)^2=\frac32\cdot\frac49=\frac23.
$$
