A circular satellite travels at $3000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at distance $4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$. Find the source's gravitational parameter.

- $7.2\cdot10^{13}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $1.8\cdot10^{13}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $3.6\cdot10^{13}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $-3.6\cdot10^{13}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$

## Explanation

Equate centripetal acceleration with the gravitational field and solve for the parameter. Multiply squared speed by the radius.

$$
\mu=v^2r,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\mu&=3000^{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 10^{6}\\[4pt]&=3.6\cdot10^{13}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
