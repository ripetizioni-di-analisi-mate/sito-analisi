At one position the minimum escape speed is $8.4\,\mathrm{km/s}$. A probe leaves radially outwards and must still move at $6.3\,\mathrm{km/s}$ at infinity. Neglecting dissipation, what launch speed is required in metres per second?

- $1.05\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.4\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.47\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.3\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Initial kinetic energy must cover the rise in potential energy and leave the required kinetic energy at infinity. The first part corresponds to the minimum escape speed. Add the squares of the two speeds, take the square root and convert to metres per second.

$$
v_i=\sqrt{v_{\mathrm{fuga}}^2+v_\infty^2}=\sqrt{8.4^2+6.3^2}\,\mathrm{km/s}=1.05\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
