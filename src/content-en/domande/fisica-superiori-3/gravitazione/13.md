From centre distance $r=7200000\,\mathrm m$ for a planet with $\mu=GM=3.94\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$, find the minimum escape speed, with no atmosphere or later thrust.

- $1.05\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20923.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5230.78\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-10461.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The escape threshold has zero total energy relative to infinity. Initial kinetic energy must equal the magnitude of potential energy.

$$
v_f=\sqrt{\frac{2\mu}r},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=\sqrt{2 \cdot 394000000000000 \cdot \frac{1}{7200000}}\\[4pt]&\simeq1.05\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
