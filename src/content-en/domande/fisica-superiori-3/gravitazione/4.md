A planet has $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. A satellite travels in a circular orbit of radius $r=7600000\,\mathrm m$. What is the period?

- $13281.1\,\mathrm s$
- $3320.27\,\mathrm s$
- $-6640.55\,\mathrm s$
- $6.64\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The satellite covers one circumference per period at constant speed. Substituting the square root of the gravitational parameter divided by radius for the speed gives the period. Inside the root, the ratio of cubic metres to the gravitational parameter has units of seconds squared.

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{(7.6\cdot10^6)^3}{3.93\cdot10^{14}}}\simeq6.64\cdot10^3\,\mathrm s.
$$
