A planet has $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. A satellite travels in a circular orbit of radius $r=7600000\,\mathrm m$. Find the period.

- $13281.1\,\mathrm s$
- $3320.27\,\mathrm s$
- $-6640.55\,\mathrm s$
- $6.64\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Period is circumference divided by circular speed. Substituting that speed leaves the radius cubed under the square root.

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{r^3}{\mu}},\qquad
\begin{aligned}T&=2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{\frac{7600000^{3}}{393000000000000}}\\[4pt]&\simeq6.64\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm s.\end{aligned}
$$
