A probe has orbital period $6000\,\mathrm s$ at radius $6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$ on a circular orbit. Find orbital speed.

- $12566.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3141.59\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6283.19\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6283.19\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

One period covers a full circumference. Speed stays constant on that orbit.

$$
v=\frac{2\pi r}{T},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=2 \cdot \pi \cdot 6 \cdot 10^{6} \cdot \frac{1}{6000}\\[4pt]&\simeq6283.19\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
