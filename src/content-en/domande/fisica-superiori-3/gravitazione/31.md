A satellite in a circular orbit takes $500\pi\,\mathrm s$ to travel from one point to the diametrically opposite point. The gravitational field along the orbit has magnitude $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the orbital speed?

- $1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $500\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Opposite points are half a revolution apart, so the period is twice the measured time. In circular motion acceleration is the speed times angular frequency, which is two pi divided by the period. The field divided by the angular frequency gives the speed.

$$
v=\frac{gT}{2\pi}=\frac{4.0\cdot1000\pi}{2\pi}=2000\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
