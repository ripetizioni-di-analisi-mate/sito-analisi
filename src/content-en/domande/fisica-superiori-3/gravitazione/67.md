How does potential energy with zero at infinity change as you move farther from a spherical source?

- It decreases to more negative values
- It stays unchanged
- It increases towards zero
- It immediately becomes positive

## Explanation

Potential energy is negative and its magnitude is inversely proportional to the distance from the centre. Increasing the distance decreases its magnitude, so the value rises. With zero chosen at infinity, the limiting value is zero while the value at every finite distance remains negative.
