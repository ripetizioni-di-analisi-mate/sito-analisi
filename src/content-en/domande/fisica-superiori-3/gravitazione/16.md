In a two-point-mass model, one mass is tripled. By what factor must the distance change to leave the attraction unchanged?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$
- $3$
- $9$
- $\sqrt3$

## Explanation

Tripling one mass at fixed distance would triple the force. To offset this, the denominator must also be multiplied by three. Since the denominator contains the square of the distance, its factor is the square root of three. Tripling the distance would divide the new force by nine.

$$
\frac{3}{\left(\frac{r_f}{r_i}\right)^2}=1,\qquad \frac{r_f}{r_i}=\sqrt3.
$$
