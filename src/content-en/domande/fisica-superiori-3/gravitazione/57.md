For fixed masses, which force-versus-distance curve is appropriate?

- Inverse-square decrease
- Increasing straight line
- Horizontal line
- Increasing parabola

## Explanation

With fixed masses the only distance dependence is the square in the denominator. Doubling the distance gives a quarter of the force, and tripling it gives a ninth. The graph falls steeply at short distances and approaches zero at large distances.
