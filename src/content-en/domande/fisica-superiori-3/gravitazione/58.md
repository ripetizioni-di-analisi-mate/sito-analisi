Why does evaluating the field inside a solid material sphere require extra care?

- A field cannot exist inside
- The point-mass equivalence for the whole mass applies outside
- Radius becomes negative
- The gravitational constant changes

## Explanation

Inside a spherically symmetric body, the field uses the mass enclosed within the point’s radius. Spherical shells outside that radius give zero net field inside them. For a uniform sphere, this makes the field increase from zero at the centre.
