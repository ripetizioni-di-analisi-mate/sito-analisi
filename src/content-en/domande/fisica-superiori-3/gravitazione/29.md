A circular satellite moves at $8000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on radius $8\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$. Find its acceleration magnitude.

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The acceleration points radially towards the source. Find its magnitude from squared speed and radius of curvature.

$$
g=v^2/r,\qquad
\begin{aligned}g&=\frac{8000^{2}}{8 \cdot 10^{6}}\\[4pt]&=8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
