A circular satellite moves at $8000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on radius $8\cdot10^6\,\mathrm m$. Find its acceleration magnitude.

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The speed is constant, but the velocity direction changes as the satellite follows the circle. Centripetal acceleration measures that change. Dividing the speed squared by the radius gives eight metres per second squared, directed towards the centre of the circle.

$$
a_c=\frac{v^2}{r}=\frac{8000^2}{8\cdot10^6}=8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
