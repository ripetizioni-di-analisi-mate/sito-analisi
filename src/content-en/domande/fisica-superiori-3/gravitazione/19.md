Isolated masses of $3$ and $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ attract one another. What is the ratio of acceleration magnitudes, smaller mass to larger mass?

- $8$
- $2$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The mutual gravitational forces have the same magnitude. To obtain the accelerations, divide that magnitude by the mass of the body receiving the force. The force cancels from the ratio. The three-kilogram body accelerates at four times the magnitude of the twelve-kilogram body.

$$
\frac{a_1}{a_2}=\frac{\frac F3}{\frac F{12}}=\frac{12}{3}=4.
$$
