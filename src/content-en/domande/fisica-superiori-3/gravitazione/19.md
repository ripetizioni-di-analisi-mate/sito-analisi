Isolated masses of $3$ and $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ attract one another. Find the ratio of acceleration magnitudes, smaller mass to larger mass.

- $8$
- $2$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Newton's third law gives equal force magnitudes. The lighter body accelerates more because the same force is divided by less mass.

$$
a_1/a_2=\frac{m_2}{m_1},\qquad
\begin{aligned}a_1/a_2&=\frac{12}{3}\\[4pt]&=4.\end{aligned}
$$
