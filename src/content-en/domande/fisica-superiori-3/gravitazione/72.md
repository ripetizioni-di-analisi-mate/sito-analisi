A body completes a closed trip in a planet's static field. What is the total gravitational work?

- Negative for every path
- Positive even on returning
- Proportional to the maximum altitude
- Zero

## Explanation

A static gravitational field is conservative, and its work is the negative of the potential energy change. A closed path ends at its starting position, where potential energy has the same value. The positive and negative amounts of work along the path sum to zero.
