In an elliptical orbit, which quantity has the same value in every interval of equal duration?

- Path length covered by the satellite
- Angle swept at every position
- Speed
- Area swept by the radius vector

## Explanation

The force exerts zero torque about the focus, so angular momentum is conserved. The area swept per unit time is proportional to that momentum and stays constant. Near the source, the shorter radius is compensated by a faster change in angle.
