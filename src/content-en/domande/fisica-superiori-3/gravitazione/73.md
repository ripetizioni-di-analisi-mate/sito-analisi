Which force supplies net centripetal force in an ideal circular orbit?

- Gravity
- An extra radial force added to gravity
- Satellite inertia
- Atmospheric friction

## Explanation

In the ideal orbit gravity points towards the centre and is the net force. The acceleration needed to turn the circular velocity points the same way. Equating gravitational and centripetal acceleration gives the speed compatible with that radius.
