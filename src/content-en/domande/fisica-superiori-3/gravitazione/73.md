Which force supplies net centripetal force in an ideal circular orbit?

- Gravity
- An extra radial force added to gravity
- Satellite inertia
- Atmospheric friction

## Explanation

Centripetal describes the net force directed towards the centre. In this model gravity is its only contribution.
