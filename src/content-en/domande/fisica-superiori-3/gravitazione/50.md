At distance $r$ from a source, a satellite’s velocity is tangent to the circle centred on the source. Its energies are $K=12\,\mathrm{MJ}$ and $U=-16\,\mathrm{MJ}$ with zero at infinity. What is the ratio of the maximum distance on the ideal orbit to $r$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The energies sum to a negative value and the orbit is bound. Their ratio gives a semimajor axis twice the current radius. Tangential velocity makes the position an apsis. Since the current radius is smaller than the semimajor axis, it is the periapsis distance.

$$
\begin{aligned}E&=12-16=-4\,\mathrm{MJ}\\[6pt]\frac a r&=\frac{U}{2E}=2\\[6pt]\frac{r_a}{r}&=\frac{2a-r}{r}=3.\end{aligned}
$$
