A circular satellite has potential energy $-16\,\mathrm{MJ}$. Find its total orbital mechanical energy.

- $-16\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $8\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

Circular kinetic energy is half the magnitude of potential energy, so their sum is half the potential energy.

$$
E=U/2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}E&=- \frac{16}{2}\\[4pt]&=-8\,\mathrm{MJ}.\end{aligned}
$$
