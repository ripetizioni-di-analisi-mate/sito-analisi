What sign does the mechanical energy of a circular satellite have with zero potential at infinity?

- Negative
- Positive
- Zero
- It changes sign every half turn

## Explanation

Circular kinetic energy is positive and equals half the magnitude of potential energy. With zero at infinity, potential energy is negative, so the total remains negative. The satellite stays bound to the source if the total is conserved. Reaching the escape threshold needs additional energy.
