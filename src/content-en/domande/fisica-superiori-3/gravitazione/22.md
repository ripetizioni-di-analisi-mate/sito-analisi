Two point masses of $9M$ and $M$ are $8\,\mathrm m$ apart. Between them the total gravitational field vanishes at one point. How far is that point from the larger mass?

- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Between the masses the fields point in opposite directions. At distance $x$ from the larger mass, the distance from the other is $8-x$ in metres. Set their magnitudes equal and take the positive root on each side because both distances are positive. The point lies closer to the smaller mass.

$$
\frac{9}{x^2}=\frac{1}{(8-x)^2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3(8-x)=x,\qquad x=6\,\mathrm m.
$$
