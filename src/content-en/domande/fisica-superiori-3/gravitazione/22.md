At the same point, one source produces a field of magnitude $14\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ pointing left and the other a field of magnitude $8\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ pointing right. Find the resultant field component with right positive.

- $-12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The fields are opposing collinear vectors. Their sum is negative because the leftward component is larger.

$$
g_x=g_{\mathrm{right}}-g_{\mathrm{left}},\qquad
\begin{aligned}g_x&=8 - 14\\[4pt]&=-6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
