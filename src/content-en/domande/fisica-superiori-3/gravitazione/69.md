What effect does changing the potential energy's additive constant have?

- Individual U values shift, differences stay unchanged
- The gravitational field changes
- Trajectories change
- Work between two points changes

## Explanation

The new constant is added to potential energy at every position. Subtracting the values at two endpoints cancels the shared addition. Gravitational work depends on that difference and therefore stays the same. The escape threshold must also be expressed relative to the new value at infinity.
