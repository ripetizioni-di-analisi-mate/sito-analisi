During a radial fall towards the source, what is the sign of gravity's work?

- Negative
- Zero
- It always alternates sign
- Positive

## Explanation

During the fall the force and radial displacement both point towards the source. Their dot product is positive. The same conclusion follows from energy because the distance decreases and potential energy falls. The work done by the field is the opposite of that negative change.
