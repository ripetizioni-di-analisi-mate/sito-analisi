For orbits with equal semimajor axes around the same source, what necessarily agrees?

- Shape
- Period
- All instantaneous speeds
- Starting position

## Explanation

Kepler’s third law relates period to semimajor axis and the source’s gravitational parameter. Both quantities agree in the two orbits, so their periods coincide. Their shapes can still differ, which can give different extreme distances and different speeds.
