At the surface of a spherical planet of radius $R$, the minimum escape speed is $6000\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At what distance from the centre, in units of $R$, does it drop to $4000\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $1.5$
- $\dfrac49$
- $2.25$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

The square of escape speed is inversely proportional to distance because the gravitational parameter is unchanged. The ratio of the required distance to the surface radius is therefore the square of the inverse speed ratio. It must exceed one because speed decreases as distance increases.

$$
\frac{r}{R}=\left(\frac{6000}{4000}\right)^2=2.25.
$$
