At a radius, $\mu/r=4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Find minimum escape speed with no atmosphere.

- $5656.85\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1414.21\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2828.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2828.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy per unit mass must match the magnitude of potential energy per unit mass. The factor of two comes from the one-half kinetic-energy coefficient.

$$
v_e=\sqrt{2\mu/r},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_e&=\sqrt{2 \cdot 4 \cdot 10^{6}}\\[4pt]&\simeq2828.43\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
