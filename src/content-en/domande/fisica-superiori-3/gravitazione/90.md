A circular probe receives enough energy to reach zero total energy and is directed outward. What outcome is possible in the ideal model?

- It remains in a circular orbit at the same radius
- It reaches arbitrarily large distances
- Potential energy grows without bound
- Gravity vanishes at a finite radius

## Explanation

After the manoeuvre, zero total energy allows the outward-moving probe to reach arbitrarily large distances. As it climbs, potential energy rises towards the zero chosen at infinity. Kinetic energy decreases and tends to zero, so the probe is exactly at the escape threshold.
