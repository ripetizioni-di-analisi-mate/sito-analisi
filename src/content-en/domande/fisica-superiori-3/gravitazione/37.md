A body is launched outward with kinetic energy $13\,\mathrm{MJ}$ and potential energy $-9\,\mathrm{MJ}$, with no further work. What kinetic energy remains at infinity?

- $4\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $8\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $2\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

Potential energy tends to zero at infinity. The initial total remains, positive relative to the escape threshold.

$$
K_\infty=K_i+U_i,\qquad
\begin{aligned}K_\infty&=13 - 9\\[4pt]&=4\,\mathrm{MJ}.\end{aligned}
$$
