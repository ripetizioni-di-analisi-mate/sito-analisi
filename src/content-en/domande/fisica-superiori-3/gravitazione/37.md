A probe starts with kinetic energy $13\,\mathrm{MJ}$ and potential energy $-9\,\mathrm{MJ}$ measured from zero at infinity. Only gravity acts on the outward leg. What is its kinetic energy when the potential energy reaches $-3\,\mathrm{MJ}$?

- $7\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $4\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $1\,\mathrm{MJ}$
- $16\,\mathrm{MJ}$

## Explanation

The total energy is four megajoules and stays constant. The requested position is still at a finite distance and its potential energy is negative. To retain the same total, kinetic energy must be three megajoules higher than its eventual value at infinity. Subtract potential energy from the total.

$$
K_f=E-U_f=13-9-(-3)=7\,\mathrm{MJ}.
$$
