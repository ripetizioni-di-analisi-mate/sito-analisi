A $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ probe in free fall towards a small asteroid has gravitational acceleration of magnitude $1.70\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude, in newtons, of the force the probe exerts on the asteroid?

- $1.19\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $1.70\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm N$
- $2.43\cdot10^{-10}\,\mathrm N$
- $1.19\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

To find the force on the probe, multiply its mass by the measured acceleration. Newton’s third law gives the same magnitude for the force the probe exerts on the asteroid. The asteroid’s mass would be needed to find its acceleration, while the force follows without it.

$$
F=70\cdot1.70\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm N=1.19\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N.
$$
