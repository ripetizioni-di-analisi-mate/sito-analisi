Two point masses $m_1=180\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=320\,\mathrm{kg}$ are separated by $r=1.8\,\mathrm m$. Find the gravitational force magnitude. Take $G=6.67\cdot10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$.

- $1.19\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $2.37156\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $5.92889\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm N$
- $-1.18578\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Use kilograms for both masses and metres for the separation. Square the separation before dividing.

$$
F=\frac{Gm_1m_2}{r^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F&=667 \cdot \frac{1}{10000000000000} \cdot 180 \cdot 320 \cdot \frac{1}{\left(\frac{9}{5}\right)^{2}}\\[4pt]&\simeq1.19\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
