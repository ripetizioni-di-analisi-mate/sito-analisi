Two satellites follow concentric circular orbits in the same plane and direction. Their periods are $2\,\mathrm h$ and $3\,\mathrm h$. Initially they lie on the same ray from the centre. How long until the first realignment on the same ray?

- $3\,\mathrm h$
- $6\,\mathrm h$
- $1\,\mathrm h$
- $12\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

In an hour the faster satellite turns through half a revolution and the other through a third. The lead grows by one sixth of a revolution each hour. The first realignment on the same ray occurs after a full revolution has been gained. Half a revolution would leave them opposite.

$$
t=\frac{1}{\frac12-\frac13}\,\mathrm h=6\,\mathrm h.
$$
