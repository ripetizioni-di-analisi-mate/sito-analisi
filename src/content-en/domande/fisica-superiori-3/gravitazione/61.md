Why does a body of unchanged mass weigh less at greater altitude?

- The gravitational field is smaller
- Its mass decreases
- $G$ decreases
- Momentum determines weight

## Explanation

Gravitational weight is the mass times the field magnitude at that point. Climbing increases the distance to the planet’s centre, and the field falls with the inverse square of the distance. The mass stays the same, so the weight follows the decrease in the field.
