A body is at distance $r_i$ from a source with $U_i=-30\,\mathrm{MJ}$, with zero at infinity. A motor transfers it to a greater distance and does $20\,\mathrm{MJ}$ of work. Kinetic energy is the same at the endpoints. What is $\dfrac{r_f}{r_i}$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac13$
- $2$
- $1.5$

## Explanation

Because kinetic energy is unchanged between the endpoints, the motor work raises potential energy by twenty megajoules. The final value is minus ten megajoules, one third of the initial magnitude. The inverse dependence on distance means that the final radius is three times the initial one.

$$
U_f=-30\,\mathrm{MJ}+20\,\mathrm{MJ}=-10\,\mathrm{MJ},\qquad \frac{r_f}{r_i}=\frac{|U_i|}{|U_f|}=3.
$$
