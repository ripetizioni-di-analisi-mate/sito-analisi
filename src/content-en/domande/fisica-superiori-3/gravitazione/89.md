Which combination makes a satellite geostationary?

- Prograde equatorial circle with sidereal period
- Inclined orbit with sidereal period
- Slow polar circle
- Retrograde equatorial circle of the same period

## Explanation

Staying above one point requires fixed altitude, latitude and longitude in Earth's frame. An equatorial orbit in the rotation sense meets these conditions at the required period.
