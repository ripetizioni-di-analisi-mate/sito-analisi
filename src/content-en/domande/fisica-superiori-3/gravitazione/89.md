Which combination makes a satellite geostationary?

- Prograde equatorial circle with sidereal period
- Inclined orbit with sidereal period
- Slow polar circle
- Retrograde equatorial circle of the same period

## Explanation

The period must equal Earth’s rotation period relative to the stars. The orbit must also lie in the equatorial plane and travel in the same direction as Earth’s rotation. A circle keeps the distance constant, and the other conditions keep the surface point directly below the satellite fixed.
