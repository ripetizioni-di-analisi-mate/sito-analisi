An object starts from rest and accelerates at $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $2\,\mathrm s$. What fraction of its two-second distance is covered in the first second?

- $\frac{1}{4}$.
- $\frac{1}{2}$.
- $\frac{1}{3}$.
- $\frac{3}{4}$.

## Explanation

The factor $\frac12a$ cancels. After one second the object has covered one quarter of its two-second distance because the mean speed in the second interval is greater.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{s_1}{s_2}&=\frac{1^2}{2^2}\\[4pt]
&= \frac{1}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$
