Two $v(t)$ graphs are distinct parallel lines. How do their accelerations compare?

- Their signs are always opposite.
- They are equal.
- One is necessarily zero.
- They are proportional to the intercepts.

## Explanation

Parallel lines have the same slope. Acceleration is that slope, while the intercept gives the velocity at time zero. Any interval of equal duration gives the same velocity increment in both motions. Their velocities can still differ because the intercepts are distinct.
