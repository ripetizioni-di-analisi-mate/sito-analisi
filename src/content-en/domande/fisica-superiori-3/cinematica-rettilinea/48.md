A position equation has the form $x=x_0+v_0t+kt^2$. How does coefficient $k$ give acceleration?

- $a=k$.
- $a=\dfrac k2$.
- $a=k^2$.
- $a=2k$.

## Explanation

For constant acceleration, the coefficient of $t^2$ is half of acceleration. Multiply the coefficient by two to recover $a$. For example, $k=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ means velocity increases by $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. The coefficient $v_0$ is the initial slope of $x(t)$.
