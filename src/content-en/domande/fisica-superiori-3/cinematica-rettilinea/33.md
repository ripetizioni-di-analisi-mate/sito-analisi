Two technicians use the same axis, but one starts their clock two seconds later. They compute mean velocity between the same events. How do their results compare?

- They coincide because the time difference is unchanged.
- They differ by two metres per second.
- They coincide only if the body is at rest.
- They have opposite signs.

## Explanation

Shifting the time origin changes both event labels by the same amount. Elapsed time and displacement stay the same.
