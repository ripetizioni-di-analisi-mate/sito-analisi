A velocity–time graph has a horizontal line above the time axis. What follows about acceleration?

- It is a positive constant.
- It is zero.
- It increases linearly.
- It depends on the area of the rectangle.

## Explanation

Acceleration is the slope of the velocity–time graph. A horizontal line has zero slope and constant velocity.
