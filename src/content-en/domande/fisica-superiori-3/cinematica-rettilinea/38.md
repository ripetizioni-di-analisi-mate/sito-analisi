A velocity–time graph has a horizontal line above the time axis. What follows about acceleration?

- It is a positive constant.
- It is zero.
- It increases linearly.
- It depends on the area of the rectangle.

## Explanation

Acceleration is the slope of the velocity–time graph. A horizontal line has zero slope and constant velocity. The graph being above the axis means velocity is positive. Its vertical coordinate gives velocity itself. For acceleration, read the change in that coordinate, which is zero here.
