A trolley has constant acceleration. Its displacement is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ in the first second and $9\,\mathrm{m}$ in the second. The axis points in the direction of those displacements. What was its initial velocity?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The interval averages are $5$ and $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At constant acceleration they equal the velocities at the two midpoints. These are one second apart, so $a=4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The first midpoint is half a second after the start. Subtracting $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from the first average gives $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
