Two trolleys on parallel tracks have equations $x_A=4t$ and $x_B=12-2t$ in SI units for $t\geq0$. At which times is their separation along the $x$ axis $6\,\mathrm{m}$?

- At $1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- Only at $1\,\mathrm{s}$.
- Only at $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- At $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The separation is $|12-6t|$. Before the encounter, the difference $x_B-x_A$ is positive and equals six at $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$. After the encounter it is negative and reaches minus six at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$. The times are symmetric about $2\,\mathrm{s}$, when the coordinates coincide.
