Two position–time graphs intersect. Which facts can be read with certainty from the intersection?

- A common velocity.
- A common acceleration.
- The time and common coordinate.
- The full distances travelled by both points.

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate is time and the vertical one is position. Two curves through the same point share position and time but may have different slopes. A rising line and a falling line can intersect. At the crossing they have the same position and velocities of opposite signs.
