Two position–time graphs cross. What can be read from the intersection?

- A common velocity.
- A common acceleration.
- The time and common coordinate.
- The full distances travelled by both points.

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate is time and the vertical one is position. Two curves through the same point share position and time but may have different slopes.
