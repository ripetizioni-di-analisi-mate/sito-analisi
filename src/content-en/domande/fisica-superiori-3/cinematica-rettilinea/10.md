An object moves from $x_i=6\,\mathrm m$ to $x_f=22\,\mathrm m$ in $\Delta t=6\,\mathrm s$. Find its average velocity.

- $1.335\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v_m=2.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5.34\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4.005\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Average velocity uses signed displacement. Subtract the initial position from the final one and divide by the elapsed time.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_m&=\frac{x_f-x_i}{\Delta t}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 2.67\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
