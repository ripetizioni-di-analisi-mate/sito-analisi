The slope of a $v(t)$ graph is positive, while the curve lies below $v=0$. What happens to speed near that point?

- It increases.
- It stays constant.
- It instantly becomes zero.
- It decreases.

## Explanation

A positive slope increases the signed value of a still-negative velocity. Its magnitude approaches zero.
