From rest at constant acceleration, how does distance change if time triples?

- It is multiplied by three.
- It is multiplied by six.
- It is multiplied by nine.
- It is unchanged.

## Explanation

The from-rest equation $s=\dfrac12at^2$ contains the square of elapsed time. Tripling time multiplies that square by nine. Starting from rest, tripling the duration triples the final velocity as well. The triangular area grows because both its dimensions change, each by a factor of three.
