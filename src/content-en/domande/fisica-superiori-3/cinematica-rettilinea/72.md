From a height of $30\,\mathrm{m}$, a sphere is given an initial velocity of $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ downwards. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does it take to hit the ground?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $-3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

Taking downwards as positive, the initial term $5t$ adds to the acceleration term. The equation factors as $(t-2)(t+3)=0$. The physical time is two seconds, which gives ten metres from the initial term and twenty from the acceleration term. The negative root lies on the extension of the parabola before launch. The relevant contact occurs after the ball receives its initial velocity.

$$
\begin{aligned}
30&=5t+5t^2\\[4pt] t&=\frac{-5+\sqrt{25+600}}{10}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
