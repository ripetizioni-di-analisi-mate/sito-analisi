An object starts from rest and accelerates at the constant rate $a=0.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $t=7\,\mathrm s$. Find the distance travelled.

- $s=22.1\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $11.05\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $44.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $33.15\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The velocity increases linearly from zero to $at$. The average is therefore half its final value, and distance is average speed times elapsed time.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\frac12at^2\\[4pt]
&\simeq 22.1\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
