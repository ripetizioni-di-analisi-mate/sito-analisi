A ball in vertical flight without air resistance passes a sensor at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ and again at $t=8\,\mathrm{s}$. At what time does it reach its highest point, using the same clock?

- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Height follows a downward-opening parabola because vertical acceleration is constant. Two passes through the same height are symmetric about the time at which vertical velocity is zero. The peak is therefore reached at the midpoint of the two times, $\dfrac{2+8}{2}\,\mathrm{s}=5\,\mathrm{s}$.
