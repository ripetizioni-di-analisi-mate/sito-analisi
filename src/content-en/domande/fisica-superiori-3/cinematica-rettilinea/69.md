Two cars start at the same speed. The first has twice the second's deceleration magnitude. How do their braking times and distances compare?

- Both are halved for the first car.
- Only time is halved for the first car.
- Only distance is halved for the first car.
- Distance is quartered and time doubled for the first car.

## Explanation

At fixed initial speed, both $v_0/d$ and $v_0^2/(2d)$ are inversely proportional to the magnitude of deceleration.
