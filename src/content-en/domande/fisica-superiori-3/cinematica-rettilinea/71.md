In two ideal experiments with no air resistance, an object falls from rest from the same height. The fall takes $2\,\mathrm{s}$ at A and $3\,\mathrm{s}$ at B. Gravity is uniform within each experiment. What is the ratio $g_A/g_B$?

- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac94$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

For each release, $g=\dfrac{2h}{t_f^2}$. The common height cancels in the ratio, leaving $\dfrac{3^2}{2^2}=\dfrac94$. The fall takes less time at A because the acceleration magnitude is larger. Using the time ratio alone would miss the squared dependence.
