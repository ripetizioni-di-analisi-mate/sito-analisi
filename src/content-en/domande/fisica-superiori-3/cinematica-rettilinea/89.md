Solving two piecewise equations gives an intersection at $t=8\,\mathrm{s}$. The branch used for one object applies only from ten seconds on. What follows?

- Discard the candidate and check the branch valid at eight seconds.
- They meet at eight seconds.
- They must meet at exactly ten seconds.
- Discard every future intersection.

## Explanation

The solved equation represents the object only while that branch applies. A root outside its domain is an algebraic intersection without meaning during the given interval. At eight seconds use the branch active at that instant. A complete check solves for equality on the applicable branches and tests each result against its own interval.
