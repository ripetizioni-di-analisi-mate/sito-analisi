A car remains at rest after it stops. Why must its linear braking velocity equation stop at the first zero?

- Extending it would assign the car a velocity in the opposite direction.
- Extending it would keep the car at rest.
- Extending it would make position discontinuous at the stop.
- Extending it would reverse the acceleration sign.

## Explanation

The decreasing line crosses the time axis and then takes negative values. After stopping, the equation for the stationary vehicle is $v=0$.
