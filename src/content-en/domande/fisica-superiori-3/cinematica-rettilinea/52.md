Two trolleys start from rest. A accelerates uniformly to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $6\,\mathrm{s}$. B reaches the same $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at constant acceleration in $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and holds that speed for another $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the ratio of B's distance to A's?

- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

A's velocity–time graph forms a triangle of area $36\,\mathrm{m}$. B has a triangle of area $18\,\mathrm{m}$ followed by a rectangle of area $36\,\mathrm{m}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{54}{36}=\dfrac32$. B covers more ground because it stays at its higher speed for part of the interval.
