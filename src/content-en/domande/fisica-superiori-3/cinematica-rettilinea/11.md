A velocity increases from $v_i=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $v_f=22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $\Delta t=7\,\mathrm s$. Calculate the average acceleration.

- $1.145\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4.58\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $a_m=2.29\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $3.435\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The change in velocity has the sign of final minus initial velocity. Division by the positive elapsed time preserves that sign.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_m&=\frac{v_f-v_i}{\Delta t}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 2.29\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
