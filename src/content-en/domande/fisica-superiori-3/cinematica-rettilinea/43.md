An object has $v_0=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its velocity after $2\,\mathrm s$ if the same acceleration persists beyond the reversal?

::grafico{f="6-4*x" punti="1.5,0,pieno;2,-2,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=2.4 ymin=-4 ymax=7 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity crosses zero before 2 s"}

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The change in velocity is $-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its magnitude exceeds the initial speed, so the body passes through $v=0$ at $1.5\,\mathrm s$ and moves in the negative direction. The requested quantity is signed velocity and retains that sign.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=v_0+at=6-4\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= -2\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
