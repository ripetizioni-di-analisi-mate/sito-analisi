A trolley follows $x(t)=2+3t+t^2$ in SI units for $t\geq0$. Within this motion, at what time $t$ does a one-second interval start during which it travels $10\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Velocity is positive throughout, so distance is $x(t+1)-x(t)$. Expanding the square gives $4+2t$ in metres. Equating it to ten gives $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$. Indeed, position changes from $20\,\mathrm{m}$ to $30\,\mathrm{m}$ between three and four seconds.
