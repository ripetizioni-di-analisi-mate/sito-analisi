In a position–time graph a line slopes downwards. What does its slope represent?

- A constant negative acceleration.
- A negative position at all times.
- A constant negative velocity.
- A velocity whose magnitude decreases with time.

## Explanation

The slope measures displacement per unit time. The coordinate here falls at a steady rate and acceleration is zero. For example, a slope of $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ means the coordinate drops by two metres every second. The negative slope gives the direction, while acceleration would require the slope itself to change.
