In a position–time graph a line slopes downwards. What does its slope represent?

- A constant negative acceleration.
- A negative position at all times.
- A constant negative velocity.
- A velocity whose magnitude decreases with time.

## Explanation

The slope measures displacement per unit time. The coordinate here falls at a steady rate and acceleration is zero.
