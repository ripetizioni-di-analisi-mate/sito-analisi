An object starts with $v_0=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and maintains acceleration $a=1.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate its final velocity.

- $4.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With constant acceleration, each second adds the same change in velocity. Add the full change $at$ to the initial velocity. The product $(1.1\cdot4)\,\mathrm{m/s}=4.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the change. The object already had $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the start, so it ends at $9.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The sum is positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=v_0+at\\[4pt]
&= 9.4\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
