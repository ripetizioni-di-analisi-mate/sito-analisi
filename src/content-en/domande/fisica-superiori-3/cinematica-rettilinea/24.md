A particle goes from $x=8\,\mathrm m$ to $x=2\,\mathrm m$. Reverse the axis while keeping its origin. What displacement results?

- $-6\,\mathrm m$.
- $-10\,\mathrm m$.
- $0\,\mathrm m$.
- $+6\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

The coordinates become $-8\,\mathrm m$ and $-2\,\mathrm m$. Final minus initial is $+6\,\mathrm m$.
