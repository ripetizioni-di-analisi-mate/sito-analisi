A particle goes from $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Reverse the axis while keeping its origin. What displacement results?

- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-10\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $+6\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The coordinates become $-8\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{m}$. Final minus initial is $+6\,\mathrm{m}$. In the old axis the result is $2-8=-6\,\mathrm{m}$. Reversing the orientation changes the sign of the difference. The magnitude stays six metres because the endpoints are the same.
