Two points move away from each other along a line. What is the sign of the change in their separation as they move apart?

- Negative.
- Always zero.
- It reverses when one passes the origin even if the separation keeps increasing.
- Positive.

## Explanation

Separation is the magnitude of the difference in coordinates. If they move apart, that magnitude increases regardless of where the origin lies. The change is positive because the smaller initial distance is subtracted from the greater final one. Reversing the axis changes the signed coordinate difference while leaving its magnitude unchanged.
