Two particles move away from each other on their respective sides. What is the sign of the change in their separation?

- Negative.
- Always zero.
- It reverses when one passes the origin even if the separation keeps increasing.
- Positive.

## Explanation

Separation is the magnitude of the difference in coordinates. If they move apart, that magnitude increases regardless of where the origin lies.
