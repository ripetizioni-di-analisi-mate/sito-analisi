A person travels $40\,\mathrm{m}$ at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, stops and returns along the same path at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Their average speed, including the stop, is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long is the stop?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The total distance is $80\,\mathrm{m}$, so average speed fixes the duration at $20\,\mathrm{s}$. The outward leg takes $8\,\mathrm{s}$ and the return $10\,\mathrm{s}$. This leaves $2\,\mathrm{s}$ for the stop. Returning to the start gives zero displacement but a positive distance travelled.
