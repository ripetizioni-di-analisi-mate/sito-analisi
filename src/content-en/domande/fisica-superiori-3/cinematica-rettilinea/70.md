Two balls in a vacuum are released from the same height $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$ apart. At $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$ after the first release, both are still in flight. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how far apart vertically are they then?

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The first ball has been falling for $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$ and the second for $1\,\mathrm{s}$. They start at the same height, so separation is the difference between the drops. This gives $\bigl(5\cdot1.5^2-5\cdot1^2\bigr)\,\mathrm{m}=6.25\,\mathrm{m}$. Substituting the release delay directly into the fall equation would ignore the speed already gained by the first ball.
