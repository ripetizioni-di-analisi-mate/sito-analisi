A car has $36\,\mathrm{m}$ available and can brake at magnitude $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. With no delay, what is the greatest initial speed compatible with stopping?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The limit uses all thirty-six metres to reach $v=0$. The time-free relation gives $v_0^2=2ds$, so take the positive square root.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{\max}&=\sqrt{2\cdot2\cdot36}\\[4pt]
&= 12\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
