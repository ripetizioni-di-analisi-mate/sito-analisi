At the highest point of a vertical throw without drag, what are velocity and acceleration with the axis pointing upwards?

- $v=g$ and $a=0$.
- $v=0$ and $a=0$.
- $v=0$ and $a=-g$.
- $v=0$ and $a=g$.

## Explanation

Velocity is zero at the instant of reversal. The uniform gravity model still gives the same downward acceleration throughout flight. With the chosen axis the values are $v=0$ and $a=-g$. In the position–time graph the apex has a horizontal tangent and the curve still opens downwards.
