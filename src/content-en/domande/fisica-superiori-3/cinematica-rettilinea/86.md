Two cyclists at speed $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ start at opposite ends of a $24\,\mathrm m$ section and ride towards each other. One stops after $1\,\mathrm s$. At what time after their common start do they meet?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

They close eight metres in the first second. After one stops, the other covers the remaining sixteen metres alone in four seconds. Total elapsed time includes the first second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=1+\frac{24-(4+4)\cdot1}{4}\\[4pt]
&= 5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
