An object has initial position $x_0=5\,\mathrm m$ and constant velocity $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its position after $t=4\,\mathrm s$.

- $x=29\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $14.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $58\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $43.5\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Constant velocity gives the displacement $vt$. To find the position, add the initial coordinate.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=x_0+vt\\[4pt]
&= 29\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
