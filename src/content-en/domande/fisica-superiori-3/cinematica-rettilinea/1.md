An object has initial position $x_0=5\,\mathrm{m}$ and constant velocity $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its position after $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$.

- $29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $35\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Constant velocity gives the displacement $vt$. To find the position, add the initial coordinate. In four seconds the object moves $6\cdot4=24\,\mathrm{m}$. The axis origin is five metres behind the starting point, so the final reading is $5+24=29\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=x_0+vt\\[4pt]
&= 29\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
