In a velocity–time plot, two segments with successive slopes $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ meet at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$. Velocity is continuous. What is the model's instantaneous acceleration at that junction?

- $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- There is no unique value.
- $+0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Instantaneous acceleration is the tangent slope of the velocity graph. At the junction the slope from the left is positive and from the right negative, so the model gives two different limiting slopes. Continuity of velocity ensures the segments meet. Averaging their slopes still leaves a corner.
