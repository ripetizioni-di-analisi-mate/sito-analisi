At a transition, the mean velocities immediately before and after are $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The position graph is straight on each side. What is the slope after minus the slope before?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The slopes give the two signed velocities. The later one is negative and the earlier one positive, so their difference is more negative than either one alone. The corner marks the transition between the two behaviours and gives no unique instantaneous velocity at the change.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta v&=(-2)-3\\[4pt]
&= -5\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
