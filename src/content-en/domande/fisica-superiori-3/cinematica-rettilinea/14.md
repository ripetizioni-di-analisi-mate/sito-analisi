A vehicle has initial speed $v_0=14\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with constant deceleration magnitude $d=2.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate its stopping distance, excluding reaction time.

- $17.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $s=35\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $70\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $52.5\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Take the initial direction as positive, so acceleration is $-d$. Set $v=0$ in $v^2=v_0^2-2ds$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\frac{v_0^2}{2d}\\[4pt]
&= 35\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
