Two exact trolley readings give $x=16\,\mathrm{m}$ at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$ at $t=5\,\mathrm{s}$. A uniform-motion model is proposed. A new reading at $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$ has a maximum error of $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$. Which reading is inconsistent with the model?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-0.12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The coordinate drops by $12\,\mathrm{m}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$, so the model has $v=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. One second after the last reading it predicts $x=0$. The discrepancy of $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ exceeds the $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ limit. All the other discrepancies have magnitudes within the allowed range.
