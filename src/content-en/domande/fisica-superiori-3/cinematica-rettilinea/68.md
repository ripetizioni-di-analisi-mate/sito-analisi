A vehicle is travelling at $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and stops in $6\,\mathrm{s}$ at constant deceleration. What distance does it cover only during the last second?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Speed falls by four metres per second each second. At five seconds it is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The final triangular area is two metres. The calculation concerns only the interval from five to six seconds. The average of its endpoint values is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which gives the distance over one second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s_6&=\frac{4+0}{2}\cdot1\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
