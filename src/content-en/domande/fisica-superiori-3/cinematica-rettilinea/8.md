Two cyclists initially separated by $d=90\,\mathrm m$ ride towards each other at constant speeds $v_1=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2=2.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate when they meet.

- $7.65\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $30.6\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $22.95\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $t=15.3\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The cyclists close the gap from both ends. In one second their separation decreases by $v_1+v_2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{d}{v_1+v_2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 15.3\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
