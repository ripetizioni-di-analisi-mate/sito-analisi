Two cyclists initially separated by $d=90\,\mathrm{m}$ ride towards each other at constant speeds $v_1=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2=2.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which value is closest to the time at which they meet?

- $25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $69.23\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.63\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15.3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The cyclists close the gap from both ends. In one second their separation decreases by $v_1+v_2$. The closing speed is $(3.6+2.3)\,\mathrm{m/s}=5.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The separation reaches zero after $\dfrac{90}{5.9}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq15.3\,\mathrm{s}$. Over that time each rider covers part of the initial gap.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{d}{v_1+v_2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 15.3\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
