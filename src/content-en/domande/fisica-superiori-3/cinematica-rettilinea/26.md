A sensor reads $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $10\,\mathrm{m}$ at times $4\,\mathrm{s}$ apart. Each position may have an error of at most $0.1\,\mathrm{m}$ in magnitude. Any pair of errors within those bounds is possible, and the time interval is exact. What is the largest error in the calculated average velocity?

- $0.025\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The two uncertainties can add when the positions are subtracted, for example if one reading is too high and the other too low. The calculated displacement can be off by at most $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$. Dividing by $4\,\mathrm{s}$ gives a maximum error of $0.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in average velocity.
