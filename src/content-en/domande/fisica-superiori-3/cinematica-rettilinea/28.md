Two motions have the same mean velocity over an interval. What is guaranteed if they last the same amount of time?

- Their paths have equal length.
- Their instantaneous velocities always match.
- Their starting positions match.
- Their signed displacements are equal.

## Explanation

Multiplying mean velocity by time gives displacement. It does not determine how motion proceeds between the two positions.
