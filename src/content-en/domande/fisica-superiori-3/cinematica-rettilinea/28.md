Two motions have the same mean velocity over an interval. What is guaranteed if they last the same amount of time?

- Their paths have equal length.
- Their instantaneous velocities always match.
- Their starting positions match.
- Their signed displacements are equal.

## Explanation

Multiplying mean velocity by time gives displacement. It does not determine how motion proceeds between the two positions. With the same duration and average velocity, both must have the same change between initial and final position. They may start at different positions and undergo different kinds of motion in between.
