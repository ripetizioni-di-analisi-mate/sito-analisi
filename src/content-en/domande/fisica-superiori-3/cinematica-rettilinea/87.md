For two uniform motions the coordinate difference is $x_B-x_A=12+2t$. For $t\geq0$, what happens?

- They meet at six seconds.
- The separation stays at twelve metres.
- Their separation grows and there is no meeting.
- They approach but do not meet.

## Explanation

The difference starts positive and increases. A meeting would require it to become zero, which cannot occur at the times under consideration.
