Two spheres of different masses are released from rest at the same height in vacuum. When do they hit the ground?

- At the same instant.
- The heavier one first.
- The lighter one first.
- It cannot be found without their masses.

## Explanation

In the model both start from rest and have acceleration $g$. The height equation contains the same $h$ and $g$ for both and does not depend on mass. From $h=\dfrac12gt^2$, both balls have the same positive flight time. The vacuum removes any contribution from air resistance.
