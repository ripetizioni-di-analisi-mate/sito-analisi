In a from-rest model, acceleration is halved and duration doubled. By what factor does total distance change?

- A factor of one.
- A factor of four.
- A factor of one half.
- A factor of two.

## Explanation

The new product $at^2$ is $\frac12\cdot4=2$ times its previous value. Duration enters quadratically and acceleration linearly.
