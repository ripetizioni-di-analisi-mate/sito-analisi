An object starts from rest and accelerates at a constant rate. In a second experiment it starts from rest at half the acceleration for twice the time. By what factor does the total distance change?

- A factor of one.
- A factor of four.
- A factor of one half.
- A factor of two.

## Explanation

The new product $at^2$ is $\dfrac12\cdot4=2$ times its previous value. Duration enters quadratically and acceleration linearly. The velocity–time graph gives a check. The product of acceleration and time leaves final speed unchanged, while the base of the triangle doubles. The area doubles as well.
