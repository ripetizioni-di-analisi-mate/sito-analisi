At the start of a test, a trolley's speed sensor reads $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. After $6\,\mathrm{s}$ it reads $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The trolley moves in the positive direction throughout. What is its average acceleration over that interval?

- $3.33\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The change in velocity has the sign of final minus initial velocity. Division by the positive elapsed time preserves that sign. Velocity increases by $20-5=15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in six seconds, giving a mean of $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The endpoint readings determine this mean while allowing different behaviour inside the interval.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_m&=\frac{v_f-v_i}{\Delta t}\\[4pt]
&= 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
