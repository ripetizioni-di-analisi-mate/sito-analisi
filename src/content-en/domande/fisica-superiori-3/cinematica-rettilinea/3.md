A velocity increases from $v_i=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $v_f=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $\Delta t=6\,\mathrm s$. Calculate the average acceleration.

- $1.25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $a_m=2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $3.75\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The change in velocity has the sign of final minus initial velocity. Division by the positive elapsed time preserves that sign.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_m&=\frac{v_f-v_i}{\Delta t}\\[4pt]
&= 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
