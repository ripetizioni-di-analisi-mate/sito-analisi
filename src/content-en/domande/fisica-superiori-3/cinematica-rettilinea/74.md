A sphere is thrown vertically upwards at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What maximum height above launch does it reach at $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $22.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $11.25\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7.5\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

At maximum height the vertical velocity passes through zero at one instant. Use the time-free equation with acceleration $-g$, giving $0=v_0^2-2gH$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
H&=\frac{v_0^2}{2g}=\frac{15^2}{2\cdot10}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 11.25\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
