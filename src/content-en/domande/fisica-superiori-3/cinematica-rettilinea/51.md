With nonzero constant acceleration, what additional information selects the sign of velocity when the time-free equation gives $v^2$?

- Knowing the unit of mass.
- Knowing the spatial origin alone.
- Knowing whether the particle moves in the positive or negative direction.
- Knowing whether elapsed time is in seconds.

## Explanation

The square root of a square gives a magnitude. Direction or the time history of motion determines which sign describes it.
