The position–time graph of an object starting from rest under positive acceleration curves upwards. What does its increasing slope indicate?

- Velocity is constant.
- Displacement becomes negative.
- Velocity increases.
- Acceleration necessarily increases.

## Explanation

Slope is velocity. It increases even if acceleration is constant because velocity then grows linearly with time. In a position–time graph the vertical coordinate gives position and the slope gives speed when the motion is positive. This parabola has increasing slopes.
