A point at $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$ moves at $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ towards another at $x=-4\,\mathrm{m}$ moving at $+2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the first particle's displacement before they meet?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-8\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The initial gap is twelve metres and closes by six metres per second. After two seconds the first particle reaches coordinate zero. Its requested displacement is $0-8=-8\,\mathrm{m}$. The second reaches zero starting from four metres to the left. The negative sign shows that the first point started to the right of the meeting point. Its distance travelled is the magnitude of that displacement.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x_1&=-4\cdot\frac{8-(-4)}{4+2}\\[4pt]
&= -8\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
