A cart moves for $4\,\mathrm{s}$ at $+3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, then for $5\,\mathrm{s}$ at $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It starts at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its final coordinate?

::grafico{curve="t,6+3*t,0,4;t,26-2*t,4,9" xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=0 ymax=21 nomi="t (s),x (m)" punti="0,6,pieno;4,18,pieno;9,8,pieno" titolo="The second stage starts from the coordinate reached in the first"}

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $28\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The cart first advances $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and reaches $18\,\mathrm{m}$. The second stage starts there and subtracts $10\,\mathrm{m}$. The overall change is $+2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the start, while the requested coordinate also includes the initial $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Use the signs of both velocities. Adding the magnitudes of their contributions would give the distance travelled, $22\,\mathrm{m}$, which is a different quantity.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_f&=6+3\cdot4+(-2)\cdot5\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
