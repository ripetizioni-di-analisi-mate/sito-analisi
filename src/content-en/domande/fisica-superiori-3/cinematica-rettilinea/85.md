Two particles follow $x_A=6t$ and $x_B=24-2t$. How much time passes from their meeting until their separation becomes $16\,\mathrm m$ again?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

After crossing they continue in opposite directions, so separation grows by eight metres per second. Divide the requested separation by this relative speed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta t&=\frac{16}{6+2}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
