Two lines on a position–time graph have the same slope and distinct intercepts. What follows?

- The particles meet when they pass the origin.
- The particles keep getting further apart.
- The particles have opposite velocities.
- The particles maintain a constant separation.

## Explanation

A common slope means equal velocities. Subtracting the equations cancels the time term and leaves the difference in intercepts. The difference is constant and nonzero, so the lines do not intersect. The point ahead stays ahead and neither gains on the other, even while both are moving.
