A trolley starts from $x=0$ and travels at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$. It then stops for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and restarts in the same direction at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At what time, measured from the start, does it pass $x=20\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The first segment ends at $4\cdot3=12\,\mathrm{m}$, and the position stays fixed during the stop. The remaining $20-12=8\,\mathrm{m}$ take $\dfrac{8}{2}\,\mathrm{s}=4\,\mathrm{s}$ after the trolley restarts. The requested time also includes the first segment and the stop, giving $(3+2+4)\,\mathrm{s}=9\,\mathrm{s}$.
