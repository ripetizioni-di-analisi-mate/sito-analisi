An object has initial position $x_0=6\,\mathrm m$ and constant velocity $v=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its position after $t=5\,\mathrm s$.

- $x=41\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $20.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $82\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $61.5\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Constant velocity gives the displacement $vt$. To find the position, add the initial coordinate.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=x_0+vt\\[4pt]
&= 41\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
