An object's velocity over a stage is $v=5-2t$, with $t$ in seconds. What is the displacement equation from time zero?

- $\Delta x=5t-2t^2$.
- $\Delta x=5t-t^2$.
- $\Delta x=5-2t^2$.
- $\Delta x=5t-t$.

## Explanation

The constant velocity term contributes $5t$. Acceleration is $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, so the coefficient of $t^2$ is $-1$. Thus $\Delta x=5t-t^2$ in SI units. At $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ the displacement is $4\,\mathrm{m}$, the duration times the mean of $5$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
