Average acceleration is $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ over $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Final velocity is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What was the initial velocity?

- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-11\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Mean acceleration gives a change of $-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Before losing that amount, the initial velocity was eight metres per second larger than the final one. This gives $v_i=3-(-8)=11\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The result uses the total change in velocity even if acceleration varied over the four seconds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_i&=v_f-a_m\Delta t=3-(-2)\cdot4\\[4pt]
&= 11\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
