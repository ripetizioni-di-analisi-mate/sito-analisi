Two points on a graph of $x(t)$ have the same coordinate at successive times. What follows without further assumptions?

- The object is at rest throughout the interval.
- Distance travelled is zero.
- Displacement between the instants is zero.
- Velocity is zero throughout.

## Explanation

The initial and final coordinates determine displacement. The path between them can go out and back, changing distance without changing the endpoints. What is guaranteed is $\Delta x=0$ and hence zero average velocity between the readings. A sensor that samples only the endpoints can miss a change of direction.
