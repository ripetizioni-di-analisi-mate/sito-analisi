On the velocity–time graph for vertical free fall under uniform gravity the slope is negative. Which way does the positive spatial axis point?

- Downwards.
- Horizontally.
- Upwards.
- Orientation does not affect the sign of the slope.

## Explanation

The slope is acceleration. Gravity points down, so a negative component means the positive axis points the other way. The axis therefore points upwards. During a fall in this coordinate system, velocity can be negative and decrease further while its magnitude grows.
