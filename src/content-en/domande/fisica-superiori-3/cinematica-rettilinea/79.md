A velocity–time graph for free fall has a constant negative slope. Which way does the spatial axis point?

- Downwards.
- Horizontally.
- Upwards.
- Orientation does not affect the sign of the slope.

## Explanation

The slope is acceleration. Gravity points down, so a negative component means the positive axis points the other way.
