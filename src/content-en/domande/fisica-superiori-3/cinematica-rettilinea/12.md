An object starts with $v_0=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and maintains acceleration $a=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $t=5\,\mathrm s$. Calculate its final velocity.

- $6.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $18.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v=12.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

With constant acceleration, each second adds the same change in velocity. Add the full change $at$ to the initial velocity.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=v_0+at\\[4pt]
&= 12.5\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
