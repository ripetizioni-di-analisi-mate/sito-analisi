Two objects are in free flight together, one just released and the other already falling. How does their velocity difference change while both remain in flight?

- It instantly becomes zero.
- It grows by $g$ every second.
- It always falls to zero.
- It remains constant.

## Explanation

Over the same interval both gain the same signed change in velocity. Subtracting the velocities cancels that change.
