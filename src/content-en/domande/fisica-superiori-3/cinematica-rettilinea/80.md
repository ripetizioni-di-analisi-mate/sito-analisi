A person starts at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ at $t=0$ and moves at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A second person starts at $x=36\,\mathrm{m}$ when $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$. What constant signed velocity must the second person have to meet the first at $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $+3.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the meeting time the first person is at $5+3\cdot6=23\,\mathrm{m}$. The second therefore needs a displacement of $-13\,\mathrm{m}$ over the $4\,\mathrm{s}$ since their own start. The quotient is $-3.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The sign agrees with the need to move towards smaller coordinates.
