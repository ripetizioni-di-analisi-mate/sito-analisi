An object starts from rest at a positive constant acceleration. How do its distances over three successive one-second intervals compare?

- They are in the ratio $1:2:3$.
- They are in the ratio $1:3:5$.
- They are in the ratio $1:4:9$.
- They are equal.

## Explanation

Cumulative distances are proportional to $1^2$, $2^2$ and $3^2$. Distances within each second are the successive differences, $1$, $3$ and $5$.
