A cart covers two equal $120\,\mathrm m$ stages at steady velocities of $4$ and $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. What is its average velocity?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

For equal distances, the slower segment takes longer. Add the thirty and twenty seconds and divide total displacement by fifty seconds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_m&=\frac{2\cdot120}{120/4+120/6}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 4.8\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
