In frame A the equation is $x_A(t)=2+3t$ in SI units. The origin of frame B is at the position that A calls $20\,\mathrm{m}$, and their positive axes point in opposite directions. Both use the same clock. What is the equation in frame B?

- $x_B(t)=-18+3t$
- $x_B(t)=22+3t$
- $x_B(t)=22-3t$
- $x_B(t)=18-3t$

## Explanation

Subtract $20$ from A's coordinate to measure from B, then reverse the sign because the axis points the other way. This gives $x_B=-(2+3t-20)=18-3t$. At one second A reads $5\,\mathrm{m}$, so B reads $15\,\mathrm{m}$ on its positive side.
