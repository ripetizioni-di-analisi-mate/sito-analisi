An object moves from $x_i=5\,\mathrm m$ to $x_f=20\,\mathrm m$ in $\Delta t=5\,\mathrm s$. Find its average velocity.

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v_m=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Average velocity uses signed displacement. Subtract the initial position from the final one and divide by the elapsed time.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_m&=\frac{x_f-x_i}{\Delta t}\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
