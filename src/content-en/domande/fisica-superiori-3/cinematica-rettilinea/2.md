An object moves from $x_i=5\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x_f=20\,\mathrm{m}$ in $\Delta t=5\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average velocity?

- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity uses signed displacement. Subtract the initial position from the final one and divide by the elapsed time. The difference is $20-5=15\,\mathrm{m}$. Divide by five seconds to get $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The positive sign means the final position lies in the direction of increasing coordinates from the initial one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_m&=\frac{x_f-x_i}{\Delta t}\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
