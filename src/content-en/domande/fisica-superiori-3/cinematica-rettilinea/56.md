Two objects start from rest together with constant accelerations $a$ and $4a$ in the same direction, with $a>0$. How long does the second take to travel the distance the first covers in $6\,\mathrm{s}$?

- After $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- After $6\,\mathrm{s}$.
- After $12\,\mathrm{s}$.
- After $3\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

At fixed distance, $at^2$ is constant. Quadrupling acceleration requires halving the time. For the second object, set $4a t^2=a\cdot6^2$. This gives $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$. The positive root represents elapsed time from the start.
