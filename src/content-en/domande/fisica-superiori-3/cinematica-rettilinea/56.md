Two objects start from rest together with constant accelerations $a$ and $4a$ in the same direction. When does the second reach the distance the first covers in $6\,\mathrm s$?

- After $1.5\,\mathrm s$.
- After $6\,\mathrm s$.
- After $12\,\mathrm s$.
- After $3\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

At fixed distance, $at^2$ is constant. Quadrupling acceleration requires halving the time.
