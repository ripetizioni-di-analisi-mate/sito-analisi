An object starts from rest and accelerates at the constant rate $a=0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$. What distance does it travel?

- $14.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $28.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity increases linearly from zero to $at$. The average is therefore half its final value, and distance is average speed times elapsed time. The final velocity is $4.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its average is $2.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which gives $14.4\,\mathrm{m}$ over six seconds. In the velocity graph this is a triangle's area with base equal to the duration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\dfrac12at^2\\[4pt]
&= 14.4\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
