An object starts from rest and accelerates at the constant rate $a=0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $t=6\,\mathrm s$. Find the distance travelled.

- $s=14.4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $28.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $21.6\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The velocity increases linearly from zero to $at$. The average is therefore half its final value, and distance is average speed times elapsed time.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\frac12at^2\\[4pt]
&= 14.4\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
