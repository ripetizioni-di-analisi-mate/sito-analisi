A table of positions gives mean velocity $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in two consecutive intervals of equal duration. Which model is consistent with this information?

- Only uniform motion.
- Only motion accelerating continuously in one direction.
- Both uniform motion and some motion with internal variations.
- Only rest between observations.

## Explanation

The secant slopes match. The measurements fix the positions at sampled times, and different time paths may pass through those points.
