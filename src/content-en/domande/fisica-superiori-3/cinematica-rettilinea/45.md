A cart starts at $v_0=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and accelerates at $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its displacement?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Velocity vanishes at one second, after a displacement of $-1\,\mathrm{m}$. During the next two seconds the object accelerates in the positive direction and covers $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Their signed sum is $3\,\mathrm{m}$, matching the position equation. The distance travelled is $1+4=5\,\mathrm{m}$. The first leg adds to the distance while reducing the signed displacement.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x&=v_0t+\tfrac12at^2=-2\cdot3+\tfrac12\cdot2\cdot3^2\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
