A trolley starts at velocity $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. For $3\,\mathrm{s}$ its acceleration is $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, then for another $2\,\mathrm{s}$ it is $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its signed final velocity?

- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

During the first leg velocity increases by $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the trolley goes from $-3$ to $+3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. During the second leg the change is $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its final velocity is $+1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. Add the total change of $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the initial value.
