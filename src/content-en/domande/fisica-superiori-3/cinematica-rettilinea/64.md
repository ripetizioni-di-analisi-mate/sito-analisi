If initial speed doubles at constant deceleration magnitude, how does braking distance change, excluding reaction?

- It doubles.
- It is unchanged.
- It halves.
- It quadruples.

## Explanation

When stopping, $s=v_0^2/(2d)$. At fixed $d$, distance depends on the square of initial speed, so the factor is four.
