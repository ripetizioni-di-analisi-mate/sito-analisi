A velocity returns to its initial value after oscillating. What follows about mean acceleration over the full interval?

- It is always positive.
- It equals the maximum acceleration.
- It is zero.
- It is distance travelled divided by time.

## Explanation

The net change of velocity is zero, so dividing it by the duration gives zero. Velocity may still change during parts of the interval. The positive and negative changes cancel in total, so velocity returns to the initial value. Instantaneous acceleration may be positive or negative during the oscillations.
