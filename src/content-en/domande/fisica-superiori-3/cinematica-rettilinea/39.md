A velocity returns to its initial value after oscillating. What follows about mean acceleration over the full interval?

- It is always positive.
- It equals the maximum acceleration.
- It is zero.
- It is distance travelled divided by time.

## Explanation

The net change of velocity is zero, so dividing it by the duration gives zero. Velocity may still change during parts of the interval.
