An object is released from rest at height $h=11\,\mathrm m$. Ignore drag and calculate its fall time. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $0.75\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $t=1.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $2.25\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

For a fall from rest, $h=\frac12gt^2$. Choose the positive square root because elapsed time is positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 1.5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
