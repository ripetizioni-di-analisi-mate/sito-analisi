An object is released from rest at height $h=11\,\mathrm{m}$. Ignore drag and calculate its fall time. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Choose the closest value.

- $1.06\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.24\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

For a fall from rest, $h=\dfrac12gt^2$. Choose the positive square root because elapsed time is positive. The square of the time is $\dfrac{22}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s^2}$, close to $2.24\,\mathrm{s^2}$. The square root is about $1.50\,\mathrm{s}$. Treating the height as a duration would mix quantities with different units.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 1.5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
