A particle starts at $-18\,\mathrm m$ with constant velocity $+3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. When does it pass the origin?

- $18\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The origin has coordinate zero. The particle must advance $18\,\mathrm m$ and covers three each second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{0-(-18)}3\\[4pt]
&= 6\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
