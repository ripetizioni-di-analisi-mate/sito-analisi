On a smooth curved portion of a position–time graph, what is read at an instant to find velocity?

- The slope of its tangent.
- The vertical coordinate of the point.
- The area between the curve and the time axis.
- The graph's intercept.

## Explanation

As the time interval shrinks, the secant slope tends to the tangent slope. That limiting ratio of position change to time is instantaneous velocity.
