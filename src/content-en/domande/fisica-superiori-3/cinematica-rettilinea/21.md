An object moves uniformly. It is at $x=5\,\mathrm m$ at $t=1\,\mathrm s$ and at $x=11\,\mathrm m$ at $t=4\,\mathrm s$. What is its coordinate at $t=0$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The slope is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At time zero the first observation is one second away, so subtract two metres from its coordinate.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x(0)&=5-\frac{11-5}{4-1}\cdot1\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
