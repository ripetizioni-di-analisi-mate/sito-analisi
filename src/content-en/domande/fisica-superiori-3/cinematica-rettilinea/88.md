A car passes a marker at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Two seconds later another leaves that marker at $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. How long does the second take to catch the first?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

When the second starts, the first has a ten-metre lead. Because the velocities have the same direction, their difference closes the lead by four metres per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{5\cdot2}{9-5}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 2.5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
