A vehicle moving in the negative direction slows down along a line. What are the signs of instantaneous velocity and acceleration?

- $v<0$ and $a>0$.
- $v<0$ and $a<0$.
- $v>0$ and $a>0$.
- $v>0$ and $a<0$.

## Explanation

Velocity remains negative but approaches zero. It rises as a signed number, so its change per unit time is positive. For example, if velocity changes from $-6$ to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, its magnitude decreases by two while signed velocity increases by two.
