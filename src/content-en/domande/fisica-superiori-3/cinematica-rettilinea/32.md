A rest stop is added to a trip. Motion then continues in the same direction and total displacement is unchanged. What happens to average speed?

- It increases because velocity is zero during the stop.
- It stays the same because displacement is the same.
- It necessarily becomes zero.
- It decreases because total duration increases.

## Explanation

For a fixed path, stopping adds elapsed time to the denominator without adding distance to the numerator. Average speed decreases.
