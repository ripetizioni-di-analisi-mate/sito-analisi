Read the velocity–time graph. What is the displacement between $0$ and $6\,\mathrm{s}$?

::grafico{curve="t,2+2*t-((t-2)+abs(t-2))-((t-4)+abs(t-4)),0,6" punti="0,2,pieno;2,6,pieno;4,6,pieno;6,2,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,6;4,0,4,6" xmin=0 xmax=6.45 ymin=0 ymax=7.3 nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="Velocity is 2 m/s at 0 and 6 s and remains 6 m/s between 2 and 4 s."}

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The graph has two trapeziums where velocity changes and a rectangle in the middle. Each trapezium has area $\dfrac{2+6}{2}\cdot2\,\mathrm{m}=8\,\mathrm{m}$. The rectangle gives $12\,\mathrm{m}$. Their sum is $28\,\mathrm{m}$. Using only the initial and final velocities would miss the section at higher speed.
