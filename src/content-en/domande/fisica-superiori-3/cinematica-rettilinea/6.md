A vehicle has initial speed $v_0=13\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with constant deceleration magnitude $d=2.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate its stopping distance, excluding reaction time.

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $32.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $65\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Take the initial direction as positive, so acceleration is $-d$. In $v^2=v_0^2-2ds$, set $v=0$. The calculation is $s=\dfrac{13^2}{2\cdot2.6}\,\mathrm{m}=32.5\,\mathrm{m}$. A check uses the five seconds of braking and the average speed $6.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over that interval.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\frac{v_0^2}{2d}\\[4pt]
&= 32.5\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
