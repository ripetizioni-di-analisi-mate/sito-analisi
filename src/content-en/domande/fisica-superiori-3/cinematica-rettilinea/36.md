A body has velocity $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=2\,\mathrm s$, $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=5\,\mathrm s$ and $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=6\,\mathrm s$. Find its mean acceleration between the first and last readings.

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The intermediate changes are $+6$ and $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Add them to get $+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, then divide by the total four seconds. A simple mean of stage accelerations would assign equal weight to unequal durations.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_m&=\frac{9-5}{6-2}\\[4pt]
&= 1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
