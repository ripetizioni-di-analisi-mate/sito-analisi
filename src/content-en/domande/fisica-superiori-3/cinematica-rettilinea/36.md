A ticker tape is marked at equal time intervals. Four consecutive marks, along a line traversed in a single direction, are at $0$, $2$, $6$ and $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which conclusion is justified even if the time interval is unknown?

- The acceleration is $2\,\mathrm{cm/s^2}$.
- The final speed is $12\,\mathrm{cm/s}$.
- The speed is constant throughout the three intervals.
- The mean speeds in the three intervals are in the ratio $1:2:3$.

## Explanation

The successive gaps are $2$, $4$ and $6$ centimetres. Mean speeds are obtained by dividing by the same duration. This preserves their ratio, while numerical speeds in time units require the duration.
