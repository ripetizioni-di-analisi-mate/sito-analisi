A mass pulled right is held by a string to the left. What is the reaction to the tension the string exerts on the mass?

- The force the mass exerts on the string.
- The force pulling the mass to the right.
- The weight of the mass.
- The normal force on the mass.

## Explanation

The interaction is between mass and string. Swapping the two bodies gives the reciprocal force, acting on the string in the opposite direction. The mass is in equilibrium because the two forces pulling it sum to zero. The requested reaction is still a force on the string even when the mass does not move.
