Two perpendicular forces of equal magnitude act on a particle. Where does their resultant point?

- Along one force.
- Along their internal angle bisector.
- Perpendicular to the plane.
- Along the external direction opposite to both.

## Explanation

The force parallelogram is a square because its sides are equal and perpendicular. Its diagonal from the shared origin is the internal angle bisector.
