Two perpendicular forces of equal magnitude act on a particle. Where does their resultant point?

- Along one force.
- Along their internal angle bisector.
- Perpendicular to the plane.
- Along the external direction opposite to both.

## Explanation

The force parallelogram is a square because its sides are equal and perpendicular. Its diagonal from the shared origin is the internal angle bisector. Projection onto axes aligned with the forces gives equal components. The resultant therefore makes an angle of $45^\circ$ to each force.
