A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ body accelerates leftwards at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. A $12\,\mathrm N$ force pulls right. What is the magnitude of the only leftward force?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

With right positive, acceleration is minus two and the net force is minus six newtons. The leftward force must balance twelve newtons to the right and supply six more to the left. Its magnitude is eighteen while its signed component is negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{\mathrm{sin}}&=F_{\mathrm{des}}-ma\\[4pt]&=12-3(-2)\\[4pt]
&= 18\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
