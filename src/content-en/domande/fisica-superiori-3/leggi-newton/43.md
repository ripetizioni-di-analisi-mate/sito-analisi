In free fall in a uniform gravitational field, what happens to gravitational weight and the reading of a scale in unrestrained contact?

- Both vanish.
- The reading rises above $mg$.
- Weight remains $mg$ and the reading is zero.
- Weight becomes negative and the reading stays $mg$.

## Explanation

In free fall gravity can supply the entire common acceleration. The scale exerts no supporting normal force, so its reading is zero.
