In free fall in a uniform gravitational field, what happens to gravitational weight and the reading of a scale falling with the body without holding it in place?

- Both vanish.
- The reading rises above $mg$.
- Weight remains $mg$ and the reading is zero.
- Weight becomes negative and the reading stays $mg$.

## Explanation

In free fall gravity can supply the entire common acceleration. The scale exerts no supporting normal force, so its reading is zero. The field still acts on the body and the scale. They fall together without the compression that the instrument uses to measure a contact force.
