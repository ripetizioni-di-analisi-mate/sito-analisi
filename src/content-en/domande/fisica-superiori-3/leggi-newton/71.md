On the same smooth-track train, which joint has greater tension when the front cart pulls all the others to the right?

- The one between the second and third carts.
- They are equal.
- The one between the first and second carts.
- It depends on the axis direction.

## Explanation

The front joint tows two carts, the next tows just the last cart. At equal acceleration the first joint pulls more mass.
