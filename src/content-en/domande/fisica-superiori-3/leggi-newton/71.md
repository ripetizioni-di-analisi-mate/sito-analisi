Three carts on a smooth track are joined in a row by two taut ideal strings. Which string has the greater tension when the first cart pulls the others to the right?

- The one between the second and third carts.
- They are equal.
- The one between the first and second carts.
- It depends on the axis direction.

## Explanation

The front joint tows two carts, the next tows just the last cart. At equal acceleration the first joint pulls more mass. Compare $T_{12}=(m_2+m_3)a$ with $T_{23}=m_3a$. On a smooth track the difference is the force needed to accelerate the middle cart.
