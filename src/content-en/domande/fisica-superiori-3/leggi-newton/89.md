On a circular horizontal track, mass doubles while velocity and trajectory stay fixed. What happens to radial acceleration and radial force?

- The former is unchanged and the latter doubles.
- Both double.
- The former doubles and the latter is unchanged.
- Both stay the same.

## Explanation

Acceleration depends only on speed and radius. The required resultant, however, is acceleration times mass. Curvature and speed stay the same, fixing $v^2/r$. Doubling mass requires twice the force from the constraint to produce that acceleration.
