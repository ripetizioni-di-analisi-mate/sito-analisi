A lift descends while its speed decreases. How does the reading compare with weight if the person stays in contact?

- It is less than weight.
- It is exactly zero.
- It always equals weight.
- It is greater than weight.

## Explanation

Slowing a descent requires upward acceleration. The normal force must exceed weight to supply that resultant.
