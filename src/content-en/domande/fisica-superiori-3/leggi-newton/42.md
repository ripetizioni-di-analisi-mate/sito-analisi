Which observation distinguishes a mass measurement from a weight measurement on the Moon and Earth?

- Weight and mass both increase on the Moon.
- Mass stays the same, while weight follows the local field.
- Mass follows the local field while weight is unchanged.
- Weight always has the same units as mass.

## Explanation

Weight is the gravitational force. Mass is a factor in that force and does not depend on the measurement location. A spring scale at rest reads a force, which is smaller on the Moon. An inertial comparison of resultant and acceleration gives the same mass in the two locations.
