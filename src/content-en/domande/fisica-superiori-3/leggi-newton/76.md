Both masses of an ideal Atwood machine double. How does acceleration magnitude change?

- It doubles.
- It halves.
- It becomes equal to $g$.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

Both the mass difference and their sum are multiplied by two. That factor cancels from the ratio.
