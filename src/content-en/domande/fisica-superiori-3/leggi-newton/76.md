Both masses of an ideal Atwood machine double. How does acceleration magnitude change?

- It doubles.
- It halves.
- It becomes equal to $g$.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

Both the mass difference and their sum are multiplied by two. That factor cancels from the ratio. The tensions do double. At the same acceleration, each doubled mass requires twice the resultant and also has twice the weight.
