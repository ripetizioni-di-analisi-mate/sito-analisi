A sphere follows a vertical curve. Why does its weight have opposite radial signs at the bottom and top?

- Weight reverses at the bottom.
- The positive inward direction reverses between the two points.
- Mass changes at the top.
- Velocity becomes perpendicular to the tangent.

## Explanation

At the bottom the inward direction points up. At the top it points down. Weight always points down, so its radial projection changes sign.
