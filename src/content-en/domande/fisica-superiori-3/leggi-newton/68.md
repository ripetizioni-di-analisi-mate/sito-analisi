The same constant horizontal net force accelerates cart A at $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and cart B at $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ in separate trials. The force now pulls the coupled carts along the same surface. What is their common acceleration?

- $9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

At the same force, B has half the acceleration and hence twice the mass of A. The combined mass is three times that of A, so the same force gives one third of A's acceleration, or $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
