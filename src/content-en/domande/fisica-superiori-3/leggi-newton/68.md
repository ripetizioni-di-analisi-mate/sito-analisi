Two carts accelerate at $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ at tension $21\,\mathrm N$. No other horizontal forces act on the rear cart. Find its mass.

- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $63\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

The only horizontal resultant on the rear cart is twenty-one newtons. Divide by the common acceleration to recover its mass.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac Ta=\frac{21}{3}\\[4pt]
&= 7\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$
