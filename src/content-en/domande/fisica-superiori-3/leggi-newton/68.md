Two carts joined by an ideal string accelerate together at $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ on a horizontal track, and the tension in the string is $21\,\mathrm N$. No other horizontal force acts on the rear cart. What is its mass?

- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $63\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

On the rear cart the only horizontal force is the tension in the string, so the net horizontal force on it is $21\,\mathrm N$. Newton's second law applied to that cart alone gives $T=ma$, therefore
$$
m=\frac{T}{a}=\frac{21}{3}\,\mathrm{kg}=7\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
The value $63\,\mathrm{kg}$ comes from multiplying the tension by the acceleration. The mass of the front cart cannot be found from these data, because the external pull also acts on it and is not known.
