Two carts of masses $m_1=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ are connected by an ideal string on a smooth horizontal surface. A horizontal force $F=35\,\mathrm N$ pulls the system. What is their common acceleration?

- $3.18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $1.59\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $6.36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4.77\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

For the system of both carts, tension is internal. The external horizontal force accelerates their combined mass. The ideal string fixes the distance between carts, so they share an acceleration while it is taut. The difference between the applied force and tension would instead be useful when isolating the cart pulled directly.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac F{m_1+m_2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
