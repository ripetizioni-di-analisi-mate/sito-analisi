An upward force acts on a box descending vertically. What must be known to decide whether it slows down?

- The direction of that force alone.
- The sign of the vertical net force.
- The initial vertical position.
- The value of horizontal velocity.

## Explanation

Descending velocity is negative on an upward axis. It slows if acceleration is positive, and its sign comes from the sum of all vertical forces. An upward force can still be smaller than the combined downward forces. Its sign alone therefore leaves the overall balance undetermined.
