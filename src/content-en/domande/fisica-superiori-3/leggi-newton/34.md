An upward force acts on a descending box. What must be known to decide whether it slows down?

- The direction of that force alone.
- The sign of the vertical net force.
- The initial vertical position.
- The value of horizontal velocity.

## Explanation

Descending velocity is negative on an upward axis. It slows if acceleration is positive, and its sign comes from the sum of all vertical forces.
