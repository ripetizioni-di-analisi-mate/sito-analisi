Two carts, with $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ at the front and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ at the rear, move rightwards joined by a taut ideal string. A force of $33\,\mathrm N$ pulls the first to the right. Forces resisting motion on the first and second are $3\,\mathrm N$ and $6\,\mathrm N$. What is the tension to the nearest tenth of a newton?

- $13.1\,\mathrm N$
- $19.1\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $33\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The net external force of $33-3-6=24\,\mathrm N$ accelerates the total mass, so $a=24/11\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. On the rear cart the string must overcome the $6\,\mathrm N$ resistance and also provide a net force of $6a$. Ignoring that resistance would give the cart’s resultant instead of the tension.

$$
T=\dfrac{210}{11}\,\mathrm N\simeq19.1\,\mathrm N.
$$
