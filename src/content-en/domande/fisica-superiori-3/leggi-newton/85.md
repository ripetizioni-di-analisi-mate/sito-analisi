At fixed mass and radius, what happens to the required radial resultant if speed doubles?

- It quadruples.
- It doubles.
- It halves.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The dependence on $v$ is quadratic. Doubling $v$ multiplies $v^2$ by four. At fixed mass and radius, the radial acceleration itself quadruples. A curve that needed $5\,\mathrm N$ at the initial speed needs $20\,\mathrm N$ at twice that speed.
