At fixed mass and radius, what happens to the required radial resultant if speed doubles?

- It quadruples.
- It doubles.
- It halves.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The dependence on $v$ is quadratic. Doubling $v$ multiplies $v^2$ by four.
