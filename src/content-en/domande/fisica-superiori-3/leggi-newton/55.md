Two attached carts of total mass $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ move under a constant pull of $20\,\mathrm N$. The total drag follows $R=kv$. At $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ they accelerate forwards at $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the acceleration when their speed reaches $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The first acceleration measurement identifies the net force on the whole system. The difference between the pull and the net force is the drag at the first speed. The linear model makes drag grow by a factor of three halves at the new speed. The pull is fixed, so the net force falls.

$$
\begin{aligned}R(2\,\mathrm{m/s})&=(20-8\cdot1.5)\,\mathrm N=8\,\mathrm N,\\ a(3\,\mathrm{m/s})&=\frac{20-\frac32\cdot8}{8}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
