Two objects are in the same gravitational field. The first weighs three times as much as the second. What is their mass ratio?

- Three to one.
- One to three.
- Nine to one.
- One to one.

## Explanation

The factor $g$ is shared and cancels from the weight ratio. The mass ratio remains.
