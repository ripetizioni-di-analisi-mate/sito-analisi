Two objects are in the same gravitational field. The first weighs three times as much as the second. What is their mass ratio?

- Three to one.
- One to three.
- Nine to one.
- One to one.

## Explanation

The factor $g$ is shared and cancels from the weight ratio. The mass ratio remains. For example, in the same location comparing $90\,\mathrm N$ with $30\,\mathrm N$ gives the same mass ratio. With different fields, the gravitational factor would have to be included.
