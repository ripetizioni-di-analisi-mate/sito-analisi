With a fixed total pulling force, a third connected cart is added on a smooth track. How does common acceleration change?

- It decreases.
- It increases.
- It stays the same.
- It becomes negative.

## Explanation

The same net force must accelerate a greater total mass. The ratio $F/m_{\mathrm{tot}}$ decreases.
