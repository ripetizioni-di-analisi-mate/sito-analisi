If the radial resultant disappears and total net force is then zero, which direction does a body initially in uniform circular motion follow?

- The outward radius.
- The inward radius.
- The tangent at release.
- The same circle.

## Explanation

At release the geometry of circular motion makes velocity tangent to the circle. With no resultant, its magnitude and direction stay fixed. The body therefore follows the line through the release point tangent to the previous path.
