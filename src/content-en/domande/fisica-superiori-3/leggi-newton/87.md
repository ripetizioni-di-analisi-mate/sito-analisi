If the radial resultant disappears and total net force is then zero, which direction does a body initially in uniform circular motion follow?

- The outward radius.
- The inward radius.
- The tangent at release.
- The same circle.

## Explanation

With zero net force, velocity stays unchanged. It was tangent just before release, so the body continues along that line.
