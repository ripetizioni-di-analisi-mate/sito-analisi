Which mass is used in $T=ma$ when tension is the only horizontal force on the trailing cart?

- The total mass of both carts.
- The leading cart's mass.
- The trailing cart's mass.
- The difference between their masses.

## Explanation

The equation concerns the trailing cart alone. Acceleration is shared, but mass belongs to the isolated body. The mass of the front cart instead enters the balance of the whole system. If the rear cart also faced a resistance, include that force in its diagram.
