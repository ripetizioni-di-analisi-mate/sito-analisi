Which mass is used in $T=ma$ when tension is the only horizontal force on the trailing cart?

- The total mass of both carts.
- The leading cart's mass.
- The trailing cart's mass.
- The difference between their masses.

## Explanation

The equation concerns the trailing cart alone. Acceleration is shared, but mass belongs to the isolated body.
