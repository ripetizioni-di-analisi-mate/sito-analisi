A lift accelerates upwards at $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and a $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ person stands still relative to the scale. A handrail exerts an upward vertical force of $80\,\mathrm N$ on the person. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find the scale reading.

- $660\,\mathrm{N}$
- $580\,\mathrm{N}$
- $740\,\mathrm{N}$
- $520\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Relative to the ground the person accelerates with the lift. Weight acts downwards. Both the scale and the handrail support the person upwards. The scale measures only its own normal force, so subtract the handrail contribution from the total upward support required.

$$
N=m(g+a)-80\,\mathrm N=[60(10+1)-80]\,\mathrm N=580\,\mathrm N.
$$
