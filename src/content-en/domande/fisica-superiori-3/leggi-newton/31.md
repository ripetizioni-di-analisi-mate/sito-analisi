A moving object in an inertial frame experiences zero net force. What is its subsequent motion?

- It stops immediately.
- It keeps its speed but may curve.
- It continues at constant velocity.
- Its speed increases without changing direction.

## Explanation

Zero net force gives zero acceleration. The velocity vector keeps both magnitude and direction. The law concerns the overall balance. Individual forces can be present and cancel out. A body starting at rest under the same condition stays at rest.
