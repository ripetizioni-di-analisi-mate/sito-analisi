In writing equations for an ideal Atwood machine, both vertical axes point up. How are the signed accelerations of the masses related?

- Equal signs and equal magnitudes.
- Their sum is always $g$.
- Their magnitudes are inversely proportional to mass.
- Opposite signs and equal magnitudes.

## Explanation

Fixed string length means that as one branch shortens, the other lengthens by the same amount. With aligned axes the changes have opposite signs.
