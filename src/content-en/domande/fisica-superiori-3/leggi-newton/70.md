A train of three $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ carts accelerates at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ on a smooth track, pulled at the first. Find the tension between the first and second carts.

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Isolate the second and third carts together. Their mutual tension is internal, while the tension from the first is their only external pull. It accelerates four kilograms.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T&=(2+2)\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
