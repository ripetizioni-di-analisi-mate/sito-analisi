An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ experiences a constant net force $F=24\,\mathrm N$. Find its acceleration magnitude in an inertial frame.

- $a=4.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $9.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $7.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

In an inertial frame, the second law relates net force to mass and acceleration. Division by the positive mass gives the magnitude.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac Fm\\[4pt]
&= 4.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
