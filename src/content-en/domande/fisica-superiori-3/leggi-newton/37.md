Sample A weighs $36\,\mathrm N$ where $g=6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Sample B weighs $45\,\mathrm N$ where $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The same horizontal net force is applied in both laboratories. Find the acceleration magnitude ratio $a_B/a_A$.

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Divide each weight by the local field strength where it was measured. The larger weight belongs to the smaller mass. Under the same net force that sample accelerates more because acceleration is inversely proportional to mass.

$$
\frac{a_B}{a_A}=\frac{m_A}{m_B}=\frac{36/6}{45/10}=\frac{4}{3}.
$$
