An object weighs $60\,\mathrm N$ where $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What does it weigh where $g=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The object still has six kilograms of mass. Weight at the second location is that same mass times two metres per second squared. The second field is one fifth of the first, so weight changes by the same factor.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_2&=P_1\frac{g_2}{g_1}=60\frac2{10}\\[4pt]
&= 12\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
