An ideal Atwood machine has $1$ and $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ masses. It starts from rest at $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What speed does it reach after $1\,\mathrm s$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The weight difference accelerates the combined mass, giving six metres per second squared. From rest, one second at that acceleration produces six metres per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\frac{10(4-1)}{4+1}\cdot1\\[4pt]
&= 6\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
