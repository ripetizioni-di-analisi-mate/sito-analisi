Two carts on the same track interact through a spring. At one instant the $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart has acceleration $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart has acceleration $+4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ along the same axis. What is the net horizontal force external to the pair, including the negligible-mass spring?

- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $-8\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The forces exerted by the connector are internal to the pair with the spring. Add the two signed resultants, $2\cdot(-2)\,\mathrm N$ and $3\cdot4\,\mathrm N$. Each contribution keeps its sign. A spring allows different accelerations, so the acceleration of either cart alone cannot be used for the total mass.

$$
\sum F_{\mathrm{ext},x}=8\,\mathrm N.
$$
