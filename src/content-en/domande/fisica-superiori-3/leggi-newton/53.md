Two carts of $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ are connected on a smooth track. A $24\,\mathrm N$ horizontal pull acts on the front cart. Find the common acceleration.

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="-2,0 -1,0 -1,0.8 -2,0.8;0.4,0 1.9,0 1.9,0.8 0.4,0.8" segmenti="-1,0.4,0.4,0.4;-2.4,0,3.5,0" vettori="1.9,0.4,3.1,0.4,F" etichette="-1.5,0.4,2;1.1,0.4,4" xmin=-2.5 xmax=3.6 ymin=-0.6 ymax=1.3 titolo="The external pull accelerates both masses, labelled in kilograms"}

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

For the two-cart system tension cancels because it acts between members of the chosen system. The only horizontal external force supplies twenty-four newtons to the full six kilograms. A taut connection gives both carts the same acceleration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac{24}{2+4}\\[4pt]
&= 4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
