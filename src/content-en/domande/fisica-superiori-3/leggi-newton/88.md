A point follows a circle while speeding up. How does the total force magnitude compare with its radial component when the tangential component is nonzero?

- It is equal.
- It is smaller.
- It is zero.
- It is greater.

## Explanation

The radial component is perpendicular to the tangential one. Total magnitude squared also includes the latter's positive square. The radial component changes the direction of velocity, and the tangential component makes its magnitude grow. Since the latter is nonzero, the vector sum has greater magnitude than the radial component alone.
