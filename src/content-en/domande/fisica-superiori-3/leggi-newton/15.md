A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart accelerates in a plane with components $(-2;1)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. One of its two horizontal forces has components $(4;-1)\,\mathrm N$. Find the components of the other force.

- $(-6;3)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(10;-4)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-10;4)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-2;2)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Multiplying mass by acceleration gives the two components of the net force. Subtract the measured force on each axis to isolate the unknown one. The known force points right and down, so the unknown force must offset it in both components.

$$
\vec F_2=\big(3(-2;1)-(4;-1)\big)\,\mathrm N=(-10;4)\,\mathrm N.
$$
