A connection between carts is elastic and can stretch or shorten. Which conclusion no longer holds as for a taut inextensible string?

- The external forces can be summed.
- Force is a vector.
- Both accelerations must always agree.
- Weight is measured in newtons.

## Explanation

Equal accelerations along the track follow from fixed separation. A connection with varying length allows that separation to change. If the connector changes length, the position difference is not constant. The two accelerations can still coincide at an instant, without the connector requiring equality throughout the motion.
