A particle experiences forces of $3$ and $5\,\mathrm N$. What orientation minimises the resultant magnitude?

- Opposite along one line.
- Along one line in the same direction.
- Perpendicular.
- Both at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal with the same direction.

## Explanation

The first force can cancel at most three newtons of the second. Opposite directions maximise cancellation and leave two newtons. Tilting the forces away from the opposing arrangement makes the smaller one cancel less of the larger, and also adds a perpendicular component, increasing the resultant magnitude.
