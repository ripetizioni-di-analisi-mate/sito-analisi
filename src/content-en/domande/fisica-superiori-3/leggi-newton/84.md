Which real force can provide the radial resultant on a level bend taken by a car without skidding?

- An additional centripetal force separate from all other forces.
- Vertical weight alone.
- The vertical normal force perpendicular to the road.
- Static friction between tyres and road.

## Explanation

On a level road weight and the normal force are vertical. Static friction can provide the horizontal component towards the centre if the tyres do not skid. At the contact point the rolling tyre grips the road, so the static limit applies. If available friction falls short, the path cannot maintain the required radius.
