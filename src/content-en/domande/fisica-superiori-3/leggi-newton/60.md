The string breaks while the pull continues only on the leading cart on a smooth surface. What happens to its acceleration immediately afterwards?

- It decreases.
- It vanishes.
- It reverses sign.
- It increases.

## Explanation

After the break the pull acts on the leading cart alone and tension vanishes. The same force accelerates less mass. The front cart’s velocity does not jump, but its velocity–time slope changes. The second cart retains its velocity if it has no other horizontal forces.
