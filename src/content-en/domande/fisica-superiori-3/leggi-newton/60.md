The string breaks while the pull continues only on the leading cart on a smooth surface. What happens to its acceleration immediately afterwards?

- It decreases.
- It vanishes.
- It reverses sign.
- It increases.

## Explanation

After the break the pull acts on the leading cart alone and tension vanishes. The same force accelerates less mass.
