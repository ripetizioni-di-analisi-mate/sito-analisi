An object's weight doubles in a stronger gravitational field. What happens to the acceleration a fixed net force would give that object?

- It doubles.
- It stays the same.
- It halves.
- It vanishes.

## Explanation

Weight depends on $g$, but mass is unchanged. At fixed net force, $F/m$ stays the same. Gravity could change the net force in an actual experiment. The question holds total resultant fixed, so it compares the same force on the same mass.
