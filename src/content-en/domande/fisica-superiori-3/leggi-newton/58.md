Three carts connected on a smooth track have masses of $2$, $3$ and $5\,\mathrm{kg}$. What total pulling force is needed for $a=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Common acceleration requires the sum of the three masses times that acceleration. The forces in the connections cancel when summed over the system.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F&=(2+3+5)\cdot3\\[4pt]
&= 30\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
