Three carts connected on a smooth track have masses of $2$, $3$ and $5\,\mathrm{kg}$. What total pulling force is needed for $a=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

With taut strings, all three carts share the acceleration. Include carts and strings in the system, so the tensions do not enter the external balance. On the smooth track the pull provides the entire horizontal resultant: $3\,\mathrm N$ is required for each of the ten kilograms.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F&=(2+3+5)\cdot3\\[4pt]
&= 30\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
