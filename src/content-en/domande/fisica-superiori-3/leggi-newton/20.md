A resultant of $24\,\mathrm N$ produces $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What magnitude gives $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ on the same mass?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $96\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

At fixed mass force is proportional to acceleration. The new acceleration is one quarter of the original, so the force is also one quarter. The first measurement gives $24/8=3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Multiplying by the new acceleration gives the same force as the proportional comparison.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_2&=24\cdot\frac28\\[4pt]
&= 6\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
