Equal and opposite horizontal forces act on opposite ends of a two-cart system. What is the total horizontal resultant?

- It is zero even if the connection is under load.
- It is twice either force.
- It points towards the heavier cart.
- It depends on the origin.

## Explanation

The two external forces cancel in the system sum. Any internal load concerns the individual carts. The horizontal centre-of-mass acceleration is zero. Finding out the tension or the forces on individual carts requires drawing their separate diagrams.
