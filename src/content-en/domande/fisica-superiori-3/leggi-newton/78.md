Why is acceleration magnitude for two positive masses in an ideal Atwood machine less than $g$?

- The pulley necessarily reduces $g$.
- The magnitude of the mass difference is less than their sum.
- The rising mass is weightless.
- Tension always exceeds the sum of weights.

## Explanation

The factor multiplying $g$ is the ratio of absolute difference to sum. For two positive masses it is less than one.
