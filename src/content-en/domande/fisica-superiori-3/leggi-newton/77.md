In an ideal Atwood machine, one mass accelerates downwards. How does tension compare with weight for that mass?

- Tension is less than weight.
- Tension is greater than weight.
- They are equal.
- Tension is negative.

## Explanation

The net force on the descending mass points down. Downward weight must therefore exceed upward tension. Taking down as positive gives $m g-T=ma>0$. If the same mass accelerated upwards, the comparison would reverse.
