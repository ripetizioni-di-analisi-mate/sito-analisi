In an ideal Atwood machine with $a>0$, how does tension compare with the weight of the descending mass?

- Tension is less than weight.
- Tension is greater than weight.
- They are equal.
- Tension is negative.

## Explanation

The net force on the descending mass points down. Downward weight must therefore exceed upward tension.
