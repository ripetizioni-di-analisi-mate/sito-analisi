A taut inextensible cord connects two carts on one track, with the ends moving along the cord axis. How do their accelerations compare?

- They are opposite.
- The first is always twice the second.
- They depend independently on the individual masses.
- Their signed components are equal.

## Explanation

Their separation stays constant. Displacements, velocities and accelerations along the axis therefore change together.
