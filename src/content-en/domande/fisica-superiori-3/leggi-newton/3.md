An object has mass $m=3\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the magnitude of its weight near Earth's surface, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $14.7\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $58.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $44.1\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Weight is a gravitational force. The product of mass and gravitational acceleration has the dimensions of force and is measured in newtons. A rough estimate near Earth uses about $10\,\mathrm N$ of weight per kilogram: three kilograms gives about thirty newtons, consistent with the more precise value of $g$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=mg\\[4pt]
&\simeq 29.4\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
