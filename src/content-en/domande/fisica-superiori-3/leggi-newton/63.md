A $30\,\mathrm N$ pull acts on a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart that tows a $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart on a smooth surface. Find the tension.

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="-2,0 -0.7,0 -0.7,0.8 -2,0.8;0.7,0 1.6,0 1.6,0.8 0.7,0.8" segmenti="-0.7,0.4,0.7,0.4" vettori="1.6,0.4,2.7,0.4,F;-0.7,0.4,0.1,0.4,T" etichette="-1.3,0.4,3;1.1,0.4,2" xmin=-2.4 xmax=3 ymin=-0.4 ymax=1.4 titolo="The 3 kg cart receives the pull through the string"}

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The pull accelerates five kilograms at six metres per second squared. Tension is the only horizontal force on the rear cart, so it must accelerate three kilograms at the same rate. The front cart has $30-18=12\,\mathrm N$, consistent with two kilograms times six.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac{30}{2+3}=6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\\[4pt]T&=3\cdot6\\[4pt]
&= 18\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
