Three blocks of $2$, $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ touch in that order from left to right on a smooth track. A $45\,\mathrm N$ force pushes the left block rightwards. What is the component of the force the right block exerts on the middle one, taking rightwards as positive?

- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $-15\,\mathrm N$
- $-20\,\mathrm N$
- $45\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The external push accelerates the $9\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The middle block’s push is the only horizontal force on the $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ right block, so it is $20\,\mathrm N$ rightwards. By Newton’s third law, the right block exerts an equally large leftward force on the middle one.

$$
F_x=-20\,\mathrm N.
$$
