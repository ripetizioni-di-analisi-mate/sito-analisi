A block is at rest on a smooth ramp inclined at $30^\circ$. A string along the slope holds it with a tension of $20\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnitude $P$ of the block’s weight?

- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Along the slope, the string balances the weight component parallel to the ramp, $P\sin30^\circ$. The normal force is perpendicular to this axis, and there is no friction. Since the sine is $1/2$, the full weight is twice the force measured in the string.

$$
P=40\,\mathrm N.
$$
