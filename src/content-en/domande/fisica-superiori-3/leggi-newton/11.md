An object has mass $m=3.5\,\mathrm{kg}$. Find the magnitude of its weight near Earth's surface. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $17.15\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $68.6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $P=34.3\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $51.45\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Weight is a gravitational force. The product of mass and gravitational acceleration has the dimensions of force and is measured in newtons.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=mg\\[4pt]
&\simeq 34.3\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
