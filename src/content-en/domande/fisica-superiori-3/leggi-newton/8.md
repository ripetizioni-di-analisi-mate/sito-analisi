An object of mass $m=0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$ follows a circle of radius $r=1.6\,\mathrm m$ at constant speed $v=2.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the net radial force?

- $2.105\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $8.42\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6.315\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $4.21\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The net radial force produces the centripetal acceleration $v^2/r$. Its magnitude is mass times that acceleration. The tangential component is zero because speed is constant. The acceleration vector points towards the centre, perpendicular to the velocity tangent to the circle.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_r&=m\frac{v^2}r\\[4pt]
&\simeq 4.21\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
