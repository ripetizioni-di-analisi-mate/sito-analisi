Two noncollinear forces act on one mass. How is acceleration direction determined?

- Always from the direction of the larger force.
- From the initial velocity direction.
- From the direction perpendicular to both.
- From the direction of their vector sum.

## Explanation

Mass is a positive scalar. Dividing the resultant by mass changes magnitude while preserving direction. Add components along each axis first, then draw the resultant vector. The magnitudes alone are insufficient to add non-collinear forces.
