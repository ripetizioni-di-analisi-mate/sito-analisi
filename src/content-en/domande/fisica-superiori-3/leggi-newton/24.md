Two noncollinear forces act on one mass. How is acceleration direction determined?

- Always from the direction of the larger force.
- From the initial velocity direction.
- From the direction perpendicular to both.
- From the direction of their vector sum.

## Explanation

Mass is a positive scalar. Dividing the resultant by mass changes magnitude while preserving direction.
