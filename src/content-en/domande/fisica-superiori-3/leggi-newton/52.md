A lift scale reads the object's weight. What is required in the model where weight and normal force are the only vertical forces?

- The lift is stationary.
- The lift is rising.
- The lift is in free fall.
- Vertical acceleration is zero.

## Explanation

A reading equal to weight means zero vertical resultant. By the second law, acceleration vanishes and velocity may have a constant nonzero value.
