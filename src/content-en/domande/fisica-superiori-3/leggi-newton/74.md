Two masses of $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hang from a taut cord. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, their measured acceleration has magnitude $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with the heavier mass descending. The two branches read $22$ and $27\,\mathrm N$, respectively. Which model is consistent with the data?

- An ideal pulley and equal tensions
- A pulley with appreciable rotational inertia
- Static equilibrium of both masses
- A completely slack cord on both sides

## Explanation

The lighter mass accelerates upwards, so its branch supplies two newtons more than its weight. The heavier mass accelerates downwards, so its branch supplies three newtons less than its weight. The unequal tensions can accelerate the rotation of a pulley with rotational inertia.

$$
T_1=2(10+1)\,\mathrm N=22\,\mathrm N,\qquad T_2=3(10-1)\,\mathrm N=27\,\mathrm N.
$$
