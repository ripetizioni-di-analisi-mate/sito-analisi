A scale reads $300\,\mathrm N$ when the lift accelerates down at $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the mass?

- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $75\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

Normal force per unit mass is $g+a$, or six newtons per kilogram. Dividing the reading by six gives fifty kilograms. With up positive, use $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for the acceleration. Check that the inferred weight is larger than the reading, as downward acceleration requires.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{N}{g+a}=\frac{300}{10-4}\\[4pt]
&= 50\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$
