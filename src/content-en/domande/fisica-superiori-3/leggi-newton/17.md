A $3.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ load rises on a vertical cord. In $0.40\,\mathrm s$ its speed decreases steadily from $1$ to $0.60\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Only tension and weight act. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the tension.

- $28.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $32\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The load keeps rising, but its acceleration points down. With upwards positive the net force is negative. The tension must be smaller than the weight in magnitude. Add the signed net force to the weight magnitude to find it.

$$
\begin{aligned}a_y&=\frac{0.60-1}{0.40}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2},\\T&=m(g+a_y)=3.2(10-1)\,\mathrm N=28.8\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
