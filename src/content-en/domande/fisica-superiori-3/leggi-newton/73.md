Masses of $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hang from the two sides of a taut ideal cord over an ideal pulley. An additional external upward force of $5\,\mathrm N$ acts on the heavier mass. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and find the tension.

- $22\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $28\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The auxiliary force reduces the imbalance that drives the heavier side. Divide the remaining imbalance by the total mass to obtain the acceleration. On the lighter side, the tension must support the weight and accelerate the mass upwards.

$$
\begin{aligned}a&=\frac{30-5-20}{2+3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=1\,\mathrm{m/s^2},\\T&=2(10+1)\,\mathrm N=22\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
