Masses of $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hang on an ideal pulley at $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the tension?

- $24\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The lighter mass rises, so tension must exceed its twenty-newton weight. Acceleration of two on two kilograms requires four more newtons. The descending mass equation also gives $30-3\cdot2=24\,\mathrm N$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=10\frac{3-2}{3+2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\\[4pt]T&=2(10+2)\\[4pt]
&= 24\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
