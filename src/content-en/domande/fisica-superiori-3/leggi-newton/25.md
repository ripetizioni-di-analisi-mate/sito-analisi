A body of mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ experiences $20\,\mathrm N$ to the right and $8\,\mathrm N$ to the left. What is its rightward acceleration?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,5,0,F_1;0,0,-2,0,F_2" xmin=-3 xmax=6 ymin=-1 ymax=1 nomi="x,y" titolo="Arrow lengths 5 and 2 match the ratio 20 to 8"}

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $\frac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The two forces lie along one line but point oppositely. With right positive, their signed sum is twelve newtons. Normal force and weight, if balanced vertically, do not enter the horizontal sum. Dividing twelve by six gives two metres per second squared.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac{20-8}{6}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
