A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ parcel is initially at rest relative to a horizontal bed that starts accelerating leftwards at $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Use $\mu_{\mathrm s}=0.40$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the horizontal friction component on the parcel, taking rightwards as positive?

- $-10\,\mathrm N$
- $-16\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

If the parcel stays on the bed without slipping, its acceleration points left. The required friction is $4(-2.5)\,\mathrm N=-10\,\mathrm N$. Its magnitude is below $\mu_{\mathrm s}mg=16\,\mathrm N$, so the assumption is consistent. The maximum is a limit rather than the force actually exerted here.

$$
F_{\mathrm s}=-10\,\mathrm N.
$$
