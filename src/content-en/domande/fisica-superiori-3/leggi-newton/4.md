A person of mass $m=56\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale in a lift accelerating upwards at $a=0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the normal force measured by the scale. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $297\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $1188\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $891\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $594\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Weight points down and the normal force up. From $N-mg=ma$, the reading exceeds the weight. The excess above weight is $56\cdot0.8\,\mathrm N=44.8\,\mathrm N$. This is the net upward force needed to accelerate the person with the scale.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=m(g+a)\\[4pt]
&\simeq 594\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
