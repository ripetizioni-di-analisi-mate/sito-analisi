An object's weight is $120\,\mathrm N$ where $g=8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its mass?

- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $960\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

Each kilogram weighs eight newtons in that location. Divide the full weight by that weight per kilogram. Taking the same mass to a different value of gravity would change its weight while leaving its inertia unchanged.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac Pg=\frac{120}{8}\\[4pt]
&= 15\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$
