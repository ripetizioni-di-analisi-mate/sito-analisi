A graph places net force $F$ on the vertical axis and acceleration $a$ horizontally. For a fixed-mass body, what is the slope?

- Mass.
- The reciprocal of mass.
- Velocity.
- The body's weight.

## Explanation

From $F=ma$ the line passes through the origin. Its slope is the linear coefficient multiplying $a$, namely $m$.
