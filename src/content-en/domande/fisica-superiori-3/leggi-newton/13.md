Two carts of masses $m_1=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=7\,\mathrm{kg}$ are connected by an ideal string on a smooth horizontal surface. A horizontal force $F=40\,\mathrm N$ pulls the system. Calculate their common acceleration.

- $a=3.08\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $1.54\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $6.16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4.62\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

For the system of both carts, tension is internal. The external horizontal force accelerates their combined mass.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac F{m_1+m_2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.08\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
