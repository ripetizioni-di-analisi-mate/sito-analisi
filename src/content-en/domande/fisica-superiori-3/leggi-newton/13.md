Two carts at rest touch on a smooth horizontal track. $A$, with mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$, is to the left of $B$, with mass $8\,\mathrm{kg}$. They are not attached. A force of $40\,\mathrm N$ to the right acts on $B$ alone. What is the component $a_B$ immediately afterwards, taking rightwards as positive?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{20}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

For both to accelerate rightwards, $B$ would have to pull $A$. Simple contact can only push, so the carts separate. The applied force is the only horizontal force on $B$, so divide it by the mass of $B$. Using total mass would give the centre-of-mass acceleration.

$$
a_B=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
