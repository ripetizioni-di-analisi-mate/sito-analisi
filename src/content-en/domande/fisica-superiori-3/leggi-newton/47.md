The floor contact equation yields a negative normal force. What does it indicate?

- The floor pushes the body down.
- Weight must be zero.
- The contact model no longer applies because the floor cannot pull the body down.
- Always take the result's magnitude.

## Explanation

A free surface can compress the object, but does not pull it in this model. A negative normal force would require that pull, so contact is lost.
