A car takes a horizontal bend of radius $20\,\mathrm m$. Static tyre friction provides the entire horizontal resultant. With $\mu_{\mathrm s}=0.50$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the greatest speed at which it can take the bend without skidding?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The bend requires $mv^2/r$ towards its centre. Maximum friction is $\mu_{\mathrm s}mg$ because the normal force is $mg$. Equate these magnitudes at the threshold and cancel the mass. The result is $v^2=100\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, so take the positive square root.

$$
v_{\max}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
