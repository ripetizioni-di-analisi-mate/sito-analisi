A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart takes a bend of radius $8\,\mathrm m$ at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of its radial resultant?

::grafico{curve="8*cos(t),8*sin(t),0,1.5708" vettori="8,0,4,0,F_r;8,0,8,4,v" xmin=-1 xmax=10 ymin=-1 ymax=9 uguale=1 titolo="The resultant is perpendicular to velocity in this uniform motion"}

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $32\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Acceleration points towards the centre at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The resultant also points towards the centre and has magnitude two kilograms times that acceleration. Radial force means the radial sum of the real forces present.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_r&=\frac{mv^2}{r}=\frac{2\cdot4^2}{8}\\[4pt]
&= 4\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
