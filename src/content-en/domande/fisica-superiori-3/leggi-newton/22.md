How can net force be found from a velocity–time graph and known mass?

- Multiply the graph's area by mass.
- Multiply the graph's slope by mass.
- Divide the vertical coordinate by mass.
- Multiply velocity by mass.

## Explanation

The slope is acceleration. Substituting it into $F=ma$ gives the signed resultant along the motion axis.
