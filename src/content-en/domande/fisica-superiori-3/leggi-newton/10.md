An object of mass $m=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ experiences $F_1=32\,\mathrm N$ to the right and $F_2=9\,\mathrm N$ to the left. Calculate its acceleration with right positive.

- $1.915\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $a=3.83\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $7.66\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $5.745\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

With the axis pointing right, the first force is positive and the second is negative. The acceleration has the sign of the net force.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac{F_1-F_2}{m}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.83\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
