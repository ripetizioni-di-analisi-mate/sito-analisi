A $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate is initially at rest on a horizontal surface. A constant horizontal force of $35\,\mathrm N$ is applied to the right. With $\mu_{\mathrm s}=0.50$, $\mu_{\mathrm d}=0.20$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the acceleration magnitude $a$?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{23}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{35}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The normal force is $60\,\mathrm N$, so static friction can reach $30\,\mathrm N$. It would take $35\,\mathrm N$ to keep the crate at rest, so it starts moving. Sliding friction acts leftwards with magnitude $12\,\mathrm N$. Divide the net $23\,\mathrm N$ to the right by $6\,\mathrm{kg}$.

$$
a=\dfrac{23}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
