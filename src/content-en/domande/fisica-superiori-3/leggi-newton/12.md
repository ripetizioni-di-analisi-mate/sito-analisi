A person of mass $m=58\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale in a lift accelerating upwards at $a=0.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the normal force measured by the scale. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $310.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $1242\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $931.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $N=621\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Weight points down and the normal force up. From $N-mg=ma$, the reading exceeds the weight.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=m(g+a)\\[4pt]
&\simeq 621\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
