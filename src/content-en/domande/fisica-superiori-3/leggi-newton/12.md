In a lift, a $58\,\mathrm{kg}$ person stands still on a scale relative to the cabin. They hold a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ parcel stationary relative to themselves. The cabin accelerates upwards at $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what normal force $N$ does the scale measure?

- $525\,\mathrm N$
- $500\,\mathrm N$
- $1260\,\mathrm N$
- $630\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Choose the person and parcel as one system, with both sharing the same acceleration. The forces between the hands and parcel are internal. The external forces are the total weight of $600\,\mathrm N$ and the scale’s normal force. The upward resultant must be $60\cdot0.50\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N$, so the normal force is $630\,\mathrm N$.

$$
N=630\,\mathrm N.
$$
