At two instants a lift has the same acceleration, but it rises in one and descends in the other. How do the readings compare for the same person, who stays on the scale?

- They are opposites.
- The rising reading is always greater.
- They are equal.
- The descending reading is always greater.

## Explanation

The reading is $m(g+a)$. Both instants have the same $a$, and velocity does not enter the equation. For example, an upward $a$ makes the reading exceed weight both at the start of an ascent and at the end of a descent that slows down.
