At two instants a lift has the same acceleration, but it rises in one and descends in the other. How do the readings compare for the same person?

- They are opposites.
- The rising reading is always greater.
- They are equal.
- The descending reading is always greater.

## Explanation

The reading is $m(g+a)$. Both instants have the same $a$, and velocity does not enter the equation.
