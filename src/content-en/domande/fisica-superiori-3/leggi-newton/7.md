Two masses $m_1=1.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=3.9\,\mathrm{kg}$, with $m_2>m_1$, hang from an inextensible string over an ideal massless, frictionless pulley. What is the magnitude of the acceleration, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $2.05\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $8.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $6.15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Write the second law along the direction of motion of each mass. Adding the equations cancels tension, so the difference in weights accelerates the total mass. The heavier mass accelerates downwards and the lighter one upwards. The denominator includes both masses, even though the lighter mass is accelerated upwards.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=g\frac{m_2-m_1}{m_1+m_2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 4.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
