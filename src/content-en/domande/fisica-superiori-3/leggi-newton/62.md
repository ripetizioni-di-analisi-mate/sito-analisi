Why can common acceleration for two carts be calculated before knowing their internal tension?

- Because tension is always zero.
- Because adding their equations cancels the mutual tensions.
- Because a string never exerts forces.
- Because masses do not affect acceleration.

## Explanation

Include the string, whose mass is negligible. In the complete system, each interaction between string and cart gives opposite internal forces. Only external forces survive the sum. To find tension, isolate just one cart, with the string outside the system.
