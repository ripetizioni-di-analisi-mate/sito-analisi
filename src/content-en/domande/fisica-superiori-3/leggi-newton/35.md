An $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass is in a lab where $g=9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of its weight.

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $98\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $78.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $76\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Mass measures inertia while weight measures gravitational interaction. Kilograms times metres per second squared gives newtons. Since $g$ is close to ten, the result is expected to be near eighty newtons, with the computed value slightly below it.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=mg=8\cdot9.8\\[4pt]
&\simeq 78.4\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
