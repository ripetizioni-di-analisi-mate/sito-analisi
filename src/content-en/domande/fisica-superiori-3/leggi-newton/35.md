A crate at rest, weighing $80\,\mathrm N$, sits on a ramp inclined at $30^\circ$. Only weight, the normal force and friction act. What is the smallest coefficient of static friction that can keep it at rest?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\sqrt3$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$

## Explanation

At rest, friction must have magnitude $80\sin30^\circ$ and point uphill. The normal force is $80\cos30^\circ$. At the threshold, $\mu_{\mathrm s}N$ equals the friction required. Dividing the two components cancels the weight and leaves $\tan30^\circ$.

$$
\mu_{\mathrm s}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}.
$$
