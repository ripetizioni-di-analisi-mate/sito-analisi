A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart passes the bottom of a dip of radius $4\,\mathrm m$ at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the normal force?

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $42\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

At the bottom the curve centre is above. Weight acts down and the normal force up, so $N-mg$ must be positive. The normal force must provide twelve newtons of resultant as well as balancing the thirty of weight, giving forty-two newtons.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=mg+\frac{mv^2}{r}=3\left(10+\frac{4^2}{4}\right)\\[4pt]
&= 42\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
