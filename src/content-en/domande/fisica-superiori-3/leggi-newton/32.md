Two isolated masses interact. What net force do the mutual forces contribute to the combined system?

- Twice one force.
- The force on the larger mass.
- The one aligned with the smaller mass's velocity.
- Zero.

## Explanation

The opposite reciprocal actions are internal to the combined system. Both enter the sum over the full system and cancel. The centre of mass of the two bodies can keep moving with constant velocity. Individually, each can accelerate under the force exerted by the other.
