On a graph of weight against mass for objects in one location, what is the slope?

- The mass of the heaviest sample.
- The number of samples.
- The value of $g$.
- The scale normal force in each experiment.

## Explanation

The equation $P=mg$ is linear at fixed $g$. The change in weight divided by the change in mass is therefore $g$.
