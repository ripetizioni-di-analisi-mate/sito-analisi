Two carts of masses $m_1=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=7\,\mathrm{kg}$ are joined by an ideal string on a smooth surface. A force $F=40\,\mathrm N$ pulls the first cart. Find the tension pulling the second.

- $10.75\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $T=21.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $43\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $32.25\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The carts have the same acceleration. Tension is the only horizontal force on the second, so it equals that cart's mass times the common acceleration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T&=m_2\frac F{m_1+m_2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 21.5\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
