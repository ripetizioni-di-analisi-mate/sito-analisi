A $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ block on a smooth $30^\circ$ ramp is connected to a hanging $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass by a taut ideal string over an ideal pulley. The branch on the ramp runs along the slope. What is the tension $T$, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-2.5,-1.6134 2.1,1.0424;2.1,1.0424 2.1,1.34;-0.846,-0.0142 1.95,1.599808;2.4,1.34 2.4,0.15" curve="2.1+0.3*cos(t),1.34+0.3*sin(t),0,2.0943951" poligoni="-1.433,-0.99735 -0.567,-0.49735 -1.125,0.46913 -1.991,-0.03087;2.04,-0.5 2.76,-0.5 2.76,0.15 2.04,0.15" etichette="-1.38,-0.18,m₁;2.32,-0.18,m₂" punti="2.1,1.34,pieno" xmin=-2.8 xmax=3.05 ymin=-1.84 ymax=2.02 titolo="One branch of the string lies along the ramp, and the other is vertical."}

- $30\,\mathrm N$
- $36\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $54\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The weight component along the slope is $30\,\mathrm N$, while the hanging mass weighs $40\,\mathrm N$ and descends. Adding the equations with each direction of motion positive, the $10\,\mathrm N$ difference accelerates $10\,\mathrm{kg}$. The hanging mass gives $T=40\,\mathrm N-4\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=36\,\mathrm N$.

$$
T=36\,\mathrm N.
$$
