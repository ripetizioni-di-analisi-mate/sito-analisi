Someone measures horizontal acceleration inside an accelerating carriage. May they directly use the second law with real forces alone in that frame?

- Yes, as long as mass is positive.
- Yes, because the carriage is rigid.
- No, the carriage frame is not inertial.
- Yes, if motion within the carriage is slow.

## Explanation

The equation with only real forces applies in an inertial frame. Accelerations measured in the carriage include the effect of the frame's own acceleration.
