An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ experiences $F_1=29\,\mathrm N$ to the right and $F_2=8\,\mathrm N$ to the left. Calculate its acceleration with right positive.

- $2.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $8.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $6.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

With the axis pointing right, the first force is positive and the second is negative. The acceleration has the sign of the net force. The horizontal resultant is $29-8=21\,\mathrm N$. The positive sign means rightwards. If the body were travelling leftwards, this acceleration would slow it down.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac{F_1-F_2}{m}\\[4pt]
&= 4.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
