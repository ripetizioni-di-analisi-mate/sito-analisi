Two people of different masses stand on scales in the same lift. What quantity is equal for both if they are at rest relative to the lift?

- The normal force reading.
- The ratio of normal force to mass.
- Gravitational weight.
- The difference between normal force and weight for nonzero $a$.

## Explanation

Dividing $N=m(g+a)$ by $m$ leaves the common value $g+a$. The reading itself scales with mass. The ratio is measured in newtons per kilogram and includes the cabin’s shared acceleration. The difference in normal forces reflects the difference in masses.
