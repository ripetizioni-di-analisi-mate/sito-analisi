Two carts of $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ tow each other on a smooth track. The same pull is applied once to the $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart and once to the $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart, each time the leading one. What is $T_2/T_6$, with the subscript denoting the cart directly pulled?

- $3$.
- $1$.
- $\frac{1}{3}$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

Total force and total mass are unchanged, so both trials have the same common acceleration. Isolate the cart behind: when two kilograms are directly pulled, the string accelerates six kilograms. In the other trial it accelerates two. Their load ratio is three.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{T_2}{T_6}&=\frac{6a}{2a}\\[4pt]
&= 3
\end{aligned}
$$
