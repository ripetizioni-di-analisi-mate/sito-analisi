Two carts of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ in front and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ behind move forwards, linked by an ideal cord that withstands tension up to $15\,\mathrm N$. Their drag forces are $2$ and $3\,\mathrm N$ respectively. Find the largest horizontal pull on the front cart allowed by the tension limit.

- $21\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Tension acts on the rear cart, which must overcome its own drag. The cord limit therefore sets the rear cart’s maximum acceleration and, with the cord taut, that of both carts. To find the total pull, add both drag forces to the net force required for that acceleration.

$$
\begin{aligned}a_{\max}&=\frac{15-3}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2},\\ F_{\max}&=[(2+6)2+2+3]\,\mathrm N=21\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
