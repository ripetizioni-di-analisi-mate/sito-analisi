A cart weighing $50\,\mathrm N$ is where $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. With a horizontal net force of $10\,\mathrm N$, what horizontal acceleration magnitude does it gain?

- $\frac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Weight gives the mass of five kilograms. Mass belongs in the second law, so divide the horizontal net force by five.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac F{P/g}=\frac{10}{50/10}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
