A cart weighing $50\,\mathrm N$ is where $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. With a horizontal net force of $10\,\mathrm N$, what horizontal acceleration magnitude does it gain?

- $\frac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Weight gives the mass of five kilograms. Mass belongs in the second law, so divide the horizontal net force by five. Fifty newtons describes the vertical gravitational force. To find the horizontal acceleration, this information is used only to determine the cart’s inertia.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac F{P/g}=\frac{10}{50/10}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
