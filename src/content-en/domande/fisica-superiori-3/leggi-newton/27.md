Three signed horizontal forces $+12$, $-3$ and $-4\,\mathrm N$ act on a cart. What additional force gives zero net force?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $-7\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $-5\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $19\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The rightward contribution exceeds the two leftward contributions by five newtons. Zero net force requires adding the opposite of that signed total. If the cart is already moving, this condition leaves velocity constant.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{\mathrm{add}}&=-(12-3-4)\\[4pt]
&= -5\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
