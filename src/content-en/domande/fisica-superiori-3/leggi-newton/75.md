If both sides of an ideal Atwood machine have equal mass, what is their acceleration with the string taut?

- Equal to $g$.
- Equal to $g/2$.
- Zero.
- It depends on which mass started higher.

## Explanation

The weights are equal and favour opposite senses of the linked motion. Their difference, which drives the acceleration, is zero.
