If both sides of an ideal Atwood machine have equal mass, what is their acceleration with the string taut?

- Equal to $g$.
- Equal to $g/2$.
- Zero.
- It depends on which mass started higher.

## Explanation

The weights are equal and favour opposite senses of the linked motion. Their difference, which drives the acceleration, is zero. The sides can remain at rest or keep moving in opposite directions with constant speed, while the string stays taut and the masses hang freely.
