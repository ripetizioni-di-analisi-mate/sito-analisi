A person of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ reads $650\,\mathrm N$ on the scale. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the lift acceleration with upwards positive.

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $23\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Weight is five hundred newtons and the reading exceeds it by one hundred and fifty. That difference is the upward net force. Dividing by fifty kilograms gives acceleration; it does not show whether the lift is moving up or down at that instant.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac Nm-g=\frac{650}{50}-10\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
