A cart moves at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over the top of a hill. At the top the radius of curvature is $r$, and the normal force must be at least half the weight. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find the minimum allowed radius.

- $6.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25.6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the top the centre of curvature lies below the cart. Weight points towards it and the normal force points away. For the normal force to remain at least half the weight, radial acceleration can be at most half gravitational acceleration. At fixed speed, a larger radius reduces radial acceleration.

$$
N=m\left(g-\frac{v^2}{r}\right)\geq\frac{mg}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r\geq\frac{2v^2}{g}=\frac{2\cdot8^2}{10}\,\mathrm m=12.8\,\mathrm m.
$$
