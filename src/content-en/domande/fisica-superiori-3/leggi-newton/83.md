An object crosses the crest of a hill of radius $9\,\mathrm m$. Use $g=9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. At the contact limit with $N=0$, what is its speed?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $81\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

At the crest the centre is below. When the normal force vanishes, weight supplies the entire radial resultant, so $mg=mv^2/r$. Cancel mass and take the positive square root. At a greater speed the contact model would require a negative normal force.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\sqrt{gr}=\sqrt{9\cdot9}\\[4pt]
&= 9\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
