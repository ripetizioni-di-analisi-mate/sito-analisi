A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ body rotates at $3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ at radius $0.5\,\mathrm m$. What radial resultant is required?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The formula $a_c=\omega^2r$ uses angular speed directly. Multiplying by four kilograms gives eighteen newtons.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_r&=m\omega^2r=4\cdot3^2\cdot0.5\\[4pt]
&= 18\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
