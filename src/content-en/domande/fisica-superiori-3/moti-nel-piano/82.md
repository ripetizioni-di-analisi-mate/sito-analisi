A point moves around a circle of radius $3\,\mathrm m$. At one instant its speed is decreasing at a rate of $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the magnitude of total acceleration is $13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its speed at that instant?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\sqrt{15}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The rate at which speed decreases gives the magnitude of the tangential component. It is perpendicular to the radial component, whose magnitude comes from subtracting the squares and taking the root. Once the inward acceleration is known, the radius gives the speed.

$$
\begin{aligned}a_c&=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=12\,\mathrm{m/s^2},\\v&=\sqrt{12\cdot3}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
