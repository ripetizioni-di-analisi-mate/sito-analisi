An object launches from a terrace $12\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground with $v_{0y}=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Air resistance is negligible and $g=8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the maximum height above the ground?

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The rise above the roof is four metres. The ground is twelve metres below launch, so the requested elevation includes both terms. After one second vertical velocity vanishes and the equation confirms $12+8-4=16\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
y_{\max}&=h+\frac{v_{0y}^2}{2g}=12+\frac{8^2}{2\cdot8}\\[4pt]
&= 16\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
