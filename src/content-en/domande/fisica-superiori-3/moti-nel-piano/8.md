In circular motion the tangential acceleration has magnitude $a_t=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the centripetal acceleration has magnitude $a_c=6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of the resultant acceleration.

- $3.905\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $15.62\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $11.71\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $a=7.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The tangent is perpendicular to the radius. Combine the component magnitudes using the Pythagorean theorem.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\sqrt{a_t^2+a_c^2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 7.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
