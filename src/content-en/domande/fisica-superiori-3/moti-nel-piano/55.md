A disc rotates uniformly at $\omega=4\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. How much time passes between successive crossings of one mark past a fixed sensor?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

To return to the sensor, the mark must rotate through one complete turn. Divide $2\pi$ by angular speed. Half a second is consistent with two turns per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T&=\frac{2\pi}{4\pi}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
