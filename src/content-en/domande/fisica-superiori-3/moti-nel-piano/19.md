Two motions trace the same spatial curve. What information can differ?

- The set of points on the curve.
- The Cartesian equation understood as the set of points.
- The velocity at which they pass the same point.
- The geometry of the points making up the curve.

## Explanation

A trajectory identifies the points visited and allows different schedules along the same points. Velocity and duration depend on those schedules.
