Two motions trace the same spatial curve. What information can differ?

- The coordinates of a given point on the curve.
- The tangent line at the same regular point.
- The velocity at which they pass the same point.
- The length of the same arc between two points on the curve.

## Explanation

A trajectory identifies the points visited and allows different schedules along the same points. Velocity and duration depend on those schedules. One trolley can take twice as long to travel along the same track as another. The possible positions are unchanged, while passage times and speeds may differ.
