An object maintains $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ around a curve of radius $3\,\mathrm m$. What is its angular speed?

- $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $\frac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

## Explanation

Each radian covers three metres of arc. At six metres per second, the point sweeps two radians per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega&=\frac vr=\frac63\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{rad/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
