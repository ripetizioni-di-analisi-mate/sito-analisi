An object maintains $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ around a circle of radius $3\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its angular speed?

- $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $\frac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

## Explanation

Each radian covers three metres of arc. At six metres per second, the point sweeps two radians per second. The relation is $\omega=\dfrac vr$, so these data give $\dfrac63\,\mathrm{rad/s}=2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The radius converts linear speed into angle swept per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega&=\frac vr=\frac63\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{rad/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
