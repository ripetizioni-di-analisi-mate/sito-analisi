A motor goes from one revolution per second to two revolutions per second. By how much does $\omega$ increase?

- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $4 \pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $2 \pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

## Explanation

The frequency difference is one turn per second. A turn has $2\pi$ radians, so the increase is $2\pi$ radians per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\omega&=2\pi(2-1)\\[4pt]
&= 2 \pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
