What happens to period if frequency doubles?

- It doubles.
- It halves.
- It quadruples.
- It remains the same.

## Explanation

Frequency counts revolutions per second. Twice as many each second means a single turn takes half as long. The reciprocal relation is $T=\dfrac1f$. For example, moving from $2\,\mathrm{Hz}$ to $4\,\mathrm{Hz}$ changes the time per revolution from $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$ to $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$.
