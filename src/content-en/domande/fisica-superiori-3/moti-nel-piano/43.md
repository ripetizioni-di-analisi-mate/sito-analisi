A ball starts with $v_{0y}=15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and moves with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air resistance. What is its vertical velocity component at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ from launch, assuming it is still in flight and upwards is positive?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $35\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The top is reached after one and a half seconds. During the next half second the component points downwards with magnitude five metres per second. The direct check is $v_y=(15-10\cdot2)\,\mathrm{m/s}=-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The sign indicates downward motion on an axis with upwards positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_y&=15-10\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= -5\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
