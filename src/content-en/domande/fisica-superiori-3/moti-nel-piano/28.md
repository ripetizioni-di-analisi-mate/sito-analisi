The initial height of a horizontal launch quadruples at the same speed. How does range change without drag?

- It quadruples.
- It halves.
- It is unchanged.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

Time varies as $\sqrt h$. Four times the height doubles time, and constant speed gives twice the distance. For example, when released from four times the height, the ball has time for two intervals of the original duration. It covers the original distance in each interval because the horizontal speed stays the same.
