The initial height of a horizontal launch quadruples at the same speed. How does range change without drag?

- It quadruples.
- It halves.
- It is unchanged.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

Time varies as $\sqrt h$. Four times the height doubles time, and constant speed gives twice the distance.
