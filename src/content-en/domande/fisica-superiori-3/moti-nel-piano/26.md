Two balls are launched horizontally above the same level floor, A from $5\,\mathrm m$ and B from $20\,\mathrm m$. They cover the same horizontal distance before impact. Neglecting air resistance, what is the initial speed of B divided by that of A?

- $0.25$
- $0.5$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Starting four times as high doubles flight time because time depends on the square root of height. The product of horizontal speed and time must be equal for the two launches. B has twice as long to cover the same distance.

$$
\frac{v_B}{v_A}=\frac{t_A}{t_B}=\sqrt{\frac{5}{20}}=0.5.
$$
