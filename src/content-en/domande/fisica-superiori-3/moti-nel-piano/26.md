An object launched horizontally from $5\,\mathrm m$ lands $7\,\mathrm m$ away. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect drag. What was its launch speed?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

From five metres the vertical time is one second. Horizontal distance is seven metres and horizontal acceleration is zero, so constant velocity covers that distance in one second. The ratio is in metres per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_0&=\frac{R}{\sqrt{2h/g}}=\frac7{\sqrt{2\cdot5/10}}\\[4pt]
&= 7\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
