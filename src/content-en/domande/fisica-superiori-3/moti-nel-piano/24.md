In $3\,\mathrm{s}$ a particle has displacement $(12,5)\,\mathrm{m}$. What minimum path length can connect the two points?

- $17\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $\frac{13}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The shortest path is the straight segment between endpoints. A curved path may be longer, but elapsed time alone does not give its shape. The segment has projections of $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Pythagoras gives $\sqrt{12^2+5^2}\,\mathrm{m}=13\,\mathrm{m}$. Adding the projections gives the length of a route following two perpendicular legs.

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\min}&=\sqrt{12^2+5^2}\\[4pt]
&= 13\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
