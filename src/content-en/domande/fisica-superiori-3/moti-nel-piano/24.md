In $3\,\mathrm s$ a particle has displacement $(12,5)\,\mathrm m$. What minimum path length can connect the two points?

- $17\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $\frac{13}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The shortest path is the straight segment between endpoints. A curved path may be longer, but elapsed time alone does not give its shape.

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\min}&=\sqrt{12^2+5^2}\\[4pt]
&= 13\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
