For the same horizontal launch towards a level floor, one calculation uses $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and another uses $g=9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Air resistance is neglected. How does the range change from the first value to the second?

- It increases by about $1\%$.
- It increases by about $2\%$.
- It decreases by about $1\%$.
- It increases by about $10\%$.

## Explanation

At fixed initial height and speed, range is inversely proportional to $\sqrt g$. The ratio of the second prediction to the first is $\sqrt{\dfrac{10}{9.8}}\simeq1.01$. Its square is about $1.02$. The weaker gravity increases the fall time and the range grows by about one per cent.
