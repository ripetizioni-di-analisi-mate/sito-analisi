A sphere is launched horizontally at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from $20\,\mathrm m$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and ignore drag. What horizontal distance does it cover?

::grafico{curve="4*t,20-5*t^2,0,2" punti="0,20,pieno;8,0,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=10 ymin=-1 ymax=22 titolo="Flight time 2 s and range 8 m"}

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Time comes from vertical free fall, which starts with zero vertical velocity. During those two seconds the horizontal component remains four metres per second. Their product gives the range, with impact at the end of the displayed arc.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_f&=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot20}{10}}=2\,\mathrm s\\[4pt]R&=4\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
