Two points on circles of different radii have the same positive tangential speed. Which has greater angular speed?

- The one at larger radius.
- Both have the same angular speed.
- The one at smaller radius.
- It depends on mass.

## Explanation

Angular speed is $\dfrac{v_t}{r}$. At fixed numerator the smaller radius gives the larger quotient. In the same time both points cover the same arc length. Dividing that length by the smaller radius gives a larger angle and hence a greater angular speed.
