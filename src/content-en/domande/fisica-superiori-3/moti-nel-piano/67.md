Two points on circles of different radii have the same positive tangential speed. Which has larger angular speed?

- The one at larger radius.
- Both have the same angular speed.
- The one at smaller radius.
- It depends on mass.

## Explanation

Angular speed is $v_t/r$. At fixed numerator the smaller radius gives the larger quotient.
