A disk rotates uniformly anticlockwise. Two pictures $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$ apart show a mark first to the right of the centre and then at the opposite end of that diameter. A sensor records the mark crossing its initial position once between the pictures, excluding the endpoints. What is the angular speed, rounded to two decimal places?

- $31.42\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $10.47\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $20.94\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $52.36\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The single return to the initial position after the start accounts for one full revolution. Another half turn takes the mark to the opposite position. The total angle is $3\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, so $\omega=\dfrac{3\pi}{0.30}\,\mathrm{rad/s}=10\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. This rounds to $31.42\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The two images alone would determine the half revolution only up to whole turns.
