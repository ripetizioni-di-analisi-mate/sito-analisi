What do velocity vectors $(3,4)$ and $(-3,4)$ have in common?

- Magnitude and horizontal component.
- Direction and orientation.
- Magnitude and vertical component.
- Only the horizontal component.

## Explanation

Only the sign along one axis reverses. Its square stays the same, so magnitude remains five and the vertical component remains four. The two tips are mirror images across the vertical axis in the component plane. Reflection preserves distances from the origin and leaves the vertical coordinate unchanged.
