What do velocity vectors $(3,4)$ and $(-3,4)$ have in common?

- Magnitude and horizontal component.
- Direction and orientation.
- Magnitude and vertical component.
- Only the horizontal component.

## Explanation

Only the sign along one axis reverses. Its square stays the same, so magnitude remains five and the vertical component remains four.
