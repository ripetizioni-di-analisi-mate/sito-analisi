A point covers a $6\,\mathrm m$ arc on a circle of radius $4\,\mathrm m$. How many radians does it sweep?

- $0.6667\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $1.5\,\mathrm{rad}$.

## Explanation

One radian corresponds to an arc as long as the radius. Six metres is one and a half times the radius, giving one and a half radians.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta&=\frac{s}{r}=\frac64\\[4pt]
&\simeq 1.5\,\mathrm{rad}
\end{aligned}
$$
