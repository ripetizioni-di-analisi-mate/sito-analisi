Two projectiles are launched at the same speed $v_0$, with the same gravity and negligible air resistance. Both initial vertical components point upward. One is twice the other. What is the ratio of its maximum rise to the other's?

- $2$.
- $1$.
- $4$.
- $8$.

## Explanation

Rise depends on the square of the vertical component. Total speed $v_0$ alone does not determine height because some of the velocity may be horizontal. In the ratio at the same $g$, the denominator $2g$ cancels. The result is the square of the ratio of the vertical components, so $2^2=4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{H_2}{H_1}&=\left(\frac{2v_{0y}}{v_{0y}}\right)^2\\[4pt]
&= 4
\end{aligned}
$$
