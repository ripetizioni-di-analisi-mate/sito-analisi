Two launches have the same $v_0$ but one has twice the vertical component. What is the ratio of its maximum rise to the other's?

- $2$.
- $1$.
- $4$.
- $8$.

## Explanation

Rise depends on the square of the vertical component. Total speed $v_0$ alone does not determine height because some of the velocity may be horizontal.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{H_2}{H_1}&=\left(\frac{2v_{0y}}{v_{0y}}\right)^2\\[4pt]
&= 4
\end{aligned}
$$
