A point slows along a circle with nonzero velocity. What acceleration components does it have relative to velocity?

- Only one towards the centre.
- One outward and one along velocity.
- Only one against velocity.
- One towards the centre and one against velocity.

## Explanation

Curvature gives a centripetal component. Speed decreases, so the tangential component points opposite to velocity.
