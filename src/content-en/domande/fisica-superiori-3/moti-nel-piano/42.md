A projectile reaches peak height after $2\,\mathrm s$. If it lands at launch height and drag is negligible, when does it land?

- After $2\,\mathrm s$.
- After $4\,\mathrm s$.
- After $1\,\mathrm s$.
- This cannot be determined without mass.

## Explanation

The vertical equation is a parabola symmetric about the peak time. The initial height is reached again after a second two-second stage.
