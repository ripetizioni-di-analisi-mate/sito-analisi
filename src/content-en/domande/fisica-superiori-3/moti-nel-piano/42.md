A projectile reaches peak height after $2\,\mathrm{s}$. If it lands at launch height and drag is negligible, when does it land?

- After $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- After $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
- After $1\,\mathrm{s}$.
- This cannot be determined without mass.

## Explanation

The vertical equation is a parabola symmetric about the peak time. The initial height is reached again after a second two-second stage. The first interval runs from $t=0$ to the vertex. The symmetric second interval ends at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$. The two intervals are equal because the landing point has the same height.
