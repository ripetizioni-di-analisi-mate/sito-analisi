Two points at equal radii cover the same arc in different times at constant speeds. Which is faster?

- The one taking more time.
- Their speeds match because the arc is the same.
- The one taking less time.
- It depends on the origin's orientation.

## Explanation

For equal distance, distance over time is larger with the smaller denominator. The same applies to angle per unit time. The radius is the same, so the same arc length subtends the same angle. The point that finishes sooner has both greater tangential speed and greater angular speed.
