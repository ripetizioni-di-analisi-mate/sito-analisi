Two points at equal radii cover the same arc in different times at constant speeds. Which is faster?

- The one taking more time.
- Their speeds match because the arc is the same.
- The one taking less time.
- It depends on the origin's orientation.

## Explanation

For equal distance, distance over time is larger with the smaller denominator. The same applies to angle per unit time.
