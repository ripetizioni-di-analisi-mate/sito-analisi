A nonzero velocity is tangent to a smooth curve. When is acceleration also tangent at that instant?

- When the normal component vanishes.
- Whenever the curve is not a straight line.
- When speed is constant on a circle.
- Whenever velocity is nonzero.

## Explanation

Tangential acceleration describes changing speed. If direction also changes, a normal component tilts acceleration away from the tangent.
