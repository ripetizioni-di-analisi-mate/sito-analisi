Two launches with no air resistance have the same initial speed and each returns to its own starting height. The first is inclined at $25^\circ$ above horizontal. The second is above $45^\circ$ and below $90^\circ$. Which second angle makes the ranges equal?

- $65^\circ$
- $25^\circ$
- $55^\circ$
- $75^\circ$

## Explanation

At equal speed and with return to the launch height, only the sine of twice the angle can differ. The first argument is $50^\circ$. The other argument is above $90^\circ$ and must be supplementary to have the same sine. Halve that second argument to find the launch angle.

$$
\theta_2=(180^\circ-2\cdot25^\circ)/2=65^\circ.
$$
