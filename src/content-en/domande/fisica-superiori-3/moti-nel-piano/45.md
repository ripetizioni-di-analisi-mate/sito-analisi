Two launches with the same speed and equal launch and landing elevations use $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. What is the ratio $R_{60}/R_{30}$?

- $1$.
- $\sqrt{3}$.
- $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

The factor $v_0^2/g$ is shared. The doubled angles are supplementary and have equal sines, so the ranges agree. Flight times differ because they depend on the sine of the launch angle itself, without doubling it.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R_{60}}{R_{30}}&=\frac{\sin(120^\circ)}{\sin(60^\circ)}\\[4pt]
&= 1
\end{aligned}
$$
