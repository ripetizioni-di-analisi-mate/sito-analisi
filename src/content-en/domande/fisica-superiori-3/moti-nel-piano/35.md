The graph shows the vertical velocity component of a ball in flight. The vertical axis is positive upwards. What is the vertical displacement between the marked times $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $1.2\,\mathrm{s}$?

::grafico{curve="t,6-10*t,0,1.25" punti="0.2,4,pieno;1.2,-6,pieno" segmenti="0.2,0,0.2,4;1.2,0,1.2,-6" etichette="0.16,-0.83,0.2;1.16,0.71,1.2;0.25,4.61,4 m/s;0.93,-6.65,−6 m/s" xmin=0 xmax=1.45 ymin=-8 ymax=8 nomi="t (s),vᵧ (m/s)" titolo="The graph goes from 4 m/s at 0.2 s to −6 m/s at 1.2 s."}

- $+1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+2.6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Displacement is the signed area between the line and the time axis. Because $v_y$ changes linearly, its average is $\dfrac{4-6}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The interval lasts one second, so displacement is $-1\,\mathrm{m}$. The negative contribution after the zero crossing is larger than the initial positive contribution.
