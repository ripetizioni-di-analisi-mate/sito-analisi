Which part of the parabola represents a horizontal launch rightward from height $h$ to level ground at height zero?

- The whole parabola, including below the ground.
- The part from release to first ground contact.
- Only the point of greatest speed.
- The branch to the left of release.

## Explanation

At physical times the object moves rightward from release. The equation changes at impact, so positions beyond first contact are excluded.
