A point at radius $2\,\mathrm{m}$ sweeps $3\,\mathrm{rad}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$ at a steady rate. What is its tangential speed?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2.667\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The arc is six metres long. Divide that distance by four seconds to obtain one and a half metres per second. You can also obtain $\omega=\dfrac34\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ from the angle and elapsed time. Multiplying by $2\,\mathrm{m}$ again gives $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_t&=r\frac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t}=2\frac34\\[4pt]
&\simeq 1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
