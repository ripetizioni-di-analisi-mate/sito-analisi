Why does $R=v_0^2\sin(2\theta)/g$ not directly apply when landing is below launch level?

- Horizontal velocity becomes zero.
- The launch angle must become a right angle.
- Weight reverses during motion.
- Impact time is not the time of return to launch height.

## Explanation

The formula uses $t_f=2v_{0y}/g$, which solves $y(t)=y_0$. With a different landing height, solve the vertical equation for that height.
