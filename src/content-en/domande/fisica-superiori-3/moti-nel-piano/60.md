A point rotates clockwise. If anticlockwise is positive, what sign does angular velocity have?

- Positive.
- Zero.
- It depends on the radius.
- Negative.

## Explanation

With this choice the angle decreases as the point rotates. Its rate of change with time is negative. For example, starting on the positive $x$ axis, a clockwise quarter turn gives $\Delta\theta=-\dfrac\pi2\,\mathrm{rad}$. Dividing by a positive time interval preserves that sign.
