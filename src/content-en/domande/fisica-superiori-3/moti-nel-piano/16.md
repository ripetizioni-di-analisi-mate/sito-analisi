An object has speed $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_x=-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find $v_y$ if it rises.

- $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Subtract the squared horizontal contribution from the squared speed, regardless of its sign. Two possibilities of magnitude twelve remain. Rising motion imposes the positive sign on an upward axis.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_y&=+\sqrt{13^2-(-5)^2}\\[4pt]
&= 12\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
