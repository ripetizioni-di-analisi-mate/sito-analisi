A trolley moves uphill at constant velocity. In a coordinate system with perpendicular axes and $y$ pointing down, its speed is $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_x=-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the signed displacement along $y$ over $0.50\,\mathrm s$?

- $-12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The squared components add to the square of the speed. The $y$ component has magnitude twelve metres per second. Moving uphill takes the trolley towards decreasing $y$, so take the negative root. Multiplying by half a second gives the signed displacement.

$$
\begin{aligned}v_y&=-\sqrt{13^2-(-5)^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-12\,\mathrm{m/s},\\\Delta y&=(-12\cdot0.50)\,\mathrm m=-6\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
