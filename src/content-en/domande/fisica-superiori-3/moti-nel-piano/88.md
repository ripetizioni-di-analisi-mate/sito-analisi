What can be read on a smooth part of a speed–time graph to obtain the instantaneous tangential acceleration?

- The speed squared.
- The area under the full graph.
- The radius alone.
- The slope of the tangent.

## Explanation

Tangential acceleration is the rate of change of speed. The slope of the tangent gives that rate at the instant of interest. On a graph of metres per second against seconds, the slope has units of metres per second squared. It is negative when the speed decreases.
