What information on a speed–time graph gives instantaneous tangential acceleration?

- The speed squared.
- The area under the full graph.
- The radius alone.
- The slope of the tangent.

## Explanation

Tangential acceleration is the rate of change of speed. The slope of the tangent gives that rate at the instant of interest.
