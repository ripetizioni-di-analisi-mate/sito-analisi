A point in uniform circular motion is to the left of the centre. Which way does $a_c$ point?

- Right.
- Left.
- Upwards.
- Downwards.

## Explanation

The radial acceleration points from the point towards the centre. At the left of the centre this is horizontally rightward. The tangent at that point is vertical. Either direction of travel along the tangent gives the same inward direction, since the centre is still to the right of the point.
