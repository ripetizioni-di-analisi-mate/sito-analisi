For an oblique launch, $v_0$ halves at the same angle and equal elevations. How does range change?

- It becomes one quarter.
- It halves.
- It doubles.
- It stays unchanged.

## Explanation

With angle unchanged, both flight time and horizontal speed halve. Their product gives one quarter of the range.
