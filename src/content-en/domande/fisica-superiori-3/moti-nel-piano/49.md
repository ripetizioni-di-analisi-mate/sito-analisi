For an oblique launch, $v_0$ halves at the same angle and equal elevations. How does range change?

- It becomes one quarter.
- It halves.
- It doubles.
- It stays unchanged.

## Explanation

With angle unchanged, both flight time and horizontal speed halve. Their product gives one quarter of the range. The factor $\sin(2\theta)$ stays the same. The only change in the range formula comes from the square of $v_0$, giving the factor $\left(\dfrac12\right)^2=\dfrac14$.
