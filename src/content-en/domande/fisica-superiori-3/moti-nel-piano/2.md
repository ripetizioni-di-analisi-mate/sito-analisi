An object is launched horizontally at $v_0=4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a height $h=11\,\mathrm{m}$. Air resistance is negligible and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Which value best approximates the horizontal distance it covers before reaching the ground?

- $4.77\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.74\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.09\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The fall time follows from the vertical motion with zero initial vertical velocity. The horizontal velocity stays constant during that time. At impact $9.81t^2=22$ in SI units. Take the positive time of about $1.50\,\mathrm{s}$ and multiply by $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to obtain about $6.74\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=v_0\sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 6.74\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
