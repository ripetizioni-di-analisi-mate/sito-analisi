An object is launched horizontally at $v_0=4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a height $h=11\,\mathrm m$. Ignore drag and find its horizontal distance before it reaches the ground. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $3.37\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $R=6.74\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $13.48\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10.11\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The fall time follows from the vertical motion with zero initial vertical velocity. The horizontal velocity stays constant during that time.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=v_0\sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 6.74\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
