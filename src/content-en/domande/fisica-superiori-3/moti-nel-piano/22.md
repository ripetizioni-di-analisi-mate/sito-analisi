A velocity has $v_x>0$ and $v_y<0$. Which way does it point on standard Cartesian axes?

- Leftwards and downwards.
- Rightwards and downwards.
- Rightwards and upwards.
- Leftwards and upwards.

## Explanation

The positive horizontal projection follows the axis to the right. The negative vertical one points against the upward axis. With the vector tail drawn at the origin, the projections put its tip in the fourth quadrant. Their magnitudes would determine its inclination, while the signs alone are enough to select the quadrant.
