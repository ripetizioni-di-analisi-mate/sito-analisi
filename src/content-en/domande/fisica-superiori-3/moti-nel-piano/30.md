During a horizontal launch without drag, how does $v_x$ change between two times in flight?

- It decreases by $g$ each second.
- It stays constant.
- It rises at the same rate as $\lvert v_y\rvert$.
- It vanishes before ground contact.

## Explanation

The only acceleration is gravity, directed vertically. Its $x$ component is zero, so $v_x$ remains unchanged. Gravity changes $v_y$ during flight. The total velocity turns progressively downwards and grows in magnitude because the same horizontal component combines with a vertical component of increasing magnitude.
