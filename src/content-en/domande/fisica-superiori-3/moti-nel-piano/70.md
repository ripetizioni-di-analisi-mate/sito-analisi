A point moves anticlockwise and is at the top of its circle. Which way does tangential velocity point on a diagram whose $x$-axis points right?

- Right.
- Left.
- Towards the centre.
- Vertically upwards.

## Explanation

At the highest point the tangent is horizontal. Following the anticlockwise path past the top moves the position to the left. To determine the direction, follow the circumference a short way past its highest point. At that instant the vertical component is zero and the horizontal component is negative.
