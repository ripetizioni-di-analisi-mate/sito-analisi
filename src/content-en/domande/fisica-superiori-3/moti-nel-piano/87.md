If $a_c$ and $a_t$ are nonzero and equal in magnitude, what angle does total acceleration make with the centripetal direction?

- $90^\circ$.
- $0^\circ$.
- $45^\circ$.
- $180^\circ$.

## Explanation

The component triangle has two equal legs and a right angle. Acceleration is its hypotenuse and meets each leg at forty-five degrees.
