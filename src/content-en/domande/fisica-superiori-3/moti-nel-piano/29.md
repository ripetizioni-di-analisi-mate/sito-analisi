Two objects start at the same height and time, one dropped and one launched horizontally. Compare their ground-contact times without drag.

- Equal.
- Longer for the launched object.
- Shorter for the launched object.
- They depend on the mass ratio.

## Explanation

Both have the same vertical equation because they start at the same height with zero vertical velocity. Horizontal motion does not change vertical time.
