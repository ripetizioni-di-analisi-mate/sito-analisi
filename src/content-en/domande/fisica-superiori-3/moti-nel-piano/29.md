Two objects start at the same height above a level floor at the same instant. One is dropped and the other is launched horizontally. Compare their times to the floor with no air resistance.

- Equal.
- Longer for the launched object.
- Shorter for the launched object.
- They depend on the mass ratio.

## Explanation

Both have the same vertical equation because they start at the same height with zero vertical velocity. Horizontal motion does not change vertical time. In both cases solve $0=h-\dfrac12gt^2$. The launched object reaches a point farther away horizontally at the same instant the dropped object touches the ground directly below the start.
