A stone is launched horizontally at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and reaches the ground after $0.8\,\mathrm s$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the speed just before impact.

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The vertical component grows from zero to magnitude eight. The horizontal component stays at six. They are perpendicular, so the result is the hypotenuse of their component triangle.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\sqrt{6^2+(10\cdot0.8)^2}\\[4pt]
&= 10\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
