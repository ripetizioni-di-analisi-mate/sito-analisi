A stone is launched horizontally at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and lands after $0.8\,\mathrm{s}$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. What is its speed just before impact?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The vertical component grows from zero to magnitude eight. The horizontal component stays at six. They are perpendicular, so the result is the hypotenuse of their component triangle. With upwards positive, the velocity components just before impact are $(6;-8)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The direction is downwards at an angle. Its magnitude $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\sqrt{6^2+(10\cdot0.8)^2}\\[4pt]
&= 10\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
