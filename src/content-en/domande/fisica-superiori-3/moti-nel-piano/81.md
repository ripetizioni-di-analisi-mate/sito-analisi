A point is at the top of a circle of radius $9\,\mathrm{m}$ and moves clockwise. With $x$ to the right and $y$ upwards, a sensor reads $\vec a=(-3;-4)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What are its tangential component measured along the direction of velocity and its speed?

- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the top, clockwise motion points right, so the tangential component is $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The inward direction points down and gives a centripetal magnitude of $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. From $a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, the speed is $v=\sqrt{4\cdot9}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The tangential sign shows that the point is slowing down.
