A point $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis starts from rest with constant angular acceleration $2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. What is the ratio of centripetal to tangential acceleration magnitudes after $2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $8$
- $4$
- $16$
- $2$

## Explanation

After two seconds the angular speed is $(2\cdot2)\,\mathrm{rad/s}=4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The centripetal component is $r\omega^2=8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the tangential component is $r\alpha=1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, giving ratio $8$. The same result follows by cancelling the factor $r$, which appears in both numerator and denominator.
