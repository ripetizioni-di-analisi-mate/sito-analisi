A point at radius $2\,\mathrm{m}$ has $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and tangential acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of $\vec a$.

- $2 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The centripetal part is two, just like the tangential part. As the parts are perpendicular, their combination has magnitude $\sqrt2$ times either one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\sqrt{(2^2/2)^2+2^2}\\[4pt]
&= 2 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
