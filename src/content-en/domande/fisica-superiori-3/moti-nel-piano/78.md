A point moves uniformly around a circle. How do its velocities a half-turn apart compare?

- Equal speeds with equal vectors.
- Equal speeds with opposite directions.
- Different speeds with the same direction.
- Both are zero.

## Explanation

After half a turn the point is on the opposite side. Its tangent direction rotates by $180^\circ$ and its magnitude stays fixed by the uniform motion.
