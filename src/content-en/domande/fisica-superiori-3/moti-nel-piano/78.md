A point rotates at constant nonzero speed. How does its initial velocity vector compare with its velocity vector half a revolution later?

- Equal speeds with equal vectors.
- Equal speeds with opposite directions.
- Different speeds with the same direction.
- Both are zero.

## Explanation

After half a turn the point is on the opposite side. Its tangent direction rotates by $180^\circ$ and its magnitude stays fixed by the uniform motion. For example, in anticlockwise rotation an upward velocity at the rightmost point becomes downward at the leftmost point. The vectors are parallel and point in opposite directions.
