A ball is launched obliquely with a nonzero horizontal component and returns to the launch height. How does its speed there compare with its launch speed, with no air resistance?

- It equals the initial speed.
- It is always lower.
- It is always greater.
- It equals only the initial vertical component.

## Explanation

The horizontal component is unchanged. The vertical one has equal magnitude and opposite direction because $v_y^2=v_{0y}^2$ at equal heights. Adding the squares of the two components gives the same speed. At the return the velocity vector points downwards at an angle because its vertical sign has changed.
