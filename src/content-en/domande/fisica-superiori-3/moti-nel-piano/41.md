A projectile returns to launch height. How does its speed there compare with launch speed, without drag?

- It equals the initial speed.
- It is always lower.
- It is always greater.
- It equals only the initial vertical component.

## Explanation

The horizontal component is unchanged. The vertical one has equal magnitude and opposite direction because $v_y^2=v_{0y}^2$ at equal heights.
