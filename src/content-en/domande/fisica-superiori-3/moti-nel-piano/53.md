Four equally spaced marks are fixed to a uniformly rotating rotor. A sensor detects twelve mark crossings per second. What is the angular speed of the rotor?

- $6\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $24\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The sensor sees each mark in sequence, so a complete turn gives four crossings. Divide by four to find the rotation frequency. The rotor makes three turns per second and each turn spans two pi radians. Treating twelve as the rotation frequency would give four times the required value.

$$
\omega=2\pi f=2\pi\frac{12}{4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}=6\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}.
$$
