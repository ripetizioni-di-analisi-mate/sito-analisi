A trolley follows the line $y=1-\dfrac43x$, with coordinates in metres. Over $4\,\mathrm{s}$ its horizontal coordinate decreases by $12\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of its average velocity?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The slope links the change in vertical coordinate to the change in horizontal coordinate. With $\Delta x=-12\,\mathrm{m}$, the result is $\Delta y=(-\dfrac43)(-12)\,\mathrm{m}=16\,\mathrm{m}$. Divide the components by four seconds to get $(-3;4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. They are perpendicular, so the average velocity has magnitude $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The intercept cancels in the difference.
