A velocity has components $v_x=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate its magnitude.

- $v=9.22\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4.61\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $18.44\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $13.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular. The Pythagorean theorem gives the square of the magnitude as the sum of their squares.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\sqrt{v_x^2+v_y^2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 9.22\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
