A projectile reaches a maximum height of $17\,\mathrm m$ above the ground. Later, while descending, it passes a window at height $12\,\mathrm m$. With upward positive, $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no air resistance, what is the vertical velocity component at the window?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-17\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The vertical component is zero at the apex. From the apex to the window the ball drops five metres. Its vertical motion can therefore be treated as a fall from rest over that height difference, independently of its horizontal speed. The root gives the magnitude, and descent fixes the negative sign.

$$
v_y=-\sqrt{2\cdot10(17-12)}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-10\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
