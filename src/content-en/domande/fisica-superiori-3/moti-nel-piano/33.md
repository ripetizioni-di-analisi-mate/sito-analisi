A ball is launched horizontally at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, when is its velocity angled $45^\circ$ below horizontal, if still in flight?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

At forty-five degrees the two components have equal magnitudes. The vertical magnitude is $gt$ and the horizontal magnitude stays at five. Solve $10t=5$ in SI units to get $t=0.5\,\mathrm{s}$. At that instant one component is positive and the other negative, so the direction is below the horizontal.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\frac{v_0}{g}=\frac5{10}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
