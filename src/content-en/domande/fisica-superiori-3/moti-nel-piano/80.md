What is the magnitude of the velocity change between opposite points of uniform circular motion at speed $v$?

- It is $v$.
- It is zero.
- It is $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$.
- It is $2v$.

## Explanation

The two arrows point oppositely. Subtracting them adds two vectors with equal magnitude and direction, giving twice $v$. If the first velocity is $\vec v_1$, the second is $-\vec v_1$. Subtracting the first from the second gives $-2\vec v_1$, with magnitude $2v$.
