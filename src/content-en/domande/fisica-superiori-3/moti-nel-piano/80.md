What is the magnitude of the velocity change between opposite points of uniform circular motion at speed $v$?

- It is $v$.
- It is zero.
- It is $v^2/r$.
- It is $2v$.

## Explanation

The two arrows point oppositely. Subtracting them adds two vectors with equal magnitude and direction, giving twice $v$.
