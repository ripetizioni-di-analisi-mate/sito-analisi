A vertical launch with initial component $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ reaches its top in $1.5\,\mathrm s$. What is the acceleration magnitude?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

In this time the velocity drops from twelve to zero. Acceleration is negative with the axis upwards and has magnitude equal to the full drop divided by duration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
g&=\frac{12}{1.5}\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
