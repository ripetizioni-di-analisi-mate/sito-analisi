An object is launched vertically at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and reaches the apex in $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$. If acceleration is constant, what is the magnitude of its vertical component?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

In this time the velocity drops from twelve to zero. Acceleration is negative with the axis upwards and has magnitude equal to the full drop divided by duration. The calculation gives $a_y=\dfrac{0-12}{1.5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The question asks for the magnitude, so the result is $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
g&=\frac{12}{1.5}\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
