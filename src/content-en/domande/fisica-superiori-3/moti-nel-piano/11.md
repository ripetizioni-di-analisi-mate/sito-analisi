In an oblique launch, a ball passes a reference height at $t=0.4\,\mathrm{s}$ and again at $t=1.2\,\mathrm{s}$. Air resistance is negligible and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How far above the reference height is its apex?

- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two passages through the same height are symmetric about the apex, so the highest point is reached at $0.8\,\mathrm{s}$. The ball takes $0.4\,\mathrm{s}$ to go from the apex to the second passage, starting with zero vertical velocity. It drops by $\dfrac12g(0.4\,\mathrm{s})^2=0.8\,\mathrm{m}$, the requested difference in height.
