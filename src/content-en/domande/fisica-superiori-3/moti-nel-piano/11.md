A projectile has initial vertical velocity $v_{0y}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Ignore drag and calculate the maximum rise above launch. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $2.55\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $H=5.1\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7.65\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The vertical component vanishes at the highest point. With the vertical axis pointing up, use $0=v_{0y}^2-2gH$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
H&=\frac{v_{0y}^2}{2g}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 5.1\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
