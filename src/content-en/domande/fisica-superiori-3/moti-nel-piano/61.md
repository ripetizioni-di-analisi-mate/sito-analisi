In $3\,\mathrm{s}$, how many revolutions does a rotor complete while maintaining $\omega=6\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$?

- $9$.
- $3$.
- $6$.
- $18$.

## Explanation

The total angle is $18\pi$ radians. Divide by the radians per turn to obtain the dimensionless number of turns. The result is $\dfrac{18\pi}{2\pi}=9$. Equivalently the rotor makes three turns each second, since its frequency is $\dfrac{6\pi}{2\pi}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Three seconds triple the count.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=\frac{\omega t}{2\pi}\\[4pt]
&= 9
\end{aligned}
$$
