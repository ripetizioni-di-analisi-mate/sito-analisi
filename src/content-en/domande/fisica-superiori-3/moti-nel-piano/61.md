How many turns does a rotor at fixed $\omega=6\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ complete in $3\,\mathrm s$?

- $9$.
- $3$.
- $6$.
- $18$.

## Explanation

The total angle is $18\pi$ radians. Divide by the radians per turn to obtain the dimensionless number of turns.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=\frac{\omega t}{2\pi}\\[4pt]
&= 9
\end{aligned}
$$
