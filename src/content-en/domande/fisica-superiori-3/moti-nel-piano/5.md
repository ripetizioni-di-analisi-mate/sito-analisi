A point completes one revolution every $T=1.6\,\mathrm s$. Calculate its angular speed in uniform circular motion.

- $\omega=3.93\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $1.965\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $7.86\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $5.895\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

## Explanation

One revolution is $2\pi$ radians. Its duration is the period, so their ratio gives the angular speed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega&=\frac{2\pi}{T}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.93\,\mathrm{rad/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
