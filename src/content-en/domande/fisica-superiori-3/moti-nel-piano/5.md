A point completes one revolution every $T=1.6\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate its angular speed in uniform circular motion. Choose the closest value.

- $3.93\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.625\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.96\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $10.05\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

One revolution is $2\pi$ radians. Its duration is the period, so their ratio gives the angular speed. The ratio $\dfrac{2\pi}{1.6}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is about $3.93\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The number $0.625$ is the frequency in hertz. Each revolution counted in that frequency corresponds to the angle of a complete turn.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega&=\frac{2\pi}{T}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.93\,\mathrm{rad/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
