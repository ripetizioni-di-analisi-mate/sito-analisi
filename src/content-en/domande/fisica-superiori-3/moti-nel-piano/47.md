For fixed $v_0$ and equal elevations, which angle maximises range without drag?

- $30^\circ$.
- $60^\circ$.
- $45^\circ$.
- $90^\circ$.

## Explanation

Range varies with $\sin(2\theta)$. The maximum occurs when the doubled angle is ninety degrees, giving a launch angle of forty-five degrees.
