A wheel rotates at angular speed $\omega=6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find the speed of a point at distance $r=0.28\,\mathrm m$ from the axis.

- $0.84\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v=1.68\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $3.36\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2.52\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Every point sweeps through the same angle in the same time. Arc length is radius times angle, so tangential speed is $\omega r$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\omega r\\[4pt]
&= 1.68\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
