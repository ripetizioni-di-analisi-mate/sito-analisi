Two disks on fixed, parallel axes touch at their rims and turn without slipping at the contact. The first has radius $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and the second radius $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$. The first has angular speed $6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the angular speed of the second?

- $2.25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

With no slipping, both rims have the same tangential speed at the contact. The first disk gives $(0.40\cdot6)\,\mathrm{m/s}=2.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Divide by the smaller radius for the second disk, $\omega_2=\dfrac{2.4}{0.15}\,\mathrm{rad/s}=16\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The smaller disk has to sweep a larger angle in the same time.
