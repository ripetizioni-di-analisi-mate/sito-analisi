At the top of an oblique launch with $v_{0x}\ne0$, how are velocity and acceleration directed?

- Both horizontal.
- Velocity horizontal and acceleration vertically downwards.
- Zero velocity and zero acceleration.
- Velocity downwards and acceleration horizontal.

## Explanation

The vertical velocity changes sign and is zero at the top. The horizontal component still has its initial value and gravity stays vertical.
