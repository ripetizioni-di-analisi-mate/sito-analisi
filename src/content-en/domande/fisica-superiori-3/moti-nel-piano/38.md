At the apex of an oblique launch without air resistance and with $v_{0x}\ne0$, which way do velocity and acceleration point?

- Both horizontal.
- Velocity horizontal and acceleration vertically downwards.
- Zero velocity and zero acceleration.
- Velocity downwards and acceleration horizontal.

## Explanation

The vertical velocity changes sign and is zero at the top. The horizontal component still has its initial value and gravity stays vertical. The speed there equals the magnitude of $v_{0x}$ and is positive. The vertical acceleration then builds up a downward component of velocity.
