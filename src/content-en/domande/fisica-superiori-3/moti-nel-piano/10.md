A ball is launched horizontally from the vertical line $x=0$. Sensors record it at height $12\,\mathrm{m}$ when $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and at height $8\,\mathrm{m}$ when $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Gravity is uniform and air resistance is negligible. At what horizontal coordinate does it meet the floor at height zero?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For a horizontal launch the trajectory has form $y=h-kx^2$. Between the two readings the height drops by $4\,\mathrm{m}$, while $x^2$ increases by $32\,\mathrm{m^2}$. This gives $k=\dfrac18\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and then $h=12.5\,\mathrm{m}$. Height reaches zero at $x^2=100\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The positive root lies beyond both sensors.
