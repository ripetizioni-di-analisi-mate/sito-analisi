A ball is launched horizontally from a point on the vertical line $x=0$. During its flight it is at height $12\,\mathrm{m}$ when $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and at height $8\,\mathrm{m}$ when $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Gravity is uniform and air resistance is negligible. At what horizontal coordinate does the ball reach the floor, at height zero?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For a horizontal launch the trajectory has equation $y=h-kx^2$, where $h$ is the launch height. Between the two points the height drops by $4\,\mathrm{m}$, while $x^2$ increases by $32\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so $k=\dfrac{4}{32}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=\dfrac18\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The first point then gives $h=\left(12+\dfrac18\cdot4\right)\mathrm{m}=12.5\,\mathrm{m}$. The height is zero for $x^2=\dfrac{h}{k}=100\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the ball reaches the floor at $x=10\,\mathrm{m}$.
