At an interior time in circular motion, the speed is differentiable and reaches a positive local maximum. Which acceleration component vanishes at that instant?

- The tangential component.
- The centripetal component.
- Both.
- Neither, because speed is positive.

## Explanation

At a smooth maximum the speed has zero slope, so tangential acceleration is zero. Speed remains positive, so $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ still has a positive magnitude. Speed is momentarily stationary at the maximum, while the direction of velocity continues to change. The acceleration vector therefore points towards the centre.
