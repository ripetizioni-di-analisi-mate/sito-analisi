The speed of a point on a circle has a smooth maximum. Which component vanishes at that instant if the maximum is positive?

- The tangential component.
- The centripetal component.
- Both.
- Neither, because speed is positive.

## Explanation

At a smooth maximum the speed has zero slope, so tangential acceleration is zero. Speed remains positive, so $v^2/r$ still has a positive magnitude.
