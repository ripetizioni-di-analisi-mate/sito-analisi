A drone moves $24\,\mathrm m$ east and $-18\,\mathrm m$ along the north-pointing axis in $6\,\mathrm s$. What is the magnitude of its mean velocity?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,24,-18,s" segmenti="0,0,24,0;24,0,24,-18" xmin=-2 xmax=26 ymin=-20 ymax=2 uguale=1 titolo="Displacement forms a right triangle with its components"}

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\frac{5}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Mean velocity is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ south. Divide the two signed displacements by the same elapsed time. The directions are perpendicular, so add their squares.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_m&=\sqrt{(24/6)^2+(-18/6)^2}\\[4pt]
&= 5\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
