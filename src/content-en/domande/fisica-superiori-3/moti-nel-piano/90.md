In circular motion at fixed radius, instantaneous speed doubles but its rate of change stays the same. Which acceleration component is multiplied by four?

- The tangential component.
- The centripetal component.
- Both.
- Neither.

## Explanation

The centripetal part varies as $v^2$, while the tangential part is the stated unchanged rate of change.
