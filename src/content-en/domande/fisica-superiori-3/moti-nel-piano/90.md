In circular motion at fixed radius, instantaneous speed doubles but its rate of change stays the same. Which acceleration component is multiplied by four?

- The tangential component.
- The centripetal component.
- Both.
- Neither.

## Explanation

The centripetal part varies as $v^2$, while the tangential part is the stated unchanged rate of change. At the same radius the change is from $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ to $\dfrac{(2v)^2}{r}=4\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. The tangential part depends on the slope of the speed–time graph, which remains the same here.
