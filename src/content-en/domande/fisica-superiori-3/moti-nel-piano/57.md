Two points at different nonzero radii lie on the same rigid disk in uniform rotation. Which quantity has the same value for both?

- Their rotation period.
- Their arc length in one turn.
- Their tangential speed.
- Their distance from the centre.

## Explanation

The disc rotates as one body, so each radius turns through the same angle in the same time. One turn has the same duration for both. The outer point travels a longer arc because distance at a given angle is proportional to radius. The distance per revolution and tangential speed can therefore differ.
