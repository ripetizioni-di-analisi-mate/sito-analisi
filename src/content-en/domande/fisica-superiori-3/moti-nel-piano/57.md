Two points at different nonzero radii lie on the same rotating rigid disc. What quantity is equal for both if they make full turns?

- Their rotation period.
- Their arc length in one turn.
- Their tangential speed.
- Their distance from the centre.

## Explanation

The disc rotates as one body, so each radius turns through the same angle in the same time. One turn has the same duration for both.
