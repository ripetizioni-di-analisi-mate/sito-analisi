What angle in radians equals $135^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

## Explanation

A straight angle is $180^\circ=\pi$ radians. Since $135/180=3/4$, the angle is three quarters of $\pi$.
