What angle in radians equals $135^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$.

## Explanation

A straight angle is $180^\circ=\pi$ radians. Since $\dfrac{135}{180}=\dfrac34$, the angle is three quarters of $\pi$. Equivalently, $135^\circ$ is a right angle plus half a right angle. Add $\dfrac\pi2$ and $\dfrac\pi4$ in radians to obtain $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.
