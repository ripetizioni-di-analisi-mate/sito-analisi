A point moves anticlockwise. Its radius makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis. Its centripetal component and its forward tangential component are both $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Where does the total acceleration point?

- Down along $y$.
- Left along $x$.
- Up along $y$.
- Right along $x$.

## Explanation

The centripetal direction points left and down. The positive tangent points left and up. With the radius at $45^\circ$ and equal component magnitudes, the two vertical projections are opposite and cancel. Both horizontal projections are negative, so the resultant points left along $x$.
