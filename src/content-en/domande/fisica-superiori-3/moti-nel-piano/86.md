Two points on the same curve have the same $a_c=4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and tangential components $+3$ and $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the ratio of their total acceleration magnitudes?

- $-1$.
- $1$.
- $7$.
- $\frac{4}{3}$.

## Explanation

Reversing the tangential sign reverses that direction, but its square in the magnitude is unchanged. Total acceleration magnitudes agree while their orientations relative to the tangents differ.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{a_1}{a_2}&=\frac{\sqrt{4^2+3^2}}{\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}}\\[4pt]
&= 1
\end{aligned}
$$
