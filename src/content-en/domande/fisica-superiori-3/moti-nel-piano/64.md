A point $0.40\,\mathrm m$ from the centre of a disk has constant angular speed $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the magnitude of its mean velocity over a quarter turn?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\frac{4}{\pi}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\frac{2\sqrt2}{\pi}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\frac{4\sqrt2}{\pi}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The ends of the arc and the centre form a right isosceles triangle. Displacement is its hypotenuse, the chord, while the arc is the travelled path. Divide half pi by the angular speed for the duration. The chord divided by that time gives the requested magnitude.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve=".4*cos(t),.4*sin(t),0,1.570796327" linee="0,.4 0,0 .4,0" segmenti="0,.4,.4,0" punti="0,0,pieno;.4,0,pieno;0,.4,pieno" etichette="-.036,-.032,O;.427,-.031,A;-.03,.428,B;.16,-.037,r" xmin=-.07 xmax=.50 ymin=-.07 ymax=.50 titolo="The chord AB gives the displacement. The arc measures the path length."}

$$
v_m=\frac{0.40\sqrt2}{(\pi/2)/5}\,\mathrm{m/s}=\frac{4\sqrt2}{\pi}\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
