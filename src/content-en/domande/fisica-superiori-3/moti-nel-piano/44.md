A $45^\circ$ launch starts and lands at the same height. With $v_0=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the range?

::grafico{f="x-0.1*x^2" xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=-0.3 ymax=3.1 punti="0,0,pieno;5,2.5,pieno;10,0,pieno" titolo="The vertex halfway along the range when launch and landing heights agree"}

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The initial components are equal and positive. Ascent and descent take the same time because the heights match. In the range expression $\sin(90^\circ)=1$, and speed squared divided by $g$ gives a length.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=\frac{v_0^2\sin(2\theta)}g=\frac{10^2\cdot1}{10}\\[4pt]
&= 10\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
