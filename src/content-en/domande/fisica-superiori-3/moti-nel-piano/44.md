A ball leaves height zero with $v_{0x}=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_{0y}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A thin bar crosses its plane of motion at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. How far does the ball pass below the bar?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate fixes the time when the ball reaches the plane of the bar. The elapsed time is $\dfrac48\,\mathrm{s}=0.5\,\mathrm{s}$, so its height is $(6\cdot0.5-5\cdot0.5^2)\,\mathrm{m}=1.75\,\mathrm{m}$. The question asks for the gap below the bar. Subtract this height from $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to obtain $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$.
