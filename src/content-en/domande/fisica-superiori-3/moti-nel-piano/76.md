For a rigid disc at fixed $\omega$, how does $a_c$ compare at double the radius?

- It halves.
- It quadruples.
- It remains unchanged.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

At fixed $\omega$, $a_c=\omega^2r$. Speed increases with radius, so $v$ cannot be treated as constant here. Substituting $2r$ in place of $r$ gives $2\omega^2r$. Using the speed form, the numerator would quadruple and the denominator would double, giving the same total effect.
