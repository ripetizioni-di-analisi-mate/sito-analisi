Which trajectory do $x=2t$ and $y=3t^2$ give for $t\geq0$?

- $y=3x^2$.
- $y=\dfrac34x^2$ with $x\geq0$.
- $y=\dfrac32x$.
- $y=\dfrac34x^2$ for all real $x$.

## Explanation

Solve the first equation for $t=\dfrac x2$. Substitute into the second and square that ratio. The domain $t\geq0$ also requires $x\geq0$. At $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$, for example, the point is $(2;3)$ in metres. The curve also gives height three at horizontal coordinate two. Its left branch would require negative times.
