What is the magnitude of the tangential velocity of a point on the fixed axis of a rotating rigid disk?

- The same as for points at the rim.
- Half the value at the rim.
- Greater than the value at the rim.
- Zero.

## Explanation

On the axis $r=0$. The point traces no arc around it, so $r\omega$ is zero. The disk continues rotating while a point exactly on the axis stays fixed. Nearby points have speeds proportional to their distances from the axis.
