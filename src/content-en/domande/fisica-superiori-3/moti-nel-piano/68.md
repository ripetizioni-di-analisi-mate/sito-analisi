What is the tangential speed of a point on the axis of a fixed rigid disc?

- It equals the period.
- It equals $\omega$ expressed in $\mathrm{m/s}$.
- The same as for points at the rim.
- Zero.

## Explanation

On the axis $r=0$. The point traces no arc around it, so $r\omega$ is zero.
