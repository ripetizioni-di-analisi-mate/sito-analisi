Launch speed doubles for a horizontal throw from the same height. How does vertical drop change at a fixed horizontal distance, before both impacts?

- It halves.
- It doubles.
- It is unchanged.
- It becomes one quarter.

## Explanation

At a fixed distance, doubling speed halves the time until the projectile is above that point. Vertical drop then depends on the square of that elapsed time.
