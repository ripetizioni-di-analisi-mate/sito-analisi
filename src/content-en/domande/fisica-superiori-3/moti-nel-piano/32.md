Launch speed doubles for a horizontal throw from the same height. How does vertical drop change at a fixed horizontal distance, before both impacts?

- It halves.
- It doubles.
- It is unchanged.
- It becomes one quarter.

## Explanation

At a fixed distance, doubling speed halves the time until the projectile is above that point. Vertical drop then depends on the square of that elapsed time. The second drop is therefore $\left(\dfrac12\right)^2=\dfrac14$ of the first. This compares equal horizontal positions. At the same time, the two balls would have dropped by the same distance.
