A wheel rotates at angular speed $\omega=5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find the speed of a point at distance $r=0.26\,\mathrm m$ from the axis.

- $0.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $1.95\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Every point sweeps through the same angle in the same time. Arc length is radius times angle, so tangential speed is $\omega r$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\omega r\\[4pt]
&= 1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
