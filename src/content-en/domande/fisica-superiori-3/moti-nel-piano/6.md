A wheel turns at angular speed $\omega=5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the speed of a point at distance $r=0.26\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis.

- $0.052\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $19.23\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Every point sweeps through the same angle in the same time. Arc length is radius times angle, so tangential speed is $\omega r$. In one second the point sweeps $5\,\mathrm{rad}$ at radius $0.26\,\mathrm{m}$. The arc length is $1.3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the speed is $1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The radius converts the angular change into a distance along the rim.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\omega r\\[4pt]
&= 1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
