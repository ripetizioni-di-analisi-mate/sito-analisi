A projectile's initial horizontal and vertical components are $6$ and $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, respectively. It returns to launch height after $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Ignoring air resistance, find the range.

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Flight time is already given. The vertical component determines that duration, but only the horizontal component enters the product for horizontal distance. The calculation is $R=(6\cdot2)\,\mathrm{m}=12\,\mathrm{m}$. Using the initial vertical component in that product would be inappropriate because that component changes during the flight.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=v_{0x}t_f=6\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= 12\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
