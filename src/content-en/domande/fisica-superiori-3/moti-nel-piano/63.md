Two points have the same constant speed on circles of radii $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. In a given interval, the point on the first circle sweeps $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$. What angle does the other point sweep over the same interval?

- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

With equal speeds over the same interval, the points travel arcs of equal length. The first covers $0.40\pi\,\mathrm{m}$. Divide the length by the second radius, $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, to obtain $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$. The smaller radius requires a larger angle to cover the same arc length.
