A velocity has components $v_x=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which value best approximates the magnitude of the velocity?

- $7.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.52\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $61\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular. The Pythagorean theorem gives the square of the magnitude as the sum of their squares. The squares are $25$ and $36$ in the same units, so the magnitude is $\sqrt{61}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq7.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The result exceeds either component by itself and is smaller than their sum.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\sqrt{v_x^2+v_y^2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 7.81\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
