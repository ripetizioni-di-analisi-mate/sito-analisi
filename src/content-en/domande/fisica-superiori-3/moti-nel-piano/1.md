A velocity has components $v_x=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate its magnitude.

- $v=7.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $3.905\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $15.62\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $11.71\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular. The Pythagorean theorem gives the square of the magnitude as the sum of their squares.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\sqrt{v_x^2+v_y^2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 7.81\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
