A skater moves uniformly through a quarter-circle, anticlockwise from the rightmost to the highest point. With axes pointing right and up, what are the signs of the mean velocity components over the arc?

- Positive $x$ and positive $y$
- Negative $x$ and positive $y$
- Zero $x$ and positive $y$
- Negative $x$ and zero $y$

## Explanation

Mean velocity points along the displacement between the endpoints. The coordinates change from $(R,0)$ to $(0,R)$, giving displacement $(-R,R)$. Dividing by positive time preserves both signs.
