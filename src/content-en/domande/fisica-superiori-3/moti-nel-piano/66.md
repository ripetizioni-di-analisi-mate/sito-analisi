At fixed $\omega$, what does halving a rotor point's radius do to its tangential speed?

- It doubles it.
- It halves it.
- It reduces it to a quarter.
- It leaves it unchanged.

## Explanation

The equation $v_t=r\omega$ is linear in radius. The same angle is swept each second, but along an arc half as long.
