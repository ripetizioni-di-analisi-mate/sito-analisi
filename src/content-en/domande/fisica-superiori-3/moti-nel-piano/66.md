At fixed $\omega$, what does halving a rotor point's radius do to its tangential speed?

- It doubles it.
- It halves it.
- It reduces it to a quarter.
- It leaves it unchanged.

## Explanation

The equation $v_t=r\omega$ is linear in radius. The same angle is swept each second, but along an arc half as long. Here the number of revolutions per second stays constant. In one revolution the point at half the radius travels half the circumference in the same period.
