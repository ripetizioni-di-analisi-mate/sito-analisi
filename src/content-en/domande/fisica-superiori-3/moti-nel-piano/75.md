Two bends have equal radius. The second is taken at three times the speed. How does centripetal acceleration change?

- It triples.
- It becomes one ninth.
- It increases by a factor of nine.
- It remains unchanged.

## Explanation

At fixed radius $a_c$ varies as $v^2$. The factor of three occurs in both speed factors. Indeed, $\dfrac{(3v)^2}{r}=9\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. On both curves the radial component points towards the centre. What changes is its magnitude.
