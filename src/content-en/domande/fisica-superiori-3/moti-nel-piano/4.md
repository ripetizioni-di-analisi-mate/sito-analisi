An object is launched at $45^\circ$ with initial speed $v_0=13\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It lands at the launch height. Ignore drag and calculate the range. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Choose the closest value.

- $8.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $34.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $17.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The launch and landing points are at the same height, so use the level-ground range formula. At $2\theta=90^\circ$, the sine is one. The numerator is $169\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Division by $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ gives about $17.2\,\mathrm{m}$. The initial components are equal and the flight time includes both the rise and the fall.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=\frac{v_0^2\sin(2\theta)}g\\[4pt]
&\simeq 17.2\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
