An object is launched at $45^\circ$ with initial speed $v_0=13\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It lands at the launch height. Ignore drag and calculate the range. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $8.6\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $34.4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $25.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $R=17.2\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The launch and landing points are at the same height, so use the level-ground range formula. At $2\theta=90^\circ$, the sine is one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=\frac{v_0^2\sin(2\theta)}g\\[4pt]
&\simeq 17.2\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
