A projectile has initial vertical velocity $v_{0y}=9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Ignore drag and calculate the maximum rise above launch. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Choose the closest value.

- $0.92\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.26\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.06\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The vertical component vanishes at the highest point. With the vertical axis pointing up, use $0=v_{0y}^2-2gH$. The initial component squared is $81\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Divide by $2g=19.62\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to obtain $H\simeq4.13\,\mathrm{m}$. The height is measured from launch and depends only on the vertical component.

$$
\begin{aligned}
H&=\frac{v_{0y}^2}{2g}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 4.13\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
