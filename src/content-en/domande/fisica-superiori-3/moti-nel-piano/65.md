A rigid wheel rotates uniformly. For points $0.20\,\mathrm m$ and $0.50\,\mathrm m$ from the axis, what is the second tangential speed divided by the first?

- $2.5$.
- $0.4$.
- $1$.
- $5$.

## Explanation

The points share angular speed. The outer point travels a longer arc in the same interval, in proportion to its radius. The ratio is therefore two and a half.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{v_2}{v_1}&=\frac{\omega r_2}{\omega r_1}=\frac{0.50}{0.20}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 2.5
\end{aligned}
$$
