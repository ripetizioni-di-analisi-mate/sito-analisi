A point sweeps a quarter revolution at constant rate in $0.5\,\mathrm s$. What is $\omega$?

::grafico{curve="2*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,1.5707963268" segmenti="0,0,2,0;0,0,0,2" xmin=-0.5 xmax=2.6 ymin=-0.5 ymax=2.6 uguale=1 titolo="The arc spans one quarter of the circle"}

- $\frac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $2 \pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $\frac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

## Explanation

A quarter revolution is $\pi/2$ radians. The given duration applies to that quarter only, so divide its angle by half a second. At the same rate a full turn would take two seconds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega&=\frac{(2\pi)/4}{0.5}\\[4pt]
&= \pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
