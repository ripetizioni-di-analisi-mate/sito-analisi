A mark moves anticlockwise through a quarter turn in $0.50\,\mathrm s$, then clockwise through half a turn in the next $0.50\,\mathrm s$. Taking anticlockwise as positive, what is the signed mean angular velocity over the entire interval?

- $\frac{3\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-\frac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\frac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-2\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The anticlockwise quarter turn counts positively. The clockwise half turn is a negative angular displacement with greater magnitude. Add the signed displacements and divide by the full second. Adding the magnitudes would measure the total angle travelled instead.

$$
\bar\omega=\frac{\pi/2-\pi}{0.50+0.50}\,\mathrm{rad/s}=-\frac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}.
$$
