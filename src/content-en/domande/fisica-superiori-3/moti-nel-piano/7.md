An object follows a circle of radius $r=2.6\,\mathrm m$ at constant speed $v=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its centripetal acceleration magnitude.

- $2.355\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $9.42\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $a_c=4.71\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $7.065\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the direction of velocity changes. The magnitude of the associated acceleration is $v^2/r$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_c&=\frac{v^2}r\\[4pt]
&\simeq 4.71\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
