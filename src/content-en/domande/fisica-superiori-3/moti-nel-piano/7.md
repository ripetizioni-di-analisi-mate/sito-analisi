An object travels round a circle of radius $r=2.6\,\mathrm{m}$ at constant speed $v=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the magnitude of its centripetal acceleration. Choose the closest value.

- $1.35\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $31.85\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.71\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.21\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the direction of velocity changes. The magnitude of the associated acceleration is $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. The speed squared is $12.25\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Dividing by $2.6\,\mathrm{m}$ gives about $4.71\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Dividing the speed itself by the radius would give angular speed instead.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_c&=\frac{v^2}r\\[4pt]
&\simeq 4.71\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
