A projectile returns to its launch height with initial components $v_{0x}=0$ and $v_{0y}>0$. What is the horizontal range?

- It equals maximum height.
- It is $\dfrac{2v_{0y}}{g}$ metres.
- It is infinite.
- It is zero.

## Explanation

Horizontal velocity stays zero because there is no acceleration along that axis. The body can rise and fall while remaining above the same horizontal coordinate. Throughout the flight $x(t)=x(0)$. On returning to the initial height, the product of flight time and horizontal velocity is still zero.
