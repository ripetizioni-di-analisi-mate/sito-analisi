A projectile returns to its launch height with initial components $v_{0x}=0$ and $v_{0y}>0$. What is the horizontal range?

- It equals maximum height.
- It is $2v_{0y}/g$ metres.
- It is infinite.
- It is zero.

## Explanation

Horizontal velocity stays zero because there is no acceleration along that axis. The body can rise and fall while remaining above the same horizontal coordinate.
