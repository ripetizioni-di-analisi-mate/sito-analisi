A ball starts at $x=0$ and height $15\,\mathrm{m}$ with initial velocity components $v_{0x}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_{0y}=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. What is its horizontal coordinate when it reaches height $11\,\mathrm{m}$ on the way down?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Set the height equal to the required value to obtain $15+8t-5t^2=11$, or $(t-2)(5t+2)=0$ in SI units. The only time after launch is $2\,\mathrm{s}$. The vertical velocity is then $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the ball is descending. With constant horizontal velocity the coordinate is $x=(6\cdot2)\,\mathrm{m}=12\,\mathrm{m}$.
