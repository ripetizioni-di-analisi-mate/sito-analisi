A motion has $v_x=0$ at all times and varying $v_y$. What is the shape of its trajectory?

- Part of a horizontal line.
- A circle.
- Necessarily a parabola.
- Part of a vertical line.

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate stays fixed because its velocity is always zero. Only height changes, so all points share the same $x$.
