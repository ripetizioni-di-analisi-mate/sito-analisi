Two objects have the same launch speed, at angles of $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ above the horizontal. Each lands at its own launch height, with negligible air resistance. Which stays in the air longer?

- The $30^\circ$ launch.
- Their flight times are equal.
- The $60^\circ$ launch.
- It depends on mass.

## Explanation

Time is proportional to vertical component $v_0\sin\theta$. At sixty degrees the sine is larger, so ascent and return take longer. At the same $v_0$, the time ratio is $\dfrac{\sin60^\circ}{\sin30^\circ}=\sqrt3$. The ranges agree because the longer flight comes with a smaller horizontal velocity component.
