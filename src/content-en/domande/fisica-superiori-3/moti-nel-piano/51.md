For complementary $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ launches with equal speed and elevations, which stays in the air longer?

- The $30^\circ$ launch.
- Their flight times are equal.
- The $60^\circ$ launch.
- It depends on mass.

## Explanation

Time is proportional to vertical component $v_0\sin\theta$. At sixty degrees the sine is larger, so ascent and return take longer.
