Ideal water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ travels steadily through a horizontal pipe. The speeds are $v_1=1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2=3.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with $v_2>v_1$. Find $p_1-p_2$.

- $12480\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3120\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-6240\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6.24\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

At equal height gravitational terms cancel. The increase in kinetic energy per volume is the pressure drop.

$$
\Delta p=\frac12\rho(v_2^2-v_1^2),\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta p&=\frac{1000 \cdot \left(- \left(\frac{7}{5}\right)^{2} + \left(\frac{19}{5}\right)^{2}\right)}{2}\\[4pt]&=6.24\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
