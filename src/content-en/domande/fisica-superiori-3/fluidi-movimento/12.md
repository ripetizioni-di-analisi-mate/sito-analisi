Water flows steadily through a real horizontal Venturi with no pumps. Pressure readings are $132\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the inlet, $116\,\mathrm{kPa}$ in the throat and $128\,\mathrm{kPa}$ after the expansion. The inlet and expanded sections have equal areas and identical velocity profiles. What is the total mechanical energy loss per unit volume, expressed in kilopascals?

- $16\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The inlet and outlet are at the same height, and the velocity profile has returned to its initial shape. The remaining pressure difference therefore gives the mechanical energy loss per unit volume. Pressure falls by $16\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from inlet to throat, and $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$ of that pressure is recovered beyond the throat.

$$
p_1-p_3=(132-128)\,\mathrm{kPa}=4\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
