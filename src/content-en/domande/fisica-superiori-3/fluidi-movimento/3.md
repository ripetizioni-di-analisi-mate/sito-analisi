An incompressible fluid travels steadily with no leaks. In the first section $A_1=0.0046\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $v_1=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the second section $A_2=0.0023\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find $v_2$.

- $6.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The rate is the same in both sections. Find it upstream as area times speed and divide it by the downstream area.

$$
v_2=A_1v_1/A_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=23 \cdot \frac{1}{5000} \cdot 8 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{1}{23 \cdot \frac{1}{10000}}\\[4pt]&=3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
