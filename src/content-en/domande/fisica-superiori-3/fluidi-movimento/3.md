An incompressible fluid flows steadily through a pipe with no leaks or branches. In the first section $A_1=0.0046\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $v_1=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the second section $A_2=0.0023\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is $v_2$?

- $6.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Because the fluid is incompressible and the pipe has no branches, the same volume crosses both sections each second. The downstream area is half the upstream one, so the speed must double. Multiplying the downstream area by its speed recovers the upstream flow rate.

$$
v_2=\frac{A_1}{A_2}v_1=\frac{0.0046}{0.0023}\cdot1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}=3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
