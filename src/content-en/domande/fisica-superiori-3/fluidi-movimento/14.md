Water flows steadily along a streamline, with no pumps and negligible friction. At point 1, $p_1=110\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $v_1=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At point 2, $p_2=119\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $v_2=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to find the signed height change $y_2-y_1$.

- $-0.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-1.6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The kinetic term increases by $16\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the pressure increases by $9\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The sum stays constant only if the height term drops by $25\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Under the given conditions each metre of water corresponds to $10\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The negative sign means that point 2 lies lower.

$$
y_2-y_1=\frac{-(9+16)\,\mathrm{kPa}}{10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}}=-2.5\,\mathrm m.
$$
