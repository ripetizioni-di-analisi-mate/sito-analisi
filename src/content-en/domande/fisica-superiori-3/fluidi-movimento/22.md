A tank holds $15\,\mathrm L$ and receives $3\,\mathrm{L/s}$ while losing $1\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Find its volume after $8\,\mathrm s$.

- $62\,\mathrm L$
- $31\,\mathrm L$
- $15.5\,\mathrm L$
- $-31\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Retain the initial volume in the balance. Net flow keeps two litres each second, adding to the initial amount.

$$
V_f=V_i+(Q_{\mathrm{in}}-Q_{\mathrm{out}})t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}V_f&=15 + \left(3 - 1\right) \cdot 8\\[4pt]&=31\,\mathrm L.\end{aligned}
$$
