A rigid tank has a constant horizontal area of $0.20\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Inflow is $6\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and a single outlet drains at a constant rate. Over the first $5\,\mathrm s$ the level rises by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many litres per second leave the outlet?

- $2\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The rise measures the volume that stayed in the tank. Multiplying by area gives ten litres stored in five seconds, a net gain of two litres per second. Six litres enter each second. The outflow is the part of the inflow that is not retained.

$$
\begin{aligned}\Delta V&=(0.20\cdot0.05)\,\mathrm{m^3}=10\,\mathrm L,\\ Q_{\mathrm{out}}&=(6-10/5)\,\mathrm{L/s}=4\,\mathrm{L/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
