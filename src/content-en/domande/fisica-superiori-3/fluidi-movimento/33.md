At fixed geometry and density, the Venturi inlet speed doubles. What factor multiplies the pressure drop?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $-4$

## Explanation

For a fixed area ratio, continuity keeps the speed ratio constant. Doubling the inlet speed therefore doubles the throat speed too. Both squared speeds become four times as large, so their difference and the corresponding pressure drop grow by that same factor.

$$
\frac{\Delta p'}{\Delta p}=\frac{4v_2^2-4v_1^2}{v_2^2-v_1^2}=4.
$$
