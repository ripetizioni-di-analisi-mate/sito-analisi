At fixed geometry and density, the Venturi inlet speed doubles. What factor multiplies the pressure drop?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The area ratio and its bracketed factor stay the same. Only the squared speed factor changes.

$$
\Delta p_2/\Delta p_1=(v_{1,\mathrm{new}}/v_{1,\mathrm{old}})^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta p_2/\Delta p_1&=2^{2}\\[4pt]&=4.\end{aligned}
$$
