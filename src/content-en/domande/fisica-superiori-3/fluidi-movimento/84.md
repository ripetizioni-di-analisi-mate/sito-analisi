If the gas over the free surface has higher pressure than at the outlet, what term is added to jet energy?

- A reduction from height alone
- A viscosity term that is always positive
- A term from the planet's whole mass
- The positive work from the pressure difference

## Explanation

The higher free-surface pressure does more work per unit volume than the outlet pressure. The difference $p_{\mathrm s}-p_{\mathrm u}$ is positive. It adds to the energy per unit volume supplied by the height difference. At the same depth the jet therefore gains more kinetic energy than with equal pressures.
