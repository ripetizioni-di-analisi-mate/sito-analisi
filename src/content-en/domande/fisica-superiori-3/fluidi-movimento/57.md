What does steady flow mean at a fixed point?

- The fluid quantities there do not change with time
- The same particle stays there at rest
- Speed and pressure agree at every point
- Velocity has to be zero

## Explanation

Imagine holding a meter fixed at one cross section. In steady flow its readings of velocity, pressure and density stay constant over time. An identical probe farther downstream may read a different value. The criterion concerns how the readings at each fixed point change with time.
