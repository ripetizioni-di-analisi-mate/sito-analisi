What does steady flow mean at a fixed point?

- The fluid quantities there do not change with time
- The same particle stays there at rest
- Speed and pressure agree at every point
- Velocity has to be zero

## Explanation

Steadiness concerns time dependence at a location. A moving particle can encounter different conditions at other places.
