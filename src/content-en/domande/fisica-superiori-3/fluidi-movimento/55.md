Which unit is appropriate for volume flow rate?

- Kilograms per second
- Pascals
- Cubic metres per second
- Metres per second

## Explanation

Volume flow rate relates each interval to the volume crossing a section. Dividing volume in cubic metres by duration in seconds gives $\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Litres per second measures the same quantity with a different volume unit. Kilograms per second measures the mass transported per second.
