Where is the lower pressure in an ideal horizontal Venturi?

- At the narrower throat
- At the wider section
- The same everywhere
- At the inlet merely because it is first

## Explanation

Continuity gives higher speed at the throat. At the same elevation, a greater kinetic energy share leaves lower pressure.
