Where is the lower pressure in an ideal horizontal Venturi?

- At the narrower throat
- At the wider section
- The same everywhere
- At the inlet merely because it is first

## Explanation

Flow rate stays constant through the Venturi and the throat has the smallest area. Speed is highest there. For ideal horizontal flow, the largest kinetic term corresponds to the lowest static pressure. This reading concerns pressure at the throat, while the flow rate is the same as at the inlet.
