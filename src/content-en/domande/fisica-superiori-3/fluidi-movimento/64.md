What observation violates steady-state conditions for an incompressible liquid in a rigid tank?

- Inlet and outlet areas differ
- Different points have different speeds
- Inflow equals outflow
- Its level rises with time

## Explanation

Rising height implies volume and mass accumulation. The volume inside is changing with time.
