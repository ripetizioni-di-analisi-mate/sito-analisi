What observation violates steady-state conditions for an incompressible liquid in a rigid tank?

- Inlet and outlet areas differ
- Different points have different speeds
- Inflow equals outflow
- Its level rises with time

## Explanation

If the level rises in a rigid tank, the amount of liquid it contains increases. For an incompressible liquid this means that more mass enters than leaves. The volume occupied changes with time and the tank process is unsteady even if the individual pipe rates remain constant.
