Flow rises linearly from zero to $6\,\mathrm{L/s}$ in $8\,\mathrm s$. What volume is transported in this interval?

- $48\,\mathrm L$
- $12\,\mathrm L$
- $24\,\mathrm L$
- $-24\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The linear rise from zero to the final flow rate forms a triangle above the time axis. The volume is half the product of duration and final height. This is equivalent to using three litres per second as the mean. Sustaining the final rate for all eight seconds would give twice the volume.

$$
\Delta V=\frac{8\cdot6}{2}\,\mathrm L=24\,\mathrm L.
$$
