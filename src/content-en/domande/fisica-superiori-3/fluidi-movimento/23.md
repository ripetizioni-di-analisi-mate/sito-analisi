Flow rises linearly from zero to $6\,\mathrm{L/s}$ in $8\,\mathrm s$. Find the transported volume.

- $48\,\mathrm L$
- $12\,\mathrm L$
- $24\,\mathrm L$
- $-24\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Use the area of the triangle. Applying the final rate for the entire interval would double the volume.

$$
V=\frac12Q_f t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}V&=6 \cdot 8 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=24\,\mathrm L.\end{aligned}
$$
