Which practical effect reduces flow rate below ideal speed times geometric area?

- Small radius alone in the absence of losses
- Using cubic metres per second
- Jet contraction and dissipation
- Increasing depth with no other changes

## Explanation

Beyond the hole the jet can occupy less area than the geometrical opening. Friction and turbulence also dissipate some of the available energy. Applying ideal speed to the full hole area can therefore overestimate flow rate. An actual estimate needs a measurement or a discharge coefficient.
