In a wide open tank the free surface is $1.20\,\mathrm m$ above the bottom. Two small holes of equal effective area are $0.20\,\mathrm m$ and $0.95\,\mathrm m$ above the bottom. In the ideal model, what is the lower hole's flow rate divided by the upper hole's?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-1.3,1.42 -1.3,0 1.25,0 1.25,.14;1.25,.26 1.25,.89;1.25,1.01 1.25,1.42;-1.3,1.20 1.25,1.20" poligoni="-1.3,0 1.25,0 1.25,1.20 -1.3,1.20" punti="1.25,.20,pieno;1.25,.95,pieno" etichette="1.39,.21,zᵦ;1.39,.96,zₐ;-.9,1.26,zₛ" xmin=-1.55 xmax=1.98 ymin=-.12 ymax=1.56 titolo="Hole heights are measured from the bottom. Depths are measured from the surface."}

- $4$
- $1$
- $2$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

Subtract the hole heights from the height of the surface. The depths are one metre and a quarter of a metre. With equal areas, the flow-rate ratio is the speed ratio. Torricelli's law uses the square root of the ratio of the depths measured from the free surface.

$$
\frac{Q_{\mathrm{b}}}{Q_{\mathrm{a}}}=\sqrt{\frac{1.20-0.20}{1.20-0.95}}=\sqrt4=2.
$$
