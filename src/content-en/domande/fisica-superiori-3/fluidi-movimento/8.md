A small sphere of radius $r=0.00106\,\mathrm m$ moves at $v=0.0013\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a fluid of viscosity $\eta=0.13\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$. The Reynolds number is very small and the walls are distant. Which value is closest to the magnitude of its Stokes drag?

- $6.75\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $1.07\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $3.38\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm N$
- $3.38\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At very small Reynolds number, Stokes drag is linear in relative speed. The radius is already given in metres. The units of viscosity times radius times speed reduce to newtons. The force opposes relative motion, while the question asks for its positive magnitude.

$$
F_v=6\pi(0.13)(0.00106)(0.0013)\,\mathrm N\simeq3.38\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N.
$$
