A small sphere of radius $r=0.00106\,\mathrm m$ moves at $v=0.0013\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a fluid of viscosity $\eta=0.13\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$. Assume very small Reynolds number and distant walls. Find its Stokes drag magnitude.

- $6.75342\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $1.68835\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $-3.37671\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $3.38\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Under the stated assumptions drag is linear in relative speed and radius. Use radius because the numerical prefactor is $6\pi$.

$$
F_v=6\pi\eta rv,\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_v&=6 \cdot \pi \cdot 13 \cdot \frac{1}{100} \cdot 53 \cdot \frac{1}{50000} \cdot 13 \cdot \frac{1}{10000}\\[4pt]&\simeq3.38\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
