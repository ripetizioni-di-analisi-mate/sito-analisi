A small sphere of radius $r=0.00106\,\mathrm m$ moves at $v=0.0013\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a fluid of viscosity $\eta=0.13\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$. Assume very small Reynolds number and distant walls. Find its Stokes drag magnitude. Choose the closest value.

- $6.75342\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $1.68835\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $-3.37671\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$
- $3.38\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At very small Reynolds number, Stokes drag is linear in relative speed. Use the radius already given in metres. The units of viscosity times radius times speed reduce to newtons. The force opposes relative motion, while the question asks for its positive magnitude.

$$
F_v=6\pi(0.13)(0.00106)(0.0013)\,\mathrm N\simeq3.38\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N.
$$
