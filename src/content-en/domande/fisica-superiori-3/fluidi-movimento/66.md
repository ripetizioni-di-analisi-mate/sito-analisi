Why need steady sections of a pipe not all have the same speed?

- Steadiness applies only to zero velocity
- Steadiness refers to time and section areas can differ
- Continuity always forces different speeds
- Liquid mass is never conserved

## Explanation

A narrower section needs a greater mean speed at the same flow rate. A sensor upstream and one in the constriction can therefore have different readings that both stay constant with time. A fluid particle travelling from the first sensor to the second accelerates even in that steady flow.
