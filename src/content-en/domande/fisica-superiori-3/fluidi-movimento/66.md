Why need steady sections of a pipe not all have the same speed?

- Steadiness applies only to zero velocity
- Steadiness refers to time and section areas can differ
- Continuity always forces different speeds
- Liquid mass is never conserved

## Explanation

For a leak-free liquid, continuity fixes area times speed. Different areas allow different speeds that remain constant locally.
