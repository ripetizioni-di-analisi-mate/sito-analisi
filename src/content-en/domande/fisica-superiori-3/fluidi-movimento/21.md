A pump delivers $4\,\mathrm{L/s}$ for $10\,\mathrm s$ and then $2\,\mathrm{L/s}$ for $5\,\mathrm s$. What is the total volume?

::grafico{linee="0,4 10,4;10,2 15,2" area="4;0;0;10|2;0;10;15" xmin=0 xmax=17 ymin=0 ymax=5.3 nomi="t (s),Q (L/s)" titolo="Add the areas of the two rectangles"}

- $50\,\mathrm L$
- $100\,\mathrm L$
- $25\,\mathrm L$
- $-50\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The rectangles in the graph have different widths. The first ten seconds transfer $40\,\mathrm L$ and the next five transfer $10\,\mathrm L$. Add the two contributions because the flow rate has the same sign in both intervals. Averaging the heights directly would give equal weight to intervals of different lengths.

$$
\Delta V=(4\cdot10+2\cdot5)\,\mathrm L=50\,\mathrm L.
$$
