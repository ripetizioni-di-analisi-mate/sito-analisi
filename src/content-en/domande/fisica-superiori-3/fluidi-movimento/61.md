In steady piping with no accumulation or branches, which rate is conserved even if density varies?

- Mass flow rate
- Necessarily volume flow rate
- Cross-sectional area
- Velocity at every point

## Explanation

Equal mass enters and exits per unit time. Equal masses can occupy different volumes.
