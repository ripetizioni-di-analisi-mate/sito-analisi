In steady piping with no accumulation or branches, which rate is conserved even if density varies?

- Mass flow rate
- Necessarily volume flow rate
- Cross-sectional area
- Velocity at every point

## Explanation

Take the fluid between the two cross sections as the system. If its mass stays constant and there are no side paths, the mass entering in one second equals the mass leaving. A less dense gas takes up more volume for the same mass, so volume flow rate can change.
