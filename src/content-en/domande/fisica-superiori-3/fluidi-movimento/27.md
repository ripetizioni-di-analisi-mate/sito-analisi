An incompressible liquid enters a steady junction with two outlets. The mean normal speeds are equal, and branch 2 has twice the area of branch 3. What fraction of the incoming flow rate leaves through branch 3?

::grafico{assi=0 vettori="-3,0,-.38,0,Qᵢₙ;.2,.12,2.48,1.35;.2,-.12,2.48,-1.35" punti="0,0,pieno" etichette="2.51,1.39,A₂;2.52,-1.37,A₃" xmin=-3.66 xmax=3.65 ymin=-1.94 ymax=1.95 titolo="The two outlets have the same mean speed and different areas."}

- $\frac14$
- $\frac12$
- $\frac13$
- $\frac23$

## Explanation

At the same speed, twice the area passes twice as much liquid each second. Branch 2 therefore carries two shares for every share carried by branch 3. The incoming flow is the sum of three shares, and the smaller branch carries one of them.

$$
\frac{Q_3}{Q_{\mathrm{in}}}=\frac{A_3v}{(2A_3+A_3)v}=\frac13.
$$
