Ideal water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ flows steadily along a streamline. Section 1 has $p_1=200000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and $v_1=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Section 2 is higher by $h=1.1\,\mathrm m$ and has $v_2=2.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With no pumps or losses find $p_2$. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $3.74818\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.87\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $93704.5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-1.87409\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The second section is both higher and faster. Both changes lower pressure relative to the first section.

$$
p_2=p_1+\frac{\rho}{2}(v_1^2-v_2^2)-\rho gh,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p_2&=- \frac{11 \cdot 981 \cdot 1000}{10 \cdot 100} + \frac{1000 \cdot \left(- \left(\frac{23}{10}\right)^{2} + \left(\frac{13}{10}\right)^{2}\right)}{2} + 200000\\[4pt]&\simeq1.87\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
