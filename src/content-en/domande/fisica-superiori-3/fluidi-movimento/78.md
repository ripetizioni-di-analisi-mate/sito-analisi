Which feature of a Venturi explains its increase in kinetic term before using Bernoulli?

- An atmospheric outlet in every case
- The smaller cross-section at constant flow rate for an incompressible fluid
- An increase in fluid mass
- The static friction law

## Explanation

First impose the same flow rate at both sections. For an incompressible liquid, $A_1v_1=A_2v_2$, so the smaller area implies a greater speed. Bernoulli's equation then relates that change to pressure. Continuity answers the speed question using geometry and the common flow rate.
