Water flows uniformly and perpendicularly through a section of area $A=0.0016\,\mathrm{m^2}$ at speed $v=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the volume flow rate.

- $0.00256\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.00512\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.00128\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $-0.00256\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

Each second the fluid advances one metre and six tenths in the direction normal to the section. That length and the area give the volume passing through. The product has units of volume per time and amounts to just over two and a half litres per second.

$$
Q=Av=(0.0016\cdot1.6)\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=0.00256\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.
$$
