Water travels uniformly through a section of area $A=0.0016\,\mathrm{m^2}$ at speed $v=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the volume flow rate.

- $0.00256\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.00512\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.00128\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $-0.00256\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

In one second water travels a distance numerically equal to its speed in metres per second. The area times that distance is the volume passing per second.

$$
Q=Av,\qquad
\begin{aligned}Q&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{625} \cdot 8 \cdot \frac{1}{5}\\[4pt]&=0.00256\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
