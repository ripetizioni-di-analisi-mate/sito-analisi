At unchanged depth, effective outlet area doubles while still much smaller than the surface. How does ideal flow rate change?

- Stays unchanged
- Doubles
- Halves
- Quadruples

## Explanation

In Torricelli's model, depth sets the ideal speed. Doubling the effective outlet area while keeping it much smaller than the free surface leaves that speed unchanged. Flow rate is the product with the effective area. The same jet length passes in one second and has twice the volume.
