At unchanged depth, effective outlet area doubles while still much smaller than the surface. How does ideal flow rate change?

- Stays unchanged
- Doubles
- Halves
- Quadruples

## Explanation

Fixed depth gives the same ideal speed. Flow equals speed times area and doubles with the area.
