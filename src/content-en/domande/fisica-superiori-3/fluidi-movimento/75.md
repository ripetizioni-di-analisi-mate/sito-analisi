In an ideal horizontal widening duct, what can accompany a drop in speed?

- A mandatory drop in static pressure
- Loss of mass
- An increase in static pressure
- Increase in height

## Explanation

In the widening section of the horizontal pipe, area increases, so mean speed falls at the same flow rate. In the ideal model this lowers the kinetic term and increases static pressure. If the area returns to its original value at the same height, the model predicts recovery of the initial pressure.
