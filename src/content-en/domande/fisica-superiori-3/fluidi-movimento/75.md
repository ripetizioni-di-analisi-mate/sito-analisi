In an ideal horizontal widening duct, what can accompany a drop in speed?

- A mandatory drop in static pressure
- Loss of mass
- An increase in static pressure
- Increase in height

## Explanation

At fixed height pressure plus kinetic energy per volume is conserved. Slowing transfers part of the latter to the first term.
