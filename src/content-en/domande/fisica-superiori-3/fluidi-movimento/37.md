Two regions of a pipe cross-section each have area $0.01\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Flow crosses the first at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction and the second at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the opposite direction. What is the net volume flow rate in the positive direction?

- $0.02\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $-0.02\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

Flow rate counts the signed volume crossing the section. The contributions are $0.03$ and $-0.01\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Their sum is $0.02\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ in the positive direction.
