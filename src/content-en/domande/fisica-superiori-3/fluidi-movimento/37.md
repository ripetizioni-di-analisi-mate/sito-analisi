Water exits at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ through an opening of effective area $0.0005\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find flow rate in litres per second.

- $2\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The jet speed is already known and crosses the effective jet area. Their product gives the volume actually discharged per second. Convert the result from cubic metres per second to litres per second. Using the full geometrical hole area could overestimate the volume when the jet contracts.

$$
Q=(0.0005\cdot4)\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=0.002\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=2\,\mathrm{L/s}.
$$
