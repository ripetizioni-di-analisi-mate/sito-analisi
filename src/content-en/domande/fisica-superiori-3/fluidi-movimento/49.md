A fluid of $\eta=0.10\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$ flows through a tube with $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, $v_m=0.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and diameter $0.020\,\mathrm m$. Find Reynolds number.

- $10$
- $20$
- $5$
- $-10$

## Explanation

For a pipe use the mean speed and diameter. Multiplying these by density gives the same units as dynamic viscosity. The ratio is therefore dimensionless. The value here is small compared with the usual transition values for turbulent flow in a straight pipe.

$$
\mathrm{Re}=\frac{\rho v_m D}{\eta}=\frac{1000\cdot0.05\cdot0.020}{0.10}=10.
$$
