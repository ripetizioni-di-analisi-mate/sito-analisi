A fluid of $\eta=0.10\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$ flows through a tube with $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, $v_m=0.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and diameter $0.020\,\mathrm m$. Find Reynolds number.

- $10$
- $20$
- $5$
- $-10$

## Explanation

The definition uses diameter and mean speed. Units cancel, and the low value is consistent with laminar flow.

$$
\mathrm{Re}=\rho v_mD/\eta,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\mathrm{Re}&=1000 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{20} \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{50} \cdot \frac{1}{1 \cdot \frac{1}{10}}\\[4pt]&=10.\end{aligned}
$$
