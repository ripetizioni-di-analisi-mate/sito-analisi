In a steady incompressible-flow junction with two outlets, which measurements determine the third flow rate?

- The area of just one branch
- Atmospheric pressure alone
- Inflow rate and one outlet rate
- Pipe density

## Explanation

Conservation of volume at the junction gives $Q_1=Q_2+Q_3$, with branch 1 entering. Measure the inlet and either outlet. Subtracting that outlet rate from the inlet gives the other one. Measuring only the inlet fixes the sum of the outlets, leaving the split undetermined.
