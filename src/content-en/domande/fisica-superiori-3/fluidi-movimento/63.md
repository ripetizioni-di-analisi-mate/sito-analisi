In a steady incompressible-flow junction with two outlets, which measurements determine the third flow rate?

- The area of just one branch
- Atmospheric pressure alone
- Inflow rate and one outlet rate
- Pipe density

## Explanation

Inflow is the sum of the two outflows. Subtract the first outflow from the inflow to find the second.
