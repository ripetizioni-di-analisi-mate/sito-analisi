The surface of a large sealed tank is at pressure $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ above the outlet. The outlet is $0.60\,\mathrm m$ below the surface. For water of density $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the ideal exit speed?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Alongside the descent, a favourable pressure difference drives the outflow. Dividing the excess pressure by density gives energy per unit mass. After multiplying by two, this contribution adds $4\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$ to the twelve from the height difference. The square root gives the jet speed.

$$
v=\sqrt{2(10)(0.60)+2\frac{2000}{1000}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
