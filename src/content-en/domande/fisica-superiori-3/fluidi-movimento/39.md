The surface of a large sealed tank is at pressure $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ above the outlet. The outlet is $0.60\,\mathrm m$ below the surface. For water and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find ideal exit speed.

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In addition to the height drop there is pressure work at entry. Add both energy contributions per mass before taking the square root.

$$
v=\sqrt{2(gh+\Delta p/\rho)},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\sqrt{2 \cdot \left(\frac{2000}{1000} + 10 \cdot 3 \cdot \frac{1}{5}\right)}\\[4pt]&=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
