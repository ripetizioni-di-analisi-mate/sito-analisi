In fully developed laminar flow through a circular pipe, the centreline speed is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its cross-sectional mean?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In the parabolic Poiseuille profile the speed on the axis is the maximum and it is zero at the wall. The average weighted by the areas of concentric rings is half the maximum. This mean determines flow rate. Applying the centre reading to the whole section would double the result.

$$
v_m=\frac{v_{\max}}{2}=\frac{6}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
