In fully developed laminar flow, the centreline speed is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its cross-sectional mean?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The profile is parabolic. Averaging with the weights of concentric areas gives half the centreline speed.

$$
v_m=v_{\max}/2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_m&=\frac{6}{2}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
