Ideal water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ travels steadily through a horizontal pipe. The speeds are $v_1=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with $v_2>v_1$. Which value is closest to $p_1-p_2$?

- $1.13\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6.48\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-5.64\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.64\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The pipe is horizontal, so the gravitational terms cancel in Bernoulli's balance. The water has more kinetic energy per volume downstream. The requested difference is upstream pressure minus downstream pressure and is therefore positive. The calculation uses the difference of the squared speeds.

$$
p_1-p_2=\frac{1000}{2}(3.6^2-1.3^2)\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq5.64\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
