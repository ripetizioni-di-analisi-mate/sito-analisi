Water travels uniformly through a section of area $A=0.0018\,\mathrm{m^2}$ at speed $v=1.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the volume flow rate.

- $0.00324\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.00648\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.00162\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $-0.00324\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

In one second water travels a distance numerically equal to its speed in metres per second. The area times that distance is the volume passing per second.

$$
Q=Av,\qquad
\begin{aligned}Q&=9 \cdot \frac{1}{5000} \cdot 9 \cdot \frac{1}{5}\\[4pt]&=0.00324\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
