Across a pipe section of $0.006\,\mathrm{m^2}$ the flow is entirely normal to the section and in the same direction. The speed is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ across one third of the area and $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ across the rest. What is the total flow rate in litres per second?

- $12\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The faster region occupies one third of the area, so it contributes one third of the weight in the mean. Each region carries $6\,\mathrm{L/s}$. A simple mean of the two readings would give too much weight to the faster region. Flow rate comes from the combined volumes crossing the section.

$$
\begin{aligned}v_m&=\left(\frac{3}{3}+\frac{2\cdot1.5}{3}\right)\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s},\\ Q&=0.012\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=12\,\mathrm{L/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
