An incompressible liquid flows steadily in a pipe with no branches or leaks. The cross-sectional area decreases from $0.010$ to $0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The upstream mean speed is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the mean speed at the smaller section.

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At fixed flow, area times speed is conserved. Speed increases inversely with area.

$$
v_2=A_1v_1/A_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=10 \cdot \frac{1}{1000} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{1}{4 \cdot \frac{1}{1000}}\\[4pt]&=5\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
