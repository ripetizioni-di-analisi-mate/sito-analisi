In a pipe carrying an incompressible liquid, the initial mean normal speed at the final section is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In a new steady flow, the flow rate drops by $25\%$ and the area of the final section halves. What mean speed is now measured at that section?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.125\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Speed is flow rate divided by area. The flow rate drops, leaving three quarters of the old numerator. Area drops further, to one half. Dividing the two scale factors shows that the final speed is one and a half times the initial speed.

$$
v_{\mathrm f}=v_{\mathrm i}\frac{Q_{\mathrm f}/Q_{\mathrm i}}{A_{\mathrm f}/A_{\mathrm i}}=3\cdot\frac{0.75}{0.50}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
