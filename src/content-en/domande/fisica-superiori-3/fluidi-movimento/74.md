What can be found from pressure drop in an ideal horizontal Venturi with known geometry and density?

- Local gravity irrespective of elevation
- Speed and thus flow rate
- Pipe material
- Total tank mass without knowing duration

## Explanation

The area ratio gives the speed ratio by continuity. At equal height the pressure difference depends on inlet speed squared, with a coefficient set by geometry and density. The positive square root gives the speed magnitude. Multiplying by the inlet area gives the flow rate magnitude.
