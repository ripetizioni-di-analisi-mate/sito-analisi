What can be found from pressure drop in an ideal horizontal Venturi with known geometry and density?

- Local gravity irrespective of elevation
- Speed and thus flow rate
- Pipe material
- Total tank mass without knowing duration

## Explanation

The area ratio relates the speeds. Bernoulli gives one squared speed from the pressure drop, and continuity gives flow.
