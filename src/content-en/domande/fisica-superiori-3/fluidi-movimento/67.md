What are the units of Bernoulli terms written per unit volume?

- Newtons
- Metres per second
- Pascals, equivalent to joules per cubic metre
- Total joules

## Explanation

Pressure is force per area, so a pascal equals $\mathrm{N/m^2}$. Multiplying numerator and denominator by a length rewrites the same unit as $\mathrm{J/m^3}$. This lets Bernoulli's equation add kinetic and gravitational potential energies per unit volume to the pressure term.
