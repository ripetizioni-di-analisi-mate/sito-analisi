A pump keeps $4\,\mathrm{L/s}$ for $10\,\mathrm s$. Find transported volume.

- $80\,\mathrm L$
- $20\,\mathrm L$
- $-40\,\mathrm L$
- $40\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Flow rate is volume per second. Four litres pass ten times over the stated duration.

$$
V=Qt,\qquad
\begin{aligned}V&=4 \cdot 10\\[4pt]&=40\,\mathrm L.\end{aligned}
$$
