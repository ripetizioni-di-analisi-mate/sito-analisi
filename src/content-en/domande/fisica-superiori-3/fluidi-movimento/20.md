A pump supplies a tank with no outlet at a constant rate. The clock reads $2\,\mathrm s$ when the volume is $14\,\mathrm L$ and $7\,\mathrm s$ when it is $29\,\mathrm L$. What time does the clock read when the volume reaches $35\,\mathrm L$?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $11\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Divide the change between the two volume readings by the time between the readings. This gives the flow rate even though the initial volume is unknown. Six litres remain from the second measurement. Add the time needed to supply them to the seven seconds already on the clock.

$$
\begin{aligned}Q&=\frac{29-14}{7-2}\,\mathrm{L/s}=3\,\mathrm{L/s},\\ t&=\left(7+\frac{35-29}{3}\right)\mathrm s=9\,\mathrm s.\end{aligned}
$$
