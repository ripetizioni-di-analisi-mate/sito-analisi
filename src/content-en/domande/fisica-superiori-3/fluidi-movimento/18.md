A fluid of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ has flow rate $3\,\mathrm{L/s}$. What is its mass flow rate?

- $4.8\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $-2.4\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

To use density in kilograms per cubic metre, convert $3\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to cubic metres per second. Then multiply by the mass in each cubic metre. The volume units cancel and the result gives the mass crossing the section in one second.

$$
\dot m=\rho Q=(800\cdot0.003)\,\mathrm{kg/s}=2.4\,\mathrm{kg/s}.
$$
