A fluid of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ has flow rate $3\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Find its mass flow rate.

- $4.8\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $-2.4\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

Convert volume per second to cubic metres per second. Multiplying by density converts passing volume to passing mass.

$$
\dot m=\rho Q,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\dot m&=800 \cdot 3 \cdot \frac{1}{1000}\\[4pt]&=2.4\,\mathrm{kg/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
