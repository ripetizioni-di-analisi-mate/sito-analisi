Which quantity relates volume and mass flow rates for a fluid of uniform density?

- Pipe weight
- Acceleration along the tube
- Absolute height of the section
- Fluid density

## Explanation

Density gives the mass in each unit of volume. Multiply it by the cubic metres crossing the section per second to obtain kilograms per second. The relation $\dot m=\rho Q$ uses one density over the whole section, as stated. A denser fluid carries more mass at the same volume flow rate.
