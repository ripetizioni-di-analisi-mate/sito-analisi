Which quantity relates volume and mass flow rates for a fluid of uniform density?

- Pipe weight
- Acceleration along the tube
- Absolute height of the section
- Fluid density

## Explanation

Mass flow rate measures the mass occupying the volume that passes per time. Density converts those volumes to masses.
