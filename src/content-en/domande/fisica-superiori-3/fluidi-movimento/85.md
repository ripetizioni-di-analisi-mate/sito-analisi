What distinguishes Poiseuille flow from the ideal model of a horizontal pipe with constant section?

- A pressure drop is needed to offset dissipation
- Mass flow is not conserved
- Velocity is equal at every point in the cross section
- Pressure rises in the flow direction without a pump

## Explanation

In the constant-area horizontal pipe, mean speed and height stay the same along the section. The ideal model would also leave pressure unchanged. In Poiseuille flow the fluid layers move at different speeds and viscosity dissipates energy. A pressure drop is therefore needed to maintain the flow rate.
