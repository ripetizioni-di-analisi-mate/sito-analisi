A Newtonian fluid of viscosity $\eta=0.056\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$ has steady laminar flow in a long tube of radius $r=0.00106\,\mathrm m$ and length $L=1.0\,\mathrm m$. The pressure difference is $\Delta p=130\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Use Poiseuille's law to find the flow rate in cubic millimetres per second ($1\,\mathrm{mm^3}=10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3}$). Choose the closest value.

- $2.30\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$
- $0.58\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$
- $1.15\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$

## Explanation

In a long tube with laminar flow, the rate is set by radius, pressure drop, viscosity and length. Use the radius in metres before raising it to the fourth power to keep track of powers of ten. Multiply the result in cubic metres per second by one billion to get cubic millimetres per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}Q&=\frac{\pi (0.00106)^4\cdot130}{8\cdot0.056\cdot1.0}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}\\&\simeq1.15\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=1.15\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
