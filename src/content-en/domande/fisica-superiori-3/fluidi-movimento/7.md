A Newtonian fluid of viscosity $\eta=0.056\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$ has steady laminar flow in a long tube of radius $r=0.00106\,\mathrm m$ and length $L=1.0\,\mathrm m$. The pressure difference is $\Delta p=130\,\mathrm{Pa}$. According to Poiseuille's law, which value is closest to the flow rate in cubic millimetres per second ($1\,\mathrm{mm^3}=10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3}$)?

- $2.30\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$
- $0.58\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$
- $1.15\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$

## Explanation

In a long tube with laminar flow, the rate is set by radius, pressure drop, viscosity and length. The radius is written in metres before being raised to the fourth power, which keeps track of the powers of ten. Multiply the result in cubic metres per second by one billion to get cubic millimetres per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}Q&=\frac{\pi (0.00106)^4\cdot130}{8\cdot0.056\cdot1.0}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}\\&\simeq1.15\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=1.15\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
