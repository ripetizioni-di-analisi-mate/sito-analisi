A Newtonian fluid of viscosity $\eta=0.056\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$ has steady laminar flow in a long tube of radius $r=0.00106\,\mathrm m$ and length $L=1.0\,\mathrm m$. The pressure difference is $\Delta p=130\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Find the Poiseuille flow rate.

- $2.30181\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $5.75451\cdot10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $1.15\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $-1.1509\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

Poiseuille's law uses radius to the fourth power. Divide pressure in pascals by viscosity, length and the factor of eight.

$$
Q=\frac{\pi r^4\Delta p}{8\eta L},\qquad
\begin{aligned}Q&=\pi \cdot \left(\frac{53}{50000}\right)^{4} \cdot 130 \cdot \frac{1}{1 \cdot 7 \cdot 8 \cdot \frac{1}{125}}\\[4pt]&\simeq1.15\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
