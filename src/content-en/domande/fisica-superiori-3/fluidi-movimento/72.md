Section 2 is higher and the fluid slows. What determines the sign of $p_2-p_1$ in the ideal model?

- Only which section is higher
- Only motion direction
- The choice of pascals or kilopascals
- Comparing pressure gain from slowing with pressure loss from rising

## Explanation

Slowing reduces the kinetic term and frees energy to raise pressure. Rising increases the gravitational term and leaves less energy available for pressure. If the first effect is larger, pressure rises. If they are equal, both points have the same pressure. The decision requires both contributions.
