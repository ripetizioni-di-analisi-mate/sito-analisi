How do drag and velocity compare for a sphere rising through fluid initially at rest?

- Both point upwards
- Drag is horizontal
- Drag is zero by definition
- Drag points downwards

## Explanation

The liquid far from the sphere is stationary, so the relative velocity points upward. Drag opposes this velocity and points downward. In this case it acts in the same direction as weight. If the liquid itself were flowing, its motion would first have to be subtracted to determine the drag direction.
