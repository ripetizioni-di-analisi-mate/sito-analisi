A steady gas passes through two sections of equal area. Its downstream density doubles and upstream speed is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the downstream speed?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For the gas, continuity conserves kilograms per second. Because the sections have equal area, the product of density and speed must agree at the two points. The density is twice as high downstream, so the same mass crosses the section at half the speed and half the volume flow rate.

$$
v_2=\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_2}v_1=\frac{8}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
