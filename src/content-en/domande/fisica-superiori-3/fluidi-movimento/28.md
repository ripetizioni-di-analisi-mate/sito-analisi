A steady gas passes through two sections of equal area. Its downstream density doubles and upstream speed is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the downstream speed.

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Mass flow rate is conserved for the gas. With unchanged area, doubling density requires halving speed.

$$
v_2=\rho_1v_1/\rho_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=\frac{8}{2}\\[4pt]&=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
