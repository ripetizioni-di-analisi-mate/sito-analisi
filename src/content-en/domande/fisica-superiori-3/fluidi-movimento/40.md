Ideal water has equal speeds at two sections, with the second $2\,\mathrm m$ higher. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to find $p_2-p_1$.

- $-40000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-10000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Kinetic terms agree. The higher pressure level is lower by the gravitational energy change per volume.

$$
p_2-p_1=-\rho g(y_2-y_1),\qquad
\begin{aligned}p_2-p_1&=\left(-1000\right) \cdot 10 \cdot 2\\[4pt]&=-20000\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
