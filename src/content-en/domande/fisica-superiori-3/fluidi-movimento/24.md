A steady flow of $0.003\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ must transfer $45\,\mathrm L$. How many seconds does it take?

- $30\,\mathrm s$
- $7.5\,\mathrm s$
- $-15\,\mathrm s$
- $15\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

A rate of three thousandths of a cubic metre per second transfers three litres per second. The requested volume is already in litres. Divide forty-five litres by three. Multiplying the resulting duration by the flow rate recovers the required volume.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{\Delta V}{Q}=\frac{45\,\mathrm L}{3\,\mathrm{L/s}}=15\,\mathrm s.
$$
