A steady flow of $0.003\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ must transfer $45\,\mathrm L$. Find the time in seconds.

- $30\,\mathrm s$
- $7.5\,\mathrm s$
- $-15\,\mathrm s$
- $15\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Express volume in cubic metres for consistency with the rate. The division gives seconds.

$$
t=V/Q,\qquad
\begin{aligned}t&=\frac{45}{1000 \cdot \frac{3}{1000}}\\[4pt]&=15\,\mathrm s.\end{aligned}
$$
