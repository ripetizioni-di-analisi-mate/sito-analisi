A pump supplies $7.2\pi\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to a circular pipe. The mean normal speed must be $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which diameter in centimetres satisfies both values?

- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Converting flow rate and dividing by speed gives the required area. The factor $\pi$ cancels when equating that area to the area of a disk. The positive square root gives the radius in metres. After converting units, double that radius to find the diameter.

$$
\begin{aligned}A&=\frac{0.0072\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.0036\pi\,\mathrm{m^2},\\d&=2\sqrt{0.0036}\,\mathrm m=12\,\mathrm{cm}.\end{aligned}
$$
