An incompressible fluid travels steadily with no leaks. In the first section $A_1=0.0048\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $v_1=1.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the second section $A_2=0.0024\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find $v_2$.

- $7.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The rate is the same in both sections. Find it upstream as area times speed and divide it by the downstream area.

$$
v_2=A_1v_1/A_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=3 \cdot \frac{1}{625} \cdot 9 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{1}{3 \cdot \frac{1}{1250}}\\[4pt]&=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
