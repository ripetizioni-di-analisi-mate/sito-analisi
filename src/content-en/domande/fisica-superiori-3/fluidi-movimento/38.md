The fixed outlet's depth decreases to one ninth of its initial value. Find the final-to-initial rate ratio in the ideal model.

- $0.666667$
- $0.333333$
- $0.166667$
- $-0.333333$

## Explanation

The outlet area stays fixed, so flow rate follows speed. The speed ratio is the square root of the depth ratio.

$$
Q_f/Q_i=\sqrt{h_f/h_i},\qquad
\begin{aligned}Q_f/Q_i&=\sqrt{1 \cdot \frac{1}{9}}\\[4pt]&\simeq0.333333.\end{aligned}
$$
