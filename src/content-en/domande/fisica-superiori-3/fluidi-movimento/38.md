In a wide open tank the fixed outlet's depth below the free surface decreases to one ninth of its initial value. Find the final-to-initial rate ratio in the ideal model.

- $0.666667$
- $0.333333$
- $0.166667$
- $-0.333333$

## Explanation

The hole area is fixed, so the flow-rate ratio equals the ideal speed ratio. Torricelli's law links speed to the square root of the depth. The square root of one ninth is one third. Flow rate does not vary directly with depth because the kinetic term depends on speed squared.

$$
\frac{Q_f}{Q_i}=\sqrt{\frac{h_f}{h_i}}=\sqrt{\frac19}=\frac13\simeq0.333333.
$$
