Two withdrawals have the same volume and different durations. Which has greater mean flow rate?

- The longer one
- They are always equal
- The shorter one
- It depends only on tank shape

## Explanation

The mean is $Q_m=\Delta V/\Delta t$. Both numerators are equal, so the shorter duration gives the larger ratio. If the first withdrawal takes half as long as the second, its mean rate is twice as high. The instantaneous rate may vary differently during the two withdrawals.
