In which regime is Stokes' law plausible for a sphere?

- Strong turbulence
- Very small Reynolds number and distant walls
- Sphere touching the walls
- Very fast jet with negligible friction

## Explanation

Stokes drag describes relative motion of a sphere when viscous effects dominate fluid inertia. This corresponds to a Reynolds number much smaller than one. Nearby walls alter the flow around the sphere even at small speeds, changing the drag coefficient.
