The graph shows the flow rate through the only pipe connected to a tank. Flow rate is positive when fluid enters. By how many litres does the volume in the tank increase over the first $10\,\mathrm s$?

::grafico{linee="0,3 4,3;4,-2 7,-2;7,4 10,4" area="3;0;0;4|0;-2;4;7|4;0;7;10" segmenti="4,0,4,3;7,0,7,-2;10,0,10,4" etichette="6.92,.30,7" xmin=0 xmax=10.9 ymin=-3 ymax=5.2 nomi="t (s),Q (L/s)" titolo="The rate is 3 L/s until 4 s, −2 L/s until 7 s and 4 L/s until 10 s."}

- $24\,\mathrm{L}$
- $18\,\mathrm{L}$
- $12\,\mathrm{L}$
- $30\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The part below the time axis represents fluid leaving the tank. Each of the two positive areas gives twelve litres. The middle interval removes six litres. Adding the magnitudes would give the volume that passed through the pipe in either direction, which is a different quantity from the increase inside the tank.

$$
\Delta V=[3\cdot4-2\cdot(7-4)+4\cdot(10-7)]\,\mathrm L=18\,\mathrm L.
$$
