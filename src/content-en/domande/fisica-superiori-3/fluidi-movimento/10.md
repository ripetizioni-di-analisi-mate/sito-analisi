A flow has a constant rate $Q=0.0028\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ for $t=40\,\mathrm s$. Find the volume transported.

- $0.224\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.112\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.056\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $-0.112\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

Flow rate is constant, so the same volume passes each second. Multiply the rate by duration in seconds.

$$
V=Qt,\qquad
\begin{aligned}V&=7 \cdot \frac{1}{2500} \cdot 40\\[4pt]&=0.112\,\mathrm{m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
