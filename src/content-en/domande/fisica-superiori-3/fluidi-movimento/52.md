At terminal downward speed, drag is $0.006\,\mathrm N$ and buoyancy is $0.004\,\mathrm N$. What is the sphere's weight?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve=".48*cos(t),.48*sin(t),0,6.283185" vettori="0,0,0,-2,P;-.3,0,-.3,1.2,F_v;.3,0,.3,.8,F_A" xmin=-1.4 xmax=1.4 ymin=-2.4 ymax=1.8 titolo="Buoyancy and drag balance weight at terminal speed"}

- $0.02\,\mathrm N$
- $0.005\,\mathrm N$
- $-0.01\,\mathrm N$
- $0.01\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At terminal speed acceleration is zero. During descent both drag and buoyancy point upward. Downward weight must balance the sum of their magnitudes. Without buoyancy, that same drag would correspond to a smaller weight.

$$
P=F_A+F_v=(0.004+0.006)\,\mathrm N=0.010\,\mathrm N.
$$
