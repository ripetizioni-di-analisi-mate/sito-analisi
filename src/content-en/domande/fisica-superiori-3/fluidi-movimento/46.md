To hold the flow rate fixed, replace a tube with one three times as long and with the same radius. Find the factor for pressure drop under Poiseuille flow.

- $6$
- $3$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

At fixed flow, pressure drop grows linearly with length. Each added length contributes resistance.

$$
\Delta p_2/\Delta p_1=L_2/L_1,\qquad
\Delta p_2/\Delta p_1=3.
$$
