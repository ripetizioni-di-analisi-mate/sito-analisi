The inlet of an ideal horizontal Venturi is twice the throat area. Its water pressure drop is $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, what is the inlet speed?

::grafico{assi=0 linee="-4,1.4 -1,1.4 1,1 4,1;-4,-1.4 -1,-1.4 1,-1 4,-1" vettori="-3.3,0,-1.9,0,v_1;1,0,3.8,0,v_2" xmin=-4.6 xmax=4.9 ymin=-2.2 ymax=2.3 titolo="Ideal flow rate is conserved through the throat"}

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The throat has half the inlet area, so speed doubles inside it. The kinetic term therefore uses $v_2^2=4v_1^2$. For horizontal flow, Bernoulli's equation relates the pressure drop to the difference of the squared speeds. Isolate the square of the inlet speed and take the positive root.

$$
v_1=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot6000}{1000(4-1)}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
