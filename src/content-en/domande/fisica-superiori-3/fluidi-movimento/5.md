An open reservoir has a free surface much larger than a small hole at depth $h=1.1\,\mathrm m$. With equal air pressure on the surface and jet and negligible losses, find exit speed. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $4.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.29129\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.32282\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4.64564\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Equal pressures cancel in the balance. With negligible surface speed, the height drop times gravity is half the squared exit speed.

$$
v=\sqrt{2gh},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\sqrt{2 \cdot 11 \cdot \frac{1}{10} \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100}}\\[4pt]&\simeq4.65\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
