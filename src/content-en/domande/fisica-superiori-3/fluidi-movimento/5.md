An open reservoir has a free surface much larger than a small hole at depth $h=1.1\,\mathrm m$. With equal air pressure on the surface and jet and negligible losses, find exit speed. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Choose the closest value.

- $4.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $21.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.28\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.32\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure acts both on the free surface and on the emerging jet. The large surface area makes its velocity negligible in the balance. The energy per unit mass associated with the depth becomes kinetic energy. Take the positive square root because the question asks for speed.

$$
v=\sqrt{2gh}=\sqrt{2\cdot9.81\cdot1.1}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq4.65\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
