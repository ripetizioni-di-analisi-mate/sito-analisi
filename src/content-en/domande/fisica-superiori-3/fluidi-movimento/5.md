An open reservoir has a free surface much larger than a small hole at depth $h=1.1\,\mathrm m$. With equal air pressure on the surface and jet , negligible losses and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, which value is closest to the exit speed?

- $4.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $21.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.28\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.32\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure acts both on the free surface and on the emerging jet. The large surface area makes its velocity negligible in the balance. The energy per unit mass associated with the depth becomes kinetic energy. The positive square root is taken because the question asks for speed.

$$
v=\sqrt{2gh}=\sqrt{2\cdot9.81\cdot1.1}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq4.65\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
