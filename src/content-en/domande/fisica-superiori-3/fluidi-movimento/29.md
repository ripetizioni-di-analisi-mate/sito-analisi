A tank receives water at a constant rate of $(7.4\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and drains at a constant rate of $(6.8\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Any combination of values in the two intervals is possible. Find the smallest volume increase over $5\,\mathrm s$.

- $1\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $5\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The smallest accumulation combines the lowest inflow with the highest outflow. Their difference is still positive, so the water increases even with both measurements at the least favourable ends of their ranges. Multiply the minimum net flow rate by all five seconds.

$$
\Delta V_{\min}=\big[(7.4-0.2)-(6.8+0.2)\big]\cdot5\,\mathrm{L}=1\,\mathrm{L}.
$$
