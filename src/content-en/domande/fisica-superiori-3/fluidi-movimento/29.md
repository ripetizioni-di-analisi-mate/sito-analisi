Inflow $7\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and outflow $5\,\mathrm{L/s}$ pass through a tank of incompressible liquid. Find its accumulation in $3\,\mathrm s$.

- $6\,\mathrm L$
- $12\,\mathrm L$
- $3\,\mathrm L$
- $-6\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

When rates differ there is accumulation, so the liquid level is not steady.

$$
\Delta V=(Q_{\mathrm{in}}-Q_{\mathrm{out}})t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta V&=\left(7 - 5\right) \cdot 3\\[4pt]&=6\,\mathrm L.\end{aligned}
$$
