An ideal horizontal Venturi has $A_1/A_2=3$ and inlet speed $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Pressure drops by $14400\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the density of the fluid?

- $1800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $450\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Continuity makes the throat speed three times the inlet speed, giving $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In a horizontal pipe the pressure drop is half the density times the difference of the squared speeds. Since the pressure and speeds are known, isolate density. Pascals divided by speed squared gives mass per volume.

$$
\rho=\frac{2\cdot14400}{6^2-2^2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
