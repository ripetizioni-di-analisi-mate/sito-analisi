An ideal horizontal Venturi has $A_1/A_2=3$ and inlet speed $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Pressure drops by $14400\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Find the density.

- $1800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $450\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The throat area gives three times the speed. Isolate density by dividing the pressure drop by half the difference in squared speeds.

$$
\rho=\frac{2\Delta p}{v_1^2[(A_1/A_2)^2-1]},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\rho&=2 \cdot 14400 \cdot \frac{1}{2^{2} \cdot \left(\left(-1\right) \cdot 1 + 3^{2}\right)}\\[4pt]&=900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
