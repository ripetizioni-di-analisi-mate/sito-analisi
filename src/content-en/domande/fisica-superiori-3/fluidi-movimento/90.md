What velocity occurs at the edge of a laminar profile in a stationary pipe with no slip at the wall?

- Equal to the centreline speed
- Zero relative to the wall
- The profile maximum
- Half the mean in every case

## Explanation

No slip makes the adjacent fluid velocity match the wall velocity. In a stationary pipe it is zero.
