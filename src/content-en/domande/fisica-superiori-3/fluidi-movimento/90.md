What is the fluid velocity at the edge of a laminar profile, measured relative to the stationary wall with no slip?

- Equal to the centreline speed
- Zero relative to the wall
- The profile maximum
- Half the mean in every case

## Explanation

With no slip, the layer of fluid in contact with the wall moves with the wall. The pipe is stationary here, so the fluid at the boundary also has zero speed in the laboratory. Farther inward the speed rises to its maximum at the axis in the Poiseuille profile.
