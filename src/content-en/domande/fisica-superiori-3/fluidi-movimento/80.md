When may the free surface speed be neglected in Torricelli's law?

- When the areas are equal
- When the hole is much larger than the surface
- Always regardless of geometry
- When the free surface is much larger than the hole

## Explanation

The volume leaving through the hole in a second equals the volume lost by the tank in that second. By continuity the speed ratio is the hole area divided by the free-surface area. If the latter is a hundred times larger, the surface speed is only $\dfrac1{100}$ of the jet speed.
