What changes when absolute pressures are replaced by gauge pressures with one common reference in Bernoulli?

- The same constant is subtracted from both sides
- Kinetic terms change sign
- Gravity always cancels
- Gauge pressure is always positive

## Explanation

Gauge pressure is obtained by subtracting a reference pressure. The same reference applies at the initial and final points, so the pressure difference remains unchanged. Speeds and heights also stay the same. Mixing an absolute reading with a gauge reading would leave the reference in the calculation once.
