Water flows steadily through a circular tube whose radius halves downstream. The upstream mean speed is $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the downstream mean speed.

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Circular area depends on radius squared. Halving the radius gives an area ratio of four to one.

$$
v_2=v_1(r_1/r_2)^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=\frac{3 \cdot 2^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
