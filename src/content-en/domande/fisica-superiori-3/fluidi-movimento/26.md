Water flows steadily through a circular tube whose radius halves downstream. The upstream mean speed is $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the downstream mean speed.

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Flow rate is conserved and the circular area is proportional to radius squared. Halving the radius leaves one quarter of the original area. Four times the speed through one quarter of the area gives the same product. A factor of two would apply if the area itself were halved.

$$
v_2=v_1\left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^2=1.5\cdot2^2\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
