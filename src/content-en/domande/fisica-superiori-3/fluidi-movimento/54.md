Stokes drag on a sphere is $3\,\mathrm{mN}$ at $2\,\mathrm{mm/s}$. At unchanged fluid and radius, what speed gives $9\,\mathrm{mN}$?

- $0.012\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.006\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.003\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.006\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Drag varies linearly with speed magnitude. Three times the force requires three times the speed, converted to metres per second.

$$
v=v_1F_2/F_1,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=2 \cdot 9 \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{1000}\\[4pt]&=0.006\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
