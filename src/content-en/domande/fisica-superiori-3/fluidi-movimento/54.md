Stokes drag on a sphere is $3\,\mathrm{mN}$ at $2\,\mathrm{mm/s}$. At unchanged fluid and radius, what speed gives $9\,\mathrm{mN}$?

- $0.012\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.006\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.003\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.006\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

While the Stokes model holds, the coefficient linking drag and relative speed depends on viscosity and radius. These are fixed, so tripling the drag requires tripling the speed. The result is six millimetres per second. Divide by one thousand to express that value in metres per second.

$$
v_2=v_1\frac{F_2}{F_1}=2\cdot\frac93\,\mathrm{mm/s}=0.006\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
