For an incompressible liquid, what does doubling section area do at fixed flow rate?

- It doubles mean velocity
- It halves mean velocity
- It quadruples mean velocity
- It leaves velocity unchanged

## Explanation

Divide the fixed flow rate by the new area to find the normal mean speed. Since $Q=Av_m$, the same quantity of liquid crosses a section of twice the area each second at half the mean speed. Check by multiplying the new area by the new speed.
