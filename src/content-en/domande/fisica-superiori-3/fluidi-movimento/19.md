A flow of $12\,\mathrm{L/s}$ crosses a section of $0.006\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find the mean normal speed.

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Dividing volume per second by area gives the length of fluid passing in one second.

$$
v=Q/A,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v&=\frac{12}{1000 \cdot \frac{6}{1000}}\\[4pt]&=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
