A flow of $12\,\mathrm{L/s}$ crosses a section of $0.006\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find the mean normal speed.

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Distribute the volume transferred per second, expressed in cubic metres, over the entire sectional area. The division gives length per second, the mean speed in the normal direction. Check the value by multiplying it by the sectional area again and converting the resulting flow rate back into litres.

$$
v_m=\frac{Q}{A}=\frac{0.012}{0.006}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
