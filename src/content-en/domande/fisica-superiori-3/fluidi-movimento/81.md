During draining, why does using initial flow rate underestimate the time needed to drain the whole volume?

- Level and flow rate decrease with time
- The hole always widens
- Flow increases until the tank is empty
- Gravity increases as the level drops

## Explanation

The hole's depth below the level falls. Velocity decreases, so initial flow overestimates volume discharged in later intervals.
