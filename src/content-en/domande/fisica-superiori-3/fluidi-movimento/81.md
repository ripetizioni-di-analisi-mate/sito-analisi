During draining, why does using initial flow rate underestimate the time needed to drain the whole volume?

- Level and flow rate decrease with time
- The hole always widens
- Flow increases until the tank is empty
- Gravity increases as the level drops

## Explanation

As the tank loses water, the level approaches the hole. The smaller height difference gives a lower exit speed and a lower flow rate through the unchanged hole. Treating the initial rate as constant overestimates how quickly the tank drains through the rest of the process.
