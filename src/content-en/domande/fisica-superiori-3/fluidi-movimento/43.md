Downstream, an ideal pipe remains at the same height and has pressure lower by $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Water enters at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find final speed. Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

- $2.45\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The height is unchanged, so the pressure drop increases the kinetic term. It contributes six square metres per square second to add to the square of the initial speed. Take the positive root after adding them. Ignoring the initial speed would retain only the contribution from pressure.

$$
v_2=\sqrt{1^2+\frac{2\cdot3000}{1000}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=\sqrt7\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq2.65\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
