Downstream, an ideal pipe remains at the same height and has pressure lower by $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Water enters at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find final speed.

- $5.2915\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.32288\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.64575\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2.64575\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Energy supplied by pressure becomes kinetic energy. Add the contribution to squared initial speed and take the root.

$$
v_2=\sqrt{v_1^2+2(p_1-p_2)/\rho},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=\sqrt{1 + 2 \cdot 3000 \cdot \frac{1}{1000}}\\[4pt]&\simeq2.64575\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
