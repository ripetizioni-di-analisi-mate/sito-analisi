How do you obtain the net volume transferred from a graph of signed flow rate against time?

- Maximum slope
- Signed area below the graph
- Final rate
- Initial rate divided by duration

## Explanation

A thin slice of the graph has time as its width and flow rate as its height, so its area is a volume. Adding the slices gives the net volume. For a signed flow rate, the part below the time axis represents flow the other way and contributes negatively.
