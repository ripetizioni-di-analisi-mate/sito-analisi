What acceleration does a sphere have at its terminal downward speed?

- Always equal to gravity
- Constant upward
- Zero
- Varies with time squared

## Explanation

At terminal speed, downward weight is balanced by the sum of upward buoyancy and drag. Zero net force implies zero acceleration. The sphere keeps its downward velocity. Forces can still be present in this phase of uniform motion along a straight line.
