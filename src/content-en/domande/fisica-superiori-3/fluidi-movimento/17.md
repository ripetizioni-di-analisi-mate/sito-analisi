Velocity is uniform across an area of $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and makes $60^\circ$ to its normal. The speed is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the volume flow rate across the face?

- $0.04\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $-0.04\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

The angle of $60^\circ$ is measured from the normal, so the component crossing the surface is found with the cosine. The tangential component is parallel to the surface. For the same area and speed magnitude, this angled flow has half the rate of a perpendicular flow.

$$
Q=Av\cos60^\circ=(0.020\cdot4\cdot0.5)\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=0.040\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.
$$
