Why do pressures cancel when calculating discharge from a large open reservoir?

- There is no pressure inside
- Absolute pressure is zero at the hole
- Free surface and emerging jet are at the same atmospheric pressure
- Water density is zero

## Explanation

The point on the free surface is in contact with air. The jet immediately outside the hole also has air around it. Both pressures are atmospheric and cancel in the balance. With a pressurised tank, the surface pressure would require its own term in the comparison.
