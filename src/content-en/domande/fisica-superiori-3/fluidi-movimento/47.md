In the same tube the first liquid flows at $0.40\,\mathrm{L/s}$ with a pressure drop of $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The second flows at $0.30\,\mathrm{L/s}$ with a drop of $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Both flows obey Poiseuille's law. What is the second liquid's viscosity divided by the first liquid's?

- $0.75$
- $1.5$
- $2$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

For the same pipe, viscosity is proportional to pressure drop divided by flow rate. The second liquid carries less volume per second despite a higher pressure drop, so it is more viscous. Multiply the pressure-drop scale factor by the reciprocal of the flow-rate scale factor.

$$
\frac{\eta_2}{\eta_1}=\frac{12}{8}\cdot\frac{0.40}{0.30}=2.
$$
