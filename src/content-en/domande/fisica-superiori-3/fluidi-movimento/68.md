When can the height terms be cancelled between two points?

- When section areas agree in all cases
- When the fluid is water
- When speeds agree but heights differ
- When their heights are equal

## Explanation

For the same liquid in uniform gravity, the height term is $\rho gy$. With both taps in the same horizontal plane, those terms agree and subtract from both sides. One can also use that plane as height zero so the terms vanish from the outset.
