Why does a pump between points prevent direct use of the conservative form of Bernoulli's equation?

- It automatically stops flow
- It adds mechanical work to the fluid
- It changes gravity
- It always imposes equal pressures

## Explanation

The pump transfers mechanical energy from outside the fluid. Between an upstream and a downstream point, the balance must include the work supplied per unit volume. The conservative form would otherwise set the sum of pressure, kinetic energy and the height term equal at both ends.
