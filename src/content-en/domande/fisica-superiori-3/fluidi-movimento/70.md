Why does a pump between points prevent direct use of the conservative form of Bernoulli's equation?

- It automatically stops flow
- It adds mechanical work to the fluid
- It changes gravity
- It always imposes equal pressures

## Explanation

The pump-free balance includes pressure work and the fluid's energy changes. A pump adds another contribution.
