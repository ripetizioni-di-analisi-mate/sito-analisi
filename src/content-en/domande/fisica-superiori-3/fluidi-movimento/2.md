A flow has a constant rate $Q=0.0026\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ for $t=35\,\mathrm s$. What volume is transported?

- $0.182\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.091\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.0455\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $-0.091\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The rate is the same in each of the thirty-five seconds, so the volume is a rectangle's area on a flow rate–time graph. The duration in seconds cancels the seconds in the denominator of the rate. As a check, two and six tenths litres per second gives ninety-one litres altogether.

$$
\Delta V=Q\Delta t=(0.0026\cdot35)\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.091\,\mathrm{m^3}.
$$
