A flow has a constant rate $Q=0.0026\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ for $t=35\,\mathrm s$. Find the volume transported.

- $0.182\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.091\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.0455\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $-0.091\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

Flow rate is constant, so the same volume passes each second. Multiply the rate by duration in seconds.

$$
V=Qt,\qquad
\begin{aligned}V&=13 \cdot \frac{1}{5000} \cdot 35\\[4pt]&=0.091\,\mathrm{m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
