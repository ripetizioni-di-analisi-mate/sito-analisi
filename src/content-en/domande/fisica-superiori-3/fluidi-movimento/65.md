Why does halving the radius of a circular section quadruple speed at fixed flow?

- Area drops to one quarter
- Area halves
- Density doubles
- Flow rate quadruples

## Explanation

The section is a disk of area $A=\pi r^2$. The new radius is half the old one, so the area is a quarter. At the same flow rate, area times mean speed must give the old product. That requires a factor of four in speed.
