Why does halving the radius of a circular section quadruple speed at fixed flow?

- Area drops to one quarter
- Area halves
- Density doubles
- Flow rate quadruples

## Explanation

Area scales with radius squared. Velocity and area are inversely proportional at fixed flow rate.
