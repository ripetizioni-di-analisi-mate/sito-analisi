In the limit of a stationary homogeneous liquid, what follows when the kinetic terms vanish?

- Pressure proportional to speed
- Equal pressure at every height
- Hydrostatic pressure with pressure plus height term constant
- Stokes' principle

## Explanation

The speeds are zero and the kinetic terms vanish. In the same homogeneous fluid, $p+\rho gy$ remains constant. Pressure is higher at lower heights because the gravitational term is smaller. Letting the speed approach zero in Bernoulli's equation thus recovers the law for fluid at rest.
