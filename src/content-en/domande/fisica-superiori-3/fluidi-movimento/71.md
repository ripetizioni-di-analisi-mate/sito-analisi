In the limit of a stationary homogeneous liquid, what follows when the kinetic terms vanish?

- Pressure proportional to speed
- Equal pressure at every height
- Hydrostatic pressure with pressure plus height term constant
- Stokes' principle

## Explanation

Pressure and gravity remain. A higher point has correspondingly lower pressure.
