A sphere moves right at $0.015\,\mathrm{m/s}$ while the liquid itself moves right at $0.010\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which speed magnitude enters Stokes' law?

- $0.005\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.0025\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.005\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Take rightward as positive and subtract the liquid's velocity from the sphere's. The difference gives how fast the sphere moves relative to the fluid it meets. Although both travel rightward, the sphere advances relative to the liquid, so its drag points to the left.

$$
\lvert v_{\mathrm{rel}}\rvert=\lvert0.015-0.010\rvert\,\mathrm{m/s}=0.005\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
