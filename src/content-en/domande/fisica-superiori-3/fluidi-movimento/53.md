A sphere moves right at $0.015\,\mathrm{m/s}$ while the liquid itself moves right at $0.010\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which speed magnitude enters Stokes' law?

- $0.005\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.0025\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.005\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Drag depends on motion relative to the liquid. Subtract velocities measured in the same frame.

$$
v_{\mathrm{rel}}=v_{\mathrm{sphere}}-v_{\mathrm{fluid}},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{\mathrm{rel}}&=\frac{15 - 10}{1000}\\[4pt]&=0.005\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
