In a tube with maximum velocity at the centre and lower values near the edges, which speed belongs in $Q=Av$?

- Maximum value
- Wall value
- The sum of velocities at two points
- The cross-sectional mean of the normal component

## Explanation

Transported volume sums contributions over small areas. The mean includes those contributions with their area weights.
