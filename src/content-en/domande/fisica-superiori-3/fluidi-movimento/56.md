In a tube with maximum velocity at the centre and lower values near the edges, which speed belongs in $Q=Av$?

- Maximum value
- Wall value
- The sum of velocities at two points
- The cross-sectional mean of the normal component

## Explanation

Each part of the cross section contributes according to its area and normal speed. The cross-sectional mean gives the same total when multiplied by the whole area. Using the centreline speed would assign the highest value even to the regions where the fluid moves more slowly.
