At equal speeds, pressure in ideal water drops by $5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ between two sections. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ what is final minus initial height?

- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.25\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Kinetic terms cancel. Positive height gain takes up the energy per volume supplied by the pressure drop.

$$
\Delta y=(p_1-p_2)/(\rho g),\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta y&=\frac{5000}{10 \cdot 1000}\\[4pt]&=0.5\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
