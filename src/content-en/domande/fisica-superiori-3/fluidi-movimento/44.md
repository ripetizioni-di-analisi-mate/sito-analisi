At equal speeds, pressure in ideal water drops by $5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ between two sections. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the final minus the initial height?

- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.25\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Since the speed is the same, the kinetic term is unchanged. A lower final pressure means that the height term increased. Divide the five thousand pascals by the specific weight of water in pascals per metre. The positive result means that the final point lies above the initial one.

$$
y_2-y_1=\frac{5000\,\mathrm{Pa}}{10000\,\mathrm{Pa/m}}=0.5\,\mathrm m.
$$
