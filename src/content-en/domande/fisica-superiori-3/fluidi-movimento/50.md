A sphere of radius $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ moves far from the walls in a Newtonian fluid of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and viscosity $0.10\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$. Use Stokes's law only up to $\mathrm{Re}=0.10$ for this exercise. What is the maximum relative speed in millimetres per second that meets the criterion?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mm/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{mm/s}$

## Explanation

The Reynolds number uses the sphere's diameter, here $0.002\,\mathrm m$. Relative speed is in the numerator, so isolate it using the largest allowed Reynolds number. The limit is $0.005\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Using the radius as the characteristic length would double the limit and admit speeds outside the required regime.

$$
\begin{aligned}v_{\max}&=\frac{\mathrm{Re}_{\max}\eta}{\rho D}=\frac{0.10\cdot0.10}{1000\cdot0.002}\,\mathrm{m/s}\\&=5\,\mathrm{mm/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
