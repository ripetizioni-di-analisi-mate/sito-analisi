A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object has velocity components $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its momentum magnitude.

::grafico{uguale=1 vettori="0,0,3,0,v_x;3,0,3,4,v_y;0,0,3,4,v" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=5 titolo="The reference directions are perpendicular"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

Speed squared is the sum of squared perpendicular components. Multiply the resulting speed by mass.

$$
p=m\sqrt{v_x^2+v_y^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=2 \cdot \sqrt{3^{2} + 4^{2}}\\[4pt]&=10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
