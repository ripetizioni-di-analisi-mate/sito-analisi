A $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ ball arrives at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and stops in $0.20\,\mathrm s$. With the incoming direction positive find the mean net force.

- $-40\,\mathrm N$
- $-10\,\mathrm N$
- $-20\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Initial momentum is positive and final momentum is zero. The change is negative and remains negative after division by time.

$$
F_m=\frac{m(v_f-v_i)}{\Delta t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_m&=- \frac{1}{2} \cdot 8 \cdot \frac{1}{1 \cdot \frac{1}{5}}\\[4pt]&=-20\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
