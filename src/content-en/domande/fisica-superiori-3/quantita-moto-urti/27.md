During a collision a ball receives a horizontal impulse of magnitude $3\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. At no instant of the collision does the magnitude of the horizontal force exceed $20\,\mathrm N$, while the time dependence of the force is unknown. Which of the following durations of the collision is impossible?

- $0.15\,\mathrm s$
- $0.20\,\mathrm s$
- $0.10\,\mathrm s$
- $0.30\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The impulse is the signed area between the force graph and the time axis. Since the magnitude of the force never exceeds $20\,\mathrm N$, that area cannot exceed the area of the rectangle of height $20\,\mathrm N$ whose base is the duration, so $|J|\le F_{\max}\,\Delta t$. Therefore the duration must be at least $\Delta t=\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm s=0.15\,\mathrm s$. In $0.10\,\mathrm s$ the impulse would be at most $20\cdot0.10\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}=2\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$, so a duration of $0.10\,\mathrm s$ is **impossible**. For each of the other durations a constant force of magnitude $\dfrac{3}{\Delta t}$ is enough, and it does not exceed $20\,\mathrm N$.
