A sensor measures a horizontal impulse of $3\,\mathrm{N\,s}$ during a collision. At no instant did the magnitude of the horizontal force exceed $20\,\mathrm N$. The shape of the force–time graph is unknown. Which duration can definitely be ruled out?

- $0.15\,\mathrm s$
- $0.20\,\mathrm s$
- $0.10\,\mathrm s$
- $0.30\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the impulse cannot exceed the maximum force times the duration. At least $\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm s=0.15\,\mathrm s$ is required. A tenth of a second could give at most $2\,\mathrm{N\,s}$. For each other duration a suitable constant force falls within the limit.
