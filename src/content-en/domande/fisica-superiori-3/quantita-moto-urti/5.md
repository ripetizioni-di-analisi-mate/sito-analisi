An initially stationary system splits with no external impulse into two bodies of masses $m_1=1.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=4.9\,\mathrm{kg}$. The first has velocity $v_1=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. Find the component $v_2$. Choose the closest value.

- $-1.14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2.28571\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.571429\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.14286\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The two bodies must have opposite momenta. The negative sign means that the second body recoils opposite to the first.

$$
v_2=-m_1v_1/m_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=- \frac{8}{5} \cdot 7 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{49 \cdot \frac{1}{10}}\\[4pt]&\simeq-1.14\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

Check by reconstructing the total for both masses: the first contributes about $+5.6\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, balanced by the second.
