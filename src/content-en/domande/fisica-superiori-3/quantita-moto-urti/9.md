A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart changes velocity from $(4;3)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $(-3;4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the $x$ component $J_x$ of the net impulse.

- $-14\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

Both speeds are $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The change in direction reveals the effect of the impulse. The $x$ component switches from positive to negative. Subtract the initial component from the final one to obtain a negative velocity change, then multiply by the mass.

$$
J_x=m(v_{fx}-v_{ix})=2(-3-4)\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}=-14\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}.
$$
