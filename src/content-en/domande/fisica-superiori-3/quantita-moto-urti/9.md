An object of mass $m=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves in the positive direction with $v=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the momentum component.

- $42\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $84\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $21\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-42\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

Multiply mass by the signed velocity. Motion is in the positive direction, so the component is positive.

$$
p=mv,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=6 \cdot 7\\[4pt]&=42\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
