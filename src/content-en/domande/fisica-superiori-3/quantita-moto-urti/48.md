Two bodies of $6$ and $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ move at $4$ and $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the centre-of-mass velocity.

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The relation $M v_{CM}=P$ lets us use the net momentum. Divide by total mass.

$$
v_{CM}=\frac{\sum_jm_jv_j}{M},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{CM}&=\frac{- 2 \cdot 2 + 6 \cdot 4}{2 + 6}\\[4pt]&=2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
