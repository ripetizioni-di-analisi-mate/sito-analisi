A cart of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and speed $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ strikes an identical stationary cart. They stick with no external impulse. How much kinetic energy is lost in the collision?

- $90\,\mathrm J$
- $22.5\,\mathrm J$
- $-45\,\mathrm J$
- $45\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The shared velocity is half the initial velocity because twice the mass carries the same momentum. The final kinetic energy is half its initial value.

$$
E_{\mathrm{loss}}=\frac14mv^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}E_{\mathrm{loss}}&=\frac{5 \cdot 6^{2}}{4}\\[4pt]&=45\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$

For two equal masses that stick, speed halves and moving mass doubles, leaving half of the kinetic energy. The comparison uses squared speeds in both energies.
