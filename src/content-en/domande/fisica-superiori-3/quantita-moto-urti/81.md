During isolated recoil from rest, which fragment has greater speed?

- The less massive one
- The more massive one
- The one that moves in the positive direction
- It depends on which comes to rest first

## Explanation

The zero initial total momentum requires equal momentum magnitudes for the two pieces. At fixed momentum magnitude, speed is inversely proportional to mass. The lighter piece therefore has the greater speed.
