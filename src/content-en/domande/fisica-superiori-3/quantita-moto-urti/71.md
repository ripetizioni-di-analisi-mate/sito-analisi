Large internal forces act during a collision. Which condition allows conservation of momentum along the track?

- Zero internal forces
- Conservation of kinetic energy
- Zero or negligible external impulse along the track
- Equal masses

## Explanation

Internal impulses cancel in pairs when the two bodies' equations are added. The horizontal total depends on the projection of external forces along the track. Estimate their effect using the force magnitudes and contact time.
