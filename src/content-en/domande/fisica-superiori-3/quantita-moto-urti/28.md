A $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ ball arrives at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and rebounds at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the opposite direction in $0.050\,\mathrm s$. Find the mean net force magnitude.

::grafico{assi=0 vettori="0,1,6,1,v_i;0,0,-4,0,v_f" xmin=-5 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=2 titolo="The rebound reverses the direction of velocity"}

- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $-40\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The velocities point in opposite directions. The magnitude of the momentum change therefore uses the sum of the two speeds.

$$
F_m=\frac{m(v_{\mathrm{in}}+v_{\mathrm{out}})}{\Delta t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_m&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot \left(4 + 6\right) \cdot \frac{1}{1 \cdot \frac{1}{20}}\\[4pt]&=40\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$

A rebound adds the change needed to leave in the opposite direction after stopping. The magnitude of the velocity change is the sum of the two speeds.
