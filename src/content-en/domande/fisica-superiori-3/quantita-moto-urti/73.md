During a collision on a track, cart A receives $-4\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$ from cart B and $+1\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$ from an external actuator. What is the change in momentum of the system that contains only A?

- $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The system boundary encloses only one cart, so both B and the actuator supply external impulse. The contributions oppose each other and their sum stays negative. Including both carts in the system would cancel the two contact impulses by Newton's third law.

$$
\Delta p_A=(-4+1)\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}=-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}.
$$
