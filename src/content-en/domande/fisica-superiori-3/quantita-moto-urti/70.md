At the same initial velocity, why might a soft net stop an object with a smaller net average force than a hard surface does?

- It eliminates the initial momentum
- It spreads the same momentum change over more time
- It increases final momentum
- It increases mass while stopping

## Explanation

The net stretches while stopping the object, allowing it to give up the same momentum over a longer interval. In the relation between impulse and average force, the denominator grows and the magnitude of the mean falls.
