Why can a soft net stop an object with a lower average force than a hard surface?

- It eliminates the initial momentum
- It spreads the same momentum change over more time
- It increases final momentum
- It increases mass while stopping

## Explanation

Dividing the same momentum change by a longer duration gives a smaller mean net force.
