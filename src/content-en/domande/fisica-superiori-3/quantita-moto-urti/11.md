An object of mass $m=0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and initial speed $v=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes to rest in $\Delta t=0.18\,\mathrm s$. Find the magnitude of the mean net force.

- $53.3333\,\mathrm N$
- $13.3333\,\mathrm N$
- $26.7\,\mathrm N$
- $-26.6667\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Final momentum is zero. The change magnitude is $mv$, so the magnitude of the mean net force is $mv$ divided by the duration.

$$
F_m=\frac{mv}{\Delta t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_m&=3 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot 8 \cdot \frac{1}{9 \cdot \frac{1}{50}}\\[4pt]&\simeq26.7\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
