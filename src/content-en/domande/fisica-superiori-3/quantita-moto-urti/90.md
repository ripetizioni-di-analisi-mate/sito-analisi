Two bodies collide while an external agent gives the system a positive horizontal impulse. How does total momentum along the track change?

- It decreases by half the impulse
- It increases by the external impulse
- It stays constant because the collision is internal
- It changes by impulse squared

## Explanation

In the full system balance, the two contact impulses cancel. The only remaining contribution comes from the external agent. Its positive sign raises the momentum component along the axis from its initial value.
