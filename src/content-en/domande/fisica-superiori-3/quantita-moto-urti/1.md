An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves in the positive direction with $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the momentum component?

- $30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

The component is the mass multiplied by the signed velocity. Motion is in the positive direction, so the component is positive.

$$
p=mv,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=5 \cdot 6\\[4pt]&=30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The product uses the velocity sign set by the axis. The mass remains positive even if the direction chosen to measure motion is reversed.
