A stationary system breaks into three fragments. Two have components $6$ and $-2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. The third has mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$. Find its signed velocity.

- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The sum of the first two momenta is positive. The third must balance it, so its velocity is negative.

$$
v_3=-\frac{p_1+p_2}{m_3},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_3&=- \frac{4}{2}\\[4pt]&=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
