Two carts have initial momentum $12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. They stick and receive an external impulse of $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. Total mass is $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Find final velocity.

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

External impulse reduces the total momentum during contact. The shared velocity is final momentum divided by total mass.

$$
v_f=\frac{P_i+J_{\mathrm{est}}}{M},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=\frac{12 - 3}{3}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The external actuator changes the total before you divide by the mass. The impulse is negative and the total decreases, while its direction remains positive after the collision.
