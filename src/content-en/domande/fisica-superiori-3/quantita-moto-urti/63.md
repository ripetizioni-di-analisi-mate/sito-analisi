Velocity reverses direction at constant speed. What happens to the momentum of a constant-mass body?

- It doubles
- It halves
- It reverses with unchanged magnitude
- The vector is unchanged

## Explanation

Mass is a positive scalar factor, so the momentum vector follows the direction of the velocity. When that direction reverses, the components change sign. The same speed keeps the momentum magnitude and kinetic energy fixed.
