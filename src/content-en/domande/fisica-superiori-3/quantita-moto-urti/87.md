Two bodies have opposite momenta before an isolated collision. Which property holds after any outcome?

- Both bodies must be stationary
- Total kinetic energy vanishes
- The total stays zero
- The heavier body necessarily has zero momentum

## Explanation

Zero external impulse keeps the total at its pre-contact value. The two initial momenta cancel, so the final contributions must cancel as well, even when kinetic energy changes.
