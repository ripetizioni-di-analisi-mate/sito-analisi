A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ object stops from $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ under a mean net force of magnitude $30\,\mathrm N$. Find the braking duration.

- $0.5\,\mathrm s$
- $1\,\mathrm s$
- $0.25\,\mathrm s$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The required impulse cancels the initial momentum. Divide its magnitude by the magnitude of the mean force to obtain time.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{mv}{F_m},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta t&=3 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{30}\\[4pt]&=0.5\,\mathrm s.\end{aligned}
$$
