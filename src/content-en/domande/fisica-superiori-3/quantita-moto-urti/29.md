A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ object stops from $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ under a mean net force of magnitude $30\,\mathrm N$. How long does the braking last?

- $0.5\,\mathrm s$
- $1\,\mathrm s$
- $0.25\,\mathrm s$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The required impulse cancels the initial momentum. Dividing its magnitude by the magnitude of the mean force gives the time.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{mv}{F_m},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta t&=3 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{30}\\[4pt]&=0.5\,\mathrm s.\end{aligned}
$$

The endpoints of braking give the change in units of impulse. Dividing by a mean force gives seconds, as required by the unknown quantity.
