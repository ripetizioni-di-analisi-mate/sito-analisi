Which checks should be applied to verify an isolated one-dimensional elastic collision outcome?

- Equal velocities for each body before and after
- Equal masses
- Zero final energy
- Equal total momentum and total energy before and after

## Explanation

Momentum balance constrains the signed velocities. Kinetic energy balance uses the squared speeds. A pair that satisfies only the first condition could describe an inelastic collision instead of an elastic one.
