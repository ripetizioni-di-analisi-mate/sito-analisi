Measurements show unchanged total momentum but lower final kinetic energy. Which description fits?

- Elastic collision
- Separation that increases kinetic energy
- Inelastic collision
- Kinetic energy has no scalar value

## Explanation

Momentum isolation is compatible with kinetic energy loss. An elastic collision also requires kinetic energy conservation.
