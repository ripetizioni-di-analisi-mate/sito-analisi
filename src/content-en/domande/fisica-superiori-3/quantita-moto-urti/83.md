On an ideal track equal masses approach with velocities $+5$ and $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. After contact the first rebounds at $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the second moves forwards at $+3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How is the collision classified?

- Elastic
- Perfectly inelastic
- Inelastic with separation
- Higher final kinetic energy

## Explanation

For equal masses, the sum of the velocity components is two both before and after, so momentum is conserved. The sum of the squares falls from thirty-four to ten, so kinetic energy decreases. The final velocities differ and the carts move apart.
