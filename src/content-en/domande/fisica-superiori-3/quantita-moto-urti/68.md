On a force–time graph a positive region has the same area as a negative one. What follows?

- Zero force at every instant
- Zero velocity throughout
- Necessarily positive work
- Zero net impulse

## Explanation

The signed area below the axis subtracts exactly what the upper area adds. The momentum component returns to its initial value at the end of the interval. It can change in between because each segment supplies an impulse.
