A cart of mass $m_1=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and velocity $v_1=4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ strikes a stationary cart of mass $m_2=6\,\mathrm{kg}$. They stick on an ideal horizontal track. Neglect external impulse and find their common velocity. Choose the closest value.

- $4.09\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The second cart starts at rest. Initial momentum is that of the first cart and, after they stick, it belongs to their combined mass moving at one velocity.

$$
v_f=\frac{m_1v_1}{m_1+m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=\frac{5\cdot4.5}{5+6}\,\mathrm{m/s}\\[4pt]&\simeq2.05\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The common velocity is positive and below the first cart's speed because the second cart was at rest. The momentum is spread over a greater mass.
