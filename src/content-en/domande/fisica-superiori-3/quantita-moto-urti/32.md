An external horizontal force on the system is at most $4\,\mathrm N$. The collision lasts $0.030\,\mathrm s$. Find the maximum horizontal external impulse magnitude.

- $0.24\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $-0.12\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

Multiplying duration by the force bound gives an upper bound on signed area magnitude. The maximum occurs with a constant force in a single direction.

$$
J_{\mathrm{max}}=F_{\mathrm{max}}\Delta t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}J_{\mathrm{max}}&=4 \cdot 3 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&=0.12\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}.\end{aligned}
$$
