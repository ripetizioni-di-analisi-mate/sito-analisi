Two carts have collinear momenta $8$ and $-3\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Find the total component.

- $10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

Write both components on the same axis. The negative contribution reduces the total component.

$$
P_x=p_{1x}+p_{2x},\qquad
\begin{aligned}P_x&=8 - 3\\[4pt]&=5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The positive eight kilogram metres per second exceeds the negative contribution in magnitude. The net component is positive and smaller than the original positive contribution.
