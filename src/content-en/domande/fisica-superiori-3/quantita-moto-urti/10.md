The force along a track rises linearly from zero to $24\,\mathrm N$ in the first $0.20\,\mathrm s$. Over the next $0.10\,\mathrm s$ it stays at $-4\,\mathrm N$. What is the signed impulse?

- $2.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $4.4\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The push forms a triangle on the force graph, while the negative part forms a rectangle. The first contribution is positive and the second must be subtracted. The abrupt jump between the two segments is modelled as instantaneous, so it has zero duration and contributes no area.

$$
J_x=\frac{24\cdot0.20}{2}\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}-4\cdot0.10\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}=2\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}.
$$
