A constant force $F=18\,\mathrm N$ acts in the positive direction for $\Delta t=0.18\,\mathrm s$. Find the impulse.

- $6.48\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $3.24\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $1.62\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $-3.24\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The force is constant, so its graph against time is a rectangle. The impulse is the height times the duration.

$$
J=F\Delta t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}J&=18 \cdot 9 \cdot \frac{1}{50}\\[4pt]&=3.24\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}.\end{aligned}
$$
