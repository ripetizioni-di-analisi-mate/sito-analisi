The horizontal braking force is $-12\,\mathrm N$ between zero and $0.25\,\mathrm s$. An actuator on the same body applies $+8\,\mathrm N$ between $0.05$ and $0.20\,\mathrm s$. Find the combined impulse of the two forces from zero to $0.25\,\mathrm s$.

- $-1.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $+1.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

Adding the forces at each instant is equivalent to summing their impulses, each over its own duration. The brake acts throughout, while the actuator acts for only fifteen hundredths of a second. Its positive contribution offsets part of the negative one.

$$
J_x=[-12\cdot0.25+8(0.20-0.05)]\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}=-1.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}.
$$
