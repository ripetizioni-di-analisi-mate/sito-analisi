Two carts of $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ initially move together at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. An internal spring separates them. Their signed final velocities satisfy $v_1-v_2=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the external impulse is zero. What is $v_2$ in the laboratory frame?

- $-2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The relation $v_1=v_2+8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is substituted into the momentum balance. The centre of mass keeps moving forwards while the second cart reverses. The first cart leaves at $5.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and offsets the negative contribution from the second.

$$
v_2=\left(\frac{(2+3)\cdot1-2\cdot8}{2+3}\right)\,\mathrm{m/s}=-2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
