A mass $m_1=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at $x_1=0$, and a mass $m_2=7\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at $x_2=5\,\mathrm m$. Find the centre-of-mass coordinate.

- $5.38462\,\mathrm m$
- $2.69\,\mathrm m$
- $1.34615\,\mathrm m$
- $-2.69231\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Weight the coordinates by their masses. The first coordinate is zero, and the weighted mean lies between the two bodies.

$$
x_{CM}=\frac{m_1x_1+m_2x_2}{m_1+m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}x_{CM}&=\frac{6 \cdot 0 + 7 \cdot 5}{6 + 7}\\[4pt]&\simeq2.69\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
