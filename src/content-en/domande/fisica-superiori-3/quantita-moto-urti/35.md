A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart travels east at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and a $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart travels north at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. They stick. Find their speed if horizontal external impulse is zero.

::grafico{uguale=1 vettori="0,0,6,0,p_1;6,0,6,8,p_2;0,0,6,8,P" xmin=-1 xmax=8 ymin=-1 ymax=9 nomi="pₓ (kg·m/s),pᵧ (kg·m/s)" titolo="The initial momentum has two nonzero components"}

- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Each momentum component is conserved. Find the total magnitude with the Pythagorean theorem, then divide by the combined mass.

$$
v_f=\frac{\sqrt{P_x^2+P_y^2}}{m_1+m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=\frac{\sqrt{\left(2 \cdot 3\right)^{2} + \left(1 \cdot 8\right)^{2}}}{1 + 2}\\[4pt]&=\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The total mass scales both components equally. The common velocity retains the north-east direction of the total momentum. Divide its magnitude by three kilograms to find the speed.
