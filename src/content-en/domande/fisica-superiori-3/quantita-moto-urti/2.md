A constant force $F=16\,\mathrm N$ acts in the positive direction for $\Delta t=0.16\,\mathrm s$. What is the impulse?

- $5.12\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $2.56\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $1.28\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $-2.56\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The force is constant, so its graph against time is a rectangle. The impulse is the height times the duration.

$$
J=F\Delta t,\qquad
\begin{aligned}J&=16 \cdot 4 \cdot \frac{1}{25}\\[4pt]&=2.56\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}.\end{aligned}
$$

To read the value from a graph, use the entire interval: at a constant height of $16\,\mathrm N$, there is no factor of one-half as there would be for a triangle.
