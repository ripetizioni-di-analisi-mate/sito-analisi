Two objects of different masses have the same nonzero velocity vector. How do their momenta compare?

- Equal magnitudes
- Opposite directions
- Magnitudes inversely proportional to mass
- Same direction, magnitudes proportional to mass

## Explanation

For a common nonzero velocity, the ratio of magnitudes equals the mass ratio. If one body is twice as massive, for example, its momentum vector is twice as long in the same direction.
