Two objects of different masses have the same velocity vector. How do their momenta compare?

- Equal magnitudes
- Opposite directions
- Magnitudes inversely proportional to mass
- Same direction, magnitudes proportional to mass

## Explanation

The shared velocity sets the direction, and the magnitude is mass times the common speed.
