In a planar collision, external impulse points along $y$. Which total is conserved between the endpoints?

- The $y$ component
- The complete vector in all cases
- Kinetic energy by definition
- The $x$ component

## Explanation

The impulse has no $x$ component. Only the $y$ component receives the external contribution.
