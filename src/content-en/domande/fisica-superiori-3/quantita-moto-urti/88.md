In a planar collision, external impulse points along $y$. Which total is conserved between the endpoints?

- The $y$ component
- The complete vector in all cases
- Kinetic energy by definition
- The $x$ component

## Explanation

You can split the balance into components. The given impulse can change momentum along $y$. Its $x$ component is zero, so that part of momentum is unchanged even if the total vector changes.
