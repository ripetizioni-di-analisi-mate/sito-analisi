A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart arrives at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and sticks to a $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart ahead. After contact, the pair travels forwards at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglect external impulse and find the signed initial velocity of the second cart. The final direction is positive.

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The carts leave together with a total mass of $5\,\mathrm{kg}$. The first cart supplied only part of the final measured momentum. Subtracting that part leaves the second cart's positive momentum. Dividing by its mass gives a speed low enough for the first cart to catch it.

$$
u_2=\frac{(m_1+m_2)v_f-m_1u_1}{m_2}=\frac{5\cdot4-2\cdot7}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
