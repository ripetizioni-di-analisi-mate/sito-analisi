A cart of mass $m_1=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and velocity $v_1=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ strikes a stationary cart of mass $m_2=7\,\mathrm{kg}$. They stick on an ideal horizontal track. Neglect external impulse and find their common velocity.

- $4.61538\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.15385\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2.30769\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.31\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The second cart starts at rest. Initial momentum is that of the first cart and, after they stick, it belongs to their combined mass moving at one velocity.

$$
v_f=\frac{m_1v_1}{m_1+m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=6 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{6 + 7}\\[4pt]&\simeq2.31\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
