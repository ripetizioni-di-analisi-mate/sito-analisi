Two spheres have momenta $12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ east and $5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ north. Find the magnitude of their total.

- $26\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-13\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

Use the Pythagorean theorem to combine vectors because the contributions are perpendicular.

$$
P=\sqrt{P_x^2+P_y^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}P&=\sqrt{5^{2} + 12^{2}}\\[4pt]&=13\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
