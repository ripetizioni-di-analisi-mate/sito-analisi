Three pucks on a plane have zero total momentum. The first two momenta, in SI units, are $\vec p_1=(7;2)$ and $\vec p_2=(-2;-5)$. What are the components of the third puck's momentum?

- $(5;-3)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $(-5;+3)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $(-5;-3)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $(5;+3)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

First add matching components of the two known vectors. Their partial sum points right and down. The third vector must bring each component sum back to zero. Reverse both signs of the partial sum to obtain the required vector.

$$
\vec p_3=-\big((7;2)+(-2;-5)\big)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}=(-5;+3)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}.
$$
