A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ sticks to a stationary unknown mass. After the isolated collision they move at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the unknown mass.

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Dividing conserved momentum by final velocity gives the combined mass. Subtract the cart's mass.

$$
m_2=\frac{m_1v_i}{v_f}-m_1,\qquad
\begin{aligned}m_2&=\left(-1\right) \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm{kg}.\end{aligned}
$$
