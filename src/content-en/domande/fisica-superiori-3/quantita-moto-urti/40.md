A $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ skater and a $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ package are initially at rest on the ice. The skater pushes the package. The external horizontal impulse is negligible. The package moves away at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the skater’s recoil speed?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The impulse from the ice along the motion during the push is negligible. The skater's final momentum has the same magnitude as the package's.

$$
v_r=\frac{m_pv_p}{m_s},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_r&=10 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{50}\\[4pt]&=1\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The parcel carries momentum in one direction and the skater must balance it in the other. The skater has five times the parcel's mass, so recoils at one fifth of its speed.
