A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ body moving at $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ receives an impulse of $10\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. What is its final velocity component?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The positive impulse increases the velocity component by five metres per second. The increase exceeds the initial speed and motion reverses.

$$
v_f=v_i+\frac Jm,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=-3 + \frac{10}{2}\\[4pt]&=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The initial momentum is negative. Reversing the motion requires more than six newton seconds in the positive direction. Of the ten available, four remain as final momentum.
