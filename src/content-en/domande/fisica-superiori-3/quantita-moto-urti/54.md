Two bodies approach at velocities $5$ and $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on one axis. In an elastic collision, what is their relative separation speed?

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Relative speed is conserved in a one-dimensional elastic collision. The bodies initially move in opposite directions, so their speeds add.

$$
v_{\mathrm{rel}}=u_1-u_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{\mathrm{rel}}&=5 - -3\\[4pt]&=8\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
