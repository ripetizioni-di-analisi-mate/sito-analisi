The same two masses stick together in two isolated collision tests. In the second test the initial relative speed doubles. By what factor does the kinetic energy loss grow?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The lost energy is that of relative motion in the centre-of-mass frame. At fixed masses it varies with the square of the relative speed.

$$
E_2/E_1=(u_{\mathrm{rel},2}/u_{\mathrm{rel},1})^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}E_2/E_1&=2^{2}\\[4pt]&=4.\end{aligned}
$$
