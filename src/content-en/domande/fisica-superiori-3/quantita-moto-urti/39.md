Two blocks of $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ initially at rest separate due to an internal spring. The first moves right at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the second velocity component?

::grafico{assi=0 vettori="0,1,3,1,v_1;0,0,-1,0,v_2" xmin=-2 xmax=4 ymin=-1 ymax=2 titolo="The lighter body has the higher speed"}

- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Total momentum is zero before and after. The second velocity points the other way and has smaller magnitude because the second mass is greater.

$$
v_2=-\frac{m_1v_1}{m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=\left(-2\right) \cdot 3 \cdot \frac{1}{6}\\[4pt]&=-1\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
