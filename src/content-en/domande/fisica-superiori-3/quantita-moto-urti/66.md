A body has zero momentum in one inertial frame. What can happen in a second inertial frame?

- It has zero momentum in every frame
- It can be moving and have nonzero momentum
- Its mass becomes zero
- Momentum magnitude is invariant between frames

## Explanation

Velocities shift by a constant vector between classical inertial frames. Momentum therefore depends on the frame.
