Two equal-mass carts collide. Initially the first travels at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the second is at rest. With no external impulse and no energy source, which final velocity pair is impossible even though it conserves momentum?

- $(-1,5)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(2,2)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(0,4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(1,3)\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Each pair of velocities sums to $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. For equal masses the kinetic energy is proportional to the sum of the squared velocities. Starting from $16$, the pair $(-1,5)$ gives $26$, which would require an energy increase.
