In an isolated collision, two bodies have initial kinetic energy $48\,\mathrm J$ and final kinetic energy $30\,\mathrm J$. What percentage of the initial kinetic energy is dissipated?

- $75\,\%$
- $37.5\,\%$
- $18.75\,\%$
- $-37.5\,\%$

## Explanation

The energies are subtracted first. The initial energy goes in the denominator because the question asks what fraction of it was transferred during impact.

$$
\eta_{\%}=100\frac{K_i-K_f}{K_i},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\eta_{\%}&=\frac{100 \cdot \left(48 - 30\right)}{48}\\[4pt]&=37.5\,\%.\end{aligned}
$$

The pre-collision value is the base of the percentage. The loss is eighteen joules. As a check, the lost and retained shares add to one hundred percent.
