A stationary $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ platform launches a $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ block. The block moves at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the platform. Find the platform's speed in the laboratory.

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

If the platform recoils at speed $v_r$, the block's ground speed is $10-v_r$. Equating the momentum magnitudes gives the recoil speed.

$$
v_r=\frac{m_bu_{\mathrm{rel}}}{m_b+m_p},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_r&=1 \cdot 10 \cdot \frac{1}{1 + 4}\\[4pt]&=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
