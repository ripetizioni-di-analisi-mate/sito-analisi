A $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ ball travels at $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is its momentum magnitude?

- $8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

Convert to metres per second for consistency with kilograms and seconds. Thirty-six kilometres per hour is ten metres per second.

$$
p=mv,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=2 \cdot \frac{1}{5} \cdot 36 \cdot 1000 \cdot \frac{1}{3600}\\[4pt]&=4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The conversion divides by $3.6$, since a kilometre is one thousand metres and an hour is 3,600 seconds. The product is in SI units after that conversion.
