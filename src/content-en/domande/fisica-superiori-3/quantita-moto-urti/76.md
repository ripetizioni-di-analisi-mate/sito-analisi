How does the centre of mass of a closed system move when the external net force is always zero?

- At increasing speed due to internal collisions
- With the acceleration of the lightest body
- Necessarily at rest
- At constant velocity

## Explanation

The centre's velocity is total momentum divided by total mass. With zero net external force the total stays constant at every instant. The distances and velocities of individual bodies relative to the centre can still change.
