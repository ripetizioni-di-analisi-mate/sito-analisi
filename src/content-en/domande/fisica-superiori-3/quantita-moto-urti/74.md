Two isolated carts have individual momenta that change during contact. How are those changes related?

- They are always identical vectors
- They are opposite vectors
- They are proportional to the squared masses
- Both must be zero

## Explanation

The collision impulse adds a component to one body and subtracts it from the other. This follows from Newton's third law over the same time interval. Adding both changes leaves the total momentum at its pre-collision value.
