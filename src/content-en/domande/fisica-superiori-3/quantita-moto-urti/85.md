In an elastic oblique collision in a plane, only initial velocities and masses are known. What additional information can be needed?

- An exit direction or contact geometry
- The unit of mass
- The zero of potential energy
- The length of a day

## Explanation

In a plane each body can have two unknown final velocity components. Two momentum balances and the energy balance supply three constraints. A measured exit direction or contact geometry can close the system.
