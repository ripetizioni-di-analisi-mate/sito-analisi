In an elastic oblique collision in a plane, only initial velocities and masses are known. What additional information can be needed?

- An exit direction or contact geometry
- The unit of mass
- The zero of potential energy
- The length of a day

## Explanation

The two momentum component equations and the energy equation give three constraints on four final components. Geometry adds a constraint.
