A $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ system moves at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and separates without external impulse. A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ fragment continues at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the velocity of the other fragment?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The first fragment carries all the system's initial momentum. The second has zero momentum and velocity in the specified frame.

$$
v_2=\frac{P_i-m_1v_1}{m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=\frac{- 4 \cdot 5 + 10 \cdot 2}{6}\\[4pt]&=0\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The first piece already carries all twenty kilogram metres per second of the system. The second mass is positive, so zero momentum means it is at rest in the laboratory.
