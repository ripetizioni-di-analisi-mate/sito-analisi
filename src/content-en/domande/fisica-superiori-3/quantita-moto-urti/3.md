An object of mass $m=0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and initial speed $v=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes to rest in $\Delta t=0.16\,\mathrm s$. Find the magnitude of the mean net force. Choose the closest value.

- $43.8\,\mathrm N$
- $0.560\,\mathrm N$
- $21.9\,\mathrm N$
- $3.50\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Final momentum is zero. The change magnitude is $mv$, so the magnitude of the mean net force is $mv$ divided by the duration.

$$
F_m=\frac{mv}{\Delta t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_m&=\frac{0.5\cdot7}{0.16}\,\mathrm N\\[4pt]&\simeq21.9\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$

With the incoming direction positive, the average net force would have a negative component. The question asks for a magnitude, so the braking force is reported as a positive number.
