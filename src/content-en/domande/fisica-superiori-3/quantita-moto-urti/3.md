An object of mass $m=0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and initial speed $v=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes to rest in $\Delta t=0.16\,\mathrm s$. Find the magnitude of the mean net force.

- $43.75\,\mathrm N$
- $10.9375\,\mathrm N$
- $21.9\,\mathrm N$
- $-21.875\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Final momentum is zero. The change magnitude is $mv$, so the magnitude of the mean net force is $mv$ divided by the duration.

$$
F_m=\frac{mv}{\Delta t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_m&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 7 \cdot \frac{1}{4 \cdot \frac{1}{25}}\\[4pt]&\simeq21.9\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
