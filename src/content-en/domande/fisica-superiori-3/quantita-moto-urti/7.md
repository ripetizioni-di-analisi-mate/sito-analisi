A mass $m_1=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $v_{1i}=4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides elastically in one dimension with a stationary mass $m_2=6\,\mathrm{kg}$. With no external impulse, which value best approximates the final velocity of the second mass?

- $4.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.09\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.409\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy and momentum are conserved. With the second body at rest initially, combining the equations gives a factor $2m_1$ in the numerator.

$$
v_{2f}=\frac{2m_1v_{1i}}{m_1+m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_{2f}&=\frac{2\cdot5\cdot4.5}{5+6}\,\mathrm{m/s}\\[4pt]&\simeq4.09\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The lighter mass rebounds at a low speed. The second cart gains most of the initial momentum and travels in the original direction of the first.
