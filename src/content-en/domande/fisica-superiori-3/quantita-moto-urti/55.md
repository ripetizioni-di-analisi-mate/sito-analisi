A body hits an effectively immovable wall elastically at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its velocity after this head-on impact with positive towards the wall.

- $-14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The wall stays essentially at rest. The normal component changes sign but keeps its magnitude in the elastic collision.

$$
v_f=-v_i,\qquad
v_f=-7\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
