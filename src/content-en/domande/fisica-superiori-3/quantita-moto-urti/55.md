A body hits an effectively immovable wall elastically at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The impact is head-on and the positive direction points towards the wall. What is the body's velocity after the impact?

- $-14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The wall stays essentially at rest. The normal component changes sign but keeps its magnitude in the elastic collision.

$$
v_f=-v_i,\qquad
v_f=-7\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The wall gives the mass an impulse opposite its initial motion. In the fixed-wall model, elasticity preserves speed and normal incidence reverses the entire velocity.
