A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass moves right at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and sticks to a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass arriving from the right. They come to rest. What was the second speed if external impulse was negligible?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With negligible external impulse, coming to rest implies zero initial total momentum. Initial momenta have equal magnitudes and opposite directions.

$$
v_2=\frac{m_1v_1}{m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_2&=5 \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=5\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$
