Two $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ masses arrive at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in opposite directions and stick. Find the kinetic energy transferred to other forms if external impulse is negligible.

::grafico{assi=0 vettori="-6,0,-1,0,v_1;6,0,1,0,v_2" xmin=-7 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=2 titolo="The two vectors have opposite directions and equal magnitudes"}

- $100\,\mathrm J$
- $50\,\mathrm J$
- $25\,\mathrm J$
- $-50\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Initial momenta cancel, so the shared final velocity is zero. Both initial energies are positive and their sum is transferred.

$$
E_{\mathrm{loss}}=K_{1i}+K_{2i},\qquad
\begin{aligned}E_{\mathrm{loss}}&=\frac12\cdot2\cdot5^2\,\mathrm J+\frac12\cdot2\cdot5^2\,\mathrm J\\[4pt]&=50\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$

The initial velocities are opposite and the masses equal, so the joined block stays at rest. The whole initial kinetic energy is converted to internal energy.
