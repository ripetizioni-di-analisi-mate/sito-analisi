Two fragments of $3$ and $9\,\mathrm{kg}$ separate from rest with no external impulse. Find the ratio of their kinetic energies, first to second.

- $3$
- $6$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The momentum magnitudes agree. Writing energy as momentum squared divided by twice the mass shows that the greater mass has lower energy.

$$
\frac{K_1}{K_2}=\frac{m_2}{m_1},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\frac{K_1}{K_2}&=\frac{9}{3}\\[4pt]&=3.\end{aligned}
$$

At a fixed momentum magnitude, writing kinetic energy as $p^2/(2m)$ shows why the lighter fragment receives a greater share of the energy.
