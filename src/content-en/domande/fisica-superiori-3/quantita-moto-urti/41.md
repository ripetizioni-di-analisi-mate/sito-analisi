Two fragments of $3$ and $9\,\mathrm{kg}$ separate from rest. Find the ratio of their kinetic energies, first to second.

- $3$
- $6$
- $1.5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The momentum magnitudes agree. Writing energy as momentum squared divided by twice the mass shows that the greater mass has lower energy.

$$
K_1/K_2=\frac{m_2}{m_1},\qquad
\begin{aligned}K_1/K_2&=\frac{9}{3}\\[4pt]&=3.\end{aligned}
$$
