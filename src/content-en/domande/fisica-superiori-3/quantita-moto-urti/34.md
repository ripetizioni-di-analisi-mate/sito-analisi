A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ meets and sticks to a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass at $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting external impulse, what is their shared velocity?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The second mass contributes negative momentum. The net value is positive, so the combined body moves in the first cart's direction.

$$
v_f=\frac{m_1v_1+m_2v_2}{m_1+m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_f&=\frac{- 1 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 4}{2 + 3}\\[4pt]&=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The cart approaching from the right contributes negative momentum. The net momentum remains positive since the first contribution is greater, so both leave moving right.
