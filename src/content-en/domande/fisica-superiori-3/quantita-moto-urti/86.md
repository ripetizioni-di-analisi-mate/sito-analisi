Why is dividing the elastic energy equation by the momentum equation valid only for a nonzero momentum exchange?

- Otherwise velocity is not measured in metres per second
- Otherwise the divisor is zero
- Otherwise mass changes
- Otherwise the geometry must be two-dimensional

## Explanation

The momentum equation expresses the exchange between bodies. For zero exchange both sides vanish and the quotient is undefined.
