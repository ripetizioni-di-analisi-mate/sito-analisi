Two pads stop the same object from the same speed in $0.10$ and $0.25\,\mathrm s$. Find the ratio of average force magnitudes for the shorter interval to the longer one.

- $5$
- $1.25$
- $2.5$
- $-2.5$

## Explanation

The two tests have the same initial and final momenta. At fixed impulse, mean force is inversely proportional to duration.

$$
F_1/F_2=\frac{\Delta t_2}{\Delta t_1},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_1/F_2&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{1 \cdot \frac{1}{10}}\\[4pt]&=2.5.\end{aligned}
$$

The numerator is the same in both trials, so the force ratio is the inverse ratio of times. The padding with longer contact produces the smaller average force.
