A uniform $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ rod extends from $x=0$ to $x=6\,\mathrm m$. A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ point mass is attached at the right end. What is the system's centre-of-mass coordinate?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The rod's uniform distribution puts its own centre of mass at its midpoint. In the numerator the rod contributes as if its mass were concentrated at three metres. The load at six metres shifts the mean rightwards, keeping it closer to the centre of the heavier component.

$$
x_{CM}=\frac{4\cdot3+2\cdot6}{4+2}\,\mathrm{m}=4\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
