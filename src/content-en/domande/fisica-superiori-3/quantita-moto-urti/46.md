A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass is at $-2\,\mathrm m$ and a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass at $5\,\mathrm m$. Find the centre-of-mass coordinate.

- $1.6\,\mathrm m$
- $0.8\,\mathrm m$
- $0.4\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The first product is negative because that mass is left of the origin. The positive sum places the centre to the right of the origin.

$$
x_{CM}=\frac{m_1x_1+m_2x_2}{m_1+m_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}x_{CM}&=\frac{3 \cdot \left(-2\right) + 2 \cdot 5}{2 + 3}\\[4pt]&=0.8\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
