Two bodies separate from rest under an internal spring. Where does the final kinetic energy come from?

- From lack of external impulse
- From the choice of axis
- From an increase in total mass
- From stored elastic energy

## Explanation

The spring does work on both bodies and loses potential energy. Internal impulses cancel in total momentum even while kinetic energies rise.
