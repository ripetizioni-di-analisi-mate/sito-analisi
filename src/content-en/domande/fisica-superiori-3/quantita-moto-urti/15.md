A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart travels left at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With right taken as positive, what is $p_x$?

- $-24\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity component is negative and mass is positive. The momentum has the same sign as the velocity.

$$
p_x=mv_x,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p_x&=\left(-4\right) \cdot 3\\[4pt]&=-12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

You can write $v_x=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directly and multiply by four kilograms. The magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ measures the size, while the negative component records leftward motion.
