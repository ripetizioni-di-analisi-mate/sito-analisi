A cart turns without changing speed. Which quantity determines the net impulse?

- Just the change in kinetic energy
- The initial momentum magnitude
- The vector difference of final and initial momentum
- The average speed

## Explanation

The components at the start and at the end of the bend are compared. If the arrow tips are at different points, the directed segment joining them represents the impulse, even when the arrows have equal lengths.
