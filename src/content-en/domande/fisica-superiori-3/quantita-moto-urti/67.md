A cart turns without changing speed. Which quantity determines the net impulse?

- Just the change in kinetic energy
- The initial momentum magnitude
- The vector difference of final and initial momentum
- The average speed

## Explanation

Velocity changes direction, so momentum changes even at constant speed. Impulse equals that vector change.
