The centre of mass has coordinate $8\,\mathrm m$. The origin shifts $3\,\mathrm m$ to the right while the positive direction is kept. Find the new coordinate.

- $10\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$
- $-5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

All positions measured from the new origin decrease by three metres. The weighted average changes by the same amount.

$$
x'_{CM}=x_{CM}-x_0,\qquad
\begin{aligned}x'_{CM}&=8 - 3\\[4pt]&=5\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
