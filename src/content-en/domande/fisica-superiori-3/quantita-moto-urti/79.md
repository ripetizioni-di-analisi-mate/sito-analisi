Which observation defines a perfectly inelastic collision?

- The bodies have equal mass
- Initial momentum is zero
- The bodies stick together after contact
- External impulse is positive

## Explanation

When they stick, the bodies share one velocity after contact. Their final relative motion is therefore zero. This distinguishes a perfectly inelastic collision from a collision in which energy is lost but the bodies subsequently separate.
