Two force curves have equal signed areas and equal durations but different peaks. How do the average forces compare?

- They are equal
- The curve with the higher peak has the larger mean
- They depend only on final velocity
- They equal the peaks

## Explanation

Divide the signed area by the duration to get the height of the equivalent rectangle. Matching both measurements gives matching averages. Knowing only the average leaves the time of the peak undetermined.
