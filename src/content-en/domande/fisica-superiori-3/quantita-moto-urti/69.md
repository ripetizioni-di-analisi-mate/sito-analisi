Two force curves have equal area and duration but different peaks. How do the average forces compare?

- They are equal
- The curve with the higher peak has the larger mean
- They depend only on final velocity
- They equal the peaks

## Explanation

The area divided by duration is the average force. The peak can vary independently of that ratio.
