Two force curves have equal signed areas and equal durations but different peaks. How do the average forces compare?

- They are equal
- The curve with the higher peak has the larger mean
- They depend only on final velocity
- They equal the peaks

## Explanation

The signed area divided by the duration gives the height of the equivalent rectangle. Matching both measurements gives matching averages. Knowing only the average leaves the time of the peak undetermined.
