Kinetic energy decreases when bodies stick. Which quantity can be conserved independently of this decrease?

- Relative velocity
- Momentum if external impulse is zero
- The kinetic energy of each body
- The magnitude of each individual momentum

## Explanation

Momentum balance depends on the external impulse. The energy loss reflects the removal of relative motion when the bodies stick. Some initial kinetic energy becomes internal energy while the common velocity satisfies the momentum balance.
