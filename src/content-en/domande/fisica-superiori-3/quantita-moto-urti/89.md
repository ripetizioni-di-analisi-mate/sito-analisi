Comparing two collisions with the same internal forces, what change can make the system less isolated along a rough track?

- Longer contact under the same external resistance
- Changing the force unit
- Reversing the positive direction
- Shifting the axis origin

## Explanation

If resistance keeps its direction, its accumulated impulse grows with duration. Coordinate conventions describe the same event.
