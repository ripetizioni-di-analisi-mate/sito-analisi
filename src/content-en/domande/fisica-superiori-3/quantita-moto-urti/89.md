In two trials the internal impulse exchange has magnitude $4\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. An external horizontal drag of constant magnitude $5\,\mathrm N$ acts throughout the collisions, which last $4\,\mathrm{ms}$ and $12\,\mathrm{ms}$ respectively. For which collisions is its impulse at most $1\%$ of the internal exchange?

- Only the first
- Both
- Only the second
- Neither

## Explanation

One hundredth of the internal exchange is $0.04\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. Converting the milliseconds gives external impulses of $0.020$ and $0.060\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. Only the first is within the limit. The threshold measures error against the exchange scale, even when total momentum is close to zero.
