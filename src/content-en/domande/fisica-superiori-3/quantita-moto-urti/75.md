Where can the centre-of-mass coordinate of three positive masses on a line lie?

- Always at the leftmost point
- Always at the unweighted arithmetic mean
- Between the extreme coordinates
- Beyond the rightmost point if masses are large

## Explanation

The formula is an average with positive weights that sum to one. Such an average lies between the minimum and maximum.
