Two equal-mass pucks collide elastically in a plane with negligible external impulse. Initially only one is moving. Both have nonzero velocities after contact. What angle do the two final velocity vectors make?

- $0^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

Momentum balance equates the initial velocity vector to the sum of the two final vectors. Kinetic energy balance equates its squared magnitude to the sum of their squared magnitudes. Both equations hold when the dot product vanishes. The two vectors are nonzero, so they are perpendicular.

$$
|\vec u|^2=|\vec v_1+\vec v_2|^2=v_1^2+v_2^2+2\vec v_1\cdot\vec v_2,\qquad \vec v_1\cdot\vec v_2=0.
$$
