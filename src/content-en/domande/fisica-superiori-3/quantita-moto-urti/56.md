A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ hits a stationary $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass elastically. What is the second body's final kinetic energy?

- $64\,\mathrm J$
- $16\,\mathrm J$
- $-32\,\mathrm J$
- $32\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Momentum balance and the relative-speed relation give four metres per second for the second body. Combining its mass and squared speed gives its energy.

$$
K_2=\frac12m_2\left(\frac{2m_1u_1}{m_1+m_2}\right)^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}K_2&=\frac{4 \cdot \left(2 \cdot 2 \cdot 6 \cdot \frac{1}{2 + 4}\right)^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=32\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
