The centre of mass of two bodies is exactly midway between their distinct positions. What follows?

- The two masses are equal
- The velocities are equal
- The second mass is twice as large
- The first mass is zero

## Explanation

With the origin at the midpoint, the two coordinates have equal magnitudes and opposite signs. For the centre of mass to be at that origin, the products of mass and coordinate must balance exactly. With equal nonzero distances, this requires equal masses.
