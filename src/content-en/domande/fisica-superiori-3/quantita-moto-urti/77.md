The centre of mass of two bodies is exactly midway between their distinct positions. What follows?

- The two masses are equal
- The velocities are equal
- The second mass is twice as large
- The first mass is zero

## Explanation

With the origin at the midpoint, equal distances give cancelling mass–distance products only when the masses are equal.
