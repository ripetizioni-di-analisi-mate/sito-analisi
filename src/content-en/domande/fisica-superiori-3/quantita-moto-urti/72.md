Why does the weight of two carts on an ideal horizontal track leave horizontal conservation intact?

- Weight acts only during collisions
- Weight cancels the mass of the system
- Weight is internal to the two carts
- Weight has zero horizontal projection

## Explanation

Along the track the horizontal impulse component matters. A vertical force has no horizontal component.
