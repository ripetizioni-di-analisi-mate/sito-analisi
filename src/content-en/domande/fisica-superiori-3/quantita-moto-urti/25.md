A force–time graph has signed area $6\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$ over $0.50\,\mathrm s$. Which constant force over the same interval would deliver the same impulse?

- $12\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $6\,\mathrm N$
- $-12\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The equivalent constant force gives a rectangle of the same area. The mean force is that area divided by the duration.

$$
F_{\mathrm m}=\frac J{\Delta t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_{\mathrm m}&=\frac{6}{1 \cdot \frac{1}{2}}\\[4pt]&=12\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$

The equivalent force is the height of a rectangle half a second wide with area six newton seconds. The original graph may have peaks above that height.
