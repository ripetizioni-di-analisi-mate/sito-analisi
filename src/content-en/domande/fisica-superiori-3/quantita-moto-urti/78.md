In which inertial frame does an isolated system have zero total momentum?

- Every frame
- The frame moving with the centre of mass
- Only the frame where the lighter body is at rest
- The frame where the velocity sum is greatest

## Explanation

Changing to the centre-of-mass frame subtracts the mass-weighted average from every velocity. The remaining contributions sum to zero. In an isolated system that average is constant, so the frame can stay inertial throughout the collision.
