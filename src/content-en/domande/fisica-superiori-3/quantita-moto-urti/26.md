Two perpendicular forces of $3$ and $4\,\mathrm N$ act together for $0.20\,\mathrm s$. Find the magnitude of the net impulse.

- $2\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The net force magnitude is five newtons. The impulses are simultaneous and perpendicular, so multiply that net magnitude by the common duration.

$$
J=\Delta t\sqrt{F_x^2+F_y^2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}J&=\frac{\sqrt{3^{2} + 4^{2}}}{5}\\[4pt]&=1\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}.\end{aligned}
$$

The forces act over the same interval, so you can find the resultant first and multiply its magnitude by the time. The two contributions are perpendicular.
