Which product of units equals kilogram metres per second?

- Newton-second $\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- Joule per second
- Newton-metre
- Joule per second squared

## Explanation

Substitute the definition of a newton and multiply by seconds to obtain $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$.
