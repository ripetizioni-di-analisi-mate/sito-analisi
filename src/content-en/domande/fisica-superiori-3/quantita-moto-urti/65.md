Which product of units equals kilogram metres per second?

- Newton-second $\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- Joule per second
- Newton-metre
- Joule per second squared

## Explanation

The newton is replaced with $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$ and multiplied by a second. This cancels one time factor in the denominator. The resulting $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ is the unit of momentum, so an impulse and the change it produces can be stated in the same unit.
