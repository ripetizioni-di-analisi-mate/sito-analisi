A $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ hits a stationary $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass elastically. What is the first body's signed final velocity?

- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The first mass is lighter, so the coefficient is negative. Its rebound leaves the second mass moving positively.

$$
v_1=\frac{m_1-m_2}{m_1+m_2}u_1,\qquad
\begin{aligned}v_1&=\left(1 - 3\right) \cdot 4 \cdot \frac{1}{1 + 3}\\[4pt]&=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}.\end{aligned}
$$

::grafico{assi=0 vettori="0,1,4,1,u_1;0,0,-2,0,v_1;0,-1,2,-1,v_2" xmin=-3 xmax=5 ymin=-2 ymax=2 titolo="The lighter mass rebounds while the heavier one moves forward"}

As a check, the second mass leaves at two metres per second. Its positive momentum is larger in magnitude than the first mass's negative momentum.
