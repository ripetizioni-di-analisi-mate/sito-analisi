Two $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ carts stick on an isolated track. The first moves at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the second is stationary. Find the kinetic energy lost.

- $36\,\mathrm J$
- $72\,\mathrm J$
- $18\,\mathrm J$
- $-36\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Momentum conservation gives a shared speed of three metres per second. Compare the energy of the single moving mass before impact with the energy of both together after.

$$
E_{\mathrm{loss}}=K_i-K_f,\qquad
\begin{aligned}E_{\mathrm{loss}}&=- \frac{3^{2} \cdot \left(4 + 4\right)}{2} + \frac{4 \cdot 6^{2}}{2}\\[4pt]&=36\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
