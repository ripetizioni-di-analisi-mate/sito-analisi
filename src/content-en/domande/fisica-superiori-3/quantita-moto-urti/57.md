Two $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ carts stick together on a track with no external impulse. They lose $18\,\mathrm J$ of kinetic energy in the collision. What is the magnitude of their relative velocity before the collision?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Sticking removes the relative motion and preserves the motion of the centre of mass. With two equal two-kilogram masses, the coefficient of $(u_1-u_2)^2$ is half a kilogram. The magnitude is the non-negative square root of the lost energy divided by that coefficient.

$$
|u_1-u_2|=\sqrt{\frac{2(m_1+m_2)\Delta K}{m_1m_2}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot4\cdot18}{2\cdot2}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
