A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ body slows from $7$ to $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $0.40\,\mathrm s$. It keeps its direction. What is the mean net force magnitude?

- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $-20\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The body keeps its direction and part of its initial momentum, so the magnitude calculation uses the speed difference.

$$
F_m=\frac{m(v_i-v_f)}{\Delta t},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_m&=\frac{2 \cdot \left(7 - 3\right)}{2 \cdot \frac{1}{5}}\\[4pt]&=20\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$

The velocity keeps its direction after slowing. The numerator therefore contains the difference of the two speeds. For a rebound the magnitude would use their sum.
