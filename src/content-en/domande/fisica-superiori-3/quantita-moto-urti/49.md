A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass stays at $x=0$ while a $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass is moved from $x=5$ to $x=7\,\mathrm m$. How far does the system's centre of mass move?

- $1.2\,\mathrm m$
- $2.4\,\mathrm m$
- $0.6\,\mathrm m$
- $-1.2\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Only the moved mass changes the numerator. Its share of the total mass multiplies its displacement.

$$
\Delta x_{CM}=\frac{m_2\Delta x_2}{M},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta x_{CM}&=\frac{3 \cdot \left(7 - 5\right)}{2 + 3}\\[4pt]&=1.2\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
