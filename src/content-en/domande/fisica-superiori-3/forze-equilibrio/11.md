A sled slides over a fixed horizontal surface. The normal force on the first runner is $20\,\mathrm N$ and on the second it is $30\,\mathrm N$. Their kinetic friction coefficients are $0.10$ and $0.15$, respectively. What is the magnitude $f_d$ of the total friction force?

- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Use the normal force on each runner separately. The contributions are $0.10\cdot20=2$ and $0.15\cdot30=4.5$ newtons. Both oppose the same sliding, so their magnitudes add. Using one coefficient for the entire normal force would ignore how the load is shared.

$$
f_d=6.5\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
