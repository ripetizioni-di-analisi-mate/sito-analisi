An object of mass $m=3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from two ideal symmetric strings making $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. Find the tension in each string. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $14.7\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $T=29.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $58.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $44.1\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

By symmetry the horizontal components cancel each other out. The two vertical components $T\sin\theta$ support the weight.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T&=\frac{mg}{2\sin\theta}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 29.4\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
