On a $60^\circ$ slope the body weighs $80\,\mathrm N$ and no other normal forces act. Find the normal force while contact lasts.

- $80\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $40 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The body has no acceleration perpendicular to the plane. The normal force balances only the component of weight pushing into the surface. The cosine of sixty degrees is one half, so the normal force equals half the weight rather than all of it.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=P\cos(60^\circ)=80\cdot\frac12\\[4pt]
&= 40\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
