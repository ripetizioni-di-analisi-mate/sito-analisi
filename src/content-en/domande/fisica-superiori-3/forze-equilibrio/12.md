An object is sliding downhill on a slope at $30^\circ$, with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_d=0.14$. Find the signed acceleration, positive down the slope. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $1.86\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $7.44\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $5.58\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $a=3.72\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The component $mg\sin\theta$ points down the slope. Kinetic friction opposes the sliding motion and equals $\mu_dmg\cos\theta$. Divide the net force by the mass.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=g(\sin\theta-\mu_d\cos\theta)\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.72\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
