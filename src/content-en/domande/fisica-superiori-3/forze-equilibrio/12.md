A block moves uphill on a ramp with initial speed $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slope has $\sin\theta=0.60$ and $\cos\theta=0.80$. Use $\mu_d=0.25$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What distance $s$ does it travel along the ramp before its speed reaches zero?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

While the block moves uphill, both the weight component and friction point downhill. Taking uphill as positive gives $a=-10(0.60+0.25\cdot0.80)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Use $0=4^2+2as$ to find the distance. Continuing the calculation beyond the stop would require checking static friction. The negative acceleration shows that the uphill component of velocity decreases during this stage.

$$
s=1\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
