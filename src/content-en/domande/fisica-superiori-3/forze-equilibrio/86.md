A beam has rotational balance because total torque is zero. What else must be checked for static equilibrium?

- That the beam is massless.
- That net force is zero and the beam is at rest.
- That every force is vertical.
- That the fulcrum is at the geometric centre.

## Explanation

Torque balance prevents angular acceleration. Translational balance still requires checking forces, and static equilibrium concerns a body at rest. A force whose line of action passes through the pivot can have zero torque yet accelerate the centre of mass. Force balance must therefore be checked separately.
