An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is on a slope at $30^\circ$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, which value is closest to the magnitude of the component of its weight along the slope?

- $24.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12.25\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $49\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $36.75\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Weight acts vertically. In its projection along the plane, the parallel side is opposite the slope angle, so use the sine. The sine of thirty degrees is one half, so the component along the slope is half the weight. The normal force plays no part in resolving the weight vector.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\parallel&=mg\sin\theta\\[4pt]
&\simeq 24.5\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
