A $20\,\mathrm N$ force acts $0.40\,\mathrm m$ from a pivot at $30^\circ$ to the position from the pivot. What is the torque magnitude?

- $4\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $4 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.

## Explanation

Radial distance differs from moment arm when force is oblique. The arm is the component of radius perpendicular to the line of action, here $0.20\,\mathrm m$. Equivalently multiply the full radial distance by the ten-newton transverse component. Both methods give the same torque.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=rF\sin\theta=0.40\cdot20\cdot\frac12\\[4pt]
&= 4\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}
\end{aligned}
$$
