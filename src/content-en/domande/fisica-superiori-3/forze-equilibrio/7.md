A force of magnitude $F=16\,\mathrm N$ acts perpendicular to a lever at distance $b=0.26\,\mathrm m$ from its pivot. What is the magnitude of the torque?

- $2.08\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $8.32\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $4.16\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $6.24\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.

## Explanation

The force is perpendicular to the lever, so the distance from the pivot is the moment arm. Torque is force times moment arm. Here the position and force are at right angles. Their units multiply, so the result is in newton metres. Giving it a sign would also require the direction of the force relative to the pivot.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=bF\\[4pt]
&= 4.16\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}
\end{aligned}
$$
