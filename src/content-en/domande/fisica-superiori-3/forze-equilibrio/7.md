A force of magnitude $F=16\,\mathrm N$ acts perpendicular to a lever at distance $b=0.26\,\mathrm m$ from its pivot. Find the magnitude of the torque.

- $2.08\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $8.32\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $M=4.16\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $6.24\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.

## Explanation

The force is perpendicular to the lever, so the distance from the pivot is the moment arm. Torque is force times moment arm.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=bF\\[4pt]
&= 4.16\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}
\end{aligned}
$$
