A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ body slides on a horizontal plane with $\mu_d=0.20$. A rope also pulls upward with component $10\,\mathrm N$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find the friction magnitude.

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="-0.6,0 0.6,0 0.6,0.7 -0.6,0.7" segmenti="-2,0,2,0" vettori="0,0.3,0,-1.2,P;0,0.3,0,1.5,N;0,0.3,0,0.65,T_y;0,0.3,-0.6,0.3,f" xmin=-2.2 xmax=2.2 ymin=-1.5 ymax=2 titolo="Weight, normal force and vertical tension enter the normal balance"}

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The body has no normal acceleration. The rope supports ten of the fifty newtons of weight, leaving forty newtons of normal force. Friction depends on this normal force rather than directly on weight. Its direction follows from the sliding relative to the surface.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=5\cdot10-10=40\,\mathrm N\\[4pt]f_d&=0.20\cdot40\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
