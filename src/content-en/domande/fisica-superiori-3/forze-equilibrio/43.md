A box weighs $60\,\mathrm N$ and has an extra $20\,\mathrm N$ downward force. It slides on a horizontal surface with $\mu_d=0.30$. Find the friction magnitude.

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Because contact prevents vertical acceleration, the normal force balances both downward forces. Apply the kinetic coefficient to that eighty-newton normal force.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_d&=0.30(60+20)\\[4pt]
&= 24\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
