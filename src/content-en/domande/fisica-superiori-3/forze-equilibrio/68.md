Only two nonzero forces act on a stationary knot. What arrangement allows equilibrium?

- Equal magnitudes and perpendicular.
- Along the same line and same direction.
- Any angle as long as magnitudes agree.
- Equal magnitudes and opposite along the same line.

## Explanation

Two vectors sum to zero exactly when one is the negative of the other. Equal magnitudes at any angle other than $180^\circ$ would leave a resultant. For a junction modelled as a particle, the vectors share an origin. Rotating one force while keeping its magnitude breaks the balance in at least one component.
