The end of a $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring is compressed by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Take extension as positive. What is the spring force component?

::grafico{f="-200*x" punti="-0.03,6,pieno" xmin=-0.045 xmax=0.045 ymin=-10 ymax=10 nomi="x,F_e" titolo="The restoring force is positive in compression"}

- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Displacement from natural length is negative because the spring is compressed. Convert three centimetres to three hundredths of a metre before multiplying. Hooke's minus sign reverses the sign of displacement, so force points towards extension.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_e&=-kx=-200(-0.03)\\[4pt]
&= 6\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
