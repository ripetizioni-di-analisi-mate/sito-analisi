A downward force acts to the right of the pivot on a horizontal lever viewed from the front. With anticlockwise positive, what is the torque sign?

- Positive.
- Negative.
- Zero.
- Undetermined even though the side of the lever is known.

## Explanation

Pushing down on the right-hand side tends to turn the lever clockwise. Under the specified convention this sense is negative. The position is to the right of the pivot and the vertical component is negative if upwards is positive. Multiplying those two components gives the same sign.
