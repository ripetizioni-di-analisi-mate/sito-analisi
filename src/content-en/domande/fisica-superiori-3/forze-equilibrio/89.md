A light horizontal $4\,\mathrm m$ beam has supports at both ends. A $120\,\mathrm N$ load is $1\,\mathrm m$ from the left end. Find the right reaction.

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The left reaction has no torque about the left support. The load has one metre of arm and the right reaction four, with opposite rotational directions. Thirty newtons on the right balance torque, leaving ninety newtons at the support nearer the load.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_R&=\frac{120\cdot1}4\\[4pt]
&= 30\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
