Why does spring force have the opposite sign to deformation in Hooke's law?

- Mass becomes negative.
- The spring tends to return its end to natural length.
- The force sign always means downward.
- The spring constant changes sign.

## Explanation

Extension moves the end away from its natural position. Restoring force points back towards that position and so acts along the same axis in the opposite direction.
