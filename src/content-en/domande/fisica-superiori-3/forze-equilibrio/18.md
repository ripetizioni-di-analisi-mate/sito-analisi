Why does spring force have the opposite sign to deformation in Hooke's law?

- Mass becomes negative.
- The spring tends to return its end to natural length.
- The force sign always means downward.
- The spring constant changes sign.

## Explanation

Extension moves the end away from its natural position. Restoring force points back towards that position and so acts along the same axis in the opposite direction. When the spring is compressed, its end lies on the other side of the natural position. The restoring force then points in the positive direction of extension.
