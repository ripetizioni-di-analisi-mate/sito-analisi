The application point moves along the same line of action without changing the force vector. How does torque about the pivot change for a rigid body?

- It stays the same.
- It always vanishes.
- It always grows.
- It depends on position along the line even though the arm is the same.

## Explanation

The perpendicular distance from the line to the pivot is unchanged. So are force magnitude and rotational sense. In the rigid-body model only the position along the line changes. Moving the force to a different parallel line could change its lever arm.
