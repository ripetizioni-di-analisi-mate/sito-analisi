A stationary crate has $N=50\,\mathrm N$ and $\mu_s=0.40$. A horizontal force of $12\,\mathrm N$ pulls it. Find static friction magnitude.

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Equilibrium requires twelve newtons of opposing friction. The twenty-newton limit exceeds that demand, so the body can remain at rest. The limit is not the force that develops in every case.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_s&=F=12,\qquad f_{s,\max}=0.40\cdot50=20\\[4pt]
&= 12\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
