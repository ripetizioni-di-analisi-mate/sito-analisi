Two equal and opposite forces act along distinct parallel lines. What follows about resultant force and total torque?

- The resultant is zero and total torque is nonzero.
- Both are zero.
- The resultant is doubled and torque is zero.
- Resultant and torque both double the force itself.

## Explanation

The arrows cancel as vectors. Their lines are separated, so they form a couple of magnitude force times the distance between their lines. The total torque is the same about any pivot. Shifting the pivot would change the total torque only if the resultant were nonzero.
