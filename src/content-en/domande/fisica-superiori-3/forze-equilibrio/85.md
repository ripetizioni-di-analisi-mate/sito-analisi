Two equal and opposite forces act along distinct parallel lines. What follows about resultant force and total torque?

- The resultant is zero and total torque is nonzero.
- Both are zero.
- The resultant is doubled and torque is zero.
- Resultant and torque both double the force itself.

## Explanation

The arrows cancel as vectors. Their lines are separated, so they form a couple of magnitude force times the distance between their lines.
