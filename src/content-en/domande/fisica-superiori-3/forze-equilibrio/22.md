Reliable readings show that doubling the extension requires triple the force over a given interval. Which assumption needs review?

- That force is measured in newtons.
- That the same constant $k$ applies throughout the interval.
- That extension is measured in metres.
- That the spring has an axis.

## Explanation

Linear proportionality requires force to double with extension. The force-to-extension ratio has changed, and a single constant does not fit the data.
