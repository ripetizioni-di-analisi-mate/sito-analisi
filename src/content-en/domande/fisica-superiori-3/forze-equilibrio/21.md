An applied-force versus extension graph doubles its slope. What unit does that slope retain?

- $\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $\mathrm{m/N}$.
- $\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

The slope divides a change of force by a change of length. Changing its value does not change the ratio's units.
