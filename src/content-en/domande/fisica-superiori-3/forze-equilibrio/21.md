An applied-force versus extension graph doubles its slope. What unit does that slope retain?

- $\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $\mathrm{m/N}$.
- $\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

The slope divides a change of force by a change of length. Changing its value does not change the ratio's units. With length marked in centimetres, the slope reads in newtons per centimetre. Convert it to newtons per metre before comparing values.
