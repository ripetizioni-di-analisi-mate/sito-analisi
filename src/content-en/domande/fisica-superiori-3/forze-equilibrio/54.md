A block weighing $50\,\mathrm N$ stays on a slope with $\cos\theta=0.80$. In trial V it is pulled vertically upwards with $12\,\mathrm N$. In trial P it is pulled outwards perpendicular to the slope with $12\,\mathrm N$. What is $N_V-N_P$?

- $-2.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The outward component of the vertical force is $12\cdot0.80=9.6$ newtons. In trial P, the full $12\,\mathrm N$ reduces the normal force. The inward weight component is the same, so subtracting the two normal forces leaves the difference between the reductions.

$$
N_V-N_P=2.4\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
