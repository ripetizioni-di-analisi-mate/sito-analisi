An extended body receives two equal vertical forces, upward left of the pivot and downward to its right. Why do both torques have the same sign?

- Every vertical force always gives positive torque.
- Reversing both side and force direction keeps the same sense of rotation.
- Zero resultant forces torque to be positive.
- Distance from the pivot changes the sign of the force itself.

## Explanation

Lifting the left side and lowering the right both favour clockwise rotation. Their torques add even though the forces cancel.
