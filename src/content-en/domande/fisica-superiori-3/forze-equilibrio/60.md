What geometric relation links the magnitudes $P_\parallel$ and $P_\perp$ to weight $P$?

- $P=P_\parallel+P_\perp$ for every slope.
- $P=P_\parallel/P_\perp$.
- $P_\parallel^2=P^2+P_\perp^2$.
- $P^2=P_\parallel^2+P_\perp^2$.

## Explanation

The projections are along perpendicular axes, so reconstruct the magnitude using Pythagoras. Directly adding magnitudes would mix perpendicular directions.
