An ideal spring of constant $k$ goes from compression $-x$ to extension $+x$, with $x>0$. What happens to its restoring force?

- It keeps both magnitude and direction.
- Its magnitude doubles.
- It vanishes in both configurations.
- It keeps its magnitude and reverses direction.

## Explanation

The law is linear and changes sign when displacement changes sign. Magnitude is $kx$ in both configurations.
