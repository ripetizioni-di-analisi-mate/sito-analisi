A crate slides and then stops with the pull still applied. What is the next check in the Coulomb model?

- Whether kinetic friction must automatically reverse motion.
- Whether available static friction can maintain rest.
- Whether gravity changes instantly.
- Whether mass decreases on stopping.

## Explanation

The kinetic law assumes sliding. After stopping, check whether the friction required for balance stays below the static limit. If the limit is enough, static friction matches the pull in magnitude and the crate stays at rest. If it is insufficient, the crate starts moving in the direction of the resultant.
