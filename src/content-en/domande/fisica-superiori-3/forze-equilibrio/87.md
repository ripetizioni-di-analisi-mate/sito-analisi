A perpendicular load is moved twice as far from the pivot. To keep its original torque, how must force magnitude change?

- It doubles.
- It stays the same.
- It halves.
- It quadruples.

## Explanation

At fixed torque the product $Fb$ stays fixed. Doubling the arm requires the reciprocal change in force. For example, when increasing the lever arm from twenty to forty centimetres, half the force gives the same torque if it remains perpendicular and keeps the same turning direction.
