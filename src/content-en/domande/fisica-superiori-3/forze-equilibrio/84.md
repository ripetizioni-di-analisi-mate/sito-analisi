Why is the fulcrum often used as the reference point for torques on a lever?

- The lever's weight always vanishes.
- All force magnitudes become zero.
- Forces far from the fulcrum produce no torque.
- The fulcrum reaction has zero moment arm about that point.

## Explanation

The reaction acts at the fulcrum itself. Its line of action crosses the point about which torques are computed, simplifying the equation. First balance the torques of the other forces about that point. Once the remaining unknowns are found, calculate the pivot reaction from force balance.
