What are the physical dimensions of torque?

- $MLT^{-2}$.
- $LT^{-2}$.
- $ML^2T^{-2}$.
- $ML^{-1}T^{-2}$.

## Explanation

Torque multiplies force, with dimensions $MLT^{-2}$, by arm length $L$. Combining the powers gives $ML^2T^{-2}$.
