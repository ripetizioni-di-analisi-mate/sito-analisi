At fixed force and distance from pivot, which angle between force and position maximises torque magnitude?

- $0^\circ$.
- $30^\circ$.
- $180^\circ$.
- $90^\circ$.

## Explanation

The formula is $M=rF\lvert\sin\theta\rvert$. With $r$ and $F$ fixed, sine governs the result and has its largest value at ninety degrees. At zero or one hundred and eighty degrees, the force acts along the radius direction and gives zero torque. A right angle uses the full available lever arm.
