At fixed force and distance from pivot, which angle between force and position maximises torque magnitude?

- $0^\circ$.
- $30^\circ$.
- $180^\circ$.
- $90^\circ$.

## Explanation

The formula is $M=rF\lvert\sin\theta\rvert$. With $r$ and $F$ fixed, sine governs the result and has its largest value at ninety degrees.
