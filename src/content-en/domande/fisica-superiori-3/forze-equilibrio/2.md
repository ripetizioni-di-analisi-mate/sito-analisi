A mass $m=0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from an ideal vertical spring of spring constant $k=160\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Find the extension from the natural length. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Choose the closest value.

- $0.0153\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.0307\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.0613\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.0460\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The upward restoring force balances the downward weight. Solve $kx=mg$ for the extension. The spring balances about five newtons. Since one centimetre of extension takes $1.6\,\mathrm N$, the result should be just over three centimetres.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=\frac{mg}k\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.0307\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
