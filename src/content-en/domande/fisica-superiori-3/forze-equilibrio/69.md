A knot is held by an oblique string on each side and supports a vertical weight. Which equation accounts for lateral cancellation?

- $T_L\cos\theta_L=T_R\cos\theta_R$ for angles from horizontal.
- $T_L+T_R=P$.
- $T_L\sin\theta_L=T_R\sin\theta_R$ for angles from horizontal.
- $T_L-T_R=P$.

## Explanation

Horizontal balance requires opposite projections of equal magnitude. With angles from horizontal, the lateral projections use the cosine.
