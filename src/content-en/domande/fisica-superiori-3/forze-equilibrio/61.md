A block has parallel weight component $36\,\mathrm N$ and total weight $60\,\mathrm N$. Find the normal component magnitude.

- $48\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The normal component is one leg of the projection triangle. Subtract thirty-six squared from sixty squared and take the positive root.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\perp&=\sqrt{60^2-36^2}\\[4pt]
&= 48\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
