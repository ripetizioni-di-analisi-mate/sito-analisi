An object moves up an inclined plane. Does resolving weight change compared with the same object moving down the plane?

- Yes, the parallel component of weight reverses.
- No, the projections depend on the axes and the slope angle.
- Yes, sine and cosine swap.
- Yes, weight becomes normal to the plane.

## Explanation

Changing direction of motion does not rotate gravity. Kinetic friction is what reverses when sliding reverses.
