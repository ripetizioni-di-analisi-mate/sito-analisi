An external force pulls a body outwards perpendicular to a plane. What must be included when finding the normal force while contact remains?

- A parallel component that increases the normal force.
- Only the parallel component of weight.
- The outward pull, which reduces the normal force below $mg\cos\theta$.
- Nothing, because the normal force always equals $mg$.

## Explanation

Weight projects inwards along the normal. The added force already points outward and balances part of that projection, leaving less for the normal force to supply. The result must remain nonnegative for the slope to keep pushing the body. A result of zero marks the threshold of release.
