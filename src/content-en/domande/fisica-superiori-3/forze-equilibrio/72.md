Two parallel forces of magnitude $30\,\mathrm N$ point in opposite directions and form a couple with torque magnitude $9\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. What is the perpendicular separation of their lines of action?

- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.33\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Choose a point on one of the lines as the pivot. That force then has zero torque. The other force produces the entire torque of the couple, with a lever arm equal to the required separation. Divide by the magnitude of just one force.

$$
b=0.30\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
