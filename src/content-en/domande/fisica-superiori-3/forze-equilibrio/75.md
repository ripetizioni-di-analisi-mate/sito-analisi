A force's line of action passes through the pivot. What is its torque about the pivot?

- Force times distance from the pivot to the application point.
- Maximum at every possible point of application.
- Zero.
- Mass of the body times force.

## Explanation

The perpendicular distance from the pivot to the line is zero. The application point may be far from the pivot, but that separation is not the moment arm in this case. For example, a push along a horizontal lever through its pivot can load the pivot without turning the lever.
