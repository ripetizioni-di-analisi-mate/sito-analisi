In a static test a mass hangs from a spring. What assumption makes $kx$ and $mg$ equal in magnitude?

- The spring always has zero length.
- The mass accelerates upwards.
- Gravity must be negligible.
- The mass is in equilibrium with only weight and spring force acting.

## Explanation

The vertical balance requires zero resultant. With only two opposite vertical forces, their magnitudes must agree.
