Two symmetric strings support fixed weight and become nearly horizontal. What happens to tension?

- It decreases to zero.
- It grows greatly because the vertical fraction falls.
- It always equals weight.
- It depends only on the vertical weight attachment.

## Explanation

From $T=P/(2\sin\theta)$, a very small sine requires large tension to keep total vertical support fixed.
