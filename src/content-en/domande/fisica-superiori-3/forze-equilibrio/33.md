A weight is removed from an ideal vertical spring. How does the new equilibrium extension compare with the previous one?

- It is smaller.
- It is larger.
- It is equal.
- It must be negative even if another weight remains attached.

## Explanation

Spring force at the new equilibrium balances a smaller total weight. With positive spring constant, reduced force requires less extension.
