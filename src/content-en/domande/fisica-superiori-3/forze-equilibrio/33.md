A weight is removed from an ideal vertical spring. How does the new equilibrium extension compare with the previous one?

- It is smaller.
- It is larger.
- It is equal.
- It must be negative even if another weight remains attached.

## Explanation

Spring force at the new equilibrium balances a smaller total weight. With positive spring constant, reduced force requires less extension. Find the new value after the transient has ended. Immediately after the removal, the remaining mass can move because the spring still has its original force at that position.
