A resting block on a horizontal surface is pushed horizontally, with a force below the static friction limit. What happens if the push increases a little but stays below that limit?

- Friction stays at its maximum.
- Friction vanishes.
- Friction magnitude increases by the same amount.
- Friction reverses direction.

## Explanation

Below the limit the block can remain in equilibrium and friction balances the new push. Its value increases until it reaches the maximum available. The force to be balanced sets the actual friction. The normal force and coefficient set the maximum available, which need not equal the friction exerted.
