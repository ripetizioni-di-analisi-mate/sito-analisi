A resting block on a horizontal surface is pushed below the static friction limit. What happens if the push increases a little but stays below that limit?

- Friction stays at its maximum.
- Friction vanishes.
- Friction magnitude increases by the same amount.
- Friction reverses direction.

## Explanation

Below the limit the block can remain in equilibrium and friction balances the new push. Its value increases until it reaches the maximum available.
