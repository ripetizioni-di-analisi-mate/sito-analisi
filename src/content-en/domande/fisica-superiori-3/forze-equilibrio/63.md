Two strings at equal inclination make $30^\circ$ above horizontal and support $50\,\mathrm N$. Find the tension in each.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 vettori="0,0,-4.33,2.5,T;0,0,4.33,2.5,T;0,0,0,-5,P" xmin=-5.1 xmax=5.1 ymin=-5.5 ymax=3.6 titolo="The two vertical components support a weight equal to one tension magnitude"}

- $25\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $\frac{50 \sqrt{3}}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The horizontal projections have equal magnitudes and opposite directions. Each string provides a vertical component equal to half its own magnitude, since the sine of thirty degrees is one half. Together they provide one full tension magnitude, which must support fifty newtons.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T&=\frac{50}{2\sin(30^\circ)}\\[4pt]
&= 50\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
