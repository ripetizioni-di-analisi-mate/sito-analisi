A stationary junction supports a $30\,\mathrm N$ load and receives a $30\,\mathrm N$ force to the right. Two strings can pull it to the left and right at $45^\circ$ above horizontal. What tension must the right string exert in this geometry?

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\sqrt2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At forty-five degrees each string has equal component magnitudes. Vertically the contributions must add to thirty newtons. Horizontally the left one must exceed the right one by the same amount. Both conditions can hold only when the right contribution vanishes.

$$
T_R=0\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
