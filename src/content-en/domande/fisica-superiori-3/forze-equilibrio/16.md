An ideal spring at $x=2\,\mathrm{cm}$ requires $5\,\mathrm N$ to remain extended. Find its spring constant.

- $\frac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $\frac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$.

## Explanation

The applied force balances the restoring force at equal magnitude. Dividing five newtons by $0.02\,\mathrm m$ gives the slope in newtons per metre. It predicts another five newtons for each additional two centimetres within the elastic range.

$$
\begin{aligned}
k&=\frac F{x}=\frac5{0.02}\\[4pt]
&= 250\,\mathrm{N/m}
\end{aligned}
$$
