A resting body on a slope satisfies $\tan\theta<\mu_s$. With only weight and contact forces, what happens?

- It must start descending at $g\sin\theta$.
- It must move uphill.
- It can stay at rest.
- Kinetic friction exceeds weight.

## Explanation

Required friction is $mg\sin\theta$. The inequality ensures this is below the available maximum $\mu_smg\cos\theta$. To maintain rest, friction points uphill against the tendency to slide downhill. The comparison shows that contact can provide the required value without reaching the threshold.
