For downhill motion with kinetic friction, $\mu_d$ increases at the same slope angle. What changes in the downslope equation?

- The weight component increases and friction stays the same.
- The term subtracted from the weight component increases.
- The normal force automatically vanishes.
- The sign of weight reverses.

## Explanation

The parallel weight component depends on $m$, $g$ and angle. The coefficient multiplies the normal force only in the friction opposing descent. At fixed inclination the normal force is unchanged. Raising the coefficient makes the signed downhill acceleration smaller and can make it negative while the body is still descending.
