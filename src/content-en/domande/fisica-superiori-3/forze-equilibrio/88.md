A lever's weight acts along a line through the pivot. In which balance may it still matter?

- The torque about the pivot, where its moment arm is always positive.
- Neither balance.
- Only horizontal balance.
- The vertical force balance.

## Explanation

Its moment arm about the pivot is zero so weight contributes no torque there. It remains a vertical force that the reaction must balance. A heavier beam can therefore need a larger reaction even when its weight leaves the torque equation about the pivot unchanged.
