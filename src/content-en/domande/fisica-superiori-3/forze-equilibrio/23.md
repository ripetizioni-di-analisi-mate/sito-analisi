An element's natural length is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and it extends to $17\,\mathrm{cm}$ under $10\,\mathrm N$. Find $k$ in the ideal model.

- $\frac{1000}{17}\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$.

## Explanation

Deformation is the difference between the lengths, five centimetres. Using the full seventeen centimetres would overstate the extension.

$$
\begin{aligned}
k&=\frac{10}{0.17-0.12}\\[4pt]
&= 200\,\mathrm{N/m}
\end{aligned}
$$
