An element's natural length is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Under a force of $10\,\mathrm N$ it extends to $17\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find $k$ in the ideal model.

- $\frac{1000}{17}\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$.

## Explanation

Deformation is the difference between the lengths, five centimetres. Using the full seventeen centimetres would overstate the extension. Five centimetres is $0.050\,\mathrm m$. Dividing the applied force by this value gives the slope of the graph in the linear range.

$$
\begin{aligned}
k&=\frac{10}{0.17-0.12}\\[4pt]
&= 200\,\mathrm{N/m}
\end{aligned}
$$
