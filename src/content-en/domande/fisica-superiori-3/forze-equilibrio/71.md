An ideal string's calculated equilibrium tension is negative. What is the correct interpretation?

- The string normally supplies compression.
- Tension has negative magnitude but supports weight.
- The assumed configuration would require compression and cannot use that taut string.
- Simply reverse the sign of weight.

## Explanation

A string can pull away from the knot along itself. An opposite sign calls for a different reaction, so the assumed constraint must be reconsidered. Redrawing the force diagram may require allowing that string to go slack, with zero tension, and checking whether the other constraints can maintain equilibrium.
