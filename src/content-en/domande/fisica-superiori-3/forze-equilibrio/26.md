A vertical $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring extends by $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a stationary mass. At $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the mass?

- $0.15\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

First find the fifteen-newton elastic force. In equilibrium its magnitude equals weight, so divide by ten newtons per kilogram. Swapping grams for kilograms or centimetres for metres would distort the relation between the two quantities.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{kx}g=\frac{250\cdot0.06}{10}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 1.5\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$
