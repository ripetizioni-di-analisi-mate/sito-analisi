A plane becomes horizontal, so $\theta=0$. Which pair gives the magnitudes of the parallel and normal weight components?

- $(mg,0)$.
- $(0,mg)$.
- $(mg,mg)$.
- $(0,0)$.

## Explanation

Vertical weight is perpendicular to the horizontal plane. The parallel projection vanishes and the perpendicular one takes the full magnitude. On a horizontal surface, with contact maintained and no other perpendicular forces, the support has to balance precisely this second component of magnitude $mg$.
