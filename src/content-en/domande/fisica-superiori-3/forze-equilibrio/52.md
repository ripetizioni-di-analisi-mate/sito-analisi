An object sliding downslope comes to a stop on the incline. Only weight and contact forces act. What must be checked to conclude it remains at rest?

- That the normal force exceeds weight for every angle.
- That the previous acceleration was zero.
- That $\mu_d$ must be zero.
- That $\mu_sN$ is at least the downslope weight component.

## Explanation

Stopping ends the sliding phase. Subsequent rest requires that contact can provide static friction up to the necessary force. The required static friction points uphill. If its maximum is below the downhill weight component, the stopping position cannot be maintained.
