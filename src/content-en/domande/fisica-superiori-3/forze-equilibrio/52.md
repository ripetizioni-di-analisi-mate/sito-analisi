An object sliding downslope comes to a stop on the incline. What must be checked to conclude it remains at rest?

- That the normal force exceeds weight for every angle.
- That the previous acceleration was zero.
- That $\mu_d$ must be zero.
- That $\mu_sN$ is at least the downslope weight component.

## Explanation

Stopping ends the sliding phase. Subsequent rest requires that contact can provide static friction up to the necessary force.
