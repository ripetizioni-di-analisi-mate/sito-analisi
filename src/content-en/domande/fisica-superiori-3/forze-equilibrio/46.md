An object moves uphill on a plane with $\sin\theta=3/5$, $\cos\theta=4/5$, $\mu_d=0.25$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find acceleration signed positive downhill.

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Weight still has a downslope component. Since the body is sliding uphill, kinetic friction also points down the slope. The two parts add instead of subtracting. Positive acceleration opposes the current velocity, so the body slows down.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=10\left(\frac35+0.25\cdot\frac45\right)\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
