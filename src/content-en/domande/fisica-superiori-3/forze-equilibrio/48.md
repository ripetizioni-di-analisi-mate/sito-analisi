A body already sliding down satisfies $\sin\theta<\mu_d\cos\theta$. What happens while it still slides down?

- It accelerates downslope.
- It maintains constant speed.
- It loses contact with the slope.
- It slows down.

## Explanation

Upslope friction exceeds the downslope component of weight. Resultant force opposes motion, so speed decreases.
