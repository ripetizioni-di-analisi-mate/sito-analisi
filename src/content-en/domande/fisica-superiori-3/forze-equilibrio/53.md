If upslope is chosen as positive, what is the sign of the weight component parallel to a slope with $0<\theta<90^\circ$?

- Negative.
- Positive.
- Zero.
- It depends on the direction of motion.

## Explanation

Weight projected along the plane always points downslope. The chosen positive direction is opposite, so the component is negative regardless of sliding. If the body moves uphill, the weight component slows it. If it moves down, the same component points with velocity. On the chosen axis its sign stays negative in both cases.
