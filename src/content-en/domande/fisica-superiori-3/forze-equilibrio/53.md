If upslope is chosen as positive, what is the sign of the weight component parallel to a slope with $0<\theta<90^\circ$?

- Negative.
- Positive.
- Zero.
- It depends on the direction of motion.

## Explanation

Weight projected along the plane always points downslope. The chosen positive direction is opposite, so the component is negative regardless of sliding.
