A hanging mass passes the position where $kx=mg$. What follows if only the spring and weight act?

- Acceleration is zero at that instant.
- Velocity must be zero.
- The mass remains still there afterwards.
- Kinetic energy must be zero.

## Explanation

The forces balance at that position, so instantaneous acceleration is zero. Velocity depends on the preceding motion and may be nonzero. A body passing through that position can keep moving and reach a point where weight and the spring force no longer balance.
