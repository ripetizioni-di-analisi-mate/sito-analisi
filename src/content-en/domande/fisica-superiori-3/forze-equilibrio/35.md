A sliding box has normal force $30\,\mathrm N$ and kinetic friction $9\,\mathrm N$. What is $\mu_d$?

- $3$.
- $0.9$.
- $0.3$.
- $3.333$.

## Explanation

The kinetic model makes friction proportional to the normal force. Divide the two forces, both in newtons, to find the constant of proportionality. The coefficient is dimensionless. Multiplying it by thirty recovers nine newtons. The comparison applies during sliding. This ratio does not determine the static coefficient.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_d&=\frac{f_d}{N}=\frac9{30}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.3
\end{aligned}
$$
