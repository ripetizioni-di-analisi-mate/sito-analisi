A sled weighing $50\,\mathrm N$ slides in contact with a slope for which $\cos\theta=4/5$. An additional force pulls outwards perpendicular to the surface. What is its magnitude $F_{\max}$ at the threshold where the normal reaction becomes zero?

- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

As long as the sled follows the surface, its normal acceleration is zero. The auxiliary force and the normal reaction point outwards and together balance $50\cos\theta$. At the threshold, the auxiliary force alone supplies that balance. Exceeding this limit would require the surface to pull inwards.

$$
F_{\max}=40\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
