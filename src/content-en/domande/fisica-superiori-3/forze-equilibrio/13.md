An object of mass $m=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ is on a slope at $30^\circ$. Find the magnitude of the component of its weight along the slope. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $P_{\parallel}=29.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $14.7\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $58.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $44.1\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Weight acts vertically. In its projection along the plane, the parallel side is opposite the slope angle, so use the sine.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\parallel&=mg\sin\theta\\[4pt]
&\simeq 29.4\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
