What shape does a graph of applied equilibrium force against extension have for an ideal spring without preload?

- A parabola with vertex at the origin.
- A horizontal line.
- A falling straight line through the origin.
- A rising straight line through the origin.

## Explanation

In the balance, the applied force is $+kx$. The coefficient $k$ is positive and zero extension needs no force.
