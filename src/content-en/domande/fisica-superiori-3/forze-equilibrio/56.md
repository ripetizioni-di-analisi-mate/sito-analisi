A body on a plane with $\sin\theta=0.8$ has a parallel weight component of $64\,\mathrm N$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its mass?

- $\frac{32}{5}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $\frac{128}{25}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

The given component is only eighty percent of weight, so full weight is eighty newtons. Divide by ten newtons per kilogram. Checking forwards, eight kilograms gives eighty newtons of weight and sixty-four down the slope.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m&=\frac{P_\parallel}{g\sin\theta}=\frac{64}{10\cdot0.8}\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$
