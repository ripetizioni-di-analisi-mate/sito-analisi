A uniform board $3.0\,\mathrm m$ long weighs $40\,\mathrm N$. The supports are at $x=0$ and $x=2.0\,\mathrm m$ measured from the left end. A load of $20\,\mathrm N$ rests somewhere on the board and the right reaction measures $54\,\mathrm N$. At what coordinate $x$ is the load?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

About the left support, the measured reaction gives $54\cdot2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. The weight of the board balances $40\cdot1.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ of this torque. The load supplies the rest, so $x=\dfrac{108-60}{20}\,\mathrm m=2.4\,\mathrm m$. Vertical force balance leaves $6\,\mathrm N$ at the left support, which remains in contact.
