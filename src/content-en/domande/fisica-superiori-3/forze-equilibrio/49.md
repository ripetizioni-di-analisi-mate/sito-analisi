Two objects of different masses slide down equally inclined smooth slopes. How do their accelerations compare?

- They are equal.
- They are proportional to the masses.
- They are inversely proportional to the masses.
- Both are zero.

## Explanation

The weight component and inertial mass increase in the same proportion. Dividing by mass gives $g\sin\theta$ for both. The more massive body has a greater force along the slope, but it also requires a proportionally greater force to achieve the same acceleration.
