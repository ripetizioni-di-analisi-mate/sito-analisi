An ideal spring of spring constant $k=140\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is extended by $x=0.04\,\mathrm m$. Find the magnitude of the elastic force.

- $F_e=5.6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $2.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $11.2\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $8.4\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Hooke's law gives a force opposite to the deformation. Its magnitude is the spring constant times the extension.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_e&=kx\\[4pt]
&= 5.6\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
