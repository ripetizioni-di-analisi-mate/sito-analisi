What additional quantity is needed besides the kinetic friction coefficient to compute the friction magnitude?

- The magnitude of the normal force.
- Weight regardless of the normal force.
- The distance already travelled.
- The time since sliding began.

## Explanation

The model gives $f_d=\mu_dN$. The normal force is the perpendicular push between the surfaces. Pressing a body against a surface adds to $N$ even if weight is unchanged. Friction therefore changes at the same coefficient.
