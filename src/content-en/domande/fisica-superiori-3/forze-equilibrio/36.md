A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate slides rightwards. The normal force is $40\,\mathrm N$, kinetic coefficient is $0.25$ and a horizontal pull is $6\,\mathrm N$ rightwards. What is its acceleration, taking rightwards as positive?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Kinetic friction points left because the crate slides right on the surface. Its ten-newton magnitude exceeds the pull. Signed net force is minus four newtons. Dividing by four gives leftward acceleration, so the crate slows while it continues sliding right.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=\frac{6-0.25\cdot40}{4}\\[4pt]
&= -1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
