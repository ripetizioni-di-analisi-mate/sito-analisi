A block is at the limit of rest on a slope with $\sin\theta=0.6$ and $\cos\theta=0.8$. Find $\mu_s$.

- $0.48$.
- $1.25$.
- $0.75$.
- $0.6$.

## Explanation

At the limit, maximum static friction equals the component of weight along the slope. Cancelling $mg$ leaves sine divided by cosine.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_s&=\tan\theta=\frac{0.6}{0.8}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.75
\end{aligned}
$$
