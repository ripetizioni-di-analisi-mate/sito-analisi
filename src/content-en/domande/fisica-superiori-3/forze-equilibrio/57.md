As slope angle rises from zero to $90^\circ$, what happens to the magnitudes of weight components at fixed mass?

- The parallel one grows and the normal one falls.
- Both grow.
- The parallel one falls and the normal one grows.
- Both stay the same.

## Explanation

In the first quadrant sine grows and cosine falls. The parallel projection uses sine while the normal one uses cosine.
