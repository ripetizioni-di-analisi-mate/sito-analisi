A $10\,\mathrm N$ force has moment arm $0.40\,\mathrm m$. If the arm falls to $0.20\,\mathrm m$ and force rises to $15\,\mathrm N$, what torque magnitude results?

- $4\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.

## Explanation

The new torque uses the product of the new values. The larger force does not make up for halving the arm, so the new product is below the original four newton metres.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=15\cdot0.20\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}
\end{aligned}
$$
