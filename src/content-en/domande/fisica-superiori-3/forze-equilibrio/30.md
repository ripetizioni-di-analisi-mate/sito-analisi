A $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass hangs from a $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring in a cabin accelerating up at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. It stays at rest relative to the cabin. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find its extension.

- $0.06667\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.08\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.05333\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.12\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Relative to the ground the mass accelerates with the cabin. The spring balances weight and supplies two newtons of upward resultant, so it exerts twelve newtons. Now divide the $12\,\mathrm N$ spring force by $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The extension exceeds the value when the mass is at rest relative to the ground.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=\frac{m(g+a)}k=\frac{12}{150}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.08\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
