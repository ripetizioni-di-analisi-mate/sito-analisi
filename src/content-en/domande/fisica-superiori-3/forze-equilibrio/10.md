A mass hangs at rest from an ideal vertical spring of stiffness $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$, initially extended by $0.080\,\mathrm m$. An additional upward force of $6\,\mathrm N$ supports the mass, and it settles at a new equilibrium. By how much does the spring shorten, $\Delta\ell$?

- $0.11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The mass has the same weight in both equilibria. With the extra force, the spring supplies $6\,\mathrm N$ less. Divide that force difference by the spring stiffness to find the shortening. The spring remains extended by $0.050\,\mathrm m$.

$$
\Delta\ell=0.03\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
