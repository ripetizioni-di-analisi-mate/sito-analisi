A mass $m=0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from an ideal vertical spring of spring constant $k=180\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Find the extension from the natural length. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $0.01635\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $x=0.0327\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.0654\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.04905\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The upward restoring force balances the downward weight. Solve $kx=mg$ for the extension.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=\frac{mg}k\\[4pt]
&= 0.0327\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
