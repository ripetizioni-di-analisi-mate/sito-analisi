At equilibrium under the same applied force, which of two ideal springs extends less?

- The one with smaller spring constant.
- Always the one with greater natural length.
- The one with greater spring constant.
- Always the one with greater mass.

## Explanation

Extension is $F/k$. At fixed force, a larger spring constant reduces extension. For example, under $10\,\mathrm N$ a $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring extends ten centimetres, while a $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring extends five. The comparison holds at the same force.
