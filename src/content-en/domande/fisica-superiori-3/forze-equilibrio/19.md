Under the same applied force, which of two ideal springs extends less?

- The one with smaller spring constant.
- Always the one with greater natural length.
- The one with greater spring constant.
- Always the one with greater mass.

## Explanation

Extension is $F/k$. At fixed force, a larger spring constant reduces extension.
