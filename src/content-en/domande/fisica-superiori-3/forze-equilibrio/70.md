Two symmetric strings at angle $\theta$ support $80\,\mathrm N$ with tension $50\,\mathrm N$ each. Find $\sin\theta$.

- $0.5$.
- $0.8$.
- $0.6$.
- $1$.

## Explanation

Each vertical component must supply forty newtons. This is four fifths of the full fifty-newton tension, the sine of the angle from horizontal. Check by multiplying the sine by one string’s tension and doubling the result to include the other.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin\theta&=\frac{P}{2T}=\frac{80}{2\cdot50}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.8
\end{aligned}
$$
