A vertical $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring already stretched by a mass receives another $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$. Between equilibria, how much does extension grow at $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $0.003\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.015\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The weight of the original mass occurs in both equilibria. Subtracting the two equations cancels it, leaving just three newtons of added weight. Extension grows by one and a half centimetres. This applies if the new state stays within the elastic range.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x&=\frac{\Delta m\,g}{k}=\frac{0.30\cdot10}{200}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.015\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
