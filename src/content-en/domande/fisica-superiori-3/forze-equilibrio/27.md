A spring extends by $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ per newton up to $3\,\mathrm N$. Above that force a second spring engages, giving an additional $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ per newton. What is the total extension at $7\,\mathrm N$?

- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The first three newtons give an extension of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The next four add $4\cdot0.5=2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Adding the extensions from the two ranges gives $8\,\mathrm{cm}$.

Theory — [Hooke's law](/en/fisica-superiori-3/forze-equilibrio/teoria#hooke).
