A spring extends by $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ per newton as long as the applied force does not exceed $3\,\mathrm N$. Above that value a second spring mounted in parallel comes into action, and each additional newton produces an extra extension of $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the total extension when the applied force is $7\,\mathrm N$?

- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Up to $3\,\mathrm N$ only the first spring works, so at $3\,\mathrm N$ the extension is $3\cdot2\,\mathrm{cm}=6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The remaining $4\,\mathrm N$ are carried by the two springs in parallel, therefore they produce an extra extension of $4\cdot0.5\,\mathrm{cm}=2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The extensions of the two ranges add up, so at $7\,\mathrm N$ the total extension is $6\,\mathrm{cm}+2\,\mathrm{cm}=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ comes from assigning the whole force to the first spring alone, and $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from applying the behaviour of the second range to the whole force.
