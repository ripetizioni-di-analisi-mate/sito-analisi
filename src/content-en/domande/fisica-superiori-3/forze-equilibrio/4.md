An object is sliding downhill on a slope at $30^\circ$, with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_d=0.13$. Find the signed acceleration, positive down the slope. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Choose the closest value.

- $1.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $7.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $5.7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $3.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The component $mg\sin\theta$ points down the slope. Kinetic friction opposes the sliding motion and equals $\mu_dmg\cos\theta$. Divide the net force by the mass. With downhill positive, the friction term is subtracted. The result is still positive because $\sin30^\circ$ exceeds $\mu_d\cos30^\circ$, so the body speeds up.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=g(\sin\theta-\mu_d\cos\theta)\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
