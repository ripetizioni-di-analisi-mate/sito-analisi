An ideal spring of spring constant $k=130\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is extended by $x=0.035\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the elastic force?

- $4.55\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $2.275\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $9.1\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6.825\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Hooke's law gives a force opposite to the deformation. Its magnitude is the spring constant times the extension. The restoring force points towards the natural length. Extension is already in metres, so multiplying $130$ by $0.035$ gives newtons directly.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_e&=kx\\[4pt]
&= 4.55\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
