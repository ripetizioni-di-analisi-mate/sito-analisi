If hanging mass halves and spring constant doubles, how does static extension change at fixed $g$?

- It stays the same.
- It halves.
- It becomes one quarter.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

The numerator weight halves and the denominator spring constant doubles. Both changes reduce the ratio by successive factors of one half. As a check, starting from four centimetres, halving the mass gives two centimetres. Doubling the stiffness halves that value again to one centimetre.
