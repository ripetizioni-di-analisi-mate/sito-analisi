An ideal vertical spring of $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ holds a stationary $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its extension?

::grafico{f="100*x" punti="0.04,4,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=0.065 ymin=0 ymax=7 nomi="x,F" titolo="Equilibrium is the point where restoring force measures 4 N"}

- $0.04\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.004\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.1\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The mass is in equilibrium, so the upward restoring force equals its four-newton weight. The constant is one hundred newtons per metre of extension. Their ratio is four hundredths of a metre, or four centimetres. The final unit is length because force is divided by force per metre.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=\frac{mg}{k}=\frac{0.40\cdot10}{100}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.04\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
