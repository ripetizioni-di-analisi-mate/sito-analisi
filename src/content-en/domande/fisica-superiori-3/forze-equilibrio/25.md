Two identical springs are mounted first in series, then in parallel so that their extensions are equal. Each assembly carries the same total force at rest. What is the ratio $x_s/x_p$ of the total extensions?

- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

In series, each spring carries the full force and their extensions add. In parallel, the same total force splits in half, and the shared extension is that of each spring. Thus the extension goes from twice that of a single spring to half of it.

$$
x_s/x_p=4.
$$
