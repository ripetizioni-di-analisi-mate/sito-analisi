A body slides rightwards relative to a moving belt. Which direction does kinetic friction on the body take?

- Rightwards.
- Always downwards.
- Opposite to the normal force.
- Leftwards.

## Explanation

Friction opposes sliding relative to the belt surface. That relative motion is enough to determine its direction without the belt's ground speed. For example, a belt moving left can slide under a body at rest from the ground. That body moves right relative to the belt and receives friction to the left.
