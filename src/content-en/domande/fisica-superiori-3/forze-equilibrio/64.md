Two symmetric strings inclined $60^\circ$ above horizontal have $40\,\mathrm N$ of tension each. What weight do they support?

- $40\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $40 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $40 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Horizontal forces cancel in pairs. Add the vertical projections of the tensions rather than their full magnitudes. Supported weight is less than eighty newtons because each string is oblique.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=2T\sin(60^\circ)=2\cdot40\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\\[4pt]
&= 40 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
