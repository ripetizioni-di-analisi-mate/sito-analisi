An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on a fixed horizontal surface with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_d=0.13$. Its weight and the normal force are the only vertical forces. Find the magnitude of friction. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $3.19\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12.76\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $f_d=6.38\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $9.57\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The net vertical force is zero, so $N=mg$. As the object is sliding, use the kinetic coefficient and calculate $\mu_dN$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_d&=\mu_dmg\\[4pt]
&\simeq 6.38\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
