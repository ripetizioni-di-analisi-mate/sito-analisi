A smooth horizontal surface supports a body at rest. Only weight and the normal force act. If weight increases without changing equilibrium, what happens to the normal force?

- It stays the same.
- It increases by the same amount.
- It decreases.
- It always doubles regardless of the added weight.

## Explanation

Weight and normal force are the only vertical forces. Zero vertical resultant requires the magnitudes to remain equal. This follows because the surface is horizontal and there are no other vertical forces. An upward pull would change the balance even without motion.
