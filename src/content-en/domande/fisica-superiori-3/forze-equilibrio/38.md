A smooth horizontal surface supports a body at rest. If weight increases without changing equilibrium, what happens to the normal force?

- It stays the same.
- It increases by the same amount.
- It decreases.
- It always doubles regardless of the added weight.

## Explanation

Weight and normal force are the only vertical forces. Zero vertical resultant requires the magnitudes to remain equal.
