Two strings support a $60\,\mathrm N$ weight, at angles above horizontal of $30^\circ$ on the left and $60^\circ$ on the right. Find left tension.

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $30 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Horizontal balance gives the right tension in terms of the left because their components must cancel. Substitution into the vertical balance leaves one unknown. The left carries thirty newtons and the right $30\sqrt3\,\mathrm N$. Their vertical contributions are fifteen and forty-five newtons and add to the weight.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_R&=T_L\frac{\cos 30^\circ}{\cos60^\circ}=\sqrt3\,T_L\\[4pt]60&=\tfrac12T_L+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}T_R=2T_L\\[4pt]T_L&=\frac{60}{2}\\[4pt]
&= 30\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
