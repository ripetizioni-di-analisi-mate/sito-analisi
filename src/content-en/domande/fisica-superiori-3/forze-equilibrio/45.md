On a slope with $\sin\theta=3/5$ and $\cos\theta=4/5$, a body slides down at constant velocity. Find $\mu_d$.

- $0.75$.
- $0.6$.
- $1.333$.
- $0$.

## Explanation

Constant velocity implies zero parallel resultant. The downslope weight component equals the coefficient times normal force. Cancelling $mg$ gives sine divided by cosine, which is the dimensionless slope tangent.

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mu_d&=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=\frac{3/5}{4/5}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.75
\end{aligned}
$$
