An object of mass $m=2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from two ideal symmetric strings making $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, which value is closest to the tension in each string?

- $12.25\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $24.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $49\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $36.75\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

By symmetry the horizontal components cancel each other out. The two vertical components $T\sin\theta$ support the weight. Each string supplies half its own magnitude vertically because $\sin30^\circ=1/2$. The two halves together equal one full tension, which has to equal the entire suspended weight.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T&=\frac{mg}{2\sin\theta}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 24.5\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
