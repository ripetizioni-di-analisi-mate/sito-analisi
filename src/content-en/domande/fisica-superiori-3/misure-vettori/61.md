What is the cosine of the angle from the positive $x$-axis for a vector of magnitude $10$ with $x$-component $-6$?

- $-\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac35$
- $-\dfrac53$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

The projection is the magnitude times the cosine. Dividing the signed component $-6$ by the positive magnitude $10$ preserves the negative sign. The ratio is $-\dfrac35$, between minus one and one as every cosine must be. The negative sign puts the tip to the left, and the sign of the vertical component would distinguish the second and third quadrants.
