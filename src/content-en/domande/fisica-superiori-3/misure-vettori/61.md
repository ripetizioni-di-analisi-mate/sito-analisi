What is the cosine of the angle from the positive $x$-axis for a vector of magnitude $10$ with $x$-component $-6$?

- $-\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac35$
- $-\dfrac53$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

The projection is the magnitude times the cosine. Dividing the signed component $-6$ by the positive magnitude $10$ preserves the negative sign.
