Two instruments report different absolute margins. What information is required to compare their relative uncertainties?

- The central values to which the margins refer.
- Only the larger margin.
- The base units of the International System.
- Only the number of readings made.

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the absolute margin divided by the magnitude of the estimate. Even a greater absolute margin can be a smaller fraction if the estimate is much larger. For example, a margin of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ on $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ is one per cent. A margin of $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ on $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ is ten per cent even though it is smaller in absolute terms.
