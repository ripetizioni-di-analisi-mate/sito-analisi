The same sensor records two positions, $32\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $44\,\mathrm{cm}$. Each reading includes the same additive calibration error with magnitude at most $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an individual error with magnitude at most $1\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the maximum uncertainty in their difference?

- $0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The common offset appears with opposite signs when the two readings are subtracted, so it cancels. The two individual errors remain and can have opposite signs while both reaching their limits. The nominal difference of $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ therefore has maximum uncertainty $(1+1)\,\mathrm{cm}=2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
