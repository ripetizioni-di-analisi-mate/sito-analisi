A nonzero force has a vertical component equal to $-\dfrac12$ its magnitude and a positive horizontal component. What is its angle, measured anticlockwise from the positive $x$-axis between $0^\circ$ and $360^\circ$?

- $30^\circ$
- $210^\circ$
- $150^\circ$
- $330^\circ$

## Explanation

The vertical component gives $\sin\theta=-\dfrac12$, so the acute reference angle is $30^\circ$. The positive horizontal component shows that the cosine is positive. Together the two conditions place the force in the fourth quadrant. On the requested scale, the angle is $360^\circ-30^\circ=330^\circ$.
