Four readings of the same length are $10$, $11$, $11$ and $16\,\mathrm{mm}$. The last reading is corrected from $16$ to $12\,\mathrm{mm}$. By how many millimetres do the mean and the half-range decrease, respectively?

- $(4,4)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(1,4)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(2,1)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(1,2)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The sum of the four readings decreases by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$, so the mean falls by $1\,\mathrm{mm}$. The maximum becomes $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the minimum stays at $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The range drops from $6$ to $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Half of this range decreases by $2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
