Three readings are $12.0$, $12.1$ and $12.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the difference between the greatest reading and the exact mean?

::grafico{punti="12,0.1,pieno;12.1,0.1,pieno;12.2,0.5,pieno;12.5,0.1,pieno" segmenti="12,0.1,12.5,0.1;12.2,0.1,12.2,0.5" xmin=11.9 xmax=12.6 ymin=-0.1 ymax=0.8 nomi="x,y" titolo="The raised mean differs from the midpoint of the endpoints"}

- $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $0.25\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The mean gives all readings the same weight, so it is $12.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The upper endpoint is $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the estimated centre. The half-range is instead $0.25\,\mathrm{cm}$ because it only uses the separation between the endpoints, divided by two.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{\max}-\bar x&=12.5-\frac{12.0+12.1+12.5}{3}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.3\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
