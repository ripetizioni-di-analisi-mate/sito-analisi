A force $\vec F=(-4,3)\,\mathrm N$ acts during a displacement $\vec s=(2,0)\,\mathrm m$. What work does the force do?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,-4,3,F;0,0,2,0,s" segmenti="-4,0,-4,3" xmin=-5 xmax=3 ymin=-1 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="The force component along the displacement is negative"}

- $-8\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

Work is a dot product. This displacement has no vertical part, so work depends on the force's $x$-component. Its projection acts against the displacement, giving the negative sign. The force can affect other aspects of motion even if one component does not contribute to the work calculated here.

$$
\begin{aligned}
W&=F_xs_x+F_ys_y\\[4pt]&=(-4)\cdot2+3\cdot0\\[4pt]
&= -8\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
