A constant force $\vec F=(3,-2)\,\mathrm N$ acts on a body while it moves $4\,\mathrm m$ along the positive $x$-axis and then moves $5\,\mathrm m$ in the positive $y$ direction. What is the total work done by the force?

- $2\,\mathrm J$
- $22\,\mathrm J$
- $-2\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Only the horizontal component contributes in the first section, giving $3\cdot4\,\mathrm J=12\,\mathrm J$. In the second section the vertical component contributes, giving $(-2)\cdot5\,\mathrm J=-10\,\mathrm J$. The sum is $2\,\mathrm J$. With the same constant force, its dot product with the net displacement gives the same work.
