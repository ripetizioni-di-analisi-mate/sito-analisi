An arrow of components $(3,0)$ is translated so that its tail is at $(2,4)$. What are its components after translation?

- $(3,0)$
- $(5,4)$
- $(2,4)$
- $(1,-4)$

## Explanation

Translation moves the tail and head by the same amount. Components are coordinate differences from tail to head, so the common translation cancels.
