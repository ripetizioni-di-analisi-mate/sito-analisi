An arrow of components $(3,0)$ is translated so that its tail is at $(2,4)$. What are its components after translation?

- $(3,0)$
- $(5,4)$
- $(2,4)$
- $(1,-4)$

## Explanation

Translation moves the tail and head by the same amount. Components are coordinate differences from tail to head, so the common translation cancels. With the new tail at $(2,4)$, the tip is at $(5,4)$. The difference remains $(5-2,4-4)=(3,0)$. Giving the tip coordinates would describe a point instead of a displacement.
