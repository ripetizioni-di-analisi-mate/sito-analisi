Two measurement intervals share only one value at their endpoints. Are the measurements compatible under the closed-interval criterion?

- No, a shared segment of positive length is always needed.
- No, the centres must agree.
- Yes, the common point belongs to both intervals.
- Yes only if the two readings have the same margin.

## Explanation

A closed interval includes its endpoints. The existence of a single common value satisfies the criterion. For example, the intervals $[1,2]$ and $[2,4]$ share only the value $2$. This suffices for a common measurement to be possible. The two intervals still describe different central estimates and margins.
