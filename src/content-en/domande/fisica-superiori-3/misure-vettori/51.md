Two readings share just one possible value at the edge of both intervals. Are they compatible under the closed-interval criterion?

- No, a shared segment of positive length is always needed.
- No, the centres must agree.
- Yes, the common point belongs to both intervals.
- Yes only if the two readings have the same margin.

## Explanation

A closed interval includes its endpoints. The existence of a single common value satisfies the criterion.
