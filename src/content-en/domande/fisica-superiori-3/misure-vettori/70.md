What is needed besides magnitude to recover both components of a nonzero plane vector?

- The distance of the point of application from the origin.
- A directed angle measured from a fixed axis.
- Only the absolute value of the angle's tangent.
- The sum of the magnitudes of the components.

## Explanation

A directed angle determines the quadrant and therefore the signs of both projections. The absolute value of the tangent loses that information. With an angle measured anticlockwise from the positive $x$-axis, its cosine determines the horizontal component and its sine the vertical component. Together with the stated magnitude, these two projections specify the whole arrow.
