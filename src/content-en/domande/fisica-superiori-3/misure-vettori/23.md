A beam travels $4.0\cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m}$ in $2.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}$. What average speed follows?

- $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $800\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2.0\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

When dividing powers of the same base, subtract the exponents. The time exponent is negative, so the subtraction becomes addition.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\frac{4.0}{2.0}\cdot 10^{5-(-3)}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 2\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

The ratio of the coefficients is $4.0$ divided by $2.0$, giving $2.0$. The exponent of the result is $5-(-3)=8$. Crossing hundreds of kilometres in milliseconds is consistent with a very large number in metres per second.
