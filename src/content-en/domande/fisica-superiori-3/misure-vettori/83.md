A force of magnitude $10\,\mathrm N$ has a signed scalar projection of $+6\,\mathrm N$ along a unit vector $\hat s$ in the same plane. What is the magnitude of the cross product $\vec F\times\hat s$?

- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $6\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The cross product with a unit vector has magnitude equal to the force component perpendicular to it. The parallel and perpendicular components are legs of a right triangle, so the required magnitude is $\sqrt{10^2-6^2}\,\mathrm N=8\,\mathrm N$. This magnitude follows without knowing which side of $\hat s$ the force points to.
