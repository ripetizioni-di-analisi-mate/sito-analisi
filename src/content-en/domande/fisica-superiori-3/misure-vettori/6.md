A force of magnitude $F=16\,\mathrm N$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis. What is the component $F_x$?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The angle is measured from the $x$-axis, so the projection onto that axis uses the cosine. The sign matches that of the cosine.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_x&=F\cos\theta\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$

The force lies in the first quadrant and its horizontal projection points along the positive half-axis. A zero angle would put the whole magnitude along $x$. At sixty degrees, only half remains along that axis.
