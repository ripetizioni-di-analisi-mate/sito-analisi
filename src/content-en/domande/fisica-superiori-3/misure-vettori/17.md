A vehicle covers one straight segment for $10\,\mathrm{s}$ at $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and another for $20\,\mathrm{s}$ at $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$ in the same direction. Find its average velocity in $\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $6.667\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $7.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The speeds correspond to $10$ and $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Each segment contributes its own displacement, here $100\,\mathrm{m}$ in both cases. Total time is $30\,\mathrm{s}$. The weighted mean is closer to $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because that speed is maintained longer.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_m&=\frac{(36/3.6)\cdot10+(18/3.6)\cdot20}{10+20}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 6.667\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
