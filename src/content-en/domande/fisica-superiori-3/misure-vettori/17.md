A vehicle covers $90\,\mathrm m$ at $27\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and another $90\,\mathrm m$ at $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$ along the same line and in the same direction. What is its average velocity in metres per second?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The two velocities are $7.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Dividing each distance by its velocity gives $12\,\mathrm s$ and $6\,\mathrm s$. The total displacement of $180\,\mathrm m$ divided by $18\,\mathrm s$ gives $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slower section carries more weight because it lasts longer.
