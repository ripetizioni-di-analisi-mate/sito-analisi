An area is computed from a square side of $(10.0\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the first-order worst-case percentage uncertainty in the area?

- $4\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{\%}$.

## Explanation

Changing the side by $\delta\ell$ changes its square by a linear term $2\ell\delta\ell$ and a quadratic term $\delta\ell^2$. The requested model neglects the quadratic term, only $0.04\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The side has a $2\%$ relative margin, so to first order the area has a $4\%$ relative margin.

$$
\begin{aligned}
100\frac{\Delta A}{A}&\simeq 100\,\frac{2\ell\Delta\ell}{\ell^2}\\[4pt]
&= 4\,\mathrm{\%}
\end{aligned}
$$
