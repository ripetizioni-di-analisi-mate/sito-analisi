A set of readings contains one unusually high value. How can it affect the mean and the half-range?

- It can only decrease both.
- It changes the mean only if equal to the minimum.
- It always leaves the half-range unchanged.
- It can increase both.

## Explanation

The high reading enters the sum and can raise the mean. If it sets a new maximum, it also increases the difference between the extremes. If readings of $10$, $11$ and $12$ are joined by a reading of $20$, the mean rises from $11$ to $13.25$. The half-range grows from $1$ to $5$ because the maximum increases and the minimum stays the same.
