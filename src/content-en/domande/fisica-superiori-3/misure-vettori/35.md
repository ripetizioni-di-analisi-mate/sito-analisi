A sensor estimates the displacement components as $s_x=(3.0\pm0.1)\,\mathrm m$ and $s_y=(4.0\pm0.1)\,\mathrm m$. Every pair of values in the two intervals is possible. Which pair gives the largest magnitude?

- $(2.9;\,3.9)\,\mathrm m$
- $(2.9;\,4.1)\,\mathrm m$
- $(3.1;\,4.1)\,\mathrm m$
- $(3.1;\,3.9)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The magnitude is the square root of the sum of the two squared components. Every value in both intervals is positive, so increasing either component increases the magnitude. The maximum uses the two upper endpoints together, $3.1\,\mathrm m$ and $4.1\,\mathrm m$.
