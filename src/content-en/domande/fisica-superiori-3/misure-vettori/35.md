Compare $(20.0\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $(5.0\pm0.1)\,\mathrm{cm}$. By what factor is the second relative uncertainty larger than the first?

- $\frac{1}{2}$.
- $4$.
- $2$.
- $1$.

## Explanation

The absolute uncertainties have the same unit but apply to different central values. The first measurement allows a one-percent deviation. The second allows two parts per hundred because its central value is four times smaller while its absolute margin is only twice as small.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\varepsilon_2}{\varepsilon_1}&=\frac{0.1/5.0}{0.2/20.0}\\[4pt]
&= 2
\end{aligned}
$$
