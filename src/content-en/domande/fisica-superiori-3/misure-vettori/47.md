The lowest and highest readings are $3.4$ and $4.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The half-range is used as uncertainty. What value is reported?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $3.8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $1.6\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The range measures the width of the observed interval. The margin on either side of its midpoint is half that width.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x&=\frac{4.2-3.4}{2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.4\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

The range is $(4.2-3.4)\,\mathrm{cm}=0.8\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the margin is $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Centring that margin at $3.8\,\mathrm{cm}$ recovers both observed endpoints exactly.
