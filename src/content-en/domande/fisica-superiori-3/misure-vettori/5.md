A displacement has components $s_x=5\,\mathrm m$ and $s_y=6\,\mathrm m$. Calculate its magnitude.

- $s=7.81\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $3.905\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $15.62\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $11.71\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The components are along perpendicular axes. By the Pythagorean theorem, the sum of their squares is the square of the magnitude.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\sqrt{s_x^2+s_y^2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 7.81\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
