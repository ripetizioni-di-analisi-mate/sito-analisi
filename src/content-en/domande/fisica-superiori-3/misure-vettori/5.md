A displacement has components $s_x=5\,\mathrm m$ and $s_y=6\,\mathrm m$. What is its magnitude?

- $7.81\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $3.905\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $15.62\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $11.71\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The components are along perpendicular axes. By the Pythagorean theorem, the sum of their squares is the square of the magnitude.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\sqrt{s_x^2+s_y^2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 7.81\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

As a geometric check, the diagonal must be longer than the longer leg of $6\,\mathrm m$. It must also be shorter than the $11\,\mathrm m$ path along both legs.
