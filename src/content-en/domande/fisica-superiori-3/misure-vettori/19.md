An area is $250\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is it in square metres?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $0.025\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $0.0025\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

## Explanation

Each side of the unit square is converted, so the factor $10^{-2}$ appears twice. Using it once leaves one side in centimetres.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=250\cdot(10^{-2})^2\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.025\,\mathrm{m^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

A square metre contains $10000$ square centimetres because each direction fits one hundred centimetre-long segments. The given area therefore corresponds to one fortieth of a square metre.
