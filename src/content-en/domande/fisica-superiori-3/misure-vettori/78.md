The horizontal components of two vectors cancel, while both vertical components are positive. Where does their resultant point?

- Horizontally to the right.
- Vertically upwards.
- Diagonally upwards to the right.
- It has no direction because it is zero.

## Explanation

The sum of horizontal components is zero. The sum of vertical components is strictly positive, leaving an upward vertical vector.
