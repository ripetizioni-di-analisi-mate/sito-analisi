The horizontal components of two vectors cancel, while both vertical components are positive. Where does their resultant point?

- Horizontally to the right.
- Vertically upwards.
- Diagonally upwards to the right.
- It has no direction because it is zero.

## Explanation

The sum of horizontal components is zero. The sum of vertical components is strictly positive, leaving an upward vertical vector. For example, $(3,2)$ and $(-3,5)$ give $(0,7)$ in the units of the components. Every other pair satisfying the condition works the same way because the two vertical contributions add.
