A route follows two consecutive sides of a parallelogram. Which element represents its total displacement?

- The diagonal pointing from the start to the opposite vertex.
- The perimeter of the parallelogram.
- The diagonal joining the two intermediate vertices.
- The sum of the side lengths, always.

## Explanation

The resultant joins the tail of the first segment to the head of the last. These are opposite vertices and their connecting arrow is the head-to-tail diagonal. The path uses the sides, so the distance travelled is the sum of their lengths. The diagonal gives the direct change in position between the endpoints, pointing from the start towards the finish.
