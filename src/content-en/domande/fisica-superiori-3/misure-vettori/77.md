A route follows two consecutive sides of a parallelogram. Which element represents its total displacement?

- The diagonal pointing from the start to the opposite vertex.
- The perimeter of the parallelogram.
- The diagonal joining the two intermediate vertices.
- The sum of the side lengths, always.

## Explanation

The resultant joins the tail of the first segment to the head of the last. These are opposite vertices and their connecting arrow is the head-to-tail diagonal.
