With fixed magnitude $F$, when do two different forces have the same positive vertical component and opposite horizontal projections?

- When the angles are opposite about the $x$-axis.
- When one angle is twice the other.
- When the angles from the positive $x$-axis are supplementary, one acute and one obtuse.
- When one force is parallel to $x$ and the other to $y$.

## Explanation

Supplementary angles in the first and second quadrants have the same positive sine and opposite cosines. Going from one to the other reflects the arrow about the vertical axis. For example, at $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$ the vertical component is half the magnitude in both cases. The horizontal projections have the same length because the arrows make the same acute angle with the vertical axis.
