A force of magnitude $F=18\,\mathrm N$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis. Calculate the component $F_x$.

- $4.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $F_x=9\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $13.5\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The angle is measured from the $x$-axis, so the projection onto that axis uses the cosine. The sign matches that of the cosine.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_x&=F\cos\theta\\[4pt]
&= 9\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
