During a displacement $\vec s=(4,2)\,\mathrm m$, a constant force $\vec F=(3,F_y)\,\mathrm N$ does zero work. What is its vertical component $F_y$?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The work of a constant force is the dot product $\vec F\cdot\vec s$. The horizontal projection gives the positive term $3\cdot4\,\mathrm J=12\,\mathrm J$. For the total to vanish, the vertical term must cancel it, so $12+2F_y=0$ in SI values. This gives $F_y=-6\,\mathrm N$, pointing opposite to the vertical component of the displacement.
