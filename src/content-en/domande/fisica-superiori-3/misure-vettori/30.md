A graph of mass $m$ against volume $V$ passes through the origin. How is a material with greater density identified?

- By its smaller slope.
- By its greater slope.
- By the same slope and a longer line.
- By the position of the origin.

## Explanation

Density is the increase in mass per unit volume. A greater slope means the same added volume produces a greater increase in mass.
