The combined mass of a container and different volumes of the same liquid is recorded. The model assumes uniform density and a constant tare. How much must the last reading change from its recorded value to agree with the first two?

| Volume (cm³) | Mass (g) |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | 40 |
| 30 | 80 |
| 50 | 130 |

- $-10\,\mathrm g$
- $+10\,\mathrm g$
- $-20\,\mathrm g$
- $0\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

From $10$ to $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ the mass increases by $40\,\mathrm g$. The same increase in volume from $30$ to $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ should produce the same mass increase. The expected final reading is $80+40=120$ in grams. Since the table gives $130\,\mathrm g$, the correction is $-10\,\mathrm g$.
