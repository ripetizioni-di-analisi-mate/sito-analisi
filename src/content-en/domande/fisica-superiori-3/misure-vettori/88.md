To find an included angle using the dot product, when is $\cos\theta=\dfrac{\vec A\cdot\vec B}{AB}$ unusable?

- When the two nonzero vectors are perpendicular.
- When both nonzero vectors are parallel.
- When the components of the vectors have units of length.
- When at least one vector is zero.

## Explanation

A zero vector has no direction and makes the denominator zero. A zero dot product between two vectors of positive magnitude is usable and gives a right angle. In the perpendicular case the numerator vanishes while the denominator stays positive, so the ratio is defined. With the zero vector, the direction needed to define the angle is absent as well.
