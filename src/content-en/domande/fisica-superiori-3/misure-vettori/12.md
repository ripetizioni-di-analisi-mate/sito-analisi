A slot is $(31.0\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and the part that must fit into it is $(29.0\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide. Assuming any pair of values from the two intervals is possible, what is the minimum clearance, meaning the slot width minus the part width?

- $0.7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The clearance decreases if the slot narrows or the part widens. Subtract the maximum part width from the minimum slot width to obtain $(30.6-29.3)\,\mathrm{mm}=1.3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The margin is positive even for the least favourable combination, so the part fits throughout the permitted range.
