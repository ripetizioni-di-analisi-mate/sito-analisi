Three readings of the same length are $12\,\mathrm{cm}$, $12.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $11.9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate their arithmetic mean.

- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $\bar\ell=12\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Add the three readings in the same unit and divide by their number. Round the result to the same decimal place as the readings.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar\ell&=\frac{\ell_1+\ell_2+\ell_3}{3}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 12\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
