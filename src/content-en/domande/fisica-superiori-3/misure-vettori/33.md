Two assembled systems have the same mass $m$. The second occupies $1.5$ times the first one's volume. What is the ratio $\dfrac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}$?

- $\frac{2}{3}$.
- $1$.
- $\frac{3}{2}$.
- $\frac{1}{2}$.

## Explanation

The equal masses cancel from the ratio. Density scales with reciprocal volume, so a larger volume gives a ratio below one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}&=\frac{m/V_2}{m/V_1}=\frac{V_1}{V_2}\\[4pt]
&= \frac{2}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

For example, if the first system occupies two units of volume, the second occupies three. With the same mass, the density ratio is the volume ratio taken in reverse order, equal to $\dfrac23$.
