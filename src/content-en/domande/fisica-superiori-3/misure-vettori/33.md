Two assembled systems have the same mass $m$. The second occupies $1.5$ times the first one's volume. What is the ratio $\rho_2/\rho_1$?

- $\frac{2}{3}$.
- $1$.
- $\frac{3}{2}$.
- $\frac{1}{2}$.

## Explanation

The equal masses cancel from the ratio. Density scales with reciprocal volume, so a larger volume gives a ratio below one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}&=\frac{m/V_2}{m/V_1}=\frac{V_1}{V_2}\\[4pt]
&= \frac{2}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
