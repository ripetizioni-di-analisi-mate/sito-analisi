A force has components $(5,-12)\,\mathrm N$ in the original axes. The instrument axes rotate $90^\circ$ clockwise while the force arrow stays fixed. What is the new horizontal component $F_{x'}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Rotating the $x$ axis clockwise makes the positive $x'$ direction point opposite to the old $y$ direction. The new projection has the same magnitude as the old vertical component and the opposite sign. Thus $F_{x'}=-F_y=12\,\mathrm N$. The force itself stays fixed in space.
