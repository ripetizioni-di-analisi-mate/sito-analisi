A displacement has components $s_x=6\,\mathrm m$ and $s_y=7\,\mathrm m$. Calculate its magnitude.

- $s=9.22\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $4.61\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $18.44\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $13.83\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The components are along perpendicular axes. By the Pythagorean theorem, the sum of their squares is the square of the magnitude.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s&=\sqrt{s_x^2+s_y^2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 9.22\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
