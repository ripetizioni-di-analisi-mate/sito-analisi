Two displacements are $\vec A=(5,2)\,\mathrm m$ and $\vec B=(-1,6)\,\mathrm m$. Calculate the magnitude of their sum.

- $4.47\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $17.88\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $R=8.94\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $13.41\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Add the corresponding components, because each axis is treated separately. Find the magnitude from the two components of the resultant.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=\sqrt{(A_x+B_x)^2+(A_y+B_y)^2}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 8.94\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
