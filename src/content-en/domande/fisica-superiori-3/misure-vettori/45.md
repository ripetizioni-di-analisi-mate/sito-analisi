A first group contains four readings with a mean of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The second group has a mean of $14\,\mathrm{cm}$. The mean of all the readings is $13\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many readings are in the second group?

- $12$
- $4$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

Relative to the combined mean, the first group has a total deviation of $4(10-13)\,\mathrm{cm}=-12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Per reading, the mean deviation in the second group is $14-13=1$ in centimetres. The second group therefore needs twelve readings for the two total deviations to cancel.
