Two displacements both have magnitude $4\,\mathrm m$. A geometric constraint restricts the included angle to between $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$, inclusive. What is the smallest possible magnitude of the resultant?

- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $4\sqrt2\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The squared resultant is $(32+32\cos\theta)\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The cosine decreases as the angle increases over the given interval, so the minimum occurs at $120^\circ$. There it equals $-\dfrac12$ and the squared resultant is $16\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The constraint excludes the $180^\circ$ angle needed for the resultant to vanish.
