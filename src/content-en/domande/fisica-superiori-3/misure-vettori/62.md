Two displacements of $1\,\mathrm m$ each form an angle of $60^\circ$. What is the magnitude of their sum?

- $\sqrt2\,\mathrm m$
- $\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The square of the sum's magnitude includes the two squares and twice the dot product. In square metres this is $1+1+2\cos60^\circ=3$. The square root gives $\sqrt3\,\mathrm m$. The value $\sqrt2\,\mathrm m$ would correspond to perpendicular directions, making the term involving the cosine vanish.
