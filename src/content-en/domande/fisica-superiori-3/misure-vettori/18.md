A flow rate is $3\,\mathrm{L/min}$. What is it in cubic centimetres per second?

- $3\,\mathrm{cm^3/s}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{cm^3/s}$.
- $180\,\mathrm{cm^3/s}$.
- $3000\,\mathrm{cm^3/s}$.

## Explanation

Each litre contains $1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, so $3000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ pass in one minute. Dividing by $60$ spreads that volume over the seconds in a minute.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=3\cdot\frac{1000}{60}\\[4pt]
&= 50\,\mathrm{cm^3/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
