A length measurement is $\ell=(18.00\pm0.16)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the percentage uncertainty, to three significant figures?

- $0.444\%$.
- $1.78\%$.
- $0.889\%$.
- $1.33\%$.

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the magnitude of the central value. Multiplication by $100$ gives the percentage.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varepsilon_{\%}&=100\frac{\Delta\ell}{\lvert\ell\rvert}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.889\%
\end{aligned}
$$

One per cent of $18.00\,\mathrm{cm}$ is $0.18\,\mathrm{cm}$. The stated margin is slightly smaller, so the percentage must be just below one per cent.
