A length measurement is $\ell=(18\pm0.16)\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate its percentage uncertainty.

- $0.4445\%$.
- $1.778\%$.
- $\varepsilon_{\%}=0.889\%$.
- $1.333\%$.

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the magnitude of the central value. Multiplication by $100$ gives the percentage.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\varepsilon_{\%}&=100\frac{\Delta\ell}{\lvert\ell\rvert}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 0.889\%
\end{aligned}
$$
