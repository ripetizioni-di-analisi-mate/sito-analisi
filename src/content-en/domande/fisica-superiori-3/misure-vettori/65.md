A force points $30^\circ$ to the right of the positive $y$-axis and has magnitude $20\,\mathrm N$. What is its horizontal component?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $10 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The angle is stated from the vertical. The horizontal leg is opposite the $30^\circ$ angle, so it equals half the magnitude.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_x&=20\sin(30^\circ)\\[4pt]
&= 10\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$

The same calculation can be done with the angle measured from the horizontal. In that case the angle is $60^\circ$ and the cosine applies. Both angle conventions give $10\,\mathrm N$ to the right because they describe the same triangle.
