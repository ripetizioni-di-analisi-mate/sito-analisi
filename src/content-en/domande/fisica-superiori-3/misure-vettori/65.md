A force of magnitude $20\,\mathrm N$ points $30^\circ$ right of the positive $y$-axis. What is its horizontal component?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $10 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The angle is stated from the vertical. The horizontal leg is opposite the $30^\circ$ angle, so it equals half the magnitude.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_x&=20\sin(30^\circ)\\[4pt]
&= 10\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
