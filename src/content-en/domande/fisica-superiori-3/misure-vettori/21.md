In a velocity–time graph, what are the units of the slope when velocity is in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time is in seconds?

- $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{m}$
- $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{s^2/m}$

## Explanation

Slope divides a velocity change by a time change. Obtain the unit by dividing $\mathrm{m/s}$ by $\mathrm{s}$.
