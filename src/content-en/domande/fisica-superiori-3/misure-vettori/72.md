A robot first moves by $(3,4)\,\mathrm m$. Its second displacement has components $(-2k,k)\,\mathrm m$ with $k>0$. The value of $k$ is chosen so the resultant points vertically upwards. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $7\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $5.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

A vertical resultant requires the horizontal sum to vanish. The equation $3-2k=0$ gives $k=1.5$. The vertical sum is then $(4+1.5)\,\mathrm m=5.5\,\mathrm m$ and is positive as required. With zero horizontal component, the magnitude equals the length of this vertical component.
