The readings $x$ of a length have a half-range of $0.30\,\mathrm{cm}$. A new calibration assigns each reading the value $L=1.20x-0.50\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the half-range of the corrected lengths?

- $0.24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The positive factor preserves the order of the readings. Subtracting the two corrected endpoints cancels the $-0.50\,\mathrm{cm}$ term and leaves $1.20$ times the original range. Halving the range gives $1.20\cdot0.30\,\mathrm{cm}=0.36\,\mathrm{cm}$. The calibration gain changes the spread, while a common shift of the endpoints leaves it unchanged.
