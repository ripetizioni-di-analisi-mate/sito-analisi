Two nonzero vectors have a negative dot product. What is the range of their included angle, between zero and $180^\circ$?

- $90^\circ<\theta\leq180^\circ$
- $0^\circ\leq\theta<90^\circ$
- $\theta=90^\circ$
- $\theta=0^\circ$

## Explanation

The magnitudes have a positive product, so the sign of the dot product is the sign of the cosine of the included angle. Cosine is negative above $90^\circ$.
