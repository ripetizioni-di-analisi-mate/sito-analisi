In the formula $x=kt^2$, $x$ is position and $t$ is time. What is the SI unit of $k$?

- $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m\cdot s^2}$
- $\mathrm{s^2/m}$
- $\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The parameter is the ratio of a length to the square of a time. Multiplying its unit by $\mathrm{s^2}$ recovers the metre.
