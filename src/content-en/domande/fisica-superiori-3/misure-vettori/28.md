Two homogeneous samples of the same metal have different volumes. Which relation holds?

- The difference in their masses is the density.
- The larger sample has greater density.
- The volume-to-mass ratio changes with volume.
- The mass-to-volume ratio is the same.

## Explanation

Mass is proportional to volume for samples of the same homogeneous material. Their common proportionality coefficient is the density. If under the same conditions the second sample takes up twice the volume, it also has twice the mass. Dividing each mass by its own volume therefore gives the same value.
