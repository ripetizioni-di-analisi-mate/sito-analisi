A vehicle is travelling at $v=57.6\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is its speed in metres per second?

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $32\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The factor $1000$ converts kilometres to metres. The factor $3600$ converts hours to seconds in the denominator.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{\mathrm{m/s}}&=\frac{1000}{3600}v_{\mathrm{km/h}}\\[4pt]
&= 16\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

The factor $\dfrac5{18}$ is less than one, so the numerical value in metres per second must decrease. The reverse conversion gives $16\cdot3.6=57.6$, recovering the starting value.
