A camera takes one image every $20\,\mathrm{ms}$. Four frames show the motion from the first mark to the second, including the two frames at the crossings. Assume each crossing occurs exactly at its recorded frame time. The marks are $0.72\,\mathrm m$ apart on a straight line. What is the magnitude of the average velocity in kilometres per hour?

- $12\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $4.32\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $43.2\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $129.6\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

Four consecutive frames delimit three intervals. The time between the crossings is therefore $3\cdot20\,\mathrm{ms}=0.060\,\mathrm s$. Dividing $0.72\,\mathrm m$ by this time gives $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Multiplying by $3.6$ gives $43.2\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Counting four intervals would include one frame interval beyond the final crossing.
