Two lengths have intervals $[7.0,7.2]$ and $[7.3,7.6]\,\mathrm{cm}$. Are they compatible according to interval overlap?

- Yes, because the uncertainties have the same order of magnitude.
- Yes, because both intervals are positive.
- Yes, because the centres are less than a centimetre apart.
- No, the first upper endpoint lies below the second lower endpoint.

## Explanation

The criterion requires at least one common value. Every value in the first interval is less than the minimum of the second, so none belongs to both. The first interval ends at $7.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the second begins at $7.3\,\mathrm{cm}$. This leaves a gap of $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$, enough to rule out any common point.
