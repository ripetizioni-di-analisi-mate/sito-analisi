Which quantity gives the torque for a displacement $\vec r$ from the pivot and a force $\vec F$?

- $\vec r\cdot\vec F$
- $\vec r\times\vec F$
- $\vec r+\vec F$
- $\vec F-\vec r$

## Explanation

Torque comes from the component of force transverse to the displacement from the pivot. The cross product incorporates that component and gives the orientation of rotation.
