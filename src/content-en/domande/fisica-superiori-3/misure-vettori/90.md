The vector $\vec r$ runs from the pivot to the point where a force $\vec F$ acts. Which product gives its torque about the pivot?

- $\vec r\cdot\vec F$
- $\vec r\times\vec F$
- $\vec r+\vec F$
- $\vec F-\vec r$

## Explanation

Torque comes from the component of force transverse to the displacement from the pivot. The cross product incorporates that component and gives the orientation of rotation. The direction follows the right-hand rule and the unit is the newton metre, the product of force and length. If the force and the position vector are parallel, the torque is zero.
