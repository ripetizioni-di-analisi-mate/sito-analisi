A piece of plastic floats, and its density is to be found from mass and total volume. Which volume belongs in $m/V$?

- The full volume of the piece.
- The submerged volume of the floating piece.
- The volume of water in the container before the test.
- The difference between the full and submerged volumes.

## Explanation

The calculation concerns mass per unit volume of the whole body. The submerged volume describes the displaced liquid while floating and accounts for only part of the body's volume.
