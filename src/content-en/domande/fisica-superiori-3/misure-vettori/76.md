Two consecutive displacements are swapped in head-to-tail addition. Which quantity is preserved?

- The intermediate point, always.
- The first vector, always.
- The direction of every parallelogram side is reversed.
- The resultant from start to finish.

## Explanation

Component addition is commutative, so both constructions reach the same final endpoint. The intermediate position depends on which displacement is made first. The second path reaches the other side vertex of the parallelogram as its intermediate point. From there the remaining segment reaches the same opposite corner, so the diagonal representing the resultant is identical.
