The axes $x',y'$ are rotated anticlockwise from the original axes. The rotation angle $\phi$ has $\cos\phi=\dfrac45$ and $\sin\phi=\dfrac35$. A force has components $(3,4)\,\mathrm N$ in the rotated axes. What is its horizontal component in the original axes?

- $3\,\mathrm N$
- $4.8\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$
- $-5\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

To reconstruct the force, add $3\,\mathrm N$ along $x'$ to $4\,\mathrm N$ along $y'$. Their projections onto the original $x$-axis are $3\cos\phi$ and $-4\sin\phi$ in newtons. Both have magnitude $2.4\,\mathrm N$ and opposite signs, so the horizontal component vanishes.
