A quotient of positive measurements has relative uncertainties of $2\%$ in the numerator and $3\%$ in the denominator. Any pair of values within the two intervals is possible. What first-order relative margin is estimated in the worst case?

- $1\,\%$.
- $5\,\%$.
- $6\,\%$.
- $3\,\%$.

## Explanation

The ratio increases with its numerator and decreases with its denominator. Raising one and lowering the other therefore increase the ratio together. The worst-case estimate adds the two relative contributions.

$$
\begin{aligned}
100\varepsilon_q&\simeq 2+3\\[4pt]
&= 5\,\%
\end{aligned}
$$

The exact ratio of the maximum to the nominal value is $\dfrac{1.02}{0.97}$, about $1.0515$. The exact increase is slightly larger than the first-order estimate.
