A quotient of positive measurements has relative uncertainties of $2\%$ in the numerator and $3\%$ in the denominator. What first-order relative margin is estimated in the worst case?

- $1\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{\%}$.

## Explanation

The ratio increases with its numerator and decreases with its denominator. Raising one and lowering the other therefore increase the ratio together. The worst-case estimate adds the two relative contributions.

$$
\begin{aligned}
100\varepsilon_q&\simeq 2+3\\[4pt]
&= 5\,\mathrm{\%}
\end{aligned}
$$
