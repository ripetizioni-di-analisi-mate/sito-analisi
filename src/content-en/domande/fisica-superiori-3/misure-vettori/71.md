A sensor records $A_x=2\,\mathrm N$ and reports a magnitude $A=1\,\mathrm N$. Which check rules out the pair?

- The two values have the same unit.
- Both values are positive.
- A component's magnitude cannot exceed the total magnitude.
- The $y$-component might be zero.

## Explanation

The squared magnitude adds the measured component's square to another nonnegative square. It must therefore be at least as large as that component's square.
