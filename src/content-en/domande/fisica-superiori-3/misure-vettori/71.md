For the same force vector, the component $A_x=2\,\mathrm N$ and the magnitude $A=1\,\mathrm N$ are given. Which observation is enough to conclude that the two data are not compatible?

- The two values have the same unit.
- Both values are positive.
- A component's magnitude cannot exceed the total magnitude.
- The $y$-component might be zero.

## Explanation

The squared magnitude is the sum of the square of $A_x$ and the square of $A_y$, which is not negative. The squared magnitude is therefore at least as large as the square of $A_x$. The square of the given magnitude is $1\,\mathrm{N^2}$, while the contribution of $A_x$ alone is $4\,\mathrm{N^2}$. Even with $A_y=0$ the magnitude would therefore be at least $2\,\mathrm N$, so the two data cannot refer to the same vector.
