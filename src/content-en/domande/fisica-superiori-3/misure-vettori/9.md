A vehicle is travelling at $v=64.8\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Express its speed in metres per second.

- $v=18\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $27\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The factor $1000$ converts kilometres to metres. The factor $3600$ converts hours to seconds in the denominator.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{\mathrm{m/s}}&=\frac{1000}{3600}v_{\mathrm{km/h}}\\[4pt]
&= 18\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
