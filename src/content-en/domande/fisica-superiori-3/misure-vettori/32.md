A homogeneous plate has mass $24\,\mathrm g$. Drilling a cylindrical hole removes a quarter of its volume. How much mass remains?

- $6\,\mathrm{g}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{g}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{g}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{g}$.

## Explanation

Density is uniform, so the fraction of mass removed equals the fraction of volume removed. Three quarters of the initial mass remain.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m_{\mathrm{rim}}&=24\left(1-\frac14\right)\\[4pt]
&= 18\,\mathrm{g}
\end{aligned}
$$
