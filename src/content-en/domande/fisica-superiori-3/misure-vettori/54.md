On a screen each horizontal division represents $1\,\mathrm m$ and each vertical division represents $2\,\mathrm m$. An arrow tip is three divisions to the right and two divisions above its tail. What is the magnitude of the displacement shown?

- $\sqrt{13}\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $7\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The three horizontal divisions represent $3\,\mathrm m$. The two vertical divisions together represent $4\,\mathrm m$. With both components in the same unit, Pythagoras gives $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\,\mathrm m=5\,\mathrm m$. Measuring the arrow directly on the screen would ignore the different scale factors of the axes.
