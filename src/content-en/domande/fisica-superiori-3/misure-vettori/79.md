An opposing horizontal flow of magnitude $3$ is added to a horizontal vector of magnitude $5$. What is the qualitative effect on the resultant?

- The resultant points with the flow.
- The resultant is perpendicular to both vectors.
- The resultant keeps the larger vector's orientation and is smaller in magnitude than both.
- The resultant is larger in magnitude than both.

## Explanation

With both vectors on the same line in opposite directions, the sum is their signed difference. Taking the larger vector's direction as positive gives $5-3=2$.
