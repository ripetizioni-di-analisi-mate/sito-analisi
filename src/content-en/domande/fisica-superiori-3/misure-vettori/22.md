The model $v=2at$ is dimensionally consistent. What does the check prove?

- The numerical coefficient must be $2$.
- The units of $at$ match those of $v$.
- The acceleration must be constant.
- The object always starts from rest.

## Explanation

Acceleration times time has the dimensions of velocity. A dimensionless factor such as $2$ leaves them unchanged, so its correctness requires further information. The equation $v=at$ also has matching dimensions. With constant acceleration and a start from rest, that is the law for the velocity. Choosing the coefficient therefore requires physical conditions as well as units.
