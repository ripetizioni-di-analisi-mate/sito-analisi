A person moves north, then the same distance south. Which quantity retains the length of the full path?

- The magnitude of the resultant displacement.
- The northward component of the resultant displacement.
- The total distance travelled.
- The eastward component of the resultant displacement.

## Explanation

The opposite displacements cancel in the vector sum. Distance is the sum of the two segment lengths. For example, with two sections of $20\,\mathrm m$, the final point coincides with the starting point. Both components of the net displacement are zero, while the path length is $40\,\mathrm m$.
