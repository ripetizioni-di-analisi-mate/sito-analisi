Two lengths are $(12.0\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $(7.0\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the largest possible difference, first minus second, using the allowed endpoints?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $5.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $5.1\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The difference increases with its first term and decreases with its second. The first upper endpoint and the second lower endpoint both make it larger than the central difference.

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\max}&=(12.0+0.2)-(7.0-0.3)\\[4pt]
&\simeq 5.5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
