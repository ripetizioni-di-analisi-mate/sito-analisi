The displacement in the diagram goes from $P$ to $Q$. What is the ratio of the vertical displacement component to the horizontal component?

::grafico{vettori="-1,2,3,-1,s" punti="-1,2,pieno,P;3,-1,pieno,Q" xmin=-2.5 xmax=5 ymin=-2 ymax=3.5 nomi="x (m),y (m)" uguale=1 titolo="P has coordinates (−1, 2) m and Q has coordinates (3, −1) m."}

- $-\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac34$
- $-\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac43$

## Explanation

From $P$ to $Q$ the horizontal coordinate increases from $-1$ to $3\,\mathrm m$, so $s_x=4\,\mathrm m$. The vertical coordinate drops from $2$ to $-1\,\mathrm m$, giving $s_y=-3\,\mathrm m$. The ratio is $-\dfrac34$. It is negative because the segment slopes down to the right, and it is dimensionless. Shifting the origin of the graph would add the same term to both coordinates along each axis. The two differences and their ratio would remain the same.
