For two vectors of magnitudes $3$ and $4$, can the resultant have magnitude $0.5$?

- Yes, when perpendicular.
- Yes, when nearly opposite.
- No, the minimum is $3$.
- No, the minimum is $1$.

## Explanation

The greatest possible cancellation occurs for opposite vectors. Their magnitude difference remains, so no angle yields a magnitude below one. As the angle moves away from $180^\circ$, its cosine increases, and so does the squared magnitude of the resultant. The value $0.5$ is therefore excluded at every angle.
