For two vectors of magnitudes $3$ and $4$, can the resultant have magnitude $0.5$?

- Yes, when perpendicular.
- Yes, when nearly opposite.
- No, the minimum is $3$.
- No, the minimum is $1$.

## Explanation

The greatest possible cancellation occurs for opposite vectors. Their magnitude difference remains, so no angle yields a magnitude below one.
