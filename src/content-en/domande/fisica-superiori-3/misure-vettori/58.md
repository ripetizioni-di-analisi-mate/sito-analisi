Two nonzero displacements have equal magnitude and opposite components. How are they oriented relative to one another?

- They are perpendicular.
- They lie along the same direction with opposite senses.
- They lie along the same direction with the same sense.
- They always make an angle of $45^\circ$.

## Explanation

Changing both signs multiplies the vector by $-1$. The magnitudes of its projections stay the same while the arrow reverses. The included angle is therefore $180^\circ$. If the vectors represent consecutive displacements, their sum is the zero vector because each component is cancelled by the opposite component.
