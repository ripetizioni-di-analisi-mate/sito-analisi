Two length vectors are $\vec A=(2,1)\,\mathrm m$ and $\vec B=(1,3)\,\mathrm m$. What is the area of the parallelogram they span?

- $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

## Explanation

The cross product measures base times height because it includes only the second vector's transverse component. In the plane its $z$-component comes from the difference between the two crossed Cartesian products.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A&=\lvert A_xB_y-A_yB_x\rvert\\[4pt]&=\lvert 2\cdot3-1\cdot1\rvert\\[4pt]
&= 5\,\mathrm{m^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
