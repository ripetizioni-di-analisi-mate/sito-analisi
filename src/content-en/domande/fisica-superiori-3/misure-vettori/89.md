A constant force of $20\,\mathrm N$ makes $60^\circ$ with a displacement. What magnitude would a force parallel to and pointing with the same displacement need in order to do the same work?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

For the same displacement, work depends only on the component along it. The new force lies wholly along the displacement, so its magnitude equals the original force's projection.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F'&=20\cos(60^\circ)\\[4pt]
&= 10\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$

The projection is $20\cos60^\circ\,\mathrm N=10\,\mathrm N$. The transverse component of the original force is perpendicular to the displacement and therefore makes no contribution to the work.
