Three readings of the same length are $11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$, $11.7\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $11.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate the arithmetic mean and round to the nearest tenth of a centimetre.

- $5.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $23\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $17.25\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Add the three readings in the same unit and divide by their number. Round the result to the same decimal place as the readings.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar\ell&=\frac{\ell_1+\ell_2+\ell_3}{3}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 11.5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

The deviations from $11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ are $0$, $+0.2$ and $-0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$. Their mean deviation is therefore positive and less than half a tenth. The requested rounding brings the result to $11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
