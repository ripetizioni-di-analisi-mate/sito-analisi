Two displacements $\vec A$ and $\vec B$ each have magnitude $5\,\mathrm m$. Their sum has magnitude $8\,\mathrm m$. What is $\vec A\cdot\vec B$?

- $7\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Expanding the squared norm of the sum gives $R^2=A^2+B^2+2\vec A\cdot\vec B$. The difference $(64-25-25)\,\mathrm{m^2}=14\,\mathrm{m^2}$ contains twice the dot product, so the product is $7\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The sign is positive because the resultant is longer than it would be with perpendicular vectors.
