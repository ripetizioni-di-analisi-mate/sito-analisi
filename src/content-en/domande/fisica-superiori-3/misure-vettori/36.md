A weighing must have a total error of at most $1.0\,\mathrm g$ in magnitude. Calibration contributes a possible additive error of at most $0.6\,\mathrm g$ in magnitude. What is the largest remaining margin for repeatability error if both errors can act in the same direction?

- $0.2\,\mathrm g$
- $0.6\,\mathrm g$
- $1.6\,\mathrm g$
- $0.4\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

In the least favourable case the two contributions have the same sign. The sum of their magnitudes must stay within $1.0\,\mathrm g$. The margin for the second error is therefore $(1.0-0.6)\,\mathrm g=0.4\,\mathrm g$. With a larger value, combining the two extreme errors could violate the requirement.
