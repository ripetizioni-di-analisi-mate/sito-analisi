A homogeneous sample has mass $54\,\mathrm{g}$ and density $2.7\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What volume does it occupy?

- $\frac{729}{5}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.

## Explanation

Density specifies the mass of each cubic centimetre. The volume is the number of portions of $2.7\,\mathrm g$ contained in the total mass. Dividing grams by grams per cubic centimetre leaves cubic centimetres. As a check, $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of the material has mass $54\,\mathrm g$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V&=\frac m\rho\\[4pt]&=\frac{54}{2.7}\\[4pt]
&= 20\,\mathrm{cm^3}
\end{aligned}
$$
