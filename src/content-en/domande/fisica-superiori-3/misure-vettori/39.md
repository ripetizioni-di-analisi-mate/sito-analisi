A reading has a central value of zero and positive absolute uncertainty. How is its uncertainty correctly reported?

- As zero percent.
- As one hundred percent.
- As an absolute margin, because the relative one is undefined.
- As a percentage equal to the numerical value of the margin.

## Explanation

Calculating the relative margin would require dividing by the central value. When it is zero, use the interval given by absolute uncertainty. For example, $(0.0\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ describes the interval from $-0.2$ to $+0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Both endpoints remain meaningful in the same unit without a reference percentage.
