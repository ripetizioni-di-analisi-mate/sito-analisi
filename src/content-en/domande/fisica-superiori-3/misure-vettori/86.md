The order of the two vectors in a nonzero cross product is reversed. How does the result change?

- It retains magnitude and orientation.
- It retains magnitude and reverses orientation.
- It doubles in magnitude and retains orientation.
- It becomes zero.

## Explanation

Reversing the vectors swaps the two terms of the determinant in the plane. The difference changes sign, reversing the perpendicular component.
