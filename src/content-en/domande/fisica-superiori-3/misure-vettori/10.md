A sample has mass $m=0.48\,\mathrm{kg}$ and volume $V=0.000228\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Calculate its density.

- $1055\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $\rho=2.11\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $4220\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $3165\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

## Explanation

Density measures mass per unit volume. The data are already in kilograms and cubic metres, so their ratio is in SI units.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho&=\frac mV\\[4pt]
&\simeq 2.11\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}
\end{aligned}
$$
