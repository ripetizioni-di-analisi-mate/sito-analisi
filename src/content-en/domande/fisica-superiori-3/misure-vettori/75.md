Two displacements of magnitudes $3\,\mathrm m$ and $4\,\mathrm m$ have a resultant of magnitude $7\,\mathrm m$. How are they oriented?

- Parallel in opposite senses.
- Perpendicular.
- Parallel and in the same sense.
- At $60^\circ$.

## Explanation

For two nonzero vectors, equality in the triangle inequality occurs when both have the same orientation. Their lengths then add without cancellation.
