Two displacements of magnitudes $3\,\mathrm m$ and $4\,\mathrm m$ have a resultant of magnitude $7\,\mathrm m$. How are they oriented?

- Parallel in opposite senses.
- Perpendicular.
- Parallel and in the same sense.
- At $60^\circ$.

## Explanation

For two nonzero vectors, equality in the triangle inequality occurs when both have the same orientation. Their lengths then add without cancellation. With perpendicular vectors, the magnitude would be only $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\,\mathrm m=5\,\mathrm m$. With opposite directions it would be $1\,\mathrm m$. The value of $7\,\mathrm m$ is the maximum and requires the same direction.
