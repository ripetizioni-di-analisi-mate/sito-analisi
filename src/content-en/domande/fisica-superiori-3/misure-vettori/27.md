Mixing two liquids conserves mass, but their measured final volume is $20\%$ less than the sum of their initial volumes. By what percentage does the final density exceed the estimate based on adding the volumes?

- $20\%$
- $50\%$
- $25\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

The two estimates have the same mass in the numerator. The volume in the denominator becomes $0.80$ times the assumed volume, so the actual density is $\dfrac1{0.80}=1.25$ times the estimate based on additive volumes. The increase is $25\%$. The percentage change in the ratio depends on the reciprocal of the volume factor.
