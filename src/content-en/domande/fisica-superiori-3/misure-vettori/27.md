A $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ piece of density $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ is joined to a $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ piece of density $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Their volumes add. What is the combined object's mean density?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- $3.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.

## Explanation

Find the masses separately, $40$ and $120\,\mathrm g$, because each piece has its own density. The ratio of total mass to total volume is a volume-weighted mean. The denser material occupies the larger share, so the mean exceeds $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}&=\frac{20\cdot2+30\cdot4}{20+30}\\[4pt]
&\simeq 3.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}
\end{aligned}
$$
