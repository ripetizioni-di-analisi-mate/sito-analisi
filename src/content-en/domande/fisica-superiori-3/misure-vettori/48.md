A reading equal to the sample mean is added. How do the mean and the half-range change?

- The mean increases and the half-range is unchanged.
- The mean stays the same and the half-range halves.
- Both increase.
- Both stay the same.

## Explanation

The sum gains one copy of the mean and the divisor increases by one, leaving their ratio unchanged. The new reading lies between the original extremes, so they stay the same. For example, adding ten to the readings eight and twelve keeps the mean at ten. The range stays four as well because the new reading lies between the original two.
