A displacement has magnitude $10\,\mathrm m$ and the absolute difference of its two components is $2\,\mathrm m$, so $|s_x-s_y|=2\,\mathrm m$. What is the product $s_xs_y$?

- $32\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $56\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The squared magnitude gives $s_x^2+s_y^2=100\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The squared difference gives $4\,\mathrm{m^2}=s_x^2+s_y^2-2s_xs_y$. Subtracting the two relations gives $2s_xs_y=96\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the product is $48\,\mathrm{m^2}$. This follows even without knowing which of the two components is larger.
