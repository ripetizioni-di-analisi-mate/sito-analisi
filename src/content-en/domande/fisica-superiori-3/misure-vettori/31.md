A density is $1.25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Express it in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

- $\frac{5}{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $125\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $1250000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

## Explanation

The mass in the numerator is multiplied by $10^{-3}$. The volume in the denominator is multiplied by $10^{-6}$, so the overall factor for their ratio is $10^3$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho&=1.25\cdot\frac{10^{-3}}{10^{-6}}\\[4pt]
&= 1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}
\end{aligned}
$$

In SI units, a cubic centimetre of material occupies $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and has mass $0.00125\,\mathrm{kg}$. Dividing that mass by the volume gives $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
