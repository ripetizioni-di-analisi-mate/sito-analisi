A horizontal force itself rotates from $0^\circ$ to $90^\circ$ at fixed magnitude. What happens to its contribution along $x$?

- It stays equal to the magnitude.
- It decreases from the magnitude to zero.
- It increases from zero to the magnitude.
- It is negative throughout the motion.

## Explanation

The horizontal projection follows the cosine of the angle from the positive $x$-axis. Cosine falls from one to zero in the first quadrant.
