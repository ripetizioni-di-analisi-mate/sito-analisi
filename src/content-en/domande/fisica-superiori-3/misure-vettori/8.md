Two forces have components $\vec A=(5,2)\,\mathrm N$ and $\vec B=(3,6)\,\mathrm N$. What is the dot product $\vec A\cdot\vec B$?

- $13.5\,\mathrm{N^2}$.
- $54\,\mathrm{N^2}$.
- $40.5\,\mathrm{N^2}$.
- $27\,\mathrm{N^2}$.

## Explanation

In orthonormal axes, only products of components along the same axis contribute. Add the horizontal contribution to the vertical one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec A\cdot\vec B&=A_xB_x+A_yB_y\\[4pt]
&= 27\,\mathrm{N^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

The two terms are $15\,\mathrm{N^2}$ and $12\,\mathrm{N^2}$. Both are positive because the signs agree on both axes. Their positive sum is consistent with an acute angle between the forces.
