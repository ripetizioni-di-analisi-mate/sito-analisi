A reading in metres is converted to centimetres. How do its absolute and relative uncertainties change?

- Both are multiplied by one hundred.
- The numerical absolute uncertainty is multiplied by one hundred, and the relative uncertainty stays the same.
- Both remain numerically unchanged.
- The absolute uncertainty is divided by a hundred and the relative one doubles.

## Explanation

The conversion multiplies both the central estimate and its uncertainty in the same unit. The factor cancels from their ratio. For example, $(0.80\pm0.02)\,\mathrm m$ becomes $(80\pm2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. In both cases the ratio of the margin to the central value is $0.025$, or $2.5\%$.
