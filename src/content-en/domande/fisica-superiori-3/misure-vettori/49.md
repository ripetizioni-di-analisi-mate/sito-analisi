If the same additive offset is corrected in every reading, how do the mean and the half-range change?

- The mean shifts by the correction and the half-range stays the same.
- Both shift by the correction.
- Only the half-range shifts.
- The mean and half-range are both unchanged.

## Explanation

The offset occurs in every term of the mean. The same amount is subtracted from both extremes, so their difference does not change. Changing the origin shifts every reading by the same amount. It can still change the relative uncertainty because the new central value appears in the denominator of the ratio.
