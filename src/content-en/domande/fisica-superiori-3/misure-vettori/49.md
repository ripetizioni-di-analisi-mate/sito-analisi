If the same additive offset is corrected in every reading, how do the mean and the half-range change?

- The mean shifts by the correction and the half-range stays the same.
- Both shift by the correction.
- Only the half-range shifts.
- The mean and half-range are both unchanged.

## Explanation

The offset occurs in every term of the mean. The same amount is subtracted from both extremes, so their difference does not change.
