A displacement has coordinates $(-3,4)\,\mathrm m$. Which angle from the positive $x$-axis is consistent with its signs?

- About $53.1^\circ$.
- About $233.1^\circ$.
- About $306.9^\circ$.
- About $126.9^\circ$.

## Explanation

The negative horizontal and positive vertical component put the vector in the second quadrant. Its acute reference angle is $\arctan\left(\dfrac43\right)\simeq53.1^\circ$, so subtract it from $180^\circ$. The sign of the tangent alone does not identify the quadrant because it is negative in the fourth quadrant too. The vector tip lies to the left and above the origin, determining the required angle.
