A displacement has coordinates $(-3,4)\,\mathrm m$. Which angle from the positive $x$-axis is consistent with its signs?

- About $53.1^\circ$.
- About $233.1^\circ$.
- About $306.9^\circ$.
- About $126.9^\circ$.

## Explanation

The negative horizontal and positive vertical component put the vector in the second quadrant. Its acute reference angle is $\arctan(4/3)\simeq53.1^\circ$, so subtract it from $180^\circ$.
