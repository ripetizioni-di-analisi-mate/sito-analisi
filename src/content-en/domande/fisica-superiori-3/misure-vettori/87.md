Two nonzero vectors have zero cross product and negative dot product. How are they oriented?

- Parallel with the same orientation.
- Perpendicular.
- Parallel with opposite orientations.
- At an angle of $45^\circ$.

## Explanation

The sine of the angle must be zero, so the angle is zero or $180^\circ$. The negative cosine selects $180^\circ$. Vectors pointing the same way would give a positive dot product. Oppositely directed vectors can have different magnitudes, but the second is a negative multiple of the first. The cross-product condition ensures they are collinear.
