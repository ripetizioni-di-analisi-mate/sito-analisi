A person starts at position $x_0=3\,\mathrm m$. From the velocity graph, what position does the person reach at $t=5\,\mathrm s$?

::grafico{linee="0,4 2,4;2,-2 5,-2" segmenti="2,-2,2,4;5,-2,5,0" poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,4 0,4;2,0 5,0 5,-2 2,-2" xmin=0 xmax=5.7 ymin=-3 ymax=5 nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="The velocity is 4 m/s for the first 2 s and −2 m/s from 2 to 5 s."}

- $17\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $11\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The first rectangle gives $4\cdot2\,\mathrm m=8\,\mathrm m$. The section below the axis gives $(-2)\cdot3\,\mathrm m=-6\,\mathrm m$. The signed area is the displacement of $2\,\mathrm m$. Add the starting coordinate to obtain the requested position, $x=3\,\mathrm m+2\,\mathrm m=5\,\mathrm m$. The sum of the area magnitudes would be $14\,\mathrm m$, the distance travelled over both sections. The final position requires the signed displacement.
