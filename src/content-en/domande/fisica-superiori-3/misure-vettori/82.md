In a right-handed coordinate system, $\vec A=(2,3)\,\mathrm m$ and $\vec B=(4,b)\,\mathrm m$ are in the $xy$ plane. The $z$-component of $\vec A\times\vec B$ is $-4\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the vertical component $B_y$?

- $-4\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In the plane the cross product has component $A_xB_y-A_yB_x$ along $z$. With values in metres this gives $2b-3\cdot4=-4$, so $b=4$. The unknown vertical component contributes $+8\,\mathrm{m^2}$, which only partly offsets the negative contribution of $-12\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
