For what component $b$ are $(2,3)$ and $(6,b)$ perpendicular?

- $4$.
- $-4$.
- $-6$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

Set the dot product to zero because both vectors are nonzero. The unknown component must contribute $-12$, balancing the positive horizontal contribution. With $b=-4$, the vertical contribution is $3(-4)$, so the sum is indeed zero.

$$
\begin{aligned}
0&=2\cdot6+3b\\[4pt] b&=-\frac{12}{3}\\[4pt]
&= -4
\end{aligned}
$$
