Two bodies meet at $t=7\,\mathrm s$ in the ground frame. An accelerating observer keeps parallel axes and shares the time origin. What time does that observer measure for their meeting?

- $7\,\mathrm s$.
- $0\,\mathrm s$.
- $14\,\mathrm s$.
- $3.5\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

With the common classical clock, subtract the same $X(t)$ from both coordinates, even when the origin accelerates. Equal coordinates remain equal at the same instant. The accelerated motion may change that common coordinate. The event is still the same meeting and the clocks have been synchronised.
