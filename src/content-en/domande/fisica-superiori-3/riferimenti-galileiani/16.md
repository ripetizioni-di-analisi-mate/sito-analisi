A moving clock is set to zero when the station clock reads $3\,\mathrm s$. Both classical clocks run at the same rate. How are their readings related?

- $t'=t+3\,\mathrm s$.
- $t'=3t$.
- $t'=t$.
- $t'=t-3\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

At the moving clock's start, $t=3\,\mathrm s$ and its own reading is zero. It then gains one second per station second. The intervals are equal. The moving time is therefore $t'=t-3\,\mathrm s$. When the station reads $7\,\mathrm s$, the moving clock reads $4\,\mathrm s$.
