A moving clock starts when the station clock reads 3 s. The two classical clocks tick at the same rate. How are the readings related?

- $t'=t+3\,\mathrm s$.
- $t'=3t$.
- $t'=t$.
- $t'=t-3\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

At the moving clock's start, $t=3\,\mathrm s$ and its own reading is zero. It then gains one second per station second. The intervals are equal.
