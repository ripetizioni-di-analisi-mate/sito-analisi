On the graph, the lines represent a point and the moving frame's origin. What do their vertical gap and its constancy represent?

::grafico{f="2*x;2*x+3" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="t (s),x (m)" legenda="\text{origin};\text{point}" titolo="Comparison of positions"}

- Constant positive relative acceleration.
- Relative velocity of three metres per second.
- Constant relative position.
- Origins always coincident.

## Explanation

The horizontal axis is time, so subtracting the vertical coordinates compares two positions at the same instant. The parallel lines stay three metres apart. The slope of the difference is zero. The point therefore has the same velocity as the moving origin and is at rest in that frame.
