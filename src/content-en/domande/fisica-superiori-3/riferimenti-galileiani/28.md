Two model cars have components $v_A=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_B=+5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same frame. What is the component of $B$ as seen from $A$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

To view the motion from the first model car subtract its velocity. The original components point in opposite directions, so the relative magnitude is the sum of their magnitudes.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_{B/A}&=v_B-v_A=5-(-3)\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

Swapping the two observers preserves the magnitude and reverses the direction. The sign reflects which one was chosen as the frame.
