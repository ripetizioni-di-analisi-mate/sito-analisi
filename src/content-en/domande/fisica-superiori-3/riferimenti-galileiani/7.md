A body has acceleration $a=4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ relative to the ground. A carriage with parallel axes accelerates in the same direction with $A=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the component of the body's acceleration relative to the carriage.

- $6.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $a’=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Relative velocity is the difference between the body and carriage velocities. Comparing their changes over the same interval means subtracting their accelerations as well. The body accelerates faster than the carriage, so its relative acceleration stays positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a'&=a-A\\[4pt]&=4.9-1.3=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
