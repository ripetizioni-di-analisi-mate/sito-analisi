An object has acceleration $a=4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ relative to the ground. A cabin whose axes are parallel to and oriented the same way as the ground axes accelerates in the same direction with $A=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the object's acceleration component relative to the cabin.

- $6.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Relative velocity is the difference between the body and carriage velocities. Comparing their changes over the same interval means subtracting their accelerations as well. The body accelerates faster than the carriage, so its relative acceleration stays positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a'&=a-A\\[4pt]&=4.9-1.3=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

If these accelerations hold for one second, the body's ground velocity increases by $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the carriage velocity increases by $1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Their difference therefore increases by $3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
