Two cyclists at $8$ and $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with the faster one $20\,\mathrm m$ behind, continue for $7\,\mathrm s$. Are they at the same position during this leg?

- Yes, they meet after ten seconds within the leg.
- Yes, they meet after two seconds.
- No, the gap grows by fourteen metres.
- No, six metres remain at the final instant.

## Explanation

The gap shrinks at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for seven seconds, dropping from twenty to six metres. The extrapolated crossing at ten seconds would fall after the leg. Over the stated stretch the gap decreases continuously to its final value.
