Two skaters are $42\,\mathrm m$ apart and move towards each other at $4$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until they meet?

- $6\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $42\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $14\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

With the axis pointing towards the second skater, the components are four and minus three. The gap decreases by seven metres per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\dfrac{42}{4-(-3)}\\[4pt]
&= 6\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
