A moving frame has axes parallel to and pointing the same way as the ground axes. At ground time $t=0$ its origin is at $X_0=52\,\mathrm m$ and moves at $u=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its clock reads $t'=t+2\,\mathrm s$. What is the origin's ground coordinate when its clock reads $7\,\mathrm s$?

- $37\,\mathrm{m}$
- $31\,\mathrm{m}$
- $52\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The zero used in the position equation belongs to the ground clock. When the moving clock reads $7\,\mathrm s$, the ground clock has advanced $(7-2)\,\mathrm s=5\,\mathrm s$ from its own zero. The required coordinate is therefore $52\,\mathrm m+(-3)\cdot5\,\mathrm m=37\,\mathrm m$. The $-15\,\mathrm m$ term is the origin's displacement, added to its initial position.
