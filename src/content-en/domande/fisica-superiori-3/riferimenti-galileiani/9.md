The origins of two frames coincide at $t=0$. The moving frame translates at $u=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. A point has moving-frame coordinate $x’=7\,\mathrm m$ at $t=5\,\mathrm s$. Find its coordinate in the stationary frame.

- $x=37\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-23\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $23\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Starting from the stationary origin, first reach the moving origin, which has travelled $ut$. Add the coordinate $x'$ measured from that moving origin. Both components are positive, so the final coordinate exceeds either component.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=ut+x'\\[4pt]&=6\cdot5+7=37\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$
