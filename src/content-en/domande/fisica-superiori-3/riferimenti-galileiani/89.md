A real force of $20\,\mathrm N$ acts in the positive direction on a $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ body. The moving frame accelerates in the same direction at $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What relative acceleration is measured?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The apparent term is minus twelve newtons because it opposes the frame acceleration. Eight newtons remain in the positive direction. Dividing by mass gives the relative velocity change per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a'&=\dfrac{20-4\cdot3}{4}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
