A particle has $v'=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $S'$ and $v=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $S$. If both are components along the same oriented axis, what is the velocity of $S'$ along it?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Apply $u=v-v'$ to the two components with the same sign. Their difference is zero. This measurement determines only the component along the chosen axis.
