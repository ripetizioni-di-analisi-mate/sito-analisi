A boat's velocity relative to the water has magnitude $v_b=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to the bank. The current parallel to the bank has magnitude $u=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the magnitude of the velocity over the ground.

- $6.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v=5.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $3.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The two velocities are perpendicular components. The magnitude is the hypotenuse of the velocity triangle. As a check, it exceeds either component and is smaller than their arithmetic sum.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\sqrt{v_b^2+u^2}\\[4pt]&=\sqrt{5^2+1.6^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq5.25\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
