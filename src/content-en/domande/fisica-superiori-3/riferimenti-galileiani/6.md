The $x$-axis points downstream and the $y$-axis towards the opposite bank. A boat's velocity relative to the water is $(-1.05,\,4.20)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The uniform current flows downstream at $4.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the boat's ground velocity?

- $5.94\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The boat points partly upstream. Adding the current gives a downstream component of $(4.20-1.05)\,\mathrm{m/s}=3.15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The transverse component remains $4.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. These are respectively three and four times $1.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so they form a triangle with side lengths proportional to three, four and five. The magnitude is $5\cdot1.05\,\mathrm{m/s}=5.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
