A moving walkway travels at $u=2.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A person walks along it in the opposite direction with relative speed $w=0.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the ground-velocity component, taking the walkway's direction as positive.

- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-2.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v=2.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The relative speed $w$ is a magnitude. Since the person walks in the negative direction, enter that motion as component $-w$. The positive result means the walkway's motion outweighs the walk in the opposite direction.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=u-w\\[4pt]&=2.8-0.7=2.1\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
