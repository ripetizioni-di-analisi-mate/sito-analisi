In $4.0\,\mathrm s$ the origin of a platform moves $1.2\,\mathrm m$ forward relative to the ground. In the same interval a box moves $7.2\,\mathrm m$ forward relative to the platform. The axes are parallel and both forward displacements are positive. What is the ground component of the box's average velocity?

- $1.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the coordinate relations at the two ends of the interval makes the ground displacement the sum of the platform and relative displacements. This gives $1.2+7.2=8.4$ metres. Dividing by $4.0\,\mathrm s$ gives $2.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The endpoints suffice for this average even if velocities change in between.
