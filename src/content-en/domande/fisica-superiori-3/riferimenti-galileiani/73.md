Two objects have velocities $\vec v_A$ and $\vec v_B$. To change frame, subtract the same $\vec u$ from each. What happens to $\vec v_A-\vec v_B$?

- It stays the same vector.
- It decreases by $\vec u$.
- It increases by $\vec u$.
- It vanishes in every case.

## Explanation

The difference becomes $(\vec v_A-\vec u)-(\vec v_B-\vec u)$. The two $\vec u$ contributions enter with opposite signs and cancel. This also holds for velocities pointing in different directions.
