Both observers reverse the orientation of their axes. What happens to the scalar relation between velocity components?

- Every component changes sign and the relation keeps the same form.
- Only the relative velocity changes sign.
- Every component keeps its sign.
- The transformation becomes a product of components.

## Explanation

The new axis assigns the opposite value to each projection. Multiplying $v=v'+u$ by $-1$ gives the same rule for the new components. The new convention changes what a positive sign means for both observers. Subtraction or addition must use components expressed in the common direction convention.
