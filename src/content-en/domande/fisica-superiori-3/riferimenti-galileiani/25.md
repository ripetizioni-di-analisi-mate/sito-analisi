The relation $x'=2\,\mathrm m$ holds at all times, and the moving origin follows $X(t)=4t$ in SI units. How does the point move on the ground?

- Ha legge $x=4t+2$.
- Ha legge $x=2$.
- Ha legge $x=4t$.
- Ha legge $x=4t-2$.

## Explanation

The point remains two metres ahead of the moving origin, which advances. Its ground coordinate is $X(t)+2$ metres at each instant.
