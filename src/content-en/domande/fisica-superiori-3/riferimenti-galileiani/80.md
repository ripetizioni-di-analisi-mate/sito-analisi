A bus moves forward and brakes. A suitcase is horizontally free on its smooth floor and initially at rest relative to the bus. How does the suitcase start to move as seen from the bus?

- It moves towards the rear.
- It stays at rest relative to the bus.
- It rises vertically.
- It moves towards the front.

## Explanation

The bus acceleration points towards the rear. The suitcase initially keeps its ground velocity and gains position relative to the slowing bus.
