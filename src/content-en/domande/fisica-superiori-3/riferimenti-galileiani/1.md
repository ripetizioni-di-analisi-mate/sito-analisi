The origins of two frames with parallel, consistently oriented axes coincide at $t=0$. The moving frame translates at $u=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. A point has relative coordinate $x'=6\,\mathrm m$ at time $t=4\,\mathrm s$. What is its coordinate in the fixed frame?

- $26\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-14\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The moving origin has travelled $ut$ from the stationary origin. The coordinate $x'$, measured from that moving origin, is added to this distance. Both components are positive, so the final coordinate exceeds either component.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=ut+x'\\[4pt]&=5\cdot4+6=26\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$

After $4\,\mathrm s$ the moving origin is at $20\,\mathrm m$. The point is another $6\,\mathrm m$ in the same direction. As a check, subtracting the origin's position from the result recovers the given relative coordinate.
