A river is $d=45\,\mathrm m$ wide. A boat points perpendicular to the banks at relative speed $v_b=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The current has speed $u=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the drift along the bank.

- $x=16.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $124.6\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The transverse velocity $v_b$ determines the crossing time. The current acts along the bank and carries the boat by $ut$ over the same interval. The answer is given to the precision of the options.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\dfrac{d}{v_b}=\dfrac{45}{3.6}\,\mathrm s\\[4pt]x&=ut=\dfrac{1.3\cdot45}{3.6}\,\mathrm m\simeq16.2\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$
