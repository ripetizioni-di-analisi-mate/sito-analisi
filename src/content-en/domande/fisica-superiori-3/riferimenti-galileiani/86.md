The only apparent-force term to be used in a moving frame is $-m\vec A$. What assumption about the motion of the axes is required in this model?

- The axes rotate around the origin.
- The axes translate and remain parallel to the inertial axes.
- The origin must be stationary.
- The mass is zero.

## Explanation

The derivation subtracts the origin's acceleration while keeping the same basis directions. If those directions change too, differentiating the positions yields additional terms. A rotating frame can have apparent terms even if its origin is momentarily at rest.
