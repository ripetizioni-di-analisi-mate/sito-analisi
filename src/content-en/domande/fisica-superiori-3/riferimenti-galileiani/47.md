A pursuer catches an athlete in $6\,\mathrm s$. The athlete started $24\,\mathrm m$ ahead and moved at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the pursuer's velocity?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Dividing the lead by time gives the closing speed of four metres per second. The pursuer's ground velocity must exceed the other runner's by this amount.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_A&=v_B+\dfrac{d}{t}=5+\dfrac{24}{6}\\[4pt]
&= 9\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

As a check, in $6\,\mathrm s$ the pursuer covers $54\,\mathrm m$. The other athlete covers $30\,\mathrm m$ beyond a $24\,\mathrm m$ head start and reaches the same point.
