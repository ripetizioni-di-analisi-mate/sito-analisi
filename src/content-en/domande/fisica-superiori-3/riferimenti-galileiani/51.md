Two bodies $A$ and $B$ both move in the positive direction along the same line. The signed separation $x_B-x_A$ is $(t-2)(t-6)$ in metres when $t$ is in seconds, for $t\geq0$. What happens at the two instants when the separation vanishes?

- $B$ passes $A$ at the first instant. At the second they move apart without meeting.
- $A$ passes $B$ at both instants.
- $A$ passes $B$ at the first instant and $B$ passes $A$ at the second.
- $A$ and $B$ have the same velocity at both instants.

## Explanation

Before $2\,\mathrm s$ both factors are negative and $B$ is ahead. Between $2$ and $6\,\mathrm s$ one factor is negative, so $A$ is ahead. After $6\,\mathrm s$ both are positive and $B$ is ahead again. The separation changes sign at each zero, so both meetings are overtakes.
