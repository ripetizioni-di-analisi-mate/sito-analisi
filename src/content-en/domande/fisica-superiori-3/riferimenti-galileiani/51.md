A target passes the pursuer's starting point before the pursuer begins to move. What information is sufficient to determine a future meeting after departure?

- The pursuer has the same velocity as the target at departure and keeps it.
- The earlier passage time alone.
- At departure the target is ahead, and from that instant both maintain constant velocities in the same direction, with the pursuer faster.
- Both move at constant velocities of any size.

## Explanation

Set the start of the pursuit at the actual departure. A positive separation with a positive constant closing speed follows a decreasing line that reaches zero in the future.
