A $15\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate stays at rest on a platform that accelerates rightwards at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the real horizontal force from the platform on the crate?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $-30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Rest on the platform means that the crate shares its ground acceleration. A real force to the right is needed to produce it. In the platform frame this balances the apparent term to the left.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_x&=mA=15\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= 30\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
