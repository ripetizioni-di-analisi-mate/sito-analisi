An athlete at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ catches a teammate at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ by the end of a leg lasting $9\,\mathrm s$. What is the largest initial gap if catching at the final instant is allowed?

- $90\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $54\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $144\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The available time bounds the recoverable distance. Four metres are gained each second. A larger gap would require an interval longer than the assigned leg.

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\max}&=(v_A-v_B)t_{\max}=(10-6)\cdot9\\[4pt]
&= 36\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

With exactly that gap the athletes reach the same position at the final allowed instant.
