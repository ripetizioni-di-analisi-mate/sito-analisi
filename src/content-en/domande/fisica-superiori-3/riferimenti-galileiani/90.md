A frame with parallel axes has zero acceleration at $t=2\,\mathrm s$ but accelerates before and afterwards. What follows about the apparent term due to translation?

- It vanishes throughout the interval.
- It vanishes at that instant.
- It depends only on the frame velocity at two seconds.
- It is greatest when acceleration vanishes.

## Explanation

The term is the instantaneous product of mass and the opposite acceleration. Vanishing at one moment recovers the inertial balance only at that moment.
