A lift accelerates upwards at $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ stays on the scale. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What does the scale read?

- $600\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $540\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $660\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The scale measures the normal force. In the ground frame, the normal must exceed the weight by sixty newtons to accelerate the person upwards. The difference is the net force.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N-mg&=mA\\[4pt]N&=60(10+1)\\[4pt]
&= 660\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
