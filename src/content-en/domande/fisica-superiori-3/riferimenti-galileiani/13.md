A boat points towards the opposite bank at a transverse velocity of $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the water and has no relative along-bank component. In the first $24\,\mathrm m$ across the river the current is $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ downstream. In the next $32\,\mathrm m$ it is $0.70\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upstream. What is the along-bank displacement, taking downstream as positive?

- $18.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $29.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-18.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23.1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The transverse component is the same in both bands, so the times are $\dfrac{24}{4.0}\,\mathrm s=6\,\mathrm s$ and $\dfrac{32}{4.0}\,\mathrm s=8\,\mathrm s$. In the first band the boat drifts downstream, in the second upstream. With the stated signs the result is $(4.0\cdot6-0.70\cdot8)\,\mathrm m=18.4\,\mathrm m$. A simple average of the currents would give them equal times.
