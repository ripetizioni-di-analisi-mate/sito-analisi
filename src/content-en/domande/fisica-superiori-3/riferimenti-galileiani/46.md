$B$ starts at the origin at $t=0$ with $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. $A$ leaves the same point $3\,\mathrm s$ later at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How many seconds does $A$ run before catching $B$?

- $5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The unknown is the running time of the later starter. At that start, the runner already moving has a twelve-metre lead. It closes at six metres per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{avvio}}&=4\cdot3=12\,\mathrm m\\[4pt]\Delta t_A&=\dfrac{12}{10-4}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
