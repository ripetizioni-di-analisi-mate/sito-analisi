A train travels at $u=16\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A passenger walks towards the front at $v’=1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the carriage. Find the velocity relative to the ground.

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v=17.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $14.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Take the train's direction as positive. Walking towards the front adds a second positive component relative to the carriage. The station therefore sees the passenger moving forward faster than the carriage.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=u+v'\\[4pt]&=16+1.2=17.2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
