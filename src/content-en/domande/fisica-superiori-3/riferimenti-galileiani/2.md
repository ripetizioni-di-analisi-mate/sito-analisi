A train travels at $u=15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A passenger walks towards the front at $v’=1.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the carriage. Find the velocity relative to the ground.

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v=16.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $1.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $13.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Take the train's direction as positive. Walking towards the front adds a second positive component relative to the carriage. The station therefore sees the passenger moving forward faster than the carriage.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=u+v'\\[4pt]&=15+1.1=16.1\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
