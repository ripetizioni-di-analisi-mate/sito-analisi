A passenger walks at constant velocity relative to the carriage. Initially the station measures $12.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the train and $13.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the passenger, taking the direction of the train as positive. The train accelerates in a straight line to $15.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the passenger's ground velocity at the final instant?

- $13.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $28.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Initially the velocity relative to the carriage is $(13.1-12.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}=1.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The passenger keeps this component in the train frame. At the final instant, add it to the new train velocity to get $(15.0+1.1)\,\mathrm{m/s}=16.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The composition is instantaneous. Both contributions refer to the final instant even though the train has accelerated.
