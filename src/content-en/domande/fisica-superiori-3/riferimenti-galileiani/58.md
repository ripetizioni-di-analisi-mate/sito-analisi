A boat must land directly opposite in a current $\vec u=(4,0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What along-bank component must its velocity relative to the water have?

- $v'_x=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v'_x=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v'_x=0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v'_x=-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The along-bank ground velocity is $4+v'_x$. Zero drift requires this component to vanish. The boat points partly upstream.
