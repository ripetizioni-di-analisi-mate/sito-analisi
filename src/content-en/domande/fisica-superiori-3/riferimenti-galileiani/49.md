A position–time graph shows two intersecting straight lines. What proves that the body previously behind is ahead after the crossing?

- Its line has the greater slope.
- Its line has the larger intercept.
- The slopes of the two lines are equal.
- The crossing time is negative.

## Explanation

After the common instant, the vertical coordinates separate in proportion to the slope difference. The line with the larger slope rises relative to the other. If the slopes are $v_A$ and $v_B$, the new position difference after the meeting is proportional to $v_A-v_B$.
