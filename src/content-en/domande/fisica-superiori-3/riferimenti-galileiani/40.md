A lift carriage rises at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A small object moves down inside it at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the carriage. With upwards positive, what is its ground velocity?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The same addition rule applies on a vertical axis. The lift's upward motion cancels part of the relative downward motion, so the ground result still points down.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=u+v'=2-3\\[4pt]
&= -1\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

The ground result is $(2-3)\,\mathrm{m/s}=-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The downward speed on the ground is smaller than the one measured from the carriage.
