A drone has velocity $(1,2.5)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to a ferry. The ferry has velocity $(0,3)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the water, and the water has velocity $(2,0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the bank. The axes are parallel and point the same way. What is the drone's speed relative to the bank? Round to two decimal places.

- $4.03\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The chain starts with the drone, then passes through the ferry and the water to the bank. Add components along each axis to get $(1+0+2,\ 2.5+3+0)\,\mathrm{m/s}=(3,5.5)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The speed is the magnitude of this vector, $\sqrt{3^2+5.5^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq6.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Adding the three speeds separately would discard the directions of the individual contributions.
