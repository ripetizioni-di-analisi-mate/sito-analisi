The position equations intersect only at $t=-4\,\mathrm s$. The interval studied starts at $t=0$ and the velocities remain the same. How should the result be interpreted?

- The bodies meet after four seconds.
- The intersection lies outside the interval studied.
- The bodies are four metres apart.
- The bodies have the same velocity.

## Explanation

The sign refers to the selected time origin. The algebraic crossing precedes the start of the motions under study, and the constant velocities give a single intersection.
