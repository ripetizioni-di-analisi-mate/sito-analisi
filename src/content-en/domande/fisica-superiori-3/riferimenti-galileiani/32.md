A sphere has relative velocity $\vec v'=(1,5)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The moving frame has velocity $\vec u=(3,-5)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which way does the sphere move on the ground?

- Towards positive $y$.
- Towards negative $x$.
- Towards negative $y$.
- Towards positive $x$.

## Explanation

Add corresponding components. The vertical component is $5-5=0$, while the horizontal component is $1+3=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right.
