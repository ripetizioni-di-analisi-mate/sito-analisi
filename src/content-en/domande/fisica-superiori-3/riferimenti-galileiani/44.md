A bicycle starts at $x=2\,\mathrm m$ at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and meets a second bicycle following $x=20-4t$ in SI units. At what position do they meet?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Equating the position laws first gives the common time. Substituting two seconds into either law gives the meeting point. The other equation gives the same coordinate.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2+5t&=20-4t\\[4pt]t&=2\,\mathrm s\\[4pt]x&=2+5\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= 12\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

Equating $2+5t=20-4t$ gives $9t=18$ in SI values. Its initial $2\,\mathrm m$ must be added to its displacement.
