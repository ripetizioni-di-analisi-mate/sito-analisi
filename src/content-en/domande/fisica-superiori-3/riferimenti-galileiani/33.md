A target is stationary on the ground. A moving platform translates at $\vec u=(3,4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What target velocity is measured from the platform?

- $(-3,-4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $(3,4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $(3,-4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $(0,0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The ground measures $\vec v=\vec 0$. Subtracting the platform velocity reverses each of its components. The velocity seen from the platform is opposite to the platform velocity seen from the target.
