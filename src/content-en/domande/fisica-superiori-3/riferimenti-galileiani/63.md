In a uniform current a boat crosses a $36\,\mathrm m$ wide channel and lands $27\,\mathrm m$ downstream. How long is the straight path observed on the ground?

- $63\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The width and drift are the two legs of the triangle. With constant velocity, the boat connects departure and landing along the hypotenuse.

$$
\begin{aligned}
L&=\sqrt{36^2+27^2}\\[4pt]
&= 45\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
