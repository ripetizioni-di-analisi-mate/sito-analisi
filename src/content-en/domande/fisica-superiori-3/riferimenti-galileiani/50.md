Two parallel belts carry markers at $7$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. The first marker moves from $2\,\mathrm m$ behind to $6\,\mathrm m$ ahead. How long does this change in separation take?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The relative displacement crosses zero. First gain two metres to line up, then six to pull ahead. Divide all eight metres by the relative velocity.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x_{\mathrm{rel}}&=6-(-2)=8\,\mathrm m\\[4pt]\Delta t&=\dfrac{8}{7-3}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
