A non-rotating carriage has acceleration $\vec A=(3,-2)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the apparent-force vector for a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass?

- $(6,-4)\,\mathrm N$.
- $(-3,2)\,\mathrm N$.
- $(-6,4)\,\mathrm N$.
- $(-6,-4)\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

Apply $-m\vec A$ component by component. The $x$ axis gives $-2\cdot3=-6$, and the $y$ axis gives $-2\cdot(-2)=4$ in newtons.
