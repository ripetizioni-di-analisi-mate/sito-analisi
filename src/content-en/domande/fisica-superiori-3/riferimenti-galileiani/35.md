Two trains move apart along the same axis in the same direction. The front train travels at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the rear at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With their direction positive, how fast does their separation increase?

- $38\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The separation is the front train's position minus the rear train's position. Both advance, but the difference increases by two metres each second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\Delta d}{\Delta t}&=v_2-v_1=20-18\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
