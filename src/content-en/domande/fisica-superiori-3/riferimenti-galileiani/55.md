A channel narrows from $60$ to $40\,\mathrm m$. The boat points perpendicular to the banks at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the uniform current stays unchanged. By how many seconds does the crossing time decrease?

- $8\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

With the same transverse component, the wide stretch takes twelve seconds and the narrow one eight. Their difference is proportional to the difference in widths.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta t&=\dfrac{60}{5}-\dfrac{40}{5}\\[4pt]
&= 4\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
