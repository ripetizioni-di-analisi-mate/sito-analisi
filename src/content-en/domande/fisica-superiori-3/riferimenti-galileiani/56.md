A boat has transverse component $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In a $32\,\mathrm m$ river it wants to land $8\,\mathrm m$ upstream of where it starts. The current is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ downstream. What must $v'_x$ be if $x$ is positive downstream?

- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

After eight seconds the ground displacement must be minus eight metres, so the ground component is minus one. The water-relative component must also overcome the two-metres-per-second current.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\dfrac{32}{4}\,\mathrm s=8\,\mathrm s\\[4pt]v'_x&=\dfrac{-8}{8}-2\\[4pt]
&= -3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
