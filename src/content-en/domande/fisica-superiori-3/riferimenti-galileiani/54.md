A boat keeps pointing perpendicular to the bank and maintains the same speed relative to the water. The uniform current doubles. How do the crossing time and drift change?

- The time halves and the drift stays the same.
- The time is unchanged and the drift doubles.
- Both time and drift double.
- The time is unchanged and the drift halves.

## Explanation

The current acts only along the bank. The time given by $\dfrac{d}{v_b}$ stays the same. Over that time the downstream velocity doubles, doubling the displacement $ut$. The landing point shifts farther downstream and the path tilts more towards the current.
