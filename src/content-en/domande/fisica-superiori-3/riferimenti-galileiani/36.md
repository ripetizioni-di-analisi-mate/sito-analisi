An observer calculates the mean ground velocity between clock readings $t=2\,\mathrm s$ and $t=6\,\mathrm s$. The carriage clock reads $t'=t+3\,\mathrm s$, and the carriage velocity varies. Over which interval on its own clock must the mean carriage velocity be calculated for a valid subtraction?

- From $2$ to $6\,\mathrm s$.
- From $3$ to $7\,\mathrm s$.
- From $-1$ to $3\,\mathrm s$.
- From $5$ to $9\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

The displacement relation is subtracted between the same two events. The second clock reads $2+3=5$ seconds at the start and $6+3=9$ seconds at the end. Both intervals last four seconds. Since the carriage velocity changes, another interval of equal duration could give a different mean.
