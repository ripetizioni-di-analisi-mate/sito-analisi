In $5\,\mathrm s$ a moving origin advances by $25\,\mathrm m$ and a body advances by $35\,\mathrm m$ on the ground. Even though the origin accelerates, what average relative velocity follows?

- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The coordinate relation remains valid with changing velocities. Subtracting displacements and dividing by the shared time gives the average without assuming constant instantaneous velocities.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline v'&=\dfrac{\Delta x-\Delta X}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{35-25}{5}\\[4pt]
&= 2\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
