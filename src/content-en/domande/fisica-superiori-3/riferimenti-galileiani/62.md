In a uniform current the pilot can choose a heading while keeping a fixed relative speed. Which direction minimises the time to reach the other bank if the landing point is unrestricted?

- Angled upstream to land directly opposite.
- Perpendicular to the banks relative to the water.
- Parallel to the bank and downstream.
- Parallel to the bank and upstream.

## Explanation

The shortest time corresponds to the largest transverse component. Pointing perpendicular makes all available speed transverse, while the current determines the landing point.
