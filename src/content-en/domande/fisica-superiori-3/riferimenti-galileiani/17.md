At $t=2\,\mathrm s$, a moving origin is at $9\,\mathrm m$ with constant velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Where was it at $t=0$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Move back six metres from the position measured at two seconds. The nine metres are a coordinate and already include the displacement.

$$
\begin{aligned}
X_0&=X(2)-u\cdot2=9-3\cdot2\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

On a position-time graph the initial value is the intercept.
