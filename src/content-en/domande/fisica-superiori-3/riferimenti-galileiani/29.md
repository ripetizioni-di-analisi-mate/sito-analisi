A tram moves $18\,\mathrm m$ in $3\,\mathrm s$. A street lamp is stationary on the ground. What is the lamp's mean velocity relative to the tram over that same interval, taking the tram's direction as positive?

- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-18\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The lamp is at rest on the ground. In the tram frame the lamp's position moves towards the rear at the same rate the tram moves away.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v'_{\mathrm L}&=v_{\mathrm L}-u=0-\dfrac{18}{3}\\[4pt]
&= -6\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

Over the given interval the tram covers $18\,\mathrm m$ in $3\,\mathrm s$, with mean velocity $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The lamp has zero displacement relative to the ground. Subtracting the tram's displacement gives relative mean velocity $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
