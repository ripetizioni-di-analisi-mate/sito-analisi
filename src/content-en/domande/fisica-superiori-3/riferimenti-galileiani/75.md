In the station frame a constant-mass body accelerates due to a horizontal net force. On a train translating at constant velocity, which balance holds?

- $\sum F_x=0$.
- $\sum F_x=-ma'_x$.
- $\sum F_x=ma'_x$ con $a'_x=a_x$.
- $\sum F_x=m(a'_x+u)$.

## Explanation

The two accelerations agree because the frame velocity is constant. Newton's second law has the same form in both inertial frames. Adding velocity and acceleration would be dimensionally inconsistent.
