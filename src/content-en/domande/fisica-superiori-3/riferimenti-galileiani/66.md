A boat maintains a transverse component of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ through two river bands, $10$ and $20\,\mathrm m$ wide. The current flows downstream at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the first band and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the second. In the first band the boat points perpendicular to the banks. What along-bank component relative to the water must it use in the second band to arrive directly opposite its starting point, with downstream positive?

- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The first band takes $\dfrac{10}{2}\,\mathrm s=5\,\mathrm s$ and adds $5\,\mathrm m$ downstream. To undo that drift in the $\dfrac{20}{2}\,\mathrm s=10\,\mathrm s$ in the second band, the ground component must be $-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Subtracting the $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ current gives the component relative to that band, $-3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Cancelling only the second current would leave the first drift.
