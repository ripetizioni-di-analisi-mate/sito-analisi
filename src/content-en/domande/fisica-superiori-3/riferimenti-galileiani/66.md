A boat points perpendicular to the banks with transverse speed $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The current is $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ across the first $10\,\mathrm m$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ across the next $20\,\mathrm m$. Find the drift.

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $35\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Add the along-bank displacements of the two legs. The two currents act for five and ten seconds respectively, so their weights differ and their simple average does not apply.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=1\cdot\dfrac{10}{2}+3\cdot\dfrac{20}{2}\\[4pt]
&= 35\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
