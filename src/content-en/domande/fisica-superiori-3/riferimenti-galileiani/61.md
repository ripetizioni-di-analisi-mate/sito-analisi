With the same transverse speed, the graph shows two crossing paths on the ground. Which path experiences a faster downstream current?

::grafico{f="2*x;x" xmin=0 xmax=13 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="x (m),y (m)" legenda="\text{A};\text{B}" titolo="Two paths observed from the bank"}

- Path B.
- Path A.
- The currents are equal.
- The boat's mass is needed to compare them.

## Explanation

At the same transverse ordinate, path B has a greater downstream coordinate. The times to reach that ordinate are equal, so B has a greater downstream velocity.
