Two boats point perpendicular to the banks relative to the water and have the same transverse velocity. The graph shows their paths as seen from the bank. Which experiences a faster downstream current?

::grafico{curve="t/2,t,0,8;t,t,0,8" segmenti="0,8,9,8" punti="4,8,pieno;8,8,pieno" etichette="4.16,8.28,A;8.16,8.28,B" xmin=0 xmax=9.5 ymin=0 ymax=9.25 uguale=1 nomi="downstream x (m),transverse y (m)" legenda="\text{A};\text{B}" titolo="Two paths observed from the bank"}

- Path B.
- Path A.
- The currents are equal.
- The boat's mass is needed to compare them.

## Explanation

At the same transverse ordinate, path B has a greater downstream coordinate. The times to reach that ordinate are equal, so B has a greater downstream velocity. For example, at $y=8\,\mathrm m$ route $A$ is at $x=4\,\mathrm m$ and route $B$ is at $x=8\,\mathrm m$. Neither boat's velocity relative to the water has an along-bank component, so the difference comes from the current.
