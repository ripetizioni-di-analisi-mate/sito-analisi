A platform follows a circle at constant speed. A frame with origin on the platform keeps its axes parallel to the ground axes. Why is the frame accelerating?

- The origin's velocity changes direction.
- The origin's speed increases.
- The velocity vector is always the same.
- Parallel axes make the acceleration zero.

## Explanation

Acceleration measures any change in the velocity vector. Changing direction produces a change even at constant magnitude. The axes can remain parallel while the origin accelerates. The origin therefore accelerates towards the centre even if the axis arrows keep fixed directions.
