Two trolleys start at the same coordinate with different constant velocities. At which times do their coordinates agree?

- At every instant.
- Only after a positive interval.
- Only at the start.
- At every whole-second interval.

## Explanation

The position difference is $(v_B-v_A)t$. Different velocities give a nonzero coefficient, so the difference vanishes only at $t=0$.
