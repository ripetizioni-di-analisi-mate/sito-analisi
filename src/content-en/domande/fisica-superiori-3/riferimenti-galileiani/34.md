In $4\,\mathrm s$ a trolley goes from $2$ to $26\,\mathrm m$ while the moving origin goes from $-3$ to $9\,\mathrm m$. What is the trolley's average velocity in the moving frame?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The relevant displacement is the trolley displacement minus the origin's displacement over the same interval. The result, twelve metres, is divided by four seconds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline v'&=\dfrac{(26-2)-[9-(-3)]}{4}\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

The initial relative coordinate is $2-(-3)=5$ and the final one is $26-9=17$ in metres. The difference of $12\,\mathrm m$ gives $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. This average uses the two pairs of simultaneous measurements.
