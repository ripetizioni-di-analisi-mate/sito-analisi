Between two photographs the moving origin advances by $9\,\mathrm m$ and a body advances by $5\,\mathrm m$ on the ground. How much does the body's relative coordinate change?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The changes are compared over the same interval. The body moves forward on the ground but loses four metres relative to the origin that advances faster.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x'&=\Delta x-\Delta X=5-9\\[4pt]
&= -4\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

This gives $-4\,\mathrm m$, a change in coordinate. Finding the new relative position itself would also require the original relative coordinate.
