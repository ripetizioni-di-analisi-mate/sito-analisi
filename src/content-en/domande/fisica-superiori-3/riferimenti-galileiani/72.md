A coin is dropped in a carriage travelling at constant horizontal velocity. Neglecting air resistance, how do the paths look from the carriage and the platform?

- Parabolic in the carriage and vertical from the platform.
- Vertical in both.
- Horizontal in both.
- Vertical in the carriage and parabolic from the platform.

## Explanation

The horizontal velocity in the carriage is zero. From the platform the coin retains the carriage's horizontal velocity and falls with constant vertical acceleration, giving a parabolic ground path.
