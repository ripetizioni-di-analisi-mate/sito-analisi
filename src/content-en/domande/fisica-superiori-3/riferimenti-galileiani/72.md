A coin is dropped from rest in a carriage travelling at nonzero constant horizontal velocity. Neglecting air resistance, how do the paths look from the carriage and the platform?

- Parabolic in the carriage and vertical from the platform.
- Vertical in both.
- Horizontal in both.
- Vertical in the carriage and parabolic from the platform.

## Explanation

The horizontal velocity in the carriage is zero. From the platform the coin retains the carriage's horizontal velocity and falls with constant vertical acceleration, giving a parabolic ground path. Both observers associate the same elapsed fall time with a given height of the coin. The ground observer adds a horizontal displacement proportional to that time, which changes the shape of the observed curve.
