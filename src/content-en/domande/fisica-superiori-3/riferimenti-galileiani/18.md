A frame translates only along $x$, but its origin has ordinate $Y=4\,\mathrm m$. A drop has $y=11\,\mathrm m$. What is its moving-frame ordinate?

- $11\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-7\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The moving origin's ordinate is constant, but it is four metres above the stationary origin. The transformation subtracts constant offsets too.

$$
\begin{aligned}
y'&=y-Y=11-4\\[4pt]
&= 7\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
