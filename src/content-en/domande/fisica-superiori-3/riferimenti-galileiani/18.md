Two events occur simultaneously in a laboratory at points $30\,\mathrm m$ apart. A train moves at a steady $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the line joining them. In the Galilean model, how far apart are the event locations in train coordinates?

- $18\,\mathrm m$
- $30\,\mathrm m$
- $42\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

For both events the train's position at the same instant is subtracted from the coordinate. That term cancels when the coordinates are subtracted, leaving the simultaneous separation at $30\,\mathrm m$.
