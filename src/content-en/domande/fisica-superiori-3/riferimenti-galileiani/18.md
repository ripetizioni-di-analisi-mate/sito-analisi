A frame with parallel axes oriented the same way as the ground axes translates along $x$ only. Its origin has vertical coordinate $Y=4\,\mathrm m$. A drop has coordinate $y=11\,\mathrm m$. What is its vertical coordinate in the moving frame?

- $11\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-7\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The moving origin's ordinate is constant, but it is four metres above the stationary origin. The transformation subtracts constant offsets too.

$$
\begin{aligned}
y'&=y-Y=11-4\\[4pt]
&= 7\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

The relative ordinate is therefore $(11-4)\,\mathrm m=7\,\mathrm m$.
