Two laboratories are at rest relative to each other, with axes pointing in the same direction and different origins. Which statement applies to the same motion measured by both?

- Same coordinates and opposite accelerations.
- Velocities differing by a nonzero constant.
- Different accelerations even on aligned axes.
- Shifted coordinates with the same velocities and accelerations.

## Explanation

Only the constant separation of the origins is added. This constant cancels from position differences over a shared interval, leaving both velocities and accelerations unchanged. The two observers may assign different coordinates to the same point. In comparing the initial and final coordinates, each subtracts two values with the same constant offset.
