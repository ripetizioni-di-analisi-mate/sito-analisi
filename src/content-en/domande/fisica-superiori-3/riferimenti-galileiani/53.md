A barge has a transverse ground-velocity component of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and an along-bank component of $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The channel is $24\,\mathrm m$ wide. How long does it take to reach the other bank?

- $8\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac{24}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac{24}{7}\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The width is perpendicular to the bank. Divide it by the transverse component alone, since that component changes how far the barge is from its departure bank.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\dfrac{24}{3}\\[4pt]
&= 8\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
