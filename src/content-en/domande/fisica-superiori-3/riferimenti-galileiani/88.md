A trolley keeps parallel axes and follows $X(t)=3t^2$ in SI units. What is the apparent-force component on a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object relative to the trolley?

- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $-12\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The quadratic coefficient in $X=X_0+u_0t+\dfrac{1}{2} At^2$ is half the acceleration. The apparent force points opposite to the trolley's positive acceleration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=2\cdot3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\\[4pt]F_{\mathrm{app},x}&=-2\cdot6\\[4pt]
&= -12\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
