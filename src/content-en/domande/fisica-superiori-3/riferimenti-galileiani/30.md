A barge advances at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the bank. On deck a belt moves forwards at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the barge. An object slides backwards on the belt at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to it. Find the ground component.

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The chain goes from object to belt, belt to barge, and barge to bank. The first component is negative. The other two components are both positive.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=v_{\mathrm{obj/c}}+v_{\mathrm{c/b}}+v_{\mathrm{b/s}}=-1+2+4\\[4pt]
&= 5\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
