A train moves at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A ball is released from rest in the carriage $5\,\mathrm m$ above the floor. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air resistance, how far does the ball advance over the ground before impact?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The vertical fall gives the same time in both frames. The horizontal ground velocity retains the value the ball had at release, namely the train's velocity.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t&=\sqrt{\dfrac{2\cdot5}{10}}\,\mathrm s=1\,\mathrm s\\[4pt]\Delta x&=6\cdot1\\[4pt]
&= 6\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

The vertical equation gives $5=\dfrac12\cdot10t^2$ in SI values, so the positive time is $1\,\mathrm s$. In that second the ball moves $6\,\mathrm m$ along the ground while falling above the same point on the carriage floor.
