Which segment of the carriage velocity graph produces a rightward horizontal apparent force, with right positive?

::grafico{f="8-2*x;5" xmin=0 xmax=3 ymin=0 ymax=9 nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" legenda="\text{falling velocity};\text{constant velocity}" titolo="Two carriage motions"}

- The constant positive-velocity line.
- Both lines.
- The line with negative slope.
- Only a segment with negative ordinate.

## Explanation

The acceleration sign comes from the slope, not the ordinate. A negative slope gives $A<0$, hence $-mA>0$. The horizontal line gives a zero term. Both velocities shown are positive, so the direction of the carriage motion alone does not distinguish the cases.
