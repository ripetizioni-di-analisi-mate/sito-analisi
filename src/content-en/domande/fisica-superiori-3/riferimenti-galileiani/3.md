A moving walkway travels at $u=2.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A person walks along it in the opposite direction with relative speed $w=0.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the ground-velocity component, taking the walkway's direction as positive.

- $3.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-1.95\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v=1.95\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The relative speed $w$ is a magnitude. Since the person walks in the negative direction, enter that motion as component $-w$. The positive result means the walkway's motion outweighs the walk in the opposite direction.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=u-w\\[4pt]&=2.6-0.65=1.95\,\mathrm{m/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
