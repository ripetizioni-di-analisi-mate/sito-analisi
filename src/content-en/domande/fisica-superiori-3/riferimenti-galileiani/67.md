An isolated puck is stationary relative to the ice. A carriage travels past at constant velocity to the right. What motion of the puck does the carriage measure?

- Uniformly accelerated motion to the left.
- Rest within the carriage frame.
- Uniform straight-line motion to the left.
- Uniform straight-line motion to the right.

## Explanation

Subtract a positive constant velocity from zero. The resulting velocity is constant and negative. Acceleration remains zero in both frames. The puck's coordinate measured from a mark in the carriage changes by the same amount in equal times.
