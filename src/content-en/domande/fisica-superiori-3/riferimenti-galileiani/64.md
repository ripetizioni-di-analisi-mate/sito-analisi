A boat points perpendicular to the current. It covers $20\,\mathrm m$ across while moving $15\,\mathrm m$ downstream. Its transverse component is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the current speed?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Both displacements take the same time. The ratio of drift to transverse displacement therefore equals the ratio of the corresponding velocity components.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{u}{4}&=\dfrac{15}{20}\\[4pt]u&=4\cdot\dfrac{15}{20}\\[4pt]
&= 3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
