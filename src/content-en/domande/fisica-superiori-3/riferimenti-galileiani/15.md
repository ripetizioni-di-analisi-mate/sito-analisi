Point $P$ is at $-5\,\mathrm m$ in the ground frame. The moving origin is at $-12\,\mathrm m$ at the same time. The axes are parallel and point the same way. What is $x'_P$?

- $-7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-17\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $17\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The distance is measured from the leftmost point to the rightmost point. The difference is positive even though both ground coordinates are negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x'_P&=x_P-X=-5-(-12)\\[4pt]
&= 7\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

The calculation gives $x'_P=(-5)-(-12)=7$ in metres.
