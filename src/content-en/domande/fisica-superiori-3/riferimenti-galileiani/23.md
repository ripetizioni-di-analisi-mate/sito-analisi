At the same instant, the moving origin is at $(4,-3)\,\mathrm m$ and a point has moving-frame coordinates $(2,4)\,\mathrm m$. The axes are parallel and point the same way. What are the ground-frame coordinates?

- $(2,-7)\,\mathrm m$.
- $(6,7)\,\mathrm m$.
- $(6,1)\,\mathrm m$.
- $(-2,7)\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

Follow the vector to the moving origin and then the relative vector. The two separate sums are $4+2=6$ along $x$ and $-3+4=1$ along $y$. The final point is therefore in the first quadrant of the ground frame.
