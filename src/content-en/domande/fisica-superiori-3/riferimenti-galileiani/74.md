A skater passes the station and has $v'=0$ in a moving inertial frame at that instant. The skater's ground acceleration is nonzero at the same instant. What is the acceleration in the moving frame?

- Zero acceleration.
- The same acceleration as on the ground.
- The acceleration opposite to the ground value.
- Twice the ground acceleration.

## Explanation

Making the velocity zero at one instant does not remove its change. The inertial origin shifts every velocity by the same constant while the acceleration stays equal.
