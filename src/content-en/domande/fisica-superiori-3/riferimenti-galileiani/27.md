A train is measured at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on the ground. A moving observer measures it at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the common positive direction. At what velocity does that observer's frame translate?

- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-11\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The ground value already includes the moving frame's velocity. Isolate the contribution that, added to seven metres per second, gives eighteen.

$$
\begin{aligned}
u&=v-v'=18-7\\[4pt]
&= 11\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

The positive sign shows that the observer's frame moves in the same direction as the train, with a smaller velocity component.
