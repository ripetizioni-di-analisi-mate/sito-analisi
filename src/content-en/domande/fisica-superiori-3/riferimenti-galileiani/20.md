A sign is at $26\,\mathrm m$ and a train door follows $X=6+5t$ in SI units. When is the sign's relative coordinate zero?

- $\dfrac{26}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac{32}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The relative coordinate vanishes when the sign passes the moving origin. The door has then travelled the difference between twenty-six metres and its six-metre initial coordinate.

$$
\begin{aligned}
0&=26-(6+5t)\\[4pt]t&=\dfrac{26-6}{5}\\[4pt]
&= 4\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

Dividing the required $20\,\mathrm m$ by $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives $4\,\mathrm s$.
