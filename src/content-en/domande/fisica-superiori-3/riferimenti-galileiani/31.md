A probe moves north at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the air. The air moves east at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the probe's speed over the ground?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The contributions are perpendicular. Ground motion points northeast and its speed is the hypotenuse rather than the sum of the speeds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v&=\sqrt{8^2+6^2}\\[4pt]
&= 10\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
