A point follows $x=8+2t$ on the ground, while the moving origin follows $X=6-3t$. What law does the moving frame see, in SI units?

- $x'=14-t$.
- $x'=2+5t$.
- $x'=2-t$.
- $x'=8+5t$.

## Explanation

Subtract the position equations to obtain the signed distance from the moving origin. The initial-position difference is two and the velocity difference is five.
