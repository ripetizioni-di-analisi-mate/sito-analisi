A carriage accelerates rightwards with magnitude $A=0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of the apparent force due to translation on a body of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $6.25\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The carriage frame includes an apparent term directed against its acceleration. For the magnitude, multiply the mass and acceleration magnitude. The units $\mathrm{kg}\cdot\mathrm{m/s^2}$ give newtons.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{\mathrm{app}}&=mA\\[4pt]&=5\cdot0.8=4\,\mathrm N.
\end{aligned}
$$

The apparent term points left because it opposes the carriage acceleration. The question asks for its magnitude, so take the positive product of mass and acceleration.
