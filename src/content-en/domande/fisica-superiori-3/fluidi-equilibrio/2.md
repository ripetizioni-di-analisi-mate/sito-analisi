In stationary water of density $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, by how much does the pressure increase on descending by $h=1.1\,\mathrm m$, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $21582\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.08\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5395.5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-10791\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Pressure increases when moving down. In water under these conditions each metre adds $9810\,\mathrm{Pa}$. For $1.1\,\mathrm m$ this gives $\Delta p=1000\cdot9.81\cdot1.1\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq1.08\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The question asks for a pressure difference between two heights, so a common pressure offset cancels.
