A sensor remains fixed to the level floor of an open water tank. A thin partition between two vertical-walled compartments with equal base areas is removed. The original levels were $0.8$ and $0.2\,\mathrm m$ above the same floor. Once the water settles, how much lower is the pressure on the initially deeper side? Use $\rho g=10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$.

- $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Because the areas are equal, volume conservation makes the final level the mean of the original heights, $0.5\,\mathrm m$. The water column above the deeper sensor loses $0.3\,\mathrm m$, equivalent to $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
