A manometer reads $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ for a level difference of $0.25\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find the liquid density.

- $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Convert the reading to pascals. Divide by gravity and level difference to find density.

$$
\rho=\frac{\Delta p}{g\Delta h},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\rho&=\frac{3000}{1 \cdot 10 \cdot \frac{1}{4}}\\[4pt]&=1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
