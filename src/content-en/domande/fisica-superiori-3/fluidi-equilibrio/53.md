A manometer reads $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ for a level difference of $0.25\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find the liquid density.

- $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

A pressure difference of $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ equals $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The measured difference is the full height gap between the two surfaces for $\Delta p=\rho g\Delta h$. Solving for $\rho$ gives $\dfrac{3000}{10\cdot0.25}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Multiplying by gravity and the height gap recovers the reading.
