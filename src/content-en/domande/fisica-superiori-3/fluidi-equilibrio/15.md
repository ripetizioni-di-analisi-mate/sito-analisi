A normal force of $120\,\mathrm N$ is uniform over $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Find the pressure.

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,.4 0,.4" vettori=".5,1,.5,.4,F;1.5,1,1.5,.4;2.5,1,2.5,.4" xmin=-.5 xmax=3.6 ymin=-.3 ymax=1.4 titolo="The force is distributed over the face and points normally to it"}

- $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $40000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-40000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Converting area squares the centimetre-to-metre factor. Thirty square centimetres are three thousandths of a square metre.

$$
p=\frac{F_\perp}{A},\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{120}{30 \cdot \frac{1}{10000}}\\[4pt]&=40000\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
