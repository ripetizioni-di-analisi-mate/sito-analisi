A support carries a total normal force of $120\,\mathrm N$ over two pads of area $10$ and $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The pads have equal mean pressures. What force acts on the smaller pad?

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="0,0 1,0 1,.4 0,.4;1.5,0 3.5,0 3.5,.4 1.5,.4" vettori=".25,1,.25,.4;.75,1,.75,.4;1.75,1,1.75,.4;2.25,1,2.25,.4;2.75,1,2.75,.4;3.25,1,3.25,.4" etichette=".41,.11,A₁;2.4,.11,A₂" xmin=-.18 xmax=3.7 ymin=-.12 ymax=1.15 titolo="The areas differ while the load per unit area is uniform."}

- $60\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $120\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At equal pressure, the larger pad takes twice the force of the smaller pad because it has twice the area. Calling the smaller force $F$, the total is $F+2F=120\,\mathrm N$. This gives $F=40\,\mathrm N$. Equal pressures correspond here to unequal forces.
