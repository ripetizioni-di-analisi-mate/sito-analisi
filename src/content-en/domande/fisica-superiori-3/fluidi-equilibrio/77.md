When can square centimetres be used in the ratio of areas without converting them?

- When both areas have the same unit
- When one area is in square metres
- When finding pressure in pascals using one unconverted area
- Always regardless of units

## Explanation

Converting both areas from square centimetres to square metres multiplies numerator and denominator by the same factor $10^{-4}$, which cancels. The ratio can therefore be calculated in square centimetres. To calculate pressure in pascals from force in newtons, though, the area must be expressed in square metres.
