At the same pressure difference, replace the manometer liquid with a less dense one. How does the level difference change?

- Increases
- Decreases
- Stays the same
- Becomes zero

## Explanation

The pressure difference is the weight per unit area of the column between the two liquid levels. A less dense liquid gives a smaller pressure difference per centimetre. The same pressure difference therefore needs more centimetres, consistent with $\Delta h$ varying inversely with $\rho$.
