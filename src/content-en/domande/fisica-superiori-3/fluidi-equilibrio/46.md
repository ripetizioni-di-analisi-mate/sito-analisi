A block floats in oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ with $25\%$ of its volume above the surface. Find the block's mean density.

- $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Use the submerged part, the complement of one quarter. Mean density is three quarters of the oil density.

$$
\rho_c=\rho_f(1-f_{\mathrm{emerged}}),\qquad
\begin{aligned}\rho_c&=3 \cdot \frac{1}{4} \cdot 800\\[4pt]&=600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
