A box of mass $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and external volume $0.012\,\mathrm{m^3}$ floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. An extra $0.002\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of the same water is put inside and the box is resealed without changing shape. What percentage of the external volume remains above the surface? Round to the nearest whole percent.

- $67\%$
- $33\%$
- $50\%$
- $17\%$

## Explanation

The water added inside adds $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ to the box. Supporting $8.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ requires $0.008\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of displaced water. This leaves $0.004\,\mathrm{m^3}$ out of $0.012\,\mathrm{m^3}$ above the surface, about $33\%$. The sealed-in water adds weight and the external volume available to displace water stays fixed.
