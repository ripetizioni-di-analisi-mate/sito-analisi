Choose the vertical axis upwards. How does pressure change with increasing height in a uniform liquid?

- It decreases with slope magnitude $\rho g$
- It increases with slope $\rho g$
- It stays constant
- It varies quadratically

## Explanation

The liquid column above gets shorter when rising. Upward-positive height gives a negative slope.
