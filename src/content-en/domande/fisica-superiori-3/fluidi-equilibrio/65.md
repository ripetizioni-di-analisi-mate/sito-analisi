Choose the vertical axis upwards. How does pressure change with increasing height in a uniform liquid?

- It decreases with slope magnitude $\rho g$
- It increases with slope $\rho g$
- It stays constant
- It varies quadratically

## Explanation

A coordinate $z$ pointing upwards increases while moving towards the surface. Pressure decreases because less liquid remains above the point. The ratio $\dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta z}=-\rho g$ is negative. If depth were the horizontal coordinate, the slope would have the same magnitude with the opposite sign.
