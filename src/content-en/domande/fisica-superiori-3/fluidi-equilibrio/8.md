A U-tube manometer contains water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The applied pressures differ by $\Delta p=2500\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Neglect the density of the gas above the liquid and find the level difference. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $0.509684\,\mathrm m$
- $0.127421\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.254842\,\mathrm m$
- $0.255\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The pressures match at the height of the lower surface when both are compared within the water. The extra column in the higher arm balances $2500\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Its height is $\Delta h=\dfrac{2500}{1000\cdot9.81}\,\mathrm m\simeq0.255\,\mathrm m$, about a quarter of a metre.
