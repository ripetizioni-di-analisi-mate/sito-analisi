A U-tube manometer contains water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The applied pressures differ by $\Delta p=2500\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Neglect the density of the gas above the liquid and find the level difference. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $0.509684\,\mathrm m$
- $0.127421\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.254842\,\mathrm m$
- $0.255\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Points at equal height in the connected liquid have equal pressure. The level difference supplies the hydrostatic pressure rise needed to balance the applied difference.

$$
\Delta h=\frac{\Delta p}{\rho g},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta h&=\frac{2500}{981 \cdot 1000 \cdot \frac{1}{100}}\\[4pt]&\simeq0.255\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
