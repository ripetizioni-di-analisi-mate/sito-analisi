An extra load is slowly placed on a boat that stays afloat. How does displaced water mass change?

- It decreases by the added mass
- It increases by the added mass
- It stays unchanged
- It grows by the added weight without conversion

## Explanation

Before loading, the displaced water mass equals that of the boat. Afterwards, buoyancy must support the boat and the load together. The boat settles lower until it displaces an additional water mass equal to the added mass. This assumes that the edge stays clear and no water enters.
