A free $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ immersed body receives $26\,\mathrm N$ of buoyancy. Neglect drag and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to find its initial upward acceleration.

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The $26\,\mathrm N$ buoyant force points upwards and the $20\,\mathrm N$ weight downwards. The initial resultant is $6\,\mathrm N$ upwards. Newton's second law divides this by $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ to give $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The resultant and the acceleration point in the same direction.
