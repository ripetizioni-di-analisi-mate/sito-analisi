A free $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ immersed body receives $26\,\mathrm N$ of buoyancy. Neglect drag and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to find its initial upward acceleration.

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Their net force is positive. The body is out of equilibrium, so divide net force by mass.

$$
a=\frac{F_A-mg}{m},\qquad
\begin{aligned}a&=\frac{26 - 2 \cdot 10}{2}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
