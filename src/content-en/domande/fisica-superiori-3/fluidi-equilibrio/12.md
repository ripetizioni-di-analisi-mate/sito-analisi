An ideal hydraulic press has pistons at the same height, of areas $A_1=0.0024\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2=0.024\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The input force on the smaller one is $F_1=90\,\mathrm N$. Neglecting piston weights and friction, find the balanced force on the second piston.

- $1800\,\mathrm N$
- $450\,\mathrm N$
- $-900\,\mathrm N$
- $900\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Added pressure is equal at both pistons at the same height. Multiplying by the larger area gives the load balanced.

$$
F_2=F_1\frac{A_2}{A_1},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_2&=90 \cdot 3 \cdot \frac{1}{125} \cdot \frac{1}{3 \cdot \frac{1}{1250}}\\[4pt]&=900\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
