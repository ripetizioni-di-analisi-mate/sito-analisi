An ideal hydraulic press filled with oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ has pistons of areas $0.0030$ and $0.030\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The larger one is $1.00\,\mathrm m$ higher. The pressure outside both pistons is the same. A downward force of $90\,\mathrm N$ acts on the smaller piston. What downward load balances the larger one? Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $900\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1140\,\mathrm{N}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N}$
- $660\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At the lower piston the excess pressure is $\dfrac{90}{0.0030}\,\mathrm{Pa}=30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Moving up a metre through the oil subtracts $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, leaving $22000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ at the second piston. The load is $22000\cdot0.030\,\mathrm N=660\,\mathrm N$. This is below the $900\,\mathrm N$ available with both pistons level.
