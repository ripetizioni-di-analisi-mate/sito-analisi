A body floats first in fresh water and then in a denser liquid. How does submerged volume change?

- It increases
- It decreases
- It always stays identical
- It exceeds total volume

## Explanation

The body floats in both liquids, so it must displace the same fluid mass to support the same weight. The denser liquid contains that mass in a smaller volume. More of the object's volume therefore lies above the surface regardless of its shape.
