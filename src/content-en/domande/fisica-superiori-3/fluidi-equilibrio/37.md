A right prism is fully submerged in a liquid, with horizontal faces of area $0.050\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Atmospheric pressure is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$. On the upper face the gauge pressure, relative to the atmosphere, is $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$, while on the lower face the absolute pressure is $110\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the buoyant force?

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="-.5,-.4 .5,-.4 .5,.4 -.5,.4" vettori="0,.8,0,.45;0,-1,0,-.47" linee="-1.5,1.15 1.5,1.15" xmin=-1.5 xmax=1.5 ymin=-1.3 ymax=1.5 titolo="The arrows show the directions of the forces on the horizontal faces."}

- $250\,\mathrm N$
- $500\,\mathrm N$
- $5250\,\mathrm N$
- $-250\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The two pressure values have different references, so both are expressed as absolute pressures. On the upper face the absolute pressure is $(100+5)\,\mathrm{kPa}=105\,\mathrm{kPa}$. On the lower face it is $110\,\mathrm{kPa}$, so the pressure difference between the faces is $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The faces have equal areas and the forces on them have opposite directions, so the buoyant force is $5000\cdot0.050\,\mathrm N=250\,\mathrm N$ upwards.
