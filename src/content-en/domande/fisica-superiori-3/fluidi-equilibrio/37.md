A right prism is fully submerged with horizontal faces of area $0.050\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The upper sensor reads $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ gauge pressure relative to an atmosphere at $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The lower sensor reads $110\,\mathrm{kPa}$ absolute. What is the buoyant force?

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="-.5,-.4 .5,-.4 .5,.4 -.5,.4" vettori="0,.8,0,.45;0,-1,0,-.47" linee="-1.5,1.15 1.5,1.15" xmin=-1.5 xmax=1.5 ymin=-1.3 ymax=1.5 titolo="The arrows show the directions of the forces on the horizontal faces."}

- $250\,\mathrm N$
- $500\,\mathrm N$
- $5250\,\mathrm N$
- $-250\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Compare readings against the same reference. At the top the absolute pressure is $(100+5)\,\mathrm{kPa}$. At the bottom it is $110\,\mathrm{kPa}$, so the difference is $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The faces have equal areas and opposing forces. Their difference gives an upward force of $5000\cdot0.050\,\mathrm N=250\,\mathrm N$.
