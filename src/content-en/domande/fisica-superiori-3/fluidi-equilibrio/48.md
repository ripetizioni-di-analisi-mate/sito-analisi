An $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ buoy has an exterior volume of $0.020\,\mathrm{m^3}$. In water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, what is the maximum mass that can be added before the buoy is fully submerged?

- $24\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

At the threshold of full immersion, the buoy displaces $1000\cdot0.020\,\mathrm{kg}=20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water. That displaced mass supports the buoy and load together. The buoy already accounts for $8\,\mathrm{kg}$, leaving $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ for the load. Below this threshold some of the buoy remains above the water.
