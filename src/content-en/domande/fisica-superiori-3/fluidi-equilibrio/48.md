An $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ buoy has an exterior volume of $0.020\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Find the maximum added mass up to full immersion in water.

- $24\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

At the threshold the buoy displaces twenty kilograms of water. Part of this supports the buoy's existing eight kilograms.

$$
m_{\mathrm{added}}=\rho_fV_{\mathrm{tot}}-m,\qquad
\begin{aligned}m_{\mathrm{added}}&=\left(-1\right) \cdot 8 + 1000 \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&=12\,\mathrm{kg}.\end{aligned}
$$
