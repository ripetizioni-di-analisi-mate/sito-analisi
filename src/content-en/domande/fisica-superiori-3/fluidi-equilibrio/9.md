A vertical rectangular wall has its upper edge $0.35\,\mathrm m$ and its lower edge $0.95\,\mathrm m$ below the water surface. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find the mean gauge pressure on the wall.

- $6500\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3500\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9500\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $13000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The wall is rectangular, so horizontal strips of equal height have equal areas. Pressure rises linearly, hence its mean occurs at depth $\dfrac{0.35+0.95}{2}\,\mathrm m=0.65\,\mathrm m$. Multiplying by $\rho g$ gives $6500\,\mathrm{Pa}$. This lies between the pressures at the two edges.
