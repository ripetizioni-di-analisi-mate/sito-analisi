A normal force $F=90\,\mathrm N$ is uniformly distributed over area $A=0.014\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find the pressure.

- $6.43\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $12857.1\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3214.29\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-6428.57\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Dividing by the area finds the normal force per square metre. The model assumes uniform pressure.

$$
p=\frac FA,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{90}{7 \cdot \frac{1}{500}}\\[4pt]&\simeq6.43\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
