A sphere of volume $1.5\,\mathrm L$ is half submerged in oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find buoyancy.

- $12\,\mathrm N$
- $3\,\mathrm N$
- $-6\,\mathrm N$
- $6\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Use only the submerged half and convert litres to cubic metres. The displaced volume holds six hundred grams of oil.

$$
F_A=\rho_f g\frac{V_{\mathrm{tot}}}{2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_A&=800 \cdot 10 \cdot 15 \cdot \frac{1}{10000} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\\[4pt]&=6\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
