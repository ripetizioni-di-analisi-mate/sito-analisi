Why does the equal-pressure rule need correction for pistons at different heights?

- The liquid volume doubles
- There is a hydrostatic pressure difference
- Density must be negative
- The smaller piston must always be higher

## Explanation

Pressure varies with height in a stationary liquid. The principle transmits a change without removing the existing gradient.
