In stationary water of density $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, find the pressure increase on descending by $h=1.3\,\mathrm m$. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $25506\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.28\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6376.5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-12753\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Only the added column contributes, with its weight per area. Initial pressure cancels in the difference.

$$
\Delta p=\rho gh,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta p&=1000 \cdot 13 \cdot \frac{1}{10} \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&\simeq1.28\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
