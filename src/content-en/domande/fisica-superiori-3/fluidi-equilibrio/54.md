Two taps are connected by a straight, water-filled pipe $2\,\mathrm m$ long. The water is stationary and the pressure rises by $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from the first to the second tap. Taking $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the sine of the pipe’s angle to the horizontal, taking a downward slope as positive?

- $-0.25$
- $+0.25$
- $+0.50$
- $+1$

## Explanation

The pressure in this water increases by $10\,\mathrm{kPa}$ for every metre of descent. A $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ rise corresponds to half a metre downward. The sine is the vertical drop divided by the pipe length, giving $+\dfrac{0.5}{2}=+0.25$.
