The applied pressures are $105\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at left and $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at right. With liquid density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much higher is the right level?

- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.25\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Greater pressure depresses the left arm. Convert the pressure difference to pascals before dividing.

$$
\Delta h=\frac{p_L-p_R}{\rho g},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta h&=\frac{105000 - 100000}{10 \cdot 1000}\\[4pt]&=0.5\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
