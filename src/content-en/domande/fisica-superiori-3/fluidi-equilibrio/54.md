The applied pressures are $105\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at left and $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at right. With liquid density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much higher is the right level?

- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.25\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The left branch has the higher applied pressure, so its liquid level is lower. The extra column on the right balances $(105-100)\,\mathrm{kPa}=5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Dividing by $1000\cdot10\,\mathrm{Pa/m}$ gives $0.5\,\mathrm m$, the full difference between the two surface heights.
