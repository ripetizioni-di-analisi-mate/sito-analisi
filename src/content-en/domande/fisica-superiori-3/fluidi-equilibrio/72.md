If both arms have the same imposed pressure, where are the surfaces of a single liquid at rest in a manometer?

- Always higher in the narrower arm
- Always higher in the wider arm
- It depends on pressure when expressed in pascals
- At the same height

## Explanation

Neglecting capillary effects, level difference is related to imposed pressure difference. Zero difference gives equal levels.
