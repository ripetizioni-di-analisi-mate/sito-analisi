Two manometer liquids measure the same pressure difference. Their densities are $1000$ and $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the second-to-first level difference ratio.

- $1.6$
- $0.4$
- $-0.8$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

Equal gravity and pressure difference give equal density times level difference. The denser liquid requires a smaller level difference.

$$
h_2/h_1=\rho_1/\rho_2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}h_2/h_1&=\frac{1000}{1250}\\[4pt]&=0.8.\end{aligned}
$$
