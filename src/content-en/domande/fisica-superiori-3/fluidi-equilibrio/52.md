A manometer must measure pressure differences up to $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The maximum readable height difference between surfaces is $0.45\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible gas density, which proposed density keeps the liquid levels within the readable range?

- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

At full scale, $\rho\geq\dfrac{\Delta p_{\rm max}}{g\Delta h_{\rm max}}$ is required. The gauge limits give a threshold of about $1333\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Of the choices, only $1500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is sufficient: it gives a $0.40\,\mathrm m$ height difference at the maximum pressure. A less dense liquid gives greater displacement for the same pressure.
