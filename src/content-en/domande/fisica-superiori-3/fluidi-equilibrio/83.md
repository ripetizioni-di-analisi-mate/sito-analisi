Why does the upper horizontal face of a submerged prism receive downward pressure force?

- Gravity cancels the normals
- Upper-face pressure is negative
- The fluid pushes normally into the prism
- Prism displacement is upward

## Explanation

The water touching the top face is above the solid and presses against it. The normal from the water into the prism points vertically downwards. On the bottom face the normal into the prism points the opposite way, so its pressure force acts upwards.
