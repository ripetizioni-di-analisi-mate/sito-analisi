Why does the upper horizontal face of a submerged prism receive downward pressure force?

- Gravity cancels the normals
- Upper-face pressure is negative
- The fluid pushes normally into the prism
- Prism displacement is upward

## Explanation

Contacting fluid pushes along the inward normal. On the upper face that normal points downwards.
