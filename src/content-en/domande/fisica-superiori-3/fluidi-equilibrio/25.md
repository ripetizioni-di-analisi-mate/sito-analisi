The graph gives absolute pressure in the liquid of a closed tank at different depths $h$ below the surface. The local atmosphere outside is at $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Use the graph to find the gauge pressure at $h=3\,\mathrm m$.

::grafico{f="100+10*x" punti="0,100,pieno;3,130,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=90 ymax=140 nomi="h (m),p (kPa)" titolo="Absolute pressure measured in the liquid."}

- $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $130\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $210\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

At $h=3\,\mathrm m$ the plotted value is $130\,\mathrm{kPa}$ absolute. The gauge zero is the local atmosphere at $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Its reading is therefore $(130-80)\,\mathrm{kPa}=50\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Subtracting the graph's intercept would instead give only the hydrostatic increase from the liquid surface.
