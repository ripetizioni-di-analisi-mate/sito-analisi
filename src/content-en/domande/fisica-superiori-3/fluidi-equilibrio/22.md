In a homogeneous liquid pressure increases by $24\,\mathrm{kPa}$ per $3\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find its density.

- $1600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Divide the observed increase by gravity and depth. The resulting density sets the slope of the hydrostatic graph.

$$
\rho=\frac{\Delta p}{gh},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\rho&=\frac{24000}{3 \cdot 10}\\[4pt]&=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
