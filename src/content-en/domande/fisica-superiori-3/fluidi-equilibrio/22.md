In a homogeneous liquid the pressure increase over $3\,\mathrm m$ is $24\,\mathrm{kPa}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find its density.

- $1600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The pressure gain per metre is $\dfrac{24000}{3}\,\mathrm{Pa/m}=8000\,\mathrm{Pa/m}$. In the homogeneous liquid this slope equals $\rho g$, giving $\rho=\dfrac{8000}{10}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. A slope in pascals per metre must be divided by gravitational acceleration to obtain density.
