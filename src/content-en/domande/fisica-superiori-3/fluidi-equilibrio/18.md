A bearing must support $450\,\mathrm N$ without exceeding $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the minimum area for uniform loading?

- $0.006\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.003\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0015\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $-0.003\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The pressure limit is reached at the minimum allowed area. Convert $150\,\mathrm{kPa}=150000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and find $A_{\rm min}=\dfrac{450}{150000}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.003\,\mathrm{m^2}$. With a larger area, the same $450\,\mathrm N$ is spread over more surface and the resulting pressure stays below the limit.
