A bearing must support $450\,\mathrm N$ without exceeding $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the minimum area for uniform loading.

- $0.006\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.003\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0015\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $-0.003\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

At fixed force, pressure decreases as area increases. The limiting pressure is attained at the stated ratio.

$$
A=\frac{F_\perp}{p_{\max}},\qquad
\begin{aligned}A&=\frac{450}{150000}\\[4pt]&=0.003\,\mathrm{m^2}.\end{aligned}
$$
