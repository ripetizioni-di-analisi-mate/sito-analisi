Piston radii at equal height and under the same outside pressure have a ratio of three to one. Find the force multiplication of the ideal press.

- $9$
- $18$
- $4.5$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The pistons are circular, so their areas scale with the square of the radius. Tripling the radius gives an area ratio of $3^2=9$. With equal excess pressure on both pistons, the force ratio is also nine. A gain of three would incorrectly use the radius ratio as the area ratio.
