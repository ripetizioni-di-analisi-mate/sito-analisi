Piston radii at equal height have a ratio of three to one. Find the force multiplication of the ideal press.

- $9$
- $18$
- $4.5$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The force ratio equals the area ratio. Circular areas depend on squared radius.

$$
F_2/F_1=(r_2/r_1)^2,\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_2/F_1&=3^{2}\\[4pt]&=9.\end{aligned}
$$
