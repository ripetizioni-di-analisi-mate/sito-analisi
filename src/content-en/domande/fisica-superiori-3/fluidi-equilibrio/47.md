A platform floats in water and receives an extra $40\,\mathrm{kg}$. Its horizontal area is constant at $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$. How far does it sink, keeping its upper edge above water?

- $0.04\,\mathrm m$
- $0.01\,\mathrm m$
- $0.02\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.02\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The added mass requires that much extra displaced water mass. Dividing by density and area gives the newly immersed thickness.

$$
\Delta h=\frac{\Delta m}{\rho_f A},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta h&=\frac{40}{2 \cdot 1000}\\[4pt]&=0.02\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
