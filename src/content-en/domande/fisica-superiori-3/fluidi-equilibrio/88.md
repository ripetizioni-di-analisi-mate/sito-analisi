For a floating object, what is needed beyond vertical force balance to study stability under a small tilt?

- Ambient temperature alone
- The mass of all liquid in the vessel
- The atmospheric absolute pressure in pascals
- Torque balance and the position of the buoyancy resultant

## Explanation

Equal vertical forces can have different lines of action. Their separation determines whether the torque restores or increases the tilt.
