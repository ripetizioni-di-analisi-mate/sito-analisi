For a floating object, what is needed beyond vertical force balance to study stability under a small tilt?

- Ambient temperature alone
- The mass of all liquid in the vessel
- The atmospheric absolute pressure in pascals
- Torque balance and the position of the buoyancy resultant

## Explanation

After a tilt, the submerged shape changes and the centre of buoyancy moves. Even when weight and buoyancy have equal magnitudes, their lines of action can differ. The direction of the resulting torque shows whether the body tends to right itself or to tilt further.
