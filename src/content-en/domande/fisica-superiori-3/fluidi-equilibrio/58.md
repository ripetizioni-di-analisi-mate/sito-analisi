A force of fixed magnitude acts normally on a surface. Its area increases. How does the mean pressure change?

- Increases
- Decreases
- Stays the same
- Becomes negative

## Explanation

Mean pressure is the normal force divided by area. If the force stays fixed and the area doubles, each square metre carries half the force on average. The ratio decreases as the denominator grows, even if local pressures along the surface differ.
