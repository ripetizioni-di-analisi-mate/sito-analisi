A force of fixed magnitude acts normally on a surface. Its area increases. How does the mean pressure change?

- Increases
- Decreases
- Stays the same
- Becomes negative

## Explanation

Area is the denominator of force divided by area. Spreading the same force over more surface gives lower pressure.
