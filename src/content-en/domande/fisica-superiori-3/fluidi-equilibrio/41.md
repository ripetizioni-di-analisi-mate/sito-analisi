A body weighs $32\,\mathrm N$ in air, neglecting air buoyancy, and its scale reads $20\,\mathrm N$ in water. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find submerged volume.

- $0.0012\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.0024\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $6\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $-0.0012\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The difference of the readings is the increase in buoyancy. Divide it by water weight per unit volume to find volume.

$$
V=\frac{P-T}{\rho_f g},\qquad
\begin{aligned}V&=\frac{32 - 20}{10 \cdot 1000}\\[4pt]&=0.0012\,\mathrm{m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
