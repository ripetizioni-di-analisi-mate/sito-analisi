An ideal hydraulic press has level pistons with equal outside pressure. It supports $1800\,\mathrm N$ on its larger piston of area $90\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. How much force is needed on the small piston of $15\,\mathrm{cm^2}$?

- $600\,\mathrm N$
- $150\,\mathrm N$
- $300\,\mathrm N$
- $-300\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

For the same excess pressure, force is proportional to piston area. Both areas are given in $\mathrm{cm^2}$, so their ratio is $\dfrac{15}{90}=\dfrac16$. The smaller piston therefore requires $1800/6\,\mathrm N=300\,\mathrm N$. Dividing each force by its respective area verifies that the ratios agree.
