Which quantity is transmitted equally throughout a confined liquid at fixed heights and density?

- The applied pressure change
- The absolute force on each piston
- The travel of every piston
- Normal force independent of area

## Explanation

The pressure change adds to the hydrostatic distribution. The resulting force also depends on the area where it acts.
