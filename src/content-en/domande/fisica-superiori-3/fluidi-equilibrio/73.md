Which quantity is transmitted equally throughout a confined liquid at fixed heights and density?

- The applied pressure change
- The absolute force on each piston
- The travel of every piston
- Normal force independent of area

## Explanation

At fixed heights and density, the hydrostatic pressure difference between two points stays constant. If a piston increases pressure at one point, the other must gain the same amount for that difference to stay fixed. Final pressures can still differ by their original hydrostatic pressure difference.
