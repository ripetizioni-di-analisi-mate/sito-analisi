Which volume is needed for buoyancy on a partly submerged object?

- The volume above water
- The total volume regardless of immersion
- The submerged volume that excludes fluid
- The vessel volume

## Explanation

The relevant weight is that of displaced fluid. Fluid would occupy only the submerged part of the body.
