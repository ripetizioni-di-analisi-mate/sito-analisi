Which volume is needed for buoyancy on a partly submerged object?

- The volume above water
- The total volume regardless of immersion
- The submerged volume that excludes fluid
- The vessel volume

## Explanation

Buoyancy equals the weight of the fluid that would fill the space excluded by the object below the surface. Use the actual submerged volume. A cavity open to the liquid is left out of that volume, while a sealed cavity contributes to the volume the liquid cannot enter.
