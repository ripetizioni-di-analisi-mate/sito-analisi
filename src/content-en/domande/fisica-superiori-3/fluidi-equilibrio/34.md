A piston receives $30\,\mathrm N$ and drops $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. In an ideal press with negligible change in the liquid's potential energy, how much work is passed to the second piston over this interval?

- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $6\,\mathrm J$
- $3\,\mathrm J$
- $-6\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The input travel is $20\,\mathrm{cm}=0.20\,\mathrm m$. Force and displacement are parallel, so the input work is $30\cdot0.20\,\mathrm J=6\,\mathrm J$. In the ideal model, with a negligible change in the liquid's potential energy, the output work equals the input work. The area ratio changes force and travel.
