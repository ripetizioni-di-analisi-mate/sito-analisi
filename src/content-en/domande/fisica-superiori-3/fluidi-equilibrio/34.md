A piston receives $30\,\mathrm N$ and drops $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the maximum work passed to the second piston over this interval?

- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $6\,\mathrm J$
- $3\,\mathrm J$
- $-6\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Convert travel to metres. An ideal press outputs the input work, while losses would reduce it.

$$
W=F_1s_1,\qquad
\begin{aligned}W&=30 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{5}\\[4pt]&=6\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
