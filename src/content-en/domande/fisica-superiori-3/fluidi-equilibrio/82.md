A fully submerged body has upward buoyancy greater than weight. What can hold it still without vertical contact with the vessel?

- A taut line attached only above
- A taut line anchored below it
- No additional force
- A further increase in buoyancy

## Explanation

Balance requires a downward contribution. A line pulls along itself, and an anchor below gives the required direction.
