A fully submerged body has upward buoyancy greater than weight. What can hold it still without vertical contact with the vessel?

- A taut line attached only above
- A taut line anchored below it
- No additional force
- A further increase in buoyancy

## Explanation

When buoyancy exceeds weight, an extra downward force is needed for equilibrium. A line anchored below the body pulls towards its anchor and can supply that force. A line hanging from above pulls in the same direction as buoyancy and would instead tend to go slack.
