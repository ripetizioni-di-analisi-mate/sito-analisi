A block of density $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in water. What percentage of its volume is submerged?

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="-1,-.6 1,-.6 1,.4 -1,.4" linee="-2.5,0 2.5,0" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-1.35 ymax=1.15 titolo="The submerged part is sixty percent of the block of uniform cross section"}

- $60\,\%$
- $120\,\%$
- $30\,\%$
- $-60\,\%$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the displaced fluid mass is the block's mass. The density ratio equals the submerged-to-exterior volume ratio.

$$
f_{\%}=100\frac{\rho_c}{\rho_f},\qquad
\begin{aligned}f_{\%}&=100 \cdot 600 \cdot \frac{1}{1000}\\[4pt]&=60\,\%.\end{aligned}
$$
