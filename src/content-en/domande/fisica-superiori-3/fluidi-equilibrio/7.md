A uniform block of density $\rho_c=460\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the percentage of its volume that is submerged.

- $92\,\%$
- $23\,\%$
- $46\,\%$
- $-46\,\%$

## Explanation

The body's weight equals the buoyancy from the submerged part. Dividing the balance by total volume gives the density ratio.

$$
f_{\%}=100\frac{\rho_c}{\rho_f},\qquad
\begin{aligned}f_{\%}&=100 \cdot 460 \cdot \frac{1}{1000}\\[4pt]&=46\,\%.\end{aligned}
$$
