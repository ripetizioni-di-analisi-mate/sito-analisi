A uniform block of density $\rho_c=460\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What percentage of its volume is submerged?

- $92\,\%$
- $23\,\%$
- $46\,\%$
- $-46\,\%$

## Explanation

The block's weight is supported by the weight of the water it displaces. Cancelling $g$ gives $\dfrac{V_{\rm imm}}{V_{\rm tot}}=\dfrac{460}{1000}=0.46$. The question asks for a percentage, so $46\%$ of the block is submerged. The volume left above the surface is the remaining fraction.
