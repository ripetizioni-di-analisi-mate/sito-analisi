Two open vessels contain the same water at the same depth, but have different shapes. How do bottom pressures compare?

- Higher in the wider vessel
- Lower in the wider vessel
- Determined solely by total volume
- Equal under the same atmosphere

## Explanation

With the same atmosphere, both surfaces start at the same pressure. Descending through the same depth of water adds the same $\rho gh$ regardless of vessel shape. A base of different area can still experience a different total force because force also depends on area.
