Which way does pressure force act on a small face in a stationary fluid?

- Tangent to the face
- Always vertically upwards
- Always horizontal
- Perpendicular to the face

## Explanation

A fluid at rest presses on the face in the direction perpendicular to it. Tilting the face also tilts the direction of the force. The vertical matters for how the pressure varies with height, but the local force direction follows the normal to the face.
