Which way does pressure force act on a small face in a stationary fluid?

- Tangent to the face
- Always vertically upwards
- Always horizontal
- Perpendicular to the face

## Explanation

In a stationary fluid pressure exerts a force normal to each surface. Its direction depends on the face.
