Which assumption is needed to infer manometer level difference from a single liquid density and gas pressures?

- The liquid is in turbulent motion
- Gravity differs between the two arms
- The gas-column weights are negligible or already accounted for
- Both gases must always be atmospheric

## Explanation

The simple formula assigns the pressure change to the stated liquid column. Include other columns if they contribute.
