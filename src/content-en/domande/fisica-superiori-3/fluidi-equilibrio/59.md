Which pressure enters the net force on a flat wall under different uniform pressures on its two sides?

- The sum of the pressures
- Atmospheric pressure alone
- The difference of the two pressures
- The product of the pressures

## Explanation

The same area is pressed from both sides. The forces follow the same normal in opposite directions. Multiply the area by the pressure difference, assigning its sign with the chosen positive direction. When the pressures are identical, the two forces cancel exactly.
