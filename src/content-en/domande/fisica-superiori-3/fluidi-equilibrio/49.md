A free $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ body floats in salt water of density $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What volume does it displace?

- $0.01\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.005\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $-0.01\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The displaced volume contains fluid of the same mass as the body. Gravity cancels when their weights are equated.

$$
V=\frac m{\rho_f},\qquad
\begin{aligned}V&=\frac{12}{1200}\\[4pt]&=0.01\,\mathrm{m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
