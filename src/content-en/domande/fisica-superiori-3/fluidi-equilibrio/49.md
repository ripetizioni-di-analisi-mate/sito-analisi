A free $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ body floats in salt water of density $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What volume does it displace?

- $0.01\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.005\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $-0.01\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The body floats freely, so the mass of displaced liquid equals the body's mass. Dividing $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ by the density of salt water gives $0.010\,\mathrm{m^3}$. This is the submerged volume. A less dense liquid would require a greater displaced volume for the same mass.
