The pistons of an ideal hydraulic press are at the same height. External pressure on the first is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$, and an applied downward force of $80\,\mathrm N$ acts over $0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The second has area $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the gas above it exerts $110\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What downward load is required on the second piston?

- $400\,\mathrm N$
- $200\,\mathrm N$
- $600\,\mathrm N$
- $2400\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Beneath the first piston there is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ plus the $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from the applied force. The second piston at the same height also has $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$ below it. The external gas at $110\,\mathrm{kPa}$ leaves a difference of $10\,\mathrm{kPa}$, which over $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ balances a $200\,\mathrm N$ load.
