What can the pressure graph look like at the boundary of two stationary immiscible liquids with negligible surface tension?

- With a mandatory pressure jump
- Horizontal in both layers
- Continuous with a change of slope
- Discontinuous even at equal densities

## Explanation

Immediately on either side of the interface, pressure balances if surface tension is negligible. The graph therefore passes through the same value. Once inside the second layer, the rate of increase per metre uses its density. Different densities thus produce a change of slope.
