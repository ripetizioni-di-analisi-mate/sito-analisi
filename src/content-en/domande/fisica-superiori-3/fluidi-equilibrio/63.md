What can the pressure graph look like at the boundary of two stationary immiscible liquids with negligible surface tension?

- With a mandatory pressure jump
- Horizontal in both layers
- Continuous with a change of slope
- Discontinuous even at equal densities

## Explanation

The two sides of the interface balance at equal pressure. The slope changes with density in the other liquid.
