Two points in the same connected fluid at rest are at the same height. What follows?

- Their pressures agree
- They differ if the vessel cross sections differ
- The nearer wall has higher pressure
- Both have zero pressure

## Explanation

The hydrostatic law relates pressure difference to height difference. Equal heights give zero difference.
