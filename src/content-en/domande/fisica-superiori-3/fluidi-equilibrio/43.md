A body weighs $50\,\mathrm N$ and gets buoyancy of $20\,\mathrm N$ in water. What is the tension when it is held with the same submerged volume in a liquid of half the density?

- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $-40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At fixed submerged volume, halving the density halves buoyancy from $20$ to $10\,\mathrm N$. The weight remains $50\,\mathrm N$. The support must therefore balance $50-10=40\,\mathrm N$. Tension rises from $30\,\mathrm N$ in water because the new liquid supports a smaller portion of the weight.
