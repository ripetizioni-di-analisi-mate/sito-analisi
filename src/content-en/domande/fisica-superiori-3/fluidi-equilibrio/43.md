A body weighs $50\,\mathrm N$ and gets buoyancy of $20\,\mathrm N$ in water. Find the tension when held at the same submerged volume in a liquid of half the density.

- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $-40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Buoyancy halves at fixed volume and gravity. Weight stays fixed and tension grows.

$$
T=P-\frac{F_{A,\mathrm{water}}}{2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}T&=50 - \frac{20}{2}\\[4pt]&=40\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
