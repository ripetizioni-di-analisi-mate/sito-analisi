A body displaces a volume $V=0.0016\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of stationary water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the buoyant force. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $15.7\,\mathrm N$
- $31.392\,\mathrm N$
- $7.848\,\mathrm N$
- $-15.696\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Buoyancy equals the displaced water's weight. Volume times density is the corresponding water mass.

$$
F_A=\rho gV,\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_A&=1 \cdot \frac{1}{625} \cdot 1000 \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&\simeq15.7\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
