A body displaces a volume $V=0.0016\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of stationary water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the buoyant force. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $15.7\,\mathrm N$
- $31.392\,\mathrm N$
- $7.848\,\mathrm N$
- $-15.696\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The mass of displaced water is $1000\cdot0.0016\,\mathrm{kg}=1.6\,\mathrm{kg}$. Buoyancy equals that mass's weight, so $F_A=1.6\cdot9.81\,\mathrm N\simeq15.7\,\mathrm N$. It acts upwards. The calculation uses the volume excluded by the body, which the question already provides.
