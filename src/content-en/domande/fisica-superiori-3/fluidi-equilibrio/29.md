A gas has gauge pressure $-5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and outside air pressure $98\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the absolute pressure?

- $93\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $186\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $46.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-93\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The value $-5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is the offset from atmospheric pressure. To reference the result to vacuum, add the current reference value of $98\,\mathrm{kPa}$. This gives $p_{\rm ass}=(98-5)\,\mathrm{kPa}=93\,\mathrm{kPa}$, five kilopascals below the atmosphere where the measurement was made.
