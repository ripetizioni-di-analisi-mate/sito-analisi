A gas has gauge pressure $-5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and outside air pressure $98\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find absolute pressure.

- $93\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $186\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $46.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-93\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Add the signed difference to the atmospheric reading. Absolute pressure here is positive as expected.

$$
p_{\mathrm{abs}}=p_{\mathrm{atm}}+p_g,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p_{\mathrm{abs}}&=98 - 5\\[4pt]&=93\,\mathrm{kPa}.\end{aligned}
$$
