In a U-tube with gases above one homogeneous liquid, which pressure corresponds to the lower surface?

- To the lower gas pressure
- Always to atmospheric pressure
- Always to zero pressure
- To the higher gas pressure

## Explanation

Moving down in the liquid increases pressure. At the lower level it reaches the pressure imposed by the adjacent gas.
