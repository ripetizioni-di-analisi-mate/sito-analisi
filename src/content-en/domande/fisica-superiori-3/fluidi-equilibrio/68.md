In a U-tube with gases above one homogeneous liquid, which pressure corresponds to the lower surface?

- To the lower gas pressure
- Always to atmospheric pressure
- Always to zero pressure
- To the higher gas pressure

## Explanation

Start at the higher surface and descend to the height of the other one, adding the pressure of the intervening column. This reaches the pressure in the liquid at the lower surface. To hold that surface in place, the gas above it must therefore exert the higher pressure.
