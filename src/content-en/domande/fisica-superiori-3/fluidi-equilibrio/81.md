What does a force scale attached above a stationary submerged object measure?

- Line tension
- Buoyancy alone
- Pressure at the vessel bottom
- Fluid mass in every case

## Explanation

The scale measures pull in the line. In equilibrium it supplies the weight not already balanced by buoyancy.
