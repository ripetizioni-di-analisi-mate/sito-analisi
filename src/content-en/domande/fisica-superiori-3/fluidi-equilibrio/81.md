What does a force scale attached above a stationary submerged object measure?

- Line tension
- Buoyancy alone
- Pressure at the vessel bottom
- Fluid mass in every case

## Explanation

The spring scale is in line with the string and reads its tension. If the object is stationary, tension plus upward buoyancy balances the weight. The reading is the apparent weight. Buoyancy itself follows by subtracting the reading in water from the one in air if air buoyancy is negligible.
