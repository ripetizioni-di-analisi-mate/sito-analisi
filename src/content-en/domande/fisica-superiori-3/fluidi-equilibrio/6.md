An object of mass $m=4.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and volume $V=0.0013\,\mathrm{m^3}$ is fully submerged in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and hangs at rest from a line. Find the tension. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $62.784\,\mathrm N$
- $31.4\,\mathrm N$
- $15.696\,\mathrm N$
- $-31.392\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Buoyancy and tension act upwards. Their sum balances weight, so subtract buoyancy from weight.

$$
T=mg-\rho gV,\qquad
\begin{aligned}T&=\left(- \frac{13 \cdot 1000}{10000} + \frac{9}{2}\right) \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&\simeq31.4\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
