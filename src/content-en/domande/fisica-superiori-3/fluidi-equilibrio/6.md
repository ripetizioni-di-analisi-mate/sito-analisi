An object of mass $m=4.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and volume $V=0.0013\,\mathrm{m^3}$ is fully submerged in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and hangs at rest from a line. Find the tension. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $62.784\,\mathrm N$
- $31.4\,\mathrm N$
- $15.696\,\mathrm N$
- $-31.392\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The displaced water has mass $1.3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Taking upwards as positive, the force balance is $T+F_A-mg=0$, so $T=9.81(4.5-1.3)\,\mathrm N\simeq31.4\,\mathrm N$. The reading is positive and below the weight in air, as expected when the line pulls upwards.
