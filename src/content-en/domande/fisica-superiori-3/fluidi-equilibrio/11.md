A chamber has a gauge pressure of $-18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ relative to the atmosphere pressing on the outside of its flat lid. The lid has area $0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Taking the direction from inside to outside as positive, find the resultant of the pressure forces in newtons.

- $4500\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-72\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-4500\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-72000\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Outwards is positive, so the difference to multiply by area is $p_{\rm int}-p_{\rm est}=-18000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. This gives $F=(-18000)\cdot0.25\,\mathrm N=-4500\,\mathrm N$. The sign gives a force pointing into the chamber. The atmospheric contribution cancels when the two pressures are subtracted.
