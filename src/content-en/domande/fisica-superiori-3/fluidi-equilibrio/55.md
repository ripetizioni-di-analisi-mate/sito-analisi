Which unit is equivalent to a pascal?

- Newton multiplied by square metre
- Joule per second
- Newton per square metre
- Kilogram per cubic metre

## Explanation

Pressure has the dimensions of force divided by area, so $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$. The relation also works in reverse. A uniform pressure of one pascal over two square metres produces a normal force of two newtons. A newton multiplied by a square metre would have different dimensions.
