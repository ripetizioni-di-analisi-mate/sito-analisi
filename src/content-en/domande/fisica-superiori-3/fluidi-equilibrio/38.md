The difference between two spring scale readings gives a buoyant force of $12\,\mathrm N$ on submerged volume $0.0015\,\mathrm{m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find the liquid density.

- $1600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The buoyancy inferred from the readings is the weight of displaced liquid. Dividing by $gV_{\rm imm}$ gives $\rho=\dfrac{12}{10\cdot0.0015}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The displaced mass is $\dfrac{12}{10}\,\mathrm{kg}=1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$, occupying one and a half litres.
