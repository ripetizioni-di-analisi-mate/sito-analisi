A force sensor measures buoyancy of $12\,\mathrm N$ on submerged volume $0.0015\,\mathrm{m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find the liquid density.

- $1600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $-800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Isolate density by dividing by gravity and displaced volume. The body's mass is not needed for this measurement.

$$
\rho_f=\frac{F_A}{gV},\qquad
\begin{aligned}\rho_f&=\frac{12}{10 \cdot 15 \cdot \frac{1}{10000}}\\[4pt]&=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.\end{aligned}
$$
