A stationary liquid of density $\rho=860\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ has surface pressure $p_0=101300\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Find absolute pressure at depth $h=2.5\,\mathrm m$. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $2.44783\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $61195.8\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.22\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-1.22392\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Add the column weight per area to absolute surface pressure. The added contribution is positive when going down.

$$
p=p_0+\rho gh,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=860 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100} + 101300\\[4pt]&\simeq1.22\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
