A stationary liquid of density $\rho=860\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ has surface pressure $p_0=101300\,\mathrm{Pa}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the absolute pressure at depth $h=2.5\,\mathrm m$?

- $2.11\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.26\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.22\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8.02\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Add the pressure from the column's weight to the pressure already present at the surface. The product $860\cdot9.81\cdot2.5$ gives about $21092\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Therefore $p=101300\,\mathrm{Pa}+21092\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq1.22\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The result exceeds the surface pressure even though the liquid is less dense than water.
