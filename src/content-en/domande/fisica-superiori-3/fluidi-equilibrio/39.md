The same body is fully submerged in fluids of densities $800$ and $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the second-to-first buoyancy ratio.

- $3$
- $0.75$
- $1.5$
- $-1.5$

## Explanation

The object is fully submerged in both cases, so it displaces the same volume. Volume and $g$ cancel in the ratio, leaving $\dfrac{1200}{800}=1.5$. The second fluid gives more buoyancy because the displaced volume contains fifty per cent more mass.
