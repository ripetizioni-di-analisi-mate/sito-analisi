The same body is fully submerged in fluids of densities $800$ and $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the second-to-first buoyancy ratio.

- $3$
- $0.75$
- $1.5$
- $-1.5$

## Explanation

Volume and gravity are shared and cancel. The ratio becomes the density ratio.

$$
F_{A2}/F_{A1}=\rho_2/\rho_1,\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_{A2}/F_{A1}&=\frac{1200}{800}\\[4pt]&=1.5.\end{aligned}
$$
