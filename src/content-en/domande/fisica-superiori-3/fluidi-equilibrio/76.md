An ideal press multiplies force by four with negligible change in the liquid's potential energy. How do the input and output work magnitudes compare?

- Output is four times input
- Output is a quarter of input
- Output work is zero
- They are equal

## Explanation

In a level ideal press, a fourfold increase in force cuts travel to one quarter by volume conservation. In the product of force and travel, four cancels its reciprocal. With negligible change in the liquid's potential energy, the input and output work have equal magnitudes.
