Which piston moves through the shorter distance in an ideal incompressible-fluid press?

- The one with smaller area
- The travels are generally equal
- The one with greater area
- The one subject to atmospheric pressure

## Explanation

Area times travel must give the same volume for each piston. A larger area gives a shorter travel.
