Which piston moves through the shorter distance in an ideal incompressible-fluid press?

- The one with smaller area
- The travels are generally equal
- The one with greater area
- The one subject to atmospheric pressure

## Explanation

The volume leaving one cylinder must enter the other. Transferred volume is area times travel, so the larger piston needs less travel. For instance, tripling the area cuts the travel to one third. The pistons move in opposite directions when both close the fluid chambers from above.
