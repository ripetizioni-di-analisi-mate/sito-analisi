A hollow object floats despite having walls made of material denser than water. Which density enters its floating ratio?

- Density of the wall material alone
- Internal air density alone
- Total mass divided by the outside volume that excludes water
- Water density added to wall density

## Explanation

Weight depends on total mass, while buoyancy depends on the volume of water excluded by the object. A sealed cavity increases that volume without adding the mass of solid material. To assess flotation, divide total mass by the exterior volume that actually excludes water.
