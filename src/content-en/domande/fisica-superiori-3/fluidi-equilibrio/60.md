A face is subjected to different local pressures. What does net normal force divided by area give?

- Maximum pressure in every case
- Minimum pressure in every case
- Fluid density
- Mean pressure over the area

## Explanation

Dividing the net normal force by area gives the uniform pressure that would produce the same force. On a vertical face in a homogeneous liquid, this mean is the pressure at the height of the geometric centroid. The pressure distribution is still needed to locate the resultant force.
