A face is subjected to different local pressures. What does net normal force divided by area give?

- Maximum pressure in every case
- Minimum pressure in every case
- Fluid density
- Mean pressure over the area

## Explanation

Net force sums the local contributions. Dividing by total area gives the area-weighted mean.
