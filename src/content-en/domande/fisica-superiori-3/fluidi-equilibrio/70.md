An absolute pressure sensor is replaced with a gauge sensor while local air pressure stays stable. How do measured pressure changes compare?

- The gauge reads zero change
- They are equal
- The gauge always reads the opposite change
- They depend on tank volume

## Explanation

At each measurement, gauge pressure is the absolute pressure minus atmospheric pressure. Subtracting two readings cancels the atmospheric value as long as it stays fixed. The instruments therefore register the same change. A change in outside pressure would also affect the gauge reading.
