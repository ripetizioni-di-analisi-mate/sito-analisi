The pressure difference between the two sides of a vertical rectangular gate varies linearly from $+4\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the upper edge to $-4\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the lower edge. What can be said about the resultant pressure force and the torque about the gate's centre?

- Zero resultant and zero torque
- Nonzero resultant and nonzero torque
- Nonzero resultant and zero torque
- Zero resultant and nonzero torque

## Explanation

The upper and lower halves produce equal forces in opposite directions, so the resultant is zero. The forces act on opposite sides of the centre. Their directions are opposite and their lever arms point in opposite directions too. Their torques therefore have the same sense and add.
