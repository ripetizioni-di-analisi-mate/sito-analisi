A normal force $F=80\,\mathrm N$ is uniformly distributed over area $A=0.013\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the pressure?

- $6.15\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $12307.7\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3076.92\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-6153.85\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Pressure is uniform, so divide the total normal force by the full area. This gives $p=\dfrac{80}{0.013}\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq6.15\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}$. As a scale check, an area of $0.01\,\mathrm{m^2}$ would give $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The actual area is slightly larger and its pressure must be lower.
