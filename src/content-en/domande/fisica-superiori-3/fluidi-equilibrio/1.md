A normal force $F=80\,\mathrm N$ is uniformly distributed over area $A=0.013\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find the pressure.

- $6.15\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $12307.7\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3076.92\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-6153.85\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Dividing by the area finds the normal force per square metre. The model assumes uniform pressure.

$$
p=\frac FA,\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{80}{13 \cdot \frac{1}{1000}}\\[4pt]&\simeq6.15\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
