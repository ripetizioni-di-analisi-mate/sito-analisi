What does zero gauge pressure mean for a tank in air?

- The tank is in perfect vacuum
- Internal density is zero
- Internal pressure equals local atmospheric pressure
- Absolute pressure is negative

## Explanation

A gauge measures the difference from reference pressure. The difference is zero when both values agree.
