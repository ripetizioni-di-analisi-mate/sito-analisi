What does zero gauge pressure mean for a tank in air?

- The tank is in perfect vacuum
- Internal density is zero
- Internal pressure at the measurement point equals local atmospheric pressure
- Absolute pressure is negative

## Explanation

The gauge compares the tank with the local air. A zero reading means the two pressures agree because the gauge measures their difference. At zero gauge pressure the tank still has the atmosphere's absolute pressure. Referencing pressure to vacuum defines a different zero.
