In water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what upward displacement reduces pressure by $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$?

- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $0.75\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Pressure decreases when moving up. In water, with the given $g$, every metre of ascent removes $10000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. A drop of $15000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ therefore takes $\dfrac{15000}{10000}\,\mathrm m=1.5\,\mathrm m$. The pressure change has a negative sign, while the upward distance is positive.
