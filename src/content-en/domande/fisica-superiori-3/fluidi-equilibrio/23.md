In water with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what upward displacement reduces pressure by $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$?

- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $0.75\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Pressure falls when ascending because the water column becomes shorter. Its change magnitude gives a positive length.

$$
h=\frac{|\Delta p|}{\rho g},\qquad
\begin{aligned}h&=\frac{15000}{10 \cdot 1000}\\[4pt]&=1.5\,\mathrm m.\end{aligned}
$$
