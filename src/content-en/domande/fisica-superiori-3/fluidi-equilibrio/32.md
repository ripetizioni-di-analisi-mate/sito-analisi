An ideal press has piston areas $0.010$ and $0.040\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The small piston drops $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the other piston travel in centimetres.

::grafico{assi=0 linee="0,1.6 0,0 4,0 4,1.6;.6,1.6 .6,.45 1.4,.45 1.4,1.6;0,1.2 .6,1.2;1.4,1.2 4,1.2" vettori=".3,1.85,.3,1.35,s_1;2.6,1.4,2.6,1.525,s_2" xmin=-.5 xmax=4.5 ymin=-.3 ymax=2.1 titolo="The pistons move in opposite directions with a four-to-one travel ratio"}

- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Displaced volume equals area times travel and is conserved. Quadruple area gives a quarter of the travel.

$$
s_2=s_1\frac{A_1}{A_2},\qquad
\begin{aligned}s_2&=12 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{100} \cdot \frac{1}{4 \cdot \frac{1}{100}}\\[4pt]&=3\,\mathrm{cm}.\end{aligned}
$$
