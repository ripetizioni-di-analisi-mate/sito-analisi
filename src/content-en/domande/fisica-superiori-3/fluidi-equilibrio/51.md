In a U-tube with oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, the left arm is $0.15\,\mathrm m$ higher. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find $p_D-p_S$, the right pressure minus the left pressure.

- $2400\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The left surface is higher. Descending $0.15\,\mathrm m$ through the oil reaches the height of the right surface and adds $800\cdot10\cdot0.15\,\mathrm{Pa}=1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$. At that height the pressures in the connected oil are equal. Therefore $p_D-p_S$ is positive.
