In a U-tube with oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, the left arm is $0.15\,\mathrm m$ higher. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find $p_\mathrm{right}-p_\mathrm{left}$.

- $2400\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The right arm is lower, so its applied pressure is higher. The positive change is that from the high level to the low level in the same liquid.

$$
\Delta p=\rho g\Delta h,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta p&=800 \cdot 10 \cdot 15 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&=1200\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
