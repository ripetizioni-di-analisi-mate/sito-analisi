A fully submerged object displaces $0.0008\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $0.0012\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the total buoyant force?

- $18.4\,\mathrm N$
- $12\,\mathrm N$
- $6.4\,\mathrm N$
- $36.8\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The two displaced volumes have different densities. The displaced oil weighs $800\cdot0.0008\cdot10\,\mathrm N=6.4\,\mathrm N$ and the displaced water weighs $1000\cdot0.0012\cdot10\,\mathrm N=12\,\mathrm N$. Their sum is $18.4\,\mathrm N$ upwards. Water contributes more because both its density and its displaced volume are larger.
