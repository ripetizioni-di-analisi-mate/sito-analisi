A body displaces a volume $V=0.0018\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of stationary water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the buoyant force. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $17.7\,\mathrm N$
- $35.316\,\mathrm N$
- $8.829\,\mathrm N$
- $-17.658\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Buoyancy equals the displaced water's weight. Volume times density is the corresponding water mass.

$$
F_A=\rho gV,\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_A&=9 \cdot \frac{1}{5000} \cdot 1000 \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&\simeq17.7\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
