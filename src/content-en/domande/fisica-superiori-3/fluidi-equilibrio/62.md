At fixed gravity, what property is proportional to the slope of the pressure–depth graph?

- Vessel area
- Density
- Absolute temperature without further assumptions
- Horizontal distance from the wall

## Explanation

On a graph with depth increasing downwards, slope is pressure increase divided by depth increase. The hydrostatic equation gives $\rho g$. With gravity fixed, the steeper line represents the denser liquid even if the two liquids have different surface pressures.
