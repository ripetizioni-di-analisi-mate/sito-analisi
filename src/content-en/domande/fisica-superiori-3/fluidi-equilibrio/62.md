At fixed gravity, what property is proportional to the slope of the pressure–depth graph?

- Vessel area
- Density
- Absolute temperature without further assumptions
- Horizontal distance from the wall

## Explanation

Pressure rises by $\rho g$ per metre. At fixed gravity this scales with density.
