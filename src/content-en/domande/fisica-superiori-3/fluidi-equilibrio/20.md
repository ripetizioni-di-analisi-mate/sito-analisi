A closed tank contains water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The water level falls by $0.40\,\mathrm m$. How much must the pressure of the gas above it change to keep the bottom pressure constant if $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $-4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $400\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $+4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

As the level falls, the water column adds $1000\cdot10\cdot0.40\,\mathrm{Pa}=4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ less to the surface pressure. Keeping the total at the bottom fixed therefore requires $\Delta p_{\rm gas}=+4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The gas pressure exactly makes up for the lost hydrostatic contribution.
