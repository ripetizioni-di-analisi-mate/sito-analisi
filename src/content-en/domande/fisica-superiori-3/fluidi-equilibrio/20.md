You descend $2\,\mathrm m$ in water with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the pressure change.

- $40000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $10000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Each metre of added water gives ten thousand pascals of weight per area. Two metres give twenty thousand pascals.

$$
\Delta p=\rho gh,\qquad
\begin{aligned}\Delta p&=1000 \cdot 10 \cdot 2\\[4pt]&=20000\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
