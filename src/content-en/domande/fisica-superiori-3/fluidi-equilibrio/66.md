Why does deriving $p=p_0+\rho gh$ with one $\rho$ require nearly constant density?

- The column area is necessarily zero
- Using a single local density for the whole column requires uniform density
- Gravity vanishes at variable density
- Pressure does not exist in gases

## Explanation

To find the column's weight, multiply its volume by a single density only when that density is nearly uniform. If the column crosses two layers, calculate the weight of each using its own density. The two contributions correspond to two different slopes on the graph.
