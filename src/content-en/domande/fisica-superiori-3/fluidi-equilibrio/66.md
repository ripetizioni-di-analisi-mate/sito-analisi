Why does deriving $p=p_0+\rho gh$ with one $\rho$ require nearly constant density?

- The column area is necessarily zero
- Column mass is density times volume only for uniform density
- Gravity vanishes at variable density
- Pressure does not exist in gases

## Explanation

A varying-density column requires adding layers with different weights. A single constant density correctly replaces all values only in the uniform model.
