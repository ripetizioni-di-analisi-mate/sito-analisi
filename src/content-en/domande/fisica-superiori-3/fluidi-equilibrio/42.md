A $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ body of volume $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ is held fully submerged by a line attached to the bottom. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ find tension.

- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $2\,\mathrm N$
- $-4\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Ten newtons of buoyancy outweigh six newtons of weight. Here the line pulls down, supplying the difference in the opposite direction to a line from above.

$$
T=\rho_f gV-mg,\qquad
\begin{aligned}T&=\left(- \frac{3}{5} + 1000 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{1000}\right) \cdot 10\\[4pt]&=4\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
