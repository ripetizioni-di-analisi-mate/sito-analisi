At $2\,\mathrm m$ below the surface of water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ the absolute pressure is $125\,\mathrm{kPa}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the absolute pressure at the surface?

- $210\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $52.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $105\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-105\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The measurement point is two metres below the surface. Going up subtracts the water contribution, $1000\cdot10\cdot2\,\mathrm{Pa}=20\,\mathrm{kPa}$. This leaves $(125-20)\,\mathrm{kPa}=105\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the surface. The original reading is absolute, so removing the column's contribution still gives an absolute pressure.
