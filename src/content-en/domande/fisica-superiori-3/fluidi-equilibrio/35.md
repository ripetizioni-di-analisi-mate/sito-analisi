A prism with vertical sides sits flat on the bottom of a tank and forms a watertight seal at its base. Water wets the sides and the entire horizontal top face. In which direction does the resultant of the water forces on the prism act?

- Upwards
- Horizontally
- Downwards
- The resultant is zero

## Explanation

On the vertical sides, pressure forces are horizontal and cancel at each height. The force on the top face acts downwards. The watertight seal keeps water off the lower face, so the upward force that would contribute to buoyancy on a freely submerged prism is absent.
