A force of $80\,\mathrm N$ acts at $60^\circ$ to the normal of a face of area $0.10\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the mean pressure due to the force?

- $400\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $800\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-400\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The angle is measured from the normal. The projection of the force in that direction is $80\cos60^\circ\,\mathrm N=40\,\mathrm N$. Over one tenth of a square metre this gives $400\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The factor $\cos60^\circ$ halves the pressure compared with applying the same force perpendicularly.
