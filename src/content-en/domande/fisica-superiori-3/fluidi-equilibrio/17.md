A force of $80\,\mathrm N$ acts at $60^\circ$ to the normal of a face of area $0.10\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find the mean pressure due to the force.

- $400\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $800\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-400\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Use the projection along the normal, forty newtons. The tangential component does not increase normal pressure.

$$
p=\frac{F\cos\theta}{A},\qquad
\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{80}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{1}{10}}\\[4pt]&=400\,\mathrm{Pa}.\end{aligned}
$$
