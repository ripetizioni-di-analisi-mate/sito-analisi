What happens when mean body density exceeds fluid density?

- Maximum buoyancy at full immersion is less than weight
- It always floats half submerged
- It floats with zero immersed volume
- Full buoyancy exceeds weight

## Explanation

At the same total volume, the body's weight exceeds the maximum weight of displaceable fluid.
