What happens when mean body density exceeds fluid density?

- Maximum buoyancy at full immersion is less than weight
- It always floats half submerged
- It floats with zero immersed volume
- Full buoyancy exceeds weight

## Explanation

Even when the body is fully submerged it can displace at most its exterior volume. The weight of displaced liquid is still below the object's weight because the body's mean density is higher. Without a support or constraint, it therefore lacks the upward force needed for equilibrium.
