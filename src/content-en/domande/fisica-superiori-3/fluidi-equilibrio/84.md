What happens to gravitational weight when an object is submerged at unchanged mass and field?

- It decreases by the buoyancy
- It increases by the tension
- It becomes zero
- It stays the same

## Explanation

Gravitational weight is the product $mg$. In the stated situation, immersing the object changes neither its mass nor the gravitational field. The liquid adds an upward force. A spring scale may therefore read less even though the object's gravitational weight stays constant.
