Which practical loss makes a hydraulic press output less work than it receives?

- A large area in the ideal model
- Piston friction
- Using the same liquid on both sides
- Choosing joules as the unit

## Explanation

Friction dissipates part of the transferred energy. The area ratio changes force and travel, but in the ideal model it preserves work.
