An ideal hydraulic press has pistons at the same height, of areas $A_1=0.0023\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2=0.023\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The input force on the smaller one is $F_1=80\,\mathrm N$. Assuming equal outside pressures on the pistons and neglecting piston weights and friction, find the balanced force on the second piston.

- $1600\,\mathrm N$
- $400\,\mathrm N$
- $-800\,\mathrm N$
- $800\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At equilibrium, equal piston heights and equal outside pressures give equal excess pressures beneath the pistons. The area ratio is $\dfrac{0.023}{0.0023}=10$, so $F_2=80\cdot10\,\mathrm N=800\,\mathrm N$. The larger area converts the same excess pressure into a greater force.
