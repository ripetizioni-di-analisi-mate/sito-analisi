An ideal hydraulic press has pistons at the same height, of areas $A_1=0.0023\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2=0.023\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The input force on the smaller one is $F_1=80\,\mathrm N$. Neglecting piston weights and friction, find the balanced force on the second piston.

- $1600\,\mathrm N$
- $400\,\mathrm N$
- $-800\,\mathrm N$
- $800\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Added pressure is equal at both pistons at the same height. Multiplying by the larger area gives the load balanced.

$$
F_2=F_1\frac{A_2}{A_1},\qquad
\begin{aligned}F_2&=80 \cdot 23 \cdot \frac{1}{1000} \cdot \frac{1}{23 \cdot \frac{1}{10000}}\\[4pt]&=800\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
