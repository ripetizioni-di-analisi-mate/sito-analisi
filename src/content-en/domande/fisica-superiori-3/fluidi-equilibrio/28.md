Absolute pressure is $82\,\mathrm{kPa}$ where atmospheric pressure is $101\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find gauge pressure.

- $-38\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-9.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $19\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-19\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Subtract the reference pressure. A negative reading means pressure below atmospheric.

$$
p_g=p_{\mathrm{abs}}-p_{\mathrm{atm}},\qquad
\begin{aligned}p_g&=82 - 101\\[4pt]&=-19\,\mathrm{kPa}.\end{aligned}
$$
