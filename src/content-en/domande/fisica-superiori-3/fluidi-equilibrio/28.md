Absolute pressure is $82\,\mathrm{kPa}$ where atmospheric pressure is $101\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the gauge pressure?

- $-38\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-9.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $19\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-19\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

A gauge reading is the absolute pressure minus atmospheric pressure. Here $p_{\rm rel}=(82-101)\,\mathrm{kPa}=-19\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The negative reading gives the amount below local atmospheric pressure. Adding back $101\,\mathrm{kPa}$ recovers the positive absolute pressure of $82\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
