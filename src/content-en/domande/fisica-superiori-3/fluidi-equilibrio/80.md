A fully submerged block moves deeper into the same uniform liquid. How does buoyancy change?

- Always grows like absolute pressure
- Decreases like weight
- Becomes equal to absolute pressure
- Stays the same

## Explanation

Lowering the block raises the pressure on its top and bottom faces together. In a homogeneous liquid their difference stays fixed because the separation between the faces is unchanged. The displaced-fluid weight gives the same result because density and submerged volume stay constant.
