A fully submerged block moves deeper into the same uniform liquid. How does buoyancy change?

- Always grows like absolute pressure
- Decreases like weight
- Becomes equal to absolute pressure
- Stays the same

## Explanation

Bottom and top pressures rise by the same amount. Their difference and the displaced volume stay unchanged.
