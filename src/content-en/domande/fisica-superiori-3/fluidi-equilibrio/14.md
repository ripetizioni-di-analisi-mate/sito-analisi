A scale beneath a tank of water reads $53\,\mathrm N$. An object hangs from a separate support. It is fully immersed and held at rest without touching the tank or spilling water. The object displaces $0.0014\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what force does the scale now read?

- $39\,\mathrm N$
- $67\,\mathrm N$
- $14\,\mathrm N$
- $53\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The water pushes upwards on the object with $1000\cdot10\cdot0.0014\,\mathrm N=14\,\mathrm N$. The object pushes downwards on the water with the same force, which adds to the original reading. The scale shows $53\,\mathrm N+14\,\mathrm N=67\,\mathrm N$. The separate support carries the rest of the object's weight.
