An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and volume $V=0.0014\,\mathrm{m^3}$ is fully submerged in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and hangs at rest from a line. Find the tension. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $70.632\,\mathrm N$
- $35.3\,\mathrm N$
- $17.658\,\mathrm N$
- $-35.316\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Buoyancy and tension act upwards. Their sum balances weight, so subtract buoyancy from weight.

$$
T=mg-\rho gV,\qquad
\begin{aligned}T&=\left(5 - 1000 \cdot 7 \cdot \frac{1}{5000}\right) \cdot 981 \cdot \frac{1}{100}\\[4pt]&\simeq35.3\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
