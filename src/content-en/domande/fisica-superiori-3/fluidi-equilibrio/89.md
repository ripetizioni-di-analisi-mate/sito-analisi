A block with mean density equal to that of a homogeneous liquid is fully submerged. Which outcome fits the ideal model?

- Equilibrium at full immersion
- Equilibrium only half submerged
- Upward acceleration independent of weight
- Absence of pressure

## Explanation

When fully submerged, the body displaces its own volume. If the densities match, buoyancy and weight have the same magnitude. With no other loads, the object can remain at equilibrium at different depths in the same uniform liquid as long as it stays fully submerged.
