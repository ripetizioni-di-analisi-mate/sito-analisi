A block with mean density equal to that of a homogeneous liquid is fully submerged. Which outcome fits the ideal model?

- Equilibrium at full immersion
- Equilibrium only half submerged
- Upward acceleration independent of weight
- Absence of pressure

## Explanation

With equal densities, full-volume buoyancy equals weight. The vertical force balance can be zero.
