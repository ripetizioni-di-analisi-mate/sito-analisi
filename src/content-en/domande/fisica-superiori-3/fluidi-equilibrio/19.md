A face has two equal-area halves at uniform pressures of $20$ and $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the mean over the whole face.

- $70\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $35\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-35\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Each half contributes its own pressure times half the surface area to the total force. Dividing by the whole area gives $\overline p=\dfrac{20+50}{2}\,\mathrm{kPa}=35\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Both halves receive the same weight in this average because their areas are equal.
