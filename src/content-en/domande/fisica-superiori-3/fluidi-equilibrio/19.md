A face has two equal-area halves at uniform pressures of $20$ and $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the mean over the whole face.

- $70\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $35\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-35\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Add forces, giving the pressures the same geometric weights as their areas.

$$
p_m=\frac{p_1A/2+p_2A/2}{A},\qquad
\begin{aligned}p_m&=\frac{20 + 50}{2}\\[4pt]&=35\,\mathrm{kPa}.\end{aligned}
$$
