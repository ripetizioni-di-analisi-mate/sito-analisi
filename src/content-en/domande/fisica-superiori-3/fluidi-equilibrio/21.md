The graph shows the pressure increase below the surface of two liquid layers. Point I at the interface gives $4\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at $0.50\,\mathrm m$. Point B at the bottom gives $14\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at $1.50\,\mathrm m$. At what depth does the sensor show an increase of $9\,\mathrm{kPa}$?

::grafico{linee="0,0 .5,4 1.5,14" punti=".5,4,pieno,I;1.5,14,pieno,B" xmin=0 xmax=1.8 ymin=0 ymax=16 nomi="h (m),Δp (kPa)" titolo="I marks the interface and B the bottom."}

- $1.00\,\mathrm m$
- $0.50\,\mathrm m$
- $0.90\,\mathrm m$
- $1.125\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The reading of $9\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is above the interface reading, so use the slope of the lower segment. There, pressure increases by $10\,\mathrm{kPa}$ per metre. Adding $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ takes another $0.50\,\mathrm m$ below the interface. The total depth is $1.00\,\mathrm m$ below the surface.
