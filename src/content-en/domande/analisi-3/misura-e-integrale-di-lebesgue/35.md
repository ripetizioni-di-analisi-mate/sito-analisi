Set $F(t)=\int_0^\infty e^{-tx}\cos(3x)\,dx$ for $t>0$. What is $F(7)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{58}$
- $\dfrac{7}{58}$
- $\dfrac{7}{40}$

## Explanation

The complex exponential has absolute value $e^{-7x}$ and is therefore integrable, so the real part can be taken after integration. The complex integral equals $\dfrac1{7-3i}$. Multiplying numerator and denominator by $7+3i$ gives real part $\dfrac7{58}$.
