On $(0,4)$, for $f(x)=\sqrt x$, what is the measure of $\{f>\dfrac32\}$?

- $\dfrac94$
- $\dfrac74$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The function is increasing and nonnegative, so the inequality can be squared without changing its direction. This gives $x>\dfrac94$, and the desired measure is the length of the interval from nine quarters to four.
