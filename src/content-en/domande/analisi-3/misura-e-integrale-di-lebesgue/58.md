Compute $\iint_D 4x\,dx\,dy$, where $D=\{0<x<y<9\}$.

- $1458$
- $486$
- $162$
- $972$

## Explanation

The domain is measurable and the integrand nonnegative, so Tonelli's theorem applies. For fixed $y$, integrating $4x$ between zero and $y$ gives $2y^2$. Integrating this between zero and nine gives $486$.
