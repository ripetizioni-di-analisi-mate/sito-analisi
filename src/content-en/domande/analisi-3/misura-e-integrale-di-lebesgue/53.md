Which integrable function on $(0,\infty)$ dominates $f_n(x)=\dfrac{x^{2}e^{-5x}}{1+\dfrac{x}{n}}$ for all $n\ge 1$?

- $x^{2} e^{- 5 x}$
- $x^{2} e^{- 6 x}$
- $x^{2}$
- $e^{- 5 x}$

## Explanation

The denominator is at least one, so dropping it gives the majorant. A polynomial times a decaying exponential is integrable here. Each other exponential candidate is below the pointwise limit somewhere and hence cannot dominate the entire sequence.
