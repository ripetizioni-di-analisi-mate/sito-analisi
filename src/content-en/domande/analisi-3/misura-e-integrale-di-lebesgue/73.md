What is the measure of the region $D=\{(x,y):x>0,\ 0<y<e^{-x}\}$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $+\infty$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The indicator of the domain is measurable and nonnegative, so Tonelli's theorem permits integration of the vertical section lengths. Each has length $e^{-x}$, whose integral on the half-line is one even though the projection of the domain is unbounded.
