Which power function belongs to $L^1(1,\infty)$?

- $x^{\frac12}$
- $x^{-1}$
- $x^{-\frac32}$
- $x^{-\frac12}$

## Explanation

At infinity the antiderivative of $x^{-\alpha}$ tends to a finite limit if $\alpha>1$. For $\alpha=1$ it grows logarithmically. Only the power with exponent minus three halves meets the tail integrability condition.
