On $(0,1)$, which simple function bounds $x^{-\frac14}$ from above on the region $[\frac1{16},1)$ alone?

- $2\mathbf1_{[\frac14,1)}$
- $2\mathbf1_{[\frac1{16},1)}$
- $\dfrac32\mathbf1_{[\frac1{16},1)}$
- $\mathbf1_{[\frac1{16},1)}$

## Explanation

The power function with negative exponent decreases. On the given interval it attains its maximum at the left endpoint, where the fourth root of the reciprocal equals two. The constant function of height two on this interval is therefore an upper bound.
