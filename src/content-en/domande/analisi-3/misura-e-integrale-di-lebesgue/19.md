Set $F(t)=\int_0^1 x^{3}e^{-7tx}\,dx$. Compute $F^\prime(0)$.

- $- \dfrac{7}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{5}$

## Explanation

The derivative with respect to the parameter exists at every point of the domain. On $\lvert t\rvert\le1$ its absolute value is bounded by the constant $7e^7$, so differentiation under the integral sign is justified. At zero the result is the integral of $-7x^4$.
