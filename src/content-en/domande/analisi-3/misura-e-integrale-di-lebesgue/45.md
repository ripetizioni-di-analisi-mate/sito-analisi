For an integrable function $f$, if $\int\lvert f\rvert\,dm=4$ and $\int f\,dm=-2$, what is $\int f^+\,dm$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

The integrals of the two parts sum to four and their difference is minus two. Adding these equations gives twice the integral of the positive part, equal to two before division.
