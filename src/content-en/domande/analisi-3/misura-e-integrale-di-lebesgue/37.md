For $f(x)=x-1$ on $(0,3)$, what is $\int f^-\,dm$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $\dfrac52$

## Explanation

The negative part equals $1-x$ between zero and one and is zero where $f$ is nonnegative. Its graph bounds a triangle of base one and height one, so its integral is one half.
