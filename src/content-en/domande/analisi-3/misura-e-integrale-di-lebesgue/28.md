What is the measure of $[0,3]\cup\bigl(\mathbb Q\cap[3,7]\bigr)$?

- $7$
- $4$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The rationals in $[3,7]$ form a countable set, so they have measure zero. The part added to the interval $[0,3]$ lies in that set and also has measure zero. Thus the union has measure three.
