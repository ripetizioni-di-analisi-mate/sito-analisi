On $(0,1)$, let $f_n=2\mathbf1_{(0,\frac12)}$ for even $n$ and $f_n=2\mathbf1_{(\frac12,1)}$ for odd $n$. What is $\int_0^1\liminf_n f_n\,dm$?

- The limit inferior is undefined.
- $0$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

At every point in the first half-interval the terms with odd indices vanish. On the second half-interval the even-indexed terms vanish. Since all functions are nonnegative, the pointwise limit inferior is zero everywhere.
