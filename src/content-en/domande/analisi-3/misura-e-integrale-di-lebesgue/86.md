For $F(t,x)=e^{-tx^2}$ on $(0,\infty)$ and $1\le t\le2$, which integrable function dominates $\lvert\partial_t F(t,x)\rvert$?

- $1$
- $x^2e^{-x^2}$
- $e^{-x^2}$
- $x^2e^{-2x^2}$

## Explanation

The derivative has absolute value $x^2e^{-tx^2}$. As $t$ increases, the exponential factor decreases, so its maximum on the interval occurs at $t=1$. A power times this Gaussian is integrable on the half-line.
