Let $\mathcal A$ be the sigma-algebra generated by $\{a,b\}$ in $X=\{a,b,c,d\}$. Which set belongs to $\mathcal A$?

- $\{b,c\}$
- $\{c,d\}$
- $\{a,b,c\}$
- $\{a\}$

## Explanation

The sigma-algebra must contain the complement of its generator, giving $\{c,d\}$. The empty set, the whole set, and the two blocks already form a family closed under complements and countable unions. Individual points within each block cannot be distinguished.
