Compute $\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^{\frac12}\sum_{k=0}^n x^k\,dx$.

- $+\infty$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $\log 2$

## Explanation

The partial sums are nonnegative and increasing, so monotone convergence gives the integral of the geometric series. On the domain the sum is $\dfrac1{1-x}$. Evaluating the antiderivative $-\log(1-x)$ at the endpoints gives $\log 2$.
