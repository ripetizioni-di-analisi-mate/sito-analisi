Suppose $f_n\to f$ almost everywhere and $\lvert f_n\rvert\le g$ with $g$ integrable. Which almost-everywhere nonnegative sequence converges to $2g$ almost everywhere and allows Fatou's lemma to give an upper bound on $\limsup_n\int\lvert f_n-f\rvert\,d\mu$?

- $2g-\lvert f_n-f\rvert$
- $2g+\lvert f_n-f\rvert$
- $g-\lvert f_n-f\rvert$
- $\lvert f_n-f\rvert$

## Explanation

The common bound gives $\lvert f\rvert\le g$ almost everywhere, so the absolute difference is at most $2g$. Subtracting the error from $2g$ gives a nonnegative sequence. After subtraction, Fatou's inequality yields an upper bound for the integral of the error.
