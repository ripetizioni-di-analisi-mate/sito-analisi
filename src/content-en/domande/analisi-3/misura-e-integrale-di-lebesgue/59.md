Let $f_n\ge-g$, with $g\ge0$ integrable and $f_n$ measurable. To which sequence should Fatou's lemma be applied to deduce $\int\liminf f_n\le\liminf\int f_n$?

- $-f_n-g$
- $f_n^+$, always subtracting $\int g$
- $f_n+g$
- $f_n-g$

## Explanation

The lower bound makes $f_n+g$ nonnegative. The term $g$ is independent of $n$, so it can be taken outside the pointwise limit inferior. Its integral is finite and can therefore be subtracted from both sides of the resulting inequality.
