A sigma-algebra has three atoms $A,B,C$. A measure satisfies $\mu(A\cup B)=7$, $\mu(A\cup C)=6$, and $\mu(B\cup C)=5$. What is $\mu(A)$?

- $6$
- $4$
- $3$
- $8$

## Explanation

The atoms are disjoint, so the sum of the first two measures is twice the mass of $A$ plus the mass of $B\cup C$. Subtracting the latter leaves eight, which must be divided by two.
