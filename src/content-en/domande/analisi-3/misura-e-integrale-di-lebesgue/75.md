In the square $(0,1)^2$ consider the set $E=\{(x,y):xy<\dfrac{1}{4}\}$. Which integral in $x$ alone gives the measure of $E$?

- $\int_0^1\dfrac{1}{4x}\,\mathrm dx$
- $\int_0^1 4x\,\mathrm dx$
- $\int_0^{\dfrac{1}{4}}1\,\mathrm dx+\int_{\dfrac{1}{4}}^1\dfrac{1}{4x}\,\mathrm dx$
- $\int_{\dfrac{1}{4}}^1(1-\dfrac{1}{4x})\,\mathrm dx$

## Explanation

For $0<x\le\dfrac{1}{4}$ the vertical section fills the full height of the square. For $\dfrac{1}{4}<x<1$ its length is $\dfrac{1}{4x}$. Tonelli’s theorem applied to the indicator adds the lengths of the two families of sections.
