Compute $\int_0^\infty\int_0^\pi e^{-x}\sin y\,dy\,dx$.

- $0$
- $\pi$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The sine is nonnegative over the integration interval, so Tonelli's theorem applies. The product separates the variables. The exponential has integral one on the half-line, and the antiderivative $-\cos y$ evaluated between zero and $\pi$ gives two.
