Compute $\int_{\mathbb R}(5\mathbf1_{[0,2]}+6\mathbf1_{(2,5]})\,dx$.

- $27$
- $29$
- $28$
- $11$

## Explanation

The intervals are measurable and disjoint. The simple function takes the value five on the first interval and six on the second, so its integral is the sum of the two contributions $5\cdot2$ and $6\cdot3$.

$$
\int f\,dx=5\cdot2+6\cdot3=28.
$$
