On a space of infinite measure, which condition guarantees that a nonnegative simple function $s=\sum_{j=1}^m a_j\mathbf1_{E_j}$ with disjoint $E_j$ has finite integral?

- All $a_j$ are less than one.
- At least one $E_j$ with $a_j>0$ has finite measure.
- Every $E_j$ with $a_j>0$ has finite measure.
- Only the zero level set has finite measure.

## Explanation

The integral is a finite sum of nonnegative products. A positive coefficient on a set of infinite measure contributes infinity. The zero level contributes zero even if its set has infinite measure.
