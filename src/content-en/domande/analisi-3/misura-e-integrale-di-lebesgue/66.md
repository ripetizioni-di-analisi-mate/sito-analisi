For $f_n(x)=\dfrac{1}{(1+x^2)(1+\frac{x}{n})}$ on $(0,\infty)$, which piecewise integrable majorant can be used?

- $x^{-1}$ for every $x$.
- $1$ for $x\le1$ and $x^{-2}$ for $x>1$.
- $1$ for every $x$.
- $x^{-2}$ for $x\le1$ and $1$ for $x>1$.

## Explanation

The factor $1+\dfrac{x}{n}$ is at least one. Near zero the first denominator factor is at least one, and beyond one it is at least $x^2$. The two bounds are integrable, respectively on a finite interval and on a tail with exponent less than minus one.
