On $(0,1)$, let $f_n=n\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1{n^2})}$ and $f=0$. For $n\ge2$, which relation distinguishes the uniform error from the error in $L^1$?

- Both norms equal $n$.
- $\lVert f_n\rVert_\infty=\dfrac1n$ and $\lVert f_n\rVert_1=n$
- $\lVert f_n\rVert_\infty=n$ and $\lVert f_n\rVert_1=\dfrac1n$
- Both norms equal $\dfrac1n$.

## Explanation

The maximum is attained on an interval of positive measure, so it equals the essential supremum. The integral of the absolute value instead multiplies the height by the support length. Thus the error in the one-norm tends to zero.
