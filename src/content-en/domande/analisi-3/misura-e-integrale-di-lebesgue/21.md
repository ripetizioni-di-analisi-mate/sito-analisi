On $[0,1)$, define $s(x)=\dfrac{\lfloor4x\rfloor}{4}$. Compute $\int_0^1s(x)\,dx$.

- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The levels are zero, one quarter, one half, and three quarters. Each occurs on an interval of length one quarter, so the integral is one quarter of their sum. This is less than the integral of $x$ because the values were rounded down.
