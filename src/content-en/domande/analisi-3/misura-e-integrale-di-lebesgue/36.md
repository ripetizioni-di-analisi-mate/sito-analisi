On $(0,1)$, the function $s$ equals $3$ on a measurable set $A$ and $1$ on its complement. If $\int_0^1s\,dm=\dfrac74$, what is the measure of $A$?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac7{12}$
- $\dfrac38$

## Explanation

Write $a$ for the measure of $A$. Its complement has measure $1-a$, and the simple-function integral gives $3a+(1-a)=1+2a$. Subtracting one and dividing by two gives three eighths.
