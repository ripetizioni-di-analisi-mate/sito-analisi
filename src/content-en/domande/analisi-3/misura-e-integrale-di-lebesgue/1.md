Find the Lebesgue measure of $[0,7]\cup[6,14]$.

- $14$
- $7$
- $0$
- $15$

## Explanation

The intersection is $[6,7]$, so the two intervals overlap. Every point between the outer endpoints belongs to at least one interval. The union therefore equals $[0,14]$ and its measure is the length of that interval.

$$
[0,7]\cup[6,14]=[0,14],\qquad m=14.
$$
