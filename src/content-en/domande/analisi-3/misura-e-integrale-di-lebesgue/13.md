Compute $\int_0^\infty x e^{-5x}\,dx$ by differentiating the formula $\int_0^\infty e^{-ax}dx=\frac1a$ with respect to $a$.

- $\frac1{25}$
- $-\frac1{25}$
- $0$
- $\frac2{25}$

## Explanation

On a compact neighborhood of the parameter value five, the absolute derivative is bounded by $xe^{-4x}$, integrable on the half-line. Differentiation under the integral sign is therefore justified. The minus sign from differentiating the exponential cancels the one from differentiating the reciprocal.

$$
-\int_0^\infty xe^{-ax}dx=-\frac1{a^2},\qquad a=5.
$$
