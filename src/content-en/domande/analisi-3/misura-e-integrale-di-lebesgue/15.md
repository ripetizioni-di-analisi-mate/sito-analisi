For $f_n(x)=5x^{-11}\mathbf1_{[\frac{1}{n},1]}(x)$ on $(0,1]$, what is $\lim_n\int_0^1 f_n\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $5$
- $\infty$
- $0$

## Explanation

The intervals $[\frac1n,1]$ increase to cover $(0,1]$. The functions are measurable, nonnegative, and increasing, so monotone convergence applies. The integral of the power $x^{-11}$ diverges near zero because the exponent is less than minus one.
