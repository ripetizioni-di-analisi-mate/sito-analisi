Compute $\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty e^{-5x-6y}\,dy\,dx$.

- $\frac1{11}$
- $0$
- $\frac1{30}$
- $30$

## Explanation

The exponential is measurable and nonnegative on the product of two half-lines with sigma-finite measures, so Tonelli's theorem applies. The exponential factors and the two elementary integrals on the half-lines are one fifth and one sixth, respectively.

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-5x}dx\int_0^\infty e^{-6y}dy=\frac1{5}\frac1{6}=\frac1{30}.
$$
