Let $G(t)=\int_0^1\dfrac{\log(1+tx)}{x}\,dx$ for $t>-1$. Compute $G'(0)$.

- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $-1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Differentiating the logarithm with respect to $t$ produces a factor $x$ that cancels the original denominator. On a neighborhood of zero the reciprocal $1$ divided by $1+tx$ is uniformly bounded. At zero the derivative of the integrand is one throughout the interval.
