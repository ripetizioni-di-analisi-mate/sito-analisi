Let $f$ be real-valued and measurable. Which union describes $\{f>t\}$ using rational thresholds?

- $\bigcup_{q\in\mathbb Q,\ q<t}\{f>q\}$
- $\bigcup_{q\in\mathbb Q,\ q>t}\{f>q\}$
- $\bigcap_{q\in\mathbb Q,\ q<t}\{f>q\}$
- $\bigcap_{q\in\mathbb Q,\ q>t}\{f>q\}$

## Explanation

If $f(x)>t$, density of the rationals gives a rational strictly between the two values. Hence the point belongs to the union over thresholds larger than $t$. Conversely, membership in that union means that the value exceeds a threshold larger than $t$, giving the required inequality.
