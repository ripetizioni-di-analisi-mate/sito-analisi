How many elements are in the $\sigma$-algebra generated by 3 distinct singletons in a set of 7 points?

- $10$
- $8$
- $16$
- $128$

## Explanation

Closure under finite unions and complements gives the three singletons and the complement of their union. These four blocks are disjoint and nonempty. Their unions already form a sigma-algebra, so there are exactly two independent choices for each block.
