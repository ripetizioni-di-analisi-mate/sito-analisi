On $(0,1)$, consider $f_n(x)=\dfrac{\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}(x)}{\sqrt x}$. Which argument justifies $\int f_n\to0$?

- Dominated convergence with $g(x)=1$.
- Uniform convergence on $(0,1)$.
- The monotone convergence theorem for increasing sequences.
- Dominated convergence with $g(x)=x^{-\frac{1}{2}}$.

## Explanation

Each positive point eventually lies outside the support, so the limit is zero. All terms have absolute value at most $x^{-\frac12}$, whose integral on $(0,1)$ is finite. Thus dominated convergence applies.
