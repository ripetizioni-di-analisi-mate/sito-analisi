Let $E_n$ be measurable, with $\mu(E_n)\le2^{-n}$ for $n\ge1$. What is the measure of the set of points belonging to infinitely many $E_n$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- It can be any value between zero and one.
- $1$

## Explanation

The desired set lies in every tail union. By subadditivity its measure is at most $\sum_{n\ge k}2^{-n}=2^{1-k}$ for every $k$. Since the upper bound tends to zero, its measure must be zero.
