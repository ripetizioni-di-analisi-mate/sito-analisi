For $t>0$, let $H(t)=\int_0^\infty\dfrac{e^{-x}-e^{-tx}}{x}\,dx$. What is $H(2)$?

- $1$
- $\log 2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $-\log 2$

## Explanation

At $t=1$ the integrand vanishes. Its derivative with respect to $t$ is $e^{-tx}$ and, locally around each positive parameter, a decaying exponential dominates it. This gives $H'(t)=\dfrac1t$, so integration of the derivative from one to two gives $\log 2$.
