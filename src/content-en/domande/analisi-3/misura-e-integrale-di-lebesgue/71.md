For $D=\{(x,y):0<x<1,\ x^2<y<1\}$, which pair of bounds allows integration in $x$ first?

- $0<y<1,\quad0<x<y^2$
- $0<y<1,\quad y^2<x<1$
- $0<y<1,\quad0<x<\sqrt y$
- $0<y<1,\quad \sqrt y<x<1$

## Explanation

The projection of the domain onto the $y$ axis is the interval from zero to one. At each such height, the condition $x^2<y$ with $x>0$ is equivalent to $x<\sqrt y$. The horizontal section therefore starts at zero.
