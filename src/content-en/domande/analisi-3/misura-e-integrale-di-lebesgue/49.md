For $n\ge1$, set $f_n(x)=\min(n,\dfrac1x)$ on $(0,1)$. Which relation holds for every $n\ge1$?

- $\int_0^1 f_n\,dx=1+\log n$
- $\int_0^1 f_n\,dx=1+\dfrac1n$
- $\int_0^1 f_n\,dx=n$
- $\int_0^1 f_n\,dx=\log n$

## Explanation

The two branches meet at $x=\dfrac1n$. To the left the truncation equals $n$, contributing one. To the right we integrate the reciprocal of the variable, giving $\log n$.
