What is $\int_{(0,1)\times(0,\infty)} e^{-xy}\,dx\,dy$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

For fixed $x>0$, the integral in $y$ of the exponential over the half-line equals $\dfrac1x$. The function is measurable and nonnegative, so Tonelli's theorem permits the iterated integration even though the outer integral diverges. The reciprocal of the variable is not integrable near zero.
