Compute $\int_D\dfrac1y\,dx\,dy$ for $D=\{(x,y):0<x<y<1\}$.

- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

The integrand is measurable and nonnegative on $D$, so Tonelli's theorem applies. For fixed $y$ the section has length $y$ and the value $1$ divided by $y$ is constant. Their product is one, and the outer integral equals one.
