For $g\in L^1(0,1)$ let $G(x)=\int_x^1g(y)\,\mathrm dy$. Which weight $w(y)$ satisfies $\int_0^1G(x)\,\mathrm dx=\int_0^1w(y)g(y)\,\mathrm dy$ for every $g$?

- $w(y)=1-y$
- $w(y)=1$
- $w(y)=y$
- $w(y)=y^2$

## Explanation

After reversing the order of integration, for fixed $y$ the variable $x$ runs over $(0,y)$. The weight is the length of that interval. Fubini applies because the double integral of the absolute value is at most $\|g\|_1$.
