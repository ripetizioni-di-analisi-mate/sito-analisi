Let $G(t)=\int_0^2\dfrac1{1+tx}\,dx$ for $t>-\dfrac12$. What is $G'(0)$?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac43$

## Explanation

For $\lvert t\rvert\le\dfrac14$ the denominator is at least one half, so the absolute derivative of the integrand is at most $4x$. This majorant is integrable on $(0,2)$. Differentiating under the integral sign and setting the parameter equal to zero gives the integral of $-x$.
