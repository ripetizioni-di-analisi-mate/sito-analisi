On $(0,1)$, let $f_n(x)=x^n$. What is $\int_0^1\lvert f_n-0\rvert\,dx$?

- $\dfrac1{n+2}$
- $\dfrac1n$
- $0$
- $\dfrac1{n+1}$

## Explanation

The pointwise limit on $(0,1)$ is zero and every term is nonnegative. The absolute error therefore equals $x^n$. Its antiderivative is $\dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}$ and vanishes at zero.
