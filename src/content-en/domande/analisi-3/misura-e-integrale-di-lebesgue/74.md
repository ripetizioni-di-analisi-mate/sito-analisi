What is $\int_{[0,1]^2}\lvert x-y\rvert\,dx\,dy$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac13$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The diagonal divides the square into two mirror-image triangles. On $y<x$, the absolute value is $x-y$. Integrating from zero to $x$ in $y$ gives $\dfrac{x^2}{2}$. One triangle contributes one sixth and the other makes the same contribution.
