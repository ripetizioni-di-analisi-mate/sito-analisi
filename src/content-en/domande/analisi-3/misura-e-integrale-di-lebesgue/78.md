For $f(x)=\sqrt x$ on $(0,4)$, which integral in $t$ equals $\int_0^4f(x)\,dx$ using the measures of $\{f>t\}$?

- $\int_0^4(4-\sqrt t)\,dt$
- $\int_0^2(4-t^2)\,dt$
- $\int_0^2(2-t^2)\,dt$
- $\int_0^2t^2\,dt$

## Explanation

Integrate the indicator of the region under the graph. At fixed height $t$ between zero and two, the section in $x$ is $(t^2,4)$ and has length $4-t^2$. Above two the section is empty. Tonelli's theorem gives the integral of these lengths.
