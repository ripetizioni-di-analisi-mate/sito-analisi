On $X=\{1,2,3,4\}$, let $A=\{1,2\}$ and $B=\{2,3\}$. How many atoms does the sigma-algebra generated by $A$ and $B$ have?

- $8$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each point is identified by whether it belongs to $A$ and whether it belongs to $B$. All four possible pairs occur, and their classes are intersections of the generators or their complements. This gives four singletons.
