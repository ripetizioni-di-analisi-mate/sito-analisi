Compute $\int_0^1\bigl|4x-1\bigr|\,dx$.

- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The line $4x-1$ vanishes at $\dfrac14$. Taking the absolute value reflects its negative portion upward to the left of that point. The two triangular regions have bases $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac34$ and heights one and three. Their total area is $\dfrac54$.
