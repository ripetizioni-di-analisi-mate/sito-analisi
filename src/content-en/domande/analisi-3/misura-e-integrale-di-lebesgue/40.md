On $(-1,1)$ let $f(x)=\dfrac1x$ for $x\ne0$, and let $f(0)=0$. Which statement holds?

- The function belongs to $L^1(-1,1)$.
- The Lebesgue integral equals zero.
- The Lebesgue integral equals $+\infty$.
- The Lebesgue integral is undefined.

## Explanation

On the positive half-interval the integral of the positive part diverges. On the negative half-interval the negative part has the same infinite integral. The definition would require subtracting two infinities, so the Lebesgue integral is undefined even though the symmetric principal value is zero.
