Let $f_n=-n\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}$ on $(0,1)$. What is the pair $(\int\liminf f_n,\ \liminf\int f_n)$?

- $(0,-1)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(-1,-1)$
- $(-1,0)$

## Explanation

At each positive point the sequence eventually vanishes, so its limit inferior has integral zero. Each term has integral minus one. Fatou's nonnegativity hypothesis is absent and its inequality fails here.
