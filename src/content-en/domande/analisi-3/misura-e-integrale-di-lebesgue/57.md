On $(0,1)$, measurable functions $f_n$ satisfy $f_n(x)\ge -x^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$ for every $n$. Which modification yields Fatou’s inequality for the functions even if they change sign?

- Apply Fatou to $f_n+x^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$ and subtract the integral of $x^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
- Apply Fatou to $-f_n$ without checking for a lower bound.
- Replace $f_n$ by $|f_n|$ and then remove the absolute values on both sides.
- Subtract $x^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$ from each term before applying Fatou.

## Explanation

The shifted functions are nonnegative. Moreover, $\int_0^1x^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}\,\mathrm dx=2$ is finite, and the fixed shift commutes with the pointwise lower limit. Subtract the same finite number from both sides.
