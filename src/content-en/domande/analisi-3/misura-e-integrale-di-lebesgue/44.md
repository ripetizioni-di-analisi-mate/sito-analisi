On $(0,1)$, which pair $(\int f,\int\lvert f\rvert)$ is obtained for $f(x)=\mathbf1_{(0,\frac13)}(x)-\mathbf1_{(\frac13,1)}(x)$?

- $(1,1)$
- $\left(\dfrac13,1\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac13,\dfrac13\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac13,1\right)$

## Explanation

The disjoint intervals have lengths one third and two thirds. The signed integral subtracts the second length from the first. The absolute value is one on both intervals, so its integral is their sum.
