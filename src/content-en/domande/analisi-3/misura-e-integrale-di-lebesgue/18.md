After two steps of the middle-thirds Cantor construction starting from $[0,1]$, what is the measure of the union of the remaining closed intervals?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac59$

## Explanation

At each step, two disjoint intervals remain from each original interval, each one third as long. After the second step, four intervals of length one ninth remain. Additivity gives four ninths.
