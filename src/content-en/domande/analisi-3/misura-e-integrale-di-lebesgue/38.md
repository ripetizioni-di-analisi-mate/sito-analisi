Which power function belongs to $L^1(0,1)$?

- $x^{-1}$
- $x^{-\frac34}$
- $x^{-\frac54}$
- $x^{-2}$

## Explanation

Only the endpoint zero requires an improper integral, since the power is continuous up to one. For the power $x^{-\alpha}$, the truncated antiderivative has a finite limit when $1-\alpha>0$. Among the given exponents, only $\alpha=\dfrac34$ satisfies that condition.
