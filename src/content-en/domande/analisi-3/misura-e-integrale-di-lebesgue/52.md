On $X$, let $f\ge0$ be measurable. Which sequence is necessarily increasing and converges pointwise to $f$?

- $f\mathbf1_{\{f>n\}}$
- $\max(f,n)$
- $\dfrac{f}{n}$
- $\min(f,n)$

## Explanation

At a fixed point, the minimum of its value and an increasing integer is increasing. If the value is finite, the minimum eventually equals it. If it is infinite, the minima are the successive integers and tend to infinity.
