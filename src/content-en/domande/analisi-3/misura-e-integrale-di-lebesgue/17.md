To cover the enumerated rationals in $(0,1)$, place an interval of length $\varepsilon 3^{-k}$ around the term $q_k$ for $k\ge1$. What is the sum of the lengths?

- $\dfrac{\varepsilon}{2}$
- $3\varepsilon$
- $\dfrac{\varepsilon}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3\varepsilon}{2}$

## Explanation

This is a cover because each enumerated rational lies in its assigned interval. The lengths form a geometric series with first term $\dfrac{\varepsilon}{3}$ and ratio one third, so their sum is half of $\varepsilon$.
