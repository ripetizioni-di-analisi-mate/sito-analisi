What is the Lebesgue measure of $\bigcup_{n\ge 1}(4,4+\dfrac{9}n)$?

- $9$
- $4$
- $13$
- $0$

## Explanation

The left endpoint is fixed and the right endpoint decreases as the index increases. Every interval is therefore contained in the first. Since the first interval is itself part of the union, the union equals it and has measure nine.
