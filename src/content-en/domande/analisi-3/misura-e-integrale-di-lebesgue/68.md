Let $f_n(x)=\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}(x)$ on $(0,\infty)$. Which function is a common integrable majorant?

- $0$
- $e^{-x}$
- The constant function one.
- $\mathbf1_{(0,1)}$

## Explanation

All supports lie in $(0,1)$ and all heights equal one. The indicator of the first support therefore dominates the whole sequence. Its integral is one, whereas the constant one has infinite integral on the whole half-line.
