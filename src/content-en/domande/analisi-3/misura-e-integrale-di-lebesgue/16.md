Compute $\int_0^{7} \bigl(3\mathbf1_{[0,3]}+7\mathbf1_{[2,7]}\bigr)\,dx$.

- $37$
- $10$
- $35$
- $44$

## Explanation

The two indicators are integrable because their intervals have finite measure. Linearity permits addition of their contributions even where the supports overlap. The lengths are three and five and the weighted contributions are nine and thirty-five.
