Let $s=4\mathbf1_A-2\mathbf1_B$ with $A,B$ measurable, $m(A)=5$, $m(B)=4$, and $m(A\cap B)=3$. Compute $\int|s|\,dm$.

- $16$
- $28$
- $12$
- $18$

## Explanation

On the disjoint pieces $A\setminus B$, $A\cap B$, and $B\setminus A$, the absolute value equals four, two, and two, respectively. Their measures are two, three, and one, so the contributions are eight, six, and two.
