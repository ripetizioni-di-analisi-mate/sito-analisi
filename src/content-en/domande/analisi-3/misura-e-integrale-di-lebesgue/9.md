For $f_n(x)=5n\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}(x)$ on $(0,1)$, compute $\lim_n\int f_n$ and $\int\lim_n f_n$, in that order.

- $(5,0)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(0,5)$
- $(5,5)$

## Explanation

At each fixed point the indicator vanishes as soon as the index exceeds the reciprocal of the coordinate. The pointwise limit is therefore zero. Each integral, however, equals the rectangle's height times its base length, which always gives five.

$$
\int_0^1 f_n\,dx=5n\frac1n=5,\qquad f_n(x)\to0\quad(x>0).
$$
