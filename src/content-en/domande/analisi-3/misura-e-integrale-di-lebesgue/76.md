For $i,j\ge1$, let $a_{ij}=\mathbf1_{\{j=i\}}-\mathbf1_{\{j=i+2\}}$. What are the two iterated sums, first summing over $j$ and first summing over $i$, respectively?

- $(2,0)$
- $(2,2)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(0,2)$

## Explanation

Each row contains one positive and one negative contribution, so its sum is zero. The first two columns contain only the positive contribution, while in each remaining column the two contributions cancel. The reverse iterated sum is therefore two.
