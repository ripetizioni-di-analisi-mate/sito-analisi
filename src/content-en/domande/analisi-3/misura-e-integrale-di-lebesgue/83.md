Let $F(t)=\int_0^1\cos(tx)\,dx$. Compute $F''(0)$.

- $0$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The first and second derivatives in $t$ are bounded in absolute value by $x$ and $x^2$, respectively, both integrable on the domain. We can differentiate twice under the integral sign. At zero the second derivative of the integrand equals $-x^2$.
