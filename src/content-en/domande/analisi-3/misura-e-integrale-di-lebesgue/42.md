On $(0,1)$, let $f(x)=x^{-\frac12}$ for $x\notin\mathbb Q$ and $f(x)=100$ for $x\in\mathbb Q$. What is $\int_0^1 f\,dm$?

- $102$
- $2$
- $100$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

Both branches are measurable, and the rational branch is supported on a null set. The function equals $x^{-\frac12}$ almost everywhere. Its integral therefore equals that of the power function, with antiderivative $2\sqrt x$.
