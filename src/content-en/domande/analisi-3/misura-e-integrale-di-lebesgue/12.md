Using Lebesgue measure, compute $m\bigl(\bigcup_{n\ge2}[\frac1n,1-\frac1n]\bigr)$.

- $0$
- $+\infty$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

The intervals increase because their left endpoints decrease and their right endpoints increase. Continuity from below gives the limit of the interval measures $1-\dfrac2n$. The endpoints zero and one remain excluded and have measure zero.
