On $\mathbb R$, for $f_n=\mathbf1_{(n,n+1)}$, which statement is true?

- The integrals tend to zero.
- The constant one is an integrable majorant.
- The pointwise limit equals one almost everywhere.
- A common majorant must have infinite integral.

## Explanation

A common majorant is at least one almost everywhere on each interval supporting a term. The intervals are disjoint and their union has infinite measure. Yet the pointwise limit is zero because each point belongs to at most one interval.
