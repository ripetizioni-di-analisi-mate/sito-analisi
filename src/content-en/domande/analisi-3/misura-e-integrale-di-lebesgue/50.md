For $I_k=(2^{-k},2^{1-k}]$ on $(0,1]$, let $f=\sum_{k\ge1} k\mathbf1_{I_k}$. Compute $\int_0^1 f\,dm$.

- $1$
- $2$
- $+\infty$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The intervals are measurable and have length $2^{-k}$. The nonnegative partial sums increase to $f$, so the integral is $\sum_{k\ge1} k2^{-k}$. Differentiating the geometric series gives the sum two.
