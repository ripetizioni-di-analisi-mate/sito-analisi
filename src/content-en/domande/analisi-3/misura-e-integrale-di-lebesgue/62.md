Let $f_n(x)=n\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1{n^2})}(x)$ on $(0,1)$. Which function dominates all the terms and is integrable?

- $x^{\frac12}$
- $x^{-\frac12}$
- $1$
- $x^{-1}$

## Explanation

Where $f_n$ is nonzero, $x<\dfrac1{n^2}$, so $x^{-\frac12}>n$. This power is integrable near zero because its exponent exceeds minus one. Outside the support the bound holds automatically.
