For nonnegative measurable functions $f_n$, which sequence is integrated before taking the limit in the proof of Fatou's lemma?

- $h_n=\inf_{1\le k\le n}f_k$
- $h_n=\sup_{1\le k\le n}f_k$
- $h_n=\inf_{k\ge n}f_k$
- $h_n=\sup_{k\ge n}f_k$

## Explanation

Removing the first term of a tail cannot decrease its infimum. The tail infima are nonnegative and increase to the limit inferior, so monotone convergence applies before comparing the integrals.
