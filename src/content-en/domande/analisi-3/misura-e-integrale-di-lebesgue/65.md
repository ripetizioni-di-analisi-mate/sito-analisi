On $[0,1]$, let $f_n(x)=\dfrac{\sin(\frac{x}{n})}{\frac1n}$. What is $\lim_n\int_0^1 f_n\,dx$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $\dfrac13$
- $1$

## Explanation

The standard limit of the sine divided by its argument gives the pointwise limit $x$. The inequality $\lvert\sin u\rvert\le\lvert u\rvert$ gives the common majorant $x$, integrable on $[0,1]$. Dominated convergence therefore permits integration of the limiting function.
