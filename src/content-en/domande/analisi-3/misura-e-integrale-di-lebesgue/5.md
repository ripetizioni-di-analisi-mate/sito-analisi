Compute $\int_0^1(x^{5}+7\mathbf1_{\mathbb Q}(x))\,dx$.

- $\frac1{6}$
- $7+\frac1{6}$
- $\frac1{7}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The rationals are countable and each singleton has measure zero, so the added indicator vanishes almost everywhere. The integrand is measurable and bounded on the domain, so its integral equals that of $x^5$.

$$
m(\mathbb Q\cap[0,1])=0,\qquad \int_0^1f\,dx=\int_0^1x^{5}dx=\frac1{6}.
$$
