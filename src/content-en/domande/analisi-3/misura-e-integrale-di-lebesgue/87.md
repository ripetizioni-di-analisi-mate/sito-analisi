Let $H(t)=\int_0^\infty e^{-tx}\,dx$ for $t>0$. Which conclusion holds when $t\downarrow0$?

- $H(t)\to0$.
- The limit oscillates between zero and one.
- $H(t)\to+\infty$.
- $H(t)\to1$.

## Explanation

Evaluating the antiderivative on the half-line gives the reciprocal of the positive parameter. This reciprocal grows without bound as the parameter approaches zero from the right. The limiting integrand is the constant one and also has infinite integral.
