Let $f_n=\mathbf1_{[0,n]}$ on $\mathbb R$. What is $\int_{\mathbb R}\lim_n f_n\,dm$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $+\infty$
- $0$

## Explanation

The supports increase to cover the closed half-line starting at zero. Thus the limit is its indicator. The functions are measurable and nonnegative, so monotone convergence gives the limit of the lengths $n$ as the integral.
