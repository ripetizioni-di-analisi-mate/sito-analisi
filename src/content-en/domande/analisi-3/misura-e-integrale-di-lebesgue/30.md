On $(0,1)$, set $s(x)=\dfrac{\lfloor8x\rfloor}{8}$. What is $\sup_{0<x<1}(x-s(x))$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac78$
- $1$

## Explanation

On each step interval, the difference is the distance from the left endpoint. It stays below one eighth but approaches this value arbitrarily closely before the right endpoint. Its supremum is therefore one eighth.
