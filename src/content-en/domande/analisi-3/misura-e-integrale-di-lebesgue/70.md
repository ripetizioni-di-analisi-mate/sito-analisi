Suppose $f_n\to f$ almost everywhere, $\lvert f_n\rvert\le g\in L^1$, and $A$ is measurable. What limit is guaranteed for $\int_A\lvert f_n-f\rvert\,d\mu$?

- It necessarily depends on whether $\mu(A)$ is finite.
- $0$
- $\int_A\lvert f\rvert\,d\mu$
- $\int_A g\,d\mu$

## Explanation

The absolute difference is bounded by $2g$ almost everywhere and tends to zero. The restriction of the majorant to $A$ is integrable regardless of the measure of $A$. Dominated convergence therefore gives a limit of zero.
