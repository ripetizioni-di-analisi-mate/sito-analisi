Compute $\int_0^1\int_0^x 5x^{11}\,dy\,dx$.

- $\dfrac{5}{14}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{13}$
- $\dfrac{5}{11}$

## Explanation

For each $x$, the inner integral runs over an interval of length $x$. The integrand is constant with respect to $y$, so the inner integral is $5x^{12}$. The antiderivative in $x$ is $\dfrac{5x^{13}}{13}$, which gives the indicated value when evaluated between zero and one.
