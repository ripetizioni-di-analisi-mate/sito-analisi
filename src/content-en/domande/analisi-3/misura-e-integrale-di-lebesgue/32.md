Let $f_n$ be real-valued measurable functions and let $g(x)=\sup_n f_n(x)$. Which identity proves that $g$ is measurable?

- $\{g>t\}=\bigcup_n\{f_n<t\}$
- $\{g>t\}=\bigcap_n\{f_n\le t\}$
- $\{g>t\}=\bigcap_n\{f_n>t\}$
- $\{g>t\}=\bigcup_n\{f_n>t\}$

## Explanation

The supremum exceeds a threshold precisely when some term exceeds it. The level set is therefore a countable union of measurable sets, and the sigma-algebra is closed under such unions.
