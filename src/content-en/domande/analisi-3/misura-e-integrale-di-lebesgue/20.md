Let $N$ have Lebesgue outer measure zero and let $A$ be one of its subsets. Which conclusion holds for every such $A$?

- $A$ must be a Borel set.
- $A$ must be countable.
- $A$ is measurable only if it is closed.
- $A$ is measurable and $m(A)=0$.

## Explanation

By monotonicity the outer measure of $A$ is at most that of $N$ and is therefore zero. Completeness of Lebesgue measure ensures that subsets of null sets are measurable irrespective of their topological structure.
