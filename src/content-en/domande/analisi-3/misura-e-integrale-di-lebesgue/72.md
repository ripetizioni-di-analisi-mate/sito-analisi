Compute $\int_{[0,1]\times[0,2]}(x+y)\,dx\,dy$.

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac32$
- $3$

## Explanation

The function is measurable and nonnegative on the rectangle, so either iterated integral may be used. The term $x$ is multiplied by the length two of the interval in $y$, and $y$ by the length one in the other direction. The contributions are one and two.
