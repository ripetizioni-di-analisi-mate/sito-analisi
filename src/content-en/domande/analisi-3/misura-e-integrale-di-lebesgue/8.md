In a finite measure space $\mu(X)=9$, two measurable sets satisfy $\mu(A)=6$, $\mu(B)=4$, and $\mu(A\cap B)=3$. What is the measure of $X\setminus(A\cup B)$?

- $7$
- $3$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

By additivity the measure of the union is the sum of the measures minus their overlap, which that sum counts twice. The union has measure seven and its complement has measure nine minus seven.
