If $\int f^+\,dm=+\infty$ and $\int f^-\,dm=3$, what is $\int f\,dm$?

- $3$
- $-\infty$
- $+\infty$
- It is undefined.

## Explanation

The signed integral exists because at least one part has finite integral. The positive part has infinite integral and the negative part has finite integral, so their difference is positive infinity.
