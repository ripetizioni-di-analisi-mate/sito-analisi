Using $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, find the transform of $f(x)=e^{-5|x|}$.

- $\dfrac{10}{25+\xi^2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{25+\xi^2}$
- $\dfrac{10}{25-\xi^2}$
- $\dfrac{10}{25+\xi}$

## Explanation

Split the integral at zero because the absolute value has different formulas on the two half-lines. On the negative half-line change the sign of the variable. The two antiderivatives give conjugate reciprocals, whose sum is real and even.
