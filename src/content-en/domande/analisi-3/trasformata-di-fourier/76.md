Let $H=\mathbf1_{(0,\infty)}$. What is the distributional derivative of $3H(x+2)-H(x-1)$?

- $3\delta_2-\delta_{-1}$
- $3\delta_{-2}+\delta_1$
- $3\delta_0-\delta_1$
- $3\delta_{-2}-\delta_1$

## Explanation

The first step switches on at minus two with a jump of three. The second switches on at one with a jump of minus one. By linearity the derivative is the sum of the two weighted point masses.
