Let $F=\widehat f$ with $f,xf\in L^1$. What is $\widehat{xf}(\xi)$?

- $iF'(\xi)$
- $-iF'(\xi)$
- $i\xi F(\xi)$
- $\xi F'(\xi)$

## Explanation

The frequency derivative acts on the kernel, giving the factor $-ix$. The product $xf$ is integrable, justifying differentiation under the integral. Multiplying by $i$ gives the required formula.
