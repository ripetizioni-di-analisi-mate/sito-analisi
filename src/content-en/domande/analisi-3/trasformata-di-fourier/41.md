With $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, compute $\widehat f(0)$ for $f(x)=3\mathbf1_{[-1,7]}(x)$.

- $24$
- $18$
- $21$
- $8$

## Explanation

At zero frequency the kernel is one. The function equals three on the interval from minus one to seven, of length eight. Integrating this constant height gives twenty-four.
