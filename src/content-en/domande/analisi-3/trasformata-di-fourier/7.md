Find the transform of $f(x)=e^{-5x^2}$.

- $\sqrt{\dfrac\pi{5}}e^{-\dfrac{\xi^2}{5}}$
- $2\sqrt{\dfrac\pi{5}}e^{-\dfrac{\xi^2}{20}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac\pi{5}}e^{-\dfrac{\xi^2}{20}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac\pi{5}}e^{\dfrac{\xi^2}{20}}$

## Explanation

Apply the Gaussian formula in the angular frequency convention. The parameter five gives the factor $\sqrt{\dfrac{\pi}{5}}$ and denominator twenty in the exponent. At zero the transform equals the integral of the original Gaussian.
