If $f,g$ are even and integrable, what property does $f*g$ have?

- It is even almost everywhere
- It is odd almost everywhere
- It is constant almost everywhere
- It is zero almost everywhere

## Explanation

To compute the convolution at $-x$, replace the integration variable $y$ by $-y$. Since both factors are even, the integrand becomes the one at $x$. Hence the integrals are equal.
