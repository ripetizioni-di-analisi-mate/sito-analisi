Let $f\in L^1$, and suppose the support of $\widehat f$ is exactly $[-1,1]$. What is the support of the transform of $e^{4ix}f(x)$?

- In $[-5,-3]$
- In $[-1,1]$
- In $[-4,4]$
- In $[3,5]$

## Explanation

Modulation by the exponential of frequency four evaluates the transform at $\xi-4$. For a nonzero value this argument must lie in the original interval, giving the shifted support.
