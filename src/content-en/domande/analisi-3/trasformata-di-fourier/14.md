Let $f\in L^1$ and $\xi_n\to\xi$. Which integrable bound justifies passing to the limit in $f(x)e^{-i\xi_nx}$ under the integral?

- The constant $1$ on the real line
- $|f(x)|$
- $|\xi_n|$ for every $x$
- $|xf(x)|$ even without a moment assumption

## Explanation

For each $x$ the kernel converges to its value at the limiting frequency. Every kernel has modulus one, so the modulus of the original function is a common bound. It is integrable by assumption.
