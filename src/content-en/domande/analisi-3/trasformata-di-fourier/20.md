Solve $u_t=3u_{xx}$ on the real line by Fourier transformation. For all Schwartz initial data $u_0$, which quantity is constant for $t>0$?

- $u(t,0)$.
- $\partial_x u(t,0)$.
- $\|u(t,\cdot)\|_2^2$.
- $\int_{\mathbb R}u(t,x)\,dx$.

## Explanation

The solution's mass is the transform at zero frequency. There the heat multiplier equals one, so the initial zero-frequency value is preserved at every positive time.
