With $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, give the transform of $g(x)=e^{i3x}f(x-7)$, assuming $\widehat f=F$.

- $e^{-i7(\xi+3)}F(\xi+3)$
- $e^{-i7(\xi-3)}F(\xi-3)$
- $e^{i7(\xi-3)}F(\xi-3)$
- $e^{-i7\xi}F(\xi-3)$

## Explanation

Spatial translation introduces a multiplicative phase factor. Modulation then shifts the frequency throughout the transform of the translated function. Thus both the argument of $F$ and the frequency in the phase become $\xi-3$.
