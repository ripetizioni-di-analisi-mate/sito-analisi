What is the distributional transform of the constant function $1$?

- $\delta_0$
- $2\pi\delta_0$
- $1$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

By duality the transform of the constant integrates the Fourier transform of the test function over the real line. Inversion of the test function at zero shows that this integral is $2\pi$ times its value at zero.
