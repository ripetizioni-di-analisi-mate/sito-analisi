Let $f=\mathbf1_{[0,1]}$ and $g=\mathbf1_{[0,3]}$. What is the full set of points satisfying $(f*g)(x)=1$?

- $[0,1]$
- $[2,4]$
- $[0,4]$
- $[1,3]$

## Explanation

The moving interval $[x-1,x]$ has length one, so its overlap has length one exactly when the entire interval lies in $[0,3]$. Its endpoint conditions give $x\ge1$ and $x\le3$. Outside their common interval the overlap is smaller.
