Which distribution is the derivative of $\mathbf1_{[1,4]}$?

- $\delta_4-\delta_1$
- $\delta_1+\delta_4$
- $\delta_1-\delta_4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Integrate the derivative of the test function over $[1,4]$ with a minus sign. The result is its value at one minus its value at four, matching the two signed jumps of the indicator.
