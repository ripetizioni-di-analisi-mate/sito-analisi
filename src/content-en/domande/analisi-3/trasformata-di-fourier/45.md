Let $f\in L^1$ and $F=\widehat f$. What is the transform of $f(3x-6)$?

- $\dfrac13 e^{-2i\xi}F\left(\dfrac\xi3\right)$
- $\dfrac13 e^{-6i\xi}F\left(\dfrac\xi3\right)$
- $3e^{-2i\xi}F(3\xi)$
- $\dfrac13F\left(\dfrac{\xi-6}3\right)$

## Explanation

Factor the argument as $3(x-2)$. The scaled function is then translated two units and acquires the corresponding phase factor. Scaling gives a factor of one third both in the coefficient and in the argument.
