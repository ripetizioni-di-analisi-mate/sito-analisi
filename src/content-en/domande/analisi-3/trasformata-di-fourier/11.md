For $g=\mathbf1_{[0,5]}$, compute $(g*g)(5)$.

- $10$
- $15$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

For the product of the indicators to equal one, both $y$ and $5-y$ must lie in the interval. At the specified point the two restrictions coincide. Integrating one over the common interval gives five.
