What is $\widehat{\delta_{-3}}(\xi)$?

- $e^{3i\xi}$
- $e^{-3i\xi}$
- $1$
- $3i\xi$

## Explanation

The delta evaluates the kernel at its center. Substituting minus three for the spatial variable reverses the sign in the kernel and gives the exponent $3i\xi$.
