Let $T=\delta_2'$. What is $\langle T,\varphi\rangle$ for a test function $\varphi$?

- $\varphi'(2)$
- $-\varphi(2)$
- $-\varphi'(2)$
- $2\varphi'(0)$

## Explanation

By definition differentiation moves onto the test function with a minus sign. This gives the action of $\delta_2$ on the derivative of $\varphi$, which evaluates that derivative at two.
