What is $\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R}\dfrac1{(1+\xi^2)^2}\,d\xi$, using Plancherel's theorem on $e^{-|x|}$?

- $\pi$
- $\dfrac\pi2$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The squared norm of the original function is the integral of $e^{-2|x|}$ and equals one. The transform is two divided by $1+\xi^2$, so its square contains a factor of four, while Plancherel's theorem gives energy $2\pi$.
