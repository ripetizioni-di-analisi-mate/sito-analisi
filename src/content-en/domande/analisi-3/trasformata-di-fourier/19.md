Using $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, find the transform of $f(x)=x e^{-4x}\mathbf1_{[0,\infty)}(x)$.

- $\dfrac{1}{\left(- i \xi + 4\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\left(i \xi + 4\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\left(i \xi + 4\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{i \xi + 4}$

## Explanation

To handle $x$, integrate by parts on the positive half-line. The boundary term vanishes at zero because of this factor and at infinity by exponential decay. The remaining exponential integral gives a second reciprocal factor of $4+i\xi$.
