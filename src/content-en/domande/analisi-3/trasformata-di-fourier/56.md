If $f$ is a Schwartz function and $F=\widehat f$, what is $\widehat{(xf)'}(\xi)$?

- $-F(\xi)-\xi F'(\xi)$
- $i\xi F'(\xi)$
- $\xi F'(\xi)$
- $-\xi F'(\xi)$

## Explanation

First transform the product $xf$ to obtain $iF'$. Differentiating the product in space then gives the multiplier $i\xi$. The two imaginary factors give the minus sign.
