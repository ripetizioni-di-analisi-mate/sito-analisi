Which symmetric interval indicator has a transform with value six at zero and first positive zero at $\dfrac\pi3$?

- $\mathbf1_{[-6,6]}$
- $\mathbf1_{[-2,2]}$
- $\mathbf1_{[-3,3]}$
- $\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}$

## Explanation

The transform at zero is the length of the symmetric interval. A length of six gives half-length three. The numerator at nonzero frequencies is $2\sin(3\xi)$, giving the specified first zero as well.
