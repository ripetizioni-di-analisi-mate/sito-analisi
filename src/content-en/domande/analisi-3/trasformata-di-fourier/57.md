With $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, express the transform of $\delta_{2}^\prime$.

- $i\xi e^{-i2\xi}$
- $e^{-i2\xi}$
- $i\xi e^{i2\xi}$
- $-i\xi e^{-i2\xi}$

## Explanation

The shifted delta evaluates the kernel at two, giving the phase $e^{-2i\xi}$. Distributional differentiation introduces the frequency factor $i\xi$. The product gives the required transform.
