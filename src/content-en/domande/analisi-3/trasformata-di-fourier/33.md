Compare the transforms of $e^{-|x|}$ and $e^{-x^2}$ as $|\xi|\to\infty$.

- The Gaussian transform decays faster
- The transform of the two-sided exponential decays faster
- Both transforms have tails proportional to $\xi^{-2}$
- Both transforms have constant tails

## Explanation

The two-sided exponential has a transform with a quadratic denominator and hence inverse-square decay. The Gaussian transform contains a negative quadratic exponential and decays faster than any inverse power.
