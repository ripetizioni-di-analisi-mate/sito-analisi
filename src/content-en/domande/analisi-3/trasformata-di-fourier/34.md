Let $G=\widehat{e^{-x^2}}$. Which condition determines the constant in the solution of $G'=-\dfrac{\xi}{2}G$?

- $G'(0)=\sqrt\pi$
- $G(0)=\sqrt\pi$
- $G(0)=\pi$
- $G(0)=0$

## Explanation

The differential equation has solution a constant times $e^{-\dfrac{\xi^2}{4}}$. At zero frequency the exponential factor is one, and the transform equals the Gaussian integral over the real line.
