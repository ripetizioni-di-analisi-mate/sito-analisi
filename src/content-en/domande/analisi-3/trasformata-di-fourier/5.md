For $f(x)=e^{-|x-5|}$, find $\widehat f(\xi)$.

- $e^{-i5\xi}\dfrac2{1+\xi^2}$
- $e^{i5\xi}\dfrac2{1+\xi^2}$
- $\dfrac2{1+(\xi-5)^2}$
- $\dfrac2{1+\xi^2}$

## Explanation

The function is a two-sided exponential translated five units. Substitution leaves the integral in the same form and introduces the phase factor $e^{-5i\xi}$. The modulus remains that of the transform of the exponential centered at the origin.
