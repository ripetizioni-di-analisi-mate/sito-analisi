Let $f=\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}$. What is $\widehat f(\dfrac\pi2)$?

- $\dfrac2\pi$
- $2$
- $0$
- $\dfrac4\pi$

## Explanation

The indicator is even and has half-length one. At nonzero frequencies its transform is twice the sine of the frequency divided by the frequency. At $\dfrac\pi2$ the sine equals one.
