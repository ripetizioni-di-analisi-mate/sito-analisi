With the transform in $L^2$ and inversion factor $\dfrac1{2\pi}$, find $f(0)$ if $f$ is continuous and $\widehat f=2\mathbf1_{[-5,5]}$.

- $10$
- $\dfrac{10}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{5}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{20}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The stated transform is integrable, so inversion gives the value of the continuous representative. At zero the inverse kernel is one, leaving the rectangle's area of twenty. Dividing by $2\pi$ gives $\dfrac{10}{\pi}$.
