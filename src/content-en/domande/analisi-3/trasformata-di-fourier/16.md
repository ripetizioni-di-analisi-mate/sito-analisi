Using $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, find the transform of $f(x)=e^{i11x}e^{-5|x|}$.

- $\dfrac{10}{\left(- \xi - 11\right)^{2} + 25}$
- $\dfrac{5}{\left(\xi - 11\right)^{2} + 25}$
- $\dfrac{10}{\xi^{2} + 25}$
- $\dfrac{10}{\left(\xi - 11\right)^{2} + 25}$

## Explanation

Modulation replaces the frequency by $\xi-11$ throughout the transform. The two-sided exponential with parameter five has a rational transform with numerator ten. After shifting, the denominator is the squared shifted frequency plus twenty-five.
