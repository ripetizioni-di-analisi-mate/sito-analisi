If $u_0$ is an odd Schwartz initial profile, what relation holds for the heat equation solution on the line obtained by convolution with the Gaussian kernel?

- $u(t,-x)=u(t,x)$
- $u(t,-x)=-u(t,x)$
- $u(t,x)=0$ for every $x$
- $u(t,0)$ is strictly positive

## Explanation

The heat kernel is even. In the convolution at $-x$, negating the integration variable preserves the kernel factor and reverses the sign of the initial profile. Thus the solution remains odd.
