Let $F=\widehat f$ and $f\in L^1$. What is the transform of $f(x-a)-f(x+a)$?

- $2i\sin(a\xi)F(\xi)$
- $2\cos(a\xi)F(\xi)$
- $-2i\sin(a\xi)F(\xi)$
- $F(\xi-a)-F(\xi+a)$

## Explanation

The two translations introduce opposite phases. After factoring out $F$, subtract the positive-exponent exponential from the negative-exponent one. The difference is minus two times $i$ times the sine.
