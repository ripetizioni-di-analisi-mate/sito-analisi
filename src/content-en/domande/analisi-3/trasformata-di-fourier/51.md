If $f$ is a Schwartz function and $F=\widehat f$, what is the transform of $xf'(x)$?

- $-\xi F'(\xi)$
- $F(\xi)+\xi F'(\xi)$
- $-F(\xi)-\xi F'(\xi)$
- $i\xi F'(\xi)$

## Explanation

First transform the derivative to obtain $i\xi F$. Multiplication of the differentiated function by $x$ then requires differentiating this product in frequency and multiplying by $i$. The derivative acts on both $\xi$ and $F$.
