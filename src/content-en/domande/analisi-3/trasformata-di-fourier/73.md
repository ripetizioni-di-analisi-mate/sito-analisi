If $f\in L^1\cap L^2$, how are the integral transform and the Plancherel extension related?

- They agree almost everywhere
- They differ by a factor of $2\pi$
- They agree only at zero frequency
- They differ whenever $f$ has unbounded support

## Explanation

The function has Schwartz approximations converging in both norms. Their transforms converge uniformly to the integral transform and in $L^2$ to the extension. On every bounded interval the two limits agree in $L^2$.
