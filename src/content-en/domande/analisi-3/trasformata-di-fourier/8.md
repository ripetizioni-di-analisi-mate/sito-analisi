A function $f\in L^1$ vanishes outside $[-2,2]$ and satisfies $\|f\|_1=1$. Which estimate holds for every $\xi,h\in\mathbb R$?

- $|\widehat f(\xi+h)-\widehat f(\xi)|=2|h|$
- $|\widehat f(\xi+h)-\widehat f(\xi)|\le2|h|^2$
- $|\widehat f(\xi+h)-\widehat f(\xi)|\ge2|h|$
- $|\widehat f(\xi+h)-\widehat f(\xi)|\le2|h|$

## Explanation

Factor out the kernel at the original frequency and use the inequality $|e^{-ihx}-1|\le |hx|$. On the support, $|x|\le2$. Integrating the upper bound gives twice $|h|$, independently of the original frequency.
