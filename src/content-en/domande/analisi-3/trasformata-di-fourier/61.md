Let $f,g\in L^1$, with $\widehat f(2)=0$. What follows for the transform of $f*g$?

- $\widehat{f*g}(2)=0$
- $\widehat{f*g}(2)=\widehat g(2)$
- $f*g$ vanishes at $x=2$
- $\widehat{f*g}(\xi)$ is zero for every $\xi$

## Explanation

Both factors are integrable, so the convolution theorem applies. At frequency two we multiply the two transform values, and the zero in one factor makes the product zero.
