Let $f$ belong to $L^2$ and suppose $\|\widehat f\|_2=4\sqrt\pi$. What is $\|f\|_2$?

- $8$
- $4\sqrt2$
- $2\sqrt2$
- $2$

## Explanation

Plancherel's theorem scales the norms themselves by $\sqrt{2\pi}$. Divide the given norm by that square root. The factor $\sqrt\pi$ cancels, leaving four divided by the square root of two.
