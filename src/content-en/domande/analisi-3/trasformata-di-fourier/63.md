For Schwartz functions $f,g$, which formula gives the transform of the pointwise product $f(x)g(x)$?

- $\widehat f\,\widehat g$
- $2\pi\widehat f*\widehat g$
- $\dfrac1{2\pi}\widehat f*\widehat g$
- $\dfrac1{2\pi}\widehat f\,\widehat g$

## Explanation

Insert the inverse transform of one factor and interchange the integrals, which is justified for Schwartz functions. The inversion factor is the reciprocal of $2\pi$, and the remaining integral is a frequency-domain convolution.
