Let $g=\mathbf1_{[0,2]}$. What is $(g*g)(\dfrac72)$?

- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The moving interval is $[\dfrac32,\dfrac72]$. Its intersection with $[0,2]$ runs from three halves to two. The two indicators have product one there, so the integral equals the intersection length.
