For $u_t=2u_{xx}$, what differential equation does $\widehat u(t,3)$ satisfy?

- $\partial_t\widehat u(t,3)=18\widehat u(t,3)$
- $\partial_t\widehat u(t,3)=-6\widehat u(t,3)$
- $\partial_t\widehat u(t,3)=-18\widehat u(t,3)$
- $\partial_t\widehat u(t,3)=-9\widehat u(t,3)$

## Explanation

The second spatial derivative gives the multiplier $-\xi^2$. The diffusivity two multiplies this factor. At frequency three the result is minus two times nine, or minus eighteen.
