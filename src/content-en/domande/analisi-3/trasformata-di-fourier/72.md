With this chapter's convention, which positive coefficient multiplying $\widehat f$ makes the transform unitary on $L^2$?

- $\dfrac1{2\pi}$
- $\sqrt{2\pi}$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}$

## Explanation

The squared norm of $\widehat f$ is $2\pi$ times that of $f$. For equal norms, multiply the transform by a positive coefficient whose square is the reciprocal of $2\pi$.
