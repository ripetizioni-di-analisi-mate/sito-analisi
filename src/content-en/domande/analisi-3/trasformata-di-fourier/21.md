With $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, find the multiplier of $\widehat u_0$ in the solution of $u_t=3u_{xx}$ at time $t=7$.

- $e^{- 21 \xi^{2}}$
- $e^{- 21 \xi}$
- $e^{21 \xi^{2}}$
- $e^{- \dfrac{7 \xi^{2}}{3}}$

## Explanation

Transforming the second spatial derivative gives an ordinary differential equation in time with coefficient $-3\xi^2$. Its solution multiplies the initial transform by the exponential of that coefficient times time. Substituting seven gives exponent $-21\xi^2$.
