What is the transform of $\delta_0''$?

- $\xi^2$
- $-\xi^2$
- $i\xi$
- $-\xi^2\delta_0$

## Explanation

The transform of the delta at the origin is the constant one. Two spatial derivatives multiply it by $(i\xi)^2$. Since the square of $i$ is minus one, the multiplier is negative real away from zero.
