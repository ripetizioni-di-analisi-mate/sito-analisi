If $f\in L^1$ and $a\in\mathbb R$, which quantity stays the same at every frequency when $f(x)$ is replaced by $f(x-a)$?

- $\operatorname{Re}\widehat f(\xi)$
- $|\widehat f(\xi)|$
- $\operatorname{Im}\widehat f(\xi)$
- The phase of $\widehat f(\xi)$ wherever it is defined

## Explanation

Substitution gives the factor $e^{-ia\xi}$. For real $a$ and $\xi$ this factor has modulus one. The product therefore has the original modulus even when its real and imaginary parts change.
