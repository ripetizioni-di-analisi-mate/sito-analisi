Let $f=\mathbf1_{[-1,2]}$ and $g=\mathbf1_{[1,4]}$. What is the support of $f*g$?

- $[-1,4]$
- $[1,2]$
- $[0,6]$
- $[-2,3]$

## Explanation

The convolution is positive exactly when the moving and fixed intervals overlap in a set of positive length. This occurs between the sum of the left endpoints and the sum of the right endpoints of the two supports. The support is the closure of that interval.
