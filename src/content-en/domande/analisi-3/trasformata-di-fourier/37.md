Compute $\widehat{\max(1-|x|,0)}(0)$ without evaluating an oscillatory integral.

- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

At zero frequency the kernel is one, leaving the area under the triangle. The base is two and the height one. The area is half their product.
