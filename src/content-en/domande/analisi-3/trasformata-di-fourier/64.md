What is the maximum of the convolution $\mathbf1_{[0,2]}*\mathbf1_{[0,5]}$?

- $5$
- $7$
- $10$
- $2$

## Explanation

The convolution is the overlap length of two intervals, at most the length of the shorter one. Translating the shorter inside the longer makes the overlap the entire shorter interval, attaining the upper bound.
