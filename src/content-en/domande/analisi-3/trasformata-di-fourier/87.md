Let $\kappa>0$. Which frequency does the heat multiplier $e^{-\kappa t\xi^2}$ attenuate most for $t>0$?

- $\xi=2$
- $\xi=1$
- $\xi=3$
- $\xi=0$

## Explanation

At positive time the multiplier decreases as the squared frequency increases. The largest square among the choices is nine, so the corresponding exponential is the smallest.
