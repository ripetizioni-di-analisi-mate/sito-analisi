For $f=\mathbf1_{[0,4]}$ and $g=\mathbf1_{[0,9]}$, what is $(f*g)(12)$?

- $9$
- $4$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

The factor $g$ restricts the integration variable to the interval from zero to nine. At $x=12$ the first factor requires the interval from eight to twelve. The intersection runs from eight to nine and has length one.
