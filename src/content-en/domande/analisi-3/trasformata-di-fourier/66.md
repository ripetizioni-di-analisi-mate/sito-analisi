For $a>0$, set $f_a(x)=a f(ax)$ and $g_a(x)=a g(ax)$, with $f,g\in L^1$. Which formula holds?

- $(f_a*g_a)(x)=a^2(f*g)(ax)$
- $(f_a*g_a)(x)=a(f*g)(ax)$
- $(f_a*g_a)(x)=(f*g)(ax)$
- $(f_a*g_a)(x)=a(f*g)\left(\dfrac xa\right)$

## Explanation

The two factors multiply the integrand by $a^2$. Under the substitution $u=ay$ the differential gives the reciprocal of $a$. This leaves $a$ outside the integral, and the first argument of the convolution becomes $ax-u$.
