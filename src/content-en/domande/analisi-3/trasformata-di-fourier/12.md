For a real function $f\in L^1$, how are the transform values at opposite frequencies related?

- $\widehat f(-\xi)=-\widehat f(\xi)$
- $\widehat f(-\xi)=\widehat f(\xi)$ for every real input function
- $\widehat f(-\xi)=-\overline{\widehat f(\xi)}$
- $\widehat f(-\xi)=\overline{\widehat f(\xi)}$

## Explanation

Conjugating the integral leaves the original function unchanged because it is real. The kernel becomes $e^{i\xi x}$, the kernel at the opposite frequency. Thus the real part is even and the imaginary part odd.
