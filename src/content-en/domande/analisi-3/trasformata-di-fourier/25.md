For $f=\mathbf1_{[-2,2]}$, what is the first strictly positive zero of $\widehat f$?

- $\dfrac\pi2$
- $\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

At nonzero frequencies the numerator of the transform is $2\sin(2\xi)$. The first positive zero occurs when the sine argument equals $\pi$. At zero the integral equals four.
