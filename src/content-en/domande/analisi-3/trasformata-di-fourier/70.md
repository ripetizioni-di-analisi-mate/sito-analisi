If $f,g$ are Schwartz functions, what factor relates $\int\widehat f\,\overline{\widehat g}$ to $\int f\overline g$?

- $\dfrac1{2\pi}$
- $2\pi$
- $1$
- $\sqrt{2\pi}$

## Explanation

In the inner product identity, conjugate the inversion formula for $g$. The inversion factor remains outside the frequency integral. Multiplying both sides by $2\pi$ gives the required factor.
