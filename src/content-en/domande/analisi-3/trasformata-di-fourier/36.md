Using $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, find the transform of $f(x)=x e^{-3|x|}$.

- $- \dfrac{12 \xi}{\left(\xi^{2} + 9\right)^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{6 i \xi}{\left(\xi^{2} + 9\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{12 i \xi}{\left(\xi^{2} + 9\right)^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{12 i \xi}{\left(\xi^{2} + 9\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

Multiplication by $x$ corresponds to $i$ times the derivative of the transform. The transform of the two-sided exponential has numerator six. Differentiating the reciprocal introduces the factor $-2\xi$ and squares the denominator.
