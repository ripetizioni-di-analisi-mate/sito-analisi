For a Schwartz function $f$ with $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,\mathrm dx$, the transform $\widehat{xf}$ vanishes on an open interval $J$. What property does $\widehat f$ have on $J$?

- It must vanish on the interval.
- Its second derivative is identically one.
- Its modulus must increase strictly.
- It is constant on the interval.

## Explanation

Differentiation under the integral gives $\dfrac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm d\xi}\widehat f(\xi)=-i\widehat{xf}(\xi)$, where the left side denotes the derivative of the transform. This derivative vanishes on $J$. Determining the constant requires its value at one point.
