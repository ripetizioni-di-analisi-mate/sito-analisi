Find $\widehat{\mathbf1_{[-5,5]}}(\xi)$ for $\xi\ne0$ using the convention adopted in this chapter.

- $\dfrac{\sin(5\xi)}{\xi}$
- $\dfrac{2\cos(5\xi)}{\xi}$
- $\dfrac{2\sin(5\xi)}{\xi}$
- $-\dfrac{2\sin(5\xi)}{\xi}$

## Explanation

The indicator restricts the integral to the symmetric interval. Evaluate the exponential antiderivative at both endpoints and use Euler's formula. At zero the transform extends with value ten, the length of the interval.
