Which limit does the Riemann–Lebesgue lemma give for every $f\in L^1$?

- $\lim_{|\xi|\to\infty}|\xi|\widehat f(\xi)=0$
- $\lim_{|\xi|\to\infty}\widehat f(\xi)=0$
- $\lim_{\xi\to0}\widehat f(\xi)=0$
- $\lim_{\xi\to+\infty}\widehat f(\xi)=\|f\|_1$

## Explanation

The lemma states that the transform tends to zero as the magnitude of the frequency increases. Integrability alone does not specify the rate. At zero, the transform equals the integral of the original function.
