For $f(x)=3\mathbf1_{[0,1]}(x)$ and $g(x)=7\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}(x)$, what is $\int_{\mathbb R}(f*g)(x)\,dx$?

- $20$
- $10$
- $21$
- $42$

## Explanation

Fubini's theorem applies because both factors are integrable. Substitution in the inner variable gives the product of their integrals for the integral of the convolution. The two rectangles have areas three and fourteen, so the product is forty-two.
