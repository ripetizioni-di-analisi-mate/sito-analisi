Compute $\widehat f(\xi)$ for $f=\mathbf1_{[1,3]}$ and $\xi\ne0$.

- $e^{2i\xi}\dfrac{2\sin\xi}{\xi}$
- $e^{-2i\xi}\dfrac{2\sin\xi}{\xi}$
- $\dfrac{2\sin(2\xi)}{\xi}$
- $e^{-i\xi}\dfrac{\sin\xi}{\xi}$

## Explanation

The interval has center two and half-length one. Translating the indicator of $[-1,1]$ by two units introduces the phase factor $e^{-2i\xi}$. The half-length stays one and determines the argument of the sine.
