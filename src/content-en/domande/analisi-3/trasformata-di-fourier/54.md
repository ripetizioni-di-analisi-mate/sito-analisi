Which expression gives $\widehat{f''+4f}$ if $f$ is a Schwartz function and $F=\widehat f$?

- $(4+\xi^2)F(\xi)$
- $(4-\xi^2)F(\xi)$
- $(4-i\xi^2)F(\xi)$
- $(4i\xi-\xi^2)F(\xi)$

## Explanation

Two spatial derivatives give the square of $i\xi$, the multiplier $-\xi^2$. Linearity adds the contribution $4F$. Both terms therefore have the common factor $F$.
