If $f,g\in L^1$ and $\|f\|_1=2$, with $\|g\|_1=3$, what upper bound is guaranteed for $\|f*g\|_1$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The integral of the modulus of the convolution is bounded by the double integral of the product of the moduli. Substitution separates this integral into the product of the two norms. The product is six, and nonnegative factors can attain equality.
