Let $F=\widehat f$ with $f\in L^1$. Compute the transform of $\overline{f(x)}$.

- $\overline{F(\xi)}$
- $F(-\xi)$
- $\overline{F(-\xi)}$
- $-F(-\xi)$

## Explanation

Conjugate the integral of $f$ at the opposite frequency. Conjugation reverses the sign in the exponent, recovering the sign of the forward kernel, and replaces the original function by its complex conjugate.
