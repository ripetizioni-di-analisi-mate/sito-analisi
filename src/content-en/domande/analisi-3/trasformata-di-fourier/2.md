Let $f$ be a complex-valued integrable function with $\|f\|_1=3$. Which estimate holds at every frequency?

- $|\widehat f(\xi)|=3$
- $|\widehat f(\xi)|\le3$
- $|\widehat f(\xi)|\le\dfrac3{|\xi|+1}$
- $|\widehat f(\xi)|\ge3$

## Explanation

The kernel has modulus one for every real frequency. The triangle inequality bounds the transform by the integral of the modulus of $f$. Integrability makes this uniform bound finite.
