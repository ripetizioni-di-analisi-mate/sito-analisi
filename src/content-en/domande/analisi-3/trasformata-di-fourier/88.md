Which property of the kernel $G_t$ ensures that Schwartz initial data are recovered uniformly as $t\downarrow0$?

- Its maximum remains one
- For every $x$, we have $G_t(x)\to1$
- Its support lies in $[-t,t]$
- It has integral one, and its mass outside every neighborhood of zero tends to zero

## Explanation

In the convolution use uniform continuity of the initial profile near zero. Away from zero the difference is bounded and is integrated against a mass tending to zero. The integral-one condition allows subtraction of the original profile.
