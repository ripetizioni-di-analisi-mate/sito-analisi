Using $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, find the transform of $f(x)=e^{-6x}\mathbf1_{[0,\infty)}(x)$.

- $\dfrac{1}{i \xi + 6}$
- $\dfrac{12}{\xi^{2} + 36}$
- $\dfrac{6}{i \xi + 6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{- i \xi + 6}$

## Explanation

Only the positive half-line contributes. The exponent has real decay rate minus six, so the exponential antiderivative vanishes at infinity. Subtracting its value at zero gives the reciprocal of $6+i\xi$.
