Let $F(\xi)=e^{-2i\xi}\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}(\xi)$ be the $L^2$ transform of a continuous function. What is $f(2)$?

- $\dfrac{\sin2}{2\pi}$
- $2$
- $0$
- $\dfrac1\pi$

## Explanation

The frequency-domain function is integrable, so pointwise inversion applies to the continuous representative. At two the inverse kernel cancels the given phase exactly, leaving the integral of the indicator divided by $2\pi$.
