Let $F=\widehat f$ with $f,xf\in L^1$ and $\int_{\mathbb R}xf(x)\,dx=2$. What is $F'(0)$?

- $2i$
- $2$
- $-2i$
- $0$

## Explanation

Differentiating the kernel with respect to frequency introduces $-ix$. This differentiation is justified by integrability of $xf$. At zero the kernel equals one, leaving the first moment times $-i$.
