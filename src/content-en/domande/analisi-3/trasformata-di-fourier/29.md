An instrument measured $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,\mathrm dx$ for one of the functions $e^{-x}\mathbf1_{[0,\infty)}(x)$ and $e^x\mathbf1_{(-\infty,0]}(x)$. If $\operatorname{Im}\widehat f(1)>0$, which property of the signal can be recovered?

- The signal is supported on the negative half-line.
- The signal is supported on the positive half-line.
- The signal must take negative values.
- The signal is even.

## Explanation

For the first signal the transform is $\dfrac{1}{1+i\xi}$, so its imaginary part is negative at $\xi=1$. Time reversal exchanges the two functions and conjugates the transform value, giving a positive imaginary part.
