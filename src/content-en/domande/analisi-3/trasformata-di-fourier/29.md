Find the transform of $f(x)=e^{2x}\mathbf1_{(-\infty,0]}(x)$.

- $\dfrac1{2-i\xi}$
- $\dfrac1{2+i\xi}$
- $\dfrac2{4+\xi^2}$
- $-\dfrac1{2-i\xi}$

## Explanation

On the negative half-line the integrand is $e^{(2-i\xi)x}$. The coefficient has positive real part, so the antiderivative vanishes as $x$ tends to minus infinity. Its value at zero gives the reciprocal.
