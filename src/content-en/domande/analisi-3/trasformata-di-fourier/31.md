With $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$ and a Schwartz function $f$, express the transform of $f^\prime+5f$ in terms of $F=\widehat f$.

- $(\xi+5)F(\xi)$
- $(i\xi-5)F(\xi)$
- $(i\xi+5)F(\xi)$
- $(-i\xi+5)F(\xi)$

## Explanation

Integration by parts gives the factor $i\xi$ in the derivative's transform, with no boundary term because of rapid decay. By linearity five times $f$ contributes $5F$. Factoring out $F$ gives the result.
