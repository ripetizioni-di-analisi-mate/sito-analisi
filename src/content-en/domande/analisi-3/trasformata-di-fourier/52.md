Which identity holds for $\widehat{f'}(0)$ when $f$ is a Schwartz function?

- $\widehat{f'}(0)=\widehat f(0)$
- $\widehat{f'}(0)=\|f'\|_1$
- $\widehat{f'}(0)=i$
- $\widehat{f'}(0)=0$

## Explanation

At zero frequency the transform of $f'$ equals its integral. By the fundamental theorem of calculus this is the difference of the two limits of $f$ at infinity, both zero by rapid decay.
