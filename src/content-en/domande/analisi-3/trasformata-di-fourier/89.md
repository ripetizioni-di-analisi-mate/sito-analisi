Let $u_0$ be a Schwartz function and suppose $\int_{\mathbb R}xu_0(x)\,dx=3$. What is the first moment of $u(t,\cdot)$, obtained by convolution with the heat kernel on the line?

- $3$
- $0$
- $3e^{-t}$
- $3t$

## Explanation

The first moment is related to the derivative of the transform at zero frequency. The heat multiplier equals one there and its derivative vanishes. The product rule thus preserves the initial derivative.
