For $f\in L^1$ with $F=\widehat f$, what is the transform of $f(x-a)e^{ib(x-a)}$?

- $e^{-ia(\xi-b)}F(\xi-b)$
- $e^{ia\xi}F(\xi+b)$
- $e^{-ib\xi}F(\xi-a)$
- $e^{-ia\xi}F(\xi-b)$

## Explanation

The expression is a translation of the modulated function $e^{ibx}f(x)$. Modulation first gives the transform $F(\xi-b)$. The subsequent translation multiplies this frequency-domain expression by a phase factor evaluated at $\xi$.
