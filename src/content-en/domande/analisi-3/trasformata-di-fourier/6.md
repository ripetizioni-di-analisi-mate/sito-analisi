If $f_n\to f$ in $L^1(\mathbb R)$, what follows for the transforms defined by the integral?

- $\widehat f_n=\widehat f$ from some index onward
- $\widehat f_n\to\widehat f$ uniformly
- $\widehat f_n\to\widehat f$ in $L^1$ in all cases
- $\widehat f_n$ is constant for every $n$

## Explanation

By linearity the difference of the transforms is the transform of the difference. The triangle inequality bounds it uniformly by the $L^1$ norm of $f_n-f$, which tends to zero by assumption.
