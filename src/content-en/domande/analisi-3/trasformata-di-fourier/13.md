For a Schwartz function $f$, express $\widehat{f^{(5)}}(\xi)$ in terms of $\widehat f$.

- $(i\xi)^{5}\widehat f(\xi)$
- $2(i\xi)^{5}\widehat f(\xi)$
- $\dfrac12(i\xi)^{5}\widehat f(\xi)$
- $0$

## Explanation

Schwartz functions and all their derivatives vanish at both ends of the line. Thus each integration by parts has zero boundary term. Five differentiations give five factors of $i\xi$.
