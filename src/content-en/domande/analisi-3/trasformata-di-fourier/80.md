For $g(x)=e^{-|x|}$, which point-mass term occurs in its second distributional derivative?

- $2\delta_0$
- $-\delta_0$
- No point mass
- $-2\delta_0$

## Explanation

The ordinary first derivative has left-hand limit one and right-hand limit minus one. The point-mass coefficient in its distributional derivative is the jump, the right-hand limit minus the left-hand limit.
