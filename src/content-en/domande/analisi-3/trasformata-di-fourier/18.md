Let $f=\mathbf1_{[0,1]}-\mathbf1_{[1,2]}$. What is $\widehat f(0)$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

At zero frequency the kernel is one. The two indicators have equal integrals because their intervals have equal length, so their opposite contributions cancel.
