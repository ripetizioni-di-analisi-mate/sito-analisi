Solve $u_t=u_{xx}$ on the line with initial data $u_0(x)=e^{-x^2}$. What is $u(1,0)$?

- $\dfrac1{\sqrt5}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $1$
- $e^{-1}$

## Explanation

In frequency the Gaussian parameter increases by the elapsed time. Inverting gives amplitude at $x=0$ equal to the reciprocal of the square root of $1+4t$. Setting time equal to one gives the reciprocal of the square root of five.
