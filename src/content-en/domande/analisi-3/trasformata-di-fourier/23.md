What is necessary for a continuous function $F$ to be the Fourier transform of a function in $L^1(\mathbb R)$?

- $F$ must belong to $L^1$
- $F$ must be real
- $F$ must tend to zero at infinity
- $F$ must have compact support

## Explanation

The Riemann–Lebesgue lemma gives decay to zero at infinity for every transform of an integrable function. For example, an interval indicator has a transform proportional to a sine divided by frequency and may have unbounded frequency support.
