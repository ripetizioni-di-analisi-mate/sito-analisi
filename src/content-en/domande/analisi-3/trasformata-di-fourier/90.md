For $u_t=\kappa u_{xx}$ with $\kappa>0$ and nonnegative Schwartz initial data, which property holds at every $t>0$ for the solution $u$ obtained with the heat kernel?

- It alternates in sign as $x$ varies
- It is nonnegative for every $x$
- It is always equal to the initial profile
- It is nonpositive for every $x$

## Explanation

The solution is the convolution of the initial profile with the Gaussian heat kernel. The kernel is positive at every point. The original profile is nonnegative, so the integrand and its integral are nonnegative.
