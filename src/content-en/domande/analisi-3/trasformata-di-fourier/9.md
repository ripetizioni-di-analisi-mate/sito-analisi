Let $f\in L^2(\mathbb R)$ with $\|f\|_2^2=5$. Find $\|\widehat f\|_2^2$ using the Plancherel extension and the convention adopted in this chapter.

- $10\pi$
- $5$
- $5\pi$
- $\dfrac{5}{2\pi}$

## Explanation

With this convention the squared frequency-domain norm is $2\pi$ times the squared spatial norm. The hypothesis $f\in L^2$ allows use of Plancherel's theorem for the extended transform. Substitution of the given value five yields $10\pi$.
