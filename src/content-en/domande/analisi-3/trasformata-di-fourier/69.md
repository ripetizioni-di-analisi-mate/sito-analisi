If $f\in L^1$ and $\widehat f\in L^1$, which formula gives $f(x)$ at a point of continuity of the chosen representative?

- $\dfrac1{2\pi}\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R}\widehat f(\xi)e^{i\xi x}\,d\xi$
- $\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R}\widehat f(\xi)e^{i\xi x}\,d\xi$
- $\dfrac1{2\pi}\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R}\widehat f(\xi)e^{-i\xi x}\,d\xi$
- $\dfrac1{2\pi}\widehat f(x)$

## Explanation

The forward transform uses a negative exponent and no prefactor. Inversion uses the positive exponent and the reciprocal of $2\pi$. Integrability of the transform gives absolute convergence of the inverse integral.
