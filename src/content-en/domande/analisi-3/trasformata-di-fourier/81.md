What is $x\delta_2$ as a distribution?

- $2\delta_2$
- $\delta_2'$
- $0$
- $2\delta_0$

## Explanation

Multiplication of a distribution by the smooth function $x$ means applying the distribution to the test function times $x$. The delta at two evaluates this product at two, giving twice the original evaluation.
