Let $G_t(x)=\dfrac1{\sqrt{4\pi\kappa t}}e^{-\dfrac{x^2}{4\kappa t}}$ with $\kappa,t>0$. What is $\int_{\mathbb R}G_t(x)\,dx$?

- $\sqrt{4\pi\kappa t}$
- $t$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt{4\pi\kappa t}}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Set $z=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{4\kappa t}}$. The differential gives $\sqrt{4\kappa t}$, which cancels the parameter-dependent part of the prefactor. The remaining Gaussian integral equals $\sqrt\pi$ and cancels the remaining factor.
