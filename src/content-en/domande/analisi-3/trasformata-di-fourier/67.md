Let $F=\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}$ be the transform of a continuous function $f$, with $f\in L^2$. What is $f(\pi)$?

- $\dfrac1\pi$
- $\pi$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

The inverse integral converges absolutely because $F$ has bounded support. The sine contribution vanishes by symmetry. The cosine contribution from minus one to one is proportional to $\sin\pi$ and is therefore zero.
