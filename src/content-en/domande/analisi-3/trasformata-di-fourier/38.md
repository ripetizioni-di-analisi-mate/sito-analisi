Let $F=\widehat f$ with $f\in L^1$. What is the transform of $f(-2x)$?

- $-\dfrac12 F\left(-\dfrac\xi2\right)$
- $\dfrac12 F\left(-\dfrac\xi2\right)$
- $2F(-2\xi)$
- $\dfrac12 F\left(\dfrac\xi2\right)$

## Explanation

The substitution $y=-2x$ reverses the endpoints and gives a negative differential. The signs cancel, leaving a positive prefactor. In the kernel the frequency is divided by minus two.
