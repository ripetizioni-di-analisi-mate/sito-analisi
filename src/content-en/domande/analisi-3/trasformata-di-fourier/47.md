If $f\in L^1$ and $\widehat f$ has a zero at $\xi=6$, at which frequency must the transform of $f(2x)$ vanish?

- $\xi=3$
- $\xi=6$
- $\xi=12$
- $\xi=0$

## Explanation

The transform of the scaled function is half of $\widehat f$ evaluated at half the frequency. To obtain the given zero this new argument must equal six, making the frequency twelve.
