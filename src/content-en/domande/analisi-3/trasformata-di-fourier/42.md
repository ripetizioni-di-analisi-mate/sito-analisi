Let $F=\widehat f$ with $f\in L^1$. Express the transform of $f(x)\cos(3x)$.

- $\dfrac{F(\xi-3)-F(\xi+3)}{2i}$
- $\dfrac{F(\xi-3)+F(\xi+3)}2$
- $\cos(3\xi)F(\xi)$
- $F(3\xi)$

## Explanation

Cosine is half the sum of two complex exponentials of opposite frequencies. Multiplication by each one shifts the transform of $f$ by the corresponding frequency. Linearity then gives half the sum of the shifted copies.
