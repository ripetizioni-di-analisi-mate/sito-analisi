With $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, which property does the transform of $f(x)=x e^{-11x^2}$ have?

- Real and odd.
- Real and even.
- Purely imaginary and odd.
- Purely imaginary and even.

## Explanation

The original function is real and odd. The cosine contribution vanishes because the product is odd. The sine contribution is purely imaginary and reverses sign when the frequency changes sign.
