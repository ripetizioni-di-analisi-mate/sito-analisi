With $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R} f(x)e^{-i\xi x}\,dx$, what is $\|f\|_2^2$ if $\|\widehat f\|_2^2=3\pi$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

With this convention Plancherel's theorem makes the squared frequency-domain norm $2\pi$ times the squared spatial norm. Divide the given value by this factor. Canceling $\pi$ leaves three halves.
