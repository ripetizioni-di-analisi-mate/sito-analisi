Evaluate the integral of $\dfrac1z$ along the boundary of the annulus $1<|z|<2$, positively oriented with respect to the annulus.

- $2 i \pi$
- $4 i \pi$
- $- 2 i \pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The outer circle is traversed counterclockwise and the inner circle clockwise. Both enclose the origin and each contribution has modulus $2\pi$. Their orientations are opposite, so the sum is zero.
