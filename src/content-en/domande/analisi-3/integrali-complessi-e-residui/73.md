Evaluate $\int_{\mathbb R}\dfrac{\cos(3x)}{x^2+4}\,dx$.

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2 e^{6}}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{e^{6}}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2 e^{3}}$
- $- \dfrac{\pi}{2 e^{6}}$

## Explanation

Consider $e^{3iz}$ in the upper half-plane, where its modulus is at most one. The arc contribution vanishes in the limit. The residue at $2i$ is $\dfrac{e^{-6}}{4i}$, and the resulting contribution is real.
