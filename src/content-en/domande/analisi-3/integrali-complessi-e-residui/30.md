What is the radius of convergence of the Taylor series of $\dfrac{\left(z - 4\right)^{2}}{\left(z + 9\right)^{3}}$ about $z=4$?

- $6$
- $13$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

The center at four is a regular point. The denominator vanishes only at minus nine, where the numerator is nonzero. The distance from the center to the pole is thirteen, which is therefore the radius of convergence.
