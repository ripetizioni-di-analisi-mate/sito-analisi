Let $f$ be meromorphic on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk, with no zeros or poles on its boundary. If it has two interior zeros counted with multiplicity and $\int_{|z|=1}\dfrac{f'}f\,dz=-2\pi i$ with positive orientation, how many poles does it have in the disk, counted with multiplicity?

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Dividing the integral by $2\pi i$ gives the number of zeros minus the number of poles. The given difference is minus one and there are two zeros counted with multiplicity, so the pole count is three.
