Evaluate $\int_{\mathbb R}\dfrac{dx}{x^4+1}$.

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\sqrt{2} \pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2} \pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The upper-half-plane poles are $e^{\frac{i\pi}{4}}$ and $e^{\frac{3i\pi}{4}}$. At a root $a$ the residue is $\dfrac1{4a^3}$. The arc estimate tends to zero because the denominator grows like $R^4$. Summing the residues gives $\dfrac{\pi}{\sqrt2}$.
