Let $f$ be holomorphic on a neighborhood of $|z-1|\le2$, with $|f|\le8$ on the circle. Using eight as an upper bound, what is the right-hand side of Cauchy's estimate for $|f^{(3)}(1)|$?

- $1$
- $8$
- $6$
- $24$

## Explanation

Cauchy's estimate uses the radius of the circle and the maximum modulus on its boundary. For the third derivative the factor is $\dfrac{3!}{2^3}$. Multiplication by eight gives six.
