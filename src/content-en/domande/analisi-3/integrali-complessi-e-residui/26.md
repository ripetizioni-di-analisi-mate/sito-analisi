Find the coefficient of $(z-3)$ in the expansion of $\dfrac{1}{\left(z - 6\right) \left(z - 3\right)}$ for $0<|z-3|<3$.

- $- \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{27}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{27}$

## Explanation

Setting $w=z-3$ expresses the reciprocal of $z-6$ as $-\dfrac13$ times the geometric series in $\dfrac w3$. Division by $w$ lowers every exponent by one, so the linear term comes from the quadratic term in the geometric series.
