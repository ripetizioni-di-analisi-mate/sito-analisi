Evaluate $\int_\gamma\overline z\,dz$ on the upper arc of $|z|=2$ directed from $2$ to $-2$.

- $2 i \pi$
- $- 4 i \pi$
- $0$
- $4 i \pi$

## Explanation

The angle increases from zero to $\pi$. Along the arc, $\overline z=2e^{-it}$ and $dz=2ie^{it}\,dt$, so their product is the constant $4i$, integrated over an interval of length $\pi$. The orientation determines the direction of traversal and hence the sign of the derivative with respect to the parameter.
