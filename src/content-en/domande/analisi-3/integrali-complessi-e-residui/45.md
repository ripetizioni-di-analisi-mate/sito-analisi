Find the coefficient of $z^{-2}$ in the expansion of $\dfrac1{z-2}$ valid for $|z|>2$.

- $2$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Factor $z$ out of the denominator. The reciprocal of $1-\dfrac2z$ has a convergent geometric series on the given domain. Multiplying the term linear in the ratio $\dfrac2z$ by $z^{-1}$ gives coefficient two.
