Evaluate $\operatorname{Res}\!\left(\dfrac{e^{5z}}{z^2},0\right)$.

- $-5$
- $10$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

Multiplication by $z^2$ gives $e^{5z}$, which is holomorphic at zero and equals one there. The pole is therefore double. The residue is the linear coefficient of the numerator, its derivative at zero, which equals five.
