Find the coefficient of $z^{-1}$ in the expansion of $\dfrac1{z(z-6)}$ for $0<|z|<6$.

- $-\dfrac1{6}$
- $\dfrac1{6}$
- $-\dfrac1{36}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Factor $-6$ out of $z-6$. The reciprocal of the remaining factor has a geometric series with ratio $\dfrac z6$. Multiplying its constant term by $-\dfrac1{6z}$ gives the required coefficient.
