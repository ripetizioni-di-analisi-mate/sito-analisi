Suppose $f$ is entire and $|f(z)|\le 4(1+|z|^2)$ for all $z$. What follows from Cauchy's estimates?

- It is constant.
- Its degree is exactly two.
- Its degree is at most one.
- It is a polynomial of degree at most two.

## Explanation

On $|z|=R$ the maximum grows at most like $R^2$. Thus Cauchy's estimate for every order $n\ge3$ tends to zero as $R$ grows. Only the coefficients of degrees zero, one, and two can remain.
