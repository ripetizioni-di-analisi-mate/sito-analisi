Find the residue of $f(z)=\dfrac1{z^2+25}$ at $z=5i$.

- $\dfrac1{10i}$
- $-\dfrac1{10i}$
- $\dfrac1{5}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The denominator vanishes at $5i$, and its derivative $2z$ takes the value $10i$ there. The zero is simple. The numerator equals one, so the residue is $\dfrac1{10i}$.
