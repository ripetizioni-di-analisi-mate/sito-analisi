How many zeros does $z^5+2z-5$ have in the open unit disk?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

On the unit circle the modulus of $z^5+2z$ is at most three. The constant term has modulus five, so Rouché's inequality is strict. The number of interior zeros is the same as for the constant.
