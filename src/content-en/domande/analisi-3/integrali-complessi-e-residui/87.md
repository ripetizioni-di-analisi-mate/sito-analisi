Compare $z^2$ and $z^2+1$ on the circle $|z|=1$. Why does the strict inequality required by Rouché's theorem for $f(z)=z^2$ and $g(z)=1$ fail?

- The constant term is not holomorphic.
- The quadratic term has no interior zeros.
- Both terms have modulus one along the boundary.
- The constant term has modulus greater than one along the boundary.

## Explanation

The quadratic power and the constant both have modulus one on the unit circle, so their moduli are equal there. In fact, their sum vanishes at the two boundary points $i$ and $-i$.
