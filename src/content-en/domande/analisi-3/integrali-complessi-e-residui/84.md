How many zeros does $(z-2)^2+z-2+\dfrac14$ have in the disk $|z-2|<2$, counted with multiplicity?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

For $w=z-2$, compare $w^2$ and $w+\dfrac14$. On the boundary the first has modulus four and the second has modulus at most $\dfrac94$. Rouché's theorem gives the polynomial the same two zeros counted with multiplicity as $w^2$.
