Evaluate $\int_{|z-1|=2}\dfrac{z^2+1}{z-i}\,dz$ counterclockwise.

- $2 i \pi$
- $4 i \pi$
- $- 2 i \pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The point $i$ lies inside because it is a distance $\sqrt2$ from the center $1$. The numerator is entire, so Cauchy's formula applies. Its value at $i$ is $i^2+1=0$, giving an integral of zero.
