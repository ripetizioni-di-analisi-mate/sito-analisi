What is the value of the extension of $\dfrac{\sin(2z)-2z}{z^3}$ at zero?

- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Subtraction cancels the linear term of $\sin(2z)$. The cubic term is $-\dfrac{(2z)^3}{3!}$, so division leaves a constant term. The higher terms tend to zero, giving the extension value $-\dfrac43$.
