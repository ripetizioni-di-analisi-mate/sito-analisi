Two functions holomorphic on the disk $|z|<1$ agree at $z=\dfrac1n$ for every integer $n\ge2$. What follows?

- They agree only on the positive real segment.
- They can differ at zero.
- They agree throughout the disk.
- They agree only at the points of the sequence and at zero.

## Explanation

The sequence consists of distinct points tending to zero, an interior point of the disk. The functions are holomorphic on the same connected domain, so the identity theorem gives equality throughout the disk.
