Evaluate $\int_{|z|=3}\dfrac{\sin z}{(z-\pi)^2}\,dz$ counterclockwise.

- $0$
- $- 2 i \pi$
- $2 i \pi$
- $i \pi$

## Explanation

The denominator vanishes only at $\pi$, which is outside the disk because $\pi>3$. Thus the integrand is holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed disk, and Cauchy's theorem gives zero.
