For $b<0$, which half-plane ensures $|e^{ibz}|\le1$ all along the semicircle used to close the real segment?

- The upper half-plane.
- Both half-planes.
- The lower half-plane.
- Neither half-plane.

## Explanation

Writing $z=x+iy$ gives the modulus $e^{-by}$. Since $b$ is negative, the exponent is nonpositive when $y\le0$. Thus one is a uniform upper bound on the semicircle in the lower half-plane.
