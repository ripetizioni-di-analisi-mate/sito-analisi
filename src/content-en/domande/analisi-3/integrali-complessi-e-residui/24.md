Evaluate the integral of $z^{2} + 2 i z$ along the segment from $1$ to $4 + i$.

- $i \left(4 + i\right)^{2} + \dfrac{\left(4 + i\right)^{3}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{\left(4 + i\right)^{3}}{3} - i \left(4 + i\right)^{2} + i$
- $-1 - 2 i + 2 i \left(4 + i\right) + \left(4 + i\right)^{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{3} - i + i \left(4 + i\right)^{2} + \dfrac{\left(4 + i\right)^{3}}{3}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the polynomial $\dfrac{z^3}{3}+iz^2$ is the integrand. It is an antiderivative on the whole plane, so evaluate it at $4+i$ and subtract its value at the starting point $1$, namely $\dfrac13+i$.
