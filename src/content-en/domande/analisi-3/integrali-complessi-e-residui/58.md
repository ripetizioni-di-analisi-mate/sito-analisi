What is the residue of $z^2e^{\frac1z}$ at zero?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The term of index $n$ in the exponential has degree $-n$. Multiplication by $z^2$ makes its degree $2-n$. To obtain $-1$ we therefore need $n=3$, whose coefficient is $\dfrac1{3!}$. This is the only term contributing to the residue.
