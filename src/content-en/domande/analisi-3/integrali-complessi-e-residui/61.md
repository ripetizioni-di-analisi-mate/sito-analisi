Evaluate $\operatorname{Res}\!\left(\dfrac{z+2}{(z-1)^2(z+1)},1\right)$.

- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The pole at one is double. After multiplying by $(z-1)^2$, differentiate the quotient $\dfrac{z+2}{z+1}$. The derivative is $-\dfrac1{(z+1)^2}$, with value $-\dfrac14$ at one. The quotient is holomorphic near one because its denominator is nonzero there.
