Evaluate $\int_{\mathbb R}\dfrac{x}{(x^2+4)^2}\,dx$.

- $0$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{16}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{\pi}{16}$

## Explanation

The integrand is odd because its numerator changes sign and its denominator is even. It is continuous on the line and decays like $|x|^{-3}$, so it is absolutely integrable. The two half-line integrals cancel.
