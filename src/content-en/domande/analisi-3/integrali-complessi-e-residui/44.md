What is the coefficient of $z^0$ in the expansion of $\dfrac{1}{z^2(1-z)}$ for $0<|z|<1$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

The geometric ratio is $z$, so the reciprocal of $1-z$ expands in nonnegative powers. Multiplication by $z^{-2}$ turns the quadratic term into the constant term, retaining its coefficient of one.
