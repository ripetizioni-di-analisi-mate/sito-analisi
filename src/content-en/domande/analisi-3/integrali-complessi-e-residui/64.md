Let $f$ be holomorphic on $0<|z|<1$ and suppose $\lim_{z\to0}z^3f(z)=2$. What type of singularity is zero?

- A pole of order two.
- An essential singularity.
- A removable singularity.
- A pole of order three.

## Explanation

The product $z^3f(z)$ has a finite limit and therefore extends across zero. Its value there is two and hence nonzero. The original function is this extension divided by $z^3$.
