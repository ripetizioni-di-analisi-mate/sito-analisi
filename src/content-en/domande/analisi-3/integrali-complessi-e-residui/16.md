Evaluate the integral of $\dfrac{z + 9}{\left(z - 4\right)^{3}}$ around $|z|=9$ clockwise.

- $i \pi$
- $26 i \pi$
- $- 2 i \pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The point four lies inside the circle, and the numerator is entire. The denominator has exponent three, so the formula uses the numerator's second derivative. This is zero because the numerator is linear. Reversing the orientation leaves the integral zero.
