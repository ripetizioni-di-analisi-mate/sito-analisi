Evaluate $\int_\gamma z\overline z\,dz$ on the segment from $0$ to $2i$.

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{8 i}{3}$
- $4 i$
- $\dfrac{8 i}{3}$

## Explanation

Use $z=it$ for $0\le t\le2$. The product $z\overline z$ equals $t^2$ and the differential is $i\,dt$. Thus the integral is $i$ times the real integral of $t^2$. The imaginary factor comes from the vertical direction of the segment.
