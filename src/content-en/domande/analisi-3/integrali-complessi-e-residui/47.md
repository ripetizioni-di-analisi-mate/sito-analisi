What is the coefficient of $z^{-1}$ in the expansion of $\dfrac1{(z-1)(z-3)}$ for $|z|>3$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

Factoring $z$ out of both factors gives $z^{-2}$ times two geometric series in $z^{-1}$. Every resulting power has exponent at most $-2$, so the required coefficient is zero.
