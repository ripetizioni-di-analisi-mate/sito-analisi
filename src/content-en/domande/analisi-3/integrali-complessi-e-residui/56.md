What is the order of the pole of $\dfrac{\cos z-1}{z^5}$ at zero?

- $5$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The cosine series has constant term one and zero linear coefficient. After subtraction the first remaining term is $-\dfrac{z^2}{2}$. Division by $z^5$ gives lowest exponent $-3$.
