With $R>2$, what bound follows for the integral of $\dfrac{e^{iz}}{(z^2+4)^2}$ on the upper semicircle of radius $R$ by multiplying the arc length by the upper bound on the modulus obtained from $|e^{iz}|\le1$ and $|z^2+4|\ge R^2-4$?

- $\dfrac{\pi R}{R^2-4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{(R^2-4)^2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi Re^R}{(R^2-4)^2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi R}{(R^2-4)^2}$

## Explanation

The exponential has modulus at most one on the upper semicircle. Squaring the denominator gives the lower bound $(R^2-4)^2$ for its modulus. Multiply the reciprocal by the arc length $\pi R$ to obtain the estimate.
