What is the integral of $\left(z + 6\right)^{13}$ around the counterclockwise boundary of the square $[-6,6]\times[-6,6]$?

- $24$
- $2 i \pi$
- $13060694016$
- $0$

## Explanation

The polynomial has an antiderivative throughout the plane. The integral on the boundary of the square is therefore the difference between the endpoint values of the antiderivative. Since the path is closed, the endpoints coincide and the difference is zero.
