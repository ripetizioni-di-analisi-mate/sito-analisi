Which hypothesis prevents direct application of the residue theorem to $\int_{|z|=1}\dfrac{dz}{z-1}$?

- The integrand must be entire.
- Every singularity must be a double pole.
- The integrand must be holomorphic along the contour.
- The contour must have radius greater than two.

## Explanation

The point one lies on the circle and the denominator vanishes there. The residue theorem allows singularities inside the contour but requires the integrand to be holomorphic near the contour itself.
