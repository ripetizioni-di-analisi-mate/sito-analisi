Evaluate the integral of $\dfrac{1}{\left(z - 2\right) \left(z + 4\right)}$ around the circle $|z|=3$ clockwise.

- $\dfrac{i \pi}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{i \pi}{6}$
- $- \dfrac{i \pi}{3}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The pole at two is inside the circle and the pole at minus four is outside. The residue at two is the value of $\dfrac1{z+4}$ there, namely $\dfrac16$. Clockwise orientation multiplies this residue by $-2\pi i$.
