Which maximal annulus centered at zero contains the circle $|z|=3$ and admits the Laurent expansion of $\dfrac{1}{(z+2)(z-5)}$?

- $0<|z|<5$
- $3<|z|<5$
- $2<|z|<5$
- $2<|z|<\infty$

## Explanation

The two poles are at distances two and five from the center. The circles of those radii contain singularities and bound the maximal annuli. The circle of radius three lies between them.
