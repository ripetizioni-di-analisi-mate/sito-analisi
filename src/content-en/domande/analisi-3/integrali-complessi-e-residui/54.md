Find the coefficient of $(z-i)^2$ in the Taylor expansion of $e^{z-i}\cos(z-i)$.

- $1$
- $0$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

In the product of the series, the exponential gives a positive contribution to the quadratic coefficient and the cosine a negative one. Both have magnitude one half. The cosine has zero linear coefficient, so there are no other contributions.
