Find the residue of $\dfrac{z^{2}}{\left(z - 2\right) \left(z + 4\right)}$ at $z=2$.

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The factor $z-2$ occurs once, and the numerator is nonzero at two. To compute the residue, multiply by $z-2$ and evaluate $\dfrac{z^2}{z+4}$ at two, giving $\dfrac23$.
