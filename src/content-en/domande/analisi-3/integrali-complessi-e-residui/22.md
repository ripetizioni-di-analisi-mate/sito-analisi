What is $\dfrac1{2\pi i}\oint_{|z|=12}\dfrac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz$ with counterclockwise orientation for $f(z)=\dfrac{\left(z - 5\right)^{11}}{\left(z + 11\right)^{5}}$?

- $-6$
- $6$
- $16$
- $11$

## Explanation

The positively oriented circle encloses both the zero at five and the pole at minus eleven, with neither on the boundary. The argument principle counts their orders. The required value is eleven minus five, or six.
