Let $f$ be holomorphic for $1<|z|<4$, with $\int_{|z|=2}z^2 f(z)\,dz=6\pi i$ taken counterclockwise. What is the coefficient $c_{-3}$ of $f$?

- $6$
- $0$
- $-3$
- $3$

## Explanation

Multiplying the Laurent series by $z^2$ turns the term $c_{-3}z^{-3}$ into $c_{-3}z^{-1}$. All other powers integrate to zero around the circle. Divide the given value by $2\pi i$.
