Evaluate $\dfrac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}(1+2e^{it})^3\,dt$.

- $1$
- $8$
- $9$
- $0$

## Explanation

The integrand uses the values of the entire function $f(z)=z^3$ on the circle with center one and radius two. By the mean value property the required quantity is the value of $f$ at the center.
