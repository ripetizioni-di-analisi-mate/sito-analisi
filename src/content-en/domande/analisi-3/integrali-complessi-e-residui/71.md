Evaluate $\int_{|z|=2}\dfrac{e^z}{z^2(z-3)}\,dz$ counterclockwise.

- $- \dfrac{2 i \pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8 i \pi}{9}$
- $- \dfrac{8 i \pi}{9}$
- $- \dfrac{2 i \pi}{9}$

## Explanation

The only interior pole is the double pole at zero. For the residue, differentiate $\dfrac{e^z}{z-3}$ at zero. The quotient rule gives $-\dfrac13-\dfrac19$, so the integral is $-\dfrac{8\pi i}{9}$.
