How many zeros does $z^4+5z-1$ have in $|z|<1$, counted with multiplicity?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

On the unit circle the linear term $5z$ has modulus five. The remainder $z^4-1$ has modulus at most two. Both polynomials are entire, so Rouché's theorem gives the same zero count as for the linear term.
