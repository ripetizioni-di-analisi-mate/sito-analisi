How many zeros does the polynomial $p(z)=(z^2-1)^2$ have in the disk $|z-1|<1$, counted with multiplicity?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The polynomial factors as $(z-1)^2(z+1)^2$. The zero at one is the center of the disk and has multiplicity two. The zero at minus one is two units from the center and lies outside.
