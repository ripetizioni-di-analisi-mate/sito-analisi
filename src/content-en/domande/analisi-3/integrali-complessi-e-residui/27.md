How is the point $z=3$ classified for $\dfrac{(z-3)^2}{(z-3)(z+3)}$?

- An essential singularity.
- A simple pole.
- A removable singularity.
- A double pole.

## Explanation

For $z\ne3$ the common factor $z-3$ cancels. The remaining quotient is $\dfrac{z-3}{z+3}$, whose denominator equals six at three. Thus it is holomorphic near that point and its extension has value zero there.
