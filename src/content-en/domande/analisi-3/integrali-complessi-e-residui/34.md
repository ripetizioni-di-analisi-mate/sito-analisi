If $\gamma$ is the circle $|z|=2$ traversed counterclockwise, what is $\int_\gamma\dfrac{e^z}{z^4}\,dz$?

- $i \pi$
- $\dfrac{i \pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2 i \pi}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{i \pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The function $e^z$ is entire and the point $0$ lies inside the circle.
Since the exponent in the denominator is $4=3+1$, Cauchy's integral formula for derivatives gives
$$
\int_\gamma\frac{e^z}{z^{4}}\,dz=\frac{2\pi i}{3!}\,f'''(0),\qquad f(z)=e^z .
$$
Since $f'''(0)=e^0=1$, the integral equals $\dfrac{2\pi i}{6}=\dfrac{i\pi}{3}$.
The value $\dfrac{2i\pi}{3}$ comes from dividing by $3$ instead of $3!$.
