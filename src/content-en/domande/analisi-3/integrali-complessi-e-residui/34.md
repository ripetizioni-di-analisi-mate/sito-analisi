If $\gamma$ is the positively oriented circle $|z|=2$, what is $\int_\gamma\dfrac{e^z}{z^4}\,dz$?

- $i \pi$
- $\dfrac{i \pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2 i \pi}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{i \pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The numerator is entire and zero lies inside the positively oriented circle. The fourth power in the denominator calls for the third derivative at zero, which is one. The formula thus gives $\dfrac{2\pi i}{3!}$.
