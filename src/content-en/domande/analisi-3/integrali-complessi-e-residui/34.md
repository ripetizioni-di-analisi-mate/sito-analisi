The function $f$ is holomorphic in a neighbourhood of the disk $|z|\le1$. On its positively oriented boundary one measures $\oint f(z)z^{-3}\,\mathrm dz=6\pi i$. What is the coefficient of $z^2$ in the Taylor series of $f$ at the origin?

- $6$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6\pi$

## Explanation

The integral formula extracts the coefficient $a_2$ multiplied by $2\pi i$. Thus $a_2=3$. The second derivative would be $2!a_2=6$, which distinguishes the two quantities.
