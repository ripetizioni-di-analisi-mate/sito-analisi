Let $f$ be holomorphic on an open set containing the closed disk centered at $a$ with radius $2$. Suppose $|f(z)|\le3$ on its boundary. What is the smallest upper bound for $|f'(a)|$ guaranteed by these hypotheses?

- $\dfrac32$
- $6$
- $\dfrac34$
- $3$

## Explanation

Cauchy's first-derivative estimate divides the upper bound on the boundary by the radius and gives $\dfrac32$. The function $f(z)=\dfrac32(z-a)$ satisfies the hypothesis and has derivative of modulus $\dfrac32$, so the estimate is sharp.
