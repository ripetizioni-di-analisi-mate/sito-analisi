How many signed turns around zero does $f(\gamma)$ make when $f(z)=z^{-3}$ and $\gamma$ is the positively oriented unit circle?

- $3$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The function has a pole of order three at zero and no zeros inside the disk. The argument principle gives winding number minus three. Indeed, on the circle we have $f(e^{it})=e^{-3it}$.
