How many zeros does $z^4-6z+2$ have in the annulus $1<|z|<2$, counted with multiplicity?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

On the inner boundary $-6z$ dominates because the remainder has modulus at most three. On the outer boundary $z^4$ dominates because the remainder has modulus at most fourteen. Rouché's theorem gives counts of one and four. Neither boundary contains zeros, so the annulus contains three.
