Compute the residue of $\dfrac{f'(z)}{f(z)}$ at $z=-1$ for $f(z)=\dfrac{(z+1)^4}{(z+1)(z-2)}$.

- $4$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $-1$

## Explanation

On a punctured disk around minus one the function simplifies to $\dfrac{(z+1)^3}{z-2}$. The remaining denominator factor is regular and nonzero there. The zero therefore has order three, equal to the residue of the logarithmic derivative.
