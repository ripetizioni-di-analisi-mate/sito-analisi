Evaluate $\displaystyle\oint_{|z-1|=2}\dfrac{z^3}{(z-1)^2(z-5)}\,dz$ counterclockwise.

- $- \dfrac{i \pi}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{13 i \pi}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{3 i \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{13 i \pi}{8}$

## Explanation

The point five is four units from the center and therefore outside the disk. Apply the formula for the first derivative of $\dfrac{z^3}{z-5}$ at one. The derivative is $-\dfrac{13}{16}$, so the integral is $-\dfrac{13\pi i}{8}$.
