If $\gamma(t)=2+e^{-2it}$ for $0\le t\le 2\pi$, what is $\int_\gamma\dfrac{dz}{z-2}$?

- $4 i \pi$
- $- 2 i \pi$
- $0$
- $- 4 i \pi$

## Explanation

The derivative of the parametrization divided by $\gamma(t)-2$ is the constant $-2i$. Integration over $[0,2\pi]$ gives $-4\pi i$, as expected for two clockwise turns. The center is off the path because the distance to the center is one.
