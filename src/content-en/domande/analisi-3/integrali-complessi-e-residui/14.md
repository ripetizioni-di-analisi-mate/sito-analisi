What is $\int_\gamma \dfrac{dz}{z}$ along the positively oriented circle $|z-3|=1$?

- $2 i \pi$
- $0$
- $- 2 i \pi$
- $2 \pi$

## Explanation

The singularity at zero is outside the disk because it is three units from the center and the radius is one. Thus the integrand is holomorphic on a neighborhood of the disk, and Cauchy's theorem applies.
