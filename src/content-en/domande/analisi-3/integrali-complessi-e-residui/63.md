For $f(z)=\dfrac{e^z-a}{z^2}$, which value of $a$ makes the pole at zero simple?

- $0$
- $-1$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The double pole reduces to a simple pole when the numerator's constant term vanishes and its linear term stays nonzero. The exponential and its derivative both equal one at zero, so we need $a=1$.
