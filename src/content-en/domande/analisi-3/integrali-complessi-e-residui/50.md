Find the coefficient of $z^1$ in the expansion of $\dfrac{1}{(z-1)(z-3)}$ on the annulus $1<|z|<3$.

- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{18}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The fraction with a pole at one gives only negative powers. The fraction with a pole at three is $\dfrac{1}{2(z-3)}$, equal to $-\dfrac16$ times the geometric series in $\dfrac z3$. Thus the linear coefficient is $-\dfrac1{18}$.
