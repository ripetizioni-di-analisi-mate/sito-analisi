What is the radius of convergence of the Taylor expansion at zero of $\dfrac{z-1}{(z-1)(z+3)}$ after extension to $z=1$?

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator share the factor $z-1$, and the quotient extends across one. The extension has only the pole $-3$ as a singularity, so its distance from zero gives the radius.
