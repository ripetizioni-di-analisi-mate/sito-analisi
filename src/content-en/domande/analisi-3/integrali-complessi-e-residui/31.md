Joining the midpoints of the sides in Goursat's triangle construction gives four boundary integrals $J_1,\ldots,J_4$. What does the identity $J=\sum_{k=1}^4 J_k$ alone guarantee for at least one $J_k$?

- $J_k=\dfrac J4$
- $|J_k|\ge |J|$
- $|J_k|\ge\dfrac{|J|}{4}$
- $J_k=0$

## Explanation

Every interior edge is traversed twice in opposite directions, so the four integrals sum to $J$. If all four moduli were less than one quarter of $|J|$, the triangle inequality would give a contradiction.
