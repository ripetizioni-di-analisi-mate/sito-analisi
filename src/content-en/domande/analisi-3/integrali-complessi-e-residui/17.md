An entire function $f$ satisfies $|f(z)|\le 4+|z|$ for every $z$. Which conclusion about its Taylor series at the origin is necessary?

- All coefficients of degree two or higher vanish.
- The linear coefficient must also vanish.
- The linear coefficient must be one.
- The constant term must have modulus four.

## Explanation

On the circle of radius $R$, the Cauchy estimates give $|a_n|\le \dfrac{4+R}{R^n}$. As $R$ tends to infinity the bound tends to zero for every $n\ge2$. Both constant and affine functions remain possible.
