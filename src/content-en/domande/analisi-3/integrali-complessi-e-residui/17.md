An entire function $f$ satisfies $\operatorname{Re}f(z)\le3$ for every $z\in\mathbb C$. Which conclusion follows?

- $f$ is constant.
- $\operatorname{Re}f$ is constant and $\operatorname{Im}f$ may not be.
- $f$ is bounded and may not be constant.
- No conclusion follows, because $|f|$ may be unbounded.

## Explanation

Consider $g=e^{f}$, which is entire as a composition of entire functions.
Since $|g(z)|=e^{\operatorname{Re}f(z)}\le e^{3}$, the function $g$ is entire and bounded, hence it is constant by Liouville's theorem.
Then $g'=f'e^{f}=0$ and, since $e^{f}$ never vanishes, we get $f'=0$ on all of $\mathbb C$.
Therefore $f$ is constant.
The hypothesis bounds only the real part, so Liouville's theorem is applied to $e^{f}$ and not directly to $f$.
