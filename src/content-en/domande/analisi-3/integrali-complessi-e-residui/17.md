Evaluate the integral of $\dfrac{z + 13}{\left(z - 6\right)^{2}}$ around $|z|=13$ counterclockwise.

- $2 i \pi$
- $0$
- $38 i \pi$
- $i \pi$

## Explanation

The numerator is entire and six lies inside the positively oriented circle. The square in the denominator calls for the first derivative of the numerator, which equals one. The factor in Cauchy's formula is $2\pi i$.
