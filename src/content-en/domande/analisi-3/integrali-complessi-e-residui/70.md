Evaluate $\int_\gamma \dfrac{e^{\frac1z}-1}{z}\,dz$ on the positively oriented circle $|z|=1$.

- $2 i \pi$
- $0$
- $- 2 i \pi$
- $i \pi$

## Explanation

Subtraction removes the constant term of the exponential. After division by $z$, all exponents are at most $-2$. The residue at the essential singularity is therefore zero, and the residue theorem gives a zero integral.
