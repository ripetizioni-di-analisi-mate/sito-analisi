What is $\int_\gamma \overline z\,dz$ along the polygonal path $0\to 2\to 2+i$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2} + 2 i$
- $\dfrac{5}{2} + 2 i$
- $\dfrac{5}{2} - 2 i$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

The integral on the real segment is $\int_0^2 t\,dt=2$. On the vertical segment set $z=2+it$. The product $(2-it)i$ equals $t+2i$, so the second segment contributes $\dfrac12+2i$. The integral on the polygonal path is the sum of these contributions.
