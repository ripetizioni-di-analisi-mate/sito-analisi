What is the maximum of $|e^z|$ on the closed disk $|z-1|\le2$?

- $e^{2}$
- $e^{3}$
- $e$
- $e^{-1}$

## Explanation

We have $|e^z|=e^{\operatorname{Re}z}$. The real part is at most three on the disk, since its center is one and its radius two. The real point $z=3$ attains the maximum.
