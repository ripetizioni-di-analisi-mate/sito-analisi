Which relation holds for the coefficients $c_n$ of an even function holomorphic on an annulus centered at zero?

- The even-indexed coefficients vanish.
- Only the negative-indexed coefficients vanish.
- The odd-indexed coefficients vanish.
- Coefficients with opposite indices always agree.

## Explanation

Substituting $-z$ into the series reverses the sign of every odd-indexed term and leaves the even-indexed terms unchanged. Since the function is even, uniqueness of the Laurent expansion forces all odd-indexed coefficients to vanish.
