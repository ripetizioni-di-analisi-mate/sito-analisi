Let $\gamma_R$ be the positively oriented circles $|z|=R$. What is $\displaystyle\int_{\gamma_3}\dfrac{dz}{(z-2)(z+1)}-\int_{\gamma_{\frac32}}\dfrac{dz}{(z-2)(z+1)}$?

- $\dfrac{2 i \pi}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{2 i \pi}{3}$
- $0$
- $2 i \pi$

## Explanation

The pole $-1$ lies inside both curves, so its contributions cancel. The pole at two lies only in the larger disk. Its residue is $\dfrac13$, giving the difference $\dfrac{2\pi i}{3}$.
