Evaluate $\int_{|z|=1}\dfrac{e^{5z}}{z^3}\,dz$ with counterclockwise orientation.

- $50\pi i$
- $-25\pi i$
- $25\pi i$
- $0$

## Explanation

The exponential is entire and zero lies inside the positively oriented circle. The cube in the denominator requires the second derivative, which equals twenty-five at zero. The formula has factor $\dfrac{2\pi i}{2!}$, giving the integral $25\pi i$.
