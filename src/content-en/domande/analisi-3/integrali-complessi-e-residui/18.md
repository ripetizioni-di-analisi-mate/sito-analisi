Find the residue of $\dfrac{e^{z}}{\left(z - 5\right)^{3}}$ at $z=5$.

- $e^{5}$
- $\dfrac{e^{5}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 e^{5}}$
- $\dfrac{e^{5}}{6}$

## Explanation

The exponential is holomorphic and nonzero at five, so the pole is triple. The residue is the quadratic coefficient in the numerator's expansion about the pole. Dividing its second derivative by $2!$ gives $\dfrac{e^5}{2}$.
