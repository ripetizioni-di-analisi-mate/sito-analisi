Evaluate $\int_\gamma \overline z\,dz$ on the segment directed from $0$ to $1+i$.

- $i$
- $1$
- $2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The parametrization is $\gamma(t)=(1+i)t$, with $0\le t\le1$. Conjugation gives the factor $(1-i)t$, and the derivative of the path gives $1+i$. Their product is $2t$, so the integral equals one. The parametrization is regular and traces the whole segment exactly once as the real parameter varies.
