Evaluate the integral of $\dfrac{1}{\left(z - 4\right) \left(z + 9\right)}$ around $|z|=10$ counterclockwise.

- $- \dfrac{2 i \pi}{13}$
- $2 i \pi$
- $\dfrac{2 i \pi}{13}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Both poles lie inside the positively oriented circle. Canceling the factor vanishing at each pole gives the residues $\dfrac1{13}$ and $-\dfrac1{13}$. Their sum is zero, so the integral vanishes.
