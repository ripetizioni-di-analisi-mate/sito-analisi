Evaluate $\int_{|z|=2}\dfrac{z^2+z}{z(z-1)}\,dz$ counterclockwise.

- $2 i \pi$
- $4 i \pi$
- $0$
- $- 4 i \pi$

## Explanation

The numerator factor $z$ cancels and the function extends across zero. A simple pole of residue two remains at one. Both points lie inside, but only the pole contributes to the integral.
