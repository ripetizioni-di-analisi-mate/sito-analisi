Evaluate $\int_\gamma z^{5}\,dz$, where $\gamma$ is the segment directed from $0$ to $2$.

- $\dfrac{64}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{64}{6}$
- $\dfrac{64}{7}$
- $0$

## Explanation

An entire antiderivative of $z^5$ is $\dfrac{z^6}{6}$. The oriented path starts at zero and ends at two. Subtract the initial value, zero, from the final value $\dfrac{64}{6}$.
