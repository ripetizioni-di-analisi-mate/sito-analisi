Let $f$ be holomorphic on the disk $|z|<3$. Which quantity must have the same value on all piecewise regular paths in the disk from $0$ to $1+i$?

- $\int_\gamma |f(z)|\,|dz|$
- $L(\gamma)$
- $\max_\gamma |f(z)|$
- $\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz$

## Explanation

The disk is simply connected, so $f$ has an antiderivative there. The complex integral is the difference of two fixed values of the antiderivative. The other quantities may vary with the points traversed by the path.
