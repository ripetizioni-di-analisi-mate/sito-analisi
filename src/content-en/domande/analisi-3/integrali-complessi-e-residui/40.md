A subset of $|z|<1$ has $1$ as its only accumulation point. If two functions holomorphic on the disk agree on this subset, can we conclude that they agree throughout the disk?

- The conclusion always holds because the set is infinite.
- The conclusion holds because the disk is simply connected.
- The conclusion holds because the accumulation point is real.
- The identity theorem requires an accumulation point inside the disk.

## Explanation

The point $1$ is on the boundary of the disk. Thus the required interior accumulation point is missing, regardless of connectedness. Infinitely many points of agreement in the disk alone do not suffice.
