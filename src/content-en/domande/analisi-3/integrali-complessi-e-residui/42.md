Let $f$ be holomorphic on a connected domain. If $|f|$ has a local maximum of zero at an interior point, what follows?

- The function has only one simple zero.
- The function vanishes identically on the domain.
- The function has a pole at the point of maximum.
- Zero can be an isolated value even if it is a local maximum.

## Explanation

The modulus is always nonnegative. If its maximum on a neighborhood is zero, $f$ must vanish throughout that neighborhood. The identity theorem extends the equality to the whole connected domain.
