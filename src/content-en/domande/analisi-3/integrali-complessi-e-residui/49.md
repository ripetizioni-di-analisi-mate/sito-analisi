Find the coefficient of $z^{-1}$ in the expansion of $\dfrac{z}{(z-1)(z-4)}$ on the annulus $1<|z|<4$.

- $- \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The decomposition gives one partial fraction with a pole at one and another with a pole at four. The first expands in negative powers and the second in nonnegative powers. Thus the required coefficient is the residue at one.
