Which function is an antiderivative of $f(z)=\dfrac1{z-1}$ on the half-plane $\operatorname{Re}z>1$?

- $\operatorname{Log}(z-1)$
- $-\dfrac1{z-1}$
- $\operatorname{Log}(z+1)$
- $\dfrac{1}{(z-1)^2}$

## Explanation

The number $z-1$ has positive real part and thus lies in the domain of the principal logarithm. The derivative of $\operatorname{Log}$ with respect to its argument is the reciprocal of that argument. The chain rule gives exactly $f$.
