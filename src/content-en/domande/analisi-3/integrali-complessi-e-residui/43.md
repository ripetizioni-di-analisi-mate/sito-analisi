Let $f$ be holomorphic and nowhere zero on a neighborhood of a closed disk. How can one locate a point where $|f|$ attains its minimum on the disk?

- Apply the maximum modulus principle to $f'$.
- Consider only the center of the disk.
- Apply the maximum modulus principle to $\dfrac1f$.
- Use the average of the values of $f$ without checking their moduli.

## Explanation

The absence of zeros makes $\dfrac1f$ holomorphic on the neighborhood of the disk. Its modulus is the reciprocal of the modulus of $f$. A boundary maximum of the reciprocal therefore gives a boundary minimum of $|f|$.
