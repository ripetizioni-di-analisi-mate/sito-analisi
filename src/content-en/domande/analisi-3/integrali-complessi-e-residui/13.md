How many zeros does $p(z)=z^{7}+z+1$ have in the disk $|z|<2$, counted with multiplicity?

- $7$
- $6$
- $8$
- $0$

## Explanation

On the boundary $|z|=2$, the sum $z+1$ has modulus at most three, while $|z^7|=128$. The polynomials are entire, so Rouché's theorem applies. The monomial has one zero of multiplicity seven.
