What is the residue of $\dfrac{1-\cos z}{z^4}$ at zero?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The numerator is even and the denominator an even power, so the expansion contains only even powers. The leading exponent is $-2$, but the coefficient of $z^{-1}$ is zero.
