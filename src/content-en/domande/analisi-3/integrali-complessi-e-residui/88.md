Under what condition on $a$ does the bound $|az+1|\le 2|a|+1$ suffice to apply Rouché's theorem when comparing $z^3+az+1$ with $z^3$ on $|z|=2$?

- $|a|\le\dfrac72$
- $|a|<\dfrac92$
- $|a|>4$
- $|a|<\dfrac72$

## Explanation

The cubic monomial has modulus eight on the boundary. The stated bound guarantees a strict inequality only if $2|a|+1<8$. Subtracting one and dividing by two gives the bound $\dfrac72$ on $|a|$.
