Let $f(z)=\dfrac{(z-1)^2(z+1)}{(z-3)^2}$. Evaluate $\int_{|z|=2}\dfrac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz$ clockwise.

- $6 i \pi$
- $- 2 i \pi$
- $- 6 i \pi$
- $- 4 i \pi$

## Explanation

The zeros at one and minus one lie inside, of multiplicities two and one, respectively. The pole at three lies outside. The argument principle gives a count of three, and clockwise orientation supplies the minus sign.
