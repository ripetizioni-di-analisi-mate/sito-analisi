Evaluate $\int_\gamma \dfrac{z+2}{z(z-3)}\,dz$ along the positively oriented boundary of the square $[-1,1]\times[-1,1]$.

- $- \dfrac{4 i \pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4 i \pi}{3}$
- $2 i \pi$
- $- \dfrac{2 i \pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The square encloses the pole at zero, with the pole at three outside. Multiplying by $z$ leaves $\dfrac{z+2}{z-3}$, whose value at zero is $-\dfrac23$. With positive orientation the integral is $2\pi i$ times this residue.
