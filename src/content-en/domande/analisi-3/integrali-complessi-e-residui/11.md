Evaluate $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\dfrac{dx}{x^2+25}$.

- $\dfrac{2\pi}{5}$
- $-\dfrac\pi{5}$
- $\dfrac\pi{5}$
- $0$

## Explanation

For an upper semicircle of radius $R>5$ the only interior pole is $5i$. Its residue is $\dfrac1{10i}$. The bound $\dfrac{\pi R}{R^2-25}$ tends to zero, so the integral over the line is $2\pi i$ times the residue.
