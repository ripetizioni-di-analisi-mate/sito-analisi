Which value of $a$ makes the singularity of $\dfrac{e^z-1-az}{z^2}$ at zero removable?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The constant term has already been subtracted. The remaining linear term has coefficient $1-a$. For division by $z^2$ to give a holomorphic extension at zero, this coefficient must vanish, requiring $a=1$.
