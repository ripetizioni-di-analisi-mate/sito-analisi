On the upper semicircle $\gamma$ centered at zero with radius three, use the upper bound $\dfrac14$ for the modulus of $(z-1)^{-2}$. What bound for $\left|\int_\gamma \dfrac{dz}{(z-1)^2}\right|$ follows upon multiplication by the length of $\gamma$?

- $\dfrac{3 \pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3 \pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3 \pi}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The semicircle has length $3\pi$. The lower bound of two for $|z-1|$ makes the modulus of the integrand at most one quarter. The required bound is therefore the product $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$.
