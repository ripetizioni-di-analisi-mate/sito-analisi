Evaluate $\int_\gamma\dfrac{dz}{z^2}$ along a closed, piecewise regular path in $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$.

- $2 i \pi$
- $1$
- $0$
- $- 2 i \pi$

## Explanation

The function $-\dfrac1z$ is an antiderivative on the entire punctured plane. The chain rule gives the difference between the values of the antiderivative at the endpoints. The endpoints coincide, so the difference is zero.
