For the two branches of $u_t+2u_x=0$ on $x\ge0$ to meet with $C^1$ regularity at $x=2t$, which conditions must $u(0,x)=f(x)$ and $u(t,0)=g(t)$ satisfy at zero?

- $f(0)=g(0)$ and $g'(0)=2f'(0)$
- $f(0)=g(0)$ and $g'(0)=f'(0)$
- $f'(0)=g'(0)=0$, with arbitrary initial values
- $f(0)=g(0)$ and $g'(0)=-2f'(0)$

## Explanation

The branches are $f(x-2t)$ and $g(t-\dfrac{x}{2})$. Their values match when the data agree at zero. The limits of the time derivatives are $-2f'(0)$ and $g'(0)$, which must also coincide.
