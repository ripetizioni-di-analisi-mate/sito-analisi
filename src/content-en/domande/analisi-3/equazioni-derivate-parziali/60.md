For $u_{tt}=u_{xx}$ on the real line, with $u(0,x)=0$ and $u_t(0,x)=x^2$, what is $u(1,0)$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Since the initial displacement is zero, only the initial velocity contributes. For the point in question, the characteristic interval is $[-1,1]$. The integral of $s^2$ is $\dfrac{2}{3}$, which must be multiplied by $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
