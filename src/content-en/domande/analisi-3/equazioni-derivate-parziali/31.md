For $u_{tt}=9u_{xx}$ on $(0,7)$ with fixed endpoints, which energy is conserved?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{7}(u^2+9u_x^2)\,dx$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{7}u_t^2\,dx$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{7}(u_t^2+9u_x^2)\,dx$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{7}(u_t^2-9u_x^2)\,dx$

## Explanation

Differentiate both terms and integrate by parts. The wave equation cancels the interior terms. The velocity vanishes at both endpoints, so the energy derivative is zero.
