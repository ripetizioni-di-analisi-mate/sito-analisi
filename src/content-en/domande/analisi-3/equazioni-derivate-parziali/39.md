If $u_t+c u_x=0$ has initial data $f$, which formula for $\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R}u(t,x)\,dx$ holds for every velocity $c$ and every $f\in C^1(\mathbb R)\cap L^1(\mathbb R)$?

- $\displaystyle e^{-ct}\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)\,dx$
- $\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)\,dx+ct$
- $\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)\,dx$
- $0$ for every $t>0$

## Explanation

The solution is the translate $f(x-ct)$. The change of variables from $x$ to $x-ct$ leaves the integration domain unchanged and has Jacobian one. Integrability therefore makes the integral independent of time.
