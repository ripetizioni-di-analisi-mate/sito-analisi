Solve $-u''=5$ on $(0,1)$ with $u(0)=u(1)=0$.

- $u(x)=\dfrac{5}{2}x(1-x)$
- $u(x)=-\dfrac{5}{2}x(1-x)$
- $u(x)=5x(1-x)$
- $u(x)=\dfrac{5}{2}x^2$

## Explanation

Integrate twice and impose both boundary conditions. The classical solution also lies in H¹ with zero trace and is therefore the weak solution of the problem.

$$
u'=-5x+C,\qquad u=-\dfrac{5}{2}x^2+Cx+D.\qquad D=0,\quad C=\dfrac{5}{2}.
$$
