Which transformation preserves the numbers of positive and negative eigenvalues of every principal matrix?

- An invertible linear change of coordinates
- Adding $u_{xx}$ to the operator
- Replacing the mixed derivative by a pure derivative
- Setting a diagonal coefficient to zero

## Explanation

An invertible linear change of coordinates transforms the principal matrix by congruence. The law of inertia for quadratic forms preserves the maximum dimensions of positive and negative subspaces and hence the equation type.
