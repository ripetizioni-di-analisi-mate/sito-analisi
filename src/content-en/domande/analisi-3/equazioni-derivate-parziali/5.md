Solve $u_{tt}=u_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with fixed endpoints, $u(0,x)=\sin(5x)$, and $u_t(0,x)=0$.

- $u(t,x)=\cos(5t)\sin(5x)$
- $u(t,x)=\sin(5t)\sin(5x)$
- $u(t,x)=e^{-25t}\sin(5x)$
- $u(t,x)=\cosh(5t)\sin(5x)$

## Explanation

The fifth mode has eigenvalue twenty-five and satisfies both endpoint conditions. Its time coefficient therefore has angular frequency five. The initial displacement sets the cosine coefficient to one, and zero initial velocity eliminates the sine.
