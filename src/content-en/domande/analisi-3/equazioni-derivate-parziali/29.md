On $x\ge0$, let $u_t+2u_x=0$ with $u(t,0)=1+t$ and $u(0,x)=1$. What is $u(3,2)$ for the continuous, piecewise classical solution?

- $3$
- $1$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Traced backward, the characteristic meets the boundary $x=0$ after one unit of time because its velocity is two. Its entry time is therefore two, where the boundary data give $1+2$.
