What is the weak derivative of $|x|$ on $(-1,1)$?

- $2\delta_0$
- $|x|$ almost everywhere
- $\operatorname{sgn}(x)$ almost everywhere
- $0$ almost everywhere

## Explanation

Integrating by parts separately on the two sides of zero gives slopes $-1$ and $1$. Since the function is continuous at zero, the interior boundary terms cancel when taking the first derivative. The sign function therefore represents the weak derivative.
