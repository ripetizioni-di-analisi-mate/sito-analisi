Which identity, for every $v\in H_0^1(0,1)$, defines a weak solution of $-(2u^\prime)^\prime=5$ on $(0,1)$ with homogeneous Dirichlet data?

- $2\int_0^1 uv\,dx=5\int_0^1 v\,dx$
- $2\int_0^1 u^\prime v^\prime\,dx=5\int_0^1 v\,dx$
- $2\int_0^1 u^\prime v^\prime\,dx=-5\int_0^1 v\,dx$
- $5\int_0^1 u^\prime v^\prime\,dx=2\int_0^1 v\,dx$

## Explanation

Multiply by the test function and integrate by parts. The zero trace removes the boundary term, leaving the product of the first derivatives.
