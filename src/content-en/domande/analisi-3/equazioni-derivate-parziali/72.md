Suppose a real harmonic function is continuous on the closed unit disk and its boundary values lie between $-2$ and $3$. What follows?

- $u$ must have a strict maximum at the center
- $u$ is positive throughout the disk
- $u$ must be constant
- $-2\le u\le 3$ throughout the interior as well

## Explanation

The disk is bounded and the function is continuous on its closure, so the maximum principle applies. Applying it to $-u$ as well gives the lower bound. Every interior value therefore lies between the two boundary extremes.
