For the wave equation with fixed ends, why does the boundary term $[u_tu_x]_0^L$ in the energy derivative vanish?

- $u_x$ vanishes at the endpoints by the Dirichlet boundary condition
- $u_t$ vanishes at the endpoints because $u$ is constant in time there
- $u_x$ always takes the same value at the two endpoints
- $u_t$ and $u_x$ are orthogonal at every point

## Explanation

The displacement at each endpoint is identically zero as a function of time. Differentiating these two identities shows that $u_t$ vanishes at both endpoints. Its product with $u_x$ therefore vanishes at each end.
