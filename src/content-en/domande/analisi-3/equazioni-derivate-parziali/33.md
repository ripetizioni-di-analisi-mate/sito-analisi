For $u_t+u_x=2u$ with $u(0,x)=x$, what expression is obtained?

- $e^{2t}(x-t)$
- $e^{-2t}(x-t)$
- $x-t+2t$
- $e^{2t}(x+t)$

## Explanation

Along the unit-speed characteristic the transported value satisfies $U'=2U$, so it grows by the factor $e^{2t}$. The initial value of $U$ is the given function evaluated at spatial coordinate $x-t$, yielding the required product.
