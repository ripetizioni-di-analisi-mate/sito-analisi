What is the spatial coordinate at time zero of the characteristic of $u_t+xu_x=0$ passing through $(t,x)$?

- $xe^{-t}$
- $xe^t$
- $x-t$
- $x(1-t)$

## Explanation

The characteristic equation is $X'=X$, so the product $e^{-s}X(s)$ is constant. At time $t$ that product is $e^{-t}x$, while at time zero it equals the initial coordinate. The formula also holds on the stationary characteristic.
