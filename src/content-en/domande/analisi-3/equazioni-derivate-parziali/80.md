What is the distributional derivative of $\mathbf{1}_{(0,1)}$ on $(-1,1)$?

- $-\delta_0$
- $0$
- $\delta_{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$
- $\delta_0$

## Explanation

The derivative acts on a test function as minus the integral of $\varphi'$ from zero to one. The test function has compact support in the open interval and vanishes near one, leaving only its value at zero with a positive sign.
