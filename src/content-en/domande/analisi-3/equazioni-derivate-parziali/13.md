On the real line, solve $u_{tt}=25u_{xx}$ with $u(0,x)=x^2$ and $u_t(0,x)=0$.

- $u(t,x)=x^2+25t^2$
- $u(t,x)=x^2-25t^2$
- $u(t,x)=x^2+50t^2$
- $u(t,x)=x^2$

## Explanation

The positive wave speed equals the given parameter. With zero initial velocity, d'Alembert's formula gives the mean of the two shifted initial values, in which the cross terms cancel.

$$
u(t,x)=\frac{(x-5t)^2+(x+5t)^2}2=x^2+25t^2.
$$
