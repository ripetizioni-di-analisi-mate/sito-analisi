For $u_t+3u_x=0$, which point on the line $t=0$ determines $u(2,1)$?

- $x=7$
- $x=-1$
- $x=4$
- $x=-5$

## Explanation

The characteristic has velocity three. Tracing backward from time two and spatial coordinate one subtracts six from the spatial coordinate. This gives $x=-5$, where the initial function must be evaluated.
