For which value of $k$ is $2u_{xx}+4u_{xy}+ku_{yy}=0$ parabolic?

- $k=1$
- $k=2$
- $k=4$
- $k=8$

## Explanation

The off-diagonal entry is half the mixed coefficient, so it is two. The determinant is $2k-4$ and vanishes for $k=2$. At that value the matrix has rank one because its first diagonal entry is nonzero.
