Which polynomial in Cartesian coordinates represents $r^3\cos(3\theta)$?

- $x^3+3xy^2$
- $3x^2y-y^3$
- $x^3-3xy^2$
- $x^3-y^3$

## Explanation

The function is the real part of $(x+iy)^3$. In the expansion, the first real term is $x^3$, and the term quadratic in $iy$ is $-3xy^2$. The terms with odd powers of $i$ contribute only to the imaginary part.
