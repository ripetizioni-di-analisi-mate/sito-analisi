If $X(0)=X(L)=0$ and $X$ is a nonzero solution of $-X''=\lambda X$ with $\lambda>0$, which condition selects the allowed values of $\lambda$?

- $\cos(\sqrt{\lambda}L)=0$
- $\sin(\lambda L)=0$
- $\sin(\sqrt{\lambda}L)=0$
- $\cos(\lambda L)=1$

## Explanation

The left zero boundary value selects the sine with frequency $\sqrt{\lambda}$. For a nonzero solution its coefficient cannot vanish. The right zero boundary value therefore requires the sine itself to vanish at $L$.
