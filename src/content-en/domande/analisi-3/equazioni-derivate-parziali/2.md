Where is the equation $u_{xx}+xu_{yy}=0$ hyperbolic?

- For $x>0$
- For $x<0$
- Only for $x=0$
- For every $x$

## Explanation

The mixed coefficient is zero, so the discriminant is $-x$. For $x<0$ the discriminant is positive and the principal part has two directions of opposite sign. On the line $x=0$ only one second-order direction remains.
