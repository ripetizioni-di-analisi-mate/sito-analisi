What coefficient is obtained by projecting $f(x)=x$ onto $\sin x$ in $L^2(0,\pi)$?

- $\pi$
- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

Integration by parts gives $\int_0^\pi x\sin x\,dx=\pi$. The squared norm of the sine is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Dividing the inner product by this squared norm gives two.
