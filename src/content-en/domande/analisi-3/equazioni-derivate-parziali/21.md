For $u_t+2u_x=0$ on the half-line $x>0$, the data are $u(0,x)=x$ and $u(t,0)=3t$. Which value reaches $(t,x)=(3,2)$?

- $6$
- $2$
- $-4$
- $9$

## Explanation

Following the characteristic of speed $2$ backwards, it reaches $x=0$ after one unit of time. The entry point is $(2,0)$, where the boundary datum equals $3\cdot2=6$. The value stays constant along the characteristic.
