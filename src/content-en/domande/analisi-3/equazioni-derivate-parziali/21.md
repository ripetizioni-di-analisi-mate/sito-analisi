For $u_t+3u_x=0$ on the line, the initial datum $u(0,x)=f(x)$ is positive on $(0,1)$ and zero outside this interval. For which times $t>0$ is $u(t,6)\neq0$?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}<t<2$
- $2<t<\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $5<t<6$
- For no $t>0$

## Explanation

The solution is constant along the characteristics $x-3t=\text{constant}$, hence $u(t,x)=f(x-3t)$.
Therefore $u(t,6)=f(6-3t)$, which is nonzero if and only if $0<6-3t<1$.
The inequality $6-3t<1$ gives $t>\dfrac{5}{3}$ and the inequality $6-3t>0$ gives $t<2$.
Hence $u(t,6)\neq0$ exactly for $\dfrac{5}{3}<t<2$.
The interval $5<t<6$ comes from forgetting the speed $3$, while $2<t<\dfrac{7}{3}$ comes from writing $f(3t-6)$ instead of $f(6-3t)$.
