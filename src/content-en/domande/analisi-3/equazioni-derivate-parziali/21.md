Solve $u_t-u_x=0$ with $u(0,x)=\cos x$ on the real line.

- $\cos(x+t)$
- $\cos(x-t)$
- $e^{-t}\cos x$
- $\cos x+\sin t$

## Explanation

The transport velocity is $-1$, so the initial spatial coordinate is $x+t$. The solution has zero derivative along a characteristic. Thus it equals the initial function evaluated at $x+t$, corresponding to a leftward shift of the profile.
