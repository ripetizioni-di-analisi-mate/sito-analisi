Classify $u_{xx}+2u_{xy}+u_{yy}+u=0$.

- Elliptic
- Hyperbolic
- First order
- Parabolic

## Explanation

Every entry of the principal matrix is one, so the determinant vanishes and the rank is one. The term $u$ is of order zero and does not affect the classification.
