At the corner $(t,x)=(0,0)$, what condition must the initial data $u(0,x)=f(x)$ and boundary data $u(t,0)=g(t)$ satisfy for the solution to be continuous?

- $f'(0)=g'(0)$
- $f(0)=g'(0)$
- $f(0)+g(0)=0$
- $f(0)=g(0)$

## Explanation

Approaching the corner along the initial time slice gives $f(0)$, while approaching along the spatial boundary gives $g(0)$. For continuity, these two limits must agree. This condition relates the values of the two data functions at zero.
