In the rectangle $(0,\pi)\times(0,1)$, solve $\Delta u=0$ with value $\sin(5x)$ on the side $y=1$ and zero on the remaining sides.

- $u(x,y)=\dfrac{\cosh(5y)}{\cosh(5)}\sin(5x)$
- $u(x,y)=y\sin(5x)$
- $u(x,y)=\dfrac{\sinh(5y)}{\sinh(5)}\sin(5x)$
- $u(x,y)=\dfrac{\sinh(5y)}{\sinh(5)}\cos(5x)$

## Explanation

The upper boundary condition normalizes the hyperbolic function. The second spatial derivatives cancel in the sum, and all four boundary conditions hold.

$$
X''=-25X,\quad Y''=25Y,\quad Y(0)=0,\quad Y(1)=1.\qquad Y(y)=\frac{\sinh(5y)}{\sinh(5)}.
$$
