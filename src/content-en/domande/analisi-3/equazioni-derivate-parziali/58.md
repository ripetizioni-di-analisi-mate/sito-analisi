For $u_t=u_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with zero boundary values and initial profile $\sin x$, what is the rate of change of the total mass at time zero?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $0$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The rate of change of the mass is the spatial derivative at the right endpoint minus the spatial derivative at the left endpoint. At time zero, $u_x(0,x)=\cos x$, so the values are $-1$ at $\pi$ and $1$ at zero. Their difference is $-2$.
