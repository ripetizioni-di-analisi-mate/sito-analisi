Which equation is linear in $u$?

- $u_t-u u_{xx}=0$
- $u_t-x^2u_{xx}+t u=x$
- $u_t-(u_x)^2=x$
- $u_t-\sin(u)=0$

## Explanation

The coefficients in $u_t-x^2u_{xx}+t u$ depend only on the independent variables. The unknown $u$ and each of its derivatives occur only to the first power. The given term $x$ is the source and does not affect linearity of the operator.
