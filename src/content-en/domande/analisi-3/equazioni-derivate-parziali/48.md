On $(0,\pi)$, let $u_t=u_{xx}$ have zero endpoint values and $u(0,x)=A\sin x+\sin(2x)$. If $u(1,\dfrac{\pi}{2})=2e^{-1}$, what is $A$?

- $2e^{-1}$
- $2e^{-4}$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

At the midpoint the second spatial sine vanishes and the first equals one. At time one the measured value is therefore $A$ times $e^{-1}$. Dividing it by the decay factor gives two.
