Two infinite strings vibrate according to $u_{tt}=4u_{xx}$ and have the same initial velocity at every point. Their initial displacements coincide outside the interval $[4,6]$ and may differ inside it. At which of the following times can the difference of the two displacements at the point $x=0$ be nonzero?

- $t=1$
- $t=\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $t=\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $t=4$

## Explanation

The difference $w$ of the two displacements solves the same equation, with zero initial velocity and initial displacement $\varphi$ vanishing outside $[4,6]$.
Since the propagation speed is $c=2$, d'Alembert's formula gives $w(t,0)=\dfrac{\varphi(-2t)+\varphi(2t)}{2}$.
For $t>0$ the point $-2t$ is negative, so $\varphi(-2t)=0$ and we are left with $w(t,0)=\dfrac{\varphi(2t)}{2}$.
This value can be nonzero only if $2t\in[4,6]$, that is, for $2\le t\le3$.
Among the proposed times, therefore, only $t=\dfrac{5}{2}$ satisfies the condition.
