On the real line, compute $u(13,x)$ if $u_{tt}=36u_{xx}$, $u(0,x)=x^3$, and $u_t(0,x)=0$.

- $\left(x + 78\right)^{3}$
- $x^{3} + 18252 x$
- $x^{3} + 234 x$
- $\left(x - 78\right)^{3}$

## Explanation

With zero initial velocity, take the average of the two shifted initial values. Expanding the cubes cancels terms odd in the shift and gives $x^{3} + 18252 x$.
