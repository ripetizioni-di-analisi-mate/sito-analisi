Two infinite strings with wave speed $c=2$ have identical initial velocities everywhere. Their initial displacements agree outside $[4,6]$ but may differ inside it. At which of the following times could a difference be observed at $x=0$?

- $t=1$
- $t=\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $t=\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $t=4$

## Explanation

The displacement difference at $x=0$ depends only on the initial values at $-2t$ and $2t$ because the velocity contributions cancel. Among the proposed times, only $t=\dfrac{5}{2}$ puts an endpoint in the modified interval, namely $2t=5$.
