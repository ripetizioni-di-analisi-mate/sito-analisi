If $u$ solves the weak Poisson problem and $w\in H_0^1(\Omega)$, what is $J(u+w)-J(u)$ for $J(v)=\dfrac{1}{2}\int|\nabla v|^2-\int fv$?

- $\int fw$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\int|\nabla w|^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\int|\nabla w|^2-\int fw$
- $\int\nabla u\cdot\nabla w$

## Explanation

Expanding the square gives the cross term $\int\nabla u\cdot\nabla w$ and the linear term $-\int fw$. The weak formulation with test function $w$ shows that these terms cancel. All that remains is one half of the squared gradient norm of $w$.
