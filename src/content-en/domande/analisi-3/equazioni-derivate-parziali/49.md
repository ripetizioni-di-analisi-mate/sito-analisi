On $(0,\pi)$, suppose $u_t=u_{xx}$ with $u(t,0)=2$, $u(t,\pi)=0$ and $u(0,x)=2\left(1-\dfrac{x}{\pi}\right)+\sin x$. What is the solution?

- $2\left(1-\dfrac{x}{\pi}\right)+e^{-t}\sin x$
- $e^{-t}\left[2\left(1-\dfrac{x}{\pi}\right)+\sin x\right]$
- $2\left(1-\dfrac{x}{\pi}\right)+e^{-2t}\sin x$
- $2\left(1-\dfrac{x}{\pi}\right)+\sin x$

## Explanation

The affine profile satisfies the boundary conditions and is stationary because its second derivative is zero. Subtracting it leaves a problem with zero boundary values and initial data $\sin x$. This mode decays at rate one, while the affine profile stays fixed.
