Classify $u_{xx}+2u_{xy}+7u_{yy}=0$ throughout the plane.

- $\text{Parabolic}$
- $\text{Hyperbolic}$
- $\text{Elliptic}$
- $\text{Changes type along }x=0$

## Explanation

The mixed term is written as $2B u_{xy}$, so $B=1$. The diagonal coefficients are one and seven. The discriminant is $1-7=-6$, which is strictly negative. Since the coefficients are constant, the equation is elliptic everywhere.
