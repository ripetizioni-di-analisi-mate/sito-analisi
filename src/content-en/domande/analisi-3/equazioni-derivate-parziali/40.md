How is $4u_{xx}+72u_{xy}+320u_{yy}=0$ classified?

- Elliptic.
- The equation is nonlinear.
- Parabolic.
- Hyperbolic.

## Explanation

The off-diagonal coefficient is half of seventy-two, namely thirty-six. The discriminant is $36^2-4\cdot320=16$ and is positive. Thus the principal matrix has two eigenvalues of opposite signs and the equation is hyperbolic.
