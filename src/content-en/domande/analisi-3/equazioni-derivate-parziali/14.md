Which quadratic form corresponds to the principal part of $3u_{xx}-4u_{xy}+u_{yy}=0$?

- $3\xi^2-2\xi\eta+\eta^2$
- $3\xi^2-4\xi\eta+\eta^2$
- $3\xi^2+4\xi\eta+\eta^2$
- $\xi^2-4\xi\eta+3\eta^2$

## Explanation

The coefficients of the pure derivatives become those of $\xi^2$ and $\eta^2$. The off-diagonal matrix entry is $-2$, and the mixed product appears twice in the quadratic form. Thus its mixed term is $-4\xi\eta$.
