What is $\lim_{t\to+\infty}u(t,x)$ for $u_t=u_{xx}+\sin x$ on $(0,\pi)$ with zero boundary values and $u(0,x)=0$?

- $0$
- $\sin x$
- $-\sin x$
- $x(\pi-x)$

## Explanation

The source has the shape of the first mode, so its time coefficient satisfies $T'+T=1$ with initial value zero. The coefficient is $1-e^{-t}$ and tends to one. The first mode alone satisfies the equation and all the data.
