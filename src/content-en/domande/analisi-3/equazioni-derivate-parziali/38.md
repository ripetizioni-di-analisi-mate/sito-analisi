For $u_t+2xu_x=1$ on the real line with $u(0,x)=x^2$, which function solves the problem?

- $e^{-2t}x^2+t$
- $e^{-4t}x^2+t$
- $e^{4t}x^2+t$
- $e^{-4t}(x^2+t)$

## Explanation

The flow of $X'=2X$ gives the starting coordinate $xe^{-2t}$. The initial value is its square. The source equals one along every characteristic, so the value increases by $t$.
