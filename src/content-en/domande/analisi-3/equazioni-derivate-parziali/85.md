Find the distributional derivative of $\mathbf 1_{(\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{2}{3})}$ on $(0,1)$.

- $\delta_{\dfrac{1}{3}}-\delta_{\dfrac{2}{3}}$
- $\delta_{\dfrac{2}{3}}-\delta_{\dfrac{1}{3}}$
- $\delta_{\dfrac{1}{3}}+\delta_{\dfrac{2}{3}}$
- $0$

## Explanation

By definition, we integrate the negative of the test function's derivative between the endpoints of the interval. The test function's value at the lower endpoint enters with a plus sign and its value at the upper endpoint with a minus sign. These evaluations are Dirac masses at the respective endpoints.
