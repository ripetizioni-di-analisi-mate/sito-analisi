What is the harmonic extension to the unit disk of the boundary values $2+\cos(2\theta)$ on the circle?

- $2+r\cos(2\theta)$
- $2+r^2\cos(2\theta)$
- $2r^2+r^2\cos(2\theta)$
- $2+r^{-2}\cos(2\theta)$

## Explanation

The constant term is already harmonic. The second angular mode has regular radial factor $r^2$ and agrees with the boundary values at $r=1$. The extension is therefore continuous at the center as well and satisfies the equation throughout the disk.
