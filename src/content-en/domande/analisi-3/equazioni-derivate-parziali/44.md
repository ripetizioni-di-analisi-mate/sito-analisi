What is the first positive eigenvalue of $-X''=\lambda X$ on $(0,\pi)$ with $X(0)=0$ and $X'(\pi)=0$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The left endpoint condition selects a sine. Its derivative at $\pi$ is proportional to $\cos(\sqrt{\lambda}\,\pi)$, so the smallest positive frequency making it vanish is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The eigenvalue is the square of this frequency.
