What is the period of the time coefficient of the second wave mode on $(0,\pi)$ with $c=3$, initial displacement $\sin(2x)$ and zero initial velocity?

- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $3\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $6\pi$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is the wave speed times the wavenumber, which gives six. The zero initial velocity selects the cosine. Its period is the time it takes for the argument to increase by $2\pi$, namely $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.
