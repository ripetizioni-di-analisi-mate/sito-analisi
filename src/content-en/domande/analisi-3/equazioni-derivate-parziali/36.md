On $(0,1)$, find the steady solution of $u_t=u_{xx}$ with $u(t,0)=6$ and $u(t,1)=13$.

- $13 - 7 x$
- $7 x^{2} + 6$
- $6 - 7 x$
- $7 x + 6$

## Explanation

A steady profile is independent of time, so the heat equation makes its second derivative zero. It must therefore be affine. The value at zero fixes the intercept at six, and the difference of the endpoint values gives slope seven.
