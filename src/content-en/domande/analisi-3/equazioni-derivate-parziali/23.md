What is $u(t,x)$ for $u_t+2u_x=x$, $u(0,x)=0$ on the real line?

- $tx+t^2$
- $t(x-2t)$
- $tx-t^2$
- $tx$

## Explanation

At time $s$ the spatial coordinate along the path is $x-2(t-s)$. The derivative of $u$ equals this coordinate along the characteristic, so integrate $x-2t+2s$ between zero and $t$. The last term contributes $t^2$.
