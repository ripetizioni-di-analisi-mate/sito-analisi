Which function belongs to $H_0^1(0,1)$?

- $x(1-x)$
- $1$
- $x$
- $\mathbf 1_{(\dfrac{1}{2},1)}$

## Explanation

The polynomial $x(1-x)$ and its derivative belong to $L^2(0,1)$, and its endpoint values vanish. The other functions have either a nonzero trace or an interior jump, which prevents membership in $H^1$.
