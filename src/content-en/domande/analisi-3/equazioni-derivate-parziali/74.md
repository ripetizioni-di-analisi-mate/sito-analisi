In the rectangle $(0,\pi)\times(0,1)$, the boundary values are $\sin x$ on $y=0$ and zero on the other sides. Which vertical factor multiplies $\sin x$?

- $\dfrac{\sinh y}{\sinh 1}$
- $\dfrac{\sinh(1-y)}{\sinh 1}$
- $1-y$
- $e^{-y}$

## Explanation

The vertical factor satisfies $Y''=Y$ with $Y(0)=1$ and $Y(1)=0$. The hyperbolic sine of $1-y$ solves the equation and vanishes on the top edge. Dividing by $\sinh 1$ gives the required value at the bottom.
