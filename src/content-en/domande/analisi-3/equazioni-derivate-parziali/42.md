Which eigenfunction of $-X''=\lambda X$ on $(0,L)$ satisfies $X'(0)=X'(L)=0$ and has eigenvalue zero?

- $X(x)=x$
- $X(x)=1$
- $X(x)=\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{L}\right)$
- $X(x)=\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{L}\right)$

## Explanation

The equation for eigenvalue zero gives an affine function. The zero derivative at both endpoints forces the slope to vanish, so every nonzero constant is an eigenfunction. A nonconstant cosine has positive eigenvalue.
