For $u_t=u_{xx}+e^{-t}\sin x$ on $(0,\pi)$ with zero initial and boundary values, what is the coefficient of the first mode?

- $te^{-t}$
- $(1-e^{-t})e^{-t}$
- $e^{-t}$
- $\dfrac{1-e^{-t}}{2}$

## Explanation

The coefficient $T$ satisfies $T'+T=e^{-t}$, because the source and the second spatial derivative both have the shape of the first mode. Multiplying by $e^t$ gives $(e^tT)'=1$. Integrating from zero and using the zero initial value gives $e^tT=t$ and hence $T=te^{-t}$.
