Solve $u_t+5u_x=0$ on the real line with $u(0,x)=e^{-x^2}$.

- $u(t,x)=e^{-(x-5t)^2}$
- $u(t,x)=e^{-(x+5t)^2}$
- $u(t,x)=e^{-x^2-5t}$
- $u(t,x)=e^{-(x-5t^2)^2}$

## Explanation

The characteristic with velocity five through $(t,x)$ meets the initial line at spatial coordinate $x-5t$. The equation requires the solution to stay constant along that line. Evaluating the initial Gaussian at $x-5t$ therefore gives $e^{-(x-5t)^2}$.
