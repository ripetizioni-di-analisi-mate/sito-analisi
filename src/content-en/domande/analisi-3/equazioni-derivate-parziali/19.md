For $u_{tt}=9u_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with zero endpoint values, $u(0,x)=0$, and $u_t(0,x)=\sin(7x)$, what is the time coefficient of $\sin(7x)$?

- $\sin{\left(21 t \right)}$
- $\cos{\left(21 t \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{\left(21 t \right)}}{21}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{\left(7 t \right)}}{7}$

## Explanation

The mode has angular frequency $21$. The initial amplitude is zero and its initial derivative is one. The coefficient of the sine is the reciprocal of the angular frequency, giving $\dfrac{\sin{\left(21 t \right)}}{21}$.
