What is the elliptic region of the equation $u_{xx}+2yu_{xy}+u_{yy}=0$?

- $|y|>1$
- $y=1$ or $y=-1$
- $y>0$
- $|y|<1$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $y^2-1$ and is negative exactly for $|y|<1$. At the two values of $y$ where it changes sign, it vanishes and the principal part still has rank one.
