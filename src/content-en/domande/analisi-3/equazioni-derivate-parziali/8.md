What is the principal part of $u_{xx}-2u_{xy}+u_{yy}+u_x=0$ in the coordinates $\xi=\dfrac{x-y}{\sqrt{2}}$ and $\eta=\dfrac{x+y}{\sqrt{2}}$?

- $2v_{\eta\eta}$
- $v_{\xi\xi}+v_{\eta\eta}$
- $2v_{\xi\eta}$
- $2v_{\xi\xi}$

## Explanation

The second-order operator is $(\partial_x-\partial_y)^2$. Since $\partial_x-\partial_y=\sqrt{2}\,\partial_\xi$, applying the corresponding first-order operator twice gives $2v_{\xi\xi}$. The term $u_x$ remains first order and does not enter the principal part.
