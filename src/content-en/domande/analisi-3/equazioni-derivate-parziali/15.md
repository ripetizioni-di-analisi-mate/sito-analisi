For $x=0$, which statement holds for the principal part of $x u_{xx}+x u_{yy}+u_y=0$?

- The principal matrix has rank one
- The principal matrix has one positive and one negative eigenvalue
- The principal matrix has rank zero
- The principal matrix is positive definite

## Explanation

On the line $x=0$ both second-order coefficients vanish and the principal matrix is zero. Only the first-order term $u_y$ remains. Parabolic classification requires a principal part of rank one.
