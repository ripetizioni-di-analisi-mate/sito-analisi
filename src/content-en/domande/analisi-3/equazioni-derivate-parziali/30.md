On $x\ge0$, which data determine the value of a solution of $u_t+3u_x=0$ at $(t,x)=(1,5)$?

- The initial value at $x=8$
- The initial value at $x=2$
- The boundary value at time $1$
- The boundary value at time $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Tracing backward reduces the spatial coordinate by three during the available unit of time. At the initial line the coordinate is still positive, equal to two. Thus the characteristic has not met the spatial boundary and the initial data apply.
