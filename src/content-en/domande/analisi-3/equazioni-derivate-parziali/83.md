Solve $-u''=2x$ on $(0,1)$ with $u(0)=u(1)=0$.

- $x-x^3$
- $\dfrac{x^3-x}{3}$
- $\dfrac{x-x^3}{3}$
- $x(1-x)$

## Explanation

Integrating once gives $u'=-x^2+C$ and integrating again gives $u=-\dfrac{x^3}{3}+Cx+D$. The left boundary condition gives $D=0$ and the right boundary condition gives $C=\dfrac{1}{3}$. The resulting polynomial has second derivative $-2x$ and vanishes at both endpoints.
