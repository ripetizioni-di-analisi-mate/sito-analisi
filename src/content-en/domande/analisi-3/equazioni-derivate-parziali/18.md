For $u_t=4u_{xx}$ on $(0,9)$ with zero endpoint values, which formula holds for $E(t)=\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{9}u^2\,dx$?

- $E'(t)=4\int_0^{9}u_x^2\,dx$
- $E'(t)=-4\int_0^{9}u_x^2\,dx$
- $E'(t)=0$
- $E'(t)=-4\int_0^{9}u^2\,dx$

## Explanation

Differentiate the energy and substitute the equation. Integration by parts removes the endpoint term and leaves the negative integral of the squared spatial derivative.
