For $u_t=u_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with zero boundary values and initial profile $\sin(2x)$ of unit amplitude, how long does it take for the amplitude to halve?

- $\dfrac{\log 2}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\log 2}{2}$
- $4\log 2$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The second mode has eigenvalue four, so its amplitude is $e^{-4t}$. Setting $e^{-4t}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and taking logarithms gives $-4t=-\log 2$, from which the required time follows.
