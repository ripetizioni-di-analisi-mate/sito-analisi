For $u(t,x)=\cos(2t)\sin(2x)$, what is the first positive time at which the displacement vanishes throughout the interval?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The spatial profile is nonzero at some points of the interval, so the displacement can vanish everywhere only when the time factor vanishes. The first positive zero of $\cos(2t)$ occurs at $2t=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
