For $u_t-2u_x=0$, the initial function vanishes outside $[1,3]$ and is positive on the open interval. What is the support at time $t=1$?

- $[3,5]$
- $[-1,1]$
- $[1,3]$
- $[-2,2]$

## Explanation

Each initial point moves two units to the left in the given time. Since its value is constant along the characteristic, the set of positivity translates and its closure is the interval $[-1,1]$.
