Compute $\Delta u$ for $u(x,y)=2x^2-5y^2+xy$.

- $0$
- $-3$
- $-6$
- $14$

## Explanation

The second derivative in $x$ is four, and the one in $y$ is minus ten. The mixed term $xy$ is affine in either variable when the other is fixed, so it contributes nothing to either pure second derivative. Their sum is minus six.
