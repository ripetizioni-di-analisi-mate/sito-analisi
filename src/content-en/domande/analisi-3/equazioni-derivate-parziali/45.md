Why does $X''=0$ with $X(0)=X(L)=0$ fail to give a Dirichlet eigenfunction?

- The only affine function vanishing at both endpoints is the zero function
- The equation has only periodic solutions
- The problem requires $X$ to have positive second derivative
- The Dirichlet condition requires slope one at the endpoints

## Explanation

The general solution of $X''=0$ is $Ax+B$. The condition at zero gives $B=0$ and the condition at $L>0$ gives $A=0$. An eigenfunction must be nonzero, so zero is excluded from the Dirichlet spectrum.
