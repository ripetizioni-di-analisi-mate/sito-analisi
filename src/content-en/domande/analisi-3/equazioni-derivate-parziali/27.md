If $u_t+t u_x=0$ and $u(0,x)=x$, what is $u(t,x)$?

- $x+\dfrac{t^2}{2}$
- $x-t$
- $x-\dfrac{t^2}{2}$
- $xe^{-t}$

## Explanation

The characteristic velocity depends only on time. The displacement accumulated between zero and $t$ is the integral of $s$, namely $\dfrac{t^2}{2}$. Subtracting it from the final spatial coordinate gives the argument of the initial function and hence the solution value.
