Solve $u_t=u_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with zero endpoint values and initial data $u(0,x)=\sin(5x)$.

- $u(t,x)=e^{25t}\sin(5x)$
- $u(t,x)=e^{-25t}\cos(5x)$
- $u(t,x)=e^{-25t}\sin(5x)$
- $u(t,x)=\cos(5t)\sin(5x)$

## Explanation

The initial profile is a single Dirichlet eigenfunction. The equation for the time factor gives exponential decay, and the solution satisfies the initial and endpoint values.

$$
X''=-25X,\qquad T'=-25T,\quad T(0)=1,\qquad T=e^{-25t}.
$$
