What value does the harmonic extension of $x$ from the boundary of a rectangle take at the interior point $(a,b)$?

- $b$
- $a+b$
- $0$
- $a$

## Explanation

The function $u(x,y)=x$ has both pure second derivatives equal to zero and matches the prescribed data on the entire boundary. It is continuous on the closed rectangle, so the maximum principle gives uniqueness. Its value at the specified point is $a$.
