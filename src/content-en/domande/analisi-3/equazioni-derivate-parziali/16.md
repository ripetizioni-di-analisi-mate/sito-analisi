Find $u(t,x)$ for $u_t+4u_x=9$ and $u(0,x)=x^2$.

- $9 t + \left(4 t + x\right)^{2}$
- $- 9 t + \left(- 4 t + x\right)^{2}$
- $\left(- 4 t + x\right)^{2} + 9$
- $9 t + \left(- 4 t + x\right)^{2}$

## Explanation

The characteristic ending at $(t,x)$ starts at spatial coordinate $x-4t$, giving initial value $(x-4t)^2$. Along the line the derivative of $u$ is nine, so the increase over time $t$ is $9t$. Add it to the initial value.
