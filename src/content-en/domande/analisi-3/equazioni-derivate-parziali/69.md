If the initial displacement and velocity on the real line are both odd, what is $u(t,0)$ for the wave equation?

- $0$ for every $t$
- The average of $f(ct)$ and $f(0)$
- The value $f(ct)$
- $\dfrac{1}{c}\displaystyle\int_0^{ct}g(s)\,ds$

## Explanation

At zero, d'Alembert's formula samples the initial displacement at the opposite points $-ct$ and $ct$. Their contributions cancel because $f$ is odd. The integral of $g$ over the symmetric interval also vanishes because $g$ is odd.
