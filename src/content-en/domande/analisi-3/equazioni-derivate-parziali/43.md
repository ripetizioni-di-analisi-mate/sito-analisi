What is the coefficient of $\sin(2x)$ in the sine expansion of $f(x)=\sin x+3\sin(2x)$ on $(0,\pi)$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The sines are orthogonal, so only the second contributes to the integral of $f(x)\sin(2x)$. Dividing by the squared norm $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ of $\sin(2x)$ recovers coefficient three.
