Which identity shows that $u(x,y)=e^x\cos y$ is harmonic?

- $u_{xx}=-u$ and $u_{yy}=-u$
- $u_{xx}=u$ and $u_{yy}=u$
- $u_{xx}=u$ and $u_{yy}=-u$
- $u_{xx}=0$ and $u_{yy}=0$

## Explanation

Differentiating twice with respect to $x$ leaves the exponential factor unchanged. Differentiating twice with respect to $y$ multiplies the cosine by $-1$. The two pure second derivatives therefore sum to zero everywhere.
