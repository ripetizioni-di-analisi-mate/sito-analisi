In the rectangle $(0,\pi)\times(0,13)$, the boundary value is $\sin(6x)$ on the top and zero on the other sides. Which factor in $y$ multiplies $\sin(6x)$ in the harmonic solution?

- $- \dfrac{\sinh{\left(6 y - 78 \right)}}{\sinh{\left(78 \right)}}$
- $e^{6 y - 78}$
- $\dfrac{\sin{\left(6 y \right)}}{\sinh{\left(78 \right)}}$
- $\dfrac{\sinh{\left(6 y \right)}}{\sinh{\left(78 \right)}}$

## Explanation

Separation gives an equation with hyperbolic functions as solutions for the vertical factor. Its value must be zero at the bottom and $1$ at the top. The ratio satisfies both conditions.
