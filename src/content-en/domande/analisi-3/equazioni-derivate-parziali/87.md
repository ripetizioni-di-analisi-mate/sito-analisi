For $u\in H_0^1(0,1)$, what estimate follows from $u(x)=\int_0^x u'(s)\,ds$ and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality after integration with respect to $x$?

- $\|u\|_2^2\ge\dfrac{1}{2}\|u'\|_2^2$
- $\|u\|_2=\|u'\|_2$
- $\|u\|_2^2\le\dfrac{1}{2}\|u'\|_2^2$
- $\|u'\|_2^2\le\dfrac{1}{2}\|u\|_2^2$

## Explanation

The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality gives $|u(x)|^2\le x\int_0^x|u'|^2$. Extending the integral up to one gives $|u(x)|^2\le x\|u'\|_2^2$. The factor $x$ integrates to one half over the whole interval, yielding the required norm estimate.
