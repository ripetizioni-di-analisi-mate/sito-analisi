On $(0,\pi)$, suppose $u_{tt}=u_{xx}$ with fixed ends, $u(0,x)=\sin x$ and $u_t(0,x)=2\sin x$. What is the time coefficient?

- $2\cos t+\sin t$
- $\cos t-2\sin t$
- $\cos t+2\sin t$
- $e^{-t}+2\sin t$

## Explanation

The first mode has angular frequency one, so its coefficient is a linear combination of cosine and sine. Evaluating at zero gives one for the cosine coefficient. Differentiating and evaluating at zero gives two for the sine coefficient.
