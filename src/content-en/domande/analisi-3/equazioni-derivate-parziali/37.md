On $H_0^1(0,1)$ with norm $\|u^\prime\|_2$, consider $A(u,v)=\int_0^1 (5+11x)u^\prime v^\prime\,dx$. What is the best coercivity constant?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$
- $16$
- $11$

## Explanation

The coefficient is everywhere at least five, so the form bounds five times the squared norm from below. For every $\varepsilon>0$ choose nonzero smooth functions supported in $(0,\varepsilon)$. On this interval their energy quotient is at most $5+11\varepsilon$, so taking the limit rules out any larger constant.
