In the rectangle $(0,\pi)\times(0,1)$, which two vertical factors give the top-edge data $\sin x+\sin(2x)$ and zero on the other sides?

- $y$ and $y^2$
- $\dfrac{\sinh y}{\sinh 1}$ and $\dfrac{\sinh(2y)}{\sinh 2}$
- $\dfrac{\sinh y}{\sinh 1}$ for both
- $\cosh y$ and $\cosh(2y)$

## Explanation

Harmonicity requires $Y_n''=n^2Y_n$ for each mode. The bottom boundary condition rules out the hyperbolic cosine, and the top boundary condition requires each factor to equal one at $y=1$. The two hyperbolic sines must therefore be divided by different normalizing factors.
