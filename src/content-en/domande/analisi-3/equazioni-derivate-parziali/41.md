For $u_t=u_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with zero endpoint values and $u(0,x)=2\sin x-\sin(3x)$, compute $u(t,\dfrac{\pi}{2})$.

- $2 e^{- t} + e^{- 9 t}$
- $2 e^{- t} - e^{- 9 t}$
- $e^{- t}$
- $e^{- t} + 2 e^{- 9 t}$

## Explanation

Modes one and three decay at rates $1$ and $9$. At the specified point the two sines equal $1$ and $-1$. Their contributions sum to $2 e^{- t} + e^{- 9 t}$.
