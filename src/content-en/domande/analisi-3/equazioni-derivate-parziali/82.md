What is the distributional source $-u''$ for $u(x)=\min\{x,1-x\}$ on $(0,1)$?

- $-2\delta_{\dfrac{1}{2}}$
- $2\delta_{\dfrac{1}{2}}$
- $\delta_{\dfrac{1}{2}}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first derivative is one to the left of the midpoint and minus one to its right, so the jump is $-2$. Its distributional derivative is a point mass of weight $-2$. The operator $-u''$ reverses the sign.
