What value does the harmonic extension of $4+\sin(3\theta)-2\cos\theta$ to the unit disk take at the center?

- $4$
- $2$
- $6$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first and third modes extend into the disk with radial factors $r$ and $r^3$, respectively. Both vanish at the center. The constant term is harmonic and remains unchanged, so only it contributes there.
