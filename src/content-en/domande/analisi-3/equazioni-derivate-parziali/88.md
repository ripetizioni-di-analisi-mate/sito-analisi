For $J(v)=\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^1|v'|^2\,dx-2\int_0^1 v\,dx$, what is $J(x(1-x))$?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The integral of $x(1-x)$ is $\dfrac{1}{6}$, and the integral of the square of its derivative is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The functional takes half the second integral and subtracts twice the first. The result is $-\dfrac{1}{6}$.
