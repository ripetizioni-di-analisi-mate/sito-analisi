What is the eigenvalue $\lambda$ of $-X^\prime\prime=\lambda X$ for mode 6 on $(0,13\pi)$ with homogeneous Dirichlet conditions?

- $\dfrac{169}{36}$
- $\dfrac{36}{13}$
- $\dfrac{6}{13}$
- $\dfrac{36}{169}$

## Explanation

With zero endpoint values the eigenfunctions are sines. The sixth mode completes six half-oscillations on the interval of length $13\pi$, so its spatial frequency is $\dfrac{6}{13}$. The operator $-X''$ multiplies the sine by the squared frequency $\dfrac{36}{169}$.
