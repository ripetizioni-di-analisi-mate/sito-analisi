On $(0,\pi)$, suppose $u_t=u_{xx}$ with zero boundary values and $u(0,x)=3\sin x-2\sin(2x)$. What is $\lim_{t\to+\infty}e^t u(t,x)$?

- $-2\sin(2x)$
- $3\sin x-2\sin(2x)$
- $3\sin x$
- $0$

## Explanation

The two coefficients decay as $e^{-t}$ and $e^{-4t}$ respectively. After multiplication by $e^t$, the first coefficient stays at three and the second gains a factor of $e^{-3t}$. Only the first mode survives in the limit.
