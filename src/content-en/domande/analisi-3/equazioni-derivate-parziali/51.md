What is the solution of $u_t=u_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with insulated ends $u_x(t,0)=u_x(t,\pi)=0$ and initial data $3+\cos x$?

- $e^{-t}(3+\cos x)$
- $3+\cos x$
- $3+e^{-t}\cos x$
- $3+e^{-t}\sin x$

## Explanation

The constant term has zero second derivative, so its coefficient stays at three. The cosine has zero first derivative at the endpoints and its second derivative is its negative. Its coefficient therefore decays at rate one.
