For the wave equation with $c=2$, both initial data vanish outside $[-1,1]$. At which point must the solution be zero at time one?

- $x=2$
- $x=4$
- $x=0$
- $x=-2$

## Explanation

For $x=4$, the interval cut out by the characteristics at time zero is $[2,6]$ and lies entirely outside the support of the initial data. Both endpoint displacement values and the integral of the velocity are therefore zero. At each of the other points the interval intersects the support.
