In the complex space $L^2(-1,1)$, what is the norm of the restriction of $\ell(f)=\int_{-1}^1 f(x)(2-ix)\,dx$ to the odd functions?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{26}{3}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac23}$
- $\dfrac23$
- $0$

## Explanation

The product of an odd function and the constant part integrates to zero on the symmetric interval. The representing vector in the subspace is therefore $ix$, which is odd. Its squared norm is the integral of $x^2$ on $(-1,1)$, equal to two thirds.
