Let $f\in L^2(0,1)$ be real with $\|f\|_2=1$. Which condition allows $\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx=1$?

- $|f|=1$ almost everywhere, with both signs occurring on sets of positive measure.
- $\int_0^1 f(x)^2\,dx=2$.
- $f$ has mean zero.
- $f=1$ almost everywhere.

## Explanation

The constant one has unit norm, so its inner product with $f$ is bounded by the norm of that function. To attain one requires equality in Cauchy–Schwarz, so $f$ is constant almost everywhere. The sign and value of the mean determine the constant.
