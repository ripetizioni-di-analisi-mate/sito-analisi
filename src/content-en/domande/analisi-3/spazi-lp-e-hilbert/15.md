On $[0,1]$, let $f(x)=x$ except that $f(\dfrac12)=8$. What is $\|f\|_\infty$?

- $8$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$

## Explanation

The value eight occurs at a single point, a set of measure zero. Every threshold below one is exceeded on a subinterval of positive measure, however. The least almost-everywhere upper bound on the absolute value is therefore one.
