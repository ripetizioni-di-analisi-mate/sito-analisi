List the indicators of the intervals $[j2^{-k},(j+1)2^{-k})$ level by level, with $k\ge0$ and $0\le j<2^k$. Which conclusion holds on $[0,1)$?

- They converge to one in $L^2$ and pointwise.
- They converge pointwise to zero and have constant norm one.
- They converge to zero in $L^2$, and there is no pointwise limit at any $x$.
- They converge to zero in the $L^\infty$ norm.

## Explanation

The interval lengths halve at each new level, so the L² norm tends to zero. Each point belongs to exactly one interval per level. From any sufficiently large level onward, it therefore sees infinitely many ones and infinitely many zeros.
