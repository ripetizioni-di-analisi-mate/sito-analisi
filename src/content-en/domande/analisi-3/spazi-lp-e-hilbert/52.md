In $L^2(0,1)$, what is the coefficient of $x$ in the projection $a+bx$ of $x^2$ onto $\operatorname{span}\{1,x\}$?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac35$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The generators have nonzero inner product. Orthogonality of the residual to both generators gives $a+\dfrac b2=\dfrac13$ and $\dfrac a2+\dfrac b3=\dfrac14$. Subtracting half the first equation from the second gives $b$ divided by twelve equal to one twelfth.
