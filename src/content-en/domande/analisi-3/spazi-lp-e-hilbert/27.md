For measurable functions $f_n$, if $\sum_{n\ge1}\mu\{|f_n|>2^{-n}\}<\infty$, what follows almost everywhere?

- $f_n(x)\to0$.
- $|f_n(x)|>1$ for infinitely many indices.
- $f_n(x)=0$ for all indices.
- $f_n(x)\to1$.

## Explanation

The union of exceptional sets from index $N$ onward has measure at most the tail of the series. The tail tends to zero, so almost every point exceeds the threshold only finitely often. For subsequent indices the absolute value is at most $2^{-n}$.
