In $\ell^2$, let $M$ be the subspace of finitely supported sequences. For $x=(2^{-n})_{n\ge1}$, which statement is true?

- $d(x,M)>0$ and the first truncation attains the minimum.
- $x$ belongs to $M$.
- $d(x,M)=0$ and no element of $M$ attains that distance.
- The zero vector attains the distance.

## Explanation

The truncations of $x$ belong to $M$, and the squared error is a geometric tail tending to zero. Hence the distance is zero. Every member of $M$ has finite support, whereas all components of $x$ are positive, so no minimizer exists.
