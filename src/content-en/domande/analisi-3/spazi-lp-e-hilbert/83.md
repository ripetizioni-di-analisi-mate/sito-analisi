Let $(e_n)$ be the standard basis of $\ell^2$. In what sense does $e_n$ converge to zero?

- It converges to zero in norm.
- It converges weakly to zero and its norm stays one.
- It converges weakly to $e_1$.
- Its inner product with every fixed vector stays one.

## Explanation

For a fixed vector $y\in\ell^2$, the inner product with $e_n$ is the conjugate of component $y_n$. The components tend to zero since their squared magnitudes are summable. This gives weak convergence to zero, whereas every standard basis vector has unit norm.
