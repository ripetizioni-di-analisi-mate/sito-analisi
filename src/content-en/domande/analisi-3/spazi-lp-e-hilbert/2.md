In the definition of $\|x^4\|_{L^2(0,1)}$, which quantity is under the square root?

- $\int_0^1 x^4\,dx$
- $\int_0^1 x^8\,dx$
- $\left(\int_0^1 x^4\,dx\right)^2$
- $\int_0^1 x^2\,dx$

## Explanation

Square the absolute value of the function before integrating. Since the monomial is real and nonnegative on this interval, its square has degree eight. The integral of that power is the squared norm.
