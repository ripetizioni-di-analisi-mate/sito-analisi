Let $e_1,e_2$ be orthonormal and let $\|u\|=5$. If $|\langle u,e_1\rangle|=4$, what is the largest value of $|\langle u,e_2\rangle|$ allowed by Bessel's inequality?

- $1$
- $9$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The sum of the two squared coefficient magnitudes is at most twenty-five. The first contributes sixteen, leaving at most nine for the square of the second. Taking the square root gives three, and the vector $4e_1+3e_2$ shows this value is attainable.
