In a Hilbert space, $M$ is a closed linear subspace and $q\in M$. What holds if $\|x-q\|=\|x-P_Mx\|$?

- $q=x$ for every $x$
- $P_Mx=0$
- $x$ is orthogonal to $M$
- $q=P_Mx$

## Explanation

The squared distance from $q$ minus the minimum squared distance equals the squared norm of $q-P_Mx$. Equality of the distances makes this norm zero, so the two candidates have zero difference.
