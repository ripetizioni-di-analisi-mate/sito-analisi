If $M\subsetneq N$ are closed linear subspaces of a Hilbert space, which relation holds for every $x$?

- $d(x,N)\ge d(x,M)$
- $d(x,N)=d(x,M)$
- $d(x,N)+d(x,M)=\|x\|$
- $d(x,N)\le d(x,M)$

## Explanation

Every candidate in the smaller subspace also belongs to the larger one. Thus the minimum distance in the larger subspace is at most the distance to the minimizer in the smaller one. The projection theorem guarantees both minima exist.
