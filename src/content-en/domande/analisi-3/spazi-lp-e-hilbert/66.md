For $f(t)=3\sin t+2\cos(2t)$ in $L^2(-\pi,\pi)$, what is the squared error in projecting onto the line spanned by $\sin t$?

- $2\pi$
- $4\pi$
- $9\pi$
- $13\pi$

## Explanation

The product of the sine and cosine is odd, so the modes are orthogonal on the symmetric interval. The projection retains the sine term. The residual is twice the cosine, so its squared norm is four times the squared norm of the cosine.
