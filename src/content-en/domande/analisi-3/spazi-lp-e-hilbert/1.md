Compute $\|x^3\|_{L^2(0,1)}$.

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt7}$
- $\dfrac17$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

For the L² norm, integrate the square of the monomial. This is a power of degree six with an antiderivative of degree seven. Evaluate at the endpoints, then take the positive square root.
