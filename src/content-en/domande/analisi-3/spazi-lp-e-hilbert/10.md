Let $M$ be the subspace of constants in $L^2(0,1)$. Which property justifies $\|x^4-P_Mx^4\|_2^2=\|x^4\|_2^2-\|P_Mx^4\|_2^2$?

- The projection has unit norm.
- The residual is orthogonal to the function $x^4$.
- The residual is a multiple of the projection.
- The residual is orthogonal to the projection.

## Explanation

The constant subspace is closed and contains the projection. The residual is orthogonal to the entire subspace, so the two terms in the decomposition of $x^4$ are orthogonal. The Pythagorean identity then gives the difference of the squared norms.
