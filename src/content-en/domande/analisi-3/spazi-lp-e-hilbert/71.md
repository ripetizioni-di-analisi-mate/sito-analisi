In a complex Hilbert space with inner product linear in the first argument, let $e_1,e_2$ be orthonormal. For $u=ie_1-2e_2$, what is the coefficient $\langle u,e_1\rangle$?

- $i$
- $-i$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

When the inner product is expanded, the contribution from $e_2$ vanishes by orthogonality. The coefficient of the term along $e_1$ factors out unchanged from the linear first argument. The inner product of $e_1$ with itself is one.
