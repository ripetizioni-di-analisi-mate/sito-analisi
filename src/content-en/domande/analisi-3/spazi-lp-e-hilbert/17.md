Let $A,B$ be measurable with $\mu(A)=3$, $\mu(B)=4$, and $\mu(A\cap B)=2$. What is $\|\mathbf1_A-\mathbf1_B\|_2^2$?

- $3$
- $7$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

The difference vanishes on the intersection and outside the union. Its squared magnitude is one only on the two regions belonging to exactly one set. Adding the measures and subtracting twice the measure of the intersection gives three.
