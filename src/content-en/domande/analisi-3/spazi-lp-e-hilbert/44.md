Let $f,g$ be the indicators of disjoint sets of measure one. What is $\big(\int |f+g|^{\frac12}\,d\mu\big)^2$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

Their sum is one on a set of measure two. The pointwise square root leaves zero and one unchanged, so its integral is two. Squaring this integral gives four, greater than the sum of the two individual values.
