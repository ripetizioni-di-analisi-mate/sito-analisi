Let $h\in L^\infty(X)$ with $\|h\|_\infty=3$, and $g\in L^1(X)$ with $\|g\|_1=5$. What upper bound does Hölder's inequality give for $\|hg\|_1$?

- $8$
- $5$
- $15$
- $25$

## Explanation

The absolute value of $h$ is at most three almost everywhere. The absolute product is therefore bounded by three times the absolute value of $g$. This majorant has integral fifteen since the norm of $g$ is five.
