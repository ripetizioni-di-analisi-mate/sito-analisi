Which pair of functions attains equality in $\|f+g\|_2\le\|f\|_2+\|g\|_2$ on $(0,1)$?

- $f(x)=x,\ g(x)=3x$
- $f(x)=x,\ g(x)=-x$
- $f(x)=x,\ g(x)=1$
- $f(x)=x,\ g(x)=1-x$

## Explanation

For $f(x)=x$ and $g(x)=3x$, the function $g$ is a positive multiple of $f$, so their sum is four times the first function. Both sides equal four times its norm. For the nonproportional pairs the inequality is strict, as follows from Cauchy–Schwarz.
