Let $\ell(x)=\langle x,y\rangle$ for an arbitrary vector $y\ne0$. What value does $\ell$ take on the unit vector $\dfrac{iy}{\|y\|}$ when the inner product is linear in the first argument?

- $-i\|y\|$
- $\|y\|$
- $i\|y\|$
- $i\|y\|^2$

## Explanation

The factor $i$ is in the first argument and therefore factors out without conjugation. The inner product of the representative with itself is its squared norm. Dividing by the norm of $y$ leaves one power of the norm multiplied by $i$.
