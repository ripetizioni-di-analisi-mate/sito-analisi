What bound does Cauchy–Schwarz give for $\int_0^1 x^{-\frac14}x\,dx$ using the L² norms of the factors?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt6}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac23}$
- $\dfrac2{\sqrt3}$

## Explanation

The singular factor has square $x^{-\frac12}$ with integral two. The other factor has square $x^2$ with integral one third. Both belong to $L^2$, so the product of the square roots of these integrals is the required bound.
