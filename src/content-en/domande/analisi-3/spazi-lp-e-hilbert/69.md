Which property does the sequence $a_n=\dfrac1n$ have for $n\ge1$?

- It belongs to $\ell^1$ but not to $\ell^2$.
- It belongs to $\ell^2$ but not to $\ell^1$.
- It belongs to both $\ell^1$ and $\ell^2$.
- It belongs to neither space.

## Explanation

The series of absolute values is the divergent harmonic series. The series of squared absolute values is a p-series with exponent two and converges. This second series gives membership in the space of square-summable sequences.
