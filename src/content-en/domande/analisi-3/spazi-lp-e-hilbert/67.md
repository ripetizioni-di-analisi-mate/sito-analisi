For $u=(2^{-n})_{n\ge1}\in\ell^2$, which sequence is the projection onto $\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_2\}$?

- $\left(1,\dfrac12,0,\ldots\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac12,\dfrac14,0,\ldots\right)$
- $\left(0,0,\dfrac18,\ldots\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac12,\dfrac14,\dfrac18,\ldots\right)$

## Explanation

The inner product with each standard basis vector returns the corresponding coordinate. The first two standard basis vectors form an orthonormal basis of the subspace, so the projection retains coordinates one and two and vanishes on the rest.
