In Euclidean $\mathbb R^3$, if $v_n\rightharpoonup v$, what follows?

- The distance $\|v_n-v\|$ must remain positive.
- A norm limit exists only if $v=0$.
- $v_n$ converges in norm to $v$.
- Only convergence of the first coordinate can be deduced.

## Explanation

Inner products with the three standard basis vectors give the three coordinates. Weak convergence therefore makes each coordinate of the difference tend to zero. The squared norm is a finite sum of their squares and hence tends to zero.
