For which $p\in[1,\infty)$ does $x^{-\frac15}\in L^p(0,1)$ hold?

- $1\le p\le5$
- $p>5$
- $p\ge5$
- $1\le p<5$

## Explanation

Integrate the power with exponent $-\dfrac p5$. Integrability at the origin requires an exponent strictly greater than minus one. At equality the antiderivative is logarithmic, so the critical value is excluded.
