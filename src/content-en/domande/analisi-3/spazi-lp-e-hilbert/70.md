In $\ell^2$, consider the system $(e_{2n})_{n\ge1}$. Which vector shows that its span is not dense in $\ell^2$?

- $e_2$
- $e_4+e_6$
- The zero vector.
- $e_1$

## Explanation

The first standard basis vector has odd index and is orthogonal to all the even-indexed vectors. Every finite linear combination of the latter remains orthogonal to $e_1$. Its distance from their span is one, so the span is not dense.
