An orthonormal system in $\mathbb R^2$ contains just one vector $e_1$. If $x$ has norm one and is orthogonal to $e_1$, what holds?

- Bessel's inequality gives $0\le1$ with strict inequality.
- Parseval's identity gives $0=1$.
- The coefficient of $x$ along $e_1$ is one.
- The projection of $x$ onto the line spanned by $e_1$ is $x$.

## Explanation

Orthogonality makes the only coefficient zero. The sum of squared coefficients is thus zero while the input has squared norm one. Bessel's inequality holds even for an incomplete system and is strict in this case.
