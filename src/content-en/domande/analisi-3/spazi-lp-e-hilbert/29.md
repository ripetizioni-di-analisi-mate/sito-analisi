Which sequence shows that convergence in measure on $(0,1)$ can hold without convergence in $L^1$?

- $\dfrac1n\mathbf1_{(0,1)}$
- $n\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}$
- $\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt n}\mathbf1_{(0,1)}$

## Explanation

For $n\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}$ the support shrinks and its measure tends to zero. The absolute value therefore exceeds any fixed positive threshold only on sets whose measures tend to zero. The integral nevertheless stays one because the height grows as the reciprocal of the width.
