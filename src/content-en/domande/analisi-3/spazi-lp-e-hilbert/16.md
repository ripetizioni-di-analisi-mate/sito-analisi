Let $f(x)=\log x$ on $(0,1)$. What is the essential supremum of its absolute value?

- It is zero.
- It is one.
- It is infinite.
- It is two.

## Explanation

For any positive threshold $M$ the interval $(0,e^{-M})$ has positive length. The absolute value of the logarithm exceeds $M$ at every point of that interval. Thus every finite upper bound fails on a set of positive measure.
