For disjoint measurable $A$ and $B$ with $\mu(A)=\mu(B)=1$, what is $\|\mathbf1_A+\mathbf1_B\|_4$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $2^{\frac14}$

## Explanation

The indicators sum to one on the union and zero elsewhere, so the fourth power equals the sum itself. The integral equals the measure of the union, two, and the norm is its fourth root.
