Let $(e_n)$ be an orthonormal system with $M=\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{e_n\}$. For which $x$ does the sum $\sum_n|\langle x,e_n\rangle|^2$ equal $\|x\|^2$?

- Exactly for $x\in M^\perp$.
- Only for the zero vector.
- For all $x$, even when $M$ is a proper subspace.
- Exactly for $x\in M$.

## Explanation

The sum of squared coefficient magnitudes is the squared norm of the projection onto $M$ because the system is complete in that subspace. By the Pythagorean identity, the difference from the original squared norm is the squared residual norm. Thus equality holds precisely when the residual is zero.
