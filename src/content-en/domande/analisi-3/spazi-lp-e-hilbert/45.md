In $\mathbb R^2$ with its Euclidean inner product, which vector is the projection of $(3,1)$ onto the line $y=x$?

- $(1,1)$
- $(1,-1)$
- $(3,3)$
- $(2,2)$

## Explanation

The line is spanned by $(1,1)$, whose squared norm is two. Its inner product with the given point is four. Dividing gives coefficient two, which multiplies the generator to produce the projection.
