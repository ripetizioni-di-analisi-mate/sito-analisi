For $\ell(f)=\int_0^1 f(x)x^4\,dx$ in $L^2(0,1)$, which function has norm one and attains $|\ell(f)|=\|\ell\|$?

- $f(x)=3x^4$
- $f(x)=x^4$
- $f(x)=9x^4$
- $f(x)=1$

## Explanation

The representing vector is the real weight. Its norm is one third because its square has integral one ninth. Multiplication by three gives a unit vector proportional to the weight, so equality holds in Cauchy–Schwarz.
