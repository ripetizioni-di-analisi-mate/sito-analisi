For $f(x)=x^{-\frac12}$ and $g(x)=1$ on $(0,1)$, which statement is true?

- Both factors belong to $L^2$.
- The product does not belong to $L^1$.
- Neither factor belongs to $L^2$.
- The product belongs to $L^1$ and $f$ does not belong to $L^2$.

## Explanation

The product equals the singular power, whose exponent is greater than minus one, so its integral is finite. The square of the function, however, is the reciprocal of $x$, whose logarithmic antiderivative diverges at the origin.
