In a Hilbert space, $x_n\rightharpoonup x$ and $\liminf_n\|x_n\|=3$. Which property necessarily follows?

- $\|x\|\ge3$
- $\|x\|\le3$
- $\|x\|=3$
- $\|x\|=0$

## Explanation

If $x$ is zero, the estimate follows from nonnegativity. Otherwise test weak convergence against the normalized vector $x$. The inner product tends to the norm of the limit, and Cauchy–Schwarz bounds its absolute value by the norm of the corresponding sequence term.
