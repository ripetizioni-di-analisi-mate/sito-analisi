On $(0,1)$, let $u_n(x)=n^{\frac14}\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}(x)$. For which pair of spaces does the sequence converge to zero in the first but have constant norm in the second?

- $L^2,\ L^4$
- $L^4,\ L^8$
- $L^4,\ L^2$
- $L^2,\ L^8$

## Explanation

The absolute value raised to $p$ equals $n^{\frac p4}$ on a support of length $\dfrac1n$. Integration and the $p$th root give norm $n^{\frac14-\frac1p}$. The exponent is negative for $p=2$, zero for $p=4$, and positive for $p=8$.
