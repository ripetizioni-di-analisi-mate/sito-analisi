In $L^2(0,1)$, project $x$ onto $\operatorname{span}\{1\}$. Which integral of the residual gives the squared distance?

- $\int_0^1(x-\dfrac12)^2\,dx$
- $\int_0^1(x-\dfrac12)\,dx$
- $\int_0^1|x-\dfrac12|\,dx$
- $\int_0^1(x^2-\dfrac12)^2\,dx$

## Explanation

The projection is the mean, one half. Subtracting this constant gives the residual. Its squared norm integrates its squared absolute value, while the signed integral of the residual vanishes by orthogonality.
