Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence in $L^2$ with $\|f_{n+1}-f_n\|_2\le2^{-n}$. What does completeness ensure?

- The sequence must converge to zero in norm.
- The sequence converges in norm to an element of $L^2$.
- All terms have norm one.
- The differences are pairwise orthogonal.

## Explanation

By the triangle inequality, the distance between two terms is bounded by the sum of the norms of the intervening differences. The geometric tail tends to zero, so the sequence is Cauchy. Completeness gives a limit in the same space.
