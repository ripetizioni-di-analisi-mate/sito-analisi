Which constant is the projection of $x^3$ onto the constants in $L^2(0,1)$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac17$

## Explanation

The residual must have zero inner product with the constant one. This inner product is the integral of the residual. Since the interval has length one, the required constant is the integral of the cubic monomial.
