What is the squared distance from $x^3$ to the constants in $L^2(0,1)$?

- $\dfrac17$
- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac9{112}$
- $\dfrac{23}{112}$

## Explanation

The constant projection is one quarter, the mean of the function. Orthogonality of the residual allows us to subtract the squared norm of the projection from the squared norm of the original function. The difference is $\dfrac17-\dfrac1{16}=\dfrac9{112}$.
