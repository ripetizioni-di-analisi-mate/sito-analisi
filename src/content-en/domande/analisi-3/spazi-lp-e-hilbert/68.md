For $u=(2^{-n})_{n\ge1}$, by what factor does the distance from $u$ change when the projection is extended from the first $N$ to the first $N+1$ standard basis vectors?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $2$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt2}$

## Explanation

The residual contains the coordinates after the retained ones. In the sum of squares, advancing the starting index of the tail by one multiplies every term by one quarter. The distance is the square root of the sum, so it is multiplied by one half.
