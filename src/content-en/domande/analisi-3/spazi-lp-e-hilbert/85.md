In a Hilbert space, let $x\ne0$. Suppose $x_n\rightharpoonup x$ and $\|x_n\|\to\|x\|$. What limit follows?

- $\|x_n-x\|\to0$.
- $\|x_n-x\|\to2\|x\|$.
- $\langle x_n,x\rangle\to0$.
- Necessarily $\|x_n-x\|\to\|x\|$.

## Explanation

In the expansion of the squared distance, the cross term involves twice the real part of $\langle x_n,x\rangle$. Weak convergence makes this inner product tend to the squared norm of $x$. The two squared norms converge to the same quantity, so the squared distance tends to zero.
