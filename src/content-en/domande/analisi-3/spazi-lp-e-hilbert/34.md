Let $f,g\in L^3(X)$ with $\|f\|_3=2$ and $\|f+g\|_3=7$. Which inequality for $\|g\|_3$ follows from Minkowski's inequality?

- $\|g\|_3\le5$
- $\|g\|_3\ge5$
- $\|g\|_3\ge9$
- $\|g\|_3=7$

## Explanation

The norm of a sum is at most the sum of the two norms. Substitution gives seven at most two plus the norm of $g$. Subtracting two gives the required lower bound.
