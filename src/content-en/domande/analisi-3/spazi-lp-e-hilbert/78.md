In Euclidean $\mathbb C^2$, find the norm of $\ell(z_1,z_2)=z_1+2iz_2$.

- $3$
- $5$
- $2$
- $\sqrt5$

## Explanation

The functional is the inner product with $(1,-2i)$, since the second argument is conjugated. The Riesz representation theorem identifies the functional norm with the vector norm. The squared magnitudes of the components are one and four.
