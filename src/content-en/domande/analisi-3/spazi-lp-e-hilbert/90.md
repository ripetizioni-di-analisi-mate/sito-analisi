Let $x_n\rightharpoonup x$ in a Hilbert space. If $M=\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_2\}$ with $e_1,e_2$ orthonormal, which conclusion holds?

- Necessarily $\|P_Mx_n\|\to0$.
- Eventually $P_Mx_n=x_n$.
- $\|P_Mx_n-P_Mx\|\to0$.
- Necessarily $\|x_n-x\|\to0$.

## Explanation

The two projection coefficients are inner products with fixed vectors, so they converge to the corresponding coefficients of the projection of the limit. The squared norm of the difference of the projections is the sum of the squared absolute values of the two coefficient differences. Since there are only two terms, this sum tends to zero.
