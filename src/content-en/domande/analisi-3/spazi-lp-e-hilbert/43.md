Which positive multiple of $g(x)=1$ has norm one in $L^2(-2,2)$?

- $\dfrac14g$
- $\dfrac12g$
- $2g$
- $g$

## Explanation

The squared norm of the constant function one is the integral of one over the interval. Its length is four, so the norm is two. Divide the function by that norm to normalize it.
