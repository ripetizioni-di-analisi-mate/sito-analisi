On $\ell^2$, let $\ell_n(x)=x_n$ for a fixed index $n\ge2$. What is $\|\ell_n\|$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $\sqrt n$
- $\dfrac1n$

## Explanation

The absolute value of one coordinate is at most the square root of the sum of all squared coordinate magnitudes. The functional is therefore continuous with norm at most one. It attains one at the standard unit vector with index $n$.
