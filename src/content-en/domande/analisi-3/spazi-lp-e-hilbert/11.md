Compute the norm of $\ell(f)=\int_0^1f(x)x^3\,dx$ in $L^2(0,1)$.

- $\dfrac1{\sqrt7}$
- $\dfrac17$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$

## Explanation

The weight is real and square-integrable, so it is the representing vector. By the Riesz representation theorem the functional has the same norm as its representative. Its squared norm is the integral of the sixth power.
