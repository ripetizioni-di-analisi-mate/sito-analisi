Let $u,v$ be unit vectors in a complex Hilbert space with $|\langle u,v\rangle|=1$. What follows?

- $u$ and $v$ are orthogonal.
- Necessarily $u=v$.
- $u$ and $v$ are linearly dependent.
- Necessarily $u=-v$.

## Explanation

The absolute inner product equals the product of the norms. Thus equality holds in Cauchy–Schwarz, which for nonzero vectors is equivalent to linear dependence. The proportionality factor can have any phase but must have absolute value one.
