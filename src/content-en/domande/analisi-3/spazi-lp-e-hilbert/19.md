On a space of total measure $9$, what is the optimal constant $C$ satisfying $\|f\|_1\le C\|f\|_2$ for every $f\in L^2$?

- $9$
- $1$
- $3$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

Cauchy–Schwarz applied to the absolute value of the function and the constant one gives the bound with the L² norm of the constant. That norm is the square root of the total measure. The constant function one attains equality, so the factor is optimal.
