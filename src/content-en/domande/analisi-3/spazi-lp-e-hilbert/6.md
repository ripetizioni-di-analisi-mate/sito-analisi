Set $f_n=n^{\frac15}\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}$. What is $\|f_n\|_1$ on $(0,1)$ for $n\ge2$?

- $n^{-\frac15}$
- $n^{-\frac45}$
- $n^{\frac15}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The norm exponent is one, so the integral of the absolute value uses the amplitude directly. The function is constant on an interval of length $\dfrac1n$ and zero elsewhere. Multiplying the two powers of $n$ adds their exponents $\dfrac15$ and $-1$.
