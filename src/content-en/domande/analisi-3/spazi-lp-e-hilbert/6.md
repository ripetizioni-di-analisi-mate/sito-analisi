On the line with Lebesgue measure let $f_n=n^{-1}\mathbf1_{(0,n)}$. Which pair describes convergence to zero in $L^\infty$ and $L^1$, in that order?

- No in $L^\infty$, yes in $L^1$.
- Yes in $L^\infty$, no in $L^1$.
- Yes in both.
- No in either.

## Explanation

The height is $\dfrac{1}{n}$, so the essential supremum norm tends to zero. The support has length $n$, keeping the integral norm at $1$. The underlying space has infinite measure.
