In $L^2(-1,1)$, project an even function $f$ onto $\operatorname{span}\{1,x\}$. What is the coefficient of $x$?

- One for every even $f$.
- The mean of $f$ on $(-1,1)$.
- The squared norm of $f$.
- Zero for every even $f$.

## Explanation

The generators are orthogonal because $x$ is odd and the interval symmetric. The product $fx$ is also odd and hence has zero integral. The coefficient of the generator $x$ is this integral divided by the squared norm of $x$.
