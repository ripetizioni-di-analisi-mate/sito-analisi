For $g_n=n\mathbf1_{(0,n^{-3})}$ on $(0,1)$, which $p\in[1,\infty)$ give convergence to zero in $L^p$?

- $1\le p\le3$
- $p>3$
- $1\le p<3$
- $1\le p<2$

## Explanation

The absolute value raised to $p$ equals $n^p$ on an interval of length $n^{-3}$. The integral is therefore $n^{p-3}$. It tends to zero precisely when the exponent is negative. When the norm exponent is three, the integral stays constant.
