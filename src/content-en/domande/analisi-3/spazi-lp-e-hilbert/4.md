For $f(x)=x^{-\frac16}$ on $(0,1)$, what is $\int_\varepsilon^1|f(x)|^6\,dx$ for $0<\varepsilon<1$?

- $\log\varepsilon$
- $1-\varepsilon$
- $\dfrac1{\varepsilon}-1$
- $-\log\varepsilon$

## Explanation

Taking the sixth power of the absolute value gives the integrand $x^{-1}$. On this positive interval its antiderivative is the natural logarithm, which vanishes at one. Subtracting the value at the lower endpoint gives a positive result that diverges as that endpoint tends to zero.
