In the complex space $L^2(0,1)$, which vector represents $\ell(f)=\int_0^1 f(x)(2+ix)\,dx$ when the inner product is linear in its first argument?

- $2+ix$
- $-2+ix$
- $2-ix$
- $-2-ix$

## Explanation

To express the functional as an inner product, compare the integrand with $f$ multiplied by the conjugate of the representing vector. The given weight is therefore that conjugate. Conjugating the weight leaves its real part unchanged and reverses the sign of its imaginary part.
