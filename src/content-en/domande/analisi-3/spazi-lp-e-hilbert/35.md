On a measure space, if $f\in L^3$ and $\|f\|_3=4$, what is $\big\||f|^2\big\|_{\frac32}$?

- $16$
- $8$
- $4$
- $64$

## Explanation

The absolute value of $|f|^2$ raised to the power three halves is $|f|^3$. Its integral is sixty-four by the given L³ norm. Raising this integral to the power two thirds gives the norm with exponent three halves.
