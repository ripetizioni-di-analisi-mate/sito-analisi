In $\mathbb C$ with $\langle u,v\rangle=u\overline v$, what is $\langle1,i\rangle$?

- $i$
- $1$
- $0$
- $-i$

## Explanation

The second argument is conjugate-linear under this convention. The conjugate of the imaginary unit is its negative. Since the first argument equals one, the product equals that conjugate. Interchanging the arguments would conjugate the result.
