Let $f_n\rightharpoonup f$ in the real space $L^2(0,1)$. Which quantity must converge to its corresponding value for $f$?

- $\int_0^1 |f_n(x)|^2\,dx$
- $\|f_n-f\|_2$
- $\int_0^1 x^2 f_n(x)\,dx$
- The point value $f_n(\dfrac12)$.

## Explanation

The weight $x^2$ is a fixed vector in $L^2$ since its square is integrable on the interval. The specified integral is the inner product with that vector and therefore converges by weak convergence. The squared norm instead involves an inner product whose two arguments both vary.
