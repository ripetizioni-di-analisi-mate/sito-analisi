On a space $X$ with $0<\mu(X)<\infty$, suppose $|g_n|\le 2$ almost everywhere and $g_n\to0$ in measure. What follows?

- Necessarily $\|g_n\|_\infty\to0$.
- $g_n$ eventually vanishes almost everywhere.
- $\|g_n\|_1\to2\mu(X)$.
- $\|g_n\|_p\to0$ for every $1\le p<\infty$.

## Explanation

To bound the integral of the power, split off the set where the absolute value exceeds a threshold. On this set use the uniform bound, and its measure tends to zero. On the complement a small threshold makes the integral small because the total measure is finite.
