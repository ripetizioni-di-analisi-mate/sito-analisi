If $p=4$, which exponent $q$ gives a conjugate pair for Hölder's inequality?

- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac34$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Conjugate exponents have reciprocals summing to one. The reciprocal of four is one quarter, so the reciprocal of the other exponent is three quarters. Inverting gives four thirds.
