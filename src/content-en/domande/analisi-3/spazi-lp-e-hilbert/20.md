If $\mu(X)=8$ and $\|f\|_6=1$, what upper bound does Hölder's inequality give directly for $\|f\|_2$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

The bound on a finite measure space raises the total measure to the exponent $\dfrac12-\dfrac16=\dfrac13$. Substituting the given sixth-power norm gives the cube root of eight. A constant function of unit L⁶ norm attains the bound.
