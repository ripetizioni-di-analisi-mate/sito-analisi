In $L^2(0,1)$, let $M=\operatorname{span}\{x\}$. For $f(x)=x^5$, what is $c$ if $P_M f=cx$?

- $\dfrac17$
- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac73$

## Explanation

The numerator of the projection coefficient is the integral of the product of $x^5$ and $x$. Integrating the sixth power gives one seventh. The denominator is the integral of $x^2$, equal to one third, so the ratio is three sevenths.
