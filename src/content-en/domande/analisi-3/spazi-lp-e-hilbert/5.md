For $n\ge2$, compute $\|n^{\frac14}\mathbf1_{(0,\frac1n)}\|_{L^4(0,1)}$.

- $n^{\frac14}$
- $n^{-\frac14}$
- $\dfrac1n$
- $1$

## Explanation

Taking the fourth power leaves the indicator unchanged. The fourth power of the amplitude is $n$, so the integral is this value times the interval length $\dfrac1n$. Its fourth root is again one.
