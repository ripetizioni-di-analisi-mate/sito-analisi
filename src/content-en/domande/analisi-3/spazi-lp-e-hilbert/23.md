If $\|f\|_3=2$, what upper bound does Markov's inequality give for $\mu\{|f|>4\}$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac1{32}$

## Explanation

Where the absolute value exceeds four, its cube exceeds sixty-four. The full integral of the cubed absolute value is eight because the L³ norm is two. Dividing by the cube of the threshold gives the upper bound.
