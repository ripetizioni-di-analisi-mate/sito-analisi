In $L^2(-\pi,\pi)$, which positive multiple of $e^{-3it}$ has norm one?

- $\dfrac{e^{-3it}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$
- $\dfrac{e^{-3it}}{2\pi}$
- $\dfrac{e^{-3it}}{\sqrt\pi}$
- $e^{-3it}$

## Explanation

The exponential has absolute value one everywhere, so its squared norm is the interval length. To normalize it, divide by the square root of that length. The sign of the frequency does not affect the absolute value.
