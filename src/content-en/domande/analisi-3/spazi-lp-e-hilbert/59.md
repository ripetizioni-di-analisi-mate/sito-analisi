In $\mathbb R^2$, which of the following vectors has zero projection onto $\operatorname{span}\{(2,1)\}$?

- $(2,1)$
- $(1,2)$
- $(1,-2)$
- $(2,-1)$

## Explanation

Projection onto a line is zero exactly for vectors orthogonal to a generator. The Euclidean inner product of $(1,-2)$ and $(2,1)$ is two minus two, hence zero. All other proposed vectors give nonzero inner products.
