For a nonzero continuous linear functional $\ell$ with representative $y$, what relation holds between $\ker\ell$ and $y$?

- $\ker\ell=\{y\}^\perp$.
- $\ker\ell=\operatorname{span}\{y\}$.
- $\ker\ell$ contains $y$.
- $\ker\ell=H$.

## Explanation

By representation, the value at $x$ is the inner product of $x$ with $y$. Thus membership in the kernel is equivalent to having zero inner product with $y$. The representative itself gives a positive value equal to its squared norm.
