In $\ell^2$, why does $u_n=(-1)^ne_1$ fail to converge weakly?

- The norms tend to infinity.
- Every inner product with a fixed vector is zero.
- The inner product with $e_2$ is $(-1)^n$.
- The inner product with $e_1$ alternates between two values.

## Explanation

Weak convergence requires convergence of the inner products with all fixed vectors. The vector $e_1$ selects the first coordinate, which alternates between one and minus one. This single test vector rules out a weak limit.
