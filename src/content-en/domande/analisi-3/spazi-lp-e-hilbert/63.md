In a complex orthonormal basis, $u$ has coefficients $2,-i,3$ in the first three positions and zero elsewhere. What is $\|u\|^2$?

- $12$
- $13$
- $14$
- $36$

## Explanation

By Parseval's identity, sum the squared magnitudes of all the coefficients. The imaginary coefficient has absolute value one, so its contribution is positive. The two real coefficients contribute four and nine. The three contributions sum to fourteen.
