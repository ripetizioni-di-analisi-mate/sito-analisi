If $\|x\|^2=18$ and the first three coefficients in an orthonormal system are $1,2,2$, what is the residual norm after projection onto the three vectors?

- $9$
- $\sqrt{18}$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The squared projection norm is the sum of the three squared coefficients, namely nine. The residual is orthogonal to the projection, so its squared norm is eighteen minus nine. Its norm is the square root of the result.
