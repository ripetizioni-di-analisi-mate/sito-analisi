In $\mathbb C^2$ with the inner product linear in its first argument, find $c$ such that $P_M(1,2)=c(1,i)$ for $M=\operatorname{span}\{(1,i)\}$.

- $\dfrac{1+2i}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1-2i}{2}$
- $1-2i$
- $\dfrac{2-i}{2}$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the inner product of the input with the generator divided by the generator's squared norm. In the numerator the generator's second coordinate is conjugated, giving $-i$. The denominator is two.
