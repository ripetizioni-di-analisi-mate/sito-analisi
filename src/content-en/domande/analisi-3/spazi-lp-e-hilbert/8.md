In $L^2(0,1)$, let $M$ be the subspace of constants and let $c$ be the value of the constant representative of $P_M(x^4)$. Where does $r(x)=x^4-c$ vanish?

- At $x=\dfrac15$.
- At $x=5^{-\frac14}$.
- At $x=\dfrac14$.
- At $x=5^{-\frac12}$.

## Explanation

The constant projection equals the mean of the monomial, one fifth. The residual vanishes where the fourth power of $x$ equals that mean. The positive interval admits a single fourth root, giving the required point.
