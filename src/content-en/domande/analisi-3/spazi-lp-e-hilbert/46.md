In Euclidean $\mathbb R^3$, what is the distance from $(1,-2,4)$ to the plane $z=0$?

- $\sqrt{21}$
- $4$
- $\sqrt5$
- $16$

## Explanation

The plane consists of vectors with third coordinate zero. Matching the input's first two coordinates leaves a residual along the third axis, orthogonal to the plane. The distance is therefore the absolute value of its third coordinate.
