For orthonormal $e_1,e_2$, compute $\|3e_1+4e_2\|^2$.

- $25$
- $7$
- $5$
- $49$

## Explanation

When the inner product of the sum with itself is expanded, orthogonality makes the cross terms vanish. Each vector has norm one, so the remaining contributions are the squares of the coefficients. They sum to twenty-five.
