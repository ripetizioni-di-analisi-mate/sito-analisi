In $\mathbb R^2$ with norm $\ell^1$, for $u=(1,0)$ and $v=(0,1)$, what are the two sides of the parallelogram identity?

- The side containing $u\pm v$ is $8$ and the other is $4$.
- Both equal $4$.
- The side containing $u\pm v$ is $4$ and the other is $8$.
- Both equal $8$.

## Explanation

The sum norm assigns the value two to both $u+v$ and $u-v$. Their squared norms sum to eight. Each original vector has norm one, so twice the sum of their squared norms is four.
