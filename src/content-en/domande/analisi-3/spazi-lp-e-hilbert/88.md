In $\ell^2$, $e_n\rightharpoonup0$ and $\langle e_n,e_n\rangle=1$. Why are the two statements compatible?

- Weak convergence implies that $\|e_n\|\to0$.
- Weak convergence requires a limit only for a fixed second argument.
- Every inner product with a variable argument tends to zero.
- The inner product vanishes when its arguments coincide.

## Explanation

The definition fixes the test vector before varying the index. If $e_n$ is also used in the second argument, the test vector varies with the first. This inner product is the squared norm, which is constant for an orthonormal sequence.
