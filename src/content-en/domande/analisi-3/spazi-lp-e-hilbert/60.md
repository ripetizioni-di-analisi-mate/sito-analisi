Let $M$ be a closed linear subspace of a Hilbert space. If $P_Mx=y$ and $z\in M$, which vector is the projection of $x+z$ onto $M$?

- $y$
- $z$
- $x+y$
- $y+z$

## Explanation

The candidate $y+z$ lies in the subspace by linearity. Subtracting it from $x+z$ gives the same residual $x-y$, already orthogonal to $M$. Orthogonality characterizes the unique projection, so this candidate is the projection.
