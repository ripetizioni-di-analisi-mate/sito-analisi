In a real Hilbert space suppose $\|u+v\|^2=25$ and $\|u-v\|^2=9$. Compute $\langle u,v\rangle$.

- $8$
- $16$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

Expanding the squared norm of the sum gives twice the inner product with positive sign. The squared norm of the difference has the opposite cross term. Subtracting cancels the individual squared norms and leaves four times the inner product.
