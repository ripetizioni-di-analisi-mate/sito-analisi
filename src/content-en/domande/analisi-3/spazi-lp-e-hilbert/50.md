Let $M$ be a closed linear subspace of a Hilbert space. If $P_Mx=y$ and $\|x\|=13$, $\|y\|=5$, what is the distance from $x$ to $M$?

- $12$
- $8$
- $144$
- $18$

## Explanation

The residual $x-y$ is orthogonal to $M$ and thus to $y$. By the Pythagorean identity the squared distance is the difference of the two given squared norms. The difference is one hundred forty-four, and the distance is its square root.
