On $(0,1)$, apply Hölder's inequality with $p=3$, $q=\dfrac32$, and $f(x)=x^2$. Which choice of $g$ gives equality?

- $g(x)=x^2$
- $g(x)=x^3$
- $g(x)=x^4$
- $g(x)=1-x$

## Explanation

Equality requires the powers of the absolute values to be proportional almost everywhere. The cube of $x^2$ is $x^6$. Raising $x^4$ to the power three halves gives the same function, so the normalized powers agree.
