On $[0,2]$, let $f$ be equal to $-3$ on $(0,1)$ and $1$ on $(1,2)$. What is $\|f\|_1$?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $10$
- $4$

## Explanation

The one-norm integrates the absolute value, so the negative level gives a positive contribution of three. Both intervals have measure one. The integral is the sum of the positive areas, giving four.
