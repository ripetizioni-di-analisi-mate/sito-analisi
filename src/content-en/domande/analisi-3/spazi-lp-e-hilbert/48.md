In $\ell^2$, which condition characterizes $M^\perp$ for $M=\operatorname{span}\{e_1\}$?

- The first component is one.
- The first component is zero.
- Every component after the first is zero.
- The first two components sum to zero.

## Explanation

The inner product with the first standard basis vector equals the first component. Orthogonality to this generator is equivalent to orthogonality to all its multiples. Thus the orthogonal complement consists precisely of square-summable sequences with first component zero.
