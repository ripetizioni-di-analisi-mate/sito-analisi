For an infinite orthonormal system $(e_n)$, which vector series converges in norm?

- $\sum_{n\ge1}e_n$
- $\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac{e_n}{n}$
- $\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac{e_n}{\sqrt n}$
- $\sum_{n\ge1}(-1)^n e_n$

## Explanation

The squared norm of a difference of partial sums equals the sum of the squared absolute values of the intervening coefficients. The partial sums are therefore Cauchy for square-summable coefficients. The reciprocal of $n$ satisfies this condition because the series of its squares converges.
