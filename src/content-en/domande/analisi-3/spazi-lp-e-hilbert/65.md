In $L^2(-\pi,\pi)$, with the inner product linear in its first argument, what is $\langle e^{2it},e^{5it}\rangle$?

- $0$
- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $2\pi i$

## Explanation

Conjugation changes the sign of the second factor's frequency. The integrand is therefore the exponential with frequency minus three. Its antiderivative has equal endpoint values because the interval contains an integer number of periods, so the integral is zero.
