What is the projection of the function $f(x)=x^2+x$ onto the constants in $L^2(-2,2)$?

- $\dfrac83$
- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac23$
- $0$

## Explanation

The constant projection is the mean on the interval. The odd part integrates to zero. For the quadratic part the antiderivative takes opposite values at the endpoints, giving an integral of sixteen thirds. Divide by the interval length four to get the mean.
