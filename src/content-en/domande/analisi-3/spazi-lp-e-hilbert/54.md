In $L^2(0,2)$, approximate $f(x)=x$ by a function equal to $a$ on $(0,1)$ and to $b$ on $(1,2)$. Which pair $(a,b)$ minimises the norm of the error?

- $(1,1)$
- $(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{3}{2})$
- $(0,2)$
- $(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{2})$

## Explanation

The indicators of the two intervals are orthogonal. The residual must integrate to zero on each interval, giving the two separate averages $\int_0^1x\,\mathrm dx=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\int_1^2x\,\mathrm dx=\dfrac{3}{2}$.
