If $T_n:X\to Y$ are continuous linear operators, $X$ is Banach, and $T_nx$ converges for every $x$, what makes the limit operator continuous?

- Operator norm convergence, which follows automatically from pointwise convergence
- A uniform bound on the norms $\|T_n\|$ from the uniform boundedness principle
- Surjectivity of every $T_n$
- The domain must be finite-dimensional

## Explanation

Every convergent sequence of images is bounded, so the family is pointwise bounded. The uniform boundedness principle gives a common bound for the norms. Passing to the limit in $\|T_nx\|\le M\|x\|$ gives continuity of the limit operator.
