On $\ell^2$, let $D(x)_n=a_nx_n$ with $a_1=1$ and $a_n=0$ for $n\ge2$. Which pair gives the rank and the norm?

- Infinite rank and norm $1$
- Rank $0$ and norm $0$
- Rank $1$ and norm $1$
- Rank $1$ and norm $0$

## Explanation

The operator retains only the first coordinate, so every image is a multiple of $e_1$, itself an image. Discarding coordinates cannot increase the Euclidean norm, and $e_1$ is fixed. The rank and norm are both one.
