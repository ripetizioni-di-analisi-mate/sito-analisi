What is the optimal contraction constant of $T(x)=\dfrac{1}{2}\cos x$ on $[0,\dfrac{1}{2}]$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\cos(\dfrac{1}{2})$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\sin(\dfrac{1}{2})$

## Explanation

The absolute derivative increases on the interval and attains its maximum at $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The mean value theorem shows this maximum is a valid constant. Difference quotients with one point fixed at the right endpoint approach the absolute value of the derivative there, so no smaller constant works.
