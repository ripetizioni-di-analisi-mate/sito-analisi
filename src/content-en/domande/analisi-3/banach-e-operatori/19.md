On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, what is the norm of $Tf(x)=\int_0^x (5+11t)f(t)\,dt$?

- $16$
- $11$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$
- $8$

## Explanation

The kernel is positive and its integral increases with $x$. The maximum occurs at $1$. The constant function $1$ attains the value $\dfrac{21}{2}$. This constant input has norm one, so the value attained equals the supremum over the unit ball.
