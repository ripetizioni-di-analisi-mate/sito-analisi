On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, find the norm of $Tf(x)=\int_0^x t^{5}f(t)\,dt$.

- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Use the supremum norm to bound the absolute value of the integral. The constant function one gives equality and therefore determines the operator norm. The weight is nonnegative, so its integral increases with the upper limit and is largest at one.

$$
|Tf(x)|\le\|f\|_\infty\frac{x^{6}}{6}\le\frac{\|f\|_\infty}{6}.\qquad T1(1)=\frac{1}{6}.
$$
