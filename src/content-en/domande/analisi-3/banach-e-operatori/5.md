On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, find the norm of $\ell(f)=5f(0)+6f(1)$.

- $11$
- $6$
- $5$
- $12$

## Explanation

The coefficients are positive, so the constant function one gives equality simultaneously at both endpoints. The upper bound equals the sum of the coefficients. The supremum defining the norm is taken over the unit ball for the supremum norm.

$$
|\ell(f)|\le(5+6)\|f\|_\infty=11\|f\|_\infty,\qquad \ell(1)=11.
$$
