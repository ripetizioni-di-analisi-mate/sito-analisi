Let $X,Y$ be Banach spaces and $T:X\to Y$ a bounded surjective linear operator. What does the open mapping theorem imply?

- The kernel is zero.
- Every ball is mapped to a ball of the same radius.
- The norm of $T$ is $1$.
- The image of a neighborhood of zero contains a neighborhood of zero.

## Explanation

A bounded surjective linear map between Banach spaces maps open sets to open sets. Apply this to an open ball centered at zero. Surjectivity and completeness of both spaces ensure the image contains a neighborhood in the codomain.
