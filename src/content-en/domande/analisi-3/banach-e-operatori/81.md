Which family in $C([0,1])$ is relatively compact for the supremum norm?

- $f_n(x)=\dfrac{x^n}{n}$
- $f_n(x)=x^n$
- $f_n(x)=n$
- $f_n(x)=\sin(2\pi nx)$

## Explanation

The family $f_n(x)=\dfrac{x^n}{n}$ converges uniformly to zero since its norms are at most $\dfrac{1}{n}$. A convergent sequence together with its limit is compact. The other families fail either boundedness or equicontinuity.
