Let $T$ be a contraction on a Banach space with constant $q=\dfrac{1}{9}$ and $\|x_1-x_0\|=4$. What is the exact value of the right-hand side of the a priori bound $\dfrac{q^2}{1-q}\|x_1-x_0\|$ for $\|x_*-x_2\|$?

- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{4}{81}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Starting at $x_2$, sum a geometric tail whose first term is at most $q^2\|x_1-x_0\|$. Dividing by $1-q$ gives $\dfrac{1}{18}$. For $q=\dfrac{1}{9}$ the first term of the bounding series is $\dfrac{4}{81}$ and its common ratio is one ninth.
