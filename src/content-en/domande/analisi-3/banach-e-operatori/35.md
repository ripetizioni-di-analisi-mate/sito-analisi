On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, what are the norms of $A$ and $A^2$ if $Af(x)=\int_0^x f(t)\,dt$?

- $\|A\|=1$ and $\|A^2\|=1$
- $\|A\|=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\|A^2\|=\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\|A\|=1$ and $\|A^2\|=\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\|A\|=1$ and $\|A^2\|=2$

## Explanation

For an input of norm at most one, $|Af(x)|\le x$. Integrating this estimate a second time gives $|A^2f(x)|\le\dfrac{x^2}{2}$. The constant function one attains both bounds at $x=1$.
