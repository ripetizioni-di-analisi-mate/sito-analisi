On $L^2([0,1];\mathbb R)$, which unit input attains the norm of $\ell(f)=\int_0^1 t f(t)\,dt$ with positive functional value?

- $f(t)=\sqrt3\,t$
- $f(t)=1$
- $f(t)=-\sqrt3\,t$
- $f(t)=\sqrt3(1-t)$

## Explanation

Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the absolute functional value by $\|t\|_2\|f\|_2$. The weight has norm $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$, so normalizing it gives the input $\sqrt3\,t$. Its inner product with the weight is positive and attains the bound.
