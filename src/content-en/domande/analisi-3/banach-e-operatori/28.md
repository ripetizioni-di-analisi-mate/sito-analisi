On $c_{00}$ with the norm of $\ell^2$, let $T_nx=nx_n$. Which hypothesis of the uniform boundedness principle fails if, for every $x$, only finitely many values $T_nx$ are nonzero?

- Linearity of the individual functionals
- Pointwise boundedness of the family
- Continuity of the individual functionals
- Completeness of the domain

## Explanation

Each $T_n$ is linear and has norm $n$, as is seen on the corresponding standard basis vector. The family is pointwise bounded because each input has finite support. The domain $c_{00}$ is incomplete, so the principle does not apply.
