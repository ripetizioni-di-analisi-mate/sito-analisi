For $T(x)=\dfrac{x}{2}+1$ with $x_0=0$, what is the actual error after three iterations?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The fixed point is two. Subtracting it from the recurrence shows that each step halves the error. The initial distance is two, so after three steps it is $2\cdot2^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.
