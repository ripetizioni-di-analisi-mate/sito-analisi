A contraction has constant $q=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $d(x_1,x_0)=2$. What is the smallest index $n$ for which the a priori bound guarantees $d(x_n,x_*)\le\dfrac{1}{8}$?

- $n=3$
- $n=4$
- $n=6$
- $n=5$

## Explanation

The a priori bound is $\dfrac{q^n}{1-q}d(x_1,x_0)=2^{2-n}$ and the required value is $2^{-3}$. The inequality is equivalent to $2-n\le -3$, so five is the first guaranteed index. After four steps the upper bound is still one quarter, above the required threshold.
