On $C([0,1])$, let $f_n(x)=x^n$. For each integer $n\ge1$, which comparison between $f_n$ and $f_{2n}$ rules out the Cauchy property in the supremum norm?

- $\|f_n-f_{2n}\|_\infty=\dfrac{1}{2n}$
- $\|f_n-f_{2n}\|_\infty=\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\|f_n-f_{2n}\|_\infty=1$
- $\|f_n-f_{2n}\|_\infty=0$

## Explanation

Setting $u=x^n$, the absolute difference is $u-u^2$ for $u\in[0,1]$. This polynomial equals $\dfrac{1}{4}-(u-\dfrac{1}{2})^2$, so its maximum is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ for every index. The distance stays bounded away from zero even for arbitrarily large indices.
