On $\mathbb R$, let $T(x)=\dfrac{x}{2}+1$. Which closed interval $I$ satisfies $T(I)\subseteq I$ and allows Banach's theorem to be applied to the restriction?

- $[0,1]$
- $[1,3]$
- $[-1,0]$
- $[3,4]$

## Explanation

Since $T$ is increasing, the image of a closed interval is determined by its endpoint values. The image of $[1,3]$ is $[\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{5}{2}]$, which lies in the original interval. Each other suggested interval has at least one endpoint mapped outside it.
