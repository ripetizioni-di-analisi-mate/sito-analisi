On $\ell^2$, set $D(x)_n=a_nx_n$ with $a_n=\dfrac{1}{n}$ for $n\ne5$ and $a_5=2$. Which pair gives the norm of $D$ and the first truncation whose error is strictly less than $1$?

- $1$ and $N=1$
- $2$ and $N=4$
- $5$ and $N=5$
- $2$ and $N=5$

## Explanation

The norm of a diagonal operator is the supremum of the absolute values of its coefficients, here a maximum of two. As long as the fifth coordinate remains in the tail, the error is at least two. Once that coordinate is removed, the remaining coefficients have absolute value at most $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
