What is the norm of $T(x,y)=(x+y,x-y)$ if the domain has the maximum norm and the codomain has the Euclidean norm?

- $\sqrt2$
- $2\sqrt2$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The sum of the squares of the two output coordinates is $2x^2+2y^2$. On the unit ball for the maximum norm, $x^2+y^2\le2$, so the output norm is at most two. The vector $(1,1)$ gives equality.
