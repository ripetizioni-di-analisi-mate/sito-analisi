For a matrix $A$ on $\mathbb R^2$, which formula gives the norm induced by the sum norm on both domain and codomain?

- The largest absolute row sum
- The largest absolute column sum
- The sum of the absolute values of all entries
- The largest absolute value of any one entry

## Explanation

A matrix applied to a vector gives a combination of its columns with the vector entries as coefficients. The triangle inequality yields the largest absolute column sum as an upper bound. The standard basis vector corresponding to that column attains it.
