What is the norm of $\ell(f)=5f(0)-11f(1)$ on $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm?

- $6$
- $11$
- $16$
- $5$

## Explanation

The triangle inequality gives the upper bound $16$. The function $1-2x$ has norm $1$ and opposite values at the endpoints, so it attains this bound. The continuous function $1-2x$ is positive at zero and negative at one, as required for the contributions to add.
