Let $D$ be diagonal on $\ell^2$ with coefficients $a_{2n}=0$ and $a_{2n-1}=\dfrac{1}{n}$. If $D_N$ retains only the first $2N$ coordinates, what is $\|D-D_N\|$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2N+1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{N+1}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{N^2}$

## Explanation

The first odd index in the tail is $2N+1$, with coefficient $\dfrac{1}{N+1}$. The subsequent odd coefficients decrease and the even ones vanish, so this is the maximum in the tail. The corresponding standard basis vector attains the norm.
