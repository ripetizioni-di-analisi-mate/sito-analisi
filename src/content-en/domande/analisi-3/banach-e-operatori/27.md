On $\ell^2$, define $T_nx=x_n$. Which pair gives the limit of $T_nx$ for every fixed input and the limit of the norms $\|T_n\|$?

- $0$ and $0$
- $1$ and $1$
- $0$ and $1$
- $1$ and $0$

## Explanation

Convergence of the sum of squared coordinates implies $x_n\to0$ for every $x\in\ell^2$. Yet each evaluation has norm one, since $|x_n|\le\|x\|_2$ and the standard basis vector $e_n$ gives equality. The two limits therefore concern different quantities.
