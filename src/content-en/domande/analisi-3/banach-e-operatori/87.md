Equip the domain and codomain of the differentiation operator $D:C^1([0,1])\to C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm. If $f_n\to f$ with $f\in C^1([0,1])$ and $Df_n\to g$ uniformly, which conclusion about the graph is correct?

- The graph is closed because $f=g$
- The graph is not closed because differentiation is unbounded
- The graph is closed because $f'=g$
- The graph is closed because $C^1([0,1])$ is complete in the supremum norm

## Explanation

Uniform convergence allows passage to the limit in $f_n(x)-f_n(0)=\int_0^x f_n'(t)\,dt$. Since $g$ is continuous, the resulting identity gives $f'=g$. The graph is closed, although the domain is incomplete in this norm.
