Let $T:X\to Y$ be a bounded linear operator between Banach spaces. If $\|Tx\|_Y\ge c\|x\|_X$ for every $x$ with $c>0$, what follows about its range?

- The range is all of $Y$
- The range is dense and proper
- The range is always finite-dimensional
- The range is closed and $T$ is injective

## Explanation

If $Tx_n$ converges, the lower bound makes $(x_n)$ Cauchy. Completeness of $X$ gives a limit, and continuity of $T$ maps that limit to $\lim Tx_n$. Thus the range is closed. The same bound gives a trivial kernel.
