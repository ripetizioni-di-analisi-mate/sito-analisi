Let $S$ and $T$ be the coordinate projections in $\mathbb R^2$ onto the horizontal and vertical axes, respectively, both of norm one. What is $\|ST\|$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The range of $T$ is the vertical axis. The projection $S$ annihilates every vector on that axis, so the composition is zero. The product of the factor norms gives only an upper bound, and the inequality is strict in this example.
