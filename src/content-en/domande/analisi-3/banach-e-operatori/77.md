With the supremum norm on the domain $C^1([0,1])$ and codomain $C([0,1])$, which sequence directly shows that differentiation is unbounded?

- $f_n(x)=\sin(nx)$
- $f_n(x)=\dfrac{\sin(nx)}{n}$
- $f_n(x)=\dfrac{x}{n}$
- $f_n(x)=1$

## Explanation

The functions $\sin(nx)$ have supremum norm at most one. Their derivatives are $n\cos(nx)$ and take the value $n$ at zero, so the output norms grow without bound. Thus a bounded sequence has unbounded images.
