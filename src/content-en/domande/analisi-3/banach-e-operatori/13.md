On $\ell^2$, is the operator $(Tx)_n=\dfrac{1}{n^{5}}x_n$ compact? What is its norm?

- $\text{Compact, with }\|T\|=1$
- $\text{Compact, with }\|T\|=0$
- $\text{Not compact, with }\|T\|=1$
- $\text{Unbounded}$

## Explanation

The truncations $T_N$ have finite rank. Their convergence in operator norm implies compactness of the limit. The first standard basis vector attains the norm of one. The coefficient of greatest absolute value in the tail has index $N+1$ and tends to zero as $N$ increases.

$$
\|T\|=\sup_{n\ge1}\frac1{n^{5}}=1,\qquad \|T-T_N\|=\frac1{(N+1)^{5}}\to0.
$$
