On $\ell^2$, let $(Tx)_n=\dfrac{x_n}{3+n}$. Which property does $T$ have?

- It is injective and its range is a proper dense subspace.
- It is bounded below by a positive constant.
- It is surjective.
- It has an infinite-dimensional kernel.

## Explanation

All coefficients are nonzero, so the kernel is zero and every finitely supported sequence lies in the range. The sequence $y_n=\dfrac{1}{3+n}$ belongs to $\ell^2$, but its only possible preimage is the constant sequence $1$, which does not belong to $\ell^2$.
