Find the fixed point of $T(x)=4-\dfrac{x}{9}$ on $\mathbb R$.

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{18}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $4$

## Explanation

In the fixed-point equation, collect all terms containing $x$ on the left. Dividing by $\dfrac{10}{9}$ gives $\dfrac{18}{5}$. The contraction constant is $\dfrac{1}{9}$ and the real line is complete, so this is the unique fixed point.
