Let $T_n:\ell^2\to\mathbb R$ be given by $T_nx=\dfrac{x_n}{n}$. What is $\sup_{n\ge1}\|T_n\|$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $+\infty$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

For each index, $|x_n|\le\|x\|_2$ gives $\|T_n\|\le\dfrac{1}{n}$. At the standard unit vector $e_n$ the value is exactly $\dfrac{1}{n}$, so the estimate is sharp. The maximum of these numbers is one, attained for $n=1$.
