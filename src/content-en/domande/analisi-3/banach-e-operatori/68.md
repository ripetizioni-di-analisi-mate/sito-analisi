The projection $P(x,y)=x$ acts from Euclidean $\mathbb R^2$ to $\mathbb R$. What is the image of the open ball centered at $(1,2)$ with radius $3$?

- $[-2,4]$
- $(-1,5)$
- $(-3,3)$
- $(-2,4)$

## Explanation

Every point of the ball satisfies $|x-1|<3$ since the Euclidean distance bounds the difference in the first coordinate. For every $x$ satisfying this condition, the point $(x,2)$ belongs to the ball. Thus the image is exactly the stated open interval.
