On $C([0,1];\mathbb R)$ with the supremum norm, which statement holds for $\ell(f)=\int_0^1(2t-1)f(t)\,dt$?

- The norm is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and no unit input attains it
- The norm is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the constant function one attains it
- The norm is $0$ since the weight has integral zero
- The norm is $1$ and an affine function attains it

## Explanation

The integral of the absolute value of the weight is one half. Its sign can be approximated by continuous functions of norm one, so the bound is exact. Equality would require constant opposite signs on the two sides of the midpoint, which is incompatible with continuity.
