Let $\ell(f)=f(0)-f(1)$ on $C([0,1];\mathbb R)$ with the supremum norm. Which condition characterizes all unit inputs for which $|\ell(f)|=\|\ell\|$?

- $|f(0)|=|f(1)|=1$ with the same sign
- $f(0)$ and $f(1)$ equal $1$ and $-1$ in either order
- $f(0)=0$ and $|f(1)|=1$
- $f(\dfrac{1}{2})=0$ with no further conditions

## Explanation

On the unit ball the two values lie in $[-1,1]$. Their difference has absolute value at most two. Equality requires them to be opposite endpoints of this interval and is attained by an affine function.
