Let $P_n$ denote truncation of $\ell^2$ to the first $n$ coordinates. Which unit input attains $\|I-P_n\|$ for every $n\ge1$?

- $e_n$
- $e_1$
- $e_{n+1}$
- $\dfrac{e_n+e_{n+1}}{\sqrt2}$

## Explanation

The operator $I-P_n$ retains only the tail, so its norm is at most one. The vector $e_{n+1}$ lies entirely in the tail and is fixed. Each other suggested vector has at least one component among the discarded coordinates.
