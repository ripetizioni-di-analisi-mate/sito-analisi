A diagonal operator $Tx=(\dfrac{x_n}{6+n})_n$ on $\ell^2$ is approximated by retaining only the first 13 coefficients. What is $\|T-T_{13}\|$?

- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{19}$

## Explanation

The norm of the difference is the largest remaining diagonal coefficient. The first tail index is $14$, so the norm is $\dfrac{1}{20}$. The fourteenth standard basis vector belongs to the unit ball and attains this coefficient exactly.
