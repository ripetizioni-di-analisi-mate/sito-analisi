Which expression gives the norm of $T(x,y)=(2x+y,x-2y)$ when both the domain and codomain carry the Euclidean norm?

- $3$
- $5$
- $2$
- $\sqrt5$

## Explanation

On expanding the squared norm of the image, the mixed terms in the two squares cancel, leaving $5x^2+5y^2=5\|(x,y)\|_2^2$. Thus the ratio of the norms is $\sqrt5$ for every nonzero vector.
