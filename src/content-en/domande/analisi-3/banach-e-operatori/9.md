In a complete metric space with metric $d(x,y)=|x-y|$, a contraction has constant $q=\dfrac{1}{7}$ and two consecutive iterates satisfy $|x_n-x_{n-1}|=1$. What a posteriori upper bound does Banach's theorem give for $|x_*-x_n|$?

- $|x_*-x_n|\le\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $|x_*-x_n|\le\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $|x_*-x_n|\le0$
- $|x_*-x_n|\le\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The tail of the distances between iterates is bounded by a geometric series. The first subsequent distance is at most $q$ times the known distance, which explains the factor $q$ in the numerator. The bound starts with the steps after the current iterate and therefore excludes the observed step of length one.

$$
|x_*-x_n|\le\sum_{j=1}^\infty q^j|x_n-x_{n-1}|=\frac{q}{1-q}=\frac{1}{6}.
$$
