In $\mathbb R^2$ with the maximum norm, let $\ell(x,0)=x$. Which linear extension preserves the norm?

- Every $\widetilde\ell(x,y)=x+ay$ with $|a|\le1$
- Only $\widetilde\ell(x,y)=x$
- Every $\widetilde\ell(x,y)=x+ay$ with $a\in\mathbb R$
- Only $\widetilde\ell(x,y)=x+y$

## Explanation

On the square unit ball, the maximum of $|x+ay|$ is $1+|a|$. Choose the coordinates with signs matching the coefficients to attain it. The original norm is one, so preserving it requires $a=0$.
