The map $T(x)=\dfrac{1}{2+x}$ acts on $[0,1]$. Which pair gives its exact range and optimal Lipschitz constant on the interval?

- $[\dfrac{1}{2},1]$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $[\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{2}]$ and $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $[0,\dfrac{1}{2}]$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $[\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{2}]$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The map is decreasing, so its maximum and minimum occur at the endpoints. The derivative has absolute value $\dfrac{1}{(2+x)^2}$, with maximum $\dfrac{1}{4}$ at zero. Difference quotients with one endpoint at zero approach that value, so the constant is optimal.
