Let $P$ be a nonzero orthogonal projection on a Hilbert space. What is $\|(I-\dfrac{1}{9}P)^{-1}\|$?

- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{9}{8}$

## Explanation

On the range of $P$ the inverse acts by multiplication by $\dfrac{9}{8}$; on the kernel it is the identity. The decomposition is orthogonal, so the norm is $\dfrac{9}{8}$. Since $P$ is nonzero, a unit vector in its range attains the maximum expansion.
