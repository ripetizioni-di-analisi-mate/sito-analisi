On $\ell^2$, let $T_n(x)=3n x_n$ be linear functionals. Which property of their norms follows by testing on the standard basis vectors?

- The norms tend to zero.
- The norms are all equal.
- The norms are not uniformly bounded.
- The norms are bounded by $1$.

## Explanation

The standard basis vector $e_n$ has norm one and $T_n(e_n)=3n$. This gives a growing lower bound for the norms. The matching upper bound is $3n$, since every coordinate is bounded in absolute value by the vector norm. Thus the growth just found is exact.
