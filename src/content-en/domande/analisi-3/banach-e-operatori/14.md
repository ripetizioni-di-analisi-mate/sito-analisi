On $\ell^2$, which sequence of unit vectors shows that the projection onto the even coordinates maps the unit ball to a set whose closure is not compact?

- $(e_{2n-1})_{n\ge1}$
- $(e_{2n})_{n\ge1}$
- $((-1)^n e_2)_{n\ge1}$
- $\bigl(\dfrac{e_2+e_{2n+1}}{\sqrt2}\bigr)_{n\ge1}$

## Explanation

The projection fixes the even-indexed basis vectors. Distinct such vectors are at distance $\sqrt2$, so their images have no convergent subsequence. The odd-indexed basis vectors, however, are all mapped to zero.
