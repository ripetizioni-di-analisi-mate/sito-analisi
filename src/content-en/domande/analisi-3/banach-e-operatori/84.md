Let $T(x,y)=(2x+y,-x+y)$ on $\mathbb R^2$ with the maximum norm on both domain and codomain. What is $\|T(1,-1)\|_\infty$ and what does this prove about $\|T\|$?

- Its value is $2$ and this proves $\|T\|\le2$
- Its value is $3$ and this proves $\|T\|\ge3$
- Its value is $1$ and this proves $\|T\|\le1$
- Its value is $2$ and this proves $\|T\|\ge2$

## Explanation

The given vector has norm one and its image is $(1,-2)$. The larger of the two absolute coordinate values is two. The operator norm is the supremum of output norms over unit inputs, so this single value provides a lower bound.
