On the line spanned by $v=(6,13)$ in Euclidean $\mathbb R^2$, let $\ell(tv)=t$. What is the norm of a norm-preserving Hahn–Banach extension?

- $1$
- $\sqrt{205}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{205}}{205}$
- $\dfrac{1}{19}$

## Explanation

On the line, the ratio of the absolute functional value to the vector norm is $\dfrac{1}{\|v\|}$. The extension preserves that norm, so $\dfrac{\sqrt{205}}{205}$. The vector $v$ has norm $\sqrt{6^2+13^2}=\sqrt{205}$. Its functional value is one, and the ratio is unchanged for nonzero multiples.
