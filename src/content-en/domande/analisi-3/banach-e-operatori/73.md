If $A$ is bounded and $\|A^2\|<1$ on a Banach space, which formula always gives the inverse of $I-A$?

- $(I+A)(I-A^2)^{-1}$
- $(I-A)(I-A^2)^{-1}$
- $(I-A^2)^{-1}$
- $I+A^2$

## Explanation

Applying the Neumann series to $A^2$ gives the inverse of $I-A^2$. The product $(I-A)(I+A)$ equals $I-A^2$. The inverse is a sum of powers of $A^2$ and therefore commutes with $A$, giving an inverse in either order.
