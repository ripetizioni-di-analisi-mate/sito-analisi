On $C([0,1])$, let $Tf(x)=\int_0^x f(t)\,dt$. Which inequality proves that the image of the unit ball is equicontinuous?

- $|Tf(x)-Tf(y)|\le1$
- $|Tf(x)-Tf(y)|\le |x-y|$
- $|Tf(x)-Tf(y)|\le \|Tf\|_\infty+1$
- $|Tf(x)-Tf(y)|\le x+y$

## Explanation

The difference equals the integral of $f$ between $y$ and $x$. For a unit input the integrand has absolute value at most one. The interval length therefore bounds the absolute difference independently of $f$.
