A contraction with constant $q=\dfrac{1}{2}$ has produced consecutive iterates $x_{n-1}=\dfrac{5}{4}$ and $x_n=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Which interval centered at $x_n$ does the a posteriori formula give for this value of $q$?

- $[\dfrac{11}{8},\dfrac{13}{8}]$
- $[\dfrac{5}{4},\dfrac{7}{4}]$
- $[1,2]$
- $[\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{7}{4}]$

## Explanation

The observed step has length $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The coefficient in the a posteriori bound is $\dfrac{q}{1-q}=1$, so the radius is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Subtract it from and add it to the center $\dfrac{3}{2}$ to find the endpoints.
