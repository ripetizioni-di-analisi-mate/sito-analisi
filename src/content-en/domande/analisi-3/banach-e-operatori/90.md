If $x\in X$ has norm $4$ and $\ell\in X^*$ has norm $2$, which pair gives the best general upper bound for $|\ell(x)|$ and states when a functional attains it?

- $8$, attained only in finite dimensions
- $8$, attainable for every fixed vector $x$
- $4$, attainable for every fixed vector $x$
- $2$, attained only in finite dimensions

## Explanation

The definition of the dual norm gives $|\ell(x)|\le2\cdot4=8$. The Hahn–Banach theorem provides a norm-one functional taking the value four at $x$. Multiplying by two gives a functional of norm two that attains the bound.
