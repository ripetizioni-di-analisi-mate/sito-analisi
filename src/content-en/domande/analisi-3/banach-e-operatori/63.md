Let $(T_n)$ be a pointwise bounded family of continuous linear operators from a Banach space $X$ to a normed space $Y$. Why is $E_m=\{x:\sup_n\|T_nx\|\le m\}$ closed?

- It is a union of open balls
- The supremum of continuous functions is always continuous
- It is the intersection of the closed sets $\{x:\|T_nx\|\le m\}$
- It is the kernel of every $T_n$

## Explanation

For each $n$, continuity of $T_n$ and of the norm makes the set defined by $\|T_nx\|\le m$ closed. The supremum bound is equivalent to all these inequalities holding simultaneously. An arbitrary intersection of closed sets is closed.
