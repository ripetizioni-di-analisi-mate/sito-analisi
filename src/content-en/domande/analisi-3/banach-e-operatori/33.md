On $\ell^2$, let $(Tx)_n=(5+\dfrac{1}{n})x_n$. Which fact proves that $T$ is not compact?

- The images of the standard basis vectors have no norm-convergent subsequence.
- The rank of $T$ is finite.
- The images of the standard basis vectors converge to zero in norm.
- The images of the standard basis vectors are all zero.

## Explanation

The images are orthogonal and each has norm at least 5. Distinct terms are therefore separated by a fixed positive distance. No subsequence of the images can be Cauchy, so the sequential compactness criterion rules out compactness.
