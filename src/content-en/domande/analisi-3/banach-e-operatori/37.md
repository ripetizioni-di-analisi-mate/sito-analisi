Let $T(x,y)=(x+2y,3x-y)$. If the domain has norm $\ell^1$ and the codomain norm $\ell^\infty$, what is $\|T\|$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

Each output coordinate is a functional on $\mathbb R^2$ with the sum norm. Its norm is the largest absolute value of its coefficients. The larger of the two coordinate norms is three, attained by the second coordinate on the vector $(1,0)$.
