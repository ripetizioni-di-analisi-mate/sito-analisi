Find the fixed point of $T(x)=\dfrac{x}{7}+1$ on $\mathbb R$.

- $\dfrac{6}{7}$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{6}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The real line is complete and the contraction factor is strictly less than one. The algebraic equation gives the unique fixed point. Moving $\dfrac{x}{7}$ to the left gives $\dfrac{6}{7}x=1$, from which the required value follows.

$$
|T(x)-T(y)|=\frac{|x-y|}{7},\qquad x=\frac{x}{7}+1\iff x=\frac{7}{6}.
$$
