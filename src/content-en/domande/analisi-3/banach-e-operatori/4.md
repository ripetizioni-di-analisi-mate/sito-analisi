On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, let $Tf(x)=\int_0^x (x-t)f(t)\,dt$. Which pair gives the norm and a unit input that attains it?

- $1$ and $f=1$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$ and $f(t)=t$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $f(t)=t$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $f=1$

## Explanation

For a unit input the absolute value of the output at $x$ is at most the integral of $x-t$, which equals $\dfrac{x^2}{2}$. The constant function one attains this bound at $x=1$, so it attains the norm.
