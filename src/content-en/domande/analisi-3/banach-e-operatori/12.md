On $\mathbb R^3$, let $A(x,y,z)=(2y,z,0)$. What is $(I-A)^{-1}(0,0,1)$?

- $(0,1,1)$
- $(-2,1,1)$
- $(2,0,1)$
- $(2,1,1)$

## Explanation

The third power of $A$ vanishes, so the inverse is $I+A+A^2$. On the given input, the first power gives $(0,1,0)$ and the second gives $(2,0,0)$. Adding the original input gives $(2,1,1)$.
