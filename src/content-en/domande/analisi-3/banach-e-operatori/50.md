In $\mathbb R^2$ with norm $\ell^1$, let $\ell(x,0)=x$. For exactly which values of $a\in\mathbb R$ does the extension $\widetilde\ell_a(x,y)=x+ay$ preserve the norm?

- $a=0$ only
- $a\in[-1,1]$
- $a\in[0,1]$
- $a\in\mathbb R$

## Explanation

The norm of the functional on $\ell^1$ is the larger of $1$ and $|a|$. The triangle inequality gives the upper bound, and the two standard basis vectors give the lower bound. The original norm is one, so we require $|a|\le1$.
