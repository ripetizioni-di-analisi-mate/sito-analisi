For $A=\dfrac{1}{4} I$ on a nonzero Banach space, what is the partial sum through the term $A^2$ and how far is it from the exact inverse?

- $\dfrac{21}{16}I$ and $\dfrac{1}{48}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4} I$ and $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{21}{16}I$ and $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3} I$ and $0$

## Explanation

The sum has coefficients $1,\dfrac{1}{4},\dfrac{1}{16}$ and equals $\dfrac{21}{16}I$. The exact inverse is $\dfrac{4}{3} I$. Since the identity has norm one, the distance is the absolute value of $\dfrac{4}{3}-\dfrac{21}{16}$, namely $\dfrac{1}{48}$. This difference equals the tail of the geometric series starting with the third power.
