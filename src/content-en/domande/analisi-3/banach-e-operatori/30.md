On $\ell^2$, set $D(x)_n=\dfrac{x_n}{n}$. If $D_N$ denotes truncation after $N$ coordinates, what is the smallest integer $N\ge1$ such that $\|D-D_N\|\le\dfrac{1}{10}$?

- $10$
- $9$
- $8$
- $11$

## Explanation

The norm of the remainder is the largest remaining coefficient, namely $\dfrac{1}{N+1}$. Since the denominators are positive, the required inequality is equivalent to $N+1\ge10$. The first admissible integer is therefore nine.
