Let $T(x)=1-\dfrac{x}{2}$ on $\mathbb R$ and take $x_0=0$. Where do the iterates lie relative to the fixed point?

- Below the fixed point at even indices and above it at odd indices
- All below the fixed point
- All above the fixed point from index one onward
- They coincide with the fixed point after the first step

## Explanation

The fixed point is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. The signed errors satisfy $x_{n+1}-\dfrac{2}{3}=-\dfrac{1}{2}(x_n-\dfrac{2}{3})$. The initial error is negative and changes sign at every step, so even indices give negative errors and odd indices positive errors.
