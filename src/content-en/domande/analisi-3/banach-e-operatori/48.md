If $\|A\|=\dfrac{1}{3}$, what is the exact value of the geometric sum $\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\|A\|^n$ that bounds $\|(I-A)^{-1}-(I+A)\|$?

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The terms $I$ and $A$ account for powers zero and one. The tail begins with $A^2$, of norm at most $\dfrac{1}{9}$. Summing the geometric series with ratio $\dfrac{1}{3}$ gives $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
