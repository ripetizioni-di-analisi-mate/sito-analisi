A bounded operator $A$ satisfies $A^3=0$ and $\|A\|=4$. Which polynomial in $A$ is nevertheless the inverse of $I-A$?

- $I-A+A^2$
- $I+A$
- $I+A+\dfrac{A^2}{4}$
- $I+A+A^2$

## Explanation

Multiplication in either order gives $(I-A)(I+A+A^2)=I-A^3=I$. The vanishing third power truncates the series exactly, even though the norm exceeds one.
