On $[0,1]$, let $T(x)=\dfrac{1}{2}(x^2+1)$. Which hypothesis of the Banach fixed-point theorem fails?

- Existence of a contraction constant strictly less than one
- Completeness of the interval
- The inclusion $T([0,1])\subseteq[0,1]$
- Continuity of the map

## Explanation

The image is $[\dfrac{1}{2},1]$ and the original interval is closed, hence complete. For $x<1$, the ratio of $T(1)-T(x)$ to $1-x$ equals $\dfrac{1+x}{2}$. These ratios approach one, ruling out a contraction constant strictly below one.
