On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, find the norm of $Tf(x)=(5+x)f(x)$.

- $6$
- $5$
- $7$
- $0$

## Explanation

The maximum of the multiplier gives the upper bound. The constant function one has norm one and attains the bound at $x=1$, so the estimate is exact. The maximum must be taken over the whole interval because the supremum norm of the product depends on its largest absolute value.

$$
\|Tf\|_\infty\le(5+1)\|f\|_\infty.\qquad f=1:\ \|Tf\|_\infty=6.
$$
