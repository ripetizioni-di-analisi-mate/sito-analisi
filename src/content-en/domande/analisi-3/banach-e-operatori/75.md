On a nonzero Banach space, let $A=-\dfrac{1}{2} I$. Which pair gives the exact norm of $(I-A)^{-1}$ and the upper bound obtained by summing the norms of the powers of $A$?

- $2$ and $2$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $2$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $2$

## Explanation

Since $I-A=\dfrac{3}{2} I$, the inverse is $\dfrac{2}{3} I$. The Neumann series bound uses the positive ratio $\|A\|=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and therefore equals two. The exact norm is smaller because the bound discards the alternating signs of the powers.
