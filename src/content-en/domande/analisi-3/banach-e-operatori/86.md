Let $X$ be a nonzero Banach space. Which family of operators on $X$ is pointwise unbounded at every nonzero vector?

- $T_n=\dfrac{1}{n} I$
- $T_n=nI$
- $T_n=(-1)^nI$
- $T_n=(1-2^{-n})I$

## Explanation

For a fixed nonzero vector, the norm of $nIx$ is $n\|x\|$, which diverges. In the other cases the scalar coefficients have absolute value at most one, so the output norms are bounded by $\|x\|$.
