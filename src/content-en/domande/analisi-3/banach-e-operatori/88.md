What is $\|D-D_N\|$ if $D(x)_n=(-1)^n x_n$ on $\ell^2$ and $D_N$ retains only the first $N$ coordinates?

- $0$ for even $N$
- $\dfrac{1}{N+1}$
- $2$ for every $N$
- $1$ for every $N$

## Explanation

After truncation, all remaining coefficients have absolute value one, regardless of sign. Summing the squared tail coordinates gives an upper bound of one, attained by the vector $e_{N+1}$.
