On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, let $Tf(x)=(6-13x)f(x)$. What is $\|T\|$?

- $13$
- $19$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

The norm is the maximum absolute value of the multiplier. The endpoints give $6$ and $7$. The constant function $1$ attains the norm $7$. An affine function has its largest absolute value at an endpoint since its interior values are convex combinations of its endpoint values.
