For $\ell(f)=f(0)-2f(\dfrac{1}{2})+f(1)$ on $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, which input in the unit ball attains $\|\ell\|$?

- $f(x)=1$
- $f(x)=4|x-\dfrac{1}{2}|-1$
- $f(x)=1-2x$
- $f(x)=2|x-\dfrac{1}{2}|-1$

## Explanation

The triangle inequality bounds the functional norm by four. The first and last evaluations must contribute with the same sign as the weighted middle evaluation. The values $1,-1,1$ are attained by $4|x-\dfrac{1}{2}|-1$, which stays in the unit ball.
