On a nonzero Banach space, let $A=\dfrac{1}{7}I$. Compute $\|(I-A)^{-1}\|$.

- $\dfrac{6}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7}{6}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The identity has norm one. The inverse is a multiple of the identity, so its norm equals the absolute value of the coefficient. Summing the geometric Neumann series gives the same result.

$$
I-A=\frac{6}{7}I,\qquad (I-A)^{-1}=\frac{7}{6}I.
$$
