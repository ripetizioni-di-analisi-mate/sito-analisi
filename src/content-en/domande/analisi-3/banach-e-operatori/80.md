Let $x,y$ be distinct vectors in a real normed space. Which value of $\ell(x)-\ell(y)$ can be attained with $\|\ell\|=1$ by applying the Hahn–Banach theorem?

- $\|x\|+\|y\|+1$
- $2\|x-y\|$
- $\|x-y\|+1$
- $\|x-y\|$

## Explanation

On the line spanned by $x-y$, construct a functional of norm one taking the value $\|x-y\|$ at that vector. Hahn–Banach extends it without changing the norm. By linearity its value at $x-y$ equals the required difference.
