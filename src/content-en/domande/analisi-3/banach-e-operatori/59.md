On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, which property makes $Tf(x)=x\int_0^1 f(t)\,dt$ compact?

- It is the identity on the space
- Its range is the entire space of continuous functions
- It is bounded and its range lies in the line spanned by $x\mapsto x$
- The unit ball of $C([0,1])$ is compact

## Explanation

The integral coefficient is bounded in absolute value by the input norm. Every output is a multiple of $x\mapsto x$, so the operator is bounded and has rank one. In finite dimensions, the closure of a bounded set is compact.
