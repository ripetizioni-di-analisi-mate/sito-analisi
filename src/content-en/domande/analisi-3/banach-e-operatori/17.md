In $C([0,5])$ with the supremum norm, consider $Y=\{f:f(0)=f(5)\}$. Which fact proves that $Y$ is complete?

- It is the kernel of a continuous linear functional.
- It is one-dimensional.
- Every element has norm zero.
- It is the preimage of an open set.

## Explanation

The difference of the two endpoint evaluations is a continuous linear functional. Its kernel is closed in a complete space and is therefore complete. Equality of the endpoint values is preserved under uniform convergence, so every Cauchy sequence in $Y$ has its limit in $Y$.
