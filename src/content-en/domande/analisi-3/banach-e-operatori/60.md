On $\ell^2$, let $L$ and $S$ be the left and right shifts, with $L(x_1,x_2,\ldots)=(x_2,x_3,\ldots)$ and $S(x_1,x_2,\ldots)=(0,x_1,x_2,\ldots)$. What are the two compositions?

- $LS=SL=I$
- $LS=I-P$ and $SL=I$
- $LS=SL=I-P$
- $LS=I$ and $SL=I-P$, with $Px=x_1e_1$

## Explanation

The right shift inserts an initial zero, which the left shift removes. In the opposite order, the initial coordinate of the input is first removed and then replaced by zero. Thus the second composition is $I-P$.
