Let $T_nx=\sum_{k=1}^{n}x_k$ on $\ell^2$. Which fixed input shows that the family is not pointwise bounded?

- $x_k=\dfrac{1}{k}$
- $x=e_1$
- $x_k=2^{-k}$
- $x_k=\dfrac{1}{k^2}$

## Explanation

The sequence with coordinates $\dfrac{1}{k}$ belongs to $\ell^2$ since the series of squares converges. Its images are the partial sums of the harmonic series and grow without bound. For each other suggested input the series of coordinates converges.
