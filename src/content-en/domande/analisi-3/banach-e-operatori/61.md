On $C([0,1])$, set $\|f\|_*=\|f\|_\infty+|f(0)|$. Which comparison proves that this norm is equivalent to the supremum norm?

- $\|f\|_\infty\le\|f\|_*\le2\|f\|_\infty$
- $\|f\|_\infty\le\|f\|_*\le|f(0)|$
- $2\|f\|_\infty\le\|f\|_*$ for every $f$
- $\|f\|_*=\|f\|_\infty$ for every $f$

## Explanation

The term $|f(0)|$ lies between zero and $\|f\|_\infty$. Adding the supremum norm gives both inequalities with fixed constants. They apply to differences as well and give the same Cauchy sequences for both norms.
