Let $T_nx=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\dfrac{x_k}{2^k}$ on $\ell^2$. What is the norm of the limit functional $Tx=\lim_n T_nx$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The Euclidean norm of the coefficient vector is the square root of the sum $\sum_{k\ge1}4^{-k}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Cauchy–Schwarz gives this upper bound, attained by the normalized coefficient vector. The norm is therefore $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$.
