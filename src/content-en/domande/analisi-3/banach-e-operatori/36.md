On $\mathbb R^2$ with norm $\|\cdot\|_\infty$, what is the norm of $T(x,y)=(6x-13y,x+y)$?

- $7$
- $205$
- $13$
- $19$

## Explanation

The induced norm is the largest absolute row sum. The first row gives $19$ and the second gives $2$. The vector $(1,-1)$ has maximum norm one and gives a first output coordinate equal to nineteen.
