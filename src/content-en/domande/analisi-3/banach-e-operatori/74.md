On $\ell^2$, let $D(x)_n=\dfrac{x_n}{n}$ and let $S$ be the right shift. What can be concluded about $SD$ and $DS$?

- Only $SD$ is compact
- Both compositions are compact
- Only $DS$ is compact
- Neither composition is compact

## Explanation

The coefficients of $D$ tend to zero, so $D$ is compact. Applying $S$ after $D$ preserves convergence of a subsequence by continuity. Applying it first sends bounded sequences to bounded sequences to which $D$ can then be applied.
