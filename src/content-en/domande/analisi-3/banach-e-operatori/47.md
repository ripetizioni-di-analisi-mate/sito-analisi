Which sequence shows that $c_{00}$ with the norm of $\ell^2$ is incomplete?

- The standard basis vectors $(e_n)_{n\ge1}$
- The vectors $\dfrac{e_n}{n}$
- The truncations of $(2^{-k})_{k\ge1}$
- The truncations of $(1)_{k\ge1}$

## Explanation

The tail of the sum of the squares of the coordinates $2^{-k}$ tends to zero, so the truncations are Cauchy. Their limit in $\ell^2$ has infinitely many nonzero coordinates and therefore does not belong to $c_{00}$.
