Let $T$ be a contraction with constant $\dfrac{1}{3}$ on a complete metric space. If a candidate $z$ satisfies $d(z,Tz)=\dfrac{1}{9}$, which bound follows from $d(z,x_*)\le d(z,Tz)+d(Tz,Tx_*)$?

- $d(z,x_*)\le\dfrac{1}{27}$
- $d(z,x_*)\le\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $d(z,x_*)\le\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $d(z,x_*)\le\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

Since $Tx_*=x_*$, the contraction property bounds the second term by $\dfrac{1}{3}d(z,x_*)$. Moving that term to the left gives $\dfrac{2}{3}d(z,x_*)\le\dfrac{1}{9}$. Dividing by two thirds gives one sixth. The residual is measured at the candidate and requires no knowledge of earlier iterates.
