Let $T=I+A$ with $\|A\|=\dfrac{5}{11}$. What is the exact value of the geometric sum $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\|A\|^n$ bounding $\|T^{-1}\|$ by the Neumann series?

- $\dfrac{11}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{11}$
- $\dfrac{11}{6}$
- $\dfrac{11}{16}$

## Explanation

The series consists of powers of $-A$. The sum of their norms is bounded by the geometric series with ratio $\dfrac{5}{11}$, whose sum is $\dfrac{11}{6}$. This bounds the norm of the limit since the norm of each power is bounded by the corresponding power of the norm.
