On $\ell^2$, set $T_nx=6\sum_{k=1}^n \dfrac{x_k}{k}$. Which uniform estimate is valid?

- $\|T_n\|=0$
- $\|T_n\|\le \dfrac{6}{2}$
- $\|T_n\|\ge 13n$
- $\|T_n\|\le 6\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Cauchy–Schwarz gives a bound using the norm of the coefficient vector. Since $\sum_{k=1}^\infty k^{-2}\le 1+\int_1^\infty x^{-2}dx=2$, the required estimate follows. The sum of the squared coefficients is bounded by a constant independent of $n$, as a uniform estimate requires.
