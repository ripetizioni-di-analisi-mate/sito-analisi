On $\mathbb R^2$ with the maximum norm, let $A(x,y)=(\dfrac{y}{2},0)$. What is the exact norm of $(I-A)^{-1}$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The square of $A$ vanishes, so the inverse is $I+A$. The absolute row sums are $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $1$, and the unit input $(1,1)$ attains the first. The norm is therefore three halves.
