Which signal does $\dfrac{e^{-s}-2e^{-2s}+e^{-3s}}{s^2}$ represent?

- A rectangular pulse of height one on $[1,3]$
- A ramp growing indefinitely after time one
- A triangular pulse with peak $(1,2)$ and support $[0,2]$
- A triangular pulse with peak $(2,1)$ and support $[1,3]$

## Explanation

The three terms represent ramps starting at one, two, and three with slope changes of one, minus two, and one, respectively. The resulting slopes are one and minus one on the active pieces and zero again after three.
