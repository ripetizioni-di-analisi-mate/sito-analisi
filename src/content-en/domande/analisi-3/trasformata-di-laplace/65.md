Choose $a$ so that the causal inverse transform of $\dfrac{s+a}{(s+1)(s+3)}$ contains no term proportional to $e^{-3t}$. Which value is required?

- $a=3$
- $a=1$
- $a=-3$
- $a=0$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $e^{-3t}$ is the residue at the simple pole $s=-3$. It vanishes when the numerator is zero there, giving $a=3$. After cancellation the transform is $\dfrac{1}{s+1}$.
