Find the inverse transform of $\dfrac{s+4}{(s+2)^2+9}$ for $t\ge0$.

- $\left(\dfrac{2 \sin{\left(3 t \right)}}{3} + \cos{\left(3 t \right)}\right) e^{- 2 t}$
- $\left(- \dfrac{2 \sin{\left(3 t \right)}}{3} + \cos{\left(3 t \right)}\right) e^{- 2 t}$
- $\left(\sin{\left(3 t \right)} + \dfrac{2 \cos{\left(3 t \right)}}{3}\right) e^{- 2 t}$
- $\left(2 \sin{\left(3 t \right)} + \cos{\left(3 t \right)}\right) e^{- 2 t}$

## Explanation

Write the numerator as $(s+2)+2$. The term proportional to the shifted parameter gives the cosine, and the constant term gives two thirds of the sine of angular frequency three. The shift introduces the factor $e^{-2t}$ in both.
