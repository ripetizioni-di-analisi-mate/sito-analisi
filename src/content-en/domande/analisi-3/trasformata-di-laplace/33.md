The inverse transform of $\dfrac{e^{-4s}-e^{-7s}}s$ equals $1$ on an interval. What is its length?

- $3$
- $10$
- $7$
- $11$

## Explanation

Each term is the transform of a delayed step. Their difference equals one while only the first step is active and returns to zero when the second switches on. The interval length is therefore the difference between the delays, namely three.
