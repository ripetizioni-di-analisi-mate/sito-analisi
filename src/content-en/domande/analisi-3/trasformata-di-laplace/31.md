Let $f(t)=2+e^{-t}$. What is $sF(s)-f(0^+)$, with $F=\mathcal L[f]$?

- $\dfrac1{s+1}$
- $\dfrac2s-\dfrac1{s+1}$
- $-\dfrac1{s+1}$
- $-\dfrac1s$

## Explanation

The initial value is three. Multiplying $F(s)=\dfrac2s+\dfrac1{s+1}$ by $s$ gives $2+\dfrac{s}{s+1}$, and subtracting three leaves $-\dfrac1{s+1}$, the transform of the derivative $-e^{-t}$. The formula applies because the signal and its derivative are regular and of exponential order.
