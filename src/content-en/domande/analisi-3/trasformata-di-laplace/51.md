What is $(e^{-2t}*e^{-2t})(t)$, where the asterisk denotes causal convolution?

- $e^{-4t}$
- $\dfrac12te^{-2t}$
- $te^{-2t}$
- $\dfrac{1-e^{-2t}}2$

## Explanation

The two exponents add and the integration variable cancels. The product is constant on an interval of length $t$, so the exponential factor is multiplied by $t$. In the Laplace domain this gives a double pole.
