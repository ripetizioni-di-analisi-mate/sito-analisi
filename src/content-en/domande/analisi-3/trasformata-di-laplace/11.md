Use the Laplace transform to solve $y'+5y=1$ with $y(0)=0$ for $t\ge0$.

- $y(t)=1-e^{-5t}$
- $y(t)=\dfrac{e^{-5t}-1}{5}$
- $y(t)=\dfrac{1-e^{-5t}}{5}$
- $y(t)=e^{-5t}$

## Explanation

The derivative transform includes the initial value, which is zero. Decompose the quotient into partial fractions and invert. Substituting the solution confirms that the derivative plus the linear term equals one.

$$
(s+5)Y=\frac1s,\qquad Y=\frac1{s(s+5)}=\frac1{5}\left(\frac1s-\frac1{s+5}\right).
$$
