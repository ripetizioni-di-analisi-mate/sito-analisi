For $y'+2y=H(t-3)$ and $y(0)=1$, what is $y'(3^+)-y'(3^-)$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $e^{-6}$

## Explanation

The solution is locally absolutely continuous, so the one-sided limits of $2y$ agree at switch-on. Subtraction of the two one-sided derivative equations leaves the step size, one, independently of the initial condition.
