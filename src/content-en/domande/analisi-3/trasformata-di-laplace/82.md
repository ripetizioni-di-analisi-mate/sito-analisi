If $F(s)=\dfrac3s+\dfrac4{(s+1)^2}$, what is the final value of the causal response?

- $7$
- $3$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The term with a pole at the origin gives the constant three. The second term gives $4te^{-t}$ and tends to zero. The final value criterion also applies because multiplication by $s$ leaves only a pole at minus one.
