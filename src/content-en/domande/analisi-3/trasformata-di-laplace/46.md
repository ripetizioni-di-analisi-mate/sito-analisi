A periodic signal $f$ has period $T>0$. In the integral over period $k$, which factor multiplies the integral over $[0,T]$?

- $e^{-sT}$ for every $k$
- $e^{-skT}$
- $e^{-ks}$ independently of $T$
- $k e^{-sT}$

## Explanation

Set $t=kT+u$ to shift the interval back to the first period. Periodicity gives $f(kT+u)=f(u)$ and the weight factors as $e^{-skT}$ times $e^{-su}$. The first factor is independent of $u$.
