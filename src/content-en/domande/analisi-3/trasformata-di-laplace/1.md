Compute $\mathcal L[t^{5}](s)$ and give a half-plane of convergence.

- $\dfrac{120}{s^{6}},\quad\operatorname{Re}s>0$
- $\dfrac{120}{s^{5}},\quad\operatorname{Re}s>0$
- $\dfrac{240}{s^{6}},\quad\operatorname{Re}s>0$
- $\dfrac{120}{s^{6}},\quad\operatorname{Re}s<0$

## Explanation

The power grows polynomially, so the exponential weight gives absolute convergence when $s$ has positive real part. Five integrations by parts give the coefficient $5!$ and the sixth power of $s$ in the denominator.

$$
\mathcal L[t^n](s)=\frac{n!}{s^{n+1}},\qquad n=5,\quad 5!=120.
$$
