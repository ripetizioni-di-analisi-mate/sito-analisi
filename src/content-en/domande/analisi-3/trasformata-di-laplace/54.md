For $t>0$, what is the derivative of the causal convolution of $1$ and $\cos t$?

- $-\sin t$
- $\cos t$
- $\sin t$
- $1$

## Explanation

The constant leaves the integrand unchanged, so the convolution is the integral of $\cos u$ from zero to $t$. Since cosine is continuous, the fundamental theorem of calculus gives the derivative by evaluating the integrand at the upper endpoint.
