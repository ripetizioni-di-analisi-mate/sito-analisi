What is the causal inverse transform of $\dfrac{1}{\left(s + 3\right)^{2} + 25}$ for $t\ge0$?

- $\dfrac{e^{3 t} \sin{\left(5 t \right)}}{5}$
- $\dfrac{e^{- 3 t} \sin{\left(5 t \right)}}{5}$
- $e^{- 3 t} \sin{\left(5 t \right)}$
- $e^{- 3 t} \cos{\left(5 t \right)}$

## Explanation

The denominator is that of the sine transform for angular frequency five with parameter $s+3$. The tabulated numerator is five, so the given transform is one fifth of the transform of the damped sine. The signal is $\dfrac{e^{-3t}\sin(5t)}5$.
