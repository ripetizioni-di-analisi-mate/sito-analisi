Let $F(s)=\dfrac{s+1}{(s+1)^2}$. What order is the pole at $s=-1$ after cancellation?

- One
- Two
- Three
- The singularity is removable

## Explanation

The numerator contains one factor $s+1$ and the denominator contains two. Canceling leaves the reciprocal of $s+1$. The remaining power is one, so the time-domain contribution is an exponential without a linear factor in $t$.
