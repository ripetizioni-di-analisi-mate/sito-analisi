If $F(s)=\dfrac1{s^2}$ is the transform of $f(t)=t$, what is $\mathcal L[t^2f(t)](s)$?

- $-\dfrac6{s^4}$
- $\dfrac6{s^4}$
- $\dfrac2{s^3}$
- $\dfrac2{s^4}$

## Explanation

Each differentiation with respect to $s$ introduces $-t$ inside the integral. Two differentiations give positive sign. Differentiating $s^{-2}$ twice gives numerator six and a fourth-power denominator.
