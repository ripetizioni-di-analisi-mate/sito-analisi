Let $Y=\mathcal L[y]$, with $y(0)=6$ and $y^\prime(0)=-11$. What is $\mathcal L[y^{\prime\prime}]$?

- $s^2Y(s)-6s+11$
- $s^2Y(s)-6s-11$
- $sY(s)-6+11$
- $s^2Y(s)+6s-11$

## Explanation

Two integrations by parts introduce the boundary terms $-sy(0)$ and $-y'(0)$ alongside $s^2Y$. The first uses the initial value of the function. Here the second is positive because the prescribed initial velocity is negative.
