If $f$ has abscissa of absolute convergence $\sigma_0=1$, what is the abscissa for $H(t-2)f(t-2)$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Substitute $u=t-2$ in the absolute integral. The original integral is multiplied by the positive constant $e^{-2\operatorname{Re}s}$. A finite positive factor preserves the set of convergence, so the abscissa is unchanged.
