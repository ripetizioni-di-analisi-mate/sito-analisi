If $F(s)=\dfrac1{(s+1)(s+2)(s+3)}$ and $t>0$, which estimates show that the length of the left semicircle of radius $R$ centered at zero times the uniform bound on the integrand's modulus tends to zero?

- $|e^{st}|\le1$ and $|F(s)|=O(1)$
- $|e^{st}|\le e^{Rt}$ and $|F(s)|=O(R^{-3})$
- $|e^{st}|\le1$ and $|F(s)|=O(R^{-3})$
- $|e^{st}|\le1$ and $|F(s)|=O(R^{-1})$

## Explanation

On the left arc the real part of $s$ is nonpositive, so the exponential has modulus at most one. The arc length is $\pi R$. Multiplication by an upper bound of order $R^{-3}$ gives a bound of order $R^{-2}$.
