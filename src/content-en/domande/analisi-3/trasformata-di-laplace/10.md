For $f(t)=t^{-1}$ on $(0,1)$ and zero elsewhere, for which $s$ is the transform defined by an absolutely convergent integral?

- For every $s$
- For no $s$
- For $\operatorname{Re}s>0$
- For $\operatorname{Re}s>1$

## Explanation

Bounded support controls only the endpoint at infinity. On $(0,1)$ the exponential weight is bounded below in modulus by a positive constant for each fixed $s$. Thus the absolute integral diverges by comparison with $\dfrac1t$ near the origin.
