What is the causal convolution of $e^{-6t}$ and $e^{-11t}$ for $t\ge0$?

- $\dfrac{e^{- 6 t}}{5} - \dfrac{e^{- 11 t}}{5}$
- $e^{- 17 t}$
- $\dfrac{e^{- 6 t}}{2} + \dfrac{e^{- 11 t}}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{e^{- 6 t}}{5} + \dfrac{e^{- 11 t}}{5}$

## Explanation

Factor $e^{-6t}$ out of the convolution integral. The remaining integral is that of $e^{-5u}$ from zero to $t$. Evaluating at the endpoints gives $\dfrac{1-e^{-5t}}5$. Multiplication by the original factor gives the difference of the two exponentials.
