Compute $\mathcal L[t e^{-5t}](s)$ on its half-plane of absolute convergence.

- $\dfrac1{(s-5)^2},\quad\operatorname{Re}s>5$
- $\dfrac1{s+5},\quad\operatorname{Re}s>-5$
- $\dfrac1{(s+5)^2},\quad\operatorname{Re}s>-5$
- $\dfrac2{(s+5)^2},\quad\operatorname{Re}s>-5$

## Explanation

Combining $e^{-5t}$ with the weight $e^{-st}$ changes the parameter in the ramp transform to $s+5$. The ramp transform converges when this parameter has positive real part, that is, when $\operatorname{Re}s>-5$.

$$
\mathcal L[t](s)=\frac1{s^2},\qquad \mathcal L[e^{-5t}t](s)=\frac1{(s+5)^2}.
$$
