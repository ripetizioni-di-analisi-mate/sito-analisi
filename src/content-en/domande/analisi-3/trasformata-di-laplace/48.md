A periodic function $f$ equals one on $(0,1)$ and zero on $(1,2)$ during the first period of length two. Does its Laplace integral converge absolutely for $s=i\pi$?

- Yes, because the phase varies over each period
- Yes, because the weight is bounded
- No, because the weight grows in modulus
- No, because the integral of the modulus equals one in each period

## Explanation

For $s=i\pi$ the exponential weight has modulus one. The integral of the modulus over each period is therefore the on-time. The sum of these positive contributions, each equal to one, diverges.
