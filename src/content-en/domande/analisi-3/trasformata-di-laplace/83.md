Let $F(s)=\dfrac{2e^{-3s}}{s^2}$. What is the slope of the inverse transform for $t>3$?

- $0$
- $3$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of $s^2$ corresponds to the unit ramp. The exponential factor shifts the ramp's origin to time three, so after switch-on its value is $2(t-3)$. The coefficient of $t$ is the required slope.
