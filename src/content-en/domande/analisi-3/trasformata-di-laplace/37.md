For $f(t)=H(t-2)$ and $s>0$, what is $sF(s)-f(0^+)$ when $F=\mathcal L[f]$?

- $e^{-2s}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{e^{-2s}}s$
- $-e^{-2s}$

## Explanation

The integral starts at $t=2$, giving transform $\dfrac{e^{-2s}}s$. Since the initial value is zero, the required product equals $e^{-2s}$. The discontinuity at $2$ prevents interpreting this product as the transform of the ordinary derivative.
