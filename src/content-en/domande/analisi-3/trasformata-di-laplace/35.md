What is the causal inverse transform of $\dfrac{1}{s \left(s + 5\right)^{2}}$ for $t\ge0$?

- $1 - e^{- 5 t}$
- $\dfrac{\left(5 t - 1\right) e^{- 5 t}}{25} + \dfrac{1}{25}$
- $- \dfrac{\left(5 t + 1\right) e^{- 5 t}}{25} + \dfrac{1}{25}$
- $t e^{- 5 t}$

## Explanation

The factor $\dfrac1s$ represents convolution with the constant one. Integrate $te^{-5t}$ from zero to the current time. Integration by parts and endpoint evaluation give $\dfrac{1-(5t+1)e^{-5t}}{25}$.
