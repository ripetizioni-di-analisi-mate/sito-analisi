Solve $y^\prime+4y=7e^{-4t}$ with $y(0)=0$ for $t\ge0$.

- $- 7 t e^{- 4 t}$
- $7 e^{- 4 t}$
- $t e^{- 7 t}$
- $7 t e^{- 4 t}$

## Explanation

After transformation the zero initial value gives $(s+4)Y$. The right-hand side has the same denominator factor. Dividing by $s+4$ gives a double pole, so the inverse is the exponential $e^{-4t}$ times $7t$.
