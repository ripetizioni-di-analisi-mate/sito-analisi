Find the unilateral Laplace transform of $t^{3} e^{- 5 t}$.

- $\dfrac{6}{\left(s + 5\right)^{4}}$
- $\dfrac{6}{\left(s - 5\right)^{4}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\left(s + 5\right)^{4}}$
- $\dfrac{6}{\left(s + 5\right)^{3}}$

## Explanation

The cubic power has transform with three factorial in the numerator and the fourth power of the parameter in the denominator. The exponential shifts the parameter to $s+5$, giving $\dfrac6{(s+5)^4}$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>-5$.
