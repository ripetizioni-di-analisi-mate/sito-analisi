Two locally integrable functions of exponential order agree for $t\ne1$ and differ only at $t=1$. How do their transforms compare?

- They differ by $e^{-s}$
- They differ by $\dfrac{e^{-s}}s$
- They agree only for real $s$
- They are equal on the common half-plane

## Explanation

Changing the value at one point does not change a Laplace integral because a singleton has measure zero. Thus the transform determines a function only up to equality almost everywhere unless continuity assumptions are added.
