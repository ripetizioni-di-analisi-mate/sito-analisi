What is $\mathcal L[1-\cos t](s)$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>0$?

- $\dfrac1s+\dfrac{s}{s^2+1}$
- $\dfrac{s}{s^2+1}$
- $\dfrac1{s(s^2+1)}$
- $\dfrac1{s^2+1}$

## Explanation

Subtract the cosine transform from the transform of the constant. Both converge absolutely on the specified half-plane. Over a common denominator the numerator is $(s^2+1)-s^2$, leaving one.
