For $F(s)=\dfrac1{s^2}$, which statement about the final value criterion is correct?

- The pole at zero cancels completely in $sF(s)$
- The criterion gives final value zero
- A pole at zero remains in $sF(s)$
- The criterion gives final value one

## Explanation

Multiplication by $s$ lowers the pole order at the origin by one, leaving $\dfrac1s$. Thus the required condition that all poles lie strictly to the left fails. The inverse transform is a ramp and grows without bound.
