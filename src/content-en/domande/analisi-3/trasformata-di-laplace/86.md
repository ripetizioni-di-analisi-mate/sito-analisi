Let $f$ equal one on $(0,1)$ and zero elsewhere. What is $(f*f)(\dfrac32)$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac32$
- $0$

## Explanation

Both factors are one when $u$ and $\dfrac32-u$ belong to $(0,1)$. The intersection runs from one half to one. The convolution is the integral of one over this interval of length one half.
