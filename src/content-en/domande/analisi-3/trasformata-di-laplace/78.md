For $F(s)=\dfrac{2s+1}{s(s+3)}$, what is $f(0^+)$ for the causal response?

- $\dfrac13$
- $2$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

The causal inverse of this proper rational function is right-continuous at zero and of exponential order. The numerator and denominator of $sF(s)$ both have degree one. Their quotient tends to the ratio of the leading coefficients, namely two.
