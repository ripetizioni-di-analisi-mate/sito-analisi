For a causal signal $f$ with continuous derivative of exponential order, $F(s)=\dfrac{3}{s}-\dfrac{4}{s^2}+O(s^{-3})$ is known as $s\to+\infty$. What is $f'(0^+)$?

- $3$
- $-4$
- $-1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The initial-value theorem gives $f(0^+)=\lim sF(s)=3$. The transform of the derivative is $sF(s)-3$. Applying the theorem again yields $f'(0^+)=\lim s(sF(s)-3)=-4$.
