Find the unilateral transform of $H(t-6)e^{-11(t-6)}$, where $H$ is the unit step.

- $\dfrac{e^{- 6 s}}{s + 6}$
- $\dfrac{e^{- 6 s}}{s + 11}$
- $\dfrac{e^{- 11 s}}{s + 6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{s + 11}$

## Explanation

The exponential active after the delay is $e^{-11(t-6)}$. Moving the time origin to six gives the transform of $e^{-11t}$ times $e^{-6s}$. The result is $\dfrac{e^{-6s}}{s+11}$ on the common half-plane of convergence.
