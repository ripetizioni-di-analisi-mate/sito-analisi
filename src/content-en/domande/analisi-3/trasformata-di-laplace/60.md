If two continuous functions of exponential order have the same Laplace transform on a common half-plane, what follows?

- They agree only at integer times
- They may differ by any constant
- They agree only after some unspecified positive time
- They agree at every point of the half-line

## Explanation

The uniqueness theorem gives equality almost everywhere. Their difference is continuous, so a nonzero value would give a whole interval of nonzero values, contradicting equality almost everywhere.
