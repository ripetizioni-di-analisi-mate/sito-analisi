What is the inverse transform of $\dfrac2{(s+1)^3}$?

- $2t^2e^{-t}$
- $2te^{-t}$
- $t^2e^t$
- $t^2e^{-t}$

## Explanation

The cubed denominator gives a quadratic polynomial in time. The transform of $t^2$ has numerator $2!$, equal to the given coefficient. Replacing $s$ by $s+1$ introduces the factor $e^{-t}$.
