Which causal signal $f$ has Laplace transform $F(s)=\log\dfrac{s+1}{s}$ for $s>0$?

- $1-e^{-t}$
- $\dfrac{1-e^{-t}}{t}$
- $\dfrac{e^{-t}-1}{t}$
- $t\left(1-e^{-t}\right)$

## Explanation

We use the rule $\mathcal L[tf(t)](s)=-F'(s)$, which links the derivative of the transform to multiplication by $t$.
Since $F(s)=\log(s+1)-\log s$, we have $-F'(s)=\dfrac1s-\dfrac{1}{s+1}$.
This is the transform of $1-e^{-t}$, hence by uniqueness of the inverse transform $tf(t)=1-e^{-t}$.
It follows that $f(t)=\dfrac{1-e^{-t}}{t}$ for $t>0$.
The answer $1-e^{-t}$ comes from forgetting the division by $t$, while $\dfrac{e^{-t}-1}{t}$ comes from losing the minus sign in the rule.
