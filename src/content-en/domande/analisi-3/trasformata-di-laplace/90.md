For a causal signal $f$ of exponential order, with transform $F$, one knows $\mathcal L[tf(t)](s)=\dfrac{1}{(s+2)^2}$ for $s>0$. Which property of the transform of a piecewise continuous function makes it possible to recover $F$ without an undetermined constant?

- $F(0)=0$ for every causal signal.
- $F(s)\to0$ as $s\to+\infty$.
- $F(s)$ is always real and positive.
- $F'(s)$ vanishes identically.

## Explanation

The differentiation rule gives $-F'(s)=\dfrac{1}{(s+2)^2}$. Integrating yields $F(s)=\dfrac{1}{s+2}+C$. Decay of the transform along the positive real axis forces $C=0$.
