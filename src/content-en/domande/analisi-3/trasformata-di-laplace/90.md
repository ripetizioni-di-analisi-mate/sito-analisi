What is $\mathcal L[t e^{-t}\cos t](s)$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>-1$?

- $\dfrac{1-(s+1)^2}{((s+1)^2+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac{(s+1)^2-1}{((s+1)^2+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac{s+1}{((s+1)^2+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac1{(s+1)^2+1}$

## Explanation

The transform of $e^{-t}\cos t$ is $s+1$ divided by $(s+1)^2+1$. Multiplication by $t$ requires the negative derivative of this quotient. The quotient rule gives numerator $(s+1)^2-1$.
