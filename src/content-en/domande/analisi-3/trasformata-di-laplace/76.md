Let $F(s)=\dfrac{s-2}{s(s-2)(s+1)}$ on its causal half-plane. What is the final value?

- $0$
- $2$
- The response diverges
- $1$

## Explanation

The factor $s-2$ cancels, so the singularity at two is removable. The reduced $sF(s)$ has only the pole $-1$, so the final value criterion applies. The reduced ratio has value one at zero.
