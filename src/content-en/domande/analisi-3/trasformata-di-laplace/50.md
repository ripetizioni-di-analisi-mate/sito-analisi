What is the causal convolution of $1$ with $t^2$?

- $t^2$
- $\dfrac{t^3}3$
- $\dfrac{t^2}2$
- $3t^2$

## Explanation

The first factor is one throughout the causal integration interval, leaving the integral of $u^2$ from zero to $t$. Evaluating the cubic antiderivative at the upper endpoint gives the result.
