Let $f$ be locally absolutely continuous and of exponential order together with $f'$, and suppose $\mathcal L[f'](s)=sF(s)-4$. What is $f(0^+)$?

- $-4$
- $0$
- $4$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

In integration by parts the lower endpoint enters with a minus sign, and the exponential weight at zero is one. Thus the term subtracted from $sF(s)$ is the initial right-hand value of the function, namely four.
