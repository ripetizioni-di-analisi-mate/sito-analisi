In the inversion of $\dfrac1{(s+1)^2}$ with $t>0$, what is the residue of $\dfrac{e^{st}}{(s+1)^2}$ at $s=-1$?

- $te^{-t}$
- $e^{-t}$
- $-te^{-t}$
- $\dfrac{t^2}{2}e^{-t}$

## Explanation

The pole is double, so the residue is the first derivative of the analytic factor after removal of the pole. This factor is $e^{st}$. Its derivative with respect to $s$ is $te^{st}$ and must be evaluated at minus one.
