What is the transform of $e^t+e^{-t}$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$?

- $\dfrac2{s^2-1}$
- $\dfrac{2s}{s^2-1}$
- $\dfrac{2s}{s^2+1}$
- $\dfrac1{s-1}$

## Explanation

The exponential transforms are the reciprocals of $s-1$ and $s+1$. With a common denominator the numerators $s+1$ and $s-1$ add, canceling the constant term.
