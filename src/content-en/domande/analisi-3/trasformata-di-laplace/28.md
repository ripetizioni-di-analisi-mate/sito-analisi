What is $\mathcal L[t\sin t](s)$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>0$?

- $\dfrac{-2s}{(s^2+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{(s^2+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac{s}{s^2+1}$
- $\dfrac{2s}{(s^2+1)^2}$

## Explanation

Multiplication by $t$ corresponds to differentiation with respect to $s$ followed by a sign reversal. Start with the reciprocal of $s^2+1$, whose derivative has numerator $-2s$. The minus sign in the transform identity makes this numerator positive.
