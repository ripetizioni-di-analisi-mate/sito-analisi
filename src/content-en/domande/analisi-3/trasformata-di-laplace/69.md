For $y'+y=H(t-2)$ with $y(0)=0$, what is $y(2^+)$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $1-e^{-2}$
- $e^{-2}$

## Explanation

Until time two the equation is homogeneous and its zero-data solution is zero. After switch-on the response $1-e^{-(t-2)}$ is added. Its right-hand limit at two is zero. The step in the forcing changes the derivative while the solution value joins continuously.
