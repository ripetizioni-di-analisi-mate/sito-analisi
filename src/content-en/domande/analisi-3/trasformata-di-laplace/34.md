Let $y(0)=0$ and $y'(0)=3$. What is the transform of $y''-y$ for a sufficiently regular function of exponential order whose derivatives are also of exponential order?

- $(s^2-1)Y(s)-3s$
- $(s^2-1)Y(s)-3$
- $(s^2+1)Y(s)-3$
- $(s^2-1)Y(s)+3$

## Explanation

The second derivative formula contains two boundary terms. The one proportional to $s$ vanishes because $y(0)=0$. The constant term is the negative of the initial velocity. Subtracting $y$ adds $-Y(s)$ to the derivative contribution.
