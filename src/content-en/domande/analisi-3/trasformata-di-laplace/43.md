For $s\ne0$, what is the transform of the signal equal to $1$ for $0<t<2$ and $-1$ for $2<t<4$, and zero after time $4$?

- $\dfrac{1-e^{-4s}}s$
- $\dfrac{e^{-2s}-e^{-4s}}s$
- $\dfrac{(1-e^{-2s})^2}{s}$
- $\dfrac{(1-e^{-2s})^2}{s^2}$

## Explanation

Integrate the weight on the first interval and subtract the corresponding integral on the second. The resulting numerator is $1-2e^{-2s}+e^{-4s}$. Factoring it as a square leaves the single factor $s$ introduced by the antiderivative.
