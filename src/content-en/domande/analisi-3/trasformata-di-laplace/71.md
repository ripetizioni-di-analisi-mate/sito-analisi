Suppose $y$ is continuous, of exponential order, and satisfies $y(t)=2+\int_0^t y(u)\,du$. What is $Y(s)$?

- $\dfrac2{s+1}$
- $\dfrac2{s^2-1}$
- $\dfrac2{s-1}$
- $\dfrac2s$

## Explanation

The integral is the convolution of the constant one with $y$, so transforming the equation gives $Y=\dfrac2s+\dfrac Ys$. Multiplying by $s$ and collecting terms involving $Y$ yields the factor $s-1$ in the denominator.
