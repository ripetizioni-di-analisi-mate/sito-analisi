For $F(s)=\dfrac1{(s+1)^2(s+2)}$, what contribution comes from the simple pole at $-2$ alone in inversion by residues?

- $-e^{-2t}$
- $e^{-2t}$
- $te^{-2t}$
- $e^{-t}$

## Explanation

Multiply the integrand by $s+2$ to remove the simple pole factor and evaluate at $s=-2$. The factor $(s+1)^2$ becomes one, leaving the exponential evaluated at minus two.
