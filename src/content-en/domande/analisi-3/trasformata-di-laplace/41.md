For $\operatorname{Re}s>0$, compute $\mathcal L[H(t-2)t](s)$.

- $e^{-2s}\left(\dfrac1{s^2}+\dfrac2s\right)$
- $\dfrac{e^{-2s}}{s^2}$
- $e^{-2s}\left(\dfrac1{s^2}-\dfrac2s\right)$
- $\dfrac1{(s+2)^2}$

## Explanation

After switch-on write $t=(t-2)+2$, so the delayed function is $u\mapsto u+2$. The ramp and constant transforms add, and shifting the time origin introduces the factor $e^{-2s}$.
