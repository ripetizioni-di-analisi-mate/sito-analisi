Find the causal inverse Laplace transform of $\dfrac1{(s+5)(s+6)}$.

- $e^{-5t}-e^{-6t}$
- $e^{-5t}+e^{-6t}$
- $e^{-6t}-e^{-5t}$
- $te^{-5t}$

## Explanation

The poles are one unit apart. Multiply by the common denominator and evaluate the resulting identity at the two poles to obtain coefficients one and minus one. Each partial fraction then gives a decaying exponential.

$$
\frac1{(s+5)(s+6)}=\frac1{s+5}-\frac1{s+6}.
$$
