Let $y''+2y'+y=0$ with $y(0)=1$ and $y'(0)=0$. What is $Y(s)$?

- $\dfrac{s}{(s+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac{s+2}{(s+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac{s+2}{s^2+1}$
- $\dfrac1{(s+1)^2}$

## Explanation

The second derivative gives the initial term $-s$. The $2y'$ term adds the initial contribution $-2$. Moving both to the right gives numerator $s+2$, while the operator polynomial is the square of $s+1$.
