A signal of period three equals two on $(0,1)$ during the first period and zero during the rest. What is $\lim_{s\to0^+}sF(s)$?

- $\dfrac23$
- $2$
- $\dfrac13$
- $0$

## Explanation

The integral over one period tends to two because the weight tends to one during the on-interval. The ratio of $s$ to $1-e^{-3s}$ tends to one third. The product therefore gives the time average of the signal.
