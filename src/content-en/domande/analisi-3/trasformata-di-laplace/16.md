Find the unilateral Laplace transform of $e^{- 4 t} \cos{\left(7 t \right)}$.

- $\dfrac{s + 4}{\left(s + 4\right)^{2} - 49}$
- $\dfrac{s - 4}{\left(s - 4\right)^{2} + 49}$
- $\dfrac{7}{\left(s + 4\right)^{2} + 49}$
- $\dfrac{s + 4}{\left(s + 4\right)^{2} + 49}$

## Explanation

The cosine of angular frequency seven has transform with numerator $s$ and denominator $s^2+49$. Multiplication by $e^{-4t}$ replaces the parameter by $s+4$ in both. Thus the transform is $\dfrac{s+4}{(s+4)^2+49}$ on $\operatorname{Re}s>-4$.
