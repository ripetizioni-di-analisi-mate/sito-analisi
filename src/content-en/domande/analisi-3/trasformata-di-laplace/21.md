What is the half-plane of absolute convergence of the unilateral transform of $t e^{6 t}$?

- $\operatorname{Re}s>6$
- $\operatorname{Re}s>-6$
- $\operatorname{Re}s<6$
- $\operatorname{Re}s\ge6$

## Explanation

The integrand has modulus $t$ times the exponential with exponent $(6-\operatorname{Re}s)t$. When the real part exceeds six, the weight dominates the linear growth. On the boundary the modulus equals $t$, and to the left it grows at least as fast. The absolute integral diverges in both cases.
