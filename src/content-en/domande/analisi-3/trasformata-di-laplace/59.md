For $F(s)=\dfrac1{(s-1)(s+2)}$ on $\operatorname{Re}s>1$, which line can be used in Bromwich inversion?

- $\operatorname{Re}s=1$
- $\operatorname{Re}s=0$
- $\operatorname{Re}s=2$
- $\operatorname{Re}s=-3$

## Explanation

The real coordinate of the integration line must lie in the half-plane of the causal transform. The rightmost pole is at one and the boundary is excluded. Of the listed values, only the number two lies to the right of this boundary.
