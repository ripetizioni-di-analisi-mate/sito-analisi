Let $f(t)=3$ on $(2,5)$ and zero elsewhere. What is $\mathcal L[f](0)$?

- $6$
- $9$
- $15$
- $0$

## Explanation

At zero the exponential weight is one throughout the interval. The signal is bounded and has bounded support, so the transform value exists and equals the area of the rectangle of height three and width three.
