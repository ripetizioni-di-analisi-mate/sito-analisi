Which differential equation and initial conditions are equivalent to $y(t)=1+\int_0^t(t-u)y(u)\,du$ when $y$ is continuous?

- $y'=y$, $y(0)=1$
- $y''=y$, $y(0)=0$, $y'(0)=1$
- $y''=-y$, $y(0)=1$, $y'(0)=0$
- $y''=y$, $y(0)=1$, $y'(0)=0$

## Explanation

In the first differentiation the upper endpoint term is zero because the kernel contains $t-u$. The remaining term is the integral of $y$ from zero to $t$, whose derivative is $y$. The integral equation and its derivative at zero give the initial conditions.
