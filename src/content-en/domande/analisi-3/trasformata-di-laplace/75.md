Which term in the transform of $y'+y=H(t-2)$ with $y(0)=3$ comes solely from the initial condition?

- $\dfrac{3e^{-2s}}{s+1}$
- $\dfrac3s$
- $\dfrac3{s+1}$
- $\dfrac{e^{-2s}}{s(s+1)}$

## Explanation

The initial value enters the derivative transform as $-3$. Moving this term to the right and dividing by $s+1$ gives the homogeneous response. The delay factor belongs to the forced response.
