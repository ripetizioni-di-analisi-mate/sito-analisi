Find the transform of $H(t-5)(t-5)^2$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>0$.

- $\dfrac{e^{-5s}}{s^3}$
- $\dfrac{2e^{5s}}{s^3}$
- $\dfrac{2e^{-5s}}{s^3}$
- $\dfrac{2e^{-5s}}{s^2}$

## Explanation

The step makes the signal zero before time five. Substitution of $u=t-5$ recovers the transform of $u^2$. The exponential weight introduces the constant $e^{-5s}$, so the delay multiplies the tabulated result by that factor.

$$
\mathcal L[t^2](s)=\frac2{s^3},\qquad \mathcal L[H(t-5)(t-5)^2]=e^{-5s}\frac2{s^3}.
$$
