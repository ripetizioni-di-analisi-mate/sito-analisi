Compute $\mathcal L[e^{-t}\sin(5t)](s)$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>-1$.

- $\dfrac{5}{(s+1)^2+25}$
- $\dfrac{5}{(s-1)^2+25}$
- $\dfrac{s+1}{(s+1)^2+25}$
- $\dfrac{10}{(s+1)^2+25}$

## Explanation

The sine transform has the angular frequency, five, in the numerator and its square in the denominator. The additional weight $e^{-t}$ combines the exponents and replaces the transform parameter by $s+1$.

$$
\mathcal L[\sin(5t)](s)=\frac{5}{s^2+25},\qquad s\mapsto s+1.
$$
