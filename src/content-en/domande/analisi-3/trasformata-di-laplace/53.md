Let $g(t)=e^{-t}$ and $f(t)=H(t-2)(t-2)$. What is $(f*g)(1)$?

- $0$
- $1-e^{-1}$
- $e^{-1}$
- $1$

## Explanation

In the convolution at time one, $u$ runs from zero to one and the first factor is evaluated at $1-u$. This argument never exceeds one, so the step with threshold two vanishes throughout the integration interval.
