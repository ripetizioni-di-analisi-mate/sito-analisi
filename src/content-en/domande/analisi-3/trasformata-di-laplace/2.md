For $f(t)=\dfrac{e^{2t}}{(1+t)^2}$, at which points of the line $\operatorname{Re}s=2$ does the Laplace integral converge absolutely?

- Only at $s=2$
- At every point
- Only for $\operatorname{Im}s\ne0$
- At no point

## Explanation

On the given line the modulus of the signal times the exponential weight is $\dfrac1{(1+t)^2}$. Its integral over the half-line is finite and independent of the imaginary part, so absolute convergence holds everywhere on the line.
