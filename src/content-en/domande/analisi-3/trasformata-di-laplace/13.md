Solve $y''+25y=0$ with $y(0)=1$ and $y'(0)=0$ for $t\ge0$.

- $y(t)=\cos(5t)$
- $y(t)=\sin(5t)$
- $y(t)=e^{-5t}$
- $y(t)=\cosh(5t)$

## Explanation

The second derivative transform contains $-sy(0)$, which here equals $-s$, and $-y'(0)$, which vanishes. The resulting quotient has numerator $s$ and denominator $s^2+25$, so its inverse is a cosine of angular frequency five.

$$
(s^2+25)Y-s=0,\qquad Y=\frac{s}{s^2+25}.
$$
