For $\operatorname{Re}s>0$, what is the transform of $3t^2-2$?

- $\dfrac3{s^3}-\dfrac2s$
- $\dfrac6{s^2}-\dfrac2s$
- $\dfrac6{s^3}-\dfrac2s$
- $\dfrac{6-2}{s^3}$

## Explanation

The quadratic power has transform with two factorial in the numerator and the cube of $s$ in the denominator. The constant has transform proportional to the reciprocal of $s$. By linearity we may subtract these contributions in the common half-plane.
