If $\gamma$ is in the half-plane of convergence, which function of $t$ has Fourier transform $F(\gamma+i\omega)$ with kernel $e^{-i\omega t}$?

- $H(t)e^{\gamma t}f(t)$
- $H(t)e^{-\gamma t}f(2t)$
- $H(t)f(t)$
- $H(t)e^{-\gamma t}f(t)$

## Explanation

At $s=\gamma+i\omega$ the Laplace weight factors as $e^{-\gamma t}$ times $e^{-i\omega t}$. The first factor modifies the signal in time. The step gives its extension by zero to negative times.
