What expression for $Y(s)$ follows from $y''+y=\sin t$ with both initial values zero?

- $\dfrac1{s^2+1}$
- $\dfrac{s}{(s^2+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac1{s(s^2+1)}$
- $\dfrac1{(s^2+1)^2}$

## Explanation

The transformed left-hand side is $(s^2+1)Y(s)$ because the initial conditions are zero. The forcing transform is the reciprocal of the same polynomial. Dividing by the left-hand factor therefore squares the denominator.
