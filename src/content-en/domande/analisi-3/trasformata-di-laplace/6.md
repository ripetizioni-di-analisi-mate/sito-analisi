What is the open half-plane of absolute convergence for the transform of $\cosh(3t)-\sinh(3t)$?

- $\operatorname{Re}s>3$
- $\operatorname{Re}s>-3$
- $\operatorname{Re}s>0$
- $\operatorname{Re}s<3$

## Explanation

In the definitions of hyperbolic cosine and sine the growing term has the same coefficient. Subtracting leaves $e^{-3t}$, so the combined weight has modulus $e^{-(\operatorname{Re}s+3)t}$. The absolute integral converges exactly when the decay rate is positive.
