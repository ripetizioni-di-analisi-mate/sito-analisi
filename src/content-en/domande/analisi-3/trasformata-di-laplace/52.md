Let $f(t)=H(t-1)$ and $g(t)=H(t-2)$. For $t>3$, what is $(f*g)(t)$?

- $t-2$
- $t-1$
- $t$
- $t-3$

## Explanation

The first factor equals one when $t-u>1$, and the second requires $u>2$. For $t>3$ both conditions hold on the interval from two to $t-1$. The integrand is one there, so the convolution equals its length.
