What final value does $F(s)=\dfrac{2 s + 3}{s \left(s + 3\right)}$ give for its causal inverse transform?

- $3$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The product $sF(s)$ has just one pole with negative real part, so the final value theorem applies. The denominator is nonzero at $s=0$, so direct substitution of zero gives one.
