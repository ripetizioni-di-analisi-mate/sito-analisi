For $f(t)=e^{2t}-3e^t$ an upper bound is $4e^{2t}$. What is the actual half-plane of absolute convergence?

- $\operatorname{Re}s>1$
- $\operatorname{Re}s>3$
- $\operatorname{Re}s>0$
- $\operatorname{Re}s>2$

## Explanation

Factoring out the growing term gives $f(t)=e^{2t}(1-3e^{-t})$. For $t\ge\log 6$ the second factor lies between one half and one. Thus the modulus is comparable to the dominant exponential, and the threshold is exactly two.
