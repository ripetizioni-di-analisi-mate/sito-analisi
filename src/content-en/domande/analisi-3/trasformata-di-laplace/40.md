Which function has transform $e^{-3s}F(s+2)$, with $F=\mathcal L[f]$?

- $H(t-3)e^{-2t}f(t-3)$
- $H(t-3)e^{2(t-3)}f(t-3)$
- $H(t-2)e^{-3(t-2)}f(t-2)$
- $H(t-3)e^{-2(t-3)}f(t-3)$

## Explanation

Replacing $s$ by $s+2$ corresponds to multiplying $f(t)$ by $e^{-2t}$. The factor $e^{-3s}$ delays this entire signal by three time units. Thus both the exponential and the argument of $f$ depend on $t-3$.
