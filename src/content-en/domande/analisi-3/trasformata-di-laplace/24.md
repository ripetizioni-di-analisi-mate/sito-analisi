What is the transform of $\sin t\cos t$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>0$?

- $\dfrac{s}{(s^2+1)^2}$
- $\dfrac2{s^2+4}$
- $\dfrac1{s^2+1}$
- $\dfrac1{s^2+4}$

## Explanation

The time-domain product simplifies by $\sin(2t)=2\sin t\cos t$. Take the transform of a sine with angular frequency two and multiply by one half. The numerator factor two cancels.
