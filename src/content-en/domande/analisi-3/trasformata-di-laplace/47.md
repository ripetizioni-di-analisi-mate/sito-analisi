A signal of period two equals one on $(0,1)$ and minus one on $(1,2)$ during its first period. Does the improper integral $\int_0^\infty f(t)\,dt$ converge?

- Yes, because the integral over every complete period is zero
- Yes, because the signal is bounded
- No, because the partial integrals oscillate between zero and one
- No, because the partial integrals tend to $+\infty$

## Explanation

Integrating up to an even integer gives zero, while integrating up to an odd integer gives one. The two sequences of upper limits both tend to infinity but give different limits for the integral, so the improper integral diverges.
