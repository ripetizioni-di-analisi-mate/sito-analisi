For $F(s)=\dfrac{s+2}{(s+1)^2+4}$, which feature of the poles ensures that the causal response tends to zero?

- Both have real part $-1$
- Both are real
- Their imaginary parts have opposite signs
- The numerator has degree one

## Explanation

The denominator roots are $-1+2i$ and $-1-2i$. Thus the sinusoidal terms in the inverse transform share the factor $e^{-t}$, which tends to zero. The opposite signs of the imaginary parts give a real combination, but the real part determines the decay.
