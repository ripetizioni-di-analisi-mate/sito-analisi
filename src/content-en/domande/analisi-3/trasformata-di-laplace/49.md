A signal of period three switches on with height two on $(1,2)$ in the first period. What is $F(s)$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>0$?

- $\dfrac{2(e^{-s}-e^{-2s})}{s(1-e^{-3s})}$
- $\dfrac{2(1-e^{-s})}{s(1-e^{-3s})}$
- $\dfrac{2(e^{-s}-e^{-2s})}{s(1-e^{-s})}$
- $\dfrac{2(e^{-s}-e^{-2s})}s$

## Explanation

The first period contributes twice the integral of the weight from time one to time two. Each further period gives a copy shifted by three units, so the contributions form a geometric series of ratio $e^{-3s}$.
