For $y'+y=e^{-t}$ with $y(0)=0$, what is the solution?

- $te^{-t}$
- $e^{-t}$
- $1-e^{-t}$
- $te^{t}$

## Explanation

The forcing and the transformed operator each put a factor $s+1$ in the denominator of $Y$, giving a double pole. The transform pair for the reciprocal of $(s+1)^2$ gives a decaying exponential multiplied by $t$.
