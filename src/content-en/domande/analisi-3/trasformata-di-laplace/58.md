What is the causal inverse transform of $\dfrac1{(s+2)(s+5)}$ as an integral with a variable upper limit?

- $\displaystyle e^{-2t}\int_0^t e^{-5u}\,du$
- $\displaystyle e^{-2t}\int_0^t e^{-3u}\,du$
- $\displaystyle e^{-7t}\int_0^t 1\,du$
- $\displaystyle \int_0^t e^{-7u}\,du$

## Explanation

The rational factors are the transforms of exponentials with decay rates two and five. Their convolution has integrand $e^{-2(t-u)}e^{-5u}$. Factoring out the part independent of $u$ leaves an exponential whose rate is the difference of those rates.
