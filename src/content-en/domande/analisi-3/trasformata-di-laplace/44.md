What value does the signal $f(t)=\max(0,t-1)-\max(0,t-3)$ take for $t>3$?

- $0$
- $t-3$
- $2t-4$
- $2$

## Explanation

After time three both ramps are active, with values $t-1$ and $t-3$. Subtracting cancels the linear terms, so the signal stays constant at the value reached at the second change in slope.
