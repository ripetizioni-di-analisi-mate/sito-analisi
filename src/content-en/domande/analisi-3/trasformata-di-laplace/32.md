What initial value does $F(s)=\dfrac{2 s + 3}{\left(s + 2\right)^{2}}$ give for its causal inverse transform?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The initial value theorem uses the limit of $sF(s)$ along the positive real axis. The numerator and denominator have equal degrees, so the quotient tends to the ratio of their leading coefficients, namely two.
