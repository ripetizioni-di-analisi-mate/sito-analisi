For $F(s)=\dfrac1{s(s-2)}$, what happens to the causal signal as $t\to+\infty$?

- It tends to $+\infty$
- It tends to $-\dfrac12$
- It tends to zero
- It oscillates between bounded values

## Explanation

The product $sF(s)$ has a pole at two, so its algebraic limit at zero is not a final value. Partial fractions give the response $\dfrac{e^{2t}-1}{2}$, which grows without bound for positive times.
