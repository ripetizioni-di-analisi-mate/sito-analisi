Let $F(s)=\dfrac{1-e^{-2s}}s$. For the corresponding causal signal, which pair gives the initial right-hand value and the final value?

- $(0,1)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(1,0)$

## Explanation

The inverse transform of the first term is the constant one, and that of the second is the step delayed by two units. Their difference is one near zero and zero after time two, giving both limits directly.
