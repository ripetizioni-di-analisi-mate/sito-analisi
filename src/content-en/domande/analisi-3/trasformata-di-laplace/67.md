What is the solution of $y''+4y=0$ with $y(0)=0$ and $y'(0)=2$?

- $y(t)=2\sin(2t)$
- $y(t)=\cos(2t)$
- $y(t)=\sin(2t)$
- $y(t)=\sin t$

## Explanation

Zero initial displacement removes the term proportional to $s$ in the second derivative transform. The initial velocity gives numerator two. The denominator $s^2+4$ gives a sine of angular frequency two with the coefficient required by the given initial velocity.
