Let $f(t)=t^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}e^{-t}$ for $t>0$. Which statement describes integrability near $t=0$ in the Laplace integral?

- The integral diverges near zero for every $s$
- Convergence near zero requires $\operatorname{Re}s>0$
- Convergence near zero requires $\operatorname{Re}s<-1$
- The integral converges near zero for every $s\in\mathbb C$

## Explanation

The exponential weight is bounded in modulus on a bounded interval containing zero. Behavior at the origin is therefore governed by the power $t^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$, whose integral on $(0,1)$ is two. Conditions on the real part of $s$ concern the tail.
