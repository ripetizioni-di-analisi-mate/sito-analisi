If $f,g$ are nonnegative, integrable on the half-line, and have integrals $2$ and $3$ respectively, what is the integral of $f*g$?

- $5$
- $1$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

Set $v=t-u$ in the convolution double integral. The region becomes the quadrant and the product is $f(v)g(u)$. The integrand is nonnegative, so Tonelli's theorem separates the integrals, giving the product of the two masses.
