For $w=e^{x+iy}$, follow the path $z(t)=t+2it$. By what factor has the modulus of $w$ increased once the point has made one complete turn around the origin?

- $e^{2\pi}$
- $e$
- $e^\pi$
- $1$

## Explanation

The argument changes by $2t$ and the modulus is $e^t$. A full turn requires an increment $\Delta t=\pi$. The ratio of the moduli is therefore $\dfrac{e^{t+\pi}}{e^t}=e^\pi$, wherever the path starts.
