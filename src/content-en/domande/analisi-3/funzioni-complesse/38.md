What is the solution set of $e^z=4 i$, with $k\in\mathbb Z$?

- $z=4+i\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi\right)$
- $z=\ln 4+2ik\pi$
- $z=\ln 4+i\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi\right)$
- $z=\ln 4+i\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi\right)$

## Explanation

The number $4i$ has modulus four and argument $\dfrac\pi2$. Since the modulus of $e^{x+iy}$ is $e^x$, the real part of $z$ is $\log 4$. The imaginary part equals $\dfrac\pi2$ up to multiples of $2\pi$.
