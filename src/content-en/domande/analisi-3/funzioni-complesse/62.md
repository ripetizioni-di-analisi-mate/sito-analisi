What is the difference $L(-i)-\operatorname{Log}(-i)$ if $L$ takes arguments in $(0,2\pi)$ and $\operatorname{Log}$ takes arguments in $(-\pi,\pi)$?

- $2\pi i$
- $-2\pi i$
- $0$
- $\pi i$

## Explanation

The two branches have the same real part because $-i$ has modulus one. The argument chosen by $L$ is $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, while the principal argument is $-\dfrac\pi2$. Subtracting gives one full positive turn, corresponding to $2\pi i$.
