Where is $f(z)=5 z + 8 \overline{z}$ complex differentiable?

- Everywhere on the real axis.
- Nowhere.
- Throughout the plane.
- Only at zero.

## Explanation

The difference quotient at every point is $5+8\dfrac{\overline h}{h}$. For real increments the second factor is one, so the quotient is $13$. For imaginary increments the factor is minus one and the quotient is $-3$. The limits differ, ruling out a derivative at every point.
