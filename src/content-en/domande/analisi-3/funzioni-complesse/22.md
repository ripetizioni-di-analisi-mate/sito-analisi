What is the derivative of the principal branch of $\operatorname{Log} z$ at $z=5 + 8 i$?

- $\dfrac{5 - 8 i}{89}$
- $5 + 8 i$
- $\dfrac{5 + 8 i}{89}$
- $- \dfrac{5 - 8 i}{89}$

## Explanation

The point has positive imaginary part and therefore lies off the cut. The derivative of the principal branch is the reciprocal of $z$. Multiplying the numerator and denominator of $\dfrac{1}{5+8i}$ by $5-8i$ gives the denominator $25+64=89$.
