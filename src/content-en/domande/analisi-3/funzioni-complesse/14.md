For $f(x+iy)=\dfrac{xy}{x^2+y^2}$ away from the origin, what value of $f(0)$ makes the function continuous at zero?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- No such value exists.
- $1$

## Explanation

On the real axis the function is zero. Along the diagonal $x=y=t\ne0$ the numerator equals $t^2$ and the denominator $2t^2$, so the quotient is $\dfrac12$. The limits along the two paths differ, so no value at the origin gives continuity.
