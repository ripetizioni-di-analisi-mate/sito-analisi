Let $z_0$ be an accumulation point of the domain. If $\operatorname{Re}f(z)\to2$ and $\operatorname{Im}f(z)\to-3$ as $z\to z_0$, what follows?

- A path still needs to be specified.
- $f(z)\to2-3i$
- $f(z)\to2+3i$
- $f(z)\to\sqrt{13}$

## Explanation

The squared distance from $2-3i$ is the sum of the squared differences of the components. Both differences tend to zero by assumption, so the distance tends to zero and the complex limit is $2-3i$.
