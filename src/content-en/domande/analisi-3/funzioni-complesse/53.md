On which horizontal strip is the exponential injective?

- $0<\operatorname{Im}z<2\pi$
- $0<\operatorname{Im}z<4\pi$
- $-2\pi<\operatorname{Im}z<2\pi$
- $0<\operatorname{Im}z<3\pi$

## Explanation

If two exponential values coincide, their exponents differ by an integer multiple of $2\pi i$. On the open strip of width $2\pi$ the difference between the imaginary parts has modulus strictly less than $2\pi$, so this multiple must be zero.
