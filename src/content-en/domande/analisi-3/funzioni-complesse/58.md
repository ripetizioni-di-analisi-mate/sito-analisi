Which expression gives the imaginary part of $\sin(\dfrac\pi2+iy)$ for every $y\in\mathbb R$?

- $\cosh y$
- $0$
- $\sinh y$
- $y$

## Explanation

The addition formula gives $\sin(x+iy)=\sin x\cosh y+i\cos x\sinh y$. For real $y$ the hyperbolic sine is real, so the coefficient of $i$ is the imaginary part. At $x=\dfrac\pi2$ the cosine vanishes, so the imaginary part vanishes for every $y$. The real part is $\cosh y$.
