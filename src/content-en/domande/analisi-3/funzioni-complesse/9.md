Compute $\operatorname{Log}\!\left(e^{5}e^{\frac{3\pi i}{4}}\right)$ using the principal argument in $(-\pi,\pi)$.

- $5+\frac{11\pi i}4$
- $-5+\frac{3\pi i}4$
- $5-\frac{\pi i}4$
- $5+\frac{3\pi i}4$

## Explanation

The given number has modulus $e^5$, so the real part of its logarithm is $5$. The angle $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ already lies in $(-\pi,\pi)$, so it directly gives the imaginary part of the principal logarithm.
