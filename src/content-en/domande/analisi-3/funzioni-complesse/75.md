On two disjoint disks $D_1,D_2$ a harmonic function $u$ has a conjugate $v$. After fixing the value of $v$ at one point of $D_1$, what freedom remains when constructing all conjugates on $D_1\cup D_2$?

- An arbitrary additive constant on $D_2$.
- An arbitrary multiplicative constant on both disks.
- No freedom on either disk.
- An arbitrary harmonic function on $D_2$.

## Explanation

The Cauchy–Riemann equations determine both derivatives of a conjugate from $u$. The difference of two conjugates has zero gradient and is constant on each connected component. The prescribed value fixes only the constant on the first disk.
