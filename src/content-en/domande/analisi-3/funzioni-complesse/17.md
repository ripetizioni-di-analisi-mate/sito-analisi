At which point does $f(z)=z^{3} + 5 z$ fail to be conformal on any neighborhood of the point?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{15} i}{3}$
- $\sqrt{5} i$

## Explanation

The derivative is $3z^2+5$ and vanishes when $z^2=-\dfrac53$. Of the listed points, $\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}3i$ satisfies this condition. There the differential vanishes and the map is not conformal, while its derivative is nonzero at all the other points listed.
