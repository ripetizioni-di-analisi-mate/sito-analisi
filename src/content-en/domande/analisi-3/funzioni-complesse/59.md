What is the image under $e^z$ of the segment $z=it$ with $0<t<\pi$?

- The open upper half-disk.
- The entire unit circle.
- The upper unit semicircle without its endpoints.
- The lower unit semicircle without its endpoints.

## Explanation

On the segment the exponent has real part zero, so the image has modulus one. The argument $t$ varies strictly between zero and $\pi$. The image therefore consists of exactly the points on the unit circle with positive imaginary part.
