What is the second derivative of $f(z)=ze^z$?

- $(z+1)e^z$
- $2e^z$
- $ze^z$
- $(z+2)e^z$

## Explanation

Both factors are entire, so apply the product rule twice. The first derivative is $(z+1)e^z$. Differentiating again adds $e^z$ and $(z+1)e^z$, giving the final coefficient $z+2$.
