What is the principal argument of $11e^{\dfrac{8 i \pi}{7}}$ in the interval $(-\pi,\pi]$?

- $\dfrac{8 \pi}{7}$
- $\dfrac{6 \pi}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{6 \pi}{7}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{7}$

## Explanation

The positive real factor $11$ changes the modulus and leaves the argument unchanged. The given angle $\dfrac{8\pi}{7}$ exceeds $\pi$. Subtracting $2\pi$ gives $-\dfrac{6\pi}{7}$, which belongs to the required interval and is congruent to the original angle.
