For $f(z)=\dfrac{1}{z-1}$, which property holds as $z\to1$?

- $|f(z)|\to0$.
- $f(z)$ tends to a finite complex limit.
- The modulus diverges only along the real axis.
- $|f(z)|\to+\infty$ along every approach.

## Explanation

The modulus of the function is the reciprocal of the distance from $z$ to $1$. That distance is positive throughout the domain and tends to zero, so its reciprocal exceeds any fixed bound when $z$ is sufficiently close to $1$.
