For rays from the origin with arguments $0$ and $\dfrac\pi6$, what angle separates their images under $f(z)=z^2$?

- $\dfrac\pi2$
- $\dfrac\pi3$
- $\dfrac\pi{12}$
- $\dfrac\pi6$

## Explanation

For $z=re^{i\theta}$ we have $z^2=r^2e^{2i\theta}$, so each ray maps to the ray whose argument is twice the original one. The two image angles are zero and $\dfrac\pi3$. At the origin the derivative vanishes, so the angle preservation criterion does not apply.
