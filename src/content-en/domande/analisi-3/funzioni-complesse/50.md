Which condition on $u,v$ at $z_0$, together with the Cauchy–Riemann equations, is sufficient for $u+iv$ to be complex differentiable at $z_0$?

- Equality of the values $u(z_0)$ and $v(z_0)$.
- Real differentiability of both components at $z_0$.
- Continuity of both components at $z_0$ alone.
- Existence of the partial derivatives at $z_0$ alone.

## Explanation

Real differentiability gives a linear approximation with error $o(|h|)$. The two equations make the linear part multiplication by a complex number. After division by $h$ the error tends to zero in modulus, giving the limit of the difference quotient.
