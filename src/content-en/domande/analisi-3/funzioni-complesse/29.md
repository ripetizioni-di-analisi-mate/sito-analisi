For $f(z)=z+\overline z^2$, compute $f'(0)$ if it exists.

- $0$
- $1$
- It does not exist.
- $2$

## Explanation

The difference quotient at zero is $1+\dfrac{\overline h^2}{h}$. Its last term has modulus $|h|$, which tends to zero in every direction. Thus the derivative exists at the point, and the remaining value is one.
