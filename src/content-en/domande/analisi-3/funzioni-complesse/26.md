Compute $f^\prime(i)$ for $f(z)=\left(z + 7\right)^{4}$.

- $4 \left(7 + i\right)^{4}$
- $4 \left(7 - i\right)^{3}$
- $\left(7 + i\right)^{3}$
- $4 \left(7 + i\right)^{3}$

## Explanation

The inner function $z+7$ has derivative one, and the fourth power is entire. The chain rule therefore gives $4(z+7)^3$. At the given point the base is $7+i$, so the derivative is $4(7+i)^3$.
