Where do $u=4 x^{2} - 7 y^{2}$ and $v=8 x y$ satisfy the Cauchy–Riemann equations?

- The line $x=0$.
- The entire plane.
- The line $y=0$.
- The single point $(0,0)$.

## Explanation

The first equation $u_x=v_y$ holds throughout the plane because both sides equal $8x$. The second equation $u_y=-v_x$ requires $-14y=-8y$ and therefore reduces to $y=0$. Both equations hold exactly on the real axis.
