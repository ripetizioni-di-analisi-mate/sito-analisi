What is $\Delta u$ for $u(x,y)=5 x^{2} + 8 y^{2}$?

- $26$
- $-6$
- $0$
- $13$

## Explanation

The Laplacian is the sum of the two pure second derivatives. The term $5x^2$ contributes $10$ in the $x$ direction, and $8y^2$ contributes $16$ in the $y$ direction. Their sum is $26$, which is nonzero.
