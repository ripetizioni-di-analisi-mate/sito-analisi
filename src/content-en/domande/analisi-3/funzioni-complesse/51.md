Let $f(z)=\overline z^2$. Where does the complex derivative exist?

- At every point on the imaginary axis.
- At every real point.
- Nowhere.
- Only at $0$.

## Explanation

The difference quotient contains $2\overline{z_0}\dfrac{\overline h}{h}$ and a remainder of modulus $|h|$. The first term changes sign between the real and the imaginary directions, so the limit exists only when $z_0=0$. There only the remainder survives, and it tends to zero.
