What is the derivative of $P(z)=e^{\frac32\operatorname{Log}z}$ on the domain of the principal branch?

- $\dfrac32 e^{\frac12\operatorname{Log}z}$
- $\dfrac32 e^{\frac32\operatorname{Log}z}$
- $\dfrac{1}{z}$
- $e^{\frac12\operatorname{Log}z}$

## Explanation

The principal logarithm is holomorphic on its domain with derivative $\dfrac1z$. The chain rule gives $\dfrac{3}{2z}e^{\frac32\operatorname{Log}z}$. Since $z=e^{\operatorname{Log}z}$ on the domain, division subtracts $\operatorname{Log}z$ from the exponent and leaves the factor $\dfrac32$.
