Compute $f^\prime(0)$ for $f(z)=\dfrac{z + 4 i}{z - 7}$.

- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{7} - \dfrac{4 i}{49}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7} + \dfrac{4 i}{49}$

## Explanation

The quotient rule applies at zero because the denominator is $-7$ there. The numerator of the derivative simplifies to $(z-7)-(z+4i)=-7-4i$. The denominator squared at zero is $49$, giving $-\dfrac17-\dfrac{4i}{49}$.
