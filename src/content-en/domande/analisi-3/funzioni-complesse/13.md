For $f(z)=z^{6}$, find the local scale factor at $z_0=2$.

- $194$
- $384$
- $192$
- $-192$

## Explanation

The polynomial is holomorphic and its derivative $6z^5$ is nonzero at the given point. The differential therefore multiplies increments by $6\cdot2^5=192$. The modulus of that number is the length scale factor.
