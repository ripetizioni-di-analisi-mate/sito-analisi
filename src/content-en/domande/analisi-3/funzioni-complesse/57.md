Which expression equals $\overline{e^{x+iy}}$ for every real pair $x,y$?

- $-e^{x+iy}$
- $e^{-x-iy}$
- $e^{-x+iy}$
- $e^{x-iy}$

## Explanation

By Euler's formula, the exponential has real part $e^x\cos y$ and imaginary part $e^x\sin y$. Conjugation reverses only the sign of the latter. Since cosine is even and sine is odd, the resulting expression is $e^{x-iy}$.
