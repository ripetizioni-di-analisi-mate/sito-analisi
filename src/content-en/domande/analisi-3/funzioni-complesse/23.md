Find $v$ such that $u+iv$ is holomorphic, with $u=4 x^{3} - 12 x y^{2}$ and $v(0,0)=0$.

- $12 x^{2} y - 4 y^{3} + 4$
- $4 x^{3} - 12 x y^{2}$
- $- 12 x^{2} y + 4 y^{3}$
- $12 x^{2} y - 4 y^{3}$

## Explanation

The first Cauchy–Riemann equation gives $v_y=12x^2-12y^2$. Integrating with respect to $y$ gives $12x^2y-4y^3+C(x)$. The second equation requires $v_x=24xy$, so $C'(x)=0$. The value at the origin sets the constant to zero.
