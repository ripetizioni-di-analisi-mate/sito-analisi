What is $\displaystyle\lim_{t\to0^+}\operatorname{Log}(-2-it)$ for the principal branch?

- $\log 2-\pi i$
- $\log 2+\pi i$
- $\log 2$
- $-\log 2-\pi i$

## Explanation

The modulus of $-2-it$ tends to two, so the real part tends to $\log 2$. The points approach the negative real axis from the lower half-plane. There the principal argument tends to $-\pi$, determining the imaginary part of the limit.
