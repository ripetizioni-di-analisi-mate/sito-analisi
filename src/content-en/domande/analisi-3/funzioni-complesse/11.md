Determine where $f(z)=|z|^{10}$ is complex differentiable and find its derivative there.

- $\text{Only in }z=0,\qquad f'(0)=1$
- $\text{Nowhere}$
- $\text{At every point, with derivative }0$
- $\text{Only in }z=0,\qquad f'(0)=0$

## Explanation

The imaginary part is zero, so the Cauchy–Riemann equations require the gradient of $(x^2+y^2)^5$ to vanish. This happens only at the origin. There the modulus of the difference quotient is $|h|^9$, which tends to zero, so the derivative is zero.
