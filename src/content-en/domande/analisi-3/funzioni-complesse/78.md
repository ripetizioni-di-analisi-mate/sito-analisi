Which calculation rules out a global harmonic conjugate of $u(z)=\log|z|$ on $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$?

- The derivative $u_x$ vanishes at some point.
- $u$ is zero on the unit circle.
- The integral of $-u_y\,dx+u_x\,dy$ around the counterclockwise unit circle is $2\pi$.
- The Laplacian of $u$ vanishes on the domain.

## Explanation

A global conjugate would have differential $-u_y\,dx+u_x\,dy$, which would integrate to zero around every closed curve. With $x=\cos t$ and $y=\sin t$, the form simplifies to $dt$. Its integral around one counterclockwise turn is $2\pi$, contradicting the existence of a potential.
