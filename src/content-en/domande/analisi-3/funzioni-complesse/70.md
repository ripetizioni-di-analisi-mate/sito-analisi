For which of the given values of $z$ does $\operatorname{Log}(e^z)=z$ hold?

- $1+2\pi i$
- $1-3\pi i$
- $1+\pi i$
- $1-i$

## Explanation

Taking the logarithm of the modulus of $e^z$ recovers the real part of $z$. The imaginary part is chosen from the angles in $(-\pi,\pi)$. The value $-1$ belongs to that interval, whereas $2\pi$ is reduced to zero and angles congruent to $\pi$ lie on the cut.
