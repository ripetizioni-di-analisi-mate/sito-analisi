What locus does the equation $|e^{z^2}|=1$ describe for $z=x+iy$?

- The two lines $y=x$ and $y=-x$.
- The unit circle.
- The two coordinate axes.
- The single point $0$.

## Explanation

The exponential has modulus $e^{\operatorname{Re}(z^2)}$. Expanding the square of $x+iy$ gives real part $x^2-y^2$. The real exponential equals one only for a zero exponent. The factorization $(x-y)(x+y)=0$ gives two lines, and every point of both lines satisfies the modulus equation.
