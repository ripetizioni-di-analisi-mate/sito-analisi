Let $f$ be holomorphic on a connected domain and take only real values. What follows?

- $f'$ can equal $i$ at some point.
- $f$ must vanish identically.
- $f$ is constant.
- $f(z)=z$ throughout the domain.

## Explanation

The imaginary part is identically zero, so the Cauchy–Riemann equations force both partial derivatives of the real part to vanish. The real part is then locally constant, and connectedness makes it constant on the entire domain.
