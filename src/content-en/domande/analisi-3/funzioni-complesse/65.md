What is the principal square root of $-2i$?

- $-1+i$
- $-1-i$
- $1-i$
- $1+i$

## Explanation

The modulus of $-2i$ is two and its principal argument is $-\dfrac\pi2$. The principal square root has modulus $\sqrt2$ and argument $-\dfrac\pi4$. In Cartesian coordinates its real part is one and its imaginary part is minus one.
