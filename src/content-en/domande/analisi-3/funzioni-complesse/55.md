Which purely imaginary number with positive imaginary part satisfies $\cos z=2$?

- $i\log(2-\sqrt3)$
- $i\log(2+\sqrt3)$
- $i\log 2$
- $i\sqrt3$

## Explanation

Setting $z=iy$ with $y>0$ gives $\dfrac{e^y+e^{-y}}2=2$. Multiplying by $2e^y$ gives a quadratic equation in $e^y$. Its roots are $2\pm\sqrt3$ and only $2+\sqrt3$ exceeds one, so $y=\log(2+\sqrt3)$. The root below one would give a negative imaginary part.
