For which $a\in\mathbb R$ is $u=x^2+axy+2y^2$ harmonic on the plane?

- For every value of $a$.
- For $a=-3$.
- For no value of $a$.
- For $a=0$.

## Explanation

Both pure second derivatives of the mixed term $axy$ vanish. Thus $x^2$ contributes $2$ to the Laplacian and $2y^2$ contributes $4$. Their sum is always six, regardless of $a$.
