What is the local length scale factor at $z=i$ for $f(z)=\dfrac{z - 3}{z + 5}$?

- $\dfrac{13}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{13}$
- $\dfrac{3}{26}$
- $\dfrac{8}{25}$

## Explanation

The map is holomorphic near $i$ and its derivative is $\dfrac8{(z+5)^2}$ with nonzero numerator. At $i$ the denominator has modulus $|5+i|^2=26$, so the scale factor equals $\dfrac4{13}$.
