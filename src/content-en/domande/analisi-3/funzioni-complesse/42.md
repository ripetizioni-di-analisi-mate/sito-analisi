At $z_0$ the real differential of $f$ has matrix $\begin{pmatrix}2&-3\\3&2\end{pmatrix}$. What is the complex derivative?

- $2-3i$
- It does not exist.
- $3+2i$
- $2+3i$

## Explanation

The columns are the images of $1$ and $i$. The second is the first multiplied by $i$, so the real differential is multiplication by $2+3i$. Since the real differential exists by assumption, this form implies complex differentiability.
