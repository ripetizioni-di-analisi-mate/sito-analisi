Let $f=u+iv$ be holomorphic, with $u_x(z_0)=1$ and $u_y(z_0)=2$. What is $f'(z_0)$?

- $1-2i$
- $2-i$
- $2+i$
- $1+2i$

## Explanation

The complex derivative may be computed using real increments and equals $u_x+iv_x$. The Cauchy–Riemann equations require $v_x=-u_y$. Substituting the data gives $1-2i$, with the sign of the imaginary part determined by the second equation.
