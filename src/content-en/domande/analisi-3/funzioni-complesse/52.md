Which purely imaginary period of the exponential has the smallest positive imaginary part?

- $2\pi$
- $i$
- $2\pi i$
- $\pi i$

## Explanation

A period $iT$ with real $T$ must satisfy $e^{iT}=1$ because the exponential never vanishes. Thus the sine of $T$ must be zero and the cosine one. The smallest positive $T$ satisfying both conditions is $2\pi$.
