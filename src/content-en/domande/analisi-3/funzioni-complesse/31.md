What is $\lim_{z\to 5 i}\dfrac{z^2+25}{z-5 i}$?

- $0$
- $- 10 i$
- $10 i$
- $5 i$

## Explanation

The numerator is the difference of squares $(z-5i)(z+5i)$. At every point of the domain $z-5i$ is nonzero and cancels. This leaves the polynomial $z+5i$, which is continuous even at the excluded point and takes the value $10i$ at $5i$.
