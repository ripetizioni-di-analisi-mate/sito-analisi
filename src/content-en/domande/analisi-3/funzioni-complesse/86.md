Let $g$ be the local inverse of $f(z)=z^2$ satisfying $g(-1)=i$. What is $g'(-1)$?

- $2i$
- $-\dfrac i2$
- $-2i$
- $\dfrac i2$

## Explanation

The branch condition specifies $i$ as the point at which to evaluate the derivative of $f$. There it equals $f'(i)=2i\ne0$, so the inverse function theorem applies. Differentiating $f(g(w))=w$ with respect to $w$ and substituting $w=-1$ gives $2i\,g'(-1)=1$. The reciprocal of $2i$ is $-\dfrac i2$.
