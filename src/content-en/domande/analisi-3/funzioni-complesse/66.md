Which $z$ satisfies $\operatorname{Log}z=2+2\pi i$ for the principal branch with argument in $(-\pi,\pi)$?

- $e^2i$
- $e^2$
- $-e^2$
- No such $z$.

## Explanation

By definition, the imaginary part of every value of the principal logarithm lies strictly between $-\pi$ and $\pi$. The proposed number has imaginary part $2\pi$, outside that interval. Its exponential is $e^2$, whose principal logarithm equals $2$.
