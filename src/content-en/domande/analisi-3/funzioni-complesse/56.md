What is the solution set of $e^z=2e^{-z}$?

- $\{\log 2+2ik\pi:k\in\mathbb Z\}$
- $\{\dfrac{\log 2}{2}+ik\pi:k\in\mathbb Z\}$
- $\{\dfrac{\log 2}{2}+2ik\pi:k\in\mathbb Z\}$
- $\{\log 2+ik\pi:k\in\mathbb Z\}$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $e^z$, which is never zero, gives $e^{2z}=2$. The modulus fixes the real part of $2z$ at $\log 2$, and the positive real value has argument zero modulo $2\pi$. Dividing the entire exponent by two gives the required family.
