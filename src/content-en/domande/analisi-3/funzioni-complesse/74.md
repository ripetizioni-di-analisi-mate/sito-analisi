On which set is $u(x,y)=\log(x^2+y^2)$ harmonic?

- Only on the line $y=0$.
- Nowhere in its domain.
- On the entire plane except the origin.
- Throughout the plane, including the origin.

## Explanation

On the punctured plane the second derivatives exist and are continuous. The one with respect to $x$ is $\dfrac{2(y^2-x^2)}{(x^2+y^2)^2}$, and the one with respect to $y$ is its negative. Their sum vanishes, so the function is harmonic on its domain.
