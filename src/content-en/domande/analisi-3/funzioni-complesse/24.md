What is $\displaystyle\lim_{z\to0}\dfrac{\overline z^2}{|z|}$?

- The limit depends on the argument.
- $-1$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

The modulus of the numerator is $|z|^2$, so the quotient has modulus exactly $|z|$. The angular factor can vary with the approach, but the distance of the value from zero tends to zero throughout the domain. This establishes the complex limit.
