The difference quotient of $f$ at $z_0$ is $3+R(h)$, with $|R(h)|\le |h|^2$ for every sufficiently small nonzero increment. What is $f'(z_0)$?

- The derivative may depend on the direction.
- $3$
- $0$
- $9$

## Explanation

The remainder estimate holds for every sufficiently small increment and its upper bound tends to zero. Therefore $R(h)$ tends to zero along every path. The definition of the complex derivative gives the limit $3$.
