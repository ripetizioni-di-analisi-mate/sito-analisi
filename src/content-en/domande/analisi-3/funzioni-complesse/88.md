What is the image under $w=\dfrac{z-2}{z+2}$ of the half-plane $\operatorname{Re}z<0$ with its pole $-2$ excluded?

- $\{w:|w|=1\}$
- $\{w:\operatorname{Re}w<0\}$
- $\{w:|w|<1\}$
- $\{w:|w|>1\}$

## Explanation

For $\operatorname{Re}z<0$ the distance to $2$ exceeds the distance to $-2$, so the modulus of the quotient exceeds one. If $\lvert w\rvert>1$, the inverse formula $z=2\dfrac{1+w}{1-w}$ is defined and its real part is $2\dfrac{1-\lvert w\rvert^2}{\lvert1-w\rvert^2}<0$. This value of $z$ differs from $-2$ because equality would imply $1+w=-(1-w)$.
