What is the range of $\dfrac{\overline z}{z}$ as $z\ne0$ varies?

- The unit circle.
- Only the real segment $[-1,1]$.
- The right half-plane.
- The open unit disk.

## Explanation

Writing $z=re^{i\theta}$ gives $e^{-2i\theta}$. The radius $r$ cancels, so the modulus is always one. As $\theta$ ranges over $\mathbb R$, $-2\theta$ covers all angles, so the entire unit circle is attained.
