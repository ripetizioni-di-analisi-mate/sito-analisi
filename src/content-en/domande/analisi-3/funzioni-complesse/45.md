Let $f(x+iy)=x^2+i\,2xy$. Where is it complex differentiable?

- On the line $x=0$.
- On the line $y=0$.
- Only at zero.
- Throughout the plane.

## Explanation

The polynomial components are real differentiable. The first equation is $2x=2x$, which holds throughout the plane. The second requires $0=-2y$, so the sufficient criterion applies exactly on the real axis.
