What is the modulus of $\dfrac{\left(5 + 8 i\right)^{2}}{89}$?

- $1$
- $13$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The modulus is multiplicative, so $\lvert(5+8i)^2\rvert=\lvert5+8i\rvert^2$. The squared modulus is the sum $5^2+8^2=89$. The denominator is positive real, so its modulus equals the denominator itself. Both moduli are eighty-nine and their ratio is one.
