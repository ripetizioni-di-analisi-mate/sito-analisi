What is the set of zeros of $\sin z$?

- $\{ik\pi:k\in\mathbb Z\}$
- $\{k\pi:k\in\mathbb Z\}$
- $\{2k\pi:k\in\mathbb Z\}$
- $\{\dfrac\pi2+k\pi:k\in\mathbb Z\}$

## Explanation

The exponential formula for sine gives $e^{iz}=e^{-iz}$. Multiplying by $e^{iz}$ yields $e^{2iz}=1$. The complex exponential equals one precisely when its argument is an integer multiple of $2\pi i$, so $2iz=2k\pi i$. Dividing by $2i$ gives the zeros $z=k\pi$.
