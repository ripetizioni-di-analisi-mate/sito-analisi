Find the modulus of $e^{4 + 7 i}$.

- $e^{-4}$
- $11$
- $e^{7}$
- $e^{4}$

## Explanation

By Euler's formula, $e^{4+7i}$ is the product of $e^4$ and $\cos7+i\sin7$. The second factor has modulus one because the squares of sine and cosine sum to one. The modulus of the product is therefore $e^4$.
