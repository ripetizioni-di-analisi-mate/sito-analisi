On the punctured plane $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$, which function is a global harmonic conjugate of $u(x,y)=x$?

- $-y$
- $y$
- No such function exists.
- $\log|z|$

## Explanation

The function $z=x+iy$ is entire, so its restriction to the punctured plane remains holomorphic. Thus its component $y$ is a global conjugate of $x$ even on this domain. Simple connectivity suffices to guarantee a conjugate for every harmonic function.
