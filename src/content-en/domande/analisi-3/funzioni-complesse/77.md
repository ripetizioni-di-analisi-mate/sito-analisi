For $f(z)=z^2=u+iv$, what are the slopes of the tangents to the level curves $u=0$ and $v=2$ at $(1,1)$, in that order?

- $1$ and $-1$
- $-1$ and $1$
- Both equal $1$.
- Both equal $0$.

## Explanation

In the first quadrant the level set $u=0$ is the line $y=x$, of slope one. The level set $v=2$ satisfies $xy=1$ and can be written as $y=\dfrac1x$. Its derivative at $x=1$ is $-1$, so the tangents are perpendicular.
