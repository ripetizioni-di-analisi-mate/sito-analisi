For the harmonic conjugate of $u=x^3-3xy^2+2y$ with $v(0,0)=0$, which term must be added to $3x^2y-y^3$?

- $2x$
- $-2x$
- $0$
- $-2y$

## Explanation

Integrating $v_y=u_x$ with respect to $y$ gives $3x^2y-y^3+C(x)$. The other equation gives $v_x=6xy-2$. Comparing this with the derivative of the antiderivative gives $C'(x)=-2$, and the condition at the origin sets the additive constant to zero.
