Compute the derivative of $f(z)=e^{z^2+iz}$ at $z=0$.

- $-i$
- $i$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The exponential and the inner polynomial are entire, so the chain rule applies. The derivative is $(2z+i)e^{z^2+iz}$. At zero the exponential factor equals one, and the inner function has derivative $i$.
