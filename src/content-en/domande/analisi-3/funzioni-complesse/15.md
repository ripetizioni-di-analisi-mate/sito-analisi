Compute $\sin\left(6 i\right)$.

- $- i \sinh{\left(6 \right)}$
- $i \sinh{\left(6 \right)}$
- $i \cosh{\left(6 \right)}$
- $\sinh{\left(6 \right)}$

## Explanation

The exponential definition of sine gives $\sin(6i)=\dfrac{e^{-6}-e^6}{2i}$. By the definition of the hyperbolic sine, the numerator is $-2\sinh 6$. The reciprocal of the imaginary unit is $-i$, so division gives $i\sinh 6$. The hyperbolic sine of a real argument is real, so the result is purely imaginary.
