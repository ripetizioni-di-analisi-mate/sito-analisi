Along which path does $f(x+iy)=\dfrac{x^2y}{x^4+y^2}$ fail to tend to zero at the origin, even though it tends to zero along every line through the origin?

- $y=0$
- $y=x$
- $x=0$
- $y=x^2$

## Explanation

Along the parabola $y=x^2$ both terms in the denominator equal $x^4$, while the numerator is $x^4$. The quotient therefore equals $\dfrac12$ everywhere on that path. The numerator vanishes on the axes, and the quotient tends to zero along the diagonal.
