Let $T$ be real linear with $T(1)=2$ and $T(i)=i$. What is the coefficient $B$ in the representation $T(h)=Ah+B\overline h$?

- $-\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

Evaluation at $1$ gives $A+B=2$. Evaluation at $i$ followed by division by $i$ gives $A-B=1$. Subtracting the second equation from the first yields $2B=1$. Thus the coefficient of the conjugate term is $\dfrac12$.
