Which of the following limits equals zero?

- $\displaystyle\lim_{z\to0}\dfrac{(\operatorname{Im}z)^3}{|z|^2}$
- $\displaystyle\lim_{z\to0}\dfrac{1}{|z|}$
- $\displaystyle\lim_{z\to0}\dfrac{\overline z}{z}$
- $\displaystyle\lim_{z\to0}\dfrac{(\operatorname{Re}z)^2}{|z|^2}$

## Explanation

Since $|\operatorname{Im}z|\le|z|$, the modulus of $\dfrac{(\operatorname{Im}z)^3}{|z|^2}$ is at most $|z|$ and tends to zero. The homogeneous quotients of degree zero vary with direction, while the reciprocal of the modulus diverges.
