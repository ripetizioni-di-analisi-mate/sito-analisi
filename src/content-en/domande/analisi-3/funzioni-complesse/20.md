Consider the disk with center $-2$ and radius $\dfrac12$. The principal branch of $\operatorname{Log} z$ excludes the nonpositive real axis. Which statement is true?

- The principal branch is defined throughout the disk.
- Every branch of the logarithm has a singularity at $-2$.
- The disk contains a zero of $z$.
- The disk admits a holomorphic branch of the logarithm because it avoids the origin.

## Explanation

The disk is simply connected and its distance from the origin is positive, so it admits a holomorphic branch of the logarithm. The center $-2$ lies on the principal branch cut, which prevents using the principal branch throughout the disk.
