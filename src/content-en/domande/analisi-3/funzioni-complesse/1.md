Compute $f'(1)$ for $f(z)=z^{6}+5z$.

- $6$
- $12$
- $11$
- $-11$

## Explanation

The polynomial is entire, so the sum and power rules apply throughout the plane. The derivative is $6z^5+5$. At $z=1$ the power equals one, giving $6+5=11$.
