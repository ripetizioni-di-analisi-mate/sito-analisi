Let $f(x+iy)=e^x\cos(2y)+i e^x\sin(2y)$. Which points have a neighborhood on which it is holomorphic?

- Only the origin.
- The real axis.
- The entire plane.
- Nowhere.

## Explanation

The partial derivatives of $u$ and $v$ are continuous. The equation $u_x=v_y$ requires $\cos(2y)=0$, while $u_y=-v_x$ requires $\sin(2y)=0$. These conditions are incompatible because the sine and cosine of the same angle cannot both vanish.
