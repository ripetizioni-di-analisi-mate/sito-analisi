Let $f(z)=\dfrac{\overline z}{z}$ for $z\ne0$. Which pair of limits do $z=t$ and $z=it$ give, in that order, with $t\to0^+$?

- $(1,-1)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(-1,1)$

## Explanation

For $z=t$ the conjugate equals $z$, so the quotient is one. For $z=it$ the conjugate is $-it$ and the quotient is minus one. These distinct values rule out a complex limit.
