What rotation does the differential of $f(z)=e^z$ induce at $z_0=\log2+\dfrac{\pi i}{2}$?

- Zero rotation.
- $\dfrac\pi2$ counterclockwise.
- $\dfrac\pi2$ clockwise.
- $\pi$.

## Explanation

The exponential is its own derivative and has value $2i$ at the given point. This is nonzero, so the differential acts by multiplication by $2i$. Its argument is $\dfrac\pi2$, giving a counterclockwise quarter turn.
