What is the image of the line $\operatorname{Re}z=1$ under $w=\dfrac1z$?

- $|w-\dfrac12|=\dfrac12$ with $w=0$ excluded.
- $|w|=1$.
- $\operatorname{Re}w=1$.
- $|w-1|=1$ with $w=0$ excluded.

## Explanation

For nonzero $w=p+iq$ the inverse has real part $\dfrac{p}{p^2+q^2}$. Setting it equal to one gives $p^2+q^2=p$. Completing the square gives center $\dfrac12$ and radius $\dfrac12$. The origin is excluded because $\dfrac1z$ never vanishes.
