Which half-axis, together with the origin, is excluded from the domain of the logarithm with argument in $(-\dfrac\pi2,\dfrac{3\pi}{2})$?

- The negative real axis.
- The positive real axis.
- The negative imaginary axis.
- The positive imaginary axis.

## Explanation

The endpoints of the interval differ by $2\pi$ and represent the same direction, that of $e^{-\frac{\pi i}{2}}=-i$. For points on that half-axis no equivalent argument belongs to the open interval, so the cut is along the negative imaginary axis.
