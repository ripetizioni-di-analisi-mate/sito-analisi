What is the image of the real axis under $w=\dfrac{z-i}{z+i}$?

- The open unit disk.
- The positive imaginary axis.
- The entire unit circle.
- The unit circle with $1$ removed.

## Explanation

Every real point is equidistant from $i$ and $-i$, so its image has modulus one. The value $w=1$ is excluded because it would require $z-i=z+i$. For every other point on the circle the quotient $\dfrac{1+w}{1-w}$ is purely imaginary. Indeed, using $\overline w=\dfrac1w$ shows that its conjugate is its negative. The inverse formula $z=i\dfrac{1+w}{1-w}$ therefore gives a real preimage.
