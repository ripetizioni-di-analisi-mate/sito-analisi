Which value at zero makes $f(z)=\dfrac{e^z-1}{z}$, defined for $z\ne0$, continuous there?

- $0$
- $1$
- $-1$
- No such value exists.

## Explanation

The quotient is the difference quotient of the exponential at zero. The exponential is entire, so the limit exists in every direction and equals its derivative at zero, namely $1$. Continuity requires the assigned value to equal that limit.
