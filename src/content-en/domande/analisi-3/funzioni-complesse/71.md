Let $L_1,L_2$ be two branches of the logarithm on a connected domain, with $L_1(z_0)=L_2(z_0)$. What follows?

- $L_1-L_2$ may vary from point to point.
- $L_1=L_2$ throughout the domain.
- $L_1=-L_2$ throughout the domain.
- The domain must be a disk.

## Explanation

Both derivatives equal $\dfrac1z$, so their difference has zero derivative and is constant on the connected domain. Equality at $z_0$ sets the constant to zero, so the branches agree everywhere.
