What is the smallest angular separation between two distinct cube roots of $8i$?

- $\dfrac\pi6$
- $\pi$
- $\dfrac\pi3$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

All cube roots have modulus two. Their arguments are one third of the arguments $\dfrac\pi2+2k\pi$ of $8i$. The three distinct values modulo $2\pi$ are thus equally spaced around the circle, with minimum separation $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.
