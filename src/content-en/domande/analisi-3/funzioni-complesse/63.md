Let $z=w=e^{\frac{2\pi i}{3}}$. What is $\operatorname{Log}(zw)-\operatorname{Log}z-\operatorname{Log}w$ for the principal branch?

- $2\pi i$
- $-2\pi i$
- $-\pi i$
- $0$

## Explanation

The arguments of the factors sum to $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$, which exceeds $\pi$. Subtracting $2\pi$ gives the principal argument of the product. All moduli are one, so the real parts vanish and only the imaginary correction $-2\pi i$ remains.
