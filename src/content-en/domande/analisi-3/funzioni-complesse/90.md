Two curves have velocities $1$ and $-i$ at $z_0$. If $f$ is holomorphic with $f'(z_0)=1+i$, what is the oriented angle from the first image tangent to the second, modulo $2\pi$?

- $-\dfrac\pi4$
- $0$
- $\dfrac\pi2$
- $-\dfrac\pi2$

## Explanation

The image velocities are obtained by multiplication by $1+i$. This nonzero factor cancels in the ratio of the second velocity to the first, giving $-i$. Its argument is $-\dfrac\pi2$ modulo $2\pi$, so the oriented angle equals the angle between the original tangents.
