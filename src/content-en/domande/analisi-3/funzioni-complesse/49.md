If $f=u+iv$ is holomorphic on a neighborhood and $\nabla u(z_0)=(3,-4)$, what is $\nabla v(z_0)$?

- $(3,4)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(-3,-4)$
- $(-4,3)$

## Explanation

The Cauchy–Riemann equations give $v_x=-u_y$ and $v_y=u_x$. From the data $u_y=-4$ and $u_x=3$, the gradient of the imaginary part has components $4$ and $3$. Its dot product with the gradient of $u$ is zero.
