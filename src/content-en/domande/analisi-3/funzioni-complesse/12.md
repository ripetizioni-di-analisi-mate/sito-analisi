What is $\displaystyle\lim_{z\to i}\dfrac{z-i}{z+i}$?

- The limit does not exist.
- $1$
- $i$
- $0$

## Explanation

At $z=i$ the denominator equals $2i$ and is nonzero. The numerator and denominator are polynomials, so their quotient is continuous near the point. Substitution gives zero divided by $2i$, hence zero.
