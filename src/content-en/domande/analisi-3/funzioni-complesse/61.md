For which positive real value of $r$ does $e^z$ map the line $\operatorname{Re}z=\log 3$ onto the circle $|w|=r$?

- $3$
- $e^3$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\log 3$

## Explanation

The exponential of $\log 3+iy$ has modulus $e^{\log 3}=3$ and argument congruent to $y$ modulo $2\pi$. As $y$ ranges over the entire real line every angle occurs, so the image is exactly the circle of radius three.
