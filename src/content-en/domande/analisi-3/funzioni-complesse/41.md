Compute $f'(1)$ for $f(z)=ze^{\frac1z}$, defined for $z\ne0$.

- $0$
- $e$
- $2e$
- $-e$

## Explanation

The product and chain rules apply near $1$, where the reciprocal is holomorphic. They give $f'(z)=e^{\frac1z}(1-\dfrac1z)$. At the given point the two contributions cancel because the factor $1-\dfrac1z$ is zero.
