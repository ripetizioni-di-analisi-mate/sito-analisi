What is the real Jacobian determinant of $f(z)=(2+i)z+1$?

- $\sqrt5$
- $-5$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The constant term does not contribute to the differential, which is multiplication by $2+i$. Its real matrix has columns $(2,1)$ and $(-1,2)$. The determinant is $4+1=5$, the squared modulus of the multiplier.
