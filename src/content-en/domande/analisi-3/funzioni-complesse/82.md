Which harmonic conjugate $v$ of $u(x,y)=xy$ satisfies $v(1,0)=0$?

- $\dfrac{y^2-x^2+1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{y^2+x^2-1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{x^2-y^2-1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{y^2-x^2}{2}$

## Explanation

The first Cauchy–Riemann equation requires $v_y=y$. Integrating with respect to $y$ at fixed $x$ gives $v=\dfrac{y^2}{2}+C(x)$. The second equation requires $v_x=-x$, so $C(x)=-\dfrac{x^2}{2}+c$. At $(1,0)$ the value is $-\dfrac12+c$. Setting it to zero determines the constant $\dfrac12$.
