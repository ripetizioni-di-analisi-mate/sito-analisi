$\dfrac{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}{16} + \dfrac{\left(y - 5\right)^{2}}{4}=1$. What is the semimajor-axis length of the ellipse?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

The denominators in standard form are the squares of the semiaxis lengths. The larger one is sixteen. The result is $4$.
