The left-hand side of the equation
$$
2x^2 - xy - y^2 + 5x + y + 2 = 0
$$
factors as the product of two first-degree factors. Which lines does the equation describe?

- $2x + y - 1 = 0$ and $x - y - 2 = 0$
- $2x - y + 1 = 0$ and $x + y + 2 = 0$
- $2x + y + 1 = 0$ and $x - y + 2 = 0$
- $2x + y + 2 = 0$ and $x - y + 1 = 0$

## Explanation

We solve the equation as a quadratic equation in $y$, written as $y^2 + (x - 1)y - (2x^2 + 5x + 2) = 0$. The discriminant is
$$
(x - 1)^2 + 4(2x^2 + 5x + 2) = 9x^2 + 18x + 9 = 9(x + 1)^2 ,
$$
which is a perfect square. So $y = \dfrac{-(x - 1) \pm 3(x + 1)}{2}$, that is, $y = x + 2$ or $y = -2x - 1$. Therefore the conic is the union of the lines $x - y + 2 = 0$ and $2x + y + 1 = 0$, intersecting at the point $(-1, 1)$.
