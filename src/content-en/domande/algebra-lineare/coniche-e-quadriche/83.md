The quadric
$$
x^2 + 2y^2 + 2x - 4z + 5 = 0
$$
is an elliptic paraboloid. What is its vertex?

- $(-1, 0, 1)$
- $(1, 0, -1)$
- $\left( -1, 0, \dfrac{5}{4} \right)$
- $(-1, 0, -1)$

## Explanation

Completing the square we have $x^2 + 2x = (x + 1)^2 - 1$, so the equation becomes $(x + 1)^2 + 2y^2 + 4 = 4z$, that is,
$$
(x + 1)^2 + 2y^2 = 4(z - 1) .
$$
With the translation $X = x + 1$, $Y = y$, $Z = z - 1$ we obtain $X^2 + 2Y^2 = 4Z$, which has its vertex at the new origin. Therefore the vertex is the point $(-1, 0, 1)$.
