What is the directrix of the parabola $y^2 = 6x$?

- $x = -\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x = -3$
- $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x = -6$

## Explanation

The parabola $y^2 = 2px$ has focus $\left( \dfrac{p}{2}, 0 \right)$ and directrix $x = -\dfrac{p}{2}$. Comparing with $y^2 = 6x$ we get $2p = 6$, so $p = 3$. Therefore the directrix is the line $x = -\dfrac{3}{2}$, while the focus is the point $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, 0 \right)$.
