## Explanation

The matrix associated with the conic is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -2 & 2 \\ -2 & -1 & -1 \\ 2 & -1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
and its determinant is $I_3 = 16$, so the conic is non-degenerate. The block of the quadratic terms has determinant $I_2 = 2 \cdot (-1) - (-2)^2 = -6 < 0$, therefore the conic is a **hyperbola**. A hyperbola is rectangular if and only if $I_1 = a_{11} + a_{22} = 0$. Here $I_1 = 2 - 1 = 1$, so the hyperbola **is not rectangular**.
