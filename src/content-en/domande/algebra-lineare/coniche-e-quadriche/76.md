$x^{2} - 8 x + y^{2} - 10 y + 25=0$. What are the coordinates of the circle center?

- $\left(5,4\right)$
- $\left(4,-5\right)$
- $\left(-4,-5\right)$
- $\left(4,5\right)$

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $(x-a)^2$ and $(y-b)^2$. The center makes both squared terms vanish. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$ gives $\left(4,5\right)$.
