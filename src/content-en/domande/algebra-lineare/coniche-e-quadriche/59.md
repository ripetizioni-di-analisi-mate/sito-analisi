What is the distance between the foci of the ellipse
$$
6x^2 + 4xy + 3y^2 - 14 = 0 ?
$$

- $2\sqrt{5}$
- $\sqrt{5}$
- $2\sqrt{7}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The block $A_0 = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ has characteristic polynomial $\lambda^2 - 9\lambda + 14 = (\lambda - 2)(\lambda - 7)$, and $I_2 = 14$. There are no linear terms, so the center is the origin and the associated matrix is block diagonal, with $I_3 = I_2 \cdot (-14)$; therefore $\dfrac{I_3}{I_2} = -14$. By the **canonical form of central conics** the equation becomes $2X^2 + 7Y^2 = 14$, that is, $\dfrac{X^2}{7} + \dfrac{Y^2}{2} = 1$. Then $c^2 = 7 - 2 = 5$, and the distance between the foci equals $2c = 2\sqrt{5}$. An orientation-preserving rigid motion preserves distances, so this is also the distance in the original plane.
