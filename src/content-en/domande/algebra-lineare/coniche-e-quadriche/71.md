$\dfrac{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}{16} + \dfrac{\left(y + 2\right)^{2}}{9}=1$. How far is each focus from the center?

- $\sqrt{7}$
- $1$
- $7$
- $5$

## Explanation

The squared semiaxis lengths are sixteen and nine. The center-to-focus distance is the square root of their difference. The result is $\sqrt{7}$.
