Which points are centers of symmetry of the conic
$$
x^2 - 2xy + y^2 - 1 = 0 ?
$$

- All the points of the line $y = x$
- Only the point $(0, 0)$
- All the points of the line $y = -x$
- No point

## Explanation

The system for the center is
$$
\begin{cases}
x - y = 0 \\[4pt]
-x + y = 0
\end{cases}
$$
and the two equations coincide, so its solutions are all the points of the line $y = x$. Indeed $x^2 - 2xy + y^2 - 1 = (x - y - 1)(x - y + 1)$, therefore the conic consists of the parallel lines $x - y = 1$ and $x - y = -1$. The line $y = x$ is equidistant from the two, so the symmetry with respect to each of its points exchanges the two lines and maps the conic onto itself.
