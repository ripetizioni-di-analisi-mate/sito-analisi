A conic has equation
$$
\begin{pmatrix} x & y & 1 \end{pmatrix} M \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = 0 ,
\qquad
M = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 2 \\ 6 & 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the matrix associated with the conic?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 & 6 \\ 4 & 2 & 2 \\ 6 & 2 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 & 0 \\ 4 & 2 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The matrix associated with a conic is symmetric, while $M$ is not, so we first write the equation. Expanding the product we obtain $x^2 + (4 + 0)xy + 2y^2 + (0 + 6)x + (2 + 0)y - 1 = 0$, that is, $x^2 + 4xy + 2y^2 + 6x + 2y - 1 = 0$. The associated matrix contains half of the coefficients of $xy$, $x$ and $y$, therefore $a_{12} = 2$, $a_{13} = 3$ and $a_{23} = 1$. This matrix coincides with $\dfrac{M + M^T}{2}$.
