What conic is the locus of the points $P$ of the plane whose distance from the point $F = (1, 0)$ is twice their distance from the line $x = 4$?

- A real ellipse
- A hyperbola
- A parabola
- A pair of intersecting lines

## Explanation

The condition $\overline{PF} = 2\, d(P, r)$ is equivalent to $\overline{PF}^2 = 4\, d(P, r)^2$, that is,
$$
(x - 1)^2 + y^2 = 4(x - 4)^2 .
$$
Expanding we obtain $3x^2 - y^2 - 30x + 63 = 0$, so $I_2 = -3 < 0$. The determinant of the associated matrix is $I_3 = 3 \cdot (-1) \cdot 63 - (-15)^2 \cdot (-1) = -189 + 225 = 36 \neq 0$, therefore the conic is non-degenerate and is a **hyperbola**. Indeed, completing the square we obtain $\dfrac{(x - 5)^2}{4} - \dfrac{y^2}{12} = 1$.
