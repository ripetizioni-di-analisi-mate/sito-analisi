$\dfrac{z^{2}}{16} + \dfrac{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}{9} + \dfrac{\left(y + 2\right)^{2}}{4}=1$. What is the maximum positive height of the ellipsoid?

- $2$
- $16$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

To maximize the $z^2$ term, set the nonnegative $x$ and $y$ terms to zero. This leaves $z^2/16=1$. The result is $4$.
