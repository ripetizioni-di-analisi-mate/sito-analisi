What are the semi-axes of the ellipse
$$
6x^2 + 4xy + 9y^2 - 8x + 14y + 1 = 0 ?
$$

- $2$ and $1$
- $1$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\sqrt{2}$ and $1$
- $\sqrt{5}$ and $\sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

The block $A_0 = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & 2 \\ 2 & 9 \end{pmatrix}$ has $I_2 = 54 - 4 = 50$ and characteristic polynomial $\lambda^2 - 15\lambda + 50 = (\lambda - 5)(\lambda - 10)$, so the eigenvalues are $5$ and $10$. The determinant of the associated matrix is $I_3 = -500$, therefore $\dfrac{I_3}{I_2} = -10$. By the **canonical form of central conics** the equation becomes $5X^2 + 10Y^2 = 10$, that is, $\dfrac{X^2}{2} + Y^2 = 1$. Hence the semi-axes are $\sqrt{2}$ and $1$.
