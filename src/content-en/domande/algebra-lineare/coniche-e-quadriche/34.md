For which values of $k$ is the conic
$$
x^2 + kxy + 4y^2 + 2x - 1 = 0
$$
a parabola?

- $k = \pm 2$
- $k = \pm 8$
- $k = \pm 4$
- $k = \pm 16$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $xy$ is $2a_{12} = k$, so $a_{12} = \dfrac{k}{2}$ and $I_2 = 4 - \dfrac{k^2}{4}$. The condition $I_2 = 0$ gives $k^2 = 16$, that is, $k = \pm 4$. The determinant of the associated matrix is $I_3 = \dfrac{k^2}{4} - 8$, which for $k = \pm 4$ equals $-4 \neq 0$. Therefore for both values the conic is non-degenerate with $I_2 = 0$, that is, a **parabola**.
