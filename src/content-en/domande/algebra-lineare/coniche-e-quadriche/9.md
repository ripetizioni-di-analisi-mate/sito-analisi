For which value of $k$ is the conic
$$
x^2 + 2xy + ky^2 + 2x + 2y = 0
$$
degenerate?

- $k = 1$
- $k = -1$
- $k = 0$
- $k = \pm 1$

## Explanation

The associated matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & k & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
and its determinant is $I_3 = 1 - k$. The conic is degenerate if and only if $I_3 = 0$, so only for $k = 1$. In this case the equation is $(x + y)^2 + 2(x + y) = 0$, that is, $(x + y)(x + y + 2) = 0$, and the conic consists of two **parallel lines**. For $k = -1$ we have $I_3 = 2 \neq 0$ and $I_1 = 0$, therefore the conic is a rectangular hyperbola.
