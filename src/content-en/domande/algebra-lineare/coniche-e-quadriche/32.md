For which value of $c$ is the conic
$$
x^2 + 6xy + 9y^2 + 2x + 6y + c = 0
$$
a pair of coincident lines?

- $c = 1$
- $c = 0$
- $c = -1$
- $c = 2$

## Explanation

The left-hand side depends on $x$ and $y$ only through $x + 3y$, and can be written as
$$
(x + 3y)^2 + 2(x + 3y) + c = (x + 3y + 1)^2 + c - 1 .
$$
For $c = 1$ the equation is $(x + 3y + 1)^2 = 0$, that is, the line $x + 3y + 1 = 0$ counted twice, and indeed $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$. For $c < 1$ we obtain two real and distinct parallel lines, for $c > 1$ two complex conjugate lines.
