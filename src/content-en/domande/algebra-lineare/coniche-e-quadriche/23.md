What kind of conic is
$$
9x^2 - 12xy + 4y^2 + x - 2y = 0 ?
$$

- A pair of parallel lines
- A parabola
- A real ellipse
- A hyperbola

## Explanation

The associated matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 9 & -6 & \frac{1}{2} \\ -6 & 4 & -1 \\ \frac{1}{2} & -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The block of the second-degree terms has $I_2 = 36 - 36 = 0$, because the second-degree part is the square $(3x - 2y)^2$. The determinant is $I_3 = -4 \neq 0$, so the conic is non-degenerate. By the **classification of non-degenerate conics**, with $I_2 = 0$ the conic is a **parabola**. Parallel lines would occur only with $I_3 = 0$.
