Consider the orientation-preserving rigid motion
$$
\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{5} \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -4 \\ 4 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} X \\ Y \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What coordinates $(X, Y)$ does the point $P = (4, 6)$ have in the new frame?

- $\left( -\dfrac{7}{5}, \dfrac{24}{5} \right)$
- $(5, 0)$
- $\left( \dfrac{36}{5}, \dfrac{2}{5} \right)$
- $(3, 4)$

## Explanation

We solve the formula for $\begin{pmatrix} X \\ Y \end{pmatrix}$, first subtracting the translation and then applying the inverse of the rotation $R$. Since $R$ is orthogonal, we have $R^{-1} = R^T$. The difference between $P$ and the new origin is $(4 - 1, 6 - 2) = (3, 4)$, so
$$
\begin{pmatrix} X \\ Y \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{5} \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 4 \\ -4 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{5} \begin{pmatrix} 25 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Therefore $P$ lies on the new $X$-axis, at distance $5$ from the new origin.
