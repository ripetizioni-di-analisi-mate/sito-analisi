$\dfrac{z^{2}}{16} + \dfrac{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}{4} + \dfrac{\left(y - 3\right)^{2}}{9}=1$. In the section $z=0$, what is the maximum value of $x$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $0$
- $6$

## Explanation

The section is an ellipse centered at first coordinate $a$. The rightmost point has $y=b$ and lies two units from the center. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$ gives $4$.
