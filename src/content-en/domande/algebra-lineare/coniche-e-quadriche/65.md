$3 x^{2} + 12 x + 16=y$. What are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola?

- $\left(-2,-4\right)$
- $\left(4,-2\right)$
- $\left(-2,4\right)$
- $\left(2,4\right)$

## Explanation

Complete the square to obtain vertex form. The x-coordinate of the vertex makes the square vanish and its y-coordinate is the remaining term. The result is $\left(-2,4\right)$.
