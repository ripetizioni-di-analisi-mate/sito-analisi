Which inequality is satisfied by all the points of the parabola
$$
(2x + y)^2 = -5(x - 2y + 1) ?
$$

- $x - 2y + 1 \geq 0$
- $x - 2y + 1 \leq 0$
- $2x + y \geq 0$
- $2x + y \leq 0$

## Explanation

The lines $2x + y = 0$ and $x - 2y + 1 = 0$ are perpendicular, since $2 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot (-2) = 0$, so they are the axis of the parabola and the tangent at the vertex. The left-hand side is a square, therefore it is greater than or equal to $0$, and then also $-5(x - 2y + 1) \geq 0$, that is, $x - 2y + 1 \leq 0$. So the whole parabola lies on the same side of the tangent at the vertex. The expression $2x + y$, instead, takes values of both signs, because the axis divides the parabola into two symmetric branches.
