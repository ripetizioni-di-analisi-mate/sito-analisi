$3 x^{2} + 12 x + 16=y$. What are the coordinates of the parabola vertex?

- $\left(-2,-4\right)$
- $\left(4,-2\right)$
- $\left(-2,4\right)$
- $\left(2,4\right)$

## Explanation

Complete the square to obtain vertex form. The vertex’s first coordinate cancels the square and its second coordinate is the remaining constant. The result is $\left(-2,4\right)$.
