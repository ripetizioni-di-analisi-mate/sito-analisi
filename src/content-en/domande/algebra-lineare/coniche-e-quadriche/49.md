$\dfrac{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}{9} - \dfrac{\left(y - 3\right)^{2}}{4}=1$. What is the positive slope of one hyperbola asymptote?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

For the asymptotes, equate the two quadratic terms in centered coordinates. The slope magnitude is $2/3$. The result is $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
