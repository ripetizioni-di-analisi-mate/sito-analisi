Let $P$ be the point of the parabola $y^2 = 8x$ with abscissa $8$ and positive ordinate. What is the distance from $P$ to the focus of the parabola?

- $6$
- $8$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

We have $2p = 8$, so $p = 4$, the focus is $(2, 0)$ and the directrix is the line $x = -2$. Every point of the parabola is equidistant from the focus and the directrix, and the distance from $P = (8, y_P)$ to the directrix equals $8 + 2 = 10$. Therefore the distance from $P$ to the focus also equals $10$. A direct computation confirms this, since $y_P^2 = 64$ gives $P = (8, 8)$ and $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10$.
