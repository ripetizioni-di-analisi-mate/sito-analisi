The matrix associated with a quadric is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 & -2 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & -1 & 1 \\ -2 & 0 & 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the equation of the quadric?

- $x^2 - z^2 + xy + 3yz - 2x + z + 4 = 0$
- $x^2 - z^2 + 2xy + 6xz - 4x + 2z + 4 = 0$
- $x^2 - z^2 + 2xy + 6yz - 4x + 2y + 4 = 0$
- $x^2 - z^2 + 2xy + 6yz - 4x + 2z + 4 = 0$

## Explanation

The diagonal entries $1$, $0$, $-1$, $4$ are the coefficients of $x^2$, $y^2$, $z^2$ and the constant term. Off the diagonal the matrix contains half of the coefficients, so each entry must be doubled. Since $a_{12} = 1$ and $a_{23} = 3$, the mixed terms are $2xy$ and $6yz$, while $a_{13} = 0$ means that there is no $xz$ term. Since $a_{14} = -2$ and $a_{34} = 1$, the first-degree terms are $-4x$ and $2z$. Therefore the equation is $x^2 - z^2 + 2xy + 6yz - 4x + 2z + 4 = 0$.
