An orientation-preserving rigid motion takes the quadric
$$
x^2 + 4xy + y^2 - 2z = 0
$$
to canonical form. Which equation is obtained?

- $3X^2 + Y^2 = 2Z$
- $3X^2 - Y^2 = 2\sqrt{3}\, Z$
- $3X^2 - Y^2 = 2Z$
- $X^2 - Y^2 = 2Z$

## Explanation

The block $A_0$ has its third row equal to zero, and its minor $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ has characteristic polynomial $\lambda^2 - 2\lambda - 3 = (\lambda - 3)(\lambda + 1)$; so the eigenvalues of $A_0$ are $3$, $-1$, $0$ and $\det A_0 = 0$. The associated matrix is block diagonal, with $a_{34} = -1$, therefore $\det A = (1 - 4)(0 - 1) = 3 \neq 0$. By the **canonical form of quadrics** with $\det A_0 = 0$ we have
$$
q^2 = -\frac{\det A}{\lambda_1 \lambda_2} = -\frac{3}{3 \cdot (-1)} = 1 .
$$
So the canonical form is $3X^2 - Y^2 = 2Z$, a **hyperbolic paraboloid**.
