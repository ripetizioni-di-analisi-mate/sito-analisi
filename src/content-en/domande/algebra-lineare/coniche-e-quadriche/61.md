$x^{2} - 6 x + y^{2} + 4 y + 4=0$. What are the coordinates of the circle center?

- $\left(-3,2\right)$
- $\left(-2,3\right)$
- $\left(3,-2\right)$
- $\left(3,2\right)$

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $(x-a)^2$ and $(y-b)^2$. The center makes both squared terms vanish. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$ gives $\left(3,-2\right)$.
