In the plane $y = 0$ consider the parabola $z = x^2$. What is the equation of the surface obtained by rotating this parabola about the $z$-axis?

- $z^2 = x^2 + y^2$
- $z = x^2 - y^2$
- $z = x^2$
- $z = x^2 + y^2$

## Explanation

The distance of the point $(x, y, z)$ from the $z$-axis is $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$. In the rotation about the $z$-axis every point describes a circle with constant height and constant distance from the axis. Hence $(x, y, z)$ belongs to the surface if and only if the point $(r, 0, z)$ of the plane $y = 0$ belongs to the parabola, that is, if $z = r^2$. Therefore the surface has equation $z = x^2 + y^2$, and it is an **elliptic paraboloid** of revolution. The equation $z^2 = x^2 + y^2$ describes instead the cone obtained by rotating the line $z = x$.
