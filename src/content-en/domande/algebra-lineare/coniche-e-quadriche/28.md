The conic $x^2 + 2xy + y^2 + 4x = 0$ is a parabola. What is the parameter $p$ of its canonical form $Y^2 = 2pX$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The associated matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
with $I_3 = \det A = -4$ and $I_1 = 2$. By the theorem on the **canonical form of the parabola**
$$
p = \sqrt{-\frac{I_3}{I_1^3}} = \sqrt{\frac{4}{8}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} .
$$
