An orientation-preserving rigid motion takes the conic
$$
x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2 - 2x + 2y = 0
$$
to the form $\lambda_1 X^2 + \lambda_2 Y^2 + c = 0$. What is $c$?

- $0$
- $-6$
- $3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

We have $I_2 = 3 - 1 = 2 \neq 0$, so the conic has a center and $c$ is the value of the left-hand side at the center. The system for the center is
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y - 1 = 0 \\[4pt]
x + 3y + 1 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting the first equation from the second we obtain $2y + 2 = 0$, so $y = -1$, $x = 2$ and the center is $(2, -1)$. Therefore $c = 4 - 4 + 3 - 4 - 2 = -3$, in agreement with $\dfrac{I_3}{I_2} = \dfrac{-6}{2}$. The constant term of the original equation equals $0$ because the conic passes through the origin, but the translation to the center changes it.
