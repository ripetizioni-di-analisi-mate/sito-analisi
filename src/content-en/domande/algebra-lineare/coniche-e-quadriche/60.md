$\left(x - 2\right)^{2} + \left(y - 3\right)^{2}=4$. What is the first coordinate of the circle’s leftmost point?

- $4$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The center has first coordinate $a$ and the radius is two. The leftmost point lies two units to the left of the center. Substituting $a=2$ gives $0$.
