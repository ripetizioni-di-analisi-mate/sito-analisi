What are the foci of the ellipse
$$
\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1 ?
$$

- $(\pm \sqrt{34}, 0)$
- $(\pm 4, 0)$
- $(0, \pm 4)$
- $(\pm 16, 0)$

## Explanation

The semi-axes are $a = 5$ on the $x$-axis and $b = 3$ on the $y$-axis. Since $a > b$, the foci lie on the $x$-axis at the points $(\pm c, 0)$, with $c^2 = a^2 - b^2 = 25 - 9 = 16$. Hence $c = 4$ and the foci are $(\pm 4, 0)$. The relation $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$ holds for the hyperbola and would give the value $\sqrt{34}$.
