The line through the focus of the parabola $y^2 = 12x$ and perpendicular to its axis meets the parabola at two points. How long is the chord joining them?

- $6$
- $12$
- $24$
- $3$

## Explanation

Comparing $y^2 = 12x$ with the canonical form $y^2 = 2px$ we get $2p = 12$, so $p = 6$. The focus is therefore the point $\left( \dfrac{p}{2}, 0 \right) = (3, 0)$. The axis of the parabola is the $x$-axis, so the line through the focus perpendicular to the axis is $x = 3$. Substituting $x = 3$ we obtain $y^2 = 36$, that is, $y = \pm 6$. The endpoints of the chord are therefore $(3, 6)$ and $(3, -6)$, and the chord has length $12 = 2p$.
