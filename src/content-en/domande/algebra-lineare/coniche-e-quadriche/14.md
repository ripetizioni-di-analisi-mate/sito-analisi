What is the matrix associated with the conic
$$
3x^2 + 4xy - y^2 - 6x + 2y + 5 = 0 ?
$$

- $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 4 & -6 \\ 4 & -1 & 2 \\ -6 & 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 4 & -3 \\ 4 & -1 & 1 \\ -3 & 1 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 & -3 \\ 2 & -1 & 1 \\ -3 & 1 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 & -6 \\ 2 & -1 & 2 \\ -6 & 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The associated matrix contains half the coefficients of $xy$, of $x$ and of $y$. The coefficient of $xy$ is $2a_{12} = 4$, so $a_{12} = 2$. The coefficients of $x$ and of $y$ are $2a_{13} = -6$ and $2a_{23} = 2$, so $a_{13} = -3$ and $a_{23} = 1$. The diagonal entries are the coefficients of $x^2$ and of $y^2$ and the constant term, that is, $3$, $-1$ and $5$.
