The ellipse in the figure has center $C = (1, 0)$, semi-major axis $2$ on the line through $C$ with direction $(1, 2)$ and semi-minor axis $1$. What is its equation?

::grafico{curve="1+(2*cos(t)-2*sin(t))/sqrt(5),(4*cos(t)+sin(t))/sqrt(5),0,6.29" segmenti="-0.5,-3,2.5,3" punti="1,0,pieno,C" xmin=-2 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=3 uguale=1 titolo="Ellipse with center $C = (1, 0)$ and major axis (dashed) in the direction $(1, 2)$"}

- $17x^2 - 12xy + 8y^2 + 34x - 12y - 3 = 0$
- $8x^2 + 12xy + 17y^2 - 16x - 12y - 12 = 0$
- $17x^2 - 12xy + 8y^2 - 34x + 12y - 3 = 0$
- $8x^2 - 12xy + 17y^2 - 16x + 12y - 12 = 0$

## Explanation

Take the reference frame with origin at $C$, $X$-axis in the direction $(1, 2)$ and $Y$-axis in the direction $(-2, 1)$. The coordinates of a point in this frame are its signed distances from the axes, that is,
$$
X = \frac{(x - 1) + 2y}{\sqrt{5}} ,
\qquad
Y = \frac{-2(x - 1) + y}{\sqrt{5}} ,
$$
and in it the ellipse has the canonical form $\dfrac{X^2}{4} + Y^2 = 1$. Substituting and multiplying by $20$ we obtain
$$
(x + 2y - 1)^2 + 4(-2x + y + 2)^2 = 20 .
$$
Expanding the squares gives $17x^2 - 12xy + 8y^2 - 34x + 12y + 17 = 20$, so the equation is $17x^2 - 12xy + 8y^2 - 34x + 12y - 3 = 0$. The result can be checked with the eigenvalues: the block $A_0$ has eigenvalues $5$, with eigenvector $(1, 2)$, and $20$, and the system for the center has solution $(1, 0)$.
