The conic
$$
x^2 - 4xy + 4y^2 + 2x - 4y - 3 = 0
$$
is degenerate. Which lines does it consist of?

- $x - 2y + 3 = 0$ and $x - 2y - 1 = 0$
- $x - 2y - 3 = 0$ and $x - 2y + 1 = 0$
- $x + 2y + 3 = 0$ and $x + 2y - 1 = 0$
- The line $x - 2y + 1 = 0$ counted twice

## Explanation

The left-hand side depends on $x$ and $y$ only through $x - 2y$, since
$$
x^2 - 4xy + 4y^2 + 2x - 4y - 3 = (x - 2y)^2 + 2(x - 2y) - 3 .
$$
Setting $t = x - 2y$, the equation $t^2 + 2t - 3 = 0$ has the roots $t = 1$ and $t = -3$. Hence the conic is the union of the **parallel lines** $x - 2y - 1 = 0$ and $x - 2y + 3 = 0$, distinct because the two roots are different.
