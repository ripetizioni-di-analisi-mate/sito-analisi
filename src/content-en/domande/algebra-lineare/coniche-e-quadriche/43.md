What is the set of values of $c$ for which the plane $z = c$ has no points in common with the hyperboloid
$$
x^2 + y^2 - \frac{z^2}{4} = -1 ?
$$

- $|c| < 4$
- $|c| > 2$
- $|c| < 2$
- $|c| > 4$

## Explanation

Substituting $z = c$ we obtain
$$
x^2 + y^2 = \frac{c^2}{4} - 1 .
$$
The left-hand side is greater than or equal to $0$, so the equation has no solutions if and only if $\dfrac{c^2}{4} - 1 < 0$, that is, $c^2 < 4$. Therefore the plane does not intersect the quadric for $|c| < 2$; for $|c| \geq 2$ the section is a circle or a point. For this reason the **hyperboloid of two sheets** consists of two separate parts.
