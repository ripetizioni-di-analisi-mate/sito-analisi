On which line does the major axis of the ellipse
$$
3x^2 + 2xy + 3y^2 - 8 = 0
$$
lie?

- $y = x$
- $y = 0$
- $x = 0$
- $y = -x$

## Explanation

There are no first-degree terms, so the center is the origin. The block $A_0 = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ has eigenvalues $2$ and $4$, with eigenvectors $(1, -1)$ and $(1, 1)$. Since $I_3 = -64$ and $I_2 = 8$, the canonical form is $2X^2 + 4Y^2 = 8$, that is, $\dfrac{X^2}{4} + \dfrac{Y^2}{2} = 1$. The major semi-axis, equal to $2$, therefore lies on the axis associated with the eigenvalue $2$, which has the smaller absolute value. Hence the major axis is the line through the origin with direction $(1, -1)$, that is, $y = -x$.
