Which of the following pairs of values of $I_1 = \operatorname{tr} A_0$ and $I_2 = \det A_0$ is not possible for any conic?

- $I_1 = 0$ and $I_2 = -3$
- $I_1 = 2$ and $I_2 = 0$
- $I_1 = -2$ and $I_2 = 1$
- $I_1 = 0$ and $I_2 = 3$

## Explanation

The matrix $A_0$ is symmetric, so it has two real eigenvalues $\lambda_1$, $\lambda_2$, with $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 = I_1$ and $\lambda_1 \lambda_2 = I_2$. If $I_1 = 0$, then $\lambda_2 = -\lambda_1$ and $I_2 = -\lambda_1^2 \leq 0$. Therefore the pair $I_1 = 0$, $I_2 = 3$ is impossible. The other pairs correspond to the eigenvalues $\pm\sqrt{3}$, or $2$ and $0$, or $-1$ and $-1$, and are obtained for instance from $\sqrt{3}\, x^2 - \sqrt{3}\, y^2 - 1 = 0$, $2x^2 - y = 0$ and $-x^2 - y^2 + 1 = 0$.
