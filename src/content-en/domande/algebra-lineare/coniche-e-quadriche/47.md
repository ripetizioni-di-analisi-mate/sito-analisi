The point $P = \left( 3, \dfrac{16}{5} \right)$ lies on the ellipse
$$
\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{16} = 1 .
$$
What is the sum of the distances from $P$ to the two foci?

- $8$
- $6$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

For the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ with $a \geq b$, the sum of the distances from each of its points to the foci equals $2a$. Here $a^2 = 25$, so $a = 5$ and the sum equals $10$. A direct computation confirms this. Since $c^2 = 25 - 16 = 9$, the foci are $(\pm 3, 0)$, and the distances from $P$ to them are $\dfrac{16}{5}$ and $\sqrt{36 + \dfrac{256}{25}} = \dfrac{34}{5}$, with sum $\dfrac{50}{5} = 10$.
