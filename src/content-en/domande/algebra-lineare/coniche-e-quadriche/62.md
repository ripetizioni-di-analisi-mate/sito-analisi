The following four conics are hyperbolas. Which of them has both asymptotes parallel to the coordinate axes?

- $x^2 - y^2 + 2x - 3 = 0$
- $2xy - 4x + y + 1 = 0$
- $3xy + y^2 - 1 = 0$
- $4x^2 - y^2 - 2y - 5 = 0$

## Explanation

The asymptotes of a hyperbola have the directions $(l, m)$ satisfying $a_{11}l^2 + 2a_{12}lm + a_{22}m^2 = 0$. The directions $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$ satisfy this equation if and only if $a_{11} = 0$ and $a_{22} = 0$, that is, if the terms in $x^2$ and $y^2$ are missing. Among the four hyperbolas only $2xy - 4x + y + 1 = 0$ has this property. Its center solves $y - 2 = 0$ and $x + \dfrac{1}{2} = 0$, so the asymptotes are the lines $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $y = 2$. For $3xy + y^2 - 1 = 0$ the equation of the directions is $m(3l + m) = 0$, therefore only one asymptote is parallel to the $x$-axis and the other has direction $(1, -3)$.
