What is the vertex of the parabola
$$
4x^2 - 4xy + y^2 - 14x - 18y + 31 = 0 ?
$$

- $(2, 3)$
- $(1, 1)$
- $(3, 0)$
- $(0, -1)$

## Explanation

The second-degree part is $(2x - y)^2$, so the equation can be written as $(2x - y + t)^2 = (14 + 4t)x + (18 - 2t)y + t^2 - 31$. The lines $2x - y + t = 0$ and $(14 + 4t)x + (18 - 2t)y + t^2 - 31 = 0$ are perpendicular if $2(14 + 4t) - (18 - 2t) = 0$, that is, for $t = -1$. With this value the equation becomes
$$
(2x - y - 1)^2 = 10(x + 2y - 3) ,
$$
therefore the axis is $2x - y - 1 = 0$ and the tangent at the vertex is $x + 2y - 3 = 0$. The vertex is the intersection of the two lines, that is, the point $(1, 1)$.
