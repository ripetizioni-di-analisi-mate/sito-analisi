What is the equation of the parabola with focus $F = (1, 1)$ and directrix the line $x + y = 0$?

- $x^2 - 2xy + y^2 - 4x - 4y + 4 = 0$
- $xy + x + y - 1 = 0$
- $x^2 + 2xy + y^2 - 4x - 4y + 4 = 0$
- $x^2 - 2xy + y^2 + 4x + 4y + 4 = 0$

## Explanation

The parabola is the locus of the points $P = (x, y)$ equidistant from the focus and the directrix. The distance of $P$ from $F$ is $\sqrt{(x - 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2}$ and the distance of $P$ from the line $x + y = 0$ is $\dfrac{|x + y|}{\sqrt{2}}$. Equating the squares we obtain
$$
(x - 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = \frac{(x + y)^2}{2} .
$$
Multiplying by $2$ and expanding we get $2x^2 + 2y^2 - 4x - 4y + 4 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2$, so the equation is $x^2 - 2xy + y^2 - 4x - 4y + 4 = 0$. Forgetting the factor $\sqrt{2}$ in the distance from the line gives instead $xy + x + y - 1 = 0$, which is a hyperbola.
