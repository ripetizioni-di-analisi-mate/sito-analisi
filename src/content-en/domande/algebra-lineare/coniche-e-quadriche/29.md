What is the axis of the parabola
$$
x^2 - 2xy + y^2 - 2x - 6y + 5 = 0 ?
$$

- $x - y + 1 = 0$
- $x - y = 0$
- $x + y - 1 = 0$
- $x - y - 1 = 0$

## Explanation

The quadratic part is $(x - y)^2$, so the equation can be written as $(x - y + t)^2 = (2 + 2t)x + (6 - 2t)y + t^2 - 5$. The lines $x - y + t = 0$ and $(2 + 2t)x + (6 - 2t)y + t^2 - 5 = 0$ are perpendicular if $(2 + 2t) - (6 - 2t) = 0$, that is, for $t = 1$. With $t = 1$ the equation becomes
$$
(x - y + 1)^2 = 4(x + y - 1) ,
$$
therefore the **axis** is the line $x - y + 1 = 0$ and the tangent at the vertex is $x + y - 1 = 0$.
