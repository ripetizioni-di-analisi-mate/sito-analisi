What is the canonical form of the quadric
$$
2xy + z^2 - 1 = 0 ?
$$

- $X^2 + Y^2 - Z^2 = 1$
- $X^2 + Y^2 - Z^2 = -1$
- $X^2 + Y^2 + Z^2 = 1$
- $2X^2 + Y^2 - 2Z^2 = 1$

## Explanation

The block $A_0 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ has characteristic polynomial $(1 - \lambda)(\lambda^2 - 1)$, so the eigenvalues are $1$, $1$, $-1$ and $\det A_0 = -1$. The determinant of the associated matrix is $\det A = 1$, therefore $\dfrac{\det A}{\det A_0} = -1$. By the theorem on the **canonical form of quadrics**, ordering the eigenvalues as $1$, $1$, $-1$, we obtain $X^2 + Y^2 - Z^2 - 1 = 0$. Hence the quadric is a **hyperboloid of one sheet**.
