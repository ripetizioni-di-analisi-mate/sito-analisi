What equation does the hyperbola $xy = 2$ take under the rotation
$$
x = \frac{X - Y}{\sqrt{2}} ,
\qquad
y = \frac{X + Y}{\sqrt{2}} ?
$$

- $X^2 - Y^2 = 2$
- $Y^2 - X^2 = 4$
- $X^2 - Y^2 = 1$
- $X^2 - Y^2 = 4$

## Explanation

Substituting we obtain
$$
xy = \frac{(X - Y)(X + Y)}{2} = \frac{X^2 - Y^2}{2} .
$$
So the equation becomes $\dfrac{X^2 - Y^2}{2} = 2$, that is, $X^2 - Y^2 = 4$. It is a rectangular hyperbola with semi-axes equal to $2$ and vertices on the $X$-axis, which in the original plane is the bisector $y = x$.
