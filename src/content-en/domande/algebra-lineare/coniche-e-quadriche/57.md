The conic with associated matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 2 & k & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & -3 \end{pmatrix}
$$
depends on the real parameter $k$. For which value of $k$ does the conic pass through the point $P = (1, -1)$?

- $5$
- $0$
- $8$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The equation of the conic is written $\begin{pmatrix} x & y & 1 \end{pmatrix} A \begin{pmatrix} x & y & 1 \end{pmatrix}^T = 0$, so $P$ belongs to the conic if and only if the product vanishes for $(x, y) = (1, -1)$. With $v = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}^T$ we have
$$
A v = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 2 - k \\ -4 \end{pmatrix} ,
\qquad
v^T A v = -2 - (2 - k) - 4 = k - 8 .
$$
Therefore the conic passes through $P$ if and only if $k = 8$. The same result follows from the equation $x^2 + 4xy + ky^2 - 2x - 3 = 0$, in which the cross and linear terms have coefficients equal to twice the off-diagonal entries of $A$.
