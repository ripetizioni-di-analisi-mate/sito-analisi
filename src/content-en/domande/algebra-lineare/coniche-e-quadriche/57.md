$y - \dfrac{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}{12}=0$. What is the y-coordinate of the focus of the parabola?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $12$
- $3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The form is $(x-a)^2=4py$, with the vertex at height zero. The parameter $p$ is the y-coordinate of the focus. Substituting $a=2$ gives $3$.
