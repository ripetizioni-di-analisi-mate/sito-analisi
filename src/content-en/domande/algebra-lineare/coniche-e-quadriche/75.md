$\left(x - 3\right)^{2} + \left(y + 2\right)^{2}=4$. What is the x-coordinate of the leftmost point of the circle?

- $1$
- $5$
- $-1$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The center has x-coordinate $a$ and the radius is two. The leftmost point lies two units to the left of the center. Substituting $a=3$ gives $1$.
