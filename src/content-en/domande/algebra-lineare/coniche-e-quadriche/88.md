What is the radius of the sphere
$$
x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 2x + 4z - 4 = 0 ?
$$

- $1$
- $3$
- $\sqrt{13}$
- $\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

We complete the squares: $x^2 - 2x = (x - 1)^2 - 1$ and $z^2 + 4z = (z + 2)^2 - 4$. Substituting, the equation becomes
$$
(x - 1)^2 + y^2 + (z + 2)^2 = 4 + 1 + 4 = 9 .
$$
The left-hand side is the square of the distance from the point $(1, 0, -2)$, so the quadric is the sphere with center $(1, 0, -2)$ and radius $3$.
