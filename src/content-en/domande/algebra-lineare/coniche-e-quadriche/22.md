What kind of conic is
$$
x^2 + xy + y^2 + 2x + 2y + 3 = 0 ?
$$

- A real ellipse
- A hyperbola
- A parabola
- An imaginary ellipse

## Explanation

The associated matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & \frac{1}{2} & 1 \\ \frac{1}{2} & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
with $I_3 = \dfrac{5}{4} \neq 0$, so the conic is non-degenerate. We have $I_2 = 1 - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{3}{4} > 0$, therefore the conic is an ellipse. Since $I_1 = 2$, the product $I_1 I_3 = \dfrac{5}{2}$ is positive, so the ellipse is **imaginary** and contains no real points.
