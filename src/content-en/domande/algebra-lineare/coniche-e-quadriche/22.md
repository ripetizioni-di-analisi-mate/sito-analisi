In the family $x^2+xy+y^2+2x+2y+c=0$ the quadratic part is positive definite. For which $c$ does the real ellipse reduce to a single point?

- $c=\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $c=-\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $c=3$
- $c=\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The gradient vanishes at the centre $x=y=-\dfrac{2}{3}$. There the polynomial attains its minimum $c-\dfrac{4}{3}$. The zero level consists only of the centre when this minimum is zero, giving $c=\dfrac{4}{3}$.
