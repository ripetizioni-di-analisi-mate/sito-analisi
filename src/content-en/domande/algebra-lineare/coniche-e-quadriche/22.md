Exactly one of the following conics is an ellipse that contains real points. Which one?

- $x^2 + xy + y^2 + 2x + 2y + 3 = 0$
- $x^2 + 3xy + y^2 + 2x - 1 = 0$
- $x^2 + 2xy + y^2 + 2x - 1 = 0$
- $x^2 + xy + y^2 - 2x - 2y = 0$

## Explanation

For the second conic $I_2 = 1 - \dfrac{9}{4} < 0$ and for the third $I_2 = 1 - 1 = 0$, so they are not ellipses. The first and the fourth have the same quadratic block, with $I_1 = 2$ and $I_2 = 1 - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{3}{4} > 0$. For the first conic we find $I_3 = \dfrac{5}{4}$, therefore $I_1 I_3 > 0$ and the ellipse is imaginary. For the fourth we find $I_3 = -1$, therefore $I_1 I_3 = -2 < 0$ and the ellipse is **real**. Consistently with this, the fourth conic passes through the origin, because its equation has no constant term.
