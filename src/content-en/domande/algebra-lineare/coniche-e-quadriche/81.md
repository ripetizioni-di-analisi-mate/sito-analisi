The quadric
$$
3x^2 + 3y^2 + 3z^2 + 2xy + 2yz + 2xz - 1 = 0
$$
is an ellipsoid of revolution. What is its axis of revolution?

- The $z$-axis
- The line $x = y$, $z = 0$
- The line $x = -y = z$
- The line $x = y = z$

## Explanation

There are no linear terms, so the center is the origin. The block $A_0$ has all diagonal entries equal to $3$ and all off-diagonal entries equal to $1$, therefore $A_0 \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = 5 \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $5$ is an eigenvalue. Since $\operatorname{tr} A_0 = 9$ and $\det A_0 = 20$, the other two eigenvalues have sum $4$ and product $4$, so both equal $2$. The canonical form is $5X^2 + 2Y^2 + 2Z^2 = 1$, with two equal semi-axes in the plane orthogonal to the eigenvector $(1, 1, 1)$. Therefore the axis of revolution is the line through the origin with direction $(1, 1, 1)$, that is, $x = y = z$.
