What is the minimum distance from the $z$-axis of the points of the hyperboloid of one sheet
$$
x^2 + y^2 - \frac{z^2}{4} = 4 ?
$$

- $4$
- $1$
- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The distance of a point $(x, y, z)$ from the $z$-axis equals $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$. From the equation we get $x^2 + y^2 = 4 + \dfrac{z^2}{4}$, which is greater than or equal to $4$ and equals $4$ only for $z = 0$. So the minimum distance equals $\sqrt{4} = 2$, and it is attained on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ of the plane $z = 0$. The sections with the planes $z = c$ are circles of radius $\sqrt{4 + \dfrac{c^2}{4}}$, which increases with $|c|$.
