$\left(x - 4\right)^{2} + \left(y - 5\right)^{2}=4$. What is the x-coordinate of the leftmost point of the circle?

- $0$
- $2$
- $-6$
- $6$

## Explanation

The center has x-coordinate $a$ and the radius is two. The leftmost point lies two units to the left of the center. Substituting $a=4$ gives $2$.
