In the conic
$$
x^2 + 3xy - y^2 + 4x - 2y + 1 = 0
$$
the origin is translated to the point $(2, -1)$ by setting $x = X + 2$ and $y = Y - 1$. What is the constant term of the equation in the coordinates $X$, $Y$?

- $1$
- $8$
- $-12$
- $20$

## Explanation

In the new equation the constant term is obtained by setting $X = 0$ and $Y = 0$, that is, $x = 2$ and $y = -1$. So the constant term is the value of the left-hand side $f(x, y)$ at the new origin, as the formula for the matrix after an orientation-preserving rigid motion shows. We have
$$
f(2, -1) = 4 - 6 - 1 + 8 + 2 + 1 = 8 .
$$
Therefore the new constant term equals $8$.
