What is the canonical form of the parabola
$$
x^2 + 2xy + y^2 - 12x + 4y + 20 = 0 ?
$$

- $Y^2 = 4\sqrt{2}\, X$
- $Y^2 = 8X$
- $Y^2 = 2\sqrt{2}\, X$
- $Y^2 = 8\sqrt{2}\, X$

## Explanation

The associated matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -6 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \\ -6 & 2 & 20 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
with $I_2 = 1 - 1 = 0$, $I_1 = 2$ and $I_3 = \det A = -64$. Since $I_2 = 0$ and $I_3 \neq 0$, the theorem on the **canonical form of the parabola** applies, with
$$
p = \sqrt{-\frac{I_3}{I_1^3}} = \sqrt{\frac{64}{8}} = 2\sqrt{2} .
$$
Hence the canonical form is $Y^2 = 2pX = 4\sqrt{2}\, X$.
