$y - \dfrac{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}{8}=0$. What is the y-coordinate of the focus of the parabola?

- $8$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The form is $(x-a)^2=4py$, with the vertex at height zero. The parameter $p$ is the y-coordinate of the focus. Substituting $a=3$ gives $2$.
