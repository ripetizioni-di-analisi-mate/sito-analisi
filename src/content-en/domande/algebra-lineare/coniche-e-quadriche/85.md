$\dfrac{y^{2}}{9} - \dfrac{z^{2}}{16} + \dfrac{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}{4}=0$. In $\mathbb R^3$, what surface does the equation describe?

- Elliptic cylinder
- Ellipsoid
- Elliptic cone
- Elliptic paraboloid

## Explanation

In centered coordinates the equation is homogeneous of degree two. The horizontal sections at nonzero height are ellipses and shrink to the vertex at height zero.
