Which of the following lines, written in parametric form with $t \in \mathbb{R}$, is contained in the hyperboloid
$$
x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 1 ?
$$

- $(x, y, z) = (0, t, t)$
- $(x, y, z) = (t, t, 1)$
- $(x, y, z) = (1, t, 2t)$
- $(x, y, z) = (1, t, t)$

## Explanation

A line is contained in the quadric if the coordinates of each of its points satisfy the equation for every $t$. For $(1, t, t)$ we have $1 + t^2 - t^2 = 1$ for every $t$, so the line is contained in the **hyperboloid of one sheet**. For $(0, t, t)$ we obtain $0 = 1$, which holds for no $t$. For $(t, t, 1)$ and $(1, t, 2t)$ we obtain $2t^2 - 1 = 1$ and $1 - 3t^2 = 1$. The first equation holds only for $t = \pm 1$ and the second only for $t = 0$, so the line $(t, t, 1)$ has only two points in common with the hyperboloid and the line $(1, t, 2t)$ only one point.
