The parabola in the figure has its vertex at the origin, its axis along the $x$-axis, and passes through the point $P = (2, 4)$. What is its equation?

::grafico{curve="t^2/8,t,-5,5" punti="2,4,pieno,P" xmin=-2 xmax=5 ymin=-5 ymax=5 uguale=1 titolo="Parabola with vertex at the origin and axis $x$, passing through $P = (2, 4)$"}

- $y^2 = 4x$
- $y^2 = 8x$
- $x^2 = y$
- $y^2 = 16x$

## Explanation

A parabola with vertex at the origin and axis along the $x$-axis has equation $y^2 = 2px$. Since $P = (2, 4)$ lies on the parabola, its coordinates satisfy the equation, so $16 = 2p \cdot 2$. It follows that $2p = 8$, therefore the parabola is $y^2 = 8x$. The parabola $x^2 = y$ also passes through $P$, but its axis is the $y$-axis.
