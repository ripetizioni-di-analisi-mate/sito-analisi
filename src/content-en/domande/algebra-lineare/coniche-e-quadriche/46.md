$x^{2} - 4 x + y^{2} - 6 y + 9=0$. What are the coordinates of the center of the circle?

- $\left(2,-3\right)$
- $\left(2,3\right)$
- $\left(-2,-3\right)$
- $\left(3,2\right)$

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $(x-a)^2$ and $(y-b)^2$. The coordinates of the center make both terms vanish. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$ gives $\left(2,3\right)$.
