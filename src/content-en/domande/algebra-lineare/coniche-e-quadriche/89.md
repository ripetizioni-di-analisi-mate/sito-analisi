The cone
$$
x^2 + y^2 = \frac{z^2}{3}
$$
has the $z$-axis as its axis. What angle do the lines of the cone through the vertex form with the axis?

- $60^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $\arctan 3$

## Explanation

The section with the plane $z = 1$ is the circle $x^2 + y^2 = \dfrac{1}{3}$, with center $(0, 0, 1)$ and radius $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$. Every line of the cone passes through the vertex $(0, 0, 0)$ and through a point of this circle. The angle $\alpha$ with the $z$-axis therefore satisfies $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$, so $\alpha = 30^\circ$.
