$\dfrac{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}{16} + \dfrac{\left(y - 3\right)^{2}}{4}=1$. What is the length of the major semi-axis of the ellipse?

- $16$
- $2$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

The denominators in canonical form are the squares of the semiaxis lengths. The larger one is sixteen. The result is $4$.
