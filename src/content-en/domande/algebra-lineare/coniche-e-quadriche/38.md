What kind of quadric is $xy - z = 0$?

- An elliptic paraboloid
- A hyperbolic cylinder
- A cone
- A hyperbolic paraboloid

## Explanation

The associated matrix has $a_{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $a_{34} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, and all other entries equal to zero. The third row of $A_0$ is zero, so $\det A_0 = 0$; the determinant of $A$, instead, equals $\dfrac{1}{16} > 0$, therefore the quadric is non-degenerate. By the theorem on the **recognition of quadrics**, with $\det A_0 = 0$ and $\det A > 0$ the quadric is a **hyperbolic paraboloid**. Indeed, the nonzero eigenvalues of $A_0$ are $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$, which have opposite signs.
