What kind of quadric is
$$
x^2 - y^2 - z^2 + 2y = 0 ?
$$

- A hyperboloid of two sheets
- A cone
- A hyperboloid of one sheet
- A hyperbolic paraboloid

## Explanation

The block $A_0$ is diagonal with eigenvalues $1$, $-1$, $-1$, so $\det A_0 = 1 \neq 0$ and the eigenvalues do not all have the same sign. The determinant of the associated matrix is $\det A = 1 > 0$, therefore by the theorem on the **recognition of quadrics** the quadric is a **hyperboloid of one sheet**. Completing the square in $y$ we obtain $x^2 - (y - 1)^2 - z^2 = -1$, that is, $(y - 1)^2 + z^2 - x^2 = 1$, a hyperboloid of one sheet with axis parallel to the $x$-axis.
