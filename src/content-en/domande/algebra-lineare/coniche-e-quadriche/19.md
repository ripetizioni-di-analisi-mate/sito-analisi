An orientation-preserving rigid motion transforms the left-hand side of the equation
$$
x^2 + 6xy + y^2 + 4x - 1 = 0
$$
into the polynomial $aX^2 + 2bXY + cY^2 + 2dX + 2eY + f$. Which equality holds for every orientation-preserving rigid motion?

- $a = 1$
- $a + c = 2$
- $f = -1$
- $ac = 1$

## Explanation

The trace of the block of the quadratic terms is an **orthogonal invariant**, so $a + c = I_1 = 1 + 1 = 2$ for every orientation-preserving rigid motion. The coefficient $a$ alone changes under rotation, and indeed the rotation that diagonalizes $A_0$ takes $a$ and $c$ to the eigenvalues $4$ and $-2$. For the same reason the product $ac$ is not invariant, whereas $ac - b^2 = I_2 = -8$ is. The constant term $f$ is the value of the left-hand side at the new origin, therefore it changes under translation; for example, with the new origin at $(1, 0)$ we obtain $f = 4$.
