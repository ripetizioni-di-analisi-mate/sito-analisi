For which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the conic
$$
x^2 + 4xy + ky^2 + 2x = 0
$$
a real ellipse?

- $k < 4$
- $k > 0$
- $k > 4$
- $0 < k < 4$

## Explanation

The associated matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & k & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
so $I_3 = -k$, $I_2 = k - 4$ and $I_1 = k + 1$. The conic is an ellipse when $I_2 > 0$, that is, for $k > 4$, and for these values $I_3 = -k \neq 0$, therefore the conic is non-degenerate. Moreover, for $k > 4$ we have $I_1 I_3 = -k(k + 1) < 0$, so the ellipse is **real** for every $k > 4$.
