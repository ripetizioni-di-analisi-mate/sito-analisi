What is the center of the conic
$$
x^2 + 4xy + 5y^2 - 2x - 6y + 1 = 0 ?
$$

- $(1, -1)$
- $(-2, 2)$
- $(2, -2)$
- $(-1, 1)$

## Explanation

We have $I_2 = 5 - 4 = 1 \neq 0$, so the conic has a unique center. With $a_{12} = 2$, $a_{13} = -1$ and $a_{23} = -3$, the center solves the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y - 1 = 0 \\[4pt]
2x + 5y - 3 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
From the first equation we obtain $x = 1 - 2y$; substituting into the second we obtain $y - 1 = 0$, so $y = 1$ and $x = -1$. Therefore the center is the point $(-1, 1)$.
