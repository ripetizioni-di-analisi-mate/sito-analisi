For the surface with equation
$$
xy + yz + xz = 1
$$
which statement is true?

- It is a hyperboloid of two sheets
- It is a hyperboloid of one sheet
- It is a real ellipsoid
- It is a hyperbolic paraboloid

## Explanation

The block $A_0$ has zero diagonal entries and all other entries equal to $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The vector $(1, 1, 1)$ is an eigenvector with eigenvalue $1$, and since $\operatorname{tr} A_0 = 0$ and $\det A_0 = \dfrac{1}{4}$, the other two eigenvalues have sum $-1$ and product $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so both equal $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The eigenvalues do not all have the same sign and $\det A_0 \neq 0$, therefore the quadric is a hyperboloid. There are no linear terms and $a_{44} = -1$, so $\det A = -\det A_0 = -\dfrac{1}{4} < 0$, and by the theorem on the **recognition of quadrics** the hyperboloid is **of two sheets**. Indeed, the canonical form is $X^2 - \dfrac{Y^2}{2} - \dfrac{Z^2}{2} = 1$.
