$y - \dfrac{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}{20}=0$. What is the y-coordinate of the focus of the parabola?

- $5$
- $20$
- $-5$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

The form is $(x-a)^2=4py$, with the vertex at height zero. The parameter $p$ is the y-coordinate of the focus. Substituting $a=4$ gives $5$.
