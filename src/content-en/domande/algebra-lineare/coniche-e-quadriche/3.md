What is the canonical form of the conic
$$
7x^2 + 6xy - y^2 - 14x - 6y - 1 = 0 ?
$$

- $\dfrac{X^2}{4} - Y^2 = 1$
- $X^2 - 4Y^2 = 1$
- $X^2 + \dfrac{Y^2}{4} = 1$
- $X^2 - \dfrac{Y^2}{4} = 1$

## Explanation

The block $A_0 = \begin{pmatrix} 7 & 3 \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ has $I_2 = -7 - 9 = -16 \neq 0$, so the theorem on the **canonical form of central conics** applies. The characteristic polynomial of $A_0$ is $\lambda^2 - 6\lambda - 16 = (\lambda - 8)(\lambda + 2)$, therefore the eigenvalues are $8$ and $-2$. The determinant of the associated matrix is $I_3 = 128$, so $\dfrac{I_3}{I_2} = -8$ and the canonical form is $8X^2 - 2Y^2 - 8 = 0$. Dividing by $8$ we obtain the hyperbola $X^2 - \dfrac{Y^2}{4} = 1$.
