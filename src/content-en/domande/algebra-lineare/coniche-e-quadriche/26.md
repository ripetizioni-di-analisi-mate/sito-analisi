The point $P$ of the hyperbola
$$
\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{16} = 1
$$
has negative abscissa and is at distance $13$ from the focus $F_1 = (5, 0)$. What is the distance of $P$ from the other focus $F_2 = (-5, 0)$?

- $19$
- $3$
- $7$
- $10$

## Explanation

We have $a = 3$ and $c^2 = a^2 + b^2 = 9 + 16 = 25$, so the foci are $(\pm 5, 0)$. The hyperbola is the locus of the points for which the difference of the distances from the foci equals $2a = 6$ in absolute value, therefore $|\overline{PF_1} - \overline{PF_2}| = 6$. For a point $(x, y)$ we have $\overline{PF_1}^2 - \overline{PF_2}^2 = (x - 5)^2 - (x + 5)^2 = -20x$, and since $x < 0$ this quantity is positive. Hence $P$ is closer to $F_2$ than to $F_1$, and $\overline{PF_1} - \overline{PF_2} = 6$. It follows that $\overline{PF_2} = 13 - 6 = 7$. Such a point exists: for example $P = (-6, 4\sqrt{3})$ lies on the hyperbola and has these two distances.
