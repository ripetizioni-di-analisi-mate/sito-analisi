$\dfrac{z^{2}}{16} + \dfrac{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}{4} + \dfrac{\left(y + 2\right)^{2}}{9}=1$. In the section $z=0$, what is the maximum value of $x$?

- $1$
- $7$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The section is an ellipse whose center has x-coordinate $a$. The rightmost point is obtained by setting $y=b$, at a distance of two from the center. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$ gives $5$.
