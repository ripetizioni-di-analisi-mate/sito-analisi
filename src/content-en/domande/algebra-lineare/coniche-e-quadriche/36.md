What kind of quadric is
$$
x^2 + y^2 + 2z^2 + 2x + 4 = 0 ?
$$

- A real ellipsoid
- A hyperboloid of two sheets
- A cone
- An imaginary ellipsoid

## Explanation

The block $A_0$ is diagonal with entries $1$, $1$, $2$, so the eigenvalues all have the same sign and $\det A_0 = 2 \neq 0$. The determinant of the associated matrix is $\det A = 6 > 0$, therefore by the theorem on the **recognition of quadrics** the quadric is an **imaginary ellipsoid**. Indeed, completing the square we obtain $(x + 1)^2 + y^2 + 2z^2 = -3$, an equation with no real solutions.
