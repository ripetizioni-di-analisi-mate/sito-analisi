What is the canonical form of the quadric
$$
x^2 + y^2 - 2z^2 - 2x + 4y + 8z - 5 = 0 ?
$$

- $\dfrac{X^2}{2} + \dfrac{Y^2}{2} - Z^2 = -1$
- $X^2 + Y^2 - \dfrac{Z^2}{2} = 1$
- $\dfrac{X^2}{2} + \dfrac{Y^2}{2} + Z^2 = 1$
- $\dfrac{X^2}{2} + \dfrac{Y^2}{2} - Z^2 = 1$

## Explanation

We complete the squares: $x^2 - 2x = (x - 1)^2 - 1$, $y^2 + 4y = (y + 2)^2 - 4$ and $-2z^2 + 8z = -2(z - 2)^2 + 8$. Substituting, the equation becomes
$$
(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 - 2(z - 2)^2 = 2 .
$$
The translation to the center $(1, -2, 2)$ and division by $2$ give $\dfrac{X^2}{2} + \dfrac{Y^2}{2} - Z^2 = 1$, so the quadric is a **hyperboloid of one sheet**, a surface of revolution because the first two semi-axes are equal.
