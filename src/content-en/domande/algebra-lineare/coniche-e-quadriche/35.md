What quadric is the locus of the points of space equidistant from the point $P = (0, 0, 1)$ and from the plane $z = 0$?

- A cone
- A sphere
- An elliptic paraboloid
- A hyperboloid of two sheets

## Explanation

The distance of the point $(x, y, z)$ from $P$ is $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + (z - 1)^2}$, and its distance from the plane $z = 0$ is $|z|$. Equating the squares we obtain $x^2 + y^2 + (z - 1)^2 = z^2$, that is,
$$
x^2 + y^2 - 2z + 1 = 0 .
$$
The terms in $z^2$ cancel, so the third row of $A_0$ is zero and $\det A_0 = 0$. The associated matrix has $\det A = -1 < 0$, therefore by the theorem on the **recognition of quadrics** the locus is an **elliptic paraboloid**, of revolution about the $z$-axis, with equation $z = \dfrac{x^2 + y^2 + 1}{2}$.
