What is the block $A_0$ of the quadratic terms of the matrix associated with the quadric
$$
x^2 + 2y^2 - z^2 + 4xy - 2yz + 6x - 1 = 0 ?
$$

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 & 0 \\ 4 & 2 & -2 \\ 0 & -2 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 2 & 2 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 2 & -1 \\ 3 & -1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The diagonal of $A_0$ contains the coefficients of $x^2$, $y^2$ and $z^2$, that is, $1$, $2$ and $-1$. The off-diagonal entries are half the coefficients of the cross terms, so $a_{12} = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2$, $a_{23} = -\dfrac{2}{2} = -1$ and $a_{13} = 0$, because there is no $xz$ term. The term $6x$ is linear, therefore it enters the fourth column of the matrix $A$ as $a_{14} = 3$ and does not appear in $A_0$.
