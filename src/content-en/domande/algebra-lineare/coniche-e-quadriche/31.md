The conic
$$
x^2 + 2y^2 - 2x + 4y + 3 = 0
$$
has $I_3 = 0$. Which real points does it contain?

- No points
- Only the point $(1, -1)$
- The points of two lines intersecting at $(1, -1)$
- The points of an ellipse with center $(1, -1)$

## Explanation

The block of the second-degree terms has $I_2 = 2 > 0$. By the **classification of degenerate conics**, with $I_3 = 0$ and $I_2 > 0$ the conic is the union of two complex conjugate lines intersecting at the center, which is its only real point. Completing the squares, the equation becomes $(x - 1)^2 + 2(y + 1)^2 = 0$. A sum of squares with positive coefficients is zero only if each term is zero, so the only real point is $(1, -1)$.
