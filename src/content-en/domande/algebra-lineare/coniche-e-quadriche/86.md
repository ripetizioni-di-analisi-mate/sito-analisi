Along which set of points does the plane $z = 0$ cut the hyperbolic paraboloid $z = x^2 - y^2$?

- A hyperbola
- A parabola
- Two lines intersecting at the origin
- Only the point $(0, 0, 0)$

## Explanation

Substituting $z = 0$ we obtain $x^2 - y^2 = 0$, that is, $(x - y)(x + y) = 0$. So the intersection consists of the lines $y = x$ and $y = -x$ of the plane $z = 0$, which meet at the origin. With a plane $z = c$ with $c \neq 0$ we obtain instead the hyperbola $x^2 - y^2 = c$.
