$\dfrac{y^{2}}{9} + \dfrac{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}{4}=1$. In $\mathbb R^3$, what surface does the equation describe?

- Ellipsoid
- Elliptic cone
- Elliptic paraboloid
- Elliptic cylinder

## Explanation

The equation describes an ellipse in the $xy$ plane, and $z$ is free. Each point of the ellipse generates a line parallel to the $z$ axis.
