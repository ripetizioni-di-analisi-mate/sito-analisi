Which of the following conics is a rectangular hyperbola?

- $x^2 - y^2 + 2x + 2y = 0$
- $2x^2 + 3xy - y^2 - 1 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 - 4x = 0$
- $2x^2 + 3xy - 2y^2 + x - 1 = 0$

## Explanation

A conic is a rectangular hyperbola if it is non-degenerate, with $I_2 < 0$ and $I_1 = 0$. For $2x^2 + 3xy - 2y^2 + x - 1 = 0$ we have $I_1 = 2 - 2 = 0$, $I_2 = -4 - \dfrac{9}{4} = -\dfrac{25}{4} < 0$ and $I_3 = \dfrac{27}{4} \neq 0$, so it is a **rectangular hyperbola**. The conic $x^2 - y^2 + 2x + 2y = 0$ also has $I_1 = 0$, but it can be written as $(x + 1)^2 - (y - 1)^2 = 0$, therefore it is degenerate and consists of two perpendicular lines. The conic $2x^2 + 3xy - y^2 - 1 = 0$ is a hyperbola with $I_1 = 1 \neq 0$, and $x^2 + y^2 - 4x = 0$ is a circle.
