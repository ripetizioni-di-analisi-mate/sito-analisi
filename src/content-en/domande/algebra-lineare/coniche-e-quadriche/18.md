With a suitable orientation-preserving rigid motion the left-hand side of the equation
$$
x^2 + 4xy + y^2 - 2x + 3 = 0
$$
becomes $aX^2 + bY^2 + c$. What are $a$ and $b$, up to order?

- $3$ and $-1$
- $1$ and $1$
- $5$ and $-3$
- $3$ and $1$

## Explanation

The block of the quadratic terms is $A_0 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, with $I_2 = 1 - 4 = -3 \neq 0$. Hence the theorem on the **canonical form of central conics** applies, and $a$ and $b$ are the eigenvalues of $A_0$. The characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2 - 2\lambda - 3 = (\lambda - 3)(\lambda + 1)$, therefore $a$ and $b$ are $3$ and $-1$. The result is consistent with the **orthogonal invariants**, since $a + b = 2 = I_1$ and $ab = -3 = I_2$.
