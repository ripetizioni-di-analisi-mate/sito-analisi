All that is known about a conic is that $I_2 = \det A_0 < 0$. Which statement is certainly true?

- The conic is a hyperbola
- The conic is non-degenerate
- $I_1 = 0$
- The conic contains infinitely many real points

## Explanation

If $I_3 \neq 0$, the conic with $I_2 < 0$ is a hyperbola, by the **classification of non-degenerate conics**. If $I_3 = 0$, by the **classification of degenerate conics** it is the union of two real intersecting lines. In both cases the conic contains infinitely many real points. The conic $x^2 - y^2 = 0$ has $I_2 = -1$ and consists of the lines $y = \pm x$, so it is degenerate and is not a hyperbola; the conic $x^2 - 2y^2 - 1 = 0$ has $I_2 < 0$ and $I_1 = -1$.
