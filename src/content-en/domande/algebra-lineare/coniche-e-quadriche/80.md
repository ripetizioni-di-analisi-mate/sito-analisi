$4 x^{2} - 40 x + 97=y$. What are the coordinates of the parabola vertex?

- $\left(-3,5\right)$
- $\left(-5,-3\right)$
- $\left(5,3\right)$
- $\left(5,-3\right)$

## Explanation

Complete the square to obtain vertex form. The vertex’s first coordinate cancels the square and its second coordinate is the remaining constant. The result is $\left(5,-3\right)$.
