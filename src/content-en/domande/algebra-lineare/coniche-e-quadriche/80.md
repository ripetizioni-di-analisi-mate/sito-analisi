$4 x^{2} - 40 x + 97=y$. What are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola?

- $\left(-3,5\right)$
- $\left(-5,-3\right)$
- $\left(5,3\right)$
- $\left(5,-3\right)$

## Explanation

Complete the square to obtain vertex form. The x-coordinate of the vertex makes the square vanish and its y-coordinate is the remaining term. The result is $\left(5,-3\right)$.
