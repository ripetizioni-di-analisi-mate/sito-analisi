Which hyperbola in canonical form has its vertices at the points $(\pm 3, 0)$ and its foci at the points $(\pm 5, 0)$?

- $\dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{16} = 1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{25} = 1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{25} - \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{34} = 1$

## Explanation

The vertices of the hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ are $(\pm a, 0)$, so $a = 3$. The foci are $(\pm c, 0)$ with $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$, therefore $c = 5$ and $b^2 = 25 - 9 = 16$. Hence the hyperbola is $\dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{16} = 1$.
