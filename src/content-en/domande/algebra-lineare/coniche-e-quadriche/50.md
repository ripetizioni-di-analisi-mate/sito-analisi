$2 x^{2} - 12 x + 19=y$. What are the coordinates of the parabola vertex?

- $\left(-3,1\right)$
- $\left(3,1\right)$
- $\left(3,-1\right)$
- $\left(1,3\right)$

## Explanation

Complete the square to obtain vertex form. The vertex’s first coordinate cancels the square and its second coordinate is the remaining constant. The result is $\left(3,1\right)$.
