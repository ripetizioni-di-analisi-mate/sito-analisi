$2 x^{2} - 12 x + 19=y$. What are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola?

- $\left(-3,1\right)$
- $\left(3,1\right)$
- $\left(3,-1\right)$
- $\left(1,3\right)$

## Explanation

Complete the square to obtain vertex form. The x-coordinate of the vertex makes the square vanish and its y-coordinate is the remaining term. The result is $\left(3,1\right)$.
