What is the transverse axis, that is, the axis that contains the vertices, of the hyperbola
$$
3x^2 + 8xy - 3y^2 - 14x - 2y + 13 = 0 ?
$$

- $x - 2y + 1 = 0$
- $x + 2y - 3 = 0$
- $2x + y - 3 = 0$
- $2x - y - 1 = 0$

## Explanation

The system for the center is $3x + 4y - 7 = 0$, $4x - 3y - 1 = 0$, with solution $(1, 1)$, and the left-hand side equals $5$ at the center, so $\dfrac{I_3}{I_2} = 5$. The block $A_0 = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 4 \\ 4 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$ has characteristic polynomial $\lambda^2 - 25$, with eigenvectors $(2, 1)$ for $\lambda = 5$ and $(1, -2)$ for $\lambda = -5$. The canonical form is $5X^2 - 5Y^2 + 5 = 0$, that is, $Y^2 - X^2 = 1$, therefore the vertices lie on the $Y$-axis, which corresponds to the eigenvalue $-5$. So the transverse axis is the line through $(1, 1)$ with direction $(1, -2)$, that is, $2x + y - 3 = 0$.
