The conic
$$
x^2 - 4xy + 4y^2 - 9 = 0
$$
consists of two parallel lines. What is the distance between them?

- $\dfrac{6\sqrt{5}}{5}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{5}}{5}$
- $2\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The quadratic part is $(x - 2y)^2$, so the equation can be written as $(x - 2y)^2 = 9$. Therefore the conic is the union of the lines $x - 2y - 3 = 0$ and $x - 2y + 3 = 0$. The distance between two parallel lines $ax + by + c = 0$ and $ax + by + c' = 0$ equals $\dfrac{|c - c'|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$, so here it equals
$$
\frac{|-3 - 3|}{\sqrt{1 + 4}} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{6\sqrt{5}}{5} .
$$
