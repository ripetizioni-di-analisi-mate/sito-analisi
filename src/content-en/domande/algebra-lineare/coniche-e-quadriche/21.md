For which value of $k$ is the conic
$$
kx^2 + (k^2 - 9)xy + (6 - k)y^2 - 6x + 1 = 0
$$
a circle?

- $k = 3$
- $k = -3$
- $k = \pm 3$
- $k = 0$

## Explanation

An ellipse is a circle if and only if $a_{12} = 0$ and $a_{11} = a_{22}$, and the two conditions must hold simultaneously:
$$
\begin{cases}
k^2 - 9 = 0 \\[4pt]
k = 6 - k
\end{cases}
$$
The first equation gives $k = \pm 3$ and the second gives $k = 3$, so the only possible value is $k = 3$. For $k = 3$ the equation is $3x^2 + 3y^2 - 6x + 1 = 0$, that is, $(x - 1)^2 + y^2 = \dfrac{2}{3}$, which is a real **circle**. For $k = -3$ we obtain $-3x^2 + 9y^2 - 6x + 1 = 0$, with $I_2 = -27 < 0$, therefore a hyperbola.
