The conic
$$
x^2 - xy - 2y^2 + x + 4y - 2 = 0
$$
consists of two intersecting lines. At which point do they meet?

- $(0, 1)$
- $(1, 0)$
- $(0, -1)$
- $(-2, 0)$

## Explanation

We have $I_2 = -2 - \dfrac{1}{4} = -\dfrac{9}{4} < 0$, so the two lines meet at the center of the conic. The system of the center, with the two equations multiplied by $2$ and by $-2$, is
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - y + 1 = 0 \\[4pt]
x + 4y - 4 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
From the first equation $y = 2x + 1$; substituting into the second we obtain $9x = 0$, so the point is $(0, 1)$. This is confirmed by the factorization $(x - 2y + 2)(x + y - 1) = 0$, since both lines pass through $(0, 1)$.
