The conic
$$
x^2 - 4xy + y^2 = 0
$$
consists of two lines through the origin. What acute angle do the two lines form?

- $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $75^\circ$

## Explanation

The equation has neither first-degree terms nor a constant term, so $I_3 = 0$, and $I_2 = 1 - 4 = -3 < 0$, therefore the conic consists of two real lines intersecting at the origin. The $y$-axis does not belong to the conic, so the lines can be written as $y = mx$, and substituting we obtain $m^2 - 4m + 1 = 0$. The roots are $m = 2 \pm \sqrt{3}$, that is, $m = \tan 75^\circ$ and $m = \tan 15^\circ$. Therefore the lines form angles of $75^\circ$ and $15^\circ$ with the $x$-axis, and the acute angle between them equals $60^\circ$.
