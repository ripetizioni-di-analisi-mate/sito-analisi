We look for the real constants $p$ and $q$ such that
$$
\frac{5x + 1}{(x - 1)(x + 2)} = \frac{p}{x - 1} + \frac{q}{x + 2}
$$
for every $x \neq 1, -2$. Equating the coefficients of the numerators gives a linear system in the unknowns $p$ and $q$. What are $p$ and $q$?

- $p = 2$, $q = 3$
- $p = 3$, $q = 2$
- $p = -4$, $q = 9$
- $p = 2$, $q = -3$

## Explanation

Bringing the right-hand side to a common denominator, the numerator becomes $p(x + 2) + q(x - 1) = (p + q)\,x + (2p - q)$. This polynomial equals $5x + 1$ if and only if the coefficients coincide, so $p$ and $q$ solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
p + q = 5 \\[4pt]
2p - q = 1
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations gives $3p = 6$, therefore $p = 2$, and from the first equation $q = 3$.
