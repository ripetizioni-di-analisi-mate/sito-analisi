For which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ can Cramer's rule be applied to the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
k x + y = 1 \\[4pt]
4x + k y = 2
\end{cases}
$$

- $k \neq 2$ and $k \neq -2$
- $k \neq 2$
- $k \neq 4$
- $k = 2$ or $k = -2$

## Explanation

The system is square, so Cramer's rule applies if and only if $\det A \neq 0$. One has
$$
\det A =
\begin{vmatrix}
k & 1 \\
4 & k
\end{vmatrix}
= k^2 - 4 = (k - 2)(k + 2) .
$$
The determinant vanishes for $k = 2$ and for $k = -2$, therefore the rule applies for $k \neq 2$ and $k \neq -2$.
