The homogeneous system $A x = 0$ in three unknowns has $\det A = 5$. To apply Cramer's rule one considers the matrix $A_2$, obtained from $A$ by replacing the second column with the column of constant terms. What is $\det A_2$?

- $5$
- $-5$
- It cannot be determined without knowing $A$
- $0$

## Explanation

In a homogeneous system all the constant terms are zero, so the second column of $A_2$ is zero. Expanding the determinant by Laplace's rule along that column, every term contains a zero factor, therefore $\det A_2 = 0$ for every matrix $A$. Since $\det A \neq 0$, **Cramer's rule** applies and gives $y = \dfrac{0}{5} = 0$; the same holds for $x$ and $z$. This shows again that a homogeneous system with $\det A \neq 0$ has only the trivial solution.
