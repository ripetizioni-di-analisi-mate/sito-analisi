Which vector spans the solution space of the following homogeneous system?
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 2y + z = 0 \\[4pt]
2x - 4y + 3z = 0
\end{cases}
$$

- $(1, 2, 0)$
- $(-2, 1, 0)$
- $(2, 1, 1)$
- $(2, 1, 0)$

## Explanation

With $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ the second equation becomes $z = 0$. The first then becomes $x - 2y = 0$, so $x = 2y$. Setting $y = t$, the solutions are $t\,(2, 1, 0)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$, therefore the solution space is $\langle (2, 1, 0) \rangle$. Its dimension is $3 - \operatorname{rk} A = 3 - 2 = 1$, in agreement with the theorem on nontrivial solutions.
