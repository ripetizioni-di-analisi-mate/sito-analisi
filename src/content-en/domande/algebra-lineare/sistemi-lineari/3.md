- $(-3 + s - t, \ s, \ 2 - t, \ t), \ s, t \in \mathbb{R}$
- $(-3 + s + t, \ s, \ 2 + t, \ t), \ s, t \in \mathbb{R}$
- $(-3 + s + t, \ s, \ 2 - t, \ t), \ s, t \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

With $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ the second row of the augmented matrix becomes $(0 \ \ 0 \ \ 1 \ \ 1 \mid 2)$. The pivots are in the columns of $x_1$ and $x_3$, so $x_2 = s$ and $x_4 = t$ are free and there are $\infty^{4 - 2} = \infty^2$ solutions. The second equation $x_3 + x_4 = 2$ gives $x_3 = 2 - t$. Substituting into the first gives
$$
x_1 = 1 + s - 2\,(2 - t) - t = -3 + s + t .
$$
The solutions are therefore $(-3 + s + t, \ s, \ 2 - t, \ t)$ with $s, t \in \mathbb{R}$.
