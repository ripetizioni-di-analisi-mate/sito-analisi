$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 4 & 2 \\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(3,6,1\right)$. How many solutions does $Az=B$ have?

- No solution
- Exactly one solution
- Infinitely many solutions with one parameter
- Infinitely many solutions with two parameters

## Explanation

The last row would require $0=c$ with a nonzero right-hand side. The system is inconsistent.
