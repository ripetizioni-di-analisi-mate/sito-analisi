Which subset of $\mathbb{R}^3$ do the solutions of the following homogeneous system form?
$$
\begin{cases}
x - y + 2z = 0 \\[4pt]
2x - 2y + 4z = 0 \\[4pt]
-x + y - 2z = 0
\end{cases}
$$

- A plane through the origin
- A line through the origin
- The single point $(0, 0, 0)$
- A plane not passing through the origin

## Explanation

The second equation is twice the first and the third is the opposite of the first, so all rows of $A$ are proportional to the row $(1, -1, 2)$ and $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$. By the theorem on **nontrivial solutions** there are $\infty^{3 - 1} = \infty^2$ solutions, forming a subspace of dimension $2$. Since the system is homogeneous, the subspace contains the origin. Therefore the solutions are the points of the **plane** $x - y + 2z = 0$, which passes through the origin.
