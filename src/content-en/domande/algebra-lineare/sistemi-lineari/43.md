For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 2 \\[4pt]
x + 2y + 3z = k \\[4pt]
x + 3y + 5z = 4
\end{cases}
$$
consistent?

- $k = 1$
- $k = -3$
- $k = 3$
- For every $k \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

The third row of $A$ is $2R_2 - R_1$, so $\det A = 0$, whereas the minor $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 1$ is nonzero; therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ for every $k$. The bordered minor of this minor with the third row and the column of constant terms equals
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & 2 \\
1 & 2 & k \\
1 & 3 & 4
\end{vmatrix}
= (8 - 3k) - (4 - k) + 2\,(3 - 2) = 6 - 2k .
$$
For $k \neq 3$ one therefore has $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3 > \operatorname{rk} A$, and the system is inconsistent. For $k = 3$ both ranks equal $2$, therefore by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the system is consistent, with $\infty^1$ solutions.
