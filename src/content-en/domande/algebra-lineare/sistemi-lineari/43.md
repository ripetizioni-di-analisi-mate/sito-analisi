The matrix $A$ has $2$ rows, $5$ columns and rank $2$. How many solutions does the homogeneous system $A^T y = 0$, of $5$ equations in the $2$ unknowns $y_1, y_2$, have?

- $\infty^3$ solutions
- $\infty^1$ solutions
- Only the trivial solution
- No solutions

## Explanation

The minors of $A^T$ are the minors of $A$, so $\operatorname{rk} A^T = \operatorname{rk} A = 2$. The system $A^T y = 0$ has $2$ unknowns, therefore the rank of its coefficient matrix equals the number of unknowns. By the theorem on nontrivial solutions of a homogeneous system, the system then has **only the trivial solution** $y = (0, 0)$. The number $\infty^{5 - 2} = \infty^3$ would be obtained by counting the equations instead of the unknowns.
