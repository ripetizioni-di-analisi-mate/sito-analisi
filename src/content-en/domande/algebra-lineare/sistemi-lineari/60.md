How many minors of order $2$ does a matrix with $3$ rows and $4$ columns have?

- $6$
- $12$
- $18$
- $24$

## Explanation

A minor of order $2$ is obtained by choosing $2$ rows and $2$ columns. The pairs of rows chosen among $3$ are $\dbinom{3}{2} = 3$ and the pairs of columns chosen among $4$ are $\dbinom{4}{2} = 6$. Each pair of rows combines with each pair of columns, so there are $3 \cdot 6 = 18$ minors of order $2$.
