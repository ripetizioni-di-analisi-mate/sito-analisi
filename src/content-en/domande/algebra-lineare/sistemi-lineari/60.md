$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 0 \\ 0 & 3\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(1,1\right)$. What is the ordered solution of the system $Az=B$?

- $\left(\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- $\left(2,3\right)$
- $\left(- \dfrac{1}{2},- \dfrac{1}{3}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{3}\right)$

## Explanation

The two equations are decoupled. Each constant term is divided by the corresponding diagonal coefficient. The result is $\left(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{3}\right)$.
