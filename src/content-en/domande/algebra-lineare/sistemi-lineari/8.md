- $(x, y, z) = (-1, 1, 2)$
- $(x, y, z) = (2, -1, 1)$
- $(x, y, z) = (1, 2, -1)$
- $(x, y, z) = (1, -1, 2)$

## Explanation

Expanding along the first row gives $\det A = 1 \cdot (1 - 2) - 1 \cdot (-2 - 1) + 1 \cdot (4 + 1) = 7$. Since $\det A \neq 0$, **Cramer's rule** applies and the solution is unique. Substituting the column of constant terms $(2, 5, -3)$ for the first, second and third columns of $A$ gives $\det A_1 = 7$, $\det A_2 = -7$ and $\det A_3 = 14$. Therefore
$$
x = \frac{7}{7} = 1, \qquad y = \frac{-7}{7} = -1, \qquad z = \frac{14}{7} = 2 .
$$
