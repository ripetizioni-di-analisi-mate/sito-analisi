For $n \geq 2$, the matrix $A \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ has in row $i$ and column $j$ the entry $a_{ij} = i + j$. What is the rank of $A$?

- $2$ for every $n \geq 2$
- $n$ for every $n \geq 2$
- $n - 1$ for every $n \geq 2$
- $1$ for every $n \geq 2$

## Explanation

Row $i$ of $A$ is $(i + 1, \ i + 2, \ \dots, \ i + n)$, that is, the first row $(2, 3, \dots, n + 1)$ plus $(i - 1)$ times the row $(1, 1, \dots, 1)$. With $R_i \to R_i - R_1$ for $i = 2, \dots, n$ row $i$ becomes $(i - 1)(1, 1, \dots, 1)$, so all rows from the second on are multiples of $(1, \dots, 1)$. With further elementary operations all rows from the third on then become zero, and the rows $(2, 3, \dots, n + 1)$ and $(1, 1, \dots, 1)$ remain, which are not proportional. Since elementary operations do not change the rank, $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ **for every $n \geq 2$**; for example for $n = 2$ we have $\det A = 2 \cdot 4 - 3 \cdot 3 = -1 \neq 0$.
