$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 \\ 2 & 6\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-2,t\right)$. For which value of $t$ is the system $Az=B$ consistent?

- $-2$
- $-4$
- $4$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The second coefficient row is twice the first. The constant terms must satisfy the same relation. The result is $-4$.
