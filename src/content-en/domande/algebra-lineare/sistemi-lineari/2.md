- $(1, \ t - 1, \ t), \ t \in \mathbb{R}$
- $(1, \ t + 1, \ t), \ t \in \mathbb{R}$
- $(1, \ 1 - t, \ t), \ t \in \mathbb{R}$
- The unique solution $(1, -1, 0)$

## Explanation

With $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$ the second and third rows of the augmented matrix both become $(0 \ \ 3 \ \ {-3} \mid -3)$, so with $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$ the third row becomes zero. Two pivots remain for three unknowns, therefore $z = t$ is free and the system has $\infty^1$ solutions. The second equation $3y - 3t = -3$ gives $y = t - 1$. The first then gives $x = 2 + y - z = 2 + (t - 1) - t = 1$. The solutions are therefore $(1, \ t - 1, \ t)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$; the triple $(1, -1, 0)$ is only the one obtained for $t = 0$.
