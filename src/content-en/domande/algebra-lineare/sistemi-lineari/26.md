For which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ does the matrix
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 1 & k \\
1 & 1 & 2 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$
have rank $2$?

- $k = 1$
- $k = -1$
- $k = 3$
- For every $k \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

The minor of the first two rows and the first two columns equals $1$, so $\operatorname{rk} A \geq 2$. Its bordered minors are obtained by adding the third row and either the third or the fourth column:
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
1 & 1 & 2
\end{vmatrix}
= 0,
\qquad
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & k \\
1 & 1 & 3
\end{vmatrix}
= (3 - k) + 2\,(0 - 1) = 1 - k .
$$
By the **bordered minors theorem** the rank is $2$ if and only if both bordered minors are zero, that is, for $k = 1$. For $k \neq 1$ the second bordered minor is nonzero, therefore the rank is $3$.
