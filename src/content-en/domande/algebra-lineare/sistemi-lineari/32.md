Let $a, b, c, d, p, q \in \mathbb{R}$ with $ad - bc \neq 0$. What is the value of $x$ in the solution of the following system in the unknowns $x, y$?
$$
\begin{cases}
a x + b y = p \\[4pt]
c x + d y = q
\end{cases}
$$

- $x = \dfrac{aq - cp}{ad - bc}$
- $x = \dfrac{pd - bq}{bc - ad}$
- $x = \dfrac{pd - bq}{ad - bc}$
- $x = \dfrac{pd - bq}{ad + bc}$

## Explanation

The determinant of the coefficient matrix is $\det A = ad - bc \neq 0$, so **Cramer's rule** applies. The matrix $A_1$ is obtained from $A$ by replacing the column of $x$ with the column of constant terms, therefore
$$
\det A_1 =
\begin{vmatrix}
p & b \\
q & d
\end{vmatrix}
= pd - bq .
$$
Then $x = \dfrac{\det A_1}{\det A} = \dfrac{pd - bq}{ad - bc}$. The expression $\dfrac{aq - cp}{ad - bc}$ is the value of $y$, which is obtained from $\det A_2$.
