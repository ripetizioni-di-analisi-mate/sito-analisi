What is the value of $y$ in the solution of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
2x + 3y = 1 \\[4pt]
4x - y = 5
\end{cases}
$$

- $y = \dfrac{3}{7}$
- $y = -\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $y = -\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $y = \dfrac{8}{7}$

## Explanation

One has $\det A = 2 \cdot (-1) - 3 \cdot 4 = -14 \neq 0$, so **Cramer's rule** applies. The matrix $A_2$ is obtained by replacing the second column of $A$ with the constant terms, and
$$
\det A_2 =
\begin{vmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
4 & 5
\end{vmatrix}
= 10 - 4 = 6 .
$$
Therefore $y = \dfrac{6}{-14} = -\dfrac{3}{7}$. The value $\dfrac{8}{7}$ is that of $x$.
