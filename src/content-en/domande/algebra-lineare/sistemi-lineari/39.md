A homogeneous linear system has $3$ equations in $5$ unknowns. Which statement is true?

- The system has at least $\infty^2$ solutions
- The system has only the trivial solution if $\operatorname{rk} A = 3$
- The system can be inconsistent
- The system always has exactly $\infty^2$ solutions

## Explanation

A homogeneous system always has the trivial solution, so it is consistent. Since $A$ has $3$ rows, one has $\operatorname{rk} A \leq 3$, and the solutions are $\infty^{5 - \operatorname{rk} A}$ with $5 - \operatorname{rk} A \geq 2$. Therefore the system has **at least** $\infty^2$ solutions. The exact number depends on the rank. For example, if the three equations are proportional, then $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$ and the solutions are $\infty^4$.
