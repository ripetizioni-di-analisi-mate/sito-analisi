$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-2,4\right)$. For $Az=B$, what is the second component of $z$?

- $-2$
- $4$
- $-14$
- $10$

## Explanation

The second equation fixes the first unknown. Substituting into the first leaves one unknown. The result is $-14$.
