$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(4,2,0\right)$. Which coordinate, numbered one to three, is free in the solution of $Az=B$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The pivots are in columns one and three. The remaining coordinate is the free parameter. The result is $2$.
