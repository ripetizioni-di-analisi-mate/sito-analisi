For a system of two equations in the unknowns $x, y$ it is known that $\det A = 4$, $\det A_1 = 8$ and $\det A_2 = -2$, where $A_j$ is the matrix obtained from $A$ by replacing column $j$ with the column of constant terms. What is the solution of the system?

- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2}, \ -2 \right)$
- $(8, \ -2)$
- $\left( 2, \ -\dfrac{1}{2} \right)$
- $\left( 2, \ \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$

## Explanation

Since $\det A = 4 \neq 0$, **Cramer's rule** applies and the system has a unique solution. Each unknown is the ratio of the determinant of the matrix $A_j$ to $\det A$. Hence
$$
x = \frac{\det A_1}{\det A} = \frac{8}{4} = 2,
\qquad
y = \frac{\det A_2}{\det A} = \frac{-2}{4} = -\frac{1}{2} .
$$
