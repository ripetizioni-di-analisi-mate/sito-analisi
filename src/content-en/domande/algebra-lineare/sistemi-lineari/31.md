For the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 2y + z = 1 \\[4pt]
-2x + 4y - 2z = 3
\end{cases}
$$
what are $\operatorname{rk} A$ and $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b)$?

- $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$ and $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 1$
- $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ and $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 2$
- $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ and $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3$
- $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$ and $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 2$

## Explanation

The second row of $A$ is $(-2, 4, -2) = -2\,(1, -2, 1)$, so the rows of $A$ are proportional and $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$. In the augmented matrix the minor of the column of $x$ and the column of constant terms equals $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ -2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 5 \neq 0$, therefore $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 2$. By the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the system is therefore inconsistent.
