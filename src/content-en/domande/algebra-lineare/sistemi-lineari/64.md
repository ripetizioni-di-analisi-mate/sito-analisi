In a matrix $A$ with $4$ rows and $5$ columns the minor formed by rows $1$, $2$ and columns $1$, $2$ is nonzero, and one of its bordered minors, the one obtained with row $3$ and column $4$, is also nonzero. Which statement is true?

- $\operatorname{rk} A = 3$
- $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$
- To know the rank, all minors of order $3$ of $A$ are needed
- $\operatorname{rk} A \geq 3$

## Explanation

The nonzero bordered minor is a nonzero minor of order $3$ of $A$, so by the definition of rank $\operatorname{rk} A \geq 3$. To establish whether the rank is exactly $3$, the **bordered minors theorem** is applied to the new minor of order $3$, of rows $1$, $2$, $3$ and columns $1$, $2$, $4$. It has $(4 - 3)(5 - 3) = 2$ bordered minors: if both are zero the rank is $3$, otherwise it is $4$. Therefore from the given data one can only conclude $\operatorname{rk} A \geq 3$.
