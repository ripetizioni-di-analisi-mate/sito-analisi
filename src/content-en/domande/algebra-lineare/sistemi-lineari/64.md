$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-2,4\right)$. What is the Cramer numerator determinant for the second unknown in $Az=B$?

- $-3$
- $-6$
- $-12$
- $6$

## Explanation

The numerator is obtained by replacing the second column of $A$ with $B$. The first column remains the column of coefficients of the first unknown. The result is $6$.
