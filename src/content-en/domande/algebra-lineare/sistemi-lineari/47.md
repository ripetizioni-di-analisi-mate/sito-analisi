$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(3,t\right)$. For which value of $t$ is the system $Az=B$ consistent?

- $6$
- $12$
- $3$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The second coefficient row is twice the first. The constant terms must satisfy the same relation. The result is $6$.
