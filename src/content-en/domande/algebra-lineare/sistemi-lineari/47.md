The augmented matrix $(A \mid b)$ of a linear system has $3$ rows and $5$ columns. How many equations and how many unknowns does the system have?

- $3$ equations and $5$ unknowns
- $5$ equations and $3$ unknowns
- $3$ equations and $3$ unknowns
- $3$ equations and $4$ unknowns

## Explanation

Each row of the augmented matrix corresponds to an equation, so there are $3$ equations. The columns of $(A \mid b)$ are the columns of the coefficients of the unknowns plus the column of constant terms. Therefore $A$ has $5 - 1 = 4$ columns, and since each column of $A$ contains the coefficients of one unknown, the system has **$4$ unknowns**.
