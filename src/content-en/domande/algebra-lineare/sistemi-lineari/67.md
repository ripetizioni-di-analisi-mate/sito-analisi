$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(4,2,0\right)$. How many solutions does $Az=B$ have?

- Infinitely many solutions with one parameter
- No solution
- Infinitely many solutions with two parameters
- Exactly one solution

## Explanation

The first two equations are independent and the third holds identically. Three unknowns and two pivots leave one free parameter.
