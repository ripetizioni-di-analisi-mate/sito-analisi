For which values of the real parameters $a$ and $b$ does the following matrix have rank $2$?
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 2 \\
1 & 1 & a \\
2 & 1 & b
\end{pmatrix}
$$

- For $a = 3$ or $b = 4$
- For $a = -3$ and $b = -4$
- For $a = 3$ and $b = 4$
- For $a = 2$ and $b = 2$

## Explanation

The minor of rows $1$, $2$ and columns $1$, $2$ equals $1$, so $\operatorname{rk} A \geq 2$. It has two bordered minors, one with row $3$ and one with row $4$, both with column $3$:
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 2 \\
1 & 1 & a
\end{vmatrix}
= a - 3,
\qquad
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 2 \\
2 & 1 & b
\end{vmatrix}
= b - 4 .
$$
By the **bordered minors theorem** the rank is $2$ when both bordered minors are zero, that is, when
$$
\begin{cases}
a - 3 = 0 \\[4pt]
b - 4 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
If only one of them is zero, the other is a nonzero minor of order $3$ and the rank is $3$. Therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ **only for $a = 3$ and $b = 4$**.
