$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-2,8,4\right)$. What is the second component of the solution of $Az=B$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $0$

## Explanation

The third equation determines the third unknown. After substituting it into the second equation, we divide the remaining term by two. The result is $2$.
