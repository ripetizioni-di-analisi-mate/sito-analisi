The following matrix is in echelon form for every value of the parameter $k \in \mathbb{R}$. For which values of $k$ is its rank less than $3$?
$$
S =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & k & 1 & 3 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & k - 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

- For $k = 0$ and for $k = 1$
- Only for $k = 1$
- For no value of $k$
- Only for $k = 0$

## Explanation

By the theorem on **rank and reduction to echelon form** the rank of an echelon matrix is the number of its pivots. For $k \neq 0$ and $k \neq 1$ the pivots are $1$, $k$ and $k - 1$, in columns $1$, $2$ and $4$, so $\operatorname{rk} S = 3$. For $k = 0$ the second row becomes $(0, 0, 1, 3)$ and its pivot moves to column $3$, while the third row $(0, 0, 0, -1)$ has its pivot in column $4$. Therefore for $k = 0$ there are still three pivots and $\operatorname{rk} S = 3$. For $k = 1$ the third row is zero and only the pivots of the first two rows remain, so $\operatorname{rk} S = 2$. Therefore the rank is less than $3$ **only for $k = 1$**.
