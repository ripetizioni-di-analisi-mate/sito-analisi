$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(5,-6,-3\right)$. What is the second component of the solution of $Az=B$?

- $-6$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $0$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The third equation determines the third unknown. Substitute into the second and divide the remaining right-hand side by two. The result is $- \dfrac{3}{2}$.
