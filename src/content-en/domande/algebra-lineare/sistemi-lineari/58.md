What is the rank of the matrix $A = u\, v^T$, the row-by-column product of the column $u$ and the row $v^T$, where
$$
u =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\ 2 \\ 3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
v^T =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1
\end{pmatrix}?
$$

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The product of a $3 \times 1$ matrix and a $1 \times 2$ matrix is a $3 \times 2$ matrix, and the entry in position $(i, j)$ is $u_i v_j$:
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
4 & -2 \\
6 & -3
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The second row is twice the first and the third is three times the first, so every minor of order $2$ has two proportional rows and is zero. Since $A$ has nonzero entries, its rank is at least $1$. Therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$.
