A system of $4$ equations in $4$ unknowns has $\det A \neq 0$. How many determinants of order $4$ are needed to find all the unknowns by Cramer's rule?

- $4$
- $8$
- $16$
- $5$

## Explanation

**Cramer's rule** gives each unknown as $x_j = \dfrac{\det A_j}{\det A}$. Hence one needs the determinant of $A$ and the determinants of the four matrices $A_1$, $A_2$, $A_3$, $A_4$, each of order $4$. Therefore there are $4 + 1 = 5$ determinants.
