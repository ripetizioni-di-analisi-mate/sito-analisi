$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(5,-3\right)$. For the system $Az=B$, what is the second component of $z$?

- $-3$
- $-7$
- $5$
- $17$

## Explanation

The second equation fixes the first unknown. Substituting it into the first equation leaves a single unknown. The result is $17$.
