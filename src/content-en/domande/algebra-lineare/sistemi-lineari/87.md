The system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 2 \\[4pt]
k x - y = 1
\end{cases}
$$
depends on the parameter $k \in \mathbb{R}$. For which value of $k$ does its solution have two equal components?

- $k = 1$
- $k = -2$
- $k = 3$
- $k = 2$

## Explanation

If $x = y$, the first equation gives $2x = 2$, so the only possible pair is $(1, 1)$. Substituting $(1, 1)$ into the second equation gives $k - 1 = 1$, therefore $k = 2$. For $k = 2$ the determinant of the coefficient matrix equals $-1 - 2 = -3 \neq 0$, so $(1, 1)$ is the unique solution of the system.
