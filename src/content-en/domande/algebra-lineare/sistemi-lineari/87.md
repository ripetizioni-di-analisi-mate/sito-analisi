$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 4 & 5\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-3,2,t\right)$. For which $t$ does the solution of the first two equations also satisfy the third?

- $-2$
- $-12$
- $-7$
- $-22$

## Explanation

The first two equations directly determine the unknowns. Substitute them into the third row to find the required right-hand side. The result is $-2$.
