What is the value of $x_1$ in the solution of the following system, in the unknowns $x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4$?
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1 - x_2 = 1 \\[4pt]
x_2 - x_3 = 1 \\[4pt]
x_3 - x_4 = 1 \\[4pt]
x_4 = 1
\end{cases}
$$

- $x_1 = 1$
- $x_1 = 3$
- $x_1 = 2$
- $x_1 = 4$

## Explanation

The system is already in echelon form, with a pivot in every column, so it is solved by back substitution. The last equation gives $x_4 = 1$. The third gives $x_3 = 1 + x_4 = 2$, and the second $x_2 = 1 + x_3 = 3$. Therefore the first gives $x_1 = 1 + x_2 = 4$.
