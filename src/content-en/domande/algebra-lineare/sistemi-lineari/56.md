$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 3\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first component of the solution to $Az=0$ with $z_3=1$?

- $-3$
- $-8$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

The second equation gives $z_2=-b$. We substitute $z_2=-b$ and $z_3=1$ into the first. Substituting $b=3$ gives $4$.
