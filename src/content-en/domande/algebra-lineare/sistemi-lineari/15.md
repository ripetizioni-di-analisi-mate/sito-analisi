The system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + a y = 2 \\[4pt]
2x + 4y = 3
\end{cases}
$$
depends on the real parameter $a$. For $a = 2$, which statement is true?

- The two lines are coincident and the system has infinitely many solutions
- The two lines are intersecting and the system is determined
- The two lines are perpendicular and the system is determined
- The two lines are parallel and distinct and the system is inconsistent

## Explanation

For $a = 2$ the equations are $x + 2y = 2$ and $2x + 4y = 3$. The coefficients of the unknowns are proportional, because $(2, 4) = 2\,(1, 2)$, so $\det A = 4 - 4 = 0$ and the two lines are parallel. Multiplying the first equation by $2$ gives $2x + 4y = 4$, which has the same left-hand side as the second but a different constant term. Therefore the lines are **parallel and distinct** and the system is **inconsistent**.
