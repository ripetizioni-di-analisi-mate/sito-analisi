$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(3,2,1\right)$. What is the second component of the solution of $Az=B$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The third equation determines the third unknown. Substitute into the second and divide the remaining right-hand side by two. The result is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
