We look for the parabola $y = a x^2 + b x + c$ through the points $(0, 1)$, $(1, 2)$ and $(2, 5)$. The three conditions form a linear system in the unknowns $a, b, c$. Which is the parabola?

- $y = x^2 + 1$
- $y = 2x^2 - x + 1$
- $y = x + 1$
- $y = x^2 + 2x + 1$

## Explanation

Substituting the coordinates of the three points into the equation $y = a x^2 + b x + c$ gives the system
$$
\begin{cases}
c = 1 \\[4pt]
a + b + c = 2 \\[4pt]
4a + 2b + c = 5
\end{cases}
$$
Since $c = 1$, the other two equations become $a + b = 1$ and $4a + 2b = 4$. Subtracting twice the first from the second gives $2a = 2$, so $a = 1$ and $b = 0$. Therefore the parabola is $y = x^2 + 1$, which indeed takes the values $1$, $2$ and $5$ at $x = 0$, $1$, $2$.
