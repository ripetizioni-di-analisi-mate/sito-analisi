$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 0 \\ 0 & -2\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(4,1\right)$. What is the ordered solution of the system $Az=B$?

- $\left(\dfrac{4}{3},- \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- $\left(-2,\dfrac{1}{3}\right)$
- $\left(12,-2\right)$
- $\left(- \dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{4}{3}\right)$

## Explanation

The two equations are decoupled. Each constant term is divided by the corresponding diagonal coefficient. The result is $\left(\dfrac{4}{3},- \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$.
