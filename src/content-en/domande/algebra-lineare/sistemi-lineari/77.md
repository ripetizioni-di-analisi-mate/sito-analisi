$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 2 & 8\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(5,t\right)$. For which value of $t$ is the system $Az=B$ consistent?

- $-10$
- $20$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

The second coefficient row is twice the first. The constant terms must satisfy the same relation. The result is $10$.
