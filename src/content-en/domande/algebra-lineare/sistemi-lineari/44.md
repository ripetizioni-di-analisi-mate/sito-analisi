For $k \neq 1$, what is the value of $y$ in the unique solution of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 2 \\[4pt]
x + k y = 3
\end{cases}
$$

- $y = \dfrac{1}{1 - k}$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{k - 1}$
- $y = \dfrac{2k - 3}{k - 1}$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{k + 1}$

## Explanation

One has $\det A = k - 1$, which is nonzero for $k \neq 1$, so the system is determined. Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $(k - 1)\,y = 1$. Since $k - 1 \neq 0$, one can divide and obtain $y = \dfrac{1}{k - 1}$. The expression $\dfrac{2k - 3}{k - 1}$ is the value of $x = 2 - y$.
