$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. How many free parameters does the solution of $Az=0$ have?

- $3$
- $0$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

The pivot columns are the first and third. Of the four variables, two remain free. The result is $2$.
