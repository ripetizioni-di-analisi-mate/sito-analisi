A system $A x = b$ in the unknowns $x, y, z$ has $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$, and the triples $(2, 1, 0)$ and $(3, -1, 1)$ are two of its solutions. What is the set of all solutions?

- $\{ (2, 1, 0) + t\,(3, -1, 1) : t \in \mathbb{R} \}$
- $\{ (2, 1, 0) + t\,(1, -2, 1) : t \in \mathbb{R} \}$
- $\{ t\,(2, 1, 0) + s\,(3, -1, 1) : s, t \in \mathbb{R} \}$
- $\{ (2, 1, 0) + t\,(5, 0, 1) : t \in \mathbb{R} \}$

## Explanation

The difference of two solutions of $A x = b$ solves the associated homogeneous system, because $A(u - v) = b - b = 0$. Hence $(3, -1, 1) - (2, 1, 0) = (1, -2, 1)$ belongs to $S_0$. Since $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$, the space $S_0$ has dimension $3 - 2 = 1$, therefore $S_0 = \langle (1, -2, 1) \rangle$. By the theorem on the **structure of the solutions** the solutions are $(2, 1, 0) + t\,(1, -2, 1)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$, that is, the points of the line through the two given solutions.
