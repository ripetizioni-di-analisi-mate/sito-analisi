$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 5\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first component of the solution to $Az=0$ with $z_3=1$?

- $-22$
- $18$
- $22$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The second equation gives $z_2=-b$. Substitute $z_2=-b$ and $z_3=1$ into the first. Substituting $b=5$ gives $18$.
