What is the dimension of the solution space of the homogeneous system
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 - x_3 + x_4 = 0 \\[4pt]
2x_1 - x_2 + x_3 = 0 \\[4pt]
3x_1 + x_4 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
in the unknowns $x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The third row of $A$ is the sum of the first two, so with $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1 - R_2$ it becomes zero. The first two rows are not proportional, therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$. The solution space of a homogeneous system in $n$ unknowns has dimension $n - \operatorname{rk} A$, so in this case the dimension is $4 - 2 = 2$.
