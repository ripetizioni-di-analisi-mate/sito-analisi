A linear system of $3$ equations in the $2$ unknowns $x, y$ has augmented matrix $(A \mid b)$, square of order $3$, with $\det (A \mid b) \neq 0$. Which statement is true?

- The system has a unique solution
- The system has infinitely many solutions
- To conclude one also needs to compute $\operatorname{rk} A$
- The system is inconsistent

## Explanation

The matrix $A$ has two columns, so $\operatorname{rk} A \leq 2$ whatever the coefficients are. Since $\det (A \mid b) \neq 0$, one has $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3$. The two ranks are different, therefore by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the system is **inconsistent**. For example, the system formed by $x = 0$, $y = 0$ and $x + y = 1$ has $\det (A \mid b) = 1$ and has no solutions.
