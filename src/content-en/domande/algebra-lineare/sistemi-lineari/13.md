For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y = 1 \\[4pt]
x - y = 4 \\[4pt]
2x + y = k
\end{cases}
$$
consistent?

- $k = -5$
- $k = 5$
- $k = 7$
- $k = 6$

## Explanation

The augmented matrix is square of order $3$, whereas $\operatorname{rk} A \leq 2$. Expanding along the first row gives
$$
\det (A \mid b) =
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 2 & 1 \\
1 & -1 & 4 \\
2 & 1 & k
\end{vmatrix}
= (-k - 4) - 2\,(k - 8) + 3 = -3\,(k - 5) .
$$
For $k \neq 5$ one therefore has $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3 > \operatorname{rk} A$, and the system is inconsistent. For $k = 5$ the first two equations give $(3, -1)$, which also satisfies the third because $6 - 1 = 5$; therefore the system is consistent **only for** $k = 5$.
