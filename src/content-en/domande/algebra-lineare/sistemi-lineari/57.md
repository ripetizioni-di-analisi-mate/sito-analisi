$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(1,2,t\right)$. For which $t$ does the solution of the first two equations also satisfy the third?

- $-4$
- $2$
- $8$
- $7$

## Explanation

The first two equations directly determine the two unknowns. Substituting their values into the third row gives the required constant term. The result is $8$.
