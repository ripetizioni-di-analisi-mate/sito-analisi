$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 5\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-3,1\right)$. With the third unknown fixed to one, what is the first component of the solution to $Az=B$?

- $-9$
- $13$
- $-21$
- $11$

## Explanation

We substitute one into the second equation to find the second unknown. The first equation then determines the first unknown. The result is $11$.
