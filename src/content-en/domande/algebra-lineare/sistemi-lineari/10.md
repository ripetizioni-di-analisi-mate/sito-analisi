Which set of vectors is a basis of the solution space of the homogeneous system
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 = 0 \\[4pt]
x_1 - x_2 + 2x_3 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
in the unknowns $x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4$?

- $\{ (-3, 1, 2, 0), \ (-1, -1, 0, 2) \}$
- $\{ (-3, 1, 2, 0) \}$
- $\{ (-3, 1, 2, 0), \ (-1, 1, 0, 2) \}$
- $\{ (3, 1, -2, 0), \ (-1, -1, 0, 2) \}$

## Explanation

The two rows of $A$ are not proportional, so $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ and the solution space has dimension $4 - 2 = 2$. With $R_2 \to R_2 - R_1$ the second equation becomes $-2x_2 + x_3 - x_4 = 0$, therefore $x_2 = \dfrac{x_3 - x_4}{2}$ and, from the first, $x_1 = -\dfrac{3x_3 + x_4}{2}$. Choosing $(x_3, x_4) = (2, 0)$ gives $(-3, 1, 2, 0)$, and choosing $(x_3, x_4) = (0, 2)$ gives $(-1, -1, 0, 2)$. These two vectors are linearly independent solutions in a space of dimension $2$, so they form a **basis**. A single vector spans a space of dimension $1$, and the vectors $(-1, 1, 0, 2)$ and $(3, 1, -2, 0)$ do not satisfy the first equation.
