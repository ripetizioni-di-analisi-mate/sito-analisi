## Explanation

The rank is computed by reducing $A$ to echelon form, because elementary row operations do not change the rank. With $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$ the second and third rows both become $(0 \ \ 3 \ \ {-3} \ \ {-2})$, so with $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$ the third row becomes zero. The resulting echelon matrix has two pivots, therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$.
