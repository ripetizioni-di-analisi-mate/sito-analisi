$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 0 \\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-3,1\right)$. What is the ordered solution of $Az=B$?

- $\left(-12,5\right)$
- $\left(- \dfrac{3}{4},\dfrac{1}{5}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{5},- \dfrac{3}{4}\right)$
- $\left(- \dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{1}{4}\right)$

## Explanation

The equations are decoupled. Divide each right-hand-side entry by the corresponding diagonal coefficient. The result is $\left(- \dfrac{3}{4},\dfrac{1}{5}\right)$.
