$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 3 & -2\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(4,2,t\right)$. For which $t$ does the solution of the first two equations also satisfy the third?

- $12$
- $-2$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

The first two equations directly determine the unknowns. Substitute them into the third row to find the required right-hand side. The result is $8$.
