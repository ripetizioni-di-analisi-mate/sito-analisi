What are the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 2 \\[4pt]
2x + 2y = 4
\end{cases}
$$
in the unknowns $x, y, z$, with $s, t \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $(2 - t, \ t, \ 0)$
- $(2 - s, \ s, \ t)$
- $(2 - t, \ t, \ t)$
- $(2 - 2s, \ s, \ t)$

## Explanation

The unknown $z$ has coefficient zero in both equations, so the column of $z$ in the coefficient matrix is zero:
$$
(A \mid b) =
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
1 & 1 & 0 & 2 \\
2 & 2 & 0 & 4
\end{array}\right).
$$
The second row is twice the first, therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = \operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 1$ and by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** there are $\infty^{3 - 1} = \infty^2$ solutions. Setting $y = s$ gives $x = 2 - s$, while $z = t$ remains free because no equation constrains it. Hence the solutions are $(2 - s, \ s, \ t)$ with $s, t \in \mathbb{R}$.
