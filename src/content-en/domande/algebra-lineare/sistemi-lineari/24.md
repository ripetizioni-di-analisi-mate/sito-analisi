What is the rank of the following matrix?
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 2 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
1 & 1 & 3 & 2 \\
2 & 1 & 5 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The third row is the sum of the first two, and the fourth is $2R_1 + R_2$. With the elementary operations $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1 - R_2$ and $R_4 \to R_4 - 2R_1 - R_2$ the last two rows become zero, and these operations do not change the rank. The resulting matrix is in echelon form with two pivots, in the first two columns. Therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$.
