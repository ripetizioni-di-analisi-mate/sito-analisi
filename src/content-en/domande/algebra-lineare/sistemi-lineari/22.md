Which of the following matrices has rank $1$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 3 \\ -2 & 4 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 3 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 3 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 3 \\ -2 & 4 & -6 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

A nonzero matrix with two rows has rank $1$ if and only if all its minors of order $2$ are zero, that is, if the two rows are proportional. In the matrix with second row $(-2, 4, -6)$ one has $(-2, 4, -6) = -2\,(1, -2, 3)$, so the rank is $1$. In the other three matrices the rows are not proportional. For example, in the matrix with second row $(-2, 4, 6)$ the minor of columns $2$ and $3$ equals $\begin{vmatrix} -2 & 3 \\ 4 & 6 \end{vmatrix} = -24 \neq 0$, therefore the rank is $2$.
