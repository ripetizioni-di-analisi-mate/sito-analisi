$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(5,-3\right)$. What is the first component of $z$ if the right-hand side of $Az=B$ is doubled?

- $22$
- $29$
- $34$
- $17$

## Explanation

With the original right-hand side, the second component is $c$ and the first is $b-ac$. By linearity, doubling the right-hand side doubles both components. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$, $c=-3$ gives $34$.
