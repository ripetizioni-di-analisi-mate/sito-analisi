What are the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 = 2 \\[4pt]
x_1 - x_2 + x_3 - x_4 = 0 \\[4pt]
2x_1 + 2x_3 = 2
\end{cases}
$$
in the unknowns $x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4$, with $s, t \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $(1 + s, \ 1 - t, \ s, \ t)$
- $(1 - s, \ 1 + t, \ s, \ t)$
- $(1 - s, \ 1 - t, \ s, \ t)$
- $(1 - s, \ 1 - t, \ -s, \ t)$

## Explanation

The third row of the augmented matrix is the sum of the first two, whereas the minor $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = -2$ of the columns of $x_1$ and $x_2$ is nonzero. Hence $\operatorname{rk} A = \operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 2$, and by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the system is consistent with $\infty^{4 - 2} = \infty^2$ solutions. Adding the first two equations gives $2x_1 + 2x_3 = 2$, that is, $x_1 = 1 - x_3$. Subtracting them gives $2x_2 + 2x_4 = 2$, that is, $x_2 = 1 - x_4$. Setting $x_3 = s$ and $x_4 = t$, the solutions are therefore $(1 - s, \ 1 - t, \ s, \ t)$.
