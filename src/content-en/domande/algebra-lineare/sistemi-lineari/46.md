The system $A x = b$ in the unknowns $x, y, z$ has
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 2 \\
3 & -1 & 0 \\
0 & 4 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
b =
\begin{pmatrix}
5 \\ 2 \\ -3
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
What is the second equation of the system?

- $-y + 4z = 2$
- $3x - y = 2$
- $3x - y = 5$
- $3x - y + z = 2$

## Explanation

In the row-by-column product the second component of $A x$ is the product of the second row of $A$ and the column of unknowns. The second row is $(3, -1, 0)$, so the second component of $A x$ is $3x - y + 0 \cdot z = 3x - y$. This component must equal the second component of $b$, which is $2$. Therefore the second equation is $3x - y = 2$. The equation $-y + 4z = 2$ is obtained by reading the second column of $A$ instead of the second row.
