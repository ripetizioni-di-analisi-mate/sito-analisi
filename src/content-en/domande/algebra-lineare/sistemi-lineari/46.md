$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(3,1\right)$. For the system $Az=B$, what is the second component of $z$?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The second equation fixes the first unknown. Substituting it into the first equation leaves a single unknown. The result is $1$.
