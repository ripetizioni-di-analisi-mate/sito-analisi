$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(3,1\right)$. After $R_2\leftarrow R_2-aR_1$ on the augmented system, what is the new second right-hand-side entry?

- $-5$
- $1$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The elimination operation also acts on the right-hand-side column. Subtract $a$ times the first entry from the second. Substituting $a=2$ gives $-5$.
