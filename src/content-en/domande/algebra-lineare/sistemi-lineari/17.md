Which of the following transformations of the augmented matrix of a system of three equations in three unknowns can produce a system with solutions different from the original ones?

- $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_3$
- $R_2 \to 0 \cdot R_2$
- $R_2 \to R_2 - 5R_1$
- $R_3 \to -2R_3$

## Explanation

Swapping two rows, multiplying a row by a nonzero number and adding to a row a multiple of another are **elementary operations**, so they produce an equivalent system. The transformation $R_2 \to 0 \cdot R_2$ multiplies a row by zero, therefore it is not an elementary operation. After the transformation the second equation becomes $0 = 0$, and its information is lost. For example, the system formed by $x = 1$, $y = 2$ and $z = 3$ has the unique solution $(1, 2, 3)$, whereas after the transformation it has the infinitely many solutions $(1, t, 3)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
