$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(3,1\right)$. What is the first component of $z$ if the right-hand side of $Az=B$ is doubled?

- $2$
- $-1$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

With the original right-hand side, the second component is $c$ and the first is $b-ac$. Linearity doubles both components when the right-hand side doubles. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$, $c=1$ gives $2$.
