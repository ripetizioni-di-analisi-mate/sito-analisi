In a matrix with $4$ rows and $5$ columns a minor of order $2$ is considered. How many bordered minors does it have?

- $5$
- $3$
- $6$
- $10$

## Explanation

A bordered minor is obtained by adding to the rows and columns of the minor *one* row and *one* column not yet chosen. There are $4 - 2 = 2$ remaining rows and $5 - 2 = 3$ remaining columns. Each pair formed by a remaining row and a remaining column gives a different bordered minor, so there are $2 \cdot 3 = 6$ bordered minors.
