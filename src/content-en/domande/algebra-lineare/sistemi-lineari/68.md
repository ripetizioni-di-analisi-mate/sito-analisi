$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 \\ t & -2\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(4,1\right)$. For which $t$ does the system $Az=B$ lose the possibility of having a unique solution?

- $-6$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

A unique solution requires an invertible coefficient matrix. Its determinant is $b-at$ so it vanishes at only one parameter value. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$ gives $- \dfrac{2}{3}$.
