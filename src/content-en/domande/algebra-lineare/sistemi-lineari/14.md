Which matrix is the augmented matrix $(A \mid b)$ of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y - z = 4 \\[4pt]
3x - y = 1
\end{cases}
$$
in the unknowns $x, y, z$?

- $\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & -1 & 4 \\ 3 & -1 & 0 & 1 \end{array}\right)$
- $\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & -1 & 4 \\ 3 & -1 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right)$
- $\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & -1 & 4 \\ 3 & 0 & -1 & 1 \end{array}\right)$
- $\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & -1 & 4 \\ 3 & -1 & 0 & -1 \end{array}\right)$

## Explanation

Row $i$ of the augmented matrix contains, in the order $x, y, z$, the coefficients of equation $i$ followed by its constant term. The first equation gives the row $(1 \ \ 2 \ \ {-1} \mid 4)$. In the second equation the unknown $z$ does not appear, so its coefficient is $0$, and the row is $(3 \ \ {-1} \ \ 0 \mid 1)$. The constant term is written with the sign it has on the right-hand side of the equation, therefore the last entry is $1$.
