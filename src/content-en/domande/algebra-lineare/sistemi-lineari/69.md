$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & -2\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(4,1\right)$. With the third unknown fixed to one, what is the first component of the solution to $Az=B$?

- $11$
- $-1$
- $-7$
- $-5$

## Explanation

We substitute one into the second equation to find the second unknown. The first equation then determines the first unknown. The result is $-7$.
