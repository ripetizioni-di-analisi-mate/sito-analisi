A system has coefficient rows $r_1=(1,2,3)$, $r_2=(4,5,6)$ and $r_3=(7,8,9)$. The first two entries on the right are $6$ and $15$. Which third entry makes the coefficient and augmented matrices have the same rank in the Rouché–Capelli theorem?

- $21$
- $30$
- $24$
- $9$

## Explanation

The first two rows are independent and $r_3=2r_2-r_1$. For this relation to hold in the right-hand column, its third entry must be $2\cdot15-6=24$. Any other value creates a third independent row in the augmented matrix.
