What are the solutions of the following system, with $t \in \mathbb{R}$?
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y + 3z = 6 \\[4pt]
4x + 5y + 6z = 15 \\[4pt]
7x + 8y + 9z = 24
\end{cases}
$$

- The unique solution $(1, 1, 1)$
- The system is inconsistent
- $(1 + t, \ 1 - 2t, \ 1 + t)$
- $(1 + t, \ 1 + 2t, \ 1 + t)$

## Explanation

Each constant term is the sum of the coefficients of its equation, so $(1, 1, 1)$ is a particular solution. The third row of $A$ is twice the second minus the first, therefore $\det A = 0$ and $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$, since the first two rows are not proportional. Hence the associated homogeneous system has $\infty^1$ solutions. To find them, $R_2 \to R_2 - 4R_1$ gives $-3y - 6z = 0$, that is, $y = -2z$, and the first homogeneous equation gives $x = -2y - 3z = z$; therefore $S_0 = \langle (1, -2, 1) \rangle$. By the theorem on the **structure of the solutions** the solutions are $(1, 1, 1) + t\,(1, -2, 1) = (1 + t, \ 1 - 2t, \ 1 + t)$.
