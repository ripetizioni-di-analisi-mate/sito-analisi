We want to reduce to echelon form the augmented matrix
$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
2 & 1 & -1 & 3 \\
4 & 1 & 1 & 5 \\
-2 & 3 & 2 & 1
\end{array}\right) .
$$
Which elementary operations, performed one after the other, make zero the entries of the first column below the pivot $2$?

- $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 + R_1$
- $R_2 \to R_2 + 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$
- $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$
- $R_1 \to R_1 - \dfrac{1}{2} R_2$ and $R_3 \to R_3 + R_1$

## Explanation

To make the entry $a_{i1}$ zero, one subtracts from row $i$ the first row multiplied by $\dfrac{a_{i1}}{a_{11}}$. For the second row the coefficient is $\dfrac{4}{2} = 2$, so the operation is $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$, which gives $(0 \ -1 \ 3 \mid -1)$. For the third row the coefficient is $\dfrac{-2}{2} = -1$, so the operation is $R_3 \to R_3 + R_1$, which gives $(0 \ 4 \ 1 \mid 4)$. The first row is unchanged, therefore the pivot $2$ stays in place. An operation that modifies the first row, such as $R_1 \to R_1 - \dfrac{1}{2} R_2$, makes the pivot zero instead of the entries below it.
