For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + k y = 1 \\[4pt]
k x + y = 1
\end{cases}
$$
inconsistent?

- $k = -1$
- $k = 1$
- $k = 1$ and $k = -1$
- $k = 0$

## Explanation

One has $\det A = 1 - k^2$, so for $k \neq \pm 1$ the system is determined. For $k = 1$ the two equations both coincide with $x + y = 1$, and the system has infinitely many solutions. For $k = -1$ the equations are $x - y = 1$ and $-x + y = 1$, and adding them gives $0 = 2$. Therefore the system is **inconsistent only for** $k = -1$.
