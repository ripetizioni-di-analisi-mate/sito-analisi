$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & -2\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first component of the solution to $Az=0$ with $z_3=1$?

- $-8$
- $2$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The second equation gives $z_2=-b$. Substitute $z_2=-b$ and $z_3=1$ into the first. Substituting $b=-2$ gives $-8$.
