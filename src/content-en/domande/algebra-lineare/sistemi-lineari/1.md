- $(x, y, z) = (3, 1, -1)$
- $(x, y, z) = (1, 1, 1)$
- $(x, y, z) = (3, -1, 1)$
- $(x, y, z) = (2, -1, 2)$

## Explanation

The augmented matrix is reduced to echelon form. With $R_2 \to R_2 - R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - 2R_1$ the last two rows become $(0 \ \ 1 \ \ {-2} \mid -3)$ and $(0 \ \ {-3} \ \ 1 \mid 4)$, and with $R_3 \to R_3 + 3R_2$ the third becomes $(0 \ \ 0 \ \ {-5} \mid -5)$.
$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
1 & 1 & 1 & 3 \\
0 & 1 & -2 & -3 \\
0 & 0 & -5 & -5
\end{array}\right)
$$
The last equation $-5z = -5$ gives $z = 1$, so the second, $y - 2z = -3$, gives $y = -1$. The first then gives $x = 3 - y - z = 3$. There are three pivots and three unknowns, therefore the system is **determined** with the unique solution $(3, -1, 1)$.
