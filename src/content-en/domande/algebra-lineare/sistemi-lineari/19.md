The augmented matrix of a system in the unknowns $x, y, z$, reduced to echelon form, is
$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
1 & 2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right) .
$$
What are the solutions of the system, with $t \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $(1 - 5t, \ 1 - 2t, \ t)$
- $(1 + 5t, \ 1 + 2t, \ t)$
- $(3 - 3t, \ 1 - 2t, \ t)$
- $(1 + 5t, \ 1 - 2t, \ t)$

## Explanation

The third row corresponds to the equation $0 = 0$ and is discarded. The pivots are in the columns of $x$ and $y$, so $z = t$ is free. The second equation $y + 2z = 1$ gives $y = 1 - 2t$. Substituting into the first one obtains
$$
x = 3 - 2y + z = 3 - 2\,(1 - 2t) + t = 1 + 5t .
$$
