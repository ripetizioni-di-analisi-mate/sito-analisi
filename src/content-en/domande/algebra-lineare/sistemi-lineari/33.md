A linear system of $4$ equations in $3$ unknowns has $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$. Which values can $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b)$ take?

- Only $2$
- $2$ or $3$
- $1$, $2$ or $3$
- $2$, $3$ or $4$

## Explanation

The matrix $A$ is a submatrix of $(A \mid b)$, so every nonzero minor of $A$ is also a minor of $(A \mid b)$ and $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) \geq 2$. The augmented matrix has only one more column than $A$, therefore $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) \leq \operatorname{rk} A + 1 = 3$, even though a $4 \times 4$ matrix could have rank $4$. Both values $2$ and $3$ occur. For the system in the unknowns $x, y, z$ formed by $x = 0$, $y = 0$, $x + y = 0$ and $x + y = c$ one has $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$, while $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b)$ equals $2$ for $c = 0$ and $3$ for $c \neq 0$.
