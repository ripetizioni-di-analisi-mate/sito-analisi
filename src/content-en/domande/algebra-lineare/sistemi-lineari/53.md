$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 \\ t & 3\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(1,1\right)$. For which $t$ does the system $Az=B$ lose the possibility of having a unique solution?

- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

A unique solution requires an invertible coefficient matrix. Its determinant is $b-at$ so it vanishes at only one parameter value. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$ gives $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
