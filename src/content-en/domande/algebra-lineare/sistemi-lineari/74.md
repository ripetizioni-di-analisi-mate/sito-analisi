To solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y - z = 3 \\[4pt]
2x + y + z = 0 \\[4pt]
x - y + 3z = 5
\end{cases}
$$
by Cramer's rule one computes $y = \dfrac{\det A_2}{\det A}$. What is the matrix $A_2$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 5 & -1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 3 & 0 & 5 \\ 1 & -1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -3 & -1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & -5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The matrix $A_2$ is obtained from $A$ by replacing the second column, that of the coefficients of $y$, with the column of constant terms $(3, 0, 5)$. The first and third columns remain those of $A$, namely $(1, 2, 1)$ and $(-1, 1, 3)$. Therefore
$$
A_2 =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 3 & -1 \\
2 & 0 & 1 \\
1 & 5 & 3
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The matrix with the first column replaced is $A_1$, which is used to compute $x$.
