$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-2,4\right)$. What is the first component of $z$ if the right-hand side of $Az=B$ is doubled?

- $-14$
- $-28$
- $-16$
- $-26$

## Explanation

With the original right-hand side, the second component is $c$ and the first is $b-ac$. By linearity, doubling the right-hand side doubles both components. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$, $c=4$ gives $-28$.
