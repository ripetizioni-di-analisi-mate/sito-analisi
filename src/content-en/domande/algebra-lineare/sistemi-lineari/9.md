For a square system with $\det A\ne0$, Cramer’s rule gives $x=2$ and $y=-1$. The right-hand side $b$ is replaced by $b+3a_1$, where $a_1$ is the first column of $A$. How do $x$ and $y$ change?

- $x=2,\ y=2$
- $x=5,\ y=-1$
- $x=6,\ y=-3$
- $x=-1,\ y=-1$

## Explanation

Multilinearity adds $3\det A$ to the numerator for the first unknown. In the numerator for the second, the extra term has two identical columns and vanishes. Dividing by $\det A$ adds three to the first unknown alone.
