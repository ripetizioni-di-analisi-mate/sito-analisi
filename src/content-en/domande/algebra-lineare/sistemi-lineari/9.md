- $x = -\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $x = -3$
- $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Expanding along the first row gives $\det A = 2 \cdot (6 - 1) - 1 \cdot (2 + 1) + 1 \cdot (-1 - 3) = 3$, so **Cramer's rule** applies. The matrix $A_1$ is obtained by replacing the first column of $A$ with the column of constant terms $(1, 2, 0)$, and
$$
\det A_1 =
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & 1 \\
2 & 3 & -1 \\
0 & -1 & 2
\end{vmatrix}
= 1 \cdot (6 - 1) - 1 \cdot (4 - 0) + 1 \cdot (-2 - 0) = -1 .
$$
Therefore $x = \dfrac{\det A_1}{\det A} = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is that of the unknown $z$.
