$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ t & 5\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(-3,1\right)$. At which $t$ does $Az=B$ cease to have a unique solution?

- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $20$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

A unique solution requires an invertible coefficient matrix. Its determinant is $b-at$ and vanishes at exactly one parameter. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$ gives $\dfrac{5}{4}$.
