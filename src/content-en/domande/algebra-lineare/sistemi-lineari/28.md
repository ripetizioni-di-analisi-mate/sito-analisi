A linear system of $4$ equations in $5$ unknowns has $\operatorname{rk} A = \operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3$. How many solutions does it have?

- $\infty^1$ solutions
- $\infty^2$ solutions
- $\infty^3$ solutions
- Exactly one solution

## Explanation

Since the two ranks are equal, by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the system is consistent. The number of free parameters is the number of unknowns minus the common rank, that is, $5 - 3 = 2$. Therefore the system has $\infty^2$ solutions. Since the rank is $3$, one of the four equations is a consequence of the other three and can be discarded.
