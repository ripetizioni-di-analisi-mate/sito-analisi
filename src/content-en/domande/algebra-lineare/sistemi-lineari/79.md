$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(5,-3\right)$. What is the Cramer numerator determinant for the second unknown in $Az=B$?

- $-8$
- $-4$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

Replace the second column of $A$ with $B$ to get the numerator. The first column stays unchanged. The result is $-8$.
