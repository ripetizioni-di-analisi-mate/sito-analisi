- The system has the unique solution $(5, -2, 0)$
- The system is inconsistent
- The system has $\infty^1$ solutions
- The system has $\infty^2$ solutions

## Explanation

With $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - 3R_1$ the last two rows of the augmented matrix become $(0 \ \ {-1} \ \ 3 \mid 2)$ and $(0 \ \ {-1} \ \ 3 \mid 3)$. With $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$ the third row becomes $(0 \ \ 0 \ \ 0 \mid 1)$, which corresponds to the equation $0 = 1$. Therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ and $\operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 3$, and by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the system is **inconsistent**. The triple $(5, -2, 0)$ satisfies the first two equations, but in the third it gives $15 - 10 = 5 \neq 6$.
