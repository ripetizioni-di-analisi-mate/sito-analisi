$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 3\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\left(1,1\right)$. With the third unknown fixed to one, what is the first component of the solution to $Az=B$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $-5$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Substitute one into the second equation to find the second unknown. The first equation then determines the first unknown. The result is $3$.
