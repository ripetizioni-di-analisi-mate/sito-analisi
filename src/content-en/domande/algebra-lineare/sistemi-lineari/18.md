In the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 1 \\[4pt]
2x + 2y + 3z = 1
\end{cases}
$$
in the unknowns $x, y, z$, which unknown cannot be chosen as the free parameter?

- $z$
- $x$
- $y$
- None of the three

## Explanation

The rows of $A$ are not proportional, so $\operatorname{rk} A = \operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 2$ and the solutions are $\infty^{3 - 2} = \infty^1$, with a single free unknown. The two unknowns that stay on the left must correspond to the columns of a nonzero minor of order $2$. The columns of $x$ and $y$ give $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 0$, whereas those of $x$ and $z$ and those of $y$ and $z$ give $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 1$, therefore the parameter can be $x$ or $y$. With $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ the second equation becomes $z = -1$, so $z$ has the same value in all the solutions $(2 - t, \ t, \ -1)$ and **cannot be chosen** as the free parameter.
