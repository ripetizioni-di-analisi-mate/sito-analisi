The diagonals of the parallelogram with sides $u$ and $v$ are the vectors $u + v$ and $u - v$. If the diagonals have length $5$ and $3$, what is the inner product $\langle u, v \rangle$?

- $4$
- $16$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

By the bilinearity and the symmetry of the inner product one has
$$
\|u + v\|^2 = \|u\|^2 + 2\langle u, v \rangle + \|v\|^2,
\qquad
\|u - v\|^2 = \|u\|^2 - 2\langle u, v \rangle + \|v\|^2 .
$$
Subtracting the second equality from the first, the terms $\|u\|^2$ and $\|v\|^2$ cancel and what remains is $\|u + v\|^2 - \|u - v\|^2 = 4\langle u, v \rangle$. Therefore
$$
\langle u, v \rangle = \frac{25 - 9}{4} = 4 ,
$$
without needing to know the lengths of the sides.
