The vectors $u$ and $v$ are orthogonal, with $\|u\| = 1$ and $\|v\| = \sqrt{3}$. What is the angle between $u$ and $u + v$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,1,0,u;0,0,0,1.732,v" xmin=-0.6 xmax=2.2 ymin=-0.6 ymax=2.3 uguale=1 titolo="The orthogonal vectors $u$ and $v$, with $\|u\| = 1$ and $\|v\| = \sqrt{3}$"}

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

By bilinearity and since $\langle u, v \rangle = 0$, one has $\langle u, u + v \rangle = \|u\|^2 + \langle u, v \rangle = 1$. By the **Pythagorean theorem** $\|u + v\|^2 = \|u\|^2 + \|v\|^2 = 4$, therefore $\|u + v\| = 2$. Hence
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{\langle u, u + v \rangle}{\|u\|\, \|u + v\|} = \frac{1}{2} ,
$$
and the angle is $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$. The angle $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ is the one between $v$ and $u + v$.
