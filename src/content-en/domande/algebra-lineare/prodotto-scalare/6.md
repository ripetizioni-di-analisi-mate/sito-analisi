Apply Gram–Schmidt to $v_1=(1,1,1,1)$, $v_2=(1,1,0,0)$, $v_3=(1,0,0,0)$ in that order, using positive normalization factors. Which fourth vector completes the result to a positively oriented orthonormal basis of $\mathbb R^4$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(0,0,1,-1)$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(0,0,-1,1)$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(0,0,1,1)$
- $(0,0,1,-1)$

## Explanation

Orthogonality to the first three vectors requires $w_1=w_2=0$ and $w_3=-w_4$. The two possible unit vectors have opposite signs. The matrix with columns $v_1,v_2,v_3,(0,0,1,-1)$ has determinant $2>0$. Subtracting projections and dividing by positive norms preserves that sign, giving the stated unit vector.
