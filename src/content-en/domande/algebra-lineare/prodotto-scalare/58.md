$u=\left(1,0,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,2,3\right)$. What is the area of the parallelogram spanned by the two orthogonal vectors?

- $3$
- $\sqrt{13}$
- $13$
- $5$

## Explanation

The base is the norm of $u$, which is one. Since $v$ is perpendicular, its norm is the height. The result is $\sqrt{13}$.
