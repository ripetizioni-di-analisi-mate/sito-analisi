Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be a symmetric matrix and let $P$ be an orthogonal matrix such that
$$
P^T A P =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & -1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 3
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is $\operatorname{tr}(A^2)$?

- $14$
- $16$
- $36$
- $4$

## Explanation

Let $D$ be the given diagonal matrix. Since $P$ is orthogonal, $P^{-1} = P^T$, hence $A = P D P^T$.

Then $A^2 = P D P^T P D P^T = P D^2 P^T$, that is, $A^2$ is similar to the matrix $D^2 = \operatorname{diag}(4, 1, 9)$.

Similar matrices have the same trace, therefore
$$
\operatorname{tr}(A^2) = \operatorname{tr}(D^2) = 4 + 1 + 9 = 14 .
$$
