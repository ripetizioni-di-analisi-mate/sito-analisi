Let
$$
S_1 =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
0 & -1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
S_2 =
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 1 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
$$
be the reflections of the plane across the $x$-axis and across the line $y = x$. Which transformation does the product $S_2 S_1$ represent, that is, the reflection $S_1$ followed by the reflection $S_2$?

- The rotation by the angle $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- The rotation by the angle $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- The reflection across the line $y = -x$
- The rotation by the angle $\pi$

## Explanation

The product of two orthogonal matrices is orthogonal, and by the multiplicativity of the determinant $\det(S_2 S_1) = (-1)(-1) = 1$, therefore $S_2 S_1$ is a rotation. The row-by-column product gives
$$
S_2 S_1 =
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & -1 \\
1 & 0
\end{pmatrix} ,
$$
which is $R_\theta$ with $\cos\theta = 0$ and $\sin\theta = 1$. Hence $S_2 S_1$ is the rotation by the angle $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The product in the opposite order, $S_1 S_2$, is the rotation by the angle $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
