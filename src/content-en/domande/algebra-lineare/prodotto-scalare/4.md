The vectors $e_1 = \dfrac{1}{3}\,(1, 2, 2)$, $e_2 = \dfrac{1}{3}\,(2, 1, -2)$, $e_3 = \dfrac{1}{3}\,(2, -2, 1)$ form an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$. What are the coordinates of $v = (3, 3, 3)$ with respect to this basis?

- $(15, 3, 3)$
- $(5, 1, 1)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5}{3}, \dfrac{1}{3}, \dfrac{1}{3} \right)$
- $(3, 3, 3)$

## Explanation

With respect to an orthonormal basis the $i$-th coordinate of $v$ is the inner product $\langle v, e_i \rangle$, therefore there is no need to solve a system. One obtains
$$
\langle v, e_1 \rangle = \frac{3 + 6 + 6}{3} = 5,
\qquad
\langle v, e_2 \rangle = \frac{6 + 3 - 6}{3} = 1,
\qquad
\langle v, e_3 \rangle = \frac{6 - 6 + 3}{3} = 1 .
$$
Therefore the coordinates are $(5, 1, 1)$, and indeed $5 e_1 + e_2 + e_3 = \dfrac{1}{3}\,(9, 9, 9) = (3, 3, 3)$.
