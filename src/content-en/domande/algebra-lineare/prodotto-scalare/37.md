Let $W = \langle (1, 0, 2), (0, 1, -1) \rangle \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$. Which Cartesian equation describes $W$?

- $2x + y + z = 0$
- $2x - y - z = 0$
- $x + y + z = 0$
- $x + 2z = 0$

## Explanation

The plane $W$ is the orthogonal complement of the line $W^\perp$, spanned by a vector $(a, b, c)$ orthogonal to both generators:
$$
\begin{cases}
a + 2c = 0 \\[4pt]
b - c = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Setting $c = 1$ one obtains $(a, b, c) = (-2, 1, 1)$, therefore $W$ is the set of vectors orthogonal to $(-2, 1, 1)$. Hence the equation of $W$ is $-2x + y + z = 0$, that is, $2x - y - z = 0$. Both generators satisfy the equation, since $2 - 0 - 2 = 0$ and $0 - 1 + 1 = 0$.
