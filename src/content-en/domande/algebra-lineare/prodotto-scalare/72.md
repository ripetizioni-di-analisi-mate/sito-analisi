$u=\left(3,-2\right)$, $v=\left(-2,-3\right)$. What is the coefficient of $u$ in the orthogonal decomposition of $w=u+2v$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $u\cdot v=0$, dividing $w\cdot u$ by $u\cdot u$ leaves only the contribution from $u$. Substituting $c=4$ gives $1$.
