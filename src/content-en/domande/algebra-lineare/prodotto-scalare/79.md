$u=\left(4,5,1\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,0\right)$. What vector results from subtracting from $u$ its projection onto the line spanned by $v$?

- $\left(4,0,1\right)$
- $\left(4,5,0\right)$
- $\left(0,0,1\right)$
- $\left(0,5,0\right)$

## Explanation

The direction of $v$ is the $y$ axis. Projection retains only the second component, so subtracting it leaves the first and third. The result is $\left(4,0,1\right)$.
