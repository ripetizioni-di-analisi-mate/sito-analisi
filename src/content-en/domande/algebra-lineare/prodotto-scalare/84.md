Let
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 2 \\
2 & 2
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the maximum value of $x^T A x$ as $x \in \mathbb{R}^2$ varies with $\|x\| = 1$?

- $5$
- $7$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 6 = (\lambda - 1)(\lambda - 6)$, therefore the eigenvalues are $6$ and $1$. By the spectral theorem there is an orthogonal $P$ with $P^T A P = \operatorname{diag}(6, 1)$, and with $y = P^T x$ one obtains
$$
x^T A x = 6 y_1^2 + y_2^2 .
$$
Since $P^T$ is orthogonal, $\|y\| = \|x\| = 1$, therefore $x^T A x = 6 - 5 y_2^2 \leq 6$. Equality holds for $y_2 = 0$, that is, for $x = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\,(2, 1)$, an eigenvector of norm $1$ associated with $6$. Hence the maximum is $6$, the largest eigenvalue of $A$.
