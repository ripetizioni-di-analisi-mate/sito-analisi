On $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ consider the inner product $\langle A, B \rangle = \operatorname{tr}(A^T B)$. What is $\langle A, B \rangle$ for $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$?

- $6$
- $0$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The number $\operatorname{tr}(A^T B)$ is the sum of the products of the entries of $A$ and $B$ in the same position. Therefore
$$
\langle A, B \rangle = 1 \cdot 3 + 2 \cdot 1 + 0 \cdot 2 + (-1) \cdot 1 = 4 .
$$
The trace of the product $AB$, without the transpose, equals $6$ and is a different number.
