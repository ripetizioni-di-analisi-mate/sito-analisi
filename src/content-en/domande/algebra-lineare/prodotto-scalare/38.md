For which $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ is the matrix
$$
Q =
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{3}{5} & a \\[4pt]
\frac{4}{5} & b
\end{pmatrix}
$$
orthogonal with $\det Q = -1$?

- $(a, b) = \left( -\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$
- $(a, b) = \left( \dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5} \right)$
- $(a, b) = \left( \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$
- $(a, b) = (4, -3)$

## Explanation

The columns of an orthogonal matrix form an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$. The first column is a unit vector, therefore the second is a unit vector orthogonal to $\left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$, that is, $(a, b) = \left( -\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$ or $(a, b) = \left( \dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5} \right)$. The determinant is $\dfrac{3}{5}\,b - \dfrac{4}{5}\,a$, and it equals $1$ in the first case and $-1$ in the second. Therefore $(a, b) = \left( \dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5} \right)$, and the matrix $Q$ is a reflection of the plane.
