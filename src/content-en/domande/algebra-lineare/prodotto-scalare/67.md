$u=\left(1,3,0\right)$, $v=\left(1,3,-2\right)$. What vector does the first Gram–Schmidt step produce from $v$ before normalization?

- $\left(0,0,-2\right)$
- $\left(0,0,2\right)$
- $\left(1,3,-2\right)$
- $\left(1,3,0\right)$

## Explanation

The difference $v-u$ is in the $z$ direction and is orthogonal to $u$. Thus the component of $v$ along $u$ is exactly $u$. The result is $\left(0,0,-2\right)$.
