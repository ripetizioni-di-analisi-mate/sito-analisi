The area of the parallelogram with sides $v_1$ and $v_2$ is the product of $\|v_1\|$ and the distance of $v_2$ from the line $\langle v_1 \rangle$. What is the area for $v_1 = (1, 1, 1, 1)$ and $v_2 = (1, 2, 3, 4)$ in $\mathbb{R}^4$?

- $2\sqrt{30}$
- $2\sqrt{5}$
- $20$
- $\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The step of the Gram-Schmidt process removes from $v_2$ its projection onto $v_1$. Since $\langle v_2, v_1 \rangle = 10$ and $\langle v_1, v_1 \rangle = 4$, one obtains
$$
u_2 = v_2 - \frac{10}{4}\, v_1 = \left( -\frac{3}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{2} \right) .
$$
The vector $u_2$ is the difference between $v_2$ and its orthogonal projection onto the line $\langle v_1 \rangle$, therefore the distance of $v_2$ from the line is $\|u_2\| = \sqrt{\dfrac{9 + 1 + 1 + 9}{4}} = \sqrt{5}$. Hence the area is $\|v_1\| \cdot \|u_2\| = 2\sqrt{5}$.
