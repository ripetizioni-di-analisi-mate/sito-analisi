Let $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^3$ with $\|u\| = 2$ and $\|v\| = 3$. Which of the following values can $\langle u, v \rangle$ take?

- $7$
- $-7$
- $12$
- $-6$

## Explanation

By the **Cauchy-Schwarz inequality** one has $|\langle u, v \rangle| \leq \|u\|\, \|v\| = 6$, therefore $\langle u, v \rangle$ belongs to the interval $[-6, 6]$. Hence the values $7$, $-7$ and $12$ are excluded. The value $-6$ is obtained, for example, with $u = (2, 0, 0)$ and $v = (-3, 0, 0)$, which are linearly dependent and attain equality.
