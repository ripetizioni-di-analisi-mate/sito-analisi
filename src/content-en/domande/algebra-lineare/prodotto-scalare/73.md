The matrix
$$
Q = \frac{1}{5}
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 4 \\
4 & -3
\end{pmatrix}
$$
is orthogonal. Which transformation of the plane does it represent?

- The rotation by the angle $\theta$ with $\cos\theta = \dfrac{3}{5}$
- The reflection across the line $y = 2x$
- The reflection across the line $y = -2x$
- The reflection across the line $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$

## Explanation

One has $\det Q = \dfrac{-9 - 16}{25} = -1$, therefore $Q$ is not a rotation and is the reflection across a line through the origin. The vectors of the line are those fixed by $Q$, that is, the eigenvectors associated with the eigenvalue $1$. The system $(Q - I)x = 0$ reduces to the equation $-\dfrac{2}{5}\,x + \dfrac{4}{5}\,y = 0$, that is, $x = 2y$. Hence the axis of the reflection is the line $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$, and indeed $Q(2, 1) = (2, 1)$. The line $y = -2x$ consists of the eigenvectors of the eigenvalue $-1$, which the reflection sends to their opposites.
