Let $W = \langle (1, 2, 0), (0, 1, 1) \rangle \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$. What is the orthogonal projection of $v = (2, -1, 1)$ onto $W$?

- $(2, -1, 1)$
- $(0, 0, 0)$
- $(1, 3, 1)$
- $(1, 2, 0)$

## Explanation

One has $\langle v, (1, 2, 0) \rangle = 2 - 2 + 0 = 0$ and $\langle v, (0, 1, 1) \rangle = 0 - 1 + 1 = 0$.

Since $v$ is orthogonal to both spanning vectors, $v$ is orthogonal to every vector of $W$, that is, $v \in W^\perp$.

Then the decomposition $v = 0 + v$ has the first summand in $W$ and the second orthogonal to $W$. By the uniqueness of the orthogonal projection, therefore, $P_W(v) = (0, 0, 0)$.
