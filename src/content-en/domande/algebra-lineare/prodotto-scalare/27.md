What is the orthogonal projection of $v = (3, 1, 0, 2)$ onto $u = (1, 1, 1, 1)$?

- $3\,(1, 1, 1, 1)$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,(1, 1, 1, 1)$
- $6\,(1, 1, 1, 1)$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}\,(3, 1, 0, 2)$

## Explanation

One has $\langle v, u \rangle = 3 + 1 + 0 + 2 = 6$ and $\langle u, u \rangle = 4$, therefore
$$
\operatorname{pr}_u(v) = \frac{6}{4}\,(1, 1, 1, 1) = \frac{3}{2}\,(1, 1, 1, 1) .
$$
The coefficient $3$ would be obtained by dividing by $\|u\| = 2$ instead of by $\langle u, u \rangle = 4$. The vector $\dfrac{3}{7}\,(3, 1, 0, 2)$ is the projection of $u$ onto $v$.
