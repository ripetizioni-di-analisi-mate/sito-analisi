Which of the following symmetric matrices $A$ satisfies $x^T A x > 0$ for every nonzero $x \in \mathbb{R}^2$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 1 \\ 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

For a symmetric matrix the condition $x^T A x > 0$ for every $x \neq 0$ holds if and only if all the eigenvalues are positive. The eigenvalues of $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ are $3$ and $1$, therefore this matrix satisfies the condition. The eigenvalues are instead $3$ and $-1$ for $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $2$ and $0$ for $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $-1$ and $-3$ for $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 1 \\ 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$. For example, with $x = (1, -1)$ the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ gives $x^T A x = -2$.
