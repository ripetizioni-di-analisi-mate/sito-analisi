Let $u, v, w \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $d(u, v) = 3$ and $d(v, w) = 5$. Which of the following values can the distance $d(u, w)$ take?

- $1$
- $9$
- $4$
- $15$

## Explanation

By the **triangle inequality** one has $d(u, w) \leq d(u, v) + d(v, w) = 8$.

The same inequality, applied to the sides of the triangle in the other order, gives $d(v, w) \leq d(v, u) + d(u, w)$, hence $d(u, w) \geq 5 - 3 = 2$.

Therefore $d(u, w)$ lies in the interval $[2, 8]$, and the values $1$, $9$ and $15$ are excluded.

The value $4$ is attained, for example, in $\mathbb{R}^2$ with $u = (0, 0)$, $v = (3, 0)$ and $w = (0, 4)$, since $d(v, w) = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.
