What is the angle between the vectors $u = (1, 0, 1)$ and $v = (0, 1, -1)$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$

## Explanation

One has $\langle u, v \rangle = 0 + 0 - 1 = -1$ and $\|u\| = \|v\| = \sqrt{2}$, therefore
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{-1}{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{2}} = -\frac{1}{2} .
$$
The angle $\theta$ belongs to $[0, \pi]$ and has negative cosine, therefore it is obtuse and equals $\theta = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.
