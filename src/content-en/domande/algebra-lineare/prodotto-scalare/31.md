The symmetric matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$ transforms the unit circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ into an ellipse. What are the lengths of the semi-axes of the ellipse?

- $2$ and $3$
- $1$ and $2$
- $\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial of $A$ is $\lambda^2 + \lambda - 6 = (\lambda - 2)(\lambda + 3)$, therefore the eigenvalues are $2$ and $-3$, with orthogonal eigenvectors $(2, 1)$ and $(1, -2)$. No eigenvalue is zero, therefore $A$ is invertible. The matrix acts as a scaling by the factor $2$ along $(2, 1)$ and by the factor $-3$ along $(1, -2)$. Therefore the image of the unit circle is an ellipse whose axes lie along these eigenvectors and whose semi-axes are equal to the absolute values of the eigenvalues, that is, $2$ and $3$.
