$u=\left(1,3,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,-2\right)$. What is the third component of a vector in $\operatorname{span}(u,v)^\perp$ whose second component is $b$, namely the third component of $v$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The dot product with $v$ is $b+bz$. Since $b$ is nonzero, orthogonality forces $z=-1$. Substituting $b=-2$ gives $-1$.
