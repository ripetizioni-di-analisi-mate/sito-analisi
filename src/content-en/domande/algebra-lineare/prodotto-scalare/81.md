A symmetric matrix $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ has a single eigenvalue, $\lambda = 3$, with algebraic multiplicity $2$. Which statement is true?

- $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $A$ has trace $3$
- $A$ is not diagonalizable
- $A = 3I_2$

## Explanation

By the **spectral theorem** there is an orthogonal matrix $P$ with $P^T A P = D$ diagonal, therefore $A$ is diagonalizable. The eigenvalues appear on the diagonal of $D$, hence $D = 3I_2$. Since $P^T = P^{-1}$, one obtains
$$
A = P D P^T = 3\, P P^T = 3I_2 .
$$
The trace of $A$ is $6$. The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ has eigenvalues $4$ and $2$.
