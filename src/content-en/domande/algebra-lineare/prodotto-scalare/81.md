$u=\left(4,0\right)$, $v=\left(5,-3\right)$. What is the cosine of the angle between $u$ and $v$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{5 \sqrt{34}}{34}$
- $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{34}}{34}$
- $- \dfrac{3 \sqrt{34}}{34}$

## Explanation

The norm of $u$ is its positive $x$ component. In the ratio of the dot product to the product of the norms, that factor cancels. The result is $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{34}}{34}$.
