The Gram-Schmidt process is applied to the vectors $v_1 = (1, 0, 1)$, $v_2 = (0, 1, 1)$, $v_3 = (1, 1, 2)$, in the given order. Which vector $u_3$ is obtained at the third step?

- $u_3 = (1, 1, -1)$
- $u_3 = (1, 1, 2)$
- $u_3 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\,(1, 1, 2)$
- $u_3 = (0, 0, 0)$

## Explanation

The vector $u_3$ is the difference between $v_3$ and its orthogonal projection onto the subspace $\langle v_1, v_2 \rangle$. Since $v_3 = v_1 + v_2$, the vector $v_3$ belongs to this subspace and coincides with its own projection. Therefore $u_3 = (0, 0, 0)$, and the process thus detects that the three vectors are **linearly dependent**. The vector $(1, 1, -1)$ is orthogonal to $v_1$ and to $v_2$, but it does not belong to $\langle v_1, v_2, v_3 \rangle$.
