Let $P$ be the $3 \times 3$ matrix of the orthogonal projection of $\mathbb{R}^3$ onto a plane $W$ through the origin. What are the eigenvalues of $P$?

- Only $1$, with algebraic multiplicity $3$
- $1$ with algebraic multiplicity $1$ and $0$ with algebraic multiplicity $2$
- $1$ with algebraic multiplicity $2$ and $0$ with algebraic multiplicity $1$
- $1$ with algebraic multiplicity $2$ and $-1$ with algebraic multiplicity $1$

## Explanation

For every $w \in W$ the projection of $w$ onto $W$ is $w$ itself, therefore $Pw = w$ and the plane $W$ is contained in the eigenspace $V_1$. For every $n \in W^\perp$ the projection onto $W$ is zero, therefore $Pn = 0$ and the line $W^\perp$ is contained in $V_0$.

Since $\dim W + \dim W^\perp = 3$ and the algebraic multiplicities add up to $3$, one has $V_1 = W$ and $V_0 = W^\perp$. Hence the eigenvalue $1$ has multiplicity $2$ and the eigenvalue $0$ has multiplicity $1$. The eigenvalue $-1$ on the line $W^\perp$ belongs instead to the orthogonal reflection across $W$.
