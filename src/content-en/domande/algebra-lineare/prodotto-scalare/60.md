$u=\left(2,1\right)$, $v=\left(1,3\right)$. What is the determinant of the Gram matrix of $(u,v)$?

- $49$
- $5$
- $13$
- $25$

## Explanation

If $A$ has columns $u$ and $v$, its Gram matrix is $A^{\mathsf T}A$. Its determinant is therefore the square of $\det A$. Substituting $a=2$ gives $25$.
