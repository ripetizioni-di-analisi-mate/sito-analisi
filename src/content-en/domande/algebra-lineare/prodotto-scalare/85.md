Let
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
1 & 2
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the entry in position $(1, 2)$ of $A^5$?

- $32$
- $243$
- $5$
- $121$

## Explanation

The eigenvalues of $A$ are $3$, with eigenvector $(1, 1)$, and $1$, with eigenvector $(1, -1)$. With the orthogonal matrix $P = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ one has $A = P D P^T$ with $D = \operatorname{diag}(3, 1)$. Since $P^T P = I_2$, the intermediate factors cancel and $A^5 = P D^5 P^T$. The computation gives
$$
A^5 = \frac{1}{2}
\begin{pmatrix}
3^5 + 1 & 3^5 - 1 \\
3^5 - 1 & 3^5 + 1
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
122 & 121 \\
121 & 122
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
Hence the entry in position $(1, 2)$ equals $121$.
