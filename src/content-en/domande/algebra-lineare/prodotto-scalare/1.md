## Explanation

The inner product is $\langle u, v \rangle = 1 + 0 + 1 + 0 = 2$, and the norms are $\|u\| = \sqrt{4} = 2$ and $\|v\| = \sqrt{2}$. By the definition of angle one therefore has
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{2}{2\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} .
$$
The angle $\theta$ belongs to $[0, \pi]$, therefore $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
