What is the component of the vector $v = (3, 4, -1)$ orthogonal to the vector $u = (1, 2, -1)$?

- $(2, 4, -2)$
- $(5, 8, -3)$
- $(1, 0, -1)$
- $(1, 0, 1)$

## Explanation

One has $\langle v, u \rangle = 3 + 8 + 1 = 12$ and $\langle u, u \rangle = 1 + 4 + 1 = 6$, therefore the orthogonal projection of $v$ onto $u$ is
$$
\operatorname{pr}_u(v) = \frac{12}{6}\,(1, 2, -1) = (2, 4, -2) .
$$
The component of $v$ orthogonal to $u$ is the difference $v - \operatorname{pr}_u(v) = (1, 0, 1)$. The inner product $\langle (1, 0, 1), u \rangle = 1 + 0 - 1 = 0$ confirms that this difference is orthogonal to $u$.
