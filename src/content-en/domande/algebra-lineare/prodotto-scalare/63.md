Let $W$ be a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ and let $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $\|v\| = 5$ and distance from $W$ equal to $3$. What is the norm of the orthogonal projection $P_W(v)$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{34}$
- $8$

## Explanation

One writes $v = P_W(v) + \left( v - P_W(v) \right)$. The first summand belongs to $W$ and the second is orthogonal to $W$, therefore the two summands are orthogonal. By the **Pythagorean theorem**
$$
\|v\|^2 = \|P_W(v)\|^2 + \|v - P_W(v)\|^2 ,
$$
and $\|v - P_W(v)\|$ is the distance of $v$ from $W$. Hence $\|P_W(v)\|^2 = 25 - 9 = 16$, that is, $\|P_W(v)\| = 4$.
