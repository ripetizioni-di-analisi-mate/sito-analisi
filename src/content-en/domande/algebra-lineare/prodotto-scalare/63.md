$u=\left(3,2\right)$, $v=\left(1,1\right)$. What is the coefficient of $v$ in the orthogonal projection of $u$ onto $\operatorname{span}(v)$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The residual $u-tv$ is orthogonal to $v$. Setting its dot product to zero gives $t=(u\cdot v)/(v\cdot v)$. Substituting $c=4$ gives $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
