What is the distance between the vectors $u = (1, 2, 3, 4)$ and $v = (2, 0, 3, 2)$ in $\mathbb{R}^4$?

- $9$
- $\sqrt{30} - \sqrt{17}$
- $3$
- $\sqrt{47}$

## Explanation

The distance is the norm of the difference, $d(u, v) = \|u - v\|$. One has $u - v = (-1, 2, 0, 2)$, therefore
$$
d(u, v) = \sqrt{1 + 4 + 0 + 4} = \sqrt{9} = 3 .
$$
The difference of the norms $\|u\| - \|v\| = \sqrt{30} - \sqrt{17}$ is a different number, smaller than the distance by the triangle inequality.
