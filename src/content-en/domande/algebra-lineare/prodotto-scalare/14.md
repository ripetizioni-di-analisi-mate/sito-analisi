What is the unit vector in the direction of the vector $v = (2, -1, 2, 4)$ in $\mathbb{R}^4$?

- $\dfrac{1}{25}\,(2, -1, 2, 4)$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}\,(2, -1, 2, 4)$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}\,(2, -1, 2, 4)$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,(2, -1, 2, 4)$

## Explanation

The norm of $v$ is $\|v\| = \sqrt{4 + 1 + 4 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$. The unit vector parallel to and pointing in the same direction as $v$ is obtained by dividing $v$ by its norm, therefore it is $\dfrac{1}{5}\,(2, -1, 2, 4)$. Dividing by $\|v\|^2 = 25$ one would obtain a vector of norm $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
