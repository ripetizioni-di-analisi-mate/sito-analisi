$u=\left(2,1\right)$, $v=\left(1,0\right)$, $w=\left(3,1\right)$. What is the dot product of the orthogonal projections of $u$ and $w$ onto the line spanned by $v$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $7$
- $0$

## Explanation

The projections preserve only the first components. Their dot product is the product of those components. The result is $6$.
