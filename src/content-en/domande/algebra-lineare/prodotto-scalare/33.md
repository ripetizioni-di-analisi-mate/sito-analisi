The Gram-Schmidt process is applied to the vectors $v_1 = (1, 1, 0, 0)$ and $v_2 = (1, 0, 1, 1)$ of $\mathbb{R}^4$, in the given order. What is the second vector $e_2$ of the resulting orthonormal basis?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\,(0, -1, 1, 1)$
- $\dfrac{1}{3\sqrt{2}}\,(3, 1, 2, 2)$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{10}}\,(1, -1, 2, 2)$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\,(1, 0, 1, 1)$

## Explanation

Since $\langle v_2, v_1 \rangle = 1$ and $\langle v_1, v_1 \rangle = 2$, the second step gives
$$
u_2 = (1, 0, 1, 1) - \frac{1}{2}\,(1, 1, 0, 0) = \left( \frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}, 1, 1 \right) .
$$
The multiple $(1, -1, 2, 2)$ has the same direction and the same orientation as $u_2$ and has norm $\sqrt{1 + 1 + 4 + 4} = \sqrt{10}$. Therefore $e_2 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{10}}\,(1, -1, 2, 2)$.
