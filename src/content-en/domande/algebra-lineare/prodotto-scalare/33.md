The line $y=mx+b$ is fitted to the points $(0,1)$, $(1,2)$, and $(2,5)$ by least squares. What is the intercept $b$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac83$
- $\dfrac23$
- $2$

## Explanation

The normal equations make the residual orthogonal to $(1,1,1)$ and $(0,1,2)$. They give $3m+3b=8$ and $5m+3b=12$. Subtraction gives $m=2$, hence $b=\dfrac23$.
