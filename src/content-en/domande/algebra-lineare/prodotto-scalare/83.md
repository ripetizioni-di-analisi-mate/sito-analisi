$u=\left(1,4,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,5\right)$. What is the third component of a vector in $\operatorname{span}(u,v)^\perp$ whose second component is $b$, namely the third component of $v$?

- $-1$
- $0$
- $1$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The dot product with $v$ is $b+bz$. Since $b$ is nonzero, orthogonality forces $z=-1$. Substituting $b=5$ gives $-1$.
