$u=\left(3,0\right)$, $v=\left(-2,4\right)$. What is the cosine of the angle between $u$ and $v$?

- $\dfrac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$

## Explanation

The norm of $u$ is its positive $x$ component. In the dot product divided by both norms, that factor cancels. The result is $- \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$.
