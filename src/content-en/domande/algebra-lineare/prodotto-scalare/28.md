What is the distance of the vector $v = (1, 2, 2)$ from the line $W = \langle (0, 1, 1) \rangle$ of $\mathbb{R}^3$?

- $1$
- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $3$
- $\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The distance of $v$ from $W$ is the norm of $v - \operatorname{pr}_u(v)$, with $u = (0, 1, 1)$. Since $\langle v, u \rangle = 4$ and $\langle u, u \rangle = 2$, the projection is $\operatorname{pr}_u(v) = 2\,(0, 1, 1) = (0, 2, 2)$. Therefore $v - \operatorname{pr}_u(v) = (1, 0, 0)$ and $d(v, W) = 1$. The number $2\sqrt{2}$ is the norm of the projection $(0, 2, 2)$.
