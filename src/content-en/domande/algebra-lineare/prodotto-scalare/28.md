Which of the following polynomials can be the characteristic polynomial of a symmetric matrix $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$?

- $\lambda^2 - \lambda - 6$
- $\lambda^2 + 1$
- $\lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 5$
- $\lambda^2 + \lambda + 1$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **eigenvalues of a symmetric matrix**, all the roots of the characteristic polynomial of $A$ are real.

The polynomials $\lambda^2 + 1$, $\lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 5$ and $\lambda^2 + \lambda + 1$ have discriminant $-4$, $-16$ and $-3$, so they have no real roots and are excluded.

The polynomial $\lambda^2 - \lambda - 6 = (\lambda - 3)(\lambda + 2)$ has the real roots $3$ and $-2$, and it is the characteristic polynomial of the symmetric matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 0 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$.
