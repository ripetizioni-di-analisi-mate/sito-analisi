Let $Q, R \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ be any two orthogonal matrices. Which of the following matrices is orthogonal for every choice of $Q$ and $R$?

- $Q + R$
- $2Q$
- $Q R^T$
- $Q - R$

## Explanation

The matrix $R^T = R^{-1}$ is orthogonal, because the inverse of an orthogonal matrix is orthogonal. The product of two orthogonal matrices is orthogonal, hence $Q R^T$ is orthogonal. A direct computation confirms it:
$$
(Q R^T)^T (Q R^T) = R\, Q^T Q\, R^T = R R^T = I_n .
$$
The other matrices are not orthogonal in general. For $Q = R = I_n$ one gets $Q + R = 2I_n$ and $Q - R = 0$, and $(2Q)^T (2Q) = 4 I_n$ for every orthogonal $Q$.
