$u=\left(4,1\right)$, $v=\left(1,5\right)$. What is the determinant of the Gram matrix of $(u,v)$?

- $19$
- $361$
- $41$
- $441$

## Explanation

If $A$ has columns $u$ and $v$, its Gram matrix is $A^{\mathsf T}A$. Its determinant is therefore the square of $\det A$. Substituting $a=4$ gives $361$.
