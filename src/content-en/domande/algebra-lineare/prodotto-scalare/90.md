Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be a symmetric matrix with $\ker A = \langle (1, 0, 1), (0, 1, 0) \rangle$. Which subspace is $\operatorname{Im} A$?

- The line $\langle (1, 0, -1) \rangle$
- The plane $x + z = 0$
- The line $\langle (1, 0, 1) \rangle$
- The plane $x - z = 0$

## Explanation

Let $w \in \ker A$ and $x \in \mathbb{R}^3$. Since $A$ is symmetric, $\langle Ax, w \rangle = \langle x, Aw \rangle = \langle x, 0 \rangle = 0$, therefore $\operatorname{Im} A \subseteq (\ker A)^\perp$. By the rank-nullity theorem $\dim \operatorname{Im} A = 3 - 2 = 1$, which is also the dimension of $(\ker A)^\perp$, hence $\operatorname{Im} A = (\ker A)^\perp$.

The complement is obtained from the conditions $x + z = 0$ and $y = 0$, therefore $\operatorname{Im} A = \langle (1, 0, -1) \rangle$. The plane $x - z = 0$ coincides with $\ker A$, and the plane $x + z = 0$ has dimension $2$, too large for the image.
