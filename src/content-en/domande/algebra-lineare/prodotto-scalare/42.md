For which of the following matrices $A$ does there exist an orthogonal matrix $P$ such that $P^T A P$ is diagonal?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

By the **real spectral theorem** there exists an orthogonal matrix that diagonalizes $A$ if and only if $A$ is symmetric. Among the four matrices only $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is symmetric, and its eigenvectors $(1, 1)$ and $(1, -1)$ are orthogonal. The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ has two distinct real eigenvalues, therefore it is diagonalizable, but the diagonalizing matrix cannot be chosen orthogonal, because $A$ is not symmetric. The other two matrices are not diagonalizable over the reals.
