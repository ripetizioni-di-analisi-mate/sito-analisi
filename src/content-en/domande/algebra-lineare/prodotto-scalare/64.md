The Gram-Schmidt process is applied to the vectors $v_1 = (2, 1, 2)$ and $v_2 = (4, -1, 1)$, in the given order, and the vectors obtained are normalized, giving the unit vectors $e_1$ and $e_2$. Then $v_1 = r_{11}\, e_1$ and $v_2 = r_{12}\, e_1 + r_{22}\, e_2$, with $r_{11} > 0$ and $r_{22} > 0$. What are $r_{11}$, $r_{12}$ and $r_{22}$?

- $r_{11} = 3$, $r_{12} = 9$, $r_{22} = 3$
- $r_{11} = 3$, $r_{12} = 3$, $r_{22} = 3\sqrt{2}$
- $r_{11} = 3$, $r_{12} = 3$, $r_{22} = 3$
- $r_{11} = 9$, $r_{12} = 9$, $r_{22} = 9$

## Explanation

The first unit vector is $e_1 = \dfrac{v_1}{\|v_1\|}$ with $\|v_1\| = \sqrt{4 + 1 + 4} = 3$, therefore $r_{11} = 3$.

Since $e_1$ and $e_2$ are orthonormal, taking the inner product of the second equality with $e_1$ gives $r_{12} = \langle v_2, e_1 \rangle = \dfrac{8 - 1 + 2}{3} = 3$. The vector $r_{12}\, e_1 = (2, 1, 2)$ is the projection of $v_2$ onto $v_1$, hence
$$
r_{22}\, e_2 = v_2 - r_{12}\, e_1 = (4, -1, 1) - (2, 1, 2) = (2, -2, -1) ,
$$
which is the vector $u_2$ of the process. Therefore $r_{22} = \|u_2\| = \sqrt{4 + 4 + 1} = 3$. The value $9$ is $\langle v_2, v_1 \rangle$, and $3\sqrt{2}$ is the norm of $v_2$.
