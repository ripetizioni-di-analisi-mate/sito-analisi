- $k = -1$ or $k = 5$
- $k = 0$ or $k = -4$
- $k = 1$ or $k = -5$
- $k = \sqrt{5}$ or $k = -\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

Two vectors are orthogonal if and only if their inner product is zero. One has $\langle u, v \rangle = k^2 - 5 + 4k$, therefore the condition is $k^2 + 4k - 5 = 0$. Since $k^2 + 4k - 5 = (k - 1)(k + 5)$, the vectors are **orthogonal** for $k = 1$ or $k = -5$.
