On $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ consider the inner product $\langle A, B \rangle = \operatorname{tr}(A^T B)$, equal to the sum of the products of the entries in the same position. The Gram-Schmidt process is applied to the matrices
$$
V_1 = I_2,
\qquad
V_2 =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
0 & 0
\end{pmatrix} ,
$$
in the given order. Which matrix $U_2$ is obtained at the second step, before normalization?

- $\begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{2} & 1 \\[4pt] 0 & -\frac{1}{2} \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} \frac{3}{2} & 1 \\[4pt] 0 & \frac{1}{2} \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The second step of the Gram-Schmidt process gives
$$
U_2 = V_2 - \frac{\langle V_2, V_1 \rangle}{\langle V_1, V_1 \rangle}\, V_1 .
$$
Adding the products of the entries in the same position one gets $\langle V_2, I_2 \rangle = 1 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot 0 + 0 \cdot 0 + 0 \cdot 1 = 1$ and $\langle I_2, I_2 \rangle = 2$.

Hence
$$
U_2 =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
0 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
- \frac{1}{2}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{1}{2} & 1 \\[4pt]
0 & -\frac{1}{2}
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
The matrix $U_2$ has zero trace, therefore $\langle U_2, I_2 \rangle = \operatorname{tr} U_2 = 0$ and $U_2$ is orthogonal to $V_1$.
