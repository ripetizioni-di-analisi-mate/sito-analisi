A set of nonzero vectors of $\mathbb{R}^4$, pairwise orthogonal, consists of vectors that are all orthogonal to $(1, 1, 1, 1)$. At most how many vectors can it contain?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

The vectors belong to the orthogonal complement $W^\perp$ of the line $W = \langle (1, 1, 1, 1) \rangle$, which has dimension $4 - 1 = 3$. By the theorem on the **independence of orthogonal vectors**, nonzero pairwise orthogonal vectors are linearly independent. Therefore $W^\perp$ cannot contain more than $3$ of them.

The value $3$ is attained, for instance with $(1, -1, 0, 0)$, $(0, 0, 1, -1)$ and $(1, 1, -1, -1)$, which are orthogonal to $(1, 1, 1, 1)$ and pairwise orthogonal to each other. Hence the maximum number is $3$.
