On $\mathbb{R}^2$ consider the inner product
$$
\langle u, v \rangle = 2u_1 v_1 + u_1 v_2 + u_2 v_1 + u_2 v_2 .
$$
What is the norm of $v = (1, 2)$ with respect to this inner product?

- $\sqrt{5}$
- $10$
- $\sqrt{8}$
- $\sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

The norm is $\|v\| = \sqrt{\langle v, v \rangle}$, with the given inner product in place of the standard one. Setting $u = v = (1, 2)$ in the formula one obtains
$$
\langle v, v \rangle = 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot 1 + 2 \cdot 2 = 10 .
$$
Therefore $\|v\| = \sqrt{10}$. With the standard inner product the norm of the same vector would be $\sqrt{5}$, so the norm depends on the chosen inner product.
