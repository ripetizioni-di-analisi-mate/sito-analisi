Let
$$
W = \left\{ x \in \mathbb{R}^5 :
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 - x_5 = 0 \\[4pt]
x_3 - 2x_4 = 0
\end{cases}
\right\} .
$$
What is $\dim W^\perp$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $5$

## Explanation

The two rows of the coefficient matrix, $(1, 1, 0, 0, -1)$ and $(0, 0, 1, -2, 0)$, are linearly independent, therefore the matrix has rank $2$. Hence $\dim W = 5 - 2 = 3$. By the theorem on the dimension of the orthogonal complement one has $\dim W^\perp = 5 - 3 = 2$, and indeed $W^\perp$ is spanned by the two rows.
