What is the distance of the vector $v = (1, 2, 3, 4)$ from the subspace
$$
W = \{ x \in \mathbb{R}^4 : x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 = 0 \} ?
$$

- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\sqrt{5}$
- $5$

## Explanation

The orthogonal complement of $W$ is spanned by the row of coefficients, that is, $W^\perp = \langle n \rangle$ with $n = (1, 1, 1, 1)$. Since $P_W(v) = v - \operatorname{pr}_n(v)$, the distance is
$$
d(v, W) = \|v - P_W(v)\| = \|\operatorname{pr}_n(v)\| = \frac{|\langle v, n \rangle|}{\|n\|} .
$$
One has $\langle v, n \rangle = 10$ and $\|n\| = 2$, therefore $d(v, W) = 5$.
