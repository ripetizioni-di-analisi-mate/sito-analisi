$u=\left(4,1\right)$, $v=\left(1,0\right)$, $w=\left(5,-3\right)$. What is the dot product of the orthogonal projections of $u$ and $w$ onto the line through $v$?

- $0$
- $5$
- $20$
- $17$

## Explanation

The projections preserve only the first components. Their dot product is the product of those components. The result is $20$.
