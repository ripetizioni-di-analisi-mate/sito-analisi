On $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ consider the inner product $\langle A, B \rangle = \operatorname{tr}(A^T B)$, equal to the sum of the products of the entries in the same position. Let $W$ be the subspace of diagonal matrices. What is the orthogonal complement $W^\perp$?

- The skew-symmetric matrices
- The matrices with zero diagonal
- The matrices with zero trace
- The symmetric matrices with zero trace

## Explanation

The subspace $W$ is spanned by $E_{11} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and $E_{22} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. For $B = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ one has $\langle E_{11}, B \rangle = a$ and $\langle E_{22}, B \rangle = d$. Therefore $B \in W^\perp$ if and only if $a = d = 0$, that is, if $B$ has zero diagonal, and indeed $\dim W^\perp = 4 - 2 = 2$.

The skew-symmetric matrices $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & b \\ -b & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ lie in $W^\perp$, but they form a subspace of dimension $1$. The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ is symmetric and has zero trace, but its inner product with $E_{11}$ equals $1$.
