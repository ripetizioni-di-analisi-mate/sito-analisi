Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be a symmetric matrix with eigenvalues $2$ and $5$, and let $V_2 = \langle (1, -1, 2) \rangle$. Which of the following vectors is an eigenvector of $A$ associated with the eigenvalue $5$?

- $(2, -2, 4)$
- $(1, 0, 1)$
- $(1, 1, 0)$
- $(0, 1, 1)$

## Explanation

By the spectral theorem $\mathbb{R}^3 = V_2 \oplus V_5$, and eigenspaces associated with distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal. Since there are two distinct eigenvalues, one has $V_5 = V_2^\perp$, that is, $V_5$ is the plane $x - y + 2z = 0$. The vector $(1, 1, 0)$ satisfies $1 - 1 + 0 = 0$, therefore it is an **eigenvector** associated with $5$. The vector $(2, -2, 4)$ belongs to $V_2$, while $(1, 0, 1)$ and $(0, 1, 1)$ are not orthogonal to $(1, -1, 2)$.
