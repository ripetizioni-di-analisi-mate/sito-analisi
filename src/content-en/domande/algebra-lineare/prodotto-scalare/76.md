Which of the following numbers can be a real eigenvalue of an orthogonal matrix?

- $2$
- $0$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Let $Q$ be orthogonal and let $Qv = \lambda v$ with $v \neq 0$. Since $Q$ preserves norms, $\|Qv\| = \|v\|$; on the other hand $\|Qv\| = \|\lambda v\| = |\lambda|\, \|v\|$. Therefore $|\lambda| = 1$, and the only possible real eigenvalues are $1$ and $-1$.

The value $-1$ actually occurs: the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ is orthogonal and has the eigenvalue $-1$ with eigenvector $(0, 1)$.
