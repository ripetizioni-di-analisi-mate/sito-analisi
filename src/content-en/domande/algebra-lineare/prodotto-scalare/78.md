$u=\left(4,2\right)$, $v=\left(1,1\right)$. What is the coefficient of $v$ in the orthogonal projection of $u$ onto $\operatorname{span}(v)$?

- $3 \sqrt{2}$
- $3$
- $1$
- $6$

## Explanation

The residual $u-tv$ is orthogonal to $v$. Setting its dot product to zero gives $t=(u\cdot v)/(v\cdot v)$. Substituting $c=-3$ gives $3$.
