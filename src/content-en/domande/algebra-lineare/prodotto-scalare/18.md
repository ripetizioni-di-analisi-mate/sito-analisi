For which $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the angle between the vectors $u = (1, k, 2)$ and $v = (3, 1, -1)$ obtuse?

- $k < -1$
- $k > -1$
- $k > 1$
- $k < 1$

## Explanation

For nonzero vectors the angle is obtuse if and only if the scalar product is negative. One has $\langle u, v \rangle = 3 + k - 2 = k + 1$, therefore the condition is $k + 1 < 0$. The vector $u$ is nonzero for every $k$, because its first component equals $1$. Therefore the angle is **obtuse** for $k < -1$.
