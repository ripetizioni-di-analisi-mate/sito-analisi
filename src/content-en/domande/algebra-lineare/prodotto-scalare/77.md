Let $Q \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be an orthogonal matrix and let $u = (1, 2, -1)$ and $v = (3, 0, 1)$. What is $\langle Qu, Qv \rangle$?

- $-2$
- $4$
- It depends on the matrix $Q$
- $2$

## Explanation

Writing the vectors as columns one has
$$
\langle Qu, Qv \rangle = (Qu)^T (Qv) = u^T Q^T Q\, v = u^T v ,
$$
because $Q^T Q = I_3$ by the definition of an orthogonal matrix. Therefore $\langle Qu, Qv \rangle = \langle u, v \rangle$ for every orthogonal matrix $Q$. The computation gives $\langle u, v \rangle = 3 + 0 - 1 = 2$.
