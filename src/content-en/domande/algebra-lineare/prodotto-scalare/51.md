On the space $\mathbb{R}_1[x]$ of polynomials of degree at most $1$ consider the inner product
$$
\langle p, q \rangle = \int_{0}^{1} p(x)\, q(x)\, dx .
$$
What is the angle between the polynomials $p(x) = 1$ and $q(x) = x$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

One computes the three inner products that are needed:
$$
\langle 1, x \rangle = \int_0^1 x \, dx = \frac{1}{2},
\qquad
\langle 1, 1 \rangle = \int_0^1 dx = 1,
\qquad
\langle x, x \rangle = \int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
Therefore $\|1\| = 1$ and $\|x\| = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$, and the cosine of the angle is
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{\langle 1, x \rangle}{\|1\|\, \|x\|} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \sqrt{3} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} .
$$
Hence $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
