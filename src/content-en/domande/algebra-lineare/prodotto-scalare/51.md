$u=\left(2,0\right)$, $v=\left(3,1\right)$. What is the cosine of the angle between $u$ and $v$?

- $\dfrac{3 \sqrt{10}}{10}$
- $- \dfrac{3 \sqrt{10}}{10}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The norm of $u$ is its positive $x$ component. In the ratio of the dot product to the product of the norms, that factor cancels. The result is $\dfrac{3 \sqrt{10}}{10}$.
