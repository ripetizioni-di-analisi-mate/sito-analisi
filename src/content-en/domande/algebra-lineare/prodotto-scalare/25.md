For which $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ are the vectors $u = (1, a, b)$, $v = (2, 1, -1)$, $w = (0, 1, 1)$ pairwise orthogonal?

- $(a, b) = (1, -1)$
- $(a, b) = (-1, 1)$
- $(a, b) = (-2, 0)$
- $(a, b) = (1, 1)$

## Explanation

The vectors $v$ and $w$ are already orthogonal, because $\langle v, w \rangle = 0 + 1 - 1 = 0$. There remain the conditions $\langle u, v \rangle = 0$ and $\langle u, w \rangle = 0$, to be satisfied simultaneously:
$$
\begin{cases}
2 + a - b = 0 \\[4pt]
a + b = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations gives $2 + 2a = 0$, therefore $a = -1$ and $b = 1$.
