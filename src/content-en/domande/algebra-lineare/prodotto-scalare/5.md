The Gram-Schmidt process is applied to the vectors $v_1 = (1, 0, 1)$ and $v_2 = (1, 2, 3)$, in the given order. What is the second vector $e_2$ of the resulting orthonormal basis of $\langle v_1, v_2 \rangle$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{14}}\,(-3, 2, -1)$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{38}}\,(3, 2, 5)$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(-1, 2, 1)$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\,(-1, 2, 1)$

## Explanation

The first step is $u_1 = v_1$. Since $\langle v_2, u_1 \rangle = 4$ and $\langle u_1, u_1 \rangle = 2$, the second vector is
$$
u_2 = (1, 2, 3) - \frac{4}{2}\,(1, 0, 1) = (-1, 2, 1) .
$$
One has $\|u_2\| = \sqrt{1 + 4 + 1} = \sqrt{6}$, therefore normalization gives $e_2 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\,(-1, 2, 1)$.
