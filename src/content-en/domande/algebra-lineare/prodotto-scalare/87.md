The quadratic form $q(x) = x_1^2 + 4x_1 x_2 + x_2^2$ can be written as $q(x) = x^T A x$ with $A$ symmetric. With the change of variables $y = P^T x$, where $P$ is an orthogonal matrix that diagonalizes $A$, which expression is obtained, up to the order of the variables?

- $5y_1^2 - 3y_2^2$
- $3y_1^2 - y_2^2$
- $3y_1^2 + y_2^2$
- $y_1^2 - 3y_2^2$

## Explanation

The mixed term $4x_1 x_2$ is split equally between the two off-diagonal positions, therefore
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
2 & 1
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
The characteristic polynomial is $(1 - \lambda)^2 - 4 = (\lambda - 3)(\lambda + 1)$, therefore the eigenvalues are $3$ and $-1$. With $y = P^T x$ one has $x^T A x = \lambda_1 y_1^2 + \lambda_2 y_2^2$, hence $q = 3y_1^2 - y_2^2$. The expression $5y_1^2 - 3y_2^2$ is obtained by putting $4$ off the diagonal.
