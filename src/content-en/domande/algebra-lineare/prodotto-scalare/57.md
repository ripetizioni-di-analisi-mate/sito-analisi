Let $\{ e_1, e_2, e_3 \}$ be an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$ and let $v = e_1 + 2e_2 - e_3$ and $w = 3e_1 + 2e_3$. What is $\langle v, w \rangle$?

- $5$
- $-1$
- It depends on the chosen basis
- $1$

## Explanation

Expanding by bilinearity one obtains a sum of terms of the form $\langle e_i, e_j \rangle$ multiplied by the coefficients of $v$ and $w$. Since the basis is orthonormal, $\langle e_i, e_j \rangle = 0$ for $i \neq j$ and $\langle e_i, e_i \rangle = 1$, therefore only the products of the coefficients in the same position remain:
$$
\langle v, w \rangle = 1 \cdot 3 + 2 \cdot 0 + (-1) \cdot 2 = 1 .
$$
The result does not depend on the orthonormal basis, because with respect to an orthonormal basis the inner product is computed on the coordinates like the standard inner product.
