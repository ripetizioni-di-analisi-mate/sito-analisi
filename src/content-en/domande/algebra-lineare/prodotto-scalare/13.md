The symmetric matrix
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & -2 & -4 \\
-2 & 4 & -2 \\
-4 & -2 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
has eigenvalues $5$ and $-4$. What is the eigenspace $V_5$?

- The line $\langle (2, 1, 2) \rangle$
- The plane $2x - y + 2z = 0$
- The plane $2x + y + 2z = 0$
- The line $\langle (1, 0, -1) \rangle$

## Explanation

The eigenspace $V_5$ is the solution set of the system $(A - 5I)x = 0$, with
$$
A - 5I =
\begin{pmatrix}
-4 & -2 & -4 \\
-2 & -1 & -2 \\
-4 & -2 & -4
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
The three rows are proportional to $(2, 1, 2)$, therefore the system reduces to the single equation $2x + y + 2z = 0$. Hence $V_5$ is a plane, and the eigenvalue $5$ is double. The line $\langle (2, 1, 2) \rangle$ is the eigenspace $V_{-4}$, which is the orthogonal complement of $V_5$ by the spectral theorem.
