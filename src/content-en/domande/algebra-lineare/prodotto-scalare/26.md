Let $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ be orthogonal, with $\|u\| = 3$ and $\|v\| = 4$. What is $\|u - 2v\|$?

- $5$
- $\sqrt{41}$
- $\sqrt{73}$
- $11$

## Explanation

The vectors $u$ and $-2v$ are orthogonal, because $\langle u, -2v \rangle = -2\langle u, v \rangle = 0$. Moreover $\|-2v\| = 2\|v\| = 8$. By the **Pythagorean theorem** one therefore has
$$
\|u - 2v\|^2 = \|u\|^2 + \|-2v\|^2 = 9 + 64 = 73 ,
$$
therefore $\|u - 2v\| = \sqrt{73}$.
