In $\mathbb R^3$ with its usual dot product, let $P$ be the orthogonal projection onto the plane $W=\{x+y-z=0\}$. We know $P(v)=(1,1,2)$ and the first coordinate of $v$ is $4$. What is $v$?

- $(4,1,2)$
- $(4,4,-1)$
- $(4,4,5)$
- $(4,-2,5)$

## Explanation

Vectors with that projection form the line through $(1,1,2)$ parallel to the normal $(1,1,-1)$. The first coordinate requires adding three times the normal, giving $(4,4,-1)$.
