Let $W = \{ (x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x + y - z = 0 \}$. What is the orthogonal projection of $v = (2, 3, 2)$ onto $W$?

- $(1, 1, -1)$
- $(1, 2, 3)$
- $(3, 4, 1)$
- $(-1, 0, 5)$

## Explanation

The orthogonal complement of the plane $W$ is the line spanned by the normal vector $n = (1, 1, -1)$, therefore $P_W(v) = v - \operatorname{pr}_n(v)$. Since $\langle v, n \rangle = 2 + 3 - 2 = 3$ and $\langle n, n \rangle = 3$, one has $\operatorname{pr}_n(v) = (1, 1, -1)$. Therefore
$$
P_W(v) = (2, 3, 2) - (1, 1, -1) = (1, 2, 3) ,
$$
and the result belongs to $W$, because $1 + 2 - 3 = 0$.
