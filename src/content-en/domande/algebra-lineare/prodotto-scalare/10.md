Which point of the plane $W = \{ (x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x - y + 2z = 0 \}$ is closest to $v = (1, 3, 4)$?

- $(1, -1, 2)$
- $(0, 4, 2)$
- $(2, 2, 6)$
- $(-1, 5, 0)$

## Explanation

The point of $W$ closest to $v$ is the orthogonal projection $P_W(v)$. The orthogonal complement of $W$ is the line spanned by the normal vector $n = (1, -1, 2)$, so $P_W(v) = v - \operatorname{pr}_n(v)$. Since $\langle v, n \rangle = 1 - 3 + 8 = 6$ and $\langle n, n \rangle = 6$, we have $\operatorname{pr}_n(v) = n$. Therefore $P_W(v) = (1, 3, 4) - (1, -1, 2) = (0, 4, 2)$, which belongs to $W$ because $0 - 4 + 4 = 0$. The vector $(1, -1, 2)$ is the projection of $v$ onto the normal, $(2, 2, 6)$ is obtained by adding the projection instead of subtracting it, and $(-1, 5, 0)$ by subtracting it twice.
