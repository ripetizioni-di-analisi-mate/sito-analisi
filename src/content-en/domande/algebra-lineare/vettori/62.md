$P=\left(3,-2,4\right)$, $Q=\left(6,-6,4\right)$, $R=\left(6,-6,6\right)$. What is the vector $\overrightarrow{PQ}+\overrightarrow{QR}$?

- $\left(3,-4,0\right)$
- $\left(3,-4,2\right)$
- $\left(0,0,2\right)$
- $\left(-3,4,-2\right)$

## Explanation

The sum is the direct displacement from $P$ to $R$. The intermediate point $Q$ cancels componentwise. The result is $\left(3,-4,2\right)$.
