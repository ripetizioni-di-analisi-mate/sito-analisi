Which of the following pairs of vectors forms an obtuse angle?

- $(2, -1, 0)$ and $(1, 1, 3)$
- $(1, 0, 2)$ and $(-2, 5, 1)$
- $(1, 2, -1)$ and $(3, -1, 2)$
- $(-1, -1, 1)$ and $(-2, 1, 1)$

## Explanation

The angle between two nonzero vectors is obtuse if and only if their dot product is negative, because the dot product has the sign of $\cos\theta$. For the pair $(1, 2, -1)$ and $(3, -1, 2)$ one has $3 - 2 - 2 = -1 < 0$, therefore the angle is **obtuse**. For the pair $(2, -1, 0)$ and $(1, 1, 3)$ the dot product equals $2 - 1 + 0 = 1$ and for the pair $(-1, -1, 1)$ and $(-2, 1, 1)$ it equals $2 - 1 + 1 = 2$, so those angles are acute. For the pair $(1, 0, 2)$ and $(-2, 5, 1)$ the dot product equals $-2 + 0 + 2 = 0$, therefore the vectors are orthogonal.
