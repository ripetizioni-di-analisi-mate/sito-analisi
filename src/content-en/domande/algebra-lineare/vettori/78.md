$P=\left(4,0,5\right)$, $Q=\left(8,-3,-5\right)$. What is the midpoint of segment $PQ$?

- $\left(4,-3,0\right)$
- $\left(6,- \dfrac{3}{2},0\right)$
- $\left(4,-3,5\right)$
- $\left(12,-3,0\right)$

## Explanation

Average the endpoint coordinates. The opposite third coordinates cancel. The result is $\left(6,- \dfrac{3}{2},0\right)$.
