Which of the following triples consists of coplanar vectors?

- $(1, 0, 0)$, $(0, 1, 0)$, $(1, 1, 1)$
- $(1, 2, 3)$, $(2, 3, 4)$, $(3, 4, 5)$
- $(1, 1, 0)$, $(0, 1, 1)$, $(1, 0, 1)$
- $(1, 2, 0)$, $(0, 1, 2)$, $(2, 0, 1)$

## Explanation

By the **coplanarity criterion** three vectors are coplanar if and only if their scalar triple product is zero. For the triple $(1, 2, 3)$, $(2, 3, 4)$, $(3, 4, 5)$ one has $(3, 4, 5) = 2\,(2, 3, 4) - (1, 2, 3)$, therefore the third vector is a linear combination of the first two and the scalar triple product is zero. For the other three triples, in the order of the list, the determinants equal $1$, $2$ and $9$, so those vectors are not coplanar.
