The vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ have magnitudes $\|\vec{u}\| = 2$ and $\|\vec{v}\| = 3$ and form an angle of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. What is $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}$?

- $3$
- $3\sqrt{3}$
- $6$
- $-3$

## Explanation

By the definition of the **dot product** one has $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \|\vec{u}\|\,\|\vec{v}\|\cos\theta$. Since $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, one obtains $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 3$. The value $3\sqrt{3}$ is obtained by using $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ in place of $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.
