The vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ satisfy $\|\vec{u} + \vec{v}\| = 7$ and $\|\vec{u} - \vec{v}\| = 5$. What is $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}$?

- $6$
- $12$
- $24$
- $3$

## Explanation

By the linearity and symmetry of the dot product
$$
\|\vec{u} + \vec{v}\|^2 = \|\vec{u}\|^2 + 2\,\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} + \|\vec{v}\|^2 ,
\qquad
\|\vec{u} - \vec{v}\|^2 = \|\vec{u}\|^2 - 2\,\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} + \|\vec{v}\|^2 .
$$
Subtracting the two equalities gives $\|\vec{u} + \vec{v}\|^2 - \|\vec{u} - \vec{v}\|^2 = 4\,\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}$. Therefore
$$
\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \frac{49 - 25}{4} = 6 .
$$
The value $12$ is obtained by dividing by $2$ instead of $4$, that is, by taking into account only one of the two mixed terms $\pm 2\,\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}$.
