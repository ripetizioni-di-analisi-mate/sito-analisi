Vectors $u,v$ determine the base of a parallelepiped in space, with $u\times v=(2,-1,2)$. The third edge $w$ must have norm one. What is the largest possible volume, and how many oriented directions of $w$ attain it?

- Volume $3$, in two directions.
- Volume $9$, in one direction.
- Volume $3$, in just one direction.
- Volume $9$, in two directions.

## Explanation

The volume is $|(u\times v)\cdot w|$. Cauchy–Schwarz bounds it by $\sqrt{4+1+4}=3$. The absolute value reaches its maximum at both unit normals, $w=\pm\dfrac{(2,-1,2)}{3}$.
