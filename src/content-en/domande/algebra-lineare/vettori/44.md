For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ are the points $A = (1, 0, 0)$, $B = (2, 0, 1)$, $C = (1, 1, 1)$, $D = (2, 1, k)$ coplanar?

- $k = 2$
- $k = 3$
- $k = 1$
- $k = -2$

## Explanation

By the **coplanarity criterion** the four points are coplanar if and only if the scalar triple product of $\overrightarrow{AB} = (1, 0, 1)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (0, 1, 1)$, $\overrightarrow{AD} = (1, 1, k)$ is zero. One has
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
1 & 1 & k
\end{vmatrix}
= 1\,(k - 1) - 0 + 1\,(0 - 1) = k - 2 ,
$$
therefore the points are **coplanar** for $k = 2$. The value $k = 3$ is obtained by using in the last row the coordinates of $D$ in place of the components of $\overrightarrow{AD}$.
