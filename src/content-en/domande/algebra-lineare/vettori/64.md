The triangle $ABC$ is equilateral with side length $2$. What is the dot product $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC}$?

- $2$
- $-2\sqrt{3}$
- $-2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The angle between two vectors is measured by applying them at the same point. Applied at $B$, the vector $\overrightarrow{AB}$ continues beyond $B$ in the direction from $A$ to $B$, so it forms with $\overrightarrow{BC}$ the exterior angle of the triangle at $B$, which equals $\pi - \dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. Therefore
$$
\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot \cos\frac{2\pi}{3} = 4 \cdot \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) = -2 .
$$
The value $2$ is obtained by using the interior angle $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, which is the angle between $\overrightarrow{BA}$ and $\overrightarrow{BC}$.
