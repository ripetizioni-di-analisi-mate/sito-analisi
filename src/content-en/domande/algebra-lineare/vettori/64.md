$u=\left(3,-2\right)$, $v=\left(6,2\right)$, $w=\left(9,0\right)$. What is the coefficient of $v$ in the combination $w=su+tv$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

The second component gives $b(s-t)=0$. Since $b$ is nonzero, $s=t$, and the first component determines their common value. Substituting $b=-2$ gives $1$.
