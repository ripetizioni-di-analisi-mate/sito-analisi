$P=\left(3,0,-2\right)$, $Q=\left(6,4,2\right)$. What is the midpoint of segment $PQ$?

- $\left(\dfrac{9}{2},2,0\right)$
- $\left(9,4,0\right)$
- $\left(3,4,0\right)$
- $\left(3,4,-2\right)$

## Explanation

The midpoint has coordinates given by the averages of the endpoint coordinates. The opposite third coordinates cancel. The result is $\left(\dfrac{9}{2},2,0\right)$.
