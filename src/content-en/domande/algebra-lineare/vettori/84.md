$u=\left(4,-8,8\right)$. What positive factor scales $u$ to a unit vector?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

The norm of $u$ is $3a$, with $a$ its positive first component. Divide the vector by its norm. Substituting $a=4$ gives $\dfrac{1}{12}$.
