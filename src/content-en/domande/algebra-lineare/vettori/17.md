What is the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $A = (-3, 4, 1)$ and $B = (5, -2, -7)$?

- $M = (4, -3, -4)$
- $M = (1, 1, -3)$
- $M = (2, 2, -6)$
- $M = (8, -6, -8)$

## Explanation

The coordinates of the midpoint are the arithmetic means of the coordinates of the endpoints. Therefore
$$
M = \left( \frac{-3 + 5}{2},\ \frac{4 - 2}{2},\ \frac{1 - 7}{2} \right) = (1, 1, -3) .
$$
The point $(4, -3, -4)$ has as coordinates half the components of $\overrightarrow{AB} = (8, -6, -8)$, and it corresponds to the vector $\dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$ applied at the origin instead of at $A$.
