$P=\left(3,-2\right)$, $Q=\left(7,0\right)$. What is the squared distance from $P$ to $Q$?

- $36$
- $6$
- $12$
- $20$

## Explanation

The displacement from $P$ to $Q$ has components $c$ and $2$, where $c$ is the horizontal change. Its squared norm is the sum of the component squares. Substituting $c=4$ gives $20$.
