## Explanation

Two vectors are orthogonal when their dot product is zero. In components one has $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = k \cdot k + 1 \cdot (-8) + k \cdot 2 = k^2 + 2k - 8$. The trinomial factors as $(k + 4)(k - 2)$, therefore it vanishes for $k = 2$ and for $k = -4$. Hence the vectors are **orthogonal** for these two values of $k$.
