What is the scalar triple product $\vec{u} \cdot (\vec{v} \times \vec{w})$ of the vectors $\vec{u} = (1, 2, 0)$, $\vec{v} = (0, 1, 3)$, $\vec{w} = (2, 0, 1)$?

- $13$
- $-13$
- $-11$
- $11$

## Explanation

The scalar triple product is the determinant of the matrix whose rows are the components of $\vec{u}$, $\vec{v}$, $\vec{w}$. Expanding along the first row gives
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 3 \\
2 & 0 & 1
\end{vmatrix}
= 1\,(1 - 0) - 2\,(0 - 6) + 0 = 1 + 12 = 13 .
$$
The value $-11$ is obtained by forgetting the minus sign in front of the second term of the expansion.
