The vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ of space are orthogonal, with $\|\vec{u}\| = 2$ and $\|\vec{v}\| = 3$. The vector $\vec{w}$ has magnitude $4$ and forms an angle of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ with $\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$. What is the volume of the parallelepiped with edges $\vec{u}$, $\vec{v}$, $\vec{w}$?

- $12$
- $24$
- $12\sqrt{3}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The base is the rectangle with sides $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$, which has area $\|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\| = 2 \cdot 3 = 6$, since the two vectors are orthogonal. The height is the component of $\vec{w}$ along the direction of $\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$, which is orthogonal to the base, therefore it equals $\|\vec{w}\|\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = 4 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 2$. Hence the volume is $6 \cdot 2 = 12$. The same result follows from the scalar triple product, $\left| \vec{w} \cdot (\vec{u} \times \vec{v}) \right| = 4 \cdot 6 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 12$. The value $12\sqrt{3}$ is obtained by using the sine of the angle instead of the cosine.
