$P=\left(2,0\right)$, $Q=\left(0,3\right)$. What are the coordinates of the point two thirds of the way from $P$ to $Q$?

- $\left(\dfrac{2}{3},2\right)$
- $\left(- \dfrac{4}{3},2\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{4}{3},1\right)$
- $\left(1,\dfrac{3}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

Add two thirds of the displacement from $P$ to $Q$ to the starting point. The coefficient of $P$ becomes one third. The result is $\left(\dfrac{2}{3},2\right)$.
