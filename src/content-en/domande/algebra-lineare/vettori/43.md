A measurement gives the signed volume $[u,v,w]=4$, with $[u,v,h]=(u\times v)\cdot h$. Only the third edge is perturbed, replacing $w$ by $w+\varepsilon h$, where $|\varepsilon|\le0.1$ and $[u,v,h]=-3$. What is the exact interval of possible unsigned volumes?

- $[1,\,7]$
- $[3.9,\,4.1]$
- $[3.7,\,4.3]$
- $[0,\,4.3]$

## Explanation

Linearity in the third argument gives the signed volume $4-3\varepsilon$. The endpoints are $3.7$ and $4.3$, both positive. Taking the absolute value therefore preserves the entire interval.
