For which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ does the parallelepiped having as edges the vectors $\vec{u} = (1, 1, 0)$, $\vec{v} = (0, 1, 1)$, $\vec{w} = (k, 0, 1)$ have volume $6$?

- Only for $k = 5$
- For $k = -5$ and for $k = 7$
- For $k = 5$ and for $k = -7$
- For $k = 35$ and for $k = -37$

## Explanation

The volume of the parallelepiped is the absolute value of the scalar triple product. Expanding along the first row one has
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
k & 0 & 1
\end{vmatrix}
= 1\,(1 - 0) - 1\,(0 - k) + 0 = 1 + k .
$$
The condition $|1 + k| = 6$ is equivalent to $1 + k = 6$ or $1 + k = -6$, therefore $k = 5$ or $k = -7$. The values $35$ and $-37$ are obtained by using the formula for the volume of the tetrahedron, with the factor $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
