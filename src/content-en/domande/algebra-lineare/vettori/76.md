The convex quadrilateral in the figure has vertices $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, $C = (5, 3)$, $D = (1, 4)$. What is its area?

::grafico{poligoni="0,0 4,0 5,3 1,4" punti="0,0,pieno,A;4,0,pieno,B;5,3,pieno,C;1,4,pieno,D" xmin=-0.8 xmax=5.8 ymin=-0.8 ymax=4.8 uguale=1 titolo="The quadrilateral $ABCD$"}

- $29$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{29}{2}$
- $8$

## Explanation

The diagonal $AC$ divides the quadrilateral into the triangles $ABC$ and $ACD$. With $\overrightarrow{AB} = (4, 0)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (5, 3)$ and $\overrightarrow{AD} = (1, 4)$ the formula for areas in the plane gives
$$
\mathcal{A}_{ABC} = \frac{1}{2}\,|4 \cdot 3 - 0 \cdot 5| = 6, \qquad
\mathcal{A}_{ACD} = \frac{1}{2}\,|5 \cdot 4 - 3 \cdot 1| = \frac{17}{2} .
$$
Therefore the area of the quadrilateral is $6 + \dfrac{17}{2} = \dfrac{29}{2}$. The value $29$ is obtained by adding the areas of the parallelograms without dividing by $2$, and $6$ is the area of the triangle $ABC$ alone.
