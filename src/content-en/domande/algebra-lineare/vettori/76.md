$u=\left(4,5,1\right)$, $v=\left(-3,2,4\right)$, $w=\left(1,-1,2\right)$. What is $2u-v+w$?

- $\left(6,11,8\right)$
- $\left(15,5,-4\right)$
- $\left(8,2,-1\right)$
- $\left(12,7,0\right)$

## Explanation

Double each component of $u$. Subtract the corresponding component of $v$ and add that of $w$. The result is $\left(12,7,0\right)$.
