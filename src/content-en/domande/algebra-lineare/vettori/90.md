$u=\left(4,5\right)$, $v=\left(0,1\right)$. What is the determinant of the ordered basis $(v,u)$ with respect to the standard basis?

- $5$
- $-4$
- $-5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first column is $v$ and the second is $u$. Reversing the column order changes the determinant sign. The result is $-4$.
