$u=\left(3,2\right)$, $v=\left(-2,4\right)$. What is the area of the triangle with vertices $0,u,v$?

- $6.0$
- $8$
- $16$
- $4.0$

## Explanation

The absolute value of the determinant gives the area of the parallelogram spanned by the two sides. The triangle occupies half of it. The result is $8$.
