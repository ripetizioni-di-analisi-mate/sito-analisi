Given $A = (1, 2, 0)$ and $B = (7, -1, 3)$, what is the point $P$ of the segment $AB$ such that $\overrightarrow{AP} = \dfrac{1}{3}\,\overrightarrow{AB}$?

- $P = (5, 0, 2)$
- $P = (3, 1, 1)$
- $P = (2, -1, 1)$
- $P = \left( \dfrac{8}{3}, \dfrac{1}{3}, 1 \right)$

## Explanation

One has $\overrightarrow{AB} = (6, -3, 3)$, therefore $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\overrightarrow{AB} = (2, -1, 1)$. The point $P$ is obtained by applying this vector at $A$, that is, by adding its components to the coordinates of $A$. Hence $P = (1 + 2,\ 2 - 1,\ 0 + 1) = (3, 1, 1)$. The point $(5, 0, 2)$ corresponds instead to $\overrightarrow{AP} = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\overrightarrow{AB}$.
