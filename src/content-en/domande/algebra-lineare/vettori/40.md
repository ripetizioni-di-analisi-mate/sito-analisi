Which of the following vectors is parallel to $\vec{u} = (2, -3, 1)$ and has the opposite orientation?

- $(-4, 6, -2)$
- $(4, -6, 2)$
- $(-2, 3, 1)$
- $(3, 2, 0)$

## Explanation

A vector is parallel to $\vec{u}$ with opposite orientation if it equals $\lambda\vec{u}$ with $\lambda < 0$. One has $(-4, 6, -2) = -2\,\vec{u}$, therefore this vector has the required property. The vector $(4, -6, 2) = 2\,\vec{u}$ has the same orientation as $\vec{u}$. The components of $(-2, 3, 1)$ are not proportional to those of $\vec{u}$, and $(3, 2, 0)$ is orthogonal to $\vec{u}$, since $6 - 6 + 0 = 0$.
