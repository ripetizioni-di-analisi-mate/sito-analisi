Given $\vec{u} = (2, -2, 3)$ and $\vec{v} = (-3, -1, 3)$, what is the magnitude of the vector $3\vec{u} - 2\vec{v}$?

- $17$
- $13$
- $169$
- $\sqrt{217}$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the scalars gives $3\vec{u} = (6, -6, 9)$ and $2\vec{v} = (-6, -2, 6)$. Subtracting component by component one has
$$
3\vec{u} - 2\vec{v} = (6 + 6,\ -6 + 2,\ 9 - 6) = (12, -4, 3) .
$$
The magnitude is the square root of the sum of the squares of the components, therefore $\|3\vec{u} - 2\vec{v}\| = \sqrt{144 + 16 + 9} = \sqrt{169} = 13$.
