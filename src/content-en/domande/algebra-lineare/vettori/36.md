What is the area of the triangle in the plane with vertices $A = (1, 1)$, $B = (4, 2)$, $C = (0, 4)$?

- $10$
- $4$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

One has $\overrightarrow{AB} = (3, 1)$ and $\overrightarrow{AC} = (-1, 3)$. The area of the parallelogram with sides $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$ is the absolute value of the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ -1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 3 \cdot 3 - 1 \cdot (-1) = 10 .
$$
The area of the triangle is half of it, therefore it equals $5$. The value $4$ is obtained by getting the sign of the product $1 \cdot (-1)$ in the determinant wrong and then dividing by $2$.
