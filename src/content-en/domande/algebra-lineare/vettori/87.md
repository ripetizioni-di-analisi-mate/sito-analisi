$u=\left(4,5,2\right)$, $v=\left(1,0,3\right)$. What is the second component of a vector $w$ satisfying $u+w=2v$?

- $-5$
- $5$
- $0$
- $-10$

## Explanation

Isolate $w=2v-u$. The second component of $v$ is zero, leaving the negative of the second component of $u$. The result is $-5$.
