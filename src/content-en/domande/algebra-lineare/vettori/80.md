$u=\left(4,5\right)$, $v=\left(8,t\right)$. For which $t$ are the two vectors parallel?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $-10$
- $10$

## Explanation

The first component of $v$ is twice that of $u$. The same factor must hold for the second component if the vectors are parallel. The result is $10$.
