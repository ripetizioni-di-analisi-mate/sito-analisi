$u=\left(3,-6,6\right)$. What positive factor scales $u$ to a unit vector?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{27}$

## Explanation

The norm of $u$ is $3a$, with $a$ its positive first component. Divide the vector by its norm. Substituting $a=3$ gives $\dfrac{1}{9}$.
