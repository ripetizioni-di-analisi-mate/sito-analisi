Given the points $A = (3, -1, 2)$ and $B = (-1, 1, 6)$, what is the unit vector in the direction of the vector $\overrightarrow{AB}$?

- $\left( \dfrac{2}{3}, -\dfrac{1}{3}, -\dfrac{2}{3} \right)$
- $\left( -\dfrac{2}{5}, \dfrac{1}{5}, \dfrac{2}{5} \right)$
- $\left( -\dfrac{2}{3}, \dfrac{1}{3}, \dfrac{2}{3} \right)$
- $\left( -\dfrac{1}{9}, \dfrac{1}{18}, \dfrac{1}{9} \right)$

## Explanation

The components of $\overrightarrow{AB}$ are the differences between the coordinates of $B$ and those of $A$, therefore $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-1 - 3,\ 1 + 1,\ 6 - 2) = (-4, 2, 4)$. Its magnitude is $\sqrt{16 + 4 + 16} = 6$. The unit vector is obtained by dividing the vector by its magnitude, so it is $\dfrac{1}{6}\,(-4, 2, 4) = \left( -\dfrac{2}{3}, \dfrac{1}{3}, \dfrac{2}{3} \right)$. The vector $\left( \dfrac{2}{3}, -\dfrac{1}{3}, -\dfrac{2}{3} \right)$ is the unit vector in the direction of $\overrightarrow{BA}$, which has the opposite orientation.
