The vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ in space have cross product $\vec{u} \times \vec{v} = (1, -2, 3)$. What is $(2\vec{v}) \times \vec{u}$?

- $(2, -4, 6)$
- $(-2, 4, -6)$
- $(-1, 2, -3)$
- $(1, -2, 3)$

## Explanation

By the linearity of the cross product one has $(2\vec{v}) \times \vec{u} = 2\,(\vec{v} \times \vec{u})$. By anticommutativity $\vec{v} \times \vec{u} = -\,\vec{u} \times \vec{v} = (-1, 2, -3)$. Therefore $(2\vec{v}) \times \vec{u} = 2\,(-1, 2, -3) = (-2, 4, -6)$.
