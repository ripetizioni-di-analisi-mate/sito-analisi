$u=\left(2,1,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,3,1\right)$, $w=\left(1,0,1\right)$. What is the volume of the parallelepiped spanned by the three vectors?

- $5$
- $-7$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

The volume is the absolute value of the scalar triple product. The determinant with the three vectors as columns has contributions $abc$ and $1$. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$, $c=1$ gives $7$.
