The points $A = (1, 0, 2)$, $B = (3, 1, 1)$, $C = (4, -1, 3)$ are three consecutive vertices of the parallelogram $ABCD$. What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex $D$?

- $D = (6, 0, 2)$
- $D = (0, 2, 0)$
- $D = \left( \dfrac{5}{2}, -\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{5}{2} \right)$
- $D = (2, -2, 4)$

## Explanation

In a parallelogram $ABCD$ the opposite sides $AB$ and $DC$ are parallel, of the same length and with the same orientation, therefore $\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{DC}$. In components this means $B - A = C - D$, so
$$
D = A + C - B = (1 + 4 - 3,\ 0 - 1 - 1,\ 2 + 3 - 1) = (2, -2, 4) .
$$
The point $(6, 0, 2) = B + C - A$ is the fourth vertex of the parallelogram $ABDC$, in which $B$ and $C$ are opposite, and $\left( \dfrac{5}{2}, -\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{5}{2} \right)$ is the midpoint of $AC$, that is, the center of the parallelogram.
