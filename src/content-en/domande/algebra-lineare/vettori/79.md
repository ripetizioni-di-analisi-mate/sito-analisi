$u=\left(4,5\right)$, $v=\left(8,-5\right)$, $w=\left(12,0\right)$. What is the coefficient of $v$ in $w=su+tv$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The second component gives $b(s-t)=0$. Since $b$ is nonzero, $s=t$, and the first component determines their common value. Substituting $b=5$ gives $1$.
