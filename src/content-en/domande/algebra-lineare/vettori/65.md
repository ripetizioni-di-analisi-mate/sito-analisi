$u=\left(3,-2\right)$, $v=\left(6,t\right)$. For which $t$ are the two vectors parallel?

- $4$
- $-1$
- $-4$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The first component of $v$ is twice that of $u$. The same factor must hold for the second component if the vectors are parallel. The result is $-4$.
