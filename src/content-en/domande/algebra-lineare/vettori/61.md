$u=\left(3,-2,1\right)$, $v=\left(4,2,3\right)$, $w=\left(1,-1,2\right)$. What is $2u-v+w$?

- $\left(0,-5,0\right)$
- $\left(11,-3,7\right)$
- $\left(3,-7,1\right)$
- $\left(-1,-9,-2\right)$

## Explanation

Double each component of $u$. Subtract the corresponding component of $v$ and add that of $w$. The result is $\left(3,-7,1\right)$.
