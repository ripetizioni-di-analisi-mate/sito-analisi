$u=\left(4,2\right)$, $v=\left(5,-3\right)$. What is the area of the triangle with vertices $0,u,v$?

- $22$
- $1.0$
- $11$
- $6.0$

## Explanation

The absolute determinant gives the area of the parallelogram spanned by the sides. The triangle occupies half of it. The result is $11$.
