Which vectors of the plane have magnitude $5$ and are orthogonal to $\vec{v} = (3, 4)$?

- $\pm\,(4, 3)$
- $\pm\left( \dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5} \right)$
- $\pm\,(-3, 4)$
- $\pm\,(4, -3)$

## Explanation

In the plane the vectors orthogonal to $(a, b) \neq \vec{0}$ are the multiples of $(-b, a)$, therefore the vectors orthogonal to $\vec{v}$ are $t\,(-4, 3)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$. The magnitude of $t\,(-4, 3)$ is $5\,|t|$, so it equals $5$ for $t = \pm 1$. The required vectors are therefore $\pm\,(-4, 3)$, that is, $\pm\,(4, -3)$. The vectors $\pm\left( \dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5} \right)$ are orthogonal to $\vec{v}$, but they have magnitude $1$.
