Let $G$ be the centroid of the triangle $ABC$. Which vector is $\overrightarrow{AG}$ equal to?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\right)$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\right)$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\right)$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} - \overrightarrow{AC}\right)$

## Explanation

Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$. Since $\overrightarrow{AM} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BC}$ and $\overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AB}$, one has $\overrightarrow{AM} = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\right)$. The centroid lies on the median $AM$ and divides it into two parts, the one on the side of the vertex being twice the other, so $\overrightarrow{AG} = \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AM}$. It follows that
$$
\overrightarrow{AG} = \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\right) = \frac{1}{3}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\right) .
$$
The vector $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\right)$ is $\overrightarrow{AM}$, which reaches the midpoint of $BC$ and not the centroid.
