$P=\left(2,0,3\right)$, $Q=\left(4,1,-3\right)$. What is the midpoint of segment $PQ$?

- $\left(6,1,0\right)$
- $\left(2,1,0\right)$
- $\left(2,1,3\right)$
- $\left(3,\dfrac{1}{2},0\right)$

## Explanation

The midpoint has coordinates given by the averages of the endpoint coordinates. The opposite third coordinates cancel. The result is $\left(3,\dfrac{1}{2},0\right)$.
