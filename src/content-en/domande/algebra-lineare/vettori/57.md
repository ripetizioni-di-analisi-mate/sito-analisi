$u=\left(2,3,2\right)$, $v=\left(1,0,3\right)$. What is the second component of a vector $w$ satisfying $u+w=2v$?

- $-6$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $0$

## Explanation

Isolate $w=2v-u$. The second component of $v$ is zero, leaving the negative of the second component of $u$. The result is $-3$.
