The vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ in space have magnitudes $\|\vec{u}\| = 2$ and $\|\vec{v}\| = 5$ and dot product $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 6$. What is $\|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\|$?

- $6$
- $10$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

By **Lagrange's identity** one has $\|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\|^2 = \|\vec{u}\|^2\,\|\vec{v}\|^2 - (\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v})^2$. Substituting the data gives $\|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\|^2 = 4 \cdot 25 - 36 = 64$, therefore $\|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\| = 8$. The same result is reached with the angle, since $\cos\theta = \dfrac{6}{10} = \dfrac{3}{5}$, so $\sin\theta = \dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\|\vec{u}\|\,\|\vec{v}\|\sin\theta = 10 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} = 8$.
