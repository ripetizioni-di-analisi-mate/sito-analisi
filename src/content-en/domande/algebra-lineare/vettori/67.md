A constant force $\vec{F} = (3, -1, 2)$, in newtons, acts on a body that moves in a straight line from the point $A = (1, 2, 0)$ to the point $B = (4, 0, 1)$, with coordinates in meters. What is the work done by the force, that is $\vec{F} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}$?

- $13\ \text{J}$
- $-13\ \text{J}$
- $9\ \text{J}$
- $5\ \text{J}$

## Explanation

The displacement is $\overrightarrow{AB} = B - A = (3, -2, 1)$. With the formula for the dot product in components one obtains
$$
\vec{F} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = 3 \cdot 3 + (-1)(-2) + 2 \cdot 1 = 9 + 2 + 2 = 13 ,
$$
therefore the work is $13\ \text{J}$. The value $-13\ \text{J}$ is obtained by using the displacement $\overrightarrow{BA}$, while $9\ \text{J}$ and $5\ \text{J}$ are obtained by getting the sign of the product $(-1)(-2)$ wrong, the second also that of the term $2 \cdot 1$.
