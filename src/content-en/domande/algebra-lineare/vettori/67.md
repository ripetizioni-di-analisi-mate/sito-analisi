$u=\left(3,1,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,-2,1\right)$, $w=\left(1,0,4\right)$. What is the volume of the parallelepiped spanned by the three vectors?

- $23$
- $25$
- $-23$
- $24$

## Explanation

The volume is the absolute value of the scalar triple product. The determinant with the vectors as columns has contributions $abc$ and $1$. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$, $c=4$ gives $23$.
