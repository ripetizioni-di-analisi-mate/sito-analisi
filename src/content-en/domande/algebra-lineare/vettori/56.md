$u=\left(2,1\right)$, $v=\left(1,0\right)$, $P=\left(3,1\right)$. What is the first component of $P$ after translation by $u-2v$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

A translation adds each displacement component to the corresponding coordinate. Here the first component is the first component of $u$ minus two. The result is $3$.
