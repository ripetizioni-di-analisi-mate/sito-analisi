$u=\left(2,3\right)$, $v=\left(4,-3\right)$, $w=\left(6,0\right)$. What is the coefficient of $v$ in the combination $w=su+tv$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The second component gives $b(s-t)=0$. Since $b$ is nonzero, $s=t$, and the first component determines their common value. Substituting $b=3$ gives $1$.
