In the triangle $ABC$ let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$ and $N$ the midpoint of $AC$. Which vector is $\overrightarrow{MN}$ equal to?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\overrightarrow{CB}$
- $2\,\overrightarrow{BC}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\right)$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\overrightarrow{BC}$

## Explanation

Since $M$ and $N$ are midpoints, one has $\overrightarrow{AM} = \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AN} = \dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AC}$. The difference of two vectors leaving $A$ goes from the head of the second to the head of the first, therefore
$$
\overrightarrow{MN} = \overrightarrow{AN} - \overrightarrow{AM} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AB}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\,\overrightarrow{BC} .
$$
The segment $MN$ is therefore parallel to $BC$, half as long and oriented from $B$ toward $C$. The vector $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\overrightarrow{CB}$ has the same length but opposite orientation.
