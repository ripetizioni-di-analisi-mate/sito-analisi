Which point of the $x$-axis is at the same distance from $A = (1, 2, 0)$ and from $B = (4, 0, 1)$?

- $\left( \dfrac{5}{2}, 0, 0 \right)$
- $(3, 0, 0)$
- $(2, 0, 0)$
- $(-2, 0, 0)$

## Explanation

A point of the $x$-axis has coordinates $P = (x, 0, 0)$. One has $d(P, A)^2 = (x - 1)^2 + 4$ and $d(P, B)^2 = (x - 4)^2 + 1$. Since distances are nonnegative, they are equal if and only if their squares are equal, therefore
$$
x^2 - 2x + 5 = x^2 - 8x + 17 .
$$
Hence $6x = 12$, that is, $x = 2$, and the required point is $(2, 0, 0)$.
