The vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ have magnitudes $\|\vec{u}\| = 3$ and $\|\vec{v}\| = 4$ and dot product $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 2$. What is $\|\vec{u} + \vec{v}\|$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $\sqrt{29}$
- $\sqrt{21}$

## Explanation

By the linearity of the dot product one has
$$
\|\vec{u} + \vec{v}\|^2 = (\vec{u} + \vec{v}) \cdot (\vec{u} + \vec{v}) = \|\vec{u}\|^2 + 2\,\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} + \|\vec{v}\|^2 .
$$
Substituting the data gives $\|\vec{u} + \vec{v}\|^2 = 9 + 4 + 16 = 29$, therefore $\|\vec{u} + \vec{v}\| = \sqrt{29}$. The value $5$ corresponds to orthogonal vectors, that is, to $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$.
