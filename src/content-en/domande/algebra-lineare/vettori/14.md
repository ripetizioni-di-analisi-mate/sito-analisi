Let $ABCD$ be a parallelogram, with the vertices in the order in which they are written. Which of the following equalities holds?

- $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{AC}$
- $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{BD}$
- $\overrightarrow{AB} - \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{BD}$
- $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{CA}$

## Explanation

In the parallelogram $ABCD$ the sides $AD$ and $BC$ are parallel, have the same length and are traversed with the same orientation, therefore $\overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AD}$. By the head-to-tail rule $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AC}$, hence $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{AC}$, which is the **parallelogram rule**. The difference $\overrightarrow{AB} - \overrightarrow{AD}$ goes from the head of $\overrightarrow{AD}$ to the head of $\overrightarrow{AB}$, therefore it is $\overrightarrow{DB}$, the negative of $\overrightarrow{BD}$.
