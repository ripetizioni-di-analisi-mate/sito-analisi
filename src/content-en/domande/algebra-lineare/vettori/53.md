$P=\left(2,3\right)$, $Q=\left(3,5\right)$. What is the squared distance from $P$ to $Q$?

- $-3$
- $9$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The displacement from $P$ to $Q$ has components $c$ and $2$, where $c$ is the horizontal change. Its squared norm is the sum of the component squares. Substituting $c=1$ gives $5$.
