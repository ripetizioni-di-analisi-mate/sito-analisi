Let $ABCD$ be a square of side $1$, with the vertices in the order in which they are written. What is $\left\| \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC} + \overrightarrow{AD} \right\|$?

- $2 + \sqrt{2}$
- $8$
- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

By the **parallelogram rule** the sum of the sides $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AD}$ is the diagonal $\overrightarrow{AC}$. Therefore
$$
\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC} + \overrightarrow{AD} = 2\,\overrightarrow{AC} .
$$
The diagonal of a square of side $1$ has length $\sqrt{2}$, so the required magnitude is $2\sqrt{2}$. The value $2 + \sqrt{2}$ is the sum of the magnitudes of the three vectors, which is greater than the magnitude of the sum, and $8$ is the square of the required magnitude.
