$P=\left(4,0\right)$, $Q=\left(0,5\right)$. What are the coordinates two thirds of the way from $P$ to $Q$?

- $\left(2,\dfrac{5}{2}\right)$
- $\left(- \dfrac{8}{3},\dfrac{10}{3}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{4}{3},\dfrac{10}{3}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{8}{3},\dfrac{5}{3}\right)$

## Explanation

Add two thirds of the displacement from $P$ to $Q$ to the starting point. The coefficient of $P$ becomes one third. The result is $\left(\dfrac{4}{3},\dfrac{10}{3}\right)$.
