## Explanation

The distance of $O$ from the line $AB$ is the height of the triangle $ABO$ relative to the side $AB$. One has $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-2, -2, -1)$ and $\overrightarrow{AO} = (-2, -1, 0)$, therefore
$$
\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AO} = \big( (-2) \cdot 0 - (-1)(-1),\ \ (-1)(-2) - (-2) \cdot 0,\ \ (-2)(-1) - (-2)(-2) \big) = (-1, 2, -2) .
$$
The magnitude of this vector equals $3$ and is the area of the parallelogram with sides $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AO}$. Dividing the area by the base $\|\overrightarrow{AB}\| = 3$ gives the height, so the distance is $1$.
