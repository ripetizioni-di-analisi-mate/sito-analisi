What is the component of the vector $\vec{v} = (3, -1, 2)$ along the vector $\vec{u} = (2, 1, -2)$, that is, the number $\dfrac{\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u}}{\|\vec{u}\|}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The dot product is $\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u} = 6 - 1 - 4 = 1$, and the magnitude of $\vec{u}$ is $\sqrt{4 + 1 + 4} = 3$. Therefore the component of $\vec{v}$ along $\vec{u}$ equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{9}$ is the coefficient $\dfrac{\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u}}{\|\vec{u}\|^2}$ that multiplies $\vec{u}$ in the orthogonal projection $\operatorname{pr}_{\vec{u}}(\vec{v})$.
