In the triangle with vertices $A = (1, 0, 1)$, $B = (3, 2, 1)$, $C = (4, 0, 3)$, what is the foot $H$ of the altitude from $C$, that is, the point of the line $AB$ such that $\overrightarrow{HC}$ is orthogonal to $\overrightarrow{AB}$?

- $H = (2, 1, 1)$
- $H = \left( \dfrac{3}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2}, 1 \right)$
- $H = \left( \dfrac{5}{2}, \dfrac{3}{2}, 1 \right)$
- $H = (4, 3, 1)$

## Explanation

One has $\overrightarrow{AB} = (2, 2, 0)$ and $\overrightarrow{AC} = (3, 0, 2)$. The vector $\overrightarrow{AH}$ is the orthogonal projection of $\overrightarrow{AC}$ onto the direction of $\overrightarrow{AB}$, therefore
$$
\overrightarrow{AH} = \frac{\overrightarrow{AC} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}}{\|\overrightarrow{AB}\|^2}\, \overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{6}{8}\,(2, 2, 0) = \left( \frac{3}{2}, \frac{3}{2}, 0 \right) .
$$
Hence $H = A + \overrightarrow{AH} = \left( \dfrac{5}{2}, \dfrac{3}{2}, 1 \right)$. Indeed $\overrightarrow{HC} = \left( \dfrac{3}{2}, -\dfrac{3}{2}, 2 \right)$ and $\overrightarrow{HC} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = 3 - 3 + 0 = 0$. The point $(2, 1, 1)$ is the midpoint of $AB$, which coincides with the foot of the altitude only when the triangle is isosceles with base $AB$.
