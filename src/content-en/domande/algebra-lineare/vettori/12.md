## Explanation

One has $\overrightarrow{AB} = (1, 0, 2)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (0, 2, 1)$, $\overrightarrow{AD} = (2, 1, 0)$. The scalar triple product is the determinant of the matrix whose rows are these three vectors:
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 2 \\
0 & 2 & 1 \\
2 & 1 & 0
\end{vmatrix}
= 1\,(0 - 1) - 0 + 2\,(0 - 4) = -9 .
$$
The volume of the tetrahedron is one sixth of the absolute value of the scalar triple product, therefore it equals $\dfrac{9}{6} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.
