The vectors $\vec{u}$, $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{x}$ satisfy $3\vec{x} + \vec{u} = 2\left(\vec{x} - \vec{v}\right) + 4\vec{u}$. What is $\vec{x}$?

- $3\vec{u} - 2\vec{v}$
- $5\vec{u} - 2\vec{v}$
- $3\vec{u} + 2\vec{v}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\vec{u} - \dfrac{2}{5}\,\vec{v}$

## Explanation

By the distributive property the right-hand side is $2\vec{x} - 2\vec{v} + 4\vec{u}$. Adding $-2\vec{x} - \vec{u}$ to both sides one obtains
$$
3\vec{x} - 2\vec{x} = 4\vec{u} - \vec{u} - 2\vec{v} ,
$$
therefore $\vec{x} = 3\vec{u} - 2\vec{v}$. The result $5\vec{u} - 2\vec{v}$ comes from moving $\vec{u}$ to the right-hand side without changing its sign, and $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\vec{u} - \dfrac{2}{5}\,\vec{v}$ from moving $2\vec{x}$ there with the wrong sign.
