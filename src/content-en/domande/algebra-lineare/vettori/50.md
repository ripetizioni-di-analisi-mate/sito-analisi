$u=\left(2,3\right)$, $v=\left(4,t\right)$. For which $t$ are the two vectors parallel?

- $3$
- $6$
- $-6$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The first component of $v$ is twice that of $u$. The same factor must hold for the second component if the vectors are parallel. The result is $6$.
