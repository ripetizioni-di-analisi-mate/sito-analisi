$P=\left(3,0\right)$, $Q=\left(0,-2\right)$. What are the coordinates of the point two thirds of the way from $P$ to $Q$?

- $\left(2,- \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$
- $\left(1,- \dfrac{4}{3}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2},-1\right)$
- $\left(-2,- \dfrac{4}{3}\right)$

## Explanation

Add two thirds of the displacement from $P$ to $Q$ to the starting point. The coefficient of $P$ becomes one third. The result is $\left(1,- \dfrac{4}{3}\right)$.
