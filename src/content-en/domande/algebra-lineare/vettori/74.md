The vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ in space have magnitudes $\|\vec{u}\| = 2$ and $\|\vec{v}\| = 3$, their cross product has magnitude $\|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\| = 3\sqrt{3}$, and their dot product is negative. What is the angle between $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the cross product equals $\|\vec{u}\|\,\|\vec{v}\| \sin\theta$, where $\theta \in [0, \pi]$ is the angle between the two vectors. Therefore
$$
6 \sin\theta = 3\sqrt{3}, \qquad \sin\theta = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} ,
$$
and in $[0, \pi]$ this equation has the two solutions $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $\theta = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. The dot product equals $\|\vec{u}\|\,\|\vec{v}\| \cos\theta$ and is negative, so $\cos\theta < 0$ and the angle is obtuse. Hence $\theta = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ has the same sine, but for it the dot product would be positive.
