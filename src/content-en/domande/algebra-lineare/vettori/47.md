Let $ABCDEF$ be a regular hexagon with center $O$, with the vertices in the order in which they are written. Which vector is $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AF}$ equal to?

- $\overrightarrow{AD}$
- $\overrightarrow{BF}$
- $\overrightarrow{AO}$
- $\overrightarrow{FB}$

## Explanation

A regular hexagon splits into six equilateral triangles with a vertex at $O$, so $OA$, $OB$ and $OF$ have the same length as the side. Therefore the quadrilateral $ABOF$ has four equal sides and is a rhombus, in particular a parallelogram. By the **parallelogram rule** the sum of the sides $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AF}$ leaving $A$ is the diagonal leaving $A$, that is $\overrightarrow{AO}$. The vector $\overrightarrow{AD}$ has the same direction and the same orientation but twice the length, because $O$ is the midpoint of $AD$.
