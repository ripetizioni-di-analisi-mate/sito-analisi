- $\vec{w} = 2\,\vec{u} + 3\,\vec{v}$
- $\vec{w} = 3\,\vec{u} + 2\,\vec{v}$
- $\vec{w} = 3\,\vec{u} - 2\,\vec{v}$
- $\vec{w} = 2\,\vec{u} + 4\,\vec{v}$

## Explanation

We look for $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $\alpha\vec{u} + \beta\vec{v} = \vec{w}$. Equating the components gives the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2\alpha + \beta = 8 \\[4pt]
\alpha - \beta = 1
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations gives $3\alpha = 9$, therefore $\alpha = 3$, and from the second equation $\beta = \alpha - 1 = 2$. Hence $\vec{w} = 3\,\vec{u} + 2\,\vec{v}$, and indeed $3\,(2, 1) + 2\,(1, -1) = (8, 1)$.
