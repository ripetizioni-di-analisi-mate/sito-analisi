Let $\vec{u} = (1, 2, 2)$ and $\vec{v} = (2, -3, 1)$. Which statement about the orthogonal projection $\operatorname{pr}_{\vec{u}}(\vec{v})$ is true?

- It has orientation opposite to that of $\vec{u}$
- It has the same orientation as $\vec{u}$
- It is the zero vector
- It has magnitude $\dfrac{2}{9}$

## Explanation

One has $\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u} = 2 - 6 + 2 = -2$ and $\|\vec{u}\|^2 = 9$, therefore
$$
\operatorname{pr}_{\vec{u}}(\vec{v}) = \frac{\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u}}{\|\vec{u}\|^2}\,\vec{u} = -\frac{2}{9}\,\vec{u} .
$$
The coefficient of $\vec{u}$ is negative, so the projection has **opposite orientation** to that of $\vec{u}$, consistently with the obtuse angle between $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$. Its magnitude is $\dfrac{2}{9}\,\|\vec{u}\| = \dfrac{2}{9} \cdot 3 = \dfrac{2}{3}$.
