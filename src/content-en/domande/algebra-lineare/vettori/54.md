$u=\left(2,-4,4\right)$. What positive factor scales $u$ to a unit vector?

- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The norm of $u$ is $3a$, with $a$ its positive first component. Divide the vector by its norm. Substituting $a=2$ gives $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
