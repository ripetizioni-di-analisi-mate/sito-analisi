## Explanation

One has $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-1, 2, -2)$ and $\overrightarrow{AC} = (2, 4, 0)$. Their cross product is
$$
\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = \big( 2 \cdot 0 - (-2) \cdot 4,\ \ (-2) \cdot 2 - (-1) \cdot 0,\ \ (-1) \cdot 4 - 2 \cdot 2 \big) = (8, -4, -8) ,
$$
which has magnitude $\sqrt{64 + 16 + 64} = 12$. The area of the triangle is half the magnitude of $\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}$, therefore it equals $6$.
