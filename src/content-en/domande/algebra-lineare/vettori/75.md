Which of the following points belongs to the sphere having as a diameter the segment with endpoints $A = (1, 0, 2)$ and $B = (3, 2, 0)$?

- $(3, 0, 2)$
- $(4, 3, 3)$
- $(3, 2, 1)$
- $(3, 2, 3)$

## Explanation

The center of the sphere is the midpoint $M = (2, 1, 1)$ of the diameter. Since $\overrightarrow{AB} = (2, 2, -2)$ has magnitude $2\sqrt{3}$, the radius is $r = \sqrt{3}$. A point lies on the sphere if and only if its distance from $M$ is $\sqrt{3}$. For $(3, 0, 2)$ one has $\overrightarrow{MP} = (1, -1, 1)$, of magnitude $\sqrt{3}$, so the point **belongs** to the sphere. For the other points the distance from $M$ equals $2\sqrt{3}$, $\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{6}$; the first of these values is the length of the diameter used instead of the radius.
