$u=\left(3,-2\right)$, $v=\left(0,1\right)$. What is the determinant of the ordered basis $(v,u)$ with respect to the standard basis?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The first column is $v$ and the second is $u$. Reversing the column order changes the determinant sign. The result is $-3$.
