The vector $\vec{v}$ has magnitude $4$. Which of the following vectors has magnitude $6$ and orientation opposite to that of $\vec{v}$?

- $-\dfrac{3}{2}\,\vec{v}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\vec{v}$
- $-6\,\vec{v}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}\,\vec{v}$

## Explanation

The vector $\lambda\vec{v}$ has magnitude $|\lambda|\,\|\vec{v}\| = 4\,|\lambda|$, and it has orientation opposite to that of $\vec{v}$ when $\lambda < 0$. The condition on the magnitude is $4\,|\lambda| = 6$, therefore $|\lambda| = \dfrac{3}{2}$. Since the orientation must be opposite, one has $\lambda = -\dfrac{3}{2}$. The vector $-6\,\vec{v}$ has orientation opposite to $\vec{v}$, but its magnitude is $24$.
