The vector $\vec{v} = (0, 4, 5)$ is written as a sum $\vec{v} = \vec{v}_{\parallel} + \vec{v}_{\perp}$, with $\vec{v}_{\parallel}$ parallel to $\vec{u} = (1, 2, 2)$ and $\vec{v}_{\perp}$ orthogonal to $\vec{u}$. What is $\vec{v}_{\perp}$?

- $(-2, 0, 1)$
- $(2, 0, -1)$
- $(2, 4, 4)$
- $(-6, -8, -7)$

## Explanation

One has $\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u} = 0 + 8 + 10 = 18$ and $\|\vec{u}\|^2 = 1 + 4 + 4 = 9$. By the formula for the **orthogonal projection**
$$
\vec{v}_{\parallel} = \frac{\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u}}{\|\vec{u}\|^2}\,\vec{u} = \frac{18}{9}\,(1, 2, 2) = (2, 4, 4) .
$$
Therefore $\vec{v}_{\perp} = \vec{v} - \vec{v}_{\parallel} = (0 - 2,\ 4 - 4,\ 5 - 4) = (-2, 0, 1)$. This vector is orthogonal to $\vec{u}$, because $-2 + 0 + 2 = 0$.
