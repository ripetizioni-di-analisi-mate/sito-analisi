$u=\left(2,3,1\right)$, $v=\left(1,2,2\right)$, $w=\left(1,-1,2\right)$. What is $2u-v+w$?

- $\left(3,1,0\right)$
- $\left(4,3,2\right)$
- $\left(2,0,1\right)$
- $\left(6,7,6\right)$

## Explanation

Double each component of $u$. Subtract the corresponding component of $v$ and add that of $w$. The result is $\left(4,3,2\right)$.
