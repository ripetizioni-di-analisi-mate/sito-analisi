In the figure the plane vectors $\vec{u}$, $\vec{v}$, $\vec{w}$ are applied at the origin and have integer components. Which equality holds?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,3,1,u;0,0,1,-1,v;0,0,1,3,w" xmin=-1.6 xmax=3.6 ymin=-1.6 ymax=3.6 uguale=1 titolo="The vectors $\vec{u}$, $\vec{v}$, $\vec{w}$ applied at the origin"}

- $\vec{w} = \vec{u} - 2\vec{v}$
- $\vec{w} = 2\vec{v} - \vec{u}$
- $\vec{w} = \vec{u} + 2\vec{v}$
- $\vec{w} = 2\vec{u} - \vec{v}$

## Explanation

The heads of the vectors can be read off the grid, so $\vec{u} = (3, 1)$, $\vec{v} = (1, -1)$ and $\vec{w} = (1, 3)$. With the operations in components one obtains
$$
\vec{u} - 2\vec{v} = (3 - 2,\ 1 + 2) = (1, 3) ,
$$
therefore $\vec{w} = \vec{u} - 2\vec{v}$. The other combinations give $2\vec{v} - \vec{u} = (-1, -3)$, which is the opposite of $\vec{w}$, then $\vec{u} + 2\vec{v} = (5, -1)$ and $2\vec{u} - \vec{v} = (5, 3)$.
