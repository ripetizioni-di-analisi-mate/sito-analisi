$u=\left(2,2\right)$, $v=\left(3,1\right)$. What is the area of the triangle with vertices $0,u,v$?

- $2$
- $4.0$
- $1.0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The absolute value of the determinant gives the area of the parallelogram spanned by the two sides. The triangle occupies half of it. The result is $2$.
