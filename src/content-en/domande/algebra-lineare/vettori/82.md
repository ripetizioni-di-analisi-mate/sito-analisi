$u=\left(4,1,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,5,1\right)$, $w=\left(1,0,-3\right)$. What is the volume of the parallelepiped spanned by the three vectors?

- $-59$
- $59$
- $60$
- $61$

## Explanation

The volume is the absolute value of the scalar triple product. The determinant with the three vectors as columns has contributions $abc$ and $1$. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$, $c=-3$ gives $59$.
