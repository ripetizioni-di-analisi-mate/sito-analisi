What is the area of the parallelogram having as sides the vectors $\vec{u} = (1, 1, 0)$ and $\vec{v} = (1, 2, 2)$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3\sqrt{2}$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

By the formula for the cross product one has
$$
\vec{u} \times \vec{v} = \big( 1 \cdot 2 - 0 \cdot 2,\ \ 0 \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot 2,\ \ 1 \cdot 2 - 1 \cdot 1 \big) = (2, -2, 1) .
$$
The area of the parallelogram is the magnitude of the cross product, therefore it equals $\sqrt{4 + 4 + 1} = 3$. The product of the magnitudes $\|\vec{u}\|\,\|\vec{v}\| = \sqrt{2} \cdot 3 = 3\sqrt{2}$ would give the area only if the two vectors were orthogonal.
