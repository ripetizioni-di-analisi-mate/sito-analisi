The parallelogram with sides the vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ of space has area $5$. What is the area of the parallelogram with sides the vectors $2\vec{u} + \vec{v}$ and $\vec{u} - 3\vec{v}$?

- $25$
- $30$
- $35$
- $5$

## Explanation

By the linearity of the cross product and since $\vec{u} \times \vec{u} = \vec{v} \times \vec{v} = \vec{0}$,
$$
(2\vec{u} + \vec{v}) \times (\vec{u} - 3\vec{v}) = -6\,\vec{u} \times \vec{v} + \vec{v} \times \vec{u} .
$$
By anticommutativity $\vec{v} \times \vec{u} = -\,\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$, therefore the product equals $-7\,\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$. The required area is its magnitude, that is, $7\,\|\vec{u} \times \vec{v}\| = 7 \cdot 5 = 35$. The value $25$ is obtained by adding $\vec{v} \times \vec{u}$ as if it were equal to $\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$.
