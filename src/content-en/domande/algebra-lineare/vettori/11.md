- For $k = 4$ and for $k = -4$
- Only for $k = -4$
- For no value of $k$
- Only for $k = 4$

## Explanation

The two vectors are nonzero for every $k$, therefore by the **parallelism criterion** they are parallel if and only if $\vec{u} \times \vec{v} = \vec{0}$. In components
$$
\vec{u} \times \vec{v} = \big( 2 \cdot (-2) - (-1)\,k,\ \ (-1) \cdot 8 - k \cdot (-2),\ \ k \cdot k - 2 \cdot 8 \big) = (k - 4,\ 2k - 8,\ k^2 - 16) .
$$
The first two components vanish only for $k = 4$, and for $k = 4$ the third one vanishes too. The value $k = -4$ makes the third component vanish but not the first two, so the vectors are **parallel** only for $k = 4$, and in this case $\vec{v} = (8, 4, -2) = 2\,\vec{u}$.
