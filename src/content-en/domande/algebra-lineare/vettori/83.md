$P=\left(4,5\right)$, $Q=\left(1,7\right)$. What is the squared distance from $P$ to $Q$?

- $13$
- $-13$
- $5$
- $1$

## Explanation

The displacement has horizontal component $c$ and vertical component $2$. Its squared norm is the sum of the component squares. Substituting $c=-3$ gives $13$.
