Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = 4$, and let $A'$ be the matrix obtained from $A$ by the operation $R_2 \to 3 R_2 - 2 R_1$. What is $\det A'$?

- $4$
- $-8$
- $12$
- $-24$

## Explanation

The operation is carried out in two steps, first $R_2 \to 3 R_2$ and then $R_2 \to R_2 - 2 R_1$. The first step multiplies the second row by $3$, so it multiplies the determinant by $3$. The second step adds to the second row a multiple of the first, so it does not change the determinant. Therefore $\det A' = 3 \cdot 4 = 12$.
