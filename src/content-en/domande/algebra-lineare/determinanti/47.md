$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 3 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the determinant of the triangular matrix?

- $3$
- $-6$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

Expansion along the first column leaves one term. Repeating on the triangular minor gives the diagonal product. The result is $6$.
