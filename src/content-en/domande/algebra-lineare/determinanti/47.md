For which value of $a \in \mathbb{R}$ are the points $P = (1, 2)$, $Q = (4, a)$ and $R = (6, 12)$ collinear?

- $a = 6$
- $a = 7$
- $a = 8$
- $a = 10$

## Explanation

The three points are collinear if and only if the vectors $Q - P = (3, a - 2)$ and $R - P = (5, 10)$ are parallel, that is, if the parallelogram having them as sides has zero area. This happens if and only if the determinant of the matrix with the two vectors as rows is zero:
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 3 & a - 2 \\ 5 & 10 \end{vmatrix} = 30 - 5(a - 2) = 40 - 5a .
$$
The determinant vanishes for $5a = 40$, so the points are collinear for $a = 8$.
