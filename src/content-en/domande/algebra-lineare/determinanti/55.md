Let
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 3 & -1 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
and let $C_{21}, C_{22}, C_{23}$ be the cofactors of the entries of the second row. What is the value of $a_{11} C_{21} + a_{12} C_{22} + a_{13} C_{23}$?

- $0$
- $-11$
- $11$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The cofactors $C_{21}, C_{22}, C_{23}$ do not depend on the entries of the second row. Hence the sum is the expansion along the second row of the determinant of the matrix obtained from $A$ by putting the first row, $(1, 2, 0)$, in place of the second. This matrix has two equal rows, therefore its determinant is zero and the sum equals $0$. The value $-11$ is $\det A$, which is obtained by using the entries of the second row with their own cofactors.
