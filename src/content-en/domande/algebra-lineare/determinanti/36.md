What is the inverse of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 4 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -2 & \dfrac{3}{2} \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -\dfrac{3}{2} & 1 \\ 2 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 \\ 1 & -\dfrac{3}{2} \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 2 & -\dfrac{3}{2} \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The determinant is $\det A = 3 \cdot 2 - 2 \cdot 4 = -2 \neq 0$, so $A$ is invertible. By the **formula for the inverse** of order $2$,
$$
A^{-1} = \frac{1}{-2} \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ -4 & 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Dividing each entry by $-2$ gives the matrix with rows $(-1, 1)$ and $\left( 2, -\dfrac{3}{2} \right)$.
