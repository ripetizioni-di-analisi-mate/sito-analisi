$d=-2$. An invertible matrix $A$ of order $3$ satisfies $\det A=d$. What is $\det(2A^{-1})$?

- $-4$
- $-1$
- $-16$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The inverse has the reciprocal determinant. Scaling all three rows by two multiplies that determinant by eight. The result is $-4$.
