$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}$. Which of $t=-a$, $t=0$, $t=a$, or $t=a+1$ makes $\det(A+tI)=0$?

- $-4$
- $0$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The matrix remains triangular with diagonal $a+t,b+t$. Among the listed values, $-a$ makes the first factor zero. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$ gives $-4$.
