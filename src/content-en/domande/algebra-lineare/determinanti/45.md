A matrix $A$ has rows $r_1,r_2,r_3$ and determinant $-5$. Matrix $B$ has rows $r_1,\,2r_2-3r_3,\,4r_3$. What is $\det B$?

- $-40$
- $-10$
- $20$
- $40$

## Explanation

Factor four from the last row. By linearity in the second row, the contribution from $-3r_3$ vanishes because it repeats the third row. The remaining term is $4\cdot2\det A=-40$.
