For which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the matrix
$$
A_k = \begin{pmatrix} k & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & k & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & k \end{pmatrix}
$$
invertible?

- $k \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-1, 0, 1\}$
- $k \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-1, 1\}$
- $k \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$
- $k \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0, 1\}$

## Explanation

The second row has the only nonzero entry $a_{22} = k$, with sign $(-1)^{2+2} = +1$. By **Laplace's theorem**, therefore,
$$
\det A_k = k \begin{vmatrix} k & 1 \\ 1 & k \end{vmatrix} = k (k^2 - 1) ,
$$
which vanishes for $k = 0$ and for $k = \pm 1$. Therefore, by the invertibility criterion, $A_k$ is invertible for $k \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-1, 0, 1\}$.
