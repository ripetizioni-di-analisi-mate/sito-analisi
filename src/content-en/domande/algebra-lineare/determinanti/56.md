$P=\left(2,0\right)$, $Q=\left(4,3\right)$, $R=\left(2,1\right)$. What is twice the signed area of triangle $PQR$ in this order?

- $6$
- $-2$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Use the vectors from $P$ to $Q$ and from $P$ to $R$ as columns. Their specified order fixes the sign. The result is $2$.
