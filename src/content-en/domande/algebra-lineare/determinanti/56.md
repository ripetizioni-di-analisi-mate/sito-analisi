Let $A = (a_{ij}) \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = 2$, and let $B \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be the matrix with entries $b_{ij} = i \, j \, a_{ij}$. What is $\det B$?

- $12$
- $36$
- $72$
- $144$

## Explanation

The matrix $B$ is obtained from $A$ by multiplying row $i$ by $i$ and then column $j$ by $j$. Multiplying the rows by $1$, $2$, $3$ multiplies the determinant by $1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 = 6$. Since $\det A^T = \det A$, the same property holds for columns, so multiplying the columns by $1$, $2$, $3$ multiplies the determinant by $6$ again. Therefore $\det B = 6 \cdot 6 \cdot 2 = 72$.
