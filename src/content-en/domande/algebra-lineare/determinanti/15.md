What is the determinant of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 & -2 \\ 4 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $7$
- $9$
- $-7$
- $-15$

## Explanation

**Sarrus' rule** applies. The products of the diagonals going down to the right are $2 \cdot 3 \cdot 0 = 0$, $0 \cdot (-2) \cdot 4 = 0$ and $1 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 1$, with sum $1$. The products of the diagonals going down to the left are $1 \cdot 3 \cdot 4 = 12$, $2 \cdot (-2) \cdot 1 = -4$ and $0 \cdot 1 \cdot 0 = 0$, with sum $8$. Hence $\det A = 1 - 8 = -7$.
