Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be the matrix such that, for every $v = (x, y, z)^T$,
$$
Av = \begin{pmatrix} x + y \\ y + z \\ x + z \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is $\det A$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Component $i$ of the product $Av$ is the product of row $i$ of $A$ with $v$, so the rows of $A$ are the coefficients of $x, y, z$ in the three components:
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
By **Sarrus' rule** the three products along the diagonals going down to the right are $1$, $1$ and $0$, while the three products along the diagonals going down to the left are all zero. Therefore $\det A = (1 + 1 + 0) - 0 = 2$.
