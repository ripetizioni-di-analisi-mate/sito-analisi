What is the cofactor matrix $\operatorname{cof} A$ of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 5 \\ -2 & 4 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 2 \\ -5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -5 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 \\ 5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -4 & -2 \\ 5 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The cofactor of $a_{ij}$ is $C_{ij} = (-1)^{i+j} \det A_{ij}$, and in order $2$ the minor $\det A_{ij}$ is the only entry left after deleting row $i$ and column $j$. Hence $C_{11} = 4$, $C_{12} = -(-2) = 2$, $C_{21} = -5$ and $C_{22} = 3$. Therefore
$$
\operatorname{cof} A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 2 \\ -5 & 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -5 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ is the transpose $(\operatorname{cof} A)^T$, that is, the adjugate matrix, which divided by $\det A = 22$ gives the inverse.
