$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 5 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & -3\end{pmatrix}$. What is the determinant of the triangular matrix?

- $20$
- $60$
- $-60$
- $6$

## Explanation

Expansion along the first column leaves one term. Repeating on the triangular minor gives the diagonal product. The result is $-60$.
