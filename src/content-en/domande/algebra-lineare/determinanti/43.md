What is the determinant of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 5 & 7 \\ 3 & 4 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 & 3 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $2$
- $24$
- $-2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The operation $R_2 \to R_2 - 3R_1$ does not change the determinant and turns the second row into $(0, -2, -14, -19)$. The first column then has the only nonzero entry $a_{11} = 1$, so by **Laplace's theorem**
$$
\det A = \begin{vmatrix} -2 & -14 & -19 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 5 & 3 \end{vmatrix} .
$$
Expanding again along the first column gives $-2 \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 5 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = -2 \cdot (6 - 5)$. Therefore $\det A = -2$.
