Let
$$
P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad D_t = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 5 & t \end{pmatrix}, \qquad t \in \mathbb{R},
$$
and let $C \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be any matrix. For which value of $t$ is the block matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} P & C \\ O & D_t \end{pmatrix} \in M_4(\mathbb{R})
$$
not invertible, whatever $C$ is?

- $t = \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $t = -2$
- $t = \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $t = 5$

## Explanation

The only nonzero entries of the first column of $A$ are $a_{11} = 1$ and $a_{21} = 3$, so by **Laplace's theorem** $\det A = 1 \cdot M_{11} - 3 \cdot M_{21}$, where $M_{11}$ and $M_{21}$ are the complementary minors. In both minors the first column has a single nonzero entry, $4$ in $M_{11}$ and $2$ in $M_{21}$, followed by the block $D_t$, therefore $M_{11} = 4 \det D_t$ and $M_{21} = 2 \det D_t$. This gives $\det A = (4 - 6) \det D_t = \det P \cdot \det D_t$, which does not depend on $C$. Since $\det P = -2$ and $\det D_t = 2t - 5$, the matrix $A$ is not invertible if and only if $t = \dfrac{5}{2}$. The value $-2$ is $\det P$, and the value $\dfrac{2}{5}$ is obtained by solving $5t - 2 = 0$ instead of $2t - 5 = 0$.
