The block matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}P&C\\0&D_t\end{pmatrix}$ has $P=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$ and $D_t=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\5&t\end{pmatrix}$. For which $t$ is it singular for every choice of the order-two block $C$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$

## Explanation

Expanding along the first two columns, the zero block leaves only $\det A=\det P\,\det D_t$. As $\det P=-2$ and $\det D_t=2t-5$, the determinant is zero at $t=\dfrac{5}{2}$, independently of the upper-right block.
