A block diagonal matrix has blocks $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix}1&a\\a&1\end{pmatrix}$. Its determinant vanishes for every $a$. For which $a$ does its kernel have dimension exactly two?

- $a=\pm1$
- $a=0$
- For every $a\in\mathbb R$.
- For no $a\in\mathbb R$.

## Explanation

The first block always has rank one. The second block must have rank one too because their nullities add. Its determinant is $1-a^2$ and its diagonal is nonzero, so exactly $a=\pm1$ work.
