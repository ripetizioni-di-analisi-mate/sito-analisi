Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be such that in every row the sum of the three entries is $0$. Which statement is true?

- $A$ is invertible
- $\det A = a_{11} a_{22} a_{33}$
- $A$ is not invertible
- $A$ is invertible if and only if it has no zeros on the diagonal

## Explanation

One performs the column operation $C_1 \to C_1 + C_2 + C_3$, which adds multiples of other columns to the first one and therefore does not change the determinant. The new entry of row $i$ in the first column is $a_{i1} + a_{i2} + a_{i3} = 0$, therefore the first column becomes zero. A matrix with a zero column has zero determinant, so $\det A = 0$. By the invertibility criterion, $A$ **is not invertible**. For example $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & -3 \\ 4 & -1 & -3 \\ 0 & 5 & -5 \end{pmatrix}$ has no zeros on the diagonal and determinant $0$.
