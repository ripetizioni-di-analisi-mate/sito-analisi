$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the coefficient of $t$ in $\det(A+tI)$?

- $3$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

The main-diagonal product is $(a+t)(c+t)$. The off-diagonal term is constant, so the linear coefficient is the sum of the diagonal entries. Substituting $a=2$, $c=1$ gives $3$.
