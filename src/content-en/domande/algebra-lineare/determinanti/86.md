$P=\left(4,0\right)$, $Q=\left(6,5\right)$, $R=\left(4,-3\right)$. What is twice the signed area of triangle $PQR$ in this order?

- $6$
- $-6$
- $-3$
- $10$

## Explanation

Use the vectors from $P$ to $Q$ and from $P$ to $R$ as columns. Their specified order fixes the sign. The result is $-6$.
