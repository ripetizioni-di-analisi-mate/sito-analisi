$d_A=4$, $d_B=5$. For two invertible matrices of equal size, $d_A=\det A$ and $d_B=\det B$. What is $\det(B^{-1}A^{\mathsf T})$?

- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $20$

## Explanation

The determinant is multiplicative. Transposition preserves $d_A$, while inversion replaces $d_B$ by its reciprocal. The result is $\dfrac{4}{5}$.
