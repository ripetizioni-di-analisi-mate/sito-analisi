Let $A, B \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be invertible with
$$
A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the inverse of $AB$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 7 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 7 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 7 & -2 \\ -3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 3 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The product $B^{-1} A^{-1}$ multiplied by $AB$ gives $B^{-1} (A^{-1} A) B = B^{-1} B = I_2$, so by the inverse from a single equality $(AB)^{-1} = B^{-1} A^{-1}$, with the factors in reverse order. The row-by-column product gives
$$
B^{-1} A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 7 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 7 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is the product $A^{-1} B^{-1}$, which in this case is different from $B^{-1} A^{-1}$.
