What is the area of the parallelogram with two consecutive sides on the vectors $u = (2, -1)$ and $v = (3, 4)$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $22$
- $11$

## Explanation

The area of the parallelogram is the absolute value of the determinant of the matrix with $u$ and $v$ as rows. The determinant equals
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 3 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 2 \cdot 4 - (-1) \cdot 3 = 8 + 3 = 11 ,
$$
so the area is $11$. The value $\dfrac{11}{2}$ is the area of the triangle with sides $u$ and $v$, which is half of the parallelogram.
