Which of the following matrices $A$ defines a map $x \mapsto Ax$ of the plane that preserves areas and reverses orientation?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The map $x \mapsto Ax$ multiplies areas by $|\det A|$, so it preserves areas if and only if $|\det A| = 1$. The square with sides $e_1, e_2$ goes to the parallelogram with sides $Ae_1, Ae_2$, which are the columns of $A$; since $\det A^T = \det A$, the sign of $\det A$ gives the direction of the shortest rotation taking $Ae_1$ to $Ae_2$. Therefore the orientation is reversed if and only if $\det A < 0$. The four determinants are
$$
2 - 1 = 1, \qquad 1 - 4 = -3, \qquad 1 - 0 = 1, \qquad -1 - 0 = -1 .
$$
Hence only $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ has $\det A = -1$ and satisfies both requirements.
