## Explanation

The operations $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$, $R_3 \to R_3 + R_1$ and $R_4 \to R_4 - 3R_1$ make the first column zero below $a_{11} = 1$, so
$$
\det A = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} .
$$
With $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$ the third row becomes $(0, 0, 4, -1)$, and with $R_4 \to R_4 + \dfrac{1}{4} R_3$ the fourth row becomes $\left(0, 0, 0, \dfrac{3}{4}\right)$. All the operations used are of the type $R_i \to R_i + h R_j$, so the determinant does not change. The final matrix is triangular, hence $\det A = 1 \cdot 1 \cdot 4 \cdot \dfrac{3}{4} = 3$.
