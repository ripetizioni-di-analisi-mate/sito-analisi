The third row has the only nonzero entry $a_{33} = 3$, with sign $(-1)^{3+3} = +1$. By **Laplace's theorem**, therefore,
$$
\det A = 3 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 3 & 2 & 0 & 4 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} .
$$
In this determinant the second column has the only nonzero entry $2$, in position $(2, 2)$ with sign $+1$, so it equals $2 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix}$. Expanding along the third column, this last determinant equals $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1 - 2 = -1$. Therefore $\det A = 3 \cdot 2 \cdot (-1) = -6$.
