In a Laplace expansion of a $5\times5$ matrix, the minor obtained by deleting the third row and third column equals $-2$. If only $a_{33}$ is increased from $3$ to $5$, by how much does the determinant change?

- $4$
- $-10$
- $-4$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The cofactor is $(-1)^{3+3}(-2)=-2$ and stays unchanged because the minor excludes the modified entry. The increment is the change in the entry times its cofactor, $(5-3)(-2)=-4$.
