Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$. For which $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$ is the matrix $A - \lambda I_2$ not invertible?

- $\lambda = 3$ and $\lambda = 4$
- $\lambda = -5$ and $\lambda = -2$
- $\lambda = 1$ and $\lambda = 6$
- $\lambda = 2$ and $\lambda = 5$

## Explanation

By the invertibility criterion, $A - \lambda I_2$ is not invertible if and only if its determinant is zero. One has
$$
\det (A - \lambda I_2) = \begin{vmatrix} 4 - \lambda & 1 \\ 2 & 3 - \lambda \end{vmatrix} = (4 - \lambda)(3 - \lambda) - 2 = \lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 10 .
$$
The polynomial factors as $(\lambda - 2)(\lambda - 5)$, so it vanishes for $\lambda = 2$ and $\lambda = 5$. The values $3$ and $4$ are obtained by forgetting the term $-2$, which comes from the off-diagonal entries.
