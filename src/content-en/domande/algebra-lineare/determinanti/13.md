- $\det A = 1$ or $\det A = -1$
- $\det A = 1$
- $\det A = 0$ or $\det A = 1$
- Every nonzero real number

## Explanation

By **Binet's theorem**, $\det (A^T A) = \det A^T \cdot \det A$, and since $\det A^T = \det A$ one has $\det (A^T A) = (\det A)^2$. On the other hand $A^T A = I_n$ and $\det I_n = 1$, so $(\det A)^2 = 1$. Therefore $\det A = 1$ or $\det A = -1$. Both values occur, because $I_n$ has determinant $1$ and the diagonal matrix with diagonal entries $-1, 1, \dots, 1$ satisfies $A^T A = I_n$ and has determinant $-1$.
