Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = 3$, and let $B = (\operatorname{cof} A)^T$ be its adjugate matrix. Which matrix is the adjugate of $B$, that is $(\operatorname{cof} B)^T$?

- $3A$
- $9A$
- $A$
- $\dfrac{1}{3} A$

## Explanation

By the **formula for the inverse**, every matrix $M$ with $\det M \neq 0$ has adjugate $(\operatorname{cof} M)^T = \det M \cdot M^{-1}$. Applied to $A$, the formula gives $B = 3A^{-1}$. The matrix has order $3$, so
$$
\det B = 3^3 \det (A^{-1}) = \frac{27}{3} = 9 \neq 0 ,
$$
and moreover $B^{-1} = \left( 3A^{-1} \right)^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{3} A$. Applying the same formula to $B$ one therefore gets
$$
(\operatorname{cof} B)^T = \det B \cdot B^{-1} = 9 \cdot \frac{1}{3} A = 3A .
$$
