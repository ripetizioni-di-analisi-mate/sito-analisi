$d=3$. Let $\det A=d$. To the second row of $A$, add three times the first row. What is the new determinant?

- $-3$
- $9$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

By multilinearity, the added term has two proportional rows and vanishes. The initial determinant remains. The result is $3$.
