Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be such that $A^2$ is the zero matrix. Which statement is true?

- $\det A = 1$
- $\det A = 1$ or $\det A = -1$
- $\det A = 0$
- $\det A$ can take any real value

## Explanation

By **Binet's theorem**, $\det (A^2) = \det A \cdot \det A = (\det A)^2$. The zero matrix has determinant $0$, so $(\det A)^2 = 0$ and therefore $\det A = 0$. By the invertibility criterion, $A$ is not invertible. Matrices of this kind exist that are different from the zero matrix, for example the one with $a_{12} = 1$ and all other entries equal to zero.
