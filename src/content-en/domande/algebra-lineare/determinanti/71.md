$P=\left(3,0\right)$, $Q=\left(5,-2\right)$, $R=\left(3,4\right)$. What is twice the signed area of triangle $PQR$ in this order?

- $8$
- $-4$
- $-8$
- $4$

## Explanation

Use the vectors from $P$ to $Q$ and from $P$ to $R$ as columns. Their specified order fixes the sign. The result is $8$.
