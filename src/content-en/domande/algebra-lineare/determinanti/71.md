Let $A \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ be such that $A^2 + 2A = I_n$. Which matrix is the inverse of $A$?

- $A - 2 I_n$
- $I_n - 2A$
- $A + 2 I_n$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}(I_n - A)$

## Explanation

Factoring $A$ out on the left, the equality becomes $A (A + 2 I_n) = I_n$. By the inverse from a single equality, a square matrix $C$ with $AC = I_n$ is the inverse of $A$. Hence $A$ is invertible and $A^{-1} = A + 2 I_n$. The result does not require computing any determinant.
