Let $A \in M_4(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = -3$. Which matrix is the product $A \, (\operatorname{cof} A)^T$ of $A$ and its adjugate matrix?

- $I_4$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3} I_4$
- $-27 I_4$
- $-3 I_4$

## Explanation

Since $\det A = -3 \neq 0$, by the **formula for the inverse** one has $A^{-1} = -\dfrac{1}{3} (\operatorname{cof} A)^T$, that is, $(\operatorname{cof} A)^T = -3 A^{-1}$. Hence $A \, (\operatorname{cof} A)^T = -3 A A^{-1} = -3 I_4$. The equality $A \, (\operatorname{cof} A)^T = \det A \cdot I_n$ holds for every square matrix, even with zero determinant, because it follows from Laplace's theorem.
