$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & -2 \\ 0 & 1 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. What is the determinant of the block matrix?

- $24$
- $18$
- $30$
- $10$

## Explanation

The top block contributes a factor $a$. The two-by-two block contributes its determinant. Substituting $a=3$ gives $30$.
