Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = 2$. What is the determinant of the adjugate matrix $(\operatorname{cof} A)^T$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $\det A \neq 0$, by the **formula for the inverse** $A^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{\det A} (\operatorname{cof} A)^T$, so $(\operatorname{cof} A)^T = \det A \cdot A^{-1} = 2 A^{-1}$. The matrix has order $3$, therefore $\det \left( 2 A^{-1} \right) = 2^3 \det (A^{-1})$. Since $\det (A^{-1}) = \dfrac{1}{2}$, the determinant of the adjugate matrix equals $8 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 4$.
