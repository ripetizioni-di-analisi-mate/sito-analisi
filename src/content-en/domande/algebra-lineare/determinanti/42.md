For $x \in \mathbb{R}$, what is the value of the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} x & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & x & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & x \end{vmatrix} ?
$$

- $x^3 - 1$
- $x^3 + 1$
- $x^3$
- $x^3 + x$

## Explanation

**Sarrus' rule** applies. The products of the diagonals going down to the right are $x \cdot x \cdot x = x^3$, $1 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 1$ and $0 \cdot 0 \cdot 0 = 0$, with sum $x^3 + 1$. Each of the products of the diagonals going down to the left, $0 \cdot x \cdot 1$, $x \cdot 1 \cdot 0$ and $1 \cdot 0 \cdot x$, contains a zero factor. Hence the determinant equals $x^3 + 1$.
