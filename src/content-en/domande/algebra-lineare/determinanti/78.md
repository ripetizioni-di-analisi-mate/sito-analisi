$d=5$. An invertible $3\times3$ matrix satisfies $\det A=d$. What is $\det(2A^{-1})$?

- $40$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The inverse has the reciprocal determinant. Scaling all three rows by two multiplies that determinant by eight. The result is $\dfrac{8}{5}$.
