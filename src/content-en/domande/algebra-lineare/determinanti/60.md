Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ with columns $c_1, c_2, c_3$ and $\det A = 2$. What is the determinant of the matrix $B$ with columns $c_1 + c_2$, $c_2 + c_3$, $c_3 + c_1$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

Each column of $B$ is $A$ times a column vector: $c_1 + c_2 = A (1, 1, 0)^T$, $c_2 + c_3 = A (0, 1, 1)^T$, $c_3 + c_1 = A (1, 0, 1)^T$. Hence $B = AM$ with
$$
M = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Expansion along the first row gives $\det M = 1 \cdot 1 - 0 + 1 \cdot 1 = 2$. By **multiplicativity of the determinant** $\det B = \det A \cdot \det M = 2 \cdot 2 = 4$.
