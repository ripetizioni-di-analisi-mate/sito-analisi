$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. What is $\det(A^{\mathsf T}A)$?

- $4$
- $-16$
- $32$
- $16$

## Explanation

Transposition preserves the determinant. The product therefore has the square of the determinant of triangular $A$. The result is $16$.
