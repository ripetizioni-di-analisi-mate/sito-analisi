$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. What is $\det(A^{\mathsf T}A)$?

- $4$
- $-16$
- $32$
- $16$

## Explanation

Transposition preserves the determinant. Therefore the determinant of the product is the square of that of $A$, which is triangular. The result is $16$.
