$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the determinant of the block matrix?

- $-1$
- $-2$
- $4$
- $10$

## Explanation

The top block contributes a factor $a$. The two-by-two block contributes its determinant. Substituting $a=2$ gives $-2$.
