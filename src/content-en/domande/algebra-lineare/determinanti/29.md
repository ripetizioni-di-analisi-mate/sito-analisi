The matrix $A$ of order four has $4$ on the diagonal and $1$ elsewhere. Subtract the same number $t$ from **every** entry. For which $t$ does the new matrix have zero row sums and hence become singular?

- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Each row initially has sum $4+1+1+1=7$. The four subtractions change it to $7-4t$, which vanishes at $t=\dfrac{7}{4}$. The column vector of all ones then belongs to the kernel.
