What is the determinant of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 4 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 4 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $189$
- $81$
- $63$
- $108$

## Explanation

Each row has sum $4 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 7$, so adding the other three columns to the first one gives the first column $(7, 7, 7, 7)$, without changing the determinant. The factor $7$ is taken out of the first column, and the first column becomes $(1, 1, 1, 1)$. The operations $R_i \to R_i - R_1$ for $i = 2, 3, 4$ do not change the determinant and give
$$
\det A = 7 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 3 \end{vmatrix} .
$$
The resulting matrix is triangular, therefore $\det A = 7 \cdot 3^3 = 189$.
