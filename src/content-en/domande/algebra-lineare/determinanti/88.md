$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 5 \\ 0 & 1 & -3\end{pmatrix}$. What is the determinant of the block matrix?

- $-4$
- $-11$
- $-24$
- $-44$

## Explanation

The top block contributes a factor $a$. The two-by-two block contributes its determinant. Substituting $a=4$ gives $-44$.
