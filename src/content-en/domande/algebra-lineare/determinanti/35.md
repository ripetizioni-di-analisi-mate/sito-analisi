Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$. What is the area of the image, under the map $x \mapsto Ax$, of a region of the plane with area $2$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $10$
- $14$

## Explanation

The absolute value $|\det A|$ is the factor by which the map $x \mapsto Ax$ multiplies areas. The determinant equals $\det A = 3 \cdot 2 - 1 \cdot 1 = 5$. Hence the image of a region with area $2$ has area $5 \cdot 2 = 10$.
