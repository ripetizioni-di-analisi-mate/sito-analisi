- $-1$
- $1$
- $2a^2 - 1$
- $a^2 - 1$

## Explanation

By the definition of the determinant of order $2$,
$$
\begin{vmatrix} a + 1 & a \\ a & a - 1 \end{vmatrix} = (a + 1)(a - 1) - a \cdot a .
$$
Since $(a + 1)(a - 1) = a^2 - 1$, the determinant equals $a^2 - 1 - a^2 = -1$. Therefore the determinant equals $-1$ for every $a \in \mathbb{R}$, that is, it **does not depend on the parameter**.
