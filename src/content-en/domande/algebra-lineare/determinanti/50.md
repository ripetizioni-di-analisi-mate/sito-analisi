The figure shows the vectors $u$ and $v$ of the plane, with integer components. What is the value of the determinant of the $2 \times 2$ matrix with $u$ as first column and $v$ as second column?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,1,3,u;0,0,2,-1,v" segmenti="1,0,1,3;0,3,1,3;2,0,2,-1;0,-1,2,-1" xmin=-1.5 xmax=3.5 ymin=-2 ymax=3.8 uguale=1 titolo="The vectors $u$ and $v$"}

- $7$
- $-7$
- $5$
- $-5$

## Explanation

From the figure one reads $u = (1, 3)$ and $v = (2, -1)$. The matrix with $u$ and $v$ as columns is
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix},
$$
so the determinant equals $1 \cdot (-1) - 2 \cdot 3 = -7$. The sign is negative because the shortest rotation taking $u$ onto $v$ is clockwise, and the absolute value $7$ is the area of the parallelogram with sides $u$ and $v$.
