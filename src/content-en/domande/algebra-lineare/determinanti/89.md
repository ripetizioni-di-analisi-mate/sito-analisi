$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ 2 & -3\end{pmatrix}$. What is the coefficient of $t$ in $\det(A+tI)$?

- $9$
- $-8$
- $1$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The main-diagonal product is $(a+t)(c+t)$. The off-diagonal term is constant, so the linear coefficient is the diagonal sum. Substituting $a=4$, $c=-3$ gives $1$.
