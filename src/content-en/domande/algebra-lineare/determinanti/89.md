For which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the determinant of the matrix
$$
A_k = \begin{pmatrix} k & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & k - 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
positive?

- $k \in (-1, 2)$
- $k \in (-\infty, -1) \cup (2, +\infty)$
- $k \in (-\infty, -2) \cup (1, +\infty)$
- $k \in (2, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The third row has only one nonzero entry, $a_{33} = 1$, so expansion along the third row gives
$$
\det A_k = 1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} k & 2 \\ 1 & k - 1 \end{vmatrix} = k(k - 1) - 2 = k^2 - k - 2 .
$$
The polynomial factors as $(k - 2)(k + 1)$ and has roots $-1$ and $2$. A polynomial of degree two with positive leading coefficient is positive outside the interval between the roots. Therefore $\det A_k > 0$ for $k \in (-\infty, -1) \cup (2, +\infty)$.
