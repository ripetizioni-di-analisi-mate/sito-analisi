$d=3$. An invertible matrix $A$ of order $3$ satisfies $\det A=d$. What is $\det(2A^{-1})$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $24$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

The inverse has the reciprocal determinant. Scaling all three rows by two multiplies that determinant by eight. The result is $\dfrac{8}{3}$.
