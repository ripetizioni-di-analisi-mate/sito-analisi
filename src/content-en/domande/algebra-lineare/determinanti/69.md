$A=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 3 & 0 \\ -2 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. What is the value of the determinant obtained by expanding along the third column?

- $6$
- $-24$
- $24$
- $-48$

## Explanation

Only the bottom entry contributes in the third column. Its minor is a two-by-two determinant with zero top-left entry. The result is $24$.
