Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ with columns $c_1, c_2, c_3$ such that $c_1 - c_2 + 2 c_3 = 0$. What is $\det A$?

- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

From the relation one gets $c_1 = c_2 - 2 c_3$, so the first column is a linear combination of the other two. The column operation $C_1 \to C_1 - C_2 + 2 C_3$ does not change the determinant and makes the first column zero. A matrix with a zero column has zero determinant, therefore $\det A = 0$. The coefficients $1$, $-1$, $2$ of the relation do not enter the value of the determinant.
