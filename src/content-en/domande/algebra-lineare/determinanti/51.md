For the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & -1 \\ 5 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
which expression is the Laplace expansion of $\det A$ along the second column?

- $1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix} + 4 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix}$
- $1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix} - 4 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix}$
- $-1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix} + 4 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix}$
- $-1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 4 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{vmatrix} + 4 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix}$

## Explanation

The entries of the second column are $a_{12} = 1$, $a_{22} = 4$ and $a_{32} = 0$, and the term with $a_{32} = 0$ is zero. The signs $(-1)^{i+2}$ of the second column are $-$, $+$, $-$, so $a_{12}$ has a minus sign and $a_{22}$ has a plus sign. The minor of $a_{12}$ is obtained by deleting the first row and the second column, and equals $\begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix}$. The minor of $a_{22}$ is obtained by deleting the second row and the second column, and equals $\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix}$. Therefore the expansion is $-1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix} + 4 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = -5 - 44 = -49$.
