$d_A=2$, $d_B=3$. For two invertible matrices of equal size, $d_A=\det A$ and $d_B=\det B$. What is $\det(B^{-1}A^{\mathsf T})$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The determinant is multiplicative. Transposition preserves $d_A$, while inversion replaces $d_B$ by its reciprocal. The result is $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
