$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. What is $\det(A^{\mathsf T}A)$?

- $-64$
- $64$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

Transposition preserves the determinant. The product therefore has the square of the determinant of triangular $A$. The result is $64$.
