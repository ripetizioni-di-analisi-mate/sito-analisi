$d=4$. A matrix of order $3$ has determinant $d$. Two columns are swapped and then one row is doubled. What is the new determinant?

- $-4$
- $-32$
- $8$
- $-8$

## Explanation

Swapping columns changes the sign. Doubling a single row multiplies the determinant by two. The result is $-8$.
