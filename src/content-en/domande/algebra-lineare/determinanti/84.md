$A=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 4 & 0 \\ 5 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & -3\end{pmatrix}$. What determinant results by expanding along the third column?

- $-60$
- $-120$
- $-20$
- $60$

## Explanation

Only the bottom entry contributes in the third column. Its minor is a two-by-two determinant with zero top-left entry. The result is $60$.
