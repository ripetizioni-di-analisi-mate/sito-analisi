What is the determinant of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 5 & -1 & 0 & 0 \\ 3 & 7 & 3 & 0 \\ -4 & 1 & 6 & \frac{1}{2} \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $3$
- $-3$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The entries above the main diagonal are all zero, so $A$ is lower triangular. By the theorem on the **determinant of a triangular matrix**, $\det A$ is the product of the diagonal entries, that is,
$$
\det A = 2 \cdot (-1) \cdot 3 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = -3 .
$$
The entries below the diagonal do not enter the computation.
