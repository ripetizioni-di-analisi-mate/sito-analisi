For which values of $t \in \mathbb{R}$ is the matrix
$$
A_t = \begin{pmatrix} t & 0 & 0 \\ 5 & t - 2 & 0 \\ 7 & 3 & t + 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
invertible?

- $t \neq 0$
- $t \notin \{ -1, 0, 2 \}$
- $t \notin \{ -2, 0, 1 \}$
- $t \notin \{ 0, 2 \}$

## Explanation

The entries above the main diagonal are zero, so $A_t$ is lower triangular. By the theorem on the **determinant of a triangular matrix** we have $\det A_t = t\,(t - 2)(t + 1)$, and the entries below the diagonal play no role. By the invertibility criterion $A_t$ is invertible if and only if $\det A_t \neq 0$, that is, for $t \notin \{ -1, 0, 2 \}$. The set $\{ -2, 0, 1 \}$ is obtained by changing the sign of the roots of the factors $t - 2$ and $t + 1$.
