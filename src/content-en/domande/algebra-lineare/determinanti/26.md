A lower triangular $4\times4$ matrix has diagonal $(2,-1,3,\dfrac{1}{2})$. Exactly one entry is changed. Which proposed change is guaranteed to double its determinant?

- Double an entry strictly below the diagonal.
- Replace the last diagonal entry by $1$.
- Change the sign of the last diagonal entry.
- Add one to the diagonal entry $-1$.

## Explanation

While the matrix stays triangular its determinant is the product of the diagonal entries. Changing $\dfrac{1}{2}$ to $1$ doubles one factor without changing the others. An entry below the diagonal has no effect on the product, and making the second diagonal entry zero makes the product vanish.
