## Explanation

The sides of the triangle starting from $P$ are the vectors $Q - P = (4, 1)$ and $R - P = (1, 4)$. The parallelogram with these two sides has area
$$
\left| \det \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix} \right| = |16 - 1| = 15 .
$$
The triangle $PQR$ is half of this parallelogram, so its area is $\dfrac{15}{2}$.
