Let $A_t \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be a matrix in which all entries are fixed except $a_{23} = t$. It is known that $\det A_0 = 3$ and $\det A_1 = 7$. What is $\det A_3$?

- $15$
- $21$
- $19$
- $12$

## Explanation

By **Laplace's theorem** along the second row, $\det A_t = a_{21} C_{21} + a_{22} C_{22} + t \, C_{23}$. The cofactors $C_{21}, C_{22}, C_{23}$ contain no entry of the second row, so they do not depend on $t$. Therefore $\det A_t = \alpha + \beta t$ with $\alpha$ and $\beta$ constant. The condition $\det A_0 = 3$ gives $\alpha = 3$, and the condition $\det A_1 = 7$ gives $\beta = 4$. Hence $\det A_3 = 3 + 4 \cdot 3 = 15$.
