For every $t \in \mathbb{R}$, what is the value of the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} \cos t & \sin t \\ \sin t & \cos t \end{vmatrix} ?
$$

- $1$
- $\cos 2t$
- $-\cos 2t$
- $\sin 2t$

## Explanation

By the definition of the determinant of order $2$, the determinant is $\cos t \cdot \cos t - \sin t \cdot \sin t = \cos^2 t - \sin^2 t$. By the double-angle formula for the cosine, $\cos^2 t - \sin^2 t = \cos 2t$, so the determinant equals $\cos 2t$. The value $1$ is obtained for the matrix with $-\sin t$ in position $(1, 2)$, whose determinant is $\cos^2 t + \sin^2 t$.
