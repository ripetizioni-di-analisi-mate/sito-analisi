## Explanation

The matrix $A^{-1} B^T$ has order $4$, so $\det \left( 2 A^{-1} B^T \right) = 2^4 \det \left( A^{-1} B^T \right)$. By **multiplicativity of the determinant**, $\det \left( A^{-1} B^T \right) = \det (A^{-1}) \cdot \det B^T$. Since $\det (A^{-1}) = \dfrac{1}{\det A} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\det B^T = \det B = 3$, one obtains
$$
\det \left( 2 A^{-1} B^T \right) = 16 \cdot \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) \cdot 3 = -24 .
$$
