Let $A, B \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = 3$ and $\det B = 2$. What is $\det \left( 3 A^{-1} B^2 \right)$?

- $12$
- $4$
- $108$
- $6$

## Explanation

The matrix $A^{-1} B^2$ has order $2$, so $\det \left( 3 A^{-1} B^2 \right) = 3^2 \det \left( A^{-1} B^2 \right)$. By **Binet's theorem**, $\det \left( A^{-1} B^2 \right) = \det (A^{-1}) \cdot (\det B)^2$. Since $\det (A^{-1}) = \dfrac{1}{\det A} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, one obtains
$$
\det \left( 3 A^{-1} B^2 \right) = 9 \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot 4 = 12 .
$$
