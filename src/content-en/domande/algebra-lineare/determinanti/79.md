$d=-3$. A matrix of order $3$ has determinant $d$. Two columns are swapped and then one row is doubled. What is the new determinant?

- $6$
- $3$
- $24$
- $-6$

## Explanation

Swapping columns changes the sign. Doubling a single row multiplies the determinant by two. The result is $6$.
