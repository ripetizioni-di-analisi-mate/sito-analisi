For $a, b, c \in \mathbb{R}$, what is the value of the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 1 & a & b + c \\ 1 & b & c + a \\ 1 & c & a + b \end{vmatrix} ?
$$

- $a + b + c$
- $(a - b)(b - c)(c - a)$
- $(a + b + c)(a - b)(b - c)(c - a)$
- $0$

## Explanation

One performs the column operation $C_3 \to C_3 + C_2$, which does not change the determinant. In every row the new entry of the third column is $a + b + c$, so the third column becomes $(a + b + c)$ times the first column $(1, 1, 1)$. A matrix with two proportional columns has zero determinant, therefore the determinant equals $0$ for all $a, b, c$.
