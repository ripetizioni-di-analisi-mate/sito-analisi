For $k \in \mathbb{R}$ let $A_k = \begin{pmatrix} k & 1 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. For which values of $k$ does $\det \left( A_k^2 \right) = 9$ hold?

- $k = 5$
- $k = 1$ and $k = -5$
- $k = -1$ and $k = 5$
- $k = \dfrac{13}{2}$

## Explanation

The determinant of $A_k$ is $k \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot 2 = k - 2$. By the **multiplicativity of the determinant** $\det \left( A_k^2 \right) = (\det A_k)^2 = (k - 2)^2$, so the equation becomes $(k - 2)^2 = 9$. Therefore $k - 2 = 3$ or $k - 2 = -3$, that is $k = 5$ or $k = -1$. With the single value $k = 5$ the solution with $k - 2 < 0$ is lost.
