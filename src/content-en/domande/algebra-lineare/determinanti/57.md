$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3\end{pmatrix}$. Which value of $t$ makes $\det(A+tI)=0$ if we require $t=-a$ or $t=0$ or $t=a$ or $t=a+1$?

- $3$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The matrix remains triangular with diagonal $a+t,b+t$. Among the listed values, $-a$ makes the first factor zero. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$ gives $-2$.
