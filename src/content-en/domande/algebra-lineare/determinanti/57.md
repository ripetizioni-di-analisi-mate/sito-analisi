Let $r_1, r_2, r_3, s$ be row vectors of $\mathbb{R}^3$. The matrix with rows $r_1, r_2, r_3$ has determinant $2$ and the matrix with rows $r_1, s, r_3$ has determinant $5$. What is the determinant of the matrix with rows $r_1$, $2 r_2 + s$, $r_3$?

- $7$
- $14$
- $-1$
- $9$

## Explanation

The second row is the sum of $2 r_2$ and $s$. By the sum property, the determinant is the sum of the determinants of the matrices with rows $r_1, 2r_2, r_3$ and $r_1, s, r_3$. The first equals $2 \cdot 2 = 4$, because the second row is multiplied by $2$; the second equals $5$. Hence the required determinant equals $4 + 5 = 9$.
