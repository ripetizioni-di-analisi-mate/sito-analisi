Let $u = (1, 2)$ and $v = (3, 1)$. Which statement about the parallelogram with two consecutive sides on $u$ and $v$ is true?

- The area is $5$ and the shortest rotation taking $u$ to $v$ is clockwise
- The area is $5$ and the shortest rotation taking $u$ to $v$ is counterclockwise
- The area is $7$ and the shortest rotation taking $u$ to $v$ is clockwise
- The area is $7$ and the shortest rotation taking $u$ to $v$ is counterclockwise

## Explanation

The determinant of the matrix with $u$ and $v$ as rows is $1 \cdot 1 - 2 \cdot 3 = -5$. The area of the parallelogram is the absolute value of the determinant, so it equals $5$. The determinant is negative, therefore the shortest rotation taking $u$ to $v$ is **clockwise**. The same can be seen from the directions of the two vectors, both in the first quadrant. The vector $u$ has slope $2$ and the vector $v$ has slope $\dfrac{1}{3}$, so $v$ forms a smaller angle with the $x$-axis than $u$ does.
