$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & -2 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. What is the determinant of the triangular matrix?

- $5$
- $-24$
- $-6$
- $24$

## Explanation

Expansion along the first column leaves one term. Repeating on the triangular minor gives the diagonal product. The result is $-24$.
