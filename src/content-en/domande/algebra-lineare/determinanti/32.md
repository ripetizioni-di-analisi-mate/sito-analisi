Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be such that $A^3 = 2 I_3$. What is $\det A$?

- $\sqrt[3]{2}$
- $8$
- $\pm 2$
- $2$

## Explanation

By **multiplicativity of the determinant**, $\det (A^3) = (\det A)^3$. The matrix $2 I_3$ has order $3$, so $\det (2 I_3) = 2^3 \det I_3 = 8$. Therefore $(\det A)^3 = 8$, and the only real solution of this equation is $\det A = 2$. The matrix $\sqrt[3]{2}\, I_3$ satisfies the hypothesis and has determinant $2$.
