For which value of $t \in \mathbb{R}$ is the matrix
$$
A_t = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 \\ 2 & t & 10 \end{pmatrix}
$$
not invertible?

- $t = -3$
- $t = 5$
- $t = -5$
- $t = \dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Expanding along the first column, which has a zero in position $(2, 1)$, gives
$$
\det A_t = 1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ t & 10 \end{vmatrix} + 2 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 1 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = (10 - 4t) + 2 \cdot 5 = 20 - 4t .
$$
By the **invertibility criterion**, $A_t$ is not invertible exactly when $\det A_t = 0$, that is, for $t = 5$. For $t = 5$ the third row $(2, 5, 10)$ equals $2R_1 + R_2$, in agreement with the result.
