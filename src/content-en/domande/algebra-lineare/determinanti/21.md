Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = 2$. What is $\det (-3A)$?

- $-54$
- $-6$
- $54$
- $-18$

## Explanation

Multiplying $A$ by $-3$ means multiplying each of the three rows by $-3$. Each row multiplied by $-3$ multiplies the determinant by $-3$, so $\det (-3A) = (-3)^3 \det A$. Since $(-3)^3 = -27$, one obtains $\det (-3A) = -27 \cdot 2 = -54$.
