Let $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^3$ be two nonzero column vectors and let $M = u \, v^T \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$. Which statement is true?

- $\det M = u^T v$
- $\det M = \left( u^T v \right)^3$
- $M$ is invertible if and only if $u^T v \neq 0$
- $\det M = 0$

## Explanation

The entry in position $(i, j)$ of $M$ is $u_i v_j$, so row $i$ of $M$ is $u_i \, v^T$. Therefore all rows of $M$ are multiples of the same row vector $v^T$. Since $u \neq 0$, at least one row is $u_k v^T$ with $u_k \neq 0$, and each of the other two rows is a multiple of it. Two proportional rows give a zero determinant, so $\det M = 0$ and $M$ is not invertible, whatever the value of $u^T v$. For example, with $u = (1, 2, 3)^T$ and $v = (1, 1, 1)^T$ one has $u^T v = 6$, while the rows of $M$ are $(1, 1, 1)$, $(2, 2, 2)$ and $(3, 3, 3)$.
