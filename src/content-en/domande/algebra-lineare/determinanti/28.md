What is the determinant of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 2 & 5 & 0 & 7 \\ -1 & 0 & 3 & -2 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 & 5 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $2$
- $6$
- $-6$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The operations $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$, $R_3 \to R_3 + R_1$ and $R_4 \to R_4 - R_1$ do not change the determinant and make the first column zero below $a_{11} = 1$, so
$$
\det A = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} ,
$$
where the second step is the expansion along the first column, which has a single term with sign $+1$. Expanding again along the first column gives $1 \cdot (4 - 2) - 2 \cdot (4 - 2) = 2 - 4$. Therefore $\det A = -2$.
