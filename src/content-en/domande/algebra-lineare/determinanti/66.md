What is the value of the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 1001 & 1002 \\ 1003 & 1004 \end{vmatrix} ?
$$

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The column operation $C_2 \to C_2 - C_1$ does not change the determinant and replaces the large numbers of the second column with $1$ and $1$:
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 1001 & 1002 \\ 1003 & 1004 \end{vmatrix}
= \begin{vmatrix} 1001 & 1 \\ 1003 & 1 \end{vmatrix}
= 1001 - 1003 = -2 .
$$
The direct computation $1001 \cdot 1004 - 1002 \cdot 1003$ gives the same result, with longer products.
