$d_A=3$, $d_B=-2$. For two invertible matrices of equal size, $d_A=\det A$ and $d_B=\det B$. What is $\det(B^{-1}A^{\mathsf T})$?

- $-6$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The determinant is multiplicative. Transposition preserves $d_A$, while inversion replaces $d_B$ by its reciprocal. The result is $- \dfrac{3}{2}$.
