## Explanation

The first two columns are copied to the right of the matrix. The products of the diagonals going down to the right are $1 \cdot (-1) \cdot 3 = -3$, $3 \cdot 1 \cdot 0 = 0$ and $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 4 = 16$, with sum $13$. The products of the diagonals going down to the left are $2 \cdot (-1) \cdot 0 = 0$, $1 \cdot 1 \cdot 4 = 4$ and $3 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 = 18$, with sum $22$. Therefore, by **Sarrus' rule**, $\det A = 13 - 22 = -9$.
