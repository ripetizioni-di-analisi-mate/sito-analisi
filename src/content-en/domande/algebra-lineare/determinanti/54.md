$A=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 2 & 0 \\ 3 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the value of the determinant obtained by expanding along the third column?

- $12$
- $-6$
- $-12$
- $6$

## Explanation

Only the bottom entry contributes in the third column. Its minor is a two-by-two determinant with zero top-left entry. The result is $-6$.
