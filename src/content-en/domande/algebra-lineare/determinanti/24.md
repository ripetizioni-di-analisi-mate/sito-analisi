It is known that
$$
\begin{vmatrix} a & b & c \\ d & e & f \\ g & h & i \end{vmatrix} = 3 .
$$
What is the value of
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 2a & 2b & 2c \\ g & h & i \\ d + a & e + b & f + c \end{vmatrix} ?
$$

- $6$
- $-3$
- $-12$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The factor $2$ is taken out of the first row, so the determinant is twice that of the matrix with rows $(a, b, c)$, $(g, h, i)$, $(d + a, e + b, f + c)$. In this matrix the operation $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$ turns the third row into $(d, e, f)$ and does not change the determinant. Swapping then the second and third rows gives the original matrix, and the swap changes the sign of the determinant. Therefore the required determinant equals $2 \cdot (-3) = -6$.
