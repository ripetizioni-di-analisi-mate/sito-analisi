Let
$$
E_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
E_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 7 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
E_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is $\det (E_1 E_2 E_3)$?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $14$
- $-14$

## Explanation

The three matrices are elementary: $E_1$ is obtained from $I_3$ by the swap $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$, $E_2$ by the operation $R_3 \to R_3 + 7R_1$, $E_3$ by the operation $R_2 \to -2R_2$. By the determinant of elementary matrices, therefore, $\det E_1 = -1$, $\det E_2 = 1$ and $\det E_3 = -2$. By the **multiplicativity of the determinant** the determinant of the product is the product of the determinants, so
$$
\det (E_1 E_2 E_3) = (-1) \cdot 1 \cdot (-2) = 2 .
$$
