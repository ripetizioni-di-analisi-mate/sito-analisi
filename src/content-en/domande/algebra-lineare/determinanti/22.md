Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = 5$. The matrix $B$ is obtained from $A$ by the operations $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_3$, then $R_2 \to 4 R_2$, then $R_3 \to R_3 + 2 R_1$. What is $\det B$?

- $20$
- $-20$
- $-40$
- $40$

## Explanation

Swapping two rows changes the sign of the determinant, which becomes $-5$. Multiplying the second row by $4$ multiplies the determinant by $4$, which becomes $-20$. The operation $R_3 \to R_3 + 2 R_1$ adds to a row a multiple of another row, so it does not change the determinant. Therefore $\det B = -20$.
