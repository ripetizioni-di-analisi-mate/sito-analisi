- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ -7 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -7 \\ -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -7 \\ -1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 7 \\ 1 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The determinant is $\det A = 4 \cdot 2 - 7 \cdot 1 = 1 \neq 0$, so by the invertibility criterion $A$ is invertible. By the **formula for the inverse** of order $2$, the entries of the main diagonal are swapped, the other two entries change sign, and everything is divided by the determinant. Since the determinant equals $1$, one obtains
$$
A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -7 \\ -1 & 4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
