For the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 1 \\ 5 & 2 & -2 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
what is the cofactor $C_{23}$?

- $9$
- $-2$
- $-9$
- $2$

## Explanation

Deleting row $2$ and column $3$ leaves the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 5 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, so the **minor** is $\det A_{23} = 2 \cdot 2 - (-1) \cdot 5 = 9$. Since $2 + 3 = 5$ is odd, the sign $(-1)^{2+3}$ equals $-1$. Therefore $C_{23} = -9$.
