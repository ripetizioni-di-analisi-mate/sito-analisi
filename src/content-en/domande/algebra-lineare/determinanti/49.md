Let $A \in M_4(\mathbb{R})$ be the matrix with entries $a_{ij} = \min(i, j)$, that is,
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 2 & 2 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 & 3 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the value of $\det A$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $24$
- $1$

## Explanation

One performs, in this order, the operations $R_4 \to R_4 - R_3$, $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$ and $R_2 \to R_2 - R_1$, which add to a row a multiple of another row and therefore do not change the determinant. The new rows are $R_2 = (0, 1, 1, 1)$, $R_3 = (0, 0, 1, 1)$ and $R_4 = (0, 0, 0, 1)$, so the matrix becomes upper triangular with all diagonal entries equal to $1$. The determinant of a triangular matrix is the product of the diagonal entries, hence $\det A = 1$. The value $24$ is the product of the diagonal entries of $A$, which is not triangular.
