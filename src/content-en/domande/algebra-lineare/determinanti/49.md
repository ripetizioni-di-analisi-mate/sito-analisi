$d=1$. A matrix of order $3$ has determinant $d$. Two columns are swapped and then one row is doubled. What is the new determinant?

- $2$
- $-1$
- $-2$
- $-8$

## Explanation

Swapping columns changes the sign. Doubling a single row multiplies the determinant by two. The result is $-2$.
