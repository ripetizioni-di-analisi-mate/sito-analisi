$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & -2 \\ 2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. What is the coefficient of $t$ in $\det(A+tI)$?

- $12$
- $7$
- $1$
- $6$

## Explanation

The main-diagonal product is $(a+t)(c+t)$. The off-diagonal term is constant, so the linear coefficient is the diagonal sum. Substituting $a=3$, $c=4$ gives $7$.
