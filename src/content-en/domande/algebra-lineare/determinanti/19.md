What is the determinant of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 4 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 5 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $-12$
- $12$
- $6$
- $-24$

## Explanation

The first column has the only nonzero entry $a_{21} = 1$, with sign $(-1)^{2+1} = -1$. By **Laplace's theorem**, the expansion along the first column gives
$$
\det A = -\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 5 & 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} .
$$
The first row of this determinant has the only nonzero entry $2$, in position $(1, 1)$, so the determinant equals $2 \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 2 \cdot (0 - 6) = -12$. Therefore $\det A = -(-12) = 12$.
