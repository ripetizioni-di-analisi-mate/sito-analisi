$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & -2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. What is $\det(A^{\mathsf T}A)$?

- $36$
- $6$
- $-36$
- $9$

## Explanation

Transposition preserves the determinant. Therefore the determinant of the product is the square of that of $A$, which is triangular. The result is $36$.
