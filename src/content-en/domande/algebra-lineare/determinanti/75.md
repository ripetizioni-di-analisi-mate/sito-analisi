$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & -2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. What is $\det(A^{\mathsf T}A)$?

- $36$
- $6$
- $-36$
- $9$

## Explanation

Transposition preserves the determinant. The product therefore has the square of the determinant of triangular $A$. The result is $36$.
