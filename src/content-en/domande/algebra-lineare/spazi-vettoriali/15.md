Which of the following subsets of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ is a vector subspace?

- The matrices with zero determinant
- The invertible matrices
- The matrices whose entry in position $(1, 1)$ equals $1$
- The matrices with zero trace

## Explanation

The trace satisfies $\operatorname{tr}(A + B) = \operatorname{tr} A + \operatorname{tr} B$ and $\operatorname{tr}(\lambda A) = \lambda \operatorname{tr} A$, and the zero matrix has trace $0$. Hence the matrices with zero trace satisfy the three conditions and, by the **subspace criterion**, they form a **subspace**. The matrices $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ have zero determinant, while their sum is the identity matrix, which has determinant $1$. The invertible matrices and those whose entry in position $(1, 1)$ equals $1$ do not include the zero matrix.
