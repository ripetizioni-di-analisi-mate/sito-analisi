What is the dimension of the subspace of $\mathbb{R}^4$ formed by the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 - x_3 + x_4 = 0 \\[4pt]
2x_1 + 2x_2 - 2x_3 + 2x_4 = 0 \\[4pt]
x_2 + x_3 = 0
\end{cases}
$$

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The second equation is twice the first, so the system is equivalent to the one formed by the first and third equations. These two equations are not proportional, therefore the coefficient matrix has rank $2$. By the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the solutions depend on $4 - 2 = 2$ free parameters, so the subspace has dimension $2$. The value $4 - 3 = 1$ would be obtained by also counting the second equation, which is not independent of the first.
