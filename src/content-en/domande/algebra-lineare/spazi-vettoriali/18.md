Let $W = \left\{ p \in \mathbb{R}_2[x] : \displaystyle\int_0^1 p(x)\,dx = 0 \right\}$, which is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$. Which sequence is a basis of $W$?

- $(1 - 2x, \ 1 - 3x^2)$
- $(1 - 2x)$
- $(1 - 2x, \ 1 - x^2)$
- $(x, \ x^2 - x)$

## Explanation

For $p = a + bx + cx^2$ we have $\displaystyle\int_0^1 p(x)\,dx = a + \frac{b}{2} + \frac{c}{3}$, so $W$ is described by the single linear equation $a + \frac{b}{2} + \frac{c}{3} = 0$ in the coefficients. Therefore $\dim W = 3 - 1 = 2$, and a sequence made of a single polynomial is not a basis.

The polynomials $1 - 2x$ and $1 - 3x^2$ lie in $W$, since $1 - \frac{2}{2} = 0$ and $1 - \frac{3}{3} = 0$. They are not proportional, so they are independent. Since they are two independent vectors in a space of dimension $2$, they form **a basis of $W$**.

The polynomial $1 - x^2$ does not lie in $W$, because its integral is $1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$. The polynomial $x$ does not lie in $W$ either, because its integral is $\frac{1}{2}$.
