Which of the following subsets of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$ is a vector subspace?

- $\{ p \in \mathbb{R}_2[x] : p(1) = 0 \}$
- $\{ p \in \mathbb{R}_2[x] : p(0) = 1 \}$
- $\{ p \in \mathbb{R}_2[x] : p(0) \cdot p(1) = 0 \}$
- $\{ p \in \mathbb{R}_2[x] : p(1) = p(0) + 1 \}$

## Explanation

For the condition $p(1) = 0$ we have $(p + q)(1) = p(1) + q(1) = 0$ and $(\lambda p)(1) = \lambda\, p(1) = 0$, and the zero polynomial satisfies it; hence, by the **subspace criterion**, the set $\{ p : p(1) = 0 \}$ is a **subspace**. The zero polynomial satisfies neither $p(0) = 1$ nor $p(1) = p(0) + 1$. The set $\{ p : p(0) \cdot p(1) = 0 \}$ contains $p = x$, since $p(0) = 0$, and $q = 1 - x$, since $q(1) = 0$. Their sum is the constant polynomial $1$, which equals $1$ both at $0$ and at $1$. Since $1 \cdot 1 \neq 0$, the sum does not belong to this set, which is therefore not closed under addition.
