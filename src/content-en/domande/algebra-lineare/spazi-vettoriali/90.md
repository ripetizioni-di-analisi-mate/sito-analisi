$u=\left(1,4\right)$, $v=\left(0,5\right)$, $w=\left(-3,0\right)$. What is the second coordinate of $w$ in the ordered basis $(u,v)$?

- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $0$
- $- \dfrac{12}{5}$

## Explanation

The first coordinate in the basis is $c$. For the second standard component to be zero, offset $ac$ by a multiple of $b$. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$, $c=-3$ gives $\dfrac{12}{5}$.
