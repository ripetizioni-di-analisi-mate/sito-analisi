The set $V = \{ x \in \mathbb{R} : x > 0 \}$, with the sum $x \oplus y = xy$ and the scalar multiplication $\lambda \odot x = x^{\lambda}$, is a real vector space. What is the opposite of the vector $4$ in this space?

- $-4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The zero vector is the element $e$ such that $x \oplus e = x$ for every $x > 0$, that is, $xe = x$; hence the zero vector of $V$ is the number $1$.

The opposite of $4$ is the element $y > 0$ such that $4 \oplus y = 1$, that is, $4y = 1$. Therefore the opposite of $4$ is $y = \dfrac{1}{4}$, which coincides with $(-1) \odot 4 = 4^{-1}$, as the rule $(-1)\,v = -v$ predicts. The number $-4$ does not belong to $V$, and the number $0$ does not belong to $V$ and is not the zero vector.
