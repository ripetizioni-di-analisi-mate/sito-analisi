$u=\left(1,2,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,3\right)$, $w=\left(1,0,t\right)$. For which $t$ are the three vectors linearly dependent?

- $-6$
- $-12$
- $6$
- $0$

## Explanation

Three vectors in $\mathbb R^3$ are dependent when the column matrix is singular. Its determinant is $t+abc$. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$, $c=1$ gives $-6$.
