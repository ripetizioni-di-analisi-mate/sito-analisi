Which Cartesian equation describes the subspace $W = \langle (1, 1, 2), (2, 0, 1), (0, 2, 3), (3, 1, 3) \rangle \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$?

- $x - 3y + 2z = 0$
- $x + 3y - 2z = 0$
- $3x + y - 2z = 0$
- $x + 3y + 2z = 0$

## Explanation

We have $(0, 2, 3) = 2\,(1, 1, 2) - (2, 0, 1)$ and $(3, 1, 3) = (1, 1, 2) + (2, 0, 1)$, so the four generators lie in the subspace spanned by $(1, 1, 2)$ and $(2, 0, 1)$, which are not proportional. Therefore $\dim W = 2$ and $W$ is a plane. A vector $(x, y, z)$ belongs to $W$ if and only if
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 2 & x \\
1 & 0 & y \\
2 & 1 & z
\end{vmatrix}
= x + 3y - 2z = 0 .
$$
The two generators satisfy the equation, since $1 + 3 - 4 = 0$ and $2 + 0 - 2 = 0$.
