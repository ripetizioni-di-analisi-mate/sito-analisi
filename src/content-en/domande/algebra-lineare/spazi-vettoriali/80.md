Which of the following is a parametric representation of the plane $W : x - y + 2z = 0$ of $\mathbb{R}^3$?

- $\{ (s, s, t) : s, t \in \mathbb{R} \}$
- $\{ (2t, 0, -t) : t \in \mathbb{R} \}$
- $\{ (s, s + 2t, t) : s, t \in \mathbb{R} \}$
- $\{ (s, s - 2t, t) : s, t \in \mathbb{R} \}$

## Explanation

Substituting $(s, s + 2t, t)$ into the equation gives $s - (s + 2t) + 2t = 0$ for every $s, t$, so the set is contained in $W$. Moreover $(s, s + 2t, t) = s\,(1, 1, 0) + t\,(0, 2, 1)$, and the two generators are not proportional, therefore the set has dimension $2 = \dim W$ and, by the theorem on the dimension of a subspace, it **coincides with $W$**.

The set $\{ (2t, 0, -t) \}$ is contained in $W$, but it is a line. The sets $\{ (s, s, t) \}$ and $\{ (s, s - 2t, t) \}$ give $2t$ and $4t$ in the equation, so they are not contained in $W$.
