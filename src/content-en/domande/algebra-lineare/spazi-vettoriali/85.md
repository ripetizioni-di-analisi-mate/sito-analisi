$u=\left(1,4,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,0,1\right)$. Which vector extends $(u,v)$ to a basis of $\mathbb R^3$?

- $\left(1,4,0\right)$
- $\left(2,8,1\right)$
- $\left(0,1,0\right)$
- $\left(0,0,2\right)$

## Explanation

Every combination of $u,v$ satisfies $y=ax$. The vector $(0,1,0)$ does not satisfy that relation and adds an independent direction.
