What are the coordinates of the vector $v = (3, 7)$ with respect to the basis $\mathcal{B} = \big( (1, 2), (1, 3) \big)$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$?

- $(1, 2)$
- $(3, 7)$
- $(2, 1)$
- $(4, -1)$

## Explanation

We look for $a, b$ such that $a\,(1, 2) + b\,(1, 3) = (3, 7)$, that is, $a + b = 3$ and $2a + 3b = 7$. Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $b = 7 - 6 = 1$, so $a = 3 - 1 = 2$. Indeed $2\,(1, 2) + 1 \cdot (1, 3) = (3, 7)$. Therefore the **coordinates** of $v$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$ are $(2, 1)$.
