Let $D$ be the subspace of diagonal matrices of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$. Which of the following subspaces $W$ satisfies $M_2(\mathbb{R}) = D \oplus W$?

- The upper triangular matrices
- The symmetric matrices
- The matrices with zero diagonal entries
- The skew-symmetric matrices

## Explanation

Let $W = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} 0 & b \\ c & 0 \end{pmatrix} \right\}$, of dimension $2$. A matrix lying in both $D$ and $W$ has zero off-diagonal entries and zero diagonal entries, so $D \cap W = \{O\}$; moreover $\dim D + \dim W = 2 + 2 = 4 = \dim M_2(\mathbb{R})$.

By the criterion for complementary subspaces we then have **$M_2(\mathbb{R}) = D \oplus W$**. The upper triangular and the symmetric matrices contain $D$, so their intersection with $D$ is not zero. The skew-symmetric matrices have zero intersection with $D$, but $2 + 1 = 3 < 4$.
