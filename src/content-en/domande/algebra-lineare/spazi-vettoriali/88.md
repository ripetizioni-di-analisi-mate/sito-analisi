$u=\left(4,5,1\right)$, $v=\left(8,10,2\right)$, $w=\left(4,5,2\right)$. What is the dimension of $\operatorname{span}(u,v)\cap\operatorname{span}(w)$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first subspace is the line spanned by $u$ since $v=2u$. The direction of $w$ is different, so the lines intersect only at the origin. The result is $0$.
