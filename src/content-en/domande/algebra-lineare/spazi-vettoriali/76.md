Which of the following real vector spaces, with the usual operations, does not admit a finite spanning set?

- $\mathbb{R}^{100}$
- $M_{5}(\mathbb{R})$
- The space $\mathbb{R}[x]$ of all polynomials with real coefficients
- $\mathbb{R}_{10}[x]$

## Explanation

Let $p_1, \dots, p_k$ be polynomials, not all zero, and let $d$ be the largest of their degrees. Every linear combination of $p_1, \dots, p_k$ has degree at most $d$, so the polynomial $x^{d + 1}$ does not belong to $\langle p_1, \dots, p_k \rangle$.

Therefore no finite set of polynomials spans $\mathbb{R}[x]$, and **$\mathbb{R}[x]$ is not finitely generated**. The other spaces have finite bases, namely the standard bases with $100$ vectors for $\mathbb{R}^{100}$, $25$ matrices for $M_5(\mathbb{R})$ and $11$ polynomials $1, x, \dots, x^{10}$ for $\mathbb{R}_{10}[x]$.
