$(p,q)$, $p(x)=1+4x$, $q(x)=x$. What is the coefficient of $q$ in the representation of $1$ in the given basis?

- $4$
- $0$
- $-8$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $p$ must be one to obtain the constant term. Its $x$ term cancels with $-a q$. Substituting $a=4$ gives $-4$.
