Let $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. What is the dimension of the subspace $W = \{ A \in M_2(\mathbb{R}) : AB = BA \}$ of the matrices that commute with $B$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$. The two products are
$$
AB = \begin{pmatrix} a & a + b \\ c & c + d \end{pmatrix} , \qquad
BA = \begin{pmatrix} a + c & b + d \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Comparing the entries in position $(1, 1)$ gives $c = 0$, and comparing those in position $(1, 2)$ gives $a = d$; the entry in position $(2, 2)$ gives no further condition. Hence $W = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & a \end{pmatrix} \right\} = \langle I, \ B - I \rangle$, and the two matrices $I$ and $B - I = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ are not proportional. Therefore **$\dim W = 2$**.
