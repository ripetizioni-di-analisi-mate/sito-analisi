Let $U$ and $W$ be subspaces of $\mathbb{R}^5$ with $\dim U = 3$ and $\dim W = 4$. What is the minimum possible value of $\dim (U \cap W)$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

By **Grassmann's formula** we have $\dim (U \cap W) = \dim U + \dim W - \dim (U + W) = 7 - \dim (U + W)$. Since $U + W$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^5$, $\dim (U + W) \leq 5$, so $\dim (U \cap W) \geq 7 - 5 = 2$. The value $2$ is attained, for example, with $U = \langle e_1, e_2, e_3 \rangle$ and $W = \langle e_2, e_3, e_4, e_5 \rangle$, whose intersection is $\langle e_2, e_3 \rangle$.
