In $\mathbb{R}^3$ consider the planes $U : x + y - z = 0$ and $W : 2x - y = 0$. Which vector spans $U \cap W$?

- $(1, 2, -1)$
- $(2, 1, 3)$
- $(1, 2, 3)$
- $(1, -2, -1)$

## Explanation

The equations of $U \cap W$ are obtained by combining the equations of the two planes into one system:
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y - z = 0 \\[4pt]
2x - y = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The second equation gives $y = 2x$, and the first $z = x + y = 3x$. Hence $U \cap W = \langle (1, 2, 3) \rangle$, a line, in agreement with **Grassmann's formula**, which for two distinct planes of $\mathbb{R}^3$ gives $\dim (U \cap W) = 2 + 2 - 3 = 1$.
