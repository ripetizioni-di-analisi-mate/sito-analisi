In $M_3(\mathbb{R})$ let $U$ be the subspace of upper triangular matrices and $W$ the subspace of lower triangular matrices. What is $\dim (U + W)$?

- $9$
- $12$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

An upper triangular matrix of order $3$ has the $6$ entries on and above the diagonal free, so $\dim U = 6$; in the same way $\dim W = 6$. A matrix that is both upper and lower triangular has all entries off the diagonal equal to zero, therefore $U \cap W$ is the subspace of diagonal matrices and $\dim (U \cap W) = 3$.

By **Grassmann's formula**
$$
\dim (U + W) = 6 + 6 - 3 = 9 ,
$$
so **$U + W = M_3(\mathbb{R})$**. The value $12$ would be obtained by adding the dimensions without subtracting that of the intersection, but no subspace of $M_3(\mathbb{R})$ has dimension greater than $9$.
