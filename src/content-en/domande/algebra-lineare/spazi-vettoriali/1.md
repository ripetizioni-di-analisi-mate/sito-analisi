The set $W = \{ (x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x + y = z, \ x = 3y \}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$. Which vector forms a basis of $W$?

- $(1, 3, 4)$
- $(3, 1, 4)$
- $(3, 1, 2)$
- $(3, -1, 4)$

## Explanation

$W$ is the solution set of a homogeneous linear system, so it is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$. The second equation gives $x = 3y$, and substituting into the first one gives $z = 3y + y = 4y$. Therefore every vector of $W$ can be written as $(x, y, z) = y\,(3, 1, 4)$ with $y \in \mathbb{R}$, and $W = \langle (3, 1, 4) \rangle$ is a line through the origin. Since $(3, 1, 4) \neq 0$, this vector is independent and forms a **basis** of $W$.
