In $\mathbb R^3$ the available generators are $e_1,e_2,e_1+e_2,e_3$. How many of these four vectors can individually be removed while leaving a set that still spans the whole space?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Any pair among the first three vectors spans the plane of the first two coordinates, so each of those three can individually be removed. Removing $e_3$ loses the entire third coordinate.
