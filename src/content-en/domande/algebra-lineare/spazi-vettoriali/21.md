The vectors $v_1 = (1, 0, 0)$, $v_2 = (0, 1, 0)$, $v_3 = (1, 1, 0)$, $v_4 = (0, 0, 1)$ span $\mathbb{R}^3$. For how many indices $i \in \{ 1, 2, 3, 4 \}$ do the three vectors left after removing $v_i$ alone still span $\mathbb{R}^3$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

We have $v_3 = v_1 + v_2$, so also $v_1 = v_3 - v_2$ and $v_2 = v_3 - v_1$. Therefore each of the vectors $v_1, v_2, v_3$ is a linear combination of the other two. By the **removal of a redundant generator**, removing any one of these three vectors leaves the span equal to $\mathbb{R}^3$. Removing $v_4$, on the other hand, leaves three vectors with zero third component, which span only the plane $z = 0$. Therefore the required indices are $1, 2, 3$, and there are **three** of them. The value $1$ is obtained by regarding only $v_3$ as redundant, since it appears as the sum of the others.
