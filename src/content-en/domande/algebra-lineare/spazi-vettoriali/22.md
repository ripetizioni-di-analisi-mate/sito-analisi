For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ are the polynomials $p_1 = 1 + x$, $p_2 = x + x^2$, $p_3 = 1 + kx^2$ of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$ linearly dependent?

- $k = -1$
- $k = 1$
- $k = 0$
- $k = 2$

## Explanation

With respect to the standard basis $(1, x, x^2)$ the polynomials have coordinates $(1, 1, 0)$, $(0, 1, 1)$, $(1, 0, k)$, and they are dependent if and only if these vectors are. The matrix having them as columns has determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 1 \\
1 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & k
\end{vmatrix}
= 1 \cdot (k - 0) - 0 + 1 \cdot (1 - 0) = k + 1 .
$$
The determinant vanishes if and only if $k = -1$, so the polynomials are **dependent** only for $k = -1$. In that case $p_1 - p_2 = 1 - x^2 = p_3$ holds.
