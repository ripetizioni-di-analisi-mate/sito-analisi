In $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$ consider the subspaces $U = \{ p : p(0) = 0 \}$ and $W = \{ p : p(1) = 0 \}$. What is $\dim (U + W)$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

Writing $p = a_0 + a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + a_3 x^3$, the condition $p(0) = 0$ is the equation $a_0 = 0$ and the condition $p(1) = 0$ is the equation $a_0 + a_1 + a_2 + a_3 = 0$. Each one is a nontrivial equation in the coordinates, so $\dim U = \dim W = 4 - 1 = 3$.

The two equations together are independent, therefore $\dim (U \cap W) = 4 - 2 = 2$. By **Grassmann's formula** we get $\dim (U + W) = 3 + 3 - 2 = 4$, that is, $U + W = \mathbb{R}_3[x]$.
