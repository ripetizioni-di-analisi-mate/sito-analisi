Let $W = \{ (x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x \geq 0, \ y \geq 0 \}$. Which condition of the subspace criterion is not satisfied by $W$?

- $0 \in W$
- Closure under addition
- Closure under scalar multiplication
- The three conditions are satisfied, so $W$ is a subspace

## Explanation

The zero vector has both components equal to $0$, so it belongs to $W$. If $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ have nonnegative components, the sum $(x_1 + x_2, \ y_1 + y_2)$ has nonnegative components too, therefore $W$ is closed under addition. The vector $(1, 1)$ lies in $W$, while $(-1) \cdot (1, 1) = (-1, -1)$ does not belong to it. Hence closure under **scalar multiplication** is not satisfied and $W$ is not a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$.
