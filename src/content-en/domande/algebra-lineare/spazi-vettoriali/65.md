Let $\mathcal{B}$ be a basis of a vector space $V$ of dimension $3$. The vectors $v$ and $w$ have coordinates $(1, 2, 0)$ and $(0, 1, 3)$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$. What are the coordinates of $2v - w$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$?

- $(2, 5, 3)$
- $(2, 3, 3)$
- $(1, 3, -3)$
- $(2, 3, -3)$

## Explanation

Passing from vectors to coordinates with respect to a basis preserves sums and scalar multiples. Hence the coordinates of $2v - w$ are obtained from the same combination of the coordinates of $v$ and $w$:
$$
2\,(1, 2, 0) - (0, 1, 3) = (2, 4, 0) - (0, 1, 3) = (2, 3, -3) .
$$
Therefore the coordinates of $2v - w$ are **$(2, 3, -3)$**.
