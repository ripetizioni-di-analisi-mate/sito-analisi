Let $W$ be the subspace of matrices of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ in which the sum of the entries of each row is zero. Which sequence is a basis of $W$?

- $\left( \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \ \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \right)$
- $\left( \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \ \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \right)$
- $\left( \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \right)$
- $\left( \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \ \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \right)$

## Explanation

A matrix $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ belongs to $W$ if and only if $b = -a$ and $d = -c$, hence
$$
\begin{pmatrix} a & -a \\ c & -c \end{pmatrix} = a \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} + c \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The two matrices obtained span $W$ and are not proportional, therefore they **form a basis** and $\dim W = 2$. The matrices with entries $1, 1$ in a row do not belong to $W$; those with zero column sums have nonzero row sums; a single matrix is not enough, because $\dim W = 2$.
