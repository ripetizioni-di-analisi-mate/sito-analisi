$u=\left(3,-2,1\right)$, $v=\left(6,-4,2\right)$, $w=\left(3,-2,2\right)$. What is the dimension of $\operatorname{span}(u,v)\cap\operatorname{span}(w)$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The first span is the line through $u$ since $v=2u$. The line through $w$ has a different direction, so their only intersection is the origin. The result is $0$.
