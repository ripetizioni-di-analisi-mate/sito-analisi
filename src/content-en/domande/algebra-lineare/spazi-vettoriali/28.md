A subspace $W$ of $\mathbb{R}^5$ has dimension $2$. What is the minimum number of homogeneous linear equations needed to describe $W$ in Cartesian form?

- $2$
- $5$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

If $W$ is the solution set of $A x = 0$ with $A \in M_{m,5}(\mathbb{R})$, then by the **Rouché–Capelli theorem** $\dim W = 5 - \operatorname{rk} A$. Since $\dim W = 2$, we get $\operatorname{rk} A = 3$, so at least $3$ equations are needed. Three equations suffice, because a subspace of dimension $2$ of $\mathbb{R}^5$ is described in Cartesian form by $5 - 2 = 3$ independent equations. Therefore the minimum number of equations is **$3$**.
