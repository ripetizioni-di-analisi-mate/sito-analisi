$u=\left(1,3\right)$, $v=\left(0,-2\right)$, $w=\left(4,0\right)$. What is the second coordinate of $w$ in the ordered basis $(u,v)$?

- $6$
- $-6$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first coordinate in the basis is $c$. For the second standard component to be zero, offset $ac$ by a multiple of $b$. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$, $c=4$ gives $6$.
