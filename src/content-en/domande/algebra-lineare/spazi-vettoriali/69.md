$(p,q)$, $p(x)=1+3x$, $q(x)=x$. What is the coefficient of $q$ in the representation of $1$ in the given basis?

- $-6$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $p$ must be one to obtain the constant term. Its $x$ term cancels with $-a q$. Substituting $a=3$ gives $-3$.
