Let $p_1, p_2, p_3, p_4$ be four linearly independent polynomials of $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$. Which statement is true?

- They span a subspace of dimension $3$
- They are dependent, because $\dim \mathbb{R}_3[x] = 3$
- They form a basis of $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$ only if one of them is the constant polynomial $1$
- They form a basis of $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$

## Explanation

The standard basis of $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$ is $(1, x, x^2, x^3)$, so $\dim \mathbb{R}_3[x] = 4$. By the **consequences of the dimension**, $4$ independent vectors in a space of dimension $4$ form a basis. Therefore $p_1, p_2, p_3, p_4$ form a **basis** of $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$, whatever they are. For example, $(1 + x, \ x, \ x^2, \ x^3)$ is a basis that does not contain the constant polynomial $1$.
