$\mathcal{B} = (1, \ 1 + x, \ 1 + x + x^2)$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$. What are the coordinates of $p(x) = 3 - 2x + 5x^2$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$?

- $(3, -2, 5)$
- $(-5, 3, 5)$
- $(15, -7, 5)$
- $(5, -7, 5)$

## Explanation

We look for $a, b, c$ such that $a \cdot 1 + b\,(1 + x) + c\,(1 + x + x^2) = 3 - 2x + 5x^2$. The left-hand side is $(a + b + c) + (b + c)\,x + c\,x^2$, so, equating the coefficients, we obtain the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a + b + c = 3 \\[4pt]
b + c = -2 \\[4pt]
c = 5
\end{cases}
$$
The third equation gives $c = 5$, the second $b = -2 - 5 = -7$ and the first $a = 3 + 7 - 5 = 5$. Therefore the **coordinates** of $p$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$ are $(5, -7, 5)$.
