Let $U$ and $W$ be subspaces of $\mathbb{R}^4$ with $U \subseteq W$ and $\dim U = \dim W = 2$. Which statement is true?

- $U = W$
- $U$ may be different from $W$
- $U + W = \mathbb{R}^4$
- $U \cap W = \{0\}$

## Explanation

$U$ is a subspace of $W$, which has dimension $2$. By the theorem on the **dimension of a subspace**, $\dim U = \dim W$ implies $U = W$. Hence $U + W = W$ has dimension $2$ and is not $\mathbb{R}^4$, and $U \cap W = U$ has dimension $2$ and is not $\{0\}$.
