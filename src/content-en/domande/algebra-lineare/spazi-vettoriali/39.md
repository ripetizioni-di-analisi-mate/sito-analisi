Which Cartesian equation describes the plane $W = \{ (s + 2t, \ s - t, \ 3t) : s, t \in \mathbb{R} \}$ of $\mathbb{R}^3$?

- $x - y - z = 0$
- $x + y - z = 0$
- $x - y + z = 0$
- $x - y - 3z = 0$

## Explanation

We set $x = s + 2t$, $y = s - t$, $z = 3t$ and eliminate the parameters. Subtracting the second equality from the first gives $x - y = 3t$, and since $3t = z$ we get $x - y = z$. Therefore $W$ has equation $x - y - z = 0$. The check on the generators confirms the result: for $s = 1$, $t = 0$ we get $(1, 1, 0)$ and for $s = 0$, $t = 1$ we get $(2, -1, 3)$, and both satisfy the equation.
