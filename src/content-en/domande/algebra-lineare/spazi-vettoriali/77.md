Let $W = \langle 1, \ x^2 \rangle \subseteq \mathbb{R}_2[x]$ and let $U$ be the subspace of the polynomials of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$ vanishing at $x = 1$. Which subspace is formed by the polynomials of $W$ vanishing at $x = 1$, that is, $U \cap W$?

- $\langle 1 + x^2 \rangle$
- $\langle x - 1 \rangle$
- $\{0\}$
- $\langle 1 - x^2 \rangle$

## Explanation

A polynomial of $W$ is written as $p = a + c\,x^2$ with $a, c \in \mathbb{R}$. It belongs to $U$ if and only if $p(1) = a + c = 0$, that is, $c = -a$.

Hence the polynomials of $U \cap W$ are $a\,(1 - x^2)$ with $a \in \mathbb{R}$, therefore **$U \cap W = \langle 1 - x^2 \rangle$**, of dimension $1$. The polynomial $1 + x^2$ takes the value $2$ at $x = 1$, so it does not lie in $U$; the polynomial $x - 1$ has a term in $x$, so it does not lie in $W$.
