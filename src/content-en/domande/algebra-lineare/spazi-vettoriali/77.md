$u=\left(1,4,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,5\right)$, $w=\left(-3,0,t\right)$. For which $t$ are the three vectors linearly dependent?

- $0$
- $-20$
- $60$
- $-60$

## Explanation

Three vectors in $\mathbb R^3$ are dependent when the column matrix is singular. Its determinant is $t+abc$. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$, $c=-3$ gives $60$.
