$u=\left(1,2\right)$, $v=\left(0,3\right)$, $w=\left(1,0\right)$. What is the second coordinate of $w$ in the ordered basis $(u,v)$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $0$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The first coordinate in the basis is $c$. For the second standard component to be zero, offset $ac$ by a multiple of $b$. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$, $c=1$ gives $- \dfrac{2}{3}$.
