In the diagram $u=(2,1)$, $v=(0,1)$ and $w=(4,0)$. For which values of $c$ are there nonnegative coefficients $a,b,c$ satisfying $au+bv+cw=2u$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,2,1,u;0,0,0,1,v;0,0,4,0,w" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1.5 ymax=2.5 uguale=1 titolo="The vectors $u = (2, 1)$, $v = (0, 1)$ and $w = (4, 0)$"}

- $c\ge0$ with no upper bound.
- $0\le c\le2$
- $0\le c\le1$
- $c=0$ only.

## Explanation

Since $w=2u-2v$, matching the coefficients of the two independent vectors gives $a=2-2c$ and $b=2c$. All three coefficients are nonnegative precisely when $0\le c\le1$.
