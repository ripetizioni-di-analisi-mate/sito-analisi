The figure shows the vectors $u = (2, 1)$, $v = (0, 1)$ and $w = (4, 0)$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$. Which linear combination of $u$ and $v$ equals $w$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,2,1,u;0,0,0,1,v;0,0,4,0,w" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1.5 ymax=2.5 uguale=1 titolo="The vectors $u = (2, 1)$, $v = (0, 1)$ and $w = (4, 0)$"}

- $w = 2u + 2v$
- $w = 2u - v$
- $w = 2u - 2v$
- $w = u - 2v$

## Explanation

We look for $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ with $a\,(2, 1) + b\,(0, 1) = (4, 0)$, that is, $2a = 4$ and $a + b = 0$.

The first equation gives $a = 2$, and the second $b = -2$. Hence $w = 2u - 2v$, and indeed $2\,(2, 1) - 2\,(0, 1) = (4, 2) - (0, 2) = (4, 0)$. The other combinations give $2u + 2v = (4, 4)$, $2u - v = (4, 1)$ and $u - 2v = (2, -1)$.
