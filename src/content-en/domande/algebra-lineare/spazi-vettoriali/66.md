$u=\left(1,3,-2\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,4\right)$, $w=\left(2,9,8\right)$. What are the coordinates of $w$ in the ordered basis $(u,v)$ of their span?

- $\left(2,2\right)$
- $\left(2,3\right)$
- $\left(2,6\right)$
- $\left(3,2\right)$

## Explanation

The first component determines the coefficient of $u$. The second, after subtracting the contribution of $u$, determines that of $v$. The result is $\left(2,3\right)$.
