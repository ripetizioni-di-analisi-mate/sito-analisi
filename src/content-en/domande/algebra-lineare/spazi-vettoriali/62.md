Let $A \in M_{3,5}(\mathbb{R})$ be a matrix whose three rows are linearly independent. Which statement about the five columns of $A$ is true?

- The columns span $\mathbb{R}^3$
- The columns are linearly independent
- At most two columns are linearly independent
- The columns span a subspace of dimension $5$

## Explanation

Since the three rows are independent, $\operatorname{rk} A = 3$. The rank is also the maximum number of independent columns, so the subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$ spanned by the columns has dimension $3$.

By the theorem on the dimension of a subspace, a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$ of dimension $3$ coincides with $\mathbb{R}^3$, therefore **the columns span $\mathbb{R}^3$**. Five vectors of $\mathbb{R}^3$, on the other hand, are always dependent, and no subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$ has dimension $5$.
