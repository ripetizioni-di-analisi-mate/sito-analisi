$u=\left(1,3,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,-2\right)$, $w=\left(4,0,t\right)$. For which $t$ are the three vectors linearly dependent?

- $-24$
- $24$
- $0$
- $6$

## Explanation

Three vectors in $\mathbb R^3$ are dependent when the column matrix is singular. Its determinant is $t+abc$. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$, $c=4$ gives $24$.
