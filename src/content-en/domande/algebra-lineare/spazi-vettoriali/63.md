On the plane $W=\{(x,y,z):x+y=z\}$ consider the bases $B=((1,0,1),(0,1,1))$ and $C=((1,0,1),(1,1,2))$. For which vectors of $W$ is the coordinate pair identical in the two bases?

- The multiples of $(0,1,1)$.
- The multiples of $(1,0,1)$.
- The multiples of $(1,1,2)$.
- All vectors of $W$.

## Explanation

If the common coordinates are $(a,b)$, the two reconstructions differ by $b((1,1,2)-(0,1,1))=b(1,0,1)$. They agree exactly when $b=0$, leaving the coordinate of the shared first vector free.
