$u=\left(1,3,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,0,1\right)$, $w=\left(-2,t,4\right)$. For which $t$ does the vector $w$ belong to the span of $u,v$?

- $-6$
- $-2$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

In the combination $su+rv$ the first component is $s$ and the second is $as$. The first component of $w$ fixes $s=b$. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$ gives $-6$.
