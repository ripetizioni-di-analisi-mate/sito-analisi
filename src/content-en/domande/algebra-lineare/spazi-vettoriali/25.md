For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ are the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} , \qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} , \qquad
C = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & k \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
linearly dependent?

- $k = 2$
- $k = 0$
- $k = 1$
- $k = -2$

## Explanation

The matrices $A$ and $B$ are not proportional, so they are independent, and the three matrices are dependent if and only if $C$ is a linear combination of $A$ and $B$. We have
$$
aA + bB = \begin{pmatrix} a & a + b \\ b & a \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
and equality with $C$ requires $a = 1$ and $b = 1$, hence $a + b = 2$. Therefore $C = A + B$ if $k = 2$, and for every other value $C$ is not a combination of $A$ and $B$. The matrices are **dependent** if and only if $k = 2$.
