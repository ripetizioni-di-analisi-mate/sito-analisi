The plane $U = \langle (1, 0, 1, 0), (0, 1, 0, 1) \rangle$ and the hyperplane $W$ with equation $x_1 = x_3$ are subspaces of $\mathbb{R}^4$. What is the dimension of the smallest subspace containing both of them?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

The generators of $U$ satisfy the equation of $W$, since $(1, 0, 1, 0)$ has $x_1 = x_3 = 1$ and $(0, 1, 0, 1)$ has $x_1 = x_3 = 0$. Hence $U \subseteq W$, and so $U + W = W$ and $U \cap W = U$.

The subspace $W$ is described by one nontrivial equation, therefore $\dim W = 4 - 1 = 3$, and the smallest subspace containing $U$ and $W$, that is, $U + W$, **has dimension $3$**. Grassmann's formula gives the same result, since $\dim U + \dim W - \dim (U \cap W) = 2 + 3 - 2 = 3$.
