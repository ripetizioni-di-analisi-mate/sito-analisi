$u=\left(1,4,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,0,1\right)$, $w=\left(5,t,-3\right)$. For which $t$ does $w$ belong to the span of $u,v$?

- $1$
- $20$
- $-20$
- $5$

## Explanation

In $su+rv$ the first component is $s$ and the second is $as$. The first component of $w$ fixes $s=b$. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$ gives $20$.
