What is the dimension of the subspace
$$
W = \{ (s + 2t, \ 2s + 4t, \ -s - 2t) : s, t \in \mathbb{R} \}
$$
of $\mathbb{R}^3$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The generic vector of $W$ can be written as
$$
(s + 2t, \ 2s + 4t, \ -s - 2t) = (s + 2t)\,(1, 2, -1) ,
$$
so $W = \langle (1, 2, -1) \rangle$. In other words, the generators $(1, 2, -1)$ and $(2, 4, -2)$ read off from the parameters are proportional. Therefore $W$ is a line and **$\dim W = 1$**, even though two parameters appear in the parametric form.
