$(p,q)$, $p(x)=1+2x$, $q(x)=x$. What is the coefficient of $q$ in the representation of $1$ in the given basis?

- $0$
- $-2$
- $-4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $p$ must be one to obtain the constant term. Its $x$ term cancels with $-a q$. Substituting $a=2$ gives $-2$.
