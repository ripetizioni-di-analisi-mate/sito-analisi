The matrices
$$
B_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} , \qquad
B_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix} , \qquad
B_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
form a basis of the subspace of symmetric matrices of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$. What are the coordinates of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ with respect to the basis $(B_1, B_2, B_3)$?

- $(3, 2, 1)$
- $(1, 2, 2)$
- $(2, 1, 2)$
- $(2, -1, 2)$

## Explanation

We look for $a, b, c$ with $a B_1 + b B_2 + c B_3 = A$, that is,
$$
\begin{pmatrix} a + b & c \\ c & a - b \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The off-diagonal entries give $c = 2$. The diagonal entries give the system $a + b = 3$, $a - b = 1$, hence $a = 2$ and $b = 1$. Therefore the coordinates of $A$ are **$(2, 1, 2)$**; the triple $(3, 2, 1)$ consists of entries of $A$, not of coordinates with respect to this basis.
