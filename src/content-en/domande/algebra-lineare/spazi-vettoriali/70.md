A vector space $V$ is spanned by three vectors $w_1, w_2, w_3$ and contains three linearly independent vectors $v_1, v_2, v_3$. Which statement is true?

- $\dim V = 3$
- $\dim V$ may be $4$
- $\dim V$ may be $2$
- Without knowing the vectors, $\dim V$ cannot be determined

## Explanation

By the theorem on extracting a basis, a basis of $V$ can be chosen among $w_1, w_2, w_3$, so $\dim V \leq 3$.

A basis of $V$ spans $V$, and $v_1, v_2, v_3$ are independent; by the **Steinitz lemma** the number of independent vectors does not exceed the number of elements of the basis, therefore $\dim V \geq 3$. The two inequalities give **$\dim V = 3$**.
