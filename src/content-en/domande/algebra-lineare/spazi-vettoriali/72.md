What is the minimum number of matrices needed to span the space $M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$?

- $2$
- $5$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The standard basis of $M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$ consists of the $2 \cdot 3 = 6$ matrices $E_{ij}$, so $\dim M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R}) = 6$ and six matrices are enough.

By the consequences of the dimension, fewer than $6$ vectors of a space of dimension $6$ do not span it. Therefore the minimum number of generators **is $6$**.
