$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 3 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the maximum number of independent columns of $A$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The first and second columns supply two pivots. The third is zero and the fourth is a combination of the first two. The result is $2$.
