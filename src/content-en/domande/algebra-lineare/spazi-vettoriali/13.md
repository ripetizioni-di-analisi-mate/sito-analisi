- $k = 0$
- $k \neq -2$
- For every $k \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

We have $\dim U + \dim W_k = 2 + 1 = 3$, so by the **criterion for complementary subspaces** $\mathbb{R}^3 = U \oplus W_k$ if and only if $U \cap W_k = \{0\}$. Since $W_k$ is a line, the intersection is $\{0\}$ if and only if the generator $(1, k, 1)$ does not belong to $U$. Substituting into the equation of $U$ gives $1 + k - 1 = k$, which vanishes only for $k = 0$. Therefore $\mathbb{R}^3 = U \oplus W_k$ if and only if $k \neq 0$; for $k = 0$ the line $W_0$ is contained in $U$.
