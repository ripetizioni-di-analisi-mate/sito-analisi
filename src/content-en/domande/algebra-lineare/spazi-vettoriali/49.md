$u=\left(1,2,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,3\right)$, $w=\left(0,0,1\right)$. How many vectors must be removed from these three to obtain a basis of $\mathbb R^3$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The column matrix is triangular with nonzero diagonal entries. All three columns already form a basis. The result is $0$.
