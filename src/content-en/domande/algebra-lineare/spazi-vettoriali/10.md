Which vectors of the standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$, added to $v_1 = (0, 0, 1, 1)$ and $v_2 = (1, 2, 0, 1)$, give a basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$?

- $e_2$ and $e_4$
- $e_3$ and $e_4$
- $e_2$ only
- $e_1$, $e_2$ and $e_4$

## Explanation

A basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$ has four vectors, so two vectors are added to $v_1, v_2$. The matrix with rows $v_2 = (1, 2, 0, 1)$ and $v_1 = (0, 0, 1, 1)$ is already in echelon form, with pivots in columns $1$ and $3$. By the **row reduction method** we add the standard vectors of the columns without a pivot, that is, $e_2$ and $e_4$, and $(v_1, v_2, e_2, e_4)$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$. The pair $e_3, e_4$ does not work, because $v_1 = e_3 + e_4$ and the four vectors are dependent.
