$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the maximum number of columns of the matrix $A$ that form an independent set?

- $3$
- $0$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The first and second columns supply two pivots. The third is zero and the fourth is a combination of the first two. The result is $2$.
