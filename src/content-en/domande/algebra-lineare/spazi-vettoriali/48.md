$u=\left(1,2,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,0,1\right)$, $w=\left(3,t,1\right)$. For which $t$ does the vector $w$ belong to the span of $u,v$?

- $3$
- $-6$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

In the combination $su+rv$ the first component is $s$ and the second is $as$. The first component of $w$ fixes $s=b$. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$ gives $6$.
