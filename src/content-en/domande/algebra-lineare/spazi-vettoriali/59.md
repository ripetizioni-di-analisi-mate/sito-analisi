Let $u, v, w$ be linearly independent vectors of a vector space $V$. Which of the following triples is linearly dependent?

- $u + v$, $v + w$, $w + u$
- $u - v$, $v - w$, $w - u$
- $u$, $u + v$, $u + v + w$
- $u + v$, $v$, $w$

## Explanation

For the triple $u - v$, $v - w$, $w - u$ we have
$$
(u - v) + (v - w) + (w - u) = 0 ,
$$
which is a zero combination with coefficients $1, 1, 1$, not all zero; hence this triple **is linearly dependent**.

For the other triples we pass to coordinates with respect to $u, v, w$. The matrices whose columns are the coordinates have determinant $2$ for $u + v$, $v + w$, $w + u$, and $1$ for the other two triples, which give triangular matrices with diagonal entries equal to $1$. Therefore the other three triples are independent.
