What is the dimension of the subspace of symmetric matrices of $M_3(\mathbb{R})$, that is, of the matrices $A$ of order $3$ with $A^T = A$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

A symmetric matrix of order $3$ has the form
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b & c \\ b & d & e \\ c & e & f \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
because the entries below the diagonal coincide with the symmetric ones above the diagonal. Hence $A$ is determined by the six numbers $a, b, c, d, e, f$, which can be chosen freely. The six symmetric matrices obtained by setting one of these numbers equal to $1$ and the others equal to $0$ span the subspace and are independent, therefore they form a basis of it. The **dimension** is $6$.
