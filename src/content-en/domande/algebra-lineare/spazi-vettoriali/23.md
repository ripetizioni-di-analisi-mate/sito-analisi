Let $v_1, v_2, v_3, v_4$ be four vectors of $\mathbb{R}^3$. Which statement is true?

- They are linearly independent if none of them is a multiple of another
- They always span $\mathbb{R}^3$
- They are always linearly dependent
- They are linearly independent if three of them are

## Explanation

$\mathbb{R}^3$ is spanned by the three vectors of the standard basis. By the **Steinitz lemma** every set of independent vectors of $\mathbb{R}^3$ has at most $3$ elements, so four vectors of $\mathbb{R}^3$ are always **dependent**. Four vectors may fail to span $\mathbb{R}^3$: for example, four multiples of $(1, 0, 0)$ span only a line.
