What is the dimension of the subspace $W = \{ A \in M_2(\mathbb{R}) : A^T = 2A \}$ of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

Let $A \in W$. Transposing the equality $A^T = 2A$ gives $A = (A^T)^T = 2A^T$.

Substituting $A^T = 2A$ we get $A = 2 \cdot 2A = 4A$, hence $3A = O$ and $A = O$. Therefore $W = \{O\}$ and, by the convention on the dimension of the zero subspace, **$\dim W = 0$**. The value $3$ is the dimension of the symmetric matrices ($A^T = A$) and the value $1$ that of the skew-symmetric ones ($A^T = -A$).
