$u=\left(1,3,0\right)$, $v=\left(0,1,-2\right)$, $w=\left(1,4,-2\right)$. What is the dimension of the span of $u,v,w$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The third vector is the sum of the first two. The first two are independent and form a basis for the subspace. The result is $2$.
