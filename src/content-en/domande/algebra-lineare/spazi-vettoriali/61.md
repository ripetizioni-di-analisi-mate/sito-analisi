Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. The matrices $I$, $A$, $A^2$ of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ are linearly dependent. Which relation is true?

- $A^2 = 2A$
- $A^2 = A + I$
- $A^2 = 2A + I$
- $A^2 = 2A - I$

## Explanation

The row-by-column product gives
$$
A^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
On the other hand $2A - I = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, hence **$A^2 = 2A - I$**, that is, $A^2 - 2A + I = O$ is a dependence relation. The other equalities are false, since $2A$, $A + I$ and $2A + I$ have diagonal entries $2$, $2$ and $3$, while $A^2$ has diagonal entries $1$.
