The figure shows the vectors $u = (2, 1)$ and $v = (-4, -2)$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$. Which set is the subspace $\langle u, v \rangle$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,2,1,u;0,0,-4,-2,v" punti="0,0,pieno" xmin=-5 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=2.5 uguale=1 titolo="The vectors $u = (2, 1)$ and $v = (-4, -2)$"}

- $\mathbb{R}^2$
- The line $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$
- The line $y = 2x$
- The line $y = -\dfrac{x}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $v = (-4, -2) = -2\,(2, 1) = -2u$, the vector $v$ is a linear combination of $u$.

By the theorem on the removal of a redundant generator we therefore have $\langle u, v \rangle = \langle u \rangle$, which is the line through the origin with the direction of $u$. Its vectors are $t\,(2, 1) = (2t, t)$, that is, the points with $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$. Therefore $\langle u, v \rangle$ **is the line $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$**, and two proportional vectors do not span $\mathbb{R}^2$.
