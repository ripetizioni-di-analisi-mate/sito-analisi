Let $V$ be a vector space with basis $(v, w)$. Which of the following pairs of vectors spans $V$?

- $v + w$, $v - w$
- $v + w$, $2v + 2w$
- $v - w$, $w - v$
- $v$, $2v$

## Explanation

With respect to the basis $(v, w)$ the vectors $v + w$ and $v - w$ have coordinates $(1, 1)$ and $(1, -1)$, and the matrix with these coordinates as columns has determinant $-1 - 1 = -2 \neq 0$. Hence $v + w$ and $v - w$ are independent.

Since $\dim V = 2$, two independent vectors form a basis of $V$ by the consequences of the dimension, therefore $v + w$ and $v - w$ **span $V$**. In the other pairs one vector is a multiple of the other ($2v + 2w = 2\,(v + w)$, $w - v = -(v - w)$, $2v = 2 \cdot v$), so each of them spans only a line.
