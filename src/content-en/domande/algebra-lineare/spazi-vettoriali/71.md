For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ does the subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$ formed by the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + kz = 0 \\[4pt]
x + ky + z = 0 \\[4pt]
kx + y + z = 0
\end{cases}
$$
have dimension $2$?

- $k = -2$
- $k = 1$
- $k = 0$
- $k = -1$

## Explanation

The solution subspace has dimension $3 - \operatorname{rk} A$, where $A$ is the coefficient matrix, so the dimension is $2$ if and only if $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$.

The determinant is $\det A = -(k - 1)^2 (k + 2)$, therefore for $k \neq 1$ and $k \neq -2$ the rank is $3$ and the only solution is the zero one. For $k = -2$ the minor $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -2 \end{vmatrix} = -3$ is nonzero, so the rank is $2$ and the dimension is $1$. For $k = 1$ the three equations coincide with $x + y + z = 0$, the rank is $1$ and the dimension **is $2$ for $k = 1$**.
