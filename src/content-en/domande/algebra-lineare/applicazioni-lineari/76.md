The endomorphism $f$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$ has matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ with respect to the standard basis. What is the area of the image of the square $[0, 1] \times [0, 1]$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,3,2;0,0,1,4" segmenti="1,0,1,1;0,1,1,1;3,2,4,6;1,4,4,6" etichette="2.2,1.05,f(e₁);0.1,3.3,f(e₂)" xmin=-0.5 xmax=4.6 ymin=-0.5 ymax=6.5 uguale=1 titolo="The square $[0, 1] \times [0, 1]$ and the parallelogram built on $f(e_1) = (3, 2)$ and $f(e_2) = (1, 4)$"}

- $12$
- $7$
- $10$
- $14$

## Explanation

Every point of the square can be written as $a e_1 + b e_2$ with $a, b \in [0, 1]$, so its image is $a\,(3, 2) + b\,(1, 4)$. Therefore the image of the square is the parallelogram built on the columns $(3, 2)$ and $(1, 4)$ of $A$. The area of a parallelogram built on two vectors of the plane is the absolute value of the determinant of the matrix having them as columns, so the area equals $|3 \cdot 4 - 1 \cdot 2| = 10$. The value $7$ is the trace of $A$ and the value $12$ is the product of the diagonal entries.
