$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 5 & -3\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(2,-1\right)$. If $f(x)=Ax$, what is the first component of $f(u)$?

- $9$
- $13$
- $3$
- $7$

## Explanation

The first product component uses the first row of $A$. Multiply its entries by the components of $u$. The result is $7$.
