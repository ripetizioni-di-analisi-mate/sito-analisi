Let $S : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ be the orthogonal reflection across the line $r : y = 2x$, that is, the linear map that fixes the vectors of $r$ and changes the sign of the vectors perpendicular to $r$. What is the matrix of $S$ with respect to the standard basis?

::grafico{f="2*x" xmin=-2.5 xmax=2.5 ymin=-2.5 ymax=3 uguale=1 etichette="1.2,2.9,r" titolo="The line $r : y = 2x$"}

- $\dfrac{1}{5} \begin{pmatrix} -3 & 4 \\ 4 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5} \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 4 \\ 4 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5} \begin{pmatrix} -3 & -4 \\ -4 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The vector $(1, 2)$ lies on $r$ and the vector $(2, -1)$ is perpendicular to $r$, so $S(1, 2) = (1, 2)$ and $S(2, -1) = (-2, 1)$. One writes $e_1 = \dfrac{1}{5}\,(1, 2) + \dfrac{2}{5}\,(2, -1)$ and $e_2 = \dfrac{2}{5}\,(1, 2) - \dfrac{1}{5}\,(2, -1)$. By linearity
$$
S(e_1) = \frac{1}{5}\,(1, 2) + \frac{2}{5}\,(-2, 1) = \left( -\frac{3}{5},\ \frac{4}{5} \right), \qquad
S(e_2) = \frac{2}{5}\,(1, 2) - \frac{1}{5}\,(-2, 1) = \left( \frac{4}{5},\ \frac{3}{5} \right) .
$$
These are the columns of the matrix. The matrix with $3$ and $-3$ swapped is the reflection across the line $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$, and $\dfrac{1}{5} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ is the orthogonal projection onto $r$.
