Let $P : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be the linear map that fixes the vectors of the plane $\pi : z = 0$ and sends the vector $d = (1, 1, 1)$ to the zero vector (projection onto $\pi$ in the direction of $d$). What is the matrix of $P$ with respect to the standard basis?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The vectors $e_1$ and $e_2$ lie in $\pi$, so $P(e_1) = e_1$ and $P(e_2) = e_2$. Since $e_3 = d - e_1 - e_2$, by linearity $P(e_3) = 0 - e_1 - e_2 = (-1, -1, 0)$. The columns of the matrix are $P(e_1)$, $P(e_2)$ and $P(e_3)$, therefore
$$
M(P) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
that is $P(x, y, z) = (x - z,\ y - z,\ 0)$. The matrix with zero third column is that of the orthogonal projection onto $\pi$, which sends $(0, 0, 1)$ to zero, not $d$.
