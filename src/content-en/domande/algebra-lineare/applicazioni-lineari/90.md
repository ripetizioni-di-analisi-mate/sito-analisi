For $k\in\mathbb R$ let $F_k(x,y,z)=(s,s,kx+y+z)$ with $s=x+y+z$. Which subspace is the intersection of the kernels of all maps $F_k$?

- $\operatorname{span}\{(1,0,-1)\}$
- $\{(x,y,z):x+y+z=0\}$
- $\operatorname{span}\{(0,1,-1)\}$
- $\{0\}$

## Explanation

Membership in every kernel requires $x+y+z=0$ and $kx+y+z=0$ for every $k$. Comparing two distinct parameter values yields $x=0$. What remains is $y+z=0$, the stated line.
