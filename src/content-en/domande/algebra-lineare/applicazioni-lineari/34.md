Let $f : M_2(\mathbb{R}) \to \mathbb{R}$, $f(A) = \operatorname{tr} A$. What is $\dim \ker f$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

The trace is not identically zero, since $\operatorname{tr} I_2 = 2$, so $\operatorname{Im} f$ is a nonzero subspace of $\mathbb{R}$ and coincides with $\mathbb{R}$. By the **rank-nullity theorem** $\dim \ker f = \dim M_2(\mathbb{R}) - \dim \operatorname{Im} f = 4 - 1 = 3$. Indeed, the kernel consists of the matrices $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & -a \end{pmatrix}$, which depend on the three free parameters $a$, $b$, $c$.
