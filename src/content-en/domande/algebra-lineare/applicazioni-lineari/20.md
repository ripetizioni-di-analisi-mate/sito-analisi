Let $T : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to \mathbb{R}_2[x]$, $T(p)(x) = p(x + 1)$. What is the matrix of $T$ with respect to the basis $(1, x, x^2)$, used in the domain and in the codomain?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The $j$-th column of the matrix contains the coordinates of the image of the $j$-th vector of the basis. One has $T(1) = 1$, $T(x) = x + 1$ and $T(x^2) = (x + 1)^2 = 1 + 2x + x^2$. The coordinates with respect to $(1, x, x^2)$ are thus $(1, 0, 0)$, $(1, 1, 0)$ and $(1, 2, 1)$. Arranging them as columns one obtains the matrix with rows $(1, 1, 1)$, $(0, 1, 2)$ and $(0, 0, 1)$.
