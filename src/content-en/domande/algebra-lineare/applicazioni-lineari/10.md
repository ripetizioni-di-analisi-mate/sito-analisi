- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ -1 & -1 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 0 \\ 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 & 2 \\ 2 & -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The columns of the matrix are the coordinates with respect to $\mathcal{C}$ of the images of the vectors of $\mathcal{B}$. One has $f(1, 1) = (1, 1, 2)$ and $f(1, -1) = (1, -1, 0)$. From $(a, b, c) = \alpha\,(1, 0, 0) + \beta\,(1, 1, 0) + \gamma\,(1, 1, 1)$ one obtains $\gamma = c$, $\beta = b - c$ and $\alpha = a - b$. Therefore $[f(1, 1)]_{\mathcal{C}} = (0, -1, 2)$ and $[f(1, -1)]_{\mathcal{C}} = (2, -1, 0)$, and these two vectors are the columns of the matrix.
