$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(2,-1\right)$. If $f(x)=Ax$, what is the first component of $f(u)$?

- $-3$
- $1$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The first component of the product uses the first row of $A$. Multiply its entries by the components of $u$. The result is $3$.
