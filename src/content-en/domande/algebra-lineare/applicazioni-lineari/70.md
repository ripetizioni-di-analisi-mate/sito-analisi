Let $T : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to \mathbb{R}_2[x]$ be the endomorphism $T(p)(x) = p(x) - p(-x)$. Which statement is true?

- $\ker T \cap \operatorname{Im} T = \{0\}$ and $\ker T + \operatorname{Im} T = \mathbb{R}_2[x]$
- $\operatorname{Im} T \subseteq \ker T$
- $\ker T = \langle x \rangle$
- $T$ is surjective

## Explanation

For $p(x) = a + bx + cx^2$ one has $p(-x) = a - bx + cx^2$, so $T(p) = 2bx$. Therefore $\operatorname{Im} T = \langle x \rangle$, and $T$ is not surjective. The kernel consists of the polynomials with $b = 0$, that is, $\ker T = \langle 1, x^2 \rangle$, in agreement with the **rank-nullity theorem** $3 = 2 + 1$. A polynomial of $\langle 1, x^2 \rangle$ that is also a multiple of $x$ is zero, so the intersection is $\{0\}$. The three polynomials $1$, $x^2$, $x$ form a basis of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$, therefore the sum of the two subspaces is all of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$. The polynomial $x$ has image $2x \neq 0$, so $\operatorname{Im} T$ is not contained in $\ker T$.
