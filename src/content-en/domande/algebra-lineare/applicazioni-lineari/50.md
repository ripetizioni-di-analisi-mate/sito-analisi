Let $T : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to \mathbb{R}$ be the linear map
$$
T(p) = \int_0^1 p(x)\, dx .
$$
What is the matrix of $T$ with respect to the basis $(1, x, x^2)$ of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$ and the basis $(1)$ of $\mathbb{R}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & \dfrac{1}{2} & \dfrac{1}{3} \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\[4pt] \dfrac{1}{2} \\[4pt] \dfrac{1}{3} \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The domain has dimension $3$ and the codomain has dimension $1$, so the matrix has one row and three columns. The columns are the images of the basis vectors:
$$
T(1) = \int_0^1 1\, dx = 1, \qquad T(x) = \int_0^1 x\, dx = \frac{1}{2}, \qquad T(x^2) = \int_0^1 x^2\, dx = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
Therefore the matrix is $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & \dfrac{1}{2} & \dfrac{1}{3} \end{pmatrix}$. The row $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is the matrix of the evaluation $p \mapsto p(1)$.
