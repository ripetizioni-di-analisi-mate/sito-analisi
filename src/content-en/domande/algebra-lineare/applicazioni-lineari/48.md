$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the dimension of the kernel of the map $x\mapsto Ax$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

There is only one pivot, so the rank is one. The domain is three-dimensional and the rank-nullity theorem gives the kernel dimension. The result is $2$.
