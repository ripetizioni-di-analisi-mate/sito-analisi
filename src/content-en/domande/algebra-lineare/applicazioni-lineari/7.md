- $f(x, y) = (-x + 2y,\ -x + y,\ 2x - y)$
- $f(x, y) = (x + 2y,\ y,\ x - y)$
- $f(x, y) = (-3x + 2y,\ -2x + y,\ 3x - y)$
- $f(x, y) = (5x + 2y,\ 2x + y,\ -x - y)$

## Explanation

The vectors $(1, 2)$ and $(0, 1)$ are not proportional, so they form a basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the map exists and is unique. Since $(1, 0) = (1, 2) - 2\,(0, 1)$, by linearity
$$
f(1, 0) = (1, 0, 1) - 2\,(2, 1, -1) = (-3, -2, 3) .
$$
The columns of the matrix with respect to the standard bases are thus $f(1, 0) = (-3, -2, 3)$ and $f(0, 1) = (2, 1, -1)$. Therefore $f(x, y) = x\, f(1, 0) + y\, f(0, 1) = (-3x + 2y,\ -2x + y,\ 3x - y)$.
