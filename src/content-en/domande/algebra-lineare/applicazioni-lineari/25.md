What is the dimension of the subspace of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ spanned by the matrices
$$
A_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} , \quad
A_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} , \quad
A_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} , \quad
A_4 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The map that assigns to a matrix in $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ its coordinates with respect to the basis $(E_{11}, E_{12}, E_{21}, E_{22})$ is an **isomorphism** with $\mathbb{R}^4$, so it preserves the dimension of subspaces. The coordinates are $(1, 0, 1, 0)$, $(0, 1, 0, 1)$, $(1, 1, 1, 1)$ and $(1, 0, 0, 1)$. We have $A_3 = A_1 + A_2$, while $A_1$, $A_2$, $A_4$ are linearly independent, since the matrix with their coordinates as rows has rank $3$. Therefore the dimension of the subspace is $3$.
