For $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$, $f(x, y) = (x + y,\ 2x - y,\ x)$, what is the Cartesian equation of $\operatorname{Im} f$?

- $x + y + 3z = 0$
- $x + y - 3z = 0$
- $x - y + z = 0$
- $3x - y - z = 0$

## Explanation

The image is spanned by the columns of the matrix, $f(e_1) = (1, 2, 1)$ and $f(e_2) = (1, -1, 0)$, which are not proportional, so $\operatorname{Im} f$ is a plane through the origin. The vector $(x, y, z)$ belongs to the plane if and only if it depends linearly on the two columns, that is, if
$$
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 1 & x \\
2 & -1 & y \\
1 & 0 & z
\end{vmatrix}
= 0 .
$$
Expanding along the third column, the determinant equals $x \cdot 1 - y \cdot (-1) + z \cdot (-3) = x + y - 3z$, therefore the equation of the image is $x + y - 3z = 0$.
