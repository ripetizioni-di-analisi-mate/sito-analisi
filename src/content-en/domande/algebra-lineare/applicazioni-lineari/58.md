Let $T : \mathbb{R}_1[x] \to \mathbb{R}_2[x]$ be the linear map $T(p) = (x + 2)\, p(x)$. Which of the following polynomials belongs to $\operatorname{Im} T$?

- $x^2 + x - 2$
- $x^2 - x - 2$
- $x^2 + 2$
- $x^2 + 4x + 2$

## Explanation

The values of $T$ are the polynomials of the form $(x + 2)\, p(x)$ with $p$ of degree at most $1$, that is, the polynomials of degree at most $2$ divisible by $x + 2$. By the factor theorem a polynomial is divisible by $x + 2$ if and only if it vanishes at $x = -2$. One has $x^2 + x - 2 = (x + 2)(x - 1)$, so this polynomial is $T(x - 1)$ and belongs to the image. The other three take the values $4$, $6$ and $-2$ at $x = -2$, respectively, therefore they do not belong to $\operatorname{Im} T$.
