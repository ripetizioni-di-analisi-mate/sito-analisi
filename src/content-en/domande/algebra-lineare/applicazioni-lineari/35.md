Let $f : M_2(\mathbb{R}) \to M_2(\mathbb{R})$, $f(A) = A - A^T$. What is $\dim \operatorname{Im} f$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

For $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ one has
$$
f(A) =
\begin{pmatrix} 0 & b - c \\ c - b & 0 \end{pmatrix}
= (b - c) \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
So the image is spanned by the single matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and has dimension $1$. The kernel consists of the matrices with $b = c$, that is, the symmetric matrices, and has dimension $3$, in agreement with the **rank-nullity theorem**, since $3 + 1 = 4$.
