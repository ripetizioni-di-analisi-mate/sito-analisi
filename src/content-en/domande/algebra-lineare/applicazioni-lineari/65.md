$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 \\ -2 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. If $f(x)=Ax$ and $g(x)=Bx$, what is the first component of $g(f(e_1))$?

- $-6$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

First apply $f$, giving the first column of $A$. Then apply $g$. The first row of $B$ preserves the first component. The result is $3$.
