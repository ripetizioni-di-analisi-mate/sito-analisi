Let $T : \mathbb{R}_3[x] \to \mathbb{R}_3[x]$, $T(p) = p''$. What is $\dim \operatorname{Im} T$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The second derivative of $p(x) = a + bx + cx^2 + dx^3$ is $p''(x) = 2c + 6dx$, so $\operatorname{Im} T$ is contained in $\mathbb{R}_1[x]$. Every polynomial $\alpha + \beta x$ is obtained by choosing $c = \dfrac{\alpha}{2}$ and $d = \dfrac{\beta}{6}$, therefore $\operatorname{Im} T = \mathbb{R}_1[x]$, which has dimension $2$. In agreement with the **rank-nullity theorem**, the kernel consists of the polynomials of degree at most one and has dimension $4 - 2 = 2$.
