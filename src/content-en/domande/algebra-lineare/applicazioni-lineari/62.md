$f(e_1)=\left(3,-2\right)$, $f(e_2)=\left(1,4\right)$. The given pairs are the images of $e_1$ and $e_2$. What is $f(2,-1)$?

- $\left(2,-6\right)$
- $\left(5,-8\right)$
- $\left(7,0\right)$
- $\left(-8,5\right)$

## Explanation

By linearity, double the first image and subtract the second. The result is $\left(5,-8\right)$.
