Let $\mathcal{B} = (v_1, v_2, v_3)$ be a basis of $V$ and let $f : V \to V$ be the endomorphism such that
$$
f(v_1) = 0, \qquad f(v_2) = v_2, \qquad f(v_3) = v_1 .
$$
What is the matrix $M^{\mathcal{B}}_{\mathcal{B}}(f)$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The $j$-th column of $M^{\mathcal{B}}_{\mathcal{B}}(f)$ contains the coordinates of $f(v_j)$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$. Since $f(v_1) = 0$, the first column is $(0, 0, 0)$. Since $f(v_2) = v_2$, the second column is $(0, 1, 0)$. Since $f(v_3) = v_1$, the third column is $(1, 0, 0)$. The matrix with the coordinates of $v_1$ written in the third *row* is the transpose of the correct one.
