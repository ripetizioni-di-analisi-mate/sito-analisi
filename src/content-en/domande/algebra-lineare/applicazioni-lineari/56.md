$u=\left(1,2\right)$, $v=\left(0,1\right)$, $f(u)=\left(3,1\right)$, $f(v)=\left(1,0\right)$. What is the first component of $f(e_1)$?

- $-1$
- $5$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

In the given basis $e_1=u-av$. By linearity, subtract $a$ times the image of $v$ from the image of $u$. Substituting $a=2$ gives $1$.
