Let $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ and let $T : M_2(\mathbb{R}) \to M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be the linear map $T(A) = AB - BA$. What is $\dim \ker T$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

For $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ one has $AB = \begin{pmatrix} a & 2b \\ c & 2d \end{pmatrix}$ and $BA = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 2c & 2d \end{pmatrix}$, so
$$
T(A) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & b \\ -c & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Hence $T(A)$ is the zero matrix if and only if $b = c = 0$, that is, the kernel consists of the diagonal matrices. The diagonal matrices form a subspace with basis $E_{11}$, $E_{22}$, therefore $\dim \ker T = 2$.
