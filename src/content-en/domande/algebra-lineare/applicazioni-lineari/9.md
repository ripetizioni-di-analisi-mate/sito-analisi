- They are not similar, because they have different determinants
- They are similar, because they have the same rank
- They are not similar, because they have different traces

## Explanation

Determinant, trace and rank are **similarity invariants**, so two similar matrices have them equal. The traces are $1 + 4 = 5$ and $2 + 3 = 5$, and the ranks also coincide and are equal to $2$. The determinants are $\det A = 4 - 6 = -2$ and $\det B = 6 - 0 = 6$. Since the determinants are different, the matrices **are not similar**. Equality of the trace or of the rank is a necessary and not sufficient condition, therefore by itself it does not allow one to conclude that two matrices are similar.
