$f(e_1)=\left(4,5\right)$, $f(e_2)=\left(1,-3\right)$. The given pairs are the images of $e_1$ and $e_2$. What is $f(2,-1)$?

- $\left(9,7\right)$
- $\left(13,7\right)$
- $\left(7,13\right)$
- $\left(3,8\right)$

## Explanation

By linearity, double the first image and subtract the second. The result is $\left(7,13\right)$.
