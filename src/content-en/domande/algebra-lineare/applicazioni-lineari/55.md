Let $f : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$, $f(x, y, z) = (x - 2y + z,\ 2x - 4y + 2z,\ 0)$, and let
$$
\mathcal{B} = \big( (1, 1, 0), (0, 1, 1), (1, 0, 1) \big), \qquad
\mathcal{C} = \big( (1, 1, 1), (0, 1, 1), (0, 0, 1) \big)
$$
be two bases of $\mathbb{R}^3$. What is the rank of $M^{\mathcal{B}}_{\mathcal{C}}(f)$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- It depends on the bases, so the matrix must be computed

## Explanation

The rank of a matrix associated with $f$, with respect to any bases, equals $\dim \operatorname{Im} f$, since the columns are the coordinates of the spanning vectors $f(v_j)$ of the image and passing to coordinates preserves linear independence. One has $f(x, y, z) = (x - 2y + z)\,(1, 2, 0)$, so $\operatorname{Im} f = \langle (1, 2, 0) \rangle$ and $\dim \operatorname{Im} f = 1$. Therefore the rank of $M^{\mathcal{B}}_{\mathcal{C}}(f)$ is $1$, with no need to write down the matrix. The rank depends on the map $f$ and not on the chosen bases.
