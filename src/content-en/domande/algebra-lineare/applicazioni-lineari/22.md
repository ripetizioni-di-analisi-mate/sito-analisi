Let $f : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be a linear map. How many rows and how many columns does a matrix associated with $f$ have?

- $4$ rows and $3$ columns
- $3$ rows and $4$ columns
- $2$ rows and $3$ columns
- $4$ rows and $2$ columns

## Explanation

The associated matrix has as many columns as the dimension of the domain and as many rows as the dimension of the codomain. The domain $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$ has basis $(1, x, x^2)$, so it has dimension $3$. The codomain $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ has dimension $4$, since a $2 \times 2$ matrix has four entries that can be chosen freely. Therefore the matrix has $4$ rows and $3$ columns, whatever bases are chosen.
