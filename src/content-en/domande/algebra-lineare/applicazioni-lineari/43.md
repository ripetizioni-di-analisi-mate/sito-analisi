Which of the following matrices is similar to $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 6 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The matrix $A$ has trace $5$ and determinant $6$, and two similar matrices have the same trace and the same determinant. This rules out $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, which has trace $4$, $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$, which has determinant $-2$, and $\begin{pmatrix} 6 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, which has trace $7$. For $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ one observes that $A\,(1, 1)^T = (3, 3)^T$ and $A\,(1, 0)^T = (2, 0)^T$. Therefore, with respect to the basis $\big( (1, 1), (1, 0) \big)$, the endomorphism $x \mapsto Ax$ has matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, and this matrix is **similar** to $A$.
