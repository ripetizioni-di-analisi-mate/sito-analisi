$f(x,y)=(x+4y,y)$. What is the preimage of the vector $(1,2)$?

- $\left(-7,1\right)$
- $\left(9,2\right)$
- $\left(-7,2\right)$
- $\left(-3,2\right)$

## Explanation

The image’s second component equals $y$. With $y=2$ fixed, the first equation determines $x$. The result is $\left(-7,2\right)$.
