Which of the following vector spaces is isomorphic to $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$?

- $M_2(\mathbb{R})$
- $\mathbb{R}^3$
- $M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$
- $\mathbb{R}_4[x]$

## Explanation

Two finite-dimensional spaces are isomorphic if and only if they have the same dimension. The space $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$ has basis $(1, x, x^2, x^3)$, so it has dimension $4$. The spaces $M_2(\mathbb{R})$, $\mathbb{R}^3$, $M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$ and $\mathbb{R}_4[x]$ have dimension $4$, $3$, $6$ and $5$, respectively. Therefore only $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ is **isomorphic** to $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$, for example via the isomorphism that sends $a + bx + cx^2 + dx^3$ to $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$.
