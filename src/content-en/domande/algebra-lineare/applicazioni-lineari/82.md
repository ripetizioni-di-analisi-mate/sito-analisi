Let $T : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to \mathbb{R}_2[x]$ be the endomorphism $T(p)(x) = p(2x)$. What is the determinant of a matrix associated with $T$ with respect to the same basis in the domain and in the codomain?

- $8$
- $7$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The matrices associated with $T$ with respect to different bases are similar, so they have the same determinant, and it is enough to compute it with respect to the basis $(1, x, x^2)$. One has $T(1) = 1$, $T(x) = 2x$ and $T(x^2) = 4x^2$, therefore the matrix is diagonal with entries $1$, $2$ and $4$. The determinant of a diagonal matrix is the product of the diagonal entries, so it equals $1 \cdot 2 \cdot 4 = 8$. The value $7$ is the trace of the same matrix.
