For the linear map $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$, $f(x, y) = (2x - y,\ x + y,\ 3y)$, what is the associated matrix with respect to the standard bases?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The domain has dimension $2$ and the codomain has dimension $3$, so the matrix has $3$ rows and $2$ columns. With respect to the standard bases the $i$-th row contains the coefficients of $x$ and $y$ in the $i$-th component of $f$. The third component is $3y = 0 \cdot x + 3y$, therefore the third row is $(0, 3)$. The columns are thus $f(e_1) = (2, 1, 0)$ and $f(e_2) = (-1, 1, 3)$, and with this matrix one obtains, for example, $f(1, -2) = (4, -1, -6)$.
