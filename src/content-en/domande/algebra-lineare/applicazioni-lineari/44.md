In $\mathbb{R}^2$ consider the basis $\mathcal{B}' = \big( (1, 2), (1, 3) \big)$. What are the coordinates of the vector $v = (2, 5)$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}'$?

- $(1, 1)$
- $(7, 19)$
- $(-4, 3)$
- $(2, 5)$

## Explanation

The coordinates are the coefficients $a$ and $b$ such that $(2, 5) = a\,(1, 2) + b\,(1, 3)$, that is, the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = 2 \\[4pt]
2a + 3b = 5
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting twice the first equation from the second one obtains $b = 1$, so $a = 1$. Therefore $[v]_{\mathcal{B}'} = (1, 1)$. In terms of matrices one has $[v]_{\mathcal{B}'} = P^{-1} v$, where $P$ has as columns the vectors of $\mathcal{B}'$.
