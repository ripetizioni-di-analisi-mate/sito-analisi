$f(x,y)=(0,3x+y)$. Which vector with first component one spans the kernel?

- $\left(1,3\right)$
- $\left(1,0\right)$
- $\left(1,1\right)$
- $\left(1,-3\right)$

## Explanation

The first image component always vanishes. With $x=1$, the second vanishes precisely when $y=-a$. Substituting $a=3$ gives $\left(1,-3\right)$.
