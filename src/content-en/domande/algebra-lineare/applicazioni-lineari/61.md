$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ -2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(2,-1\right)$. If $f(x)=Ax$, what is the first component of $f(u)$?

- $2$
- $7$
- $5$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The first component of the product uses the first row of $A$. Multiply its entries by the components of $u$. The result is $5$.
