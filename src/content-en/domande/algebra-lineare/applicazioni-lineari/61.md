For the linear map $f : \mathbb{R}^4 \to \mathbb{R}^3$
$$
f(x, y, z, w) = (x + y + 2z,\ y + z + w,\ x + 2y + 3z + w) ,
$$
which of the following is a basis of $\operatorname{Im} f$?

- $(1, 1, 2, 0),\ (0, 1, 1, 1)$
- $(1, 0, 1),\ (1, 1, 2),\ (2, 1, 3)$
- $(-1, -1, 1, 0),\ (1, -1, 0, 1)$
- $(1, 0, 1),\ (1, 1, 2)$

## Explanation

The matrix $A$ of $f$ with respect to the standard bases has rows $(1, 1, 2, 0)$, $(0, 1, 1, 1)$ and $(1, 2, 3, 1)$. The third row is the sum of the first two, so the reduction $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1 - R_2$ turns it into zero and the row echelon form has its pivots in the first two columns. Therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ and a basis of the image is formed by the first two columns of $A$, that is, $(1, 0, 1)$ and $(1, 1, 2)$. The rows of $A$ are vectors of $\mathbb{R}^4$ and do not belong to the codomain $\mathbb{R}^3$. Three vectors in a subspace of dimension $2$ are linearly dependent. The vectors $(-1, -1, 1, 0)$ and $(1, -1, 0, 1)$ form a basis of the kernel.
