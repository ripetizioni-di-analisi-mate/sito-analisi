The linear map $F:M_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R^2$ is $F(X)=(\operatorname{tr}X,x_{12}-x_{21})$. The domain basis is changed and one new basis vector is $E_{12}+E_{21}$. What can be deduced about the corresponding column without computing the entire new matrix?

- It is zero only in the standard codomain basis.
- Its second component is $2$ in the standard basis.
- It is a zero column in every codomain basis.
- It becomes zero only if the domain basis is orthogonal.

## Explanation

The chosen matrix has zero trace and both off-diagonal entries equal one, so their difference vanishes. The vector belongs to the kernel of $F$. Its image is the zero vector, whose coordinates vanish in every basis.
