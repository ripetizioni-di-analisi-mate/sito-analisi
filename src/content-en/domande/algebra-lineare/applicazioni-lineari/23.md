Let $f : M_2(\mathbb{R}) \to \mathbb{R}^2$, $f \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} = (a + d,\ b - c)$. Denoting by $E_{ij}$ the matrix with $1$ in position $(i, j)$ and $0$ elsewhere, what is the matrix of $f$ with respect to the basis $(E_{11}, E_{12}, E_{21}, E_{22})$ of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ and the standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The domain has dimension $4$ and the codomain has dimension $2$, so the matrix has $2$ rows and $4$ columns. The columns are the images of the basis vectors, in the order $f(E_{11}) = (1, 0)$, $f(E_{12}) = (0, 1)$, $f(E_{21}) = (0, -1)$ and $f(E_{22}) = (1, 0)$. The coordinates of a matrix with respect to this basis are $(a, b, c, d)$, therefore the first row contains the coefficients of $a + d$ and the second those of $b - c$.
