For the endomorphism $f(x, y, z) = (x - y,\ y - z,\ x - z)$ of $\mathbb{R}^3$, what is the Cartesian equation of $\operatorname{Im} f$?

- $x - y - z = 0$
- $x + y - z = 0$
- $x + y + z = 0$
- $x - y + z = 0$

## Explanation

A vector in the image has the form $(a - b,\ b - c,\ a - c)$, and its third component is the sum of the first two, since $(a - b) + (b - c) = a - c$. So every vector $(x, y, z)$ in $\operatorname{Im} f$ satisfies $x + y - z = 0$. The first two rows of the matrix, $(1, -1, 0)$ and $(0, 1, -1)$, are not proportional and the third, $(1, 0, -1)$, is their sum, therefore the rank is $2$ and $\operatorname{Im} f$ has dimension $2$. The image is a subspace of dimension $2$ contained in the plane $x + y - z = 0$, so it coincides with this plane.
