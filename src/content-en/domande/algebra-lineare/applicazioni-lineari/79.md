$A=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 5 & -3\end{pmatrix}$. What is the dimension of the image of $x\mapsto Ax$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The two columns are independent because the minor in the first two rows has determinant one. They span a two-dimensional image. The result is $2$.
