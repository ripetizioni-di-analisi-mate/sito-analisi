$f(e_1)=\left(2,3\right)$, $f(e_2)=\left(1,1\right)$. The given pairs are the images of $e_1$ and $e_2$. What is $f(2,-1)$?

- $\left(3,5\right)$
- $\left(5,3\right)$
- $\left(1,2\right)$
- $\left(5,7\right)$

## Explanation

By linearity, double the first image and subtract the second. The result is $\left(3,5\right)$.
