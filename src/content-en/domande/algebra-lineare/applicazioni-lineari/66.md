$f(x,y)=(x+3y,y)$. What is the preimage of $(1,2)$?

- $\left(-2,2\right)$
- $\left(-5,2\right)$
- $\left(-5,1\right)$
- $\left(7,2\right)$

## Explanation

The image’s second component equals $y$. With $y=2$, the first equation determines $x$. The result is $\left(-5,2\right)$.
