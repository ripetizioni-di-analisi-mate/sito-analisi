What are the coordinates of the polynomial $p(x) = 1 + x + x^2$ with respect to the basis $\big( 1,\ x - 1,\ (x - 1)^2 \big)$ of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$?

- $(1, 1, 1)$
- $(1, 3, 1)$
- $(3, 3, 1)$
- $(3, -1, 1)$

## Explanation

One looks for $a$, $b$, $c$ with $1 + x + x^2 = a + b\,(x - 1) + c\,(x - 1)^2$. Setting $x = 1$ gives $a = p(1) = 3$. Differentiating and setting $x = 1$ gives $b = p'(1) = 1 + 2 = 3$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is $c$ on the right and $1$ on the left, so $c = 1$. One checks that $3 + 3\,(x - 1) + (x - 1)^2 = 3 + 3x - 3 + x^2 - 2x + 1 = x^2 + x + 1$. The coordinates $(1, 1, 1)$ are those with respect to the basis $(1, x, x^2)$.
