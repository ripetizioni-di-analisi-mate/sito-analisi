$r(t)=(3,0,1)+t(1,2,0)$. How is $r$ positioned relative to the plane $z=0$?

- The line lies in the plane
- The line is parallel and disjoint
- They meet at one point
- The line is perpendicular to the plane

## Explanation

The direction vector has zero vertical component. All points are at height one, so the plane at height zero is never reached.
