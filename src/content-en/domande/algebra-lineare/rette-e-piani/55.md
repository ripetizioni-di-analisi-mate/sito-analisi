What are the parametric equations of the line through $P = (2, 1, 3)$ that intersects the $z$-axis and is parallel to the plane $z = 0$?

- $x = 2t,\ y = t,\ z = 3t$
- $x = 2 + t,\ y = 1 + t,\ z = 3$
- $x = 2,\ y = 1,\ z = 3 + t$
- $x = 2t,\ y = t,\ z = 3$

## Explanation

The line is parallel to the plane $z = 0$ if its direction vector has zero third component, so all its points have the same height as $P$, that is, $z = 3$. The $z$-axis has a single point with $z = 3$, the point $H = (0, 0, 3)$, therefore the required line passes through $H$ and through $P$. Its direction vector is $\overrightarrow{HP} = (2, 1, 0)$ and the parametric equations are $x = 2t$, $y = t$, $z = 3$, which give $H$ for $t = 0$ and $P$ for $t = 1$. The line $x = 2t$, $y = t$, $z = 3t$ meets the $z$-axis at the origin but is not parallel to the plane $z = 0$. The line $x = 2 + t$, $y = 1 + t$, $z = 3$ is parallel to the plane but does not meet the $z$-axis, because the condition $x = 0$ gives $y = -1$.
