For the skew lines $r : x = t$, $y = 0$, $z = 0$ and $s : x = 1$, $y = u$, $z = 2 + u$, what are the points $H \in r$ and $K \in s$ where the common perpendicular meets the two lines?

- $H = (1, 0, 0)$, $K = (1, -1, 1)$
- $H = (0, 0, 0)$, $K = (1, 0, 2)$
- $H = (1, 0, 0)$, $K = (1, 1, 3)$
- $H = (1, 0, 0)$, $K = (1, -2, 0)$

## Explanation

The generic points of the two lines are $H = (t, 0, 0)$ and $K = (1, u, 2 + u)$, so $\overrightarrow{HK} = (1 - t,\ u,\ 2 + u)$. The common perpendicular is orthogonal to the direction vectors $\vec{v} = (1, 0, 0)$ and $\vec{w} = (0, 1, 1)$, therefore $t$ and $u$ satisfy the system
$$
\begin{cases}
\overrightarrow{HK} \cdot \vec{v} = 1 - t = 0 \\[4pt]
\overrightarrow{HK} \cdot \vec{w} = u + (2 + u) = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The system gives $t = 1$ and $u = -1$, so $H = (1, 0, 0)$ and $K = (1, -1, 1)$. The distance between the lines is $\|\overrightarrow{HK}\| = \|(0, -1, 1)\| = \sqrt{2}$.
