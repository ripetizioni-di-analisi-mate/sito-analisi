$r(t)=(2,0,0)+t(0,1,1)$, $P=(0,3,1)$. What is the first coordinate of the orthogonal projection of $P$ onto $r$?

- $0$
- $3$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

The projection lies on the line. All points of $r$ have the same first coordinate because the direction vector has zero first component. The result is $2$.
