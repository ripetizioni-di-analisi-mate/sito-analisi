The line $r$ has equations $x - 2y = 0$, $z + 1 = 0$. As $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, the equation $(x - 2y) + k\,(z + 1) = 0$ describes planes containing $r$. Which of the following planes contains $r$ but is not obtained for any value of $k$?

- $z + 1 = 0$
- $x - 2y = 0$
- $x - 2y + z + 1 = 0$
- $x - 2y - 2z - 2 = 0$

## Explanation

The pencil with axis $r$ consists of the planes $\lambda\,(x - 2y) + \mu\,(z + 1) = 0$, with $(\lambda, \mu) \neq (0, 0)$. For $\lambda \neq 0$ one can divide by $\lambda$ and set $k = \dfrac{\mu}{\lambda}$, so the equation $(x - 2y) + k\,(z + 1) = 0$ describes all the planes of the pencil with $\lambda \neq 0$. The only plane left out is the one with $\lambda = 0$, that is, $z + 1 = 0$. The other three planes are obtained for $k = 0$, $k = 1$ and $k = -2$.
