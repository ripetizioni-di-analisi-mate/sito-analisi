$n=\left(4,1,5\right)$, $v=\left(1,t,0\right)$. For which $t$ is the direction $v$ parallel to a plane with normal $n$?

- $-4$
- $0$
- $4$
- $-5$

## Explanation

A direction parallel to the plane is perpendicular to its normal. Here their dot product is $a+t$. Substituting $a=4$ gives $-4$.
