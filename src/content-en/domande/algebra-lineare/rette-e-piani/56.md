$\pi\colon x+2y+z=0$. What is the first coordinate of the point on $\pi$ with $y=1$ and $z=2$?

- $-2$
- $4$
- $0$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The fixed coordinates contribute $a$ and $2$ in the plane equation. To make the sum zero, $x$ is the negative of their sum. Substituting $a=2$ gives $-4$.
