Which of the following points belongs to the line $x = 2 - t$, $y = 1 + 3t$, $z = 4t$?

- $(1, 4, 3)$
- $(3, -2, 4)$
- $(4, -5, 8)$
- $(0, 7, 8)$

## Explanation

A point belongs to the line if there is a value of $t$ that satisfies the three equations simultaneously. For $(0, 7, 8)$ the first equation gives $t = 2$, and with $t = 2$ one obtains $y = 7$ and $z = 8$, so the point belongs to the line. For $(1, 4, 3)$ the first two equations give $t = 1$, while the third gives $t = \dfrac{3}{4}$, therefore the point does not belong to the line. In the same way $(3, -2, 4)$ and $(4, -5, 8)$ satisfy the first two equations with $t = -1$ and with $t = -2$, but for these values the third equation gives $z = -4$ and $z = -8$.
