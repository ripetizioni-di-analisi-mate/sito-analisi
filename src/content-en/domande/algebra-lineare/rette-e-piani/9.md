## Explanation

By the formula for the **distance from a point to a plane**, the coordinates of $P$ are substituted into the left-hand side of the equation of $\pi$ and the result is divided by the norm of the normal vector $(1, -2, 2)$. The numerator is $|2 - 2 \cdot (-1) + 2 \cdot 3 + 1| = |2 + 2 + 6 + 1| = 11$. The denominator is $\sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = 3$, therefore $d(P, \pi) = \dfrac{11}{3}$.
