$\pi\colon x+4y+z=0$. What is the first coordinate of the point on $\pi$ with $y=1$ and $z=2$?

- $6$
- $-6$
- $-2$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The fixed coordinates contribute $a$ and $2$ in the plane equation. To make the sum zero, $x$ is the negative of their sum. Substituting $a=4$ gives $-6$.
