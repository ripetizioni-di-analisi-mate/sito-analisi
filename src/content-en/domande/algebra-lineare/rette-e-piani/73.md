$r(t)=(0,1,0)+t(1,0,3)$, $\pi\colon z=6$. Which point is the intersection?

- $\left(1,1,6\right)$
- $\left(2,0,6\right)$
- $\left(2,1,6\right)$
- $\left(2,1,3\right)$

## Explanation

On the line the third coordinate is $at$. Set it to $2a$ to get $t=2$ and find the other coordinates. Substituting $a=3$ gives $\left(2,1,6\right)$.
