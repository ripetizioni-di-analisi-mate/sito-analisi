The point $T = (3, 1, 4)$ lies on the sphere with equation $(x - 1)^2 + y^2 + (z - 2)^2 = 9$. What is the tangent plane to the sphere at $T$?

- $3x + y + 4z - 26 = 0$
- $2x + y + 2z - 6 = 0$
- $x + 2z - 11 = 0$
- $2x + y + 2z - 15 = 0$

## Explanation

The sphere has center $C = (1, 0, 2)$ and radius $3$, and $\overrightarrow{CT} = (2, 1, 2)$ has norm $3$. The plane $\pi$ through $T$ orthogonal to $\overrightarrow{CT}$ has equation $2(x - 3) + (y - 1) + 2(z - 4) = 0$, that is, $2x + y + 2z - 15 = 0$. For every point $Q \neq T$ of $\pi$ the triangle $CTQ$ has a right angle at $T$, so by the **Pythagorean theorem** $CQ^2 = 9 + TQ^2 > 9$, and $Q$ lies outside the sphere. Therefore $\pi$ has only the point $T$ in common with the sphere, that is, it is the tangent plane at $T$. The plane $2x + y + 2z - 6 = 0$ has the same normal vector but passes through the center $C$. The planes $3x + y + 4z - 26 = 0$ and $x + 2z - 11 = 0$ pass through $T$, but their normal vectors are the coordinate vectors of $T$ and of $C$, which are not parallel to $\overrightarrow{CT}$.
