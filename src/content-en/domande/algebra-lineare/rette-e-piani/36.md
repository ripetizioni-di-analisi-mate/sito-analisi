For which values of $d$ does the plane $x + 2y + 2z + d = 0$ have distance $2$ from the origin?

- $d = 2$ or $d = -2$
- $d = 18$ or $d = -18$
- $d = 6$ or $d = -6$
- $d = 2\sqrt{5}$ or $d = -2\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

By the formula for the **distance from a point to a plane**, the distance from the origin to the plane is
$$
\frac{|0 + 0 + 0 + d|}{\sqrt{1 + 4 + 4}} = \frac{|d|}{3} .
$$
The condition $\dfrac{|d|}{3} = 2$ is equivalent to $|d| = 6$, so $d = 6$ or $d = -6$. The two planes obtained are parallel and lie on opposite sides of the origin.
