Which of the following lines is skew to the $z$-axis?

- $x = 1,\ y = t,\ z = 0$
- $x = t,\ y = t,\ z = 1$
- $x = 1,\ y = 2,\ z = t$
- $x = t,\ y = 0,\ z = t$

## Explanation

The $z$-axis consists of the points with $x = y = 0$ and has direction vector $(0, 0, 1)$. The line $x = 1$, $y = 2$, $z = t$ has direction vector $(0, 0, 1)$, so it is parallel to the axis. The lines $x = t$, $y = t$, $z = 1$ and $x = t$, $y = 0$, $z = t$ pass, for $t = 0$, through $(0, 0, 1)$ and through the origin respectively, which lie on the axis, so they intersect the axis. The line $x = 1$, $y = t$, $z = 0$ has direction vector $(0, 1, 0)$, not parallel to $(0, 0, 1)$, and none of its points has $x = 0$; therefore it has no points in common with the axis and is **skew** to it.
