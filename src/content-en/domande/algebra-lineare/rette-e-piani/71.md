The plane $\pi : 2x - y + 2z = 15$ intersects the sphere with equation $(x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 + (z - 3)^2 = 25$ in a circle. What is the radius of the circle?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{34}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The sphere has center $C = (1, 2, 3)$ and radius $R = 5$. The distance from the center to the plane is
$$
d(C, \pi) = \frac{|2 - 2 + 6 - 15|}{\sqrt{4 + 1 + 4}} = \frac{9}{3} = 3 .
$$
The center of the circle is the orthogonal projection $H$ of $C$ onto $\pi$, and for every point $Q$ of the circle the triangle $CHQ$ has a right angle at $H$, with hypotenuse $CQ = R$. By the **Pythagorean theorem** the radius is therefore $\sqrt{R^2 - d^2} = \sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$. The value $2$ is the difference $R - d$, the value $3$ is the distance $d$, and $\sqrt{34}$ is obtained by adding the squares instead of subtracting them.
