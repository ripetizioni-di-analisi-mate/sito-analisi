$\pi\colon x+3y+z=0$. What is the first coordinate of the point on $\pi$ with $y=1$ and $z=2$?

- $-5$
- $-2$
- $5$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The fixed coordinates contribute $a$ and $2$ in the plane equation. To make the sum zero, $x$ is the negative of their sum. Substituting $a=3$ gives $-5$.
