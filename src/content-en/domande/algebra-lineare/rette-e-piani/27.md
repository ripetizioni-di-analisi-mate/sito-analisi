Which statement about the lines $r : x = 1 + 2t$, $y = -t$, $z = 3 + 4t$ and $s : x = 3 - 4u$, $y = -1 + 2u$, $z = 7 - 8u$ is true?

- The lines are parallel and distinct
- The lines intersect at a single point
- The lines are skew
- The lines coincide

## Explanation

The direction vectors are $\vec{v} = (2, -1, 4)$ and $\vec{w} = (-4, 2, -8) = -2\,\vec{v}$, so the lines are parallel. Two parallel lines with a point in common coincide. The point $B = (3, -1, 7)$ of $s$ is obtained from the equations of $r$ with $t = 1$, therefore it also belongs to $r$, and the lines are **coincident**.
