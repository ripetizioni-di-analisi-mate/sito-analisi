The orthogonal projection of $P=(3,2,1)$ onto the line $r=(t,1,t)$ is $H=(2,1,2)$. If $P$ is displaced by $(0,a,0)$, how does the foot of the perpendicular change as $a$ varies?

- It moves by $(0,a,0)$.
- It moves by $(a,0,a)$.
- It remains $H$ for every displacement.
- It equals $H$ only for $a=0$.

## Explanation

The direction of $r$ is $(1,0,1)$, perpendicular to every displacement $(0,a,0)$. The component of the point along the line is therefore unchanged. The whole family has the same foot.
