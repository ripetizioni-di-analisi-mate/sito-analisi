The point $H = (1, 0, 1)$ is the orthogonal projection of the point $P = (3, 1, 2)$ onto a plane $\pi$. What is the equation of $\pi$?

- $2x + y + z = 9$
- $3x + y + 2z = 5$
- $2x + y + z = 3$
- $2x + y + z = 6$

## Explanation

The segment $PH$ is perpendicular to $\pi$, so the vector $\overrightarrow{HP} = (2, 1, 1)$ is a normal vector of $\pi$. Therefore $\pi$ has equation $2x + y + z = d$ for some $d \in \mathbb{R}$. The point $H$ belongs to $\pi$, so $d = 2 \cdot 1 + 0 + 1 = 3$, and the plane is $2x + y + z = 3$. The plane $2x + y + z = 9$ passes through $P$ instead of $H$, the plane $2x + y + z = 6$ passes through the midpoint of $PH$, and the plane $3x + y + 2z = 5$ uses the coordinates of $P$ as the normal.
