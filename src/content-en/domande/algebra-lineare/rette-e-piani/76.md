What are the parametric equations of the line through $P = (3, 2, 1)$ that meets the line $r : x = t$, $y = 1$, $z = t$ and is perpendicular to it?

- $x = 3 + t,\ y = 2,\ z = 1 + t$
- $x = 3 + t,\ y = 2,\ z = 1 - t$
- $x = 3 - t,\ y = 2 - t,\ z = 1 + t$
- $x = 3 + 3t,\ y = 2 + t,\ z = 1 + t$

## Explanation

The required line passes through $P$ and through the orthogonal projection $H$ of $P$ onto $r$. With $A = (0, 1, 0)$ and $\vec{v} = (1, 0, 1)$ we have $\overrightarrow{AP} = (3, 1, 1)$, so the parameter of $H$ is
$$
t = \frac{\overrightarrow{AP} \cdot \vec{v}}{\|\vec{v}\|^2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2 ,
$$
and $H = (2, 1, 2)$. The vector $\overrightarrow{PH} = (-1, -1, 1)$ is orthogonal to $\vec{v}$, and the line $PH$ has equations $x = 3 - t$, $y = 2 - t$, $z = 1 + t$. The line $x = 3 + t$, $y = 2$, $z = 1 - t$ is perpendicular to $r$ but does not meet it, because on it $y = 2$ while on $r$ we have $y = 1$. The line $x = 3 + 3t$, $y = 2 + t$, $z = 1 + t$ meets $r$ at $A$ but is not perpendicular to $r$.
