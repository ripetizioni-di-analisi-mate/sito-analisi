What is the symmetric of the point $P = (1, 2, 0)$ with respect to the line $r : x = t$, $y = t$, $z = 0$?

- $(2, 1, 0)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, \dfrac{3}{2}, 0 \right)$
- $(-1, -2, 0)$
- $(5, 4, 0)$

## Explanation

The line passes through the origin $O$ with direction vector $\vec{v} = (1, 1, 0)$. The orthogonal projection $H$ of $P$ onto $r$ corresponds to the parameter value
$$
t = \frac{\overrightarrow{OP} \cdot \vec{v}}{\|\vec{v}\|^2} = \frac{3}{2} ,
$$
so $H = \left( \dfrac{3}{2}, \dfrac{3}{2}, 0 \right)$. The point $H$ is the midpoint of the segment $PP'$, therefore $P' = 2H - P = (3, 3, 0) - (1, 2, 0) = (2, 1, 0)$.
