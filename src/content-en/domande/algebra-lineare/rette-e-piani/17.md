What is the Cartesian equation of the plane with parametric equations $P = (1, 0, 2) + s\,(1, 1, 0) + t\,(0, 1, -1)$, with $s, t \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $x - y - z + 1 = 0$
- $x + y + z - 3 = 0$
- $x - y + z - 3 = 0$
- $x + y - z + 1 = 0$

## Explanation

The plane is parallel to the vectors $\vec{u} = (1, 1, 0)$ and $\vec{w} = (0, 1, -1)$, which are not parallel to each other, so a normal vector is
$$
\vec{u} \times \vec{w} = \bigl( 1 \cdot (-1) - 0 \cdot 1,\; 0 \cdot 0 - 1 \cdot (-1),\; 1 \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot 0 \bigr) = (-1, 1, 1) .
$$
The plane passes through $(1, 0, 2)$, therefore it has equation $-(x - 1) + y + (z - 2) = 0$, that is, $-x + y + z - 1 = 0$. Multiplying by $-1$ gives $x - y - z + 1 = 0$.
