$r(t)=(0,1,0)+t(1,0,4)$, $\pi\colon z=8$. Which point is the intersection?

- $\left(2,0,8\right)$
- $\left(2,1,4\right)$
- $\left(1,1,8\right)$
- $\left(2,1,8\right)$

## Explanation

On the line the third coordinate is $at$. Set it to $2a$ to get $t=2$ and find the other coordinates. Substituting $a=4$ gives $\left(2,1,8\right)$.
