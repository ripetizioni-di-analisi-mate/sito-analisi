In the unit cube consider the diagonal line $r=(t,t,t)$ and the edge line $s=(1,0,u)$. Translate $s$ by the vector $(a,-a,0)$. For which value of $a$ do the two lines intersect?

- $a=\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $a=-1$
- $a=-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $a=0$

## Explanation

The first two coordinates on the translated line are $1+a$ and $-a$, and on the diagonal they agree. Solving $1+a=-a$ gives $a=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The third coordinate is free on $s$, so the intersection exists.
