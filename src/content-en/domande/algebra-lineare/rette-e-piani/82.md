$\pi_1\colon z=4$, $\pi_2\colon 2z=5$. How far apart are the two planes?

- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Divide the second equation by two. The distance between these horizontal planes is the absolute difference of their heights. The result is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
