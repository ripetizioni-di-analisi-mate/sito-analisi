Which statement about the line $r : x = 1 + 2t$, $y = -t$, $z = 3t$ and the plane $\pi : 4x - 2y + 6z = 5$ is true?

- The line is parallel to the plane
- The line is contained in the plane
- The line is perpendicular to the plane
- The line forms an angle of $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ with the plane

## Explanation

The direction vector of the line is $\vec{v} = (2, -1, 3)$ and the normal vector of the plane is $\vec{n} = (4, -2, 6) = 2\,\vec{v}$. A line is perpendicular to a plane if and only if its direction vector is parallel to the normal vector, so $r$ is **perpendicular** to $\pi$. Consequently the angle between $r$ and $\pi$ is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, and the line meets the plane at a single point, since $\vec{n} \cdot \vec{v} = 28 \neq 0$.
