- $3x - y + z - 3 = 0$
- $4x + y - z - 4 = 0$
- $x - 7y + 7z - 1 = 0$
- $3x - y + z + 3 = 0$

## Explanation

The planes containing $r$ are those of the pencil $\lambda\,(x - y + z - 1) + \mu\,(2x + y - z - 2) = 0$, with normal vector $(\lambda + 2\mu,\ -\lambda + \mu,\ \lambda - \mu)$. A plane of the pencil is parallel to $s$ if its normal vector is orthogonal to the direction vector $(1, 2, -1)$ of $s$, that is, if
$$
(\lambda + 2\mu) + 2(-\lambda + \mu) - (\lambda - \mu) = -2\lambda + 5\mu = 0 .
$$
The condition is satisfied by $\lambda = 5$ and $\mu = 2$, which give the plane $9x - 3y + 3z - 9 = 0$. Dividing by $3$ gives $3x - y + z - 3 = 0$.
