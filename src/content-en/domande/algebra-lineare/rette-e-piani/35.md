What is the distance from the line $r : x = 1 + t$, $y = 2 + t$, $z = -t$ to the plane $\pi : x - y = 3$?

- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $0$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

## Explanation

The direction vector of $r$ is $\vec{v} = (1, 1, -1)$ and the normal vector of $\pi$ is $\vec{n} = (1, -1, 0)$. Since $\vec{n} \cdot \vec{v} = 1 - 1 + 0 = 0$, the line is parallel to the plane, and its point $P_0 = (1, 2, 0)$ does not lie in the plane, because $1 - 2 = -1 \neq 3$. A line parallel to a plane has, from the plane, the distance of any one of its points, so
$$
d(r, \pi) = d(P_0, \pi) = \frac{|1 - 2 - 3|}{\sqrt{1 + 1}} = \frac{4}{\sqrt{2}} = 2\sqrt{2} .
$$
