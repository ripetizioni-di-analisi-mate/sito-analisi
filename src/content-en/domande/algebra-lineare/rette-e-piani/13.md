What is the distance between the skew lines $r : x = 1 + t$, $y = 2 + t$, $z = 1$ and $s : x = 1 + u$, $y = -1$, $z = -2 + u$?

- $6$
- $2\sqrt{3}$
- $2$
- $3\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The line $r$ passes through $A = (1, 2, 1)$ with direction vector $\vec{v} = (1, 1, 0)$, and the line $s$ passes through $B = (1, -1, -2)$ with direction vector $\vec{w} = (1, 0, 1)$. The cross product is $\vec{v} \times \vec{w} = (1, -1, -1)$, of norm $\sqrt{3}$, and $\overrightarrow{AB} = (0, -3, -3)$. By the formula for the **distance between two skew lines**
$$
d(r, s) = \frac{|\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot (\vec{v} \times \vec{w})|}{\|\vec{v} \times \vec{w}\|} = \frac{|0 + 3 + 3|}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{3}} = 2\sqrt{3} .
$$
