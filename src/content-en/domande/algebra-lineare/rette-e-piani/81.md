$\pi_1\colon x=4$, $\pi_2\colon y=5$. What is the sum of the coordinates of the point shared by the planes with $z=0$?

- $0$
- $-1$
- $9$
- $1$

## Explanation

The intersection has fixed $x$ and $y$, with $z$ free. Setting $z=0$ leaves the sum of the two fixed coordinates. The result is $9$.
