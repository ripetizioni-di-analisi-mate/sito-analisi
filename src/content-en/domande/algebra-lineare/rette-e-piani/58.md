$r(t)=(0,1,0)+t(1,0,2)$, $\pi\colon z=4$. Which point is the intersection?

- $\left(2,1,2\right)$
- $\left(2,1,4\right)$
- $\left(1,1,4\right)$
- $\left(2,0,4\right)$

## Explanation

On the line the third coordinate is $at$. Setting it equal to $2a$ gives $t=2$ and yields the other coordinates. Substituting $a=2$ gives $\left(2,1,4\right)$.
