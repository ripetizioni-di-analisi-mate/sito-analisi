What are the Cartesian equations of the line through the points $A = (2, 1, -1)$ and $B = (4, 0, 1)$?

- $\begin{cases} x - 2y = 0 \\ 2y - z - 3 = 0 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x + 2y - 4 = 0 \\ 2y - z - 3 = 0 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} 2x - y + 2z - 1 = 0 \\ x + 2y - 4 = 0 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x + 2y - 4 = 0 \\ 2y + z - 1 = 0 \end{cases}$

## Explanation

The vector $\overrightarrow{AB} = (2, -1, 2)$ is a direction vector of the line, so the parametric equations are $x = 2 + 2t$, $y = 1 - t$, $z = -1 + 2t$. From the second equation one obtains $t = 1 - y$. Substituting into the first gives $x = 2 + 2(1 - y) = 4 - 2y$, that is, $x + 2y - 4 = 0$. Substituting into the third gives $z = -1 + 2(1 - y) = 1 - 2y$, that is, $2y + z - 1 = 0$. The two planes obtained in this way contain the line and are not parallel, therefore their equations are Cartesian equations of the line.
