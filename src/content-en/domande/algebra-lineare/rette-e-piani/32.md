What are the parametric equations of the line through $P = (1, -2, 0)$ perpendicular to the plane $3x - y + 2z = 4$?

- $x = 1 + 3t,\ y = -2 - t,\ z = 2t$
- $x = 3 + t,\ y = -1 - 2t,\ z = 2$
- $x = 1 + 3t,\ y = -2 + t,\ z = 2t$
- $x = 1 + t,\ y = -2 + t,\ z = -t$

## Explanation

A line is perpendicular to a plane if and only if its direction vector is parallel to the normal vector of the plane. The normal vector is $\vec{n} = (3, -1, 2)$, so the required line passes through $P$ with direction vector $\vec{n}$. Its parametric equations are therefore $x = 1 + 3t$, $y = -2 - t$, $z = 2t$. The vector $(1, 1, -1)$ satisfies $(1, 1, -1) \cdot \vec{n} = 0$, so the line with this direction vector is parallel to the plane.
