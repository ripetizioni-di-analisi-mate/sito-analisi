Which is the plane through $P = (1, 2, -1)$ parallel to the plane $\pi : 2x - 3y + z = 5$?

- $2x - 3y + z - 5 = 0$
- $2x - 3y + z + 3 = 0$
- $2x - 3y + z + 5 = 0$
- $x + 2y - z - 6 = 0$

## Explanation

The planes parallel to $\pi$ form the improper pencil $2x - 3y + z + k = 0$, with $k \in \mathbb{R}$. The plane of the pencil passes through $P$ if $2 \cdot 1 - 3 \cdot 2 + (-1) + k = 0$, that is, if $k - 5 = 0$. Hence $k = 5$, and the required plane is $2x - 3y + z + 5 = 0$.
