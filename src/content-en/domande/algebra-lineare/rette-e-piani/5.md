For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the line $r : x = 1 + t$, $y = 1 - t$, $z = 2 + t$ parallel to the plane $\pi_k : x + 2y + kz = 4$?

- $k = -1$
- $k = 1$
- $k = -3$
- $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The line has direction vector $\vec{v} = (1, -1, 1)$ and the plane has normal vector $\vec{n} = (1, 2, k)$. By the theorem on the **relative position of a line and a plane**, the line is parallel to the plane only if $\vec{n} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$. Since $\vec{n} \cdot \vec{v} = 1 - 2 + k = k - 1$, the condition gives $k = 1$. For $k = 1$ the point $P_0 = (1, 1, 2)$ on the line does not lie in the plane, because $1 + 2 + 2 = 5 \neq 4$; therefore the line is parallel to the plane and has no points in common with it.
