$n=\left(2,1,3\right)$, $v=\left(1,t,0\right)$. For which $t$ is direction $v$ parallel to a plane with normal $n$?

- $-3$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

A direction parallel to the plane is perpendicular to its normal. Here their dot product is $a+t$. Substituting $a=2$ gives $-2$.
