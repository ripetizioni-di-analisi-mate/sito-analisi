What are the parametric equations of the line through $P = (1, 0, 2)$, parallel to the plane $\pi : x + y + z = 0$ and perpendicular to the line $r : x = 1 + t$, $y = 2t$, $z = 3$?

- $x = 1 + t,\ y = t,\ z = 2 + t$
- $x = 1 - 2t,\ y = t,\ z = 2 + t$
- $x = 1 + t,\ y = 2t,\ z = 2$
- $x = 1 + 2t,\ y = t,\ z = 2 + t$

## Explanation

Let $\vec{d}$ be the direction vector of the required line. The line is parallel to $\pi$ if $\vec{d}$ is orthogonal to $\vec{n} = (1, 1, 1)$, and it is perpendicular to $r$ if $\vec{d}$ is orthogonal to $\vec{w} = (1, 2, 0)$. Since $\vec{n}$ and $\vec{w}$ are not parallel, one can take
$$
\vec{d} = \vec{n} \times \vec{w} = (-2, 1, 1) ,
$$
so the line has equations $x = 1 - 2t$, $y = t$, $z = 2 + t$. The vector $(1, 1, 1)$ gives the line perpendicular to $\pi$ and the vector $(1, 2, 0)$ the line parallel to $r$. For $(2, 1, 1)$ we have $(2, 1, 1) \cdot (1, 1, 1) = 4 \neq 0$, therefore the corresponding line is not parallel to $\pi$.
