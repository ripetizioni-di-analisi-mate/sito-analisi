What is the distance between the parallel planes $2x - y + 2z - 1 = 0$ and $4x - 2y + 4z + 7 = 0$?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The formula for the distance between parallel planes applies to two equations with the same coefficients of $x$, $y$, $z$. Dividing the second equation by $2$ gives $2x - y + 2z + \dfrac{7}{2} = 0$, so $d_1 = -1$ and $d_2 = \dfrac{7}{2}$. The norm of the normal vector $(2, -1, 2)$ is $3$, therefore
$$
d = \frac{|d_1 - d_2|}{3} = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{2} = \frac{3}{2} .
$$
