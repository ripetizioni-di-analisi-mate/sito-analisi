What is the distance from the point $P = (1, 2, 3)$ to the $x$-axis?

- $1$
- $\sqrt{14}$
- $5$
- $\sqrt{13}$

## Explanation

The $x$-axis passes through the origin $O$ with direction vector $\vec{v} = (1, 0, 0)$, of norm $1$. The cross product $\overrightarrow{OP} \times \vec{v} = (1, 2, 3) \times (1, 0, 0) = (0, 3, -2)$ has norm $\sqrt{13}$, so by the formula for the **distance from a point to a line** we have $d = \sqrt{13}$. The same value is obtained from the orthogonal projection of $P$ onto the axis, which is $H = (1, 0, 0)$, since $\|\overrightarrow{HP}\| = \|(0, 2, 3)\| = \sqrt{13}$.
