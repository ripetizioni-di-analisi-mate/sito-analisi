In the cube with a vertex at $O = (0, 0, 0)$ and the opposite vertex at $V = (1, 1, 1)$, what is the sine of the angle between the diagonal $OV$ and the face lying in the plane $z = 0$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$

## Explanation

The diagonal has direction vector $\vec{v} = (1, 1, 1)$ and the plane $z = 0$ has normal vector $\vec{n} = (0, 0, 1)$. By the definition of the angle between a line and a plane
$$
\sin\theta = \frac{|\vec{v} \cdot \vec{n}|}{\|\vec{v}\|\, \|\vec{n}\|} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} .
$$
The same value comes from the triangle $OHV$, with a right angle at $H = (1, 1, 0)$, the projection of $V$ onto the face. The leg opposite the angle is $HV = 1$ and the hypotenuse is $OV = \sqrt{3}$, so the sine equals $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3} = \dfrac{OH}{OV}$ is the cosine of the same angle.
