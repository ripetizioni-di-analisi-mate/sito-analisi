Which of the following planes contains the line $r : x = 1 + 2t$, $y = 3 - t$, $z = 2 + t$?

- $2x - y + z - 1 = 0$
- $x + 2y - 7 = 0$
- $x - 2y + 5 = 0$
- $y - z - 1 = 0$

## Explanation

All four planes pass through the point $P_0 = (1, 3, 2)$ of $r$. By the theorem on the **relative position of a line and a plane**, a plane through $P_0$ contains $r$ if and only if its normal vector is orthogonal to the direction vector $\vec{v} = (2, -1, 1)$. For the plane $x + 2y - 7 = 0$ we have $(1, 2, 0) \cdot (2, -1, 1) = 0$, therefore this plane contains the line. For the planes $2x - y + z - 1 = 0$, $x - 2y + 5 = 0$ and $y - z - 1 = 0$ the dot products are $6$, $4$ and $-2$ respectively, so the line meets them only at the point $P_0$.
