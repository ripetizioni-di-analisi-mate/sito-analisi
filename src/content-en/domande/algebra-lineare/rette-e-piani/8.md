## Explanation

The normal vectors of the two planes defining $r$ are $(1, -1, 0)$ and $(0, 0, 1)$, so a direction vector of $r$ is $(1, -1, 0) \times (0, 0, 1) = (-1, -1, 0)$, parallel to $\vec{v} = (1, 1, 0)$. The plane has normal vector $\vec{n} = (0, 1, -1)$. Since $\vec{v} \cdot \vec{n} = 1$ and $\|\vec{v}\| = \|\vec{n}\| = \sqrt{2}$, we have
$$
\sin\theta = \frac{|\vec{v} \cdot \vec{n}|}{\|\vec{v}\| \, \|\vec{n}\|} = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
The dot product with $\vec{n}$ measures the angle $\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \theta$ between the line and the normal to the plane, therefore the formula contains the sine of $\theta$, and $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
