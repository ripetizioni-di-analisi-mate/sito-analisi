What is the equation of the plane through $P_0 = (2, -1, 1)$ orthogonal to the vector $\vec{n} = (3, 1, -2)$?

- $3x + y - 2z + 3 = 0$
- $2x - y + z - 3 = 0$
- $3x + y - 2z - 7 = 0$
- $3x + y - 2z - 3 = 0$

## Explanation

The vector $\vec{n}$ is normal to the plane, so the coefficients of $x$, $y$, $z$ in the equation are $3$, $1$, $-2$. The point $P$ belongs to the plane if and only if $\vec{n} \cdot \overrightarrow{P_0 P} = 0$, that is,
$$
3(x - 2) + (y + 1) - 2(z - 1) = 0 .
$$
Expanding the products gives $3x + y - 2z - 6 + 1 + 2 = 0$, therefore the plane has equation $3x + y - 2z - 3 = 0$.
