- $x + 3y + 4z + 7 = 0$
- $x - 3y + 4z + 5 = 0$
- $x + 3y + 4z - 7 = 0$
- $x + 3y - 4z - 7 = 0$

## Explanation

The vectors $\overrightarrow{AB} = (2, -2, 1)$ and $\overrightarrow{AC} = (-1, -1, 1)$ are parallel to the plane and are not parallel to each other, so their cross product
$$
\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = \bigl( (-2) \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot (-1),\; 1 \cdot (-1) - 2 \cdot 1,\; 2 \cdot (-1) - (-2) \cdot (-1) \bigr) = (-1, -3, -4)
$$
is a normal vector to the plane. The normal vector is defined up to a factor, therefore one can take $\vec{n} = (1, 3, 4)$. The plane through $A$ with this normal vector has equation $(x - 1) + 3(y - 2) + 4z = 0$, that is, $x + 3y + 4z - 7 = 0$.
