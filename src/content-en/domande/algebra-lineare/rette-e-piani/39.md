What is the orthogonal projection of the point $P = (2, 3, -1)$ onto the plane $x + y + z = 1$?

- $(3, 4, 0)$
- $(0, 1, -3)$
- $(1, 2, -2)$
- $(-1, 0, -4)$

## Explanation

The orthogonal projection is the point where the plane meets the line through $P$ parallel to the normal vector $\vec{n} = (1, 1, 1)$, that is, the line $(2 + t,\ 3 + t,\ -1 + t)$. Substituting into the equation of the plane gives $4 + 3t = 1$, so $t = -1$. Therefore the orthogonal projection is $H = (1, 2, -2)$. The point $(0, 1, -3) = 2H - P$ is instead the symmetric of $P$ with respect to the plane.
