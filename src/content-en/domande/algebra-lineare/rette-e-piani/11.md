What is the reflection $P'$ of $P = (3, -1, 2)$ across the plane $\pi : 2x - y + z = 3$?

- $(1, 0, 1)$
- $(7, -3, 4)$
- $(-1, 1, 0)$
- $(-2, 1, -1)$

## Explanation

The orthogonal projection $H$ of $P$ onto $\pi$ is the point where the plane meets the line through $P$ parallel to the normal vector $\vec{n} = (2, -1, 1)$, that is, the line $(3 + 2t,\ -1 - t,\ 2 + t)$. Substituting into the equation of the plane gives $2(3 + 2t) - (-1 - t) + (2 + t) = 3$, that is, $9 + 6t = 3$, so $t = -1$ and $H = (1, 0, 1)$. The point $H$ is the midpoint of the segment $PP'$, therefore
$$
P' = 2H - P = (2, 0, 2) - (3, -1, 2) = (-1, 1, 0) .
$$
