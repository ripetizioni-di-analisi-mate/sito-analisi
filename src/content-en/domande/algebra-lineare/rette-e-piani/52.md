$\pi_1\colon z=2$, $\pi_2\colon 2z=3$. How far apart are the two planes?

- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The second equation is divided by two. The distance between these horizontal planes is the absolute difference of their heights. The result is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
