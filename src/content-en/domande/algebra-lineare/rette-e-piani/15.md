At which points does the plane $x - 2y + 3z = 6$ intersect the coordinate axes?

- $(6, 0, 0)$, $(0, 3, 0)$, $(0, 0, 2)$
- $(1, 0, 0)$, $(0, -2, 0)$, $(0, 0, 3)$
- $(6, 0, 0)$, $(0, -3, 0)$, $(0, 0, 2)$
- $(6, 0, 0)$, $(0, -12, 0)$, $(0, 0, 18)$

## Explanation

The intersection point with the $x$-axis has $y = z = 0$, so it satisfies $x = 6$ and is $(6, 0, 0)$. In the same way, setting $x = z = 0$ gives $-2y = 6$, that is, $y = -3$, and setting $x = y = 0$ gives $3z = 6$, that is, $z = 2$. Dividing the equation by $6$ gives the form
$$
\frac{x}{6} + \frac{y}{-3} + \frac{z}{2} = 1 ,
$$
in which the denominators are the nonzero coordinates of the three intersection points.
