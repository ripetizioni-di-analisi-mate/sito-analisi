$n=\left(3,1,-2\right)$, $v=\left(1,t,0\right)$. For which $t$ is direction $v$ parallel to a plane with normal $n$?

- $0$
- $3$
- $2$
- $-3$

## Explanation

A direction parallel to the plane is perpendicular to its normal. Here their dot product is $a+t$. Substituting $a=3$ gives $-3$.
