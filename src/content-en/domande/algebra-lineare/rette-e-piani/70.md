$r(t)=(0,0,0)+t(1,3,0)$, $s(u)=(0,1,0)+u(0,1,0)$. For which $u$ is the intersection point obtained?

- $3$
- $-1$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

Every point on $s$ has first coordinate zero, so the intersection requires $t=0$. The second coordinate of $s$ must then vanish. The result is $-1$.
