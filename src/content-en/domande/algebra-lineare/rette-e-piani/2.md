- $x - y - z = 0$
- $x + y - z + 2 = 0$
- $x - y + z - 4 = 0$
- $x + 2y - z + 3 = 0$

## Explanation

The plane is parallel to the two lines, so its normal vector is orthogonal to the direction vector $\vec{v} = (1, 2, -1)$ of $r$ and to the direction vector $\vec{w} = (1, 0, 1)$ of $s$. The two vectors are not parallel, therefore one can take
$$
\vec{n} = \vec{v} \times \vec{w} = \bigl( 2 \cdot 1 - (-1) \cdot 0,\; (-1) \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot 1,\; 1 \cdot 0 - 2 \cdot 1 \bigr) = (2, -2, -2) ,
$$
or the parallel vector $(1, -1, -1)$. The plane through $P = (1, -1, 2)$ with this normal vector has equation $(x - 1) - (y + 1) - (z - 2) = 0$, that is, $x - y - z = 0$.
