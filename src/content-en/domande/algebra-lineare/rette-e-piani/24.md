Which statement about the line $r : x = 1 + t$, $y = 2 - t$, $z = 3$ and the plane $\pi : x + y - 4z = -9$ is true?

- The line is contained in the plane
- The line is parallel to the plane and has no points in common with it
- The line and the plane intersect at a single point
- The line is perpendicular to the plane

## Explanation

The direction vector of the line is $\vec{v} = (1, -1, 0)$ and the normal vector of the plane is $\vec{n} = (1, 1, -4)$. Since $\vec{n} \cdot \vec{v} = 1 - 1 + 0 = 0$, the line is either parallel to the plane or contained in it. The point $P_0 = (1, 2, 3)$ of the line satisfies the equation of the plane, because $1 + 2 - 12 = -9$. Therefore, by the theorem on the **relative position of a line and a plane**, the line is **contained** in the plane.
