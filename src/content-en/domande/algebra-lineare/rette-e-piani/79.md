What is the reflection of the plane $\pi : x + 2y - z = 3$ through the point $C = (1, 1, 1)$?

- $x + 2y - z + 3 = 0$
- $x + 2y - z - 1 = 0$
- $x + 2y - z + 1 = 0$
- $x + 2y - z - 2 = 0$

## Explanation

The reflection of $P = (x, y, z)$ through $C$ is $P' = 2C - P = (2 - x,\ 2 - y,\ 2 - z)$. The point $P$ belongs to the reflected plane if and only if $P'$ belongs to $\pi$, that is, if and only if
$$
(2 - x) + 2(2 - y) - (2 - z) = 3 .
$$
Expanding gives $-x - 2y + z + 4 = 3$, that is, $x + 2y - z - 1 = 0$. The plane obtained is parallel to $\pi$, and $C$ is equidistant from the two planes, since substituting $C$ the left-hand sides $x + 2y - z - 3$ and $x + 2y - z - 1$ equal $-1$ and $1$. The plane $x + 2y - z + 1 = 0$ is obtained by using $P' = C - P$, and the plane $x + 2y - z - 2 = 0$ is the plane through $C$ parallel to $\pi$.
