Let $A = (1, 0, 1)$ and $B = (2, 2, 3)$. What is the length of the orthogonal projection of the segment $AB$ onto the plane $\pi : x + y + z = 1$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The vector $\overrightarrow{AB} = (1, 2, 2)$ splits into a component parallel to the normal vector $\vec{n} = (1, 1, 1)$ and a component parallel to the plane, and the projection of the segment has the length of this second component. The component along $\vec{n}$ has length
$$
\frac{|\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \vec{n}|}{\|\vec{n}\|} = \frac{5}{\sqrt{3}} ,
$$
and $\|\overrightarrow{AB}\| = 3$. The two components are orthogonal, so by the **Pythagorean theorem** the length of the projection is
$$
\sqrt{9 - \frac{25}{3}} = \sqrt{\frac{2}{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{3}$ is the length of the component along $\vec{n}$, and $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is the square of the required length.
