$r(t)=(0,0,3)+t(2,1,0)$, $s(u)=(0,0,0)+u(2,1,0)$. How are the lines positioned in space?

- Coincident
- Skew
- Intersecting
- Distinct and parallel

## Explanation

The directions agree. The points on the two lines have different constant heights, so the lines are parallel and distinct.
