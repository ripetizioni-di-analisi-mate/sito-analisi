What is the intersection point of the line $x = 2t$, $y = 1 - t$, $z = 3 + t$ with the plane $x + y + z = 2$?

- $(2, 0, 4)$
- $(-4, 3, 1)$
- $(-2, 2, 2)$
- $(0, 1, 3)$

## Explanation

Substituting the parametric equations of the line into the equation of the plane gives
$$
2t + (1 - t) + (3 + t) = 2 ,
$$
that is, $2t + 4 = 2$. The equation has the unique solution $t = -1$, so the line and the plane have a single common point, the one corresponding to $t = -1$. Its coordinates are $x = -2$, $y = 2$, $z = 2$, therefore the point is $(-2, 2, 2)$.
