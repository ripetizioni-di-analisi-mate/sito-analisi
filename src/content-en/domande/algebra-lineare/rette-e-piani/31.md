For which value of $k$ are the planes $2x + ky - z = 1$ and $x - 3y + kz = 0$ perpendicular?

- $k = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $k = 1$
- $k = 2$

## Explanation

Two planes are perpendicular if and only if their normal vectors are orthogonal. The normal vectors are $(2, k, -1)$ and $(1, -3, k)$, and their dot product is $2 - 3k - k = 2 - 4k$. Therefore the planes are perpendicular if and only if $2 - 4k = 0$, that is, for $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
