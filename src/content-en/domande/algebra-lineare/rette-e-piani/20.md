Let $r$ be the line with Cartesian equations
$$
\begin{cases}
x - y + 2z = 1 \\[4pt]
2x + y - z = 0
\end{cases}
$$
What are the parametric equations of the line through $P = (0, 1, 2)$ parallel to $r$?

- $x = -t,\ y = 1 - 5t,\ z = 2 + 3t$
- $x = -t,\ y = 1 + 5t,\ z = 2 + 3t$
- $x = t,\ y = 1 - t,\ z = 2 + 2t$
- $x = 3t,\ y = 1,\ z = 2 + t$

## Explanation

The line $r$ lies in the two planes, so its direction vector is orthogonal to the normal vectors $\vec{n}_1 = (1, -1, 2)$ and $\vec{n}_2 = (2, 1, -1)$. One can therefore take
$$
\vec{v} = \vec{n}_1 \times \vec{n}_2 = \bigl( (-1) \cdot (-1) - 2 \cdot 1,\; 2 \cdot 2 - 1 \cdot (-1),\; 1 \cdot 1 - (-1) \cdot 2 \bigr) = (-1, 5, 3) .
$$
Two parallel lines have parallel direction vectors, so the required line passes through $P$ with direction vector $\vec{v}$. Its parametric equations are $x = -t$, $y = 1 + 5t$, $z = 2 + 3t$.
