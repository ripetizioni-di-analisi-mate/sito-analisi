Let $r$ be the line with Cartesian equations
$$
\begin{cases}
x - y = 1 \\[4pt]
y + z = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Which is the plane through $P = (2, 0, -1)$ perpendicular to $r$?

- $x - y + z - 1 = 0$
- $x + y - z - 3 = 0$
- $x + y - z + 3 = 0$
- $x - y - 2 = 0$

## Explanation

The direction vector of $r$ is orthogonal to the normal vectors $(1, -1, 0)$ and $(0, 1, 1)$, so one can take
$$
\vec{v} = (1, -1, 0) \times (0, 1, 1) = \bigl( (-1) \cdot 1 - 0 \cdot 1,\; 0 \cdot 0 - 1 \cdot 1,\; 1 \cdot 1 - (-1) \cdot 0 \bigr) = (-1, -1, 1) .
$$
A plane perpendicular to $r$ has as normal vector a direction vector of $r$, for example $(1, 1, -1)$. The plane through $P$ with this normal vector has equation $(x - 2) + y - (z + 1) = 0$, that is, $x + y - z - 3 = 0$.
