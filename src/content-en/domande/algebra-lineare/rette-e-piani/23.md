Which statement about the planes $\pi_1 : x - y + 2z = 3$ and $\pi_2 : -2x + 2y - 4z = -6$ is true?

- The planes are parallel and distinct
- The planes intersect in a line
- The planes coincide
- The planes are perpendicular

## Explanation

The normal vector of $\pi_2$ is $(-2, 2, -4) = -2\,(1, -1, 2)$, so the normal vectors are parallel and the planes are either parallel or coincident. Multiplying the whole equation of $\pi_1$ by $-2$ gives $-2x + 2y - 4z = -6$, which is the equation of $\pi_2$. The two equations are proportional, therefore the planes are **coincident**.
