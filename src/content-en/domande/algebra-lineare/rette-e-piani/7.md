## Explanation

The normal vectors are $\vec{n}_1 = (2, 1, 2)$ and $\vec{n}_2 = (0, 1, 1)$, with $\|\vec{n}_1\| = 3$ and $\|\vec{n}_2\| = \sqrt{2}$. Since $\vec{n}_1 \cdot \vec{n}_2 = 0 + 1 + 2 = 3$, the definition of the angle between two planes gives
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{|\vec{n}_1 \cdot \vec{n}_2|}{\|\vec{n}_1\| \, \|\vec{n}_2\|} = \frac{3}{3\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} .
$$
The angle belongs to the interval $\left[0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$, hence $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
