What is the distance from the point $P = (4, 1, 2)$ to the line $r : x = 2t$, $y = 1 + 2t$, $z = 1 + t$?

- $6\sqrt{2}$
- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$
- $\sqrt{17}$

## Explanation

The line passes through $A = (0, 1, 1)$ and has direction vector $\vec{v} = (2, 2, 1)$, of norm $3$. The vector $\overrightarrow{AP}$ is $(4, 0, 1)$, and
$$
\overrightarrow{AP} \times \vec{v} = \bigl( 0 \cdot 1 - 1 \cdot 2,\; 1 \cdot 2 - 4 \cdot 1,\; 4 \cdot 2 - 0 \cdot 2 \bigr) = (-2, -2, 8) ,
$$
which has norm $\sqrt{72} = 6\sqrt{2}$. By the formula for the **distance from a point to a line** this norm is divided by $\|\vec{v}\|$, so $d(P, r) = \dfrac{6\sqrt{2}}{3} = 2\sqrt{2}$.
