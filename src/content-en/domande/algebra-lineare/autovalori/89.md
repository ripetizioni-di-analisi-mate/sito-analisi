$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first component of a zero-eigenvalue eigenvector with second component one?

- $-5$
- $0$
- $- \dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

The zero eigenspace is the kernel. The first row with second component one gives $ax+b=0$. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$ gives $- \dfrac{5}{4}$.
