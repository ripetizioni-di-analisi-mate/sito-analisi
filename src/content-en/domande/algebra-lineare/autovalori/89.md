Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be invertible and diagonalizable, with $P^{-1}AP = D$ and $D$ diagonal. Which matrix is $P^{-1}A^{-1}P$?

- $D^{-1}$
- $D$
- $PD^{-1}P^{-1}$
- $-D$

## Explanation

Since $A$ is invertible, $0$ is not an eigenvalue, so the diagonal entries of $D$ are nonzero and $D$ is invertible. The inverse of a product is the product of the inverses in reverse order, therefore
$$
(P^{-1}AP)^{-1} = P^{-1}A^{-1}(P^{-1})^{-1} = P^{-1}A^{-1}P .
$$
It follows that $P^{-1}A^{-1}P = D^{-1}$, the diagonal matrix with the reciprocals of the eigenvalues of $A$, and the same $P$ also diagonalizes $A^{-1}$. The matrix $PD^{-1}P^{-1}$ is instead equal to $A^{-1}$.
