Let $f : M_2(\mathbb{R}) \to M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be the endomorphism defined by $f(X) = BX$, with $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$. What is the eigenspace $V_2$ of $f$?

- The matrices with zero first column
- The matrices with zero first row
- The diagonal matrices
- The multiples of $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Let $X = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$. The product $BX$ multiplies the first row of $X$ by $1$ and the second by $2$, so
$$
f(X) = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 2c & 2d \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The equality $f(X) = 2X$ is equivalent to $a = 2a$ and $b = 2b$, that is, to $a = b = 0$, with $c$ and $d$ free. Hence $V_2$ consists of the **matrices with zero first row** and has dimension $2$. With respect to the basis $E_{11}, E_{12}, E_{21}, E_{22}$ the matrix associated with $f$ is diagonal with entries $1, 1, 2, 2$, so $f$ is diagonalizable and $V_1$ consists of the matrices with zero second row.
