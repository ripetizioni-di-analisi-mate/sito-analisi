$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 0 \\ 0 & -3\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(0,1\right)$. What eigenvalue of $A^2-A$ corresponds to $u$?

- $-3$
- $12$
- $-7$
- $6$

## Explanation

On the vector $u$, $A$ acts by a factor of $c$. Thus $A^2$ acts by $c^2$, and their difference acts by $c^2-c$. Substituting $c=-3$ gives $12$.
