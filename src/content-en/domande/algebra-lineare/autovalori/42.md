The vector $v = (1, -1, 1)$ is an eigenvector of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & -2 \\ 0 & -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the eigenvalue associated with $v$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

The row-by-column product gives
$$
Av = \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 1 + 0 \\ 1 - 2 - 2 \\ 0 + 1 + 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -3 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The result is $3(1, -1, 1) = 3v$, so $v$ is an eigenvector associated with the eigenvalue $3$. The second component of $Av$ equals $-3$ because the second component of $v$ equals $-1$, and the ratio between the two is still $3$.
