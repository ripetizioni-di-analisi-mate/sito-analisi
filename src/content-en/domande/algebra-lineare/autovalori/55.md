What is $\operatorname{tr}(A^{10})$ for the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$?

- $1$
- $10$
- $1025$
- $-1023$

## Explanation

We have $\operatorname{tr} A = 1$ and $\det A = -2$, so $p_A(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - \lambda - 2 = (\lambda - 2)(\lambda + 1)$ and the eigenvalues are $2$ and $-1$, distinct. The matrix is therefore diagonalizable, with $A^{10} = PD^{10}P^{-1}$ and $D^{10} = \begin{pmatrix} 2^{10} & 0 \\ 0 & (-1)^{10} \end{pmatrix}$. The matrices $A^{10}$ and $D^{10}$ are similar, so they have the same trace, and
$$
\operatorname{tr}(A^{10}) = 2^{10} + (-1)^{10} = 1024 + 1 = 1025 .
$$
The value $1 = (\operatorname{tr} A)^{10}$ is obtained by raising the trace to the tenth power, which does not give the trace of the power.
