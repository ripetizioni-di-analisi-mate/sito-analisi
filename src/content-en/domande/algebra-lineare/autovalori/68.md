$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ 0 & -2\end{pmatrix}$. Which eigenvalue of $A^{-1}$ corresponds to the eigenvector $e_1$ of $A$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

We have $Ae_1=ae_1$ and $A$ is invertible. Applying the inverse changes the factor to $1/a$. Substituting $a=3$ gives $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
