$A=\begin{pmatrix}0 & -9 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. Which description fits the real spectrum?

- One repeated real eigenvalue
- Zero and a nonzero real eigenvalue
- Two distinct real eigenvalues
- No real eigenvalues

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2+a^2$, with $a>0$. It has no real roots.
