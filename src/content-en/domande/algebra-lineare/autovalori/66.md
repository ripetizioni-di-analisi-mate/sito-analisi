A matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ has the eigenvalue $4$, with $\operatorname{rk}(A - 4I_3) = 1$, and has $\operatorname{tr} A = 10$. What are the eigenvalues of $A$, each repeated as many times as its algebraic multiplicity?

- $4,\ 3,\ 3$
- $4,\ 4,\ 2$
- $4,\ 4,\ 4$
- $4,\ 1,\ 5$

## Explanation

By the computation of eigenspaces $m_g(4) = 3 - \operatorname{rk}(A - 4I_3) = 3 - 1 = 2$. By the relation between the multiplicities $m_a(4) \geq m_g(4) = 2$, so $4$ appears at least twice among the roots of $p_A$. If $m_a(4) = 3$, the trace would be $4 + 4 + 4 = 12 \neq 10$, therefore $m_a(4) = 2$ and the third eigenvalue $\mu$ is real and different from $4$. Since the trace is the sum of the eigenvalues, $4 + 4 + \mu = 10$, so $\mu = 2$. The triples $4, 3, 3$ and $4, 1, 5$ have sum $10$, but in them $m_a(4) = 1 < m_g(4)$, which is impossible.
