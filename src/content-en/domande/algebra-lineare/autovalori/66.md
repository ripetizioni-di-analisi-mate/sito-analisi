$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & -2 \\ 0 & 3\end{pmatrix}$. Is the matrix diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$?

- No, because it has no real eigenvalues
- No, because its only eigenspace has dimension one
- Yes, because every triangular matrix is diagonalizable
- Yes, because it has two independent eigenvectors

## Explanation

The diagonal entry is the only eigenvalue, with algebraic multiplicity two. Since the off-diagonal entry is nonzero, the eigenspace has only one direction.
