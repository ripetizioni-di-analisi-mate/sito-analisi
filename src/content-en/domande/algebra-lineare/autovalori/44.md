Let $f : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be the endomorphism defined by $f(x, y, z) = (x + y,\ x + y,\ 2z)$. What is the eigenspace $V_2$ of $f$?

- $\langle (1, 1, 0),\ (0, 0, 1) \rangle$
- $\langle (0, 0, 1) \rangle$
- $\langle (1, -1, 0) \rangle$
- $\langle (1, 1, 0) \rangle$

## Explanation

The matrix associated with $f$ with respect to the standard basis is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Expanding $\det(A - \lambda I_3)$ along the third row gives $p_A(\lambda) = (2 - \lambda)\left[(1 - \lambda)^2 - 1\right] = (2 - \lambda)\lambda(\lambda - 2)$, so $m_a(2) = 2$. The system $(A - 2I_3)v = 0$ reduces to the equation $-x + y = 0$, with $z$ free, so $V_2 = \{(x, x, z) : x, z \in \mathbb{R}\}$, spanned by $(1, 1, 0)$ and $(0, 0, 1)$. The vector $(1, -1, 0)$ instead spans $V_0$, since $f(1, -1, 0) = (0, 0, 0)$.
