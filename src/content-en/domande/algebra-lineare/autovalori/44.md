What are the eigenvalues of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 2 & 6 & 7 \\ 1 & 3 & -3 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $5,\ 2,\ 1,\ -1$
- $4,\ 3,\ 0,\ 0$
- $5,\ 2,\ 0,\ 0$
- $5,\ 2,\ 1,\ 1$

## Explanation

The matrix $A - \lambda I_4$ has a zero $2 \times 2$ block in the lower left corner, so its determinant is the product of the determinants of the two diagonal blocks. Therefore
$$
p_A(\lambda) = \det \begin{pmatrix} 4 - \lambda & 2 \\ 1 & 3 - \lambda \end{pmatrix} \cdot \det \begin{pmatrix} -\lambda & 1 \\ 1 & -\lambda \end{pmatrix} = (\lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 10)(\lambda^2 - 1) .
$$
The first factor equals $(\lambda - 5)(\lambda - 2)$ and the second equals $(\lambda - 1)(\lambda + 1)$. Hence $p_A(\lambda) = (\lambda - 5)(\lambda - 2)(\lambda - 1)(\lambda + 1)$, and the eigenvalues are $5$, $2$, $1$ and $-1$. The entries of the upper right block do not affect the eigenvalues.
