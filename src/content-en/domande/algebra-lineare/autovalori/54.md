The figure shows the graph of the characteristic polynomial $p_A(\lambda)$ of a matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$. What is $\operatorname{tr} A$?

::grafico{f="-(x-1)^2*(x-3)" xmin=-0.4 xmax=3.8 ymin=-7 ymax=7 punti="1,0;3,0" nomi="λ,p" titolo="The graph of $p_A(\lambda)$: it touches the axis at $\lambda = 1$ and crosses it at $\lambda = 3$"}

- $4$
- $5$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

The polynomial $p_A$ has degree $3$ and vanishes only at $\lambda = 1$ and at $\lambda = 3$. At $\lambda = 1$ the graph touches the axis without crossing it, so $1$ is a root of even multiplicity, that is, a double root; at $\lambda = 3$ the graph crosses the axis, so $3$ is a simple root. Hence the eigenvalues, counted with algebraic multiplicity, are $1$, $1$ and $3$. Since the trace is the sum of the eigenvalues counted with their multiplicity, $\operatorname{tr} A = 1 + 1 + 3 = 5$. The value $4$ is obtained by counting the eigenvalue $1$ only once.
