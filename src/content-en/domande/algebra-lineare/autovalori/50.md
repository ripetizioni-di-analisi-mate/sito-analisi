The characteristic polynomial of a matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ has the simple roots $1$, $2$ and $4$. What is the trace of the inverse matrix $A^{-1}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$

## Explanation

The number $0$ is not an eigenvalue, so $A$ is invertible. If $Av = \lambda v$ with $\lambda \neq 0$, multiplying on the left by $A^{-1}$ gives $v = \lambda A^{-1} v$, that is, $A^{-1} v = \dfrac{1}{\lambda} v$. Hence the eigenvalues of $A^{-1}$ are $1$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$, each simple. The trace is the sum of the eigenvalues, therefore
$$
\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}) = 1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{4} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{1}{7}$ is the reciprocal of $\operatorname{tr} A$, which in general differs from the trace of the inverse.
