- It is diagonalizable, because $m_g(2) = m_a(2) = 2$
- It is not diagonalizable, because $m_g(2) = 1 < m_a(2) = 2$
- It is not diagonalizable, because $p_A$ has non-real roots
- It is diagonalizable, because all its eigenvalues are real

## Explanation

The only nonzero entry in the third column of $A - \lambda I_3$ is $2 - \lambda$, so expanding along this column gives $p_A(\lambda) = (2 - \lambda)(-1 - \lambda)(2 - \lambda) = (2 - \lambda)^2(-1 - \lambda)$. Therefore the eigenvalue $2$ has $m_a(2) = 2$. The matrix
$$
A - 2I_3 = \begin{pmatrix} -3 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 3 & -2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
has a zero second row and the other two rows independent, so it has rank $2$ and $m_g(2) = 3 - 2 = 1$. Since $m_g(2) < m_a(2)$, by the **diagonalizability criterion** the matrix **is not diagonalizable**.
