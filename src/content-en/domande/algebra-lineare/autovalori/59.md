$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first component of an eigenvector associated with eigenvalue zero, with its second component fixed at one?

- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The eigenspace corresponding to zero is the kernel. The first row, with the second component set to one, gives $ax+b=0$. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$ gives $- \dfrac{3}{2}$.
