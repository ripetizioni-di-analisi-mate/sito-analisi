Let $f : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be the orthogonal projection onto the plane $\pi : x + y + z = 0$. What is the characteristic polynomial $p_f(\lambda) = \det(A - \lambda I_3)$, where $A$ is the matrix associated with $f$ with respect to any basis?

- $-\lambda^2(\lambda - 1)$
- $-(\lambda - 1)^3$
- $-\lambda(\lambda - 1)^2$
- $-\lambda(\lambda + 1)^2$

## Explanation

Every vector of the plane $\pi$ coincides with its own projection, so $f(v) = v$ for $v \in \pi$, and $\pi$, which has dimension $2$, is contained in $V_1$. The normal vector $(1, 1, 1)$ has zero projection, so $f(1, 1, 1) = 0$ and $(1, 1, 1)$ is an eigenvector associated with the eigenvalue $0$. A basis of $\pi$ together with $(1, 1, 1)$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$ made of eigenvectors, and with respect to it the matrix of $f$ is $D = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. The characteristic polynomial does not depend on the basis, therefore
$$
p_f(\lambda) = (1 - \lambda)^2(0 - \lambda) = -\lambda(\lambda - 1)^2 .
$$
