In the space $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$ of polynomials of degree at most $2$ consider the endomorphism $f$ that maps $p(x)$ to $p(1 - x)$, that is, swaps the values at $x$ and at $1 - x$. Which polynomial is an eigenvector of $f$ associated with the eigenvalue $-1$?

- $2x - 1$
- $x^2 - x$
- $x$
- $1 - x$

## Explanation

A polynomial $p$ is an eigenvector associated with $-1$ if $p \neq 0$ and $p(1 - x) = -p(x)$. For $p(x) = 2x - 1$ we have $p(1 - x) = 2(1 - x) - 1 = 1 - 2x = -p(x)$, so $2x - 1$ is an **eigenvector** associated with $-1$. For $x^2 - x$ we have $(1 - x)^2 - (1 - x) = x^2 - x$, so this polynomial is an eigenvector associated with the eigenvalue $1$. Finally $f(x) = 1 - x$ and $f(1 - x) = x$, and neither result is a multiple of the original polynomial.
