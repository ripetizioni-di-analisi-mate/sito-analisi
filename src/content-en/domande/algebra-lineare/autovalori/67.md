$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & -2 \\ -2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(1,-1\right)$. What eigenvalue corresponds to $u$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

The first component of $Au$ is $a-b$. The second is its negative, so $Au$ is a multiple of $u$. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$ gives $5$.
