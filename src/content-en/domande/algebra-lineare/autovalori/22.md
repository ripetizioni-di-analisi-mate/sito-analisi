Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = 3I_2$. Which statement is true?

- $A$ and $B$ are similar, because they have the same characteristic polynomial
- $A$ and $B$ are not similar, because they have different traces
- $A$ and $B$ have the same characteristic polynomial and are not similar
- $A$ and $B$ are similar, because they are triangular with the same diagonal

## Explanation

Both matrices are triangular with diagonal entries $3$ and $3$, so they have characteristic polynomial $(3 - \lambda)^2$ and the same trace $6$. For every invertible matrix $P$ we have $P^{-1}(3I_2)P = 3P^{-1}P = 3I_2$, so the only matrix similar to $B$ is $B$ itself. Since $A \neq 3I_2$, the matrix $A$ **is not similar** to $B$. Hence equality of the characteristic polynomials is a *necessary* condition for similarity, and this example shows that it is not *sufficient*.
