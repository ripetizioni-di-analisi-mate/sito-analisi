$A=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 4 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the larger of the two real eigenvalues?

- $0$
- $-2$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2-a^2$. The roots are negatives of each other and the positive one is $a$. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$ gives $2$.
