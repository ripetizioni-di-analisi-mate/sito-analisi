Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & \dfrac{1}{2} \\[4pt] 0 & \dfrac{1}{2} \end{pmatrix}$. To which matrix does $A^n$ tend as $n \to +\infty$, entry by entry?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & \dfrac{1}{2} \\[4pt] 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The matrix is triangular with eigenvalues $1$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$; we have $V_1 = \langle (1, 0) \rangle$ and, from the equation $\dfrac{1}{2}x + \dfrac{1}{2}y = 0$, $V_{1/2} = \langle (1, -1) \rangle$. With $P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$, which satisfies $P^{-1} = P$, we have
$$
A^n = P \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{1}{2^n} \end{pmatrix} P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 - \frac{1}{2^n} \\ 0 & \frac{1}{2^n} \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{1}{2^n} \to 0$, the matrix $A^n$ tends to $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ is the limit of $D^n$, which coincides with the limit of $A^n$ only if $P = I_2$.
