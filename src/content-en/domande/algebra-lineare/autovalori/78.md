$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ 0 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. What is the dimension of the eigenspace for $\lambda=a$, where $a$ is the diagonal entry?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

Subtracting $aI$ leaves a single nonzero entry. The resulting matrix has a one-dimensional kernel. Substituting $a=4$ gives $1$.
