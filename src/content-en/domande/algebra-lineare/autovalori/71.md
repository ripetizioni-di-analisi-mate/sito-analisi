$A=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 9 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the larger of the two real eigenvalues?

- $3$
- $0$
- $-3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2-a^2$. The roots are negatives of each other and the positive one is $a$. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$ gives $3$.
