## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2 - 5\lambda + 6 = (\lambda - 2)(\lambda - 3)$, so the eigenvalues are $2$ and $3$, distinct, and $A$ is diagonalizable. From $A - 2I_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$ we obtain the equation $x = y$, so $V_2 = \langle (1, 1) \rangle$. From $A - 3I_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$ we obtain the equation $x = 2y$, so $V_3 = \langle (2, 1) \rangle$. The columns of $P$ are eigenvectors and the diagonal of $D$ contains the corresponding eigenvalues in the same order, so the first column $(1, 1)$ corresponds to $2$ and the second column $(2, 1)$ corresponds to $3$.
