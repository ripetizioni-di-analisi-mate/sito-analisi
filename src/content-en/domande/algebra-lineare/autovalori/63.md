The figure shows the vectors $e_1 = (1, 0)$ and $e_2 = (0, 1)$ of the standard basis and their images under a matrix $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$. What is the eigenspace $V_1$ of $A$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,2,0,Ae₁;0,0,1,0,e₁;0,0,0,1,e₂;0,0,1,1,Ae₂" xmin=-0.6 xmax=2.6 ymin=-0.6 ymax=1.6 uguale=1 titolo="The vectors $e_1$, $e_2$ with the images $Ae_1 = (2, 0)$ and $Ae_2 = (1, 1)$"}

- $\langle (1, 0) \rangle$
- $\langle (1, 1) \rangle$
- $\langle (1, -1) \rangle$
- $\langle (0, 1) \rangle$

## Explanation

The columns of the matrix are the images of the vectors of the standard basis, so
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The matrix is triangular, hence the eigenvalues are $2$ and $1$. The system $(A - I_2)v = 0$, with $A - I_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, reduces to the equation $x + y = 0$, so $V_1 = \langle (1, -1) \rangle$. As a check, $A(1, -1) = Ae_1 - Ae_2 = (2, 0) - (1, 1) = (1, -1)$. The line $\langle (1, 0) \rangle$ is instead the eigenspace $V_2$, since $Ae_1 = 2e_1$.
