The matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 \\ -4 & 4 & 3 \end{pmatrix}
$$
has the double eigenvalue $3$. Which is a basis of the eigenspace $V_3$?

- $(1, 1, 0)$
- $(1, 1, 0),\ (0, 0, 1)$
- $(1, 0, 2)$
- $(1, -1, 0),\ (0, 0, 1)$

## Explanation

The eigenspace $V_3$ is the kernel of the matrix
$$
A - 3I_3 = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ -4 & 4 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The third row is twice the first, so the matrix has rank $1$ and $\dim V_3 = 3 - 1 = 2$. The system reduces to the equation $-2x + 2y = 0$, that is, $y = x$, with $z$ free. Therefore $V_3 = \{(x, x, z) : x, z \in \mathbb{R}\}$, and a basis is given by $(1, 1, 0)$ and $(0, 0, 1)$. The vector $(1, 0, 2)$ spans the eigenspace $V_1$, since $A(1, 0, 2) = (1, 0, 2)$.
