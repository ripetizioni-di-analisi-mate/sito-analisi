$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}$. Which eigenvalue of $A^{-1}$ corresponds to the eigenvector $e_1$ of $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

We have $Ae_1=ae_1$ and $A$ is invertible. Applying the inverse changes the factor to $1/a$. Substituting $a=4$ gives $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
