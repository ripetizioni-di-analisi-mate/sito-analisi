Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$, let $v$ be an eigenvector of $A$ associated with the eigenvalue $3$ and let $B = P^{-1}AP$, with $P \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ invertible. Which vector is certainly an eigenvector of $B$ associated with the eigenvalue $3$?

- $v$
- $Pv$
- $P^{-1}v$
- $P^T v$

## Explanation

Applying $B$ to the vector $P^{-1}v$ gives
$$
B(P^{-1}v) = P^{-1}AP\,P^{-1}v = P^{-1}Av = P^{-1}(3v) = 3P^{-1}v .
$$
Since $P^{-1}$ is invertible and $v \neq 0$, also $P^{-1}v \neq 0$, so $P^{-1}v$ is an **eigenvector** of $B$ associated with $3$. Similar matrices have the same eigenvalues, but in general different eigenvectors. The vector $v$ is an eigenvector of $B$ only for particular choices of $P$, for example $P = I_3$.
