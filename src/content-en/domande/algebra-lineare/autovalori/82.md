A matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ has the eigenvalue $2$, and the complex number $1 + i$ is a root of its characteristic polynomial. What is the characteristic polynomial $p_A(\lambda) = \det(A - \lambda I_3)$?

- $-(\lambda - 2)(\lambda^2 + 2\lambda + 2)$
- $-(\lambda - 2)(\lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 2)$
- $-(\lambda - 2)(\lambda^2 - 2\lambda)$
- $-(\lambda - 2)(\lambda - 1)^2$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial $p_A$ has real coefficients, so together with the root $1 + i$ it also has the conjugate root $1 - i$. Since $p_A$ has degree $3$ and leading coefficient $-1$, its roots are exactly $2$, $1 + i$ and $1 - i$, and $p_A(\lambda) = -(\lambda - 2)(\lambda - 1 - i)(\lambda - 1 + i)$. The product of the two complex factors is
$$
(\lambda - 1)^2 - i^2 = (\lambda - 1)^2 + 1 = \lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 2 ,
$$
hence $p_A(\lambda) = -(\lambda - 2)(\lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 2)$. The factor $\lambda^2 - 2\lambda$ comes from writing $-1$ instead of $-i^2 = +1$, and the factor $\lambda^2 + 2\lambda + 2$ has the roots $-1 \pm i$.
