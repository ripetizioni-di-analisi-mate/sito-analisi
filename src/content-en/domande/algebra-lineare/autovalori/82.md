$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ 5 & 4\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(1,-1\right)$. What eigenvalue corresponds to $u$?

- $-9$
- $-1$
- $9$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first component of $Au$ is $a-b$. The second is its negative, so $Au$ is a multiple of $u$. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$ gives $-1$.
