For which $k \in \mathbb{R}$ are both eigenvalues of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & k \\ k & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ strictly positive?

- $k > -1$
- $0 < k < 1$
- $k < -1$ or $k > 1$
- $-1 < k < 1$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is $p_A(\lambda) = (1 - \lambda)^2 - k^2 = (1 - \lambda - k)(1 - \lambda + k)$, so the eigenvalues are $1 + k$ and $1 - k$. Both are positive if the conditions
$$
\begin{cases}
1 + k > 0 \\[4pt]
1 - k > 0
\end{cases}
$$
hold together, that is, $k > -1$ and $k < 1$. Hence the eigenvalues are positive for $-1 < k < 1$. The condition $k > -1$ only guarantees that $1 + k$ is positive.
