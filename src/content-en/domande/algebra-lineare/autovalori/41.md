A matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ has eigenvalues $1$, $2$ and $3$. What is $\det(A^2)$?

- $14$
- $12$
- $36$
- $6$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial of $A$ has the three real roots $1$, $2$, $3$, so the determinant of $A$ is their product, $\det A = 6$. By **Binet's theorem** we have $\det(A^2) = (\det A)^2 = 36$. The same number is obtained by observing that $A$ is diagonalizable, because it has three distinct eigenvalues, and that $A^2 = PD^2P^{-1}$ has eigenvalues $1$, $4$, $9$, whose product is $36$. The number $14 = 1 + 4 + 9$ is instead the trace of $A^2$.
