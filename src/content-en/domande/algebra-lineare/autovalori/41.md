A two-state system has column transition matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}\dfrac12&\dfrac14\\[3pt]\dfrac12&\dfrac34\end{pmatrix}$. What is the probability of the first state in a stationary distribution?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The distribution $(p,1-p)$ must be fixed by the transformation. The first component after a transition is $\dfrac p2+\dfrac{1-p}{4}$. Setting it equal to $p$ gives $3p=1$.
