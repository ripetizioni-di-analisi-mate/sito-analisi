What is the characteristic polynomial of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$?

- $\lambda^2 + 6\lambda + 11$
- $\lambda^2 - 6\lambda - 1$
- $\lambda^2 - 6\lambda + 11$
- $\lambda^2 - 6\lambda + 5$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is
$$
p_A(\lambda) = \det \begin{pmatrix} 5 - \lambda & -2 \\ 3 & 1 - \lambda \end{pmatrix} = (5 - \lambda)(1 - \lambda) - (-2) \cdot 3 .
$$
The product of the diagonal entries is $\lambda^2 - 6\lambda + 5$ and the term $-(-2) \cdot 3$ equals $+6$, so $p_A(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - 6\lambda + 11$. The result agrees with the formula $p_A(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - (\operatorname{tr} A)\lambda + \det A$, since $\operatorname{tr} A = 6$ and $\det A = 5 + 6 = 11$.
