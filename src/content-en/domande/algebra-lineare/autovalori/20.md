For which value of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ does the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} k & 2 \\ 3 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$ have the eigenvalue $0$?

- $k = 0$
- $k = -1$
- $k = -6$
- $k = 1$

## Explanation

Since $p_A(0) = \det A$, the number $0$ is an eigenvalue of $A$ if and only if $\det A = 0$. We have $\det A = 6k - 6$, so the condition is satisfied for $k = 1$. For $k = 1$ the characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2 - 7\lambda = \lambda(\lambda - 7)$, with eigenvalues $0$ and $7$. For $k = 0$ we have $\det A = -6 \neq 0$, so $0$ is not an eigenvalue, even though a diagonal entry is zero.
