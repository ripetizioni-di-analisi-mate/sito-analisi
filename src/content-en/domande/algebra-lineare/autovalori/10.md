## Explanation

The matrix is triangular, so its eigenvalues are $2$, with $m_a(2) \geq 2$, and $k$. We have
$$
A - 2I_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & k & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & k - 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
If $k \neq 0$ and $k \neq 2$ this matrix has rank $2$, so $m_g(2) = 1 < 2 = m_a(2)$. If $k = 2$ the eigenvalue $2$ is triple and the rank is $1$, so $m_g(2) = 2 < 3$. If $k = 0$ the rank is $1$ and $m_g(2) = 2 = m_a(2)$, so $A$ is **diagonalizable if and only if** $k = 0$.
