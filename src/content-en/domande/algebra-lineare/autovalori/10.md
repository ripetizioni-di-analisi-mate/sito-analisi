The matrices $A_k=\begin{pmatrix}1&k\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ are diagonalizable for every $k\in\mathbb R$. For which $h$ can one basis diagonalize both $A_h$ and $A_1$?

- $h=0$
- For every $h\in\mathbb R$.
- For every $h\ne1$.
- $h=1$

## Explanation

The eigenspace for eigenvalue one is always spanned by $(1,0)$. The one for eigenvalue two is spanned by $(k,1)$ and agrees with the line for $k=1$ only if $h=1$. A common basis requires both lines.
