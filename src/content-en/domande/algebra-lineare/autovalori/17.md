What are the eigenvalues of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 5 & -1 \\ 0 & -3 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
each repeated as many times as its algebraic multiplicity?

- $2,\ 5,\ -1$
- $-1,\ 4,\ 2$
- $2,\ -3,\ 4$
- $2,\ 2,\ -3$

## Explanation

The matrix is upper triangular, so $A - \lambda I_3$ is triangular as well and its determinant is the product of the diagonal entries. Therefore
$$
p_A(\lambda) = (2 - \lambda)(-3 - \lambda)(2 - \lambda) = (2 - \lambda)^2(-3 - \lambda) .
$$
Hence the eigenvalues are $2$, with $m_a(2) = 2$, and $-3$, with $m_a(-3) = 1$. The entries above the diagonal do not appear in the characteristic polynomial of a triangular matrix.
