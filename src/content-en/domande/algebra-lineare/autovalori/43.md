Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be a diagonalizable matrix with $p_A(\lambda) = -(\lambda - 2)^2(\lambda + 1)$. What is $\operatorname{rk}(A - 2I_3)$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $A$ is diagonalizable, by the **diagonalizability criterion** we have $m_g(2) = m_a(2) = 2$. On the other hand $m_g(2) = 3 - \operatorname{rk}(A - 2I_3)$, so $\operatorname{rk}(A - 2I_3) = 3 - 2 = 1$. Rank $3$ is excluded, because $2$ is an eigenvalue and therefore $\det(A - 2I_3) = 0$. Rank $0$ is excluded, because it would give $A = 2I_3$, whose only eigenvalue is $2$.
