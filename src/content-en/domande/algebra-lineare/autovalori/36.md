A matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ is diagonalizable and has characteristic polynomial $p_A(\lambda) = -(\lambda - 1)^2(\lambda - 4)$. How many diagonal matrices are similar to $A$?

- $3$
- $1$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

If $D$ is a diagonal matrix similar to $A$, by the **invariance under similarity** we have $p_D = p_A$. The characteristic polynomial of a diagonal matrix is the product of the factors $d_{ii} - \lambda$, so the diagonal entries of $D$ are $1$, $1$ and $4$ in some order. The entry $4$ can occupy any of the three positions, therefore the possible matrices are
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 4 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
They are all similar to $A$. Indeed, if $P^{-1}AP = D$ and two columns of $P$ are swapped, the resulting matrix is still invertible and diagonalizes $A$, with the two corresponding entries of $D$ swapped. Hence there are **$3$** diagonal matrices similar to $A$.
