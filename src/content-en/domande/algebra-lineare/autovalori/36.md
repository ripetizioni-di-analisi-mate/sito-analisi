For which $k \in \mathbb{R}$ is the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & k \end{pmatrix}
$$
diagonalizable?

- For no value of $k$
- For $k \neq 1$
- For $k = 1$
- For every $k \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

The matrix is triangular, so its eigenvalues are $1$, with $m_a(1) \geq 2$, and $k$. We have
$$
A - I_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & k - 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
If $k \neq 1$ the rank is $2$, so $m_g(1) = 1 < 2 = m_a(1)$. If $k = 1$ the rank is $1$, so $m_g(1) = 2 < 3 = m_a(1)$. In both cases the condition $m_g(1) = m_a(1)$ is not satisfied, so $A$ **is not diagonalizable** for any value of $k$.
