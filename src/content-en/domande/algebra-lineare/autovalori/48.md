$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 0 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. What is the dimension of the eigenspace associated with the eigenvalue $\lambda=a$, where $a$ is the diagonal entry?

- $0$
- $2$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

Subtracting $aI$ leaves a single nonzero entry. The kernel of the resulting matrix is a line. Substituting $a=2$ gives $1$.
