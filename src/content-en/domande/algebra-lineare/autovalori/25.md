The matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -1 \\ 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ has the single eigenvalue $3$. What is the eigenspace $V_3$?

- $\langle (1, 2) \rangle$
- $\langle (2, 1) \rangle$
- $\mathbb{R}^2$
- $\langle (1, -2) \rangle$

## Explanation

We have $p_A(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - 6\lambda + 9 = (\lambda - 3)^2$, so $3$ is the only eigenvalue, with $m_a(3) = 2$. The matrix $A - 3I_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 4 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$ has its second row equal to twice the first, so it has rank $1$ and $V_3$ is a line. The system reduces to the equation $2x - y = 0$, that is, $y = 2x$, so $V_3 = \langle (1, 2) \rangle$. The eigenspace would be all of $\mathbb{R}^2$ only for $A = 3I_2$.
