Let $f : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to \mathbb{R}_2[x]$ be the endomorphism defined by $f(p(x)) = p(0)\, x^2$. The only eigenvalue of $f$ is $0$. What are its algebraic multiplicity and its geometric multiplicity?

- $m_a(0) = 3$, $m_g(0) = 3$
- $m_a(0) = 3$, $m_g(0) = 2$
- $m_a(0) = 1$, $m_g(0) = 1$
- $m_a(0) = 2$, $m_g(0) = 2$

## Explanation

With respect to the basis $\{1, x, x^2\}$ we have $f(1) = x^2$, $f(x) = 0$ and $f(x^2) = 0$, so the associated matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The matrix is triangular with zero diagonal, therefore $p_A(\lambda) = -\lambda^3$ and $m_a(0) = 3$. Since $A$ has rank $1$, we get $m_g(0) = 3 - 1 = 2$. In agreement with this computation, $V_0 = \ker f$ consists of the polynomials with $p(0) = 0$, that is, $V_0 = \langle x,\ x^2 \rangle$.
