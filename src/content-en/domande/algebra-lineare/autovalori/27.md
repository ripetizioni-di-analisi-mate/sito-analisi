For the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
what are the algebraic multiplicity and the geometric multiplicity of the eigenvalue $3$?

- $m_a(3) = 2$, $m_g(3) = 2$
- $m_a(3) = 2$, $m_g(3) = 1$
- $m_a(3) = 1$, $m_g(3) = 1$
- $m_a(3) = 3$, $m_g(3) = 1$

## Explanation

Expanding $\det(A - \lambda I_3)$ along the first row gives
$$
p_A(\lambda) = (3 - \lambda)\left[(2 - \lambda)^2 - 1\right] = (3 - \lambda)(1 - \lambda)(3 - \lambda) ,
$$
so $p_A(\lambda) = (3 - \lambda)^2(1 - \lambda)$ and $m_a(3) = 2$. The matrix
$$
A - 3I_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
has its last two rows independent, so it has rank $2$ and $m_g(3) = 3 - 2 = 1$.
