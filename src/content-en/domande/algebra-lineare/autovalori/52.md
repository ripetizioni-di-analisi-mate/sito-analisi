$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 3 & 2\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(1,-1\right)$. What eigenvalue corresponds to $u$?

- $2$
- $-5$
- $5$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The first component of $Au$ is $a-b$. The second is its negative, so $Au$ is a multiple of $u$. Substituting $a=2$, $b=3$ gives $-1$.
