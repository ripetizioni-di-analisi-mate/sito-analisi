Let $f : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be the rotation by the angle $\pi$ about the line $r = \langle (1, 1, 0) \rangle$. What is the eigenspace $V_{-1}$ of $f$?

- The line $\langle (1, 1, 0) \rangle$
- The plane $x - y = 0$
- The plane $x + y = 0$
- The line $\langle (1, -1, 0) \rangle$

## Explanation

The vectors of the axis $r$ stay fixed, so $r \subseteq V_1$. A vector $v$ orthogonal to $r$ rotates by an angle $\pi$ in the plane orthogonal to the axis, hence $f(v) = -v$. The plane orthogonal to $(1, 1, 0)$ is $x + y = 0$, and it has dimension $2$, so it is contained in $V_{-1}$. Since $\dim V_1 + \dim V_{-1} \leq 3$, we get $V_1 = r$ and $V_{-1}$ is the **plane** $x + y = 0$. As a check, the matrix of $f$ with respect to the standard basis is $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$, which maps $(1, -1, 0)$ to $(-1, 1, 0)$ and $(0, 0, 1)$ to $(0, 0, -1)$. The plane $x - y = 0$ contains the axis $r$, whose vectors do not change.
