The characteristic polynomial of a matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ is
$$
p_A(\lambda) = -\lambda^3 + 4\lambda^2 - \lambda - 6 .
$$
What are $\operatorname{tr} A$ and $\det A$?

- $\operatorname{tr} A = 4$, $\det A = 6$
- $\operatorname{tr} A = -4$, $\det A = -6$
- $\operatorname{tr} A = 1$, $\det A = -6$
- $\operatorname{tr} A = 4$, $\det A = -6$

## Explanation

Setting $\lambda = 0$ in the definition gives $p_A(0) = \det(A - 0 \cdot I_3) = \det A$, so $\det A = -6$. For a matrix of order $3$ we have $p_A(\lambda) = -\lambda^3 + (\operatorname{tr} A)\lambda^2 + \dots$, hence $\operatorname{tr} A$ is the coefficient of $\lambda^2$, that is, $4$. As a check, $p_A(\lambda) = -(\lambda + 1)(\lambda - 2)(\lambda - 3)$, with eigenvalues of sum $-1 + 2 + 3 = 4$ and product $(-1) \cdot 2 \cdot 3 = -6$. The value $1$ is the opposite of the coefficient of $\lambda$, which does not coincide with the trace.
