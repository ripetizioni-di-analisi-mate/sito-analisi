$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -2\end{pmatrix}$. What is the algebraic multiplicity of the repeated eigenvalue?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The matrix is triangular. Its characteristic polynomial has the factor for the first diagonal entry twice. The result is $2$.
