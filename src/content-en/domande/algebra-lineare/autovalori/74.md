$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & -2 \\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first component of a zero-eigenvalue eigenvector with second component one?

- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The zero eigenspace is the kernel. The first row with second component one gives $ax+b=0$. Substituting $a=3$, $b=-2$ gives $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
