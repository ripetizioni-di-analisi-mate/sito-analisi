The matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & -2 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ -1 & -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
has characteristic polynomial $p_A(\lambda) = -(\lambda - 2)^2(\lambda + 1)$. Which triple of vectors is a basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$ made of eigenvectors of $A$?

- $(1, 0, 1)$, $(-1, 1, 0)$, $(1, -1, 0)$
- $(1, 0, 1)$, $(-1, 1, 0)$, $(-2, 0, 1)$
- $(1, 0, 1)$, $(-1, 1, 0)$, $(0, 0, 1)$
- $(1, 0, -1)$, $(-1, 1, 0)$, $(-2, 0, 1)$

## Explanation

The matrix
$$
A - 2I_3 = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & -1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}
$$
has rank $1$, so $V_2$ is the plane $x + y + 2z = 0$, with basis $(-1, 1, 0)$, $(-2, 0, 1)$. The system $(A + I_3)v = 0$ gives $y = 0$ and $x = z$, so $V_{-1} = \langle (1, 0, 1) \rangle$. By the remark on the union of bases of eigenspaces, the three vectors $(1, 0, 1)$, $(-1, 1, 0)$, $(-2, 0, 1)$ are independent and form a **basis of eigenvectors**. In the triple containing $(1, -1, 0)$ two vectors are proportional; the vectors $(0, 0, 1)$ and $(1, 0, -1)$ are not eigenvectors, since $A(0, 0, 1) = (-2, 0, 0)$ and $A(1, 0, -1) = (3, 0, -1)$.
