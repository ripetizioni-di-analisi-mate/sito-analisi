Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$. What is $A^5 v$ for $v = (3, 1)$?

- $(307, 243)$
- $(96, 243)$
- $(64, 243)$
- $(307, 64)$

## Explanation

The matrix is triangular with eigenvalues $2$ and $3$; we have $V_2 = \langle (1, 0) \rangle$ and, from the equation $-x + y = 0$ of the system $(A - 3I_2)v = 0$, $V_3 = \langle (1, 1) \rangle$. In the basis of eigenvectors the vector $v$ is written as $(3, 1) = 2(1, 0) + 1 \cdot (1, 1)$. Since $A^5$ multiplies each eigenvector by the fifth power of its eigenvalue, we get
$$
A^5 v = 2 \cdot 2^5 (1, 0) + 3^5 (1, 1) = (64, 0) + (243, 243) = (307, 243) .
$$
The vector $(96, 243)$ is obtained by multiplying the components of $v$ by $2^5$ and $3^5$, as if $A$ were diagonal.
