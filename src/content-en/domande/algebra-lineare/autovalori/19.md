A matrix $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ has eigenvalues $3$ and $-2$. What is $\det(A - I_2)$?

- $-7$
- $6$
- $-2$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial of $A$ has roots $3$ and $-2$ and leading coefficient $1$, so $p_A(\lambda) = (3 - \lambda)(-2 - \lambda)$. By definition $p_A(\lambda) = \det(A - \lambda I_2)$, so setting $\lambda = 1$ we obtain
$$
\det(A - I_2) = p_A(1) = (3 - 1)(-2 - 1) = -6 .
$$
The same result follows from the fact that $Av = \lambda v$ implies $(A - I_2)v = (\lambda - 1)v$, so the eigenvalues of $A - I_2$ are $2$ and $-3$, and the determinant is their product.
