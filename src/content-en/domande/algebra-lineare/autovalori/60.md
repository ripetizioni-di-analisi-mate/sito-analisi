$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(0,1\right)$. What eigenvalue of $A^2-A$ corresponds to $u$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $0$

## Explanation

On $u$, $A$ acts by a factor of $c$. Thus $A^2$ acts by $c^2$, and their difference acts by $c^2-c$. Substituting $c=1$ gives $0$.
