Let $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ and let $v$ be an eigenvector of $A$ associated with the eigenvalue $2$. For which eigenvalue is $v$ an eigenvector of the matrix $B = A^2 - 3A + I_3$?

- $11$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Since $Av = 2v$, we have $A^2 v = A(2v) = 2Av = 4v$. By linearity of the product we therefore get
$$
Bv = A^2 v - 3Av + v = 4v - 6v + v = -v .
$$
Hence $v$ is an eigenvector of $B$ associated with the eigenvalue $-1$, which is the value of the polynomial $\lambda^2 - 3\lambda + 1$ at $\lambda = 2$. The value $-2$ is obtained by neglecting the term $I_3$, which contributes $+v$.
