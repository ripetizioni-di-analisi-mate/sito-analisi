For the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
which vector spans the eigenspace $V_2$?

- $(2, 1, 1)$
- $(0, 1, 0)$
- $(2, -1, 1)$
- $(1, 0, 0)$

## Explanation

Expanding $\det(A - \lambda I_3)$ along the second row gives $p_A(\lambda) = (2 - \lambda)(1 - \lambda)(3 - \lambda)$, so $2$ is a simple eigenvalue and $V_2$ is a line. The second row of $A - 2I_3$ is zero, so the system $(A - 2I_3)v = 0$ is
$$
\begin{cases}
-x + 2z = 0 \\
y + z = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Setting $z = 1$ we obtain $x = 2$ and $y = -1$, so $V_2 = \langle (2, -1, 1) \rangle$. The vector $(0, 1, 0)$ does not belong to $V_2$, since $A(0, 1, 0) = (0, 2, 1)$.
