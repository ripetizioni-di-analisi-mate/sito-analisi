The eigenspace of $A$ for eigenvalue $2$ is spanned by $v=(2,-1,1)$. A matrix $B$ commutes with $A$ and the first component of $Bv$ is $6$. What is $Bv$?

- $(6,-1,1)$
- $(6,3,-3)$
- $(6,-3,3)$
- $(6,-2,2)$

## Explanation

Commutation gives $A(Bv)=B(Av)=2Bv$. Thus $Bv$ lies in the same one-dimensional eigenspace. Since the first component is $6$, the required multiple of $v$ is $3v$.
