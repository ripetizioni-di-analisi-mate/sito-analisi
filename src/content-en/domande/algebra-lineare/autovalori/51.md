For which values of $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$ does the homogeneous system $(A - \lambda I_3)x = 0$ have nonzero solutions, where
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $\lambda = 0$ and $\lambda = 2$
- Only $\lambda = 2$
- $\lambda = 2$, $\lambda = 1$ and $\lambda = -1$
- $\lambda = 1$ and $\lambda = 2$

## Explanation

The square homogeneous system $(A - \lambda I_3)x = 0$ has nonzero solutions if and only if $\det(A - \lambda I_3) = 0$, that is, if and only if $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of $A$. The second row of $A - \lambda I_3$ has $2 - \lambda$ as its only nonzero entry, so the Laplace expansion along this row gives
$$
p_A(\lambda) = (2 - \lambda) \det \begin{pmatrix} -\lambda & 1 \\ 1 & -\lambda \end{pmatrix} = (2 - \lambda)(\lambda^2 - 1) .
$$
Since $\lambda^2 - 1 = (\lambda - 1)(\lambda + 1)$, the required values are $2$, $1$ and $-1$. The matrix is not triangular, therefore the diagonal entries $0$ and $2$ alone do not give the required values; for example $\det A = -2 \neq 0$, so for $\lambda = 0$ the system has only the zero solution.
