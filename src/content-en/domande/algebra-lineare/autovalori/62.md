The matrix
$$
A = \frac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix} -3 & 4 \\ 4 & 3 \end{pmatrix}
$$
represents the reflection of the plane across a line through the origin. What is this line?

- $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x$
- $y = 2x$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x$
- $y = -2x$

## Explanation

The axis of reflection consists of the vectors that stay fixed, that is, it is the eigenspace $V_1$. We have
$$
A - I_2 = \frac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix} -8 & 4 \\ 4 & -2 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
whose rows are proportional, and the system $(A - I_2)v = 0$ reduces to the equation $-8x + 4y = 0$, that is, $y = 2x$. Hence the axis is the line $y = 2x$, spanned by $(1, 2)$; indeed $A(1, 2) = \dfrac{1}{5}(5, 10) = (1, 2)$. The line $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x$, spanned by $(2, -1)$, is the eigenspace $V_{-1}$, made of the vectors that reverse direction.
