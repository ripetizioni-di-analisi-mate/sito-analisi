For the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$, what is the eigenspace associated with the eigenvalue $1$?

- $V_1 = \langle (1, 1) \rangle$
- $V_1 = \langle (2, -1) \rangle$
- $V_1 = \langle (1, 2) \rangle$
- $V_1 = \langle (1, -2) \rangle$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is $p_A(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - 5\lambda + 4 = (\lambda - 1)(\lambda - 4)$, so $1$ is a simple eigenvalue. The eigenspace $V_1$ is the solution set of the homogeneous system $(A - I_2)v = 0$, with
$$
A - I_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The two rows are equal, so the system reduces to the equation $2x + y = 0$, that is, $y = -2x$. Therefore $V_1$ is the line spanned by $(1, -2)$. The vector $(1, 1)$ instead spans the eigenspace $V_4$, since $A(1, 1) = (4, 4)$.
