$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ 0 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. Is the matrix diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$?

- Yes, because every triangular matrix is diagonalizable
- Yes, because it has two independent eigenvectors
- No, because its only eigenspace has dimension one
- No, because it has no real eigenvalues

## Explanation

The diagonal entry is the only eigenvalue, with algebraic multiplicity two. The nonzero off-diagonal entry leaves only one eigendirection.
