Which of the following matrices is similar to the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The matrix $A$ is triangular with eigenvalues $1$ and $3$, real and distinct, so it is diagonalizable and is similar to the diagonal matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$. Similar matrices have the same trace and the same determinant, and for $A$ we have $\operatorname{tr} A = 4$ and $\det A = 3$. The matrices $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ have trace $4$ and determinant $4$, while $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ has determinant $-1$. Therefore none of these three matrices is similar to $A$.
