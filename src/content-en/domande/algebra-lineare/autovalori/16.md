What are the eigenvalues of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 6 \\ 1 & 0 & -11 \\ 0 & 1 & 6 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $1,\ 2,\ 3$
- $0,\ 0,\ 6$
- $-1,\ -2,\ -3$
- $1,\ -2,\ 3$

## Explanation

The matrix is not triangular, so the diagonal entries are not its eigenvalues. Expanding $\det(A - \lambda I_3)$ along the first row gives
$$
p_A(\lambda) = -\lambda \left[ -\lambda(6 - \lambda) + 11 \right] + 6 \cdot 1 = -\lambda^3 + 6\lambda^2 - 11\lambda + 6 .
$$
Since $p_A(1) = -1 + 6 - 11 + 6 = 0$, the polynomial is divisible by $\lambda - 1$, and the division gives
$$
p_A(\lambda) = -(\lambda - 1)(\lambda^2 - 5\lambda + 6) = -(\lambda - 1)(\lambda - 2)(\lambda - 3) .
$$
Therefore the eigenvalues are $1$, $2$ and $3$. As a check, their sum $6$ equals $\operatorname{tr} A$.
