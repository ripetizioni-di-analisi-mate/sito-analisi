What are the eigenvalues of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$?

- $5$ and $-1$
- $-5$ and $1$
- $1$ and $3$
- The matrix has no real eigenvalues

## Explanation

For a matrix of order $2$ we have $p_A(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - (\operatorname{tr} A)\lambda + \det A$, with $\operatorname{tr} A = 4$ and $\det A = 3 - 8 = -5$. Hence $p_A(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - 4\lambda - 5 = (\lambda - 5)(\lambda + 1)$, and the eigenvalues are $5$ and $-1$. As a check, their sum is $4 = \operatorname{tr} A$ and their product is $-5 = \det A$. The diagonal entries $1$ and $3$ would be the eigenvalues if the matrix were triangular.
