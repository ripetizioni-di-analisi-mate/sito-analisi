Let $f : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to \mathbb{R}_2[x]$ be the endomorphism defined by $f(p(x)) = x\, p'(x)$. What are the eigenvalues of $f$?

- $1$, $2$ and $3$
- Only $0$
- $1$ and $2$
- $0$, $1$ and $2$

## Explanation

With respect to the basis $\{1, x, x^2\}$ we have $f(1) = 0$, $f(x) = x \cdot 1 = x$ and $f(x^2) = x \cdot 2x = 2x^2$. Hence the associated matrix is the diagonal matrix with diagonal entries $0$, $1$, $2$, and the eigenvalues of $f$ are the eigenvalues of this matrix. The equality $f(1) = 0 = 0 \cdot 1$ shows that the constant polynomial $1$ is an eigenvector associated with the eigenvalue $0$. Since there are three distinct eigenvalues, $f$ is also diagonalizable.
