$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 0 \\ 0 & 4\end{pmatrix}$, $u=\left(0,1\right)$. What eigenvalue of $A^2-A$ corresponds to $u$?

- $12$
- $20$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

On the vector $u$, $A$ acts by a factor of $c$. Thus $A^2$ acts by $c^2$, and their difference acts by $c^2-c$. Substituting $c=4$ gives $12$.
