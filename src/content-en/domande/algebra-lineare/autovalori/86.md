$A=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 16 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. What is the larger of the two real eigenvalues?

- $-4$
- $4$
- $16$
- $0$

## Explanation

The characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2-a^2$. The roots are negatives of each other and the positive one is $a$. Substituting $a=4$, $b=5$ gives $4$.
