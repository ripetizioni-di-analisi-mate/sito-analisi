The sequence defined by $x_0 = 0$, $x_1 = 3$ and $x_{n+1} = x_n + 2x_{n-1}$ for $n \geq 1$ satisfies
$$
\begin{pmatrix} x_{n+1} \\ x_n \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_n \\ x_{n-1} \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Which formula gives $x_n$ for every $n \geq 0$?

- $x_n = 2^n + (-1)^n$
- $x_n = 2^n - (-1)^n$
- $x_n = 3 \cdot 2^{n-1}$
- $x_n = 2^{n+1} - (-1)^n$

## Explanation

The matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ has $\operatorname{tr} A = 1$ and $\det A = -2$, so $p_A(\lambda) = (\lambda - 2)(\lambda + 1)$, with eigenvectors $(2, 1)$ associated with $2$ and $(-1, 1)$ associated with $-1$. We have $(x_n, x_{n-1}) = A^{n-1}(x_1, x_0)$, and the initial vector is written as $(3, 0) = (2, 1) - (-1, 1)$. Since $A^{n-1}$ multiplies each eigenvector by the power of its eigenvalue, we get
$$
\begin{pmatrix} x_n \\ x_{n-1} \end{pmatrix} = 2^{n-1}\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} - (-1)^{n-1}\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
and the first component gives $x_n = 2^n - (-1)^n$. As a check, the formula gives $x_0 = 0$, $x_1 = 3$, $x_2 = 3$ and $x_3 = 9$, in agreement with the relation $x_3 = x_2 + 2x_1$.
