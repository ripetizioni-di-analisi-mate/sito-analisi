$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ 0 & -2\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 0 \\ 1 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. Which matrix equals $(AB)^{\mathsf T}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}13 & -2 \\ 2 & -4\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}-13 & 2 \\ -2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}12 & 3 \\ 4 & -3\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}13 & 2 \\ -2 & -4\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Transposing a product reverses the order of the transposed factors. We can therefore compute $B^{\mathsf T}A^{\mathsf T}$. Substituting $a=3$ gives $\begin{pmatrix}13 & -2 \\ 2 & -4\end{pmatrix}$.
