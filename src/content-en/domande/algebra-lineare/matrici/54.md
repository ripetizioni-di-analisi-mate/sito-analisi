A matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ has all entries below the main diagonal equal to zero and coincides with its own transpose. What can be concluded about $A$?

- $A$ is the zero matrix
- $A = I_3$
- $A$ is diagonal
- $A$ has all diagonal entries equal to zero

## Explanation

Since $A$ is upper triangular, $a_{ij} = 0$ for $i > j$. Since $A$ is symmetric, for $i < j$ we have $a_{ij} = a_{ji}$, and $a_{ji} = 0$ because $j > i$. Hence all the entries off the diagonal are zero, that is, $A$ is **diagonal**. The two conditions put no constraint on the diagonal entries, and indeed $\operatorname{diag}(1, 2, 3)$ is upper triangular and symmetric, but it is not zero, it is not $I_3$ and its diagonal is not zero.
