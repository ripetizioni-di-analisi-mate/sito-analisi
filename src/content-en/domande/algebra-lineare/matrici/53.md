$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. After the operation $R_2\leftarrow R_2+2R_1$, what is entry $(2,1)$?

- $-3$
- $7$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

Add twice the first row to the second. In the first column, this adds twice $a$ to $c$. Substituting $a=2$, $c=1$ gives $5$.
