Let $A \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ be invertible. Which equality holds for every such matrix $A$?

- $(2A)^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2}\, A^{-1}$
- $(2A)^{-1} = 2A^{-1}$
- $(A^T)^{-1} = A^{-1}$
- $(A + I_n)^{-1} = A^{-1} + I_n$

## Explanation

We have $(2A) \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} A^{-1} = \dfrac{2}{2}\, A A^{-1} = I_n$, so by the theorem on the right inverse and the left inverse $(2A)^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2} A^{-1}$. The formula with $2A^{-1}$ would give $(2A)(2A^{-1}) = 4I_n$. The correct equality for the transpose is $(A^T)^{-1} = (A^{-1})^T$, which differs from $A^{-1}$ when $A^{-1}$ is not symmetric. For $A = I_n$ we have $(A + I_n)^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2} I_n$, while $A^{-1} + I_n = 2I_n$.
