$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 5 & -3\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. Which matrix is $AB$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ 1 & -3\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 5 & -3\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ -3 & 5\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}5 & -3 \\ 4 & 1\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The first product column is $Ae_2$ and the second is $Ae_1$. This swaps the two columns of $A$. The result is $\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ -3 & 5\end{pmatrix}$.
