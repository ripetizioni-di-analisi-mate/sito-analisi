$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. Which matrix equals $(AB)^{\mathsf T}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}-3 & -3 \\ -2 & -6\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 2 \\ 1 & 7\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 3 & 6\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 3 \\ 2 & 6\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Transposing a product reverses the order of the transposed factors. We can therefore compute $B^{\mathsf T}A^{\mathsf T}$. Substituting $a=2$ gives $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 3 \\ 2 & 6\end{pmatrix}$.
