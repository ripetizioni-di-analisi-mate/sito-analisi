$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 5 & -3\end{pmatrix}$. What is the trace of $A^{\mathsf T}A$?

- $289$
- $51$
- $1$
- $35$

## Explanation

Each diagonal entry of $A^{\mathsf T}A$ is the squared norm of the corresponding column of $A$. Their sum is the sum of the squares of all entries. Substituting $a=4$ gives $51$.
