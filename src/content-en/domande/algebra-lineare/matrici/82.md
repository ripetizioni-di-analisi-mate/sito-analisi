Which elementary row operation transforms
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 4 & 2 \end{pmatrix}
\qquad \text{into} \qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & -5 & -1 \\ 0 & 4 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \ ?
$$

- $R_2 \to R_2 + 3R_1$
- $R_2 \to R_2 - 3R_1$
- $R_1 \to R_1 - 3R_2$
- $R_2 \to 3R_2 - R_1$

## Explanation

The rows $R_1$ and $R_3$ are the same in $A$ and in $B$, so the operation changes only $R_2$. To eliminate the entry $3$ below the pivot $1$, three times the first row is subtracted from the second row:
$$
(3 \ \ 1 \ \ {-1}) - 3\,(1 \ \ 2 \ \ 0) = (0 \ \ {-5} \ \ {-1}),
$$
which is the second row of $B$. Therefore the operation is $R_2 \to R_2 - 3R_1$. The operation $R_2 \to R_2 + 3R_1$ gives $(6 \ \ 7 \ \ {-1})$, and $R_1 \to R_1 - 3R_2$ would change the first row.
