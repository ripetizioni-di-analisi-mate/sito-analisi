Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
$$
which matrix is $(A + B)^2 - A^2 - B^2$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

By the distributive property $(A + B)^2 = A^2 + AB + BA + B^2$, so $(A + B)^2 - A^2 - B^2 = AB + BA$. The row-by-column product gives $AB = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and $BA = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, therefore the sum is $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. The answer $2AB$ assumes that $A$ and $B$ commute, and here $AB \neq BA$.
