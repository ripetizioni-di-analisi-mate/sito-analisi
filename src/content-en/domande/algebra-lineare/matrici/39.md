Which of the following matrices is in row echelon form?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 0 & 1 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

In $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ the zero row is at the bottom and the pivots $1$ and $3$ lie in columns $1$ and $3$, each to the right of the previous one, so the matrix is **in row echelon form**. In $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ the zero row comes before a nonzero row. In $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ the pivot of the second row lies in column $1$, to the left of that of the first row. In $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 0 & 1 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$ the first nonzero entries of the second and the third rows both lie in column $2$.
