$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 2 & -1\end{pmatrix}$. Which matrix is $(A+B)^{\mathsf T}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 5 & 0\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 4 \\ 3 & 0\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 3 \\ 4 & 0\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 5 \\ 2 & 0\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Addition is performed entry by entry. Transposing swaps the positions of the off-diagonal entries. The result is $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 5 \\ 2 & 0\end{pmatrix}$.
