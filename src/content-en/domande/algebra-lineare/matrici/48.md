Which matrix is the augmented matrix $\left( A \mid b \right)$ of the linear system in the unknowns $x, y, z$
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2z = 1 \\[4pt]
3y - z = 0 \\[4pt]
x - y = 4
\end{cases}
$$

- $\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 3 & -1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & -1 & 0 & 4 \end{array}\right)$
- $\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 & -1 & 0 \\ 2 & -1 & 0 & 4 \end{array}\right)$
- $\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 & -1 & 0 \\ 1 & -1 & 0 & 4 \end{array}\right)$
- $\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & 4 \end{array}\right)$

## Explanation

Each row of the augmented matrix contains the coefficients of one equation, in the order $x, y, z$, followed by the constant term. The first equation does not contain $y$, so the first row is $(1 \ \ 0 \ \ 2 \mid 1)$. The second equation does not contain $x$ and the coefficient of $z$ is $-1$, so the second row is $(0 \ \ 3 \ \ {-1} \mid 0)$. The third equation does not contain $z$, therefore the third row is $(1 \ \ {-1} \ \ 0 \mid 4)$. Writing the coefficients without the $0$ of the missing unknown moves the others into the wrong column, and the system represented is no longer the same.
