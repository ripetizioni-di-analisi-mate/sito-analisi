Which of the following matrices $N \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ satisfies $N^2 = O$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 \\ 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -4 \\ 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

For $N = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -4 \\ 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$ we compute
$$
N^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 4 & -8 + 8 \\ 2 - 2 & -4 + 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix},
$$
so $N$ is a nonzero matrix with zero square, that is, nilpotent. For the other matrices the square is $8 I_2$, $I_2$ and $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ respectively. For matrices, unlike real numbers, $N^2 = O$ does not imply $N = O$.
