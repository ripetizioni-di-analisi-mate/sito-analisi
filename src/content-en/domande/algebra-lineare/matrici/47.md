$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 2 & -1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the $(2,1)$ entry of $BA-AB$?

- $-4$
- $5$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The products use rows and columns in a different order. Compute both $(2,1)$ entries and subtract the one from $AB$. The result is $-4$.
