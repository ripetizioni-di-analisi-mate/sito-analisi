- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & -1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & -1 \\ -2 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 & -1 \\ -1 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 & -2 \\ 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 & -2 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

We reduce the matrix $\left( A \mid I_3 \right)$. The operations $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$, and then $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$, bring the left block to triangular form, and we obtain
$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|ccc}
1 & 0 & 2 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & -1 & -2 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & -1 & 1
\end{array}\right) .
$$
The operations $R_2 \to R_2 + R_3$ and $R_1 \to R_1 - 2R_3$ make the entries above the third pivot equal to zero, and we obtain
$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|ccc}
1 & 0 & 0 & -1 & 2 & -2 \\
0 & 1 & 0 & -1 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & -1 & 1
\end{array}\right) .
$$
The left block is $I_3$, so by the **Gauss-Jordan theorem** $A$ is invertible and the right block is $A^{-1}$.
