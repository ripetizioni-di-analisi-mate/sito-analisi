Which statement about the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 4 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
is true?

- $A$ is upper triangular
- $A$ is in row echelon form
- $A$ is lower triangular
- $A$ is symmetric

## Explanation

The entries above the main diagonal are $a_{12}$, $a_{13}$ and $a_{23}$, and they all equal $0$; therefore $A$ is **lower triangular**. The zero in position $(3, 3)$ is allowed, since the definition imposes conditions only on the entries off the diagonal. The entry $a_{21} = 1$ is different from $0$ and from $a_{12}$, so $A$ is neither upper triangular nor symmetric. The first nonzero entry of the second row lies in column $1$, like that of the first row, therefore $A$ is not in row echelon form.
