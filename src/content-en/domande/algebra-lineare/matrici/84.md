Row reduction of the augmented matrix of a linear system in the unknowns $x, y, z$ gave
$$
\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
1 & 2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 3 & 6
\end{array}\right) .
$$
What is the solution of the system?

- $(x, y, z) = (-5, -3, 2)$
- $(x, y, z) = (-5, 5, 2)$
- $(x, y, z) = (7, -3, 2)$
- $(x, y, z) = (11, -3, 2)$

## Explanation

Elementary operations do not change the solutions, so we solve the echelon system starting from the last equation. The equation $3z = 6$ gives $z = 2$. Substituting into the second equation $y + 2z = 1$ gives $y = 1 - 4 = -3$. Finally the first equation $x + 2y - z = 3$ gives
$$
x = 3 - 2y + z = 3 + 6 + 2 = 11 .
$$
Therefore the solution is $(11, -3, 2)$. The value $x = 7$ is obtained by subtracting $z$ instead of adding it, and $x = -5$ by changing the sign of both $-2y$ and $z$.
