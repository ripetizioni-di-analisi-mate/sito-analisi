Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 3 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
$$
what is the trace of the matrix $AB \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $10$
- $20$

## Explanation

The trace is the sum of the diagonal entries, so only the entries of $AB$ in positions $(1, 1)$, $(2, 2)$ and $(3, 3)$ are needed. The entry in position $(i, i)$ is the product of row $i$ of $A$ and column $i$ of $B$. We get
$$
(AB)_{11} = 2 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot 3 = 5, \qquad (AB)_{22} = (-1) \cdot 0 + 0 \cdot (-1) = 0, \qquad (AB)_{33} = 1 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 1 = 5 .
$$
Therefore $\operatorname{tr}(AB) = 10$. The same value comes from the matrix $BA = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 7 \\ 8 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$, whose diagonal entries $4$ and $6$ add up to $10$.
