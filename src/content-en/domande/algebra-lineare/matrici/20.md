Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ -1 & 0 \\ 2 & -3 \end{pmatrix},
$$
what is the entry in position $(2, 1)$ of the product $AB$?

- $9$
- $-5$
- $-2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The entry in position $(2, 1)$ of $AB$ is the product of the second row of $A$, $(0 \ \ 4 \ \ 1)$, by the first column of $B$, $(1, -1, 2)$. Therefore $(AB)_{21} = 0 \cdot 1 + 4 \cdot (-1) + 1 \cdot 2 = -2$. The value $9$ is the entry in position $(1, 1)$, the product of the first row by the first column, and $-5$ is the entry in position $(1, 2)$.
