$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 0 \\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ -3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is entry $(2,1)$ of $AB$?

- $-3$
- $5$
- $-12$
- $-15$

## Explanation

Left multiplication by a diagonal matrix scales rows. The second row of $B$ is multiplied by the second diagonal entry. The result is $-15$.
