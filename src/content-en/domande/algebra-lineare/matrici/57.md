Let $A \in M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$ and $B \in M_{3,2}(\mathbb{R})$. Which of the following expressions is defined?

- $A + B^T$
- $A + B$
- $AB + A$
- $BA + B$

## Explanation

Addition is defined only between matrices of the same size. Since $B^T \in M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$, the sum $A + B^T$ is between two $2 \times 3$ matrices, so it is **defined**. The sum $A + B$ combines a $2 \times 3$ matrix and a $3 \times 2$ matrix, therefore it is not defined. The product $AB$ is $2 \times 2$ and cannot be added to the $2 \times 3$ matrix $A$; the product $BA$ is $3 \times 3$ and cannot be added to the $3 \times 2$ matrix $B$.
