$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $C=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. If $AX=C$, what is $X_{12}$?

- $-2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

Solve for the second column. The bottom row gives $X_{22}=C_{22}$, and the top row determines $X_{12}$. The result is $-1$.
