Let $A \in M_{3,5}(\mathbb{R})$. What is the maximum number of pivots that an echelon form of $A$ can have?

- $3$
- $5$
- $15$
- $2$

## Explanation

In an echelon matrix every nonzero row contains exactly one pivot, so there are at most as many pivots as rows, that is $3$. Every column contains at most one pivot, therefore there are also at most $5$ pivots, and the first condition is the more restrictive one. The value $3$ is attained, for example by the matrix with $I_3$ in the first three columns and zeros in the last two. Therefore the maximum number of pivots is $3$.
