A symmetric matrix $A \in M_4(\mathbb{R})$ is determined by some of its entries, because the others follow from the condition $a_{ji} = a_{ij}$. How many entries of $A$ can be chosen freely?

- $16$
- $6$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

Since $a_{ji} = a_{ij}$, every entry below the diagonal equals the symmetric entry above the diagonal, so it is enough to assign the diagonal and the entries above it. The diagonal contains $4$ entries. Above the diagonal there are $3$ entries in the first row, $2$ in the second and $1$ in the third, that is, $6$ entries. Therefore the free entries are $4 + 6 = 10$. The value $6$ is the number of free entries of a skew-symmetric matrix of order $4$, which has zero diagonal, and $16$ is the number of all entries.
