$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ -2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 2 & -1\end{pmatrix}$. What is $(A+B)^{\mathsf T}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 0 \\ 5 & 3\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ 0 & 3\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 2 \\ 3 & 3\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 3 \\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Add the matrices entrywise. Transposing swaps the off-diagonal positions. The result is $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 0 \\ 5 & 3\end{pmatrix}$.
