Let $A \in M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$. Which statement is true for every such matrix?

- $A^T A$ is a $3 \times 3$ symmetric matrix
- $A^T A$ is a $2 \times 2$ symmetric matrix
- $AA^T$ is not defined
- $A + A^T$ is symmetric

## Explanation

The transpose $A^T$ is $3 \times 2$, so $A^T A$ is defined and is $(3 \times 2) \cdot (2 \times 3) = 3 \times 3$. By the properties of the transpose $(A^T A)^T = A^T (A^T)^T = A^T A$, therefore $A^T A$ is **symmetric**. The product $AA^T$ is defined and is $2 \times 2$. The sum $A + A^T$ is not defined, since $A$ and $A^T$ have different dimensions.
