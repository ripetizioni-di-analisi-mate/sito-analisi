Which of the following equalities holds for every pair of matrices $A, B \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$?

- $(A + B)^2 = A^2 + 2AB + B^2$
- $(A + B)(A - B) = A^2 - B^2$
- $(AB)^2 = A^2 B^2$
- $(A + B)^2 = A^2 + AB + BA + B^2$

## Explanation

By the distributive property $(A + B)^2 = (A + B)(A + B) = A^2 + AB + BA + B^2$, and this equality always holds. The formula with $2AB$ holds if and only if $AB + BA = 2AB$, that is, if and only if $AB = BA$; the same happens for $(A + B)(A - B) = A^2 - AB + BA - B^2$. The product $(AB)^2 = ABAB$ coincides with $A^2 B^2 = AABB$ when $A$ and $B$ commute. With $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ we have $AB \neq BA$, and the three equalities other than $(A + B)^2 = A^2 + AB + BA + B^2$ turn out to be false.
