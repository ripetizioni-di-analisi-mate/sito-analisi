Consider the matrix equality
$$
2\begin{pmatrix} x & 1 \\ 0 & y \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ -3 & y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 0 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Which real values of $x$ and $y$ satisfy it?

- $x = 2$, $y = 4$
- $x = 3$, $y = \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $x = 2$, $y = \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $x = 3$, $y = 4$

## Explanation

The left-hand side, computed entry by entry, is $\begin{pmatrix} 2x - 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 2y - y \end{pmatrix}$. Two matrices are equal if the entries in the same position coincide; the entries in positions $(1, 2)$ and $(2, 1)$ are already equal, so the remaining conditions are
$$
\begin{cases} 2x - 1 = 5 \\ y = 4 \end{cases}
$$
Therefore $x = 3$ and $y = 4$. The value $y = \dfrac{4}{3}$ is obtained by adding $2y$ and $y$ instead of subtracting them, and $x = 2$ is obtained by adding $1$ instead of subtracting it.
