$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $C=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & -3\end{pmatrix}$. If $AX=C$, what is $X_{12}$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $-3$
- $1$

## Explanation

Solve the system for the second column. The bottom row gives $X_{22}=C_{22}$, and the top row determines $X_{12}$. The result is $4$.
