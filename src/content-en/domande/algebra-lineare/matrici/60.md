$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 1 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is entry $(1,2)$ of the skew-symmetric part $(A-A^{\mathsf T})/2$?

- $2$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Entry $(1,2)$ of $A^{\mathsf T}$ is entry $(2,1)$ of $A$. Subtract and divide by two. Substituting $a=2$ gives $1$.
