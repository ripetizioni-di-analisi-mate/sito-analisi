For
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad x = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}
$$
the product $x^T A x$ is a real number. Which one?

- $2$
- $10$
- $-2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The product $x^T A x$ follows the pattern $(1 \times 2) \cdot (2 \times 2) \cdot (2 \times 1) = 1 \times 1$, so it is a number. By associativity we first compute
$$
Ax = \begin{pmatrix} 2 + 2 \\ 3 - 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix},
$$
and then $x^T (Ax) = 1 \cdot 4 + 2 \cdot 1 = 6$. Written out, $x^T A x = 2x_1^2 + (1 + 3)\, x_1 x_2 - x_2^2$, and both off-diagonal entries contribute. Counting only $a_{12}$ twice gives $2$, counting only $a_{21}$ twice gives $10$.
