$C=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 2 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the $(1,2)$ entry of $C^{-1}$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-2$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The second inverse column solves $Cz=e_2$. The second row gives $z_2=1$, and the first then gives $az_1+2=0$. Substituting $a=4$ gives $- \dfrac{1}{2}$.
