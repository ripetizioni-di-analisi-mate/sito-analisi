Let $A, B, C \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ with $A$ and $B$ invertible. Which matrix $X$ satisfies $AXB = C$?

- $X = B^{-1} C A^{-1}$
- $X = C A^{-1} B^{-1}$
- $X = A^{-1} B^{-1} C$
- $X = A^{-1} C B^{-1}$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides on the left by $A^{-1}$ gives $XB = A^{-1} C$, since $A^{-1} A = I_n$. Multiplying then on the right by $B^{-1}$ gives
$$
X = A^{-1} C B^{-1} .
$$
Each inverse goes on the same side as the matrix it removes, because the product is not commutative. With the other expressions the inverses are on the wrong side, and in general they do not satisfy the equation.
