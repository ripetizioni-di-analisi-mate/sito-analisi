Let $D = \operatorname{diag}(2, -1, 3)$. Which matrix is $D^4$?

- $\operatorname{diag}(16, -1, 81)$
- $\operatorname{diag}(8, -4, 12)$
- $\operatorname{diag}(16, 1, 81)$
- $\operatorname{diag}(8, -1, 27)$

## Explanation

The powers of a diagonal matrix are computed entry by entry, that is, $\operatorname{diag}(d_1, d_2, d_3)^k = \operatorname{diag}(d_1^{\,k}, d_2^{\,k}, d_3^{\,k})$. Therefore $D^4 = \operatorname{diag}\!\left(2^4, (-1)^4, 3^4\right) = \operatorname{diag}(16, 1, 81)$. Since the exponent is even, the entry $-1$ becomes $1$. The matrix $\operatorname{diag}(8, -4, 12)$ is $4D$, the product of $D$ by the scalar $4$.
