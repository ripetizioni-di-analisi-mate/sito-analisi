The matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ is invertible with
$$
A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the solution of the system $Ax = b$ with $b = (1, 2, 3)$?

- $x = (-1, 5, -1)$
- $x = (7, 1, -1)$
- $x = (7, 1, 1)$
- $x = (7, 3, -1)$

## Explanation

Since $A$ is invertible, multiplying both sides of $Ax = b$ on the left by $A^{-1}$ gives the unique solution $x = A^{-1} b$. Hence
$$
x = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 + 6 \\ -1 + 2 \\ 2 - 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The vector $(-1, 5, -1)$ is $(A^{-1})^T b$, obtained by multiplying $b$ by the columns of $A^{-1}$ instead of the rows.
