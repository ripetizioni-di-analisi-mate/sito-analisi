- $A^n = A$ for every $n$
- $A^n = I_2$ for even $n$ and $A^n = A$ for odd $n$
- $A^n = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & n \\ 0 & (-1)^n \end{pmatrix}$ for every $n$
- $A^n = I_2$ for odd $n$ and $A^n = A$ for even $n$

## Explanation

The row-by-column product gives
$$
A^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 + 0 & 1 - 1 \\ 0 & (-1)^2 \end{pmatrix} = I_2 .
$$
For even $n = 2m$ we therefore have $A^n = (A^2)^m = I_2$. For odd $n = 2m + 1$ we have $A^n = (A^2)^m A = A$. The formula with $n$ in position $(1, 2)$ is already wrong for $n = 2$, since the entry in position $(1, 2)$ of $A^2$ equals $0$.
