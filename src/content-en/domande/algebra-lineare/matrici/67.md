For the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
the products $AB$ and $BA$ are compared. What is the outcome?

- $AB \neq O$ e $BA = O$
- $AB = BA = O$
- $AB = O$ e $BA \neq O$
- $AB = BA \neq O$

## Explanation

The product $AB$ has as rows the first row of $B$ and the zero row, since the first row of $A$ selects the first row of $B$ and the second row of $A$ is zero. The first row of $B$ is zero, so $AB = O$. In the product $BA$ the second row is the first row of $A$, therefore
$$
BA = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \neq O .
$$
The two matrices are therefore zero divisors, and a zero product in one order does not imply a zero product in the other.
