$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ -2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. What is the trace of $A^{\mathsf T}A$?

- $30$
- $21$
- $196$
- $49$

## Explanation

Each diagonal entry of $A^{\mathsf T}A$ is the squared norm of the corresponding column of $A$. Summing gives the squares of all entries. Substituting $a=3$ gives $30$.
