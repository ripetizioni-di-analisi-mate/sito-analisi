$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & -2 & 1 \\ 2 & 4 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $v=\left(1,2,-1\right)$. What is the column vector $Av$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}0 \\ 10\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}1 \\ 6\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}0 \\ 6\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}-2 \\ 10\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Each product component is a row of $A$ dotted with the column vector $v$. The result is $\begin{pmatrix}-2 \\ 10\end{pmatrix}$.
