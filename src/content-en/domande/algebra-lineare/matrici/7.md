- $\begin{pmatrix} -3 & 5 \\ 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -5 \\ -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

We place the identity matrix next to $A$ and reduce. With the swap $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$ and then with $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ we obtain
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cc|cc}
1 & 3 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & -1 & 1 & -2
\end{array}\right) .
$$
With $R_2 \to -R_2$ and then with $R_1 \to R_1 - 3R_2$ we obtain
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cc|cc}
1 & 0 & 3 & -5 \\
0 & 1 & -1 & 2
\end{array}\right) .
$$
The left block is $I_2$, so by the **Gauss-Jordan theorem** $A$ is invertible and the right block is $A^{-1}$.
