Given
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad x = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix},
$$
what is $Ax$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 5 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 5 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 7 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 7 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The product $Ax$ is the linear combination of the columns of $A$ with coefficients the components of $x$, so
$$
Ax = 2\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 7 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The second component is $2 \cdot 3 - (-1) = 7$, since the second column of $A$ has $-1$ in the second place. The vector $\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 5 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is obtained with the coefficient $1$ instead of $-1$.
