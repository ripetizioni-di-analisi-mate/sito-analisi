- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 & 1 \\ 7 & 3 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 & -5 \\ 1 & 9 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & -3 \\ 3 & 7 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 & -5 \\ 1 & 3 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The linear combination is computed entry by entry. We have
$$
2A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & -2 \\ 4 & 6 & 2 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad 3B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 3 & 3 \\ 3 & -3 & 6 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Subtracting the entries in the same position gives
$$
2A - 3B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 & -5 \\ 1 & 9 & -4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
In the second row the middle entry is $6 - (-3) = 9$, since $3B$ has $-3$ in that position.
