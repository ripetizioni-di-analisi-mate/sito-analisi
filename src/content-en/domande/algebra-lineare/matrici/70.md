Let $A, B \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{R})$. Which matrix equals $(A^T B)^T$ for every such pair?

- $A^T B$
- $B^T A$
- $A B^T$
- $B A^T$

## Explanation

By the rule for the transpose of a product, $(XY)^T = Y^T X^T$, with the order of the factors reversed. Applied with $X = A^T$ and $Y = B$, it gives
$$
(A^T B)^T = B^T (A^T)^T = B^T A,
$$
since transposing twice gives back the original matrix. The matrices $A B^T$ and $B A^T$ are $m \times m$, while $(A^T B)^T$ is $n \times n$. The matrix $A^T B$ coincides with $B^T A$ only when $A^T B$ is symmetric.
