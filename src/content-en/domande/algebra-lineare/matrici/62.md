$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ -2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 2 & -1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the $(2,1)$ entry of $BA-AB$?

- $-2$
- $2$
- $0$
- $6$

## Explanation

The products use rows and columns in a different order. Compute both $(2,1)$ entries and subtract the one from $AB$. The result is $2$.
