The matrix $A \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ maps the vectors $e_1 = (1, 0)$ and $e_2 = (0, 1)$ to the vectors $Ae_1 = (2, 1)$ and $Ae_2 = (-1, 1)$, as in the figure. Which matrix is $A$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,1,0,e₁;0,0,0,1,e₂;0,0,2,1,Ae₁;0,0,-1,1,Ae₂" xmin=-1.8 xmax=2.8 ymin=-0.8 ymax=1.8 uguale=1 titolo="The vectors $e_1 = (1, 0)$, $e_2 = (0, 1)$ and their images $Ae_1 = (2, 1)$, $Ae_2 = (-1, 1)$"}

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The product of $A$ and $e_1$ is the combination of the columns of $A$ with coefficients $1$ and $0$, so $Ae_1$ is the first column of $A$. In the same way $Ae_2$ is the second column of $A$. Therefore the images of the two vectors are written as columns, in order:
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Writing the images as rows gives the transpose $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, which maps $e_1$ to $(2, -1)$.
