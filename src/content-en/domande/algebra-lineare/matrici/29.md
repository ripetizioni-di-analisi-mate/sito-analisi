The matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}
$$
is written as $A = S + K$ with $S$ symmetric and $K$ skew-symmetric. Which matrix is $K$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 \\ -4 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ -2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -2 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Transposing $A = S + K$ gives $A^T = S^T + K^T = S - K$. Subtracting the two equalities gives $A - A^T = 2K$, so $K = \dfrac{1}{2}(A - A^T)$. Since $A - A^T = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 \\ -4 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, we obtain $K = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ -2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ is the symmetric part $S = \dfrac{1}{2}(A + A^T)$.
