Let $S, T \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ be two symmetric matrices. Which of the following matrices is symmetric for every such pair?

- $ST$
- $ST - TS$
- $ST + TS$
- $S^2 T$

## Explanation

By the rule $(XY)^T = Y^T X^T$ and since $S^T = S$ and $T^T = T$, we have $(ST)^T = TS$. Hence
$$
(ST + TS)^T = TS + ST = ST + TS ,
$$
therefore $ST + TS$ is **symmetric** for every pair. The product $ST$ has transpose $TS$, which in general is different from $ST$: with $S = \operatorname{diag}(1, 2)$ and $T = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ we get $ST = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, which is not symmetric. With the same matrices $ST - TS$ and $S^2 T$ are not symmetric either, and $ST - TS$ is in fact skew-symmetric.
