The skew-symmetric matrix
$$
K = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 & -1 \\ -2 & 0 & 4 \\ 1 & -4 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
is multiplied by itself. What is the trace of $K^2$?

- $42$
- $0$
- $-21$
- $-42$

## Explanation

The diagonal entry in position $(i, i)$ of $K^2$ is the product of row $i$ of $K$ and column $i$ of $K$. Since $K$ is skew-symmetric, column $i$ is the opposite of row $i$, so that product is the opposite of the sum of the squares of the entries of row $i$. Therefore
$$
(K^2)_{11} = -(4 + 1) = -5, \qquad (K^2)_{22} = -(4 + 16) = -20, \qquad (K^2)_{33} = -(1 + 16) = -17 .
$$
Adding up gives $\operatorname{tr}(K^2) = -42$. The value $-21$ counts each off-diagonal entry only once, while each of them appears in two rows.
