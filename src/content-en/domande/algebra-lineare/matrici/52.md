$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the trace of $A^{\mathsf T}A$?

- $11$
- $1$
- $9$
- $15$

## Explanation

Each diagonal entry of $A^{\mathsf T}A$ is the squared norm of the corresponding column of $A$. Summing gives the squares of all entries. Substituting $a=2$ gives $15$.
