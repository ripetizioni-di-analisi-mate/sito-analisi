$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 2 & -1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the $(1,2)$ entry of $AB$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Multiply the first row of $A$ by the second column of $B$, summing the two products. The result is $1$.
