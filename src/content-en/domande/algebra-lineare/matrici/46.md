Let $A = (a_{ij}) \in M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$ be the matrix with $a_{ij} = i - 2j$. Which matrix is $A$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & -3 & -5 \\ 0 & -2 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 & 1 \\ -3 & -2 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ -3 & -2 \\ -5 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 3 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The first index $i$ gives the row and ranges from $1$ to $2$, the second index $j$ gives the column and ranges from $1$ to $3$. In the first row $i = 1$, so the entries are $1 - 2 = -1$, $1 - 4 = -3$ and $1 - 6 = -5$. In the second row $i = 2$, so the entries are $0$, $-2$ and $-4$. Therefore
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -3 & -5 \\ 0 & -2 & -4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The matrix with entries $j - 2i$ is obtained by exchanging the roles of the two indices, and the $3 \times 2$ matrix is the transpose of $A$, which does not belong to $M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$.
