$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ -2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 2 & -1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the $(1,2)$ entry of $AB$?

- $17$
- $4$
- $11$
- $5$

## Explanation

Multiply the first row of $A$ by the second column of $B$, summing the two products. The result is $11$.
