Let $A \in M_{3,n}(\mathbb{R})$. Let $E_1$ be the elementary matrix of the operation $R_2 \to R_2 - R_1$ and $E_2$ that of the swap $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$. First $R_2 \to R_2 - R_1$ is applied to $A$ and then, to the resulting matrix, $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$. Which product is the final matrix?

- $E_1 E_2 A$
- $A E_1 E_2$
- $E_2 E_1 A$
- $A E_2 E_1$

## Explanation

By the theorem on elementary matrices, applying a row operation to a matrix is the same as multiplying it **on the left** by the corresponding elementary matrix. The first operation therefore produces $E_1 A$. The second is applied to $E_1 A$, so it produces $E_2 (E_1 A) = E_2 E_1 A$. The product $E_1 E_2 A$ corresponds to the same operations in the opposite order, and multiplications on the right act on the columns.
