Let
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -1 & 2 \\ 0 & 5 & 4 \\ 1 & 7 & -6 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the trace of $A$?

- $8$
- $14$
- $15$
- $2$

## Explanation

The trace is the sum of the entries on the main diagonal, that is, of $a_{11}$, $a_{22}$ and $a_{33}$. For this matrix we have $\operatorname{tr} A = 3 + 5 + (-6) = 2$. The value $8$ is the sum of the entries on the other diagonal, $a_{13} + a_{22} + a_{31} = 2 + 5 + 1$, and the value $15$ is the sum of all the entries.
