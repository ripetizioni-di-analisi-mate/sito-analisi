$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$, $v=\left(1,2,-1\right)$. What is the column vector $Av$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}4 \\ 3\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}9 \\ 4\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}7 \\ 4\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}5 \\ 3\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Each product component is a row of $A$ dotted with the column vector $v$. The result is $\begin{pmatrix}7 \\ 4\end{pmatrix}$.
