A matrix $A$ has $12$ entries and the product $A^T A$ is a matrix in $M_4(\mathbb{R})$. How many rows does $A$ have?

- $4$
- $12$
- $16$
- $3$

## Explanation

Let $A \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{R})$. Then $A^T \in M_{n,m}(\mathbb{R})$, and the product $A^T A$ follows the pattern $(n \times m) \cdot (m \times n) = n \times n$. Since $A^T A \in M_4(\mathbb{R})$, we have $n = 4$, that is, $A$ has $4$ columns. The number of entries is $mn = 12$, so $m = \dfrac{12}{4} = 3$. Therefore $A$ has **$3$ rows**, and it is the product $A A^T$ that is $3 \times 3$.
