Which matrix $E \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ has the property that, for every $A \in M_{3,n}(\mathbb{R})$, the product $EA$ is obtained from $A$ by the operation $R_2 \to R_2 + 3R_3$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The required matrix is the elementary matrix obtained by applying the same operation to $I_3$. The second row of $I_3$ is $(0 \ \ 1 \ \ 0)$ and the third is $(0 \ \ 0 \ \ 1)$, so the new second row is $(0 \ \ 1 \ \ 3)$. Row $i$ of $EA$ is the combination of the rows of $A$ with coefficients the entries of row $i$ of $E$, therefore the second row of $EA$ is $R_2 + 3R_3$. The matrix with $3$ in position $(3, 2)$ corresponds to $R_3 \to R_3 + 3R_2$, and the one with $-3$ in position $(2, 3)$ to the inverse operation.
