Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & -1 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix},
$$
which matrix is the product $BA$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 1 \\ 5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 5 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 5 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Since $B$ has three columns and $A$ has three rows, the product $BA$ is defined and is a $2 \times 2$ matrix. The entry in position $(i, k)$ is the product of row $i$ of $B$ by column $k$ of $A$, so
$$
BA = \begin{pmatrix} 2 + 0 + 3 & 4 + 0 + 1 \\ 1 + 0 + 0 & 2 + 1 + 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 5 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 1 \\ 5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ is the transpose of $BA$, that is, the product $A^T B^T$.
