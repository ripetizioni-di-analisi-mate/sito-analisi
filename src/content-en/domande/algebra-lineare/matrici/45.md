Let $A, B \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be such that $AB = I_3$. Which statement is true?

- $A$ and $B$ are invertible and $BA = I_3$
- $A$ is invertible, but $BA$ may be different from $I_3$
- $B$ is invertible, but $A$ may not be
- Neither of the two matrices is necessarily invertible

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **right inverse and left inverse**, if $A$ and $B$ are square and $AB = I_3$, then $A$ is invertible and $B = A^{-1}$. It follows that $BA = A^{-1}A = I_3$. Hence $B$ is also invertible, with inverse $A$. For square matrices one of the two equalities in the definition of the inverse guarantees the other.
