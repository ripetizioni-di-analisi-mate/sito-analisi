$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 0 \\ 0 & -2\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 4 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is entry $(2,1)$ of $AB$?

- $12$
- $4$
- $-8$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Left multiplication by a diagonal matrix scales rows. The second row of $B$ is multiplied by the second diagonal entry. The result is $-8$.
