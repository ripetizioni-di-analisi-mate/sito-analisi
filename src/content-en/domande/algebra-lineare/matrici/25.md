Let
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ -4 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Which of the following matrices $B$ satisfies $AB = O$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -4 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ -2 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Column $k$ of $AB$ is the product of $A$ by column $k$ of $B$, so $AB = O$ if and only if $A$ sends every column of $B$ to the zero vector. We have $A\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2x - y \\ -4x + 2y \end{pmatrix}$, which is zero if and only if $y = 2x$. The columns $(1, 2)$ and $(2, 4)$ of $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ satisfy $y = 2x$, therefore this matrix satisfies $AB = O$. For $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -4 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$ we have $BA = O$, while $AB$ is different from $O$.
