- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The solution is obtained by reducing the augmented matrix $\left( A \mid B \right)$ to $\left( I_2 \mid X \right)$. With $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ and with the swap $R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$ we obtain
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cc|cc}
1 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\
2 & 1 & 1 & 0
\end{array}\right) .
$$
With $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$, then with $R_2 \to -R_2$ and with $R_1 \to R_1 - R_2$ we obtain
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cc|cc}
1 & 0 & 1 & -1 \\
0 & 1 & -1 & 2
\end{array}\right) .
$$
The left block is $I_2$, so $A$ is invertible and the right block is $X = A^{-1}B$. The matrix $BA^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ instead solves the equation $XA = B$.
