Let $A, B \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be invertible with
$$
A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad B^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Which matrix is $(AB)^{-1}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 7 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 7 & -2 \\ -3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 7 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ -3 & 7 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

In the inverse of a product the order of the factors is reversed, so $(AB)^{-1} = B^{-1} A^{-1}$. The row-by-column product gives
$$
B^{-1} A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 7 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 7 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is the product $A^{-1} B^{-1}$, with the factors in the order of $AB$.
