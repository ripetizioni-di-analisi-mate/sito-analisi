$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}-3 & 0 \\ 1 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. Which matrix equals $(AB)^{\mathsf T}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}-12 & 4 \\ -3 & 11\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}-11 & 5 \\ 2 & 10\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}-11 & 2 \\ 5 & 10\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}11 & -5 \\ -2 & -10\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Transposing a product reverses the order of the transposed factors. Thus compute $B^{\mathsf T}A^{\mathsf T}$. Substituting $a=4$ gives $\begin{pmatrix}-11 & 5 \\ 2 & 10\end{pmatrix}$.
