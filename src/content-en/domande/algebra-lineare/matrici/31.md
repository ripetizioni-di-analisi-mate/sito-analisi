Let
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Which matrix is $A^5$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 32 & 80 \\ 0 & 32 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 32 & 5 \\ 0 & 32 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 32 & 16 \\ 0 & 32 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 32 & 160 \\ 0 & 32 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

We write $A = 2I_2 + N$ with $N = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, which satisfies $N^2 = O$. Since $I_2$ commutes with $N$, the **binomial formula** applies, and the terms with $N^j$ for $j \geq 2$ are zero; so
$$
A^5 = (2I_2)^5 + 5\,(2I_2)^4 N = 32 I_2 + 80 N = \begin{pmatrix} 32 & 80 \\ 0 & 32 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The entry in position $(1, 2)$ is $5 \cdot 2^4 = 80$; the value $5$ corresponds to the binomial coefficient alone, without the factor $2^4$.
