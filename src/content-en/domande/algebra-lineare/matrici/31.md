For $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$, consider a power $A^m$ with $m$ a positive integer. The ratio of the entry at position $(1,2)$ to the entry at position $(1,1)$ in this power is $\dfrac{5}{2}$. What is the exponent?

- $5$
- $2$
- $4$
- $10$

## Explanation

Write $A=2I+N$ with $N^2=0$ and $N_{12}=1$. The binomial theorem gives $A^m=2^mI+m2^{m-1}N$. The observed ratio equals $\dfrac{m}{2}$, so $m=5$.
