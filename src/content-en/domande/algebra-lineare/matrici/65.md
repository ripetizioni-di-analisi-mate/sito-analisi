The matrices $A, B \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ share a property: in each of them the sum of the entries of every row is $1$. What is the sum of all the entries of the product $AB$?

- $3$
- $1$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

Let $u = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$. The product $Au$ has as components the sums of the entries of the rows of $A$, so the property of $A$ reads $Au = u$, and in the same way $Bu = u$. By the associative property
$$
(AB)u = A(Bu) = Au = u ,
$$
therefore every row of $AB$ also has sum $1$. The matrix $AB$ has three rows, so the sum of all its entries is $3$. The value $9$ is the product of the sums of all the entries of $A$ and of $B$, which does not coincide with the sum of the entries of $AB$.
