$C=\begin{pmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the $(1,2)$ entry of $C^{-1}$?

- $-2$
- $0$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The second inverse column solves $Cz=e_2$. The second row gives $z_2=1$, and the first then gives $az_1+2=0$. Substituting $a=3$ gives $- \dfrac{2}{3}$.
