In the polynomial $p(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - 3\lambda + 2$ the variable is replaced by the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}
$$
and the constant term $2$ by the matrix $2I_2$. What is $p(A) = A^2 - 3A + 2I_2$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $2I_2$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

We compute $A^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 + 4 \\ 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 6 \\ 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$. The constant term of the polynomial becomes the multiple $2I_2$ of the identity, so
$$
A^2 - 3A + 2I_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 - 3 + 2 & 6 - 6 + 0 \\ 0 & 4 - 6 + 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Therefore the polynomial $\lambda^2 - 3\lambda + 2$ evaluated at $A$ gives the zero matrix. Squaring entry by entry would give $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ instead of $A^2$ and the result $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$; adding $2$ to all entries instead of $2I_2$ would give $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$.
