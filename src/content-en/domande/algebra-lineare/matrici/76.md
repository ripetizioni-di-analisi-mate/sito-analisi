$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 1 \\ 5 & -3\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1 & -3 \\ 2 & -1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the $(1,2)$ entry of $AB$?

- $-3$
- $-2$
- $10$
- $-13$

## Explanation

Multiply the first row of $A$ by the second column of $B$, summing the two products. The result is $-13$.
