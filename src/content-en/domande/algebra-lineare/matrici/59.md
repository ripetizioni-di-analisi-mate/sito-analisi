$A=\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $B=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. Which matrix is obtained from $AB$?

- $\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 1 & 3\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 1 \\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3 \\ 1 & 1\end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The first column of the product is $Ae_2$ and the second is $Ae_1$. This swaps the two columns of $A$. The result is $\begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 1 & 3\end{pmatrix}$.
