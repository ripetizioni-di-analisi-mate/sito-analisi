$A=\begin{pmatrix}3 & -2 \\ 4 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is entry $(1,2)$ of the skew-symmetric part $(A-A^{\mathsf T})/2$?

- $-3$
- $-6$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Entry $(1,2)$ of $A^{\mathsf T}$ is entry $(2,1)$ of $A$. Subtract and divide by two. Substituting $a=3$ gives $-3$.
