Which of the following transformations of the rows $R_1, R_2, R_3$ of a $3 \times 3$ matrix is an elementary row operation?

- $R_2 \to 0 \cdot R_2$
- $R_2 \to R_2 - 3R_1$
- $R_2 \to R_1 + R_3$
- $R_2 \to R_2 + (1 \ \ 1 \ \ 1)$

## Explanation

The elementary row operations are the swap of two rows, the multiplication of a row by a nonzero scalar and the replacement $R_i \to R_i + kR_j$ with $j \neq i$. The transformation $R_2 \to R_2 - 3R_1$ is of the third type, with $k = -3$. Multiplication by $0$ is excluded by the definition, since it cannot be undone by another operation and it changes the solutions of the system. In the transformation $R_2 \to R_1 + R_3$ the new second row does not contain $R_2$, and adding the fixed row $(1 \ \ 1 \ \ 1)$ does not correspond to any of the three types.
