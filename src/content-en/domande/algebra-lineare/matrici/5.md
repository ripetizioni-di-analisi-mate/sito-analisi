Reducing the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 & -2 \\ 1 & 3 & 0 \\ 3 & 5 & -4 \end{pmatrix}
$$
to row echelon form, how many pivots are obtained and in which columns?

- Three pivots, in columns $1$, $2$ and $3$
- Two pivots, in columns $1$ and $3$
- One pivot, in column $1$
- Two pivots, in columns $1$ and $2$

## Explanation

The operations $R_2 \to R_2 - \dfrac{1}{2} R_1$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - \dfrac{3}{2} R_1$ make the entries of the first column below the pivot $2$ equal to zero, and we obtain
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 & -2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The operation $R_3 \to R_3 + R_2$ then makes the third row zero, so a row echelon form is
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 & -2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The pivots are $2$ and $1$, in columns $1$ and $2$, therefore we obtain **two pivots**. The number of pivots does not depend on the operations chosen, so every other reduction gives two.
