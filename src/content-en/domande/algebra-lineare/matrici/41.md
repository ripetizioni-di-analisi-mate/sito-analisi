For which values of the real parameter $k$ is the matrix
$$
A_k = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & k & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & k \\ k & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
invertible?

- $k \neq -1$
- $k \neq 1$
- $k \neq 0$
- $k \neq 1$ and $k \neq -1$

## Explanation

With $R_3 \to R_3 - kR_1$ the third row becomes $(0 \ \ {-k^2} \ \ 1)$, and with $R_3 \to R_3 + k^2 R_2$ it becomes $(0 \ \ 0 \ \ 1 + k^3)$. The row echelon form therefore has three pivots if and only if $1 + k^3 \neq 0$. Since $1 + k^3 = (1 + k)(k^2 - k + 1)$ and $k^2 - k + 1 > 0$ for every $k$, this happens if and only if $k \neq -1$. By the **Gauss-Jordan theorem** $A_k$ is therefore invertible if and only if $k \neq -1$.
