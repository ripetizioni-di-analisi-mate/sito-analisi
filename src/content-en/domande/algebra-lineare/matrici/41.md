Let $B \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$ be a matrix whose second column is zero. Which statement holds for every matrix $A \in M_3(\mathbb{R})$?

- The second column of $AB$ is zero
- The second row of $AB$ is zero
- The second column of $BA$ is zero
- The product $AB$ is the zero matrix

## Explanation

Column $k$ of the product $AB$ is the product of $A$ and column $k$ of $B$. Since the second column of $B$ is the zero vector, the second column of $AB$ is $A \cdot 0 = 0$, whatever $A$ is. The other three statements are already false for
$$
B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Indeed $AB$ has its only nonzero entry in position $(2, 1)$, so its second row is not zero and $AB \neq O$. The product $BA$, on the other hand, has its only nonzero entry in position $(1, 2)$, therefore its second column is not zero.
