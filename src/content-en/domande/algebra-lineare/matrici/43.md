The matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ updates two readings by $x_{n+1}=\dfrac12 A x_n$. Starting from the column vector $x_0=(2,8)^\top$, which reading results after one hundred updates?

- $(10,10)^\top$
- $(2,8)^\top$
- $(5,5)^\top$
- $(0,0)^\top$

## Explanation

One update replaces both components by their mean, giving $(5,5)^\top$. Every vector with equal components is fixed by $\dfrac{A}{2}$. Every subsequent update therefore preserves the same vector.
