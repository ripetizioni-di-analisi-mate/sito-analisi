Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix},
$$
which matrix is $(AB)^T$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 6 & 5 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ 1 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 0 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 6 & 0 \\ 5 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The row-by-column product gives $AB = \begin{pmatrix} 0 - 1 & 3 - 2 \\ 0 & 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 0 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$, so $(AB)^T = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ 1 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$. The same result is obtained as $B^T A^T$, since in the transpose of a product the order of the factors is reversed. The matrix $A^T B^T$ is instead the transpose of $BA$, that is, $\begin{pmatrix} 6 & 5 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$.
