Let $A$ have size $2\times3$, $B$ have size $3\times4$ and $y\in\mathbb R^2$ be a column vector. We need the column vector whose dot product with every $x\in\mathbb R^4$ equals $y^\top ABx$. Which expression gives it without forming $AB$?

- $A(By)$
- $B^\top(A^\top y)$
- $B(A^\top y)$
- $A^\top(B^\top y)$

## Explanation

Transposing the row $y^\top AB$ reverses the factors. First $A^\top y$ has three components, then $B^\top$ produces the four components needed for the dot product with $x$.
