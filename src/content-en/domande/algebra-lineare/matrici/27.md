Let $A \in M_{2,3}(\mathbb{R})$, $B \in M_{3,4}(\mathbb{R})$ and let $y \in \mathbb{R}^2$ be a column vector. The product $y^T A B$ is a row vector with $4$ entries. Which column vector is its transpose $(y^T A B)^T$?

- $A^T B^T y$
- $B^T A^T y$
- $B A^T y$
- $A B y$

## Explanation

The transpose of a product is the product of the transposes in reverse order, so $(y^T A B)^T = B^T A^T (y^T)^T$. Since $(y^T)^T = y$, we obtain $(y^T A B)^T = B^T A^T y$. The sizes are compatible, because $A^T y$ has $3$ entries and $B^T \in M_{4,3}(\mathbb{R})$, therefore the result has $4$ entries. In the other three expressions the order of the factors is not reversed or a transpose is missing, and none of them is defined, because each contains a product of matrices of incompatible sizes.
