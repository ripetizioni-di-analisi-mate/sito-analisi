$A=\begin{pmatrix}4 & 5 \\ -3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$. What is entry $(1,2)$ of the skew-symmetric part $(A-A^{\mathsf T})/2$?

- $1$
- $4$
- $-4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Entry $(1,2)$ of $A^{\mathsf T}$ is entry $(2,1)$ of $A$. Subtract and divide by two. Substituting $a=4$ gives $4$.
