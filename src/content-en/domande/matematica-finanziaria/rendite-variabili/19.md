The effective interest rate is $10\%$ per half-year. Receipts of $220$ euros and $363$ euros are due in six and twelve months, respectively. What is their present value?

- $583\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $530\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The rate is semiannual, so time is measured in half-years and $v=\dfrac{1}{1.1}$. The payments are one and two half-years from today, giving
$$
V_0=\frac{220}{1.1}+\frac{363}{1.1^2}=200+300=500\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The nominal sum is $583$ euros. The value of $530$ euros discounts both payments for one half-year.
