A fund earning an effective annual rate of $20\%$ receives three year-end deposits. The first is $100$ euros and subsequent deposits grow by $10\%$. What is the balance at time $3$ immediately after the last deposit?

- $331\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $476.40\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $397\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $436.70\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The deposits are $100$, $110$, and $121$ euros at times $1$, $2$, and $3$. The first earns two years' interest, the second one year's interest, and the third enters at face value, giving
$$
M_3=100\cdot1.2^2+110\cdot1.2+121=144+132+121=397\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The nominal sum is $331$ euros. The value of $436.70$ euros applies growth even to the first deposit, while $476.40$ euros accumulates every deposit for one extra year.
