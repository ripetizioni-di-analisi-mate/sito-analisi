The balance immediately after the deposit at time $2$ is $2100$ euros. Another $300$ euros is deposited at time $3$. At an effective annual rate of $10\%$, what is the balance immediately after that deposit?

- $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2640\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2310\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2610\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The balance $B_k$ immediately after the deposit at time $k$ follows the recurrence $B_k=qB_{k-1}+R_k$. From time $2$ to time $3$ the balance earns one year's interest and grows to $2100\cdot1.1=2310$ euros. Add the deposit of $300$ euros to obtain $B_3=2310+300=2610$ euros. The value of $2400$ euros ignores interest, while $2640$ euros also accrues interest on the new deposit.
