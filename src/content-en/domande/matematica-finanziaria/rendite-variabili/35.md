Two payments in arrears start at $120$ euros and grow by $20\%$. At an interest rate of $10\%$, what is the ratio of the second payment's present value to the first's?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{12}{11}$
- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac{11}{10}$

## Explanation

The second payment is $120\cdot1.2$ euros and falls a year after the first. Its present value is $120\cdot1.2\,v^2$ and that of the first is $120v$. Their ratio is
$$
\rho=(1+g)v=\frac{1.2}{1.1}=\frac{12}{11}.
$$
The value $\dfrac65$ counts growth but omits the extra year of discounting. The value $\dfrac{11}{12}$ is the reciprocal of $\rho$.
