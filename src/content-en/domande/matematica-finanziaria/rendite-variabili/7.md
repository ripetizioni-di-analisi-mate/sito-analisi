The force of interest is $0.02\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$ during the first year and $0.06\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$ during the next two years. Find the value at time $0$ of a payment of $1000$ euros due at the end of year three. Give the exact answer in exponential form.

- $1000e^{-0.18}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000e^{-0.14}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000e^{-0.08}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000e^{-0.06}\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The discount factor is the exponential of the negative integral of the force between $0$ and $3$. The force equals $0.02$ for one year and $0.06$ for two years, so
$$
\int_0^3\delta(u)\,du=0.02\cdot1+0.06\cdot2=0.14 .
$$
The payment is therefore worth $1000e^{-0.14}$ euros today. The exponent $-0.18$ applies the force $0.06$ for all three years, while $-0.08$ adds the two forces without weighting them by their durations.
