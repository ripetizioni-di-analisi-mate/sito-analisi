At an effective annual rate of $10\%$, the goal is $2420$ euros at time $2$. Equal deposits are made today and at time $1$. What is each deposit?

- $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $\dfrac{24200}{21}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $\dfrac{22000}{21}\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The deposits occur at times $0$ and $1$. By time $2$ they have earned interest for two and one years, respectively. The savings equation is
$$
R\left(1.1^2+1.1\right)=2.31\,R=2420 ,
$$
giving $R=\dfrac{2420}{2.31}=\dfrac{22000}{21}\approx1047.62$ euros. The value of $\dfrac{24200}{21}$ euros uses exponents $1$ and $0$ as for deposits made at times $1$ and $2$. The value of $1210$ euros ignores interest.
