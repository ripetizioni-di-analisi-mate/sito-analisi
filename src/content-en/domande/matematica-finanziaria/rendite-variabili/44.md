A perpetuity pays $90$ euros immediately and increases by $2\%$ at each annual payment date. What is its present value at an effective annual rate of $5\%$?

- $3000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3060\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3150\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2910\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Include the immediate payment of $90$ euros at face value. The remaining payments form a perpetuity payable in arrears whose first payment of $90\cdot1.02=91.80$ euros falls at time $1$. They are worth $\dfrac{91.80}{0.05-0.02}=3060$ euros, giving $V_0=90+3060=3150$ euros in total. The value of $3000$ euros treats the annuity as payable in arrears with a first payment of $90$ euros. The value of $3060$ euros omits the immediate payment.
