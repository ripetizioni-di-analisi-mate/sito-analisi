A constant continuous stream has a present value of $240$ euros and ends in two years. At a constant annual force of interest $\dfrac{\log 2}{2}$, what is its accumulated value on the final date?

- $480\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $960\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At a constant force of interest $M_T=e^{\delta T}V_0$ holds because every accumulation factor equals its discount factor times $e^{\delta T}$. With $\delta T=\dfrac{\log 2}{2}\cdot2=\log 2$ we have $e^{\delta T}=2$, giving $M_2=2\cdot240=480$ euros. The value of $120$ euros divides by $2$ instead of multiplying. The value of $960$ euros uses the factor $e^{2\delta T}=4$.
