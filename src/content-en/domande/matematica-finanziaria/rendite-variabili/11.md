A perpetuity whose payments grow by $4\%$ per year is worth $6000$ euros at time $0$. The first payment is due at time $2$ and the effective annual interest rate is $10\%$. Find the first payment.

- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $396\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $435.60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first payment falls at time $2$, so the growing-perpetuity formula gives the value at time $1$, one period before the first payment. It equals $6000\cdot1.1=6600$ euros. From $6600=\dfrac{R_1}{0.10-0.04}$ we obtain $R_1=6600\cdot0.06=396$ euros. The value of $360$ euros overlooks the deferral, and $435.60$ euros accumulates for two years instead of one.
