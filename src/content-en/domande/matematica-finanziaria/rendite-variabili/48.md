A perpetuity payable in arrears grows by $5\%$ annually and is worth $2400$ euros today at an effective rate of $10\%$. What is its value at time $1$ immediately after the first payment?

- $2520\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2640\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2280\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At time $1$ the accumulated initial value is $2400\cdot1.1=2640$ euros. The first payment of $R_1=2400\cdot(0.10-0.05)=120$ euros falls at time $1$, leaving a value of $2640-120=2520$ euros for the remaining payments. The same result follows from $\dfrac{120\cdot1.05}{0.05}$. The value of $2640$ euros still includes the first payment, while $2280$ euros deducts it without first accumulating the initial value.
