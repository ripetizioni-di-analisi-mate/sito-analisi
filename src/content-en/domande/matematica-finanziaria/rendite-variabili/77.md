A continuous stream is valued at time $2$. Its payment rate $c(t)$ applies from time $2$ to time $4$. Which factor multiplies the payment rate in the integral from $2$ to $4$?

- $\exp\!\left(-\int_0^t\delta(u)\,du\right)$
- $\exp\!\left(\int_2^t\delta(u)\,du\right)$
- $\exp\!\left(-\int_2^t\delta(u)\,du\right)$
- $\exp\!\left(-\int_t^4\delta(u)\,du\right)$

## Explanation

To value at time $2$ a payment due at time $t\geq2$, discount it from time $t$ to time $2$. The factor is $v(2,t)=\exp\left(-\int_2^t\delta(u)\,du\right)$, with the integral starting at the valuation date. Integrating from $0$ to $t$ discounts the payment all the way to today. Omitting the minus sign would accumulate instead of discounting.
