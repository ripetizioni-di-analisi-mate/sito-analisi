A savings plan includes an initial deposit of $500$ euros and continuous deposits at a constant rate $c$ for one year. The annual force of interest is constant at $\log 2$ and the target is $1800$ euros at time $1$. Find $c$.

- $\dfrac{800}{\log 2}\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$
- $1300\log 2\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$
- $800\log 2\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$

## Explanation

At time $1$ the initial deposit is worth $500e^{\log 2}=1000$ euros. The accumulated value of the continuous deposits is
$$
\frac{c\left(e^{\log 2}-1\right)}{\log 2}=\frac{c}{\log 2}.
$$
The savings equation is $1000+\dfrac{c}{\log 2}=1800$, giving $c=800\log 2\approx554.52$ euros per year. The value $1300\log 2$ leaves the initial deposit unaccumulated. The value $\dfrac{800}{\log 2}$ divides by $\log 2$ instead of multiplying.
