Three year-end receipts grow at $20\%$, equal to the effective annual interest rate. Together they are worth $750$ euros today. What is the first receipt?

- $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $225\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Payment growth and the interest rate both equal $20\%$, so $\rho=1$ and the three payments have equal present values. Each is worth $\dfrac{750}{3}=250$ euros today. The first falls due in one year and its nominal amount is $250\cdot1.2=300$ euros. The value of $250$ euros is its present value without the year of accumulation, while $360$ euros is the second payment.
