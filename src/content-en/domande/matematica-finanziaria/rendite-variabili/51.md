A growing perpetuity's first payment is positive and fixed. What is the limiting present value as the valuation rate falls toward $g$ from above?

- $+\infty$
- $0$
- $R_1$
- $g$

## Explanation

The present value of a growing perpetuity is $V_0=\dfrac{R_1}{i-g}$ for $i>g$. As $i$ approaches $g$ from above, the denominator $i-g$ is positive and approaches $0$. The numerator $R_1$ is fixed and positive, so the quotient approaches $+\infty$. For $i=g$ the partial sums grow without bound, in agreement with that limit.
