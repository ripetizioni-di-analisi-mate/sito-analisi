Three year-end payments decrease by $40$ euros each year. At an effective annual rate of $25\%$ the annuity is worth $323.84$ euros. What is its first payment?

- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $165.90\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $131.80\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With $v=\dfrac{1}{1.25}=0.8$, payment $k$ is $R_1-40(k-1)$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
V_0&=R_1\left(v+v^2+v^3\right)-40\left(v^2+2v^3\right)\\[6pt]
&=1.952\,R_1-66.56 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Setting $V_0=323.84$ gives $1.952\,R_1=390.40$, hence $R_1=200$ euros. The value of $165.90$ euros omits the decrease. The value of $131.80$ euros treats the decrease as an increase. The value of $240$ euros counts $k$ decreases in payment $k$.
