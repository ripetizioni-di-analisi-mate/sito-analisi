A deposit of $100$ euros is moved from time $1$ to time $2$ without changing its amount. At an effective annual rate of $20\%$, by what factor is its present value multiplied?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{25}{36}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

A deposit made at time $1$ is worth $100v$ today, and the same deposit made at time $2$ is worth $100v^2$ today. The ratio of the new present value to the original is
$$
\frac{100v^2}{100v}=v=\frac{1}{1.2}=\frac56 .
$$
Delaying a payment for one year introduces one discount factor. The value $\dfrac65$ is the accumulation factor, while $\dfrac{25}{36}$ is $v^2$, the two-year discount factor.
