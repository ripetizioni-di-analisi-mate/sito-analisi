Two schedules have the same target date $3$ and effective annual rate $10\%$. They differ only because the second adds $200$ euros at time $1$. By how much does its final balance increase?

- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $266.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The schedules differ only by the $200$ euros deposited at time $1$. By time $3$ this deposit has earned two years' interest and contributes $200\cdot1.1^2=242$ euros. The final balance therefore increases by $242$ euros. The value of $220$ euros accumulates the extra deposit for one year, while $266.20$ euros accumulates it for three years starting at time $0$.
