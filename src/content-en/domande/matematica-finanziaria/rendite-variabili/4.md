A perpetuity pays $144$ euros one year from now. Each subsequent annual payment is $2\%$ larger than the previous one. Find its present value at an effective annual interest rate of $6\%$.

- $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3672\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first payment of the perpetuity is $R_1=144$ euros in one year and its growth rate is $g=2\%$. Since $i=6\%>g$, the present value is finite and equals
$$
V_0=\frac{144}{0.06-0.02}=\frac{144}{0.04}=3600\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $2400$ euros neglects growth by dividing by $0.06$. The value of $1800$ euros adds the two rates in the denominator instead of subtracting them.
