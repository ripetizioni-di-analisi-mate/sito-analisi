A fund must hold €1000 in one year. It currently has €900 earning $5\%$ over that year. The largest additional deposit allowed today is €50 and it earns the same rate. How much will still be missing at maturity if the maximum is deposited?

- €2.50
- €5
- €50
- €0

## Explanation

The deposit cap raises the initial balance to at most €950. The largest final balance is $950\cdot1.05=997.50$ euros. This leaves the fund €2.50 short.
