The target is $1500$ euros at time $3$. Scheduled deposits provide $1170$ euros on that date. What additional deposit at time $2$ covers the shortfall at an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $330\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $363\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $297\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The shortfall at time $3$ is $1500-1170=330$ euros. The deposit $X$ at time $2$ earns a year's interest before the target date, so $1.1X=330$ and hence $X=\dfrac{330}{1.1}=300$ euros. The amount of $330$ euros would be required at time $3$. The value of $363$ euros accumulates the shortfall instead of discounting it.
