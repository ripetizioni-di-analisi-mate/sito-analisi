The force of interest is $0.04\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$ in the first year and $0.08\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$ in the second. What is the exact factor for discounting from time $2$ to time $0$?

- $e^{-0.08}$
- $e^{-0.06}$
- $e^{-0.16}$
- $e^{-0.12}$

## Explanation

The factor for discounting from time $2$ to time $0$ is $\exp\left(-\int_0^2\delta(u)\,du\right)$. The force is $0.04$ in year one and $0.08$ in year two, giving
$$
\int_0^2\delta(u)\,du=0.04\cdot1+0.08\cdot1=0.12 .
$$
The factor is therefore $e^{-0.12}$. The exponent $-0.16$ applies the force $0.08$ in both years, while $-0.08$ counts only the second year.
