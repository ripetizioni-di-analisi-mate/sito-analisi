A €100 payment at time $2$ is discounted at a force of $3\%$ in year one and $7\%$ in year two. If the two forces are swapped, what happens to the present value of this payment and to that of a positive payment at time $1$?

- Both lose value.
- Both keep their values.
- The time-$2$ payment gains value and the time-$1$ payment loses value.
- The time-$2$ payment keeps its present value and the time-$1$ payment loses value.

## Explanation

Over two years the cumulative force is still $0.10$, so the discount factor is still $e^{-0.10}$. Over one year the factor falls from $e^{-0.03}$ to $e^{-0.07}$.
