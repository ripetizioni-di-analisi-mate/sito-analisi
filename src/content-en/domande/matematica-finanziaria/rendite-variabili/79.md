An initial deposit of $2000$ euros earns an effective annual rate of $10\%$ for two years. What additional deposit at time $2$ is needed to reach $2600$ euros?

- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At time $2$ the initial deposit is worth $2000\cdot1.1^2=2420$ euros. The additional deposit is made on the target date, so its factor is $1$. The additional deposit required is $2600-2420=180$ euros. The value of $600$ euros leaves the initial deposit unaccumulated, while $400$ euros accumulates it for only one year.
