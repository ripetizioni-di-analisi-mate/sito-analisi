Two schedules have the same nominal total and positive effective interest rate. The second moves a single positive deposit one year earlier. What happens to the accumulated value at the same final date?

- It increases under the second schedule.
- It decreases under the second schedule.
- It is equal under both schedules.
- It increases only if the final date is also moved earlier.

## Explanation

The schedules differ only in one deposit, made a year earlier under the second schedule. That deposit $R$ contributes $Rq^{n-t}$ under the first schedule and $Rq^{n-t+1}$ under the second. Since $i>0$, we have $q>1$, so its contribution is larger under the second. All other terms are equal, making the accumulated value larger under the second schedule.
