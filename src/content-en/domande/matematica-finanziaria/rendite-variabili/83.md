A three-year savings schedule earns an effective rate of $10\%$ and has year-end deposits that grow by $10\%$. How large must the first deposit be to reach $1452$ euros at the final date?

- $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $440\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The deposits of $R_1$, $1.1R_1$, and $1.1^2R_1$ fall at times $1$, $2$, and $3$. By time $3$ the first has earned two years' interest and the second one year's interest, so each is worth $1.1^2R_1=1.21R_1$. Thus $3\cdot1.21R_1=1452$ and $R_1=\dfrac{1452}{3.63}=400$ euros. The value of $484$ euros divides the target by three, ignoring both interest and deposit growth.
