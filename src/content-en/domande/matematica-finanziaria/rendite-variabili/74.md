Under a multiplicative accumulation law the discount factor between times $0$ and $1$ is $0.9$, and between $1$ and $2$ it is $0.8$. What is the value at time $0$ of $1000$ euros due at time $2$?

- $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $850\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under a multiplicative accumulation law the discount factor between $0$ and $2$ is the product of the factors for the consecutive intervals. The combined factor is $0.9\cdot0.8=0.72$, so $1000$ euros at time $2$ are worth $1000\cdot0.72=720$ euros today. The value of $850$ euros uses the mean of the factors instead of the product. The value of $800$ euros includes only the second interval.
