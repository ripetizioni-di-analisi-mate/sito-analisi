Compare a payment of $900$ euros at year-end with a continuous stream paying at a constant rate of $900\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$ over the same year. Which has the higher present value at a positive constant force of interest?

- The year-end payment.
- Both have the same present value.
- The continuous stream.
- It depends on the precise value of the positive force of interest.

## Explanation

Both arrangements pay $900$ euros in total. The continuous stream is worth $\int_0^1 900e^{-\delta t}\,dt$ and the year-end payment $900e^{-\delta}$. Since $\delta>0$, for each $t<1$ we have $e^{-\delta t}>e^{-\delta}$, so each part of the stream is discounted for a shorter time. The continuous stream therefore has higher present value at every positive force of interest.
