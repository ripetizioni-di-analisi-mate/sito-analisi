A continuous stream pays at a rate of $120\log 2$ euros per year for one year. The constant annual force of interest is $\log 2$. What is its accumulated value at year-end?

- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $83.18\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

To find the accumulated value at time $1$, accumulate each contribution from time $t$ to time $1$ with factor $e^{(1-t)\log 2}=2^{1-t}$. This gives
$$
M_1=\int_0^1 120\log 2\cdot2^{1-t}\,dt=120\,(2-1)=120\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $60$ euros is the present value at time $0$, namely $\dfrac{120}{2}$. The value of $83.18$ euros is the sum of deposits, $120\log 2$ euros, without interest.
