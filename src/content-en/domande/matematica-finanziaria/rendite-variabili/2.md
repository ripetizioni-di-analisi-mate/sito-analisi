An annuity makes four year-end payments, starting at $80$ euros and increasing by $20$ euros each year. What is its present value at an effective annual rate of $25\%$?

- $188.93\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $294.02\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $246.78\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $440\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The discount factor is $v=\dfrac{1}{1.25}=0.8$. Since payment $k$ includes $k-1$ increments, split the annuity into four payments of $80$ euros plus the increments, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
V_0&=80\left(v+v^2+v^3+v^4\right)+20\left(v^2+2v^3+3v^4\right)\\[6pt]
&=80\cdot2.3616+20\cdot2.8928=246.784\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The value of $188.93$ euros omits the increments. The value of $294.02$ euros counts $k$ increments in payment $k$ and therefore includes one even in the first payment. The nominal sum is $440$ euros.
