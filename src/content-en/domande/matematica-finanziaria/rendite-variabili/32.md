Payments increase by $30$ euros at each due date, starting at $150$ euros. Which pair gives the percentage increases from the first to the second payment and from the second to the third?

- $20\%$ and $20\%$
- $20\%$ and $\dfrac{50}{3}\%$
- $30\%$ and $30\%$
- $20\%$ and $\dfrac{100}{7}\%$

## Explanation

The fixed increment gives payments of $150$, $180$, and $210$ euros. The first percentage change is $\dfrac{30}{150}=20\%$. Using the new base of $180$ euros gives a second change of $\dfrac{30}{180}=\dfrac{50}{3}\%$. In an arithmetic sequence with $d>0$, the percentage increases get smaller. The pair $20\%$ and $20\%$ would describe a geometric sequence.
