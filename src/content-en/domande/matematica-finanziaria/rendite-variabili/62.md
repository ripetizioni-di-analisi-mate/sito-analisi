A continuous stream paying at a rate of $c(t)=140e^{0.06t}$ euros per year begins today. The annual force of interest is $0.06\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$. How long must it run to have a present value of $700$ euros?

- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $6\,\mathrm{years}$
- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $7\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

The discounted payment rate is constant at $140e^{0.06t}e^{-0.06t}=140$ euros per year. Growth equals the force of interest, so case $\delta=\gamma$ gives $V_0=c_0T$. Setting $140T=700$ gives $T=5$ years. The result is independent of the common value of $\delta$ and $\gamma$ because discounting exactly offsets payment growth at every instant.
