A four-payment geometric annuity starts at $110$ euros. Its growth rate and effective interest rate both equal $10\%$. How much does adding a fifth payment under the same rule increase the present value?

- $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $161.05\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The fifth payment follows the same rule and equals $110\cdot1.1^4\approx161.05$ euros at time $5$. Its present value is
$$
\frac{110\cdot1.1^4}{1.1^5}=\frac{110}{1.1}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The other payments are unchanged, so present value increases by $100$ euros. The amount of $161.05$ euros is the fifth payment before discounting.
