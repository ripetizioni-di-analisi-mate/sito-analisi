An annuity pays $200$ euros in one year and $260$ euros in two years. The second payment is reduced to $140$ euros. At an effective annual rate of $10\%$, by how much does the present value fall?

- $99.17\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $109.09\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $49.59\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first payment is unchanged, so the change in present value comes entirely from the second payment. It falls by $260-140=120$ euros at time $2$, giving
$$
\Delta V_0=120v^2=\frac{120}{1.1^2}\approx99.17\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $120$ euros omits discounting. The value of $109.09$ euros discounts for only one year. The value of $49.59$ euros considers only the original increase of $60$ euros.
