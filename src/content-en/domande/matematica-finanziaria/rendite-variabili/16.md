A contract pays $110$ euros at time $1$ and an amount $X$ at time $2$. Its present value is $300$ euros and the effective annual rate is $10\%$. What is $X$?

- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $210\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first payment is worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100$ euros today. The total present value is $300$ euros, leaving $200$ euros as the present value of the second payment. That payment is due at time $2$, giving $X=200\cdot1.1^2=242$ euros. The value of $200$ euros leaves its present value unaccumulated to time $2$, while $220$ euros accumulates it for just one year.
