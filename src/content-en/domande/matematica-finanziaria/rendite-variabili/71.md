The annual force of interest is $\delta(t)=0.04t$ for $0\leq t\leq3$, with $t$ in years. What is the exact factor for discounting from time $3$ to time $1$?

- $e^{-0.08}$
- $e^{-0.18}$
- $e^{-0.24}$
- $e^{-0.16}$

## Explanation

To discount from time $3$ to time $1$, integrate the force between $1$ and $3$. This gives
$$
\int_1^3 0.04u\,du=\frac{0.04}{2}\left(3^2-1^2\right)=0.02\cdot8=0.16 ,
$$
so the discount factor is $e^{-0.16}$. The exponent $-0.18$ integrates from $0$ to $3$, starting today instead of at the valuation date. The exponent $-0.24$ multiplies the force at time $3$ by the length of the interval.
