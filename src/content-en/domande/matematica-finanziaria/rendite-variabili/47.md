A perpetuity pays $200$ euros in one year and decreases by $10\%$ at each subsequent payment. What is its present value at a zero rate?

- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At a zero rate $v=1$ and present value equals the sum of the payment amounts. They form a geometric sequence with first term $200$ euros and ratio $0.9$. Since $i=0>g=-0.10$, the series converges and
$$
V_0=\frac{200}{0-(-0.10)}=\frac{200}{1-0.9}=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $1800$ euros begins with the second payment and omits the first receipt.
