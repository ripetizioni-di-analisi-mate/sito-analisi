An annuity pays $330$ euros one year from now and $1331$ euros three years from now. Find its value at time $0$ at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

- $1661\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1430\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At time $0$, add the payment amounts after discounting each to today using $v=\dfrac{1}{1.1}$ for the time until it falls due. This gives
$$
V_0=\frac{330}{1.1}+\frac{1331}{1.1^3}=300+1000=1300\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $1661$ euros simply adds the nominal amounts without discounting. The value of $1400$ euros discounts the second payment for two years instead of three.
