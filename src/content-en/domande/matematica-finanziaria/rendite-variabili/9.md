An account earning an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$ receives $300$ euros today, $500$ euros one year from now, and $250$ euros two years from now. Find the balance immediately after the last deposit.

- $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1130\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1279.30\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1163\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The balance immediately after the final deposit is the accumulated value at time $2$. Today's deposit earns two years' interest, the deposit at time $1$ earns one year's interest, and the one at time $2$ enters at face value, giving
$$
M_2=300\cdot1.1^2+500\cdot1.1+250=363+550+250=1163\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $1050$ euros simply adds the deposits. The value of $1279.30$ euros accumulates each deposit for one extra year.
