A continuous cash flow is paid at a rate of $c(t)=320e^{0.04t}$ euros per year from today to time $3$. Find its present value when the force of interest is $\delta=0.04\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$.

- $320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1280\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $960\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Multiply the payment rate by the discount factor $e^{-0.04t}$. The discounted payment rate is $320e^{0.04t}e^{-0.04t}=320$ euros per year for every $t$. Since growth equals the force of interest, the case $\delta=\gamma$ applies and
$$
V_0=\int_0^3 320\,dt=320\cdot3=960\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $8000$ euros divides $320$ by the force of interest as though growth did not offset discounting. The value of $1280$ euros counts four years in place of three.
