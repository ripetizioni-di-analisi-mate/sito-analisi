Two infinite continuous payment streams have the same positive initial payment rate. The first grows continuously at $2\%$ and is discounted at a force of $6\%$. The second grows at $4\%$. Which discount force makes the present values equal on every time interval?

- $8\%$
- $4\%$
- $6\%$
- $2\%$

## Explanation

The first discounted payment density is $c_0e^{(0.02-0.06)t}$. The two integrals agree on every interval when their discounted densities agree pointwise. Thus $0.04-\delta=-0.04$, giving $\delta=0.08$.
