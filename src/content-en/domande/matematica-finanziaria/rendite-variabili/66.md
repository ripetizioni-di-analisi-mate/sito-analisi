A concession produces a constant continuous payment stream of $300\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$ starting at time $2$ and continuing forever. The force of interest is constant at $\delta=0.05\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$. What is the stream worth today?

- $5429.02\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $6631.03\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $570.98\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At time $2$ the stream is a continuous perpetuity with constant density, so on that date it is worth $\dfrac{300}{0.05}=6000$ euros. The factor $e^{-0.05\cdot2}=e^{-0.1}$ brings this value back to today, so
$$
V_0=\int_2^{+\infty}300e^{-0.05t}\,dt=6000\,e^{-0.1}\approx5429.02\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $6000$ euros ignores the deferral, while $6631.03$ euros multiplies by $e^{0.1}$ instead of discounting. The value of $570.98$ euros is the present value of a stream between $0$ and $2$, that is, over the interval in which the concession pays nothing.
