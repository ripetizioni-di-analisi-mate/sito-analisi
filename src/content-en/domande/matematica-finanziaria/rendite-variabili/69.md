A continuous stream pays from today to finite time $T>0$ at a rate of $c_0e^{\gamma t}$ with $c_0>0$. The growth rate $\gamma$ exceeds the force of interest $\delta$. Which statement describes its present value?

- It is infinite.
- It is zero.
- It is finite and less than $c_0T$.
- It is finite and greater than $c_0T$.

## Explanation

The discounted payment rate is $c_0e^{(\gamma-\delta)t}$ with $\gamma-\delta>0$. It is continuous on $[0,T]$, so the integral is finite. For $t>0$ the exponent is positive and the integrand exceeds $c_0$, making the integral greater than $\int_0^Tc_0\,dt=c_0T$. An infinite value occurs only over an infinite horizon, as in a perpetuity.
