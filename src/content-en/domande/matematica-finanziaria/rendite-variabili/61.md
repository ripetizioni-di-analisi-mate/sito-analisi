A continuous payment stream has density $c(t)=500e^{0.03t}$ euros per year from today until time $10$. The force of interest is constant at $\delta=0.08\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$, and $e^{-0.5}\approx0.6065$. What is the present value of the stream, rounded to the nearest euro?

- $10000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3442\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3935\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5831\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The discounted density is $c(t)e^{-\delta t}=500e^{0.03t}e^{-0.08t}=500e^{-0.05t}$ euros per year. Since the growth rate differs from the force of interest, the present value is
$$
V_0=\int_0^{10}500e^{-0.05t}\,dt=\frac{500}{0.05}\left(1-e^{-0.5}\right).
$$
With $e^{-0.5}\approx0.6065$ this gives $V_0\approx10000\cdot0.3935=3935$ euros. The value of $10000$ euros is the value of the perpetuity with the same density and ignores the end of the stream at time $10$. The value of $3442$ euros ignores the growth and discounts a constant density of $500$ euros per year, while $5831$ euros integrates the density without discounting it.
