Receipts of $110$ euros and $133.10$ euros are due at times $1$ and $3$, respectively. At an effective annual rate of $10\%$, what are they worth at time $1$ immediately before the first is received?

- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $231\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $243.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At the valuation time $1$, the payment of $110$ euros enters at face value. Discount the payment at time $3$ for two years to obtain $\dfrac{133.10}{1.1^2}=110$ euros. Their total value at time $1$ is $110+110=220$ euros. The value of $200$ euros refers to time $0$, while $243.10$ euros simply adds the nominal amounts.
