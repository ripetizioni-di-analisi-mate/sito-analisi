Four payments form a geometric sequence, starting at $100$ euros and decreasing by $20\%$ per payment. What is their present value at a zero rate?

- $280\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $295.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Payment $k$ is $100\cdot0.8^{k-1}$ euros because each payment equals $80\%$ of the preceding one. The four payments are $100$, $80$, $64$, and $51.20$ euros. At a zero rate their present value is their sum, $295.20$ euros, which agrees with $\dfrac{100}{0.2}\left(1-0.8^4\right)$. The value of $280$ euros treats the percentage decline as a fixed decrease of $20$ euros. The value of $400$ euros ignores the decline.
