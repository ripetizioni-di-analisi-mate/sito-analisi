A continuous stream pays at a constant rate of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$ from today to time $1$, with a constant annual force of interest $\log 2$. What single payment at time $1$ is equivalent to the stream?

- $\dfrac{500}{\log 2}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $\dfrac{1000}{\log 2}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The equivalent single payment at time $1$ is the accumulated value of the stream at that date. A contribution made at time $t$ earns interest with factor $e^{(1-t)\log 2}=2^{1-t}$, so
$$
M_1=\int_0^1 1000\cdot2^{1-t}\,dt=\frac{1000\,(2-1)}{\log 2}=\frac{1000}{\log 2}\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $\dfrac{500}{\log 2}$ euros refers to time $0$. The value of $1000$ euros ignores interest, while $2000$ euros accumulates the entire stream for a full year.
