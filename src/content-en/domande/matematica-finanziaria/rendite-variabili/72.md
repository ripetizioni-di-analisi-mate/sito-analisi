The force of interest is $\log 2$ per year until time $1$ and zero between $1$ and $2$. What is the present value of $600$ euros receivable at time $2$?

- $150\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The factor for discounting from time $2$ to today is $\exp\left(-\int_0^2\delta(u)\,du\right)$. The integral equals $\log 2\cdot1+0\cdot1=\log 2$ because the force is zero in the second year. The factor is therefore $e^{-\log 2}=\dfrac12$ and the receipt is worth $\dfrac{600}{2}=300$ euros today. The value of $150$ euros applies the force $\log 2$ in both years. The value of $1200$ euros accumulates instead of discounting.
