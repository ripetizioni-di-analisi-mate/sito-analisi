A fund earning an effective annual interest rate of $20\%$ receives three year-end deposits. The first is $250$ euros and each subsequent deposit is $20\%$ larger than the previous one. How much must be added at time $3$ to reach $1200$ euros immediately after the last deposit?

- $290\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $144\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The payments are $250$, $300$, and $360$ euros at times $1$, $2$, and $3$. At time $3$ the first has earned two years' interest and the second one year's interest, so
$$
250\cdot1.2^2+300\cdot1.2+360=360+360+360=1080\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Payment growth equals the interest rate, so each payment contributes $360$ euros. The additional deposit required is $1200-1080=120$ euros. The value of $290$ euros subtracts the nominal sum of $910$ euros.
