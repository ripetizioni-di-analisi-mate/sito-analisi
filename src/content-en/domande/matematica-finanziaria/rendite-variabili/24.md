An arithmetic annuity pays $120$ euros on the first due date and $240$ euros on the fifth. What is its third payment?

- $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $150\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $168\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $210\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

In an arithmetic annuity, payment $k$ equals $R_k=R_1+(k-1)d$. There are four increments between the first and fifth payments, so $4d=240-120=120$ and $d=30$ euros. The third contains two increments and equals $120+2\cdot30=180$ euros. The value of $168$ euros divides $120$ by five instead of four. The value of $150$ euros is the second payment.
