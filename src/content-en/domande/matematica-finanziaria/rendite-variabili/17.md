A receipt of $440$ euros in one year is followed by a payment of $242$ euros in two years. At an effective annual rate of $10\%$, what is the net present value for the party receiving the first payment?

- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $198\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

From the perspective of the person receiving the first payment, cash received is positive and cash paid is negative. Their present values are $\dfrac{440}{1.1}=400$ euros for the receipt and $\dfrac{242}{1.1^2}=200$ euros for the payment. The net present value is $400-200=200$ euros. The value of $600$ euros counts the payment as a receipt, while $198$ euros is the difference between the nominal amounts.
