A perpetuity payable in arrears starts at $180$ euros, grows by $2\%$ annually, and is worth $3000$ euros today. What is the effective annual valuation rate?

- $6\%$
- $8\%$
- $4\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

Rearranging $V_0=\dfrac{R_1}{i-g}$ gives $i-g=\dfrac{R_1}{V_0}=\dfrac{180}{3000}=0.06$. Growth is $g=0.02$, so $i=0.06+0.02=0.08$, or $8\%$ per year. This rate satisfies $i>g$, as required for the growing-perpetuity formula. The value $6\%$ is the difference $i-g$ before adding growth. The value $4\%$ subtracts growth instead of adding it.
