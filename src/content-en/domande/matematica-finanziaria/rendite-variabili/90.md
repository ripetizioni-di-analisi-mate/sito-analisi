A savings plan at an effective rate of $i>0$ includes a deposit at time $n\geq2$, its target date. Which accumulation factor applies to that deposit if $q=1+i$ and $v=q^{-1}$?

- $q$
- $q^n$
- $v^n$
- $1$

## Explanation

In the accumulated-value equation, multiply the deposit at time $t_k$ by $q^{n-t_k}$ to allow for interest earned until the target date $n$. For a deposit at the target date $t_k=n$, so the factor is $q^0=1$ and the deposit enters at face value. The factor $q^n$ belongs to a deposit made at time $0$.
