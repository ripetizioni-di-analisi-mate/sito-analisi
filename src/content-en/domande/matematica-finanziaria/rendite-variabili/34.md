The first of three annual annuity payments falls at time $0$. It is $150$ euros and subsequent payments grow by $10\%$ per year. What is the present value at an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $495\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $409.09\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first payment at time $0$ enters at face value, $150$ euros. The subsequent payments are $150\cdot1.1$ euros at time $1$ and $150\cdot1.1^2$ euros at time $2$. Growth equals interest, so each is worth $150$ euros today, giving
$$
V_0=150+150+150=450\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $409.09$ euros treats the annuity as payable in arrears. The value of $495$ euros accumulates the result for one year.
