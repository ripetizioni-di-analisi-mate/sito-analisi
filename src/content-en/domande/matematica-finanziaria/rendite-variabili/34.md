A statement assigns the same present value to four payments assumed to be annual and growing by $5\%$ per payment at a constant $5\%$ annual rate. The third payment actually falls two years after the second, with just one $5\%$ increase between them. What is the ratio of their present values $V_3$ and $V_2$?

- $\dfrac{V_3}{V_2}=1$
- $\dfrac{V_3}{V_2}=\dfrac1{1.05}$
- $\dfrac{V_3}{V_2}=1.05$
- $\dfrac{V_3}{V_2}=\dfrac1{1.05^2}$

## Explanation

The payment grows by a factor of $1.05$ but must be discounted for two extra years. The ratio is $\dfrac{1.05}{1.05^2}=\dfrac1{1.05}$. Growth exactly offset interest only when payments were a year apart.
