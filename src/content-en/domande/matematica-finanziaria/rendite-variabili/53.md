A continuous stream pays $200\log 2$ euros per year at a constant rate from today to time $1$. The constant annual force of interest is $\log 2$. What is its present value?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At force of interest $\log 2$ the discount factor at time $t$ is $2^{-t}$. For a constant payment rate on $[0,1]$ this gives
$$
V_0=\frac{200\log 2}{\log 2}\left(1-e^{-\log 2}\right)=200\left(1-\frac12\right)=100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $200$ euros is the accumulated value at time $1$, the present value accumulated for one year. The undiscounted sum of deposits is $200\log 2\approx138.63$ euros.
