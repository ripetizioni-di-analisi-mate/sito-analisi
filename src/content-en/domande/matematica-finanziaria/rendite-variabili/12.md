A continuous cash flow is paid at a constant rate of $500\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$ from time $1$ to time $2$. Find its exact present value at a constant annual force of interest of $\log 2$.

- $\dfrac{250}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $125 \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $\dfrac{375}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $\dfrac{125}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With force of interest $\log 2$ the discount factor at time $t$ is $e^{-t\log 2}=2^{-t}$. The present value is
$$
V_0=\int_1^2 500\cdot2^{-t}\,dt=\frac{500}{\log 2}\left(2^{-1}-2^{-2}\right)=\frac{125}{\log 2}\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $\dfrac{250}{\log 2}$ euros refers to time $1$, omitting the year of discount before the stream starts. The value of $\dfrac{375}{\log 2}$ euros integrates from $0$ to $2$.
