A continuous stream is valued using its payment rate $c(t)=500\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$. If time is measured in years, what are the units of the elementary contribution $c(t)\,dt$?

- Euros.
- Euros per year.
- Years.
- Euros per year squared.

## Explanation

The payment rate $c(t)$ is measured in euros per year and the time increment $dt$ in years. Their product $c(t)\,dt$ is therefore an amount in euros, the payment during the short interval of length $dt$. Because the discount factor is dimensionless, the integral $\int_0^T c(t)e^{-\delta t}\,dt$ is also measured in euros.
