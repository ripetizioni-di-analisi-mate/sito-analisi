A perpetuity pays $132$ euros at time $2$ and grows by $4\%$ annually thereafter. What is its present value at an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first payment falls at time $2$, so the growing-perpetuity formula gives the value at time $1$, one period before the first payment. This is $\dfrac{132}{0.10-0.04}=2200$ euros. Discounting for one more year gives today's value of $V_0=\dfrac{2200}{1.1}=2000$ euros. The value of $2200$ euros ignores the deferral, while $2420$ euros accumulates instead of discounting.
