An annuity makes five year-end payments. The first is $132$ euros and each subsequent payment is $10\%$ larger than the previous one. Find the present value at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

- $660\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $480\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Both the payment growth rate and the valuation rate equal $10\%$, so $\rho=(1+g)v=1$ and all payments have the same present value. The first is worth $\dfrac{132}{1.1}=120$ euros today, making the five payments worth $V_0=\dfrac{5\cdot132}{1.1}=600$ euros. The value of $660$ euros adds $132$ euros five times without discounting the first payment.
