A continuous stream pays $c$ euros per year for two years. Its present value is $300$ euros at a constant annual force of interest $\log 2$. What is the exact payment rate $c$?

- $400 \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$
- $300 \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$
- $200 \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$
- $\dfrac{400}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$

## Explanation

With force of interest $\log 2$ and a two-year term, the present value is
$$
V_0=\frac{c}{\log 2}\left(1-e^{-2\log 2}\right)=\frac{c}{\log 2}\cdot\frac34=\frac{3c}{4\log 2}.
$$
Setting $V_0=300$ gives $3c=1200\log 2$ and hence $c=400\log 2$ euros per year. The value $300\log 2$ omits the factor $\dfrac34$. The value $\dfrac{400}{\log 2}$ divides by $\log 2$ instead of multiplying.
