Two schedules each pay $1000$ euros in total. The first pays the entire amount in one year and the second in three years. Which comparison holds at a negative effective annual rate strictly greater than $-1$?

- The payment due in three years is worth more today.
- The payment due in one year is worth more today.
- The two present values are equal.
- Both present values are below $1000$ euros.

## Explanation

If $-1<i<0$, then $0<1+i<1$ and therefore $v=\dfrac{1}{1+i}>1$. The payment due in one year is worth $1000v$ euros today and the one due in three years is worth $1000v^3$ euros today. Since $v>1$, we have $v^3>v$, and the later payment is worth more. Both present values exceed $1000$ euros, which rules out the claim that they are below $1000$ euros.
