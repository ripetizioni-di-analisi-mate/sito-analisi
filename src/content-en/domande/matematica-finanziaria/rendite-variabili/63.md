A continuous perpetuity pays at a rate of $c(t)=280e^{0.02t}$ euros per year and is worth $7000$ euros today. What is its constant annual force of interest, expressed as a percentage?

- $2\%$
- $4\%$
- $8\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

A continuous perpetuity with $\delta>\gamma$ satisfies $V_0=\dfrac{c_0}{\delta-\gamma}$. Rearranging gives
$$
\delta-\gamma=\frac{280}{7000}=0.04\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}.
$$
Since $\gamma=0.02$, the force of interest is $\delta=0.04+0.02=0.06$, or $6\%$ per year. The value $4\%$ is the difference $\delta-\gamma$ before adding growth. The value $2\%$ subtracts growth instead of adding it.
