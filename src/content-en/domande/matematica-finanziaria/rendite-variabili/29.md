A contract makes two payments in arrears. The first is $100$ euros and the second is $20$ euros larger. What is their accumulated value at the last payment date at an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $230\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $210\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The accumulated value is measured at the last payment date, time $2$. The amounts are $100$ and $120$ euros. The first earns one year's interest and the second enters at face value, giving
$$
M_2=100\cdot1.1+120=110+120=230\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The nominal sum is $220$ euros. The value of $242$ euros also accumulates the second payment for a year even though it falls on the valuation date.
