A perpetuity payable in arrears grows by $2\%$ annually. It is worth $4000$ euros at time $0$ and its effective annual valuation rate is $5\%$. What is the first payment?

- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $126\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A growing perpetuity payable in arrears satisfies $V_0=\dfrac{R_1}{i-g}$, giving $R_1=V_0(i-g)=4000\cdot(0.05-0.02)=4000\cdot0.03=120$ euros. The value of $200$ euros multiplies by the interest rate $0.05$ alone, ignoring growth. The value of $80$ euros multiplies by growth, while $126$ euros is the second payment.
