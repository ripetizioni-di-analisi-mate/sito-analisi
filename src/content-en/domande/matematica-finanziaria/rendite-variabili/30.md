An arithmetic annuity makes four payments in arrears with increment $d$. What coefficient multiplies $d$ in its present value if the periodic discount factor is $v$?

- $v+2v^2+3v^3+4v^4$
- $2v^2+3v^3+4v^4$
- $v^2+2v^3$
- $v^2+2v^3+3v^4$

## Explanation

Payment $k$ contains $k-1$ increments, so the coefficient of $d$ in its present value is $(k-1)v^k$. For $k=1,2,3,4$ the coefficients are $0$, $v^2$, $2v^3$, and $3v^4$, making the coefficient of $d$ equal to $G_4(v)=v^2+2v^3+3v^4$. The expression $v+2v^2+3v^3+4v^4$ counts $k$ increments in payment $k$, incorrectly including an increment in the first payment.
