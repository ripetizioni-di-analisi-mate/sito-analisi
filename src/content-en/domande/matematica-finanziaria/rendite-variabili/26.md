A schedule includes only strictly positive receipts. The first is $310$ euros and each subsequent payment is $50$ euros smaller. What is the maximum number of positive payments?

- $5$
- $7$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

Payment $k$ is $310-50(k-1)$ euros. It is strictly positive when $50(k-1)<310$, or $k-1<6.2$. The largest admissible integer is $k=7$. The seventh payment is $310-300=10$ euros and the eighth would be $-40$ euros, so there can be at most seven positive payments. The value $6$ counts increments instead of payments.
