An annuity pays at times $0$, $1$, and $4$. Which exponents of the annual accumulation factor $q$ apply to the three payments, in order, when finding their value at time $4$?

- $4$, $3$, $0$
- $0$, $1$, $4$
- $3$, $2$, $1$
- $4$, $2$, $1$

## Explanation

In the accumulated value at time $T$, the payment at time $t_k$ is multiplied by $q^{T-t_k}$ because it earns interest from its due date to the final date. With $T=4$ the exponents are $4-0=4$, $4-1=3$, and $4-4=0$. The payment at time $4$ therefore enters at face value. The triple $0$, $1$, $4$ uses payment dates in place of the times remaining to the final date.
