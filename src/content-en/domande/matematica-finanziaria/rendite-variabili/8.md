An account earning an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$ receives $1000$ euros today and two equal deposits at the ends of years one and two. How large must each deposit be for the balance to reach $2050$ euros at the end of year two?

- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $840\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At time $2$ the initial deposit is worth $1000\cdot1.1^2=1210$ euros. The deposit at time $1$ earns one year's interest and the one at time $2$ enters with factor $1$, giving the savings equation
$$
1210+1.1R+R=2050 .
$$
This gives $2.1R=840$ and therefore $R=400$ euros. The value of $500$ euros leaves the original $1000$ euros unaccumulated. The value of $840$ euros assigns the entire shortfall to a single deposit.
