The force of interest is positive before time $1$ and zero between $1$ and $2$. A receipt of $500$ euros is postponed from time $1$ to time $2$. What happens to its present value?

- It decreases.
- It stays the same.
- It increases.
- It becomes equal to $500$ euros.

## Explanation

The discount factor at time $t$ is $\exp\left(-\int_0^t\delta(u)\,du\right)$. The force is zero from $1$ to $2$, so
$$
\int_0^2\delta(u)\,du=\int_0^1\delta(u)\,du .
$$
The discount factors at times $1$ and $2$ are equal, so the receipt's present value stays the same. It remains below $500$ euros because the force is positive during the first year.
