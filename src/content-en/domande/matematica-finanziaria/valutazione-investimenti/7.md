A loan with face amount $1040\,\mathrm{EUR}$ withholds $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ upfront and requires a payment of $1080\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Calculate the effective annual rate on the borrower's cash flow.

- $12\%$
- $8\%$
- $4\%$
- $3.85\%$

## Explanation

The borrower receives the face amount less the amount withheld, $1040-40=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The effective rate makes the NPV of the borrower's cash flow zero, so $1000=\dfrac{1080}{1+i}$ and $1+i=1.08$. The rate is therefore $8\%$. The answer $3.85\%$ compares the repayment with the loan's face amount of $1040\,\mathrm{EUR}$, which the borrower never receives in full.
