All of a project's receipts are nonnegative. At the final date their total present value is below the initial outlay. What follows about discounted payback?

- It occurs on the final date.
- It occurs on the first date with a positive receipt.
- It does not occur within the project's horizon.
- It occurs between the final two dates.

## Explanation

Since receipts are nonnegative, their cumulative present value never decreases and reaches its maximum at the final date. Even this value is below the initial outlay, so the cumulative totals on all earlier dates are also below it. Discounted payback does not occur within the project's horizon, and NPV is negative.
