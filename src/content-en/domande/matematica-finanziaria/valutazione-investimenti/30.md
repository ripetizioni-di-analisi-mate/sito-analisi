Cash flows due in $6$ and $18$ months are written as a polynomial in $v$ using six months as the time unit. What period does the rate obtained from a positive root cover?

- A year and a half.
- One year.
- Six months.
- One month.

## Explanation

With six months as the time unit, maturities of $6$ and $18$ months correspond to times $1$ and $3$, and $v$ is a six-month discount factor. The positive root gives $i=v^{-1}-1$, a six-month rate. The equivalent annual rate is $(1+i)^2-1$ because one year contains two six-month periods.
