For a positive initial outlay followed by a single positive future receipt, what does NPV approach as the rate tends to $+\infty$?

- Zero.
- The negative of the initial outlay.
- It tends to $+\infty$.
- The amount of the future receipt.

## Explanation

NPV is $-C_0+M(1+i)^{-T}$, where $C_0>0$ is the initial outlay and $M>0$ the receipt after $T>0$ periods. As $i\to+\infty$, the factor $(1+i)^{-T}$ tends to zero, so the present value of the receipt also tends to zero. NPV therefore tends to $-C_0$, the negative of the initial outlay. The limit $+\infty$ occurs when $i\to-1^+$.
