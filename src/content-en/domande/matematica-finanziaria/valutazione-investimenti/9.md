An investment generates receipts of $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. It costs $133.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ to dispose of it in three years. At $10\%$, what is the maximum initial outlay consistent with a nonnegative NPV?

- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $328.9\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At the maximum initial outlay, NPV is zero, so the outlay equals the sum of the present values of all future cash flows. At $10\%$ this gives
$$
\frac{220}{1.1}+\frac{242}{1.1^2}-\frac{133.10}{1.1^3}=200+200-100=300\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the disposal cost, while $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ treats it as a receipt.
