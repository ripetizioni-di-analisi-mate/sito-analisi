With nonnegative future receipts and a positive annual rate, how do simple and discounted payback compare if both occur?

- Discounted payback always occurs strictly later.
- Discounted payback always occurs earlier.
- They always occur on the same date.
- Discounted payback occurs on the same date or later.

## Explanation

For $i>0$ every discount factor $(1+i)^{-t_k}$ is less than $1$, so the cumulative discounted amount at each date is at most the cumulative nominal amount. If discounted receipts reach the cost, nominal receipts have already reached it. Discounted payback therefore occurs on the same date as simple payback or later. The dates match when the same receipt is enough under both calculations.
