A project exchanges $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today for $1331\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years. What is the annual IRR?

- $21\%$
- $10\%$
- $11.0333\%$
- $33.1\%$

## Explanation

With one receipt in three years the IRR satisfies $1000(1+i^*)^3=1331$. The three-year factor is $1.331$ and, since $1.1^3=1.331$, the annual factor is $\sqrt[3]{1.331}=1.1$. The annual IRR is therefore $10\%$. The answer $11.0333\%$ divides the total return of $33.1\%$ by three as under simple interest.
