One project costs €100 today and makes a single payment in a year. A second project costs €300 and also makes a single payment in a year. Their IRRs are $20\%$ and $4\%$ respectively. What is the IRR of the combined investment?

- $12\%$
- $8\%$
- $16\%$
- $24\%$

## Explanation

The receipts are €120 and €312. The combined project turns €400 into €432 in one year, so its return is $\dfrac{32}{400}=8\%$. For these one-year investments the weights are the amounts invested.
