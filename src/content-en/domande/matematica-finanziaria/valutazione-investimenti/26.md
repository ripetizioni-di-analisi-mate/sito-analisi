A project costs $100$ euros today, has a single receipt in one year and an IRR of $20\%$. A second project costs $300$ euros today, also has a single receipt in one year and an IRR of $4\%$. What is the IRR of the investment that undertakes both projects?

- $12\%$
- $8\%$
- $16\%$
- $24\%$

## Explanation

With a single receipt in one year, the IRR equals the one-year return, so the receipts are $100\cdot1.2=120$ euros and $300\cdot1.04=312$ euros. The combined investment costs $400$ euros today and receives $432$ euros in one year. Its IRR is therefore $\dfrac{432-400}{400}=8\%$. The value $12\%$ is the simple average of the two IRRs and ignores the different amounts invested, while $16\%$ swaps the weights of the two projects. The value $24\%$ is the sum of the two IRRs.
