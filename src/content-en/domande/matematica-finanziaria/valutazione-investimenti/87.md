At a rate of $10\%$, a receipt of $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year is replaced by an equivalent receipt in year two. What is the new amount?

- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $266.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The equivalent receipt in year two must have the same present value. Moving an amount one year forward requires accumulating it for one year, so the new amount is $220\cdot1.1=242\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Indeed, $\dfrac{242}{1.21}=\dfrac{220}{1.1}=200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts the amount instead of accumulating it, while $266.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accumulates it for two years.
