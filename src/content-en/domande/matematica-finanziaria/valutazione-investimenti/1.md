A project costs $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and pays $330\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $363\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Calculate its NPV at an annual rate of $10\%$.

- $-100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $130\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $193\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Calculate NPV by discounting every cash flow to time $0$ using the factor $1.1^{-t}$. The first year's receipt is worth $\dfrac{330}{1.1}=300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and the second year's is worth $\dfrac{363}{1.21}=300\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Therefore
$$
\operatorname{NPV}(0.10)=-500+300+300=100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $193\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the cost from the nominal sum of receipts without discounting.
