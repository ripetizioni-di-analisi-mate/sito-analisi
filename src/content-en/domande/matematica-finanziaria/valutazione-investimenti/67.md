An investment costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and receives $40$, $60$ and $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the ends of the first three years. When does simple payback occur?

- In year two.
- In year one.
- The cost is never recovered.
- In year three.

## Explanation

Simple payback is the first date when nominal receipts total at least the cost, without discounting. After one year the sum is $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$, below $100$. After year two it is $40+60=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, exactly the cost, and equality counts as recovery. Simple payback therefore occurs in year two. The year-three receipt does not affect that date.
