A cash flow has NPV $-100+210v-110v^2$. What is the set of admissible IRRs?

- $\{0\%\}$.
- $\{-10\%,10\%\}$.
- $\{0\%,10\%\}$.
- $\{10\%\}$.

## Explanation

The polynomial factors as
$$
-100+210v-110v^2=-(10v-10)(11v-10),
$$
with roots $v=1$ and $v=\dfrac{10}{11}$, both positive. Using $i=v^{-1}-1$, the root $v=1$ gives $i=0\%$ and the root $v=\dfrac{10}{11}$ gives $i=10\%$. The IRRs are therefore $0\%$ and $10\%$. The rate $0\%$ can also be checked by summing the nominal cash flows, $-100+210-110=0$.
