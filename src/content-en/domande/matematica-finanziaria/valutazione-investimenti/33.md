A project costs $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $80$ and $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on the next two dates. Which set contains its IRR?

- The project has no IRR.
- $(0,+\infty)$.
- Only at $i=0$.
- $(-1,0)$.

## Explanation

At a zero rate NPV equals the nominal sum of the cash flows, $-200+80+100=-20\,\mathrm{EUR}$. This conventional cash flow has a strictly decreasing NPV that approaches $+\infty$ as $i\to-1^+$. Since NPV is already negative at $i=0$, the unique IRR lies to the left of zero in the interval $(-1,0)$. An IRR exists because at least one future receipt is positive.
