A project costs $205\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and pays $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Using a rate of $10\%$, calculate how much of the cost remains unrecovered at the second date, in euros as of the initial date.

- $95\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $26\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At $10\%$ the receipts are worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $\dfrac{121}{1.21}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. At the second date their cumulative present value is $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$, below the cost of $205\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The unrecovered cost in euros as of the initial date is therefore $205-200=5\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $26\,\mathrm{EUR}$ compares the cost with the nominal sum of the receipts, $231\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
