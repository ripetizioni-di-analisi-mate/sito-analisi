What single receipt in two years gives an annual IRR of $5\%$ on an initial outlay of $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

- $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $882\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $840\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The IRR is $5\%$ when the present value of the receipt at that rate equals the outlay, so $\dfrac{M}{1.05^2}=800$. The receipt therefore equals the accumulated value of the outlay after two years,
$$
M=800\cdot1.05^2=800\cdot1.1025=882\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies simple interest at $10\%$ over the two years, while $840\,\mathrm{EUR}$ compounds for only one year.
