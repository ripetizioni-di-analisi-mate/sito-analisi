A project costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, receives $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year, pays $24.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years and receives $13.31\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years. Which statement is true at an annual rate of $10\%$?

- The cost is recovered in year one and remains covered to the end.
- The cost is recovered in year one and NPV is negative.
- The cost is never recovered and NPV is negative.
- The cost is recovered in year one and NPV is zero.

## Explanation

At $10\%$ the future cash flows are worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, $-\dfrac{24.20}{1.21}=-20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $\dfrac{13.31}{1.331}=10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Their cumulative present value is $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year one, equal to the cost, so the cost is covered. After the year-two payment it falls to $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$, then rises to $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year three. NPV is therefore $90-100=-10\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and the first time the cumulative amount reaches the cost does not imply permanent recovery.
