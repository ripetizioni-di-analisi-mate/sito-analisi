The incremental cash flow $B-A$ has NPV $-17\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the given rate. Which conclusion follows?

- Both NPVs are negative.
- The NPV of $A$ exceeds that of $B$ by $17\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- The NPV of $A$ is $-17\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- The NPV of $B$ exceeds that of $A$ by $17\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

## Explanation

By linearity of discounting, $\operatorname{NPV}_{B-A}=\operatorname{NPV}_B-\operatorname{NPV}_A$. This difference is $-17\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so $\operatorname{NPV}_A=\operatorname{NPV}_B+17\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The NPV of $A$ therefore exceeds that of $B$ by $17\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Knowing the difference alone does not determine the sign of either individual NPV.
