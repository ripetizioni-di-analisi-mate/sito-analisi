Project $A$ costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $130\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Project $B$ costs $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $366\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on the same date. What is their annual crossover rate?

- $22\%$
- $8\%$
- $26\%$
- $18\%$

## Explanation

The crossover rate makes the NPV of the incremental cash flow $B-A$ zero. The difference is $-300+100=-200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $0$ and $366-130=236\,\mathrm{EUR}$ after one year, so
$$
-200+\frac{236}{1+i}=0,\qquad 1+i=\frac{236}{200}=1.18.
$$
The rate is therefore $18\%$. The answer $22\%$ is the IRR of $B$, while $8\%$ is the difference between the projects' IRRs, $30\%$ and $22\%$.
