A project's NPV is $-(11v-10)(12v-10)$. Which of the proposed rates gives a positive NPV?

- $15\%$
- $25\%$
- $10\%$
- $5\%$

## Explanation

The factors vanish at $v=\dfrac{10}{11}$ and $v=\dfrac{10}{12}$, corresponding to $10\%$ and $20\%$. For $v$ between $\dfrac{5}{6}$ and $\dfrac{10}{11}$, the factor $11v-10$ is negative and $12v-10$ is positive. The leading minus sign therefore makes NPV positive. Since $i=v^{-1}-1$ decreases with $v$, NPV is positive for $10\%<i<20\%$, and only $15\%$ among the proposed rates falls in that interval.
