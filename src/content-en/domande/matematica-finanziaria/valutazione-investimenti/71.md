A project costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and has only future receipts. Their cumulative present value is $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ and $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $3$. There are no other receipts between the two dates. What is the payback date?

- $3$.
- $1$.
- The initial cost is never recovered.
- $2$.

## Explanation

Cumulative discounted receipts change only on receipt dates. At time $1$ the total is $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$, below the cost of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and there are no receipts between time $1$ and time $3$, so the total stays at $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At time $3$ the total reaches $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and exceeds the cost for the first time. The payback date is therefore $3$.
