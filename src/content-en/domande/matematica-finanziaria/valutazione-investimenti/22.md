Equipment costs $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and generates receipts of $110$ and $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one and two years. What immediate grant would give zero NPV at $10\%$?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $31\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $19\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At $10\%$ the receipts are worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $\dfrac{121}{1.21}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, so without the grant the NPV is $-250+200=-50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. An immediate grant is a receipt at time $0$ and adds its full amount to NPV. A grant of $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ therefore gives zero NPV. The answer $19\,\mathrm{EUR}$ compares the cost with nominal receipts.
