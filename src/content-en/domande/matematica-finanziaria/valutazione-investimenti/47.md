Two mutually exclusive projects have NPVs of $-8$ and $-3\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the same rate. Declining both is an option. Which choice gives the highest NPV?

- Choose the project with an NPV of $-3\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- Decline both projects.
- Declining both and choosing the second project have equal value.
- Choose the project with an NPV of $-8\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

## Explanation

Declining both projects produces no cash flows and has zero NPV. The projects have NPVs of $-8$ and $-3\,\mathrm{EUR}$, both negative. Since $0>-3>-8$, declining both gives the highest NPV. The project with NPV $-3\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the better of the two projects but is worth less than declining both.
