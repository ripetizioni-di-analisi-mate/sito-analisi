Projects $A$ and $B$ have identical cash flows on every date. Which admissible rates are crossover rates?

- Only $0\%$.
- None.
- Only the IRR of $A$.
- All of them.

## Explanation

If the two cash flow streams match on every date, the incremental cash flow $B-A$ is zero on every date. Its NPV is a sum of zero terms and is therefore zero for every rate $i>-1$. Hence $\operatorname{NPV}_B(i)=\operatorname{NPV}_A(i)$ at every admissible rate, and every rate is a crossover rate. This conclusion is independent of the IRR of $A$ and is not restricted to a zero rate.
