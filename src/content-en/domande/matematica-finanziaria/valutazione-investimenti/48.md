A two-year project can be repeated on identical terms after two years. Its NPV at the start of each cycle is $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and the annual rate is $10\%$. What is the NPV of both cycles at time zero, to the nearest cent?

- $164.38\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $171.82\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $74.38\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The second cycle's NPV of $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is expressed in year-$2$ euros because it is measured at the renewal date. To add it to the first cycle's NPV, discount it to time $0$ by dividing by $1.1^2=1.21$. Therefore
$$
90+\frac{90}{1.21}\approx90+74.38=164.38\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the two NPVs without discounting, while $171.82\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts the second for only one year.
