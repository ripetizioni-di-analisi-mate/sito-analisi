A project has two positive IRRs, $10\%$ and $20\%$, and its NPV is positive only between them. At $22\%$ its NPV is

- zero.
- positive if the initial outlay is below the nominal receipt.
- negative.
- positive.

## Explanation

NPV is positive only between $10\%$ and $20\%$ and vanishes at those two rates. The rate of $22\%$ exceeds $20\%$, so it is outside the positive interval and differs from both IRRs. At $22\%$ the NPV is therefore negative. The given NPV signs already determine this result without comparing nominal outlays and receipts.
