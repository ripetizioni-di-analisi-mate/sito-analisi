A project with deterministic cash flows is compared with the option to decline it. Its NPV is negative at the rate representing the opportunity cost in the model. Which alternative has the higher value?

- Declining the project has the higher NPV.
- Both values are equal.
- The IRR must be known first.
- The project has the higher NPV.

## Explanation

Declining the project produces no cash flows and therefore has NPV $0$. The project's NPV is negative at the rate representing the opportunity cost of funds. A negative value is below zero, so declining the project gives the higher NPV. The comparison uses present values at the same rate and does not require the IRR.
