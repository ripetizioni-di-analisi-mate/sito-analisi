A conventional project has a unique annual IRR of $6\%$. What is the sign of NPV at a valuation rate of $9\%$?

- Negative.
- Zero.
- Positive.
- It depends on the number of receipts.

## Explanation

The project is conventional, so NPV is strictly decreasing in $i$ and vanishes only at the IRR of $6\%$. By the uniqueness theorem NPV is positive for $i<6\%$ and negative for $i>6\%$. The valuation rate $9\%$ exceeds the IRR, so NPV at $9\%$ is negative regardless of the number of receipts.
