A conventional project has an NPV of $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at $4\%$ and $-9\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at $8\%$. Which statement is guaranteed?

- The IRR is below $4\%$.
- The IRR exceeds $8\%$.
- There is exactly one IRR, between $4\%$ and $8\%$.
- The IRR is exactly $6\%$.

## Explanation

NPV is continuous in the rate, with a value of $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at $4\%$ and $-9\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at $8\%$. By the intermediate value theorem it vanishes at a rate between $4\%$ and $8\%$. Since the project is conventional, NPV is strictly decreasing and this rate is the only IRR. The two observations do not establish that it equals $6\%$ exactly.
