A project's only receipt is $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of year two, and its NPV at $10\%$ is zero. What was the initial cost?

- $440\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Zero NPV means the initial cost equals the present value of the single receipt. It arrives after two years, so the discount factor is $1.1^2=1.21$ and the cost is
$$
C_0=\frac{484}{1.21}=400\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $440\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for only one year, while $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits discounting altogether.
