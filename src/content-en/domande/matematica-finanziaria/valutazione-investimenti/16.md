A receipt of $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is brought forward from the end of year two to the end of year one. How much does NPV change at an annual rate of $10\%$?

- $-10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $21\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Before the change the receipt is worth $\dfrac{121}{1.1^2}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. With receipt in year one, its present value is $\dfrac{121}{1.1}=110\,\mathrm{EUR}$. All other cash flows stay the same, so NPV increases by $110-100=10\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $21\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the difference between the nominal receipt and its two-year present value.
