A loan with face amount $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ requires a repayment of $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. What upfront fee gives an effective annual cost of $10\%$?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $55\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At the effective cost of $10\%$, the net proceeds must equal the present value of the repayment, $\dfrac{1100}{1.1}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Net proceeds equal the face amount less the upfront fee, so $1050-S=1000$ and the fee is $S=50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the difference between the repayment and net proceeds, the total interest cost.
