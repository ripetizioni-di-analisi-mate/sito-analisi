A conventional six-month investment has an effective six-month IRR of $20\%$. What is the equivalent effective annual rate?

- $4\%$
- $40\%$
- $20\%$
- $44\%$

## Explanation

A year contains two six-month periods. Applying the six-month factor twice gives the equivalent annual factor $1.2^2=1.44$. The effective annual rate is therefore $1.44-1=44\%$. The answer $40\%$ doubles the six-month rate and ignores the interest earned in the second period on the first period's return.
