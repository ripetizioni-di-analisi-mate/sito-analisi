An expense of $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is deferred from year one to year two. How does this affect NPV at an annual rate of $10\%$?

- It increases by $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- It decreases by $21\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- It stays the same.
- It decreases by $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

## Explanation

Due in one year, the expense has a present value of $\dfrac{121}{1.1}=110\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Due in two years, it has a present value of $\dfrac{121}{1.21}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The expense enters NPV with a negative sign, so its contribution changes from $-110$ to $-100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. NPV therefore increases by $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $21\,\mathrm{EUR}$ compares the nominal amount with its present value.
