Discounted payback occurs at time $2$. A positive receipt is added at time $4$, with all other data unchanged. How do payback and NPV change?

- Both stay the same.
- Payback moves to time $4$ and NPV increases.
- Payback stays at time $2$ and NPV decreases.
- Payback stays at time $2$ and NPV increases.

## Explanation

The cumulative discounted amounts through time $2$ include only receipts at dates $t_k\leq2$, so an extra receipt at time $4$ leaves them unchanged. Payback depends on the first date at which the cumulative amount reaches the cost and remains at time $2$. NPV includes all cash flows, so it rises by the positive present value of the new receipt.
