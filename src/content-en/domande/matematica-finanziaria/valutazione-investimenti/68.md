A project costs $1000$ euros today and receives $600$ euros at the end of each of the first three years. At an annual rate of $25\%$, when does the discounted payback occur?

- In year three.
- In year two.
- In year one.
- The cost is never recovered.

## Explanation

At $25\%$ the annual discount factor is $v=\dfrac{1}{1.25}=0.8$, so the receipts are worth $600\cdot0.8=480$ euros, $600\cdot0.64=384$ euros and $600\cdot0.512=307.20$ euros today. The cumulative discounted value is $480$ euros after year one and $864$ euros after year two, both below the cost of $1000$ euros. After year three the cumulative value becomes $864+307.20=1171.20$ euros and exceeds the cost, so the discounted payback occurs in year three. Year two is the date of the simple payback, because the nominal sum $600+600=1200$ euros already exceeds the cost.
