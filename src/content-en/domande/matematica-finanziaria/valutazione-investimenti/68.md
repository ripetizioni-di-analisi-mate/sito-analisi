A project costs $210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and receives $132\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. When does it recover its cost at an annual rate of $10\%$?

- At the end of year two.
- At the end of year one.
- The initial cost is never recovered.
- Between years one and two.

## Explanation

At $10\%$ the receipts have present values of $\dfrac{132}{1.1}=120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $\dfrac{121}{1.21}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Their cumulative present value after one year is $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$, below the cost of $210\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and after two years it is $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Payback therefore occurs at the end of year two. Because the cash flows occur on discrete dates, assigning payback to an intermediate date would require an additional assumption about the payments.
