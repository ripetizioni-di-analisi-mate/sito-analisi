A project costs €100. Its subsequent cash flows, already discounted to today, are $+120$ in year one, $-50$ in year two and $+40$ in year three. At which year-end does the cumulative balance first become nonnegative and stay nonnegative through the end of the project?

- Year three.
- Year one.
- Year two.
- The cost is never recovered.

## Explanation

Including the initial cost, the cumulative balances are €20, −€30 and €10. The recovery in year one is temporary. The balance returns to a nonnegative value in year three and stays there through the end.
