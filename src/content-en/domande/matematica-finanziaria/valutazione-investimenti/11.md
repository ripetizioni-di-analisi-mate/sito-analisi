A project costs €100 today and promises one receipt in two years. The committee requires an annual IRR of at least $10\%$ even if the promised receipt is cut by $20\%$. What is the smallest receipt that must be promised before the cut?

- €145.20
- €121
- €151.25
- €137.50

## Explanation

After the cut the receipt must still be at least $100(1.10)^2=121$ euros. For a promised amount $X$ the constraint is $0.8X\ge121$, giving $X\ge151.25$ euros.
