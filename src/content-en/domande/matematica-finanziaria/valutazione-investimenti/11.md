An investment costs $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and returns $810\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Calculate its annual IRR.

- $-9.5\%$
- $-19\%$
- $-10\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

With one receipt in two years the IRR satisfies $1000(1+i^*)^2=810$. The two-year factor is $\dfrac{810}{1000}=0.81=0.9^2$, so the annual factor is $0.9$ and the IRR equals $0.9-1=-10\%$. The rate is negative because the final payment is smaller than the initial outlay. The answer $-19\%$ is the loss over both years, while $-9.5\%$ divides it by two.
