A project costs $330\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and pays $100$ and $230\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one and two years. What is its IRR?

- $0\%$
- $33\%$
- $10\%$
- $-10\%$

## Explanation

At a zero rate every discount factor is $1$, so NPV equals the nominal sum of the cash flows, $-330+100+230=0$. Thus $0\%$ is an IRR. The initial outlay is followed by positive receipts, so by the uniqueness theorem NPV is strictly decreasing and $0\%$ is the only IRR. At $10\%$, for example, NPV is negative.
