A project's NPV is $-100(v-0.875)^2-48.4375$. Which conclusion follows?

- NPV is positive for $v>1$.
- NPV has two positive roots.
- NPV is zero when $v=0.875$.
- NPV is negative at every admissible rate.

## Explanation

The term $-100(v-0.875)^2$ is nonpositive for every $v$. NPV therefore never exceeds $-48.4375$, attained at $v=0.875$, and never vanishes. NPV is negative at every admissible rate and the cash flow has no IRR. Claiming that NPV is zero at $v=0.875$ mistakes the vertex of the parabola for a root.
