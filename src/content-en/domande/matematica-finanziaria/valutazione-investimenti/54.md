For two distinct projects the incremental NPV is $0$ at a rate of $5\%$. What does this establish?

- Their cash flows match on every date.
- Each project has an IRR of $5\%$.
- Both NPVs are zero at $5\%$.
- The two NPVs are equal at $5\%$.

## Explanation

Incremental NPV is $\operatorname{NPV}_B-\operatorname{NPV}_A$. It vanishes at $5\%$ exactly when the projects have equal NPVs at $5\%$. The common value can be positive, zero or negative, so this does not determine the individual IRRs. The cash flows can differ because the equality concerns only the present values at $5\%$ and generally does not hold at other rates.
