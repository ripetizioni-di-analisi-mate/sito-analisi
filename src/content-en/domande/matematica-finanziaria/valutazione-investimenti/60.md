A borrower receives net proceeds of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, then pays $255\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one month and $260.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two months. Which monthly rate makes NPV zero?

- $1\%$
- $2\%$
- $4\%$
- $3.02\%$

## Explanation

The required monthly rate satisfies $500=\dfrac{255}{1+i}+\dfrac{260.10}{(1+i)^2}$. At $2\%$ this gives
$$
\frac{255}{1.02}+\frac{260.10}{1.0404}=250+250=500,
$$
so the borrower's cash flow has zero NPV. The borrower receives net proceeds and then makes only payments. The rate is therefore unique and equals $2\%$ per month.
