A project costs $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and receives $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year, $144\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years and $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years. For which positive annual rates does discounted payback occur by the end of year two?

- $i\geq20\%$
- $0<i\leq32\%$
- $0<i\leq44\%$
- $0<i\leq20\%$

## Explanation

The first receipt alone is worth less than $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and cannot cover the cost. By the end of year two the cumulative discounted amount is $\dfrac{120}{1+i}+\dfrac{144}{(1+i)^2}$, which decreases as $i$ rises. At $20\%$ it is
$$
\frac{120}{1.2}+\frac{144}{1.44}=100+100=200\,\mathrm{EUR},
$$
equal to the cost. Payback therefore occurs by the end of year two exactly when $0<i\leq20\%$. The answer $32\%$ is the nominal gain $\dfrac{64}{200}$.
