A loan has a face amount of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and requires a repayment of $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. A fee of $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ can be withheld at disbursement or paid with the repayment. Which arrangement gives the higher effective annual rate?

- Paying the fee with the repayment, $11.11\%$ versus $11\%$.
- The two arrangements give the same rate, $11\%$.
- Withholding the fee at disbursement, $11.11\%$ versus $11\%$.
- The two arrangements give the same rate, $10\%$.

## Explanation

With the fee withheld, the borrower has $990\,\mathrm{EUR}$ available and repays $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so $1+i=\dfrac{1100}{990}$ and $i\approx11.11\%$. With the fee due at maturity, the borrower receives $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $1110\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving $i=11\%$. Withholding gives the higher rate because the upfront fee has a present value equal to its full amount, while the fee paid a year later is worth less today. The answer $10\%$ ignores the fee.
