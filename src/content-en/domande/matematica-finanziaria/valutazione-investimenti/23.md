A project requires two future payments, one in six months and one in a year. The rate is effective annual and compound discounting uses fractional powers. What exponents apply to the annual discount factor $v$?

- $1$ and $2$.
- $0$ and $1$.
- $6$ and $12$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $1$.

## Explanation

Under compound interest the discount factor for a time $t$ is $v^t$, where $t$ is expressed in the rate's time unit, here a year. Six months correspond to $t=\dfrac{6}{12}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and twelve months to $t=1$. The exponents are therefore $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $1$. The exponents $6$ and $12$ would apply to a monthly discount factor.
