In a conventional project with a positive initial IRR, the final receipt is deferred and all amounts stay the same. How does the IRR change?

- It stays the same.
- It must become negative.
- It increases.
- It decreases.

## Explanation

Let $i^*>0$ be the original IRR. Deferring the final receipt lowers its present value at every positive rate, so at $i^*$ the new NPV is negative. The new cash flow is still conventional and NPV is strictly decreasing. The new NPV therefore vanishes at a rate below $i^*$, so the IRR falls.
