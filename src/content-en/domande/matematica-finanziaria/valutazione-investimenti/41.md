The NPV polynomial is proportional to $(5v-4)(v+1)$ with a nonzero constant of proportionality. What admissible annual IRR follows?

- $25\%$
- $-20\%$
- $-200\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

The polynomial vanishes at $v=\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $v=-1$. An admissible rate requires $v>0$, so discard the root $v=-1$. From $v=\dfrac{4}{5}$ we obtain
$$
i=v^{-1}-1=\frac{5}{4}-1=25\%.
$$
The answer $-20\%$ calculates $v-1$ instead of $v^{-1}-1$, while $-200\%$ comes from the negative root.
