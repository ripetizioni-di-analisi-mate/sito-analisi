In a bisection search for the IRR of a conventional project, NPV is positive at $7\%$ and negative at $11\%$. At the midpoint, $9\%$, it is still positive. Which interval should be retained?

- From $4\%$ to $7\%$.
- From $7\%$ to $11\%$.
- From $7\%$ to $9\%$.
- From $9\%$ to $11\%$.

## Explanation

Bisection retains the interval with opposite NPV signs at its endpoints because the intermediate value theorem guarantees a root there. NPV is positive at $9\%$, as at $7\%$, and negative at $11\%$. The IRR therefore lies between $9\%$ and $11\%$, and $9\%$ replaces $7\%$ as the left endpoint.
