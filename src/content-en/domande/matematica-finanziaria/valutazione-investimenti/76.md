A project receives $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year one and $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year three. At an annual rate of $10\%$, what is the largest cost recoverable by year two?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $231\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Only the first year's receipt is available by year two, since the other arrives in year three. At $10\%$ it is worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, so the cumulative discounted amount in year two is $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The maximum cost recoverable by then is therefore $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts the receipt of $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ as if it arrived in year two.
