A borrower receives $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. In one year the borrower repays $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays a fee of $11\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Calculate the effective annual rate on this cash flow.

- $11.1\%$
- $1.1\%$
- $12.1\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

The repayment and the fee fall on the same date, so combine them into the single outflow $1100+11=1111\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The effective rate sets the borrower's NPV to zero, giving $1000=\dfrac{1111}{1+i}$ and $i=\dfrac{1111}{1000}-1=11.1\%$. The answer $10\%$ ignores the fee, while $1.1\%$ includes only the fee.
