Project $A$ costs $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $385\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Project $B$ costs $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $726\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on the same date. Calculate $\operatorname{NPV}_B-\operatorname{NPV}_A$ at an annual rate of $10\%$.

- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $41\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Find the incremental cash flow $B-A$ by subtracting the amounts on each date. At time $0$ it equals $-600+300=-300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and after one year it equals $726-385=341\,\mathrm{EUR}$. By linearity of discounting,
$$
\operatorname{NPV}_B-\operatorname{NPV}_A=-300+\frac{341}{1.1}=-300+310=10\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $41\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the incremental cash flows without discounting the future receipt.
