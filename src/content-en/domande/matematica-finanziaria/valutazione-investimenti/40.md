A project has cash flows $-100$, $240$ and $-144\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at times $0$, $1$ and $2$. What is its unique IRR?

- $-20\%$
- $20\%$
- $16\%$
- $44\%$

## Explanation

Let $v=(1+i)^{-1}$. The NPV is $-100+240v-144v^2$. The polynomial is the negative of a square,
$$
-100+240v-144v^2=-(12v-10)^2,
$$
so it vanishes only at $v=\dfrac{10}{12}=\dfrac{5}{6}$. From $i=v^{-1}-1$ we obtain $i=\dfrac{6}{5}-1=20\%$. The answer $44\%$ corresponds to $1.2^2-1$, the two-year rate, while $-20\%$ has the wrong sign.
