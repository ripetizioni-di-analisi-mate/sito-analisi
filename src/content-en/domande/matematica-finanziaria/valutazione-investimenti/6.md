Two projects have the same initial cost. Project $A$ pays $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and project $B$ pays $460\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find the annual crossover rate.

- $32.25\%$
- $7.5\%$
- $15\%$
- $-13.04\%$

## Explanation

The projects have the same initial cost, so it cancels in the difference between their NPVs. Let $q=1+i$. Equal NPVs require
$$
\frac{400}{q}=\frac{460}{q^2},
$$
which gives $q=\dfrac{460}{400}=1.15$ and $i=15\%$. The answer $7.5\%$ divides the return of $15\%$ by two, while $32.25\%$ is $1.15^2-1$ and confuses the annual rate with the two-year return.
