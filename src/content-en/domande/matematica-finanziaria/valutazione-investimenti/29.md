An equation in $v=(1+i)^{-1}$ has only two real roots, $0$ and $-2$. How many admissible IRRs do they give?

- Two.
- One, from $v=-2$.
- Zero.
- One, from $v=0$.

## Explanation

Every admissible rate satisfies $i>-1$, so $1+i>0$ and $v=(1+i)^{-1}$ is positive and finite. The root $v=-2$ is negative and corresponds to no rate. The root $v=0$ would require $(1+i)^{-1}=0$, which no real number satisfies. The equation therefore yields no admissible IRR.
