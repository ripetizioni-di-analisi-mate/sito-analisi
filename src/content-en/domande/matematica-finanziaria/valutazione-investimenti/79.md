A project costs $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and pays $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in six months. The effective annual rate is $21\%$, and fractional periods use exponential compounding. What is its NPV?

- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-38.18\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under exponential compounding six months equal $t=\dfrac{1}{2}$ of a year, giving the discount factor $1.21^{-\frac{1}{2}}=\dfrac{1}{1.1}$. The receipt is worth $\dfrac{220}{1.1}=200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, so NPV is $200-220=-20\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $-38.18\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for a full year by dividing by $1.21$.
