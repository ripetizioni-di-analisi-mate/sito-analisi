The effective rate on a loan, calculated using six months as the time unit, is $3\%$. What is the equivalent effective annual rate?

- $0.09\%$
- $6.09\%$
- $6\%$
- $3\%$

## Explanation

A year contains two six-month periods, so the equivalent annual factor is the square of the six-month factor, $1.03^2=1.0609$. The effective annual rate is therefore $1.0609-1=6.09\%$. The answer $6\%$ doubles the six-month rate and ignores the interest earned in the second period on the first period's interest.
