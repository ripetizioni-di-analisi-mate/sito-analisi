A project's NPV at the annual valuation rate is zero. What does this mean?

- The valuation rate is zero.
- All cash flows occur today.
- The nominal sums of receipts and payments are equal.
- The present values of receipts and payments are equal.

## Explanation

NPV is the sum of all cash flows discounted to time $0$, or the present value of receipts minus the present value of payments. Zero NPV therefore means that the present values are equal at the valuation rate. The nominal sums can differ because cash flows on different dates have different discount factors. The valuation rate can be any value $i>-1$.
