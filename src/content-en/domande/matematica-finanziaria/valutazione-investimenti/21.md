At time $2$ a project's cash flow statement shows a receipt of $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a payment of $125\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What net cash flow should be discounted for that date?

- $295\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-125\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $545\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Amounts due on the same date have the same discount factor $(1+i)^{-2}$. Combine them before discounting, taking the payment with a negative sign. The net cash flow at time $2$ is $420-125=295\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $545\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the payment as though it were a receipt, while $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores it.
