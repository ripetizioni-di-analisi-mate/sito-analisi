Two alternative machines provide the same service and last two and four years, respectively. The service is required for four years. Which information affects the cash flows of the first alternative?

- The currency of the cash flows, which is the same.
- The terms for renewal or replacement service from year three onward.
- Only the IRR of the four-year machine.
- The sign of the initial cost of the four-year machine.

## Explanation

Compare both choices over the same four-year horizon. The two-year machine covers only the first two years, so specify how the service will be provided in years $3$ and $4$, through renewal or a replacement. The costs and receipts of that choice belong in the first alternative's cash flows and therefore in its NPV. The four-year machine's IRR does not determine those cash flows.
