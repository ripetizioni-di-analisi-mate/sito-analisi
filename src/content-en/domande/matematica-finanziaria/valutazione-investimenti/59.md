A loan has one repayment. Its amount and maturity stay fixed, but the fee withheld at disbursement increases and the net proceeds remain positive. How does the effective borrowing cost change?

- It must become zero.
- It decreases.
- It increases.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

With a single repayment $R$ after $T$ periods and net proceeds $C-S$, the effective rate satisfies
$$
(1+i)^T=\frac{R}{C-S}.
$$
A larger fee $S$ reduces the net proceeds, increasing the ratio $\dfrac{R}{C-S}$. Since $1+i$ is the $T$-th root of that ratio and the root is increasing in its argument, the effective cost rises.
