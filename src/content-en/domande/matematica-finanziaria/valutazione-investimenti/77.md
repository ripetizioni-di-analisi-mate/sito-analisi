At an annual rate of $7\%$, a conventional project's cumulative discounted receipts exactly recover its cost on the final date. What follows?

- The IRR is positive and exceeds $7\%$.
- The IRR is zero.
- The IRR equals $7\%$.
- The project has no IRR.

## Explanation

On the final date the cumulative discounted amount includes every project receipt. Exact recovery means that this amount equals the cost, so NPV at $7\%$ is zero. The project is conventional, so the uniqueness theorem gives NPV a single zero. Its IRR is therefore $7\%$. A higher rate cannot be its IRR because NPV is already zero at the given rate.
