An investment of $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today pays $216\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. What is its NPV at an annual rate of $20\%$?

- $-36\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $36\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The receipt is due in two years, so use the discount factor $1.2^2=1.44$. Its present value is $\dfrac{216}{1.44}=150\,\mathrm{EUR}$, hence
$$
\operatorname{NPV}(0.20)=-180+150=-30\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for only one year, since $\dfrac{216}{1.2}=180$. The answer $36\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the nominal gain.
