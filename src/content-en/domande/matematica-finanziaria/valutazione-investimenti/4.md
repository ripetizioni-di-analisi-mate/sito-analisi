A project costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, receives $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and pays out $156\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find all its annual IRRs.

- $20\%$ and $30\%$.
- $20\%$ and $50\%$.
- Only $25\%$.
- No IRR.

## Explanation

Let $v=(1+i)^{-1}$. The NPV is $-100+250v-156v^2$. The polynomial factors as
$$
-100+250v-156v^2=-(12v-10)(13v-10).
$$
The roots are $v=\dfrac{5}{6}$ and $v=\dfrac{10}{13}$, both positive. Since $i=v^{-1}-1$, the corresponding rates are $\dfrac{6}{5}-1=20\%$ and $\dfrac{13}{10}-1=30\%$. The cash flow changes sign twice, so uniqueness of the IRR is not guaranteed.
