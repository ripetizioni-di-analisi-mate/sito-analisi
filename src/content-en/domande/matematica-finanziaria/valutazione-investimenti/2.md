An investment costs $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and produces a single receipt of $605\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Calculate the annual IRR.

- $10\%$
- $10.5\%$
- $21\%$
- $-10\%$

## Explanation

With a single receipt after two years, the IRR satisfies $500(1+i^*)^2=605$. The growth factor over the two years is $\dfrac{605}{500}=1.21$, so the annual factor is $\sqrt{1.21}=1.1$ and the IRR is $10\%$. The answer $21\%$ is the total two-year return, while $10.5\%$ divides that return by two as under simple interest.
