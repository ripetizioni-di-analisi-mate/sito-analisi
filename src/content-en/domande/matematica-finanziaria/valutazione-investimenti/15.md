A machine costs $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$. In one year it generates revenue of $495\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and an expense of $55\,\mathrm{EUR}$. It is sold in two years for $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is its NPV at $10\%$?

- $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $171\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first year's revenue and expense fall on the same date, so discount the net cash flow $495-55=440\,\mathrm{EUR}$, which is worth $\dfrac{440}{1.1}=400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. The sale proceeds are worth $\dfrac{121}{1.21}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Therefore
$$
\operatorname{NPV}(0.10)=-450+400+100=50\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the expense of $55\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
