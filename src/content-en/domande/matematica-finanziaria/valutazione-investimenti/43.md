Two positive roots of the NPV polynomial are $v_1=0.8$ and $v_2=0.9$. Which gives the higher IRR?

- The root $v_1$.
- The root $v_2$.
- They give the same rate.
- Neither root is admissible.

## Explanation

The rate is given by $i=v^{-1}-1$. The root $v_1=0.8$ gives $i=1.25-1=25\%$, while $v_2=0.9$ gives $i=\dfrac{1}{0.9}-1\approx11.1\%$. Since $v^{-1}-1$ is decreasing for $v>0$, the smaller root gives the higher rate. Both roots are positive and admissible, and they give different rates.
