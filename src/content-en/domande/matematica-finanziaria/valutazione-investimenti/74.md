Projects $A$ and $B$ have the same initial cost and the same discounted payback. Their cash flows coincide through the payback date. After that date $B$ receives an additional payment, the only difference between them. Which project has the higher NPV?

- It depends on the signs of the NPVs.
- The NPVs are equal.
- Project $A$.
- Project $B$.

## Explanation

The cash flows of $A$ and $B$ differ only by the additional receipt for $B$ after payback. The incremental NPV $\operatorname{NPV}_B-\operatorname{NPV}_A$ therefore equals the positive present value of that receipt. Project $B$ consequently has the higher NPV, whatever the signs of the two NPVs. Their equal payback periods do not distinguish the projects.
