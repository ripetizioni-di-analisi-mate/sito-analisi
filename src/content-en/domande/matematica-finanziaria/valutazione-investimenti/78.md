An immediate cost reduction of $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is available for a project costing $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$ that pays $121$ and $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one and two years. What is the outcome at $10\%$?

- The new NPV is zero.
- The new NPV is $55\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- The new NPV is $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- The new NPV is $-40\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

## Explanation

At $10\%$ the receipts are worth $\dfrac{121}{1.1}=110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $\dfrac{484}{1.21}=400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, for a total of $510\,\mathrm{EUR}$. With the original cost, NPV is $510-550=-40\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The reduction of $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is a cash flow at time $0$ and increases NPV by its full amount, giving a new NPV of $0$. The answer $-40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the NPV before the reduction.
