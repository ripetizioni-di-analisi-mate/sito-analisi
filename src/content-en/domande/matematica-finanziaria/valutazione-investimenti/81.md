Projects have the same initial cost. Project $A$ receives $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $B$ receives $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. What is the annual crossover rate in the domain $i>-1$?

- $20\%$
- $-20\%$
- $-36\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The common initial cost cancels. Let $q=1+i$. Equal NPVs require $\dfrac{200}{q}=\dfrac{160}{q^2}$, giving $q=\dfrac{160}{200}=0.8$ and $i=-20\%$. Since $-20\%>-100\%$, this rate belongs to the domain $i>-1$. The answer $25\%$ uses the inverted ratio $\dfrac{200}{160}$, while $-36\%$ corresponds to $0.8^2-1$.
