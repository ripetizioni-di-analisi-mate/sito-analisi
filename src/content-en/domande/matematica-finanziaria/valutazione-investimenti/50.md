Both projects are conventional, and the first has a higher IRR. What further information is needed to choose between them by NPV?

- Only the date of the first receipt.
- The valuation rate and the projects' cash flows, or their NPVs at that rate.
- Only the number of sign changes.
- Only the higher IRR.

## Explanation

Ranking the projects requires their NPVs at the valuation rate. A higher IRR need not give a higher NPV. At $10\%$, for example, a project costing $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with an IRR of $30\%$ has NPV $\dfrac{200}{11}\,\mathrm{EUR}$, while a project costing $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with an IRR of $20\%$ has NPV $\dfrac{500}{11}\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The comparison therefore needs the valuation rate and the cash flows, or the NPVs at that rate.
