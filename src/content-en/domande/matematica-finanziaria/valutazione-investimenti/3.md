A project costs $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and pays $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $181.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. What is its unique annual IRR?

- $16.6\%$
- $0\%$
- $10\%$
- $5\%$

## Explanation

At $10\%$ the two receipts are worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $\dfrac{181.50}{1.21}=150\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Their sum is $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$, equal to the cost, so $\operatorname{NPV}(10\%)=0$. The initial outlay is followed by positive receipts, so NPV is strictly decreasing and $10\%$ is the unique IRR. The answer $16.6\%$ is the undiscounted nominal gain $\dfrac{41.5}{250}$.
