Reverse the sign of every cash flow to switch from the borrower's perspective to the lender's, keeping all dates and amounts. How do the rates that make NPV zero change?

- They all become the reciprocals of the original rates.
- They all change sign.
- They stay the same.
- They all become zero.

## Explanation

Reversing every cash flow sign while keeping the dates multiplies every NPV term by $-1$. At every rate the lender's NPV is therefore the negative of the borrower's. A function and its negative have the same zeros, so the rates that make NPV zero stay the same. The borrower's effective rate thus equals the lender's yield.
