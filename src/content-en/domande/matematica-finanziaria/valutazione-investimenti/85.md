A project costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, receives $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and pays $13.31\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years. What is the sign of NPV at $10\%$?

- Positive.
- The sign depends on the accounting year-end.
- Zero.
- Negative.

## Explanation

At $10\%$ the year-one receipt is worth $\dfrac{121}{1.1}=110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and the year-three payment is worth $\dfrac{13.31}{1.331}=10\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Therefore
$$
\operatorname{NPV}(0.10)=-100+110-10=0
$$
and NPV is zero. The final payment enters with a negative sign. Omitting it would give a positive NPV of $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
