A project can be started today or in one year, and the two choices are mutually exclusive. In both cases the NPV computed at the starting date is $110$ euros, at an annual rate of $10\%$. By how much, in today's euros, does the NPV of starting today exceed that of the deferred start?

- $0$ euros
- $11$ euros
- $10$ euros
- $21$ euros

## Explanation

Two NPVs can be compared only if they refer to the same date. The NPV of the deferred start is computed at time $1$, so today it is worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100$ euros. The NPV of starting today already refers to today and equals $110$ euros. Therefore the advantage of starting today is $110-100=10$ euros. The answer of $0$ euros compares NPVs at different dates, while $11$ euros is the difference measured at time $1$.
