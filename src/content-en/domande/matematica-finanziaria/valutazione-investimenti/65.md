A borrower receives positive net proceeds today and subsequently makes only payments, at least one of which is nonzero. How many effective rates does this model allow for $i>-1$?

- One per payment.
- A number that depends on the nominal rate.
- One.
- Zero.

## Explanation

The borrower's positive initial inflow is followed only by payments. Reversing all signs gives an initial outlay followed by nonnegative receipts, at least one of them positive, with the same NPV zeros. The uniqueness theorem applies to this cash flow, so exactly one rate $i>-1$ makes NPV zero, regardless of the number of payments.
