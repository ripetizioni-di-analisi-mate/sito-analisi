A borrower receives net proceeds of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and repays the loan in two equal annual installments of $R$ at the ends of years one and two. The effective rate is $20\%$ per year. Find $R$ to the nearest cent.

- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $378.79\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $654.55\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The effective rate makes the borrower's NPV zero, so the amount received equals the present value of the installments,
$$
1000=R\left(\frac{1}{1.2}+\frac{1}{1.44}\right)=\frac{55}{36}R.
$$
Hence $R=\dfrac{36000}{55}\approx654.55\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$ halves the accumulated amount $1000\cdot1.44$, while $378.79\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accumulates the installments instead of discounting them and $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores interest.
