A loan charges an origination fee at disbursement and a collection fee with the first installment. How do the fees enter the borrower's cash flow?

- Both increase the first outflow.
- The first reduces the net proceeds and the second increases the first outflow.
- Both increase the net proceeds.
- Both reduce the net proceeds.

## Explanation

Each fee enters the borrower's cash flow when it is paid. The origination fee is paid at disbursement and reduces the funds received, hence the net proceeds. The collection fee is paid with the first installment and increases that outflow. The fees have different payment dates and therefore different discount factors.
