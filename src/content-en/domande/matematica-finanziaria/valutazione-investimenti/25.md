An investment costs $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and returns a single payment of $1464.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The annual IRR is $10\%$. After how many years is the payment received?

- $4$ years
- $3$ years
- $5$ years
- $4.641$ years

## Explanation

The annual IRR of $10\%$ sets NPV to zero, so $1000\cdot1.1^T=1464.10$ and $1.1^T=1.4641$. Since $1.1^2=1.21$ and $1.21^2=1.4641$, we get $1.1^4=1.4641$ and hence $T=4$ years. The answer of $4.641$ years divides the total return of $46.41\%$ by the annual rate as under simple interest.
