A project costs $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find the discounted payback period at an annual rate of $10\%$.

- The initial cost is never recovered.
- $2$ years.
- $1$ year.
- $3$ years.

## Explanation

At $10\%$ each receipt is worth $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today because $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100$ and $\dfrac{121}{1.21}=100$. The cumulative present value is $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ after one year, below the cost, and $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ after two years. Equality with the cost counts as recovery, so the discounted payback period is $2$ years. The first year's cumulative receipt covers only half the cost.
