A bond has an effective semiannual yield of $4\%$. What is the equivalent effective annual yield?

- $8\%$
- $4.08\%$
- $8.16\%$
- $4\%$

## Explanation

Compound for two half-years at $4\%$ to find the equivalent effective annual yield. The annual factor is
$$
1.04^2=1.0816,
$$
so the effective annual yield is $8.16\%$. Doubling the semiannual rate gives $8\%$, the nominal annual rate compounded semiannually. It omits the interest earned in the second half-year on the first half-year's interest.
