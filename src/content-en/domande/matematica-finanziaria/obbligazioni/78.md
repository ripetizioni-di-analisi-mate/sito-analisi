A bond is held to maturity and all intermediate coupons are reinvested at the initial yield to maturity. Which effective annual return produces the resulting terminal wealth?

- The initial yield to maturity.
- The market rate observed on the redemption date.
- The current yield alone.
- The nominal coupon rate regardless of the price paid.

## Explanation

The pricing equation equates the price to the cash flows discounted at the yield to maturity. Multiply both sides by $(1+i)^T$, where $T$ is the maturity. The left-hand side is the price accumulated at that yield, and the right-hand side is the redemption payment plus all coupons accumulated at the same yield. Terminal wealth therefore equals the price accumulated at the initial yield to maturity, so the realized annual return equals that yield.
