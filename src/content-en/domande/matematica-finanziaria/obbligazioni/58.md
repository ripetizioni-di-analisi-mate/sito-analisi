At settlement one quarter of the coupon period has elapsed. Under the same time convention as for accrued interest, how long until the second remaining coupon?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a period.
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$ periods.
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a period.
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$ periods.

## Explanation

At settlement $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the period has elapsed, so the first remaining coupon is $1-\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a period away. Subsequent coupons are one period apart, so the second is due in
$$
t_2=2-\frac{1}{4}=\frac{7}{4}\ \text{periods}.
$$
The value $\dfrac{9}{4}$ adds the elapsed fraction instead of subtracting it, while $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is the time to the first remaining coupon.
