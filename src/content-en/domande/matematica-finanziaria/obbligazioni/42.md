A bond with face value $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has a nominal annual coupon rate of $5\%$ and pays four equal coupons per year. What is the amount of each coupon?

- $24\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The nominal annual coupon rate of $5\%$ gives $2400\cdot0.05=120$ euros of coupon income per year. Since the four coupons are equal, each is
$$
C=\frac{2400\cdot0.05}{4}=30\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The annual amount is $120$ euros. Dividing by two as for semiannual coupons gives $60$ euros, and $24$ euros is $1\%$ of face value.
