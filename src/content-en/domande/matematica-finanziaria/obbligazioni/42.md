A bond with face value $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ pays semiannual coupons at a nominal annual rate of $6\%$, computed on the principal not yet repaid. After six months, together with the first coupon, it repays $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal. What is the sum of the two coupons paid in the first year?

- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $48\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $36\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $448\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The coupon rate for one half-year is half of $6\%$, that is $3\%$. The first coupon is computed on the whole face value, so it equals $0.03\cdot1000=30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. After the partial repayment the outstanding principal is $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$, therefore the second coupon equals $0.03\cdot600=18\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The coupons of the first year thus add up to $30+18=48\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the partial repayment, $36\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies $6\%$ to the remaining principal only and $448\,\mathrm{EUR}$ also includes the principal repaid.
