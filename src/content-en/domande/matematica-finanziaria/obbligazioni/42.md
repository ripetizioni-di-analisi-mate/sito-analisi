A bond pays semiannual coupons at a $6\%$ nominal annual coupon rate on the principal outstanding. At midyear it pays the coupon and repays €400 of the original €1000 principal. How many euros of coupons, excluding principal, are paid in year one?

- €60
- €48
- €36
- €448

## Explanation

The semiannual coupon rate is $3\%$. The first coupon is €30 on €1000. After the repayment, €600 remains outstanding, so the second coupon is €18. The two coupons total €48.
