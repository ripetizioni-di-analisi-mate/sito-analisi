A portfolio holds three units of a bond priced at $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and four units of a bond priced at $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the market-value weight of the first bond?

- $42.86\%$
- $60\%$
- $66.67\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

A bond's market-value weight is its share of portfolio value. The first holding is worth $3\cdot120=360$ euros and the second $4\cdot60=240$ euros, so portfolio value is $600$ euros and
$$
w_1=\frac{360}{600}=60\%.
$$
The value $42.86\%$ is the share by number of units, $\dfrac{3}{7}$, which ignores prices. The value $66.67\%$ uses only the unit prices, $\dfrac{120}{180}$.
