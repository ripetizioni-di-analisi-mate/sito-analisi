A bond with face value $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has a clean price quote of $98.6$ per $100$ of face value. Its coupon is $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Under the given day count, one third of the period has elapsed. Calculate the dirty price in euros using linear accrual.

- $2032\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1952\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1972\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1992\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The quote of $98.6$ applies per $100$ euros of face value. The bond contains $20$ such units, giving a clean price of $98.6\cdot20=1972$ euros. Accrued interest is the elapsed fraction of the coupon, or $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot60=20$ euros. The dirty price is therefore $1972+20=1992$ euros. Subtracting accrued interest gives $1952$, whereas adding the whole coupon gives $2032$.
