A bond is bought for $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$. One year later the holder receives a coupon of $45\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and sells the bond at a dirty price of $990\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the realized return?

- $11.5\%$
- $10\%$
- $5\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

The realized return is $h=\dfrac{P_H+W_H-P_0}{P_0}$. The sale price exceeds the purchase price by $990-900=90$ euros and the coupon adds $45$ euros, so
$$
h=\frac{990+45-900}{900}=\frac{135}{900}=15\%.
$$
The value $10\%$ counts only the price change, while $5\%$ counts only the coupon.
