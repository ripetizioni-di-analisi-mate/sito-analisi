A bond costs €100. In one year its owner will receive a certain €6 coupon and immediately sell the bond. The ex-coupon sale price will be €112 with probability $p$ or €82 with probability $1-p$. For which $p$ is the expected one-year return zero?

- $60\%$
- $20\%$
- $50\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

Including the coupon, the final proceeds in the two states are €118 and €88. Expected return is zero when expected proceeds equal the original cost. Solving $118p+88(1-p)=100$ gives $30p=12$ and $p=0.40$.
