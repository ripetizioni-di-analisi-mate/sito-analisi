A zero-coupon bond costs $1250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. What is its effective yield?

- $4.17\%$
- $4\%$
- $-4\%$
- $-4.17\%$

## Explanation

The effective yield on a one-year zero-coupon bond is $y=\dfrac{M}{P}-1$. The receipt of $1200$ euros is less than the initial outlay of $1250$ euros, so the yield is negative:
$$
y=\frac{1200}{1250}-1=-\frac{50}{1250}=-4\%.
$$
Dividing the loss by the final receipt instead of the purchase price gives $-4.17\%$. The positive answers omit the sign of the loss.
