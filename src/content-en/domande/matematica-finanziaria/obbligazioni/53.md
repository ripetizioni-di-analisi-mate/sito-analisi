A bond's dirty price is $1016\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and its coupon is $64\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Settlement occurs one quarter of the way through the period. Calculate the clean price under linear accrual.

- $1032\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $952\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1016\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Accrued interest is the elapsed fraction of the coupon, or $A=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot64=16$ euros. The dirty price equals the clean price plus accrued interest. Subtracting accrued interest gives the clean price:
$$
P_{\mathrm{s}}=1016-16=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1032$ adds accrued interest instead of subtracting it, while $952$ subtracts the entire coupon.
