A bond with face value $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ redeems at par and pays annual coupons of $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Immediately after a coupon payment three coupons remain, starting in one year. At an effective annual yield of $6\%$, how far below par is the price?

- $28.30\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $80.19\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $25.19\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

For a bond redeemed at face value and valued immediately after a coupon payment, $P-N=(C-iN)a_{\overline n|i}$ holds. Here $C-iN=30-60=-30$ euros and
$$
a_{\overline 3|0.06}=\frac{1-1.06^{-3}}{0.06}\approx2.6730,
$$
so $P-N\approx-30\cdot2.6730=-80.19$ euros and the price is $80.19$ euros below par. The value $90$ adds the three differences without discounting, $28.30$ discounts a single difference for one year, and $25.19$ discounts one for three years.
