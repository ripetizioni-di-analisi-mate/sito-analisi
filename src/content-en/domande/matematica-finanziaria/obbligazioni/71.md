You buy a bond for €1000. Six months later you receive a €40 coupon and reinvest it until you sell the bond for €980 another six months later. There are no other cash flows. Your final wealth is €1022. What half-year return did you earn on the reinvested coupon?

- $2.2\%$
- $2\%$
- $5\%$
- $4\%$

## Explanation

Of the final €1022, €980 came from the sale. The coupon therefore grew to €42. The half-year return on the reinvested amount was $\dfrac{42-40}{40}=5\%$.
