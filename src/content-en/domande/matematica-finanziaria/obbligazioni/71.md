A bond is bought for $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. After six months it pays a coupon of $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$, which is reinvested until the bond is sold six months later for $980\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At the sale the total wealth is $1022\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What was the half-year return on the reinvested coupon?

- $2.2\%$
- $2\%$
- $5\%$
- $4\%$

## Explanation

The final wealth consists of the sale price and the value of the reinvested coupon, so the reinvested coupon is worth $1022-980=42\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The return on the reinvestment compares this value with the coupon received, therefore it is $\dfrac{42-40}{40}=5\%$ for the half-year. The value $2.2\%$ is the return on the whole investment, $\dfrac{1022-1000}{1000}$, while $4\%$ is the ratio of the coupon to the purchase price.
