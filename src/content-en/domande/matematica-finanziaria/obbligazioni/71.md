A bond is bought for $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and sold for $970\,\mathrm{EUR}$ one year later, immediately after the holder receives a coupon of $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the realized return?

- $-6\%$
- $3\%$
- $0\%$
- $-3\%$

## Explanation

The realized return compares the initial outlay with wealth at sale. Since the sale immediately follows the coupon payment, terminal wealth includes both the sale proceeds and coupon. Hence
$$
h=\frac{970+30-1000}{1000}=0.
$$
The coupon exactly offsets the price loss of $30$ euros. The value $-3\%$ counts only the price change, while $3\%$ counts only the coupon.
