A bond costing $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ pays $792\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in principal and a coupon of $48\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. By how many percentage points does its current yield exceed its yield to maturity?

- $5$ percentage points
- $6$ percentage points
- $0$ percentage points
- $1$ percentage point

## Explanation

Current yield is $\dfrac{48}{800}=6\%$ because it divides coupon income alone by the price. Yield to maturity includes the full receipt of $48+792=840$ euros, so it equals $\dfrac{840}{800}-1=5\%$. The redemption payment of $792$ euros is less than the price, and the capital loss therefore brings yield to maturity below current yield. The difference is $6\%-5\%$, or one percentage point.
