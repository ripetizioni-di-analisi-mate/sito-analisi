A zero-coupon bond with face value $2500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is quoted at $92$ per $100$ of face value. What is the initial outlay?

- $2250\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $92\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2300\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A quote of $92$ per $100$ means that each $100$ euros of face value costs $92$ euros. A face value of $2500$ euros comprises $25$ units of $100$ euros, so the outlay is
$$
\frac{92}{100}\cdot2500=2300\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The face value received at maturity is $2500$ euros, while $92$ euros is the price per single unit of $100$ euros of face value.
