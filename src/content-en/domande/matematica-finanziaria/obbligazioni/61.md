Three quarters of the coupon period have elapsed, giving accrued interest equal to three quarters of the coupon. Under the same convention, over what fraction of a period should the next coupon be discounted?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a period.
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$ periods.
- One full period.
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a period.

## Explanation

Under a common convention, accrual and discounting refer to two parts of the coupon period. Accrued interest covers the elapsed $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of the period, while discounting the next coupon covers the remaining fraction. The coupon is therefore discounted over
$$
1-\frac{3}{4}=\frac{1}{4}\ \text{per period}.
$$
The value $\dfrac{3}{4}$ confuses the remaining and elapsed fractions.
