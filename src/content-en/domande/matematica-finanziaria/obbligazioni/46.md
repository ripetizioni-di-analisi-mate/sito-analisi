A semiannual coupon bond with face value $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has a nominal annual coupon rate of $4\%$. It repays $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in six months. The effective annual yield is $4.04\%$. What is its price immediately after the current coupon payment?

- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $960.78\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $980.39\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1020\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The semiannual coupon is $\dfrac{1000\cdot0.04}{2}=20$ euros, payable in six months with the principal. The half-year yield $i$ satisfies $(1+i)^2=1.0404$, so $1+i=1.02$ and
$$
P=\frac{1020}{1.02}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $980.39$ omits the coupon. The bond trades at par because the coupon rate and yield per half-year both equal $2\%$.
