A quarterly coupon bond matures one and a half years after settlement. Which exponent is used in its compounding factor when the yield is quoted per quarter?

- The exponent is $18$.
- The exponent is $1.5$.
- The exponent is $6$.
- The exponent is $4$.

## Explanation

The yield applies to one quarter, so the exponent of $(1+i)^t$ counts the quarters from settlement to redemption. One and a half years equals eighteen months, and each quarter lasts three months. Hence the exponent is $\dfrac{18}{3}=6$. The exponent $1.5$ expresses the term in years and applies to an annual yield, while $18$ counts months.
