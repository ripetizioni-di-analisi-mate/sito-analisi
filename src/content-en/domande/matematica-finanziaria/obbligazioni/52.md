The next coupon is $72\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and $60$ days of a $180$-day period have elapsed. What is accrued interest under linear accrual using actual days?

- $24\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $36\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $72\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $48\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under linear accrual, multiply the coupon by the fraction of the period elapsed. With an actual day count, this fraction is $\dfrac{60}{180}=\dfrac{1}{3}$, so
$$
A=72\cdot\frac{1}{3}=24\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $48$ euros uses the remaining fraction $\dfrac{2}{3}$, which is used to discount the coupon rather than calculate accrued interest. The value $36$ euros is half the coupon.
