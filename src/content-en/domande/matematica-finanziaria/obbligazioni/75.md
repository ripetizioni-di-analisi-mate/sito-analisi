A bond with no intermediate payments is bought for $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and sold for $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ after two years. What effective annual return was realized?

- $10.5\%$
- $21\%$
- $10\%$
- $11\%$

## Explanation

With no intermediate payments, the sale-to-purchase price ratio is $\dfrac{1210}{1000}=1.21$, the two-year accumulation factor. The equivalent effective annual return $y$ satisfies $(1+y)^2=1.21$, so $1+y=1.1$ and $y=10\%$. The value $21\%$ is the total two-year return. Dividing that return by two gives $10.5\%$ and uses simple interest.
