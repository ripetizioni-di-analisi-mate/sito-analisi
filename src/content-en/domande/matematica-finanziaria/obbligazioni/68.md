A bond has fixed, positive future cash flows. The required annual yield rises from $4\%$ to $6\%$. What happens to the theoretical price on the same valuation date?

- It stays the same.
- It decreases only if the redemption payment exceeds par.
- It decreases.
- It increases.

## Explanation

The theoretical price is $P(i)=\sum_k F_k(1+i)^{-t_k}$, with cash flows $F_k>0$ and times $t_k>0$. As the yield rises from $4\%$ to $6\%$, each factor $(1+i)^{t_k}$ increases, so each present value $F_k(1+i)^{-t_k}$ decreases. The theoretical price therefore falls regardless of the redemption amount. This is the strict decrease of price as a function of yield.
