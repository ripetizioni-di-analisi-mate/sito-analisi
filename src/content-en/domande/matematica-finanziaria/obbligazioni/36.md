A bond with a yield to maturity of $10\%$ costs $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays a single coupon of $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with principal in one year. What is its redemption payment?

- $950\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $960\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $940\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $990\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At a one-year yield to maturity of $10\%$, the price accumulated for one year equals the total receipt at maturity. The coupon and principal therefore total $900\cdot1.1=990$ euros. Subtracting the coupon of $40$ euros gives the principal repayment
$$
M=990-40=950\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The amount $990$ euros is the total receipt, including the coupon.
