A bond costs $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. One year later it is sold at a clean price of $980\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with accrued interest of $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$. A coupon of $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ was received during the year and kept in cash. What is the realized return?

- $7\%$
- $3\%$
- $2\%$
- $5\%$

## Explanation

The seller receives the dirty price, equal to the clean price plus accrued interest, or $980+20=1000$ euros. The coupon of $50$ euros received during the year was held in cash and enters terminal wealth at its full amount. Hence
$$
h=\frac{1000+50-1000}{1000}=5\%.
$$
The value $3\%$ uses the clean price instead of the dirty price. The value $7\%$ counts accrued interest twice.
