Two investors buy the same bond with fixed positive cash flows on the same date at different prices. Who obtains the higher yield to maturity?

- The investor who paid a price closer to face value.
- The investor who paid the higher price.
- The investor who paid the lower price.
- Both obtain the same yield to maturity.

## Explanation

The yield to maturity solves $P_{\mathrm{obs}}=\sum_k F_k(1+i)^{-t_k}$. The right-hand side is strictly decreasing in $i$ because the cash flows are positive. Since the investors receive the same cash flows on the same dates, a lower price corresponds to a higher yield. The buyer who paid less therefore earns the higher yield to maturity. The distance from face value does not enter the equation.
