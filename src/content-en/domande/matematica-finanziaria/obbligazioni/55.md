A bond's clean price quote is $97$ per $100$ of face value. The position has a face value of $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and total accrued interest of $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the dirty price per $100$ of face value?

- $98$
- $96$
- $101$
- $97$

## Explanation

The face value of $4000$ euros comprises $40$ units of $100$ euros. Total accrued interest of $40$ euros therefore corresponds to $\dfrac{40}{40}=1$ euros per $100$ of face value. The dirty price per hundred is the clean price plus accrued interest, both per hundred, giving $97+1=98$. The value $96$ subtracts accrued interest, while $101$ divides it by $10$ as though the face value of the position were $1000$ euros.
