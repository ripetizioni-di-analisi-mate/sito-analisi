A bond costs $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and redeems for $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. What coupon paid on the same date gives a yield to maturity of $4\%$?

- $92\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $42\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A yield to maturity of $4\%$ requires a total receipt in one year equal to the price accumulated for one year, or $1050\cdot1.04=1092$ euros. It includes principal of $1000$ euros, so the coupon is
$$
C=1092-1000=92\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $40$ euros applies $4\%$ to the redemption payment. The value $42$ euros applies $4\%$ to the price and omits the capital loss of $50$ euros between purchase and redemption.
