A bond makes its final payment of $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in half a period. The accumulation factor for the full period is $1.21$. What is its dirty price today?

- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $909.09\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The dirty price is the present value of the only remaining payment, due in half a period. Under the exponential convention the half-period factor is $1.21^{\frac{1}{2}}=1.1$, so
$$
P_{\mathrm{tq}}=\frac{1100}{1.1}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $909.09$ discounts for a full period, $1100$ ignores discounting, and $1210$ compounds instead of discounting.
