A bond with at least one positive intermediate coupon is held to maturity. All intermediate coupons are reinvested at an effective annual rate below the initial yield to maturity. Which comparison is correct?

- The comparison depends on a resale price before maturity.
- The two yields coincide for every reinvestment rate.
- The realized annual return is below the initial yield to maturity.
- The realized annual return exceeds the initial yield to maturity.

## Explanation

Terminal wealth equals the redemption payment plus coupons accumulated to maturity at the reinvestment rate. Reinvestment at the yield to maturity would give a realized return equal to that yield. Here at least one positive coupon earns interest for a positive length of time at a lower rate, so its accumulated value is lower. Terminal wealth is therefore lower, and the realized annual return is below the initial yield to maturity.
