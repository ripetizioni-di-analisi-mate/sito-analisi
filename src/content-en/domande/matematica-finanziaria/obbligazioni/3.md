A bond pays $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Calculate its price at an effective annual yield of $5\%$.

- $952.38\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The price is the sum of the present values of the two cash flows. The $1050$ euros due on the second date include the final coupon and redemption payment, so they are discounted together for two years:
$$
P=\frac{50}{1.05}+\frac{1050}{1.05^2}\approx47.62+952.38=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The amount $952.38$ includes only the final cash flow and omits the first coupon. The amount $1100$ is the undiscounted total. The bond trades at par because its coupon rate of $5\%$ equals its yield.
