A bond will repay its face value of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pay a coupon of $71\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. At an effective annual yield of $5\%$, by how much does the price exceed face value?

- $71\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $21\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The coupon and principal are received together in one year, so the price is $P=\dfrac{1071}{1.05}=1020$ euros and exceeds face value by $20$ euros. The same result follows from $P-N=(C-iN)v$, with $C-iN=71-50=21$ euros and $v=\dfrac{1}{1.05}$. The amount $21$ euros is the undiscounted difference, while $71$ euros confuses the premium with the coupon.
