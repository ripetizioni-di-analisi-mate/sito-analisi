Two bonds with equal prices pay either €100 in one year and €110 in two years, or nothing in one year and €220 in two years. An analyst uses the same effective annual yield to calculate both prices. Which yield is consistent with that equality?

- $10\%$
- $0\%$
- $5\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

At year two the first bond's cash flows are worth $100(1+i)+110$ and the second bond's are worth €220. Equal prices today require $100(1+i)=110$, giving $i=10\%$.
