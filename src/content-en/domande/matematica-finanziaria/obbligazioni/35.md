Bond A pays $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Bond B pays only $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. At what effective annual yield, the same for both bonds, are their prices equal?

- $10\%$
- $0\%$
- $5\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

Under compound interest the prices are equal if the values of the cash flows are equal at a common date, and the second year is a convenient choice. On that date bond A is worth $100(1+i)+110$ and bond B is worth $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The equality $100(1+i)+110=220$ gives $1+i=1.1$, so the yield is $10\%$. At $0\%$ the prices would be the nominal sums, $210$ and $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$, which are different.
