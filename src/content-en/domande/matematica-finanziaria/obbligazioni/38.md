A bond with positive future cash flows has a theoretical price of $940\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at a rate of $7\%$. The price paid is $920\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Where does its yield to maturity lie?

- Exactly at $7\%$.
- Above $7\%$.
- It could lie on either side of $7\%$.
- Below $7\%$.

## Explanation

The present value of positive cash flows decreases strictly with the rate. At $7\%$ their present value is $940$ euros, above the purchase price of $920$ euros. A higher rate is needed to bring it down to $920$ euros. Therefore, the yield to maturity exceeds $7\%$. Strict monotonicity rules out a yield equal to or below $7\%$.
