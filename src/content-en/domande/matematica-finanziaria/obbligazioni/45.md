A bond pays regular quarterly coupons. Settlement occurs immediately after a coupon payment. Redemption is due with the final coupon in twenty-one months. How many coupons does the buyer receive?

- $8$
- $7$
- $6$
- $21$

## Explanation

Quarterly coupons are three months apart. Since settlement immediately follows a coupon payment, the buyer's first coupon is in three months. The last is in twenty-one months, or $\dfrac{21}{3}=7$ quarters, so the buyer receives seven coupons. The value $8$ also counts the coupon just paid to the seller, while $21$ counts months.
