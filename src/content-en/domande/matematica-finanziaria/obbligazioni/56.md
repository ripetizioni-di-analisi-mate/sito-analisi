During one day without a coupon payment, the dirty price of a bond rises by €0.10. The coupon for the period is €72 and accrues linearly over 180 actual days. How did the clean price change over the same day?

- It rose by €0.50.
- It fell by €0.30.
- It rose by €0.10.
- It fell by €0.40.

## Explanation

Accrued interest increases by $\dfrac{72}{180}=0.40$ euros in one day. Dirty price equals clean price plus accrued interest, so the change in clean price is $0.10-0.40=-0.30$ euros.
