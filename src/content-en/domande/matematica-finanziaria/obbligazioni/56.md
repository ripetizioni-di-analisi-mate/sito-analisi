A semiannual coupon is $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The previous coupon was paid $54$ days ago and the next is due in $126$ days. What is accrued interest under linear accrual using actual days?

- $35\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $15\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $18\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The coupon period includes the days elapsed and those remaining, for a total of $54+126=180$ days. The elapsed fraction is $\dfrac{54}{180}=\dfrac{3}{10}$, so accrued interest is
$$
A=50\cdot\frac{54}{180}=15\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $35$ euros uses the remaining fraction $\dfrac{126}{180}$, while $25$ euros is half the coupon.
