A zero-coupon bond pays $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Its effective annual yield is $10\%$. Calculate its price today.

- $1464.1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1190\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A zero-coupon bond's price is its redemption payment discounted over the remaining term at the effective annual yield. Two years remain, so the divisor for discounting is $1.1^2=1.21$ and
$$
P=\frac{1210}{1.21}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Multiplying by $1.21$ instead of dividing gives $1464.1$, while discounting for only one year gives $1100$.
