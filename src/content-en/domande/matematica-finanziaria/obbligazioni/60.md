Two bonds have the same clean price in euros and the same positive coupon amount. A larger fraction of the coupon period has elapsed for the first. How do their dirty prices compare under linear accrual?

- The second bond has the higher dirty price.
- The first bond has the higher dirty price.
- The dirty prices are equal.
- The comparison depends on the principal repayment date.

## Explanation

Under linear accrual the dirty price is $P_{\mathrm{tq}}=P_{\mathrm{s}}+\alpha C$. Since the bonds have identical clean prices and coupons, their dirty prices differ only through the elapsed fraction $\alpha$. Since $C>0$ and the first bond has the larger elapsed fraction, it has more accrued interest. Its dirty price is therefore higher. The redemption date does not enter the accrual calculation.
