Two bonds cost $450$ and $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$ respectively. Each makes a single payment in one year, of $495$ and $605\,\mathrm{EUR}$ respectively. What is the portfolio yield to maturity?

- $11\%$
- $10\%$
- $20\%$
- $5\%$

## Explanation

Portfolio yield to maturity uses the total price and aggregate cash flows. The total price is $450+550=1000$ euros and the single aggregate payment in one year is $495+605=1100$ euros. Thus
$$
i=\frac{1100}{1000}-1=10\%.
$$
Here each individual bond also yields $10\%$ to maturity because $\dfrac{495}{450}=\dfrac{605}{550}=1.1$. The value $20\%$ adds the two yields.
