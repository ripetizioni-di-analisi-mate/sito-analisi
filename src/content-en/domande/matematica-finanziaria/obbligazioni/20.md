A zero-coupon bond has price $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and redemption value $1089\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At an annual compound yield of $10\%$, how many years remain until redemption?

- $1$ year
- $2.1$ years
- $3$ years
- $2$ years

## Explanation

The time to redemption $T$ satisfies $900\cdot1.1^T=1089$. The growth factor over the full term is $\dfrac{1089}{900}=1.21$, and since $1.21=1.1^2$, the exponent is $T=2$. Two years therefore remain. The result of $2.1$ years divides the total return of $21\%$ by the annual rate of $10\%$, which uses simple interest.
