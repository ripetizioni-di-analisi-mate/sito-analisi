A bond with face value $1600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ pays two equal coupons per year at a nominal annual coupon rate of $6\%$. Calculate the amount of one coupon.

- $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $48\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $24\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The nominal annual coupon rate of $6\%$ gives coupon payments of $1600\cdot0.06=96$ euros over a full year. Since the two coupons are equal, each is
$$
C=\frac{1600\cdot0.06}{2}=48\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The total for the year is $96$ euros. Dividing by four, as if coupons were quarterly, gives $24$ euros.
