Accrued interest under a linear convention is $15\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on a coupon of $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What fraction of the period has elapsed?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Under linear accrual, accrued interest is $A=\alpha C$, where $\alpha$ is the elapsed fraction of the period. Hence $\alpha$ is accrued interest divided by the coupon:
$$
\alpha=\frac{A}{C}=\frac{15}{60}=\frac{1}{4}.
$$
In a period of $180$ days, for example, $45$ days have elapsed. The value $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is the fraction remaining until the next coupon.
