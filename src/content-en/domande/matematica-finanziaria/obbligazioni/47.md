A bond matures in one quarter with a total final payment of $1030\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is its price today at a nominal annual yield of $12\%$ compounded quarterly?

- $919.64\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $971.7\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1030\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A nominal annual yield of $12\%$ compounded quarterly corresponds to a quarterly yield of $\dfrac{12\%}{4}=3\%$. Since payment is one quarter away, the present value is
$$
P=\frac{1030}{1.03}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $919.64$ discounts for one quarter using the annual rate of $12\%$. The value $971.7$ uses $6\%$, obtained by dividing the nominal rate by two.
