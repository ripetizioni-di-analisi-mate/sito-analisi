A bond is bought for $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and sold in one year immediately after a coupon of $45\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is received. What sale proceeds give a return of $10\%$?

- $990\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1035\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $945\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A return of $10\%$ requires terminal wealth of $900\cdot1.1=990$ euros. Terminal wealth consists of the coupon received and the sale proceeds, so the sale proceeds must be
$$
P_H=990-45=945\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The amount $990$ euros is the entire terminal wealth, including the coupon. The value $1035$ euros adds the coupon instead of subtracting it.
