A zero-coupon bond costs $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in six months. Calculate its effective annual yield.

- $20\%$
- $21\%$
- $10.25\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

The ratio of the redemption payment to the price is $\dfrac{1100}{1000}=1.1$, so the half-year accumulation factor is $1.1$. A year contains two half-years, giving an annual factor of $1.1^2=1.21$ and an effective annual yield of $21\%$. Doubling the half-year rate gives $20\%$ under simple interest. The return for the half-year alone is $10\%$.
