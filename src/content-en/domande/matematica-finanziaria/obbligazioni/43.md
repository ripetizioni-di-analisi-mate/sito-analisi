A bond with quarterly coupons is priced at a nominal annual yield of $12\%$ compounded quarterly. Which rate applies to one quarter?

- $4\%$
- $12\%$
- $6\%$
- $3\%$

## Explanation

By definition the nominal annual yield $j^{(4)}$ compounded quarterly is four times the effective quarterly yield. The quarterly rate is therefore
$$
i=\frac{j^{(4)}}{4}=\frac{12\%}{4}=3\%.
$$
The value $6\%$ divides by two as for semiannual compounding. The value $4\%$ divides by three, and $12\%$ applies the annual rate to a single quarter.
