A bond pays four quarterly coupons of $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has a face value of $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is its nominal annual coupon rate?

- $4\%$
- $2\%$
- $1\%$
- $8\%$

## Explanation

The nominal annual coupon rate equals the sum of coupons for one year divided by face value. The four quarterly coupons total $4\cdot12=48$ euros, so
$$
r_c=\frac{48}{1200}=4\%.
$$
The value $1\%$ divides only one coupon by face value, giving the quarterly coupon rate. The value $2\%$ counts only half a year's coupons.
