A bond pays three annual coupons of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, starting in one year, and repays $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with the last coupon. What is its value at an effective annual yield of $5\%$?

- $863.84\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1136.16\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The price is the present value of the coupon annuity plus the present value of the redemption payment. At $5\%$ the annuity factor is $a_{\overline 3|0.05}=\dfrac{1-1.05^{-3}}{0.05}\approx2.7232$, so
$$
P=100\cdot2.7232+\frac{1000}{1.05^3}\approx272.32+863.84=1136.16\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $863.84$ omits the coupons, and $1300$ adds the cash flows without discounting. The bond trades above par because its coupon rate of $10\%$ exceeds its yield.
