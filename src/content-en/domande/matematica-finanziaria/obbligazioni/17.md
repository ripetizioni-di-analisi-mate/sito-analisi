A zero-coupon bond costs $810\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. What is the accumulation factor over its full term?

- $1.2346$
- $1.1111$
- $0.2346$
- $0.81$

## Explanation

The accumulation factor for the full term is the amount received at maturity divided by the price paid, so
$$
\frac{M}{P}=\frac{1000}{810}=\frac{100}{81}\approx1.2346.
$$
The annual factor $\sqrt{\dfrac{100}{81}}=\dfrac{10}{9}\approx1.1111$ measures growth over just one year. The value $0.2346$ is the total return, while $0.81$ is the discount factor.
