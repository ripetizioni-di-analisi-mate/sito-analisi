Halfway through an annual coupon period, a single payment of $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains, including a coupon of $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The effective annual yield is $21\%$. Calculate the clean price in euros using linear accrual and the same fraction of a period for discounting.

- $1045\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $945\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1155\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The dirty price is the present value of the single payment due half a period from now. The discounting calculation uses $1.21^{\frac{1}{2}}=1.1$, giving a dirty price of $\dfrac{1210}{1.1}=1100$ euros. Under linear accrual, accrued interest is half the coupon, or $55$ euros, so the clean price is
$$
P_{\mathrm{s}}=1100-55=1045\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The dirty price is $1100$. Adding accrued interest instead of subtracting it gives $1155$.
