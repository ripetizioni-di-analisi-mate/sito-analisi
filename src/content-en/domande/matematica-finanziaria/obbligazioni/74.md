A coupon of $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ received in year one is reinvested until year three at an annual compound rate of $10\%$. How much does it contribute to terminal wealth?

- $88\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $106.48\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $96.8\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The coupon is received in year one and remains invested until year three, earning interest for $3-1=2$ years. At a compound rate of $10\%$, its contribution to terminal wealth is
$$
80\cdot1.1^2=80\cdot1.21=96.80\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $106.48$ compounds for three years and $88$ for one year. Simple interest gives $96$ euros from $80\cdot(1+2\cdot0.1)$.
