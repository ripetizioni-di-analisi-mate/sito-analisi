A portfolio holds two bonds with different maturities and observed prices. Which information determines its yield to maturity?

- The total cost and the aggregate amounts due on each payment date.
- The average of the two bonds' coupon rates.
- Only the total cost and the face value of the longer bond.
- Only the individual yields to maturity, without the cash flows.

## Explanation

The portfolio yield to maturity is the rate $i$ satisfying $P=\sum_k F_k(1+i)^{-t_k}$, where $P$ is the total cost and $F_k$ is the aggregate receipt on date $t_k$. The equation therefore requires the total cost, aggregate cash flows, and their dates. Individual yields to maturity are insufficient because the portfolio yield generally differs from their average.
