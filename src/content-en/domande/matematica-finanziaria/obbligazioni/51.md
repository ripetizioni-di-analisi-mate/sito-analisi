How do you convert an effective quarterly yield to a nominal annual yield compounded quarterly?

- Raise the rate to the fourth power.
- Multiply the rate by four.
- Multiply the accumulation factor by four.
- Subtract one from the fourth power of the accumulation factor.

## Explanation

By definition, the nominal annual yield compounded quarterly is $j^{(4)}=4i$, where $i$ is the effective quarterly yield. Multiply the quarterly rate by four to obtain the nominal yield. The expression $(1+i)^4-1$ gives the equivalent effective annual yield. For example, with $i=2\%$, the nominal rate is $8\%$ and the effective annual rate is approximately $8.24\%$.
