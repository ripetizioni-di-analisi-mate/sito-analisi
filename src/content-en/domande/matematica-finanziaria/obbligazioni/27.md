A bond with face value $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ pays annual coupons at $4\%$ and redeems at $102\%$ of face value. How much does the holder receive on the final date?

- $1530\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1590\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1560\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The redemption payment is $102\%$ of face value, or $1.02\cdot1500=1530$ euros. The annual coupon is calculated on face value and is $0.04\cdot1500=60$ euros. On the last date the holder receives the final coupon and the redemption payment, for a total of $1530+60=1590$ euros. The value $1530$ omits the coupon, while $1560$ adds the coupon to face value rather than to the redemption payment.
