A bond with a positive coupon costs exactly the undiscounted sum of its positive future receipts. What is its yield to maturity?

- The bond's coupon rate.
- A strictly negative rate.
- A strictly positive rate.
- $0\%$.

## Explanation

At a rate of $i=0$ each discount factor $(1+i)^{-t_k}$ equals one, so present value equals the undiscounted sum of the receipts. Since the price equals this sum, $i=0$ solves the yield-to-maturity equation. Present value decreases strictly with the rate, so the solution is unique and the yield to maturity is $0\%$. The positive coupon rate differs from this yield.
