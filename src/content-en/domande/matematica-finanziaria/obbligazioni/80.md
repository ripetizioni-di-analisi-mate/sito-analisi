An investor sells a bond immediately after receiving a coupon. How does the sale-date coupon enter the holding-period return?

- It earns interest for one more period before being added.
- It is added to the sale proceeds.
- It is included among the future coupons payable to the buyer.
- It is subtracted from the sale proceeds.

## Explanation

The seller receives the coupon and sale price on the same date, the end of the holding period. The sale immediately follows the coupon payment, so the buyer's price covers only subsequent cash flows and excludes that coupon. The coupon is therefore added to the sale price in the numerator $P_H+W_H-P_0$. It earns no further interest because no time elapses between receipt and the end date.
