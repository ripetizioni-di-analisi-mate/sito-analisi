Two one-year bonds with the same redemption payment each have one coupon remaining. The first coupon exceeds the second by $24\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At a yield of $20\%$, what is the difference between their prices?

- $4.8\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $24\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $28.8\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The bonds have the same redemption amount and maturity, so their price difference is the present value of the difference between the coupons. The first coupon is $24$ euros higher and is due in one year, so the price difference is
$$
\frac{24}{1.2}=20\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $24$ euros omits discounting, $28.8$ compounds instead of discounting, and $4.8$ is only the interest for one year.
