A bond costs €100. In one year it pays a €6 coupon and repays €100 of principal. A fixed fee is charged only at redemption. What is the largest fee compatible with a net annual return of at least $4\%$?

- €4
- €2
- €6
- €0

## Explanation

To earn at least $4\%$, the €100 investment must pay at least €104 net. The gross proceeds are €106, so the fee can be at most $106-104=2$ euros.
