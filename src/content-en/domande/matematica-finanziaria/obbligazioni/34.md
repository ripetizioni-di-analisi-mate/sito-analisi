A bond costs $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Its only payment, due in one year, consists of a coupon of $35\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a redemption payment of $1025\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is its yield to maturity?

- $2.5\%$
- $6\%$
- $5.85\%$
- $3.5\%$

## Explanation

The bond's only receipt is $35+1025=1060$ euros in one year. Its yield to maturity $i$ satisfies $1000=\dfrac{1060}{1+i}$, so
$$
i=\frac{1060}{1000}-1=6\%.
$$
The value $3.5\%$ is the current yield, which counts only the coupon. The value $2.5\%$ accounts only for the capital gain at redemption. Yield to maturity includes both components.
