A bond has a single remaining payment of $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in six months, coupon included. The clean price is $975\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the accrued interest is $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the buyer's effective annual yield to maturity?

- $5\%$
- $10.25\%$
- $15.98\%$
- $7.69\%$

## Explanation

The buyer pays the dirty price, that is $975+25=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so this is the outlay that enters the yield equation. The payment falls in half a year, therefore $1000=\dfrac{1050}{(1+i)^{\frac{1}{2}}}$ and $(1+i)^{\frac{1}{2}}=1.05$. It follows that $1+i=1.05^2=1.1025$, and the yield to maturity is $10.25\%$. The value $5\%$ is the half-year return. The values $15.98\%$ and $7.69\%$ use the clean price instead of the dirty price, on an annual and a half-year basis respectively.
