A bond pays two annual coupons of $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and repays $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year two. It is held to redemption and the first coupon is reinvested at $5\%$ for one year. Calculate terminal wealth.

- $1126\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1123\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1063\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first coupon is received in year one and reinvested for one year at $5\%$, so it is worth $60\cdot1.05=63$ euros in year two. The second coupon and the principal are received in year two and earn no further interest. Terminal wealth is therefore
$$
63+60+1000=1123\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Without reinvestment the result is $1120$, while $1126$ also compounds the second coupon.
