A bond pays annual coupons of $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and repays $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years. Which sequence gives the receipts over the next three years?

- $(1040,40,40)$.
- $(40,40,1000)$.
- $(0,0,1120)$.
- $(40,40,1040)$.

## Explanation

The bond pays a coupon of $40$ euros at the end of each of the three years. The principal of $1000$ euros is paid only on the final date, giving a third payment of $40+1000=1040$ euros and the sequence $(40,40,1040)$. The sequence $(40,40,1000)$ omits the final coupon, $(1040,40,40)$ moves the principal repayment to year one, and $(0,0,1120)$ treats the bond as a zero-coupon bond.
