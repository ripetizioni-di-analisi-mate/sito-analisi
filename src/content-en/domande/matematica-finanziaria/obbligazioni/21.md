Two zero-coupon bonds have the same redemption payment and both yield $4\%$ annually. Bond A matures in one year and bond B in three years. Which comparison is correct?

- Bond B has the higher price.
- The prices are equal.
- The comparison depends on A's face value.
- Bond A has the higher price.

## Explanation

The price of a zero-coupon bond is $P=\dfrac{M}{1.04^T}$, where $T$ is its term in years. The redemption value $M$ and yield are the same for both bonds, so only their discount factors differ. Since $1.04^3>1.04$, B has the lower price and A the higher price. The comparison does not depend on the amount $M$, which appears in both prices.
