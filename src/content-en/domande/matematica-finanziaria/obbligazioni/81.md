A portfolio holds two bonds worth $98\,\mathrm{EUR}$ each and three bonds worth $105\,\mathrm{EUR}$ each. What is its value on the quotation date?

- $511\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $203\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $493\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $525\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The market value on the quotation date is $\sum_j x_jP_j$, the sum of each unit price multiplied by the quantity held. The two units of the first bond are worth $2\cdot98=196$ euros and the three units of the second $3\cdot105=315$ euros. Total value is therefore $196+315=511$ euros. The amount $203$ euros adds the unit prices without accounting for quantities, while $525$ values every unit at the higher price.
