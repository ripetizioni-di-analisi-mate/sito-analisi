A zero-coupon bond repays $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in eighteen months. What is its price at an effective annual yield of $21\%$, rounded to the cent?

- $751.31\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $826.45\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $683.01\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $909.09\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The remaining term in years is $T=\dfrac{3}{2}$, so the price is $P=\dfrac{1000}{1.21^{\frac{3}{2}}}$. Since $1.21=1.1^2$, we have $1.21^{\frac{3}{2}}=1.1^3=1.331$. Therefore
$$
P=\frac{1000}{1.331}\approx751.31\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $826.45\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for one year only and $683.01\,\mathrm{EUR}$ for two years. The value $909.09\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for a single half-year at the semiannual rate of $10\%$.
