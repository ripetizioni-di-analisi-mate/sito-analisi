A zero-coupon bond pays $864\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years. At an effective annual yield of $20\%$, what price does the buyer pay today?

- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $691.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The price is the redemption payment discounted for three years at $20\%$. The three-year accumulation factor is $1.2^3=1.728$, so
$$
P=\frac{864}{1.728}=500\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $720$ discounts for one year and $600$ for two years. Subtracting $20\%$ from the redemption payment gives $691.2$ and applies one year of simple commercial discount.
