You need exactly €1500 in two years and can buy indivisible zero-coupon bonds, each redeeming for €400. You can set aside cash earning no interest in addition to the bonds. What is the smallest amount of cash that makes it possible to meet the target exactly?

- €300
- €100
- €0
- €400

## Explanation

Three bonds pay €1200 and four pay €1600, already above the target. Buying the largest admissible whole number of bonds leaves $1500-1200=300$ euros to set aside.
