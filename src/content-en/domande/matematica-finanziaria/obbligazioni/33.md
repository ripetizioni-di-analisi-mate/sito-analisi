A bond costs $950\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays an annual coupon of $38\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is its current yield?

- $5\%$
- $8\%$
- $4\%$
- $3.8\%$

## Explanation

Current yield is the annual coupon divided by the purchase price, so
$$
\frac{C}{P_{\mathrm{obs}}}=\frac{38}{950}=0.04=4\%.
$$
This measure ignores the redemption payment and its date. Dividing the coupon by $1000$ euros instead of the purchase price gives $3.8\%$, confusing current yield with a coupon rate.
