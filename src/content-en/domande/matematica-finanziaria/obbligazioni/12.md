A bond bought for $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is sold for $810\,\mathrm{EUR}$ one year later, immediately after the holder receives a coupon of $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Calculate the realized return.

- $1.25\%$
- $5\%$
- $3.75\%$
- $4.94\%$

## Explanation

The realized return compares the initial outlay with wealth on the sale date. Since the sale takes place immediately after the coupon payment, the seller receives the coupon in addition to the sale price. Hence
$$
h=\frac{810+30-800}{800}=\frac{40}{800}=5\%.
$$
The value $1.25\%$ counts only the price change and $3.75\%$ only the coupon, while $4.94\%$ divides the gain by the sale price.
