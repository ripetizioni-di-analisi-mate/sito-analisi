A bond is sold in year one immediately after a coupon payment of $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Its only remaining payment is $1122\,\mathrm{EUR}$ one year later. The market yield at sale is $10\%$. How much does the seller receive in total in year one?

- $1172\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1020\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1122\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1070\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The buyer pays the present value of the only remaining payment, which is due in one year, or $\dfrac{1122}{1.1}=1020$ euros. The coupon of $50$ euros paid immediately before the sale belongs to the seller. In year one the seller therefore receives a total of
$$
1020+50=1070\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1020$ omits the coupon. The value $1122$ is the undiscounted remaining cash flow, and $1172$ adds the coupon to it.
