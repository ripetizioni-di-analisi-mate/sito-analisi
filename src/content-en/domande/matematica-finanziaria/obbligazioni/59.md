A coupon of $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is paid today. The dirty price immediately after payment is $980\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What was the price immediately before payment, including entitlement to today's coupon, if the value of subsequent cash flows was the same?

- $980\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $930\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1030\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Immediately before payment, the holder is also entitled to today's coupon of $50$ euros. It is payable now, so its present value is $50$ euros without discounting. The subsequent cash flows are worth $980$ euros today, so the price including the coupon is $980+50=1030$ euros. The value $930$ subtracts the coupon, while $980$ omits it.
