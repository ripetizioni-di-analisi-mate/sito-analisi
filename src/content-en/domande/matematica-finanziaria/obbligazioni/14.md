A portfolio holds four zero-coupon bonds that each repay $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year, and two coupon bonds that each pay an annual coupon of $15\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and mature in two years. What is the total cash flow in one year?

- $415\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $460\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $430\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The aggregate cash flow in one year includes all receipts on that date. The four zero-coupon bonds repay $4\cdot100=400$ euros. The two coupon bonds pay only their coupons because their principal is due in two years. The total is therefore $400+2\cdot15=430$ euros. The value $415$ counts only one coupon and $400$ omits both coupons.
