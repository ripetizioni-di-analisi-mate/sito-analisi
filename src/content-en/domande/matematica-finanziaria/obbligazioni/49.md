Two bonds pay identical semiannual coupons. One quotes a nominal annual yield of $8\%$ compounded semiannually, and the other an effective annual yield of $8\%$. Which quote implies a higher semiannual yield?

- The comparison depends on the coupon amounts.
- Both quotes imply the same semiannual yield.
- The nominal annual quote compounded semiannually.
- The effective annual quote.

## Explanation

A nominal rate of $8\%$ compounded semiannually gives a semiannual rate of $4\%$ by definition. An effective annual yield of $8\%$ corresponds to the semiannual rate $i$ satisfying $(1+i)^2=1.08$, so $i=\sqrt{1.08}-1\approx3.92\%$. Since $3.92\%<4\%$, the nominal quote implies the higher semiannual yield. The coupon amounts play no role because the comparison concerns rates alone.
