A zero-coupon bond trades at face value but redeems at $102\%$ of face value in one year. What is its yield?

- It is $2\%$.
- It is $-2\%$.
- It is $102\%$.
- It is zero.

## Explanation

The price equals face value, so the buyer pays $N$ at settlement. At maturity the holder receives $102\%$ of face value, or $1.02N$. The annual yield is therefore
$$
y=\frac{1.02N}{N}-1=0.02=2\%,
$$
for any $N$. The yield would be zero only if the redemption payment equaled the price. The value $102\%$ is the accumulation factor written as a percentage.
