A portfolio holds one bond paying $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and another paying $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years, each with no other payments. The annual yield to maturity on the portfolio is $10\%$. What is its total initial value?

- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $290.91\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $352\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The portfolio yield to maturity equates its total initial value to the present value of its aggregate cash flows. At $10\%$, the first payment is worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100$ euros and the second $\dfrac{242}{1.21}=200$ euros today. The initial value is therefore $100+200=300$ euros. The value $352$ adds the undiscounted payments, $320$ discounts both for one year, and $290.91$ discounts both for two years.
