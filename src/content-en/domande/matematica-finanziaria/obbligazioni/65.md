A one-year bond has face value $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, pays a coupon of $22\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and is quoted at $102$ per $100$ of face value. If the yield is $10\%$, how much principal does it repay at maturity?

- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1020\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1122\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At a price of $102$ per $100$ of face value, the buyer pays $1.02\cdot1000=1020$ euros. At a yield to maturity of $10\%$, the total receipt in one year is $1020\cdot1.1=1122$ euros. This includes a coupon of $22$ euros, so the principal repayment is
$$
M=1122-22=1100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1122$ also includes the coupon, while $1000$ is the face value.
