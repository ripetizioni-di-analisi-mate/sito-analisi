A bond pays $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $968\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years, including principal. What is its price at an effective annual yield of $10\%$?

- $1078\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $980\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The bond's value is the sum of its cash flows discounted from their respective dates. At $10\%$ annually, the first payment is worth $\dfrac{110}{1.1}=100$ euros today and the second, including principal, is worth $\dfrac{968}{1.21}=800$ euros. The price is therefore $900$ euros. The value $800$ omits the first coupon, $1078$ adds the undiscounted payments, and $980$ discounts their sum for only one year.
