The discount factors for years one and two are $0.96$ and $0.90$. A bond repays €100 in year two and has a face total of €20 to split between its two coupons. If its price must be €108.60, how much of the €20 goes to the first coupon?

- €20
- €6
- €18
- €10

## Explanation

If $x$ is the first coupon, the second-year payment is $120-x$ euros. The price is $0.96x+0.90(120-x)=108+0.06x$. A price of €108.60 requires €10 in year one.
