A portfolio invests $25\%$ in bond A and the balance in bond B. Over the same year, including coupons and with no intermediate transactions, A returns $12\%$ and B returns $4\%$. What is the portfolio return?

- $16\%$
- $10\%$
- $6\%$
- $8\%$

## Explanation

With no intermediate transactions, the portfolio return is the weighted average of the bonds' returns using their initial weights. The weight of B is $1-0.25=0.75$, so
$$
h=0.25\cdot12\%+0.75\cdot4\%=3\%+3\%=6\%.
$$
The value $8\%$ is a simple arithmetic mean that assigns equal weights, while $16\%$ adds the two returns.
