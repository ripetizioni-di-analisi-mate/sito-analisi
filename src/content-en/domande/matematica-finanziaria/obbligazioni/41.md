Between two coupon dates a bond's clean price is $990\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and accrued interest is $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Which outlay enters the buyer's yield-to-maturity calculation?

- $978\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- $990\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
- $1002\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

## Explanation

Yield to maturity uses the amount the buyer actually pays. Between coupon dates this is the dirty price, including accrued interest, so
$$
P_{\mathrm{tq}}=P_{\mathrm{s}}+A=990+12=1002\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The clean price of $990$ euros excludes the accrued coupon interest that the buyer pays to the seller and subsequently receives with the next coupon. The value $978$ euros subtracts accrued interest instead of adding it.
