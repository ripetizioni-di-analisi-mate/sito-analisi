At least two coupons remain when settlement occurs immediately after a coupon payment. The first remaining coupon is due in one year. What does the first term in the price sum represent?

- Accrued interest for the period just ended.
- The full repayment of principal.
- The value of the coupon just paid.
- The present value of the next coupon.

## Explanation

At settlement immediately after a coupon payment, the coupon just paid belongs to the seller and accrued interest is zero. The buyer's first receipt is the next coupon, one period away. The first term is therefore $Cv$, the present value of that coupon. Principal appears only in the last term, with the final coupon.
