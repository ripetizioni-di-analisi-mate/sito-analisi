A bond costs $990\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays a coupon of $49\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and principal of $1040\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Calculate its yield to maturity.

- $9.52\%$
- $4.95\%$
- $10\%$
- $5.05\%$

## Explanation

There is just one receipt, $49+1040=1089$ euros in one year. The yield to maturity equates the price to this receipt discounted for one year, so
$$
1+i=\frac{1089}{990}=1.1
$$
and the yield to maturity is $10\%$. The value $4.95\%$ is the current yield $\dfrac{49}{990}$, which counts only coupon income and omits the capital gain at redemption.
