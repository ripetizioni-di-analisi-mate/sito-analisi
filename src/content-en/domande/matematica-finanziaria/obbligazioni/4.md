A bond pays a coupon of $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and another of $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. It repays $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with the last coupon. Calculate its price at an effective annual yield of $20\%$.

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $125\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $83.33\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The redemption payment accompanies the second coupon, so the cash flows are $20$ euros in year one and $30+90=120$ euros in year two. Discounting each from its own payment date at $20\%$ gives
$$
P=\frac{20}{1.2}+\frac{120}{1.44}=\frac{50}{3}+\frac{250}{3}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Omitting the first coupon gives $83.33$, while $120$ is the undiscounted second payment.
