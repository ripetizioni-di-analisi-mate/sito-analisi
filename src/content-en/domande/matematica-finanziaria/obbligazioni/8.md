The effective annual yield is $12.36\%$. Calculate the effective yield for one half-year.

- $12.36\%$
- $6.18\%$
- $3\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

The half-year yield $i$ is equivalent to the effective annual yield if $(1+i)^2=1.1236$. Thus $1+i=\sqrt{1.1236}=1.06$, and the half-year yield is $6\%$. Dividing the annual rate by two gives $6.18\%$. That conversion applies to a nominal annual rate compounded semiannually. Dividing by four gives $3\%$.
