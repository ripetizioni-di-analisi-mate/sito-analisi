A portfolio worth $3000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ invests $40\%$ of its value in a bond priced at $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ per unit. How many units does it hold?

- $15$
- $24$
- $12$
- $20$

## Explanation

The allocation of $40\%$ is the share of portfolio value invested in the bond, or $0.4\cdot3000=1200$ euros. Dividing the amount invested by the unit price gives the holding:
$$
x=\frac{1200}{80}=15.
$$
The value $12$ divides by $100$ euros, using face value instead of the unit price.
