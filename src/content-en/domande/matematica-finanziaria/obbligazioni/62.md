A bond repays its face value of $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays an annual coupon of $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. It trades at par immediately after a coupon payment. What is its effective annual yield?

- $6\%$
- $4\%$
- $3\%$
- $8\%$

## Explanation

For a bond redeemed at face value and valued immediately after a coupon payment, $P-N=(C-iN)a_{\overline n|i}$ holds. Since the bond trades at par, $P=N$, and since $a_{\overline n|i}>0$, we have $C=iN$. The yield therefore equals the coupon rate:
$$
i=\frac{C}{N}=\frac{60}{1500}=4\%.
$$
The value $6\%$ divides the coupon by $1000$ euros instead of by face value.
