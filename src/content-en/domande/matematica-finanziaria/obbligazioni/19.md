A zero-coupon bond with face value $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ redeems at $105\%$ of face value in one year. What is its price today at an annual yield of $5\%$?

- $952.38\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1102.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The redemption payment is $105\%$ of face value, or $M=1.05\cdot1000=1050$ euros. Discounting this amount for one year at $5\%$ gives
$$
P=\frac{1050}{1.05}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Discounting face value instead of the redemption payment gives $952.38$. The undiscounted redemption amount is $1050$, and $1102.5$ compounds it instead of discounting it.
