A schedule has payments at times $2,3,4$ and interest rate $i>0$. Which formula gives its present value using the annuity-due factor?

- $R v^2\ddot a_{\overline3|i}$
- $R v^2 a_{\overline3|i}$
- $R v^4\ddot a_{\overline3|i}$
- $R v\ddot a_{\overline3|i}$

## Explanation

The annuity-due factor $\ddot a_{\overline3|i}$ values the three payments on the first payment date, time $2$. Discount it for two periods to time zero to obtain $V(0)=Rv^2\ddot a_{\overline3|i}$. The expression $Rv^2a_{\overline3|i}$ describes the dates $3,4,5$ because the ordinary annuity factor values payments one period before the first one. Using that factor, the same stream is worth $Rv\,a_{\overline3|i}$.
