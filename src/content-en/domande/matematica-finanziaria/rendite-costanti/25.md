Two annual payments of $120$ euros are due in advance. What is their value immediately before the first payment at an effective annual interest rate of $20\%$?

- $200$ euros
- $220$ euros
- $264$ euros
- $240$ euros

## Explanation

The payments fall at times $0$ and $1$, so apply the annuity-due factor $\ddot a_{\overline2|i}=1+v$ with $v=\dfrac{1}{1.2}$.
$$
V(0)=120+\frac{120}{1.2}=120+100=220\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The first payment is due on the valuation date and enters at face value. The value of $264$ euros is the value at time $1$, while $240$ euros is the nominal sum.
