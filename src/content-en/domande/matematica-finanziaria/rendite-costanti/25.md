Two annuities of four annual installments of $100$ euros differ only in their payment dates. The first pays at times $0,1,2,3$ and the second at times $1,2,3,4$. At the same rate, the first is worth $36$ euros more than the second today. What is the discount factor $v^4$ from time $4$ to today?

- $0.36$
- $0.64$
- $1.36$
- $0.89$

## Explanation

The two annuities share the installments at times $1$, $2$ and $3$, so these contributions cancel in the difference of the present values. What remains is the installment at time $0$, worth $100$ euros, and the installment at time $4$, worth $100v^4$ euros today. Therefore $100-100v^4=36$, and the discount factor is $v^4=0.64$. The answer $0.36$ confuses the difference in value with the factor sought, while $1.36$ comes from a sign error. The answer $0.89$ is approximately the annual factor $v=\sqrt[4]{0.64}$.
