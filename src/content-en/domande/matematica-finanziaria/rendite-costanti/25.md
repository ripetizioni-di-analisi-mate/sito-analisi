Two annuities each pay four instalments of €100. One pays at times $0,1,2,3$ and the other at times $1,2,3,4$. The first is worth €36 more today. What discount factor from time $4$ to today follows directly?

- $0.36$
- $0.64$
- $1.36$
- $0.90$

## Explanation

The payments at times $1,2,3$ cancel when the plans are compared. What remains is €100 today minus the present value of €100 at time $4$. Thus $100-100v^4=36$ gives $v^4=0.64$.
