A receivable of $144$ euros is due in one year and an equal amount in two years. The effective annual interest rate is $20\%$. What payment today settles both claims?

- $220$ euros
- $288$ euros
- $264$ euros
- $240$ euros

## Explanation

The two amounts are due at times $1$ and $2$ and form an ordinary annuity with $v=\dfrac{1}{1.2}=\dfrac56$.
$$
V(0)=144\cdot\frac56+144\cdot\frac{25}{36}=120+100=220\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Their present value is the sum that would settle both claims today. The nominal sum is $288$ euros, while $240$ euros discounts both payments for just one year.
