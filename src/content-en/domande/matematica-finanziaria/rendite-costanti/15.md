A contract specifies two equal payments. At zero interest their total value today is €300. The incomplete schedule lists two possibilities, payments at times $0,2$ or at times $1,2$. As the positive annual rate increases without bound, their present value tends to €150. Which schedule is consistent?

- One payment today and one in two years.
- One payment in one year and one in two years.
- Both schedules.
- Neither schedule.

## Explanation

At zero interest each payment has its face value, so each is €150. As the rate grows without bound the present value of every future payment tends to zero. Only a payment due today can account for the €150 limiting value.
