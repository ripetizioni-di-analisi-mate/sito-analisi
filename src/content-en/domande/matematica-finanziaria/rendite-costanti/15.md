A piece of equipment is paid for with ten six-monthly installments of $500$ euros, each due at the end of the half-year. The effective annual rate is $8.16\%$ and $1.04^{-10}\approx0.6756$. What is the equivalent price paid today, rounded to the nearest euro?

- $4055$ euros
- $5000$ euros
- $6003$ euros
- $3331$ euros

## Explanation

The installments are six-monthly, so the annuity factor must be computed with the effective six-month rate $i_2$ equivalent to $8.16\%$ a year. Under compound interest $(1+i_2)^2=1.0816$, and since $1.04^2=1.0816$ we get $i_2=4\%$. The installments fall at the end of half-years $1$ to $10$, so today's price is
$$
500\,a_{\overline{10}|0.04}=500\cdot\frac{1-1.04^{-10}}{0.04}\approx500\cdot\frac{0.3244}{0.04}=4055\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $5000$ euros is the nominal sum of the installments, while $6003$ euros is the accumulated value at the end of the tenth half-year, obtained with $s_{\overline{10}|0.04}$ instead of $a_{\overline{10}|0.04}$. The value of $3331$ euros applies the annual rate of $8.16\%$ to each half-year.
