Starting with a zero balance, a saver wants to have $630$ euros immediately after making two equal year-end deposits. What deposit is required at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$?

- $300$ euros
- $272.73$ euros
- $363$ euros
- $315$ euros

## Explanation

The first deposit earns one year's interest and the second is made on the final date, so the accumulated value is $R\,s_{\overline2|0.1}=R(1.1+1)=2.1R$. The equation $2.1R=630$ gives $R=\dfrac{630}{2.1}=300$ euros. The value of $315$ euros divides the final balance by two and ignores interest. The value of $272.73$ euros treats the deposits as made in advance using the factor $1.1\cdot2.1=2.31$.
