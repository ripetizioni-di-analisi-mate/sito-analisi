Two equal payments are due at times $4$ and $5$. Together they are worth €210 at time $3$. At time $4$, just after the first receipt, the remaining claim is worth €110. At $10\%$ annual interest, how much was the first receipt?

- €100
- €110
- €231
- €121

## Explanation

Immediately before payment the plan is worth $210\cdot1.10=231$ euros. Its value immediately after payment is €110. The drop of €121 is the cash received at that date.
