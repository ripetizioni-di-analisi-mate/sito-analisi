Two payments in arrears of $121$ euros are due at times $4$ and $5$. What is their value at time $3$ at an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $231$ euros
- $242$ euros
- $200$ euros
- $210$ euros

## Explanation

At time $3$ the payments are one and two years away, so use a two-payment ordinary annuity.
$$
V(3)=121\cdot\frac{10}{11}+121\cdot\left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^2=110+100=210\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $231$ euros is the value at time $4$, when the first installment is included at face value. The value of $200$ euros discounts both payments for two periods.
