A loan of $10000$ euros granted today requires the payment of interest only at the end of the first and second years. The principal is then repaid with three equal annual installments at times $3$, $4$ and $5$. The effective annual rate is $12\%$ and $a_{\overline3|0.12}\approx2.4018$. What is the installment, rounded to the nearest euro?

- $5223$ euros
- $5849$ euros
- $3333$ euros
- $4164$ euros

## Explanation

At the end of each of the first two years the interest accrued, $10000\cdot0.12=1200$ euros, is paid, so the debt does not grow and at time $2$ it is still $10000$ euros. The installments at times $3$, $4$ and $5$ form an ordinary annuity whose value at time $2$, one period before the first installment, is $R\,a_{\overline3|0.12}$. The equation of value at time $2$ is therefore $10000=R\,a_{\overline3|0.12}$, which gives
$$
R=\frac{10000}{2.4018}\approx4164\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $5223$ euros accumulates the debt for two years as if the interest were not paid, while $5849$ euros accumulates it for three years. The value of $3333$ euros divides the debt by three and ignores interest.
