Two payments in advance of $100$ euros, due at times $0$ and $1$, have a price of $180$ euros. What effective annual rate is implied?

- $25\%$
- $20\%$
- $7.32\%$
- $11.11\%$

## Explanation

The payment at time zero enters at face value, so the equation is $100+100v=180$. This gives $v=0.8$ and therefore
$$
i=\frac1v-1=\frac{1}{0.8}-1=0.25=25\%.
$$
The value $20\%$ comes from $1-v$, confusing the interest rate with the discount rate. The value $7.32\%$ treats the installments as payable in arrears and solves $100(v+v^2)=180$.
