At an effective annual rate of $20\%$, two annual payments of $144$ euros in advance are worth more than the same payments in arrears. By how much?

- $44$ euros
- $68$ euros
- $24$ euros
- $20$ euros

## Explanation

The annuity due pays at times $0$ and $1$, and the ordinary annuity at times $1$ and $2$. The common payment at time $1$ cancels. Today's payment of $144$ euros and the payment at time $2$, worth $\dfrac{144}{1.2^2}=100$ euros today, remain. Their difference is $144-100=44$ euros. The value of $24$ euros compares today's payment with the one at time $1$, worth $120$ euros today.
