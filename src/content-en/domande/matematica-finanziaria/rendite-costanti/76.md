A debt of $3000$ euros bears an effective annual interest rate of $5\%$. The first installment is $100$ euros. What balance remains immediately afterward?

- $2950$ euros
- $3150$ euros
- $2900$ euros
- $3050$ euros

## Explanation

On the first due date the balance before payment is $3000\cdot1.05=3150$ euros. Paying $100$ euros leaves $3150-100=3050$ euros. The debt has grown because the payment falls short of the period's interest, $150$ euros, corresponding to case $R<Ci$. The value of $2900$ euros subtracts the payment from the principal without adding interest.
