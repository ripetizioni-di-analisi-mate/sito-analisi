A saver wants $364.10$ euros at the end of year three and will deposit the same amount at the beginning of each year. Starting from zero at an effective annual rate of $10\%$, what deposit is required?

- $90$ euros
- $121$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $110$ euros

## Explanation

The deposits occur at times $0,1,2$ and the balance is measured at time $3$, so they earn interest for three, two, and one years, respectively. The factor is
$$
q\,s_{\overline3|0.1}=1.331+1.21+1.1=3.641.
$$
The equation $3.641\,R=364.10$ gives $R=100$ euros. The value of $110$ euros divides by $s_{\overline3|0.1}=3.31$, treating the deposits as payable in arrears.
