Three end-of-period repayments of $8$ euros are due one period apart. What is their present value at an effective rate of $-50\%$ per period?

- $24$ euros
- $64$ euros
- $112$ euros
- $56$ euros

## Explanation

With $i=-0.5$ we have $q=\dfrac12$ and therefore $v=2$. The present value is
$$
V(0)=8(2+2^2+2^3)=16+32+64=112\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
At a negative interest rate each repayment is worth more today than its face amount, and the most distant contributes the most. The value of $56$ euros uses the powers $v^0$, $v$, and $v^2$, treating the payments as made in advance. The nominal sum is $24$ euros.
