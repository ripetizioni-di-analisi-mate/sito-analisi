The present value of an additional final payment alone equals the original debt. What equal regular installments can be added on the earlier payment dates?

- The difference between the nominal final payment and the original debt.
- Zero.
- The value of the final payment divided by the number of installments.
- Each equals the full original debt.

## Explanation

The final payment and the debt have the same present value, leaving a present value of zero for the regular installments to cover. Their equation is $Ra_{\overline n|i}=0$. Since $a_{\overline n|i}$ is a sum of positive terms, the only solution is $R=0$. The nominal final payment exceeds the initial debt by the interest that accrues to its due date.
