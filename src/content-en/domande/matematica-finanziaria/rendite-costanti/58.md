A perpetuity due and its corresponding perpetuity payable in arrears each pay $75$ euros per year. How much do their present values differ at an effective annual rate of $6\%$?

- $0$ euros
- $75$ euros
- $1325$ euros
- $1250$ euros

## Explanation

From time $1$ onward the two schedules coincide, so those payments cancel in the difference. The perpetuity with an immediate first payment includes today's payment at face value. The difference is $75$ euros. Indeed, the two present values are $\dfrac{75}{0.06}=1250$ euros and $1250+75=1325$ euros.
