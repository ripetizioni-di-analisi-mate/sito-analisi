A loan with year-end installments has a theoretical term of $4.3$ years. If regular installments are paid through the last whole-year due date before that theoretical term, when is the remaining balance paid?

- At time $4.3$, an annual payment date.
- On the fourth due date, in addition to the fourth installment.
- On the fifth due date.
- On the sixth due date.

## Explanation

The theoretical term $4.3$ lies between $4$ and $5$, so regular installments are paid on the first four due dates. The balance after the fourth installment is positive. Accumulate it to the next annual payment date, time $5$, and pay it off then. Time $4.3$ is a theoretical term outside the annual payment schedule.
