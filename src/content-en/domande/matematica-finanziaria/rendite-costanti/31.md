An annuity due makes four annual payments starting today. How many years from today is the last payment due?

- $5$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

An annuity due with $n$ payments pays at times $0,1,\ldots,n-1$. With $n=4$, the payment dates are $0,1,2,3$, ending at time $3$. The value $4$ is the last date for a four-payment ordinary annuity. The dates differ by one period because each payment of the annuity due occurs one period earlier.
