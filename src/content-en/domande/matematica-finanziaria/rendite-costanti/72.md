The target amount $M$ at time $n$ is known, and $n$ equal deposits in arrears are to begin at time $1$. The rate is positive. Which equation represents this arrangement?

- $M=Rs_{\overline n|i}$
- $M=Rq s_{\overline n|i}$
- $M=Ra_{\overline n|i}$
- $M=Rv^{n+1} s_{\overline n|i}$

## Explanation

The principal $M$ is specified at time $n$, and the last deposit is made at time $n$. Each deposit earns interest for the time between its date and the final date, giving the factor $s_{\overline n|i}$ and the equation $M=Rs_{\overline n|i}$. The equation $M=Ra_{\overline n|i}$ would treat $M$ as a time-zero value. The factor $q\,s_{\overline n|i}$ would apply to deposits in advance at $0,\ldots,n-1$.
