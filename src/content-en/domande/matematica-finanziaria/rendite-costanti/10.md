Three annual payments of $363$ euros begin today. At an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$, what is the value of the remaining payments immediately after the first is received?

- $726$ euros
- $630$ euros
- $1089$ euros
- $993$ euros

## Explanation

The payment dates are $0,1,2$. Immediately after the payment at time zero, the installments at times $1$ and $2$ remain, forming a two-payment ordinary annuity.
$$
363\cdot\frac{10}{11}+363\cdot\left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^2=330+300=630\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $993$ euros still includes the payment already received, while $726$ euros adds the two remaining payments without discounting them.
