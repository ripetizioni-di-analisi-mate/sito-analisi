Payments of $121$ euros at times $1,2,3$ are valued together at time $2$. What is their value at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$?

- $364.1$ euros
- $363$ euros
- $231$ euros
- $110$ euros

## Explanation

To value the payments at time $2$, accumulate the one at time $1$ for one period, include the one at time $2$ at face value, and discount the one at time $3$ for one period.
$$
V(2)=121\cdot1.1+121+\frac{121}{1.1}=133.10+121+110=364.10\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $110$ euros counts only the future payment still to be received after the second installment. The nominal sum is $363$ euros.
