Three beginning-of-year payments are moved to the end of their respective years. At a positive interest rate, which operation gives the new present value from the original one?

- Multiply it by $v^3$.
- Multiply it by $q$.
- Multiply it by $v$.
- Leave it unchanged.

## Explanation

Moving a payment from the beginning to the end of the year delays it by one period and multiplies its present value by $v$. All three payments move, so the entire present value is multiplied by $v$, as $a_{\overline3|i}=v\,\ddot a_{\overline3|i}$ confirms. The factor $q$ moves each payment one period earlier, while $v^3$ would delay each by three periods.
