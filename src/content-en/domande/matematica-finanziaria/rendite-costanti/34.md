A fund receives three year-end deposits of $100$ euros. What is its value on the third deposit date at an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $300$ euros
- $331$ euros
- $364.1$ euros
- $330$ euros

## Explanation

The three deposits are made at times $1,2,3$ and the balance is measured at time $3$. They earn interest for two, one, and zero years.
$$
M=100\,s_{\overline3|0.1}=121+110+100=331\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $364.10$ euros accumulates each deposit for one extra year, as with an annuity due. The value of $330$ euros uses simple interest on each deposit instead of compound interest.
