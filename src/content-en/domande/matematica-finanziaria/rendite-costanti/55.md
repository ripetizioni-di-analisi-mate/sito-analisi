A perpetuity pays $25$ euros at the end of each quarter. What is its value just before the first quarter begins at an effective quarterly rate of $1\%$?

- $2525$ euros
- $625$ euros
- $2500$ euros
- $10000$ euros

## Explanation

Both the payment and the rate refer to a quarter, so no conversion is needed. The first payment is due at the end of the first quarter, giving an initial value of
$$
V(0)=\frac{25}{0.01}=2500\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $625$ euros uses the rate of $4\%$, combining a quarterly payment with an annual rate. The value of $2525$ euros adds an immediate payment.
