The target balance in one year is $620$ euros. The account starts with $200$ euros and earns an effective annual rate of $10\%$. What single year-end deposit is required?

- $440$ euros
- $420$ euros
- $400$ euros
- $620$ euros

## Explanation

The opening balance earns one year's interest and grows to $200\cdot1.1=220$ euros. The deposit is made on the target date and added at face value. The required deposit is $620-220=400$ euros. The value of $420$ euros omits interest on the opening balance, while $440$ euros also accrues interest on the deposit.
