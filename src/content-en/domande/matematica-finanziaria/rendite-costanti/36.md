At an effective annual rate of $10\%$, two schedules make deposits of $100$ euros at times $0,1$ and $1,2$, respectively. By how much do their accumulated values differ at time $2$?

- $21$ euros
- $10$ euros
- $20$ euros
- $31$ euros

## Explanation

The deposit at time $1$ is common to both schedules and cancels in their difference. The first schedule also has the deposit at time zero, worth $100\cdot1.1^2=121$ euros at time $2$. The second also has the deposit at time $2$, worth $100$ euros. The difference in accumulated values is $121-100=21$ euros. The value of $20$ euros uses simple interest on the first deposit.
