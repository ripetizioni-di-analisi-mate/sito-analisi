A debt of $520$ euros is repaid with two equal year-end installments and an additional payment of $121$ euros at the end of year two. Find the regular installment at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

- $299.62$ euros
- $242$ euros
- $210$ euros
- $229.9$ euros

## Explanation

The additional payment is due at time $2$ and is worth $121\cdot\left(\dfrac{10}{11}\right)^2=100$ euros today. The regular installments must cover $520-100=420$ euros. Since $a_{\overline2|0.1}=\dfrac{10}{11}+\dfrac{100}{121}=\dfrac{210}{121}$, the installment is
$$
R=420\cdot\frac{121}{210}=242\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $299.62$ euros divides the entire debt by the annuity factor, ignoring the additional payment.
