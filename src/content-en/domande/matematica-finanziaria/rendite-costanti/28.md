An annuity due is worth $399$ euros immediately before its first payment of $169$ euros today. What are the remaining payments worth immediately afterward?

- $169$ euros
- $399$ euros
- $230$ euros
- $568$ euros

## Explanation

Both values refer to time zero. The value of $399$ euros includes the payment due immediately, at face value of $169$ euros. After it is received, only future payments remain, worth $399-169=230$ euros. The value of $568$ euros adds the payment already received instead of subtracting it.
