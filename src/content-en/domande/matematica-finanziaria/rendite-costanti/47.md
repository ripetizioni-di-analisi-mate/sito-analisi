A calculation uses $v^3 R a_{\overline4|i}$. Which schedule of four payments does this expression represent?

- $1,2,3,4$
- $2,3,4,5$
- $4,5,6,7$
- $3,4,5,6$

## Explanation

The product $Ra_{\overline4|i}$ values four payments in arrears one period before the first. Multiplying by $v^3$ discounts a value at time $3$ to time zero. Thus the first payment is at time $4$ and the dates are $4,5,6,7$. The dates $3,4,5,6$ would require the factor $v^2$, since $Ra_{\overline4|i}$ would then refer to time $2$.
