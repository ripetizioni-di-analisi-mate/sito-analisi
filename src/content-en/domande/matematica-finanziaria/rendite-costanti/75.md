A debt of $2000$ euros bears annual interest at $5\%$ and receives year-end payments of $100$ euros. How does the balance immediately after each installment change from year to year?

- It decreases by $100$ euros each year.
- It stays at $2000$ euros.
- It is paid off on the twentieth due date.
- It increases by $100$ euros each year.

## Explanation

Each year the debt of $2000$ euros earns $2000\cdot0.05=100$ euros in interest. The installment of $100$ euros covers exactly that interest, leaving the same debt of $2000$ euros after every payment. This is case $R=Ci$ in the term theorem and the theoretical term is infinite. The ratio $\dfrac{2000}{100}=20$ would give the term only at a zero rate.
