Holding the original principal and positive rate fixed, the installment of a loan payable in arrears rises and continues to exceed first-period interest. What happens to the theoretical term?

- It stays the same.
- It decreases.
- It halves whenever the payment increases.
- It increases.

## Explanation

For a fixed term, the present value $Ra_{\overline n|i}$ increases with the payment. It also increases with the term because every additional payment contributes a positive amount. Increasing the payment covers the same principal in less time, so the theoretical term decreases. The formula gives the same result because $1-\dfrac{Ci}{R}$ increases with $R$ and therefore $-\log\left(1-\dfrac{Ci}{R}\right)$ decreases.
