A debt of $175$ euros is repaid with year-end installments of $100$ euros at a zero rate. What is its theoretical term before adjusting the payments to whole-year due dates?

- $1.75$ years
- $1$ year
- $0.57$ years
- $2$ years

## Explanation

At a zero rate $a_{\overline n|0}=n$, so $C=Ra_{\overline n|i}$ becomes $C=nR$. Thus $175=100n$ and the theoretical term is $n=1.75$ years. Since the term is fractional, the actual schedule consists of one regular payment and a smaller final payment on the next due date. The value of $0.57$ years uses the reciprocal ratio $\dfrac{100}{175}$.
