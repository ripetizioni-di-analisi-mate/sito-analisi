A debt of $175$ euros is repaid with year-end installments of $100$ euros at a zero rate. If the first installment is paid in full, what payment on the second due date settles the debt?

- $25$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $75$ euros
- $175$ euros

## Explanation

At a zero rate the debt earns no interest. The balance after the first installment is $175-100=75$ euros and is still $75$ euros at the second due date. The final payment is therefore $75$ euros, less than a regular installment. A full installment of $100$ euros would overpay the debt by $25$ euros. The amount $25$ euros is exactly that excess.
