A debt of $130$ euros is repaid with regular year-end installments of $100$ euros at an effective annual rate of $10\%$. What payment on the second due date settles the debt?

- $47.3$ euros
- $57.3$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $30$ euros

## Explanation

At time $1$ the debt is $130\cdot1.1=143$ euros and the regular installment leaves $43$ euros. That balance accrues interest for another year, so the amount due at the second payment date is
$$
43\cdot1.1=47.30\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $57.3$ euros accumulates the debt for two years and subtracts the first installment without accumulating it. The value of $30$ euros subtracts the payment from the principal without adding interest.
