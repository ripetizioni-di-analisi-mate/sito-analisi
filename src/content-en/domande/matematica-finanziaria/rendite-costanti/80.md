An account earns $10\%$ annually. A full €100 is withdrawn at each year-end for as long as the available balance allows. The initial balance is $B$ and overdrafts are forbidden. With $v=\dfrac1{1.10}$, which initial balances allow exactly two full withdrawals?

- $100(v+v^2)\le B<100(v+v^2+v^3)$
- $100(v+v^2)<B\le100(v+v^2+v^3)$
- $200\le B<300$
- $100v^2\le B<100v^3$

## Explanation

To fund the first two withdrawals the opening balance must at least match their present value, $100(v+v^2)$. Equality is sufficient. A balance of $100(v+v^2+v^3)$ already funds all three withdrawals, so this upper endpoint must be excluded.
