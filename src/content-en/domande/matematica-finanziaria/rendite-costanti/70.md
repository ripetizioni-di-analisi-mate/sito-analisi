Capital of $2000$ euros is available one period before the first payment on a perpetuity. What annual payment can it sustain at an effective annual rate of $5\%$?

- $200$ euros
- $95$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $105$ euros

## Explanation

The formula $\dfrac Ri$ values a perpetuity payable in arrears one period before the first payment, the date on which the principal is available. The equation $2000=\dfrac{R}{0.05}$ gives $R=2000\cdot0.05=100$ euros. The payment equals the interest earned annually on the principal, leaving $2000$ euros after every payment, so the payments can continue indefinitely.
