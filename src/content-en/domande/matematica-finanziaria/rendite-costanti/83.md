Three year-end payments of $85$ euros are exchanged for an immediate payment of $255$ euros. What interest rate makes the two schedules equivalent?

- $5\%$
- $-5\%$
- $15\%$
- $0\%$

## Explanation

At a zero rate the present value of the three installments is their nominal sum, $3\cdot85=255$ euros, equal to the immediate payment. The present value $V(i)$ decreases strictly and takes the value $255$ euros only once. The rate making the schedules equivalent is therefore $0\%$. A positive rate would give less than $255$ euros, and a negative rate more.
