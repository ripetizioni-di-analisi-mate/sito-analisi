An account receives three month-end deposits of $10$ euros and earns an effective monthly interest rate of $1\%$. What is the accumulated value immediately after the third deposit, to the nearest cent?

- $30.61$ euros
- $30.3$ euros
- $30$ euros
- $30.1$ euros

## Explanation

The third deposit falls on the valuation date, the second one month earlier, and the first two months earlier. The accumulated value is therefore
$$
M=10\cdot1.01^2+10\cdot1.01+10=10.201+10.10+10=30.301\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
To the nearest cent this is $30.30$ euros. The value of $30$ euros ignores all interest, while $30.10$ euros allows only one month's interest on the first deposit.
