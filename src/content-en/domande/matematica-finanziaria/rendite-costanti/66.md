At an effective annual rate of $10\%$, equal receipts due at times $2$ and $3$ are together worth $210$ euros at time zero. How large is each receipt?

- $133.1$ euros
- $121$ euros
- $105$ euros
- $110$ euros

## Explanation

The receipts are due at times $2$ and $3$, so the appropriate factor is
$$
v^2+v^3=\frac{1}{1.21}+\frac{1}{1.331}=\frac{2.1}{1.331}=\frac{2100}{1331}.
$$
The equation $R\cdot\dfrac{2100}{1331}=210$ gives $R=\dfrac{1331}{10}=133.10$ euros. The value of $121$ euros ignores the deferral and uses dates $1$ and $2$. The value of $105$ euros divides $210$ by two, ignoring interest.
