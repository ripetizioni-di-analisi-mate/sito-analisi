A schedule has three payments at times $4,5,6$. With $i>0$, which sum gives the value of the remaining payments immediately after the payment at time $4$?

- $R(q+q^2)$
- $R(v^4+v^5+v^6)$
- $R(1+v+v^2)$
- $R(v+v^2)$

## Explanation

After the payment at time $4$, the payments at times $5$ and $6$ remain. From the valuation date $4$ they are one and two periods away, so discount them by $v$ and $v^2$. Their value is $R(v+v^2)=Ra_{\overline2|i}$. The expression $R(1+v+v^2)$ includes the payment already made, while $R(v^4+v^5+v^6)$ is the time-zero value of the entire schedule.
