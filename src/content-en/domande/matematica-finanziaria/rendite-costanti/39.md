At an effective rate of $-20\%$, two annual deposits of $100$ euros are made at times $1,2$. What is the account balance immediately after the second deposit?

- $225$ euros
- $200$ euros
- $160$ euros
- $180$ euros

## Explanation

With $i=-0.2$ the accumulation factor is $q=0.8$. The first deposit earns one year's interest and becomes $100\cdot0.8=80$ euros. Add the second at face value at time $2$ to obtain the balance
$$
M=80+100=180\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $225$ euros divides by $0.8$ instead of multiplying, using $v$ in place of $q$. At a negative rate the accumulated value falls below the sum deposited.
