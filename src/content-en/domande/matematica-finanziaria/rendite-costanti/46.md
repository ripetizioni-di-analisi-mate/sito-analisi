A fund was due to receive €100 at each of the first three year-ends. At $10\%$ annual interest its balance just after year three is €110 below target. Exactly one deposit was missed. Which one?

- The first.
- The second.
- The third.
- Any one of the three is consistent.

## Explanation

At the third year-end the three deposits would have contributed €121, €110 and €100 respectively. The shortfall matches the deposit that would have earned one year of interest.
