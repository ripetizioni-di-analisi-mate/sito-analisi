Three payments of $100$ euros fall at times $4,5,6$. What is their combined value at time $6$ at an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $300$ euros
- $331$ euros
- $364.1$ euros
- $210$ euros

## Explanation

At time $6$, accumulate the payment from time $4$ for two years and the payment from time $5$ for one year. Include the payment at time $6$ at face value. This gives
$$
V(6)=100\cdot1.1^2+100\cdot1.1+100=121+110+100=331\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
This is the accumulated value $100\,s_{\overline3|0.1}$. Only the time from each payment to the valuation date enters the exponent. The value of $364.10$ euros accumulates each payment for one year too many.
