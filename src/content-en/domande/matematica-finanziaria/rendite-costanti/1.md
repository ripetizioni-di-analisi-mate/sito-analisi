Two equal annual payments of $363$ euros are due at the end of the next two years. Find their present value at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

- $693$ euros
- $726$ euros
- $572.73$ euros
- $630$ euros

## Explanation

The installments fall at times $1$ and $2$, so use the ordinary annuity factor with $v=\dfrac{10}{11}$.
$$
V(0)=363\cdot\frac{10}{11}+363\cdot\left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^2=330+300=630\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The undiscounted nominal sum is $726$ euros. The value of $572.73$ euros discounts each receipt for one extra period.
