For positive payments in arrears, a trial rate of $4\%$ gives a present value of $215$ euros and the price is $200$ euros. In which direction should the search for the implied rate proceed?

- The implied rate is exactly $4\%$.
- Among the rates below $4\%$.
- Among the rates above $4\%$.
- The direction cannot be determined without the number of installments.

## Explanation

The present value $V(i)$ decreases strictly as the rate rises. At $4\%$ the value is $215$ euros, above the price of $200$ euros. A higher rate is needed to bring the present value down to the price, so the implied rate exceeds $4\%$. This conclusion holds for any number of payments because every ordinary annuity with positive payments has this monotonicity property.
