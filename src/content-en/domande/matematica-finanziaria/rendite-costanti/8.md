Two annual payments of $363$ euros, due at the ends of years one and two, have a price of $630$ euros. Find the effective annual interest rate.

- $5\%$
- $0\%$
- $20\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

The price must equal $363(v+v^2)$. With $v=\dfrac{10}{11}$, the present values are $330$ and $300$ euros, summing to $630$ euros. Present value decreases strictly as the rate increases, so the unique solution is $i=v^{-1}-1=10\%$. A rate of $0\%$ would give the nominal sum of $726$ euros, above the price.
