A perpetuity pays $60$ euros annually, starting in one year, at an effective annual interest rate of $20\%$. What is the present value of the stream remaining after excluding its first two payments? Round to the nearest cent.

- $41.67$ euros
- $250$ euros
- $208.33$ euros
- $300$ euros

## Explanation

The remaining stream begins at time $3$, so the formula $\dfrac Ri$ gives its value at time $2$. Discounting for two years gives
$$
V(2)=\frac{60}{0.2}=300\,\mathrm{EUR},\qquad V(0)=\frac{300}{1.2^2}\approx208.33\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $250$ euros discounts for only one year. The value of $41.67$ euros is the present value of the second installment, one of the payments excluded.
