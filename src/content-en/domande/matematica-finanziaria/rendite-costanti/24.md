A quarterly annuity pays first in three months and last in eighteen months. How many payments does it make?

- $5$
- $7$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

Each quarter is three months. The first payment falls after $3$ months and the last after $18$ months, giving the dates
$$
3,\ 6,\ 9,\ 12,\ 15,\ 18\ \text{months}.
$$
There are therefore $\dfrac{18}{3}=6$ payments. The value $5$ comes from $\dfrac{18-3}{3}$, which counts the intervals between the first and last dates rather than the dates themselves.
