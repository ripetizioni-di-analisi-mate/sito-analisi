A debt of $331$ euros is repaid in three equal annual installments in advance, starting today. What is each installment at an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $121$ euros
- $133.10$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $146.41$ euros

## Explanation

The payments fall at times $0,1,2$, so use the annuity-due factor.
$$
\ddot a_{\overline3|0.1}=1+\frac{1}{1.1}+\frac{1}{1.21}=\frac{3.31}{1.21}.
$$
The equation $331=R\,\ddot a_{\overline3|0.1}$ gives $R=331\cdot\dfrac{1.21}{3.31}=121$ euros. The value of $133.10$ euros uses an ordinary annuity with payments at $1,2,3$. The value of $100$ euros divides by $s_{\overline3|0.1}=3.31$, treating the principal as an accumulated value.
