Three annual payments in advance are worth $331$ euros today at an annual rate of $10\%$. By how much does their present value change if each payment moves one year later? Round to the nearest cent.

- $33.1$ euros
- $0$ euros
- $82.31$ euros
- $30.09$ euros

## Explanation

Delaying every payment by one year multiplies the present value by $v=\dfrac{1}{1.1}$. The new value is $\dfrac{331}{1.1}\approx300.91$ euros, so the change is
$$
331-300.91=30.09\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $33.10$ euros comes from $331\cdot0.1$, one year's interest on the annuity-due value, rather than the difference between the two present values.
