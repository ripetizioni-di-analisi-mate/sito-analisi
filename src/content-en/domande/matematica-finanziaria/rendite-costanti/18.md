For two semiannual installments the quoted effective annual rate is $21\%$. What is the equivalent effective semiannual rate?

- $4.41\%$
- $10.5\%$
- $21\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

The period is six months, and there are two periods per year. The semiannual rate equivalent to the effective annual rate satisfies
$$
(1+i)^2=1.21,
$$
so $1+i=1.1$ and $i=10\%$. The value $10.5\%$ divides the annual rate by two, a conversion that applies to a nominal annual rate compounded semiannually. The given annual rate is effective.
