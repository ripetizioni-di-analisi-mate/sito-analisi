Two equal receipts at times $1$ and $2$ are discounted to time zero at a positive interest rate. Which contributes more to their present value?

- The second, because it earns interest for one extra period.
- The first, because it is discounted for only one period.
- Their contributions are equal because their amounts are equal.
- The second, because it is discounted for two periods.

## Explanation

At a positive rate the discount factor satisfies $0<v<1$, so $v^2<v$. The receipt at time $1$ contributes $Rv$ and the one at time $2$ contributes $Rv^2$. The first contributes more because it is discounted for just one period. The second arrives a period later and has a smaller present value because it is discounted once more.
