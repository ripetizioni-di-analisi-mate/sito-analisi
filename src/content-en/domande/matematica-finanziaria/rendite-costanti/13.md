A perpetuity pays $80$ euros at time zero and annually thereafter. Find its present value, including the first payment, at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

- $720$ euros
- $960$ euros
- $800$ euros
- $880$ euros

## Explanation

The payments from time $1$ onward form a perpetuity payable in arrears, worth $\dfrac{80}{0.1}=800$ euros today. Add the time-zero payment at face value to obtain
$$
V(0)=80+800=880\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $800$ euros omits today's payment, while $720$ euros subtracts it instead of adding it.
