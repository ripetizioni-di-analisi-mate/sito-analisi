Two deposits in advance are made at times $0$ and $1$. With $i>0$, which factor multiplies the deposit amount to give the accumulated value immediately after the last deposit?

- $q+q^2$
- $1+q$
- $v+v^2$
- $1+v$

## Explanation

At time $1$ the first deposit has earned one period's interest and is worth $Rq$. The second is made at time $1$ and is worth $R$. Immediately after the last deposit, the accumulated value is $R(1+q)=Rs_{\overline2|i}$. The factor $q+q^2$ gives the value at time $2$, one period after the final deposit, while $1+v$ is the present-value factor for the annuity due.
