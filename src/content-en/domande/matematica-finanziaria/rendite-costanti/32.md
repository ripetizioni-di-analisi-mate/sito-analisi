A single payment of $500$ euros is due immediately. What happens to its price just before receipt if the valuation rate rises?

- It stays at $500$ euros.
- It falls because the discount factor decreases.
- It falls only if the initial rate is positive.
- It rises because the accumulation factor increases.

## Explanation

The payment is due today, so its time-zero valuation factor is $q^0=1$ at every rate $i>-1$. Its price is therefore $500$ euros regardless of the rate. The discount factor decreases as the rate rises, but its exponent here is zero. For the same reason, the first payment of an annuity due always enters its present value at face value.
