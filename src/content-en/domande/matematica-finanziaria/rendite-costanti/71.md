Two schedules use deposits in arrears, the same positive rate, and the same target date. One starts earlier and has more deposits. Which needs the lower deposit to reach the same balance?

- The payment is the same.
- Which payment is lower depends on the target amount.
- The schedule with fewer deposits.
- The schedule with more deposits.

## Explanation

At the common target date the accumulated value is $M=R\,s_{\overline n|i}$ with $s_{\overline n|i}=1+q+\cdots+q^{n-1}$. The earlier schedule has more deposits and its factor includes more positive terms, so the factor is larger. The payment $R=\dfrac{M}{s_{\overline n|i}}$ is therefore smaller for the schedule with more deposits. This comparison holds for any target $M$ because it depends only on the factor.
