An ordinary annuity makes its first payment at time $5$. To what date does the value obtained using the factor $a_{\overline n|i}$ alone refer?

- At time $4$.
- At time $5$.
- At time zero.
- At time $6$.

## Explanation

The factor $a_{\overline n|i}$ values an ordinary annuity one period before the first payment. That payment is at time $5$, so $Ra_{\overline n|i}$ refers to time $5-1=4$. Multiply by $v^4$ to obtain today's value. Time $5$ is the valuation date for the annuity-due factor $\ddot a_{\overline n|i}$.
