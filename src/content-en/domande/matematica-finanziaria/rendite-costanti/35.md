At time $2$ an account holds $500$ euros immediately after its final deposit. What is its balance at time $4$ with no further deposits and an effective annual rate of $10\%$?

- $550$ euros
- $600$ euros
- $605$ euros
- $413.22$ euros

## Explanation

After the last deposit the balance stays invested from time $2$ to time $4$, two years with no further deposits. Compounding multiplies it by $q^2$, giving
$$
500\cdot1.1^2=605\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $600$ euros uses simple interest, adding $50$ euros twice without earning interest on interest. The value of $550$ euros accumulates for only one year.
