Three annual payments in advance are the same size as three corresponding payments in arrears worth $600$ euros today. What are the payments in advance worth at an effective annual interest rate of $5\%$?

- $571.43$ euros
- $600$ euros
- $605$ euros
- $630$ euros

## Explanation

The payments in advance fall at times $0,1,2$, one period before the corresponding payments in arrears at $1,2,3$. Multiply each present value by $q$, as confirmed by $\ddot a_{\overline3|i}=q\,a_{\overline3|i}$. The value is $600\cdot1.05=630$ euros. Dividing by $q$ gives $571.43$ euros and would move each payment one period later.
