One bisection step is used to bracket the implied rate of an ordinary annuity with positive payments between $2\%$ and $8\%$. At the midpoint rate of $5\%$ the present value is below the price. Which subinterval is retained?

- From $5\%$ to $8\%$.
- From $2\%$ to $8\%$.
- From $2\%$ to $5\%$.
- From $0\%$ to $2\%$.

## Explanation

Present value decreases strictly as the rate rises. At $5\%$ it falls below the price, so a lower rate is needed to reach that price. The solution is below $5\%$ and we retain the interval from $2\%$ to $5\%$. We would retain the interval from $5\%$ to $8\%$ if the midpoint value exceeded the price. The iteration reduces the interval width from $6$ to $3$ percentage points.
