A foundation pays $40$ euros annually in perpetuity starting at the end of year one. What deposit today can fund those payments at an effective annual return of $4\%$?

- $4000$ euros
- $1040$ euros
- $960$ euros
- $1000$ euros

## Explanation

Payments begin at the end of year one and continue indefinitely. The present value of this perpetuity payable in arrears is
$$
V(0)=\frac Ri=\frac{40}{0.04}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The deposit of $1000$ euros earns $40$ euros of annual interest, covering each payment while preserving the principal. The value of $1040$ euros includes an immediate payment absent from the schedule.
