A perpetuity with its first payment one year from now is worth $1200$ euros today. What is its value immediately before the first payment at an effective annual rate of $5\%$?

- $1260$ euros
- $1142.86$ euros
- $1140$ euros
- $1200$ euros

## Explanation

Accumulate today's value for one year to obtain the value at time $1$ immediately before the first payment. This gives
$$
V(1)=1200\cdot1.05=1260\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The payment of $R=1200\cdot0.05=60$ euros gives the same result because $V(1)=R+\dfrac Ri=60+1200$. The value of $1142.86$ euros divides by $1.05$, discounting instead of accumulating.
