Three payments of $175$ euros are due at the ends of three successive months. What is their value at time zero if the effective monthly interest rate is zero?

- $700$ euros
- $350$ euros
- $525$ euros
- $175$ euros

## Explanation

At a zero interest rate the discount factor is $v=1$ and each repayment is worth its face amount today. The ordinary annuity factor is therefore $a_{\overline3|0}=3$ and the present value is $3\cdot175=525$ euros. The values of $350$ and $700$ euros count one fewer or one more repayment than the three scheduled.
