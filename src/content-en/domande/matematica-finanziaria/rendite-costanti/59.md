A perpetuity paying $R$ each period begins at time $5$. Which expression gives its value today with $i>0$?

- $v^5\dfrac Ri$
- $v\dfrac Ri$
- $q^4\dfrac Ri$
- $v^4\dfrac Ri$

## Explanation

The formula $\dfrac Ri$ values a perpetuity one period before its first payment. The first payment is at time $5$, so $\dfrac Ri$ gives the value at time $4$. Discounting it for four periods to time zero gives $V(0)=v^4\dfrac Ri$. The expression $v^5\dfrac Ri$ would place the first payment at time $6$.
