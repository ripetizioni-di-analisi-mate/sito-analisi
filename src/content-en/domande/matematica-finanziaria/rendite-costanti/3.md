An account starts with a zero balance and receives a deposit of $150$ euros at the end of each of the first two years. At an effective annual interest rate of $20\%$, what is the balance immediately after the second deposit?

- $360$ euros
- $300$ euros
- $330$ euros
- $396$ euros

## Explanation

The first deposit at time $1$ earns one year's interest and is worth $150\cdot1.2=180$ euros at time $2$. The second deposit at time $2$ is added without interest. The balance is $150\,s_{\overline2|0.2}=180+150=330$ euros. The value of $360$ euros also accrues interest on the second deposit, while $300$ euros simply adds the deposits without interest.
