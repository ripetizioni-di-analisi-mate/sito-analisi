Two otherwise identical positive annuities differ only in that one starts one period later. The periodic rate is negative and greater than $-100\%$. Which annuity is worth more today?

- The number of payments is needed for the comparison.
- The annuity with the later payments.
- The annuity with the earlier payments.
- Both annuities have the same value.

## Explanation

For $-1<i<0$, we have $0<q<1$ and hence $v=\dfrac1q>1$. Delaying every payment by one period multiplies present value by $v$. Since $v>1$, the value increases and the deferred annuity is worth more. At a positive rate the ordering reverses. The same factor $v$ applies to all payments, so the comparison is independent of their number.
