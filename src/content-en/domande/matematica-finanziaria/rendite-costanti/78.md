A debt of $1000$ euros is repaid in year-end installments of $150$ euros at an effective annual rate of $5\%$. Which expression gives the theoretical term in years?

- $\dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 1.05}$
- $\dfrac{1000}{150}$
- $\dfrac{\ln 1.05}{\ln 1.5}$
- $\dfrac{\ln 1.5}{\ln 1.05}$

## Explanation

The equation is $1000=150\,\dfrac{1-1.05^{-n}}{0.05}$. Since $\dfrac{Ci}{R}=\dfrac{1000\cdot0.05}{150}=\dfrac13$, isolating the power gives $1.05^{-n}=1-\dfrac13=\dfrac23$. Taking logarithms gives
$$
n=-\frac{\ln\frac23}{\ln1.05}=\frac{\ln1.5}{\ln1.05}\approx8.31\ \text{years}.
$$
The expression containing $\ln3$ uses $\dfrac{Ci}{R}$ instead of $1-\dfrac{Ci}{R}$, while $\dfrac{1000}{150}$ is the term at a zero interest rate.
