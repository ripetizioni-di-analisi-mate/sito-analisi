An ordinary annuity costs $250$ euros. Its present value is $275$ euros at $3\%$ and $230$ euros at $7\%$. Which interval contains the implied rate?

- Between zero and $3\%$.
- Between $-100\%$ and zero.
- Above $7\%$.
- Between $3\%$ and $7\%$.

## Explanation

Present value is continuous and strictly decreasing in the rate. At $3\%$ the value is $275$ euros, above the price, and at $7\%$ it is $230$ euros, below the price. By continuity a rate between $3\%$ and $7\%$ gives a value of $250$ euros. Strict monotonicity makes it unique. The implied rate therefore lies between $3\%$ and $7\%$.
