A perpetuity pays $110$ euros annually, starting at the end of the second year. What is its present value at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$?

- $909.09$ euros
- $1000$ euros
- $1210$ euros
- $1100$ euros

## Explanation

The first payment is at time $2$. The formula $\dfrac Ri$ values the perpetuity one period earlier, at time $1$, giving
$$
V(1)=\frac{110}{0.1}=1100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Discounting for one year gives $V(0)=\dfrac{1100}{1.1}=1000$ euros. The value of $1100$ euros overlooks the deferral, while $909.09$ euros discounts for two years instead of one.
