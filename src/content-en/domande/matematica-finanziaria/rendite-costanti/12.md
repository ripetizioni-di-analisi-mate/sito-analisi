An ordinary annuity with four payments is deferred for two periods from time zero. On what dates are its payments due?

- $3,4,5,6$
- $4,5,6,7$
- $2,3,4,5$
- $3,4,5,6,7$

## Explanation

An ordinary annuity deferred for $m$ periods pays at times $m+1,\ldots,m+n$. With $m=2$ and $n=4$, the first payment is at $3$ and the last at $2+4=6$, giving the dates $3,4,5,6$. The dates $2,3,4,5$ describe an annuity due deferred for two periods, while $4,5,6,7$ corresponds to a three-period deferral.
