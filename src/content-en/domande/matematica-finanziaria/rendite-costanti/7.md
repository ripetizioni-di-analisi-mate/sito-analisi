A debt of $420$ euros is repaid in equal year-end installments of $242$ euros at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$. How many installments are required?

- $1$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Compare the debt with the present value of the installments. The first payment is worth $242\cdot\dfrac{10}{11}=220$ euros today and the second $242\cdot\left(\dfrac{10}{11}\right)^2=200$ euros. Since $220+200=420$ euros, two payments have the same present value as the debt. One payment has a present value of $220$ euros, leaving $200$ euros of the debt uncovered.
