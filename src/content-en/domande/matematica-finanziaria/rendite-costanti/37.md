Six equal deposits are made in arrears. For how many periods does the first earn interest if the balance is measured immediately after the sixth?

- Five periods.
- Six periods.
- Four periods.
- One period.

## Explanation

Of six deposits made in arrears, the first occurs at time $1$ and the last at time $6$. The accumulated value is measured at time $6$, so the first deposit earns interest for $6-1=5$ periods. It contributes $Rq^5$, the term with the largest exponent in $s_{\overline6|i}=1+q+\cdots+q^5$. Six periods of interest would require a deposit at time zero, as in a schedule with deposits in advance.
