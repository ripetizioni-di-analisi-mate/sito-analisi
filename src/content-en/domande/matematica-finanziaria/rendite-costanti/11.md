Deposits of $100$ euros are made at times $0$ and $1$. The effective annual interest rate is $10\%$ and the account starts with a zero balance. What is the balance at time $2$?

- $200$ euros
- $242$ euros
- $210$ euros
- $231$ euros

## Explanation

The first deposit stays invested from time $0$ to time $2$ and grows to $100\cdot1.1^2=121$ euros. The second grows to $110$ euros in one year. The balance is $100\,q\,s_{\overline2|0.1}=121+110=231$ euros. The value of $210$ euros is the balance at time $1$, immediately after the last deposit, and leaves out the following year.
