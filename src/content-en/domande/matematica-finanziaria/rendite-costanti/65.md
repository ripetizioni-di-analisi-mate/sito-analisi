A debt of $630$ euros is repaid in three equal monthly installments at a zero effective monthly rate. What is each installment?

- $630$ euros
- $315$ euros
- $157.5$ euros
- $210$ euros

## Explanation

At a zero rate $a_{\overline3|0}=3$, so $C=Ra_{\overline n|i}$ reduces to $630=3R$. Each payment is $\dfrac{630}{3}=210$ euros. Their sum equals the debt, since without interest an amount has the same value at every date. The value of $315$ euros divides the balance by two payments instead of three.
