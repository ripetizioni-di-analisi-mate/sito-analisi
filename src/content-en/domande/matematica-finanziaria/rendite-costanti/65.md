An interest-free debt of €100 must be repaid in three monthly instalments. The system accepts whole cents only. The first two payments must be equal and the third may differ from them by at most one cent. Which schedule works?

- $33.34$; $33.34$; $33.32$
- $33.33$; $33.33$; $33.33$
- $33.34$; $33.33$; $33.33$
- $33.33$; $33.33$; $33.34$

## Explanation

In cents, $10000=3\cdot3333+1$. Paying $3333$ cents twice leaves $3334$ for the final payment. The total is exact and the payments differ by a single cent.
