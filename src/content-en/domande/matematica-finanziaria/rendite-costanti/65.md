An account earning an effective annual rate of $6\%$ holds $4000$ euros today. To have $10000$ euros in five years, the same amount is added at the end of each year. It is given that $1.06^5\approx1.3382$ and $s_{\overline5|0.06}\approx5.6371$. What amount is deposited each year, rounded to the nearest euro?

- $1064$ euros
- $1774$ euros
- $929$ euros
- $824$ euros

## Explanation

The initial balance earns interest for five years, so at time $5$ it is worth $4000\cdot1.3382=5352.80$ euros. The five end-of-year deposits must cover the rest of the target, that is, $10000-5352.80=4647.20$ euros on the date of the fifth deposit. The deposit therefore satisfies $4647.20=R\,s_{\overline5|0.06}$, which gives
$$
R=\frac{4647.20}{5.6371}\approx824\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $1064$ euros subtracts the $4000$ euros from the target without accumulating them, while $1774$ euros ignores the initial balance. The value of $929$ euros divides the rest of the target by five and ignores the interest on the deposits.
