For $i>0$, which expression gives the present value of three equal payments of $R$ in arrears?

- $R(1+v+v^2)$
- $R(1+q+q^2)$
- $R(v+v^2+v^3)$
- $R(v^2+v^3+v^4)$

## Explanation

A three-payment ordinary annuity pays at times $1,2,3$. Discounting each payment for the time from zero to its due date gives
$$
V(0)=R(v+v^2+v^3)=Ra_{\overline3|i}.
$$
The expression $R(1+v+v^2)$ uses the dates $0,1,2$, those of an annuity due. The expression $R(1+q+q^2)$ gives the accumulated value at time $3$, a different valuation date.
