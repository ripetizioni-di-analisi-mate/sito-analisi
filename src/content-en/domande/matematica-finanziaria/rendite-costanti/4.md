Two annual payments of $1331$ euros are due at times $2$ and $3$. Find their value at time zero at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

- $1909.09$ euros
- $2100$ euros
- $2310$ euros
- $2662$ euros

## Explanation

The first payment is due at time $2$, making this an ordinary annuity deferred for one period. Discount each payment for the time between its due date and time zero.
$$
V(0)=\frac{1331}{1.1^2}+\frac{1331}{1.1^3}=1100+1000=2100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $2310$ euros ignores the deferral and uses times $1$ and $2$. The value of $1909.09$ euros discounts for one period too many.
