A receivable of $120$ euros due in one year is bought for $100$ euros. What is the implied effective annual interest rate?

- $20\%$
- $10\%$
- $25\%$
- $16.67\%$

## Explanation

The payment is due at time $1$, giving the equation of value $100(1+i)=120$. Thus $1+i=\dfrac{120}{100}=1.2$ and the implied interest rate is $20\%$. The value $16.67\%$ divides interest of $20$ euros by $120$ euros, expressing it as a fraction of the final amount rather than the price paid. That fraction is the discount rate for the transaction.
