A schedule calls for four quarterly payments. The nominal annual interest rate compounded quarterly is $12\%$. Which effective rate belongs in the annuity factor?

- $1\%$
- $4\%$
- $3\%$
- $12\%$

## Explanation

The nominal annual rate compounded quarterly is $j^{(4)}=12\%$. Divide by the number of quarters per year to find the effective quarterly rate, giving
$$
i=\frac{j^{(4)}}{4}=\frac{0.12}{4}=0.03=3\%.
$$
The payment period is one quarter, so this is the rate used in the annuity factor. The value $12\%$ treats the annual rate as quarterly, while $1\%$ divides by twelve as for a monthly rate.
