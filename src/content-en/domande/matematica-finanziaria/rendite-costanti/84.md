Three payments of $100$ euros in arrears have a price of $340$ euros. What is the sign of the implied effective rate?

- The price is incompatible with every rate greater than $-100\%$.
- Zero.
- Negative.
- Positive.

## Explanation

At a zero rate the three installments are worth $3\cdot100=300$ euros and the price of $340$ euros exceeds this nominal sum. Since $V(i)$ is strictly decreasing, the rate satisfying $V(i)=340$ must be negative. The price is compatible with a rate greater than $-100\%$ because $V(i)\to+\infty$ as $i\to-1^+$, so every positive price is attained.
