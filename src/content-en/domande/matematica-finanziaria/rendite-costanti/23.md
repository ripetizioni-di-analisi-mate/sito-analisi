At a positive annual interest rate, how does the present value of eight year-end payments of $50$ euros compare with their nominal sum of $400$ euros?

- The present value is exactly $400$ euros.
- The present value is strictly less than $400$ euros.
- The present value is strictly greater than $400$ euros.
- Depending on the payments, the present value can be above or below $400$ euros.

## Explanation

All eight payments fall after today. At a positive rate every discount factor $v^k$ is less than one, so each payment contributes less than $50$ euros to present value. Adding eight amounts below $50$ euros gives $V(0)<8\cdot50=400$ euros. The present value equals the nominal sum at a zero rate and exceeds it only at a negative rate.
