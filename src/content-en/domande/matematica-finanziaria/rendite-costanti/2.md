A payment of $242$ euros is due today and again one and two years from today. What is the present value immediately before the first payment at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$?

- $662$ euros
- $726$ euros
- $601.82$ euros
- $420$ euros

## Explanation

The payments fall at times $0,1,2$. This is an annuity due and today's payment enters at face value.
$$
V(0)=242+242\cdot\frac{10}{11}+242\cdot\left(\frac{10}{11}\right)^2=242+220+200=662\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value of $601.82$ euros treats the payments as payable in arrears and discounts the first one. The value of $420$ euros omits today's payment.
