Six equal quarterly payments start eleven months from today. A table gives their value $A$ one quarter before the first payment. With effective quarterly rate $j$ and compound interest for fractional quarters, how do you obtain today's value?

- $A(1+j)^{-11/3}$
- $A(1+j)^{-8}$
- $A(1+j)^{-8/3}$
- $A(1+j)^{8/3}$

## Explanation

The table values the payments at month $11-3=8$. That is $\dfrac83$ quarters from today. Discount $A$ over that number of quarters using the quarterly rate $j$.
