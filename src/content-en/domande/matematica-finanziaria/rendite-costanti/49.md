The first of five quarterly payments is due in one year. To which month does the value calculated using the ordinary annuity factor refer?

- In month three.
- At time zero.
- In month nine.
- In month twelve.

## Explanation

The annuity period is a quarter, or three months. The ordinary annuity factor $a_{\overline5|i}$ gives the value one period before the first payment. Since the first payment falls in month twelve, the value refers to month $12-3=9$. Month twelve is the annuity-due valuation date. Month three counts one quarter from time zero instead of counting back from the first payment.
