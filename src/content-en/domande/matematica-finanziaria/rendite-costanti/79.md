For a debt of $C$ euros at rate $i>0$ with installment $R>0$, when is the quantity $1-\dfrac{Ci}{R}$ in the term formula positive?

- When $R=i$.
- When $R>Ci$.
- When $R=Ci$.
- When $R<Ci$.

## Explanation

The term formula contains $\log\left(1-\dfrac{Ci}{R}\right)$, whose argument must be positive. Since $R>0$, multiplying by $R$ makes $1-\dfrac{Ci}{R}>0$ equivalent to $R-Ci>0$, or $R>Ci$. The product $Ci$ is the first period's interest, so the payment must exceed that interest. With $R=Ci$ the argument is zero and the debt remains constant.
