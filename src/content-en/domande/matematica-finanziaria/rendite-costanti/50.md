Four payments of $45$ euros are deferred for three periods. The interest rate is zero. What are the payments worth today?

- $180$ euros
- $90$ euros
- $225$ euros
- $135$ euros

## Explanation

At a zero rate, $v=1$ and therefore $v^3=1$. Deferral leaves the value unchanged. The annuity factor equals $a_{\overline4|0}=4$, so the present value is
$$
V(0)=v^3\cdot45\,a_{\overline4|0}=4\cdot45=180\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The values of $135$ and $225$ euros count three and five payments in place of four, confusing the payment count with the number of periods of deferral.
