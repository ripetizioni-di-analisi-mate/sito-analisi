A bullet loan of $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ repays all principal after two years. What is its principal-only value at inception at an annual valuation rate of $10\%$?

- $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A bullet loan has a single principal repayment, the entire amount of $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of the second year. The principal-only value is its value at inception, giving
$$
N=\frac{2420}{1.1^2}=\frac{2420}{1.21}=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for only one year. The value $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the nominal amount.
