A balance of $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ immediately after an installment is refinanced with two equal installments at $10\%$. The desired installment is $605\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What additional payment must be made immediately?

- $890\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1495\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At $10\%$ the two new installments of $605\,\mathrm{EUR}$ can repay a debt equal to their present value, namely
$$
605\cdot\left(\frac{1}{1.1}+\frac{1}{1.21}\right)=550+500=1050\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The immediate payment covers the remainder of the balance and equals $2100-1050=1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $890\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the nominal installments without discounting. The value $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts both installments for one year.
