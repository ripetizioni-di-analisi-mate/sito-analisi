Two successive principal portions of a level-payment loan are $1500$ and $1590\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the effective interest rate per period.

- $106\%$
- $5.66\%$
- $2.96\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

Successive principal portions of a level-payment loan satisfy $C_{k+1}=(1+i)\,C_k$. Thus
$$
1+i=\frac{1590}{1500}=1.06,
$$
so the rate per period is $i=6\%$. The value $106\%$ omits the subtraction of one. The value $5.66\%$ divides the difference $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ by the second principal portion instead of the first.
