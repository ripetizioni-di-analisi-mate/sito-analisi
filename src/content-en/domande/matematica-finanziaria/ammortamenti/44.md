A four-year bullet loan of $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $5\%$. How much total interest is paid to the lender?

- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1293.04\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $6300\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The bullet loan's balance remains $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ for all four years because the principal is repaid only at maturity. Each year's interest payment is therefore $0.05\cdot6000=300\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving total interest of
$$
4\cdot300=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1293.04\,\mathrm{EUR}$ compounds the interest as if it were left unpaid every year. The value $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is one year's interest.
