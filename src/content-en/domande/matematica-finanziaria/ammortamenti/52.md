A level-payment loan is paid off by the last installment of $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The interest rate is $10\%$ per period. What balance remained immediately after the penultimate installment?

- $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $968\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $88\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

After the penultimate installment, only the final payment of $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains, due in one period. Its present value at the contractual rate is the prospective balance, giving
$$
D_{n-1}=\frac{880}{1.1}=800\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The last installment therefore consists of $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal and $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of interest. The value $968\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies by $1.1$ instead of dividing.
