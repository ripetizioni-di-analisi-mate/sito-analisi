A fund earns $10\%$ annually and receives $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at each of three year-ends. What is its balance immediately after the last deposit?

- $3310\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3641\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3210\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At the third due date the first deposit has earned two years' interest, the second one year's interest, and the third none. The accumulated value is
$$
1000\,(1.1^2+1.1+1)=1000\cdot3.31=3310\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $3000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the sum deposited without interest. The value $3641\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accrues interest even on the third deposit, as if payments were made at the beginning of each year.
