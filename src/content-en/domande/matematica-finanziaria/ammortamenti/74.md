In an equal-principal loan the original principal, payment dates, and valuation rate remain fixed. Only the contractual rate rises. Which component is unchanged?

- The interest-only value.
- Each full installment.
- The combined value of all installments.
- The principal-only value.

## Explanation

In an equal-principal loan the principal portions are $C=\dfrac{D_0}{n}$ and depend only on the original principal and the number of installments. The principal-only value $N(j)=C\,a_{\overline n|j}$ uses those portions and the valuation rate, so it stays unchanged. Raising the contractual rate increases the interest portions. The interest-only value, each full installment, and their combined value all increase.
