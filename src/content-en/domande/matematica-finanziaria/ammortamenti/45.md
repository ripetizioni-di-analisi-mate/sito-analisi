A loan of $4600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $5\%$. The borrower pays interest and makes annual deposits in a non-interest-bearing fund to repay the principal in four years. What is the annual outlay?

- $1150\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1380\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $230\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1610\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The fund earns no interest, so $s_{\overline 4|0}=4$ and each deposit is $Q=\dfrac{4600}{4}=1150\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The entire principal $4600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains owed to the lender until maturity, giving annual interest of $0.05\cdot4600=230\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The borrower's annual outlay is
$$
1150+230=1380\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1150\,\mathrm{EUR}$ includes only the deposit into the fund.
