A renegotiated loan bears positive interest. The regular installment is unchanged and exceeds first-period interest. Immediately before the final payment the accumulated debt is less than a regular installment. What amount pays off the loan exactly?

- The full regular installment.
- The balance after the penultimate installment without the interest accrued since.
- Only the interest for the final period.
- The accumulated balance immediately before the final payment.

## Explanation

Immediately before the final payment the debt is $qD_{n-1}$, the balance after the penultimate installment plus the final period's interest. To leave $D_n=0$, the balance equation $D_n=qD_{n-1}-R_n$ requires $R_n=qD_{n-1}$. Thus the payoff equals the accumulated balance. The full regular installment would exceed the amount owed.
