A level-payment loan bears a positive effective rate. Which sequence increases geometrically?

- The interest portions.
- The total installments.
- The positive outstanding balances.
- The principal portions.

## Explanation

In a level-payment loan $C_{k+1}=qC_k$ with $q=1+i>1$, so the principal portions form a strictly increasing geometric sequence. The interest portions are $I_k=R-C_k$ and decrease because $R$ is constant. Total installments are all equal. The outstanding balances also decrease because each principal portion is positive.
