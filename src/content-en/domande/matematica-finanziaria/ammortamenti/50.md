Two funds earn the same positive rate and must reach the same target on the same date. Each receives two equal deposits over the same periods. One receives deposits at the beginning of the periods and the other at the end. Which requires smaller deposits?

- The fund with deposits in arrears.
- The fund with deposits in advance.
- Both require the same deposits.
- The comparison depends on the loan rate.

## Explanation

Both funds must reach the same balance on the same date. With deposits in advance each deposit earns one additional period of interest, so one euro deposited accumulates to $1+j$ times as much. At a positive rate the annuity-due accumulation factor exceeds the ordinary annuity factor. The advance deposits are therefore smaller, in the ratio $\dfrac{1}{1+j}$.
