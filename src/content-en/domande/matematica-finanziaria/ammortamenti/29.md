A €1000 loan is repaid in two equal annual ordinary principal portions plus a €400 balloon on the second date. Interest is charged at $10\%$ per year on outstanding principal. A spreadsheet incorrectly halves the entire initial principal to obtain the ordinary principal portions. What is the interest in the second payment after correcting this error?

- €50
- €70
- €60
- €40

## Explanation

The €400 balloon is already part of the €1000 principal. That leaves €600 for two ordinary principal payments of €300 each. The balance after the first is €700, which incurs €70 of interest in year two.
