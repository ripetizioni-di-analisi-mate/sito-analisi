A loan of $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid with two equal year-end installments at $10\%$. An additional final payment has a present value of $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How large is each regular installment?

- $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3457.14\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2600\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The additional payment already has a time-zero value of $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$, leaving $6000-1800=4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ for the regular installments to cover. Since $a_{\overline 2|0.1}=\dfrac{210}{121}$, each installment is
$$
R=4200\cdot\frac{121}{210}=2420\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ treats $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ as a nominal amount due on the second payment date and discounts it for two years. The value $3457.14\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the additional payment, while $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides by two without discounting the installments.
