After renegotiation the balance is $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the rate is $5\%$ per period. The regular installment remains $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What final payment is required on the second due date?

- $588\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $560\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At the first due date the debt is $1200\cdot1.05=1260\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the installment of $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reduces it to $560\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Interest of $0.05\cdot560=28\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accrues before the second due date, so the payment that settles the loan is
$$
560\cdot1.05=588\,\mathrm{EUR},
$$
which is smaller than the regular installment. The value $560\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits interest for the final period.
