An equal-principal loan repays $1400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal in each installment at a periodic rate of $5\%$. By how much does the installment fall between consecutive due dates?

- $1400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $70\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1470\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under equal-principal amortization the principal portion stays at $C=1400\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so only interest changes between installments. The opening balance falls by $C$ from one period to the next, reducing interest by
$$
iC=0.05\cdot1400=70\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The installments therefore form an arithmetic sequence with common difference $70\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The amount $1400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the principal portion, which stays constant.
