A loan has end-of-period installments and a constant interest rate $i$. For $k\geq2$, which balance determines the interest portion of installment $k$?

- The balance immediately after installment $k$.
- The balance immediately before installment $k$.
- The original principal in every period.
- The balance immediately after installment $k-1$.

## Explanation

Installment $k$ is paid at the end of period $k$. During that period the borrower still has the principal remaining after the preceding installment, $D_{k-1}$, so $I_k=iD_{k-1}$. The balance immediately before installment $k$ is $qD_{k-1}$ and already includes the interest. Using it as the interest base would count interest twice. The balance after installment $k$ applies to the following period.
