A separate sinking fund for a bullet loan of $4800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has accumulated $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ by the end of year two. All principal is due in year four. How much principal is still owed to the lender?

- $3300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4800\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The fund and the loan are separate. The $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accumulated in the fund belongs to the borrower and has not been paid to the lender. Until the fourth due date the lender receives only interest. The entire principal of $4800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is therefore still owed. The value $3300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ deducts the fund from the balance as if principal had already been partly repaid.
