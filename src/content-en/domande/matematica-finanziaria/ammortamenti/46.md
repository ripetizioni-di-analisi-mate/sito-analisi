A fund receives $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of year one and $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of year two. It earns $10\%$ annually and must provide $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on the second date. What is the shortfall?

- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At the second due date the first deposit has earned one year's interest and is worth $1000\cdot1.1=1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The second is worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The fund holds $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, leaving a shortfall of
$$
2400-2100=300\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the fund's return. The value $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ also accrues interest on the second deposit.
