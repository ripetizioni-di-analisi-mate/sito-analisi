A loan is paid off with its fourth installment. The principal portions of the first three are known. What additional information suffices to find the fourth principal portion without knowing the rate?

- The interest portion of the first installment.
- The original principal.
- The loan origination date.
- The entire fourth installment, including interest, without its breakdown.

## Explanation

The principal portions satisfy $C_k=D_{k-1}-D_k$. When they are added, the intermediate balances cancel, leaving $D_0-D_4$. The fourth payment pays off the loan, so $D_4=0$ and hence $C_1+C_2+C_3+C_4=D_0$. Given the original principal, the fourth principal portion is $C_4=D_0-(C_1+C_2+C_3)$. The fourth installment alone is insufficient because separating interest from principal requires the interest rate.
