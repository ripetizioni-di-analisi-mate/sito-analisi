An equal-principal loan of $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid in four annual installments at an interest rate of $4\%$. Find the total interest paid.

- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $960\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The principal portion is $\dfrac{6000}{4}=1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The four opening balances are $6000$, $4500$, $3000$, and $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Their sum is $15000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so total interest is
$$
0.04\cdot15000=600\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The formula $iD_0\dfrac{n+1}{2}$ gives the same result. The value $960\,\mathrm{EUR}$ charges interest on the full original principal every year.
