A table shows the first closing balance rounded to cents, while the contract uses full precision until maturity. Which balance should be used to calculate interest for the second period?

- The first closing balance shown to the nearest cent, regardless of its unrounded value.
- The original principal.
- The first closing balance at full precision.
- The first interest portion.

## Explanation

The contract uses full precision for the actual payments. Rounding to cents is only for displaying the schedule. Second-period interest is $I_2=iD_1$, and in the balance recurrence $D_1$ is the actual balance after the first installment. Use its full precision. A rounded balance would apply only if the installments themselves had been rounded when paid.
