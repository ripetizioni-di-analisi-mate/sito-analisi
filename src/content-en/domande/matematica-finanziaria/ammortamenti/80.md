After two years a loan has €1000 outstanding. For the next year alone the rate falls from $6\%$ to $2\%$ while the payment stays at €240. Relative to the original schedule, how much more principal does this payment repay?

- €20
- €60
- €40
- €9.60

## Explanation

Interest for the period falls from €60 to €20. With the payment fixed, the €40 interest saving goes toward principal. The principal component rises from €180 to €220.
