A loan with a balance of $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ moves to a zero rate for its three remaining periods. Which level installment will pay it off on schedule?

- $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With the new rate at zero, the annuity factor for three installments is $a_{\overline 3|0}=3$ because every discount factor equals $1$. The new level installment is therefore
$$
R=\frac{1800}{3}=600\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Each payment repays principal alone and the three installments sum to $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides the balance by two periods instead of three.
