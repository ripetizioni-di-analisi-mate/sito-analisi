A loan of $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid in two equal annual principal portions. Find the initial principal-only value at a valuation rate of $10\%$.

- $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The two equal principal portions are $C=\dfrac{2420}{2}=1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The principal-only value is their present value at inception, using the valuation rate, giving
$$
N=\frac{1210}{1.1}+\frac{1210}{1.1^2}=1100+1000=2100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the sum of the principal portions without discounting. The value $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts the whole amount for just one year.
