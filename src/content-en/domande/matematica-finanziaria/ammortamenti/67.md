The borrower pays off the loan immediately after an installment, with no additional fees. Which rate determines the prospective balance?

- The market yield on a new loan, whatever it may be.
- The return earned by the sinking fund.
- The contractual rate for the remaining payments.
- A zero rate because the loan is paid off immediately.

## Explanation

The payoff replaces the borrower's future installments. In the absence of fees, the contract requires the outstanding balance $D_k$, obtained prospectively by discounting those payments at the contractual rate. A purchaser uses the market rate when valuing the loan, while the fund rate belongs to a separate arrangement. A zero rate would simply add the nominal installments.
