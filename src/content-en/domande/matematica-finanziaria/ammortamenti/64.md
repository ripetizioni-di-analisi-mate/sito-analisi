The balance immediately after the current installment is $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the installment is $620\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What single payment, excluding fees, would settle the loan on that same date immediately before the installment?

- $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4380\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5620\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $620\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Both balances refer to the same date. Immediately before payment the borrower still owes the current installment, so the debt at that moment is
$$
qD_{k-1}=D_k+R_k=5000+620=5620\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
This amount would pay off the loan in the absence of fees. The value $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the balance after the installment. The value $4380\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the installment instead of adding it.
