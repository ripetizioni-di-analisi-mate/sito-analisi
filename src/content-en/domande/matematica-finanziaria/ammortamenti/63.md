A final installment of $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is due in one year. The annual contractual and market rates are $10\%$ and $0\%$, respectively. By how much does market value exceed the contractual balance?

- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Discounting the installment at the contractual rate gives the contractual balance $\dfrac{2200}{1.1}=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At a zero market rate the discount factor is $1$ and the market value is $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Their difference is
$$
2200-2000=200\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ calculates $10\%$ of the installment instead of discounting it. The value $-200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reverses the subtraction.
