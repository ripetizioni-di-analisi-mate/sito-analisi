The first installment in a three-payment equal-principal loan is $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the rate per period is $5\%$. What was the original principal, to the nearest cent?

- $3960\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3771.43\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3443.48\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $26400\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first equal-principal installment is $R_1=\dfrac{D_0}{3}+0.05\,D_0$, as interest initially accrues on the entire principal. Therefore $R_1=D_0\cdot\dfrac{23}{60}$ and
$$
D_0=1320\cdot\frac{60}{23}\approx3443.48\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $3960\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies the installment by three. The value $3771.43\,\mathrm{EUR}$ charges interest on one-third of the principal.
