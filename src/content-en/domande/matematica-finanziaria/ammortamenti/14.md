One contractual payment of $1452\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is due in one year. The contractual interest rate is $10\%$ and the market rate is $21\%$. Find the loan's current market value.

- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1452\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1756.92\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The market value is the present value of the contractual payments at the market rate. The contract specifies only the payment amount $1452\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
V=\frac{1452}{1.21}=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts at the contractual rate of $10\%$ and equals the contractual balance. The value $1756.92\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies by $1.21$ instead of dividing.
