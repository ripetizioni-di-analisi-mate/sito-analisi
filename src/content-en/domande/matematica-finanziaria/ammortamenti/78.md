Following a rate change, $4100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is refinanced with two equal annual installments at $5\%$. What is the new installment?

- $2205\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2152.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2260.13\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At the revision date the balance of $4100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ becomes the principal of a new level-payment loan at $5\%$. Its annuity factor is
$$
a_{\overline 2|0.05}=\frac{1}{1.05}+\frac{1}{1.05^2}=\frac{820}{441},
$$
so the installment is $R=4100\cdot\dfrac{441}{820}=2205\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $2050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides the balance by two without interest. The value $2260.13\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accumulates the balance for two years before dividing it.
