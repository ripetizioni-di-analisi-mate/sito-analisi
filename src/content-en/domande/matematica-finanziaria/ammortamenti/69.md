The interest portions due at the ends of years one and two are $121$ and $60.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the interest-only value at inception using an annual valuation rate of $10\%$.

- $181.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $165\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The interest-only value is the present value at inception of the interest portions, discounted at the valuation rate. The first is worth $\dfrac{121}{1.1}=110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the second $\dfrac{60.50}{1.21}=50\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving
$$
U=110+50=160\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $181.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the undiscounted sum. The value $165\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts both portions for only one year.
