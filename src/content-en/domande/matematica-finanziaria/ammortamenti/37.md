A loan of $8400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid in seven equal principal portions. What balance remains immediately after the fourth installment, irrespective of the interest rate?

- $4800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $8400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under equal-principal amortization the principal portion $C=\dfrac{8400}{7}=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is independent of the interest rate. Four installments repay four principal portions, so
$$
D_4=8400-4\cdot1200=3600\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Three principal portions remain. The value $4800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the principal already repaid after four installments.
