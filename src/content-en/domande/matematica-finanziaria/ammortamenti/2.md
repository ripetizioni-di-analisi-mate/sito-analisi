The principal portion of the first installment in a three-year level-payment loan is $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The effective annual interest rate is $10\%$. Find the principal portion of the third installment.

- $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $968\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $960\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1064.80\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

In a level-payment loan each principal portion is $q=1.1$ times its predecessor because $C_{k+1}=qC_k$. There are two steps from the first portion to the third, so
$$
C_3=800\cdot1.1^2=800\cdot1.21=968\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The amount $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the second principal portion. The value $960\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds $10\%$ of $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ twice without compounding.
