A loan of $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $10\%$. Its first two installments, due at the ends of years one and two, are $500$ and $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the outstanding balance immediately after the second installment.

- $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1870\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1270\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

For the retrospective calculation, accumulate the original principal and the first installment to the second due date. Their accumulated values are $2000\cdot1.1^2=2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $500\cdot1.1=550\,\mathrm{EUR}$, respectively. Subtract the second installment at face value because it falls on the valuation date, giving
$$
D_2=2420-550-600=1270\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the interest on the first installment.
