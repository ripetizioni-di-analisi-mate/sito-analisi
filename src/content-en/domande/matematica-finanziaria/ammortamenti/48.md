With the loan and its term fixed, the return on the sinking fund rises. What happens to the required level deposit?

- It increases if there are at least two deposits in arrears.
- It stays the same for any term.
- It decreases if there are at least two deposits in arrears.
- It decreases only if the loan rate also falls.

## Explanation

The deposit is $Q=\dfrac{D_0}{s_{\overline n|j}}$ with $s_{\overline n|j}=\sum_{k=1}^n(1+j)^{n-k}$. With at least two deposits in arrears, at least one term has a positive exponent and increases as $j$ rises. Thus $s_{\overline n|j}$ increases and $Q$ decreases. For a single end-of-period deposit $s_{\overline 1|j}=1$, so the deposit is independent of the fund's rate.
