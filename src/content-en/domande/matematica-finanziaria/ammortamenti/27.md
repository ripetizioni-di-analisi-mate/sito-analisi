The final installment of a level-payment loan is $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the rate per period is $10\%$. What is its principal portion?

- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1188\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $132\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The final installment pays off the loan, so $D_n=0$ and $R=qD_{n-1}$. The balance at the start of the final period is therefore
$$
D_{n-1}=\frac{1320}{1.1}=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Since $D_n=D_{n-1}-C_n=0$, the principal portion of the last installment is $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and interest is $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $1188\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts $10\%$ of the installment instead of $10\%$ of the opening balance.
