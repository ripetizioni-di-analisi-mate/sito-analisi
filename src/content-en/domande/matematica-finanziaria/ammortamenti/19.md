The accumulated debt immediately before the final installment is $807.43\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The theoretical installment is $807.40\,\mathrm{EUR}$, but the actual final installment must pay off the loan. By how much does it exceed the theoretical installment?

- $807.43\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $807.40\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-0.03\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The last installment must leave a zero balance, so it equals the accumulated debt immediately before payment, $807.43\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The theoretical installment is $807.40\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so the actual final installment exceeds it by
$$
807.43-807.40=0.03\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $-0.03\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reverses the subtraction.
