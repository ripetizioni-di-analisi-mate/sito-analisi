The outstanding balance immediately after the current installment is $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The borrower pays off the loan six months later, with no intervening payments or fees. Assuming an effective annual interest rate of $21\%$ and exponential accrual for fractions of a year, find the payoff amount.

- $2652\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2904\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2640\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Six months is half an annual period. Under exponential accrual, multiply the balance by $q^{\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{1.21}=1.1$. The payment is therefore
$$
2400\cdot1.1=2640\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2652\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies simple interest by adding half of $21\%$. The value $2904\,\mathrm{EUR}$ charges interest for an entire year.
