The outstanding balance after an installment is $2500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. For settlement halfway through the next period the contract uses simple interest at a periodic rate of $4\%$. What amount has accrued?

- $2550\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2549.51\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2500\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under simple interest, the charge is proportional to time. For half a period at $4\%$, it is $2\%$ of the balance, or $0.02\cdot2500=50\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving an accumulated debt of
$$
2500\cdot\left(1+\frac{0.04}{2}\right)=2550\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2549.51\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses the exponential convention $2500\cdot1.04^{\frac{1}{2}}$, which the contract does not specify. The value $2600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ counts a full period.
