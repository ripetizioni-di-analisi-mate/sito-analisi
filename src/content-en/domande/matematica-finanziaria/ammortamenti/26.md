In a level-payment loan the first principal portion is $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the interest rate is $5\%$. By how much does the interest portion fall between the first and second installments?

- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first principal portion reduces the balance by $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving $D_1=D_0-1000$. Since $I_1=iD_0$ and $I_2=iD_1$, the difference between the two interest charges is
$$
I_1-I_2=i\,(D_0-D_1)=0.05\cdot1000=50\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
With a level installment, the second principal portion increases by $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ to $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
