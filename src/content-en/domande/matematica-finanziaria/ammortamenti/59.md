A constant interest rate is used to calculate the retrospective balance immediately after installment four. How is the second installment treated?

- Subtract its accumulated value over two periods.
- Subtract its present value discounted for two periods.
- Subtract its nominal amount without adjusting for time.
- Omit it because it has already been paid.

## Explanation

Under the retrospective method $D_4=D_0q^4-\sum_{h=1}^4R_hq^{4-h}$. The exponent for the second installment is $4-2=2$, so accumulate it for two periods and subtract its accumulated value $R_2q^2$. It was paid at the second due date and must be carried forward to compare it with the principal at the fourth due date. Discounting would carry it to an earlier date.
