The interest portions of a three-period equal-principal loan bearing interest at $5\%$ on an original principal of $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ are valued at a zero rate. What is the interest-only value?

- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

In this equal-principal loan each principal portion is $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving opening balances of $2400$, $1600$, and $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At $5\%$ the interest charges are $120$, $80$, and $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At a zero valuation rate every discount factor is $1$, so
$$
U=120+80+40=240\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$ charges interest on the full original principal every period.
