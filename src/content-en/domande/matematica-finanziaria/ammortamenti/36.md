The three installments of an equal-principal loan are $1260$, $1230$, and $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How much principal does each repay?

- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1230\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1140\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1170\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The installment decreases by $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$ each period, so $iC=30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Only one principal portion remains at the beginning of the final period, and the final interest charge equals $iC=30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The final installment is $C+iC$, giving
$$
C=1200-30=1170\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Indeed, first-period interest is $3\cdot30=90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $1170+90=1260\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The amount $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the final installment including interest.
