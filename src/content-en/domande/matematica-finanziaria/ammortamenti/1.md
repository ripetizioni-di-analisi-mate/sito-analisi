A loan of $2800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid in full with three installments of $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The installments consist entirely of principal and interest. Find the total interest paid.

- $350\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3150\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The three installments sum to $3\cdot1050=3150\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Since the loan is paid off, their principal portions sum to the original principal, $2800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The installments contain only principal and interest, so total interest is
$$
3150-2800=350\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $3150\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the sum of the installments before deducting the principal repaid.
