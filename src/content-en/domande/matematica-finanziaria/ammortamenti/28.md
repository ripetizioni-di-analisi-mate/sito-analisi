A four-installment level-payment loan has principal portions of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in the first installment and $605\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in the third. What is the effective rate per period?

- $21\%$
- $10\%$
- $10.50\%$
- $110\%$

## Explanation

In a level-payment loan $C_{k+1}=qC_k$, so the factor between the first and third principal portions is $q^2$. Thus
$$
q^2=\frac{605}{500}=1.21,
$$
and the positive root gives $q=1.1$, or $i=10\%$. The value $21\%$ treats the portions as consecutive. The value $10.50\%$ divides $21\%$ by two instead of taking the square root.
