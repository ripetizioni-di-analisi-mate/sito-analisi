A loan has two equal annual installments, an interest rate of $20\%$, and an initial principal of $2750\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the installment.

- $1375\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1650\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1980\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The two-payment annuity factor at $20\%$ is
$$
a_{\overline 2|0.2}=\frac{1}{1.2}+\frac{1}{1.44}=\frac{55}{36}.
$$
The installment is therefore $R=2750\cdot\dfrac{36}{55}=1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The first interest charge is $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$, the balance falls to $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and the second installment $1500\cdot1.2=1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ pays it off. The value $1375\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides the principal by two without allowing for interest.
