A balance of $4400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ can be settled immediately after the current installment for a fee of $1\%$ of the balance. What is the total payoff amount?

- $4400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4444\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $44\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4356\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Immediately after the installment, paying off the loan without fees would require the outstanding balance $4400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The fee is $1\%$ of the balance, or $0.01\cdot4400=44\,\mathrm{EUR}$, payable on the same date. The total payment is therefore
$$
4400+44=4444\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $4356\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the fee instead of adding it. The value $44\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the fee alone.
