The interest rate is revised immediately after the fourth installment. The fifth is one period away. Which rate determines the interest portion of the fourth installment?

- The new rate applied to the fourth installment just paid.
- The mean of the old and new rates.
- The market rate for the fifth installment.
- The rate in force during the fourth period before the revision.

## Explanation

The fourth installment's interest portion $I_4=iD_3$ accrues during the fourth period. The revision occurs immediately after the fourth installment, so the rate previously in force applies to that period. The new rate takes effect in the fifth period and determines $I_5$. Past payments retain the amounts calculated at the applicable rates.
