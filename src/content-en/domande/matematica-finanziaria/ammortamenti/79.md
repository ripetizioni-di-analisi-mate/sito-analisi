The balance after an installment is $3400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and an additional $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is paid immediately. At a new effective rate of $4\%$ per period, what interest accrues in the first period of the new schedule?

- $136\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $16\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $152\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The extra payment immediately after the installment reduces the balance on that date. The new principal is $3400-400=3000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. First-period interest accrues on this balance at the new rate and equals
$$
I=0.04\cdot3000=120\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $136\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the extra payment. The value $16\,\mathrm{EUR}$ charges interest on the extra payment alone.
