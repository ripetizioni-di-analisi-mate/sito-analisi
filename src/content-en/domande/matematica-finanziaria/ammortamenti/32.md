A three-year loan has equal semiannual payments and a quoted effective annual rate. What conversion is required to construct its schedule?

- Always halve the effective annual rate and use six payments.
- Use the annual rate and six payments.
- Convert the annual rate to its equivalent semiannual rate and use six payments.
- Use the equivalent semiannual rate and three payments.

## Explanation

Payments are semiannual, so there are six half-year periods in three years. Use the equivalent semiannual rate $i_2=\sqrt{1+i}-1$, which gives the same accumulated value over one year as the annual rate $i$. Halving the effective annual rate and compounding the result over two half-years would produce a return above $i$.
