Immediately after an interest rate change, the outstanding principal is $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and one installment remains, due a year later. The new effective annual interest rate is $5\%$. Find the final installment.

- $1890\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1714.29\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

After the rate change the balance of $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earns one year's interest at the new rate, amounting to $0.05\cdot1800=90\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The last installment pays off the loan, so it includes the outstanding principal and that interest, giving
$$
R=1800\cdot1.05=1890\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1714.29\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides by $1.05$ instead of multiplying. The value $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the interest portion alone.
