A loan of $3000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $5\%$ and has a first installment of $1150\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the balance immediately after that installment.

- $1850\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3150\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1792.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The principal remains outstanding for a year and is worth $3000\cdot1.05=3150\,\mathrm{EUR}$ just before the installment. From $D_1=qD_0-R_1$ it follows that
$$
D_1=3150-1150=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The installment includes $150\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of interest and $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal. The value $1850\,\mathrm{EUR}$ treats the entire installment as principal. The value $3150\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the balance before the payment.
