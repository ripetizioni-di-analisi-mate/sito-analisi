A loan at $10\%$ per year has two installments one year apart. The borrower pays an additional amount together with the first installment, so that the second installment falls by $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the loan is still repaid on the second date. How large must the additional amount be?

- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

An amount $X$ paid on the first date reduces the outstanding balance by $X$, so it reduces the debt accrued on the second date by $1.1X$, because during the second year that amount would have earned interest at $10\%$. Since the second installment must fall by $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$, we require $1.1X=220$. Therefore
$$
X=\frac{220}{1.1}=200\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accumulates the reduction instead of discounting it, while $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is only the interest saved.
