A loan at $10\%$ annual interest already has two payments scheduled one year apart. The borrower can move part of the second payment forward to the first payment date. By how much must the first payment rise to reduce the second by €220 while keeping the final balance unchanged?

- €220
- €242
- €20
- €200

## Explanation

Each extra euro paid on the first date reduces the debt on the second date by €1.10. A €220 reduction on the second date therefore requires an advance payment of $\dfrac{220}{1.10}=200$ euros.
