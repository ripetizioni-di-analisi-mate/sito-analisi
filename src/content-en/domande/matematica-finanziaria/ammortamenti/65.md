The remaining installments are $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $1102.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. What is the loan's present market price at an annual market rate of $5\%$?

- $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2152.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1952.38\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The loan's market price is the present value of the remaining installments at the market rate. Since $1050=1000\cdot1.05$ and $1102.50=1000\cdot1.05^2$, we obtain
$$
V=\frac{1050}{1.05}+\frac{1102.50}{1.05^2}=1000+1000=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2152.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ simply adds the nominal installments. The value $2050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts both installments for one year.
