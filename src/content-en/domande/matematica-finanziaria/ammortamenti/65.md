A fixed contractual rate loan has two outstanding payments, €900 in one year and €1100 in two years. In its market valuation the first payment's discount factor stays fixed, while the second one's changes from $0.96$ to $0.92$. At the same valuation date, what happens to the market price and the contractual balance?

- The price falls by €44 and the contractual balance is unchanged.
- Both fall by €44.
- The price rises by €44 and the contractual balance is unchanged.
- The price falls by €80 and the contractual balance is unchanged.

## Explanation

Only the second cash flow changes the market price, by $1100(0.92-0.96)=-44$ euros. The contractual balance uses the loan's agreed rate and scheduled payments. Both are unchanged at the comparison date.
