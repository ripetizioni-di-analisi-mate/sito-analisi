A bullet loan of $7500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $4\%$. Interest is paid at the end of each year and all principal is repaid at the end of year three. Find the amount paid to the lender at the third payment date.

- $7500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $7800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $8400\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

In a bullet loan the principal outstanding remains $7500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ until maturity. Interest of $0.04\cdot7500=300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is paid annually and the first two years' interest has already been paid. On the third due date the lender therefore receives
$$
7500+300=7800\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $8400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds all three years' interest to the principal.
