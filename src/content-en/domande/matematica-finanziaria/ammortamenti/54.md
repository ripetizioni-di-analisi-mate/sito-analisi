In an amortization schedule at an annual rate of $8\%$, a year-end installment of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ contains $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of interest. What is the outstanding balance immediately after this installment is paid?

- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1380\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1620\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The interest component is the rate times the outstanding balance at the start of the year, so that balance is $D_{k-1}=\dfrac{120}{0.08}=1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The principal component is the rest of the installment, namely $C_k=500-120=380\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The balance decreases by the principal component only, therefore
$$
D_k=1500-380=1120\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the whole installment, while $1380\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the interest component instead of the principal component. The value $1620\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the debt accrued just before the installment.
