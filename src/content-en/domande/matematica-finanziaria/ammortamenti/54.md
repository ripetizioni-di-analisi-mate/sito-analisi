Just after the first payment the debt stands at €1000. One year later the statement shows a total payment of €260 and a remaining principal of €800. At $5\%$ annual interest, how much of the payment is a fee that repays neither principal nor interest?

- €60
- €50
- €10
- €0

## Explanation

The principal repaid is $1000-800=200$ euros. Interest on the opening balance is €50. Of the €260 paid, $260-200-50=10$ euros must be a fee.
