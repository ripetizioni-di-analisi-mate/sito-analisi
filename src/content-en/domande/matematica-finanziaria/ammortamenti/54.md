A loan of $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $5\%$ and has an outstanding balance of $1250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ immediately after the first installment. What was the installment?

- $750\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1312.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $850\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1150\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Immediately before the installment the balance is $2000\cdot1.05=2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, including interest of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. From $D_1=qD_0-R_1$ we obtain
$$
R_1=2100-1250=850\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The installment therefore contains $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of interest and $750\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal. The value $750\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the principal portion alone, while $1312.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the closing balance accumulated for a year.
