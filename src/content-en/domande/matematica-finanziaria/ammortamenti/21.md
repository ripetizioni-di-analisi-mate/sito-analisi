An installment exactly covers the positive interest accrued during the period. Which relationship holds?

- $D_k=0$.
- $D_k=D_{k-1}-I_k$.
- $D_k=D_{k-1}+I_k$.
- $D_k=D_{k-1}$.

## Explanation

If $R_k=I_k$, the principal portion is $C_k=R_k-I_k=0$. Since $D_k=D_{k-1}-C_k$, the closing balance equals the opening balance, giving $D_k=D_{k-1}$. The equality $D_k=D_{k-1}+I_k$ describes a period with no payment and capitalized interest. The equality $D_k=D_{k-1}-I_k$ incorrectly treats interest as a repayment of principal.
