An equal-principal loan of $3600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid in three annual installments at an interest rate of $5\%$. Find the second installment.

- $1380\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1260\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

In an equal-principal loan the principal portion is $C=\dfrac{3600}{3}=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The first installment leaves a balance of $3600-1200=2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$, on which second-period interest of $0.05\cdot2400=120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accrues. The second installment is therefore $1200+120=1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $1380\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the first installment, whose interest accrues on the entire original principal.
