A loan has four regular end-of-period installments of $R>0$ and an additional payment of $B>0$ on the fourth due date. At a rate of $i>0$ per period, which equation gives the original principal?

- $D_0=R a_{\overline{4}|i}+Bv^4$.
- $D_0=R a_{\overline{3}|i}+Bv^4$.
- $D_0=R a_{\overline{4}|i}+Bv^3$.
- $D_0=R a_{\overline{4}|i}+B$.

## Explanation

At inception the principal equals the present value of all payments. The four regular installments are worth $R\,a_{\overline 4|i}$. The extra amount $B$ is due with the fourth installment and has present value $Bv^4$ after four periods of discounting, giving $D_0=R\,a_{\overline 4|i}+Bv^4$. The equation containing $a_{\overline 3|i}$ omits the regular installment due alongside $B$. The equation using an undiscounted $B$ ignores the payment date.
