A loan starts with one interest-only period followed by three installments that repay principal. What balance is used to calculate those three installments?

- The original principal less the interest payment.
- The original principal plus the interest payment.
- The original principal, because the initial payment leaves the balance unchanged.
- One-third of the original principal, regardless of the repayment schedule.

## Explanation

During the interest-only period the payment equals the interest charge and its principal portion is zero. Since $D_1=D_0-C_1$ and $C_1=0$, the balance when principal repayments begin is still the original principal. Use $D_0$ to calculate the three installments. Deducting the interest payment from the principal would treat it as a principal repayment.
