A debt of $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is due in two years. A sinking fund earning $10\%$ per year receives two equal year-end deposits. Find each deposit to the nearest cent.

- $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1818.18\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3818.18\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Let $Q$ be the deposit. The first earns a year's interest in the fund and becomes $1.1\,Q$. The second is made at maturity and earns no interest. Their accumulated value $s_{\overline 2|0.1}\,Q=2.1\,Q$ must equal $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
Q=\frac{4200}{2.1}=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides by two, ignoring the fund's return.
