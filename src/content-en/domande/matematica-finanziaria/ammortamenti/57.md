A loan has at least six installments. In the prospective balance immediately after the fifth installment, which payment has exponent $-1$ on the periodic accumulation factor $q$?

- The fifth installment.
- The fourth installment.
- The sixth installment.
- The original principal.

## Explanation

Under the prospective method $D_5=\sum_{h=6}^n R_hq^{5-h}$. Discount each future installment for the number of periods from the valuation date to its due date. The sixth installment is one period after the fifth due date, giving it exponent $5-6=-1$. The fifth installment has just been made and is excluded. The fourth installment and the original principal enter only the retrospective calculation.
