A loan of $3100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $10\%$. It is repaid with two equal regular installments and an additional principal payment of $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of year two. Find the regular installment.

- $1786.19\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1089\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1550\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The extra payment of $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of year two is worth $\dfrac{1210}{1.1^2}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at inception. The regular installments must therefore cover $3100-1000=2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Since $a_{\overline 2|0.1}=\dfrac{1}{1.1}+\dfrac{1}{1.21}=\dfrac{210}{121}$, the installment is
$$
R=2100\cdot\frac{121}{210}=1210\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1089\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the additional payment without discounting it.
