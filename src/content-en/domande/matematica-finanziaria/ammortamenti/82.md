With a balance of $3500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a new periodic rate of $4\%$, what threshold must the level installment exceed for the balance to decrease after the first payment?

- $3500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $140\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3640\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The first installment's principal portion is $R-I_1$ with $I_1=0.04\cdot3500=140\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The balance falls if that portion is positive, or $R>140\,\mathrm{EUR}$. With $R=140\,\mathrm{EUR}$, the balance stays at $3500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. A smaller payment makes the debt grow if unpaid interest is capitalized. The value $3640\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the accumulated balance immediately before payment.
