All future installments are positive. The valuation rate rises while the contract is held fixed. What is the effect?

- The contractual balance increases.
- The market value of the loan decreases.
- The remaining installments decrease.
- The market value of the loan increases.

## Explanation

The market value is $V_k(j)=\sum_{h=k+1}^nR_h(1+j)^{k-h}$ with negative exponents $k-h$. As $j$ rises, each factor $(1+j)^{k-h}$ falls. The remaining payments are positive and fixed by contract, so every term decreases and the market value falls. The contractual balance is unchanged because it is calculated at the contractual rate.
