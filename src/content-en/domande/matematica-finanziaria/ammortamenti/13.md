Immediately after an installment, the remaining payments are $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $726\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. The contractual interest rate is $10\%$ per year. Find the current outstanding balance.

- $1460\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1606\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under the prospective method the outstanding balance is the present value of future installments at the contractual rate. The payment in one year is worth $\dfrac{880}{1.1}=800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the payment in two years $\dfrac{726}{1.21}=600\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving
$$
D=800+600=1400\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1460\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts both payments for only one year. The value $1606\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the undiscounted sum.
