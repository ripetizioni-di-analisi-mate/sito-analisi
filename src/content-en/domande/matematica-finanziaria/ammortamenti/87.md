A loan of $2500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears interest at $4\%$ in the first period and $6\%$ in the second. A payment of $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is made at the end of period one. What final payment settles the loan at the end of period two?

- $2173\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2080\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2156\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Interest of $0.04\cdot2500=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accrues in the first period, leaving a balance of $2600-600=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ after payment. The rate in the second period is $6\%$, making the amount due at the end
$$
2000\cdot1.06=2120\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2173\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies $6\%$ in both periods. The value $2156\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accumulates the original principal for two periods without also accumulating the payment.
