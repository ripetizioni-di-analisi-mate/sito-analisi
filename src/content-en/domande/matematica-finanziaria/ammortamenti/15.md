A debt of $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earns interest at $5\%$ in the first period. The installment at the end of that period is $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How much principal does it repay?

- $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $310\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $190\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Interest is charged on the opening balance, giving $I_1=0.05\cdot1200=60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The rest of the installment repays principal, so
$$
C_1=250-60=190\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
After the payment the balance falls to $1200-190=1010\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $310\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the interest to the installment instead of subtracting it.
