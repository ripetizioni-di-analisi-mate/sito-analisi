A loan will be fully repaid at maturity. Which relationship holds at inception when its installments are discounted at the contractual rate?

- $U(i)=D_0$.
- $U(i)+N(i)=D_0$.
- $N(i)=D_0$ even with positive interest.
- $U(i)+N(i)$ is the undiscounted sum of the installments.

## Explanation

Each installment satisfies $R_k=I_k+C_k$, so at the contractual rate $U(i)+N(i)=\sum R_k(1+i)^{-k}$. The equation of value at inception equates the present value of all installments at that rate to the original loan. Therefore $U(i)+N(i)=D_0$. When the rate is positive the undiscounted installments sum to more than $D_0$ because they include interest.
