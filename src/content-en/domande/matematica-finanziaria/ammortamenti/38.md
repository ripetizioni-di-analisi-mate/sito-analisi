A bank lends $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at $6\%$, with principal repaid in five equal portions. Which installment equals $1120\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The principal portion is $\dfrac{5000}{5}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so the installment of $1120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ includes $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of interest. Since interest equals $6\%$ of the opening balance, that balance is $\dfrac{120}{0.06}=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. A balance of $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains after three payments because $5000-3\cdot1000=2000$. The installment in question is therefore the fourth.
