Find the level installment on an interest-free loan of $2700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ repaid in six payments in arrears.

- $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2700\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $540\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At a zero rate the discount factor is $v=1$, so the annuity factor is $a_{\overline 6|0}=6$. The level installment is
$$
R=\frac{D_0}{a_{\overline 6|0}}=\frac{2700}{6}=450\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Each installment repays principal alone because no interest accrues. The value $540\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides by five payments instead of six.
