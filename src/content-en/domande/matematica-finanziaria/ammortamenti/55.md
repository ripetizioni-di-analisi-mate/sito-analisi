Two regular installments of $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remain, due in one and two years. An additional payment of $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is due with the second. What is the current balance at the contractual rate of $10\%$?

- $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2342\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2530\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The current balance is the present value of the future payments at the contractual rate. The one-year payment is worth $\dfrac{1210}{1.1}=1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The total due in two years is $1210+242=1452\,\mathrm{EUR}$, worth $\dfrac{1452}{1.21}=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. This gives
$$
D=1100+1200=2300\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the final additional payment. The value $2342\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds it without discounting it.
