A loan of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at $10\%$ per year is repaid over three years with principal components of $200$, $300$ and $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the interest component of the second installment?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The interest component of an installment is computed on the outstanding balance at the start of the period. After the first installment the balance has fallen by the first principal component, so at the start of the second year it is $D_1=1000-200=800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Therefore the second interest component is
$$
I_2=0.1\cdot800=80\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses the initial principal and $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses the balance left after the second installment. The value $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies the rate to the principal component instead of the balance.
