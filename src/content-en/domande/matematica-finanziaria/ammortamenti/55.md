A loan has a fixed contractual rate and all remaining payments are positive. Just after a payment the contractual balance is €1000, while the bank values exactly the same remaining payments at €970. What follows about the bank's constant valuation rate?

- It is below the contractual rate.
- It exceeds the contractual rate.
- It equals the contractual rate.
- It must be negative.

## Explanation

The balance is the value of the future instalments at the contractual rate. As every remaining payment is positive and in the future, raising the rate lowers each present value. The €970 valuation therefore requires a higher rate.
