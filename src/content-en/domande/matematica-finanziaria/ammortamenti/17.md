A balance of $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears interest at $5\%$. An installment of $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is paid at the end of the period and the contract capitalizes any unpaid interest. What balance remains?

- $4080\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3880\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The period's interest is $0.05\cdot4000=200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The payment of $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ covers only part of it, leaving unpaid interest of $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The contract capitalizes this amount by adding it to the debt, raising the balance to
$$
D_1=4000\cdot1.05-120=4080\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The principal portion is negative and equals $-80\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $3880\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the payment without adding interest.
