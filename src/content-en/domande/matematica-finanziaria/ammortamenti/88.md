In the first year of a loan of $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ the borrower pays only interest at $5\%$. Principal is repaid in equal portions over the next two years and the rate remains $5\%$. What is the second installment in the overall schedule?

- $2310\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2205\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1610\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Only interest of $0.05\cdot4200=210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is paid in the first year, leaving the balance at $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The principal portion in each of the next two years is $\dfrac{4200}{2}=2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Second-year interest still accrues on $4200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and equals $210\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so the second installment in the overall schedule is
$$
2100+210=2310\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2205\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the third installment. The value $1610\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides the principal into three portions.
