At inception, the installments are worth $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ when discounted at the contractual rate. The principal portions are worth $4300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at that same rate. What is the interest-only value?

- $4300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $9300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At a common valuation rate the installments are worth $U+N$ because each is the sum of its interest and principal portions. At the contractual rate the installments are worth $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the principal portions are worth $4300\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving
$$
U=5000-4300=700\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $9300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the given amounts instead of subtracting them.
