A loan has an outstanding balance of $1600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ immediately after its third installment of $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What was the balance immediately before that installment?

- $2300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The balances immediately before and after the third installment refer to the same date, so no interest accrues between them. The payment of $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reduces the debt by exactly $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Before the installment the balance was therefore
$$
qD_2=1600+700=2300\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the installment instead of adding it.
