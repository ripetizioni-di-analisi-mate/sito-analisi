The balance after an installment is $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The rate for the next period is $5\%$ and the required balance after the next payment is $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What installment is needed?

- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $390\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $375\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Before the installment the balance is $1800\cdot1.05=1890\,\mathrm{EUR}$, including interest of $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$. From $D_k=qD_{k-1}-R_k$ we obtain
$$
R=1890-1500=390\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The installment therefore includes $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of principal and $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of interest. The value $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the principal portion alone. The value $375\,\mathrm{EUR}$ charges interest on the closing balance of $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
