A nonzero balance remains due after the final regular installment, and the sum of installments excludes that final balance. What must be added to the prospective calculation at an earlier date?

- The final balance accumulated to the valuation date.
- The discounted original principal.
- Only the discounted interest portion of the final installment.
- The final balance discounted to the valuation date.

## Explanation

The outstanding balance is the present value of everything the borrower still owes. In addition to the installments, a nonzero balance is due at the final date. Discount it to the valuation date and add it to the present value of the installments. Accumulating it would carry the future amount even further forward in time.
