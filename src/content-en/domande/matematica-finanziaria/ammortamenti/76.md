An investor buys the interest portions alone of an existing loan. Which rate determines the cash flow amounts and which determines their price?

- The valuation rate determines the interest portion and the contractual rate determines its price.
- The contractual rate determines the interest portion and the valuation rate determines its price.
- The contractual rate determines the price for every investor, whatever yield that investor requires.
- The valuation rate determines both the original interest portion and its price.

## Explanation

The repayment schedule fixes the interest cash flows. Calculate each interest portion at the contractual rate, as in $I_k=iD_{k-1}$. An investor values those cash flows at the valuation rate $j$ and pays their present value $U(j)=\sum I_k(1+j)^{-k}$. Thus the contractual rate sets the amounts and the valuation rate sets the price.
