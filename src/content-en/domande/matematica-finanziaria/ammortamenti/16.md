Immediately after the first installment the balance is $950\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The effective rate in the next period is $4\%$. What is the balance immediately before the second installment?

- $950\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $38\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $988\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $913.46\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The balance $D_1=950\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains outstanding throughout the following period and earns interest of $0.04\cdot950=38\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Immediately before the second installment, the balance includes that interest and equals
$$
qD_1=950\cdot1.04=988\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $38\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the interest alone. The value $913.46\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides by $1.04$, discounting instead of accumulating.
