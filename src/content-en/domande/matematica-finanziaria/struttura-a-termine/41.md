An annual forward rate applies from year three to year five. To what power should the two-year accumulation factor be raised to obtain the annual factor?

- The power $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
- The power $2$.
- The power $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- The power $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

## Explanation

The interval from year three to year five lasts $5-3=2$ years, so the forward accumulation factor is $(1+f_{3,5})^2$. To annualize, raise the two-year factor to the power $\dfrac{1}{2}$, which takes its square root. The exponent $\dfrac{1}{5}$ uses the final date instead of the interval length, while $\dfrac{1}{3}$ uses the initial date.
