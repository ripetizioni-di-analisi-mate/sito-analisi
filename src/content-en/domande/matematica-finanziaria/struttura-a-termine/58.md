In a bootstrap pricing equation, the last payment is $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. If the price rises by $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ while earlier contributions stay fixed, how much does the new discount factor increase?

- $0.2$
- $0.002$
- $0.02$
- $2$

## Explanation

In the bootstrap equation
$$
v(0,t_n)=\frac{P-\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}C_k\,v(0,t_k)}{C_n}
$$
the numerator rises by $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ when the price rises by $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the earlier contributions stay fixed. The denominator is the final payment, $C_n=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The factor therefore increases by $\dfrac{2}{100}=0.02$. The values $0.002$ and $0.2$ move the decimal point by one place.
