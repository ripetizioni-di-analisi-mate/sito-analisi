The two-year factor falls from $0.9$ to $0.89$ while the one-year factor stays the same. How much does the price of a bond paying $5$ and $105\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in years one and two decrease?

- $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1.05\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The unchanged one-year factor leaves the first coupon's value unchanged. Only the value of the year-two cash flow changes. It includes coupon and principal for a payment of $105\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The decrease is therefore
$$
105\,(0.9-0.89)=1.05\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$ counts only the principal. The value $1.1\,\mathrm{EUR}$ also applies the change to the first coupon.
