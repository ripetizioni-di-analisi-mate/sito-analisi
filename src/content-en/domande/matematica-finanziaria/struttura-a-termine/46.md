The two- and five-year discount factors are $e^{-0.06}$ and $e^{-0.21}$. Find the average continuously compounded forward rate between these dates.

- $15\%$
- $3\%$
- $5\%$
- $4.2\%$

## Explanation

The logarithms of the discount factors are $\log v(0,2)=-0.06$ and $\log v(0,5)=-0.21$. The average continuously compounded forward rate is therefore
$$
\phi_{2,5}=\frac{-0.06-(-0.21)}{5-2}=\frac{0.15}{3}=5\% .
$$
The value $15\%$ omits division by the term. The value $3\%$ divides the difference by the final date, and $4.2\%$ is the five-year continuously compounded spot rate, $\dfrac{0.21}{5}$.
