The one-year discount factor is $0.9$ and the two-year factor is $0.9375$. Calculate the annual forward rate between those maturities.

- $4\%$
- $-4\%$
- $-3.75\%$
- $4.17\%$

## Explanation

One euro invested from year one to year two accumulates to
$$
1+f_{1,2}=\frac{0.9}{0.9375}=0.96 .
$$
The interval lasts one year, so $f_{1,2}=-4\%$. The forward rate is negative because the two-year discount factor exceeds the one-year factor. The value $4.17\%$ uses the reciprocal ratio $\dfrac{0.9375}{0.9}$, and $4\%$ has the opposite sign.
