The one-year discount factor is $0.95$. A two-year bond with face value $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ redeems at par, pays an annual coupon of $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and costs $99.25\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the two-year discount factor.

- $0.945$
- $0.9925$
- $0.9$
- $0.8976$

## Explanation

The price equals the present value of the first coupon plus that of the final payment of $105\,\mathrm{EUR}$, which includes coupon and principal. The first coupon is worth $5\cdot0.95=4.75\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, so
$$
v(0,2)=\frac{99.25-4.75}{105}=\frac{94.50}{105}=0.9 .
$$
The value $0.945$ divides by the principal repayment of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ alone. The value $0.8976$ subtracts the nominal coupon of $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ instead of its present value.
