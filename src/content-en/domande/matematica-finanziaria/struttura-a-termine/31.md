The annually compounded spot rate for a five-year maturity is zero. Calculate the discount factor for that maturity.

- $1$
- $0$
- $0.2$
- $5$

## Explanation

At a zero spot rate the annual accumulation factor is $1+s_5=1$, so the discount factor is
$$
v(0,5)=(1+0)^{-5}=1 ,
$$
and one euro in five years is worth one euro today. The value $0.2$ divides one by the term. The value $0$ would give a certain euro a zero price, whereas a discount factor is always positive.
