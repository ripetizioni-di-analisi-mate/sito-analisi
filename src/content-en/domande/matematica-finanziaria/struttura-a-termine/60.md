A discounted coupon of $9.60\,\mathrm{EUR}$ must be subtracted from a bond price of $9\,\mathrm{EUR}$. If the final cash flow is $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, which discount factor does the equation imply?

- $0.006$
- $0.09$
- $0.096$
- $-0.006$

## Explanation

The missing factor is the residual price divided by the final cash flow. The residual is $9-9.60=-0.60\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
v=\frac{-0.60}{100}=-0.006 .
$$
The result is negative, inconsistent with a positive price for a euro payable with certainty. The given data are therefore inconsistent. The value $0.006$ has the opposite sign. The value $0.09$ divides the price by the final cash flow without deducting the coupon.
