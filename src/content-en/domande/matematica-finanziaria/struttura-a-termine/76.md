A strategy pairs a receipt of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and an outlay of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year two. At inception, $99\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is received and $98$ spent. What is the net future cash flow in year two?

- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

In year two the strategy receives $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $100$, so the net cash flow at that date is $100-100=0$. The opening balance $99-98=1\,\mathrm{EUR}$ pertains only to the initial date. The strategy therefore has a positive initial receipt and zero future cash flows, constituting an arbitrage. The value $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$ assigns the opening balance to year two, and $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the amounts without regard to sign.
