The one- and two-year factors are $0.98$ and $0.82$. How much does the price increase if $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is moved from year two to year one?

- $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $20.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Moving $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds that amount in year one and removes it in year two. Present value increases by $25\cdot0.98=24.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and decreases by $25\cdot0.82=20.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The net change is therefore
$$
25\,(0.98-0.82)=4\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The values $24.5$ and $20.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ each account for only one of these changes, and $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the nominal amount moved.
