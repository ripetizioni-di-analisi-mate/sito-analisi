A single-payment bond costs $99\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Its maturity discount factor is $0.9$. How much does it repay?

- $89.1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $108.9\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The price of a single-payment bond equals its redemption amount $N$ times the discount factor, so $99=0.9\,N$. It follows that
$$
N=\frac{99}{0.9}=110\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $89.1\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies the price by the factor instead of dividing. The value $108.9\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies the price by $1.1$ as if $1.1$ were the reciprocal of $0.9$.
