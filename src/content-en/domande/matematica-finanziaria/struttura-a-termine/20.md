An institution can pay $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today or $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. At a one-year discount factor of $0.94$, how much greater is the present cost of the second option?

- $6\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $94\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The payment of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year is worth $100\cdot0.94=94\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. The immediate payment of $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is due on the valuation date, so its present value remains $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Comparing $94$ and $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$, the second option costs $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$ more. The value $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ compares undiscounted nominal amounts, while $6\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the discount on the future payment, $100-94$.
