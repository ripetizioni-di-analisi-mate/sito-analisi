The year-one discount factor is $0.98$ and the year-two factor is $0.9$. There is a receipt of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the first date and a payment of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the second. Calculate the net present value.

- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $188\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $8\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The year-one receipt is worth $100\cdot0.98=98\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and the year-two outlay is worth $100\cdot0.9=90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. The outlay carries a negative sign, so the net value is
$$
98-90=8\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the signed nominal flows without discounting, and $-8\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has the opposite sign. The value $188\,\mathrm{EUR}$ treats the outflow as another receipt.
