Only the factors at years $1$ and $3$ are known. What is needed to value a payment in year $2$ without observing further prices?

- An explicit interpolation convention.
- Only the spot rate at year $3$.
- The sign of the forward rate between years $1$ and $3$.
- Only the face value of the three-year bond.

## Explanation

Many year-two factors are consistent with the observed year-one and year-three nodes. Without additional prices, a choice of interpolation convention is required for $v(0,2)$. For example, linear interpolation of the factors gives the arithmetic mean, while log-linear interpolation gives the geometric mean. Both reproduce the same observed nodes and give different prices for a payment in year two.
