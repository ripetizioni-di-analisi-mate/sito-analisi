The continuously compounded spot rate curve is $z(T)=0.01+0.02T$. What is the instantaneous forward rate at two years?

- $5\%$
- $4\%$
- $9\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

Since $-\log v(0,T)=T\,z(T)$, the instantaneous forward rate is the derivative of $T\,z(T)=0.01T+0.02T^2$. Thus
$$
g(T)=0.01+0.04T,\qquad g(2)=0.01+0.08=9\% .
$$
The same result follows from $g(T)=z(T)+T\,z'(T)=0.05+2\cdot0.02$. The value $5\%$ is the spot rate $z(2)$ and omits the term $T\,z'(T)$. The value $10\%$ evaluates $T\,z(T)$ at $T=2$ without differentiation.
