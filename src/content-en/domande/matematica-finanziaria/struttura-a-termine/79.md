Two bonds have identical certain payments at maturity and different prices. Short selling is prohibited. Which step of the usual law-of-one-price strategy is unavailable to someone who owns neither bond?

- Buying the cheaper bond.
- Selling the more expensive bond without owning it.
- Comparing the payment dates.
- Calculating the price difference.

## Explanation

The usual strategy sells the more expensive bond short and uses the proceeds to buy the cheaper bond, giving a negative initial cost and offsetting payments at maturity. The short-sale prohibition prevents an investor who owns neither bond from selling the expensive one. The purchase can no longer be financed with sale proceeds and would require initial funds. Buying the cheaper bond and comparing prices remain possible.
