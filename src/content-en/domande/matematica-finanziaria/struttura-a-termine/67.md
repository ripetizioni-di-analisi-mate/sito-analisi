Given $v(0,1)=0.9$ and $v(0,3)=0.8$, use linear interpolation of the factors. What is the price of a payment of $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year two?

- $68\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $64\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $72\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Year two is midway between the year-one and year-three nodes. Linear interpolation of the factors gives their arithmetic mean
$$
v(0,2)=\frac{0.9+0.8}{2}=0.85 ,
$$
and the price is $80\cdot0.85=68\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The values $72$ and $64\,\mathrm{EUR}$ use the year-one or year-three factor, respectively.
