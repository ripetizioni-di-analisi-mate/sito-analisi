The one-year discount factor rises from $0.95$ to $0.97$. How much does the price of a certain payment of $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year increase?

- $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The redemption payment $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is fixed, so the price changes only because the factor changes. The price moves from $250\cdot0.95=237.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ to $250\cdot0.97=242.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving an increase of
$$
250\,(0.97-0.95)=5\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ assumes a payment of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, while $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ counts the change twice.
