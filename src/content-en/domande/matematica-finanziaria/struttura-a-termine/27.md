A three-year zero-coupon bond costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and repays $133.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the effective annual spot rate?

- $10\%$
- $33.1\%$
- $11.03\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

The three-year accumulation factor is $\dfrac{133.10}{100}=1.331$. The annually compounded spot rate satisfies $(1+s_3)^3=1.331$. Since $1.1^3=1.331$, we obtain $1+s_3=1.1$, or $s_3=10\%$. The value $33.1\%$ is the total three-year return. The value $11.03\%$ divides that return by three, as under simple interest.
