Two price lists quote the two-year spot rate using different conventions. One gives $10\%$ effective annually, the other a constant annual force $\delta$. Which continuously compounded quote produces exactly the same price for any payment due in two years?

- $\delta=\ln(1.10)$
- $\delta=0.10$
- $\delta=2\ln(1.10)$
- $\delta=\ln(1.20)$

## Explanation

The two discount factors must agree for every possible redemption amount. From $e^{-2\delta}=(1.10)^{-2}$ we obtain $-2\delta=-2\ln(1.10)$. The annual force is $\ln(1.10)$.
