The effective annual spot rates are $s_1=5\%$ for one year and $s_2=4\%$ for two years. What is the effective annual forward rate $f_{1,2}$ between years one and two?

- $3.01\%$
- $4.5\%$
- $4\%$
- $-1\%$

## Explanation

The discount factors are $v(0,1)=1.05^{-1}$ and $v(0,2)=1.04^{-2}$, so the forward rate satisfies
$$
1+f_{1,2}=\frac{v(0,1)}{v(0,2)}=\frac{1.04^2}{1.05}=\frac{1.0816}{1.05}\approx1.0301.
$$
Therefore $f_{1,2}\approx3.01\%$. Since the curve is decreasing, the average annual return over the two years is lower than the return of the first year, and consequently the second year earns less than $4\%$. The value $4.5\%$ is the average of the two spot rates, while $-1\%$ is the difference between the two-year and the one-year spot rates. The value $4\%$ coincides with the two-year spot rate and ignores the first year.
