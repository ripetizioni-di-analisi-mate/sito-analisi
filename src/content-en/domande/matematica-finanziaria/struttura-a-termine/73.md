A bond costs $98\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and its replicating portfolio costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Both can be bought and sold short without costs. What is the initial receipt from buying the bond and selling the portfolio?

- $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $198\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The replicating portfolio produces the bond's cash flows date by date. Buying the bond and selling the portfolio therefore cancels all future cash flows. Today the sale brings in $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the bond costs $98$, leaving $100-98=2\,\mathrm{EUR}$. This is an arbitrage, so the prices violate the theorem on the **price of a replicable cash flow**. The value $-2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has the opposite sign.
