A two-year zero-coupon bond costs $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at maturity. What is its annually compounded spot rate?

- $21\%$
- $10.5\%$
- $10\%$
- $-10\%$

## Explanation

The two-year accumulation factor is the redemption payment divided by the price, $\dfrac{484}{400}=1.21$. With annual compounding the spot rate satisfies $(1+s_2)^2=1.21$. Therefore $1+s_2=\sqrt{1.21}=1.1$ and $s_2=10\%$. The value $21\%$ is the total two-year return. Dividing that return by two gives $10.5\%$, as under simple interest.
