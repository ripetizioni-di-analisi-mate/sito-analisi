The discount curve is $v(0,T)=e^{-0.02T-0.01T^2}$. What is the instantaneous forward rate in year three?

- $5\%$
- $8\%$
- $4\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

The instantaneous forward rate is $g(T)=-\dfrac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dT}\log v(0,T)$. Here $-\log v(0,T)=0.02T+0.01T^2$, so $g(T)=0.02+0.02T$. At year three this gives $g(3)=0.02+0.06=8\%$. The value $15\%$ is $-\log v(0,3)=0.06+0.09$ without differentiation. The value $5\%$ is the continuously compounded spot rate $z(3)=\dfrac{0.15}{3}$, which averages the instantaneous forward rate from zero to year three.
