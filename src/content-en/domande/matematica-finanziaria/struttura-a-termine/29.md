The effective annual two-year spot rate is $20\%$. What is the price of a single payment of $144\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years?

- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $115.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The price equals the payment multiplied by $v(0,2)=(1+s_2)^{-2}$. With $s_2=20\%$ we have $1.2^2=1.44$, so
$$
P=\frac{144}{1.44}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for just one year. The value $115.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies the payment by $1-0.2=0.8$, subtracting the rate instead of dividing by the accumulation factor.
