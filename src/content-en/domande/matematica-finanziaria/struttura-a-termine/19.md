If the half-year discount factor is $1.02$, what is today's price of a certain payment of $75\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in six months?

- $75\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $76.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $73.53\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The discount factor is today's price of one euro payable at maturity, so $75\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in six months is worth $75\cdot1.02=76.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. A discount factor above one corresponds to a negative rate, making the price greater than the future amount. The value $73.53\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides by $1.02$ as if it were an accumulation factor. The value $1.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is only the difference between the price and the future amount.
