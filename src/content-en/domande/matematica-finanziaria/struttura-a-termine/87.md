Every available bond pays only in year one. The target is a positive payment in year two, using only these bonds with no storage of cash. Which statement is correct?

- Selling one of the one-year bonds short is sufficient.
- Increasing all quantities purchased is sufficient.
- It is possible if the two-year price exceeds the one-year price.
- The available instruments cannot replicate the year-two cash flow.

## Explanation

Because all available bonds pay only in year one, every combination has zero cash flow in year two. The year-two row of $Ax=b$ becomes $0=b_2$ with $b_2>0$, which has no solution. The target cash flow **cannot be replicated**. Short positions or larger holdings change only the year-one flow.
