If the annual continuously compounded rate is $z$, what effective annual rate gives the same accumulation over one year?

- $e^{-z}-1$.
- $\log(1+z)$.
- $1+e^z$.
- $e^z-1$.

## Explanation

Under continuous compounding one euro grows to $e^z$ in one year. The equivalent effective annual rate $i$ gives the same amount, so $1+i=e^z$ and $i=e^z-1$. For example, $z=5\%$ gives $i\approx5.13\%$. The expression $\log(1+z)$ performs the reverse conversion, finding the continuously compounded rate from an effective rate $z$.
