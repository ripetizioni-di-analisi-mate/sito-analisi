The three-year annual spot rate is $10\%$. What total return does a three-year zero-coupon bond earn if bought today and held to maturity?

- $30\%$
- $33.1\%$
- $10\%$
- $21\%$

## Explanation

The total return on a zero-coupon bond held to maturity is its accumulation factor minus one. At an annual spot rate of $10\%$ for three years, the accumulation factor is
$$
1.1^3=1.331 ,
$$
so the total return is $33.1\%$. The value $30\%$ multiplies the rate by three and omits compounding of interest. The value $21\%$ is the return over only two years.
