A bond has nonzero payments on two dates whose discount factors are both unknown. Only its price is given. What additional information can determine both factors?

- Another observation of the same price.
- The nominal sum of the two receipts on the same bond.
- A second price equation independent of the first.
- Knowledge of the factor $v(0,0)=1$.

## Explanation

The bond price gives only one equation,
$$
P=C_1v_1+C_2v_2
$$
in the two unknowns $v_1$ and $v_2$, with infinitely many solutions. Determining both requires an independent equation, namely the price of another bond whose cash flows are not proportional to the first bond's. Observing the same price again repeats the equation. The condition $v(0,0)=1$ concerns only the valuation date.
