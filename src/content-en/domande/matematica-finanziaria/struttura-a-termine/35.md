From year one to year two, the discount factor changes from $0.96$ to $0.8$. What is the annual forward rate?

- $20\%$
- $16\%$
- $83.33\%$
- $-16.67\%$

## Explanation

The accumulation factor for one euro from year one to year two is the ratio of the discount factors,
$$
1+f_{1,2}=\frac{0.96}{0.8}=1.2 .
$$
The interval lasts one year, so subtracting one gives the forward rate $f_{1,2}=20\%$. The value $16\%$ takes the difference between factors instead of their ratio. The value $-16.67\%$ reverses the ratio, $\dfrac{0.8}{0.96}$.
