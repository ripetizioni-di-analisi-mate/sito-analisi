The logarithms of the discount factors at dates $1$ and $3$ are $-0.04$ and $-0.02$ respectively. Calculate the average continuously compounded forward rate between the two dates.

- $-1\%$
- $1\%$
- $-2\%$
- $3\%$

## Explanation

The average continuously compounded forward rate is $\phi_{s,t}=\dfrac{\log v(0,s)-\log v(0,t)}{t-s}$. With $s=1$ and $t=3$ this gives
$$
\phi_{1,3}=\frac{-0.04-(-0.02)}{2}=\frac{-0.02}{2}=-1\% .
$$
The forward rate is negative because the year-three discount factor $e^{-0.02}$ exceeds the year-one factor $e^{-0.04}$. The value $1\%$ reverses the difference, and $-2\%$ omits division by the interval length.
