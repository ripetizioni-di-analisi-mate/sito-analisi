The four-year discount factor is $e^{-0.12}$. What is the annual continuously compounded spot rate?

- $12\%$
- $3\%$
- $48\%$
- $-3\%$

## Explanation

Under continuous compounding, $v(0,T)=e^{-z(T)T}$, so $z(T)=-\dfrac{\log v(0,T)}{T}$. Here $-\log v(0,4)=0.12$ and the term is four years, giving
$$
z(4)=\frac{0.12}{4}=3\% .
$$
The value $12\%$ omits division by the term, while $48\%$ multiplies by the term instead of dividing. The value $-3\%$ has the opposite sign.
