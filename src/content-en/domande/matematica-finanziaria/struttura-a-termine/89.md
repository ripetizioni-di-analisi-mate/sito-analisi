In the cash flow matrix for a replication problem, what does one column represent?

- The payments of one instrument at the different maturities.
- The initial prices of all instruments.
- The payments of all instruments at a single maturity.
- The quantities of the positions required.

## Explanation

In $Ax=b$, column $j$ of $A$ contains the payments of instrument $j$ across maturities, with one row per date. The product $Ax$ multiplies each column by its holding $x_j$ and sums the results. Each row of $Ax$ therefore gives the portfolio cash flow on one date, to be matched to the corresponding component of $b$.
