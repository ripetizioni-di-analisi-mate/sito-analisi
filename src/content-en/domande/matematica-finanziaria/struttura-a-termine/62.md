After subtracting the contributions from known dates, the residual price is zero. The final redemption payment is positive. What does bootstrapping imply?

- A final discount factor of one.
- A zero spot rate at the final maturity.
- A zero final discount factor, incompatible with a positive price for one certain euro.
- A positive final discount factor in all cases.

## Explanation

Bootstrapping gives a factor equal to the residual price divided by the final payment $C_n>0$. A zero residual gives a factor of $\dfrac{0}{C_n}=0$. A zero discount factor prices one certain euro at zero at the final maturity, violating positivity of discount factors. A factor of one, corresponding to a zero spot rate, would require a residual equal to $C_n$.
