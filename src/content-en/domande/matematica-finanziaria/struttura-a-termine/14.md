Bond $A$ pays $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year one. Bond $B$ pays $20$ and $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in years one and two. Negative holdings are allowed. How much of $A$ is required to replicate a single receipt of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year two?

- $0.2$
- $-0.2$
- $-1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Bond $B$ alone makes a payment in year two. Receiving $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on that date requires one unit of $B$. This also pays $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year one, which must be offset. A holding of $x_A$ units of $A$ pays $100x_A$ in year one, so $20+100x_A=0$ and $x_A=-0.2$. The negative sign denotes a short position. The value $0.2$ has the opposite sign and doubles the coupon instead of canceling it.
