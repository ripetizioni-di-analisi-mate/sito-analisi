A six-month investment earns a return of $5\%$ over the half-year. What is the corresponding effective annual spot rate?

- $10\%$
- $5\%$
- $2.47\%$
- $10.25\%$

## Explanation

A year contains two half-years. Under compound interest the accumulation factors multiply, giving the annual factor
$$
1.05^2=1.1025 ,
$$
and hence an effective annual spot rate of $10.25\%$. The value $10\%$ adds the two half-year returns and omits interest earned in the second half on first-half interest. The value $2.47\%$ takes a square root instead of squaring.
