A default-free bond and a bond exposed to default mature on the same date. Is the first bond's price enough to value the second?

- A model of the cash flows under default is also needed.
- It is enough that their face values coincide.
- It is enough to multiply both promised payments by the same default-free discount factor.
- It is enough to know that the two bonds have the same issue price.

## Explanation

The factor obtained from the default-free bond is the price of one euro payable *with certainty* at maturity. The second bond pays in full only in the absence of default, so its payment differs in certainty. A common factor applies only to equally certain payments. Valuing the second bond therefore also requires a model of cash flows in the event of default.
