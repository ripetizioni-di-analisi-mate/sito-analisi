The one-year discount factor is $0.945$ and the effective annual forward rate from year one to year two is $5\%$. What is the two-year discount factor?

- $0.9923$
- $0.895$
- $0.945$
- $0.9$

## Explanation

The ratio of discount factors is the accumulation factor from year one to year two, so $1+f_{1,2}=\dfrac{v(0,1)}{v(0,2)}$. Hence
$$
v(0,2)=\frac{v(0,1)}{1.05}=\frac{0.945}{1.05}=0.9 .
$$
The two-year discount factor is lower than the one-year factor, as a positive forward rate implies. The value $0.9923$ multiplies by $1.05$ instead of dividing. The value $0.895$ subtracts the rate from the factor.
