The factors $0.96$ and $0.9$ are known for the first two annual dates. A bond with an annual coupon of $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a principal payment of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year three costs $93.30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the third discount factor.

- $0.84$
- $0.8$
- $0.8886$
- $0.848$

## Explanation

The price is the sum of the discounted coupons and principal. The first two coupons are worth $5\cdot0.96=4.80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $5\cdot0.9=4.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Year three brings coupon and principal together, or $105\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
v(0,3)=\frac{93.30-4.80-4.50}{105}=\frac{84}{105}=0.8 .
$$
The value $0.84$ divides by the principal of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ alone. The value $0.8886$ omits subtraction of the first two present values.
