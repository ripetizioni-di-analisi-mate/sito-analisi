The bid and ask prices of a zero-coupon bond paying €100 in a year are €94 and €96. For this exercise, a two-year instrument with payments of €10 in one year and €100 in two is valued at exactly €99. If the first discount factor can range across the quoted spread, what is the compatible range for the second factor?

- $[0.94,\,0.96]$
- $[0.894,\,0.896]$
- $[0.89,\,0.99]$
- $[0.904,\,0.906]$

## Explanation

The first factor lies between $0.94$ and $0.96$. The price gives $v_2=\dfrac{99-10v_1}{100}$. The upper endpoint for $v_1$ produces the lower endpoint for $v_2$, namely $0.894$. The opposite endpoint is $0.896$.
