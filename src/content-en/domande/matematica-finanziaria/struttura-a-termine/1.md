A zero-coupon bond costs $188\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and repays $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. What is the two-year discount factor?

- $0.88$
- $1.0638$
- $1.94$
- $0.94$

## Explanation

The discount factor is today's price of one euro payable at maturity. Dividing the bond price by its redemption amount gives
$$
v(0,2)=\frac{188}{200}=0.94 .
$$
The value $1.0638$ is the reciprocal ratio $\dfrac{200}{188}$, the two-year accumulation factor. The value $0.88$ counts the discount of $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$ once for each of the two years.
