A bond pays $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year one and $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year two. The discount factors are $0.95$ and $0.9$, respectively. Calculate the price implied by the curve.

- $126\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $133\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $127\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $140\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Multiply each payment by the discount factor for its payment date and add the present values:
$$
P=20\cdot0.95+120\cdot0.9=19+108=127\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $140\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the undiscounted nominal sum. The values $133\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $126\,\mathrm{EUR}$ apply the same discount factor to both payments, using the first-year or second-year factor, respectively.
