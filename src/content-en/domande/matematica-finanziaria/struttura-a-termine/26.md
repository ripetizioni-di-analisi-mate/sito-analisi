A zero-coupon bond costs $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and pays $980\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Find the annual spot rate.

- $2\%$
- $-2\%$
- $-2.04\%$
- $98\%$

## Explanation

The one-year accumulation factor is the redemption payment divided by the price, $\dfrac{980}{1000}=0.98$. The term is one year, so $s_1=0.98-1=-2\%$. The payment is below the price, hence the spot rate is negative. The value $-2.04\%$ divides the loss of $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ by the redemption payment instead of by the price. The value $2\%$ has the opposite sign.
