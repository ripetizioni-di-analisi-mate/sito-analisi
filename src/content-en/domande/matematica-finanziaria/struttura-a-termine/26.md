The only available datum is a €97 price for a bond paying €10 in one year and €100 in two. Without assuming a flat curve, which extra information is sufficient to identify the two individual discount factors?

- The price of two units of the same bond.
- The price of a zero-coupon bond paying €100 in one year.
- The sum of the two face amounts.
- The price of half a unit of the same bond.

## Explanation

The quoted price gives one equation, $10v_1+100v_2=97$. The zero-coupon bond directly determines $v_1$, leaving just one unknown. Prices for multiples of the original bond repeat the same equation.
