The effective annual spot rates are $s_1=10\%$ for one year and $s_2=25\%$ for two years. What is the price on the curve of a bond paying $22$ euros in one year and $125$ euros in two years?

- $123.31$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $147$ euros
- $80$ euros

## Explanation

With the spot rate $s_T$ the discount factor for maturity $T$ is $v(0,T)=(1+s_T)^{-T}$, so each payment is discounted with the spot rate of its own maturity. The payment in one year is worth $\dfrac{22}{1.1}=20$ euros today. The payment in two years is worth $\dfrac{125}{1.25^2}=\dfrac{125}{1.5625}=80$ euros today. The price on the curve is therefore $20+80=100$ euros. The value of $123.31$ euros uses the one-year spot rate for the second maturity as well, while $147$ euros is the nominal sum of the payments. The value of $80$ euros ignores the first-year payment.
