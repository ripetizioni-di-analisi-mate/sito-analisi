The log discount factor is interpolated linearly between two nodes. What holds for the average continuously compounded forward rate on any subinterval between them?

- It always depends on the subinterval length.
- It must equal the spot rate at the final node.
- It is the same on all subintervals.
- It is zero for every choice of nodes.

## Explanation

If $\log v(0,T)$ is affine between the nodes, its slope $m$ is constant. For any subinterval $[s,t]$ the average continuously compounded forward rate is therefore
$$
\phi_{s,t}=\frac{\log v(0,s)-\log v(0,t)}{t-s}=-m .
$$
Thus the forward rate is identical on every subinterval, regardless of length. It is zero only if the two nodes have the same discount factor.
