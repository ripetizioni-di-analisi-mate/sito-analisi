Two bonds have identical cash flows. The first can be bought for $95.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the second sold for $95.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the initial net receipt from buying the first and selling the second?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-0.3\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-0.7\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The future cash flows cancel, leaving only the initial balance. The sale brings in $95.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the purchase costs $95.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
95.20-95.50=-0.30\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The trade requires an initial outlay of $0.30\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and its cash flows cancel at maturity. It is therefore not an arbitrage. The value $0.3\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has the opposite sign.
