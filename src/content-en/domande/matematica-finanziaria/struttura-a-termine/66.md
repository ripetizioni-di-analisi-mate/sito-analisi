A bond of face value $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ redeems at par in two years. The discount factors are $0.98$ and $0.82$. What level annual coupon gives a price of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

- $9\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $18\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $21.95\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The price is $c\,v(0,1)+(c+100)\,v(0,2)$. The principal is worth $100\cdot0.82=82\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, so the coupons must contribute $18\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Since both coupons are equal,
$$
c\,(0.98+0.82)=18,\qquad c=\frac{18}{1.8}=10\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $21.95\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides only by the year-two factor. The value $9\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides $18\,\mathrm{EUR}$ by two without discounting the coupons.
