A portfolio holds three zero-coupon bonds each paying $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and two more each paying $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on the same date. Its price is $388\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the one-year discount factor?

- $0.97$
- $0.776$
- $1.1086$
- $0.03$

## Explanation

All payments are certain and due on the same date, so divide the total price by the total redemption payment. The total payment is $3\cdot100+2\cdot50=400\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving
$$
v(0,1)=\frac{388}{400}=0.97 .
$$
The value $0.776$ assigns $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ to each of the five bonds. The value $1.1086$ counts only one of the two bonds paying $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and $0.03$ is the discount per euro, $1-0.97$.
