A bond pays a coupon of $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of each year and principal of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year two. What amount multiplies $v(0,2)$ in its price equation?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $104\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $108\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The factor $v(0,2)$ multiplies the full year-two payment. On that date the bond pays both a coupon of $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and principal of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving the coefficient $104\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the price equation $P=4\,v(0,1)+104\,v(0,2)$. The value $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the final coupon. The value $108\,\mathrm{EUR}$ also assigns the first-year coupon to year two.
