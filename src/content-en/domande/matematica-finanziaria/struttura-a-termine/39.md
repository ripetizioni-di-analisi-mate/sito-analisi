The forward rate for the first year of a future loan is $5\%$ and for its second year $20\%$. What is the accumulation factor for the full term?

- $1.26$
- $1.25$
- $1.2$
- $1.125$

## Explanation

Forward accumulation factors multiply because the amount accumulated in the first year is reinvested in the second. The factor for the entire interval is therefore
$$
1.05\cdot1.20=1.26 .
$$
The value $1.25$ adds the rates and ignores second-year interest on the first year's interest. The value $1.125$ applies their average for only one year.
