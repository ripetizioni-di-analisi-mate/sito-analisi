A portfolio has the same nominal total of payments as a bond, but the payment dates differ. Does the portfolio already replicate the bond?

- Yes, because the nominal total is sufficient.
- Yes, provided all amounts are positive.
- No, because replication requires cash flows to match on every date.
- Yes, whenever the portfolio costs less.

## Explanation

A portfolio replicates a bond if their cash flow vectors match date by date, that is, if $Ax=b$. A total alone does not distinguish payment dates. For example, the cash flows $(100,0)$ and $(0,100)$ have the same sum, yet under factors $v(0,1)=0.96$ and $v(0,2)=0.9$ they are worth $96$ and $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The portfolio therefore **does not replicate the bond**.
