Two bonds with identical cash flows trade at the same price, with no bid–ask spread or transaction costs. What is the result of buying one and selling the other?

- A positive receipt at time zero.
- Zero net cash flows at every date.
- A positive receipt at maturity.
- A receipt equal to the sum of the initial prices.

## Explanation

Both bonds have price $P$, so buying one and selling the other gives an initial balance of $P-P=0$. Their identical future cash flows have opposite signs in the two positions and cancel at every date. The strategy therefore has **zero net cash flows** throughout. This accords with the law of one price and yields no gain.
