A payment falls on the valuation date itself. Which factor applies?

- The one-year discount factor.
- $0$.
- The spot rate for the first future date.
- $1$.

## Explanation

The factor $v(0,T)$ is today's price of one euro payable on date $T$. A payment due on the valuation date is already available. One euro of that payment is worth exactly one euro, so $v(0,0)=1$. The one-year factor applies to a euro payable in one year. A factor of $0$ would give an immediate certain payment a price of zero.
