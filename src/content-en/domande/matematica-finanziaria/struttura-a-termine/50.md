The instantaneous forward rate rises linearly from $2\%$ to $6\%$ between years one and three. Find the average continuously compounded forward rate over the same interval.

- $2\%$
- $4\%$
- $6\%$
- $8\%$

## Explanation

The average continuously compounded forward rate is the integral average of the instantaneous forward rate, $\phi_{1,3}=\dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle\int_1^3 g(u)\,\mathrm du$. For an affine function the integral is the area of a trapezoid, so the integral average is the arithmetic mean of the endpoint values:
$$
\phi_{1,3}=\frac{2\%+6\%}{2}=4\% .
$$
The value $8\%$ adds the endpoints without dividing. The values $2\%$ and $6\%$ are the instantaneous forward rates at just one endpoint each.
