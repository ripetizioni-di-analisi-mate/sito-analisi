A bond pays $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year one and an unknown amount in year two. Its price is $99.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the discount factors are $0.95$ and $0.9$. Find the final payment.

- $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $99.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The price is $10\cdot0.95+C\cdot0.9$, where $C$ is the final payment. The first coupon is worth $9.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, leaving a present value of $99.50-9.50=90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ for the final payment. Hence
$$
C=\frac{90}{0.9}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the present value of the final payment before division by the factor, while $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the first-year coupon to the final payment.
