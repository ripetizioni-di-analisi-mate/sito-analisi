The one- and three-year discount factors are $0.972$ and $0.675$. Calculate the effective annual forward rate between these maturities.

- $44\%$
- $22\%$
- $20\%$
- $-16.67\%$

## Explanation

The accumulation factor from year one to year three is the ratio of the discount factors,
$$
\frac{v(0,1)}{v(0,3)}=\frac{0.972}{0.675}=1.44 .
$$
The interval is two years, so $1+f_{1,3}=\sqrt{1.44}=1.2$ and $f_{1,3}=20\%$. The value $44\%$ gives the return over both years, and $22\%$ divides it by two instead of taking the appropriate root. The value $-16.67\%$ reverses the ratio.
