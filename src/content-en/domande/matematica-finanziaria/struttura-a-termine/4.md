The continuously compounded spot rates are $z(1)=2\%$ and $z(3)=4\%$. Find the average continuously compounded forward rate from year one to year three.

- $3\%$
- $5\%$
- $2\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

Under continuous compounding, $\log v(0,T)=-z(T)\,T$, so $\log v(0,1)=-0.02$ and $\log v(0,3)=-0.12$. The average continuously compounded forward rate is
$$
\phi_{1,3}=\frac{-0.02-(-0.12)}{3-1}=\frac{0.10}{2}=5\% .
$$
The value $3\%$ averages the spot rates and ignores their different terms. The value $2\%$ is their difference, and $10\%$ omits division by the interval length.
