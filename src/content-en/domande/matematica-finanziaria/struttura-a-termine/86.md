Two bonds cost $91\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $44\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Replication requires buying $2$ units of the first and selling $1$ unit of the second. What is the replication cost?

- $138\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $226\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $47\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $182\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A portfolio costs $\sum_j x_jP_j$, where purchases have positive quantities and sales negative quantities. Here $x_1=2$ and $x_2=-1$, giving
$$
2\cdot91-1\cdot44=182-44=138\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $226\,\mathrm{EUR}$ treats the sale as a purchase. The value $182\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the sale proceeds, and $47\,\mathrm{EUR}$ counts only one unit of the first bond.
