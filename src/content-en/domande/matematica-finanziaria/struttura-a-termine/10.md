The one- and three-year discount factors are $0.9$ and $0.729$. What two-year factor results from linear interpolation of their logarithms?

- $0.81$
- $0.8145$
- $0.6561$
- $0.171$

## Explanation

Year two is midway between the nodes. Linear interpolation of log discount factors gives the average of the logarithms there. Exponentiating gives the geometric mean
$$
v(0,2)=\sqrt{0.9\cdot0.729}=\sqrt{0.6561}=0.81 .
$$
The value $0.8145$ is the arithmetic mean obtained by interpolating the factors themselves. The value $0.6561$ is their product without taking its square root.
