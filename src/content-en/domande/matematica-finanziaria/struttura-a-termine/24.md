We observe $v(0,1)=0.97$ and $v(0,2)=0.98$. What follows from these factors alone?

- There is an arbitrage in every market.
- The implied forward rate between the two dates is positive.
- The first factor is incompatible with a positive price.
- The implied forward rate between the two dates is negative.

## Explanation

The forward rate from year one to year two satisfies
$$
1+f_{1,2}=\frac{v(0,1)}{v(0,2)}=\frac{0.97}{0.98}\approx0.9898 .
$$
The ratio is below one, so $f_{1,2}\approx-1.02\%$ and the forward rate is **negative**. Both factors are positive and therefore consistent with positive prices. Inferring arbitrage would require another assumption, such as costless storage of money from year one to year two.
