Given $v(0,3)=0.8$ and an annual forward rate of $15\%$ from year two to year three, calculate $v(0,2)$.

- $0.6957$
- $0.95$
- $0.85$
- $0.92$

## Explanation

The forward rate from year two to year three satisfies $1+f_{2,3}=\dfrac{v(0,2)}{v(0,3)}$, so
$$
v(0,2)=1.15\cdot v(0,3)=1.15\cdot0.8=0.92 .
$$
The year-two factor exceeds the year-three factor, consistent with a positive forward rate. The value $0.6957$ divides by $1.15$ rather than multiplying. The value $0.95$ adds the rate to the factor.
