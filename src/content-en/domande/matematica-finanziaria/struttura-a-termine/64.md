A cash flow stream pays $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year one, $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year two, and $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year three. The factors are $0.96$, $0.9$, and $0.8$. Calculate the price.

- $175\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $168\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $157.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $149\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Multiply each payment by the discount factor for its date, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=25\cdot0.96+50\cdot0.9+100\cdot0.8\\[6pt]
 &=24+45+80=149\,\mathrm{EUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The value $175\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the nominal sum. The value $168\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies the year-one factor to every payment, while $157.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies the year-two factor to each.
