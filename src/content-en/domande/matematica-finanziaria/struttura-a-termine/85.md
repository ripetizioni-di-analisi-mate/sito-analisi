Bond $B$ is the only available instrument paying in year three. Its payment then is $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How many units of $B$ replicate a year-three payment of $150\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

- $2.5$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

Bond $B$ alone pays in year three, so its year-three equation contains just one unknown, $60\,x_B=150$. Thus
$$
x_B=\frac{150}{60}=2.5 .
$$
The value $0.4$ is the inverse ratio $\dfrac{60}{150}$. The values $2$ and $3$ round the holding to a whole number, producing $120$ or $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year three instead of $150$.
