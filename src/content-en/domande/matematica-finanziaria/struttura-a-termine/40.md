The one-year annual spot rate is $10\%$ and the two-year annual spot rate is $10\%$. Calculate the forward rate from year one to year two.

- $21\%$
- $0\%$
- $5\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

Divide the two-year accumulation factor by the one-year factor to obtain the forward rate from year one to year two:
$$
1+f_{1,2}=\frac{1.1^2}{1.1}=1.1
$$
and $f_{1,2}=10\%$. On a flat spot curve the forward rate also equals the common spot rate. The value $21\%$ is the total two-year return, while $0\%$ subtracts the spot rates and ignores the accumulation factors.
