The two- and four-year discount factors are $0.9$ and $0.625$. Find the annual forward rate between those dates.

- $44\%$
- $22\%$
- $-30.56\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The accumulation factor from year two to year four is
$$
\frac{v(0,2)}{v(0,4)}=\frac{0.9}{0.625}=1.44 .
$$
The interval lasts two years, so $1+f_{2,4}=\sqrt{1.44}=1.2$ and the forward rate is $20\%$. The value $44\%$ is the return over the entire interval. The value $22\%$ divides it by two instead of taking the root, and $-30.56\%$ reverses the ratio without annualizing it.
