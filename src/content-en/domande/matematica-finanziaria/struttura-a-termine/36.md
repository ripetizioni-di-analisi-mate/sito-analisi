The one-year discount factor is $0.96$. The effective forward rate between years one and two ranges from $0\%$ to $20\%$, inclusive. What is the range of possible prices for a zero-coupon bond paying €100 at time $2$?

- From €96 to €115.20.
- From €76.80 to €96.
- From €83.33 to €100.
- From €80 to €96.

## Explanation

For forward rate $f$ the price is $\dfrac{100\cdot0.96}{1+f}$. It decreases as $f$ increases. At the endpoints its values are $\dfrac{96}{1.20}=80$ euros and €96.
