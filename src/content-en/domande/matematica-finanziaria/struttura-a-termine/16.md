Four bonds paying one euro each at the same maturity cost $3.8\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in total. What is a payment of $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on that date worth today?

- $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $72.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $76\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $84\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A unit bond pays one euro, so its price is the discount factor for that maturity, $\dfrac{3.8}{4}=0.95$. A redemption payment of $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on the same date is equivalent to $80$ unit bonds and is therefore worth
$$
80\cdot0.95=76\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $72.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies the factor twice. The value $84\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the discount of $5\%$ instead of subtracting it.
