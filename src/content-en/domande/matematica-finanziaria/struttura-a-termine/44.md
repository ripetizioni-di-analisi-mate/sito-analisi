The one- and two-year discount factors both equal $0.9$. What holds for the forward rate from year one to year two?

- It equals the two-year spot rate.
- It is zero.
- It is negative.
- It equals the one-year discount factor.

## Explanation

The forward rate from year one to year two satisfies
$$
1+f_{1,2}=\frac{v(0,1)}{v(0,2)}=\frac{0.9}{0.9}=1 ,
$$
so $f_{1,2}=0$ and one euro in year one is equivalent to one euro in year two. The two-year spot rate is positive because $s_2=0.9^{-\frac{1}{2}}-1\approx5.41\%$. The number $0.9$ is a price per euro, not an interest rate.
