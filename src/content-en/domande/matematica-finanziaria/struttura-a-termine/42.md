A lender entering a forward loan today must pay one euro at $s$ and receive $K$ euros at $t$. Which positions replicate these cash flows?

- Sell one unit zero-coupon bond maturing at $s$ today and buy $K$ unit bonds maturing at $t$.
- Buy one zero-coupon bond maturing at $s$ and sell $K$ maturing at $t$.
- Buy zero-coupon bonds at both maturities.
- Sell zero-coupon bonds at both maturities.

## Explanation

The lender pays a euro at time $s$ and receives $K$ at time $t$. Selling short a unit zero-coupon bond maturing at $s$ creates an obligation to pay one euro at $s$, replicating the outflow. Buying $K$ unit zero-coupon bonds maturing at $t$ pays $K$ euros at $t$, replicating the inflow. The opposite positions replicate the borrower's cash flows.
