Two bonds pay the same amount on the same date. The first can be bought for $94\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the second sold short for $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Without transaction costs, how much does this strategy bring in initially?

- $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $190\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The purchased bond's payment exactly covers the short position's obligation because the amounts and dates coincide. The net future cash flow is zero. Today the short sale brings in $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the purchase costs $94$, leaving a receipt of $96-94=2\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $-2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has the opposite sign because it measures the cost instead of the receipt.
