The annual accumulation factor under continuous compounding is $e^{0.03}$. What exponent appears in the three-year accumulation factor at the same rate?

- $0.09$
- $0.03$
- $0.01$
- $-0.09$

## Explanation

Under continuous compounding the multiyear factor is the product of the annual factors. Multiplying exponentials adds their exponents, so
$$
e^{0.03}\cdot e^{0.03}\cdot e^{0.03}=e^{3\cdot0.03}=e^{0.09}
$$
and the exponent is $0.09$. The value $0.01$ divides the rate by three instead of multiplying. The value $-0.09$ is the exponent of the three-year discount factor.
