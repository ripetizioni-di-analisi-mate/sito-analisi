The number of units held of a zero-coupon bond is tripled at a fixed unit price. Which quantity stays the same?

- The total price.
- The implied discount factor.
- The total redemption payment.
- The number of bonds.

## Explanation

Tripling the holdings triples both the total price $P$ and the total redemption amount $N$, while leaving the maturity unchanged. The discount factor is the ratio of price to redemption amount, so
$$
\frac{3P}{3N}=\frac{P}{N}=v(0,T).
$$
the **implied factor** stays the same. The total price, total redemption amount, and number of bonds all triple.
