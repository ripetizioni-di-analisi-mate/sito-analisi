More than one set of holdings replicates a bond. In an arbitrage-free market where the instruments can be bought and sold, how do the replication costs compare?

- The solution with more bonds always costs more.
- The solution with fewer bonds always costs less.
- They may differ as long as the cash flows are positive.
- They are equal.

## Explanation

Two replicating portfolios have identical cash flows on every date. If one cost less, buying the cheaper and selling the other would give an initial receipt, while future payments would cancel. This would be an arbitrage. Replication costs in an arbitrage-free market are therefore **equal** regardless of the number of instruments used.
