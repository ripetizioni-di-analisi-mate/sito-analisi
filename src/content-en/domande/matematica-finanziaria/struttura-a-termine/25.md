A one-year claim paying $105\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is quoted at $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the effective annual spot rate.

- $4.76\%$
- $5\%$
- $10.5\%$
- $-5\%$

## Explanation

The one-year accumulation factor is the claim's promised payment divided by its price, $\dfrac{105}{100}=1.05$. Subtracting one gives the one-year spot rate $s_1=1.05-1=5\%$. The value $4.76\%$ divides interest of $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ by the redemption payment $105\,\mathrm{EUR}$ instead of by the price. The value $-5\%$ has the opposite sign. The rate is positive because the payment exceeds the price.
