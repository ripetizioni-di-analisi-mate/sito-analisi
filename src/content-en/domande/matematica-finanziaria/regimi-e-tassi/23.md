An investment lasts two years at a simple annual rate of $i=-5\%$. What is its accumulation factor?

- $1.10$
- $0.90$
- $0.9025$
- $0.95$

## Explanation

The simple-interest accumulation factor is $1+it$ even when the rate is negative. With $i=-0.05$ and $t=2$ this gives
$$
1+(-0.05)\cdot2=0.90.
$$
The answer $0.9025=0.95^2$ is the compound-interest factor. The answer $1.10$ ignores the sign of the rate, while $0.95$ is the one-year factor.
