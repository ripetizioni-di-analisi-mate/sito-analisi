A continuous accumulation factor depends only on the term, equals $1$ for a zero term and satisfies $r(s+t)=r(s)r(t)$. If $r(1)=0.9$, what is $r\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$?

- $0.45$
- $\sqrt{0.9}$
- $0.81$
- $0.9$

## Explanation

With $s=t=\dfrac{1}{2}$, multiplicativity gives
$$
r(1)=r\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2=0.9.
$$
The accumulation factor is positive, so $r\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)=\sqrt{0.9}\approx0.9487$. The answer $0.45$ halves the factor instead of taking its square root. The answer $0.81$ is the two-year factor. The answer $0.9$ is the full-year factor and would assume that the factor is independent of the term.
