A saver invests €100 and receives €105 one year later. Over the same year the price of a basket rises from €100 to €110. What fraction of the initial purchasing power was lost?

- $\dfrac1{20}$
- $\dfrac1{22}$
- $\dfrac1{21}$
- $\dfrac{21}{22}$

## Explanation

The initial balance bought one basket. The final balance buys $\dfrac{105}{110}=\dfrac{21}{22}$ of a basket. The fraction lost is $1-\dfrac{21}{22}=\dfrac1{22}$.
