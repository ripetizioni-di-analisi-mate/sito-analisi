A deposit earning $4\%$ simple annual interest gains $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in eight months. What would the same principal earn in one quarter on the same terms?

- $32\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $36\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $24\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $48\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

For fixed principal and rate, simple interest $I=Cit$ is proportional to the term. The deposit earns $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in eight months, or $\dfrac{96}{8}=12$ in one month. A quarter contains three months, giving interest of $3\cdot12=36\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $32\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides $96$ by three, while $48\,\mathrm{EUR}$ corresponds to four months.
