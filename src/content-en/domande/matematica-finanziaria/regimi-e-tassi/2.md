A deposit of $7200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earns $45\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in interest at $5\%$ simple annual interest. How many actual days does this represent on a $360$-day basis?

- $90$ days
- $45$ days
- $125$ days
- $36$ days

## Explanation

Under simple interest, $I=Cit$, so $t=\dfrac{I}{Ci}$. The interest for one year is $7200\cdot0.05=360\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving
$$
t=\frac{45}{360}\ \text{years}.
$$
On a $360$-day basis, one year counts as $360$ days, so the term is $\dfrac{45}{360}\cdot360=45$ days. The answer of $125$ days reads the decimal fraction $0.125$ as a number of days.
