A receivable is discounted one year before maturity at a commercial discount rate of $10\%$. The proceeds are $4500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is its maturity value?

- $4050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4950\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Commercial discount is calculated on the maturity value $M$, so the proceeds are $V=M(1-dt)$. With $d=0.10$ and $t=1$ we have $V=0.90M$, hence
$$
M=\frac{4500}{0.90}=5000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $4950\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds $10\%$ of the proceeds to the proceeds themselves, as if the discount were calculated on $V$. The answer $4050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ deducts a further discount from the proceeds.
