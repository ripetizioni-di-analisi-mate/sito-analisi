At an effective annual compound rate of $10\%$, after how many whole years is the accumulated value first more than $50\%$ above the initial principal?

- $4$ years
- $6$ years
- $5$ years
- $3$ years

## Explanation

Under compound interest the accumulated value after $n$ years is $C\cdot1.1^n$. Find the smallest integer $n$ for which $1.1^n>1.5$. We have $1.1^4=1.4641$, which is below $1.5$, and $1.1^5=1.61051$, which is above $1.5$. The factor increases with $n$, so the answer is $5$ years. The answer of $4$ years rounds the exact term $\dfrac{\log1.5}{\log1.1}\approx4.25$ down.
