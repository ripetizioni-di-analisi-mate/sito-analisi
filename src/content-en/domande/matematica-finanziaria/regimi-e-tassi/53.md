A deposit of $3600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earns $5\%$ simple annual interest from March 1 to July 1, an interval of $122$ actual days. How much more interest does Actual/360 give than 30/360?

- $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $61\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Both conventions use a denominator of $360$, so each day counted earns $\dfrac{3600\cdot0.05}{360}=0.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Actual/360 counts $122$ actual days and gives $61\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The 30/360 convention counts four thirty-day months, or $120$ days, and gives $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The difference is $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ treats the difference in days as if it were the difference in euros.
