A nominal annual rate $j^{(m)}$ is compounded $m$ times a year. What accumulation factor applies over a single subperiod?

- $1+\dfrac{j^{(m)}}{m}$
- $\left(1+\dfrac{j^{(m)}}{m}\right)^m$
- $1+j^{(m)}$
- $1+mj^{(m)}$

## Explanation

The effective rate for one subperiod is $i_m=\dfrac{j^{(m)}}{m}$ because the nominal rate is $m$ times that rate. Accumulating over just one subperiod applies the factor once, giving $1+\dfrac{j^{(m)}}{m}$. The expression $\left(1+\dfrac{j^{(m)}}{m}\right)^m$ gives the annual factor by applying the subperiod factor $m$ times, while $1+j^{(m)}$ uses the nominal rate as the subperiod rate.
