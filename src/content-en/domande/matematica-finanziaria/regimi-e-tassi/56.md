The effective annual rate under compound interest is $-19\%$. What is the equivalent six-month rate?

- $-19\%$
- $-9.5\%$
- $10\%$
- $-10\%$

## Explanation

The annual factor is $1+i=1-0.19=0.81$ and the six-month factor $q_2$ satisfies $q_2^2=0.81$. The factor is positive, so $q_2=\sqrt{0.81}=0.90$, giving a six-month rate of $-10\%$. The answer $-9.5\%$ halves the annual rate as under simple interest. The answer $10\%$ has the opposite sign and would give the annual factor $1.21$.
