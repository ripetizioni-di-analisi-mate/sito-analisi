An account starts with €100. At the end of each half-year it earns $3\%$ on its balance, then incurs a €2 fee. What is the net return over the full year?

- $2.09\%$
- $2\%$
- $6.09\%$
- $2.03\%$

## Explanation

After the first fee the balance is €101. Six months later it is $101\cdot1.03-2=102.03$ euros. A gain of €2.03 on €100 is a net return of $2.03\%$.
