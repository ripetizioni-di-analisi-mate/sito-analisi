The annual force of interest at time $t$ is $\delta(t)=0.01+0.02t$. Calculate the equivalent constant force of interest over the two years from $0$ to $2$.

- $0.03$
- $0.06$
- $0.02$
- $0.05$

## Explanation

The two-year accumulation factor is $\exp\left(\int_0^2\delta(u)\,du\right)$, and its exponent is the integral
$$
\int_0^2(0.01+0.02u)\,du=0.01\cdot2+0.01\cdot2^2=0.06.
$$
A constant force of interest $\bar\delta$ gives the exponent $2\bar\delta$. The factors agree when $2\bar\delta=0.06$, so $\bar\delta=0.03$. The answer $0.06$ is the integral itself, while $0.05$ is the force of interest at the end of the two years.
