What nominal annual rate compounded semiannually is equivalent to an effective annual compound rate of $12.36\%$?

- $12\%$
- $6.18\%$
- $6\%$
- $12.36\%$

## Explanation

The annual factor $1.1236$ is the square of the effective six-month factor $1+i_2$, so
$$
1+i_2=\sqrt{1.1236}=1.06,
$$
and $i_2=6\%$. The nominal annual rate compounded twice a year is $j^{(2)}=2i_2=12\%$. The answer $6.18\%$ halves the effective annual rate. The answer $6\%$ is the six-month rate, which must be multiplied by the compounding frequency, while $12.36\%$ is the effective annual rate.
