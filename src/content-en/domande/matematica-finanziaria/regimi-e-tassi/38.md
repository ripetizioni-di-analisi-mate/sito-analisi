A receivable of $10000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is discounted at an annual commercial discount rate of $8\%$. By how much do the proceeds fall if the discount period increases from three months to nine?

- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The commercial discount $D=Mdt$ is proportional to the term. It is $10000\cdot0.08\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ for three months and $10000\cdot0.08\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ for nine months. The proceeds therefore fall by $600-200=400\,\mathrm{EUR}$, the discount for the additional six months. The answers $200$ and $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ are the discounts for the two terms, while $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is a full year's discount.
