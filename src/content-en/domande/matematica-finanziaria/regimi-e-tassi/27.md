At an effective annual compound rate of $10\%$, how many years does it take for $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ to grow to $1331\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

- $2$ years
- $3.31$ years
- $4$ years
- $3$ years

## Explanation

Under compound interest the term satisfies $1.1^t=\dfrac{M}{C}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{1331}{1000}=1.331$ and $1.1^3=1.1\cdot1.21=1.331$. Since $t\mapsto1.1^t$ is strictly increasing, the term is unique and equals $3$ years. The answer of $3.31$ years divides the increase of $33.1\%$ by the annual rate as under simple interest.
