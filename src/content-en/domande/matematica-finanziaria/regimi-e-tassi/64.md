What nominal annual rate compounded monthly corresponds to an effective monthly rate of $1.5\%$?

- $12\%$
- $1.5\%$
- $18\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

By definition, the nominal rate compounded $m$ times a year is $j^{(m)}=mi_m$. For monthly compounding $m=12$, so
$$
j^{(12)}=12\cdot1.5\%=18\%.
$$
The nominal rate does not compound the monthly interest and therefore differs from the effective annual rate $1.015^{12}-1\approx19.56\%$. The answer $1.5\%$ is the monthly rate, while $12\%$ confuses a rate with the number of compounding periods.
