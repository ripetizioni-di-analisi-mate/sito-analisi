What principal grows to $12100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years at an effective annual rate of $10\%$ under compound interest?

- $10000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $9680\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $11000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $9801\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under compound interest, divide the accumulated value by the factor $q^t$ to obtain the principal. For $t=2$, the two-year factor is $1.1^2=1.21$, so
$$
C=\frac{12100}{1.21}=10000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $11000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for just one year. The answer $9680\,\mathrm{EUR}$ deducts $10\%$ of the accumulated value twice, as under commercial discount, while $9801\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies the accumulated value by $0.9^2$.
