A receivable with a maturity value of $2700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is discounted to $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ by rational discount at $5\%$ simple annual interest. How long is the term?

- $2.5$ years
- $2$ years
- $3$ years
- $1.25$ years

## Explanation

Under rational discount, $M=V(1+it)$, so $1+it=\dfrac{M}{V}=\dfrac{2700}{2400}=1.125$. It follows that $it=0.125$ and, with $i=0.05$,
$$
t=\frac{0.125}{0.05}=2.5\ \text{years}.
$$
The amounts give the same result. The present value earns $2400\cdot0.05=120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year, and the discount of $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is two and a half times that amount. The answer of $1.25$ years is half the correct term.
