An effective compound interest rate is quoted for four months. How many times does its factor apply in one year?

- Twelve.
- Three.
- Four.
- Six.

## Explanation

One period is four months and one year is twelve months, so the factor applies $\dfrac{12}{4}=3$ times per year. The annual factor is therefore the cube of the four-month factor. Four is the period length in months, twelve is the number of months in a year, and six would be the number of two-month periods in a year.
