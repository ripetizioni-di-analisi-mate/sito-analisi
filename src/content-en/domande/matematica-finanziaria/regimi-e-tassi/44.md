Under rational discount at $8\%$ simple annual interest, a payment due in nine months is worth $2500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. What is its maturity value?

- $2550\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2700\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2650\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2350\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under rational discount the maturity value equals the present value accumulated at simple interest, $M=V(1+it)$. Nine months is $t=\dfrac{3}{4}$, so $it=0.08\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=0.06$ and
$$
M=2500\cdot1.06=2650\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $2700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses a full year in place of nine months, while $2350\,\mathrm{EUR}$ deducts the discount instead of adding it.
