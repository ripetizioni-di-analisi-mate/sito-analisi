What principal earns $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in four months at $6\%$ simple annual interest?

- $2500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $833.33\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2550\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1250\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The rate is annual, so four months is $t=\dfrac{4}{12}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a year. From $I=Cit$ we obtain
$$
C=\frac{I}{it}=\frac{50}{0.06\cdot\frac{1}{3}}=\frac{50}{0.02}=2500\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $833.33\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the term and divides $50$ by $0.06$. The answer $2550\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the accumulated value rather than the initial principal.
