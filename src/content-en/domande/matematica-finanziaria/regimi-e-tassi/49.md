Under rational discount the future value is $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the discount is $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What value of the product $it$ gives this discount?

- $0.25$
- $0.80$
- $0.75$
- $0.20$

## Explanation

Rational discount applies to the present value, so $D=Vit$. The proceeds are $V=1500-300=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving
$$
it=\frac{D}{V}=\frac{300}{1200}=0.25.
$$
The answer $0.20=\dfrac{300}{1500}$ uses the future value as its base, as under commercial discount. The answer $0.80=\dfrac{1200}{1500}$ is the discount factor, while $0.75$ is the difference between $1$ and $0.25$.
