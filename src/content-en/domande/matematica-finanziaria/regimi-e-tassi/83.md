A one-year deposit at $5\%$ simple annual interest is renewed for a second year with its interest reinvested. What is the overall two-year factor?

- $1.10$
- $1.1025$
- $0.1025$
- $1.05$

## Explanation

The first deposit grows from $C$ to $1.05C$. The second one-year deposit starts with that entire balance, multiplying it again by $1.05$. The overall factor is
$$
1.05\cdot1.05=1.1025.
$$
The answer $1.10$ is the factor for a two-year simple-interest deposit, which calculates both years' interest on the initial principal. The answer $0.1025$ is the increase rather than the full accumulation factor.
