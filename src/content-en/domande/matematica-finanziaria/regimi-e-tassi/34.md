An account holds €1000 today, €1100 after one year and €1089 after two. A clerk uses only the first and final balances to conclude that the rate was constant. With no other transactions, which observation refutes that conclusion?

- The final balance exceeds the initial balance.
- The difference between final and initial balances is positive.
- All three balances are positive.
- The first annual factor is $1.10$ and the second is $0.99$.

## Explanation

The observed factors are $\dfrac{1100}{1000}=1.10$ and $\dfrac{1089}{1100}=0.99$. The two-year factor determines an equivalent rate, while the individual years had different returns.
