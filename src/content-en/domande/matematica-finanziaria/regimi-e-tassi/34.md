A principal of $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ falls to $3240\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years under compound interest. What is the effective annual rate?

- $-9.5\%$
- $-19\%$
- $10\%$
- $-10\%$

## Explanation

Under compound interest, $(1+i)^2=\dfrac{M}{C}$. Thus
$$
\frac{3240}{4000}=0.81=0.9^2.
$$
Since $i>-1$, the annual factor $1+i$ is positive and equals $\sqrt{0.81}=0.9$, so $i=-10\%$. The answer $-19\%$ is the change over the whole two-year period. The answer $-9.5\%$ halves that change as under simple interest, while $10\%$ has the wrong sign.
