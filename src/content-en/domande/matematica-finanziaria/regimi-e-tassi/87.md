Which equality verifies that $r(t)=e^{0.04t}$ is multiplicative for consecutive periods of $1$ and $2$ years?

- $e^{0.12}=e^{0.04}e^{0.12}$
- $e^{0.12}=e^{0.08-0.04}$
- $e^{0.12}=e^{0.04}e^{0.08}$
- $e^{0.12}=e^{0.04}+e^{0.08}$

## Explanation

For periods of one and two years, multiplicativity requires $r(1+2)=r(1)\,r(2)$. We have $r(3)=e^{0.12}$, $r(1)=e^{0.04}$ and $r(2)=e^{0.08}$. Multiplying two exponentials adds their exponents, so $e^{0.04}e^{0.08}=e^{0.12}$ and the equality holds. The sum $e^{0.04}+e^{0.08}\approx2.12$ exceeds $2$ and therefore differs from $e^{0.12}\approx1.13$.
