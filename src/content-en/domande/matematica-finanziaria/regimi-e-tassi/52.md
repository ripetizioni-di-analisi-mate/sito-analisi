A receivable is valued by rational discount at a positive interest rate. As the term grows without bound, which quantity approaches the maturity value?

- The discount factor.
- The present value.
- The annual rate.
- The discount in euros.

## Explanation

Under rational discount, $V=\dfrac{M}{1+it}$. With $i>0$, as $t\to+\infty$ the denominator tends to $+\infty$, so the discount factor and the present value tend to zero. The discount is $D=M-V$ and therefore tends to $M$. For very long terms the discount accounts for almost the entire maturity value. The annual rate $i$ is constant and independent of the term.
