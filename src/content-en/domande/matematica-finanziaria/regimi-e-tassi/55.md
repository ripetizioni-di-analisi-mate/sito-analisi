An investment earns an effective compound return of $12\%$ over two years. What is its one-year accumulation factor?

- $1.12^2$
- $\sqrt{0.12}$
- $\sqrt{1.12}$
- $\dfrac{1.12}{2}$

## Explanation

The two-year factor is $1.12$. Under compound interest it is the product of two identical annual factors, so $q^2=1.12$. Since $q>0$, we have $q=\sqrt{1.12}\approx1.0583$. The expression $\sqrt{0.12}$ takes the square root of the rate instead of the factor. The answer $\dfrac{1.12}{2}$ divides the factor by the term instead of taking its root, while $1.12^2$ is the four-year factor.
