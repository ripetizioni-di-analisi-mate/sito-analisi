A nominal annual rate of $24\%$ is compounded quarterly. What effective rate applies each quarter?

- $2\%$
- $24\%$
- $8\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

A nominal rate compounded $m$ times a year is $j^{(m)}=mi_m$, where $i_m$ is the effective rate per subperiod. With $m=4$ this gives
$$
i_4=\frac{j^{(4)}}{4}=\frac{24\%}{4}=6\%.
$$
The answer $24\%$ takes the nominal annual rate as the quarterly rate. The answer $2\%$ divides by twelve, as for monthly compounding, while $8\%$ divides by three.
