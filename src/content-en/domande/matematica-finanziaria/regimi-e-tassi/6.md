A receivable of $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is discounted at an annual commercial discount rate of $10\%$. What is the longest term in years that leaves proceeds of at least $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

- $8$ years
- $2$ years
- $1$ year
- $10$ years

## Explanation

Under commercial discount the proceeds are $V=M(1-dt)$. The requirement is $5000(1-0.10t)\geq4000$, giving $1-0.10t\geq0.8$ and hence $t\leq2$. The discount can therefore reach $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and each additional year deducts $5000\cdot0.10=500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer of $8$ years divides the proceeds by $500$ instead of using the amount deducted. A term of $10$ years would leave zero proceeds.
