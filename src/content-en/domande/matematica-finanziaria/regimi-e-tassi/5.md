A receivable of $4800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ due in six months is discounted at an annual commercial discount rate of $10\%$. Calculate its present value.

- $4320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4560\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5040\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under commercial discount, the discount amount is $D=Mdt$. Six months is $t=\dfrac{1}{2}$ of a year, so
$$
D=4800\cdot0.10\cdot\frac{1}{2}=240\,\mathrm{EUR},\qquad V=4800-240=4560\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the discount deducted from the payment. The answer $4320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies a full year's discount, while $5040\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds the discount to the maturity value instead of subtracting it.
