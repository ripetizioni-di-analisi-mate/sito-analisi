A time-homogeneous, multiplicative accumulation law has a one-year factor of $1.05$. What is its three-year factor?

- $1.05^3$
- $1.05^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$
- $3\cdot1.05$
- $1+3\cdot0.05$

## Explanation

A time-homogeneous, multiplicative accumulation law satisfies $r(s+t)=r(s)r(t)$. Applying this property twice gives
$$
r(3)=r(1)\,r(1)\,r(1)=1.05^3\approx1.1576.
$$
The answer $1+3\cdot0.05=1.15$ is the simple-interest factor, which does not satisfy multiplicativity here. The expression $3\cdot1.05$ adds the factors instead of multiplying them, while $1.05^{\frac{1}{3}}$ is the factor for one third of a year.
