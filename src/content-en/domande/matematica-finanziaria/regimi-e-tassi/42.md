Two offers apply commercial discount to the same receivable, one at $6\%$ for twelve months and the other at $9\%$ for eight months. Which deducts the smaller amount?

- The offer at $9\%$.
- They deduct the same amount.
- The offer at $6\%$.
- It depends on the amount receivable.

## Explanation

The commercial discount is $D=Mdt$. Since the receivable is the same, only the product $dt$ can cause the discounts to differ. For the first offer, $dt=0.06\cdot1=0.06$. For the second, eight months is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of a year, giving $dt=0.09\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=0.06$. The products are equal, so the offers deduct the same amount for any maturity value.
