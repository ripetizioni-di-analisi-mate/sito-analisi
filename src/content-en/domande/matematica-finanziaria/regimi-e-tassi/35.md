A receivable of $3600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is discounted three months before it falls due, giving proceeds of $3510\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the annual commercial discount rate?

- $10\%$
- $2.5\%$
- $7.5\%$
- $30\%$

## Explanation

The amount withheld is $D=3600-3510=90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the term is $t=\dfrac{3}{12}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a year. From $D=Mdt$ we obtain
$$
d=\frac{D}{Mt}=\frac{90}{3600\cdot\frac{1}{4}}=0.10.
$$
The annual discount rate is therefore $10\%$. The answer $2.5\%$ is the relative discount for one quarter alone. The answer $7.5\%$ multiplies $2.5\%$ by three and $30\%$ multiplies it by twelve, whereas a year contains four quarters.
