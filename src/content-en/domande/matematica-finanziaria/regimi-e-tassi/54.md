Which expression gives the equivalent effective quarterly rate under compound interest for any effective annual rate $i>-1$?

- $i^{\dfrac{1}{4}}$
- $(1+i)^{\dfrac{1}{4}}-1$
- $(1+i)^4-1$
- $\dfrac{i}{4}$

## Explanation

The equivalent quarterly rate $i_4$ applies four times a year, so $(1+i_4)^4=1+i$ must hold. Since $1+i>0$, taking the positive fourth root gives
$$
i_4=(1+i)^{\frac{1}{4}}-1.
$$
The expression $\dfrac{i}{4}$ is the proportional quarterly rate and is equivalent only under simple interest. The expression $(1+i)^4-1$ converts in the opposite direction, from quarterly to annual.
