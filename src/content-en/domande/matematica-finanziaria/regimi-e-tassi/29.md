A deposit earns $21\%$ per year under compound interest, using exponential compounding for fractional periods. What is $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ worth after six months?

- $4840\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under exponential compounding the accumulated value is $M=Cq^t$ even for fractional terms. Six months is $t=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the factor is
$$
1.21^{\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{1.21}=1.1,
$$
and $M=4000\cdot1.1=4400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $4420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses the simple-interest factor $1+0.21\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=1.105$, while $4840\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the accumulated value after one year.
