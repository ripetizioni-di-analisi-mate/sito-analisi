After two years the compound-interest factor at an annual rate of $i$ exceeds the simple-interest factor at the same rate. What is the difference for $i=5\%$?

- $0.10$
- $0.05$
- $0.0025$
- $0.1025$

## Explanation

The two-year compound-interest factor is $(1+i)^2=1+2i+i^2$ and the two-year simple-interest factor is $1+2i$. Their difference is $i^2$, the second year's interest on the first year's interest. For $i=0.05$ this gives
$$
1.1025-1.10=0.05^2=0.0025.
$$
The answer $0.1025$ is the total two-year increase under compound interest and $0.10$ is the increase under simple interest. The answer $0.05$ is the annual rate.
