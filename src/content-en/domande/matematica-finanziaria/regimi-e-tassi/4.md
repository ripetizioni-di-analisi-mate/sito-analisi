A deposit of $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ grows to $6050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years under compound interest. Calculate the effective annual rate.

- $5\%$
- $21\%$
- $10\%$
- $10.5\%$

## Explanation

Under compound interest, $M=C(1+i)^2$, so the two-year accumulation factor is
$$
(1+i)^2=\frac{6050}{5000}=1.21.
$$
The annual factor is the positive square root, $1+i=\sqrt{1.21}=1.1$, giving $i=10\%$. The rate $21\%$ applies to the whole two-year period. The answer $10.5\%$ divides the two-year increase of $21\%$ by two, as under simple interest.
