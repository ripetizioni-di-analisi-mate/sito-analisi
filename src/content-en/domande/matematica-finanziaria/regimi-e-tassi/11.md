Find the effective annual rate equivalent to a nominal annual rate of $20\%$ compounded twice a year.

- $20\%$
- $44\%$
- $10\%$
- $21\%$

## Explanation

The nominal annual rate compounded twice a year is twice the effective six-month rate, so $i_2=\dfrac{20\%}{2}=10\%$. Applying the six-month factor twice gives the annual factor, hence
$$
1+i=1.10^2=1.21.
$$
The effective annual rate is therefore $21\%$. The answer $20\%$ treats the nominal rate as an effective rate, while $44\%$ squares $1.20$.
