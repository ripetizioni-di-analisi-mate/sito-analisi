A principal of $3200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is invested at $6\%$ simple annual interest for five months. How much interest does it earn?

- $16\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3280\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $192\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The rate is annual, so the term in years is $t=\dfrac{5}{12}$. Under simple interest, $I=Cit$, giving
$$
I=3200\cdot0.06\cdot\frac{5}{12}=80\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The amount $3280\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the accumulated value, which includes both principal and interest. The answer $192\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses a full year in place of five months, while $16\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses just one month.
