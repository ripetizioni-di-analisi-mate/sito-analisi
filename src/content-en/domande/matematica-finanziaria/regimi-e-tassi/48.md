A receivable is discounted for the same positive term using the same positive numerical rate under commercial and rational discount. Both methods give positive proceeds. Which gives the larger amount?

- Commercial discount.
- They give the same proceeds.
- It depends on the amount receivable.
- Rational discount.

## Explanation

With $x=it>0$ the rational discount factor is $\dfrac{1}{1+x}$ and the commercial discount factor is $1-x$, which is positive by assumption. Their difference is
$$
\frac{1}{1+x}-(1-x)=\frac{1-(1-x^2)}{1+x}=\frac{x^2}{1+x}>0.
$$
The rational discount factor is larger. Multiplying both factors by the same positive maturity value therefore gives larger proceeds under rational discount. Rational discount calculates the deduction on the present value, which is smaller than the maturity value.
