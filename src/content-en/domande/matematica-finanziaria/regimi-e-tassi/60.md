If $q_{\mathrm{annual}}=1.21$, what is the six-month compound discount factor?

- $1.1$
- $\dfrac{1}{1.1}$
- $0.895$
- $\dfrac{1}{1.21}$

## Explanation

The six-month accumulation factor $q_2$ satisfies $q_2^2=1.21$, so $q_2=\sqrt{1.21}=1.1$. The discount factor is its reciprocal, $\dfrac{1}{1.1}\approx0.909$. The answer $1.1$ is the accumulation factor, $\dfrac{1}{1.21}$ is the annual discount factor, and $0.895=1-\dfrac{0.21}{2}$ is a simple commercial discount factor.
