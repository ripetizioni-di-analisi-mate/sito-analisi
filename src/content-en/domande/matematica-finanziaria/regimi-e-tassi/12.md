A principal of $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is invested for three years at a constant annual force of interest $\delta=0.02$. Calculate the exact accumulated value.

- $1500e^{0.02}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1500e^{0.06}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1500e^{-0.06}\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1500\left(e^{0.06}-1\right)\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At a constant force of interest $\delta$, the accumulation factor over $t$ years is $e^{\delta t}$. Here $\delta t=0.02\cdot3=0.06$, so
$$
M=1500e^{0.06}\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $1500e^{0.02}\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies the force of interest for just one year, while $1500\left(e^{0.06}-1\right)\,\mathrm{EUR}$ gives only the interest earned. A negative exponent would give the present value of $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ payable in three years.
