A one-year deposit pays $10\%$ simple interest and can be renewed at year-end. If the first year's interest is set aside without earning interest and only the original principal is reinvested, what total amount will an initial deposit of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ produce after two years?

- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At the end of the first year only the original principal of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is reinvested. Each year earns $1000\cdot0.10=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in interest, and the first year's interest is set aside without earning more. The total after two years is therefore $1000+100+100=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ would result from reinvesting the $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earned in year one as well.
