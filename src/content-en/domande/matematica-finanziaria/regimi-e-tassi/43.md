With commercial discount and $dt=0.04$, what percentage of the maturity value is received immediately?

- $96\%$.
- $92\%$.
- $104\%$.
- $4\%$.

## Explanation

The commercial discount is $D=Mdt$, a fraction $dt$ of the maturity value. The proceeds are $V=M-D=M(1-dt)$. With $dt=0.04$ the factor is $1-0.04=0.96$, so the amount received is $96\%$ of the maturity value. The answer $4\%$ is the discount, while $104\%$ adds the discount instead of deducting it.
