A deposit earns simple interest at a positive rate on a positive principal. If the term increases from four to eight months with everything else fixed, which quantity doubles?

- The initial principal.
- The annual rate.
- The interest earned.
- The total accumulated value.

## Explanation

Under simple interest the interest earned is $I=Cit$. With $C$ and $i$ fixed, $I$ is proportional to $t$, so doubling the term from four months to eight doubles the interest. The accumulated value $M=C+Cit$ includes the constant term $C$ and therefore increases by less than a factor of two. For example, with $C=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $i=10\%$ the interest increases from $40$ to $80\,\mathrm{EUR}$, while the accumulated value increases from $1240$ to $1280\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
