A deposit earns $5\%$ per year under compound interest. Its balance after one year is $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How much interest does it earn in the second year?

- $105\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $205\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under compound interest, the first year's interest is added to the principal. In year two the rate of $5\%$ applies to the balance of $2100\,\mathrm{EUR}$, earning $2100\cdot0.05=105\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The initial principal was $\dfrac{2100}{1.05}=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the first year's interest, the amount that would be earned again under simple interest. The answer $205\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the total interest for the two years.
