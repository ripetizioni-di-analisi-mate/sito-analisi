Under rational discount a receivable due in one year provides $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today in exchange for $840\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at maturity. What is the simple annual interest rate?

- $\dfrac{40}{840}$
- $10\%$
- $5\%$
- $4\%$

## Explanation

Under rational discount, $D=Vit$, so interest is calculated on the present value. The discount is $840-800=40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $t=1$, giving
$$
i=\frac{D}{Vt}=\frac{40}{800}=0.05.
$$
The rate is $5\%$. Using the maturity value as the base gives $\dfrac{40}{840}$, the equivalent commercial discount rate.
