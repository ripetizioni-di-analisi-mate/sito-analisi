On a €1000 claim, a bank withholds a fixed fee plus a commercial discount proportional to the time until maturity. The proceeds are €980 three months before maturity and €950 nine months before maturity. How large is the fixed fee alone?

- €5
- €20
- €30
- €15

## Explanation

The amount withheld rises from €20 to €50 over six months. The time-dependent part therefore rises by €5 per month. It accounts for €15 at three months, leaving €5 of the €20 withholding as the fixed fee.
