What annual commercial discount rate deducts $6\%$ of the maturity value over nine months?

- $8\%$
- $12\%$
- $4.5\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

Under commercial discount the fraction of the maturity value deducted is $\dfrac{D}{M}=dt$. Nine months is $t=\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a year, so
$$
d\cdot\frac{3}{4}=0.06,\qquad d=\frac{0.06\cdot4}{3}=0.08.
$$
The annual rate is $8\%$. The answer $4.5\%$ multiplies by $\dfrac{3}{4}$ instead of dividing. The answer $6\%$ ignores the term, while $12\%$ uses six months.
