A deposit has an annual compound interest rate of $-10\%$ and an opening balance of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is its balance after two years?

- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $810\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A rate of $-10\%$ gives an annual factor of $q=1-0.10=0.90$. Under compound interest this factor applies to the current balance once per year, so
$$
M=1000\cdot0.90^2=810\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses simple interest and deducts $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ twice. The answer $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the balance after one year, while $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the sign of the rate.
