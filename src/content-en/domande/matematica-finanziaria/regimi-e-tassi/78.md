A principal doubles in four years under a constant annual force of interest. What is that force of interest?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4\log 2$
- $\log 2$
- $\dfrac{\log 2}{4}$

## Explanation

At a constant force of interest the four-year factor is $e^{4\delta}$. Doubling the principal requires $e^{4\delta}=2$. Taking natural logarithms of both sides gives $4\delta=\log2$, so
$$
\delta=\frac{\log2}{4}\approx0.1733.
$$
The answer $4\log2$ multiplies by the term instead of dividing. The answer $\log2$ is the force of interest that doubles the principal in one year.
