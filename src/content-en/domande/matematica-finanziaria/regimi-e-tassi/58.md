Two deposits earn compound interest at effective rates of $2\%$ per month and $6\%$ per quarter, respectively. With equal initial principal, which has the larger balance at the end of the quarter?

- The deposit earning $2\%$ per month.
- The balances are equal.
- The deposit earning $6\%$ per quarter.
- It depends on the amount invested.

## Explanation

With equal principal, compare the accumulation factors over the quarter. The monthly deposit applies the factor $1.02$ three times, giving
$$
1.02^3=1.061208.
$$
The quarterly deposit has factor $1.06$. Since $1.061208>1.06$, the deposit earning $2\%$ per month gives a larger balance for any positive principal. The difference $0.001208$ is the interest earned on the interest from the first two months.
