The principal is $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the simple annual interest rate is $70\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the slope of the accumulated value as a function of the term in years?

- $70$ euros per year
- $0.07$ euros per year
- $1070$ euros per year
- $1000$ euros per year

## Explanation

Under simple interest, $M(t)=C+Cit$ is linear in $t$. The principal $C=1000$ is the intercept at $t=0$, and the coefficient of $t$ is $Ci$, one year's interest. The slope is therefore $70$ euros per year. The answer $0.07$ is the annual rate, which must be multiplied by the principal. The answer $1070$ is the accumulated value after one year.
