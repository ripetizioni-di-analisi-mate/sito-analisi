A deposit of $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earns an effective annual rate of $10\%$ under compound interest. Calculate its accumulated value after two years.

- $2420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under compound interest, each year's interest is added to the principal. The balance after one year is $2000\cdot1.10=2200\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so the second year earns $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in interest on that balance. Therefore
$$
M=2000\cdot1.10^2=2420\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $2400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the accumulated value under simple interest, which calculates both years' interest on the initial $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the total interest earned.
