A contract quotes a nominal annual rate of $12\%$ without stating the compounding frequency. What further information is needed to calculate the effective annual compound rate?

- The commercial discount rate.
- The number of compounding periods per year.
- The amount invested.
- The day on which the month begins.

## Explanation

The effective annual rate corresponding to a nominal rate is $i=\left(1+\dfrac{j^{(m)}}{m}\right)^m-1$, so it depends on $m$. The number of compounding periods determines both the rate per period and how many times its factor applies in one year. For example, a nominal rate of $12\%$ gives an effective annual rate of approximately $12.68\%$ for $m=12$ and approximately $12.55\%$ for $m=4$. The formula does not depend on the amount invested.
