The force of interest is $\delta(u)=0.02u$, with $u$ in years. What is the accumulation factor for the second year alone, from $u=1$ to $u=2$?

- $e^{0.03}$
- $e^{0.01}$
- $e^{0.02}$
- $e^{0.04}$

## Explanation

The factor from time $1$ to time $2$ is $\exp\left(\int_1^2\delta(u)\,du\right)$. An antiderivative of $0.02u$ is $0.01u^2$, so
$$
\int_1^2 0.02u\,du=0.01\cdot(4-1)=0.03,
$$
and the factor is $e^{0.03}$. The answer $e^{0.04}$ uses the integral from $0$ to $2$. The answer $e^{0.01}$ uses the first year's integral, which is smaller because the force of interest increases over time.
