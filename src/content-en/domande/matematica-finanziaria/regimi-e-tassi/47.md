A receivable of $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ due in one year is valued by rational discount at $10\%$. What is the discount in euros?

- $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $132\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under rational discount the present value is $V=\dfrac{M}{1+it}$. Substituting $M=1320$, $i=0.10$ and $t=1$ gives $V=\dfrac{1320}{1.10}=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The discount is $D=M-V=120\,\mathrm{EUR}$, or $10\%$ of $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $132\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the commercial discount, calculated on the maturity value, while $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the present value of the receivable.
