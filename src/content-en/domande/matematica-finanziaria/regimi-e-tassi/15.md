A period lasts $72$ actual days. Which day-count basis makes the year fraction exactly $\dfrac{1}{5}$?

- A $366$-day basis.
- A $72$-day basis.
- A $360$-day basis.
- A $365$-day basis.

## Explanation

Divide the actual day count by the number of days in the basis to obtain the year fraction. On a $360$-day basis this gives $\dfrac{72}{360}=\dfrac{1}{5}$ because $72\cdot5=360$. A $365$-day basis gives $\dfrac{72}{365}$ and a $366$-day basis gives $\dfrac{72}{366}$. Both fractions are below $\dfrac{1}{5}$ because the denominator is greater than $360$. A $72$-day basis would give $1$.
