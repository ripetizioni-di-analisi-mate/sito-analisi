A receivable of $6600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ due in one year is valued by rational discount at $10\%$ simple annual interest. Calculate its present value.

- $660\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5940\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $7260\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under rational discount the present value $V$ is the principal that would grow under simple interest to the amount due, so $M=V(1+it)$. With $t=1$ this gives
$$
V=\frac{6600}{1.10}=6000\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $5940\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses commercial discount and deducts $10\%$ of $6600\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $7260\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies by $1.10$ instead of dividing.
