The effective monthly compound interest rate is $2\%$. Which expression gives the equivalent quarterly accumulation factor?

- $1+3\cdot0.02$
- $1+0.02^3$
- $1.02^3$
- $1.02^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$

## Explanation

A quarter contains three months. Under compound interest the factor for the whole term is the product of the factors for its subperiods. The monthly factor is $1.02$, so the quarterly factor is $1.02^3$. The expression $1+3\cdot0.02$ simply adds three months' interest without compounding, as under simple interest. The expression $1.02^{\frac{1}{3}}$ converts in the opposite direction.
