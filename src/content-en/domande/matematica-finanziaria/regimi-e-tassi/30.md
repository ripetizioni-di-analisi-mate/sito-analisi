The annual accumulation factor for a compound-interest deposit is $q=1.25$. What is the annual discount factor?

- $0.75$
- $0.8$
- $1.25$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The discount factor $v$ converts an amount due in one year into its present value, so it is the reciprocal of the accumulation factor, $v=q^{-1}$. This gives
$$
v=\frac{1}{1.25}=0.8.
$$
The answer $0.75$ subtracts the rate of $25\%$ from $1$, as under commercial discount. The answer $0.25$ is the annual rate and $1.25$ is the accumulation factor.
