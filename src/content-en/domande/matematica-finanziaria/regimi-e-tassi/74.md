What effective annual rate is equivalent to the constant annual force of interest $\delta=\log(1.08)$?

- $108\%$
- $\log(1.08)\cdot100\%$
- $0.8\%$
- $8\%$

## Explanation

Under a constant force of interest the annual accumulation factor is $e^\delta$ and the equivalent effective annual rate is $i=e^\delta-1$. With $\delta=\log(1.08)$ we obtain
$$
e^{\log(1.08)}=1.08,
$$
because the exponential and natural logarithm are inverse functions. The effective annual rate is therefore $8\%$. The answer $\log(1.08)\cdot100\%\approx7.70\%$ expresses the force of interest as a percentage, while $108\%$ is the accumulation factor.
