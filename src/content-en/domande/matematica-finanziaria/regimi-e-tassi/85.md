A positive accumulation law satisfies $r(a,b)=r(a,c)r(c,b)$. Given $r(0,1)=1.04$ and $r(0,2)=1.092$, what is the factor for the second year?

- $1.04$
- $0.052$
- $1.052$
- $1.05$

## Explanation

Using $a=0$, $c=1$ and $b=2$ in the given identity gives $r(0,2)=r(0,1)\,r(1,2)$. The second-year factor is therefore
$$
r(1,2)=\frac{r(0,2)}{r(0,1)}=\frac{1.092}{1.04}=1.05.
$$
The answer $0.052$ subtracts the factors instead of dividing, while $1.052$ adds $1$ to that difference. The answer $1.04$ assumes that the factors are the same in both years.
