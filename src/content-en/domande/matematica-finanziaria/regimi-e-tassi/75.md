The accumulation factor is $r(t)=e^{0.01t^2}$, with $t$ measured in years. What is the force of interest at $t=2$?

- $0.02$
- $0.08$
- $0.01$
- $0.04$

## Explanation

The force of interest is $\delta(t)=\dfrac{r'(t)}{r(t)}$, the derivative of $\log r(t)$. Here $\log r(t)=0.01t^2$, so differentiating gives
$$
\delta(t)=0.02t,\qquad \delta(2)=0.04.
$$
The answer $0.02$ is the force of interest after one year. The answer $0.01$ is the coefficient in the exponent, while $0.08$ multiplies $0.02$ by $t^2$ instead of $t$.
