At a constant annual force of interest $\delta=0.06$, what is the six-month discount factor?

- $1-0.03$
- $e^{-0.03}$
- $e^{0.03}$
- $e^{-0.06}$

## Explanation

At a constant force of interest the accumulation factor for $t$ years is $e^{\delta t}$. Six months is $t=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the accumulation factor is $e^{0.06\cdot\frac{1}{2}}=e^{0.03}$. Its reciprocal gives the discount factor, $e^{-0.03}$. The expression $1-0.03$ is a simple commercial discount factor, while $e^{-0.06}$ is the one-year discount factor.
