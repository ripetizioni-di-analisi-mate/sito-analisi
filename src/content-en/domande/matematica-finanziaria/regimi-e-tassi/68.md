Two contracts quote the same nominal annual rate of $20\%$. One compounds annually and the other semiannually. How much do their effective annual rates differ?

- Two percentage points.
- One percentage point.
- Ten percentage points.
- They are equal.

## Explanation

With annual compounding the nominal and effective annual rates agree, so the first contract earns $20\%$. The second has a six-month rate of $10\%$ and an annual factor of
$$
1.10^2=1.21,
$$
giving an effective annual rate of $21\%$. The difference is one percentage point, equal to $0.10^2=0.01$, the second half-year's interest on the first half-year's interest.
