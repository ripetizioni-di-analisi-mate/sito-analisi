Convert a quarterly compound interest rate of $3\%$ to its equivalent effective six-month rate.

- $6.09\%$
- $3.09\%$
- $6\%$
- $12.55\%$

## Explanation

Six months contain two quarters, so under compound interest the six-month factor is the product of two quarterly factors. This gives
$$
1.03^2=1.0609,
$$
and the six-month rate is $6.09\%$. The answer $6\%$ adds the two quarterly rates and ignores the interest earned on the first quarter's interest. The answer $12.55\%$ is the annual rate $1.03^4-1$.
