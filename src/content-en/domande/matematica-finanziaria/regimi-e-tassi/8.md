What annual commercial discount rate gives the same present value over one year as rational discount at $25\%$ simple annual interest?

- $80\%$
- $20\%$
- $75\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

Two rates are equivalent over a term $t$ when they give the same discount factor, so $1-dt=\dfrac{1}{1+it}$. With $t=1$ and $i=0.25$ we have
$$
1-d=\frac{1}{1.25}=0.80,
$$
giving $d=0.20$, or $20\%$. The answer $80\%$ is the discount factor. The answer $25\%$ assumes that the two quoted rates are equal.
