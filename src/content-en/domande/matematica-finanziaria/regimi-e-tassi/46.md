What annual simple interest rate under rational discount is equivalent to a commercial discount rate of $10\%$ for a receivable due in two years?

- $25\%$
- $20\%$
- $10\%$
- $12.5\%$

## Explanation

Equivalent rates give the same discount factor. The two-year commercial discount factor is $1-0.10\cdot2=0.80$, so
$$
\frac{1}{1+2i}=0.80,\qquad 1+2i=1.25,\qquad i=0.125.
$$
The equivalent annual rate under rational discount is $12.5\%$. The answer $25\%$ gives the increase over both years and must be divided by the term. The answer $10\%$ assumes the rates are equal.
