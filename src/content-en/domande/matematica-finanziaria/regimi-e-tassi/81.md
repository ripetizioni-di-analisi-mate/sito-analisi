A deposit has accumulation factor $r(t)=1+0.05t$. Which expression gives its force of interest at $t$?

- $0.05$
- $\log(1+0.05t)$
- $\dfrac{0.05}{1+0.05t}$
- $0.05(1+0.05t)$

## Explanation

The force of interest is $\delta(t)=\dfrac{r'(t)}{r(t)}$, the rate of change of the accumulation factor divided by its current value. For $r(t)=1+0.05t$ we have $r'(t)=0.05$, so
$$
\delta(t)=\frac{0.05}{1+0.05t}.
$$
The constant $0.05$ is the derivative of the accumulation factor before division by $r(t)$. Under simple interest the force of interest decreases over time because the same growth in euros is measured against an increasing balance.
