Convert a compound annual rate of $44\%$ to its equivalent effective six-month rate.

- $22\%$
- $10\%$
- $44\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

Under compound interest the equivalent six-month rate $i_2$ satisfies $(1+i_2)^2=1+i$. The annual factor is $1.44$, so
$$
1+i_2=\sqrt{1.44}=1.2,
$$
and the six-month rate is $20\%$. The answer $22\%$ halves the annual rate, ignoring the interest that the first six months' earnings generate in the second half of the year. The answer $44\%$ is the annual rate itself.
