A statement shows consecutive quarterly returns of $+10\%$ and $-10\%$. A customer concludes that the half-year return was zero. How much must be added after the second quarter to restore an initial €200 investment?

- €0
- €2
- €20
- €4

## Explanation

The second return applies to €220. The balance is $220\cdot0.90=198$ euros, leaving €2 to restore the original principal.
