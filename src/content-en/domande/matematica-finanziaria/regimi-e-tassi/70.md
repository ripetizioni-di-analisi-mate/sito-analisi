A nominal annual rate of $40\%$ is compounded quarterly. What is the effective six-month compound rate?

- $46.41\%$
- $21\%$
- $10\%$
- $18.32\%$

## Explanation

The effective quarterly rate is $i_4=\dfrac{40\%}{4}=10\%$. Six months contain two quarters, so under compound interest the six-month factor is
$$
1.10^2=1.21,
$$
and the effective six-month rate is $21\%$. The answer $18.32\%$ treats the nominal $40\%$ as an effective annual rate and takes its square root. The answer $46.41\%$ is the effective annual rate $1.1^4-1$.
