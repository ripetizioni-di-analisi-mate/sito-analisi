Under simple interest a principal of $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ grows to $4060\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one quarter. What is the annual rate?

- $3\%$
- $1.5\%$
- $8\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

The interest earned is $4060-4000=60\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the term is $t=\dfrac{1}{4}$ of a year. From $I=Cit$ we obtain
$$
i=\frac{I}{Ct}=\frac{60}{4000\cdot\frac{1}{4}}=0.06,
$$
or $6\%$ per year. The answer $1.5\%$ is the return for one quarter and must be divided by the term in years to annualize it. The answer $3\%$ would correspond to a return of $1.5\%$ in six months.
