The force of interest is $0.02$ during year one and $0.06$ during year two. What is the accumulation factor over both years?

- $e^{0.12}$
- $e^{0.08}$
- $1.08$
- $e^{0.04}$

## Explanation

The two-year accumulation factor is $\exp\left(\int_0^2\delta(u)\,du\right)$. The force of interest is $0.02$ in year one and $0.06$ in year two, so the integral is
$$
0.02\cdot1+0.06\cdot1=0.08,
$$
and the factor is $e^{0.08}$. The answer $e^{0.12}$ applies the second year's force of interest to both years, while $1.08$ adds the two forces of interest as though they were simple interest rates.
