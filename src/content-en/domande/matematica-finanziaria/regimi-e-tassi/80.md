A constant annual force of interest equals $-0.03$. What can be said about the balance after two years if the initial principal is positive?

- The accumulated value is negative.
- The accumulated value equals the initial principal.
- The balance increases by $6\%$.
- The accumulated value is positive but less than the initial principal.

## Explanation

At a constant force of interest the two-year factor is $e^{2\delta}=e^{-0.06}\approx0.9418$. An exponential is always positive and, with a negative exponent, is less than $1$. The balance therefore remains positive but falls below the initial principal by approximately $5.8\%$. It cannot be negative because it is the product of $C>0$ and $e^{-0.06}>0$.
