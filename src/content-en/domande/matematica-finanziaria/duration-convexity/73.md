A single-payment bond matures in five years. What is its convexity with respect to the continuously compounded yield?

- $30$
- $25$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

As a function of the force of interest, its price is $Ce^{-5\delta}$. Differentiating twice gives $25\,Ce^{-5\delta}$, so convexity with respect to $\delta$ is
$$
\mathcal C_\delta=\frac{25\,Ce^{-5\delta}}{Ce^{-5\delta}}=5^2=25.
$$
This result is independent of the rate level. The value $30=5\cdot6$ is the factor $T(T+1)$ in convexity with respect to $y$. The value $5$ is duration, derived from the first derivative.
