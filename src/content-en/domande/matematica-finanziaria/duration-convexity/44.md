A bond is worth $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and its price derivative with respect to effective annual yield is $-1000$. What is the DV01?

- $0.03\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The derivative $P'(y)=-1000$ indicates a change of approximately $-1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ per unit of yield. One basis point is $10^{-4}$, so the estimated loss for one basis point is
$$
\mathrm{DV01}=1000\cdot10^{-4}=0.10\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Using $P\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,10^{-4}$ gives the same result because $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\dfrac{1000}{250}=4$. The value $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses $10^{-3}$, treating one basis point as one thousandth.
