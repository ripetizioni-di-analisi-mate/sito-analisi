A is worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has modified duration $6$. B is worth $3000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has modified duration $3$. For the same yield increase, which position loses more euros to first order?

- B, because it has the higher DV01.
- They lose the same amount.
- A, because its modified duration is higher.
- A, because its price is lower.

## Explanation

For the same shift, the loss in euros is proportional to DV01, hence to $P\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,10^{-4}$. For A this is $1000\cdot6\cdot10^{-4}=0.60\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and for B it is $3000\cdot3\cdot10^{-4}=0.90\,\mathrm{EUR}$. B therefore loses more euros. Choosing A because of its larger modified duration confuses the change in euros with the percentage change, which is indeed larger for A.
