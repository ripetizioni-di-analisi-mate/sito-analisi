At an effective annual yield of $10\%$, what is the convexity of a perpetuity with constant positive payments in arrears?

- $10$
- $200$
- $100$
- $20$

## Explanation

For a perpetuity paying $R$ in arrears, the price is $P=\dfrac{R}{y}$ and the second derivative is $P''=\dfrac{2R}{y^3}$. Convexity is the second derivative divided by the price, so
$$
\mathcal C=\frac{P''}{P}=\frac{2}{y^2}=\frac{2}{0.01}=200.
$$
The value $100$ omits the factor $2$ in the second derivative. The value $20$ is $\dfrac2y$, and $10$ is modified duration $\dfrac1y$.
