A bond pays in one and two years. At an effective annual yield of $20\%$, the payments have equal present values. Calculate convexity to four decimal places.

- $4.1667$
- $2.7778$
- $3.3333$
- $4$

## Explanation

The present values are equal, giving weights of $0.5$ and $0.5$. The factors $t_k(t_k+1)$ are $2$ and $6$, so the numerator of convexity is $0.5\cdot2+0.5\cdot6=4$. Dividing by $(1+y)^2=1.44$ gives
$$
\mathcal C=\frac{4}{1.44}=\frac{25}{9}\approx2.7778.
$$
The value $4$ omits the divisor, while $3.3333$ divides by $1.2$ only once.
