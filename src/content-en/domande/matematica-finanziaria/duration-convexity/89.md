Three prices of a bond with a convex price–yield curve, rounded to cents, form an arithmetic progression at equally spaced yields. What convexity does the centered estimate give using the rounded prices?

- Zero.
- The reciprocal of the central price.
- One.
- A negative number.

## Explanation

Three prices in arithmetic progression satisfy $P(y-h)-P(y)=P(y)-P(y+h)$. This gives $P(y+h)+P(y-h)=2P(y)$, so the numerator of the second difference is zero and estimated convexity is zero. This does not capture the bond's convex price curve: rounding to cents removes the difference, as for $100.40$, $100.00$, and $99.60\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
