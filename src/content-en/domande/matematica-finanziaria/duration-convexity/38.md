Two portfolios have the same price and modified duration. For the same small parallel yield shift, what determines the difference between their second-order estimated prices?

- The direction of the shock alone.
- The difference in convexity.
- The difference in initial prices.
- The difference in DV01.

## Explanation

To second order, $\dfrac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-D_{\mathrm{mod}}\Delta y+\dfrac{\mathcal C}{2}(\Delta y)^2$. The portfolios have the same price and modified duration, so their constant and linear terms agree. Their estimates therefore differ by
$$
P\,\frac{\mathcal C_1-\mathcal C_2}{2}\,(\Delta y)^2,
$$
which depends on the **difference in convexity**. Their DV01s are equal because $\mathrm{DV01}=P\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,10^{-4}$.
