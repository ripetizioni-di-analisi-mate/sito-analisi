Suppose the error in euros in the numerator of the second difference stays fixed. By what factor does its effect on the estimated second derivative change when the step size is halved?

- $0.5$
- $0.25$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

In the second-derivative estimate the numerator is divided by $h^2$. A fixed error $\varepsilon$ in the numerator therefore produces error $\dfrac{\varepsilon}{h^2}$. With step size $\dfrac h2$ this becomes
$$
\frac{\varepsilon}{\left(\frac h2\right)^2}=\frac{4\varepsilon}{h^2},
$$
so the effect is multiplied by $4$. The value $2$ treats the divisor as $h$, and $0.25$ reverses the ratio.
