At an effective annual yield of $25\%$, a bond paying at $1$ and $3$ years has convexity $4.48$. What share of the price comes from the three-year payment?

- $0.5$
- $0.75$
- $0.248$
- $0.7$

## Explanation

Multiplying convexity by $(1+y)^2=1.5625$ recovers the numerator $\sum_kt_k(t_k+1)w_k=4.48\cdot1.5625=7$. The factors for the two payment dates are $2$ and $12$. If $w$ is the three-year payment's weight, then
$$
2(1-w)+12w=2+10w=7,
$$
which gives $w=0.5$. The value $0.248$ solves $2+10w=4.48$ without multiplying by $1.5625$. The value $0.75$ uses $t_k^2$ in place of $t_k(t_k+1)$.
