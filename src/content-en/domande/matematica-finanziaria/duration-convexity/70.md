A zero-coupon bond with redemption payment $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ matures in nine months. What is its Macaulay duration in years?

- $0.09\,\mathrm{years}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1.3333\,\mathrm{years}$
- $9\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

The bond makes one payment, so the whole price is attributable to one date and Macaulay duration equals time to maturity. The redemption amount $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has no effect because that payment's weight is one. The term in years is
$$
D=\frac{9}{12}=0.75\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The value $9\,\mathrm{years}$ leaves the term in months, and $1.3333\,\mathrm{years}$ reverses the ratio.
