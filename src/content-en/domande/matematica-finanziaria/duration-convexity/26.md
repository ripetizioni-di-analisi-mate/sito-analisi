A bond has Macaulay duration $4.8\,\mathrm{years}$ and modified duration $4$. What is its effective annual yield?

- $12\%$
- $20\%$
- $25\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

The relationship $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\dfrac{D}{1+y}$ shows that the ratio of the durations is the annual accumulation factor,
$$
1+y=\frac{D}{D_{\mathrm{mod}}}=\frac{4.8}{4}=1.2.
$$
The effective annual yield is therefore $y=0.2$, or $20\%$. The value $80\%$ treats the difference $4.8-4$ as a yield.
