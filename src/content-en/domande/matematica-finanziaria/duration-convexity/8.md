A bond is worth $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has modified duration $3$. Estimate its new price to first order after a yield increase of $50$ basis points.

- $2030\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1997\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1970\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Fifty basis points corresponds to $\Delta y=50\cdot10^{-4}=0.005$. The first-order change in euros is
$$
\Delta P\approx-P\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,\Delta y=-2000\cdot3\cdot0.005=-30\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The yield rises, so the price falls, giving an estimated new price of $2000-30=1970\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $2030\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has the wrong sign, while $1997\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses $\Delta y=0.0005$, or five basis points.
