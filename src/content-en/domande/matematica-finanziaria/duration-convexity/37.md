For fixed positive cash flows with at least one future payment, compare the exact price with the tangent drawn at the initial yield. Where is the tangent at another yield?

- Above the exact price as yield rises and below it as yield falls.
- Below the exact price as yield rises and above it as yield falls.
- Below the exact price for either direction of the shift.
- Above the exact price for either direction of the shift.

## Explanation

With fixed positive cash flows, convexity is strictly positive, so price is a strictly convex function of yield. A strictly convex function lies above its tangent away from the point of tangency. The tangent is therefore **below** the exact price whether the yield rises or falls. The first-order approximation underestimates the price in both cases.
