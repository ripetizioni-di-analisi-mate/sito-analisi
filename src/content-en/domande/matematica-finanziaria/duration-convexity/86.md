With prices quoted at yields $y-0.002$ and $y+0.002$, what denominator is used in the centered first difference?

- $0$
- $0.004$
- $0.001$
- $0.002$

## Explanation

The numerator $P(y+h)-P(y-h)$ of the centered first difference compares prices whose yields are $2h$ apart. The denominator is therefore this distance,
$$
2h=2\cdot0.002=0.004.
$$
The value $0.002$ is just the step size $h$ and would double the estimated derivative. The value $0.001$ halves the step rather than doubling it.
