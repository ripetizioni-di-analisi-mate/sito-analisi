A bond is worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, with modified duration $5$ and convexity $40$. Estimate its price to second order after a two-percentage-point drop in yield.

- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1108\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1092\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1116\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A two-percentage-point drop gives $\Delta y=-0.02$. The linear term is $-D_{\mathrm{mod}}\Delta y=-5\cdot(-0.02)=0.10$ and the convexity adjustment is $\dfrac{\mathcal C}{2}(\Delta y)^2=20\cdot0.0004=0.008$. The relative change is therefore $0.108$ and
$$
P\approx1000\cdot1.108=1108\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ stops at first order. The value $1092\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the convexity adjustment, which is positive for shifts in either direction.
