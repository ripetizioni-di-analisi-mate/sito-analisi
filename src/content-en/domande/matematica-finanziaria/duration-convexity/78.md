A zero-coupon bond's redemption payment doubles and its yield stays fixed. Which pair of measures remains unchanged?

- DV01 and price.
- Price and convexity.
- Modified duration and convexity.
- Price and modified duration.

## Explanation

The zero-coupon bond has price $C(1+y)^{-T}$. Doubling $C$ doubles the price and its derivatives, leaving the ratios $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=-\dfrac{P'}{P}=\dfrac{T}{1+y}$ and $\mathcal C=\dfrac{P''}{P}=\dfrac{T(T+1)}{(1+y)^2}$ unchanged. The two measures depend only on $T$ and $y$. DV01 doubles because it equals $P\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,10^{-4}$ and includes the price.
