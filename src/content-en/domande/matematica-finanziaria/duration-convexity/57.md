Compare the linear estimate, quadratic estimate, and exact price. Which difference measures the signed error in the quadratic estimate?

- Exact price minus initial price.
- Quadratic price estimate minus exact price.
- Quadratic price estimate minus linear price estimate.
- Linear price estimate minus initial price.

## Explanation

The signed error equals the estimated price minus the exact price. For the quadratic estimate it is therefore
$$
\text{error}=P_{\text{quadratic}}-P_{\text{exact}} .
$$
The difference between the quadratic and linear estimates is the convexity contribution, $P\,\dfrac{\mathcal C}{2}(\Delta y)^2$. The exact price minus the initial price is the actual change $\Delta P$, which involves no approximation.
