A convexity estimate uses the numerator $N=P(y-h)-2P(y)+P(y+h)$. Both outer prices are exact, but the central price is recorded €0.05 too high. By how much does the numerator change?

- It increases by €0.10.
- It decreases by €0.05.
- It decreases by €0.10.
- It is unchanged.

## Explanation

The central price has coefficient $-2$ in the numerator. Adding €0.05 to that observation changes $N$ by $-2\cdot0.05=-0.10$ euros. The outer prices contribute no error.
