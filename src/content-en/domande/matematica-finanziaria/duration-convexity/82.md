The central price is $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At yields $y-0.01$ and $y+0.01$ the prices are $255$ and $245.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$, respectively. What is the finite-difference estimate of convexity?

- $40$
- $5000$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

The centered second difference estimates the second price derivative,
$$
P''(y)\approx\frac{255+245.5-2\cdot250}{0.01^2}=\frac{0.5}{0.0001}=5000.
$$
Convexity is the second derivative divided by the central price, so $\mathcal C\approx\dfrac{5000}{250}=20$. The value $5000$ omits division by price. The value $10$ also divides by $2$, as in the quadratic Taylor term.
