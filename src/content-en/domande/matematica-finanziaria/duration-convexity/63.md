Two positions are each worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Their modified durations are $2$ and $5$. The first yield rises by $50$ basis points and the second stays constant. What is the first-order change in total value?

- $-10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-35\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

To first order $\Delta V\approx-\sum_jP_jD_{\mathrm{mod},j}\Delta y_j$. Since the second yield does not change, its contribution is zero. Only the first position contributes, with $\Delta y=0.005$,
$$
-1000\cdot2\cdot0.005=-10\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $-35\,\mathrm{EUR}$ applies the shift to both positions. The value $-25\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses the duration of the position whose yield is unchanged.
