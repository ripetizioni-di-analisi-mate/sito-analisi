A bond has price $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and second derivative with respect to effective annual yield equal to $2000$. What is its convexity?

- $8$
- $0.125$
- $2$
- $2000$

## Explanation

Convexity is the second derivative of price divided by price, $\mathcal C=\dfrac{P''(y)}{P(y)}$. With the given data this gives
$$
\mathcal C=\frac{2000}{250}=8.
$$
This ratio is dimensionless and can be compared across bonds with different prices. The value $0.125$ is the reciprocal ratio $\dfrac{250}{2000}$, while $2000$ is the second derivative before normalization by price.
