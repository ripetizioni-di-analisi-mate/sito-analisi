At a yield of $5\%$, a bond is worth $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At $4\%$ and $6\%$, its prices are $206.20$ and $194.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the estimated modified duration at the central yield?

- $600$
- $-3$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The outer yields are each $h=0.01$ from the center, giving the following centered estimate of the first derivative:
$$
P'(y)\approx\frac{194.20-206.20}{0.02}=\frac{-12}{0.02}=-600.
$$
Modified duration is the negative of the derivative divided by the central price, giving $D_{\mathrm{mod}}\approx\dfrac{600}{200}=3$. The value $6$ divides by $h$ instead of by $2h$. The value $-3$ omits the sign change, and $600$ omits division by the price.
