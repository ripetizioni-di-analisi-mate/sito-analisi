With modified duration $4$, what relative price change does the first-order approximation give for a drop in yield of $75$ basis points?

- $3\%$
- $0.3\%$
- $1.875\%$
- $-3\%$

## Explanation

A fall of $75$ basis points corresponds to $\Delta y=-75\cdot10^{-4}=-0.0075$. To first order the relative change is $-D_{\mathrm{mod}}\Delta y$, so
$$
\frac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-4\cdot(-0.0075)=0.03=+3\%.
$$
The price rises because yield falls. The value $-3\%$ has the wrong sign because it treats the drop as an increase. The value $0.3\%$ uses $\Delta y=-0.00075$, or seven and a half basis points.
