A bond makes a positive payment in one year and another in three years. Which interval contains its Macaulay duration for every effective annual yield above $-100\%$?

- $3<D<4$ years.
- $0<D<1$ year.
- $1<D<3$ years.
- $D>4$ years.

## Explanation

For $y>-1$ the discount factor is positive. Both present values are therefore positive, and their weights $w_1$ and $w_3$ are positive and sum to one. Duration can be written as
$$
D=1\cdot w_1+3\cdot w_3=1+2w_3,
$$
with $0<w_3<1$. This gives $1<D<3$ years for every admissible yield. Duration therefore lies strictly between the first and last payment dates.
