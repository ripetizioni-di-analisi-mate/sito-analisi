Two positive payments fall due in one and five years. Macaulay duration is three years. What share of the price comes from the second payment?

- $75\%$
- $40\%$
- $60\%$
- $50\%$

## Explanation

Let $w$ be the share attributable to the five-year payment. Since the shares sum to one, the one-year payment has weight $1-w$. Macaulay duration is the weighted mean of the times, so
$$
D=1\cdot(1-w)+5w=1+4w.
$$
Setting $1+4w=3$ gives $w=\dfrac12$, or $50\%$ of the price. The value $60\%$ is the ratio $\dfrac35$ of duration to final maturity and ignores the first date.
