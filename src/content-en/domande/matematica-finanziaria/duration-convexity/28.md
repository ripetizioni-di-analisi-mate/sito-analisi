The same bond is valued using its effective semiannual yield $i$ or its nominal annual yield $j=2i$. How do the modified sensitivities with respect to the two parameters compare?

- $D_{\mathrm{mod},j}=D_{\mathrm{mod},i}$.
- $D_{\mathrm{mod},j}=2D_{\mathrm{mod},i}$.
- $D_{\mathrm{mod},j}=\dfrac{D_{\mathrm{mod},i}}{2}$.
- $D_{\mathrm{mod},j}=D_{\mathrm{mod},i}^2$.

## Explanation

Price is a function of the semiannual rate $i$ and $i=\dfrac j2$. By the chain rule,
$$
\frac{\mathrm dP}{\mathrm dj}=\frac{\mathrm dP}{\mathrm di}\cdot\frac12 .
$$
Dividing by $-P$ gives $D_{\mathrm{mod},j}=\dfrac{D_{\mathrm{mod},i}}{2}$. Sensitivity with respect to $j$ is lower because a given change in $j$ produces half as large a change in $i$. The expression $D_{\mathrm{mod},j}=2D_{\mathrm{mod},i}$ reverses the chain rule relationship.
