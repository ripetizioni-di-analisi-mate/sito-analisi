For a bond, a second-order model predicts a €31 gain when the yield falls by one percentage point and a €29 loss when it rises by the same amount. How many euros does the convexity term add to the linear prediction in each scenario?

- €2
- €30
- €0
- €1

## Explanation

The linear term changes sign when the shock reverses, while the quadratic term keeps its sign and magnitude. The average of the two signed changes is $\dfrac{31-29}{2}=1$ euro. This isolates the quadratic contribution.
