The initial price is $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, modified duration is $3$, and convexity is $20$. What is the second-order estimated price if the yield rises by one percentage point?

- $970\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $972\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1031\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $971\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A one-percentage-point rise gives $\Delta y=0.01$. The linear term is $-1000\cdot3\cdot0.01=-30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The convexity adjustment is $1000\cdot\dfrac{20}{2}\cdot0.01^2=1\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and is positive. The second-order price estimate is therefore
$$
1000-30+1=971\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $970\,\mathrm{EUR}$ stops at first order, $972\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the factor $\dfrac12$, and $1031\,\mathrm{EUR}$ gives the linear term the wrong sign.
