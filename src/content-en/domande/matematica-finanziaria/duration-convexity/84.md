With step size $0.01$, the centered estimate of the first derivative is $-300$ and the price at the lower yield is $103\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the price at the higher yield?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $102.97\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $97\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $109\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The centered estimate is $P'(y)\approx\dfrac{P(y+h)-P(y-h)}{2h}$, with $2h=0.02$. It follows that
$$
P(y+h)-P(y-h)=0.02\cdot(-300)=-6\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The price at the higher yield is therefore $103-6=97\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses $h$ instead of $2h$. The value $109\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reverses the derivative's sign, and $102.97\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies by $10^{-4}$.
