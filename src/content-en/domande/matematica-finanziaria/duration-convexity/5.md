At an effective annual yield of $20\%$, a bond pays $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $43.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years. Calculate its convexity with respect to the effective annual yield.

- $3.125$
- $4.5$
- $3.75$
- $2.0833$

## Explanation

The present values are $\dfrac{90}{1.2}=75\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $\dfrac{43.20}{1.2^3}=25\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so their weights are $0.75$ and $0.25$. The numerator of convexity is the weighted mean of the factors $t_k(t_k+1)$, namely $2\cdot0.75+12\cdot0.25=4.5$. Dividing by $(1+y)^2=1.44$ gives
$$
\mathcal C=\frac{4.5}{1.44}=3.125.
$$
The value $4.5$ omits the denominator, while $3.75$ divides by $1.2$ only once. The value $2.0833$ uses the factors $t_k^2$.
