At the initial force of interest, two payments at $1$ and $3$ years contribute equally to the price. Find the convexity with respect to the force of interest.

- $7$
- $10$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

With the force of interest as parameter, each present value is $C_ke^{-\delta t_k}$. Differentiating twice multiplies each term by $t_k^2$. Thus convexity with respect to $\delta$ is $\sum_k w_kt_k^2$. Since the contributions to the price are equal, both weights are $\dfrac12$ and
$$
\mathcal C_\delta=\frac{1^2+3^2}{2}=5.
$$
The value $7$ uses the factors $t_k(t_k+1)$ that arise when differentiating with respect to $y$. The value $4$ is squared duration, $2^2$.
