The central price is $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. With step size $0.01$, estimated centered convexity is $20$ and the price at the lower yield is $103.10\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the price at the higher yield?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $97.3\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $96.9\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $97.1\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The estimated convexity is $\dfrac{P(y+h)-2P(y)+P(y-h)}{h^2\,P(y)}$, so the numerator is $\mathcal C\,P(y)\,h^2=20\cdot100\cdot0.0001=0.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Hence
$$
P(y+h)=0.20+2\cdot100-103.10=97.10\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The price at the higher yield is lower because price decreases with yield. The value $96.90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the numerator, assuming zero convexity. The value $97.30\,\mathrm{EUR}$ doubles the numerator, and $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the central price.
