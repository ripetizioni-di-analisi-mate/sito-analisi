A program halves the yield step from $h$ to $h/2$ when estimating the second derivative of price. At either step the total absolute rounding error in the numerator can be as large as $\varepsilon$ euros. How does the upper bound on the rounding contribution to the derivative estimate change?

- It doubles.
- It halves.
- It stays the same.
- It quadruples.

## Explanation

At step $h$ the rounding error in the second difference quotient is bounded by $\dfrac{\varepsilon}{h^2}$. At step $\dfrac h2$ its denominator becomes $\dfrac{h^2}{4}$. The bound is multiplied by four.
