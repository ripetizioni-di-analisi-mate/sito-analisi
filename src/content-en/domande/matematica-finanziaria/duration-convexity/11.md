Two positions are worth $800$ and $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and have modified durations of $2$ and $4$, respectively. Find the portfolio's modified duration for a parallel shift.

- $3$
- $3.2$
- $6$
- $2.8$

## Explanation

The portfolio is worth $800+1200=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so its market-value weights are $\dfrac{800}{2000}=0.4$ and $\dfrac{1200}{2000}=0.6$. For a parallel shift, portfolio modified duration is the weighted average of the modified durations, giving
$$
D_{\mathrm{mod},V}=0.4\cdot2+0.6\cdot4=3.2.
$$
The value $3$ is the unweighted mean, $2.8$ reverses the weights, and $6$ adds the two durations.
