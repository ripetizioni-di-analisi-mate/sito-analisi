To second order, the estimated gain for a drop of $100$ basis points is $41\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and the estimated loss for an equal rise is $39\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the quadratic correction in each scenario?

- $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Let $L$ be the absolute value of the linear term and $Q$ the quadratic correction, the same for both scenarios. The gain on a drop is $L+Q=41$ and the loss on a rise is $L-Q=39$ because $Q$ is positive in both cases. Subtracting the two equations gives $2Q=2$, hence $Q=1\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the difference before division by two, while $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the linear term $L$.
