A bond is worth $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$, has DV01 of $0.72\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and experiences a yield increase of $10$ basis points. What is its estimated first-order price?

- $1807.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1728\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1799.28\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1792.8\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

DV01 is the estimated loss for a one-basis-point increase. The first-order loss for ten basis points is therefore ten times DV01,
$$
\Delta P\approx-0.72\cdot10=-7.20\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
giving an estimated price of $1800-7.20=1792.80\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $1799.28\,\mathrm{EUR}$ allows for only one basis point. The value $1807.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has the wrong sign and $1728\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses one hundred basis points.
