The yield changes from $6.4\%$ to $6.65\%$. Which decimal increment belongs in the modified duration formula?

- $0.0003$
- $0.0025$
- $0.025$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The modified duration formula uses the absolute yield change expressed as a decimal. The difference between the yields is $6.65\%-6.4\%=0.25$ percentage points, or $25$ basis points. In decimal form it is
$$
\Delta y=\frac{0.25}{100}=0.0025.
$$
The value $0.25$ gives the shift in percentage points. The value $0.025$ divides by $10$ instead of by $100$.
