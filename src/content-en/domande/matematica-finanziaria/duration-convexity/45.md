A bond has DV01 of $0.35\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Which scenario gives a first-order estimated price increase of $3.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

- The yield rises by ten basis points.
- The yield falls by one percentage point.
- The yield falls by one basis point.
- The yield falls by ten basis points.

## Explanation

To first order, a shift of $n$ basis points gives a price change of $-\mathrm{DV01}\cdot n$. A price increase therefore requires $n<0$, a drop in yield. From $-0.35\,n=3.50$ we get $n=-10$, so the yield falls by ten basis points. A ten-basis-point increase gives a loss of $3.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$, whereas a one-basis-point decrease gives a gain of only $0.35\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
