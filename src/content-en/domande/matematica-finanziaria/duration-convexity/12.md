Position A is worth $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has modified duration $2$. Position B is worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has modified duration $3$. A's yield falls by $50$ basis points and B's rises by $50$. Estimate the first-order portfolio change in euros.

- $-10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The yields shift by different amounts, so add the position changes, $\Delta V\approx-\sum_j P_jD_{\mathrm{mod},j}\Delta y_j$. For A, $\Delta y=-0.005$ gives a change of $-500\cdot2\cdot(-0.005)=+5\,\mathrm{EUR}$. For B, $\Delta y=0.005$ gives a change of $-1000\cdot3\cdot0.005=-15\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The net change is $-10\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$ assumes the opposite shifts cancel, and $-20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ counts A's gain as a loss.
