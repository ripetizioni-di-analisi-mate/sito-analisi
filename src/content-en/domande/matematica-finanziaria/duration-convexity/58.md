At an effective annual yield of $25\%$ a bond has Macaulay duration $4\,\mathrm{years}$. What is the relative sensitivity $-\dfrac{1}{P}\dfrac{\mathrm dP}{\mathrm d\delta}$ with respect to the force of interest $\delta$?

- $3.2$
- $5$
- $4$
- $16$

## Explanation

With $\delta=\log(1+y)$, each present value is $C_ke^{-\delta t_k}$, and differentiation with respect to $\delta$ multiplies each term by $-t_k$. Dividing by $-P$ therefore gives
$$
-\frac{1}{P}\frac{\mathrm dP}{\mathrm d\delta}=\sum_kw_kt_k=D=4.
$$
The divisor $1+y$ does not arise because differentiation of the exponential introduces no such factor. The value $3.2=\dfrac{4}{1.25}$ is modified duration with respect to $y$. The value $16$ is $D^2$, and $5$ multiplies by $1+y$.
