A zero-coupon bond and a bond with positive coupons both mature in five years and have the same yield. The coupon bond pays at least one coupon before redemption. Which has the greater Macaulay duration?

- Face value is needed to compare them.
- The coupon bond.
- The zero-coupon bond.
- Their durations are equal.

## Explanation

The zero-coupon bond's entire price comes from its maturity payment, so its Macaulay duration is $5\,\mathrm{years}$. The coupon bond also has positive weights on earlier dates because the coupons and discount factors are positive. Its weighted average payment time is therefore strictly less than $5$ years. The **zero-coupon bond** has greater duration. Face value is unnecessary because only the price weights matter.
