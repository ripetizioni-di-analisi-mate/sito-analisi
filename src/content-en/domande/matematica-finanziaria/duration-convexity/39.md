At an effective annual yield of $25\%$, a bond has Macaulay duration $3\,\mathrm{years}$. Its one-year payment has price weight $0.2$. What is the derivative of that weight with respect to yield?

- $-0.32$
- $1.6$
- $0.32$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

The derivative of a cash flow weight is $w'_k=\dfrac{D-t_k}{1+y}\,w_k$. Substitution of $D=3$, $t_k=1$, $w_k=0.2$, and $y=0.25$ gives
$$
w'_k=\frac{3-1}{1.25}\cdot0.2=0.32.
$$
The sign is positive because the payment falls before the duration and gains weight as yield increases. The value $-0.32$ uses $t_k-D$, while $0.4$ omits the divisor $1+y$. The value $1.6$ omits the weight $w_k$.
