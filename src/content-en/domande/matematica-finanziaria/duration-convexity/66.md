A portfolio holds equal market values of two bonds with different modified durations. Which weights enter portfolio modified duration under a parallel shift?

- Weights proportional to the individual durations.
- Weights proportional to the quantities held.
- One half for each bond.
- Weights proportional to the final maturities.

## Explanation

The weights in portfolio duration are the market-value shares $\omega_j=\dfrac{x_jP_j}{V}$. The positions have equal value, so each has weight $\dfrac12$ regardless of the number of units held. Portfolio modified duration is therefore the simple average of the two modified durations. Weighting by quantity ignores the unit prices that enter the position values.
