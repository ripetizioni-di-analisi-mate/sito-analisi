A position worth $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has a DV01 of $0.90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ per basis point. What is its modified duration?

- $0.45$
- $4.5$
- $9$
- $2.25$

## Explanation

DV01 measures the estimated loss for one basis point, so $\mathrm{DV01}=P\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,10^{-4}$. With $P=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, the product $P\cdot10^{-4}$ equals $0.2$, giving
$$
D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\frac{0.90}{0.2}=4.5.
$$
The value $0.45$ uses $10^{-3}$ in place of $10^{-4}$. The value $9$ divides DV01 by $0.1$ rather than $0.2$.
