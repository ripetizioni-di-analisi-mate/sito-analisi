An analyst estimates the relative price change for a yield increase from $8\%$ to $9\%$. What increment should be multiplied by the negative of modified duration?

- $1$
- $0.125$
- $0.1$
- $0.01$

## Explanation

In the estimate $\dfrac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-D_{\mathrm{mod}}\Delta y$, the shift $\Delta y$ is the absolute difference between the yields in decimal form. Thus
$$
\Delta y=0.09-0.08=0.01.
$$
The value $0.125$ is the ratio $\dfrac{0.01}{0.08}$, the relative change in yield, which does not enter the formula. The value $1$ is the shift in percentage points.
