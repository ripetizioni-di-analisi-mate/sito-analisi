The annual effective yield of a bond is $8\%$ and it increases by $12.5\%$ of its initial value. What value of $\Delta y$ is used in the first-order estimate $\dfrac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,\Delta y$?

- $0.125$
- $0.09$
- $0.00125$
- $0.01$

## Explanation

An increase of $12.5\%$ of the initial value brings the yield to $0.08\cdot1.125=0.09$, that is, to $9\%$. In the first-order estimate $\Delta y$ is the absolute difference between the two yields in decimal form, therefore
$$
\Delta y=0.09-0.08=0.01.
$$
The value $0.125$ is the relative change of the yield, which does not appear in the formula. The value $0.09$ is the new yield and the value $0.00125$ corresponds to $12.5$ basis points.
