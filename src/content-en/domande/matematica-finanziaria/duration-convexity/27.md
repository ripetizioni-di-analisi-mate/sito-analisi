A bond has Macaulay duration $2\,\mathrm{years}$. The nominal annual yield compounded quarterly is $8\%$. Estimate the first-order relative price change if the nominal yield rises by $50$ basis points.

- $+0.98\%$
- $-3.92\%$
- $-9.80\%$
- $-0.98\%$

## Explanation

A cash flow at time $t$ in years has discount factor $\left(1+\dfrac j4\right)^{-4t}$, so differentiation with respect to $j$ introduces the factor $-\dfrac{t}{1+\frac j4}$. The sensitivity to the nominal yield is therefore $D_{\mathrm{mod},j}=\dfrac{2}{1.02}\approx1.9608$. With $\Delta j=0.005$ this gives
$$
\frac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-1.9608\cdot0.005\approx-0.98\%.
$$
The value $-3.92\%$ uses sensitivity to the quarterly rate, which is four times as large. The value $-9.80\%$ treats $50$ basis points as $0.05$.
