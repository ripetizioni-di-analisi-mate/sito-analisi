A five-year zero-coupon bond is valued at an effective annual rate of $25\%$. What is its modified duration?

- $6.25$
- $5$
- $3.75$
- $4$

## Explanation

A zero-coupon bond's entire value comes from the payment at maturity, so its Macaulay duration is $D=T=5\,\mathrm{years}$. Divide by $1+y$ to obtain modified duration:
$$
D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\frac{5}{1.25}=4.
$$
The value $6.25$ multiplies by $1.25$ instead of dividing, and $5$ is Macaulay duration. The value $3.75$ subtracts $1.25$ from the term to maturity.
