A two-year zero-coupon bond is valued at an effective annual yield of $-20\%$. What is its modified duration?

- $1.6$
- $2.5$
- $2$
- $1.6667$

## Explanation

The yield $y=-0.2$ is admissible because $y>-1$. The bond has Macaulay duration $D=2\,\mathrm{years}$ and annual factor $1+y=0.8$. Its modified duration is therefore
$$
D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\frac{2}{0.8}=2.5.
$$
At a negative yield, modified duration exceeds Macaulay duration. The value $1.6$ multiplies by $0.8$, and $1.6667$ divides by $1.2$ as though the yield were positive.
