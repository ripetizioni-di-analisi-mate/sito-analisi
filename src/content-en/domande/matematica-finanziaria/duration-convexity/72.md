A portfolio imposes a modified-duration limit of $4$ years on each individual position. A zero-coupon bond yields $-20\%$ effective annually and the available maturities are positive whole years. What is the longest admissible maturity?

- $4$ years
- $3$ years
- $5$ years
- $2$ years

## Explanation

At maturity $T$ the modified duration is $\dfrac{T}{1+y}=\dfrac{T}{0.8}$. The cap requires $T\le3.2$. Three years is the longest whole-year maturity that meets it.
