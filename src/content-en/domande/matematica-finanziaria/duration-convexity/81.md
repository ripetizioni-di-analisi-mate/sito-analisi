Two quoted yields are each $0.01$ from the central yield. The price at the lower yield is $202$ and at the higher yield is $198\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the centered estimate of the first derivative?

- $200$
- $-4$
- $-200$
- $-400$

## Explanation

The centered finite-difference estimate of the first derivative is
$$
P'(y)\approx\frac{P(y+h)-P(y-h)}{2h}=\frac{198-202}{0.02}=-200.
$$
The denominator $0.02$ is the distance between the two outer yields. The sign is negative because price falls as yield rises. The value $-400$ divides by $0.01$, the value $200$ has the wrong sign, and $-4$ gives the price difference without division.
