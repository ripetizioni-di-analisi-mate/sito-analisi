At an effective annual yield of $25\%$, modified duration is $2.4$. Find Macaulay duration.

- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $2.65\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1.92\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

The relationship $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\dfrac{D}{1+y}$ implies $D=(1+y)\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}$. With $y=0.25$ this gives
$$
D=1.25\cdot2.4=3\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
Macaulay duration exceeds modified duration because the yield is positive and $1+y$ exceeds one. The value $1.92\,\mathrm{years}$ divides by $1.25$ instead of multiplying. The value $2.65\,\mathrm{years}$ adds the yield to modified duration.
