A and B have positive convexity. How does portfolio convexity for a parallel shift change if all holdings are doubled?

- It becomes the sum of the individual convexities.
- It stays the same.
- It doubles.
- It is halved.

## Explanation

Doubling all quantities doubles the portfolio value $V=\sum_jx_jP_j$ and its second derivative $V''=\sum_jx_jP_j''$. Portfolio convexity is their ratio $\dfrac{V''}{V}$, so it **stays the same**. Market-value weights also stay the same because each numerator and denominator doubles. The claim that convexity doubles confuses the ratio with the second derivative.
