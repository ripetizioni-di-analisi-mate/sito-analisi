A portfolio holds two units of A at $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ each and one unit of B at $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Their convexities are $10$ and $25$. What is portfolio convexity for a parallel yield shift?

- $19$
- $35$
- $16$
- $17.5$

## Explanation

The positions are worth $2\cdot300=600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$, giving a total of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and market-value weights of $0.6$ and $0.4$. Under a parallel shift, portfolio convexity is the weighted mean of the individual convexities,
$$
\mathcal C_V=0.6\cdot10+0.4\cdot25=16.
$$
The value $17.5$ is the unweighted mean, $19$ reverses the weights, and $35$ adds the two convexities.
