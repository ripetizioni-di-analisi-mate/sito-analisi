At an effective annual yield of $20\%$, a bond has Macaulay duration $2.4\,\mathrm{years}$. Calculate its modified duration.

- $2.4$
- $2$
- $2.2$
- $2.88$

## Explanation

Modified duration with respect to the effective annual yield is $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=-\dfrac{P'}{P}=\dfrac{D}{1+y}$ because differentiating $(1+y)^{-t_k}$ introduces the factor $-\dfrac{t_k}{1+y}$ for each cash flow. With $y=0.2$ this gives
$$
D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\frac{2.4}{1.2}=2.
$$
The value $2.88$ multiplies by $1.2$ instead of dividing. The value $2.2$ subtracts the yield from duration, and $2.4$ is Macaulay duration itself.
