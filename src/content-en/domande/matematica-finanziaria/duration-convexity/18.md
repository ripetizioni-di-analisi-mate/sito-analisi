Two cash flows have equal present values, and the first is due in one year. Macaulay duration is $2\,\mathrm{years}$. When is the second payment due?

- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $2\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

Since the present values are equal, each payment has weight $\dfrac12$. If $t$ is the time of the second payment, duration is the simple average of the two times, so
$$
\frac{1+t}{2}=2.
$$
This equation gives $1+t=4$, hence $t=3\,\mathrm{years}$. The value $4\,\mathrm{years}$ doubles duration without subtracting the first payment time, and $2\,\mathrm{years}$ confuses the payment date with duration.
