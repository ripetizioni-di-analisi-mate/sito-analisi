A zero-coupon bond matures in four years and is valued at an effective annual yield of $25\%$. What is its convexity with respect to that yield?

- $16$
- $10.24$
- $20$
- $12.8$

## Explanation

For a zero-coupon bond maturing at $T$, convexity with respect to effective annual yield is $\mathcal C=\dfrac{T(T+1)}{(1+y)^2}$ because the second derivative of $(1+y)^{-T}$ is $T(T+1)(1+y)^{-T-2}$. With $T=4$ and $y=0.25$ this gives
$$
\mathcal C=\frac{20}{1.5625}=12.8.
$$
The value $20$ omits the divisor. The value $16$ is $T^2$, convexity with respect to the force of interest, while $10.24$ divides $T^2$ by $1.5625$.
