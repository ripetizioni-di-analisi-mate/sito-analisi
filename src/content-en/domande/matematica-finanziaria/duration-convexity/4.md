A zero-coupon bond matures in two and a half years. Its nominal annual yield compounded semiannually is $10\%$. Calculate modified duration with respect to the nominal yield, to four decimal places.

- $4.7619$
- $5$
- $1.1905$
- $2.3810$

## Explanation

There are $5$ half-years to maturity and the semiannual rate is $\dfrac{j}{2}=0.05$. The price is proportional to $\left(1+\dfrac j2\right)^{-5}$, so differentiation with respect to $j$ also introduces the inner derivative $\dfrac12$. This gives
$$
D_{\mathrm{mod},j}=\frac{5}{2\cdot1.05}\approx2.3810.
$$
The value $4.7619$ is the sensitivity to the semiannual rate, and $5$ is the number of half-years. The value $1.1905$ divides by $2$ twice.
