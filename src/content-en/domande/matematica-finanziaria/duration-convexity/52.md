A bond is worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. For a rise of two percentage points, its second-order price estimate exceeds its linear estimate by $6\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is its convexity?

- $30$
- $15$
- $60$
- $3$

## Explanation

The difference between the second-order and linear estimates is the convexity adjustment $P\,\dfrac{\mathcal C}{2}(\Delta y)^2$. With $\Delta y=0.02$ this gives
$$
1000\cdot\frac{\mathcal C}{2}\cdot0.0004=0.2\,\mathcal C .
$$
Setting $0.2\,\mathcal C=6$ gives $\mathcal C=30$. The value $15$ omits the factor $\dfrac12$, while $60$ applies it twice.
