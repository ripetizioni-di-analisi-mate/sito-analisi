The linear price estimate after a one-percentage-point yield drop is $1040\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The initial price is $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and convexity is $50$. What is the second-order estimate?

- $1045\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1040\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1042.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1037.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The quadratic correction is based on the initial price and is positive for a nonzero shift in either direction, including a yield decline. With $\Delta y=-0.01$ it equals
$$
1000\cdot\frac{50}{2}\cdot0.01^2=2.50\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The second-order price is therefore $1040+2.50=1042.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The value $1037.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts the correction, while $1045\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the factor $\dfrac12$.
