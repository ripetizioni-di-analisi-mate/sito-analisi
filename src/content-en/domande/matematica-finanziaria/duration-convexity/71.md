Two zero-coupon bonds mature in two and six years. At the common yield they contribute equally to total price. What is the Macaulay duration of the combined cash flows?

- $2\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $8\,\mathrm{years}$
- $6\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

The combined cash flows fall in years two and six. Since the two bonds contribute equally to the price, each weight is $\dfrac12$. Macaulay duration is therefore the simple mean of the two times,
$$
D=\frac{2+6}{2}=4\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The value $8\,\mathrm{years}$ adds the maturities without dividing. The values $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{years}$ are the individual maturities.
