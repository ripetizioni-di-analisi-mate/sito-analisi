The price is $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and a yield increase of $20$ basis points gives a first-order estimated loss of $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the modified duration?

- $4$
- $0.4$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

To first order, the relative loss is $D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,\Delta y$. The relative loss here is $\dfrac{4}{500}=0.008$ and the yield shift is $\Delta y=20\cdot10^{-4}=0.002$, so
$$
D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\frac{0.008}{0.002}=4.
$$
The value $0.4$ uses $\Delta y=0.02$, or two hundred basis points. The value $8$ uses $\Delta y=0.001$, or ten basis points.
