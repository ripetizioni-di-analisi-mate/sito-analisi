Two cash flows due in one and three years have present-value weights of $0.2$ and $0.8$ at the initial yield. What is convexity with respect to the force of interest?

- $2.6$
- $10$
- $9.6$
- $7.4$

## Explanation

With the force of interest as parameter, each present value is $C_ke^{-\delta t_k}$ and the second derivative multiplies each term by $t_k^2$. Convexity with respect to $\delta$ is therefore the weighted mean of the squared times,
$$
\mathcal C_\delta=0.2\cdot1^2+0.8\cdot3^2=0.2+7.2=7.4.
$$
The value $10$ uses the factors $t_k(t_k+1)$ that arise for differentiation with respect to $y$. The value $2.6$ is duration $0.2\cdot1+0.8\cdot3$, the weighted mean of the times themselves.
