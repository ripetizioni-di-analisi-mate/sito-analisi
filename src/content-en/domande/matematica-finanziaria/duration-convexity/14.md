At yield $4\%$ the price is $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At $3\%$ and $5\%$ the respective prices are $104.20$ and $96.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Estimate convexity at the central yield using a centered finite difference.

- $4000$
- $4$
- $20$
- $40$

## Explanation

The outer yields are each $h=0.01$ from the central yield. The centered finite difference estimates the second price derivative,
$$
P''(y)\approx\frac{104.20-2\cdot100+96.20}{0.01^2}=\frac{0.40}{0.0001}=4000.
$$
Convexity is the second price derivative divided by the price, giving $\mathcal C\approx\dfrac{4000}{100}=40$. The value $4000$ omits division by price, while $20$ also divides by $2$ as in the quadratic Taylor term.
