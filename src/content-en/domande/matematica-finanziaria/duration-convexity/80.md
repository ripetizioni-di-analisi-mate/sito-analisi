A zero-coupon bond matures in eight years. Its yield rises while the valuation date stays fixed. Which pair of effects is correct?

- Macaulay duration falls and modified duration stays fixed.
- Macaulay duration stays fixed and modified duration falls.
- Both durations increase.
- Both durations stay fixed.

## Explanation

A zero-coupon bond makes a single payment, so Macaulay duration equals its eight-year maturity at every yield. Modified duration is $\dfrac{8}{1+y}$, which decreases as $y$ rises. Macaulay duration decreases with yield for bonds with at least two distinct payment dates. For a single payment the dispersion of payment times around duration is zero.
