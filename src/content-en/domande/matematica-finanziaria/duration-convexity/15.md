A bond pays only in year one and year four. Its Macaulay duration is $3.4$ years and its price is €500. How much of that price is the present value of the later payment?

- €400
- €100
- €340
- €425

## Explanation

If $w$ is the present-value weight on the four-year payment, then $1(1-w)+4w=3.4$. Thus $w=0.8$ and the later payment accounts for $0.8\cdot500=400$ euros of the price.
