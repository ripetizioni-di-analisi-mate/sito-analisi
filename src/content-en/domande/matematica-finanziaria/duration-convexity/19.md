A portfolio contains two units of bond A, each with DV01 of $0.60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How many units of bond B, with DV01 of $0.40\,\mathrm{EUR}$ per unit, must be added to give a portfolio DV01 of $2.40\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Portfolio DV01 is the sum of the position DV01s. The two units of A contribute $2\cdot0.60=1.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so B must contribute $2.40-1.20=1.20\,\mathrm{EUR}$. If $x$ is the number of B units, $0.40\,x=1.20$ gives $x=3$. The value $6$ ignores A's contribution. The value $4$ divides $2.40$ by A's DV01, while $2$ divides the remaining amount by $0.60$.
