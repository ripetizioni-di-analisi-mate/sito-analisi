A bond is worth $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has convexity $25$. What is the quadratic adjustment alone for a two-percentage-point yield shock?

- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The quadratic adjustment in euros is $P\,\dfrac{\mathcal C}{2}(\Delta y)^2$. With $\Delta y=0.02$ we have $(\Delta y)^2=0.0004$, so
$$
2000\cdot\frac{25}{2}\cdot0.0004=2000\cdot12.5\cdot0.0004=10\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The correction is positive for a rise or a fall in yield. The value $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the factor $\dfrac12$, and $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides by two a second time.
