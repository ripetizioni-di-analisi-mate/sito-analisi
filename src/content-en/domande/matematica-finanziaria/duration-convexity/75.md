A perpetuity pays $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of each year and has an effective annual yield of $20\%$. What is its modified duration?

- $5$
- $6$
- $20$
- $100$

## Explanation

A perpetuity payable in arrears has price $P=\dfrac{R}{y}=\dfrac{20}{0.2}=100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and derivative $P'=-\dfrac{R}{y^2}$. Modified duration is therefore
$$
D_{\mathrm{mod}}=-\frac{P'}{P}=\frac1y=\frac{1}{0.2}=5.
$$
The payment cancels in the ratio. The value $6$ is Macaulay duration $\dfrac{1+y}{y}$, while $100$ is the price and $20$ is the payment.
