Given modified duration $5$ and convexity $40$, what relative change does the second-order Taylor approximation predict for a yield increase of $100$ basis points?

- $-4.8\%$
- $-5\%$
- $-5.2\%$
- $-4.6\%$

## Explanation

One hundred basis points corresponds to $\Delta y=0.01$. The second-order Taylor expansion gives
$$
\frac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-5\cdot0.01+\frac{40}{2}\cdot0.01^2=-0.05+0.002=-0.048.
$$
The estimated relative change is therefore $-4.8\%$. The value $-5\%$ stops at first order. The value $-5.2\%$ subtracts the quadratic adjustment, and $-4.6\%$ omits the factor $\dfrac12$.
