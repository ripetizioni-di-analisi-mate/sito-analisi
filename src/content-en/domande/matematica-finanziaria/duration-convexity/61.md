For a parallel shift, a portfolio of two bonds with modified durations $2$ and $7$ has modified duration $5$. What is the market-value weight of the bond with duration $7$?

- $60\%$
- $40\%$
- $70\%$
- $50\%$

## Explanation

For a parallel shift, portfolio modified duration is the market-value-weighted average of the two durations. If $w$ is the weight of the bond with duration $7$, the other has weight $1-w$, so
$$
2(1-w)+7w=2+5w=5.
$$
This yields $5w=3$ and hence $w=0.6$, or $60\%$. The value $40\%$ is the weight of the bond with duration $2$. A weight of $50\%$ would imply total duration $4.5$.
