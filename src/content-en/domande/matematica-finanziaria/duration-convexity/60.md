The per-unit DV01s of A and B are $0.20$ and $0.55\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The portfolio holds two units of A and three of B. What is its DV01?

- $0.75\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2.05\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Portfolio DV01 is the sum of the position DV01s. Each position's DV01 equals the unit DV01 times its quantity, so
$$
\mathrm{DV01}_V=2\cdot0.20+3\cdot0.55=0.40+1.65=2.05\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $0.75\,\mathrm{EUR}$ adds unit DV01s without accounting for quantities. The value $1.7\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reverses the quantities, and $3.75\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies $0.75$ by the total number of units.
