At an effective annual yield of $25\%$, two payments at $1$ and $3$ years each account for a share $0.5$ of the price. What is the derivative of Macaulay duration with respect to yield?

- $0.8$
- $-1$
- $-0.8$
- $-1.25$

## Explanation

With equal weights duration is $D=\dfrac{1+3}{2}=2\,\mathrm{years}$. The dispersion of the times around duration is $\sum_kw_k(t_k-D)^2=0.5\cdot1+0.5\cdot1=1$. The duration derivative is the negative of this dispersion divided by $1+y$, so
$$
D'(y)=-\frac{1}{1.25}=-0.8.
$$
The value $0.8$ has the wrong sign, $-1$ omits the divisor $1+y$, and $-1.25$ multiplies instead of dividing.
