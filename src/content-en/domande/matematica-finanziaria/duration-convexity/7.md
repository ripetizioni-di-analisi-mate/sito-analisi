A position is worth $1600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has modified duration $4$. Calculate its DV01 in euros per basis point.

- $0.16\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0.64\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

DV01 is the first-order estimated loss for a one-basis-point increase in yield, or an increase of $10^{-4}$ in decimal form. The linear price change satisfies $\Delta P\approx-P\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\,\Delta y$, so
$$
\mathrm{DV01}=1600\cdot4\cdot10^{-4}=0.64\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $6.4\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses $10^{-3}$, treating a basis point as one thousandth. The value $0.16\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits modified duration.
