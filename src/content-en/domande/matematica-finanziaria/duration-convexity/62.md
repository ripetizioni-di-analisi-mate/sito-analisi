A and B are worth $500$ and $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and have modified durations $2$ and $4$, respectively. What is the first-order change for a parallel upward shift of $+20$ basis points?

- $14\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-28\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A parallel rise of $20$ basis points increases all yields by $\Delta y=0.002$. The total sensitivity in euros per unit of yield is $500\cdot2+1500\cdot4=7000$. To first order this gives
$$
\Delta V\approx-7000\cdot0.002=-14\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The value $14\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has the wrong sign. The value $-28\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses $\Delta y=0.004$, while $-7\,\mathrm{EUR}$ uses $\Delta y=0.001$.
