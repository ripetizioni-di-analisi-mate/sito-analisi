The yield on A rises by $10$ basis points and the yield on B falls by $15$. The positions have DV01s of $1.20$ and $0.80\,\mathrm{EUR}$, respectively. What is the estimated net change?

- $24\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

DV01 gives the loss per basis point of yield increase, so each basis point of yield decrease produces an equal first-order gain. A loses $1.20\cdot10=12\,\mathrm{EUR}$ because its yield rises ten basis points. B gains $0.80\cdot15=12\,\mathrm{EUR}$ because its yield falls fifteen basis points. The net first-order change is therefore zero. The value $-24\,\mathrm{EUR}$ counts B's gain as a loss.
