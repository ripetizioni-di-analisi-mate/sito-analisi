The assets are worth $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and have convexity $10\,\mathrm{years}^2$. The liabilities are worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and have convexity $11\,\mathrm{years}^2$. Calculate the second derivative of the surplus with respect to the force of interest.

- $-1000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$
- $12000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$
- $-1200\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$

## Explanation

The second derivative of a present value with respect to the force of interest equals that value times its convexity. Thus $A''=1200\cdot10=12000$ and $L''=1000\cdot11=11000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$, so
$$
S''=A''-L''=12000-11000=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2 .
$$
The answer $-1000$ uses $L_0(C_A-C_L)$, which applies only when the assets and liabilities have equal present values.
