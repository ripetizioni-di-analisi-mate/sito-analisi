Two debts due at times $2$ and $5$ have present values of $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$, respectively. Two zero-coupon bonds with maturities $1$ and $7$ are used to match their value and duration. Calculate the market value of the holding in the longer bond.

- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The liabilities are worth $300+600=900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and their duration is
$$
D_L=\frac{300\cdot2+600\cdot5}{900}=\frac{3600}{900}=4\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The fraction in the longer bond is $w_2=\dfrac{4-1}{7-1}=\dfrac12$, giving a holding worth $900\cdot\dfrac12=450\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ copies the second liability without matching the overall duration, while $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ copies the first.
