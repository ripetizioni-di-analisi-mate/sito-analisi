A liability with a duration of $2$ years must be covered with two zero-coupon bonds maturing in $2$ and $9$ years. Which allocation with nonnegative holdings matches the duration?

- A strictly positive weight in the nine-year bond.
- The entire investment in the two-year bond.
- Half the investment in each bond.
- The entire investment in the nine-year bond.

## Explanation

With nonnegative weights $w_1,w_2$ summing to $1$, portfolio duration is $2w_1+9w_2$, a weighted average between $2$ and $9$ years. Any positive weight in the nine-year bond raises this average above $2$, so duration equals $2$ exactly when $w_2=0$. The formula $w_2=\dfrac{D_L-T_1}{T_2-T_1}=\dfrac{2-2}{9-2}=0$ gives the same result. The entire investment goes into the two-year bond.
