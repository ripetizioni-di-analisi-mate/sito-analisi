The flat force of interest is $\log(1.1)$. The holding in the longer bond has a market value of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. If the bond matures in three years, what will it pay at maturity?

- $751.31\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1331\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The force of interest $\log(1.1)$ corresponds to an effective annual rate of $10\%$ because $e^{\log(1.1)}=1.1$. The redemption payment on a zero-coupon bond is the accumulated value of the investment at maturity, so it equals
$$
1000\cdot1.1^3=1000\cdot1.331=1331\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accumulates the investment for only one year, while $751.31\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts the investment instead of accumulating it.
