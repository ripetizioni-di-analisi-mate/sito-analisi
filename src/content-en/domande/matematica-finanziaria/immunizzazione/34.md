The derivative of an asset's value with respect to the force of interest is $-3200\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$. The asset is worth $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Calculate its duration.

- $0.25\,\mathrm{years}$
- $8\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

A present value calculated using the discount factors $e^{-\delta t}$ satisfies $A'(\delta)=-A(\delta)D_A$. Solving for duration gives
$$
D_A=-\frac{A'}{A}=-\frac{-3200}{800}=4\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The minus sign in the formula cancels the negative sign of the derivative, giving a positive duration. The answer $0.25\,\mathrm{years}$ uses the inverted ratio $\dfrac{800}{3200}$.
