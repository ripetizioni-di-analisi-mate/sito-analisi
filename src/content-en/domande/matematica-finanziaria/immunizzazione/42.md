A stream of liability payments has a total present value of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a duration of $3$ years. An existing holding maturing at time $1$ is worth $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. What maturity must the bond purchased with the remaining capital have?

- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $6\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

The existing holding is worth $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$, leaving $500-250=250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ for the new bond. Both weights equal $\dfrac12$, so portfolio duration is the simple average of the maturities. The condition $\dfrac{1+T}{2}=3$ then gives $T=5$ years.

The answer $3\,\mathrm{years}$ sets the maturity of the new bond equal to liability duration and ignores the existing bond.
