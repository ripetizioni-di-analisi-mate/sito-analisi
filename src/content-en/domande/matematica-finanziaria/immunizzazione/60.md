The exact surplus after a parallel shift $h$ is $100x(x-1)^2(2-x)\,\mathrm{EUR}$, where $x=e^{-h}$. Calculate the surplus for $h=-\log 3$.

- $-1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

For $h=-\log3$ we have $x=e^{-h}=e^{\log3}=3$. Substitution in the expression for the surplus gives
$$
100\cdot3\cdot(3-1)^2\cdot(2-3)=100\cdot3\cdot4\cdot(-1)=-1200\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
Near $h=0$, we have $x\approx1$ and the surplus is approximately $100(x-1)^2$, which is positive. For this large shock, however, the factor $2-x$ is negative. The answer $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the sign of $2-x$, while $-400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the first factor $x$.
