At time $1$ two zero-coupon bonds with original maturity dates $3$ and $9$ are used to cover liabilities worth $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with a remaining duration of $4$ years. Calculate the target value of the holding in the longer bond.

- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At time $1$ the two bonds have $3-1=2$ and $9-1=8$ years left to maturity. The fraction in the longer bond is
$$
w_2=\frac{D_L-T_1}{T_2-T_1}=\frac{4-2}{8-2}=\frac13,
$$
so its target value is $1200\cdot\dfrac13=400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The answer $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the target value of the shorter bond, while $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides the investment equally.
