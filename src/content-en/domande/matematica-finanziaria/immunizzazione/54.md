Two zero-coupon bonds mature at times $3$ and $6$. What is the ratio of the longer bond's convexity to the shorter bond's convexity with respect to the force of interest?

- $0.25$
- $8$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

A zero-coupon bond has a single cash flow, so its convexity with respect to the force of interest equals its squared maturity. The $3$-year bond has convexity $9\,\mathrm{years}^2$, and the $6$-year bond has convexity $36\,\mathrm{years}^2$. The ratio for the longer bond relative to the shorter is therefore $\dfrac{36}{9}=4$.

The answer $2$ is the ratio of the maturities, hence of the durations, while $0.25$ takes the convexity ratio in reverse order.
