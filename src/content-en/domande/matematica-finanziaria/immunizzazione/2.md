The effective annual rate is $20\%$. A receipt of $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ must cover a single payment at time $2$. Find the payment that gives an initial surplus of zero.

- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1008\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $864\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The initial surplus is zero when the receipt and the payment have equal values at a common date. Use time $2$ as the comparison date. The receipt at time $1$ earns interest for one year, so at time $2$ it is worth $720\cdot1.2=864\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The payment that gives zero surplus is therefore $864\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

The answer $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts the receipt instead of accumulating it.
