At the reference force of interest a surplus satisfies $S_0=S'_0=0$ and $S''_0=5000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$. Find the second-order estimate for a shift of $h=-0.02\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$.

- $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Since $S_0=S'_0=0$, only the quadratic term remains in the Taylor expansion of the surplus, so
$$
S(\delta_0+h)\approx\frac12S''_0h^2=\frac12\cdot5000\cdot(-0.02)^2=2500\cdot0.0004=1\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The shift is squared, so the negative sign of $h$ does not affect the result. The answer $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the factor $\dfrac12$.
