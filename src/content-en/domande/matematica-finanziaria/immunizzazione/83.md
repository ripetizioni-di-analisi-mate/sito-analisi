Two liabilities require $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years. Which redemption payments match the cash flows exactly?

- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years.
- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years.
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years.
- $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years.

## Explanation

Cash flow matching requires the receipt on each date to equal the payment due. The liabilities require $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years, so the redemption payments must match those amounts on those dates. The surplus is then zero for any yield curve. A single payment of $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years gives the same nominal total on a different date.
