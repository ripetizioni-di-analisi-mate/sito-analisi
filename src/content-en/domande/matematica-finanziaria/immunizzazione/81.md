A bond has just matured, and its redemption proceeds are still available at rebalancing. How do the proceeds enter the new value and duration equations?

- As a future cash flow with a duration of one year.
- As a liability worth the redemption payment.
- As a bond with its original maturity.
- As cash with zero remaining maturity.

## Explanation

A matured bond has already paid its redemption amount, which is available as cash at rebalancing. Cash has zero remaining maturity, so its full amount enters portfolio value and it contributes zero to the weighted sum of maturities. The proceeds therefore add to portfolio value and lower its duration. Maturities are measured from the new valuation date, so the bond's original maturity no longer enters the calculation.
