Assets and liabilities are each worth $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Their durations are $5$ and $3$ years, respectively. Calculate the derivative of the surplus with respect to the flat force of interest.

- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $-1000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $-4000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the surplus with respect to the force of interest is $S'(\delta_0)=-A_0D_A+L_0D_L$. With equal present values $A_0=L_0=500\,\mathrm{EUR}$, we have $S'(\delta_0)=L_0(D_L-D_A)$, so
$$
S'(\delta_0)=500(3-5)=-1000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}.
$$
The sign is negative because the assets have a longer duration, so their value falls more when the force of interest rises. The answer $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$ interchanges $D_A$ and $D_L$.
