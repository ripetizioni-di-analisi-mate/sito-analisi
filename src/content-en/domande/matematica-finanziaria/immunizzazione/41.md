Matching value and duration requires investing $40\%$ of the portfolio's market value in the longer bond. The two bonds have different prices per unit. How do you find the number of units of the longer bond?

- Take forty percent of the total number of units.
- Multiply the amount invested in the longer bond by its price per unit.
- Divide the amount invested in the longer bond by its price per unit.
- Divide the target duration by the price per unit.

## Explanation

The value and duration equations give the amounts to invest, measured in euros at market value. The investment in the longer bond equals the number of units times the price per unit. Dividing the amount invested by the price per unit therefore gives the number of units. Forty percent of the total number of units gives the right result only if the two unit prices are equal.
