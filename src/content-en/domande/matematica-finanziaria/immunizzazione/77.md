A long position is worth $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, and its target value after rebalancing is $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What proceeds does the required trade generate if there are no transaction costs?

- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-300\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Rebalancing moves the long position from its current value of $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ to its target value of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Sell an amount equal to the difference. Without transaction costs, this gives proceeds of $800-500=300\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The proceeds are a cash inflow and therefore positive. The answer $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the value of the holding left after the sale.
