Assets and liabilities have equal durations and equal present values. Asset maturities have a greater variance. How do their convexities with respect to the force of interest compare?

- The convexities are equal.
- The assets have greater convexity.
- Asset convexity is negative.
- The liabilities have greater convexity.

## Explanation

Convexity with respect to the force of interest equals the variance of the maturities plus the square of duration, $C=\sigma^2+D^2$. The durations are equal, so the term $D^2$ is the same for both. The ordering of convexities therefore follows the ordering of variances, and asset convexity is **greater**. The common present value does not enter this comparison.
