The flat force of interest is zero. The assets pay $320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ and $580\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $4$. The liabilities require $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $2$. What is the initial surplus?

- $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $480\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At a force of interest of $\delta=0$, the discount factor is $e^{-0\cdot t}=1$ for every maturity, so present values equal nominal amounts. The assets are worth $320+580=900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the liability is worth $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The surplus is therefore $900-420=480\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

The answer $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$ gives only the value of the assets, while $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the receipt at time $1$.
