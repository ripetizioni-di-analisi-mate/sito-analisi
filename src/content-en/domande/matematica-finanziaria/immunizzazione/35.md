A liability has a present value of €1000 and a duration of $4$ years. The only instruments are two- and six-year zero-coupon bonds. At most $40\%$ of capital may be invested in the longer bond and short sales are prohibited. What is the greatest duration attainable when all €1000 is invested?

- $4$ years
- $4.4$ years
- $2.4$ years
- $3.6$ years

## Explanation

If $w$ is the long-bond weight, duration is $2(1-w)+6w=2+4w$. It grows with $w$ and reaches $3.6$ at the largest permitted weight, $w=0.4$. Matching the four-year liability would require a $50\%$ long-bond weight.
