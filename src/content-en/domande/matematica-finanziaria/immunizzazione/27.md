At an effective annual rate of $25\%$, an asset has a Macaulay duration of $5$ years. Calculate its modified duration with respect to the annual rate.

- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{years}$
- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $20\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

For the effective annual rate, $A'(i)=-A(i)\dfrac{D_A}{1+i}$ holds, and the ratio $\dfrac{D_A}{1+i}$ is modified duration. With $D_A=5$ years and $i=0.25$, this gives
$$
\frac{D_A}{1+i}=\frac{5}{1.25}=4\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The answer $6.25\,\mathrm{years}$ multiplies by $1+i$ instead of dividing, while $20\,\mathrm{years}$ divides by $i$ instead of $1+i$.
