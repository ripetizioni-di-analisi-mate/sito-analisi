At rebalancing, the tradable assets are worth $740\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the available cash is $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The target portfolio is worth $920\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and must be fully invested. Calculate the external contribution needed.

- $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The internal resources available for rebalancing are the tradable assets and cash, totaling $740+60=800\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The target portfolio is worth $920\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and must be fully invested, so the shortfall of $920-800=120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ must come from outside the portfolio.

The answer $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$ ignores the available cash, while $60\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts it a second time.
