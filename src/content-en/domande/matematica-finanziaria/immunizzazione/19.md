The initial force of interest is $\delta_0=\log(1.2)$. What is the present value of a payment of $1728\,\mathrm{EUR}$ due in three years?

- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1440\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1728\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Since $e^{\delta_0}=1.2$, the force of interest $\delta_0=\log(1.2)$ corresponds to an effective annual rate of $20\%$. The three-year discount factor is therefore $e^{-3\delta_0}=1.2^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{1.728}$, giving a present value of $\dfrac{1728}{1.728}=1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

The answer $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for two years rather than three, while $1440\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts for only one year.
