A liability is worth $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has a duration of $8$ years. The available bonds mature in $2$ and $6$ years. If short selling is allowed, what position in the shorter bond matches value and duration?

- $-300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-600\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Without restrictions on the signs of the holdings, the weight in the shorter bond is
$$
w_1=\frac{T_2-D_L}{T_2-T_1}=\frac{6-8}{6-2}=-\frac12 .
$$
The weight is negative because the target duration of $8$ years exceeds the longer maturity, so the shorter bond must be sold short. The position in the shorter bond is worth $1200\cdot\left(-\dfrac12\right)=-600\,\mathrm{EUR}$, while $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the position in the longer bond.
