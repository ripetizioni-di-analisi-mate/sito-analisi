At the reference force of interest, the liability due at time $0$ has a present value of $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and the liability due at time $4$ has a present value of $900\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is their duration?

- $2\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1\,\mathrm{year}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

Duration is the average of maturities weighted by present values, which total $300+900=1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The immediate obligation has time to maturity $0$, so it enters the denominator but contributes zero to the numerator. Thus
$$
D_L=\frac{300\cdot0+900\cdot4}{1200}=\frac{3600}{1200}=3\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The answer $4\,\mathrm{years}$ excludes the immediate obligation from the calculation.
