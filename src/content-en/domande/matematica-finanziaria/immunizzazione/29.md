Assets and liabilities both have a duration of $3$ years and have respective present values of $900$ and $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Calculate the derivative of the surplus with respect to the force of interest.

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $-300\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the surplus with respect to the force of interest is $S'=-A\,D_A+L\,D_L$. With a common duration of $3$ years, this gives
$$
S'=-900\cdot3+1000\cdot3=-2700+3000=300\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}.
$$
Equal durations and different present values give a nonzero derivative. The answer $-300$ reverses the signs of the two sensitivities, while $100$ is just the difference in present values.
