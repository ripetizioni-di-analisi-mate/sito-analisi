A portfolio with positive cash flows has a duration of $4$ years. The present-value-weighted variance of the maturities is $7\,\mathrm{years}^2$. Calculate convexity with respect to the force of interest.

- $16\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $11\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $7\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $23\,\mathrm{years}^2$

## Explanation

Convexity with respect to the force of interest is the weighted mean of the squared maturities, their second moment. The variance is the second moment minus the square of the mean, so $C=\sigma^2+D^2$. Therefore
$$
C=7+4^2=23\,\mathrm{years}^2 .
$$
The answer $16\,\mathrm{years}^2$ gives only the square of duration, while $11\,\mathrm{years}^2$ adds $7$ and $4$ without squaring duration.
