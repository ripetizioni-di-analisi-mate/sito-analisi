Two portfolios cover the same liability. Both have zero initial surplus and zero first derivative with respect to the force of interest. Their quadratic surplus terms for small shocks $h$ are €$300h^2$ and €$75h^2$, respectively. What shock magnitude on the second portfolio matches the estimated surplus produced by $h$ on the first?

- $4|h|$
- $2|h|$
- $\dfrac{|h|}{2}$
- $|h|$

## Explanation

Equal predicted surpluses require $75k^2=300h^2$. Thus $k^2=4h^2$ and $|k|=2|h|$. The sign of the shock is immaterial in this quadratic approximation.
