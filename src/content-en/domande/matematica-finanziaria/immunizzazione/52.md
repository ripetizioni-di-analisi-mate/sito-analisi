The assets are worth $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and have a duration of $4$ years and convexity $20\,\mathrm{years}^2$. A liability of equal value is due entirely at time $4$. Calculate the second derivative of the surplus with respect to the force of interest.

- $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$
- $8000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$
- $40000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$
- $32000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$

## Explanation

The liability has a single maturity at time $4$, so its convexity is $C_L=4^2=16\,\mathrm{years}^2$. Both sides are worth $2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
S''(\delta_0)=L_0(C_A-C_L)=2000(20-16)=8000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2 .
$$
The answer $40000$ gives only the second derivative for the assets, while $32000$ takes the liability's duration $4$ as its convexity instead of squaring it.
