After a shift in the force of interest, the surplus in euros has the expansion $S(\delta_0+h)=20-300h+1000h^2+o(h^2)$. Use the terms through second order to find its value for $h=0.05$.

- $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Substitute $h=0.05$ into the terms through second order. The linear term is $-300\cdot0.05=-15\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the quadratic term is $1000\cdot0.05^2=1000\cdot0.0025=2.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The estimate is therefore
$$
20-15+2.5=7.5\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the first-order estimate, $37.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reverses the sign of the linear term, and $2.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reverses the sign of the quadratic term.
