A liability is due on the valuation date. How does its present value respond when the flat force of interest applied to the other cash flows changes?

- It depends on asset duration.
- It remains equal to the amount due.
- It falls as the force of interest rises.
- It rises as the force of interest rises.

## Explanation

A payment due on the valuation date has time to maturity $t=0$. Its discount factor is $e^{-\delta\cdot0}=1$ at any force of interest $\delta$, so its present value equals the amount due. A change in the flat force of interest therefore affects the other cash flows but leaves the immediate liability unchanged. For the same reason its contribution to the weighted sum of maturities in the numerator of duration is zero.
