To match a liability duration of $5$ years, a portfolio invests in two zero-coupon bonds maturing in $2$ and $6$ years. Find the fraction of the portfolio value invested in the $6$-year bond.

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $0.25$
- $0.75$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

Matching value and duration gives the following fraction in the longer bond.
$$
w_2=\frac{D_L-T_1}{T_2-T_1}=\frac{5-2}{6-2}=\frac34=0.75 .
$$
With fractions $0.25$ and $0.75$, the portfolio duration is indeed $0.25\cdot2+0.75\cdot6=5$ years. The answer $0.25$ is the fraction in the shorter bond, while $\dfrac56$ is the ratio of the target duration to the longer maturity.
