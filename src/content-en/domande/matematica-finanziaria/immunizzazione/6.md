The effective annual rate is $20\%$. Two zero-coupon bonds maturing in $1$ and $5$ years are used to match a liability with a present value of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a duration of $3$ years. Calculate the face value of the holding in the shorter bond.

- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The target duration of $3$ years is midway between maturities $1$ and $5$, so the fraction invested in the shorter bond is $w_1=\dfrac{5-3}{5-1}=\dfrac12$. This puts $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in the shorter bond. Its face value is the accumulated value of this investment after one year at $20\%$, which is $500\cdot1.2=600\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

The amount $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is the investment today, a present value, while $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$ accumulates it for two years.
