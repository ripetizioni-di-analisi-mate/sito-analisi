With a constant force of interest $\delta=\log(1.1)$, no payments falling due and no trades, a portfolio has a duration of $5$ years at time $0$. What is its duration at time $2$?

- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $7\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4.13\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

From time $0$ to time $2$ every remaining present value is multiplied by the same factor $e^{2\delta}=1.1^2=1.21$, so the weights in the average stay the same. Each remaining maturity decreases by $2$ years. Duration, the weighted average of the remaining maturities, therefore falls by $2$ years to $5-2=3$ years.

The answer $4.13\,\mathrm{years}$ divides duration by the factor $1.21$, which applies to values rather than maturities.
