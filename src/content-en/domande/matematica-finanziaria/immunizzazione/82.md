At time $1$ a portfolio has $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in cash and a bond worth $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at market value that matures at the original time $7$. Calculate portfolio duration on the revaluation date.

- $\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{years}$
- $6\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

At time $1$ the cash has zero remaining maturity and the bond has $7-1=6$ years left to maturity. The portfolio is worth $300+600=900\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
D=\frac{300\cdot0+600\cdot6}{900}=\frac{3600}{900}=4\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The answer $\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{years}$ uses the original maturity $7$, while $6\,\mathrm{years}$ excludes cash from the average.
