Two asset payments due at times $1$ and $5$ have equal present values. The entire liability falls due at time $3$. Calculate asset convexity minus liability convexity with respect to the force of interest.

- $0\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $13\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $16\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}^2$

## Explanation

Convexity with respect to the force of interest is the present-value-weighted average of the squared maturities. The asset weights are each $\dfrac12$ at times $1$ and $5$, so $C_A=\dfrac{1^2+5^2}{2}=13\,\mathrm{years}^2$. The liability has a single maturity, so $C_L=3^2=9\,\mathrm{years}^2$. The difference is $C_A-C_L=13-9=4\,\mathrm{years}^2$.

The answer $13\,\mathrm{years}^2$ gives only the convexity of the assets.
