A portfolio satisfies Redington's conditions, and the quadratic term in the surplus is $900h^2$. Find the ratio of the quadratic estimates for shocks $-0.02$ and $0.01$, in that order.

- $4$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

Under Redington's conditions the quadratic estimate of the surplus is $900h^2$. For $h=-0.02$ it equals $900\cdot0.0004=0.36\,\mathrm{EUR}$, and for $h=0.01$ it equals $900\cdot0.0001=0.09\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The ratio is therefore
$$
\frac{0.36}{0.09}=\left(\frac{-0.02}{0.01}\right)^2=(-2)^2=4 .
$$
The answer $-2$ takes the ratio of the shocks without squaring, while $0.25$ takes the requested ratio in reverse order.
