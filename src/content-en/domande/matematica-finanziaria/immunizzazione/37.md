A strategy invests $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in a $2$-year zero-coupon bond and $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in a $6$-year zero-coupon bond. What liability duration would match the portfolio duration?

- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $6\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

The duration of a zero-coupon bond equals its maturity. Portfolio duration is therefore the average of the maturities weighted by the investments. The total value is $200+600=800\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
D=\frac{200\cdot2+600\cdot6}{800}=\frac{4000}{800}=5\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The debt must have the same duration. The answer $4\,\mathrm{years}$ is the simple average of $2$ and $6$ without their weights.
