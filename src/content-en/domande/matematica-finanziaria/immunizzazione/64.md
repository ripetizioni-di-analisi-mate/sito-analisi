A portfolio has zero initial surplus and first derivative. Around the reference force, $S(h)=200h^2+R(h)$ euros, with $|R(h)|\le10000|h|^3$. For which of the following nonzero shocks does the error bound alone guarantee a strictly positive surplus?

- $h=0.02$
- $h=-0.03$
- $h=0.04$
- $h=0.01$

## Explanation

Even in the worst case, $S(h)\ge h^2(200-10000|h|)$. For $0<|h|<0.02$ the lower bound is strictly positive. At $|h|=0.02$ the estimate still allows zero.
