Assets and liabilities are both worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Both have a duration of $3$ years. Their convexities with respect to the force of interest are $15$ and $11\,\mathrm{years}^2$. What is the second-order estimate of the surplus for $h=0.05\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$?

- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The values and durations match, so $S_0=S'_0=0$ and only the quadratic term remains in the Taylor expansion of the surplus through second order. The second derivative is $S''(\delta_0)=L_0(C_A-C_L)=1000(15-11)=4000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$. Therefore
$$
S(\delta_0+h)\approx\frac12\cdot4000\cdot0.05^2=2000\cdot0.0025=5\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ omits the factor $\dfrac12$, while $-5\,\mathrm{EUR}$ reverses the sign of the difference in convexities.
