At a given force of interest one portfolio is worth $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has a duration of $2$ years. A second portfolio is worth $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has a duration of $7$ years. What is the duration of the combined portfolio?

- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

The duration of the combined portfolio is the average of the component durations weighted by their market values at the same force of interest. Their combined value is $1200+800=2000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so
$$
D=\frac{1200\cdot2+800\cdot7}{2000}=\frac{2400+5600}{2000}=4\,\mathrm{years}.
$$
The answer $4.5\,\mathrm{years}$ is the unweighted average of $2$ and $7$.
