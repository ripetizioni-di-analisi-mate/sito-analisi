At an effective annual rate of $25\%$, an asset due in one year is worth $480\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. The rate rises to $50\%$. Calculate its new present value.

- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $576\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $320\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A present value of $480\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at $25\%$ corresponds to a payment of $480\cdot1.25=600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Discounting that payment for one year at the new rate of $50\%$ gives a present value of $\dfrac{600}{1.5}=400\,\mathrm{EUR}$.

The answer $320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ discounts the old present value at $50\%$ instead of discounting the payment due at maturity, while $576\,\mathrm{EUR}$ multiplies the present value by $1.2$.
