The initial surplus is zero. The future asset and liability cash flows are certain and have different maturities. Which equality follows?

- Assets and liabilities have equal present values on the reference curve.
- The two cash flow streams match at every date.
- The total nominal receipts and payments are equal.
- Asset and liability durations are equal.

## Explanation

The surplus is the difference between the present values, $S(\delta_0)=A(\delta_0)-L(\delta_0)$. Thus $S(\delta_0)=0$ is equivalent to $A(\delta_0)=L(\delta_0)$ and refers only to the reference force of interest. Individual dates can have nonzero net cash flows, and the total nominal amounts can differ because they are discounted over different terms. Equal duration is an additional condition on the first derivative of the surplus.
