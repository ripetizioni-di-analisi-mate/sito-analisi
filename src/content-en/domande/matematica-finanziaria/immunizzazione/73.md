At an effective annual rate of $10\%$ a portfolio has a present value of $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. If the rate stays flat and constant, what is its equivalent value at a two-year horizon?

- $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1815\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1239.67\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1350\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With a flat, constant force of interest the horizon value is $W_H=e^{\delta H}V$, where $V$ is the present value, since a common factor carries every present value to the target date. At $10\%$ the two-year factor is $1.1^2=1.21$, so
$$
W_2=1500\cdot1.21=1815\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $1239.67\,\mathrm{EUR}$ divides by $1.21$ instead of multiplying, while $1350\,\mathrm{EUR}$ subtracts $10\%$.
