The payments from a position at times $2$ and $8$ have equal present values. Calculate the present-value-weighted variance of their maturities.

- $9\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $36\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $34\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $25\,\mathrm{years}^2$

## Explanation

With equal present values, the weights of maturities $2$ and $8$ are both $\dfrac12$, so duration is $D=\dfrac{2+8}{2}=5$ years. Variance is the weighted average of the squared deviations from duration. Both dates lie $3$ years from $5$, giving a variance of $3^2=9\,\mathrm{years}^2$.

The answer $34\,\mathrm{years}^2$ is the convexity $\dfrac{2^2+8^2}{2}$, the second moment before subtracting $D^2$.
