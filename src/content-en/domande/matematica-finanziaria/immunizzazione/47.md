At an effective annual rate of $25\%$, a zero-coupon bond matures in $3$ years. Calculate its convexity with respect to the effective annual rate.

- $9$
- $5.76$
- $7.68$
- $12$

## Explanation

At the effective rate $i$, the discount factor is $(1+i)^{-t}$ and its second derivative is $t(t+1)(1+i)^{-t-2}$. Dividing by $(1+i)^{-t}$ gives the convexity $\dfrac{t(t+1)}{(1+i)^2}$. With $t=3$ and $i=0.25$, this gives
$$
\frac{3\cdot4}{1.25^2}=\frac{12}{1.5625}=7.68 .
$$
The answer $9$ is convexity with respect to the force of interest, $t^2$, while $5.76$ uses $t^2$ as the numerator instead of $t(t+1)$.
