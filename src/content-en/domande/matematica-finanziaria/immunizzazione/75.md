A liability originally due at time $8$ is revalued at time $3$. Calculate its remaining duration.

- $8\,\mathrm{years}$
- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $11\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

A liability with a single payment has duration equal to its time to maturity. At the revaluation date $s=3$ its remaining maturity is $8-3=5$ years, so its remaining duration is $5$ years.

The answer $8\,\mathrm{years}$ measures maturity from the old valuation date, while $11\,\mathrm{years}$ adds the two dates instead of subtracting them.
