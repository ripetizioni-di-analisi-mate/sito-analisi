Assets and liabilities have equal present values. Asset duration is shorter. What is the sign of the derivative of the surplus with respect to the flat force of interest?

- Zero.
- Negative.
- Positive.
- It depends on the difference in convexity.

## Explanation

The derivative of the surplus with respect to the force of interest is $S'=-A\,D_A+L\,D_L$. With equal values $A=L=L_0>0$, this becomes $S'=L_0(D_L-D_A)$. Since asset duration is shorter, the difference $D_L-D_A$ is positive and the derivative is **positive**. Convexity enters only the second derivative, so the sign of $S'$ depends solely on the durations.
