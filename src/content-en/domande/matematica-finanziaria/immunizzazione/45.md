At the reference force of interest, receipts at times $2$ and $6$ are worth $750\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, respectively. Calculate convexity with respect to the force of interest.

- $9\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $12\,\mathrm{years}^2$
- $20\,\mathrm{years}^2$

## Explanation

Convexity with respect to the force of interest is the present-value-weighted average of the squared maturities. The total value is $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$, so the weights are $\dfrac34$ and $\dfrac14$. Therefore
$$
C=\frac34\cdot2^2+\frac14\cdot6^2=3+9=12\,\mathrm{years}^2.
$$
The answer $9\,\mathrm{years}^2$ is the square of duration, which is $3$ years, while $20\,\mathrm{years}^2$ is the simple average of the squared maturities.
