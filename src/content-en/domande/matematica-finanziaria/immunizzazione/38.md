At an effective annual rate of $20\%$, an investor buys a zero-coupon bond that pays $864\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. What fraction of a portfolio worth $1500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ does its market value represent?

- $0.4$
- $0.6$
- $0.576$
- $0.48$

## Explanation

At $20\%$, discount the redemption payment for two years to obtain the zero-coupon bond's market value, $\dfrac{864}{1.2^2}=\dfrac{864}{1.44}=600\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Dividing this by the total portfolio value gives a portfolio weight of $\dfrac{600}{1500}=0.4$.

The answer $0.576$ uses the nominal redemption payment of $864\,\mathrm{EUR}$, while $0.48$ discounts the payment for only one year.
