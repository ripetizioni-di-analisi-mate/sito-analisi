At the maturity node $3$ the net cash flow has a present value of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The discount factor is $e^{-3\delta_3}$. Calculate the derivative of the surplus with respect to the force of interest $\delta_3$ at this node alone.

- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $-600\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $-1800\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$

## Explanation

The net cash flow at node $3$ has present value $N_3e^{-3\delta_3}=200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Differentiating with respect to the force of interest $\delta_3$ at this node introduces the factor $-3$. The other terms in the surplus are independent of $\delta_3$. Thus
$$
\frac{\partial S}{\partial\delta_3}=-3N_3e^{-3\delta_3}=-3\cdot200=-600\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}.
$$
The answer $-1800$ multiplies by $3^2$, as for a second derivative, while $600$ has the opposite sign.
