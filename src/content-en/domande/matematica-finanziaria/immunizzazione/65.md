A fund receives $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$. At time $3$ it receives another $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and must pay a liability of $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What total accumulation factor from time $1$ to time $3$, applied to the reinvestment of the $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$, makes the coverage exact?

- $1.20$
- $2.40$
- $0.83$
- $1.44$

## Explanation

The receipt at time $3$ is available on the liability date, so it directly covers $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ of it. The remaining $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ must come from reinvesting the $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ received at time $1$. Writing $F$ for the accumulation factor from time $1$ to time $3$, we require $500F=600$, therefore $F=\dfrac{600}{500}=1.20$. The value $2.40$ ignores the receipt at time $3$, while $0.83$ is the inverted ratio. The value $1.44$ treats $1.20$ as an annual factor and squares it.
