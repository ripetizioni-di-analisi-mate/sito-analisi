Two bonds pay $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ and $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $2$. If the effective annual rate is constant at $10\%$ after the shock, what is the portfolio worth at horizon $H=3$?

- $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $909.09\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1331\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With a flat rate of $10\%$, value each cash flow at time $H=3$. Both cash flows precede the horizon, so accumulate the receipt at time $1$ for two years and the receipt at time $2$ for one year. Thus
$$
W_3=500\cdot1.1^2+550\cdot1.1=605+605=1210\,\mathrm{EUR}.
$$
The answer $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$ gives the nominal sum, while $909.09\,\mathrm{EUR}$ gives the present value at time $0$ instead of the horizon value.
