A fund receives €500 at time $1$ and €600 at time $3$, when it also owes €1200. What total reinvestment factor from time $1$ to time $3$ exactly covers the liability without additional deposits?

- $1.20$
- $1.10$
- $2.40$
- $0.80$

## Explanation

The receipt on the liability date already covers €600. The earlier receipt has to supply the remaining €600, so $500F=600$ and $F=1.20$. This is the factor for the whole two-year interval.
