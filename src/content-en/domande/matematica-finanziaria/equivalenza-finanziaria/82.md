Under a positive multiplicative accumulation law, which expression discounts an amount from time $t$ to the earlier time $s$ if the unit account is $a$?

- $a(s)a(t)$
- $\dfrac{a(t)}{a(s)}$
- $a(t)-a(s)$
- $\dfrac{a(s)}{a(t)}$

## Explanation

With unit account $a$ the accumulation factor from $s$ to $t$ is $r(s,t)=\dfrac{a(t)}{a(s)}$. To bring an amount from $t$ back to the earlier date $s$, divide by this factor, or multiply by
$$
\frac{a(s)}{a(t)}.
$$
The expression $\dfrac{a(t)}{a(s)}$ carries the amount forward from $s$ to $t$, while $a(t)-a(s)$ is the interest earned by the unit account between the dates.
