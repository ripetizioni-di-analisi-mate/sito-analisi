Under which convention does the equivalent average due date equal the amount-weighted arithmetic mean of the dates for every admissible nonzero rate if discount factors from time zero are positive?

- Under commercial discount.
- Under continuous compounding at a constant force of interest.
- Under compound interest at a constant rate.
- Under rational discount at simple interest.

## Explanation

Under commercial discount the time-zero value of $C$ due at $t$ is $C(1-dt)$, an affine function of $t$. The equation of value between $S(1-dT)$ and $\sum_kC_k(1-dt_k)$ simplifies because $S=\sum_kC_k$. Dividing by $d\ne0$ gives
$$
T=\sum_kw_kt_k.
$$
Under compound interest, continuous compounding, and rational discount the factor is strictly convex, so the equivalent average due date differs from the arithmetic mean.
