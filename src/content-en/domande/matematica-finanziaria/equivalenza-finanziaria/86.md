Two schedules have positive present costs of $400$ and $420$ euros. How do they compare when the cash flows carry their signs from the borrower's perspective?

- Both become positive.
- The first has the greater signed value because it equals $-400$ euros.
- The second has the greater signed value because it costs more.
- The comparison cannot be made without the individual due dates.

## Explanation

With outflows negative for the borrower, the two costs become $-400$ euros and $-420$ euros. Since $-400>-420$, the first schedule has the greater signed value. It also has the lower present cost, $400$ euros versus $420$ euros, making it the cheaper schedule. Both present costs already refer to the same date, so the individual due dates are unnecessary for the comparison.
