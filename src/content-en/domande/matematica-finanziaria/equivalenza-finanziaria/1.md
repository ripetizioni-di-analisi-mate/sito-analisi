Find the value at time $1$ of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $0$ and $330\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $2$ using an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$. Time is measured in years.

- $583\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $520\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $530\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

To value both amounts at time $1$, accumulate the payment from time $0$ for one year and discount the payment from time $2$ for one year, using $q=1.1$. This gives
$$
V(1)=200\cdot1.1+\frac{330}{1.1}=220+300=520.
$$
The required value is $520$ euros. The value of $583$ euros also accumulates the payment at time $2$, while $530$ euros simply adds the nominal amounts and $500$ euros leaves the payment at time $0$ unchanged.
