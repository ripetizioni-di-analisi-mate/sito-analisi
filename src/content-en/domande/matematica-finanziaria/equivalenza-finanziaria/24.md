Two debts of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $0$ and $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ are replaced by one payment at time $2$. Find it at an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$.

- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $672\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $648\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The single payment at time $2$ falls after both debts are due. Accumulate both to that date. With $q=1.2$ this gives
$$
X=200\cdot1.2^2+300\cdot1.2=288+360=648.
$$
The required amount is $648$ euros. The nominal sum is $500$ euros, while $600$ euros accumulates both debts for only one year.
