At time $1$ a ledger lists the same €110 repayment as both a receivable and cash just collected. When valuing only receivables still outstanding, how much is this repayment worth immediately after collection?

- €110
- €220
- €100
- €0

## Explanation

Collection extinguishes the receivable. The money received is cash and must not be counted again among future receivables. Its contribution to outstanding receivables is zero.
