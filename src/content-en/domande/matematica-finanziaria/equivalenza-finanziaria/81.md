Which relation defines the constant effective rate $i$ equivalent to two years with arbitrary positive factors $q_1$ and $q_2$ and no intervening transactions?

- $1+i=\dfrac{q_1+q_2}{2}$
- $1+i=q_1q_2$
- $(1+i)^2=q_1q_2$
- $(1+i)^2=q_1+q_2$

## Explanation

Without intervening transactions, principal $C$ becomes $C\,q_1q_2$ after two years. At constant rate $i$ the same principal becomes $C(1+i)^2$. Equating the accumulated values gives
$$
(1+i)^2=q_1q_2.
$$
The relation $1+i=q_1q_2$ confuses the annual factor with the two-year factor, while $1+i=\dfrac{q_1+q_2}{2}$ uses the arithmetic mean of the factors. That exceeds their geometric mean when $q_1\ne q_2$.
