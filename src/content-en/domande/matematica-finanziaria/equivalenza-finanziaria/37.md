An account earns $20\%$ in year one and loses $10\%$ in year two. What does $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ grow to after two years with no intervening transactions?

- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $108\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1080\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With no intervening transactions, multiply the annual factors to get the accumulated value. The factors are $1.2$ in the first year and $1-0.1=0.9$ in the second, giving
$$
1000\cdot1.2\cdot0.9=1080.
$$
The final value is $1080$ euros. The value of $1100$ euros adds the rates, $20\%-10\%=10\%$, overlooking that the loss applies to $1200$ euros. The value of $1200$ euros omits the second year.
