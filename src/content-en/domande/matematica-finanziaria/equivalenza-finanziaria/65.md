One debt is replaced by two installments due on different dates. The original debt, rate, and all three dates are known. What further information determines both installments?

- Both amounts must be positive.
- The equation of value must be written both today and at maturity.
- The second installment must be twice the first.
- The first installment must precede the second, as the dates already specify.

## Explanation

The given dates and rate yield one linear equation for the two installments $R_1$ and $R_2$. Writing it at another date multiplies both sides by the same factor $q^{\sigma-\tau}$ and supplies no new information. Both amounts require a second relation such as $R_2=2R_1$. Then $R_1$ can be factored out and found, giving $R_2$ as well.
