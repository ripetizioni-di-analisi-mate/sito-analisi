An account earns $i_1$ in year one and $i_2$ in year two. Which rate affects a deposit made immediately after first-year interest is credited?

- Only $i_2$.
- The mean of the two rates.
- Only $i_1$.
- The product of the two rates.

## Explanation

The rate $i_1$ applies from $0$ to $1$ and the rate $i_2$ from $1$ to $2$. The deposit is made at time $1$, after the first year's interest has accrued, so its factor to time $2$ is $r(1,2)=1+i_2$. Only $i_2$ affects the deposit. The initial principal receives both factors, $(1+i_1)(1+i_2)$.
