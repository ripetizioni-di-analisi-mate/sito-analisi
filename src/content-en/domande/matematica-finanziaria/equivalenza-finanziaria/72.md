The compound interest rate is negative and the schedule's present value is positive. What relationship between the replacement payment $X$ and the present value $V$ permits a strictly future due date?

- $X=V$
- $X>V$
- Any relationship between $X$ and $V$.
- $X<V$

## Explanation

At a negative interest rate the annual factor satisfies $0<q<1$. The due date $T$ satisfies $Xq^{-T}=V$, or $X=Vq^T$. For $T>0$ the power $q^T$ is less than one, so $X<V$. Conversely, if $X<V$ then $\log\dfrac{X}{V}<0$ and hence $\log q<0$, giving a positive due date $T=\dfrac{\log\frac{X}{V}}{\log q}$. With $X=V$ we would have $T=0$.
