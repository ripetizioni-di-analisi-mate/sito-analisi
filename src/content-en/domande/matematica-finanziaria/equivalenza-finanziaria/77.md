A schedule includes both a receipt and an outflow. Why does the proof locating the equivalent average due date for positive receivables fail here?

- The algebraic sum of all dates is always zero.
- At least one weight may be negative.
- The equivalent average due date is defined only for integer dates.
- All discount factors become negative when a schedule includes outflows.

## Explanation

The proof expresses $q^{-T}$ as the mean of the factors $q^{-t_k}$ with weights $w_k=\dfrac{C_k}{S}$. Positive weights put the result between the original factors. An outflow makes its $C_k$ negative, so at least one weight is negative. Such a combination can lie outside the original range, allowing the equivalent date to fall outside the date interval. The factors $q^{-t_k}$ themselves remain positive.
