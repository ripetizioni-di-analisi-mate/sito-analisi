Two interest-rate paths have the same two-year accumulation factor but different second-year factors. For which cash flow do their final accumulated values coincide?

- For an initial deposit and a positive withdrawal at the end of year one.
- For one initial deposit and no subsequent transactions.
- For an initial deposit and a positive deposit at the end of year one.
- For a single positive deposit at the end of year one.

## Explanation

Let $F$ be the common two-year factor. The initial deposit $C$ accumulates to $CF$ along either path. A cash flow $M$ at the end of year one receives only the second-year factor, which differs between paths, so its final value changes. The final balances therefore agree for a single initial deposit with no intervening transactions.
