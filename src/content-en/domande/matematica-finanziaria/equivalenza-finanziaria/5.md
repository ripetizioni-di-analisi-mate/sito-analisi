A debt of $221\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ is replaced by two equal installments at times $0$ and $2$. Find each installment at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

- $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $105.24\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $110.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100.45\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Write the equation of value at time $1$, the debt's due date. Accumulate the installment at time $0$ for one year and discount the installment at time $2$ for one year, giving
$$
221=Rq+Rq^{-1}=R\left(1.1+\frac{1}{1.1}\right)=R\cdot\frac{2.21}{1.1}.
$$
This gives $R=\dfrac{221\cdot1.1}{2.21}=110$ euros. The value of $110.5$ euros halves the debt without accounting for the dates.
