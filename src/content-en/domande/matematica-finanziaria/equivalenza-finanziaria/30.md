At an annual compound interest rate of $25\%$, how far in the future must a payment of $625\,\mathrm{EUR}$ fall to be equivalent to $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today?

- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1\,\mathrm{year}$
- $2\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

A payment of $625$ euros at time $T$ is equivalent to $400$ euros today if $625\,q^{-T}=400$, or
$$
1.25^T=\frac{625}{400}=1.5625.
$$
Since $1.25^2=1.5625$, the payment is due in $T=2$ years. After $1$ year the $400$ euros would have grown to only $400\cdot1.25=500$ euros, while after $3$ years they would amount to $781.25$ euros.
