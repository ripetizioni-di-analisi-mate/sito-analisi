A positive replacement payment equals the present value of a schedule. At an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$, what is its equivalent due date?

- At time $-1$.
- At time zero.
- At time $1$.
- At time $2$.

## Explanation

The common due date $T$ satisfies $Xq^{-T}=V$. Since $X=V>0$, this gives $q^{-T}=1$. With $q=1.1$ the function $T\mapsto q^{-T}$ is strictly decreasing and takes the value $1$ only for $T=0$. The common due date is therefore time zero. At any other date the discount factor differs from one and the present value differs from $V$.
