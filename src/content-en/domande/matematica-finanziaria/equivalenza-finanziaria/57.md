A receipt is due six months after the focal date. The effective annual compound rate is positive and its accumulation factor is $q$. By which factor should the receipt be multiplied?

- $q^{\frac12}$
- $q^{-\frac12}$
- $q^{-\frac1{12}}$
- $q^{-6}$

## Explanation

In $V(\tau)=\sum_k C_kq^{\tau-t_k}$ the exponent is the signed time difference $\tau-t_k$. Six months is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of a year and the receipt falls after the focal date, so $\tau-t_k=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The factor is therefore $q^{-\frac{1}{2}}$. The factor $q^{\frac{1}{2}}$ reverses the exponent's sign and accumulates. The factor $q^{-6}$ measures time in months but uses an annual rate.
