A debt of $350\,\mathrm{EUR}$ due in one year is replaced by $X$ today and $3X$ in two years. Find $X$ to the nearest cent at an annual compound interest rate of $25\%$.

- $95.89\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $102.94\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $87.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $70\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Write the equation of value at time $1$ with $q=1.25$ and $q^{-1}=0.8$. Accumulate the payment $X$ from time $0$ and discount the payment $3X$ from time $2$, giving
$$
350=1.25\,X+3\cdot0.8\,X=3.65\,X.
$$
The result is $X=\dfrac{350}{3.65}\approx95.89$ euros. The value of $102.94$ euros leaves the first payment unaccumulated, while $87.5$ euros divides the debt by $4$ without taking the dates into account.
