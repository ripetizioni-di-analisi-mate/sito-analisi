Three annual interest rates are reordered in an account with no intervening transactions. What is guaranteed to stay the same?

- Each individual year's effective rate.
- The balance after year one.
- The final accumulated value.
- The balance after year two.

## Explanation

With no intervening cash flows an initial principal $C$ becomes $C\,q_1q_2q_3$ after three years. The product is independent of the order of its factors, so the final balance is unchanged. The balance after one year is $C$ times whichever factor comes first and changes when the rates are exchanged. The second-year balance also changes, as do the rates assigned to the individual years.
