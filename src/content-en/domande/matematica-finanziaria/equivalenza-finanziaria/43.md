If $r(2,5)=1.25$, what is the value at time $2$ of $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ due at time $5$?

- $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $750\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $480\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The factor $r(2,5)=1.25$ carries one euro from time $2$ to time $5$. To bring an amount back from time $5$ to time $2$, divide by the same factor, giving
$$
\frac{600}{1.25}=480.
$$
The value is $480$ euros. The value of $750$ euros multiplies by the factor and moves in the opposite direction, while $450$ euros uses the discount $1-0.25=0.75$.
