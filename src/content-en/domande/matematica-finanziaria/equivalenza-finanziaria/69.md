A replacement payment is less than the original schedule's present value. At a positive compound interest rate, where would the equivalent due date fall relative to today?

- It is today.
- It falls after today.
- It could fall either before or after today.
- It falls before today.

## Explanation

The common due date $T$ satisfies $Xq^{-T}=V$, or $q^T=\dfrac{X}{V}$. By assumption $X<V$, so $q^T<1$. At a positive rate $q>1$, and the power $q^T$ is less than one only for $T<0$. The equivalent due date therefore lies in the past. A payment below today's value would have to be made before today to earn interest up to the valuation date.
