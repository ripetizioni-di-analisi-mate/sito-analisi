From the borrower's perspective, find the signed present value of outflows of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

- $-221\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-210\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Outflows are negative for the borrower. The first is worth $-100$ euros today and the second $-\dfrac{110}{1.1}=-100$ euros today, giving the schedule a value of
$$
-100-100=-200.
$$
The value of $-210$ euros adds the nominal amounts without discounting, while $200$ euros omits the negative signs on the outflows.
