Debts of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years are replaced by a single payment of $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the equivalent average due date at an annual commercial discount rate of $10\%$, using today as the valuation date.

- $2\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

Under commercial discount, $C$ due at time $t$ is worth $C(1-dt)$ today, an affine function of $t$. The debts have present value $100\cdot0.9+300\cdot0.7=300$ euros and $400(1-0.1\,T)=300$ gives $T=2.5$ years. The result is the amount-weighted mean of the dates, given by
$$
T=\frac{100\cdot1+300\cdot3}{400}=2.5.
$$
The value of $2$ years is the unweighted arithmetic mean of the dates.
