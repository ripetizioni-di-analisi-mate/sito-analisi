Schedule A costs $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Schedule B costs $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $312.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. At an annual compound interest rate of $25\%$, what additional immediate fee would make B equivalent to A?

- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $25\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $62.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With $q=1.25$, the installments in schedule B have present value
$$
\frac{250}{1.25}+\frac{312.5}{1.25^2}=200+200=400.
$$
The present costs of A and B are therefore equal, so the additional fee required for equivalence is $0$ euros. The value of $50$ euros discounts the second installment for only one year.
