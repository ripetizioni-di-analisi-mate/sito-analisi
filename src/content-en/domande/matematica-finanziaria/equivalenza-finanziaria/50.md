Two plans are equivalent at an annual compound rate of $10\%$. Plan A gains a €55 receipt in one year and a €121 payment in two years. Which single change to B today preserves equivalence from the recipient’s perspective?

- A payment of €50.
- A receipt of €50.
- A payment of €66.
- A receipt of €150.

## Explanation

The net present value added to A is $\dfrac{55}{1.1}-\dfrac{121}{1.1^2}=50-100=-50$ euros. Since the plans started out equivalent, B must lose the same value today.
