A replacement payment of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ has a present value of $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What discount factor corresponds to its equivalent due date?

- $0.25$
- $0.8$
- $1.25$
- $0.2$

## Explanation

At the common due date $Xq^{-T}=V$ holds, where $q^{-T}$ is the discount factor. With $X=500$ euros and $V=400$ euros we obtain
$$
q^{-T}=\frac{V}{X}=\frac{400}{500}=0.8.
$$
The value $1.25$ is the reciprocal ratio $\dfrac{X}{V}$, the accumulation factor for that same date. The value $0.2$ is the relative discount $1-0.8$.
