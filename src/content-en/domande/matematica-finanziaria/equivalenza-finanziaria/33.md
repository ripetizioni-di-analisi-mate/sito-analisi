Using an annual commercial discount rate of $5\%$, find the equivalent average due date of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$, and $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $3$.

- $2\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1.33\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

Under commercial discount the equivalent average due date is the amount-weighted mean of the dates. The payments sum to $800$ euros, so their weights are $\dfrac{200}{800}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and $\dfrac{400}{800}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. This gives
$$
T=\frac{1}{4}\cdot0+\frac{1}{4}\cdot1+\frac{1}{2}\cdot3=1.75.
$$
The answer of $1.33$ years is the unweighted mean of dates $0$, $1$, and $3$.
