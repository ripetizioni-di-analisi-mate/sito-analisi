A multiplicative accumulation law has factors $r(0,2)=1.08$ and $r(2,5)=1.25$. Find the accumulation factor from today to time $5$.

- $2.33$
- $1.35$
- $1.33$
- $0.864$

## Explanation

Under a multiplicative law the factor over an interval is the product of the factors over its consecutive subintervals. The intervals from $0$ to $2$ and from $2$ to $5$ are consecutive, giving
$$
r(0,5)=r(0,2)\,r(2,5)=1.08\cdot1.25=1.35.
$$
The value $2.33$ adds the two factors, while $1.33$ adds the rates $0.08$ and $0.25$, omitting interest on interest.
