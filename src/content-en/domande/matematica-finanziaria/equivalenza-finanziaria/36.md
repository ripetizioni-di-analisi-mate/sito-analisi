With annual commercial discount of $5\%$, payments of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ and $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $t$ have an equivalent average due date of four years. What is $t$?

- $5\,\mathrm{years}$
- $6\,\mathrm{years}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

Under commercial discount the equivalent average due date is the amount-weighted mean of the original dates. The amounts sum to $800$ euros, giving
$$
4=\frac{200\cdot1+600\,t}{800}.
$$
Thus $600\,t=3200-200=3000$ and the answer is $t=5$ years. The value of $4$ years is the equivalent average due date. With positive weights that date lies strictly between $1$ and $t$, so $t$ must exceed $4$.
