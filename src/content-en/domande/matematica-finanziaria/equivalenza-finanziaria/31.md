A schedule is worth $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $2$. An equivalent single payment is $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$. When is the payment due?

- $1\,\mathrm{year}$
- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $2\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

Under compound interest a schedule valued at time $2$ is valued at time $T$ by multiplying by $q^{T-2}$. The payment of $720$ euros must therefore satisfy $600\cdot1.2^{T-2}=720$, or $1.2^{T-2}=1.2$. This gives $T-2=1$ and places the deposit at time $3$. The answer of $1$ year mistakes the time elapsed from $2$ for the date of the deposit.
