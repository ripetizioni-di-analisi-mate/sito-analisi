The annual compound interest rate is $25\%$. A replacement payment of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ must be equivalent to an outflow of $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. If settlement moves to the end of year two, what payment preserves equivalence?

- $625\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $640\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At $q=1.25$, a payment of $500$ euros is equivalent to $400$ euros today because $400\cdot1.25=500$, placing its due date in one year. At the new two-year due date the payment $X$ must satisfy $Xq^{-2}=400$, giving
$$
X=400\cdot1.25^2=625.
$$
The value of $600$ euros applies simple interest $400(1+2\cdot0.25)$, while $500$ euros leaves the payment unchanged despite moving its due date.
