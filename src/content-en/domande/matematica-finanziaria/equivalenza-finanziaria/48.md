A store offers a price of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today or $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. At an annual compound interest rate of $25\%$, what is the present cost of the deferred payment?

- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $480\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $750\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The deferred payment is due a year from today and has discount factor $q=1.25$. Its present cost is
$$
\frac{600}{1.25}=480.
$$
This gives $480$ euros, less than the immediate payment of $500$ euros. The value of $600$ euros is the undiscounted amount, while $750$ euros accumulates instead of discounting.
