Two debt schedules each have present cost $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$, what is their common cost at time $2$?

- $208.33\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $432\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under compound interest every schedule's value is multiplied by the same factor $q^{\sigma-\tau}$ when its valuation date changes. From time $0$ to time $2$ the factor is $1.2^2=1.44$, so their common cost is
$$
300\cdot1.44=432.
$$
The value of $420$ euros uses the simple-interest factor $1+2\cdot0.2=1.4$, while $360$ euros accumulates for only one year.
