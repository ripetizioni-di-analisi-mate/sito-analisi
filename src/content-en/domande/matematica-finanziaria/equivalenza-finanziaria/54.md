Three quotations give the equivalent value of all cash flows of the same plan at three reference dates a year apart. At an annual compound rate of $10\%$, the quoted values are €100, €110 and €120. How should the third value be corrected?

- Reduce it by €1.
- Increase it by €10.
- Increase it by €1.
- Leave it at €120.

## Explanation

The first two values agree with the factor $1.10$. Carrying the value of the plan forward one more year gives $110\cdot1.10=121$ euros. The third quotation is one euro short.
