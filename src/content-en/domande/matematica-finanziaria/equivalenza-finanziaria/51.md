A buyer can pay $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today or $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. What annual compound interest rate makes the options equivalent?

- $10.5\%$
- $10\%$
- $20\%$
- $21\%$

## Explanation

Today's equation of value is $400=\dfrac{484}{(1+i)^2}$. This gives
$$
(1+i)^2=\frac{484}{400}=1.21,
$$
and hence $1+i=1.1$ and $i=10\%$. Indeed, $400\cdot1.1^2=400\cdot1.21=484$. The value $21\%$ is the rate for the full two years, while $10.5\%$ halves that rate as if interest were simple.
