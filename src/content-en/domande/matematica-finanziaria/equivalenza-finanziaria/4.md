A debt of $726\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is due in two years. The borrower pays $275\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and settles the balance in three years. Find the balance due at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

- $423.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $496.1\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $465.85\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $523.6\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Use the original due date, time $2$, as the focal date. The down payment is worth $275\cdot1.1=302.5$ euros at that date, leaving $726-302.5=423.5$ euros outstanding. Settlement occurs a year later, so accumulate the balance for one further year to obtain
$$
X=423.5\cdot1.1=465.85.
$$
The value of $423.5$ euros omits this final year's interest, while $496.1$ euros subtracts the down payment without accumulating it.
