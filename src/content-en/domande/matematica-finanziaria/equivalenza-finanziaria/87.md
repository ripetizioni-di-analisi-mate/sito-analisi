A fee under schedule B is due today and all other payments match those of A. How does the fee affect their difference in present cost?

- B's present cost exceeds A's by the entire fee.
- The present costs stay equal.
- B's present cost exceeds A's by the fee multiplied by $q$.
- B's present cost exceeds A's by the fee discounted for one year.

## Explanation

Present cost is the sum of the cash flows valued today. The two schedules differ only in the fee, so their present-cost difference is the fee's present value. The fee is due today, has factor $q^0=1$, and enters at face value. B therefore costs more by the entire fee. Multiplying by $q$ or discounting it for a year would put the fee on a different date.
