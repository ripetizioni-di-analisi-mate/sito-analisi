A schedule is worth $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. In how many years would a payment of $864\,\mathrm{EUR}$ be equivalent to it at an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$?

- $4\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3.64\,\mathrm{years}$
- $2\,\mathrm{years}$
- $3\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

The due date $T$ satisfies $864\,q^{-T}=500$, or $q^T=\dfrac{864}{500}=1.728$. Since $1.2^3=1.728$, the answer is $T=3$ years. The value of $3.64$ years uses simple interest and solves $1+0.2\,T=1.728$. The answer of $2$ years corresponds to a factor of $1.2^2=1.44$ and a payment of $720$ euros.
