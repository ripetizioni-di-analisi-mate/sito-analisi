For a multiplicative accumulation law, $r(0,1)=1.2$ and $r(0,3)=1.5$ are known. Find the accumulation factor $r(1,3)$.

- $1.8$
- $1.25$
- $0.8$
- $1.3$

## Explanation

Multiplicativity makes direct accumulation from $0$ to $3$ equivalent to accumulation in two stages through $1$, so $r(0,3)=r(0,1)\,r(1,3)$. This gives
$$
r(1,3)=\frac{r(0,3)}{r(0,1)}=\frac{1.5}{1.2}=1.25.
$$
The value $1.8$ multiplies the two factors instead of dividing, $0.8$ is the reciprocal ratio, and $1.3$ subtracts the factors as if successive factors were added.
