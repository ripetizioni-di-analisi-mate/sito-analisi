A receivable of $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is due today. What is its value at time $2$ at an effective annual compound interest rate of $20\%$?

- $350\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $173.61\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The receivable is due today and valued two years later. Accumulate it for two periods using $q=1.2$ to obtain
$$
250\cdot1.2^2=250\cdot1.44=360.
$$
The value is $360$ euros. The value of $350$ euros uses simple interest $250(1+2\cdot0.2)$, while $300$ euros accumulates for just one year and $173.61$ euros divides by $1.44$, discounting instead of accumulating.
