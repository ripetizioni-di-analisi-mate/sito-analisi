A deterministic force of interest equals $0.02$ for the first two years and $0.06$ for the third. What is the logarithm of the overall accumulation factor?

- $0.06$
- $0.12$
- $0.1$
- $0.08$

## Explanation

With force of interest $\delta(t)$ the accumulation factor on $[0,3]$ is $\exp\!\left(\int_0^3\delta(u)\,\mathrm du\right)$, so its logarithm is the integral of the force. Since the force is piecewise constant,
$$
\int_0^3\delta(u)\,\mathrm du=2\cdot0.02+1\cdot0.06=0.1.
$$
The value $0.08$ adds the two forces without weighting them by their durations, while $0.12$ adds $3\cdot0.02$ and $0.06$, counting the third year twice.
