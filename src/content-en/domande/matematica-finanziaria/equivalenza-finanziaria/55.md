A schedule includes an outflow of $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and a refund of $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. What is its net present cost at an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$?

- $280\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $520\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

A refund is a receipt for the person paying the costs, so it reduces present cost. The refund due in a year is worth $\dfrac{120}{1.2}=100$ euros today. The net cost is therefore
$$
400-100=300.
$$
The value of $280$ euros subtracts the nominal refund, while $500$ euros and $520$ euros add it as though it were a cost.
