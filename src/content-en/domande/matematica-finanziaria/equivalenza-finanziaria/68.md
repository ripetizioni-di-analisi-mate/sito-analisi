What coefficient multiplies $X$ at time $1$ if payments of $X$ are made at times $0$ and $3$ under annual compound interest with factor $q>1$?

- $2q^{-1}$
- $q+q^{-2}$
- $q^{-1}+q^2$
- $1+q^{-3}$

## Explanation

At time $1$, a deposit from time $t$ has factor $q^{1-t}$. The deposit at time $0$ has factor $q^{1-0}=q$ and the one at time $3$ has factor $q^{1-3}=q^{-2}$. The resulting coefficient of $X$ is
$$
q+q^{-2}.
$$
The expression $q^{-1}+q^2$ reverses the sign of every exponent, while $1+q^{-3}$ is the coefficient at time $0$.
