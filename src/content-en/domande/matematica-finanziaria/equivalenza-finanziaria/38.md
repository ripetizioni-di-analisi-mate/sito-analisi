An account must hold $1260\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years with no intervening deposits. It earns $5\%$ in year one and $20\%$ in year two. How much is needed today?

- $1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1050\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1587.6\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With no intervening deposits the two-year factor is the product $1.05\cdot1.2=1.26$. The initial principal $C$ satisfies $1.26\,C=1260$, so
$$
C=\frac{1260}{1.26}=1000.
$$
The required principal is $1000$ euros. The value of $1050$ euros discounts only for year two, $1200$ euros only for year one, and $1587.6$ euros multiplies by the factor instead of dividing.
