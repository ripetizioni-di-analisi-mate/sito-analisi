A deposit of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is made at the end of year one in an account earning $5\%$ in year one and $20\%$ in year two. By how much does its final balance fall if the rates are reversed and the initial principal is held fixed?

- $40\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The initial principal receives both factors, $1.05$ and $1.2$, whose product is independent of order. The deposit at the end of year one earns only the second year's rate. It contributes $200\cdot1.2=240$ euros under the original order and $200\cdot1.05=210$ euros after the rates are swapped. The final balance therefore falls by $30$ euros. The value of $0$ euros considers only the initial principal.
