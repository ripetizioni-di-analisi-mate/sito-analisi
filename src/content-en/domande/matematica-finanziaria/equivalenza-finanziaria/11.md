An investment earns $0\%$ in the first year and $44\%$ in the second. Find the equivalent constant annual compound interest rate if there are no intervening cash flows.

- $20\%$
- $44\%$
- $22\%$
- $21\%$

## Explanation

With no intervening cash flows the two-year factor is the product of the annual factors, $1\cdot1.44=1.44$. The equivalent constant rate $i$ satisfies $(1+i)^2=1.44$, so $1+i=\sqrt{1.44}=1.2$ and $i=20\%$. The value $22\%$ is the arithmetic mean of the rates and omits compounding. The value $44\%$ is the rate for the entire two years.
