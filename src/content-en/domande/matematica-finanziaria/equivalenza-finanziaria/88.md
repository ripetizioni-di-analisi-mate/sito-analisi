A deferred payment with a fee today is equivalent to an immediate payment at rate $i_*$. With fixed amounts and a positive deferred payment, which schedule is cheaper if the rate rises above $i_*$?

- The deferred schedule, because the future payment has a lower present value.
- The schedules stay equivalent because the fee is fixed.
- The immediate schedule, because the entire deferred schedule is accumulated.
- The immediate schedule, because it is less sensitive to the rate.

## Explanation

Both the immediate price and the fee due today have factor $q^0=1$, making their present values independent of interest. The positive payment $C$ due in $t$ years has present value $Cq^{-t}$, which decreases as the rate rises. At $i_*$ the schedules have the same cost. Above $i_*$ the deferred schedule becomes cheaper, as its present cost falls and the immediate price stays fixed.
