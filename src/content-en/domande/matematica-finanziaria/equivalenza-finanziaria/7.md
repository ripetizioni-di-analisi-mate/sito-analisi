A debtor replaces payments of €40 in one year and €60 in three years with a single €100 payment. If the compound rate is exactly zero, which nonnegative payment dates make the promises equivalent?

- Only two years.
- All nonnegative dates.
- Only $2.2$ years.
- None.

## Explanation

At a zero rate every transfer factor is one. The two payments are worth $40+60=100$ at every date, so the value equation does not determine the date of the replacement payment.
