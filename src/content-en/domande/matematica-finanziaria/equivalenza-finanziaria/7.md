A schedule is worth $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Find the equivalent payment date for $405\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at an annual compound interest rate of $-10\%$.

- $3\,\mathrm{years}$
- $2\,\mathrm{years}$
- $1\,\mathrm{year}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{years}$

## Explanation

At a rate of $-10\%$ the annual factor is $q=0.9$. A payment of $405$ euros at time $T$ is equivalent to the schedule if $405\,q^{-T}=500$, or $0.9^T=\dfrac{405}{500}=0.81$. Since $0.9^2=0.81$, the due date is $T=2$ years from today. At a negative rate an amount below the present value is due in the future. The answer of $-2$ years gives the exponent the wrong sign.
