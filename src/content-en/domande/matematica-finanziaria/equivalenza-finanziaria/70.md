At a compound interest rate of $0\%$, the replacement payment equals the sum of the original payments. How many nonnegative due dates give equivalence?

- Only today.
- All of them.
- Only the mean of the dates.
- None.

## Explanation

With $i=0$ the factor is $q=1$ and changing dates leaves amounts unchanged. At every date the schedule is therefore worth the nominal sum $S$. The replacement payment equals $S$, so $S\cdot1^{-T}=S$ holds for every $T$. All nonnegative dates are equivalent, as the theory predicts for $i=0$ and $X=V$.
