A contract specifies a receipt of $660\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a cost of $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the same time $1$. What is their net present value at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$?

- $605\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $700\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The receipt and expense fall on the same date, so they may be netted first. The net amount at time $1$ is $660-110=550$ euros, with present value
$$
\frac{550}{1.1}=500.
$$
The present value is $500$ euros. The amount of $550$ euros omits discounting, while $605$ euros accumulates the balance instead of discounting it.
