A lender accepts one payment at time $1$ in place of $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $2$ and $720\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $3$. What amount is equivalent at an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$?

- $1320\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1756.8\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1100\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The single payment is at time $1$, before either receivable is due. Discount both. With $q=1.2$ this gives
$$
X=\frac{600}{1.2}+\frac{720}{1.2^2}=500+500=1000.
$$
The equivalent amount is $1000$ euros. The nominal sum is $1320$ euros, while $1100$ euros discounts even the second receivable for only one year.
