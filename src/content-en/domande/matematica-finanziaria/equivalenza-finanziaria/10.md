An account opens with $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. A deposit of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is made at the end of year one. Find the balance after two years when the effective annual rates are $10\%$ and $20\%$, respectively.

- $1452\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1420\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1440\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Follow the account balance year by year. At the end of year one it is $1000\cdot1.1=1100$ euros, and the deposit increases it to $1200$ euros. Only the rate of $20\%$ applies during year two, giving a final balance of
$$
1200\cdot1.2=1440.
$$
The value of $1452$ euros also applies the first year's factor to the deposit, while $1420$ euros gives the deposit no interest.
