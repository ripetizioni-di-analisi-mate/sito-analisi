Schedule A costs $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Schedule B requires $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find the present cost of B minus the present cost of A at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

- $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $62\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $0\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

With $q=1.1$, the installments under schedule B have present value
$$
\frac{220}{1.1}+\frac{242}{1.1^2}=200+200=400.
$$
The present cost of B is $400$ euros, equal to that of A, giving a difference of $0$ euros. The value of $62$ euros compares B's nominal total of $462$ euros with A's present cost. The value of $20$ euros discounts the second installment for only one year.
