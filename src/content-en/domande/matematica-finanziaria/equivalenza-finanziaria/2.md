A business pays $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and receives $396\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find the net value at time $1$ using an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

- $146\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $635\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $85\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

For the business, today's payment is negative and the receipt is positive. With $q=1.1$ and focal date $1$ we have
$$
V(1)=-250\cdot1.1+\frac{396}{1.1}=-275+360=85.
$$
The net value is therefore $85$ euros. The value of $146$ euros takes the difference between nominal amounts, $110$ euros adjusts only the receipt, and $635$ euros adds both values without applying the negative sign to the outflow.
