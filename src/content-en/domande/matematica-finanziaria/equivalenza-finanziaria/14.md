A purchase can be paid for with $320\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today or $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ now plus $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Find the annual compound interest rate at which the two offers are equivalent.

- $18.75\%$
- $26.67\%$
- $20\%$
- $12.5\%$

## Explanation

Write the equation of value at time zero. The installment of $20$ euros is immediate, so the deferred payment must be worth $320-20=300$ euros today, giving
$$
300=\frac{360}{1+i}.
$$
It follows that $1+i=\dfrac{360}{300}=1.2$ and hence $i=20\%$. The value $12.5\%$ compares $360$ with $320$ without allowing for the immediate installment, while $18.75\%$ compares $380$ with $320$.
