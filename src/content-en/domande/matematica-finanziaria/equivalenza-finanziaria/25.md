A debt of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today is settled with $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ immediately and the balance in one year. What final payment preserves equivalence at an annual compound interest rate of $25\%$?

- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $525\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $625\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Write the equation of value today. The immediate payment of $100$ euros reduces the time-zero debt to $400$ euros. The final payment $X$ due in one year must therefore be worth $400$ euros today, giving
$$
X=400\cdot1.25=500.
$$
The final payment is $500$ euros. The value of $625$ euros accumulates the whole debt without deducting the initial payment, while $400$ euros ignores interest during the year of deferral.
