Under a multiplicative accumulation law the discount factors from today to times $1$ and $4$ are $0.96$ and $0.768$. Which factor discounts an amount from time $4$ to time $1$?

- $0.8$
- $0.7373$
- $0.768$
- $1.25$

## Explanation

Under a multiplicative law, discounting from time $4$ to today can be split into discounting from $4$ to $1$ and from $1$ to $0$. Thus $v(0,4)=v(0,1)\,v(1,4)$ and
$$
v(1,4)=\frac{0.768}{0.96}=0.8.
$$
The value $0.768$ discounts from time $4$ all the way to today. The value $1.25$ is the reciprocal of $0.8$, the accumulation factor from $1$ to $4$.
