Schedule A costs $420\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and B costs $528\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. At an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$, what is B's cost minus A's cost at time $1$?

- $-24\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $108\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $24\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

At time $1$, A's cost, paid today, has accumulated for a year and equals $420\cdot1.2=504$ euros. The cost of B is due at time $1$ and equals $528$ euros. Their difference is therefore
$$
528-504=24.
$$
The value of $108$ euros is the difference between nominal amounts, $20$ euros is the difference valued today, and $-24$ euros reverses the requested subtraction.
