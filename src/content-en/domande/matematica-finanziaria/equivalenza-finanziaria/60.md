A receipt and an outflow of equal size fall in different years. Under compound interest at a positive rate, can any choice of focal date make their net value zero?

- The date exactly halfway between the two due dates.
- The date of the receipt.
- No focal date.
- The date of the outflow.

## Explanation

Let $C$ be the receipt at time $t_1$, and $C$ the outflow at time $t_2\ne t_1$. At the focal date $\tau$ the net value is
$$
C\left(q^{\tau-t_1}-q^{\tau-t_2}\right)=C\,q^{\tau}\left(q^{-t_1}-q^{-t_2}\right).
$$
With $q>1$ the powers $q^{-t_1}$ and $q^{-t_2}$ differ and $q^{\tau}>0$, so the net value is nonzero at every date. Changing the focal date multiplies the value by a positive factor, so it cannot bring the net value to zero.
