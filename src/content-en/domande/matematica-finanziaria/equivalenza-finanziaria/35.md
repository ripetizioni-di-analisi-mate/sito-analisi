Payments of $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year are replaced by $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. What is the discount factor at the equivalent average due date under annual compound interest of $25\%$?

- $0.95$
- $0.85$
- $0.8$
- $0.9$

## Explanation

With $q^{-1}=0.8$ the two sums have a present value of $300+100\cdot0.8=380$ euros. The replacement payment of $400$ euros must have that same present value, giving a discount factor of
$$
q^{-T}=\frac{380}{400}=0.95.
$$
The value $0.9$ is the unweighted mean of the factors $1$ and $0.8$, omitting weights $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The value $0.8$ is the factor for the second date.
