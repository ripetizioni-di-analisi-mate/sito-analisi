Two positive receivables have discount factors $0.90$ and $0.70$. The discount factor corresponding to their equivalent mean date is $0.85$. What percentage of the combined face value belongs to the first receivable?

- $75\%$
- $25\%$
- $50\%$
- $85\%$

## Explanation

With $w$ the first receivable’s weight, equivalence requires $0.90w+0.70(1-w)=0.85$. The weight is $w=\dfrac{0.15}{0.20}=0.75$.
