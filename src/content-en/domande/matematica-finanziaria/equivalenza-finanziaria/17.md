From a company's perspective, what is the value at time $2$ of an outflow of $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ occurring at that same time $2$? The annual compound interest rate is $20\%$.

- $-600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-720\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The payment is an outflow for the business and its signed cash flow is $(2,-600)$. The due date is also the focal date, giving exponent $2-2=0$ and factor $q^0=1$. Its value is therefore $-600$ euros. The value of $-500$ euros discounts for one year, $-720$ euros accumulates for one year, and $600$ euros ignores the negative sign.
