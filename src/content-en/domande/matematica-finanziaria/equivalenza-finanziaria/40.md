An account opens with $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and earns $10\%$ in year one and $20\%$ in year two. What deposit at the end of year one is needed to reach $780\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of year two?

- $50\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $150\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

In year two the balance grows by $20\%$ to the required $780$ euros, so immediately after the deposit it must be $\dfrac{780}{1.2}=650$ euros. Before the deposit the account holds $500\cdot1.1=550$ euros, giving a deposit of
$$
650-550=100.
$$
The value of $150$ euros omits the first year's return because $650-500=150$. The value of $120$ euros accumulates the correct deposit for the second year.
