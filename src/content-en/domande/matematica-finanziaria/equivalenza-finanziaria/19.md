A schedule is worth $440\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$. What is its value at time $3$ at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$?

- $532.4\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $484\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $528\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $363.64\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under compound interest, multiply the value of a schedule by $q^{\sigma-\tau}$ to change its valuation date. From $\tau=1$ to $\sigma=3$ the exponent is $2$, giving
$$
V(3)=440\cdot1.1^2=440\cdot1.21=532.4.
$$
The required value is $532.4$ euros. The value of $484$ euros accumulates for only one year, $528$ euros uses the simple-interest factor $1+2\cdot0.1=1.2$, and $363.64$ euros divides by $1.21$ instead of multiplying.
