Two installments of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ are due at times $1$ and $3$. What equivalent single payment is due at time $2$ at an annual compound interest rate of $25\%$?

- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $410\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The single payment at time $2$ is due after the first installment and before the second. With $q=1.25$, accumulate the first and discount the second to obtain
$$
200\cdot1.25+\frac{200}{1.25}=250+160=410.
$$
The equivalent payment is $410$ euros. The value of $400$ euros is the nominal sum, $450$ euros leaves the second payment at face value, and $500$ euros accumulates the second payment as well.
