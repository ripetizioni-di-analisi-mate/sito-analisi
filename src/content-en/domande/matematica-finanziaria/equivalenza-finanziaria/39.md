An account grows from $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today to $528\,\mathrm{EUR}$ after two years with no intervening transactions. Its return in year one is $10\%$. What is the effective return in year two?

- $16\%$
- $32\%$
- $10\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The account is worth $400\cdot1.1=440$ euros after year one. In year two $440$ euros grows to $528$ euros, so the second-year factor is
$$
\frac{528}{440}=1.2
$$
and the rate is $20\%$. The value $32\%$ is the rate for the full two years, $\dfrac{528}{400}-1$, while $16\%$ halves that rate without removing the contribution from year one.
