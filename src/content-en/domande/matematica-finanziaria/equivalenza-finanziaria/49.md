A purchase requires $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and a processing fee of $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. What is its present cost at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$?

- $520\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $518.18\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $570\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Present cost adds the cash flows after discounting each to today. The payment in one year is worth $\dfrac{550}{1.1}=500$ euros today, and the processing fee is due immediately and enters at face value. The cost is therefore
$$
500+20=520.
$$
The value of $518.18$ euros discounts the processing fee as well, as $\dfrac{570}{1.1}\approx518.18$ shows. The nominal total is $570$ euros.
