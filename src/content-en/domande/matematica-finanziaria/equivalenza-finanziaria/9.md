Receipts of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ are replaced by $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The annual compound interest rate is $25\%$. Find the discount factor for the equivalent average due date to four decimal places.

- $0.9$
- $1$
- $0.8667$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

With $q=1.25$ the one-year discount factor is $0.8$. The receipts have a present value of $100+200\cdot0.8=260$ euros. A single payment of $300$ euros at the equivalent average due date must have the same present value, giving a discount factor of
$$
q^{-T}=\frac{260}{300}=\frac{13}{15}\approx0.8667.
$$
The value $0.8$ is the factor for the second due date, while $0.9$ averages $1$ and $0.8$ without weighting by the amounts.
