A unit account under a multiplicative law has balances $a(1)=1.1$, $a(2)=1.21$, and $a(3)=1.452$. At time $2$, find the net value of a receipt of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ and an outflow of $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $3$.

- $310\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-140\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-178\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-90\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under the unit-account representation, the factor between $s$ and $t$ is $\dfrac{a(t)}{a(s)}$. At time $2$ the receipt is worth $100\cdot\dfrac{1.21}{1.1}=110$ euros and the outflow $-240\cdot\dfrac{1.21}{1.452}=-200$ euros. The net amount is
$$
110-200=-90.
$$
The value of $-140$ euros is the difference between nominal amounts, while $310$ euros adds both values without making the outflow negative.
