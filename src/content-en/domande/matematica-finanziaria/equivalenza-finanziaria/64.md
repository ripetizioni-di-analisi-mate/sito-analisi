Two schedules have the same present value. What addition at time $3$ preserves their equivalence at the same compound interest rate?

- Only for a positive amount equal to the value of both schedules.
- Only if opposite signs are used for the two additions.
- Only for a zero amount.
- For any identical amount added with the same sign to both.

## Explanation

Let $V_A(0)=V_B(0)$. Adding amount $C$ at time $3$ to both schedules adds the same term $Cq^{-3}$ to each present value. Their difference therefore remains
$$
V_A(0)+Cq^{-3}-\bigl(V_B(0)+Cq^{-3}\bigr)=0
$$
for every $C$, whether positive, negative, or zero. Adding the amount with opposite signs would change the difference to $2Cq^{-3}$.
