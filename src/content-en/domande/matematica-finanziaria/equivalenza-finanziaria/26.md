A payment of $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ is replaced by $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $2$ and a payment today. Find the immediate payment to the nearest cent at an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$.

- $150\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $166.67\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $180.56\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Use today as the focal date with $q=1.2$. The payment of $300$ euros is worth $\dfrac{300}{1.2}=250$ euros today. Subtract the present value of the installment due at time $2$ to obtain the immediate payment, giving
$$
X=250-\frac{100}{1.2^2}\approx250-69.44=180.56.
$$
The value of $166.67$ euros discounts the installment for only one year, while $150$ euros subtracts its nominal amount.
