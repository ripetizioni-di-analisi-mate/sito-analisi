A debt of $D$ is due at time $2$. A partial payment is made at time $1$ in the amount $A$. What balance remains at the original due date if the annual compound accumulation factor is $q$?

- $D-Aq^{-1}$
- $D-Aq$
- $D-A$
- $(D-A)q$

## Explanation

Find the outstanding balance at the original due date, time $2$. The down payment $A$ was made one year earlier and is worth $Aq$ on that date. The balance at time $2$ is therefore
$$
D-Aq.
$$
The expression $D-A$ deducts the down payment without accumulating it, and $D-Aq^{-1}$ discounts it as if the payment were made after the due date. The expression $(D-A)q$ is the balance carried to time $3$.
