Two schedules are equivalent at time $2$ under simple annual interest of $10\%$. Schedule A pays $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. What single outlay must B make at time $2$?

- $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $166.67\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $240\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under simple interest, principal $C$ available today grows to $C(1+it)$ after $t$ years. At the focal date $2$, schedule A is therefore worth $200(1+0.1\cdot2)=240$ euros. Schedule B's payment is due at time $2$ and enters at face value, so it must be $240$ euros. The value of $242$ euros uses the compound factor $1.1^2$, while $220$ euros accumulates for just one year.
