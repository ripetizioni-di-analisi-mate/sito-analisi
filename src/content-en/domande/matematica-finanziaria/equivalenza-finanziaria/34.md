Two debts of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ each are due today and in two years. At an annual compound interest rate of $25\%$, what is the present value of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ payable at their equivalent average due date?

- $164\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $180\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

By definition a payment of $200$ euros on the equivalent average due date has the same present value as the two debts. With $q^{-1}=0.8$ its present value is
$$
100+100\cdot0.8^2=100+64=164.
$$
The payment is worth $164$ euros today. The value of $200$ euros is its nominal amount, $180$ euros discounts the second debt for only one year, and $160$ euros discounts $200$ euros for one year, the arithmetic mean of the original dates.
