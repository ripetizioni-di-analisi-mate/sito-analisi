Debts of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $363\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years are to be replaced by a single payment in two years. Find that payment at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

- $913\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $949.3\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $863\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The single payment is due at time $2$, so write the equation of value on that date. Accumulate the debt from time $1$ for one year and discount the debt from time $3$ for one year, giving
$$
X=500\cdot1.1+\frac{363}{1.1}=550+330=880.
$$
The value of $863$ euros is the nominal sum. The value of $949.3$ euros also accumulates the debt at time $3$.
