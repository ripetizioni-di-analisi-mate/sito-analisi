A statement adds €100 today to €121 due in two years, at $10\%$ annual compound interest. Which pair gives the total value today and the total value in two years, in that order?

- $(221,221)$ euros
- $(200,242)$ euros
- $(210,231)$ euros
- $(200,221)$ euros

## Explanation

Today the future €121 is worth $\dfrac{121}{1.21}=100$ euros, so the total is €200. Carrying the entire value forward two years gives $200\cdot1.21=242$. Adding the face amounts directly mixes valuation dates.
