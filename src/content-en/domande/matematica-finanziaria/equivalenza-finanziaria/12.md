Under a multiplicative accumulation law the discount factors from today to times $1$ and $3$ are $0.9$ and $0.72$. Find the value at time $1$ of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ due at time $3$.

- $450\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $625\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $360\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

Under a multiplicative accumulation law the value at time $1$ of one euro due at time $3$ is the ratio of the discount factors from today, giving
$$
v(1,3)=\frac{v(0,3)}{v(0,1)}=\frac{0.72}{0.9}=0.8.
$$
The required value is $500\cdot0.8=400$ euros. The amount $360$ euros uses $0.72$ and discounts all the way to today, while $450$ euros uses the first year's factor $0.9$.
