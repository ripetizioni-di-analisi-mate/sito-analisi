Three point charges $q_1$, $q_2$ and $q_3$ lie on the $x$ axis, in vacuum, and are subject only to their mutual electric forces. The total force on $q_1$ has component $F_{1x} = +0.40\,\mathrm{N}$ and the one on $q_2$ has component $F_{2x} = -0.25\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the component $F_{3x}$ of the total force on $q_3$?

- $+0.15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-0.65\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+0.65\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-0.15\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The forces that the charges exert on one another come in pairs $\vec{F}_{ij} = -\vec{F}_{ji}$, so the **sum of the internal forces** on all the charges of the system is zero. Therefore $F_{1x} + F_{2x} + F_{3x} = 0$, from which
$$
F_{3x} = -(0.40 - 0.25)\,\mathrm{N} = -0.15\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The result does not require knowing the values of the charges or their positions.
