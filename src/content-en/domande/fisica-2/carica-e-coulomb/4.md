Two identical isolated conducting spheres are far from other charges. After contact and separation each carries $+2\,\mathrm{nC}$. During contact, electrons carrying a charge of magnitude $3\,\mathrm{nC}$ moved from A to B. What were the original charges on A and B?

- $(+5,-1)\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $(-3,+3)\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $(+2,+2)\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $(-1,+5)\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

Losing electrons increases the charge on A by $3\,\mathrm{nC}$, so it began at $-1$ to finish at $+2$. B gained electrons, so its original charge in nanocoulombs was $2+3=5$.
