Three identical conducting spheres, initially carrying charges of $+12$, $-6$ and $+3\,\mathrm{nC}$, are connected and then separated. What is the final charge of each sphere?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $12\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $3\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The total charge is $12-6+3=9\,\mathrm{nC}$. Since the spheres are identical and connected, the charge is divided into three equal parts. The result is $3\,\mathrm{nC}$.
