A neutral sphere is grounded while a positive rod is nearby. The ground connection is removed first and the rod is then moved away. What is the sphere's final charge?

- Positive
- Zero
- It periodically alternates in sign
- Negative

## Explanation

The positive rod draws electrons from the ground to the sphere. Disconnecting the ground first leaves the excess electrons on the sphere. Moving the rod away redistributes the charge but does not remove it.
