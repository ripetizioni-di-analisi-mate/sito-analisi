Two charges $1\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $4\,\mu\mathrm C$ interact with a force of magnitude $0{,}90\,\mathrm N$. What is their separation? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Coulomb's law gives $r^2=\dfrac{k|q_1q_2|}{F}$. The separation is the positive square root of this expression. The result is $0{,}2\,\mathrm m$.
