A sensor measures the repulsion between two small isolated charged spheres. A second trial uses the same separation and medium, but leakage reduces the first charge by $20\%$ and the second by $50\%$, without reversing either sign. What fraction of the original force remains?

- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

At fixed separation the force is proportional to the product of the charges. The remaining fractions are $\dfrac45$ and $\dfrac12$, whose product is $\dfrac25$.
