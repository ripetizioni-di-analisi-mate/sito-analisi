A positive charge experiences a force of $7\,\mathrm N$ to the right and a force of $3\,\mathrm N$ to the left. What is the component of the resultant, taking the rightward direction as positive?

- $-4\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $-10\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The forces are added with their signs. With the axis pointing to the right, we obtain $F_x=7-3$. The result is $4\,\mathrm N$.
