Two charges are immersed in a homogeneous linear dielectric with $\varepsilon_r>1$. With the charges and separation fixed, what effect occurs compared with a vacuum?

- The force magnitude increases
- The force magnitude decreases
- The interaction always becomes attractive
- The charges necessarily change sign

## Explanation

In the homogeneous model, the Coulomb constant is $\dfrac{k}{\varepsilon_r}$. Therefore, the force magnitude is smaller. The sign of the product of the charges remains unchanged.
