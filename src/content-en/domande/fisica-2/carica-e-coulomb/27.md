Two point charges are separated by $0{,}60\,\mathrm m$. When their separation is changed to $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$, by what factor does the force magnitude change?

- $4{,}5$
- $18$
- $9$
- $36$

## Explanation

The ratio of the initial separation to the final separation is three. Since the force magnitude varies as $r^{-2}$, the ratio of the forces is $3^2$. The result is $9$.
