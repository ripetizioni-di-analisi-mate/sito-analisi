Two identical conducting spheres with initially positive charges are brought into contact and then separated. They exchange no charge with their surroundings. Given the initial charge of the first sphere $q_{1} = 10\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the initial charge of the second sphere $q_{2} = 6\,\mathrm{nC}$, what is the final charge of each sphere $q$?

- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $16\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The total charge is conserved. The identical spheres, separated symmetrically after contact, share the charge equally. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{q_{1}}{2} + \dfrac{q_{2}}{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $8\,\mathrm{nC}$.
