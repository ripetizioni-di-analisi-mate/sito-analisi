The initial force between two point charges is measured as $F_0$. The separation is changed by a factor of $b$, keeping the charges and medium unchanged. Given the initial force magnitude $F_{0} = 36\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the ratio of final to initial separation $b = 3$, what is the final force magnitude $F$?

- $4\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $324\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

According to Coulomb's law, the magnitude varies inversely with the square of the separation. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{F_{0}}{b^{2}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $4\,\mathrm{mN}$.
