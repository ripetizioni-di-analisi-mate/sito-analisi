A sphere $A$ carrying a charge of $16\,\mathrm{nC}$ touches an identical neutral sphere $B$. After being separated from $B$, it touches a third identical neutral sphere $C$. What charge remains on $A$?

- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $16\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

At the first contact, the charge on $A$ is halved to $8\,\mathrm{nC}$. At the second contact, it is halved again, because $C$ is neutral and identical to $A$. The result is $4\,\mathrm{nC}$.
