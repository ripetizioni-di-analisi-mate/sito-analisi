Two equal positive charges of $2\,\mu\mathrm C$ are fixed at $x=-0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ and $x=+0{,}10\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the force on a charge $1\,\mu\mathrm C$ at the origin? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $1{,}8\,\mathrm N$
- $3{,}6\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}9\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The two forces have the same magnitude by symmetry. Since they point in opposite directions, their sum is zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm N$.
