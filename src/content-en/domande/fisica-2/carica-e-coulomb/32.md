Which charge is compatible with quantization, if $e$ is the elementary charge?

- $0{,}5e$
- $1{,}2e$
- $\sqrt2\,e$
- $-3e$

## Explanation

The net charge of an ordinary body is an integer multiple of $e$. The number $-3$ is an integer. The other proposed coefficients are not.
