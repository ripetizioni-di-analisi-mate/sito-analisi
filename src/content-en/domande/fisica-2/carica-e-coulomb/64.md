Three equal point charges $q > 0$ occupy three vertices of a regular tetrahedron with edge $a$, in vacuum, and the charge $q_0 > 0$ occupies the fourth vertex. What is the magnitude of the total force on $q_0$?

- $3\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$
- $\sqrt{6}\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$
- $\sqrt{3}\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$
- $\sqrt{2}\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$

## Explanation

Each charge $q$ is at distance $a$ from $q_0$, so it exerts on $q_0$ a repulsive force of magnitude $\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$, directed along the edge joining them. The three charges $q$ form an equilateral triangle and $q_0$ lies on the line perpendicular to the triangle through its center, therefore by symmetry the components of the three forces parallel to the plane of the triangle cancel. The center of the triangle is at distance $\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{3}}$ from each vertex, so the height of the tetrahedron is
$$
h = \sqrt{a^2 - \frac{a^2}{3}} = \sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}\,a .
$$
Each edge leaving $q_0$ makes with the height the angle $\alpha$ such that $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{h}{a} = \sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}}$. By the **superposition principle** the total force is directed along the height, away from the tetrahedron, and has magnitude
$$
F = 3 \cdot \frac{kqq_0}{a^2}\,\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}} = \sqrt{6}\,\frac{kqq_0}{a^2} .
$$
