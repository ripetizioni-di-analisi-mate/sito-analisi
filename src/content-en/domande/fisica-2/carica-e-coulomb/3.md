Two identical conducting spheres have charges of $+8\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$. They are separated after contact. What is the charge of each sphere?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $3\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $12\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The total charge is conserved and equals $6\,\mathrm{nC}$. Since the spheres are identical, each receives half the total charge. The result is $3\,\mathrm{nC}$.
