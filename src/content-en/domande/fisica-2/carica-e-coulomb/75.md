Three equal point charges $q > 0$ occupy three vertices of a square of side $a$, in vacuum. What is the magnitude of the force on the charge $q_0 > 0$ placed at the fourth vertex?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 2,0 2,2 0,2 0,0" punti="0,0,pieno;2,0,pieno;0,2,pieno;2,2,pieno" etichette="-0.3,-0.25,q;2.25,-0.25,q;-0.3,2.2,q;2.3,2.2,q₀;1,-0.3,a" xmin=-0.9 xmax=2.9 ymin=-0.6 ymax=2.5 titolo="Three equal charges $q$ at three vertices of the square of side $a$ and the charge $q_0$ at the fourth vertex"}

- $\left(2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$
- $\sqrt{2}\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$
- $\left(\sqrt{2} + \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$
- $\left(\sqrt{2} + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$

## Explanation

Place $q_0$ at the vertex $(a, a)$ and the other charges at the vertices $(0, 0)$, $(a, 0)$ and $(0, a)$. The two adjacent charges are at distance $a$ and repel $q_0$ along the sides, with perpendicular forces of magnitude $\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$; their sum has magnitude $\sqrt{2}\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{a^2}$ and is directed along the diagonal, outwards from the square. The opposite charge is at distance $\sqrt{2}\,a$ and repels $q_0$ along the same diagonal, with magnitude $\dfrac{kqq_0}{2a^2}$. The two forces have the same direction and the same orientation, therefore
$$
F = \left(\sqrt{2} + \frac{1}{2}\right)\frac{kqq_0}{a^2} \approx 1.91\,\frac{kqq_0}{a^2} .
$$
