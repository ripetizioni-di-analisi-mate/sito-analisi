Two equal point charges $Q > 0$ are fixed in vacuum at the points $(-a, 0)$ and $(a, 0)$, with $a = 6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. A charge $q_0 > 0$ moves along the positive $y$ semi-axis. At what $y$ coordinate is the magnitude of the force on $q_0$ maximum?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

On the perpendicular bisector of the segment the force is directed along the $y$ axis and equals $F_y = \dfrac{2kQq_0\,y}{\left(a^2 + y^2\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}}$. Its derivative with respect to $y$ is
$$
F_y'(y) = 2kQq_0\,\frac{a^2 - 2y^2}{\left(a^2 + y^2\right)^{\frac{5}{2}}} ,
$$
so $F_y$ increases for $y < \dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$ and decreases for $y > \dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$. Therefore the maximum is at
$$
y = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{6.0}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{cm} \approx 4.2\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
