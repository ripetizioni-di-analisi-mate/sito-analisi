A small negative charge is deposited at a point on the surface of a glass sphere and at a point on the surface of an isolated metal sphere. Where is the charge of each sphere shortly afterwards?

- On both spheres it spreads over the outer surface
- On the glass it spreads over the whole surface, on the metal it stays where it was deposited
- On both spheres it spreads uniformly through the volume
- On the glass it stays where it was deposited, on the metal it spreads over the outer surface

## Explanation

Glass is an insulator, so the deposited electrons stay bound to the molecules of the region where they are. The metal is a conductor, therefore the excess electrons move freely and repel each other. They move until they reach an equilibrium configuration, in which the charge lies on the **outer surface** of the sphere.
