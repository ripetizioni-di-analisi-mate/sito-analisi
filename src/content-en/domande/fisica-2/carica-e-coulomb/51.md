Two point charges remain in a homogeneous medium. The force they would exert in a vacuum at the same separation would have magnitude $F_0$. Given the magnitude of the force in a vacuum $F_{0} = 18\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the relative permittivity of the medium $\epsilon = 3$, what is the magnitude of the force in the medium $F$?

- $6\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $54\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The permittivity changes from $\varepsilon_0$ to $\varepsilon\varepsilon_0$. The force magnitude is inversely proportional to the permittivity. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{F_{0}}{\epsilon}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $6\,\mathrm{mN}$.
