Two charges have an initial force of $F$. One becomes three times larger and the other only changes sign. At an unchanged separation, what is the ratio of the new magnitude to $F$?

- $1{,}5$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

The force magnitude depends on the absolute value of the product of the charges. Changing the sign reverses the direction of the interaction, while tripling one charge triples the magnitude. The result is $3$.
