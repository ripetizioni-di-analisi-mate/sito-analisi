Two point charges are at rest in a vacuum at a separation of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Use $k=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. Given the magnitude of the first charge $q_{1} = 3\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the magnitude of the second charge $q_{2} = 8\,\mathrm{nC}$, what is the magnitude of the Coulomb force $F$?

- $480\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $240\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

The force is $kq_1q_2/r^2$. With $r=0{,}03\,\mathrm m$, the product of the magnitudes in nanocoulombs must be multiplied by $10$ to obtain micronewtons. In the indicated units, $F = 10 \cdot q_{1} \cdot q_{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $240\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
