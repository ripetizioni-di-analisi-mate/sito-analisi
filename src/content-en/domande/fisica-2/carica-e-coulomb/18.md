Three equal charges of $1\,\mu\mathrm C$ are at the vertices of an equilateral triangle of side length $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the force on one of them? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $0{,}779\,\mathrm N$
- $1{,}56\,\mathrm N$
- $3{,}12\,\mathrm N$
- $6{,}24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Each of the two forces has magnitude $0{,}90\,\mathrm N$. The angle between them is $60^\circ$, so the resultant has a magnitude $\sqrt3$ times the magnitude of one force. The result is $1{,}56\,\mathrm N$.
