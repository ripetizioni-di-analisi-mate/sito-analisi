Two isolated droplets merge without losing charge. The resulting droplet has charge $-e$, where $e>0$ is the elementary charge. Which pair of original charges satisfies both conservation and quantisation of droplet charge?

- $+2e,\;-3e$
- $-\dfrac{e}{2},\;-\dfrac{e}{2}$
- $+e,\;+2e$
- $-2e,\;-e$

## Explanation

The two charges must be integer multiples of $e$ and add to $-e$. The pair $+2e,-3e$ satisfies both requirements. Two half-charges have the required sum but cannot occur on ordinary isolated droplets.
