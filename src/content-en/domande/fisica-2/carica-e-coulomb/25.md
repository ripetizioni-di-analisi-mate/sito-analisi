Two charged, isolated water drops merge into a single drop, without exchanging charge with the surroundings. The final drop has charge $-e$, where $e > 0$ is the elementary charge. Which pair of initial charges is compatible with both conservation and quantization of charge?

- $+2e$ and $-3e$
- $-\dfrac{e}{2}$ and $-\dfrac{e}{2}$
- $+e$ and $+2e$
- $-2e$ and $-e$

## Explanation

By quantization the charge of each drop is an integer multiple of $e$, so the pair $-\dfrac{e}{2}$ and $-\dfrac{e}{2}$ is excluded, even though its sum is $-e$.

By conservation the sum of the initial charges equals the final charge $-e$. The pair $+e$ and $+2e$ has sum $+3e$ and the pair $-2e$ and $-e$ has sum $-3e$, therefore neither of them is compatible.

The pair $+2e$ and $-3e$ consists of integer multiples of $e$ and has sum $-e$, so it is the only one that satisfies both conditions.
