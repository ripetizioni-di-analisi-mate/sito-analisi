A neutral body loses $2{,}0\cdot10^{12}$ electrons. What is its final charge in microcoulombs? Use $e=1{,}6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$.

- $0{,}32\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $0{,}16\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $0{,}64\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $1{,}28\,\mu\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Losing electrons leaves a positive charge. The charge magnitude is $q=Ne$, then converted from coulombs to microcoulombs. The result is $0{,}32\,\mu\mathrm C$.
