Two identical conducting spheres with initially positive charges are brought into contact and then separated. They exchange no charge with their surroundings. Given the initial charge of the second sphere $q_{2} = 6\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the final charge of each sphere $q = 8\,\mathrm{nC}$, what is the initial charge of the first sphere $q_{1}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $10\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $20\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The total charge is conserved. The identical spheres, separated symmetrically after contact, share the charge equally. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{q_{1}}{2} + \dfrac{q_{2}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $q_{1} = 2 \cdot q - q_{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $10\,\mathrm{nC}$.
