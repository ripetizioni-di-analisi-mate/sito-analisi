A proton and an electron are at rest in vacuum at some distance, subject only to their mutual electric force. Which statement is true at the instant they are released?

- The forces have the same magnitude and the acceleration of the electron has the larger magnitude
- The force on the electron has the larger magnitude, because its mass is smaller
- The forces have the same magnitude and the accelerations also have the same magnitude
- The force on the proton has the larger magnitude, because its mass is larger

## Explanation

By **Coulomb's law** the forces that the two charges exert on each other have magnitude $k\,\dfrac{e^2}{r^2}$, the same line of action and opposite directions, so they have the same magnitude $F$. By Newton's second law the magnitude of the acceleration of each particle is $\dfrac{F}{m}$. The mass of the electron is about $1800$ times smaller than that of the proton, therefore the acceleration of the electron has the **larger** magnitude.
