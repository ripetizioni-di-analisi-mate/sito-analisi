Two positive charges $q$ and $4q$ are fixed at $x=0$ and $x=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. At what coordinate between them is the force on a test charge zero?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The fields between the charges point in opposite directions. The condition $\dfrac{q}{x^2}=\dfrac{4q}{(30-x)^2}$ gives $30-x=2x$, so $x=10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The result is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
