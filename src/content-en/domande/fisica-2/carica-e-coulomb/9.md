The separation between two fixed charges changes from $r$ to $3r$. If the initial force magnitude is $18\,\mathrm N$, what does it become?

- $2\,\mathrm N$
- $1\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The Coulomb force is inversely proportional to the square of the separation. Tripling the separation divides the force magnitude by nine. The result is $2\,\mathrm N$.
