The initial force between two point charges is measured as $F_0$. The separation is changed by a factor of $b$, keeping the charges and medium unchanged. Given the ratio of final to initial separation $b = 3$ and the final force magnitude $F = 4\,\mathrm{mN}$, what is the initial force magnitude $F_{0}$?

- $72\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

According to Coulomb's law, the magnitude varies inversely with the square of the separation. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{F_{0}}{b^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F_{0} = F \cdot b^{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $36\,\mathrm{mN}$.
