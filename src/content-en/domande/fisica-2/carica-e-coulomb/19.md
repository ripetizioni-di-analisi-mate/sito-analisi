Two equal positive charges are separated by $0{,}40\,\mathrm m$. A third charge is at the midpoint. What is the ratio of the magnitudes of the two forces exerted on the third charge?

- $0{,}5$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The two sources have equal charges and are both at a distance of $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$ from the midpoint. Coulomb's law therefore gives two equal magnitudes. The result is $1$.
