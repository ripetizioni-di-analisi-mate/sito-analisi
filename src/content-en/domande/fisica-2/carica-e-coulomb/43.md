When using Coulomb's law to treat two small spheres as point charges, which condition makes the approximation good?

- The radii are greater than the distance between the centers
- The spheres must necessarily be neutral
- The radii are much smaller than the distance between the centers
- The distance must be zero

## Explanation

The point-charge description neglects the extent of the bodies. When the radii are small compared with the distance, geometric variations in the separation are reduced. The effects of charge redistribution are also less significant.
