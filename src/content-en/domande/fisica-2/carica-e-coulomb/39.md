A third charge is added near two fixed point charges. How is the force on one of the first two calculated?

- The magnitudes are always added
- The magnitudes are always subtracted
- The forces from the other charges are added vectorially
- Only the nearest charge is used

## Explanation

The superposition principle applies to each pair of charges. The individual forces have their own directions. The resultant force is therefore their vector sum.
