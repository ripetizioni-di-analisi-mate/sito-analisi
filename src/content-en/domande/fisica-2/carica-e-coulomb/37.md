A positively charged electroscope is touched by a positively charged conducting body. Which conclusion follows from the signs of the charges alone?

- The transfer depends on the potentials of the two bodies
- Electrons always pass to the electroscope
- Electrons always leave the electroscope
- Both always become neutral

## Explanation

The direction of transfer is determined by the potential difference. Two positively charged bodies can have different potentials because of their charge and geometry. The sign alone does not determine the direction of the charge current.
