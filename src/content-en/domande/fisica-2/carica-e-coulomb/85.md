Two positive source charges lie on either side of a positive test charge on the same line. The contribution from the source on the left has the greater magnitude. Given the magnitude of the force from the source on the left $F_{1} = 13\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the magnitude of the force from the source on the right $F_{2} = 8\,\mathrm{mN}$, what is the magnitude of the resultant force $F$?

- $21\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\sqrt{233}\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The contributions are repulsive and point in opposite directions. The rightward force is greater, so the magnitudes are subtracted. In the indicated units, $F = F_{1} - F_{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $5\,\mathrm{mN}$.
