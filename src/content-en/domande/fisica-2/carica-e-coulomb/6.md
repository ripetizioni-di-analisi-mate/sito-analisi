Two charges $2\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $3\,\mu\mathrm C$ are separated by $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the force between them? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $0{,}3\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}6\,\mathrm N$
- $1{,}2\,\mathrm N$
- $2{,}4\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Coulomb's law gives $F=\dfrac{k|q_1q_2|}{r^2}$. The charges in microcoulombs are converted to coulombs before the calculation. The result is $0{,}6\,\mathrm N$.
