Which of the following configurations has an equilibrium position that is stable for all possible displacements?

- A positive charge at the center of a cube with eight positive charges fixed at the vertices, subject only to electric forces
- A negative charge at the center of a square with four positive charges fixed at the vertices, subject only to electric forces
- A charged small sphere inside a vertical insulating tube, above a fixed charge of the same sign
- Three free collinear charges, in equilibrium under their mutual forces alone

## Explanation

By **Earnshaw's theorem** a charge subject only to the electrostatic forces of fixed charges has no position of equilibrium that is stable in every direction, so the cube and the square are ruled out. The equilibrium of three free collinear charges is unstable too. In the vertical tube the small sphere can move only along the tube, because the walls exert the constraint forces. If the sphere moves up, the repulsion decreases and the weight brings it back down; if it moves down, the repulsion increases and brings it back up. Therefore the equilibrium in the tube is **stable**.
