A positive test charge lies between a positive source and a negative source, along their line. The sources are fixed. Given the magnitude of the repulsion from the positive source $F_{1} = 5\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the magnitude of the attraction from the negative source $F_{2} = 12\,\mathrm{mN}$, what is the magnitude of the total force $F$?

- $34\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $17\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $7\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $13\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The positive source repels the test charge toward the negative one. The attraction also points toward the negative source. In the indicated units, $F = F_{1} + F_{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $17\,\mathrm{mN}$.
