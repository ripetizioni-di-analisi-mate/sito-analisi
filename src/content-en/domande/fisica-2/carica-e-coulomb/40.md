Two positive charges are fixed on the same straight line. Where can a finite point of zero field be located?

- Only beyond the larger charge
- Only beyond the smaller charge
- At every point on the line
- Between the two charges

## Explanation

Outside the segment, the two fields point in the same direction. Inside it, they point in opposite directions. There is therefore a point between the charges where their magnitudes are equal.
