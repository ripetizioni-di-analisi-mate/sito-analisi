Two point charges remain in a homogeneous medium. The force they would exert in a vacuum at the same separation would have magnitude $F_0$. Given the magnitude of the force in a vacuum $F_{0} = 18\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the magnitude of the force in the medium $F = 6\,\mathrm{mN}$, what is the relative permittivity of the medium $\epsilon$?

- $2$
- $6$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The permittivity changes from $\varepsilon_0$ to $\varepsilon\varepsilon_0$. The force magnitude is inversely proportional to the permittivity. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{F_{0}}{\epsilon}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\epsilon = \dfrac{F_{0}}{F}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $3$.
