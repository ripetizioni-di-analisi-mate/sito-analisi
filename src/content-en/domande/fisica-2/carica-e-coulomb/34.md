A charged rod attracts an initially neutral piece of paper. Which explanation is correct?

- The paper necessarily acquires the same charge as the rod
- The charge in the paper becomes polarized
- The rod creates new net charge in the paper
- Gravity reverses direction

## Explanation

The rod's field slightly displaces the bound charges in the paper. On average, the charges of opposite sign remain closer to the rod. The resultant can therefore be attractive even when the net charge is zero.
