The charges $+1\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $-1\,\mu\mathrm C$ are fixed at $x=-0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ and $x=+0{,}10\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the force on $+1\,\mu\mathrm C$ at the origin? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $1{,}8\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}9\,\mathrm N$
- $3{,}6\,\mathrm N$
- $7{,}2\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Each force equals $\dfrac{kq^2}{r^2}=0{,}90\,\mathrm N$. Both point toward the negative charge, so their magnitudes add. The result is $1{,}8\,\mathrm N$.
