Two positive charges $q$ and $9q$ are separated by $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far from the charge $9q$ is the point between them where the field is zero?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the fields to cancel, the ratio of the distances must equal the square root of the ratio of the charges. The distances are therefore in the ratio $1:3$ and add up to $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. The result is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
