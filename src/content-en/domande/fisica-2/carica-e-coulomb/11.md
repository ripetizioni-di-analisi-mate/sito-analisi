Two charges exert a force of $12\,\mathrm N$ in a vacuum. What force do they exert at the same separation in a homogeneous dielectric with relative permittivity $\varepsilon_r=3$?

- $2\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

In the homogeneous dielectric model, the Coulomb constant is divided by $\varepsilon_r$. Therefore, the force magnitude is also divided by three. The result is $4\,\mathrm N$.
