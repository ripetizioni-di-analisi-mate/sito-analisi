An isolated system of three bodies has total charge $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$. Two bodies have charges of $+9\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-7\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is the charge of the third?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $3\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $12\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The sum of the three charges must equal the total charge. Therefore, $q_3=5-9+7$ in nanocoulombs. The result is $3\,\mathrm{nC}$.
