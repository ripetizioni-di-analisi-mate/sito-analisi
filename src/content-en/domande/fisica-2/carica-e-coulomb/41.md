Two equal and opposite charges are fixed at two distinct points. What happens at the midpoint?

- The field is nonzero
- The field is zero by symmetry
- Both forces on a positive charge are repulsive
- The force on any charge is zero

## Explanation

The field of the positive charge points away from it. The field of the negative charge points toward it. At the midpoint, the two contributions point in the same direction and add.
