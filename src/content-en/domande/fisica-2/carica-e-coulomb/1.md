A body receives $5{,}0\cdot10^{10}$ electrons. What charge does it acquire, in nC? Use $e=1{,}6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$.

- $-8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

Each electron carries a charge of $-e$. Therefore, the acquired charge is $q=-Ne$. The result is $-8\,\mathrm{nC}$.
