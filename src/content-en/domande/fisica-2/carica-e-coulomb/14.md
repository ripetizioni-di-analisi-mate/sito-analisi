Two perpendicular electric forces have magnitudes of $3\,\mathrm N$ and $4\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $1\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$
- $7\,\mathrm N$
- $25\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular. Therefore, the magnitude of their sum is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\,\mathrm N$. The result is $5\,\mathrm N$.
