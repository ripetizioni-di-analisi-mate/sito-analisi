Four equal point charges $q > 0$ are fixed in vacuum on a semicircle of center $O$ and radius $R$, at the points corresponding to the angles $0^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $120^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ measured from the diameter. What is the magnitude of the force on the charge $q_0 > 0$ placed at $O$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="2*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,3.1416" linee="-2,0 2,0" punti="2,0,pieno;1,1.732,pieno;-1,1.732,pieno;-2,0,pieno;0,0,pieno" etichette="2.25,0.2,q;1.2,1.95,q;-1.2,1.95,q;-2.25,0.2,q;0,-0.3,O;1,-0.25,R" xmin=-2.8 xmax=2.8 ymin=-0.6 ymax=2.4 legenda=0 titolo="Four equal charges $q$ on the semicircle of radius $R$ and the charge $q_0$ at the center $O$"}

- $\sqrt{3}\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{R^2}$
- $4\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{R^2}$
- $\left(1 + \sqrt{3}\right)\dfrac{kqq_0}{R^2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

All the charges are at distance $R$ from $O$, so each exerts on $q_0$ a force of magnitude $\dfrac{kqq_0}{R^2}$, directed from the charge towards $O$ because the charges repel each other. Choose the $x$ axis along the diameter and the $y$ axis towards the semicircle; the charge at the position of angle $\alpha$ gives the contribution $-\dfrac{kqq_0}{R^2}\left(\cos\alpha,\ \sin\alpha\right)$. The $x$ components cancel, because $\cos 0^\circ + \cos 60^\circ + \cos 120^\circ + \cos 180^\circ = 0$. The $y$ components add up, and $\sin 0^\circ + \sin 60^\circ + \sin 120^\circ + \sin 180^\circ = \sqrt{3}$, therefore the force has magnitude $\sqrt{3}\,\dfrac{kqq_0}{R^2}$ and is perpendicular to the diameter, directed away from the semicircle.
