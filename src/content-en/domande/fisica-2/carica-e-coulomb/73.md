The source charges are both positive. A positive test charge lies outside the segment joining them and on their line. Given the magnitude of the force from the first source $F_{1} = 7\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the magnitude of the total force $F = 10\,\mathrm{mN}$, what is the magnitude of the force from the second source $F_{2}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The two repulsive forces point in the same direction because the test charge is outside the segment. The magnitudes add. In the indicated units, $F = F_{1} + F_{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F_{2} = F - F_{1}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $3\,\mathrm{mN}$.
