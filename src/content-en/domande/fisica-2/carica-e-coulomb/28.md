A small sphere of mass $2\,\mathrm g$ is subject to a horizontal electric force of $0{,}006\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnitude of its horizontal acceleration?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The mass in SI units is $0{,}002\,\mathrm{kg}$. Newton's second law gives $a_x=\dfrac{F_x}{m}$. The result is $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
