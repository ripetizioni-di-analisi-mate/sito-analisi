A negative charge is placed at a point where the electric field points to the right. In which direction does the electric force act?

- To the right
- Always upward
- The force is always zero
- To the left

## Explanation

The force is $\vec F=q\vec E$. A coefficient $q<0$ reverses the vector's direction. Therefore, the force points to the left.
