The source charges are both positive. A positive test charge lies outside the segment joining them and on their line. Initially, the given quantities are the magnitude of the force from the first source $F_{1} = 7\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the magnitude of the force from the second source $F_{2} = 3\,\mathrm{mN}$. In the second configuration, we change the magnitude of the force from the second source to $7\,\mathrm{mN}$, with all other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $F$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{7}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7 \cdot \sqrt{29}}{29}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$

## Explanation

The two repulsive forces point in the same direction because the test charge is outside the segment. The magnitudes add. Applying $F = F_{1} + F_{2}$ gives $10\,\mathrm{mN}$ before the change and $14\,\mathrm{mN}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{7}{5}$.
