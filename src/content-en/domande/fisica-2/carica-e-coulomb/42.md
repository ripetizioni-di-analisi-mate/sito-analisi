A charged conducting sphere touches a second conducting sphere of a different radius. Which property characterizes equilibrium during contact?

- The spheres always have the same charge
- The spheres have the same potential
- The spheres always have the same surface charge density
- The total charge becomes zero

## Explanation

Contact allows charges to move between the two spheres. Transfer stops when a single potential is reached. For different radii, this does not require equal charges.
