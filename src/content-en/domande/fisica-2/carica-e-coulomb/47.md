In beta decay a free neutron turns into a proton, an electron and an antineutrino. What charge does the antineutrino have?

- $+e$
- $-e$
- $+2e$
- $0$

## Explanation

The decay takes place in an isolated system, so by the **conservation of charge** the total charge before and after the decay is the same. Before the decay there is only the neutron, with charge $0$. After the decay there are the proton, with charge $+e$, and the electron, with charge $-e$, whose sum is already $0$. Therefore the antineutrino has **zero** charge.
