The initial force between two point charges is measured as $F_0$. The separation is changed by a factor of $b$, keeping the charges and medium unchanged. Given the initial force magnitude $F_{0} = 36\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the final force magnitude $F = 4\,\mathrm{mN}$, what is the ratio of final to initial separation $b$?

- $6$
- $9$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

According to Coulomb's law, the magnitude varies inversely with the square of the separation. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{F_{0}}{b^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{\sqrt{F_{0}}}{\sqrt{F}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $3$.
