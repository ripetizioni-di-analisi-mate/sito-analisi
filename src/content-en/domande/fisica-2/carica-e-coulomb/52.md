Two point charges remain in a homogeneous medium. The force they would exert in a vacuum at the same separation would have magnitude $F_0$. Given the relative permittivity of the medium $\epsilon = 3$ and the magnitude of the force in the medium $F = 6\,\mathrm{mN}$, what is the magnitude of the force in a vacuum $F_{0}$?

- $24\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The permittivity changes from $\varepsilon_0$ to $\varepsilon\varepsilon_0$. The force magnitude is inversely proportional to the permittivity. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{F_{0}}{\epsilon}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F_{0} = F \cdot \epsilon$. Using the values given in the problem gives $18\,\mathrm{mN}$.
