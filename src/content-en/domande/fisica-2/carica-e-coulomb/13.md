The charges $+q$ and $-4q$ are fixed at $x=0$ and $x=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. At what finite coordinate is the electric field zero?

- $-30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Between the charges, the fields point in the same direction. To the left of the smaller charge, the condition $\dfrac{1}{x^2}=\dfrac{4}{(30-x)^2}$, with $x<0$, gives $30-x=-2x$. The result is $-30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
