A charge $q_1$ exerts on $q_2$ a force of $0{,}80\,\mathrm N$. The magnitude of $q_2$ is five times that of $q_1$. What is the magnitude of the force exerted by $q_2$ on $q_1$?

- $0{,}8\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm N$
- $1{,}6\,\mathrm N$
- $3{,}2\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The interaction forces form an action–reaction pair. Therefore, they have the same magnitude even when the two charges are different. The result is $0{,}8\,\mathrm N$.
