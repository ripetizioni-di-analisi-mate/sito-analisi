Two isolated charges interact electrically. What is the sum of only the internal forces in the system?

- The zero vector
- A vector always pointing toward the positive charge
- A vector with magnitude equal to the sum of the magnitudes
- It depends on the ratio of the masses

## Explanation

The mutual electric forces have equal magnitudes and opposite directions. Their vector sum is zero. The result does not depend on the masses of the particles.
