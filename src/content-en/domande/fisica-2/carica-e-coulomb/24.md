Two identical small spheres initially have charges of $+6q$ and $-2q$. After contact, they are returned to their initial separation. What is the ratio of the final force magnitude to its initial magnitude?

- $3$
- $0{,}667$
- $1$
- $0{,}333$

## Explanation

After contact, each sphere has charge $2q$. The products of the charge magnitudes are initially $12q^2$ and then $4q^2$, so the ratio is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The result is $0{,}333$.
