A droplet acquires electrons in two stages. The magnitude of the elementary charge is $0{,}16\,\mathrm{aC}$. Given the number of electrons in the first stage $N_{1} = 20$ and the number of electrons in the second stage $N_{2} = 30$, what is the magnitude of the acquired charge $q$?

- $80\,\mathrm{aC}$
- $16\,\mathrm{aC}$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{aC}$
- $8\,\mathrm{aC}$

## Explanation

Each electron adds $-0{,}16\,\mathrm{aC}$. The numbers of electrons acquired in the two stages add. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{4 \cdot N_{1}}{25} + \dfrac{4 \cdot N_{2}}{25}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $8\,\mathrm{aC}$.
