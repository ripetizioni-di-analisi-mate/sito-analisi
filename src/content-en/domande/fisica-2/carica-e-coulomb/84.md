Two small spheres of equal mass hang from the same point on two identical insulating threads. The first has charge $q$ and the second charge $3q$. What relation holds, at equilibrium, between the angles $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$ that the two threads make with the vertical?

- $\theta_2 = 3\,\theta_1$
- $\theta_1 = \theta_2$
- $\theta_1 = 3\,\theta_2$
- $\tan\theta_2 = 3\tan\theta_1$

## Explanation

The electric forces that the two spheres exert on each other have the same magnitude $F = k\,\dfrac{3q^2}{r^2}$, because they are action and reaction forces. Each sphere is in equilibrium under its weight, the tension and the electric force, so the angle of its thread satisfies $\tan\theta = \dfrac{F}{mg}$. The masses are equal and the electric forces have the same magnitude, therefore $\tan\theta_1 = \tan\theta_2$ and the two angles are **equal**.
