Two opposite point charges have different magnitudes. There is a finite point on their line where the force on a test charge is zero. Where is it?

- Between the charges, closer to the one of greater magnitude
- Outside the segment, on the side of the charge of smaller magnitude
- Between the charges, at the midpoint
- Outside the segment, on the side of the charge of greater magnitude

## Explanation

Between the sources, the contributions point in the same direction. Outside, they oppose each other and, for their magnitudes to be equal, the point must be closer to the weaker source.
