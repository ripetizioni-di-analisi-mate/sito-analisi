A metal rod held in the hand and a plastic rod are rubbed with the same wool cloth. At the end the plastic rod is charged and the metal rod is uncharged. Which fact explains the difference?

- Rubbing transfers electrons only to insulators
- In the metal rubbing creates equal positive and negative charges, which cancel
- The metal and the human body are conductors, and the charge flows to the ground through the hand
- The metal gives the received electrons back to the cloth as soon as the rubbing ends

## Explanation

Rubbing transfers electrons between the cloth and both rods. Plastic is an insulator, so the charge stays in the rubbed region. The metal and the human body are conductors, therefore the charge acquired by the metal rod moves through the hand and the body to the Earth. A metal rod held by an insulating handle, on the other hand, stays charged.
