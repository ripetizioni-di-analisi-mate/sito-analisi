Two equal charges separated by $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$ repel each other with a force of $0{,}40\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnitude of each charge, in microcoulombs? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $1\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $4\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $2\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm C$

## Explanation

For two equal charges, $F=\dfrac{kq^2}{r^2}$. Therefore, $|q|=r\sqrt{\dfrac{F}{k}}$. The result is $2\,\mu\mathrm C$.
