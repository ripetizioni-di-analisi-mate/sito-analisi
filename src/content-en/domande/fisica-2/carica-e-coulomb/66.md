A test charge experiences two mutually perpendicular Coulomb forces, one horizontal and one vertical. Given the magnitude of the horizontal force $F_{x} = 9\,\mathrm{\mu N}$ and the magnitude of the vertical force $F_{y} = 12\,\mathrm{\mu N}$, what is the magnitude of the resultant force $F$?

- $3\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $21\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

Superposition adds the forces as vectors. The two components are perpendicular, so the Pythagorean theorem applies. In the indicated units, $F = \sqrt{F_{x}^{2} + F_{y}^{2}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $15\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
