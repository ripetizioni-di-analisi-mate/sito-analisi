Two electric forces of equal magnitude $5\,\mathrm N$ form an angle of $120^\circ$. What is the magnitude of their sum?

- $2{,}5\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

For the sum of two vectors, $F^2=F_1^2+F_2^2+2F_1F_2\cos120^\circ$ holds. Since $\cos120^\circ=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, we obtain $F^2=25\,\mathrm{N^2}$. The result is $5\,\mathrm N$.
