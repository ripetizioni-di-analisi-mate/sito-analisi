An isolated system consists of two spheres. Before the charge transfer, the total is positive and the first sphere has zero charge. After the transfer, both charges are positive. Given the total charge $q = 22\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the final charge of the second sphere $q_{2} = 16\,\mathrm{nC}$, what is the final charge of the first sphere $q_{1}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $9\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $12\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The internal transfer leaves the total charge unchanged. The second charge is the total minus the first. In the indicated units, $q_{2} = q - q_{1}$. Solving for the unknown gives $q_{1} = q - q_{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $6\,\mathrm{nC}$.
