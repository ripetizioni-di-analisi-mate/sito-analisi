A body has a charge of $+3{,}2\,\mathrm{nC}$. How many electrons is it missing relative to neutrality? Use $e=1{,}6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$.

- $1\cdot 10^{10}$
- $2\cdot 10^{10}$
- $4\cdot 10^{10}$
- $8\cdot 10^{10}$

## Explanation

The positive charge corresponds to a deficit of electrons. Their number is therefore $N=\dfrac{q}{e}$. The result is $2\cdot 10^{10}$.
