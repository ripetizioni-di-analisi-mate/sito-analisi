Two point charges remain in a homogeneous medium. The force they would exert in a vacuum at the same separation would have magnitude $F_0$. Initially, the given quantities are the magnitude of the force in a vacuum $F_{0} = 18\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the relative permittivity of the medium $\epsilon = 3$. In the second configuration, we change the magnitude of the force in a vacuum to $24\,\mathrm{mN}$ and the relative permittivity of the medium to $2$, with all other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $F$?

- $6\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The permittivity changes from $\varepsilon_0$ to $\varepsilon\varepsilon_0$. The force magnitude is inversely proportional to the permittivity. Applying $F = \dfrac{F_{0}}{\epsilon}$ gives $6\,\mathrm{mN}$ before the change and $12\,\mathrm{mN}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $6\,\mathrm{mN}$.
