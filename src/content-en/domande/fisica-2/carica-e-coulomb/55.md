A small sphere $A$ is repelled by a glass rod rubbed with silk, which has a positive charge. The same sphere $A$ is attracted by a second small sphere $B$. What can be concluded about the charge of $B$?

- $B$ is certainly negative
- $B$ is certainly positive
- $B$ is certainly neutral
- $B$ is negative or neutral

## Explanation

Repulsion occurs only between bodies charged with the same sign, so $A$ is positive. A positive body attracts a negative body, and it also attracts a neutral body, because it polarizes it. Therefore the attraction between $A$ and $B$ only rules out that $B$ is positive, and $B$ is **negative or neutral**.
