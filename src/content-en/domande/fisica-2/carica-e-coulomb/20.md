A rod with charge $-7\,\mathrm{nC}$ transfers electrons to a neutral body and is left with $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$. What charge does the body acquire?

- $5\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $9\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The system conserves its total charge, initially equal to $-7\,\mathrm{nC}$. The charge of the second body is therefore $-7-(-2)\,\mathrm{nC}$. The result is $-5\,\mathrm{nC}$.
