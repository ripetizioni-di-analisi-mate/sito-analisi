At a separation of $r$, two charges exert a force of $8\,\mathrm N$. At what separation, expressed as a multiple of $r$, does the force equal $2\,\mathrm N$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The force magnitude must become one quarter of its initial value. Since $F$ varies inversely with the square of the separation, the separation must double. The result is $2$.
