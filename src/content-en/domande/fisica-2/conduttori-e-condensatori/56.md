Two ideal capacitors are connected in parallel to a $20\,\mathrm V$ source. Given the first capacitance $C_{1} = 3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the second capacitance $C_{2} = 7\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, what is the total charge supplied to the pair of positive plates $q$?

- $400\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $42\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $100\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $200\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

In parallel, both have the same voltage across them. The charges on the two positive plates add. In the indicated units, $q = 20 \cdot C_{1} + 20 \cdot C_{2}$. Using the given values, we obtain $200\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
