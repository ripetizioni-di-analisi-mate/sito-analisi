The field in vacuum between two plates is $E=100\,\mathrm{V/m}$. What is the ratio of the energy density to $\varepsilon_0$?

- $5000\,\mathrm{V^2/m^2}$
- $2500\,\mathrm{V^2/m^2}$
- $1\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{V^2/m^2}$
- $2\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{V^2/m^2}$

## Explanation

The electric energy density is $u=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0E^2}{2}$. Dividing by $\varepsilon_0$ leaves $\dfrac{E^2}{2}$. The result is $5000\,\mathrm{V^2/m^2}$.
