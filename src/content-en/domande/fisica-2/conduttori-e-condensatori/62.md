An isolated capacitor has constant charge and is initially in vacuum. A linear dielectric then fills the entire space between its plates. The geometry remains fixed. Given the relative permittivity $\epsilon = 4$ and the final voltage $U_{e} = 12\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the initial voltage $U_{0}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{V}$
- $48\,\mathrm{V}$
- $60\,\mathrm{V}$
- $96\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

At fixed charge, the voltage is inversely proportional to the capacitance. The dielectric multiplies the capacitance by the relative permittivity. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{U_{0}}{\epsilon}$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{0} = U_{e} \cdot \epsilon$. Using the given values, we obtain $48\,\mathrm{V}$.
