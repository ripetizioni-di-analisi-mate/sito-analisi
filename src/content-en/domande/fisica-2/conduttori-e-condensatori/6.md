The capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor is $4\,\mathrm{nF}$. The overlap area triples, while the separation remains unchanged. What is the new capacitance?

- $6\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $12\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $24\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $48\,\mathrm{nF}$

## Explanation

The capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor is proportional to the overlap area. Tripling this area also triples the capacitance. The result is $12\,\mathrm{nF}$.
