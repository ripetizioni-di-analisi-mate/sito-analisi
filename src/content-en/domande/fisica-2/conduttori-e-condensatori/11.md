Two capacitors of $2$ and $4\,\mu\mathrm F$ are connected in series and hold $12\,\mu\mathrm C$. What is the voltage across the $2\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor?

- $3\,\mathrm V$
- $12\,\mathrm V$
- $6\,\mathrm V$
- $24\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

In series, both capacitors carry the same charge magnitude. The required voltage is therefore $V_1=\dfrac{Q}{C_1}$. The result is $6\,\mathrm V$.
