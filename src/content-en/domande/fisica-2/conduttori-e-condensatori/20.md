A voltage of $60\,\mathrm V$ is applied across a parallel-plate capacitor whose plates are separated by $3\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the magnitude of the uniform field between them?

- $1\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $4\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $8\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $2\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

For a parallel-plate capacitor, neglecting edge effects, $E=\dfrac{V}{d}$. The separation must be converted to meters. The result is $2\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
