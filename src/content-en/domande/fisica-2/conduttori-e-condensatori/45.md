An ideal capacitor in vacuum has charges $+Q$ and $-Q$. Which value is used in $C=\dfrac{Q}{V}$?

- The magnitude of the charge on a single plate
- The algebraic sum of the two charges
- Twice the positive charge
- The difference between the masses of the plates

## Explanation

Capacitance relates the voltage to the magnitude of the separated charge. The plates carry opposite charges of equal magnitude. Thus $Q$ is used, without adding them algebraically.
