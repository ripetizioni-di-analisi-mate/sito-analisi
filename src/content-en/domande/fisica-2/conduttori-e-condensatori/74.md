## Explanation

Apply the potential difference $\Delta V$ between $A$ and $B$ to the uncharged network, and suppose for a moment that $C_5$ is absent. In the upper branch $C_1$ and $C_2$ are in series and carry the same charge, so their voltages are in the inverse ratio of the capacitances, therefore $C_1$ has $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\Delta V$. For the same reason, in the lower branch $C_3$ has $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\Delta V$.

The two middle nodes therefore have the same potential, so when $C_5$ is connected between them it stays uncharged and nothing changes. The network is then equivalent to two series combinations in parallel:
$$
C_{\text{eq}} = \frac{C_1 C_2}{C_1 + C_2} + \frac{C_3 C_4}{C_3 + C_4} = \left( \frac{4}{3} + 2 \right) \mu\mathrm{F} \approx 3.3\,\mu\mathrm{F} .
$$
