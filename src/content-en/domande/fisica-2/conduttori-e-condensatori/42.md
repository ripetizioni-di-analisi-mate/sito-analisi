A parallel-plate capacitor with a dielectric is maintained at constant voltage and separation. How does the average field compare with that in vacuum?

- It is always divided by $\varepsilon_r$
- It remains unchanged
- It is always multiplied by $\varepsilon_r$
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

The average field between the plates is $E=\dfrac{V}{d}$. The battery holds $V$ fixed and the separation remains fixed. The field therefore remains unchanged while the free charge increases.
