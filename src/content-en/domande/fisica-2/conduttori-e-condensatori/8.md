Two capacitors of $3$ and $6\,\mu\mathrm F$ are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent capacitance?

- $2\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $3\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $18\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $9\,\mu\mathrm F$

## Explanation

In parallel, the voltages are equal and the charges add. Thus $C_{eq}=C_1+C_2$. The result is $9\,\mu\mathrm F$.
