A capacitor remains connected to a $9\,\mathrm V$ battery while it is filled with a dielectric. What is the final voltage?

- $4{,}5\,\mathrm V$
- $18\,\mathrm V$
- $36\,\mathrm V$
- $9\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The ideal battery fixes the potential difference. The dielectric changes the capacitance and charge, while the voltage remains equal to the battery voltage. The result is $9\,\mathrm V$.
