A capacitor holds $Q=12\,\mu\mathrm C$ when the voltage is $4\,\mathrm V$. What is its capacitance?

- $3\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $1{,}5\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $6\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $12\,\mu\mathrm F$

## Explanation

Capacitance is defined by the ratio $C=\dfrac{Q}{V}$. Charge and voltage are proportional here. The result is $3\,\mu\mathrm F$.
