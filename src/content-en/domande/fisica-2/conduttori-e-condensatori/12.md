A capacitor of $4\,\mu\mathrm F$ is charged to $10\,\mathrm V$. How much energy does it store?

- $100\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $400\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $800\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $200\,\mu\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The energy is $U=\dfrac{CV^2}{2}$. The result is then expressed in microjoules. The result is $200\,\mu\mathrm J$.
