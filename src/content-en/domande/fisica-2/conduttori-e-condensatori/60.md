Two ideal capacitors are connected in parallel to a $20\,\mathrm V$ source. Initially, the given quantities are the first capacitance $C_{1} = 3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the second capacitance $C_{2} = 7\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. In the second configuration, we change the first capacitance to $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the second capacitance to $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $q$?

- $-12\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

In parallel, both have the same voltage across them. The charges on the two positive plates add. Applying $q = 20 \cdot C_{1} + 20 \cdot C_{2}$ gives $200\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ before the change and $160\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $-40\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
