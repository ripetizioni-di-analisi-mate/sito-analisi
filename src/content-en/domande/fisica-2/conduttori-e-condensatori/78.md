An ideal capacitor is charged and then isolated. Its capacitance and the magnitude of the charge on one plate are known. Given the charge magnitude $q = 12\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the stored energy $U = 12\,\mathrm{\mu J}$, what is the capacitance $C$?

- $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $9\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

The total work required to charge it is obtained by integrating $q'/C$ from zero to $q$. The result is $q^2/(2C)$. In the indicated units, $U = \dfrac{q^{2}}{2 \cdot C}$. Solving for the unknown gives $C = \dfrac{q^{2}}{2 \cdot U}$. Using the given values, we obtain $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
