A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum, with plates $0.50\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart, is connected to a $200\,\mathrm{V}$ source. Neglecting edge effects, what is the charge density on the positive plate?

- $1.8\,\mu\mathrm{C/m^2}$
- $3.5\,\mu\mathrm{C/m^2}$
- $7.1\,\mu\mathrm{C/m^2}$
- $0.35\,\mu\mathrm{C/m^2}$

## Explanation

The field between the plates is uniform and equals
$$
E = \frac{\Delta V}{d} = \frac{200}{5.0 \cdot 10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{V/m} = 4.0 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{V/m} .
$$
The charge lies on the inner face of the plate and, by **Coulomb's theorem**, the field just outside that face is $E = \dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$. Therefore
$$
\sigma = \varepsilon_0 E = 8.85 \cdot 10^{-12} \cdot 4.0 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{C/m^2} \approx 3.5 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{C/m^2} .
$$
