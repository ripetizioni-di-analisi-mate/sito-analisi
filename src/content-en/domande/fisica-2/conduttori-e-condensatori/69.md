A capacitor remains connected to an ideal source. A linear dielectric completely fills the gap, with the geometry unchanged. Initially, the given quantities are the relative permittivity $\epsilon = 3$ and the initial stored energy $U_{0} = 24\,\mathrm{\mu J}$. In the second configuration, we change the relative permittivity to $6$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

At fixed voltage, the energy is $CU_e^2/2$. It is therefore multiplied by the same factor as the capacitance. Applying $U = U_{0} \cdot \epsilon$ gives $72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ before the change and $144\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ afterward. The ratio is $2$.
