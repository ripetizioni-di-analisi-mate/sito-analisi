A capacitor remains connected to an ideal source. A linear dielectric completely fills the gap, with the geometry unchanged. Given the relative permittivity $\epsilon = 3$ and the final stored energy $U = 72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$, what is the initial stored energy $U_{0}$?

- $48\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $216\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

At fixed voltage, the energy is $CU_e^2/2$. It is therefore multiplied by the same factor as the capacitance. In the indicated units, $U = U_{0} \cdot \epsilon$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{0} = \dfrac{U}{\epsilon}$. Using the given values, we obtain $24\,\mathrm{\mu J}$.
