The uniform field in a parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum exerts pressure $p=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0E^2}{2}$. If $E$ triples, by what factor does the pressure increase?

- $4{,}5$
- $18$
- $36$
- $9$

## Explanation

The pressure is proportional to the square of the field. The required factor is therefore $3^2$. The result is $9$.
