Two initially uncharged capacitors are connected in series. Find the equivalent capacitance seen at the terminals. Given the second capacitance $C_{2} = 3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the equivalent capacitance $C = 2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, what is the first capacitance $C_{1}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $9\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

In series, the charge magnitude is the same and the voltages add. The equivalent capacitance is the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals. In the indicated units, $C = \dfrac{C_{1} \cdot C_{2}}{C_{1} + C_{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $C_{1} = - \dfrac{C \cdot C_{2}}{C - C_{2}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
