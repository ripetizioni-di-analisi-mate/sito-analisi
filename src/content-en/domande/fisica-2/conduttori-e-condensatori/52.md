## Explanation

The potential at a point is the sum of the potentials produced by the two shells, each with its charge distributed uniformly.

On its own surface the inner shell has the potential of a point charge $q_1$ placed at the center, that is $\dfrac{kq_1}{R_1}$. The field of the outer shell is zero at the points inside that shell, so at all those points its potential is constant and equal to that of its surface, $\dfrac{kq_2}{R_2}$.

Therefore the inner shell has potential
$$
V_1 = k \left( \frac{q_1}{R_1} + \frac{q_2}{R_2} \right) = 8.99 \cdot 10^{9} \left( \frac{2.0 \cdot 10^{-9}}{0.10} + \frac{3.0 \cdot 10^{-9}}{0.20} \right) \mathrm{V} \approx 315\,\mathrm{V} .
$$
