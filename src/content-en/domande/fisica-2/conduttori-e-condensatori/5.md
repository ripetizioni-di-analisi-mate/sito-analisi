A $6\,\mathrm{nF}$ parallel-plate capacitor is modified by doubling the separation between its plates, with the area and medium unchanged. What is its new capacitance?

- $3\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $6\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $12\,\mathrm{nF}$

## Explanation

The capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor is inversely proportional to the separation. Doubling $d$ therefore halves $C$. The result is $3\,\mathrm{nF}$.
