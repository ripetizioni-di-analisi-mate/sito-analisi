An empty cavity is completely enclosed by a conductor in equilibrium. What field do external electrostatic sources produce in the cavity?

- Equal to the external field
- Inversely proportional to the metal thickness
- Zero
- Always pointing toward the opening

## Explanation

The cavity wall is equipotential. There are no charges in the cavity. The internal electrostatic solution therefore has constant potential and zero field.
