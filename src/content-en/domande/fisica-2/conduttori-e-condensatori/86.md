A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum remains connected to an ideal source while the separation between its plates increases. Edge effects are neglected. Given the ratio of the new separation to the old separation $b = 3$ and the final charge on the same plate $q = 6\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, what is the initial charge on one plate $q_{0}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $18\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $54\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $36\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The source maintains a constant voltage. The capacitance is divided by $b$, and so is the charge. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{q_{0}}{b}$. Solving for the unknown gives $q_{0} = b \cdot q$. Using the given values, we obtain $18\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
