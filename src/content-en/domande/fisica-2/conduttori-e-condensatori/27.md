Two $2\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitors, charged to $10$ and $4\,\mathrm V$, are connected in parallel with matching polarities and without a battery. What is the final voltage?

- $3\,\mathrm V$
- $14\,\mathrm V$
- $7\,\mathrm V$
- $28\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The total charge is $2\cdot10+2\cdot4=28\,\mu\mathrm C$. Dividing by the total capacitance $4\,\mu\mathrm F$ gives the common potential. The result is $7\,\mathrm V$.
