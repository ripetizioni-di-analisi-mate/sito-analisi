A capacitor of capacitance $C$ is disconnected from its battery and carries charges $\pm Q$. A second, uncharged capacitor of capacitance $3C$ is connected in parallel. What fraction of the original electrostatic energy remains in the two capacitors after the transient?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac14$
- $1$

## Explanation

The total charge magnitude on each connected group of plates remains $Q$, while the equivalent capacitance becomes $4C$. Using $U=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C_{\rm eq}}$ gives one quarter of the initial energy.
