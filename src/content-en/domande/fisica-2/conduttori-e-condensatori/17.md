An isolated capacitor has initial energy $18\,\mathrm{mJ}$. What final energy is obtained when a dielectric that triples the capacitance is inserted?

- $6\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $24\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

The charge remains constant because the capacitor is isolated. From the formula $U=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C}$, tripling the capacitance divides the energy by three. The result is $6\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
