A charged capacitor and an uncharged capacitor are connected in parallel without a battery. Why can the final electrostatic energy be lower?

- The total charge is destroyed
- The equivalent capacitance becomes negative
- Some energy is transferred to heat and radiation during the transient
- The principle of conservation of energy does not hold

## Explanation

The connection generates a current transient. Resistances and radiation can receive energy from the field. Total energy is conserved, while the final electrostatic energy decreases.
