A capacitor of $6\,\mu\mathrm F$ charged to $12\,\mathrm V$ is disconnected and connected in parallel, with matching polarities, to an identical uncharged capacitor. What is the final voltage?

- $3\,\mathrm V$
- $6\,\mathrm V$
- $12\,\mathrm V$
- $24\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The initial total charge is $72\,\mu\mathrm C$. The final capacitance is $12\,\mu\mathrm F$, so $V_f=\dfrac{Q_{tot}}{C_{tot}}$. The result is $6\,\mathrm V$.
