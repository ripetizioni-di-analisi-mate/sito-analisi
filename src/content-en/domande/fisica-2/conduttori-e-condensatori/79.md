Two small charged spheres in vacuum repel each other with a force of $1.6\,\mathrm{N}$. The same spheres, at the same distance and with the same charges, are immersed in distilled water, with $\varepsilon_r = 80$. What force do they exert on each other?

- $128\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.020\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In the dielectric around each sphere, polarization charges of sign opposite to the charge of the sphere arrange themselves, and the field they produce reduces the field of the sphere by a factor $\varepsilon_r$. Therefore two point charges immersed in a homogeneous dielectric repel each other with a force $\varepsilon_r$ times smaller than in vacuum:
$$
F = \frac{F_0}{\varepsilon_r} = \frac{1.6}{80}\,\mathrm{N} = 0.020\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
