A capacitor remains connected to an ideal source. A linear dielectric completely fills the gap, with the geometry unchanged. Initially, the given quantities are the relative permittivity $\epsilon = 3$ and the initial stored energy $U_{0} = 24\,\mathrm{\mu J}$. In the second configuration, we change the relative permittivity to $2$ and the initial stored energy to $36\,\mathrm{\mu J}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $U$?

- $72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $-72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

At fixed voltage, the energy is $CU_e^2/2$. It is therefore multiplied by the same factor as the capacitance. Applying $U = U_{0} \cdot \epsilon$ gives $72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ before the change and $72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $0\,\mathrm{\mu J}$.
