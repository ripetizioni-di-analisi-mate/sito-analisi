A capacitor connected to a battery stores $4\,\mathrm{mJ}$. A dielectric doubles its capacitance at constant voltage. What is the final energy?

- $4\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $32\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

The voltage remains fixed by the battery. The formula $U=\dfrac{CV^2}{2}$ shows that the energy is proportional to the capacitance. The result is $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
