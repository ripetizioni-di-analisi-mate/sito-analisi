A capacitor of $6\,\mu\mathrm F$ is in series with two capacitors of $3\,\mu\mathrm F$ each connected in parallel. A voltage of $12\,\mathrm V$ is applied to the two terminals of the initially uncharged network. What is the magnitude of the charge on each $3\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor?

- $36\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $18\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $12\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $72\,\mu\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The two parallel capacitors are equivalent to $6\,\mu\mathrm F$. The two equivalent elements in series therefore have equal capacitances and share the voltage equally. Each capacitor in the parallel combination has $6\,\mathrm V$ across it and therefore holds $3\cdot6\,\mu\mathrm C=18\,\mu\mathrm C$.
