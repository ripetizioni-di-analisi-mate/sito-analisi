A capacitor of $5\,\mu\mathrm F$ is connected to $12\,\mathrm V$. What is the magnitude of the charge on each plate?

- $30\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $60\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $120\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $240\,\mu\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The constitutive relation is $Q=CV$. This value refers to a single plate, while the other carries the opposite charge. The result is $60\,\mu\mathrm C$.
