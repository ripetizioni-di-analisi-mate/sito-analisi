An isolated conducting sphere with charge $4\,\mathrm{nC}$ has radius $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is its potential relative to infinity? Take $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $90\,\mathrm V$
- $360\,\mathrm V$
- $180\,\mathrm V$
- $720\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The surface potential is $V=\dfrac{kQ}{R}$. The entire conductor in equilibrium has the same potential. The result is $180\,\mathrm V$.
