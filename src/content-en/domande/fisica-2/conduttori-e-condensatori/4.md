A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum has area $A=0{,}020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and separation $d=1\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is $\dfrac{C}{\varepsilon_0}$?

- $10\,\mathrm m$
- $40\,\mathrm m$
- $80\,\mathrm m$
- $20\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Neglecting edge effects, $C=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0A}{d}$. The separation must be expressed in meters. The result is $20\,\mathrm m$.
