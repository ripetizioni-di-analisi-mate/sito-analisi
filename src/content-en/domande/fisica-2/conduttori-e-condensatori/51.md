Two initially uncharged capacitors are connected in series. Find the equivalent capacitance seen at the terminals. Given the first capacitance $C_{1} = 6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the second capacitance $C_{2} = 3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, what is the equivalent capacitance $C$?

- $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $9\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

In series, the charge magnitude is the same and the voltages add. The equivalent capacitance is the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals. In the indicated units, $C = \dfrac{C_{1} \cdot C_{2}}{C_{1} + C_{2}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
