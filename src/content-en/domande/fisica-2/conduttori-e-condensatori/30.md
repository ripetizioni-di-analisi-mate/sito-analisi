A spherical capacitor in vacuum has radii $a=0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ and $b=0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is $\dfrac{C}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}$?

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The capacitance of a spherical capacitor is $C=\dfrac{4\pi\varepsilon_0ab}{b-a}$. Substitute the radii expressed in meters. The result is $0{,}2\,\mathrm m$.
