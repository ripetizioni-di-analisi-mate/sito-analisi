A capacitor of $8\,\mu\mathrm F$ holds $40\,\mu\mathrm C$. What is the potential difference?

- $2{,}5\,\mathrm V$
- $10\,\mathrm V$
- $5\,\mathrm V$
- $20\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

From $Q=CV$ we obtain $V=\dfrac{Q}{C}$. The micro prefixes in the numerator and denominator cancel. The result is $5\,\mathrm V$.
