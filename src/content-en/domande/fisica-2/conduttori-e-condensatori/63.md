An isolated capacitor has constant charge and is initially in vacuum. A linear dielectric then fills the entire space between its plates. The geometry remains fixed. Given the initial voltage $U_{0} = 48\,\mathrm{V}$ and the final voltage $U_{e} = 12\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the relative permittivity $\epsilon$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $3$
- $8$

## Explanation

At fixed charge, the voltage is inversely proportional to the capacitance. The dielectric multiplies the capacitance by the relative permittivity. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{U_{0}}{\epsilon}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\epsilon = \dfrac{U_{0}}{U_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $4$.
