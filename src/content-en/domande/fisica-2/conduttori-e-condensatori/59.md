Two ideal capacitors are connected in parallel to a $20\,\mathrm V$ source. Initially, the given quantities are the first capacitance $C_{1} = 3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the second capacitance $C_{2} = 7\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. In the second configuration, we change the second capacitance to $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $q$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{8}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

In parallel, both have the same voltage across them. The charges on the two positive plates add. Applying $q = 20 \cdot C_{1} + 20 \cdot C_{2}$ gives $200\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ before the change and $300\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
