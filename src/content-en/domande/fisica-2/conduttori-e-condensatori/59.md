Let $a$ and $\ell$ be two lengths. Which of the following expressions is measured in farads?

- $\dfrac{\varepsilon_0\,a}{\ell^2}$
- $\varepsilon_0\,a\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{\varepsilon_0\,\ell^2}{a}$
- $\dfrac{\varepsilon_0}{a}$

## Explanation

The capacitance of the parallel-plate capacitor is $C = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$, so $\varepsilon_0 = \dfrac{C\,d}{A}$ is measured in $\dfrac{\mathrm{F \cdot m}}{\mathrm{m^2}} = \mathrm{F/m}$.

Therefore an expression is measured in farads when $\varepsilon_0$ is multiplied by a single length. In $\dfrac{\varepsilon_0\,\ell^2}{a}$ the lengths give $\dfrac{\mathrm{m^2}}{\mathrm{m}} = \mathrm{m}$, so the expression is measured in $\mathrm{F/m} \cdot \mathrm{m} = \mathrm{F}$.

The other expressions are measured in $\mathrm{F/m^2}$, in $\mathrm{F \cdot m}$ and again in $\mathrm{F/m^2}$.
