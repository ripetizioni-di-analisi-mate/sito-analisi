Two initially uncharged capacitors are connected in series. Find the equivalent capacitance seen at the terminals. Initially, the given quantities are the first capacitance $C_{1} = 6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the second capacitance $C_{2} = 3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. In the second configuration, we change the second capacitance to $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $C$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

In series, the charge magnitude is the same and the voltages add. The equivalent capacitance is the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals. Applying $C = \dfrac{C_{1} \cdot C_{2}}{C_{1} + C_{2}}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ before the change and $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ afterward. The ratio is $2$.
