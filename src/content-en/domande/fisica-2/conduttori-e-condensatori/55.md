Two initially uncharged capacitors are connected in series. Find the equivalent capacitance seen at the terminals. Initially, the given quantities are the first capacitance $C_{1} = 6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the second capacitance $C_{2} = 3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. In the second configuration, we change the first capacitance to $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the second capacitance to $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $C$?

- $1\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $0\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $7\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

In series, the charge magnitude is the same and the voltages add. The equivalent capacitance is the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals. Applying $C = \dfrac{C_{1} \cdot C_{2}}{C_{1} + C_{2}}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ before the change and $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $1\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
