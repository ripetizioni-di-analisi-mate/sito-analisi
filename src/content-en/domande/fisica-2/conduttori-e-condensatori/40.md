Two initially neutral capacitors are charged in series without adding charges to the isolated intermediate node. Which relation holds?

- They always have equal voltages
- They always have equal energies
- The charge on the larger one is always twice as large
- They carry charges of equal magnitude

## Explanation

The intermediate node retains zero total charge. Its two plates acquire opposite charges of equal magnitude. The two capacitors consequently carry the same charge magnitude.
