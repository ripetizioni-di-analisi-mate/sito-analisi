A conductor has a cavity containing a point charge $+6\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is the total charge induced on the inner surface?

- $6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $12\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

A Gaussian surface entirely within the metal has zero field. It must therefore enclose zero total charge, requiring $Q_{int}=-q$. The result is $-6\,\mathrm{nC}$.
