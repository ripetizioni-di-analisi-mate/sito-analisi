An isolated conducting sphere has radius $0{,}18\,\mathrm m$. What is its capacitance in picofarads? Take $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $10\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $20\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $40\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $80\,\mathrm{pF}$

## Explanation

For an isolated sphere, with infinity as the reference, $C=4\pi\varepsilon_0R=\dfrac{R}{k}$. The result must be converted to picofarads. The result is $20\,\mathrm{pF}$.
