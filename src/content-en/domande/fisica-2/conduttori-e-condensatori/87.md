In a parallel-plate capacitor a dielectric with $\varepsilon_r = 5.0$ fills the whole thickness over one quarter of the plate area, and the rest is empty. The capacitor carries the charge $Q$. Neglecting edge effects, what fraction of $Q$ lies on the part of the plates in contact with the dielectric?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The two parts of the capacitor share the plates, so they have the same potential difference; therefore they behave as two capacitors in parallel, and each carries a charge proportional to its own capacitance.

The part with the dielectric has capacitance $\varepsilon_r\,\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{4d} = \dfrac{5}{4}\,\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$ and the empty part has capacitance $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$. The fraction of charge on the part with the dielectric is therefore
$$
\frac{\dfrac{5}{4}}{\dfrac{5}{4} + \dfrac{3}{4}} = \frac{5}{8} .
$$
