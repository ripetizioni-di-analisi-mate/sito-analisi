A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum remains connected to an ideal source while the separation between its plates increases. Edge effects are neglected. Given the initial charge on one plate $q_{0} = 18\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the final charge on the same plate $q = 6\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, what is the ratio of the new separation to the old separation $b$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The source maintains a constant voltage. The capacitance is divided by $b$, and so is the charge. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{q_{0}}{b}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{q_{0}}{q}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3$.
