How does the equivalent capacitance of two positive capacitances in series compare with each individual capacitance?

- It is greater than both
- It is less than both
- It is always the arithmetic mean
- It equals the larger capacitance

## Explanation

In series, the reciprocals add. The reciprocal of the equivalent capacitance is greater than either individual reciprocal. The equivalent capacitance is therefore less than both.
