A capacitor of $2\,\mu\mathrm F$ holds $16\,\mu\mathrm C$. What is its energy?

- $64\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $32\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $128\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $256\,\mu\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The energy as a function of charge is $U=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C}$. The charge must be squared. The result is $64\,\mu\mathrm J$.
