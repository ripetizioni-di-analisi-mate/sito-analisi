Two capacitors of $3$ and $6\,\mu\mathrm F$ are connected in series. What is their equivalent capacitance?

- $2\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $3\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $9\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $18\,\mu\mathrm F$

## Explanation

In series, the charge magnitudes are equal and the voltages add. Thus $C_{eq}=\dfrac{C_1C_2}{C_1+C_2}$. The result is $2\,\mu\mathrm F$.
