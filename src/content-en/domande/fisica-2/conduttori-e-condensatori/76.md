An ideal capacitor is charged and then isolated. Its capacitance and the magnitude of the charge on one plate are known. Given the charge magnitude $q = 12\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the capacitance $C = 6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, what is the stored energy $U$?

- $6\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The total work required to charge it is obtained by integrating $q'/C$ from zero to $q$. The result is $q^2/(2C)$. In the indicated units, $U = \dfrac{q^{2}}{2 \cdot C}$. Using the given values, we obtain $12\,\mathrm{\mu J}$.
