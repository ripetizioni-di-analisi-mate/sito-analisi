An isolated capacitor has constant charge and is initially in vacuum. A linear dielectric then fills the entire space between its plates. The geometry remains fixed. Given the initial voltage $U_{0} = 48\,\mathrm{V}$ and the relative permittivity $\epsilon = 4$, what is the final voltage $U_{e}$?

- $192\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\dfrac{48}{5}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $24\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

At fixed charge, the voltage is inversely proportional to the capacitance. The dielectric multiplies the capacitance by the relative permittivity. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{U_{0}}{\epsilon}$. Using the given values, we obtain $12\,\mathrm{V}$.
