A capacitor remains connected to an ideal source. A linear dielectric completely fills the gap, with the geometry unchanged. Given the initial stored energy $U_{0} = 24\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ and the final stored energy $U = 72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$, what is the relative permittivity $\epsilon$?

- $3$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

At fixed voltage, the energy is $CU_e^2/2$. It is therefore multiplied by the same factor as the capacitance. In the indicated units, $U = U_{0} \cdot \epsilon$. Solving for the unknown gives $\epsilon = \dfrac{U}{U_{0}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3$.
