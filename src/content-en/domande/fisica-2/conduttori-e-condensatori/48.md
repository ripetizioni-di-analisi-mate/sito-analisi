A parallel-plate capacitor is connected to an ideal source that fixes the potential difference. Edge effects are neglected. Given the capacitance $C = 8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the magnitude of the charge on each plate $q = 96\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, what is the voltage across it $U_{e}$?

- $24\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $18\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The definition of capacitance is $C=q/U_e$. The product of microfarads and volts gives microcoulombs. In the indicated units, $q = C \cdot U_{e}$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{e} = \dfrac{q}{C}$. Using the given values, we obtain $12\,\mathrm{V}$.
