Two identical parallel-plate capacitors are charged. The first remains connected to an ideal source, while the second is isolated. The separation between the plates of both is doubled, neglecting edge effects. What happens?

- The charge on both is halved
- The charge on the first is halved and the voltage across the second doubles
- The voltage across both doubles
- The voltage across the first doubles and the charge on the second is halved

## Explanation

The capacitance of both is halved. In the first, the source imposes the voltage, so the charge is halved. In the second, the charge remains fixed, so the voltage doubles.
