In a charged conductor in equilibrium with no charges in its cavities, where is the excess charge located?

- On the outer surface
- Uniformly throughout the volume
- Only at the geometric center
- In a single internal point charge

## Explanation

The field within the metal is zero. Gauss's law rules out a net volume charge density in equilibrium. In the absence of charges in the cavities, the excess remains on the outer surface.
