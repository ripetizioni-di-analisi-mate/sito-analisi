A $2\,\mathrm{nF}$ parallel-plate capacitor is completely filled with a dielectric with $\varepsilon_r=5$. What is its new capacitance?

- $5\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $20\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $10\,\mathrm{nF}$
- $40\,\mathrm{nF}$

## Explanation

For complete filling, $C=\varepsilon_rC_0$. The dielectric therefore increases the capacitance by a factor of five. The result is $10\,\mathrm{nF}$.
