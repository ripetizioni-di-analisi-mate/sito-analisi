A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum is isolated. The separation between the plates is increased and edge effects are negligible. Given the ratio of the new separation to the old separation $b = 3$ and the final voltage $U_{e} = 45\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the initial voltage $U_{0}$?

- $135\,\mathrm{V}$
- $5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $30\,\mathrm{V}$
- $15\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The capacitance decreases by a factor of $b$ because it is inversely proportional to the separation. The charge remains constant, so the voltage increases by a factor of $b$. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = U_{0} \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{0} = \dfrac{U_{e}}{b}$. Using the given values, we obtain $15\,\mathrm{V}$.
