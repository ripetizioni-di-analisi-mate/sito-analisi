An isolated parallel-plate capacitor has voltage $15\,\mathrm V$. The separation between the plates is doubled without changing the area or medium. What is the final voltage?

- $15\,\mathrm V$
- $60\,\mathrm V$
- $30\,\mathrm V$
- $120\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The capacitance is halved and the charge remains constant. The relation $V=\dfrac{Q}{C}$ therefore gives twice the voltage. The result is $30\,\mathrm V$.
