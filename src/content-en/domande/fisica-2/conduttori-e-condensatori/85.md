A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum remains connected to an ideal source while the separation between its plates increases. Edge effects are neglected. Given the initial charge on one plate $q_{0} = 18\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the ratio of the new separation to the old separation $b = 3$, what is the final charge on the same plate $q$?

- $54\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The source maintains a constant voltage. The capacitance is divided by $b$, and so is the charge. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{q_{0}}{b}$. Using the given values, we obtain $6\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
