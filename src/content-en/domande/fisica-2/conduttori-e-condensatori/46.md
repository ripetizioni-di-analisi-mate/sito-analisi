A parallel-plate capacitor is connected to an ideal source that fixes the potential difference. Edge effects are neglected. Given the capacitance $C = 8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the voltage across it $U_{e} = 12\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the magnitude of the charge on each plate $q$?

- $144\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $96\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $48\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $192\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The definition of capacitance is $C=q/U_e$. The product of microfarads and volts gives microcoulombs. In the indicated units, $q = C \cdot U_{e}$. Using the given values, we obtain $96\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
