Two ideal capacitors are connected in parallel to a $20\,\mathrm V$ source. Given the second capacitance $C_{2} = 7\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the total charge supplied to the pair of positive plates $q = 200\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, what is the first capacitance $C_{1}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $13\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

In parallel, both have the same voltage across them. The charges on the two positive plates add. In the indicated units, $q = 20 \cdot C_{1} + 20 \cdot C_{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $C_{1} = - C_{2} + \dfrac{q}{20}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
