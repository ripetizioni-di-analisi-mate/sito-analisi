An isolated $3\,\mathrm{nF}$ capacitor has voltage $20\,\mathrm V$. A dielectric with $\varepsilon_r=4$ completely fills it. What is the final voltage?

- $2{,}5\,\mathrm V$
- $10\,\mathrm V$
- $5\,\mathrm V$
- $20\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The capacitor is isolated, so its charge remains constant. Since the capacitance quadruples, $V=\dfrac{Q}{C}$ is divided by four. The result is $5\,\mathrm V$.
