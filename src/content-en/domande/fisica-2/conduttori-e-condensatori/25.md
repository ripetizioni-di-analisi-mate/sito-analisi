An initially neutral conductor contains a charge $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$ in a cavity, without contact. What is the total charge on the outer surface?

- $5\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $2{,}5\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $10\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $20\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The inner surface carries $-5\,\mathrm{nC}$. The conductor remains neutral overall, so $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$ appears on the outer surface. The result is $5\,\mathrm{nC}$.
