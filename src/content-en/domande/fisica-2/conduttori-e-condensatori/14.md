A capacitor of fixed capacitance has energy $5\,\mathrm{mJ}$. What energy does it store if the voltage is doubled?

- $10\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $40\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $80\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

At constant capacitance, the energy is proportional to $V^2$. Doubling the voltage therefore quadruples the energy. The result is $20\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
