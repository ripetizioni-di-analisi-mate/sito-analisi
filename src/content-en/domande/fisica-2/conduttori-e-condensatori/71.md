In a parallel-plate capacitor, half the area is filled with a linear dielectric occupying the full thickness. The other half is in vacuum. Edge effects are neglected. Given the capacitance of the entire capacitor in vacuum $C_{0} = 10\,\mathrm{pF}$ and the relative permittivity of the dielectric $\epsilon = 5$, what is the overall capacitance $C$?

- $30\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $60\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $50\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $\dfrac{50}{3}\,\mathrm{pF}$

## Explanation

The two halves have the same voltage, so they are equivalent to capacitances in parallel. Their values are $C_0/2$ and $\varepsilon C_0/2$. In the indicated units, $C = \dfrac{C_{0} \cdot \left(\epsilon + 1\right)}{2}$. Using the given values, we obtain $30\,\mathrm{pF}$.
