A capacitor remains connected to an ideal source. A linear dielectric completely fills the gap, with the geometry unchanged. Given the relative permittivity $\epsilon = 3$ and the initial stored energy $U_{0} = 24\,\mathrm{\mu J}$, what is the final stored energy $U$?

- $216\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $144\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

At fixed voltage, the energy is $CU_e^2/2$. It is therefore multiplied by the same factor as the capacitance. In the indicated units, $U = U_{0} \cdot \epsilon$. Using the given values, we obtain $72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$.
