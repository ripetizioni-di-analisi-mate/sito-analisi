A parallel-plate capacitor is connected to an ideal source that fixes the potential difference. Edge effects are neglected. Given the voltage across it $U_{e} = 12\,\mathrm{V}$ and the magnitude of the charge on each plate $q = 96\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, what is the capacitance $C$?

- $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $16\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

The definition of capacitance is $C=q/U_e$. The product of microfarads and volts gives microcoulombs. In the indicated units, $q = C \cdot U_{e}$. Solving for the unknown gives $C = \dfrac{q}{U_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
