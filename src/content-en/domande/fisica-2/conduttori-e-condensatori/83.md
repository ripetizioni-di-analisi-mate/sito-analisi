The membrane of a cell can be treated as a parallel-plate capacitor whose dielectric has thickness $7.0\,\mathrm{nm}$ and relative permittivity $\varepsilon_r = 7.0$. Neglecting the curvature of the membrane, what is its capacitance per unit area?

- $8.9\,\mu\mathrm{F/cm^2}$
- $0.13\,\mu\mathrm{F/cm^2}$
- $0.089\,\mu\mathrm{F/cm^2}$
- $0.89\,\mu\mathrm{F/cm^2}$

## Explanation

The capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor filled with a dielectric is $C = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r A}{d}$, so the capacitance per unit area is
$$
\frac{C}{A} = \frac{\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r}{d} = \frac{8.85 \cdot 10^{-12} \cdot 7.0}{7.0 \cdot 10^{-9}}\,\mathrm{F/m^2} \approx 8.9 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{F/m^2} .
$$
Since $1\,\mathrm{m^2} = 10^{4}\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and $1\,\mathrm{F} = 10^{6}\,\mu\mathrm{F}$, one has $1\,\mathrm{F/m^2} = 10^{2}\,\mu\mathrm{F/cm^2}$, therefore the capacitance per unit area is about $0.89\,\mu\mathrm{F/cm^2}$.
