A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum is isolated. The separation between the plates is increased and edge effects are negligible. Given the initial voltage $U_{0} = 15\,\mathrm{V}$ and the final voltage $U_{e} = 45\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the ratio of the new separation to the old separation $b$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The capacitance decreases by a factor of $b$ because it is inversely proportional to the separation. The charge remains constant, so the voltage increases by a factor of $b$. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = U_{0} \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{U_{e}}{U_{0}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3$.
