What qualitative effect does a sharp point have on an isolated charged conductor?

- It always imposes zero field outside
- It makes the volume charge density uniform
- It makes the capacitance negative
- It can increase the local field near the point

## Explanation

High curvature can concentrate the surface charge density. The field just outside is related to this density. It can therefore become stronger near sharp points.
