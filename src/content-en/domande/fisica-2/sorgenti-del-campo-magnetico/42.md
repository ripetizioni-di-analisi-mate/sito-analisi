Which property characterizes hysteresis in a ferromagnet?

- The magnetization is always zero
- The magnetization also depends on the history of the field
- The permeability is always exactly one
- The field cannot penetrate the material

## Explanation

In a ferromagnet, the response can follow a hysteresis loop. The same applied field can correspond to different magnetizations. The value therefore also depends on prior treatments.
