In the core of an ideal toroid, the field is $300\,\mu\mathrm T$ at radius $r$. What is it at radius $1{,}5r$, still within the core?

- $100\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $200\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $400\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $800\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

In the toroidal model, $B$ is inversely proportional to the radius. Dividing by $1{,}5$ gives the required value. The result is $200\,\mu\mathrm T$.
