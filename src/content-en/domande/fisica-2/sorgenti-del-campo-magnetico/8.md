A straight current-carrying segment is aligned with the observation point, which is outside the segment. What is its contribution to the magnetic field at that point?

- $1\,\mathrm T$
- $2\,\mathrm T$
- $4\,\mathrm T$
- $0\,\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The Biot–Savart law contains $d\vec l\times\hat r$. The two vectors are parallel throughout the given geometry, so the contribution is zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm T$.
