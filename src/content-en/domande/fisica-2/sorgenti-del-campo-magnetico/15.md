An ideal solenoid of length $0{,}50\,\mathrm m$ has $500$ turns and current $1\,\mathrm A$. What is its turn density?

- $500\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The turn density is $n=\dfrac{N}{L}$. The length must be expressed in meters. The result is $1000\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
