A circular arc of radius $0{,}1\,\mathrm m$ carries current. Its central angle is $\theta$ in radians. Consider only the contribution of the arc, with $\mu_0/(4\pi)=10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the current $I_{e} = 4\,\mathrm{A}$ and the field magnitude at the center of the arc $B = 2 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$, what is the central angle $\theta$?

- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

In the Biot–Savart law, the distance is constant and the wire element is perpendicular to the radius. Integrating over the length $R\theta$ gives $\mu_0 I\theta/(4\pi R)$. In the indicated units, $B = I_{e} \cdot \theta$. Solving for the unknown gives $\theta = \dfrac{B}{I_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
