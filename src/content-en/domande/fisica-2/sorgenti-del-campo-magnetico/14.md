An ideal solenoid has $n=1000$ turns per meter and current $2\,\mathrm A$. What is its internal field in milliteslas? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $1{,}26\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $2{,}51\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $5{,}03\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $10{,}1\,\mathrm{mT}$

## Explanation

For an ideal long solenoid, $B=\mu_0nI$. The field is uniform far from the ends. The result is $2{,}51\,\mathrm{mT}$.
