The magnetic field has zero flux through every closed surface. Which property does this describe?

- The absence of magnetic fields
- The absence of electric currents
- The absence of isolated magnetic sources in the classical model
- The presence of zero total electric charge

## Explanation

The law expresses $\nabla\cdot\vec B=0$. Magnetic lines neither begin nor end on monopoles in the classical model. The field can still be nonzero.
