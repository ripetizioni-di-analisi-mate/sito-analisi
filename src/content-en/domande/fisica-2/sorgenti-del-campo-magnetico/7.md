Concentric semicircles of radii $R$ and $2R$ are joined at their ends by radial segments to form one loop. Viewed from the front, current flows anticlockwise on the inner arc. What field does the entire loop produce at the centre?

- $\dfrac{3\mu_0 I}{8R}$ towards the observer
- $\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{8R}$ away from the observer
- $\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{8R}$ towards the observer
- Zero

## Explanation

The current elements on the radial segments are parallel to the line to the centre, so their field there is zero. The two arcs carry current in opposite senses and produce field magnitudes $\dfrac{\mu_0I}{4R}$ and $\dfrac{\mu_0I}{8R}$. The inner arc dominates.
