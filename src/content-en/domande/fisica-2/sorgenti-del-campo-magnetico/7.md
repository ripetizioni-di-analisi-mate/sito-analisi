A quarter-circle arc has radius $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$ and current $2\,\mathrm A$. What is its field at the center, in microteslas? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $0{,}785\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $3{,}14\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $1{,}57\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $6{,}28\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The field of the arc is proportional to its central angle. Here $\alpha=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{8R}$. The result is $1{,}57\,\mu\mathrm T$.
