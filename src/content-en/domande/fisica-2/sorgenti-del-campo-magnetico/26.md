A linear medium with relative permeability $\mu_r=5$ fills a solenoid. At fixed current, the field in vacuum was $2\,\mathrm{mT}$. What is the new ideal field?

- $5\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $40\,\mathrm{mT}$

## Explanation

In the linear model, $B=\mu_r\mu_0nI$. At fixed current and geometry, the field is multiplied by $\mu_r$. The result is $10\,\mathrm{mT}$.
