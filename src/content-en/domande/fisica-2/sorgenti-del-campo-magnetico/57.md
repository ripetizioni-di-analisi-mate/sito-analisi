A circular loop in vacuum carries current. Its radius is expressed in centimeters. Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the loop radius $R = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the field magnitude at the center $B = 10 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$, what is the current $I_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $6\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The Biot–Savart law integrated around the circle gives $\mu_0 I/(2R)$. The contributions are all along the normal. In the indicated units, $B = \dfrac{20 \cdot \pi \cdot I_{e}}{R}$. Solving for the unknown gives $I_{e} = \dfrac{B \cdot R}{20 \cdot \pi}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{A}$.
