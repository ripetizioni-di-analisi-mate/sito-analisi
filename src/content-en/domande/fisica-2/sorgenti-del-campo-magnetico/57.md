A small flat coil produces, at the point of its axis $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center, much farther than the radius of the coil, a magnetic field of magnitude $2.0 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T}$. What is the magnitude of the magnetic moment of the coil?

- $5.4 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$
- $2.7 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$
- $9.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$
- $1.4 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

The distance is much larger than the radius, so the coil behaves as a magnetic dipole and the field on the axis is
$$
B \approx \frac{\mu_0\,\mu}{2\pi z^3} .
$$
Solving for the magnetic moment gives
$$
\mu = \frac{2\pi z^3 B}{\mu_0} = \frac{0.30^3 \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{-7}}{2 \cdot 10^{-7}}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2} = 2.7 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2} ,
$$
where $\dfrac{\mu_0}{2\pi} = 2 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$ has been used.

The value $5.4 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$ would be obtained by writing the field with $4\pi$ instead of $2\pi$ in the denominator.
