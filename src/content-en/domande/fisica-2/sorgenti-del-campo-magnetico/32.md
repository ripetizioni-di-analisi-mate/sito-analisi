Three straight parallel wires lie in the order A, B, C along a line perpendicular to them, with $AB=BC$. All currents flow in the same direction, and the currents in A and C are $I$ and $3I$ respectively. Which way is the net force on B?

- Towards A
- Perpendicular to the plane of the three wires
- The net force is zero
- Towards C

## Explanation

The attractions on the middle wire point in opposite directions. At equal separations the force per unit length is proportional to the other wire's current, so the attraction towards C is three times that towards A.
