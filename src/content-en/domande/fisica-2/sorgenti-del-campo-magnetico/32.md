Two parallel wires carry currents in opposite directions. How do they interact?

- They attract each other
- They produce no field
- They interact only if they have a net charge
- They repel each other

## Explanation

Reversing one current reverses the direction of the force between the wires. For opposite currents, the forces push the wires apart. Their net charge can be zero.
