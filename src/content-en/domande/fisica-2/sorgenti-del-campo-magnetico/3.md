At $0{,}020\,\mathrm m$ from an infinite wire, the measured field is $40\,\mu\mathrm T$. What current flows? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $16\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The wire formula gives $I=\dfrac{2\pi rB}{\mu_0}$. The field in microteslas must be converted to teslas. The result is $4\,\mathrm A$.
