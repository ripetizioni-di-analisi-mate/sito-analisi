An ideal coaxial cable carries equal and opposite currents $+I$ and $-I$. What is the field outside both conductors?

- $0\,\mathrm T$
- $1\,\mathrm T$
- $2\,\mathrm T$
- $4\,\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The total current enclosed by an external path is zero. Cylindrical symmetry therefore allows us to infer that the external field is zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm T$.
