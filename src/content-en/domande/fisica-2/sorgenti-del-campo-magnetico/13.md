A path encloses an algebraic current $8\,\mathrm A$. What is the magnetostatic circulation in $\mu\mathrm{T\,m}$? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $10{,}1\,\mu\mathrm{T\,m}$
- $5{,}03\,\mu\mathrm{T\,m}$
- $20{,}1\,\mu\mathrm{T\,m}$
- $40{,}2\,\mu\mathrm{T\,m}$

## Explanation

Ampère's law gives $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l=\mu_0I_{conc}$. Teslas are converted to microteslas in the product of field and length. The result is $10{,}1\,\mu\mathrm{T\,m}$.
