A uniform magnetic field passes through a plane surface. The chosen normal makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. Given the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the area $A = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what is the oriented magnetic flux $\Phi$?

- $\dfrac{3}{50}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{50}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $\dfrac{3}{100}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $\dfrac{3}{25}\,\mathrm{Wb}$

## Explanation

The flux of a uniform field is $BA\cos\theta$. The angle is measured from the normal, so $\cos60^\circ$ is used here. In the indicated units, $\Phi = \dfrac{A \cdot B}{2}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{50}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
