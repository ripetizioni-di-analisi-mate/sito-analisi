An ideal solenoid has a homogeneous linear core filling its interior. The geometry and current are the same as in the measurement without a core. Given the field magnitude without the core $B_{0} = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{mT}$ and the field magnitude with the core $B = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{mT}$, what is the relative permeability of the core $\mu$?

- $\sqrt{5}$
- $10$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The magnetizing field remains fixed by the free current. The permeability is multiplied by $\mu$, so the magnitude of $\vec B$ increases by the same factor. In the indicated units, $B = B_{0} \cdot \mu$. Solving for the unknown gives $\mu = \dfrac{B}{B_{0}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $5$.
