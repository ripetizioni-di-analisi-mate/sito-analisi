A segment of wire $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ long carries the current $5.0\,\mathrm{A}$. The point $P$ is $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the segment, and the vector from the segment to $P$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the direction of the current. What is the magnitude of the contribution $dB$ of the segment to the magnetic field at $P$?

- $6.3 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $5.4 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $3.1 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $1.3 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

By the Biot-Savart law the magnitude of the contribution is
$$
dB = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\,\frac{I\,dl\,\sin\varphi}{r^2} ,
$$
where $\varphi$ is the angle between $d\vec{l}$ and the vector from the segment to $P$.

With $\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi} = 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$ and $\sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$ we get
$$
dB = 10^{-7} \cdot \frac{5.0 \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 0.50}{(4.0 \cdot 10^{-2})^2}\,\mathrm{T} \approx 3.1 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T} .
$$
The value $6.3 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T}$ would be obtained without the factor $\sin\varphi$, that is, with the segment perpendicular to the vector $\vec{r}$.
