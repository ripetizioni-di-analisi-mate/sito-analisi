The field produced by a very long straight wire is measured. The distance is in centimeters. Use $\mu_0/(2\pi)=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the current $I_{e} = 6\,\mathrm{A}$ and the distance from the wire $r = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the field magnitude $B$?

- $80\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $40\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $\dfrac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

For a long wire, $B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$. Converting the distance to meters and the result to microteslas gives the coefficient $20$. In the indicated units, $B = \dfrac{20 \cdot I_{e}}{r}$. Using the given values, we obtain $40\,\mathrm{\mu T}$.
