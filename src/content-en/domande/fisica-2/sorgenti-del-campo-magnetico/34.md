A loop carries a counterclockwise current when viewed from the front. What is the direction of the field at its center?

- Away from the observer
- Toward the observer
- Tangent to the loop
- Zero by symmetry

## Explanation

The fingers of the right hand follow the current. The thumb indicates the field along the axis. For a counterclockwise current, the field points out of the plane toward the observer.
