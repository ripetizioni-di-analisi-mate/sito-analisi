A circular loop in vacuum carries current. Its radius is expressed in centimeters. Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the loop radius $R = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the field magnitude at the center $B$?

- $20 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $5 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $10 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

The Biot–Savart law integrated around the circle gives $\mu_0 I/(2R)$. The contributions are all along the normal. In the indicated units, $B = \dfrac{20 \cdot \pi \cdot I_{e}}{R}$. Using the given values, we obtain $10 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$.
