Two long parallel wires are separated by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The current magnitudes are positive. Use $\mu_0/(2\pi)=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Initially, the given quantities are the current in the first wire $I_{1} = 8\,\mathrm A$ and the current in the second wire $I_{2} = 6\,\mathrm A$. In the second configuration, we change the current in the first wire to $12\,\mathrm A$ and the current in the second wire to $4\,\mathrm A$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $b$?

- $192\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$

## Explanation

The field of the first wire at the second is $\mu_0 I_1/(2\pi d)$. To find the force per unit length, multiply by $I_2$. Applying $b = 4 \cdot I_{1} \cdot I_{2}$ gives $192\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$ before the change and $192\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $0\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$.
