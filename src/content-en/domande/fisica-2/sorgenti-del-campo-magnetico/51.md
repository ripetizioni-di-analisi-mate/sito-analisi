A loop shaped as an equilateral triangle of side $L = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries the current $2.0\,\mathrm{A}$. What is the magnitude of the magnetic field at the center $O$ of the triangle?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-1,-0.577 1,-0.577 0,1.155 -1,-0.577" vettori="0.25,-0.577,0.75,-0.577" punti="0,0,pieno,O" segmenti="0,0,0,-0.577" etichette="0.75,0.5,L;0.15,-0.3,d;0.5,-0.8,I" xmin=-1.6 xmax=1.6 ymin=-1.1 ymax=1.5 titolo="Equilateral triangular loop of side $L$ carrying the current $I$; the center $O$ is at distance $d$ from each side"}

- $1.2 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $3.6 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $4.2 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $2.1 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The center is at distance $d = \dfrac{L}{2\sqrt{3}} \approx 2.89\,\mathrm{cm}$ from each side, and the foot of the perpendicular is the midpoint of the side. The endpoints are $\dfrac{L}{2}$ from the foot, so $\tan\theta = \dfrac{L}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{L} = \sqrt{3}$ and the endpoints are seen under the angles $\pm 60^\circ$.

By the field of a straight segment, each side produces at $O$ the field $B_1 = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi d}\,(\sin 60^\circ + \sin 60^\circ) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,\mu_0 I}{4\pi d}$, perpendicular to the plane of the triangle.

The current runs along the three sides with the same sense of rotation about $O$, so the three contributions point the same way and
$$
B = 3B_1 = \frac{9\,\mu_0 I}{2\pi L} = \frac{9 \cdot 2 \cdot 10^{-7} \cdot 2.0}{0.10}\,\mathrm{T} = 3.6 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T} .
$$
