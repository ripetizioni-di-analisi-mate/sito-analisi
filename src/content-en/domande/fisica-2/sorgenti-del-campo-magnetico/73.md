An ideal toroid in air carries current. Its winding has $200$ turns. The measurement point is in the core of the toroid. Use $\mu_0/(2\pi)=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the field magnitude along the circle $B = 600\,\mathrm{\mu T}$, what is the radius of the Amperian circle $r$?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, the circulation is $2\pi rB$. The enclosed current is $200I$. In the indicated units, $B = \dfrac{40 \cdot I_{e}}{r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $r = \dfrac{40 \cdot I_{e}}{B}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$.
