What limitation applies to the form $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l=\mu_0I_{conc}$ used without displacement current?

- It holds only for zero currents
- It is the magnetostatic form, for steady currents
- It holds only in dielectrics
- It describes every time-varying situation without corrections

## Explanation

The indicated form assumes steady currents. In time-varying situations, the Maxwell term may be needed. Displacement current then completes the law.
