A uniform magnetic field passes through a plane surface. The chosen normal makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. Given the area $A = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the oriented magnetic flux $\Phi = \dfrac{3}{50}\,\mathrm{Wb}$, what is the magnetic field magnitude $B$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{2 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{15}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The flux of a uniform field is $BA\cos\theta$. The angle is measured from the normal, so $\cos60^\circ$ is used here. In the indicated units, $\Phi = \dfrac{A \cdot B}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $B = \dfrac{2 \cdot \Phi}{A}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$.
