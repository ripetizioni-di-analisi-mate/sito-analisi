The field of an infinite wire is $60\,\mu\mathrm T$ at distance $r$. What is it at distance $3r$?

- $10\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $20\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $40\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $80\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The field of an infinite wire varies as $\dfrac{1}{r}$. Tripling the distance divides the field by three. The result is $20\,\mu\mathrm T$.
