A circular loop doubles its radius while maintaining the same current. What is the ratio of the final field at the center to the initial field?

- $2$
- $0{,}5$
- $4$
- $0{,}25$

## Explanation

The field at the center is $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2R}$. At fixed current, it is therefore inversely proportional to the radius. The result is $0{,}5$.
