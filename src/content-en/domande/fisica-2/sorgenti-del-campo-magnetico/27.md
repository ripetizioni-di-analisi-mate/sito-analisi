Two concentric loops of radii $R$ and $2R$ carry the same current in opposite directions. The field of the smaller loop at the center is $8\,\mu\mathrm T$. What is the resultant magnitude?

- $2\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $4\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $16\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The field at the center is inversely proportional to the radius. The larger loop produces $4\,\mu\mathrm T$ in the opposite direction, so the magnitudes subtract. The result is $4\,\mu\mathrm T$.
