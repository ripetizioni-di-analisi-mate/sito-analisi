Two long parallel wires carry currents of constant magnitude. In the quasistatic regime they flow in the same direction for three quarters of a cycle and in opposite directions for the remaining quarter. If the instantaneous force magnitude per unit length is $f_0$, what is the cycle-averaged force per unit length on one wire?

- $f_0$, towards the other wire.
- $f_0/2$, away from the other wire.
- $f_0/2$, towards the other wire.
- Zero.

## Explanation

The force is attractive for matching current directions and repulsive for opposite directions. Its signed average is $(3/4)f_0-(1/4)f_0=f_0/2$, attractive.
