Two parallel wires have a force per unit length of $8\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$. If only the distance is doubled, what does it become?

- $2\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $4\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $16\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The force per unit length varies as $\dfrac{1}{d}$. Doubling the distance therefore halves the magnitude. The result is $4\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$.
