A solid cylindrical wire of radius $R$ carries current uniformly across its cross section. The field at its surface is $40\,\mu\mathrm T$. What is it at $\dfrac{R}{4}$?

- $5\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $20\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $10\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $40\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The enclosed current increases as $r^2$. Ampère's law gives an internal field proportional to $r$. The result is $10\,\mu\mathrm T$.
