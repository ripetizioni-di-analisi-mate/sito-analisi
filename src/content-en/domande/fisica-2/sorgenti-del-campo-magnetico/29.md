Two infinite parallel wires with opposite currents each generate a contribution of $6\,\mu\mathrm T$ at the midpoint. What is the magnitude of the total field?

- $12\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $6\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $24\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $48\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

With opposite currents, the fields at the midpoint point in the same direction. The two magnitudes therefore add directly. The result is $12\,\mu\mathrm T$.
