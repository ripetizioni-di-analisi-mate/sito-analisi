An ideal toroid in air carries current. Its winding has $200$ turns. The measurement point is in the core of the toroid. Use $\mu_0/(2\pi)=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Initially, the given quantities are the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the radius of the Amperian circle $r = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$. In the second configuration, we change the current to $4\,\mathrm{A}$ and the radius of the Amperian circle to $\dfrac{4}{25}\,\mathrm{m}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $B$?

- $400\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $200\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $3250\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $-400\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, the circulation is $2\pi rB$. The enclosed current is $200I$. Applying $B = \dfrac{40 \cdot I_{e}}{r}$ gives $600\,\mathrm{\mu T}$ before the change and $1000\,\mathrm{\mu T}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $400\,\mathrm{\mu T}$.
