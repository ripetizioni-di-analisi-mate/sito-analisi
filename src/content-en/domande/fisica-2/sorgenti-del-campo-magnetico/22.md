A uniform field $B=0{,}40\,\mathrm T$ passes through a surface of area $0{,}20\,\mathrm{m^2}$ whose normal points in the same direction as the field. What is the flux?

- $0{,}04\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0{,}08\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0{,}16\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0{,}32\,\mathrm{Wb}$

## Explanation

The magnetic flux is $\Phi=BA\cos\theta$. For a normal pointing in the same direction, $\cos\theta=1$. The result is $0{,}08\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
