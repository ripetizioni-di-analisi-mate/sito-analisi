A straight current points out of the page. As seen by the reader, in which direction do its field lines circulate?

- Counterclockwise
- Clockwise
- Radially outward
- Radially inward

## Explanation

Point the right thumb in the direction of the current. The other fingers indicate the field direction. For a current pointing out of the page, the direction is counterclockwise.
