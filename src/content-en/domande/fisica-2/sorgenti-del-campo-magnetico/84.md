A long solenoid is filled with a homogeneous material, and the product of the number of turns per unit length and the current is $nI = 500\,\mathrm{A/m}$. Inside, far from the ends, the magnetic field is $1.0\,\mathrm{mT}$. What is the magnetization of the material?

- $8.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $1.3 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $3.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{A/m}$

## Explanation

The field inside is the sum of the field of the turns and that of the material, $B = \mu_0\,(nI + M)$.

Hence
$$
M = \frac{B}{\mu_0} - nI = \frac{1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}}{4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}}\,\mathrm{A/m} - 500\,\mathrm{A/m} \approx 796\,\mathrm{A/m} - 500\,\mathrm{A/m} \approx 3.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{A/m} .
$$
The magnetization is positive, therefore it points the same way as the field of the turns.
