In a long solenoid in air, edge effects are neglected. The solenoid carries direct current. Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the number of turns per meter $n = 500\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and the internal field magnitude $B = 400 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$, what is the current $I_{e}$?

- $1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $4\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The circulation along a line of length $l$ inside the solenoid is $Bl$. It equals $\mu_0$ times the enclosed current $nlI$. In the indicated units, $B = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot I_{e} \cdot n}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $I_{e} = \dfrac{5 \cdot B}{2 \cdot \pi \cdot n}$. Using the given values, we obtain $2\,\mathrm{A}$.
