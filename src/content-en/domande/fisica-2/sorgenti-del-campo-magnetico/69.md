In a long solenoid in air, edge effects are neglected. The solenoid carries direct current. Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Initially, the given quantities are the number of turns per meter $n = 500\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and the current $I_{e} = 2\,\mathrm{A}$. In the second configuration, we change the current to $4\,\mathrm{A}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $B$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The circulation along a line of length $l$ inside the solenoid is $Bl$. It equals $\mu_0$ times the enclosed current $nlI$. Applying $B = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot I_{e} \cdot n}{5}$ gives $400 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$ before the change and $800 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$ afterward. The ratio is $2$.
