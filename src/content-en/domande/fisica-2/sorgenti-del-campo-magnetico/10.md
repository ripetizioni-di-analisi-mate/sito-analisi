Two parallel wires have a force per unit length of $9\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$. If one current triples and the other remains unchanged, what does it become?

- $13{,}5\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $27\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $54\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $108\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The force per unit length is proportional to the product of the currents. Tripling just one current triples the magnitude. The result is $27\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$.
