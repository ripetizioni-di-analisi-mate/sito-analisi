A circular loop of radius $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ carries $5\,\mathrm A$. What is the field at its center, in microteslas? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $15{,}7\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $62{,}8\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $126\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $31{,}4\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

At the center of a loop, $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2R}$. The contributions of all the elements have the same direction, normal to the plane. The result is $31{,}4\,\mu\mathrm T$.
