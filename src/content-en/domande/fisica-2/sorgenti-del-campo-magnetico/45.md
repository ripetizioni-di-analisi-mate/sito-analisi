A loop is observed from a distance much greater than its radius. Which model describes the dominant term of its field?

- A magnetic dipole
- An isolated point magnetic charge
- A uniform field at any distance
- An infinite uniformly charged plane

## Explanation

The loop's moment is $\vec\mu=IA\hat n$. At large distances, the dominant field is the dipole field. Its magnitude decreases as the inverse cube of distance.
