An ideal toroid in air carries current. Its winding has $200$ turns. The measurement point is in the core of the toroid. Use $\mu_0/(2\pi)=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the radius of the Amperian circle $r = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the field magnitude along the circle $B$?

- $600\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $300\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, the circulation is $2\pi rB$. The enclosed current is $200I$. In the indicated units, $B = \dfrac{40 \cdot I_{e}}{r}$. Using the given values, we obtain $600\,\mathrm{\mu T}$.
