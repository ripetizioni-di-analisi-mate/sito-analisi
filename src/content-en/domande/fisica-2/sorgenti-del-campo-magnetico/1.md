An infinite wire carries $5\,\mathrm A$. What is the field at $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ from its axis, in microteslas? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $10\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $5\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $20\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $40\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

For an infinite wire, $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$. The distance is measured from the wire axis. The result is $10\,\mu\mathrm T$.
