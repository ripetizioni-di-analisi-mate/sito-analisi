A linear material has $\mu_r$ slightly less than one. How is it classified?

- Paramagnetic
- Ferromagnetic and necessarily saturated
- A perfect conductor by definition
- Diamagnetic

## Explanation

In the linear regime, the susceptibility is $\chi_m=\mu_r-1$. Here it is negative. This corresponds to diamagnetic behavior.
