- $4.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $3.5 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $2.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $2.8 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The currents are in the same direction, so each pair of wires attracts with the force per unit length
$$
\frac{F}{\ell} = \frac{\mu_0 I^2}{2\pi d} = \frac{2 \cdot 10^{-7} \cdot 10^2}{0.10}\,\mathrm{N/m} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N/m} .
$$
Two forces of this magnitude act on the chosen wire, directed toward the other two vertices, and they form an angle of $60^\circ$.

The resultant is directed along the bisector of that angle, toward the center of the triangle, and has magnitude $2 \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{-4} \cos 30^\circ\,\mathrm{N/m} = 2.0\sqrt{3} \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N/m} \approx 3.5 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
