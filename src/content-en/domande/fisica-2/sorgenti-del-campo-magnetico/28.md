Two infinite parallel wires are separated by $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$ and carry equal currents in the same direction. What is the field at the midpoint?

- $1\,\mathrm T$
- $2\,\mathrm T$
- $4\,\mathrm T$
- $0\,\mathrm T$

## Explanation

At the midpoint, the distances and contribution magnitudes are equal. The right-hand rule gives opposite directions, so the total field vanishes. The result is $0\,\mathrm T$.
