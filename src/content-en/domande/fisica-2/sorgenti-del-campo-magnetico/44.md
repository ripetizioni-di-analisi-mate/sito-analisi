To apply Ampère's law directly to the magnitude of $B$, what additional information is often needed?

- Only the value of the wire's mass
- Zero electric potential at infinity
- The absence of insulating materials
- A symmetry that makes the field along the path known

## Explanation

Ampère's law gives an integral along a curve. To obtain the local field, its direction and variation along the path must be known. A suitable symmetry makes this possible.
