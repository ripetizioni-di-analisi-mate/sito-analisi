A coil of $30$ identical turns has a flux of $2\,\mathrm{mWb}$ per turn. What is the total flux linkage?

- $30\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $120\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $240\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mWb}$

## Explanation

When all turns have the same flux, the flux linkage is $\Psi=N\Phi$. The flux through one turn is therefore multiplied by the number of turns. The result is $60\,\mathrm{mWb}$.
