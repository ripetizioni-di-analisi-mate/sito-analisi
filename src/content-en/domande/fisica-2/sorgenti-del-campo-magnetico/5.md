For ten coincident circular loops, the field at the center due to a single loop is $3\,\mu\mathrm T$. If they carry the same current in the same direction, what is the total field?

- $30\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $15\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $60\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $120\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The field obeys the superposition principle. Ten equal contributions pointing in the same direction add. The result is $30\,\mu\mathrm T$.
