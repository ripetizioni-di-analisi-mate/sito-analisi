In an ideal toroid, what geometry do the field lines in the core follow?

- Radial straight lines
- Straight lines parallel to the vertical axis
- Spirals ending on the copper
- Circles centered on the axis

## Explanation

Toroidal symmetry selects an azimuthal direction. The field is tangent to circles around the axis. Its magnitude depends on the radius.
