A magnetic field passes through a closed surface. What is the net outward flux according to Gauss's law for magnetism?

- $0\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $1\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $2\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $4\,\mathrm{Wb}$

## Explanation

The magnetic field has zero divergence. The net flux through every closed surface is therefore zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
