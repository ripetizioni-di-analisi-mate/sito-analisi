An Amperian path encloses $5\,\mathrm A$ in the direction of the normal and $2\,\mathrm A$ in the opposite direction. What is the algebraic enclosed current?

- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $7\,\mathrm A$
- $-3\,\mathrm A$
- $3\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The enclosed current is the signed sum of the currents crossing the surface. Thus $I_{conc}=5-2$. The result is $3\,\mathrm A$.
