A cylindrical wire carries uniformly distributed current. What fraction of the total current is contained within radius $\dfrac{R}{2}$?

- $0{,}5$
- $0{,}125$
- $0{,}75$
- $0{,}25$

## Explanation

The current density is uniform. The current fraction therefore equals the area fraction $\dfrac{r^2}{R^2}$. The result is $0{,}25$.
