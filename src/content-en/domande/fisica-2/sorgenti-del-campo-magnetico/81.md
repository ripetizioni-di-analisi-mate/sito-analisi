A uniform magnetic field passes through a plane surface. The chosen normal makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. Given the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the oriented magnetic flux $\Phi = \dfrac{3}{50}\,\mathrm{Wb}$, what is the area $A$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The flux of a uniform field is $BA\cos\theta$. The angle is measured from the normal, so $\cos60^\circ$ is used here. In the indicated units, $\Phi = \dfrac{A \cdot B}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{2 \cdot \Phi}{B}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
