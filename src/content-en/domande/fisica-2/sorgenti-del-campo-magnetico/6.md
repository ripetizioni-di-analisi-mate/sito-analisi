A semicircular arc of radius $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ carries $2\,\mathrm A$. What is the field at its center due to the arc alone, in microteslas? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $3{,}14\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $6{,}28\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $12{,}6\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $25{,}1\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

An arc of angle $\alpha$ generates $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I\alpha}{4\pi R}$. For a semicircle, $\alpha=\pi$. The result is $6{,}28\,\mu\mathrm T$.
