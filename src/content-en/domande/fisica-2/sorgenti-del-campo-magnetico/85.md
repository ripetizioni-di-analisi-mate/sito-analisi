A small bismuth sphere hangs from a thread near the pole of an electromagnet, where the field is strong and nonuniform. What happens to the sphere when the electromagnet is switched on?

- It is strongly attracted toward the pole
- It is weakly pushed toward the region of weaker field
- It is weakly attracted toward the pole
- It experiences no force, because bismuth does not become magnetized

## Explanation

Bismuth is a diamagnet: its magnetic susceptibility is negative and very small in absolute value.

In the presence of the field the sphere therefore acquires a weak magnetization opposite to the applied field.

A diamagnet is weakly repelled by regions of strong field, therefore the sphere moves slightly away from the pole.
