An ideal toroid in air carries current. Its winding has $200$ turns. The measurement point is in the core of the toroid. Use $\mu_0/(2\pi)=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the radius of the Amperian circle $r = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ and the field magnitude along the circle $B = 600\,\mathrm{\mu T}$, what is the current $I_{e}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, the circulation is $2\pi rB$. The enclosed current is $200I$. In the indicated units, $B = \dfrac{40 \cdot I_{e}}{r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $I_{e} = \dfrac{B \cdot r}{40}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{A}$.
