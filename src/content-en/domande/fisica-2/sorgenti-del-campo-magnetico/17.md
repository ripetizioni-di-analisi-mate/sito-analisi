An ideal toroid in vacuum has $N=100$, $I=2\,\mathrm A$ and observation radius $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is the field in microteslas? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $200\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $100\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $400\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $800\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

In the toroid, $B=\dfrac{\mu_0NI}{2\pi r}$. The radius is that of the observation point inside the winding. The result is $200\,\mu\mathrm T$.
