A solenoid has internal field $4\,\mathrm{mT}$. With the number of turns and current unchanged, its length doubles. What is the new ideal field?

- $1\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mT}$

## Explanation

With the total number fixed, $n=\dfrac{N}{L}$ is halved. Since $B=\mu_0nI$, the field is also halved. The result is $2\,\mathrm{mT}$.
