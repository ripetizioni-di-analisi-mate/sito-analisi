A field $B=0{,}50\,\mathrm T$ passes through an area $0{,}10\,\mathrm{m^2}$, whose normal makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. What is the flux?

- $0{,}0125\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0{,}05\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0{,}025\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0{,}1\,\mathrm{Wb}$

## Explanation

The flux depends on the angle between the field and the normal. We use $\Phi=BA\cos60^\circ$ with a cosine of $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result is $0{,}025\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
