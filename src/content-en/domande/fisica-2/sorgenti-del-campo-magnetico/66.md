In a long solenoid in air, edge effects are neglected. The solenoid carries direct current. Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the number of turns per meter $n = 500\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and the current $I_{e} = 2\,\mathrm{A}$, what is the internal field magnitude $B$?

- $400\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $400 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $800 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $200 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

The circulation along a line of length $l$ inside the solenoid is $Bl$. It equals $\mu_0$ times the enclosed current $nlI$. In the indicated units, $B = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot I_{e} \cdot n}{5}$. Using the given values, we obtain $400 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{\mu T}$.
