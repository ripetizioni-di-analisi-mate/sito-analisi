A linear material has $\mu_r$ slightly greater than one and shows no hysteresis. How is it classified?

- Paramagnetic
- Diamagnetic
- Ferromagnetic with mandatory hysteresis
- Without a magnetic response

## Explanation

The susceptibility $\chi_m=\mu_r-1$ is small and positive. The magnetization points in the same direction as the applied field. In the indicated model, the material is paramagnetic.
