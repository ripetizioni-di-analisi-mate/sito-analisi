An ideal toroid in air carries current. Its winding has $200$ turns. The measurement point is in the core of the toroid. Use $\mu_0/(2\pi)=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Initially, the given quantities are the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the radius of the Amperian circle $r = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$. In the second configuration, we change the radius of the Amperian circle to $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $B$?

- $\dfrac{16}{25}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $1$

## Explanation

By symmetry, the circulation is $2\pi rB$. The enclosed current is $200I$. Applying $B = \dfrac{40 \cdot I_{e}}{r}$ gives $600\,\mathrm{\mu T}$ before the change and $480\,\mathrm{\mu T}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{4}{5}$.
