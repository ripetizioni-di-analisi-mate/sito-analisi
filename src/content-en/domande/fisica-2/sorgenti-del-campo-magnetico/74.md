An ideal toroid of $500$ turns, carrying the current $2.0\,\mathrm{A}$, has inner radius $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and outer radius $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the magnetic field in the equatorial plane of the toroid, $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from its axis?

- $0$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $2.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $2.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The point lies in the central hole, at a distance from the axis smaller than the inner radius.

By rotational symmetry the field is tangent to the coaxial circle of radius $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and has constant magnitude along it. This circle is not crossed by any turn, so the enclosed current is zero and by Ampère's law $B\,2\pi r = 0$.

Therefore the field is **zero**; the expression $\dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{2\pi r}$, which at $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ would give $5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$, holds only for $a < r < b$.
