An oriented closed path encloses two wires. The first current crosses the surface in the direction of the chosen normal, and the second in the opposite direction. The first has the larger magnitude. Given the magnitude of the first current $I_{1} = 8\,\mathrm A$ and the magnitude of the second current $I_{2} = 3\,\mathrm A$, what is the circulation of $\vec B$ divided by $\mu_0$ in vacuum $b$?

- $10\,\mathrm A$
- $5\,\mathrm A$
- $11\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

Ampère's law relates circulation to the algebraic sum of the enclosed currents. The second contributes with a negative sign. In the indicated units, $b = I_{1} - I_{2}$. Using the given values, we obtain $5\,\mathrm A$.
