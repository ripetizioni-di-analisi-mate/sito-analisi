Two infinite parallel wires carry $I_1=3\,\mathrm A$ and $I_2=4\,\mathrm A$ and are separated by $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is the force per unit length, in micronewtons per meter? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $12\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $6\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $24\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$
- $48\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $\dfrac{F}{L}=\dfrac{\mu_0|I_1I_2|}{2\pi d}$. Convert the result from newtons to micronewtons. The result is $12\,\mu\mathrm{N/m}$.
