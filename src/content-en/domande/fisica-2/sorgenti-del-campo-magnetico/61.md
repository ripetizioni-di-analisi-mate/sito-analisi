Two long parallel wires are separated by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The current magnitudes are positive. Use $\mu_0/(2\pi)=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$. Given the current in the first wire $I_{1} = 8\,\mathrm A$ and the current in the second wire $I_{2} = 6\,\mathrm A$, what is the magnitude of the force per unit length $b$?

- $96\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $192\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $384\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$

## Explanation

The field of the first wire at the second is $\mu_0 I_1/(2\pi d)$. To find the force per unit length, multiply by $I_2$. In the indicated units, $b = 4 \cdot I_{1} \cdot I_{2}$. Using the given values, we obtain $192\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$.
