A ring has charge $5\,\mathrm{nC}$ and radius $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$. What is the potential at its center with zero at infinity? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $75\,\mathrm V$
- $150\,\mathrm V$
- $300\,\mathrm V$
- $600\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Each element of the ring is at a distance of $R$ from the center. Therefore, $V=\dfrac{kQ}{R}$. The result is $150\,\mathrm V$.
