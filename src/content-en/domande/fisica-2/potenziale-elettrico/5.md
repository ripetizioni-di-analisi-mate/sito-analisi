Two charges $+2\,\mathrm{nC}$ are both at a distance of $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$ from a point. What is the total potential with zero at infinity? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $180\,\mathrm V$
- $90\,\mathrm V$
- $360\,\mathrm V$
- $720\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Each charge contributes $\dfrac{kq}{r}=90\,\mathrm V$. Since the potential adds as a scalar, the two contributions add. The result is $180\,\mathrm V$.
