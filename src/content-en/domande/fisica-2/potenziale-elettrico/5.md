Two positive point charges $q$ and $4q$ are separated by $L$. The potential has an interior minimum on the segment joining them. At what distance from $q$ is that minimum?

- $\dfrac L3$
- $\dfrac L5$
- $\dfrac L2$
- $\dfrac{2L}3$

## Explanation

At the minimum the field component along the segment vanishes. The two fields there are opposite, so $\dfrac{q}{x^2}=\dfrac{4q}{(L-x)^2}$. Both distances are positive, giving $L-x=2x$. The potential rises towards both ends.
