In a region, the potential is $V(x)=ax^2+b$ with $a>0$. A point with a positive coordinate is considered. Initially, the given quantities are the quadratic coefficient $a = 5\,\mathrm{V/m^2}$ and the point's coordinate $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. In the second configuration, we change the point's coordinate to $5\,\mathrm{m}$, with all other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $E$?

- $\dfrac{25}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The component is $E_x=-dV/dx=-2ax$. The value of $b$ disappears upon differentiation. Applying $E = 2 \cdot a \cdot x$ gives $30\,\mathrm{N/C}$ before the change and $50\,\mathrm{N/C}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
