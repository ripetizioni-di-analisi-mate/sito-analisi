A positive particle moves against the uniform field along a field line until it stops. Its charge-to-mass ratio is $2\,\mathrm{C/kg}$. Given the initial speed $u = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the electric field magnitude $E = 4\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the stopping distance $d$?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The electric force removes kinetic energy by doing work of magnitude $qEd$. Equating it to $mu^2/2$ gives the distance. In the indicated units, $d = \dfrac{u^{2}}{4 \cdot E}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
