A uniform field $\vec E=200\hat\imath\,\mathrm{V/m}$ acts between $A$ and $B$, with $x_B-x_A=0{,}30\,\mathrm m$. What is $V_B-V_A$?

- $60\,\mathrm V$
- $-120\,\mathrm V$
- $120\,\mathrm V$
- $-60\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The potential difference is the negative of the integral of the field. In the uniform case, $V_B-V_A=-E_x(x_B-x_A)$ holds. The result is $-60\,\mathrm V$.
