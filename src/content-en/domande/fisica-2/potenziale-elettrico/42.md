Two thin concentric rings of radii $R$ and $2R$ initially carry $Q>0$ and zero charge. A fraction of the charge on the inner ring is transferred and spread uniformly onto the outer ring. The central potential relative to infinity drops to $3/4$ of its initial value. What fraction was transferred?

- $1/4$
- $1/2$
- $3/4$
- $1/8$

## Explanation

For a transferred fraction $f$, the total potential is $kQ[(1-f)/R+f/(2R)]$. The ratio to $kQ/R$ is $1-f/2$; equating it to $3/4$ gives $f=1/2$.
