A uniformly charged ring has zero field at its center. What is its central potential relative to infinity?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{kQ}{R}$
- $\dfrac{kQ}{R^2}$
- $\dfrac{2kQ}{R^2}$

## Explanation

All elements of the ring are at the same distance $R$ from the center. The scalar contributions to the potential add. Therefore, $V=\dfrac{kQ}{R}$ even though the field is zero.
