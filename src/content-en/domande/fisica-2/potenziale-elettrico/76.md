A uniformly charged ring of radius $R$ is in a vacuum. The potential at the center is measured relative to infinity. Use $k=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. Given the total positive charge $q = 3\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the radius of the ring $R = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the potential at the center $U_{e}$?

- $225\,\mathrm{V}$
- $900\,\mathrm{V}$
- $45\,\mathrm{V}$
- $450\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Each charge element is at the same distance $R$ from the center. In the potential integral, the factor $1/R$ is factored out. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{900 \cdot q}{R}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $450\,\mathrm{V}$.
