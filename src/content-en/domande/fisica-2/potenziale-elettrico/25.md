A conservative field does work $3\,\mathrm J$ in going from $A$ to $B$. How much work does it do along another path from $A$ to $B$?

- $3\,\mathrm J$
- $1{,}5\,\mathrm J$
- $6\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Electrostatic work depends only on the endpoints. Changing the path without changing the initial and final points therefore leaves the work unchanged. The result is $3\,\mathrm J$.
