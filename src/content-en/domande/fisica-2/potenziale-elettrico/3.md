A charge $3\,\mathrm{nC}$ generates a potential in a vacuum. What is its value at $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$, with zero at infinity? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $45\,\mathrm V$
- $180\,\mathrm V$
- $90\,\mathrm V$
- $360\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

For a point charge, the potential is $V=\dfrac{kq}{r}$. Its sign is the same as that of the source. The result is $90\,\mathrm V$.
