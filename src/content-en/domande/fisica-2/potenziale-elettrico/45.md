A positive particle comes momentarily to rest while approaching another fixed positive charge. Which relation holds at the point of minimum separation?

- The initial kinetic energy has been converted into potential energy
- The total energy has become zero
- The charges have canceled each other
- The electric force has become zero

## Explanation

The interaction is repulsive and conservative. As the particle approaches, its potential energy increases. At the momentary stop, its kinetic energy is zero and conservation of energy applies.
