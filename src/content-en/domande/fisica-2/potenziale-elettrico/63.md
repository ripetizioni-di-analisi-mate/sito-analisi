A negative charge is moved from a point at lower potential toward one at higher potential. Given the charge magnitude $q = 4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the change in the charge's potential energy $U = -100\,\mathrm{nJ}$, what is the increase in potential $U_{e}$?

- $25\,\mathrm{V}$
- $50\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\dfrac{75}{2}\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The relation $\Delta U=q_{\mathrm{carica}}\Delta V$ holds. Here, $q_{\mathrm{carica}}=-q$ and $\Delta V>0$, so the potential energy decreases. In the indicated units, $U = - U_{e} \cdot q$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{e} = - \dfrac{U}{q}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $25\,\mathrm{V}$.
