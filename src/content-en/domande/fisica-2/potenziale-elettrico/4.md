Two charges $+2\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$ are both at a distance of $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ from a point. What is the potential at that point with zero at infinity?

- $180\,\mathrm V$
- $-180\,\mathrm V$
- $360\,\mathrm V$
- $0\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The potential is the scalar sum of the contributions $\dfrac{kq_i}{r_i}$. The two contributions have equal magnitudes and opposite signs. The result is $0\,\mathrm V$.
