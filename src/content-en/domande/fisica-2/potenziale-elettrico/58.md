A positive charge moves from a point at higher potential to one at lower potential. The electric force is the only force doing work. Given the charge $q = 3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the increase in kinetic energy $K = 240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$, what is the potential drop $U_{e}$?

- $120\,\mathrm{V}$
- $80\,\mathrm{V}$
- $160\,\mathrm{V}$
- $40\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The work done by the electric force is the loss of potential energy, namely the charge times the potential drop. The work–energy theorem gives the same increase in kinetic energy. In the indicated units, $K = U_{e} \cdot q$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{e} = \dfrac{K}{q}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $80\,\mathrm{V}$.
