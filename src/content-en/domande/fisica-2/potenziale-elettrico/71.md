In a region, the potential is $V(x)=ax^2+b$ with $a>0$. A point with a positive coordinate is considered. Given the quadratic coefficient $a = 5\,\mathrm{V/m^2}$ and the point's coordinate $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the magnitude of the component along $x$ of the field $E$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The component is $E_x=-dV/dx=-2ax$. The value of $b$ disappears upon differentiation. In the indicated units, $E = 2 \cdot a \cdot x$. Using the values given in the problem gives $30\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
