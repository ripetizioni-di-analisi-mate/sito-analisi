A particle of mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and charge $+1\,\mathrm C$ starts from rest and passes through a potential drop of $9\,\mathrm V$. Neglecting other forces, what is its final speed?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The decrease in potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Therefore, $\dfrac{mv^2}{2}=q(V_A-V_B)=9\,\mathrm J$. The result is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
