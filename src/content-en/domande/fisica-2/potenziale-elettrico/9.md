A uniform field of $300\,\mathrm{V/m}$ points along $x$. What is the potential difference between two points with the same $x$ and separated along $y$?

- $0\,\mathrm V$
- $100\,\mathrm V$
- $300\,\mathrm V$
- $-300\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The displacement is perpendicular to the field. The dot product in the potential integral is therefore zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm V$.
