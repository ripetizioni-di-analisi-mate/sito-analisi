A uniform spherical shell has surface potential $100\,\mathrm V$ relative to infinity. What is the potential at the center?

- $50\,\mathrm V$
- $200\,\mathrm V$
- $400\,\mathrm V$
- $100\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Inside the shell, the field is zero. The potential is therefore constant and equal to the value on the surface. The result is $100\,\mathrm V$.
