A negative charge is moved from a point at lower potential toward one at higher potential. Given the charge magnitude $q = 4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the increase in potential $U_{e} = 25\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the change in the charge's potential energy $U$?

- $100\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $-100\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{nJ}$

## Explanation

The relation $\Delta U=q_{\mathrm{carica}}\Delta V$ holds. Here, $q_{\mathrm{carica}}=-q$ and $\Delta V>0$, so the potential energy decreases. In the indicated units, $U = - U_{e} \cdot q$. Using the values given in the problem gives $-100\,\mathrm{nJ}$.
