At a point at distance $r$ from a positive point charge $Q$ the potential, zero at infinite distance, is $600\,\mathrm{V}$ and the magnitude of the field is $2.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{V/m}$. What are $r$ and $Q$?

- $r = 3.3\,\mathrm{m}$, $Q = 2.2 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $r = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, $Q = 6.0\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $r = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, $Q = 20\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $r = 0.55\,\mathrm{m}$, $Q = 37\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

For a point charge $V = \dfrac{kQ}{r}$ and $E = \dfrac{kQ}{r^2}$, so the ratio of the two quantities is $\dfrac{V}{E} = r$.

Therefore $r = \dfrac{600}{2.0 \cdot 10^3}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$.

From the formula for the potential one then gets
$$
Q = \frac{V\,r}{k} = \frac{600 \cdot 0.30}{8.99 \cdot 10^9}\,\mathrm{C} \approx 2.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C} = 20\,\mathrm{nC} .
$$
