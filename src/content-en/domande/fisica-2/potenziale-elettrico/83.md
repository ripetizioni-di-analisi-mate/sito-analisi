Two points lie in a region of uniform electric field. Their separation has a component along the field equal to half the magnitude of the separation and pointing in the same direction as the field. Given the distance between the two points $d = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ and the potential drop from the first point to the second $U_{e} = 42\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the electric field magnitude $E$?

- $140\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $210\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $280\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The potential drop is the integral of the field along the path. For a uniform field, it equals the dot product with the displacement. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{E \cdot d}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $E = \dfrac{2 \cdot U_{e}}{d}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $140\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
