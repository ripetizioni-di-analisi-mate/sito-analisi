Four point charges occupy the vertices of a square of side $a$, with alternating signs: $+q$, $-q$, $+q$, $-q$ going around the boundary. What is the electrostatic energy of the system?

- $(\sqrt{2} - 4)\,\dfrac{kq^2}{a}$
- $(4 - \sqrt{2})\,\dfrac{kq^2}{a}$
- $-4\,\dfrac{kq^2}{a}$
- $(2\sqrt{2} - 8)\,\dfrac{kq^2}{a}$

## Explanation

The energy of the system is the sum of the terms $\dfrac{k\,q_i q_j}{r_{ij}}$ over all pairs, each counted once. With four charges there are six pairs.

The four pairs of adjacent charges have opposite signs and are at distance $a$, so they give $-4\,\dfrac{kq^2}{a}$. The two pairs on the diagonals have equal signs and are at distance $\sqrt{2}\,a$, so they give $2\,\dfrac{kq^2}{\sqrt{2}\,a} = \sqrt{2}\,\dfrac{kq^2}{a}$.

Therefore $U = (\sqrt{2} - 4)\,\dfrac{kq^2}{a} \approx -2.6\,\dfrac{kq^2}{a}$; the negative sign indicates that separating the charges requires positive external work.
