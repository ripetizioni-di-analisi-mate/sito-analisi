A positive charge travels along a segment parallel to and in the same direction as the uniform electric field. The path has length $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$. Initially, the given quantities are the charge $q = 5\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the field magnitude $E = 600\,\mathrm{N/C}$. In the second configuration, we change the field magnitude to $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$, with all other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $W$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The force is constant and parallel to the displacement. The product $qEd$ is positive and is expressed in microjoules when using microcoulombs. Applying $W = \dfrac{2 \cdot E \cdot q}{5}$ gives $1200\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ before the change and $600\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
