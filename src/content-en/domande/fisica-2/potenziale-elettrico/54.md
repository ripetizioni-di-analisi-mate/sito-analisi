In a region there is the uniform field $\vec{E} = (300\,\hat{\imath} + 400\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{V/m}$. What is the potential difference $V_B - V_A$ between the points $A = (0;\ 0)$ and $B = (2.0\,\mathrm{m};\ -1.0\,\mathrm{m})$?

- $+200\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-1.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-1.1 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

In a uniform field $V_B - V_A = -\vec{E} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}$, with $\overrightarrow{AB} = (2.0\,\hat{\imath} - 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$.

The dot product is $\vec{E} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = (300 \cdot 2.0 + 400 \cdot (-1.0))\,\mathrm{V} = 200\,\mathrm{V}$.

Therefore $V_B - V_A = -200\,\mathrm{V}$. The $y$-component of the displacement is opposite to $E_y$, so its contribution has the opposite sign to that of the $x$-component.
