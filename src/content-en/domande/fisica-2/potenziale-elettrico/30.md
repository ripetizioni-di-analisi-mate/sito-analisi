An electron moves from $V_A=0$ to $V_B=+20\,\mathrm V$. What is the change in potential energy in electronvolts?

- $20\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $40\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The change is $\Delta U=q\Delta V$. For an electron, $q=-e$, so an increase in potential reduces its potential energy. The result is $-20\,\mathrm{eV}$.
