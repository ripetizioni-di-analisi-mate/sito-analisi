A uniformly charged solid insulating sphere has surface potential $80\,\mathrm V$ relative to infinity. What is the potential at the center?

- $120\,\mathrm V$
- $40\,\mathrm V$
- $80\,\mathrm V$
- $160\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

For the uniform sphere, $V(0)=\dfrac{3kQ}{2R}$ holds. The surface value is $\dfrac{kQ}{R}$, so the central value is one and a half times as large. The result is $120\,\mathrm V$.
