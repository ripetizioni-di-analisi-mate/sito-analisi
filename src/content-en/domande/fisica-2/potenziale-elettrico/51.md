A positive charge travels along a segment parallel to and in the same direction as the uniform electric field. The path has length $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$. Given the charge $q = 5\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the field magnitude $E = 600\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the work done by the electric force $W$?

- $1200\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The force is constant and parallel to the displacement. The product $qEd$ is positive and is expressed in microjoules when using microcoulombs. In the indicated units, $W = \dfrac{2 \cdot E \cdot q}{5}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $1200\,\mathrm{\mu J}$.
