Two points lie in a region of uniform electric field. Their separation has a component along the field equal to half the magnitude of the separation and pointing in the same direction as the field. Given the electric field magnitude $E = 140\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the potential drop from the first point to the second $U_{e} = 42\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the distance between the two points $d$?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The potential drop is the integral of the field along the path. For a uniform field, it equals the dot product with the displacement. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{E \cdot d}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{2 \cdot U_{e}}{E}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$.
