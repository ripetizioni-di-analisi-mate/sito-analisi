A distant dipole has $\dfrac{kp}{r^2}=40\,\mathrm V$. What is its potential at an angle of $60^\circ$ relative to the dipole moment, with zero at infinity?

- $10\,\mathrm V$
- $40\,\mathrm V$
- $20\,\mathrm V$
- $80\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The far-field dipole potential is $V=\dfrac{kp\cos\theta}{r^2}$. Since $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$, the value is halved. The result is $20\,\mathrm V$.
