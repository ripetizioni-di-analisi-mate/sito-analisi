In a region, the potential is $V(x)=ax^2+b$ with $a>0$. A point with a positive coordinate is considered. Given the point's coordinate $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the magnitude of the component along $x$ of the field $E = 30\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the quadratic coefficient $a$?

- $10\,\mathrm{V/m^2}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{V/m^2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{V/m^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{V/m^2}$

## Explanation

The component is $E_x=-dV/dx=-2ax$. The value of $b$ disappears upon differentiation. In the indicated units, $E = 2 \cdot a \cdot x$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = \dfrac{E}{2 \cdot x}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $5\,\mathrm{V/m^2}$.
