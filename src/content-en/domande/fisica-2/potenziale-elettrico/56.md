A positive charge moves from a point at higher potential to one at lower potential. The electric force is the only force doing work. Given the charge $q = 3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the potential drop $U_{e} = 80\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the increase in kinetic energy $K$?

- $360\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $480\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The work done by the electric force is the loss of potential energy, namely the charge times the potential drop. The work–energy theorem gives the same increase in kinetic energy. In the indicated units, $K = U_{e} \cdot q$. Using the values given in the problem gives $240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$.
