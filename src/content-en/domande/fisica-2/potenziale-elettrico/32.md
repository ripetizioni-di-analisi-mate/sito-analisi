The electrostatic field is zero throughout a connected region. How does the potential vary in the region?

- It is necessarily zero
- It always increases linearly
- It is inversely proportional to the distance
- It is constant

## Explanation

The relation $\vec E=-\nabla V$ holds. Therefore, the gradient of the potential is zero. In a connected region, the potential is constant, with a value depending on the chosen zero.
