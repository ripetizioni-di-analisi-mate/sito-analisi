The potential is $V(x)=3x^2$ in SI units. What is $E_x$ at $x=2\,\mathrm m$?

- $12\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The field is the negative of the derivative of the potential. Therefore, $E_x=-6x$ and the required value is negative. The result is $-12\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
