Two opposite charges of equal magnitude $q$ are separated by a distance of $r$. The potential energy of the pair is zero at infinity. Use $k=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. Given the magnitude of each charge $q = 4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the potential energy of the pair $U = -1800\,\mathrm{nJ}$, what is the separation $r$?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The energy of the pair is $kq_1q_2/r$. The product of the charges is negative and only one pair is counted. In the indicated units, $U = - \dfrac{900 \cdot q^{2}}{r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $r = - \dfrac{900 \cdot q^{2}}{U}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
