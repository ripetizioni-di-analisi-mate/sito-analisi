What energy in joules corresponds to $5\,\mathrm{eV}$? Use $e=1{,}6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$.

- $4\cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$
- $1{,}6\cdot 10^{-18}\,\mathrm J$
- $8\cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$
- $3{,}2\cdot 10^{-18}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The conversion is $1\,\mathrm{eV}=1{,}6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$. This value is therefore multiplied by five. The result is $8\cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$.
