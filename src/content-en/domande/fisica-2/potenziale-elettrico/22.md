A charge $+2\,\mu\mathrm C$ is moved along an equipotential. How much work does the electric force do?

- $1\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $2\,\mathrm J$
- $4\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

On an equipotential, $V_A=V_B$. The work $W=q(V_A-V_B)$ is therefore zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm J$.
