A negative charge is moved from a point at lower potential toward one at higher potential. Given the increase in potential $U_{e} = 25\,\mathrm{V}$ and the change in the charge's potential energy $U = -100\,\mathrm{nJ}$, what is the charge magnitude $q$?

- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $6\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The relation $\Delta U=q_{\mathrm{carica}}\Delta V$ holds. Here, $q_{\mathrm{carica}}=-q$ and $\Delta V>0$, so the potential energy decreases. In the indicated units, $U = - U_{e} \cdot q$. Solving for the unknown gives $q = - \dfrac{U}{U_{e}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $4\,\mathrm{nC}$.
