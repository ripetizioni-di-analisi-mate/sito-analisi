Three equal charges $q=1\,\mu\mathrm C$ are at the vertices of an equilateral triangle of side length $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$. What is the total electrostatic energy? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $0{,}045\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}18\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}09\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}36\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

There are three distinct pairs of charges. Each contributes $\dfrac{kq^2}{a}=0{,}030\,\mathrm J$, so three contributions are added. The result is $0{,}09\,\mathrm J$.
