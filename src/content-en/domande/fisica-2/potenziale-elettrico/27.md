An external force slowly moves a charge against the field, increasing its potential energy by $7\,\mathrm{mJ}$. What is the external work?

- $3{,}5\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $14\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $7\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $28\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

In slow motion, the change in kinetic energy is negligible. The external work is therefore equal to the increase in potential energy. The result is $7\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
