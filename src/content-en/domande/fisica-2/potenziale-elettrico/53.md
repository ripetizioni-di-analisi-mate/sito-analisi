A positive charge travels along a segment parallel to and in the same direction as the uniform electric field. The path has length $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$. Given the charge $q = 5\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the work done by the electric force $W = 1200\,\mathrm{\mu J}$, what is the field magnitude $E$?

- $1200\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The force is constant and parallel to the displacement. The product $qEd$ is positive and is expressed in microjoules when using microcoulombs. In the indicated units, $W = \dfrac{2 \cdot E \cdot q}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $E = \dfrac{5 \cdot W}{2 \cdot q}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $600\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
