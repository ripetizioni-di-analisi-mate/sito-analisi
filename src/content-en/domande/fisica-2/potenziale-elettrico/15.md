A uniformly charged thin ring lies in the plane $z=0$. A positive test charge moves along its axis from $z=-a$ to $z=+a$, where $a>0$. What is the total work done by the ring's field on the test charge?

- Positive, because the test charge approaches the ring during the first part.
- Negative, because the test charge moves away from the ring during the second part.
- Zero, because the endpoints are at the same potential.
- It depends on the speed at the centre.

## Explanation

Each ring element is at distance $\sqrt{R^2+a^2}$ from both endpoints. Their potentials agree, giving zero field work $q[V(-a)-V(+a)]$, although the force can act along the path.
