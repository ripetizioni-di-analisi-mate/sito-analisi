A ring has charge $5\,\mathrm{nC}$ and radius $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$. What is the potential on its axis at $0{,}40\,\mathrm m$ from the center? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $45\,\mathrm V$
- $180\,\mathrm V$
- $90\,\mathrm V$
- $360\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

All elements are at a distance of $\sqrt{R^2+x^2}=0{,}50\,\mathrm m$ from the point. The potential is therefore $V=\dfrac{kQ}{\sqrt{R^2+x^2}}$. The result is $90\,\mathrm V$.
