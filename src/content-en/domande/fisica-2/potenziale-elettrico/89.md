A positive particle moves against the uniform field along a field line until it stops. Its charge-to-mass ratio is $2\,\mathrm{C/kg}$. Given the electric field magnitude $E = 4\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the stopping distance $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the initial speed $u$?

- $32\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The electric force removes kinetic energy by doing work of magnitude $qEd$. Equating it to $mu^2/2$ gives the distance. In the indicated units, $d = \dfrac{u^{2}}{4 \cdot E}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = 2 \cdot \sqrt{E} \cdot \sqrt{d}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
