The potential of a point charge is $60\,\mathrm V$ at a distance of $r$, with zero at infinity. What is it at a distance of $3r$?

- $10\,\mathrm V$
- $20\,\mathrm V$
- $40\,\mathrm V$
- $80\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The point-charge potential varies as $\dfrac{1}{r}$. Tripling the distance divides the potential by three. The result is $20\,\mathrm V$.
