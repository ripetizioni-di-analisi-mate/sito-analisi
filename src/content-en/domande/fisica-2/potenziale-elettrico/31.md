The potential is zero at a point. Which conclusion about the field is correct?

- The field is always zero
- There can be no nearby charges
- The field can be nonzero
- The field necessarily points radially

## Explanation

The field depends on the gradient of the potential. The value of the potential at a single point does not determine its derivatives. At the midpoint of two opposite charges, the potential is zero and the field is nonzero.
