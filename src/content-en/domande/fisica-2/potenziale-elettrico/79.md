Two opposite charges of equal magnitude $q$ are separated by a distance of $r$. The potential energy of the pair is zero at infinity. Use $k=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. Given the magnitude of each charge $q = 4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the separation $r = 8\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the potential energy of the pair $U$?

- $-1800\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $-900\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $-3600\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{nJ}$

## Explanation

The energy of the pair is $kq_1q_2/r$. The product of the charges is negative and only one pair is counted. In the indicated units, $U = - \dfrac{900 \cdot q^{2}}{r}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $-1800\,\mathrm{nJ}$.
