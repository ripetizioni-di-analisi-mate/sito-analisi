A positive particle moves from $20\,\mathrm V$ to $5\,\mathrm V$ under the action of the electric force alone. How do its electric potential energy and kinetic energy change?

- Both increase
- The potential energy decreases and the kinetic energy increases
- The potential energy increases and the kinetic energy decreases
- Both decrease

## Explanation

For a positive charge, $U=qV$ decreases along with the potential. The force is conservative and mechanical energy is conserved, so the kinetic energy increases by the same amount.
