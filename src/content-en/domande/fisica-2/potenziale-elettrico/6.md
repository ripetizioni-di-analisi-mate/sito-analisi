Two charges $+1\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $+2\,\mu\mathrm C$ change their separation from $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$ to $0{,}60\,\mathrm m$. Use $k=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. What total work do the electric forces do on the two charges?

- $-0{,}03\,\mathrm J$
- $+0{,}03\,\mathrm J$
- $+0{,}06\,\mathrm J$
- $+0{,}09\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The Coulomb force is conservative. The potential energy of the pair changes from $kq_1q_2/0{,}30=0{,}06\,\mathrm J$ to $kq_1q_2/0{,}60=0{,}03\,\mathrm J$. The total work is the initial energy minus the final energy, namely $+0{,}03\,\mathrm J$.
