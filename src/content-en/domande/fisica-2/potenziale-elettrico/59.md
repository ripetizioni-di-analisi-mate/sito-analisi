A positive charge moves from a point at higher potential to one at lower potential. The electric force is the only force doing work. Initially, the given quantities are the charge $q = 3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the potential drop $U_{e} = 80\,\mathrm{V}$. In the second configuration, we change the potential drop to $120\,\mathrm{V}$, with all other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $K$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The work done by the electric force is the loss of potential energy, namely the charge times the potential drop. The work–energy theorem gives the same increase in kinetic energy. Applying $K = U_{e} \cdot q$ gives $240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ before the change and $360\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
