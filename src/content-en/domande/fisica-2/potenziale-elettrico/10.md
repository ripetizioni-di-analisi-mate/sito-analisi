An electron gains kinetic energy by crossing an accelerating potential difference of magnitude $150\,\mathrm V$. How much energy does it gain in electronvolts?

- $75\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $150\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $300\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $600\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

One electronvolt is the energy gained by an elementary charge moving through an accelerating potential difference of one volt. Therefore, the numerical value in eV equals the number of volts crossed. The result is $150\,\mathrm{eV}$.
