Two charges $+1\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $-2\,\mu\mathrm C$ are separated by $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$. What is their potential energy relative to infinity? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $0{,}06\,\mathrm J$
- $-0{,}12\,\mathrm J$
- $-0{,}06\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The energy of the pair is $U=\dfrac{kq_1q_2}{r}$. The product of the charges is negative, so the energy is also negative. The result is $-0{,}06\,\mathrm J$.
