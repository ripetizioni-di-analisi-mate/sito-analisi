A charge $+2\,\mu\mathrm C$ crosses a potential difference $V_B-V_A=-30\,\mathrm V$. What is the work done by the electric force in microjoules?

- $60\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $30\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $120\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $240\,\mu\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The work is $W=q(V_A-V_B)$. The negative potential difference therefore produces positive work. The result is $60\,\mu\mathrm J$.
