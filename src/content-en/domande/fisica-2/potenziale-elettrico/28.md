The potential is redefined by adding $50\,\mathrm V$ at every point. By how much does the potential difference between two points change?

- $25\,\mathrm V$
- $50\,\mathrm V$
- $100\,\mathrm V$
- $0\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The constant is added to both values. The two constant contributions cancel in their difference. The result is $0\,\mathrm V$.
