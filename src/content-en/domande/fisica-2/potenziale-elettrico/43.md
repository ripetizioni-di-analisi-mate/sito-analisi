When calculating the energy of a system of charges, how are the pairs counted?

- Twice without a correction factor
- The interaction of each charge with itself is included
- Only once for each distinct pair
- Only pairs of opposite signs are counted

## Explanation

The interaction energy is $\dfrac{\sum_{i<j}kq_iq_j}{r_{ij}}$. The condition $i<j$ avoids double counting. Point-charge self-energies are not included in this sum.
