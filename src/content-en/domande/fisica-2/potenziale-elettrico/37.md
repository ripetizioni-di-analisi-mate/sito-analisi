Two positive charges are moved farther apart. With zero energy at infinity, how does their potential energy change?

- It decreases while remaining positive
- It increases while remaining positive
- It becomes more negative
- It remains unchanged

## Explanation

The energy is $U=\dfrac{kq_1q_2}{r}$. The numerator is positive and constant. As $r$ increases, the value decreases toward zero.
