In a region, the potential is $V(x)=ax^2+b$ with $a>0$. A point with a positive coordinate is considered. Given the quadratic coefficient $a = 5\,\mathrm{V/m^2}$ and the magnitude of the component along $x$ of the field $E = 30\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the point's coordinate $x$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The component is $E_x=-dV/dx=-2ax$. The value of $b$ disappears upon differentiation. In the indicated units, $E = 2 \cdot a \cdot x$. Solving for the unknown gives $x = \dfrac{E}{2 \cdot a}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
