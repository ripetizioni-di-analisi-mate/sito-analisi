A negative charge is moved from a point at lower potential toward one at higher potential. Initially, the given quantities are the charge magnitude $q = 4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the increase in potential $U_{e} = 25\,\mathrm{V}$. In the second configuration, we change the increase in potential to $50\,\mathrm{V}$, with all other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U$?

- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The relation $\Delta U=q_{\mathrm{carica}}\Delta V$ holds. Here, $q_{\mathrm{carica}}=-q$ and $\Delta V>0$, so the potential energy decreases. Applying $U = - U_{e} \cdot q$ gives $-100\,\mathrm{nJ}$ before the change and $-200\,\mathrm{nJ}$ afterward. The ratio is $2$.
