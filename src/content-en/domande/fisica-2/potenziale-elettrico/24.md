The electrostatic potential along the $x$ axis is $V=12-4x$ in SI units. What is $E_x$?

- $-4\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The potential is differentiated with respect to $x$. The field is the negative of the derivative, so $E_x=-(-4)$. The result is $4\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
