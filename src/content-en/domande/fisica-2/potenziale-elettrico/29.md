A positive particle initially has kinetic energy $12\,\mathrm J$ and loses $5\,\mathrm J$ of potential energy. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $17\,\mathrm J$
- $7\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $24\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

When only the electrostatic force is present, $K+U$ is conserved. Therefore, $K_f=K_i-\Delta U$. The result is $17\,\mathrm J$.
