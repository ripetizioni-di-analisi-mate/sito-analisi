The field does work $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$ on a charge. What is the change in potential energy?

- $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

For a conservative force, $W=-\Delta U$ holds. Therefore, the increase in work corresponds to an equal decrease in potential energy. The result is $-8\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
