A charge $+4\,\mu\mathrm C$ moves from potential $10\,\mathrm V$ to $30\,\mathrm V$. By how much does the potential energy increase, in microjoules?

- $40\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $160\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $320\,\mu\mathrm J$
- $80\,\mu\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The change is $\Delta U=q(V_B-V_A)$. The positive charge moves to a higher potential, so its energy increases. The result is $80\,\mu\mathrm J$.
