The potential is $V(x,y)=2x-5y$ in SI units. What is the field magnitude?

- $5{,}39\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $29\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The gradient is $(2,-5)$. Therefore, the field is $(-2,5)$ and its magnitude is $\sqrt{2^2+5^2}$. The result is $5{,}39\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
