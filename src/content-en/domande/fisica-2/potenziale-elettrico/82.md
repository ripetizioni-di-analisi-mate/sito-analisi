A point charge $Q = 5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ is at rest at the origin, and its potential is $V = \dfrac{kQ}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}}$. What is the component $E_x$ of the field at the point $P = (3.0\,\mathrm{cm};\ 4.0\,\mathrm{cm};\ 0)$?

- $1.1 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $-1.1 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $1.8 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $1.4 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

By the field as the gradient of the potential, $E_x = -\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}$. Differentiating gives
$$
E_x = \frac{kQ\,x}{(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}} .
$$
At the point $P$ the distance from the origin is $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, so
$$
E_x = \frac{8.99 \cdot 10^9 \cdot 5.0 \cdot 10^{-9} \cdot 0.030}{(0.050)^3}\,\mathrm{V/m} \approx 1.1 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m} .
$$
The magnitude of the field at $P$ is $\dfrac{kQ}{r^2} \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$, and $E_x$ is the fraction $\dfrac{x}{r} = \dfrac{3}{5}$ of it.
