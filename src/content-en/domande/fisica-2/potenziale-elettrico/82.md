Two points lie in a region of uniform electric field. Their separation has a component along the field equal to half the magnitude of the separation and pointing in the same direction as the field. Given the electric field magnitude $E = 140\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the distance between the two points $d = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the potential drop from the first point to the second $U_{e}$?

- $63\,\mathrm{V}$
- $42\,\mathrm{V}$
- $84\,\mathrm{V}$
- $21\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The potential drop is the integral of the field along the path. For a uniform field, it equals the dot product with the displacement. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{E \cdot d}{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $42\,\mathrm{V}$.
