During the free decay of an RL circuit, current is $0.80\,\mathrm A$ and reads $0.40\,\mathrm A$ another $20\,\mathrm{ms}$ later. The exact time of source disconnection is unknown. What value of $\tau$ can be obtained from the two readings?

- $20\,\mathrm{ms}\cdot\ln 2$
- $40\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $20\,\mathrm{ms}/\ln 2$
- It cannot be obtained without the initial current.

## Explanation

The ratio of two readings on the same decay cancels the initial amplitude and the time origin. Since $0.40/0.80=\exp(-20\,\mathrm{ms}/\tau)$, $\tau=20\,\mathrm{ms}/\ln2$.
