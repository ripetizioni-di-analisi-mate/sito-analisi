The discharge current in an RL circuit starts from $12\,\mathrm A$. What is its value at $t=\tau\ln3$?

- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $16\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

During discharge, we have $I=I_0e^{-\dfrac{t}{\tau}}$. The exponential factor at the given instant is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The result is $4\,\mathrm A$.
