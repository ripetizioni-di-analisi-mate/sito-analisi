Two coupled coils have constant mutual inductance. The current in the first varies linearly with time. The secondary circuit is open. Given the mutual inductance $M = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm H$ and the magnitude of the emf in the second coil $U_{e} = \dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$, what is the magnitude of the derivative of the primary current $b$?

- $3\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{A/s}$

## Explanation

The flux linkage in the second coil due to the first is $MI_1$. The magnitude of its derivative is $M\lvert dI_1/dt\rvert$. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = M \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{U_{e}}{M}$. With the given values, we obtain $6\,\mathrm{A/s}$.
