Two coils have $L_1=0{,}50\,\mathrm H$, $L_2=0{,}080\,\mathrm H$ and $M=0{,}20\,\mathrm H$. What is the coupling coefficient $\dfrac{|M|}{\sqrt{L_1L_2}}$?

- $0{,}5$
- $2$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The product of the self-inductances is $0{,}040\,\mathrm{H^2}$. Its square root equals $|M|$, so ideal coupling has a coefficient of one. The result is $1$.
