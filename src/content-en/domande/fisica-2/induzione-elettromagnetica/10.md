A rotating coil has $N=10$, $A=0{,}010\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $B=0{,}20\,\mathrm T$ and $\omega=100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the maximum emf?

- $1\,\mathrm V$
- $2\,\mathrm V$
- $4\,\mathrm V$
- $8\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The flux linkage varies as $NBA\cos\omega t$. Differentiating gives an emf of amplitude $NBA\omega$. The result is $2\,\mathrm V$.
