A loop of area $0{,}50\,\mathrm{m^2}$ has its normal pointing in the same direction as a uniform field increasing at $\dfrac{dB}{dt}=2\,\mathrm{T/s}$. What is the emf oriented according to the right-hand rule with respect to this normal?

- $1\,\mathrm V$
- $-2\,\mathrm V$
- $-1\,\mathrm V$
- $2\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The oriented flux is $\Phi=BA$. Faraday's law gives $\mathcal E=-A\,\dfrac{dB}{dt}$, so the sign is negative. The result is $-1\,\mathrm V$.
