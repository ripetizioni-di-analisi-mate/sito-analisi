A uniform magnetic field perpendicular to a flat coil changes linearly with time. The coil has $30$ turns, each of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Given the magnitude of the field change $b = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm T$ and the magnitude of the induced electromotive force $U_{e} = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm V$, what is the duration of the change $t$?

- $\dfrac{1}{150}\,\mathrm s$
- $5\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The flux through each turn changes by $Ab$. By Faraday's law, the magnitude of the emf is the change in flux linkage divided by the duration. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = \dfrac{3 \cdot b}{5 \cdot t}$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{3 \cdot b}{5 \cdot U_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm s$.
