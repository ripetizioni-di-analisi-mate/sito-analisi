An ideal alternator has $50$ turns of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The coil rotates uniformly about an axis in the plane of the turns and perpendicular to the uniform magnetic field. Initially, the magnitude of the magnetic field $B = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the angular velocity $\omega = 50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ are given. In the second configuration, the angular velocity becomes $100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The flux linkage is sinusoidal with amplitude $NBA$. Differentiating it with respect to time multiplies the emf amplitude by the angular velocity. Applying $U_{e} = B \cdot \omega$ gives $20\,\mathrm V$ before the change and $40\,\mathrm V$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
