An RL circuit with $R=4\,\Omega$ is powered by an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ battery. What is the steady-state current?

- $3\,\mathrm A$
- $1{,}5\,\mathrm A$
- $6\,\mathrm A$
- $12\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

In the DC steady state, we have $\dfrac{dI}{dt}=0$, so the self-induced emf is zero. The relation $I_\infty=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R}$ remains. The result is $3\,\mathrm A$.
