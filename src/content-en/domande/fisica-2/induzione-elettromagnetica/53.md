A rod of length $0{,}5\,\mathrm m$ translates along two rails. The uniform magnetic field is perpendicular to the plane of the rails. The rod is perpendicular to the velocity. Given the magnitude of the magnetic field $B = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the magnitude of the motional emf $U_{e} = \dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$, what is the speed $v$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The component of $\vec v\times\vec B$ along the rod has magnitude $vB$. Integrating it along the rod gives $vBL$. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = \dfrac{B \cdot v}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot U_{e}}{B}$. With the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
