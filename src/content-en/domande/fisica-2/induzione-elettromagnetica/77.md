Two coupled coils have constant mutual inductance. The current in the first varies linearly with time. The secondary circuit is open. Given the magnitude of the derivative of the primary current $b = 6\,\mathrm{A/s}$ and the magnitude of the emf in the second coil $U_{e} = \dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$, what is the mutual inductance $M$?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm H$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm H$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm H$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm H$

## Explanation

The flux linkage in the second coil due to the first is $MI_1$. The magnitude of its derivative is $M\lvert dI_1/dt\rvert$. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = M \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $M = \dfrac{U_{e}}{b}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm H$.
