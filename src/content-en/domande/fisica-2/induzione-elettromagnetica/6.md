A circuit with total resistance $2\,\Omega$ has a constant induced emf of magnitude $0{,}60\,\mathrm V$. Neglecting self-induction, what current flows?

- $0{,}15\,\mathrm A$
- $0{,}3\,\mathrm A$
- $0{,}6\,\mathrm A$
- $1{,}2\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

In the purely resistive circuit, $I=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R}$ holds. The stated resistance includes the entire circuit. The result is $0{,}3\,\mathrm A$.
