The inductance of an ideal solenoid is constant. The field energy is calculated with a steady current. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the current $I_{e} = 5\,\mathrm{A}$, what is the stored energy $U$?

- $10\,\mathrm J$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm J$
- $5\,\mathrm J$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The source must supply work while the current increases. Integrating $L_e I\,dI$ gives $L_e I_e^2/2$. In the stated units, we have $U = \dfrac{I_{e}^{2} \cdot L_{e}}{2}$. With the given values, we obtain $5\,\mathrm J$.
