An inductor has flux linkage $0{,}12\,\mathrm{Wb}$ when the current is $3\,\mathrm A$. What is its inductance in the linear regime?

- $0{,}04\,\mathrm H$
- $0{,}02\,\mathrm H$
- $0{,}08\,\mathrm H$
- $0{,}16\,\mathrm H$

## Explanation

In the linear regime the flux linkage is $\Psi=LI$. Thus we obtain $L=\dfrac{\Psi}{I}$. The result is $0{,}04\,\mathrm H$.
