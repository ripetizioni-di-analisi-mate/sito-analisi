An ideal source is switched on in an RL circuit with finite resistance and initially no current. What is the current immediately after switching on?

- $1\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $0\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

With finite voltage, the current in the inductor cannot jump. It therefore starts from its initial value of zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm A$.
