A conducting loop leaves a region with a uniform field directed into the page. As it leaves, the field remains constant and the area of the loop immersed in the field decreases. Neglect self-induction. What does the reader observe?

- A counterclockwise current while the loop is leaving, zero once it is entirely outside.
- A constant clockwise current even when the loop is entirely outside.
- Zero current while the loop is leaving because the field has constant magnitude.
- A clockwise current while the loop is leaving, zero once it is entirely outside.

## Explanation

As the loop leaves, the flux into the page through it decreases. The induced current produces a field into the page to oppose the decrease, so it is clockwise. Once the loop has left, the flux is constantly zero and the current is zero in the stated model.
