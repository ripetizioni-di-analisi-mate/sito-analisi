A $0{,}20\,\mathrm H$ inductor has voltage $V_L=0{,}60\,\mathrm V$ in the passive sign convention. What is $\dfrac{dI}{dt}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{A/s}$

## Explanation

In the passive sign convention, $V_L=L\,\dfrac{dI}{dt}$ holds. Thus the derivative of the current is $\dfrac{V_L}{L}$. The result is $3\,\mathrm{A/s}$.
