A series RL circuit discharges through the resistor alone after the source is disconnected. The current falls to one quarter of its initial value. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 3\,\Omega$ and the required time $t = \dfrac{2 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}{5}\,\mathrm s$, what is the inductance $L_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}{10}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

The time constant is $L_e/R_e$ and the remaining fraction of the current is $e^{-tR_e/L_e}$. Setting this fraction equal to $1/4$ gives the logarithm of four. In the stated units, we have $t = \log{\left(4^{\dfrac{L_{e}}{R_{e}}} \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $L_{e} = \dfrac{R_{e} \cdot t}{\log{\left(4 \right)}}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$.
