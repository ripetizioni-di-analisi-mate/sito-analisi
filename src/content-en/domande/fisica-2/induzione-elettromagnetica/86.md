A coil carries a current increasing at a constant rate. Its inductance is fixed. Given the rate of increase of the current $b = 8\,\mathrm{A/s}$ and the magnitude of the self-induced emf $U_{e} = \dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm V$, what is the inductance $L_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

For self-induction, we have $\mathcal E=-L_e\,dI/dt$. The sign indicates that the emf opposes the increase in current. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = L_{e} \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $L_{e} = \dfrac{U_{e}}{b}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{H}$.
