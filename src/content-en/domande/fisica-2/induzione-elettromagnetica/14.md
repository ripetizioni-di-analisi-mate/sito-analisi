A coil's flux linkage rises from $15$ to $19\,\mathrm{mWb}$ as its current rises from $2$ to $3\,\mathrm A$. The relationship is linear over this interval and a constant external flux is also present. What is the incremental inductance?

- $7.5\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $19\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $15\,\mathrm{mH}$

## Explanation

Incremental inductance is the slope $\Delta\lambda/\Delta I$. The constant external flux cancels in the difference, giving $\dfrac{19-15}{3-2}=4$ millihenries.
