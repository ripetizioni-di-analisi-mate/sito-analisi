A linear $0{,}20\,\mathrm H$ inductor carries current $3\,\mathrm A$. What is the flux linkage?

- $0{,}3\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0{,}6\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $1{,}2\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $2{,}4\,\mathrm{Wb}$

## Explanation

The linear relation is $\Psi=LI$. The inductance is constant in the given model. The result is $0{,}6\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
