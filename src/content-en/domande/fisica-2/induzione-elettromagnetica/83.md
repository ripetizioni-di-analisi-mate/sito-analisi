A rod of length $0{,}5\,\mathrm m$ slides on rails in a magnetic field perpendicular to the plane. The total resistance is $2\,\Omega$. Friction and self-induction are neglected. Given the speed $v = 5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnitude of the magnetic braking force $F = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm N$, what is the magnitude of the magnetic field $B$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{8}{25}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{2 \cdot \sqrt{2}}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The emf is $BLv$ and the current is $BLv/R$. The force on the rod is $BLI$, directed against the motion. In the stated units, we have $F = \dfrac{B^{2} \cdot v}{8}$. Solving for the unknown gives $B = \dfrac{2 \cdot \sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{F}}{\sqrt{v}}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$.
