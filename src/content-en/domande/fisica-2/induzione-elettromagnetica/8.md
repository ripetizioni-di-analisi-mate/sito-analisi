A rod is kept moving at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ against a magnetic braking force of $0{,}060\,\mathrm N$. What mechanical power is supplied?

- $0{,}09\,\mathrm W$
- $0{,}36\,\mathrm W$
- $0{,}72\,\mathrm W$
- $0{,}18\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

At constant velocity the external force balances the braking force. The power supplied is therefore $P=Fv$. The result is $0{,}18\,\mathrm W$.
