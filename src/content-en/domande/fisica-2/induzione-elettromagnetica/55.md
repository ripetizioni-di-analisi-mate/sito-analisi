A rod of length $0{,}5\,\mathrm m$ translates along two rails. The uniform magnetic field is perpendicular to the plane of the rails. The rod is perpendicular to the velocity. Initially, the magnitude of the magnetic field $B = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the speed $v = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ are given. In the second configuration, the magnitude of the magnetic field becomes $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the speed becomes $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $U_{e}$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $- \dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $- \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The component of $\vec v\times\vec B$ along the rod has magnitude $vB$. Integrating it along the rod gives $vBL$. Applying $U_{e} = \dfrac{B \cdot v}{2}$ gives $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$ before the change and $1\,\mathrm V$ after it. The final value minus the initial value is $- \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm V$.
