An ideal alternator has $50$ turns of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The coil rotates uniformly about an axis in the plane of the turns and perpendicular to the uniform magnetic field. Given the angular velocity $\omega = 50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the amplitude of the induced emf $U_{e} = 20\,\mathrm V$, what is the magnitude of the magnetic field $B$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The flux linkage is sinusoidal with amplitude $NBA$. Differentiating it with respect to time multiplies the emf amplitude by the angular velocity. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = B \cdot \omega$. Solving for the unknown gives $B = \dfrac{U_{e}}{\omega}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$.
