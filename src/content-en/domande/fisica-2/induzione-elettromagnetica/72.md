A toroid in air has $500$ turns wound on a rectangular cross section: the inner radius is $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, the outer radius is $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the height is $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Inside the winding, at distance $r$ from the axis, the field is $B = \dfrac{\mu_0 N i}{2\pi r}$. What is the inductance of the toroid?

- $2.2\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $6.9\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $0.55\,\mathrm{mH}$

## Explanation

The field is not uniform over the cross section, so the flux through one turn is computed by dividing the cross section into strips of height $h$ and width $dr$:
$$
\Phi_1 = \int_a^b \frac{\mu_0 N i}{2\pi r}\,h\,dr = \frac{\mu_0 N i h}{2\pi} \ln\frac{b}{a} .
$$
The flux linkage with the $N$ turns is $N \Phi_1$, therefore by the definition of **inductance**
$$
L = \frac{\mu_0 N^2 h}{2\pi} \ln\frac{b}{a} = 2 \cdot 10^{-7} \cdot 500^2 \cdot 0.020 \cdot \ln 3\,\mathrm{H} \approx 1.1 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{H} .
$$
The value $6.9\,\mathrm{mH}$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $2\pi$ in the field.
