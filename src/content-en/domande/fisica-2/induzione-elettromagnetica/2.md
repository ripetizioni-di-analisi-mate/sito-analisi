A coil of $100$ identical turns experiences a decrease in flux per turn of $3\,\mathrm{mWb}$ in $0{,}10\,\mathrm s$. What is the magnitude of the average emf?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm V$
- $3\,\mathrm V$
- $6\,\mathrm V$
- $12\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The flux linkage is $N\Phi$. Thus the magnitude of the average emf is $\dfrac{N|\Delta\Phi|}{\Delta t}$. The result is $3\,\mathrm V$.
