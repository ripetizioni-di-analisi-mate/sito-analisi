A coil carries a current increasing at a constant rate. Its inductance is fixed. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the rate of increase of the current $b = 8\,\mathrm{A/s}$, what is the magnitude of the self-induced emf $U_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{24}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

For self-induction, we have $\mathcal E=-L_e\,dI/dt$. The sign indicates that the emf opposes the increase in current. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = L_{e} \cdot b$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm V$.
