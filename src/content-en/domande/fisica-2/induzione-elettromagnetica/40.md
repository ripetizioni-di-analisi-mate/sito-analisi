What constraint on the direction of the induced current does Lenz's law express?

- The induced effect always cancels the external field
- The current always follows the direction of the field
- The current destroys the charge in the circuit
- The induced effect opposes the change in flux

## Explanation

Lenz's law concerns the change in flux linkage. An induced field may point in the same direction as the external field if the latter is decreasing. This direction prevents spontaneous energy production.
