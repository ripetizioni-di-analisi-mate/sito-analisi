A coil carries a current increasing at a constant rate. Its inductance is fixed. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the magnitude of the self-induced emf $U_{e} = \dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm V$, what is the rate of increase of the current $b$?

- $8\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{A/s}$

## Explanation

For self-induction, we have $\mathcal E=-L_e\,dI/dt$. The sign indicates that the emf opposes the increase in current. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = L_{e} \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{U_{e}}{L_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $8\,\mathrm{A/s}$.
