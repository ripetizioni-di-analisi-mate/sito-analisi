An alternator consists of a coil of $100$ turns of area $0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$ rotating at $50$ revolutions per second in a uniform magnetic field of $0.50\,\mathrm{T}$, perpendicular to the axis of rotation. The ends of the coil are connected to a resistor of $50\,\Omega$, and the resistance of the coil is negligible. What is the average power dissipated in the resistor?

- $2.5 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{W}$
- $4.9 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.2 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{W}$
- $6.3\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega = 2\pi \cdot 50\,\mathrm{rad/s} \approx 314\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, so the maximum value of the emf is
$$
\mathcal{E}_{\max} = N B A \omega = 100 \cdot 0.50 \cdot 0.010 \cdot 314\,\mathrm{V} \approx 157\,\mathrm{V} .
$$
The instantaneous power is $\dfrac{\mathcal{E}_{\max}^2}{R} \sin^2(\omega t)$, and the average value of $\sin^2(\omega t)$ over a period is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the average power is $\dfrac{\mathcal{E}_{\max}^2}{2 R} = \dfrac{157^2}{100}\,\mathrm{W} \approx 2.5 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{W}$. The value $4.9 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{W}$ is the maximum instantaneous power.
