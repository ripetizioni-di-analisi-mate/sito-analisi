In a $2\,\mathrm H$ inductor the current increases at $3\,\mathrm{A/s}$. What is the self-induced emf oriented in the positive direction of the current?

- $6\,\mathrm V$
- $-3\,\mathrm V$
- $-6\,\mathrm V$
- $12\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The self-induced emf is $\mathcal E_L=-L\,\dfrac{dI}{dt}$. It opposes the increase in current. The result is $-6\,\mathrm V$.
