A rod of length $0{,}5\,\mathrm m$ translates along two rails. The uniform magnetic field is perpendicular to the plane of the rails. The rod is perpendicular to the velocity. Initially, the magnitude of the magnetic field $B = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the speed $v = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ are given. In the second configuration, the speed becomes $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U_{e}$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The component of $\vec v\times\vec B$ along the rod has magnitude $vB$. Integrating it along the rod gives $vBL$. Applying $U_{e} = \dfrac{B \cdot v}{2}$ gives $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$ before the change and $\dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm V$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
