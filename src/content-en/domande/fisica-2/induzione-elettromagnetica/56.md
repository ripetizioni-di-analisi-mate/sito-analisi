A field perpendicular to a fixed loop increases uniformly. The area of the loop is $0{,}04\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and self-induction is negligible. Given the rate of increase of the field $b = 5\,\mathrm{T/s}$ and the resistance $R_{e} = 2\,\Omega$, what is the magnitude of the induced current $I_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm A$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm A$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm A$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The emf has magnitude $A\,dB/dt$. To find the current, divide by the resistance of the loop. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{b}{25 \cdot R_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm A$.
