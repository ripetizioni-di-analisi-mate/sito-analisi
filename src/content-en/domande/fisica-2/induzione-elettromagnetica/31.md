The field directed into the page through a loop is increasing. What is the direction of the induced current as seen by the reader?

- Clockwise
- Always zero
- Counterclockwise
- It depends only on the resistance

## Explanation

The induced current must oppose the increase in flux into the page. It therefore produces a field out of the page. The right-hand rule gives a counterclockwise direction.
