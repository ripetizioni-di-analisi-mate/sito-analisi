A rod of length $0{,}50\,\mathrm m$ moves at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to $B=0{,}40\,\mathrm T$. The rod, velocity and field are mutually perpendicular. What is the emf?

- $0{,}6\,\mathrm V$
- $0{,}3\,\mathrm V$
- $1{,}2\,\mathrm V$
- $2{,}4\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The charges in the rod experience the magnetic force. In the given geometry the motional emf has magnitude $\mathcal E=BLv$. The result is $0{,}6\,\mathrm V$.
