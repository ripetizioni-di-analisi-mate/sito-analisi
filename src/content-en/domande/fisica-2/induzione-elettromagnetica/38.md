An ideal inductor is in the DC steady state. What is its voltage?

- Infinite
- Zero
- Always equal to the source emf
- Always equal to $LI$

## Explanation

In the steady state the current is constant. Thus we obtain $\dfrac{dI}{dt}=0$. The voltage $V_L=L\,\dfrac{dI}{dt}$ is zero.
