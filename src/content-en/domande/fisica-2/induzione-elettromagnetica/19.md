An RL circuit has $L=0{,}20\,\mathrm H$ and $R=10\,\Omega$. What is its time constant?

- $0{,}01\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}04\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}02\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}08\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is $\tau=\dfrac{L}{R}$. The unit henry divided by ohm is equivalent to the second. The result is $0{,}02\,\mathrm s$.
