Two coupled coils have constant mutual inductance. The current in the first varies linearly with time. The secondary circuit is open. Given the mutual inductance $M = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm H$ and the magnitude of the derivative of the primary current $b = 6\,\mathrm{A/s}$, what is the magnitude of the emf in the second coil $U_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The flux linkage in the second coil due to the first is $MI_1$. The magnitude of its derivative is $M\lvert dI_1/dt\rvert$. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = M \cdot b$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$.
