A current in an inductor tends to decrease. What is the effect of the self-induced emf?

- It tends to maintain the current in the same direction
- It cancels the current instantaneously
- It reverses the current instantaneously
- It is always zero during the decrease

## Explanation

Self-induction opposes the change in current. If the current decreases, the emf tends to sustain it. The inductor returns energy to the circuit.
