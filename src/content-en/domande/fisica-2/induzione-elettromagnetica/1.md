The flux through a loop changes by $0{,}12\,\mathrm{Wb}$ in $0{,}020\,\mathrm s$. What is the magnitude of the average electromotive force?

- $6\,\mathrm V$
- $3\,\mathrm V$
- $12\,\mathrm V$
- $24\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Faraday's law gives $|\mathcal E_{med}|=\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi|}{\Delta t}$. The flux through a single loop is used. The result is $6\,\mathrm V$.
