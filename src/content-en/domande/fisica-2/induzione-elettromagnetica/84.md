A rod of length $0{,}5\,\mathrm m$ slides on rails in a magnetic field perpendicular to the plane. The total resistance is $2\,\Omega$. Friction and self-induction are neglected. Given the magnitude of the magnetic field $B = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the magnitude of the magnetic braking force $F = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm N$, what is the speed $v$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The emf is $BLv$ and the current is $BLv/R$. The force on the rod is $BLI$, directed against the motion. In the stated units, we have $F = \dfrac{B^{2} \cdot v}{8}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{8 \cdot F}{B^{2}}$. With the given values, we obtain $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
