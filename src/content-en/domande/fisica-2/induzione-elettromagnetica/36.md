What is the relation between induced emf and flux when the circuit contains $N$ identical turns?

- $\mathcal E=-\dfrac{\Phi}{N}$
- $\mathcal E=N\Phi$
- $\mathcal E=\dfrac{d\Phi}{dt^2}$
- $\mathcal E=-N\,\dfrac{d\Phi}{dt}$

## Explanation

The emfs of the turns connected in series add. The flux linkage is $N\Phi$. Differentiating it and changing the sign gives the stated relation.
