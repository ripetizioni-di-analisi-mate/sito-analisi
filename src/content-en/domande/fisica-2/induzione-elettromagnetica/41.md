What property distinguishes an induced electric field from an electrostatic field in a region with changing magnetic flux?

- It can have nonzero circulation
- It is always zero in vacuum
- It is always radial
- It exerts no force on charges

## Explanation

Faraday's law relates the electric field circulation to the derivative of the magnetic flux. If this derivative is nonzero, the circulation is also nonzero. The field then cannot be described solely by a static scalar potential.
