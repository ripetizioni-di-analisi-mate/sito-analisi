An ideal solenoid in vacuum has $N=1000$, area $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and length $1\,\mathrm m$. What is its inductance in mH? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}$.

- $0{,}628\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $1{,}26\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $2{,}51\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $5{,}03\,\mathrm{mH}$

## Explanation

For a long solenoid, we have $L=\dfrac{\mu_0N^2A}{\ell}$. The result is then converted to millihenries. The result is $1{,}26\,\mathrm{mH}$.
