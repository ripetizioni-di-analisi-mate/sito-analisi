A rigid loop translates without rotating, entirely within a region of uniform, constant field. Edge effects are neglected. What is the total emf around the loop?

- It is $BLv$ for every orientation of the loop
- It is zero because the flux does not change
- It is nonzero because every segment moves
- It depends only on the resistance of the loop

## Explanation

The field, area and orientation of the loop remain constant. When calculating around the closed contour, the motional contributions of opposite segments cancel.
