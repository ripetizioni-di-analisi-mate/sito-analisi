A coil makes $25$ revolutions per second in a uniform field, producing a sinusoidal emf. What is the frequency of the emf?

- $12{,}5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $100\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $25\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The flux completes one cycle in one revolution. The induced emf therefore has the same frequency as the rotation. The result is $25\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
