A square loop of side $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ moves with constant velocity $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and crosses a region $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide containing a uniform magnetic field perpendicular to the plane of the loop. At the instant $t = 0$ the front side of the loop enters the region. Between $t = 0$ and $t = 0.20\,\mathrm{s}$, in which time interval is the induced current zero?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="3,0 6,0 6,3 3,3" linee="1,1 2,1 2,2 1,2 1,1" vettori="2.2,1.5,3.0,1.5,v" etichette="3.75,0.6,⊗;5.25,0.6,⊗;3.75,2.4,⊗;5.25,2.4,⊗;4.5,1.5,⊗;1.5,2.35,10 cm;4.5,3.4,30 cm" xmin=0.5 xmax=6.5 ymin=-0.3 ymax=3.8 titolo="Square loop approaching the region of the field $\vec{B}$, pointing into the page"}

- From $0$ to $0.050\,\mathrm{s}$
- From $0.050\,\mathrm{s}$ to $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$
- From $0.050\,\mathrm{s}$ to $0.15\,\mathrm{s}$
- From $0.15\,\mathrm{s}$ to $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The loop is completely inside the region after traveling its own side, that is, at the instant $\dfrac{0.10}{2.0}\,\mathrm{s} = 0.050\,\mathrm{s}$. The front side reaches the opposite boundary after $\dfrac{0.30}{2.0}\,\mathrm{s} = 0.15\,\mathrm{s}$, and the loop is completely out at $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$. Between $0.050\,\mathrm{s}$ and $0.15\,\mathrm{s}$ the loop is entirely in the uniform field, so the flux linkage is constant. By the **Faraday-Neumann-Lenz law** the emf is zero in that interval, and therefore the current is zero as well. During entry and exit the flux changes and current flows, in opposite directions in the two cases.
