The charging current in an RL circuit tends to $8\,\mathrm A$. What is its value at $t=\tau\ln2$?

- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$
- $16\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The relation is $I=I_\infty(1-e^{-\dfrac{t}{\tau}})$. At the given instant the exponential term is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result is $4\,\mathrm A$.
