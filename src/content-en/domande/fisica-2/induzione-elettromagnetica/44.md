In a linear coil, what factors mainly determine the inductance?

- Only the instantaneous current
- Only the sign of the voltage
- Only the external resistance
- Geometry, number of turns and permeability of the medium

## Explanation

The constant $L$ relates flux and current in the linear regime. It depends on the circuit configuration and the magnetic response of the medium. Simply reversing the current does not change it.
