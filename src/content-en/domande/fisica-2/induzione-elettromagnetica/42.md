A moving rod produces current and Joule heat. What source supplies energy when the motion is kept uniform?

- The static magnetic field does net work on the charges
- The work of the external force maintaining the motion
- The resistance creates energy
- The total charge is converted into energy

## Explanation

The magnetic force on the circuit brakes the motion. Maintaining the motion requires an external force. Its work supplies the dissipated energy.
