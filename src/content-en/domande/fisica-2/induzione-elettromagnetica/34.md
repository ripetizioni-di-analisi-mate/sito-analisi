A loop leaves a region with a uniform field. What effect does the magnetic force due to the induced current have on its motion?

- It always assists the loop in leaving
- It opposes the loop leaving
- It is always perpendicular to the plane of the loop
- It cancels the resistance

## Explanation

The flux linkage decreases as the loop leaves the region. The induced current tends to oppose this change. The resulting force therefore brakes the motion.
