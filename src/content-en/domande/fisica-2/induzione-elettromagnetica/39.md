The voltage across the inductor remains finite. Can the current change instantaneously by a finite jump?

- Yes, by any jump
- Only if the inductance is positive
- No, the current remains continuous
- Only if there is no capacitor

## Explanation

The voltage is proportional to the derivative of the current. A finite jump would require an impulsive voltage contribution. With finite voltage, the current is therefore continuous.
