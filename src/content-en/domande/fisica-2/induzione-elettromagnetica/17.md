The current in an inductor of constant inductance doubles. If the initial energy is $3\,\mathrm{mJ}$, what is the final energy?

- $12\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $24\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $48\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

The energy is proportional to the square of the current. Doubling $I$ therefore quadruples $U$. The result is $12\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
