A series RL circuit discharges through the resistor alone after the source is disconnected. The current falls to one quarter of its initial value. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the resistance $R_{e} = 3\,\Omega$, what is the required time $t$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{2 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}{5}\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{4 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}{5}\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{\log{\left(2 \right)}}{5}\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The time constant is $L_e/R_e$ and the remaining fraction of the current is $e^{-tR_e/L_e}$. Setting this fraction equal to $1/4$ gives the logarithm of four. In the stated units, we have $t = \log{\left(4^{\dfrac{L_{e}}{R_{e}}} \right)}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{2 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}{5}\,\mathrm s$.
