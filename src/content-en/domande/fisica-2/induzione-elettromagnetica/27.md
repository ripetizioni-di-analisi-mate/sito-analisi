In a uniform magnetic field in vacuum we have $B=0{,}20\,\mathrm T$. What is $u_B\mu_0$, where $u_B$ is the energy density?

- $0{,}01\,\mathrm{T^2}$
- $0{,}04\,\mathrm{T^2}$
- $0{,}02\,\mathrm{T^2}$
- $0{,}08\,\mathrm{T^2}$

## Explanation

The energy density is $u_B=\dfrac{B^2}{2\mu_0}$. Multiplying by $\mu_0$ leaves $\dfrac{B^2}{2}$. The result is $0{,}02\,\mathrm{T^2}$.
