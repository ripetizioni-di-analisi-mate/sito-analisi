A coil of $50$ flat turns, each of area $40\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, is in a uniform magnetic field of $0.30\,\mathrm{T}$. The normal to the turns makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. What is the magnetic flux linkage of the coil?

- $5.2 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $3.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $6.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $6.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$

## Explanation

For flat turns in a uniform field the flux linkage is $\Phi_B = N B A\cos\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the field and the normal. The area of one turn is $40 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $\cos 60^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore
$$
\Phi_B = 50 \cdot 0.30 \cdot 40 \cdot 10^{-4} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{Wb} = 3.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Wb} .
$$
The value $5.2 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ is obtained by using the sine of the angle, which would be correct only if $60^\circ$ were the angle between the field and the plane of the turns.
