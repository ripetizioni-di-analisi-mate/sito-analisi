The inductance of an ideal solenoid is constant. The field energy is calculated with a steady current. Given the current $I_{e} = 5\,\mathrm{A}$ and the stored energy $U = 5\,\mathrm J$, what is the inductance $L_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

The source must supply work while the current increases. Integrating $L_e I\,dI$ gives $L_e I_e^2/2$. In the stated units, we have $U = \dfrac{I_{e}^{2} \cdot L_{e}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $L_{e} = \dfrac{2 \cdot U}{I_{e}^{2}}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$.
