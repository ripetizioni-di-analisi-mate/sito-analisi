An RL circuit initially carrying no current is connected to $9\,\mathrm V$. What is the initial magnitude of the voltage across the ideal inductor?

- $4{,}5\,\mathrm V$
- $18\,\mathrm V$
- $36\,\mathrm V$
- $9\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The initial current is zero, so the voltage drop across the resistor is zero. The full source voltage initially appears across the inductor. The result is $9\,\mathrm V$.
