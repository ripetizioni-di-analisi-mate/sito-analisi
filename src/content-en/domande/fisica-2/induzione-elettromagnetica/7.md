A rod of length $0{,}50\,\mathrm m$ slides at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on rails in $B=0{,}40\,\mathrm T$. The circuit has $R=2\,\Omega$ and negligible inductive effects. What is the magnitude of the braking force?

- $0{,}03\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}12\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}06\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The induced current is $I=\dfrac{BLv}{R}$. The magnetic force is $F=BIL$, so $F=\dfrac{B^2L^2v}{R}$. The result is $0{,}06\,\mathrm N$.
