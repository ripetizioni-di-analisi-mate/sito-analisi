A resistive coil of $20$ turns has $R=4\,\Omega$. The flux per turn changes by a total of $0{,}020\,\mathrm{Wb}$. Neglecting self-induction, what is the magnitude of the charge that passes through it?

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm C$
- $0{,}05\,\mathrm C$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm C$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Integrating $I=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R}$ over time gives $|\Delta Q|=\dfrac{N|\Delta\Phi|}{R}$. The result depends on the total change in flux. The result is $0{,}1\,\mathrm C$.
