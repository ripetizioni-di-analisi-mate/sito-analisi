The current in an inductor is halved at constant inductance. If the initial energy is $20\,\mathrm{mJ}$, what is the final energy?

- $5\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $2{,}5\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

The magnetic energy is proportional to $I^2$. Halving the current therefore reduces the energy to one quarter. The result is $5\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
