Two identical coils have self-inductance $L$ and positive mutual inductance $M$. Each carries current $I$. Only the second current is reversed, with the magnitudes held constant. By how much does the magnetic energy decrease from the aiding to the opposing configuration?

- $2MI^2$
- $MI^2$
- $LI^2$ for every $M$
- Zero for every $M$

## Explanation

The self-inductance terms add to $LI^2$ in both configurations. The mutual term changes from $+MI^2$ to $-MI^2$, so the decrease is $2MI^2$.
