The inductance of an ideal solenoid is constant. The field energy is calculated with a steady current. Initially, the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the current $I_{e} = 5\,\mathrm{A}$ are given. In the second configuration, the current becomes $10\,\mathrm{A}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U$?

- $1$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The source must supply work while the current increases. Integrating $L_e I\,dI$ gives $L_e I_e^2/2$. Applying $U = \dfrac{I_{e}^{2} \cdot L_{e}}{2}$ gives $5\,\mathrm J$ before the change and $20\,\mathrm J$ after it. The ratio is $4$.
