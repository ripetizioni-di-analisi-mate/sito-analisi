A $0{,}20\,\mathrm H$ inductor carries $3\,\mathrm A$. How much magnetic energy does it store?

- $0{,}45\,\mathrm J$
- $1{,}8\,\mathrm J$
- $3{,}6\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}9\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The magnetic energy is $U=\dfrac{LI^2}{2}$. The current appears squared. The result is $0{,}9\,\mathrm J$.
