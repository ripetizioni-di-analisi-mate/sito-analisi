A uniform magnetic field perpendicular to a flat coil changes linearly with time. The coil has $30$ turns, each of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Initially, the magnitude of the field change $b = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm T$ and its duration $t = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm s$ are given. In the second configuration, the duration of the change becomes $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm s$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U_{e}$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The flux through each turn changes by $Ab$. By Faraday's law, the magnitude of the emf is the change in flux linkage divided by the duration. Applying $U_{e} = \dfrac{3 \cdot b}{5 \cdot t}$ gives $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm V$ before the change and $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm V$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
