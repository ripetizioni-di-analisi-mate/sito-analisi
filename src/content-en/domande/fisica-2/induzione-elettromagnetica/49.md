The magnetic flux linkage of a circuit, oriented with the right-hand rule, varies in time as shown in the figure. What is the oriented emf $\mathcal{E}$ in the intervals $0 < t < 2\,\mathrm{s}$, $2\,\mathrm{s} < t < 4\,\mathrm{s}$ and $4\,\mathrm{s} < t < 5\,\mathrm{s}$, in this order?

::grafico{linee="0,0 2,6 4,6 5,0" segmenti="0,6,2,6" punti="2,6,pieno;4,6,pieno" nomi="t,Φ" xmin=-0.5 xmax=6.5 ymin=-1 ymax=7.5 titolo="Flux linkage $\Phi_B$, in hundredths of a weber, as a function of the time $t$, in seconds"}

- $-0.030\,\mathrm{V}$, $0$, $+0.060\,\mathrm{V}$
- $+0.030\,\mathrm{V}$, $0$, $-0.060\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-0.030\,\mathrm{V}$, $-0.060\,\mathrm{V}$, $+0.060\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-0.060\,\mathrm{V}$, $0$, $+0.060\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

By the **Faraday-Neumann-Lenz law** $\mathcal{E} = -\dfrac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$, and in each interval the graph is a segment, so the emf is constant and equal to the opposite of the slope. In the first interval the flux increases by $0.060\,\mathrm{Wb}$ in $2\,\mathrm{s}$, therefore $\mathcal{E} = -0.030\,\mathrm{V}$. In the second interval the flux is constant, so $\mathcal{E} = 0$. In the third the flux decreases by $0.060\,\mathrm{Wb}$ in $1\,\mathrm{s}$, therefore $\mathcal{E} = +0.060\,\mathrm{V}$. The emf depends on how fast the flux changes, and for this reason it is zero where the flux is maximum but constant.
