A series RL circuit discharges through the resistor alone after the source is disconnected. The current falls to one quarter of its initial value. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the required time $t = \dfrac{2 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}{5}\,\mathrm s$, what is the resistance $R_{e}$?

- $6\,\Omega$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\Omega$
- $\dfrac{6}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}\,\Omega$
- $3\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The time constant is $L_e/R_e$ and the remaining fraction of the current is $e^{-tR_e/L_e}$. Setting this fraction equal to $1/4$ gives the logarithm of four. In the stated units, we have $t = \log{\left(4^{\dfrac{L_{e}}{R_{e}}} \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = \dfrac{2 \cdot L_{e} \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}{t}$. With the given values, we obtain $3\,\Omega$.
