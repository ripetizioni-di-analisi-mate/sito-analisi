The oriented flux through a loop is $\Phi(t)=3t^2$ in SI units. What is the oriented emf at $t=2\,\mathrm s$?

- $12\,\mathrm V$
- $-6\,\mathrm V$
- $6\,\mathrm V$
- $-12\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The emf is the negative of the derivative of the flux. Thus we obtain $\mathcal E(t)=-6t$. The result is $-12\,\mathrm V$.
