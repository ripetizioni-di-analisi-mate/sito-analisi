A coil of inductance $L$ is rebuilt with twice as many turns and the same area, length and medium, in the ideal-solenoid model. What is the ratio of the final inductance to the initial inductance?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

For the solenoid, $L=\dfrac{\mu N^2A}{\ell}$. The number of turns appears squared, so doubling it quadruples the inductance. The result is $4$.
