A rigid loop translates entirely within a uniform, constant field, maintaining its area and orientation. What is the total induced emf?

- Zero
- Always $BLv$, whatever the circuit
- Always increasing with time
- Determined only by the perimeter

## Explanation

The flux through the loop remains constant. Faraday's law therefore gives zero total emf. The local emfs on opposite sides cancel.
