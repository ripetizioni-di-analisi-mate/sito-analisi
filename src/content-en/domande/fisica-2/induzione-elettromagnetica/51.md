A rod of length $0{,}5\,\mathrm m$ translates along two rails. The uniform magnetic field is perpendicular to the plane of the rails. The rod is perpendicular to the velocity. Given the magnitude of the magnetic field $B = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the speed $v = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the magnitude of the motional emf $U_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The component of $\vec v\times\vec B$ along the rod has magnitude $vB$. Integrating it along the rod gives $vBL$. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = \dfrac{B \cdot v}{2}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm V$.
