An ideal alternator has $50$ turns of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The coil rotates uniformly about an axis in the plane of the turns and perpendicular to the uniform magnetic field. Given the magnitude of the magnetic field $B = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the angular velocity $\omega = 50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, what is the amplitude of the induced emf $U_{e}$?

- $20\,\mathrm V$
- $30\,\mathrm V$
- $10\,\mathrm V$
- $40\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The flux linkage is sinusoidal with amplitude $NBA$. Differentiating it with respect to time multiplies the emf amplitude by the angular velocity. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = B \cdot \omega$. With the given values, we obtain $20\,\mathrm V$.
