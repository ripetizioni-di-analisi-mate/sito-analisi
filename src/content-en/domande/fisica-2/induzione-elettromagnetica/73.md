An ideal alternator has $50$ turns of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The coil rotates uniformly about an axis in the plane of the turns and perpendicular to the uniform magnetic field. Given the magnitude of the magnetic field $B = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the amplitude of the induced emf $U_{e} = 20\,\mathrm V$, what is the angular velocity $\omega$?

- $100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $75\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The flux linkage is sinusoidal with amplitude $NBA$. Differentiating it with respect to time multiplies the emf amplitude by the angular velocity. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = B \cdot \omega$. Solving for the unknown gives $\omega = \dfrac{U_{e}}{B}$. With the given values, we obtain $50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
