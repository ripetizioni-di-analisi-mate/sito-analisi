A loop rotates uniformly in a constant field. At the instant when its flux is at its positive maximum, what is the instantaneous emf?

- $1\,\mathrm V$
- $2\,\mathrm V$
- $0\,\mathrm V$
- $4\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

At the maximum of the flux, the time derivative is zero. Faraday's law relates the emf to the negative of this derivative. The result is $0\,\mathrm V$.
