Two coils have mutual inductance $M=0{,}040\,\mathrm H$. The primary current increases at $5\,\mathrm{A/s}$. What is the magnitude of the secondary emf?

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm V$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm V$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm V$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the mutual emf is $|\mathcal E_2|=|M\,\dfrac{dI_1}{dt|}$. The winding orientation determines its sign, not its magnitude. The result is $0{,}2\,\mathrm V$.
