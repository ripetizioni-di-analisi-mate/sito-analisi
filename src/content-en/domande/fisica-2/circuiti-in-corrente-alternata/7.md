An ideal $15\,\Omega$ resistor is supplied with sinusoidal AC. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $7{,}5\,\Omega$
- $30\,\Omega$
- $15\,\Omega$
- $60\,\Omega$

## Explanation

For an ideal resistor the impedance is real and equals $Z=R$. Its magnitude equals the resistance and is independent of frequency. The result is $15\,\Omega$.
