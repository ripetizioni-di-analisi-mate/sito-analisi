In a free series RLC circuit with $R<2\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}$, what regime occurs?

- Critical damping
- Damped oscillations
- An overdamped regime without oscillations
- Oscillations of increasing amplitude without a source

## Explanation

The discriminant of the characteristic equation is negative. The solutions contain a sinusoid multiplied by a decaying exponential. The regime is therefore underdamped.
