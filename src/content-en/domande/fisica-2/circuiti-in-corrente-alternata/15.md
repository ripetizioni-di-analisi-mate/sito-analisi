A series RLC circuit has $R=12\,\Omega$ and operates at current resonance. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $6\,\Omega$
- $24\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $48\,\Omega$

## Explanation

At resonance the inductive and capacitive reactances cancel. Only the real part $R$ remains. The result is $12\,\Omega$.
