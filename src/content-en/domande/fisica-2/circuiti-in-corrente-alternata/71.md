An ideal transformer has $200$ turns in the primary. A sinusoidal voltage is applied to the primary and the rms voltage at the secondary is measured. Given the rms primary voltage $U_{1} = 120\,\mathrm V$ and the number of secondary turns $N = 50$, what is the rms secondary voltage $U_{e}$?

- $30\,\mathrm V$
- $60\,\mathrm V$
- $480\,\mathrm V$
- $15\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The flux per turn is shared by both windings. By Faraday's law the voltages are proportional to the number of turns. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = \dfrac{N \cdot U_{1}}{200}$. With the given values, we obtain $30\,\mathrm V$.
