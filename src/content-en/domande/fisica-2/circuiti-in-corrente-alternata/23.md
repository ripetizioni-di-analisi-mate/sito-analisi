An ideal transformer has $\dfrac{N_2}{N_1}=4$ and rms primary voltage $12\,\mathrm V$. What is the rms secondary voltage?

- $24\,\mathrm V$
- $96\,\mathrm V$
- $48\,\mathrm V$
- $192\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

In an ideal transformer, we have $\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}=\dfrac{N_2}{N_1}$. Thus the secondary voltage is four times the primary voltage. The result is $48\,\mathrm V$.
