An ideal inductor is supplied with a sinusoidal voltage. Rms values are used and the frequency is $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the rms current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm A$, what is the rms voltage $U_{e}$?

- $120 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm V$
- $30 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm V$
- $60\,\mathrm V$
- $60 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $2\pi fL$. The rms voltage is its product with the rms current. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = 100 \cdot \pi \cdot I_{e} \cdot L_{e}$. With the given values, we obtain $60 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm V$.
