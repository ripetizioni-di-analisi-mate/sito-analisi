A series $RLC$ circuit without a source has quality factor $Q = \dfrac{\omega_0 L}{R} = 50$ and oscillates with light damping. Approximately what fraction of its energy does it dissipate during one period of oscillation?

- $12\%$
- $2\%$
- $6\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

With light damping the energy decreases as $U(t) \approx U_0\,e^{-\frac{R}{L} t}$, and the period is about $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega_0}$.

After one period the energy is therefore $U_0\,e^{-\frac{R}{L} \cdot \frac{2\pi}{\omega_0}} = U_0\,e^{-\frac{2\pi}{Q}}$. The dissipated fraction is therefore
$$
1 - e^{-\frac{2\pi}{50}} \approx 0.12 ,
$$
that is, about $12\%$.
