An ideal LC circuit has $L=0{,}25\,\mathrm H$ and $C=100\,\mu\mathrm F$. What is its natural angular frequency?

- $200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $400\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $800\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The natural angular frequency is $\omega_0=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$. The capacitance must be expressed in farads. The result is $200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
