A free series RLC circuit has $R=4\,\Omega$ and $L=2\,\mathrm H$. What is the damping coefficient $\alpha=\dfrac{R}{2L}$?

- $0{,}5\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The dissipative term determines $\alpha=\dfrac{R}{2L}$. The coefficient has dimensions of inverse time. The result is $1\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
