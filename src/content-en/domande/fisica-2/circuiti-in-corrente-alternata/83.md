A sinusoidal load has a positive power factor. The active and apparent power are known. Given the apparent power $S = 600\,\mathrm{VA}$ and the power factor $b = \dfrac{4}{5}$, what is the active power $P$?

- $480\,\mathrm W$
- $720\,\mathrm W$
- $960\,\mathrm W$
- $240\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The apparent power is the product of the rms values. Multiplying it by the power factor gives the active power. In the stated units, we have $b = \dfrac{P}{S}$. Solving for the unknown gives $P = S \cdot b$. With the given values, we obtain $480\,\mathrm W$.
