A series RLC circuit has quality factor $Q=5$ and rms source voltage $10\,\mathrm V$. At resonance, what is the rms voltage across the inductor?

- $50\,\mathrm V$
- $25\,\mathrm V$
- $100\,\mathrm V$
- $200\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

At resonance, we have $V_{L,eff}=I_{eff}\omega_0L$. Using $I_{eff}=\dfrac{V_{eff}}{R}$ gives $V_{L,eff}=QV_{eff}$. The result is $50\,\mathrm V$.
