An ideal capacitor supplied in the sinusoidal regime has capacitance $20\,\mu\mathrm F$. Its voltage changes from zero to $100\,\mathrm V$. How much energy does it receive during this interval?

- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}05\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}20\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}10\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The capacitor's energy is $U=CV^2/2$ at every instant. Between the two instants it increases from zero to $\dfrac{20\cdot10^{-6}\cdot100^2}{2}\,\mathrm J=0{,}10\,\mathrm J$. The balance over a complete period would instead be zero.
