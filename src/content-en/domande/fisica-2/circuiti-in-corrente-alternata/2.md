An ideal LC circuit oscillates with angular frequency $200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is its period?

- $0{,}0157\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}0314\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}0628\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}126\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The period is related to the angular frequency by $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega_0}$. We therefore divide $2\pi$ by two hundred. The result is $0{,}0314\,\mathrm s$.
