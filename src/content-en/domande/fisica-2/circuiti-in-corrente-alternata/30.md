A series RLC circuit has $R=30\,\Omega$ and impedance of magnitude $50\,\Omega$. What is its power factor?

- $0{,}8$
- $0{,}6$
- $1{,}67$
- $0{,}2$

## Explanation

For the series circuit, $\cos\varphi=\dfrac{R}{|Z|}$ holds. The resistive part determines the active power. The result is $0{,}6$.
