An ideal inductor is supplied with a sinusoidal voltage in the steady state. What is the average power absorbed over one period?

- $1\,\mathrm W$
- $2\,\mathrm W$
- $0\,\mathrm W$
- $4\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Voltage and current have a phase difference of $90^\circ$. The power factor is zero, so the inductor exchanges energy without absorbing a net amount over the period. The result is $0\,\mathrm W$.
