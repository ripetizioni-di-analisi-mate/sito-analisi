An ideal transformer transfers average power $60\,\mathrm W$ to a resistive load. What average power does it absorb at the primary?

- $60\,\mathrm W$
- $30\,\mathrm W$
- $120\,\mathrm W$
- $240\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

There are no losses in the ideal model. The average input power therefore equals the power transferred to the load. The result is $60\,\mathrm W$.
