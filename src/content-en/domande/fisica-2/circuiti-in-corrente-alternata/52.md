An ideal capacitor is connected to a sinusoidal voltage. Rms values are considered and the capacitance is $100\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. Given the rms voltage $U_{e} = 20\,\mathrm V$ and the rms current $I_{e} = 200 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$, what is the frequency $f$?

- $50 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $25\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $100\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $1/(2\pi f C)$. Dividing the voltage by this reactance and converting to milliamperes gives the required value. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{\pi \cdot U_{e} \cdot f}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $f = \dfrac{5 \cdot I_{e}}{\pi \cdot U_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
