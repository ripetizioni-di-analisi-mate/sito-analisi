An ideal transformer has $200$ turns in the primary. A sinusoidal voltage is applied to the primary and the rms voltage at the secondary is measured. Given the number of secondary turns $N = 50$ and the rms secondary voltage $U_{e} = 30\,\mathrm V$, what is the rms primary voltage $U_{1}$?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm V$
- $240\,\mathrm V$
- $60\,\mathrm V$
- $120\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The flux per turn is shared by both windings. By Faraday's law the voltages are proportional to the number of turns. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = \dfrac{N \cdot U_{1}}{200}$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{1} = \dfrac{200 \cdot U_{e}}{N}$. With the given values, we obtain $120\,\mathrm V$.
