A resistor of resistance $50\,\Omega$ and a capacitor of capacitance $20\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ are connected in series and supplied at angular frequency $\omega = 1.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the complex impedance of the combination?

- $(50 - 50j)\,\Omega$
- $(50 + 50j)\,\Omega$
- $(50 - 8.0j)\,\Omega$
- $(50 - 0.020j)\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The complex impedance of the capacitor is $Z_C = -\dfrac{j}{\omega C}$. Since $\omega C = 1.0 \cdot 10^3 \cdot 20 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\Omega^{-1} = 0.020\,\Omega^{-1}$, we have $Z_C = -50j\,\Omega$.

For components in series the complex impedances add, therefore $Z = (50 - 50j)\,\Omega$. The imaginary part is negative, so the circuit is capacitive.
