An ideal transformer has $200$ turns in the primary. A sinusoidal voltage is applied to the primary and the rms voltage at the secondary is measured. Initially, the rms primary voltage $U_{1} = 120\,\mathrm V$ and the number of secondary turns $N = 50$ are given. In the second configuration, the number of secondary turns becomes $100$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The flux per turn is shared by both windings. By Faraday's law the voltages are proportional to the number of turns. Applying $U_{e} = \dfrac{N \cdot U_{1}}{200}$ gives $30\,\mathrm V$ before the change and $60\,\mathrm V$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
