A series RLC circuit has $R=30\,\Omega$, $X_L=60\,\Omega$ and $X_C=20\,\Omega$. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $10\,\Omega$
- $50\,\Omega$
- $70\,\Omega$
- $110\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The total reactance is $X_L-X_C=40\,\Omega$. The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The result is $50\,\Omega$.
