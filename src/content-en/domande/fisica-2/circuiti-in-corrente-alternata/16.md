A series RLC circuit is at resonance, with rms voltage $24\,\mathrm V$ and $R=8\,\Omega$. What is the rms current?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm A$
- $6\,\mathrm A$
- $12\,\mathrm A$
- $3\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

At resonance, we have $|Z|=R$. Thus we obtain $I_{eff}=\dfrac{V_{eff}}{R}$. The result is $3\,\mathrm A$.
