An ideal transformer supplies a resistor. The ratio of secondary turns to primary turns is $4$. Given the secondary resistance $R_{e} = 60\,\Omega$ and the rms primary current $I_{e} = 8\,\mathrm A$, what is the rms primary voltage $U_{1}$?

- $30\,\mathrm V$
- $120\,\mathrm V$
- $480\,\mathrm V$
- $60\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The secondary receives four times the primary voltage. Since the transformer is ideal, the primary draws four times the secondary current. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{16 \cdot U_{1}}{R_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{1} = \dfrac{I_{e} \cdot R_{e}}{16}$. With the given values, we obtain $30\,\mathrm V$.
