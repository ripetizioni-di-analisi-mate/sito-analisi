An ideal transformer has $200$ turns in the primary. A sinusoidal voltage is applied to the primary and the rms voltage at the secondary is measured. Given the rms primary voltage $U_{1} = 120\,\mathrm V$ and the rms secondary voltage $U_{e} = 30\,\mathrm V$, what is the number of secondary turns $N$?

- $800$
- $100$
- $50$
- $25$

## Explanation

The flux per turn is shared by both windings. By Faraday's law the voltages are proportional to the number of turns. In the stated units, we have $U_{e} = \dfrac{N \cdot U_{1}}{200}$. Solving for the unknown gives $N = \dfrac{200 \cdot U_{e}}{U_{1}}$. With the given values, we obtain $50$.
