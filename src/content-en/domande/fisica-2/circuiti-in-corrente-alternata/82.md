A sinusoidal load has a positive power factor. The active and apparent power are known. Given the active power $P = 480\,\mathrm W$ and the apparent power $S = 600\,\mathrm{VA}$, what is the power factor $b$?

- $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2 \cdot \sqrt{5}}{5}$

## Explanation

The apparent power is the product of the rms values. Multiplying it by the power factor gives the active power. In the stated units, we have $b = \dfrac{P}{S}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{4}{5}$.
