A series $RLC$ circuit is supplied at the angular frequency $\omega_+$, the upper edge of the bandwidth. What is the phase angle between voltage and current?

- $45^\circ$, with the current leading
- $90^\circ$, with the current lagging
- $0^\circ$, as at resonance
- $45^\circ$, with the current lagging

## Explanation

At the edges of the bandwidth the impedance equals $\sqrt{2}\,R$, so the net reactance $X_L - X_C$ equals $\pm R$. At the upper edge the angular frequency exceeds the resonant one, therefore $X_L > X_C$ and $X_L - X_C = R$.

It follows that $\tan\varphi = \dfrac{X_L - X_C}{R} = 1$, that is, $\varphi = 45^\circ$. Since $\varphi > 0$, the circuit is inductive and the current **lags** the voltage.
