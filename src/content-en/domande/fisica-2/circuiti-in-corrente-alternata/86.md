An ideal transformer supplies a resistor. The ratio of secondary turns to primary turns is $4$. Given the rms primary current $I_{e} = 8\,\mathrm A$ and the rms primary voltage $U_{1} = 30\,\mathrm V$, what is the secondary resistance $R_{e}$?

- $15\,\Omega$
- $60\,\Omega$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}\,\Omega$
- $120\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The secondary receives four times the primary voltage. Since the transformer is ideal, the primary draws four times the secondary current. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{16 \cdot U_{1}}{R_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = \dfrac{16 \cdot U_{1}}{I_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $60\,\Omega$.
