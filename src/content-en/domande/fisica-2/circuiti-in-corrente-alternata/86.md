A series $RLC$ circuit with $R = 40\,\Omega$ is supplied by a source with rms voltage $200\,\mathrm{V}$. A voltmeter connected across the resistor reads $160\,\mathrm{V}$. What average power does the circuit absorb?

- $800\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{W}$
- $512\,\mathrm{W}$
- $640\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

In the series $RLC$ circuit the inductor and the capacitor absorb no power on average, so all the average power is dissipated in the resistor. The rms voltage across the resistor is $160\,\mathrm{V}$, therefore
$$
P = \frac{V_R^2}{R} = \frac{160^2}{40}\,\mathrm{W} = 640\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
The same value follows from $V_{\text{eff}} I_{\text{eff}}\cos\varphi$, with $I_{\text{eff}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{A}$ and $\cos\varphi = \dfrac{V_R}{V_{\text{eff}}} = 0.80$.
