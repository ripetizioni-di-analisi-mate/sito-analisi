A power plant must transmit the power $1.0\,\mathrm{MW}$ along a line of total resistance $5.0\,\Omega$, with power factor $1$. What is the minimum rms transmission voltage for which at most $1\%$ of the transmitted power is lost in the line?

- $7.1\,\mathrm{kV}$
- $71\,\mathrm{kV}$
- $22\,\mathrm{kV}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{kV}$

## Explanation

With rms voltage $V$ the current in the line is $I = \dfrac{P}{V}$, so the power lost is $R_\ell I^2 = \dfrac{R_\ell P^2}{V^2}$.

The condition $\dfrac{R_\ell P^2}{V^2} \leq 0.01\,P$ is equivalent to $V^2 \geq 100\,R_\ell P$. Therefore
$$
V \geq \sqrt{100 \cdot 5.0 \cdot 1.0 \cdot 10^6}\,\mathrm{V} \approx 2.2 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V} = 22\,\mathrm{kV} .
$$
