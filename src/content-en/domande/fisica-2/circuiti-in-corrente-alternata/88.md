An ideal resistor carries a sinusoidal current. The current amplitude is known and the average power is sought. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 12\,\Omega$ and the peak current $I_{0} = 5\,\mathrm A$, what is the average dissipated power $P$?

- $75\,\mathrm W$
- $225\,\mathrm W$
- $300\,\mathrm W$
- $150\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The rms current is the peak current divided by $\sqrt2$. The average power is $R_e I_{\mathrm{eff}}^2$. In the stated units, we have $P = \dfrac{I_{0}^{2} \cdot R_{e}}{2}$. With the given values, we obtain $150\,\mathrm W$.
