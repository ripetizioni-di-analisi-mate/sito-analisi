A series $RLC$ circuit, supplied at the resonant angular frequency with a fixed rms voltage, absorbs the average power $40\,\mathrm{W}$. What average power does it absorb, with the same rms voltage, at the angular frequency $\omega_+$, the upper edge of the bandwidth?

- $28\,\mathrm{W}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W}$
- $40\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

In the series $RLC$ circuit all the average power is dissipated in the resistor, so it equals $R\,I_{\text{eff}}^2$.

At the edges of the bandwidth the rms current is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ of its value at resonance, so its square is one half. Therefore the average power is $\dfrac{40}{2}\,\mathrm{W} = 20\,\mathrm{W}$.
