A series RLC circuit is at resonance and the components are ideal. The source imposes a sinusoidal voltage. Given the rms source voltage $U_{e} = 60\,\mathrm V$ and the rms current $I_{e} = 4\,\mathrm A$, what is the resistance $R_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\Omega$
- $15\,\Omega$
- $30\,\Omega$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}\,\Omega$

## Explanation

At resonance the inductive reactance cancels the capacitive reactance. The impedance seen by the source is the resistance alone. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{U_{e}}{R_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = \dfrac{U_{e}}{I_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $15\,\Omega$.
