A free series RLC circuit has $L=0{,}010\,\mathrm H$ and $C=100\,\mu\mathrm F$. What is the resistance for critical damping?

- $10\,\Omega$
- $40\,\Omega$
- $20\,\Omega$
- $80\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Critical damping requires $R_c=2\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}$. Here the square root is $10\,\Omega$. The result is $20\,\Omega$.
