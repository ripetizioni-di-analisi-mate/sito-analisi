In a series RLC circuit, we have $R=30\,\Omega$ and $X_L-X_C=40\,\Omega$. What is $\tan\varphi$ for the phase shift of the voltage relative to the current?

- $0{,}75$
- $-1{,}33$
- $2{,}33$
- $1{,}33$

## Explanation

The phase of the impedance satisfies $\tan\varphi=\dfrac{(X_L-X_C)}{R}$. The positive difference indicates inductive behavior. The result is $1{,}33$.
