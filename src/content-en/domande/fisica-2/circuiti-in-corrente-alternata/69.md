A load is supplied in the sinusoidal regime. The current has a phase shift of $60^\circ$ relative to the voltage. Initially, the rms voltage $U_{e} = 120\,\mathrm V$ and the rms current $I_{e} = 4\,\mathrm A$ are given. In the second configuration, the rms current becomes $6\,\mathrm A$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $P$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The average power is the product of the rms values and $\cos\varphi$. For $60^\circ$, the power factor is $1/2$. Applying $P = \dfrac{I_{e} \cdot U_{e}}{2}$ gives $240\,\mathrm W$ before the change and $360\,\mathrm W$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
