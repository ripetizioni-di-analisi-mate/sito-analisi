An ideal capacitor is connected to a sinusoidal voltage. Rms values are considered and the capacitance is $100\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. Given the frequency $f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and the rms current $I_{e} = 200 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$, what is the rms voltage $U_{e}$?

- $20 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm V$
- $10\,\mathrm V$
- $20\,\mathrm V$
- $40\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $1/(2\pi f C)$. Dividing the voltage by this reactance and converting to milliamperes gives the required value. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{\pi \cdot U_{e} \cdot f}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{e} = \dfrac{5 \cdot I_{e}}{\pi \cdot f}$. With the given values, we obtain $20\,\mathrm V$.
