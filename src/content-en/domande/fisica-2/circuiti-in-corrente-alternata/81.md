An underdamped series RLC circuit is left to evolve freely after the source is disconnected. The decay rate of the charge envelope is measured. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 8\,\Omega$ and the decay rate $\beta = 8\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, what is the inductance $L_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $1\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $2\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

After dividing the equation $L_e\ddot q+R_e\dot q+q/C=0$ by $L_e$, the coefficient of $\dot q$ is $2\beta=R_e/L_e$. In the stated units, we have $\beta = \dfrac{R_{e}}{2 \cdot L_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $L_{e} = \dfrac{R_{e}}{2 \cdot \beta}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{H}$.
