A $10\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor operates at $\omega=1000\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is its capacitive reactance?

- $50\,\Omega$
- $200\,\Omega$
- $400\,\Omega$
- $100\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the capacitive reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{\omega C}$. The capacitance must be converted to farads. The result is $100\,\Omega$.
