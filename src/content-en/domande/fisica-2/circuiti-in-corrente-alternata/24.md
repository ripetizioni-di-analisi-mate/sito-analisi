An ideal transformer quadruples the voltage and draws rms primary current $4\,\mathrm A$. What rms current does it supply at the secondary?

- $0{,}5\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $1\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

An ideal transformer conserves power between the primary and secondary. Quadrupling the voltage therefore divides the current by four. The result is $1\,\mathrm A$.
