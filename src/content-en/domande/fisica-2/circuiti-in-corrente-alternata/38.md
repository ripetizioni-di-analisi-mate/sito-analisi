In an ideal LC circuit, where is the energy when the capacitor is uncharged?

- Entirely in the electric field of the capacitor
- Entirely in the magnetic field of the inductor
- It has all been dissipated
- In the absent ideal resistor

## Explanation

Zero charge implies electric energy $\dfrac{Q^2}{2C}=0$. The total energy remains constant. It is therefore in the inductor, with current of maximum magnitude.
