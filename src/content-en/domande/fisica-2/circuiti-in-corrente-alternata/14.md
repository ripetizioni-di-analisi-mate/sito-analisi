The capacitance of a series RLC circuit quadruples while $L$ remains unchanged. What is the ratio of the final resonant frequency to the initial resonant frequency?

- $2$
- $0{,}5$
- $4$
- $0{,}25$

## Explanation

The resonant frequency is proportional to $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt C}$. Quadrupling the capacitance therefore halves the frequency. The result is $0{,}5$.
