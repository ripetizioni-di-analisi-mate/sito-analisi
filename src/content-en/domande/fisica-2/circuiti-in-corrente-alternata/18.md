The rms current in a $10\,\Omega$ resistor is $2\,\mathrm A$. What is the average power dissipated?

- $20\,\mathrm W$
- $40\,\mathrm W$
- $80\,\mathrm W$
- $160\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

In an ideal resistor, $P=I_{eff}^2R$ holds. The rms value allows the same formula as for direct current. The result is $40\,\mathrm W$.
