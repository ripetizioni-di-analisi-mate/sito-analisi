An impedance is $Z=(3+4i)\,\Omega$. What is its magnitude?

- $1\,\Omega$
- $5\,\Omega$
- $7\,\Omega$
- $25\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The magnitude of a complex number is the square root of the sum of the squares of its real and imaginary parts. Thus we obtain $|Z|=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}$. The result is $5\,\Omega$.
