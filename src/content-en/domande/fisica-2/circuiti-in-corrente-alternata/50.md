At a fixed frequency, $X_L=14\,\Omega$, $X_C=8\,\Omega$ and $R=8\,\Omega$ are measured in a series RLC circuit. Which pair describes the impedance magnitude and the phase of the current?

- $10\,\Omega$, current leading
- $10\,\Omega$, current lagging
- $30\,\Omega$, current lagging
- $6\,\Omega$, current in phase

## Explanation

The net reactance is positive and equals $6\,\Omega$, so the circuit is inductive. With $R=8\,\Omega$, the impedance has magnitude $\sqrt{8^2+6^2}\,\Omega=10\,\Omega$.
