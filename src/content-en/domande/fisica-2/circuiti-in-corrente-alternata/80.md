The rms current in a series $RLC$ circuit is maximum at the frequency $10\,\mathrm{kHz}$, and the bandwidth is $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ wide in frequency. What is the quality factor of the circuit?

- $40$
- $20$
- $126$
- $0.050$

## Explanation

The width of the band in angular frequency is $\Delta\omega = \dfrac{\omega_0}{Q}$. Dividing both sides by $2\pi$ gives $\Delta f = \dfrac{f_0}{Q}$, so $Q = \dfrac{f_0}{\Delta f}$.

Therefore $Q = \dfrac{10\,000}{500} = 20$. The factor $2\pi$ does not appear, because angular frequencies and frequencies are in the same ratio.
