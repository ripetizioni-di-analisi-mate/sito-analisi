An underdamped series RLC circuit is left to evolve freely after the source is disconnected. The decay rate of the charge envelope is measured. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the decay rate $\beta = 8\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, what is the resistance $R_{e}$?

- $16\,\Omega$
- $4\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$
- $8\,\Omega$

## Explanation

After dividing the equation $L_e\ddot q+R_e\dot q+q/C=0$ by $L_e$, the coefficient of $\dot q$ is $2\beta=R_e/L_e$. In the stated units, we have $\beta = \dfrac{R_{e}}{2 \cdot L_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = 2 \cdot L_{e} \cdot \beta$. With the given values, we obtain $8\,\Omega$.
