The primary of an ideal transformer has $500$ turns and is connected to the grid, with rms voltage $230\,\mathrm{V}$ and frequency $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the amplitude of the magnetic flux through each turn?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $2.1\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $13\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $9.2\,\mathrm{mWb}$

## Explanation

Let $\Phi_B(t) = \Phi_0\sin(\omega t)$ be the flux through one turn. The resistance of the primary is negligible, so the applied voltage equals in magnitude the induced emf, whose magnitude is $N_1\,\dfrac{d\Phi_B}{dt} = N_1\omega\Phi_0\cos(\omega t)$; therefore the amplitude of the voltage is $V_0 = N_1\omega\Phi_0$.

With $V_0 = 230\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{V} \approx 325\,\mathrm{V}$ and $\omega = 100\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ we get
$$
\Phi_0 = \frac{V_0}{N_1\omega} = \frac{325}{500 \cdot 314}\,\mathrm{Wb} \approx 2.1\,\mathrm{mWb} .
$$
