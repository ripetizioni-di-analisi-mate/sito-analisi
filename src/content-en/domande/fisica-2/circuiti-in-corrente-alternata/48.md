Two capacitors of capacitance $4.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ and $12\,\mu\mathrm{F}$, connected in series with each other, form an oscillating circuit of negligible resistance together with an inductor of inductance $L = 20\,\mathrm{mH}$. What is the natural angular frequency of the circuit?

- $4.1 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.8 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3.5 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6.5 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The two capacitors in series are equivalent to a single capacitor of capacitance $C$ with $\dfrac{1}{C} = \dfrac{1}{4.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}} + \dfrac{1}{12\,\mu\mathrm{F}}$, so $C = 3.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$.

The natural angular frequency of the $LC$ circuit is then
$$
\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2.0 \cdot 10^{-2} \cdot 3.0 \cdot 10^{-6}}}\,\mathrm{rad/s} \approx 4.1 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The corresponding frequency is $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2\pi} \approx 650\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
