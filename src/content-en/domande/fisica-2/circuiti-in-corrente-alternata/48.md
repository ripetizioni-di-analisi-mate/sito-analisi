In an ideal LC circuit the capacitor is initially charged and the initial current is zero. The capacitance is in microfarads and the inductance in henries. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the period of the oscillations $T = 4 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{ms}$, what is the capacitance $C$?

- $32\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $24\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $16\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

The LC circuit has angular frequency $1/\sqrt{LC}$. When converting the microfarads, the square root of $LC$ expressed in seconds acquires a factor $10^{-3}$. In the stated units, we have $T = 2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{C} \cdot \sqrt{L_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $C = \dfrac{T^{2}}{4 \cdot \pi^{2} \cdot L_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $16\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
