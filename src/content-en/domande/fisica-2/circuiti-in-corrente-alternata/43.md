What effect does a suitable power-factor correction capacitor have on a load that is inductive overall?

- It always cancels the active power of the load
- It necessarily makes the voltage zero
- It reduces the reactive power required from the source
- It always increases the inductive phase shift

## Explanation

The capacitor absorbs reactive power of the opposite sign to the inductive reactive power. The two contributions can partially cancel. The overall power factor can therefore approach one.
