In an ideal LC circuit the capacitor is initially charged and the initial current is zero. The capacitance is in microfarads and the inductance in henries. Given the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the capacitance $C = 16\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, what is the period of the oscillations $T$?

- $8 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $4 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $2 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

The LC circuit has angular frequency $1/\sqrt{LC}$. When converting the microfarads, the square root of $LC$ expressed in seconds acquires a factor $10^{-3}$. In the stated units, we have $T = 2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{C} \cdot \sqrt{L_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $4 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{ms}$.
