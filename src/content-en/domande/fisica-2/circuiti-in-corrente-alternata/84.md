A sinusoidal load has a positive power factor. The active and apparent power are known. Given the active power $P = 480\,\mathrm W$ and the power factor $b = \dfrac{4}{5}$, what is the apparent power $S$?

- $300\,\mathrm{VA}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{VA}$
- $900\,\mathrm{VA}$
- $600\,\mathrm{VA}$

## Explanation

The apparent power is the product of the rms values. Multiplying it by the power factor gives the active power. In the stated units, we have $b = \dfrac{P}{S}$. Solving for the unknown gives $S = \dfrac{P}{b}$. With the given values, we obtain $600\,\mathrm{VA}$.
