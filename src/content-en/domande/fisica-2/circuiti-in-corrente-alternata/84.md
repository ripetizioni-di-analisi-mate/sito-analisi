An electric heater, which can be modeled as a resistor, absorbs the average power $1.0\,\mathrm{kW}$ from the grid. What is the maximum value of the instantaneous power it absorbs?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

In a resistor voltage and current are in phase, so the instantaneous power is $p(t) = V_0 I_0\sin^2(\omega t)$.

The mean value of $\sin^2(\omega t)$ over one period is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, therefore the average power is $P = \dfrac{1}{2} V_0 I_0$, while the maximum value of $p(t)$ is $V_0 I_0 = 2P$. The instantaneous power therefore oscillates between $0$ and $2.0\,\mathrm{kW}$.
