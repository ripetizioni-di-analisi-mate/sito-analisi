A load is supplied in the sinusoidal regime. The current has a phase shift of $60^\circ$ relative to the voltage. Given the rms voltage $U_{e} = 120\,\mathrm V$ and the rms current $I_{e} = 4\,\mathrm A$, what is the average active power $P$?

- $240 \cdot \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm W$
- $240\,\mathrm W$
- $120\,\mathrm W$
- $480\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The average power is the product of the rms values and $\cos\varphi$. For $60^\circ$, the power factor is $1/2$. In the stated units, we have $P = \dfrac{I_{e} \cdot U_{e}}{2}$. With the given values, we obtain $240\,\mathrm W$.
