A sinusoidal voltage has rms value $230\,\mathrm V$. What is its peak value?

- $163\,\mathrm V$
- $325\,\mathrm V$
- $651\,\mathrm V$
- $1{,}3\cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The peak value is $V_{max}=\sqrt2V_{eff}$. The rms voltage is therefore smaller than the amplitude. The result is $325\,\mathrm V$.
