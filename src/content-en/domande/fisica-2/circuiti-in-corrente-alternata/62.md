In a series RLC circuit the net reactance is positive. The impedance magnitude is measured at the fixed frequency. Given the net reactance $X = 5\,\Omega$ and the impedance magnitude $Z = 13\,\Omega$, what is the resistance $R_{e}$?

- $8\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $24\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The resistance and reactance are the real and imaginary parts of the impedance, respectively. The magnitude follows from the Pythagorean theorem. In the stated units, we have $Z = \sqrt{R_{e}^{2} + X^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = \sqrt{- X^{2} + Z^{2}}$. With the given values, we obtain $12\,\Omega$.
