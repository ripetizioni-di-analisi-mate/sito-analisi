In a series RLC circuit the net reactance is positive. The impedance magnitude is measured at the fixed frequency. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 12\,\Omega$ and the net reactance $X = 5\,\Omega$, what is the impedance magnitude $Z$?

- $17\,\Omega$
- $7\,\Omega$
- $13\,\Omega$
- $26\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The resistance and reactance are the real and imaginary parts of the impedance, respectively. The magnitude follows from the Pythagorean theorem. In the stated units, we have $Z = \sqrt{R_{e}^{2} + X^{2}}$. With the given values, we obtain $13\,\Omega$.
