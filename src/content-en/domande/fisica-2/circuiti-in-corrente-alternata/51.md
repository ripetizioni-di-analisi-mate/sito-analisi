An ideal capacitor is connected to a sinusoidal voltage. Rms values are considered and the capacitance is $100\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. Given the frequency $f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and the rms voltage $U_{e} = 20\,\mathrm V$, what is the rms current $I_{e}$?

- $200 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $100 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $200\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $400 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $1/(2\pi f C)$. Dividing the voltage by this reactance and converting to milliamperes gives the required value. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{\pi \cdot U_{e} \cdot f}{5}$. With the given values, we obtain $200 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$.
