The primary of an ideal transformer with $N_2 = 4N_1$ is connected to an alternating voltage with frequency $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the frequency of the voltage at the secondary?

- $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $200\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $0\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The voltage applied to the primary equals in magnitude the emf $-N_1\,\dfrac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$, so the flux in the core varies with the frequency of $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

By the Faraday-Neumann-Lenz law the emf in the secondary is $-N_2\,\dfrac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$, and the derivative of a sinusoidal function is sinusoidal with the same frequency. Therefore the voltage at the secondary has frequency $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$; the turns ratio changes the amplitude, which is four times that of the primary.
