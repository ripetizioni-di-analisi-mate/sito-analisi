An ideal resistor carries a sinusoidal current. The current amplitude is known and the average power is sought. Given the peak current $I_{0} = 5\,\mathrm A$ and the average dissipated power $P = 150\,\mathrm W$, what is the resistance $R_{e}$?

- $24\,\Omega$
- $18\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The rms current is the peak current divided by $\sqrt2$. The average power is $R_e I_{\mathrm{eff}}^2$. In the stated units, we have $P = \dfrac{I_{0}^{2} \cdot R_{e}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = \dfrac{2 \cdot P}{I_{0}^{2}}$. With the given values, we obtain $12\,\Omega$.
