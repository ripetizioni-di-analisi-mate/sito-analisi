What equivalence defines the rms value of a current?

- The same instantaneous maximum current
- The same net algebraic charge over one period
- The same frequency as a direct current
- The same average power dissipated in a resistor by a direct current

## Explanation

The average resistive power depends on the average of $I^2$. The rms value is the square root of this average. It therefore produces the same power as a direct current of that value.
