A series RLC circuit has $\omega_0=1000\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $L=0{,}20\,\mathrm H$ and $R=10\,\Omega$. What is its quality factor?

- $20$
- $10$
- $40$
- $80$

## Explanation

The quality factor of the series circuit is $Q=\dfrac{\omega_0L}{R}$. It is a dimensionless number. The result is $20$.
