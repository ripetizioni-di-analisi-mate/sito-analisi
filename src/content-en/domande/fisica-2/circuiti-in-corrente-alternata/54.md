An ideal capacitor is connected to a sinusoidal voltage. Rms values are considered and the capacitance is $100\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. Initially, the frequency $f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and the rms voltage $U_{e} = 20\,\mathrm V$ are given. In the second configuration, the frequency becomes $100\,\mathrm{Hz}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $I_{e}$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $1/(2\pi f C)$. Dividing the voltage by this reactance and converting to milliamperes gives the required value. Applying $I_{e} = \dfrac{\pi \cdot U_{e} \cdot f}{5}$ gives $200 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$ before the change and $400 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
