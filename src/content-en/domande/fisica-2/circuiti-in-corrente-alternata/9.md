A $0{,}20\,\mathrm H$ inductor operates at $\omega=200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is its inductive reactance?

- $40\,\Omega$
- $20\,\Omega$
- $80\,\Omega$
- $160\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. It increases linearly with angular frequency. The result is $40\,\Omega$.
