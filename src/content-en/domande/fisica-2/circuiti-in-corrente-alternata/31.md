In an ideal capacitor supplied sinusoidally, what is the phase shift of the current relative to the voltage?

- It lags by $90^\circ$
- It is always in phase
- It leads by $90^\circ$
- It is in opposite phase

## Explanation

The current is $I=C\,\dfrac{dV}{dt}$. Differentiating a sinusoid advances its phase by one quarter of a period. Thus the current leads the voltage by $90^\circ$.
