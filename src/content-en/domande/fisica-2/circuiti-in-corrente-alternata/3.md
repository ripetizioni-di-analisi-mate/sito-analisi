In an LC circuit, the maximum charge is $1\,\mathrm{mC}$ and $\omega_0=200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the maximum current?

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm A$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm A$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm A$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The current is the derivative of the sinusoidal charge. Its amplitude is therefore $I_{max}=\omega_0Q_{max}$. The result is $0{,}2\,\mathrm A$.
