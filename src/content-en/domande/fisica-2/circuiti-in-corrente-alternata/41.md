Why does an ordinary transformer require a changing flux to induce a voltage at the secondary?

- The secondary emf depends on the time derivative of the flux
- A constant field cannot exist in the core
- Direct current always has zero charge
- The voltage is directly equal to the flux

## Explanation

The induced voltage follows Faraday's law. A constant flux has zero derivative. Thus a steady direct current does not produce the usual secondary emf.
