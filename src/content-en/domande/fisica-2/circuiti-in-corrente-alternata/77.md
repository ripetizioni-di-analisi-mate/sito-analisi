A series RLC circuit is at resonance and the components are ideal. The source imposes a sinusoidal voltage. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 15\,\Omega$ and the rms current $I_{e} = 4\,\mathrm A$, what is the rms source voltage $U_{e}$?

- $120\,\mathrm V$
- $30\,\mathrm V$
- $60\,\mathrm V$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

At resonance the inductive reactance cancels the capacitive reactance. The impedance seen by the source is the resistance alone. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{U_{e}}{R_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{e} = I_{e} \cdot R_{e}$. With the given values, we obtain $60\,\mathrm V$.
