A series $RLC$ circuit has $R = 100\,\Omega$, $L = 0.10\,\mathrm{H}$ and $C = 10\,\mu\mathrm{F}$. What is the upper edge $\omega_+$ of the bandwidth?

- $2.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.1 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.6 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6.2 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

At the edges of the bandwidth the impedance equals $\sqrt{2}\,R$, so the net reactance equals $\pm R$; at the upper edge $\omega L - \dfrac{1}{\omega C} = R$.

Multiplying by $\dfrac{\omega}{L}$ gives $\omega^2 - \dfrac{R}{L}\,\omega - \dfrac{1}{LC} = 0$, that is, with $\omega$ in $\mathrm{rad/s}$, $\omega^2 - 1000\,\omega - 10^6 = 0$. The positive solution is
$$
\omega_+ = \frac{1000 + \sqrt{10^6 + 4 \cdot 10^6}}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 500\left( 1 + \sqrt{5} \right)\mathrm{rad/s} \approx 1.6 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The other root is negative, and the lower edge $\omega_- \approx 6.2 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ follows from the equation with $-R$.
