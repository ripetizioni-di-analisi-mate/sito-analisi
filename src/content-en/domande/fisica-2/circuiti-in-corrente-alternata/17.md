A sinusoidal load has $V_{eff}=100\,\mathrm V$, $I_{eff}=2\,\mathrm A$ and power factor $0{,}60$. What is the average power?

- $120\,\mathrm W$
- $60\,\mathrm W$
- $240\,\mathrm W$
- $480\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The average active power is $P=V_{eff}I_{eff}\cos\varphi$. The power factor must be included in the product. The result is $120\,\mathrm W$.
