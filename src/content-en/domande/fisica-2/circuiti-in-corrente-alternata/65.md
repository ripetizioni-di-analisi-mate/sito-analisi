In a series RLC circuit the net reactance is positive. The impedance magnitude is measured at the fixed frequency. Initially, the resistance $R_{e} = 12\,\Omega$ and the net reactance $X = 5\,\Omega$ are given. In the second configuration, the resistance becomes $8\,\Omega$ and the net reactance becomes $6\,\Omega$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $Z$?

- $3\,\Omega$
- $10\,\Omega$
- $-3\,\Omega$
- $-5\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The resistance and reactance are the real and imaginary parts of the impedance, respectively. The magnitude follows from the Pythagorean theorem. Applying $Z = \sqrt{R_{e}^{2} + X^{2}}$ gives $13\,\Omega$ before the change and $10\,\Omega$ after it. The final value minus the initial value is $-3\,\Omega$.
