An ideal transformer has $200$ turns in the primary. A sinusoidal voltage is applied to the primary and the rms voltage at the secondary is measured. Initially, the rms primary voltage $U_{1} = 120\,\mathrm V$ and the number of secondary turns $N = 50$ are given. In the second configuration, the rms primary voltage becomes $240\,\mathrm V$ and the number of secondary turns becomes $25$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $U_{e}$?

- $0\,\mathrm V$
- $1440\,\mathrm V$
- $30\,\mathrm V$
- $1\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The flux per turn is shared by both windings. By Faraday's law the voltages are proportional to the number of turns. Applying $U_{e} = \dfrac{N \cdot U_{1}}{200}$ gives $30\,\mathrm V$ before the change and $30\,\mathrm V$ after it. The final value minus the initial value is $0\,\mathrm V$.
