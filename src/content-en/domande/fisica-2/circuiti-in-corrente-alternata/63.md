In a series RLC circuit the net reactance is positive. The impedance magnitude is measured at the fixed frequency. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 12\,\Omega$ and the impedance magnitude $Z = 13\,\Omega$, what is the net reactance $X$?

- $5\,\Omega$
- $1\,\Omega$
- $10\,\Omega$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The resistance and reactance are the real and imaginary parts of the impedance, respectively. The magnitude follows from the Pythagorean theorem. In the stated units, we have $Z = \sqrt{R_{e}^{2} + X^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $X = \sqrt{- R_{e}^{2} + Z^{2}}$. With the given values, we obtain $5\,\Omega$.
