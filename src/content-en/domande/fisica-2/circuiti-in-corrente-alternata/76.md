A series RLC circuit is at resonance and the components are ideal. The source imposes a sinusoidal voltage. Given the rms source voltage $U_{e} = 60\,\mathrm V$ and the resistance $R_{e} = 15\,\Omega$, what is the rms current $I_{e}$?

- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$
- $900\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

At resonance the inductive reactance cancels the capacitive reactance. The impedance seen by the source is the resistance alone. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{U_{e}}{R_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $4\,\mathrm A$.
