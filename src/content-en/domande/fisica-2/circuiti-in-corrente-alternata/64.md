In a series RLC circuit the net reactance is positive. The impedance magnitude is measured at the fixed frequency. Initially, the resistance $R_{e} = 12\,\Omega$ and the net reactance $X = 5\,\Omega$ are given. In the second configuration, the net reactance becomes $9\,\Omega$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $Z$?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{13}{15}$
- $\dfrac{21}{17}$
- $\dfrac{15}{13}$

## Explanation

The resistance and reactance are the real and imaginary parts of the impedance, respectively. The magnitude follows from the Pythagorean theorem. Applying $Z = \sqrt{R_{e}^{2} + X^{2}}$ gives $13\,\Omega$ before the change and $15\,\Omega$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{15}{13}$.
