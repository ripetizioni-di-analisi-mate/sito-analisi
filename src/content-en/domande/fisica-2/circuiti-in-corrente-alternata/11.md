A circuit has impedance of magnitude $50\,\Omega$ and rms voltage $100\,\mathrm V$. What is the rms current?

- $1\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

In the sinusoidal regime, $I_{eff}=\dfrac{V_{eff}}{|Z|}$ holds. Both values must be rms values. The result is $2\,\mathrm A$.
