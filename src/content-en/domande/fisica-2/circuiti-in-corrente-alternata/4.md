An ideal LC circuit has total energy $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$. When the charge is half its maximum value, how much energy is in the inductor?

- $2\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

The electric energy is proportional to $Q^2$. At half the charge it is one quarter of the total, so the magnetic energy is the remaining three quarters. The result is $6\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
