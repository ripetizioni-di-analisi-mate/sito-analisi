A series RLC circuit has $R=10\,\Omega$ and $L=0{,}20\,\mathrm H$. What is its half-power bandwidth expressed in angular frequency?

- $25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

For the series circuit the half-power bandwidth is $\Delta\omega=\dfrac{R}{L}$. It is also equal to $\dfrac{\omega_0}{Q}$. The result is $50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
