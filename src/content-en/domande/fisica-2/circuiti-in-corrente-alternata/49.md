In an ideal LC circuit the capacitor is initially charged and the initial current is zero. The capacitance is in microfarads and the inductance in henries. Initially, the inductance $L_{e} = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{H}$ and the capacitance $C = 16\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ are given. In the second configuration, the capacitance becomes $64\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $T$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The LC circuit has angular frequency $1/\sqrt{LC}$. When converting the microfarads, the square root of $LC$ expressed in seconds acquires a factor $10^{-3}$. Applying $T = 2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{C} \cdot \sqrt{L_{e}}$ gives $4 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{ms}$ before the change and $8 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{ms}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
