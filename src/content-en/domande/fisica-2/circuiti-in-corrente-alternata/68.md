A load is supplied in the sinusoidal regime. The current has a phase shift of $60^\circ$ relative to the voltage. Given the rms voltage $U_{e} = 120\,\mathrm V$ and the average active power $P = 240\,\mathrm W$, what is the rms current $I_{e}$?

- $8\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $\dfrac{4 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{3}\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The average power is the product of the rms values and $\cos\varphi$. For $60^\circ$, the power factor is $1/2$. In the stated units, we have $P = \dfrac{I_{e} \cdot U_{e}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $I_{e} = \dfrac{2 \cdot P}{U_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $4\,\mathrm A$.
