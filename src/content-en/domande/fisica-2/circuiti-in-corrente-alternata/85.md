An ideal transformer supplies a resistor. The ratio of secondary turns to primary turns is $4$. Given the secondary resistance $R_{e} = 60\,\Omega$ and the rms primary voltage $U_{1} = 30\,\mathrm V$, what is the rms primary current $I_{e}$?

- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$
- $16\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The secondary receives four times the primary voltage. Since the transformer is ideal, the primary draws four times the secondary current. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{16 \cdot U_{1}}{R_{e}}$. With the given values, we obtain $8\,\mathrm A$.
