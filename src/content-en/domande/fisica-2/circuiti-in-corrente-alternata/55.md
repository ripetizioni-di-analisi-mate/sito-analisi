An ideal capacitor is connected to a sinusoidal voltage. Rms values are considered and the capacitance is $100\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. Initially, the frequency $f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and the rms voltage $U_{e} = 20\,\mathrm V$ are given. In the second configuration, the frequency becomes $25\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and the rms voltage becomes $12\,\mathrm V$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $I_{e}$?

- $- 140 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $-140\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $140 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $- 70 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $1/(2\pi f C)$. Dividing the voltage by this reactance and converting to milliamperes gives the required value. Applying $I_{e} = \dfrac{\pi \cdot U_{e} \cdot f}{5}$ gives $200 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$ before the change and $60 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$ after it. The final value minus the initial value is $- 140 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{mA}$.
