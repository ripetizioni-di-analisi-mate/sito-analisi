In a series $RLC$ circuit the resistance $R$ is one tenth of the critical resistance $R_c = 2\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}$. What is the quality factor $Q$ of the circuit?

- $10$
- $20$
- $2.5$
- $5$

## Explanation

The quality factor is $Q = \dfrac{1}{R}\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}$.

Since $R_c = 2\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}$, we have $\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}} = \dfrac{R_c}{2}$, so $Q = \dfrac{R_c}{2R}$. With $R = \dfrac{R_c}{10}$ we get $Q = \dfrac{10}{2} = 5$.
