In an ideal inductor supplied sinusoidally, what is the phase shift of the current relative to the voltage?

- It leads by $90^\circ$
- It is always in phase
- It is always zero
- It lags by $90^\circ$

## Explanation

The voltage is $V=L\,\dfrac{dI}{dt}$. It therefore leads the current by one quarter of a period. The current lags the voltage by $90^\circ$.
