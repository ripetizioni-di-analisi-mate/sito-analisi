A series RLC circuit has $L=0{,}10\,\mathrm H$ and $C=1\,\mathrm{mF}$. What is the angular frequency of current resonance?

- $100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $400\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Current resonance requires $X_L=X_C$. Thus we obtain $\omega_0=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$. The result is $100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
