At series resonance, can the voltages across the inductor and capacitor exceed the source voltage?

- Yes, because their phasors are opposite and cancel in the sum
- No, no voltage can exceed the source voltage
- Only if the resistance is negative
- Only if the current is zero

## Explanation

The two reactive voltages have equal magnitudes at resonance. Their phasors are opposite. They can therefore be large individually while canceling in the total sum.
