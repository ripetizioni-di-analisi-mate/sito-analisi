A sinusoidal current has amplitude $4\sqrt2\,\mathrm A$. What is its rms value?

- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$
- $16\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

For a sinusoid the rms value is the amplitude divided by $\sqrt2$. This definition gives the same average Joule power. The result is $4\,\mathrm A$.
