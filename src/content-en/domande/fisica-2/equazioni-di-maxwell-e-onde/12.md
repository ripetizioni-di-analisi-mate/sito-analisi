A wave has $\omega=6\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $k=2\,\mathrm{rad/m}$. What is its phase velocity?

- $1{,}5\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}2\cdot 10^{9}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The phase velocity is the ratio $v=\dfrac{\omega}{k}$. The angular units cancel in the ratio. The result is $3\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
