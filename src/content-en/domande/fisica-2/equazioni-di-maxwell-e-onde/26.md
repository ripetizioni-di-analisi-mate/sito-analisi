A light pulse in vacuum carries energy $0{,}60\,\mathrm J$. What is the magnitude of its momentum? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $1\cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $2\cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $4\cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $8\cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

For radiation in vacuum, $p=\dfrac{U}{c}$ holds. The total energy of the pulse is used. The result is $2\cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$.
