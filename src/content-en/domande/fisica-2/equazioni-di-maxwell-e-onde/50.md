A plane electromagnetic wave in vacuum propagates toward $+x$. At a point the electric field is directed toward $+y$. In which direction is the magnetic field directed at that same instant?

- Toward $-z$
- Toward $+z$
- Toward $+x$
- Toward $-y$

## Explanation

The direction of propagation is the direction of $\vec E\times\vec B$. The cross product of a vector directed toward $+y$ with one directed toward $+z$ points toward $+x$.
