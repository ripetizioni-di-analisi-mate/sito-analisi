The electric field of a sinusoidal wave has amplitude $10\sqrt2\,\mathrm{V/m}$. What is its rms value?

- $5\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The rms value of a sinusoid is its amplitude divided by $\sqrt2$. This relation also applies to the electric field. The result is $10\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
