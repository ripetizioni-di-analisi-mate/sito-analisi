A plane surface absorbs all the incident light. The angle between the beam and the normal to the surface is $60^\circ$. The intensity is measured on a plane perpendicular to the beam. Given the beam intensity $J = 240\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and the area $A = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what is the absorbed power $P$?

- $60 \cdot \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm W$
- $60\,\mathrm W$
- $30\,\mathrm W$
- $120\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The area exposed to the beam is the projection $A\cos60^\circ$. The absorbed power is the intensity times the projected area. In the stated units, we have $P = \dfrac{A \cdot J}{2}$. With the given values, we obtain $60\,\mathrm W$.
