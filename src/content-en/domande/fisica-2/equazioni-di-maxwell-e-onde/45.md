In a homogeneous, nondispersive dielectric, if the wave speed decreases at fixed frequency, how does the wavelength change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It necessarily remains unchanged
- It becomes negative

## Explanation

We have $\lambda=\dfrac{v}{f}$. The frequency remains fixed during the transition between stationary media. Thus the wavelength decreases along with the speed.
