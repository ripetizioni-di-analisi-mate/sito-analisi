A wave has frequency $50\,\mathrm{MHz}$. What is its angular frequency?

- $1{,}57\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6{,}28\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3{,}14\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1{,}26\cdot 10^{9}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega=2\pi f$. Fifty megahertz corresponds to $5\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The result is $3{,}14\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
