A plane electromagnetic wave propagates in a transparent, nondispersive medium. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Initially, the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the frequency $f = 100\,\mathrm{MHz}$ are given. In the second configuration, the refractive index becomes $2$ and the frequency becomes $150\,\mathrm{MHz}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $\lambda$?

- $-1\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The frequency remains that of the source. The speed is $c/n$ and the wavelength is the speed divided by the frequency. Applying $\lambda = \dfrac{300}{f \cdot n}$ gives $2\,\mathrm m$ before the change and $1\,\mathrm m$ after it. The final value minus the initial value is $-1\,\mathrm m$.
