A uniform radiation pressure of $2\,\mu\mathrm{Pa}$ acts on an area $0{,}50\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the force?

- $0{,}5\,\mu\mathrm N$
- $2\,\mu\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mu\mathrm N$
- $1\,\mu\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The force is pressure times area. Thus we obtain $F=pA$. The result is $1\,\mu\mathrm N$.
