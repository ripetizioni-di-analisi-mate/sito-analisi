A plane electromagnetic wave propagates in a transparent, nondispersive medium. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Given the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the wavelength in the medium $\lambda = 2\,\mathrm m$, what is the frequency $f$?

- $50\,\mathrm{MHz}$
- $200\,\mathrm{MHz}$
- $100\,\mathrm{MHz}$
- $225\,\mathrm{MHz}$

## Explanation

The frequency remains that of the source. The speed is $c/n$ and the wavelength is the speed divided by the frequency. In the stated units, we have $\lambda = \dfrac{300}{f \cdot n}$. Solving for the unknown gives $f = \dfrac{300}{\lambda \cdot n}$. With the given values, we obtain $100\,\mathrm{MHz}$.
