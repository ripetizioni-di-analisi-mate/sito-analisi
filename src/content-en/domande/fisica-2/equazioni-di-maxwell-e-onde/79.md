A harmonic plane wave has wavelength $\lambda$ and a positive phase difference of less than one cycle between two points measured at the same instant. Given the distance along the direction of propagation $d = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm m$ and the wavelength $\lambda = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm m$, what is the magnitude of the phase difference $\phi$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $8 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

At a fixed time the phase difference has magnitude $kd$. The wave number is $2\pi/\lambda$. In the stated units, we have $\phi = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot d}{\lambda}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
