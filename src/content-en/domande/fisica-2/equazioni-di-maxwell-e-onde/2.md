Light in vacuum has wavelength $500\,\mathrm{nm}$. What is its frequency? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $3\cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $6\cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1{,}2\cdot 10^{15}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2{,}4\cdot 10^{15}\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

We have $f=\dfrac{c}{\lambda}$. The nanometers are converted to meters. The result is $6\cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
