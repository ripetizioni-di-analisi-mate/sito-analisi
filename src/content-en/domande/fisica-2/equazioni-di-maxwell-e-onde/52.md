A plane electromagnetic wave propagates in a transparent, nondispersive medium. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Given the frequency $f = 100\,\mathrm{MHz}$ and the wavelength in the medium $\lambda = 2\,\mathrm m$, what is the refractive index $n$?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The frequency remains that of the source. The speed is $c/n$ and the wavelength is the speed divided by the frequency. In the stated units, we have $\lambda = \dfrac{300}{f \cdot n}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{300}{f \cdot \lambda}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
