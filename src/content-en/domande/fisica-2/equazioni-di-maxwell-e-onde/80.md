A harmonic plane wave has wavelength $\lambda$ and a positive phase difference of less than one cycle between two points measured at the same instant. Given the wavelength $\lambda = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm m$ and the magnitude of the phase difference $\phi = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the distance along the direction of propagation $d$?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{16}{5}\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

At a fixed time the phase difference has magnitude $kd$. The wave number is $2\pi/\lambda$. In the stated units, we have $\phi = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot d}{\lambda}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{\lambda \cdot \phi}{2 \cdot \pi}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm m$.
