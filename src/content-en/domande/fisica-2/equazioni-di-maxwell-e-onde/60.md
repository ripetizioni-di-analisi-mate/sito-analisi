A plane electromagnetic wave crosses a homogeneous transparent region. The propagation speed is expressed in megameters per second and the magnetic field in nanoteslas. Initially, the magnetic field amplitude $B = 50\,\mathrm{nT}$ and the propagation speed $b = 200\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$ are given. In the second configuration, the magnetic field amplitude becomes $75\,\mathrm{nT}$ and the propagation speed becomes $160\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $E$?

- $4\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

For a plane wave the amplitudes satisfy $E=vB$. Converting megameters per second and nanoteslas gives the factor $10^{-3}$. Applying $E = \dfrac{B \cdot b}{1000}$ gives $10\,\mathrm{V/m}$ before the change and $12\,\mathrm{V/m}$ after it. The final value minus the initial value is $2\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
