A wave in vacuum has frequency $100\,\mathrm{MHz}$. What is its wavelength? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $1{,}5\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The propagation relation is $c=\lambda f$. The frequency must be expressed in hertz. The result is $3\,\mathrm m$.
