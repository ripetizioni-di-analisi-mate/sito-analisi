A plane electromagnetic wave crosses a homogeneous transparent region. The propagation speed is expressed in megameters per second and the magnetic field in nanoteslas. Initially, the magnetic field amplitude $B = 50\,\mathrm{nT}$ and the propagation speed $b = 200\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$ are given. In the second configuration, the magnetic field amplitude becomes $100\,\mathrm{nT}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $E$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

For a plane wave the amplitudes satisfy $E=vB$. Converting megameters per second and nanoteslas gives the factor $10^{-3}$. Applying $E = \dfrac{B \cdot b}{1000}$ gives $10\,\mathrm{V/m}$ before the change and $20\,\mathrm{V/m}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
