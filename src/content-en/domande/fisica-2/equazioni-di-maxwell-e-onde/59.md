A proton, with charge $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, moves with velocity $\vec{v} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{5}\,\hat{\imath}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ in a region with electric field $\vec{E} = 4.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{V/m}$ and magnetic field $\vec{B} = 0.10\,\hat{k}\ \mathrm{T}$. What is the force acting on the proton?

- $9.6 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{N}$
- $6.4 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{N}$
- $3.2 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{N}$
- $-3.2 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is given by the **Lorentz force** $\vec{F} = e\left( \vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B} \right)$.
Since $\hat{\imath} \times \hat{k} = -\hat{\jmath}$, we have $\vec{v} \times \vec{B} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{5} \cdot 0.10\,(-\hat{\jmath})\ \mathrm{V/m} = -2.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{V/m}$.
So $\vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{V/m}$, and
$$
\vec{F} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{N} = 3.2 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{N} .
$$
The magnetic force is opposite to the electric force and cancels half of it.
