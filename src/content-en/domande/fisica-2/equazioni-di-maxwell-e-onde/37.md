What law relates a changing magnetic field to the circulation of the electric field?

- Faraday's law
- Coulomb's law alone
- Ohm's law
- Malus's law

## Explanation

Faraday's law states $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec l=-\dfrac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$. A changing magnetic flux therefore generates electric field circulation. The resulting field may be nonconservative.
