A plane electromagnetic wave propagates in a transparent, nondispersive medium. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Given the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the frequency $f = 100\,\mathrm{MHz}$, what is the wavelength in the medium $\lambda$?

- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The frequency remains that of the source. The speed is $c/n$ and the wavelength is the speed divided by the frequency. In the stated units, we have $\lambda = \dfrac{300}{f \cdot n}$. With the given values, we obtain $2\,\mathrm m$.
