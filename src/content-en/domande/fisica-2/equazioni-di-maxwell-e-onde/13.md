In a wave with $\lambda=4\,\mathrm m$, two points are separated by $1\,\mathrm m$ along the direction of propagation. What is the magnitude of their phase difference at the same instant?

- $1{,}57\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0{,}785\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $3{,}14\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $6{,}28\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The spatial phase difference is $|\Delta\varphi|=k\Delta x$. Since $k=\dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}$, the given distance corresponds to one quarter of a cycle. The result is $1{,}57\,\mathrm{rad}$.
