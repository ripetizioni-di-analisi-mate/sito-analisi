A plane wave in vacuum has electric field amplitude $E_0=600\,\mathrm{V/m}$. What is its magnetic field amplitude in microteslas? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $1\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $4\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $2\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

For a plane wave in vacuum, we have $E_0=cB_0$. Thus we obtain $B_0=\dfrac{E_0}{c}$. The result is $2\,\mu\mathrm T$.
