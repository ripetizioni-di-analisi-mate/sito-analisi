An isotropic source emits $100\pi\,\mathrm W$. What is the intensity at $5\,\mathrm m$, with no absorption?

- $0{,}5\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The power is distributed over a sphere of area $4\pi r^2$. Thus we obtain $I=\dfrac{P}{4\pi r^2}$. The result is $1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
