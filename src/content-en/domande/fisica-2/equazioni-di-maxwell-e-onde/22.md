A beam in vacuum is normally incident on a perfectly absorbing surface with intensity $300\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the radiation pressure? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $5\cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

For complete absorption and normal incidence, we have $p=\dfrac{I}{c}$. The pressure results from momentum transfer. The result is $1\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
