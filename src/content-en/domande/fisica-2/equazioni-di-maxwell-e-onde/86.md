A beam is normally incident on a surface that absorbs it completely. The incident power is constant over a finite interval. Use $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Given the pulse duration $t = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm s$ and the magnitude of the mechanical impulse $J = 6\,10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,s}$, what is the beam power $P$?

- $6\,\mathrm W$
- $12\,\mathrm W$
- $24\,\mathrm W$
- $18\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The light transfers momentum to the surface equal to the incident energy $Pt$ divided by $c$. In the stated units, we have $J = \dfrac{P \cdot t}{3}$. Solving for the unknown gives $P = \dfrac{3 \cdot J}{t}$. With the given values, we obtain $12\,\mathrm W$.
