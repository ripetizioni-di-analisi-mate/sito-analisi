A uniform beam is normally incident on a detector. The intensity remains constant during the measurement and the detector absorbs all the beam that strikes it. Given the area $A = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$ and the absorbed energy $U = 200\,\mathrm J$, what is the average intensity $J$?

- $200\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $300\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $400\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The flux of the average Poynting vector gives the power $JA$ through the detector. Multiplying by the duration gives the energy. In the stated units, we have $U = A \cdot J \cdot t$. Solving for the unknown gives $J = \dfrac{U}{A \cdot t}$. With the given values, we obtain $200\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
