For a plane wave in vacuum, how do the instantaneous electric and magnetic energy densities compare?

- The electric energy density is always twice as large
- The magnetic energy density is always zero
- They have opposite signs
- They are equal

## Explanation

The densities are $u_E=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0E^2}{2}$ and $u_B=\dfrac{B^2}{2\mu_0}$. Using $E=cB$ and $c^2=\dfrac{1}{\varepsilon_0\mu_0}$ gives equality. Both are nonnegative.
