What current appears between the plates of an ideal capacitor in vacuum during charging?

- Only electronic conduction current in ideal vacuum
- No current term in the complete law
- Displacement current without conduction through the vacuum
- A current always opposite to the current in the wires

## Explanation

Ideally, the charges do not cross the empty space between the plates. The electric field changes with time. Its derivative therefore produces the displacement current term.
