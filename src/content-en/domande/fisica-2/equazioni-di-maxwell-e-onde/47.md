A light signal travels in a straight line between an emitter and a detector in a transparent medium. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Given the distance between emitter and detector $d = 600\,\mathrm m$ and the transit time $t = 3\,\mathrm{\mu s}$, what is the refractive index $n$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Light propagates at speed $c/n$. Dividing the distance by this speed and converting the time to microseconds gives the delay. In the stated units, we have $t = \dfrac{d \cdot n}{300}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{300 \cdot t}{d}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
