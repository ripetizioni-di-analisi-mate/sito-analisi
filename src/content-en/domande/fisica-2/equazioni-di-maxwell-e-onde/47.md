The voltage $v(t) = V_0 \sin(\omega t)$, with $V_0 = 50\,\mathrm{V}$ and frequency $f = 1.0\,\mathrm{kHz}$, is applied across a capacitor of capacitance $C = 10\,\mathrm{nF}$. What is the maximum value of the displacement current between the plates?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The charge on the positive plate is $q = C v$, so the current in the wires is $i = C\dfrac{dv}{dt} = C \omega V_0 \cos(\omega t)$.
Between the plates the displacement current equals the conduction current in the wires, therefore its maximum value is
$$
i_{s,\max} = C\,\omega\,V_0 = 2\pi f\, C\, V_0 = 2\pi \cdot 1.0 \cdot 10^{3} \cdot 10 \cdot 10^{-9} \cdot 50\,\mathrm{A} \approx 3.1 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{A} .
$$
Hence the maximum displacement current is **about $3.1\,\mathrm{mA}$**; the value $2.2\,\mathrm{mA}$ is the rms value.
