In vacuum, we have $\varepsilon_0\mu_0=\dfrac{1}{9\cdot10^{16}}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^2}$. What speed do Maxwell's equations predict?

- $3\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}5\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}2\cdot 10^{9}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The wave speed is $v=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_0\mu_0}}$. The square root of the given denominator is $3\cdot10^8$. The result is $3\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
