A harmonic plane wave has phase $kx-\omega t$. Two instants at the same point are compared, separated by an interval shorter than the period. Given the frequency $f = 25\,\mathrm{MHz}$ and the time interval $t = \dfrac{1}{100}\,\mathrm{\mu s}$, what is the magnitude of the phase difference $\phi$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

At a fixed position the phase change is $-\omega t$. Its magnitude is $2\pi ft$, with consistent megahertz and microsecond units. In the stated units, we have $\phi = 2 \cdot \pi \cdot f \cdot t$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
