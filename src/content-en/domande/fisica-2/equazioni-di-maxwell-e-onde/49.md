A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum with circular plates of radius $R = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is charged by a current $i = 2.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Between the plates, parallel and coaxial to them, there is a disk of radius $r = 4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Neglecting edge effects, what displacement current crosses the disk?

- $0.32\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.13\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The field between the plates is uniform, so the flux through the disk is the fraction $\dfrac{\pi r^2}{\pi R^2}$ of the flux through the whole area of the plates.
Through the whole area the displacement current equals the charging current $i$.
Therefore through the disk
$$
i_s = i\,\frac{r^2}{R^2} = 2.0 \cdot \frac{(4.0)^2}{(10)^2}\,\mathrm{A} = 0.32\,\mathrm{A} .
$$
