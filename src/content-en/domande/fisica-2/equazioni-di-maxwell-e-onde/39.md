What does the Poynting vector represent?

- The electric charge density
- Only the force on a stationary charge
- The electromagnetic energy flux density
- The frequency of the field

## Explanation

Its magnitude measures the power crossing a unit area normal to the vector. Its direction indicates the energy flow. In vacuum, $\vec S=\dfrac{(\vec E\times\vec B)}{\mu_0}$ holds.
