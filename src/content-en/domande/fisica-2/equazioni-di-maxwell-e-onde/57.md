A plane electromagnetic wave crosses a homogeneous transparent region. The propagation speed is expressed in megameters per second and the magnetic field in nanoteslas. Given the propagation speed $b = 200\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$ and the electric field amplitude $E = 10\,\mathrm{V/m}$, what is the magnetic field amplitude $B$?

- $100\,\mathrm{nT}$
- $25\,\mathrm{nT}$
- $50\,\mathrm{nT}$
- $5\,\mathrm{nT}$

## Explanation

For a plane wave the amplitudes satisfy $E=vB$. Converting megameters per second and nanoteslas gives the factor $10^{-3}$. In the stated units, we have $E = \dfrac{B \cdot b}{1000}$. Solving for the unknown gives $B = \dfrac{1000 \cdot E}{b}$. With the given values, we obtain $50\,\mathrm{nT}$.
