The electric field of a harmonic plane wave in vacuum is $E(x,t) = E_0 \sin(kx - \omega t)$, with frequency $f = 50\,\mathrm{MHz}$. At the point $x = \dfrac{\lambda}{4}$ the field equals $E_0$ at the instant $t = 0$. After how much time does the field at that point vanish for the first time?

- $10\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $20\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

At the point $x = \dfrac{\lambda}{4}$ we have $kx = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so $E = E_0 \sin\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \omega t \right) = E_0 \cos(\omega t)$.
The cosine vanishes for the first time when $\omega t = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, that is, after a quarter of a period.
The period is $T = \dfrac{1}{f} = 20\,\mathrm{ns}$, therefore the field vanishes after $\dfrac{T}{4} = 5.0\,\mathrm{ns}$.
