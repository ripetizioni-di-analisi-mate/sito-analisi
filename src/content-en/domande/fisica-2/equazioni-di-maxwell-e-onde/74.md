In the air gap of an ideal parallel-plate capacitor, the electric field normal to the plates increases uniformly. Use $\varepsilon_0=8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$. Initially, the rate of increase of the field $b = 2\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$ and the plate area $A = \dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ are given. In the second configuration, the rate of increase of the field becomes $4\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $I_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The displacement current is $\varepsilon_0\,d\Phi_E/dt$. In the ideal capacitor the flux is $AE$. Applying $I_{e} = \dfrac{177 \cdot A \cdot b}{20}$ gives $\dfrac{177}{100}\,\mathrm{mA}$ before the change and $\dfrac{177}{50}\,\mathrm{mA}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
