A uniform beam is normally incident on an ideal mirror. The light is completely reflected. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Given the beam intensity $J = 90\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and the illuminated area $A = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what is the magnitude of the radiation force $F$?

- $60\,\mathrm{nN}$
- $240\,\mathrm{nN}$
- $120\,\mathrm{nN}$
- $180\,\mathrm{nN}$

## Explanation

Reflection reverses the normal momentum. The force is twice the incident power $JA$ divided by the speed of light. In the stated units, we have $F = \dfrac{20 \cdot A \cdot J}{3}$. With the given values, we obtain $120\,\mathrm{nN}$.
