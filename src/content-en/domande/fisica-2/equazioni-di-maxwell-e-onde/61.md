In a region of vacuum the static electric field is $\vec{E} = \left( 300\, x\,\hat{\imath} + 200\, y\,\hat{\jmath} \right)\ \mathrm{V/m}$, with $x$ and $y$ in meters. What is the volume charge density in the region?

- $4.4 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/m^3}$
- $2.7 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/m^3}$
- $1.8 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/m^3}$
- $5.6 \cdot 10^{13}\,\mathrm{C/m^3}$

## Explanation

By the first Maxwell equation in local form, $\nabla \cdot \vec{E} = \dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$, so $\rho = \varepsilon_0\, \nabla \cdot \vec{E}$.
The divergence is
$$
\nabla \cdot \vec{E} = \frac{\partial (300\,x)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (200\,y)}{\partial y} = 500\,\mathrm{V/m^2} .
$$
Therefore $\rho = 8.85 \cdot 10^{-12} \cdot 500\,\mathrm{C/m^3} \approx 4.4 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/m^3}$, uniform throughout the region.
