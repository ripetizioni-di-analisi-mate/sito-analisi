In a traveling plane wave in vacuum, what is the phase relation between the electric and magnetic field oscillations?

- In phase
- Always with a phase difference of $90^\circ$
- In opposite phase at every point
- With different frequencies

## Explanation

The traveling-wave solutions of Maxwell's equations have the same phase factor for both fields. Their maxima occur together. The amplitudes satisfy $E_0=cB_0$.
