In a plane wave in vacuum, what geometric relation connects the fields and the direction of propagation?

- The electric field is parallel to the direction of propagation
- The two fields are always parallel
- The magnetic field is parallel to the direction of propagation
- The three directions are mutually perpendicular

## Explanation

An electromagnetic wave in vacuum is transverse. Both fields are perpendicular to the direction of propagation. Moreover, $\vec E$ and $\vec B$ are perpendicular to each other.
