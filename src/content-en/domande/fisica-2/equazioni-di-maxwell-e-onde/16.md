A plane wave in vacuum has average intensity $90\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is its average total energy density? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $1{,}5\cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $6\cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $1{,}2\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $3\cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$

## Explanation

For a plane wave, $I=c\langle u\rangle$ holds. Thus we obtain $\langle u\rangle=\dfrac{I}{c}$. The result is $3\cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$.
