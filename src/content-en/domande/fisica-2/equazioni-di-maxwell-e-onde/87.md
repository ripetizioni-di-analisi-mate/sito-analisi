A beam is normally incident on a surface that absorbs it completely. The incident power is constant over a finite interval. Use $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Given the beam power $P = 12\,\mathrm W$ and the magnitude of the mechanical impulse $J = 6\,10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,s}$, what is the pulse duration $t$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm s$
- $3\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The light transfers momentum to the surface equal to the incident energy $Pt$ divided by $c$. In the stated units, we have $J = \dfrac{P \cdot t}{3}$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{3 \cdot J}{P}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm s$.
