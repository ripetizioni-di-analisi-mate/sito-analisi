A light signal travels in a straight line between an emitter and a detector in a transparent medium. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Given the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the transit time $t = 3\,\mathrm{\mu s}$, what is the distance between emitter and detector $d$?

- $1350\,\mathrm m$
- $300\,\mathrm m$
- $1200\,\mathrm m$
- $600\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Light propagates at speed $c/n$. Dividing the distance by this speed and converting the time to microseconds gives the delay. In the stated units, we have $t = \dfrac{d \cdot n}{300}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{300 \cdot t}{n}$. With the given values, we obtain $600\,\mathrm m$.
