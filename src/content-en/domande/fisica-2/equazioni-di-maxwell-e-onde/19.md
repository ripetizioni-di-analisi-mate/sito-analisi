A uniform beam has intensity $20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and strikes an area $0{,}30\,\mathrm{m^2}$ normally. What is the incident power?

- $3\,\mathrm W$
- $12\,\mathrm W$
- $6\,\mathrm W$
- $24\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Intensity is power per unit perpendicular area. Thus we obtain $P=IA$. The result is $6\,\mathrm W$.
