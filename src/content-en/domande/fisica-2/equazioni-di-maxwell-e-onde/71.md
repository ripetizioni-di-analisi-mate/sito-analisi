In the air gap of an ideal parallel-plate capacitor, the electric field normal to the plates increases uniformly. Use $\varepsilon_0=8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$. Given the rate of increase of the field $b = 2\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$ and the plate area $A = \dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what is the magnitude of the displacement current $I_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{177}{100}\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $\dfrac{531}{200}\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $\dfrac{177}{200}\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $\dfrac{177}{50}\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The displacement current is $\varepsilon_0\,d\Phi_E/dt$. In the ideal capacitor the flux is $AE$. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{177 \cdot A \cdot b}{20}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{177}{100}\,\mathrm{mA}$.
