What description characterizes the linear polarization of a plane wave?

- The electric field is parallel to the direction of propagation
- The magnetic field is zero
- The electric field is constant in time
- The electric field oscillates along a fixed transverse direction

## Explanation

Polarization describes the orientation of the electric field. In the linear case the direction of oscillation is fixed. It remains perpendicular to the direction of propagation.
