An ideal capacitor in vacuum charges with current $0{,}40\,\mathrm A$. Neglecting edge effects, what is the displacement current between the plates?

- $0{,}4\,\mathrm A$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm A$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm A$
- $1{,}6\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The change in the field between the plates is due to the arriving charge. The term $\varepsilon_0\,\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$ therefore equals the current in the wires. The result is $0{,}4\,\mathrm A$.
