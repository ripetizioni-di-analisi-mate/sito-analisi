A sinusoidal plane wave has $E_0=300\,\mathrm{V/m}$ in a medium with impedance $Z=375\,\Omega$. What is its average intensity?

- $60\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $120\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $240\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $480\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

For a sinusoidal plane wave, we have $I=\dfrac{E_0^2}{2Z}$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ comes from the time average of the squared cosine. The result is $120\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
