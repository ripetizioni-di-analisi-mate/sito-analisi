A uniform beam is normally incident on a detector. The intensity remains constant during the measurement and the detector absorbs all the beam that strikes it. Given the average intensity $J = 200\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$, the area $A = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the absorbed energy $U$?

- $300\,\mathrm J$
- $200\,\mathrm J$
- $100\,\mathrm J$
- $400\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The flux of the average Poynting vector gives the power $JA$ through the detector. Multiplying by the duration gives the energy. In the stated units, we have $U = A \cdot J \cdot t$. With the given values, we obtain $200\,\mathrm J$.
