In vacuum two plane electromagnetic pulses travel along the $x$ axis in opposite directions, toward each other. At the instant $t = 0$ their centers are $12\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. After how much time do the centers of the two pulses overlap?

- $40\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $10\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $20\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $80\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

By d'Alembert's formula the field is the sum $f(x - ct) + g(x + ct)$ of a pulse moving toward increasing $x$ and one moving toward decreasing $x$, both with speed $c$.
The distance between the two centers therefore decreases by $2c$ every second.
Hence the centers overlap after
$$
t = \frac{12}{2 \cdot 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{s} = 20\,\mathrm{ns} .
$$
The pulses pass through each other without changing shape, because the wave equation is linear.
