An isotropic point source produces intensity $20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ at a distance $r$. What is the intensity at $2r$, with no absorption?

- $5\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $2{,}5\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The spherical-wave intensity varies as $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$. Doubling the distance divides it by four. The result is $5\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
