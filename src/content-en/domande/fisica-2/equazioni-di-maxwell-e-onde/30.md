An oscilloscope records $12$ peaks of the electric field of a harmonic wave, counting both the first and the last peak. The elapsed time from first to last is $22\,\mathrm{ns}$. What is the period?

- $\dfrac{11}{6}\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $2\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $22\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $1\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

Twelve consecutive peaks enclose eleven complete intervals. Dividing $22\,\mathrm{ns}$ by eleven gives $2\,\mathrm{ns}$ per interval.
