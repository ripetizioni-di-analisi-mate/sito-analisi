A radio wave in vacuum has period $10\,\mathrm{ns}$. What is its wavelength? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In one period the wavefront travels one wavelength. Thus we obtain $\lambda=cT$. The result is $3\,\mathrm m$.
