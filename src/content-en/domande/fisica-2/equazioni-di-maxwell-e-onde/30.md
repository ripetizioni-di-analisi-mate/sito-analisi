At a fixed point in space the electric field of a harmonic electromagnetic wave traveling in vacuum goes from a maximum to the next minimum in $2.5\,\mathrm{ns}$. What is the wavelength? Use $c = 3.0 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At a fixed point the field varies sinusoidally in time, and half a period elapses between a maximum and the next minimum. The period is therefore $T = 2 \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{ns} = 5.0\,\mathrm{ns}$.

In one period the wave travels one wavelength, therefore
$$
\lambda = cT = 3.0 \cdot 10^{8} \cdot 5.0 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.5\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by taking $2.5\,\mathrm{ns}$ as the period, and $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ by taking it as a quarter of a period.
