What quantity determines the spectral color of light when it passes from one stationary medium to another?

- The wavelength in the medium, which always stays the same
- The frequency, which remains unchanged
- The speed, which always stays the same
- The net charge of the beam

## Explanation

The frequency is imposed by the source and does not change during the transition. The speed and wavelength can change. Spectral identification therefore refers to the frequency or the vacuum wavelength.
