A harmonic plane wave has phase $kx-\omega t$. Two instants at the same point are compared, separated by an interval shorter than the period. Given the frequency $f = 25\,\mathrm{MHz}$ and the magnitude of the phase difference $\phi = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the time interval $t$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{50}\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{200}\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{\mu s}$

## Explanation

At a fixed position the phase change is $-\omega t$. Its magnitude is $2\pi ft$, with consistent megahertz and microsecond units. In the stated units, we have $\phi = 2 \cdot \pi \cdot f \cdot t$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{\phi}{2 \cdot \pi \cdot f}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{100}\,\mathrm{\mu s}$.
