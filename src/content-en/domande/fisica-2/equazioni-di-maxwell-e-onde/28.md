A wave has vacuum wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ and enters a nondispersive medium of refractive index $1{,}5$. What is its wavelength in the medium?

- $200\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $800\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $400\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The frequency remains fixed by the source. The speed decreases by a factor $n$, so $\lambda$ is also divided by $n$. The result is $400\,\mathrm{nm}$.
