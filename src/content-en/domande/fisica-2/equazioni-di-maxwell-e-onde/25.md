A light pulse has constant power $50\,\mathrm W$ for $2\,\mathrm{ms}$. What energy does it carry?

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}05\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The pulse energy is $U=P\Delta t$. The duration must be converted to seconds. The result is $0{,}1\,\mathrm J$.
