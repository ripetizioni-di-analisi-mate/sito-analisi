What term completes Ampère's law in time-varying situations?

- The total charge of the conductor divided by its mass
- The displacement current
- The constant magnetic potential
- The kinetic energy of the electrons

## Explanation

Maxwell adds $\varepsilon_0\,\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$ to the conduction current. This term accounts for the changing electric field. It makes the law consistent with charge conservation.
