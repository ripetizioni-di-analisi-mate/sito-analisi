A harmonic plane wave in vacuum has intensity $I = 50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the maximum value reached by the magnitude of the Poynting vector at a point crossed by the wave?

- $100\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $71\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the Poynting vector is $S = \dfrac{E^2}{\mu_0 c} = \dfrac{E_0^2}{\mu_0 c}\,\sin^2(kx - \omega t)$, so its maximum value is $\dfrac{E_0^2}{\mu_0 c}$.
The intensity is the average value of $S$, that is, $I = \dfrac{E_0^2}{2\mu_0 c}$, because the average value of $\sin^2$ over a period is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
Therefore the maximum value is $S_{\max} = 2I = 100\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
