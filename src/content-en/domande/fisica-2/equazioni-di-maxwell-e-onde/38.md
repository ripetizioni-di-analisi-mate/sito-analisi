A wave is written as $E(x,t)=E_0\cos(kx+\omega t)$ with $k,\omega>0$. In which direction does it propagate?

- Toward $+x$
- Toward $-x$
- Toward $+y$
- It does not propagate

## Explanation

A constant phase satisfies $kx+\omega t=costante$. Differentiating gives $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=-\dfrac{\omega}{k}$. The negative sign indicates propagation toward $-x$.
