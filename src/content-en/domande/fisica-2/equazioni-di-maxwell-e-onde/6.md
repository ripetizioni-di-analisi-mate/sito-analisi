The electric flux through a surface increases at $2\cdot10^{11}\,\mathrm{V\,m/s}$. What is the displacement current in vacuum? Use $\varepsilon_0=8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$.

- $0{,}885\,\mathrm A$
- $1{,}77\,\mathrm A$
- $3{,}54\,\mathrm A$
- $7{,}08\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The displacement current is $I_d=\varepsilon_0\,\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$. This term depends on the change in electric flux. The result is $1{,}77\,\mathrm A$.
