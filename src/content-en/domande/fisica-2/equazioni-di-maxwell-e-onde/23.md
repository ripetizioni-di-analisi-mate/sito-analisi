A beam in vacuum is normally incident on a perfect mirror with intensity $300\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the radiation pressure? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $1\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Reflection reverses the normal momentum of the light. The pressure is therefore $p=\dfrac{2I}{c}$. The result is $2\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
