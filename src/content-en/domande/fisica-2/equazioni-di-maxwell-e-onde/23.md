Two brief light pulses hit opposite sides of an initially stationary perfect mirror normally and simultaneously. The pulse from the left carries $6\,\mathrm{mJ}$ and that from the right $4\,\mathrm{mJ}$. Neglecting recoil during reflection, what net mechanical impulse does the mirror receive?

- $(2\,\mathrm{mJ})/c$, to the right.
- $(20\,\mathrm{mJ})/c$, to the right.
- $(4\,\mathrm{mJ})/c$, to the right.
- $(4\,\mathrm{mJ})/c$, to the left.

## Explanation

Reflection of a pulse transfers $2E/c$ of momentum to the mirror in the direction of incidence. The opposing contributions subtract, giving $2(6-4)\,\mathrm{mJ}/c$.
