Which Maxwell equation expresses the absence of magnetic monopoles in the classical model?

- $\nabla\cdot\vec E=0$ in the presence of any charge
- $\nabla\times\vec E=0$ in every situation
- $\vec B=0$ everywhere
- $\nabla\cdot\vec B=0$

## Explanation

The magnetic divergence is zero. By the divergence theorem, the flux through every closed surface is also zero. This does not imply the absence of a magnetic field.
