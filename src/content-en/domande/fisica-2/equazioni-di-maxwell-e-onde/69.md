An antenna is driven by an $LC$ circuit with $L = 2.0\,\mu\mathrm{H}$ and $C = 5.0\,\mathrm{pF}$, and the charges in the antenna oscillate at the natural frequency of the circuit. What is the wavelength of the waves emitted in vacuum?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.95\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The natural frequency of the circuit is
$$
f = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{2.0 \cdot 10^{-6} \cdot 5.0 \cdot 10^{-12}}}\,\mathrm{Hz} \approx 5.0 \cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{Hz} .
$$
The antenna emits a wave of the same frequency, so the wavelength is
$$
\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = 2\pi c \sqrt{LC} \approx 6.0\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.95\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by dropping the factor $2\pi$, that is, by using the angular frequency instead of the frequency.
