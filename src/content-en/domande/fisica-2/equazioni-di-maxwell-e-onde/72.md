In the air gap of an ideal parallel-plate capacitor, the electric field normal to the plates increases uniformly. Use $\varepsilon_0=8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$. Given the plate area $A = \dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the magnitude of the displacement current $I_{e} = \dfrac{177}{100}\,\mathrm{mA}$, what is the rate of increase of the field $b$?

- $4\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$
- $1\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$
- $3\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$
- $2\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$

## Explanation

The displacement current is $\varepsilon_0\,d\Phi_E/dt$. In the ideal capacitor the flux is $AE$. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{177 \cdot A \cdot b}{20}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{20 \cdot I_{e}}{177 \cdot A}$. With the given values, we obtain $2\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$.
