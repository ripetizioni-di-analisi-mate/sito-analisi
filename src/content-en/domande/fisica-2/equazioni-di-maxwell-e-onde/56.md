A plane electromagnetic wave crosses a homogeneous transparent region. The propagation speed is expressed in megameters per second and the magnetic field in nanoteslas. Given the magnetic field amplitude $B = 50\,\mathrm{nT}$ and the propagation speed $b = 200\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$, what is the electric field amplitude $E$?

- $5\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

For a plane wave the amplitudes satisfy $E=vB$. Converting megameters per second and nanoteslas gives the factor $10^{-3}$. In the stated units, we have $E = \dfrac{B \cdot b}{1000}$. With the given values, we obtain $10\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
