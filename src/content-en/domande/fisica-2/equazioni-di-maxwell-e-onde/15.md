The electric field amplitude of a plane wave doubles in the same medium. If the initial intensity is $7\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$, what is the final intensity?

- $14\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $56\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $28\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $112\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The intensity is proportional to the square of the electric field amplitude. Doubling the amplitude therefore quadruples the intensity. The result is $28\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
