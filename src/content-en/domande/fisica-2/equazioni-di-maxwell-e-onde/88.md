In a time-dependent electric field, a fixed plane surface is oriented with its normal parallel to the uniform field. The positive change in flux is measured. Given the area $A = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the increase in the field magnitude $b = 90\,\mathrm{V/m}$, what is the increase in electric flux $\Phi$?

- $18\,\mathrm{V\,m}$
- $54\,\mathrm{V\,m}$
- $72\,\mathrm{V\,m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{V\,m}$

## Explanation

The dot product is $AE$ because the field and the normal point in the same direction. The area is fixed, so the change is $A$ times the increase in the field. In the stated units, we have $\Phi = A \cdot b$. With the given values, we obtain $36\,\mathrm{V\,m}$.
