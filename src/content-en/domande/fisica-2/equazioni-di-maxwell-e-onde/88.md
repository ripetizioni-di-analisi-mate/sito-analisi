Which of the following lists of bands of the electromagnetic spectrum is ordered by increasing frequency?

- Gamma rays, X-rays, ultraviolet, visible light, infrared, microwaves, radio waves
- Radio waves, microwaves, visible light, infrared, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays
- Radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, gamma rays, X-rays
- Radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays

## Explanation

In vacuum all waves have speed $c$, so the frequency $f = \dfrac{c}{\lambda}$ increases as the wavelength decreases.
Wavelengths decrease going from radio waves (above $1\,\mathrm{m}$) to microwaves, infrared (down to $700\,\mathrm{nm}$), visible light ($700$-$400\,\mathrm{nm}$), ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays (below $0.01\,\mathrm{nm}$).
Therefore the list in order of increasing frequency starts with radio waves and ends with gamma rays, with infrared before visible light.
