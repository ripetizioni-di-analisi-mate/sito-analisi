A light signal travels in a straight line between an emitter and a detector in a transparent medium. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Given the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the distance between emitter and detector $d = 600\,\mathrm m$, what is the transit time $t$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu s}$

## Explanation

Light propagates at speed $c/n$. Dividing the distance by this speed and converting the time to microseconds gives the delay. In the stated units, we have $t = \dfrac{d \cdot n}{300}$. With the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{\mu s}$.
