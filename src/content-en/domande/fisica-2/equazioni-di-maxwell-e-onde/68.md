Which of the following sources emits electromagnetic waves?

- A charge at rest at a point
- An electron in uniform straight-line motion in vacuum
- A straight wire carrying a constant direct current
- An electron oscillating back and forth along an antenna

## Explanation

Electromagnetic waves are produced by **accelerated** charges.
A charge at rest produces an electrostatic field, and a charge in uniform straight-line motion has no acceleration.
In a wire with constant direct current the fields are stationary, so there is no propagating field.
An electron oscillating along an antenna has an acceleration that periodically reverses, therefore it emits a wave with the same frequency as the oscillation.
