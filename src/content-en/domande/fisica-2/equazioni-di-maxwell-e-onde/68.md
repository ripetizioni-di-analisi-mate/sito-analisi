A plane surface absorbs all the incident light. The angle between the beam and the normal to the surface is $60^\circ$. The intensity is measured on a plane perpendicular to the beam. Given the beam intensity $J = 240\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and the absorbed power $P = 60\,\mathrm W$, what is the area $A$?

- $1\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The area exposed to the beam is the projection $A\cos60^\circ$. The absorbed power is the intensity times the projected area. In the stated units, we have $P = \dfrac{A \cdot J}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{2 \cdot P}{J}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
