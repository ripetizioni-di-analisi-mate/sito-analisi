In a region of vacuum, a uniform field increases at $10^6\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$. What is the magnitude of the displacement current density? Use $\varepsilon_0=8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$.

- $4{,}42\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$
- $1{,}77\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$
- $8{,}85\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$
- $3{,}54\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$

## Explanation

The displacement current density is $\vec J_d=\varepsilon_0\,\dfrac{\partial\vec E}{\partial t}$. The derivative of the field is multiplied by the permittivity. The result is $8{,}85\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$.
