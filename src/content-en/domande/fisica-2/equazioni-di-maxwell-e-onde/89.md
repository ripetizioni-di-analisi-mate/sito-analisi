In a time-dependent electric field, a fixed plane surface is oriented with its normal parallel to the uniform field. The positive change in flux is measured. Given the increase in the field magnitude $b = 90\,\mathrm{V/m}$ and the increase in electric flux $\Phi = 36\,\mathrm{V\,m}$, what is the area $A$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The dot product is $AE$ because the field and the normal point in the same direction. The area is fixed, so the change is $A$ times the increase in the field. In the stated units, we have $\Phi = A \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{\Phi}{b}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
