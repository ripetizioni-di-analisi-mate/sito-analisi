A harmonic plane wave travels in a homogeneous glass with refractive index $n = 1.5$ and $\mu_r = 1$, and the amplitude of its electric field is $E_0 = 300\,\mathrm{V/m}$. What is the amplitude of the magnetic field?

- $1.5\,\mu\mathrm{T}$
- $1.0\,\mu\mathrm{T}$
- $0.67\,\mu\mathrm{T}$
- $2.3\,\mu\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

In the medium Maxwell's equations have the same form as in vacuum, with $\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r$ and $\mu_0 \mu_r$ in place of $\varepsilon_0$ and $\mu_0$, so the relation between the fields becomes $E = vB$, where $v$ is the speed of the wave in the medium.
Since $v = \dfrac{c}{n}$, we have
$$
B_0 = \frac{E_0}{v} = \frac{n E_0}{c} = \frac{1.5 \cdot 300}{3.00 \cdot 10^{8}}\,\mathrm{T} = 1.5 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T} .
$$
For the same electric field, in glass the magnetic field is $n$ times that of a wave in vacuum.
