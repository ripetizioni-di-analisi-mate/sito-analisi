A linear, nondispersive medium has $\varepsilon_r=4$ and $\mu_r=1$. What is the speed of electromagnetic waves? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $7{,}5\cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}5\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The speed is $v=\dfrac{c}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_r\mu_r}}$. The factor in the denominator is two. The result is $1{,}5\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
