In a plane wave in vacuum, $\vec E$ is along $+y$ and $\vec B$ is along $+z$. In which direction does the energy flow?

- Toward $-x$
- Toward $+y$
- Toward $+x$
- Toward $+z$

## Explanation

The Poynting vector is proportional to $\vec E\times\vec B$. Since $\hat\jmath\times\hat k=\hat\imath$, the flux is directed along $+x$. This is the direction of propagation.
