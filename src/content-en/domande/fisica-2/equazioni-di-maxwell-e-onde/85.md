A laser beam strikes a perfectly reflecting flat mirror at an angle of $30^\circ$ to the normal to the mirror. What is the direction of the average force that the beam exerts on the mirror?

- Perpendicular to the mirror, pushing into the mirror
- Along the incident beam
- Parallel to the surface of the mirror
- Along the reflected beam

## Explanation

In reflection the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence, so the component of the radiation's momentum parallel to the mirror stays the same.
The perpendicular component reverses, therefore the radiation's momentum changes only in the direction perpendicular to the mirror, pointing outward.
The mirror receives an equal and opposite momentum, so by the impulse-momentum theorem the force is **perpendicular to the mirror and pushes into it**.
