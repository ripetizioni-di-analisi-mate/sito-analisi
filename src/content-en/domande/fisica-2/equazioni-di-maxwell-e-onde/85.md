A beam is normally incident on a surface that absorbs it completely. The incident power is constant over a finite interval. Use $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Given the beam power $P = 12\,\mathrm W$ and the pulse duration $t = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm s$, what is the magnitude of the mechanical impulse $J$?

- $12\,10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $3\,10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $6\,10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $9\,10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,s}$

## Explanation

The light transfers momentum to the surface equal to the incident energy $Pt$ divided by $c$. In the stated units, we have $J = \dfrac{P \cdot t}{3}$. With the given values, we obtain $6\,10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,s}$.
