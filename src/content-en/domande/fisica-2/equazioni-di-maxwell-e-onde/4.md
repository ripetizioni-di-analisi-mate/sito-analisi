A travelling plane wave in vacuum has instantaneous $\vec E$ pointing north and $\vec B$ upwards. At another point a second observer finds that both fields point in their opposite directions. How do the two propagation directions compare?

- The first points east and the second west.
- Both point north.
- The second points downwards.
- Both point east.

## Explanation

With east, north and up forming a right-handed frame, $\vec E\times\vec B$ points east. Reversing both factors leaves their cross product unchanged.
