A plane wave in vacuum has magnetic field amplitude $B_0=0{,}50\,\mu\mathrm T$. What is its electric field amplitude? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $75\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $600\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $150\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The amplitudes satisfy $E_0=cB_0$. The magnetic field must be expressed in teslas. The result is $150\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
