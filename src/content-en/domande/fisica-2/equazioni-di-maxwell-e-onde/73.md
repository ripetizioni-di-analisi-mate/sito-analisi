In the air gap of an ideal parallel-plate capacitor, the electric field normal to the plates increases uniformly. Use $\varepsilon_0=8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$. Given the rate of increase of the field $b = 2\,10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$ and the magnitude of the displacement current $I_{e} = \dfrac{177}{100}\,\mathrm{mA}$, what is the plate area $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The displacement current is $\varepsilon_0\,d\Phi_E/dt$. In the ideal capacitor the flux is $AE$. In the stated units, we have $I_{e} = \dfrac{177 \cdot A \cdot b}{20}$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{20 \cdot I_{e}}{177 \cdot b}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
