A plane wave in vacuum has average total energy density $8\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$. What is the average contribution of the magnetic field?

- $4\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $2\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $16\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$

## Explanation

In a plane wave in vacuum the electric and magnetic contributions are equal. Each therefore accounts for half the total energy. The result is $4\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$.
