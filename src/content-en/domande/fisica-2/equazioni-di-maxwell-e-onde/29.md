A wave of frequency $5\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$ passes from vacuum into a stationary transparent medium. What is its frequency in the medium?

- $5\cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2{,}5\cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1\cdot 10^{15}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2\cdot 10^{15}\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

At the interface the fields oscillate at the same rate on both sides. Thus the frequency remains unchanged during the transition. The result is $5\cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
