A plane electromagnetic wave crosses a homogeneous transparent region. The propagation speed is expressed in megameters per second and the magnetic field in nanoteslas. Given the magnetic field amplitude $B = 50\,\mathrm{nT}$ and the electric field amplitude $E = 10\,\mathrm{V/m}$, what is the propagation speed $b$?

- $400\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$
- $200\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$
- $100\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$

## Explanation

For a plane wave the amplitudes satisfy $E=vB$. Converting megameters per second and nanoteslas gives the factor $10^{-3}$. In the stated units, we have $E = \dfrac{B \cdot b}{1000}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{1000 \cdot E}{B}$. With the given values, we obtain $200\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$.
