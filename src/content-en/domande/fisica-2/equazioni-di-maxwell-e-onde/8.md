An Amperian surface is crossed by conduction current $3\,\mathrm A$ and displacement current $2\,\mathrm A$, both in the direction of the normal. What is the total current in the Ampère-Maxwell term?

- $1\,\mathrm A$
- $3\,\mathrm A$
- $6\,\mathrm A$
- $5\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The complete law adds the conduction current and displacement current. Since they have the same direction, both values are added with a positive sign. The result is $5\,\mathrm A$.
