A wave has wavelength $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is its angular wave number $k$?

- $15{,}7\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $31{,}4\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $62{,}8\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $126\,\mathrm{rad/m}$

## Explanation

The wave number is $k=\dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}$. It measures the change in phase per unit length. The result is $31{,}4\,\mathrm{rad/m}$.
