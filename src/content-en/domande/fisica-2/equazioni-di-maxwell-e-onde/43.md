For the same intensity and normal incidence, which surface receives greater radiation pressure?

- A perfect absorber compared with a perfect mirror
- Both pressures are always zero
- A perfect mirror compared with a perfect absorber
- It depends only on the mass of the surface

## Explanation

Absorption transfers the incident momentum. Normal reflection reverses the direction of the light. The momentum transfer and pressure are therefore doubled.
