A plane electromagnetic wave propagates in a transparent, nondispersive medium. Use $c=300\,\mathrm{Mm/s}$. Initially, the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the frequency $f = 100\,\mathrm{MHz}$ are given. In the second configuration, the refractive index becomes $3$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $\lambda$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The frequency remains that of the source. The speed is $c/n$ and the wavelength is the speed divided by the frequency. Applying $\lambda = \dfrac{300}{f \cdot n}$ gives $2\,\mathrm m$ before the change and $1\,\mathrm m$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
