A single slit is illuminated normally by monochromatic light of wavelength $500\,\mathrm{nm}$. The small-angle approximation is used. Given the slit width $b = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the distance from the center to the first minimum $d = 4\,\mathrm{mm}$, what is the screen distance $L$?

- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The first minimum satisfies $b\sin\theta=\lambda$. At small angles, we have $\sin\theta\simeq d/L$. In the stated units, we have $d = \dfrac{L}{2 \cdot b}$. Solving for the unknown gives $L = 2 \cdot b \cdot d$. With the given values, we obtain $2\,\mathrm m$.
