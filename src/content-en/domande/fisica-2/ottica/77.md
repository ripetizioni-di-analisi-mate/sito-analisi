In a Young experiment with light of wavelength $500\,\mathrm{nm}$, slits $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart and a screen at $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$, at what distance from the center of the screen is the third dark fringe, counted from the center toward one side?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The dark fringes lie in the directions $d\sin\theta = \left( m + \dfrac{1}{2} \right) \lambda$, so for small angles at the distances $y = \left( m + \dfrac{1}{2} \right) \dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$ from the center.

On one side of the center the dark fringes correspond to $m = 0, 1, 2, \dots$, therefore the third one has $m = 2$. The fringe spacing is $\dfrac{\lambda L}{d} = \dfrac{5.00 \cdot 10^{-7} \cdot 1.0}{2.5 \cdot 10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{m} = 2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$, so
$$
y = 2.5 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{mm} = 5.0\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
