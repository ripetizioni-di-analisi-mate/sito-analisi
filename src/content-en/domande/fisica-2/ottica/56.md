A ray passes from a medium of higher refractive index to one of lower refractive index. Angles are measured relative to the normal. Given the refractive index of the incident medium $n_{1} = \dfrac{8}{5}$ and the refractive index of the second medium $n_{2} = \dfrac{6}{5}$, what is the sine of the critical angle $b$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

At the critical angle the refracted ray is grazing. In Snell's law the sine of the angle of refraction is one, so we obtain $\sin i_{\mathrm{lim}}=n_2/n_1$. In the stated units, we have $b = \dfrac{n_{2}}{n_{1}}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
