A small lamp lies on the bottom of a swimming pool, at a depth of $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$, and the water surface ($n = 1.33$) is flat and still. The light of the lamp leaves into the air only through a disk of the surface centered on the vertical through the lamp. What is the radius of the disk?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A ray from the lamp leaves into the air only if it strikes the surface at an angle smaller than the critical angle; beyond the critical angle it is totally reflected. The critical angle between water and air is $\theta_c = \arcsin\dfrac{1}{1.33} \approx 48.8^\circ$.

The edge of the disk is therefore reached by the rays that form the angle $\theta_c$ with the vertical, hence the radius of the disk is
$$
r = h\tan\theta_c = \frac{h}{\sqrt{n^2 - 1}} = \frac{2.0}{\sqrt{1.33^2 - 1}}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2.3\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
