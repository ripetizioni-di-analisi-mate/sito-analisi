Light travels through a medium of refractive index $n=1{,}5$. What is its speed? Use $c=3{,}0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $2\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The refractive index is $n=\dfrac{c}{v}$. Thus we obtain $v=\dfrac{c}{n}$. The result is $2\cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
