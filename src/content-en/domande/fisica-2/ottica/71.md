In a Young double-slit experiment the light has wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$. The screen is far away and the small-angle approximation holds. Given the screen distance $L = 2\,\mathrm m$ and the slit separation $b = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$, what is the distance between two consecutive maxima $d$?

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The path difference is approximately $by/L$. To reach the next maximum it must increase by one wavelength. Conversion to millimeters gives the coefficient $0{,}6$. In the stated units, we have $d = \dfrac{3 \cdot L}{5 \cdot b}$. With the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{mm}$.
