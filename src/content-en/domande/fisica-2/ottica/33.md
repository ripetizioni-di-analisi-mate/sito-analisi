A ray passes through a parallel-sided slab immersed in the same medium on both sides. How does it emerge?

- Parallel to the incident ray, with a possible lateral displacement
- Always perpendicular to the slab
- With twice the angular deviation
- Always along the same straight line with no displacement

## Explanation

Snell's law at the two interfaces preserves $n\sin\theta$. The initial and final refractive indices are equal. The initial and final external angles are equal, while the internal path can produce a lateral displacement.
