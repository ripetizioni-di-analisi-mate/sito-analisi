In a Young experiment with light of wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$, one of the two slits is covered with a thin glass sheet, $6.0\,\mu\mathrm{m}$ thick and with refractive index $n = 1.50$; the rest of the apparatus is in air. By how many fringes does the interference pattern shift on the screen?

- $15$
- $10$
- $2.5$
- $5$

## Explanation

In the sheet the wavelength is $\dfrac{\lambda}{n}$, so across the thickness $s$ the wave performs $\dfrac{n\,s}{\lambda}$ oscillations, while in air it would perform $\dfrac{s}{\lambda}$.

The sheet therefore introduces the same phase delay as a path in air longer by $(n - 1)\,s$. Each increase of the path difference by $\lambda$ shifts the pattern by one fringe, so the shift is
$$
\frac{(n - 1)\,s}{\lambda} = \frac{0.50 \cdot 6.0 \cdot 10^{-6}}{6.00 \cdot 10^{-7}} = 5
$$
fringes, toward the side of the covered slit.
