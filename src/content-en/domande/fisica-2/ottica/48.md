A ray enters a transparent material from vacuum. The vacuum wavelength is used and the material is nondispersive over the range considered. Given the vacuum wavelength $b = 600\,\mathrm{nm}$ and the wavelength in the material $\lambda = 400\,\mathrm{nm}$, what is the refractive index $n$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The frequency does not change at the interface. The speed in the medium is divided by $n$, and the same applies to the wavelength. In the stated units, we have $\lambda = \dfrac{b}{n}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{b}{\lambda}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
