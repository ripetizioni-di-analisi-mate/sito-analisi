Two thin lenses in contact have optical powers $+2$ and $+3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. What is the equivalent optical power?

- $1\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $1{,}2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

For thin lenses in contact, the optical powers add. Thus we obtain $D_{eq}=D_1+D_2$. The result is $5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
