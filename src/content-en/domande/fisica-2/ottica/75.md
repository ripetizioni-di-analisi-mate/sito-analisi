In a Young experiment the two slits produce waves of equal amplitude on the screen, and at the center of the screen the intensity is $I_{\max}$. At a point $P$ of the screen the path difference between the two waves is $\dfrac{\lambda}{3}$. What is the intensity at $P$?

- $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,I_{\max}$
- $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{3}$

## Explanation

The path difference $\delta = \dfrac{\lambda}{3}$ corresponds to the phase difference $\varphi = \dfrac{2\pi\,\delta}{\lambda} = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

For waves of equal amplitude the intensity is $I = I_{\max} \cos^2\dfrac{\varphi}{2}$, so
$$
I = I_{\max} \cos^2\frac{\pi}{3} = I_{\max} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^2 = \frac{I_{\max}}{4} .
$$
