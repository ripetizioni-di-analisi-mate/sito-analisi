Two rays of the same wavelength accumulate a path difference $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$. What is the magnitude of the phase difference?

- $1{,}57\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0{,}785\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $3{,}14\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $6{,}28\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The phase difference is $\Delta\varphi=\dfrac{2\pi\Delta}{\lambda}$. A path difference of one quarter of a wavelength corresponds to $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The result is $1{,}57\,\mathrm{rad}$.
