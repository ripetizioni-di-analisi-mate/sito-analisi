An object is $2\,\mathrm m$ in front of a plane mirror. What is the distance between the object and its image?

- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $16\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The image is the reflection of the object across the mirror. It is therefore two meters behind the mirror, giving a total distance of four meters. The result is $4\,\mathrm m$.
