For two initially in-phase coherent waves of $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$, what is the smallest positive path difference that produces destructive interference?

- $150\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $300\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $600\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The first minimum requires $\Delta=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$. This difference introduces a phase shift of $\pi$. The result is $300\,\mathrm{nm}$.
