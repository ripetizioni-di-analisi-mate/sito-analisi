What condition justifies geometrical optics in relation to diffraction?

- Aperture and obstacle dimensions much larger than the wavelength
- Apertures always smaller than the wavelength
- Light necessarily of zero intensity
- Necessarily zero frequency

## Explanation

When the geometrical dimensions are much larger than $\lambda$, the angular spreading due to diffraction is small. Rays then describe propagation well. Apertures comparable to $\lambda$ require the wave model.
