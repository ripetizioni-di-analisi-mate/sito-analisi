What is the effect of increasing the number of illuminated slits in a grating at fixed spacing?

- The principal maxima become narrower
- Their angular positions necessarily change
- The wavelength changes
- The light always loses its polarization

## Explanation

The principal positions depend on the spacing and wavelength. The number of slits determines the sharpness of the interference pattern. Increasing it narrows the maxima.
