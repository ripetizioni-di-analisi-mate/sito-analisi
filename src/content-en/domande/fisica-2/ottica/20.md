A slit of width $0{,}20\,\mathrm{mm}$ is illuminated with $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$. On a screen at $2\,\mathrm m$, what is the width of the central maximum at small angles?

- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The first minima are at $y=\dfrac{\pm\lambda L}{a}$. The central width is therefore $\dfrac{2\lambda L}{a}$. The result is $10\,\mathrm{mm}$.
