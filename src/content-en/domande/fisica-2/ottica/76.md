A Young experiment is performed with light of vacuum wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$, slits $0.30\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart and a screen at $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$, with the whole apparatus immersed in water ($n = 1.33$). What is the fringe spacing?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The waves travel the paths from the slits to the screen in water, so the interference conditions are written with the wavelength in water, $\lambda_n = \dfrac{\lambda}{n} = \dfrac{600}{1.33}\,\mathrm{nm} \approx 451\,\mathrm{nm}$.

Therefore the fringe spacing is
$$
\Delta y = \frac{\lambda_n L}{d} = \frac{4.51 \cdot 10^{-7} \cdot 1.2}{3.0 \cdot 10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.8\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
