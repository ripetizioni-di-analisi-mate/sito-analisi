A biconvex glass lens with refractive index $1.50$ has focal length $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ in air. The lens is immersed in water, with index $1.33$. For a lens immersed in a medium of index $n_m$, in the lensmaker's formula the factor $n - 1$ is replaced by $\dfrac{n}{n_m} - 1$. What is the focal length of the lens in water?

- $59\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $78\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

By the **lensmaker's formula** in air $\dfrac{1}{f} = (n - 1)\,K$, where $K = \dfrac{1}{R_1} - \dfrac{1}{R_2}$ depends only on the shape of the lens. Hence $K = \dfrac{1}{0.50 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{cm}} = 0.10\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$.

In water the shape does not change, therefore
$$
\frac{1}{f_{\mathrm{w}}} = \left( \frac{1.50}{1.33} - 1 \right) \cdot 0.10\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}} \approx 0.128 \cdot 0.10\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}} ,
$$
so $f_{\mathrm{w}} \approx 78\,\mathrm{cm}$. The lens remains converging, but much less than in air, because the index of the glass is close to that of water.
