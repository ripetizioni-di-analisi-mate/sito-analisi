An object is placed exactly at the focus of a concave spherical mirror. In the paraxial approximation, where does the image form?

- The reflected rays are parallel to each other and the image does not form at a finite distance
- At the center of curvature, real and inverted
- At the focus itself, real and upright
- Behind the mirror, at distance $f$ from the vertex, virtual and upright

## Explanation

With $p = f$ the **mirror equation** gives
$$
\frac{1}{q} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{f} = 0 ,
$$
and no finite value of $q$ satisfies it.

The same result follows from the principal rays. Every ray through the focus is reflected parallel to the axis, so the reflected rays leaving the object point on the axis are all parallel and do not meet. One says that the image forms at infinity, and headlights and flashlights, with the bulb at the focus, are based on this.
