A ray enters a transparent material from vacuum. The vacuum wavelength is used and the material is nondispersive over the range considered. Given the vacuum wavelength $b = 600\,\mathrm{nm}$ and the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$, what is the wavelength in the material $\lambda$?

- $800\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $400\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $900\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $200\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The frequency does not change at the interface. The speed in the medium is divided by $n$, and the same applies to the wavelength. In the stated units, we have $\lambda = \dfrac{b}{n}$. With the given values, we obtain $400\,\mathrm{nm}$.
