A thin converging lens has focal length $0{,}50\,\mathrm m$. What is its optical power?

- $2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The optical power is $D=\dfrac{1}{f}$, with the focal length in meters. The inverse-meter unit is called the diopter. The result is $2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
