A converging lens forms a real, inverted image. The absolute values of the heights are considered. The object-lens distance is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. Given the object height $h_{0} = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the image height $h = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the image distance from the lens $d$?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

By similarity, the magnitude of the magnification is the ratio of image distance to object distance. A negative sign would describe the inversion. In the stated units, we have $h = \dfrac{d \cdot h_{0}}{30}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{30 \cdot h}{h_{0}}$. With the given values, we obtain $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
