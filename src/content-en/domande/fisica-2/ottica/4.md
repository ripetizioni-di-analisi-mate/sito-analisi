A ray passes from air of refractive index $1$ into a medium of refractive index $\sqrt2$, with angle of incidence $45^\circ$. What is the angle of refraction?

- $15\,{}^\circ$
- $45\,{}^\circ$
- $60\,{}^\circ$
- $30\,{}^\circ$

## Explanation

Snell's law gives $\sin\theta_2=\dfrac{\sin45^\circ}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. In the physical interval between zero and ninety degrees, we obtain $\theta_2=30^\circ$. The result is $30\,{}^\circ$.
