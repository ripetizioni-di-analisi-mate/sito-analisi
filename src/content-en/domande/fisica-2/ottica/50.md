A thin converging lens forms a real image on a screen. The object moves closer to the lens but remains beyond the focus. In which direction must the screen be moved to obtain a sharp image again?

- Move it closer to the lens
- Move it farther from the lens
- Leave it stationary
- Move it to the object side

## Explanation

In the equation $1/d=1/f-1/p$, decreasing the object distance $p$ increases $1/p$. A smaller positive value remains for $1/d$, so the new real image is farther away.
