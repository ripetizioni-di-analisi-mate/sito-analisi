A diverging lens forms the image of a real object. What is the image like in the thin-lens model?

- Always real and inverted
- Always real and magnified
- Virtual and always inverted
- Virtual, upright and reduced

## Explanation

With $f<0$ and $p>0$, the formula gives $q<0$. The magnification $-\dfrac{q}{p}$ is positive and less than one. Thus the image is virtual, upright and reduced.
