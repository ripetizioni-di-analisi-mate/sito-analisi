A plane mirror moves perpendicularly away from a fixed object. The object is in front of the mirror. Given the initial distance between object and image $d_{0} = 30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the mirror displacement $x = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the final distance between object and image $d$?

- $35\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $80\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front. Each centimeter traveled by the mirror increases the object-image distance by two centimeters. In the stated units, we have $d = d_{0} + 2 \cdot x$. With the given values, we obtain $40\,\mathrm{cm}$.
