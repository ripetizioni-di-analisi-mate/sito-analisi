Sunlight reflected by the horizontal surface of a lake is largely polarized. How must the transmission axis of the lenses of a pair of polarized sunglasses be oriented to reduce these reflections as much as possible?

- Horizontal
- Inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal
- Vertical
- Any, because the reduction does not depend on the orientation

## Explanation

Light reflected by a transparent surface is polarized, completely at Brewster's angle and partially at the other angles, perpendicular to the plane of incidence.

The plane of incidence contains the ray and the normal to the surface of the lake, which is vertical, so it is a vertical plane. Therefore the reflected light is polarized in the horizontal direction.

By **Malus's law** a polarizer with its axis perpendicular to the direction of polarization does not transmit the polarized light, so the lenses must have a **vertical** axis.
