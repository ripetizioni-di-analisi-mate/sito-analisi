A plane mirror moves perpendicularly away from a fixed object. The object is in front of the mirror. Given the initial distance between object and image $d_{0} = 30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the final distance between object and image $d = 40\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the mirror displacement $x$?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front. Each centimeter traveled by the mirror increases the object-image distance by two centimeters. In the stated units, we have $d = d_{0} + 2 \cdot x$. Solving for the unknown gives $x = \dfrac{d}{2} - \dfrac{d_{0}}{2}$. With the given values, we obtain $5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
