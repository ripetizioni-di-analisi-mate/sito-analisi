In Young's experiment, how does increasing the slit separation at fixed wavelength and screen position affect the fringe spacing?

- It increases
- It stays the same
- It decreases
- It always becomes zero for any separation

## Explanation

For small angles, we have $\Delta y=\dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$. The slit separation is in the denominator. Thus the fringe spacing decreases when $d$ increases.
