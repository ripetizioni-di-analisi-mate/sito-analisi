A ray travels from a medium of refractive index $n$ into vacuum. The incidence is below the critical angle. Initially, the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the sine of the angle of incidence $a = \dfrac{2}{5}$ are given. In the second configuration, the sine of the angle of incidence becomes $\dfrac{3}{5}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $b$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

By Snell's law, we have $n\sin i=\sin r$ because the refractive index of the second medium is one. Applying $b = a \cdot n$ gives $\dfrac{3}{5}$ before the change and $\dfrac{9}{10}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
