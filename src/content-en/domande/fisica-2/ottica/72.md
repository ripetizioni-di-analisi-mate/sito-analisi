The disk of the Sun is seen from the Earth under an angle of $0.53^\circ$. With a converging lens of focal length $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ a sharp image of the Sun is formed on a sheet of paper. What is the diameter of the image?

- $2.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4.6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The Sun is extremely far away, so by the **thin lens equation** its image forms at distance $q = f$, in the focal plane.

The rays that leave the two opposite edges of the solar disk and pass through the center of the lens are not deviated, and they form the angle $0.53^\circ$ with each other. These rays meet the focal plane at the points that are the images of the two edges, therefore the diameter of the image is
$$
D = 2 f \tan\frac{0.53^\circ}{2} = 2 \cdot 500\,\mathrm{mm} \cdot \tan 0.265^\circ \approx 4.6\,\mathrm{mm} .
$$
