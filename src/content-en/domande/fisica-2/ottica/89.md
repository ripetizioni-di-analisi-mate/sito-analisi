A converging lens forms a real, inverted image. The absolute values of the heights are considered. The object-lens distance is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. Given the image distance from the lens $d = 15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the image height $h = 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the object height $h_{0}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

By similarity, the magnitude of the magnification is the ratio of image distance to object distance. A negative sign would describe the inversion. In the stated units, we have $h = \dfrac{d \cdot h_{0}}{30}$. Solving for the unknown gives $h_{0} = \dfrac{30 \cdot h}{d}$. With the given values, we obtain $4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
