A grating of spacing $2\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ is illuminated normally. An existing maximum of positive order is considered. Given the order of the maximum $m = 2$ and the sine of the angle of the maximum $b = \dfrac{3}{5}$, what is the wavelength $\lambda$?

- $1200\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $600\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $300\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

For a maximum, the path difference $d\sin\theta$ is $m\lambda$. The grating spacing corresponds to $2000\,\mathrm{nm}$. In the stated units, we have $b = \dfrac{\lambda \cdot m}{2000}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\lambda = \dfrac{2000 \cdot b}{m}$. With the given values, we obtain $600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
