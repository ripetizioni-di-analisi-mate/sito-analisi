A grating of spacing $d=2\,\mu\mathrm m$ receives light at $500\,\mathrm{nm}$ at normal incidence. At what angle is the maximum of order $m=2$?

- $30\,{}^\circ$
- $15\,{}^\circ$
- $45\,{}^\circ$
- $60\,{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The grating satisfies $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$. Here the ratio $\dfrac{m\lambda}{d}$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result is $30\,{}^\circ$.
