Under normal dispersion in glass, which light does a prism deviate more?

- Red light compared with violet light
- All colors in the same way
- Only unpolarized light
- Violet light compared with red light

## Explanation

In this regime the refractive index of glass is greater at shorter wavelengths. Violet light is therefore refracted more. The prism thus separates the colors.
