A concave spherical mirror has radius of curvature $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its paraxial focal length?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $80\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For a spherical mirror in the paraxial approximation, we have $f=\dfrac{R}{2}$. The sign is positive for a concave mirror. The result is $20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
