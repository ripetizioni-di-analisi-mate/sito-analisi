A slit of width $0{,}10\,\mathrm{mm}$ is illuminated with $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$. What is $\sin\theta$ for the first diffraction minimum?

- $0{,}0025$
- $0{,}01$
- $0{,}005$
- $0{,}02$

## Explanation

The minima of a slit satisfy $a\sin\theta=m\lambda$. For the first minimum, we have $m=1$, so we obtain $\sin\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{a}$. The result is $0{,}005$.
