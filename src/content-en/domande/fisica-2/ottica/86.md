A plane mirror moves perpendicularly away from a fixed object. The object is in front of the mirror. Given the mirror displacement $x = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the final distance between object and image $d = 40\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the initial distance between object and image $d_{0}$?

- $35\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front. Each centimeter traveled by the mirror increases the object-image distance by two centimeters. In the stated units, we have $d = d_{0} + 2 \cdot x$. Solving for the unknown gives $d_{0} = d - 2 \cdot x$. With the given values, we obtain $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
