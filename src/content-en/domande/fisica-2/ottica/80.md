A slit of width $a$ is illuminated perpendicularly with monochromatic light of wavelength $\lambda$, and the intensity at the center of the diffraction pattern is $I_0$. What is the intensity in the direction $\theta$ for which $a\sin\theta = \dfrac{\lambda}{2}$?

- $0.64\,I_0$
- $0.41\,I_0$
- $0$
- $0.50\,I_0$

## Explanation

The intensity of the diffraction pattern is $I = I_0 \left( \dfrac{\sin\beta}{\beta} \right)^2$, with $\beta = \dfrac{\pi\,a\sin\theta}{\lambda}$.

In the direction considered $\beta = \dfrac{\pi}{\lambda} \cdot \dfrac{\lambda}{2} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so
$$
I = I_0 \left( \frac{2}{\pi} \right)^2 = \frac{4}{\pi^2}\, I_0 \approx 0.41\,I_0 .
$$
