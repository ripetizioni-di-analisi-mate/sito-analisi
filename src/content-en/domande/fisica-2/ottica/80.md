A grating of spacing $2\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ is illuminated normally. An existing maximum of positive order is considered. Given the wavelength $\lambda = 600\,\mathrm{nm}$ and the sine of the angle of the maximum $b = \dfrac{3}{5}$, what is the order of the maximum $m$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

For a maximum, the path difference $d\sin\theta$ is $m\lambda$. The grating spacing corresponds to $2000\,\mathrm{nm}$. In the stated units, we have $b = \dfrac{\lambda \cdot m}{2000}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{2000 \cdot b}{\lambda}$. With the given values, we obtain $2$.
