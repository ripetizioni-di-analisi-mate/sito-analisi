Light has vacuum wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$. What wavelength does it have in glass of refractive index $1{,}5$?

- $200\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $800\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $400\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The frequency does not change at the transition. The wavelength therefore becomes $\lambda_n=\dfrac{\lambda_0}{n}$. The result is $400\,\mathrm{nm}$.
