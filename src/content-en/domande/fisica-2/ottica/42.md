Two ideal polarizers are crossed at $90^\circ$. What is the ideal final intensity of light that has passed through both?

- Equal to the incident intensity
- Zero
- Always half the incident intensity
- Always one quarter of the incident intensity

## Explanation

After the first polarizer, the light is linearly polarized along its axis. The second has a perpendicular axis. Malus's law gives a factor $\cos^290^\circ=0$.
