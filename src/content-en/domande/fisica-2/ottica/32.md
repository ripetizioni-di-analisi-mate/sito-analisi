What condition is necessary for total internal reflection?

- Passing into a higher refractive index at any angle
- Normal incidence on any glass
- The mandatory presence of a metallic mirror
- Passing into a lower refractive index with incidence beyond the critical angle

## Explanation

The sine of the angle of refraction must be able to reach one at the limit. This requires $n_1>n_2$. Beyond the critical angle there is no propagating refracted ray in the second medium.
