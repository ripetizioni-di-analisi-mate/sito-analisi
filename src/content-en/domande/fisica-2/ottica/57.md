A glass prism with refractive index $1.50$ has an isosceles right-triangle cross section. A ray enters perpendicular to one leg and strikes the hypotenuse from inside at an angle of $45^\circ$. The prism is immersed, one at a time, in the media listed below. In which medium does total internal reflection occur at the hypotenuse?

- Water, with $n = 1.33$
- Air, with $n = 1.00$
- Ethyl alcohol, with $n = 1.36$
- Olive oil, with $n = 1.47$

## Explanation

At the hypotenuse the light passes from the glass to the outer medium of index $n_m$, and total internal reflection occurs if $n_m < 1.50$ and the angle of incidence exceeds the critical angle, that is, if
$$
\sin 45^\circ > \frac{n_m}{1.50} .
$$
This condition is equivalent to $n_m < 1.50 \cdot \sin 45^\circ \approx 1.06$. Among the listed media only air satisfies it, so total internal reflection occurs **only in air**. In water, for example, the critical angle is $\arcsin\dfrac{1.33}{1.50} \approx 62.5^\circ$, larger than $45^\circ$, and part of the light leaves through the hypotenuse.
