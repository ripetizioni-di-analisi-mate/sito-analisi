A ray enters a transparent material from vacuum. The vacuum wavelength is used and the material is nondispersive over the range considered. Given the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the wavelength in the material $\lambda = 400\,\mathrm{nm}$, what is the vacuum wavelength $b$?

- $300\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $\dfrac{800}{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $600\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The frequency does not change at the interface. The speed in the medium is divided by $n$, and the same applies to the wavelength. In the stated units, we have $\lambda = \dfrac{b}{n}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \lambda \cdot n$. With the given values, we obtain $600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
