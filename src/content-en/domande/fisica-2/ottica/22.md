A grating has spacing $2{,}4\,\mu\mathrm m$ and receives light at $500\,\mathrm{nm}$ at normal incidence. What is the largest positive integer order allowed by $|\sin\theta|\le1$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The condition is $\dfrac{m\le d}{\lambda}$. The ratio is $4{,}8$, so the last allowed integer order is four. The result is $4$.
