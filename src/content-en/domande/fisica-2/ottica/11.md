A bundle of rays parallel to the axis reaches a thin diverging lens. One ray enters $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the axis and, $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ beyond the lens, passes $3.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ above it. What focal length is found by extending the outgoing ray backwards?

- $-15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $+30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-45\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Moving to the right, the ray rises $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ in $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. To cancel its initial $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ height, its extension must run $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the left. The virtual focus gives a negative focal length.
