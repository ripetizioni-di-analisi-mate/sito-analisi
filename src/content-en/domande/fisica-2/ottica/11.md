A diverging lens has $f=-20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object at $p=20\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $q$, with negative distances for virtual images?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The thin lens equation gives $\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{p}$. We obtain $\dfrac{1}{q}=-\dfrac{1}{20}-\dfrac{1}{20}=-\dfrac{1}{10}$ in inverse centimeters. The result is $-10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
