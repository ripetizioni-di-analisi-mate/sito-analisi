A lens forms a real image at $q=15\,\mathrm{cm}$ of a real object at $p=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the signed transverse magnification?

- $0{,}5$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $-0{,}5$

## Explanation

The transverse magnification is $m=-\dfrac{q}{p}$. The negative sign indicates an inverted image. The result is $-0{,}5$.
