A thin converging lens forms a sharp image of an arrow on a screen. The upper half of the lens is covered while all distances stay fixed. How does the image appear in geometrical optics?

- The upper half of the arrow disappears.
- It remains complete in the same position but dimmer.
- The lower half of the arrow disappears.
- It becomes virtual and vanishes from the screen.

## Explanation

Rays from each object point pass through different parts of the lens and converge on the same image point. The reduced aperture collects less light from every point, while the focal length and distances remain unchanged.
