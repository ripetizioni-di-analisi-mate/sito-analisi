A thin converging lens produces a real image of a real object. The object is beyond the focus. Given the object distance from the lens $p = 30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the image distance from the lens $d = 15\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the focal length $f$?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Taking real distances as positive, the lens equation is $1/f=1/p+1/d$. We subtract $1/p$ and then take the reciprocal. In the stated units, we have $d = \dfrac{f \cdot p}{- f + p}$. Solving for the unknown gives $f = \dfrac{d \cdot p}{d + p}$. With the given values, we obtain $10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
