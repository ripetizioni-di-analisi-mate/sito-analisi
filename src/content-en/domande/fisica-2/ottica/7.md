The bottom of a tank is at depth $3\,\mathrm m$ in a liquid of refractive index $1{,}5$. Viewed from air almost normally, at what depth does it appear?

- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In the paraxial approximation, we have $h_{app}=\dfrac{h}{n}$ for observation from air. The bottom therefore appears closer to the surface. The result is $2\,\mathrm m$.
