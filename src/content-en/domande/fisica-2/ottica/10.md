A converging lens has $f=10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object at $p=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the image distance $q$, using the convention that real images have positive distances?

- $-15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The thin lens satisfies $\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{1}{f}$. Thus we obtain $\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{1}{10}-\dfrac{1}{30}=\dfrac{1}{15}$ in inverse centimeters. The result is $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
