A ray strikes a mirror at an angle $35^\circ$ to the normal. What is the angle of reflection relative to the normal?

- $55\,{}^\circ$
- $35\,{}^\circ$
- $70\,{}^\circ$
- $17\,{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The law of reflection equates the angles relative to the normal. The angle of reflection therefore equals the angle of incidence. The result is $35\,{}^\circ$.
