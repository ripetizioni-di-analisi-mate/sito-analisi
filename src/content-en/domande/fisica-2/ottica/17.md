Two coherent waves of $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$ interfere constructively at order $m=2$. What is the path difference?

- $1\,\mu\mathrm m$
- $0{,}5\,\mu\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mu\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mu\mathrm m$

## Explanation

A constructive maximum requires $\Delta=m\lambda$. Two wavelengths of $500\,\mathrm{nm}$ make one micrometer. The result is $1\,\mu\mathrm m$.
