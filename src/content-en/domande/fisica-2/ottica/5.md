Light passes from a medium of refractive index $\sqrt2$ into air of refractive index $1$. What is the critical angle?

- $45\,{}^\circ$
- $30\,{}^\circ$
- $60\,{}^\circ$
- $90\,{}^\circ$

## Explanation

At the critical angle the refracted ray is grazing. Thus we obtain $\sin\theta_c=\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$. The result is $45\,{}^\circ$.
