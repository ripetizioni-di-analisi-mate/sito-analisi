A thin lens in air has $n=1{,}5$, $R_1=+0{,}20\,\mathrm m$ and $R_2=-0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is its optical power in the formula $D=(n-1)(\dfrac{1}{R_1}-\dfrac{1}{R_2})$?

- $2{,}5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The difference in curvatures is $5-(-5)=10\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The factor $n-1$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result is $5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
