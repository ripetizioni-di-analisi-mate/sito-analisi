A ray travels from a medium of refractive index $n$ into vacuum. The incidence is below the critical angle. Given the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and the sine of the angle of incidence $a = \dfrac{2}{5}$, what is the sine of the angle of refraction $b$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

By Snell's law, we have $n\sin i=\sin r$ because the refractive index of the second medium is one. In the stated units, we have $b = a \cdot n$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
