Unpolarized light of intensity $100\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ passes through two ideal polarizers whose axes are at $45^\circ$ to each other. What is the final intensity?

- $25\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $12{,}5\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The first polarizer halves the intensity to $50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. The second applies Malus's law with $\cos^245^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result is $25\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
