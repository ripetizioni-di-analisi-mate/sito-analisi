A thin converging lens produces a real image of a real object. The object is beyond the focus. Given the focal length $f = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the image distance from the lens $d = 15\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the object distance from the lens $p$?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Taking real distances as positive, the lens equation is $1/f=1/p+1/d$. We subtract $1/p$ and then take the reciprocal. In the stated units, we have $d = \dfrac{f \cdot p}{- f + p}$. Solving for the unknown gives $p = \dfrac{d \cdot f}{d - f}$. With the given values, we obtain $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
