A converging lens receives a beam parallel to its axis. Where do the rays converge in the paraxial model?

- At the center of the lens
- Always at infinity
- At the image focus
- At a point necessarily in front of the lens

## Explanation

The parallel beam corresponds to an object at infinity. In the lens formula, $\dfrac{1}{p}$ tends to zero. The image distance therefore tends to $f$.
