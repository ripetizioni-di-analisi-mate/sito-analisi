The figure shows a concave spherical mirror with the center of curvature $C$, the focus $F$ and the vertex $V$, and an object placed between $F$ and $C$. What is the image of the object like?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="-6+6*cos(t),6*sin(t),-0.35,0.35" linee="-10,0 0.5,0" vettori="-4.5,0,-4.5,1.2" punti="-6,0,pieno,C;-3,0,pieno,F;0,0,pieno,V" etichette="-5.4,1.5,object" xmin=-10.2 xmax=1 ymin=-2.4 ymax=2.4 titolo="Concave mirror with the object between the focus $F$ and the center of curvature $C$"}

- Real, inverted and reduced, between $F$ and $C$
- Real, inverted and enlarged, beyond $C$
- Virtual, upright and enlarged, behind the mirror
- Real, upright and enlarged, beyond $C$

## Explanation

The center of curvature is at distance $R = 2f$ from the vertex, so the object has $f < p < 2f$.

By the **mirror equation** $\dfrac{1}{q} = \dfrac{1}{f} - \dfrac{1}{p}$. Since $p < 2f$, we have $\dfrac{1}{p} > \dfrac{1}{2f}$, so $0 < \dfrac{1}{q} < \dfrac{1}{2f}$ and therefore $q > 2f$, that is, the image is real and forms beyond $C$.

The magnification is $m = -\dfrac{q}{p}$, with $q > 2f > p$, so $m < -1$ and the image is **inverted and enlarged**.
