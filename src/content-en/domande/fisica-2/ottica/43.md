At Brewster's angle, what is the relation between the reflected and refracted rays?

- They are parallel
- They coincide along the normal
- They are perpendicular
- They are always both grazing

## Explanation

At Brewster's angle, $\theta_B+\theta_2=90^\circ$ holds. The law of reflection assigns the reflected ray the angle $\theta_B$. The two rays are therefore perpendicular.
