In Young's experiment, the wavelength is $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$, the screen distance is $L=2\,\mathrm m$ and the slit separation is $d=0{,}50\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the fringe spacing at small angles?

- $1{,}2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4{,}8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9{,}6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2{,}4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For small angles the fringe spacing is $\Delta y=\dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$. The wavelength and slit separation are converted to the same units. The result is $2{,}4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
