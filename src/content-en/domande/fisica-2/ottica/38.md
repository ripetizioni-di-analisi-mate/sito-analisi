What property must two waves have for stable interference fringes to be observed?

- Very different frequencies with no relation
- A phase difference that is stable in time
- Necessarily orthogonal polarizations
- Necessarily zero intensity at one slit

## Explanation

The fringes depend on the phase difference. If it changes randomly and rapidly, the interference term averages to zero. Coherent waves maintain a stable phase relation.
