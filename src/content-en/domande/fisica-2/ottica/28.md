Two coherent waves of equal amplitude arrive with phase difference $\pi$. What is the ideal resultant intensity?

- $1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The two instantaneous amplitudes have opposite signs. Their sum is zero at every instant, so the resultant intensity vanishes. The result is $0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
