A converging lens has a real object placed beyond $2f$. What is the image like?

- Real, inverted and reduced
- Virtual, upright and magnified
- Real, upright and magnified
- Always at infinity

## Explanation

The lens formula gives $f<q<2f$. The magnification $-\dfrac{q}{p}$ is negative and less than one in magnitude. The image is therefore real, inverted and reduced.
