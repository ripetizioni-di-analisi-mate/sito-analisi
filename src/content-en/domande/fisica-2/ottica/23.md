Linearly polarized light of intensity $80\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ passes through an analyzer at $60^\circ$ to the polarization direction. What is the transmitted intensity?

- $10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $40\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $80\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

Malus's law is $I=I_0\cos^2\theta$. For $60^\circ$, the factor is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The result is $20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
