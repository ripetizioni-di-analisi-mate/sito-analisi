A ray passes from a medium of higher refractive index to one of lower refractive index. Angles are measured relative to the normal. Initially, the refractive index of the incident medium $n_{1} = \dfrac{8}{5}$ and the refractive index of the second medium $n_{2} = \dfrac{6}{5}$ are given. In the second configuration, the refractive index of the incident medium becomes $\dfrac{9}{5}$ and the refractive index of the second medium becomes $\dfrac{3}{2}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $b$?

- $- \dfrac{2}{15}$
- $\dfrac{19}{144}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

At the critical angle the refracted ray is grazing. In Snell's law the sine of the angle of refraction is one, so we obtain $\sin i_{\mathrm{lim}}=n_2/n_1$. Applying $b = \dfrac{n_{2}}{n_{1}}$ gives $\dfrac{3}{4}$ before the change and $\dfrac{5}{6}$ after it. The final value minus the initial value is $\dfrac{1}{12}$.
