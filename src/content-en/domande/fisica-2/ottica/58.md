A ray passes from a medium of higher refractive index to one of lower refractive index. Angles are measured relative to the normal. Given the refractive index of the incident medium $n_{1} = \dfrac{8}{5}$ and the sine of the critical angle $b = \dfrac{3}{4}$, what is the refractive index of the second medium $n_{2}$?

- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{32}{15}$
- $\dfrac{4 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{5}$

## Explanation

At the critical angle the refracted ray is grazing. In Snell's law the sine of the angle of refraction is one, so we obtain $\sin i_{\mathrm{lim}}=n_2/n_1$. In the stated units, we have $b = \dfrac{n_{2}}{n_{1}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n_{2} = b \cdot n_{1}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{6}{5}$.
