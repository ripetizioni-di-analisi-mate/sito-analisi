A glass prism with refractive index $n = 1.50$ has an equilateral-triangle cross section and is in air. A ray lying in the plane of the cross section crosses the prism symmetrically, that is, inside the prism it is parallel to the base. By what angle is the emerging ray deviated with respect to the incident ray?

- $49^\circ$
- $19^\circ$
- $97^\circ$
- $37^\circ$

## Explanation

The lateral faces form angles of $60^\circ$ with the base and the inner ray is parallel to the base, so it forms the angle $60^\circ$ with each face and the angle $30^\circ$ with its normal.

By **Snell's law** at the first face $\sin\theta_1 = 1.50 \sin 30^\circ = 0.75$, so the angle of incidence is $\theta_1 \approx 48.6^\circ$. By symmetry the ray leaves the second face at the same angle $48.6^\circ$.

At each face the ray is deviated by $48.6^\circ - 30^\circ = 18.6^\circ$, always toward the base, therefore the total deviation is $2 \cdot 18.6^\circ \approx 37^\circ$.
