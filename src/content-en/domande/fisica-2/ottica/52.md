A ray travels from a medium of refractive index $n$ into vacuum. The incidence is below the critical angle. Given the sine of the angle of incidence $a = \dfrac{2}{5}$ and the sine of the angle of refraction $b = \dfrac{3}{5}$, what is the refractive index $n$?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

By Snell's law, we have $n\sin i=\sin r$ because the refractive index of the second medium is one. In the stated units, we have $b = a \cdot n$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{b}{a}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
