A ray passes obliquely from air into glass. How does its direction change relative to the normal?

- It bends away from the normal
- It always remains unchanged
- It bends toward the normal
- It always becomes parallel to the surface

## Explanation

Glass has a higher refractive index than air. Snell's law therefore requires a smaller sine of the angle of refraction. The angle relative to the normal decreases.
