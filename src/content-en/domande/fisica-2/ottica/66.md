A person $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ tall stands in front of a vertical plane mirror. What is the minimum height of the mirror that allows the person to see their whole body?

- $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$, at any distance from the mirror
- $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$, only if the person stands $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ from the mirror
- A value that decreases as the person moves away from the mirror
- $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$, at any distance from the mirror

## Explanation

If the person stands at distance $D$ from the mirror, their image is $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ tall and lies at distance $D$ behind the mirror, because it is symmetric to the person with respect to the mirror.

The eye sees the head and the feet of the image along two lines that start from the eye and reach the image, at distance $2D$. The mirror is halfway, so by similar triangles these lines cut the plane of the mirror at two points whose distance is half the height of the image.

The mirror must contain those two points, therefore it must be at least $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ tall. The result does not contain $D$, so it holds at any distance.
