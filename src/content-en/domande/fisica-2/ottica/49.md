A ray enters a transparent material from vacuum. The vacuum wavelength is used and the material is nondispersive over the range considered. Initially, the vacuum wavelength $b = 600\,\mathrm{nm}$ and the refractive index $n = \dfrac{3}{2}$ are given. In the second configuration, the refractive index becomes $2$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $\lambda$?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The frequency does not change at the interface. The speed in the medium is divided by $n$, and the same applies to the wavelength. Applying $\lambda = \dfrac{b}{n}$ gives $400\,\mathrm{nm}$ before the change and $300\,\mathrm{nm}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
