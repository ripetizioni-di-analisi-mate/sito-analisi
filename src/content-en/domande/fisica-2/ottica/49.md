A light ray passes from air into a glass with refractive index $n = 1.50$, with angle of incidence $50^\circ$. At the surface part of the light is reflected and part is refracted. What angle do the reflected ray and the refracted ray form with each other?

- $99^\circ$
- $81^\circ$
- $19^\circ$
- $121^\circ$

## Explanation

By **Snell's law** $\sin\theta_2 = \dfrac{\sin 50^\circ}{1.50} \approx 0.511$, so the angle of refraction is $\theta_2 \approx 30.7^\circ$.

By the law of reflection the reflected ray forms the angle $50^\circ$ with the normal pointing into the air. The refracted ray forms the angle $30.7^\circ$ with the normal pointing into the glass, and the two rays lie on the same side of the normal. The two half-lines of the normal form a straight angle, therefore the angle between the reflected ray and the refracted ray is
$$
180^\circ - 50^\circ - 30.7^\circ \approx 99^\circ .
$$
