Unpolarized light of intensity $100\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ passes through an ideal polarizer. What is the transmitted intensity?

- $25\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $200\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

For unpolarized light, we average over all transverse directions. The average value of $\cos^2\theta$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so half the intensity is transmitted. The result is $50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
