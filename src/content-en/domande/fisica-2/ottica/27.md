Two identical coherent waves in phase each have intensity $3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the intensity at the interference maximum?

- $6\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $48\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The amplitudes add and double. Intensity is proportional to the square of the amplitude, so it is four times the individual intensity. The result is $12\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
