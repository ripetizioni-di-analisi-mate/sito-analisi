A single slit is illuminated normally by monochromatic light of wavelength $500\,\mathrm{nm}$. The small-angle approximation is used. Given the screen distance $L = 2\,\mathrm m$ and the distance from the center to the first minimum $d = 4\,\mathrm{mm}$, what is the slit width $b$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The first minimum satisfies $b\sin\theta=\lambda$. At small angles, we have $\sin\theta\simeq d/L$. In the stated units, we have $d = \dfrac{L}{2 \cdot b}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{L}{2 \cdot d}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{mm}$.
