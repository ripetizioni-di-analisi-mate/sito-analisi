Two coherent in-phase sources send onto a region of a screen waves with the same polarization, which alone would give the intensities $4.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $1.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the intensity at the maxima of the interference pattern?

- $9.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The intensity is proportional to the square of the amplitude, and the intensities are in the ratio $4$, so the amplitudes are in the ratio $2$ and can be written as $2E_0$ and $E_0$, with $E_0^2$ corresponding to $1.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.

At the maxima the waves arrive in phase and the fields add, therefore the resulting amplitude is $3E_0$ and the intensity is
$$
I_{\max} = 3^2 \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2} = 9.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2} .
$$
At the minima the amplitude is $E_0$ and the intensity is $1.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$, so with different amplitudes the dark fringes are not completely dark.
