With a converging lens of focal length $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ one wants to form on a screen the real image of an object. What is the minimum distance between object and screen for which this is possible?

- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The image is real for $p > f$, and from the **thin lens equation** $q = \dfrac{p f}{p - f}$. The distance between object and screen is therefore
$$
D(p) = p + q = \frac{p^2}{p - f} , \qquad p > f .
$$
The derivative is $D'(p) = \dfrac{p\,(p - 2f)}{(p - f)^2}$, negative for $f < p < 2f$ and positive for $p > 2f$, therefore the minimum occurs at $p = 2f$. In that case also $q = 2f$, and the minimum distance is $4f = 60\,\mathrm{cm}$.
