A ray passes from air of refractive index $1$ into a medium of refractive index $\sqrt3$. What is Brewster's angle?

- $30\,{}^\circ$
- $60\,{}^\circ$
- $45\,{}^\circ$
- $90\,{}^\circ$

## Explanation

Brewster's law gives $\tan\theta_B=\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}$. Since $\tan60^\circ=\sqrt3$, the angle is sixty degrees. The result is $60\,{}^\circ$.
