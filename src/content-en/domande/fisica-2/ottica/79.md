A grating of spacing $2\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ is illuminated normally. An existing maximum of positive order is considered. Given the order of the maximum $m = 2$ and the wavelength $\lambda = 600\,\mathrm{nm}$, what is the sine of the angle of the maximum $b$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$

## Explanation

For a maximum, the path difference $d\sin\theta$ is $m\lambda$. The grating spacing corresponds to $2000\,\mathrm{nm}$. In the stated units, we have $b = \dfrac{\lambda \cdot m}{2000}$. With the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
