An infinite wire has linear charge density $\lambda=6\pi\varepsilon_0\,\mathrm V$. What is the field at a distance of $1\,\mathrm m$?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

By cylindrical symmetry, $E=\dfrac{|\lambda|}{2\pi\varepsilon_0r}$. The value of $\lambda$ allows $\pi\varepsilon_0$ to be canceled directly. The result is $3\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
