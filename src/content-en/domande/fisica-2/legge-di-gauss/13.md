An infinite uniformly charged insulating cylinder has radius $R$. What fraction of the charge per unit length lies within $r=\dfrac{R}{2}$?

- $0{,}25$
- $0{,}5$
- $0{,}125$
- $0{,}75$

## Explanation

The charge per unit length is proportional to the cross-sectional area. Therefore, the ratio is $\dfrac{r^2}{R^2}$. The result is $0{,}25$.
