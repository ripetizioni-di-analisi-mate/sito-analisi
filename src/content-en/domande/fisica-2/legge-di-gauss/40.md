Which quantity appears on the right-hand side of the local form of Gauss's law in a vacuum?

- $\rho\varepsilon_0$
- $\vec E\times\vec B$
- $\nabla\times\vec E$
- $\dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$

## Explanation

The local form is $\nabla\cdot\vec E=\dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$. The divergence measures the net flux per unit volume. The local charge density is its source.
