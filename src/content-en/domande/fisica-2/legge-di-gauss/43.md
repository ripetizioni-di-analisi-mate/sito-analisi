At a point free of charge, $\nabla\cdot\vec E=0$. Which field is compatible with this condition?

- Only the zero field
- Only a radial field pointing outward from the point
- A nonzero uniform field
- Only a time-varying field

## Explanation

A uniform field has zero spatial derivatives. Its divergence is therefore zero. This does not require the field vector to be zero.
