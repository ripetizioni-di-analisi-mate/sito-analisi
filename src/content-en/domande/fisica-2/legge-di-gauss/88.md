Between two ideal infinite parallel sheets with opposite charges, the fields of the two sheets point in the same direction. The second sheet produces a field of a different magnitude from that of the first. Given the field magnitude of the first sheet $E_{1} = 80\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the field magnitude of the second sheet $E_{2} = 40\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the electric field magnitude $E$?

- $40 \cdot \sqrt{5}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Between the sheets, the field leaving the positive sheet and the field entering the negative sheet point in the same direction. By superposition, the magnitudes add. In the indicated units, $E = E_{1} + E_{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
