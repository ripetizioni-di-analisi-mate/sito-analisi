A triangle has its vertices at the points $(1, 0, 0)$, $(0, 1, 0)$ and $(0, 0, 1)$, with coordinates in meters, and is oriented with the normal that has positive components. The triangle is in the uniform field $\vec{E} = E_0\,\hat{k}$, with $E_0 = 400\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the flux of the field through the triangle?

- $3.5 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$
- $2.0 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$
- $2.3 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$
- $4.0 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The triangle lies in the plane $x + y + z = 1$, whose unit normal is $\hat{n} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$. The triangle is equilateral with side $\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$, so its area is $A = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 2\,\mathrm{m^2} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

The field is uniform and the surface is flat, therefore
$$
\Phi_E = \vec{E} \cdot \hat{n}\,A = E_0 \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2} = \frac{E_0}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 2.0 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C} .
$$
The product $A \cos\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is the area of the projection of the triangle on the $xy$-plane, the right triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$.
