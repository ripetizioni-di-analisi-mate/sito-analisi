A uniformly charged sphere has a surface field of $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the field at a distance of $3R$ from the center?

- $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Outside, all the charge is enclosed. The field decreases as $r^{-2}$, so at $3R$ it is one ninth of the surface field. The result is $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
