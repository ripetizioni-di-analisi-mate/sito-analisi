Across an ideal charged surface, which field component has a jump determined by $\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$?

- The tangential component
- The normal component
- The magnitude of every component in the same way
- The component always parallel to the $x$ axis

## Explanation

A small pillbox Gaussian surface crosses the charged sheet. In the limit, the two bases contribute. This gives the jump in the normal component.
