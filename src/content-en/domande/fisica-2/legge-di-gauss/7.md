A uniformly charged spherical shell has radius $R$. What is the field at a point at a distance of $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from the center?

- $1\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

An internal Gaussian sphere encloses no charge. Spherical symmetry allows us to write $E4\pi r^2=0$, so the internal field is zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
