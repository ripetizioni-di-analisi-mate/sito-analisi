A uniform field is parallel to a plane surface of area $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the flux through the surface?

- $2\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The field is perpendicular to the normal. Therefore, $\vec E\cdot\vec n=0$ at every point. The result is $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
