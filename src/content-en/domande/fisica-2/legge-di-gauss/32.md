Which surface facilitates calculating the field of an infinite uniform wire?

- A sphere tangent to the wire
- A cube with the wire along one edge
- A plane parallel to the wire
- A cylinder coaxial with the wire

## Explanation

Symmetry makes the field radial and constant on the lateral surface. On the bases, the field is tangential. The flux therefore reduces to $E2\pi rL$.
