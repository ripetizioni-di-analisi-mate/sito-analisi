The electric field outside a uniformly charged spherical shell is measured. Initially, the measurement point is outside. The distance from the center is increased without changing the shell. Given the initial field magnitude $E_{0} = 180\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the final field magnitude $E = 20\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the ratio of final to initial distance $b$?

- $3$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $6$

## Explanation

Gauss's law makes $E$ constant for every concentric surface. The same enclosed charge gives $E\,4\pi r^2$ constant outside. In the indicated units, $E = \dfrac{E_{0}}{b^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{\sqrt{E_{0}}}{\sqrt{E}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $3$.
