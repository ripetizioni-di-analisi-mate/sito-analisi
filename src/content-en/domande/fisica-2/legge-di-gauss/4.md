Two closed surfaces $S_1$ and $S_2$ surround adjacent volumes sharing a face. Their outward fluxes are $+7$ and $-2\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. What is the flux through the outer boundary of their union?

- $9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The shared face has opposite outward normals for the two volumes. Its contributions cancel when the fluxes are added, leaving $7-2=5$ through the outer boundary.
