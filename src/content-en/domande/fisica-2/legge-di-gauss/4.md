A closed surface has outward flux $2000\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. What is the enclosed charge, in nC? Use $\varepsilon_0=8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$.

- $8{,}85\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $35{,}4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $70{,}8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $17{,}7\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

Gauss's law gives $Q_{\mathrm{int}}=\varepsilon_0\Phi$. The result is then converted to nanocoulombs. The result is $17{,}7\,\mathrm{nC}$.
