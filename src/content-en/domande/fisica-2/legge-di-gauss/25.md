A charge is moved from the center of a sphere to another point inside. The initial flux was $60\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. What is the final flux?

- $60\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

Gauss's law depends on the enclosed charge and not on its position inside. Since the charge does not cross the surface, the flux remains the same. The result is $60\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
