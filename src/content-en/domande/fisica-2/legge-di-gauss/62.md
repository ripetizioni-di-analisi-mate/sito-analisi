The electric field outside a uniformly charged spherical shell is measured. Initially, the measurement point is outside. The distance from the center is increased without changing the shell. Given the ratio of final to initial distance $b = 3$ and the final field magnitude $E = 20\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the initial field magnitude $E_{0}$?

- $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $\dfrac{20}{9}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $360\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Gauss's law makes $E$ constant for every concentric surface. The same enclosed charge gives $E\,4\pi r^2$ constant outside. In the indicated units, $E = \dfrac{E_{0}}{b^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $E_{0} = E \cdot b^{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
