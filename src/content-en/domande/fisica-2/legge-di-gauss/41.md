Two different closed surfaces enclose exactly the same charges. How do the net fluxes compare?

- They are equal
- The flux through the larger surface is greater
- The flux through the smaller surface is greater
- They always depend on the number of faces

## Explanation

Gauss's law depends on the enclosed charge. This is the same for both surfaces. The net fluxes are therefore also equal.
