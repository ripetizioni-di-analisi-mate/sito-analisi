A cube encloses a positive point charge at its center. A second positive charge is added at the same point. Given the initial charge $q_{1} = 18\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the added charge $q_{2} = 30\,\mathrm{nC}$, what is the flux through one face multiplied by $\varepsilon_0$ $q$?

- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $16\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The total flux is the sum of the charges divided by $\varepsilon_0$. By the symmetry of the cube, the flux is divided into six equal contributions. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{q_{1}}{6} + \dfrac{q_{2}}{6}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $8\,\mathrm{nC}$.
