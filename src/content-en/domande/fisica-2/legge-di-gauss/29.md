The field normal to a charged surface has values $E_{n,+}=8\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and $E_{n,-}=3\,\mathrm{N/C}$, measured along the same normal. What is $\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $2{,}5\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The jump condition is $E_{n,+}-E_{n,-}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$. The two values must be subtracted with the assigned signs. The result is $5\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
