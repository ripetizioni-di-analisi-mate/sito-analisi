An infinite charged cylinder has surface field $90\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is it at a distance of $3R$ from the axis?

- $15\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Outside, the enclosed charge per unit length is constant. Gauss's law gives $\dfrac{E\propto1}{r}$. The result is $30\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
