Just outside a conductor in equilibrium, the normal field is $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is $\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$?

- $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $800\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The field inside the metal is zero. The jump in the normal component is $\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$, so it coincides with the external field. The result is $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
