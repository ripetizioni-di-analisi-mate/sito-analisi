A Gaussian surface encloses $+12\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is the algebraic enclosed charge?

- $4{,}5\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $18\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $9\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $36\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The charge used in Gauss's law is the algebraic sum of the enclosed charges. Therefore, $Q_{\mathrm{int}}=12-3$ in nanocoulombs. The result is $9\,\mathrm{nC}$.
