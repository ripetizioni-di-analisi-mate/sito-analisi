An infinite cylindrical shell is uniformly charged. What is the field in the empty coaxial cavity?

- Uniform and parallel to the axis
- Zero
- Inversely proportional to the radius
- Maximum on the axis

## Explanation

An internal cylindrical surface encloses no charge. Symmetry requires a radial field that is constant over the lateral surface. Gauss's law therefore gives zero field.
