A point charge is entirely outside a closed surface. What is its contribution to the net flux through the surface?

- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The enclosed charge associated with this source is zero. By Gauss's law, its net flux is therefore zero, even though the field on the surface is nonzero. The result is $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
