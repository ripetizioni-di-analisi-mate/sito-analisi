A closed surface has zero flux. Which conclusion follows from Gauss's law?

- The field is zero at every point
- There are never any charges inside
- The algebraic enclosed charge is zero
- All external charges are zero

## Explanation

Gauss's law relates the flux to the algebraic sum of the enclosed charges. Opposite charges can cancel each other. Even a nonzero uniform field has zero total flux.
