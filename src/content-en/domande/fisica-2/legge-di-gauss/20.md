A thin charged sheet lies in the plane $x=0$. In vacuum the normal field component is $+5\,\mathrm{N/C}$ immediately to the right and $-3\,\mathrm{N/C}$ immediately to the left, using the same $x$ axis. What is $\sigma/\varepsilon_0$ on the sheet?

- $2\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Apply Gauss's law to a thin pillbox of face area $A$ crossing the sheet. Its end faces have opposite outward normals, so the flux is $A[5-(-3)]$ and the enclosed charge is $\sigma A$.
