A field has components $E_x=2x$, $E_y=3y$, $E_z=-z$, in SI units. What is $\dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$

## Explanation

The charge density is related to the field divergence. The sum of the three derivatives is $2+3-1$. The result is $4\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$.
