A central charge produces a total flux of $100\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$ through a sphere. What is the flux through only the curved surface of one of its hemispheres?

- $25\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The radial field has the same magnitude over the entire sphere. Each hemisphere occupies half its area, so it receives half the flux. The result is $50\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
