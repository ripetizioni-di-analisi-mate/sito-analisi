An irregularly shaped closed surface is immersed in the field of a point charge that remains outside. Which conclusion follows from Gauss's law?

- The field is zero over the entire surface
- The net flux is zero, even though the field on the surface may be nonzero
- The net flux is the magnitude of the external charge divided by $\varepsilon_0$
- The net flux is positive because the surface has finite area

## Explanation

Gauss's law uses the enclosed charge, which is zero. The field of the external charge may pass through the surface locally, but the inward and outward fluxes balance each other.
