A point charge $q = 2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ is at the midpoint of an edge of a cube. What is the outward flux of the field of the charge through the four faces of the cube that do not contain that edge?

- $28\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$
- $1.1 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$
- $56\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$
- $38\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$

## Explanation

Four equal cubes placed around the edge form a rectangular box that contains the charge in its interior. By **Gauss's law** the outward flux through the box is $\dfrac{q}{\varepsilon_0}$.

The surface of the box is made of the faces of the four cubes that do not contain the edge, and the four cubes are arranged symmetrically around the charge. Each cube therefore receives one quarter of the total flux:
$$
\Phi_E = \frac{q}{4\varepsilon_0} = \frac{2.0 \cdot 10^{-9}}{4 \cdot 8.85 \cdot 10^{-12}}\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C} \approx 56\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{q}{8\varepsilon_0} \approx 28\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$ corresponds to the charge placed at a vertex of the cube.
