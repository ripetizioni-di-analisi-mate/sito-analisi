A charge is at the center of a cube and generates total flux $120\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. What is the flux through one face?

- $10\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The six faces are equivalent by symmetry relative to the central charge. The total flux is therefore divided into six equal parts. The result is $20\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
