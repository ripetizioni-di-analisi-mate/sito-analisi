A uniform electric field passes through a plane surface. The angle between the oriented normal and the field is $60^\circ$. Given the electric field magnitude $E = 180\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the area $A = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what is the oriented electric flux $\Phi$?

- $9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $18 \cdot \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The dot product with the area vector contains the cosine of the angle to the normal. Here, this cosine equals $1/2$. In the indicated units, $\Phi = \dfrac{A \cdot E}{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $18\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
