A closed surface encloses a shell of charge $-8\,\mathrm{nC}$ and a central charge $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$. What charge determines the flux?

- $3\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-13\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $13\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

All enclosed charges are added algebraically. The distribution and the distance from the center do not change this sum. The result is $-3\,\mathrm{nC}$.
