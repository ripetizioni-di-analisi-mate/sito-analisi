An external charge is brought closer to a closed surface without crossing it. What can change?

- The local field, while the net flux due to the external charge remains unchanged
- The enclosed charge necessarily
- The net flux always becomes positive
- Gauss's law ceases to hold

## Explanation

The field on the surface also depends on external sources. Their contribution to the net flux remains zero. The enclosed charge does not change.
