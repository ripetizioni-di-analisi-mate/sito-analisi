A uniform electric field passes through a plane surface. The angle between the oriented normal and the field is $60^\circ$. Given the electric field magnitude $E = 180\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the oriented electric flux $\Phi = 18\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$, what is the area $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{15}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The dot product with the area vector contains the cosine of the angle to the normal. Here, this cosine equals $1/2$. In the indicated units, $\Phi = \dfrac{A \cdot E}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{2 \cdot \Phi}{E}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
