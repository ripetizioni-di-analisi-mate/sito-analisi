A uniform field $E=100\,\mathrm{N/C}$ passes through a plane surface of area $0{,}20\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The normal makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. What is the flux?

- $10\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The flux is $\Phi=EA\cos\theta$. The given angle is measured relative to the normal. The result is $10\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
