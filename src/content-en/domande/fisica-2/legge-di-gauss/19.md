In a region, $\vec E=a(x\hat\imath+y\hat\jmath+z\hat k)$, with $a=4\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$. What is $\dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$

## Explanation

The local form of Gauss's law is $\dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}=\nabla\cdot\vec E$. The three derivatives all equal $a$, so the divergence is $3a$. The result is $12\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$.
