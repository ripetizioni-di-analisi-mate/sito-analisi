A uniformly charged insulating sphere has total charge $64\,\mathrm{nC}$ and radius $R$. How much charge is contained within the radius $\dfrac{R}{2}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $16\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $32\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

With uniform density, the charge is proportional to the volume. Halving the radius divides the volume by eight. The result is $8\,\mathrm{nC}$.
