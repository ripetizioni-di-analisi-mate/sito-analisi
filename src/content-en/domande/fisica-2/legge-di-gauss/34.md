Does Gauss's law alone allow the field to be determined point by point for any distribution?

- Yes, simply divide by the area in every case
- Suitable symmetry is also needed to extract the field from the flux
- Yes, the field is always uniform
- No, it applies only to point charges

## Explanation

The law provides an integral of the field. Additional information is needed to extract a local value. Spherical, cylindrical and planar symmetries often provide this information.
