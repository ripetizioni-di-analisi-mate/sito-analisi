An infinite insulating slab occupies $-a<x<a$ with uniform density. The field at its boundary is $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is its magnitude at $x=\dfrac{a}{3}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Inside the slab, the field is $E_x=\dfrac{\rho x}{\varepsilon_0}$. Therefore, the magnitude is proportional to the distance from the midplane. The result is $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
