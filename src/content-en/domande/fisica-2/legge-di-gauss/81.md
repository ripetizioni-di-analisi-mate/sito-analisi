A cube encloses a positive point charge at its center. A second positive charge is added at the same point. Given the initial charge $q_{1} = 18\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the flux through one face multiplied by $\varepsilon_0$ $q = 8\,\mathrm{nC}$, what is the added charge $q_{2}$?

- $15\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $30\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $60\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The total flux is the sum of the charges divided by $\varepsilon_0$. By the symmetry of the cube, the flux is divided into six equal contributions. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{q_{1}}{6} + \dfrac{q_{2}}{6}$. Solving for the unknown gives $q_{2} = 6 \cdot q - q_{1}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $30\,\mathrm{nC}$.
