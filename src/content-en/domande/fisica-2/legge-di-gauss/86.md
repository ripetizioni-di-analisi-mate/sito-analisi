A right pyramid with a square base, with the base horizontal of side $a = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ and the apex at the top, is in a uniform vertical electric field pointing upward, of magnitude $E = 4.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$. The pyramid contains no charge. What is the total outward flux through the four lateral faces?

::grafico{unicolore=1 assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-1.5,0 1.5,0 0,2 -1.5,0" vettori="-2.4,-0.6,-2.4,1.2,E;2.4,-0.6,2.4,1.2;-0.8,-1,-0.8,-0.2;0.8,-1,0.8,-0.2" etichette="0,-0.25,a" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-1.1 ymax=2.3 titolo="The pyramid seen from the side in the uniform vertical field $\vec{E}$"}

- $-3.6 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$
- $0$
- $90\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$
- $3.6 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The surface of the pyramid is closed and contains no charge, so by **Gauss's law** the total outward flux is zero.

On the base the outward normal points down, opposite to the field, therefore the outward flux through the base is $-E a^2 = -4.0 \cdot 10^3 \cdot 0.090\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C} = -3.6 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$.

It follows that the outward flux through the lateral faces is $+3.6 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$, whatever the height of the pyramid. The value $90\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$ is the flux through a single lateral face.
