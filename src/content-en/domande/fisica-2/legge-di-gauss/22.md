A closed surface encloses both charges of a dipole $+q,-q$ and no other charge. What is the net flux?

- $1\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The algebraic enclosed charge is $q-q=0$. Gauss's law therefore requires zero total flux. The result is $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
