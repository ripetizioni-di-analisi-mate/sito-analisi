A uniform field $\vec E=50\hat\imath\,\mathrm{N/C}$ passes through a face of area $0{,}20\,\mathrm{m^2}$ with normal $-\hat\imath$. What is the oriented flux?

- $10\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The field and the normal point in opposite directions. The dot product therefore gives $\Phi=-EA$. The result is $-10\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
