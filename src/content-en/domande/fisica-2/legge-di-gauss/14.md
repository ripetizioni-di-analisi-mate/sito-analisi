An infinite solid cylinder has uniform density and surface field $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the field at $r=\dfrac{R}{4}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Inside, the field is $E=\dfrac{\rho r}{2\varepsilon_0}$. Therefore, at one quarter of the radius, the field is one quarter of the surface field. The result is $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
