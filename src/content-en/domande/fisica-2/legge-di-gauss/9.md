A solid insulating sphere is uniformly charged. The field at its surface is $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is it at a distance of $\dfrac{R}{3}$ from the center?

- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $160\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Inside, the enclosed charge grows as $r^3$. Dividing by the Gaussian surface area, the field grows linearly with $r$. The result is $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
