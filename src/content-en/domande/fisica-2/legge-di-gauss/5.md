A cube is immersed in a uniform electric field and contains no charges. What is the net outward flux?

- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The inward flux through one face is balanced by the outward flux through the opposite face. The enclosed charge is zero, so the total flux is also zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
