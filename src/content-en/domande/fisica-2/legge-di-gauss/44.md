For a uniform spherical shell, what allows a zero internal field to be deduced from zero flux?

- The zero flux value alone for any distribution
- The presence of a material that is necessarily metallic
- The fact that the shell has zero mass
- Spherical symmetry

## Explanation

Symmetry requires a radial field with constant magnitude on every concentric sphere. The flux therefore becomes $E4\pi r^2$. Only with this information does zero flux imply $E=0$.
