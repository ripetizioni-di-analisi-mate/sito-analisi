For an infinite uniformly charged slab, the field just outside is $70\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is it at a point outside the slab three times as far from the midplane?

- $35\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $140\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $280\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Outside the slab, the charge enclosed by a symmetric Gaussian surface is constant. Therefore, the external field does not depend on the distance. The result is $70\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
