The electric field outside a uniformly charged spherical shell is measured. Initially, the measurement point is outside. The distance from the center is increased without changing the shell. Given the initial field magnitude $E_{0} = 180\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the ratio of final to initial distance $b = 3$, what is the final field magnitude $E$?

- $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1620\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Gauss's law makes $E$ constant for every concentric surface. The same enclosed charge gives $E\,4\pi r^2$ constant outside. In the indicated units, $E = \dfrac{E_{0}}{b^{2}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
