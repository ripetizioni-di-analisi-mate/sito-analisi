In a model of the hydrogen atom the electron cloud has charge density $\rho(r) = \rho_0\,e^{-\frac{r}{a}}$, where $r$ is the distance from the nucleus and $a$ is a positive constant. What is the total charge of the cloud?

- $4\pi\rho_0 a^3$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\rho_0 a^3$
- $8\pi\rho_0 a^3$
- $2\pi\rho_0 a^3$

## Explanation

The density depends only on $r$, so the charge is obtained by adding the spherical shells of radius $r$ and thickness $dr$, of volume $4\pi r^2\,dr$:
$$
Q = \int_0^{+\infty} \rho_0\, e^{-\frac{r}{a}}\, 4\pi r^2\, dr .
$$
Integrating by parts twice gives $\displaystyle\int_0^{+\infty} r^2 e^{-\frac{r}{a}}\,dr = 2a^3$, therefore $Q = 8\pi\rho_0 a^3$.

The value $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\rho_0 a^3$ corresponds to a sphere of radius $a$ with uniform density $\rho_0$, and neglects the charge at distances greater than $a$.
