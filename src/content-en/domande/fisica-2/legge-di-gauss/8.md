A sphere with a spherically symmetric distribution carries $Q=4\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is the field at $0{,}30\,\mathrm m$ from the center, outside the sphere? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $800\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Outside, a spherically symmetric distribution is equivalent to a charge at the center. Therefore, $E=\dfrac{kQ}{r^2}$. The result is $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
