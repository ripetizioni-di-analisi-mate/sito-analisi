A Gaussian surface encloses a negative total charge. What is the sign of the net outward flux?

- Negative
- Positive
- Always zero
- It depends only on the shape

## Explanation

The vacuum permittivity is positive. The ratio $\dfrac{Q_{\mathrm{int}}}{\varepsilon_0}$ therefore has the sign of the enclosed charge. The net flux is negative.
