The field lines in a region are drawn so that each one corresponds to a flux of $20\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$. From a closed surface $7$ lines leave and $3$ lines enter. What is the charge enclosed by the surface?

- $0.71\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $0.53\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

Outgoing lines give a positive contribution to the flux and incoming lines a negative contribution, so the outward flux is $(7 - 3) \cdot 20\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C} = 80\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$.

By **Gauss's law** the enclosed charge is $q_{\text{int}} = \varepsilon_0 \Phi_E = 8.85 \cdot 10^{-12} \cdot 80\,\mathrm{C} \approx 7.1 \cdot 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{C}$, that is about $0.71\,\mathrm{nC}$.

Adding the incoming lines to the outgoing ones would give the value $1.8\,\mathrm{nC}$, which ignores the sign of the contributions.
