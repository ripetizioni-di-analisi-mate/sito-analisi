A uniform electric field passes through a plane surface. The angle between the oriented normal and the field is $60^\circ$. Given the area $A = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the oriented electric flux $\Phi = 18\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$, what is the electric field magnitude $E$?

- $360\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $60 \cdot \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The dot product with the area vector contains the cosine of the angle to the normal. Here, this cosine equals $1/2$. In the indicated units, $\Phi = \dfrac{A \cdot E}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $E = \dfrac{2 \cdot \Phi}{A}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
