A cube encloses a positive point charge at its center. A second positive charge is added at the same point. Given the added charge $q_{2} = 30\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the flux through one face multiplied by $\varepsilon_0$ $q = 8\,\mathrm{nC}$, what is the initial charge $q_{1}$?

- $27\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $36\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $9\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $18\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The total flux is the sum of the charges divided by $\varepsilon_0$. By the symmetry of the cube, the flux is divided into six equal contributions. In the indicated units, $q = \dfrac{q_{1}}{6} + \dfrac{q_{2}}{6}$. Solving for the unknown gives $q_{1} = 6 \cdot q - q_{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $18\,\mathrm{nC}$.
