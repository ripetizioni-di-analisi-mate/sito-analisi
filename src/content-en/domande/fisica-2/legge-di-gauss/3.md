A closed surface encloses $8{,}85\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is the outward flux? Use $\varepsilon_0=8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$.

- $500\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

Gauss's law gives $\Phi=\dfrac{Q_{\mathrm{int}}}{\varepsilon_0}$. The positive sign indicates a net outward flux. The result is $1000\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
