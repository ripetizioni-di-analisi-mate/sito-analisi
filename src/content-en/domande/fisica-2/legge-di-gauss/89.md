Between two ideal infinite parallel sheets with opposite charges, the fields of the two sheets point in the same direction. The second sheet produces a field of a different magnitude from that of the first. Given the field magnitude of the second sheet $E_{2} = 40\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the electric field magnitude $E = 120\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the field magnitude of the first sheet $E_{1}$?

- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $160\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Between the sheets, the field leaving the positive sheet and the field entering the negative sheet point in the same direction. By superposition, the magnitudes add. In the indicated units, $E = E_{1} + E_{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $E_{1} = E - E_{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
