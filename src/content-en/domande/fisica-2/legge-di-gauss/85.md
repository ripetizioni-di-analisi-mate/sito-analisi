A field has components $E_x=ax$, $E_y=by$ and $E_z=0$. The coordinates are in meters and the components are in newtons per coulomb. A region with positive charge density is considered. Given the coefficient of the component $x$ $a = 8\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$ and the coefficient of the component $y$ $b = 3\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$, what is the charge density divided by $\varepsilon_0$ $q$?

- $5\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $22\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$

## Explanation

The divergence is the sum of the three derivatives of the components with respect to their respective coordinates. The local form of Gauss's law equates it to $\rho/\varepsilon_0$. In the indicated units, $q = a + b$. Using the values given in the problem gives $11\,\mathrm{N/(C\,m)}$.
