A sphere of radius $R$ has density $\rho(r)=ar$, with $a>0$. What fraction of the total charge lies within $\dfrac{R}{2}$?

- $0{,}5$
- $0{,}25$
- $0{,}125$
- $0{,}0625$

## Explanation

The enclosed charge is proportional to $\int_0^r r^{\prime3}\,dr^\prime$, and therefore to $r^4$. The required ratio is $(\dfrac{1}{2})^4$. The result is $0{,}0625$.
