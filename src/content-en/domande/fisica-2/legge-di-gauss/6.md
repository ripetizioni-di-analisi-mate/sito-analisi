A radial field has constant magnitude on a sphere of radius $2\,\mathrm m$. If the outward flux is $400\pi\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$, what is the field?

- $12{,}5\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

On the sphere, the field is parallel to the normal and has constant magnitude. Therefore, $\Phi=4\pi r^2E$. The result is $25\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
