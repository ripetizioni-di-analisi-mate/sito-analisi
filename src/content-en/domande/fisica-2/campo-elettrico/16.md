A ring of radius $R=0{,}30\,\mathrm m$ carries $Q=5\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is the field magnitude on its axis at a distance of $x=0{,}40\,\mathrm m$ from the center? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $72\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $288\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $576\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $144\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The axial field is $E=\dfrac{kQx}{(R^2+x^2)^{\dfrac{3}{2}}}$. Here, $R^2+x^2=0{,}25\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the transverse components cancel. The result is $144\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
