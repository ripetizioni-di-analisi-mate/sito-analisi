A small sphere of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{g}$ and charge $q = +5.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ is launched upward with speed $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a uniform vertical field $E = 2.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$ pointing downward. With $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no friction, what maximum height does it reach above the launch point?

- $46\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $61\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $94\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The charge is positive and the field points downward, so the electric force $qE$ has the same direction as the weight. The downward acceleration is
$$
a = g + \frac{qE}{m} = \left( 9.81 + \frac{5.0 \cdot 10^{-6} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{3}}{2.0 \cdot 10^{-3}} \right)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 14.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The motion is uniformly decelerated until it stops, therefore $h = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2a} = \dfrac{9.0}{2 \cdot 14.8}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$.

Without the field the sphere would rise to $46\,\mathrm{cm}$.
