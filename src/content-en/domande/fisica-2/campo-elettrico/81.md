A particle enters a uniform electric field with an initial velocity perpendicular to the field. Along the field, the acceleration has constant magnitude. Weight is neglected. Given the acceleration magnitude $a = 8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the magnitude of the displacement along the field $d = 1\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the elapsed time $t$?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Along the field, the initial velocity is zero, so only the quadratic term remains in the position as a function of time. In the indicated units, $d = \dfrac{a \cdot t^{2}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{d}}{\sqrt{a}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$.
