A volume of $0{,}020\,\mathrm{m^3}$ has a uniform density $3\,\mu\mathrm{C/m^3}$. What is its total charge in nanocoulombs?

- $30\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $60\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $120\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $240\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

With uniform volume charge density, $Q=\rho\mathcal V$ holds. The product is $0{,}060\,\mu\mathrm C$, which is converted to nanocoulombs. The result is $60\,\mathrm{nC}$.
