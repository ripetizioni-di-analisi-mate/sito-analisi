The positive linear charge density on a thin rod of length $L$ grows as $\lambda(x)=kx$, with $x$ measured from one end. What fraction of the charge lies on the last half of the rod?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The charge between $0$ and $x$ is the integral of the density, $\dfrac12 kx^2$. The first half of the rod therefore contains one quarter of the total, leaving three quarters on the last half.
