A positively charged grain is in a uniform electric field. Only the electric force is considered. Initially, the given quantities are the grain charge $q = 4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the field magnitude $E = 300\,\mathrm{N/C}$. In the second configuration, we change the field magnitude to $450\,\mathrm{N/C}$, with all other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $F$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The definition of field is $\vec F=q\vec E$. Multiplying microcoulombs by newtons per coulomb gives micronewtons. Applying $F = E \cdot q$ gives $1200\,\mathrm{\mu N}$ before the change and $1800\,\mathrm{\mu N}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
