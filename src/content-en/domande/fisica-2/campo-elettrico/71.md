A very large plane and a probe are in a vacuum. The probe is far from the edges. The charge density of the plane is uniform and equals $2\varepsilon_0 E$. Given the magnitude of the probe charge $q = 6\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the magnitude of the force on the probe $F = 3000\,\mathrm{nN}$, what is the magnitude of the field generated by the plane $E$?

- $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $750\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $250\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

In the infinite-plane model, the field is $\sigma/(2\varepsilon_0)$. To find the force, we multiply by the magnitude of the probe charge. In the indicated units, $F = E \cdot q$. Solving for the unknown gives $E = \dfrac{F}{q}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
