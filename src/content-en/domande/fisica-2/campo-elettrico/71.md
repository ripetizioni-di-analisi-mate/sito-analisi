A thin ring of radius $R = 5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ has charge $Q = 2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ uniformly distributed and is fixed in vacuum. An electron is released from rest on the axis, at a distance from the center much smaller than $R$. What is the period of its oscillations about the center?

- $6.3 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.8 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

For $|z| \ll R$ the field on the axis is $E_z \approx \dfrac{kQz}{R^3}$, so the force on the electron is $F_z = -eE_z = -\dfrac{keQ}{R^3}\,z$, a restoring force toward the center proportional to the displacement.

The motion is therefore harmonic, with
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{keQ}{m_e R^3}} = \sqrt{\frac{8.99 \cdot 10^{9} \cdot 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{-9}}{9.11 \cdot 10^{-31} \cdot 0.050^3}}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}} \approx 1.6 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}} .
$$
Hence the period is $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega} \approx 4.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{s}$.
