The field of an infinite uniformly charged plane is measured at two different distances on the same side. How do the magnitudes compare?

- Inversely proportional to the distance
- Inversely proportional to the square of the distance
- Directly proportional to the distance
- Equal

## Explanation

Planar symmetry and Gauss's law give $E=\dfrac{|\sigma|}{2\varepsilon_0}$. The distance does not appear in the formula. Therefore, the two magnitudes are equal.
