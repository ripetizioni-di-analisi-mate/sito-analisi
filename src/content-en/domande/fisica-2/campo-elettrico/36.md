An ideal dipole is immersed in a uniform electric field oblique to its axis. What effect occurs?

- Nonzero resultant force and zero torque
- Force and torque both always zero
- Translation with constant acceleration and no rotation
- Zero resultant force and nonzero torque

## Explanation

The forces on the two charges are equal and opposite because the field is uniform. Their lines of action are distinct when the dipole is oblique. They therefore form a couple with torque $\vec p\times\vec E$.
