A positive point charge is at a distance of $r$ from a measurement point. The charge is replaced by another of twice the magnitude and the distance is increased by a factor of $b$. Given the ratio of final to initial distance $b = 3$ and the new field magnitude $E = 16\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the initial field magnitude $E_{0}$?

- $72\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $144\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The field is proportional to the source charge and inversely proportional to the square of the distance. The two factors are applied together. In the indicated units, $E = \dfrac{2 \cdot E_{0}}{b^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $E_{0} = \dfrac{E \cdot b^{2}}{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $72\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
