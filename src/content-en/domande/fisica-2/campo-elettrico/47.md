A positively charged grain is in a uniform electric field. Only the electric force is considered. Given the field magnitude $E = 300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the electric force magnitude $F = 1200\,\mathrm{\mu N}$, what is the grain charge $q$?

- $6\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The definition of field is $\vec F=q\vec E$. Multiplying microcoulombs by newtons per coulomb gives micronewtons. In the indicated units, $F = E \cdot q$. Solving for the unknown gives $q = \dfrac{F}{E}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
