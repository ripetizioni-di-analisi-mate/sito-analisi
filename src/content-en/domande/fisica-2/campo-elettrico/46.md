A positively charged grain is in a uniform electric field. Only the electric force is considered. Given the grain charge $q = 4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the field magnitude $E = 300\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the electric force magnitude $F$?

- $1800\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

The definition of field is $\vec F=q\vec E$. Multiplying microcoulombs by newtons per coulomb gives micronewtons. In the indicated units, $F = E \cdot q$. Using the values given in the problem gives $1200\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
