An initially stationary positive charge is free in a uniform field. Other forces are neglected. Its mass is $2\,\mathrm{mg}$. Given the charge $q = 4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, the field magnitude $E = 30\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the elapsed time $t = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the speed $v$?

- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The constant acceleration is $qE/m$ and the velocity increases from zero. Here, the ratio of a charge in microcoulombs to a mass in milligrams is directly in coulombs per kilogram. In the indicated units, $v = \dfrac{E \cdot q \cdot t}{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
