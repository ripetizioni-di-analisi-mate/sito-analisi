Which SI unit is equivalent to the unit of electric field?

- $\mathrm{V\,m}$
- $\mathrm{V/m}$
- $\mathrm{C/N}$
- $\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Potential difference is work per unit charge. Therefore, $1\,\mathrm V=1\,\mathrm{N\,m/C}$. Dividing by one meter gives $1\,\mathrm{V/m}=1\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
