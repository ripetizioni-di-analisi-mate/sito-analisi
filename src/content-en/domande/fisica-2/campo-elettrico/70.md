A very large plane and a probe are in a vacuum. The probe is far from the edges. The charge density of the plane is uniform and equals $2\varepsilon_0 E$. Given the magnitude of the field generated by the plane $E = 500\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the magnitude of the force on the probe $F = 3000\,\mathrm{nN}$, what is the magnitude of the probe charge $q$?

- $9\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $12\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $3\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

In the infinite-plane model, the field is $\sigma/(2\varepsilon_0)$. To find the force, we multiply by the magnitude of the probe charge. In the indicated units, $F = E \cdot q$. Solving for the unknown gives $q = \dfrac{F}{E}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $6\,\mathrm{nC}$.
