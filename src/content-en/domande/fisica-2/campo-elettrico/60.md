An electric dipole is perpendicular to a uniform field. The magnitude of each of the two charges is $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. Initially, the given quantities are the field magnitude $E = 300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the separation between the charges $d = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$. In the second configuration, we change the field magnitude to $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the separation between the charges to $3\,\mathrm{cm}$, with all other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $\tau$?

- $24000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$

## Explanation

The torque is $pE$ because the dipole is perpendicular to the field. The dipole moment is $qd$. With $q=2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $d$ in centimeters, the coefficient in nanonewton meters is $20$. Applying $\tau = 20 \cdot E \cdot d$ gives $24000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$ before the change and $24000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $0\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$.
