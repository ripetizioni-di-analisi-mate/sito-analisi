A dipole far from the sources generates a field of $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$ on the axis. Doubling the distance while remaining within the dipole approximation, what field is obtained?

- $5\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The far field of a dipole is proportional to $r^{-3}$. Doubling the distance divides it by eight. The result is $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
