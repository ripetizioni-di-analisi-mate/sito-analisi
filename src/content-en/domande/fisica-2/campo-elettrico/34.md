A positive charge enters with velocity perpendicular to a uniform electric field. Neglecting other forces, what shape is the trajectory?

- Uniform circular
- Parabolic
- Uniform straight-line
- Helical at constant speed

## Explanation

The velocity in the initial direction remains constant. A constant acceleration appears in the field direction. Combining the two motions gives a parabola.
