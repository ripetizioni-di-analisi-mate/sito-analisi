The charges $+2\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$ are separated by $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the field at the midpoint? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $1800\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $7200\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1{,}44\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The midpoint is at a distance of $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ from each charge. The two contributions, each of magnitude $1800\,\mathrm{N/C}$, point in the same direction and add. The result is $3600\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
