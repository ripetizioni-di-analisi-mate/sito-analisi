The figure shows some field lines around a point charge $Q$, with their sense, and two points $A$ and $B$. Which statement is true?

- $Q$ is positive and the field at $A$ is stronger than at $B$
- $Q$ is negative and the field at $B$ is stronger than at $A$
- $Q$ is positive and the field has the same magnitude at $A$ and at $B$
- $Q$ is negative and the field at $A$ is stronger than at $B$

## Explanation

The arrows on the field lines point toward $Q$, so the lines enter the charge and $Q$ is **negative**.

The lines are rays starting from $Q$, therefore they spread apart as the distance increases. The point $A$ is closer to the charge, where the lines are denser, so the field at $A$ is stronger than at $B$, as the dependence $E = k\,\dfrac{|Q|}{r^2}$ also requires.
