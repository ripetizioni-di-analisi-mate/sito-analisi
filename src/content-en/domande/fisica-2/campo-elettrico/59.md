For a dipole with charges $\pm q$ at distance $d$, the exact field on the axis at distance $r$ from the center is $E = \dfrac{2kqd\,r}{\left( r^2 - \dfrac{d^2}{4} \right)^2}$. At the point with $r = 5d$, by how much is the approximate value $\dfrac{2kqd}{r^3}$ smaller than the exact value, as a percentage of the exact value?

- $1.0\,\%$
- $2.0\,\%$
- $4.0\,\%$
- $0.50\,\%$

## Explanation

The ratio of the approximate value to the exact value is
$$
\frac{\dfrac{2kqd}{r^3}}{\dfrac{2kqd\,r}{\left( r^2 - \dfrac{d^2}{4} \right)^2}} = \frac{\left( r^2 - \dfrac{d^2}{4} \right)^2}{r^4} = \left( 1 - \frac{d^2}{4r^2} \right)^2 .
$$
For $r = 5d$ we have $\dfrac{d^2}{4r^2} = \dfrac{1}{100}$, so the ratio is $0.99^2 = 0.9801$.

Therefore the approximate value is smaller than the exact one by about $2.0\,\%$.
