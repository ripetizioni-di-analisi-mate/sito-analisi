An initially stationary negative particle is between two plates in a uniform field pointing to the right. Weight is neglected. In which direction does it start moving and how does its speed change?

- To the right with increasing speed
- To the left with increasing speed
- To the left at constant velocity from the start
- It remains at rest because the field is uniform

## Explanation

The force $q\vec E$ points to the left because the charge is negative. The force is constant and nonzero, so from rest the particle gains velocity in that direction.
