A particle with ratio $\dfrac{q}{m}=5\,\mathrm{C/kg}$ is in a uniform field $E=12\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the acceleration magnitude?

- $30\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $120\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $240\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The force is $qE$. Newton's second law gives $a=\dfrac{|q|E}{m}$. The result is $60\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
