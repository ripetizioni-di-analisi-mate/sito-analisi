A charge $4\,\mathrm{nC}$ is at the origin. What is the magnitude of its field at $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $450\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $900\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

For a point charge, $E=\dfrac{k|q|}{r^2}$ holds. The nanocoulombs are converted to coulombs. The result is $900\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
