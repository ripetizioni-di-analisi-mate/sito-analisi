A positive point source generates a field in a vacuum. Use $k=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. The distance is expressed in centimeters. Given the source charge value $q = 4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the distance from the source $r = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the electric field magnitude $E$?

- $10000\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $60000\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $20000\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

We apply $E=kq/r^2$. For the conversion, one nanocoulomb equals $10^{-9}\,\mathrm C$ and one centimeter equals $10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$. In the indicated units, $E = \dfrac{90000 \cdot q}{r^{2}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $10000\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
