An infinite plane has surface charge density $\sigma=4\varepsilon_0\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the field magnitude on each side?

- $1\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The field of a single uniformly charged plane is $E=\dfrac{|\sigma|}{2\varepsilon_0}$. The value is independent of distance. The result is $2\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
