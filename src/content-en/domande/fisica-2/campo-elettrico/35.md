In which direction does the electric dipole moment point?

- From the positive charge to the negative charge
- Always in the direction of the external field
- From the negative charge to the positive charge
- Always perpendicular to the segment joining the charges

## Explanation

By definition, $\vec p=q\vec d$, with $q>0$. The vector $\vec d$ points from the negative charge to the positive charge. The external field may have a different direction.
