At a distance of $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$, the field of a point charge is $800\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is it at $0{,}40\,\mathrm m$?

- $25\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The field of a point charge decreases as $r^{-2}$. The distance quadruples, so the field is divided by sixteen. The result is $50\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
