A uniform field has magnitude $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the magnitude of the force on $-3\,\mu\mathrm C$?

- $0{,}0006\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}0012\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}0024\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}0048\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The electric force is $\vec F=q\vec E$. Its magnitude is $|q|E$, even for a negative charge. The result is $0{,}0012\,\mathrm N$.
