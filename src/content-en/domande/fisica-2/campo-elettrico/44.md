The total field is zero at a point free of charges. Which conclusion is correct?

- All sources necessarily have zero charge
- Each contribution must be zero separately
- The test charge must be negative
- The source contributions can be nonzero and cancel each other

## Explanation

The total field is a vector sum. Two or more nonzero vectors can have a zero sum. Cancellation therefore does not require the absence of sources.
