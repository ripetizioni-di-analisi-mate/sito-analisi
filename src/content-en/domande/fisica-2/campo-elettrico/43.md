An ideal dipole is in an electric field that varies appreciably from one end to the other. Which property may no longer hold compared with a uniform field?

- Conservation of the dipole's total charge
- The relation between force and field on each charge
- Zero resultant force
- The opposite charges of the two ends

## Explanation

In a nonuniform field, the two charges may experience forces of different magnitudes. Their sum may therefore be nonzero. The local relation $\vec F=q\vec E$ continues to hold.
