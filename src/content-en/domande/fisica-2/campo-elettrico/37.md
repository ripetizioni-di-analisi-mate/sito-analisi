Which orientation of a dipole in a uniform field corresponds to stable equilibrium?

- Parallel to the field and pointing in the same direction
- Parallel to the field and pointing in the opposite direction
- Perpendicular to the field
- Any orientation with the same stability

## Explanation

The dipole energy is $U=-pE\cos\theta$. It has a minimum at $\theta=0$. A small rotation therefore generates a torque that tends to restore alignment in the same direction.
