A positive ring is uniformly charged. At a point on its axis other than the center, which field components cancel by symmetry?

- The component parallel to the axis
- The components perpendicular to the axis
- All components at every point
- Only those generated by negative charges

## Explanation

Diametrically opposite elements of the ring produce opposite transverse components. The axial components point in the same direction. Therefore, only the component along the axis remains.
