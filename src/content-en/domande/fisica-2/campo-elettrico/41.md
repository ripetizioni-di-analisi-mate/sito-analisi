An initially stationary electron is released in a uniform field pointing upward. Neglecting its weight, in which direction does it accelerate?

- Downward
- Upward
- Horizontally
- It remains at rest

## Explanation

The electron's charge is negative. In the relation $\vec a=\dfrac{q\vec E}{m}$, the coefficient of the field is therefore negative. The acceleration is opposite to the field.
