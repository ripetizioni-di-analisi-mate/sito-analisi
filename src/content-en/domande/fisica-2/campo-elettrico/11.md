A dipole has $p=6\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C\,m}$. In a field $E=200\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is its potential energy when it is parallel to the field and points in the same direction?

- $1{,}2\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm J$
- $-2{,}4\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm J$
- $-1{,}2\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

With the conventional zero at $90^\circ$, the energy is $U=-pE\cos\theta$. For alignment in the same direction, $\theta=0$ and therefore $U=-pE$. The result is $-1{,}2\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm J$.
