A uniformly charged ring has radius $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$. What is the electric field at its center?

- $90\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $360\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Each charge element has a diametrically opposite element. The fields at the center have equal magnitudes and opposite directions, so they cancel. The result is $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
