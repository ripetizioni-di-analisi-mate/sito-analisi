Two perpendicular fields have magnitudes of $600$ and $800\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the magnitude of the resultant field?

- $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The fields add vectorially. Since they are perpendicular, the Pythagorean theorem applies. The result is $1000\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
