Two equal positive point charges are fixed at some distance from each other. How many field lines go from one charge to the other?

- One, along the segment joining them
- Half of the lines leaving each charge
- All the lines leaving each charge
- None

## Explanation

A field line leaves a positive charge and ends on a negative charge or at infinity, so it cannot enter a positive charge.

The two charges are both positive, therefore **no** line goes from one to the other: all the lines go off to infinity. Even along the segment joining them, the lines start from each charge and stop at the midpoint, where the field is zero.
