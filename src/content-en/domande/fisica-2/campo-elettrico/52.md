Two equal charges $+q$ are fixed at $(-a, 0)$ and $(a, 0)$, with $a = 4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. On the $y$-axis the field is $E_y = \dfrac{2kq\,y}{\left( a^2 + y^2 \right)^{\frac{3}{2}}}$. At what distance from the origin, for $y > 0$, is the field maximum?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The field is zero at the origin and tends to $0$ as $y \to +\infty$, so it has a maximum at an intermediate point, where the derivative vanishes.

Differentiating gives
$$
\frac{dE_y}{dy} = 2kq\,\frac{\left( a^2 + y^2 \right) - 3y^2}{\left( a^2 + y^2 \right)^{\frac{5}{2}}} = 2kq\,\frac{a^2 - 2y^2}{\left( a^2 + y^2 \right)^{\frac{5}{2}}} ,
$$
which vanishes for $y = \dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$.

Therefore the maximum is at $\dfrac{4.0}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{cm} \approx 2.8\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the origin.
