A positive point source generates a field in a vacuum. Use $k=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. The distance is expressed in centimeters. Given the distance from the source $r = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the electric field magnitude $E = 10000\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the source charge value $q$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

We apply $E=kq/r^2$. For the conversion, one nanocoulomb equals $10^{-9}\,\mathrm C$ and one centimeter equals $10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$. In the indicated units, $E = \dfrac{90000 \cdot q}{r^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $q = \dfrac{E \cdot r^{2}}{90000}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $4\,\mathrm{nC}$.
