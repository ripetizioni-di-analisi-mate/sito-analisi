An initially stationary positive charge is free in a uniform field. Other forces are neglected. Its mass is $2\,\mathrm{mg}$. Given the charge $q = 4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, the elapsed time $t = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the speed $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the field magnitude $E$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The constant acceleration is $qE/m$ and the velocity increases from zero. Here, the ratio of a charge in microcoulombs to a mass in milligrams is directly in coulombs per kilogram. In the indicated units, $v = \dfrac{E \cdot q \cdot t}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $E = \dfrac{2 \cdot v}{q \cdot t}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $30\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
