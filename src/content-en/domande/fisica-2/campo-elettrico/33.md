At a point, the electrostatic field has a single nonzero vector value. What does this imply for the field lines?

- Two distinct lines cannot intersect at that point
- They must always be circles
- They must always be parallel
- They can intersect at any angle

## Explanation

The tangent to a field line coincides with the field direction. An intersection with different tangents would assign two directions to the same vector. Therefore, distinct lines do not intersect at that point.
