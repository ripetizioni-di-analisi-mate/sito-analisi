A test charge $+2\,\mu\mathrm C$ experiences a force of $0{,}006\,\mathrm N$. What is the field magnitude?

- $3000\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1{,}2\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The field is the force per unit positive charge. Therefore, $E=\dfrac{F}{q}$. The result is $3000\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
