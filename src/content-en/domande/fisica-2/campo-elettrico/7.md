Two equal positive charges are equidistant from the midpoint of the segment joining them. What is the field at the midpoint?

- $100\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The two contributions have equal magnitudes by symmetry. At the midpoint, they point in opposite directions, so they cancel. The result is $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
