A dipole with $p=2\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C\,m}$ is in a field $E=500\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and makes an angle of $90^\circ$ with it. What is the torque magnitude?

- $5\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $2\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $4\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

For a uniform dipole, $\tau=pE\sin\theta$ holds. At $90^\circ$, the sine equals one and the torque is maximum. The result is $1\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
