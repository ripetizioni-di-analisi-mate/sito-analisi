A dipole consists of charges $\pm3\,\mathrm{nC}$ separated by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the magnitude of the dipole moment, in $\mathrm{nC\,m}$?

- $0{,}012\,\mathrm{nC\,m}$
- $0{,}006\,\mathrm{nC\,m}$
- $0{,}024\,\mathrm{nC\,m}$
- $0{,}048\,\mathrm{nC\,m}$

## Explanation

The dipole moment has magnitude $p=qd$, where $d$ is the separation between the charges. The millimeters are converted to meters. The result is $0{,}012\,\mathrm{nC\,m}$.
