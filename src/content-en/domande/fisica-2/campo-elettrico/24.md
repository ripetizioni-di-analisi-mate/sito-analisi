A particle starts from rest with constant electric acceleration $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What distance does it travel in $0{,}50\,\mathrm s$?

- $0{,}5\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The motion along the field is uniformly accelerated. With zero initial velocity, $s=\dfrac{at^2}{2}$ holds. The result is $1\,\mathrm m$.
