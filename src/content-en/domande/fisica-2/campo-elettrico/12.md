A wire of length $0{,}50\,\mathrm m$ has uniform linear charge density $4\,\mathrm{nC/m}$. What is its total charge?

- $1\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

For uniform density, $Q=\lambda L$ holds. The length is already expressed in meters. The result is $2\,\mathrm{nC}$.
