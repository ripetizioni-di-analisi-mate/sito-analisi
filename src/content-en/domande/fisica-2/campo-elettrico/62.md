Two small spheres of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{g}$ each, with charges $+100\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-100\,\mathrm{nC}$, are fixed at the ends of an insulating rod of negligible mass and length $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, free to rotate in a horizontal plane about a vertical axis through its center. A uniform field $E = 1.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N/C}$ acts in the plane. What is the period of small oscillations about the position of stable equilibrium?

- $7.1 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.31\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.89\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.44\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia about the axis is $I = 2m\left( \dfrac{d}{2} \right)^2 = 2 \cdot 5.0 \cdot 10^{-4} \cdot \left( 1.0 \cdot 10^{-2} \right)^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, and the dipole moment is $p = qd = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C\cdot m}$.

The torque is $-pE\sin\theta$, so for small angles, with $\sin\theta \approx \theta$, the equation of motion is $I\,\ddot{\theta} = -pE\,\theta$, that is, a harmonic motion with $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{pE}{I}}$.

Therefore
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I}{pE}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{1.0 \cdot 10^{-7}}{2.0 \cdot 10^{-9} \cdot 1.0 \cdot 10^{4}}}\,\mathrm{s} \approx 0.44\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
