An electric dipole is perpendicular to a uniform field. The magnitude of each of the two charges is $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. Given the field magnitude $E = 300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the separation between the charges $d = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the torque magnitude $\tau$?

- $36000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$
- $12000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$
- $48000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$
- $24000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$

## Explanation

The torque is $pE$ because the dipole is perpendicular to the field. The dipole moment is $qd$. With $q=2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $d$ in centimeters, the coefficient in nanonewton meters is $20$. In the indicated units, $\tau = 20 \cdot E \cdot d$. Using the values given in the problem gives $24000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$.
