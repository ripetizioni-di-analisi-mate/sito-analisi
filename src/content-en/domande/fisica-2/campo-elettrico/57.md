An electric dipole is perpendicular to a uniform field. The magnitude of each of the two charges is $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. Given the separation between the charges $d = 4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the torque magnitude $\tau = 24000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$, what is the field magnitude $E$?

- $450\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The torque is $pE$ because the dipole is perpendicular to the field. The dipole moment is $qd$. With $q=2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $d$ in centimeters, the coefficient in nanonewton meters is $20$. In the indicated units, $\tau = 20 \cdot E \cdot d$. Solving for the unknown gives $E = \dfrac{\tau}{20 \cdot d}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
