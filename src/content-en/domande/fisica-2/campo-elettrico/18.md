The field of an infinite wire is $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$ at $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is it at $0{,}60\,\mathrm m$?

- $20\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $160\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

For an infinite wire, $E$ is proportional to $\dfrac{1}{r}$. Tripling the distance divides the field by three. The result is $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
