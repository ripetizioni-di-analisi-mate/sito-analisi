A field-line drawing contains only two point charges $q_A$ and $q_B$. Twelve lines leave $q_A$ and $6$ lines enter $q_B$. What relation holds between the two charges?

- $q_B = \dfrac{q_A}{2}$
- $q_B = -2\,q_A$
- $q_B = -\dfrac{q_A}{2}$
- $q_B = 2\,q_A$

## Explanation

Field lines leave positive charges and enter negative charges, so $q_A > 0$ and $q_B < 0$.

The number of lines leaving or entering a charge is proportional to the magnitude of the charge. Since twice as many lines leave $q_A$ as enter $q_B$, we have $|q_B| = \dfrac{|q_A|}{2}$.

Therefore $q_B = -\dfrac{q_A}{2}$; the $6$ lines that do not enter $q_B$ go off to infinity.
