Very far from a uniformly charged disk of finite radius, how does the axial field magnitude depend on the distance $x$?

- It is constant at every distance
- It is proportional to $x$
- Like that of a point charge, proportional to $x^{-2}$
- It is proportional to $x^{-1}$

## Explanation

For $x$ much greater than the radius, the distribution appears concentrated relative to the observation distance. The dominant term depends on the total charge. The field of a point charge is therefore obtained to first order.
