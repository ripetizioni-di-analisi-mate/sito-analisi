For the same dipole, at a large distance $r$, what is the ratio of the field magnitude on the axis to that on the equatorial plane?

- $1$
- $4$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

On the axis, the far field is $\dfrac{2kp}{r^3}$. On the equatorial plane, the magnitude is $\dfrac{kp}{r^3}$, so the ratio is two. The result is $2$.
