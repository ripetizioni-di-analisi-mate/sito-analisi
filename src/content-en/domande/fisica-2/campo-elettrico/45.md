A test charge strongly modifies the distribution of the source charges. What is required to measure the unperturbed field?

- The limit of a sufficiently small test charge
- An ever larger test charge
- A test charge necessarily equal to the source
- A test charge of infinite mass

## Explanation

The experimental definition of the field refers to unperturbed sources. A sufficiently small test charge alters their distribution negligibly. The force/charge ratio then gives the original field.
