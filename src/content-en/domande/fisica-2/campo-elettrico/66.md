A thin rod of length $L = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$ has charge $Q = 3.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ uniformly distributed. What is the field at the point $P$ on the extension of the rod, at distance $b = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the nearer end?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 4,0" punti="6,0,pieno,P" segmenti="0,-0.5,4,-0.5;4,-0.5,6,-0.5" etichette="2,0.35,Q;2,-0.85,L;5,-0.85,b" xmin=-0.5 xmax=6.6 ymin=-1.2 ymax=0.8 titolo="Rod of length $L$ with uniform charge $Q$ and point $P$ on its extension"}

- $9.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $2.7 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $3.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $6.7 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

With the rod between $x = 0$ and $x = L$ and the point at $x = L + b$, the element $dq = \lambda\,dx$ is at distance $L + b - x$ from $P$. All the elements lie on the line through $P$, so their fields have the same direction and sense and add without being resolved into components.

We get
$$
E = \int_0^L \frac{k\lambda\,dx}{(L + b - x)^2} = k\lambda \left( \frac{1}{b} - \frac{1}{L + b} \right) = \frac{kQ}{b\,(L + b)} ,
$$
where we used $\lambda L = Q$.

Therefore $E = \dfrac{8.99 \cdot 10^{9} \cdot 3.0 \cdot 10^{-9}}{0.10 \cdot 0.30}\,\mathrm{N/C} \approx 9.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N/C}$, directed from $Q$ toward $P$.
