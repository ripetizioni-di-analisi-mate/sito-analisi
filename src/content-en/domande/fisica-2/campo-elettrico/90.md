A positive charge passes between two regions in which the uniform fields are parallel and point in the same direction. The second field is stronger than the first. Given the charge $q = 3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, the field magnitude in the first region $E_{1} = 200\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the increase in force magnitude $d = 900\,\mathrm{\mu N}$, what is the field magnitude in the second region $E_{2}$?

- $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

In each region, the force is $qE$. The charge is the same, so the increase is proportional to the difference in field magnitudes. In the indicated units, $d = q \cdot \left(- E_{1} + E_{2}\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $E_{2} = E_{1} + \dfrac{d}{q}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
