A positive charge generates $E=225\,\mathrm{N/C}$ at $0{,}40\,\mathrm m$. What is the charge in nanocoulombs? Assume $k=9{,}0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $16\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The law $E=\dfrac{kq}{r^2}$ gives $q=\dfrac{Er^2}{k}$. The result in coulombs must then be expressed in nanocoulombs. The result is $4\,\mathrm{nC}$.
