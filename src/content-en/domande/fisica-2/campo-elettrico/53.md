A positive point source generates a field in a vacuum. Use $k=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. The distance is expressed in centimeters. Given the source charge value $q = 4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the electric field magnitude $E = 10000\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the distance from the source $r$?

- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \sqrt{10}}{50}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

We apply $E=kq/r^2$. For the conversion, one nanocoulomb equals $10^{-9}\,\mathrm C$ and one centimeter equals $10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$. In the indicated units, $E = \dfrac{90000 \cdot q}{r^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $r = \dfrac{300 \cdot \sqrt{q}}{\sqrt{E}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
