Two equal charges $q = 4.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ are fixed at $x = 0$ and $x = d = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the magnitude of the electric field at the point $x = 2d$?

- $2.7 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $7.2 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $3.6 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $4.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The point $x = 2d$ lies to the right of both charges, so the two fields point outward, both along $\hat{\imath}$, and their magnitudes add.

The charge at $x = d$ is at distance $d$ and the charge at the origin is at distance $2d$, therefore
$$
E = k\,\frac{q}{d^2} + k\,\frac{q}{4d^2} = \frac{5}{4}\,k\,\frac{q}{d^2} .
$$
Since $k\,\dfrac{q}{d^2} = \dfrac{8.99 \cdot 10^{9} \cdot 4.0 \cdot 10^{-9}}{0.10^2}\,\mathrm{N/C} \approx 3.6 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$, the field is about $4.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
