A disk of area $0{,}030\,\mathrm{m^2}$ has a uniform surface charge density $2\,\mu\mathrm{C/m^2}$. What is its total charge in microcoulombs?

- $0{,}06\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $0{,}03\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $0{,}12\,\mu\mathrm C$
- $0{,}24\,\mu\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The surface charge density is charge per unit area. Therefore, $Q=\sigma A$. The result is $0{,}06\,\mu\mathrm C$.
