Two infinite planes have equal and opposite uniform surface charge densities. What is the field in the region outside the two planes?

- $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Outside, the fields of the two planes have the same magnitude. Since they point in opposite directions, the total field is zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
