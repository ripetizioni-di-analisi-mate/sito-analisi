An electric dipole is perpendicular to a uniform field. The magnitude of each of the two charges is $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. Given the field magnitude $E = 300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the torque magnitude $\tau = 24000\,\mathrm{nN\,m}$, what is the separation between the charges $d$?

- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The torque is $pE$ because the dipole is perpendicular to the field. The dipole moment is $qd$. With $q=2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $d$ in centimeters, the coefficient in nanonewton meters is $20$. In the indicated units, $\tau = 20 \cdot E \cdot d$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{\tau}{20 \cdot E}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
