A particle enters a uniform electric field with an initial velocity perpendicular to the field. Along the field, the acceleration has constant magnitude. Weight is neglected. Given the elapsed time $t = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the magnitude of the displacement along the field $d = 1\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the acceleration magnitude $a$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Along the field, the initial velocity is zero, so only the quadratic term remains in the position as a function of time. In the indicated units, $d = \dfrac{a \cdot t^{2}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = \dfrac{2 \cdot d}{t^{2}}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
