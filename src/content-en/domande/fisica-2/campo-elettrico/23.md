A particle starts from rest with constant electric acceleration $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its velocity after $0{,}30\,\mathrm s$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The uniform field produces constant acceleration. Starting from rest, $v=at$ holds. The result is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
