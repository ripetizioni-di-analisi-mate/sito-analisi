A dipole with $pE=3\,\mathrm{mJ}$ rotates from parallel alignment in the same direction as the field to parallel alignment in the opposite direction. By how much does its potential energy increase?

- $3\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $24\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

The initial energy is $-pE$ and the final energy is $+pE$. Therefore, the increase is $\Delta U=2pE$. The result is $6\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
