A positively charged grain is in a uniform electric field. Only the electric force is considered. Given the grain charge $q = 4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the electric force magnitude $F = 1200\,\mathrm{\mu N}$, what is the field magnitude $E$?

- $600\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $450\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The definition of field is $\vec F=q\vec E$. Multiplying microcoulombs by newtons per coulomb gives micronewtons. In the indicated units, $F = E \cdot q$. Solving for the unknown gives $E = \dfrac{F}{q}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
