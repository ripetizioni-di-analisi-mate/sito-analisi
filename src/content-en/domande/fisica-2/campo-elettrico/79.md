A particle enters a uniform electric field with an initial velocity perpendicular to the field. Along the field, the acceleration has constant magnitude. Weight is neglected. Given the acceleration magnitude $a = 8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the elapsed time $t = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the magnitude of the displacement along the field $d$?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Along the field, the initial velocity is zero, so only the quadratic term remains in the position as a function of time. In the indicated units, $d = \dfrac{a \cdot t^{2}}{2}$. Using the values given in the problem gives $1\,\mathrm{m}$.
