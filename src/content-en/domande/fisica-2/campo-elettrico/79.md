An electron enters with speed $v_0 = 1.0 \cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, perpendicular to the field, a region of length $\ell = 3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ where there is a uniform field $E = 2.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What angle does its velocity form with the initial direction when it leaves the region?

- $3.0^\circ$
- $12^\circ$
- $6.0^\circ$
- $84^\circ$

## Explanation

At the exit the velocity component along $\vec{v}_0$ is still $v_0$, while the component along the field is $v_y = a\,t_u = \dfrac{eE}{m_e}\,\dfrac{\ell}{v_0}$.

Hence
$$
\tan\alpha = \frac{v_y}{v_0} = \frac{eE\ell}{m_e v_0^2} = \frac{1.60 \cdot 10^{-19} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{3} \cdot 0.030}{9.11 \cdot 10^{-31} \cdot \left( 1.0 \cdot 10^{7} \right)^2} \approx 0.105 .
$$
Therefore $\alpha \approx 6.0^\circ$.
