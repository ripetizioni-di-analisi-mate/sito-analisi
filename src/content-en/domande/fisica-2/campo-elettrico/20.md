Two infinite planes have opposite surface charge densities $\pm\sigma$, with $\sigma=6\varepsilon_0\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the field magnitude between the planes?

- $3\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The field of each plane has magnitude $\dfrac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$. Between the planes, the two contributions point in the same direction, so $E=\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$. The result is $6\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
