A beam of identical particles enters two consecutive electric-field regions horizontally. The vertical acceleration is $+a$ for a time $\tau$ and then $-a$ for the same time. The incoming vertical velocity is zero. How does the beam emerge?

- Horizontally at its original height.
- Horizontally, displaced upwards by $a\tau^2$.
- Tilted upwards with vertical velocity $2a\tau$.
- Horizontally, displaced downwards by $a\tau^2$.

## Explanation

The second field cancels the vertical velocity gained in the first. Vertical velocity stays positive in both regions. The two triangular areas on its time graph sum to $\dfrac12a\tau^2+\dfrac12a\tau^2=a\tau^2$.
