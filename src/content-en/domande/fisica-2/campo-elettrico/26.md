A particle enters with no vertical velocity into a field that gives it $a_y=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. If it remains in the region for $0{,}20\,\mathrm s$, what is its vertical deflection?

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The vertical motion has constant acceleration and zero initial velocity. Therefore, $y=\dfrac{a_yt^2}{2}$. The result is $0{,}2\,\mathrm m$.
