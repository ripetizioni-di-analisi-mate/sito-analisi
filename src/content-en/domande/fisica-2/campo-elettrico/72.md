A very large plane and a probe are in a vacuum. The probe is far from the edges. The charge density of the plane is uniform and equals $2\varepsilon_0 E$. Initially, the given quantities are the magnitude of the probe charge $q = 6\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the magnitude of the field generated by the plane $E = 500\,\mathrm{N/C}$. In the second configuration, we change the magnitude of the field generated by the plane to $1000\,\mathrm{N/C}$, with all other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $F$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

In the infinite-plane model, the field is $\sigma/(2\varepsilon_0)$. To find the force, we multiply by the magnitude of the probe charge. Applying $F = E \cdot q$ gives $3000\,\mathrm{nN}$ before the change and $6000\,\mathrm{nN}$ afterward. The ratio is $2$.
