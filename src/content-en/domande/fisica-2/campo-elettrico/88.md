A positive charge passes between two regions in which the uniform fields are parallel and point in the same direction. The second field is stronger than the first. Given the charge $q = 3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, the field magnitude in the second region $E_{2} = 500\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the field magnitude in the first region $E_{1} = 200\,\mathrm{N/C}$, what is the increase in force magnitude $d$?

- $1500\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $2100\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $900\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

In each region, the force is $qE$. The charge is the same, so the increase is proportional to the difference in field magnitudes. In the indicated units, $d = q \cdot \left(- E_{1} + E_{2}\right)$. Using the values given in the problem gives $900\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
