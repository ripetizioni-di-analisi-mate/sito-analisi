A field has components $E_x=-300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and $E_y=400\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the field magnitude?

- $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $700\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $2500\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The magnitude does not depend on the signs of the components. It is calculated using $E=\sqrt{E_x^2+E_y^2}$. The result is $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
