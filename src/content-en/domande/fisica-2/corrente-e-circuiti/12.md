A resistor of $3\,\Omega$ is in series with the parallel combination of $6\,\Omega$ and $12\,\Omega$. The network is supplied with $21\,\mathrm V$. What current flows through the $12\,\Omega$ branch?

- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $3\,\mathrm A$
- $0{,}5\,\mathrm A$
- $1\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The parallel combination is equivalent to $\dfrac{6\cdot12}{6+12}\,\Omega=4\,\Omega$. The total current is $21/(3+4)\,\mathrm A=3\,\mathrm A$, so the voltage drop across the parallel combination is $12\,\mathrm V$. Dividing by $12\,\Omega$ gives $1\,\mathrm A$.
