A capacitor charges from zero through a resistor connected to an ideal source. The time at which the voltage reaches half its final value is measured. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 8\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ and the capacitance $C = 25\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, what is the required time $t$?

- $400 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $100 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $100\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $200 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

The fraction charged is $1-e^{-t/(R_e C)}$. When it is $1/2$, the exponential is $1/2$. In the indicated units, $t = C \cdot R_{e} \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}$. Using the given values, we obtain $200 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$.
