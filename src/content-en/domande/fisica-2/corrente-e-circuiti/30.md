An ideal source maintains the same voltage across a resistor. If the resistance doubles, what is the ratio of the final power to the initial power?

- $2$
- $0{,}5$
- $4$
- $0{,}25$

## Explanation

At constant voltage, $P=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$. The power is therefore inversely proportional to the resistance. The result is $0{,}5$.
