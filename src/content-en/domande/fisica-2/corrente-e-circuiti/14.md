Two resistors of $4$ and $8\,\Omega$ are connected in parallel across $12\,\mathrm V$. What current flows through the $8\,\Omega$ branch?

- $0{,}75\,\mathrm A$
- $1{,}5\,\mathrm A$
- $3\,\mathrm A$
- $6\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The branch has the same voltage as the battery. Its current is therefore $I=\dfrac{12}{8}$ in amperes. The result is $1{,}5\,\mathrm A$.
