An initially uncharged capacitor charges through $R=4\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ from an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ battery. What is the initial current?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

At the initial instant, the voltage across the capacitor is zero. The entire voltage is therefore across the resistor and $I_0=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R}$. The result is $3\,\mathrm{mA}$.
