A resistor of $12\,\Omega$ is connected to $6\,\mathrm V$. What is the power?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm W$
- $6\,\mathrm W$
- $3\,\mathrm W$
- $12\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The power can be written as $P=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$. This form directly uses voltage and resistance. The result is $3\,\mathrm W$.
