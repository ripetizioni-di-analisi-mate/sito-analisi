A resistance is $100\,\Omega$ at $T_0$. The temperature coefficient is $0{,}004\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. What is the resistance after an increase of $50\,\mathrm K$, in the linear model?

- $80\,\Omega$
- $120\,\Omega$
- $100\,\Omega$
- $150\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The linear model gives $R=R_0[1+\alpha(T-T_0)]$. The correction factor is $1+0{,}004\cdot50=1{,}2$. The result is $120\,\Omega$.
