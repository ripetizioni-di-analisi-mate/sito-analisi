A real source supplies current to a load. How does its terminal voltage compare with its electromotive force?

- It is always greater
- It is always twice as large
- It is always zero
- It is lower if the internal resistance is positive

## Explanation

The current causes an internal voltage drop of $Ir$. The voltage available to the load is $\mathcal E-Ir$. For supplied current and positive resistance, it is therefore less than $\mathcal E$.
