A wire is replaced with one of the same material, twice as long and with half the cross-sectional area. By what factor does the resistance increase?

- $4$
- $2$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

The resistance is proportional to $\dfrac{L}{A}$. Doubling the length and halving the area therefore multiplies $R$ by four. The result is $4$.
