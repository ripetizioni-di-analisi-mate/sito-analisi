Two resistors of $6$ and $3\,\Omega$ are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $3\,\Omega$
- $9\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$
- $18\,\Omega$

## Explanation

In parallel, the conductances add. For two resistors, $R_{eq}=\dfrac{R_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}$. The result is $2\,\Omega$.
