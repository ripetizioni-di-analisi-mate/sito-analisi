A resistor of $5\,\Omega$ carries a current of $2\,\mathrm A$. What power does it dissipate?

- $10\,\mathrm W$
- $20\,\mathrm W$
- $40\,\mathrm W$
- $80\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The Joule heating power is $P=I^2R$. The current must be squared. The result is $20\,\mathrm W$.
