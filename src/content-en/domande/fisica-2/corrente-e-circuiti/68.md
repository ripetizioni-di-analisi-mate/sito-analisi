An ohmic resistor remains at constant temperature. Calculate the dissipated power from the known voltage and resistance. Given the voltage across it $U_{e} = 24\,\mathrm{V}$ and the dissipated power $P = 48\,\mathrm{W}$, what is the resistance $R_{e}$?

- $24\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$

## Explanation

For Joule heating, $P=U_e I_e$. Ohm's law allows the substitution $I_e=U_e/R_e$. In the indicated units, $P = \dfrac{U_{e}^{2}}{R_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = \dfrac{U_{e}^{2}}{P}$. Using the given values, we obtain $12\,\Omega$.
