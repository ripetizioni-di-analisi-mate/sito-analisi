A battery with an electromotive force of $18\,\mathrm V$ supplies a resistor. The battery has nonnegligible internal resistance. Given the internal resistance $r = 1\,\Omega$ and the terminal voltage $U_{e} = 16\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the load resistance $R_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}\,\Omega$
- $8\,\Omega$
- $17\,\Omega$
- $16\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The current is $18/(R_e+r)$. The voltage measured at the terminals is the drop across the load, which is the current multiplied by $R_e$. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{18 \cdot R_{e}}{R_{e} + r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = - \dfrac{U_{e} \cdot r}{U_{e} - 18}$. Using the given values, we obtain $8\,\Omega$.
