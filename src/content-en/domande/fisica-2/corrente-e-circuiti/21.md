An RC circuit has $R=20\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ and $C=5\,\mu\mathrm F$. What is its time constant?

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}05\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm s$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The time constant is $\tau=RC$. The kilo and micro prefixes give an overall factor of $10^{-3}$. The result is $0{,}1\,\mathrm s$.
