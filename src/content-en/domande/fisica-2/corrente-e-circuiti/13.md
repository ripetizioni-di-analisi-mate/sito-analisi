A resistor of $2\,\Omega$ is in series with a $4\,\Omega$ resistor connected to an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ battery. What is the voltage across the $4\,\Omega$ resistor?

- $8\,\mathrm V$
- $4\,\mathrm V$
- $16\,\mathrm V$
- $32\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The series current is $\dfrac{12}{2+4}=2\,\mathrm A$. The required voltage is therefore $V_2=IR_2$. The result is $8\,\mathrm V$.
