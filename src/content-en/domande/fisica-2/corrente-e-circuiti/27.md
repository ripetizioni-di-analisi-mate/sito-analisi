Two resistors of $3$ and $6\,\Omega$ are connected in parallel, and the combination is in series with $4\,\Omega$. What is the total resistance?

- $9\,\Omega$
- $13\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$
- $18\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The parallel resistance is $\dfrac{3\cdot6}{3+6}=2\,\Omega$. The $4\,\Omega$ series resistor is then added. The result is $6\,\Omega$.
