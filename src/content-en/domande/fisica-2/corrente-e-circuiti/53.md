A homogeneous wire is uniformly stretched to three times its initial length while its volume and resistivity remain constant. What is the ratio of final to initial resistance?

- $3$
- $1$
- $9$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The volume $AL$ is constant, so the new cross-section is $A/3$. In $R=\rho L/A$, tripling the length and reducing the cross-section to one third together give a factor of nine.
