Two resistors are in series and carry the same current. The total voltage drop across the pair is $24\,\mathrm V$. Given the first resistance $R_{1} = 4\,\Omega$ and the current $I_{e} = 2\,\mathrm{A}$, what is the second resistance $R_{2}$?

- $16\,\Omega$
- $4\,\Omega$
- $8\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$

## Explanation

In series, the voltage drops add and are $I_e R_1$ and $I_e R_2$. In the indicated units, $I_{e} = \dfrac{24}{R_{1} + R_{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{2} = - R_{1} + \dfrac{24}{I_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $8\,\Omega$.
