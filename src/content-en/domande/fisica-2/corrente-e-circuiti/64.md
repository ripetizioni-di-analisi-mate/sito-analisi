A battery with an electromotive force of $18\,\mathrm V$ supplies a resistor. The battery has nonnegligible internal resistance. Initially, the given quantities are the load resistance $R_{e} = 8\,\Omega$ and the internal resistance $r = 1\,\Omega$. In the second configuration, we change the internal resistance to $4\,\Omega$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The current is $18/(R_e+r)$. The voltage measured at the terminals is the drop across the load, which is the current multiplied by $R_e$. Applying $U_{e} = \dfrac{18 \cdot R_{e}}{R_{e} + r}$ gives $16\,\mathrm{V}$ before the change and $12\,\mathrm{V}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
