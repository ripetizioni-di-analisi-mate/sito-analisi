A component has two ends $A$ and $B$ with $V_A - V_B = 6.0\,\mathrm{V}$. A steady current of $2.0\,\mathrm{A}$ enters the component at $B$ and leaves it at $A$. What does the component do?

- It absorbs a power of $12\,\mathrm{W}$ from the circuit
- It delivers a power of $12\,\mathrm{W}$ to the circuit
- It absorbs a power of $3.0\,\mathrm{W}$ from the circuit
- It delivers a power of $3.0\,\mathrm{W}$ to the circuit

## Explanation

The power received by a component carrying a current $I$ that enters at one end and leaves at the other is the product of $I$ and the potential of the entry end minus that of the exit end. Here the current enters at $B$, so
$$
P = (V_B - V_A)\, I = -6.0 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{W} = -12\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
The power is negative, therefore the component **delivers** $12\,\mathrm{W}$ to the circuit: the current crosses it from the lower to the higher potential, as in a source that supplies current.
