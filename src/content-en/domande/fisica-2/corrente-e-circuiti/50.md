An ideal battery supplies two resistors in parallel. If one branch is opened, how do the current in the other branch and the current supplied by the battery change?

- Both currents decrease
- The current in the remaining branch stays the same and the total current decreases
- The current in the remaining branch increases and the total current stays the same
- Both currents stay the same

## Explanation

The ideal battery maintains the same voltage across the remaining branch, so its current does not change. The total current loses the contribution from the open branch.
