## Explanation

Removing the first cell leaves an infinite ladder identical to the original one, so between its terminals there is the same resistance $R_{\text{eq}}$. The first cell therefore consists of $R$ in series with the parallel combination of $R$ and $R_{\text{eq}}$:
$$
R_{\text{eq}} = R + \frac{R\, R_{\text{eq}}}{R + R_{\text{eq}}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_{\text{eq}}^2 - R\, R_{\text{eq}} - R^2 = 0 .
$$
The equivalent resistance is positive, so the positive root is taken, $R_{\text{eq}} = \dfrac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}\, R \approx 1.62 \cdot 2.0\,\Omega \approx 3.2\,\Omega$. The value $1.2\,\Omega$ is the magnitude of the negative root, and $4.0\,\Omega$ is the series of the two resistors of the first cell, which neglects the rest of the ladder.
