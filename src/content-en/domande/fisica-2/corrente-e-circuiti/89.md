A capacitor charges from zero through a resistor connected to an ideal source. The time at which the voltage reaches half its final value is measured. Given the capacitance $C = 25\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ and the required time $t = 200 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$, what is the resistance $R_{e}$?

- $16\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$
- $4\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$
- $8\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$
- $16 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$

## Explanation

The fraction charged is $1-e^{-t/(R_e C)}$. When it is $1/2$, the exponential is $1/2$. In the indicated units, $t = C \cdot R_{e} \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R_{e} = \dfrac{t}{C \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $8\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$.
