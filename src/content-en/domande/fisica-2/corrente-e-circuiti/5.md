In a wire, $n|q|=8\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{C/m^3}$ and $J=4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$. What is the magnitude of the drift velocity?

- $0{,}0005\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0{,}00025\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0{,}001\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0{,}002\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For a single type of carrier, $J=n|q|v_d$. Thus $v_d=\dfrac{J}{n|q|}$. The result is $0{,}0005\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
