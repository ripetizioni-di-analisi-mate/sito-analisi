A battery with an electromotive force of $18\,\mathrm V$ supplies a resistor. The battery has nonnegligible internal resistance. Given the load resistance $R_{e} = 8\,\Omega$ and the terminal voltage $U_{e} = 16\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the internal resistance $r$?

- $1\,\Omega$
- $64\,\Omega$
- $\dfrac{8}{17}\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The current is $18/(R_e+r)$. The voltage measured at the terminals is the drop across the load, which is the current multiplied by $R_e$. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{18 \cdot R_{e}}{R_{e} + r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $r = \dfrac{R_{e} \cdot \left(18 - U_{e}\right)}{U_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $1\,\Omega$.
