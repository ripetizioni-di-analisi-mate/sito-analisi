A capacitor discharges through a resistor. Which quantity decays as $e^{-\dfrac{2t}{\tau}}$?

- The charge
- The voltage
- The stored energy
- The magnitude of the current

## Explanation

Charge and voltage decay as $e^{-\dfrac{t}{\tau}}$. The energy is proportional to the square of the voltage. It therefore decays as $e^{-\dfrac{2t}{\tau}}$.
