A single-loop circuit contains two opposing ideal sources and a total resistance of $4\,\Omega$. The first electromotive force is greater than the second. Given the second electromotive force $U_{2} = 8\,\mathrm{V}$ and the magnitude of the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$, what is the first electromotive force $U_{1}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{V}$
- $40\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $14\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The electromotive forces enter the loop rule with opposite signs. Their difference balances the resistive voltage drop. In the indicated units, $I_{e} = \dfrac{U_{1}}{4} - \dfrac{U_{2}}{4}$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{1} = 4 \cdot I_{e} + U_{2}$. Using the given values, we obtain $20\,\mathrm{V}$.
