A battery with an electromotive force of $18\,\mathrm V$ supplies a resistor. The battery has nonnegligible internal resistance. Given the load resistance $R_{e} = 8\,\Omega$ and the internal resistance $r = 1\,\Omega$, what is the terminal voltage $U_{e}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $14\,\mathrm{V}$
- $16\,\mathrm{V}$
- $32\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The current is $18/(R_e+r)$. The voltage measured at the terminals is the drop across the load, which is the current multiplied by $R_e$. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = \dfrac{18 \cdot R_{e}}{R_{e} + r}$. Using the given values, we obtain $16\,\mathrm{V}$.
