A loop contains a $15\,\mathrm V$ battery and two resistive voltage drops, one of $6\,\mathrm V$. What is the other voltage drop?

- $9\,\mathrm V$
- $6\,\mathrm V$
- $15\,\mathrm V$
- $21\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The loop rule requires the algebraic sum of the voltages to be zero. The two voltage drops must therefore add up to $15\,\mathrm V$. The result is $9\,\mathrm V$.
