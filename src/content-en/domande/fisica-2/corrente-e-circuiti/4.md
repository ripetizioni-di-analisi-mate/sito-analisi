A wire carries $3\,\mathrm A$ through a cross-sectional area of $2\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. What is the average current density?

- $7{,}5\cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$
- $3\cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$
- $6\cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$
- $1{,}5\cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$

## Explanation

For uniform current across the cross section, $J=\dfrac{I}{A}$. The cross-sectional area is $2\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The result is $1{,}5\cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$.
