The time constant $RC$ is doubled. How does the time required to reach the same fraction of the final charge change?

- It is halved
- It quadruples
- It doubles
- It remains unchanged

## Explanation

The fraction of the charge depends on the ratio $\dfrac{t}{\tau}$. To keep it the same, this ratio must remain the same. Doubling $\tau$ therefore doubles the time.
