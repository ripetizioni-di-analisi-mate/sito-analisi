The charge that has passed is $Q(t)=3t^2+2t$ in SI units. What is the current at $t=2\,\mathrm s$?

- $7\,\mathrm A$
- $10\,\mathrm A$
- $14\,\mathrm A$
- $28\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The instantaneous current is the derivative of $Q$. Here $I(t)=6t+2$. The result is $14\,\mathrm A$.
