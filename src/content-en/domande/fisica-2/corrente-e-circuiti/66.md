A battery with electromotive force $24\,\mathrm{V}$ and internal resistance $2.0\,\Omega$ supplies a $6.0\,\Omega$ resistor. What fraction of the power delivered by the electromotive force reaches the resistor?

- $25\,\%$
- $50\,\%$
- $33\,\%$
- $75\,\%$

## Explanation

The current is the same in the internal resistance and in the resistor, so the delivered power is $\mathcal{E} I = (R + r)\, I^2$ and the power on the resistor is $R I^2$. The efficiency is therefore
$$
\eta = \frac{R I^2}{(R + r)\, I^2} = \frac{R}{R + r} = \frac{6.0}{8.0} = 0.75 .
$$
The value $50\,\%$ holds only when $R = r$, that is, under the maximum power transfer condition, and $25\,\%$ is the fraction dissipated in the internal resistance.
