An ohmic resistor remains at constant temperature. Calculate the dissipated power from the known voltage and resistance. Given the voltage across it $U_{e} = 24\,\mathrm{V}$ and the resistance $R_{e} = 12\,\Omega$, what is the dissipated power $P$?

- $288\,\mathrm{W}$
- $48\,\mathrm{W}$
- $96\,\mathrm{W}$
- $24\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

For Joule heating, $P=U_e I_e$. Ohm's law allows the substitution $I_e=U_e/R_e$. In the indicated units, $P = \dfrac{U_{e}^{2}}{R_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $48\,\mathrm{W}$.
