Two resistors are in series and carry the same current. The total voltage drop across the pair is $24\,\mathrm V$. Initially, the given quantities are the first resistance $R_{1} = 4\,\Omega$ and the second resistance $R_{2} = 8\,\Omega$. In the second configuration, we change the first resistance to $6\,\Omega$ and the second resistance to $10\,\Omega$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $I_{e}$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $- \dfrac{13}{5}\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

In series, the voltage drops add and are $I_e R_1$ and $I_e R_2$. Applying $I_{e} = \dfrac{24}{R_{1} + R_{2}}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{A}$ before the change and $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $- \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$.
