A charge of $18\,\mathrm C$ passes through a cross section in $3\,\mathrm s$. What is the average current?

- $6\,\mathrm A$
- $3\,\mathrm A$
- $12\,\mathrm A$
- $24\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The average current is the transferred charge divided by the time interval. Thus $I_{med}=\dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}$. The result is $6\,\mathrm A$.
