A capacitor in an RC circuit is charged by a $6\,\mathrm V$ battery. In the final steady state, it holds $30\,\mu\mathrm C$. What is its capacitance?

- $5\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $2{,}5\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $10\,\mu\mathrm F$
- $20\,\mu\mathrm F$

## Explanation

In steady state, the current is zero and the voltage across the capacitor equals the battery voltage. Thus $C=\dfrac{Q}{\mathcal E}$. The result is $5\,\mu\mathrm F$.
