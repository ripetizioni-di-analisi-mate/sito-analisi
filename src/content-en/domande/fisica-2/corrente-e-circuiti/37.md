An ideal capacitor in a DC-powered RC circuit has finished charging. How does it behave in steady state?

- Like an open circuit for direct current
- Like a permanent short circuit
- Like a resistor always equal to $R$
- Like a constant-current source

## Explanation

In steady state, the charge on the capacitor does not change. Thus $I=\dfrac{dQ}{dt}=0$. The capacitive branch does not conduct direct current.
