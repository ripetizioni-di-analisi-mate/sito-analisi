An initially charged capacitor discharges through a resistor. The time required for the voltage to fall to a quarter of its initial value is measured. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 10\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ and the capacitance $C = 20\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, what is the required time $t$?

- $400 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $800 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $200 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $800\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

The voltage is proportional to $e^{-t/(R_e C)}$. Imposing the factor $1/4$ gives $\ln 4$. The product of kilohms and microfarads is expressed in milliseconds. In the indicated units, $t = \log{\left(4^{C \cdot R_{e}} \right)}$. Using the given values, we obtain $400 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$.
