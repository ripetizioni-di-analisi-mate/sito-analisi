A single-loop circuit contains two opposing ideal sources and a total resistance of $4\,\Omega$. The first electromotive force is greater than the second. Given the first electromotive force $U_{1} = 20\,\mathrm{V}$ and the second electromotive force $U_{2} = 8\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the magnitude of the current $I_{e}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $7\,\mathrm{A}$
- $6\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The electromotive forces enter the loop rule with opposite signs. Their difference balances the resistive voltage drop. In the indicated units, $I_{e} = \dfrac{U_{1}}{4} - \dfrac{U_{2}}{4}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{A}$.
