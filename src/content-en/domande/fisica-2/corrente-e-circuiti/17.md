A battery of $12\,\mathrm V$ with internal resistance $0{,}50\,\Omega$ supplies $2\,\mathrm A$. What is the terminal voltage?

- $11\,\mathrm V$
- $8\,\mathrm V$
- $14\,\mathrm V$
- $24\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

When the battery supplies current, the internal voltage drop is $Ir$. The terminal voltage is therefore $V=\mathcal E-Ir$. The result is $11\,\mathrm V$.
