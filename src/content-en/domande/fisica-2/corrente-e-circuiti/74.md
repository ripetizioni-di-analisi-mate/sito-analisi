Three $9.0\,\Omega$ resistors are connected in a triangle (delta), that is, each one between two of the three vertices $A$, $B$, $C$. What is the equivalent resistance between the vertices $A$ and $B$?

- $27\,\Omega$
- $3.0\,\Omega$
- $6.0\,\Omega$
- $4.5\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Between $A$ and $B$ there are two paths: the resistor connecting them directly, of $9.0\,\Omega$, and the series of the two resistors passing through $C$, of $18\,\Omega$. The two paths are connected between the same nodes, so they are in parallel, and
$$
R_{\text{eq}} = \frac{9.0 \cdot 18}{9.0 + 18}\,\Omega = 6.0\,\Omega .
$$
The value $3.0\,\Omega$ treats the three resistors as all in parallel, and $27\,\Omega$ as all in series.
