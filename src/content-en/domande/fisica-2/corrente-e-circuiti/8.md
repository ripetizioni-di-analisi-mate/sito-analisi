A wire has resistivity $2\cdot10^{-8}\,\Omega\,\mathrm m$, length $10\,\mathrm m$ and cross-sectional area $1\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. What is its resistance?

- $0{,}1\,\Omega$
- $0{,}4\,\Omega$
- $0{,}8\,\Omega$
- $0{,}2\,\Omega$

## Explanation

For a uniform wire, $R=\dfrac{\rho L}{A}$. The cross-sectional area must be converted to square meters. The result is $0{,}2\,\Omega$.
