Two resistors of $4$ and $6\,\Omega$ are connected in series. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $2\,\Omega$
- $10\,\Omega$
- $2{,}4\,\Omega$
- $24\,\Omega$

## Explanation

In series, the current is common and the voltage drops add. Thus $R_{eq}=R_1+R_2$. The result is $10\,\Omega$.
