A resistor of $8\,\Omega$ has a voltage of $24\,\mathrm V$ across it. What current flows through it?

- $1{,}5\,\mathrm A$
- $3\,\mathrm A$
- $6\,\mathrm A$
- $12\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

For an ohmic resistor, $V=RI$. We therefore obtain $I=\dfrac{V}{R}$. The result is $3\,\mathrm A$.
