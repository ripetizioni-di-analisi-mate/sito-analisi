A battery with an electromotive force of $18\,\mathrm V$ supplies a resistor. The battery has nonnegligible internal resistance. Initially, the given quantities are the load resistance $R_{e} = 8\,\Omega$ and the internal resistance $r = 1\,\Omega$. In the second configuration, we change the load resistance to $12\,\Omega$ and the internal resistance to $3\,\Omega$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $U_{e}$?

- $- \dfrac{16}{5}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\dfrac{72}{5}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $- \dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The current is $18/(R_e+r)$. The voltage measured at the terminals is the drop across the load, which is the current multiplied by $R_e$. Applying $U_{e} = \dfrac{18 \cdot R_{e}}{R_{e} + r}$ gives $16\,\mathrm{V}$ before the change and $\dfrac{72}{5}\,\mathrm{V}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $- \dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{V}$.
