A capacitor discharges through a $10\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ resistor. The graph shows the voltage across it and the tangent to the graph at the initial instant. What is the capacitance of the capacitor?

::grafico{f="(x>=0?1:NaN)*10*exp(-x/2);(x>=0&&x<=2.2?1:NaN)*(10-5*x)" punti="0,10,pieno" xmin=-0.5 xmax=9.5 ymin=-0.8 ymax=11.6 nomi="t,ΔV" titolo="Voltage across the capacitor during the discharge, with $t$ in seconds and $\Delta V$ in volts; the dashed line is the tangent to the graph at $t = 0$ and meets the time axis at $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$"}

- $290\,\mu\mathrm{F}$
- $200\,\mu\mathrm{F}$
- $100\,\mu\mathrm{F}$
- $20\,\mu\mathrm{F}$

## Explanation

In the discharge $\Delta V(t) = \Delta V_0\, e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}$, so the derivative at the initial instant is $-\dfrac{\Delta V_0}{\tau}$. The tangent at $t = 0$ has equation $\Delta V = \Delta V_0 \left( 1 - \dfrac{t}{\tau} \right)$, and it meets the time axis at $t = \tau$. The graph therefore gives $\tau = 2\,\mathrm{s}$, and
$$
C = \frac{\tau}{R} = \frac{2}{10 \cdot 10^{3}}\,\mathrm{F} = 200\,\mu\mathrm{F} .
$$
The value $290\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ is obtained by reading $2\,\mathrm{s}$ as the half-life $\tau \ln 2$.
