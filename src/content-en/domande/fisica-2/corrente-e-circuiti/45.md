An ideal battery is connected to a wire of exactly zero resistance with no other elements. What limitation of the model arises?

- The model does not determine a finite current
- The current is necessarily zero
- The battery becomes a capacitor
- The current is always one ampere

## Explanation

Applying $I=\dfrac{V}{R}$ with nonzero voltage and zero resistance does not give a finite value. A real circuit contains resistance and inductance. These elements limit the current.
