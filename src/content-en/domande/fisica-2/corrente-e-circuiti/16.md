A battery has electromotive force $12\,\mathrm V$ and internal resistance $1\,\Omega$. It supplies a $5\,\Omega$ load. What is the current?

- $1\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $8\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The internal resistance is in series with the load. Thus $I=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R+r}$. The result is $2\,\mathrm A$.
