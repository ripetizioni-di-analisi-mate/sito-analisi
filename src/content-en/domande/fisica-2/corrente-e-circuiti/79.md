Two currents flow into a node. A current of $1\,\mathrm A$ leaves the node through a third branch, and another leaves through a fourth branch. The node does not accumulate charge. Given the first incoming current $I_{1} = 4\,\mathrm{A}$ and the second incoming current $I_{2} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$, what is the outgoing current in the fourth branch $I_{e}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{A}$
- $8\,\mathrm{A}$
- $12\,\mathrm{A}$
- $7\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The sum of the incoming currents equals the sum of the outgoing currents. The ampere leaving through the third branch is subtracted from the incoming total. In the indicated units, $I_{e} = I_{1} + I_{2} - 1$. Using the given values, we obtain $6\,\mathrm{A}$.
