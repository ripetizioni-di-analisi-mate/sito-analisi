A network of sources and resistors has $4$ nodes and $6$ branches. To solve it with Kirchhoff's laws, how many independent equations are obtained from the loop rule?

- $3$
- $4$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

The unknowns are the $b = 6$ branch currents. The junction rule gives $n - 1 = 3$ independent equations, because the equation of the last node follows from the others. The missing equations come from the loops, which therefore give
$$
b - n + 1 = 6 - 4 + 1 = 3 .
$$
Altogether there are $3 + 3 = 6$ equations, as many as the unknowns.
