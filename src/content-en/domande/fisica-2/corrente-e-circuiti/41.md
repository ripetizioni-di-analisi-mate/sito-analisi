An ohmic resistor is heated while the resistivity is kept constant in the model used. If the voltage doubles, how does the current change?

- It doubles
- It is halved
- It quadruples
- It remains constant

## Explanation

In the constant-resistance model, $I=\dfrac{V}{R}$. The current is proportional to the voltage. It therefore doubles along with it.
