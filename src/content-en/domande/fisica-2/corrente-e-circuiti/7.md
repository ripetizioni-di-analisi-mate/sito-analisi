A current of $0{,}50\,\mathrm A$ flows through a resistor with voltage $6\,\mathrm V$ across it. What is the resistance?

- $6\,\Omega$
- $24\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $48\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Ohm's law gives $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$. The current is given in amperes. The result is $12\,\Omega$.
