A resistor has resistance $100\,\Omega$ at the reference temperature. Its temperature coefficient is constant and positive over the interval considered. Given the temperature coefficient $a = 4\,10^{-3}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ and the temperature increase $T = 50\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the final resistance $R_{e}$?

- $20100\,\Omega$
- $110\,\Omega$
- $120\,\Omega$
- $240\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The linear approximation uses $R=R_0(1+\alpha\Delta T)$. The numerical value of $a$ is expressed in thousandths per kelvin. In the indicated units, $R_{e} = \dfrac{T \cdot a}{10} + 100$. Using the given values, we obtain $120\,\Omega$.
