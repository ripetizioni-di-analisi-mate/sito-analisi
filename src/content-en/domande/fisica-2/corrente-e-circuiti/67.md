Four identical cells, each with electromotive force $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$ and internal resistance $0.40\,\Omega$, are connected in series, with the positive terminal of each one connected to the negative terminal of the next, and supply a $2.4\,\Omega$ resistor. What current flows through the resistor?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2.1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.54\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The cells are in series with the same orientation, so the electromotive forces add up, $\mathcal{E}_{\text{tot}} = 4 \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{V} = 6.0\,\mathrm{V}$. The internal resistances are in series too, and their sum is $4 \cdot 0.40\,\Omega = 1.6\,\Omega$. The circuit has a single loop, therefore
$$
I = \frac{6.0}{2.4 + 1.6}\,\mathrm{A} = 1.5\,\mathrm{A} .
$$
The value $2.1\,\mathrm{A}$ counts only one internal resistance, and $2.5\,\mathrm{A}$ neglects them all.
