An ohmic resistor remains at constant temperature. Calculate the dissipated power from the known voltage and resistance. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 12\,\Omega$ and the dissipated power $P = 48\,\mathrm{W}$, what is the voltage across it $U_{e}$?

- $24\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $48\,\mathrm{V}$
- $24 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For Joule heating, $P=U_e I_e$. Ohm's law allows the substitution $I_e=U_e/R_e$. In the indicated units, $P = \dfrac{U_{e}^{2}}{R_{e}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $U_{e} = \sqrt{P} \cdot \sqrt{R_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $24\,\mathrm{V}$.
