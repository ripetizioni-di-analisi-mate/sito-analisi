A constant current of $2\,\mathrm A$ flows for $5\,\mathrm{min}$. How much charge passes through a cross section?

- $300\,\mathrm C$
- $600\,\mathrm C$
- $1200\,\mathrm C$
- $2400\,\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The charge is $Q=It$. Five minutes correspond to $300\,\mathrm s$. The result is $600\,\mathrm C$.
