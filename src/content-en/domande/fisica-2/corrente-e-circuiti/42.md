Which material has higher conductivity at the same temperature?

- The one with higher resistivity
- The one with lower resistivity
- The one with greater length
- The one with smaller cross-sectional area

## Explanation

Conductivity is the reciprocal of resistivity. Lower resistivity therefore corresponds to higher conductivity. Length and cross-sectional area describe the sample, rather than this property of the material.
