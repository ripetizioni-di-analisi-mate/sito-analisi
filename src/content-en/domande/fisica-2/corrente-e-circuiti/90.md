A capacitor charges from zero through a resistor connected to an ideal source. The time at which the voltage reaches half its final value is measured. Given the resistance $R_{e} = 8\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ and the required time $t = 200 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{ms}$, what is the capacitance $C$?

- $50\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $25\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $50 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

The fraction charged is $1-e^{-t/(R_e C)}$. When it is $1/2$, the exponential is $1/2$. In the indicated units, $t = C \cdot R_{e} \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $C = \dfrac{t}{R_{e} \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $25\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
