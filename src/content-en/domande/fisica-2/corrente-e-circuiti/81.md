Two currents flow into a node. A current of $1\,\mathrm A$ leaves the node through a third branch, and another leaves through a fourth branch. The node does not accumulate charge. Given the first incoming current $I_{1} = 4\,\mathrm{A}$ and the outgoing current in the fourth branch $I_{e} = 6\,\mathrm{A}$, what is the second incoming current $I_{2}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The sum of the incoming currents equals the sum of the outgoing currents. The ampere leaving through the third branch is subtracted from the incoming total. In the indicated units, $I_{e} = I_{1} + I_{2} - 1$. Solving for the unknown gives $I_{2} = - I_{1} + I_{e} + 1$. Using the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{A}$.
