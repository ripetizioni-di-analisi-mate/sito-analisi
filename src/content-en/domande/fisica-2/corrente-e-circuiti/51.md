Two resistors are in series and carry the same current. The total voltage drop across the pair is $24\,\mathrm V$. Given the first resistance $R_{1} = 4\,\Omega$ and the second resistance $R_{2} = 8\,\Omega$, what is the current $I_{e}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $9\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $4\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

In series, the voltage drops add and are $I_e R_1$ and $I_e R_2$. In the indicated units, $I_{e} = \dfrac{24}{R_{1} + R_{2}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $2\,\mathrm{A}$.
