An appliance rated at $100\,\mathrm W$ remains on for $2\,\mathrm h$. What energy does it consume in kilowatt-hours?

- $0{,}1\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

The energy is $E=Pt$. The power is $0{,}100\,\mathrm{kW}$ and the time is expressed in hours. The result is $0{,}2\,\mathrm{kWh}$.
