Two ideal sources of $12$ and $5\,\mathrm V$ are connected in opposition and supply a $7\,\Omega$ resistor. What is the magnitude of the current?

- $0{,}5\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $1\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The opposing electromotive forces subtract. The resulting voltage is $7\,\mathrm V$, so $I=\dfrac{7}{7}$. The result is $1\,\mathrm A$.
