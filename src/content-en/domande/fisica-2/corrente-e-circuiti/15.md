Currents of $4$ and $3\,\mathrm A$ enter a node. One outgoing branch carries $2\,\mathrm A$. What current leaves through the other branch?

- $1\,\mathrm A$
- $7\,\mathrm A$
- $5\,\mathrm A$
- $9\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

In steady state, the sum of the incoming currents equals the sum of the outgoing currents. Therefore $I=4+3-2$. The result is $5\,\mathrm A$.
