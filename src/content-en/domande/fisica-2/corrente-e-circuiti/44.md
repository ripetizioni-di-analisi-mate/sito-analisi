A resistor dissipates energy through Joule heating. What is the sign of the dissipated power?

- Always negative
- It depends on the chosen sign of the current
- It is zero for any direct current
- Nonnegative

## Explanation

For $R>0$, $P=I^2R$. The square of the current is nonnegative. The dissipation therefore remains nonnegative regardless of the conventional direction.
