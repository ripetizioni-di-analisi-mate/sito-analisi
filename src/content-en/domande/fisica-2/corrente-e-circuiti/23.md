An initially uncharged capacitor in an RC circuit charges toward $10\,\mathrm V$. What is its voltage at $t=\tau\ln2$?

- $2{,}5\,\mathrm V$
- $10\,\mathrm V$
- $5\,\mathrm V$
- $20\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The charging law is $V_C=\mathcal E(1-e^{-\dfrac{t}{\tau}})$. At the given instant, the exponential is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result is $5\,\mathrm V$.
