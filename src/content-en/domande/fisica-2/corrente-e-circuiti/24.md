A capacitor initially at $12\,\mathrm V$ discharges through a resistor. What is the voltage after $t=\tau\ln3$?

- $2\,\mathrm V$
- $8\,\mathrm V$
- $16\,\mathrm V$
- $4\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

During discharge, $V_C=V_0e^{-\dfrac{t}{\tau}}$. At the given instant, the exponential factor is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The result is $4\,\mathrm V$.
