A particle with ratio $\dfrac{|q|}{m}=50\,\mathrm{C/kg}$ moves perpendicular to $B=0{,}20\,\mathrm T$ with $v=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the centripetal acceleration?

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $120\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is centripetal and equals $|q|vB$. Dividing by the mass gives $a=(\dfrac{|q|}{m})vB$. The result is $30\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
