In a uniform magnetic field, the perpendicular velocity of a particle doubles. With mass and charge fixed, what is the ratio of the final period to the initial period in the nonrelativistic regime?

- $1$
- $0{,}5$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The period is $T=\dfrac{2\pi m}{|q|B}$. It does not depend on speed, so it remains unchanged. The result is $1$.
