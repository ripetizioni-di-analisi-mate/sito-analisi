A straight current-carrying wire is immersed in a uniform perpendicular magnetic field. The immersed segment has length $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$. Given the current $I_{e} = 5\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnitude of the force on the wire $F = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the magnetic field magnitude $B$?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{3}{25}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The cross product of the directed segment and the field has magnitude $LB$ because the vectors are perpendicular. It is multiplied by the current. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $B = \dfrac{5 \cdot F}{2 \cdot I_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$.
