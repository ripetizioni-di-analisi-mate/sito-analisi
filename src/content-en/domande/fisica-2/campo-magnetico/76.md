A particle follows a helix in a uniform magnetic field. The displacement parallel to the field in one revolution is measured. Given the velocity component parallel to the field $v_{z} = 30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the period of rotation $T = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the pitch of the helix $d$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is perpendicular to the field. The parallel velocity component remains constant throughout the revolution. In the indicated units, $d = T \cdot v_{z}$. Using the given values, we obtain $6\,\mathrm{m}$.
