A charge of $2\,\mu\mathrm C$ travels at $3000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a magnetic field $B=0{,}50\,\mathrm T$. What is the magnitude of the magnetic force?

- $0{,}003\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}0015\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}006\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}012\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the Lorentz force is $F=|q|vB\sin\theta$. Here $\theta=90^\circ$, so the sine is one. The result is $0{,}003\,\mathrm N$.
