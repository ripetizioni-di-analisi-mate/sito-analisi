Current flows through a rectangular sample with a single type of carrier. The magnetic field is perpendicular to the current. The concentration, current and thickness along the field do not change. Given the initial magnitude of the Hall voltage $U_{0} = 10\,\mathrm{mV}$ and the ratio of the new field magnitude to the old one $b = 3$, what is the final magnitude of the Hall voltage $U_{e}$?

- $30\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $90\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{mV}$

## Explanation

At fixed current, the magnitude of the Hall voltage is proportional to $B$. Indeed, $\lvert U_H\rvert=I B/(n\lvert q\rvert s)$, where $s$ is the thickness along the field. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = U_{0} \cdot b$. Using the given values, we obtain $30\,\mathrm{mV}$.
