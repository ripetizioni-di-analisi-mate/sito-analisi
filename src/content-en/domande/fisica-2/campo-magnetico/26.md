A particle follows a semicircle of radius $0{,}15\,\mathrm m$ in a uniform field. What is the distance between entry and exit along the diameter?

- $0{,}15\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}3\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}6\,\mathrm m$
- $1{,}2\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Entry and exit are opposite ends of the diameter. Their separation is therefore $d=2r$. The result is $0{,}3\,\mathrm m$.
