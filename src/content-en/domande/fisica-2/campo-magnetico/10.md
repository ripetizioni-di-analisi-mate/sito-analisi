A particle enters a magnetic field with speed $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and parallel component $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the perpendicular component?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The two components are perpendicular. Thus $v_\perp=\sqrt{v^2-v_\parallel^2}$. The result is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
