A charge of $q=-2\,\mathrm C$ has $\vec v=3\hat\imath\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\vec B=4\hat k\,\mathrm T$. What is the component $F_y$?

- $-24\,\mathrm N$
- $12\,\mathrm N$
- $-12\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Since $\hat\imath\times\hat k=-\hat\jmath$, the product $\vec v\times\vec B$ points along $-y$. The negative charge reverses this direction. The result is $24\,\mathrm N$.
