A charged particle moves parallel to a nonzero uniform magnetic field. What is the magnitude of the magnetic force?

- $1\,\mathrm N$
- $2\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The force is proportional to the sine of the angle between velocity and field. For parallel vectors, this sine is zero. The result is $0\,\mathrm N$.
