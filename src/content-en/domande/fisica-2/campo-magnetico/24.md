In a material with a single type of carrier, $n|q|=2\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{C/m^3}$. What is the magnitude of the Hall coefficient?

- $2{,}5\cdot 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m^3/C}$
- $1\cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3/C}$
- $2\cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3/C}$
- $5\cdot 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m^3/C}$

## Explanation

In the single-carrier model, $R_H=\dfrac{1}{nq}$. Its magnitude is therefore $\dfrac{1}{n|q|}$. The result is $5\cdot 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m^3/C}$.
