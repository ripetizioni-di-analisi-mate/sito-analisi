A positive charge enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. All other forces are neglected. Given the charge $q = 2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, the speed $v = 300\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$, what is the magnitude of the magnetic force $F$?

- $450\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $150\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the magnetic force is $qvB\sin\theta$. The velocity is perpendicular to the field, so the sine is one. In the indicated units, $F = B \cdot q \cdot v$. Using the given values, we obtain $300\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
