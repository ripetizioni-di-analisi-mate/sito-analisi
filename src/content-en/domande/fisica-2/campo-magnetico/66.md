A planar coil contains $20$ identical turns, each of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The normal vector is perpendicular to the uniform field. Given the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$, what is the magnitude of the net torque $\tau$?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{100}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic moment has magnitude $NIA$. The torque is $\mu B$ when the magnetic moment and field are perpendicular. In the indicated units, $\tau = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
