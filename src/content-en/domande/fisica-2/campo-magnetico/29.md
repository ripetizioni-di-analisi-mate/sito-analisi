A square loop of side length $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ carries current $4\,\mathrm A$. What is the magnitude of its magnetic moment?

- $0{,}04\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $0{,}02\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $0{,}08\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $0{,}16\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$

## Explanation

For a single loop, $\mu=IA$. The area of the square is the square of its side length. The result is $0{,}04\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$.
