A charged particle enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. The ratio of charge magnitude to mass is $200\,\mathrm{C/kg}$. Initially, the given quantities are the speed $v = 80\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$. In the second configuration, we change the speed to $120\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnetic field magnitude to $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $r$?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is centripetal. Equating $\lvert q\rvert vB$ to $mv^2/r$ gives the radius. Applying $r = \dfrac{v}{200 \cdot B}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $0\,\mathrm{m}$.
