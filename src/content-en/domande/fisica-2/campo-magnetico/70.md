A planar coil contains $20$ identical turns, each of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The normal vector is perpendicular to the uniform field. Initially, the given quantities are the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$. In the second configuration, we change the current to $5\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnetic field magnitude to $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $\tau$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic moment has magnitude $NIA$. The torque is $\mu B$ when the magnetic moment and field are perpendicular. Applying $\tau = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$ gives $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ before the change and $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
