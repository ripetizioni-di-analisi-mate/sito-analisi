What is the magnetic moment of a coil with $50$ turns, area $0{,}002\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and current $3\,\mathrm A$?

- $0{,}15\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $0{,}6\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $1{,}2\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $0{,}3\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the magnetic moment is $\mu=NIA$. The areas of the turns are taken to be equal. The result is $0{,}3\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$.
