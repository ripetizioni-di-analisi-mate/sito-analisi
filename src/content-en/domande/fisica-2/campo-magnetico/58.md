In a velocity selector, the electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular. The beam enters perpendicular to both, so the two forces are opposite. Given the electric field magnitude $E = 120\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the speed of the particles that pass through without deflection $v = 400\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the magnetic field magnitude $B$?

- $\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The path remains straight when the magnitudes $\lvert q\rvert E$ and $\lvert q\rvert vB$ are equal. The charge cancels. In the indicated units, $v = \dfrac{E}{B}$. Solving for the unknown gives $B = \dfrac{E}{v}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$.
