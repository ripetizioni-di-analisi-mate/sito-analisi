In a plate, the drift speed is $0{,}020\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the transverse field is $B=0{,}40\,\mathrm T$. What is the magnitude of the Hall field?

- $0{,}004\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $0{,}008\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $0{,}016\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $0{,}032\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

In transverse equilibrium, the electric force balances the magnetic force. Thus $E_H=v_dB$. The result is $0{,}008\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
