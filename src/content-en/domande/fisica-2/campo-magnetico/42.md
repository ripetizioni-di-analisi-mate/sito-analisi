A charge follows a circular path in a uniform field. If only the field is reversed, what changes?

- The speed doubles
- The direction of curvature
- The radius doubles
- The mass changes sign

## Explanation

Reversing $\vec B$ reverses the magnetic force. The field magnitude remains unchanged. The radius and speed remain the same, while the direction of curvature reverses.
