In a velocity selector, how does the selected speed depend on the magnitude of the charge?

- It is proportional to the charge magnitude
- It is inversely proportional to the square of the charge
- It is zero for positive charges
- It is independent of the charge magnitude

## Explanation

The equilibrium condition is $q\vec E+q\vec v\times\vec B=0$. For $q\ne0$, the charge cancels. The remaining geometric relation gives $v=\dfrac{E}{B}$.
