In a velocity selector, the electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular. The beam enters perpendicular to both, so the two forces are opposite. Given the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the speed of the particles that pass through without deflection $v = 400\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the electric field magnitude $E$?

- $240\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $\dfrac{4000}{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The path remains straight when the magnitudes $\lvert q\rvert E$ and $\lvert q\rvert vB$ are equal. The charge cancels. In the indicated units, $v = \dfrac{E}{B}$. Solving for the unknown gives $E = B \cdot v$. Using the given values, we obtain $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
