A wire of length $0{,}8\,\mathrm m$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the uniform magnetic field. Current flows through the wire. Given the current $I_{e} = 4\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$, what is the magnitude of the force on the wire $F$?

- $\dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{4 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $ILB\sin30^\circ$. Only the part of the wire perpendicular to the field contributes. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$.
