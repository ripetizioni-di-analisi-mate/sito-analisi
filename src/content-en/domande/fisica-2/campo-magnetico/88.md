A uniform magnetic field causes a particle with ratio $\lvert q\rvert/m=120\,\mathrm{C/kg}$ to rotate. The period is measured starting with an initial field of magnitude $B$, and the field magnitude is then multiplied by $b$. Given the initial field magnitude $B = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the factor by which the field increases $b = 3$, what is the final period of rotation $T$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{36}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{24}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{72}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\lvert q\rvert B_{\mathrm f}/m$. With $B_{\mathrm f}=bB$, the period is found by dividing $2\pi$ by the angular frequency. In the indicated units, $T = \dfrac{\pi}{60 \cdot B \cdot b}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{\pi}{72}\,\mathrm{s}$.
