Which orientation of a magnetic moment in a uniform field is stable?

- Parallel to and pointing opposite to the field
- Parallel to and pointing in the same direction as the field
- Perpendicular to the field
- Any orientation

## Explanation

The energy is $U=-\mu B\cos\theta$. Its minimum occurs for $\theta=0$. Small rotations therefore produce a restoring torque.
