A velocity selector uses crossed fields $E=300\,\mathrm{V/m}$ and $B=0{,}020\,\mathrm T$. What speed passes through without deflection?

- $7500\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}5\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For straight-line passage, the two forces must balance. Thus $|q|E=|q|vB$ and $v=\dfrac{E}{B}$. The result is $1{,}5\cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
