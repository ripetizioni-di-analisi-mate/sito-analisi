A planar coil of $N = 50$ turns, each of area $6.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, carries the current $I = 0.40\,\mathrm{A}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.25\,\mathrm{T}$. The plane of the coil makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the direction of the field. What is the magnitude of the torque on the coil?

- $1.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $3.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $2.6 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $5.2 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The angle $\theta$ in the formula $\tau = \mu B \sin\theta$ is the angle between the normal $\hat{n}$ and the field. The normal is perpendicular to the plane of the coil, so $\theta = 90^\circ - 30^\circ = 60^\circ$.

The magnetic moment is $\mu = N I A = 50 \cdot 0.40 \cdot 6.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2} = 1.2 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$. Therefore
$$
\tau = \mu B \sin 60^\circ = 1.2 \cdot 10^{-2} \cdot 0.25 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} \approx 2.6 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The value $1.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is obtained by using the $30^\circ$ angle between the plane and the field instead of $\theta$.
