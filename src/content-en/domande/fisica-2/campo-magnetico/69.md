A planar coil contains $20$ identical turns, each of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The normal vector is perpendicular to the uniform field. Initially, the given quantities are the current $I_{e} = 3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$. In the second configuration, we change the magnetic field magnitude to $1\,\mathrm{T}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $\tau$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The magnetic moment has magnitude $NIA$. The torque is $\mu B$ when the magnetic moment and field are perpendicular. Applying $\tau = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$ gives $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ before the change and $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ afterward. The ratio is $2$.
