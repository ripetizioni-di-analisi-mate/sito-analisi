A planar coil contains $20$ identical turns, each of area $0{,}02\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The normal vector is perpendicular to the uniform field. Given the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the magnitude of the net torque $\tau = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, what is the current $I_{e}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $6\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $60\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The magnetic moment has magnitude $NIA$. The torque is $\mu B$ when the magnetic moment and field are perpendicular. In the indicated units, $\tau = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $I_{e} = \dfrac{5 \cdot \tau}{2 \cdot B}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3\,\mathrm{A}$.
