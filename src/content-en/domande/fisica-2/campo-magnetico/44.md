What is the direction of the magnetic force on a moving charge when the force is nonzero?

- Parallel to the velocity
- Parallel to the field
- Always directed toward the source of the field
- Perpendicular to both the velocity and the field

## Explanation

The force contains the cross product $\vec v\times\vec B$. This product is perpendicular to both factors. The sign of the charge can only reverse its direction.
