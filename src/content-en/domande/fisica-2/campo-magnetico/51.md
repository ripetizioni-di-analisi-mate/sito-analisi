A charged particle enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. The ratio of charge magnitude to mass is $200\,\mathrm{C/kg}$. Given the speed $v = 80\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$, what is the orbital radius $r$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{2}{25}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is centripetal. Equating $\lvert q\rvert vB$ to $mv^2/r$ gives the radius. In the indicated units, $r = \dfrac{v}{200 \cdot B}$. Using the given values, we obtain $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
