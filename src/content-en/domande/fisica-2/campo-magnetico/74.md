A loop can rotate in a uniform magnetic field. Its magnetic moment changes from parallel to the field to perpendicular, while its magnitude remains constant. Initially, the given quantities are the magnetic moment magnitude $b = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$. In the second configuration, we change the magnetic field magnitude to $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $U$?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{16}{5}$

## Explanation

The energy is $-\vec\mu\cdot\vec B$. Initially, it is $-\mu B$, and when the vectors are perpendicular, it is zero. Applying $U = B \cdot b$ gives $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $\dfrac{12}{25}\,\mathrm{J}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{8}{5}$.
