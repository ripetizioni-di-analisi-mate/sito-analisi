A charged-particle beam follows an exactly straight path through a uniform magnetic field. After the beam direction is rotated by $90^\circ$ at unchanged speed, it curves. In the absence of other forces, what can be inferred about the initial motion?

- The velocity was perpendicular to the field.
- The velocity was parallel or antiparallel to the field.
- Each particle was uncharged.
- The field was necessarily vertical.

## Explanation

Curvature in the second trial shows that $q$ and $B$ are nonzero. Zero initial magnetic force therefore requires $\sin\theta=0$, which means parallel or antiparallel directions.
