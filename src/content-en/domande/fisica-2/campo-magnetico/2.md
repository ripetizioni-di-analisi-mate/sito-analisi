A charge of $4\,\mu\mathrm C$ moves at $1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at an angle of $30^\circ$ to a magnetic field $B=0{,}20\,\mathrm T$. What is the magnetic force?

- $0{,}0002\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}0004\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}0008\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}0016\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

We have $F=|q|vB\sin\theta$. For $30^\circ$, the angular factor is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result is $0{,}0004\,\mathrm N$.
