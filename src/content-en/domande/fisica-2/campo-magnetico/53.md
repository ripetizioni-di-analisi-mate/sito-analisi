In a region there are an electric field $\vec{E} = 2.0 \cdot 10^4\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{V/m}$ and a magnetic field $\vec{B} = 0.10\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{T}$. A proton passes through it with velocity $\vec{v} = 1.0 \cdot 10^5\,\hat{\imath}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the total force on the proton at that instant?

- $1.6 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.8 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-1.6 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.2 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The total force is $\vec{F} = e\,(\vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B})$.

Since $\hat{\imath} \times \hat{k} = -\hat{\jmath}$, the magnetic term is $\vec{v} \times \vec{B} = 1.0 \cdot 10^5 \cdot 0.10\,(-\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{V/m} = -1.0 \cdot 10^4\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{V/m}$. Hence
$$
\vec{F} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19} \left( 2.0 \cdot 10^4 - 1.0 \cdot 10^4 \right) \hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N} = 1.6 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The two forces point in opposite directions, but the speed is less than $\dfrac{E}{B} = 2.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, therefore the electric force prevails.
