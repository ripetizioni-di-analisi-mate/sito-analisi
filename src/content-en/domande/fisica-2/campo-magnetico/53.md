A charged particle enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. The ratio of charge magnitude to mass is $200\,\mathrm{C/kg}$. Given the speed $v = 80\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the orbital radius $r = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the magnetic field magnitude $B$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $5\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is centripetal. Equating $\lvert q\rvert vB$ to $mv^2/r$ gives the radius. In the indicated units, $r = \dfrac{v}{200 \cdot B}$. Solving for the unknown gives $B = \dfrac{v}{200 \cdot r}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$.
