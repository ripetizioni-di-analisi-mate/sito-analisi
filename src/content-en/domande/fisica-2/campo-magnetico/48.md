A positive charge enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. All other forces are neglected. Given the charge $q = 2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the magnitude of the magnetic force $F = 300\,\mathrm{\mu N}$, what is the speed $v$?

- $600\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $150\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $450\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the magnetic force is $qvB\sin\theta$. The velocity is perpendicular to the field, so the sine is one. In the indicated units, $F = B \cdot q \cdot v$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{F}{B \cdot q}$. Using the given values, we obtain $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
