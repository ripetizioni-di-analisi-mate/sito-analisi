A bar magnet has its north pole on the right and its south pole on the left. What is the direction of the magnetic field at the points inside the magnet, on its axis?

- From the south pole to the north pole, that is, to the right
- From the north pole to the south pole, that is, to the left
- The field inside the magnet is zero
- Perpendicular to the axis of the magnet

## Explanation

Outside the magnet, the magnetic field lines leave the north pole and enter the south pole.

Magnetic field lines are **closed**, because there are no isolated magnetic charges on which they could end. Therefore a line that has entered the south pole continues inside the magnet up to the north pole, from which it leaves again. Hence inside, on the axis, the field points from the south pole to the north pole, that is, to the right.
