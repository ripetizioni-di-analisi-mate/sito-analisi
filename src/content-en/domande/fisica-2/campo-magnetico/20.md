Two ions with equal charge and speed enter the same field perpendicularly. The mass of the second is three times as large. What is the ratio of the radii $\dfrac{r_2}{r_1}$?

- $1{,}5$
- $6$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

The radius is $r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}$. At fixed charge, speed and field, the radius is proportional to the mass. The result is $3$.
