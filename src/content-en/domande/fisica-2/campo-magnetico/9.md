A particle follows a helical path with period $0{,}020\,\mathrm s$ and velocity parallel to the field $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the pitch of the helix?

- $0{,}06\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}03\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}12\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}24\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The parallel velocity component remains constant. The pitch is the distance traveled along the field in one period, $h=v_{\parallel}T$. The result is $0{,}06\,\mathrm m$.
