A positive charge has velocity along $+x$ in a field along $+z$. What is the direction of the magnetic force?

- Toward $+y$
- Toward $+x$
- Toward $-y$
- Toward $+z$

## Explanation

The direction is obtained from the cross product. We have $\hat\imath\times\hat k=-\hat\jmath$. For a positive charge, the direction remains that of the product.
