In the Bohr model an electron travels a circular orbit in a horizontal plane, counterclockwise as seen from above. What is the direction of the magnetic moment associated with the orbit?

- Vertical, downward
- Vertical, upward
- Horizontal, toward the center of the orbit
- The magnetic moment is zero, because the average current is zero

## Explanation

The motion of the electron is equivalent to a loop carrying a current of magnitude $\dfrac{e}{T}$, where $T$ is the period of the orbit.

The charge of the electron is negative, so the current flows opposite to the motion, that is, clockwise as seen from above. By the right-hand rule, with the fingers curled clockwise as seen from above the thumb points downward. Therefore the magnetic moment is **vertical, downward**, opposite to the orbital angular momentum, which instead points upward.
