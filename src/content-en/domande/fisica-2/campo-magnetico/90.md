A uniform magnetic field causes a particle with ratio $\lvert q\rvert/m=120\,\mathrm{C/kg}$ to rotate. The period is measured starting with an initial field of magnitude $B$, and the field magnitude is then multiplied by $b$. Given the initial field magnitude $B = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the final period of rotation $T = \dfrac{\pi}{72}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the factor by which the field increases $b$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\lvert q\rvert B_{\mathrm f}/m$. With $B_{\mathrm f}=bB$, the period is found by dividing $2\pi$ by the angular frequency. In the indicated units, $T = \dfrac{\pi}{60 \cdot B \cdot b}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{\pi}{60 \cdot B \cdot T}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3$.
