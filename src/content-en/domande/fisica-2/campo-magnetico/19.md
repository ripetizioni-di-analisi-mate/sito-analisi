In a spectrometer, a particle with $|q|=2\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$ and velocity $10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ follows a path of radius $0{,}10\,\mathrm m$ in $B=0{,}50\,\mathrm T$. What is its mass?

- $5\cdot 10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\cdot 10^{-25}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1\cdot 10^{-25}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\cdot 10^{-25}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The circular motion satisfies $r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}$. We therefore obtain $m=\dfrac{|q|Br}{v}$. The result is $1\cdot 10^{-25}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
