An electron enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly and remains in the field region. Only the magnetic force acts. How do its speed and direction of motion change?

- The speed increases and the direction remains constant
- The speed remains constant and the direction changes
- The speed decreases and the direction changes
- The speed and direction remain constant

## Explanation

The Lorentz force is perpendicular to the velocity, so it does no work. In this case, it is nonzero and acts as a centripetal force.
