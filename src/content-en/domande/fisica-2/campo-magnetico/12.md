A selector must allow particles at $2\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to pass through with $B=0{,}010\,\mathrm T$. What electric field is required?

- $1000\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The condition for transverse equilibrium is $E=vB$. The fields must be oriented to produce opposite forces. The result is $2000\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
