A particle has kinetic energy $20\,\mathrm J$ and experiences only a magnetic force. What is its kinetic energy after following a curved path?

- $10\,\mathrm J$
- $40\,\mathrm J$
- $20\,\mathrm J$
- $80\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is perpendicular to the velocity. It does no work, so kinetic energy is conserved. The result is $20\,\mathrm J$.
