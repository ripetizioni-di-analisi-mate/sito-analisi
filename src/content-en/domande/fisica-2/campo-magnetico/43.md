In a mass spectrometer, two ions have the same charge and kinetic energy. How does the radius vary with mass?

- It is proportional to the square of the mass
- It is independent of the mass
- It is proportional to the square root of the mass
- It is inversely proportional to the mass

## Explanation

The nonrelativistic momentum is $p=\sqrt{2mK}$. The radius is $r=\dfrac{p}{|q|B}$. At fixed energy and charge, it is therefore proportional to $\sqrt m$.
