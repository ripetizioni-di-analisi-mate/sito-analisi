In the velocity selector in the figure the upper plate is positive, the lower one negative, and the magnetic field is into the page. An electron enters from the left, parallel to the plates, with speed greater than $\dfrac{E}{B}$. The weight is negligible. Where is the electron deflected?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,1.2 6,1.2;0,-1.2 6,-1.2" vettori="-1.4,0,-0.2,0,v" punti="-1.4,0,pieno" etichette="-1.75,-0.45,e⁻;0.6,1.45,+;1.8,1.45,+;3,1.45,+;4.2,1.45,+;5.4,1.45,+;0.6,-1.75,−;1.8,-1.75,−;3,-1.75,−;4.2,-1.75,−;5.4,-1.75,−;0.9,0.35,⊗;0.9,-0.75,⊗;2.4,0.35,⊗;2.4,-0.75,⊗;3.9,0.35,⊗;3.9,-0.75,⊗;5.4,0.35,⊗;5.4,-0.75,⊗" xmin=-2.1 xmax=6.6 ymin=-2.1 ymax=1.9 titolo="Velocity selector: upper plate positive, lower plate negative, magnetic field into the page"}

- Toward the positive plate
- It continues in a straight line
- Out of the plane of the page, opposite to the field
- Toward the negative plate

## Explanation

The electric field points from the positive plate to the negative one, so it points downward, and the electric force on the electron, whose charge is negative, points upward.

With $\hat{\imath}$ to the right and $\vec{B} = -B\,\hat{k}$, one has $\vec{v} \times \vec{B} = -v B\,(\hat{\imath} \times \hat{k}) = v B\,\hat{\jmath}$; the charge is negative, therefore the magnetic force points downward. The two forces are opposite, as the selector requires.

The speed is greater than $\dfrac{E}{B}$, so $e\,v B > e\,E$ and the magnetic force prevails. The electron is therefore deflected downward, that is, **toward the negative plate**.
