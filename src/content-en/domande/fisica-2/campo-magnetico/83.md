A rigid square loop of side $a = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$ lies in the $xy$ plane and is hinged along the side lying on the $y$ axis, like a door. It carries the current $I = 1.5\,\mathrm{A}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.50\,\mathrm{T}$ directed along the $x$ axis. What is the magnitude of the torque of the magnetic forces about the hinge axis?

- $0.015\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0.060\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $7.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0.030\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The two sides parallel to the $x$ axis are parallel to the field, so by the **force on a straight wire** they experience no force.

The side on the hinge and the opposite side are perpendicular to the field and experience forces parallel to the $z$ axis, of magnitude $I a B$. The side on the hinge has zero lever arm, while the opposite side has lever arm $a$. Therefore the torque about the hinge is
$$
\tau = I a B \cdot a = 1.5 \cdot 0.20^2 \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 0.030\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The result coincides with $I A B$, because the total force on the loop is zero and the torque does not depend on the pivot.
