A positive charge enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. All other forces are neglected. Initially, the given quantities are the charge $q = 2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, the speed $v = 300\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$. In the second configuration, we change the magnetic field magnitude to $1\,\mathrm{T}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $F$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the magnetic force is $qvB\sin\theta$. The velocity is perpendicular to the field, so the sine is one. Applying $F = B \cdot q \cdot v$ gives $300\,\mathrm{\mu N}$ before the change and $600\,\mathrm{\mu N}$ afterward. The ratio is $2$.
