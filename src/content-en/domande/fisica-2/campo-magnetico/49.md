Which combination of base SI units (kilogram, meter, second, ampere) is equivalent to the tesla?

- $\mathrm{kg \cdot m/(A \cdot s^2)}$
- $\mathrm{A \cdot s^2/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg/(A \cdot s^3)}$
- $\mathrm{kg/(A \cdot s^2)}$

## Explanation

By the **Lorentz force** law, for a charge moving perpendicular to the field $F = |q|\,v B$, so $B = \dfrac{F}{|q|\,v}$ and $1\,\mathrm{T} = 1\,\mathrm{N \cdot s/(C \cdot m)}$.

Since $1\,\mathrm{C} = 1\,\mathrm{A \cdot s}$, the product $\mathrm{C \cdot m}$ becomes $\mathrm{A \cdot s \cdot m}$ and $1\,\mathrm{T} = 1\,\mathrm{N/(A \cdot m)}$.

The newton equals $1\,\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s^2}$, therefore
$$
1\,\mathrm{T} = 1\,\frac{\mathrm{kg \cdot m}}{\mathrm{s^2 \cdot A \cdot m}} = 1\,\mathrm{kg/(A \cdot s^2)} .
$$
The combination $\mathrm{kg \cdot m/(A \cdot s^2)}$ is the newton divided by the ampere, without the meter in the denominator.
