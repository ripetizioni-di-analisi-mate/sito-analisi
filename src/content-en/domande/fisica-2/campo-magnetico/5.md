A particle with $\dfrac{m}{|q|}=0{,}020\,\mathrm{kg/C}$ enters a magnetic field $B=0{,}50\,\mathrm T$ perpendicularly with velocity $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the radius?

- $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm m$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm m$
- $1{,}6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The magnetic force supplies the centripetal force. From $|q|vB=\dfrac{mv^2}{r}$, we obtain $r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}$. The result is $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$.
