In a velocity selector, the electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular. The beam enters perpendicular to both, so the two forces are opposite. Given the electric field magnitude $E = 120\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$, what is the speed of the particles that pass through without deflection $v$?

- $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $800\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The path remains straight when the magnitudes $\lvert q\rvert E$ and $\lvert q\rvert vB$ are equal. The charge cancels. In the indicated units, $v = \dfrac{E}{B}$. Using the given values, we obtain $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
