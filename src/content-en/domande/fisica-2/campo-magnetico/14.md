A wire carries $2\,\mathrm A$ in $B=0{,}50\,\mathrm T$ at an angle of $30^\circ$ to the field. The immersed length is $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$. What is the force?

- $0{,}05\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}1\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}2\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The force on a wire is $F=ILB\sin\theta$. The given angle introduces a factor of $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The result is $0{,}1\,\mathrm N$.
