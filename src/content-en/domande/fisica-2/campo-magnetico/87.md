A wire of length $0{,}8\,\mathrm m$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the uniform magnetic field. Current flows through the wire. Given the current $I_{e} = 4\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnitude of the force on the wire $F = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the magnetic field magnitude $B$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $1\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $ILB\sin30^\circ$. Only the part of the wire perpendicular to the field contributes. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $B = \dfrac{5 \cdot F}{2 \cdot I_{e}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$.
