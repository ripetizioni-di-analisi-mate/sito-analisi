A particle has cyclotron angular frequency $\omega_c=20\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is its frequency?

- $5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $20\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $40\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Frequency is the number of revolutions per second. Thus $f=\dfrac{\omega_c}{2\pi}$. The result is $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
