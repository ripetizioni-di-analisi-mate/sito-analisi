A wire of length $0{,}40\,\mathrm m$ carries $5\,\mathrm A$ perpendicular to $B=0{,}30\,\mathrm T$. What is the magnetic force?

- $0{,}6\,\mathrm N$
- $0{,}3\,\mathrm N$
- $1{,}2\,\mathrm N$
- $2{,}4\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

For a straight wire, $F=ILB\sin\theta$. The wire is perpendicular to the field, so the sine is one. The result is $0{,}6\,\mathrm N$.
