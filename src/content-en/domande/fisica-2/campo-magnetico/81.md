Current flows through a rectangular sample with a single type of carrier. The magnetic field is perpendicular to the current. The concentration, current and thickness along the field do not change. Given the initial magnitude of the Hall voltage $U_{0} = 10\,\mathrm{mV}$ and the final magnitude of the Hall voltage $U_{e} = 30\,\mathrm{mV}$, what is the ratio of the new field magnitude to the old one $b$?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$
- $\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

At fixed current, the magnitude of the Hall voltage is proportional to $B$. Indeed, $\lvert U_H\rvert=I B/(n\lvert q\rvert s)$, where $s$ is the thickness along the field. In the indicated units, $U_{e} = U_{0} \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{U_{e}}{U_{0}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $3$.
