A planar loop lies in the $xy$ plane and has magnetic moment $\vec{\mu} = 0.20\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$. The magnetic field is uniform and equals $\vec{B} = \left( 0.30\,\hat{\imath} + 0.40\,\hat{k} \right)\mathrm{T}$. What is the torque of the magnetic forces on the loop?

- $0.060\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-0.060\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0.080\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0.10\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The torque is $\vec{\tau} = \vec{\mu} \times \vec{B}$.

The component of the field along $\hat{k}$ is parallel to $\vec{\mu}$, so it gives no contribution, because $\hat{k} \times \hat{k} = \vec{0}$. The component along $\hat{\imath}$ remains, and since $\hat{k} \times \hat{\imath} = \hat{\jmath}$,
$$
\vec{\tau} = 0.20 \cdot 0.30\,(\hat{k} \times \hat{\imath})\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 0.060\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The value $0.10\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is obtained by using the magnitude $0.50\,\mathrm{T}$ of the whole field, as if it were perpendicular to $\vec{\mu}$.
