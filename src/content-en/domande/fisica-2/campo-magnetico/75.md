A loop can rotate in a uniform magnetic field. Its magnetic moment changes from parallel to the field to perpendicular, while its magnitude remains constant. Initially, the given quantities are the magnetic moment magnitude $b = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$. In the second configuration, we change the magnetic moment magnitude to $1\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$ and the magnetic field magnitude to $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{T}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $U$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{20}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{40}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The energy is $-\vec\mu\cdot\vec B$. Initially, it is $-\mu B$, and when the vectors are perpendicular, it is zero. Applying $U = B \cdot b$ gives $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{J}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $- \dfrac{1}{20}\,\mathrm{J}$.
