A positive charge enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. All other forces are neglected. Given the speed $v = 300\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the magnitude of the magnetic force $F = 300\,\mathrm{\mu N}$, what is the charge $q$?

- $3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $1\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the magnetic force is $qvB\sin\theta$. The velocity is perpendicular to the field, so the sine is one. In the indicated units, $F = B \cdot q \cdot v$. Solving for the unknown gives $q = \dfrac{F}{B \cdot v}$. Using the given values, we obtain $2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
