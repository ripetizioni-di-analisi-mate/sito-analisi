A wire of length $0{,}8\,\mathrm m$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the uniform magnetic field. Current flows through the wire. Given the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the magnitude of the force on the wire $F = \dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the current $I_{e}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $4\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{4 \cdot \sqrt{3}}{3}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $8\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $ILB\sin30^\circ$. Only the part of the wire perpendicular to the field contributes. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $I_{e} = \dfrac{5 \cdot F}{2 \cdot B}$. Using the given values, we obtain $4\,\mathrm{A}$.
