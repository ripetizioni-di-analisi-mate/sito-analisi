A straight current-carrying wire is immersed in a uniform perpendicular magnetic field. The immersed segment has length $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$. Given the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the magnitude of the force on the wire $F = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the current $I_{e}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $10\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The cross product of the directed segment and the field has magnitude $LB$ because the vectors are perpendicular. It is multiplied by the current. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $I_{e} = \dfrac{5 \cdot F}{2 \cdot B}$. Using the given values, we obtain $5\,\mathrm{A}$.
