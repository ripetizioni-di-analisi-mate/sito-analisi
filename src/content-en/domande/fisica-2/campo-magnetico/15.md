A coil of $20$ turns, area $0{,}010\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and current $2\,\mathrm A$ has its normal perpendicular to $B=0{,}30\,\mathrm T$. What is the torque?

- $0{,}06\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0{,}24\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0{,}12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0{,}48\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The torque is $\tau=NIAB\sin\theta$, with $\theta$ measured from the normal. In this case, it is a maximum because $\theta=90^\circ$. The result is $0{,}12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
