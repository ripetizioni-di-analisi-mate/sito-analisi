A charged particle enters a uniform magnetic field with a velocity that makes the angle $\varphi$ with the field, and describes a helix. For what value of $\varphi$ is the pitch of the helix equal to the length of the circle that the particle describes when projected onto the plane perpendicular to the field?

- $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $81^\circ$

## Explanation

For **helical motion** the pitch is $p = \dfrac{2\pi m\, v\cos\varphi}{|q|\,B}$ and the radius is $r = \dfrac{m v\sin\varphi}{|q|\,B}$.

The projected circle has length
$$
2\pi r = \frac{2\pi m\, v\sin\varphi}{|q|\,B} .
$$
Hence $p = 2\pi r$ if and only if $\cos\varphi = \sin\varphi$, that is, $\tan\varphi = 1$ and $\varphi = 45^\circ$. The value $81^\circ$ instead solves $p = r$, that is, $\tan\varphi = 2\pi$.
