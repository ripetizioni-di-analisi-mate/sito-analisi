A straight current-carrying wire is immersed in a uniform perpendicular magnetic field. The immersed segment has length $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$. Given the current $I_{e} = 5\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$, what is the magnitude of the force on the wire $F$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The cross product of the directed segment and the field has magnitude $LB$ because the vectors are perpendicular. It is multiplied by the current. In the indicated units, $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$.
