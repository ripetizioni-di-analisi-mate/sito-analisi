A particle has $\dfrac{|q|}{m}=10^8\,\mathrm{C/kg}$ in a field $B=0{,}20\,\mathrm T$. What is its nonrelativistic cyclotron angular frequency?

- $1\cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4\cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2\cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $8\cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency of the circular motion is $\omega_c=\dfrac{|q|B}{m}$. In the nonrelativistic regime, it is independent of speed. The result is $2\cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
