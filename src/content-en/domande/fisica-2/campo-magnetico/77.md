A particle follows a helix in a uniform magnetic field. The displacement parallel to the field in one revolution is measured. Given the period of rotation $T = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the pitch of the helix $d = 6\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the velocity component parallel to the field $v_{z}$?

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is perpendicular to the field. The parallel velocity component remains constant throughout the revolution. In the indicated units, $d = T \cdot v_{z}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v_{z} = \dfrac{d}{T}$. Using the given values, we obtain $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
