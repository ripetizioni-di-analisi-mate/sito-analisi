A straight current-carrying wire is immersed in a uniform perpendicular magnetic field. The immersed segment has length $0{,}4\,\mathrm m$. Initially, the given quantities are the current $I_{e} = 5\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{T}$. In the second configuration, we change the current to $3\,\mathrm{A}$ and the magnetic field magnitude to $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the final value minus the initial value of $F$?

- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The cross product of the directed segment and the field has magnitude $LB$ because the vectors are perpendicular. It is multiplied by the current. Applying $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot B \cdot I_{e}}{5}$ gives $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$ before the change and $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$ afterward. The final value minus the initial value is $0\,\mathrm{N}$.
