A loop can rotate in a uniform magnetic field. Its magnetic moment changes from parallel to the field to perpendicular, while its magnitude remains constant. Given the magnetic moment magnitude $b = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$, what is the increase in potential energy $U$?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The energy is $-\vec\mu\cdot\vec B$. Initially, it is $-\mu B$, and when the vectors are perpendicular, it is zero. In the indicated units, $U = B \cdot b$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{J}$.
