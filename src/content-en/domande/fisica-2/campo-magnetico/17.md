A loop has magnetic moment $0{,}20\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$ parallel to and pointing in the same direction as $B=0{,}50\,\mathrm T$. What is the energy $U=-\vec\mu\cdot\vec B$?

- $-0{,}1\,\mathrm J$
- $0{,}1\,\mathrm J$
- $-0{,}2\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The formula is $U=-\mu B\cos\theta$. For alignment in the same direction, $\cos\theta=1$, so the energy is negative. The result is $-0{,}1\,\mathrm J$.
