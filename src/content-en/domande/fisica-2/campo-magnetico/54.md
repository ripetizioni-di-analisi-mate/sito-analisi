A charged particle enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. The ratio of charge magnitude to mass is $200\,\mathrm{C/kg}$. Initially, the given quantities are the speed $v = 80\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$. In the second configuration, we change the magnetic field magnitude to $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$, with the other data unchanged. What is the ratio of the final value to the initial value of $r$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is centripetal. Equating $\lvert q\rvert vB$ to $mv^2/r$ gives the radius. Applying $r = \dfrac{v}{200 \cdot B}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $1\,\mathrm{m}$ afterward. The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
