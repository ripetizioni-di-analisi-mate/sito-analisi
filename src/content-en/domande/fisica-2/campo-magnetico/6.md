A particle travels around a circle of radius $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$ in $B=0{,}40\,\mathrm T$. If $\dfrac{|q|}{m}=100\,\mathrm{C/kg}$, what is its speed?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The radius satisfies $r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}$. Thus $v=(\dfrac{|q|}{m})Br$. The result is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
