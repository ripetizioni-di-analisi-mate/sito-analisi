A loop can rotate in a uniform magnetic field. Its magnetic moment changes from parallel to the field to perpendicular, while its magnitude remains constant. Given the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the increase in potential energy $U = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the magnetic moment magnitude $b$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$

## Explanation

The energy is $-\vec\mu\cdot\vec B$. Initially, it is $-\mu B$, and when the vectors are perpendicular, it is zero. In the indicated units, $U = B \cdot b$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{U}{B}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$.
