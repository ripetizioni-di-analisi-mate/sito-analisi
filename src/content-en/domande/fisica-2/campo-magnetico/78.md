A particle follows a helix in a uniform magnetic field. The displacement parallel to the field in one revolution is measured. Given the velocity component parallel to the field $v_{z} = 30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the pitch of the helix $d = 6\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the period of rotation $T$?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is perpendicular to the field. The parallel velocity component remains constant throughout the revolution. In the indicated units, $d = T \cdot v_{z}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{d}{v_{z}}$. Using the given values, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
