A plate of width $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ has a uniform Hall field $0{,}60\,\mathrm{V/m}$. What is the magnitude of the Hall voltage?

- $0{,}0015\,\mathrm V$
- $0{,}006\,\mathrm V$
- $0{,}003\,\mathrm V$
- $0{,}012\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The Hall voltage is the product of the transverse field and the width. Thus $|V_H|=E_Hw$. The result is $0{,}003\,\mathrm V$.
