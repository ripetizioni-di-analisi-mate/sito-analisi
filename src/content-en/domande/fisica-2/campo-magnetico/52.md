A charged particle enters a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly. The ratio of charge magnitude to mass is $200\,\mathrm{C/kg}$. Given the magnetic field magnitude $B = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{T}$ and the orbital radius $r = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the speed $v$?

- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $160\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is centripetal. Equating $\lvert q\rvert vB$ to $mv^2/r$ gives the radius. In the indicated units, $r = \dfrac{v}{200 \cdot B}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = 200 \cdot B \cdot r$. Using the given values, we obtain $80\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
