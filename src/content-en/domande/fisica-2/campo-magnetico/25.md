A wire with current $I=3\,\mathrm A$ and immersed length $0{,}20\,\mathrm m$ experiences a maximum force of $0{,}12\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnetic field?

- $0{,}2\,\mathrm T$
- $0{,}1\,\mathrm T$
- $0{,}4\,\mathrm T$
- $0{,}8\,\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The force is a maximum when the wire is perpendicular to the field. From $F=ILB$, we obtain $B=\dfrac{F}{IL}$. The result is $0{,}2\,\mathrm T$.
