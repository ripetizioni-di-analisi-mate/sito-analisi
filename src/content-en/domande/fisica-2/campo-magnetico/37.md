A current-carrying loop is in a uniform magnetic field. What net force acts on the entire closed loop?

- Zero
- Always equal to $IAB$
- Always parallel to the field
- Always tangent to the loop

## Explanation

The total force is $I\oint d\vec l\times\vec B$. The uniform field can be taken outside the integral. Since $\oint d\vec l=0$, the total force is zero.
