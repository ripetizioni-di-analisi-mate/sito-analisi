What motion does a particle follow when it has nonzero velocity components both parallel and perpendicular to a uniform field?

- Uniformly accelerated rectilinear motion along the field
- Helical motion
- Parabolic motion with constant acceleration
- Circular motion in a plane necessarily perpendicular to the total velocity

## Explanation

The parallel component remains constant. The perpendicular component produces circular motion. Their combination gives a helix.
