What can the sign of the Hall coefficient determine in the single-carrier model?

- The sign of the charge carriers
- The total mass of the plate
- The sign of the gravitational field
- The number of turns in the external coil

## Explanation

In the model, $R_H=\dfrac{1}{nq}$. The number density $n$ is positive. The sign of $R_H$ therefore matches that of the carrier charge.
