A particle increases its kinetic energy fourfold before entering the same magnetic field perpendicularly. What is the ratio of the final radius to the initial radius in the nonrelativistic regime?

- $1$
- $4$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

At fixed mass, $v$ is proportional to $\sqrt K$. The radius is proportional to $v$, so quadrupling $K$ doubles the radius. The result is $2$.
