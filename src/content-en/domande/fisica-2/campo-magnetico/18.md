A magnetic moment with $\mu B=4\,\mathrm{mJ}$ rotates from alignment in the same direction as the field to the opposite direction. By how much does the potential energy increase?

- $4\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $32\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

The energy changes from $-\mu B$ to $+\mu B$. The change is therefore $\Delta U=2\mu B$. The result is $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
