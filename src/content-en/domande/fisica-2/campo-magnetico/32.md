A negative charge has the same velocity as a positive charge in the same field. How do the directions of the magnetic forces compare?

- They are always the same
- They are perpendicular to each other
- They depend only on the mass
- They are opposite

## Explanation

The force is $q\vec v\times\vec B$. Changing the sign of $q$ reverses the force vector. Mass and charge magnitude do not determine its direction.
