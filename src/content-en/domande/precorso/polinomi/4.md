- $6x^2 y^2\,(2x - 3y)$
- $6x^2 y^2\,(2x + 3y + 1)$
- $6xy\,(2x - 3y + 1)$
- $6x^2 y^2\,(2x - 3y + 1)$

## Explanation

The common factor consists of the greatest common divisor of the coefficients and of every letter common to all the terms, taken with the *smallest* exponent. The greatest common divisor of $12$, $18$ and $6$ is $6$, and both $x$ and $y$ appear in all the terms with smallest exponent $2$, so we factor out $6x^2 y^2$. Dividing each term by $6x^2 y^2$ gives $2x$, $-3y$ and $1$, therefore
$$
12x^3 y^2 - 18x^2 y^3 + 6x^2 y^2 = 6x^2 y^2\,(2x - 3y + 1) .
$$
The term $1$ comes from $6x^2 y^2$, which divided by itself gives $1$.
