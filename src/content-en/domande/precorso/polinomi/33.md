What are the quotient and the remainder of the division of $x^4 - 3x^2 + 5$ by $x - 2$?

- $Q(x) = x^3 + 2x^2 + x + 2$ and $R = 9$
- $Q(x) = x - 1$ and $R = 3$
- $Q(x) = x^3 - 2x^2 + x - 2$ and $R = 9$
- $Q(x) = x^2 + 2x + 1$ and $R = 7$

## Explanation

In **Ruffini's rule** the missing degrees $x^3$ and $x$ are written with coefficient $0$, so the first row is $1, 0, -3, 0, 5$ and we use $c = 2$.
$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc|c}
 & 1 & 0 & -3 & 0 & 5 \\
2 &  & 2 & 4 & 2 & 4 \\ \hline
 & 1 & 2 & 1 & 2 & 9
\end{array}
$$
The last number is the remainder, and the numbers before it are the coefficients of the quotient, which has degree $4 - 1 = 3$. Therefore $Q(x) = x^3 + 2x^2 + x + 2$ and $R = 9$, in agreement with the **remainder theorem**, since $P(2) = 16 - 12 + 5 = 9$.
