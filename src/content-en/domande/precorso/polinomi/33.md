In division by $x-2$, a zero is mistakenly entered for the linear coefficient of the dividend. The calculated remainder is 7 and the correct one is 13. What coefficient was omitted?

- $3$
- $6$
- $12$
- $-3$, using $-2$ as the root of the divisor.

## Explanation

If the missing term is $ax$, the remainder theorem gives a difference of $2a$ between the remainders. Hence $2a=13-7=6$ and $a=3$.
