What is $1003^2 - 997^2$?

- $12000$
- $36$
- $6000$
- $2000$

## Explanation

By the **difference of squares** $A^2 - B^2 = (A - B)(A + B)$ with $A = 1003$ and $B = 997$ we have $1003^2 - 997^2 = (1003 - 997)(1003 + 997)$. The two factors are $6$ and $2000$, therefore the difference equals $6 \cdot 2000 = 12000$. The number $36 = 6^2$ would be obtained by writing the difference of the squares as the square of the difference.
