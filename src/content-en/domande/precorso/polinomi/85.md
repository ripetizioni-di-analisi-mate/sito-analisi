The polynomial $x^4 - 2x^2 + 1$ is written as a product of linear factors. Which one?

- $\left( x^2 - 1 \right)^2$
- $(x - 1)^4$
- $(x - 1)^2 (x + 1)^2$
- $(x - 1)(x + 1)\left( x^2 + 1 \right)$

## Explanation

The terms $x^4$ and $1$ are the squares of $x^2$ and $1$, and $-2x^2$ is twice the product $-2 \cdot x^2 \cdot 1$. Therefore $x^4 - 2x^2 + 1 = \left( x^2 - 1 \right)^2$ is a **square of a binomial**. The factor $x^2 - 1$ is a **difference of squares** and equals $(x - 1)(x + 1)$, so $\left( x^2 - 1 \right)^2 = (x - 1)^2 (x + 1)^2$. The roots $1$ and $-1$ are both double.
