What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in $- \left(x - 5\right)^{3} + \left(x + 5\right)^{3}$?

- $15$
- $10$
- $30$
- $0$

## Explanation

The cube $\left(x + 5\right)^{3}$ contains the term $15 x^{2}$, while $\left(x - 5\right)^{3}$ contains $- 15 x^{2}$. Since $\left(x - 5\right)^{3}$ has a minus sign in front, both terms contribute $+15 x^{2}$, so the coefficient is $30$.
