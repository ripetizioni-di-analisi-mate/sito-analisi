For $x \neq 4$, the fraction $\dfrac{x^3 - 64}{x - 4}$ coincides with a polynomial. Which one?

- $x^2 - 4x + 16$
- $x^2 + 8x + 16$
- $x^2 + 16$
- $x^2 + 4x + 16$

## Explanation

Since $64 = 4^3$, the numerator is the **difference of cubes** $A^3 - B^3$ with $A = x$ and $B = 4$. The formula $A^3 - B^3 = (A - B)\left( A^2 + AB + B^2 \right)$ gives $x^3 - 64 = (x - 4)\left( x^2 + 4x + 16 \right)$. For $x \neq 4$ the factor $x - 4$ is different from $0$ and cancels, therefore the fraction is equal to $x^2 + 4x + 16$. The second factor is the **quadratic that is not a perfect square**, which contains the product $AB = 4x$ in place of twice the product, $8x$.
