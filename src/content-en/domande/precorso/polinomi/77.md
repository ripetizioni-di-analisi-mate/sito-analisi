The remainder of the division of a polynomial $P(x)$ by $x - 3$ is $4$. What is the remainder of the division of $P(x) + x^2$ by $x - 3$?

- $4$
- $7$
- $13$
- $-5$

## Explanation

By the **remainder theorem** the remainder of the division of $P(x)$ by $x - 3$ is $P(3)$, so $P(3) = 4$. By the same theorem, the remainder of the division of $S(x) = P(x) + x^2$ by $x - 3$ is $S(3) = P(3) + 3^2$. Therefore the remainder is $4 + 9 = 13$.
