Consider the polynomials $P(x) = (m - 1)x^2 + 2x + m$, where $m$ is a real parameter, and $Q(x) = 3x^2 + 2x + 5$. For which value of $m$ is $P = Q$?

- $m = 4$
- $m = 5$
- $m = 4$ or $m = 5$
- No value of $m$

## Explanation

By the **identity principle** $P$ and $Q$ are equal if and only if they have the same coefficients, term by term. The coefficients of $x$ are both $2$. For the coefficients of $x^2$ and for the constant terms the following conditions must hold together
$$
\begin{cases}
m - 1 = 3 \\[2pt]
m = 5
\end{cases}
$$
The first gives $m = 4$ and the second $m = 5$, so the system has no solutions and **no value** of $m$ makes the two polynomials equal.
