What is the factorization of $x^2 - 11x + 24$?

- $(x + 3)(x + 8)$
- $(x - 4)(x - 6)$
- $(x - 2)(x - 12)$
- $(x - 3)(x - 8)$

## Explanation

For the **special trinomial** $x^2 + sx + p = (x + a)(x + b)$ we need two numbers $a$ and $b$ with sum $s = -11$ and product $p = 24$. The product is positive and the sum is negative, so both numbers are negative. The pairs of negative numbers with product $24$ are $-1$ and $-24$, $-2$ and $-12$, $-3$ and $-8$, $-4$ and $-6$, and among these only $-3$ and $-8$ have sum $-11$. Therefore $x^2 - 11x + 24 = (x - 3)(x - 8)$.
