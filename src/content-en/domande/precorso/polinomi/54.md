The power $(3x - 2)^5$ is expanded and the resulting polynomial is put in order. What is the sum of its coefficients?

- $211$
- $243$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The sum of the coefficients of a polynomial $P(x)$ is the value $P(1)$, since at $x = 1$ every power of $x$ equals $1$. Therefore the sum is computed without expanding the power, and it equals $P(1) = (3 \cdot 1 - 2)^5 = 1^5 = 1$. The number $211 = 243 - 32$ is the sum of the coefficients of $(3x)^5$ and $(-2)^5$ alone, which neglects the mixed terms of the expansion.
