Which product of factors that cannot be factored further is equal to $x^4 - x^3 - 8x + 8$?

- $(x - 1)\left( x^3 - 8 \right)$
- $(x - 1)(x - 2)\left( x^2 + 2x + 4 \right)$
- $(x - 1)(x - 2)\left( x^2 - 2x + 4 \right)$
- $(x - 1)(x + 2)\left( x^2 - 2x + 4 \right)$

## Explanation

**Factoring by grouping**, we factor $x^3$ out of the first two terms and $-8$ out of the last two, and we get
$$
x^4 - x^3 - 8x + 8 = x^3 (x - 1) - 8\,(x - 1) = (x - 1)\left( x^3 - 8 \right) .
$$
The factor $x^3 - 8$ is a **difference of cubes** with $A = x$ and $B = 2$, so $x^3 - 8 = (x - 2)\left( x^2 + 2x + 4 \right)$. The quadratic $x^2 + 2x + 4 = (x + 1)^2 + 3$ has no real roots, therefore the factorization $(x - 1)(x - 2)\left( x^2 + 2x + 4 \right)$ is complete.
