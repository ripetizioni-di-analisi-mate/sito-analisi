What is the factorization of $(x + 1)^2 - 9$?

- $(x - 2)(x + 4)$
- $(x + 2)(x - 4)$
- $(x - 2)(x - 4)$
- $(x - 8)(x + 10)$

## Explanation

Since $9 = 3^2$, the expression is a **difference of squares** $A^2 - B^2$ with $A = x + 1$ and $B = 3$. The formula $A^2 - B^2 = (A - B)(A + B)$ gives
$$
(x + 1)^2 - 9 = (x + 1 - 3)(x + 1 + 3) = (x - 2)(x + 4) .
$$
Expanding the two expressions gives the same polynomial, since $(x - 2)(x + 4) = x^2 + 2x - 8$ and $(x + 1)^2 - 9 = x^2 + 2x + 1 - 9 = x^2 + 2x - 8$.
