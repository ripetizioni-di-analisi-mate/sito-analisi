What are the degree and the leading coefficient of the polynomial $P(x) = \left( 2x^3 - x + 1 \right)\left( 3x^2 + 4 \right)^2$?

- Degree $7$ and leading coefficient $6$
- Degree $12$ and leading coefficient $18$
- Degree $7$ and leading coefficient $18$
- Degree $5$ and leading coefficient $18$

## Explanation

In the product of two polynomials the degrees add up and the leading coefficient is the product of the leading coefficients. The factor $\left( 3x^2 + 4 \right)^2$ is the product of $3x^2 + 4$ with itself, so it has degree $2 + 2 = 4$ and leading coefficient $3 \cdot 3 = 9$. The factor $2x^3 - x + 1$ has degree $3$ and leading coefficient $2$. Therefore the highest-degree term of the expansion is $2x^3 \cdot 9x^4 = 18x^7$, and $P$ has degree $7$ and leading coefficient $18$.
