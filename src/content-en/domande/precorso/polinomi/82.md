The polynomial $P(x) = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3) \cdots (x - 10)$ is the product of ten linear factors. What is the coefficient of $x^9$ in its expansion?

- $55$
- $-10$
- $-55$
- $-45$

## Explanation

In a product the degrees add up, so $P$ has degree $10$. When the product is expanded, each term is obtained by choosing from each factor $x - k$ either the monomial $x$ or the number $-k$. A term of degree $9$ is obtained by choosing $x$ in nine factors and the number $-k$ in the remaining factor, and it therefore equals $-k\,x^9$. There are ten factors, so the terms of degree $9$ are $-x^9, -2x^9, \dots, -10x^9$. Adding them gives the coefficient $-(1 + 2 + \dots + 10) = -55$.
