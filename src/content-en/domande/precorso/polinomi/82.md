What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in $- \left(x - 3\right)^{3} + \left(x + 3\right)^{3}$?

- $6$
- $9$
- $0$
- $18$

## Explanation

The cube $\left(x + 3\right)^{3}$ contains the term $9 x^{2}$, while $\left(x - 3\right)^{3}$ contains $- 9 x^{2}$. Since $\left(x - 3\right)^{3}$ has a minus sign in front, both terms contribute $+9 x^{2}$, so the coefficient is $18$.
