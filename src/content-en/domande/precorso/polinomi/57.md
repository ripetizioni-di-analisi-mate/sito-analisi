Into which product does $x^2 + 6x + 9 - y^2$ factor?

- $(x + 3 - y)^2$
- $(x - 3 - y)(x - 3 + y)$
- $\left( x + 3 - y^2 \right)\left( x + 3 + y^2 \right)$
- $(x + 3 - y)(x + 3 + y)$

## Explanation

The first three terms form the **square of a binomial** $x^2 + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)^2$, since $6x = 2 \cdot x \cdot 3$. The polynomial becomes $(x + 3)^2 - y^2$, which is a **difference of squares** with $A = x + 3$ and $B = y$. Therefore $x^2 + 6x + 9 - y^2 = (x + 3 - y)(x + 3 + y)$.
