What is the quotient when $x^{3} + 2 x^{2} - 3 x + 5$ is divided by $x^{2} + 2$?

- $x$
- $x - 2$
- $x + 2$
- $x + 1$

## Explanation

Dividing the term of degree $3$ by $x^2$ gives $x$. The first subtraction leaves the term $2 x^{2}$, which adds $2$ to the quotient. The remainder has degree below $2$, so the quotient is $x + 2$.
