From a square sheet of side $10$, four squares of side $x$, with $0 < x < 5$, are cut at the four corners, and the edges are folded up to form an open box, as in the figure. The volume of the box is $V(x) = x\,(10 - 2x)^2$. Which polynomial is obtained by expanding $V(x)$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-2 xmax=12 ymin=-2 ymax=12 poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,2 0,2;8,0 10,0 10,2 8,2;8,8 10,8 10,10 8,10;0,8 2,8 2,10 0,10" linee="0,0 10,0 10,10 0,10 0,0" segmenti="2,2,8,2;8,2,8,8;8,8,2,8;2,8,2,2" etichette="1,-0.8,x;-0.8,1,x;5,10.8,10" titolo="The sheet of side $10$ with the four squares of side $x$ to be cut and the dashed fold lines"}

- $4x^3 + 100x$
- $4x^3 - 20x^2 + 100x$
- $2x^3 - 40x^2 + 100x$
- $4x^3 - 40x^2 + 100x$

## Explanation

By the **square of a binomial** $(A - B)^2 = A^2 - 2AB + B^2$ with $A = 10$ and $B = 2x$ we have $A^2 = 100$ and $B^2 = (2x)^2 = 4x^2$. Twice the product is $2AB = 2 \cdot 10 \cdot 2x = 40x$, so $(10 - 2x)^2 = 100 - 40x + 4x^2$. Multiplying by $x$ gives $V(x) = 100x - 40x^2 + 4x^3$. Therefore, written in decreasing powers, the volume is $V(x) = 4x^3 - 40x^2 + 100x$.
