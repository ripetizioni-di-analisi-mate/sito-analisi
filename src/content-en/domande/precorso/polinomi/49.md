The polynomial $P(x) = x^3 + 2x^2 - 5x - 6$ is divisible by only one of the following binomials. Which one?

- $x + 2$
- $x - 2$
- $x - 1$
- $x - 3$

## Explanation

By the **factor theorem** the binomial $x - c$ divides $P$ if and only if $P(c) = 0$. The binomial $x - 2$ vanishes at $2$, and $P(2) = 8 + 8 - 10 - 6 = 0$, so $x - 2$ is a factor of $P$. The other binomials vanish at $-2$, $1$ and $3$, where $P(-2) = 4$, $P(1) = -8$ and $P(3) = 24$. Therefore none of them divides $P$.
