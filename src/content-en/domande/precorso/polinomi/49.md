Set $Q(x)=P(x)-P(4)$. Which factor must divide $Q$ regardless of the coefficients of $P$?

- $x+4$
- $x-4$
- $x$
- $(x-4)^2$

## Explanation

By construction $Q(4)=P(4)-P(4)=0$. The factor theorem guarantees $x-4$. The example $P(x)=x$ shows that none of the other proposed factors is guaranteed.
