What is a factorization of $2 x^{2} + x - 3$?

- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(2 x - 3\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(2 x + 3\right)$
- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(2 x - 3\right)$
- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(2 x + 3\right)$

## Explanation

Group the terms as the sum of $2 x \left(x - 1\right)$ and $3 x - 3$. Their common factor is $x-1$.
