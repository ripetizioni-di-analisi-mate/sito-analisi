The polynomial $(x + 1)^2 - 3(x + 1) - 10$ is equal to which product of linear factors?

- $(x - 5)(x + 2)$
- $(x - 4)(x + 3)$
- $(x + 6)(x - 1)$
- $(x - 6)(x + 1)$

## Explanation

With the substitution $t = x + 1$ the polynomial becomes the trinomial $t^2 - 3t - 10$. Two numbers with sum $-3$ and product $-10$ are $-5$ and $2$, so by the **monic quadratic trinomial** rule $t^2 - 3t - 10 = (t - 5)(t + 2)$. Going back to the variable $x$ we have $t - 5 = x - 4$ and $t + 2 = x + 3$. Therefore the polynomial is equal to $(x - 4)(x + 3)$.
