What is the expansion of $(2x - 1)^3$?

- $8x^3 - 1$
- $8x^3 - 12x^2 + 6x - 1$
- $8x^3 - 6x^2 + 6x - 1$
- $8x^3 - 12x^2 - 6x - 1$

## Explanation

We apply the **cube of a binomial** $(A - B)^3 = A^3 - 3A^2 B + 3AB^2 - B^3$ with $A = 2x$ and $B = 1$. Since $A^2 = 4x^2$, the four terms are $A^3 = 8x^3$, $3A^2 B = 3 \cdot 4x^2 = 12x^2$, $3AB^2 = 3 \cdot 2x = 6x$ and $B^3 = 1$. The signs alternate starting from the positive sign of $A^3$, so
$$
(2x - 1)^3 = 8x^3 - 12x^2 + 6x - 1 .
$$
