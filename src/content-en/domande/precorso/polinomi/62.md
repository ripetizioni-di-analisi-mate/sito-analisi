The division algorithm is applied to $A(x) = x^3 - 2x^2 + 5$ and $B(x) = x^2 + x$. Which partial remainder is obtained after the first step?

- $2x^3 - x^2 + 5$
- $-3x^2 + 5$
- $-x^2 + 5$
- $-2x^2 - x + 5$

## Explanation

The first term of the quotient is the ratio of the terms of highest degree, $\dfrac{x^3}{x^2} = x$. The divisor multiplied by $x$ gives $x^3 + x^2$, and this product is subtracted from the dividend:
$$
x^3 - 2x^2 + 5 - \left( x^3 + x^2 \right) = -3x^2 + 5 .
$$
The partial remainder $-3x^2 + 5$ has degree $2$, equal to the degree of the divisor, so the algorithm continues with the term $\dfrac{-3x^2}{x^2} = -3$ of the quotient.
