- $(x - 2)(x + 2)(x - 3)(x + 3)$
- $(x - 4)(x - 9)$
- $(x - 2)^2 (x - 3)^2$
- $\left( x^2 + 4 \right)\left( x^2 + 9 \right)$

## Explanation

The polynomial contains only even powers of $x$, so with the substitution $t = x^2$ it becomes the trinomial $t^2 - 13t + 36$. Since $(-4) + (-9) = -13$ and $(-4) \cdot (-9) = 36$, the **special trinomial** gives $t^2 - 13t + 36 = (t - 4)(t - 9)$. Returning to the variable $x$ we obtain $x^4 - 13x^2 + 36 = \left( x^2 - 4 \right)\left( x^2 - 9 \right)$. Both factors are differences of squares, therefore $x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$ and $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$, and the complete factorization is $(x - 2)(x + 2)(x - 3)(x + 3)$.
