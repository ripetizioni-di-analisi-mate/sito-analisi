What is a factorization of $6 x^{2} + x - 7$?

- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(6 x - 7\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(6 x + 7\right)$
- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(6 x - 7\right)$
- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(6 x + 7\right)$

## Explanation

Group the terms as the sum of $6 x \left(x - 1\right)$ and $7 x - 7$. Their common factor is $x-1$.
