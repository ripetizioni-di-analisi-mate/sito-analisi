Let $P(x) = 2x^3 - x + 1$ and $Q(x) = -2x^3 + 4x^2 - 5$. What is the degree of $P(x) + Q(x)$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

In the sum of two polynomials the coefficients of the terms of the same degree are added. The terms of degree $3$ are $2x^3$ and $-2x^3$, whose sum is $0$, so the third-degree term cancels. We get $P(x) + Q(x) = 4x^2 - x - 4$, whose leading coefficient is $4 \neq 0$. Therefore the sum has degree $2$, less than the degree of the two addends.
