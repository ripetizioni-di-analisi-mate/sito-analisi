The real numbers $a$ and $b$ satisfy $a + b = 5$ and $ab = 3$. What is $a^2 + b^2$?

- $25$
- $22$
- $19$
- $31$

## Explanation

From the **square of a binomial** $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ it follows that $a^2 + b^2 = (a + b)^2 - 2ab$. Substituting the data gives $a^2 + b^2 = 25 - 6 = 19$. The computation uses only the sum and the product of $a$ and $b$, without finding the two numbers.
