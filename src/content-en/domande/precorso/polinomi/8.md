## Explanation

By **Ruffini's theorem**, the binomial $x - 3$ divides $P(x)$ if and only if $P(3) = 0$. Substituting $x = 3$ gives $P(3) = 27 - 3k + 6 = 33 - 3k$. The equation $33 - 3k = 0$ is equivalent to $3k = 33$, so $k = 11$. Therefore $x - 3$ divides $P(x)$ for $k = 11$, and for no other value of $k$.
