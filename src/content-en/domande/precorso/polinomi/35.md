What is the complete factorization of $x^3 - 7x - 6$?

- $(x - 1)(x - 2)(x + 3)$
- $(x + 1)(x - 2)(x + 3)$
- $(x - 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)$
- $(x + 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)$

## Explanation

The leading coefficient is $1$, so any rational roots are integer divisors of the constant term $-6$. Since $(-1)^3 - 7 \cdot (-1) - 6 = -1 + 7 - 6 = 0$, the number $-1$ is a root, and **Ruffini's rule** with $c = -1$ gives
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
 & 1 & 0 & -7 & -6 \\
-1 &  & -1 & 1 & 6 \\ \hline
 & 1 & -1 & -6 & 0
\end{array}
$$
The quotient $x^2 - x - 6$ is a special trinomial, since $2 + (-3) = -1$ and $2 \cdot (-3) = -6$, so $x^2 - x - 6 = (x + 2)(x - 3)$. Therefore $x^3 - 7x - 6 = (x + 1)(x + 2)(x - 3)$.
