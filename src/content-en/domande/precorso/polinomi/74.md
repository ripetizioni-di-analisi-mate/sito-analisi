Which polynomial is obtained by multiplying $x^2 + x + 1$ by $x^2 - x + 1$?

- $x^4 + 1$
- $x^4 - x^2 + 1$
- $x^4 + x^2 + 1$
- $x^4 + 3x^2 + 1$

## Explanation

The two factors can be written $\left( x^2 + 1 \right) + x$ and $\left( x^2 + 1 \right) - x$, so the product has the form $(A + B)(A - B)$ with $A = x^2 + 1$ and $B = x$. By the **difference of squares** the product equals $A^2 - B^2 = \left( x^2 + 1 \right)^2 - x^2$. By the **square of a binomial** we have $\left( x^2 + 1 \right)^2 = x^4 + 2x^2 + 1$. Therefore the product is equal to $x^4 + 2x^2 + 1 - x^2 = x^4 + x^2 + 1$.
