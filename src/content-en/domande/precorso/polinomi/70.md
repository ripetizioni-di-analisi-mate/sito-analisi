For which natural numbers $n \geq 1$ is the polynomial $x^n + 1$ divisible by $x + 1$?

- For even $n$
- For odd $n$
- For every $n \geq 1$
- Only for $n = 1$

## Explanation

By the **factor theorem** the binomial $x + 1$ divides $x^n + 1$ if and only if the polynomial vanishes at $x = -1$, that is, if $(-1)^n + 1 = 0$. The power $(-1)^n$ equals $-1$ for odd $n$ and $1$ for even $n$. Therefore $(-1)^n + 1 = 0$ if and only if $n$ is odd. For example $x^3 + 1 = (x + 1)\left( x^2 - x + 1 \right)$, while $x^2 + 1$ equals $2$ at $x = -1$.
