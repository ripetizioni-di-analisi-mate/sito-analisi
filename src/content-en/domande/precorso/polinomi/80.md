The roots of the third-degree polynomial $P(x)$ are $1$, $2$ and $3$, and its leading coefficient is $2$. What is $P(0)$?

- $-6$
- $12$
- $6$
- $-12$

## Explanation

By the **factor theorem** each root $c$ gives a factor $x - c$, so $P(x) = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)\,Q(x)$. In a product the degrees add up, therefore $\deg Q = 3 - 3 = 0$, and $Q$ is the constant equal to the leading coefficient $2$. Hence $P(x) = 2(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)$, and $P(0) = 2 \cdot (-1) \cdot (-2) \cdot (-3) = -12$.
