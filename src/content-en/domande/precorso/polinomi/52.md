What is the quotient when $x^{3} + 3 x^{2} - 4 x + 7$ is divided by $x^{2} + 2$?

- $x + 3$
- $x + 2$
- $x - 3$
- $x$

## Explanation

Dividing the cubic term by $x^2$ gives $x$. The first subtraction leaves $3 x^{2}$ as the leading term, adding $3$ to the quotient. The remainder has degree below $2$, so the quotient is $x + 3$.
