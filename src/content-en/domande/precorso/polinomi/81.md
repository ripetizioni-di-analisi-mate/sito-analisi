The trinomial $x + y - 1$ is squared. Which polynomial is obtained?

- $x^2 + y^2 + 2xy - 2x - 2y + 1$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 1$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 2xy + 2x + 2y + 1$
- $x^2 + y^2 + xy - x - y + 1$

## Explanation

The trinomial is written as the binomial $A - B$ with $A = x + y$ and $B = 1$. By the **square of a binomial** $(A - B)^2 = A^2 - 2AB + B^2$ we get $(x + y)^2 - 2(x + y) + 1$. Expanding further $(x + y)^2 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2$, the result is $x^2 + y^2 + 2xy - 2x - 2y + 1$. Each pair of terms of the trinomial gives twice their product, with the sign of the product of the two terms.
