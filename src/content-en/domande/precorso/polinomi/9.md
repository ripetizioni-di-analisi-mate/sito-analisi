- $(x + 1)(x + 2)(x - 4)$
- $(x - 1)(x + 2)(x - 4)$
- $(x - 1)(x - 2)(x + 4)$
- $(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 4)$

## Explanation

The leading coefficient is $1$, so the possible rational roots are integers that divide the constant term $8$. Since $P(1) = 1 + 1 - 10 + 8 = 0$, the number $1$ is a root, and **Ruffini's rule** with $c = 1$ gives
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
 & 1 & 1 & -10 & 8 \\
1 &  & 1 & 2 & -8 \\ \hline
 & 1 & 2 & -8 & 0
\end{array}
$$
The quotient $x^2 + 2x - 8$ is a special trinomial, since $4 + (-2) = 2$ and $4 \cdot (-2) = -8$, so $x^2 + 2x - 8 = (x + 4)(x - 2)$. Therefore $x^3 + x^2 - 10x + 8 = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x + 4)$.
