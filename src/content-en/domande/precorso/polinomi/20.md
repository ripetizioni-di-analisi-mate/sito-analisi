What is the factorization of $8x^3 + 125$?

- $(2x + 5)\left( 4x^2 + 10x + 25 \right)$
- $(2x - 5)\left( 4x^2 + 10x + 25 \right)$
- $(2x + 5)\left( 4x^2 - 20x + 25 \right)$
- $(2x + 5)\left( 4x^2 - 10x + 25 \right)$

## Explanation

Since $8x^3 = (2x)^3$ and $125 = 5^3$, we apply the **sum of cubes** $A^3 + B^3 = (A + B)\left( A^2 - AB + B^2 \right)$ with $A = 2x$ and $B = 5$. The second factor is $A^2 - AB + B^2 = 4x^2 - 10x + 25$, so $8x^3 + 125 = (2x + 5)\left( 4x^2 - 10x + 25 \right)$. The factor $4x^2 - 10x + 25$ is a **quadratic that is not a perfect square**, since it contains the product $AB = 10x$ in place of the double product $2AB = 20x$. Therefore it has no real roots and the factorization is complete.
