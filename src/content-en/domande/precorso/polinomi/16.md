What is the expansion of $(3x - 2y)^2$?

- $9x^2 - 4y^2$
- $9x^2 - 6xy + 4y^2$
- $9x^2 + 12xy + 4y^2$
- $9x^2 - 12xy + 4y^2$

## Explanation

We apply the **square of a binomial** $(A - B)^2 = A^2 - 2AB + B^2$ with $A = 3x$ and $B = 2y$. The squares are $A^2 = 9x^2$ and $B^2 = 4y^2$, and the double product is $2AB = 2 \cdot 3x \cdot 2y = 12xy$. The binomial contains a minus sign, so the double product has a negative sign. Therefore $(3x - 2y)^2 = 9x^2 - 12xy + 4y^2$.
