Which of the following polynomials factors as the product of two linear factors with real coefficients?

- $x^2 + 9$
- $x^2 + 2x + 5$
- $x^2 - 7$
- $x^2 + x + 1$

## Explanation

Since $7 = \left( \sqrt{7} \right)^2$, the polynomial $x^2 - 7$ is a **difference of squares**, so $x^2 - 7 = \left( x - \sqrt{7} \right)\left( x + \sqrt{7} \right)$. The other three polynomials are positive for every real $x$, since $x^2 + 9 \geq 9$, $x^2 + 2x + 5 = (x + 1)^2 + 4 \geq 4$ and $x^2 + x + 1 = \left( x + \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^2 + \dfrac{3}{4} \geq \dfrac{3}{4}$. Therefore none of the three has real roots, and by the **factor theorem** none of the three is divisible by a binomial $x - c$ with $c$ real.
