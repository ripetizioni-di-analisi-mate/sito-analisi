The remainder when $P(x)$ is divided by $2x-1$ is $3$. Let $Q(x)=P(2x+1)$. Which monic divisor guarantees the same remainder for $Q$ whatever $P$ is?

- $x-\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $x-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x+\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x+\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The given remainder tells us $P(\dfrac{1}{2})=3$. To obtain this same value in $Q$, solve $2x+1=\dfrac{1}{2}$, giving $x=-\dfrac{1}{4}$. The monic divisor vanishing there is $x+\dfrac{1}{4}$.
