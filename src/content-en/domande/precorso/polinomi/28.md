What is the remainder of the division of $P(x) = 4x^3 - 2x + 3$ by $2x - 1$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $31$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

By **division with remainder**, $P(x) = (2x - 1)\,Q(x) + R$, and the remainder $R$ is a constant since the divisor has degree $1$. The divisor vanishes at $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so evaluating the equality at $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $R = P\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$. Since $4 \cdot \dfrac{1}{8} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 1$, we have $P\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right) = \dfrac{1}{2} - 1 + 3 = \dfrac{5}{2}$. Therefore the remainder is $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
