What is the factorization of $x^3 - 6x^2 + 12x - 8$?

- $(x + 2)^3$
- $(x - 2)\left( x^2 + 2x + 4 \right)$
- $(x - 2)^3$
- $(x - 2)^2 (x + 2)$

## Explanation

The outer terms are the cubes $x^3$ and $-8 = (-2)^3$, so the polynomial may be the **cube of a binomial** $(A - B)^3$ with $A = x$ and $B = 2$. The middle terms of this cube are $3A^2 B = 3 \cdot x^2 \cdot 2 = 6x^2$ and $3AB^2 = 3 \cdot x \cdot 4 = 12x$, with alternating signs $-6x^2$ and $+12x$. Since all four terms coincide with those of the given polynomial, $x^3 - 6x^2 + 12x - 8 = (x - 2)^3$.
