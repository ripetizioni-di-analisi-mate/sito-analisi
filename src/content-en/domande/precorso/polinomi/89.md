A polynomial of degree $5$ is the product of three polynomials of positive degree. Which triple of degrees is possible for the three factors?

- $2$, $2$ and $2$
- $1$, $1$ and $2$
- $1$, $2$ and $3$
- $1$, $2$ and $2$

## Explanation

In a product of polynomials the degrees add up, so the degrees of the three factors have sum $5$. The triples $2, 2, 2$ and $1, 2, 3$ have sum $6$, and the triple $1, 1, 2$ has sum $4$. Therefore among the proposed triples only $1, 2, 2$ is possible, as in $x\left( x^2 + 1 \right)\left( x^2 + 4 \right)$.
