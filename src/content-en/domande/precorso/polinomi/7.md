What is the remainder of the division of $P(x) = x^5 - 2x^3 + x - 4$ by $x + 2$?

- $14$
- $42$
- $-54$
- $-22$

## Explanation

By the **remainder theorem**, the remainder of the division of $P(x)$ by $x - c$ is $P(c)$. The divisor $x + 2$ is written as $x - (-2)$, so $c = -2$. Since $(-2)^5 = -32$ and $(-2)^3 = -8$, we have
$$
P(-2) = -32 - 2 \cdot (-8) + (-2) - 4 = -32 + 16 - 2 - 4 = -22 .
$$
Therefore the remainder is $-22$.
