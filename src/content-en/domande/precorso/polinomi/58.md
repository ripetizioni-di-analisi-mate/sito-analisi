The trinomial $x^2 + bx + c$ has the roots $2$ and $-5$. What is $b$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $-10$
- $7$

## Explanation

By the **factor theorem** the roots $2$ and $-5$ give the factors $x - 2$ and $x + 5$. The trinomial has leading coefficient $1$, so $x^2 + bx + c = (x - 2)(x + 5) = x^2 + 3x - 10$. By the identity principle $b = 3$ and $c = -10$. The coefficient of $x$ is therefore the opposite of the sum of the roots, which is $2 + (-5) = -3$.
