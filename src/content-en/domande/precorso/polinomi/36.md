What is the complete factorization of $P(x) = x^4 + x^3 - 7x^2 - x + 6$?

- $(x - 1)(x + 1)(x - 3)(x + 2)$
- $(x - 1)(x + 1)(x + 3)(x - 2)$
- $(x - 1)^2 (x + 3)(x - 2)$
- $\left( x^2 + 1 \right)(x + 3)(x - 2)$

## Explanation

Since $P(1) = 1 + 1 - 7 - 1 + 6 = 0$ and $P(-1) = 1 - 1 - 7 + 1 + 6 = 0$, by the **factor theorem** the polynomial is divisible by $x - 1$ and by $x + 1$. **Synthetic division** with $c = 1$ gives the quotient $x^3 + 2x^2 - 5x - 6$, and the rule with $c = -1$ applied to this quotient gives $x^2 + x - 6$, both with remainder $0$. Hence $P(x) = (x - 1)(x + 1)\left( x^2 + x - 6 \right)$. The factor $x^2 + x - 6$ is a monic quadratic trinomial, since $3 + (-2) = 1$ and $3 \cdot (-2) = -6$, therefore $P(x) = (x - 1)(x + 1)(x + 3)(x - 2)$.
