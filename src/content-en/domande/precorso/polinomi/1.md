What is the expansion of $(x + 3)^3$?

- $x^3 + 9x^2 + 27x + 27$
- $x^3 + 27$
- $x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 27$
- $x^3 + 9x^2 + 9x + 27$

## Explanation

The expansion is obtained from the **cube of a binomial** $(A + B)^3 = A^3 + 3A^2 B + 3A B^2 + B^3$ with $A = x$ and $B = 3$, so
$$
(x + 3)^3 = x^3 + 3 \cdot x^2 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot x \cdot 3^2 + 3^3 .
$$
The middle terms equal $3 \cdot x^2 \cdot 3 = 9x^2$ and $3 \cdot x \cdot 9 = 27x$, while the last term is $3^3 = 27$. Therefore $(x + 3)^3 = x^3 + 9x^2 + 27x + 27$.
