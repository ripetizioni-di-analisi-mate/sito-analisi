What is the quotient when $x^{3} + 5 x^{2} - 6 x + 11$ is divided by $x^{2} + 2$?

- $x + 5$
- $x$
- $x - 5$
- $x + 4$

## Explanation

Dividing the cubic term by $x^2$ gives $x$. The first subtraction leaves $5 x^{2}$ as the leading term, adding $5$ to the quotient. The remainder has degree below $2$, so the quotient is $x + 5$.
