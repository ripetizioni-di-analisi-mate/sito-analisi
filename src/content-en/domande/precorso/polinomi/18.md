Which of the following trinomials is the square of a binomial?

- $4x^2 - 12x + 9$
- $4x^2 - 6x + 9$
- $x^2 + 4x - 4$
- $9x^2 + 12x - 4$

## Explanation

A trinomial is a **square of a binomial** when two terms are squares and the third is the double product of the two bases. In $4x^2 - 12x + 9$ the terms $4x^2$ and $9$ are the squares of $2x$ and $3$, and $-12x = -2 \cdot 2x \cdot 3$ is the double product, so $4x^2 - 12x + 9 = (2x - 3)^2$. In $4x^2 - 6x + 9$ the term $-6x$ is the product $-2x \cdot 3$ without the factor $2$, therefore the trinomial is not a square. In the trinomials $x^2 + 4x - 4$ and $9x^2 + 12x - 4$ the constant term $-4$ is negative, so it is not the square of a real number. The linear term is not the square of a monomial either, therefore these two trinomials do not contain two squares.
