The polynomial $P(x) = 4 - x^2 + 2x^3 - x$ has its terms in no particular order. What are its degree, its leading coefficient and its constant term?

- Degree $3$, leading coefficient $4$, constant term $-1$
- Degree $4$, leading coefficient $2$, constant term $4$
- Degree $3$, leading coefficient $-1$, constant term $4$
- Degree $3$, leading coefficient $2$, constant term $4$

## Explanation

Written in decreasing powers of $x$, the polynomial is $P(x) = 2x^3 - x^2 - x + 4$. The term of highest degree is $2x^3$, so the **degree** is $3$ and the **leading coefficient** is $2$. The constant term is the term of degree $0$, that is, $4$. The number of terms, which is $4$, is a different quantity from the degree.
