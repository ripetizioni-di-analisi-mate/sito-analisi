What is the quotient when $x^{3} + 4 x^{2} - 5 x + 9$ is divided by $x^{2} + 2$?

- $x - 4$
- $x + 4$
- $x$
- $x + 3$

## Explanation

Dividing the term of degree $3$ by $x^2$ gives $x$. The first subtraction leaves the term $4 x^{2}$, which adds $4$ to the quotient. The remainder has degree below $2$, so the quotient is $x + 4$.
