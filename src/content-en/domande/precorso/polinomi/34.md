What is the quotient of the division of $2x^3 + 5x^2 - 4x - 3$ by $x + 3$?

- $2x^2 + 11x + 29$
- $2x^3 - x^2 - x$
- $2x^2 - x - 1$
- $x^2 - x - 1$

## Explanation

The divisor is written $x + 3 = x - (-3)$, so in **Ruffini's rule** we use $c = -3$.
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
 & 2 & 5 & -4 & -3 \\
-3 &  & -6 & 3 & 3 \\ \hline
 & 2 & -1 & -1 & 0
\end{array}
$$
The remainder is $0$, so the division is exact. The numbers $2, -1, -1$ are the coefficients of the quotient, which has degree $3 - 1 = 2$, therefore the quotient is $2x^2 - x - 1$.
