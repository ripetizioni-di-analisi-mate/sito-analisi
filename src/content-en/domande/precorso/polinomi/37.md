What are the rational candidates for the roots of $P(x) = 3x^3 - x^2 + 2x - 4$?

- $\pm 1$, $\pm 2$, $\pm 4$, $\pm\dfrac{1}{3}$, $\pm\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\pm\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\pm 1$, $\pm 3$, $\pm\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\pm\dfrac{3}{2}$, $\pm\dfrac{1}{4}$, $\pm\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\pm 1$, $\pm 2$, $\pm 4$
- $\pm 1$, $\pm 2$, $\pm 3$, $\pm 4$, $\pm 6$, $\pm 12$

## Explanation

By the criterion for **finding rational roots**, if the fraction $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms is a root, then $p$ divides the constant term $-4$ and $q$ divides the leading coefficient $3$. The divisors of $-4$ are $\pm 1$, $\pm 2$, $\pm 4$, and the positive divisors of $3$ are $1$ and $3$. Therefore the candidates are the integers $\pm 1$, $\pm 2$, $\pm 4$ and the fractions $\pm\dfrac{1}{3}$, $\pm\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\pm\dfrac{4}{3}$.
