Factoring out the common factor, what is $3x(x - 2) + 5(x - 2)$ equal to?

- $3x + 5$
- $(x - 2)(3x - 5)$
- $8x\,(x - 2)$
- $(x - 2)(3x + 5)$

## Explanation

The two addends $3x(x - 2)$ and $5(x - 2)$ have the factor $x - 2$ in common. By **factoring out the common factor** $x - 2$, the other factor is the sum of what remains of the two addends, that is, $3x + 5$. Therefore $3x(x - 2) + 5(x - 2) = (x - 2)(3x + 5)$. The monomials $3x$ and $5$ have different degrees, so $3x + 5$ stays a binomial.
