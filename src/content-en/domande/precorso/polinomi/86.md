What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in $- \left(x - 2\right)^{3} + \left(x + 2\right)^{3}$?

- $4$
- $12$
- $6$
- $0$

## Explanation

The cube $\left(x + 2\right)^{3}$ contains the term $6 x^{2}$, while $\left(x - 2\right)^{3}$ contains $- 6 x^{2}$. Since $\left(x - 2\right)^{3}$ has a minus sign in front, both terms contribute $+6 x^{2}$, so the coefficient is $12$.
