What is the complete factorization of $5x^3 + 10x^2 - 15x$?

- $5x\,(x - 3)(x + 1)$
- $5x\,(x + 3)(x - 1)$
- $5\,(x + 3)(x - 1)$
- $5x\,(x + 3)(x + 1)$

## Explanation

The factor $5x$ is common to all the terms, so **factoring out the common factor** gives $5x^3 + 10x^2 - 15x = 5x\left( x^2 + 2x - 3 \right)$. The factor $x^2 + 2x - 3$ is a **special trinomial**, since $3 + (-1) = 2$ and $3 \cdot (-1) = -3$, so $x^2 + 2x - 3 = (x + 3)(x - 1)$. Therefore the complete factorization is $5x\,(x + 3)(x - 1)$, in which all the non-constant factors have degree $1$.
