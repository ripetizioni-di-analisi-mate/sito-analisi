To divide $P(x) = 2x^3 - 7x + 4$ by $x + 2$, the following synthetic division scheme was built. What are the quotient and the remainder?
$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
 & 2 & 0 & -7 & 4 \\
-2 &  & -4 & 8 & -2 \\ \hline
 & 2 & -4 & 1 & 2
\end{array}
$$

- $Q(x) = 2x^3 - 4x^2 + x$ and $R = 2$
- $Q(x) = 2x^2 - 4x + 1$ and $R = -2$
- $Q(x) = 2x^2 - 4x + 1$ and $R = 2$
- $Q(x) = 2x^2 + 4x + 1$ and $R = 2$

## Explanation

In synthetic division the last number in the row of results is the **remainder**, so $R = 2$. The numbers preceding it, $2$, $-4$ and $1$, are the coefficients of the quotient. The dividend has degree $3$, therefore the quotient has degree $3 - 1 = 2$ and equals $Q(x) = 2x^2 - 4x + 1$. The remainder coincides with $P(-2) = -16 + 14 + 4 = 2$, as the remainder theorem states.
