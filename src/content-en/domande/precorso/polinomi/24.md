Two polynomials both leave remainder $x+2$ on division by $x^2+1$. What is the remainder of their product?

- $4x+3$
- $4x+5$
- $2x+4$
- $x+2$

## Explanation

Every term containing the divisor is still divisible by it. It suffices to reduce $(x+2)^2=x^2+4x+4$. Since $x^2$ has remainder $-1$, the result is $4x+3$.
