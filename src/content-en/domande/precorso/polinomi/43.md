What is the factorization of $x^2 + xy - 2x - 2y$?

- $(x + y)(x + 2)$
- $(x - y)(x - 2)$
- $(x + y)(x - 2)$
- $x\,(x + y - 2)$

## Explanation

We group the first two terms and the last two, and factor $x$ out of the first group and $-2$ out of the second, obtaining
$$
x^2 + xy - 2x - 2y = x\,(x + y) - 2\,(x + y) .
$$
Factoring out the minus sign together with $2$ makes the same factor $x + y$ as in the first group appear in the second group. The two groups therefore have the common factor $x + y$, and **factoring by grouping** gives $(x + y)(x - 2)$.
