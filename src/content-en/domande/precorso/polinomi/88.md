What is a factorization of $3 x^{2} + x - 4$?

- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(3 x - 4\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(3 x + 4\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(3 x - 4\right)$
- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(3 x + 4\right)$

## Explanation

Group the terms as the sum of $3 x \left(x - 1\right)$ and $4 x - 4$. Their common factor is $x-1$.
