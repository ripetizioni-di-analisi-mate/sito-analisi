Division by $x^2-1$ was stopped with the identity $P=(x^2-1)Q+(3x^2+x)$. Which change gives the final quotient without repeating the division?

- $Q+3x$
- $Q+3$
- $Q-3$
- No correction: the quotient stays $Q$ and the remainder is $3x^2+x$.

## Explanation

Write $3x^2+x=3(x^2-1)+(x+3)$. The term $3$ goes into the quotient and the new remainder $x+3$ has degree less than two.
