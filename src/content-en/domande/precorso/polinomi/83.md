What is a factorization of $4 x^{2} + x - 5$?

- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(4 x - 5\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(4 x - 5\right)$
- $\left(x - 1\right) \left(4 x + 5\right)$
- $\left(x + 1\right) \left(4 x + 5\right)$

## Explanation

Group the terms as the sum of $4 x \left(x - 1\right)$ and $5 x - 5$. Their common factor is $x-1$.
