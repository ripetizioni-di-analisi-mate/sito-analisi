What is the remainder of the division of $P(x) = x^{10} + x^5 + 1$ by $x + 1$?

- $3$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The divisor is written $x + 1 = x - (-1)$, so by the **remainder theorem** the remainder is $P(-1)$. The exponent $10$ is even and the exponent $5$ is odd, therefore $(-1)^{10} = 1$ and $(-1)^5 = -1$. This gives $P(-1) = 1 - 1 + 1 = 1$, so the remainder is $1$ and the division is not exact.
