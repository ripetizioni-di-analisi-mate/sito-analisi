What is the multiplicity of the root $x = 1$ of the polynomial $P(x) = x^4 - 2x^3 + 2x - 1$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

**Synthetic division** with $c = 1$ gives remainder $0$ and quotient $x^3 - x^2 - x + 1$, so $P(x) = (x - 1)\left( x^3 - x^2 - x + 1 \right)$. Factoring by grouping gives $x^3 - x^2 - x + 1 = x^2 (x - 1) - (x - 1) = (x - 1)\left( x^2 - 1 \right)$, and $x^2 - 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1)$. Therefore $P(x) = (x - 1)^3 (x + 1)$. The factor $Q(x) = x + 1$ satisfies $Q(1) = 2 \neq 0$, so the root $1$ has **multiplicity** $3$.
