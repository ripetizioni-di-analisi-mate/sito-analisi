What is the factorization of $2x^2 + 5x - 3$?

- $(2x - 1)(x + 3)$
- $(2x + 1)(x - 3)$
- $(x - 1)(2x + 3)$
- $\left( x - \dfrac{1}{2} \right)(x + 3)$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x^2$ is $2$, so the trinomial is not a monic quadratic trinomial and is factored by finding rational roots. Among the candidates $\pm 1$, $\pm 3$, $\pm\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\pm\dfrac{3}{2}$ we find the root $-3$, since $2 \cdot 9 - 15 - 3 = 0$. **Synthetic division** with $c = -3$ gives the quotient $2x - 1$ with remainder $0$, therefore $2x^2 + 5x - 3 = (2x - 1)(x + 3)$. The product $\left( x - \dfrac{1}{2} \right)(x + 3)$ has the same roots but leading coefficient $1$, so it is a different polynomial.
