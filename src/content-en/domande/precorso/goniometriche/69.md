Which $x$ in the interval $[-\pi, \pi]$ satisfy the inequality $\cos x \geq \dfrac{1}{2}$?

- $-\dfrac{\pi}{3} < x < \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $-\pi \leq x \leq -\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ or $\dfrac{\pi}{3} \leq x \leq \pi$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{3} \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{6} \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{6}$

## Explanation

In $[-\pi, \pi]$ the equation $\cos x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ has the solutions $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, which determine two points of the unit circle symmetric with respect to the $x$-axis.

The points of the circle with $x$-coordinate greater than or equal to $\dfrac{1}{2}$ form the arc containing the point $(1, 0)$, of angle $0$. Therefore the solutions are $-\dfrac{\pi}{3} \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

The endpoints are included because the inequality is non-strict and the cosine equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$ there.
