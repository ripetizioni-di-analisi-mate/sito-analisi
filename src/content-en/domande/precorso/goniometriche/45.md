What are the solutions of the inequality $\cos x \geq 1$?

- The inequality has no solutions
- Every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- $x = 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$ we have $\cos x \leq 1$, so the inequality $\cos x > 1$ is never satisfied and the given inequality is equivalent to the equation $\cos x = 1$.

The cosine equals $1$ only at the point $(1, 0)$ of the unit circle, so the solutions are $x = 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

The family $x = n\pi$ also includes $x = \pi$, where the cosine equals $-1$.
