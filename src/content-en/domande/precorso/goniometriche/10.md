- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \pi + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{12} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{13\pi}{12} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

In the equation $a\sin x + b\cos x = c$ one has $a = 1$ and $b = -1$, so $R = \sqrt{1 + 1} = \sqrt{2}$. The angle $\varphi$ with $\cos\varphi = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ and $\sin\varphi = -\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ is $\varphi = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

With the auxiliary angle method the equation becomes $\sqrt{2}\,\sin\left(x - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = 1$, that is, $\sin\left(x - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. Therefore
$$
x - \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi}{4} + 2n\pi \quad \text{or} \quad x - \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{3\pi}{4} + 2n\pi, \qquad n \in \mathbb{Z} .
$$
Adding $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ gives the solutions $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \pi + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
