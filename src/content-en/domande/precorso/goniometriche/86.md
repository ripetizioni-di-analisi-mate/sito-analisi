What are the solutions of $4\cos\left(x\right)=- 2 \sqrt{2}$ in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{3 \pi}{4}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right\}$

## Explanation

The required cosine is negative, so the angles lie in the second and third quadrants. Both are $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ away from $\pi$, giving $\left\{\dfrac{3 \pi}{4}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}\right\}$.
