In which quadrant does the point of the unit circle determined by the angle $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{3}$ lie, and what are the signs of $\sin x$ and $\cos x$?

- Third quadrant, with $\sin x < 0$ and $\cos x < 0$
- Second quadrant, with $\sin x > 0$ and $\cos x < 0$
- Fourth quadrant, with $\sin x < 0$ and $\cos x > 0$
- Fourth quadrant, with $\sin x > 0$ and $\cos x < 0$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < \dfrac{5\pi}{3} < 2\pi$, the point $P$ determined by the angle lies in the fourth quadrant.

The points of the fourth quadrant have positive abscissa and negative ordinate, so $\cos x > 0$ and $\sin x < 0$.

Indeed $\dfrac{5\pi}{3} = 2\pi - \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, so by periodicity and the identities for related angles, we have $\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{3} = \cos\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{3} = \sin\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
