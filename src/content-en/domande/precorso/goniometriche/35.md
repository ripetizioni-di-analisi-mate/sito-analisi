What are the solutions of the equation $\tan^2 x = 3$?

- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $\tan x = \sqrt{3}$ or $\tan x = -\sqrt{3}$.

From the table of special values $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \sqrt{3}$, and since $\tan(-x) = -\tan x$ we have $\tan\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\sqrt{3}$.

The tangent has period $\pi$, so the solutions are $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + n\pi$ or $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3} + n\pi$, that is, $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. The family with step $2n\pi$ excludes, for example, $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, where the tangent equals $-\sqrt{3}$.
