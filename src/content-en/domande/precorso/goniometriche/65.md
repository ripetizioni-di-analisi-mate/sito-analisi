On a Ferris wheel the height above the ground of a car, in meters, $t$ minutes after the start is $h(t) = 20 - 18\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{10}$. After how many minutes is the car at $29$ meters above the ground for the first time?

- $\dfrac{10}{3}$ minutes
- $\dfrac{40}{3}$ minutes
- $\dfrac{20}{3}$ minutes
- $\dfrac{25}{3}$ minutes

## Explanation

The condition $h(t) = 29$ reads $20 - 18\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{10} = 29$, so $-18\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{10} = 9$, that is, $\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{10} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

For $t \geq 0$ the smallest angle $\dfrac{\pi t}{10}$ with cosine $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ is $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. Therefore $\dfrac{\pi t}{10} = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, which gives $t = \dfrac{20}{3}$ minutes, that is, $6$ minutes and $40$ seconds.

The time $\dfrac{40}{3}$ minutes corresponds to the angle $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ and is the second time the car passes $29$ meters, on the way down. The value $\dfrac{10}{3}$ minutes comes from getting the sign of $9$ wrong, that is, from solving $\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{10} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
