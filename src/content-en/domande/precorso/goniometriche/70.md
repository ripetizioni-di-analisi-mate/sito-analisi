Which values in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$ satisfy $6\sin\left(x\right)\geq 3$?

- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{6}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right]$
- $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{7 \pi}{6}, \dfrac{11 \pi}{6}\right]$

## Explanation

The sine equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$ at the endpoints $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\dfrac{5 \pi}{6}$. Between them it stays at least as large, so the solution is $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right]$.
