Which of the four numbers $\sin 1$, $\sin 2$, $\sin 3$, $\sin 4$ is the largest? The angles are measured in radians.

- $\sin 1$
- $\sin 2$
- $\sin 3$
- $\sin 4$

## Explanation

Since $\pi$ is about $3.14$, one has $\pi < 4 < 2\pi$, so the point of angle $4$ lies below the $x$-axis and $\sin 4 < 0$. The other three angles lie between $0$ and $\pi$ and have positive sine.

By the related-angle formulas $\sin 2 = \sin(\pi - 2)$ and $\sin 3 = \sin(\pi - 3)$, with $\pi - 2 \approx 1.14$ and $\pi - 3 \approx 0.14$. The angles $0.14$, $1$ and $1.14$ lie between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2} \approx 1.57$, where the sine increases as the angle increases.

Therefore $\sin 3 < \sin 1 < \sin 2$, and the largest of the four numbers is $\sin 2$.
