The angle $\alpha$ lies between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and has $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{8}{17}$. What is $\cos(\pi - \alpha)$?

- $\dfrac{15}{17}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{17}$
- $-\dfrac{15}{17}$
- $\dfrac{8}{17}$

## Explanation

From the fundamental identity $\cos^2\alpha = 1 - \dfrac{64}{289} = \dfrac{225}{289}$. Since $\alpha$ lies in the first quadrant, the cosine is positive, so $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{15}{17}$.

The points of angles $\alpha$ and $\pi - \alpha$ are symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis and have opposite $x$-coordinates, so $\cos(\pi - \alpha) = -\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{15}{17}$.

The value $\dfrac{8}{17}$ is $\sin(\pi - \alpha)$.
