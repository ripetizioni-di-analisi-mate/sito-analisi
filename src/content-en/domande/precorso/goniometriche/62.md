What is the least positive period of $5\sin\left(6 x + \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $12 \pi$
- $\dfrac{2 \pi}{5}$

## Explanation

The argument must increase by $2\pi$ for one complete cycle. The coefficient of $x$ is 6, so the period is $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.
