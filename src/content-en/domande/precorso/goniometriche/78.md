What are the solutions of $5\cos\left(x\right)=- \dfrac{5}{2}$ in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}, \dfrac{4 \pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2 \pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{3}\right\}$

## Explanation

The required cosine is negative, so the angles lie in the second and third quadrants. Both are $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ away from $\pi$, giving $\left\{\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}, \dfrac{4 \pi}{3}\right\}$.
