At which points of $[0, 2\pi)$ is the cosine negative?

- $\pi < x < 2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2} \leq x \leq \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $0 \leq x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ or $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < x < 2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < x < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The cosine is the $x$-coordinate of the point on the unit circle, so $\cos x < 0$ when the point lies to the left of the $y$-axis.

The points of the circle on the $y$-axis correspond to the angles $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, and the arc to the left of the axis is the one through the point of angle $\pi$.

Therefore the solution is $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < x < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, with the endpoints excluded because the cosine equals $0$ there. The interval $\pi < x < 2\pi$ is where the sine is negative.
