Which values in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$ satisfy $5\sin\left(x\right)\geq \dfrac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2}$?

- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{4 \pi}{3}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{4 \pi}{3}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{3}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2 \pi}{3}\right]$
- $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2 \pi}{3}\right)$

## Explanation

The sine equals $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ at the endpoints $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}$. Between them it stays at least as large, so the solution is $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2 \pi}{3}\right]$.
