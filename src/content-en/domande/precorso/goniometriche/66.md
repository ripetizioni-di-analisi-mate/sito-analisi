Does the equation $\tan x \cdot \cos x = 1$ have real solutions? If so, which ones?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- The equation has no solutions
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

The tangent is defined for $\cos x \neq 0$, so the solutions must be sought among the $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$.

For these $x$ one has $\tan x \cdot \cos x = \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}\cdot\cos x = \sin x$, and the equation becomes $\sin x = 1$, with the solutions $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$.

At these points, however, $\cos x = 0$, so the domain condition is not satisfied. Therefore the equation has no solutions.
