What is the least positive period of $6\sin\left(7 x + \dfrac{6 \pi}{7}\right)$?

- $14 \pi$
- $\dfrac{2 \pi}{7}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{7}$

## Explanation

The argument must increase by $2\pi$ for one complete cycle. The coefficient of $x$ is 7, so the period is $\dfrac{2 \pi}{7}$.
