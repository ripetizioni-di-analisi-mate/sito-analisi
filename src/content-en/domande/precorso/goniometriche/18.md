Given that $\cos x = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < x < 2\pi$, what is the value of $\sin 2x$?

- $\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{9}$
- $-\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{9}$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean identity $\sin^2 x = 1 - \dfrac{1}{9} = \dfrac{8}{9}$. In the fourth quadrant the sine is negative, so $\sin x = -\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$.

The double-angle formula for the sine gives
$$
\sin 2x = 2\sin x\cos x = 2 \cdot \left(-\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\right) \cdot \frac{1}{3} = -\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{9} .
$$
The value $-\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{9}$ is the product $\sin x\cos x$, without the factor $2$ of the formula.
