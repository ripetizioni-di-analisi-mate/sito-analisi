Which values in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$ satisfy $7\sin\left(x\right)\geq \dfrac{7 \sqrt{2}}{2}$?

- $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{5 \pi}{4}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{4}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right]$

## Explanation

The sine equals $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ at the endpoints $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3 \pi}{4}$. Between them it stays at least as large, so the solution is $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right]$.
