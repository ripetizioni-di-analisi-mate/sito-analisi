What are the solutions of $6\sin\left(x\right)=3 \sqrt{2}$ in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{5 \pi}{4}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}\right\}$

## Explanation

Dividing by 6 gives a positive sine. The angles with that sine lie in the first and second quadrants, giving $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right\}$.
