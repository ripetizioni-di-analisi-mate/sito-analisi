Does the function $f(x) = \sin^2 x$ have the same period as the sine?

- Yes, its smallest period is $2\pi$
- No, its smallest period is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- No, its smallest period is $\pi$
- No, its smallest period is $4\pi$

## Explanation

From the double-angle formula $\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x$ it follows that
$$
\sin^2 x = \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2} .
$$
The function $\cos 2x$ has smallest period $\dfrac{2\pi}{2} = \pi$, so $\sin^2 x$ also has smallest period $\pi$.

The same result can be read from $\sin(x + \pi) = -\sin x$, which squared gives $\sin^2(x + \pi) = \sin^2 x$. There is no positive period smaller than $\pi$, because $\sin^2 x$ vanishes only for $x = n\pi$ and two consecutive zeros are $\pi$ apart.
