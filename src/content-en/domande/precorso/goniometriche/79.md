Which $x$ satisfy the equation $\cos 2x = \cos^2 x$?

- $x = n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

With the double-angle formula $\cos 2x = 2\cos^2 x - 1$ the equation becomes $2\cos^2 x - 1 = \cos^2 x$, that is, $\cos^2 x = 1$.

So $\cos x = 1$ or $\cos x = -1$, with the solutions $x = 2n\pi$ and $x = \pi + 2n\pi$. Together the two families give the integer multiples of $\pi$, so the solutions are $x = n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

The family $x = 2n\pi$ only takes $\cos x = 1$ into account. At the points $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$ one has $\cos 2x = -1$ and $\cos^2 x = 0$.
