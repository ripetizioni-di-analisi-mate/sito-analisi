Which $x$ satisfy the equation $\tan\dfrac{x}{2} = -1$?

- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{8} + \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

Since $\tan\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = -1$ and the tangent has period $\pi$, the equation is equivalent to
$$
\frac{x}{2} = -\frac{\pi}{4} + n\pi, \qquad n \in \mathbb{Z} .
$$
Multiplying every term by $2$, including $n\pi$, gives $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$.

The family $-\dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$ multiplies only the first term by $2$ and contains $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, for which $\tan\dfrac{x}{2} = \tan\dfrac{\pi}{4} = 1$. The family $-\dfrac{\pi}{8} + \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$ comes from dividing by $2$ instead of multiplying.
