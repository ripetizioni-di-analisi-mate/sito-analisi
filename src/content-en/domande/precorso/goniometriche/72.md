What is the least positive period of $2\sin\left(3 x + \dfrac{2 \pi}{3}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}$
- $\pi$
- $6 \pi$

## Explanation

The argument must increase by $2\pi$ for one complete cycle. The coefficient of $x$ is 3, so the period is $\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}$.
