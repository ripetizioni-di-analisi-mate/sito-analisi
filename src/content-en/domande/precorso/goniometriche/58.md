What are the solutions of $5\sin\left(x\right)=\dfrac{5}{2}$ in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{7 \pi}{6}, \dfrac{11 \pi}{6}\right\}$

## Explanation

Dividing by 5 gives a positive sine. The angles with that sine lie in the first and second quadrants, giving $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right\}$.
