It is known that $\cos(\pi - x) = \dfrac{1}{2}$. What are all the possible values of $x$?

- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

By the related-angle formulas $\cos(\pi - x) = -\cos x$, so the equation is equivalent to $-\cos x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, that is, to $\cos x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

An angle with cosine $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ is $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, because $\cos\dfrac{2\pi}{3} = -\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the solutions are $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

The family $\pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$ comes from writing $\cos(\pi - x) = \cos x$, without the change of sign. The family with step $n\pi$ contains $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, for which $\cos(\pi - x) = \cos\dfrac{4\pi}{3} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
