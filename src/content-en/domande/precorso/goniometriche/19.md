What is the value of $\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{5\pi}{12} = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{\pi}{6}$, the addition formula for the cosine applies:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) &= \cos\frac{\pi}{4}\cos\frac{\pi}{6} - \sin\frac{\pi}{4}\sin\frac{\pi}{6} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$, with the plus sign, is $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{12}$. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}}{2}$ is greater than $1$, so it is not the cosine of any angle.
