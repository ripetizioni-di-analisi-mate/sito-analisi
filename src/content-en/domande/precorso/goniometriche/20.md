Which expression equals $\sin(\pi + x) + \cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - x\right) + \cos(-x)$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $2\sin x + \cos x$
- $-\cos x$
- $\cos x$
- $\cos x - 2\sin x$

## Explanation

By the identities for related angles, we have $\sin(\pi + x) = -\sin x$, $\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - x\right) = \sin x$ and $\cos(-x) = \cos x$.

Substituting, the expression becomes
$$
-\sin x + \sin x + \cos x = \cos x .
$$
The result $2\sin x + \cos x$ is obtained by writing $\sin(\pi + x) = \sin x$, which is the relation that holds for $\sin(\pi - x)$.
