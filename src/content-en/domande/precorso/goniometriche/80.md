What are the solutions of $4\sin\left(x\right)=2 \sqrt{3}$ in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2 \pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{4 \pi}{3}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{4 \pi}{3}\right\}$

## Explanation

Dividing by 4 gives a positive sine. The angles with that sine lie in the first and second quadrants, giving $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2 \pi}{3}\right\}$.
