Two angles $x$ and $y$ satisfy $\sin y = \sin x$ and $\cos y = -\cos x$. Which relation necessarily links $y$ to $x$?

- $y = -x + 2n\pi$, for some integer $n$
- $y = \pi + x + 2n\pi$, for some integer $n$
- $y = \dfrac{\pi}{2} - x + 2n\pi$, for some integer $n$
- $y = \pi - x + 2n\pi$, for some integer $n$

## Explanation

The points of the unit circle determined by $x$ and by $y$ have the same $y$-coordinate and opposite $x$-coordinates, so they are symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis.

The point symmetric to the point of angle $x$ with respect to the $y$-axis is the point of angle $\pi - x$; indeed $\sin(\pi - x) = \sin x$ and $\cos(\pi - x) = -\cos x$. Two angles that determine the same point differ by a multiple of $2\pi$, so $y = \pi - x + 2n\pi$.

With $y = \pi + x$ one would have $\sin y = -\sin x$, and with $y = -x$ one would have $\cos y = \cos x$.
