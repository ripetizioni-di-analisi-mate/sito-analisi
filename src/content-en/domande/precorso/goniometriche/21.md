Which expression equals $\cos^4 x - \sin^4 x$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $1$
- $\cos 4x$
- $1 - 2\cos^2 x$
- $\cos 2x$

## Explanation

The expression is a difference of squares, so
$$
\cos^4 x - \sin^4 x = (\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x)(\cos^2 x + \sin^2 x) .
$$
The second factor equals $1$ by the Pythagorean identity, and the first equals $\cos 2x$ by the double-angle formula for the cosine. Therefore $\cos^4 x - \sin^4 x = \cos 2x$.

The expression $1 - 2\cos^2 x$ equals $-\cos 2x$.
