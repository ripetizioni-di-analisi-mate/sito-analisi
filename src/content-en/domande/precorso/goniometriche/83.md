For the $x$ at which it is defined, the fraction $\dfrac{1 - \cos 2x}{\sin 2x}$ coincides with only one of the following expressions. Which one?

- $\tan x$
- $2\tan x$
- $\dfrac{\cos x}{\sin x}$
- $\dfrac{\tan x}{2}$

## Explanation

From the double-angle formula $\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x$ it follows that $1 - \cos 2x = 2\sin^2 x$, and moreover $\sin 2x = 2\sin x\cos x$. If $\sin 2x \neq 0$, then $\sin x$ and $\cos x$ are also different from $0$, so one can simplify:
$$
\frac{1 - \cos 2x}{\sin 2x} = \frac{2\sin^2 x}{2\sin x\cos x} = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x} = \tan x .
$$
The expression $\dfrac{\cos x}{\sin x}$ comes from the form $\cos 2x = 1 - 2\cos^2 x$, which has the wrong sign. The expressions $2\tan x$ and $\dfrac{\tan x}{2}$ come from forgetting the factor $2$ in one of the two double-angle formulas.
