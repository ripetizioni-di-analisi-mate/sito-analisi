What is the minimal period of the function $f(x) = \tan\dfrac{x}{2}$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $4\pi$

## Explanation

The tangent has period $\pi$, and $\pi$ is the smallest positive period of the tangent.

We have $f(x + 2\pi) = \tan\left(\dfrac{x}{2} + \pi\right) = \tan\dfrac{x}{2} = f(x)$, so $2\pi$ is a period of $f$.

If $T > 0$ is a period of $f$, then $\tan\left(\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{T}{2}\right) = \tan\dfrac{x}{2}$ for every $x$, that is, $\dfrac{T}{2}$ is a period of the tangent. Therefore $\dfrac{T}{2} \geq \pi$, and the minimal period of $f$ is $2\pi$.

The value $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ is obtained by dividing the period of the tangent by $2$ instead of multiplying it.
