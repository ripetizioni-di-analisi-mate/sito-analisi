What is the least positive period of $3\sin\left(4 x + \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right)$?

- $8 \pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The argument must increase by $2\pi$ for one complete cycle. The coefficient of $x$ is 4, so the period is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
