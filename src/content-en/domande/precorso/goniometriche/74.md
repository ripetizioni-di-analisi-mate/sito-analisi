As $x$ ranges over $\mathbb{R}$, what is the minimum of $5 + 3\sin x - 4\cos x$?

- $4$
- $-20$
- $0$
- $-2$

## Explanation

With the auxiliary angle method, for $a = 3$ and $b = -4$ one has $R = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$. So there is an angle $\varphi$ such that $3\sin x - 4\cos x = 5\sin(x + \varphi)$ for every $x$.

The sine takes all values between $-1$ and $1$, so $3\sin x - 4\cos x$ takes all values between $-5$ and $5$, and the minimum of $f$ is $5 - 5 = 0$.

The value $4$ comes from giving $3\sin x - 4\cos x$ the minimum $-1$ of the sine, and $-20$ comes from forgetting the square root in $R$.
