What are the solutions of $3\cos x\sin\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=0$ in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$?

- $\left\{0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}, \pi, \dfrac{3 \pi}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}\right\}$

## Explanation

The product vanishes when the cosine is zero or the sine argument is a multiple of $\pi$. The union of the two sets gives $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{2}\right\}$.
