An angle $\alpha$ has $\sin\alpha = 0.28$. What is $\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)$?

- $-0.28$
- $0.28$
- $0.96$
- $0.72$

## Explanation

The angles $\alpha$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \alpha$ determine two points of the unit circle symmetric with respect to the bisector of the first quadrant, so the $x$-coordinate of one is the $y$-coordinate of the other.

Therefore $\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \alpha\right) = \sin\alpha = 0.28$, whatever the quadrant of $\alpha$.

The value $0.96$ is $|\cos\alpha|$, which follows from $\cos^2\alpha = 1 - 0.0784 = 0.9216$. The value $0.72$ comes from the incorrect relation $\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha = 1$.
