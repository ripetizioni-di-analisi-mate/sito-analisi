- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, $x = \dfrac{7\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

With the double-angle formula $\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x$ the equation contains only the sine and becomes
$$
2\sin^2 x + \sin x - 1 = 0 .
$$
Setting $t = \sin x$, the equation $2t^2 + t - 1 = 0$ has roots $t = \dfrac{-1 \pm 3}{4}$, that is, $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $t = -1$. Both satisfy the condition $-1 \leq t \leq 1$, so they are **acceptable**.

From $\sin x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ it follows that $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ and $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$. The sine equals $-1$ only at the lowest point of the unit circle, so from $\sin x = -1$ it follows that $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
