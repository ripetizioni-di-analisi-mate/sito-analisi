What is the value of $\tan\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$?

- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $-\sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{5\pi}{6} = \pi - \dfrac{\pi}{6}$, the identities for related angles give $\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{6} = \sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{6} = -\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

The tangent is the ratio of sine to cosine, so
$$
\tan\frac{5\pi}{6} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \left(-\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\right) = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} .
$$
The value $-\sqrt{3}$ is the tangent of $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} = \pi - \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.
