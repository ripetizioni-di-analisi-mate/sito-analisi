Two angles $x$ and $y$ have sum $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. What is $\cos x\cos y - \sin x\sin y$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

By the cosine addition formula $\cos x\cos y - \sin x\sin y = \cos(x + y)$.

Since $x + y = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, the expression equals $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, whatever $x$ and $y$ are.

The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ is $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, and the value $1$ comes from confusing the expression with $\cos^2 x + \sin^2 x$.
