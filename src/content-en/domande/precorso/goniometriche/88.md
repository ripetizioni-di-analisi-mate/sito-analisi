What is the least positive period of $4\sin\left(5 x + \dfrac{4 \pi}{5}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{5}$
- $10 \pi$
- $\dfrac{2 \pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The argument must increase by $2\pi$ for one complete cycle. The coefficient of $x$ is 5, so the period is $\dfrac{2 \pi}{5}$.
