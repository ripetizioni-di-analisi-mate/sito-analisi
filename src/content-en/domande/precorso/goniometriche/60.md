What are the solutions of $3\sin\left(x\right)=\dfrac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2}$ in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{5 \pi}{4}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right\}$

## Explanation

Dividing by 3 gives a positive sine. Of the angles with that sine, one lies in the first quadrant and one in the second, giving $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3 \pi}{4}\right\}$.
