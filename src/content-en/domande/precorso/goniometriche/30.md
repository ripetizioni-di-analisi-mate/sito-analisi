What are the solutions of the equation $\sin 3x = \sin x$?

- $x = n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

Two angles have the same sine if and only if they coincide or are supplementary, up to multiples of $2\pi$. So the equation is equivalent to
$$
3x = x + 2n\pi \quad \text{or} \quad 3x = \pi - x + 2n\pi, \qquad n \in \mathbb{Z} .
$$
From the first alternative $2x = 2n\pi$, that is, $x = n\pi$. From the second $4x = \pi + 2n\pi$, and dividing all terms by $4$ gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$.

The second family contains the angles $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ of each turn, none of which is a multiple of $\pi$, so both families are needed.
