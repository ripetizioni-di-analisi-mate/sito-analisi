For which $x \in \mathbb{R}$ is $\tan 2x$ defined?

- $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$, for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + n\pi$, for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x \neq \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$, for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$, for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

The tangent of an angle $t$ is defined when $\cos t \neq 0$, that is, for $t \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$.

With $t = 2x$ the condition becomes $2x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$. Dividing both terms by $2$ gives $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$, for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

The condition $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + n\pi$ divides only the first term by $2$ and allows $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, where $\cos 2x = \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = 0$ and therefore $\tan 2x$ is not defined.
