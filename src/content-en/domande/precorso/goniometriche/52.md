The equality $\sqrt{3}\sin x = \cos x$ holds for which $x$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{6} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

The points with $\cos x = 0$ are not solutions, because at them $\sin x = \pm 1$ and the left-hand side equals $\pm\sqrt{3}$. So one can divide by $\cos x$, and the equation becomes
$$
\tan x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} .
$$
From the table of special values $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$, and the tangent has period $\pi$, so the solutions are $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

The angle $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ has tangent $\sqrt{3}$, so it solves the equation $\sin x = \sqrt{3}\cos x$.
