In a town the temperature, in degrees Celsius, $t$ hours after midnight is $T(t) = 15 - 5\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{12}$, with $0 \leq t < 24$. At which hours of the day does the temperature exceed $17.5\ ^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$?

- $8 < t < 16$
- $0 \leq t < 8$ or $16 < t < 24$
- $4 < t < 20$
- $8 \leq t \leq 16$

## Explanation

The condition $T(t) > 17.5$ reads $-5\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{12} > 2.5$. Dividing by $-5$ reverses the inequality, so $\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{12} < -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

For $0 \leq t < 24$ the angle $\dfrac{\pi t}{12}$ ranges over $[0, 2\pi)$, and the cosine is less than $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ on the arc containing the point of angle $\pi$, that is, for $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} < \dfrac{\pi t}{12} < \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$. Multiplying by $\dfrac{12}{\pi}$ gives $8 < t < 16$, so the temperature exceeds $17.5\ ^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ between 8 a.m. and 4 p.m.

The result $0 \leq t < 8$ or $16 < t < 24$ comes from not reversing the inequality, and $4 < t < 20$ from getting the sign of $2.5$ wrong.
