- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

From the table of special values, the angle $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ has tangent equal to $\sqrt{3}$.

The tangent has period $\pi$, because adding $\pi$ to the angle changes the sign of both sine and cosine and their ratio stays the same. Hence the solutions are obtained by adding $n\pi$ to the angle $\alpha$:
$$
x = \frac{\pi}{3} + n\pi, \qquad n \in \mathbb{Z} .
$$
The family $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$ is contained in this one and does not include, for example, $x = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$, which is a solution because $\tan\dfrac{4\pi}{3} = \sqrt{3}$.
