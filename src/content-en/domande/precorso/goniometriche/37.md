What are the solutions of the equation $\sin 2x = \cos x$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$, $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$, $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

With the double-angle formula the equation becomes $2\sin x\cos x - \cos x = 0$, and factoring out $\cos x$ gives
$$
\cos x\,(2\sin x - 1) = 0 .
$$
From $\cos x = 0$ it follows that $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$. From $\sin x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ it follows that $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ and $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

Dividing by $\cos x$ would have left only the equation $\sin x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and therefore would have lost the solutions $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + n\pi$.
