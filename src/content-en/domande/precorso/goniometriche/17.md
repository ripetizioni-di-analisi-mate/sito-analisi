Given that $\cos x = -\dfrac{5}{13}$ and $\pi < x < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, what is the value of $\sin x$?

- $\dfrac{12}{13}$
- $-\dfrac{12}{13}$
- $-\dfrac{144}{169}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{13}$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean identity
$$
\sin^2 x = 1 - \cos^2 x = 1 - \frac{25}{169} = \frac{144}{169} ,
$$
so $\sin x = \dfrac{12}{13}$ or $\sin x = -\dfrac{12}{13}$.

The angle lies in the third quadrant, where the points of the unit circle have negative ordinate, so $\sin x = -\dfrac{12}{13}$.

The value $-\dfrac{144}{169}$ equals $-\sin^2 x$.
