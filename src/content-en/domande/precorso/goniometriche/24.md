How many solutions does the equation $\cos x = 1$ have in the interval $[0, 4\pi]$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The cosine equals $1$ only at the point $(1, 0)$ of the unit circle, so the solutions of the equation are $x = 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

The interval $[0, 4\pi]$, which contains both endpoints, contains the values $0$, $2\pi$ and $4\pi$. Therefore the equation has $3$ solutions.

On the graph these are the maximum points of the cosine, and two of them coincide with the endpoints of the interval.
