What is 3 times $\sin\left(\dfrac{5 \pi}{4}\right)$?

- $-3$
- $\dfrac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

## Explanation

The angle is in the third quadrant and its sine is the opposite of $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. Multiplying by 3 gives $- \dfrac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2}$.
