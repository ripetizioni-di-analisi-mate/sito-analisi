What are the solutions of $3\cos\left(x\right)=- \dfrac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2}$ in $\left[0, 2 \pi\right)$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{5 \pi}{6}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{11 \pi}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{5 \pi}{6}\right\}$

## Explanation

The required cosine is negative, so the angles lie in the second and third quadrants. Both are $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ away from $\pi$, giving $\left\{\dfrac{5 \pi}{6}, \dfrac{7 \pi}{6}\right\}$.
