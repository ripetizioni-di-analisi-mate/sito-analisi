The variable $x$ ranges over the interval $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right]$. Between which values does $\sin x$ vary?

- $\left[\dfrac{1}{2}, 1\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, 1\right]$
- $[0, 1]$

## Explanation

On the interval $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ the sine is increasing and goes from $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ to $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 1$, taking all intermediate values.

On the interval $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right]$ the sine is decreasing and goes from $1$ to $\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{4} = \sin\dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, which is greater than $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

Therefore the minimum is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, the maximum is $1$, and $\sin x$ takes all and only the values of the interval $\left[\dfrac{1}{2}, 1\right]$.

The interval $\left[\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right]$ only takes into account the values at the endpoints and ignores the maximum $1$, attained at $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
