- $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

Setting $t = \cos x$, the equation becomes $2t^2 - 7t + 3 = 0$, whose discriminant is $49 - 24 = 25$. The roots are therefore
$$
t = \frac{7 \pm 5}{4}, \qquad \text{that is,} \quad t = 3 \quad \text{and} \quad t = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
The root $t = 3$ is discarded, because the condition $-1 \leq t \leq 1$ is not satisfied.

What remains is the elementary equation $\cos x = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The angle $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ has cosine $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and the points with $x$-coordinate $\dfrac{1}{2}$ are symmetric with respect to the $x$-axis, so the solutions are $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
