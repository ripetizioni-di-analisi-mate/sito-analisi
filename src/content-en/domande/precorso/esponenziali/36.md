A radioactive substance halves every $4$ hours, so of an initial amount of $96$ mg, after $t$ hours there remain $m(t) = 96 \cdot \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{t}{4}}$ mg. For which $t \geq 0$ do less than $6$ mg remain?

- $t > 16$
- $t < 16$
- $t > 4$
- $t > 64$

## Explanation

The condition is $96 \cdot \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{t}{4}} < 6$, and dividing both sides by $96$ gives $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{t}{4}} < \dfrac{1}{16}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{16} = \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{4}$, the inequality can be written as $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{t}{4}} < \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{4}$. The base $\dfrac{1}{2}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, so in passing to the exponents the direction of the inequality is **reversed**, and we obtain $\dfrac{t}{4} > 4$. Therefore less than $6$ mg remain for $t > 16$, that is, after $16$ hours.
