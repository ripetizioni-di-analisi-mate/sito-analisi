What are the solutions of the inequality $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{x} \geq \dfrac{9}{4}$?

- $x \geq -2$
- $x \leq -2$
- $x \leq 2$
- $x \geq 2$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{9}{4} = \left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^{2} = \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-2}$, the inequality can be written as $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{x} \geq \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-2}$. The base $\dfrac{2}{3}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, so the exponential function is decreasing and, in passing to the exponents, the direction of the inequality **is reversed**. Therefore the solutions are $x \leq -2$.
