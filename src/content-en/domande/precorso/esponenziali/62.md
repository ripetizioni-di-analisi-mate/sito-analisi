The product $\left(5^{x} - 1\right)\left(5^{x} - 25\right)$ is zero for $x = 0$ and for $x = 2$. On which intervals is it positive?

- $x > 2$
- $x < 0$ or $x > 2$
- $0 < x < 2$
- $x < 1$ or $x > 25$

## Explanation

With the substitution $t = 5^{x}$, $t > 0$, the product becomes $(t - 1)(t - 25)$, a second-degree trinomial in $t$ with roots $1$ and $25$. The trinomial is positive outside the roots, so $t < 1$ or $t > 25$. Intersecting with $t > 0$ gives $0 < t < 1$ or $t > 25$, and the condition $t > 0$ is satisfied for every $x$. Since $1 = 5^{0}$, $25 = 5^{2}$ and the base $5$ is greater than $1$, from $5^{x} < 5^{0}$ it follows that $x < 0$ and from $5^{x} > 5^{2}$ it follows that $x > 2$. Therefore the solutions are $x < 0$ or $x > 2$.
