Which values satisfy $\left(3\right)^{x}\geq 81$?

- $\left(-\infty, 4\right]$
- $\left[81, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left[4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential function with base 3 is increasing and equals $81$ at $x=4$. Including the point where equality holds gives $\left[4, \infty\right)$.
