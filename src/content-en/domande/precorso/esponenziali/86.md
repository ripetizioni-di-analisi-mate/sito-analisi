Which values satisfy $\left(3\right)^{2 x}-90\cdot \left(3\right)^{x}+729<0$?

- $\left(2, 4\right)$
- $\left(9, 81\right)$
- $\left[2, 4\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The quadratic in $3^{x}$ is negative between $9$ and $81$. The exponential function is increasing, so the answer is $\left(2, 4\right)$.
