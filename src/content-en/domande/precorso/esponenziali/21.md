What are the solutions of the equation $5^{x^{2} - 3x} = \dfrac{1}{25}$?

- The equation has no solutions
- $x = -1$ and $x = -2$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 3$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 2$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{1}{25} = 5^{-2}$, the equation can be written as $5^{x^{2} - 3x} = 5^{-2}$. By the **equality of powers with the same base** it is equivalent to $x^{2} - 3x = -2$, that is, to $x^{2} - 3x + 2 = 0$. We have $x^{2} - 3x + 2 = (x - 1)(x - 2)$, therefore the solutions are $x = 1$ and $x = 2$.
