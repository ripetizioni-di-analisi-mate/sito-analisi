What is the solution of the equation $\sqrt[3]{2^{x+1}} = 4^{x}$?

- $x = -3$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $x = \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The cube root is the power with exponent $\dfrac{1}{3}$, so $\sqrt[3]{2^{x+1}} = 2^{\frac{x+1}{3}}$. Since $4^{x} = 2^{2x}$, by the **equality of powers with the same base** the equation is equivalent to
$$
\frac{x + 1}{3} = 2x .
$$
Multiplying both sides by $3$ gives $x + 1 = 6x$, that is, $5x = 1$, therefore the solution is $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$.
