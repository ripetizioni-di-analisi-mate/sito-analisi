Which expression is equal to $\dfrac{4^{x} \cdot 2^{x+1}}{8^{x}}$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $2^{x+1}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $2^{2x+1}$

## Explanation

All the powers are written in base $2$. Since $4^{x} = 2^{2x}$ and $8^{x} = 2^{3x}$, by the product rule the numerator equals $2^{2x} \cdot 2^{x+1} = 2^{3x+1}$. By the quotient rule we then have
$$
\frac{2^{3x+1}}{2^{3x}} = 2^{3x+1-3x} = 2^{1} .
$$
Hence the expression equals $2$ for every $x$.
