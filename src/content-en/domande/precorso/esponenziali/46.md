The graph of $f(x) = 2^{x-3}$ is obtained from the graph of $y = 2^{x}$ by which transformation?

- A translation of $3$ units to the left
- A translation of $3$ units downward
- A translation of $3$ units to the right
- A translation of $3$ units upward

## Explanation

For every $x_0$ we have $f(x_0 + 3) = 2^{x_0}$, so the point $\left(x_0, 2^{x_0}\right)$ of the graph of $y = 2^{x}$ corresponds to the point $\left(x_0 + 3, 2^{x_0}\right)$ of the graph of $f$. For example, the point $(0, 1)$ becomes the point $(3, 1)$, because $f(3) = 2^{0} = 1$. Therefore every point moves $3$ units to the right, and the graph of $f$ is obtained by a **horizontal translation** of $3$ units to the right.
