What is the range of $\left(3\right)^{x}+7$?

- $\left(0, \infty\right)$
- $\left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The power takes every positive value and only positive values. Adding 7 to each value gives $\left(7, \infty\right)$.
