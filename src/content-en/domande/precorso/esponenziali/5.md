- $x = \dfrac{\log_2 6 - 1}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{1 + \log_2 6}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{1 + \log_6 2}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{3} + \log_2 6$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is positive, therefore the equation $2^{3x-1} = 6$ is equivalent to
$$
3x - 1 = \log_2 6 ,
$$
because $\log_2 6$ is by definition the exponent to which $2$ must be raised to obtain $6$, and here the exponent is the whole expression $3x - 1$. We obtain a first-degree equation: adding $1$ to both sides gives $3x = 1 + \log_2 6$. Dividing both sides by $3$, the solution is $x = \dfrac{1 + \log_2 6}{3}$.
