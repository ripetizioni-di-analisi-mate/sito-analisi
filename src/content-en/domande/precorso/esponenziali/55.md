With $x \neq 0$, for which value of $x$ does $8^{\frac{1}{x}} = 2$ hold?

- $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 3$
- $x = -3$

## Explanation

The equation makes sense for $x \neq 0$. Since $8 = 2^{3}$, the left-hand side is $\left(2^{3}\right)^{\frac{1}{x}} = 2^{\frac{3}{x}}$ and the equation becomes $2^{\frac{3}{x}} = 2^{1}$. By the equality of powers with the same base it follows that $\dfrac{3}{x} = 1$, so $x = 3$, which satisfies the condition $x \neq 0$.
