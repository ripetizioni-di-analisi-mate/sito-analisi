Which of the following powers is equal to half of $4^{10}$?

- $2^{19}$
- $4^{5}$
- $2^{11}$
- $4^{9}$

## Explanation

Since $4 = 2^{2}$, by the power-of-a-power rule $4^{10} = \left(2^{2}\right)^{10} = 2^{20}$. Half of $2^{20}$ is the quotient $\dfrac{2^{20}}{2^{1}}$, and by the quotient rule $\dfrac{2^{20}}{2^{1}} = 2^{20 - 1} = 2^{19}$. Therefore half of $4^{10}$ is $2^{19}$, while $4^{5} = 2^{10}$ and $4^{9} = 2^{18}$.
