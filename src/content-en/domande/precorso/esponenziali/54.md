What is the range of $\left(5\right)^{x}+5$?

- $\left(0, \infty\right)$
- $\left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The power takes every positive value and only positive values. Adding 5 to each value gives $\left(5, \infty\right)$.
