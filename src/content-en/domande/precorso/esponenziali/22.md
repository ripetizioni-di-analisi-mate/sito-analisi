What is the solution of the equation $\left(\dfrac{1}{9}\right)^{x} = 27$?

- $x = -\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x = -\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Both sides are written as powers of $3$. Since $\dfrac{1}{9} = 3^{-2}$, the left-hand side equals $\left(3^{-2}\right)^{x} = 3^{-2x}$, while the right-hand side is $27 = 3^{3}$. By the **equality of powers with the same base** the equation is equivalent to $-2x = 3$, therefore the solution is $x = -\dfrac{3}{2}$.
