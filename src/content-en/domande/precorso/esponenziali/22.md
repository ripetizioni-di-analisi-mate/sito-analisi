What is the value of $\left(\dfrac{1}{8}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $4$
- $64$

## Explanation

By the rule $a^{-x} = \dfrac{1}{a^{x}}$, a power with a negative exponent is the reciprocal of the power with the opposite exponent. The reciprocal of a power of $\dfrac{1}{8}$ is the power of $8$ with the same exponent, so $\left(\dfrac{1}{8}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}} = 8^{\frac{2}{3}}$. Since $8 = 2^{3}$, the power-of-a-power rule gives
$$
8^{\frac{2}{3}} = \left(2^{3}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}} = 2^{2} = 4 .
$$
Therefore the required value is $4$.
