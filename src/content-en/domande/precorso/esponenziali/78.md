The number $\dfrac{2^{2024} - 2^{2022}}{2^{2022} + 2^{2021}}$ is an integer. Which one?

- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

In the numerator we factor out $2^{2022}$ and obtain $2^{2022}\left(2^{2} - 1\right) = 3 \cdot 2^{2022}$. In the denominator we factor out $2^{2021}$ and obtain $2^{2021}(2 + 1) = 3 \cdot 2^{2021}$. Therefore the quotient is
$$
\frac{3 \cdot 2^{2022}}{3 \cdot 2^{2021}} = 2^{2022 - 2021} = 2 .
$$
