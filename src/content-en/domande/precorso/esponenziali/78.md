What is the solution set of $\left(5\right)^{2 x}-3150\cdot \left(5\right)^{x}+78125=0$?

- $\left\{25, 3125\right\}$
- $\left\{2, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, -2\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$

## Explanation

Set $t = 5^{x}$ to obtain a quadratic with roots $25$ and $3125$. Returning to $x$ gives $\left\{2, 5\right\}$.
