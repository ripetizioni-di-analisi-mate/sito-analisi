Which pair $(x, y)$ solves the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
2^{x} \cdot 2^{y} = 32 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{2^{x}}{2^{y}} = 2
\end{cases}
$$

- $(x, y) = (2, 3)$
- $(x, y) = (8, 4)$
- $(x, y) = (3, 2)$
- $(x, y) = \left(\dfrac{5}{2}, \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

By the laws of exponents $2^{x} \cdot 2^{y} = 2^{x+y}$ and $\dfrac{2^{x}}{2^{y}} = 2^{x-y}$, while $32 = 2^{5}$ and $2 = 2^{1}$. By the equality of powers with the same base, the system is therefore equivalent to
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 5 \\
x - y = 1
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations gives $2x = 6$, so $x = 3$, and from the first equation it follows that $y = 2$. Therefore the solution is the pair $(3, 2)$.
