Which values satisfy $\left(2\right)^{2 x}-10\cdot \left(2\right)^{x}+16<0$?

- $\left[1, 3\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right) \cup \left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(1, 3\right)$
- $\left(2, 8\right)$

## Explanation

The quadratic in $2^{x}$ is negative between $2$ and $8$. The exponential function is increasing, so the answer is $\left(1, 3\right)$.
