What can be said about the solutions of the inequality $3^{x-2} > -9$?

- Every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- $x > -7$
- $x > 2$
- The inequality has no solutions

## Explanation

For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$ the power $3^{x-2}$ is defined and positive. Every positive number is greater than $-9$, so the inequality $3^{x-2} > -9$ holds for every $x$. Therefore the solutions are **all real numbers**.
