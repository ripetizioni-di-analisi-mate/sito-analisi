Which values satisfy $\left(3\right)^{2 x}-2268\cdot \left(3\right)^{x}+177147<0$?

- $\left(81, 2187\right)$
- $\left[4, 7\right]$
- $\left(4, 7\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right) \cup \left(7, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The quadratic in $3^{x}$ is negative between $81$ and $2187$. The exponential function is increasing, so the answer is $\left(4, 7\right)$.
