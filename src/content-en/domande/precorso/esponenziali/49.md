Exactly one of the following equations has no real solutions. Which one?

- $2^{x} = \dfrac{1}{8}$
- $3^{x} = 1$
- $4^{x} = -2$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{x} = 5$

## Explanation

Since $4^{x} > 0$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$, the power never takes the value $-2$, and the equation $4^{x} = -2$ **has no solutions**. The other three equations have a positive right-hand side, so each of them has a solution. Indeed $2^{x} = \dfrac{1}{8} = 2^{-3}$ for $x = -3$, $3^{x} = 1 = 3^{0}$ for $x = 0$, and $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{x} = 5$ for $x = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 5$.
