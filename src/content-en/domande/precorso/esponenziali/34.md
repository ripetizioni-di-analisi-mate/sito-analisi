Let $t=2^{\dfrac{x}{2}}$. Given $t+\dfrac{1}{t}=3$, find $2^x+2^{-x}$.

- $7$
- $9$
- $6$
- It cannot be deduced, because $(2^{\dfrac{x}{2}})^2$ would have exponent $\dfrac{x}{2}+2$.

## Explanation

Squaring gives $t^2+2+t^{-2}=9$. The required sum is $t^2+t^{-2}=9-2=7$.
