What are the solutions of the equation $2^{x} - 2^{-x} = \dfrac{3}{2}$?

- $x = 1$
- $x = 1$ and $x = -1$
- $x = 2$
- $x = -1$

## Explanation

By the rules of powers $2^{-x} = \dfrac{1}{2^{x}}$, so with the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t - \dfrac{1}{t} = \dfrac{3}{2}$. Multiplying both sides by $2t$, which is positive, gives
$$
2t^{2} - 3t - 2 = 0 .
$$
The solutions are $t = 2$ and $t = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, and the second one is discarded because $t > 0$. Therefore $2^{x} = 2$ gives $x = 1$.
