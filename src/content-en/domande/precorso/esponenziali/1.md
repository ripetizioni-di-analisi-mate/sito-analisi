## Explanation

The number $32$ is written as a power of $2$, because $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 32$, that is $32 = 2^{5}$. The equation therefore becomes $2^{x} = 2^{5}$, with the two powers in the same base. Since the exponential function is injective, by the **equality of powers with the same base** the exponents coincide. Therefore the solution is $x = 5$.
