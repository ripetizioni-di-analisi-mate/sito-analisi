The equation $4^{x} = 8^{\sqrt{x}}$ makes sense for $x \geq 0$. What are its solutions?

- $x = \dfrac{9}{4}$
- $x = 0$ and $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x = 0$ and $x = \dfrac{9}{4}$
- $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The equation makes sense for $x \geq 0$. Since $4^{x} = 2^{2x}$ and $8^{\sqrt{x}} = 2^{3\sqrt{x}}$, by the equality of powers with the same base the equation is equivalent to $2x = 3\sqrt{x}$. Since $x = \left(\sqrt{x}\right)^{2}$, this can be written as $\sqrt{x}\left(2\sqrt{x} - 3\right) = 0$, so $\sqrt{x} = 0$ or $\sqrt{x} = \dfrac{3}{2}$. We obtain $x = 0$ and $x = \dfrac{9}{4}$, both of which satisfy the condition $x \geq 0$.
