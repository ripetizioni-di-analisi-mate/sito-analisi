The equation $4^x=k\,2^x$ has an integer solution for just one of the following values of $k$. Which one?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac13$
- $6$, because this would give $2^3=2\cdot3$.
- $-2$

## Explanation

Since $2^x>0$, division gives $2^x=k$. Among the listed values only $\dfrac{1}{8}=2^{-3}$ is a power of two with an integer exponent.
