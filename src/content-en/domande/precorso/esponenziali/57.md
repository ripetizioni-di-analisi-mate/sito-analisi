The equation $4^{x} = 3 \cdot 2^{x}$ has exactly one solution. Which one?

- $x = \log_2 3$
- $x = \log_3 2$
- $x = \log_4 3$
- $x = 0$ and $x = \log_2 3$

## Explanation

Since $4^{x} = \left(2^{x}\right)^{2}$, with the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t^{2} = 3t$, that is, $t \, (t - 3) = 0$. The solution $t = 0$ does not satisfy the condition $t > 0$ and is discarded, because $2^{x}$ is never zero. The value $t = 3$ remains, and from $2^{x} = 3$ it follows that $x = \log_2 3$.
