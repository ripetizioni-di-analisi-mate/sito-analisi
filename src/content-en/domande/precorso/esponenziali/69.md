What is the domain of the function $f(x) = \sqrt{4^{x} - 8}$?

- $x \geq 2$
- $x > \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x \leq \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x \geq \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The square root is defined when the radicand is greater than or equal to $0$, so the domain is given by $4^{x} - 8 \geq 0$, that is, $4^{x} \geq 8$. Since $4^{x} = 2^{2x}$ and $8 = 2^{3}$, the inequality can be written as $2^{2x} \geq 2^{3}$. The base $2$ is greater than $1$, so the direction of the inequality is preserved and we obtain $2x \geq 3$. Therefore the domain is $x \geq \dfrac{3}{2}$.
