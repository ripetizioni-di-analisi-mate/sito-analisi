Which value of $x$ solves $\left(2\right)^{x}=7$?

- $\ln{\left(\dfrac{7}{2} \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(7 \right)}}{\ln{\left(2 \right)}}$
- $\ln{\left(2 \right)} \ln{\left(7 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 \right)}}{\ln{\left(7 \right)}}$

## Explanation

Taking the natural logarithm of both sides gives $x$ times the logarithm of the base. Dividing by that factor gives $\dfrac{\ln{\left(7 \right)}}{\ln{\left(2 \right)}}$.
