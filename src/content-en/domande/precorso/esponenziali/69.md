Which value of $x$ solves $\left(2\right)^{x}=7$?

- $\log{\left(\dfrac{7}{2} \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\log{\left(7 \right)}}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}$
- $\log{\left(2 \right)} \log{\left(7 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\log{\left(2 \right)}}{\log{\left(7 \right)}}$

## Explanation

Taking natural logarithms gives $x$ times the logarithm of the base. Dividing by that factor gives $\dfrac{\log{\left(7 \right)}}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}$.
