What is the solution of the equation $2^{x} + 2^{x+1} = 15$?

- $x = \log_2 15 - 1$
- $x = \log_2 5$
- $x = \dfrac{\log_2 15 - 1}{2}$
- $x = \log_5 2$

## Explanation

By the product rule $2^{x+1} = 2 \cdot 2^{x}$, so the left-hand side equals $2^{x} + 2 \cdot 2^{x} = 3 \cdot 2^{x}$. The equation becomes $3 \cdot 2^{x} = 15$, and dividing both sides by $3$ gives $2^{x} = 5$. The right-hand side is positive, therefore the solution is $x = \log_2 5$.
