For which $x$ is the product $2^{x} \cdot 3^{x}$ equal to $5$?

- $x = \log_5 6$
- $x = \log_2 \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $x = \log_2 5 + \log_3 5$
- $x = \log_6 5$

## Explanation

The two factors have the same exponent $x$, so their product is the power with base $2 \cdot 3 = 6$, that is, $2^{x} \cdot 3^{x} = 6^{x}$. The equation becomes $6^{x} = 5$, with a positive right-hand side. By the rule for an exponential equation with a constant right-hand side, the solution is therefore $x = \log_6 5$.
