Into which equation is $9^{x} - 2 \cdot 3^{x+1} + 5 = 0$ transformed by the substitution $t = 3^{x}$?

- $t^{2} - 6t + 5 = 0$
- $t^{2} - 2t + 5 = 0$
- $t^{2} - 2t - 1 = 0$
- $t^{3} - 6t + 5 = 0$

## Explanation

Since $9^{x} = (3^{2})^{x} = (3^{x})^{2}$, the first term becomes $t^{2}$. By the product rule $3^{x+1} = 3 \cdot 3^{x}$, so $2 \cdot 3^{x+1} = 6 \cdot 3^{x} = 6t$. Therefore the equation becomes $t^{2} - 6t + 5 = 0$, with the condition $t > 0$. Its solutions $t = 1$ and $t = 5$ are positive, hence both acceptable.
