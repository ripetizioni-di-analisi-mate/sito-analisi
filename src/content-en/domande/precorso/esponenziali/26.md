What is the solution of the equation $e^{x} = 5$, where $e$ is Euler's number?

- $x = \ln 5$
- $x = \log_5 e$
- $x = \dfrac{5}{e}$
- $x = e^{5}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side $5$ is positive, so the equation has a solution. By the rule for an exponential equation with a constant right-hand side, $e^{x} = 5$ gives $x = \log_e 5$. The logarithm to base $e$ is the **natural logarithm** and is denoted by $\ln$, therefore the solution is $x = \ln 5$, the exponent to which $e$ must be raised to obtain $5$.
