A quantity is multiplied by $e^k$ every hour. It rises from 7 to 35 in three hours. Which value of $k$ fits both observations?

- $\dfrac{\ln 5}{3}$
- $3\ln 5$
- $\ln 28$
- $\dfrac{\ln 35}{3}$

## Explanation

The overall factor is $\dfrac{35}{7}=5$, so $e^{3k}=5$. Taking natural logarithms gives $3k=\ln 5$.
