What is the solution of the equation $5^{x+1} = 3$?

- $x = \log_5 3 - 1$
- $x = \log_5 3 + 1$
- $x = \log_3 5 - 1$
- $x = \log_5 2$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is positive, so the equation is solved with the logarithm in base $5$, which gives the value of the whole exponent $x + 1$. This yields the linear equation $x + 1 = \log_5 3$. Subtracting $1$ from both sides, the solution is $x = \log_5 3 - 1$.
