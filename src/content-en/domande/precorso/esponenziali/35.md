Written in terms of $t = 2^{x}$, the equation $8^{x} - 3 \cdot 4^{x} + 2 \cdot 2^{x} = 0$ is of third degree. What are its solutions in $x$?

- $x = 1$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 1$
- $x = 0$, $x = 1$ and $x = 2$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 2$

## Explanation

Since $8^{x} = \left(2^{x}\right)^{3}$ and $4^{x} = \left(2^{x}\right)^{2}$, with the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes
$$
t^{3} - 3t^{2} + 2t = t \, (t - 1)(t - 2) = 0 .
$$
The solutions in $t$ are $t = 0$, $t = 1$ and $t = 2$. The value $t = 0$ does not satisfy the condition $t > 0$, so it is discarded. From $2^{x} = 1 = 2^{0}$ it follows that $x = 0$, and from $2^{x} = 2$ it follows that $x = 1$, therefore the solutions are $x = 0$ and $x = 1$.
