What is the solution set of $\left(5\right)^{2 x - 2} = 3125$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{7}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is 5 to the power 5. Equating the exponents gives $\left\{\dfrac{7}{2}\right\}$.
