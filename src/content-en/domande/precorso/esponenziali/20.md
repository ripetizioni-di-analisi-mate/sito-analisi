What is the solution of the equation $2^{x} \cdot 4^{x} = 64$?

- $x = 3$
- $x = 2$
- $x = 6$
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

Since $4^{x} = (2^{2})^{x} = 2^{2x}$, by the product rule the left-hand side equals $2^{x} \cdot 2^{2x} = 2^{3x}$. The right-hand side is written as $64 = 2^{6}$, so the equation becomes $2^{3x} = 2^{6}$. By the **equality of powers with the same base** we have $3x = 6$, therefore the solution is $x = 2$.
