What is the range of $\left(2\right)^{x}+6$?

- $\left(-\infty, 6\right)$
- $\left(0, \infty\right)$
- $\left(6, \infty\right)$
- $\left[6, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The power takes every positive value and only positive values. Adding 6 to each value gives $\left(6, \infty\right)$.
