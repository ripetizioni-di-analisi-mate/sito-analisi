What is the solution set of $\left(2\right)^{2 x}-72\cdot \left(2\right)^{x}+512=0$?

- $\left\{3, 6\right\}$
- $\left\{9\right\}$
- $\left\{-6, -3\right\}$
- $\left\{8, 64\right\}$

## Explanation

Set $t = 2^{x}$ to obtain a quadratic with roots $8$ and $64$. Returning to $x$ gives $\left\{3, 6\right\}$.
