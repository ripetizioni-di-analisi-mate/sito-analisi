Setting $t = 3^{x}$, which expression in $t$ is equal to $9^{x} + 3^{-x}$?

- $t^{2} + t$
- $3t + \dfrac{1}{t}$
- $t^{2} - t$
- $t^{2} + \dfrac{1}{t}$

## Explanation

By the power-of-a-power rule $9^{x} = \left(3^{2}\right)^{x} = \left(3^{x}\right)^{2} = t^{2}$. By the rule for negative exponents $3^{-x} = \dfrac{1}{3^{x}} = \dfrac{1}{t}$. Therefore the expression is equal to $t^{2} + \dfrac{1}{t}$.
