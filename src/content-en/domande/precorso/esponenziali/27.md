Applying the natural logarithm to both sides, which value of $x$ solves $2^{x} = 3^{x-1}$?

- $x = \dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 2 - \ln 3}$
- $x = \dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 3 - \ln 2}$
- $x = \ln 3 \, (\ln 3 - \ln 2)$
- $x = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 3 - \ln 2}$

## Explanation

Both sides are positive for every $x$, so the natural logarithm can be applied to both. By the property of the logarithm of a power we obtain
$$
x \ln 2 = (x - 1) \ln 3 = x \ln 3 - \ln 3 .
$$
Moving the terms with $x$ to the right-hand side and $-\ln 3$ to the left-hand side gives $\ln 3 = x \ln 3 - x \ln 2 = x \, (\ln 3 - \ln 2)$. Since $\ln 3 - \ln 2 = \ln \dfrac{3}{2} > 0$, both sides are divided by this number, and the solution is $x = \dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 3 - \ln 2}$.
