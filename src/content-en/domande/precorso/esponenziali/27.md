What is the solution of the equation $3^{2x} = 7$?

- $x = 2 \log_3 7$
- $x = \log_3 7 - 2$
- $x = \dfrac{\log_3 7}{2}$
- $x = \log_3 \dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side $7$ is positive and is not a power of $3$ with a recognizable rational exponent, so the equation is solved with the logarithm in base $3$. Therefore the exponent $2x$ equals $\log_3 7$. Dividing both sides of $2x = \log_3 7$ by $2$, the solution is $x = \dfrac{\log_3 7}{2}$.
