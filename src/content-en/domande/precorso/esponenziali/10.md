- $x \geq 1$
- $x \leq 1$
- $x > 1$
- $x \geq \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $125 = 5^{3}$, the inequality can be written as $5^{2x+1} \geq 5^{3}$. The base $5$ is greater than $1$, therefore the exponential function is increasing and the direction **is preserved** when passing to the exponents. The inequality is therefore equivalent to $2x + 1 \geq 3$, that is $2x \geq 2$. The solutions are $x \geq 1$, with the endpoint $x = 1$ included because the symbol is $\geq$.
