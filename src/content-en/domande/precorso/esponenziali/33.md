What are the solutions of the equation $4^{x} - 2^{x+2} + 4 = 0$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 0$
- The equation has no solutions
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

By the rules of powers $4^{x} = (2^{x})^{2}$ and $2^{x+2} = 4 \cdot 2^{x}$. With the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t^{2} - 4t + 4 = 0$, that is, $(t - 2)^{2} = 0$. The only solution is $t = 2$, which is positive and hence acceptable. Therefore $2^{x} = 2$ gives $x = 1$.
