Which value of $x$ solves $\left(3\right)^{x}=13$?

- $\dfrac{\log{\left(3 \right)}}{\log{\left(13 \right)}}$
- $\log{\left(3 \right)} \log{\left(13 \right)}$
- $\log{\left(\dfrac{13}{3} \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\log{\left(13 \right)}}{\log{\left(3 \right)}}$

## Explanation

Taking natural logarithms gives $x$ times the logarithm of the base. Dividing by that factor gives $\dfrac{\log{\left(13 \right)}}{\log{\left(3 \right)}}$.
