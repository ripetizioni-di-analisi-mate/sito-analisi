Which values satisfy $\left(2\right)^{2 x}-72\cdot \left(2\right)^{x}+512<0$?

- $\left[3, 6\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right) \cup \left(6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(8, 64\right)$
- $\left(3, 6\right)$

## Explanation

The quadratic in $2^{x}$ is negative between $8$ and $64$. The exponential is increasing, so the answer is $\left(3, 6\right)$.
