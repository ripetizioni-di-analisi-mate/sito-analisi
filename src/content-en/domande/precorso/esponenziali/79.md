Which values satisfy $\left(5\right)^{x}\geq 25$?

- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right]$
- $\left[25, \infty\right)$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential function with base 5 is increasing and equals $25$ at $x=2$. Including the point where equality holds gives $\left[2, \infty\right)$.
