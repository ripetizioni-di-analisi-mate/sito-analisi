- $1 \leq x \leq 3$
- $0 \leq x \leq \log_2 3$
- $x \leq 0$ or $x \geq \log_2 3$
- $0 \leq x \leq \log_3 2$

## Explanation

Since $2^{x+2} = 4 \cdot 2^{x}$ and $4^{x} = (2^{x})^{2}$, with the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the inequality becomes $t^{2} - 4t + 3 \leq 0$. The trinomial has roots $t = 1$ and $t = 3$ and is negative or zero between the roots, therefore $1 \leq t \leq 3$, an interval already contained in $t > 0$. We return to the variable $x$ with $1 \leq 2^{x} \leq 3$. The base $2$ is greater than $1$, so the direction is preserved and, since $1 = 2^{0}$ and $3 = 2^{\log_2 3}$, the solutions are $0 \leq x \leq \log_2 3$.
