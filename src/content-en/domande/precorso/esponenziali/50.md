Solving an exponential equation with the substitution $t = 2^{x}$ leads to an equation in $t$ whose solutions are $t = 8$ and $t = -2$. What are the solutions of the exponential equation?

- $x = 8$ and $x = -2$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 3$ and $x = -1$
- $x = 3$

## Explanation

The variable $t = 2^{x}$ is positive for every $x$, so the value $t = -2$ does not satisfy the condition $t > 0$ and is discarded. The value $t = 8$ remains, and we return to the variable $x$ with the equation $2^{x} = 8 = 2^{3}$. By the equality of powers with the same base it follows that $x = 3$, which is the only solution.
