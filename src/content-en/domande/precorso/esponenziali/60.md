What is the solution set of $\left(3\right)^{2 x}-90\cdot \left(3\right)^{x}+729=0$?

- $\left\{9, 81\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, -2\right\}$
- $\left\{6\right\}$
- $\left\{2, 4\right\}$

## Explanation

Set $t = 3^{x}$ to obtain a quadratic with roots $9$ and $81$. Returning to $x$ gives $\left\{2, 4\right\}$.
