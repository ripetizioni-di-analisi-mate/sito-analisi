The common factor is factored out of the expression $5 \cdot 5^{x} - 5^{x}$. What is obtained?

- $20^{x}$
- $5$
- $4 \cdot 5^{x}$
- $4^{x}$

## Explanation

Factoring out the common factor $5^{x}$ gives $5 \cdot 5^{x} - 5^{x} = (5 - 1) \cdot 5^{x} = 4 \cdot 5^{x}$. The coefficient $4$ multiplies the power and is not part of the base, so $4 \cdot 5^{x}$ is different from $20^{x}$. For example, for $x = 2$ we have $4 \cdot 5^{2} = 100$, while $20^{2} = 400$.
