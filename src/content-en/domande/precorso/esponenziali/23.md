What is the solution of the equation $2^{x+3} - 2^{x} = 56$?

- $x = \log_2 7$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 3$
- $x = 2$

## Explanation

By the product rule $2^{x+3} = 2^{3} \cdot 2^{x} = 8 \cdot 2^{x}$, so, factoring out $2^{x}$, the left-hand side equals $8 \cdot 2^{x} - 2^{x} = 7 \cdot 2^{x}$. The equation becomes $7 \cdot 2^{x} = 56$, and dividing both sides by $7$ gives $2^{x} = 8 = 2^{3}$. By the **equality of powers with the same base** the solution is $x = 3$.
