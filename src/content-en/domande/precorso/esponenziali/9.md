- $x = 1$ and $x = 4$
- $x = 0$
- $x = 2$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 2$

## Explanation

By the rules of powers $2^{2-x} = 2^{2} \cdot 2^{-x} = \dfrac{4}{2^{x}}$. With the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t + \dfrac{4}{t} = 5$, and multiplying both sides by $t$, which is positive, we obtain
$$
t^{2} - 5t + 4 = 0 .
$$
The solutions are $t = 1$ and $t = 4$, both positive and therefore acceptable. From $2^{x} = 1$ it follows that $x = 0$, and from $2^{x} = 4 = 2^{2}$ it follows that $x = 2$.
