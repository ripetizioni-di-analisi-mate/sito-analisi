A cup of coffee at $80\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is left in a room at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, and after $t$ minutes its temperature is $T(t) = 20 + 60\, e^{-\frac{t}{10}}$ degrees. After how many minutes is the temperature of the coffee $50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$?

- $t = 10 \ln \dfrac{6}{5}$
- $t = \dfrac{\ln 2}{10}$
- $t = 10 \ln 2$
- $t = 10 \ln \dfrac{8}{5}$

## Explanation

The condition is $20 + 60\, e^{-\frac{t}{10}} = 50$. Subtracting $20$ from both sides gives $60\, e^{-\frac{t}{10}} = 30$, and dividing by $60$ gives $e^{-\frac{t}{10}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The right-hand side is positive, so, applying the natural logarithm to both sides, $-\dfrac{t}{10} = \ln \dfrac{1}{2} = -\ln 2$. Therefore, multiplying both sides by $-10$, we obtain $t = 10 \ln 2$, that is, about $6.9$ minutes.
