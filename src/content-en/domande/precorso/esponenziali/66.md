What is the solution set of $\left(2\right)^{2 x}-10\cdot \left(2\right)^{x}+16=0$?

- $\left\{-3, -1\right\}$
- $\left\{1, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{2, 8\right\}$
- $\left\{4\right\}$

## Explanation

Set $t = 2^{x}$ to obtain a quadratic with roots $2$ and $8$. Returning to $x$ gives $\left\{1, 3\right\}$.
