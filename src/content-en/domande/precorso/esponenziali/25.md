What are the solutions of the equation $3^{x} = 2^{x}$?

- Every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- $x = 1$
- The equation has no solutions
- $x = 0$

## Explanation

Since $2^{x} > 0$ for every $x$, both sides can be divided by $2^{x}$. By the rules of powers $\dfrac{3^{x}}{2^{x}} = \left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^{x}$, so the equation is equivalent to $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^{x} = 1 = \left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^{0}$. By the **equality of powers with the same base** the only solution is therefore $x = 0$.
