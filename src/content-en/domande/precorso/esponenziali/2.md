- $x = -1$
- $x = 2$
- The equation has no solutions
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

Since $27 = 3^{3}$, by the power of a power rule we have $27^{x} = (3^{3})^{x} = 3^{3x}$. The equation becomes $3^{x+2} = 3^{3x}$, and by the **equality of powers with the same base** it is equivalent to $x + 2 = 3x$. From this we obtain $2x = 2$, therefore the solution is $x = 1$.
