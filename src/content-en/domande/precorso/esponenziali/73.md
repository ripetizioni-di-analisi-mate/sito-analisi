Which value of $x$ solves $\left(5\right)^{x}=26$?

- $\ln{\left(5 \right)} \ln{\left(26 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(26 \right)}}{\ln{\left(5 \right)}}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(5 \right)}}{\ln{\left(26 \right)}}$
- $\ln{\left(\dfrac{26}{5} \right)}$

## Explanation

Taking the natural logarithm of both sides gives $x$ times the logarithm of the base. Dividing by that factor gives $\dfrac{\ln{\left(26 \right)}}{\ln{\left(5 \right)}}$.
