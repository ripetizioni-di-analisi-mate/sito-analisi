A colony of bacteria doubles every $20$ minutes, so starting from $500$ bacteria, after $t$ minutes it counts $N(t) = 500 \cdot 2^{\frac{t}{20}}$ bacteria. After how many minutes are there $8000$ bacteria?

- $80$
- $160$
- $4$
- $320$

## Explanation

We solve the equation $500 \cdot 2^{\frac{t}{20}} = 8000$, which after dividing by $500$ becomes $2^{\frac{t}{20}} = 16 = 2^{4}$. By the equality of powers with the same base it follows that $\dfrac{t}{20} = 4$, so $t = 80$. There are $8000$ bacteria after $80$ minutes, that is, after four doublings.
