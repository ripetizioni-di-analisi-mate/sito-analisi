What is the solution set of $\left(2\right)^{2 x - 3} = 64$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{9}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{9\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is 2 to the power 6. Equating the exponents gives $\left\{\dfrac{9}{2}\right\}$.
