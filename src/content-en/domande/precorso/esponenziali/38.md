What are the solutions of the inequality $2^{x^{2}} < 16$?

- $-2 < x < 2$
- $x < 2$
- $x < -2$ or $x > 2$
- $-4 < x < 4$

## Explanation

Since $16 = 2^{4}$, the inequality can be written as $2^{x^{2}} < 2^{4}$. The base $2$ is greater than $1$, so the direction **is preserved** and the inequality is equivalent to $x^{2} < 4$. The trinomial $x^{2} - 4$ is negative between the roots $-2$ and $2$, therefore the solutions are $-2 < x < 2$.
