Which values satisfy $\left(2\right)^{x}\geq 2$?

- $\left(1, \infty\right)$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right]$
- $\left[1, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential function with base 2 is increasing and equals $2$ at $x=1$. Including the point where equality holds gives $\left[1, \infty\right)$.
