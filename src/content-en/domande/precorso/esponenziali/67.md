Denoting by $\log_{10}$ the logarithm to base $10$, what is the solution of the equation $7^{x} = 10$?

- $x = \log_{10} 7$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{\log_{10} 7}$
- $x = \log_{10} \dfrac{10}{7}$
- $x = \dfrac{10}{7}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is positive, so the solution is $x = \log_7 10$. By the change-of-base formula, since $\log_{10} 10 = 1$, we have
$$
\log_7 10 = \frac{\log_{10} 10}{\log_{10} 7} = \frac{1}{\log_{10} 7} .
$$
Therefore the solution is $x = \dfrac{1}{\log_{10} 7}$.
