For the equation $2^t=7$, the bounds $1.94<\ln 7<1.95$ and $0.69<\ln 2<0.70$ are known. Which interval is guaranteed to contain the solution?

- $2.70<t<2.75$
- $2.77<t<2.83$
- $2.85<t<2.90$
- $0.35<t<0.37$

## Explanation

Divide $\ln7$ by $\ln2$, which is positive. The quotient exceeds $\dfrac{1.94}{0.70}>2.77$ and is less than $\dfrac{1.95}{0.69}<2.83$. The larger denominator gives the lower bound.
