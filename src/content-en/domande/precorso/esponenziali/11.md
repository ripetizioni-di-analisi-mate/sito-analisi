- $x < -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x > \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x < \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

We write $9$ as a power of $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Since $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{-2} = 3^{2} = 9$, the inequality becomes
$$
\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2x-1} < \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{-2} .
$$
The base $\dfrac{1}{3}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, therefore the exponential function is decreasing and the direction **is reversed** when passing to the exponents. The inequality is therefore equivalent to $2x - 1 > -2$, that is $2x > -1$, and the solutions are $x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
