- $x = 2$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -3$
- $x = 1$
- $x = \log_2 3$

## Explanation

Since $4^{x} = (2^{x})^{2}$, with the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t^{2} + t - 6 = 0$. We have $t^{2} + t - 6 = (t + 3)(t - 2)$, therefore the solutions are $t = -3$ and $t = 2$. Since $2^{x} > 0$ for every $x$, the value $t = -3$ **is discarded**. What remains is $2^{x} = 2$, so the solution is $x = 1$.
