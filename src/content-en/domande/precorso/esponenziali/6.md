- $x = \log_{21} 4$
- $x = \log_3 \dfrac{7}{4}$
- $x = \dfrac{\log_3 4}{7}$
- $x = \log_3 \dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

The coefficient $7$ multiplies the power $3^{x}$, therefore to isolate the power both sides are divided by $7$, giving $3^{x} = \dfrac{4}{7}$. The right-hand side is positive, so the equation is solved with the logarithm in base $3$. The solution is $x = \log_3 \dfrac{4}{7}$, which is a negative number because $\dfrac{4}{7} < 1$.
