For which $x \in \mathbb{R}$ does the inequality $2^{x} > 3^{x}$ hold?

- $x > 0$
- $x < 0$
- For every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- For no $x \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

Since $3^{x} > 0$ for every $x$, both sides can be divided by $3^{x}$ without changing the direction. By the rules of powers $\dfrac{2^{x}}{3^{x}} = \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{x}$, so the inequality is equivalent to $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{x} > 1 = \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{0}$. The base $\dfrac{2}{3}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, therefore the direction **is reversed** and the solutions are $x < 0$.
