At which points of its domain does the function $f(x) = 3^{\frac{x+1}{x}}$ take values less than or equal to $9$?

- $x \geq 1$
- $x < 0$ or $x \geq 1$
- $0 < x \leq 1$
- $x \leq 0$ or $x \geq 1$

## Explanation

The exponent $\dfrac{x+1}{x}$ is defined for $x \neq 0$, so the domain of $f$ consists of the real numbers other than $0$. Since $9 = 3^{2}$, the condition $f(x) \leq 9$ can be written as $3^{\frac{x+1}{x}} \leq 3^{2}$. The base $3$ is greater than $1$, so in passing to the exponents the direction of the inequality is preserved and we obtain $\dfrac{x+1}{x} \leq 2$. The denominator $x$ can be positive or negative, therefore $2$ is moved to the left-hand side instead of multiplying by $x$, and we obtain
$$
\frac{x + 1 - 2x}{x} = \frac{1 - x}{x} \leq 0 .
$$
The numerator is positive for $x < 1$ and the denominator is positive for $x > 0$, so the fraction is negative for $x < 0$ and for $x > 1$, positive for $0 < x < 1$, and zero for $x = 1$. Therefore the solutions are $x < 0$ or $x \geq 1$.
