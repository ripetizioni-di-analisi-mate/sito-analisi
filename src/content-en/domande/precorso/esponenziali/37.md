What are the solutions of the inequality $3^{x} > 5$?

- $x < \log_3 5$
- $x > \log_5 3$
- $x > \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $x > \log_3 5$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is positive, so it is written as a power of $3$ by means of the logarithm, $5 = 3^{\log_3 5}$. The inequality becomes $3^{x} > 3^{\log_3 5}$. The base $3$ is greater than $1$, therefore in passing to the exponents the direction **is preserved** and the solutions are $x > \log_3 5$.
