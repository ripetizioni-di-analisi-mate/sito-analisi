The inequality $8^{x} - 4^{x} - 2 \cdot 2^{x} \geq 0$ contains three powers of $2$. What are its solutions?

- $x \geq 2$
- $x > 1$
- $x \leq 1$
- $x \geq 1$

## Explanation

Since $8^{x} = \left(2^{x}\right)^{3}$ and $4^{x} = \left(2^{x}\right)^{2}$, with the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the inequality becomes $t^{3} - t^{2} - 2t \geq 0$. Factoring out $t$ and factoring the trinomial gives
$$
t \, (t - 2)(t + 1) \geq 0 .
$$
For $t > 0$ the factors $t$ and $t + 1$ are positive, so the product has the same sign as $t - 2$ and the inequality is equivalent to $t \geq 2$. From $2^{x} \geq 2^{1}$, since the base $2$ is greater than $1$, it follows that $x \geq 1$.
