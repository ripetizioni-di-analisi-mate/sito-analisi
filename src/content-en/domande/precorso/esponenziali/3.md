- $x = \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $x = 3$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $x = -\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Both bases can be written as powers of $2$. Since $\dfrac{1}{4} = 2^{-2}$, the left-hand side equals $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{x} = 2^{-2x}$; since $8 = 2^{3}$, the right-hand side equals $8^{x-1} = 2^{3(x-1)} = 2^{3x-3}$, with the factor $3$ multiplying the term $-1$ as well. By the **equality of powers with the same base** the equation is therefore equivalent to $-2x = 3x - 3$. From this we obtain $-5x = -3$, therefore the solution is $x = \dfrac{3}{5}$.
