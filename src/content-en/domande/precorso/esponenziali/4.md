- $x = \log_2 5$
- $x = \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $x = \log_5 2$
- $x = \sqrt[5]{2}$

## Explanation

The number $2$ is not a power of $5$ with a recognizable rational exponent, therefore the equation is solved with the logarithm. By definition, the logarithm $\log_5 2$ is the exponent to which $5$ must be raised to obtain $2$, that is $5^{\log_5 2} = 2$. Therefore the equation $5^{x} = 2$ has the unique solution $x = \log_5 2$. The base of the logarithm is the base of the power, while the argument is the right-hand side.
