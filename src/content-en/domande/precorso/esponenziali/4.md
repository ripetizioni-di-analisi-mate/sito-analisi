Two clocks use different step sizes. The first reads $x$ when $5^x=2$. The second reads $s$ when $2^s=5$. What is the exact relationship between their readings?

- $x=s$
- $x+s=1$
- $xs=1$
- $x-s=1$

## Explanation

The readings are $x=\log_5 2$ and $s=\log_2 5$. Using a common base gives $xs=\dfrac{\ln2}{\ln5}\dfrac{\ln5}{\ln2}=1$. Thus the second reading is the reciprocal of the first.
