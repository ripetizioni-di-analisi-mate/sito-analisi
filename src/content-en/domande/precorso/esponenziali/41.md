What are the solutions of the following system of inequalities?
$$
\begin{cases}
3^{x} > 1 \\
3^{x} \leq 9
\end{cases}
$$

- $1 < x \leq 9$
- $0 \leq x \leq 2$
- $x \leq 2$
- $0 < x \leq 2$

## Explanation

Since $1 = 3^{0}$ and $9 = 3^{2}$, the two inequalities can be written as $3^{x} > 3^{0}$ and $3^{x} \leq 3^{2}$. The base $3$ is greater than $1$, so in both the direction is preserved and we obtain $x > 0$ and $x \leq 2$. The solutions of the system are the values that satisfy both conditions, therefore they are $0 < x \leq 2$, with the endpoint $0$ excluded because the first inequality is strict.
