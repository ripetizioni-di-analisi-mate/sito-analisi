What are the solutions of the equation $4^{x} + 3 \cdot 2^{x} + 2 = 0$?

- $x = -1$ and $x = -2$
- The equation has no solutions
- $x = 0$ and $x = 1$
- $x = 0$

## Explanation

Since $4^{x} = (2^{x})^{2}$, with the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t^{2} + 3t + 2 = 0$. We have $t^{2} + 3t + 2 = (t + 1)(t + 2)$, so the solutions in $t$ are $t = -1$ and $t = -2$. Both are negative and are discarded, because $2^{x} > 0$ for every $x$. Therefore the equation **has no solutions**.
