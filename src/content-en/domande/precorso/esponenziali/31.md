What are the solutions of the equation $9^{x} - 5 \cdot 3^{x} + 6 = 0$?

- $x = 2$ and $x = 3$
- $x = \log_2 3$ and $x = 1$
- $x = \log_3 2$ and $x = 1$
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

Since $9^{x} = (3^{x})^{2}$, with the substitution $t = 3^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t^{2} - 5t + 6 = 0$. We have $t^{2} - 5t + 6 = (t - 2)(t - 3)$, so $t = 2$ or $t = 3$, both positive and acceptable. From $3^{x} = 3$ we get $x = 1$. The number $2$ is not a power of $3$ with a recognizable rational exponent, therefore $3^{x} = 2$ gives $x = \log_3 2$.
