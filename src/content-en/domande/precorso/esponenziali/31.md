A monic quadratic in $t=2^x$ must give exactly the two real solutions $x=-1$ and $x=2$. What is its constant term?

- $8$, dropping the negative exponent sign in $2^{-1}$ and multiplying $2$ by $4$.
- $\dfrac92$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The two values of $t$ are $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $4$. The constant term of $(t-\dfrac{1}{2})(t-4)$ is their product, namely $2$.
