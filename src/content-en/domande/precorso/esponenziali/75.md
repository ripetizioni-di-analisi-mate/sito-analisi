For which $x$ is the power $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{x}$ less than $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{x^{2}}$?

- $0 < x < 1$
- $x < 0$ or $x > 1$
- $0 \leq x \leq 1$
- $x > 1$

## Explanation

The base $\dfrac{1}{2}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, so in passing to the exponents the direction of the inequality is **reversed** and the inequality is equivalent to $x > x^{2}$, that is, to $x^{2} - x < 0$. The trinomial $x^{2} - x = x \, (x - 1)$ has roots $0$ and $1$ and is negative between the roots. Therefore the solutions are $0 < x < 1$.
