What is the solution set of $\left(3\right)^{2 x}-2268\cdot \left(3\right)^{x}+177147=0$?

- $\left\{-7, -4\right\}$
- $\left\{81, 2187\right\}$
- $\left\{11\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 7\right\}$

## Explanation

Set $t = 3^{x}$ to obtain a quadratic with roots $81$ and $2187$. Returning to $x$ gives $\left\{4, 7\right\}$.
