To which power of $2$ is the product $\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt[3]{2}$ equal?

- $2^{\frac{1}{6}}$
- $2^{\frac{2}{5}}$
- $2^{5}$
- $2^{\frac{5}{6}}$

## Explanation

The $n$-th root of $2$ is the power of $2$ with exponent $\dfrac{1}{n}$, so $\sqrt{2} = 2^{\frac{1}{2}}$ and $\sqrt[3]{2} = 2^{\frac{1}{3}}$. In the product of two powers with the same base the exponents are added, and $\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{5}{6}$. Therefore $\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt[3]{2} = 2^{\frac{5}{6}}$.
