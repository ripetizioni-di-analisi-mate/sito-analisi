- $x = 0$ and $x = 2$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 9$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 3$
- $x = 2$

## Explanation

Since $9^{x} = (3^{x})^{2}$, with the **substitution method** $t = 3^{x}$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes
$$
t^{2} - 10t + 9 = 0 .
$$
The solutions are $t = 1$ and $t = 9$, both positive and therefore acceptable. The values found are those of $t$, so we return to the variable $x$. From $3^{x} = 1 = 3^{0}$ it follows that $x = 0$, and from $3^{x} = 9 = 3^{2}$ it follows that $x = 2$.
