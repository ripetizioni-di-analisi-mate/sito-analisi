For which $x$ is the inequality $2^{x}\left(2^{x} - 4\right) \leq 0$ satisfied?

- $0 \leq x \leq 2$
- $x \leq 2$
- $x \leq 4$
- $0 < x \leq 4$

## Explanation

The factor $2^{x}$ is positive for every $x$, so the product has the same sign as $2^{x} - 4$. Dividing both sides by $2^{x}$, the inequality is therefore equivalent to $2^{x} - 4 \leq 0$, that is, to $2^{x} \leq 2^{2}$. The base $2$ is greater than $1$, so in passing to the exponents the direction of the inequality is preserved and the solutions are $x \leq 2$.
