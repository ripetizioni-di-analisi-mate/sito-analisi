What is the solution set of $\left(3\right)^{2 x - 4} = 2187$?

- $\left\{11\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{11}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is 3 to the power 7. Equating the exponents gives $\left\{\dfrac{11}{2}\right\}$.
