Which value of $x$ solves $\left(3\right)^{x}=22$?

- $\log{\left(\dfrac{22}{3} \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\log{\left(22 \right)}}{\log{\left(3 \right)}}$
- $\log{\left(3 \right)} \log{\left(22 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\log{\left(3 \right)}}{\log{\left(22 \right)}}$

## Explanation

Taking the natural logarithm of both sides gives $x$ times the logarithm of the base. Dividing by that factor gives $\dfrac{\log{\left(22 \right)}}{\log{\left(3 \right)}}$.
