Which of the following equalities holds for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $2^{x} + 2^{x} = 4^{x}$
- $\left(2^{x}\right)^{2} = 2^{x^{2}}$
- $2^{x} \cdot 3^{x} = 6^{x}$
- $2^{x} \cdot 2^{x} = 4^{2x}$

## Explanation

The product of two powers with the same exponent is the power of the product of the bases, so $2^{x} \cdot 3^{x} = (2 \cdot 3)^{x} = 6^{x}$ for every $x$. The other three equalities do not hold for $x = 3$. Indeed $2^{3} + 2^{3} = 16$ and $4^{3} = 64$, then $\left(2^{3}\right)^{2} = 2^{6}$ and $2^{3^{2}} = 2^{9}$, and finally $2^{3} \cdot 2^{3} = 64$ and $4^{6} = 4096$. The correct equalities are $2^{x} + 2^{x} = 2^{x+1}$, $\left(2^{x}\right)^{2} = 2^{2x}$ and $2^{x} \cdot 2^{x} = 4^{x}$.
