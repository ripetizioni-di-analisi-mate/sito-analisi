Which value of $x$ solves $\left(3\right)^{x}=22$?

- $\ln{\left(\dfrac{22}{3} \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(22 \right)}}{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}$
- $\ln{\left(3 \right)} \ln{\left(22 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}{\ln{\left(22 \right)}}$

## Explanation

Taking the natural logarithm of both sides gives $x$ times the logarithm of the base. Dividing by that factor gives $\dfrac{\ln{\left(22 \right)}}{\ln{\left(3 \right)}}$.
