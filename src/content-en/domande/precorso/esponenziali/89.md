Which values satisfy $\left(5\right)^{2 x}-3150\cdot \left(5\right)^{x}+78125<0$?

- $\left[2, 5\right]$
- $\left(25, 3125\right)$
- $\left(2, 5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right) \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The quadratic in $5^{x}$ is negative between $25$ and $3125$. The exponential is increasing, so the answer is $\left(2, 5\right)$.
