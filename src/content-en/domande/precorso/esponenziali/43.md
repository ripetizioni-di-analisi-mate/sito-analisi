On which interval is the sum $3^{x} + 3^{1-x}$ less than $4$?

- $1 < x < 3$
- $x < 0$ or $x > 1$
- $0 \leq x \leq 1$
- $0 < x < 1$

## Explanation

Since $3^{1-x} = \dfrac{3}{3^{x}}$, with the substitution $t = 3^{x}$, $t > 0$, the inequality becomes $t + \dfrac{3}{t} < 4$. The number $t$ is positive, so multiplying both sides by $t$ preserves the direction of the inequality and gives $t^{2} - 4t + 3 < 0$. The trinomial has roots $t = 1$ and $t = 3$ and is negative between the roots, therefore $1 < t < 3$, an interval contained in $t > 0$. From $3^{0} < 3^{x} < 3^{1}$, since the base $3$ is greater than $1$, it follows that $0 < x < 1$.
