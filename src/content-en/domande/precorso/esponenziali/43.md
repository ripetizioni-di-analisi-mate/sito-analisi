What are the solutions of the inequality $9^{x} - 3^{x+1} + 2 \geq 0$?

- $0 \leq x \leq \log_3 2$
- $x \leq 1$ or $x \geq 2$
- $x \leq 0$ or $x \geq \log_3 2$
- $x \leq 0$ or $x \geq \log_2 3$

## Explanation

Since $9^{x} = (3^{x})^{2}$ and $3^{x+1} = 3 \cdot 3^{x}$, with the substitution $t = 3^{x}$, $t > 0$, the inequality becomes $t^{2} - 3t + 2 \geq 0$. The trinomial has roots $t = 1$ and $t = 2$ and is positive or zero outside the roots, so, intersecting with $t > 0$, we obtain $0 < t \leq 1$ or $t \geq 2$. The base $3$ is greater than $1$, therefore in returning to the variable $x$ the direction is preserved. From $3^{x} \leq 1 = 3^{0}$ we get $x \leq 0$, and from $3^{x} \geq 2 = 3^{\log_3 2}$ we get $x \geq \log_3 2$.
