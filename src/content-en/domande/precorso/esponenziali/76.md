What is the range of $\left(3\right)^{x}+4$?

- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right)$
- $\left(0, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The power takes every positive value and only positive values. Adding 4 to each value gives $\left(4, \infty\right)$.
