The figure shows the graph of $y = 3^{x}$ and the line $y = \dfrac{1}{3}$. For which $x$ does $3^{x} \geq \dfrac{1}{3}$ hold?

::grafico{f="3^x;1/3" xmin=-3 xmax=2 ymin=-0.5 ymax=5 punti="-1,0.3333,pieno" segmenti="-1,0,-1,0.3333" titolo="The graph of $y = 3^{x}$ and the line $y = \dfrac{1}{3}$"}

- $x \leq -1$
- $x \geq -1$
- $x > -1$
- $x \geq \dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The graph of $y = 3^{x}$ meets the line at the point with abscissa $-1$, because $3^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The function is increasing, so the graph lies above the line for $x > -1$ and meets it at $x = -1$. The inequality is non-strict, therefore $x = -1$ is also a solution and the solutions are $x \geq -1$. The same result is obtained by writing $\dfrac{1}{3} = 3^{-1}$ and passing to the exponents with the direction of the inequality preserved, because the base $3$ is greater than $1$.
