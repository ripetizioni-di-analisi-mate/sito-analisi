Which values satisfy $\left(2\right)^{x}\geq 8$?

- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[8, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The exponential with base 2 is increasing and equals $8$ at $x=3$. Including the equality point gives $\left[3, \infty\right)$.
