By studying the sign of the two factors, for which $x$ does $(x - 1)\left(2^{x} - 4\right) \geq 0$ hold?

- $1 \leq x \leq 2$
- $x \leq 1$ or $x \geq 2$
- $x \geq 2$
- $x \leq 1$ or $x \geq 4$

## Explanation

We study the sign of the two factors. The first factor, $x - 1$, is positive for $x > 1$. The second is positive for $2^{x} > 4 = 2^{2}$, that is, for $x > 2$, since the base $2$ is greater than $1$. The product is therefore positive for $x < 1$ and for $x > 2$, negative for $1 < x < 2$, and zero for $x = 1$ and for $x = 2$. Therefore the solutions are $x \leq 1$ or $x \geq 2$.
