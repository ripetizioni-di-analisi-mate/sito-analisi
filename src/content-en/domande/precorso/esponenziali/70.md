For which $x$ is the expression $25^{x} - 2 \cdot 5^{x} + 1$ positive?

- Every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- $x \neq 1$
- $x \neq 0$
- The inequality has no solutions

## Explanation

Since $25^{x} = \left(5^{x}\right)^{2}$, with the substitution $t = 5^{x}$, $t > 0$, the inequality becomes $t^{2} - 2t + 1 > 0$, that is, $(t - 1)^{2} > 0$. The square $(t - 1)^{2}$ is positive for $t \neq 1$ and equals $0$ for $t = 1$, so the inequality is satisfied for every $t > 0$ other than $1$. Since $5^{x} = 1$ only for $x = 0$, the solutions are $x \neq 0$.
