Which values satisfy $\left(3\right)^{x}\geq 9$?

- $\left[9, \infty\right)$
- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right]$

## Explanation

The exponential with base 3 is increasing and equals $9$ at $x=2$. Including the equality point gives $\left[2, \infty\right)$.
