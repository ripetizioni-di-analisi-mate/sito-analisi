What are the solutions of the inequality $4^{x} \leq 8^{x-1}$?

- $x \leq 3$
- $x \geq 1$
- $x \geq 3$
- $x \leq -3$

## Explanation

Both sides are written as powers of $2$. Since $4^{x} = 2^{2x}$ and $8^{x-1} = 2^{3(x-1)} = 2^{3x-3}$, the inequality becomes $2^{2x} \leq 2^{3x-3}$. The base $2$ is greater than $1$, so the direction is preserved and the inequality is equivalent to $2x \leq 3x - 3$. This gives $-x \leq -3$, and multiplying by $-1$ reverses the direction, therefore the solutions are $x \geq 3$.
