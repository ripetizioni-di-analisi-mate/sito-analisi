For which $x$ does the fraction $\dfrac{2^{x} + 4}{2^{x} - 1}$ take the value $3$?

- $x = \log_2 \dfrac{7}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{7}{2}$
- The equation has no solutions
- $x = \log_2 \dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

The fraction is defined for $2^{x} \neq 1$, that is, for $x \neq 0$. With the substitution $t = 2^{x}$, $t > 0$, $t \neq 1$, and multiplying both sides by $t - 1$, we obtain $t + 4 = 3(t - 1) = 3t - 3$, so $2t = 7$ and $t = \dfrac{7}{2}$. This value is positive and different from $1$, therefore it is acceptable. From $2^{x} = \dfrac{7}{2}$ it follows that $x = \log_2 \dfrac{7}{2}$.
