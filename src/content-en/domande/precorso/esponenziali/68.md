What is the range of $\left(2\right)^{x}+3$?

- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$
- $\left(0, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The power takes every positive value and only positive values. Adding 3 to each value gives $\left(3, \infty\right)$.
