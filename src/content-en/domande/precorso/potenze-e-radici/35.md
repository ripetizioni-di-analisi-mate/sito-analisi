For $x > 0$, which power of $x$ is equal to $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x^2} \cdot \sqrt{x}}{x}$?

- $x^{\frac{1}{6}}$
- $x^{\frac{7}{6}}$
- $x^{-\frac{2}{3}}$
- $x^{-\frac{1}{6}}$

## Explanation

By the definition of a power with rational exponent, $\sqrt[3]{x^2} = x^{\frac{2}{3}}$ and $\sqrt{x} = x^{\frac{1}{2}}$. The properties of powers still hold, so in the product the exponents are added and in the quotient the exponent $1$ of the denominator is subtracted, and we obtain
$$
x^{\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{2} - 1} = x^{\frac{4 + 3 - 6}{6}} = x^{\frac{1}{6}} .
$$
Multiplying the exponents $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ instead of adding them would give $x^{\frac{1}{3} - 1} = x^{-\frac{2}{3}}$.
