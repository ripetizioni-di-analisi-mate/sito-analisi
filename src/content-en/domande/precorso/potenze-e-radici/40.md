The root of a fraction can be written with a rational denominator. Which number is $\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}}$ equal to?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{9}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$

## Explanation

By the property of the root of a quotient $\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}}$. The denominator is a single root, so numerator and denominator are multiplied by $\sqrt{3}$, and since $\sqrt{3} \cdot \sqrt{3} = 3$ we obtain
$$
\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{3}}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} .
$$
The root applies to both numerator and denominator; writing $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ would apply it to the numerator only.
