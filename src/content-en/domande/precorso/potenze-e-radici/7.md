- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{20}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$

## Explanation

The denominator is a single root, so the numerator and the denominator are multiplied by $\sqrt{10}$. Since $\sqrt{10} \cdot \sqrt{10} = 10$, we obtain
$$
\frac{5}{\sqrt{10}} = \frac{5\sqrt{10}}{\sqrt{10} \cdot \sqrt{10}} = \frac{5\sqrt{10}}{10} .
$$
The factor $5$ of the numerator cancels with the $10$ of the denominator, therefore the result is $\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$.

In the initial fraction the $5$ is outside the radical sign and the $10$ is under the root, so the two numbers cannot be simplified with each other.
