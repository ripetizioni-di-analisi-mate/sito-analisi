For $a, b \neq 0$, what is $\left(2a^{-1}b^{2}\right)^{-2}$ equal to?

- $\dfrac{a^2}{2b^4}$
- $\dfrac{a^2}{4b^4}$
- $\dfrac{4b^4}{a^2}$
- $\dfrac{b^4}{4a^2}$

## Explanation

The power of a product is the product of the powers of the factors, and by the power of a power property the exponents are multiplied, so
$$
\left(2a^{-1}b^2\right)^{-2} = 2^{-2} \, a^{(-1)(-2)} \, b^{2 \cdot (-2)} = 2^{-2} \, a^{2} \, b^{-4} .
$$
Since $2^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$ and $b^{-4} = \dfrac{1}{b^4}$, the expression is equal to $\dfrac{a^2}{4b^4}$.

The exponent $-2$ also applies to the coefficient $2$; leaving the coefficient unchanged would give $\dfrac{a^2}{2b^4}$.
