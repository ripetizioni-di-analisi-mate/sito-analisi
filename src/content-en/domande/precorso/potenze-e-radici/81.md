A colony of bacteria A doubles every hour for $10$ hours, while a colony B triples every hour for $6$ hours. By which factor is each colony multiplied, and which one grows more?

- A by $2^{10} = 1024$, B by $3^{6} = 729$; A grows more
- A by $2 \cdot 10 = 20$, B by $3 \cdot 6 = 18$; A grows more
- A by $10^{2} = 100$, B by $6^{3} = 216$; B grows more
- A by $2^{9} = 512$, B by $3^{5} = 243$; A grows more

## Explanation

Every hour colony A is multiplied by $2$, so in $10$ hours it is multiplied by the product of $10$ factors equal to $2$, that is, by $2^{10} = 1024$. In the same way, in $6$ hours colony B is multiplied by $3^{6} = 729$. Since $1024 > 729$, colony A grows more, even though its base $2$ is smaller than $3$.

A power is a product of equal factors, therefore the growth factor of A is different from $2 \cdot 10 = 20$.
