What is the value of $\left(\dfrac{11}{2}\right)^{-3}\cdot\left(\dfrac{11}{2}\right)^{2}$?

- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{11}$
- $\dfrac{8}{1331}$
- $1$

## Explanation

When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents. Their sum is $-1$, so the result is the reciprocal of the base, namely $\dfrac{2}{11}$.
