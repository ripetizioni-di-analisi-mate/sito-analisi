For $a > 0$ we want to rationalize the fraction $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[5]{a^{2}}}$. By which factor are numerator and denominator multiplied?

- $\sqrt[5]{a^{2}}$
- $\sqrt[5]{a^{3}}$
- $\sqrt[5]{a}$
- $\sqrt[3]{a^{5}}$

## Explanation

The denominator becomes rational when a power of $a$ with exponent a multiple of $5$ appears under the fifth root. Since $\sqrt[5]{a^{2}} \cdot \sqrt[5]{a^{3}} = \sqrt[5]{a^{5}} = a$, the factor that completes the fifth power is $\sqrt[5]{a^{3}}$, and we obtain
$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt[5]{a^{2}}} = \frac{\sqrt[5]{a^{3}}}{a} .
$$
Multiplying by the root itself, the denominator would become $\sqrt[5]{a^{4}}$, which still contains a root.
