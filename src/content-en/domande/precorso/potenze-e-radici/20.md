Which of the following equalities is true?

- $2^{10} + 2^{10} = 2^{20}$
- $2^{10} + 2^{10} = 4^{10}$
- $2^{10} + 2^{10} = 4^{20}$
- $2^{10} + 2^{10} = 2^{11}$

## Explanation

The sum of two equal terms is twice the term, so $2^{10} + 2^{10} = 2 \cdot 2^{10}$. By the product of powers with the same base, $2 \cdot 2^{10} = 2^{1 + 10} = 2^{11}$.

The properties of powers concern products and quotients, therefore adding the exponents of the two terms would give $2^{20}$, which is different from $2^{11}$. Also $4^{10} = \left(2^2\right)^{10} = 2^{20}$ is different from $2^{11}$.
