Which statement about the number $3^{-\sqrt{2}}$ is true?

- It is negative
- It lies between $0$ and $1$
- It is greater than $1$
- It is not defined, because the exponent is irrational

## Explanation

For $a > 0$ the power $a^x$ is defined for every real $x$ and is positive, so $3^{-\sqrt{2}}$ exists and is greater than $0$. The base $3$ is greater than $1$, therefore the power increases as the exponent increases. Since $-\sqrt{2} < 0$, it follows that $3^{-\sqrt{2}} < 3^0 = 1$. Hence the number $3^{-\sqrt{2}}$ lies between $0$ and $1$.
