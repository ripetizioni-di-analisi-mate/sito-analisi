The power $a^{\frac{m}{n}}$, with $m$ an integer and $n \geq 2$ a natural number, is defined as $\sqrt[n]{a^m}$ only for a base $a > 0$. Which of the following expressions is meaningful according to this definition?

- $2^{-\frac{1}{3}}$
- $(-8)^{\frac{1}{3}}$
- $0^{-\frac{1}{2}}$
- $(-4)^{\frac{1}{2}}$

## Explanation

In the expression $2^{-\frac{1}{3}}$ the base $2$ is positive and the exponent is the fraction $\dfrac{-1}{3}$, with integer numerator and denominator $3$. The definition applies, so $2^{-\frac{1}{3}} = \sqrt[3]{2^{-1}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{2}}$ is meaningful.

The bases $-8$ and $-4$ are negative, therefore $(-8)^{\frac{1}{3}}$ and $(-4)^{\frac{1}{2}}$ are excluded by the definition; for the cube root of $-8$ one uses the notation $\sqrt[3]{-8}$. The base $0$ is excluded as well, and the expression $0^{-\frac{1}{2}}$ would require dividing by $\sqrt{0} = 0$.
