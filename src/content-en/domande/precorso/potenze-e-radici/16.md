What is $\sqrt[6]{25}$ equal to?

- $\sqrt[3]{25}$
- $\sqrt{5}$
- $\sqrt[6]{5}$
- $\sqrt[3]{5}$

## Explanation

Since $25 = 5^2$, we have $\sqrt[6]{25} = \sqrt[6]{5^2}$. By the **invariance property** $\sqrt[n]{a^k} = \sqrt[np]{a^{kp}}$, read from right to left with $p = 2$, the index and the exponent of the radicand are both divided by $2$, so $\sqrt[6]{5^2} = \sqrt[3]{5}$. The same result follows from the power with rational exponent, because $\sqrt[6]{5^2} = 5^{\frac{2}{6}} = 5^{\frac{1}{3}} = \sqrt[3]{5}$.

Dividing only the index by $2$ would give $\sqrt[3]{25}$, which is different from $\sqrt[3]{5}$.
