Which of the following numbers is negative?

- $(-3)^{4}$
- $(-5)^{-2}$
- $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{0}$
- $(-2)^{-3}$

## Explanation

With a negative base the sign of the power depends on the parity of the exponent, and the negative exponent leads to the reciprocal while leaving the sign unchanged. Therefore $(-3)^4 = 81$ and $(-5)^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{25}$ are positive, because the exponents $4$ and $-2$ are even. The power $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^0$ equals $1$, because the base is nonzero. The exponent $-3$ is odd, so $(-2)^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{(-2)^3} = -\dfrac{1}{8}$ is **negative**.
