What is the value of $-3^2 + (-2)^3$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $17$
- $-17$

## Explanation

In the expression $-3^2$ the exponentiation comes before the change of sign, so $-3^2 = -9$. In the expression $(-2)^3$ the base is $-2$ and the exponent $3$ is odd, therefore $(-2)^3 = -8$. Hence
$$
-3^2 + (-2)^3 = -9 - 8 = -17 .
$$
Reading $-3^2$ as $(-3)^2 = 9$ would give $9 - 8 = 1$.
