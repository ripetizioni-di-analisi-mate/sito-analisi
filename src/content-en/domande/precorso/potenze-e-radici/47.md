Which of the following powers is equal to half of the number $4^{10}$?

- $4^{5}$
- $2^{9}$
- $2^{19}$
- $4^{9}$

## Explanation

Since $4 = 2^2$, by the power of a power $4^{10} = \left(2^{2}\right)^{10} = 2^{20}$. Dividing by $2 = 2^{1}$ means, by the quotient of powers with the same base, subtracting $1$ from the exponent, so
$$
\frac{4^{10}}{2} = \frac{2^{20}}{2^{1}} = 2^{19} .
$$
Half of a power is different from the power with the exponent halved, because $4^{5} = 2^{10}$, which is much smaller than $2^{19}$. In the same way $4^{9} = \dfrac{4^{10}}{4}$ is one quarter of $4^{10}$.
