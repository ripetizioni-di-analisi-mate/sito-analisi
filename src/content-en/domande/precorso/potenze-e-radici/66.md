For $a > 0$, the radicand of $\sqrt{9a^{2} + 16a^{2}}$ is a sum of two monomials. Which expression is this root equal to?

- $7a$
- $5a$
- $25a^{2}$
- $\sqrt{7}\,a$

## Explanation

The two terms of the radicand are like monomials, so they are added and $9a^{2} + 16a^{2} = 25a^{2}$. Since $25a^{2} = (5a)^{2}$ and $5a > 0$ for $a > 0$, we have
$$
\sqrt{9a^{2} + 16a^{2}} = \sqrt{25a^{2}} = 5a .
$$
The root of a sum is different from the sum of the roots, therefore the result is different from $\sqrt{9a^{2}} + \sqrt{16a^{2}} = 3a + 4a = 7a$.
