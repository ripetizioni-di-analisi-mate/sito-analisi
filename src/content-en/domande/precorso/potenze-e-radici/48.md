A cube has an edge of length $2^{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is its volume, expressed in $\mathrm{mm^3}$?

- $2^{8}$
- $2^{15}$
- $3 \cdot 2^{5}$
- $2^{125}$

## Explanation

The volume of a cube is the cube of the edge length, so it equals $\left(2^{5}\right)^{3}\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. By the power of a power the exponents are multiplied, therefore
$$
\left(2^{5}\right)^{3} = 2^{5 \cdot 3} = 2^{15} .
$$
The volume is thus $2^{15}\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, that is, $32\,768\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.

Adding the exponents one would get $2^{8} = 2^{5} \cdot 2^{3}$, which is the product of $2^{5}$ and $8$, different from the cube of $2^{5}$.
