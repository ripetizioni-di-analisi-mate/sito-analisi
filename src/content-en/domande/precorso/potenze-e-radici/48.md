What is the value of $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{-4}\cdot\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{3}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{16}{625}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

When powers have the same base, add the exponents. Their sum is $-1$, so the result is the reciprocal of the base, namely $\dfrac{2}{5}$.
