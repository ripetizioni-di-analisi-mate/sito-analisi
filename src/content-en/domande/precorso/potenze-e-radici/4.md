- $\sqrt{33}$
- $9\sqrt{3}$
- $3\sqrt{3}$
- $5\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Each radicand is the product of a square and $3$; indeed $48 = 16 \cdot 3$, $27 = 9 \cdot 3$ and $12 = 4 \cdot 3$. With the property $\sqrt{ab} = \sqrt{a}\,\sqrt{b}$ the factors are taken out of the radical sign, and we obtain
$$
\sqrt{48} = 4\sqrt{3}, \qquad \sqrt{27} = 3\sqrt{3}, \qquad \sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3} .
$$
The three terms are like radicals, so their coefficients are added, and $4 - 3 + 2 = 3$. Therefore the expression equals $3\sqrt{3}$.

In general the sum of roots is different from the root of the sum. In this case $\sqrt{48 - 27 + 12} = \sqrt{33}$, while $3\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{27}$.
