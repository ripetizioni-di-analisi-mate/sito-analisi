The differences $\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{5} - 2$, $\sqrt{7} - \sqrt{6}$ and $\sqrt{10} - 3$ are all positive. Which of the four is the smallest?

- $\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{5} - 2$
- $\sqrt{7} - \sqrt{6}$
- $\sqrt{10} - 3$

## Explanation

Since $2 = \sqrt{4}$ and $3 = \sqrt{9}$, each difference has the form $\sqrt{n + 1} - \sqrt{n}$, with $n$ equal to $2$, $4$, $6$ and $9$. Multiplying and dividing by the conjugate binomial we obtain
$$
\sqrt{n + 1} - \sqrt{n} = \frac{(n + 1) - n}{\sqrt{n + 1} + \sqrt{n}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n + 1} + \sqrt{n}} .
$$
The four fractions have numerator $1$, so the smallest is the one with the largest denominator. The denominator increases as $n$ increases, therefore the smallest difference is the one with $n = 9$, that is, $\sqrt{10} - 3$.
