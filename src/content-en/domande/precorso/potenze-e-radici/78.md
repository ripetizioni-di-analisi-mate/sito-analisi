Which of the following numbers is greater than $1$?

- $\left(\dfrac{11}{12}\right)^{2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{11}{12}\right)^{-2}$
- $\dfrac{12}{13}$
- $\left(\dfrac{12}{11}\right)^{-3}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. Hence $\left(\dfrac{11}{12}\right)^{-2} = \dfrac{144}{121}$ is greater than $1$.
