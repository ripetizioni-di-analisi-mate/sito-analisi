The product $\left(a^{\frac{1}{2}} + b^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)\left(a^{\frac{1}{2}} - b^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)$, with $a, b > 0$, is a sum times a difference of the same terms. Which expression is it equal to?

- $a + b$
- $a - b$
- $b - a$
- $a^{\frac{1}{4}} - b^{\frac{1}{4}}$

## Explanation

The product is a sum times a difference of the same terms, so it equals the difference of squares $\left(a^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{2} - \left(b^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{2}$. By the power of a power $\left(a^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{2} = a^{\frac{1}{2} \cdot 2} = a$, and in the same way $\left(b^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{2} = b$. Therefore the product equals $a - b$.

The square of $a^{\frac{1}{2}}$ is obtained by multiplying the exponent by $2$; squaring the exponent $\dfrac{1}{2}$ one would get $a^{\frac{1}{4}}$.
