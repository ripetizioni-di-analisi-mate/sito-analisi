- $5^5$
- $5$
- $5^9$
- $5^{\frac{3}{2}}$

## Explanation

The numerator is a product of powers with the same base, so the exponents are added, each with its own sign, and we obtain
$$
5^7 \cdot 5^{-4} = 5^{7 + (-4)} = 5^3 .
$$
For the quotient of powers with the same base the exponent of the denominator is subtracted, therefore
$$
\frac{5^3}{5^2} = 5^{3 - 2} = 5 .
$$
Adding the exponent $-4$ as if it were $+4$ would give $5^{7 + 4 - 2} = 5^9$.
