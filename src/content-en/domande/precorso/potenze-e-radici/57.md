The total surface area $S$ of a cube is obtained from its volume $V$ by the formula $S = 6\,V^{\frac{2}{3}}$. What is $S$ for a cube of volume $V = 125\,\mathrm{cm^3}$?

- $150\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $500\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

By the definition of a power with rational exponent
$$
125^{\frac{2}{3}} = \left(\sqrt[3]{125}\right)^{2} = 5^{2} = 25 ,
$$
because $5^{3} = 125$. So $S = 6 \cdot 25\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 150\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The same value comes from the cube itself, because the edge measures $\sqrt[3]{125}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$, each face has area $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and there are six faces.

Taking only the cube root, without squaring, one would get $6 \cdot 5 = 30$, while $25$ is the area of a single face.
