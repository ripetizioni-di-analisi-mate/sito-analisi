What is the value of $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{-3}\cdot\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{2}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{125}{27}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents. Their sum is $-1$, so the result is the reciprocal of the base, namely $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
