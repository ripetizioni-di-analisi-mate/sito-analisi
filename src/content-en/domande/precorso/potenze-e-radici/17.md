The number $N = 2^{10} \cdot 5^{12}$ is a positive integer. How many digits does its decimal representation have?

- $12$
- $11$
- $13$
- $23$

## Explanation

We split $5^{12} = 5^{10} \cdot 5^{2}$, so that $2^{10}$ and $5^{10}$ have the same exponent. By the product of powers with the same exponent, $2^{10} \cdot 5^{10} = (2 \cdot 5)^{10} = 10^{10}$, so
$$
N = 5^{2} \cdot 10^{10} = 25 \cdot 10^{10} .
$$
Multiplying $25$ by $10^{10}$ appends ten zeros to the two digits of $25$, therefore $N$ has $2 + 10 = 12$ digits.

The factor $25$ left after factoring out counts for two digits; neglecting it, one would count the $11$ digits of $10^{10}$.
