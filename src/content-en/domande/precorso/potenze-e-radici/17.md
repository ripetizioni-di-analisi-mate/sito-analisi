What is the value of $\dfrac{3^{10} \cdot 9^{-2}}{27^{2}}$?

- $3$
- $1$
- $9$
- $3^{8}$

## Explanation

The bases $9 = 3^2$ and $27 = 3^3$ are powers of $3$, so by the power of a power property $9^{-2} = 3^{-4}$ and $27^2 = 3^6$. The three powers now have the same base, therefore by the product and quotient of powers with the same base the exponents are added and subtracted, and we obtain
$$
\frac{3^{10} \cdot 3^{-4}}{3^{6}} = 3^{10 - 4 - 6} = 3^0 = 1 .
$$
Rewriting $9^{-2}$ as $3^{-2}$ would give $3^{10 - 2 - 6} = 9$.
