For $a \geq 0$, what is $\sqrt{18a^3}$ equal to?

- $9a\sqrt{2a}$
- $3a\sqrt{2a}$
- $3a\sqrt{2}$
- $3a^2\sqrt{2a}$

## Explanation

The radicand factors as $18a^3 = 9a^2 \cdot 2a$, where $9a^2 = (3a)^2$ is a square. Both factors are greater than or equal to zero, so the property $\sqrt{ab} = \sqrt{a} \, \sqrt{b}$ gives $\sqrt{18a^3} = \sqrt{9a^2} \, \sqrt{2a}$. Since $a \geq 0$, we have $\sqrt{9a^2} = 3|a| = 3a$, therefore
$$
\sqrt{18a^3} = 3a\sqrt{2a} .
$$
What comes out of the radical sign is the square root of the factor $9a^2$, that is $3a$; taking out $9a$ would give $9a\sqrt{2a}$.
