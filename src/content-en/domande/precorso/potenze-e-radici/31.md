Which of the following statements is true?

- $\sqrt[4]{16} = \pm 2$
- $\sqrt[3]{-27}$ does not exist in $\mathbb{R}$
- $\sqrt[4]{-16} = -2$
- $\sqrt[4]{16} = 2$

## Explanation

With even index, the root of a number greater than or equal to zero is by definition the unique number $x \geq 0$ such that $x^n$ equals the radicand. Since $2 \geq 0$ and $2^4 = 16$, it follows that $\sqrt[4]{16} = 2$. The equation $x^4 = 16$ has the two solutions $x = \pm 2$, while the symbol $\sqrt[4]{16}$ denotes only $2$.

The root $\sqrt[3]{-27}$ has odd index, so it exists and equals $-3$. The root $\sqrt[4]{-16}$ has even index and negative radicand, therefore it does not exist in $\mathbb{R}$, because every power with even exponent is greater than or equal to zero.
