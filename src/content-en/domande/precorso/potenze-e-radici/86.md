What is the domain of the function $f(x) = \sqrt{x^{2} - 9}$, which has a radicand of degree two?

- $x \geq 3$
- $x \leq -3$ or $x \geq 3$
- $-3 \leq x \leq 3$
- $x < -3$ or $x > 3$

## Explanation

The square root has an even index, so it requires $x^{2} - 9 \geq 0$. The polynomial $x^{2} - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$ vanishes at $-3$ and at $3$ and is positive outside the interval between these two values, therefore the inequality is satisfied for $x \leq -3$ or $x \geq 3$. The endpoints $\pm 3$ are included, because at those points the radicand equals $0$.

Negative values with $|x| \geq 3$ also belong to the domain; for example $f(-5) = \sqrt{16} = 4$.
