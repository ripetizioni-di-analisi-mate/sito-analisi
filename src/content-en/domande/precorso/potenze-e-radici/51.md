The sixth power of the cube root of $5$ is an integer. What is the value of $\left(\sqrt[3]{5}\right)^{6}$?

- $25$
- $125$
- $5^{18}$
- $10$

## Explanation

By the definition of a power with rational exponent, $\sqrt[3]{5} = 5^{\frac{1}{3}}$. By the power of a power the exponents are multiplied, so
$$
\left(\sqrt[3]{5}\right)^{6} = \left(5^{\frac{1}{3}}\right)^{6} = 5^{\frac{6}{3}} = 5^{2} = 25 .
$$
In the expression $\sqrt[3]{5} = 5^{\frac{1}{3}}$ the index $3$ is in the denominator of the exponent; moving it to the numerator one would get $\left(5^{3}\right)^{6} = 5^{18}$.
