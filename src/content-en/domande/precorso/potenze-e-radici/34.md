The decimal exponent $0.6$ can be written as a fraction. What is the value of the power $32^{0.6}$?

- $8$
- $2$
- $19.2$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

Since $0.6 = \dfrac{6}{10} = \dfrac{3}{5}$, by the definition of a power with rational exponent
$$
32^{0.6} = 32^{\frac{3}{5}} = \left(\sqrt[5]{32}\right)^{3} .
$$
We have $\sqrt[5]{32} = 2$, because $2^{5} = 32$, so $32^{0.6} = 2^{3} = 8$.

Stopping at the fifth root without cubing one would get $2$, while the product $32 \cdot 0.6 = 19.2$ treats the exponent as a factor.
