What is the value of $16^{-\frac{3}{4}}$?

- $-8$
- $8$
- $-12$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

By the definition of a power with negative exponent, $16^{-\frac{3}{4}} = \dfrac{1}{16^{\frac{3}{4}}}$. By the definition of a power with rational exponent, $16^{\frac{3}{4}} = \left(\sqrt[4]{16}\right)^3 = 2^3 = 8$. Therefore $16^{-\frac{3}{4}} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

The value is positive, because for $a > 0$ every power $a^x$ is positive. Mistaking the exponent for a factor would give $16 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{3}{4}\right) = -12$.
