What is the value of $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-2}\cdot\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{1}$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

When powers have the same base, add the exponents. Their sum is $-1$, so the result is the reciprocal of the base, namely $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
