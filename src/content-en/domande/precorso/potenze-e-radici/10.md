- $\sqrt{2} < \sqrt[3]{2} < \sqrt[4]{3}$
- $\sqrt[3]{2} < \sqrt[4]{3} < \sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt[3]{2} < \sqrt{2} < \sqrt[4]{3}$
- $\sqrt[4]{3} < \sqrt[3]{2} < \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The three roots have indices $3$, $4$ and $2$, so to compare them they are brought to the same index with the **invariance property**. The least common multiple of the indices is $12$, therefore
$$
\sqrt[3]{2} = \sqrt[12]{2^4} = \sqrt[12]{16}, \qquad
\sqrt[4]{3} = \sqrt[12]{3^3} = \sqrt[12]{27}, \qquad
\sqrt{2} = \sqrt[12]{2^6} = \sqrt[12]{64} .
$$
Roots with the same index are compared through their radicands, and $16 < 27 < 64$. Hence $\sqrt[3]{2} < \sqrt[4]{3} < \sqrt{2}$.
