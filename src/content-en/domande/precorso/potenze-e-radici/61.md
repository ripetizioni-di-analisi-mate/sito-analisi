The roots $\sqrt{8}$ and $\sqrt{18}$ are irrational numbers. What is the value of their product $\sqrt{8} \cdot \sqrt{18}$?

- $\sqrt{26}$
- $12$
- $6$
- $144$

## Explanation

By the property $\sqrt{a}\,\sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$, with $a, b \geq 0$, the product of two square roots is the root of the product of the radicands, so
$$
\sqrt{8} \cdot \sqrt{18} = \sqrt{144} = 12 .
$$
The same result is obtained by taking factors out of the roots, because $\sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$ and $2\sqrt{2} \cdot 3\sqrt{2} = 6 \cdot 2 = 12$. Neglecting the factor $\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{2} = 2$ one would get $6$.
