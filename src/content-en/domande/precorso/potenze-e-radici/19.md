For $x \neq 0$, which power of $x$ is equal to $\dfrac{\left(x^{3}\right)^{2} \cdot x^{-4}}{x^{-3}}$?

- $x^{-1}$
- $x^{4}$
- $x^{5}$
- $x^{13}$

## Explanation

By the power of a power property the exponents are multiplied, so $\left(x^3\right)^2 = x^6$, and the numerator is $x^6 \cdot x^{-4} = x^2$. By the quotient of powers with the same base the exponent of the denominator is subtracted, and subtracting $-3$ is the same as adding $3$, therefore
$$
\frac{x^2}{x^{-3}} = x^{2 - (-3)} = x^5 .
$$
Writing $\left(x^3\right)^2$ as $x^{3 + 2} = x^5$ would give $x^{5 - 4 + 3} = x^4$.
