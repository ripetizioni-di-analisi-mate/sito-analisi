Taking the largest possible factor out of the radical sign, how is $\sqrt[4]{48}$ written?

- $4\sqrt[4]{3}$
- $2\sqrt[4]{3}$
- $2\sqrt{3}$
- $2\sqrt[4]{6}$

## Explanation

Since $48 = 16 \cdot 3$ and $16 = 2^{4}$ is a fourth power, by the property $\sqrt[n]{ab} = \sqrt[n]{a}\,\sqrt[n]{b}$ we obtain
$$
\sqrt[4]{48} = \sqrt[4]{2^{4}} \cdot \sqrt[4]{3} = 2\sqrt[4]{3} .
$$
The factor $16$ comes out of the fourth root as $\sqrt[4]{16} = 2$; using the square root of $16$ instead one would get $4\sqrt[4]{3}$.
