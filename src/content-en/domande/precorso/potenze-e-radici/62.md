What is the value of $\left(\dfrac{7}{3}\right)^{-2}\cdot\left(\dfrac{7}{3}\right)^{1}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{49}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents. Their sum is $-1$, so the result is the reciprocal of the base, namely $\dfrac{3}{7}$.
