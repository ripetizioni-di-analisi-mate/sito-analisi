Which expression is obtained by expanding the square of the binomial $\left(\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2}\right)^{2}$?

- $7 - 2\sqrt{10}$
- $3$
- $7 - \sqrt{10}$
- $3 - 2\sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

By the square of a binomial $(p - q)^{2} = p^{2} - 2pq + q^{2}$, with $p = \sqrt{5}$ and $q = \sqrt{2}$. We have $p^{2} = 5$ and $q^{2} = 2$, while by the property $\sqrt{a}\,\sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$ we have $pq = \sqrt{10}$. So
$$
\left(\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2}\right)^{2} = 5 - 2\sqrt{10} + 2 = 7 - 2\sqrt{10} .
$$
The power of a difference is different from the difference of the powers, therefore the result is different from $5 - 2 = 3$.
