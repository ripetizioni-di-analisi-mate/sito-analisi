What is the value of $(-3)^{-2}$?

- $-9$
- $-\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $9$

## Explanation

By the definition of a power with negative exponent, $a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n}$ with $a \neq 0$, so $(-3)^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{(-3)^2}$. The base is negative and the exponent $2$ is even, therefore $(-3)^2 = 9$. Hence $(-3)^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{9}$.

The negative exponent leads to the reciprocal of the power, while the sign of the result depends only on the parity of the exponent.
