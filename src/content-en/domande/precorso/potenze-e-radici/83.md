Which of the following numbers is greater than $1$?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{3}$
- $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-3}$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^{-4}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. Hence $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-3} = \dfrac{27}{8}$ is greater than $1$.
