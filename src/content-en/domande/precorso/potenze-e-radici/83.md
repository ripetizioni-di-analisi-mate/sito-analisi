The cube root $\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1}{x - 2}}$ has an odd index and a fraction as radicand. For which real $x$ is it defined?

- $x > 2$
- $x \neq 2$
- $x \geq 2$
- For every real $x$

## Explanation

A root of odd index exists for every real radicand, so it imposes no condition on the sign of $\dfrac{1}{x - 2}$. The radicand is a fraction, therefore its denominator must be different from zero, that is, $x - 2 \neq 0$. Hence the expression is defined for $x \neq 2$; for example, for $x = 1$ it equals $\sqrt[3]{-1} = -1$.
