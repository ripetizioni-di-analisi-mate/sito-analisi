Which power or fraction exceeds $1$ among these expressions involving 2 and 3?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{3}$
- $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-3}$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^{-4}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. Here it has value $\dfrac{27}{8}$.
