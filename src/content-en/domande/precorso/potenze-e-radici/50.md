Which of the following numbers is greater than $1$?

- $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^{-2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^{2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{6}{5}\right)^{-3}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. Hence $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^{-2} = \dfrac{36}{25}$ is greater than $1$.
