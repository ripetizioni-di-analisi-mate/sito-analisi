Which power or fraction exceeds $1$ when the bases involve 5 and 6?

- $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^{-2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^{2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{6}{5}\right)^{-3}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. The first type of power gives $\dfrac{36}{25}$.
