A real number $a$ satisfies the equality $2^{a} = 5$. What is then the value of $4^{a + 1}$?

- $40$
- $29$
- $20$
- $100$

## Explanation

By the product of powers with the same base, $4^{a+1} = 4^{a} \cdot 4$. Since $4 = 2^{2}$, by the power of a power $4^{a} = \left(2^{2}\right)^{a} = \left(2^{a}\right)^{2}$, and from $2^{a} = 5$ it follows that $4^{a} = 25$. Therefore
$$
4^{a+1} = 25 \cdot 4 = 100 .
$$
Writing $4^{a}$ as $2^{a}$, that is, changing the base without changing the exponent, one would get $5 \cdot 4 = 20$. Writing $4^{a+1}$ as $4^{a} + 4$ one would get $29$.
