What is the value of $3^{-1} + 3^{-2}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{27}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $-12$

## Explanation

By the definition of a power with negative exponent, $3^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $3^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{9}$. The two fractions are added with the common denominator $9$, and we obtain
$$
\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{9} = \frac{3 + 1}{9} = \frac{4}{9} .
$$
The properties of powers concern products and quotients, so the sum $3^{-1} + 3^{-2}$ is different from $3^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{27}$.
