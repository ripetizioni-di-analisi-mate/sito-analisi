- $\dfrac{a^3}{b^6}$
- $a^3 b^6$
- $\dfrac{a}{b^2}$
- $\dfrac{a^6}{b^6}$

## Explanation

For $a, b \neq 0$ the fraction $\dfrac{a^2 b}{a b^3}$ is simplified letter by letter with the quotient of powers with the same base, and we obtain
$$
\frac{a^2 b}{a b^3} = a^{2 - 1}\, b^{1 - 3} = a\, b^{-2} .
$$
The cube of a product is the product of the cubes, and by the power of a power property the exponents are multiplied, so
$$
\left(a\, b^{-2}\right)^3 = a^3\, b^{-6} .
$$
Since $b^{-6} = \dfrac{1}{b^6}$, the expression equals $\dfrac{a^3}{b^6}$.
