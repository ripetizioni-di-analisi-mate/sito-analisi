Which of the following equalities is true for all $a, b > 0$?

- $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + b^2$
- $a^2 \cdot b^3 = (ab)^5$
- $a^3 \cdot b^3 = (ab)^3$
- $a^2 + a^3 = a^5$

## Explanation

By the product of powers with the same exponent, $a^n \cdot b^n = (ab)^n$, so $a^3 \cdot b^3 = (ab)^3$ is **true** for all $a, b > 0$.

The other three equalities are false in general, as the values $a = 1$ and $b = 2$ show. Indeed $(1 + 2)^2 = 9$, while $1^2 + 2^2 = 5$. For $a^2 \cdot b^3$ we get $1 \cdot 8 = 8$, while $(1 \cdot 2)^5 = 32$. Finally $1^2 + 1^3 = 2$, while $1^5 = 1$.
