Which number is the cube $\left(2\sqrt[3]{2}\right)^{3}$ equal to?

- $4$
- $8\sqrt[3]{2}$
- $16$
- $6\sqrt[3]{2}$

## Explanation

The power of a product is the product of the powers of the factors, so $\left(2\sqrt[3]{2}\right)^{3} = 2^{3} \cdot \left(\sqrt[3]{2}\right)^{3}$. By the definition of cube root $\left(\sqrt[3]{2}\right)^{3} = 2$, therefore
$$
\left(2\sqrt[3]{2}\right)^{3} = 8 \cdot 2 = 16 .
$$
The exponent $3$ also applies to the coefficient $2$; leaving it unchanged one would get $2 \cdot 2 = 4$.
