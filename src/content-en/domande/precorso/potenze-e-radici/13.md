Let $n$ be a natural number. What is the value of the sum $(-1)^{2n+1} + (-1)^{2n}$, whatever $n$ is?

- $0$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The exponent $2n$ is even for every natural $n$, so $(-1)^{2n} = 1$. The exponent $2n + 1$ is odd, therefore $(-1)^{2n+1} = -1$. Hence the sum equals $-1 + 1 = 0$ for every $n$.

The properties of powers concern products and quotients, so the sum is different from $(-1)^{(2n+1) + 2n} = (-1)^{4n+1} = -1$.
