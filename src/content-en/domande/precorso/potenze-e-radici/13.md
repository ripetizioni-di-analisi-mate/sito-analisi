What is the value of $\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)^{-2}$?

- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{25}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{25}$
- $-\dfrac{25}{4}$

## Explanation

For $a \neq 0$ we have $a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n}$, so a fraction with a negative exponent equals the reciprocal fraction with the opposite exponent, and
$$
\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^{-2} = \frac{1}{\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^{2}} = \left(\frac{5}{2}\right)^{2} .
$$
By the quotient of powers with the same exponent, numerator and denominator are squared, therefore $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{25}{4}$. The negative exponent leads to the reciprocal and leaves the sign unchanged, so the result is positive.
