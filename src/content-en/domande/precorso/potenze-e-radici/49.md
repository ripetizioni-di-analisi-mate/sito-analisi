For $a \neq 0$, which expression is equal to the product $(-a)^{3} \cdot (-a)^{-1}$?

- $-a^{2}$
- $a^{4}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{a^{3}}$
- $a^{2}$

## Explanation

The two factors are powers with the same base $-a$, so by the product of powers with the same base the exponents are added, each with its own sign, and we obtain
$$
(-a)^{3} \cdot (-a)^{-1} = (-a)^{3 - 1} = (-a)^{2} .
$$
The exponent $2$ is even, therefore $(-a)^{2} = a^{2}$ whatever the sign of $a$.

Adding the exponent $-1$ as if it were $+1$ one would get $(-a)^{4} = a^{4}$.
