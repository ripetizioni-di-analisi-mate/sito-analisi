## Explanation

By the power of a power property the exponents are multiplied, so $\left(2^3\right)^5 = 2^{15}$.

The denominator has base $4 = 2^2$, therefore $4^6 = \left(2^2\right)^6 = 2^{12}$.

The two powers now have the same base, so by the quotient of powers with the same base the exponents are subtracted, and we obtain
$$
\frac{2^{15}}{2^{12}} = 2^{15 - 12} = 2^3 = 8 .
$$
