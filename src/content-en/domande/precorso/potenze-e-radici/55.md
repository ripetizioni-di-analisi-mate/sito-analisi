For $x > 0$, which expression is equal to the power of a power $\left(x^{-\frac{2}{3}}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}}$?

- $\sqrt{x}$
- $x^{-\frac{17}{12}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$
- $x^{\frac{8}{9}}$

## Explanation

By the power of a power the exponents are multiplied, and the product of two negative numbers is positive, so
$$
\left(x^{-\frac{2}{3}}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}} = x^{\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right)} = x^{\frac{6}{12}} = x^{\frac{1}{2}} .
$$
By the definition of a power with rational exponent, $x^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{x}$.

Adding the exponents one would get $x^{-\frac{2}{3} - \frac{3}{4}} = x^{-\frac{17}{12}}$.
