The radicand $x^{6}$ is greater than or equal to zero for every real $x$. Which expression is $\sqrt{x^{6}}$ equal to for every $x$?

- $x^{3}$
- $\pm x^{3}$
- $x^{4}$
- $|x|^{3}$

## Explanation

Since $x^{6} = \left(x^{3}\right)^{2}$, by the square root of a square $\sqrt{x^{6}} = \left|x^{3}\right|$. The absolute value of a product is the product of the absolute values, so $\left|x^{3}\right| = |x|^{3}$.

For $x = -1$ we have $\sqrt{(-1)^{6}} = \sqrt{1} = 1 = |-1|^{3}$, while $(-1)^{3} = -1$; therefore $x^{3}$ is different from $\sqrt{x^{6}}$ for $x < 0$.
