For $n$ natural, what is $\dfrac{3^{n+2} - 3^{n}}{8}$ equal to?

- $3^{n}$
- $\dfrac{9}{8}$
- $3^{n+1}$
- $\dfrac{3^{n+1}}{4}$

## Explanation

By the product of powers with the same base, $3^{n+2} = 3^n \cdot 3^2 = 9 \cdot 3^n$. So $3^n$ can be factored out of the numerator, and we obtain
$$
3^{n+2} - 3^n = 3^n \, (9 - 1) = 8 \cdot 3^n .
$$
Therefore $\dfrac{3^{n+2} - 3^n}{8} = \dfrac{8 \cdot 3^n}{8} = 3^n$.

The properties of powers concern products and quotients, so the difference $3^{n+2} - 3^n$ is different from $3^{(n+2) - n} = 3^2$; this mistake would give $\dfrac{9}{8}$.
