What is the value of $\dfrac{6^5}{2^5 \cdot 3^3}$?

- $9$
- $3$
- $27$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

By the quotient of powers with the same exponent, $\dfrac{6^5}{2^5} = \left(\dfrac{6}{2}\right)^5 = 3^5$. The expression therefore becomes $\dfrac{3^5}{3^3}$, and by the quotient of powers with the same base we obtain $3^{5 - 3} = 3^2 = 9$.

The same result follows from factoring $6^5 = 2^5 \cdot 3^5$ and canceling the factor $2^5$.
