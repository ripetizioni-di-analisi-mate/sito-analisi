What is the value of $\sqrt[3]{-54} + \sqrt[3]{16}$?

- $\sqrt[3]{2}$
- $-\sqrt[3]{38}$
- $-5\sqrt[3]{2}$
- $-\sqrt[3]{2}$

## Explanation

The cube root has an odd index and $\left(-\sqrt[3]{54}\right)^3 = -54$, so $\sqrt[3]{-54} = -\sqrt[3]{54}$. Since $54 = 27 \cdot 2$ and $16 = 8 \cdot 2$, the property $\sqrt[n]{ab} = \sqrt[n]{a} \, \sqrt[n]{b}$ allows us to take factors out of the radical sign, and we obtain
$$
\sqrt[3]{54} = 3\sqrt[3]{2}, \qquad \sqrt[3]{16} = 2\sqrt[3]{2} .
$$
The terms $-3\sqrt[3]{2}$ and $2\sqrt[3]{2}$ are like radicals, therefore their coefficients are added, and $-3 + 2 = -1$. The expression is thus equal to $-\sqrt[3]{2}$.
