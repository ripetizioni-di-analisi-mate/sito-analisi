The powers $0.9^{20}$, $0.8^{20}$, $0.9^{30}$ and $0.8^{30}$ have bases between $0$ and $1$. Which of the four is the greatest?

- $0.9^{20}$
- $0.8^{20}$
- $0.9^{30}$
- $0.8^{30}$

## Explanation

The base $0.9$ lies between $0$ and $1$, so the power with base $0.9$ decreases as the exponent increases. Since $20 < 30$, we have $0.9^{30} < 0.9^{20}$, and for the same reason $0.8^{30} < 0.8^{20}$.

The powers $0.8^{20}$ and $0.9^{20}$ have the same positive exponent, therefore the greater one is the one with the greater base. Since $0.8 < 0.9$, we have $0.8^{20} < 0.9^{20}$. Hence the greatest of the four powers is $0.9^{20}$.

With a positive exponent the inequality between the bases keeps its direction even when the bases are less than $1$; the direction is reversed only with a negative exponent.
