Which ordering from smallest to largest is correct for $2^{100}$, $3^{75}$ and $5^{50}$?

- $2^{100} < 3^{75} < 5^{50}$
- $5^{50} < 3^{75} < 2^{100}$
- $3^{75} < 5^{50} < 2^{100}$
- $2^{100} < 5^{50} < 3^{75}$

## Explanation

The exponents $100$, $75$ and $50$ are multiples of $25$, so the power of a power property rewrites the three powers with exponent $25$, and we obtain
$$
2^{100} = \left(2^4\right)^{25} = 16^{25}, \qquad
3^{75} = \left(3^3\right)^{25} = 27^{25}, \qquad
5^{50} = \left(5^2\right)^{25} = 25^{25} .
$$
The three powers now have the same positive exponent, therefore by the **comparison of powers** the one with the greater base is greater. Since $16 < 25 < 27$, it follows that $2^{100} < 5^{50} < 3^{75}$.
