We look for the number $x > 0$ such that $x^{\frac{3}{2}} = 27$. What is the value of $x$?

- $3$
- $18$
- $9$
- $81\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Both sides, which are positive, are raised to the power $\dfrac{2}{3}$, the reciprocal of $\dfrac{3}{2}$. By the power of a power $\left(x^{\frac{3}{2}}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}} = x^{\frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3}} = x$, so
$$
x = 27^{\frac{2}{3}} = \left(\sqrt[3]{27}\right)^{2} = 3^{2} = 9 .
$$
Indeed $9^{\frac{3}{2}} = \left(\sqrt{9}\right)^{3} = 3^{3} = 27$. Taking only the cube root of $27$, without squaring, one would get $3$.
