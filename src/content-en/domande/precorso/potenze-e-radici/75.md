The number $\sqrt{50}$ is irrational. Between which two consecutive integers does it lie?

- Between $6$ and $7$
- Between $8$ and $9$
- Between $24$ and $25$
- Between $7$ and $8$

## Explanation

Roots with the same index are compared through their radicands. Since $49 < 50 < 64$, we have $\sqrt{49} < \sqrt{50} < \sqrt{64}$, that is, $7 < \sqrt{50} < 8$. So $\sqrt{50}$ lies between $7$ and $8$.

The square root of $50$ is the positive number whose square is $50$, and it is different from half of $50$; this is why the interval between $24$ and $25$ is excluded.
