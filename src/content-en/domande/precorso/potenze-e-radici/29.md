What is $\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt[3]{4}$ equal to?

- $\sqrt[6]{8}$
- $\sqrt[5]{8}$
- $2\sqrt[6]{2}$
- $\sqrt[6]{32}$

## Explanation

The two roots have different indices, so they are brought to the common index $6$ with the **invariance property**, and we obtain $\sqrt{2} = \sqrt[6]{2^3}$ and $\sqrt[3]{4} = \sqrt[3]{2^2} = \sqrt[6]{2^4}$. The product of roots with the same index is the root of the product, therefore
$$
\sqrt[6]{2^3} \cdot \sqrt[6]{2^4} = \sqrt[6]{2^7} .
$$
Since $2^7 = 2^6 \cdot 2$, the factor $2$ is taken out of the radical sign, and the result is $2\sqrt[6]{2}$.
