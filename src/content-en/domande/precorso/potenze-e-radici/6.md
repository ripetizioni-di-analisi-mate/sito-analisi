- $2^{60} > 5^{20}$, because $2^{60} = 8^{20}$ and $8 > 5$
- $2^{60} < 5^{20}$, because $5 > 2$
- $2^{60} < 5^{20}$, because $2^{60} = 2^3 \cdot 2^{20}$ and $8 \cdot 2^{20} < 5^{20}$
- $2^{60} < 5^{20}$, because $2^{60} = 4^{30}$ and $4 < 5$

## Explanation

The two powers have different bases and different exponents. Since $60 = 3 \cdot 20$, the power of a power property rewrites $2^{60}$ with exponent $20$, and we obtain
$$
2^{60} = 2^{3 \cdot 20} = \left(2^3\right)^{20} = 8^{20} .
$$
The powers $8^{20}$ and $5^{20}$ have the same positive exponent, so by the **comparison of powers** the one with the greater base is greater. Since $8 > 5$, it follows that $2^{60} > 5^{20}$.

When the exponents are different, as for $2^{60}$ and $5^{20}$ or for $4^{30}$ and $5^{20}$, comparing the bases alone does not lead to a conclusion.
