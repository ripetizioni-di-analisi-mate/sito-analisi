Which of the following numbers is the greatest?

- $9^{-\frac{1}{2}}$
- $2^{-3}$
- $3^{-2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{2}$

## Explanation

Each number is computed with the definitions of a power with negative and with rational exponent. We have
$$
9^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{9}} = \frac{1}{3}, \qquad
2^{-3} = \frac{1}{8}, \qquad
3^{-2} = \frac{1}{9}, \qquad
\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{2} = \frac{1}{16} .
$$
The four fractions have numerator $1$, so the greatest is the one with the smallest denominator. Therefore the greatest number is $9^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
