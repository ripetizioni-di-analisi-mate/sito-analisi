Which of the following numbers is greater than $1$?

- $\left(\dfrac{8}{7}\right)^{-4}$
- $\left(\dfrac{7}{8}\right)^{-3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\left(\dfrac{7}{8}\right)^{3}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. Hence $\left(\dfrac{7}{8}\right)^{-3} = \dfrac{512}{343}$ is greater than $1$.
