Which power or fraction exceeds $1$ among these expressions involving 7 and 8?

- $\left(\dfrac{8}{7}\right)^{-4}$
- $\left(\dfrac{7}{8}\right)^{-3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\left(\dfrac{7}{8}\right)^{3}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. Here it has value $\dfrac{512}{343}$.
