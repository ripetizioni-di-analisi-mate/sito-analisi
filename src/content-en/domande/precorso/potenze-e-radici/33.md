What is the value of $\left(\dfrac{9}{4}\right)^{-\frac{3}{2}}$?

- $\dfrac{27}{8}$
- $-\dfrac{27}{8}$
- $\dfrac{8}{27}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The negative exponent leads to the reciprocal fraction, so $\left(\dfrac{9}{4}\right)^{-\frac{3}{2}} = \left(\dfrac{4}{9}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}$. By the definition of a power with rational exponent, $a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m} = \left(\sqrt[n]{a}\right)^m$, therefore the square root can be computed first and then the cube, and we obtain
$$
\left(\frac{4}{9}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} = \left(\sqrt{\frac{4}{9}}\right)^{3} = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{3} = \frac{8}{27} .
$$
The result is positive, because the base $\dfrac{9}{4}$ is positive.
