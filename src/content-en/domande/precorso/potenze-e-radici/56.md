The powers $2^{\frac{1}{3}}$ and $4^{\frac{1}{3}}$ have the same exponent. What is the value of their product $2^{\frac{1}{3}} \cdot 4^{\frac{1}{3}}$?

- $\sqrt[6]{8}$
- $4$
- $\sqrt[9]{8}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The product of powers with the same exponent, $a^{n} \cdot b^{n} = (ab)^{n}$, also holds for rational exponents, because the bases are positive. So
$$
2^{\frac{1}{3}} \cdot 4^{\frac{1}{3}} = (2 \cdot 4)^{\frac{1}{3}} = 8^{\frac{1}{3}} = \sqrt[3]{8} = 2 .
$$
The bases $2$ and $4$ are multiplied and the common exponent $\dfrac{1}{3}$ stays the same. Written as roots, the two factors are $\sqrt[3]{2}$ and $\sqrt[3]{4}$, with the same index; adding the indices one would get $\sqrt[6]{8} = \sqrt{2}$, which is different from $2$.
