The radicand of $\sqrt{x^{2} - 4x + 4}$ is the square of a binomial. For $x < 2$, which expression without roots is it equal to?

- $x - 2$
- $2 - x$
- $x + 2$
- $-x - 2$

## Explanation

By the square of a binomial $x^{2} - 4x + 4 = (x - 2)^{2}$, so, by the square root of a square, $\sqrt{x^{2} - 4x + 4} = |x - 2|$. For $x < 2$ the number $x - 2$ is negative, therefore its absolute value is the opposite $2 - x$.

For example, for $x = 0$ the root equals $\sqrt{4} = 2$, while $x - 2 = -2$.
