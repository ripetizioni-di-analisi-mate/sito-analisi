Which of the following numbers is greater than $1$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{5}$
- $\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^{-6}$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{-5}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. Hence $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{-5} = \dfrac{1024}{243}$ is greater than $1$.
