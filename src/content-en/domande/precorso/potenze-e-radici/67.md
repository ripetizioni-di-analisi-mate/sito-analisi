Which power or fraction exceeds $1$ among these expressions involving 3 and 4?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{5}$
- $\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^{-6}$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{-5}$

## Explanation

A power with positive base below $1$ exceeds $1$ when the exponent is negative. Here it has value $\dfrac{1024}{243}$.
