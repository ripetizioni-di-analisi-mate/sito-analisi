For every real $x$, which expression is the cube root $\sqrt[3]{-8x^{6}}$ equal to?

- $-2x^{2}$
- $2x^{2}$
- $-2x^{3}$
- $-8x^{2}$

## Explanation

The radicand is the cube of $-2x^{2}$, because $\left(-2x^{2}\right)^{3} = (-2)^{3}\left(x^{2}\right)^{3} = -8x^{6}$. A root of odd index exists for every real radicand and is the unique real number whose cube is the radicand, so
$$
\sqrt[3]{-8x^{6}} = -2x^{2} .
$$
The radicand $-8x^{6}$ is less than or equal to zero, therefore its cube root is also less than or equal to zero, while $2x^{2}$ is greater than or equal to zero.
