A factor outside a root can be moved under the radical sign. Which radical is equal to the number $2\sqrt[3]{5}$?

- $\sqrt[3]{10}$
- $\sqrt[3]{20}$
- $\sqrt{20}$
- $\sqrt[3]{40}$

## Explanation

For $k, a \geq 0$ we have $k\sqrt[n]{a} = \sqrt[n]{k^{n}}\,\sqrt[n]{a} = \sqrt[n]{k^{n} a}$, so a factor outside enters under the radical sign raised to the index of the root. Here the index is $3$, therefore the factor $2$ enters as $2^{3} = 8$, and we obtain
$$
2\sqrt[3]{5} = \sqrt[3]{2^{3} \cdot 5} = \sqrt[3]{40} .
$$
Moving $2$ inside without raising it one would get $\sqrt[3]{10}$, and squaring it as for a square root one would get $\sqrt[3]{20}$.
