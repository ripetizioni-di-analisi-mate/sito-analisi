By squaring, the positive numbers $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$, $\sqrt{5}$ and $\sqrt{10}$ can be compared. Which ordering from smallest to largest is correct?

- $\sqrt{5} < \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} < \sqrt{10}$
- $\sqrt{5} < \sqrt{10} < \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{5} = \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} < \sqrt{10}$
- $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} < \sqrt{5} < \sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

By the comparison of powers with the same positive exponent, two positive numbers are in the same order as their squares. By the square of a binomial $\left(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}\right)^{2} = 5 + 2\sqrt{6}$, while the squares of the other two numbers are $5$ and $10$. Since $2\sqrt{6} = \sqrt{24}$ and $16 < 24 < 25$, we have $4 < 2\sqrt{6} < 5$, so $5 < 5 + 2\sqrt{6} < 10$. Therefore $\sqrt{5} < \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} < \sqrt{10}$.

A sum of roots is in general different from the root of the sum, so $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$ is different from $\sqrt{5}$.
