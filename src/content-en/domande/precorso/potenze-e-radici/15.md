Which of the following equalities is true?

- $-1^{100} = 1$
- $(-5)^0 = -1$
- $(-1)^{101} = -1$
- $(-2)^{-2} = -\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The base $-1$ raised to the odd exponent $101$ gives a negative result, so $(-1)^{101} = -1$ and the equality is true.

In the expression $-1^{100}$ the exponentiation comes before the change of sign, therefore $-1^{100} = -\left(1^{100}\right) = -1$. For every nonzero base the power with exponent $0$ equals $1$, so $(-5)^0 = 1$. Finally $(-2)^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{(-2)^2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$, which is positive.
