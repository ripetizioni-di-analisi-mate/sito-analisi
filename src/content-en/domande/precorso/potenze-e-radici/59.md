Powers with real exponents and positive base have the same properties as those with integer exponents. Which power of $2$ is the number $\left(\sqrt{2}\right)^{\sqrt{8}}$ equal to?

- $2^{2\sqrt{2}}$
- $2^{\frac{1}{2} + 2\sqrt{2}}$
- $2$
- $2^{\sqrt{2}}$

## Explanation

Since $\sqrt{2} = 2^{\frac{1}{2}}$ and $\sqrt{8} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 2} = 2\sqrt{2}$, by the power of a power the exponents are multiplied, so
$$
\left(\sqrt{2}\right)^{\sqrt{8}} = \left(2^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{2\sqrt{2}} = 2^{\frac{1}{2} \cdot 2\sqrt{2}} = 2^{\sqrt{2}} .
$$
Writing the base $\sqrt{2}$ as $2$ without multiplying the exponent by $\dfrac{1}{2}$ one would get $2^{2\sqrt{2}}$, and adding the exponents one would get $2^{\frac{1}{2} + 2\sqrt{2}}$.
