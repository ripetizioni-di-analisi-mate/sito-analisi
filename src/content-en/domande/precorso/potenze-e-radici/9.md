- $\sqrt[3]{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{9}}{3}$
- $3\sqrt[3]{3}$
- $\sqrt[3]{9}$

## Explanation

Since $9 = 3^2$, the denominator is $\sqrt[3]{3^2}$. To rationalize a cube root we multiply by the factor that completes the cube under the root, in this case $\sqrt[3]{3}$, because $\sqrt[3]{3^2} \cdot \sqrt[3]{3} = \sqrt[3]{27} = 3$. Then
$$
\frac{3}{\sqrt[3]{9}} = \frac{3\,\sqrt[3]{3}}{\sqrt[3]{27}} = \frac{3\,\sqrt[3]{3}}{3} = \sqrt[3]{3} .
$$
With the factor $\sqrt[3]{9}$ the denominator would become $\sqrt[3]{9} \cdot \sqrt[3]{9} = \sqrt[3]{81}$, which is different from $9$ and is still irrational, because $81$ is not the cube of an integer.
