The inequality $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x - 1) < \log_{\frac{1}{2}} (7 - x)$ is equivalent to a system made of the inequality between the arguments and one domain condition. Which statement is true?

- The direction is preserved and the condition $x - 1 > 0$ is added
- The direction is reversed and the condition $7 - x > 0$ is added
- The direction is reversed and the condition $x - 1 > 0$ is added
- The direction is preserved and the condition $7 - x > 0$ is added

## Explanation

The base $\dfrac{1}{2}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, so the logarithmic function is decreasing and the direction **is reversed**, giving $x - 1 > 7 - x$. The system contains the domain condition of the smaller argument, which is $7 - x$, because the positivity of $x - 1$ follows from $x - 1 > 7 - x > 0$. The system with $x - 1 > 7 - x$ and $7 - x > 0$ gives $4 < x < 7$. With the condition $x - 1 > 0$ in place of $7 - x > 0$ one would obtain $x > 4$, which includes values such as $x = 8$ for which $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (7 - x)$ does not exist.
