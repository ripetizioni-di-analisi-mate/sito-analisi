In the equation $(\log_2 x)^{2} + 3 \log_2 x + 2 = 0$ the substitution $t = \log_2 x$ gives $t = -1$ and $t = -2$. Which statement is true?

- Both values of $t$ are discarded because they are negative, so the equation has no solutions
- The values of $t$ give $x = -1$ and $x = -2$, which are discarded
- The values of $t$ give $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$, which are discarded because they are less than $1$
- The values of $t$ give $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$, and both are solutions

## Explanation

The logarithm can take any real value, so no value of $t$ is discarded because of its sign. By the definition of logarithm, $t = -1$ gives $x = 2^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and $t = -2$ gives $x = 2^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. The only domain restriction is $x > 0$ and both values satisfy it, therefore **both are solutions**.
