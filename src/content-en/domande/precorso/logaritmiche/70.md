What is the solution set of $\left[\log_{2}\left(x\right)\right]^2-7\cdot \log_{2}\left(x\right)+10=0$?

- $\left\{2, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{128\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 32\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{32}, \dfrac{1}{4}\right\}$

## Explanation

Substitute $t=\log_{2}\left(x\right)$ to get a quadratic with roots 2 and 5. Converting back to $x$ gives $\left\{4, 32\right\}$.
