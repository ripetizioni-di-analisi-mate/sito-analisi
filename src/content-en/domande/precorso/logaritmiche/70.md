How does $\log_2 \bigl(8 \cdot 2^{x}\bigr)$ simplify for every real $x$?

- $3x$
- $x + 8$
- $x + 3$
- $8x$

## Explanation

The argument $8 \cdot 2^{x}$ is positive for every $x$. By the property of the logarithm of a product, $\log_2 \bigl(8 \cdot 2^{x}\bigr) = \log_2 8 + \log_2 \bigl(2^{x}\bigr)$. Since $\log_2 8 = 3$ and $\log_2 \bigl(2^{x}\bigr) = x$, we obtain $x + 3$. The same result follows from $8 \cdot 2^{x} = 2^{x + 3}$.
