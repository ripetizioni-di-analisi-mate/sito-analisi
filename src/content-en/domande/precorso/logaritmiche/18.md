What is the value of $\log_9 27$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

By the change of base formula, choosing base $3$, we have
$$
\log_9 27 = \frac{\log_3 27}{\log_3 9} .
$$
Since $3^{3} = 27$ and $3^{2} = 9$, the numerator equals $3$ and the denominator equals $2$. Therefore $\log_9 27 = \dfrac{3}{2}$, and indeed $9^{\frac{3}{2}} = \bigl(\sqrt{9}\bigr)^{3} = 27$.
