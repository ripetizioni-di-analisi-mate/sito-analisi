What are the domain conditions of the expression $\log_2 x + \log_2 (x^{2} - 9)$?

- $x > 0$
- $x > 3$
- $0 < x < 3$
- $x > -3$

## Explanation

The arguments of both logarithms must be positive, so the conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\
x^{2} - 9 > 0
\end{cases}
$$
The inequality $x^{2} - 9 > 0$ is satisfied for $x < -3$ or $x > 3$. Among these values only those with $x > 3$ are positive, therefore the domain conditions are $x > 3$.
