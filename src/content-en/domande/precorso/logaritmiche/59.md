The figure shows the graph of a function $f$ defined for $x > 0$, with vertical asymptote $x = 0$; the graph passes through the points $(1, -1)$, $(3, 0)$ and $(9, 1)$. Which expression does $f$ have?

::grafico{f="log(x)/log(3)-1" xmin=-1 xmax=10 ymin=-3 ymax=2 punti="1,-1,pieno;3,0,pieno;9,1,pieno" legenda=0 titolo="The graph of $y = f(x)$"}

- $f(x) = \log_3 (x - 1)$
- $f(x) = \log_3 x - 1$
- $f(x) = \log_3 x + 1$
- $f(x) = \log_{\frac{1}{3}} x - 1$

## Explanation

The function $\log_3 x - 1$ is defined for $x > 0$ and equals $\log_3 1 - 1 = -1$ at $x = 1$, $\log_3 3 - 1 = 0$ at $x = 3$ and $\log_3 9 - 1 = 1$ at $x = 9$, so its graph passes through the three points. The function $\log_3 (x - 1)$ is defined only for $x > 1$ and has asymptote $x = 1$. The function $\log_3 x + 1$ equals $2$ at $x = 3$. The function $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} x - 1$ is decreasing, while the graph in the figure is increasing.
