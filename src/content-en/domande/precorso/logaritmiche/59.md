What is the solution set of $\left[\log_{3}\left(x\right)\right]^2-10\cdot \log_{3}\left(x\right)+21=0$?

- $\left\{59049\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2187}, \dfrac{1}{27}\right\}$
- $\left\{3, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{27, 2187\right\}$

## Explanation

Substitute $t=\log_{3}\left(x\right)$ to get a quadratic with roots 3 and 7. Converting back to $x$ gives $\left\{27, 2187\right\}$.
