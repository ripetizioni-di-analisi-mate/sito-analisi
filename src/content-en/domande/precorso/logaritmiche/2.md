- $x = 6$
- $x = -3$ and $x = 6$
- $x = -3$
- $x = -6$ and $x = 3$

## Explanation

The logarithms have the same base $5$, so the equation is equivalent to the equality of the arguments, in a system with the positivity of one of them:
$$
\begin{cases}
x^{2} - 9 = 3x + 9 \\
3x + 9 > 0
\end{cases}
$$
The equation $x^{2} - 3x - 18 = 0$ factors as $(x - 6)(x + 3) = 0$, therefore it gives $x = 6$ or $x = -3$. For $x = -3$ the argument $3x + 9$ equals $0$, so the logarithms do not exist and the value **is discarded**. For $x = 6$ both arguments equal $27 > 0$, therefore the only solution is $x = 6$.
