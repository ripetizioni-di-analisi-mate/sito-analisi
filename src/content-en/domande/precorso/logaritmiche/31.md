What are the solutions of the equation $(\log_2 x)^{2} - \log_2 (x^{4}) = 5$?

- $x = 32$
- $x = 32$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x = 5$ and $x = -1$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{32}$ and $x = 2$

## Explanation

The term $(\log_2 x)^{2}$ requires $x > 0$, so the domain condition is $x > 0$. For these values the property of the logarithm of a power applies, $\log_2 (x^{4}) = 4 \log_2 x$, and with $t = \log_2 x$ the equation becomes $t^{2} - 4t - 5 = 0$. The trinomial factors as $(t - 5)(t + 1)$, so $t = 5$ or $t = -1$. By the definition of logarithm we get $x = 2^{5} = 32$ or $x = 2^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and both values are positive.
