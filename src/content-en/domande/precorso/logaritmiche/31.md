How many solutions of $(\log_2 x)^2-4\log_2 x+3=0$ belong to $[1,4]$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$, counting $1$ and $3$, the two roots in the substituted variable, directly.
- $3$

## Explanation

On the stated interval $t=\log_2 x$ ranges from 0 to 2. The quadratic has roots $1$ and $3$. Only the first belongs to the interval for $t$, so it gives one admissible solution.
