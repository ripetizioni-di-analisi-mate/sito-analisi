What are the solutions of the equation $\log_2 x + \log_2 (x + 3) = \log_2 (x - 1)$?

- $x = -1$
- $x = 1$
- The equation has no solutions
- $x = -1$ and $x = 1$

## Explanation

The domain restrictions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\
x + 3 > 0 \\
x - 1 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff x > 1 .
$$
Under these conditions the property of the logarithm of a product applies, and equating the arguments gives $x(x + 3) = x - 1$, that is $x^{2} + 2x + 1 = 0$. The left-hand side is the square $(x + 1)^{2}$, so the algebraic equation has the single solution $x = -1$. Since $-1$ does not satisfy the condition $x > 1$, the equation **has no solutions**.
