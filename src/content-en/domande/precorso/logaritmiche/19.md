What is the value of the product $\log_2 5 \cdot \log_5 8$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $8$

## Explanation

By the change of base formula we have $\log_5 8 = \dfrac{\log_2 8}{\log_2 5}$. Substituting into the product, the factor $\log_2 5$, which is different from $0$, cancels and $\log_2 8$ remains. Since $2^{3} = 8$, the product equals $3$.
