Which values satisfy $\log_{3}\left(x + 6\right)> \log_{3}\left(11 - x\right)$?

- $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}, \infty\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{5}{2}, 11\right)$
- $\left(-6, \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}, 11\right)$

## Explanation

On the domain $\left(-6, 11\right)$ the logarithm is increasing. We compare the arguments, and $x + 6 > 11 - x$ gives $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}, 11\right)$.
