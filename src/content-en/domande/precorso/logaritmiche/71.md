How many solutions does the equation $\log_2 x = 3 - x$ have, and what are they?

- Two, $x = 1$ and $x = 2$
- None
- Only one, $x = 3$
- Only one, $x = 2$

## Explanation

The function $\log_2 x$ is strictly increasing, because the base $2$ is greater than $1$, while $3 - x$ is strictly decreasing. Therefore the difference $\log_2 x - (3 - x)$ is strictly increasing for $x > 0$, so it vanishes at most at one point and the equation has at most one solution. For $x = 2$ we have $\log_2 2 = 1$ and $3 - 2 = 1$, therefore $x = 2$ is the only solution. The equation is solved by monotonicity, because $x$ appears both in the argument of the logarithm and outside it.
