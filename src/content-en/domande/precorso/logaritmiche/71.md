What is the solution set of $\left[\log_{5}\left(x\right)\right]^2-13\cdot \log_{5}\left(x\right)+36=0$?

- $\left\{625, 1953125\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{1953125}, \dfrac{1}{625}\right\}$
- $\left\{1220703125\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 9\right\}$

## Explanation

Substitute $t=\log_{5}\left(x\right)$ to get a quadratic with roots 4 and 9. Converting back to $x$ gives $\left\{625, 1953125\right\}$.
