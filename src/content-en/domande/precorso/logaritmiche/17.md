What is the value of $2 \log_5 10 - \log_5 4$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $5$
- $25$

## Explanation

By the property of the logarithm of a power $2 \log_5 10 = \log_5 (10^{2}) = \log_5 100$. By the property of the quotient we then obtain $\log_5 100 - \log_5 4 = \log_5 \dfrac{100}{4} = \log_5 25$. Since $5^{2} = 25$, the value of the expression is $2$.
