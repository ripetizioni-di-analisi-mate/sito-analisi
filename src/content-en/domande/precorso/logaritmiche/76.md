What is the solution set of $\left[\log_{2}\left(x\right)\right]^2-13\cdot \log_{2}\left(x\right)+40=0$?

- $\left\{32, 256\right\}$
- $\left\{5, 8\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{256}, \dfrac{1}{32}\right\}$
- $\left\{8192\right\}$

## Explanation

Substitute $t=\log_{2}\left(x\right)$ to get a quadratic with roots 5 and 8. Converting back to $x$ gives $\left\{32, 256\right\}$.
