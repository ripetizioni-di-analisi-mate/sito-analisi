The argument of $f(x) = \log_2 \dfrac{x - 1}{x + 2}$ is a fraction. What is the domain of $f$?

- $x > -2$
- $-2 < x < 1$
- $x < -2$ or $x \geq 1$
- $x < -2$ or $x > 1$

## Explanation

We require the argument to be positive, that is $\dfrac{x - 1}{x + 2} > 0$. The numerator is positive for $x > 1$ and the denominator is positive for $x > -2$. The quotient is positive where numerator and denominator have the same sign, that is for $x < -2$ or $x > 1$. The value $x = 1$ makes the argument zero and the value $x = -2$ makes the denominator zero, therefore both are excluded.
