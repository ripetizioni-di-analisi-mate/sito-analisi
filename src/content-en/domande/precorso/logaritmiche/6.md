- $x = 8$ and $x = -2$
- $x = 8$

## Explanation

The domain conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\
x - 6 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff x > 6 .
$$
Under these conditions the property of the logarithm of a product applies, therefore the equation becomes $\log_2 \bigl(x(x - 6)\bigr) = 4$. By the definition of logarithm we have $x(x - 6) = 2^{4} = 16$, that is $x^{2} - 6x - 16 = 0$, with solutions $x = 8$ and $x = -2$. The value $x = -2$ does not satisfy the condition $x > 6$ and is discarded, so the only solution is $x = 8$.
