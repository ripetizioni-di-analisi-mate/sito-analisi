Where is the expression $\log_{3}\left(x + 3\right)-\log_{3}\left(x - 7\right)$ defined?

- $\left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Each argument must be positive. The intersection of the two rays is $\left(7, \infty\right)$.
