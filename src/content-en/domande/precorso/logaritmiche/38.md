What are the solutions of the inequality $\log_2 x + \log_2 (x - 2) < 3$?

- $-2 < x < 4$
- $x > 4$
- $2 < x < 4$
- $0 < x < 4$

## Explanation

The domain conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\
x - 2 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff x > 2 .
$$
Under these conditions the property of the logarithm of a product applies, and the inequality becomes $\log_2 \bigl(x(x - 2)\bigr) < \log_2 8$. The base is greater than $1$, so the direction is preserved and we obtain $x^{2} - 2x - 8 < 0$, that is $(x - 4)(x + 2) < 0$, satisfied for $-2 < x < 4$. Intersecting with the condition $x > 2$, the solutions are $2 < x < 4$.
