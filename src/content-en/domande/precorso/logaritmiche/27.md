What are the solutions of the equation $\log_{10} (x^{2} + 36) = 2$?

- $x = 8$
- $x = 8$ and $x = -8$
- $x = -8$
- $x = 2\sqrt{34}$ and $x = -2\sqrt{34}$

## Explanation

The argument $x^{2} + 36$ is positive for every real $x$, so the domain condition is always satisfied. By the definition of logarithm the equation is equivalent to $x^{2} + 36 = 10^{2} = 100$, that is to $x^{2} = 64$. Therefore the solutions are $x = 8$ and $x = -8$.
