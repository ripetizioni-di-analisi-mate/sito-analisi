- $x = 3$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $x = \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $x = -\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

By the definition of logarithm, the equation is equivalent to $5x + 1 = 4^{\frac{1}{2}}$. The power with exponent $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the square root, therefore $4^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{4} = 2$. From $5x + 1 = 2$ we obtain $5x = 1$, that is $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$. For this value the argument equals $2 > 0$, so the domain condition is satisfied.
