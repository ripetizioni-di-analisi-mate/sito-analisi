The product $\log_2 (4x) \cdot \log_2 x$ equals $3$ for two values of $x$. Which ones?

- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = 8$
- $x = 1$ and $x = -3$
- $x = 2$ and $x = 8$
- $x = 2$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x > 0$. By the property of the logarithm of a product, $\log_2 (4x) = 2 + \log_2 x$. With the substitution $t = \log_2 x$ the equation becomes $(2 + t)\, t = 3$, that is $t^{2} + 2t - 3 = 0$, which factors as $(t + 3)(t - 1) = 0$. The value $t = 1$ gives $x = 2$ and the value $t = -3$ gives $x = 2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$, both positive and therefore acceptable.
