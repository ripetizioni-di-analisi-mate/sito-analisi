A sensor has response $F(x)=(\log_2x)^2+\log_2x$ for $x>0$. For which positive input does multiplying the input by four leave the reading unchanged?

- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $2^{\dfrac{3}{2}}$, because $4t+6=0$ would give $4t=6$.
- $2^{-2}$
- $2^{-\dfrac{3}{2}}$

## Explanation

Setting $t=\log_2x$, multiplication by four replaces $t$ by $t+2$. Equality of the readings requires $(t+2)^2+(t+2)-(t^2+t)=4t+6=0$. The quadratic term cancels, so $t=-\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $x=2^{-\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
