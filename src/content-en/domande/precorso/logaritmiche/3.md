## Explanation

By the definition of logarithm, $\log_3 (x - 2) = 3$ is equivalent to $x - 2 = 3^{3}$. Since $3^{3} = 27$, we obtain $x - 2 = 27$, that is $x = 29$. The domain condition $x - 2 > 0$ is satisfied, because $x - 2 = 27 > 0$.
