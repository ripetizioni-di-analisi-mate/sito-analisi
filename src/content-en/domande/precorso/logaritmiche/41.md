For which values of $x$ does the equality $\log_3 (x^{2}) = 2 \log_3 x$ hold?

- For every real $x$
- For every $x \neq 0$
- For $x > 0$
- For $x \geq 1$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is defined for $x^{2} > 0$, that is for every $x \neq 0$, while the right-hand side is defined only for $x > 0$. For $x > 0$ the property of the logarithm of a power applies, so the equality holds. For $x < 0$ the right-hand side is undefined, therefore the equality holds exactly for $x > 0$. For example, with $x = -3$ the left-hand side equals $\log_3 9 = 2$ and the right-hand side is not defined.
