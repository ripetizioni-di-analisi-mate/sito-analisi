A capital $C$ invested at $5\%$ annual compound interest becomes $C \cdot 1.05^{n}$ after $n$ years. Which expression gives the number of years $n$ after which the capital doubles?

- $n = \dfrac{\log_{10} 1.05}{\log_{10} 2}$
- $n = \log_{10} \dfrac{2}{1.05}$
- $n = \dfrac{\log_{10} 2}{0.05}$
- $n = \dfrac{\log_{10} 2}{\log_{10} 1.05}$

## Explanation

The capital doubles when $C \cdot 1.05^{n} = 2C$, that is when $1.05^{n} = 2$. By the definition of logarithm, $n = \log_{1.05} 2$. By the change of base formula, with the new base $10$, we obtain $n = \dfrac{\log_{10} 2}{\log_{10} 1.05}$, which is about $14.2$ years.
