Which values satisfy $\log_{\dfrac{1}{5}}\left(x + 4\right)\leq 0$?

- $\left[-4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-4, -3\right]$
- $\left[-3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The base is below $1$, so the logarithm is decreasing and is zero when its argument is $1$. Require $x + 4 \geq 1$ to obtain $\left[-3, \infty\right)$.
