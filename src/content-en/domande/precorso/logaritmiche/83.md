What is the real solution set of $\log_{2}\left(x - 5\right)+\log_{2}\left(x + 5\right)=0$?

- $\left\{- \sqrt{26}, \sqrt{26}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{26}\right\}$
- $\left\{6\right\}$
- $\left\{\sqrt{26}\right\}$

## Explanation

The common domain requires $x>5$. The sum is the logarithm of the product, and the equation becomes $x^{2} - 25 = 1$. On the domain the result is $\left\{\sqrt{26}\right\}$.
