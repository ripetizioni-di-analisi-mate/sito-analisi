What is the real solution set of $\log_{2}\left(x - 2\right)+\log_{2}\left(x + 2\right)=0$?

- $\left\{3\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{5}, \sqrt{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{\sqrt{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{5}\right\}$

## Explanation

The common domain requires $x>2$. The sum is the logarithm of the product, and the equation becomes $x^{2} - 4 = 1$. On the domain the result is $\left\{\sqrt{5}\right\}$.
