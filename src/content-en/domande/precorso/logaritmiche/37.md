What are the solutions of the inequality $\log_{\frac{1}{5}} (x + 2) \geq \log_{\frac{1}{5}} (3x)$?

- $x \geq 1$
- $x \leq 1$
- $0 < x \leq 1$
- $-2 < x \leq 1$

## Explanation

The base $\dfrac{1}{5}$ lies between $0$ and $1$, so the direction **is reversed** when passing to the arguments and gives $x + 2 \leq 3x$. The system contains the domain condition of the smaller argument, which is $x + 2$:
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2 \leq 3x \\
x + 2 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x \geq 1 \\
x > -2
\end{cases}
$$
The positivity of $3x$ follows from $3x \geq x + 2 > 0$, so the solutions are $x \geq 1$.
