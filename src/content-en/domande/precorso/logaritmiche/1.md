- $x = 2$
- $x = -3$
- $x = 3$
- $x = 6$

## Explanation

The arguments of the two logarithms must be positive, therefore the domain conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 5 > 0 \\
3x - 1 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff x > \frac{1}{3} .
$$
The two logarithms have the same base $2$ and the logarithmic function is injective, so the equation is equivalent to the equality of the arguments $x + 5 = 3x - 1$. From this we obtain $2x = 6$, that is $x = 3$. Since $3 > \dfrac{1}{3}$, the value found satisfies the domain conditions and **is the solution**.
