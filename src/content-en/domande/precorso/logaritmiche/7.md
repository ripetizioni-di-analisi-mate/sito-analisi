- $x = 7$
- $x = 3$
- $x = -5$
- No solution

## Explanation

The domain conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2 > 0 \\
x - 4 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff x > 4 .
$$
Under these conditions the property of the logarithm of a quotient applies, therefore the equation becomes $\log_3 \dfrac{x + 2}{x - 4} = 1$. By the definition of logarithm we have $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 4} = 3$, and multiplying by $x - 4 \neq 0$ we obtain $x + 2 = 3x - 12$. Hence $2x = 14$, that is $x = 7$, which satisfies the condition $x > 4$ and **is the solution**.
