How are the numbers $\log_3 2$, $1$ and $\log_2 3$ ordered?

- $\log_2 3 < 1 < \log_3 2$
- $1 < \log_3 2 < \log_2 3$
- $\log_3 2 < 1 < \log_2 3$
- $\log_3 2 < \log_2 3 < 1$

## Explanation

The number $1$ can be written as $\log_3 3$ and also as $\log_2 2$. The bases $2$ and $3$ are greater than $1$, so both logarithmic functions are increasing. Since $2 < 3$, we have $\log_3 2 < \log_3 3 = 1$. Since $3 > 2$, we have $\log_2 3 > \log_2 2 = 1$. Therefore $\log_3 2 < 1 < \log_2 3$.
