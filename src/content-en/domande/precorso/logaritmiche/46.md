What is the solution set of $\left[\log_{3}\left(x\right)\right]^2-17\cdot \log_{3}\left(x\right)+66=0$?

- $\left\{129140163\right\}$
- $\left\{729, 177147\right\}$
- $\left\{6, 11\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{177147}, \dfrac{1}{729}\right\}$

## Explanation

Substitute $t=\log_{3}\left(x\right)$ to get a quadratic with roots 6 and 11. Converting back to $x$ gives $\left\{729, 177147\right\}$.
