Which condition on $x$ ensures that $\log_3 (5 - 2x)$ exists?

- $x > \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x < \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x \leq \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x > -\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

The logarithm is defined for strictly positive arguments, so we require $5 - 2x > 0$, that is $-2x > -5$. Dividing by $-2$, which is negative, reverses the direction of the inequality, therefore $x < \dfrac{5}{2}$. The value $x = \dfrac{5}{2}$ makes the argument zero and is excluded from the domain.
