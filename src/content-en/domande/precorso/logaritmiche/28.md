What are the solutions of the equation $\log_2 (\log_3 x) = 1$?

- $x = 8$
- $x = 3$
- $x = 6$
- $x = 9$

## Explanation

By the definition of logarithm the equation is equivalent to $\log_3 x = 2^{1} = 2$. Applying the definition again gives $x = 3^{2} = 9$. The domain conditions require $x > 0$ and $\log_3 x > 0$, that is $x > 1$, and the value $x = 9$ satisfies them.
