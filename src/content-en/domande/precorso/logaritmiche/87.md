Applying the logarithm in base $10$ to both sides solves $x^{\log_{10} x} = 100$, with $x > 0$. Which solutions are obtained?

- $x = 10^{\sqrt{2}}$
- $x = 10^{\sqrt{2}}$ and $x = 10^{-\sqrt{2}}$
- $x = 10$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{10}$
- $x = \sqrt{2}$ and $x = -\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Both sides are positive, so the equation is equivalent to the one obtained by applying the logarithm in base $10$ to both sides. By the power property, $\log_{10} \bigl(x^{\log_{10} x}\bigr) = \log_{10} x \cdot \log_{10} x = (\log_{10} x)^{2}$, and $\log_{10} 100 = 2$. With the substitution $t = \log_{10} x$ we obtain $t^{2} = 2$, that is $t = \sqrt{2}$ or $t = -\sqrt{2}$. By the definition of logarithm, $x = 10^{\sqrt{2}}$ or $x = 10^{-\sqrt{2}}$, both positive.
