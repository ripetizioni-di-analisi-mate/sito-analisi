What are the solutions of the inequality $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x - 1) < 2$?

- $1 < x < \dfrac{5}{4}$
- $x > \dfrac{5}{4}$
- $x < \dfrac{5}{4}$
- $x > 5$

## Explanation

The number $2$ is written as a logarithm in base $\dfrac{1}{2}$, that is $2 = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} \dfrac{1}{4}$, because $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. The base lies between $0$ and $1$, so the direction **is reversed** and we obtain $x - 1 > \dfrac{1}{4}$, that is $x > \dfrac{5}{4}$. The domain condition $x - 1 > 0$ is satisfied by these values, because $x - 1 > \dfrac{1}{4} > 0$. The solutions are therefore $x > \dfrac{5}{4}$.
