What is the value of $\log_6 9 + \log_6 4$?

- $\log_6 13$
- $2$
- $13$
- $36$

## Explanation

The arguments $9$ and $4$ are positive, so the property of the logarithm of a product applies and gives $\log_6 9 + \log_6 4 = \log_6 (9 \cdot 4) = \log_6 36$. Since $6^{2} = 36$, by the definition of logarithm $\log_6 36 = 2$.
