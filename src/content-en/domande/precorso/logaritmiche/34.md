How many integers $n$ satisfy $\log_2(n+3)<3$?

- $8$, including $n=5$.
- $5$
- $7$
- Infinitely many.

## Explanation

The argument must be positive and less than $8$. Hence $-3<n<5$. The allowed integers run from $-2$ through $4$ inclusive.
