What is the value of $\log_3 54 - \log_3 2 + \log_3 \dfrac{1}{9}$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $-5$
- $1$

## Explanation

The arguments are positive numbers, so the properties of logarithms apply. By the property of the quotient $\log_3 54 - \log_3 2 = \log_3 27$, and $\log_3 27 = 3$ because $3^{3} = 27$. Moreover $\dfrac{1}{9} = 3^{-2}$, therefore $\log_3 \dfrac{1}{9} = -2$. The value of the expression is therefore $3 - 2 = 1$.
