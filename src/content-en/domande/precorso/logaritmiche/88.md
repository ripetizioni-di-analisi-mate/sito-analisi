Which values satisfy $\log_{\dfrac{1}{2}}\left(x + 5\right)\leq 0$?

- $\left[-4, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, -4\right]$
- $\left(-4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The base is below $1$, so the logarithm is decreasing and is zero when its argument is $1$. We require $x + 5 \geq 1$ to obtain $\left[-4, \infty\right)$.
