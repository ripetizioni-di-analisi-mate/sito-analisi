What are the solutions of the equation $\log_4 (x^{2} - 2x) = \log_4 (x + 4)$?

- $x = 4$ and $x = -1$
- $x = 4$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 1$ and $x = -4$

## Explanation

The logarithms have the same base, so the equation is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^{2} - 2x = x + 4 \\
x + 4 > 0
\end{cases}
$$
The equation $x^{2} - 3x - 4 = 0$ factors as $(x - 4)(x + 1) = 0$ and gives $x = 4$ or $x = -1$. For $x = 4$ the argument $x + 4$ equals $8$, for $x = -1$ it equals $3$, therefore **both values are solutions**. The domain restriction concerns the arguments, so the negative value $x = -1$ is also acceptable.
