What are the solutions of the equation $\log_2 x + \log_4 x = 6$?

- $x = 16$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 8$
- $x = 64$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x > 0$. By the change of base formula $\log_4 x = \dfrac{\log_2 x}{\log_2 4} = \dfrac{1}{2} \log_2 x$, so the equation becomes $\dfrac{3}{2} \log_2 x = 6$. Hence $\log_2 x = 4$, and by the definition of logarithm $x = 2^{4} = 16$, which satisfies $x > 0$.
