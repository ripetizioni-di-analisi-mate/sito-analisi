- $x = 1$ and $x = 3$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{27}$
- $x = 3$ and $x = 27$
- $x = 3$ and $x = 9$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x > 0$. With the substitution $t = \log_3 x$ the equation becomes $t^{2} - 4t + 3 = 0$, which factors as $(t - 1)(t - 3) = 0$ and gives $t = 1$ or $t = 3$. These are the values of the logarithm, therefore by the definition of logarithm we obtain $x = 3^{1} = 3$ or $x = 3^{3} = 27$. Both values are positive, so they **are acceptable**.
