Given that $\log_{10} 2 \approx 0.301$, between which powers of $10$ does the number $2^{100}$ lie?

- Between $10^{3}$ and $10^{4}$
- Between $10^{30}$ and $10^{31}$
- Between $10^{50}$ and $10^{51}$
- Between $10^{100}$ and $10^{101}$

## Explanation

By the power property, $\log_{10} \bigl(2^{100}\bigr) = 100 \log_{10} 2 \approx 30.1$. Since $2^{100} = 10^{\log_{10} (2^{100})}$ and the function $10^{x}$ is increasing, $30 < 30.1 < 31$ implies $10^{30} < 2^{100} < 10^{31}$. Hence $2^{100}$ written in base ten has $31$ digits.
