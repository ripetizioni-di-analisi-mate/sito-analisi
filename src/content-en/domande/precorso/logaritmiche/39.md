What are the solutions of the inequality $(\log_2 x)^{2} - \log_2 x - 2 < 0$?

- $-1 < x < 2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} < x < 4$
- $0 < x < 4$
- $x < \dfrac{1}{2}$ or $x > 4$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x > 0$. With the substitution $t = \log_2 x$ the inequality becomes $t^{2} - t - 2 < 0$, that is $(t - 2)(t + 1) < 0$, satisfied for $-1 < t < 2$. Hence $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{2} < \log_2 x < \log_2 4$, and since the base $2$ is greater than $1$ the direction is preserved and gives $\dfrac{1}{2} < x < 4$. These values are positive, therefore they satisfy the domain restriction.
