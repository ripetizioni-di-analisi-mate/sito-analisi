The sound level of a sound of intensity $I$ is $L = 10 \log_{10} \dfrac{I}{I_0}$ decibels, where $I_0$ is a reference intensity. If the intensity $I$ is multiplied by $1000$, by how much does the sound level increase?

- $3$ decibels
- $1000$ decibels
- $30$ decibels
- $10\,000$ decibels

## Explanation

With intensity $1000\, I$ the level becomes $10 \log_{10} \dfrac{1000\, I}{I_0}$. By the property of the logarithm of a product,
$$
10 \log_{10} \frac{1000\, I}{I_0} = 10 \log_{10} 1000 + 10 \log_{10} \frac{I}{I_0} = 30 + L ,
$$
because $\log_{10} 1000 = 3$. Hence the sound level increases by $30$ decibels, whatever the initial intensity.
