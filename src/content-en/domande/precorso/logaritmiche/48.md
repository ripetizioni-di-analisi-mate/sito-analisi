Which values satisfy $\log_{\dfrac{1}{3}}\left(x + 6\right)\leq 0$?

- $\left(-5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-6, -5\right]$

## Explanation

The base is below $1$, so the logarithm is decreasing and is zero when its argument is $1$. We require $x + 6 \geq 1$ to obtain $\left[-5, \infty\right)$.
