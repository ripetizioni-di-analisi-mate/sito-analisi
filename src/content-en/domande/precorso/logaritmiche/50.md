Where is the quotient $\dfrac{\log_2 x}{x - 3}$ negative?

- $0 < x < 1$ or $x > 3$
- $0 < x < 3$
- $x < 3$
- $1 < x < 3$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires $x > 0$ and the denominator requires $x \neq 3$, so the domain restrictions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\
x \neq 3
\end{cases}
$$
The base $2$ is greater than $1$, therefore the numerator $\log_2 x$ is negative for $0 < x < 1$, zero for $x = 1$ and positive for $x > 1$. The denominator $x - 3$ is negative for $x < 3$ and positive for $x > 3$. The quotient is negative where numerator and denominator have opposite signs, that is for $1 < x < 3$.
