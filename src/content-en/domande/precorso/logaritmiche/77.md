Which number is obtained by computing $2^{\log_4 9}$?

- $9$
- $81$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

By the change of base formula, with the new base $2$, we have $\log_4 9 = \dfrac{\log_2 9}{\log_2 4} = \dfrac{\log_2 9}{2}$. Since $9 = 3^{2}$, by the power property $\log_2 9 = 2 \log_2 3$, so $\log_4 9 = \log_2 3$. By the identity $a^{\log_a x} = x$ we conclude that $2^{\log_4 9} = 2^{\log_2 3} = 3$.
