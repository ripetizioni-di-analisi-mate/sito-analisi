What is the real solution set of $\log_{5}\left(x - 4\right)+\log_{5}\left(x + 4\right)=0$?

- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{\sqrt{17}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{17}, \sqrt{17}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{17}\right\}$

## Explanation

The common domain requires $x>4$. Adding the logarithms gives the log of the product, so $x^{2} - 16 = 1$. On the domain the result is $\left\{\sqrt{17}\right\}$.
