In $(\log_3 x)^2=2\log_3 x$ both sides are divided by $\log_3 x$. Which value must be checked separately, and why?

- $x=0$, because its logarithm is zero.
- $x=3$, because the divisor is one.
- $x=1$, because the divisor vanishes and the original equation holds.
- $x=9$, because the divisor equals two.

## Explanation

The base-three logarithm is zero at $x=1$. The original equality reads $0=0$, but division discards that value.
