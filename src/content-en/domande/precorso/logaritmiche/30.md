What are the solutions of the equation $(\log_3 x)^{2} - 2 \log_3 x = 0$?

- $x = 9$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 9$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 9$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 6$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x > 0$. With the substitution $t = \log_3 x$ the equation becomes $t^{2} - 2t = 0$, that is $t(t - 2) = 0$, with solutions $t = 0$ and $t = 2$. By the definition of logarithm we get $x = 3^{0} = 1$ or $x = 3^{2} = 9$. Both values are positive, therefore **they are acceptable**.
