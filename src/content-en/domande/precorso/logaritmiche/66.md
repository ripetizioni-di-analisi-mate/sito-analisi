The exponential equation $3 \cdot 2^{x} = 15$ is solved with a logarithm. What is $x$?

- $x = \log_6 15$
- $x = \log_5 2$
- $x = \log_2 5$
- $x = \dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides by $3$ we get $2^{x} = 5$. By the definition of logarithm, the exponent to which $2$ must be raised to obtain $5$ is $\log_2 5$, so $x = \log_2 5$. This value lies between $2$ and $3$, because $2^{2} = 4 < 5 < 8 = 2^{3}$.
