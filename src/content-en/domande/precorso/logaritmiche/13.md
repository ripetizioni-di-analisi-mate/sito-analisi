What is the value of $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{8} + \log_3 \sqrt{3}$?

- $-\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The number $\dfrac{1}{8}$ is written as $2^{-3}$, so by the definition of logarithm $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{8} = -3$. The square root is the power with exponent $\dfrac{1}{2}$, therefore $\sqrt{3} = 3^{\frac{1}{2}}$ and $\log_3 \sqrt{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The sum is therefore $-3 + \dfrac{1}{2} = -\dfrac{5}{2}$.
