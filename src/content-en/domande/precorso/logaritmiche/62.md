Which values of $x$ make the cube of $\log_2 x$ equal to $4\log_2 x$?

- $x = 4$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $x = 1$, $x = 4$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $x = 0$, $x = 2$ and $x = -2$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 4$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x > 0$. With the substitution $t = \log_2 x$ the equation becomes $t^{3} - 4t = 0$, that is $t(t - 2)(t + 2) = 0$, with solutions $t = 0$, $t = 2$ and $t = -2$. The value $t = 0$ must be kept, and dividing both sides by $t$ would lose it. By the definition of logarithm we obtain $x = 2^{0} = 1$, $x = 2^{2} = 4$ and $x = 2^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$, all positive.
