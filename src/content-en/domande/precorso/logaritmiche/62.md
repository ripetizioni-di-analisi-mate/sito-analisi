What is the real domain of $\log_{2}\left(\left(5 - x\right) \left(x + 2\right)\right)$?

- $\left(-\infty, -2\right) \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-2, 5\right]$
- $\left(-2, 5\right)$
- $\left(-2, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm’s argument must be strictly positive. The product is positive between its two zeros, giving the domain $\left(-2, 5\right)$.
