Given that $\log_2 3 = a$, what is the value of $\log_2 \dfrac{9}{8}$?

- $2a - 3$
- $a^{2} - 3$
- $\dfrac{2a}{3}$
- $2a + 3$

## Explanation

By the property of the logarithm of a quotient, $\log_2 \dfrac{9}{8} = \log_2 9 - \log_2 8$. Since $9 = 3^{2}$, by the power property $\log_2 9 = 2 \log_2 3 = 2a$. Moreover $\log_2 8 = 3$, so $\log_2 \dfrac{9}{8} = 2a - 3$.
