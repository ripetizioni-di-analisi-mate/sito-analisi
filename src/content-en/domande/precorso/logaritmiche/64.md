What is the real domain of $\log_{3}\left(\left(7 - x\right) \left(x + 3\right)\right)$?

- $\left[-3, 7\right]$
- $\left(-3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm’s argument must be strictly positive. The product is positive between its two zeros, giving the domain $\left(-3, 7\right)$.
