What is the real solution set of $\log_{3}\left(x - 6\right)+\log_{3}\left(x + 6\right)=0$?

- $\left\{\sqrt{37}\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{37}, \sqrt{37}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{37}\right\}$

## Explanation

The common domain requires $x>6$. The sum is the logarithm of the product, and the equation becomes $x^{2} - 36 = 1$. On the domain the result is $\left\{\sqrt{37}\right\}$.
