What is the solution of $\log_2 \sqrt{x + 7} = \log_2 (x + 1)$, where one argument contains a root?

- $x = 2$ and $x = -3$
- $x = 2$
- $x = -3$
- $x = 3$

## Explanation

The domain restrictions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 7 > 0 \\
x + 1 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff x > -1 .
$$
Since the logarithmic function is injective, the equation is equivalent to $\sqrt{x + 7} = x + 1$. For $x > -1$ both sides are positive, so squaring gives the equivalent equation $x + 7 = x^{2} + 2x + 1$, that is $x^{2} + x - 6 = 0$. Its solutions are $x = 2$ and $x = -3$, and only $x = 2$ satisfies the condition $x > -1$.
