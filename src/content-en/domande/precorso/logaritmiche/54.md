To which exponent must $\sqrt{2}$ be raised to obtain $8$, that is, what is $\log_{\sqrt{2}} 8$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $4$

## Explanation

By definition, $\log_{\sqrt{2}} 8$ is the exponent $c$ such that $(\sqrt{2})^{c} = 8$. Since $\sqrt{2} = 2^{\frac{1}{2}}$, we have $(\sqrt{2})^{c} = 2^{\frac{c}{2}}$, while $8 = 2^{3}$. The equality holds for $\dfrac{c}{2} = 3$, so $\log_{\sqrt{2}} 8 = 6$.
