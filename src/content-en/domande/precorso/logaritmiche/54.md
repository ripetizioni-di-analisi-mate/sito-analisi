What is the real solution set of $\log_{3}\left(x - 3\right)+\log_{3}\left(x + 3\right)=0$?

- $\left\{4\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{10}\right\}$
- $\left\{\sqrt{10}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \sqrt{10}, \sqrt{10}\right\}$

## Explanation

The common domain requires $x>3$. The sum is the logarithm of the product, and the equation becomes $x^{2} - 9 = 1$. On the domain the result is $\left\{\sqrt{10}\right\}$.
