What is the value of $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 16$?

- $-4$
- $4$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $-2$

## Explanation

By definition, $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 16$ is the exponent $c$ such that $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{c} = 16$. Since $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{c} = 2^{-c}$ and $16 = 2^{4}$, the equality holds for $-c = 4$. Hence $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 16 = -4$, a negative number because the base is less than $1$ and the argument is greater than $1$.
