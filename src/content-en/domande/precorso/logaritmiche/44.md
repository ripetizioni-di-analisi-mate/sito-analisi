What is the value of $\log_{\frac{1}{4}} 8$?

- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-2$

## Explanation

By the definition of logarithm we look for the exponent $c$ such that $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{c} = 8$. Since $\dfrac{1}{4} = 2^{-2}$ and $8 = 2^{3}$, the equation becomes $2^{-2c} = 2^{3}$. Two powers of $2$ are equal only if they have the same exponent, so $-2c = 3$ and $c = -\dfrac{3}{2}$.
