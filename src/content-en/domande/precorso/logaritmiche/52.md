Where is the expression $\log_{3}\left(x + 6\right)-\log_{3}\left(x - 11\right)$ defined?

- $\left(-6, \infty\right)$
- $\left[11, \infty\right)$
- $\left(11, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right) \cup \left(11, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Each argument must be positive. The intersection of the two rays is $\left(11, \infty\right)$.
