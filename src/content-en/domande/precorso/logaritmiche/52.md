For which value of $x$ is the logarithm $\log_2 (3x)$ equal to $0$?

- $x = 0$
- $x = 3$
- $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $3x > 0$, that is $x > 0$. By the definition of logarithm the equality $\log_2 (3x) = 0$ is equivalent to $3x = 2^{0} = 1$, so $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The logarithm equals $0$ when the argument equals $1$; for $x = 0$ the argument is zero and the logarithm does not exist.
