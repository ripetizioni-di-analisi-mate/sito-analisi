Which of the following expressions represents a real number?

- $\log_2 0$
- $\log_1 3$
- $\log_{-3} 9$
- $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} 9$

## Explanation

The logarithm $\log_a b$ is defined for $a > 0$, $a \neq 1$ and $b > 0$. In $\log_2 0$ the argument is zero, in $\log_1 3$ the base equals $1$ and in $\log_{-3} 9$ the base is negative, therefore these expressions do not represent numbers. In $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} 9$ the base lies between $0$ and $1$ and the argument is positive, so the logarithm exists. Its value is $-2$, because $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{-2} = 9$.
