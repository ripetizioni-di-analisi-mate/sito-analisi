Let $a > 0$ with $a \neq 1$. Which expression equals $\log_a \dfrac{1}{b}$ for every $b > 0$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\log_a b}$
- $\log_a b - 1$
- $-\log_a b$
- $\log_{\frac{1}{a}} \dfrac{1}{b}$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{1}{b} = b^{-1}$, by the power property $\log_a \dfrac{1}{b} = -\log_a b$. The same result follows from the quotient property, because $\log_a \dfrac{1}{b} = \log_a 1 - \log_a b$ and $\log_a 1 = 0$. For example, $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{8} = -3$, while $\dfrac{1}{\log_2 8} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
