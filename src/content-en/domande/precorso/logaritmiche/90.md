What is the real domain of $\log_{2}\left(\left(8 - x\right) \left(x + 5\right)\right)$?

- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(8, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, 8\right)$
- $\left[-5, 8\right]$

## Explanation

The logarithm’s argument must be strictly positive. The product is positive between its two zeros, giving the domain $\left(-5, 8\right)$.
