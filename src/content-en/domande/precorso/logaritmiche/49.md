Where is the expression $\log_{2}\left(x + 2\right)-\log_{2}\left(x - 5\right)$ defined?

- $\left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right) \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[5, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Each of the two arguments must be positive. The intersection of the two rays is $\left(5, \infty\right)$.
