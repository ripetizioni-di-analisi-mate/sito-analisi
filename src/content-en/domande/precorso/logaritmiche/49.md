If $\log_6 x + \log_6 4 = 2$, what is $x$?

- $x = 32$
- $x = 3$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x = 9$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x > 0$. By the property of the logarithm of a product the equation becomes $\log_6 (4x) = 2$. By the definition of logarithm, $4x = 6^{2} = 36$, so $x = 9$, which satisfies the domain restriction.
