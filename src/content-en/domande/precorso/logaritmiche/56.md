Comparing logarithms in different bases, for which $x$ do we have $\log_2 x > \log_4 (x + 6)$?

- $x < -2$ or $x > 3$
- $0 < x < 3$
- $x > 3$
- $x < -2$

## Explanation

The domain restrictions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\
x + 6 > 0
\end{cases}
\iff x > 0 .
$$
By the change of base formula, $\log_2 x = \dfrac{\log_4 x}{\log_4 2} = 2 \log_4 x$, and by the power property $2 \log_4 x = \log_4 (x^{2})$. The inequality becomes $\log_4 (x^{2}) > \log_4 (x + 6)$, and since the base $4$ is greater than $1$ the direction is preserved, so $x^{2} > x + 6$. This inequality is equivalent to $(x - 3)(x + 2) > 0$, satisfied for $x < -2$ or $x > 3$. Intersecting with the condition $x > 0$, the solutions are $x > 3$.
