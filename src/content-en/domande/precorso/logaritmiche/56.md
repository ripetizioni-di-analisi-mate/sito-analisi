Which values satisfy $\log_{3}\left(x + 3\right)> \log_{3}\left(7 - x\right)$?

- $\left[2, 7\right)$
- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, 2\right)$
- $\left(2, 7\right)$

## Explanation

On the domain $\left(-3, 7\right)$ the logarithm is increasing. Comparing the arguments with $x + 3 > 7 - x$ gives $\left(2, 7\right)$.
