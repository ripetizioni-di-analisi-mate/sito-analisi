What is the real domain of $\log_{3}\left(\left(11 - x\right) \left(x + 6\right)\right)$?

- $\left(-6, 11\right)$
- $\left[-6, 11\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right) \cup \left(11, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-6, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm’s argument must be strictly positive. The product is positive between its two zeros, giving the domain $\left(-6, 11\right)$.
