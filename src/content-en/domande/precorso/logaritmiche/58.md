Which values satisfy $\log_{2}\left(x + 2\right)> \log_{2}\left(5 - x\right)$?

- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-2, \dfrac{3}{2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, 5\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{3}{2}, 5\right)$

## Explanation

On the domain $\left(-2, 5\right)$ the logarithm is increasing. We compare the arguments, and $x + 2 > 5 - x$ gives $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, 5\right)$.
