The pH of an aqueous solution is $\mathrm{pH} = -\log_{10} c$, where $c$ is the concentration of hydrogen ions in moles per liter. What is $c$ in a solution whose pH equals $4$?

- $10^{4}$ moles per liter
- $4^{-10}$ moles per liter
- $0.4$ moles per liter
- $10^{-4}$ moles per liter

## Explanation

From the equality $-\log_{10} c = 4$ we get $\log_{10} c = -4$. By the definition of logarithm this equality is equivalent to $c = 10^{-4}$, that is $c = 0.0001$ moles per liter. The value is positive, as the argument of a logarithm must be.
