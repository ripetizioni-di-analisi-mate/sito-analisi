What is the vertical asymptote of the graph of $y = 2 + \log_5 (x + 4)$?

- $x = 4$
- $x = -4$
- $x = 0$
- $x = -3$

## Explanation

The function is defined for $x + 4 > 0$, that is for $x > -4$. Its graph is obtained from that of $y = \log_5 x$ by a translation of $4$ units to the left and $2$ units upward. Therefore the vertical asymptote $x = 0$ of $y = \log_5 x$ becomes the line $x = -4$, and the upward translation leaves it unchanged.
