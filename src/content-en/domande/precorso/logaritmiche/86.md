Where is the expression $\log_{2}\left(x + 5\right)-\log_{2}\left(x - 8\right)$ defined?

- $\left(8, \infty\right)$
- $\left[8, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(8, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Each argument must be positive. The intersection of the two rays is $\left(8, \infty\right)$.
