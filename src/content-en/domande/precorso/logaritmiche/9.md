In $(\log_2x)^2+\log_2x-2=0$ use the sum of the roots of the quadratic in $t=\log_2x$. What is the product of the two solutions in $x$ without finding them separately?

- $-1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

If the quadratic has roots $t_1,t_2$, then $t_1+t_2=-1$. The required product is $2^{t_1}2^{t_2}=2^{-1}$.
