- $x = 2$
- $x = 2$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = 4$
- $x = 1$ and $x = -2$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x > 0$. With the substitution $t = \log_2 x$ the equation becomes $t^{2} + t - 2 = 0$, which factors as $(t - 1)(t + 2) = 0$ and gives $t = 1$ or $t = -2$. The logarithm can take any real value, therefore $t = -2$ is also acceptable. By the definition of logarithm we obtain $x = 2^{1} = 2$ or $x = 2^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$, and both values satisfy $x > 0$.
