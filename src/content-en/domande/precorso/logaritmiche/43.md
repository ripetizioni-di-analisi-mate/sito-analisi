The graph of the function $y = \log_a x$ passes through the point $(9, 2)$. What is the base $a$?

- $81$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The point $(9, 2)$ lies on the graph, so $\log_a 9 = 2$. By the definition of logarithm this equality is equivalent to $a^{2} = 9$. The base of a logarithm is positive, therefore $a = 3$.
