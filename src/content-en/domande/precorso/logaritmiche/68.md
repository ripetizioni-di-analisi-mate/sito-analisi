The logarithm $\log_2 \sqrt[3]{16}$ is a rational number. What is its value?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$

## Explanation

Since $16 = 2^{4}$, the cube root is written as a power with a fractional exponent, $\sqrt[3]{16} = 2^{\frac{4}{3}}$. By the definition of logarithm, $\log_2 \bigl(2^{\frac{4}{3}}\bigr) = \dfrac{4}{3}$. The result lies between $1$ and $2$, consistently with $2 < \sqrt[3]{16} < 4$.
