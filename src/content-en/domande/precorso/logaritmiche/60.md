Where is the expression $\log_{5}\left(x + 4\right)-\log_{5}\left(x - 9\right)$ defined?

- $\left(-4, \infty\right)$
- $\left[9, \infty\right)$
- $\left(9, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -4\right) \cup \left(9, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Each argument must be positive. The intersection of the two rays is $\left(9, \infty\right)$.
