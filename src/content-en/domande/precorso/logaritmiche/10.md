- $x < 3$
- $x > \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3} < x < 3$
- $x > 3$

## Explanation

The number $3$ is written as a logarithm in base $2$, that is $3 = \log_2 (2^{3}) = \log_2 8$. The base $2$ is greater than $1$, therefore the logarithmic function is increasing and the direction **is preserved** when passing to the arguments, giving $3x - 1 > 8$, that is $x > 3$. The domain condition $3x - 1 > 0$ is satisfied by these values, because $3x - 1 > 8 > 0$.
