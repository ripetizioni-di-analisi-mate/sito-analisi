What is the value of $5^{\log_5 7 + 1}$?

- $8$
- $12$
- $35$
- $7$

## Explanation

By the property of powers $a^{m + n} = a^{m} \cdot a^{n}$ we have $5^{\log_5 7 + 1} = 5^{\log_5 7} \cdot 5^{1}$. By the identity $a^{\log_a x} = x$, valid for $x > 0$, the first factor equals $7$. The required value is therefore $7 \cdot 5 = 35$.
