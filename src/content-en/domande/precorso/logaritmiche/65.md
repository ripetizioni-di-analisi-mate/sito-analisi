Which values satisfy $\log_{5}\left(x + 4\right)> \log_{5}\left(9 - x\right)$?

- $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}, 9\right)$
- $\left(-4, \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{5}{2}, 9\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

On the domain $\left(-4, 9\right)$ the logarithm is increasing. Comparing the arguments with $x + 4 > 9 - x$ gives $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}, 9\right)$.
