Where is the function $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{\log_2 (x - 1)}$ defined?

- $x > 1$
- $x \geq 1$ and $x \neq 2$
- $x \neq 1$ and $x \neq 2$
- $x > 1$ and $x \neq 2$

## Explanation

The logarithm exists for $x - 1 > 0$ and the denominator must be different from $0$, so the conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 1 > 0 \\
\log_2 (x - 1) \neq 0
\end{cases}
$$
The logarithm equals $0$ when the argument equals $1$, that is for $x - 1 = 1$, therefore the second condition is equivalent to $x \neq 2$. The domain consists of the numbers $x > 1$ different from $2$.
