- $x = -\dfrac{11}{4}$
- $x = 1$
- $x = -7$
- $x = -5$

## Explanation

By the definition of logarithm, the equation is equivalent to
$$
x + 3 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-2} .
$$
The negative exponent is handled by taking the reciprocal, therefore $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-2} = 2^{2} = 4$. From $x + 3 = 4$ it follows that $x = 1$. The domain restriction $x + 3 > 0$ is satisfied, because for $x = 1$ the argument equals $4$.
