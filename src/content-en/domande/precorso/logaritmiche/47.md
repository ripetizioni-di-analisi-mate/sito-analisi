A sensor measures only $|\log_{10}x|$. What is the product of two distinct positive inputs giving the same reading?

- $1$
- $0$
- $10$
- No two distinct inputs exist, because a logarithm would always be positive.

## Explanation

Equal absolute values require equal or opposite logarithms. Distinct inputs have different logarithms, so their sum is zero. The logarithm of the product is zero, giving a product of one.
