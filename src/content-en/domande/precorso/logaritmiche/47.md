The equation $(\log_{10} x)^{2} = 4$ is solved with the substitution $t = \log_{10} x$. What are its solutions?

- $x = 100$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{100}$
- $x = 100$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -2$
- $x = 100$ and $x = -100$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x > 0$. With the substitution $t = \log_{10} x$ the equation becomes $t^{2} = 4$, whose solutions are $t = 2$ and $t = -2$. The logarithm can take any real value, so neither value is discarded. By the definition of logarithm we obtain $x = 10^{2} = 100$ or $x = 10^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{100}$, both positive and therefore acceptable.
