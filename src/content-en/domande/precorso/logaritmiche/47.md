What is the real domain of $\log_{5}\left(\left(9 - x\right) \left(x + 4\right)\right)$?

- $\left(-4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-4, 9\right)$
- $\left[-4, 9\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -4\right) \cup \left(9, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm’s argument must be strictly positive. The product is positive between its two zeros, giving the domain $\left(-4, 9\right)$.
