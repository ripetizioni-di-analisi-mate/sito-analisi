Which values satisfy $\log_{2}\left(x + 5\right)> \log_{2}\left(8 - x\right)$?

- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, \dfrac{3}{2}\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{3}{2}, 8\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, 8\right)$

## Explanation

On the domain $\left(-5, 8\right)$ the logarithm is increasing. We compare the arguments, and $x + 5 > 8 - x$ gives $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, 8\right)$.
