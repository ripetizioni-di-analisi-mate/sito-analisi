What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 3}\right| < 4$?

- $\left(-7, 1\right)$
- $\left(-4, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right) \cup \left(1, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-7, 1\right]$

## Explanation

The points must be less than 4 from -3. The endpoints are exactly 4 away, so the result is $\left(-7, 1\right)$.
