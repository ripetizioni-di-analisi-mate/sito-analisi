The inequality $|x - 2| \leq -(x - 2)^2$ is true for which $x$?

- No real number
- Every real number
- Only $x = 2$
- Every $x \neq 2$

## Explanation

The left-hand side $|x - 2|$ is greater than or equal to zero, while the right-hand side $-(x - 2)^2$ is less than or equal to zero. Hence the inequality can hold only if both sides are zero, that is, for $x - 2 = 0$. At $x = 2$ we have $0 \leq 0$, which is true, therefore the only solution is $x = 2$.
