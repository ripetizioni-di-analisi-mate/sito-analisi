- $-3 \leq x \leq 7$
- $x < -3 \ \vee \ x > 7$
- $-7 < x < 3$
- $-3 < x < 7$

## Explanation

Since $5 > 0$, the inequality $|x - 2| < 5$ is equivalent to the double inequality
$$
-5 < x - 2 < 5 .
$$
Adding $2$ to all members gives $-3 < x < 7$. The inequality is strict, so the endpoints $-3$ and $7$ are excluded, because at those points $|x - 2|$ equals exactly $5$. The solutions are the points at **distance less than $5$** from $2$.
