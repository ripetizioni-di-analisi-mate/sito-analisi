What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 3}\right| + \left|{x + 2}\right| = 5$?

- $\left[-2, 3\right]$
- $\left(-2, 3\right)$
- $\left\{-2, 3\right\}$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right] \cup \left[3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The sum of the distances is 5 between -2 and 3, including the endpoints. It is larger outside, giving $\left[-2, 3\right]$.
