Which of the following equalities holds for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $|x - 2| = x - 2$
- $|x + 2| = |x| + 2$
- $|x^2 - 1| = x^2 - 1$
- $|{-2x}| = 2\,|x|$

## Explanation

By the property $|ab| = |a|\,|b|$ we have $|{-2x}| = |-2|\,|x| = 2\,|x|$ for every real $x$. The other three equalities are false for some value of $x$. At $x = 0$ we have $|0 - 2| = 2$ while $0 - 2 = -2$, and $|0^2 - 1| = 1$ while $0^2 - 1 = -1$. At $x = -1$ we have $|-1 + 2| = 1$ while $|-1| + 2 = 3$.
