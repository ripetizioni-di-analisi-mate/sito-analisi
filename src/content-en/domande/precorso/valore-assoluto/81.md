For which real $x$ is the fraction $\dfrac{|x - 1|}{x + 2}$ greater than or equal to zero?

- $x > -2$
- $x \geq -2$
- $x < -2 \ \vee \ x \geq 1$
- $x \neq -2$

## Explanation

The fraction is defined for $x \neq -2$. The numerator $|x - 1|$ is greater than or equal to zero for every $x$ and vanishes only at $x = 1$. For $x > -2$ the denominator is positive, so the fraction is greater than or equal to zero; in particular at $x = 1$ it equals $0$, which satisfies the non-strict inequality. For $x < -2$ the numerator is positive and the denominator is negative, so the fraction is negative. Therefore the solutions are $x > -2$.
