What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 5}\right| < 7 - x$?

- $\left(-\infty, 1\right]$
- $\left(-5, 1\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$
- $\left(1, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Both $x + 5 < 7 - x$ and $x + 5 > -\left(7 - x\right)$ must hold. The second always holds because 5 exceeds -7. The first gives $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$.
