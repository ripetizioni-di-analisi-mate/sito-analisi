What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 5}\right| < 7$?

- $\left(-12, 2\right)$
- $\left(-7, 7\right)$
- $\left[-12, 2\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -12\right) \cup \left(2, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The points must be less than 7 from -5. The endpoints are exactly 7 away, so the result is $\left(-12, 2\right)$.
