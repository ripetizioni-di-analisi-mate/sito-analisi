What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 3}\right| < 4 - x$?

- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{2}\right]$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

Both $x + 3 < 4 - x$ and $x + 3 > -\left(4 - x\right)$ must hold. The second always holds because 3 exceeds -4. The first gives $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$.
