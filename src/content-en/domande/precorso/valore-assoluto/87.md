What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 4}\right| = 5 - x$?

- $\left\{4, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{9}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

For $x<4$ both sides have the same slope and different intercepts. For $x\geq 4$ we have $x - 4 = 5 - x$, whose solution belongs to this interval and gives $\left\{\dfrac{9}{2}\right\}$.
