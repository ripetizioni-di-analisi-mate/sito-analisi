What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 4}\right| = 5 - x$?

- $\left\{4, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{9}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

For $x<4$ both sides have the same slope and different intercepts. For $x\geq 4$ it becomes $x - 4 = 5 - x$. The solution lies in this region, giving $\left\{\dfrac{9}{2}\right\}$.
