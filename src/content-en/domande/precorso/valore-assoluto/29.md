What are the solutions of the equation $|x^2 - 3x| = x$?

- $x = 4$
- $x = 2 \ \vee \ x = 4$
- $x = 0 \ \vee \ x = 2 \ \vee \ x = 4$
- $x = 0 \ \vee \ x = 4$

## Explanation

The right-hand side contains the variable and the left-hand side is never negative, so we impose the condition $x \geq 0$. The equation $x^2 - 3x = x$ becomes $x^2 - 4x = 0$, that is, $x(x - 4) = 0$, and gives $x = 0$ or $x = 4$. The equation $x^2 - 3x = -x$ becomes $x^2 - 2x = 0$, that is, $x(x - 2) = 0$, and gives $x = 0$ or $x = 2$. All three values satisfy $x \geq 0$, so the solutions are $x = 0$, $x = 2$ and $x = 4$.
