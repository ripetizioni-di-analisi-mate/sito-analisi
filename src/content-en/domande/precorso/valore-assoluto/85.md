What is the smallest value taken by $f(x) = |x - 1| + |x - 5|$ as $x$ ranges over $\mathbb{R}$?

- $4$
- $0$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

The arguments vanish at $1$ and at $5$. For $x < 1$ we have $f(x) = (1 - x) + (5 - x) = 6 - 2x$, which is greater than $4$. For $1 \leq x \leq 5$ we have $f(x) = (x - 1) + (5 - x) = 4$. For $x > 5$ we have $f(x) = 2x - 6$, which is greater than $4$. Hence the minimum value is $4$, the distance between $1$ and $5$, and it is attained at every point between $1$ and $5$.
