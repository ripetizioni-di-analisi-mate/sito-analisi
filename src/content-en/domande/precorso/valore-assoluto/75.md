What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 4}\right| + \left|{x + 3}\right| = 7$?

- $\left(-\infty, -3\right] \cup \left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, 4\right)$
- $\left\{-3, 4\right\}$
- $\left[-3, 4\right]$

## Explanation

The sum of the distances is 7 between -3 and 4, including the endpoints. It is larger outside, giving $\left[-3, 4\right]$.
