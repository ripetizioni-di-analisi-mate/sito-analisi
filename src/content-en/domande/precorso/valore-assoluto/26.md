What are the solutions of the equation $|x^2 - 4| = |x^2 - 2x|$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = -1 \ \vee \ x = 2$
- $x = -2 \ \vee \ x = 1$
- $x = -2 \ \vee \ x = 2$

## Explanation

Two numbers have the same absolute value when they are equal or opposite, so the equation is equivalent to $x^2 - 4 = x^2 - 2x \ \vee \ x^2 - 4 = -(x^2 - 2x)$. The first reduces to $2x = 4$, that is, $x = 2$. The second becomes $2x^2 - 2x - 4 = 0$, that is, $x^2 - x - 2 = 0$, with solutions $x = 2$ and $x = -1$. The union of the solutions is therefore $x = -1 \ \vee \ x = 2$.
