What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 3}\right| = 4 - x$?

- $\left\{3, 4\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{7}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{4\right\}$

## Explanation

For $x<3$ both sides have the same slope and different intercepts. For $x\geq 3$ it becomes $x - 3 = 4 - x$. The solution lies in this region, giving $\left\{\dfrac{7}{2}\right\}$.
