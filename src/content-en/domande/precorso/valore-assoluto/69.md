What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 4}\right| < 5 - x$?

- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{2}\right]$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- $\left(-4, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

Both $x + 4 < 5 - x$ and $x + 4 > -\left(5 - x\right)$ must hold. The second always holds because 4 exceeds -5. The first gives $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$.
