The equality $2\,|x - 1| - 3 = 5$ holds for exactly which $x$?

- $x = -3 \ \vee \ x = 5$
- $x = -7 \ \vee \ x = 9$
- $x = 5$
- $x = 0 \ \vee \ x = 2$

## Explanation

Adding $3$ to both sides gives $2\,|x - 1| = 8$, and dividing by $2$ gives $|x - 1| = 4$. Since $4 > 0$, the equation is equivalent to $x - 1 = 4 \ \vee \ x - 1 = -4$, therefore $x = 5$ or $x = -3$. The two solutions are the points at distance $4$ from $1$.
