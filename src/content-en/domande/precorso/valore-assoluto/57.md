A gauge displays only $|x-1|$. If two consecutive readings are both 4, what is the greatest possible displacement in magnitude between the two positions?

- $8$
- $4$
- $2$
- $0$, assigning only position $x=5$ to both readings.

## Explanation

The possible positions are $1-4=-3$ and $1+4=5$. The greatest displacement in magnitude occurs when the object changes sides, giving $5-(-3)=8$.
