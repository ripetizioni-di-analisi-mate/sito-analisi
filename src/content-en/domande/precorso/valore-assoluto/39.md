What are the solutions of the inequality $|x + 2| \geq 3x$?

- $x \leq -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0 \leq x \leq 1$
- $x \leq 1$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2} \leq x \leq 1$

## Explanation

The inequality $|A| \geq B$ is equivalent to $A \geq B \ \vee \ A \leq -B$, even when $B$ contains the variable, so it is equivalent to $x + 2 \geq 3x \ \vee \ x + 2 \leq -3x$. The first gives $2 \geq 2x$, that is, $x \leq 1$. The second gives $4x \leq -2$, that is, $x \leq -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The **union** of the two half-lines is $x \leq 1$. The values $x < 0$ are also solutions, because there the right-hand side $3x$ is negative while the left-hand side is not.
