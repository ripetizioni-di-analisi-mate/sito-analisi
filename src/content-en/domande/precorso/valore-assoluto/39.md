What are the solutions of the inequality $|x - 3| \leq x - 5$?

- $x \geq 4$
- $x \geq 5$
- The inequality has no solutions
- $4 \leq x \leq 5$

## Explanation

The inequality $|A| \leq B$ is equivalent to the system of the two conditions $A \leq B$ and $A \geq -B$, even when $B$ contains the variable:
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 3 \leq x - 5 \\
x - 3 \geq -x + 5
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition reduces to $-3 \leq -5$, which is false for every $x$, while the second gives $2x \geq 8$, that is, $x \geq 4$. Since the two conditions must be satisfied together and the first never is, the inequality **has no solutions**. The same result follows from the sign of the right-hand side: for $x < 5$ it is negative, while for $x \geq 5$ the left-hand side equals $x - 3$, which is greater than $x - 5$.
