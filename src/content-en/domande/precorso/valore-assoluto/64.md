What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 2}\right| = \left|{x - 3}\right|$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{-2, 3\right\}$

## Explanation

The two expressions are distances from -2 and 3. The only equidistant point is their midpoint, so we obtain $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$.
