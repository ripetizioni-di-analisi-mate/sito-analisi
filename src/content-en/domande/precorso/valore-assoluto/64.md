The equation $|x| = x + a$ depends on the real parameter $a$. For which values of $a$ does it have at least one solution?

- $a \leq 0$
- $a \geq 0$
- $a < 0$
- For every $a \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

A solution must satisfy the condition $x + a \geq 0$. The case $x = x + a$ reduces to $a = 0$, so for $a = 0$ every $x \geq 0$ is a solution, and for $a \neq 0$ this case gives no solutions. The case $x = -(x + a)$ gives $x = -\dfrac{a}{2}$, and at this point the right-hand side equals $-\dfrac{a}{2} + a = \dfrac{a}{2}$. The condition $\dfrac{a}{2} \geq 0$ is satisfied if and only if $a \geq 0$. Therefore the equation has solutions for $a \geq 0$, while for $a < 0$ it has none.
