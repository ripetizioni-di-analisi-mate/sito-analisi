What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 6}\right| = 8 - x$?

- $\left\{6, 8\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$

## Explanation

For $x<6$ both sides have the same slope and different intercepts. For $x\geq 6$ we have $x - 6 = 8 - x$, whose solution belongs to this interval and gives $\left\{7\right\}$.
