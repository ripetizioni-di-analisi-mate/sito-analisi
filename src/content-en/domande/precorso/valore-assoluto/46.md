What is the value of the expression $|3 - \pi| + |\pi - 4|$?

- $-1$
- $7 - 2\pi$
- $1$
- $2\pi - 7$

## Explanation

Since $\pi$ lies between $3$ and $4$, we have $3 - \pi < 0$, so by the definition of absolute value $|3 - \pi| = \pi - 3$. We also have $\pi - 4 < 0$, therefore $|\pi - 4| = 4 - \pi$. The sum is $(\pi - 3) + (4 - \pi) = 1$, which is the distance between $3$ and $4$.
