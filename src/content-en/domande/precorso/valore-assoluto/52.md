What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 2}\right| \geq 3$?

- $\left(-\infty, -1\right) \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right] \cup \left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-1, 5\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right]$

## Explanation

The distance from 2 must be at least 3. The points lie on the two outer rays, including the endpoints, giving $\left(-\infty, -1\right] \cup \left[5, \infty\right)$.
