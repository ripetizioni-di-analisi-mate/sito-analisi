If $a$ and $b$ are real numbers with $|a| < |b|$, which of the following inequalities is true in every case?

- $a < b$
- $a^2 < b^2$
- $|a| < b$
- $a^3 < b^3$

## Explanation

The numbers $|a|$ and $|b|$ are not negative, and for two non-negative numbers squaring preserves the direction of the inequality. Hence $|a| < |b|$ implies $|a|^2 < |b|^2$, and by the property $|a|^2 = a^2$ we obtain $a^2 < b^2$. The other three inequalities are false for $a = 1$ and $b = -2$, which satisfy $|1| < |-2|$. Indeed, in this case $1 < -2$ and $|1| < -2$ are false, and $1^3 < (-2)^3$, that is, $1 < -8$, is false too.
