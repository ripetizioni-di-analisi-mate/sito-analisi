What are the solutions of the inequality $|x^2 - 4x| \geq -1$?

- Every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- $x \leq 0 \ \vee \ x \geq 4$
- $0 \leq x \leq 4$
- No $x \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is an absolute value, so $|x^2 - 4x| \geq 0$ for every real $x$. Since $0 > -1$, it follows that $|x^2 - 4x| \geq 0 > -1$ for every $x$. Therefore the inequality is **satisfied by every** real number, and the argument $x^2 - 4x$ requires no sign analysis.
