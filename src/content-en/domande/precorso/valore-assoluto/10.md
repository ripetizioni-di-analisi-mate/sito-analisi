- $x \leq 3$
- $x \leq \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $x < 3$
- $\dfrac{5}{3} \leq x \leq 3$

## Explanation

The inequality $|A| \geq B$ is equivalent to $A \geq B \ \vee \ A \leq -B$, even when $B$ contains the variable, so
$$
x - 1 \geq 2x - 4 \ \vee \ x - 1 \leq -(2x - 4) .
$$
The first gives $x \leq 3$. In the second the right-hand side equals $-2x + 4$, therefore $3x \leq 5$, that is, $x \leq \dfrac{5}{3}$. The solutions are the **union** of the two half-lines, that is, $x \leq 3$; the endpoint $3$ is included, because at $x = 3$ both sides equal $2$.
