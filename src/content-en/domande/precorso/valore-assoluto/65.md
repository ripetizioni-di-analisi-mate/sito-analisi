What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 6}\right| = 8$?

- $\left\{2\right\}$
- $\left\{-2, 14\right\}$
- $\left\{-14\right\}$
- $\left\{-14, 2\right\}$

## Explanation

The distance from -6 must be 8. The two points are found by adding 8 to and subtracting it from -6, so the solution set is $\left\{-14, 2\right\}$.
