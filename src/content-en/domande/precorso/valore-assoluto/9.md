- $-2 < x < 5$
- $x < -5 \ \vee \ x > 2$
- $x < -2 \ \vee \ x > 5$
- $x \leq -2 \ \vee \ x \geq 5$

## Explanation

Since $7 > 0$, the inequality $|2x - 3| > 7$ is equivalent to
$$
2x - 3 < -7 \ \vee \ 2x - 3 > 7 .
$$
The first gives $2x < -4$, that is, $x < -2$; the second gives $2x > 10$, that is, $x > 5$. The solution is the **union of the two half-lines**, with the endpoints excluded because the inequality is strict.
