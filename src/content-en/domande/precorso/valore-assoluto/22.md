For which values of the real parameter $k$ does the equation $|2x - 3| = k - 1$ have exactly one solution?

- $k = 1$
- $k = 0$
- $k > 1$
- $k \geq 1$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is an absolute value, so the number of solutions depends on the sign of the right-hand side $k - 1$. If $k - 1 > 0$, the equation is equivalent to $2x - 3 = k - 1 \ \vee \ 2x - 3 = -(k - 1)$ and has two distinct solutions. If $k - 1 < 0$, it has no solutions, because an absolute value is never negative. If $k - 1 = 0$, that is, $k = 1$, the equation becomes $|2x - 3| = 0$ and has the single solution $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$.
