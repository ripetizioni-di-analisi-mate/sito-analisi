What are the solutions of the equation $|x - 4| = 2x - 2$?

- $x = -2$
- $x = -2 \ \vee \ x = 2$
- $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $x = 2$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is never negative, so we impose the condition $2x - 2 \geq 0$, that is, $x \geq 1$. The equation $x - 4 = 2x - 2$ gives $x = -2$, which does not satisfy the condition and is **discarded**. The equation $x - 4 = -(2x - 2)$ becomes $x - 4 = -2x + 2$, so $3x = 6$ and $x = 2$, which satisfies $x \geq 1$. The only solution is therefore $x = 2$.
