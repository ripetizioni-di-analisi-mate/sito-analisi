What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 6}\right| \geq 8$?

- $\left[-2, 14\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right) \cup \left(14, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right] \cup \left[14, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The distance from 6 must be at least 8. The points lie on the two outer half-lines, including the endpoints, giving $\left(-\infty, -2\right] \cup \left[14, \infty\right)$.
