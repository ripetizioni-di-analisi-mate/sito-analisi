What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 6}\right| < 8$?

- $\left[-14, 2\right]$
- $\left(-14, 2\right)$
- $\left(-8, 8\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -14\right) \cup \left(2, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The points must be less than 8 from -6. The endpoints are exactly 8 away, so the result is $\left(-14, 2\right)$.
