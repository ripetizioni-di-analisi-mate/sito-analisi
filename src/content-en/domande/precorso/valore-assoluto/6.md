- $x = 6$
- $x = 2 \ \vee \ x = 6$
- $x = \dfrac{4}{3} \ \vee \ x = 6$
- $x = -\dfrac{4}{3} \ \vee \ x = 6$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is never negative and the right-hand side contains the variable, so the equation requires the condition $x + 1 \geq 0$, that is, $x \geq -1$, together with one of the two equations
$$
2x - 5 = x + 1 \ \vee \ 2x - 5 = -(x + 1) .
$$
The first gives $x = 6$. In the second the right-hand side equals $-x - 1$, therefore $3x = 4$ and $x = \dfrac{4}{3}$. Both values are greater than $-1$, so they satisfy the condition and are **both solutions**.
