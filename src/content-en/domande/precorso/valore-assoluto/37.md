What are the solutions of the inequality $|x^2 - 2| > 2$?

- $x < -2 \ \vee \ x > 2$
- $-2 < x < 2$
- $x > 2$
- $x < -\sqrt{2} \ \vee \ x > \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Since $2 > 0$, the inequality is equivalent to $x^2 - 2 < -2 \ \vee \ x^2 - 2 > 2$. The first becomes $x^2 < 0$, which is satisfied by no real $x$. The second becomes $x^2 > 4$, which holds for $x < -2$ and for $x > 2$. The solutions are therefore $x < -2 \ \vee \ x > 2$.
