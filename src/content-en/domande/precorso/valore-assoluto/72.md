What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 7}\right| + \left|{x + 5}\right| = 12$?

- $\left\{-5, 7\right\}$
- $\left[-5, 7\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right] \cup \left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The sum of the distances is 12 between -5 and 7, including the endpoints. It is larger outside, giving $\left[-5, 7\right]$.
