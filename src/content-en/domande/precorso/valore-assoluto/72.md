How many real numbers $x$ satisfy $|x - 2| = |x^2 - 4|$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Since $x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$, by the property $|ab| = |a|\,|b|$ the equation becomes $|x - 2| = |x - 2|\,|x + 2|$, that is, $|x - 2|\,(|x + 2| - 1) = 0$. A product is zero if and only if one of its factors is zero, so $x = 2$ or $|x + 2| = 1$. The equation $|x + 2| = 1$ is equivalent to $x + 2 = 1 \ \vee \ x + 2 = -1$, that is, $x = -1$ or $x = -3$. Hence the required numbers are $-3$, $-1$ and $2$, that is, **three**.
