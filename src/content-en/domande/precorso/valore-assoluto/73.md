What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 3}\right| = \left|{x - 4}\right|$?

- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 4\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

These expressions are distances from -3 and 4. The midpoint is the only equidistant point, giving $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$.
