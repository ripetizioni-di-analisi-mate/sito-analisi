What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 3}\right| + \left|{x + 2}\right| = 10$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{9}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{-2, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{11}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{9}{2}, \dfrac{11}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

Between -2 and 3, the sum is 5. On each outer region it is linear. Setting it equal to 10 in both regions gives $\left\{- \dfrac{9}{2}, \dfrac{11}{2}\right\}$.
