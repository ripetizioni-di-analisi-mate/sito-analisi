What are the solutions of the equation $|x - 1| = |x + 5|$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = -2 \ \vee \ x = 2$
- $x = -2$
- $x = -5 \ \vee \ x = 1$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $x - 1 = x + 5 \ \vee \ x - 1 = -(x + 5)$. The first reduces to $-1 = 5$, which is false, so it gives no solutions. The second gives $2x = -4$, that is, $x = -2$. The result can be interpreted in terms of distance: $x = -2$ is the midpoint between $1$ and $-5$, the only point equidistant from both.
