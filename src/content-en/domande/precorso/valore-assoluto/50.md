What are the real solutions of the equation $x\,|x| = -9$?

- $x = 3$
- $x = -3 \ \vee \ x = 3$
- The equation has no solutions
- $x = -3$

## Explanation

We distinguish the two cases of the definition of absolute value. For $x \geq 0$ we have $|x| = x$, and the equation becomes $x^2 = -9$, which has no real solutions. For $x < 0$ we have $|x| = -x$, and the equation becomes $-x^2 = -9$, that is, $x^2 = 9$, with solutions $x = \pm 3$. Of these only $x = -3$ satisfies the condition $x < 0$, so the only solution is $x = -3$.
