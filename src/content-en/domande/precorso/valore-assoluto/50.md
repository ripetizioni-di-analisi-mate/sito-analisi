What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 3}\right| \geq 4$?

- $\left(-\infty, -1\right) \cup \left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-1, 7\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right] \cup \left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right]$

## Explanation

The distance from 3 must be at least 4. The points lie on the two outer rays, including the endpoints, giving $\left(-\infty, -1\right] \cup \left[7, \infty\right)$.
