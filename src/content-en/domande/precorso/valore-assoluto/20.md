What are the solutions of the equation $|4x + 1| = 9$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = -2 \ \vee \ x = \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x = -\dfrac{5}{2} \ \vee \ x = 2$
- $x = -2 \ \vee \ x = 2$

## Explanation

Since $9 > 0$, the equation is equivalent to $4x + 1 = 9 \ \vee \ 4x + 1 = -9$. The first gives $4x = 8$, so $x = 2$. The second gives $4x = -10$, so $x = -\dfrac{10}{4} = -\dfrac{5}{2}$. The solutions are therefore $x = -\dfrac{5}{2}$ and $x = 2$.
