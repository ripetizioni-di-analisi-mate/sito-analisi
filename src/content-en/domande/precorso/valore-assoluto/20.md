What are the solutions of the inequality $|3x - 6| > 0$?

- Every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- $x > 2$
- $x \neq 2$
- $x \neq -2$

## Explanation

An absolute value is greater than or equal to $0$ and equals $0$ *only* where its argument is zero. So $|3x - 6| > 0$ is equivalent to $3x - 6 \neq 0$, that is, to $x \neq 2$. At $x = 2$ the left-hand side equals $0$ and the strict inequality $0 > 0$ is false. At every other point the left-hand side is positive, therefore the solutions are **all real numbers other than $2$**.
