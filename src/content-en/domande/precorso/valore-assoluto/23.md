Which of the following equations has no real solutions?

- $|x - 2| - 1 = 0$
- $3 - |x| = 1$
- $|2x + 3| = 0$
- $|x| + 5 = 2$

## Explanation

The equation $|x| + 5 = 2$ is equivalent to $|x| = -3$; since an absolute value is never negative, it **has no solutions**. The others reduce to the form $|A| = k$ with $k \geq 0$. The equation $|x - 2| - 1 = 0$ is equivalent to $|x - 2| = 1$ and has solutions $x = 1$ and $x = 3$. The equation $3 - |x| = 1$ is equivalent to $|x| = 2$, with solutions $x = \pm 2$, and the equation $|2x + 3| = 0$ has the single solution $x = -\dfrac{3}{2}$.
