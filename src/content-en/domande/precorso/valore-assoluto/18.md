What are the solutions of the inequality $|x - 1| < |x + 3|$?

- $x < -1$
- $x > -1$
- $x > 1$
- $-3 < x < 1$

## Explanation

The two sides are never negative, so the inequality is equivalent to the one between their squares, $(x - 1)^2 < (x + 3)^2$. Expanding gives $x^2 - 2x + 1 < x^2 + 6x + 9$, that is, $-8 < 8x$, so $x > -1$. The result can also be interpreted in terms of distance: the points closer to $1$ than to $-3$ are those to the right of the midpoint $-1$.
