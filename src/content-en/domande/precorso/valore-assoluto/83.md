Three sites are at $0$, $2$ and $9$ on a line. Where should $x$ be placed to minimise the sum $|x|+|x-2|+|x-9|$?

- $x=2$
- $x=3$
- $x=\dfrac92$
- $x=9$, considering only $x\ge9$, where the sum $3x-11$ is increasing.

## Explanation

The total distance to the outer sites is at least $9$ and equals $9$ throughout $[0,9]$. On that interval the distance to the middle site vanishes only at $x=2$. The minimum sum is therefore $9$.
