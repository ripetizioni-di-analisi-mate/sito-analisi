What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 6}\right| < 8 - x$?

- $\left(-\infty, 1\right]$
- $\left(-6, 1\right)$
- $\left(1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$

## Explanation

Both $x + 6 < 8 - x$ and $x + 6 > -\left(8 - x\right)$ must hold. The second always holds because 6 exceeds -8. The first gives $\left(-\infty, 1\right)$.
