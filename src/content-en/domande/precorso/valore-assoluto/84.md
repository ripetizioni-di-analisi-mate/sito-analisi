What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 5}\right| = 7$?

- $\left\{-12\right\}$
- $\left\{-12, 2\right\}$
- $\left\{2\right\}$
- $\left\{-2, 12\right\}$

## Explanation

The distance from -5 must be 7. The two points are found by adding 7 to and subtracting it from -5, so the solution set is $\left\{-12, 2\right\}$.
