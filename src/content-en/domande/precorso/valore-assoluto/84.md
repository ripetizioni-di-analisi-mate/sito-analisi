What can be said about the solutions of the equation $|x - 3| + |x + 3| = 0$?

- The solutions are $x = -3$ and $x = 3$
- The equation has no solutions
- The only solution is $x = 0$
- The only solution is $x = 3$

## Explanation

A sum of two numbers greater than or equal to zero is zero if and only if both are zero. So we would need $x - 3 = 0$ and $x + 3 = 0$ together, that is, $x = 3$ and $x = -3$, which is impossible. Therefore the equation **has no solutions**; for example, at $x = 3$ the left-hand side equals $0 + 6 = 6$.
