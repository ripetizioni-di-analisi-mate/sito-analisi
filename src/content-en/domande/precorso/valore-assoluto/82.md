What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 2}\right| = 3 - x$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{5}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{3\right\}$
- $\left\{2, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

For $x<2$ both sides have the same slope and different intercepts. For $x\geq 2$ we have $x - 2 = 3 - x$, whose solution belongs to this interval and gives $\left\{\dfrac{5}{2}\right\}$.
