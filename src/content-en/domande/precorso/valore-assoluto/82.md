What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 2}\right| = 3 - x$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{5}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{3\right\}$
- $\left\{2, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

For $x<2$ both sides have the same slope and different intercepts. For $x\geq 2$ it becomes $x - 2 = 3 - x$. The solution lies in this region, giving $\left\{\dfrac{5}{2}\right\}$.
