What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 5}\right| \geq 7$?

- $\left(-\infty, -2\right] \cup \left[12, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right) \cup \left(12, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-2, 12\right]$

## Explanation

The distance from 5 must be at least 7. The points lie on the two outer half-lines, including the endpoints, giving $\left(-\infty, -2\right] \cup \left[12, \infty\right)$.
