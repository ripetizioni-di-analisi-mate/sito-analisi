What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 5}\right| + \left|{x + 4}\right| = 18$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{17}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{19}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{17}{2}, \dfrac{19}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, 5\right\}$

## Explanation

Between -4 and 5, the sum is 9. On each outer region it is linear. Setting it equal to 18 in both regions gives $\left\{- \dfrac{17}{2}, \dfrac{19}{2}\right\}$.
