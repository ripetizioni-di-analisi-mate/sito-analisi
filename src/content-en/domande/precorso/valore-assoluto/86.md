What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 2}\right| < 3$?

- $\left(-3, 3\right)$
- $\left(-5, 1\right)$
- $\left[-5, 1\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(1, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The points must be less than 3 from -2. The endpoints are exactly 3 away, so the result is $\left(-5, 1\right)$.
