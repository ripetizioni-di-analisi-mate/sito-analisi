What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 2}\right| = 3$?

- $\left\{-5, 1\right\}$
- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{-5\right\}$
- $\left\{-1, 5\right\}$

## Explanation

The distance from -2 must be 3. The two points are found by adding 3 to and subtracting it from -2, so the solution set is $\left\{-5, 1\right\}$.
