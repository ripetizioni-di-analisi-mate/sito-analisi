What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 4}\right| < 5$?

- $\left(-9, 1\right)$
- $\left[-9, 1\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -9\right) \cup \left(1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The points must be less than 5 from -4. The endpoints are exactly 5 away, so the result is $\left(-9, 1\right)$.
