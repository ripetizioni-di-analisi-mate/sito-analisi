What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 8}\right| + \left|{x + 6}\right| = 28$?

- $\left\{15\right\}$
- $\left\{-6, 8\right\}$
- $\left\{-13\right\}$
- $\left\{-13, 15\right\}$

## Explanation

Between -6 and 8, the sum is 14. It is linear on the two outer intervals, and setting it equal to 28 on each gives $\left\{-13, 15\right\}$.
