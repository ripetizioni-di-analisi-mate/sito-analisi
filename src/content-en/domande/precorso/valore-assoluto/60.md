What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 4}\right| = \left|{x - 5}\right|$?

- $\left\{-4, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{9\right\}$

## Explanation

These expressions are distances from -4 and 5. The midpoint is the only equidistant point, giving $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$.
