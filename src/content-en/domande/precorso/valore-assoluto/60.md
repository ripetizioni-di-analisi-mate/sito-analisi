Which condition is necessary for a real number $x$ to be a solution of the equation $|x^2 - 9| = 4 - x$?

- $x \geq 4$
- $x \leq 4$
- $x \leq -3 \ \vee \ x \geq 3$
- $-3 \leq x \leq 3$

## Explanation

The left-hand side $|x^2 - 9|$ is never negative, so at a solution the right-hand side is also greater than or equal to zero. Hence the condition is $4 - x \geq 0$, that is, $x \leq 4$. The sign of $x^2 - 9$ gives no condition, because the equation $|A| = B$ is equivalent to $A = B \ \vee \ A = -B$ with the single condition $B \geq 0$. In fact the equation has solutions both with $x^2 - 9 > 0$ and with $x^2 - 9 < 0$, for example $x = \dfrac{1 + \sqrt{21}}{2}$, for which $x^2 - 9 < 0$.
