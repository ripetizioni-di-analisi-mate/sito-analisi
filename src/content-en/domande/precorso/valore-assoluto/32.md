In a workshop a part is accepted if its length $L$, measured in millimeters, satisfies $|L - 40| \leq 0.3$. Which of the following parts is rejected?

- The part of length $40.3$ mm
- The part of length $39.6$ mm
- The part of length $39.8$ mm
- The part of length $40.25$ mm

## Explanation

Since $0.3 > 0$, the inequality is equivalent to $-0.3 \leq L - 40 \leq 0.3$, that is, $39.7 \leq L \leq 40.3$. The part of $39.6$ mm has $|39.6 - 40| = 0.4 > 0.3$, so it is **rejected**. The part of $40.3$ mm gives $|40.3 - 40| = 0.3$, and the non-strict inequality allows equality, so it is accepted. The parts of $39.8$ mm and $40.25$ mm differ from $40$ mm by less than $0.3$ mm and are accepted.
