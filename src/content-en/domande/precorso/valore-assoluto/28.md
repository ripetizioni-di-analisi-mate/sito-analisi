What are the solutions of the equation $|2x + 1| = x - 3$?

- The equation has no solutions
- $x = -4$
- $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $x = -4 \ \vee \ x = \dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is never negative, so the solutions must satisfy the condition $x - 3 \geq 0$, that is, $x \geq 3$. The equation $2x + 1 = x - 3$ gives $x = -4$. The equation $2x + 1 = -(x - 3)$ becomes $2x + 1 = -x + 3$, so $3x = 2$ and $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$. Neither value satisfies $x \geq 3$, therefore the equation **has no solutions**.
