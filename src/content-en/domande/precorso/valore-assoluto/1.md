- $x = 3$
- $x = -\dfrac{5}{3} \ \vee \ x = 3$
- $x = -3 \ \vee \ x = \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $x = -3 \ \vee \ x = 3$

## Explanation

The right-hand side equals $7 > 0$, so the equation $|3x - 2| = 7$ is equivalent to
$$
3x - 2 = 7 \ \vee \ 3x - 2 = -7 .
$$
The first gives $3x = 9$, that is, $x = 3$. The second gives $3x = -5$, that is, $x = -\dfrac{5}{3}$. The solutions are therefore $x = -\dfrac{5}{3}$ and $x = 3$, the two values for which $3x - 2$ is at distance $7$ from the origin.
