What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 5}\right| = \left|{x - 7}\right|$?

- $\left\{12\right\}$
- $\left\{-1\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{1\right\}$

## Explanation

The two expressions are distances from -5 and 7. The only equidistant point is their midpoint, so we obtain $\left\{1\right\}$.
