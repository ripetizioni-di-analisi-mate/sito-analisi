What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 5}\right| = \left|{x - 7}\right|$?

- $\left\{12\right\}$
- $\left\{-1\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{1\right\}$

## Explanation

These expressions are distances from -5 and 7. The midpoint is the only equidistant point, giving $\left\{1\right\}$.
