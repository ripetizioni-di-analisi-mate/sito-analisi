Which values of $k$ make the solution set of $|x - 1| \leq k$ contain the whole interval $0 \leq x \leq 4$?

- $k \geq 1$
- $k \geq 3$
- $k \geq 2$
- $1 \leq k \leq 3$

## Explanation

For $k < 0$ the inequality has no solutions, so we need $k \geq 0$. For $k \geq 0$ the inequality is equivalent to $1 - k \leq x \leq 1 + k$. This interval contains $0 \leq x \leq 4$ if and only if the two conditions hold together:
$$
\begin{cases}
1 - k \leq 0 \\
1 + k \geq 4
\end{cases}
$$
that is, $k \geq 1$ and $k \geq 3$. Therefore $k \geq 3$, and $3$ is the distance from $1$ of the farthest point of the interval, namely $x = 4$.
