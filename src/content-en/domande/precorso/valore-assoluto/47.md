What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 7}\right| + \left|{x + 5}\right| = 24$?

- $\left\{-5, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{-11, 13\right\}$
- $\left\{-11\right\}$
- $\left\{13\right\}$

## Explanation

Between -5 and 7, the sum is 12. It is linear on the two outer intervals, and setting it equal to 24 on each gives $\left\{-11, 13\right\}$.
