Which expression is equal to $\sqrt{(x - 2)^2}$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $x - 2$
- $2 - x$
- $|x - 2|$
- $|x| - 2$

## Explanation

By the property $|a|^2 = a^2$ we have $(x - 2)^2 = |x - 2|^2$. The square root of the square of a nonnegative number is the number itself, and $|x - 2| \geq 0$, so $\sqrt{(x - 2)^2} = |x - 2|$. The expression $x - 2$ agrees with the root only for $x \geq 2$, because for $x = 0$ the root equals $\sqrt{4} = 2$ while $x - 2 = -2$.
