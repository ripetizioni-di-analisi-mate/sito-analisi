What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 7}\right| + \left|{x + 5}\right| = 24$?

- $\left\{-5, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{-11, 13\right\}$
- $\left\{-11\right\}$
- $\left\{13\right\}$

## Explanation

Between -5 and 7, the sum is 12. On each outer region it is linear. Setting it equal to 24 in both regions gives $\left\{-11, 13\right\}$.
