- $x > \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x \geq 2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} < x < 2$
- $x > 2$

## Explanation

The inequality $|A| < B$ is equivalent to the system of the two conditions $A < B$ and $A > -B$, to be satisfied simultaneously:
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 1 < 3x - 3 \\
x + 1 > -3x + 3
\end{cases}
\qquad
\begin{cases}
x > 2 \\
x > \dfrac{1}{2}
\end{cases}
$$
The solutions are the **intersection** of the two half-lines, that is, $x > 2$. The endpoint $2$ is excluded, because at $x = 2$ both sides equal $3$ and the inequality is strict.
