For which $x$ is the absolute value of $x$ equal to $3x$?

- Every $x \geq 0$
- No $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- $x = 0$
- $x \leq 0$

## Explanation

The equation is $|x| = 3x$. The left-hand side is never negative, so a solution must satisfy $3x \geq 0$, that is, $x \geq 0$. For $x \geq 0$ we have $|x| = x$, and the equation becomes $x = 3x$, that is, $2x = 0$. Therefore the only solution is $x = 0$, and indeed $|0| = 0 = 3 \cdot 0$.
