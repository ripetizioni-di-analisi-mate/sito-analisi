What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 4}\right| = 5$?

- $\left\{-9\right\}$
- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{-1, 9\right\}$
- $\left\{-9, 1\right\}$

## Explanation

The distance from -4 must be 5. The two points are found by adding 5 to and subtracting it from -4, so the solution set is $\left\{-9, 1\right\}$.
