Which of the following inequalities is satisfied by every real number $x$?

- $|x - 2| > x - 2$
- $|x - 2| \geq x - 2$
- $|x - 2| \geq x$
- $|x - 2| > 0$

## Explanation

For every real number $a$ we have $|a| \geq a$, because if $a \geq 0$ the two sides are equal and if $a < 0$ the left-hand side is positive and the right-hand side is negative. With $a = x - 2$ we obtain that $|x - 2| \geq x - 2$ holds for every $x$. The other three inequalities are false at $x = 2$, where the left-hand side equals $0$, because $0 > 0$ and $0 \geq 2$ are false.
