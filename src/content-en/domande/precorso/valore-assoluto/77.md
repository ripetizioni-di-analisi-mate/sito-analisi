What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 4}\right| + \left|{x + 3}\right| = 14$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{13}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{13}{2}, \dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 4\right\}$

## Explanation

Between -3 and 4, the sum is 7. On each outer region it is linear. Setting it equal to 14 in both regions gives $\left\{- \dfrac{13}{2}, \dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$.
