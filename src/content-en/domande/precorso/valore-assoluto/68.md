What solutions does the equation $|5x| = |x|$ have?

- Only $x = 0$
- Every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- No solutions
- Every $x \geq 0$

## Explanation

By the property $|ab| = |a|\,|b|$ we have $|5x| = 5\,|x|$, so the equation becomes $5\,|x| = |x|$, that is, $4\,|x| = 0$. Therefore $|x| = 0$, and the only solution is $x = 0$. The two cases $5x = x$ and $5x = -x$ lead to the same result, since both give $x = 0$.
