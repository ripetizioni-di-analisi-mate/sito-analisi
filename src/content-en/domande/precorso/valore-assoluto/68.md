What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 5}\right| + \left|{x + 4}\right| = 9$?

- $\left\{-4, 5\right\}$
- $\left(-\infty, -4\right] \cup \left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-4, 5\right]$
- $\left(-4, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The sum of the distances is 9 between -4 and 5, including the endpoints. It is larger outside, giving $\left[-4, 5\right]$.
