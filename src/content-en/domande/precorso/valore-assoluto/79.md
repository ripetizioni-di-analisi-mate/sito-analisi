What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 3}\right| = 4$?

- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{-7, 1\right\}$
- $\left\{-7\right\}$
- $\left\{-1, 7\right\}$

## Explanation

The distance from -3 must be 4. The two points are found by adding 4 to and subtracting it from -3, so the solution set is $\left\{-7, 1\right\}$.
