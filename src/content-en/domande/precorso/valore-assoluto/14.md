Which inequality has as its solutions exactly the real numbers $x$ with $2 \leq x \leq 8$?

- $|x - 3| \leq 5$
- $|x - 5| \leq 3$
- $|x + 5| \leq 3$
- $|x - 5| \geq 3$

## Explanation

The interval from $2$ to $8$ has midpoint $\dfrac{2 + 8}{2} = 5$, and each endpoint is at distance $3$ from $5$. Its points are therefore those at distance less than or equal to $3$ from $5$. Since $|x - 5|$ is the **distance** between $x$ and $5$, the condition is written $|x - 5| \leq 3$. Indeed $|x - 5| \leq 3$ is equivalent to $-3 \leq x - 5 \leq 3$, that is, to $2 \leq x \leq 8$.
