What are the solutions of the inequality $|x - 2| \geq |2x + 1|$?

- $x \leq -3 \ \vee \ x \geq \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3} \leq x \leq 3$
- $-3 < x < \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-3 \leq x \leq \dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Since the two sides are not negative, the inequality is equivalent to $(x - 2)^2 \geq (2x + 1)^2$. Expanding gives $x^2 - 4x + 4 \geq 4x^2 + 4x + 1$, that is, $3x^2 + 8x - 3 \leq 0$. The trinomial $3x^2 + 8x - 3 = (3x - 1)(x + 3)$ vanishes at $x = -3$ and at $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$, and it is less than or equal to zero between the two roots. The solutions are therefore $-3 \leq x \leq \dfrac{1}{3}$, with the endpoints included because the inequality is non-strict.
