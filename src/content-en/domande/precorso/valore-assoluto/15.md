Which real numbers $x$ are at distance $4$ from $-1$?

- $x = -3 \ \vee \ x = 5$
- $x = 3$
- $x = -5 \ \vee \ x = 3$
- $x = -4 \ \vee \ x = 4$

## Explanation

The distance between $x$ and $-1$ is $|x - (-1)| = |x + 1|$, so the condition is the equation $|x + 1| = 4$. Since $4 > 0$, it is equivalent to $x + 1 = 4 \ \vee \ x + 1 = -4$. The first gives $x = 3$, the second $x = -5$. The two points lie on opposite sides of $-1$, each at distance $4$.
