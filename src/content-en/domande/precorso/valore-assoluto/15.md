An equation $|x-a|=r$ with $r>0$ has exactly the two solutions $-7$ and $3$. Find $a+r^2$.

- $27$, choosing $a=2$ so that $x-a$ vanishes at the midpoint $-2$.
- $3$
- $23$
- $98$

## Explanation

The centre is the midpoint $a=\dfrac{-7+3}{2}=-2$. The radius is half the distance between the endpoints, so $r=5$. Thus $a+r^2=-2+25=23$.
