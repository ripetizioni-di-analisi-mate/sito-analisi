Where does the inequality $|x - 1| > x^2 - 1$ hold?

- $-2 < x < 1$
- $x < -2 \ \vee \ x > 1$
- $x < 1$
- $-2 \leq x \leq 1$

## Explanation

The inequality $|A| > B$ is equivalent to the union $A > B \ \vee \ A < -B$, with no condition on the sign of $B$. The inequality $x - 1 > x^2 - 1$ becomes $x^2 - x < 0$, which holds for $0 < x < 1$. The inequality $x - 1 < 1 - x^2$ becomes $x^2 + x - 2 < 0$, that is, $(x + 2)(x - 1) < 0$, which holds for $-2 < x < 1$. The **union** of the two intervals is $-2 < x < 1$.
