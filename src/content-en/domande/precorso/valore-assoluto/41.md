What are the solutions of the inequality $|x - 1| > 2x$?

- $0 \leq x < \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $x < -1$
- $x < \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-1 < x < \dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The inequality $|A| > B$ is equivalent to $A > B \ \vee \ A < -B$ without discussing the sign of $B$, so it is equivalent to $x - 1 > 2x \ \vee \ x - 1 < -2x$. The first gives $x < -1$. The second gives $3x < 1$, that is, $x < \dfrac{1}{3}$. The **union** of the two half-lines is $x < \dfrac{1}{3}$. Negative values are included, because for $x < 0$ the right-hand side $2x$ is negative while the left-hand side is not.
