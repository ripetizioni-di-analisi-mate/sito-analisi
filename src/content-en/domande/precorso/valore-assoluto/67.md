What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 4}\right| \geq 5$?

- $\left[-1, 9\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right] \cup \left[9, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right) \cup \left(9, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The distance from 4 must be at least 5. The points lie on the two outer half-lines, including the endpoints, giving $\left(-\infty, -1\right] \cup \left[9, \infty\right)$.
