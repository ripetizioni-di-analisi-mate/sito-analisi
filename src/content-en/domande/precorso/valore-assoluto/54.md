What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 8}\right| + \left|{x + 6}\right| = 14$?

- $\left\{-6, 8\right\}$
- $\left[-6, 8\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right] \cup \left[8, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-6, 8\right)$

## Explanation

The sum of the distances is 14 between -6 and 8, including the endpoints. It is larger outside, giving $\left[-6, 8\right]$.
