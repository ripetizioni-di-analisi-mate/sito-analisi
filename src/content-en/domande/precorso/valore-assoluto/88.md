What are the real solutions of $\left|{x + 6}\right| = \left|{x - 8}\right|$?

- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{14\right\}$
- $\left\{-1\right\}$
- $\left\{-6, 8\right\}$

## Explanation

The two expressions are distances from -6 and 8. The only equidistant point is their midpoint, so we obtain $\left\{1\right\}$.
