What is the real solution set of $\left|{x + 6}\right| = \left|{x - 8}\right|$?

- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{14\right\}$
- $\left\{-1\right\}$
- $\left\{-6, 8\right\}$

## Explanation

These expressions are distances from -6 and 8. The midpoint is the only equidistant point, giving $\left\{1\right\}$.
