The graph of $y = |x + 4| + 1$ has the shape of a V. What are the coordinates of the vertex of the V?

- $(4,\,1)$
- $(-4,\,0)$
- $(0,\,5)$
- $(-4,\,1)$

## Explanation

Since $|x + 4| \geq 0$, we have $y \geq 1$, and the value $y = 1$ is attained only for $x + 4 = 0$, that is, for $x = -4$. For $x \geq -4$ the function is $y = x + 5$, while for $x < -4$ it is $y = -x - 3$. Hence the two half-lines meet at the point $(-4,\,1)$, which is the lowest point of the graph.
