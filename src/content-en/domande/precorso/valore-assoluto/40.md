What are the solutions of the inequality $|x - 4| > x - 6$?

- $x > 5$
- Every $x \in \mathbb{R}$
- $x < 5$
- No $x \in \mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

The inequality $|A| > B$ is equivalent to $A > B \ \vee \ A < -B$, so it is equivalent to $x - 4 > x - 6 \ \vee \ x - 4 < -x + 6$. The first condition reduces to $-4 > -6$, which is true for every $x$. Since the solutions are the union of the two conditions, the inequality is **satisfied by every** real number. The second condition, $x < 5$, is already contained in the first.
