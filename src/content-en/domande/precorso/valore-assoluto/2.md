- $x = 2$
- $x = -3 \ \vee \ x = -2$
- $x = -2 \ \vee \ x = 2$
- $x = 2 \ \vee \ x = 3$

## Explanation

Since the right-hand side equals $1 > 0$, the equation $|5 - 2x| = 1$ is equivalent to
$$
5 - 2x = 1 \ \vee \ 5 - 2x = -1 .
$$
The first gives $-2x = -4$, so $x = 2$. The second gives $-2x = -6$, so $x = 3$. The solutions are therefore $x = 2$ and $x = 3$.
