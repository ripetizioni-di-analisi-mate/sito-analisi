What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 5}\right| = 7 - x$?

- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{5, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{6\right\}$

## Explanation

For $x<5$ both sides have the same slope and different intercepts. For $x\geq 5$ it becomes $x - 5 = 7 - x$. The solution lies in this region, giving $\left\{6\right\}$.
