How many real solutions does the equation $|x^2 - 4x + 2| = 2$ have?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $2 > 0$, the equation is equivalent to $x^2 - 4x + 2 = 2 \ \vee \ x^2 - 4x + 2 = -2$. The first becomes $x^2 - 4x = 0$, that is, $x(x - 4) = 0$, with solutions $x = 0$ and $x = 4$. The second becomes $x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0$, that is, $(x - 2)^2 = 0$, with the single solution $x = 2$. Hence the distinct solutions are $0$, $2$ and $4$, that is, **three**.
