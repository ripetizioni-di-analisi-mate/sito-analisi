The figure shows the graph of $y = |2x - 2|$ and the line $y = 4$. Which $x$ solve the inequality $|2x - 2| > 4$?

::grafico{f="abs(2*x-2);4" xmin=-3 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=8}

- $-1 < x < 3$
- $x \leq -1 \ \vee \ x \geq 3$
- $x < -3 \ \vee \ x > 1$
- $x < -1 \ \vee \ x > 3$

## Explanation

The solutions are the abscissas of the points where the graph of $y = |2x - 2|$ lies *above* the line $y = 4$. Since $4 > 0$, the inequality is equivalent to $2x - 2 < -4 \ \vee \ 2x - 2 > 4$, that is, $x < -1 \ \vee \ x > 3$. The endpoints are excluded, because at $x = -1$ and at $x = 3$ both sides equal $4$, and at these points the graph meets the line.
