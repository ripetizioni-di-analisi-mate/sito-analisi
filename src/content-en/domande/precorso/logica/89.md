Let $x$ be an integer. Which condition says that $x$ differs from both 7 and 16?

- $x\ne 7\ \land\ x\ne 16$
- $x=7\ \land\ x=16$
- $x\ne 7\ \lor\ x\ne 16$
- $x=7\ \lor\ x=16$

## Explanation

Both exclusions must hold at once, so a conjunction is needed. With a disjunction, meeting just one exclusion would be enough.
