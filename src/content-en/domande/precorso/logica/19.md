Which proposition has the same truth table as $\lnot (\lnot p \land q)$?

- $p \land \lnot q$
- $\lnot p \lor q$
- $p \lor q$
- $p \lor \lnot q$

## Explanation

By **De Morgan's laws** the negation of a conjunction is the disjunction of the negations, so $\lnot (\lnot p \land q) \equiv \lnot (\lnot p) \lor \lnot q$.

Double negation gives back the original proposition, therefore $\lnot (\lnot p)$ has the same truth table as $p$ and
$$
\lnot (\lnot p \land q) \equiv p \lor \lnot q .
$$
The proposition $p \land \lnot q$ negates the two parts without swapping "and" with "or". When $p$ and $q$ are both true it is false, while $\lnot (\lnot p \land q)$ is true, so the two truth tables are different.
