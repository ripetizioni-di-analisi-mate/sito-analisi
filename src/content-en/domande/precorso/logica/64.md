A rule accepts a positive integer only if it is a multiple of 28. What follows?

- Every multiple of 28 is accepted.
- Every multiple of 4 is accepted.
- Every accepted number is divisible by 4.
- The number 28 is certainly accepted.

## Explanation

“Only if” makes divisibility by 28 necessary for acceptance. Since 4 divides 28, every accepted number is also divisible by 4.
