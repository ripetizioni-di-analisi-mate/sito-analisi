Let $p$ be the proposition "$3 > 5$" and $q$ the proposition "$3 > 1$". Which of the implications $p \Rightarrow q$ and $q \Rightarrow p$ are true?

- Only $p \Rightarrow q$
- Only $q \Rightarrow p$
- Both
- Neither of them

## Explanation

The proposition $p$ is false, because $3 < 5$, and the proposition $q$ is true.

The implication $p \Rightarrow q$ has a false hypothesis, so it is true. The implication $q \Rightarrow p$ has a true hypothesis and a false conclusion, so it is false.

Therefore **only** $p \Rightarrow q$ is true.
