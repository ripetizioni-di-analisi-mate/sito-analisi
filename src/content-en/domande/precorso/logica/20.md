Let $p$ be true, $q$ false and $r$ false. Which of the following propositions is true?

- $(p \land q) \lor r$
- $p \land (q \lor r)$
- $(p \lor q) \land \lnot r$
- $\lnot p \lor q$

## Explanation

Since $p$ is true, the disjunction $p \lor q$ is true. Since $r$ is false, the negation $\lnot r$ is true. So $(p \lor q) \land \lnot r$ is the conjunction of two true propositions, and it is **true**.

The conjunction $p \land q$ is false and $r$ is false, therefore $(p \land q) \lor r$ is false. The disjunction $q \lor r$ is false, therefore $p \land (q \lor r)$ is also false. Finally, $\lnot p$ and $q$ are both false, so $\lnot p \lor q$ is false.
