For every integer $n$ the equality $n + (n + 1) + (n + 2) = 3(n + 1)$ holds. Which property is proved directly by this equality?

- The sum of three consecutive integers is even
- The sum of three consecutive integers is a multiple of $3$
- The sum of three consecutive integers is a multiple of $6$
- The sum of three consecutive integers is odd

## Explanation

Three consecutive integers can be written as $n$, $n + 1$, $n + 2$ with $n$ an integer. Their sum equals $3n + 3 = 3(n + 1)$, and $n + 1$ is an integer, so the sum is a **multiple of $3$**.

The other properties are false. Indeed $0 + 1 + 2 = 3$ is odd and is not a multiple of $6$, while $1 + 2 + 3 = 6$ is even.
