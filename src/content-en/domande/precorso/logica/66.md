Which implication is the contrapositive of “if $n$ is a multiple of 28, then it is a multiple of 4”?

- If 4 divides $n$, then 28 divides $n$.
- If 28 does not divide $n$, then 4 does not divide $n$.
- If 28 divides $n$, then 4 does not divide $n$.
- If 4 does not divide $n$, then 28 does not divide $n$.

## Explanation

Contraposition reverses the hypothesis and the conclusion and negates both. Thus failure of divisibility by the factor implies failure of divisibility by the product.
