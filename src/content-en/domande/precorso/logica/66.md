For real $a$ and $b$, which of the following equalities is equivalent to the conjunction $a = 0 \land b = 0$?

- $ab = 0$
- $a^2 + b^2 = 0$
- $a + b = 0$
- $a^2 - b^2 = 0$

## Explanation

The numbers $a^2$ and $b^2$ are greater than or equal to zero, so their sum is zero only if both are zero. Therefore $a^2 + b^2 = 0$ implies $a = 0 \land b = 0$, and conversely for $a = b = 0$ the sum equals $0$. The equality $a^2 + b^2 = 0$ is thus **equivalent** to the conjunction.

The equality $ab = 0$ is equivalent to the disjunction $a = 0 \lor b = 0$ and is satisfied by $a = 0$, $b = 1$. The equalities $a + b = 0$ and $a^2 - b^2 = 0$ are satisfied by $a = 1$, $b = -1$.
