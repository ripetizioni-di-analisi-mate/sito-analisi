One proves by contradiction that there is no even number greater than all the other even numbers. Assuming that $N$ is the largest even number, which number provides the contradiction?

- $N + 1$
- $N + 2$
- $N - 2$
- $\dfrac{N}{2}$

## Explanation

By contradiction, $N$ is even and every even number is less than or equal to $N$.

The number $N + 2$ is even, because $N = 2k$ implies $N + 2 = 2(k + 1)$, and it is greater than $N$. This contradicts the assumption, so $N + 2$ provides the **contradiction** and the largest even number does not exist.

The number $N + 1$ is odd, so it does not contradict the assumption. The number $N - 2$ is less than $N$, and $\dfrac{N}{2}$ is not always even, because for $N = 2$ it equals $1$.
