- Sufficient and not necessary
- Necessary and not sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient
- Neither necessary nor sufficient

## Explanation

Let $p$ be the condition "$x^2 > 0$" and $q$ the condition "$x > 0$".

If $x > 0$, the product $x \cdot x$ of two positive numbers is positive, so $x^2 > 0$. Therefore the implication $q \Rightarrow p$ is true and $p$ is a **necessary condition** for $q$.

For $x = -1$ we have $x^2 = 1 > 0$ and $x < 0$, so the implication $p \Rightarrow q$ is false. Consequently $x^2 > 0$ is a necessary and not sufficient condition for $x > 0$.
