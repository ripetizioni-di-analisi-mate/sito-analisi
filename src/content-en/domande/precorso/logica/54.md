In the diagram $P$ and $Q$ are the truth sets of the predicates $p(x)$ and $q(x)$, and $P$ is contained in $Q$. Which implication is true for every $x$?

::venn{tipo="incluso" nomi="Q,P" universo=1 titolo="The truth sets $P$ and $Q$"}

- $q(x) \Rightarrow p(x)$
- $\lnot p(x) \Rightarrow \lnot q(x)$
- $p(x) \Rightarrow \lnot q(x)$
- $p(x) \Rightarrow q(x)$

## Explanation

Every $x$ that makes $p(x)$ true belongs to $P$. Since $P$ is contained in $Q$, the same $x$ belongs to $Q$ and makes $q(x)$ true. Therefore the implication $p(x) \Rightarrow q(x)$ is **true for every $x$**.

An element of $Q$ that is not in $P$ makes $q(x)$ true and $p(x)$ false, so for it $q(x) \Rightarrow p(x)$ and $\lnot p(x) \Rightarrow \lnot q(x)$ are false. For an element of $P$ both $p(x)$ and $q(x)$ are true, therefore $p(x) \Rightarrow \lnot q(x)$ is false.
