Let $x$ be an integer. Which condition says that $x$ differs from both 3 and 8?

- $x\ne 3\ \land\ x\ne 8$
- $x\ne 3\ \lor\ x\ne 8$
- $x=3\ \lor\ x=8$
- $x=3\ \land\ x=8$

## Explanation

Both exclusions must hold at once, so a conjunction is needed. With a disjunction, meeting just one exclusion would be enough.
