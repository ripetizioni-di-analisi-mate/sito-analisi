It is known that the proposition $p \lor q$ is true and that $p$ is false. What can be deduced about $q$?

- $q$ is true
- $q$ is false
- $q \Rightarrow p$ is true
- The truth value of $q$ cannot be determined

## Explanation

The disjunction $p \lor q$ is true when at least one of its two parts is true. Since $p$ is false, the true part is $q$.

Therefore **$q$ is true**.

With $q$ true and $p$ false the implication $q \Rightarrow p$ has a true hypothesis and a false conclusion, so it is false.
