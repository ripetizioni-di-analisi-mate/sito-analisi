For real $x$, what kind of condition is "$x^2 = x$" for "$x = 1$"?

- Sufficient and not necessary
- Necessary and not sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient
- Neither necessary nor sufficient

## Explanation

Let $p$ be the condition "$x^2 = x$" and $q$ the condition "$x = 1$".

If $x = 1$, then $x^2 = 1 = x$, so the implication $q \Rightarrow p$ is true and $p$ is a **necessary condition** for $q$.

The equation $x^2 = x$ is equivalent to $x(x - 1) = 0$, whose solutions are $x = 0$ and $x = 1$. For $x = 0$ we have $x^2 = x$ and $x \neq 1$, therefore the implication $p \Rightarrow q$ is false and the condition is necessary and not sufficient.
