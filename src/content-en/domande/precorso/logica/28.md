Which of the following implications is true for every real $x$?

- $x^2 > 1 \Rightarrow x > 1$
- $x > -1 \Rightarrow x^2 > 1$
- $x > 1 \Rightarrow x^2 > 1$
- $x^2 > 1 \Rightarrow x > -1$

## Explanation

If $x > 1$, multiplying both sides by the positive number $x$ gives $x^2 > x$. Since $x > 1$, it follows that $x^2 > 1$, so the implication $x > 1 \Rightarrow x^2 > 1$ is **true** for every real $x$.

For $x = -2$ the hypothesis $x^2 > 1$ is true, while $x > 1$ and $x > -1$ are false. Therefore the implications $x^2 > 1 \Rightarrow x > 1$ and $x^2 > 1 \Rightarrow x > -1$ are false for $x = -2$.

For $x = 0$ the hypothesis $x > -1$ is true and the conclusion $x^2 > 1$ is false, so $x > -1 \Rightarrow x^2 > 1$ is also false.
