Which proposition is the negation of "all prime numbers are odd"?

- All prime numbers are even
- There exists an odd prime number
- There exists an even prime number
- There exists an even number that is not prime

## Explanation

The proposition has the form "for every prime number, $p$ holds", where $p$ is the predicate "is odd". By the **negation of quantifiers**, "for every" is replaced by "there exists" and the predicate is negated.

The negation of "is odd" is "is even", so the negation is "there exists an even prime number". This proposition is true, because $2$ is prime and even.

The sentence "all prime numbers are even" negates the predicate without changing the quantifier, and "there exists an odd prime number" changes the quantifier without negating the predicate.
