A rule accepts a positive integer only if it is a multiple of 40. What follows?

- Every multiple of 5 is accepted.
- The number 40 is certainly accepted.
- Every multiple of 40 is accepted.
- Every accepted number is divisible by 5.

## Explanation

“Only if” makes divisibility by 40 necessary for acceptance. Since 5 divides 40, every accepted number is also divisible by 5.
