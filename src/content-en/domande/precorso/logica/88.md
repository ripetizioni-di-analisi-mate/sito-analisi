It is known that $p$ is a sufficient condition for $q$ and that $q$ is a sufficient condition for $r$. Which statement is correct?

- $r$ is a sufficient condition for $p$
- $p$ is a necessary condition for $r$
- $p$ and $r$ are equivalent
- $r$ is a necessary condition for $p$

## Explanation

The assumptions say that $p \Rightarrow q$ and $q \Rightarrow r$ hold. If $p$ is true, then $q$ is true and so $r$ is true, therefore $p \Rightarrow r$ holds.

From the implication $p \Rightarrow r$ it follows that $r$ is a **necessary condition** for $p$, and that $p$ is a sufficient condition for $r$.

The implication $r \Rightarrow p$ does not follow from the assumptions. For example, with $p$ the condition $x > 3$, $q$ the condition $x > 2$ and $r$ the condition $x > 1$, the value $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ makes $r$ true and $p$ false. Therefore $r$ is not sufficient for $p$, $p$ is not necessary for $r$ and the two conditions are not equivalent.
