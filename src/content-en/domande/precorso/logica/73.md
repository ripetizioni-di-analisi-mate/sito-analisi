Which implication is the contrapositive of “if $n$ is a multiple of 63, then it is a multiple of 7”?

- If 7 does not divide $n$, then 63 does not divide $n$.
- If 63 divides $n$, then 7 does not divide $n$.
- If 63 does not divide $n$, then 7 does not divide $n$.
- If 7 divides $n$, then 63 divides $n$.

## Explanation

Contraposition reverses the hypothesis and the conclusion and negates both. Thus failure of divisibility by the factor implies failure of divisibility by the product.
