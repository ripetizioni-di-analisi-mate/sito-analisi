Let $x$ be an integer. Which condition says that $x$ differs from both 5 and 13?

- $x=5\ \lor\ x=13$
- $x\ne 5\ \land\ x\ne 13$
- $x=5\ \land\ x=13$
- $x\ne 5\ \lor\ x\ne 13$

## Explanation

Both exclusions must hold at once, so a conjunction is needed. With a disjunction, meeting just one exclusion would be enough.
