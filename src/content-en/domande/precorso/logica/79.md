Let $x$ be an integer. Which condition says that $x$ differs from both 2 and 5?

- $x=2\ \land\ x=5$
- $x\ne 2\ \land\ x\ne 5$
- $x=2\ \lor\ x=5$
- $x\ne 2\ \lor\ x\ne 5$

## Explanation

Both exclusions must hold at once, so a conjunction is needed. With a disjunction, meeting just one exclusion would be enough.
