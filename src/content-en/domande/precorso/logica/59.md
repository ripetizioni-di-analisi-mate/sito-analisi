What is the negation of the sentence "Marco studies physics or studies chemistry"?

- Marco does not study physics or does not study chemistry
- Marco studies neither physics nor chemistry
- Marco studies physics and studies chemistry
- Marco studies only one of the two subjects

## Explanation

The sentence is the disjunction $p \lor q$, with $p$ "Marco studies physics" and $q$ "Marco studies chemistry".

By **De Morgan's laws** $\lnot (p \lor q) \equiv \lnot p \land \lnot q$, so the negation is "Marco does not study physics and does not study chemistry", that is, "Marco studies neither physics nor chemistry".

The sentence "Marco does not study physics or does not study chemistry" negates the two parts without swapping "or" with "and". The sentences "studies physics and chemistry" and "studies only one of the two subjects" make the original disjunction true, so they do not negate it.
