What is the negation of “every integer $n>5$ is divisible by 8”?

- No integer $n>5$ is divisible by 8.
- There is an integer $n\leq 5$ that is not divisible by 8.
- There is an integer $n>5$ divisible by 8.
- There is an integer $n>5$ that is not divisible by 8.

## Explanation

One counterexample in the original set disproves “every”. For example, 9 exceeds 5 and leaves remainder 1 on division by 8.
