The sets $P$ and $Q$ in the diagram contain the values of $x$ that make $p(x)$ and $q(x)$ true, respectively. Which proposition is true exactly in the shaded region?

::venn{zone="a" nomi="P,Q" universo=1 titolo="The shaded region and the truth sets $P$ and $Q$"}

- $p(x) \land \lnot q(x)$
- $p(x) \lor \lnot q(x)$
- $\lnot p(x) \land q(x)$
- $\lnot \bigl( p(x) \land q(x) \bigr)$

## Explanation

The shaded region contains the elements of $P$ that do not belong to $Q$. For these elements $p(x)$ is true and $q(x)$ is false, that is, $p(x)$ and $\lnot q(x)$ are true.

A conjunction is true when both parts are true, so the shaded region is the truth set of $p(x) \land \lnot q(x)$.

The disjunction $p(x) \lor \lnot q(x)$ is true also in the part common to $P$ and $Q$ and outside both. The proposition $\lnot (p(x) \land q(x))$ is true in the whole region outside $P \cap Q$, and $\lnot p(x) \land q(x)$ describes the part of $Q$ outside $P$.
