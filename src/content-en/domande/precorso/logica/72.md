How does “an integer is a multiple of 6” relate to “the integer is a multiple of 2”?

- Neither necessary nor sufficient
- Sufficient only
- Necessary and sufficient
- Necessary only

## Explanation

Every multiple of 6 is a multiple of 2, so the condition is sufficient. The number 2 shows that a multiple of 2 can fail to satisfy it.
