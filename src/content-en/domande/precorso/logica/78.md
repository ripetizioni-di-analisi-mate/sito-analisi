For real $x$, which condition expresses the negation of $x^2 < 1$?

- $x \geq 1$
- $x \leq -1 \lor x \geq 1$
- $x < -1 \lor x > 1$
- $-1 \leq x \leq 1$

## Explanation

The negation of $x^2 < 1$ is $x^2 \geq 1$.

The inequality $x^2 \geq 1$ is equivalent to $|x| \geq 1$, so it is satisfied for $x \leq -1$ or for $x \geq 1$. Therefore the negation is $x \leq -1 \lor x \geq 1$.

The condition $x \geq 1$ excludes negative values such as $x = -2$, for which $x^2 = 4 \geq 1$. The condition $x < -1 \lor x > 1$ excludes $x = 1$, for which $x^2 = 1$ is not less than $1$.
