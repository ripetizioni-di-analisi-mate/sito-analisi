Which implication is the contrapositive of “if $n$ is a multiple of 40, then it is a multiple of 5”?

- If 40 divides $n$, then 5 does not divide $n$.
- If 5 divides $n$, then 40 divides $n$.
- If 5 does not divide $n$, then 40 does not divide $n$.
- If 40 does not divide $n$, then 5 does not divide $n$.

## Explanation

Contraposition reverses the hypothesis and the conclusion and negates both. Thus failure of divisibility by the factor implies failure of divisibility by the product.
