How does “an integer is a multiple of 40” relate to “the integer is a multiple of 5”?

- Neither necessary nor sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient
- Sufficient only
- Necessary only

## Explanation

Every multiple of 40 is a multiple of 5, so the condition is sufficient. The number 5 shows that a multiple of 5 can fail to satisfy it.
