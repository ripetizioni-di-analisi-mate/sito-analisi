Which implication is the contrapositive of “if $n$ is a multiple of 6, then it is a multiple of 2”?

- If 6 does not divide $n$, then 2 does not divide $n$.
- If 2 does not divide $n$, then 6 does not divide $n$.
- If 6 divides $n$, then 2 does not divide $n$.
- If 2 divides $n$, then 6 divides $n$.

## Explanation

Contraposition reverses the hypothesis and the conclusion and negates both. Thus failure of divisibility by the factor implies failure of divisibility by the product.
