Let $P$ mean “40 is divisible by 5” and $Q$ mean “9 is divisible by 8”. Which of $P\land Q$ and $P\lor Q$ are true?

- Only the first is true
- Both true
- Only the second is true
- Both false

## Explanation

$P$ is true and $Q$ is false. A conjunction requires both to be true, whereas a disjunction requires at least one.
