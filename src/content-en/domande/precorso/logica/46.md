For any proposition $p$, which of the following propositions is true in every case?

- $p \land \lnot p$
- $p \lor \lnot p$
- $\lnot p$
- $p \Rightarrow \lnot p$

## Explanation

Of the two propositions $p$ and $\lnot p$, one is true and the other is false. A disjunction is true when at least one of its two parts is true, so $p \lor \lnot p$ is **true in every case**.

The conjunction $p \land \lnot p$ requires $p$ and $\lnot p$ to be true together, therefore it is false in every case. The proposition $\lnot p$ is false when $p$ is true.

The implication $p \Rightarrow \lnot p$ is false when $p$ is true, because in that case the hypothesis is true and the conclusion $\lnot p$ is false.
