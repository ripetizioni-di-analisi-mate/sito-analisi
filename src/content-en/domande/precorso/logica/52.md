For which of the following natural numbers $n$ is the proposition "$n$ is even or $n$ is a multiple of $3$" false?

- $n = 6$
- $n = 9$
- $n = 8$
- $n = 7$

## Explanation

A disjunction is false only when both parts are false.

The number $7$ is odd and is not a multiple of $3$, so for $n = 7$ both parts are false and the proposition is **false**.

For $n = 9$ the second part is true and for $n = 8$ the first is true, therefore in these cases the disjunction is true. For $n = 6$ both parts are true, and in mathematics "or" is inclusive, so for $n = 6$ the proposition is true as well.
