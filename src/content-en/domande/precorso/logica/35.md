Which of the following propositions is true?

- $\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 + 1 > 0$
- $\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 > x$
- $\exists x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 + 1 = 0$
- $\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : |x| > 0$

## Explanation

For every real $x$ we have $x^2 \geq 0$, so $x^2 + 1 \geq 1 > 0$. Therefore the proposition $\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 + 1 > 0$ is **true**.

Since $x^2 + 1 \geq 1$ for every real $x$, no real number satisfies $x^2 + 1 = 0$, and the existential proposition is false.

For $x = 0$ we have $x^2 = 0 = x$ and $|x| = 0$, so $x = 0$ is a counterexample both to $\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 > x$ and to $\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : |x| > 0$.
