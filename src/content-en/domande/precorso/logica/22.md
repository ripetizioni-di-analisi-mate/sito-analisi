What is the truth value of the implication "if $2 + 2 = 5$, then $3$ is even"?

- False, because the conclusion is false
- False, because the hypothesis and the conclusion are both false
- It has no truth value, because the hypothesis and the conclusion are unrelated
- True, because the hypothesis is false

## Explanation

The hypothesis $2 + 2 = 5$ is false and the conclusion "$3$ is even" is also false.

An implication is false in only one case, when the hypothesis is true and the conclusion is false. Here the hypothesis is false, so the implication is **true**, whatever the truth value of the conclusion.

The truth value of $p \Rightarrow q$ depends only on the truth values of $p$ and $q$, therefore the implication is a proposition even when the two parts are unrelated in content.
