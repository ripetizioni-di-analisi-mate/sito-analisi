What is the contrapositive of the implication $x > 3 \Rightarrow x^2 > 9$, with $x$ real?

- $x^2 > 9 \Rightarrow x > 3$
- $x^2 \leq 9 \Rightarrow x \leq 3$
- $x \leq 3 \Rightarrow x^2 \leq 9$
- $x^2 < 9 \Rightarrow x < 3$

## Explanation

The contrapositive of $p \Rightarrow q$ is $\lnot q \Rightarrow \lnot p$, and it is obtained by swapping hypothesis and conclusion and negating both.

The negation of $x^2 > 9$ is $x^2 \leq 9$ and the negation of $x > 3$ is $x \leq 3$, so the contrapositive is
$$
x^2 \leq 9 \Rightarrow x \leq 3 .
$$
This implication is true, because $x^2 \leq 9$ implies $-3 \leq x \leq 3$.

The implication $x^2 > 9 \Rightarrow x > 3$ is the converse, which only swaps hypothesis and conclusion, and it is false for $x = -4$.
