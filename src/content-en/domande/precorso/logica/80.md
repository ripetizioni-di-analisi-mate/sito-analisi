What is the negation of “every integer $n>2$ is divisible by 3”?

- There is an integer $n>2$ that is not divisible by 3.
- There is an integer $n>2$ divisible by 3.
- No integer $n>2$ is divisible by 3.
- There is an integer $n\leq 2$ that is not divisible by 3.

## Explanation

One counterexample in the original set disproves “every”. For example, 4 exceeds 2 and leaves remainder 1 on division by 3.
