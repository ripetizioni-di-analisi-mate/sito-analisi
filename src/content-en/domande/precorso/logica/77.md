Which statement is true for real $x$ and $y$ subject to $y=x+2$?

- For every $x$ there is a suitable $y$.
- There are no pairs $(x,y)$.
- Every $y$ works for each $x$.
- There is exactly one $y$ that works for every $x$.

## Explanation

Once $x$ is fixed, we can choose $y=x+2$. Two different values of $x$ require two different values of $y$.
