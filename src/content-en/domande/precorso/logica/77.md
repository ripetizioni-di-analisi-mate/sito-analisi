In how many of the eight rows of the truth table of $p \land (q \lor r)$ is the proposition true?

- $1$
- $3$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The conjunction $p \land (q \lor r)$ is true when $p$ is true and $q \lor r$ is true.

The disjunction $q \lor r$ is false only when $q$ and $r$ are both false, so it is true in $3$ of the $4$ combinations of values of $q$ and $r$.

The rows with $p$ true are $4$, and in $3$ of them $q \lor r$ is true. Therefore the proposition is true in **$3$ rows**.
