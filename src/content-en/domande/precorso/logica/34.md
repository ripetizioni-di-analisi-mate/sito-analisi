For real $x$, which of the following is a sufficient condition for $x^2 > 4$?

- $x > 1$
- $x > -2$
- $x^2 > 2$
- $x > 3$

## Explanation

A condition $p$ is sufficient for $q$ when the implication $p \Rightarrow q$ holds.

If $x > 3$, both sides are positive and squaring preserves the order between positive numbers, so $x^2 > 9$. Since $9 > 4$, it follows that $x^2 > 4$, therefore "$x > 3$" is a **sufficient** condition.

The value $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ satisfies $x > 1$, $x > -2$ and $x^2 = \dfrac{9}{4} > 2$, and for it we have $x^2 = \dfrac{9}{4} < 4$. So none of the other three conditions is sufficient.
