What kind of condition is “an integer is a multiple of 15” for “the integer is a multiple of 3”?

- Sufficient only
- Neither necessary nor sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient
- Necessary only

## Explanation

Every multiple of 15 is a multiple of 3, so the condition is sufficient. The number 3 shows that a multiple of 3 can fail to satisfy it.
