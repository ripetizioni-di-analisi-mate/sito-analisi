How does “an integer is a multiple of 63” relate to “the integer is a multiple of 7”?

- Necessary and sufficient
- Necessary only
- Sufficient only
- Neither necessary nor sufficient

## Explanation

Every multiple of 63 is a multiple of 7, so the condition is sufficient. The number 7 shows that a multiple of 7 can fail to satisfy it.
