Which value of $n$ shows that the implication "if $n$ is even, then $n^2$ is a multiple of $8$" is false?

- $n = 4$
- $n = 3$
- $n = 6$
- $n = 8$

## Explanation

To show that an implication is false we need a value for which the hypothesis is true and the conclusion is false.

For $n = 6$ the hypothesis is true, because $6$ is even, and the conclusion is false, because $36 = 8 \cdot 4 + 4$ leaves remainder $4$ when divided by $8$. So $n = 6$ makes the implication **false**.

For $n = 4$ and for $n = 8$ the squares $16$ and $64$ are multiples of $8$, therefore the hypothesis and the conclusion are both true. For $n = 3$ the hypothesis is false, so for that value the implication is true.
