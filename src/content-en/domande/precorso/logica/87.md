What is the negation of “every integer $n>4$ is divisible by 7”?

- There is an integer $n>4$ divisible by 7.
- There is an integer $n\leq 4$ that is not divisible by 7.
- There is an integer $n>4$ that is not divisible by 7.
- No integer $n>4$ is divisible by 7.

## Explanation

One counterexample in the original set disproves “every”. For example, 8 exceeds 4 and leaves remainder 1 on division by 7.
