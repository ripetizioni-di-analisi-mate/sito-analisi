A student wants to prove that the sum of an even integer and an odd integer is odd. They write the integers as $2k$ and $2k+1$. Which change makes the proof valid for every pair?

- Require $k>0$.
- Use $2k$ and $2h+1$ with independent integers $k,h$.
- Check only $k=1$.
- Swap the two summands.

## Explanation

With one parameter the two numbers are consecutive. With two parameters the sum is $2k+2h+1=2(k+h)+1$, which is odd for every pair of integers.
