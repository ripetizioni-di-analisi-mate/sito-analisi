A rule accepts a positive integer only if it is a multiple of 63. What follows?

- Every multiple of 7 is accepted.
- The number 63 is certainly accepted.
- Every accepted number is divisible by 7.
- Every multiple of 63 is accepted.

## Explanation

“Only if” makes divisibility by 63 necessary for acceptance. Since 7 divides 63, every accepted number is also divisible by 7.
