For real $x$, which inequality is equivalent to the sentence "it is not true that $x$ is not greater than $2$"?

- $x \leq 2$
- $x < 2$
- $x \geq 2$
- $x > 2$

## Explanation

The sentence "$x$ is not greater than $2$" is the negation of $x > 2$, that is, $x \leq 2$.

The full sentence in turn negates $x \leq 2$, so it is the **double negation** of $x > 2$. A double negation has the same truth table as the original proposition, therefore the sentence is equivalent to $x > 2$.

The inequality $x \leq 2$ corresponds to a single negation. The inequalities $x < 2$ and $x \geq 2$ are not equivalent to $x > 2$, because the first excludes $x = 3$ and the second includes $x = 2$.
