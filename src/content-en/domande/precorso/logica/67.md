Let $A$ and $B$ be two sets. Which proposition is equivalent to $x \notin A \cap B$?

- $x \notin A \land x \notin B$
- $x \in A \land x \notin B$
- $x \notin A \lor x \notin B$
- $x \in A \lor x \in B$

## Explanation

The statement $x \in A \cap B$ is equivalent to the conjunction $x \in A \land x \in B$, so $x \notin A \cap B$ is its negation.

By **De Morgan's laws** the negation of a conjunction is the disjunction of the negations. Therefore $x \notin A \cap B$ is equivalent to $x \notin A \lor x \notin B$.

The proposition $x \notin A \land x \notin B$ excludes an element that is in $A$ and not in $B$, which does not belong to $A \cap B$ either. The proposition $x \in A \land x \notin B$ excludes an element that lies in neither set, and $x \in A \lor x \in B$ is true also for an element of $A \cap B$.
