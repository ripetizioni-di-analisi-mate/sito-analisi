Which implication is the contrapositive of “if $n$ is a multiple of 15, then it is a multiple of 3”?

- If 15 does not divide $n$, then 3 does not divide $n$.
- If 3 divides $n$, then 15 divides $n$.
- If 3 does not divide $n$, then 15 does not divide $n$.
- If 15 divides $n$, then 3 does not divide $n$.

## Explanation

Contraposition reverses the hypothesis and the conclusion and negates both. Thus failure of divisibility by the factor implies failure of divisibility by the product.
