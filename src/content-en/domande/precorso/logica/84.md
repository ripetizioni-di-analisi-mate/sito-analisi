Let $x$ be an integer. Which condition says that $x$ differs from both 4 and 11?

- $x\ne 4\ \land\ x\ne 11$
- $x=4\ \land\ x=11$
- $x=4\ \lor\ x=11$
- $x\ne 4\ \lor\ x\ne 11$

## Explanation

Both exclusions must hold at once, so a conjunction is needed. With a disjunction, meeting just one exclusion would be enough.
