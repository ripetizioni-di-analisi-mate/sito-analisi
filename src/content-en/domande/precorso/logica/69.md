What is the negation of “every integer $n>3$ is divisible by 5”?

- There is an integer $n>3$ divisible by 5.
- No integer $n>3$ is divisible by 5.
- There is an integer $n\leq 3$ that is not divisible by 5.
- There is an integer $n>3$ that is not divisible by 5.

## Explanation

One counterexample in the original set disproves “every”. For example, 6 exceeds 3 and leaves remainder 1 on division by 5.
