For real $x$, what kind of condition is "$x > 5$" for "$x > 2$"?

- Sufficient and not necessary
- Necessary and not sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient
- Neither necessary nor sufficient

## Explanation

Let $p$ be the condition "$x > 5$" and $q$ the condition "$x > 2$".

If $x > 5$, then $x > 2$, because $5 > 2$. So the implication $p \Rightarrow q$ is true and $p$ is a **sufficient condition** for $q$.

For $x = 3$ we have $x > 2$ and $x < 5$, so the implication $q \Rightarrow p$ is false. Therefore "$x > 5$" is a sufficient and not necessary condition for "$x > 2$".
