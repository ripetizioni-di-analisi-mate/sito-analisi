For which value of $x$ is the implication "if $x^2 \geq 9$, then $x \geq 3$" false?

- $x = 3$
- $x = -4$
- $x = 1$
- $x = 4$

## Explanation

An implication is false only when the hypothesis is true and the conclusion is false.

For $x = -4$ the hypothesis is true, because $(-4)^2 = 16 \geq 9$, and the conclusion is false, because $-4 < 3$. So for $x = -4$ the implication is **false**.

For $x = 3$ and for $x = 4$ the hypothesis and the conclusion are both true, therefore the implication is true. For $x = 1$ the hypothesis $1 \geq 9$ is false, and an implication with a false hypothesis is true.
