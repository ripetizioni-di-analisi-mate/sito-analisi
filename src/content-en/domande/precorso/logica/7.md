- Sufficient and not necessary
- Necessary and not sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient
- Neither necessary nor sufficient

## Explanation

Adding $4$ to both sides, the equation $2x - 4 = 0$ becomes $2x = 4$. Dividing both sides by $2$ we obtain $x = 2$.

From $x = 2$ we return to $2x - 4 = 0$ by multiplying by $2$ and subtracting $4$, so the two steps are equivalences and
$$
2x - 4 = 0 \iff x = 2 .
$$
Therefore both the implication from $2x - 4 = 0$ to $x = 2$ and the opposite one are true, and each condition is **necessary and sufficient** for the other.
