A rule accepts a positive integer only if it is a multiple of 6. What follows?

- The number 6 is certainly accepted.
- Every multiple of 2 is accepted.
- Every multiple of 6 is accepted.
- Every accepted number is divisible by 2.

## Explanation

“Only if” makes divisibility by 6 necessary for acceptance. Since 2 divides 6, every accepted number is also divisible by 2.
