A certificate requires at least 80% attendance and a pass in the final test. Together these conditions suffice. Which observation shows that attendance alone is insufficient?

- A person with 90% attendance who passes the test.
- A person with 90% attendance who fails the test.
- Someone with 70% attendance who passes the test, testing the necessity of attendance instead.
- A person with 70% attendance who fails the test.

## Explanation

To refute sufficiency, someone must meet the attendance requirement yet fail to earn the certificate. A person who attends 90% but fails the test meets both conditions.
