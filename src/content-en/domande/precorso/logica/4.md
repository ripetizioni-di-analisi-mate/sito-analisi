What is the negation of the proposition $\exists y \in \mathbb{R} \ \forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 \geq y$?

- $\forall y \in \mathbb{R} \ \exists x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 < y$
- $\exists y \in \mathbb{R} \ \forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 < y$
- $\forall x \in \mathbb{R} \ \exists y \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 < y$
- $\forall y \in \mathbb{R} \ \exists x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 \leq y$

## Explanation

With two quantifiers we proceed from the outside in, so $\exists y$ becomes $\forall y$ and $\forall x$ becomes $\exists x$. The order of the quantifiers stays the original one, first $y$ and then $x$, because swapping it changes the meaning of the proposition.

Finally the predicate is negated, and the negation of $x^2 \geq y$ is $x^2 < y$. Therefore the required negation is
$$
\forall y \in \mathbb{R} \ \exists x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 < y .
$$
Of the two propositions, the original one is true, because for $y = 0$ we have $x^2 \geq 0$ for every real $x$.
