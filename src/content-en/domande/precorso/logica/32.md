For real $x$, which statement about the conditions $x > 0$ and $x^2 > 1$ is correct?

- The condition $x > 0$ is sufficient for $x^2 > 1$
- The condition $x > 0$ is necessary for $x^2 > 1$
- Neither of the two conditions implies the other
- The condition $x^2 > 1$ is sufficient for $x > 0$

## Explanation

For $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ we have $x > 0$ and $x^2 = \dfrac{1}{4} < 1$, so $x > 0$ does not imply $x^2 > 1$. Therefore $x > 0$ is not sufficient for $x^2 > 1$.

For $x = -2$ we have $x^2 = 4 > 1$ and $x < 0$, so $x^2 > 1$ does not imply $x > 0$. Therefore $x > 0$ is not necessary for $x^2 > 1$, and $x^2 > 1$ is not sufficient for $x > 0$.

Neither of the two implications is true, so **neither of the two conditions implies the other**.
