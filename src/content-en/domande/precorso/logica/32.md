For real $x$, what kind of condition is "$x > 0$" for "$x^2 > 1$"?

- Sufficient and not necessary
- Necessary and not sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient
- Neither necessary nor sufficient

## Explanation

For $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ we have $x > 0$ and $x^2 = \dfrac{1}{4} < 1$, so the implication from "$x > 0$" to "$x^2 > 1$" is false and the condition is not sufficient.

For $x = -2$ we have $x^2 = 4 > 1$ and $x < 0$, so the implication from "$x^2 > 1$" to "$x > 0$" is also false and the condition is not necessary.

Therefore "$x > 0$" is **neither necessary nor sufficient** for "$x^2 > 1$".
