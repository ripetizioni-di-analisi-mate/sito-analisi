A rule accepts a positive integer only if it is a multiple of 15. What follows?

- The number 15 is certainly accepted.
- Every accepted number is divisible by 3.
- Every multiple of 15 is accepted.
- Every multiple of 3 is accepted.

## Explanation

“Only if” makes divisibility by 15 necessary for acceptance. Since 3 divides 15, every accepted number is also divisible by 3.
