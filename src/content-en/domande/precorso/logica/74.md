Each card bears a letter on one side and a number on the other. The visible faces are A, B, 4 and 7. Which cards must be turned over to test the rule that a vowel implies an even number?

- A and 4.
- B and 7.
- B and 4.
- A and 7.

## Explanation

The rule fails only for a vowel paired with an odd number. The number behind A and the letter behind 7 must be checked. B is already a consonant and 4 is already even.
