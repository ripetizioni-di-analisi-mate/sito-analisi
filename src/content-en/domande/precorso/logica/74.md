What is the negation of “every integer $n>7$ is divisible by 9”?

- There is an integer $n>7$ that is not divisible by 9.
- There is an integer $n>7$ divisible by 9.
- No integer $n>7$ is divisible by 9.
- There is an integer $n\leq 7$ that is not divisible by 9.

## Explanation

One counterexample in the original set disproves “every”. For example, 10 exceeds 7 and leaves remainder 1 on division by 9.
