Let $p$ be the proposition "$12$ is divisible by $5$" and $q$ the proposition "$12$ is divisible by $4$". Which of the following propositions is true?

- $p \land q$
- $\lnot q$
- $\lnot p \land \lnot q$
- $p \lor q$

## Explanation

The proposition $p$ is false, because $12 = 5 \cdot 2 + 2$ leaves remainder $2$ when divided by $5$. The proposition $q$ is true, because $12 = 4 \cdot 3$.

The disjunction $p \lor q$ is true when at least one of the two parts is true, so $p \lor q$ is **true**.

The conjunction $p \land q$ is false, because $p$ is false. The negation $\lnot q$ is false, because $q$ is true, and consequently $\lnot p \land \lnot q$ is also false.
