Which value of $n$ proves by example that the proposition $\exists n \in \mathbb{N} : n^2 - 10n + 21 < 0$ is true?

- $n = 3$
- $n = 7$
- $n = 8$
- $n = 5$

## Explanation

An existential proposition is proved by exhibiting an element that satisfies the predicate.

For $n = 5$ we have $25 - 50 + 21 = -4 < 0$, so $n = 5$ satisfies the inequality and the proposition is **true**.

We have $n^2 - 10n + 21 = (n - 3)(n - 7)$. For $n = 3$ and for $n = 7$ the expression equals $0$, which is not less than $0$. For $n = 8$ it equals $5 \cdot 1 = 5 > 0$.
