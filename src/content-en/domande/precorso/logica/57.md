How does “an integer is a multiple of 28” relate to “the integer is a multiple of 4”?

- Neither necessary nor sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient
- Necessary only
- Sufficient only

## Explanation

Every multiple of 28 is a multiple of 4, so the condition is sufficient. The number 4 shows that a multiple of 4 can fail to satisfy it.
