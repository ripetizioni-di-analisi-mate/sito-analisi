The figure shows the graph of the line $y = mx + q$. Which statement about $m$ and $q$ is true?

::grafico{legenda=0 f="-0.5*x+2" xmin=-2 xmax=6 ymin=-1.5 ymax=4 titolo="Line $y = mx + q$"}

- $m < 0$ and $q > 0$
- $m > 0$ and $q > 0$
- $m < 0$ and $q < 0$
- $m > 0$ and $q < 0$

## Explanation

The line meets the $y$-axis at the point $(0, q)$, which in the figure lies above the origin, so $q > 0$. Moving to the right the line goes down, that is, an increase of $x$ corresponds to a decrease of $y$. Since $m$ is the ratio between the change in $y$ and the change in $x$, it follows that $m < 0$.
