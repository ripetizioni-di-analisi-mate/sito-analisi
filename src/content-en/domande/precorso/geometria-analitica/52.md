Which conic is described by $x^{2} - 12 x - y^{2} + 16 y - 149 = 0$?

- Circle
- Parabola
- Hyperbola
- Ellipse

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $\left(x - 6\right)^{2} - \left(y - 8\right)^{2} = 121$. A difference of squares with a positive right-hand side gives a hyperbola.
