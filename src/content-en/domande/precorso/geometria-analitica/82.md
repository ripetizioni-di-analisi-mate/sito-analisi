A line passes through $(0,4)$ and $(3,0)$. How far is it from the origin?

- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $\dfrac{12}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4 \sqrt{5}}{5}$
- $12$

## Explanation

The line has equation $4 x + 3 y = 12$. Divide the absolute constant term by the norm of the coefficients to get the distance from the origin, namely $\dfrac{12}{5}$.
