The centroid $G$ of a triangle has as coordinates the averages of the coordinates of the three vertices. What are the coordinates of the centroid of the triangle with vertices $A(-1, 2)$, $B(5, 0)$ and $C(2, 7)$?

- $G(2, 3)$
- $G\left(3, \dfrac{9}{2}\right)$
- $G(6, 9)$
- $G(2, 1)$

## Explanation

The average of three numbers is their sum divided by $3$. The sum of the $x$-coordinates of the vertices is $-1 + 5 + 2 = 6$ and the sum of the $y$-coordinates is $2 + 0 + 7 = 9$. Therefore $x_G = \dfrac{6}{3} = 2$ and $y_G = \dfrac{9}{3} = 3$, so the centroid is $G(2, 3)$. The point $(2, 1)$ is instead the midpoint of the side $AB$.
