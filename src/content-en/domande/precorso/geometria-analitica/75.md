For which values of $a$ does the point $P(a, 4)$ lie on the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 24 = 0$?

- $a = -4$ or $a = 2$
- $a = -2\sqrt{2}$ or $a = 2\sqrt{2}$
- For no value of $a$
- $a = -2$ or $a = 4$

## Explanation

The point $P$ lies on the circle if and only if its coordinates satisfy the equation. Substituting $x = a$ and $y = 4$ we obtain $a^2 + 16 - 2a - 24 = 0$, that is, $a^2 - 2a - 8 = 0$. Since $a^2 - 2a - 8 = (a - 4)(a + 2)$, the solutions are $a = 4$ and $a = -2$. Therefore the points of the circle with $y$-coordinate $4$ are $(4, 4)$ and $(-2, 4)$.
