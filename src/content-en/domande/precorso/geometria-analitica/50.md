$ABCD$ is a parallelogram with consecutive vertices in this order, and $A(1, 1)$, $B(5, 2)$, $C(6, 5)$. What are the coordinates of $D$?

- $D(10, 6)$
- $D(0, -2)$
- $D\left(\dfrac{7}{2}, 3\right)$
- $D(2, 4)$

## Explanation

In a parallelogram the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ bisect each other, so they have the same midpoint. The midpoint of $AC$ is $M\left(\dfrac{1 + 6}{2}, \dfrac{1 + 5}{2}\right) = M\left(\dfrac{7}{2}, 3\right)$. Since $M$ is also the midpoint of $BD$, we have $x_D = 2x_M - x_B = 7 - 5 = 2$ and $y_D = 2y_M - y_B = 6 - 2 = 4$. Therefore $D(2, 4)$.
