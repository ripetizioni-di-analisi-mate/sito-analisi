Among all rectangles with perimeter $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the maximum area?

- $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

If one side measures $x$ centimeters, with $0 < x < 10$, the other measures $10 - x$, because the sum of two consecutive sides is half the perimeter. The area is therefore $A = x(10 - x) = -x^2 + 10x$, and its graph is a parabola with downward concavity, since the coefficient of $x^2$ is negative. The maximum value of the area is the $y$-coordinate of the vertex, whose $x$-coordinate is $x_V = -\dfrac{10}{2 \cdot (-1)} = 5$. Therefore the maximum area is $5 \cdot 5 = 25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and the corresponding rectangle is the square with side $5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
