Which statement about the point $P(3, 1)$ and the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 4y - 4 = 0$ is true?

- $P$ lies outside the circle
- $P$ lies on the circle
- $P$ is the center of the circle
- $P$ lies inside the circle

## Explanation

Completing the squares we obtain $(x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 1 + 4 + 4 = 9$, therefore the circle has center $C(1, 2)$ and radius $r = 3$. The distance from $P$ to the center is
$$
\overline{CP} = \sqrt{(3 - 1)^2 + (1 - 2)^2} = \sqrt{5} .
$$
Since $\sqrt{5} < 3$, the point $P$ is closer to the center than the radius. Therefore $P$ lies **inside** the circle.
