What are the foci of the hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{7} = 1$?

- $(\pm\sqrt{2}, 0)$
- $(\pm 4, 0)$
- $(0, \pm 4)$
- $(\pm 16, 0)$

## Explanation

From the equation we read $a^2 = 9$ and $b^2 = 7$. The positive term is the one in $x$, therefore the foci lie on the $x$-axis and are the points $(\pm c, 0)$. For the hyperbola $c^2 = a^2 + b^2 = 9 + 7 = 16$, so $c = 4$. Therefore the foci are $(\pm 4, 0)$.
