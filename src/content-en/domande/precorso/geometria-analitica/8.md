- $C(4, -3)$, $r = 5$
- $C(-4, 3)$, $r = 5$
- $C(4, -3)$, $r = 25$
- $C(4, -3)$, $r = \sqrt{7}$

## Explanation

We group the terms in $x$ and those in $y$ and complete each square. Since $x^2 - 8x = (x - 4)^2 - 16$ and $y^2 + 6y = (y + 3)^2 - 9$, the equation becomes
$$
(x - 4)^2 - 16 + (y + 3)^2 - 9 = 0 ,
$$
that is $(x - 4)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 25$. This is the standard equation of a circle with $x_0 = 4$, $y_0 = -3$ and $r^2 = 25$. Therefore the center is $C(4, -3)$ and the radius is $r = 5$.
