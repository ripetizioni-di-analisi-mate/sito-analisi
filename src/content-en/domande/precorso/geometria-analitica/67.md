A circle has center $C(3, 2)$ and is tangent to the line $3x + 4y + 8 = 0$. What is its equation?

- $(x - 3)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 625$
- $(x - 3)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 5$
- $(x - 3)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 25$
- $(x + 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25$

## Explanation

A line is tangent to a circle if and only if the distance from the center to the line equals the radius. So the radius is
$$
r = \frac{|3 \cdot 3 + 4 \cdot 2 + 8|}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}} = \frac{25}{5} = 5 .
$$
The standard equation of the circle with center $(3, 2)$ and radius $5$ is $(x - 3)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 25$.
