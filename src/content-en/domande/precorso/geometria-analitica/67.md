Which conic is described by $x^{2} - 8 x - y^{2} + 10 y - 58 = 0$?

- Hyperbola
- Parabola
- Circle
- Ellipse

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $\left(x - 4\right)^{2} - \left(y - 5\right)^{2} = 49$. A difference of squares with a positive right-hand side gives a hyperbola.
