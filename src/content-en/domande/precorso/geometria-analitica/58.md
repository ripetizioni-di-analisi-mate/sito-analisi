For every $m \neq 0$ consider the lines $y = mx + 1$ and $x + my - 4 = 0$. Which statement is true?

- They are perpendicular for every $m \neq 0$
- They are parallel for every $m \neq 0$
- They are perpendicular only for $m = 1$
- They are parallel only for $m = -1$

## Explanation

The first line has slope $m$. In the second the coefficient of $y$ is $m \neq 0$, so the line is not vertical and its slope is $-\dfrac{1}{m}$. The product of the two slopes is $m \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{m}\right) = -1$ for every $m \neq 0$. Two non-vertical lines are perpendicular if and only if the product of the slopes is $-1$, therefore the two lines are perpendicular for every $m \neq 0$. Consequently for $m = 1$ and for $m = -1$ the lines are perpendicular, and for no value of $m$ are they parallel.
