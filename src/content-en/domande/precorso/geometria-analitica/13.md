What is the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $A(-3, 5)$ and $B(7, -1)$?

- $M(5, -3)$
- $M(4, 4)$
- $M(2, 2)$
- $M(-5, 3)$

## Explanation

The coordinates of the midpoint are the averages of the coordinates of the endpoints. The abscissa is $\dfrac{-3 + 7}{2} = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2$ and the ordinate is $\dfrac{5 + (-1)}{2} = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2$. Therefore the midpoint is $M(2, 2)$. The half-differences $\dfrac{7 - (-3)}{2} = 5$ and $\dfrac{-1 - 5}{2} = -3$ give instead the displacement that takes $A$ to $M$, indeed $(-3 + 5,\; 5 - 3) = (2, 2)$.
