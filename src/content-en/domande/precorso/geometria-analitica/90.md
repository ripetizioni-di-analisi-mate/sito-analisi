What are the points common to the hyperbola $x^2 - y^2 = 5$ and the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 13$?

- $(2, 3)$, $(2, -3)$, $(-2, 3)$ and $(-2, -3)$
- $(3, 2)$, $(3, -2)$, $(-3, 2)$ and $(-3, -2)$
- $(\sqrt{13}, 0)$ and $(-\sqrt{13}, 0)$
- They have no points in common

## Explanation

The coordinates of the common points satisfy the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - y^2 = 5 \\
x^2 + y^2 = 13
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations we obtain $2x^2 = 18$, so $x^2 = 9$. Subtracting the first equation from the second we obtain $2y^2 = 8$, so $y^2 = 4$. Therefore $x = \pm 3$ and $y = \pm 2$, and every choice of signs gives a common point, so the common points are $(3, 2)$, $(3, -2)$, $(-3, 2)$ and $(-3, -2)$.
