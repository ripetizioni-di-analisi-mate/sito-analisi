What is the equation of the circle with center $C(2, -1)$ passing through the point $P(5, 3)$?

- $(x + 2)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 25$
- $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 5$
- $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 25$
- $(x - 5)^2 + (y - 3)^2 = 25$

## Explanation

The radius is the distance from the center to a point of the circle, therefore
$$
r = \overline{CP} = \sqrt{(5 - 2)^2 + \bigl(3 - (-1)\bigr)^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5 .
$$
The standard equation of the circle with center $(x_0, y_0)$ and radius $r$ is $(x - x_0)^2 + (y - y_0)^2 = r^2$. With $x_0 = 2$, $y_0 = -1$ and $r^2 = 25$ we obtain $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 25$.
