What are the vertices of the ellipse $16x^2 + 9y^2 = 144$?

- $(\pm 4, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 3)$
- $(\pm 9, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 16)$
- $(\pm 16, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 9)$
- $(\pm 3, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 4)$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides by $144$ we obtain the standard form
$$
\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{16} = 1 .
$$
Therefore $a^2 = 9$ and $b^2 = 16$, that is the semi-axes are $a = 3$ and $b = 4$. The ellipse intersects the $x$-axis at the points $(\pm a, 0)$ and the $y$-axis at the points $(0, \pm b)$, so the vertices are $(\pm 3, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 4)$.
