What does the equation $x^2 - 4y^2 + 2x + 8y - 7 = 0$ represent?

- A hyperbola with center $(1, -1)$
- An ellipse with center $(-1, 1)$
- A hyperbola with center $(-1, 1)$
- An ellipse with center $(1, -1)$

## Explanation

The coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ are $1$ and $-4$, with opposite signs, therefore the equation represents a hyperbola, apart from a degenerate case. Factoring $-4$ out of the terms in $y$ and completing the squares we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
(x^2 + 2x) - 4(y^2 - 2y) - 7 &= 0 \\[4pt]
(x + 1)^2 - 1 - 4(y - 1)^2 + 4 - 7 &= 0 \\[4pt]
(x + 1)^2 - 4(y - 1)^2 &= 4 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Dividing by $4$ we get $\dfrac{(x + 1)^2}{4} - (y - 1)^2 = 1$, so the conic is a **hyperbola** with center $(-1, 1)$.
