- $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x - 1$
- $y = 2x + 1$
- $y = -2x - 1$
- $y = 2x - 1$

## Explanation

Since the abscissas of $A(0, -1)$ and $B(2, 3)$ are different, the line is not vertical and its slope is
$$
m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{3 - (-1)}{2 - 0} = \frac{4}{2} = 2 .
$$
The line passes through $A(0, -1)$, which lies on the $y$-axis, therefore the $y$-intercept is $q = -1$. So the line has equation $y = 2x - 1$. Substituting $x = 2$ gives $y = 3$, therefore $B$ also lies on the line.
