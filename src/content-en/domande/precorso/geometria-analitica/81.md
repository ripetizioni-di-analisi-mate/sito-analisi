A line passes through $(0,8)$ and $(6,0)$. How far is it from the origin?

- $\dfrac{24}{7}$
- $48$
- $\dfrac{24}{5}$
- $\dfrac{48 \sqrt{157}}{157}$

## Explanation

The line has equation $8 x + 6 y = 48$. Divide the absolute constant term by the norm of the coefficients to get the distance from the origin, namely $\dfrac{24}{5}$.
