For every point $P$ of the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$ the sum $\overline{PF_1} + \overline{PF_2}$ of the distances from $P$ to the foci is the same. What is its value?

- $10$
- $5$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

Since $a = 5 > b = 3$, the foci lie on the $x$-axis and $c^2 = 25 - 9 = 16$, so they are $F_1(-4, 0)$ and $F_2(4, 0)$. The sum is the same for every point of the ellipse, so it can be computed at the vertex $P(5, 0)$. We have $\overline{PF_1} = 9$ and $\overline{PF_2} = 1$, therefore the sum is $10$, that is, $2a$, the length of the major axis.
