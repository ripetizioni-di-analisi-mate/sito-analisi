For which values of $k$ does the parabola $y = x^2 - 4x + k$ not intersect the $x$-axis?

- $k > 4$
- $k < 4$
- $k > 16$
- $k \geq 4$

## Explanation

The intersections with the $x$-axis are the solutions of $x^2 - 4x + k = 0$, therefore the parabola does not intersect the $x$-axis if and only if the discriminant is negative. The discriminant is $\Delta = 16 - 4k$. The condition $16 - 4k < 0$ is equivalent to $4k > 16$, so $k > 4$. For $k = 4$ we have $\Delta = 0$, and the parabola touches the $x$-axis at the vertex $(2, 0)$.
