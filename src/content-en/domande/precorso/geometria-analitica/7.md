What is the general form of the equation of the circle with center $C(-1, 3)$ and radius $r = 2$?

- $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 6y + 6 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 6y + 8 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 6y + 6 = 0$
- $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 6y - 6 = 0$

## Explanation

The standard equation of the circle with center $(x_0, y_0)$ and radius $r$ is $(x - x_0)^2 + (y - y_0)^2 = r^2$. With $x_0 = -1$, $y_0 = 3$ and $r = 2$ we obtain
$$
(x + 1)^2 + (y - 3)^2 = 4 .
$$
Expanding the squares gives $x^2 + 2x + 1 + y^2 - 6y + 9 = 4$. Moving $4$ to the left-hand side, the constant becomes $1 + 9 - 4 = 6$. Hence the general form is $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 6y + 6 = 0$.
