The arch of a bridge has the shape of half an ellipse: the base, on the road, is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and the maximum height, at the center, is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. How high is the arch at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center of the base?

- $2.56\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

We fix the coordinate system with the origin at the center of the base and the $x$-axis along the road. Then the arch is the part with $y \geq 0$ of the ellipse with semi-axes $a = 5$, half the base, and $b = 4$, the maximum height:
$$
\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{16} = 1 .
$$
For $x = 3$ we obtain $\dfrac{y^2}{16} = 1 - \dfrac{9}{25} = \dfrac{16}{25}$, so $y^2 = \dfrac{256}{25}$ and $y = \dfrac{16}{5}$. Therefore at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center the arch is $3.2\,\mathrm{m}$ high.
