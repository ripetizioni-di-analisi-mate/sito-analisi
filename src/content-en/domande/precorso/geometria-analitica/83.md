What is the slope of the rising asymptote of $\dfrac{x^{2}}{36} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{16} = 1$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

For the asymptotes, set the quadratic part equal to zero. From $\dfrac{x^{2}}{36} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{16} = 0$, the positive slope is $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
