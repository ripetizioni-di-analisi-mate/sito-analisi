Which conic is described by $x^{2} - 6 x - y^{2} + 8 y - 43 = 0$?

- Parabola
- Circle
- Ellipse
- Hyperbola

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $\left(x - 3\right)^{2} - \left(y - 4\right)^{2} = 36$. A difference of squares with a positive right-hand side gives a hyperbola.
