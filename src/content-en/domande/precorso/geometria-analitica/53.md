The points $A(2, 5)$ and $B(2, -1)$ have the same $x$-coordinate. What is the equation of the line $AB$?

- $y = 5$
- $y = 2$
- $x = 2$
- $x = 5$

## Explanation

Since $x_A = x_B$, the denominator $x_2 - x_1$ of the slope is zero, so the line $AB$ has no slope and is vertical. A vertical line has equation $x = c$, and all its points have $x$-coordinate $c$. Since $A$ and $B$ have $x$-coordinate $2$, the line $AB$ has equation $x = 2$.
