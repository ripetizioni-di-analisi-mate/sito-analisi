For which values of $k$ does the equation $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 2y + k = 0$ represent a circle?

- $k > 5$
- $k < 5$
- $k < 20$
- $k \leq 5$

## Explanation

Completing the squares we have $x^2 - 4x = (x - 2)^2 - 4$ and $y^2 + 2y = (y + 1)^2 - 1$, therefore the equation becomes
$$
(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 5 - k .
$$
The left-hand side is the square of the distance from the point $(x, y)$ to the point $(2, -1)$, so the equation represents a circle if and only if $5 - k > 0$, that is $k < 5$. For $k = 5$ the equation is satisfied only by the point $(2, -1)$, and for $k > 5$ by no point.
