Which of the following equations represents an ellipse?

- $x^2 - 3y^2 + 6 = 0$
- $4x^2 + 4y^2 - 1 = 0$
- $3x^2 - 2y + 1 = 0$
- $2x^2 + 5y^2 - 4x - 3 = 0$

## Explanation

The type of conic is read from the coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$. In $x^2 - 3y^2 + 6 = 0$ the coefficients have opposite signs, therefore the equation represents a hyperbola; in $4x^2 + 4y^2 - 1 = 0$ they are equal, therefore it represents a circle; in $3x^2 - 2y + 1 = 0$ only $x^2$ appears and the variable $y$ is of the first degree, therefore it represents a parabola. In $2x^2 + 5y^2 - 4x - 3 = 0$ the coefficients $2$ and $5$ have the same sign and are different, and completing the square gives $2(x - 1)^2 + 5y^2 = 5$, with a positive right-hand side. So this equation represents an **ellipse**.
