What kind of quadrilateral is $ABCD$, with $A(0, 0)$, $B(4, 2)$, $C(3, 4)$ and $D(-1, 2)$?

- A square
- A parallelogram that is not a rectangle
- A trapezoid that is not a parallelogram
- A rectangle that is not a square

## Explanation

The slopes of the sides are $m_{AB} = \dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $m_{BC} = \dfrac{4 - 2}{3 - 4} = -2$, $m_{CD} = \dfrac{2 - 4}{-1 - 3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $m_{DA} = \dfrac{0 - 2}{0 - (-1)} = -2$. Since $m_{AB} = m_{CD}$ and $m_{BC} = m_{DA}$, opposite sides are parallel, so $ABCD$ is a parallelogram. Since $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot (-2) = -1$, consecutive sides are perpendicular, so $ABCD$ is a rectangle. The sides have lengths $\overline{AB} = \sqrt{20}$ and $\overline{BC} = \sqrt{5}$, which are different, therefore $ABCD$ is a **rectangle that is not a square**.
