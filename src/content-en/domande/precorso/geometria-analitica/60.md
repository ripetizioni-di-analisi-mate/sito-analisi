What is the slope of the rising asymptote of $\dfrac{x^{2}}{25} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{9} = 1$?

- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

For the asymptotes, set the quadratic part equal to zero. From the equation $\dfrac{x^{2}}{25} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{9} = 0$, the positive slope is $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
