A circle passes through $A(1, 0)$ and $B(3, 0)$ and has its center on the line $y = x$. What is its equation?

- $(x - 2)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 4$
- $(x + 2)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 5$
- $(x - 2)^2 + y^2 = 1$
- $(x - 2)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 5$

## Explanation

The center is equidistant from $A$ and $B$, so it lies on the perpendicular bisector of the segment $AB$, that is, on the vertical line $x = 2$ through the midpoint $(2, 0)$. Since the center also lies on the line $y = x$, the center is $C(2, 2)$. The radius is the distance from $C$ to $A$, that is, $r = \sqrt{(2 - 1)^2 + (2 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{5}$. Therefore the circle has equation $(x - 2)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 5$.
