A line passes through $(0,7)$ and $(5,0)$. How far is it from the origin?

- $\dfrac{35 \sqrt{74}}{74}$
- $\dfrac{35 \sqrt{106}}{106}$
- $35$
- $\dfrac{35}{12}$

## Explanation

The line has equation $7 x + 5 y = 35$. Divide the absolute constant term by the norm of the coefficients to get the distance from the origin, namely $\dfrac{35 \sqrt{74}}{74}$.
