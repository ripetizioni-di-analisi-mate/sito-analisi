Which of the following parabolas has vertex $V(1, 2)$ and opens downward?

- $y = -x^2 - 2x + 1$
- $y = x^2 - 2x + 3$
- $y = -x^2 + 2x + 2$
- $y = -x^2 + 2x + 1$

## Explanation

The parabola opens downward if and only if $a < 0$, therefore the parabola $y = x^2 - 2x + 3$ is ruled out, even though it has vertex $(1, 2)$. For $y = -x^2 + 2x + 1$ the abscissa of the vertex is $x_V = -\dfrac{2}{2 \cdot (-1)} = 1$ and the ordinate is $-1 + 2 + 1 = 2$, therefore the vertex is $V(1, 2)$. The parabola $y = -x^2 + 2x + 2$ has the same axis $x = 1$ but vertex $(1, 3)$, while $y = -x^2 - 2x + 1$ has vertex $(-1, 2)$. So the required parabola is $y = -x^2 + 2x + 1$.
