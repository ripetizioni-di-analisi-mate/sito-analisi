Which of the following parabolas has its vertex on the $y$-axis?

- $y = x^2 - 2x$
- $y = (x - 2)^2$
- $y = 3x^2 - 2$
- $y = -x^2 + 4x + 4$

## Explanation

The vertex lies on the $y$-axis if and only if its $x$-coordinate $-\dfrac{b}{2a}$ is zero, that is, if and only if $b = 0$. The parabola $y = 3x^2 - 2$ has $b = 0$, so its vertex is $(0, -2)$, on the $y$-axis. The other three parabolas have vertex at $(1, -1)$, at $(2, 0)$ and at $(2, 8)$. Therefore the required parabola is $y = 3x^2 - 2$.
