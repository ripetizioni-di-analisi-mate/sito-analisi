How long is the chord cut by the line $y = x + 2$ on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 10$?

- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $8$
- $32$
- $4\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The $x$-coordinates of the endpoints of the chord are the solutions of $x^2 + (x + 2)^2 = 10$, that is, of $2x^2 + 4x - 6 = 0$, which is equivalent to $x^2 + 2x - 3 = 0$. The solutions are $x = 1$ and $x = -3$, so the endpoints of the chord are $(1, 3)$ and $(-3, -1)$. Therefore the chord has length
$$
\sqrt{\bigl(1 - (-3)\bigr)^2 + \bigl(3 - (-1)\bigr)^2} = \sqrt{16 + 16} = 4\sqrt{2} .
$$
