A line passes through $(0,5)$ and $(4,0)$. How far is it from the origin?

- $\dfrac{4 \sqrt{65}}{13}$
- $\dfrac{20 \sqrt{41}}{41}$
- $20$
- $\dfrac{20}{9}$

## Explanation

The line has equation $5 x + 4 y = 20$. Divide the absolute constant term by the norm of the coefficients to get the distance from the origin, namely $\dfrac{20 \sqrt{41}}{41}$.
