What is the slope of the line $3x + 2y - 6 = 0$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $3$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

We solve the equation for $y$. Moving $3x - 6$ to the right-hand side we obtain $2y = -3x + 6$, and dividing by $2$ we get $y = -\dfrac{3}{2}x + 3$. Therefore the slope is $m = -\dfrac{3}{2}$. The same value comes from the formula $m = -\dfrac{a}{b}$ with $a = 3$ and $b = 2$.
