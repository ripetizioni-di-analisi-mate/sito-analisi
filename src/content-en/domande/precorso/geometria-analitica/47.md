In the triangle with vertices $A(-2, 1)$, $B(6, -5)$ and $C(1, 7)$, let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the sides $AC$ and $BC$. What is the length of the segment $MN$?

- $10$
- $7$
- $5$
- $25$

## Explanation

The coordinates of a midpoint are the averages of the coordinates of the endpoints, so $M\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}, 4\right)$ and $N\left(\dfrac{7}{2}, 1\right)$. The difference of the $x$-coordinates is $\dfrac{7}{2} - \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = 4$ and that of the $y$-coordinates is $1 - 4 = -3$. Therefore
$$
\overline{MN} = \sqrt{4^2 + (-3)^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5 .
$$
The side $AB$ has length $\sqrt{8^2 + (-6)^2} = 10$, twice $\overline{MN}$.
