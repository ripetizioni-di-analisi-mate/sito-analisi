For the parabola $y = -x^2 + 4x$, what are the vertex and the concavity?

- $V(2, 4)$, opens upward
- $V(-2, -12)$, opens downward
- $V(2, -4)$, opens downward
- $V(2, 4)$, opens downward

## Explanation

The coefficients are $a = -1$, $b = 4$ and $c = 0$. Since $a < 0$, the parabola opens downward and the vertex is its highest point. The abscissa of the vertex is
$$
x_V = -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{4}{-2} = 2 ,
$$
and substituting $x = 2$ into the equation gives $y_V = -4 + 8 = 4$. Therefore the vertex is $V(2, 4)$ and the parabola opens **downward**.
