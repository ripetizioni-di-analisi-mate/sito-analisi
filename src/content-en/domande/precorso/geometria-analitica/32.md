A circle has as a diameter the segment with endpoints $A(-1, 2)$ and $B(5, -6)$. What are its center and its radius?

- $C(2, -2)$, $r = 5$
- $C(2, -2)$, $r = 10$
- $C(3, -4)$, $r = 5$
- $C(2, -2)$, $r = 25$

## Explanation

The center of a circle is the midpoint of each of its diameters, therefore $C\left(\dfrac{-1 + 5}{2}, \dfrac{2 - 6}{2}\right) = C(2, -2)$. The diameter measures
$$
\overline{AB} = \sqrt{(5 + 1)^2 + (-6 - 2)^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10 ,
$$
and the radius is half the diameter. Therefore $r = 5$.
