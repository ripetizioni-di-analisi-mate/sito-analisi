The origin $O(0, 0)$ lies on only one of the following circles. Which one?

- $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 5$
- $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 25$
- $x^2 + y^2 = 25$
- $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y - 25 = 0$

## Explanation

A circle passes through the origin if and only if the coordinates $(0, 0)$ satisfy its equation. Substituting $x = 0$ and $y = 0$ into the equation $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 25$ we obtain $9 + 16 = 25$, which is true. In the other three equations the substitution gives $25 = 5$, $0 = 25$ and $-25 = 0$, which are false. Therefore only the circle with center $(3, -4)$ and radius $5$, equal to the distance from the center to the origin, passes through the origin.
