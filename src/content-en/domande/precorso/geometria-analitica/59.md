The perpendicular bisector of a segment is the line perpendicular to the segment through its midpoint. What is the equation of the perpendicular bisector of the segment with endpoints $A(1, 2)$ and $B(5, 6)$?

- $y = x + 1$
- $y = -x + 7$
- $y = -x + 4$
- $y = -x + 3$

## Explanation

The midpoint of $AB$ is $M\left(\dfrac{1 + 5}{2}, \dfrac{2 + 6}{2}\right) = M(3, 4)$. The line $AB$ has slope $\dfrac{6 - 2}{5 - 1} = 1$, so the perpendicular bisector, which is perpendicular to $AB$, has slope $-1$. The line with slope $-1$ through $M$ is $y - 4 = -(x - 3)$. Therefore the perpendicular bisector of the segment is $y = -x + 7$.
