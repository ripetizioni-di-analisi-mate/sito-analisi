- $y = -2x + 5$
- $y = 2x - 7$
- $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x + \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $y = -2x + 7$

## Explanation

Solving the equation $x - 2y + 4 = 0$ for $y$ gives $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + 2$, therefore the given line has slope $m_1 = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Two non-vertical lines are perpendicular if and only if $m_1 \, m_2 = -1$, so the required line has slope $m_2 = -2$. The line with slope $-2$ passing through $P(3, -1)$ is
$$
y - (-1) = -2\,(x - 3) ,
$$
that is $y + 1 = -2x + 6$. Hence the required line is $y = -2x + 5$.
