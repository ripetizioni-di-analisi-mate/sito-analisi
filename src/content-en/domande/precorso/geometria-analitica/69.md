The parabola $y = x^2 - 4x + 7$ passes through the point $(5, 12)$. Which other point of the parabola has $y$-coordinate $12$?

- $(-5, 12)$
- $(-1, 12)$
- $(-9, 12)$
- $(3, 12)$

## Explanation

The parabola is symmetric with respect to its axis, the line $x = x_V$ with $x_V = -\dfrac{-4}{2} = 2$. The reflection of $(5, 12)$ across the line $x = 2$ has the same $y$-coordinate and $x$-coordinate $2 \cdot 2 - 5 = -1$. Therefore the other point is $(-1, 12)$; indeed for $x = -1$ we obtain $y = 1 + 4 + 7 = 12$.
