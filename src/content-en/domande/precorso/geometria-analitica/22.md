Which statement about the line with equation $2y - 6 = 0$ is true?

- It is parallel to the $y$-axis and passes through $(3, 0)$
- It has slope $2$
- It is parallel to the $x$-axis and passes through $(0, 3)$
- It passes through the origin and has slope $3$

## Explanation

In the equation $2y - 6 = 0$ the variable $x$ does not appear, and dividing by $2$ we obtain $y = 3$. Therefore the line consists of all the points with ordinate $3$, whatever the abscissa. It is the line $y = mx + q$ with $m = 0$ and $q = 3$, so it is parallel to the $x$-axis and intersects the $y$-axis at the point $(0, 3)$.
