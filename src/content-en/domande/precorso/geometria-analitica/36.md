What is the line tangent to the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 25$ at its point $P(3, 4)$?

- $4x + 3y - 24 = 0$
- $3x + 4y - 25 = 0$
- $4x - 3y = 0$
- $3x - 4y + 7 = 0$

## Explanation

The tangent at $P$ is perpendicular to the radius $OP$, which has slope $\dfrac{4}{3}$, therefore the tangent has slope $-\dfrac{3}{4}$. The line through $P$ with this slope is $y - 4 = -\dfrac{3}{4}(x - 3)$. Multiplying by $4$ we obtain $4y - 16 = -3x + 9$, that is $3x + 4y - 25 = 0$. In agreement with the tangency condition, the distance from the center $O$ to this line is $\dfrac{|-25|}{\sqrt{9 + 16}} = 5$, equal to the radius.
