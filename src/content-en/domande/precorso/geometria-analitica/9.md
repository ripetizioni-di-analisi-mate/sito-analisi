What are the foci of the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{16} + \dfrac{y^2}{7} = 1$?

- $(\pm\sqrt{23}, 0)$
- $(0, \pm 3)$
- $(\pm 9, 0)$
- $(\pm 3, 0)$

## Explanation

From the equation we read $a^2 = 16$ and $b^2 = 7$, therefore the semi-axes are $a = 4$ and $b = \sqrt{7}$. Since $a > b$, the major semi-axis lies on the $x$-axis and the foci are the points $(\pm c, 0)$. For the ellipse we have
$$
c^2 = a^2 - b^2 = 16 - 7 = 9 ,
$$
so $c = 3$. Hence the foci are $(\pm 3, 0)$.
