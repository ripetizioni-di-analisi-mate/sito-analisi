What is the position of the line $x + y - 4 = 0$ with respect to the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 9$?

- The line is tangent to the circle
- The line does not meet the circle
- The line is a secant of the circle
- The line passes through the center of the circle

## Explanation

The circle has center $O(0, 0)$ and radius $r = 3$. The distance from the center to the line is
$$
d = \frac{|0 + 0 - 4|}{\sqrt{1^2 + 1^2}} = \frac{4}{\sqrt{2}} = 2\sqrt{2} .
$$
Since $(2\sqrt{2})^2 = 8 < 9 = r^2$, the distance from the center to the line is less than the radius. Therefore the line is **a secant** and intersects the circle at two points.
