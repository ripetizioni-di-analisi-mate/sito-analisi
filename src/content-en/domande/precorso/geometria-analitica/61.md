For which value of $a$ is the line through the origin $O$ and the point $P(a, 3)$ perpendicular to the line $y = 2x - 5$?

- $6$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-6$

## Explanation

For $a = 0$ the line $OP$ is the $y$-axis, which is not perpendicular to the given line. For $a \neq 0$ the line $OP$ has slope $\dfrac{3 - 0}{a - 0} = \dfrac{3}{a}$. The two lines are perpendicular if and only if the product of the slopes is $-1$, that is, if $\dfrac{3}{a} \cdot 2 = -1$. So $\dfrac{6}{a} = -1$, and therefore $a = -6$.
