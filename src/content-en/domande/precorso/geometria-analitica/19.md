At which points does the line $2x - 5y + 10 = 0$ intersect the coordinate axes?

- $(5, 0)$ and $(0, -2)$
- $(-2, 0)$ and $(0, 5)$
- $(-5, 0)$ and $(0, 2)$
- $(5, 0)$ and $(0, 2)$

## Explanation

The points of the $x$-axis have ordinate zero, therefore we set $y = 0$ and obtain $2x + 10 = 0$, that is $x = -5$. The points of the $y$-axis have abscissa zero, therefore we set $x = 0$ and obtain $-5y + 10 = 0$, that is $y = 2$. So the line intersects the axes at the points $(-5, 0)$ and $(0, 2)$.
