A line passes through $(0,3)$ and $(2,0)$. How far is it from the origin?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{6 \sqrt{13}}{13}$
- $\dfrac{6 \sqrt{29}}{29}$

## Explanation

The line has equation $3 x + 2 y = 6$. Divide the absolute constant term by the norm of the coefficients to get the distance from the origin, namely $\dfrac{6 \sqrt{13}}{13}$.
