A line passes through $(0,3)$ and $(2,0)$. How far is it from the origin?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{6 \sqrt{13}}{13}$
- $\dfrac{6 \sqrt{29}}{29}$

## Explanation

The line has equation $3 x + 2 y = 6$. The distance from the origin is obtained by dividing the absolute value of the constant term by the norm of the coefficients, giving $\dfrac{6 \sqrt{13}}{13}$.
