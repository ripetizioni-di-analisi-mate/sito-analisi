The points $P$ and $Q$ divide the segment with endpoints $A(-1, 2)$ and $B(5, 5)$ into three equal parts, and $P$ is the one closer to $A$. What are the coordinates of $P$?

- $P(3, 4)$
- $P\left(2, \dfrac{7}{2}\right)$
- $P(2, 1)$
- $P(1, 3)$

## Explanation

Since $\overline{AP} = \overline{PQ} = \overline{QB}$ and the four points are collinear, $P$ is the midpoint of $AQ$ and $Q$ is the midpoint of $PB$. For the $x$-coordinates this means $2x_P = x_A + x_Q$ and $2x_Q = x_P + x_B$. Substituting $x_Q = 2x_P - x_A$ into the second equality we obtain $4x_P - 2x_A = x_P + x_B$, that is,
$$
x_P = \frac{2x_A + x_B}{3} = \frac{-2 + 5}{3} = 1 ,
$$
and in the same way $y_P = \dfrac{2y_A + y_B}{3} = \dfrac{4 + 5}{3} = 3$. Therefore $P(1, 3)$, and the second point is $Q(3, 4)$.
