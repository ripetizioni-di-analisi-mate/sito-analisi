A square has one side on the line $x + 2y - 4 = 0$ and a vertex at the point $P(4, 3)$, which does not lie on this line. What is the area of the square?

- $36$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{36}{5}$
- $\dfrac{36}{25}$

## Explanation

The vertex $P$ does not lie on the line, so it belongs to the opposite side, which is parallel to the line and whose distance from it equals the side $\ell$ of the square. Therefore $\ell$ is the distance from $P$ to the line:
$$
\ell = \frac{|4 + 2 \cdot 3 - 4|}{\sqrt{1^2 + 2^2}} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{5}} .
$$
So the area of the square is $\ell^2 = \dfrac{36}{5}$.
