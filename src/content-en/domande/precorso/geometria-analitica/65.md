Which points of the $y$-axis are at distance $2$ from the line $3x + 4y - 2 = 0$?

- $(0, 1)$ and $(0, 0)$
- $(0, 4)$ and $(0, -3)$
- $(0, 3)$ and $(0, -2)$
- $(0, 13)$ and $(0, -12)$

## Explanation

A point of the $y$-axis has coordinates $P(0, y)$, and its distance from the line is $\dfrac{|4y - 2|}{\sqrt{9 + 16}} = \dfrac{|4y - 2|}{5}$. Setting the distance equal to $2$ we obtain $|4y - 2| = 10$. So $4y - 2 = 10$ or $4y - 2 = -10$, that is, $y = 3$ or $y = -2$. Therefore the required points are $(0, 3)$ and $(0, -2)$.
