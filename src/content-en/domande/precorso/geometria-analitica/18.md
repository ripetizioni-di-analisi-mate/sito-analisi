At which point do the lines $2x - y = 1$ and $x + y = 5$ intersect?

- $(2, 3)$
- $(3, 2)$
- $(1, 4)$
- $(2, -3)$

## Explanation

The coordinates of the intersection point satisfy both equations, therefore we solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - y = 1 \\
x + y = 5
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations side by side we obtain $3x = 6$, therefore $x = 2$. Substituting $x = 2$ into the second equation we get $y = 5 - 2 = 3$. So the lines intersect at the point $(2, 3)$.
