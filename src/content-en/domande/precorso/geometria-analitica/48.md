Which point of the $x$-axis is equidistant from $A(1, 3)$ and $B(5, 1)$?

- $(3, 0)$
- $(3, 2)$
- $(2, 0)$
- $(4, 0)$

## Explanation

A point of the $x$-axis has coordinates $P(x, 0)$. Since distances are positive, the condition $\overline{PA} = \overline{PB}$ is equivalent to $\overline{PA}^2 = \overline{PB}^2$, that is, to
$$
(x - 1)^2 + 9 = (x - 5)^2 + 1 .
$$
Expanding the squares we obtain $x^2 - 2x + 10 = x^2 - 10x + 26$, so $8x = 16$ and $x = 2$. Therefore the required point is $(2, 0)$. The midpoint $(3, 2)$ of the segment $AB$ is equidistant from $A$ and $B$, but it does not lie on the $x$-axis.
