Given the points $A(-2, 1)$ and $B(4, 9)$, what is the distance $\overline{AB}$?

- $2\sqrt{17}$
- $10$
- $14$
- $100$

## Explanation

The distance between two points is computed with the formula $\overline{AB} = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$, which is the Pythagorean theorem applied to the horizontal and vertical legs. The difference of the abscissas is $4 - (-2) = 6$ and that of the ordinates is $9 - 1 = 8$. Therefore
$$
\overline{AB} = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10 .
$$
Hence the distance between $A$ and $B$ is $10$.
