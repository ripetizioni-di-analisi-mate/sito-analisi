What are the foci of the conic with equation $4x^2 + 9y^2 - 16x - 20 = 0$?

- $(-\sqrt{5}, 0)$ and $(\sqrt{5}, 0)$
- $(2 - \sqrt{13}, 0)$ and $(2 + \sqrt{13}, 0)$
- $(2, -\sqrt{5})$ and $(2, \sqrt{5})$
- $(2 - \sqrt{5}, 0)$ and $(2 + \sqrt{5}, 0)$

## Explanation

The coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ are $4$ and $9$, of the same sign and different, so the conic is an ellipse, to be confirmed after the reduction. Factoring $4$ out of the terms in $x$ and completing the square we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
4(x^2 - 4x) + 9y^2 - 20 &= 0 \\[4pt]
4(x - 2)^2 - 16 + 9y^2 - 20 &= 0 \\[4pt]
4(x - 2)^2 + 9y^2 &= 36 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Dividing by $36$ we have $\dfrac{(x - 2)^2}{9} + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$, so the ellipse has center $(2, 0)$, horizontal semi-axis $3$ and vertical semi-axis $2$. Since the major semi-axis is horizontal, the foci lie on the line $y = 0$ at distance $c$ from the center, with $c^2 = 9 - 4 = 5$. Therefore the foci are $(2 - \sqrt{5}, 0)$ and $(2 + \sqrt{5}, 0)$.
