What is the slope of the rising asymptote of $\dfrac{x^{2}}{121} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{64} = 1$?

- $\dfrac{64}{121}$
- $- \dfrac{8}{11}$
- $\dfrac{8}{11}$
- $\dfrac{11}{8}$

## Explanation

For the asymptotes, set the quadratic part equal to zero. From the equation $\dfrac{x^{2}}{121} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{64} = 0$, the positive slope is $\dfrac{8}{11}$.
