What is the equation in standard form of the ellipse that has a vertex at $(5, 0)$ and a focus at $(4, 0)$?

- $\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{16} = 1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{41} = 1$
- $\dfrac{x^2}{5} + \dfrac{y^2}{3} = 1$

## Explanation

In standard form the ellipse has equation $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$, and the vertex $(5, 0)$ on the $x$-axis gives $a = 5$. The focus $(4, 0)$ lies on the $x$-axis, therefore the major semi-axis is $a$ and $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$ with $c = 4$. So $b^2 = a^2 - c^2 = 25 - 16 = 9$. Therefore the ellipse has equation $\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$.
