Which of the following points is closest to the line $2x - y + 1 = 0$?

- $(2, 4)$
- $(1, 1)$
- $(-1, 2)$
- $(3, 2)$

## Explanation

By the point-to-line distance formula, the point $P(x_0, y_0)$ is at distance
$$
d = \frac{|2x_0 - y_0 + 1|}{\sqrt{2^2 + (-1)^2}} = \frac{|2x_0 - y_0 + 1|}{\sqrt{5}}
$$
from the line $2x - y + 1 = 0$. The denominator is the same for all the points, so the point closest to the line is the one with the smallest numerator. The numerator equals $|4 - 4 + 1| = 1$ for $(2, 4)$, $|2 - 1 + 1| = 2$ for $(1, 1)$, $|-2 - 2 + 1| = 3$ for $(-1, 2)$ and $|6 - 2 + 1| = 5$ for $(3, 2)$. Therefore the closest point is $(2, 4)$, at distance $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$ from the line.
