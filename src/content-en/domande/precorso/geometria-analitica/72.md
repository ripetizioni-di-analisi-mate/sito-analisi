The equation $x = y^2 - 4y + 1$ represents a parabola with a horizontal axis of symmetry. What is its vertex?

- $V(2, -3)$
- $V(-3, 2)$
- $V(13, -2)$
- $V(3, 2)$

## Explanation

Completing the square in the terms in $y$ we have $y^2 - 4y = (y - 2)^2 - 4$, so the equation becomes $x = (y - 2)^2 - 3$. Since $(y - 2)^2 \geq 0$, every point of the parabola has $x$-coordinate $x \geq -3$, and the $x$-coordinate equals $-3$ only for $y = 2$. Therefore the vertex is $V(-3, 2)$, and the axis of symmetry is the horizontal line $y = 2$.
