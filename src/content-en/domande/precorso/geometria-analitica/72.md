Which conic is described by $x^{2} - 10 x - y^{2} + 14 y - 105 = 0$?

- Circle
- Parabola
- Hyperbola
- Ellipse

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $\left(x - 5\right)^{2} - \left(y - 7\right)^{2} = 81$. A difference of squares with a positive right-hand side gives a hyperbola.
