The parabola in the figure has vertex $V(-1, 2)$ and meets the $y$-axis at the point $(0, 1)$. What is its equation?

::grafico{legenda=0 f="-x^2-2*x+1" xmin=-4 xmax=2.5 ymin=-4 ymax=3.5 punti="-1,2,pieno,V;0,1,pieno" titolo="Parabola with vertex $V(-1, 2)$ through $(0, 1)$"}

- $y = -x^2 - 2x + 1$
- $y = x^2 + 2x + 3$
- $y = -x^2 + 2x + 1$
- $y = -x^2 - 2x + 2$

## Explanation

The intersection with the $y$-axis is the point $(0, c)$, so $c = 1$. The $x$-coordinate of the vertex is $-\dfrac{b}{2a} = -1$, so $b = 2a$. The vertex $(-1, 2)$ lies on the parabola, therefore $a - b + 1 = 2$, that is, $a - 2a = 1$, and so $a = -1$ and $b = -2$. The parabola is $y = -x^2 - 2x + 1$, and the coefficient $a = -1$ is negative, in agreement with the downward concavity in the figure.
