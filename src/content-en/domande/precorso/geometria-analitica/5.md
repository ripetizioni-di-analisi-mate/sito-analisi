What is the vertex of the parabola $y = x^2 - 6x + 5$?

- $V(-3, 32)$
- $V(3, -4)$
- $V(3, 4)$
- $V(3, 14)$

## Explanation

In the parabola $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ the coefficients are $a = 1$, $b = -6$ and $c = 5$. The abscissa of the vertex is
$$
x_V = -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{-6}{2} = 3 .
$$
The ordinate is obtained by substituting $x = 3$ into the equation, therefore $y_V = 9 - 18 + 5 = -4$. The same value comes from the formula $c - \dfrac{b^2}{4a} = 5 - \dfrac{36}{4} = -4$. Therefore the vertex is $V(3, -4)$.
