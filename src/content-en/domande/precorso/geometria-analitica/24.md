What is the distance from the point $P(3, -2)$ to the line $y = 4$?

- $6$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The line $y = 4$ is horizontal, so the perpendicular drawn from $P$ to the line is vertical and meets it at the point $H(3, 4)$. Therefore the distance is $\overline{PH} = |4 - (-2)| = 6$. The same value is obtained with the point-to-line distance formula, writing the line as $y - 4 = 0$:
$$
d = \frac{|-2 - 4|}{\sqrt{0^2 + 1^2}} = 6 .
$$
