What is the distance from the origin $O(0, 0)$ to the line $y = 2x + 5$?

- $\sqrt{5}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{3}}$

## Explanation

The formula for the distance from a point to a line uses the general form, therefore we write $y = 2x + 5$ as $2x - y + 5 = 0$, with $a = 2$, $b = -1$ and $c = 5$. Substituting the coordinates of the origin we obtain
$$
d = \frac{|2 \cdot 0 - 1 \cdot 0 + 5|}{\sqrt{2^2 + (-1)^2}} = \frac{5}{\sqrt{5}} = \sqrt{5} .
$$
Therefore the distance from the origin to the line is $\sqrt{5}$.
