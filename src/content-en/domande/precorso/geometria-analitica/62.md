What is the reflection of $P(0, 4)$ across the line $r\colon y = 2x - 1$?

- $(2, 3)$
- $(4, 2)$
- $\left(1, \dfrac{7}{2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{2}, -1\right)$

## Explanation

The reflection $P'$ lies on the perpendicular to $r$ through $P$, and the point $H$ where this perpendicular meets $r$ is the midpoint of $PP'$. The line $r$ has slope $2$, so the perpendicular through $P$ is $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x + 4$. Equating the two expressions of $y$ we obtain $2x - 1 = -\dfrac{1}{2}x + 4$, that is, $\dfrac{5}{2}x = 5$, so $x = 2$ and $H(2, 3)$. Since $H$ is the midpoint of $PP'$, we have $x_{P'} = 2 \cdot 2 - 0 = 4$ and $y_{P'} = 2 \cdot 3 - 4 = 2$. Therefore $P'(4, 2)$.
