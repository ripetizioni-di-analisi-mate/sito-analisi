What are the foci of the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{5} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$?

- $(\pm 2, 0)$
- $(0, \pm\sqrt{14})$
- $(0, \pm 2)$
- $(0, \pm 4)$

## Explanation

From the equation we read $a^2 = 5$ and $b^2 = 9$, therefore $a = \sqrt{5}$ and $b = 3$. Since $b > a$, the major semi-axis lies on the $y$-axis and the foci are the points $(0, \pm c)$ with $c^2 = b^2 - a^2$. We have $c^2 = 9 - 5 = 4$, so $c = 2$. Therefore the foci are $(0, \pm 2)$.
