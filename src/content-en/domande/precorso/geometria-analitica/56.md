The line $y = mx + 6$, with $m < 0$, forms with the coordinate axes a triangle of area $9$. What is the value of $m$?

- $-2$
- $-4$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The line meets the $y$-axis at $(0, 6)$ and the $x$-axis at the point where $mx + 6 = 0$, that is, at $\left(-\dfrac{6}{m}, 0\right)$, which has a positive $x$-coordinate since $m < 0$. The triangle is right-angled at the origin and has legs of length $6$ and $-\dfrac{6}{m}$, so its area is
$$
\frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot \left(-\frac{6}{m}\right) = -\frac{18}{m} .
$$
Setting $-\dfrac{18}{m} = 9$ we obtain $m = -2$.
