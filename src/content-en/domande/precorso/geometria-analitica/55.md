What is the slope of the rising asymptote of $\dfrac{x^{2}}{49} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{25} = 1$?

- $\dfrac{25}{49}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{5}$

## Explanation

For the asymptotes, set the quadratic part equal to zero. From the equation $\dfrac{x^{2}}{49} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{25} = 0$, the positive slope is $\dfrac{5}{7}$.
