The line with equation $(2k - 1)x + (k + 1)y - 3 = 0$ passes through the point $P(2, 1)$. What is its slope?

- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Substituting the coordinates of $P$ into the equation we obtain $2(2k - 1) + (k + 1) - 3 = 0$, that is, $5k - 4 = 0$, so $k = \dfrac{4}{5}$. With this value the coefficients of $x$ and $y$ are $a = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $b = \dfrac{9}{5}$. Since $b \neq 0$, the line is not vertical and its slope is
$$
m = -\frac{a}{b} = -\frac{3}{5} \cdot \frac{5}{9} = -\frac{1}{3} .
$$
