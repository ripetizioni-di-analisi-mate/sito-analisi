Which conic is described by $x^{2} - 4 x - y^{2} + 6 y - 30 = 0$?

- Parabola
- Hyperbola
- Ellipse
- Circle

## Explanation

Completing the squares gives $\left(x - 2\right)^{2} - \left(y - 3\right)^{2} = 25$. A difference of squares with a positive right-hand side gives a hyperbola.
