Which parabola $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ passes through the points $(0, 1)$, $(1, 0)$ and $(2, 3)$?

- $y = x^2 - 2x + 1$
- $y = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$
- $y = -2x^2 + x + 1$
- $y = 3x^2 - 4x + 1$

## Explanation

Passing through $(0, 1)$ gives $c = 1$. Passing through $(1, 0)$ and $(2, 3)$ gives the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a + b + 1 = 0 \\
4a + 2b + 1 = 3
\end{cases}
$$
From the first equation we get $b = -a - 1$, and substituting into the second we obtain $4a - 2a - 2 + 1 = 3$, that is, $2a = 4$. So $a = 2$ and $b = -3$, and the parabola is $y = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$. The other three parabolas pass through $(0, 1)$ and $(1, 0)$, but not through $(2, 3)$.
