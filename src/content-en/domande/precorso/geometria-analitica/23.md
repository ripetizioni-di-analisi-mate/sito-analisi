What is the distance between the parallel lines $3x + 4y - 2 = 0$ and $3x + 4y + 8 = 0$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The two lines have the same coefficients $a = 3$ and $b = 4$, therefore they are parallel and their distance is the distance from any point of the first line to the second one. The point $P(2, -1)$ belongs to the first line, since $3 \cdot 2 + 4 \cdot (-1) - 2 = 0$. With the formula for the distance from a point to a line we obtain
$$
d = \frac{|3 \cdot 2 + 4 \cdot (-1) + 8|}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}} = \frac{10}{5} = 2 .
$$
Therefore the distance between the two lines is $2$.
