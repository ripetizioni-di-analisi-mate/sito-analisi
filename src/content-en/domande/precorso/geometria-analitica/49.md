The points $A(0, 0)$ and $B(4, 0)$ are two vertices of the equilateral triangle $ABC$, with $C(2, h)$ and $h > 0$. What is the value of $h$?

- $2$
- $2\sqrt{3}$
- $12$
- $4$

## Explanation

The side $AB$ has length $4$. The point $C$ has $x$-coordinate $2$, so $\overline{AC} = \overline{BC} = \sqrt{4 + h^2}$ for every $h$, and the triangle is equilateral if and only if $\overline{AC} = 4$. This condition is equivalent to $4 + h^2 = 16$, that is, to $h^2 = 12$. Since $h > 0$, we obtain $h = \sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3}$.
