A line is at distance $2$ from the origin. Which of the following four equations can be its equation?

- $3x + 4y - 10 = 0$
- $x + y - 2 = 0$
- $3x + 4y - 14 = 0$
- $3x + 4y - 50 = 0$

## Explanation

Substituting $x = 0$ and $y = 0$ in the point-to-line distance formula, the distance from the origin to the line $ax + by + c = 0$ turns out to be $\dfrac{|c|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$. For $3x + 4y - 10 = 0$ we have $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$, so the distance is $\dfrac{10}{5} = 2$. For the other three lines the distance is $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$, $\dfrac{14}{5}$ and $\dfrac{50}{5} = 10$. Therefore the required line is $3x + 4y - 10 = 0$.
