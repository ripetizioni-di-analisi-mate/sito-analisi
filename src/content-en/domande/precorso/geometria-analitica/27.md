The parabola $y = x^2 + bx + c$ passes through the point $(0, 3)$ and has the line $x = 2$ as its axis of symmetry. What is its equation?

- $y = x^2 + 4x + 3$
- $y = x^2 - 2x + 3$
- $y = x^2 - 4x + 3$
- $y = x^2 - 4x - 3$

## Explanation

Substituting the coordinates of the point $(0, 3)$ into the equation we obtain $3 = c$. The axis of symmetry is the line $x = x_V$ with $x_V = -\dfrac{b}{2a}$, and here $a = 1$, so we need $-\dfrac{b}{2} = 2$, that is $b = -4$. Therefore the parabola is $y = x^2 - 4x + 3$.
