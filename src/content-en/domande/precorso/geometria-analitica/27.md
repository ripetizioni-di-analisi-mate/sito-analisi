A stone thrown upward from the ground is, after $t$ seconds, at height $h = 20t - 5t^2$ meters. After how many seconds does it fall back to the ground?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The graph of $h = 20t - 5t^2$ as a function of $t$ is a parabola with downward concavity, and the stone is on the ground when $h = 0$. Factoring out $5t$ we obtain $5t(4 - t) = 0$, so $h = 0$ for $t = 0$ or for $t = 4$. The instant $t = 0$ is the launch, therefore the stone falls back to the ground after $4\,\mathrm{s}$. The instant $t = 2$, the $x$-coordinate of the vertex, is when the stone reaches its maximum height, $h = 20\,\mathrm{m}$.
