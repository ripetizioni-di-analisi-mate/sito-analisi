Which statement about the triangle with vertices $A(0, 0)$, $B(2, 4)$ and $C(4, 0)$ is true?

- It is equilateral
- It is isosceles with $\overline{AB} = \overline{BC}$
- It has a right angle at $B$
- It is isosceles with $\overline{AB} = \overline{AC}$

## Explanation

With the distance formula we compute the three sides: $\overline{AB} = \sqrt{2^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{20}$, $\overline{BC} = \sqrt{2^2 + (-4)^2} = \sqrt{20}$ and $\overline{AC} = \sqrt{4^2 + 0^2} = 4$. Therefore $\overline{AB} = \overline{BC}$ and the triangle is **isosceles** with base $AC$. Since $\overline{AC} = \sqrt{16}$ is different from $\sqrt{20}$, the triangle is not equilateral. If the angle at $B$ were a right angle, by the Pythagorean theorem we would have $\overline{AB}^2 + \overline{BC}^2 = \overline{AC}^2$; instead $20 + 20 = 40 \neq 16$, so the triangle does not have a right angle at $B$.
