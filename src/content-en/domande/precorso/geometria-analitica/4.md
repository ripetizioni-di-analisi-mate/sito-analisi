## Explanation

The line is already written in general form $ax + by + c = 0$ with $a = 4$, $b = 3$ and $c = -10$, therefore the formula for the distance from a point to a line applies directly. Substituting the coordinates of $P(1, -2)$ we obtain
$$
d(P, r) = \frac{|4 \cdot 1 + 3 \cdot (-2) - 10|}{\sqrt{4^2 + 3^2}} = \frac{|4 - 6 - 10|}{\sqrt{25}} = \frac{12}{5} .
$$
The denominator is $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = 5$ and the numerator is the absolute value of $-12$. Therefore the distance is $\dfrac{12}{5}$.
