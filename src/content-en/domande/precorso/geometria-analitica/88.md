Which line, among the following, does not meet the hyperbola $x^2 - y^2 = 1$?

- $y = 2$
- $x = 2$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x$
- $y = x$

## Explanation

Substituting $y = x$ into the equation we obtain $x^2 - x^2 = 1$, that is, $0 = 1$, which is never true. So the line $y = x$ has no points in common with the hyperbola; it is one of the two asymptotes $y = \pm\dfrac{b}{a}x$, with $a = b = 1$. The other three lines meet the hyperbola, because $y = 2$ gives $x^2 = 5$, $x = 2$ gives $y^2 = 3$ and $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x$ gives $\dfrac{3}{4}x^2 = 1$, equations that have real solutions.
