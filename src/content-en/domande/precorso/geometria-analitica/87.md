In the family of equations $x^2 + (k - 2)y^2 = 4$, which values of the parameter $k$ give a hyperbola?

- $k > 2$
- $k < 2$
- $k = 3$
- $k \neq 2$

## Explanation

The coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ are $1$ and $k - 2$, and the term $xy$ does not appear. The equation represents a hyperbola if and only if these coefficients have opposite signs, that is, if $k - 2 < 0$. Therefore $k < 2$; for example with $k = 1$ we obtain $x^2 - y^2 = 4$, that is, $\dfrac{x^2}{4} - \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$. For $k = 3$ the equation represents the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$, for $k > 2$ with $k \neq 3$ an ellipse, and for $k = 2$ the pair of lines $x = \pm 2$.
