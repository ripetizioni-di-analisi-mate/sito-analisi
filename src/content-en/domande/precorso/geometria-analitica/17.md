Which line passes through $P(2, -3)$ and meets the vertical line $x = 4$ at a right angle?

- $x = 2$
- $y = -\dfrac{1}{4}x - \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $y = -3$
- $x = -3$

## Explanation

The line $x = 4$ is vertical, so it has no slope and the condition $m_1 \, m_2 = -1$ does not apply. A line perpendicular to a vertical line is horizontal, so it has equation $y = c$. Since the required line passes through $P(2, -3)$, we have $c = -3$. Therefore the line is $y = -3$.
