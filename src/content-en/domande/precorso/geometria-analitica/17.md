What is the equation of the line through $P(-1, 4)$ parallel to the line $y = 3x - 2$?

- $y = 3x + 1$
- $y = 3x + 7$
- $y = -\dfrac{1}{3}x + \dfrac{11}{3}$
- $y = 3x - 7$

## Explanation

Two non-vertical lines are parallel if and only if they have the same slope, therefore the required line has $m = 3$. The line with slope $3$ through $P(-1, 4)$ is
$$
y - 4 = 3\,\bigl(x - (-1)\bigr) = 3\,(x + 1) .
$$
Expanding we obtain $y = 3x + 3 + 4$. Therefore the required line is $y = 3x + 7$.
