For which value of $k$ is the line $(k - 1)x + 2y + 3 = 0$ parallel to the line $y = 2x + 1$?

- $k = 5$
- $k = 2$
- $k = 3$
- $k = -3$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $y$ is $2 \neq 0$, therefore the line is not vertical and its slope is $m = -\dfrac{a}{b} = -\dfrac{k - 1}{2}$. Two non-vertical lines are parallel if and only if they have the same slope, so we need
$$
-\frac{k - 1}{2} = 2 .
$$
Multiplying both sides by $-2$ we obtain $k - 1 = -4$. Therefore $k = -3$.
