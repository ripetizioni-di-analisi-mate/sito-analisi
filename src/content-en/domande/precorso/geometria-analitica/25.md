For which values of $q$ is the line $y = x + q$ tangent to the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 8$?

- $q = \pm 2\sqrt{2}$
- $q = \pm 4$
- $q = \pm 8$
- $q = 4$

## Explanation

The circle has center $O(0, 0)$ and radius $r = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$. The line is tangent to the circle if and only if the distance from the center to the line equals the radius. In general form the line is $x - y + q = 0$, therefore the distance from $O$ to the line is $\dfrac{|q|}{\sqrt{1 + 1}} = \dfrac{|q|}{\sqrt{2}}$. Setting $\dfrac{|q|}{\sqrt{2}} = 2\sqrt{2}$ we obtain $|q| = 4$, so $q = \pm 4$.
