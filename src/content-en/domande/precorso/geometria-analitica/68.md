What is the slope of the rising asymptote of $\dfrac{x^{2}}{81} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{49} = 1$?

- $\dfrac{9}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{9}$
- $\dfrac{49}{81}$

## Explanation

For the asymptotes, set the quadratic part equal to zero. From the equation $\dfrac{x^{2}}{81} - \dfrac{y^{2}}{49} = 0$, the positive slope is $\dfrac{7}{9}$.
