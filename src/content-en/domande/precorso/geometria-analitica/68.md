The points $A(1, 1)$, $B(5, 4)$ and $C(-1, 5)$ are the vertices of a triangle. What is its area?

- $11$
- $22$
- $55$
- $\dfrac{11}{5}$

## Explanation

We take $AB$ as the base. Its length is $\overline{AB} = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5$, and the line $AB$ has slope $\dfrac{3}{4}$, so it has equation $y - 1 = \dfrac{3}{4}(x - 1)$, that is, $3x - 4y + 1 = 0$. The height relative to $AB$ is the distance from $C$ to this line:
$$
h = \frac{|3 \cdot (-1) - 4 \cdot 5 + 1|}{\sqrt{9 + 16}} = \frac{22}{5} .
$$
Therefore the area of the triangle is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot \dfrac{22}{5} = 11$.
