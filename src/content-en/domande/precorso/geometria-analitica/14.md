The point $M(4, 1)$ is the midpoint of the segment $AB$, with $A(1, -2)$. What are the coordinates of $B$?

- $B(7, 4)$
- $B\left(\dfrac{5}{2}, -\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- $B(3, 3)$
- $B(-2, -5)$

## Explanation

Let $x_B$ and $y_B$ be the coordinates of $B$. Since $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$, its coordinates are the averages of the coordinates of $A$ and $B$, therefore
$$
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{1 + x_B}{2} = 4 \\[8pt]
\dfrac{-2 + y_B}{2} = 1
\end{cases}
$$
From the first equation it follows that $1 + x_B = 8$, that is $x_B = 7$. From the second it follows that $-2 + y_B = 2$, that is $y_B = 4$. Therefore $B(7, 4)$.
