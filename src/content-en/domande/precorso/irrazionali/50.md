What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 5}\right| = 8$?

- $\left\{-3\right\}$
- $\left\{-13, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{13\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 13\right\}$

## Explanation

The absolute value $\left|x - 5\right|$ is the distance of $x$ from $5$. This distance must equal $8$, so we obtain $\left\{-3, 13\right\}$.
