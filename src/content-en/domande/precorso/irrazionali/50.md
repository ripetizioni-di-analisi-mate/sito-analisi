What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 5}\right| = 8$?

- $\left\{-3\right\}$
- $\left\{-13, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{13\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 13\right\}$

## Explanation

The square root of a square is the absolute value of $x - 5$. The distance from 5 is 8, which gives $\left\{-3, 13\right\}$.
