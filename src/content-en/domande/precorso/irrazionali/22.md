What is the domain of the expression $\dfrac{\sqrt{x + 2}}{x - 3}$?

- $x \geq -2$ and $x \neq 3$
- $x > -2$ and $x \neq 3$
- $x \geq -2$
- $x \geq 2$ and $x \neq 3$

## Explanation

The expression is defined where the root exists and the denominator is different from $0$, so the two conditions must hold together.
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2 \geq 0 \\
x - 3 \neq 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition gives $x \geq -2$, with the endpoint included because at $x = -2$ the root equals $\sqrt{0} = 0$. The second condition excludes $x = 3$. Therefore the domain consists of the values $x \geq -2$ with $x \neq 3$.
