Squaring both sides of the equation $\sqrt{x + 4} = x + 2$ gives two values of $x$. Which of the two is an extraneous solution?

- $x = 0$
- $x = -4$
- $x = -3$
- $x = -2$

## Explanation

Squaring gives $x + 4 = x^2 + 4x + 4$, that is, $x^2 + 3x = 0$, which has roots $x = 0$ and $x = -3$. The original equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = B$, so it also requires $x + 2 \geq 0$. For $x = -3$ the left-hand side equals $\sqrt{1} = 1$ and the right-hand side equals $-1$, therefore $x = -3$ is an **extraneous solution**. For $x = 0$ we have $\sqrt{4} = 2 = 0 + 2$, so $x = 0$ is the only solution. The values $x = -4$ and $x = -2$ do not even solve the squared equation.
