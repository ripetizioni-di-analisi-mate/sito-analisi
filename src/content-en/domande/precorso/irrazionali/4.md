## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = \sqrt{B}$, so it is equivalent to the system formed by the equality of the radicands and by the domain condition of the simpler radicand.
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 6 \geq 0 \\
4x - 3 = x + 6
\end{cases}
$$
Moving the terms with $x$ to the left-hand side and the constants to the right-hand side gives $3x = 9$, that is, $x = 3$. Since $3 + 6 = 9 \geq 0$, the condition is satisfied, and both radicands equal $9$. Therefore the solution is $x = 3$.
