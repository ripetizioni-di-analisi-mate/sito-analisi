What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 6} < 10$?

- $\left(-6, 94\right)$
- $\left[-6, 94\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 94\right)$
- $\left[-6, 94\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative and below the square of 10. The two conditions together give $\left[-6, 94\right)$.
