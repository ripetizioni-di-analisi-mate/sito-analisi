Where is the quotient $\dfrac{x - 2}{\sqrt{5 - x}}$ positive?

- $x < 2$
- $x > 2$
- $2 < x \leq 5$
- $2 < x < 5$

## Explanation

The quotient is defined where $5 - x > 0$, because the root requires $5 - x \geq 0$ and the denominator must be different from $0$; so the domain is $x < 5$. On the domain the denominator is positive, therefore the quotient has the sign of the numerator. The numerator is positive for $x > 2$, so the quotient is positive for $2 < x < 5$.
