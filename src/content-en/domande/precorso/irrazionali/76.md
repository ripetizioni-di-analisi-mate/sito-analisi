What is the real solution set of $\sqrt[3]{2 x + 3}$ $=-3$?

- $\left\{-15\right\}$
- $\left\{-3\right\}$
- $\left\{-12\right\}$
- $\left\{12\right\}$

## Explanation

Cubing is strictly increasing on the reals. Cubing both sides gives $2 x + 3 = -27$, hence $\left\{-15\right\}$.
