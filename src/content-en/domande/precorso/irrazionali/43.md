What are the solutions of the equation $\sqrt[3]{x^3 - 7} = x - 1$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 1 \ \vee \ x = -2$
- $x = -1 \ \vee \ x = 2$
- $x = -1$

## Explanation

The cube root is defined for every real number and can be negative, so the equation is equivalent to $x^3 - 7 = (x - 1)^3$, with no condition on the sign of the right-hand side.
$$
x^3 - 7 = x^3 - 3x^2 + 3x - 1
$$
The terms $x^3$ cancel and what remains is $3x^2 - 3x - 6 = 0$, that is, $x^2 - x - 2 = 0$, with roots $x = 2$ and $x = -1$. For $x = -1$ the right-hand side is negative, and indeed $\sqrt[3]{-8} = -2 = -1 - 1$. For $x = 2$ we have $\sqrt[3]{1} = 1 = 2 - 1$. Therefore the solutions are $x = -1$ and $x = 2$.
