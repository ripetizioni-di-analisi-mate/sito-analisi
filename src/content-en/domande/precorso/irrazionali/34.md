Which system is equivalent to the inequality $\sqrt{x - 3} < x - 5$?

- $\begin{cases} x - 3 \geq 0 \\ x - 5 > 0 \\ x - 3 < (x - 5)^2 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x - 3 \geq 0 \\ x - 3 < (x - 5)^2 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x - 3 \geq 0 \\ x - 5 > 0 \\ x - 3 < x^2 - 25 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x - 3 \geq 0 \\ x - 5 < 0 \\ x - 3 < (x - 5)^2 \end{cases}$

## Explanation

The inequality has the form $\sqrt{A} < B$, so it is equivalent to the system formed by the domain restriction $x - 3 \geq 0$, the condition $x - 5 > 0$ and the inequality between the squares. The condition $x - 5 > 0$ is necessary, because the root is nonnegative and cannot be less than a negative number or zero; therefore the system that omits it and the one with $x - 5 < 0$ are not equivalent to the inequality. The square of $x - 5$ is $x^2 - 10x + 25$, which is different from $x^2 - 25$. In the correct system the third condition becomes $x^2 - 11x + 28 > 0$, that is, $x < 4$ or $x > 7$, so together with $x > 5$ the solution is $x > 7$.
