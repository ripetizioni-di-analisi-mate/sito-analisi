In the Cartesian plane the point $P(x, 3)$ is at distance $5$ from the origin, that is, $\sqrt{x^2 + 9} = 5$. Which values can $x$ take?

- $x = -1 \ \vee \ x = 1$
- $x = 4$
- $x = -4 \ \vee \ x = 4$
- No real value

## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = B$ with $B = 5 > 0$, so it is equivalent to $A = B^2$.
$$
x^2 + 9 = 25
$$
Therefore $x^2 = 16$, which has the two solutions $x = -4$ and $x = 4$. Both are accepted, because for both of them the radicand equals $25$ and the root equals $5$. Indeed the points $(-4, 3)$ and $(4, 3)$ are both at distance $5$ from the origin.
