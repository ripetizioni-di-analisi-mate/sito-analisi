What are the solutions of the equation $\sqrt{x + 5} - \sqrt{x} = 1$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 16$
- $x = 4$
- The equation has no solutions

## Explanation

The two roots exist for $x \geq 0$. Moving $\sqrt{x}$ to the right-hand side gives $\sqrt{x + 5} = 1 + \sqrt{x}$, with both sides nonnegative, so squaring preserves the equality.
$$
x + 5 = 1 + 2\sqrt{x} + x
$$
Simplifying gives $2\sqrt{x} = 4$, that is, $\sqrt{x} = 2$, hence $x = 4$. The value satisfies the condition $x \geq 0$, and indeed $\sqrt{9} - \sqrt{4} = 3 - 2 = 1$. Therefore the solution is $x = 4$.
