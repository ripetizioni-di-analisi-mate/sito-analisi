What are the zeros of the function $f(x) = x\sqrt{x - 1}$?

- $x = -1 \ \vee \ x = 0$
- $x = 0 \ \vee \ x = 1$
- $x = 0$
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

The function is defined for $x - 1 \geq 0$, that is, for $x \geq 1$. A product equals $0$ when one of the factors equals $0$, so $x = 0$ or $\sqrt{x - 1} = 0$. The value $x = 0$ does not belong to the domain, because the radicand equals $-1$, therefore it is discarded. From $\sqrt{x - 1} = 0$ it follows that $x = 1$, so the only zero of $f$ is $x = 1$.
