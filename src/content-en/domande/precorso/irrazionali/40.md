For which inequality does the solution set coincide with the domain of the root, that is, with $x \geq 4$?

- $\sqrt{x - 4} > 0$
- $\sqrt{x - 4} < 2$
- $\sqrt{x - 4} > -2$
- $\sqrt{x - 4} > 2$

## Explanation

On the domain $x \geq 4$ the root is nonnegative, so it is always greater than $-2$; therefore the solutions of the inequality $\sqrt{x - 4} > -2$ are all the values $x \geq 4$. The inequality $\sqrt{x - 4} > 0$ excludes $x = 4$, where the root equals $0$, and has solutions $x > 4$. The inequality $\sqrt{x - 4} < 2$ is equivalent to $0 \leq x - 4 < 4$, that is, $4 \leq x < 8$. Finally, $\sqrt{x - 4} > 2$ is equivalent to $x - 4 > 4$, that is, $x > 8$.
