Which of the following equivalences holds for every real number $x$?

- $\sqrt{x - 1} < 2 \iff x - 1 < 4$
- $\sqrt[3]{x - 1} < 2 \iff 0 \leq x - 1 < 8$
- $\sqrt[3]{x - 1} < 2 \iff x - 1 < 8$
- $\sqrt{x - 1} > -2 \iff x - 1 > 4$

## Explanation

The cube root is defined for every real number and cubing preserves the order, so $\sqrt[3]{x - 1} < 2$ is equivalent to $x - 1 < 8$ with no other conditions. The second equivalence adds the condition $x - 1 \geq 0$, which excludes values such as $x = 0$, for which $\sqrt[3]{-1} = -1 < 2$. The first ignores the domain condition, because for $x = 0$ we have $x - 1 = -1 < 4$, but $\sqrt{-1}$ is not defined. The fourth squares with a negative right-hand side, and indeed for $x = 1$ the root equals $0 > -2$, while $x - 1 = 0$ is not greater than $4$.
