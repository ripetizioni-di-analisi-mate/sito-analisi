What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 6} = \sqrt{10 - x}$?

- $\left\{-2\right\}$
- $\left\{4\right\}$
- $\left\{2\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$

## Explanation

On the common domain $\left[-6, 10\right]$, the two roots are equal if the radicands are equal. Their equality gives $\left\{2\right\}$.
