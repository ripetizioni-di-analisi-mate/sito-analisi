What are the solutions of the equation $x + \sqrt{x - 1} = 7$?

- $x = 10$
- $x = 5$
- $x = 5 \ \vee \ x = 10$
- $x = -5$

## Explanation

Isolating the root gives $\sqrt{x - 1} = 7 - x$, which has the form $\sqrt{A} = B$; so the equation is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
7 - x \geq 0 \\
x - 1 = (7 - x)^2
\end{cases}
$$
Expanding $(7 - x)^2 = x^2 - 14x + 49$, the second equation becomes $x^2 - 15x + 50 = 0$, that is, $(x - 5)(x - 10) = 0$. The value $x = 10$ does not satisfy the condition $x \leq 7$ and is discarded. For $x = 5$ we have $5 + \sqrt{4} = 7$, therefore the only solution is $x = 5$.
