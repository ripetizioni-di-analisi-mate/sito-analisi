For which real $a$ does $x+\sqrt{x-1}=a$ have at least one real solution?

- $a>1$, excluding the value that makes the radicand zero.
- $a\ge1$
- $a\ge0$
- $a\le1$

## Explanation

The domain is $x\ge1$. Both terms increase and their sum is continuous, equals $1$ at the endpoint and grows without bound. Its range is therefore $[1,+\infty)$.
