What is the value of the number $\sqrt{(1 - \sqrt{2})^2}$?

- $\pm(\sqrt{2} - 1)$
- $1 - \sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{2} - 1$
- $3 - 2\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

For every real number $a$ we have $\sqrt{a^2} = |a|$, so $\sqrt{(1 - \sqrt{2})^2} = |1 - \sqrt{2}|$. Since $\sqrt{2} > 1$, the number $1 - \sqrt{2}$ is negative, therefore its absolute value is its opposite. Hence $\sqrt{(1 - \sqrt{2})^2} = \sqrt{2} - 1$, which is positive, like the square root of every positive number.
