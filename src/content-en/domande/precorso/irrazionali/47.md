What is the real solution set of $\sqrt[3]{3 x + 5}$ $=-5$?

- $\left\{-40\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{10}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{130}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{40\right\}$

## Explanation

Cubing is strictly increasing on the reals. Cubing both sides gives $3 x + 5 = -125$, hence $\left\{- \dfrac{130}{3}\right\}$.
