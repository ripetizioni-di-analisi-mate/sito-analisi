- $\dfrac{1}{2} \leq x \leq 1 \ \vee \ x \geq 5$
- $x \leq 1 \ \vee \ x \geq 5$
- $x \geq 5$
- $x > 5$

## Explanation

The inequality has the form $\sqrt{A} \leq B$, so it is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - 1 \geq 0 \\
x - 2 \geq 0 \\
2x - 1 \leq (x - 2)^2
\end{cases}
$$
The third condition becomes $x^2 - 6x + 5 \geq 0$, that is, $(x - 1)(x - 5) \geq 0$, which is satisfied for $x \leq 1$ or $x \geq 5$. The second condition requires $x \geq 2$, so of the two intervals only $x \geq 5$ remains. For these values the first condition, $x \geq \dfrac{1}{2}$, is also satisfied. The endpoint $x = 5$ is included because the inequality is non-strict, and indeed $\sqrt{9} = 3 = 5 - 2$. Therefore the solution is $x \geq 5$.
