What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 36} = x - 6$?

- $\left\{13\right\}$
- $\left\{0\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 13\right\}$
- $\left\{6\right\}$

## Explanation

We require $x\geq 6$. After squaring, we find $0$ and 13, so only the second value satisfies the condition and the result is $\left\{13\right\}$.
