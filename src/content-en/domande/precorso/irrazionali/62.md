Which of the following expressions is defined at $x = -3$?

- $\sqrt{x + 3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x + 3}}$
- $\sqrt{x^2 - 10}$
- $\sqrt{-x - 4}$

## Explanation

At $x = -3$ the radicand $x + 3$ equals $0$, and $\sqrt{0} = 0$; so $\sqrt{x + 3}$ is defined at $x = -3$. The quotient $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x + 3}}$ has denominator equal to $0$ at $x = -3$, therefore it is not defined. The radicand $x^2 - 10$ equals $9 - 10 = -1$ and the radicand $-x - 4$ equals $3 - 4 = -1$, so these two roots are not defined at $x = -3$ either.
