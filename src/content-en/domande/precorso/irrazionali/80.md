What are the real solutions of $\sqrt[3]{6 x + 10}$ $=-10$?

- $\left\{165\right\}$
- $\left\{-165\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{10}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{505}{3}\right\}$

## Explanation

The cube function is increasing on all real numbers. Cubing both sides gives $6 x + 10 = -1000$, hence $\left\{- \dfrac{505}{3}\right\}$.
