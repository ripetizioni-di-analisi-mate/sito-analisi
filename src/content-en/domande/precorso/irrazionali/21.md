What are the solutions of the equation $\sqrt{x^2 - 3x + 3} = x$?

- $x = 1$
- $x = 1 \ \vee \ x = 3$
- $x = 3$
- $x = -1$

## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = B$, so it is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x \geq 0 \\
x^2 - 3x + 3 = x^2
\end{cases}
$$
In the second equation the terms $x^2$ cancel and what remains is $-3x + 3 = 0$, that is, $x = 1$. The value satisfies the condition $x \geq 0$, and indeed $\sqrt{1 - 3 + 3} = \sqrt{1} = 1$. Therefore the solution is $x = 1$. The values $x = 1$ and $x = 3$ are obtained if the radicand is set equal to $x$ instead of $x^2$.
