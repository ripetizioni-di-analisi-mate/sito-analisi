## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = B$ with $B = 4$. Since $4 > 0$, the condition $B \geq 0$ is satisfied, so the equation is equivalent to $A = B^2$.
$$
3x + 1 = 16
$$
Therefore $3x = 15$, that is, $x = 5$. The domain condition need not be added, because the radicand equals $16 \geq 0$, and indeed $\sqrt{16} = 4$.
