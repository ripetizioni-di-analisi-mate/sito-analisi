Only positive solutions of $\sqrt{1+x^2}=a x$ are sought. Which interval gives the possible values of $a$?

- $[1,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $(0,1)$
- Every $a<0$ as well, allowing a negative square root.

## Explanation

For $x>0$ we have $a=\sqrt{1+\dfrac{1}{x^2}}>1$. Conversely, for each $a>1$ the value $x=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a^2-1}}$ is positive and satisfies the equation. The endpoint $1$ is never reached.
