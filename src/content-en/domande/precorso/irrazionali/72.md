What is the real solution set of $\sqrt[3]{5 x + 8}$ $=-8$?

- $\left\{-104\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{504}{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{16}{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{504}{5}\right\}$

## Explanation

Cubing is strictly increasing on the reals. Cubing both sides gives $5 x + 8 = -512$, hence $\left\{-104\right\}$.
