What are the solutions of the equation $\sqrt{5x + 6} = x + 2$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 1 \ \vee \ x = -2$
- $x = -1 \ \vee \ x = 2$

## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = B$, so it is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2 \geq 0 \\
5x + 6 = (x + 2)^2
\end{cases}
$$
The second equation becomes $x^2 - x - 2 = 0$, that is, $(x - 2)(x + 1) = 0$, with roots $x = 2$ and $x = -1$. Both values satisfy the condition $x \geq -2$, so neither of them is discarded. The check gives $\sqrt{1} = 1 = -1 + 2$ and $\sqrt{16} = 4 = 2 + 2$, therefore the solutions are $x = -1$ and $x = 2$.
