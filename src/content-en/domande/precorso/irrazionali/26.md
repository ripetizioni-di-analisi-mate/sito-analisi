The two solutions of $\sqrt{(x-2)^2}=\sqrt{k}$ must be 6 units apart on the real line. Which value of $k$ is required?

- $9$
- $3$
- $36$, because only $x=2+\sqrt k$ is considered and its distance from the centre is set to $6$.
- $6$

## Explanation

For $k>0$ the solutions are $2-\sqrt{k}$ and $2+\sqrt{k}$. Their distance is $2\sqrt{k}$, so $\sqrt{k}=3$ and $k=9$.
