What are the solutions of the equation $\sqrt{x^2 - 4} = \sqrt{3x}$?

- $x = 4$
- $x = -1 \ \vee \ x = 4$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 1 \ \vee \ x = -4$

## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = \sqrt{B}$, so it is equivalent to the system formed by the equality of the radicands and the domain condition of the simpler radicand.
$$
\begin{cases}
3x \geq 0 \\
x^2 - 4 = 3x
\end{cases}
$$
The second equation becomes $x^2 - 3x - 4 = 0$, that is, $(x - 4)(x + 1) = 0$, with roots $x = 4$ and $x = -1$. The condition $3x \geq 0$ is equivalent to $x \geq 0$, so the value $x = -1$ is discarded; indeed, for $x = -1$ both radicands equal $-3$. For $x = 4$ the two radicands equal $12$, therefore the only solution is $x = 4$.
