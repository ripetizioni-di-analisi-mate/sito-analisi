What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 6}\right| = 10$?

- $\left\{-4\right\}$
- $\left\{16\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, 16\right\}$
- $\left\{-16, 4\right\}$

## Explanation

The square root of a square is the absolute value of $x - 6$. The distance from 6 is 10, which gives $\left\{-4, 16\right\}$.
