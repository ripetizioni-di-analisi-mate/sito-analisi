What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 6}\right| = 10$?

- $\left\{-4\right\}$
- $\left\{16\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, 16\right\}$
- $\left\{-16, 4\right\}$

## Explanation

The absolute value $\left|x - 6\right|$ is the distance of $x$ from $6$. This distance must equal $10$, so we obtain $\left\{-4, 16\right\}$.
