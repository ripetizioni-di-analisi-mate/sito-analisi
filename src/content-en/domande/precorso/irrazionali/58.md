What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 2} \geq 3$?

- $\left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-2, 7\right]$
- $\left[1, \infty\right)$
- $\left(7, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Both sides are nonnegative on the domain, so their squares can be compared. This gives $\left[7, \infty\right)$.
