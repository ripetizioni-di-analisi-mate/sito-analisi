What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 3} \geq 5$?

- $\left[22, \infty\right)$
- $\left(22, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-3, 22\right]$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Both sides are nonnegative on the domain, so compare their squares. This gives $\left[22, \infty\right)$.
