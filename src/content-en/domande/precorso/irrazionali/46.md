What is the real domain of $\sqrt{\left(5 - x\right) \left(x + 3\right)}$?

- $\left[-5, 3\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right] \cup \left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-3, 5\right]$
- $\left(-3, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative where the two factors have the same sign or one is zero. The result is $\left[-3, 5\right]$.
