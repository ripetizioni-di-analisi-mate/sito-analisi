What are the solutions of the equation $\sqrt[3]{x^2 - 1} = 2$?

- $x = -3 \ \vee \ x = 3$
- $x = 3$
- $x = -\sqrt{5} \ \vee \ x = \sqrt{5}$
- $x = -\sqrt{7} \ \vee \ x = \sqrt{7}$

## Explanation

The cube root is defined for every real number and cubing preserves the equality, so the equation is equivalent to $x^2 - 1 = 2^3 = 8$. Therefore $x^2 = 9$, which has the two solutions $x = -3$ and $x = 3$. Neither of them is discarded, because for both the radicand equals $8$ and $\sqrt[3]{8} = 2$. The values $\pm\sqrt{5}$ are obtained by squaring instead of cubing.
