What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 2}\right| = 3$?

- $\left\{-5, 1\right\}$
- $\left\{-1, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{-1\right\}$

## Explanation

The absolute value $\left|x - 2\right|$ is the distance of $x$ from $2$. This distance must equal $3$, so we obtain $\left\{-1, 5\right\}$.
