What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 2}\right| = 3$?

- $\left\{-5, 1\right\}$
- $\left\{-1, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{-1\right\}$

## Explanation

The square root of a square is the absolute value of $x - 2$. The distance from 2 is 3, which gives $\left\{-1, 5\right\}$.
