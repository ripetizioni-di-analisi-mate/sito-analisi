What is the real domain of $\sqrt{\left(10 - x\right) \left(x + 6\right)}$?

- $\left[-6, 10\right]$
- $\left(-6, 10\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right] \cup \left[10, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-10, 6\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative where the two factors have the same sign or one is zero. The result is $\left[-6, 10\right]$.
