- $x = -1$
- $x = 2$
- $x = 3$
- The equation has no solutions

## Explanation

The two roots exist for $x \geq -2$, and for these values both sides are nonnegative, so squaring preserves the equality. The square of the right-hand side contains the double product $2 \cdot 2 \cdot \sqrt{x + 2}$.
$$
x + 10 = 4 + 4\sqrt{x + 2} + x + 2
$$
Simplifying gives $4 = 4\sqrt{x + 2}$, that is, $\sqrt{x + 2} = 1$, so $x + 2 = 1$ and $x = -1$. Since $-1 \geq -2$, the value satisfies the domain condition, and indeed $\sqrt{9} = 3 = 2 + \sqrt{1}$. Therefore the solution is $x = -1$.
