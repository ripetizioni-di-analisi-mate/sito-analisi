For which $x$ does the root $\sqrt{x^2 + 9}$ exceed $3$?

- Every real number $x$
- $x \neq 0$
- $x > 0$
- No real number

## Explanation

The radicand $x^2 + 9$ is positive for every $x$, so the root is always defined. The right-hand side $3$ is positive, therefore squaring preserves the direction and gives $x^2 + 9 > 9$, that is, $x^2 > 0$. A square is positive for every $x \neq 0$, so the solution is $x \neq 0$. At $x = 0$ we have $\sqrt{9} = 3$, which does not exceed $3$.
