For which value of $k$ is the number $x = 2$ a solution of the equation $\sqrt{x + k} = 3$?

- $k = 1$
- $k = 7$
- $k = 4$
- $k = 11$

## Explanation

Substituting $x = 2$ gives $\sqrt{2 + k} = 3$. The right-hand side is positive, so the equation is equivalent to $2 + k = 3^2 = 9$. Therefore $k = 9 - 2 = 7$, and indeed $\sqrt{2 + 7} = \sqrt{9} = 3$.
