What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 5} < 8$?

- $\left(-5, 59\right)$
- $\left[-5, 59\right)$
- $\left[-5, 59\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 59\right)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative and below the square of 8. The two conditions together give $\left[-5, 59\right)$.
