What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 5} < 8$?

- $\left(-5, 59\right)$
- $\left[-5, 59\right)$
- $\left[-5, 59\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 59\right)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative and below the square of 8. Together those conditions give $\left[-5, 59\right)$.
