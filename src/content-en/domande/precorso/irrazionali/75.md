What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 25} = x - 5$?

- $\left\{0\right\}$
- $\left\{11\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 11\right\}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side requires $x\geq 5$. Squaring produces $0$ and 11, so only the second meets the condition and the result is $\left\{11\right\}$.
