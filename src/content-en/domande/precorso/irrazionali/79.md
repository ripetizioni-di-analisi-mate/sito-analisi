What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{5 - x} > -3$?

- $\left(-\infty, -4\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right)$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$

## Explanation

On its domain the root is always nonnegative, so it is greater than the negative term -3. The solution coincides with the domain, namely $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$.
