What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{5 - x} > -3$?

- $\left(-\infty, -4\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right)$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$

## Explanation

On its domain the square root is nonnegative, hence greater than -3. The solution is the domain $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$.
