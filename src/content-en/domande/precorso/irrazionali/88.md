In the equation $\sqrt{x^2 + 2x + 1} = 3x$ the radicand is the square of a binomial. What are the solutions?

- The equation has no solutions
- $x = -\dfrac{1}{4} \ \vee \ x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The radicand is $(x + 1)^2$, so the equation becomes $|x + 1| = 3x$. The left-hand side is not negative, so the right-hand side cannot be negative either, and we get the condition $x \geq 0$. For $x \geq 0$ we have $x + 1 > 0$, therefore $|x + 1| = x + 1$ and the equation becomes $x + 1 = 3x$, that is, $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The value satisfies $x \geq 0$, and indeed $\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{4}} = \dfrac{3}{2} = 3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}$.
