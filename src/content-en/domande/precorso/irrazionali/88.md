What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 3} = \sqrt{5 - x}$?

- $\left\{4\right\}$
- $\left\{-1\right\}$
- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{2\right\}$

## Explanation

On the common domain $\left[-3, 5\right]$, the two roots are equal if the radicands are equal. Their equality gives $\left\{1\right\}$.
