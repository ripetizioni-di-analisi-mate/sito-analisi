What is the real domain of $\sqrt{\left(3 - x\right) \left(x + 2\right)}$?

- $\left(-2, 3\right)$
- $\left[-3, 2\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right] \cup \left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-2, 3\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative where the two factors have the same sign or one is zero. The result is $\left[-2, 3\right]$.
