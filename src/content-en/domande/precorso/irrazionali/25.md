What are the solutions of the equation $\sqrt{x - 5} = \sqrt{2x - 3}$?

- $x = -2$
- $x = 2$
- $x = -8$
- The equation has no solutions

## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = \sqrt{B}$, so it is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 5 \geq 0 \\
x - 5 = 2x - 3
\end{cases}
$$
Setting the radicands equal gives $x = -2$. For $x = -2$ the radicand $x - 5$ equals $-7 < 0$, so the domain condition is not satisfied and the value is discarded. Therefore the equation **has no solutions**. At $x = -2$ the second radicand also equals $-7$, and neither root is defined.
