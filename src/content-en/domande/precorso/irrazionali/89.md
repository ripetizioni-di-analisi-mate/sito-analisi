What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 9} = x - 3$?

- $\left\{3\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{0\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 7\right\}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side requires $x\geq 3$. Squaring produces $0$ and 7, so only the second meets the condition and the result is $\left\{7\right\}$.
