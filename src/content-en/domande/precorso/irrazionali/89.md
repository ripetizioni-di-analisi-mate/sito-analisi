What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 9} = x - 3$?

- $\left\{3\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{0\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 7\right\}$

## Explanation

We require $x\geq 3$. After squaring, we find $0$ and 7, so only the second value satisfies the condition and the result is $\left\{7\right\}$.
