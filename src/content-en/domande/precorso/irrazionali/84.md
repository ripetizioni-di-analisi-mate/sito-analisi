Which of the following expressions is nonnegative at every point of its domain?

- $(x - 1)\sqrt{x}$
- $x\sqrt{x + 1}$
- $\sqrt{x} - x$
- $x\sqrt{x}$

## Explanation

The expression $x\sqrt{x}$ is defined for $x \geq 0$, and on this domain both factors are nonnegative, so the product is nonnegative. The expression $x\sqrt{x + 1}$ is defined for $x \geq -1$ and is negative for $-1 < x < 0$, because the first factor is negative and the root is positive. The expression $\sqrt{x} - x$ equals $2 - 4 = -2$ for $x = 4$. Finally, $(x - 1)\sqrt{x}$ is negative for $0 < x < 1$, because the first factor is negative and the second is positive.
