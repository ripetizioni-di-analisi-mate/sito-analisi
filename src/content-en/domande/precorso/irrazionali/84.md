What is the real domain of $\sqrt{\left(8 - x\right) \left(x + 5\right)}$?

- $\left[-8, 5\right]$
- $\left(-5, 8\right)$
- $\left[-5, 8\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right] \cup \left[8, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative where the factors have the same sign or one is zero. This gives $\left[-5, 8\right]$.
