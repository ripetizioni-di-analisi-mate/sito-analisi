- $x = -9 \ \vee \ x = 4$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 9 \ \vee \ x = -4$
- $x = -6 \ \vee \ x = 1$

## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = B$ with $B = 6 > 0$, so it is equivalent to $A = B^2$.
$$
x^2 + 5x = 36
$$
The equation $x^2 + 5x - 36 = 0$ factors as $(x + 9)(x - 4) = 0$, therefore the roots are $x = -9$ and $x = 4$. For both values the radicand equals $36$, so the domain condition is satisfied and $\sqrt{36} = 6$. Therefore the solutions are $x = -9$ and $x = 4$.
