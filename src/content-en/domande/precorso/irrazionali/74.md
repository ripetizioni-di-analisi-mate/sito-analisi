What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 4} < 7$?

- $\left(-4, 45\right)$
- $\left[-4, 45\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 45\right)$
- $\left[-4, 45\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative and below the square of 7. The two conditions together give $\left[-4, 45\right)$.
