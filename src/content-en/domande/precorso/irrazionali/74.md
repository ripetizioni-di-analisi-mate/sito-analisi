What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 4} < 7$?

- $\left(-4, 45\right)$
- $\left[-4, 45\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 45\right)$
- $\left[-4, 45\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative and below the square of 7. Together those conditions give $\left[-4, 45\right)$.
