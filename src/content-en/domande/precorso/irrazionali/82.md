What is the solution set of $\sqrt{x^2 - 1} \leq 2x$?

- $x > 1$
- $x \geq 0$
- $x \geq 1$
- $x \leq -1 \ \vee \ x \geq 1$

## Explanation

The inequality has the form $\sqrt{A} \leq B$, so it is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 1 \geq 0 \\
2x \geq 0 \\
x^2 - 1 \leq 4x^2
\end{cases}
$$
The third condition becomes $3x^2 \geq -1$, which is true for every $x$. The first one is satisfied for $x \leq -1$ or $x \geq 1$, and the second requires $x \geq 0$. Therefore the solution is $x \geq 1$, with the endpoint included because at $x = 1$ the root equals $0 \leq 2$.
