What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{10 - x} > -6$?

- $\left(-\infty, 10\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 10\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -26\right]$
- $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

On its domain the root is always nonnegative, so it is greater than the negative term -6. The solution coincides with the domain, namely $\left(-\infty, 10\right]$.
