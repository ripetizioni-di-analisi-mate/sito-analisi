What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{10 - x} > -6$?

- $\left(-\infty, 10\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 10\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -26\right]$
- $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

On its domain the square root is nonnegative, hence greater than -6. The solution is the domain $\left(-\infty, 10\right]$.
