For which values of $x$ is the expression $\sqrt{x^2 - 5x + 6}$ defined?

- $2 \leq x \leq 3$
- $x < 2 \ \vee \ x > 3$
- $x \leq 2 \ \vee \ x \geq 3$
- $x \geq 3$

## Explanation

The square root is defined when the radicand is not negative, so we solve the inequality $x^2 - 5x + 6 \geq 0$. The trinomial factors as $(x - 2)(x - 3)$ and vanishes at $x = 2$ and at $x = 3$. Since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, the trinomial is nonnegative outside the interval between the roots, endpoints included. Therefore the expression is defined for $x \leq 2$ or $x \geq 3$, and at the endpoints the root equals $0$.
