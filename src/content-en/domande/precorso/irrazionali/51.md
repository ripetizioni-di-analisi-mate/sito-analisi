What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 3} < 5$?

- $\left[-3, 22\right]$
- $\left[-3, 22\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 22\right)$
- $\left(-3, 22\right)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative and below the square of 5. The two conditions together give $\left[-3, 22\right)$.
