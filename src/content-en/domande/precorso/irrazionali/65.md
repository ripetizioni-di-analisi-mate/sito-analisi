What is the real solution set of $\sqrt[3]{4 x + 7}$ $=-7$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{7}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{84\right\}$
- $\left\{-84\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{175}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

Cubing is strictly increasing on the reals. Cubing both sides gives $4 x + 7 = -343$, hence $\left\{- \dfrac{175}{2}\right\}$.
