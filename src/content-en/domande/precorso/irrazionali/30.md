What are the solutions of the inequality $\sqrt{x^2 + 1} < x$?

- $x > 0$
- $x \geq 1$
- Every real number $x$
- The inequality has no solutions

## Explanation

The inequality has the form $\sqrt{A} < B$, so it is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + 1 \geq 0 \\
x > 0 \\
x^2 + 1 < x^2
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition is satisfied for every $x$. The third reduces to $1 < 0$, which is false for every $x$, so the system has no solutions. Therefore the inequality **has no solutions**. The same result follows from a direct comparison, because for $x > 0$ we have $\sqrt{x^2 + 1} > \sqrt{x^2} = x$.
