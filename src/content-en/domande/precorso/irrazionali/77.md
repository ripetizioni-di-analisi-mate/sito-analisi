What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 5} \geq 8$?

- $\left[-5, 59\right]$
- $\left(59, \infty\right)$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[59, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Both sides are nonnegative on the domain, so their squares can be compared. This gives $\left[59, \infty\right)$.
