What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{8 - x} > -5$?

- $\left(-\infty, 8\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 8\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -17\right]$
- $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

On its domain the square root is nonnegative, hence greater than -5. The solution is the domain $\left(-\infty, 8\right]$.
