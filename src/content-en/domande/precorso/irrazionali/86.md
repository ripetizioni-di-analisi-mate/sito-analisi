What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{8 - x} > -5$?

- $\left(-\infty, 8\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 8\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -17\right]$
- $\mathbb{R}$

## Explanation

On its domain the root is always nonnegative, so it is greater than the negative term -5. The solution coincides with the domain, namely $\left(-\infty, 8\right]$.
