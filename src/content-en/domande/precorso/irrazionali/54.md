What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 4} = \sqrt{7 - x}$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{11}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{3\right\}$

## Explanation

On the common domain $\left[-4, 7\right]$, the roots agree exactly when the radicands agree. Their equality gives $\left\{\dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$.
