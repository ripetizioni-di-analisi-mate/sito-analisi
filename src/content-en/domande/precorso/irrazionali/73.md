What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 2} = \sqrt{3 - x}$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{5}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{1\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

On the common domain $\left[-2, 3\right]$, the roots agree exactly when the radicands agree. Their equality gives $\left\{\dfrac{1}{2}\right\}$.
