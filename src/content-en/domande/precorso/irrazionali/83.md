The revenue of a small production is $R(x) = 10\sqrt{x}$ and the cost is $C(x) = x + 9$, where $x \geq 0$ is the quantity produced. For which $x$ does the revenue exceed the cost?

- $1 \leq x \leq 81$
- $1 < x < 81$
- $0 \leq x < 1 \ \vee \ x > 81$
- $x > 1$

## Explanation

We solve the inequality $10\sqrt{x} > x + 9$ for $x \geq 0$. For these values the right-hand side is positive, so only the system with a nonnegative right-hand side matters, and in it squaring preserves the direction.
$$
100x > (x + 9)^2 = x^2 + 18x + 81
$$
The inequality becomes $x^2 - 82x + 81 < 0$, that is, $(x - 1)(x - 81) < 0$, which is satisfied for $1 < x < 81$. At $x = 1$ and at $x = 81$ the revenue and the cost are equal, therefore the endpoints are excluded. Hence the revenue exceeds the cost for $1 < x < 81$.
