What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 6} \geq 10$?

- $\left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-6, 94\right]$
- $\left[94, \infty\right)$
- $\left(94, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Both sides are nonnegative on the domain, so their squares can be compared. This gives $\left[94, \infty\right)$.
