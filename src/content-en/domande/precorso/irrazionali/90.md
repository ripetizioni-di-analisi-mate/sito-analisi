What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{7 - x} > -4$?

- $\left(-\infty, 7\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -9\right]$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right)$

## Explanation

On its domain the square root is nonnegative, hence greater than -4. The solution is the domain $\left(-\infty, 7\right]$.
