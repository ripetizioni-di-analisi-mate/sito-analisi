What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{7 - x} > -4$?

- $\left(-\infty, 7\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -9\right]$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right)$

## Explanation

On its domain the root is always nonnegative, so it is greater than the negative term -4. The solution coincides with the domain, namely $\left(-\infty, 7\right]$.
