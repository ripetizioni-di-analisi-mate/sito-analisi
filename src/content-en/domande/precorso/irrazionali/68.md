What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 3}\right| = 5$?

- $\left\{-2\right\}$
- $\left\{-2, 8\right\}$
- $\left\{-8, 2\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$

## Explanation

The absolute value $\left|x - 3\right|$ is the distance of $x$ from $3$. This distance must equal $5$, so we obtain $\left\{-2, 8\right\}$.
