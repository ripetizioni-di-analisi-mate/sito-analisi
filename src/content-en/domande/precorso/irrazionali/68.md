Studying the sign of the product $(x - 4)\sqrt{x + 1}$, for which $x$ do we have $(x - 4)\sqrt{x + 1} \geq 0$?

- $x \geq 4$
- $x = -1 \ \vee \ x \geq 4$
- $x \geq -1$
- $-1 \leq x \leq 4$

## Explanation

The product is defined for $x \geq -1$. At $x = -1$ the root equals $0$, so the product equals $0$ and the inequality is satisfied. For $x > -1$ the root is positive, therefore the product has the sign of $x - 4$ and is nonnegative for $x \geq 4$. Hence the solution is $x = -1$ or $x \geq 4$.
