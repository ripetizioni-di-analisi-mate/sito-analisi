What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 3}\right| = 5$?

- $\left\{-2\right\}$
- $\left\{-2, 8\right\}$
- $\left\{-8, 2\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$

## Explanation

The square root of a square is the absolute value of $x - 3$. The distance from 3 must equal 5, so we obtain $\left\{-2, 8\right\}$.
