What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 2} < 3$?

- $\left[-2, 7\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right)$
- $\left[-2, 7\right)$
- $\left(-2, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative and below the square of 3. Together those conditions give $\left[-2, 7\right)$.
