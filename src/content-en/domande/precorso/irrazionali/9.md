- $x > 8$
- $x \geq 8$
- $x > 2$
- $-1 \leq x < 8$

## Explanation

The inequality has the form $\sqrt{A} > B$ with $B = 3 \geq 0$, so only the second system remains, in which both sides are nonnegative and squaring preserves the direction.
$$
x + 1 > 9
$$
Therefore $x > 8$. The domain condition $x + 1 \geq 0$ need not be added, because it follows from $x + 1 > 9$. The endpoint $x = 8$ is excluded, because at that point the root equals $3$ and the inequality is strict.
