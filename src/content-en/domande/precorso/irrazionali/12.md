- $x \geq 4$
- $x \leq 0 \ \vee \ x \geq 3$
- $x \leq 0 \ \vee \ x \geq 4$
- $x < 2 \ \vee \ x \geq 4$

## Explanation

The inequality has the form $\sqrt{A} \geq B$, so it is equivalent to the union of two systems.
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 2 < 0 \\
x^2 - 3x \geq 0
\end{cases}
\ \cup \
\begin{cases}
x - 2 \geq 0 \\
x^2 - 3x \geq (x - 2)^2
\end{cases}
$$
The condition $x^2 - 3x \geq 0$ is satisfied for $x \leq 0$ or $x \geq 3$, so together with $x < 2$ the first system gives $x \leq 0$. In the second, expanding $(x - 2)^2 = x^2 - 4x + 4$, the inequality becomes $x - 4 \geq 0$, that is, $x \geq 4$, which also satisfies $x \geq 2$. The **union** of the two systems gives $x \leq 0$ or $x \geq 4$.
