What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 4} = x - 2$?

- $\left\{2\right\}$
- $\left\{0\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 5\right\}$

## Explanation

We require $x\geq 2$. After squaring, we find $0$ and 5, so only the second value satisfies the condition and the result is $\left\{5\right\}$.
