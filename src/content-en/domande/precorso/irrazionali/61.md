What is the real domain of $\sqrt{\left(7 - x\right) \left(x + 4\right)}$?

- $\left(-\infty, -4\right] \cup \left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-4, 7\right]$
- $\left(-4, 7\right)$
- $\left[-7, 4\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand is nonnegative where the factors have the same sign or one is zero. This gives $\left[-4, 7\right]$.
