On which set is the inequality $\sqrt{x + 1} > \sqrt{x}$ true?

- $x \geq 0$
- $x \geq -1$
- $x > 0$
- No real number

## Explanation

The two roots are both defined for $x \geq 0$. For these values both sides are nonnegative, so squaring preserves the direction and the inequality is equivalent to $x + 1 > x$, that is, $1 > 0$, which is true for every $x$. Therefore the inequality holds for every $x \geq 0$; at $x = 0$ we have $\sqrt{1} = 1 > 0$.
