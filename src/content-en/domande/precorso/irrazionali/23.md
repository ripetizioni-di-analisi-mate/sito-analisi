The vertex of $y=(x-3)^2$ lies on the horizontal axis. Intersections with $y=4x$ are used to solve $2\sqrt{x}=x-3$. Which part of the parabola must be retained?

- The branch where $x\le3$.
- Both branches where $x\ge0$.
- Only the vertex.
- The branch where $x\ge3$.

## Explanation

The left side of the original equation is nonnegative, so $x-3$ must also be nonnegative. Squaring discards this information. Keep the right branch, including its vertex, before finding intersections.
