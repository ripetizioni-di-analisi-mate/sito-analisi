What are the solutions of the equation $2\sqrt{x} = x - 3$?

- $x = 1 \ \vee \ x = 9$
- $x = 1$
- $x = 3$
- $x = 9$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is nonnegative, so the right-hand side must be nonnegative too, and the equation is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 3 \geq 0 \\
4x = (x - 3)^2
\end{cases}
$$
The square of the left-hand side is $4x$, because both the coefficient $2$ and the root are squared. The second equation becomes $x^2 - 10x + 9 = 0$, that is, $(x - 1)(x - 9) = 0$, with roots $x = 1$ and $x = 9$. For $x = 1$ the right-hand side equals $-2 < 0$, so the value is discarded, while for $x = 9$ we have $2\sqrt{9} = 6 = 9 - 3$. Therefore the only solution is $x = 9$.
