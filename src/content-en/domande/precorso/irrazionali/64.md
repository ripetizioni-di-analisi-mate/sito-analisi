What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 16} = x - 4$?

- $\left\{9\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 9\right\}$
- $\left\{0\right\}$
- $\left\{4\right\}$

## Explanation

We require $x\geq 4$. After squaring, we find $0$ and 9, so only the second value satisfies the condition and the result is $\left\{9\right\}$.
