What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 16} = x - 4$?

- $\left\{9\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 9\right\}$
- $\left\{0\right\}$
- $\left\{4\right\}$

## Explanation

The right-hand side requires $x\geq 4$. Squaring produces $0$ and 9, so only the second meets the condition and the result is $\left\{9\right\}$.
