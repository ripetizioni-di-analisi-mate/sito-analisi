What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{3 - x} > -2$?

- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$

## Explanation

On its domain the square root is nonnegative, hence greater than -2. The solution is the domain $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$.
