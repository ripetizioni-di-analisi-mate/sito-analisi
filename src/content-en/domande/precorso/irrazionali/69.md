What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{3 - x} > -2$?

- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\left(-\infty, -1\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$

## Explanation

On its domain the root is always nonnegative, so it is greater than the negative term -2. The solution coincides with the domain, namely $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$.
