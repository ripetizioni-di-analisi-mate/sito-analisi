What are the real solutions of $\sqrt{x + 5} = \sqrt{8 - x}$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{3\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{13}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

On the common domain $\left[-5, 8\right]$, the two roots are equal if the radicands are equal. Their equality gives $\left\{\dfrac{3}{2}\right\}$.
