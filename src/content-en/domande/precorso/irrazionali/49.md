What does one obtain by solving the equation $\sqrt{x} = \sqrt{x - 4}$?

- Every $x \geq 4$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 2$
- The equation has no solutions

## Explanation

The equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = \sqrt{B}$, so it is equivalent to the system formed by $x - 4 \geq 0$ and by the equality of the radicands $x = x - 4$. The equality reduces to $0 = -4$, which is false for every $x$. Therefore the equation **has no solutions**. Indeed, on the domain $x \geq 4$, the radicand $x$ exceeds the radicand $x - 4$ by $4$, so $\sqrt{x} > \sqrt{x - 4}$.
