What is the real solution set of $\sqrt{x + 4} \geq 7$?

- $\left(45, \infty\right)$
- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-4, 45\right]$
- $\left[45, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Both sides are nonnegative on the domain, so compare their squares. This gives $\left[45, \infty\right)$.
