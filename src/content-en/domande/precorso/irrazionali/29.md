What is the solution of the equation $\sqrt{x + 3} = 2\sqrt{x}$?

- $x = 1$
- $x = 3$
- $x = -1$
- The equation has no solutions

## Explanation

For $x \geq 0$ the right-hand side can be written as $2\sqrt{x} = \sqrt{4x}$, so the equation has the form $\sqrt{A} = \sqrt{B}$ and is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x \geq 0 \\
x + 3 = 4x
\end{cases}
$$
The second equation gives $3x = 3$, that is, $x = 1$, which satisfies $x \geq 0$. Indeed $\sqrt{4} = 2 = 2\sqrt{1}$, therefore the solution is $x = 1$.
