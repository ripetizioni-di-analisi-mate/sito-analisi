What is the real solution set of $\left|{x - 4}\right| = 7$?

- $\left\{11\right\}$
- $\left\{-11, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 11\right\}$

## Explanation

The square root of a square is the absolute value of $x - 4$. The distance from 4 is 7, which gives $\left\{-3, 11\right\}$.
