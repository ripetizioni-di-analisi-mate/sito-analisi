What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 4}\right| = 7$?

- $\left\{11\right\}$
- $\left\{-11, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 11\right\}$

## Explanation

The square root of a square is the absolute value of $x - 4$. The distance from 4 must equal 7, so we obtain $\left\{-3, 11\right\}$.
