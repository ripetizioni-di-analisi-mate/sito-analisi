What are the real solutions of $\left|{x - 4}\right| = 7$?

- $\left\{11\right\}$
- $\left\{-11, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 11\right\}$

## Explanation

The absolute value $\left|x - 4\right|$ is the distance of $x$ from $4$. This distance must equal $7$, so we obtain $\left\{-3, 11\right\}$.
