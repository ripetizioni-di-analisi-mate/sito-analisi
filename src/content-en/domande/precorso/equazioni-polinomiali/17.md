What is the solution of the equation $\dfrac{x - 1}{2} - \dfrac{x + 1}{3} = 1$?

- $x = 7$
- $x = 6$
- $x = 11$
- $x = -11$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of the denominators is $6$. Multiplying both sides by $6$, by the **equivalence principles** we obtain the equivalent equation $3\,(x - 1) - 2\,(x + 1) = 6$. The minus sign in front of the second fraction applies to both terms of $x + 1$, so the expansion is $3x - 3 - 2x - 2 = 6$, that is, $x - 5 = 6$. Therefore the only solution is $x = 11$.
