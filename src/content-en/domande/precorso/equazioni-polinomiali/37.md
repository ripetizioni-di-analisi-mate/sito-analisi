What are the solutions of the inequality $(x - 2)^2 (x + 1) \geq 0$?

- $x \geq -1$ and $x \neq 2$
- $x \geq 2$
- $-1 \leq x \leq 2$
- $x \geq -1$

## Explanation

By the **sign of a factor raised to a power**, the factor $(x - 2)^2$ has an even exponent, so it is positive for $x \neq 2$ and zero for $x = 2$. For $x \neq 2$ the product therefore has the sign of $x + 1$, which is positive or zero for $x \geq -1$. The value $x = 2$ makes the product zero, so it satisfies the direction $\geq$ and belongs to the solutions. Therefore the solutions are $x \geq -1$.
