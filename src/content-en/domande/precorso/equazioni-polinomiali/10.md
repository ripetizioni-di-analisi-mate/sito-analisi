- $x \geq 2$
- $x \leq 2$
- $x < 2$
- $x \leq 3$

## Explanation

Expanding the left-hand side gives $2 - 2x \geq x - 4$. Subtracting $x$ and $2$ from both sides gives $-3x \geq -6$, with the same direction. Dividing by $-3$, which is a negative number, the direction is **reversed**, so $x \leq 2$. The endpoint $2$ belongs to the solutions, because for $x = 2$ both sides equal $-2$.
