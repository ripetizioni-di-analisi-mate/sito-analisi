Today a father is $40$ years old and his son is $12$. In how many years will the father's age be twice the son's age?

- In $14$ years
- In $16$ years
- Never, because the equation has no solutions
- In $28$ years

## Explanation

Letting $x$ be the number of years, in $x$ years the father will be $40 + x$ years old and the son $12 + x$. The condition is the equation $40 + x = 2\,(12 + x)$. The factor $2$ multiplies both terms of $12 + x$, so the right-hand side is $24 + 2x$. Moving $x$ to the right-hand side and $24$ to the left-hand side, with the sign changed, gives $40 - 24 = 2x - x$, that is, $x = 16$. Indeed in $16$ years the father will be $56$ and the son $28$, which is half of $56$.
