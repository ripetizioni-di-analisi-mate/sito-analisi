What are the real solutions of $11 - 6 x \geq 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{11}{6}\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{11}{6}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, - \dfrac{11}{6}\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{11}{6}\right]$

## Explanation

Isolating the term in $x$ gives $- 6 x \geq -11$. Dividing by the negative coefficient reverses the inequality, giving $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{11}{6}\right]$.
