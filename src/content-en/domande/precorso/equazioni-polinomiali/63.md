What are the real solutions of $x \left(x - 7\right) \left(x + 4\right) = 0$?

- $\left\{-7, 0, 4\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, 0, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, 7\right\}$

## Explanation

The factor $x$ gives the solution $0$. Setting the other two factors equal to zero gives $\left\{-4, 0, 7\right\}$.
