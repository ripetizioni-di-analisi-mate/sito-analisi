Two quadratic inequalities form the following system. Which real numbers satisfy both?
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - x - 6 < 0 \\[2pt]
x^2 - 4x > 0
\end{cases}
$$

- $0 < x < 3$
- $-2 \leq x \leq 0$
- No real number
- $-2 < x < 0$

## Explanation

The trinomial $x^2 - x - 6$ has roots $-2$ and $3$, because their sum is $1$ and their product is $-6$. Since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, the trinomial is negative between the roots, so the first inequality has solutions $-2 < x < 3$. Factoring out $x$, the second inequality becomes $x\,(x - 4) > 0$, and the trinomial $x^2 - 4x$, with roots $0$ and $4$, is positive outside the interval of the roots, so its solutions are $x < 0$ or $x > 4$. The solutions of the system are the **intersection** of the two sets. Since $4 > 3$, the half-line $x > 4$ has no points in common with $-2 < x < 3$, so the solutions of the system are $-2 < x < 0$.
