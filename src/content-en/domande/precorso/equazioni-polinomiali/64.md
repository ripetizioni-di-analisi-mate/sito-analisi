What is the real solution set of $\dfrac{x + 6}{x - 11} \leq 0$?

- $\left[-6, 11\right]$
- $\left(-6, 11\right]$
- $\left[-6, 11\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right] \cup \left(11, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The numerator vanishes at -6 and the denominator at 11. The fraction is negative between them and zero at -6, so the result is $\left[-6, 11\right)$.
