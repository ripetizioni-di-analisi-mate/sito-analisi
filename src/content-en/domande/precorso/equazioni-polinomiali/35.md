What are the solutions of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x - 1}{x + 2} \geq 0 \\[8pt]
x^2 < 9
\end{cases}
$$

- $-3 < x \leq -2$ or $1 \leq x < 3$
- $-3 < x < -2$ or $1 \leq x < 3$
- $1 \leq x < 3$
- $-3 < x < -2$ or $1 < x < 3$

## Explanation

In the first inequality the domain restriction is $x \neq -2$. The numerator is positive for $x > 1$ and the denominator for $x > -2$, so the quotient is positive for $x < -2$ and for $x > 1$. The inequality sign $\geq$ includes the zero $1$ of the numerator and excludes $-2$, therefore the first inequality has solutions $x < -2$ or $x \geq 1$. The second inequality is equivalent to $x^2 - 9 < 0$; the trinomial $x^2 - 9$ has roots $\pm 3$ and is negative between the roots, so its solutions are $-3 < x < 3$. The solutions of the system are the **intersection** of the two sets, that is, $-3 < x < -2$ or $1 \leq x < 3$.
