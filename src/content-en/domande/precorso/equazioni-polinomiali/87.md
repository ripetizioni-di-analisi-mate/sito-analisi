For which values of $k$ is the trinomial $x^2 + kx + 4$ positive for every real $x$?

- $k < -4$ or $k > 4$
- $-4 \leq k \leq 4$
- $k < 4$
- $-4 < k < 4$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, so by the **sign of the quadratic trinomial** the trinomial is positive for every $x$ if and only if the discriminant is negative. The discriminant is $\Delta = k^2 - 16$, and the condition $k^2 - 16 < 0$ is satisfied for $-4 < k < 4$. For $k = 4$ and for $k = -4$ we have $\Delta = 0$ and the trinomial becomes $(x + 2)^2$ or $(x - 2)^2$, which vanishes at one point. Therefore the required values are $-4 < k < 4$.
