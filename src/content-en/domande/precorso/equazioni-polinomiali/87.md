What is the real solution set of $x \left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 2\right) < 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, -2\right) \cup \left(0, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right] \cup \left[0, 4\right]$
- $\left(-2, 4\right)$
- $\left(-2, 0\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The zeros are $\left\{-2, 0, 4\right\}$, and one factor changes sign at each zero. The product is positive to the right of 4, so the negative intervals give $\left(-\infty, -2\right) \cup \left(0, 4\right)$.
