Which equation has the solutions $x = -2$ and $x = 5$?

- $x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0$
- $x^2 - 3x + 10 = 0$
- $x^2 - 7x + 10 = 0$
- $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **sum and product of the roots**, the equation $x^2 + bx + c = 0$ with roots $x_1$ and $x_2$ has $b = -(x_1 + x_2)$ and $c = x_1 \cdot x_2$. Here the sum is $-2 + 5 = 3$ and the product is $(-2) \cdot 5 = -10$, so $b = -3$ and $c = -10$. Therefore the required equation is $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$, which can also be written $(x + 2)(x - 5) = 0$. The equation $x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0$ instead has the roots $2$ and $-5$.
