The trinomial $2x^2 - x - 3$ has leading coefficient $2$. Which product of two linear factors is equal to the trinomial?

- $\left(x - \dfrac{3}{2}\right)(x + 1)$
- $(2x + 3)(x - 1)$
- $(2x - 1)(x + 3)$
- $(2x - 3)(x + 1)$

## Explanation

The discriminant of $2x^2 - x - 3$ is $\Delta = 1 + 24 = 25$, so the roots are $x = \dfrac{1 \pm 5}{4}$, that is, $x_1 = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and $x_2 = -1$. By the factorization $ax^2 + bx + c = a\,(x - x_1)(x - x_2)$ we have
$$
2x^2 - x - 3 = 2\left(x - \frac{3}{2}\right)(x + 1) = (2x - 3)(x + 1) .
$$
The product $\left(x - \dfrac{3}{2}\right)(x + 1)$ equals $x^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}x - \dfrac{3}{2}$, so without the factor $a = 2$ the factorization is not correct.
