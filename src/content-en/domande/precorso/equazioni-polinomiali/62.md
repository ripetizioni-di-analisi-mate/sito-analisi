What is the real solution set of $6 x - 66 = 11 x$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{66}{17}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{66}{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{66}{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{66\right\}$

## Explanation

Expand and collect terms in $x$ to get $- 5 x = 66$. Divide by -5 to obtain $\left\{- \dfrac{66}{5}\right\}$.
