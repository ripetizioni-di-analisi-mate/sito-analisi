In the sign chart of the product $(x + 2)(x - 1)(x - 3)$ the columns are, in order, $x < -2$, $-2 < x < 1$, $1 < x < 3$, $x > 3$. Which sequence of signs appears in the row of the product?

- $+$, $-$, $+$, $-$
- $-$, $-$, $-$, $+$
- $-$, $+$, $-$, $+$
- $+$, $+$, $-$, $+$

## Explanation

Every linear factor with a positive coefficient of $x$ is negative before its zero and positive after it. For $x < -2$ all three factors are negative, so the product is negative. Past each of the three zeros exactly one factor changes sign, so the product changes sign at every zero. The row of the product is therefore $-$, $+$, $-$, $+$.
