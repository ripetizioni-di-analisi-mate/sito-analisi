What are the real solutions of $6 x - 66 = 11 x$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{66}{17}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{66}{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{66}{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{66\right\}$

## Explanation

Expanding and collecting the terms in $x$ gives $- 5 x = 66$. After dividing by -5, we obtain the solution set $\left\{- \dfrac{66}{5}\right\}$.
