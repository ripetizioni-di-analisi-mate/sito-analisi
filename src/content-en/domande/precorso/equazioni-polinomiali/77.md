What are the real solutions of $x^{2} - 2 x - 8 = 0$?

- $\left\{-4, 2\right\}$
- $\left\{-2, 4\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, -2\right\}$
- $\left\{2, 4\right\}$

## Explanation

The polynomial factors as $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 2\right)$. The product is zero if at least one factor vanishes, so the solution set is $\left\{-2, 4\right\}$.
