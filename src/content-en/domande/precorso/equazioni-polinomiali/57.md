Which of the following numbers is a root of the polynomial $P(x) = x^3 - 2x^2 - 5x + 6$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The integer roots of a polynomial with integer coefficients and leading coefficient $1$ are divisors of the constant term, here $6$, and all the proposed numbers are divisors of $6$. Substituting gives $P(3) = 27 - 18 - 15 + 6 = 0$, so $3$ is a root and the binomial $x - 3$ divides $P$. On the other hand $P(2) = -4$, $P(-1) = 8$ and $P(-3) = -24$ are nonzero, so $2$, $-1$ and $-3$ are not roots.
