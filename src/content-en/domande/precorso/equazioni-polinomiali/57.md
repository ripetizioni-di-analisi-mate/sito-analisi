What is the real solution set of $x \left(x - 11\right) \left(x + 6\right) < 0$?

- $\left(-6, 0\right) \cup \left(11, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-6, 11\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right] \cup \left[0, 11\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right) \cup \left(0, 11\right)$

## Explanation

The zeros are $\left\{-6, 0, 11\right\}$, and one factor changes sign at each zero. The product is positive to the right of 11, so the negative intervals give $\left(-\infty, -6\right) \cup \left(0, 11\right)$.
