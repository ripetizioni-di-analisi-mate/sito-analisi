What is the real solution set of $\dfrac{x + 4}{x - 7} \leq 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, -4\right] \cup \left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-4, 7\right]$
- $\left[-4, 7\right)$
- $\left[-4, 7\right]$

## Explanation

The numerator vanishes at -4 and the denominator at 7. The fraction is negative between them and zero at -4, so the result is $\left[-4, 7\right)$.
