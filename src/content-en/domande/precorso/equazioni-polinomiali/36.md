A rectangle has perimeter $20$ cm and one side of length $x$ cm, with $0 < x < 10$. For which values of $x$ is the area of the rectangle greater than $21\ \mathrm{cm}^2$?

- $3 < x < 7$
- $0 < x < 3$ or $7 < x < 10$
- $3 \leq x \leq 7$
- $0 < x < 7$

## Explanation

The sum of two adjacent sides is half the perimeter, so the other side measures $10 - x$ cm. The area is greater than $21\ \mathrm{cm}^2$ when $x\,(10 - x) > 21$, that is, $-x^2 + 10x - 21 > 0$. Multiplying by $-1$ and reversing the direction gives $x^2 - 10x + 21 < 0$. The trinomial has roots $3$ and $7$, because $3 + 7 = 10$ and $3 \cdot 7 = 21$, and since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive it is negative between the roots. Therefore the area is greater than $21\ \mathrm{cm}^2$ for $3 < x < 7$, values that all lie between $0$ and $10$.
