What are the solutions of the inequality $3x^2 - 5x - 2 \leq 0$?

- $x \leq -\dfrac{1}{3}$ or $x \geq 2$
- $-2 \leq x \leq \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3} \leq x \leq 2$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3} < x < 2$

## Explanation

The trinomial has discriminant $\Delta = 25 + 24 = 49 > 0$ and roots $x = \dfrac{5 \pm 7}{6}$, that is, $2$ and $-\dfrac{1}{3}$. Since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, by the **sign of the quadratic trinomial** the trinomial is negative between the roots and zero at the roots. The direction $\leq$ includes the roots, so the solutions are $-\dfrac{1}{3} \leq x \leq 2$.
