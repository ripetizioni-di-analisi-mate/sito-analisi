For which values of the parameter $k$ does the equation $x^2 + kx + 9 = 0$ have a double real solution?

- $k = 6$
- $k = \pm 6$
- $k = \pm 3$
- For no value of $k$

## Explanation

A quadratic equation has a **double real solution** when the discriminant is zero. Here $a = 1$, $b = k$, $c = 9$, so $\Delta = k^2 - 36$. The condition $k^2 - 36 = 0$ is equivalent to $k^2 = 36$, therefore $k = 6$ or $k = -6$. For $k = 6$ the trinomial is $(x + 3)^2$, for $k = -6$ it is $(x - 3)^2$.
