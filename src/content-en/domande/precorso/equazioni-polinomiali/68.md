What is the real solution set of $x \left(x - 9\right) \left(x + 5\right) = 0$?

- $\left\{0, 9\right\}$
- $\left\{-9, 0, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, 0, 9\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, 9\right\}$

## Explanation

The factor $x$ gives the solution $0$. Setting the other two factors equal to zero gives $\left\{-5, 0, 9\right\}$.
