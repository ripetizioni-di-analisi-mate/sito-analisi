Given that $x^2 - 2x - 8 = (x + 2)(x - 4)$, for which $x$ do we have $x^2 - 2x - 8 > 0$?

- $-2 < x < 4$
- $x \leq -2$ or $x \geq 4$
- $x < -2$ or $x > 4$
- $x < -4$ or $x > 2$

## Explanation

Since $x^2 - 2x - 8 = (x + 2)(x - 4)$, the roots of the trinomial are $-2$ and $4$. The coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, so by the **sign of the quadratic trinomial** the trinomial is positive outside the interval of the roots and zero at the roots. The direction $>$ excludes the roots, so the solutions are $x < -2$ or $x > 4$.
