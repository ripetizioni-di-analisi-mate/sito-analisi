- $x = -2$
- $x = 0$
- $x = 1$
- $x = 2$

## Explanation

Expanding the products gives $3x - 6 + 5 = 2x + 2 - 1$, that is, $3x - 1 = 2x + 1$. Subtracting $2x$ and adding $1$ to both sides, by the **equivalence principles** we obtain the equivalent equation $x = 2$. Therefore the equation has the unique solution $x = 2$, and indeed for $x = 2$ both sides equal $5$.
