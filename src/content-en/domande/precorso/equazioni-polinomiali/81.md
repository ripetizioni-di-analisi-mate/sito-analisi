Which values satisfy both $x^{2} \leq 81$ and $x > 4$?

- $\left(4, 9\right)$
- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left[4, 9\right]$
- $\left(4, 9\right]$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $\left[-9, 9\right]$ and the second keeps values above its endpoint. Their intersection is $\left(4, 9\right]$.
