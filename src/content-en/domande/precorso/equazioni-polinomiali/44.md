What are the solutions of the inequality $\dfrac{x^2 - x - 6}{x - 1} \leq 0$?

- $-2 \leq x < 1$ or $x \geq 3$
- $x \leq -2$ or $1 \leq x \leq 3$
- $x \leq -2$ or $1 < x \leq 3$
- $-2 \leq x \leq 3$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x \neq 1$. The numerator factors as $x^2 - x - 6 = (x - 3)(x + 2)$, so the zeros of the factors are $-2$, $1$ and $3$, and each first-degree factor is negative before its own zero and positive after it. From the **sign chart** the quotient is negative for $x < -2$ and for $1 < x < 3$, positive for $-2 < x < 1$ and for $x > 3$. The direction $\leq$ includes the zeros $-2$ and $3$ of the numerator, while $1$ is excluded by the domain condition, so the solutions are $x \leq -2$ or $1 < x \leq 3$.
