What are the solutions of the inequality $3\,(x - 1) - 3x > -2$?

- Every real $x$
- $x > 0$
- $x < 0$
- No real $x$

## Explanation

The factor $3$ multiplies both terms of $x - 1$, so expanding the product we obtain $3x - 3 - 3x > -2$, that is, $-3 > -2$. The terms in $x$ cancel, therefore the inequality does not depend on $x$. Since $-3 > -2$ is false, no real number satisfies the inequality, which therefore **has no solutions**.
