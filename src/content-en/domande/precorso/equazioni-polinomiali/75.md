What is the real solution set of $x \left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 3\right) = 0$?

- $\left\{-3, 0, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{-3, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{0, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, 0, 3\right\}$

## Explanation

The factor $x$ gives the solution $0$. Setting the other two factors equal to zero gives $\left\{-3, 0, 5\right\}$.
