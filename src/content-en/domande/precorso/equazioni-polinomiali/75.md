Which values of $x$ make the product $(2 - x)(x + 1)(x + 4)$ positive?

- $-4 < x < -1$ or $x > 2$
- $x \leq -4$ or $-1 \leq x \leq 2$
- $x < -4$ or $-1 < x < 2$
- $x < -4$ or $x > 2$

## Explanation

The factor $2 - x$ has a negative coefficient of $x$, so it is positive for $x < 2$ and negative for $x > 2$. The other two factors are positive for $x > -1$ and for $x > -4$ respectively. In the **sign chart** the columns $x < -4$, $-4 < x < -1$, $-1 < x < 2$, $x > 2$ give the product the signs $+$, $-$, $+$, $-$. Therefore the product is positive for $x < -4$ or $-1 < x < 2$.
