What is the real solution set of $x^{2} - 5 x - 66 = 0$?

- $\left\{6, 11\right\}$
- $\left\{-11, -6\right\}$
- $\left\{-6, 11\right\}$
- $\left\{-11, 6\right\}$

## Explanation

The polynomial factors as $\left(x - 11\right) \left(x + 6\right)$. The product is zero when either factor is zero, giving $\left\{-6, 11\right\}$.
