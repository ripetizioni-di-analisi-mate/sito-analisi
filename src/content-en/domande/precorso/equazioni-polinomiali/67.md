The figure shows the lines $y = 4 - 2x$ and $y = x - 2$, which meet at the point with $x$-coordinate $2$. For which $x$ does the first line lie above the second?

::grafico{f="4-2*x;x-2" xmin=-2 xmax=5 ymin=-5 ymax=7 punti="2,0,pieno" legenda="y = 4 - 2x;y = x - 2"}

- $x > 2$
- $x < 2$
- $x \leq 2$
- $x < 0$

## Explanation

The first line lies above the second where $4 - 2x > x - 2$. Moving the terms gives $-3x > -6$, and dividing by $-3$, with the direction reversed, gives $x < 2$. At $x = 2$ the two lines have the same $y$-coordinate $0$, so this value is excluded. The graph confirms that to the left of the intersection point the line $y = 4 - 2x$ is higher.
