The figure shows the graph of the parabola $y = f(x)$, which meets the $x$-axis at the points with $x$-coordinates $-2$ and $1$. For which $x$ does $f(x) \leq 0$ hold?

::grafico{f="x^2+x-2" xmin=-4 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=5 punti="-2,0,pieno;1,0,pieno" legenda="y = f(x)"}

- $x \leq -2$ or $x \geq 1$
- $-2 \leq x \leq 1$
- $-2 < x < 1$
- $-1 \leq x \leq 2$

## Explanation

The value $f(x)$ is the $y$-coordinate of the point of the graph with $x$-coordinate $x$, so $f(x) \leq 0$ where the parabola lies below the $x$-axis or meets it. The graph lies below the $x$-axis between the two intersection points, that is, for $-2 < x < 1$, and it meets the axis at $x = -2$ and at $x = 1$. The direction $\leq$ includes these two points, so the solutions are $-2 \leq x \leq 1$.
