What is the real solution set of $\dfrac{x + 5}{x - 9} \leq 0$?

- $\left[-5, 9\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right] \cup \left(9, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, 9\right]$
- $\left[-5, 9\right)$

## Explanation

The numerator vanishes at -5 and the denominator at 9. The fraction is negative between them and zero at -5, so the result is $\left[-5, 9\right)$.
