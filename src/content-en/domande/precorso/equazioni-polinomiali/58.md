Which of the following equations has the same solutions as $3x - 6 = 0$?

- $3x = -6$
- $\dfrac{x}{2} = 1$
- $x\,(3x - 6) = 0$
- $x - 6 = 0$

## Explanation

The equation $3x - 6 = 0$ has the unique solution $x = 2$. Multiplying both sides of $\dfrac{x}{2} = 1$ by $2$ gives $x = 2$, so by the second equivalence principle the two equations are **equivalent**. The equation $x\,(3x - 6) = 0$ also has the solution $x = 0$, because the factor $x$ can be zero. The equations $3x = -6$ and $x - 6 = 0$ have the solutions $x = -2$ and $x = 6$.
