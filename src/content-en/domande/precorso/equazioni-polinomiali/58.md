Which values satisfy both $x^{2} \leq 16$ and $x > 2$?

- $\left(2, 4\right]$
- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left[2, 4\right]$
- $\left(2, 4\right)$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $\left[-4, 4\right]$ and the second keeps values above its endpoint. Their intersection is $\left(2, 4\right]$.
