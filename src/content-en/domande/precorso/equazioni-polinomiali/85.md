What is the real solution set of $x^{2} - 2 x - 15 = 0$?

- $\left\{-3, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, 3\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, -3\right\}$
- $\left\{3, 5\right\}$

## Explanation

The polynomial factors as $\left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 3\right)$. The product is zero when either factor is zero, giving $\left\{-3, 5\right\}$.
