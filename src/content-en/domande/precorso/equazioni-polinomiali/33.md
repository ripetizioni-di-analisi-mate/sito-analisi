What are the solutions of the inequality $x^2 - 5x < 0$?

- $x < 0$ or $x > 5$
- $x < 5$
- $0 \leq x \leq 5$
- $0 < x < 5$

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ we obtain $x\,(x - 5) < 0$, so the trinomial has roots $0$ and $5$. Since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, by the **sign of the quadratic trinomial** the trinomial is negative between the roots. The direction $<$ excludes the roots, so the solutions are $0 < x < 5$. The value $x = -1$ satisfies $x < 5$ but not the original inequality, since $1 + 5 = 6 > 0$; therefore dividing by $x$ without knowing its sign produces a wrong solution set.
