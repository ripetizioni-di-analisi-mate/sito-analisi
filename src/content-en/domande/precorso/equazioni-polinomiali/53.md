What are the real solutions of $x \left(x - 9\right) \left(x + 5\right) < 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(0, 9\right)$
- $\left(-5, 9\right)$
- $\left(-5, 0\right) \cup \left(9, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right] \cup \left[0, 9\right]$

## Explanation

The zeros are $\left\{-5, 0, 9\right\}$, and one factor changes sign at each zero. The product is positive to the right of 9, so the intervals where the product is negative are those in $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(0, 9\right)$.
