A bike rental offers two rates. Rate A costs a fixed $15$ euros plus $4$ euros per hour, rate B costs $9$ euros per hour with no fixed fee. After how many hours of rental do the two rates cost the same?

- $\dfrac{15}{13}$ hours
- $\dfrac{15}{4}$ hours
- $3$ hours
- $5$ hours

## Explanation

After $h$ hours rate A costs $15 + 4h$ euros and rate B costs $9h$ euros. The two rates cost the same when $15 + 4h = 9h$. Moving $4h$ to the right-hand side with the sign changed gives $15 = 5h$, so $h = 3$. Indeed after $3$ hours both rates cost $27$ euros.
