At which points of the real line does $(x - 1)^2 (x^2 - 5x + 6) \leq 0$ hold?

- $x = 1$ or $2 \leq x \leq 3$
- $2 \leq x \leq 3$
- $x \leq 2$ or $x \geq 3$
- $x = 1$ or $2 < x < 3$

## Explanation

The trinomial factors as $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3)$, so it is negative for $2 < x < 3$ and zero at $2$ and at $3$. The factor $(x - 1)^2$ is positive for $x \neq 1$, so for $x \neq 1$ the left-hand side has the sign of the trinomial. At $x = 1$ the factor $(x - 1)^2$ equals zero and the left-hand side is zero, so the direction $\leq$ includes this point too. The solutions are $x = 1$ or $2 \leq x \leq 3$.
