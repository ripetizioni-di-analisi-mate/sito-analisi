Which of the following inequalities is satisfied by every real number?

- $x^2 - 2x + 1 > 0$
- $x^2 - 2x + 1 \geq 0$
- $x^2 + 2x - 1 \geq 0$
- $x^2 - 4x + 5 \leq 0$

## Explanation

The trinomial $x^2 - 2x + 1$ is the square $(x - 1)^2$, positive for $x \neq 1$ and zero for $x = 1$, so $x^2 - 2x + 1 \geq 0$ holds for every real $x$. The strict inequality $x^2 - 2x + 1 > 0$, on the other hand, fails at $x = 1$. The trinomial $x^2 + 2x - 1$ has $\Delta = 4 + 4 = 8 > 0$ and is negative between its roots, for example at $x = 0$. The trinomial $x^2 - 4x + 5$ has $\Delta = -4 < 0$ and a positive coefficient of $x^2$, so it is positive for every $x$ and the inequality $x^2 - 4x + 5 \leq 0$ has no solutions.
