What are the real solutions of $5 - 3 x \geq 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, - \dfrac{5}{3}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{5}{3}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{5}{3}\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{5}{3}\right]$

## Explanation

Isolating the term in $x$ gives $- 3 x \geq -5$. Dividing by the negative coefficient reverses the inequality, giving $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{5}{3}\right]$.
