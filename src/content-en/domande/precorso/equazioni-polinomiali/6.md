- $x = 3$ and $x = -3$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 3$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 9$
- $x = 0$, $x = 3$, $x = -3$

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ gives $x\,(x^2 - 9) = 0$. Since $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$ is a difference of squares, the equation is equivalent to $x\,(x - 3)(x + 3) = 0$. By the **zero product property** the solutions are $x = 0$, $x = 3$ and $x = -3$.
