What is the real solution set of $\dfrac{x + 3}{x - 5} = 4$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{13}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{17}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{23}{3}\right\}$

## Explanation

Exclude $x=5$ before multiplying by the denominator. The resulting linear equation is $3 x = 23$ and gives $\left\{\dfrac{23}{3}\right\}$.
