What are the solutions of the equation $\dfrac{x + 3}{x} = \dfrac{x - 1}{x - 2}$?

- $x = -3$
- $x = 2$
- $x = 3$
- The equation has no solutions

## Explanation

The **domain conditions** are $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq 2$. Moving the right-hand side and reducing to the common denominator $x\,(x - 2)$, the numerator becomes
$$
(x + 3)(x - 2) - x\,(x - 1) = x^2 + x - 6 - x^2 + x = 2x - 6 .
$$
By the **zero of a fraction** theorem the solutions are the zeros of the numerator that satisfy the domain conditions. The numerator $2x - 6$ vanishes at $x = 3$, which is different from $0$ and from $2$, so the only solution is $x = 3$.
