What are the solutions of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 3x \leq 0 \\[2pt]
4 - x > 2
\end{cases}
$$

- $0 \leq x \leq 3$
- $x < 2$
- $0 \leq x \leq 2$
- $0 \leq x < 2$

## Explanation

The first inequality is equivalent to $x\,(x - 3) \leq 0$. The trinomial has roots $0$ and $3$ and a positive coefficient of $x^2$, so it is negative or zero for $0 \leq x \leq 3$. The second is equivalent to $-x > -2$, and multiplying by $-1$ with the direction reversed we obtain $x < 2$. The two inequalities must hold simultaneously, therefore the solutions of the system are the **intersection** of the two sets, that is, $0 \leq x < 2$.
