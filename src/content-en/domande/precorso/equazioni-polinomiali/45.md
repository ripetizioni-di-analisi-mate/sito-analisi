The following system consists of two linear inequalities. What do we obtain by solving it?
$$
\begin{cases}
3x - 2 > 4 \\[2pt]
2x + 1 < 3
\end{cases}
$$

- The system has no solutions
- $x < 1$ or $x > 2$
- $1 < x < 2$
- $x > 2$

## Explanation

The first inequality is equivalent to $3x > 6$, so its solutions are $x > 2$. The second is equivalent to $2x < 2$, so its solutions are $x < 1$. The solutions of the system are the **intersection** of the two sets, and no number is both greater than $2$ and less than $1$. Therefore the system **has no solutions**.
