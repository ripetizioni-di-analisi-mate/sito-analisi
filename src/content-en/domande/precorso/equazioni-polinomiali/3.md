What are the solutions of the equation $3x^2 + 7x = 0$?

- $x = 0$ and $x = -\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $x = -\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $x = 0$ and $x = \dfrac{7}{3}$
- $x = 0$ and $x = -\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

The equation is **incomplete**, because the constant term is zero. Factoring out $x$ gives $x\,(3x + 7) = 0$. By the **zero product property** we have $x = 0$ or $3x + 7 = 0$, that is, $x = -\dfrac{7}{3}$.
