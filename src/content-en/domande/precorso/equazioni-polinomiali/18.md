For which value of the parameter $k$ is the equation $(k - 2)\,x = k + 2$ inconsistent?

- $k = 2$
- $k = -2$
- $k = \pm 2$
- For no value of $k$

## Explanation

For $k \neq 2$ the coefficient of $x$ is nonzero, so the equation has the unique solution $x = \dfrac{k + 2}{k - 2}$. In particular, for $k = -2$ the equation becomes $-4x = 0$, which has the unique solution $x = 0$. For $k = 2$ the equation becomes $0 \cdot x = 4$, and no real number satisfies it. Therefore the equation is **inconsistent** only for $k = 2$.
