What is the real solution set of $\dfrac{x + 6}{x - 11} = 7$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{61}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{83}{6}\right\}$
- $\left\{11\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{71}{6}\right\}$

## Explanation

Exclude $x=11$ before multiplying by the denominator. The resulting linear equation is $6 x = 83$ and gives $\left\{\dfrac{83}{6}\right\}$.
