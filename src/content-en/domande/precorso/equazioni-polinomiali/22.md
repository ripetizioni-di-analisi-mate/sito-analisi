How many real solutions does the equation $3x^2 - 5x + 4 = 0$ have?

- None
- One, double
- Two, both positive
- Two, of opposite sign

## Explanation

The coefficients are $a = 3$, $b = -5$, $c = 4$, so the discriminant is $\Delta = 25 - 48 = -23$. Since $\Delta < 0$, the equation **has no real solutions**. The sum and product relations for the roots require $\Delta \geq 0$, so the positive values of $-\dfrac{b}{a} = \dfrac{5}{3}$ and of $\dfrac{c}{a} = \dfrac{4}{3}$ do not indicate the existence of two positive roots.
