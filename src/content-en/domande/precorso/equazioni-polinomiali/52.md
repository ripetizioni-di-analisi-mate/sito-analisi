What are all the real numbers whose square is greater than or equal to $16$?

- $x \geq 4$
- $-4 \leq x \leq 4$
- $x < -4$ or $x > 4$
- $x \leq -4$ or $x \geq 4$

## Explanation

The condition is $x^2 \geq 16$, that is, $x^2 - 16 \geq 0$. The binomial $x^2 - 16$ has roots $-4$ and $4$ and, since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, it is positive outside the interval between the roots and zero at the roots. Therefore the solutions are $x \leq -4$ or $x \geq 4$. Negative numbers such as $-5$ are solutions too, because $(-5)^2 = 25$.
