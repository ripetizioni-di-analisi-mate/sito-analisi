What is the real solution set of $9 - 5 x \geq 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{9}{5}\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{9}{5}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{9}{5}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, - \dfrac{9}{5}\right]$

## Explanation

Isolating the term in $x$ gives $- 5 x \geq -9$. Dividing by the negative coefficient reverses the inequality, giving $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{9}{5}\right]$.
