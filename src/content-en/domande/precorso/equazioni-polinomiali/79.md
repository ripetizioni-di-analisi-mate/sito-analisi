What are the real solutions of $3 x - 15 = 5 x$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{15}{8}\right\}$
- $\left\{15\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

Expanding and collecting the terms in $x$ gives $- 2 x = 15$. After dividing by -2, we obtain the solution set $\left\{- \dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$.
