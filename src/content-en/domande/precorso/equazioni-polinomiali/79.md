What is the real solution set of $3 x - 15 = 5 x$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{15}{8}\right\}$
- $\left\{15\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

Expand and collect terms in $x$ to get $- 2 x = 15$. Divide by -2 to obtain $\left\{- \dfrac{15}{2}\right\}$.
