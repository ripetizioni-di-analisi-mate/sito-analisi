- $x = 3$, $x = 2$, $x = -2$
- $x = -3$ and $x = 2$
- $x = -3$, $x = 2$, $x = -2$
- $x = -3$, $x = 4$, $x = -4$

## Explanation

The first two terms have the common factor $x^2$ and the last two the common factor $-4$, so **factoring by grouping** gives
$$
x^3 + 3x^2 - 4x - 12 = x^2 (x + 3) - 4\,(x + 3) = (x + 3)(x^2 - 4) .
$$
Since $x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$, the equation is equivalent to $(x + 3)(x - 2)(x + 2) = 0$. By the **zero product property** the solutions are $x = -3$, $x = 2$ and $x = -2$.
