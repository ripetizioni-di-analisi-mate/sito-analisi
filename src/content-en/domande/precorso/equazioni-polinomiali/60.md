Where is the fraction $\dfrac{5}{x - 1}$ positive?

- Every $x \neq 1$
- $x < 1$
- $x \geq 1$
- $x > 1$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x \neq 1$. The numerator $5$ is positive, so by the **sign of a quotient** theorem the fraction is positive exactly where the denominator is positive. Since $x - 1 > 0$ for $x > 1$, the solutions are $x > 1$. For $x < 1$ the denominator is negative and the fraction is negative; for example at $x = 0$ it equals $-5$.
