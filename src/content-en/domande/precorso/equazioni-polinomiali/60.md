What are the real solutions of $5 x - 45 = 9 x$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{45}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{45}{14}\right\}$
- $\left\{45\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{45}{4}\right\}$

## Explanation

Expanding and collecting the terms in $x$ gives $- 4 x = 45$. After dividing by -4, we obtain the solution set $\left\{- \dfrac{45}{4}\right\}$.
