What is the real solution set of $5 x - 45 = 9 x$?

- $\left\{- \dfrac{45}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{45}{14}\right\}$
- $\left\{45\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{45}{4}\right\}$

## Explanation

Expand and collect terms in $x$ to get $- 4 x = 45$. Divide by -4 to obtain $\left\{- \dfrac{45}{4}\right\}$.
