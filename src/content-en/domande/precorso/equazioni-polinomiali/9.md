What are the real solutions of the equation $x^4 - 3x^2 - 4 = 0$?

- $x = \pm 2$ and $x = \pm 1$
- $x = 4$ and $x = -1$
- $x = \pm 1$
- $x = \pm 2$

## Explanation

The equation is **biquadratic**. With the substitution $t = x^2$ it becomes $t^2 - 3t - 4 = 0$, which has roots with sum $3$ and product $-4$, that is, $t = 4$ and $t = -1$. From the equation $x^2 = 4$ it follows that $x = \pm 2$. The equation $x^2 = -1$ has no real solutions, because a square is never negative. Therefore the real solutions are $x = 2$ and $x = -2$.
