What are the solutions of the inequality $\dfrac{3}{x - 2} \geq 1$?

- $x \leq 5$
- $2 < x \leq 5$
- $2 \leq x \leq 5$
- $x < 2$ or $x \geq 5$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x \neq 2$. Moving $1$ to the left-hand side and reducing to the same denominator we obtain
$$
\frac{3 - (x - 2)}{x - 2} \geq 0 , \quad \text{that is} \quad \frac{5 - x}{x - 2} \geq 0 .
$$
The numerator is positive for $x < 5$ and the denominator for $x > 2$, so by the **sign of a quotient** the fraction is positive for $2 < x < 5$. The direction $\geq$ includes the zero $5$ of the numerator and excludes $2$, therefore the solutions are $2 < x \leq 5$. The value $x = 0$ satisfies $x \leq 5$, but $\dfrac{3}{0 - 2} = -\dfrac{3}{2}$ is less than $1$.
