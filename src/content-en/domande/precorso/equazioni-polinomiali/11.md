What are the solutions of the inequality $-x^2 + x + 6 \geq 0$?

- $-2 \leq x \leq 3$
- $x \leq -2$ or $x \geq 3$
- $-2 < x < 3$
- $-3 \leq x \leq 2$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $-1$ and reversing the direction, the inequality becomes $x^2 - x - 6 \leq 0$. The trinomial $x^2 - x - 6$ has discriminant $\Delta = 1 + 24 = 25 > 0$ and roots $x = \dfrac{1 \pm 5}{2}$, that is, $-2$ and $3$. Since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, the trinomial is negative between the roots and zero at the roots. The direction $\leq$ includes the roots, so the solutions are $-2 \leq x \leq 3$.
