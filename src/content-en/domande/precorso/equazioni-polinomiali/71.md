What is the real solution set of $x^{2} - 4 x - 45 = 0$?

- $\left\{5, 9\right\}$
- $\left\{-9, 5\right\}$
- $\left\{-9, -5\right\}$
- $\left\{-5, 9\right\}$

## Explanation

The polynomial factors as $\left(x - 9\right) \left(x + 5\right)$. The product is zero when either factor is zero, giving $\left\{-5, 9\right\}$.
