The binomial $x - 3$ appears on both sides of the equation $x^2 (x - 3) = 4\,(x - 3)$. What are the solutions of the equation?

- $x = 2$ and $x = -2$
- $x = 3$ and $x = 2$
- $x = 3$ and $x = 4$
- $x = 3$, $x = 2$, $x = -2$

## Explanation

Moving $4\,(x - 3)$ to the left-hand side and factoring out the binomial $x - 3$ gives $(x - 3)(x^2 - 4) = 0$. By the **zero product property** we have $x - 3 = 0$ or $x^2 = 4$, so $x = 3$, $x = 2$, $x = -2$. Dividing both sides by $x - 3$ would remove the solution $x = 3$, because for $x = 3$ the divisor is zero.
