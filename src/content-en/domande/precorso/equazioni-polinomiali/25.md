What are the solutions of the equation $(x - 1)^2 = 2x + 6$?

- $x = 5$ and $x = -1$
- $x = -5$ and $x = 1$
- $x = 1 \pm \sqrt{6}$
- $x = 5$

## Explanation

Expanding the square of the binomial we obtain $x^2 - 2x + 1 = 2x + 6$. Moving every term to the left-hand side we arrive at the equation $x^2 - 4x - 5 = 0$, which has discriminant $\Delta = 16 + 20 = 36$. By the **quadratic formula** we have $x = \dfrac{4 \pm 6}{2}$, so the solutions are $x = 5$ and $x = -1$.
