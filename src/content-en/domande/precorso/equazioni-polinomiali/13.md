- $-1 \leq x \leq 0$ or $x \geq 1$
- $-1 \leq x \leq 1$
- $x \leq -1$ or $0 < x < 1$
- $x \leq -1$ or $0 \leq x \leq 1$

## Explanation

Factoring out $x$ gives $x^3 - x = x\,(x^2 - 1)$, and since $x^2 - 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1)$ the polynomial factors as $x\,(x - 1)(x + 1)$, with zeros $-1$, $0$ and $1$. Each first-degree factor is negative before its own zero and positive after it. From the **sign chart** the product is negative for $x < -1$ and for $0 < x < 1$, positive for $-1 < x < 0$ and for $x > 1$. The direction $\leq$ includes the zeros of the product, so the solutions are $x \leq -1$ or $0 \leq x \leq 1$.
