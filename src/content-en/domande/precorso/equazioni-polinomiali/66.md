Which values satisfy both $x^{2} \leq 49$ and $x > 3$?

- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left[3, 7\right]$
- $\left(3, 7\right]$
- $\left(3, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $\left[-7, 7\right]$ and the second keeps values above its endpoint. Their intersection is $\left(3, 7\right]$.
