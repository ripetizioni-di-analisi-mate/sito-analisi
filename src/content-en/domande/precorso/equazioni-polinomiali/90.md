What is the real solution set of $7 - 4 x \geq 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, - \dfrac{7}{4}\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{7}{4}\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{7}{4}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{7}{4}, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Isolating the term in $x$ gives $- 4 x \geq -7$. Dividing by the negative coefficient reverses the inequality, giving $\left(-\infty, \dfrac{7}{4}\right]$.
