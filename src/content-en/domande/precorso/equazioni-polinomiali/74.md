What is the real solution set of $x \left(x - 7\right) \left(x + 4\right) < 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, -4\right) \cup \left(0, 7\right)$
- $\left(-4, 7\right)$
- $\left(-4, 0\right) \cup \left(7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -4\right] \cup \left[0, 7\right]$

## Explanation

The zeros are $\left\{-4, 0, 7\right\}$, and one factor changes sign at each zero. The product is positive to the right of 7, so the negative intervals give $\left(-\infty, -4\right) \cup \left(0, 7\right)$.
