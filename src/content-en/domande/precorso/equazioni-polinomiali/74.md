The area of a trapezoid with bases $B$ and $b$ and height $h \neq 0$ is $S = \dfrac{(B + b)\,h}{2}$. Which expression gives the shorter base $b$ in terms of $S$, $B$ and $h$?

- $b = \dfrac{2S}{h} + B$
- $b = \dfrac{2S - B}{h}$
- $b = \dfrac{2S}{h} - B$
- $b = \dfrac{S}{2h} - B$

## Explanation

The formula is a linear equation in the unknown $b$. Multiplying both sides by $2$ gives $2S = (B + b)\,h$, and dividing by $h \neq 0$ gives $B + b = \dfrac{2S}{h}$. Moving $B$ to the right-hand side with the sign changed gives $b = \dfrac{2S}{h} - B$.
