What are the solutions of the equation $x\,(x - 3) = 2x$?

- $x = 5$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 5$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 3$
- $x = 0$ and $x = -5$

## Explanation

Moving every term to the left-hand side we obtain $x^2 - 3x - 2x = 0$, that is, the **quadratic with no constant term** $x^2 - 5x = 0$. Factoring out $x$ we have $x\,(x - 5) = 0$, and by the **zero product property** the solutions are $x = 0$ and $x = 5$. Dividing both sides by $x$ assumes $x \neq 0$, so that step excludes the solution $x = 0$.
