What is the real solution set of $x \left(x - 11\right) \left(x + 6\right) = 0$?

- $\left\{0, 11\right\}$
- $\left\{-6, 0, 11\right\}$
- $\left\{-6, 11\right\}$
- $\left\{-11, 0, 6\right\}$

## Explanation

The factor $x$ gives the solution $0$. Setting the other two factors equal to zero gives $\left\{-6, 0, 11\right\}$.
