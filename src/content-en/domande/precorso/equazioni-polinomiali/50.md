What are the real solutions of $\left(x - 5\right)^{2} \left(x + 3\right) \geq 0$?

- $\left[-3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right] \cup \left\{5\right\}$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right]$
- $\left[-5, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The squared factor is positive except at 5, where it is zero. The linear factor is nonnegative from -3 onward and that interval already contains 5, so the result is $\left[-3, \infty\right)$.
