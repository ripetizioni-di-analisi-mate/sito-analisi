What do we obtain by solving the equation $2x^2 + 18 = 0$ over the real numbers?

- No real solution
- $x = \pm 3$
- $x = -9$
- $x = -3$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides by $2$ gives $x^2 = -9$. The square of a real number is never negative, so no real $x$ satisfies $x^2 = -9$. Therefore the equation **has no real solutions**. The values $\pm 3$ satisfy $x^2 = 9$ instead.
