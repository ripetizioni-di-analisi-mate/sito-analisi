What are the solutions of the inequality $x^2 - 6x + 9 > 0$?

- Every $x > 3$
- Every real $x$
- Every $x \neq 3$
- No real $x$

## Explanation

The trinomial has discriminant $\Delta = 36 - 36 = 0$ and double root $x = 3$; indeed $x^2 - 6x + 9 = (x - 3)^2$. By the **sign of the quadratic trinomial** with $a > 0$ and $\Delta = 0$, the trinomial is positive for every $x \neq 3$ and is zero for $x = 3$. The direction $>$ excludes the value at which the trinomial vanishes, so the solutions are all real numbers different from $3$.
