What is the real solution set of $\dfrac{x + 3}{x - 5} \leq 0$?

- $\left(-3, 5\right]$
- $\left[-3, 5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right] \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-3, 5\right]$

## Explanation

The numerator vanishes at -3 and the denominator at 5. The fraction is negative between them and zero at -3, so the result is $\left[-3, 5\right)$.
