The dimensions of a rectangle differ by $2$ cm and its area is $24\ \mathrm{cm}^2$. How long is the shorter side?

- $4$ cm
- $6$ cm
- $\sqrt{22}$ cm
- $12$ cm

## Explanation

Letting $x$ be the shorter side in centimeters, the longer side measures $x + 2$ and the area gives the equation $x\,(x + 2) = 24$, that is, $x^2 + 2x - 24 = 0$. Two numbers with sum $-2$ and product $-24$ are $4$ and $-6$, so the solutions are $x = 4$ and $x = -6$. A length is positive, so $x = -6$ is discarded and the shorter side measures $4$ cm. The longer side measures $6$ cm, and indeed $4 \cdot 6 = 24$.
