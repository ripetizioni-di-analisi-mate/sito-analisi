- $x = 1$, $x = 2$, $x = -3$
- $x = -1$, $x = -2$, $x = 3$
- $x = 1$, $x = -2$, $x = 3$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 6$

## Explanation

Let $P(x) = x^3 - 7x + 6$. Since $P(1) = 1 - 7 + 6 = 0$, the binomial $x - 1$ divides $P$. With **Ruffini's rule**, writing the coefficient $0$ of the $x^2$ term, we obtain $P(x) = (x - 1)(x^2 + x - 6)$. The trinomial $x^2 + x - 6$ has roots with sum $-1$ and product $-6$, that is, $2$ and $-3$, so $P(x) = (x - 1)(x - 2)(x + 3)$. By the **zero product property** the solutions are $x = 1$, $x = 2$ and $x = -3$.
