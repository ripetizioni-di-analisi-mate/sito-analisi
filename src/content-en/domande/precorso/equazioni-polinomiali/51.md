What are the real solutions of $4 x - 28 = 7 x$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{28}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{28}{11}\right\}$
- $\left\{28\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{28}{3}\right\}$

## Explanation

Expanding and collecting the terms in $x$ gives $- 3 x = 28$. After dividing by -3, we obtain the solution set $\left\{- \dfrac{28}{3}\right\}$.
