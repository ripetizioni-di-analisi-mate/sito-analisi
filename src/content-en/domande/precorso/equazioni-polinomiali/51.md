What is the real solution set of $4 x - 28 = 7 x$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{28}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{28}{11}\right\}$
- $\left\{28\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{28}{3}\right\}$

## Explanation

Expand and collect terms in $x$ to get $- 3 x = 28$. Divide by -3 to obtain $\left\{- \dfrac{28}{3}\right\}$.
