How many real solutions does $x^3 + 8 = 0$ have, and what are they?

- Only $x = 2$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -2$
- Only $x = -\dfrac{8}{3}$
- Only $x = -2$

## Explanation

The binomial $x^3 + 8$ is a sum of cubes, so it factors as $(x + 2)(x^2 - 2x + 4)$. By the **zero product property** we have $x + 2 = 0$ or $x^2 - 2x + 4 = 0$. The trinomial has discriminant $\Delta = 4 - 16 = -12 < 0$ and is never zero for real $x$. Therefore the only real solution is $x = -2$, and indeed $(-2)^3 = -8$.
