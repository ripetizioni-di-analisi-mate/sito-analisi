Which value of $x$ satisfies the equation $0.2x + 1.5 = 0.5x - 0.3$?

- $x = -6$
- $x = 6$
- $x = -\dfrac{18}{7}$
- $x = \dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Multiplying every term on both sides by $10$ gives the equivalent equation $2x + 15 = 5x - 3$. Moving $5x$ to the left-hand side and $15$ to the right-hand side, with the sign changed, we get $2x - 5x = -3 - 15$, that is, $-3x = -18$. Dividing by $-3$ gives $x = 6$, because the quotient of two negative numbers is positive.
