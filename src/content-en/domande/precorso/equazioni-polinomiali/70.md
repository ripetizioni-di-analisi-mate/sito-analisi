Which values satisfy both $x^{2} \leq 121$ and $x > 5$?

- $\left(5, 11\right]$
- $\left[5, 11\right]$
- $\left(5, 11\right)$
- $\left(5, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $\left[-11, 11\right]$ and the second keeps values above its endpoint. Their intersection is $\left(5, 11\right]$.
