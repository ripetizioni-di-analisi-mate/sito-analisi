Let $a$ be a real parameter. What are the solutions of the equation $x^2 - (a + 2)\,x + 2a = 0$ for every value of $a$?

- $x = 2$ and $x = a$
- $x = -2$ and $x = -a$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -a$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 2a$

## Explanation

The numbers $2$ and $a$ have sum $a + 2$ and product $2a$, so by the **sum and product of the roots** the trinomial factors as $(x - 2)(x - a)$. The same result follows from the discriminant $\Delta = (a + 2)^2 - 8a = (a - 2)^2$, which is never negative. By the zero product property the solutions are $x = 2$ and $x = a$. For $a = 2$ the two solutions coincide and $x = 2$ is a double solution.
