What is the real solution set of $4 - 2 x \geq 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, 2\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right]$
- $\left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right)$

## Explanation

Isolating the term in $x$ gives $- 2 x \geq -4$. Dividing by the negative coefficient reverses the inequality, giving $\left(-\infty, 2\right]$.
