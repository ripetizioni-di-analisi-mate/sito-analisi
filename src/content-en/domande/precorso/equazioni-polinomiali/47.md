Which values satisfy both $x^{2} \leq 25$ and $x > 2$?

- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(2, 5\right)$
- $\left(2, 5\right]$
- $\left[2, 5\right]$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $\left[-5, 5\right]$ and the second keeps values above its endpoint. Their intersection is $\left(2, 5\right]$.
