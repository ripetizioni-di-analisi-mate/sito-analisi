A taxi charges a fixed fee of $5$ euros plus $2$ euros for every kilometer traveled. With a budget of at most $29$ euros, what is the greatest number of kilometers that can be traveled?

- $14.5$ km
- $12$ km
- $17$ km
- $24$ km

## Explanation

Letting $x$ be the number of kilometers, the cost is $5 + 2x$ euros, and the condition is the inequality $5 + 2x \leq 29$. Subtracting $5$ from both sides gives $2x \leq 24$. Dividing by the positive number $2$ preserves the direction, so $x \leq 12$. Therefore at most $12$ km can be traveled.
