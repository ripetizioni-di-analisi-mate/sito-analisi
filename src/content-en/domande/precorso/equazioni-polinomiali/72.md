What are the real solutions of $\left(x - 11\right)^{2} \left(x + 6\right) \geq 0$?

- $\left[-11, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right] \cup \left\{11\right\}$
- $\left[-6, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -6\right]$

## Explanation

The squared factor is positive except at 11, where it is zero. The linear factor is nonnegative from -6 onward and that interval already contains 11, so the result is $\left[-6, \infty\right)$.
