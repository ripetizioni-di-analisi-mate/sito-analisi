The sum of a real number $x \neq 0$ and its reciprocal equals $\dfrac{5}{2}$. Which values can $x$ take?

- $x = -2$ and $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x = 2$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x = 4$ and $x = 1$
- Only $x = 2$

## Explanation

The condition is the rational equation $x + \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{5}{2}$, with the domain restriction $x \neq 0$. Multiplying every term by $2x$, which is nonzero, gives $2x^2 + 2 = 5x$, that is, $2x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$. The discriminant is $\Delta = 25 - 16 = 9$, so $x = \dfrac{5 \pm 3}{4}$, that is, $x = 2$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Both values are nonzero, so they are solutions; indeed $2 + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{5}{2}$.
