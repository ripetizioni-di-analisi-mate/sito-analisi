What are the real solutions of $2 x - 8 = 4 x$?

- $\left\{4\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{4}{3}\right\}$
- $\left\{8\right\}$
- $\left\{-4\right\}$

## Explanation

Expanding and collecting the terms in $x$ gives $- 2 x = 8$. After dividing by -2, we obtain the solution set $\left\{-4\right\}$.
