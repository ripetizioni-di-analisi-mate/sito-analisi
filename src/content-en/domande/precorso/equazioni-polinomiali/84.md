Determine where the product $(x^2 - 9)(x^2 + 2x + 1)$ is negative.

- $-3 < x < 3$
- $x < -3$ or $x > 3$
- $-3 < x < -1$ or $-1 < x < 3$
- $x < -3$ or $-1 < x < 3$

## Explanation

The second factor is the square $x^2 + 2x + 1 = (x + 1)^2$, positive for $x \neq -1$ and zero at $x = -1$. By the **sign of a factor raised to a power** the product therefore has the sign of $x^2 - 9$ for every $x \neq -1$, while at $x = -1$ it equals zero. The binomial $x^2 - 9$ is negative for $-3 < x < 3$. The direction $<$ excludes the point $x = -1$, so the solutions are $-3 < x < -1$ or $-1 < x < 3$.
