What are the solutions of the inequality $\dfrac{x}{2} - 1 < \dfrac{2x + 1}{3}$?

- $x < -8$
- $x > -7$
- $x > -8$
- $x > 8$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by the positive number $6$ preserves the direction, and we obtain $3x - 6 < 2\,(2x + 1)$, that is, $3x - 6 < 4x + 2$. Subtracting $4x$ and adding $6$ to both sides we have $-x < 8$. Multiplying by $-1$ **reverses** the direction, so the solutions are $x > -8$.
