What are the real solutions of $x \left(x - 5\right) \left(x + 3\right) < 0$?

- $\left(-3, 0\right) \cup \left(5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(0, 5\right)$
- $\left(-3, 5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right] \cup \left[0, 5\right]$

## Explanation

The zeros are $\left\{-3, 0, 5\right\}$, and one factor changes sign at each zero. The product is positive to the right of 5, so the intervals where the product is negative are those in $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(0, 5\right)$.
