What are the solutions of the equation $\dfrac{x^2 + 2x - 3}{x^2 - 1} = 0$?

- $x = -3$ and $x = 1$
- $x = 1$
- $x = -1$ and $x = 1$
- $x = -3$

## Explanation

The denominator factors as $x^2 - 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1)$, so the **domain conditions** are $x \neq 1$ and $x \neq -1$. The numerator $x^2 + 2x - 3 = (x + 3)(x - 1)$ vanishes at $x = -3$ and at $x = 1$. By the **zero of a fraction** theorem only the zeros of the numerator that satisfy the domain conditions are solutions. The value $1$ makes the denominator vanish and is discarded, so the only solution is $x = -3$.
