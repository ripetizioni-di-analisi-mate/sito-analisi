What are the real solutions of $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 4} \leq 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, -2\right] \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left[-2, 4\right)$
- $\left(-2, 4\right]$
- $\left[-2, 4\right]$

## Explanation

The numerator vanishes at -2 and the denominator at 4. The fraction is negative between them and zero at -2, so the result is $\left[-2, 4\right)$.
