For which real numbers is the cube less than $8$, that is, $x^3 < 8$?

- $-2 < x < 2$
- $x > 2$
- $x < 2$
- $x < \dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

Moving $8$ to the left-hand side and factoring the difference of cubes gives $(x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4) < 0$. The trinomial $x^2 + 2x + 4$ has discriminant $\Delta = 4 - 16 = -12 < 0$ and a positive coefficient of $x^2$, so it is positive for every $x$. The sign of the product is therefore that of $x - 2$, and the solutions are $x < 2$. Numbers less than $-2$ are solutions too, because their cube is negative.
