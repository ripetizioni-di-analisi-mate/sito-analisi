What are the solutions of the inequality $-x^2 + 2x - 3 < 0$?

- Every real $x$
- No real $x$
- $x < -1$ or $x > 3$
- Every $x \neq 1$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $-1$ and reversing the direction we obtain $x^2 - 2x + 3 > 0$. The trinomial $x^2 - 2x + 3$ has discriminant $\Delta = 4 - 12 = -8 < 0$ and a positive coefficient of $x^2$. By the **sign of the quadratic trinomial** the trinomial is then positive for every $x$, so the inequality is satisfied by **every real number**.
