To produce $x$ items, with $x > 0$, a company spends $200 + 5x$ euros, so the average cost per item is $\dfrac{200 + 5x}{x}$ euros. For which $x$ is the average cost at most $7$ euros?

- $x \leq 100$
- $x \geq 40$
- $x \geq 100$
- $x \geq \dfrac{200}{7}$

## Explanation

The condition is the rational inequality $\dfrac{200 + 5x}{x} \leq 7$. The denominator $x$ is positive, so multiplying both sides by $x$ preserves the direction and gives $200 + 5x \leq 7x$. Moving $5x$ to the right-hand side gives $200 \leq 2x$, that is, $x \geq 100$. Therefore the average cost is at most $7$ euros from $100$ items on.
