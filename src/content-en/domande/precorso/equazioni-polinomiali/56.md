What is the real solution set of $\dfrac{x + 5}{x - 9} = 6$?

- $\left\{9\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{59}{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{41}{5}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{49}{5}\right\}$

## Explanation

Exclude $x=9$ before multiplying by the denominator. The resulting linear equation is $5 x = 59$ and gives $\left\{\dfrac{59}{5}\right\}$.
