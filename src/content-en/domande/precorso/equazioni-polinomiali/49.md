What are the real solutions of $x^{2} - 3 x - 28 = 0$?

- $\left\{-7, -4\right\}$
- $\left\{4, 7\right\}$
- $\left\{-7, 4\right\}$
- $\left\{-4, 7\right\}$

## Explanation

The polynomial factors as $\left(x - 7\right) \left(x + 4\right)$. The product is zero if at least one factor vanishes, so the solution set is $\left\{-4, 7\right\}$.
