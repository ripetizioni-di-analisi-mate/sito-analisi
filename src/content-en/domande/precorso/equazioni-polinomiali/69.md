What are the real solutions of $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 4} = 3$?

- $\left\{4\right\}$
- $\left\{-3\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$
- $\left\{5\right\}$

## Explanation

We exclude $x=4$ before multiplying by the denominator. The resulting linear equation is $2 x = 14$ and gives $\left\{7\right\}$.
