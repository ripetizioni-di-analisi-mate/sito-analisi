What is the factorization of the trinomial $x^2 - 7x + 12$?

- $(x + 3)(x + 4)$
- $(x - 3)(x - 4)$
- $(x - 2)(x - 6)$
- $(x - 3)(x + 4)$

## Explanation

Since $a = 1$, by the theorem on the **sum and product of the roots** the roots $x_1$ and $x_2$ of the equation $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$ satisfy $x_1 + x_2 = 7$ and $x_1 \cdot x_2 = 12$. The two numbers with sum $7$ and product $12$ are $3$ and $4$, so the solutions are $x = 3$ and $x = 4$. Therefore the trinomial factors as $x^2 - 7x + 12 = (x - 3)(x - 4)$.
