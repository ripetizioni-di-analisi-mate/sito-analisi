- $x \geq 0$
- $-1 \leq x \leq 0$ or $x \geq 1$
- $-1 < x \leq 0$ or $x > 1$
- $x < -1$ or $0 \leq x < 1$

## Explanation

The domain conditions are $x \neq 1$ and $x \neq -1$. Moving the right-hand side and reducing to the common denominator we obtain
$$
\frac{(x + 1)^2 - (x - 1)^2}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} \geq 0 , \quad \text{that is} \quad \frac{4x}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} \geq 0 .
$$
The numerator vanishes at $0$, the factors of the denominator at $1$ and at $-1$, and each first-degree factor is negative before its own zero and positive after it. From the **sign chart** the quotient is positive for $-1 < x < 0$ and for $x > 1$, negative for $x < -1$ and for $0 < x < 1$. The direction $\geq$ includes the zero $0$ of the numerator, while $-1$ and $1$ are excluded by the domain conditions, so the solutions are $-1 < x \leq 0$ or $x > 1$.
