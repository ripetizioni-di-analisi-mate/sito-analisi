Which values of $x$ make the equality $(2x - 1)^2 = 9$ true?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 5$ and $x = -4$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -1$
- $x = 1$ and $x = -2$

## Explanation

A number has square $9$ if and only if it equals $3$ or $-3$, so $2x - 1 = 3$ or $2x - 1 = -3$. The first equality gives $2x = 4$, that is, $x = 2$. The second gives $2x = -2$, that is, $x = -1$. Therefore the solutions are $x = 2$ and $x = -1$.
