What are the real solutions of $\dfrac{x + 4}{x - 7} = 5$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{31}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{\dfrac{39}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{- \dfrac{25}{4}\right\}$
- $\left\{7\right\}$

## Explanation

We exclude $x=7$ before multiplying by the denominator. The resulting linear equation is $4 x = 39$ and gives $\left\{\dfrac{39}{4}\right\}$.
