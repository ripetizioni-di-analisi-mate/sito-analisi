What are the solutions of the following system?
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 4 \leq 0 \\[2pt]
2x + 1 > 0
\end{cases}
$$

- $-\dfrac{1}{2} < x \leq 2$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2} \leq x \leq 2$
- $x \geq -2$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2} < x < 2$

## Explanation

The first inequality is equivalent to $x^2 \leq 4$, so its solutions are $-2 \leq x \leq 2$. The second is equivalent to $2x > -1$, so its solutions are $x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The two inequalities must be satisfied simultaneously, therefore the solutions of the system are the **intersection** of the two sets. Since $-2 < -\dfrac{1}{2}$, the intersection is $-\dfrac{1}{2} < x \leq 2$. The endpoint $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ is excluded because the direction of the second inequality is $>$, while the endpoint $2$ is included because the direction of the first is $\leq$.
