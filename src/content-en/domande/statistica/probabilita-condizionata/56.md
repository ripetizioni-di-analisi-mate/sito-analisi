Only $P(A)=\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $P(B)=\dfrac{1}{2}$ are known. What value must $P(A\cap B)$ have for the two events to be independent?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

Under independence, the proportion of $A$ among $B$ equals the proportion in the whole population, $\dfrac15$. Since $B$ accounts for half the population, the proportion satisfying both conditions is $\dfrac15\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac1{10}$. This is the intersection required by the product criterion. It is also compatible with the marginals because it is smaller than both.
