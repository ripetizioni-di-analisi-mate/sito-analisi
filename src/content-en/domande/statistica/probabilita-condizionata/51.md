Line A makes one tenth of all parts and B the other nine tenths. A sensor gives a signal three times as often on a part from A as on a part from B. The same part is tested twice. The tests are independent conditional on the line and have the same rates. Which origin is more likely after two positive signals?

- A and B are equally likely
- A with probability $3/4$
- B with probability $3/4$
- B with probability $9/10$

## Explanation

Before testing, the odds for A versus B are $1:9$. Conditional independence makes each signal multiply those odds by $3$. After two signals the odds are $9:9$, or one to one.
