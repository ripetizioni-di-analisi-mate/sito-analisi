Two production lines make equal numbers of parts. The rejection probabilities are $2\%$ on line A and $5\%$ on line B. Given that a part was rejected, what is the probability that it came from A?

- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}$

## Explanation

The lines have equal outputs, so consider $100$ parts from each. On average these parts include two rejections from A and five from B. Given that the part was rejected, the possible cases are these seven, two of which come from A, so the required probability is $\dfrac27$. A's initial share is one half, but its lower rejection rate reduces its share among rejected parts.
