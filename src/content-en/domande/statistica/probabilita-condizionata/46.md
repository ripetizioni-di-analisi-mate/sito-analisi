Of $90$ requests, $50$ arrive online. Among the online requests, $10$ are rejected and $20$ require manual review. Eight rejected online requests required that review. Given that an online request was rejected, what is the probability that it was reviewed manually?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{25}$

## Explanation

The condition gives two facts at once: online submission and rejection. The reference group is therefore the $10$ rejected online requests. Of these, $8$ required manual review, giving $\dfrac8{10}=\dfrac45$. Dividing by $20$ would reverse the roles of review and rejection among online requests and give the probability of rejection given review.
