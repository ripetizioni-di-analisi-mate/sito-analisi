One group accounts for half of all customers and has purchase rate $\dfrac{1}{5}$. The reported overall purchase rate is $\dfrac{7}{10}$. What follows?

- The data are inconsistent because the required rate for the other group would exceed one.
- The other group's rate is one half.
- The other group's rate is three fifths.
- The other group's rate equals the overall rate.

## Explanation

The first group has share $\dfrac12$ and contributes $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac1{10}$ to the overall purchase rate. To reach the stated rate $\dfrac7{10}$, the other group would need to contribute $\dfrac6{10}$ of purchases relative to all customers. Since it accounts for only half the customers, its internal rate would have to be $\dfrac{\dfrac6{10}}{\dfrac12}=\dfrac65$. This exceeds one, so the data are inconsistent. Even if every other customer purchased, the total could reach at most $\dfrac35$.
