Two pumps work independently with probabilities $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Calculate the probability that at least one pump works.

- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

At least one pump works in every case except one, the case in which both pumps fail. The individual failure probabilities are $1-\dfrac34=\dfrac14$ and $1-\dfrac23=\dfrac13$. Since the operating events are independent, the two failures are independent as well, so both pumps fail with probability $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac1{12}$. Subtracting from one gives $\dfrac{11}{12}$, which exceeds the probability that each individual pump works.
