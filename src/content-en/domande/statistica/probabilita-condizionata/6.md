Two pumps work independently with probabilities $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Calculate the probability that at least one pump works.

- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The circuit operates even when both pumps work. The only excluded case is therefore simultaneous failure. The individual failure probabilities are $1-\dfrac34=\dfrac14$ and $1-\dfrac23=\dfrac13$. The failures are independent, as are the operating events, so the circuit stops with probability $\dfrac1{12}$. Subtracting from one gives $\dfrac{11}{12}$, which exceeds each individual reliability.
