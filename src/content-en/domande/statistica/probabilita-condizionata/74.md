Three events $A$, $B$ and $C$ have positive probabilities and satisfy $P(A\cap B\cap C)=P(A)P(B)P(C)$. Which further check would establish mutual independence?

- Verify that all three events have equal probabilities.
- Check that the triple intersection has positive probability.
- Verify the factorization for each of the three pairs.
- Verify the factorization for any one pair.

## Explanation

Mutual independence requires the intersection of every subset of at least two events to have probability equal to the product of its marginal probabilities. For three events these subsets are the three pairs and the triple. The triple factorization is given, so compare $P(A\cap B)$, $P(A\cap C)$ and $P(B\cap C)$ with their respective products. Mutual independence follows only when every identity holds.
