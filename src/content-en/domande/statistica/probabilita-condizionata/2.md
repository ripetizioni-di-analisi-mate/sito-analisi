A parcel is sent for manual inspection with probability $\dfrac{3}{10}$. Given that it is sent for inspection, it is reopened with probability $\dfrac{2}{5}$. Calculate the probability that a parcel is both sent for manual inspection and reopened.

- $\dfrac{3}{25}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Let $M$ denote manual inspection and $R$ reopening. The rate $\dfrac25$ applies only to parcels sent for manual inspection, which account for $\dfrac3{10}$ of all parcels. Hence $P(M\cap R)=P(M)P(R\mid M)=\dfrac3{10}\cdot\dfrac25=\dfrac3{25}$. Out of $100$ parcels, the model predicts $30$ manual inspections and $12$ reopened parcels within that group.
