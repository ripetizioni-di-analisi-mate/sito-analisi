Two systems use the same three components, each of reliability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, with mutually independent operating events. One system requires all the components and the other requires at least one. What is the difference in their reliabilities?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The series system requires all three components to work and has reliability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\dfrac18$. The parallel system stops only if all three fail, an event that likewise has probability $\dfrac18$. The parallel reliability is therefore $\dfrac78$ and the difference is $\dfrac78-\dfrac18=\dfrac34$. The difference corresponds to the six mixed configurations in which at least one component works and at least one fails.
