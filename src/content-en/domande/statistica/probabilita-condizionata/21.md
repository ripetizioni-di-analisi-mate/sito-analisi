In a probability tree the first branch $B$ has probability $\dfrac{3}{5}$. The only branches from node $B$ are $A$ and $A^c$, and branch $A$ has probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. What is the probability of leaf $B\cap A^c$?

- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The two branches from $B$ describe complementary cases at the same node. Hence $P(A^c\mid B)=1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$. To reach the required leaf, follow $B$ and then its branch $A^c$, giving $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac9{20}$. The value $\dfrac34$ gives only the proportion within $B$. The leaf probability is measured relative to all paths in the tree.
