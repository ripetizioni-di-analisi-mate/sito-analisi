Of all requests, $30\%$ are urgent. An attachment is required for $40\%$ of urgent requests and $10\%$ of the others. Is the overall rate of requests requiring an attachment closer to $10\%$ or to $40\%$?

- It is below $10\%$ because both rates must be weighted.
- To $40\%$ because it is the higher rate.
- To $10\%$ because the group with that rate accounts for $70\%$ of requests.
- It is equally far from both rates because there are two groups.

## Explanation

Out of $100$ requests, the model predicts $30$ urgent and $70$ nonurgent requests. The two groups have $30\cdot0.40=12$ and $70\cdot0.10=7$ requests requiring an attachment, respectively, giving $19$ out of $100$ overall. The rate $19\%$ is nine percentage points from $10\%$ and twenty-one from $40\%$. The average is closer to the rate for the larger group.
