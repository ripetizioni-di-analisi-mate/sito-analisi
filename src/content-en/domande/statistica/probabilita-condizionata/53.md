A filter detects five of nine genuinely fraudulent transactions and also flags two legitimate transactions. What is its sensitivity?

- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$

## Explanation

Sensitivity concerns only genuinely fraudulent transactions. The filter flags five of these nine and leaves four unflagged, so sensitivity is $\dfrac59$. The two legitimate transactions it flags lie outside the fraudulent group. They would enter the denominator of positive predictive value, which compares the five flagged fraudulent transactions with all seven flagged transactions.
