Events $A$ and $B$ are independent and have probabilities $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$. What is $P(A^c\cap B)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The outcomes in $B$ split into those belonging to $A$ and those belonging to $A^c$. Independence gives $P(A\cap B)=\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac16$. Subtracting this contribution from $P(B)$ gives $\dfrac14-\dfrac16=\dfrac1{12}$. Alternatively, multiply $P(A^c)=\dfrac13$ directly by $P(B)=\dfrac14$, since taking the complement of an event preserves independence.
