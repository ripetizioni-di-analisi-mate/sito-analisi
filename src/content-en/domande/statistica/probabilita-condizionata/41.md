An application can receive only one priority level. Both urgent and standard priority have positive probability. How are the events “urgent” and “standard” related?

- They can occur together and are independent.
- They can occur together and are dependent.
- They are disjoint and independent.
- They are disjoint and dependent.

## Explanation

An application can receive only one priority level, so “urgent” and “standard” have empty intersection and are disjoint. Independence would require the intersection probability to equal the product of the marginal probabilities. That product is positive by assumption, while the intersection has probability zero. The events are therefore dependent. Knowing that an application is urgent rules out standard priority.
