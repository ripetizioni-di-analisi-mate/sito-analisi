Three devices operate with probabilities $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{4}{5}$. Their operating events are mutually independent. Calculate the probability that the system stops if operation requires all three devices to work.

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{40}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{39}{40}$

## Explanation

All three devices must operate simultaneously for the system to work. Mutual independence allows us to multiply the three reliabilities, obtaining $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac45=\dfrac3{10}$. The system stops if at least one device fails, the complement of simultaneous operation. Its probability is $1-\dfrac3{10}=\dfrac7{10}$. Failure of all three devices is just one way the system can stop.
