An urn contains three white tokens and two black tokens. After each draw, the token is replaced and the urn is mixed. What is the probability of drawing a black token followed by a white one?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{6}{25}$

## Explanation

Black has probability $\dfrac25$ on the first draw. After replacement the urn again has three white tokens out of five, so the conditional probability of white on the second draw is $\dfrac35$. The black–white sequence has probability $\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac6{25}$. The event “one token of each color” would also include the reverse order. Here the order is specified, so use just one path.
