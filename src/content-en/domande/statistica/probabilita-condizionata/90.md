Three groups forming a partition are used to find the overall probability of delay. One group has probability zero. How should its contribution be handled?

- Give the group the same probability as the others.
- Assign probability zero to the intersection of that group with delay.
- Also exclude a positive-probability group to retain two groups.
- Require the conditional delay probability within that group to equal zero.

## Explanation

Let $G$ denote the group of probability zero. The event “delay within group $G$” is contained in $G$ and therefore has probability zero. In contrast, $P(\text{delay}\mid G)$ is undefined because its denominator would be $P(G)=0$. We can add the intersection probabilities directly, including the zero term. The conditional-probability version uses only positive-probability groups and gives the same total.
