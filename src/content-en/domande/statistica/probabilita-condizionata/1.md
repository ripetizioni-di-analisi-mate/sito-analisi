Of $120$ students, $40$ attend the morning section. Of the morning students, $10$ arrived late, as did $15$ students from the other sections. Calculate the probability of a late arrival for a student chosen at random from the morning section.

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{24}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

Selection is uniform within the morning section, so the denominator is its enrollment. The numerator counts the late arrivals in the same group. This gives $\dfrac{10}{40}=\dfrac{1}{4}=25\%$. Dividing by $120$ would give the probability of the intersection among all students. The $15$ late arrivals in other sections would be needed to calculate the overall late arrival rate.
