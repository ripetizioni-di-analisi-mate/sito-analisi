A fair die is rolled. If it shows $1$ or $2$, a ball is drawn from an urn with $3$ red and $2$ white balls; otherwise a ball is drawn from an urn with $1$ red and $3$ white balls. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{17}{40}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{11}{30}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The die selects the first urn with probability $\dfrac26=\dfrac13$ and the second with probability $\dfrac23$. The two choices form a partition. In the first urn red has probability $\dfrac35$, in the second $\dfrac14$. By the law of total probability, $P(\text{red})=\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac35+\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac15+\dfrac16=\dfrac{11}{30}$. The simple average $\dfrac{17}{40}$ would give the two urns equal weight, while the second is chosen twice as often. The value $\dfrac49$ pools the balls of the two urns as if they were in a single urn.
