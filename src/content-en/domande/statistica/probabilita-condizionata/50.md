Line A supplies $20\%$ of all parts and line B supplies the rest. The rejection rates are $5\%$ on A and $9\%$ on B. What is the overall rejection rate?

- $7\%$
- $9\%$
- $8.2\%$
- $5\%$

## Explanation

The rate $5\%$ applies to parts from line A, which account for $20\%$ of the total. Their contribution to the overall rejection rate is $0.20\cdot0.05=0.010$. Line B supplies the rest and contributes $0.80\cdot0.09=0.072$. The sum is $0.082$, or $8.2\%$. It is closer to $9\%$ because B produces four times as many parts as A.
