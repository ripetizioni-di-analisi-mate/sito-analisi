A sorting machine flags tiles with a suspected defect. Which description gives its positive predictive value?

- The proportion of defective tiles in all production before inspection.
- The proportion of unflagged tiles among nondefective tiles.
- The proportion of genuinely defective tiles among flagged tiles.
- The proportion of flagged tiles among genuinely defective tiles.

## Explanation

After a positive result, the reference group consists of all flagged tiles. Positive predictive value is the fraction of genuinely defective tiles in that group. The denominator includes true positives and false positives, while the numerator counts only true positives. Dividing by all defective tiles would calculate sensitivity, the probability of being flagged given a defect.
