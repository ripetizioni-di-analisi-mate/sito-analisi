A website accounts for $75\%$ of all orders, with the rest placed at a counter. Complaints arise from $4\%$ of website orders and $12\%$ of counter orders. Given that an order generated a complaint, calculate the probability that it was placed at the counter.

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{100}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The reference group is the set of orders with a complaint. Out of $100$ orders, the model assigns $75\cdot0.04=3$ complaints to the website and $25\cdot0.12=3$ to the counter. There are $6$ complaints in total, half of which concern the counter, giving $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$. Its complaint rate, $12\%$, is three times that of the website but applies to one third as many orders.
