Of $100$ items, $30$ are fragile, $25$ are bulky and $10$ have both characteristics. Given that an item is fragile or bulky, what is the probability that it is fragile?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{6}{11}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$

## Explanation

The condition “fragile or bulky” includes items with both characteristics. Adding $30+25$ would count those $10$ items twice, so the reference group contains $30+25-10=45$ items. All $30$ fragile items belong to the group, giving probability $\dfrac{30}{45}=\dfrac23$. The ratio $\dfrac{30}{100}$ refers to the whole batch before we know that at least one characteristic is present.
