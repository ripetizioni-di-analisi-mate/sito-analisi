The defective fraction is $2\%$ in group A and $10\%$ in group B. The same inspection has sensitivity $0.9$ and false positive rate $0.1$ in both groups. In which group is a flagged part more likely to be defective?

- The probabilities are equal because the false positive rates are equal.
- The probabilities are equal because the sensitivities are equal.
- Group B.
- Group A.

## Explanation

Of $1000$ parts in group A, $20$ are defective and $980$ are nondefective. The inspection flags $18$ defective parts and $98$ nondefective parts. In group B the corresponding counts are $90$ and $90$. The defective fraction among flagged parts therefore rises from $\dfrac{18}{116}=\dfrac{9}{58}$ to $\dfrac{90}{180}=\dfrac12$. In group B the prevalence, that is, the defective fraction, is higher, so for batches of the same size there are more true positives (flagged defective parts) and fewer false positives (flagged nondefective parts).
