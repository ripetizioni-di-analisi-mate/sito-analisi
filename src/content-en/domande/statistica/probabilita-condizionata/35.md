The defective fraction is $2\%$ in group A and $10\%$ in group B. The same inspection has sensitivity $0.9$ and false positive rate $0.1$ in both groups. In which group is a flagged part more likely to be defective?

- The probabilities are equal because the false positive rates are equal.
- The probabilities are equal because the sensitivities are equal.
- Group B.
- Group A.

## Explanation

Of $1000$ parts in group A, $20$ are defective and $980$ are nondefective. The inspection flags $18$ defective parts and $98$ nondefective parts. In group B the corresponding counts are $90$ and $90$. The defective fraction among flagged parts therefore rises from $\dfrac{18}{116}$ to $\dfrac{90}{180}=\dfrac12$. For batches of the same size, the higher prevalence in group B increases true positives and decreases false positives.
