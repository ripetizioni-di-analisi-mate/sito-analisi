Three groups of applications have shares $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{6}$. The probabilities of an application being flagged in the three groups are $\dfrac{1}{5}$, $\dfrac{3}{10}$ and $\dfrac{3}{5}$, respectively. Calculate the probability that a flagged application belongs to the second group.

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

The reference group consists of flagged applications. The contributions from the three groups are $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac1{10}$, $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac3{10}=\dfrac1{10}$ and $\dfrac16\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac1{10}$. Divide the second contribution by their sum, $\dfrac3{10}$, to obtain $\dfrac13$. The groups have different sizes and different flagging rates, but each contributes the same proportion of flagged applications to the entire population.
