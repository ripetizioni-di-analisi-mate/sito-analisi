Departments A and B initially each produce half of all orders. Their return rates are $4\%$ and $12\%$. The overall rate then rises even though both departmental rates remain fixed. What change in composition is necessary if these are the only departments?

- B’s share of orders must decrease
- The total number of orders must double
- B’s share of orders must increase
- Both departments must keep equal shares

## Explanation

If B’s share is $w$, the overall rate is $0.04(1-w)+0.12w=0.04+0.08w$. It is strictly increasing in $w$, regardless of the total number of orders.
