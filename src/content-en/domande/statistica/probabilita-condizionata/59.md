Three identical components work independently with probability $p$, where $0<p<1$. Observing the system reveals that exactly one component has failed. Given this information, what is the probability that it is the third?

- $\dfrac13$
- $1-p$ for every $p$
- $p^2(1-p)$
- $\dfrac{1}{1+p}$

## Explanation

Each possible location of the single failure has probability $(1-p)p^2$. Conditioning on exactly one failure divides the probability of one location by the sum of the three equal probabilities.
