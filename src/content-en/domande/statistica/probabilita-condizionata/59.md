Three components operate mutually independently and fail with probabilities $\dfrac{1}{5}$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1}{10}$. What is the probability that only the third fails?

- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}$

## Explanation

“Only” requires two working components and one failure. The first two components work with probabilities $1-\dfrac15=\dfrac45$ and $1-\dfrac12=\dfrac12$, respectively. For the third, use the failure probability $\dfrac1{10}$. The specified states remain independent when complements are used, so their product is $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac1{10}=\dfrac1{25}$.
