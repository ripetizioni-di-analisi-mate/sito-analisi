Workshops $A$ and $B$ have equal production shares. The rejection rate for $B$ is three times the positive rate for $A$. What is the probability that a rejected part came from $B$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Let $r>0$ be the rejection rate for A. Since the workshops produce equal quantities, their contributions to overall rejection are $\dfrac r2$ and $\dfrac{3r}{2}$. Among rejected parts, B's share is therefore $\dfrac{3r}{r+3r}=\dfrac34$. The rate $r$ cancels because we need the ratio of the two contributions, whose production weights are equal.
