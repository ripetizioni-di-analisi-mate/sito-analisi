Three lines produce $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of all parts, respectively. Their rejection rates are $\dfrac{1}{10}$, $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\dfrac{2}{5}$. Calculate the probability that a part chosen at random from the combined output is rejected.

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{30}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Each part comes from exactly one line, so the rejected parts from the three lines form disjoint groups. Their contributions to total production are $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac1{10}=\dfrac1{20}$, $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac1{20}$ and $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac25=\dfrac1{10}$, summing to $\dfrac4{20}=\dfrac15$. Although the third line makes a quarter of the parts, it accounts for half of the rejected parts because it has the highest rejection rate.
