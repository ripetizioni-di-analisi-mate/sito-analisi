An inspection has sensitivity $0.75$ and specificity $0.80$. If the defective fraction is $0.20$, what is the probability that a randomly chosen part is classified correctly?

- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{31}{40}$
- $\dfrac{16}{25}$
- $\dfrac{79}{100}$

## Explanation

Correct classification means flagging a defective part or leaving a nondefective part unflagged. These cases belong to disjoint groups. Their probabilities are $0.20\cdot0.75=0.15$ and $0.80\cdot0.80=0.64$, respectively, giving an overall probability of correct classification of $0.79$. Sensitivity and specificity receive different weights because nondefective parts account for $80\%$ of production.
