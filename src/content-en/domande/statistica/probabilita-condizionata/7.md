Two cards are drawn without replacement from a box containing five red cards and three blue cards. Calculate the probability that their colors differ.

- $\dfrac{15}{56}$
- $\dfrac{13}{28}$
- $\dfrac{15}{32}$
- $\dfrac{15}{28}$

## Explanation

Different colors occur in two disjoint sequences. A red first draw leaves three blue cards among seven, so that path has probability $\dfrac58\cdot\dfrac37=\dfrac{15}{56}$. A blue first draw leaves five red cards among seven, and the product $\dfrac38\cdot\dfrac57$ gives the same probability. Their sum is $\dfrac{15}{28}$. The denominator for the second draw is seven because the first card remains out of the box.
