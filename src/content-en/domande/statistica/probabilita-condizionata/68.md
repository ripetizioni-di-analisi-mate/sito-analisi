In a hypothetical batch, $10\%$ of parts are defective. An inspection has sensitivity $0.8$ and specificity one. What can be concluded about a flagged part?

- Its probability of being defective equals the initial defective fraction.
- It is defective with probability one.
- Its probability of being defective equals the sensitivity.
- It is nondefective with probability one.

## Explanation

Specificity one means every nondefective part is unflagged, so false positives have probability zero. Flagged defective parts have probability $0.10\cdot0.8=0.08$, which is positive. The flagged group therefore has positive probability and consists entirely of defective parts, that is, $P(D\mid T)=1$. Sensitivity below one means some defective parts are unflagged.
