Carrier A handles $40\%$ of all deliveries and carrier B handles the rest. On-time deliveries account for $80\%$ of all deliveries and $65\%$ of carrier A's deliveries. Calculate carrier B's on-time rate.

- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{27}{50}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

On-time deliveries handled by A account for $0.40\cdot0.65=0.26$ of all deliveries. Since all on-time deliveries account for $0.80$, those handled by B account for $0.80-0.26=0.54$. To express this proportion within group B, divide by $0.60$, giving $\dfrac{0.54}{0.60}=0.90$. Out of $100$ deliveries, $54$ arrive on time among the $60$ handled by B.
