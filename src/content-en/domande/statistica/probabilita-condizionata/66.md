Three machines produce $600$, $300$ and $100$ parts, with defect rates of $2\%$, $5\%$ and $8\%$, respectively. Given that a randomly chosen part is defective, what is the probability that it came from the first or third machine?

- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}$

## Explanation

Use the output counts to calculate the defective parts from each machine, obtaining $600\cdot0.02=12$, $300\cdot0.05=15$ and $100\cdot0.08=8$. The condition restricts the choice to the $35$ defective parts. Of these, $12+8=20$ come from the first or third machine, so the probability is $\dfrac{20}{35}=\dfrac47$. These machines supply $70\%$ of all parts, a different share from their share among defective parts.
