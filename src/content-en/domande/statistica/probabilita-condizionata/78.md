Two marbles are drawn without replacement from an urn containing $2$ black and $3$ white marbles. Given that they have the same color, what is the probability that they are black?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

If the colors match, the only possible paths are black–black and white–white. Their probabilities are $\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac1{10}$ and $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac24=\dfrac3{10}$. To condition on matching colors, divide the black–black probability by the sum of both probabilities. This gives $\dfrac{\dfrac1{10}}{\dfrac4{10}}=\dfrac14$. Both colors remain possible after conditioning, but the sequences have different probabilities.
