For a fair die, let $A=\{1,2\}$ and $B=\{1,3,5\}$. What is $P(B\mid A)$, and how are the events related?

- It equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and the events are dependent.
- It equals $\dfrac{1}{6}$ and the events are independent.
- It equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the events are dependent.
- It equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the events are independent.

## Explanation

When $A$ occurs, only faces $1$ and $2$ remain possible and they are equally likely. Only $1$ belongs to $B$, so $P(B\mid A)=\dfrac12$. Before we learn $A$, event $B$ contains three of the six faces and has the same probability $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$. Since $P(A)>0$, equality of the two probabilities suffices to conclude that the events are independent.
