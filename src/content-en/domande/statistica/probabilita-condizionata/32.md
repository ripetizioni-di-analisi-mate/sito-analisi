Domestic orders account for $60\%$ of all orders. Domestic orders arrive on time with probability $\dfrac{5}{6}$ and other orders with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Given that an order arrived late, what is the probability that it is domestic?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Subtract the on-time rates from one to obtain the delay rates, $\dfrac16$ for domestic orders and $\dfrac12$ for the others. Their contributions to the overall probability of delay are $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac16=\dfrac1{10}$ and $\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac15$. Late orders have total probability $\dfrac3{10}$, and the domestic share among them is $\dfrac13$. The value $\dfrac16$ refers to conditioning in the opposite direction.
