A sample contains $40$ online receipts and $60$ counter receipts. Corrections were made to $8$ online receipts and $12$ counter receipts. What is the ratio of the correction probability among online receipts to the correction probability among all receipts?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The correction rate among online receipts is $\dfrac8{40}=\dfrac15$. For all receipts, add the $20$ corrected cases and divide by $100$ to obtain $\dfrac15$ again. The required ratio is therefore one. The rates agree because the correction rate at the counter is also $\dfrac{12}{60}=\dfrac15$. Knowing that a receipt was issued online leaves the relative frequency of correction unchanged in this sample.
