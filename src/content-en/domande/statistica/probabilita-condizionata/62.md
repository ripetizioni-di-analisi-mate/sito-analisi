In a class, $60\%$ of students submitted an assignment. Among those who submitted it, $25\%$ passed the check. What is the probability that a student submitted the assignment and failed the check?

- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$

## Explanation

The pass rate is given among students who submitted the assignment. Its complement within that group is $\dfrac34$. This rate applies only to $60\%$ of students. To express the probability relative to the whole class, multiply $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac9{20}$. Out of $100$ students, we would expect $60$ to submit the assignment and $15$ of those to pass the check. The remaining $45$ submitted it and failed.
