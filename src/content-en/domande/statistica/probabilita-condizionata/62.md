In a class, $60\%$ of students submitted an assignment. Of all students, $15\%$ submitted the assignment and passed the check. Among the students who submitted it, what fraction failed the check?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{17}{20}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The $15\%$ is a fraction of the whole class, while the question concerns only the students who submitted. By the definition of conditional probability, the fraction passing the check among those who submitted is $\dfrac{0.15}{0.60}=\dfrac14$. The complement within the same group gives $1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$. Out of $100$ students, $60$ submitted and $15$ of them passed the check, so $45$ out of $60$ failed, and $\dfrac{45}{60}=\dfrac34$.
