Three events $A$, $B$ and $C$ are mutually independent, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is $P(A\cap B^c\cap C)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Event $A\cap C$ splits into the outcomes with $B$ and those with $B^c$. Its probability is $\dfrac14$ by pairwise independence. The first group has probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\dfrac18$ by mutual independence. The second therefore has probability $\dfrac14-\dfrac18=\dfrac18$. Subtraction selects exactly the outcomes in which $A$ and $C$ occur and $B$ does not.
