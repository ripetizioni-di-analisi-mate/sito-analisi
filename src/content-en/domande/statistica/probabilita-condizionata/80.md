Two lines contribute $0.03$ and $0.02$, respectively, to the overall probability of rejection. The first line produces $60\%$ of all parts. What is the rejection rate among parts from the first line?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{30}$
- $\dfrac{3}{100}$

## Explanation

Let $L_1$ denote production by the first line and $S$ rejection. The contribution $0.03$ is $P(S\cap L_1)$, a proportion of all production. For the rate within the line, the reference group is the $60\%$ of parts produced by that line. This gives $P(S\mid L_1)=\dfrac{0.03}{0.60}=0.05$. Using all rejected parts as the denominator, the ratio $\dfrac{0.03}{0.05}$ would instead give the probability of the source given rejection.
