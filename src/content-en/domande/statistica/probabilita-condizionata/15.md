A fair die is rolled. Let $A=\{1,2,3\}$ and $B=\{1,3,5,6\}$. What is $P(A^c\mid B)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The condition restricts the outcomes to the four faces in $B$, namely $1$, $3$, $5$ and $6$. Since the die is fair, the remaining faces are equally likely. Faces $5$ and $6$ also belong to $A^c$, so the probability is $\dfrac24=\dfrac12$. Face $4$ belongs to $A^c$ but is excluded because it is outside the reference group.
