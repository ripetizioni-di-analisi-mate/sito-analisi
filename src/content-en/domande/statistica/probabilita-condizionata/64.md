Two tokens are drawn at random without replacement from a box containing three red tokens and two green tokens. Before the first draw is observed, what is the probability that the second token is green?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The second draw is green in two disjoint cases. If the first is red, both green tokens remain among four tokens and the sequence has probability $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac24=\dfrac3{10}$. If the first is green, one of four remaining tokens is green and the sequence has probability $\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac1{10}$. The sum is $\dfrac25$. Before the first outcome is known, both paths ending in green must be included.
