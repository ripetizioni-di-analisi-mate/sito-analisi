Two departments produce all the parts. Their rejection rates are $\dfrac{1}{10}$ and $\dfrac{3}{10}$, and the overall rate is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. What share of production comes from the second department?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Let $p$ be the second department's share. The first department produces the rest, so the overall rate is $\dfrac1{10}(1-p)+\dfrac3{10}p=\dfrac1{10}+\dfrac15p$. Subtracting $\dfrac1{10}$ from the given rate $\dfrac14$ leaves $\dfrac3{20}$, which equals $\dfrac15p$. Dividing by $\dfrac15$ gives $p=\dfrac34$. The overall rate is closer to the second department's rate, consistent with its larger share.
