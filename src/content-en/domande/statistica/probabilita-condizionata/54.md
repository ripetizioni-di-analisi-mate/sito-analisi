Devices A and B work independently with probabilities $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{4}{5}$. Given that A works, what is the probability that B has failed?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

Let $F_A$ and $F_B$ denote operation of the devices. Independence of $F_A$ and $F_B$ gives $P(F_B\mid F_A)=\dfrac45$. Within group $F_A$, operation and failure of B are complementary, so $P(F_B^c\mid F_A)=1-\dfrac45=\dfrac15$. A's reliability guarantees that the condition has positive probability. Its numerical value would cancel in the ratio.
