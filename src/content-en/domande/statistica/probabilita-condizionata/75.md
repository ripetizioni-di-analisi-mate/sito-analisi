Channel A handles $25\%$ of deliveries. The on-time rate is $75\%$ in channel A and $95\%$ in channel B. What is the overall probability of a delay?

- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

Within channel A, delay is the complement of on-time arrival and has rate $1-0.75=0.25$. In B the rate is $1-0.95=0.05$. Weighting the two rates by their channel shares gives $0.25\cdot0.25+0.75\cdot0.05=0.0625+0.0375=0.10$. The result is closer to B's rate because three quarters of deliveries use that channel.
