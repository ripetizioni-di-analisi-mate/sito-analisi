Every order is placed online or at a counter. To calculate the overall return rate, which pair directly gives a partition for weighting the return rates by channel?

- Counter orders and orders that were not returned.
- Returned online orders and returned counter orders.
- Online orders and counter orders.
- Online orders and returned orders.

## Explanation

A partition assigns each order to exactly one group and covers the whole population. The online and counter channels satisfy these conditions because every order uses one of the two. Multiply each channel's return rate by its share and add. Returned online orders and returned counter orders are disjoint, but leave out every order that was kept.
