Of $180$ applications, $45$ are urgent, $54$ require review and $18$ are both urgent and in need of review. What is the probability that an application requiring review is urgent?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The application is chosen from the $54$ requiring review. Of these, the $18$ in the intersection are urgent, so the ratio is $\dfrac{18}{54}=\dfrac13$. The number $45$ counts all urgent applications and would be the denominator for the probability of review given urgency. Reversing the condition changes the reference group while preserving the numerator.
