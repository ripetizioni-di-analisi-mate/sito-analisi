Only candidates who pass the first assessment can take the final one. Of all candidates, $75\%$ pass the first, and $40\%$ of those also pass the final assessment. What fraction of all candidates pass the first assessment and fail the final one?

- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

The final assessment is open only to those who pass the first. Within that group the probability of failing the final assessment is $1-0.40=0.60$. The eligible group represents $75\%$ of the total, so the share of all candidates who pass the first assessment and fail the final one is $0.75\cdot0.60=0.45=\dfrac9{20}$. The denominator for $60\%$ includes only candidates admitted to the final stage, while the question asks for a fraction of all candidates.
