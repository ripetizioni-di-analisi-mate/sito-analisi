In a shop, $30\%$ of customers buy bread and $20\%$ buy milk. Among the customers who buy milk, $60\%$ also buy bread. Among the customers who buy bread, what fraction also buy milk?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{25}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Let $A$ be the event “buys bread” and $B$ the event “buys milk.” By the multiplication rule, the fraction of customers who buy both is $P(A\cap B)=P(B)P(A\mid B)=0.20\cdot0.60=0.12$. By the definition of conditional probability, $P(B\mid A)=\dfrac{0.12}{0.30}=\dfrac25$. The value $\dfrac35$ is $P(A\mid B)$, whose reference group is the customers who buy milk. The value $\dfrac{3}{25}=0.12$ is the fraction of all customers who buy both.
