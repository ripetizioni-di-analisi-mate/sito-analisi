Express shipments account for $40\%$ of all shipments, and $15\%$ of express shipments are late. Which probability is obtained by subtracting $0.15$ from one?

- The probability that a shipment is both express and on time.
- The on-time probability among all shipments.
- The probability of on-time arrival given express shipment.
- The probability of a delay given standard shipment.

## Explanation

The rate $15\%$ is calculated among express shipments only. Within this group, delay and on-time arrival are complementary, so the on-time rate is $1-0.15=0.85$. Out of $100$ shipments, the model predicts $40$ express shipments, of which $34$ arrive on time. The ratio $\dfrac{34}{40}$ gives $85\%$, while $\dfrac{34}{100}$ would give the proportion of all shipments that are express and on time.
