Two production lines make $90\%$ and $10\%$ of all parts. Their rejection rates are $2\%$ and $12\%$, respectively. Which line is more likely to have produced a part known to have been rejected?

- The data allow the rejection rates to be compared but do not determine the more likely source.
- The second line.
- The first line.
- The two lines are equally likely.

## Explanation

Compare the rejected parts contributed by the two lines across all production. Out of $1000$ parts, the first line makes $900$ and rejects $900\cdot0.02=18$. The second makes $100$ and rejects $100\cdot0.12=12$. Given rejection, their shares are $\dfrac{18}{30}$ and $\dfrac{12}{30}$, so the first line is more likely. Its lower rejection rate applies to nine times as many parts.
