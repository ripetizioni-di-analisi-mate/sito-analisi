A machine uses mode A in $60\%$ of its cycles. In mode A, $30\%$ of cycles stop, and half the stopped mode A cycles need a manual restart. What is the probability that a cycle is in mode A, stops and needs a manual restart?

- $\dfrac{9}{50}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{9}{100}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

Mode A cycles account for $0.60$ of all cycles. Multiplying by the stop rate in this mode, $0.30$, gives $0.18$, the proportion of all cycles that are in mode A and stop. Half of these need a manual restart, giving $0.18\cdot\dfrac12=0.09=\dfrac9{100}$. The final factor refers to cycles satisfying both previous conditions.
