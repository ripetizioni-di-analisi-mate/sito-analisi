Two fair dice are rolled independently. Given that the first die shows $2$, what is the probability that the sum is at least $6$?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Given the first result, the sum is $2$ plus the second die's result. To reach at least $6$, the second result must be at least $4$. The three possible faces are $4$, $5$ and $6$. By independence, knowing the first result leaves the six faces of the second die equally likely, giving probability $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$.
