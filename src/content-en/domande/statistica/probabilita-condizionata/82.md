An inspection flags $60\%$ of defective parts and $20\%$ of nondefective parts. If $25\%$ of parts are defective, what is the probability that a flagged part is nondefective?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Out of $100$ parts, the model predicts $25$ defective and $75$ nondefective parts. The inspection flags $25\cdot0.60=15$ defective parts and $75\cdot0.20=15$ nondefective parts. Of all $30$ flagged parts, $15$ are nondefective, so the required probability is $\dfrac{15}{30}=\dfrac12$. The flagging rate for nondefective parts is lower but applies to a group three times as large.
