An application belongs to A with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$. The approval probability is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ for A and $\dfrac{1}{10}$ for other applications. What is the probability that an approved application belongs to A?

- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

Among all applications, those in A that are approved have probability $\dfrac15\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac1{10}$. Other approved applications have probability $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac1{10}=\dfrac2{25}$. All approved applications therefore have probability $\dfrac1{10}+\dfrac2{25}=\dfrac9{50}$. The share of A within this group is $\dfrac{\dfrac1{10}}{\dfrac9{50}}=\dfrac59$. Despite its smaller size, A contributes more approvals because its approval rate is five times as high.
