A reviewer compares two groups A and B, both with positive probability and together forming a partition. A signal $S$ has probability $0.6$ in A and $0.2$ in B. Which prior odds $P(A)/P(B)$ make A and B equally likely after the signal?

- $\dfrac13$
- $3$
- $1$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Equal posterior probabilities require the two joint signal probabilities to match: $0.6P(A)=0.2P(B)$. The signal favours A by a factor of three, requiring prior odds of one to three.
