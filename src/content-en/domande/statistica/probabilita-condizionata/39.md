A system operates when at least two of its three components work. The components operate mutually independently, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is the system's reliability?

- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

Every configuration of the three components has probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\dfrac18$ because operation and failure each have probability $\dfrac12$ and the component states are independent. Three configurations have exactly two working components, one for each choice of the failed component. Include the configuration in which all three work. The system therefore operates with probability $\dfrac48=\dfrac12$.
