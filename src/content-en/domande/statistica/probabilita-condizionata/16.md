A system records $80$ operations, of which $20$ take place at night. Of these, $8$ generate an alert. If one operation is chosen at random from all $80$, what is the probability that it took place at night and generated an alert?

- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

The operation is chosen from all $80$ records, each equally likely to be selected. The favorable cases are the $8$ operations with both characteristics. The intersection therefore has probability $\dfrac8{80}=\dfrac1{10}$. The ratio $\dfrac8{20}$ would instead give the alert rate given that an operation took place at night.
