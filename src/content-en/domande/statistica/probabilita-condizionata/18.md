Given $P(A)=\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $P(B)=\dfrac{1}{2}$, which of the following is a possible value of $P(A\cap B)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$

## Explanation

The intersection is contained in both events, so its probability is at most $\min\{\dfrac34,\dfrac12\}=\dfrac12$. Also, $P(A\cup B)\le1$ implies $P(A\cap B)\ge\dfrac34+\dfrac12-1=\dfrac14$. The value $\dfrac38$ is possible. In eighths, the probabilities of the overlap, $A$ only, $B$ only and the region outside both are $3$, $3$, $1$ and $1$, respectively. They are all nonnegative and sum to eight eighths.
