A hypothetical inspection has specificity $0.96$. Among nondefective parts, what is the probability of a positive result?

- $\dfrac{24}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{96}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$

## Explanation

The reference group consists of nondefective parts. Within this group, a positive result and a negative result are complementary. Specificity gives the second probability, so the first is $1-0.96=0.04=\dfrac1{25}$. Among $100$ nondefective parts, the model predicts four positive results. Determining what fraction of all positive results come from nondefective parts would also require data on defective parts.
