Two cards are drawn without replacement from a deck of nine cards, two of them green. Given that the first is green, what is the probability that the second is also green?

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$

## Explanation

After a green first card is observed, the deck contains seven cards of other colors and just one green card. The second card is chosen uniformly from the remaining eight, so the probability is $\dfrac18$. The initial proportion $\dfrac29$ applies to the first draw or to the second before the first result is known. Here we know the color removed, so update both counts.
