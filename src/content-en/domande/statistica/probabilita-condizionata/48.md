Two cards are drawn without replacement from a deck of nine cards, three of them green. Only the second card is observed, and it is green. What is the probability that the first card is also green?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$

## Explanation

Let $V_1$ and $V_2$ be the events “green first card” and “green second card.” By definition $P(V_1\mid V_2)=\dfrac{P(V_1\cap V_2)}{P(V_2)}$. By the multiplication rule, $P(V_1\cap V_2)=\dfrac39\cdot\dfrac28=\dfrac1{12}$. The marginal probability for the second card equals the initial proportion of green cards, because every card is equally likely to be drawn second, so $P(V_2)=\dfrac39=\dfrac13$. Therefore $P(V_1\mid V_2)=\dfrac{\frac1{12}}{\frac13}=\dfrac14$. The result equals $\dfrac28$, because, if a green card was drawn second, the first card is one of the other eight, and two of these are green.
