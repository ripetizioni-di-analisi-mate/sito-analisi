Of $400$ parts, $40$ are defective. An inspection flags $30$ defective parts and $18$ nondefective parts. What is the false negative rate?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{40}$
- $\dfrac{5}{176}$

## Explanation

A false negative is a defective part that the inspection fails to flag. There are $40-30=10$ false negatives. The rate compares them with all $40$ defective parts, giving $\dfrac{10}{40}=\dfrac14$. The ratio $\dfrac{10}{400}$ would give the joint probability of a defect and a negative result across the whole batch. The $18$ flagged nondefective parts are false positives and belong to a different group.
