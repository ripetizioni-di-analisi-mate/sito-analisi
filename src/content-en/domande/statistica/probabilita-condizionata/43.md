Three trials yield events $A$, $B$ and $C$. All three pairs are independent and each event has probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. For which value of $P(A\cap B\cap C)$ are the events mutually independent?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

Mutual independence of three events requires three pairwise factorizations and one factorization of the triple intersection. The assumptions already guarantee the first three. It remains to check that $P(A\cap B\cap C)$ equals $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac18$. Comparing it with $\dfrac14$ would use only two factors, giving the intersection probability for a pair of independent events.
