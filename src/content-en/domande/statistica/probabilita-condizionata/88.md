Two categories $A$ and $B$ have shares $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$. An alert has probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$ in $A$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in $B$. Given that no alert appeared, what is the probability that the case belongs to $B$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The condition is the absence of an alert. In group A, no alert appears with probability $\dfrac34$, contributing $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac12$ to the total. In group B, cases without an alert have probability $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac16$. The total probability of no alert is $\dfrac23$, so B's share among those cases is $\dfrac{\dfrac16}{\dfrac23}=\dfrac14$. Group B's share falls from its initial value because absence of an alert is less frequent there.
