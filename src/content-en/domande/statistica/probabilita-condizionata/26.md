Let $P(A)=0$ and $P(B)=\dfrac{1}{2}$. What follows from the definition of independence?

- $A$ and $B$ are dependent.
- $P(B\mid A)=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $A$ and $B$ are necessarily complementary.
- $A$ and $B$ are independent.

## Explanation

The intersection $A\cap B$ is contained in $A$ and therefore has probability zero. The product $P(A)P(B)=0\cdot\dfrac12$ is also zero, satisfying the definition of independence. The conditional probability $P(B\mid A)$ remains undefined because it would require division by zero. The product criterion using the marginal probabilities still settles the question.
