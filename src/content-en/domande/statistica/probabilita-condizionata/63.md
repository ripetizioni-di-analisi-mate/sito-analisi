A box contains four white slips and two black slips. Three are drawn without replacement. What is the probability of the order black, white, black?

- $\dfrac{1}{30}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $\dfrac{2}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$

## Explanation

Two of the six slips are black on the first draw. Removing one black slip leaves all four white slips and reduces the total to five. After drawing a white slip, one of the four remaining slips is black. Multiplying $\dfrac26$, $\dfrac45$ and $\dfrac14$ gives $\dfrac1{15}$. This calculation follows the specified order. Other orders with two black slips and one white slip would answer a different question.
