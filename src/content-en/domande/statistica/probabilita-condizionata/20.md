A box contains two red tokens and four green tokens. Two tokens are drawn without replacement. Given that the first is green, what is the probability that the second is red?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Knowing that the first token is green determines the remaining composition. Both red tokens remain, along with three of the four original green tokens. The second draw is therefore from five equally likely tokens, giving probability $\dfrac25$. Since the first color is already known, calculate the probability directly from the updated contents of the box.
