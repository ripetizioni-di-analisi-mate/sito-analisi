A die assigns probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$ to face $1$ and probability $\dfrac{3}{20}$ to each of the other five faces. Given that the result is odd, calculate the probability of rolling $1$.

- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Knowing that the result is odd leaves faces $1$, $3$ and $5$. These faces have different probabilities, so the denominator is $\dfrac14+\dfrac3{20}+\dfrac3{20}=\dfrac{11}{20}$. The only favorable outcome is face $1$, with probability $\dfrac14=\dfrac5{20}$. The ratio of these probabilities is $\dfrac5{11}$. Counting one face out of three would assume that the remaining faces were equally likely.
