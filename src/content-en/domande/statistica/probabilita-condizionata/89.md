An inspection has sensitivity $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and false positive probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. What defective fraction makes the probabilities of a true positive and a false positive equal across all production?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Let $p$ denote the defective fraction. A true positive requires a defective part and a positive result, so its probability is $\dfrac34p$. A false positive requires a nondefective part and a positive result, so its probability is $\dfrac14(1-p)$. Equating the contributions and multiplying by four gives $3p=1-p$, hence $p=\dfrac14$. Out of $100$ parts, the two expected contributions are equal because defective parts are three times as likely to be flagged and one third as numerous as nondefective parts.
