The mean conditional variance is $4$ and the overall variance is $13$. What is the variance of the conditional means?

- $9$
- $4$
- $13$
- $17$

## Explanation

The law of total variance adds the mean of the conditional variances and the variance of the conditional means. The second component must bring $4$ up to $13$, so it equals $13-4=9$. The value $4$ measures average dispersion within groups, while $9$ measures the dispersion of their centers.
