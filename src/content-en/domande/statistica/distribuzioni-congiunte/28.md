For a joint probability mass table, $P(Y=0\mid X=1)=\dfrac35$ and $P(X=1)=\dfrac14$ are known. Find $P(X=1,Y=0)$.

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac3{20}$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac5{12}$

## Explanation

The conditional probability $\dfrac35$ is the proportion satisfying $Y=0$ among outcomes in the group defined by $X=1$. The whole group has mass $\dfrac14$. Taking the specified proportion of this mass gives the joint mass, $P(X=1,Y=0)=\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac3{20}$. Division by the group total reverses this procedure and recovers the conditional probability from the joint mass.
