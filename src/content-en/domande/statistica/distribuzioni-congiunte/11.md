For the transformation $U=2X+Y$, $V=Y$, determine the absolute value of the Jacobian determinant of the inverse.

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

To find the inverse, first solve the second equation for $y=v$ and then the first for $x=\dfrac{u-v}{2}$. With respect to $(u,v)$, the rows of the derivative matrix are $(\dfrac12,-\dfrac12)$ and $(0,1)$. The matrix is triangular, so its determinant equals the product of its diagonal entries. The required absolute value is $\dfrac12$.
