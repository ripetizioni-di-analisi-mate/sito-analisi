Let $X$ and $Y$ be independent, with $E[X]=-2$ and $E[Y]=3$. Calculate $E[5XY]$ if the moments are finite.

- $5$
- $-6$
- $30$
- $-30$

## Explanation

By linearity we may take $5$ outside the expectation, leaving $E[XY]$ to compute. Independence gives $E[XY]=E[X]E[Y]$ when the indicated moments are finite. Substitution yields $5(-2)\cdot3=-30$. Linearity accounts for the constant factor, while factoring the expected product uses independence.
