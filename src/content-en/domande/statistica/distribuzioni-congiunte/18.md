Given $p_X(1)=\dfrac58$, $p_Y(1)=\dfrac12$, and $p_{11}=\dfrac38$ for two indicators, calculate $p_{00}$.

- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Both indicators are zero on the complement of the union of their success events. The union has probability $\dfrac58+\dfrac12-\dfrac38=\dfrac34$ because a double success is included in both marginal probabilities. This gives $p_{00}=1-\dfrac34=\dfrac14$. Subtracting the joint mass avoids counting $(1,1)$ twice. Failing to add back the intersection when computing the complement would even give a negative mass.
