Given $p_X(1)=\dfrac58$, $p_Y(1)=\dfrac12$, and $p_{11}=\dfrac38$ for two indicators, with $p_{ij}=P(X=i,\ Y=j)$, calculate $p_{00}$.

- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Both indicators are zero when neither success occurs, that is, on the complement of the union of the two success events. The pair $(1,1)$ is included in both $p_X(1)$ and $p_Y(1)$, so $p_{11}$ is subtracted once in the union. The union therefore has probability $\dfrac58+\dfrac12-\dfrac38=\dfrac34$. This gives $p_{00}=1-\dfrac34=\dfrac14$.
