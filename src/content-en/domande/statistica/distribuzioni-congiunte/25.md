A density equals $3x$ on $0<y<x<1$. Determine $f_Y(y)$ for $0<y<1$.

- $3y^2$
- $\dfrac32(1-y^2)$
- $3(1-y)$
- $\dfrac32y^2$

## Explanation

Fix $y$ with $0<y<1$. On the support $0<y<x<1$, the possible values of $x$ range only from $y$ to $1$. Integrating with respect to the first coordinate gives $f_Y(y)=[\dfrac32 x^2]_y^1=\dfrac32(1-y^2)$. The lower limit depends on $y$ because the cross section gets shorter toward the top of the triangle.
