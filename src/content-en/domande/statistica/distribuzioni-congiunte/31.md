For $X\in\{0,1\}$, the rows of the joint probability table for $Y=0,1,2$ are $(0.20,0.20,0.10)$ and $(0.15,0.25,0.10)$. Calculate $P(X=1\mid Y\ge1)$.

- $\dfrac7{10}$
- $\dfrac7{20}$
- $\dfrac7{13}$
- $\dfrac6{13}$

## Explanation

The constraint $Y\ge1$ selects the last two columns. Their four cells sum to $P(Y\ge1)=0.65$. Among the selected cells, those in row $X=1$ sum to $0.25+0.10=0.35$. The ratio $\dfrac{0.35}{0.65}=\dfrac7{13}$ is the proportion satisfying $X=1$ within the two admissible columns.
