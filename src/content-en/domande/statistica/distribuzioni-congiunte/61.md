Two measurements have the same variance $4$ and covariance $2$. What is the variance of their arithmetic mean?

- $2$
- $6$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The arithmetic mean is the linear combination whose two coefficients are $\dfrac12$. Each individual variance contributes $\dfrac14\cdot4=1$. The cross term is $2\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12\cdot2=1$, giving $1+1+1=3$. Positive covariance makes the mean more variable than the mean of independent measurements with the same variances.
