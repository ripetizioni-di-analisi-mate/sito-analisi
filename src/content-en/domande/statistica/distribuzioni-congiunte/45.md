If $X$ and $Y$ both have mean zero and $E[XY]=-\dfrac73$, what is their covariance?

- $0$
- $\dfrac73$
- $-\dfrac37$
- $-\dfrac73$

## Explanation

Covariance is the expected product of the deviations from the respective means. Both means are zero here, so the deviations are $X$ and $Y$ themselves. Their product has expectation $-\dfrac73$, which is also the covariance. The second term in $E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]$ vanishes.
