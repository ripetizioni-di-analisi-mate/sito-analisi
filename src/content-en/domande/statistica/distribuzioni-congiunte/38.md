The pairs $(1,2)$ and $(3,1)$ have masses $\dfrac34$ and $\dfrac14$. Calculate $E[(X-Y)^2]$.

- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac74$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac52$

## Explanation

The quantity to average over the pairs is $(X-Y)^2$. For $(1,2)$ the difference is $-1$ and its square is $1$. For $(3,1)$ the difference is $2$ and its square is $4$. The weighted mean is $1\cdot\dfrac34+4\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac74$. Squaring the mean difference gives a different quantity. Indeed, the mean difference is $-\dfrac14$, whose square is $\dfrac1{16}$.
