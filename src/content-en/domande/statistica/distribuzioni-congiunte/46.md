If $X$ is measured in meters and $Y$ in seconds, what are the units of their covariance?

- $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m}\cdot\mathrm{s}$
- $\mathrm{m}^2$
- It is dimensionless

## Explanation

The definition of covariance multiplies the deviation of $X$ from its mean by the deviation of $Y$ from its mean. The deviations retain their original units, meters and seconds respectively, so their product has units $\mathrm{m}\cdot\mathrm{s}$. Taking its expectation changes the numerical value while preserving the units of the product.
