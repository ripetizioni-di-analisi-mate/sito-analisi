A pair has density $4xy$ on $(0,1)^2$ and zero elsewhere. Determine the probability of $(0,\dfrac12)\times(0,\dfrac12)$.

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The rectangle has constant integration limits in each coordinate, and the density is the product of $2x$ and $2y$. The double integral therefore factors into two identical integrals. Each equals $[x^2]_0^{\frac12}=\dfrac14$, so the probability is $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac1{16}$. The density varies across the rectangle, and its area alone does not determine the probability.
