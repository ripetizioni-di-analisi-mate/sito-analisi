If $X$ and $Y$ are indicators, which event has probability $E[X+Y-XY]$?

- At least one success
- Exactly one success
- Two successes
- No successes

## Explanation

The sum $X+Y$ counts successes and can equal zero, one, or two. The product $XY$ is one precisely when there are two successes. Subtracting the product gives the value one in that case as well. Thus $X+Y-XY$ is one when there is at least one success and zero otherwise. Its expectation equals the probability of the event it indicates.
