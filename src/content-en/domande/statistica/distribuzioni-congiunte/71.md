Two independent lifetimes are exponential with rates $2$ and $5$. What is the exponential rate of their minimum?

- $10$
- $3$
- $\dfrac72$
- $7$

## Explanation

For $t\ge0$, the minimum exceeds $t$ if both lifetimes exceed that threshold. Their survival probabilities are $e^{-2t}$ and $e^{-5t}$. Independence allows multiplication, giving $e^{-7t}$. This is the survival function of an exponential distribution with rate $7$. The rates add because the exponents add.
