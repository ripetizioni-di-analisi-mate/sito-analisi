Two variables satisfy $E[X]=3$, $E[Y]=-2$, and $E[XY]=-4$. Calculate $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$.

- $-10$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $10$

## Explanation

Covariance compares the mixed moment $E[XY]$ with the product of the means. The product $3\cdot(-2)$ equals $-6$, so the required difference is $-4-(-6)=2$. Retain the sign of the second term when subtracting. Although the mixed moment is negative, it exceeds the product of the means, so the covariance is positive. Reversing the subtraction would reverse its sign.
