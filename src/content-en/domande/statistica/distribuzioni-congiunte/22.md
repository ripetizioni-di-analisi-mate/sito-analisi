A pair is uniformly distributed on the rectangle $0<x<2$, $0<y<3$. Calculate $P(X<1,Y<1)$.

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac56$

## Explanation

Under a uniform density, probabilities are proportional to areas within the support. The full rectangle has area $2\cdot3=6$. Intersecting it with $X<1$ and $Y<1$ gives the square $(0,1)\times(0,1)$, of area one. The ratio of the two areas is $\dfrac16$. Points with negative coordinates lie outside the support.
