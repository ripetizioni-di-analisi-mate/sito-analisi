A pair has positive density on the unit disk and zero density outside it. Its two marginal densities are positive for $-1<x<1$ and $-1<y<1$. What follows?

- The variables are independent by symmetry
- The variables are dependent
- Independence follows from the equality of the marginal distributions
- Independence follows from the finite area of the disk

## Explanation

Independence would require the joint density to equal the product of the marginal densities almost everywhere. That product is positive throughout the open square $(-1,1)^2$. Inside the square there are regions of positive area outside the disk, for example near $(0.9,0.9)$. The joint density is zero there. Factorization fails, so the variables are dependent.
