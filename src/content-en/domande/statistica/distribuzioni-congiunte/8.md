Given $\operatorname{Var}(X)=2$, $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=9$, and $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=2$, calculate $\operatorname{Var}(2X-Y)$.

- $9$
- $17$
- $25$
- $13$

## Explanation

In the variance of a linear combination, the coefficients are squared in the individual variance terms. Their contributions are $2^2\cdot2=8$ and $(-1)^2\cdot9=9$. The cross term uses the signed product of the coefficients and equals $2\cdot2\cdot(-1)\cdot2=-8$. The variance is therefore $8+9-8=9$. The value $17$ would omit the cross term.
