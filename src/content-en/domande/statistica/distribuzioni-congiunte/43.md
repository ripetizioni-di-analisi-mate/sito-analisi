Two indicators have marginal success probabilities $0.3$ and $0.4$. The probability of two successes is $0.18$. Calculate their covariance.

- $0.12$
- $0.18$
- $0.06$
- $-0.06$

## Explanation

The product $XY$ is one only when both indicators record a success, so $E[XY]=0.18$. The individual expectations are $0.3$ and $0.4$, whose product is $0.12$. The covariance is $0.18-0.12=0.06$. Its positive sign means that joint successes occur more often than the product of the marginal probabilities predicts.
