For two independent variables, which event has probability $F_X(t)F_Y(t)$?

- Both are at most $t$
- Exactly one is at most $t$
- Both exceed $t$
- At least one exceeds $t$

## Explanation

The marginal distribution functions give $F_X(t)=P(X\le t)$ and $F_Y(t)=P(Y\le t)$. Since the variables are independent, the corresponding events are independent. Their product is therefore the probability that $X\le t$ and $Y\le t$ both hold. Equivalently, it is the probability that the maximum is at most $t$. The complement requires at least one variable to exceed the threshold.
