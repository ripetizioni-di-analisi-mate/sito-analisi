Two independent indicators satisfy $P(X=0)=\dfrac25$ and $P(Y=1)=\dfrac34$. Calculate $P(X=1,Y=1)$.

- $\dfrac9{20}$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac7{20}$
- $\dfrac3{20}$

## Explanation

Since $X$ is an indicator, $X=0$ and $X=1$ are the only possible outcomes. Thus $P(X=1)=1-\dfrac25=\dfrac35$. Independence allows us to multiply the probability of this success by the probability of success for $Y$, giving $P(X=1,Y=1)=\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac9{20}$. Using $\dfrac25$ in the product would select the event with $X=0$.
