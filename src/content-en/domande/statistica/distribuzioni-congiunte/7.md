Given $\sigma_X=2$, $\sigma_Y=5$, and $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=-4$, calculate $\operatorname{Corr}(X,Y)$.

- $-\dfrac45$
- $-\dfrac2{25}$
- $-\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

Divide the covariance by the product of the two standard deviations to find the correlation. The denominator is $2\cdot5=10$, giving $\operatorname{Corr}(X,Y)=\dfrac{-4}{10}=-\dfrac25$. Both standard deviations are positive, so the quotient retains the negative sign. It is dimensionless because the denominator has the same units as the covariance. Use the standard deviations themselves in the denominator, without squaring them.
