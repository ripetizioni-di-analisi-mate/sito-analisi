Two independent indicators satisfy $P(X=1,Y=0)=\dfrac3{10}$ and $P(X=1)=\dfrac12$. What is $P(Y=1)$?

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac3{20}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac3{10}$

## Explanation

By independence, $P(X=1,Y=0)$ is the product of $P(X=1)$ and $P(Y=0)$. The first factor is positive, giving $P(Y=0)=\dfrac{\frac3{10}}{\frac12}=\dfrac35$. Since $Y$ takes only the values zero and one, taking the complement yields $P(Y=1)=\dfrac25$. The intermediate ratio is the probability of failure for $Y$.
