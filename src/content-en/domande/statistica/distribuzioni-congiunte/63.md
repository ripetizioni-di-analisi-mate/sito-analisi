Two independent random variables $X,Y$ each take only $-1$ and $1$, equally likely. A program saves $S=X+Y$ and $D=X-Y$. Which pair $(S,D)$ can occur in the file?

- $(2,2)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(-2,-2)$

## Explanation

For $X=1$ and $Y=-1$, $(S,D)=(0,2)$. Equal inputs give difference zero and sum $2$ or $-2$. Unequal inputs give sum zero and difference $2$ or $-2$. These possibilities exclude the other answers.
