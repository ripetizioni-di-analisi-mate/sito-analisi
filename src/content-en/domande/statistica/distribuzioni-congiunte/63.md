Let $X,Y$ be independent, with $P(X=0)=\dfrac25$, $P(X=2)=\dfrac35$, $P(Y=-1)=\dfrac14$, and $P(Y=1)=\dfrac34$. Calculate $P(X+Y=1)$.

- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac9{20}$
- $\dfrac3{20}$
- $\dfrac{11}{20}$

## Explanation

The equation $X+Y=1$ admits two pairs with positive mass, $(0,1)$ and $(2,-1)$. By independence their contributions are $\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac3{10}$ and $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac3{20}$. The pairs are disjoint, so adding gives $\dfrac9{20}$. Each value of $X$ requires a different value of $Y$.
