Let $X$ be uniform on $(0,1)$ and, given $X=x$, let $Y$ be uniform on $(0,x)$. Calculate $E[Y]$.

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

Given $X=x$, the distribution of $Y$ is uniform on $(0,x)$, so its mean is the midpoint, $E[Y\mid X=x]=\dfrac x2$. Therefore $E[Y\mid X]=\dfrac X2$. By the law of total expectation
$$
E[Y]=E\left[\frac X2\right]=\frac12E[X]=\frac12\cdot\frac12=\frac14.
$$
The value $\dfrac12$ is the mean of $X$; the value $\dfrac13$ would result if $X$ had density $2x$, as in the triangle $0<y<x<1$ with density $2$.
