An observation comes from the group with mean $4$ with probability $\dfrac14$, and otherwise from the group with mean $12$. Calculate the overall mean.

- $10$
- $8$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

The group with mean $4$ has weight $\dfrac14$, so the group with mean $12$ has weight $1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$. The law of total expectation weights the two conditional means accordingly, giving $\dfrac14\cdot4+\dfrac34\cdot12=1+9=10$. The result is closer to $12$ because the second group has greater weight.
