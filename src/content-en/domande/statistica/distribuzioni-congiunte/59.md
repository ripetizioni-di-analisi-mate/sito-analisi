Given $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\operatorname{Var}(Y)=5$ and covariance $-2$, which combination has the larger variance?

- $X+Y$
- $X-Y$
- They have the same variance
- The means must be known

## Explanation

The individual variance terms contribute $5+5=10$ to both combinations. For the sum the cross term is $2\cdot(-2)=-4$, giving variance $6$. For the difference the product of the coefficients changes sign, making the cross term $4$. The variance of the difference is therefore $14$, the larger value. Only the sign of the covariance determines which variance is larger because both coefficients have absolute value one.
