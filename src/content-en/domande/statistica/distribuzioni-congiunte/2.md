A pair has constant density $c$ on $0<y<x<4$ and zero elsewhere. Find $c$.

- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac14$
- $8$

## Explanation

The density is constant on its support, so its integral equals $c$ times the area of the triangle. The line $y=x$ divides the square of side $4$ into two equal triangles. The support thus has area $\dfrac12\cdot4\cdot4=8$. Normalization requires $8c=1$, giving $c=\dfrac18$. The area $16$ would also include points where $y>x$.
