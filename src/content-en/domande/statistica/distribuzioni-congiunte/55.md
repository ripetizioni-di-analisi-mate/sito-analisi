A pair is jointly normal with zero covariance. Which property follows?

- The two variables are equal almost surely
- Both means are zero
- The two variables are independent
- The two variances must be equal

## Explanation

The correlation is $\rho=\dfrac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}$, so zero covariance gives $\rho=0$. With $\rho=0$ the exponent of the joint normal density becomes $-\dfrac{z_x^2+z_y^2}{2}$, and the exponential of a sum is the product of the exponentials. The joint density is therefore the product of the two marginal normal densities. By the factorization theorem the two variables are independent. The means and variances can be arbitrary.
