A pair is jointly normal with zero covariance. Which property follows?

- The two variables are equal almost surely
- Both means are zero
- The two variables are independent
- The two variances must be equal

## Explanation

For a jointly normal pair, zero covariance makes the covariance matrix diagonal. The joint characteristic function then factors into the product of the two marginal characteristic functions, which implies independence. This property uses joint normality of the pair. For arbitrary joint distributions zero covariance can coexist with nonlinear dependence.
