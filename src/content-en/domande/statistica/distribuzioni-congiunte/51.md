Suppose $Y=7-2X$ almost surely and $\operatorname{Var}(X)>0$ is finite. What is the correlation?

- $1$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $-1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The given relationship implies $Y-E[Y]=-2(X-E[X])$. Hence $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=-2\sigma_X^2$ and $\sigma_Y=2\sigma_X$. Since $\sigma_X>0$, dividing the covariance by $\sigma_X\sigma_Y=2\sigma_X^2$ gives $-1$. The decreasing linear relationship produces perfect negative correlation. The intercept $7$ cancels when centering $Y$ at its mean.
