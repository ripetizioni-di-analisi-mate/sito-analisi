Two independent uniform variables on $(0,1)$ have sum $S$. What is $P(S<\dfrac12)$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac78$

## Explanation

Independence makes the joint density uniform on the unit square. The line $x+y=\dfrac12$ meets the axes at $\dfrac12$, so the event is the triangle between the line and the axes inside the square. Its area is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac18$. The density on the square is one, so the probability equals this area.
