Groups A and B have means $3$ and $9$. Their overall mean is $7$. What weight does group B have?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac47$
- $\dfrac79$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Let $w$ be the weight of group B. Group A then has weight $1-w$. The overall mean satisfies $7=3(1-w)+9w=3+6w$. Subtracting $3$ and dividing by $6$ gives $w=\dfrac46=\dfrac23$. This agrees with the overall mean being closer to $9$ than to $3$.
