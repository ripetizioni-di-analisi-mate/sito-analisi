Suppose $Y=X$ almost surely and $F_X(t)=0.7$. Calculate $P(\max(X,Y)\le t)$.

- $0.49$
- $0.7$
- $0.91$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

Since $Y=X$ almost surely, $\max(X,Y)=X$ almost surely as well. The event that the maximum is at most $t$ therefore has the same probability as $X\le t$, namely $0.7$. Multiplication of the marginal probabilities would require independence. Here the two threshold events coincide. The value $0.49$ would be the product for two independent events of equal probability.
