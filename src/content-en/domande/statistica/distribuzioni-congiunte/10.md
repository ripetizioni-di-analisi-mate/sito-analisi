Two independent variables satisfy $F_X(t)=\dfrac23$ and $F_Y(t)=\dfrac35$. Calculate $P(\max(X,Y)>t)$.

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac2{15}$
- $\dfrac{13}{15}$

## Explanation

The complement of $\max(X,Y)>t$ requires both variables to be at most $t$. By independence its probability is $F_X(t)F_Y(t)=\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac25$. Subtracting from one gives $\dfrac35$. For the maximum to exceed the threshold, it suffices for either variable to exceed it.
