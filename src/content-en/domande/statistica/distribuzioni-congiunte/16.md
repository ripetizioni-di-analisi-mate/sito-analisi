Which limit of the joint cumulative distribution function gives $F_Y(y)$?

- $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-\infty}F_{X,Y}(x,y)$
- $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to+\infty}F_{X,Y}(x,y)$
- $\displaystyle\lim_{y\to+\infty}F_{X,Y}(x,y)$
- $F_{X,Y}(y,y)$

## Explanation

By definition, $F_{X,Y}(x,y)$ is the probability that both $X\le x$ and $Y\le y$ hold. To obtain the marginal distribution function of $Y$, fix the cutoff $y$ and let $x$ increase. The resulting events increase to include every outcome satisfying $Y\le y$. Continuity of probability from below gives $F_Y(y)=\lim_{x\to+\infty}F_{X,Y}(x,y)$.
