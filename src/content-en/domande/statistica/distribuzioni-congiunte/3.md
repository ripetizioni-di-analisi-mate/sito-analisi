For a discrete pair, $P(X=2,Y=1)=\dfrac18$ and $P(X=2)=\dfrac13$. Calculate $P(Y=1\mid X=2)$.

- $\dfrac1{24}$
- $\dfrac5{24}$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac38$

## Explanation

Conditioning on $X=2$ restricts the possible outcomes to those with that first coordinate. Among these, the outcomes satisfying $Y=1$ have joint mass $\dfrac18$. The total mass of the admissible outcomes is $\dfrac13$. Dividing gives $P(Y=1\mid X=2)=\dfrac{\frac18}{\frac13}=\dfrac38$, the proportion with $Y=1$ among those with $X=2$.
