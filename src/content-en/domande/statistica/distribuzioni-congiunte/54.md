Let $X$ be uniform on $\{-2,-1,1,2\}$ and let $Y=|X|$. Which pair of properties holds?

- Zero covariance and independence
- Positive covariance and independence
- Zero covariance and dependence
- Negative covariance and dependence

## Explanation

The values of $X$ are symmetric about $0$, so $E[X]=\dfrac{-2-1+1+2}{4}=0$. The product $XY=X|X|$ takes the values $-4$, $-1$, $1$, $4$, so $E[XY]=0$. Hence $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]=0-0\cdot E[Y]=0$. However, $P(Y=1\mid X=2)=0$, whereas $P(Y=1)=P(X=-1)+P(X=1)=\dfrac12$. The probability changes under conditioning, so the variables are dependent.
