If $X$ is uniform on $\{-2,0,2\}$ and $Y=X^2$, which pair of properties holds?

- Zero covariance and independence
- Positive covariance and independence
- Zero covariance and dependence
- Negative covariance and dependence

## Explanation

Since $X$ takes the values $-2,0,2$ with equal probabilities, symmetry gives $E[X]=E[X^3]=0$. With $Y=X^2$ we have $XY=X^3$, so the covariance is zero. However, given $X=0$, the event $Y=0$ occurs with certainty, while its marginal probability is $\dfrac13$. The change in probability under conditioning establishes dependence.
