Two independent indicators have success probabilities $p$ and $q$. What is the parameter of the Bernoulli distribution of their minimum?

- $p+q-pq$
- $1-pq$
- $p+q$
- $pq$

## Explanation

The minimum of two indicators takes only the values zero and one. It is one if both are one and zero as soon as either is zero. It is thus the indicator of a double success. By independence the probability of that event is $pq$, the required Bernoulli parameter.
