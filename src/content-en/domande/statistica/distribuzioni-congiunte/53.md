The variances of $X$ and $Y$ are $4$ and $9$. Which covariance is impossible?

- $-6$
- $5$
- $0$
- $7$

## Explanation

The absolute covariance is bounded by the product of the standard deviations. The variances $4$ and $9$ give standard deviations $2$ and $3$. Their product is $6$. A covariance of $7$ would imply the impossible correlation $\dfrac76>1$. The extremes $-6$ and $6$ correspond to perfect correlations. The comparison uses the absolute value because negative covariance also has a lower bound.
