For fixed $X=x$, the conditional distribution of $Y$ is uniform on $(x,x+2)$. Calculate $E[Y\mid X=x]$.

- $\dfrac{x+2}{2}$
- $2x+2$
- $x+1$
- $1$

## Explanation

For fixed $X=x$, $Y$ is uniform on the interval with endpoints $x$ and $x+2$. By symmetry its expectation is the midpoint, $\dfrac{x+(x+2)}{2}=x+1$. This value depends on $x$ because both endpoints move with it.
