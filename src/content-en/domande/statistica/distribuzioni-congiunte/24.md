A pair has density $8x$ on $0<x<\dfrac12$, $0<y<1$, and zero elsewhere. What is $f_Y(\dfrac34)$?

- $1$
- $6$
- $2$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

To find $f_Y(\dfrac34)$, fix the second coordinate and integrate the joint density over all admissible values of $x$. Since $\dfrac34$ belongs to $(0,1)$, these values span $(0,\dfrac12)$. The integral is $[4x^2]_0^{\frac12}=1$. The result is the marginal density at the specified point. Larger $x$ values contribute more because the joint density increases with $x$.
