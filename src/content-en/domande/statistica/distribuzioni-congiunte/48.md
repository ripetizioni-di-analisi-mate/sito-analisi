Let $X,Y$ be indicators with marginal success probabilities $\dfrac12$. If $P(X=Y)=0$, what is their covariance?

- $0$
- $\dfrac14$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $-\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The condition $P(X=Y)=0$ rules out a double success. Thus $XY=0$ almost surely and the mixed moment is zero. Both marginal expectations are $\dfrac12$. Subtracting their product gives $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0-\dfrac14=-\dfrac14$. The negative sign reflects the fact that one success rules out the other.
