Three independent lifetimes exceed $t$ with respective probabilities $\dfrac12$, $\dfrac23$, and $\dfrac34$. What is the probability that their minimum exceeds $t$?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac1{24}$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac{23}{24}$

## Explanation

The minimum exceeds $t$ only if all three lifetimes exceed $t$. Independence makes the intersection probability the product of the three survival probabilities, giving $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac14$. If even one lifetime were at most $t$, the minimum would already be at most $t$. The three probabilities are different, and each occurs once in the product.
