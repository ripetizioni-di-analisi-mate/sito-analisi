The only pairs with positive mass are $(-1,0)$, $(0,1)$, and $(1,1)$, with respective masses $\dfrac15$, $\dfrac15$, and $\dfrac35$. Calculate $P(X+Y=1)$.

- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

Compute the sum for each pair with positive mass. For $(-1,0)$ the sum is $-1$, for $(0,1)$ it is $1$, and for $(1,1)$ it is $2$. Only the middle pair belongs to the specified event, so $P(X+Y=1)=\dfrac15$. The mass of pair $(1,1)$ contributes to the probability that the sum equals $2$.
