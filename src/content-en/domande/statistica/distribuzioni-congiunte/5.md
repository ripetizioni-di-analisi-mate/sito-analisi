The pair $(X,Y)$ takes values $(0,3)$, $(1,2)$, and $(2,4)$ with respective probabilities $\dfrac12$, $\dfrac14$, and $\dfrac14$. Calculate $E[XY]$.

- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac{27}8$
- $\dfrac92$

## Explanation

To find the expected product, evaluate $XY$ at each pair and weight it by the pair's probability mass. The three products are $0$, $2$, and $8$, respectively. Thus $E[XY]=0\cdot\dfrac12+2\cdot\dfrac14+8\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac52$. The first outcome has the greatest mass but contributes zero because its first coordinate is zero.
