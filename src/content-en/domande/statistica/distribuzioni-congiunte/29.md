If $p_X(2)=0$, what can be said about the formula $\dfrac{p_{X,Y}(2,y)}{p_X(2)}$?

- The conditional probability is zero for every $y$
- The conditional distribution equals the marginal distribution of $Y$
- The conditional probability is one for every $y$
- The formula is indeterminate

## Explanation

The marginal mass $p_X(2)$ is the sum of the joint masses with first coordinate $2$. All these masses are nonnegative and sum to zero, so each is zero. The proposed ratio therefore has zero numerator and denominator for every $y$. The formula does not define a conditional mass for a row of probability zero.
