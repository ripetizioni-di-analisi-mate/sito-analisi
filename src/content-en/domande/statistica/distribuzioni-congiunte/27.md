A pair has density $2$ on the triangle $0<x<y<1$ and zero elsewhere. Calculate $P(Y<\dfrac12)$.

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

Within the support $0<x<y<1$, imposing $Y<\dfrac12$ leaves the triangle $0<x<y<\dfrac12$. Its legs have length $\dfrac12$, giving area $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac18$. The density is constant at $2$ on the region, so the probability is $2\cdot\dfrac18=\dfrac14$. Integrating over the entire rectangle with sides $\dfrac12$ would include points where $x>y$.
