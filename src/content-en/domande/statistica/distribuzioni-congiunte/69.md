Four independent variables have the same cumulative probability $F(t)=\dfrac12$ at the threshold $t$. Calculate $P(\min_i X_i\le t)$.

- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac{15}{16}$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The complement requires all four variables to exceed $t$. Each exceedance probability is $1-F(t)=\dfrac12$, so independence gives intersection probability $(\dfrac12)^4=\dfrac1{16}$. Subtracting from one gives $\dfrac{15}{16}$. Even one value at or below the threshold makes the minimum at most $t$.
