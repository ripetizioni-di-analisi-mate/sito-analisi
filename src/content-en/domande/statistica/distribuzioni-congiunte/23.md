A pair is uniformly distributed on the triangle $x>0$, $y>0$, $x+y<1$. Calculate $P(X+Y<\dfrac12)$.

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The condition $X+Y<\dfrac12$ selects a triangle similar to the original, with legs half as long. Its area is one-quarter of the original area because both base and height are halved. Under a uniform density, the probability equals this ratio, $\dfrac14$. Equivalently, the large triangle has area $\dfrac12$ and the density is $2$, so the smaller area $\dfrac18$ gives the same result.
