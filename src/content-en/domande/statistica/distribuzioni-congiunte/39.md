The pair has density $2$ for $0<y<x<1$ and zero elsewhere. Calculate $E[Y]$.

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

For fixed $y$ between zero and one, the joint density is $2$ for $y<x<1$. Integrating over this cross section gives $f_Y(y)=2(1-y)$. To find the expectation, integrate $y$ against this marginal density, giving $E[Y]=\int_0^1 2y(1-y)\,dy=1-\dfrac23=\dfrac13$. The sections are longer for small $y$, so the marginal density decreases.
