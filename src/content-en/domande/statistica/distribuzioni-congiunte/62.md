Let $X,Y$ be independent and uniformly distributed on the discrete sets $\{0,1\}$ and $\{0,1,2\}$, respectively. What is $P(X+Y\ge2)$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Independence and the two uniform distributions give six pairs of equal mass $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac16$. When $X=0$, only $Y=2$ is admissible. When $X=1$, both $Y=1$ and $Y=2$ are admissible. The three favorable pairs have total probability $3\cdot\dfrac16=\dfrac12$. Sums exactly equal to two are included.
