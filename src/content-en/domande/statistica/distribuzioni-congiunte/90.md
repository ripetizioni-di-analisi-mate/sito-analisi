For a variable with finite second moment, which inequality always holds?

- $\operatorname{Var}(Y)\le E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]$
- $E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]=\operatorname{Var}(Y)$
- $E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]\le\operatorname{Var}(Y)$
- $E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]<0$

## Explanation

With a finite second moment, the law of total variance expresses $\operatorname{Var}(Y)$ as the sum of $E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]$ and $\operatorname{Var}(E[Y\mid X])$. The second term is a variance and hence nonnegative. The first term is therefore at most the overall variance. Equality holds when the conditional mean is almost surely constant.
