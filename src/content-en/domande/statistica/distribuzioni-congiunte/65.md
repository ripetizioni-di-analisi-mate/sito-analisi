Let $X$ be uniform on $(0,2)$ and $Y$ uniform on $(0,3)$, independently. What is the density of their sum at $s=\dfrac52$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac5{12}$

## Explanation

In the convolution at $s=\dfrac52$, the product of the densities equals $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac16$ where both $0<x<2$ and $0<\dfrac52-x<3$ hold. The second constraint is equivalent to $-\dfrac12<x<\dfrac52$, which contains all of $(0,2)$. The integral is therefore height times length, $\dfrac16\cdot2=\dfrac13$. This computes a density at a point by integrating only with respect to $x$.
