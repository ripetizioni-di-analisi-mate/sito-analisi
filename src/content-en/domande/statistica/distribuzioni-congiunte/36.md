The rows of the joint probability table for two indicators are $(0.10,0.30)$ and $(0.15,0.45)$. Which result holds?

- The indicators are dependent because the rows differ
- The indicators are dependent because $p_{11}>p_{00}$
- The indicators are independent
- The indicators are independent only when all four masses are equal

## Explanation

The row sums give the marginal probabilities of $X$, namely $0.40$ and $0.60$. The column sums give those of $Y$, namely $0.25$ and $0.75$. The products for the first row are $0.10$ and $0.30$, and those for the second are $0.15$ and $0.45$. They match the joint masses in every cell, so the indicators are independent.
