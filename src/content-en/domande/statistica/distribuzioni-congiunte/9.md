Two independent indicators have success probabilities $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac12$. Calculate the probability that their sum equals $1$.

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

Two indicators sum to one when exactly one success occurs. The two possibilities, $(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$, are disjoint. By independence their probabilities are $\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac38$ and $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac18$. Adding gives $\dfrac12$. Using just one of these masses would count only the success of one particular indicator.
