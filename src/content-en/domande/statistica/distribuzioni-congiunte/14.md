A pair has density $2$ on the triangle $0<y<x<1$ and zero elsewhere. Use the conditional density to calculate $P(Y>\dfrac14\mid X=\dfrac12)$.

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

For fixed $X=\dfrac12$, the vertical section of the triangle is $0<y<\dfrac12$. The joint density is $2$ throughout this section and the marginal density at $x=\dfrac12$ is $2\cdot\dfrac12=1$. The conditional distribution is therefore uniform on the interval with density $2$. The part above $\dfrac14$ has length $\dfrac14$, so the required probability is $2\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac12$.
