If $E[Y\mid X]=5-X$ and $E[X]=2$, what is $E[Y]$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The law of total expectation gives $E[Y]=E[E[Y\mid X]]$. Substituting the conditional expression leaves the expectation of $5-X$. By linearity the constant remains $5$, and the expectation of $X$ is $2$. Thus $E[Y]=5-2=3$. The calculation uses the mean of $X$ and the expression for the conditional mean.
