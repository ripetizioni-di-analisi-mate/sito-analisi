Two groups have the same mean and variances $2$ and $10$. Their weights are $\dfrac34$ and $\dfrac14$. Calculate the overall variance.

- $6$
- $4$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The conditional means coincide, so their variance is zero. The remaining term is the weighted mean of the within-group variances. Multiplying each by its group weight gives $\dfrac34\cdot2+\dfrac14\cdot10=\dfrac32+\dfrac52=4$. All overall dispersion thus comes from within the groups because their centers coincide. If their means differed, the variance of the conditional means would have to be added.
