Two departments handle fractions $\dfrac23$ and $\dfrac13$ of all orders. Their mean processing times are $6\,\mathrm{min}$ and $15\,\mathrm{min}$, respectively. Calculate the mean processing time for an order selected from all orders.

- $7\,\mathrm{min}$
- $9\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12\,\mathrm{min}$
- $10.5\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

A randomly selected order comes from the first department with probability $\dfrac23$ and from the second with probability $\dfrac13$. The law of total expectation uses these proportions to weight the department means. The calculation gives $\dfrac23\cdot6+\dfrac13\cdot15=4+5=9\,\mathrm{min}$. The mean is closer to $6\,\mathrm{min}$ because the first department supplies more orders. Equal weights would assign the same number of orders to both departments.
