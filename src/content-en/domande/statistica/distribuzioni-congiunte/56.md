The means of $X,Y$ are $-2$ and $5$. Without assuming independence, what is $E[3X+2Y]$?

- $-16$
- $4$
- $9$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Linearity of expectation holds for any pair with finite means, so $E[3X+2Y]=3E[X]+2E[Y]$. Substitution gives $3(-2)+2\cdot5=-6+10=4$. Dependence can affect the dispersion of the linear combination, but its mean only requires the two marginal expectations.
