If $E[Y\mid X]=\mu$ almost surely for a constant $\mu$, which component of total variance is zero?

- Neither
- The mean of the conditional variances
- Both for every distribution of $Y$
- The variance of the conditional means

## Explanation

The variable $E[Y\mid X]$ equals the same constant $\mu$ almost surely. Its deviation from its own mean is therefore zero almost surely, so its variance is zero. The between-group component vanishes. The conditional dispersion of $Y$ within each group may still be positive.
