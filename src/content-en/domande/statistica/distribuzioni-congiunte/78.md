What is the area of the image of the unit square under the linear transformation $u=3x+y$, $v=y$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac13$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The linear transformation maps the sides of the square to the vectors $(3,0)$ and $(1,1)$. Its image is a parallelogram with horizontal base $3$ and height $1$, giving area $3\cdot1=3$. The absolute determinant of the forward transformation is $3$, the same area scaling factor from the original plane to the transformed plane.
