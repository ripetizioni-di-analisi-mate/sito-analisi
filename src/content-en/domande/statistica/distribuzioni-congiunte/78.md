What is the area of the image of the unit square under the linear transformation $u=3x+y$, $v=y$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac13$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The linear transformation maps the sides of the square to the vectors $(3,0)$ and $(1,1)$. Its image is a parallelogram with horizontal base $3$ and height $1$, giving area $3\cdot1=3$. The Jacobian matrix of the forward transformation has rows $(3,1)$ and $(0,1)$, so its determinant equals $3$, the factor by which areas are multiplied when passing from the $(x,y)$ plane to the $(u,v)$ plane. The Jacobian determinant of the inverse, which appears in the change-of-variables formula, is the reciprocal, $\dfrac13$.
