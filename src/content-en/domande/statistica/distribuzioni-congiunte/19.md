In a discrete probability table, one row has marginal mass zero. What must hold for every cell in that row?

- Positive and negative masses cancel
- All the masses equal one
- All the masses are zero
- The cells may have arbitrary positive masses

## Explanation

A row's marginal mass is the sum of its cell masses. Each mass is a probability and hence nonnegative. If even one cell had positive mass, the row sum would be at least that large and therefore positive. Thus every cell in a row with zero marginal mass has mass zero.
