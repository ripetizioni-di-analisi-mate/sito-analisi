Given $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=4$, calculate $\operatorname{Cov}(-2X+7,3Y-1)$.

- $24$
- $-8$
- $13$
- $-24$

## Explanation

Centering the transformed variables removes the constants $7$ and $-1$. The resulting deviations are $-2$ times the deviation of $X$ and $3$ times the deviation of $Y$. Their product is $-6$ times the original product. The covariance is thus $(-2)\cdot3\cdot4=-24$. Its sign changes because one of the scale changes reverses the direction of the deviations.
