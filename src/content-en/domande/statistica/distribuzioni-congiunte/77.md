Start with the square $0<x<1$, $0<y<1$, and set $u=x+y$, $v=y$. Which constraints describe the transformed support?

- $0<v<1$ and $v<u<v+1$
- $0<v<1$ and $0<u<1$
- $0<v<1$ and $u<v$
- $0<u<1$ and $u<v<u+1$

## Explanation

The second equation gives $y=v$, so the constraint $0<y<1$ becomes $0<v<1$. The first equation gives $x=u-v$. The constraint $0<x<1$ therefore becomes $0<u-v<1$, or $v<u<v+1$. At each height $v$, the horizontal cross section has length one and its endpoints depend on $v$.
