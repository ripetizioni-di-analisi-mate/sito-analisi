Suppose $X=4$ almost surely and $\operatorname{Var}(Y)>0$. What can be said about $\operatorname{Corr}(X,Y)$?

- It is zero
- It is undefined
- It is one
- It equals the mean of $Y$

## Explanation

The variable $X$ is almost surely constant. Its mean is $4$ and its deviation from the mean is zero, so its standard deviation is zero. Correlation divides by the product of the standard deviations, which is zero here if the standard deviation of $Y$ is finite. The correlation of the constant variable with $Y$ is therefore undefined. Both standard deviations must be positive and finite for the definition to apply.
