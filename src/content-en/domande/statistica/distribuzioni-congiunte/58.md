Two independent variables have variances $3$ and $12$. What is $\operatorname{Var}(\dfrac{X+Y}{2})$?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}$
- $\dfrac94$

## Explanation

Independence makes the covariance zero, so the variance of the sum is $3+12=15$. The mean of the two values is their sum times $\dfrac12$. Its variance is multiplied by the square of that coefficient, $\dfrac14$, giving $\dfrac{15}{4}$.
