If $X$ is uniform on $(1,2)$ and $Y$ is uniform on $(3,5)$, independently, on which open interval is the density of $X+Y$ positive?

- $(3,5)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(4,7)$
- $(1,7)$

## Explanation

The extreme possible sums are the sum of the two lower endpoints, $1+3=4$, and the sum of the two upper endpoints, $2+5=7$. For every $s$ strictly between $4$ and $7$, the intervals $(1,2)$ and $(s-5,s-3)$ overlap over a positive length. The convolution is therefore positive on $(4,7)$. At the endpoints the length of the overlap is zero.
