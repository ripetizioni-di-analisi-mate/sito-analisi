Three equally likely groups have means $0$, $3$, and $6$ and within-group variances $2$, $5$, and $2$. What is the between-group component of total variance?

- $6$
- $3$
- $9$
- $12$

## Explanation

The between-group component is the variance of a variable taking the conditional means $0$, $3$, and $6$ with equal probabilities. Its mean is $3$, so the mean squared deviation is $\dfrac{(0-3)^2+(3-3)^2+(6-3)^2}{3}=6$. Within-group variances determine the other component of total variance. If all group means coincided, the between-group component would vanish even with dispersion within the groups.
