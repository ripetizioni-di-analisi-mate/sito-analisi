Given $\operatorname{Var}(X)=4$, $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=9$, and $\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)=21$, determine the covariance.

- $8$
- $4$
- $-4$
- $17$

## Explanation

The variance of a sum equals the sum of the individual variances plus twice the covariance. Here $21=4+9+2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$, so the cross term is $21-13=8$. Dividing it by two gives $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=4$. The variance of the sum exceeds $13$ because the covariance is positive.
