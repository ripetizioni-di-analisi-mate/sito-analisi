If $E[X]=3$, $E[Y]=10$, $\sigma_X=2$, $\sigma_Y=4$, and $\rho=1$, which relationship holds almost surely?

- $Y=2X+10$
- $Y=\dfrac X2+4$
- $Y=4X-2$
- $Y=2X+4$

## Explanation

A correlation of one means that the standardized deviations coincide almost surely, so $\dfrac{Y-10}{4}=\dfrac{X-3}{2}$. Multiplying by $4$ gives $Y-10=2(X-3)$ and hence $Y=2X+4$. The coefficient $2$ is the ratio of the standard deviations, and the intercept makes the line pass through the point whose coordinates are the means.
