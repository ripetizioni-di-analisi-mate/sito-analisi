Let $0<x<2$, and let $f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)=\dfrac2x$ for $0<y<\dfrac x2$ and zero elsewhere. Calculate $P(Y>\dfrac x4\mid X=x)$.

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac{x}{4}$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Given $X=x$, the density is constant on $(0,\dfrac x2)$. The threshold $\dfrac x4$ is the midpoint of the interval. Its upper portion has length $\dfrac x2-\dfrac x4=\dfrac x4$, so the probability is $\dfrac2x\cdot\dfrac x4=\dfrac12$. As $x$ decreases, the density increases while the selected length decreases in the same proportion.
