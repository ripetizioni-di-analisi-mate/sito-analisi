For a pair with a joint density, what is $P(Y=2X)$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- It depends on the mean of $Y$

## Explanation

The event $Y=2X$ is the line $y=2x$ in the plane of ordered pairs. A line has zero two-dimensional area. Probabilities are integrals of the joint density with respect to area, so the integral over this line is zero. Even if the density is positive at some points on it, the line has probability zero.
