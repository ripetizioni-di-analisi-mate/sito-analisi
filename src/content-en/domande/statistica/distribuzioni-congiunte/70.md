Let $X,Y$ be independent and uniform on $(0,1)$. What is the density of $M=\max(X,Y)$ for $0<t<1$?

- $t^2$
- $2(1-t)$
- $1$
- $2t$

## Explanation

The maximum is at most $t$ if both uniform variables are at most that value. On $0<t<1$ each distribution function equals $t$, so independence gives $F_M(t)=t^2$. Differentiating with respect to $t$ gives $f_M(t)=2t$. The function $t^2$ is the cumulative probability up to the threshold, and $2t$ is its density. Outside $(0,1)$ the distribution function is constant and the density is zero.
