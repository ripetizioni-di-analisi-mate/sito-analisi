For a one-to-one transformation, the Jacobian determinant of the inverse is $-2$ and the original density at the preimage is $\dfrac15$. What is the transformed density at the point?

- $\dfrac25$
- $-\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac1{10}$
- $-\dfrac1{10}$

## Explanation

The transformed density equals the original density evaluated at the preimage times the absolute determinant of the inverse. The absolute value converts areas, which are nonnegative even when the transformation reverses orientation. Here the factor is $|-2|=2$, giving density $\dfrac15\cdot2=\dfrac25$ at the point. Using the signed determinant would give a negative value, which a density cannot take.
