If $Y=2X+1$ almost surely and $\operatorname{Var}(X)=3$, what is $E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]$?

- $3$
- $0$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

Once $X$ is known, the relationship $Y=2X+1$ completely determines $Y$. Its deviation from its conditional mean is therefore zero almost surely. Consequently $\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)=0$ almost surely, and its expectation is zero. The marginal variance of $X$ may contribute to variation across the values of $E[Y\mid X]$.
