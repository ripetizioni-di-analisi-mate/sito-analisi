Let $X,Y$ be independent and uniform on $(0,1)$. Set $U=4X$ and $V=2Y$. What is the joint density of $(U,V)$ in the interior of its support?

- $8$
- $\dfrac14$
- $1$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The transformation stretches the unit square by a factor of $4$ in the first direction and $2$ in the second. The transformed support has area $8$. The density stays constant and must integrate to one, so it is $\dfrac18$. The determinant of the inverse gives the same factor, since its scale factors are $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac12$.
