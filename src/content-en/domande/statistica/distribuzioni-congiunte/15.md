Given $F_{X,Y}(3,4)=0.70$, $F_{X,Y}(1,4)=0.35$, $F_{X,Y}(3,2)=0.40$, and $F_{X,Y}(1,2)=0.20$, calculate $P(1<X\le3,\,2<Y\le4)$.

- $0.25$
- $0.05$
- $0.35$
- $0.15$

## Explanation

The value $F_{X,Y}(3,4)$ covers the entire lower rectangle up to $(3,4)$. Subtract the strip satisfying $X\le1$ and the strip satisfying $Y\le2$. Their common corner is subtracted twice, so add it back. The required rectangle has probability $0.70-0.35-0.40+0.20=0.15$. Omitting this last addition would subtract twice the mass shared by the two strips.
