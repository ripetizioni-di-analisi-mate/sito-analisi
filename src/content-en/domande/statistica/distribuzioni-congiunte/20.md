Let $X$ be an indicator with $P(X=1)=\dfrac25$, and let $Y=1-X$. What is $P(X=1,Y=0)$?

- $\dfrac4{25}$
- $\dfrac6{25}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

The constraint $Y=1-X$ determines the second coordinate from the first. When $X=1$, $Y$ must equal zero. Thus the event $\{X=1,Y=0\}$ is the same as $\{X=1\}$, with probability $\dfrac25$. The entire marginal mass at $X=1$ is concentrated in one pair. There is no need for a second probability as a multiplier.
