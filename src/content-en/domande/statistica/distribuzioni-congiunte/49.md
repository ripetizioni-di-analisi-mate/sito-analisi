If $\operatorname{Corr}(X,Y)=\dfrac23$, what is $\operatorname{Corr}(X,4-3Y)$?

- $\dfrac23$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $-\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The transformation $4-3Y$ multiplies the deviations of $Y$ from its mean by $-3$. The covariance with $X$ is therefore multiplied by $-3$. The standard deviation of $Y$ is multiplied by $3$, since it measures the magnitude of dispersion. The factor remaining in the ratio is $-1$, giving the new correlation $-\dfrac23$.
