The pair $(X,Y)$ has a density that is zero outside the region $y>0$. For the change $U=XY$, $V=Y$, which factor multiplies $f_{X,Y}\left(\dfrac uv,v\right)$ in the density of $(U,V)$?

- $v$
- $\dfrac1v$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{u}{v^2}$

## Explanation

The inverse of the change is $x=\dfrac uv$, $y=v$, with $v>0$. Its Jacobian matrix has in its first row the derivatives of $x$, namely $\dfrac1v$ and $-\dfrac{u}{v^2}$, and in its second row the derivatives of $y$, namely $0$ and $1$. The matrix is triangular, so its determinant is the product of the diagonal entries, $\dfrac1v\cdot1=\dfrac1v$. Since $v>0$, the absolute value is $\dfrac1v$, and the density is $f_{U,V}(u,v)=f_{X,Y}\left(\dfrac uv,v\right)\dfrac1v$.
