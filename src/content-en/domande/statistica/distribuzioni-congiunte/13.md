Two equally likely groups have means $4$ and $8$ and within-group variances $3$ and $5$. Calculate the overall variance.

- $4$
- $6$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The within-group variances contribute their average, $\dfrac{3+5}{2}=4$. The overall mean is $6$, so both group means lie at distance $2$ from it. Their variance is $\dfrac{(4-6)^2+(8-6)^2}{2}=4$. The law of total variance adds the two components to give $8$. The first term alone describes dispersion about the group means.
