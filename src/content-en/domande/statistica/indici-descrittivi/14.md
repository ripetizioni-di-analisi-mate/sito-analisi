Five measurements in centimeters have numerical sum $20$ and sum of squares $95$. Find their descriptive variance.

- $15\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $19\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The sums give the mean $\bar x=\dfrac{20}{5}=4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the mean of the squares $\dfrac{95}{5}=19\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The descriptive variance is the mean of the squares minus the square of the mean, $19-4^2=3\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Both terms in the difference have the same units because the mean in the second term is squared.
