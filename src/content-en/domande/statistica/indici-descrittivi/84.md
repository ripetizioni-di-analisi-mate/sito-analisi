After $8$ is added to every observation, the mean is $11$ and the descriptive variance is $6$. Find the mean of the squares of the original observations.

- $6$
- $9$
- $15$
- $127$

## Explanation

The shift by $8$ takes the original mean from $3$ to $11$. Since the mean also shifts by $8$, the deviations are identical and the original descriptive variance is $6$. The variance identity gives the mean of the squares as the variance plus the square of the mean. Using the original values gives $6+3^2=15$.
