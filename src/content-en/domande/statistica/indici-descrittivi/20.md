A sample has third quartile $12$ and upper fence $18$. Its only observations greater than $12$ are $15$, $18$, and $21$. Where does the upper whisker end?

- $12$
- $15$
- $21$
- $18$

## Explanation

The upper whisker ends at the largest observation at or below the upper fence, here $18$. Of those above the third quartile, $15$ and $18$ are admissible, whereas $21$ lies strictly beyond the fence. The whisker therefore ends at $18$. Equality is allowed, so its endpoint can coincide with the fence.
