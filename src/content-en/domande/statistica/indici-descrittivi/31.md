Three observations lie in $[2,6)$ and one in $[6,10)$. Assuming real-valued measurements, which interval contains exactly the means compatible with the table?

- $[3,7)$
- $[2,10)$
- $[3,7]$
- $[4,8)$

## Explanation

The smallest mean occurs at the left endpoints and equals $\dfrac{3\cdot2+6}{4}=3$. Moving all values toward their right endpoints makes the mean approach $\dfrac{3\cdot6+10}{4}=7$, which is excluded. To obtain every intermediate value, add the same quantity $t\in[0,4)$ to each of the four left endpoints. The mean becomes $3+t$, so the set of possible means is $[3,7)$.
