Four times are in class $[0,4)$ and two in class $[4,12)$, in minutes. Estimate the mean using the class midpoints.

- $5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{min}$
- $2\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Replace the four times in the first class by its midpoint $\dfrac{0+4}{2}=2$ and the two in the second by $\dfrac{4+12}{2}=8$. The classes contribute $4\cdot2=8$ and $2\cdot8=16$ minutes. Dividing the estimated total by six gives $4\,\mathrm{min}$. This is an estimate because the individual times within the classes are unknown.
