Two data sets with different shapes are standardized, and afterwards both have mean zero and standard deviation one. What can be concluded about the proportion of observations below the same standardized score?

- The proportion is the same in both sets for every standardized score.
- A score of zero has half the observations below it in both sets.
- After standardization the two sets have the same shape.
- The same standardized score can correspond to different proportions.

## Explanation

Standardization shifts and rescales the data but preserves the ordering and the shape of the distribution. For example, the lists $-1,-1,2$ and $-2,1,1$ both have mean zero and the same standard deviation, so the value $0$ has standardized score $0$ in both. In the first list two of the three observations are below $0$, in the second only one. The same standardized score therefore corresponds to different proportions in the two sets.
