Two histograms have different shapes. After standardization both sets have mean zero and standard deviation one. What follows about their percentiles?

- The percentiles agree for every standardized score.
- A score of zero always corresponds to the 50th percentile.
- Both distributions become normal.
- The same standardized score can correspond to different percentiles.

## Explanation

Standardization centers and rescales a data set while preserving its ordering and shape. For example, the lists $-1,-1,2$ and $-2,1,1$ both have mean zero and the same standard deviation. After standardization, two observations in the first list and one in the second lie below zero. Thus the same standardized score can have different percentile ranks in the two distributions.
