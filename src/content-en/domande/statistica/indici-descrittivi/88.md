Two samples have median $5$. The first has range $8$ and IQR $3$. The second has range $15$ and IQR $2$. Which has greater spread according to the IQR?

- The second.
- They are equal.
- The standard deviations are needed.
- The first.

## Explanation

The IQR measures the distance between the first and third quartiles. This distance is $3$ in the first sample and $2$ in the second, so the first has greater central spread according to this measure. The second sample's larger range concerns the distance between its extremes and can increase even if its quartiles remain close together.
