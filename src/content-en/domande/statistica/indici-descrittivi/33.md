Every observation in a sample equals 18. What happens when the usual standardized score formula is applied?

- Each observation has score 1.
- Each observation has score 18.
- The formula gives every observation score 0.
- The score is undefined because the standard deviation is zero.

## Explanation

When every observation equals $18$, the mean is $18$ and every deviation is zero. The sum of squared deviations and thus the standard deviation are zero. The standardized score formula would require the undefined ratio $\dfrac{0}{0}$ for each observation. Without spread there is no positive unit in which to measure distance from the mean.
