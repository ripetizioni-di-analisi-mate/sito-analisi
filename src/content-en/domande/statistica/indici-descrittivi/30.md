The data are $0,2,2,3,4,6,10,14$. Rounding ranks up for the quartiles, how many observations lie strictly beyond Tukey's fences?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

With eight observations, the quartile ranks are $2$ and $6$. The corresponding values are $2$ and $6$, so the interquartile range is $4$. Extending the box by $1.5\cdot4=6$ on either side gives fences $-4$ and $12$. Only $14$ is strictly outside the interval, so the count is one.
