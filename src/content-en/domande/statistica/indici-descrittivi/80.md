For the data $2,4,5,6,8,10,18$, rounding the ranks up gives $Q_1=4$ and $Q_3=10$. The maximum is corrected to $20$. Under Tukey's rule, how does the upper whisker change?

- It moves from $18$ to $20$.
- It stays at $18$.
- It moves from $10$ to $18$.
- It moves from $18$ to $10$.

## Explanation

The corrected maximum remains last, so the quartiles remain $4$ and $10$. With IQR $6$, the upper fence is $10+1.5\cdot6=19$. The original value $18$ was within the fence and formed the upper whisker endpoint. The new value $20$ lies outside, so the whisker retreats to $10$, the largest remaining observation within the fence.
