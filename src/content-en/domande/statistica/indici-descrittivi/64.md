Group A contains $3,5,7$, and group B contains $1,5,9$. How are their descriptive variances related?

- $v_B=2v_A$
- $v_B=4v_A$
- $v_B=v_A$
- $v_B=8v_A$

## Explanation

Both groups have mean $5$. The deviations are $-2,0,2$ in A and $-4,0,4$ in B. The deviations in B are twice those in A, so their squares are four times as large. Since the groups have equal sizes, dividing each sum of squares by three gives $v_B=4v_A$.
