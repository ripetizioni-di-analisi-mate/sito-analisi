The ordered observations are $5,5,5,5,6,6,7,9,20$. Using the upper-rank rule for the quartiles and Tukey's rule, where does the lower whisker of the box plot end?

- At $2$
- At $4$
- At $5$
- At $0$

## Explanation

With nine observations the quartiles have ranks $\lceil9\cdot0.25\rceil=3$ and $\lceil9\cdot0.75\rceil=7$, so $Q_1=5$ and $Q_3=7$. The interquartile range is $2$, so the lower fence is $L=5-1.5\cdot2=2$. The lower whisker extends to the smallest observation greater than or equal to $2$, which is $5$. The whisker therefore ends at $5$, on the left side of the box, and has length zero because no observation is less than $Q_1$. The number $2$ is the fence, which is not an observation.
