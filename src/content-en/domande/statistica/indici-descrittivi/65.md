For the observations $-8,1,2,3,4,5,6,16$, which values lie beyond Tukey's fences when ranks are rounded up for the quartiles?

- Only $16$.
- $-8$ and $16$.
- Only $-8$.
- No observations.

## Explanation

With eight observations, the first and third quartiles have ranks $2$ and $6$ and values $1$ and $5$. The interquartile range is $4$, and one and a half times that width is $6$. The fences are $1-6=-5$ and $5+6=11$. The observations $-8$ and $16$ lie below and above the respective fences, and all other observations lie between them.
