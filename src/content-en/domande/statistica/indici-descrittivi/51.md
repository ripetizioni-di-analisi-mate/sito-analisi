Two disjoint groups of three observations each have means $4$ and $10$. The sums of squared deviations from each group's own mean are $6$ and $12$. What is the descriptive variance of all six observations together?

- $3$
- $14.4$
- $12$
- $9$

## Explanation

The overall mean is $\dfrac{3\cdot4+3\cdot10}{6}=7$. The sum of squared deviations of the combined data is the sum of the within-group spreads and of the terms due to the distance between the group means and the overall mean:
$$
D=6+12+3\cdot(4-7)^2+3\cdot(10-7)^2=18+27+27=72.
$$
The descriptive variance divides by the number of observations, so it equals $\dfrac{72}{6}=12$. The value $3$ would result from using only the within-group spreads, $\dfrac{18}{6}$.
