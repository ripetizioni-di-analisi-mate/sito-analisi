Each of two groups contains two observations equal to its own center. The centers are $2$ and $6$. Find the descriptive variance of the four combined observations.

- $0$
- $4$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

The observations are $2,2,6,6$. The overall mean is $\dfrac{2+2+6+6}{4}=4$, giving deviations $-2,-2,2,2$. All four squared deviations equal $4$, so the descriptive variance is $\dfrac{4\cdot4}{4}=4$. Each group has zero internal spread, but the combined data vary because the group centers differ.
