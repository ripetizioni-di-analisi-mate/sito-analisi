Two prices are $12$ and $18$ euros. Their weighted mean is $16$ euros. If the lower price has weight $1$, what is the weight of the higher price?

- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The price of $12$ euros has weight one, and the price of $18$ euros has unknown weight $w$. The weighted mean must satisfy $\dfrac{12+18w}{1+w}=16$. Multiplying by the total weight gives $12+18w=16+16w$, hence $2w=4$ and $w=2$. The larger weight goes to the higher price, consistent with the mean being closer to $18$.
