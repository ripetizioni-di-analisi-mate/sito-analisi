A sample has $Q_1=Q_3=6$ and includes the value $7$. How does Tukey's rule represent $7$?

- As an individual point above the box.
- As the upper whisker endpoint.
- As the median.
- As the upper edge of the box.

## Explanation

The interquartile range is $6-6=0$, so the term $1.5\operatorname{IQR}$ is zero. Both fences equal $6$, and $6$ is the only value that can lie between them. Since $7>6$, the observation $7$ is plotted separately beyond the upper fence. With the box collapsed, the fences coincide with the quartiles.
