A weighted mean uses the values $4,8,20$ and weights $2,3,0$. Find the mean.

- $8$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$
- $\dfrac{32}{5}$
- $\dfrac{52}{5}$

## Explanation

The zero weight assigns value $20$ a zero contribution. The numerator is therefore $2\cdot4+3\cdot8=32$, and the denominator is the sum of weights $2+3+0=5$. This gives $\dfrac{32}{5}=6.4$. It lies between $4$ and $8$ and closer to $8$, which has the larger weight.
