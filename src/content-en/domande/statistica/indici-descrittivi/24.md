Two disjoint groups have means $10$ and $22$, with overall mean $18$. If the second group has six members, how many are in the first?

- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the size of the first group. Since the overall mean is $18$, the total must satisfy $10n+22\cdot6=18(n+6)$. Canceling common terms gives $8n=24$, so $n=3$. In terms of deviations from $18$, three units at $-8$ balance six at $+4$.
