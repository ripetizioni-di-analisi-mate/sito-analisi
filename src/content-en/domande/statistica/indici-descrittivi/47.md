Five observations have mean $4$ and sum of squares $100$. Find the corrected sample variance $s^2$.

- $4$
- $20$
- $25$
- $5$

## Explanation

First obtain the sum of squared deviations from the mean by subtracting $n\bar x^2$ from the sum of the squared observations. Here this gives $D=100-5\cdot4^2=20$. The corrected sample variance divides by $5-1=4$, giving $s^2=5$. Dividing by five would give the descriptive variance.
