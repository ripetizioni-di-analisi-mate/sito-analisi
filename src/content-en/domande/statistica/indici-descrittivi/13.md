Values $1$, $4$, and $8$ have counts $2$, $4$, and $3$, respectively. Find the median.

- $4$
- $1$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

The counts total $2+4+3=9$, so the fifth observation is the middle one. The first two observations equal $1$. The next four, from third to sixth, equal $4$. The fifth observation is in that group, so the median is $4$. Cumulative frequencies identify the middle rank even when the data are not listed individually.
