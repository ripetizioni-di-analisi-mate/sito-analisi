A test has mean $70$ and standard deviation $6$. Find the standardized score for $58$.

- $2$
- $-12$
- $-2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Standardization expresses the deviation from the mean in standard deviations. The score $58$ lies $12$ points below the mean, so its signed deviation is $58-70=-12$. Dividing by $6$ gives $z=\dfrac{-12}{6}=-2$. The sign identifies the side of the mean, and the absolute value gives the distance from it in standard deviations.
