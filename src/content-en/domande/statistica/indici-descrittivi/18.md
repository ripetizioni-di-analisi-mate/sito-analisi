The ordered observations are $4,4,5,8,10,10,12$. What is the empirical quantile of order $p=0.4$ when the rank is rounded up?

- $4$
- $8$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The quantile of order $0.4$ is the observation at the rank obtained by rounding $7\cdot0.4=2.8$ up. This gives rank $3$, so take the third value, $5$. The two occurrences of $4$ occupy separate positions. Stopping at $4$ would include only two observations out of seven, less than the required forty percent.
