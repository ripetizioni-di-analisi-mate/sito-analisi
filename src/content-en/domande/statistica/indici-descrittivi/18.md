An archive of ten sorted times uses the upper-rank quantile $q_p=x_{(\lceil10p\rceil)}$. The third, fourth and fifth times are all $7$, and no other time is $7$. For which levels $0<p\le1$ does the archive return $7$?

- $0.3\le p<0.5$
- $0.2\le p<0.5$
- $0.2<p\le0.5$
- $0.3<p\le0.6$

## Explanation

The quantile returns $7$ at ranks $3$, $4$ or $5$. Rounding up gives these ranks exactly when $2<10p\le5$. At $p=0.2$ the second value is still selected.
