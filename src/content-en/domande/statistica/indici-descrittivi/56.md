In a sample of ten, the values $1$, $3$, $5$, and $9$ have counts $2$, $3$, $4$, and $1$, respectively. Find $q_{0.6}$ by rounding the rank up.

- $3$
- $6$
- $5$
- $9$

## Explanation

Rounding up gives rank $\lceil10\cdot0.6\rceil=6$. The first two positions hold $1$ and the next three $3$. The cumulative count up to $3$ is therefore $5$. The sixth observation is the first with value $5$. The required quantile is $5$, the first value at which the cumulative count reaches the required rank.
