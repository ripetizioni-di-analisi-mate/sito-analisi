The data $1,1,4,4,9,9,9,12$ are ordered. How many observations are at or below $q_{0.75}$ calculated by rounding the rank up?

- $6$
- $5$
- $8$
- $7$

## Explanation

Rounding up gives rank $\lceil8\cdot0.75\rceil=6$, so the quantile is the sixth observation, $9$. Counting observations at or below $9$ requires every occurrence, including the one in seventh position. Only $12$ exceeds the quantile, so the count is seven. The rank defining a quantile can therefore differ from its cumulative frequency.
