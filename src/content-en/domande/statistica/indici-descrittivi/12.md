Six measurements had a reported mean of $14\,\mathrm{mm}$. One observation of $22\,\mathrm{mm}$ had been entered as $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the corrected mean.

- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $14.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $26\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The old mean corresponds to a recorded total of $6\cdot14=84\,\mathrm{mm}$. The correction replaces one measurement, so the count stays six. Subtract the recorded $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and add the actual $22\,\mathrm{mm}$ to get $96\,\mathrm{mm}$. The corrected mean is $\dfrac{96}{6}=16\,\mathrm{mm}$. Equivalently, recovering twelve millimeters in the total raises the mean by two millimeters.
