The constant $10$ is added to every observation. Which pair of measures stays the same?

- The mean and range.
- The descriptive variance and range.
- The median and third quartile.
- The mean and descriptive variance.

## Explanation

Adding $10$ to each observation raises the mean by $10$. Each new deviation therefore equals $(x_i+10)-(\bar x+10)=x_i-\bar x$ and the descriptive variance is unchanged. Both endpoints also increase by $10$, preserving their difference. The two unchanged measures are both measures of spread based on differences.
