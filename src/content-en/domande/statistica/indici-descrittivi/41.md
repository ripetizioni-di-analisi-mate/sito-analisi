Five observations have mean square $36$ and mean $-6$. What is their descriptive variance?

- $0$
- $36$
- $42$
- $72$

## Explanation

Subtract the square of the mean from the mean of the squares. The mean is negative, but its square is $(-6)^2=36$. This gives $36-36=0$. Since all squared deviations are nonnegative, each must be zero, so all five values equal $-6$.
