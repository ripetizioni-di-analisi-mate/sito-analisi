A sorted sample contains eleven values. Its second observation is replaced by a smaller value that becomes the minimum. Which observation in the original list remains the median?

- The sixth.
- The fifth.
- The seventh.
- The mean of the fifth and sixth.

## Explanation

With eleven values the median is the sixth observation. The changed second value moves ahead of the first, and the first becomes second. Positions three through eleven therefore retain their original values. In particular, the median remains the original sixth observation because the replacement affects only the lowest two ranks.
