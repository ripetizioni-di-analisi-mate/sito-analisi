Arrange the values $5,13,10,3,12,8,10,5$ in increasing order and find their median.

- $8$
- $10$
- $9$
- $7.5$

## Explanation

The median uses positions in the ordered list. Sorting gives $3,5,5,8,10,10,12,13$, retaining both occurrences of repeated values. With eight observations the middle positions are the fourth and fifth, occupied by $8$ and $10$. Their mean is $\dfrac{8+10}{2}=9$. A median obtained this way can lie between two observations.
