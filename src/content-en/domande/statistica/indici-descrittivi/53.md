A box plot has $Q_1=5$, $Q_3=9$, and upper whisker endpoint $12$. The maximum observation is $19$. How far is the maximum from the upper fence?

- $7$
- $4$
- $10$
- $13$

## Explanation

Find the upper fence by starting at $Q_3$ and adding one and a half interquartile ranges. The interquartile range is $9-5=4$, so $U=9+1.5\cdot4=15$. The distance from the maximum $19$ to this threshold is $19-15=4$. The whisker at $12$ marks the last observation inside the fence, so the requested distance is measured from $15$.
