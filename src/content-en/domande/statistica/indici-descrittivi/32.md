A table lists $0$ three times and $4$ once. Find the descriptive variance.

- $4$
- $3$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The frequency tells how many times each value contributes to the sums. The mean is $\dfrac{3\cdot0+1\cdot4}{4}=1$ and the mean of the squares is $\dfrac{3\cdot0^2+1\cdot4^2}{4}=4$. Thus the descriptive variance is $4-1^2=3$. Both calculations divide by four because the table represents four observations, although it lists only two distinct values.
