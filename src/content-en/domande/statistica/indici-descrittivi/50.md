Nine observations have mean $21$. The values $15$ and $22$ are removed. What is the mean of the remaining seven? Round to two decimal places.

- $21.71$
- $20.14$
- $16.89$
- $24.14$

## Explanation

The original mean gives the sum of the nine values, $9\cdot21=189$. Subtract the two removed values to get $189-15-22=152$. This sum now covers seven observations, so their mean is $\dfrac{152}{7}\approx21.71$. Round at the end. The new sample size is required because the removed observations no longer contribute.
