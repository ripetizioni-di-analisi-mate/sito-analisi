Four lengths in meters have corrected sample variance $0\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What follows?

- The mean length is zero.
- The measurements are symmetric and distinct.
- All four measurements are equal.
- The variance divisor is zero.

## Explanation

With four observations, the corrected sample variance divides the sum of squared deviations by $4-1=3$. To give zero, the numerator must be zero. Every square is nonnegative, so each deviation from the mean must be zero. Thus all four lengths are equal, although their common length cannot be recovered from their variance.
