A sample with positive sample standard deviation is standardized using $s$. What is the sum $\sum_i z_i^2$?

- $n$
- $n-1$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Each standardized score is a deviation divided by $s$, so its square has denominator $s^2$. By the definition of the corrected sample variance, the original sum of squared deviations is $(n-1)s^2$. Dividing that sum by $s^2$ gives $\sum_i z_i^2=n-1$. This agrees with a corrected sample variance of one on the standardized scale because the sum is divided by $n-1$.
