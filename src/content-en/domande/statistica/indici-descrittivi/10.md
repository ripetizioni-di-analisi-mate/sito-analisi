For the ordered data $1,2,4,4,7,9,12,14,18$, use $q_p=x_{(\lceil np\rceil)}$ to find the third quartile.

- $9$
- $12$
- $14$
- $7$

## Explanation

Rounding the rank up identifies the first position where at least the required proportion has been reached. For nine observations, compute $9\cdot0.75=6.75$ and take the smallest integer at least that large, $7$. At the seventh position the ordered value is $12$, so $Q_3=12$. The number $7$ is the position, whereas the quartile is the value at that position.
