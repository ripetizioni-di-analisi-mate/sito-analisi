A data set has mean $14$ and sum $84$. How many observations does it contain?

- $7$
- $12$
- $6$
- $70$

## Explanation

The mean distributes the total equally across the observations. If $n$ is their number, then $84=14n$. Dividing the total by the mean gives $n=\dfrac{84}{14}=6$. The reverse check recovers the original total: six observations with mean $14$ sum to $6\cdot14=84$.
