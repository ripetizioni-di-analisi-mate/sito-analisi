Box plot A has $Q_1=4$ and $Q_3=10$. Box plot B has $Q_1=7$ and $Q_3=12$. Which group has the larger IQR, and by how much?

- B, by $1$.
- A, by $3$.
- Their IQRs are equal.
- A, by $1$.

## Explanation

The interquartile range is the horizontal distance between the box edges, at $Q_1$ and $Q_3$. The box for A runs from $4$ to $10$, giving width $6$. The box for B runs from $7$ to $12$, giving width $5$. A is wider by $6-5=1$.
