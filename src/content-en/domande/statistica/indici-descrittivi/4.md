Find the descriptive variance, with divisor $n$, of the measurements $3,3,7,7$ in meters.

- $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The descriptive variance is the mean squared deviation. The total is $20\,\mathrm{m}$, so the mean is $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The deviations are $-2,-2,2,2$ meters and all their squares equal $4\,\mathrm{m}^2$. The variance is therefore $\dfrac{4\cdot4}{4}=4\,\mathrm{m}^2$. The divisor is the number of observations, as specified.
