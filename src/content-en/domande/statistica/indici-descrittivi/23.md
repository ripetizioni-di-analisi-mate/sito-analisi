A score scale is reversed using $y=40-x$. The mean of $x$ is $15$ and its sample standard deviation is $4$. What is the pair $(\bar y,s_y)$?

- $(25,-4)$
- $(55,4)$
- $(25,4)$
- $(25,16)$

## Explanation

The mean follows the same transformation as the data and becomes $40-15=25$. For spread, examine the deviation $y_i-\bar y=(40-x_i)-(40-\bar x)=-(x_i-\bar x)$. The deviations change sign, but their squares and the divisor in the corrected sample variance stay the same. The standard deviation therefore remains $4$ and the required pair is $(25,4)$.
