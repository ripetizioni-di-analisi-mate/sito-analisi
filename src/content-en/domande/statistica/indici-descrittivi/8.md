A batch has mean mass $150\,\mathrm{g}$ and standard deviation $9\,\mathrm{g}$. Find its coefficient of variation as a percentage.

- $6\,\%$
- $0.06\,\%$
- $9\,\%$
- $16.7\,\%$

## Explanation

The coefficient of variation divides the standard deviation by the mean, expressing spread relative to the batch's typical mass. The calculation is $\dfrac{9\,\mathrm{g}}{150\,\mathrm{g}}=0.06$. Grams cancel and the decimal corresponds to $6\,\%$. Thus one standard deviation equals six percent of the mean mass.
