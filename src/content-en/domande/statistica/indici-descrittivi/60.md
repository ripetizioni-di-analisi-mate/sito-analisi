Two students on the same test have standardized scores $z_A=-0.5$ and $z_B=1.5$. The standard deviation is $6$ points. By how many points does $B$ exceed the raw score of $A$?

- $12$ points.
- $6$ points.
- $9$ points.
- $18$ points.

## Explanation

Both scores use the same mean and standard deviation. Subtracting the raw scores cancels the mean, leaving $s(z_B-z_A)$. The standardized difference is $1.5-(-0.5)=2$, so the raw difference is $6\cdot2=12$ points. The two results are therefore two test standard deviations apart.
