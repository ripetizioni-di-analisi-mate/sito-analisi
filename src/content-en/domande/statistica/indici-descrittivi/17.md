A laboratory measures masses in grams and converts every observation to kilograms. Which statement about the coefficient of variation is correct?

- It is divided by a thousand.
- It is multiplied by a thousand.
- It becomes a quantity in kilograms.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

Converting to kilograms divides each mass by a thousand. The mean is also divided by a thousand and each deviation is scaled in the same way, so the standard deviation is divided by a thousand. The conversion factors cancel in $\operatorname{CV}=\dfrac{s}{\bar x}$. The coefficient is therefore unchanged and dimensionless.
