In an ordered list of $20$ observations, which observation is the 90th percentile under the upper-rank rule?

- The 18th.
- The 19th.
- The mean of the 18th and 19th.
- The 2nd.

## Explanation

The 90th percentile is the quantile of order $p=0.9$. Its rank is $\lceil np\rceil$, and here $np=20\cdot0.9=18$. Since $18$ is already an integer, $\lceil18\rceil=18$, and the percentile is the 18th observation in the ordered list. Up to that rank the proportion of observations is $\dfrac{18}{20}=0.9$, which reaches the required order exactly.
