Two tests account for $30\,\%$ and $70\,\%$ of a grade. The scores are $80$ and $60$, respectively. Calculate the weighted grade.

- $66$
- $70$
- $74$
- $64$

## Explanation

The percentages assign different shares of the final grade to the tests. With decimal weights, the first test contributes $0.30\cdot80=24$ and the second $0.70\cdot60=42$. The weights sum to one, so the weighted grade is $24+42=66$. It lies closer to $60$, the result on the more heavily weighted test.
