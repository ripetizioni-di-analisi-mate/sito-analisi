For a sample of $7$ processing times, the sum of squared deviations from the mean is $30\,\mathrm{min^2}$. Calculate the corrected sample variance.

- $\dfrac{30}{7}\,\mathrm{min^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{min^2}$
- $\sqrt5\,\mathrm{min^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{min^2}$

## Explanation

The sum of squared deviations is already given. For the corrected sample variance, divide by $n-1$ because deviations from the sample's own mean are constrained to sum to zero. Here the divisor is $7-1=6$, so $s^2=\dfrac{30}{6}=5\,\mathrm{min}^2$. Dividing by seven would give the descriptive variance.
