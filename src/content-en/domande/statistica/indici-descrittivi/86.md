Six observations have mean $20$. Add $6$ to each of two observations and subtract $3$ from each of the other four. What is the new mean?

- $23$
- $17$
- $20$
- $21$

## Explanation

Mean $20$ gives an initial sum of $6\cdot20=120$. The increases add $2\cdot6=12$ to the sum and the decreases remove $4\cdot3=12$. These contributions cancel, so the total remains $120$. The sample size stays six, so the mean is still $20$. Updating the mean depends on the net change in the total, here zero.
