Four observations have mean $8$ and descriptive variance $4$. A value of $8$ is added. Find the new descriptive variance.

- $\dfrac{16}{5}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac45$
- $5$

## Explanation

The added observation equals the original mean, so the new mean stays $8$ and the added deviation is zero. The original variance gives a sum of squared deviations of $4\cdot4=16$. With five observations the descriptive variance becomes $\dfrac{16}{5}=3.2$. The divisor increases while the numerator stays fixed.
