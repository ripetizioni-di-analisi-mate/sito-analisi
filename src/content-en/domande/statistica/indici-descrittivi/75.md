A sample contains the values $1,1,4,4,7$. One additional observation equals $7$. What are the modes?

- Only $7$.
- $1$ and $4$.
- $1$, $4$ and $7$.
- $4$ and $7$.

## Explanation

Before the addition, $1$ and $4$ occur twice each and $7$ occurs once. The new observation increases only the frequency of $7$, bringing it to two. The maximum frequency is now shared by three values, so $1$, $4$, and $7$ are all modes. When counts tie, every value with the highest count is a mode.
