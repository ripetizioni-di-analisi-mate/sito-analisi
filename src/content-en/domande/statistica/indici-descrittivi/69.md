A company adopts the score $y=2x-10$. The scores $x$ have median $12$ and range $7$. Find these two measures for $y$.

- Median $14$ and range $14$.
- Median $14$ and range $4$.
- Median $24$ and range $14$.
- Median $2$ and range $7$.

## Explanation

The positive factor $2$ preserves score order. The median thus follows the same transformation and becomes $2\cdot12-10=14$. The range is the maximum minus the minimum. In this difference the additive term $-10$ cancels and the factor $2$ remains, giving the new range $2\cdot7=14$.
