The measurements $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ are in meters. By what factor does the numerical value of $s^2$ change on conversion to centimeters?

- $10\,000$
- $100$
- $1\,000$
- $1$

## Explanation

Conversion from meters to centimeters multiplies every observation and the mean by $100$. Each deviation is therefore multiplied by $100$ and each squared deviation by $100^2$. The divisor $n-1$ stays fixed because the count is unchanged. The numerical value of $s^2$ is thus multiplied by $10\,000$.
