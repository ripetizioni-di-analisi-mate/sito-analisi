In the data $1,2,5,5,8,9$, one occurrence of $5$ is replaced by $12$. Find the new median.

- $6.5$
- $5$
- $8$
- $8.5$

## Explanation

Replacing one occurrence of $5$ by $12$ gives the ordered list $1,2,5,8,9,12$. The sample size remains six, so the median uses positions three and four. After sorting they contain $5$ and $8$, whose mean is $\dfrac{5+8}{2}=6.5$. The former fourth observation is no longer in the middle, and $8$ has taken its place.
