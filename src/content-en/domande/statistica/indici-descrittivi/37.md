Under the conversion $y=4x+3$, the original quartiles $Q_1=2$ and $Q_3=5$ become two new quartiles. Find the new IQR.

- $15$
- $12$
- $20$
- $3$

## Explanation

The increasing transformation $y=4x+3$ preserves order and maps the quartiles to $4\cdot2+3=11$ and $4\cdot5+3=23$. Their new distance is $23-11=12$. The common additive term $3$ cancels in the difference. Multiplying the original interquartile range $5-2=3$ by four gives the same result.
