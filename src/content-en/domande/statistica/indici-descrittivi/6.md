A set of values $x_i$ has descriptive variance $2$. Find the descriptive variance of the transformed values $y_i=15-3x_i$.

- $6$
- $18$
- $17$
- $9$

## Explanation

The transformed mean is $\bar y=15-3\bar x$. Subtracting it from each $y_i$ cancels $15$ and leaves deviation $-3(x_i-\bar x)$. Thus every squared deviation is multiplied by $(-3)^2=9$. Since descriptive variance is the mean of these squares, it becomes $9\cdot2=18$. Reversing the signs of the deviations leaves their squares positive.
