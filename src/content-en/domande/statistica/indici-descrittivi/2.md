Four classes last $8$ minutes on average, and six other classes last $13$ minutes on average. Find the mean duration of all ten classes.

- $10.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $10\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12\,\mathrm{min}$
- $11\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Multiply each group's mean duration by its number of classes. This gives $4\cdot8=32$ and $6\cdot13=78$ minutes, or $110$ minutes altogether. The overall mean is $\dfrac{110}{4+6}=11\,\mathrm{min}$. It lies closer to $13$ than to $8$ because the second group contains more classes.
