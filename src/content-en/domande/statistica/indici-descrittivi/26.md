The sum of squared deviations from the mean for four observations is $12$. The mean is $5$. What is the sum of squared deviations from the center $7$?

- $12$
- $28$
- $20$
- $16$

## Explanation

Moving the center away from the mean increases the sum of squared deviations by $n(c-\bar x)^2$. The distance between $7$ and the mean $5$ is $2$, so the increase for four observations is $4\cdot2^2=16$. Adding it to the original sum gives $12+16=28$. The cross term vanishes because deviations from the mean sum to zero.
