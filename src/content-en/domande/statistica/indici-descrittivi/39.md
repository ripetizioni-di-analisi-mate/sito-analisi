A raw threshold of $42$ corresponds to $z=0.5$. The standard deviation is $8$. Find the mean.

- $46$
- $34$
- $38$
- $40$

## Explanation

A standardized score of $0.5$ places the threshold half a standard deviation above the mean. On the original scale the distance is $0.5\cdot8=4$. Thus $42=\bar x+4$ and the mean is $42-4=38$. The positive sign of $z$ means that the distance must be subtracted from the threshold to recover the center.
