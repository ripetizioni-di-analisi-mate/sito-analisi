On a test, the scores $50$ and $74$ have standardized scores $-1$ and $2$. What are the mean and standard deviation of the test?

- Mean $62$ and standard deviation $8$.
- Mean $58$ and standard deviation $8$.
- Mean $58$ and standard deviation $12$.
- Mean $62$ and standard deviation $12$.

## Explanation

By the inverse formula $x=\bar x+sz$, the two scores give the equations $50=\bar x-s$ and $74=\bar x+2s$. Subtracting the first from the second gives $24=3s$, so $s=8$. Substituting into the first equation gives $\bar x=50+8=58$. As a check, $\dfrac{74-58}{8}=2$.
