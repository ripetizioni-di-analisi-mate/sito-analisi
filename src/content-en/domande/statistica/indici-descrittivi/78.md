The two middle observations in six ordered measurements are $7$ and $11$. Which measure of location leaves at least half the data at or below it and at least half at or above it, following the median convention used in this course?

- $9$
- $7$
- $11$
- $4$

## Explanation

For six measurements the middle positions are the third and fourth. Every number between $7$ and $11$ inclusive has at least three observations at or below it and at least three at or above it. The course convention selects a single number by averaging the middle values, giving $\dfrac{7+11}{2}=9$.
