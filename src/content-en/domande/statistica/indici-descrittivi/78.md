For six ordered measurements the two middle observations are $7$ and $11$. Every number between $7$ and $11$ has at least three observations at or below it and at least three at or above it. Which of these numbers is the median under the convention used in this course?

- $9$
- $7$
- $11$
- $4$

## Explanation

With six measurements the middle observations have ranks three and four. Every number between $7$ and $11$ inclusive leaves at least half of the observations on each side. The course convention selects in that interval the mean of the two middle observations, so the median is $\dfrac{7+11}{2}=9$.
