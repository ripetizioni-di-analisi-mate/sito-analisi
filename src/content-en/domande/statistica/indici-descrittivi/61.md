The standard deviation of $x$ is $12$. After the transformation $y=3+bx$, with $b<0$, the standard deviation of $y$ is $3$. Find $b$.

- $\dfrac14$
- $-4$
- $-\dfrac14$
- $-\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

In an affine transformation the additive term $3$ shifts both data and mean and cancels in the deviations. The factor $b$ multiplies the deviations, so the standard deviation is multiplied by $|b|$. The relation $3=12|b|$ gives $|b|=\dfrac14$. Since the sign is specified as negative, the required value is $b=-\dfrac14$.
