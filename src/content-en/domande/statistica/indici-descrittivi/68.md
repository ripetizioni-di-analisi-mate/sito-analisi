A set of masses has mean $50\,\mathrm{g}$ and standard deviation $4\,\mathrm{g}$. The mass of a $30\,\mathrm{g}$ container is added to each mass. What is the new coefficient of variation?

- $8\,\%$
- $5\,\%$
- $12.8\,\%$
- $13.3\,\%$

## Explanation

Adding $30\,\mathrm{g}$ to each mass is the affine change $y=30+x$. The mean becomes $50+30=80\,\mathrm{g}$, while the standard deviation stays $4\,\mathrm{g}$ because the deviations from the mean do not change. The new coefficient of variation is therefore $\dfrac{4}{80}=0.05$, or $5\,\%$, compared with the initial $8\,\%$, $\dfrac{4}{50}$.
