A process produces items of mean mass $80$ and standard deviation $4$. What standard deviation is needed to reduce the coefficient of variation to $2.5\,\%$ at the same mean?

- $2$
- $1$
- $2.5$
- $3$

## Explanation

A coefficient of variation of $2.5\,\%$ requires the standard deviation to equal $0.025$ times the mean. With the mean fixed at $80$, the new standard deviation must be $0.025\cdot80=2$. The original ratio was $\dfrac4{80}=0.05$. With a fixed mean, halving the relative spread from five to two and a half percent requires halving the standard deviation.
