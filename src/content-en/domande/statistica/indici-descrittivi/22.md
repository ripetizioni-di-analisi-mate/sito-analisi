An observation has standardized score $-1.5$. Given a mean of $19$ and a standard deviation of $4$, find the original observation.

- $25$
- $17.5$
- $6$
- $13$

## Explanation

A standardized score is the deviation from the mean divided by the standard deviation. To return to the original scale, multiply $-1.5$ by $4$ to get a deviation of $-6$. Adding it to the mean gives $19-6=13$. The negative standardized score is consistent with a measurement below the mean.
