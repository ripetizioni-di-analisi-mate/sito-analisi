Five measurements have mean $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Four of them are $8$, $10$, $13$, and $14\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the fifth.

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The mean is the sum of the measurements divided by their number, so begin with the required total, $5\cdot12=60\,\mathrm{cm}$. The four known measurements contribute $8+10+13+14=45\,\mathrm{cm}$. The fifth is the only missing part and therefore equals $60-45=15\,\mathrm{cm}$. Adding it gives the total $60\,\mathrm{cm}$, and dividing by five recovers the stated mean.
