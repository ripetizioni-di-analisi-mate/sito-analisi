Two groups of mass measurements have the same positive mean. The first has twice the corrected sample variance of the second. If the variances are positive, what is the ratio $\dfrac{\operatorname{CV}_1}{\operatorname{CV}_2}$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\sqrt2$

## Explanation

Since the means are equal and positive, the common mean cancels in the ratio of coefficients of variation, leaving the ratio $\dfrac{s_1}{s_2}$ of the standard deviations. The first variance is twice the second, so $s_1^2=2s_2^2$. Both standard deviations are positive, hence $\dfrac{s_1}{s_2}=\sqrt{2}$.
