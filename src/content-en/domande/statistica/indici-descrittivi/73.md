Four observations have descriptive variance $9$. What value would be obtained by mistakenly using the divisor $n-1$?

- $12$
- $6.75$
- $3$
- $36$

## Explanation

The descriptive variance divides the sum of squared deviations by four. Its value $9$ gives the sum $4\cdot9=36$. Using the divisor $n-1=3$ instead gives $\dfrac{36}{3}=12$. The deviations are the same and only the divisor changes. The result $12$ is the corrected sample variance, whereas the requested measure is the descriptive variance.
