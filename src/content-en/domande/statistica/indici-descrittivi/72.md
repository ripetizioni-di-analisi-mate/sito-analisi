A box plot has observed minimum $1$, lower whisker endpoint $4$, $Q_1=6$, and $\operatorname{IQR}=2$. Find the lower fence.

- $1$
- $4$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

Subtract one and a half interquartile ranges from $Q_1$ to get the lower fence. Since $1.5\cdot2=3$, the calculation gives $L=6-3=3$. The observation $1$ lies below that threshold, so the whisker can end at $4$, the first observation inside. The fence can therefore lie between an individually plotted point and the whisker endpoint.
