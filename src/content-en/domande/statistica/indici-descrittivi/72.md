The two Tukey fences of a box plot are $-2$ and $22$. What is the interquartile range?

- $6$
- $24$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The fences are $L=Q_1-1.5\operatorname{IQR}$ and $U=Q_3+1.5\operatorname{IQR}$. Their distance is therefore $U-L=(Q_3-Q_1)+3\operatorname{IQR}=4\operatorname{IQR}$. Since $U-L=22-(-2)=24$, we get $\operatorname{IQR}=\dfrac{24}{4}=6$. Consequently the quartiles are $Q_1=-2+1.5\cdot6=7$ and $Q_3=22-1.5\cdot6=13$.
