Three observations have mean $0$ and sample standard deviation $2$. After standardization, what is their descriptive variance using divisor $3$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac32$
- $4$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Since the original sample standard deviation is $2$, the sum of squared deviations is $(3-1)\cdot2^2=8$. Standardization divides each deviation by $2$ and hence its square by $4$. On the new scale the sum is $2$. Dividing by the descriptive divisor $3$ gives $\dfrac23$. The value $1$ would correspond to the corrected divisor $3-1$.
