Two mass samples have means $40$ and $100\,\mathrm{g}$ and standard deviations $4$ and $8\,\mathrm{g}$, respectively. Which comparison is correct?

- The second has a larger CV and a smaller standard deviation.
- The first has a larger CV and a smaller standard deviation.
- The CVs are equal.
- The first has both the larger CV and the larger standard deviation.

## Explanation

The second sample has the larger standard deviation in grams, since $8>4$. To compare relative spread, divide each standard deviation by its own mean. This gives $\dfrac4{40}=10\,\%$ versus $\dfrac8{100}=8\,\%$. The first sample has less spread in grams, but one standard deviation represents a larger share of its mean mass.
