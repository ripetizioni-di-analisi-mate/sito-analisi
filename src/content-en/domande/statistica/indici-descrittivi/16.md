In a survey, green, white, blue, and red receive 4, 7, 7, and 2 votes, respectively. Which colors are modes?

- White only.
- White and blue.
- Blue only.
- Green and red.

## Explanation

Compare the counts for the colors. White and blue each have frequency $7$, greater than $4$ for green and $2$ for red. Both attain the maximum frequency and are modes. The number $7$ is their shared frequency. The modes are the names of the categories attaining it.
