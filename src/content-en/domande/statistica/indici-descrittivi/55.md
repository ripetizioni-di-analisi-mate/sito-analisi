The values $6,8,10,x$ have mean $9$. Replace $x$ with $x+4$. Find the new mean.

- $9$
- $11$
- $10$
- $13$

## Explanation

The initial mean $9$ corresponds to a total of $4\cdot9=36$. Replacing $x$ with $x+4$ increases the total by $4$ without changing the sample size. The new sum is $40$ and the new mean $\dfrac{40}{4}=10$. The value of $x$ is unnecessary because updating the mean requires only the change in the total.
