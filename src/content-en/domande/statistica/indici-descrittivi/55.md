Three shops stand along a straight road, at kilometers $2$, $3$, and $11$. A warehouse is to be placed at a point on the road so that the sum of its distances from the three shops is as small as possible. What is this minimum sum, in kilometers?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{34}{3}$
- $10$
- $17$

## Explanation

The sum of the distances of the observations from a number $c$, namely $S(c)=|2-c|+|3-c|+|11-c|$, is minimized when $c$ is the median of the observations. With three observations the median is the middle one, $3$. Hence the warehouse goes at kilometer $3$, and the minimum sum is $S(3)=1+0+8=9$. At the mean $\dfrac{16}{3}$ the sum is $\dfrac{10}{3}+\dfrac{7}{3}+\dfrac{17}{3}=\dfrac{34}{3}$, which is greater than $9$. At the two outer shops the sum is $S(2)=0+1+9=10$ and $S(11)=9+8+0=17$.
