A variable records department names with no ordering. Which measure of location can be determined from the frequencies alone?

- The numerical median.
- The mode.
- The arithmetic mean of the names.
- The first quartile of the names.

## Explanation

Department names are unordered categories. The category with maximum frequency gives the mode, and several departments can tie. The median and quartiles require an ordering, whereas the arithmetic mean requires numerical values for which addition and division are meaningful.
