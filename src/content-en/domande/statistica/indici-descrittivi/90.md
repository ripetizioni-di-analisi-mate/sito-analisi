The data $x$ have positive standard deviation. Define $y=10-2x$ and standardize the two data sets separately. If an observation has $z_x=1.3$, what is its corresponding $z_y$?

- $1.3$
- $-2.6$
- $7.4$
- $-1.3$

## Explanation

The mean of $y$ is $10-2\bar x$, so the transformed deviation is $-2(x_i-\bar x)$. The standard deviation is instead multiplied by the absolute value of the factor, $2$. Dividing the new deviation by the new standard deviation gives $z_y=-z_x=-1.3$. The distance in standard deviations stays the same, but the reversed scale changes the side of the mean.
