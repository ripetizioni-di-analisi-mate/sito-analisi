Four values have mean $6$. What value must be added for the five observations to have mean $7$?

- $7$
- $10$
- $11$
- $13$

## Explanation

The four values sum to $4\cdot6=24$. To give five values a mean of $7$, their total must reach $5\cdot7=35$. Hence the added value is $35-24=11$. It exceeds the new mean because the total must increase enough to offset division by five instead of four.
