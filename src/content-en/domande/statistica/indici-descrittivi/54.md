Sensor readings have mean $250$ and coefficient of variation $4\,\%$. Find the standard deviation.

- $4$
- $62.5$
- $10$
- $100$

## Explanation

The coefficient of variation is the standard deviation divided by the mean. Since $4\,\%$ equals $0.04$, the standard deviation is four percent of $250$. Multiplication gives $s=0.04\cdot250=10$. The coefficient is dimensionless, so $10$ has the same units as the observations.
