Two tests have (mean, standard deviation) pairs $(60,5)$ and $(70,10)$, respectively. The scores are $65$ and $82$. Which is further above its mean in standard deviations?

- The first, with $z=1$.
- They are equally far above their means, with $z=1$.
- The second, with $z=1.2$.
- The second, with $z=2.4$.

## Explanation

Divide each deviation by its test's standard deviation. The first score is $5$ points above its mean and gives $z_1=\dfrac{5}{5}=1$. The second is $12$ points above its mean and gives $z_2=\dfrac{12}{10}=1.2$. The second therefore lies further above its own center even on the standardized scale.
