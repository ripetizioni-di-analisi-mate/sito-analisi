For the ordered data $1,3,7,11$, which statement follows the convention used in this course for the quantile of order $0.5$?

- $q_{0.5}=3$, the observation at rank two.
- $q_{0.5}=5$, the mean of the middle pair.
- $q_{0.5}=7$, the observation at rank three.
- $q_{0.5}=5.5$, the mean of all four observations.

## Explanation

This course uses the median convention for order $0.5$. With four values the middle lies between the second and third, $3$ and $7$. Their midpoint is $\dfrac{3+7}{2}=5$. Rounding the rank up here would give $3$, whereas the question specifies the convention for the median.
