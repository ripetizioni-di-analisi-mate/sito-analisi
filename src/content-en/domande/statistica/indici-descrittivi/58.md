A technician compares coefficients of variation for samples of the same quantity. When is the ratio particularly sensitive to small changes in the mean?

- When all measurements are converted from meters to centimeters.
- When the ratio is expressed as a percentage.
- When the positive mean is very close to zero.
- When the observations are sorted in increasing order.

## Explanation

The coefficient of variation divides the standard deviation by the mean. With a positive mean near zero, even a small absolute change can be a large change relative to the denominator. For example, if the mean changes from $0.02$ to $0.01$ at constant standard deviation, the ratio doubles. A multiplicative unit conversion, however, applies the same factor to both quantities.
