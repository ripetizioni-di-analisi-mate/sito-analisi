A box plot has $Q_1=4$ and $Q_3=10$. Find Tukey's lower fence.

- $-9$
- $-5$
- $1$
- $-11$

## Explanation

Start at the first quartile and move toward smaller values. The box has width $10-4=6$, so one and a half times this width is $1.5\cdot6=9$. Subtracting that distance from $Q_1$ gives $L=4-9=-5$. The fence is a threshold calculated from the quartiles and need not be an observed value.
