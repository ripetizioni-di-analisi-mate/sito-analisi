Two data sets have quartiles $4$ and $8$. The first has maximum $13$ and the second maximum $20$. What does Tukey's rule imply about the maxima?

- Both are within the fences.
- The first is within the fences and the second beyond.
- Both are beyond the fences.
- The first is beyond the fences and the second within.

## Explanation

The quartiles agree, so both sets have IQR $8-4=4$ and the same upper fence $8+1.5\cdot4=14$. The maximum $13$ is less than $14$, so it lies within the fence and forms the upper whisker endpoint. The maximum $20$ exceeds the fence and is plotted separately in the second set.
