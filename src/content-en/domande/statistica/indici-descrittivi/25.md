In the data $4,7,8,9,12$, the maximum is corrected from $12$ to $22$. Which measure increases by $10$?

- The median.
- The interquartile range with ranks rounded up.
- The mean.
- The range.

## Explanation

The range is the maximum minus the minimum. Before correction it is $12-4=8$ and afterward $22-4=18$, an increase of $10$. The mean increases only by $\dfrac{10}{5}=2$ because the change in the total is spread across five observations. The median and quartiles stay the same since their positions precede the maximum.
