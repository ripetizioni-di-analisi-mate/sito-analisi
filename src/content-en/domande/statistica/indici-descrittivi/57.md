The values $-1,1$ have descriptive variance $1$. Each is recorded twice, producing four observations. What are $(v,s^2)$ now?

- $\left(1,\dfrac43\right)$
- $(1,2)$
- $\left(\dfrac12,1\right)$
- $\left(2,\dfrac83\right)$

## Explanation

Duplicating both observations gives $-1,-1,1,1$, with mean still zero. The sum of squared deviations becomes $4$. Dividing by four leaves the descriptive variance at $v=1$ because both the sum and the count doubled. The corrected variance instead divides by $4-1=3$, so $s^2=\dfrac43$.
