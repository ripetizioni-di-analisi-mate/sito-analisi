For eight observations, the corrected sample variance is $16\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find the descriptive variance.

- $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{128}{7}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The corrected sample variance divides the sum of squared deviations by seven. Multiplying $16$ by $7$ therefore recovers $D=112\,\mathrm{m}^2$. The descriptive variance divides the same sum by $8$ and equals $\dfrac{112}{8}=14\,\mathrm{m}^2$. It is smaller because its denominator is larger and the deviations are unchanged.
