Two box plots agree in $Q_1=5$, median $8$, $Q_3=12$, and both whiskers. Which other quantity must agree?

- The arithmetic mean.
- The interquartile range.
- The sample standard deviation.
- The number of observations.

## Explanation

The interquartile range depends only on the positions of the two box edges. Both plots have edges at $5$ and $12$, so both have IQR $12-5=7$. Equal whiskers and medians give additional information about the most extreme observations within the fences and the center, but leave many individual values undetermined, as well as the mean and variance.
