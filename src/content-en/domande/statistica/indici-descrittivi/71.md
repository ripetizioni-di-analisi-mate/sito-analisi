A student's credit-weighted mean is $25$ over $60$ credits. What grade is needed on a $12$-credit exam to raise the weighted mean to $25.5$?

- $25.5$
- $26$
- $31$
- $28$

## Explanation

The weighted mean is the total of the grades multiplied by their credits divided by the total number of credits. The current total is $25\cdot60=1500$. With the new exam there are $72$ credits, so the required total is $25.5\cdot72=1836$. The grade $x$ must satisfy $1500+12x=1836$, which gives $12x=336$ and $x=28$.
