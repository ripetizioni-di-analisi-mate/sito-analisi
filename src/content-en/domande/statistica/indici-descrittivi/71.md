In a semester, the mean grade on $4$ exams worth $6$ credits each is $27$. A fifth exam worth $12$ credits earns a grade of $24$. Find the credit-weighted mean.

- $26$
- $25.5$
- $26.4$
- $27$

## Explanation

The credits are the weights of the grades. The first four exams each have weight $6$, so together they represent $24$ credits with mean $27$ and contribute $24\cdot27=648$. The fifth contributes $12\cdot24=288$. The weighted total is $936$ and the total weight is $24+12=36$, giving mean $\dfrac{936}{36}=26$.
