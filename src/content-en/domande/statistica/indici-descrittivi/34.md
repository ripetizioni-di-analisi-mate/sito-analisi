Given the values $2,3,10$, which center $c$ minimizes $\sum_i(x_i-c)^2$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The mean of these data is $\dfrac{2+3+10}{3}=5$. The sum of squared deviations from a center $c$ is the sum of squared deviations from $5$ plus $3(c-5)^2$. The added term is nonnegative and vanishes exactly when $c=5$. Thus the mean is the unique minimizer, even though the middle observation in the sorted list is $3$.
