A set of volume measurements has corrected sample variance $25\,\mathrm{L^2}$. What does $5\,\mathrm{L}$ represent for the same measurements?

- The interquartile range.
- The sum of squared deviations.
- The sample standard deviation.
- The mean volume.

## Explanation

The corrected sample variance is in square liters because each deviation is squared. Taking its square root returns to the volume scale. Here $\sqrt{25\,\mathrm{L}^2}=5\,\mathrm{L}$ is the sample standard deviation. It measures spread around the mean. Finding the mean volume would instead require the sum of the observations.
