Three histogram classes have widths 1, 3, and 2 and counts 4, 9, and 6. Which bar is tallest when area represents count?

- The first.
- The second.
- The third.
- The second and third are tied.

## Explanation

Each rectangle's area gives its class count, so divide frequency by class width to find height. The heights are $\dfrac41=4$, $\dfrac93=3$, and $\dfrac62=3$. The first bar is tallest because it concentrates four observations in one unit of width. The second class has the largest area.
