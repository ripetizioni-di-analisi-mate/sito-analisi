Three distinct measurements are standardized. What is the sum of their three standardized scores?

- $0$
- $1$
- $3$
- It depends on the units.

## Explanation

Standardization subtracts the same mean from each measurement and divides by a common positive standard deviation. The deviations sum to zero because $\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)=\sum_i x_i-3\bar x=0$. Dividing their sum by the standard deviation again gives zero. Thus the arithmetic mean on the standardized scale is the origin.
