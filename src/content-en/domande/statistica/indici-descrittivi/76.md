Five observations have mean $6$ and sum of squares $200$. Find the sum of squared deviations from the mean.

- $40$
- $20$
- $170$
- $194$

## Explanation

Expanding the squared deviations gives $D=\sum_i x_i^2-n\bar x^2$ because $\sum_i x_i=n\bar x$. The term to subtract is $5\cdot6^2=180$, so the stated sum of squares gives $D=200-180=20$. The question asks for a sum, so stop before the division that would produce a variance.
