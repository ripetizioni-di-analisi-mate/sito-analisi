For a data set the descriptive variance is $12$ and the corrected sample variance is $15$. How many observations does the set contain?

- $4$
- $5$
- $3$
- $15$

## Explanation

The two variances have the same numerator $D$, the sum of squared deviations, with divisors $n$ and $n-1$. Thus $D=12n$ and $D=15(n-1)$. Setting them equal gives $12n=15n-15$, so $3n=15$ and $n=5$. As a check, $D=60$, and indeed $\dfrac{60}{5}=12$ and $\dfrac{60}{4}=15$.
