Two distributions of nine values have the same median and first quartile. The second distribution's third quartile exceeds the first's by $3$. How does its interquartile range change?

- It increases by $3$.
- It stays the same.
- It increases by $6$.
- It increases by $1.5$.

## Explanation

The interquartile range is the third quartile minus the first. The first stays fixed and the third increases by three units, so the new interquartile range is $(Q_3+3)-Q_1=(Q_3-Q_1)+3$. The median can remain fixed even as the two edges of the central region move further apart.
