To test a variance, $17$ independent normal observations are available. How many degrees of freedom does the chi-square statistic have?

- $32$
- $15$
- $16$
- $17$

## Explanation

The chi-square statistic uses the sum of squared deviations from the sample mean estimated from $17$ observations. The deviations sum to zero, so the last is determined by the others. This leaves $17-1=16$ free components. With independent normal observations the reference distribution under $H_0$ is chi-square with $16$ degrees of freedom.
