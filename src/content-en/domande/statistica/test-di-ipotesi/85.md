In a table of subscription type by outcome, one row has twice the total of another, but the percentage distribution of outcomes is identical across all rows. How do observed and expected counts compare under independence?

- They agree only if all column totals are equal.
- They agree only in the larger row.
- They agree in every cell.
- They must differ in the smaller row.

## Explanation

For each outcome the overall proportion is the average of its row proportions weighted by row size. Since the row proportions coincide, their overall proportion equals each of them. Expected counts under independence are each row total times that proportion. This reproduces the observed count in every cell.
