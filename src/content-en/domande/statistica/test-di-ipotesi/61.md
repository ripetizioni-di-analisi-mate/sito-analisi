A laboratory wants to detect any departure of the mean concentration from the declared value $5$, in either direction. Which alternative expresses this question?

- $H_1:\mu=5$
- $H_1:\mu\ne5$
- $H_1:\mu>5$
- $H_1:\mu<5$

## Explanation

The question concerns both mean concentrations above $5$ and those below $5$. The null hypothesis sets the mean equal to the declared value. The alternative includes all other values and is written $H_1:\mu\ne5$. It is two-sided, so the decision rule must consider sufficiently large absolute deviations in either direction.
