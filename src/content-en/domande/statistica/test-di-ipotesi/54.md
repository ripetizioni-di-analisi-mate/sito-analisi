A sample mean is normal with standard error $2$. A two-sided test rejects if $\bar X\le16$ or $\bar X\ge24$. If the true mean is $24$, calculate the power using $\Phi(-4)\approx0.00003$.

- $0.5$
- $0.49997$
- $0.00003$
- $0.50003$

## Explanation

Power adds the probabilities of the two rejection regions under the true mean $24$. The upper threshold equals the distribution's center, so the area to its right is $0.5$. The lower threshold corresponds to $\dfrac{16-24}{2}=-4$ with left-tail area approximately $0.00003$. The regions are disjoint, giving approximate power $0.5+0.00003=0.50003$.
