In a table of $96$ units, a row has total $24$ and a column has total $32$. Their cell has observed count $11$. What is its contribution to Pearson's statistic?

- $1.125$
- $3$
- $9$
- $0.375$

## Explanation

Under independence the column's marginal proportion $\dfrac{32}{96}=\dfrac13$ also applies to the $24$ units in the row. The cell's expected count is $24\cdot\dfrac13=8$. The observed count $11$ exceeds it by $3$ units. The contribution to Pearson's statistic is $\dfrac{(11-8)^2}{8}=\dfrac98=1.125$, using the expected count of that cell as denominator.
