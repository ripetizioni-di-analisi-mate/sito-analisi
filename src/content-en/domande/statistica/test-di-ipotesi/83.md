Two independent proportions are compared using a score test for equality. Why does the formula use a pooled proportion?

- The two sample proportions are always identical.
- The pooled proportion makes the samples dependent.
- The groups must have the same sample size.
- Under the null hypothesis both groups have the same population proportion.

## Explanation

Under the equality hypothesis the two groups share one success probability. Estimate it by dividing total successes by total trials across both groups. This gives each observation equal weight even when group sizes differ. The pooled proportion estimates the null parameter in the variance of the difference.
