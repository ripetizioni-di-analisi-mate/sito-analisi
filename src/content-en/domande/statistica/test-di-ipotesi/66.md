A very large study estimates an increase of $0.01$ units with $p=0.001$. What information is needed to assess practical importance?

- A threshold for practical importance in the original units and the uncertainty of the estimate.
- The prior probability that the null hypothesis is true, obtained from $p$.
- Only the number of decimal places in the p-value.
- The opposite direction for the test, selected after seeing the data.

## Explanation

With many observations the standard error can be small enough for even a difference of $0.01$ units to give a large statistic and low p-value. Practical relevance depends on the effect's magnitude in the original units. Compare the estimate and its uncertainty, for example a confidence interval, with a threshold of practical importance specified for the setting.
