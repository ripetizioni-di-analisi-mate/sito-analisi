Two distinct, independent groups are compared on their mean scores. The variances are unknown and may differ. Which procedure matches this design?

- Welch's test for two independent means.
- An exact binomial test for one proportion.
- A paired t test on individual differences.
- A z test for two means with known variances.

## Explanation

The two groups contain distinct independent units, so compare their sample means via the difference. The variances are unknown and may differ, requiring a separate standard-error estimate for each mean. Welch's test uses both estimates and their respective samples. A paired test would require observations matched in pairs across the groups.
