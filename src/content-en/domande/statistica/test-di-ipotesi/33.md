Two independent samples each contain $50$ observations, with $30$ and $20$ successes. Calculate the score statistic for $H_0:p_1=p_2$, subtracting the second proportion from the first.

- $4$
- $2$
- $0.2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The observed proportions are $\dfrac{30}{50}=0.60$ and $\dfrac{20}{50}=0.40$, giving numerator $0.20$. Under equality the common proportion is estimated by $\dfrac{30+20}{100}=0.50$. Independence allows the variances of the two sample proportions to be added. The estimated standard error under $H_0$ is $\sqrt{0.25\left(\dfrac1{50}+\dfrac1{50}\right)}=0.10$, so the difference divided by the standard error is $\dfrac{0.20}{0.10}=2$.
