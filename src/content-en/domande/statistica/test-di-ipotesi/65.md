A small study gives $p=0.45$ in a test of no effect. What conclusion follows at level $0.05$?

- The test proves that the effect is zero.
- The power of the test is $0.55$.
- The probability that the effect is zero is $0.45$.
- The test fails to reject. This result alone leaves open the possibility of an effect.

## Explanation

Reject the null hypothesis if the p-value is at most the level. Here $0.45>0.05$, so the test fails to reject. The study is small and sampling variability may leave appreciable effects compatible with the data. A confidence interval based on the same model shows which effect sizes remain plausible.
