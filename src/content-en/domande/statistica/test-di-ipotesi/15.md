With known standard error $0.8$, the lower threshold for a z test of $H_0:\mu\ge12$ is $12+z_{0.05}\cdot0.8$. Calculate it using $z_{0.05}=-1.645$.

- $10.04$
- $10.684$
- $13.316$
- $11.355$

## Explanation

The quantile $z_{0.05}$ marks the lower tail on the standard normal scale. Multiply by the standard error $0.8$ and add the null center $12$ to express it on the sample-mean scale, giving $c=12+(-1.645)\cdot0.8=12-1.316=10.684$. The threshold is below $12$ because sufficiently small sample means lead to rejection.
