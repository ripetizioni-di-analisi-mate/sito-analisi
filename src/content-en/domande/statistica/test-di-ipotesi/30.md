In this exercise, a score test of $p_0=0.02$ requires at least five expected successes and five expected failures. What is the smallest sample size meeting both constraints?

- $200$
- $245$
- $100$
- $250$

## Explanation

Under the null value the expected success and failure counts are $0.02n$ and $0.98n$. The first constraint gives $n\ge\dfrac{5}{0.02}=250$. Checking the second at $n=250$ gives $0.98\cdot250=245$ expected failures. This satisfies the second condition, and smaller sizes fail the first, so the minimum sample size is $250$.
