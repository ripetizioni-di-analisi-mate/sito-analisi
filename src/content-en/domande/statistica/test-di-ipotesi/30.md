A company uses the score test to check that the proportion of conforming items is $p_0=0.975$; a conforming item counts as a success. In the exercises the test requires at least five expected successes and at least five expected failures. What is the smallest sample size that satisfies both conditions?

- $6$
- $195$
- $205$
- $200$

## Explanation

Under the null value the expected successes are $0.975n$ and the expected failures are $(1-0.975)n=0.025n$. The condition on successes, $0.975n\ge5$, already holds for $n\ge6$, because $\dfrac{5}{0.975}\approx5.13$. The condition on failures, $0.025n\ge5$, requires $n\ge\dfrac{5}{0.025}=200$. Both conditions must hold together, so the more restrictive one decides, and the smallest sample size is $200$. With $n=200$ the expected numbers are $195$ conforming items and $5$ nonconforming ones.
