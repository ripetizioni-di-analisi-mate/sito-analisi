The observed z statistic equals $-1.8$. Calculate the two-sided p-value using $\Phi(1.8)=0.9641$.

- $0.0359$
- $0.9641$
- $0.9282$
- $0.0718$

## Explanation

A two-sided test considers deviations from the null mean in both directions. A result is at least as extreme as $-1.8$ if $Z\le-1.8$ or $Z\ge1.8$. The right-tail probability is $1-\Phi(1.8)=1-0.9641=0.0359$. By symmetry the left tail has the same probability. These regions are disjoint, so the p-value is $2\cdot0.0359=0.0718$.
