Given $100$ trials, null hypothesis $p_0=\dfrac12$, and observed score statistic $-1.6$, how many successes were observed?

- $48$
- $58$
- $42$
- $34$

## Explanation

Under $H_0$ the success count has expectation $100\cdot\dfrac12=50$ and standard deviation $\sqrt{100\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12}=5$. The score statistic expresses the difference from the expected count in units of that standard deviation. Let $x$ denote the observed count. Then $\dfrac{x-50}{5}=-1.6$. From $x-50=-8$ we obtain $x=42$, eight successes below expectation.
