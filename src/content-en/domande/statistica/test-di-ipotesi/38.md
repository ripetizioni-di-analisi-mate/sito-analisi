One category has $O=12$ and $E=16$. Calculate the standardized Pearson residual, defined as $\dfrac{O-E}{\sqrt E}$.

- $-1$
- $4$
- $-0.25$
- $1$

## Explanation

The raw difference is $O-E=12-16=-4$. The specified standardized residual divides it by $\sqrt E=\sqrt{16}=4$, giving $\dfrac{-4}{4}=-1$. Its sign shows that the category has fewer observations than expected. The cell's contribution to Pearson's statistic is the square of the residual, $1$.
