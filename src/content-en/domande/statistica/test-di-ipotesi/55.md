An upper-tailed normal test rejects if $Z\ge1.645$. After inspecting the sign, rejection is also allowed when $Z\le-1.645$. If under $H_0$ each tail has probability $0.05$, what is the level of the new rule?

- $0.025$
- $0.05$
- $0.1$
- $0.95$

## Explanation

The modified rule rejects above $1.645$ in the upper tail or up to $-1.645$ in the lower tail. Under $H_0$ the rejection events are disjoint and each has probability $0.05$. The new level is therefore $0.05+0.05=0.10$. To retain level $0.05$ in a symmetric two-sided test, each tail would receive $0.025$.
