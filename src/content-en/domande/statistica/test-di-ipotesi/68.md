Upper- and lower-tailed p-values are calculated for the same positive observed z statistic. Which is smaller?

- The p-value for the lower-tailed alternative.
- The p-value for the upper-tailed alternative.
- It depends on the chosen significance level.
- They are equal by symmetry.

## Explanation

For a positive observed statistic, the standard normal area to its left exceeds one-half and the area to its right is less than one-half. The upper-tailed test uses the right tail and the lower-tailed test the left tail at the same point. The upper-tailed p-value is therefore smaller.
