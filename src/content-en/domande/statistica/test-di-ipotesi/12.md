For $25$ independent pairs, the after-minus-before differences are normal with observed mean $1.5$ and sample standard deviation $2.5$ calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction. Calculate the t statistic for $H_0:\mu_D=0$.

- $-3$
- $1.5$
- $0.6$
- $3$

## Explanation

In paired analysis each observation is the after-minus-before difference for one pair. The $25$ independent differences give estimated standard error $\dfrac{s_D}{\sqrt{25}}=\dfrac{2.5}{5}=0.5$. The observed mean minus the null mean is $1.5-0$, so $t_{\mathrm{obs}}=\dfrac{1.5}{0.5}=3$. The positive sign depends on the order used to define the differences.
