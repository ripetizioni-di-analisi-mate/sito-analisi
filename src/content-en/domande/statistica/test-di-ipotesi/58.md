At the required fixed significance level, variance, and power, the theoretical sample-size threshold is $18$ to detect an increase of $6$ units. What is the threshold to detect an increase of $2$ units?

- $36$
- $162$
- $6$
- $54$

## Explanation

In planning a z test at fixed variance, level, and power, the increase to be detected times $\sqrt n$ must reach the same threshold. Going from an increase of $6$ to one of $2$ reduces the effect by a factor of three, requiring $\sqrt n$ to triple. The lower bound on $n$ is therefore multiplied by $3^2=9$ and becomes $18\cdot9=162$.
