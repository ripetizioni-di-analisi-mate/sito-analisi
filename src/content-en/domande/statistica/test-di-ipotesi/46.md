A two-sided variance test uses equal tails. The chi-square cumulative distribution function at the observed statistic equals $0.015$. What is the p-value?

- $0.97$
- $0.03$
- $0.015$
- $0.985$

## Explanation

The distribution function gives the left-tail area $0.015$. The right-tail area starting at the same point is $1-0.015=0.985$, placing the observed value toward the lower end. The equal-tailed two-sided test doubles the smaller tail probability, giving $p=2\cdot0.015=0.03$. The rule compares tail probabilities because the chi-square distribution is asymmetric.
