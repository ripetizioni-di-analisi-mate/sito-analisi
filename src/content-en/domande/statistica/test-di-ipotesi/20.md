An exact one-sample t procedure is reported with $11$ degrees of freedom. What was the size of the independent normal sample?

- $10$
- $12$
- $11$
- $22$

## Explanation

In a one-sample t test the variance is estimated from deviations from the sample mean. The deviations sum to zero, so one is determined by the others. This leaves $\nu=n-1$ denominator degrees of freedom. Under normality the statistic has a t distribution with those same degrees of freedom. From $n-1=11$ we obtain $n=12$.
