A p-value of $0.18$ belongs to a family of six tests. What is its Bonferroni-adjusted p-value, capped at one?

- $1.08$
- $1$
- $0.03$
- $0.18$

## Explanation

Bonferroni comparison of a p-value with $\dfrac{\alpha}{6}$ is equivalent to multiplying it by the number of tests and comparing with $\alpha$. Here the product is $6\cdot0.18=1.08$. A p-value must belong to $[0,1]$, so capping at its upper endpoint gives $p_{\mathrm{corr}}=\min\{1,1.08\}=1$.
