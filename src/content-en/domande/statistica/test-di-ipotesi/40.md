Two groups of $30$ independent units have $20$ and $10$ successes. Calculate the chi-square statistic for independence without a continuity correction. Round to two decimal places.

- $1.67$
- $3.33$
- $6.67$
- $10$

## Explanation

The first group has $20$ successes and $10$ failures and the second $10$ successes and $20$ failures. Each row and each column totals $30$ out of $60$ units. Every expected count under independence is therefore $\dfrac{30\cdot30}{60}=15$. All differences have magnitude $5$. Without a correction, adding the four contributions $\dfrac{5^2}{15}$ gives $Q=4\cdot\dfrac{25}{15}\approx6.67$.
