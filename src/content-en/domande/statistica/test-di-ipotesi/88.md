For a normal population the aim is to detect a variance below the declared value. Which rejection region is used for $Q_\sigma$?

- The right tail of the reference chi-square distribution.
- The left tail of the reference chi-square distribution.
- The left tail of the standard normal distribution.
- Two tails symmetric about zero.

## Explanation

For a normal sample, $Q_\sigma=\dfrac{(n-1)S^2}{\sigma_0^2}$ is the unbiased sample variance times a fixed positive factor. A smaller true variance therefore tends to give a small statistic. Reject in the left tail of the chi-square distribution under $H_0$ using the lower quantile for the chosen level. The region is one-sided because the alternative specifies only a decrease.
