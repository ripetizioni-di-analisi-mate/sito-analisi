A test for an increase has power $0.82$ for an increase of $4$ units. Which interpretation is correct?

- If the true increase is $4$, the procedure rejects with probability $0.82$.
- The procedure rejects with the same probability for every positive increase.
- Given that the test rejects, the probability that the increase is $4$ is $0.82$.
- The significance level of the test is $0.82$.

## Explanation

Power is the rejection probability under the data distribution for a specific alternative. Here that value represents a true increase of $4$ units. Repeated sampling at this increase and application of the same rule would give about $82$ rejections in $100$ trials. Other increases may give different data distributions and powers.
