A test asks whether mean emissions exceed a threshold. The null says that the mean is at most the threshold. Which event is a Type I error?

- Failing to reject when the mean exceeds the threshold.
- Rejecting when the mean exceeds the threshold.
- Rejecting when the true mean is at most the threshold.
- Failing to reject when the mean is below the threshold.

## Explanation

The null hypothesis includes means at or below the threshold. Rejecting when the true mean belongs to this set is a Type I error. When the true mean exceeds the threshold, rejection is correct. The possible error there is a nonrejection, a Type II error.
