A small sample consists of independent observations and the variance is unknown. Which model assumption justifies the exact t distribution of the usual test statistic for the mean?

- The observations share a normal distribution with positive variance.
- The observations are sorted from smallest to largest.
- Only the mean of the distribution is known to be finite.
- The alternative is lower-tailed.

## Explanation

For independent observations from the same normal distribution with positive variance, the sample mean is independent of the sample variance. The standardized sum of squared deviations has a chi-square distribution with $n-1$ degrees of freedom. The t statistic divides a standard normal variable by the square root of this independent chi-square variable divided by its degrees of freedom. This gives the exact $t_{n-1}$ distribution.
