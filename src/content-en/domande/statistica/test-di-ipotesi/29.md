Six independent Bernoulli trials yield one success. Calculate the exact p-value for $H_0:p=\dfrac12$ against a lower-tailed alternative.

- $\dfrac{7}{64}$
- $\dfrac{57}{64}$
- $\dfrac{6}{64}$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$

## Explanation

Under a lower-tailed alternative, zero or one success is at least as extreme as the observed count. Under $H_0$ all $2^6=64$ outcome sequences are equally likely. One sequence has zero successes and six have one, depending on its position. The resulting tail probability is $\dfrac{\binom60+\binom61}{64}=\dfrac7{64}$.
