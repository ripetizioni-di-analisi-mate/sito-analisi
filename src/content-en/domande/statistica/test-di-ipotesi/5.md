In $400$ independent Bernoulli trials there are $221$ successes. Calculate the score statistic for $H_0:p=0.50$ without a continuity correction.

- $4.2$
- $0.105$
- $1.05$
- $2.1$

## Explanation

The score statistic uses the distribution of the success count $X$ under the null value $p_0=0.50$. In $400$ trials its expectation is $400p_0=200$ and its variance is $400p_0(1-p_0)=100$, giving count standard deviation $10$. The question specifies no continuity correction, so calculate $\dfrac{221-200}{10}=2.1$ directly. The numerator is the excess of $21$ successes above expectation.
