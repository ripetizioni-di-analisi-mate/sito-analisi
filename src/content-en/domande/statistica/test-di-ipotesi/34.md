In a goodness-of-fit test, one category has theoretical probability $0.18$. What is its expected count in $150$ independent draws?

- $18$
- $27$
- $15$
- $30$

## Explanation

The expected frequency is the mean of a category count under the null model. Write the count as a sum of $150$ indicators, one per draw. Each has expectation $0.18$, giving total expectation $150\cdot0.18=27$. The value $0.18$ is a single-draw probability, while $27$ is the expected count in the sample.
