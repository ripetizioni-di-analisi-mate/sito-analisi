For two independent sample means the estimated sample variances are $16$ and $25$. The sample sizes are $16$ and $25$, respectively. What is the estimated standard error of the difference, to three decimal places?

- $2$
- $1.414$
- $1$
- $6.403$

## Explanation

To estimate the variance of each mean, divide the estimated variance of individual observations by that group's sample size. The results are $\dfrac{16}{16}=1$ and $\dfrac{25}{25}=1$. Independence makes the covariance of the means zero, so the variance of their difference is the sum. Its estimated standard error is the square root of that sum, $\sqrt{1+1}=\sqrt2\approx1.414$.
