Two independent samples have sample variances (divisor $n-1$) $16$ and $25$ and sample sizes $16$ and $25$, respectively. Approximately, what is the estimated standard error of the difference between the two sample means?

- $2$
- $1.414$
- $1$
- $6.403$

## Explanation

To estimate the variance of each mean, divide that group's sample variance by its sample size. The results are $\dfrac{16}{16}=1$ and $\dfrac{25}{25}=1$. Independence makes the covariance of the means zero, so the variance of their difference is the sum. Its estimated standard error is the square root of that sum, $\sqrt{1+1}=\sqrt2\approx1.414$.
