Four independent Bernoulli trials yield four successes. Calculate the exact binomial p-value for $H_0:p=\dfrac12$ against $H_1:p>\dfrac12$.

- $0.3125$
- $0.125$
- $0.0625$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

Under the upper-tailed alternative, larger counts provide stronger evidence. The exact p-value is $P_{\frac12}(X\ge4)$, where $X$ counts successes in four trials. Since $4$ is the largest possible count, the tail includes only $X=4$. The only qualifying sequence contains four successes, so independence gives $p=\left(\dfrac12\right)^4=\dfrac1{16}=0.0625$.
