A discrete test specifies $\alpha=0.05$ and has rejection probability at most $0.04$ at every null value. Which statement is correct?

- The Type II error probability is $0.01$.
- The test violates the significance level because the error probability must equal $0.05$ exactly.
- Power is at most $0.04$ for every alternative.
- The test meets the required level and is conservative relative to $0.05$.

## Explanation

The required level $0.05$ bounds the rejection probability at every null value. Since this probability is at most $0.04$ everywhere under the null, the test satisfies the bound with room to spare. It is conservative relative to the required level. In discrete models critical regions change in steps, and adding one outcome may push the risk above the bound.
