Three independent pairs have observed, normally distributed differences $1$, $2$, and $3$. Calculate the paired t statistic for a null mean difference of zero, to three decimal places.

- $3.464$
- $6$
- $2.449$
- $2$

## Explanation

The unit of analysis is the within-pair difference. The three differences have mean $2$ and deviations $-1$, $0$, and $1$. The unbiased sample variance is $\dfrac{1+0+1}{3-1}=1$, giving estimated standard error $\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$. Dividing the deviation from the null mean, $2-0$, by this error gives $t_{\mathrm{obs}}=2\sqrt3\approx3.464$. The calculation uses three observations, one per pair.
