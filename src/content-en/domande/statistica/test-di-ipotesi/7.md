Three types of packaging and four lifetime categories are compared in a table with all marginal totals positive. How many degrees of freedom does the chi-square reference distribution have for the independence test?

- $12$
- $5$
- $11$
- $6$

## Explanation

The table has three rows and four columns. With fixed margins the cells in the first two rows and first three columns can be chosen freely. Row totals determine the last column and column totals determine the last row. Positive margins allow the usual table dimension, leaving $(3-1)(4-1)=6$ degrees of freedom.
