In a table of $120$ units, the second row has total $48$ and the first column has total $35$. Calculate the expected count in the cell where they intersect, assuming independence.

- $14$
- $42$
- $35$
- $20$

## Explanation

The first column contains $35$ of the $120$ units, giving marginal proportion $\dfrac{35}{120}$. Under independence this proportion also applies to the second row. That row contains $48$ units, so the expected count at the intersection is $48\cdot\dfrac{35}{120}=14$. Use the row total for the cell and the column total to determine the proportion.
