A test rejects with probability $0.03$ at one null value and probability $0.72$ at a particular alternative value. What is the Type II error probability at the alternative value?

- $0.72$
- $0.28$
- $0.03$
- $0.97$

## Explanation

The probability $0.72$ is evaluated at the specified alternative and equals the power. At that value a nonrejection is a Type II error. Rejection and nonrejection are complementary, so the required probability is $1-0.72=0.28$. The probability $0.03$ applies to a null distribution and measures the Type I error at that point.
