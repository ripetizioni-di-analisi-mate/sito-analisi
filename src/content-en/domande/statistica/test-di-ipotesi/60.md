A test has power $0.72$ at a certain alternative mean. The same study is repeated in $50$ laboratories, independently and always with that true mean. How many laboratories are expected not to reject the null hypothesis?

- $36$
- $14$
- $28$
- $25$

## Explanation

With the alternative mean true, each laboratory fails to reject with probability $\beta=1-0.72=0.28$, because rejection and nonrejection are complementary events. The $50$ studies are independent and have the same probability, so the number of nonrejections has a binomial distribution with $n=50$ and probability $0.28$. Its expected value is $50\cdot0.28=14$.
