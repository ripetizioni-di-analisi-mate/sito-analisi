A two-sided z test of $\mu$ uses $\bar x=24$ and known standard error $2$. If $z_{0.975}=1.960$, what is the lower endpoint of the inverted $95\%$ interval?

- $19.92$
- $20.08$
- $27.92$
- $22.04$

## Explanation

Inverting the two-sided test collects the null values compatible with the data at level $0.05$. The interval is centered at the observed mean $24$ and its half-width equals the quantile times the standard error, $1.960\cdot2=3.92$. Subtracting this from the mean gives lower endpoint $24-3.92=20.08$. Adding it would give the upper endpoint.
