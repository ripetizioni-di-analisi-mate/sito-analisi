Two independent tests are applied to two true null hypotheses. Each has exact rejection probability $0.10$. Calculate the probability of at least one rejection.

- $0.81$
- $0.01$
- $0.2$
- $0.19$

## Explanation

The complement of at least one rejection is failure to reject both hypotheses. Each nonrejection has probability $1-0.10=0.90$. Independence gives joint probability $0.90^2=0.81$, so its complement has probability $1-0.81=0.19$. Adding the individual risks directly would count two rejections twice.
