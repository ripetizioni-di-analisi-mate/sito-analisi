At a particular alternative value, the test is expected to fail to reject in $35$ samples out of $100$ in the long run. What is its power at that alternative?

- $0.05$
- $0.95$
- $0.35$
- $0.65$

## Explanation

Evaluate power when the specified alternative is true. Nonrejections then constitute Type II errors and occur in about $35$ of every $100$ repetitions, giving $\beta=0.35$. Rejection and nonrejection are complementary. The probability of rejection under this alternative is therefore $1-0.35=0.65$, about $65$ rejections per $100$ repetitions.
