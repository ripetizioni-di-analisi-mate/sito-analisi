An observed mean exceeds its reference value. The upper-tailed z test gives p-value $0.032$. What is the symmetric two-sided p-value for the same data?

- $0.016$
- $0.936$
- $0.968$
- $0.064$

## Explanation

The observed mean exceeds the null mean, giving a positive z statistic. The given one-sided p-value is the area to the right of the observation. A two-sided alternative also includes values to the left of its negative. Standard normal symmetry makes the added area $0.032$. The sum of the tails is $2\cdot0.032=0.064$.
