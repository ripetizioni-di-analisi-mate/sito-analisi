A goodness-of-fit test with seven categories has one parameter estimated under regularity conditions. Two categories are combined before calculation, and one parameter is still estimated from the grouped counts. How many degrees of freedom are used?

- $4$
- $5$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

Combining two categories replaces them with one, leaving six counts for the test. The fixed sample total determines one count from the others and leaves five degrees of freedom. Estimating one regular parameter from the grouped counts uses one more. The reference distribution is chi-square with $6-1-1=4$ degrees of freedom.
