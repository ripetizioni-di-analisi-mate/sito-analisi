In a goodness-of-fit test the observations are divided into seven categories. Before the calculation two categories with small expected counts are merged, and one parameter of the null distribution is estimated by maximum likelihood from the counts of the resulting categories. How many degrees of freedom are used?

- $4$
- $5$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

Combining two categories replaces them with one, leaving six counts for the test. The fixed sample total determines one count from the others and leaves five degrees of freedom. The parameter estimated from the same counts removes one more. The reference distribution is chi-square with $6-1-1=4$ degrees of freedom.
