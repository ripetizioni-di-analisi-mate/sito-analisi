A family contains eight tests. Find the significance level for each test under the Bonferroni procedure that controls the familywise error rate at $0.04$.

- $0.008$
- $0.005$
- $0.04$
- $0.32$

## Explanation

The familywise error rate concerns at least one false rejection among the eight tests. The Bonferroni bound controls this probability by the sum of the individual risks. Allocating equal levels requires $8\alpha_{\mathrm{ind}}\le0.04$. The usual per-test level is $\alpha_{\mathrm{ind}}=\dfrac{0.04}{8}=0.005$, for which the sum is at most $0.04$ even if the tests are dependent.
