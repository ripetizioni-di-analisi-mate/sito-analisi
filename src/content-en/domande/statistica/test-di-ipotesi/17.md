A study gives $p=0.037$. Of the levels $0.01$, $0.025$, $0.05$, and $0.10$, which is the smallest that leads to rejection?

- $0.025$
- $0.1$
- $0.01$
- $0.05$

## Explanation

Compare the p-value with the level $\alpha$. Rejection occurs when $p\le\alpha$, so levels $0.01$ and $0.025$, which are below $0.037$, do not lead to rejection. Both larger levels do. The smallest of those is $0.05$, although the exact transition occurs at the p-value itself.
