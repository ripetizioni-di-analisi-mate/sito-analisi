In a z test, the null mean is $80$ and the known standard error is $4$. If the observed statistic is $-1.25$, what was the sample mean?

- $75$
- $78.75$
- $70$
- $85$

## Explanation

The statistic $z_{\mathrm{obs}}$ measures the deviation of the sample mean from its null value in standard-error units. Multiplying $-1.25$ by $4$ recovers the deviation in the original units, $-5$. Hence $\bar x=80+4(-1.25)=75$. A negative statistic indicates an observed mean below $80$, consistent with the result.
