In an independent normal sample of size $10$, the descriptive variance with divisor $10$ is $3$. Calculate the test statistic for $H_0:\sigma^2=2$.

- $1.5$
- $13.5$
- $15$
- $30$

## Explanation

Divide the sum of squared deviations by the null variance to obtain the variance statistic. The stated descriptive variance divides that sum by $10$, so the original sum is $10\cdot3=30$. Substituting $\sigma_0^2=2$ gives $Q_\sigma=\dfrac{30}{2}=15$. With the unbiased sample variance, divisor $9$ would recover the same sum of squared deviations.
