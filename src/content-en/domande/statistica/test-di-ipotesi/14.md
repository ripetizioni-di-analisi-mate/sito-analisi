Two independent normal samples each have $16$ observations. Their sample means are $15$ and $8$ and their sample standard deviations, calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction, are $8$ and $6$. Calculate Welch's statistic for $H_0:\mu_1-\mu_2=2$.

- $2$
- $5$
- $2.8$
- $1.25$

## Explanation

Welch's test uses the separate variance estimates of the two independent groups. The numerator subtracts the null difference from the observed difference, giving $(15-8)-2=5$. The estimated variances of the means are $\dfrac{8^2}{16}=4$ and $\dfrac{6^2}{16}=2.25$. Independence allows addition, giving standard error $\sqrt{4+2.25}=2.5$. The ratio is $\dfrac{5}{2.5}=2$.
