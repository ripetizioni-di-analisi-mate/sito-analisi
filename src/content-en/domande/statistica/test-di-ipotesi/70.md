In an upper-tailed threshold test, the significance level is reduced while the model and sample size stay the same. What happens to power at a fixed alternative?

- It stays the same by definition.
- It must increase.
- It equals the new significance level for every alternative.
- It may decrease because the rejection region shrinks.

## Explanation

To lower the level of an upper-tailed test, move the threshold higher so rejection under $H_0$ becomes less likely. At a fixed alternative the distribution of the statistic stays the same. The new critical region is a subset of the previous one, so its probability cannot increase. With a discrete model, power can remain equal if the effective regions coincide.
