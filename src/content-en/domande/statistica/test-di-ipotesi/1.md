The durations of $36$ independent trials are normally distributed with known standard deviation $18\,\mathrm{s}$. The observed mean duration is $93\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the z statistic for $H_0:\mu=90\,\mathrm{s}$.

- $0.1667$
- $1$
- $6$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The individual trials have known standard deviation and are independent. The standard error of the mean of $36$ trials is therefore $\dfrac{18}{\sqrt{36}}=3\,\mathrm{s}$. The deviation from the null mean is $93-90=3\,\mathrm{s}$, so $z_{\mathrm{obs}}=\dfrac{93-90}{3}=1$. The positive sign places the observed mean one standard error above the null value. The denominator applies to the sample mean and changes with sample size.
