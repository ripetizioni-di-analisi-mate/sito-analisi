An independent normal sample of size $13$ has sample standard deviation $s=3$ calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction. Calculate the chi-square statistic for $H_0:\sigma^2=6$.

- $6$
- $19.5$
- $18$
- $9$

## Explanation

The variance statistic compares the sum of squared deviations with the null variance. The sample standard deviation $s=3$ gives $s^2=9$. Since the divisor in $s^2$ is $n-1=12$, the sum of squared deviations is $12\cdot9=108$. Dividing by $\sigma_0^2=6$ gives $Q_\sigma=\dfrac{108}{6}=18$. Normality gives a chi-square distribution with $12$ degrees of freedom under $H_0$.
