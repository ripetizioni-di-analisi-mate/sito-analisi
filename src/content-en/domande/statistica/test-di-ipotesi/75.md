A researcher takes the same measurement on a single instrument every minute. Nearby readings are strongly dependent. Which step in the ordinary t test needs scrutiny?

- The reference distribution assumes independent observations, and dependence can change the standard error.
- The mean estimate always ceases to exist.
- Dependence makes the population variance known.
- A large sample automatically makes the ordinary t distribution exact.

## Explanation

The ordinary t test derives its standard error from the variance of a sum of independent observations. For dependent readings, covariances between terms also enter that variance. The actual standard error may differ from the ordinary one and the t reference may be inappropriate. Sample size alone cannot establish the dependence assumptions.
