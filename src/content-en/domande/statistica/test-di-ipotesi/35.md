A cell with expected count $25$ contributes $4$ to Pearson's statistic. Given that the observed count exceeds the expected count, find the observed count.

- $35$
- $45$
- $29$
- $15$

## Explanation

A cell's contribution is the squared difference divided by the expected count. Thus $\dfrac{(O-25)^2}{25}=4$, and multiplication by $25$ gives $(O-25)^2=100$. The magnitude of the difference is $10$. The observed count exceeds the expected count, so take the positive sign to obtain $O=25+10=35$. A count of $15$ would give the same contribution with the opposite sign of the difference.
