Under a simple null hypothesis, $T$ takes values $0$, $1$, and $2$ with respective probabilities $0.70$, $0.26$, and $0.04$. An upper-tailed test rejects $H_0$ when $T\ge c$, with $c$ an integer, and must have level at most $0.05$. What is the actual level of the largest rejection region of this form?

- $0.04$
- $0.05$
- $0.30$
- $0.26$

## Explanation

For an upper-tailed test, critical regions are built starting at the highest statistic value. Including only $T=2$ gives rejection probability $0.04$ under $H_0$, within the bound $0.05$. Also including $T=1$ would give $0.04+0.26=0.30$, exceeding the bound. The largest region of the form $T\ge c$ with level at most $0.05$ is therefore $\{2\}$, with actual level $0.04$.
