Under a simple null hypothesis, $T$ takes values $0$, $1$, and $2$ with respective probabilities $0.70$, $0.26$, and $0.04$. For a deterministic upper-tailed test of level at most $0.05$, what is the actual level of the largest admissible rejection region?

- $0.04$
- $0.05$
- $0.3$
- $0.26$

## Explanation

For an upper-tailed test build critical regions starting at the highest statistic value. Including only $T=2$ gives null rejection probability $0.04$, within the bound $0.05$. Also including $T=1$ would give $0.04+0.26=0.30$, exceeding the bound. For a deterministic rule the largest admissible region is therefore $\{2\}$, with actual level $0.04$.
