A sample mean is normally distributed with standard error $3$. The test rejects $H_0:\mu\le40$ if $\bar X\ge46$. Calculate its power at $\mu=49$ using $\Phi(-1)=0.1587$.

- $0.5$
- $0.8413$
- $0.1587$
- $0.9772$

## Explanation

Power is the probability of rejection when the true mean is $49$. The rejection threshold stays at $46$, corresponding to $\dfrac{46-49}{3}=-1$ when standardized under the alternative. The rejection region lies to its right. The given left-tail area yields $\pi(49)=1-\Phi(-1)=1-0.1587=0.8413$. The area $0.1587$ is the probability of failing to reject under this alternative.
