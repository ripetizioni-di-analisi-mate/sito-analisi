Each participant is measured before and after an intervention. Which standard deviation enters the standard error for the paired t test?

- The standard deviation of only the final measurements.
- The mean of the before and after standard deviations.
- The standard deviation of the within-participant differences.
- The standard deviation of the combined before and after measurements.

## Explanation

Compute one difference per participant, for example after minus before. The paired t test applies a one-sample mean test to these differences. Its denominator is their sample standard deviation divided by the square root of the number of participants. Each difference uses two measurements from the same person, so their relationship enters the variability of the differences.
