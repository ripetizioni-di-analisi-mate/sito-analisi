A test of equality of two means gives $p=0.03$. What does $0.03$ represent?

- The fraction of observations that differ between groups.
- The probability that the population means are equal.
- The probability of having made a Type II error.
- The probability, under the null model, of a result at least as extreme according to the test rule.

## Explanation

To calculate the p-value, fix the null model and locate the observed result in the distribution of the statistic. Include every result at least as extreme in the direction or directions specified by the alternative. The value $0.03$ is the total probability of that region under the null model. The probability refers to sampling outcomes assuming $H_0$.
