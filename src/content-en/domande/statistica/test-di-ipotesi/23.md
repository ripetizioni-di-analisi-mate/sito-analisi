An independent normal sample of size $25$ gives $\bar x-\mu_0=1.6$. The z statistic is $2$. What population standard deviation was used?

- $0.8$
- $1.6$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The value $z=2$ means the observed difference $1.6$ equals two standard errors of the mean. One standard error is therefore $\dfrac{1.6}{2}=0.8$. For an independent sample of size $25$ the standard error of the mean is $\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{25}}$. This gives $\sigma=0.8\sqrt{25}=4$. The values $0.8$ and $4$ refer to the mean and a single observation, respectively.
