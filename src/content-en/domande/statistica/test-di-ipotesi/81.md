A score test of $H_0:p=0.30$ gives $\widehat p=0.40$. Which value is used to calculate the variance of the sample proportion in the denominator?

- The null value $0.30$.
- The observed proportion $0.40$.
- The complement of the observed proportion, $0.60$.
- Their average, $0.35$.

## Explanation

The score test measures the distance of the observed proportion from the null value using the variability expected under $H_0$. Its denominator is $\sqrt{\dfrac{p_0(1-p_0)}{n}}$ with $p_0=0.30$. The observed proportion $0.40$ enters the numerator through the deviation $0.40-0.30=0.10$. Substituting it into the variance as well would give a differently standardized statistic.
