A table has five columns, all positive marginal totals, and $12$ degrees of freedom for a test of independence. How many rows does it have?

- $5$
- $3$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

With fixed margins in a table of $r$ rows and five columns, the free cells occupy $r-1$ rows and $5-1=4$ columns. The totals determine the last row and column. The test reports $12$ degrees of freedom, so $4(r-1)=12$. This gives $r-1=3$, meaning the table has $4$ rows.
