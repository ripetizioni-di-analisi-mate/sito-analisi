An upper-tailed z test with known standard deviation $8$ aims to detect an increase of $4$ and uses $z_{1-\alpha}+z_{1-\beta_\star}=2.20$. What is the smallest sample size ensuring the required power?

- $44$
- $19$
- $20$
- $5$

## Explanation

In the upper-tailed z test the distance between alternative and null means in standard-error units is $\dfrac{4\sqrt n}{8}$. For the requested power it must be at least $2.20$, the given sum of quantiles. This yields $\sqrt n\ge4.40$ and hence $n\ge19.36$. Since $n$ is an integer and the inequality imposes a minimum, choose $20$.
