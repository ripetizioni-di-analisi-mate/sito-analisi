Four tests give p-values $0.006$, $0.010$, $0.011$, and $0.040$. How many hypotheses are rejected under Bonferroni with familywise error rate $0.04$ if equality at the threshold also leads to rejection?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

With four comparisons Bonferroni assigns level $\dfrac{0.04}{4}=0.010$ to each test. The rule rejects when $p\le0.010$. The values $0.006$ and $0.010$ meet the criterion, while $0.011$ and $0.040$ exceed the threshold. This gives two rejections. The second is included because the rule allows equality at the cutoff.
