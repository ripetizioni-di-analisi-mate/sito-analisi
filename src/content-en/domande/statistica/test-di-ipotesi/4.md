In a sample of $9$ independent observations from a normal population, the mean is $26$ and the sample standard deviation calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction is $3$. Calculate the t statistic for $H_0:\mu=28$.

- $2$
- $-0.6667$
- $-2$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The population standard deviation is estimated from independent normal observations, so use the t statistic. The sample standard deviation $3$ gives estimated standard error $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt9}=1$. Subtracting the null mean from the observed mean gives $26-28=-2$. The ratio is $-2$, placing the observed mean two estimated standard errors below the reference.
