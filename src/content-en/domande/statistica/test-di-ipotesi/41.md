In a test of independence using a three-column table, one row has total $30$. Its first two expected counts are $6$ and $9$. What is its third expected count?

- $12$
- $21$
- $15$
- $24$

## Explanation

Multiply the row total by each of the three column proportions to obtain its expected counts. The column proportions sum to one, so the expected counts sum to the row total $30$. The first two cells account for $6+9=15$ units, making the third expected count $30-15=15$.
