A sample mean is normal with standard error $2$. For $H_0:\mu\le8$ the test rejects if $\bar X\ge12$. What is the rejection probability if the true mean is $\mu=6$, a value inside the null hypothesis? Use $\Phi(2)=0.97725$ and $\Phi(3)=0.99865$.

- $0.02275$
- $0.99865$
- $0.00270$
- $0.00135$

## Explanation

If $\mu=6$, the sample mean is normal with center $6$ and standard error $2$. The threshold $12$ is $12-6=6$ from the center, or $\dfrac62=3$ standard errors away. The rule rejects high values, so the rejection probability is the area to the right of $3$, $1-\Phi(3)=1-0.99865=0.00135$. This is less than the probability at the boundary $\mu=8$, which equals $1-\Phi(2)=0.02275$, because the rejection probability increases with $\mu$.
