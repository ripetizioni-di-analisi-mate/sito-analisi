A sample mean is normal with standard error $2$. The test rejects the null hypothesis if $\bar X\ge12$. If the null boundary is $\mu_0=8$, what is the rejection probability at the boundary using $\Phi(2)=0.9772$?

- $0.0456$
- $0.1587$
- $0.9772$
- $0.0228$

## Explanation

At the null boundary the sample mean is centered at $8$. The rejection threshold is $12-8=4$ from that center, or $\dfrac42=2$ standard errors away. The rule rejects high values, so take the area to the right of $2$ to obtain $1-\Phi(2)=1-0.9772=0.0228$. At the null boundary this is a false-rejection probability.
