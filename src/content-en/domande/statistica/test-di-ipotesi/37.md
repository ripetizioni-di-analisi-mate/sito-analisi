Four categories each have expected count $10$, with observed counts $6$, $10$, $10$, and $14$. Calculate the goodness-of-fit statistic.

- $32$
- $1.6$
- $3.2$
- $0$

## Explanation

To compute $Q$, find each category's contribution. The observed-minus-expected differences are $-4$, $0$, $0$, and $4$. The two zero differences contribute nothing. The other two have the same square $16$ and denominator $10$. The statistic is therefore $Q=\dfrac{16}{10}+\dfrac{16}{10}=3.2$. Differences of opposite signs are added after squaring.
