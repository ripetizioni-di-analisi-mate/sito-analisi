A two-sided t test with $20$ degrees of freedom gives $t_{\mathrm{obs}}=2.6$. The tables give $t_{20,0.975}=2.086$ and $t_{20,0.995}=2.845$. What can be said about the p-value?

- It is less than $0.01$.
- It lies between $0.01$ and $0.05$.
- It lies between $0.05$ and $0.10$.
- It is greater than $0.10$.

## Explanation

The p-value is the smallest level at which the observed result leads to rejection. At level $0.05$ the two-sided test rejects if $|t_{\mathrm{obs}}|\ge t_{20,0.975}=2.086$. Since $2.6>2.086$, the test rejects at level $0.05$, so the p-value is less than $0.05$. At level $0.01$ the threshold is $t_{20,0.995}=2.845$. Since $2.6<2.845$, the test does not reject at level $0.01$, so the p-value is greater than $0.01$. The exact calculation gives about $0.017$.
