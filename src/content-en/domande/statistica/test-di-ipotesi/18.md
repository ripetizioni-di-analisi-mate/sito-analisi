A two-sided t test gives $t_{\mathrm{obs}}=-2.1$. The reference t cumulative distribution function at $2.1$ is $0.970$. Calculate the p-value.

- $0.03$
- $0.94$
- $0.06$
- $0.97$

## Explanation

The reference t statistic is symmetric and the test is two-sided. Values at least as extreme as $-2.1$ fall to the left of $-2.1$ or to the right of $2.1$. The distribution function gives right-tail probability $1-0.970=0.030$. The left tail has equal probability, giving p-value $2\cdot0.030=0.06$. The negative observation does not change the magnitudes of the cutoffs.
