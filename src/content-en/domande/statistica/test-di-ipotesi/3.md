A lower-tailed z test gives $z_{\mathrm{obs}}=-1.2$. Calculate the p-value using $\Phi(1.2)=0.8849$.

- $0.1151$
- $0.8849$
- $0.7698$
- $0.2302$

## Explanation

The alternative specifies a decrease, so values of $Z$ less than or equal to $-1.2$ are at least as extreme as the observation. The given value of $\Phi$ refers to the positive point. By symmetry the area to the left of $-1.2$ equals the area to the right of $1.2$, giving $p=\Phi(-1.2)=1-0.8849=0.1151$. The opposite tail would address a different question.
