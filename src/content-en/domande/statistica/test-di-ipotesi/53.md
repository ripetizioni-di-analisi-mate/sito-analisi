For a normally distributed sample mean with standard error $3$, a lower-tailed test rejects if $\bar X\le18$. What is the Type II error probability when the true mean is $15$? Use $\Phi(1)=0.8413$.

- $0.5$
- $0.1587$
- $0.3174$
- $0.8413$

## Explanation

When the true mean is $15$, the threshold $18$ corresponds to $\dfrac{18-15}{3}=1$ on the standardized scale. The lower-tailed rule rejects to its left. A Type II error is the complementary event $\bar X>18$ because the alternative is true and the test fails to reject. Its probability is $1-\Phi(1)=1-0.8413=0.1587$.
