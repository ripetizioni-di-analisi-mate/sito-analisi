In Welch's test, the sample sizes are $9$ and $9$, and the two contributions to the variance of the difference are equal and positive. What are the approximate degrees of freedom?

- $18$
- $16$
- $8$
- $17$

## Explanation

Denote the identical variance contribution of each mean in Welch's formula by $V$. The sum in the numerator is $2V$ and its square is $4V^2$. Each group contributes $\dfrac{V^2}{9-1}=\dfrac{V^2}{8}$ to the denominator. Since $V>0$, the ratio simplifies to $4V^2\cdot\dfrac{8}{2V^2}=16$. The symmetry of the contributions makes this equal to the sum of the eight degrees of freedom from each group.
