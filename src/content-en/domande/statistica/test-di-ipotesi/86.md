A $2\times2$ table cross-classifies before and after outcomes for the same individuals. Which approach tests for a change in the proportion of positive outcomes?

- A procedure for paired binary data based on discordant pairs.
- The score test for proportions in two independent samples.
- A goodness-of-fit test using only the final counts.
- A t test for two independent means with known variance.

## Explanation

Both measurements are on the same person, so before and after outcomes are paired. Concordant pairs contribute equally to the positive counts before and after. The change therefore comes from discordant pairs, negative-to-positive and positive-to-negative. A procedure for paired binary data compares these transitions while respecting the sampling design.
