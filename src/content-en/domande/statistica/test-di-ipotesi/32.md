One group has $12$ successes in $30$ trials, and another $33$ successes in $70$ trials. What pooled estimate is used under equality of the proportions?

- $0.4$
- $0.4714$
- $0.9$
- $0.45$

## Explanation

The equality hypothesis assigns the same success probability to both groups. Estimate it by pooling successes, $12+33=45$, and trials, $30+70=100$. The pooled proportion is $\dfrac{45}{100}=0.45$. It is the mean of the two observed proportions with weights $30$ and $70$ because every trial contributes equally to the common estimate.
