Twelve independent trials are used to test $H_0:p=0.02$ against an increase. Which procedure avoids using the normal approximation when the expected number of successes is very small?

- Use the upper tail of a t distribution with eleven degrees of freedom.
- Calculate the upper tail of a binomial distribution with parameters $12$ and $0.02$.
- Perform a chi-square test for a variance.
- Replace $0.02$ with the observed proportion and use the score test.

## Explanation

Under $H_0$ the success count has an exact binomial distribution with parameters $12$ and $0.02$. The expected success count is $12\cdot0.02=0.24$, so the distribution is highly skewed and a normal approximation is unsuitable. For an increasing alternative, sum the binomial probabilities of counts at least as large as the observed count. This uses the exact distribution.
