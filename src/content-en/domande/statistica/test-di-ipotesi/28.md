The number of defects in a roll of fabric has a Poisson distribution. Under $H_0$ the mean is $1$ defect per roll, and the rule rejects $H_0$ if at least $4$ defects are observed in a roll. What is the Type I error probability?

- $0.0803$
- $0.9810$
- $0.0190$
- $0.0037$

## Explanation

A Type I error is a rejection when $H_0$ is true, so its probability is $P(X\ge4)$ with $X$ Poisson with mean $1$. Use the complement, $P(X\ge4)=1-P(X\le3)$. For the Poisson distribution with mean $1$, $P(X=k)=\dfrac{e^{-1}}{k!}$, so
$$
P(X\le3)=e^{-1}\left(1+1+\frac12+\frac16\right)=\frac83\,e^{-1}\approx0.9810.
$$
The Type I error probability is therefore $1-0.9810=0.0190$.
