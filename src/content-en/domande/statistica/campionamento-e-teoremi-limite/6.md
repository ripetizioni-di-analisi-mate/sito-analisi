Draw $16$ i.i.d. observations from a normal population. Find $P\left(\bar X_{16}-\mu>1.75\dfrac S4\right)$ given that a variable $T$ with distribution $t_{15}$ satisfies $P(T\le1.75)\approx0.95$.

- $0.9$
- $0.05$
- $0.1$
- $0.95$

## Explanation

In an i.i.d. normal sample, the deviation of the sample mean from $\mu$ divided by the estimated standard error $\dfrac S4$ has distribution $t_{15}$. Dividing both sides by $\dfrac S4$, which is positive with probability one, the inequality becomes $T>1.75$. The value given in the question is the area to the left of the threshold, so the right-tail probability is approximately $1-0.95=0.05$.
