A nonnegative processing time has mean $9\,\mathrm{min}$. Use Markov's inequality to find an upper bound on the probability that processing takes at least $36\,\mathrm{min}$.

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The duration $Y$ is nonnegative, so Markov's inequality bounds its tail using only its expectation. On the event $Y\ge36\,\mathrm{min}$, every duration contributes at least $36\,\mathrm{min}$ to the calculation of the mean. Hence $P(Y\ge36)\le\dfrac{E[Y]}{36}=\dfrac9{36}=\dfrac14$. The ratio is an upper bound and the mean duration is the only moment it uses.
