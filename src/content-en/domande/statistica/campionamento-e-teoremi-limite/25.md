The standard deviation of the measurements is constant and positive. How does the standard error change when the size of an i.i.d. sample doubles?

- It is unchanged
- It is multiplied by $\dfrac12$
- It is multiplied by $\sqrt2$
- It is multiplied by $\dfrac1{\sqrt2}$

## Explanation

For sample size $n$ the standard error is $\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$. At sample size $2n$ it becomes $\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{2n}}$. The individual spread stays the same, so the ratio reduces to $\dfrac{\sqrt n}{\sqrt{2n}}=\dfrac1{\sqrt2}$. The factor $\dfrac12$ would apply to the variance of the mean, which has $n$ in the denominator.
