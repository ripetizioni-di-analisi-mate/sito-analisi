Each measurement in a session has the form $X_i=\mu+B+E_i$. The calibration error $B$ is common to the whole session and has variance $4$. The errors $E_i$ have variance $36$ and are independent of each other and of $B$. What is the variance of the mean of $9$ measurements from the same session?

- $\dfrac{40}{9}$
- $4$
- $40$
- $8$

## Explanation

The mean of the nine measurements is $\mu+B+\bar E_9$, where $\bar E_9$ is the mean of the errors $E_i$. The component $B$ appears with coefficient one, so it retains its variance $4$. The mean of the errors has variance $\dfrac{36}{9}=4$. Since $B$ and $\bar E_9$ are independent, their variances add, giving $4+4=8$. The value $\dfrac{40}{9}$ would result from treating the nine measurements as independent, each with variance $4+36=40$.
