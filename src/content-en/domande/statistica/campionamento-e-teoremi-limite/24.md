An i.i.d. sample of $36$ measurements gives $s=9\,\mathrm{mm}$. The population standard deviation is unknown. What is the estimated standard error of the sample mean?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The theoretical standard error is $\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$. Since $\sigma$ is unknown, estimate it using the observed sample standard deviation $s=9\,\mathrm{mm}$. The resulting estimate is $\dfrac{9}{\sqrt{36}}\,\mathrm{mm}=1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The value $9\,\mathrm{mm}$ describes spread within the sample, while $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ estimates the spread of means across repeated samples.
