In a sequence of i.i.d. trials with success probability $0.6$, to what value does the proportion of failures converge in probability?

- $0$
- $0.6$
- $0.4$
- $1$

## Explanation

A failure indicator equals $1$ when the trial fails. Its expectation is the probability of failure, $1-0.6=0.4$. The observed proportion is the mean of these i.i.d. indicators. They have finite variance, so the weak law ensures that the mean converges in probability to $0.4$.
