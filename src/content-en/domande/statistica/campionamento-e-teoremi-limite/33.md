Four i.i.d. measurements have distribution $N(20,4)$. Which property allows $P(\bar X_4<19)$ to be calculated exactly?

- Chebyshev's bound with tolerance $1$
- The weak law of large numbers with $n=4$
- Linear combinations of independent normal variables are normally distributed
- The central limit theorem with $n=4$

## Explanation

The mean is a linear combination of independent normal variables, so it is exactly normal even with four observations. Here its center is $20$ and its variance $\dfrac44=1$, so the exact probability is $\Phi(-1)$. This property of the normal family holds at every sample size.
