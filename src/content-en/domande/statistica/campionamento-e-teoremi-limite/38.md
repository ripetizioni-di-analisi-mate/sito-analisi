An i.i.d. sample of three measurements comes from a discrete distribution with only two possible values. Why might $\dfrac{\bar X_3-\mu}{\dfrac{S}{\sqrt3}}$ be undefined?

- There is a positive probability that all the measurements are equal
- The mean of three measurements is always zero
- A discrete variable always has zero variance
- The number of degrees of freedom is negative

## Explanation

Let $p$ be the probability of one of the two possible values. Both belong to the support, so $0<p<1$. By independence, all three observations coincide with probability $p^3+(1-p)^3>0$. In that case all deviations from their mean vanish, giving $S=0$. The proposed ratio then has a zero denominator.
