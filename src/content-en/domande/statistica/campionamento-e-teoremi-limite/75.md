For $K\sim\operatorname{Bin}(100,0.5)$, what is the continuity-corrected approximation to $P(K>54)$ using $\Phi(0.9)\approx0.8159$?

- $0.3682$
- $0.8159$
- $0.5$
- $0.1841$

## Explanation

The binomial distribution has mean $100\cdot0.5=50$ and standard deviation $\sqrt{100\cdot0.5\cdot0.5}=5$. The first admissible integer is $55$, so the continuous cutoff is $54.5$. Standardizing gives $\dfrac{54.5-50}{5}=0.9$. The event includes the values above the cutoff, so take the right-tail probability to obtain $1-\Phi(0.9)\approx0.1841$.
