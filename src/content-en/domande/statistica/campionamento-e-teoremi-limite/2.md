Given i.i.d. measurements with standard deviation $13\,\mathrm{mm}$, find the smallest $n$ for which the standard error of the mean is at most $2\,\mathrm{mm}$.

- $169$
- $42$
- $43$
- $7$

## Explanation

For i.i.d. measurements the standard error is $\dfrac{13}{\sqrt n}\,\mathrm{mm}$. Since the denominator is positive, the constraint is equivalent to $\sqrt n\ge\dfrac{13}{2}=6.5$. Squaring gives $n\ge42.25$. The sample size must be an integer, so the minimum is $43$. The value $42$ falls short of the required sample size.
