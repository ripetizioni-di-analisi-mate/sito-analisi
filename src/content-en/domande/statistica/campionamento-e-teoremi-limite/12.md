In $64$ i.i.d. Bernoulli trials the success probability is $0.5$. Calculate the variance of the sample proportion of successes.

- $\dfrac{1}{128}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{256}$
- $16$

## Explanation

In each trial the success indicator has variance $p(1-p)=0.5\cdot0.5=\dfrac14$. The proportion is the mean of $64$ indicators and the trials are independent. Divide the individual variance by $64$ to obtain the variance of the mean, $\dfrac1{4\cdot64}=\dfrac1{256}$. Its square root would be the standard deviation of the proportion.
