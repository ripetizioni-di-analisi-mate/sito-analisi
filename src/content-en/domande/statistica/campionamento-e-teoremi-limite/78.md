All $15$ units of a finite population with positive variance are sampled without replacement. What is the standard error of the mean?

- $\dfrac{\sigma_N}{\sqrt{15}}$
- $0$
- $\sigma_N$
- $1$

## Explanation

Each sample drawn without replacement contains all $15$ units in the population, so the sample mean always has the same value. A constant statistic has zero variance and zero standard deviation. The correction factor confirms this because $\dfrac{15-15}{15-1}=0$. The individual population values can vary even when the sample mean has no variability.
