Each observation $X_i$ repeats the same random value $Y$, with $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=9$. What is the variance of the mean of $16$ observations?

- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $9$
- $144$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Every realization satisfies $X_1=\cdots=X_{16}=Y$. The sum is $16Y$, and dividing by $16$ gives $Y$ again. The mean thus has the same distribution as $Y$ and variance $9$. Formula $\dfrac{9}{16}$ would require zero covariance between distinct measurements. Here every pair repeats the same random value.
