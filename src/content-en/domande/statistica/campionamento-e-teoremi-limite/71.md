A program replaces the mass of each integer $k$ by the area under a normal curve between $k-0.5$ and $k+0.5$. Which regions should it add to approximate “$K$ is even and $10\le K\le14$”?

- The entire interval from $9.5$ to $14.5$
- Three intervals of width $1$ centred at $10,12,14$
- Two intervals of width $1$ centred at $11,13$
- The single interval from $10$ to $14$

## Explanation

The event consists of three nonadjacent integers. Their continuity intervals are $[9.5,10.5]$, $[11.5,12.5]$ and $[13.5,14.5]$. A single interval across them would also include the odd integers.
