Let $K$ be a binomial count. Which continuous boundary is used to approximate $P(K\ge18)$?

- $16.5$
- $17.5$
- $18.5$
- $18$

## Explanation

The event includes count $18$ and every larger integer. The continuity correction represents the mass at $18$ by the cell extending from $17.5$ to $18.5$. Since the whole cell is included, the normal area starts at its left boundary $18-0.5=17.5$. Using boundary $18.5$ would omit the mass at $18$.
