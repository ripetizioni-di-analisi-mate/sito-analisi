For an i.i.d. sample of $16$ measurements from $N(100,64)$, calculate $P(98<\bar X_{16}<102)$ using $\Phi(1)\approx0.841345$. Round to four decimal places.

- $0.3174$
- $0.1587$
- $0.8413$
- $0.6827$

## Explanation

The observations are independent and normal, so their mean is also normal, with center $100$ and standard deviation $\dfrac{8}{\sqrt{16}}=2$. The bounds $98$ and $102$ become $-1$ and $1$. By symmetry the area is $\Phi(1)-\Phi(-1)=2\Phi(1)-1$. Substitution gives $0.682690$, which rounds to $0.6827$ at four decimal places.
