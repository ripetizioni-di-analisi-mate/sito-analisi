In $200$ i.i.d. Bernoulli trials there are $34$ successes. What is the observed proportion?

- $0.17$
- $0.83$
- $0.034$
- $0.34$

## Explanation

The observed proportion is the mean of the success indicators. They sum to $34$ because each success contributes one and each failure zero. Dividing by the number of trials gives $\dfrac{34}{200}=0.17$. This describes the observed sample and can be used to estimate $p$.
