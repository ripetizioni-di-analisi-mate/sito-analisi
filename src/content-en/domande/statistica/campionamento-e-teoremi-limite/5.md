Take $13$ i.i.d. observations from a normal population with variance $4$. The sample variance calculated with divisor $n-1$ is $6$. Calculate the observed value of $Q=\dfrac{(n-1)S^2}{\sigma^2}$.

- $1.5$
- $12$
- $18$
- $19.5$

## Explanation

The sample variance uses divisor $n-1=12$. Multiplying $6$ by $12$ therefore recovers the sum of squared deviations, $72$. The ratio $Q$ divides this sum by the population variance, $4$. Thus the observed value is $\dfrac{72}{4}=18$ and has no units.
