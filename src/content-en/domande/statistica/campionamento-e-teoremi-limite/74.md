A count $K$ is approximated by a normal variable with mean $40$ and standard deviation $5$. After the continuity correction, which standardized thresholds are used for $P(K=40)$?

- $(-1,1)$
- $(-0.5,0.5)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(-0.1,0.1)$

## Explanation

To approximate the probability mass at $40$, use the cell between $39.5$ and $40.5$. Relative to the center of the normal distribution, the two boundaries are displaced by $-0.5$ and $0.5$, respectively. Dividing by the standard deviation $5$ gives $-0.1$ and $0.1$. The area between these two standardized values represents the discrete probability mass.
