A single unit is drawn without replacement from $N=26$ units. What is the correction factor for the variance of the sample mean relative to drawing with replacement?

- $\dfrac{25}{26}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

When the population variance uses divisor $N$, the correction factor for the variance of the mean is $\dfrac{N-n}{N-1}$. Substituting $N=26$ and $n=1$ gives $\dfrac{25}{25}=1$. The sample consists of a single draw, which has the same distribution under either design. The correction matters when subsequent draws depend on earlier ones.
