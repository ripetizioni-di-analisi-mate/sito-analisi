In a population of $25$ units the variance with divisor $N$ is positive. For what sample size is the variance of the mean without replacement half its value with replacement?

- $24$
- $25$
- $13$
- $12$

## Explanation

For a fixed sample size, the ratio of the variances without and with replacement is $\dfrac{25-n}{25-1}$. The positive population variance makes this ratio well defined. Setting $\dfrac{25-n}{24}=\dfrac12$ gives $25-n=12$, hence $n=13$. At this sample size the dependence between draws halves the variance of the sample mean.
