Given $Y\ge0$ and $E[Y]=5$, what lower bound on $P(Y<20)$ follows directly by applying Markov's inequality to the complementary event?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Since $Y\ge0$, apply Markov's inequality at the threshold $20$ to obtain $P(Y\ge20)\le\dfrac{E[Y]}{20}=\dfrac5{20}=\dfrac14$. The event $Y<20$ is the complement of the event just bounded, so its probability is at least $1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$. Taking complements reverses the direction of the inequality.
