With $n$ fixed, for which value of $p$ is the variance $\dfrac{p(1-p)}{n}$ of the sample proportion largest?

- $0.25$
- $0.5$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

For fixed sample size, maximizing the variance of the proportion is equivalent to maximizing $p(1-p)$ for $0\le p\le1$. Completing the square gives $p(1-p)=\dfrac14-\left(p-\dfrac12\right)^2$. The subtracted term is nonnegative and reaches zero at $p=\dfrac12$. The variance there is $\dfrac1{4n}$, and success and failure are equally likely.
