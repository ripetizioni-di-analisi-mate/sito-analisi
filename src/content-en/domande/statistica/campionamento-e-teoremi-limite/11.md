The $64$ i.i.d. variables $X_i$ have mean $3$ and standard deviation $2$. With $T=\sum_{i=1}^{64}X_i$, approximate $P(T>208)$ using the central limit theorem and $\Phi(1)\approx0.8413$.

- $0.3174$
- $0.1587$
- $0.8413$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

For the total $T$, linearity of expectation gives mean $64\cdot3=192$. Independence gives variance $64\cdot2^2=256$ and hence standard deviation $16$. On the approximating normal scale the threshold $208$ becomes $\dfrac{208-192}{16}=1$. Since an exceedance is required, take the right tail to obtain $1-0.8413=0.1587$.
