Take the maximum of three i.i.d. Bernoulli observations with $p=\dfrac12$. What is the probability that the maximum is zero?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The maximum of the three indicators equals zero exactly when all three are zero. An individual failure has probability $1-p=\dfrac12$. By independence, multiply the three probabilities to obtain $P(\max_i X_i=0)=\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\dfrac18$. Even one success would make the maximum one.
