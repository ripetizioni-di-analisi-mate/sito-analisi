The normal approximation for a sum of i.i.d. observations has mean $120$ and standard deviation $10$. Given $\Phi(0.5)\approx0.6915$ and $\Phi(1.5)\approx0.9332$, what is the approximate probability that the sum lies between $125$ and $135$?

- $0.3085$
- $0.9332$
- $0.0668$
- $0.2417$

## Explanation

The standardized thresholds are $\dfrac{125-120}{10}=0.5$ and $\dfrac{135-120}{10}=1.5$. The cumulative probability at the upper threshold includes the area to the left of the lower one. Subtracting the two cumulative probabilities leaves just the area between the thresholds. Substitution gives the approximate probability $\Phi(1.5)-\Phi(0.5)\approx0.9332-0.6915=0.2417$.
