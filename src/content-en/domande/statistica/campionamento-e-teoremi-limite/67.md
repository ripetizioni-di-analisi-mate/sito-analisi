Two Bernoulli samples are i.i.d. within groups and have equal sample sizes. Their success probabilities are $0.2$ and $0.8$. How do their variances compare?

- The first is four times the second
- The second is four times the first
- They are equal
- The second is zero

## Explanation

An individual success indicator has variance $p(1-p)$. This equals $0.2\cdot0.8=0.16$ in the first sample and $0.8\cdot0.2=0.16$ in the second. The two sample proportions also have equal variances since both divide this value by the same sample size $n$. Exchanging success and failure changes the center of the proportion and preserves its spread.
