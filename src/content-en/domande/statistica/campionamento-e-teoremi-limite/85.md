Of $16$ population units, $8$ are type A. In a simple random sample of $4$ units without replacement, what is the variance of the sample proportion of type A units?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{64}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$

## Explanation

Assign each unit an indicator of membership in type A. The population proportion is $\dfrac8{16}=\dfrac12$ and the population variance is $\dfrac14$ with divisor $N$. With replacement, the variance of the mean of the four indicators would be $\dfrac1{16}$. Without replacement, apply the factor $\dfrac{16-4}{16-1}=\dfrac45$ to obtain $\dfrac1{16}\cdot\dfrac45=\dfrac1{20}$.
