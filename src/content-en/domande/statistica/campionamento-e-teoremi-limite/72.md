Which continuous interval approximates the event $5<K<10$ for the binomial count $K$?

- $(5.5,9.5)$
- $(5.5,10.5)$
- $(4.5,10.5)$
- $(6.5,9.5)$

## Explanation

Since $K$ is an integer, the strict inequalities allow counts $6$, $7$, $8$, and $9$. The corresponding continuous cells are adjacent. Their union starts at $6-0.5=5.5$ and ends at $9+0.5=9.5$. The normal area between these bounds approximates the probability because it includes each admissible count's full cell.
