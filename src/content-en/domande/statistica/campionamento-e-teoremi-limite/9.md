In $200$ independent Bernoulli trials the success probability is $0.2$. Calculate Chebyshev's upper bound on $P(|\hat p-0.2|\ge0.1)$.

- $0.08$
- $0.008$
- $0.16$
- $0.92$

## Explanation

The proportion $\hat p$ is the mean of the $200$ independent success indicators. Its expectation is $0.2$ and its variance is $\dfrac{0.2\cdot0.8}{200}=0.0008$. The event requires an absolute deviation of at least $0.1$, so the denominator in Chebyshev's bound is $0.1^2=0.01$. The upper bound $\dfrac{0.0008}{0.01}=0.08$ covers both tails.
