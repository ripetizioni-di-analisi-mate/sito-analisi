The mean of $25$ i.i.d. observations has standard error $2$. What upper bound on $P(\bar X_{25}\ge\mu+8)$ follows by applying Chebyshev's inequality to an absolute deviation of at least $8$?

- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{15}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

If $\bar X_{25}\ge\mu+8$, then the absolute deviation of the mean from $\mu$ is at least $8$. The Chebyshev bound for this larger event therefore also applies. Standard error $2$ gives variance $2^2=4$ and hence $P(\bar X_{25}\ge\mu+8)\le\dfrac4{8^2}=\dfrac1{16}$. For these data the bound applies to both tails.
