Two independent Bernoulli trials each have success probability $\dfrac14$. What is the probability that their mean equals $\dfrac12$?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

The mean of two indicators equals $\dfrac12$ when their sum is $1$. The possible outcomes are $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$. Each has probability $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac3{16}$ by independence. They are mutually exclusive, so their probabilities add to $\dfrac3{16}+\dfrac3{16}=\dfrac38$. The factor of two counts the two possible orders of a success and a failure.
