An i.i.d. sample of $100$ observations comes from a population with mean $8$ and standard deviation $5$. Approximate $P(\bar X_{100}<7)$ using the central limit theorem and $\Phi(-2)\approx0.0228$.

- $0.9772$
- $0.0456$
- $0.0228$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

The central limit theorem approximates the distribution of the mean because the sample is i.i.d. with finite variance. Its mean is $8$ and its standard error is $\dfrac5{\sqrt{100}}=0.5$. Standardizing the threshold gives $\dfrac{7-8}{0.5}=-2$. The event is in the left tail, so its probability is approximately $\Phi(-2)=0.0228$.
