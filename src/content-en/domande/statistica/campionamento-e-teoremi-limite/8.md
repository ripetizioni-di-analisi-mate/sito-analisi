A measurement $X$ has mean $50$ and variance $9$. Find a lower bound on $P(44<X<56)$ by applying Chebyshev's inequality to the complementary event.

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The center of the interval is $50$, and each endpoint is $6$ from the center. The interval is open, so its complement contains every value satisfying $|X-50|\ge6$. Chebyshev's inequality bounds that probability above by $\dfrac9{6^2}=\dfrac14$. Subtracting this bound from $1$ gives the guarantee $P(44<X<56)\ge\dfrac34$.
