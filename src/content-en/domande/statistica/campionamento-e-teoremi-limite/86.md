A simple random sample of $2$ units is drawn without replacement from a population with values $0$, $2$, and $4$. What is the probability that its mean is at least $2$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Under simple random sampling, the pairs $\{0,2\}$, $\{0,4\}$, and $\{2,4\}$ are equally likely. Their means are $1$, $2$, and $3$, respectively. The threshold is inclusive, so even the pair with mean exactly $2$ qualifies. Two of the three pairs meet the condition, giving probability $\dfrac23$.
