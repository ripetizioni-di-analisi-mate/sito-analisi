A population has variance $36$. What is the smallest i.i.d. sample size for which Chebyshev's inequality gives $P(|\bar X_n-\mu|\ge3)\le0.1$?

- $120$
- $40$
- $12$
- $4$

## Explanation

The variance of the i.i.d. sample mean is $\dfrac{36}{n}$. Chebyshev's inequality at tolerance $3$ gives the upper bound $\dfrac{36}{9n}=\dfrac4n$. To make it at most $0.1$ requires $n\ge\dfrac4{0.1}=40$. At $n=40$ the ratio is exactly $0.1$. At every smaller sample size this bound does not provide the required guarantee.
