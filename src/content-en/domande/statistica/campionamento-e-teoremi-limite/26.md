Two measurements have the same mean, standard deviations $3$, and covariance $4$. What is the variance of $\dfrac{X_1+X_2}{2}$?

- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $13$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$

## Explanation

Since the covariance is $4$, the variance of the sum includes the term $2\cdot4$. The sum therefore has variance $3^2+3^2+8=26$. The mean equals the sum times $\dfrac12$, so its variance is multiplied by $\left(\dfrac12\right)^2$, giving $\dfrac{26}{4}=\dfrac{13}{2}$. Omitting the covariance would treat the measurements as uncorrelated.
