Two nonnegative times have expectations $7$ and $8$ minutes and may be dependent. What upper bound does Markov's inequality give for the probability that their sum reaches $45$ minutes?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{45}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{45}$

## Explanation

Let $Y$ be the sum of the two times. Linearity of expectation applies even under dependence, giving $E[Y]=7+8=15$ minutes. The times are nonnegative, so $Y$ is also nonnegative and Markov's inequality applies. At threshold $45$ the upper bound is $\dfrac{15}{45}=\dfrac13$. Covariances are not needed for this calculation.
