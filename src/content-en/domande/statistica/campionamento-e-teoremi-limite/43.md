Let $Y$ equal $12$ with probability $\dfrac13$ and zero otherwise. What upper bound does Markov's inequality give for $P(Y\ge12)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The only positive value of $Y$ is $12$, with probability $\dfrac13$. Its expectation is therefore $12\cdot\dfrac13+0\cdot\dfrac23=4$. Markov's inequality at threshold $12$ gives $P(Y\ge12)\le\dfrac4{12}=\dfrac13$. The bound is attained because every tail value equals the threshold and all remaining values are zero.
