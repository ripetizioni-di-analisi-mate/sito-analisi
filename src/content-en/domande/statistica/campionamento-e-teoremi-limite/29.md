Four independent observations are drawn from $N(10,25)$. What is the exact distribution of their mean?

- $N(10,25)$
- $N(10,\dfrac{25}{4})$
- $N(40,100)$
- $N(10,\dfrac{25}{2})$

## Explanation

The sample mean is a linear combination of four independent normal variables and hence is normal. Its center is $10$, since the arithmetic mean of the four expectations is $10$. Independence gives variance $\dfrac{25}{4}$. Thus the distribution is $N\left(10,\dfrac{25}{4}\right)$, whose second parameter denotes the variance of the sample mean.
