For $10$ i.i.d. observations from a normal population with positive variance, which two statistics are independent?

- The mean $\bar X_{10}$ and the sum of the observations
- The mean $\bar X_{10}$ and twice that mean
- The variance $S^2$ and its square root $S$
- The mean $\bar X_{10}$ and the sample variance $S^2$

## Explanation

In the normal sample the mean depends on the projection of the observation vector onto the common direction $(1,\ldots,1)$. Deviations from the mean lie in the orthogonal subspace. The normal coordinates in the two projections are independent, so the mean is independent of every function of the deviations, including $S^2$. This property uses normality.
