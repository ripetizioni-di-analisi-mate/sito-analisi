The mean of an i.i.d. sample has variance $\dfrac{20}{n}$. For a fixed tolerance $2$, which Chebyshev bound directly shows convergence in probability?

- $\dfrac{10}{n}$
- $\dfrac5n$
- $\dfrac4n$
- $\dfrac{20}{n^2}$

## Explanation

For a deviation of at least $2$ from the population mean, Chebyshev's bound is the variance of the sample mean divided by $2^2$, giving $\dfrac{20}{4n}=\dfrac5n$. This also bounds the smaller event that the deviation is strictly greater than $2$. As $n$ increases the bound tends to zero.
