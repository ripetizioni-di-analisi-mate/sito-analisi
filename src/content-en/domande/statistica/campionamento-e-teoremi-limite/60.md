Two independent samples of $100$ observations each have population standard deviation $10$. Observations are i.i.d. within each sample. What is the standard deviation of the difference between their means?

- $\sqrt2$
- $2$
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Each mean has standard error $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt{100}}=1$ and variance $1$. The samples are independent, so their means are independent. For the difference, the coefficient $-1$ of the second mean is squared when calculating the variance. This gives $1+1=2$ and standard deviation $\sqrt2$.
