Compare the means of $100$ and $300$ i.i.d. observations from the same population with positive finite variance. For the same positive tolerance, what is the ratio of the Chebyshev bounds for $n=300$ and $n=100$ before either bound is capped at $1$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

With population variance $\sigma^2>0$ and tolerance $a>0$, the two Chebyshev bounds are $\dfrac{\sigma^2}{300a^2}$ and $\dfrac{\sigma^2}{100a^2}$. Before capping the bounds, common factors cancel, leaving $\dfrac{100}{300}=\dfrac13$. Sample size enters the variance of the mean directly, so tripling it divides the bound by three.
