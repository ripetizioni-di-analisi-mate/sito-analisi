A binomial count $K$ has parameters $n=120$ and $p=0.5$. Which threshold $b$ should be used to approximate $P(K<65)$ by the normal area to the left of $b$ after applying the continuity correction?

- $65$
- $63.5$
- $64.5$
- $65.5$

## Explanation

The count is integer-valued, so the largest count in $K<65$ is $K=64$. The continuity correction associates each integer $k$ with a unit-width cell centered at $k$. The cell for $64$ extends to $64+0.5=64.5$. The desired probability is approximated by the normal area to the left of this boundary.
