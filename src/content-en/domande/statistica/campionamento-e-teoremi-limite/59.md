Take the sum of $49$ i.i.d. weights with mean $4$ and standard deviation $3$. What are the mean and standard deviation of the total?

- $(196,441)$
- $(196,21)$
- $(196,147)$
- $(4,\dfrac37)$

## Explanation

For the total, summing the $49$ individual expectations gives $49\cdot4=196$. Independence allows the variances to be added, giving $49\cdot3^2=441$. The total has standard deviation $\sqrt{441}=21$. The requested pair is $(196,21)$, with the second value in the same units as the weights.
