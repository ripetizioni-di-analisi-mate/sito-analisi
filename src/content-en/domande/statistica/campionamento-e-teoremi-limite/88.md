For an i.i.d. sample of $64$ observations with mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $8$, we want to bound $P(|\bar X_{64}-\mu|\ge3)$ using only the moments. Which result is guaranteed?

- The probability is exactly $2\bigl(1-\Phi(3)\bigr)$
- The probability is exactly $\dfrac19$
- The probability is at least $\dfrac89$
- The probability is at most $\dfrac19$

## Explanation

With $64$ i.i.d. observations, the variance of the sample mean is $\dfrac{8^2}{64}=1$. Chebyshev's inequality bounds the probability of an absolute deviation of at least $3$ by $\dfrac1{3^2}=\dfrac19$. The bound uses only the mean and the variance. An exact probability would require more information about the distribution of the sample mean.
