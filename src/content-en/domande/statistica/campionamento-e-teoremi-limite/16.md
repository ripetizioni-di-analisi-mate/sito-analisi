Three readings are taken from the same sensor and their mean is recorded. Which expression denotes the sample mean before the readings are observed?

- $\bar X_3$
- $\mu$
- $S$
- $\bar x$

## Explanation

Before the three measurements are taken, each is represented by a random variable $X_i$. The ratio $\dfrac{X_1+X_2+X_3}{3}$ is also random and is denoted by $\bar X_3$. Substitution of the actual measurements then gives a number, written $\bar x$. The uppercase letter denotes the statistic before its realization is observed.
