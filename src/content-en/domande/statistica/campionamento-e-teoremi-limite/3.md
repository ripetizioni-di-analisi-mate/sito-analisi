Consider an i.i.d. sample of nine observations from $N(30,9)$. Calculate $P(\bar X_9>31)$ using $\Phi(1)\approx0.8413$.

- $0.5$
- $0.3174$
- $0.8413$
- $0.1587$

## Explanation

The observations are independent and normal, so their mean is exactly normal. Each has variance $9$, giving the mean standard deviation $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt9}=1$. The threshold $31$ is $\dfrac{31-30}{1}=1$ standard errors from the center. The event is in the right tail, so its probability is $1-\Phi(1)\approx0.1587$.
