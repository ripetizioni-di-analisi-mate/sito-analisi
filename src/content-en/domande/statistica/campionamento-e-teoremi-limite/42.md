Which information allows Markov's inequality to be applied directly to $P(Y\ge10)$?

- $Y$ is nonnegative and $E[Y]=2$
- $Y$ has mean $2$ and may take negative values
- $Y$ takes only positive values and only its median is known
- $Y$ has median $2$ and may take negative values

## Explanation

Markov's inequality for $P(Y\ge10)$ uses the expectation and requires $Y$ to be nonnegative. If $E[Y]=2$, then $P(Y\ge10)\le\dfrac2{10}=0.2$. Nonnegativity ensures that values below the threshold cannot reduce the tail's contribution to the expectation. The median alone does not provide the required numerator.
