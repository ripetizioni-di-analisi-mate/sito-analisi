For an i.i.d. sample from $N(0,1)$, set $\varepsilon_n=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt n}$. Calculate $P(|\bar X_n|>\varepsilon_n)$ using $\Phi(1)\approx0.841345$ and round to four decimal places.

- $0$
- $0.3173$
- $0.1587$
- $0.6826$

## Explanation

The sample mean is normal with center zero and standard deviation $\dfrac1{\sqrt n}$. Because the tolerance equals that standard deviation, the standardized event is $|Z|>1$. Symmetry gives the combined tail area $2(1-\Phi(1))$. Substitution gives $2\cdot0.158655=0.317310$, or $0.3173$ to four decimal places. The probability stays constant because the tolerance varies with $n$.
