Nine i.i.d. observations are drawn from a normal population with mean $16$. Given $\bar x=18$ and $s=6$, what is the observed value of $t=\dfrac{\bar x-16}{\dfrac{s}{\sqrt9}}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The numerator is the observed deviation from the population mean, $18-16=2$. The denominator estimates the standard error of the mean and equals $\dfrac6{\sqrt9}=2$. The result is $t=\dfrac22=1$. The observed mean is one estimated standard error above the reference $16$.
