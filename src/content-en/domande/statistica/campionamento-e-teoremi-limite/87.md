From a finite population of $101$ units, $11$ units are drawn. Which statement compares sample means with and without replacement, given simple random sampling and a positive population variance?

- The two variances are equal
- The two means have the same expectation and the mean without replacement has smaller variance
- Without replacement the mean has smaller expectation
- With replacement the variance of the mean is zero

## Explanation

Under both designs, each draw has expectation equal to the population mean. By linearity, the expected sample mean is therefore the same under both designs. Without replacement, its variance equals the variance with replacement times $\dfrac{101-11}{101-1}=0.9$. The initial variance is positive and the factor is less than one, so the variance strictly decreases.
