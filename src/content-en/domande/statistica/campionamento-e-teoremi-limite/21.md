Four independent measurements have variances $1$, $1$, $4$, and $10$, respectively. Calculate the standard error of their arithmetic mean.

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Independence makes the covariances zero, so the sum of the four measurements has variance $1+1+4+10=16$. Dividing the sum by $4$ multiplies its variance by $\dfrac1{4^2}$. The mean therefore has variance $1$ and standard deviation $\sqrt1=1$. All four variances enter because they are different.
