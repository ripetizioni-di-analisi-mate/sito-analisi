For an i.i.d. sample of $25$ weights in grams, the standard error of the mean is $4\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the standard deviation of an individual observation?

- $20\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{g}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g}$
- $100\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

For an i.i.d. sample, the standard error of the mean equals the individual standard deviation divided by $\sqrt n$. Here $4\,\mathrm{g}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{25}}$. To recover $\sigma$, multiply by $5$, giving $20\,\mathrm{g}$. The mean of twenty-five observations has one-fifth the spread of an individual observation.
