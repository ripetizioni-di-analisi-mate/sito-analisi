For an i.i.d. sample of $25$ observations from $N(40,25)$, find the threshold that the sample mean exceeds with probability $0.025$. Use $z_{0.975}\approx1.96$.

- $40.392$
- $41.96$
- $38.04$
- $49.8$

## Explanation

The mean of independent normal observations has center $40$ and standard error $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{25}}=1$. A threshold exceeded with probability $0.025$ has the remaining probability $0.975$ to its left. The standard normal quantile is therefore $1.96$. Converting back to the scale of the sample mean gives $40+1.96\cdot1=41.96$, above the center of the distribution.
