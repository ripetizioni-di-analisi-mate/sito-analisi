Two i.i.d. samples of the same size from the same population yield different observed standard deviations. Which quantity changes from one sample to the other?

- The estimated standard error
- The population mean
- The theoretical distribution of the sample mean
- The theoretical standard error

## Explanation

The theoretical standard error is $\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$. Population and sample size are the same, so it is identical for the two samples. To estimate it, replace $\sigma$ by the sample standard deviation $s$. The resulting estimates $\dfrac{s_1}{\sqrt n}$ and $\dfrac{s_2}{\sqrt n}$ differ because the observed numerator changes.
