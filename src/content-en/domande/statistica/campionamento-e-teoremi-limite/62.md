For $n=100$, the sample mean has center $15$ and standard error $0.5$. Approximate its distribution by a normal distribution. What threshold has probability $0.10$ to its left? Use $z_{0.90}\approx1.28$.

- $14.36$
- $15.64$
- $14.72$
- $13.72$

## Explanation

For probability $10\,\%$ to lie to the left, the standard normal quantile must be negative. By symmetry, the quantile of order $0.10$ is the negative of the quantile of order $0.90$ and is about $-1.28$. Returning to the sample mean's scale gives $15-1.28\cdot0.5=14.36$. The negative sign places the threshold below the center $15$.
