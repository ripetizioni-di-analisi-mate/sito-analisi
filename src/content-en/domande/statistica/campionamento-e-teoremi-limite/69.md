The success count in $100$ i.i.d. Bernoulli trials has mean $20$. What is the standard deviation of the proportion of successes?

- $0.16$
- $0.2$
- $0.04$
- $4$

## Explanation

The binomial count satisfies $E[K]=100p$, so $p=\dfrac{20}{100}=0.2$. The proportion is the mean of $100$ independent indicators and has variance $\dfrac{0.2\cdot0.8}{100}=0.0016$. Taking the square root gives $0.04$. Passing from the count to the proportion changes the scale of the spread.
