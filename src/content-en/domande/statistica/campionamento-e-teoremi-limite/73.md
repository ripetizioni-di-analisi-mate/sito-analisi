In $80$ Bernoulli trials, which threshold on the count scale is used to approximate $P(\hat p\le0.30)$ by a normal distribution with continuity correction?

- $24$
- $23.5$
- $24.5$
- $25.5$

## Explanation

The success proportion is $\hat p=\dfrac K{80}$. Multiplying the constraint by $80$ gives $K\le80\cdot0.30=24$. The inequality allows $24$, so the normal area must include its entire cell, from $23.5$ to $24.5$. The upper boundary on the count scale is therefore $24.5$. Take the area to its left.
