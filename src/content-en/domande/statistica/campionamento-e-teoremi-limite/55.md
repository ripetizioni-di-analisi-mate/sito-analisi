For the mean of an i.i.d. sample, which tolerance can be fixed directly in the statement of the weak law?

- The quantity $\dfrac1{\sqrt n}$ for every distribution with finite variance
- A positive constant such as $0.01$
- The value $0$
- The observed deviation $|\bar X_n-\mu|$

## Explanation

In the weak law a positive tolerance $\varepsilon$ is chosen and held fixed as $n$ grows. Therefore $\varepsilon=0.01$ can be used directly. The denominator of the Chebyshev bound contains $n\varepsilon^2$, which grows for this choice. When the tolerance varies, this behavior must be checked separately and can change.
