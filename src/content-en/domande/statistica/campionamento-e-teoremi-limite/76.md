Let $K\sim\operatorname{Bin}(100,0.5)$. With the continuity correction, over which interval is the approximating normal density integrated for the event $|K-50|\le2$?

- $(48,52)$
- $(48.5,51.5)$
- $(47,53)$
- $(47.5,52.5)$

## Explanation

The event $|K-50|\le2$ is equivalent to $-2\le K-50\le2$, that is, to $48\le K\le52$. The admissible integers are therefore $48$, $49$, $50$, $51$, and $52$. With the continuity correction the lower endpoint becomes $48-0.5=47.5$ and the upper endpoint becomes $52+0.5=52.5$. The interval from $47.5$ to $52.5$ covers the five bars of width one centered on the admissible counts.
