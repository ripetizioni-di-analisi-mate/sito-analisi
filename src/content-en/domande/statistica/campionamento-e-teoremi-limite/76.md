A binomial count ranges from $0$ to $20$. A continuous normal approximation also assigns positive area below $-0.5$ and above $20.5$. Which procedure makes the approximate masses of the $21$ integers sum to exactly one while preserving their ratios?

- Multiply each area by $21$
- Subtract the same area from every band
- Add all the excluded area to the band at $10$ only
- Divide each band area by the normal area from $-0.5$ to $20.5$

## Explanation

The $21$ adjacent bands exactly cover the stated interval. Their areas sum to $A$, the normal probability of that interval. Dividing them all by $A$ gives a sum of one and preserves every ratio.
