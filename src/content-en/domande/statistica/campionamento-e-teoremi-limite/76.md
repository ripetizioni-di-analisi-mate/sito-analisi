To approximate the binomial probability mass at $k=21$ with a normal distribution, which area should be used?

- The area over the interval from $21$ to $21.5$
- The area over the interval from $20$ to $21$
- The area over the single point $21$
- The area over the interval from $20.5$ to $21.5$

## Explanation

Each integer count corresponds to an interval of width one centered on that count. For $k=21$ the endpoints are $21-0.5=20.5$ and $21+0.5=21.5$. Integrate the normal density between the two endpoints. An area is required because a continuous normal distribution assigns probability zero to the single point $21$.
