Using the rule of thumb $np\ge10$ and $n(1-p)\ge10$, find the smallest $n$ for which to use the normal approximation to a binomial with parameter $p=0.2$.

- $40$
- $50$
- $13$
- $10$

## Explanation

With $p=0.2$ the two conditions become $0.2n\ge10$ and $0.8n\ge10$. The first requires $n\ge50$ and the second $n\ge12.5$. Both must hold, so the tighter constraint is $n\ge50$. For $n=50$ the expected counts of successes and failures are $10$ and $40$, satisfying the criterion.
