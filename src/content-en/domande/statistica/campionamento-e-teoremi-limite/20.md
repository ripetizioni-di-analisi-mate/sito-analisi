Two independent measurements have the same mean but variances $4$ and $12$. Does this pair satisfy the i.i.d. assumption?

- Dependent, with different distributions
- Identically distributed because of independence
- Independent, with different distributions
- I.i.d. because the means are equal

## Explanation

Independence is a property of the joint distribution and is given here. Identical distribution concerns each measurement's marginal distribution. Two variables with finite variance and the same distribution must have the same variance. Here $4$ and $12$ differ, so the distributions differ even though the means agree.
