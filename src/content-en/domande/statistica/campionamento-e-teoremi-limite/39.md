For an i.i.d. normal sample of size $25$, what is the distribution of $\dfrac{\bar X_{25}-\mu}{\dfrac S5}$?

- $N(0,1)$
- $t_{24}$
- $t_{25}$
- $\chi^2_{24}$

## Explanation

The numerator is the deviation of the sample mean from $\mu$. The denominator is its estimated standard error $\dfrac S{\sqrt{25}}=\dfrac S5$. In a normal sample, the mean and sample variance are independent and the ratio has Student's t distribution. Since the deviations are measured from the estimated mean, there are $25-1=24$ degrees of freedom.
