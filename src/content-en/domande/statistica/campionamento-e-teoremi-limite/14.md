Take a simple random sample of $10$ units without replacement from a population of $41$ units. The population variance with divisor $N$ is $20$. Calculate the variance of the sample mean.

- $1.55$
- $2$
- $0.775$
- $1.5$

## Explanation

When the population variance uses divisor $N$, the reference variance with replacement is $\dfrac{20}{10}=2$. For a simple random sample without replacement, apply the factor $\dfrac{N-n}{N-1}=\dfrac{31}{40}$. The product is $2\cdot\dfrac{31}{40}=1.55$. The reduction reflects the dependence between draws without replacement.
