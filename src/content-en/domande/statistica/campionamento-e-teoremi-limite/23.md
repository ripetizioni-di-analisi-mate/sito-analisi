Two laboratories use the same sample size. Individual measurements have positive standard deviation in laboratory B and three times that standard deviation in laboratory A. If measurements are i.i.d. within each sample, what is the ratio of the variances of the two sample means, A to B?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The variance of an i.i.d. sample mean is $\dfrac{\sigma^2}{n}$. The sample size $n$ is the same in the two laboratories and cancels in the ratio A to B. For $\sigma_B>0$ the ratio is defined, and since $\sigma_A=3\sigma_B$ it equals $\dfrac{(3\sigma_B)^2}{\sigma_B^2}=9$. The factor of three applies to standard deviations and must therefore be squared.
