Design A selects a pair without replacement from a finite population. Design B draws the first unit from the same population and selects the second after excluding a larger group of units. Which condition justifies using the finite population correction?

- The two draws must give the same result
- Each unit must have a distinct value
- The first draw must have positive finite variance
- All unordered pairs must be equally likely

## Explanation

The finite population correction assumes simple random sampling without replacement. For a sample of two, every pair of units must have the same probability. The additional restriction in design B can give some pairs probability zero and others positive probability. Sampling without replacement alone does not establish the design the formula requires.
