A population contains $N=5$ units and its variance with divisor $N$ is $8$. What is its variance with divisor $N-1$?

- $2$
- $10$
- $6.4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Changing the divisor leaves the population values, their mean, and the sum of squared deviations unchanged. With divisor $N=5$, that sum is $5\cdot8=40$. With divisor $N-1=4$ the variance is therefore $\dfrac{40}{4}=10$. The result is greater than $8$ because the same sum is divided by a smaller number.
