The population mean $\mu$ is unknown. Which quantity is a statistic of the sample $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ that could be considered when estimating this mean?

- $\dfrac{\bar X_n-\mu}{S}$
- $\mu+X_1$
- $\max_i X_i-\min_i X_i$
- $\bar X_n-\mu$

## Explanation

A statistic uses only observations and known constants. The sample maximum and minimum can be found and subtracted even when the population mean is unknown, so the sample range meets the definition. Expressions involving $\mu$ require a parameter the given data do not determine.
