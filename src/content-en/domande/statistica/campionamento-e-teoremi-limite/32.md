The mean of a normal sample has quantile $46$ at order $0.25$ and quantile $54$ at order $0.75$. What is the population mean?

- $8$
- $46$
- $54$
- $50$

## Explanation

The quantiles of orders $0.25$ and $0.75$ of the normal sample mean leave equal areas in the outer tails. Symmetry places them at equal distances from the center. Their midpoint is $\dfrac{46+54}{2}=50$. The expectation of the sample mean equals the population mean, so $50$ is also the desired parameter.
