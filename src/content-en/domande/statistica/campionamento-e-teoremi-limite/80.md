The population variance with divisor $N$ is $12$, and $N=13$. Under simple random sampling without replacement, what is the covariance between two distinct draws?

- $1$
- $-12$
- $0$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Once a unit has been drawn, it cannot be drawn again, so the two draws are dependent. For simple random sampling without replacement, when the population variance uses divisor $N$, the covariance is $-\dfrac{\sigma_N^2}{N-1}$. Here it equals $-\dfrac{12}{13-1}=-1$. Its negative sign helps reduce the variance of the mean.
