Let $K$ be the number of successes in $50$ Bernoulli trials and $\hat p=\dfrac{K}{50}$. With the continuity correction, which upper boundary is used to approximate $P(\hat p<0.42)$ with a normal distribution?

- $21$
- $21.5$
- $20.5$
- $19.5$

## Explanation

Since $\hat p=\dfrac K{50}$, the condition is equivalent to $K<50\cdot0.42=21$. The largest admissible count is $20$, and its cell ends at $20+0.5=20.5$. Therefore the upper boundary of the area under the normal curve is $20.5$. The answer $21.5$ comes from including count $21$, which the strict inequality excludes even though the product $50\cdot0.42$ is an integer.
