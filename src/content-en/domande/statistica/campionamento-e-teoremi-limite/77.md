With $n=50$ a report gives $\widehat p=K/50$ for an integer count $K$. Which of $\widehat p<0.41$ and $\widehat p\le0.41$ requires the higher continuous cutoff under a continuity correction?

- The first, because it excludes a boundary value
- The second, because it includes an outcome at exactly $0.41$
- Neither: the events coincide and the cutoff on the count scale is $20.5$
- The first has the higher cutoff when $p>0.5$

## Explanation

The value $0.41$ would require the impossible count $20.5$. Both events therefore mean $K\le20$, with the same corrected upper boundary $20.5$.
