For $50$ Bernoulli trials, which continuity-corrected upper boundary should be used to approximate $P(\hat p<0.42)$?

- $21$
- $21.5$
- $20.5$
- $19.5$

## Explanation

Since $\hat p=\dfrac K{50}$, the condition is equivalent to $K<50\cdot0.42=21$. The largest admissible count is $20$, and its cell ends at $20+0.5=20.5$. This gives the upper boundary of the normal area. The strict inequality excludes count $21$ even though the product $50\cdot0.42$ is an integer.
