Nonnegative daily expenditure has mean $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$. For which threshold does Markov's inequality bound the probability of reaching it by exactly $0.1$?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $120\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $12\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

For nonnegative expenditure, Markov's inequality gives $P(Y\ge a)\le\dfrac{12}{a}$ at every threshold $a>0$. The required ratio is $0.1$. Multiplying by $a$ gives $12=0.1a$, hence $a=120\,\mathrm{EUR}$. At this threshold the inequality gives the upper bound $0.1$.
