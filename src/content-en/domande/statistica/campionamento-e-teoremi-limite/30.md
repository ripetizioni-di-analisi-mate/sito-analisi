The observations are i.i.d. normal with mean $\mu$. If $\sigma>0$, what is $P(\bar X_n>\mu)$ for each $n\ge1$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac1{2\sqrt n}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The mean of i.i.d. normal observations is normal with expectation $\mu$ and variance $\dfrac{\sigma^2}{n}>0$. Its density is symmetric about $\mu$, so the areas to the left and right agree. The single point $\mu$ has probability zero by continuity. The two areas sum to one, and each equals $\dfrac12$.
