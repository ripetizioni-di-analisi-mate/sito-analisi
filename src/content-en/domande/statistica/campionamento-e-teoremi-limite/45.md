A measurement has mean $20$ and standard deviation $5$. What is Chebyshev's bound on the probability of a deviation of at least $15$ from the mean?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{45}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$

## Explanation

The variance of the measurement is $5^2=25$. The deviation is measured in absolute value, so the event is $|X-20|\ge15$. Dividing the variance by the squared tolerance gives Chebyshev's bound $\dfrac{25}{225}=\dfrac19$. The tolerance is three standard deviations, so the ratio is the reciprocal of three squared.
