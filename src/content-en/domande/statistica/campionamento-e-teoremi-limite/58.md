An i.i.d. sample has population mean $10$, population standard deviation $6$, and size $36$. What is the standardized coordinate of the threshold $8.5$ in the normal approximation for the sample mean?

- $-9$
- $1.5$
- $-0.25$
- $-1.5$

## Explanation

The sample mean has center $10$, and for $36$ i.i.d. observations its standard error is $\dfrac6{\sqrt{36}}=1$. The threshold $8.5$ lies $1.5$ units below the center, giving standardized value $\dfrac{8.5-10}{1}=-1.5$. The denominator is the standard error of the mean because the approximation concerns this statistic's distribution.
