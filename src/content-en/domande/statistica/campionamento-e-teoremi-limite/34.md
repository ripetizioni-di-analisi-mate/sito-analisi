The mean of $36$ i.i.d. normal observations has distribution $N(12,4)$. What is the population variance?

- $24$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $144$
- $4$

## Explanation

The second parameter of $N(12,4)$ gives the sample mean's variance. For $36$ i.i.d. observations, $\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_{36})=\dfrac{\sigma^2}{36}$. Equating this ratio to $4$ gives $\sigma^2=4\cdot36=144$. The standard deviation of individual measurements would be the square root of $144$.
