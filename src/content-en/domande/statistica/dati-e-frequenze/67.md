A histogram described as a relative frequency density histogram includes all classes. Their areas are exactly $0.25$, $0.40$, and $0.30$. What follows?

- The stated areas are incompatible with the definition
- The areas are valid if the class widths differ
- The areas are valid because each is smaller than 1
- The last area must remain unchanged and the first must be zero

## Explanation

The three areas sum to $0.25+0.40+0.30=0.95$. In a relative frequency density histogram, each area is the proportion in its class. If the classes cover all observations, their proportions must add to $1$. Because the stated areas are exact, the discrepancy of $0.05$ makes them incompatible with the definition.
