A simple random sample of 10 units is drawn without replacement from a population of 100. Which property is required?

- The first 10 units on the list are always selected
- Every subset of 10 units has the same probability of selection
- Successive draws are independent
- One unit can appear twice in the sample

## Explanation

Simple random sampling without replacement considers sets of $10$ distinct units. Each of the $\binom{100}{10}$ possible subsets must have the same probability of selection. Drawing uniformly from the remaining units at each step satisfies this condition: each ordered sequence of ten distinct units is equally likely, and every subset corresponds to the same number of sequences.
