A simple random sample of 10 units is to be drawn without replacement from a population of 100. Which property is required?

- The first 10 units on the list are always selected
- Every group of 10 units has the same probability of selection
- The first 10 units to answer an invitation are chosen
- One unit can appear twice in the sample

## Explanation

A simple random sample of $10$ units is obtained by drawing $10$ distinct units by lot, so that every group of $10$ units in the population has the **same probability** of being chosen. Without replacement, a unit already drawn is not returned to the pool, so it cannot appear twice. Taking the first $10$ units on the list or the first $10$ to answer is not a random draw, because all the other groups of $10$ units have no chance of being chosen.
