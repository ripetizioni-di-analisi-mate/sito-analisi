A sample contains exactly 12 observations. Which value can its empirical distribution function take?

- $\dfrac7{10}$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac7{12}$
- $\dfrac{11}{13}$

## Explanation

At each threshold, the distribution function is an integer count of observations at or below the threshold divided by $12$. Multiplying an admissible value by $12$ must therefore give an integer from $0$ to $12$. Among the options, $\dfrac7{12}$ meets this criterion. Its numerator $7$ corresponds to a threshold that includes seven observations.
