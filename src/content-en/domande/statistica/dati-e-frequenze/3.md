The numbers of deliveries per customer are $0,1,2,3$, with respective absolute frequencies $8,10,5,7$. Find the number of customers with at most two deliveries.

- $7$
- $18$
- $5$
- $23$

## Explanation

“At most two” means the number of deliveries is less than or equal to $2$. Include the $8$ customers with no deliveries, the $10$ with one, and the $5$ with two. The cumulative count at $2$ is $8+10+5=23$. The $7$ customers with three deliveries are above the threshold.

[Theory behind this method](/en/statistica/dati-e-frequenze/teoria#cumulate).
