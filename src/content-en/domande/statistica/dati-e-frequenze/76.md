A first session has 20 people, of whom $60\%$ completed a task. A second has 80 people, of whom $35\%$ completed it. Find the overall completion proportion.

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{19}{40}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The first session contributes $20\cdot0.60=12$ completions and the second $80\cdot0.35=28$. Together this gives $12+28=40$ completions among $20+80=100$ people, hence a proportion of $0.40$. It is closer to the second session's $35\%$ because that session has four times as many people.
