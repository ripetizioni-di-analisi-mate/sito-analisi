The ordered values $2,4,6,8$ have cumulative counts $5,14,14,20$. What is the count for the value 6?

- $0$
- $6$
- $9$
- $14$

## Explanation

The cumulative count up to $4$ includes $14$ observations. At the next value the cumulative count up to $6$ adds only observations equal to $6$. Their count is therefore $14-14=0$. Equal consecutive cumulative counts mean the running total did not increase.
