In a frequency density histogram based on counts, adjacent classes $[0,2)$ and $[2,8)$ have heights 5 and 3. What is the height of class $[0,8)$ when they are combined?

- $8$
- $4$
- $28$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

With absolute frequency density, area represents a count. The adjacent classes contain $2\cdot5=10$ and $(8-2)\cdot3=18$ observations. After combining, the count is $28$ and the width is $8$, giving height $\dfrac{28}{8}=3.5$. The area of the new rectangle therefore equals the sum of the original areas.
