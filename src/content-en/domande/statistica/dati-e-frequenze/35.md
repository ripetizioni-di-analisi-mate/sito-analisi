In a pie chart, one category occupies an angle of $72^\circ$. Find its relative frequency.

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{18}{25}$

## Explanation

A sector's angle is proportional to its relative frequency. All categories together occupy $360^\circ$, so the sector's proportion is $\dfrac{72^\circ}{360^\circ}=\dfrac15$. The angles have the same unit, which cancels in the ratio. A relative frequency of one-fifth corresponds to $20\%$ of the observations.
