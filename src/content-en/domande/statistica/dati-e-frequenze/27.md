In a survey, $64$ students are asked how they get to school. Each student names a single means of transport: $19$ answer bus, $17$ bicycle and all the others car. What is the relative frequency of the category "car"?

- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{7}{16}$
- $\dfrac{19}{64}$
- $\dfrac{17}{64}$

## Explanation

Each student names a single means of transport, so the absolute frequencies of the three categories add up to $64$. The students who come by car are therefore $64-19-17=28$. The relative frequency is the ratio of the absolute frequency to the total number of students, so it equals $\dfrac{28}{64}=\dfrac{7}{16}$.
