A database has 64 records in three mutually exclusive and exhaustive categories. The first two contain 19 and 17 records. Find the relative frequency of the third.

- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{7}{16}$
- $\dfrac{19}{64}$
- $\dfrac{17}{64}$

## Explanation

Every record belongs to exactly one of the three categories. Subtracting the first two counts from the total gives $64-19-17=28$ records in the third category. Divide by all $64$ records to get the relative frequency $\dfrac{28}{64}=\dfrac7{16}$.
