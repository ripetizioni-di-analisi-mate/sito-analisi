Of 100 students, 45 attend the evening session and 66 pass a test. Among evening students, 30 pass. What fraction of those who passed attend the evening session?

- $\dfrac{5}{11}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

“Of those who passed” identifies the $66$ students who passed as the reference group. The number who both passed and attended in the evening is $30$, so the fraction is $\dfrac{30}{66}=\dfrac5{11}$. Dividing by $45$ would answer a different question: what proportion of evening students passed.
