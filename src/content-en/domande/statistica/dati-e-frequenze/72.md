Among $60$ people, $30$ cycle and $24$ take the train. An analyst wants the fraction of cyclists who take the train. Which single extra piece of information, together with the two totals, is enough?

- The number using at least one of the two modes
- The number who do not cycle
- The percentage of all respondents taking the train
- The ratio of the two totals already recorded

## Explanation

If $u$ people use at least one mode, $30+24-u$ use both. The requested fraction is therefore $(54-u)/30$. The other answers follow from the known totals and do not determine the overlap.
