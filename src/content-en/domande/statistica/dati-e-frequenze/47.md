The measurements $0,2,2,4,5,6,9$ are grouped into classes $[0,2)$, $[2,6)$, and $[6,9]$. How many belong to the second class?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

The second class $[2,6)$ includes its lower endpoint $2$ and excludes its upper endpoint $6$. It contains the two occurrences of $2$, the value $4$, and the value $5$, for four observations altogether. The value $6$ belongs to the next class, which includes it as its left endpoint.
