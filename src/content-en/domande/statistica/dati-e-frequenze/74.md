In a two-row table, the first row totals zero and the second totals 32. Which proportion is undefined?

- The proportion in the first column within the first row
- The marginal relative frequency of the first row
- The marginal relative frequency of the second row
- The proportion of the grand total in the first cell of the first row

## Explanation

The proportion in a column within the first row divides its cell count by the first row total. Since that total is zero, the cell count is also zero and the ratio $\dfrac00$ is undefined. For a share of the grand total, the denominator is $32$, so an empty cell has relative frequency zero.
