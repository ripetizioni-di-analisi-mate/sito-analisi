A class is $[2.5,6.5)$ centimeters. What is its width in millimeters?

- $90$
- $65$
- $40$
- $4$

## Explanation

The class width is the difference between its endpoints, $(6.5-2.5)\,\mathrm{cm}=4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Each centimeter is ten millimeters, giving $40\,\mathrm{mm}$. This can be checked by first converting the endpoints to $25\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $65\,\mathrm{mm}$ and then subtracting.
