A relative frequency density histogram has exactly three classes. The first two areas are $0.25$ and $0.35$. The third class has width $5\,\mathrm{s}$. Find its height in $\mathrm{s}^{-1}$.

- $\dfrac{3}{25}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{25}$
- $2$

## Explanation

Since the three classes cover all observations, their rectangle areas sum to $1$. The third area is therefore $1-(0.25+0.35)=0.40$. This area represents relative frequency. Dividing by the width $5\,\mathrm{s}$ gives the height $\dfrac{0.40}{5}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}=0.08\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$. The unit follows from dividing a proportion by a duration.
