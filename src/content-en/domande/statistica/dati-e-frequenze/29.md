A category appears in 21 of 70 records. Ten records, all outside the category, are removed. Find its relative frequency after their removal.

- $\dfrac{31}{60}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{11}{60}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}$

## Explanation

The category is absent from the $10$ removed records, so its count stays $21$. The new set has $70-10=60$ records. The relative frequency is therefore $\dfrac{21}{60}=0.35$. The proportion exceeds the original $0.30$ because the same count is divided by a smaller total.
