A study aims to describe all orders in one month. It checks every invoice for that month individually, with one invoice per order. Which design does it use?

- A census
- A convenience sample
- A stratified sample
- A simple random sample

## Explanation

The population is all orders in the month. Since each order has an invoice and all invoices for the month are checked, every population unit is observed. This is therefore a **census**. The criterion is complete coverage of the defined population, even though the study covers only one month.
