Four thickness measurements are made on each of 40 selected plates. The plates are compared using each plate's mean thickness. How many values enter the comparison?

- $10$
- $40$
- $4$
- $160$

## Explanation

The plate is the unit of comparison. Each plate has four raw measurements, but averaging the four gives one value to compare with the other plates. Thus $40$ values enter the comparison. The product $40\cdot4=160$ counts the original measurements and tells us how many readings were taken.
