In a survey, 18 of the 72 participants arrive by train. Find the relative frequency of participants who arrive by train.

- $\dfrac{9}{50}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Relative frequency measures the proportion of participants who arrive by train. The $18$ people in the category form the numerator, and all $72$ participants form the denominator. Since $72=4\cdot18$, the ratio $\dfrac{18}{72}$ reduces to $\dfrac14$. As a percentage this is $25\%$. The number $18$ is the absolute frequency.
