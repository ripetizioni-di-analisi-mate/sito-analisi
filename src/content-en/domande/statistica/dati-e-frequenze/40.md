The minimum in a sample is $-2$ and occurs three times among 12 observations. What is the value of the empirical distribution function at $-2$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac1{12}$

## Explanation

The empirical distribution function at $-2$ counts all observations less than or equal to $-2$. Since $-2$ is the minimum, this includes exactly the three observations equal to it. Their proportion is $\dfrac{3}{12}=\dfrac14$. Just below the threshold the function is zero, so the jump at $-2$ has size $\dfrac14$.
