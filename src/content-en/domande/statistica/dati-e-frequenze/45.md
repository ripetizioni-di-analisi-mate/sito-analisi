Of 60 measurements, 39 are at most 20, and 6 equal 20. How many measurements are at least 20?

- $39$
- $33$
- $27$
- $21$

## Explanation

The $39$ measurements at most $20$ include the $6$ equal to $20$. Subtracting gives $39-6=33$ measurements strictly below the threshold. The required group is their complement and therefore has $60-33=27$ measurements. Equivalently, add the $6$ equal to the threshold to the $60-39=21$ above it.
