An empirical distribution function jumps at $x=5$. Which point on the graph gives $F_n(5)$?

- The endpoint on the step to the left
- Any point on the vertical segment between the two heights
- The midpoint between the two heights
- The endpoint on the step to the right

## Explanation

The empirical distribution function counts values at or below the threshold. The step before $5$ includes only the proportion below $5$. At $5$, the observations equal to $5$ are added, so take the endpoint on the right-hand step. The lower point gives the left-hand limit $F_n(5^-)$.
