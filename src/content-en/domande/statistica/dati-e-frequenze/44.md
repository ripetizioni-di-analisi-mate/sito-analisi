The graph of an empirical distribution function jumps at $x=5$. Which point on the graph gives the value $F_n(5)$?

- The open dot at the end of the lower step
- Any point on the vertical segment between the two heights
- The midpoint between the two heights
- The solid dot at the start of the higher step

## Explanation

The empirical distribution function at $5$ counts the observations less than or equal to $5$. The lower step, which runs up to $5$, includes only the proportion of observations strictly less than $5$. At $5$ exactly, the observations equal to $5$ are added, so $F_n(5)$ is the height of the higher step, marked by the solid dot at its start. The open dot at the end of the lower step shows the value just before the jump, $F_n(5^-)$.
