Suppose $F_n(3^-)=0.2$, $F_n(3)=0.5$, and $F_n(7)=0.9$. Find the relative frequency of the interval $[3,7]$.

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$

## Explanation

The proportion $F_n(7)=0.9$ includes all observations at or below $7$. To retain the values equal to $3$, subtract only the proportion strictly below $3$, namely $F_n(3^-)=0.2$. This gives $0.9-0.2=0.7$. Subtracting $F_n(3)$ would also exclude the observations equal to the first endpoint.
