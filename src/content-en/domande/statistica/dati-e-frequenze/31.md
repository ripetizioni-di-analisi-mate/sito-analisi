Three exact percentages are each $\dfrac{100}{3}\,\%$. What sum results if each is first rounded to the nearest whole percent?

- $102\%$
- $101\%$
- $99\%$
- $100\%$

## Explanation

The value $\dfrac{100}{3}\,\%$ lies between $33\%$ and $33.5\%$, so its nearest integer is $33\%$. Rounding separately gives $33\%+33\%+33\%=99\%$. The exact proportions sum to $100\%$. The one-point difference arises because each rounding lowers the corresponding percentage by one-third of a point.
