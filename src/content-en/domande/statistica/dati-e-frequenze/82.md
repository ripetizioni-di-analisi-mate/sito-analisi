Of 90 students invited, 72 respond. Among respondents, 27 choose A. Find the response rate.

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

The response rate compares the number responding with the number invited. The numerator is $72$ and the denominator $90$, giving $\dfrac{72}{90}=\dfrac45=0.80$. The count $27$ describes the content of the responses and would be used to calculate the proportion choosing A once the reference group is specified.
