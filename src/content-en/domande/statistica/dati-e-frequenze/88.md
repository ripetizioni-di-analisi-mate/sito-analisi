A yes proportion is calculated using only valid answers to a question. What additional information determines the proportion of recorded yes answers among all invitees?

- The number of questions on the questionnaire
- The order in which responses arrived
- The proportion of valid responses among all invitees
- The name of the most frequent category

## Explanation

Let $S$ be the number of recorded yes answers, $R$ the number of valid answers, and $N$ the number invited. The known proportion is $\dfrac SR$, whereas the required proportion is $\dfrac SN$. To go from the first to the second, one needs $\dfrac RN$, the proportion of valid answers among all invitees. Indeed, $\dfrac SR\cdot\dfrac RN=\dfrac SN$. The product counts only recorded yes answers in its numerator.
