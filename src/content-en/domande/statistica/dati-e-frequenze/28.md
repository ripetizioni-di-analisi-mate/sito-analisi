A category accounts for 12 of 48 observations. Another 12 observations are added, 6 of which belong to the category. Find the new relative frequency.

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

All $12$ new observations change the total, but only the $6$ in the category change its count. The new proportion is therefore $\dfrac{12+6}{48+12}=\dfrac{18}{60}=0.30$. The increase is consistent with the category accounting for $6$ out of $12$ observations in the added group, a higher proportion than in the original group.
