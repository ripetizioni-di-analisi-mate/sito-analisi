Among 200 records, a category is reported as $42\%$ after rounding to the nearest whole percent, with halves rounded up. What is the smallest compatible count?

- $85$
- $84$
- $82$
- $83$

## Explanation

Under this rule, an exact percentage rounds to $42\%$ if it is at least $41.5\%$ and strictly less than $42.5\%$. The lower endpoint is included because halves round up. Among $200$ records the lower bound corresponds to $200\cdot0.415=83$ records, already an integer. A count of $82$ would give $41\%$.
