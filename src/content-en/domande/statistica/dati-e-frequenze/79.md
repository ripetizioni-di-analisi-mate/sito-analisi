To estimate travel times for all enrolled students, a survey interviews students attending an eight o'clock class. Which group do the resulting frequencies describe directly?

- All students with afternoon classes
- All students absent from the class
- The students interviewed in that class
- All enrolled students by definition

## Explanation

The frequencies are calculated from the people actually interviewed in that class. Their denominator is therefore the number interviewed. Students who attend at other times may have different travel times. Using these frequencies to estimate travel times for all students requires considering the selection procedure, for example whether absent students had any chance of inclusion.
