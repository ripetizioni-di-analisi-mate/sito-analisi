For the data $0,1,2,2,3,5,5,8$, how many observations belong to $(2,5]$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The open left endpoint includes only values strictly greater than $2$, so both occurrences of $2$ are excluded. The closed right endpoint includes values equal to $5$. The interval contains $3,5,5$. These are three observations because the two occurrences of $5$ are separate measurements even though their values agree.
