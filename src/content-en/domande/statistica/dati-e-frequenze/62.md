In a relative frequency density histogram of durations, the class from 0 to 4 minutes has height $0.15\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$. If the horizontal axis is converted to seconds, what is the new height in $\mathrm{s}^{-1}$?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{400}$
- $\dfrac{3}{80}$

## Explanation

The proportion in the class does not depend on the units on the horizontal axis. Its width changes from $4\,\mathrm{min}$ to $240\,\mathrm{s}$, while its area remains $4\cdot0.15=0.60$. Dividing area by the new width gives $\dfrac{0.60}{240}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}=0.0025\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$. Numerically, the width is multiplied by $60$ and the height divided by $60$.
