Of 140 questionnaires, 25 are missing A and 18 are missing B. Nine are missing both. How many contain both A and B?

- $106$
- $97$
- $131$
- $115$

## Explanation

Adding the $25$ missing A to the $18$ missing B counts the $9$ missing both twice. Thus $25+18-9=34$ questionnaires are missing at least one item. The complement is the group with complete data on A and B, giving $140-34=106$ questionnaires.
