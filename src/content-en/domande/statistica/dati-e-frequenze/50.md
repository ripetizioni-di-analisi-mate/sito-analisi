Classes $[0,3)$, $[3,8)$, and $[8,10]$ contain 10, 13, and 7 measurements. How many are strictly below 8?

- $10$
- $23$
- $30$
- $13$

## Explanation

All observations in $[0,3)$ and $[3,8)$ are below $8$, since the second interval excludes its right endpoint. All observations in $[8,10]$ are at least $8$. The required count is therefore $10+13=23$. The strict inequality means there is no need to know how many observations in the final class equal $8$.
