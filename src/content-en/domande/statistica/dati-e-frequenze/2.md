Exactly $17.5\%$ of 240 orders require an additional check. Find the number of orders to be checked.

- $198$
- $42$
- $40$
- $17$

## Explanation

The percentage means $17.5$ orders require a check for every $100$ orders. The proportion to apply to $240$ is therefore $\dfrac{17.5}{100}=0.175$. Multiplying by the total gives $240\cdot0.175=42$. Because the percentage is exact, the count is determined. A rounded percentage could correspond to several integer counts.
