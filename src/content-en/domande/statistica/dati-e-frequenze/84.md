Of 100 invitees, 78 answer a question and 26 answer yes. What is the smallest possible proportion of yes responses among all invitees, given the available information?

- $\dfrac{13}{50}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{12}{25}$
- $\dfrac{11}{50}$

## Explanation

Of the $100$ invitees, $100-78=22$ did not respond. The $26$ observed yes responses set the minimum. None of the nonrespondents must necessarily support yes. If all $22$ would give another answer, the total yes count would stay $26$. Thus the minimum proportion among invitees is $\dfrac{26}{100}=\dfrac{13}{50}$.
