Of 100 invitees, 80 answer a question and 20 of them answer yes. How many of the 20 nonrespondents would have to answer yes for the proportion of yes answers among all 100 invitees to equal the proportion among respondents?

- $0$
- $5$
- $20$
- $25$

## Explanation

Among respondents, the proportion of yes answers is $\dfrac{20}{80}=0.25$. For the proportion among all $100$ invitees to be $0.25$ as well, there must be $100\cdot0.25=25$ yes answers. Since $20$ yes answers are already recorded, the $20$ nonrespondents must contribute $25-20=5$. This is the same proportion as among respondents, because $\dfrac{5}{20}=0.25$. The proportion among respondents therefore equals the proportion among all invitees only if the nonrespondents split between yes and no in the same way as the respondents.
