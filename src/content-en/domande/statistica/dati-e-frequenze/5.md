Class $[10,14)$, measured in minutes, contains 15 of the 50 observations. Find its rectangle's height in $\mathrm{min}^{-1}$ in a relative frequency density histogram.

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{40}$

## Explanation

In a relative frequency density histogram, rectangle area represents the proportion of observations in the class. Here the proportion is $\dfrac{15}{50}=0.30$ and the width is $(14-10)\,\mathrm{min}=4\,\mathrm{min}$. Dividing area by width gives the height $\dfrac{0.30}{4}\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}=0.075\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$. Multiplying by the class width recovers the original proportion.
