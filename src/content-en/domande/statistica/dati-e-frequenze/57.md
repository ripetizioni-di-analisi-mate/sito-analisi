A relative frequency density histogram has two classes with widths 3 and 5 and heights $0.10$ and $0.14$, respectively. What is the relative frequency of the less frequent class?

- $\dfrac{7}{50}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$

## Explanation

The area of each rectangle is its relative frequency. Multiplying each width by the corresponding height gives $3\cdot0.10=0.30$ and $5\cdot0.14=0.70$. The first class therefore occurs less often, with proportion $0.30$. The value $0.10$ is instead its relative frequency per unit of width.
