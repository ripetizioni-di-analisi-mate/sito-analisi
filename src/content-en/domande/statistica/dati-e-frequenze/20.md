Which variable is a discrete count?

- Total length of a shift
- Number of interruptions during a shift
- Temperature recorded to the nearest tenth of a degree
- Mass of a parcel measured to the nearest gram

## Explanation

The number of interruptions counts distinct events. There may be zero, one, two, or more whole interruptions within an interval, so the count is **discrete**. A duration, mass, or length can take intermediate values between two readings. The precision of a recorded continuous measurement limits the recorded values without turning the physical quantity into a count.
