Of 120 items, 72 come from machine A and 48 from B. Of the items from A, $25\%$ need inspection, compared with $50\%$ of those from B. How many items do not need inspection?

- $54$
- $78$
- $84$
- $42$

## Explanation

For machine A, $72\cdot0.25=18$ items need inspection. For B, the count is $48\cdot0.50=24$. The two groups are disjoint, so $18+24=42$ items need inspection in total. Their complement contains $120-42=78$ items.
