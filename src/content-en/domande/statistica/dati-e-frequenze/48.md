A table groups times into two adjacent intervals, in increasing order. The first interval has midpoint $6$ and width $4$; the second has midpoint $11$. What must the second width be if their boundaries meet?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $10$

## Explanation

The first class runs from $4$ to $8$. The next one starts at $8$ and its midpoint is three units further along. Its width is twice that distance, namely $6$.
