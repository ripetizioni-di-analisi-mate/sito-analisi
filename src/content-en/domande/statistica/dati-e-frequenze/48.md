Class $[a,b)$ has left endpoint $a=14$ and midpoint 19. Find the right endpoint $b$.

- $5$
- $38$
- $24$
- $19$

## Explanation

The midpoint $19$ is equally distant from the two endpoints. Adding $19-14=5$ to the left endpoint $14$ gives the midpoint. Moving the same distance again to the right gives $b=19+5=24$. The class width is therefore $24-14=10$, twice the distance from the midpoint to an endpoint.
