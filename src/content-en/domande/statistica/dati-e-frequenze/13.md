Classes $[0,4)$, $[4,9)$, and $[9,15]$ contain 8, 12, and 10 observations, respectively. Find the largest possible number of observations strictly below 6 consistent with the table.

- $12$
- $30$
- $20$
- $8$

## Explanation

The $8$ observations in the first class are all below $6$. The last class starts at $9$, so all its observations are above the threshold. To obtain the maximum, all $12$ observations in the middle class can fall in $[4,6)$ without contradicting the table. This gives $8+12=20$. The table does not reveal the actual positions of these $12$ observations.
