Samples are repeatedly drawn from the same batch. Which quantity can change from one sample to the next?

- The total number of items in the batch
- The percentage of defective items observed in the sample
- The actual number of defective items in the entire batch
- The actual percentage of defective items in the entire batch

## Explanation

Each selection may include different items, so the percentage defective among selected items can change. It is a **sample statistic**. The total size of the batch, its number of defective items, and their percentage are defined for the entire population, which stays the same from one selection to the next.
