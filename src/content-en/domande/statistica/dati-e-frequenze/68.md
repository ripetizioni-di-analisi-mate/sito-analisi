Two column labels in a contingency table are accidentally exchanged, leaving every count in place. Which information can still be published under the original category names without any correction?

- Each column total under its own name
- Each row total
- The frequency of each named category pair
- The name of the most frequent column category in each row

## Explanation

Each row total adds every cell in that row and does not depend on the names assigned to the columns. Results that distinguish columns may now be attributed to the wrong categories.
