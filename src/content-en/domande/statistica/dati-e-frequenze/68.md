A table has rows $(12,8)$ and $(6,24)$. What is the marginal relative frequency of the second row?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{12}{25}$

## Explanation

Summing the two cells in the second row gives its marginal count $6+24=30$. The grand total also includes the $12+8=20$ units in the first row and is therefore $50$. The marginal relative frequency is $\dfrac{30}{50}=\dfrac35$. The denominator is the grand total because the question asks for the row's share of the entire table.
