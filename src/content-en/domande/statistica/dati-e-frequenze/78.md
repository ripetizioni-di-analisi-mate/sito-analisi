A column represents the same percentage within each of two rows of unequal sizes. Which statement follows?

- The column counts must agree in the two rows
- Each row total equals the column total
- The column's share of the grand total is that same percentage
- The percentage of the grand total doubles

## Explanation

Let $n_1$ and $n_2$ be the row totals and $p$ the common column proportion. The rows contribute $pn_1$ and $pn_2$ units, respectively. The overall proportion is therefore $\dfrac{pn_1+pn_2}{n_1+n_2}=p$. Different group sizes change the counts, but the proportion stays the same because it agrees in both groups.
