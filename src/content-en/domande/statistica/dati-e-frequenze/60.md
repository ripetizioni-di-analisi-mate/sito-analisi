A relative frequency density histogram contains exactly two classes with widths 4 and 6. The first has height $0.10$. What is the height of the second?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$

## Explanation

The first class accounts for a proportion $4\cdot0.10=0.40$. Since the classes cover the entire sample, the second must have area $1-0.40=0.60$. Dividing by its width $6$ gives height $\dfrac{0.60}{6}=0.10$. The heights are equal, but the wider second class contains a larger proportion.
