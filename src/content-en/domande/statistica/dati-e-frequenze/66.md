In a frequency density histogram based on counts, the sum of all class areas is 70 and one class has area 14. What is its relative frequency?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $5$

## Explanation

With absolute frequency density, each rectangle's area is the number of observations in its class. The sum of the areas therefore gives the total count, $70$. Dividing the specified area by that total gives the relative frequency $\dfrac{14}{70}=\dfrac15$. This represents $20\%$ of the observations.
