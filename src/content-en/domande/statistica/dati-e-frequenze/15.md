To describe delivery times for all parcels shipped in October, 200 parcels shipped that month are selected. What is the statistical unit?

- The mean delivery time
- The set of 200 parcels
- The day of the month
- An individual parcel shipped in October

## Explanation

The statistical unit is the individual entity to which a measurement refers. One delivery time is recorded per parcel, so the unit is the **individual parcel**. The $200$ selected parcels make up the sample. All parcels shipped in October form the population the study seeks to describe.
