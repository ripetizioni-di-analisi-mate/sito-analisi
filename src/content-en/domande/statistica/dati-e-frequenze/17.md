From a batch of 800 items, 50 are selected and the percentage defective among these 50 is calculated. Which quantity is a parameter of the batch?

- The proportion defective among all 800 items
- The proportion obtained after selecting a new set of 50 items
- The proportion defective among the 50 selected items
- The number 50 of selected items

## Explanation

A parameter describes the population specified in the question, here the entire batch of $800$ items. The percentage defective among all $800$ items is therefore a **parameter of the batch**. The percentage among the $50$ selected items is a statistic because it depends on the items in the sample and can change if a new sample is drawn. The number $50$ is the sample size, a choice made by whoever runs the survey, and it does not describe the batch.
