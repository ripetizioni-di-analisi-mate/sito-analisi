In a relative frequency density histogram of 40 measurements, a class of width 2 has height $0.175$. How many measurements belong to it?

- $8$
- $4$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

Multiplying width by height gives the area $2\cdot0.175=0.35$. In a relative frequency density histogram, this is the proportion in the class. Multiplying by the $40$ measurements in the sample gives $40\cdot0.35=14$. The result accounts for $35\%$ of the measurements.
