In a table of 80 observations, the first row has 30 units and the second column has 35. Their common cell contains 12 units. Find its joint relative frequency.

- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{12}{35}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{16}$

## Explanation

The $12$ units in the cell meet both conditions: they belong to the first row and the second column. The joint relative frequency compares that cell with all $80$ observations, giving $\dfrac{12}{80}=0.15$. The respective row and column totals, $30$ and $35$, would be used for conditional proportions within that row or column.
