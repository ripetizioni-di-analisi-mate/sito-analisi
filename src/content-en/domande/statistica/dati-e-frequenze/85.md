Two groups have the same percentage of yes responses among their respondents. In the first, $95\%$ of invitees respond, and in the second, $40\%$ respond. Which group has the wider range of overall proportions compatible with the missing answers?

- The second group
- The first group
- The two ranges have equal widths
- The width is zero in both groups

## Explanation

The lower bound assigns no yes answers to the nonrespondents; the upper bound assigns a yes to each. The difference between the proportions is therefore the fraction who did not respond. It is $1-0.95=0.05$ in the first group and $1-0.40=0.60$ in the second. The second range is wider even though the observed proportions among respondents agree.
