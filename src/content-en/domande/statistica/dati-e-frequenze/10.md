Given $F_n(12^-)=0.55$ and $F_n(12)=0.65$, find the proportion of observations strictly greater than 12.

- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{13}{20}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}$

## Explanation

The cumulative frequency $F_n(12)$ includes observations below $12$ and those equal to $12$. Its complement therefore consists exactly of values strictly above $12$ and has proportion $1-0.65=0.35$. The complement of $F_n(12^-)$ would be $0.45$ because it would also include the jump of size $0.10$ due to observations equal to $12$.
