One department has 12 of 40 items requiring rework. Another has 9 of 60. Find the overall relative frequency of items requiring rework.

- $\dfrac{9}{40}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{21}{100}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$

## Explanation

To pool the departments, add both the numbers requiring rework and the numbers inspected. This gives $12+9=21$ cases among $40+60=100$ items, so the overall proportion is $\dfrac{21}{100}=0.21$. Since the departments have different sizes, an unweighted mean of $0.30$ and $0.15$ would give the two departments equal weight.
