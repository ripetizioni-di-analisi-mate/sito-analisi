Classes $[0,5)$ and $[5,10]$ contain 9 and 11 observations. What can be said about the number at or below 5?

- It is between 0 and 9 inclusive
- It is between 9 and 20 inclusive
- It is exactly 9
- It is exactly 11

## Explanation

The $9$ observations in $[0,5)$ are all strictly below $5$. Of the observations in $[5,10]$, only those exactly equal to $5$ count, and their number can range from zero to $11$. The required count is therefore between $9$ and $9+11=20$. Both bounds are compatible with the table.
