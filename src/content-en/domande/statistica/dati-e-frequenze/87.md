Two departments have 30 and 70 employees. In the two departments combined, $40\%$ of the employees use the cafeteria, and in the first department the proportion is $0.60$. Find the proportion who use the cafeteria in the second department.

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{11}{35}$
- $\dfrac{11}{50}$

## Explanation

The combined departments have $30+70=100$ employees, so $100\cdot0.40=40$ of them use the cafeteria. In the first department, $30\cdot0.60=18$ employees use it. In the second department, therefore, $40-18=22$ of the $70$ employees use it, and the proportion is $\dfrac{22}{70}=\dfrac{11}{35}\approx0.31$. With the theorem on pooled groups the weights are $w_1=0.30$ and $w_2=0.70$, and indeed $0.30\cdot0.60+0.70\cdot\dfrac{11}{35}=0.18+0.22=0.40$. The value $\dfrac15$ would result from treating $0.40$ as the simple average of the two proportions, which gives the same weight to departments of different sizes.
