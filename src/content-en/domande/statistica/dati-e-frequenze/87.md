One group provides 30 responses to 40 invitations, and another provides 42 responses to 60 invitations. Find the overall response rate.

- $\dfrac{29}{40}$
- $\dfrac{18}{25}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$

## Explanation

First add the responses from the two groups to obtain $30+42=72$. The total number of invitations is $40+60=100$, so the rate is $\dfrac{72}{100}=\dfrac{18}{25}=0.72$. The second group's rate receives greater weight because that group received more invitations. A simple average of the two rates would weight the groups equally.
