A percentage falls from $40\%$ to $30\%$. What is the decrease in percentage points?

- $10$
- $25$
- $75$
- $70$

## Explanation

The decrease in percentage points is the difference between the two percentages. Subtracting $30$ from $40$ gives $10$ percentage points. A relative change would also use the starting percentage as its denominator. Here it would be $\dfrac{40-30}{40}=0.25$, or $25\%$ of the initial value.
