A university first divides its enrollment list by school, then selects students at random within each school. Which design is it using?

- Stratified sampling by school
- A census
- Voluntary selection with no predefined groups
- Convenience sampling

## Explanation

The schools are disjoint groups defined before selection. Since students are drawn randomly within each school, the design is **stratified sampling**. Each school contributes the units specified in the plan. Selecting several schools and observing only students in the selected schools would instead select at the group level.
