A university first divides its enrollment list by school, then selects students at random within each school. Which design is it using?

- Stratified sampling by school
- A census
- A simple random sample from the whole list
- Convenience sampling

## Explanation

The schools are disjoint groups defined before selection. Since students are drawn randomly within each school, the design is **stratified sampling**, with the schools as strata. In this way the number of students from each school is fixed before the draw. In a simple random sample from the whole list, by contrast, the number of students from each school would depend on the draw.
