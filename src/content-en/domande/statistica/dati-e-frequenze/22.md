Which statement remains true when two positive durations are converted from minutes to seconds?

- The numerical difference between the durations stays the same
- The ratio of the durations stays the same
- The longer duration becomes the shorter one
- The numerical value of each duration stays the same

## Explanation

If the durations are $a$ and $b$ minutes with $b>0$, their values in seconds are $60a$ and $60b$. Their ratio stays $\dfrac{60a}{60b}=\dfrac ab$ because both are multiplied by the same factor. Their numerical difference, however, is multiplied by $60$. For example, the ratio of $2$ to $4$ minutes is still $\dfrac12$ when using $120$ and $240$ seconds.
