In group A, 18 of 90 people have a characteristic. In group B, 14 of 40 have it. Which comparison is correct?

- The count is higher in B and the proportion is higher in A
- Both the count and the proportion are higher in B
- The count is higher in A and the proportion is higher in B
- Both the count and the proportion are higher in A

## Explanation

Group A has $18$ cases, four more than B. To compare how common the characteristic is, divide each count by its own group size. The result is $\dfrac{18}{90}=0.20$ in A and $\dfrac{14}{40}=0.35$ in B. Thus the absolute frequency is higher in A and the relative frequency higher in B.
