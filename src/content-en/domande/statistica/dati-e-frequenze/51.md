Some waiting times between 0 and 12 minutes are to be grouped into classes. The table should then give the exact number of waits strictly shorter than 8 minutes. Which choice of classes makes this possible?

- $[0,5)$, $[5,10)$, and $[10,12]$
- $[0,4)$, $[4,8)$, and $[8,12]$
- $[0,6)$ and $[6,12]$
- $[0,3)$, $[3,9)$, and $[9,12]$

## Explanation

The number of waits shorter than $8$ minutes can be read exactly when $8$ is a boundary between two classes. With the classes $[0,4)$, $[4,8)$, and $[8,12]$, every wait in the first two classes is shorter than $8$, and every wait in the last class is at least $8$. The required count is therefore the sum of the counts of the first two classes.

In the other tables the threshold $8$ falls inside a class, namely $[5,10)$, $[6,12]$, or $[3,9)$. The waits in that class can lie on either side of the threshold, so the table gives only a minimum and a maximum.
