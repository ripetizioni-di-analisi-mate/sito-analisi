Classes $[0,4)$, $[4,7)$, and $[7,12]$ contain 6, 11, and 8 observations. What is the smallest possible number of observations strictly below 5?

- $0$
- $6$
- $17$
- $11$

## Explanation

All $6$ observations in $[0,4)$ are below $5$. All the observations in $[7,12]$ exceed $5$. The distribution within $[4,7)$ is unknown, so all $11$ observations there could lie in $[5,7)$, where the strict inequality does not hold. This gives the minimum count, $6$.
