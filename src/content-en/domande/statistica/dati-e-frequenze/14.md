Of 80 people invited, 60 answer a question and 18 answer yes. Find the largest possible proportion of yes responses among all invitees consistent with the available responses.

- $\dfrac{19}{40}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{40}$

## Explanation

Of $80$ invitees, $80-60=20$ did not respond. There are already $18$ yes responses, and the other respondents gave a different answer. The maximum is reached by assigning a yes to every nonrespondent. The maximum proportion is therefore $\dfrac{18+20}{80}=0.475$. The denominator is the total number invited because the question concerns the entire group.
