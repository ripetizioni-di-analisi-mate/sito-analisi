For the observations $1,2,2,5,7,7,9,10$, find $F_8(6)$, where $F_8$ is the empirical distribution function.

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The empirical distribution function $F_8(6)$ gives the proportion of observations less than or equal to $6$. Count $1$, both occurrences of $2$, and $5$. Repeated observations count individually. Thus $4$ out of $8$ observations satisfy the condition, giving $F_8(6)=\dfrac48=\dfrac12$.
