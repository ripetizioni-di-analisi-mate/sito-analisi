A table has rows $(7,12,4)$ and $(9,5,13)$. Find the marginal count for the second column.

- $27$
- $22$
- $17$
- $16$

## Explanation

The second column's marginal count includes all observations in that category, regardless of row. Add the second entry of the first row, $12$, and the second entry of the other row, $5$. The count is $12+5=17$. Dividing it by the grand total would give the marginal relative frequency.
