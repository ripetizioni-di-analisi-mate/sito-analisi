The “closed” column of a table contains 30 routine and 20 complex cases. There are 75 routine cases in all, including those still open. Find the proportion closed among routine cases.

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Only routine cases are at issue, so the denominator is their total $75$, including those still open. The numerator is $30$, the count of cases both routine and closed. The proportion is $\dfrac{30}{75}=0.40$. Using $30+20=50$ as the denominator would give the proportion of routine cases among all closed cases.
