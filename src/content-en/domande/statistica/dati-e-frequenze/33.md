In a group of $n$ people, 24 represent exactly $30\%$. Find $n$.

- $80$
- $120$
- $72$
- $60$

## Explanation

The count $24$ is $30\%$ of the unknown total $n$. The product $0.30n$ must therefore equal $24$. Dividing by $0.30$ gives the total $n=\dfrac{24}{0.30}=80$. As a check, three-tenths of $80$ is exactly $24$ people.
