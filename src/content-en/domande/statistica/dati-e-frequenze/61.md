Two classes have relative frequencies $0.45$ and $0.55$ and respective widths of 3 and 11. Which rectangle is taller in a relative frequency density histogram?

- They have the same height
- The number of observations is needed for the comparison
- The second
- The first

## Explanation

To find the height, divide the proportion by the class width. The heights are $\dfrac{0.45}{3}=0.15$ for the first class and $\dfrac{0.55}{11}=0.05$ for the second. The first rectangle is therefore three times as tall. The second class is nevertheless more frequent because its wider base gives it a larger area.
