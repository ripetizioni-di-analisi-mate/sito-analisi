To measure satisfaction among all users of a service, a questionnaire is sent only to those who renewed their subscription. Users who did not renew therefore cannot enter the sample. Which change removes this flaw?

- Round percentages to two decimal places
- Send the questionnaire to users who did not renew as well
- Send more invitations to users who renewed
- Sort valid responses by their arrival time

## Explanation

The population of the survey includes both those who renewed and those who left the service. If the questionnaire reaches only those who renewed, the second group is excluded from the outset, and its answers, which may differ, do not appear in the data. Sending the questionnaire to users who did not renew as well gives this group a chance of being selected. Sending more invitations to users who renewed gathers more responses from the same group and leaves the way units are selected unchanged.
