To measure satisfaction among all users, a questionnaire is sent only to those who renewed the service. Which change directly addresses this coverage problem?

- Round percentages to two decimal places
- Include users who did not renew in the sampling frame
- Send more invitations to users who renewed
- Sort valid responses by their arrival time

## Explanation

The population includes both those who renewed and those who left the service. If the sampling frame includes only renewals, the second group is excluded from the outset. Including users who did not renew directly addresses this undercoverage. Sending more invitations to users who renewed would gather more responses from the same group.
