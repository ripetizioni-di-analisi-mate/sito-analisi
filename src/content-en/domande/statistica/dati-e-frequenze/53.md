Adjacent classes $[1,5)$ and $[5,11)$ are combined. Find the midpoint of the new class.

- $6$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The classes share boundary $5$ and together cover $[1,11)$. The combined endpoints are therefore $1$ and $11$. Averaging them gives the midpoint $\dfrac{1+11}{2}=6$. The shared boundary $5$ lies inside the new class and does not determine its midpoint.
