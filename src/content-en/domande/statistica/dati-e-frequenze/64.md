A class $[10,20)$ contains 16 observations, and the histogram shows a constant density within it. What can we deduce about the original data between 10 and 15?

- Their exact count is unknown
- There are exactly 8
- There are exactly 16
- There are exactly 4

## Explanation

The displayed density is constant because the rectangle spreads the class frequency across its entire width. It uses only the class endpoints and the count of $16$ observations. All observations could lie in the first half, or all in the second, with the same information. To determine how many are between $10$ and $15$, the original observations or an additional count are needed.
