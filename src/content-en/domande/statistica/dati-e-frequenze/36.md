In a sample of 60 units, the relative frequency of a category is $\dfrac{7}{20}$. How many units belong to its complement?

- $53$
- $40$
- $21$
- $39$

## Explanation

The category and its complement partition the $60$ units into two disjoint groups. Subtracting the category's proportion from one gives the complementary proportion $1-\dfrac7{20}=\dfrac{13}{20}$. Its count is $60\cdot\dfrac{13}{20}=39$. Subtracting the $60\cdot\dfrac7{20}=21$ units in the category from the total gives the same result.
