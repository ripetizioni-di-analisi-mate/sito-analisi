A class that includes its left endpoint has midpoint 31 and width 10. Find its left endpoint.

- $36$
- $26$
- $21$
- $31$

## Explanation

The midpoint is equidistant from the endpoints. Since the full width is $10$, each endpoint is $\dfrac{10}{2}=5$ from the midpoint $31$. The left endpoint is therefore $31-5=26$ and the right endpoint is $31+5=36$. Since the class is closed on the left, $26$ belongs to it.
