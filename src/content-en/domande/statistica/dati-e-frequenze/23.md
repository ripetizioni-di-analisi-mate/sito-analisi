Two temperatures are $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Which quantity keeps exactly the same numerical value when they are converted to kelvins?

- The difference between the two measurements
- The ratio of the measurements
- The value of the lower measurement
- The sum of the measurements

## Explanation

Converting from degrees Celsius to kelvins adds $273.15$ to each reading. The two temperatures become $283.15\,\mathrm{K}$ and $293.15\,\mathrm{K}$. On subtraction the constant cancels, so the numerical difference is still $10$. The ratio changes because adding the same amount to numerator and denominator generally changes their quotient.
