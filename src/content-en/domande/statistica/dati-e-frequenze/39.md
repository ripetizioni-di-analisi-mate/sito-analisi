In a sample of 50 measurements, the empirical distribution function jumps from $0.32$ just before 4 to $0.46$ at 4. How many measurements equal 4?

- $7$
- $23$
- $16$
- $14$

## Explanation

The value just before $4$ includes observations strictly less than $4$. At $4$ all measurements equal to the threshold are added. The jump size is $0.46-0.32=0.14$, their relative frequency. Multiplying it by the $50$ measurements gives the count $50\cdot0.14=7$.
