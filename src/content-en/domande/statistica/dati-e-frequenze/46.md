For a sample, $F_n(3)=F_n(8)$. What follows?

- No observation is equal to 3
- No observation belongs to $(3,8]$
- All observations are greater than 8
- At least one observation equals 8

## Explanation

The distribution function at $8$ includes all values up to $8$, whereas its value at $3$ includes all values up to $3$. Their difference is therefore the proportion in $(3,8]$. Since the two function values agree, that proportion is zero and the interval contains no observations. The value $3$ can still occur because both cumulative counts already include it.
