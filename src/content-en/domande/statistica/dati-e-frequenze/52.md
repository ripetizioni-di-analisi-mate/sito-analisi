Classes $[0,4)$ and $[4,10]$ contain 3 and 2 observations. Replace each observation by the midpoint of its class and find the mean of the five resulting values.

- $5$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The midpoints are $\dfrac{0+4}{2}=2$ and $\dfrac{4+10}{2}=7$. Substitution gives three values equal to $2$ and two equal to $7$, so the sum is $3\cdot2+2\cdot7=20$. Dividing by five observations gives $4$. Each midpoint receives its class count as its weight because the mean is taken over five replacement values.
