Of 40 people, 16 work the morning shift and 24 the evening shift. Ten morning workers travel by car. Find the proportion who travel by car among the morning workers.

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$

## Explanation

The word “among” identifies the reference group. Of the $16$ morning workers, $10$ travel by car. The required proportion is therefore $\dfrac{10}{16}=\dfrac58$. Dividing by $40$ would give the proportion of all workers who both work the morning shift and travel by car.
