The largest of 20 measurements is 15 and occurs four times. Find $F_{20}(15^-)$.

- $\dfrac{19}{20}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The symbol $15^-$ denotes the proportion reached just before including $15$. Since $15$ is the maximum, every other measurement is smaller. There are $20-4=16$ such measurements, so $F_{20}(15^-)=\dfrac{16}{20}=\dfrac45$. At $15$ the distribution function reaches $1$ because it now includes the four maximum values.
