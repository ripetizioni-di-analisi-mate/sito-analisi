Service quality is rated as poor, adequate, good, or excellent. Which operation uses only the information these ratings provide?

- Count responses rated good or better
- Measure the numerical distance between good and excellent
- Calculate the mean of the codes as a measured difference in quality
- Declare excellent to be twice as good as good

## Explanation

The ratings form an ordered scale: excellent ranks above good, and good above adequate. Thus the number rated at least good can be found by adding the frequencies of good and excellent. Only the ordering is given. Measuring distances between ratings or averaging them would require a rule giving those distances quantitative meaning.
