Two independent samples have $n_X=36$ and $n_Y=16$, and the population standard deviations are $\sigma_X=18$ and $\sigma_Y=16$. Calculate the standard error of $\bar X-\bar Y$.

- $5$
- $25$
- $7$
- $1$

## Explanation

Because the samples are independent, the variance of the difference is the sum of the variances of their means. The contributions are $\dfrac{18^2}{36}=9$ and $\dfrac{16^2}{16}=16$, giving variance $25$. The standard error is the standard deviation of the difference, so taking the square root gives $5$.
