After $30$ successes in $30$ independent Bernoulli trials, what Wald interval is obtained?

- $[1,1]$
- $[0,1]$
- A symmetric interval with a positive margin centered at $1$
- $[0,0]$

## Explanation

Thirty successes in thirty trials give observed proportion $\widehat p=1$. The Wald estimated standard error is $\sqrt{\dfrac{\widehat p(1-\widehat p)}{n}}$. Its factor $1-\widehat p$ is zero, so the margin vanishes for any finite quantile. Both endpoints equal $1$. This shows how the formula degenerates at the boundary even with thirty trials.
