A confidence interval for a mean is $[48,52]$. Which other quantity requires a different procedure because it has variability of its own in addition to uncertainty about the mean?

- The half-width of the reported interval
- The upper endpoint of the reported interval
- The value of a new individual observation
- The center of the reported interval

## Explanation

The interval concerns the population mean. A new observation also depends on variation among individual units around that mean. Even exact knowledge of the mean would leave this individual variability. A prediction interval accounts for both uncertainty in the center and variation in the future observation.
