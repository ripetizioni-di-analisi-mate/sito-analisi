An interval for a proportion is obtained by retaining the candidates $p$ for which $n(\widehat p-p)^2\leq z^2p(1-p)$. Which construction is this?

- The Wald interval, replacing $p$ with $\widehat p$ in the variance
- The exact binomial interval obtained from the tails
- A t interval for the mean of two samples
- The Wilson interval obtained by inverting the score test

## Explanation

The inequality is the acceptance criterion for the score test at each candidate $p$. The test is recognizable because the variance on the right is computed under the candidate value using $p(1-p)$. Collecting terms in $p$ gives a quadratic inequality. The candidates between its two roots form the Wilson interval.
