Two independent normal samples have unknown, possibly unequal population variances. Which interval uses the two contributions $\dfrac{S_X^2}{n_X}$ and $\dfrac{S_Y^2}{n_Y}$ and estimated degrees of freedom?

- The chi-square interval for a variance
- Welch's interval
- The normal interval for known variances
- The paired t interval

## Explanation

The unknown variances may differ, so each group estimates its own variance. Independence lets us add the contributions $\dfrac{S_X^2}{n_X}$ and $\dfrac{S_Y^2}{n_Y}$. The square root of their sum is the estimated standard error of the difference. Welch's interval uses a t quantile with degrees of freedom calculated from the same contributions and has approximate coverage.
