A two-sided normal procedure with known variance and quantile $z=2$ gives the interval $[18,26]$ for the mean. Recover the standard error of the sample mean.

- $4$
- $1$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

In a two-sided normal interval the margin of error is the distance from the center to either endpoint. The width is $26-18=8$, so the margin is $\dfrac82=4$. The margin equals the quantile $2$ times the standard error of the mean. Dividing by the quantile therefore gives $\dfrac42=2$.
