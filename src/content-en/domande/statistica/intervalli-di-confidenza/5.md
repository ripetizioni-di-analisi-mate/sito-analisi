In $100$ independent Bernoulli trials, $50$ successes are observed. Calculate the Wald interval using $z=2$.

- $[0.45,0.55]$
- $[0.40,0.60]$
- $[0.49,0.51]$
- $[0.30,0.70]$

## Explanation

The observed proportion is $\widehat p=\dfrac{50}{100}=0.50$. The Wald method substitutes this estimate into the formula for the variance of the sample proportion, giving estimated standard error $\sqrt{\dfrac{0.50\cdot0.50}{100}}=0.05$. With the given quantile the margin is $2\cdot0.05=0.10$. The interval is centered at $0.50$ and has endpoints $0.40$ and $0.60$.
