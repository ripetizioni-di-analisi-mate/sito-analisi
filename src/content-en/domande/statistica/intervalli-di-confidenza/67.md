Two measurements on the same unit have variances $4$ and $9$ and covariance $3$. What is the variance of their difference?

- $13$
- $7$
- $19$
- $10$

## Explanation

For the difference of measurements $X$ and $Y$, the variance is $\operatorname{Var}(X)+\operatorname{Var}(Y)-2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$. The minus sign comes from the product of the coefficients $1$ and $-1$. Substituting the values gives $4+9-2\cdot3=7$. Positive covariance reduces the variance of the difference below the sum of the two individual variances.
