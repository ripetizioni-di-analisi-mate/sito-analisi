A normal interval for a mean has center $20$ and margin $4$. After a correction, every measurement and the mean increase by $3$, while the known standard deviation stays the same. What is the new interval?

- $[19,27]$
- $[16,27]$
- $[19,24]$
- $[16,24]$

## Explanation

Adding $3$ to every observation also adds $3$ to the sample mean, moving the center to $20+3=23$. The standard deviation and sample size stay the same, so the margin is still $4$. Subtracting and adding the same margin at the new center gives endpoints $23-4=19$ and $23+4=27$.
