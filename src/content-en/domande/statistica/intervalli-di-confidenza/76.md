For a $99\,\%$ lower confidence bound on a normal mean with known standard deviation, which positive lower-tail quantile enters the formula?

- $z_{0.01}$
- $z_{0.995}$
- $z_{0.95}$
- $z_{0.99}$

## Explanation

A lower limit uses only one tail, leaving the full exclusion probability $1-0.99=0.01$ to the right of the positive pivot quantile. A value with probability $0.01$ to its right has cumulative probability $0.99$ to its left. The lower limit therefore subtracts the margin calculated using $z_{0.99}$.
