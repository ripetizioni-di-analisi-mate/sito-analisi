Two samples have the same size and mean. The known population standard deviations are $4$ for the first normal population and $10$ for the second. At the same confidence level, what is the ratio of the second interval's width to the first?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Each interval has width twice its margin, $2z\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$. Equal sample sizes and confidence levels make the factors $2$, $z$, and $\sqrt n$ cancel in the ratio. This leaves the ratio of the standard deviations, $\dfrac{10}{4}=\dfrac52$. The common mean determines only the centers of the intervals.
