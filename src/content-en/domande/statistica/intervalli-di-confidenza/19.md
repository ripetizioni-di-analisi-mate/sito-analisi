In $100$ repetitions of a procedure with exact coverage $95\,\%$, $92$ intervals contain the parameter. Which statement is correct?

- The parameter changes in eight repetitions
- An exact procedure must cover the parameter in exactly $95$ repetitions
- After these repetitions the theoretical level is $92\,\%$ by definition
- A count of $92$ is compatible with exact coverage $95\,\%$

## Explanation

The theoretical coverage is the probability of success on any one repetition, where success means that the interval contains the parameter. In a hundred repetitions the expected count is $100\cdot0.95=95$. Expectation is an average over repeated sequences, so a particular sequence may have count $92$. A difference from the expected count alone is consistent with the stated level.
