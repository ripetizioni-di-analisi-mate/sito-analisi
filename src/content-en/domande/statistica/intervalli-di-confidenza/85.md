A normal sample from a population with known variance has mean $10$ and standard error $2$. Using $z=2$, what is the distance from the upper endpoint of the interval to the candidate value $\mu=12$?

- $6$
- $0$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Under a normal model with known variance, the margin equals the quantile times the standard error. Here it is $2\cdot2=4$, giving upper limit $10+4=14$. The candidate $\mu=12$ is below the limit, and the required distance is $14-12=2$. The reference point for this distance is $14$. In this particular case the candidate is also $2$ from the center.
