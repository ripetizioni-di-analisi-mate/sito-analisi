A two-sided t interval is $[6,14]$ and the t quantile used is $2$. The sample size is $16$. What sample standard deviation was used?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $16$

## Explanation

The t interval is symmetric, so its margin is half the distance between the endpoints, $\dfrac{14-6}{2}=4$. With quantile $2$, the estimated standard error is $\dfrac42=2$. This error equals $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{16}}$. Multiplication by $\sqrt{16}=4$ recovers the sample standard deviation $s=8$.
