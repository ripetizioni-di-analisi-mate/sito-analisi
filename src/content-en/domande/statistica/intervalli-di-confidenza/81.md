For the same data and standard error, a procedure gives wider intervals at higher confidence levels. What follows for symmetric intervals centered at the same estimate?

- The higher-level interval is disjoint from the other interval
- The higher-level interval contains the lower-level interval
- The two intervals exchange lower endpoints
- The higher-level interval always has a larger center

## Explanation

For the same data the two intervals have the same center $c$. Writing them as $[c-h_1,c+h_1]$ and $[c-h_2,c+h_2]$, a higher confidence level gives $h_2\geq h_1$. The new lower endpoint is at most the old one and the new upper endpoint is at least the old one. The interval at the higher level contains the interval at the lower level.
