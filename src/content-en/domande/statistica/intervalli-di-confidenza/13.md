Plan a normal interval for a proportion with margin at most $0.10$ and critical value $z=2$. Use the conservative choice in the absence of pilot data. Find the minimum sample size.

- $100$
- $25$
- $50$
- $400$

## Explanation

Without pilot data, plan for the value of $p$ giving the largest margin. The product $p(1-p)$ attains its maximum at $p=\dfrac12$ and equals $\dfrac14$ there. Substituting into the constraint $z\sqrt{\dfrac{p(1-p)}{n}}\leq0.10$ gives $n\geq\dfrac{2^2\cdot\frac14}{0.10^2}=100$. This conservative choice applies to the margin in the normal approximation.
