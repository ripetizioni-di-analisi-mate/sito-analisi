Two samples have equal sizes and observed proportions $0.20$ and $0.50$. What is the ratio of their Wald standard errors, first to second?

- $1$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac{16}{25}$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

The two Wald standard errors are the square roots of the estimated variances $\dfrac{\widehat p(1-\widehat p)}{n}$. The common sample size cancels in the ratio of the variances, giving $\dfrac{0.20\cdot0.80}{0.50\cdot0.50}=\dfrac{16}{25}$. To compare standard errors, take the square root of this ratio to obtain $\dfrac45$.
