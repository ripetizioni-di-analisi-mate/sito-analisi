Five independent observations from a normal population are $4,6,6,6,8$. Calculate the unbiased sample variance used in the t interval.

- $2$
- $\dfrac85$
- $\dfrac25$
- $8$

## Explanation

The five values sum to $30$, giving sample mean $6$. Only $4$ and $8$ differ from the mean, with deviations $-2$ and $2$. The sum of squared deviations is $(-2)^2+2^2=8$. The unbiased sample variance uses divisor $5-1=4$ because the mean was estimated from the same sample. This gives $s^2=2$.
