For a Wilson interval with $n=36$, $K=18$, and $z=2$, which expression gives its half-width?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{40}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{40}}$
- $\dfrac1{20}$

## Explanation

With $K=18$ in $36$ trials, $\widehat p=\dfrac12$. In the expression for the Wilson half-width with denominator $n+z^2$, the quantity under the square root is $36\cdot\dfrac14+\dfrac{2^2}{4}=10$. Thus the half-width is $\dfrac{2\sqrt{10}}{40}$. Since $\sqrt{40}=2\sqrt{10}$, the same quantity can be written as $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{40}}$.
