The contribution $v_Y$ to the variance of a difference becomes negligible relative to $v_X>0$ while both sample sizes remain fixed. What limit do the Welch degrees of freedom approach?

- $n_Y-1$
- $n_X+n_Y-2$
- $n_X-1$
- $n_X+n_Y$

## Explanation

Dividing the numerator and denominator of Welch's formula by $v_X^2$ makes the numerator $(1+\dfrac{v_Y}{v_X})^2$, which tends to one. In the denominator the group Y contribution tends to zero while the group X contribution equals $\dfrac{1}{n_X-1}$. The ratio tends to $n_X-1$, the degrees of freedom of the group providing nearly all the estimated variance.
