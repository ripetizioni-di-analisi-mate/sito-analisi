The condition on sample size for a maximum margin is $n\geq48.01$. Which choice meets the margin with the smallest sample?

- Choose $49$ units
- Choose $47$ units
- Choose $50$ units
- Choose $48$ units

## Explanation

The sample size must be an integer satisfying $n\geq48.01$. A size of $48$ still violates the constraint, however small the shortfall. A size of $49$ satisfies it and every larger integer requires additional observations. Round up to obtain $49$ units.
