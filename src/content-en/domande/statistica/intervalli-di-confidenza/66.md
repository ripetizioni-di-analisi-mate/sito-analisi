Each of twenty units provides two paired measurements. The differences are normally distributed and independent across units. How many degrees of freedom does the t interval for their mean use?

- $39$
- $38$
- $20$
- $19$

## Explanation

Each unit supplies the difference between its own two measurements. The t interval is therefore based on twenty independent normal differences. Estimating their mean uses one degree of freedom in their sample variance, leaving $20-1=19$ degrees of freedom for the pivot.
