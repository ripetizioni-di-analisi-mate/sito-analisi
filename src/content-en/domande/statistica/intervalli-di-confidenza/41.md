A Wilson interval is calculated with $n=16$, $K=4$, and $z=2$. What is its center?

- $0.40$
- $0.30$
- $0.20$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The Wilson center is $\dfrac{K+\frac{z^2}{2}}{n+z^2}$. With $z=2$ we have $z^2=4$, so add $2$ to the count $K=4$ and $4$ to the sample size $16$. This gives $\dfrac6{20}=0.30$. The observed proportion is $\dfrac4{16}=0.25$, so the centers differ.
