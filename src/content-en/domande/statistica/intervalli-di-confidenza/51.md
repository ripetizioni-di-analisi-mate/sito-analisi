In a normal model, an interval for the variance has endpoints $\dfrac{D}{b}$ and $\dfrac{D}{a}$ with $0<a<b$. What is the ratio of the upper to the lower endpoint for $D>0$?

- $\dfrac ab$
- $\dfrac ba$
- $\dfrac{b-a}{D}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The upper endpoint is $\dfrac{D}{a}$ because $a$ is the smaller quantile. The lower endpoint is $\dfrac{D}{b}$. Dividing the first by the second multiplies $\dfrac{D}{a}$ by $\dfrac{b}{D}$. The observed numerator $D$ is positive and cancels, leaving $\dfrac ba$. This ratio exceeds one because $b>a$.
