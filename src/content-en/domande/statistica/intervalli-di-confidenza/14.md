From a normal population with known standard deviation $10$, $25$ independent observations yield a sample mean of $32$. Use $z_{0.95}=1.645$ to find the one-sided $95\,\%$ upper confidence bound.

- $33.645$
- $28.71$
- $35.92$
- $35.29$

## Explanation

For a one-sided upper limit, all excluded probability lies in one tail. Use the quantile of order $0.95$ specified in the question. The standard error of the mean is $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt{25}}=2$. Add the margin $1.645\cdot2=3.29$ to the observed mean $32$ to obtain the upper limit $35.29$.
