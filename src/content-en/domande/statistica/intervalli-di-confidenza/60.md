Welch's formula gives $\nu_W=13.6$. Only integer degrees of freedom are tabulated. Which integer gives the closest two-sided critical value that is at least as large as the critical value for $13.6$ degrees of freedom?

- $13$
- $15$
- $27$
- $14$

## Explanation

At a fixed two-sided confidence level, the positive t quantile decreases as the degrees of freedom increase. To obtain a quantile at least as large as the one with $13.6$ degrees of freedom, choose an integer no larger than $13.6$. The largest such integer is $13$, so it yields the closest quantile meeting the requirement.
