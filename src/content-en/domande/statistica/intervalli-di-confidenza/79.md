With observed mean $20$, standard error $1$, and one-sided quantile $1.645$, lower and upper bounds are calculated, each at level $95\,\%$. Ignoring rounding of the quantile, what is the two-sided coverage of the interval between them?

- $90\,\%$
- $97.5\,\%$
- $95\,\%$
- $99\,\%$

## Explanation

Each one-sided limit excludes probability approximately $0.05$ in one tail. To lie between both limits, the pivot must avoid both tails. They are disjoint, with combined probability approximately $0.10$. The central area and hence the two-sided coverage are approximately $1-0.10=0.90$.
