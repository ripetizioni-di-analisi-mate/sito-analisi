Four independent times are observed from a strongly skewed population. The sample mean and standard deviation are available. Which statement about exact coverage of the t interval is correct?

- Independence of the observations alone guarantees a t distribution
- Using the sample standard deviation guarantees a t distribution
- Positive times guarantee a t distribution
- An assumption yielding a t-distributed pivot is needed, such as normality of the observations

## Explanation

The sample mean and standard deviation suffice to calculate endpoints. Establishing exact coverage also requires the distribution of the pivot. For a normal sample, the distributions and independence of the mean and sample variance give the t distribution. Independence alone for four strongly skewed observations does not give these properties.
