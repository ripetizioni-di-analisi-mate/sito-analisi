A procedure for $\theta$ has coverage $0.94$ at $\theta=2$ and at least $0.95$ for every other admissible value. What is the largest confidence level guaranteed for every $\theta$?

- $94\,\%$
- $100\,\%$
- $94.5\,\%$
- $95\,\%$

## Explanation

The guaranteed level must hold at every admissible parameter value. At $\theta=2$ the coverage is exactly $0.94$, so a higher level would fail there. Elsewhere the coverage is at least $0.95$ and hence at least $0.94$. The highest level satisfying both conditions is $94\,\%$.
