At a fixed confidence level, the same margin for a normal mean must be met both when $\sigma=4$ and when $\sigma=6$. Which standard deviation should be used to plan a sample size that covers both cases?

- $\sqrt{24}$
- $6$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

With level and sample size fixed, the margin $z\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$ increases with $\sigma$. Of the two standard deviations, $6$ therefore gives the larger margin. Calculating the sample size using $\sigma=6$ also meets the requirement at $\sigma=4$, whose margin at the same size is two-thirds of that obtained with $6$.
