The before measurements for three subjects are $(5,8,10)$ and the after measurements in the same order are $(6,7,13)$. What is the mean of the after-minus-before differences?

- $-1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Compute the after-minus-before difference using the two measurements on the same subject. In the stated order the differences are $6-5=1$, $7-8=-1$, and $13-10=3$. The second subject contributes a negative value. The sum is $3$, which after division by the number of subjects gives the mean $1$.
