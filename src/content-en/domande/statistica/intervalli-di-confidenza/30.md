Two normal samples of the same size have sample standard deviations $3$ and $6$, respectively, both calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction. At the same confidence level, what is the ratio of the second t margin to the first?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

The t margin of error is $t\dfrac{s}{\sqrt n}$. Both samples have the same size and hence the same degrees of freedom. Equal confidence levels give the same quantile. The quantile and $\sqrt n$ cancel in the ratio, so the second margin is $\dfrac63=2$ times the first.
