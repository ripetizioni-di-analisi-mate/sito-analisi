For a fixed sample, the two-sided critical value increases from $1.645$ to $1.96$. If the standard error is $2$, how much does the interval width increase?

- $0.315$
- $1.26$
- $0.63$
- $3.92$

## Explanation

The margin equals the quantile times the standard error. Holding the standard error fixed at $2$, the increase in the margin is $(1.96-1.645)\cdot2=0.63$. The new interval extends by that amount on each side, so its total width increases by $2\cdot0.63=1.26$.
