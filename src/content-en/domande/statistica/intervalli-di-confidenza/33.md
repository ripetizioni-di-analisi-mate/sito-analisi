A t procedure with $9$ degrees of freedom and a normal procedure both have level $95\,\%$ and the same numerical standard error. The critical values are $2.262$ and $1.96$. What is the difference between the margins if the standard error is $5$?

- $0.302$
- $3.02$
- $11.31$
- $1.51$

## Explanation

Each margin equals its own quantile times the standard error. The latter is $5$ for both procedures, so the difference is $5(2.262-1.96)$. The quantiles differ by $0.302$, making the t margin larger than the normal margin by $1.51$. The difference in total widths would be twice as large.
