In Welch’s formula let $v_i=s_i^2/n_i$ and $\nu=(v_1+v_2)^2/[v_1^2/(n_1-1)+v_2^2/(n_2-1)]$. With fixed, distinct sample sizes $n_1,n_2>2$ and $v_1>0$, what does $\nu$ approach as $v_2$ becomes negligible relative to $v_1$?

- $n_1-1$
- $n_2-1$ for every choice of sample sizes
- $n_1+n_2-2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Divide numerator and denominator by $v_1^2$. All terms containing $v_2/v_1$ vanish, leaving $1/[1/(n_1-1)]=n_1-1$, the degrees of freedom of the group dominating the uncertainty.

[Theory behind this method](/en/statistica/intervalli-di-confidenza/teoria#welch).
