Two independent groups give $v_X=2$, $v_Y=1$, $n_X=5$, and $n_Y=3$. Calculate the Welch degrees of freedom.

- $6$
- $4$
- $8$
- $3$

## Explanation

The numerator in Welch's formula is the square of $v_X+v_Y$, here equal to $(2+1)^2=9$. In the denominator each squared contribution is divided by its own group's degrees of freedom, giving $\dfrac{2^2}{5-1}+\dfrac{1^2}{3-1}=1+\dfrac12=\dfrac32$. The ratio is $\dfrac{9}{\frac32}=6$.
