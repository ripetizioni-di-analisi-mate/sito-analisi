A Wald interval centered at $0.40$ has upper endpoint $0.47$. What is its lower endpoint?

- $0.33$
- $0.35$
- $0.53$
- $0.40$

## Explanation

The Wald interval is symmetric about the observed proportion, here $0.40$. The upper endpoint is $0.47-0.40=0.07$ from the center. By symmetry, the lower endpoint is the same distance to its left. Subtracting $0.07$ from $0.40$ gives $0.33$. Subtracting the full width from the center would count the margin twice.
