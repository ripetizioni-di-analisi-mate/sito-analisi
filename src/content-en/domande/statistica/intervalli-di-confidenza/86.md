For a chi-square pivot, two quantiles leave probability $0.02$ to the left and $0.08$ to the right. What coverage is obtained by inverting this region?

- $96\,\%$
- $92\,\%$
- $90\,\%$
- $98\,\%$

## Explanation

The pivot is between the quantiles when it lies in neither tail. The tails are disjoint and have total probability $0.02+0.08=0.10$, leaving central probability $0.90$. Inverting rewrites the same event as the parameter belonging to the interval, so it preserves the $90\,\%$ coverage.
