A normal population has known standard deviation and the observed mean is $18$. The standard error is $2$ and the given one-sided quantile is $1.645$. What is the lower bound?

- $21.29$
- $16.355$
- $14.08$
- $14.71$

## Explanation

The population is normal and its standard deviation known, so use the standard normal quantile given in the question. The one-sided margin is $1.645\cdot2=3.29$. Subtract it from the observed mean for a lower limit, obtaining $18-3.29=14.71$. The confidence set includes all values at or above that threshold.
