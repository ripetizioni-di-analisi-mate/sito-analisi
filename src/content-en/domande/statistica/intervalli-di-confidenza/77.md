A manufacturer wants to guarantee a minimum mean battery life. For $16$ batteries, with normal and independent lifetimes and unknown standard deviation, $\bar x=18\,\mathrm{h}$ and $s=8\,\mathrm{h}$ are observed. The values $t_{15,0.95}\approx1.753$ and $t_{15,0.975}\approx2.131$ are given. What is the $95\,\%$ lower bound for the mean lifetime?

- $13.738\,\mathrm{h}$
- $21.506\,\mathrm{h}$
- $14.710\,\mathrm{h}$
- $14.494\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

The standard deviation is unknown, so Student's t with $\nu=16-1=15$ degrees of freedom is used. The estimated standard error is $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt n}=\dfrac{8}{\sqrt{16}}=2\,\mathrm{h}$. The bound is one-sided, so the entire exclusion probability $0.05$ lies on one side and the quantile has order $0.95$. The lower bound is $18-1.753\cdot2=18-3.506=14.494\,\mathrm{h}$, and the confidence set is $[14.494,+\infty)$. The two-sided quantile $2.131$ would give $13.738$, the normal quantile $1.645$ would give $14.710$, and $21.506$ is the upper bound.
