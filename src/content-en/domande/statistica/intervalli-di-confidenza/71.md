A lab can take $25$ independent measurements with known standard deviation $10$, or $16$ independent measurements with known standard deviation $8$. The same normal quantile is used for the same mean. Which design gives the narrower theoretical interval?

- The first, because it has more observations
- The second, because it has fewer observations
- The observed mean is needed to compare the widths
- Neither: the two intervals have the same width

## Explanation

The width is $2z\sigma/\sqrt n$. The standard errors are $10/5=2$ and $8/4=2$. The second design’s higher precision per measurement exactly offsets its smaller sample size.
