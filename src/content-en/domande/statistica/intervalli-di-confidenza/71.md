Given $z=2$ and $\sigma=6$, a normal interval for the mean is planned with total width at most $4$. What is the minimum sample size?

- $6$
- $9$
- $16$
- $36$

## Explanation

A width of at most $4$ means a margin of at most $2$, since the width comprises two half-widths. With $z=2$ and $\sigma=6$ the normal margin is $\dfrac{12}{\sqrt n}$. Requiring it to be at most $2$ gives $\sqrt n\geq6$ and hence $n\geq36$. At $n=36$ the margin is exactly $2$.
