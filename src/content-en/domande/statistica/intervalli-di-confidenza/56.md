Two independent samples have $n_X=9$, $n_Y=16$, $\sigma_X=6$, and $\sigma_Y=4$. Which sample contributes more to the variance of the difference?

- The first, with contribution $4$ versus $1$
- The second, with contribution $4$ versus $1$
- Both contribute $1$
- The first, with contribution $6$ versus $4$

## Explanation

Divide each population variance by its own sample size to compare contributions. The first group contributes $\dfrac{6^2}{9}=4$ and the second $\dfrac{4^2}{16}=1$. The first group contributes more because its ratio of population variance to number of observations is larger.
