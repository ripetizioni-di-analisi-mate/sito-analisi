A sample has $n=50$ and two successes. Which feature suggests that the Wald interval may give poor coverage?

- The observed proportion is exactly one-half
- The success and failure counts both equal $25$
- The success count is small even though the total is $50$
- The number of trials must equal the t degrees of freedom

## Explanation

The Wald method uses a normal approximation for the binomial sample proportion and estimates its variance by substituting the observed proportion. The counts are $2$ successes and $48$ failures. The success count is very small, so the sample proportion may have a skewed distribution concentrated near zero. The total $50$ alone does not ensure a good approximation.
