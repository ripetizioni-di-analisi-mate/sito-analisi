Given pilot proportion $p_*=0.20$ and quantile $z=2$, what is the minimum planned sample size for a normal margin of at most $0.10$?

- $64$
- $80$
- $16$
- $100$

## Explanation

When planning for a proportion, substitute the pilot value $p_*=0.20$ into the normal formula. The anticipated variance factor is $p_*(1-p_*)=0.20\cdot0.80=0.16$. The margin requirement gives $n\geq\dfrac{2^2\cdot0.16}{0.10^2}=64$. This threshold is already an integer, so the minimum admissible size is $64$.
