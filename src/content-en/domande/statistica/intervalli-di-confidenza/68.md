Pressure is measured in the same subjects before and after an experimental condition. What observation constitutes one row in the sample used for the paired interval?

- A mean calculated by pooling all subjects
- A randomly chosen measurement from each group
- The difference between the two measurements on the same subject
- The difference between measurements on two distinct subjects

## Explanation

Before and after measurements on the same subject form a pair. A paired interval uses individual changes, so each sample entry is the difference between the two measurements on that subject. This preserves the pairing and allows the variability in changes across subjects to be estimated.
