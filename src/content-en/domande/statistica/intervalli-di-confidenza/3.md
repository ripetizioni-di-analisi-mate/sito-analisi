A sample of $16$ independent observations from a normal population has mean $30$ and sample standard deviation $4$, calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction. Use $t_{15,0.975}=2.131$ to calculate the two-sided $95\,\%$ interval.

- $[25.738,34.262]$
- $[28.040,31.960]$
- $[27.869,32.131]$
- $[28.9345,31.0655]$

## Explanation

The population standard deviation is unknown and the observations are independent and normal. Using the sample standard deviation gives a pivot with a t distribution with $16-1=15$ degrees of freedom. The estimated standard error is $\dfrac4{\sqrt{16}}=1$, so the margin is $2.131$. The endpoints are $30-2.131=27.869$ and $30+2.131=32.131$.
