A lower t bound for a mean is based on $n=10$ independent normal measurements with unknown variance at confidence level $90\,\%$. Which degrees of freedom and quantile order should be used?

- $9$ and $0.90$
- $10$ and $0.95$
- $10$ and $0.90$
- $9$ and $0.95$

## Explanation

The unknown standard deviation is estimated from the normal sample, so the pivot for the mean has a t distribution with $10-1=9$ degrees of freedom. The lower limit is one-sided. The level $0.90$ places all of $0.10$ in one tail, so the positive quantile has order $0.90$. The requested values are $9$ and $0.90$.
