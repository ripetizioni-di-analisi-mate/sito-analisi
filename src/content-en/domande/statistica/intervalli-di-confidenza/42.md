All $12$ independent Bernoulli trials are successes. Using $z=2$, what is the lower endpoint of the Wilson interval?

- $1$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

With successes on every trial, $\widehat p=1$ is observed. Inverting the score test requires $12(1-p)^2\leq4p(1-p)$. For $p<1$, division by $1-p>0$ gives $12(1-p)\leq4p$ and hence $p\geq\dfrac{12}{16}=\dfrac34$. The value $p=1$ directly satisfies the original inequality, so the resulting set is $[\dfrac34,1]$.
