With $12$ successes in $12$ trials, a Wilson interval for the success probability is $[0.75,1]$. The observations are recoded with $1$ meaning failure. Which interval from the corresponding procedure should be reported for the failure probability?

- $[0.25,1]$
- $[0.75,1]$
- $[-0.25,0]$
- $[0,0.25]$

## Explanation

Failure probability is $q=1-p$. This transformation reverses the order of the endpoints: $1-1=0$ and $1-0.75=0.25$. The interval is reflected about $1/2$.
