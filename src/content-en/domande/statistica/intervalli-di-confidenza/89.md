In the independent-differences model, each measurement has variance $9$ and the within-pair covariance is $5$. With $8$ pairs, what is the theoretical standard error of the mean difference?

- $\sqrt2$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\sqrt8$
- $1$

## Explanation

Within each pair the difference has variance $9+9-2\cdot5=8$ because twice the covariance is subtracted. The eight differences are independent, so their mean has variance $\dfrac88=1$. Its theoretical standard error is the square root of that variance, $1$. Include the within-pair covariance before dividing by the sample size.
