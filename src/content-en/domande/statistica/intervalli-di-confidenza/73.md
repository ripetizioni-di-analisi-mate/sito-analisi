Two studies use the same target margin and standard deviation. The normal critical values for planning are $1.5$ and $2$. Before rounding, what is the ratio of the second sample size to the first?

- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac{16}{9}$

## Explanation

The theoretical sample size for a specified margin is $\left(\dfrac{z\sigma}{E}\right)^2$. The two studies have the same $\sigma$ and $E$, so only the squared quantiles remain in the ratio. The second study uses $z=2$ and the first uses $z=1.5=\dfrac32$, giving ratio $\dfrac{2^2}{(\frac32)^2}=\dfrac{16}{9}$.
