A normal sample of $13$ measurements gives $s^2=4$. What is the numerator of the chi-square pivot for $\sigma^2$?

- $48$
- $12$
- $4$
- $52$

## Explanation

The chi-square pivot divides the sum of squared deviations from the sample mean by $\sigma^2$. The unbiased sample variance $s^2$ is this sum divided by $n-1$. Recover the numerator by multiplying $s^2=4$ by $13-1=12$. Its observed value is $12\cdot4=48$.
