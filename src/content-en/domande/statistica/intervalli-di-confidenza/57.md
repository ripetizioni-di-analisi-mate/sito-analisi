Two independent normal samples have unknown standard deviations. The researcher explicitly assumes equal population variances. Which t procedure is exact under these assumptions?

- The paired t procedure applied to the two groups after sorting them by size
- The normal procedure with $\sigma_X=\sigma_Y=1$
- Welch's procedure with an exact t distribution in every configuration
- The pooled-variance procedure with $n_X+n_Y-2$ degrees of freedom

## Explanation

Assuming a common variance allows both groups' sums of squared deviations to estimate that variance. With independent normal samples, the normalized sums combine into a chi-square variable. The degrees of freedom add to $(n_X-1)+(n_Y-1)=n_X+n_Y-2$. Under these assumptions the pooled-variance t pivot is exact.
