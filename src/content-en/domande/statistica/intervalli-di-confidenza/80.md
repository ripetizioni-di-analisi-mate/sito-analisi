A study asks whether the mean can be shown to exceed a minimum threshold. Which one-sided construction directly addresses this question?

- A lower bound for the standard deviation
- A lower bound for the mean
- An interval for a single future observation
- An upper bound for the mean

## Explanation

To conclude that the mean exceeds a minimum threshold, every value in the confidence set must exceed it. A lower limit gives a set of the form $[L,+\infty)$. If $L$ exceeds the required threshold, all larger values do too. This is why the comparison uses the lower limit.
