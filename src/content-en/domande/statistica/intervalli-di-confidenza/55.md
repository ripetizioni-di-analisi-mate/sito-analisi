In a comparison of independent means with known variances, the contributions to the variance of the difference are $4$ and $1$. Only the first sample is quadrupled. What is the new standard error?

- $\sqrt5$
- $\sqrt2$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt5}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

Each variance contribution equals the population variance divided by its sample size. Quadrupling the first sample size reduces its contribution by a factor of four, from $4$ to $1$. The second stays at $1$. Independence allows the contributions to be added. The standard error is the square root of the new total, $\sqrt2$.
