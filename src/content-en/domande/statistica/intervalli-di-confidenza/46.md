With $n=8$ and three successes, at least $95\,\%$ coverage is required for every success probability. Which procedure guarantees this under the binomial model?

- The Wilson interval with a normal quantile
- The Wald interval with a normal quantile
- A t interval for the mean of the indicators
- The exact equal-tailed binomial interval

## Explanation

The equal-tailed exact binomial interval uses the binomial distribution of the count $K$ directly, with no normal approximation. With $k=3$ successes, the upper endpoint is the value of $p$ for which $P_p(K\leq3)=0.025$ and the lower endpoint is the value of $p$ for which $P_p(K\geq3)=0.025$. Since $K$ takes only integer values, the coverage cannot equal $0.95$ exactly for every $p$; the construction guarantees that it is at least $0.95$ for every $p$. The Wald and Wilson intervals use the normal quantile and have only approximate coverage, and the t interval for the indicators has no exact distribution with binary data.
