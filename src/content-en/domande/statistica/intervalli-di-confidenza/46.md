With $n=8$ and three successes, at least $95\,\%$ coverage is required for every success probability. Which procedure guarantees this under the binomial model?

- The Wilson interval with a normal quantile
- The Wald interval with a normal quantile
- A t interval for the mean of the indicators
- The exact equal-tailed binomial interval

## Explanation

For eight trials we can use the binomial distribution of the count directly. For each candidate $p$, the equal-tailed exact interval inverts the acceptance criteria based on binomial probabilities in the two tails. Discreteness allows coverage above the nominal level, and the construction guarantees coverage of at least $95\,\%$ for every $p$.
