The interval for the difference $\mu_X-\mu_Y$ is $[-3,8]$. What interval does the same symmetric procedure give for $\mu_Y-\mu_X$?

- $[-8,3]$
- $[-8,-3]$
- $[3,8]$
- $[-3,8]$

## Explanation

Going from $\mu_X-\mu_Y$ to $\mu_Y-\mu_X$ changes the sign of every candidate value. The symmetric procedure retains the same half-width and changes the sign of the center. Multiplying the endpoints by $-1$ reverses their order. The negative of $8$ is the new lower endpoint and the negative of $-3$ is the new upper endpoint, giving $[-8,3]$.
