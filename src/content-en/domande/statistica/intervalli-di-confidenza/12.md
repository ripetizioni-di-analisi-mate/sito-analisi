Given known standard deviation $\sigma=7$, required margin $E=3$, and two-sided critical value $z=2$, calculate the minimum sample size for estimating the mean.

- $49$
- $22$
- $5$
- $21$

## Explanation

The margin of error for the normal interval is $2\cdot\dfrac7{\sqrt n}=\dfrac{14}{\sqrt n}$. Requiring a margin of at most $3$ means $\sqrt n\geq\dfrac{14}{3}$. Both sides are positive, so squaring gives $n\geq\dfrac{196}{9}\approx21.78$. The minimum sample size is $22$ because with $21$ measurements the margin would still exceed the requirement.
