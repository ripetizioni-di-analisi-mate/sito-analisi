A report gives only the Wald interval $[0.30,0.50]$ for a proportion, computed with $z=2$. How many independent Bernoulli trials were observed?

- $24$
- $384$
- $96$
- $48$

## Explanation

The Wald interval is centered at the observed proportion, so $\widehat p=\dfrac{0.30+0.50}{2}=0.40$, and the margin is $\dfrac{0.50-0.30}{2}=0.10$. Dividing the margin by $z=2$ gives the estimated standard error $0.05$. For the Wald interval, $\sqrt{\dfrac{\widehat p(1-\widehat p)}{n}}=0.05$, that is, $\dfrac{0.40\cdot0.60}{n}=0.0025$. Hence $n=\dfrac{0.24}{0.0025}=96$.
