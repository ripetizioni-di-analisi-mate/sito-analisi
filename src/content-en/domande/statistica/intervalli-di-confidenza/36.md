In $400$ independent Bernoulli trials, $200$ successes are observed. Using $z=2$, what is the full width of the Wald interval?

- $0.20$
- $0.05$
- $0.025$
- $0.10$

## Explanation

The observed proportion is $\dfrac{200}{400}=0.50$. The Wald estimate of the variance of the sample proportion is $\dfrac{0.50(1-0.50)}{400}$. Its square root is $0.025$, so the margin on each side is $2\cdot0.025=0.05$. The question asks for the total width, which includes both margins and equals $0.10$.
