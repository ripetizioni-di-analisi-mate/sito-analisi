Two independent samples from normal populations have $n_X=n_Y=9$ and $s_X^2=s_Y^2=18$. Calculate the Welch degrees of freedom.

- $18$
- $17$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

The contributions to the estimated variance of the difference are $v_X=v_Y=\dfrac{18}{9}=2$. The numerator in Welch's degrees-of-freedom formula is the square of their sum, $16$. In the denominator, divide each squared contribution by its group's degrees of freedom to obtain $\dfrac48+\dfrac48=1$. The resulting degrees of freedom are $16$.
