Two independent normal samples have means $11$ and $7$ and a standard error of the difference of $2$. Given known variances and $z=2$, what is the interval for the first population mean minus the second?

- $[0,8]$
- $[2,6]$
- $[-8,0]$
- $[-2,10]$

## Explanation

Estimate the first mean minus the second with the observed difference $11-7=4$. The normal samples are independent and their variances are known, so use the given normal quantile. The standard error of the difference is already given, yielding margin $2\cdot2=4$. Subtracting and adding this margin at $4$ gives $[0,8]$.
