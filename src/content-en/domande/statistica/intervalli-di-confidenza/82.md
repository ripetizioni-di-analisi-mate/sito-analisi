A simulation with parameter $\theta=10$ yields the four intervals $[8,10]$, $[9,12]$, $[11,13]$, and $[10,11]$. What fraction of the intervals cover the parameter?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $1$

## Explanation

Count the intervals containing $10$ to find the observed coverage. The first and fourth include it as an endpoint because they are closed intervals. The second includes it in its interior, while the third starts at $11$ and excludes it. Three of the four intervals cover, giving the observed fraction $\dfrac34$.
