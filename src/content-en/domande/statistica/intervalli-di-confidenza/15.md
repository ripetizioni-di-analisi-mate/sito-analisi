A laboratory applies the same procedure, with exact coverage $92\,\%$, to $250$ samples. What is the expected number of intervals that contain the parameter?

- $20$
- $230$
- $250$
- $125$

## Explanation

For each interval, define an indicator equal to one if the interval contains the parameter and zero otherwise. Exact coverage means each indicator has expectation $0.92$. The number of intervals covering the parameter is the sum of the indicators. Linearity of expectation gives $250\cdot0.92=230$, although the observed count may differ.
