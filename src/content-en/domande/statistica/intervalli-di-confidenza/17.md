A sample gives the interval $[5,7]$. In a validation experiment the true parameter is known to equal $7.2$. What is the coverage indicator for this interval?

- $1$
- $0.2$
- $0.95$
- $0$

## Explanation

Evaluate the indicator by checking whether the realized interval contains the true parameter. Both $5\leq7.2$ and $7.2\leq7$ are required. The first condition holds and the second fails. This particular interval therefore excludes the parameter and its coverage indicator is zero. The confidence level of the procedure refers to all possible repetitions.
