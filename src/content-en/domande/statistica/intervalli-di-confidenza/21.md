Let $Z$ be standard normal. If $P(Z\leq1.282)\approx0.90$, what is $P(-1.282\leq Z\leq1.282)$?

- Approximately $0.98$
- Approximately $0.90$
- Approximately $0.10$
- Approximately $0.80$

## Explanation

The standard normal distribution is symmetric about zero. Since the probability to the left of $1.282$ is approximately $0.90$, the right tail has probability approximately $0.10$. The tail to the left of $-1.282$ has the same probability. Removing both tails leaves a central area of approximately $1-2\cdot0.10=0.80$.
