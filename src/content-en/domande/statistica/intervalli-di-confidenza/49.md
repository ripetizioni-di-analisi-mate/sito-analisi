The chi-square interval for a variance is $[3,12]$. The sample measurements and the population are expressed in a new unit that multiplies each value by $2$. What is the new variance interval?

- $[9,144]$
- $[3,12]$
- $[6,24]$
- $[12,48]$

## Explanation

Multiplying every observation by $2$ also multiplies the sample mean and each deviation from it by $2$. The squared deviations and the variance are multiplied by $2^2=4$. The chi-square quantiles depend only on the level and sample size, so they stay the same. Multiplying both endpoints by $4$ gives $[12,48]$.
