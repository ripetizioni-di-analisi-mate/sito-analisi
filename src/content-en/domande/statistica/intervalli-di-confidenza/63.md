Two independent groups give $n_X=8$, $n_Y=18$, $s_X^2=16$, and $s_Y^2=36$. What is $v_X+v_Y$ in Welch's interval?

- $\dfrac{52}{18}$
- $4$
- $52$
- $2$

## Explanation

Welch's formula divides each unbiased sample variance by the corresponding sample size, giving $v_X=\dfrac{16}{8}=2$ and $v_Y=\dfrac{36}{18}=2$. Independence allows the contributions to be added, giving $v_X+v_Y=4$. The square root, $2$, would be the standard error. The question asks for the sum before taking its square root.
