For a normal sample of $10$ observations, the unknown standard deviation is estimated from the data. Which quantile is needed for a two-sided $90\,\%$ interval for the mean?

- $t_{9,0.90}$
- $t_{9,0.95}$
- $z_{0.95}$
- $t_{10,0.90}$

## Explanation

The standard deviation is estimated from the same normal sample, so use a t distribution with $10-1=9$ degrees of freedom. A two-sided level of $90\,\%$ leaves probability $0.10$ outside the interval. With equal tails, $0.05$ remains to the right of the positive quantile. The required quantile is $t_{9,0.95}$.
