A confidence interval for the mean after-minus-before difference is $[-1,4]$. Which statement correctly describes whether a mean change of zero is compatible with this interval?

- The two population means are certainly equal
- Zero lies in the interval for the mean difference
- Every observed difference lies between $-1$ and $4$
- Every individual change is positive

## Explanation

The parameter is the mean after-minus-before difference. Zero therefore represents no average change in the specified direction. Since $-1\leq0\leq4$, zero lies in the interval and is compatible with the procedure. Some positive and negative values are included as well, leaving both signs possible.
