A confidence interval for the variance of a normal population is $[4,25]\,\mathrm{mm}^2$. Find the corresponding interval for the standard deviation.

- $[2,5]\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $[4,25]\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $[16,625]\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $[2,25]\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Standard deviation is the square root of variance. The square root is increasing on nonnegative values, so it maps the lower endpoint to $\sqrt4=2$ and the upper endpoint to $\sqrt{25}=5$, preserving their order. Taking square roots also changes the unit from square millimeters to millimeters, giving the interval $[2,5]\,\mathrm{mm}$.
