For $25$ normal observations, $\bar x=12$ and $s^2=100$. Which value should multiply the t quantile to obtain the margin?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac25$
- $20$

## Explanation

The margin equals the t quantile times the estimated standard error $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt n}$. The given value $100$ is the sample variance, so take its square root first to obtain $s=10$. The square root of the sample size is $\sqrt{25}=5$. The factor multiplying the quantile is therefore $\dfrac{10}{5}=2$.
