For a normal mean with known standard deviation, the interval $[16,24]$ is obtained using $z=2$ and $n=9$. What was the population standard deviation?

- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $18$

## Explanation

The distance between the endpoints is $24-16=8$, giving two-sided margin $4$. Dividing by the given quantile $z=2$ gives the standard error of the mean, $2$. With known standard deviation, $2=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt9}$ holds. Since $\sqrt9=3$, the standard deviation of individual observations is $\sigma=6$.
