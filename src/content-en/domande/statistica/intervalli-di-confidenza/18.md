A $95\,\%$ procedure returns $[8,12]$. Which probability has the frequentist interpretation of coverage?

- The probability that the observed mean lies in its own symmetric interval
- The probability, before observing the sample, that the random endpoints enclose the fixed parameter
- The probability that a new observation lies in $[8,12]$
- The probability that the parameter changes between repetitions

## Explanation

Before observing the data, the sample is random, as are the endpoints $L$ and $U$. The parameter $\theta$ is fixed, and coverage is the probability of $L\leq\theta\leq U$. After observation the endpoints take the values $8$ and $12$. Whether the parameter belongs to that interval is then determined by the fixed value of $\theta$.
