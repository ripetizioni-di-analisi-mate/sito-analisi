The observed sample proportion is $0.80$. With a positive critical value, where is the center of the Wilson interval?

- Between zero and $0.50$
- Above $0.80$
- Always exactly at $0.80$
- Between $0.50$ and $0.80$

## Explanation

The center can be written as $\dfrac{n\widehat p+z^2\cdot\frac12}{n+z^2}$, a weighted average of the observed proportion $0.80$ and $0.50$. The sample size is positive and the positive quantile gives $z^2>0$. Both values have strictly positive weights, so the center lies strictly between $0.50$ and $0.80$.
