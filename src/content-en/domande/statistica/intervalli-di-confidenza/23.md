A normal interval for the mean has width $12$. The sample size is quadrupled while the standard deviation and confidence level stay fixed. What is the new width?

- $24$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

The width of the normal interval is $2z\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$. The quantile and standard deviation stay fixed, so replacing $n$ with $4n$ changes only the denominator. Since $\sqrt{4n}=2\sqrt n$, the new width is half the original, giving $\dfrac{12}{2}=6$.
