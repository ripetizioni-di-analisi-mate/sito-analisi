A sample of $25$ independent observations from a normal population has mean $40$. The population standard deviation is $5$. Calculate the two-sided interval using the quantile $z=1.96$.

- $[30.20,49.80]$
- $[39.02,40.98]$
- $[38.04,41.96]$
- $[39,41]$

## Explanation

The measurements are independent and normal, with known population standard deviation, so use the normal pivot for the mean. The standard error is $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{25}}=1$ and the margin of error is $1.96\cdot1=1.96$. Subtracting the margin from the observed mean $40$ and adding it to that mean gives $[38.04,41.96]$. The standard deviation $5$ measures the spread of individual measurements.
