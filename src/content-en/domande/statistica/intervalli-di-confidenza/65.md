The within-pair differences for four independent pairs are $-2,0,0,2$. Calculate the unbiased sample variance of the differences.

- $8$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac83$
- $2$

## Explanation

The four differences have mean $\dfrac{-2+0+0+2}{4}=0$, so they equal their deviations from the mean. Their squares sum to $(-2)^2+0^2+0^2+2^2=8$. The unbiased sample variance uses divisor $4-1=3$ because the center was estimated from the four pairs. This gives $s_D^2=\dfrac83$.
