A paired study records four before-and-after pairs. By mistake a technician adds a different offset to each pair, adding the same offset to both readings within that pair. How does this affect the t interval for the mean difference?

- The centre stays fixed but the interval widens
- Each endpoint increases by the average offset
- Both endpoints stay exactly the same
- The degrees of freedom are halved

## Explanation

For pair $i$ the difference becomes $(\mathrm{after}_i+c_i)-(\mathrm{before}_i+c_i)=\mathrm{after}_i-\mathrm{before}_i$. The differences, their mean, variance and number all stay the same, so the interval endpoints do too.
