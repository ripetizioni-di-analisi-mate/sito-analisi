In a Welch calculation the contributions are $v_X=9$ and $v_Y=16$. What is the margin for t quantile $2.1$?

- $14.7$
- $10.5$
- $5$
- $52.5$

## Explanation

In Welch's formula, $v_X$ and $v_Y$ are contributions to the estimated variance of the difference. Add $9$ and $16$ before taking the square root. The estimated standard error is $\sqrt{25}=5$. The t quantile is given, so the margin is $2.1\cdot5=10.5$ without any additional degrees-of-freedom calculation.
