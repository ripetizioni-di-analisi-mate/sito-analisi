For a normal sample of size $n=9$, which chi-square quantiles are used for the two-sided $98\,\%$ interval for the variance?

- $\chi^2_{8,0.01}$ and $\chi^2_{8,0.99}$
- $\chi^2_{9,0.01}$ and $\chi^2_{9,0.99}$
- $\chi^2_{8,0.49}$ and $\chi^2_{8,0.51}$
- $\chi^2_{8,0.02}$ and $\chi^2_{8,0.98}$

## Explanation

Under the normal model the variance pivot has a chi-square distribution with $n-1=8$ degrees of freedom. The central region must have probability $0.98$, leaving $\dfrac{1-0.98}{2}=0.01$ in each tail. The lower quantile has order $0.01$ and the upper quantile has order $0.99$. Both use the same degrees of freedom.
