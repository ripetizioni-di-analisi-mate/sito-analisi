With the same quantile and known standard deviation, the margin for the mean must change from $3$ to $1$. By what factor must the theoretical sample size be multiplied?

- $6$
- $3$
- $9$
- $\dfrac19$

## Explanation

The theoretical margin is $E=z\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$. Holding $z$ and $\sigma$ fixed gives $n=\left(\dfrac{z\sigma}{E}\right)^2$ before rounding. Changing from $E=3$ to $E=1$ triples the ratio inside the square. The theoretical sample size must therefore be multiplied by $3^2=9$.
