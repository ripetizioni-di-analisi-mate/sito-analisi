For a two-sided, equal-tailed $98\,\%$ interval, what are the orders of the two lower-tail quantiles?

- $0.49$ and $0.51$
- $0.02$ and $0.98$
- $0.01$ and $0.99$
- $0.01$ and $0.98$

## Explanation

The required central area is $0.98$, leaving a total tail area of $1-0.98=0.02$. Equal tails therefore each have probability $0.01$. The lower quantile has order $0.01$. The upper quantile leaves $0.01$ to its right, so its cumulative probability is $1-0.01=0.99$.
