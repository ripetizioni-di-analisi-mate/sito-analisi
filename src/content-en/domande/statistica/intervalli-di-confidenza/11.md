Sixteen independent units give sixteen normally distributed paired differences with sample standard deviation $8$, calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction. Use $t_{15,0.975}=2.131$ to find the margin for the two-sided $95\,\%$ interval.

- $2.131$
- $1.0655$
- $4.262$
- $8.524$

## Explanation

The unit of analysis is the difference within a pair. The sixteen independent normal differences form a sample for a t interval with $15$ degrees of freedom. The estimated standard deviation of their mean is $\dfrac8{\sqrt{16}}=2$. Multiplying by the given quantile yields the margin $2.131\cdot2=4.262$.
