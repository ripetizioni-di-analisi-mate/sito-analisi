A table assigns masses $a$, $1-a$ and $a^2$ to three distinct points. For which $a$ does it define a probability distribution?

- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- For every $a\in[0,1]$

## Explanation

A probability distribution requires nonnegative masses summing to $1$. The proposed sum is $a+(1-a)+a^2=1+a^2$. Normalization therefore requires $a^2=0$, giving $a=0$. The masses are then $0$, $1$ and $0$. Nonnegativity alone would allow every $a$ between zero and one.
