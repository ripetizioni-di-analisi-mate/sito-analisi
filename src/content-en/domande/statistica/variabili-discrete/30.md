A sensor detects a signal with probability $0.7$. If $I$ is the detection indicator, what is the Bernoulli parameter of $1-I$?

- $0.7$
- $0.21$
- $1$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

The indicator $I$ equals $1$ when the sensor detects the signal and $0$ when it misses it. Consequently, $1-I$ equals $1$ precisely for a missed detection. The Bernoulli parameter is the probability that the new indicator equals one, so it is $P(1-I=1)=P(I=0)=1-0.7=0.3$. The value $0.7$ is the probability that the new indicator equals zero.
