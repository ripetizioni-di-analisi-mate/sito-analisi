A cumulative distribution function is constant and equal to $0.4$ for $1<t<3$. Which probability follows from this information?

- $P(X=1)=0$
- $P(1<X<3)=0$
- $P(X=3)=0$
- $P(1\le X\le3)=0.4$

## Explanation

The mass at a point $t$ is the jump of the cumulative distribution function, $p_X(t)=F_X(t)-F_X(t^-)$. For every $t$ with $1<t<3$ the function equals $0.4$ both at $t$ and just before $t$, so $p_X(t)=0.4-0.4=0$. All values strictly between $1$ and $3$ therefore have zero mass, and $P(1<X<3)$, which is the sum of these masses, equals $0$. The given information concerns the open interval, so additional data are needed to determine any jump at $1$ or $3$.
