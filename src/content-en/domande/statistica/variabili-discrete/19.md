A cumulative distribution function is constant and equal to $0.4$ for $1<t<3$. Which probability follows from this information?

- $P(X=1)=0$
- $P(1<X<3)=0$
- $P(X=3)=0$
- $P(1\le X\le3)=0.4$

## Explanation

If $1<a<b<3$, then $P(a<X\le b)=F_X(b)-F_X(a)=0$. We can choose an increasing sequence of such intervals whose union covers all points strictly between $1$ and $3$. By continuity of probability, $P(1<X<3)=0$ as well. The given information concerns the open interval, so additional data are needed to determine any jump at $1$ or $3$.
