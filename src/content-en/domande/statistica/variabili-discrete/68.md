A batch contains eight nondefective and two defective parts. Two parts are drawn without replacement, uniformly at each step. Given that the first is defective, what is the probability that the second is also defective?

- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac1{10}$

## Explanation

The condition tells us that the first part is one of the two defective parts. It is removed from the batch because there is no replacement. Nine parts remain, one defective. The second part is chosen uniformly from these nine, so the conditional probability is $\dfrac19$. The initial fraction $\dfrac2{10}$ applies to the first draw.
