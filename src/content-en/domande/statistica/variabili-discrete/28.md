A nonnegative variable has mass $p_X(k)=\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}$ for $k=1,2,\dots$. What is true of $E[X]$?

- It is infinite.
- It equals $1$.
- It equals $2$.
- It equals $0$.

## Explanation

To find the expected value, multiply each mass by the value at which it is assigned. The point $k$ contributes $k\cdot\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac1{k+1}$. Summing from $k=1$ onward gives the harmonic series without its first term, which diverges to $+\infty$. Since $X$ takes no negative values, one sets $E[X]=+\infty$ in this case. The masses still sum to $1$, because $\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac1k-\dfrac1{k+1}$ and the sum of these terms equals $1$.
