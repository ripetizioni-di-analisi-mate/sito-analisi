A number is chosen uniformly from $\{1,2,3,4\}$. The variable $X$ is its remainder upon division by $3$. Which value of $X$ has mass $\dfrac12$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Dividing $1$, $2$, $3$ and $4$ by $3$ gives respective remainders $1$, $2$, $0$ and $1$. Uniform selection assigns probability $\dfrac14$ to each original number. Since both $1$ and $4$ give remainder $1$, the mass at that remainder is $\dfrac14+\dfrac14=\dfrac12$. Remainders $0$ and $2$ each come from just one number.
