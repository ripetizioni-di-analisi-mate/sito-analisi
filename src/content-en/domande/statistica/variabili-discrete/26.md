A variable is reported to satisfy $E[X]=3$ and $E[X^2]=8$. Which conclusion is correct?

- The data are consistent and the variance is $1$.
- The data are consistent and the variance is $5$.
- The data are inconsistent because they would give negative variance.
- Consistency depends on the signs of the support values.

## Explanation

Variance is the expectation of a square and must be nonnegative. Substituting the data in the identity $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-E[X]^2$ gives $8-3^2=-1$. The two reported moments are therefore inconsistent. A variable with mean $3$ must satisfy $E[X^2]\ge9$, with equality only if it is almost surely constant.
