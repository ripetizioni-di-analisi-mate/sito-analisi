Let $X$ have support $\{0,2,3\}$, with $P(X\le2)=\dfrac34$ and $P(X\ge2)=\dfrac12$. What is $P(X=2)$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $0$

## Explanation

Events $\{X\le2\}$ and $\{X\ge2\}$ cover all outcomes. Adding their probabilities counts the outcomes with $X=2$ twice and all other outcomes once. Subtracting the total probability gives the required mass, $\dfrac34+\dfrac12-1=\dfrac14$. The value $2$ is the only point in the intersection.
