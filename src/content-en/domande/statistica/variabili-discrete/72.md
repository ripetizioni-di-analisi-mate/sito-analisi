Two independent Poisson counts with parameters $1$ and $4$ refer to the same interval. What is the probability that their sum is two?

- $\dfrac{25}{2}e^{-5}$
- $5e^{-5}$
- $25e^{-5}$
- $4e^{-5}$

## Explanation

The sum of two independent Poisson counts is also Poisson, with parameter equal to the sum of the individual parameters. Here the total has parameter $\lambda=1+4=5$. Use the mass at $k=2$ for a total of two events, giving $P(X+Y=2)=e^{-5}\dfrac{5^2}{2!}=\dfrac{25}{2}e^{-5}$. The total includes cases in which both events come from the same counter.
