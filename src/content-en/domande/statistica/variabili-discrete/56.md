A Poisson count has standard deviation $3$. What is its mean?

- $9$
- $3$
- $\sqrt3$
- $6$

## Explanation

Standard deviation is the square root of variance, so $\sigma=3$ gives $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\sigma^2=9$. For a Poisson count, both variance and mean equal the parameter $\lambda$. Thus $E[X]=9$. The number $3$ is the standard deviation of the count, and squaring it gives the parameter.
