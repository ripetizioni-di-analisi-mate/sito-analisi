Which feature always distinguishes $\operatorname{Bin}(100,0.02)$ from $\operatorname{Pois}(2)$?

- The Poisson distribution assigns positive mass even to counts above $100$.
- The Poisson and binomial distributions have different means.
- Only the binomial distribution assigns mass to zero.
- The binomial distribution assigns mass to negative counts.

## Explanation

The binomial counts successes in one hundred trials, so its support ends at $100$. The Poisson distribution with parameter $2$ assigns strictly positive mass $e^{-2}\dfrac{2^k}{k!}$ to each integer $k\ge0$. For example, the count $101$ has probability zero under the binomial distribution and positive probability under the Poisson. The distributions share mean $2$ and have different supports.
