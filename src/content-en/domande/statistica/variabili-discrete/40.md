What further assumption is needed for the number of successes in six independent Bernoulli trials to have a binomial distribution?

- A common success probability for all six trials.
- The possibility of at least two successes per trial.
- A random number of trials.
- A success probability greater than $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

A sum of indicators is binomial when the number of trials is fixed, the trials are independent and every trial has the same success probability. The first two conditions are given. The six Bernoulli trials must therefore have a common parameter $p$. This equality gives every sequence with $k$ successes the same probability $p^k(1-p)^{6-k}$.
