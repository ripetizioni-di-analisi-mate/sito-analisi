In an inspection, $I$ indicates a positive result and has parameter $p=0.2$. Which property holds for $I$ and $1-I$?

- They have the same variance.
- They have the same mean.
- They are independent.
- They can both equal $1$ in the same outcome.

## Explanation

The indicator $I$ has parameter $0.2$, while $1-I$ equals one with probability $0.8$. In the Bernoulli variance formula the two parameters exchange the factors $p$ and $1-p$. Both variances therefore equal $0.2\cdot0.8=0.16$. Their means equal their respective parameters and are different. Also, whenever one indicator is one the other is zero.
