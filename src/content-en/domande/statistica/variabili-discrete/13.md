In independent trials with success probability $\dfrac12$, calculate the probability that the second success occurs on the fourth trial.

- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac3{16}$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

If the fourth trial gives the second success, the first three must contain one success and the fourth must succeed. The first success can occupy any of the first three positions. By independence, each such four-outcome sequence has probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^4=\dfrac1{16}$. Adding the three probabilities gives $\dfrac3{16}$.
