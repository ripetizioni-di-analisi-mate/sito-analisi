Arrivals follow a homogeneous Poisson process at rate $r$ per hour. What distribution does the number of arrivals in two hours have?

- Poisson with parameter $\dfrac r2$.
- Geometric with parameter $r$.
- Binomial with two trials and probability $r$.
- Poisson with parameter $2r$.

## Explanation

In a homogeneous Poisson process the count parameter is the mean number of arrivals in the observed interval. The rate is $r$ per hour and the duration is two hours, so the parameter is $r\cdot2=2r$. The count has a Poisson distribution with parameter $2r$. Doubling the duration in this case doubles both the mean and the variance.
