For a variable with $E[X]=6$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)=4$, define $Z=\dfrac{X-6}{2}$. What is the pair $(E[Z],\operatorname{Var}(Z))$?

- $(0,2)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(0,1)$

## Explanation

By linearity, $E[Z]=\dfrac{E[X]-6}{2}=0$ because subtraction removes the mean exactly. The constant $6$ leaves the variance unchanged. Dividing by $2$ multiplies deviations from the mean by $\dfrac12$ and their squares by $\dfrac14$. This gives $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=\dfrac{4}{2^2}=1$, so the required pair is $(0,1)$.
