Independent trials with success probability $p>0$ are repeated until the fifth success. What is the smallest possible total number of trials, including the last?

- $4$
- $0$
- $1$
- $5$

## Explanation

Each trial adds at most one success, so there can be at most four successes after four trials. The fifth success can occur on trial five if the first five trials all succeed. By independence and the assumption $p>0$, this event has probability $p^5>0$. Thus $5$ is the smallest possible total number of trials.
