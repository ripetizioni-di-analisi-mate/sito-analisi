A homogeneous Poisson process records an average of two events per minute. Given that no events were observed in the first minute, what is the probability of zero events in the second minute?

- $e^{-4}$
- $1-e^{-2}$
- $1$
- $e^{-2}$

## Explanation

The first and second minutes are disjoint intervals, so their counts are independent in a Poisson process. Knowing that the first interval was empty leaves the distribution for the second unchanged. Its duration is one minute, so the parameter of its count is $\lambda=2$. The probability of zero events is therefore $e^{-2}\dfrac{2^0}{0!}=e^{-2}$.
