The cumulative distribution function of $X$ equals $0$ for $t<0$, $0.1$ for $0\le t<1$, $0.6$ for $1\le t<2$ and $1$ for $t\ge2$. What is $F_X(1.8)$?

- $0.1$
- $0.5$
- $0.6$
- $1$

## Explanation

By definition, $F_X(1.8)$ is the accumulated probability up to and including $1.8$. The cutoff lies between $1$ inclusive and $2$ exclusive. The cumulative distribution function is constant throughout this interval, so $F_X(1.8)=0.6$. Its jump to $1$ occurs at $2$, beyond the required cutoff. The level $0.6$ already includes the masses at $0$ and $1$, so adding them again would count them twice.
