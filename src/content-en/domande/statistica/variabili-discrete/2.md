A prize $X$ pays $0$, $5$ or $10$ euros with probabilities $\dfrac35$, $\dfrac15$ and $\dfrac15$. Calculate the expected prize.

- $3\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $2\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The mean prize is a weighted sum. Zero occurs more often, so a simple average of $0$, $5$ and $10$ would give the three outcomes the wrong weights. Their contributions to the expected prize are $0\cdot\dfrac35=0$, $5\cdot\dfrac15=1$ and $10\cdot\dfrac15=2$ euros, summing to $3\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
