A basketball player takes a series of free throws. Each throw scores with probability $\dfrac34$, independently of the others. What is the probability that the first basket comes on the second or third throw?

- $\dfrac{63}{64}$
- $\dfrac3{16}$
- $\dfrac{15}{64}$
- $\dfrac3{64}$

## Explanation

The number $T$ of the throw on which the first basket comes has a geometric distribution with parameter $p=\dfrac34$, so $P(T=k)=\left(\dfrac14\right)^{k-1}\dfrac34$. For $k=2$ we get $P(T=2)=\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac3{16}$. For $k=3$ we get $P(T=3)=\left(\dfrac14\right)^2\dfrac34=\dfrac3{64}$. The two events are mutually exclusive, so their probabilities add up to $\dfrac{12}{64}+\dfrac3{64}=\dfrac{15}{64}$.
