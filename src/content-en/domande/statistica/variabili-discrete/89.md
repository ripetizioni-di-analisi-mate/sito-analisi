A device is tested independently with success probability $\dfrac34$. What is the probability that the first success occurs on the second or third trial?

- $\dfrac{63}{64}$
- $\dfrac3{16}$
- $\dfrac{15}{64}$
- $\dfrac3{64}$

## Explanation

A first success on trial two requires a failure followed by a success, with probability $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac3{16}$. On trial three it requires two failures followed by a success, with probability $\left(\dfrac14\right)^2\dfrac34=\dfrac3{64}$. The events are disjoint, so add their masses to obtain $\dfrac{12}{64}+\dfrac3{64}=\dfrac{15}{64}$.
