Two devices are tested together until the first time at least one works. The success probabilities at each time are $1/2$ and $1/3$, independently across devices and times. At the stopping time, what is the probability that both work?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

At one testing time the probability both work is $1/6$, while at least one works with probability $1-(1/2)(2/3)=2/3$. All previous times were failures and, by independence, do not change the relative chances of the outcomes at the final time. The required probability is $(1/6)/(2/3)=1/4$.
