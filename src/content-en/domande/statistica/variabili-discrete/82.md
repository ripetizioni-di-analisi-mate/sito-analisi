After three failures, the probability of success on the fourth trial is $\dfrac25$. Under the geometric model, what is the success probability of a single trial before any outcomes are observed?

- $\dfrac35$
- $\left(\dfrac35\right)^3\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac25$
- $1-\left(\dfrac35\right)^4$

## Explanation

Let $p$ denote the success probability on one trial. In the geometric model the trials are independent with a common parameter, so the first three failures leave the fourth-trial success probability unchanged. The given conditional probability therefore equals $p=\dfrac25$. The probability of the first success on exactly trial four would also include the factor for the first three failures.
