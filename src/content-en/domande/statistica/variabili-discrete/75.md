Two independent counters have variances $2$ and $3$. What is the variance of the difference between their counts?

- $1$
- $6$
- $5$
- $13$

## Explanation

Let $X$ and $Y$ denote the counts. Independence gives zero covariance. The formula $\operatorname{Var}(X-Y)=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\operatorname{Var}(Y)-2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$ therefore reduces to the sum of the variances, $2+3=5$. The minus sign applies to the count $Y$, but the deviations of $-Y$ are squared and contribute the same variance as $Y$.
