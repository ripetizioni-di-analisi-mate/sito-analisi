Two independent counters have variances $2$ and $3$. What is the variance of the difference between their counts?

- $1$
- $6$
- $5$
- $13$

## Explanation

Let $X$ and $Y$ denote the counts. The difference is the sum $X+(-Y)$, and $X$ and $-Y$ are independent because $X$ and $Y$ are. For independent random variables the variance of the sum is the sum of the variances, so $\operatorname{Var}(X-Y)=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\operatorname{Var}(-Y)$. By the rule for affine transformations with $b=-1$, $\operatorname{Var}(-Y)=(-1)^2\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\operatorname{Var}(Y)$. Therefore $\operatorname{Var}(X-Y)=2+3=5$.
