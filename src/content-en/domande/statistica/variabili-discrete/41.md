In three independent checks with common success probability $p$, the probability of zero successes is $\dfrac18$. What is $p$?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac78$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Zero successes means that all three checks fail. By independence, this event has probability $(1-p)^3$. Taking the cube root in $(1-p)^3=\dfrac18=\left(\dfrac12\right)^3$ gives $1-p=\dfrac12$. This is the failure probability, so the success parameter is $p=1-\dfrac12=\dfrac12$.
