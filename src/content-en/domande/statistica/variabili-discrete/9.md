Independent tests are repeated with success probability $\dfrac15$. Calculate the expected number of tests up to and including the first successful one.

- $4$
- $5$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac54$

## Explanation

The tests are independent and all have success probability $p=\dfrac15$. The variable $T$ counts trials up to and including the first success. It is therefore geometric on the positive integers with mean $E[T]=\dfrac1p=5$. Counting only failed tests before the success would give the variable $T-1$ and mean $4$.
