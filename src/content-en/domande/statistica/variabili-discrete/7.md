Four items are drawn uniformly without replacement from eight items, four of them marked. Calculate the variance of the number of marked items drawn.

- $\dfrac47$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac17$

## Explanation

Drawing without replacement from a finite batch gives a hypergeometric count. Here $N=8$, the sample size is $n=4$ and the initial marked fraction is $p=\dfrac48=\dfrac12$. The term $np(1-p)$ equals $1$ and the correction factor is $\dfrac{N-n}{N-1}=\dfrac47$. The required variance is therefore $1\cdot\dfrac47=\dfrac47$.
