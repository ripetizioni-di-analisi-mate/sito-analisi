Three messages are sent independently. Each is delivered with probability $\dfrac23$. Calculate the probability that at least one is delivered.

- $\dfrac1{27}$
- $\dfrac{26}{27}$
- $\dfrac8{27}$
- $\dfrac49$

## Explanation

The event includes one, two or three delivered messages. Its complement contains just one sequence, in which all three transmissions fail. Each fails with probability $1-\dfrac23=\dfrac13$ and outcomes are independent, so the complement has probability $\left(\dfrac13\right)^3=\dfrac1{27}$. Subtracting from one gives $\dfrac{26}{27}$.
