Let $X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(6,p)$ and suppose $E[X]=2$. What is the success probability $p$?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

A binomial count in six trials is a sum of six success indicators. Each indicator has mean $p$, so $E[X]=6p$. The given value $E[X]=2$ implies $6p=2$ and hence $p=\dfrac26=\dfrac13$. The mean $2$ counts expected successes over the whole series, while $p$ is the probability of success on a single trial.
