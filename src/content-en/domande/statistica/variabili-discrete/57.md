For $X\sim\operatorname{Pois}(2)$, what is $E[X^2]$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

The Poisson parameter $2$ gives both $E[X]=2$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)=2$. Isolate $E[X^2]$ in the variance identity to obtain the second moment, $E[X^2]=\operatorname{Var}(X)+(E[X])^2=2+2^2=6$. The term $4$ is the square of the mean, while the remaining $2$ comes from dispersion around the mean.
