A variable takes the values $-2$, $1$ and $4$ with masses $\dfrac14$, $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac14$. What is its variance?

- $\dfrac92$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The extreme values are symmetric about $1$ and have equal masses, so the mean is $1$, as the calculation $-2\cdot\dfrac14+1\cdot\dfrac12+4\cdot\dfrac14=1$ confirms. The deviations are $-3$, $0$ and $3$. The variance is the weighted mean of their squares, giving $9\cdot\dfrac14+0\cdot\dfrac12+9\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac92$.
