Exactly two successes are observed in four independent checks with common success probability $\dfrac14$. What is the conditional probability that the first check succeeded?

- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

Given a count of two, consider only the $\binom42=6$ sequences with two successes. They are equally likely because each contains two factors of $\dfrac14$ and two of $\dfrac34$. If the first trial succeeds, the other success can be in any of the three remaining positions. The conditional probability is therefore $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$.
