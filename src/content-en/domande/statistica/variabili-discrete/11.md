The cumulative distribution function of a discrete variable satisfies $F_X(2)=\dfrac38$ and $F_X(6)=\dfrac78$. Calculate $P(2<X\le6)$.

- $\dfrac78$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac38$

## Explanation

The quantity $F_X(6)$ includes all masses up to and including $6$. The requested interval excludes the values up to and including $2$, whose total probability is $F_X(2)$. Therefore calculate $F_X(6)-F_X(2)=\dfrac78-\dfrac38=\dfrac48=\dfrac12$. Subtraction removes any mass at $2$ as well, as required by the open left endpoint.
