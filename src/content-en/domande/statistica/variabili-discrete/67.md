Compare counts with and without replacement from a batch of $20$ units with the same success proportion, strictly between $0$ and $1$. For $5$ draws, what factor multiplies the variance with replacement to give the variance without replacement?

- $\dfrac{19}{15}$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac{15}{19}$

## Explanation

With replacement, the count has binomial variance $np(1-p)$. Without replacement, one outcome changes the composition available for the next draw, so the draw indicators are dependent. The hypergeometric variance is the same expression multiplied by $\dfrac{N-n}{N-1}$. With $N=20$ and $n=5$ the factor is $\dfrac{20-5}{20-1}=\dfrac{15}{19}$, less than one.
