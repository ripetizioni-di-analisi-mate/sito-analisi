An urn contains $5$ red balls and $5$ white balls. Three balls are drawn one at a time, uniformly and without replacement. What is the probability that the first and the third balls drawn are both red?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac5{18}$
- $\dfrac1{12}$
- $\dfrac29$

## Explanation

Let $I_i$ be the indicator that the ball in position $i$ is red. By symmetry of the positions, the probability that two distinct positions both contain a red ball is the same as for the first two draws, so
$$
P(I_1=1,\,I_3=1)=\frac{R(R-1)}{N(N-1)}=\frac{5\cdot4}{10\cdot9}=\frac29.
$$
The same value follows by distinguishing the color of the second ball: $\dfrac5{10}\cdot\dfrac49\cdot\dfrac38+\dfrac5{10}\cdot\dfrac59\cdot\dfrac48=\dfrac{60}{720}+\dfrac{100}{720}=\dfrac29$. The value $\dfrac14=\left(\dfrac12\right)^2$ would be correct with replacement, while $\dfrac1{12}$ is the probability that all three are red.
