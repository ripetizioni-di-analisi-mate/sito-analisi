A homogeneous Poisson process records an average of four arrivals per hour. Calculate the probability of zero arrivals in half an hour.

- $e^{-4}$
- $1-e^{-2}$
- $e^{-2}$
- $2e^{-2}$

## Explanation

The rate is per hour, while the Poisson parameter must refer to the observation interval. For half an hour the parameter is $\lambda=4\cdot\dfrac12=2$. Substituting $k=0$ in the mass function gives $P(X=0)=e^{-2}\dfrac{2^0}{0!}=e^{-2}$. The factor multiplying the exponential equals one because $2^0=0!=1$.
