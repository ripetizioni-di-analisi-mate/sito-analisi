From a batch of $20$ numbered parts, $5$ are drawn uniformly without replacement. What is the probability that part number $7$ is in the sample?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac1{20}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac4{19}$

## Explanation

Treat part number $7$ as the only unit of interest, so $N=20$, $R=1$, and $n=5$. The number $X$ of units of interest in the sample equals $1$ when part $7$ is drawn, and it has a hypergeometric distribution. Hence
$$
P(X=1)=\frac{\binom11\binom{19}4}{\binom{20}5}=\frac{3876}{15504}=\frac14.
$$
The numerator counts the samples that contain part $7$, because the other $4$ parts are chosen from the remaining $19$. The result equals $\dfrac nN=\dfrac5{20}$, the mean $np$ with $p=\dfrac1{20}$.
