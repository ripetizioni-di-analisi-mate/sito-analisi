Five units are drawn uniformly without replacement from six, four of them defective. What is the support of the number of defective units in the sample?

- $\{3,4\}$
- $\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$
- $\{2,3,4\}$
- $\{4,5\}$

## Explanation

The population has four defective and two nondefective units. A sample of five can include at most two nondefective units, so it contains at least $5-2=3$ defective units. It can include at most four because only four are available. Both bounds are attainable by excluding a defective unit or a nondefective unit, respectively. The support is therefore $\{3,4\}$.
