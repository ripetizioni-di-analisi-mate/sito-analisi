A variable assigns mass $\dfrac12$ to $0$ and mass $\dfrac14$ to each of $2$ and $4$. What is the support of $Y=X^2$?

- $\{0,2,4\}$
- $\{0,2,16\}$
- $\{0,4,16\}$
- $\{0,4,8\}$

## Explanation

The support of $Y$ consists of the values with positive probability after transformation. Squaring $0$, $2$ and $4$ gives $0$, $4$ and $16$, respectively. The three images are distinct, so each retains the mass of its original value and no masses need to be added. The support is therefore $\{0,4,16\}$.
