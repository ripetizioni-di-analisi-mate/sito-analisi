An integer-valued variable satisfies $F_X(2)=0.6$ and $F_X(3)=0.9$. What is the probability that it is at least $3$?

- $0.1$
- $0.4$
- $0.3$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

Since $X$ is integer-valued, values strictly below $3$ are precisely those at most $2$. The complementary event is therefore $\{X\le2\}$. Its probability is $F_X(2)=0.6$, so $P(X\ge3)=1-0.6=0.4$. Subtracting $F_X(3)$ from one would give the probability of strictly exceeding $3$.
