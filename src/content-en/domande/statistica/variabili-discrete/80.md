A discrete variable takes values $-2$, $2$ or $3$ with masses $\dfrac14$, $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac14$. What is $P(X^2=4)$?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $1$

## Explanation

Two support values, $-2$ and $2$, satisfy the equation $X^2=4$. They both give the same transformed value. Events $\{X=-2\}$ and $\{X=2\}$ are disjoint, so their combined probability is $\dfrac14+\dfrac12=\dfrac34$. The third possible value is $3$, whose square is $9$, so its mass belongs to a different value of $X^2$.
