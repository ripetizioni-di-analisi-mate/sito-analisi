Two independent counters give Poisson counts with parameters $2$ and $6$ over the same interval. Calculate the expected total count.

- $4$
- $8$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

Let $X$ and $Y$ be the two counts. The parameter of a Poisson variable is its mean, so $E[X]=2$ and $E[Y]=6$. Linearity of expectation gives $E[X+Y]=2+6=8$. Independence also shows that the total has a Poisson distribution. Linearity alone suffices to add the means.
