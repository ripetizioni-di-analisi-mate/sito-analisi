A variable takes only the values $0$ and $5$ and has mean $2$. What is $P(X=5)$?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac15$

## Explanation

Let $q=P(X=5)$. Since $X$ can take only two values, the other mass is $P(X=0)=1-q$. The contribution of zero to the expectation is $0\cdot(1-q)=0$, so $E[X]=5q$. Setting this equal to the given mean yields $5q=2$ and $q=\dfrac25$. The remaining mass at zero is $\dfrac35$.
