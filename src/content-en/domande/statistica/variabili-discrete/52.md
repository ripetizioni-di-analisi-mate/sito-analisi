A homogeneous Poisson process produces an average of $12$ events per hour. What is the variance of the count in $10\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $12$
- $4$
- $2$
- $\dfrac16$

## Explanation

The rate is $12$ events per hour. Convert the ten-minute duration to hours, giving $\dfrac{10}{60}=\dfrac16$. The count parameter is therefore $\lambda=12\cdot\dfrac16=2$. A Poisson count has variance equal to its parameter, so the variance over this interval is $2$. The value $12$ would refer to a full hour.
