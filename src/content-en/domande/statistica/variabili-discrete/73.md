Two counts have means $1$ and $5$, and their dependence is unknown. What can be concluded about the mean of their sum?

- It is $5$.
- It is $3$.
- Their dependence must be known.
- It is $6$.

## Explanation

Let $X$ and $Y$ denote the counts. Linearity of expectation gives $E[X+Y]=E[X]+E[Y]$ even for dependent counts, provided their means are finite, as here. Substituting the two values gives $1+5=6$. Dependence would matter for the variance of the sum because that formula also contains the covariance.
