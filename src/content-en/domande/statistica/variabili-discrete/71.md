Let $X$ and $Y$ be independent with respective distributions $\operatorname{Bin}(1,\dfrac15)$ and $\operatorname{Bin}(1,\dfrac45)$. What is $P(X+Y=0)$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac{16}{25}$
- $\dfrac4{25}$
- $\dfrac1{25}$

## Explanation

Both variables are zero or one, so their sum is zero only when both are zero. The first parameter gives $P(X=0)=1-\dfrac15=\dfrac45$ and the second gives $P(Y=0)=1-\dfrac45=\dfrac15$. Since $X$ and $Y$ are independent, the joint probability is $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac4{25}$.
