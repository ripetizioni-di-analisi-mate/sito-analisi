A trial succeeds with probability $1$ and is repeated until the first success. What is the support of the number of trials performed?

- $\{1\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{0,1\}$
- $\{1,2,3,\dots\}$

## Explanation

The success probability is one, so the first trial ends the experiment with certainty. The count includes the successful trial and assigns mass one to $1$. The support consists of values with positive probability, so it is $\{1\}$. Performing two or more trials would require an initial failure, which here has probability zero.
