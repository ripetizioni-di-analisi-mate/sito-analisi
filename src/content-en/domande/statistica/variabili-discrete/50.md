In independent trials with success probability $\dfrac23$, what is the expected number of trials up to and including the fourth success?

- $\dfrac32$
- $6$
- $2$
- $12$

## Explanation

The total number of trials until the fourth success splits into four waiting times, each up to and including the next successful trial. Each waiting time is geometric with parameter $\dfrac23$ and therefore has mean $\dfrac1{\frac23}=\dfrac32$. By linearity, the expected total number of trials is $4\cdot\dfrac32=6$. This decomposition counts each of the four successes once.
