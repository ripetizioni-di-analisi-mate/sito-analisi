Eight units are drawn uniformly without replacement from twelve, three of them marked. What is the expected number of marked units in the sample?

- $3$
- $2$
- $\dfrac14$
- $6$

## Explanation

Number the sample positions and give each an indicator equal to one when it holds a marked unit. By symmetry of uniform sampling, each position individually has probability $\dfrac3{12}=\dfrac14$ of holding a marked unit. Linearity of expectation applies even though the positions are dependent. The mean count is therefore $8\cdot\dfrac14=2$.
