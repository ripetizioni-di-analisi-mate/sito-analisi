In independent trials with success probability $\dfrac14$, what is the probability that the first success occurs by the second trial?

- $\dfrac9{16}$
- $\dfrac3{16}$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac7{16}$

## Explanation

Let $T$ be the trial on which the first success occurs. Event $T\le2$ includes a first-trial success or a second-trial success after a failure. Its complement $T>2$ requires two consecutive failures. By independence their probability is $\left(\dfrac34\right)^2=\dfrac9{16}$. Hence $P(T\le2)=1-\dfrac9{16}=\dfrac7{16}$.
