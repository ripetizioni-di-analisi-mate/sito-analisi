Let $X$ count successes in five independent trials with success probability $\dfrac13$. What distribution does $5-X$ have?

- $\operatorname{Bin}\left(5,\dfrac13\right)$
- $\operatorname{Pois}\left(\dfrac{10}{3}\right)$
- $\operatorname{Bin}\left(4,\dfrac23\right)$
- $\operatorname{Bin}\left(5,\dfrac23\right)$

## Explanation

In each of the five trials, the failure indicator is the complement of the success indicator. It equals one with probability $1-\dfrac13=\dfrac23$. Transforming each outcome separately preserves independence across trials. Since $5-X$ sums these five failure indicators with a common parameter, it has distribution $\operatorname{Bin}\left(5,\dfrac23\right)$.
