Let $X\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\dfrac12)$ and set $Y=X$. What is $\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $Y=X$, the sum equals $2X$ in every outcome. A Bernoulli variable with $p=\dfrac12$ has variance $\dfrac12\left(1-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac14$. Multiplying the variable by two doubles deviations from its mean and thus multiplies its variance by four. The result is $4\cdot\dfrac14=1$. Simply adding the two variances would incorrectly treat the counts as independent.
