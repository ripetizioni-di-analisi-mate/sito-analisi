Two units are drawn uniformly without replacement from seven, three of which have a characteristic of interest. What is the probability of at least one unit with the characteristic?

- $\dfrac57$
- $\dfrac27$
- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac{33}{49}$

## Explanation

At least one unit of interest includes samples with one and samples with two. The complement contains only samples drawn entirely from the four other units. Of the $\binom72=21$ equally likely samples, $\binom42=6$ therefore have no units of interest. This leaves $21-6=15$ favorable samples, giving probability $\dfrac{15}{21}=\dfrac57$.
