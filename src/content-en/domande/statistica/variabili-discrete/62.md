A box contains five red balls and three blue balls. If two balls are drawn uniformly without replacement, what is the probability that both are blue?

- $\dfrac9{64}$
- $\dfrac3{28}$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac{15}{28}$

## Explanation

Drawing without replacement gives samples of two distinct balls. Each pair can appear in two equally likely orders, so all unordered pairs are equally likely. There are $\binom82=28$ pairs in total and $\binom32=3$ pairs made up of two blue balls. The probability is therefore $\dfrac3{28}$. Drawing one ball at a time gives the same value: the first is blue with probability $\dfrac38$ and, after a blue first draw, two of the seven remaining balls are blue, so $\dfrac38\cdot\dfrac27=\dfrac3{28}$.
