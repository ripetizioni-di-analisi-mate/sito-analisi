Four independent checks each succeed with probability $\dfrac12$. Calculate the probability of exactly two successes.

- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac58$

## Explanation

A favorable sequence contains two successes and two failures. The success positions can be chosen in $\binom42=6$ ways, each of which fixes the failure positions as well. By independence, one such sequence has probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^4=\dfrac1{16}$. The six sequences are disjoint, so the required probability is $6\cdot\dfrac1{16}=\dfrac38$.
