A check succeeds with probability $p$. When is the variance of its success indicator greatest?

- For $p=0$.
- For $p=1$.
- For $p=\dfrac12$.
- For $p=\dfrac14$.

## Explanation

The indicator variance is $p(1-p)$. Completing the square gives $\dfrac14-\left(p-\dfrac12\right)^2$. The squared term is nonnegative throughout $0\le p\le1$ and zero only at $p=\dfrac12$. The variance is therefore greatest at that point and equals $\dfrac14$. At the endpoints the outcome is certain and the variance is zero.
