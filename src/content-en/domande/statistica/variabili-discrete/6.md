In independent trials with success probability $\dfrac12$, calculate the probability that the first success occurs on the fourth trial.

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac{15}{16}$
- $\dfrac1{16}$

## Explanation

For the first success to occur on the fourth trial, the first three must fail and the fourth must succeed. Each outcome's position is fixed, so calculate the probability of just one sequence. Independence gives $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac1{16}$. A success on any of the first three trials would make the first success earlier.
