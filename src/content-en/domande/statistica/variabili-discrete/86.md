Each item in a batch of $500$ is defective with probability $0.004$, independently of the others. Using the Poisson approximation, what is the probability that the batch contains no defective items?

- $e^{-0.004}$
- $e^{-2}$
- $1-e^{-2}$
- $2e^{-2}$

## Explanation

The number $X$ of defective items has a binomial distribution with $n=500$ trials and probability $p=0.004$. There are many trials and $p$ is small, so $X$ is approximated by a Poisson distribution with parameter $np=500\cdot0.004=2$. The probability of no defective items is then $P(X=0)\approx e^{-2}\dfrac{2^0}{0!}=e^{-2}\approx0.135$. The exact binomial value is $0.996^{500}\approx0.135$.
