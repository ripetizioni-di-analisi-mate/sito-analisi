A ticket costs $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $10\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with probability $\dfrac14$, otherwise zero. What is the expected net gain?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{EUR}$

## Explanation

The cost $4\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is paid in every outcome, while the prize is received only with probability $\dfrac14$. The expected gross prize is $10\cdot\dfrac14=2.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$. By linearity the expected net gain is $(2.5-4)\,\mathrm{EUR}=-1.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$. In a single play the gain is $6$ euros or $-4$ euros, so the expectation is the weighted mean of the two outcomes.
