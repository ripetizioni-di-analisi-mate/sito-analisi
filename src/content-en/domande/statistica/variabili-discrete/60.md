For a Poisson count with parameter $4$, which two values have the greatest probability?

- $4$ and $5$
- $2$ and $3$
- $3$ and $4$
- $4$ and $6$

## Explanation

Let $p_k=P(X=k)$. For this distribution, the ratio $\dfrac{p_{k+1}}{p_k}$ equals $\dfrac4{k+1}$. It is greater than one for $k=0,1,2$, equal to one for $k=3$ and less than one for $k\ge4$. The masses therefore increase up to $3$, stay the same at $4$ and then decrease. The two maxima occur at $3$ and $4$.
