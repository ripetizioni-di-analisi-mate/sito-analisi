One unit is drawn uniformly from a finite population of $N$ units, of which $R>0$ belong to a group of interest. What distribution does the indicator of group membership have?

- Bernoulli with parameter $\dfrac RN$.
- Poisson with parameter $\dfrac RN$.
- Geometric with parameter $\dfrac RN$, counting the trial of the first success.
- Bernoulli with parameter $\dfrac RN \cdot \dfrac12$.

## Explanation

The indicator equals $1$ if the unit belongs to the group and $0$ otherwise. All $N$ units are equally likely and $R$ give the value $1$, so $P(I=1)=\dfrac RN$. The remaining mass at zero is $1-\dfrac RN$. These two masses define a Bernoulli distribution with parameter $\dfrac RN$, including the degenerate case $R=N$.
