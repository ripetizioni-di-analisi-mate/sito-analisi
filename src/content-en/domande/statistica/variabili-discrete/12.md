An indicator equals $1$ when a document is incomplete, an event of probability $\dfrac25$. Calculate its variance.

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac6{25}$
- $\dfrac4{25}$
- $\dfrac9{25}$

## Explanation

Let $I$ be the indicator of an incomplete document and $p=\dfrac25$ the probability that it equals $1$. Since $I$ can only be zero or one, $I^2=I$ and hence $E[I^2]=E[I]=p$. The variance is $p-p^2$. Substituting the data gives $\dfrac25-\dfrac4{25}=\dfrac6{25}$, the probability of an incomplete document times the probability of a complete one.
