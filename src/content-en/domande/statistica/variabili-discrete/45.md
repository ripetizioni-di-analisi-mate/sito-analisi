Independent trials succeed with probability $\dfrac13$. Given that the first five trials failed, what is the probability that the next two also fail?

- $\left(\dfrac23\right)^7$
- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac59$

## Explanation

The five initial failures are the observed condition. By independence, conditioning on them leaves the distributions of the sixth and seventh trials unchanged. Each fails with probability $1-\dfrac13=\dfrac23$, so the product for two new failures is $\left(\dfrac23\right)^2=\dfrac49$. Multiplying seven factors would give the joint probability of the initial five failures and the following two.
