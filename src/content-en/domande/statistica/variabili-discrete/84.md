Nine parts are drawn uniformly without replacement from ten, four of them marked. What is the probability that exactly three of the parts drawn are marked?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac49$

## Explanation

Drawing nine of ten parts is equivalent to choosing the one left out. Precisely three of the original four marked parts remain in the sample if the omitted part is marked. Uniform sampling gives each of the ten parts the same probability of being omitted. Four are marked, so the probability is $\dfrac4{10}=\dfrac25$.
