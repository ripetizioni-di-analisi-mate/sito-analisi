Calls to a switchboard arrive according to a homogeneous Poisson process with rate $3$ calls per hour. For which observation period is the probability of receiving at least one call equal to $1-e^{-1}$?

- $1\,\mathrm{h}$
- $3\,\mathrm{h}$
- $10\,\mathrm{min}$
- $20\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Let $t$ be the period in hours. The number of calls in that period has a Poisson distribution with parameter $3t$. The event "at least one call" is the complement of "no calls", so its probability is $1-e^{-3t}$. This probability equals $1-e^{-1}$ when $3t=1$, that is, $t=\dfrac13\,\mathrm{h}=20\,\mathrm{min}$. With $1\,\mathrm{h}$ the parameter would be $3$ and the probability $1-e^{-3}$.
