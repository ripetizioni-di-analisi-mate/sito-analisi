Let $X$ be the number of events in an interval of a Poisson process, and suppose $P(X=0)=a$ with $0<a<1$. What is the probability of zero events in both of two disjoint intervals with the same duration and parameter?

- $a$
- $2a$
- $1-a^2$
- $a^2$

## Explanation

Each interval has probability $a$ of zero events because their parameters are equal. Counts in disjoint intervals of a Poisson process are independent. Both counts must be zero, so multiply their probabilities to obtain $a\cdot a=a^2$. Since $0<a<1$, we have $a^2<a$. Requiring both intervals to be empty is more restrictive than requiring just one to be empty.
