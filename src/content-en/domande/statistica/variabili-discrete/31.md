An indicator $I$ satisfies $E[I]=\dfrac13$. What is $E[I^4]$?

- $\dfrac1{81}$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Raising an indicator to a positive power preserves both possible values because $0^4=0$ and $1^4=1$. Thus $I^4=I$ for every outcome and $E[I^4]=E[I]=\dfrac13$. Equivalently, the term $1^4$ receives weight $P(I=1)=\dfrac13$ and the term $0^4$ contributes zero. Raising the mean to the fourth power would calculate a different quantity.
