Eight independent inspections each flag a defect with probability $\dfrac14$. Calculate the variance of the number of flags.

- $2$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac3{16}$

## Explanation

For each inspection, the flag indicator has success probability $p=\dfrac14$ and variance $p(1-p)=\dfrac3{16}$. The number of flags is the sum of eight independent indicators, so their variances add. This gives $8\cdot\dfrac3{16}=\dfrac32$. The product $8p=2$ would give the mean number of flags.
