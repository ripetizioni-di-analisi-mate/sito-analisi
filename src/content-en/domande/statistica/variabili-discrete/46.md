Independent trials are repeated with success probability $\dfrac23$. Let $G$ count failures before the first success. What is $P(G=0)$?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac13$
- $0$
- $\dfrac29$

## Explanation

The variable $G$ counts failures before the first success. For $G=0$, the first trial must succeed. This gives $P(G=0)=\dfrac23$, the success probability for a single trial. If all trials up to and including the successful one were counted, the variable would be $G+1$ and the same event would correspond to $1$.
