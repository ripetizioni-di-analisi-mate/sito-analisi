In independent trials with success probability $\dfrac12$, let $T$ be the trial number of the first success. What is $\operatorname{Var}(T-1)$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Subtracting one from $T$ reduces its expectation by the same amount, leaving deviations from the mean unchanged. Thus $\operatorname{Var}(T-1)=\operatorname{Var}(T)$. Since $T$ counts independent trials until the first success, it is geometric with $p=\dfrac12$. Its variance is $\dfrac{1-p}{p^2}=\dfrac{\frac12}{\frac14}=2$. The variables count different quantities with the same dispersion.
