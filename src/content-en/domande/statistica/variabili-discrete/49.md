A geometric variable $T$ counts trials up to the first success and satisfies $0<p<1$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{P(T=5)}{P(T=4)}$?

- $1-p$
- $p$
- $\dfrac1{1-p}$
- $1$

## Explanation

A first success on trial five requires four failures followed by a success. On trial four it requires three failures followed by a success. Setting $q=1-p$, the two masses are $q^4p$ and $q^3p$. Since both are positive, their ratio is $q^4p$ divided by $q^3p$, or $q=1-p$.
