During a particular time period the observed mean count is $5$. What can be concluded from this fact alone?

- The mean is known, while the distribution of the count is undetermined.
- The count has a Poisson distribution with parameter $5$.
- The count has a binomial distribution with five trials.
- The count is always $5$.

## Explanation

The mean describes the expected number of events in the period, but different distributions can share an expectation. For example, a count always equal to $5$ has mean $5$ and zero variance. A Poisson count with parameter $5$ also has mean $5$ but variance $5$. The given information fixes the mean while leaving the choice of model open.
