Two independent counts have distributions $\operatorname{Bin}(3,\dfrac14)$ and $\operatorname{Bin}(2,\dfrac14)$. What is the distribution of their sum?

- $\operatorname{Bin}(5,\dfrac12)$
- $\operatorname{Bin}(5,\dfrac14)$
- $\operatorname{Bin}(6,\dfrac14)$
- $\operatorname{Pois}(\dfrac54)$

## Explanation

The binomial counts are independent with common success probability $p=\dfrac14$. Their sum therefore counts successes in two independent groups of trials with that same parameter. There are $3+2=5$ trials in total, so the distribution is $\operatorname{Bin}\left(5,\dfrac14\right)$. Add the group sizes while keeping the individual success probability $\dfrac14$.
