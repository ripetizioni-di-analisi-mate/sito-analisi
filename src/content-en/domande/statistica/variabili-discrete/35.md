Five independent checks each succeed with probability $0.8$. What is the probability that all five succeed?

- $5\cdot0.8^5$
- $1-0.2^5$
- $0.2^5$
- $0.8^5$

## Explanation

The required event is the intersection of the five successes. Independence makes its probability the product of the five marginal probabilities. Each factor is $0.8$, giving $(0.8)^5$. For the binomial count this is the mass at $k=5$. The coefficient $\binom55$ is one because there is only one all-success sequence.
