Three independent trials succeed with probability $\dfrac12$. What is the probability of at most one success?

- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac78$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

“At most one success” includes zero and one success. Every sequence of three trials has probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\dfrac18$. There is one sequence without successes and three with one success, one for each possible position of the successful trial. These events are disjoint, so the required probability is $(1+3)\cdot\dfrac18=\dfrac12$.
