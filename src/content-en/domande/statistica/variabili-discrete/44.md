The trial number of the first success is geometric, with success probability $p$ on each independent trial. A log groups consecutive trials into nonoverlapping pairs and records the number of the first pair containing a success. What success probability per pair gives the new geometric distribution?

- $p^2$
- $2p$
- $1-(1-p)^2$
- $p(1-p)$

## Explanation

A pair fails only when both its trials fail, with probability $(1-p)^2$. Different pairs consist of distinct independent trials, so the first successful pair is geometric with parameter $1-(1-p)^2$.
