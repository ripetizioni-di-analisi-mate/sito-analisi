The probability that the first success occurs after the fourth independent trial is $\dfrac{16}{81}$. If the success probability is constant, what is it?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac{16}{81}$

## Explanation

A first success after trial four requires the first four trials all to fail. By independence this initial sequence has probability $(1-p)^4$. The stated value gives $(1-p)^4=\dfrac{16}{81}=\left(\dfrac23\right)^4$. Since $1-p$ is a probability and is nonnegative, $1-p=\dfrac23$. The success parameter is therefore $p=\dfrac13$.
