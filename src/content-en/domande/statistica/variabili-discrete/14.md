Five units are drawn uniformly without replacement from ten, seven of which have a characteristic of interest. What is the smallest possible number of units drawn with the characteristic?

- $0$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

The population contains $10-7=3$ units outside the group of interest. To minimize the number of units of interest in a sample of five, take all three other units. This leaves $5-3=2$ places for units of interest. Such a sample is possible because seven units of interest are available.
