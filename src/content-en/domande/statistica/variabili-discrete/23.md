If $X$ takes the values $-2$, $0$ and $2$ with masses $\dfrac14$, $\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac14$, what is $E[\lvert X\rvert]$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

The required expectation is for $|X|$, which equals $2$ both when $X=-2$ and when $X=2$. These outcomes are disjoint, so $P(|X|=2)=\dfrac14+\dfrac14=\dfrac12$. The other value of $|X|$ is zero, with probability $\dfrac12$. Hence $E[|X|]=2\cdot\dfrac12=1$. Taking the mean of $X$ first would cause the two extreme values to cancel.
