Codes are generated independently and each has the same probability of meeting a criterion. Assuming $0<p<1$, which variable has a geometric distribution on the positive integers?

- The number of acceptable codes among the first twenty.
- The number of codes generated up to and including the first acceptable one.
- The number of rejected codes before the first acceptable one.
- The total number of acceptable codes among twenty distinct codes drawn without replacement.

## Explanation

Call the generation of an acceptable code a success. The trials are independent with a constant success probability $p$. The number $T$ of codes generated up to the first acceptable one therefore has mass $(1-p)^{k-1}p$ at $k\ge1$. The count includes the acceptable code. Counting only rejected codes would give $T-1$, whose support starts at zero.
