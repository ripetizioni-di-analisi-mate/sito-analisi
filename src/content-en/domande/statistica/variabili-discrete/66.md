A population of ten units has four defective units. If all ten are drawn uniformly without replacement, what is the variance of the number of defective units drawn?

- $0$
- $4$
- $2.4$
- $1$

## Explanation

When all ten units are drawn without replacement, each appears exactly once. The order can vary, but the number of defective units in the sample is always $4$. The count equals its mean in every outcome, so the mean squared deviation is zero. The hypergeometric formula also gives zero because the factor $\dfrac{N-n}{N-1}$ has zero numerator.
