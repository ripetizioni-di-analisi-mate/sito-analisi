The cumulative distribution function of $X$ equals $0.3$ immediately to the left of $4$ and $0.8$ at $4$. What is $P(X=4)$?

- $0.8$
- $0.3$
- $0.2$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

The cumulative distribution function at $4$ includes the probability of all values $x\le4$. Its left-hand limit includes only the probability of the values $x<4$. The difference therefore isolates the mass at $4$ and equals $F_X(4)-F_X(4^-)=0.8-0.3=0.5$. The value $0.8$ also includes probability accumulated before $4$.
