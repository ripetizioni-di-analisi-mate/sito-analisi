A nonnegative integer-valued variable satisfies $P(X=0)=0.4$ and $P(X=1)=0.35$. What is the smallest integer $k$ such that $F_X(k)\ge0.5$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Because the variable takes nonnegative integer values, its cumulative distribution function is zero at negative integers. At zero, $F_X(0)=P(X=0)=0.4$, below the threshold $0.5$. At one, add the mass $0.35$, giving $F_X(1)=0.75$. The first integer at which the accumulated probability reaches at least $0.5$ is therefore $1$.
