Four independent trials are performed with success probability $\dfrac34$. What is the probability of more than two successes?

- $\dfrac{27}{64}$
- $\dfrac{189}{256}$
- $\dfrac{81}{256}$
- $\dfrac{243}{256}$

## Explanation

The count is integer-valued and there are four trials, so more than two means three or four. Three successes leave one failure in any of four positions, giving $P(X=3)=4\left(\dfrac34\right)^3\dfrac14=\dfrac{108}{256}$. Four successes give $P(X=4)=\left(\dfrac34\right)^4=\dfrac{81}{256}$. Adding the masses yields $\dfrac{189}{256}$.
