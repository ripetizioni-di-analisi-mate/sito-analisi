Let $X\sim U(0,1)$ and $Y=e^X$. What is the density of $Y$ for $1<y<e$?

- $\dfrac1y$
- $e^y$
- $\log y$
- $1$

## Explanation

The function $e^x$ is strictly increasing and maps the interval $(0,1)$ onto $(1,e)$. Its inverse is $x=\log y$, with derivative $\dfrac1y$. The density of $X$ equals $1$ on $(0,1)$, so the formula for a monotone transformation gives

$$
f_Y(y)=1\cdot\left|\frac{1}{y}\right|=\frac1y,\qquad 1<y<e.
$$

The area is one, because $\displaystyle\int_1^e\frac1y\,dy=\log e-\log 1=1$. The density decreases because the function $e^x$ stretches intervals more and more as $x$ increases.
