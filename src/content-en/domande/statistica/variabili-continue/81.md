Let $X\sim U(-1,2)$ and $Y=X^2$. What is $P(Y\le1)$?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

Condition $X^2\le1$ requires $X$ to lie between the roots $-1$ and $1$. The entire interval is inside the uniform support from $-1$ to $2$. The density is constant, so divide the favorable length, two, by the support length, three.

$$
P(Y\le1)=\frac{1-(-1)}{2-(-1)}=\frac23.
$$

Negative values between $-1$ and $0$ also contribute because their squares are at most one.
