The density of $X$ equals $\dfrac x2$ on $(0,2)$ and zero elsewhere. Calculate its expected value.

- $1$
- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac83$

## Explanation

The expectation weights each value $x$ by its density. On the support $(0,2)$, multiply $x$ by $\dfrac x2$. The density is zero outside the support, so that region contributes nothing to the integral.

$$
E[X]=\int_0^2 \frac{x^2}{2}\,dx
=\left[\frac{x^3}{6}\right]_0^2
=\frac86=\frac43.
$$

The result exceeds the midpoint $1$ because the increasing density gives more weight to values near $2$.
