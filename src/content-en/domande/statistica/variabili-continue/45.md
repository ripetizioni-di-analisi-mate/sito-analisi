A time has an exponential distribution with mean $20\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its rate per minute?

- $3\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$
- $\dfrac13\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$
- $\dfrac1{20}\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$
- $20\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$

## Explanation

The rate is the reciprocal of the mean duration expressed in the chosen time unit. Twenty seconds is $\dfrac{20}{60}=\dfrac13$ of a minute, so

$$
\lambda=\frac{1}{\frac13\,\mathrm{min}}=3\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}.
$$

This ensures that rate times duration is dimensionless. With time measured in seconds, the same model has rate $\dfrac1{20}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$.
