An exponential duration satisfies $P(T>4)=\dfrac12$. What is $P(4<T<8)$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

For the interval between $4$ and $8$, subtract from the tail beyond $4$ all durations longer than $8$. The exponential tail at twice the time is the square of the original tail probability, so $P(T>8)=(\dfrac12)^2=\dfrac14$.

$$
P(4<T<8)=\frac12-\frac14=\frac14.
$$

The endpoints have zero probability. The difference is the unconditional probability of falling in the interval, before conditioning on survival through $4$.
