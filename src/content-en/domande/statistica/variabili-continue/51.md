The lifetime of a light bulb is exponential, and $10\%$ of the bulbs last longer than $3000$ hours. What is the mean lifetime?

- About $1303$ hours
- About $6908$ hours
- About $300$ hours
- $3000$ hours

## Explanation

The exponential survival function gives $P(T>3000)=e^{-3000\lambda}$, which must equal $0.10$. Taking natural logarithms gives $-3000\lambda=\log 0.10=-\log 10$, so $\lambda=\dfrac{\log 10}{3000}$ per hour. The mean lifetime is the reciprocal of the rate.

$$
E[T]=\frac1\lambda=\frac{3000}{\log 10}\approx\frac{3000}{2.3026}\approx1303\ \text{hours}.
$$

The mean is less than $3000$ hours, because only one tenth of the bulbs exceed that threshold.
