A uniform variable has mean $8$ and variance $3$. Find its support.

- $[2,14]$
- $[6,10]$
- $[5,11]$
- $[7,9]$

## Explanation

The length and center determine the support. The mean of a uniform distribution is its center, so the support is symmetric about $8$. Denoting the length by $L$, the variance formula gives

$$
\frac{L^2}{12}=3,\qquad L=\sqrt{36}=6.
$$

Each endpoint lies $3$ from the center. The endpoints are $8-3=5$ and $8+3=11$, so the closed support is $[5,11]$.
