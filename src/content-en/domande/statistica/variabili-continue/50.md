The mean of an exponential waiting time is $6\,\mathrm{h}$. What is its standard deviation?

- $\sqrt6\,\mathrm{h}$
- $6\,\mathrm{h}$
- $36\,\mathrm{h}$
- $\dfrac16\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

The exponential mean is the reciprocal of the rate. A mean of six hours therefore corresponds to rate $\dfrac16\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$. The variance is the squared reciprocal of the rate and equals $36\,\mathrm{h}^2$. Take the square root to express dispersion in hours, giving $\sqrt{36\,\mathrm{h}^2}=6\,\mathrm{h}$. The numerical equality of mean and standard deviation follows from the two formulas.
