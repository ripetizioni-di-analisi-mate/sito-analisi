Let $X$ be exponential with rate $2$ and let $Y=3X$. What distribution does $Y$ have?

- Exponential with rate $6$.
- Exponential with rate $\dfrac23$.
- Exponential with rate $2$.
- Uniform on $(0,3)$.

## Explanation

To identify the transformed distribution, calculate its tail. For $y\ge0$, the condition $3X>y$ is equivalent to $X>\dfrac y3$ because the scale factor is positive. Substitute this threshold in the exponential tail of $X$.

$$
P(Y>y)=P\!\left(X>\frac y3\right)=e^{-2\cdot\frac y3}=e^{-\frac23y}.
$$

The new distribution is exponential with rate $\dfrac23$. Tripling durations triples their mean, so the rate is divided by three.
