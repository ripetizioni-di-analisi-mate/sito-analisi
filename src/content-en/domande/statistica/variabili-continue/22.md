The cumulative distribution function $F_X$ is constant at $0.7$ on $[2,4]$. What is $P(2<X\le4)$?

- $0.3$
- $0$
- $0.7$
- $1$

## Explanation

The value $F_X(2)$ includes all outcomes up to $2$, including any exactly equal to $2$. Subtracting it from $F_X(4)$ leaves exactly the outcomes with $2<X\le4$. The cumulative distribution function is horizontal over the interval, so no further probability accumulates there.

$$
P(2<X\le4)=F_X(4)-F_X(2)=0.7-0.7=0.
$$

The level $0.7$ is the probability already accumulated before this interval.
