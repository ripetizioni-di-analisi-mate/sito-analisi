Let $X\sim U(1,5)$ and $Y=10-X$. What is the closed support of $Y$?

- $[5,9]$
- $[1,5]$
- $[9,13]$
- $[-5,-1]$

## Explanation

For $Y=10-X$, every increase in $X$ produces an equal decrease in $Y$. The smallest value of $Y$ occurs at the right endpoint of the original support, $X=5$, and equals $10-5=5$. The largest occurs at the left endpoint, $X=1$, and equals $10-1=9$. Thus the closed support is $[5,9]$, with the transformed endpoints in increasing order.
