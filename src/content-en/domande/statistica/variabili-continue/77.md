Let $X\sim U(0,2)$ and $Y=4-X$. What is $F_Y(\dfrac52)$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $0$

## Explanation

The transformation $Y=4-X$ is decreasing. Writing $Y\le\dfrac52$ in terms of $X$ gives $4-X\le\dfrac52$, or $X\ge\dfrac32$. The inequality reverses when both sides are multiplied by $-1$. Within the support $(0,2)$, this leaves the interval from $\dfrac32$ to $2$. Its length is $\dfrac12$ and the total length is $2$, so

$$
F_Y\!\left(\frac52\right)=\frac{\frac12}{2}=\frac14.
$$
