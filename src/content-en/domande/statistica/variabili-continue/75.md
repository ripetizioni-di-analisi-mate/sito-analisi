Let $X\sim U(0,1)$ and $Y=\sqrt X$. What is $F_Y(y)$ for $0<y<1$?

- $\sqrt y$
- $y$
- $y^2$
- $1-y^2$

## Explanation

The cumulative distribution function at $y$ is the probability of $\sqrt X\le y$. Since $0<y<1$ and the left side is nonnegative, squaring preserves the inequality. The event is therefore equivalent to $X\le y^2$. The uniform distribution on $(0,1)$ has a cumulative distribution function equal to the threshold in the interior of the support. Since $y^2$ lies in $(0,1)$, this gives $F_Y(y)=y^2$.
