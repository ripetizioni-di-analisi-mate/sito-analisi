A continuous density has a cumulative distribution function that increases strictly around a threshold $s$ with $P(X>s)=0.20$. What is the order of the quantile $s$?

- $0.80$
- $0.20$
- $0.40$
- $0.90$

## Explanation

The order of a quantile is the cumulative probability to its left. If twenty percent lies to the right of $s$, eighty percent lies to its left.

$$
F_X(s)=P(X\le s)=1-P(X>s)=1-0.20=0.80.
$$

Strict increase around $s$ makes this the point at which the function reaches $0.80$. Thus $s$ is the quantile of order $0.80$.
