A variable with a density satisfies $F_X(3)=0.8$ and $F_X(1)=0.2$. What is $P(X<1\text{ or }X>3)$?

- $0.6$
- $0.4$
- $0.2$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

The event contains two disjoint tails. Since $X$ has a density, the mass at $1$ is zero and $P(X<1)=F_X(1)=0.2$. The right tail is the complement of $F_X(3)$, giving $1-0.8=0.2$. Adding the two probabilities gives $0.4$. The difference $F_X(3)-F_X(1)=0.6$ represents the central interval, which the event excludes.
