For a standard normal variable, what is $\Phi(-2)+\Phi(2)$?

- $0$
- $2\Phi(2)$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The standard normal density is symmetric about zero. Reflecting the area to the left of $-2$ gives the area to the right of $2$. This second area is $1-\Phi(2)$ because $\Phi(2)$ accumulates probability up to the threshold. It follows that

$$
\Phi(-2)+\Phi(2)=\bigl(1-\Phi(2)\bigr)+\Phi(2)=1.
$$

Symmetry gives the exact value without requiring the two areas to be known separately.
