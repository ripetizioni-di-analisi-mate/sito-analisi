What shape and rate does the gamma distribution equivalent to $\chi^2_6$ have?

- Shape $6$ and rate $2$.
- Shape $3$ and rate $2$.
- Shape $\dfrac12$ and rate $3$.
- Shape $3$ and rate $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

A chi-square distribution with $\nu$ degrees of freedom is gamma with shape $\dfrac{\nu}{2}$ and rate $\dfrac12$. Substituting $\nu=6$ gives shape $3$ and leaves the rate at $\dfrac12$. The density confirms this because its exponential factor contains $e^{-\frac x2}$. In the scale parameterization the scale is $2$, the reciprocal of the rate.
