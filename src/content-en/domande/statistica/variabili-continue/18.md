A density equals $2(1-x)$ for $0<x<1$ and zero elsewhere. What is the probability that $X>\dfrac12$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The threshold lies inside the support, so the required probability is the area from $\dfrac12$ to $1$. The density decreases linearly from $2\left(1-\dfrac12\right)=1$ to zero. The relevant area is therefore a triangle of base $\dfrac12$ and height $1$.

$$
P\!\left(X>\frac12\right)=\frac{\frac12\cdot1}{2}=\frac14.
$$

The right half of the support contains one quarter of the probability because the density is lower there.
