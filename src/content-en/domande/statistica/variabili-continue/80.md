Let $X$ be a variable with a density and let $Y=2X$. What does the factor $\dfrac12$ represent in the formula $f_Y(y)=\dfrac12f_X(\dfrac y2)$?

- The probability that $X$ is positive.
- The value of the cumulative distribution function at $0$.
- The mean of $X$.
- The derivative of the inverse transformation.

## Explanation

The inverse of $y=2x$ is $g^{-1}(y)=\dfrac y2$. Its derivative is $\dfrac12$ and converts a small length on the $Y$ scale to the corresponding length on the $X$ scale. Thus an interval of length $h$ on the new scale corresponds to one of length $\dfrac h2$ on the original scale. Multiplying the density by this factor preserves area, the probability of the corresponding interval.
