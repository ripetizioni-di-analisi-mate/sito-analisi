A duration is uniform on $(0,8)$ and has already reached $4$. What is the probability that it lasts at least two more units?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

Knowing that the duration has reached $4$ leaves the values between $4$ and $8$. To last two more units the total duration must reach $6$, so the favorable part of the conditioning interval runs from $6$ to $8$. Use the ratio of lengths because the density is constant.

$$
P(T>6\mid T>4)=\frac{8-6}{8-4}=\frac12.
$$

The denominator includes only durations compatible with the information already known.
