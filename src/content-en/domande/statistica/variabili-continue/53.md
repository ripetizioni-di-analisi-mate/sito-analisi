Two models describe a lifetime $T$: one is uniform on $(0,8)$, the other is exponential with mean $4$. The object has survived for $7$ units. Which model gives a positive probability of surviving two additional units?

- Only the exponential model
- Only the uniform model
- Both, with identical probabilities
- Neither model

## Explanation

The requested survival takes the lifetime beyond $9$, outside the uniform support. In the exponential model the residual lifetime remains exponential and the chance of surviving two more units is $e^{-2/4}>0$.
