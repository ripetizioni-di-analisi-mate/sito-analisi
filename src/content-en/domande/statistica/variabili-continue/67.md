A gamma distribution has mean $6$ and variance $3$. Determine the rate.

- $\dfrac12$
- $3$
- $2$
- $12$

## Explanation

The mean and variance of a gamma distribution both depend on its shape $k$. To isolate the rate, divide the mean by the variance so that $k$ cancels.

$$
\frac{E[X]}{\operatorname{Var}(X)}
=\frac{\frac{k}{\lambda}}{\frac{k}{\lambda^2}}
=\lambda.
$$

Substituting the given values gives $\lambda=\dfrac63=2$. With this rate, the mean also requires $k=6\cdot2=12$, which gives variance $\dfrac{12}{2^2}=3$.
