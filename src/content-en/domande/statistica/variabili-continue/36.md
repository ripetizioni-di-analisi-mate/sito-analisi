The density of $X$ equals $2x$ on $(0,1)$. Calculate $E[X^3]$.

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

The third moment is the expectation of $X^3$. In the integral, weight $x^3$ by the density $2x$ on $(0,1)$. The product is $2x^4$, with antiderivative $\dfrac{2x^5}{5}$. Evaluating it at the endpoints gives

$$
E[X^3]=\int_0^1 2x^4\,dx=\frac25.
$$

Cube the values before taking their mean.
