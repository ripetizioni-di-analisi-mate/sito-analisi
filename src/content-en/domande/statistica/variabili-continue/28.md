A cumulative distribution function increases strictly up to $F_X(2)=\dfrac12$, is constant at $\dfrac12$ from $2$ to $5$ and then increases again. What value does the infimum definition of $q_{\frac12}$ give?

- $\dfrac72$
- $5$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$

## Explanation

The quantile is the infimum of the thresholds for which $F_X(x)\ge\dfrac12$. Before $2$ the function is below that level, while at $2$ it reaches it. Every point from $2$ to $5$ attains the same level. The infimum selects the first of these points, so $q_{\frac12}=2$. The midpoint of the horizontal segment plays no role.
