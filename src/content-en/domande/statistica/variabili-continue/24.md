A continuous cumulative distribution function equals $\dfrac{x}{4}$ on $[0,2]$. What is $P(X>2)$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $0$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

By definition, $F_X(2)$ is the probability of a value at most $2$. The requested tail contains all remaining outcomes and is therefore the complement. The value at the boundary of the specified interval is $F_X(2)=\dfrac24=\dfrac12$.

$$
P(X>2)=1-F_X(2)=1-\frac12=\frac12.
$$

Knowing the cumulative distribution function at $2$ suffices to find this tail even though its shape beyond the threshold is unspecified.
