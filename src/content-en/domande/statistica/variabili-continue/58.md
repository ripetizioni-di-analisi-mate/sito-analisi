Let $X\sim N(10,4)$ and $Y\sim N(20,16)$. How do $P(X>12)$ and $P(Y>24)$ compare?

- The first is greater.
- They are equal.
- The second is greater.
- Both equal $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

Compare the probabilities on the standardized scale. For $X$ the standard deviation is $2$, and the threshold $12$ is two units above the mean $10$. For $Y$ the standard deviation is $4$, and the threshold $24$ is four units above the mean $20$. In both cases the deviation is one standard deviation, so both probabilities equal $1-\Phi(1)$.
