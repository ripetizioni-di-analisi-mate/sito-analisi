Let $X\sim U(1,3)$ and $Y=7-2X$. Find the density of $Y$ in the interior of its support.

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The transformation $y=7-2x$ is affine and decreasing. As $x$ increases from $1$ to $3$, the transformed value decreases from $5$ to $1$. The new support is $(1,5)$, with length $4$. Equal-length intervals on the new scale have equal-length preimages on the scale of $X$. The density therefore remains constant. To give area one, it must have height $\dfrac14$.
