A distribution has a strictly positive density throughout its support, which is an interval. How are $q_{0.20}$ and $q_{0.80}$ ordered?

- $q_{0.20}>q_{0.80}$
- $q_{0.20}<q_{0.80}$
- $q_{0.20}=q_{0.80}$
- The order depends on the variance.

## Explanation

Between two distinct interior support points the positive density assigns positive area. The cumulative distribution function therefore increases strictly throughout the interval where it goes from zero to one. The quantiles of orders $0.20$ and $0.80$ lie there. The level $0.80$ is reached at a strictly higher threshold than $0.20$. Hence $q_{0.20}<q_{0.80}$, regardless of the variance.
