Let $X\sim U(3,15)$. Find the distance between the quantiles of orders $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac34$.

- $3$
- $6$
- $9$
- $12$

## Explanation

The uniform cumulative distribution function increases linearly from zero to one over the twelve units between $3$ and $15$. The first quartile is one quarter of the way from $3$, giving $3+3=6$. The third quartile is three quarters of the way, giving $3+9=12$. Their distance is $12-6=6$. The interval between them contains half the probability and spans half the support.
