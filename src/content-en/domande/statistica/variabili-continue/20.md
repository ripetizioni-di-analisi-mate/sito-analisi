A density equals $4$ on $(0,\dfrac14)$ and zero elsewhere. What is $P(0<X<\dfrac18)$?

- $\dfrac18$
- $1$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

A density measures probability area per unit of length. The height $4$ is consistent with normalization because the support has length only $\dfrac14$. The requested segment has the same height and width $\dfrac18$. Its area is therefore

$$
P\!\left(0<X<\frac18\right)=4\cdot\frac18=\frac12.
$$

The probability is half the total area because the requested segment occupies half the base of the rectangle.
