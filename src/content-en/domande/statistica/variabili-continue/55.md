Let $X\sim N(100,64)$. Which threshold for $X$ corresponds to the standardized value $z=-\dfrac12$?

- $104$
- $92$
- $96$
- $68$

## Explanation

Standardization expresses the distance of a threshold from the mean in standard deviations, with its sign. To recover the value of $X$, use $x=\mu+\sigma z$. Here $\sigma=\sqrt{64}=8$ and $z=-\dfrac12$, so

$$
x=100+8\left(-\frac12\right)=96.
$$

Half a standard deviation corresponds to four units on the original scale. The negative sign places the threshold four units below the mean.
