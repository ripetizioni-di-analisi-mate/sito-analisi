Let $X\sim U(-2,6)$. Calculate $P(|X|<3)$.

- $\dfrac58$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The condition $|X|<3$ requires $X$ to lie between $-3$ and $3$. The uniform variable takes values between $-2$ and $6$, so only the intersection $(-2,3)$ contributes. The density is constant across the support, giving a ratio of lengths.

$$
P(|X|<3)=\frac{3-(-2)}{6-(-2)}=\frac58.
$$

The segment from $-3$ to $-2$ falls outside the support.
