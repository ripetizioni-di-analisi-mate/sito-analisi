A density equals $1$ on $(0,1)$. Only its value at $x=\dfrac12$ is changed, from $1$ to $9$. How does $P(\dfrac14<X<\dfrac34)$ change?

- It increases to $1$.
- It decreases to $\dfrac14$.
- It increases by $\dfrac19$.
- It stays at $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

The probability of the interval is the integral of the density between $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac34$. The change alters the function height at a single point. That point has zero length and contributes nothing to the integral. The area remains that of a rectangle of height $1$ and base $\dfrac34-\dfrac14=\dfrac12$, giving $\dfrac12$ again. A change over an interval would have a positive width over which area could accumulate.
