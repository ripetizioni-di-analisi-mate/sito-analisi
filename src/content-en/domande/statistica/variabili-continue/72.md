A Student's t distribution is symmetric and has positive density in a neighborhood of zero. What is its median?

- $1$
- The number of degrees of freedom.
- Half the degrees of freedom.
- $0$

## Explanation

Symmetry of the Student's t density about zero gives equal areas on either side of zero. The center point has probability zero, so the cumulative distribution function equals exactly $\dfrac12$ at $0$. The density is positive around the center, so the cumulative distribution function increases strictly through that level. The median is therefore $0$. The degrees of freedom change the shape of the curve while preserving its center.
