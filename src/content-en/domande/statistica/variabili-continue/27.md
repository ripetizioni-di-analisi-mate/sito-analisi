A proposed cumulative distribution function has $F_X(2)=0.7$ and $F_X(5)=0.6$. Which necessary condition do these values contradict?

- Its values lie in $[0,1]$.
- Its limit at the right is $1$.
- It is nondecreasing.
- It is right-continuous.

## Explanation

Every outcome with $X\le2$ also satisfies $X\le5$ because $2<5$. The second probability therefore includes the entire first probability plus any outcomes between $2$ and $5$. It follows that $F_X(2)\le F_X(5)$, whereas the proposed values give $0.7>0.6$. This violates monotonicity. Both numbers lie between zero and one, but that condition alone is insufficient.
