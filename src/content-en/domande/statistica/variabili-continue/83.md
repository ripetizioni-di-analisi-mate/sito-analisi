Let $X$ be absolutely continuous. What distribution does $Y=g(X)$ have if $g(x)=2$ for every $x$?

- It is uniform on $(0,2)$.
- It has constant density $2$.
- It is concentrated at the point $2$.
- It has a normal distribution with variance $2$.

## Explanation

The transformation returns $2$ for every input. Thus whatever value $X$ takes, event $Y=2$ occurs. All the probability of $Y$ is concentrated at the single point $2$. Its cumulative distribution function is zero before $2$ and one from $2$ onward. The jump shows that the transformed variable has a point mass even though the original variable has a density.
