Let $Z_1,Z_2,Z_3,Z_4$ be independent standard normal variables. Calculate the variance of $Y=\dfrac12(Z_1^2+Z_2^2+Z_3^2+Z_4^2)$.

- $1$
- $4$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

The sum of the squares of four independent standard normal variables is chi-square with four degrees of freedom. Its variance is twice the degrees of freedom, so it equals $8$. The factor $\dfrac12$ multiplies each deviation from the mean. Because variance is the mean squared deviation, the factor must be squared.

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\left(\frac12\right)^2\cdot 8=2.
$$
