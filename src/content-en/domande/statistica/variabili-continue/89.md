Let $X\sim N(6,9)$. What distribution does $Y=1-2X$ have?

- $N(-11,36)$
- $N(-11,18)$
- $N(13,36)$
- $N(-12,36)$

## Explanation

An affine transformation of a normal variable with a nonzero coefficient is again normal. The mean undergoes the same transformation as the values, while the variance is multiplied by the square of the coefficient.

$$
E[Y]=1-2\cdot6=-11,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=(-2)^2\cdot9=36.
$$

The term $1$ shifts the center and leaves deviations unchanged. This gives $Y\sim N(-11,36)$, where the second parameter is the variance.
