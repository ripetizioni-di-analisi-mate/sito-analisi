Let $X\sim N(8,4)$. By symmetry about the mean, which interval has the same probability as $(9,12)$?

- $(5,8)$
- $(4,8)$
- $(4,7)$
- $(6,9)$

## Explanation

Reflecting a value $x$ about the mean $8$ replaces it by $2\cdot8-x$. The endpoint $9$ lies one unit above the mean and reflects to $7$. The endpoint $12$ lies four units above the mean and reflects to $4$. Reflection reverses the order of the endpoints, giving the interval $(4,7)$. The densities at reflected points are equal and reflection preserves lengths, so it preserves area.
