Let $X\sim N(30,16)$. Using $\Phi(1)=0.8413$, calculate $P(X<26)$.

- $0.8413$
- $0.1587$
- $0.3413$
- $0.6826$

## Explanation

The second parameter of $N(30,16)$ is the variance, so the standard deviation is $\sqrt{16}=4$. The threshold $26$ is one standard deviation below the mean because $(26-30)$ equals $-4$. On the standardized scale the limit is $-1$.

$$
P(X<26)=\Phi(-1)=1-\Phi(1)=1-0.8413=0.1587.
$$

The complement follows from symmetry: the area to the left of $-1$ equals the area to the right of $1$.
