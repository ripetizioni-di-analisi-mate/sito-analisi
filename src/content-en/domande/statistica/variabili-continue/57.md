Let $X\sim N(20,4)$. Using $\Phi(1)=0.8413$ and $\Phi(2)=0.9772$, what is $P(22<X<24)$?

- $0.1359$
- $0.1587$
- $0.0228$
- $0.8185$

## Explanation

The standard deviation is $2$. The threshold $22$ is one standard deviation above $20$, while $24$ is two standard deviations above it. Both limits are on the right half of the curve. The cumulative distribution function at $2$ includes the area accumulated through $1$. Subtract the latter to obtain the required band.

$$
P(22<X<24)=\Phi(2)-\Phi(1)
=0.9772-0.8413=0.1359.
$$
