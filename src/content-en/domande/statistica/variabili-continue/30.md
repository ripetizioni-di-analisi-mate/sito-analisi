The cumulative distribution function equals $F_X(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$, zero to the left and one to the right. Determine $q_{\frac9{16}}$.

- $\dfrac9{16}$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac7{16}$
- $\dfrac38$

## Explanation

Find the first threshold where the cumulative distribution function reaches $\dfrac9{16}$. The level lies strictly between zero and one, so the threshold belongs to $[0,1]$, where the function equals $x^2$ and increases strictly.

$$
q^2=\frac9{16},\qquad q=\sqrt{\frac9{16}}=\frac34.
$$

The equation also has a negative root, outside the interval where the expression applies.
