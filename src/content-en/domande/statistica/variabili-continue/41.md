Two intervals lying entirely within the support of a uniform distribution have widths $2$ and $5$, respectively. What is the ratio of the first probability to the second?

- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac52$
- $\dfrac27$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Let $h$ be the constant density height. Both intervals are wholly inside the support, so their probabilities are the areas of rectangles with bases $2$ and $5$, giving $2h$ and $5h$. In the ratio of the first to the second, $h$ cancels, leaving $\dfrac{2h}{5h}=\dfrac25$. The positions of the intervals do not matter as long as both remain in the support.
