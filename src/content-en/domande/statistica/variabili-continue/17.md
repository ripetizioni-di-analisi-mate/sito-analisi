The density of $X$ equals $\dfrac12$ on $(0,1)$ and $k$ on $(1,4)$. It is zero elsewhere. Determine $k$.

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The two pieces of the density form rectangles. From $0$ to $1$ the height is $\dfrac12$, accounting for half the total area. This leaves $\dfrac12$ units of area to spread uniformly from $1$ to $4$, an interval three units long.

$$
\frac12(1-0)+k(4-1)=1,
\qquad 3k=\frac12,
\qquad k=\frac16.
$$

The resulting value is positive, so the second piece also meets the nonnegativity requirement.
