An exponential waiting time satisfies $P(T>5)=\dfrac13$. What is $P(T>10)$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

The exponential tail at time $t$ is $e^{-\lambda t}$. Going from five to ten time units doubles the product of rate and time in the exponent, so the new tail probability is the square of the known one.

$$
P(T>10)=e^{-10\lambda}=(e^{-5\lambda})^2
=\left(\frac13\right)^2=\frac19.
$$

The square corresponds to surviving two consecutive five-unit periods. Given survival through the first, the probability of surviving the second remains $\dfrac13$.
