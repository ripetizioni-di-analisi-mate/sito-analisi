Let $X$ have a density that is symmetric about $0$, that is, $f_X(-x)=f_X(x)$ for every $x$, and let $E[|X|^3]$ be finite. What is the third moment $E[X^3]$?

- $E[|X|^3]$
- $0$
- $\operatorname{Var}(X)$
- $1$

## Explanation

The third moment is the integral of $x^3f_X(x)$ over the whole line. The assumption that $E[|X|^3]$ is finite ensures that this integral exists. The function $x^3$ is odd and the density is even, so the product $x^3f_X(x)$ is odd.

$$
E[X^3]=\int_{-\infty}^{0}x^3f_X(x)\,dx+\int_{0}^{+\infty}x^3f_X(x)\,dx=0.
$$

The substitution $x=-u$ turns the first integral into the negative of the second, so the sum is zero. The opposite values $x$ and $-x$ have the same density and opposite cubes, so their contributions cancel. By contrast, $E[|X|^3]$ is positive, because $|x|^3f_X(x)$ is never negative and has positive area.
