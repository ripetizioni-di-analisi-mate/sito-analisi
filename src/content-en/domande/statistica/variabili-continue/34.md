Let $X$ be symmetric about $0$ and suppose $E[|X|^3]$ is finite and $P(X\ne0)>0$. What is $E[X^3]$?

- $E[|X|^3]$
- $0$
- $\operatorname{Var}(X)$
- $1$

## Explanation

Symmetry about zero means $X$ and $-X$ have the same distribution. The absolute moment assumption ensures that the expectations of both cubes are finite. Since $(-X)^3=-X^3$, the expectation must equal its own negative.

$$
E[X^3]=E[(-X)^3]=-E[X^3].
$$

Hence $E[X^3]=0$. The contributions of opposite values cancel even when the variable takes nonzero values.
