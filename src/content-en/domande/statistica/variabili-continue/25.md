A cumulative distribution function equals $0$ for $x<1$, jumps to $\dfrac25$ at $1$ and is continuous for $x>1$. What is $P(X=1)$?

- $\dfrac25$
- $0$
- $\dfrac35$
- $1$

## Explanation

The left-hand limit of the cumulative distribution function at $1$ counts outcomes strictly less than $1$. Its value at the point also includes outcomes equal to $1$. The difference therefore isolates the point mass.

$$
P(X=1)=F_X(1)-F_X(1^-)=\frac25-0=\frac25.
$$

Continuity beyond $1$ concerns later thresholds. The jump at the given threshold gives the required probability.
