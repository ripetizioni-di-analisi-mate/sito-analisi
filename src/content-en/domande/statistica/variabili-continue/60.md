A normal variable has mean $40$ and standard deviation $5$. What is $P(X\le40)$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The mean $40$ is the axis of symmetry of the normal density. Reflecting one half about this axis gives the other half with the same area. The point $40$ itself has probability zero. The two halves have total area one, so each has area $\dfrac12$, giving $P(X\le40)=\dfrac12$. The standard deviation changes the width of the curve while leaving the probability to the left of the center unchanged.
