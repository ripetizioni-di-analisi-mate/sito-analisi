The cumulative distribution function equals $0$ for $x\le 0$, $\dfrac x3$ for $0<x<3$ and $1$ for $x\ge 3$. Which distribution describes $X$?

- Uniform on $(0,3)$.
- Exponential with rate $\dfrac13$.
- Normal with mean $\dfrac32$.
- Uniform on $(0,1)$.

## Explanation

The cumulative distribution function is flat before zero and after three, so its entire increase from zero to one takes place on $(0,3)$. There its constant slope is $\dfrac13$, and differentiation gives a constant density of that height. A constant density on an interval of length three is precisely the uniform density on $(0,3)$. Continuity at the boundaries rules out endpoint masses.
