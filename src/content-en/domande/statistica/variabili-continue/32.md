The density of $X$ equals $\dfrac12$ on $(-2,-1)$ and on $(3,4)$ and is zero elsewhere. What is $E[X]$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $\dfrac52$
- $1$

## Explanation

Each interval has length $1$ and density $\dfrac12$, and so contains half the probability. Within each interval the density is constant, so the conditional mean is its midpoint. The centers are $-\dfrac32$ for the left interval and $\dfrac72$ for the right. Weighting them by their probabilities gives

$$
E[X]=\frac12\left(-\frac32\right)+\frac12\cdot\frac72=1.
$$

The mean can fall in the gap between the intervals, where the density is zero.
