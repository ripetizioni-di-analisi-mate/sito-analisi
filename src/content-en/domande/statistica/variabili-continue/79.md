Let $X\sim U(0,1)$ and $Y=-\log X$. What is $P(Y>1)$?

- $1-e^{-1}$
- $0$
- $e^{-1}$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Express the event on the scale of $X$. The inequality $-\log X>1$ is equivalent to $\log X<-1$ because both sides are multiplied by $-1$. The exponential function is increasing, giving $X<e^{-1}$. This threshold lies in the support $(0,1)$. For the standard uniform, the probability to the left of the threshold is the length from zero to that threshold, namely $e^{-1}$.
