Which distribution is obtained from a gamma distribution with shape $1$ and rate $3$?

- Normal with variance $3$.
- Exponential with rate $3$.
- Uniform on $(0,3)$.
- Exponential with rate $\dfrac13$.

## Explanation

In the gamma density, shape $k=1$ gives the factor $x^{k-1}=x^0=1$ on the positive support. Since $\Gamma(1)=1$, the normalization constant also simplifies.

$$
\frac{3^1}{\Gamma(1)}x^{1-1}e^{-3x}
=3e^{-3x},\qquad x>0.
$$

This is the exponential density with rate $3$. The reciprocal $\dfrac13$ is the scale parameter, that is, the mean duration.
