Fix $\lambda>1$. Which distribution is obtained from a gamma distribution with shape $1$ and rate $\lambda$?

- Normal with variance $\lambda$.
- Exponential with rate $\lambda$.
- Uniform on $(0,\lambda)$.
- Exponential with rate $\dfrac1\lambda$.

## Explanation

In the gamma density, shape $k=1$ gives the factor $x^{k-1}=x^0=1$ on the positive support. Since $\Gamma(1)=1$, the normalization constant also simplifies.

$$
\frac{\lambda^1}{\Gamma(1)}x^{1-1}e^{-\lambda x}
=\lambda e^{-\lambda x},\qquad x>0.
$$

This is the exponential density with rate $\lambda$. The reciprocal $\dfrac1\lambda$ is the scale, or mean duration.
