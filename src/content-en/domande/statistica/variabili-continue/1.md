The function $f_X$ equals $cx^2$ on $(0,3)$ and zero elsewhere. Determine $c$ so that it is a density.

- $c=\dfrac19$
- $c=\dfrac1{27}$
- $c=\dfrac13$
- $c=\dfrac29$

## Explanation

A density must be nonnegative and have total area one. The function is zero outside $(0,3)$, so its entire area is the integral over that interval. An antiderivative of $x^2$ is $\dfrac{x^3}{3}$. The factor $c$ remains constant during integration.

$$
1=c\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^3=9c.
$$

Dividing by $9$ gives $c=\dfrac19$. This value is positive and the square is nonnegative, so the sign condition is also satisfied.
