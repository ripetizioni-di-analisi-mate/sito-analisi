For an exponential time with rate $\lambda>0$, what is $P(T\le \dfrac1\lambda)$?

- $e^{-1}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1-e^{-1}$
- $1-e^{-2}$

## Explanation

The threshold $\dfrac1\lambda$ is the mean exponential duration. Multiplying it by the rate gives one, so the probability of exceeding it is $e^{-1}$. The question asks for the cumulative probability through the threshold. Take the complement.

$$
P\!\left(T\le\frac1\lambda\right)
=1-e^{-\lambda\cdot\frac1\lambda}=1-e^{-1}.
$$

The value exceeds $\dfrac12$, so more than half of durations in this model are below the mean.
