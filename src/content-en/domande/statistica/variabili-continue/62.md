For a normal variable with mean $100$, the quantile of order $0.90$ is $112$. What is the quantile of order $0.10$?

- $90$
- $88$
- $100$
- $12$

## Explanation

The quantiles of orders $0.10$ and $0.90$ each leave ten percent in their respective outer tails. By symmetry, the two thresholds lie at equal distances on opposite sides of the mean. The threshold $112$ is twelve units above the mean $100$, so the lower threshold is $100-12=88$. Ten percent describes an area. The distance of twelve units comes from the known threshold.
