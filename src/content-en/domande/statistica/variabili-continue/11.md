The cumulative distribution function of $X$ equals $0$ for $x\le0$, $x^3$ for $0<x<1$ and $1$ for $x\ge1$. Calculate $P(\dfrac12<X<1)$.

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac78$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The cumulative distribution function accumulates probability from the left up to the threshold. Subtract the area accumulated through $\dfrac12$ from the area reached at $1$ to obtain the requested interval. Continuity rules out masses at the endpoints, so the open interval has the same probability.

$$
P\!\left(\frac12<X<1\right)
=F_X(1)-F_X\!\left(\frac12\right)
=1-\left(\frac12\right)^3=\frac78.
$$
