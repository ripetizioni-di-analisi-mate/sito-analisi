A density equals $c$ on $(-2,0)$ and $2c$ on $(0,3)$, and is zero elsewhere. What is $P(X>0)$?

- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The negative part of the support has length $2$ and height $c$, giving area $2c$. The positive part has length $3$ and height $2c$, giving area $6c$. The sum must be one, yielding $8c=1$. Event $X>0$ includes the entire rectangle to the right of zero, so its probability is $6c=\dfrac68=\dfrac34$. A ratio of lengths alone would ignore the different heights.
