Let $X\sim U(0,1)$ and $Y=3X$. What is the density of $Y$ for $0<y<3$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac13$
- $3$
- $\dfrac19$

## Explanation

The transformation $Y=3X$ maps endpoints $0$ and $1$ to $0$ and $3$. An interval of length $h$ on the new scale has a preimage of length $\dfrac h3$ on the original scale. Since $X$ is uniform on an interval of length one, that preimage has probability $\dfrac h3$. The transformed density is therefore constant and equals $\dfrac13$ on $(0,3)$. The rectangle has base $3$ and area one.
