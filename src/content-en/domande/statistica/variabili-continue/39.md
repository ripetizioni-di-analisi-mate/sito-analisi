Let $X\sim U(2,10)$. What is $P(X<4\text{ or }X>9)$?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac58$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

For the uniform distribution on $(2,10)$, each unit of length within the support has probability $\dfrac18$. The first inequality gives the interval $(2,4)$ of length $2$. The second gives $(9,10)$ of length $1$. The intervals are disjoint, so add their areas.

$$
P(X<4\text{ or }X>9)=\frac{(4-2)+(10-9)}{10-2}=\frac38.
$$

The central segment between $4$ and $9$ belongs to the complementary event.
