Let $Z\sim N(0,1)$ and $U\sim\chi^2_8$ be independent. What is the distribution of $Z\sqrt{\dfrac8U}$?

- $\chi^2_8$
- $t_8$
- $F_{1,8}$
- $N(0,1)$

## Explanation

The factor $\sqrt{\dfrac8U}$ is the reciprocal of $\sqrt{\dfrac U8}$, so the expression can be written as

$$
\frac{Z}{\sqrt{\frac U8}}.
$$

The numerator is standard normal. The denominator is the square root of a chi-square variable divided by its degrees of freedom, with the division inside the square root. Since $Z$ and $U$ are independent, this defines a Student's t distribution with eight degrees of freedom.
