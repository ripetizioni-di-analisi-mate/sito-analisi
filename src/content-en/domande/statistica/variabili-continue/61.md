For a standard normal variable, a central $98\%$ interval is required. What is the order of the quantile giving the upper endpoint?

- $0.99$
- $0.98$
- $0.02$
- $0.975$

## Explanation

The central interval contains $98\%$ of the probability, leaving $2\%$ across the two tails. By symmetry, each tail contains one percent. The upper endpoint leaves one percent to its right, so its cumulative probability is $1-0.01=0.99$, the required quantile order. The value $98\%$ is the area between the two thresholds.
