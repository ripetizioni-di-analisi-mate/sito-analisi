A gamma distribution has shape $4$ and rate $2$. What is its variance?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Under the parameterization with shape $k$ and rate $\lambda$, the gamma variance is $\dfrac{k}{\lambda^2}$. Here the shape is $4$ and the rate is $2$. The squared rate is $4$, giving

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{4}{2^2}=1.
$$

Dividing by the rate alone would give $2$, the mean in this parameterization. The rate is squared because variance measures squared deviations.
