The density of a time measured in minutes is given as $f_T(t)$. What are the units of $f_T$?

- $\mathrm{min}^{-1}$
- $\mathrm{min}$
- $\mathrm{min}^{-2}$
- It is dimensionless.

## Explanation

A probability is dimensionless, so the integral of $f_T(t)$ over a time interval must be dimensionless. The element $dt$ measures a duration in minutes. Its product with the density must have units of one. Hence $f_T$ has units $\mathrm{min}^{-1}$. For example, a constant height of $\dfrac12\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$ over two minutes produces area one.
