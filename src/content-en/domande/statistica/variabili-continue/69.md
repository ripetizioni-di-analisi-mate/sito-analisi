Let $X\sim\chi^2_7$. Which pair gives the mean and variance, in that order?

- $(7,14)$
- $(14,7)$
- $(7,7)$
- $(7,49)$

## Explanation

A chi-square distribution with $\nu$ degrees of freedom has mean $\nu$ and variance $2\nu$. These also follow from the equivalent gamma distribution, with shape $\dfrac{\nu}{2}$ and rate $\dfrac12$. Dividing the shape by the rate gives $7$, and dividing by the squared rate gives $14$.

$$
(E[X],\operatorname{Var}(X))=(7,14).
$$

The two quantities are listed in the requested order.
