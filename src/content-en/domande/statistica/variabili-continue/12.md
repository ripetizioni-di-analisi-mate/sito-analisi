Let $X\sim U(-1,3)$. Calculate $P(X^2\le 4)$.

- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

A square at most $4$ corresponds to a value of $X$ at distance at most $2$ from zero. The event therefore requires $X$ to lie between $-2$ and $2$. The support starts at $-1$, so only the segment from $-1$ to $2$ contributes. Since the density is uniform, divide the contributing length by the total length.

$$
P(X^2\le 4)=\frac{2-(-1)}{3-(-1)}=\frac34.
$$
