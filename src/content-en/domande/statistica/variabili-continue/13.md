A gamma distribution has shape $3$ and rate $2$. Calculate the second moment $E[X^2]$.

- $3$
- $\dfrac94$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

In the gamma model parameterized by shape $k$ and rate $\lambda$, the mean is $\dfrac{k}{\lambda}$ and the variance is $\dfrac{k}{\lambda^2}$. Substituting $k=3$ and $\lambda=2$ gives $\dfrac32$ and $\dfrac34$, respectively. Isolate $E[X^2]$ in the variance identity to obtain the second moment.

$$
E[X^2]=\operatorname{Var}(X)+E[X]^2
=\frac34+\left(\frac32\right)^2=3.
$$

The squared mean therefore contributes to the second moment along with the variance.
