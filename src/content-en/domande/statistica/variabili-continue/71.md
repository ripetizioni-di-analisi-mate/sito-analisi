Let $U\sim\chi^2_3$ and $V\sim\chi^2_9$ be independent. What is the distribution of $\dfrac{\frac V9}{\frac U3}$?

- $F_{3,9}$
- $t_9$
- $F_{9,3}$
- $\chi^2_{12}$

## Explanation

An F distribution is the ratio of two independent chi-square variables, each divided by its own degrees of freedom. The numerator is $\dfrac V9$, so the first degrees-of-freedom parameter is $9$. The denominator is $\dfrac U3$, so the second is $3$. The expression has distribution $F_{9,3}$. The order matters because interchanging numerator and denominator gives the reciprocal.
