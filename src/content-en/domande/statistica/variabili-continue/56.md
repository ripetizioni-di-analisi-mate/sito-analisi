Let $X\sim N(5,4)$. Using $\Phi(1)\approx0.84134$, calculate $P(3<X<7)$ to four decimal places.

- $0.8413$
- $0.3173$
- $0.1587$
- $0.6827$

## Explanation

The standard deviation is $\sqrt4=2$. The limits $3$ and $7$ are each two units from the mean $5$, so they become $-1$ and $1$ on the standardized scale. To obtain the central area, subtract the cumulative distribution function at $-1$ from its value at $1$. Symmetry gives the first value as $1-\Phi(1)$.

$$
P(3<X<7)=\Phi(1)-\Phi(-1)
\approx 2\cdot0.84134-1=0.68268.
$$

To four decimal places the result is $0.6827$.
