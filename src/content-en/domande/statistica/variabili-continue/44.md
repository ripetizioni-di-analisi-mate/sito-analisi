The variance of a uniform distribution on an interval is $4$. What is the variance if the width is halved around the same center?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

If the support has width $L$, the variance is $\dfrac{L^2}{12}$. Halving the width about the same center halves the distances from the center as well. Variance uses squared deviations, so they become one quarter as large.

$$
\frac{\left(\frac L2\right)^2}{12}=\frac14\cdot\frac{L^2}{12}
=\frac14\cdot4=1.
$$

The mean stays at the specified center. The change is in the dispersion around that center.
