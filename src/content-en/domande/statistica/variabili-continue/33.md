A variable $X$ has mean $2$ and variance $5$. What is $\operatorname{Var}(7-3X)$?

- $45$
- $15$
- $22$
- $52$

## Explanation

To see how the transformation affects dispersion, subtract the new mean. Since $E[7-3X]=7-3\cdot2=1$, the transformed deviation is $7-3X-1=-3(X-2)$. Each deviation is multiplied by $-3$. Squaring removes the sign and leaves a factor of $9$.

$$
\operatorname{Var}(7-3X)=(-3)^2\operatorname{Var}(X)=9\cdot5=45.
$$

The constant term cancels when the mean is subtracted.
