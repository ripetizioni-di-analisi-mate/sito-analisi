A time $T$ has density $\dfrac12e^{-\frac t2}$ for $t>0$. What is $P(T>2)$?

- $\dfrac12e^{-1}$
- $1-e^{-1}$
- $e^{-2}$
- $e^{-1}$

## Explanation

Comparing $\dfrac12e^{-\frac t2}$ with the exponential density $\lambda e^{-\lambda t}$ identifies the rate $\lambda=\dfrac12$. The question asks for the area to the right of $2$, so use the survival function.

$$
P(T>2)=e^{-\lambda\cdot2}=e^{-\frac12\cdot2}=e^{-1}.
$$

The density contains the factor $\dfrac12$. Integrating the tail cancels it with the reciprocal rate in the antiderivative.
