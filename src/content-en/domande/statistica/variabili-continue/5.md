An exponential waiting time has rate $\dfrac13\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$. Find the threshold exceeded with probability $\dfrac18$.

- $9\log 2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\log 2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\log 8}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\log 7\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The given probability is the probability of **exceeding** the threshold. Thus set the exponential tail probability, which at a threshold $q$ in seconds is $e^{-\frac q3}$, equal to $\dfrac18$. Taking the natural logarithm isolates $q$.

$$
-\frac q3=\log\left(\frac18\right)=-\log 8=-3\log 2.
$$

This gives $q=9\log 2\,\mathrm{s}$. The probability to the left of the threshold is $\dfrac78$, so it is the quantile of order $\dfrac78$.
