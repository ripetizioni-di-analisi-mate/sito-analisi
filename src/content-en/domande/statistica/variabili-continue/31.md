A continuous variable has mean $3$ and second moment $13$. Calculate its standard deviation.

- $4$
- $\sqrt{13}$
- $2$
- $\sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

The second moment is the mean of the squared values. To obtain the mean squared deviation from the mean, that is, the variance, subtract the squared mean.

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-E[X]^2=13-3^2=4.
$$

The standard deviation expresses dispersion on the same scale as the values of $X$, so take the positive square root of $4$. The result is $2$.
