A variable with a density takes values only in $(2,6)$. Which value can its mean have?

- $1$
- $6$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Since values lie strictly between $2$ and $6$, both $X-2$ and $6-X$ are positive with probability one. Their expectations are therefore positive, giving $E[X]>2$ and $E[X]<6$. Among the choices, $4$ is the only interior value and is indeed possible. For example, the uniform density on $(2,6)$ has mean $\dfrac{2+6}{2}=4$.
