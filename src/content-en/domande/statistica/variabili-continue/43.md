Let $X\sim U(a,b)$ with $a<b$. The first quartile is $4$ and the third quartile is $10$. What is the mean?

- $6$
- $8$
- $7$
- $5$

## Explanation

The uniform cumulative distribution function has constant slope, so equal increases in quantile order correspond to equal distances along the support. Levels $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac34$ are both $\dfrac14$ from the median level. The thresholds $4$ and $10$ are therefore equidistant from the center, $\dfrac{4+10}{2}=7$. The center of the support is the mean of the uniform distribution.
