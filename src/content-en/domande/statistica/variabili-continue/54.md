Let $X\sim N(18,9)$. Which standardized value corresponds to $X=12$?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-\dfrac23$
- $-6$

## Explanation

In the model $N(18,9)$, the mean is $18$ and the standard deviation is $\sqrt9=3$. A standardized value measures the deviation from the mean in standard deviations. The threshold $12$ is six units below the mean, so

$$
z=\frac{12-18}{3}=-2.
$$

The magnitude $2$ means a distance of two standard deviations. The negative sign places the value on the left side of the normal curve.
