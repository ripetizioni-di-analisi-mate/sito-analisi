Of $50$ documents, $21$ are signed, $15$ are registered and $6$ are both signed and registered. How many are signed but not registered?

- $21$
- $15$
- $30$
- $24$

## Explanation

The $21$ signed documents split into two disjoint groups. One contains the $6$ signed and registered documents. The other contains the signed documents that are unregistered, so it has $21-6=15$ documents. The total number of registered documents also includes unsigned documents, which already fall outside the $21$ signed documents and do not enter the subtraction.
