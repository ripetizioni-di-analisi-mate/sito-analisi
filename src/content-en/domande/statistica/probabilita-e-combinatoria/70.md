An ordered pair $(x,y)$ with $x,y\in\{1,2,3\}$ is chosen uniformly at random. What is the probability that $x+y$ is even?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The sum is even when both numbers are odd or both are even. The two odd numbers are $1$ and $3$, so the first case gives $2\cdot2=4$ ordered pairs. The only even number is $2$, giving just the pair $(2,2)$. Five of the $3\cdot3=9$ equally likely pairs are favorable, so the probability is $\dfrac59$.
