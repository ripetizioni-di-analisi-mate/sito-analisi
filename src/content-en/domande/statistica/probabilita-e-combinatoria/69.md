Three uniform, independent draws with replacement are made from an urn containing one blue ball and two red balls. Which expression gives the probability of drawing at least one blue ball?

- $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3$
- $1-\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3$
- $3\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^2$
- $1-\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^3$

## Explanation

The complement of “at least one blue ball” is “all red.” Replacement leaves two red balls out of three at each trial, and independence allows us to multiply the three probabilities. The all-red sequence has probability $\left(\dfrac23\right)^3=\dfrac8{27}$. Subtracting it from $1$ gives $1-\left(\dfrac23\right)^3=\dfrac{19}{27}$ and includes every possible positive number of blue draws.
