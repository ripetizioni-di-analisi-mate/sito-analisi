Three uniform, independent draws with replacement are made from an urn containing one blue ball and two red balls. Which expression gives the probability of drawing at least one blue ball?

- $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3$
- $1-\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3$
- $3\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^2$
- $1-\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^3$

## Explanation

The complement of “at least one blue ball” is “all red.” Label the balls $B$, $R_1$ and $R_2$. With replacement, each draw chooses uniformly among the same three balls and the draws are independent, so the $3^3=27$ sequences of three draws are equally likely. There are $2^3=8$ all-red sequences, so the event “all red” has probability $\dfrac{8}{27}=\left(\dfrac23\right)^3$. By the complement rule, the probability of at least one blue ball is $1-\left(\dfrac23\right)^3=\dfrac{19}{27}$, which covers the sequences with one, two or three blue balls.
