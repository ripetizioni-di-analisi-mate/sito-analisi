An urn contains one blue and two red balls. Draws are made with replacement. A game stops at the first blue or, if no blue has appeared, after the second draw. Every draw up to the stopping point is recorded. What is the ratio of the probability of recording only blue to the probability of recording red followed by blue?

- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac32$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

“Blue only” has probability $1/3$. “Red, blue” requires a red followed by an independent blue and has probability $(2/3)(1/3)=2/9$. The probabilities of the two terminal paths therefore have ratio $(1/3)/(2/9)=3/2$.
