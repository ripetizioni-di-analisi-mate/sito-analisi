A die with faces numbered from $1$ to $6$ is rolled. A coin is then tossed only if the number rolled is even. The outcome of every roll or toss is recorded. How many elementary outcomes are in the sample space?

- $6$
- $12$
- $9$
- $8$

## Explanation

An odd number ends the experiment, so the three odd numbers give three elementary outcomes. Each even number leads to a coin toss with two possible results. The three even numbers therefore give $3\cdot2=6$ complete outcomes. Since each outcome records the initial number, the groups are disjoint and their counts sum to $3+6=9$ outcomes.
