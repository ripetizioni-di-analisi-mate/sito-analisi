Four distinct folders are arranged in a row. In how many arrangements are folders A and B nonadjacent?

- $18$
- $12$
- $6$
- $24$

## Explanation

Without restrictions, the four distinct folders can be arranged in $4!=24$ ways. When A and B are adjacent, treat them as a block and arrange it with the other two folders in $3!=6$ ways. The block can have internal order AB or BA, giving $6\cdot2=12$ rows with A and B adjacent. The required rows are the remaining $24-12=12$.
