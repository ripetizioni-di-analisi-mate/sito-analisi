Four projects must be selected from eight distinct projects. One particular project is excluded. How many groups can be selected?

- $70$
- $56$
- $35$
- $840$

## Explanation

Excluding one project from the outset leaves seven eligible projects. Selecting four of them identifies the same group however we list its members, so use the binomial coefficient $\binom74$. Equivalently, choose the three projects to leave out from the remaining seven, which gives $\binom73=\dfrac{7\cdot6\cdot5}{3\cdot2\cdot1}=35$.
