Five distinct proposals are ranked from first to fifth. Only the first and second places are published. How many complete rankings correspond to the same published result?

- $6$
- $120$
- $20$
- $10$

## Explanation

The published result fixes both the first-place proposal and the second-place proposal. Three distinct proposals and three distinct positions, third through fifth, remain. There are three choices for third place, two for fourth and one for fifth. This gives $3\cdot2\cdot1=3!=6$ rankings with the published top two.
