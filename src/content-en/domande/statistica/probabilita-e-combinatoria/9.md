A fair coin is tossed independently three times. Calculate the probability of at least one head.

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The complement of “at least one head” is the single sequence of three tails, $CCC$. In three independent tosses of a fair coin the $2^3=8$ sequences of results are equally likely, so the sequence $CCC$ has probability $\dfrac18$. By the complement rule, the required probability is $1-\dfrac18=\dfrac78$. Using the complement covers the sequences with one, two or three heads in a single calculation.
