A fair coin is tossed independently three times. Calculate the probability of at least one head.

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The complement of “at least one head” is the single sequence of three tails. The coin is fair and the tosses are independent, so that sequence has probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\dfrac18$. Subtracting it from $1$ gives $\dfrac78$. Using the complement accounts for all sequences with one, two or three heads in a single calculation.
