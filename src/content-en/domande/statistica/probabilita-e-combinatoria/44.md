How many orders of five distinct signals place A, B and C in that relative order, whether or not they are consecutive?

- $24$
- $6$
- $60$
- $20$

## Explanation

The three positions for A, B and C can be chosen in $\binom53=10$ ways. Once the positions are fixed, inserting A, B and C in that order gives just one arrangement of these three signals. The other two signals can switch places in the remaining positions. These choices determine the full row, so the total is $10\cdot2!=20$.
