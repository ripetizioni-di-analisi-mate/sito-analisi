Six distinct photos are arranged in a row. Three particular photos must form a consecutive block in a prescribed order. How many arrangements are possible?

- $24$
- $120$
- $36$
- $144$

## Explanation

Treat the three specified photos as a block because they occupy consecutive positions. The block and the three individual photos form four distinct units, which can be arranged in $4!=24$ ways. The internal order of the block is fixed, so each arrangement of the units determines one row of six photos. Multiplying by $3!$ would include internal orders excluded by the problem.
