Six distinct plaques are arranged in a row. Two particular plaques must occupy the two ends, in either order. Count the arrangements.

- $72$
- $48$
- $120$
- $24$

## Explanation

Choose which of the two specified plaques goes at the first end. The other must go at the opposite end. For each of these two choices, the four remaining plaques can occupy the middle positions in $4!=24$ ways. These two decisions determine the whole row and give $2\cdot24=48$ arrangements.
