Four tiles are drawn uniformly at random without replacement from nine distinct tiles, four of them marked. Which expression gives the probability of drawing exactly three marked tiles?

- $\dfrac{\binom{4}{3}\binom{5}{1}}{\binom{9}{3}}$
- $\dfrac{\binom{4}{3}\binom{5}{1}}{\binom{9}{4}}$
- $\dfrac{\binom{4}{3}}{\binom{9}{4}}$
- $\dfrac{\binom{5}{3}\binom{4}{1}}{\binom{9}{4}}$

## Explanation

The four-tile samples are equally likely and there are $\binom94$ of them. For a favorable sample choose three of the four marked tiles and one of the five unmarked tiles. The choices come from different groups, so multiply the counts. The ratio is $\dfrac{\binom43\binom51}{\binom94}=\dfrac{20}{126}=\dfrac{10}{63}$. Drawing all four marked tiles would be a different event.
