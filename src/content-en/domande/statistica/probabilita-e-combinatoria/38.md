An application is chosen uniformly at random from a set of $40$. Twelve belong to A, fourteen to B and four to both. What is the probability that the application belongs to just one group?

- $\dfrac{13}{20}$
- $\dfrac{11}{20}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

Remove the four common applications from each group to obtain the exclusive parts. There are $12-4=8$ applications in A alone and $14-4=10$ in B alone. The regions are disjoint, giving $8+10=18$ favorable applications. The selection is uniform among forty applications, so the probability is $\dfrac{18}{40}=\dfrac9{20}$.
