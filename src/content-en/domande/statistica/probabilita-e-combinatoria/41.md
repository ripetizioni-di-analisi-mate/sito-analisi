A card is drawn uniformly at random from a standard $52$-card deck. What is the probability that it is a face card (jack, queen or king) or a heart?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{13}$
- $\dfrac{11}{26}$
- $\dfrac{25}{52}$

## Explanation

Each suit contains three face cards, so the deck has $4\cdot3=12$ face cards. There are thirteen hearts, three of which are also face cards. Adding $12$ and $13$ counts these three cards twice. Subtract them once to obtain $12+13-3=22$ favorable cards. The draw is uniform, so the probability is $\dfrac{22}{52}=\dfrac{11}{26}$.
