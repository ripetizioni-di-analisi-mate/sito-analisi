Two tiles are drawn uniformly at random without replacement from four green tiles and three white tiles. What is the probability that they have the same color?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$

## Explanation

All two-tile samples are equally likely under uniform sampling without replacement. For the colors to match, draw two green tiles or two white tiles, possible in $\binom42=6$ and $\binom32=3$ ways, respectively. These cases are disjoint. Of $\binom72=21$ pairs in total, $6+3=9$ are favorable, giving probability $\dfrac9{21}=\dfrac37$.
