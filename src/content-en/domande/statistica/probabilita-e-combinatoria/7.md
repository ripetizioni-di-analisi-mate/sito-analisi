A code consists of three distinct digits chosen from $0,1,2,3,4,5$. The first digit is nonzero and the last is even. Calculate the number of codes.

- $60$
- $48$
- $40$
- $52$

## Explanation

The number of choices for the first digit depends on the last. With zero at the end, there are five choices for the first digit and four for the middle one, giving $20$. With $2$ or $4$ at the end, there are four nonzero choices for the first digit and again four choices for the middle one. The cases are disjoint, so the total is $20+2\cdot16=52$.
