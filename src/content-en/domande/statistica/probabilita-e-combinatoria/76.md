Two cards are drawn uniformly at random without replacement from a box containing six cards numbered from $1$ to $6$. What is the probability that the two numbers differ by exactly one?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The event depends only on the two numbers drawn, so use unordered pairs. Their absolute difference is one when the larger number is the successor of the smaller. The smaller can be any of the five numbers from $1$ to $5$, giving five favorable pairs. The $\binom62=15$ pairs are equally likely, so the probability is $\dfrac5{15}=\dfrac13$.
