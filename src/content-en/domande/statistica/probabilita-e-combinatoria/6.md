Five symbols, two As, two Bs and one C, are rearranged. Calculate the number of distinct strings.

- $10$
- $60$
- $120$
- $30$

## Explanation

First choose the two positions for the As in $\binom52=10$ ways. Choose two of the three remaining positions for the Bs in $\binom32=3$ ways. The position of C is then determined. Each string identifies exactly one such pair of subsets of positions, so the product counts $10\cdot3=30$ strings without duplication.
