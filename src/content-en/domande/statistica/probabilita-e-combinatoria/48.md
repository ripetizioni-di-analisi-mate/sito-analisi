Five runners finish a race without ties. How many ordered podium finishes include both Ada and Bruno?

- $60$
- $18$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

Two podium places must go to Ada and Bruno, so the third person is chosen from the other three runners. For each choice, the three distinct runners can occupy the podium positions in $3!=6$ ways. Different choices for the third person give disjoint groups of podium finishes. The required number is therefore $3\cdot6=18$.
