A spinner gives the outcomes $A,B,C,D$ with probabilities $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{4}$, $\dfrac{1}{8}$ and $\dfrac{1}{8}$, respectively. Calculate the probability of event $\{B,C,D\}$.

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The three outcomes $B,C,D$ are distinct and thus mutually exclusive. The event contains all three, so by additivity its probability is $\dfrac14+\dfrac18+\dfrac18=\dfrac12$. Equivalently, the only outcome outside the event is $A$, with probability $\dfrac12$. The three favorable outcomes have different probabilities, so add their assigned values.
