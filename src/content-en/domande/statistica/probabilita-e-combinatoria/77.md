A game gives outcomes $A,B,C$ with probabilities $p$, $p$ and $1-2p$. What restriction on $p$ makes this a valid model?

- $0\le p\le\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\le p\le 1$
- $p=\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $0\le p\le 1$

## Explanation

The three probabilities sum to $p+p+1-2p=1$ for any $p$. Each probability must also be nonnegative. The first two require $p\ge0$, and the third requires $1-2p\ge0$, or $p\le\dfrac12$. The interval is therefore $0\le p\le\dfrac12$. At the endpoints some probabilities are zero, which the axioms permit.
