A string uses two As and three Bs, with identical copies of each letter. How many strings have nonadjacent As?

- $6$
- $4$
- $10$
- $3$

## Explanation

Write the three Bs first. They form four gaps for the As: one before the first B, two between consecutive Bs and one after the last. Putting one A in each of two distinct gaps ensures that at least one B separates the As. Every permitted string determines the pair of gaps used, so the count is $\binom42=6$.
