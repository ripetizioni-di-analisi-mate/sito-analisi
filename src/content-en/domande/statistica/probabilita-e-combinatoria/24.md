Five people are chosen from eight. Which quantity counts the same selections by considering the people left out?

- $\binom{8}{3}$
- $\binom{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8!}{3!}$
- $8^3$

## Explanation

Each group of five determines the three people left out. Conversely, naming the three excluded people identifies exactly one group of five, made up of everyone else. This correspondence lets us count the groups using unordered three-person subsets. The result is $\binom83$, equal to $\binom85$ precisely because the two descriptions identify the same selections.
