Two fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability that the first result is strictly greater than the second?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$

## Explanation

Of $6^2=36$ ordered pairs, six are doubles and fail the strict inequality. Match the thirty remaining outcomes in pairs by swapping their values. In each matched pair, one outcome has a larger first value and the other a larger second value. There are therefore $\dfrac{30}{2}=15$ favorable outcomes. All outcomes are equally likely, so the probability is $\dfrac{15}{36}=\dfrac5{12}$.
