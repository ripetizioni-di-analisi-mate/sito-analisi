A system issues alarm $A$ with probability $0.40$ and alarm $B$ with probability $0.30$. Which value is impossible for $P(A\cup B)$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The probability of the union is $P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$. The intersection has nonnegative probability, so the largest possible probability for the union is $0.40+0.30=0.70$, attained when the alarms are disjoint. The value $\dfrac45=0.80$ exceeds that maximum. It would require the overlap to have negative probability, which violates the axioms.
