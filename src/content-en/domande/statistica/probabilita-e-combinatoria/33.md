A key has two letters chosen from $A,B,C$ followed by two digits chosen from $0,1$. The letters must differ and the digits must match. How many keys are allowed?

- $6$
- $18$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

The letters occupy two different positions. There are three choices for the first and two for the second because the letter already used is excluded. The digit pair is determined by a single choice between $0$ and $1$, giving $00$ or $11$. Each of the $3\cdot2=6$ letter pairs allows two endings, yielding $6\cdot2=12$ keys.
