A subset is chosen from seven elements. How many subsets have cardinality zero?

- $7$
- $0$
- $128$
- $1$

## Explanation

A subset of cardinality zero contains exactly zero elements. The choice therefore consists of leaving out all seven elements. Any selection that includes at least one has positive cardinality. The empty set is the only subset with no elements and is itself a well-defined choice. Hence the binomial coefficient equals $\binom70=1$.
