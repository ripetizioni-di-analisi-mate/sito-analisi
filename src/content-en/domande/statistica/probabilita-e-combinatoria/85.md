A subset is chosen from seven elements. How many subsets contain no elements?

- $7$
- $0$
- $128$
- $1$

## Explanation

A subset with no elements is obtained by leaving out all seven elements. Any selection that includes at least one element contains at least one element. The empty set is therefore the only subset with no elements, and it is a well-defined choice. Hence the binomial coefficient equals $\binom70=\dfrac{7!}{0!\,7!}=1$.
