Four objects are drawn without replacement from seven objects, five red and two blue. What is the smallest possible number of red objects in the draw?

- $0$
- $3$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

To minimize the number of red objects, draw as many blue objects as possible. There are only two blue objects and there is no replacement, so at most two of the four objects can be blue. The other two must be red. Drawing both blue objects and two of the five red ones attains the bound, so the minimum is $2$.
