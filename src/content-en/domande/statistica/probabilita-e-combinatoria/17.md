Two events satisfy $A\subseteq B$ and $P(B)=0.4$. Which value is possible for $P(A)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$

## Explanation

Since $A\subseteq B$, event $B$ splits into the disjoint events $A$ and $B\setminus A$. It follows that $P(B)=P(A)+P(B\setminus A)$ and hence $0\le P(A)\le0.4$. The value $\dfrac15=0.2$ lies in this interval. It can be attained by assigning probability $0.2$ both to $A$ and to the rest of $B$, and probability $0.6$ to the region outside.
