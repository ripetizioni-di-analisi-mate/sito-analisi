Five people greet each other, with one greeting per pair. Which count gives the number of greetings?

- $2^5$
- $\binom{5}{2}$
- $5!$
- $5\cdot4$

## Explanation

Each greeting involves two distinct people. The pair Anna and Bruno is the same pair when named in the opposite order, so count two-person subsets. The required count is $\binom52$. Counting $5\cdot4$ ordered pairs and dividing by two gives the same result, $10$, because each greeting appears in two orders.
