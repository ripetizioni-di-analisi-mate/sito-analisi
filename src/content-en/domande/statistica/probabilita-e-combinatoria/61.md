Three items are drawn uniformly at random without replacement from six nondefective items and two defective ones. Which expression gives the probability that all three items are nondefective?

- $\dfrac{\binom{6}{3}}{\binom{8}{3}}$
- $\dfrac{\binom{6}{2}}{\binom{8}{3}}$
- $\left(\dfrac{6}{8}\right)^3$
- $\dfrac{\binom{2}{1}\binom{6}{2}}{\binom{8}{3}}$

## Explanation

There are eight distinct items and all three-item samples are equally likely. The denominator is therefore $\binom83=56$. For the numerator, count the samples containing only nondefective items: three of the six nondefective items can be chosen in $\binom63=20$ ways. This gives $\dfrac{\binom63}{\binom83}=\dfrac5{14}$. Both numerator and denominator count unordered samples, so each three-item group is counted once.
