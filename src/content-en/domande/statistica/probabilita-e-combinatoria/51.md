A fair die is rolled. Only if the result is even is a fair coin tossed independently. The entire sequence is recorded. Which statement about the probabilities of individual outcomes is correct?

- The outcome “$2$ then heads” has the same probability as “$3$.”
- All recorded outcomes are equally likely.
- The outcome “$1$” is twice as likely as “$2$ then heads.”
- The outcome “$1$” is half as likely as “$2$ then heads.”

## Explanation

When the die shows $1$ the experiment ends, so this outcome has probability $\dfrac16$. The outcome “$2$ then heads” requires two successive events: rolling $2$ on the die and getting heads on the coin. Independence gives $\dfrac16\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac1{12}$. The outcome that ends after the die roll is therefore twice as likely as the one that also specifies the coin toss.
