Three fair dice are rolled independently. What is the probability that the three values are all different?

- $\dfrac{5}{54}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

An outcome is the ordered sequence of the three values, with repetition allowed, so the outcomes are the $6^3=216$ ordered selections with repetition. They are equally likely because the dice are fair and rolled independently. The sequences with three different values are the ordered selections without repetition, $D_{6,3}=6\cdot5\cdot4=120$. The probability is therefore $\dfrac{120}{216}=\dfrac59$. Counting only the sets of three values, $\binom63=20$, would give $\dfrac{20}{216}=\dfrac5{54}$, because the numerator would ignore order while the denominator takes it into account.
