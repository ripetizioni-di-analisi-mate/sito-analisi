A device assigns two distinct keys, one for entry and one for exit, from a set of five keys. Which expression counts the assignments?

- $5!$
- $5\cdot4$
- $5^2$
- $\binom{5}{2}$

## Explanation

There are five choices for the entry key. Once it is chosen, the exit key must differ and can be any of the other four. The roles differ, so switching the entry and exit keys gives another assignment. The multiplication principle gives $5\cdot4=20$ assignments, with no division by two.
