A chair and a secretary are chosen from seven candidates. The two positions must go to different people, and Anna cannot be chair. Count the assignments.

- $36$
- $30$
- $21$
- $42$

## Explanation

Choose the chair first. The restriction on Anna leaves six candidates. Once the chair is chosen, the secretary can be any of the other six people, including Anna. The choices are ordered because the positions are different. The multiplication principle gives $6\cdot6=36$ assignments, one for each pair formed this way.
