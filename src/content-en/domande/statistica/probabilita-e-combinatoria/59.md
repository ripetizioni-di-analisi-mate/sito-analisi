The letters of PAPPA are rearranged. Which expression counts the distinct strings that begin with A?

- $2\cdot\dfrac{4!}{3!}$
- $4!$
- $\dfrac{5!}{3!\,2!}$
- $\dfrac{4!}{3!}$

## Explanation

With one A fixed first, arrange three Ps and one A in the remaining four positions. Labeling the Ps would give $4!$ arrangements. Each visible string would then have $3!$ labeled versions, obtained by exchanging the P labels among their three positions. This gives $\dfrac{4!}{3!}=4$, the same result as choosing the position of the second A.
