A register contains the identifiers $1,2,3,4,5,6$. Let $A=\{1,3,5\}$ and $B=\{3,4,5\}$. Which set is the complement of their union?

- $\{1,2,4,6\}$
- $\{2,6\}$
- $\{3,5\}$
- $\{1,4\}$

## Explanation

The union contains every identifier that appears in at least one of the two sets. Combining their elements gives $A\cup B=\{1,3,4,5\}$. Take the complement relative to the six identifiers in the register. Removing the members of the union leaves $2$ and $6$. In particular, $3$ and $5$, which appear in both sets, also belong to the union and are excluded from the complement.
