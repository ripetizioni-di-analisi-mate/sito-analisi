In a finite space, five disjoint events with positive probability form a partition of $\Omega$. How do you find the probability of the union of three cells?

- Add all the probabilities in the partition.
- Multiply the probabilities of the three cells.
- Divide the sum of the three cell probabilities by three.
- Add the probabilities of the three selected cells.

## Explanation

A partition divides the space into disjoint cells. Every outcome in the specified union therefore belongs to exactly one of the three selected cells. By additivity, add the three probabilities. Positive probability for all five cells does not imply that they are equally likely. Dividing the sum of the three probabilities by three would give their average, which is smaller than the probability of the union.
