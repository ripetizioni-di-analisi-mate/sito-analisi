An integer is selected uniformly at random from $1$ to $20$. What is the probability that it is divisible by both $4$ and $6$?

- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Divisibility by both $4=2^2$ and $6=2\cdot3$ requires all the factors of $2^2\cdot3=12$. The favorable numbers are therefore the multiples of $12$ between $1$ and $20$. The only one is $12$ because the next is $24$. With uniform selection, one favorable outcome among twenty gives probability $\dfrac1{20}$.
