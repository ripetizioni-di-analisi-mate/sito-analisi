The outcomes of three trials are recorded, each as S or F. Which outcome belongs to the event “exactly one S and an F on the last trial”?

- $\mathrm{FSF}$
- $\mathrm{FFS}$
- $\mathrm{FFF}$
- $\mathrm{SSF}$

## Explanation

The last trial is fixed at F, so the only S can be in the first or second position. The remaining trial must give F. The event therefore contains the two sequences $\mathrm{SFF}$ and $\mathrm{FSF}$. Among the listed outcomes, $\mathrm{FSF}$ has exactly one S, in the second position, and an F in the third.
