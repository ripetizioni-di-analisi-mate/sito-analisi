Three of nine candidates are chosen for a team. Marco must be included and Sara is unavailable. Count the possible teams.

- $28$
- $42$
- $35$
- $21$

## Explanation

Reserve one place for Marco and exclude Sara. This leaves seven candidates for the two open places. The team has no assigned roles, so count each pair only once. There are $\binom72=\dfrac{7\cdot6}{2}=21$ possibilities. Dividing by two accounts for the two orders in which the same pair can be chosen.
