The union of three disjoint categories has probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$. The first two categories have probabilities $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{8}$. What is the probability of the third category?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Since the categories are disjoint, the probability of their union is the sum of their three probabilities. Subtract the first two probabilities from the total to find the third. Writing everything in eighths gives $\dfrac34-\dfrac14-\dfrac18=\dfrac68-\dfrac28-\dfrac18=\dfrac38$. Direct subtraction works because the categories share no outcomes.
