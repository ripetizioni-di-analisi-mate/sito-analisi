Three objects are drawn uniformly at random without replacement from four red, three blue and two green objects. What is the probability of drawing one object of each color?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$

## Explanation

The unordered three-object samples are equally likely because each corresponds to $3!$ draw orders. There are $\binom93=84$ samples in total. Each favorable sample is determined by one red, one blue and one green object, which can be chosen in $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ ways. The ratio is $\dfrac{24}{84}=\dfrac27$. The numerator counts each sample containing all three colors once.
