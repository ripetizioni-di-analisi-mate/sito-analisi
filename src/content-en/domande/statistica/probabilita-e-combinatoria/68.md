One item is selected uniformly at random from a batch of $30$. Eight items have a shape defect, $11$ have a surface defect and $17$ have at least one of the two defects. How many have both?

- $19$
- $11$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

Adding the eight items with shape defects to the eleven with surface defects gives nineteen instances of group membership. An item with both defects appears twice in that sum and once among the seventeen with at least one defect. The difference $19-17=2$ counts precisely those items. The batch size of thirty would be needed to convert the count to a probability.
