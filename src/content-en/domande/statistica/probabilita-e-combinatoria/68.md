In a class of $30$ students, $8$ play an instrument, $11$ play a sport and $2$ do both. A student is chosen uniformly at random. What is the probability that the student does neither activity?

- $\dfrac{11}{30}$
- $\dfrac{17}{30}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{13}{30}$

## Explanation

By the inclusion–exclusion formula applied to counts, the number of students doing at least one activity is $8+11-2=17$, because the two students who do both appear among the $8$ and among the $11$. The students who do neither activity form the complement, so there are $30-17=13$ of them. Every student has the same probability of being chosen, so the required probability is $\dfrac{13}{30}$. Subtracting only $8+11=19$ from $30$ would give $\dfrac{11}{30}$, because the two students doing both activities would be removed twice.
