Three items are drawn uniformly at random without replacement from a batch of seven distinct items, two of them defective. Calculate the probability that both defective items are drawn.

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$

## Explanation

Every three-item subset is equally likely because, in uniform sampling without replacement, each corresponds to $3!$ equally likely ordered draws. There are $\binom73=35$ possible subsets. A favorable subset contains the two specified defective items and one of the five nondefective items. There are therefore five favorable subsets and the probability is $\dfrac5{35}=\dfrac17$.
