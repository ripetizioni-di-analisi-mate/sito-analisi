Of $100$ reservations, $25$ include an overnight stay, $40$ include breakfast and $55$ include at least one of the two services. How many include only breakfast?

- $10$
- $40$
- $30$
- $15$

## Explanation

Adding the $25$ reservations with an overnight stay and the $40$ with breakfast counts those with both services twice. Since the union contains $55$ reservations, $25+40-55=10$ include both. Subtract these ten from the forty with breakfast to obtain $40-10=30$ reservations with breakfast only. Reservations with neither service fall outside both groups.
