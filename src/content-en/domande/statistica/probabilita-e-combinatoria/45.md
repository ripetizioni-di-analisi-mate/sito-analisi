Three indistinguishable red flags and two indistinguishable white flags are arranged in a row. How many arrangements have a white flag at each end?

- $1$
- $2$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

The two ends must be white, using both white flags. The three middle positions must therefore be red. The only color sequence is white, red, red, red, white. Swapping flags of the same color leaves the arrangement unchanged. Since the color in every position is fixed, there is just one arrangement.
