Six distinct exams are to be divided among three graders, Anna, Bruno and Carla, so that each grades exactly two. How many divisions are possible?

- $720$
- $15$
- $360$
- $90$

## Explanation

Line up the six exams, from the first to the sixth, and write under each the initial of its grader. Each division corresponds to exactly one six-letter word with two As, two Bs and two Cs. By the formula for permutations with indistinguishable objects, there are $\dfrac{6!}{2!\,2!\,2!}=\dfrac{720}{8}=90$ such words. The same number is obtained by choosing Anna's two exams from six in $\binom62=15$ ways and then Bruno's from the remaining four in $\binom42=6$ ways. Carla's two exams are the ones left, so there are $15\cdot6=90$ divisions.
