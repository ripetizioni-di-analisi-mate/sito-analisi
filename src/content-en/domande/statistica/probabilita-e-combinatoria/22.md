How many distinct rows can be formed from the symbols X, X, X, Y, Z, Z?

- $120$
- $720$
- $180$
- $60$

## Explanation

Temporarily labeling the three Xs and the two Zs makes all six symbols distinct, giving $6!$ rows. For each visible row, the X labels can be permuted in $3!$ ways and the Z labels in $2!$ ways. Each row therefore appears $3!\cdot2!$ times in the labeled count, giving $\dfrac{6!}{3!\,2!}=60$.
