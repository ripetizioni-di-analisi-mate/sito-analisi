A fair die has six faces numbered from $1$ to $6$. Let $A$ be the event “the result is odd” and $B$ the event “the result is greater than $3$.” Find the set of outcomes in which $B$ occurs and $A$ does not.

- $\{1,3,5\}$
- $\{5\}$
- $\{4,6\}$
- $\{4,5,6\}$

## Explanation

Event $B$ contains $4$, $5$ and $6$. For $A$ not to occur, the result must be even. Start with $B$ and remove its only odd outcome, $5$. This leaves $4$ and $6$, giving $B\setminus A=\{4,6\}$. Although $2$ is even, it falls outside $B$ because it is less than $4$.
