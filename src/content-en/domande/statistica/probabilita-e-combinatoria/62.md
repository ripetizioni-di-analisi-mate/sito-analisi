A team makes four independent attempts, each with success probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is the probability of at least one success and at least one failure?

- $\dfrac{15}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$

## Explanation

The four trials give $2^4=16$ equally likely sequences because they are independent and each outcome has probability $\dfrac12$. The required event excludes the all-success sequence and the all-failure sequence. Every other sequence contains both outcomes. This leaves $16-2=14$ favorable sequences, giving probability $\dfrac{14}{16}=\dfrac78$.
