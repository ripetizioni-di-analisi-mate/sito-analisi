Five distinct documents are arranged in a stack. A must precede B and C must precede D, with A, B, C and D distinct. How many arrangements are allowed?

- $60$
- $24$
- $20$
- $30$

## Explanation

Group the arrangements that use the same positions for the pair A and B and the same positions for the pair C and D. Within each group, A and B can be swapped and C and D can be swapped independently. This gives four distinct arrangements, only one of which meets both ordering requirements. Of the $5!=120$ possible arrangements, $\dfrac{120}{4}=30$ are therefore allowed.
