A test yields exactly one of three results: valid, inconclusive or rejected. The probability of “inconclusive or rejected” is $\dfrac{2}{7}$. What is the probability of a valid result?

- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$

## Explanation

The three outcomes are mutually exclusive and cover all test results. A valid result therefore cannot be inconclusive or rejected. Every result that is not valid falls into one of those two categories, so “valid” is the complement of “inconclusive or rejected.” Its probability is $1-\dfrac27=\dfrac57$.
