A badge contains one letter chosen from $A,B$ followed by three bits. How many badges have an odd number of bits equal to $1$?

- $8$
- $4$
- $16$
- $6$

## Explanation

Among three positions an odd count of bits equal to $1$ is either one or three. With just one $1$, its position can be chosen in three ways. With three bits equal to $1$, the sequence is fixed. There are $3+1=4$ permitted bit strings, each of which can be preceded by A or B. The product gives $4\cdot2=8$ badges.
