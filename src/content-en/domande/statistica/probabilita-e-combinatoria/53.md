A three-outcome model assigns probabilities $p$, $2p$ and $3p$. What must $p$ equal?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The three outcomes are distinct and exhaust the sample space, so their probabilities must sum to $1$. The first contributes $p$, the second $2p$ and the third $3p$, giving $6p=1$. Solving gives $p=\dfrac16$. The three probabilities are $\dfrac16$, $\dfrac13$ and $\dfrac12$, all nonnegative, so they do define the model.
