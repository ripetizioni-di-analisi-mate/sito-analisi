In a certain place, the probability that there is a strong wind on an April day is $0.60$, the probability that it rains is $0.35$ and the probability that at least one of the two occurs is $0.75$. What is the probability that on an April day there is a strong wind and it rains?

- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Let $A$ be the event "there is a strong wind" and $B$ the event "it rains". By the inclusion-exclusion formula, $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$. Hence $P(A\cap B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cup B)=0.60+0.35-0.75=0.20$. Therefore the probability that there is a strong wind and it rains is $0.20=\dfrac15$.
