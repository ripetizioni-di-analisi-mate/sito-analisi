In an archive, the probability that a file contains a technical attachment is $0.60$, the probability that it contains an invoice is $0.35$, and the probability that it contains at least one of the two documents is $0.75$. Calculate the probability that it contains both.

- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Let $A$ denote the presence of the attachment and $B$ the presence of the invoice. Adding $P(A)$ and $P(B)$ gives $0.95$ because files with both documents are counted twice. The union has probability $0.75$ and counts each file once. The difference is therefore the probability of the overlap, $0.95-0.75=0.20=\dfrac15$.
