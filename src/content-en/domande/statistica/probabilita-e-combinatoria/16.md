An experiment has four possible outcomes. The first three have probabilities $0.12$, $0.24$ and $0.36$. What probability must the fourth outcome have?

- $\dfrac{18}{25}$
- $\dfrac{7}{25}$
- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{17}{25}$

## Explanation

The four outcomes exhaust the sample space and are pairwise mutually exclusive. Their probabilities must therefore sum to $1$. The first three have total probability $0.12+0.24+0.36=0.72$. The remaining probability, $1-0.72=0.28=\dfrac7{25}$, belongs to the fourth outcome. This is nonnegative, so together the four values define a probability measure on the space of the four outcomes.
