A label contains a digit from $1$ to $4$ followed by a letter chosen from $A,B,C$. If the digit is odd, the letter must be A. If it is even, the letter is unrestricted. How many labels are allowed?

- $12$
- $10$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

The count depends on whether the initial digit is odd or even. Only A is allowed after $1$ and $3$, giving $2\cdot1=2$ labels. Any of the three letters can follow $2$ or $4$, giving $2\cdot3=6$ labels. Their initial digits distinguish the two groups, so adding the counts gives $2+6=8$.
