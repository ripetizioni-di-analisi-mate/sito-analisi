A code consists of three digits from $0$ to $9$, with repetition allowed. How many codes contain at least two equal digits?

- $720$
- $270$
- $280$
- $1000$

## Explanation

By the multiplication principle there are $10\cdot10\cdot10=1000$ codes. The complement of “at least two equal digits” is “three distinct digits.” There are $10\cdot9\cdot8=720$ codes with three distinct digits, because the second digit is chosen from the nine that differ from the first and the third from the eight that differ from the first two. The required number of codes is therefore $1000-720=280$. The value $270$ counts only the codes with exactly two equal digits and leaves out the ten codes with three equal digits, such as $000$ or $777$.
