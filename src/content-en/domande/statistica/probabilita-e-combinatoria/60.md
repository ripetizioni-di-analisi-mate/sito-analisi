Which choice gives an ordered selection of two elements from six without repetition?

- Assign the positions of chair and secretary to two different people.
- Print a two-symbol code, choosing each symbol freely from the six.
- Choose two people for a committee with no assigned positions.
- Arrange all six people in a row.

## Explanation

An ordered selection without repetition assigns distinct elements to distinguishable positions. Here chair and secretary are the positions, and each person can fill at most one. Anna as chair and Bruno as secretary is a different outcome from Bruno as chair and Anna as secretary. Choosing the chair first and then one of the five remaining people gives $6\cdot5$ assignments.
