Three members are chosen from four representatives of department A and four of department B. Both departments must be represented. How many groups are possible?

- $56$
- $24$
- $52$
- $48$

## Explanation

First count all $\binom83=56$ groups of three. Exclude the groups with all three members from the same department. Each department contributes $\binom43=4$ such groups and the two sets of groups are disjoint. Subtracting them leaves $56-4-4=48$ groups representing both departments. In every permitted group one department has two members and the other has one.
