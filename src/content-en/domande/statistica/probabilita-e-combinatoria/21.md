Five candidates sit in a row of five distinct chairs. Which statement describes the arrangements in which Marta sits before Paolo?

- They are exactly the arrangements in which Marta sits immediately before Paolo.
- They make up one fifth of all arrangements.
- They make up half of all arrangements.
- They are all the arrangements with Paolo in the last chair.

## Explanation

For every row in which Marta comes before Paolo, switch their seats and leave the other three candidates in place. In the resulting row Paolo comes before Marta. Switching again recovers the original row, so this correspondence is one-to-one. In every row Marta either comes before Paolo or after him, so the two cases cover all $5!=120$ rows. The correspondence shows that the two cases contain the same number of rows, so each contains $\dfrac{5!}{2}=60$ arrangements.
