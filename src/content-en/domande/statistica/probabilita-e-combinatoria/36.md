Two distinct positions are assigned to different people from a pool of nine candidates. By what factor does the number of assignments exceed the number of unordered pairs?

- $9$
- $2!$
- $8$
- $\binom{9}{2}$

## Explanation

Fix an unordered pair of candidates, say Anna and Bruno. Assigning the first position to Anna gives one assignment, and reversing the roles gives another. Every pair produces exactly these two assignments and each assignment comes from just one pair. The ordered count is therefore $2!\binom92$, with factor $2!$.
