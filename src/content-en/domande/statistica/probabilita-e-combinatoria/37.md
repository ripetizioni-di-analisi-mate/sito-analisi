A batch contains six sound bolts and four defective ones. Two bolts are drawn uniformly at random without replacement. What is the probability that both are sound?

- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Every pair of bolts is equally likely because its two possible draw orders have the same probability as those of any other pair. There are $\binom{10}{2}=45$ possible pairs among ten bolts. The favorable pairs use only the six sound bolts, giving $\binom62=15$. The ratio is $\dfrac{15}{45}=\dfrac13$. Alternatively, multiply $\dfrac6{10}$ by $\dfrac59$, since after one sound bolt is drawn five of the nine remaining bolts are sound.
