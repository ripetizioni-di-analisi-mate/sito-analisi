A teacher draws three students uniformly at random, without replacement, from a class of twelve to answer questions. What is the probability that Luca is among the three drawn?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{3}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{220}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on draws without replacement, the groups of three students are equally likely, and there are $\binom{12}{3}=220$ of them. A group containing Luca is determined by the other two students, chosen from the remaining eleven in $\binom{11}{2}=55$ ways. The probability is therefore $\dfrac{55}{220}=\dfrac14$. This is the formula of the theorem with $N=12$, $R=1$, $n=3$ and $k=1$, and the result equals $\dfrac{3}{12}$, the number of students drawn divided by the number of students.
