A two-person committee is chosen from four members of department A and three of department B. How many pairs include one member from each department?

- $7$
- $21$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

Each mixed pair has one representative from A and one from B. The representative from A can be chosen in four ways and, for each choice, the representative from B in three ways. Department membership distinguishes the two people, so no pair is counted twice. This gives $4\cdot3=12$ pairs.
