A spinner has outcomes R, S and T, with $P(R)=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $P(S)=P(T)$. What is $P(T)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The three outcomes are mutually exclusive and exhaust the possibilities, so their probabilities sum to $1$. Outcome R accounts for half the total probability. That leaves $1-\dfrac12=\dfrac12$ to divide between S and T. Their probabilities are equal by assumption, so each receives half the remainder, namely $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac14$.
