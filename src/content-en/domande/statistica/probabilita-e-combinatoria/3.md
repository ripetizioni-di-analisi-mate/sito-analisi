Two fair six-sided dice are rolled independently. Calculate the probability that the sum is $5$ or $12$.

- $\dfrac{2}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{36}$

## Explanation

Because the dice are fair and independent, all $6\cdot6=36$ ordered pairs are equally likely. For a sum of $5$, the first die can show any number from $1$ to $4$ and the second is then determined, giving four pairs. A sum of $12$ requires double sixes. The two sums are mutually exclusive, so there are five favorable pairs and the probability is $\dfrac5{36}$.
