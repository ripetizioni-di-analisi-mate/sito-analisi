A finite sample space has three outcomes with probabilities $0$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Which statement is correct?

- The first outcome belongs to the sample space and has probability zero.
- The model violates normalization.
- The other two outcomes are equally likely.
- An elementary outcome must always have positive probability.

## Explanation

The sample space contains three outcomes by assumption. Their probabilities $0$, $\dfrac23$ and $\dfrac13$ are all nonnegative and sum to $1$, satisfying the axioms of a finite probability model. The first outcome can therefore be included in the space and assigned probability zero. Its membership is part of the model specification, while zero is its assigned probability.
