In the enrolment register for courses A, B and C, a person already attending A and B adds C. They were not enrolled in C before, and no other record changes. How do the number enrolled in at least one course and the sum of the three pairwise intersection counts change?

- Both increase by $1$
- The first is unchanged and the second increases by $2$
- The first increases by $1$ and the second by $2$
- The first is unchanged and the second increases by $3$

## Explanation

The person was already in the union and in the A–B intersection. Their additional enrolment adds one presence to A–C and one to B–C, so the sum of the three pairwise counts increases by two.
