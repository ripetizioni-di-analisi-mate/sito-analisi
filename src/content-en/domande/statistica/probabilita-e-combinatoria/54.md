Of $60$ students, $24$ take course A, $20$ take B and $18$ take C. The intersections A–B, A–C and B–C contain $8$, $7$ and $5$ students, respectively. Four students take all three courses. How many take exactly two?

- $12$
- $8$
- $16$
- $20$

## Explanation

The three pairwise intersections also include the four students taking all three courses. To isolate the students taking exactly two, subtract the triple intersection from each pairwise intersection. This leaves $8-4=4$, $7-4=3$ and $5-4=1$ students in the three regions. The regions are now disjoint and their total is $4+3+1=8$.
