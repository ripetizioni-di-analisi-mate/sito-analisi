A code consists of two digits from $0$ to $4$, with repetition allowed. Only codes with an even digit sum are permitted. How many are there?

- $13$
- $25$
- $12$
- $5$

## Explanation

The sum is even if both digits are even or both are odd. Between $0$ and $4$ there are three even digits and two odd ones. The positions are ordered and repetition is allowed, so the first case gives $3\cdot3=9$ codes and the second $2\cdot2=4$. The cases are disjoint, giving $9+4=13$ codes.
