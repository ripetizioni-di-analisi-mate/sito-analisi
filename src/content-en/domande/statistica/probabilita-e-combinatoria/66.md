An application may be late ($R$) or incomplete ($I$). Which expression is equivalent to “the application is on time and complete”?

- $(R\cap I)^c$
- $R^c\cap I^c$
- $R\cap I$
- $R^c\cup I^c$

## Explanation

An on-time application belongs to $R^c$, and a complete application belongs to $I^c$. The word “and” requires both conditions to hold for the same application, so take $R^c\cap I^c$. This set also equals $(R\cup I)^c$ because it excludes every application with at least one of the two problems.
