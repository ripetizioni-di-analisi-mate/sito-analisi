Four types of stamp are available. Three impressions are made in three numbered positions, and a stamp type can be used more than once. How many distinct sequences are possible?

- $64$
- $24$
- $81$
- $12$

## Explanation

The three positions are numbered, so placing stamp A first and stamp B second gives a different outcome from swapping them. All four stamp types are available at every position because a type can be used again. The multiplication principle gives $4\cdot4\cdot4=4^3=64$ sequences. There are four possibilities at every stage.
