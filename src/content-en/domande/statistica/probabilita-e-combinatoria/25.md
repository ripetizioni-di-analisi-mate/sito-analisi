How many two-element subsets of $\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7\}$ contain at least one of $1$ and $2$?

- $11$
- $12$
- $15$
- $10$

## Explanation

Start with all $\binom72=21$ unordered pairs. The complement of “contains at least one of $1$ and $2$” is “excludes both.” The excluded pairs use only the five numbers from $3$ to $7$ and number $\binom52=10$. Subtraction leaves $21-10=11$ pairs, including $\{1,2\}$, which satisfies the requirement.
