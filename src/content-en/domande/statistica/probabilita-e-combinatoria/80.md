A fair six-sided die is rolled and a fair coin is tossed independently. What is the probability of rolling a multiple of $3$ and getting tails?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The die and coin are fair and independent, so all $6\cdot2=12$ die–coin pairs are equally likely. The die must show $3$ or $6$. For each value, only tails is favorable. There are two favorable pairs, giving probability $\dfrac2{12}=\dfrac16$. Equivalently, use independence to multiply $\dfrac26$ by $\dfrac12$.
