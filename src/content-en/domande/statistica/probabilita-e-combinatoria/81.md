Three squares in a row are colored using four colors. Each square after the first must differ in color from the immediately preceding square. How many colorings are possible?

- $48$
- $36$
- $24$
- $64$

## Explanation

The first square can take any of four colors. The second excludes the color just used, leaving three choices. The third excludes only the second square's color, again leaving three choices. The third square can therefore match the first. The multiplication principle gives $4\cdot3\cdot3=36$ colorings.
