A fair coin is tossed independently four times. Which event is the complement of “at least two heads”?

- Four tails.
- At least two tails.
- At most one head.
- Exactly one head.

## Explanation

The number of heads can be $0$, $1$, $2$, $3$ or $4$. “At least two” includes $2$, $3$ and $4$. The complement contains all the excluded counts, namely $0$ and $1$. These two cases are described by “at most one head.” Saying “exactly one head” would also leave out the sequence of four tails.
