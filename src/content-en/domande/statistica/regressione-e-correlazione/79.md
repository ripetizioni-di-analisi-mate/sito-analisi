In several towns, ice cream sales and electricity consumption both increase on hotter days. Which explanation is consistent with these data?

- Regression proves that ice cream causes electricity consumption to rise.
- Positive correlation rules out common explanatory variables.
- Temperature may be associated with both variables.
- Interchanging predictor and response establishes the direction of causation.

## Explanation

On hotter days, temperature can increase demand for ice cream and electricity use, for example through air conditioning. The two measures can rise together even without a direct link. The plot describes the observed association. Studying a causal effect would require information distinguishing the role of temperature from that of sales.
