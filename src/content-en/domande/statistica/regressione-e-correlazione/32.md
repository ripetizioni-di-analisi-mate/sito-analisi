The fitted line with an intercept slopes downward and has $R^2=0.49$. What is $r$?

- $-0.7$
- $0.7$
- $-0.49$
- $0.49$

## Explanation

In simple linear regression with an intercept, $R^2=r^2$. Taking the square root of $0.49$ gives the magnitude $0.7$, leaving two possible signs. The line slopes downward, so $b=\dfrac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}$ is negative. Since $r$ also has the sign of $S_{xy}$, choose $r=-0.7$.
