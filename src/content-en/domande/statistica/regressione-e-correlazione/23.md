For $(0,1)$, $(1,2)$, and $(2,5)$, compare the candidate lines $A\colon y=1+2x$ and $B\colon y=1+x$. Which has the smaller residual sum of squares?

- $B$, with sum $1$.
- $A$, with sum $1$.
- $A$, with sum $4$.
- Both sums equal $4$.

## Explanation

Compute the vertical deviations at the three observed predictor values. Line $A$ gives fitted values $(1,3,5)$, residuals $(0,-1,0)$, and sum of squared residuals $1$. Line $B$ gives fitted values $(1,2,3)$, residuals $(0,0,2)$, and sum of squared residuals $4$. Both lines pass through two points. The squared deviation at the remaining point decides the comparison in favor of $A$.
