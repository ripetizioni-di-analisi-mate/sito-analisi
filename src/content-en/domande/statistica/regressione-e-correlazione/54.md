For $n=20$, $\bar x=1$, and $x_0=3$, the result is $h_0=0.25$. What is $S_{xx}$?

- $16$
- $20$
- $10$
- $25$

## Explanation

Subtract the sample-size contribution $\dfrac1{20}=0.05$ from the weight $h_0=0.25$, leaving $0.20$ for the squared-distance term. With $x_0-\bar x=3-1=2$ this gives $\dfrac{4}{S_{xx}}=0.20$. Solving yields $S_{xx}=\dfrac4{0.20}=20$. Using the entire weight in the denominator would also attribute the sample-size contribution to the distance.
