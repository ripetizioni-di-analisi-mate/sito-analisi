Both the mean response at $x_0$ and an interval for a new unit at $x_0$ are required. Which source of uncertainty enters only the second interval?

- Uncertainty in estimating the slope.
- Uncertainty in estimating the intercept.
- Variability of the new individual error.
- The distance between $x_0$ and the mean of the predictor values.

## Explanation

Both cases use the same fitted line and therefore include uncertainty in its coefficients. A future individual response may also differ from its mean because of the new error. Its variance adds a term to the prediction variance provided the new error is independent of the sample. Estimating the mean alone accounts only for uncertainty in the line.
