A line has $R^2=0.98$ and its residuals show considerable variation near the largest observed predictor value. What can be concluded?

- The fraction of variation explained is high, but local precision may be low.
- $98\%$ of predictions fall within every positive tolerance.
- The prediction error has the same magnitude at every predictor value.
- The model must satisfy every assumption about the residuals.

## Explanation

A value of $R^2=0.98$ indicates a small residual sum of squares relative to the total sum of squares. Both use all observations. The residual spread near the predictor maximum concerns one particular part of the sample. Deviations there can be large even when the overall ratio indicates that the model explains much of the total variability.
