A sample contains only the pairs $(1,8)$ and $(4,2)$. What is Pearson's coefficient?

- $1$
- $-2$
- It is undefined because there are only two pairs.
- $-1$

## Explanation

The means are $\bar x=2.5$ and $\bar y=5$, giving centered vectors $(-1.5,1.5)$ and $(3,-3)$. The second is the first multiplied by $-2$. Normalization in Pearson's formula removes the magnitude of the factor and retains its negative sign. Two points with both coordinates distinct lie on a line, which in this case slopes downward.
