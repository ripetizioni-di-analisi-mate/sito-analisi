In a regression with an intercept, $n=10$, $\bar x=5$, $S_{xx}=90$, and $s_e=2$. What is the standard error of the mean response at $x_0=8$?

- $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{10}}$
- $2\sqrt{\dfrac65}$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

To estimate the mean response at $x_0=8$, calculate the weight $h_0=\dfrac1n+\dfrac{(x_0-\bar x)^2}{S_{xx}}$. The distance from the predictor mean is $3$, giving $h_0=\dfrac1{10}+\dfrac9{90}=\dfrac15$. Multiplying $s_e=2$ by $\sqrt{h_0}$ gives $\dfrac2{\sqrt5}$. The extra individual variance term applies when predicting a new observation.
