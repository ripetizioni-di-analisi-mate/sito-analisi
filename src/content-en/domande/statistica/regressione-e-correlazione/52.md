In a regression with an intercept, $n=10$, $\bar x=5$, and $S_{xx}=90$. For which values $x_0$ is the estimated variance of the mean response twice its value at $x_0=\bar x$?

- $x_0=2$ and $x_0=8$
- $x_0=-4$ and $x_0=14$
- $x_0=0$ and $x_0=10$
- $x_0=5-\sqrt{90}$ and $x_0=5+\sqrt{90}$

## Explanation

The estimated variance of the mean response at $x_0$ is $s_e^2h_0$, with $h_0=\dfrac1n+\dfrac{(x_0-\bar x)^2}{S_{xx}}$. At $x_0=\bar x$ the second term is zero, so $h_0=\dfrac1{10}$. The variance is twice as large when $h_0=\dfrac2{10}$, that is, when $\dfrac{(x_0-5)^2}{90}=\dfrac1{10}$. Hence $(x_0-5)^2=9$, so $x_0-5=3$ or $x_0-5=-3$. Therefore the values are $x_0=2$ and $x_0=8$, at the same distance from the mean. The pair $-4$ and $14$ comes from setting $|x_0-5|=9$ without taking the square root, and the pair $5\pm\sqrt{90}$ comes from setting $(x_0-5)^2=S_{xx}$.
