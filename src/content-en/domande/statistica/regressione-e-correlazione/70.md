Every observed predictor value equals $4$, while the responses vary. What can be determined by least squares with an intercept?

- The slope must be zero and the intercept is unique.
- The fitted value at $x=4$ is determined, but the slope is not identifiable.
- The slope is the mean of the response values.
- Both the intercept and the slope are determined by $S_{xy}$.

## Explanation

If every predictor value equals $4$, the line assigns every observation the same fitted value $a+4b$. Minimizing the sum of squared deviations from one value gives the minimum at $\bar y$. The condition $a+4b=\bar y$ allows a pair of coefficients for each choice of $b$. The data determine the fitted value at $4$ but leave the slope undetermined.
