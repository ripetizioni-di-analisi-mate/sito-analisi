Given $\hat y(x)=2-2x$, set $u=x-3$ and $v=y+5$. What is the intercept of the line of $v$ on $u$?

- $7$
- $1$
- $13$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Since $u=x-3$, the original predictor value corresponding to $u=0$ is $x=3$. The line assigns it the fitted value $\hat y(3)=2-2\cdot3=-4$. The new response adds $5$, giving $\hat v(0)=-4+5=1$. This determines the intercept at the new predictor origin. Substituting $x=u+3$ in the equation also gives $\hat v(u)=1-2u$ directly.
