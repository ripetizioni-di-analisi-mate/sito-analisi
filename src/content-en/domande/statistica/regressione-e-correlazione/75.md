A future response is to be predicted for a unit already in the sample. Its new error may depend on its previous error. Which assumption of the standard interval requires attention?

- The existence of a sample mean for the predictor values.
- Independence of the new response from the sample at a fixed predictor value.
- The use of squared residuals to fit the line.
- The intercept is greater than zero.

## Explanation

The standard prediction interval adds the variance of the new error to the variance of the estimated mean. This addition is justified when the new response is independent of the sample conditional on the predictors. If its error depends on an earlier error from the same unit, it may also depend on the estimate using that observation. A covariance term can then enter the variance of the difference.
