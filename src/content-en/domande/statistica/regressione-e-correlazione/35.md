The correlation between $x$ and $y$ is $-0.4$. Set $u=10-x$ and $v=3y+2$. What is the correlation between $u$ and $v$?

- $-0.4$
- $1.2$
- $-1.2$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

The constant shifts cancel when the variables are centered. The deviations of $u$ are the negatives of those of $x$, and the deviations of $v$ are three times those of $y$. Pearson's numerator is thus multiplied by $-3$. The norms in the denominator introduce a positive factor $3$, leaving a change of sign and giving $0.4$.
