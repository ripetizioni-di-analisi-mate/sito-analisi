In a model with $n=20$ and $S_{xx}=20$, consider two predictor values at distances $1$ and $2$ from $\bar x$. What is the ratio of their mean response weights $h_0$, second to first?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac52}$
- $\dfrac52$

## Explanation

The weight for the mean response equals $\dfrac1n$ plus the squared distance from $\bar x$ divided by $S_{xx}$. At distance $1$ it is $h_1=\dfrac1{20}+\dfrac1{20}=\dfrac1{10}$, and at distance $2$ it is $h_2=\dfrac1{20}+\dfrac4{20}=\dfrac14$. The second-to-first ratio is $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot10=\dfrac52$. Because of the common term this ratio differs from the square of $2$.
