In a linear regression with an intercept, $n=7$ and $SSE=20$. What is the estimate $s_e^2$ of the residual variance?

- $\dfrac{20}{7}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The model estimates two coefficients, the intercept and the slope. The variance estimate therefore divides the residual sum of squares by the remaining degrees of freedom, $n-2$. With $n=7$ observations, $5$ degrees of freedom remain, giving $s_e^2=\dfrac{20}{5}=4$. The residual standard deviation is its square root, $2$.
