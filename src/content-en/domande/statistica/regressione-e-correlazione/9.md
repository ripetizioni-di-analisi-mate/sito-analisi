A normal regression model with an intercept gives $n=10$, $b=-0.6$, and $SE(b)=0.2$. Test $H_0\colon\beta=0$ against a two-sided alternative at level $5\%$, using $t_{8,0.975}=2.306$.

- Fail to reject $H_0$ because $T$ is negative.
- Reject $H_0$ with $T=-0.12$.
- Fail to reject $H_0$ because $\lvert T\rvert=0.6$.
- Reject $H_0$ with $T=-3$.

## Explanation

Under the null hypothesis, measure the distance of the estimate from zero in standard-error units. This gives $T=\dfrac{-0.6-0}{0.2}=-3$ with $10-2=8$ degrees of freedom. The alternative is two-sided, so both tails count and the comparison uses $\lvert T\rvert=3$. This value exceeds $2.306$, placing the observation in the rejection region for $H_0$.
