The fitted model is $\hat y(x)=3+2x$. At which value of $x$ is the fitted response $17$?

- $7$
- $8.5$
- $10$
- $14$

## Explanation

The required predictor value must give fitted response $17$. Solve $3+2x=17$ by subtracting the intercept $3$ and dividing the difference $14$ by the slope $2$. This gives $x=7$. Substituting into the line recovers $3+14=17$. The question asks for a value assigned by the model, so its equation suffices.
