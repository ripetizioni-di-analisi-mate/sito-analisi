Delivery time is to be estimated from distance. How should the two variables be placed on the axes of a scatterplot for this regression?

- Time on the horizontal axis and distance on the vertical axis.
- Both on the vertical axis because both are quantitative.
- Distance on the horizontal axis and time on the vertical axis.
- Time goes on the horizontal axis if it has the larger mean.

## Explanation

Distance is used to estimate duration, so it serves as the predictor $x$ on the horizontal axis. Duration is the response $y$ on the vertical axis. Each point thus links the distance and time for the same delivery, with a fitted response for each predictor value.
