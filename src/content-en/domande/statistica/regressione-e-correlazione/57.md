At a given predictor value, the estimated standard error of the mean response is $3$ and the residual standard deviation is $s_e=4$. What is the standard error for predicting a new independent response?

- $7$
- $5$
- $25$
- $1$

## Explanation

A new independent response adds the variability of its own error to that of the fitted mean. Independence allows their variances to be added. Their standard deviations are $3$ and $4$, giving $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{25}=5$. Adding $3$ and $4$ directly would treat standard deviations as variances.
