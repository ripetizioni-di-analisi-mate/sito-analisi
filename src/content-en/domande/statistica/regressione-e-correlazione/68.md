An observed point has predictor value $x_i$. Which deviation enters the usual least-squares criterion for regressing $y$ on $x$?

- The vertical difference $y_i-\hat y(x_i)$.
- The shortest perpendicular distance from the line.
- The horizontal difference at a fixed response value.
- The distance from the point to the origin.

## Explanation

In the regression of $y$ on $x$, each predictor value is fixed at its observed value. Use the response $\hat y(x_i)$ fitted by the line at that predictor value. Subtract it from $y_i$ to obtain the vertical residual $e_i=y_i-\hat y(x_i)$. Least squares sums the squares of these residuals. A perpendicular distance would generally use a different point on the line.
