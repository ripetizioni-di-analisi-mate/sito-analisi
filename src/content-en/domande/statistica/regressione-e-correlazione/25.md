The predictor values are $0,1,2$ and the first residual from the line with an intercept is $2$. What is the residual of the second observation?

- $-4$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The line with an intercept satisfies two conditions on the residuals. For the slope, $\sum_i x_i e_i=0$ becomes $e_2+2e_3=0$ at the given predictor values. For the intercept, $2+e_2+e_3=0$. Substituting $e_2=-2e_3$ into the second condition gives $e_3=2$ and hence $e_2=-4$. The zero predictor value removes $e_1$ from the weighted sum, while that residual still enters the ordinary sum.
