A residual plot stays centered near zero, but its vertical spread increases systematically to the right. Which assumption does this most directly call into question?

- The intercept is positive.
- The predictor has a normal marginal distribution.
- The errors have constant variance.
- The predictor has a finite mean.

## Explanation

The center of the residuals reflects the shape of the mean response, while their vertical spread describes variability around it. Here the center stays near zero and the spread grows to the right. The pattern therefore primarily calls the constant-error-variance assumption into question. Greater dispersion at high predictor values is consistent with variance depending on the predictor.
