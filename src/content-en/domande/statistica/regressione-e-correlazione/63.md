The pairs are $(1,5)$, $(2,5)$, and $(3,5)$. What are the slope of the regression with an intercept and Pearson's correlation coefficient?

- Slope $0$ and correlation $0$.
- Slope $5$ and correlation $1$.
- Undefined slope and correlation $0$.
- Slope $0$ and undefined correlation.

## Explanation

All responses equal their mean $5$. Their deviations vanish, so $S_{xy}=0$ and $S_{yy}=0$. The predictor values vary, giving $S_{xx}>0$ and fitted slope $b=\dfrac{0}{S_{xx}}=0$ for a line with an intercept. Pearson's correlation divides by $\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}$, which is zero here. The two calculations have different denominators, and only the slope is defined in this example.
