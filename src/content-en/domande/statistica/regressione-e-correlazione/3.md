The fitted line is $\hat y(x)=8-x$ and one observed point is $(2,7)$. Calculate its residual.

- $-1$
- $6$
- $1$
- $7$

## Explanation

A residual compares the observed response with the fitted response at the same predictor value. At the specified point evaluate $\hat y(2)=8-2=6$. The observed response is $7$, so the residual is $e=7-6=1$. Its positive sign places the point one unit above the line. Reversing the subtraction would reverse the interpretation of the sign.
