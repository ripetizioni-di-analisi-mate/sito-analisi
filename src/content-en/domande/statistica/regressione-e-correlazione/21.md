A regression with an intercept gives $\bar x=4$, $a=2$, and $b=\dfrac34$. What is $\bar y$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The least-squares line with an intercept passes through the point of means. The mean response is therefore the fitted value at $\bar x=4$, namely $\bar y=a+b\bar x=2+\dfrac34\cdot4=5$. This property follows from the zero sum of the residuals and recovers $\bar y$ without requiring every observation.
