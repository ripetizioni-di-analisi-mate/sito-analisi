An estimated slope gives $SE(b)=0.6$ and $s_e=3$. Find $S_{xx}$.

- $5$
- $1.8$
- $25$
- $9$

## Explanation

The relation $SE(b)=\dfrac{s_e}{\sqrt{S_{xx}}}$ lets us isolate the square root of $S_{xx}$. The observed values give $\sqrt{S_{xx}}=\dfrac3{0.6}=5$. Squaring yields $S_{xx}=25$. The value $5$ is the norm of the vector of predictor deviations, while the question asks for their sum of squares.
