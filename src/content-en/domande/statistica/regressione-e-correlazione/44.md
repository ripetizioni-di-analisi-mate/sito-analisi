Four pieces are cut, two of thickness $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and two of thickness $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. The regression of cutting time on thickness has residual standard deviation $s_e=2$. What is $SE(b)$?

- $\dfrac18$
- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The mean thickness is $\bar x=\dfrac{2+2+6+6}{4}=4$, so the deviations are $-2,-2,2,2$ and $S_{xx}=4+4+4+4=16$. The standard error of the slope is $SE(b)=\dfrac{s_e}{\sqrt{S_{xx}}}=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{16}}=\dfrac12$. The value $\dfrac18$ comes from dividing by $S_{xx}$ instead of its square root. With thicknesses further apart, $S_{xx}$ would be larger and the standard error smaller.
