For the slope, the observed quantities are $b=2.1$ and $SE(b)=0.4$. Use the t quantile $2.5$ corresponding to the specified confidence level to calculate the two-sided interval.

- $[1.7;\,2.5]$
- $[1.1;\,3.1]$
- $[-0.4;\,4.6]$
- $[0.1;\,4.1]$

## Explanation

The two-sided interval is centered on the estimated slope. Multiplying the given quantile by the standard error gives margin $2.5\cdot0.4=1$. The lower limit is $2.1-1=1.1$ and the upper limit $2.1+1=3.1$. Each endpoint is one unit from the estimate. The full interval length is twice the margin.
