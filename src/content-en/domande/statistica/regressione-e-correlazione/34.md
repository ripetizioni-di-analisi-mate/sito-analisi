Given $S_{xx}=4$ and $S_{yy}=9$, what is the largest possible value of $\lvert S_{xy}\rvert$?

- $36$
- $6$
- $13$
- $4$

## Explanation

By the theorem relating correlation and determination, $|r|\le1$. Since $r=\dfrac{S_{xy}}{\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}}$, it follows that $|S_{xy}|\le\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}=\sqrt{4\cdot9}=6$. The value $6$ is reached when $|r|=1$, that is, when the points lie exactly on a line. The value $36$ is the product $S_{xx}S_{yy}$ without the square root, and $13$ is the sum $4+9$.
