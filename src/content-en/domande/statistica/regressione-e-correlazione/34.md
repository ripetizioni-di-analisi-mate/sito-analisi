Given $S_{xx}=4$ and $S_{yy}=9$, what is the largest possible value of $\lvert S_{xy}\rvert$?

- $36$
- $6$
- $13$
- $4$

## Explanation

The sum $S_{xy}$ is the dot product of the two vectors of deviations from their means. The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality bounds its magnitude by the product of their norms, $\sqrt{S_{xx}}\sqrt{S_{yy}}=2\cdot3=6$. Equality is possible when the centered vectors are proportional. The sign of the proportionality factor determines whether their dot product is $6$ or $-6$.
