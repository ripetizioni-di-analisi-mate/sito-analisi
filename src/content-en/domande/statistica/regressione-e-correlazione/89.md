An experiment observes multiple responses at the same value of $x$. Which comparison helps assess whether the error variance is constant?

- Compare only the mean predictor values.
- Compare the spread of responses across groups defined by fixed predictor values.
- Compare the signs of intercepts estimated in each individual group.
- Discard all measurements sharing a predictor value.

## Explanation

At a fixed predictor value the model gives a single mean response. The spread of repeated measurements there therefore provides information about error variability within that group. Comparing spread across groups at different values of $x$ shows whether it stays similar. Changes in group means describe the mean response pattern.
