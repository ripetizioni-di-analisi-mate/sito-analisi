The two-sided confidence interval for the slope is $[1;\,3]$ and the t critical value is $2$. What is the standard error of $b$?

- $0.5$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The interval's endpoints differ by $3-1=2$. The two-sided interval is symmetric about the estimate, so the margin is half its width, $1$. The margin equals the t quantile times the standard error. Dividing $1$ by the quantile $2$ therefore gives $SE(b)=0.5$.
