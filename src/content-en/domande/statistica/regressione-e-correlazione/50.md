For the observed statistic $T=2$, the t table gives $P(t_\nu\ge2)=0.04$. What is the $p$-value for a two-sided alternative?

- $0.04$
- $0.02$
- $0.08$
- $0.96$

## Explanation

In a two-sided test the outcomes at least as extreme are those with $t_\nu\ge2$ or $t_\nu\le-2$. Symmetry of the t distribution gives the second tail probability $0.04$ as well. The two regions are disjoint, so their probabilities add to $p=0.04+0.04=0.08$. The table value referred to a single tail.
