A line with an intercept has positive total variability and $SSR=SSE$. What is $R^2$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- The value is indeterminate.

## Explanation

With an intercept, the decomposition gives $SST=SSR+SSE$. If the two components are equal, the total is $2SSR$. The total variability is positive, so the ratio simplifies to $R^2=\dfrac{SSR}{2SSR}=\dfrac12$. The line explains half the observed variability around the mean and leaves the other half in the residual component.
