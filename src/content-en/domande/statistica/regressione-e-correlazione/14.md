The residuals at predictor values $1,2,3,4,5$ are $3,-2,-2,-2,3$. What problem does their pattern suggest?

- The error variance must be zero.
- The mean response may have a curved pattern.
- The predictor must have mean zero.
- The slope must be positive.

## Explanation

Positive residuals at the extremes mean that the line predicts too low there. The three central residuals are negative, indicating fitted values that are too high. This change of sign across the predictor axis suggests curvature in the mean response. The residuals can sum to zero while displaying this pattern.
