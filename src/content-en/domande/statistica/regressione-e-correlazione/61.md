The predictor values are $-2,0,1,9$. Which observation has the greatest leverage in regression with an intercept?

- The observation at predictor value $-2$.
- The observation at predictor value $9$.
- The observation at predictor value $0$.
- The observation at predictor value $1$.

## Explanation

With an intercept, all leverages share the term $\dfrac1n$. Their differences depend only on the squared distances of the predictor values from $\bar x$. The mean here is $\dfrac{-2+0+1+9}{4}=2$, and the respective distances are $4$, $2$, $1$, and $7$. The predictor value $9$ has the greatest distance and hence the highest leverage.
