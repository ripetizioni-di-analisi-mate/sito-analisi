Each variable is centered at its own mean and divided by its positive sample standard deviation. What is the slope when the standardized response is regressed on the standardized predictor?

- The coefficient of determination $R^2$.
- Pearson's correlation coefficient $r$.
- The negative of the coefficient $r$.
- The residual standard deviation $s_e$.

## Explanation

After centering and dividing by the sample standard deviations, both centered sums of squares equal $n-1$. The standardized regression slope divides the centered sum of cross-products by $n-1$. Pearson's correlation divides the same numerator by $\sqrt{(n-1)(n-1)}=n-1$. The standardization factors are positive, so the original correlation is preserved and equals the required slope.
