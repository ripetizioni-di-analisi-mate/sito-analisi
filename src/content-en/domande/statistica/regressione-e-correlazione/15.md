Five pairs give $\sum_i x_i=15$, $\sum_i y_i=10$, and $\sum_i x_i y_i=34$. What is $S_{xy}$?

- $34$
- $-4$
- $4$
- $30$

## Explanation

Start with the sum of the raw cross-products and subtract the contribution of the means. For five pairs the correction term is $\dfrac{(\sum_i x_i)(\sum_i y_i)}n=\dfrac{15\cdot10}{5}=30$, leaving $S_{xy}=34-30=4$. This is the sum of the products of deviations. The value $34$ also includes the contribution from the means of the variables.
