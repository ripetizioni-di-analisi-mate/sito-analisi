With the fitted line fixed, the absolute value of a residual changes from $2$ to $4$. How does this observation's contribution to the residual sum of squares change?

- It quadruples.
- It doubles.
- It stays the same.
- It is halved.

## Explanation

The sum to be minimized uses the square of each residual. Before the change the observation contributes $2^2=4$. After the change it contributes $4^2=16$, four times the original amount. Squaring removes the residual's sign. Doubling the magnitude of the vertical distance multiplies its contribution by four.
