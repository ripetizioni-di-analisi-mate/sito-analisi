Two measurements are negatively correlated. If the sign of both measurements is reversed, what is the resulting correlation?

- The correlation becomes positive.
- The same negative correlation.
- The correlation becomes zero.
- The absolute correlation doubles.

## Explanation

Reversing the signs of both variables also reverses the signs of both means. Each centered product becomes $[-(x_i-\bar x)][-(y_i-\bar y)]$, equal to the original product. The sums of squares in the denominator are unchanged. The correlation retains both its magnitude and its negative sign. Reversing only one variable would change the sign of the numerator.
