The correlation between study time and exam grade is $r=0.7$. The correlation is calculated again with the roles of predictor and response interchanged. Which statement is correct?

- $r$ becomes $\dfrac{1}{0.7}$ and $R^2$ stays $0.49$.
- $r$ stays $0.7$ and $R^2$ stays $0.49$.
- $r$ becomes $-0.7$ because the axes are interchanged.
- $r$ stays $0.7$ and $R^2$ becomes $0.7$.

## Explanation

The formula $r=\dfrac{S_{xy}}{\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}}$ does not change when $x$ and $y$ are interchanged, because $S_{xy}=S_{yx}$ and the product $S_{xx}S_{yy}$ stays the same. Therefore $r$ stays $0.7$. In regression with an intercept $R^2=r^2$, so the coefficient of determination equals $0.7^2=0.49$ in both regressions. The slope does change: it equals $\dfrac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}$ in the regression of grade on time and $\dfrac{S_{xy}}{S_{yy}}$ in the regression of time on grade.
