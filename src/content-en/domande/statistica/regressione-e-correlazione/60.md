For four observations, three leverages are $0.4$, $0.4$, and $0.6$. In simple regression with an intercept, what is the fourth?

- $0.2$
- $0.6$
- $0.4$
- $1.4$

## Explanation

The leverages sum to the number of coefficients estimated in the regression. A simple line with an intercept has two coefficients, so the total must be $2$. The three given leverages sum to $0.4+0.4+0.6=1.4$. The missing value is $2-1.4=0.6$. The identity concerns the observations collectively and gives average leverage $\dfrac24=0.5$.
