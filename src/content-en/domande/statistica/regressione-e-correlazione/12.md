In a regression with an intercept, $n=16$, $\bar x=6$, $S_{xx}=64$, and $s_e=4$. Calculate the estimated variance of the prediction error for a new independent response at $x_0=10$.

- $21$
- $5$
- $16$
- $\sqrt{21}$

## Explanation

For a new independent response, add the variance of the new error to the variance of the fitted mean. The weight for the latter is $h_0=\dfrac1{16}+\dfrac{(10-6)^2}{64}=\dfrac5{16}$. With residual standard deviation $4$, the required variance is $s_e^2(1+h_0)=16\left(1+\dfrac5{16}\right)=21$. The term $1$ supplies the variance component for the new response.
