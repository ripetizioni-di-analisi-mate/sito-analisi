Two experiments have the same sample size and the same positive residual standard deviation. In the second, the predictor values span a wider interval and $S_{xx}$ is larger. Which estimate is more precise at a fixed confidence level?

- The slope in the first experiment.
- The slopes always have the same standard error if $n$ is the same.
- The signs of the two slopes must be known.
- The slope in the second experiment.

## Explanation

Compare slope precision using its standard error $SE(b)=\dfrac{s_e}{\sqrt{S_{xx}}}$. The two experiments have equal residual spread and hence the same numerator. In the second experiment the larger $S_{xx}$ increases the denominator and reduces the standard error. Equal sample sizes and confidence levels give the same quantile, so its slope interval is narrower as well.
