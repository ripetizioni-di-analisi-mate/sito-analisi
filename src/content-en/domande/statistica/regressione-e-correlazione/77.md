A simple regression experiment has $S_{xx}>0$. Two observations may be added, with the error standard deviation in the model held fixed. Which choice reduces slope variance the most if the existing mean predictor value is $\bar x$?

- Both at $\bar x$
- One at $\bar x-1$ and one at $\bar x+1$
- One at $\bar x-1/2$ and one at $\bar x+1/2$
- One at $\bar x-2$ and one at $\bar x+2$

## Explanation

Every proposed pair preserves the predictor mean. A pair symmetric about the mean at distance $d$ contributes $2d^2$ to $S_{xx}$. For $d=0,\dfrac12,1,2$ the contributions are $0,\dfrac12,2,8$, respectively. Since the slope variance is $\sigma^2/S_{xx}$, the greatest increase gives the smallest variance.
