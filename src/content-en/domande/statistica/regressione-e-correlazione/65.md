Training hours and a score are recorded for each worker. A colleague sorts only the score column before calculating the correlation. What problem does this introduce?

- The means of the two columns change.
- Repeated values in each column disappear.
- The centered sum of squares of the scores increases.
- The scores may be matched with other workers' training hours.

## Explanation

Each pair must link the hours and the score of the same worker. Sorting only the scores preserves the mean and spread of each list but changes the centered cross-products because hours and scores can now belong to different people. Correlation depends on those cross-products. Reordering complete rows preserves all the original pairs.
