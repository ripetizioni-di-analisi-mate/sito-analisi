A normal regression model with an intercept has $n=8$, $S_{xx}=8$, and $x_0-\bar x=1$. Given $\hat y_0=10$, $s_e=4$, and critical value $2.447$, calculate the interval for the mean response.

- $[7.553;\,12.447]$
- $[0.212;\,19.788]$
- $[8;\,12]$
- $[5.106;\,14.894]$

## Explanation

The mean-response weight is $h_0=\dfrac18+\dfrac{1^2}{8}=\dfrac14$, giving estimated standard error $4\sqrt{\dfrac14}=2$. The stated quantile yields margin $2.447\cdot2=4.894$. Subtracting and adding the margin at the fitted response $10$ gives $[5.106;\,14.894]$. The interval is centered on the fitted line and describes uncertainty in the mean at that predictor value.
