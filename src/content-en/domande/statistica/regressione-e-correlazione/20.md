A fitted line gives $\hat y(2)=10$ and $\hat y(6)=4$. What is its slope?

- $\dfrac32$
- $-\dfrac23$
- $-6$
- $-\dfrac32$

## Explanation

Divide the change in the fitted response by the corresponding predictor change. From $x=2$ to $x=6$ the response changes from $10$ to $4$, giving slope $\dfrac{4-10}{6-2}=-\dfrac64=-\dfrac32$. Using the same subtraction order in numerator and denominator preserves the correct sign.
