For the pairs $(1,5)$, $(3,7)$, and $(5,3)$, calculate the centered sum of products $S_{xy}$.

- $4$
- $-8$
- $-4$
- $45$

## Explanation

We measure how deviations from the two means, $\bar x=3$ and $\bar y=5$, vary together. For each pair subtract the corresponding mean from each coordinate before multiplying. The first two points have at least one coordinate at its mean and contribute zero. At the third point the deviations have opposite signs, giving $S_{xy}=(-2)\cdot0+0\cdot2+2\cdot(-2)=-4$.
