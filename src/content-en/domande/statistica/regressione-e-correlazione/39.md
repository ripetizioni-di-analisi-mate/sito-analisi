The pairs are $(-2,4)$, $(-1,1)$, $(1,1)$, and $(2,4)$. What is Pearson's correlation coefficient?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $0$
- It is undefined because the predictor values are symmetric.

## Explanation

The predictor has mean zero and the responses have mean $2.5$. Points symmetric about the vertical axis have the same response, so their centered cross-products have opposite signs. The products are $-3$, $1.5$, $-1.5$, and $3$. Their sum is zero and both variables have positive dispersion, so Pearson's correlation is zero. The symmetric pattern still shows a curved relationship.
