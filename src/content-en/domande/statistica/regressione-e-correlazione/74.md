For an observed data point, $y_i$ is known and $\hat y_i$ can be calculated. Which quantity can be obtained directly?

- The error $y_i-(\alpha+\beta x_i)$ involving unknown parameters.
- The residual $y_i-\hat y_i$.
- The true variance $\sigma^2$ of the model.
- The true slope $\beta$ of the model.

## Explanation

The residual uses two quantities available after fitting. The first is the observed response $y_i$ and the second is the fitted value $\hat y_i$ calculated from the estimated coefficients. Their difference $y_i-\hat y_i$ is observable. Finding the true error would require the true mean response $\alpha+\beta x_i$, which depends on unknown model parameters.
