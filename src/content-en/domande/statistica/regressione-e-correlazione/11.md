A normal regression model with an intercept has $n=25$ and $s_e=5$. Calculate the estimated standard error of the mean response at $x_0=\bar x$.

- $1$
- $5$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\sqrt{26}$

## Explanation

Estimate the mean response at the mean of the predictor by the fitted value at $x_0=\bar x$. There $\dfrac{(x_0-\bar x)^2}{S_{xx}}$ is zero, so the weight for the fitted mean is just $\dfrac1n$. The estimated standard error becomes $\dfrac{s_e}{\sqrt n}=\dfrac5{\sqrt{25}}=1$. The additional variation of a new observation enters only for an individual prediction.
