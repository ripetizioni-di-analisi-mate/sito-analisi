In a calculation of Spearman's rank correlation, the predictor values are $(40,20,20,30)$. Determine the rank assigned to the second value, using average ranks for ties.

- $1$
- $2$
- $2.5$
- $1.5$

## Explanation

Assign ranks after sorting the predictor values. The sorted sequence is $(20,20,30,40)$, so the two observations equal to $20$ occupy positions $1$ and $2$. Each receives their average, $\dfrac{1+2}{2}=1.5$. The second predictor value in the original sequence is one of these tied observations.
