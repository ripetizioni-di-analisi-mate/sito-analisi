Four pairs with distinct predictor values have responses that strictly increase with the predictor. The points do not lie on a single line. Which statement is correct?

- Both Pearson's and Spearman's coefficients equal $1$.
- Spearman's coefficient is less than $1$ because the points are not collinear.
- Spearman's coefficient is $1$ and Pearson's coefficient is less than $1$.
- Pearson's coefficient equals $0$ because the relationship may be curved.

## Explanation

Sorting the pairs by predictor value puts the responses in strictly increasing order as well. The rank sequences coincide, giving Spearman correlation $1$. Pearson's correlation uses the original measurements and attains $1$ only if the response deviations are a positive multiple of the predictor deviations. That would make the points collinear, which the question excludes, so Pearson's correlation is less than $1$.
