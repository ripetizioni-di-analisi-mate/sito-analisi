A regression with an intercept gives $SST=36$ and $SSE=18$. By what percentage does the residual sum of squares decrease relative to the model that always predicts $\bar y$?

- $50\%$
- $18\%$
- $36\%$
- $100\%$

## Explanation

A model predicting the mean for every observation has sum of squared deviations $SST=36$. With the fitted line this falls to $SSE=18$, an absolute reduction of $36-18=18$. Relative to the starting value the reduction is $\dfrac{18}{36}=0.5$, or $50\%$. This percentage reduction corresponds to $R^2$.
