Paired data have $S_{xx}=9$, $S_{yy}=25$, and $S_{xy}=12$. Calculate Pearson's correlation coefficient.

- $0.64$
- $\dfrac43$
- $\dfrac{12}{34}$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

Pearson's correlation divides the sum of centered cross-products by a measure of the spread of each variable to give a dimensionless number. The denominator is $\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}=\sqrt{9\cdot25}=15$. The numerator is $12$, giving $r=\dfrac{12}{15}=0.8$. The positive sign indicates that deviations from the two means tend to have the same sign in this sample.
