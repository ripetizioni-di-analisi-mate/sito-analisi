A horizontal line reproduces all the responses exactly because they are equal. What happens to the ratio defining $R^2$?

- It is undefined because $SST=0$.
- It equals $1$ because $SSE=0$.
- It equals $0$ because the slope is zero.
- It equals $-1$ because the dispersion is zero.

## Explanation

If all responses are equal, each equals their mean and the total sum of squares is $SST=0$. The horizontal line also has zero residuals, so $SSE=0$. However, definition $1-\dfrac{SSE}{SST}$ involves the undefined ratio $\dfrac00$. The fit is exact, while an explained fraction requires a positive total.
