Six pairs give $S_{xy}=15$. What is the sample covariance using the corrected divisor $n-1$?

- $3$
- $2.5$
- $15$
- $5$

## Explanation

The unbiased sample covariance divides the sum of centered cross-products by $n-1$ degrees of freedom. For six pairs the denominator is $5$, giving $\dfrac{15}{5}=3$. The numerator is already centered, so use it directly. Division by six would give the descriptive covariance with divisor $n$.
