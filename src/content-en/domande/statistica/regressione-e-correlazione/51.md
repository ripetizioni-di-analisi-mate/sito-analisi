A line with an intercept is fitted to four pairs under the normal model assumptions. How many degrees of freedom does the reference distribution for the slope test have?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The slope statistic uses the variance estimated from the residuals. In the normal model with an intercept, estimating two coefficients leaves $n-2$ residual degrees of freedom. Four pairs give $4-2=2$. The reference distribution is therefore Student's t with two degrees of freedom. The subtraction accounts for the parameters fitted to the sample.
