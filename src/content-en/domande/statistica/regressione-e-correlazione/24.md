The first three residuals in a regression with an intercept and four observations are $1,-4,2$. What is the fourth residual?

- $-1$
- $-7$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

With a freely fitted intercept, the normal equation for that intercept requires the residuals to sum to zero. The first three residuals sum to $1-4+2=-1$, so the fourth must equal $1$. This condition concerns the signed sum, and their sum of squares remains positive.
