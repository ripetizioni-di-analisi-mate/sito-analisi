Three pairs have predictor values $(4,4,9)$ and responses $(1,2,3)$. What is Spearman's coefficient using average ranks for ties?

- $\dfrac34$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first two predictor values occupy ranks $1$ and $2$ and both receive the average rank $1.5$. The response ranks are $(1,2,3)$. Subtracting the mean $2$ from both sequences gives $(-0.5,-0.5,1)$ and $(-1,0,1)$. Their cross-products sum to $1.5$ and their sums of squares are $1.5$ and $2$. Pearson's correlation of the ranks is therefore $\dfrac{1.5}{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
