With fixed predictor values, changing only the response $y_i$ by $\Delta$ changes its fitted value by $h_i\Delta$. If $h_i=0.75$ and $y_i$ increases by $4$, how much does $\hat y_i$ increase?

- $4$
- $0.75$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

With predictor values fixed, leverage gives the fraction of a change in $y_i$ absorbed by its own fitted value. Here $h_i=0.75$ and $\Delta=4$, so the change in $\hat y_i$ is $0.75\cdot4=3$. The remaining unit is added to the residual because the observed response rose by four while the fitted line rose by three at that point.
