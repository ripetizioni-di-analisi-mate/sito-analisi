For a regression with an intercept, $SST=50$ and $R^2=0.64$. Calculate $SSE$.

- $32$
- $36$
- $18$
- $0.36$

## Explanation

With an intercept, $R^2$ is the fraction of the total sum of squares explained by the regression. The residual fraction is therefore $1-0.64=0.36$. Multiplying this fraction by the total gives $SSE=50\cdot0.36=18$. The product $50\cdot0.64=32$ is the explained component. Adding the two components recovers the total $50$.
