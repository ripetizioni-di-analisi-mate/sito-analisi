Given $r=-\dfrac12$, $S_{xx}=9$, and $S_{yy}=36$, what is the slope in the regression of $y$ on $x$ with an intercept?

- $-\dfrac14$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac12$
- $1$

## Explanation

For the regression of $y$ on $x$, start from $b=\dfrac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}$. Since $S_{xy}=r\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}$, the ratio becomes $b=r\sqrt{\dfrac{S_{yy}}{S_{xx}}}$. The given data give square root $\sqrt{\dfrac{36}{9}}=2$ and hence $b=-\dfrac12\cdot2=-1$. Correlation determines the sign, and the ratio of the measures of spread converts the result into slope units.
