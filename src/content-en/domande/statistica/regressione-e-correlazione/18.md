The observations are $(2,7)$, $(4,8)$, and $(6,9)$. What are the coordinates of the second point after subtracting each variable's mean?

- $(0,4)$
- $(0,0)$
- $(4,8)$
- $(2,1)$

## Explanation

Center the predictor and response separately. Their means are $\bar x=\dfrac{2+4+6}{3}=4$ and $\bar y=\dfrac{7+8+9}{3}=8$. The second observation has coordinates $(4,8)$, exactly the point of means. Subtracting the respective means gives $(4-4,8-8)=(0,0)$, the origin of the centered coordinate system.
