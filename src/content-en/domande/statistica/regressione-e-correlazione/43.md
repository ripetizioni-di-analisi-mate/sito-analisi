Two regressions have the same positive residual standard deviation. In the second, $S_{xx}$ is four times its value in the first. What is the ratio of the standard errors of the slopes, second to first?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

In both regressions the standard error of the slope is $\dfrac{s_e}{\sqrt{S_{xx}}}$. The common residual standard deviation cancels in the ratio. If $S_{xx}$ in the second regression is four times its value in the first, its square root increases by a factor of $2$. This factor in the denominator halves the standard error, so the second-to-first ratio is $\dfrac12$.
