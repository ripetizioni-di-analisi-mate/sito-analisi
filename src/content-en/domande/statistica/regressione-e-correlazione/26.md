For line $\hat y(x)=1+x$, the observations are $(1,2)$, $(2,1)$, and $(3,5)$. Which predictor value gives the largest absolute residual?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- All the absolute values are equal.

## Explanation

Compare each observed response with the fitted value at the same predictor value. The fitted values are $(2,3,4)$ and their residuals are $(0,-2,1)$. The absolute residuals are $(0,2,1)$, with the maximum at $x=2$. The negative residual there places the point below the line, while its vertical distance is positive.
