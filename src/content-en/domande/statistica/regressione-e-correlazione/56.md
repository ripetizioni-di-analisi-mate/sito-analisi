A fitted line has $\hat y(x)=1+3x$. Under the normal model, an interval for the mean response and one for a new observation are constructed at $x_0=5$. What are their centers?

- The first is $16$ and the second is $17$.
- Both equal $16$.
- The first is $5$ and the second is $16$.
- Both equal $15$.

## Explanation

For both the mean and a new response the center is the fitted response at the specified predictor value. With $x_0=5$ this gives $\hat y(5)=1+3\cdot5=16$. Variation in a new observation changes the variance and hence the margin of the prediction interval. The center stays the same because the new error has mean zero.
