The regression of $y$ on $x$ has slope $0.8$ and $R^2=0.36$. What is the slope of the regression of $x$ on $y$ with an intercept for the same data?

- $1.25$
- $0.8$
- $0.45$
- $0.288$

## Explanation

The two slopes with intercepts are $\dfrac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}$ and $\dfrac{S_{xy}}{S_{yy}}$. Their product is $\dfrac{S_{xy}^2}{S_{xx}S_{yy}}=r^2=R^2$, giving the requested slope $\dfrac{0.36}{0.8}=0.45$. Swapping the variables makes least squares measure deviations along the new response axis. The product gives the relationship between the slopes.
