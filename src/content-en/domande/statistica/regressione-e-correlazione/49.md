The observed values are $b=2$ and $SE(b)=0.5$. The t quantile for a one-sided upper bound is $1.8$. What is the upper bound?

- $2.9$
- $1.1$
- $2.5$
- $3.8$

## Explanation

For an upper limit, add the margin to the estimate in the direction of larger slopes. With the given quantile the margin is $1.8\cdot0.5=0.9$, so the upper limit is $2+0.9=2.9$. A one-sided interval has just one finite endpoint. Subtracting the margin would give a limit in the opposite direction.

[Theory behind this method](/en/statistica/regressione-e-correlazione/teoria#inferenza).
