Measurements taken consecutively over time give long runs of positive residuals followed by long runs of negative residuals. Which check is appropriate?

- Check for possible dependence of errors over time.
- Check that predictor and response have the same units.
- Check that more residuals are positive than negative.
- Check that the intercept is zero.

## Explanation

In time order, neighboring residuals tend to have the same sign over long stretches. This suggests checking whether deviations close in time are associated. Classical inference on the slope assumes, among other things, independent errors. A plot of residuals against time helps examine a sequence that a count of signs cannot describe.
