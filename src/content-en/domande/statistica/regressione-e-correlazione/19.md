The centered form of a fitted line is $\hat y(x)=7-2(x-3)$. What is its intercept on the original $x$ scale?

- $13$
- $7$
- $1$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The intercept is the fitted value when the original predictor is zero. In centered form, at $x=0$ the deviation from the mean $3$ is $-3$, giving $\hat y(0)=7-2(0-3)=7+6=13$. The value $7$ is the fitted response at $x=3$. The two fitted values coincide only in special cases, such as a zero slope.
