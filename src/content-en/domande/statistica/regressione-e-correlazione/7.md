The regression line of $y$ on $x$ has slope $6$. Set $u=2x$ and $v=y+4$. Calculate the slope of the regression line of $v$ on $u$.

- $6$
- $3$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

A one-unit increase in $u=2x$ corresponds to half a unit on the $x$ scale. On the original line the response therefore increases by $6\cdot\dfrac12=3$. The transformation $v=y+4$ shifts every response equally and preserves vertical differences. The new slope is the ratio of the vertical increase $3$ to the horizontal increase $1$.
