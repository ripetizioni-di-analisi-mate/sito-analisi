A regression with an intercept fitted to $10$ units has residual standard deviation $s_e=1.5$. What residual sum of squares gives this value?

- $12$
- $15$
- $22.5$
- $18$

## Explanation

Recover the residual sum of squares by multiplying the estimated residual variance by its degrees of freedom. First square the standard deviation $1.5$, giving $s_e^2=2.25$. Estimating the intercept and slope leaves $10-2=8$ degrees of freedom, so $SSE=8\cdot2.25=18$. Multiplying by the standard deviation itself would produce a quantity with different units.
