A regression with an intercept fitted to $12$ units has $SST=50$ and $R^2=0.8$. What is the residual standard deviation $s_e$?

- $2$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac56}$
- $10$
- $1$

## Explanation

From $R^2=1-\dfrac{SSE}{SST}$ we get $SSE=(1-0.8)\cdot50=10$. The residual variance divides $SSE$ by the degrees of freedom $n-2=10$, because the intercept and the slope have been estimated, so $s_e^2=\dfrac{10}{10}=1$ and $s_e=1$. Dividing by $n=12$ would give $\sqrt{\dfrac{10}{12}}=\sqrt{\dfrac56}$, which ignores the two estimated coefficients. The value $2$ comes from using $SSR=40$ instead of $SSE$ by mistake.
