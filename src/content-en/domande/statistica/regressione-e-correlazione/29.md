A regression with an intercept gives $R^2=0.3$ and $SSE=14$. What is $SST$?

- $9.8$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{140}{3}$
- $20$

## Explanation

Since $R^2=1-\dfrac{SSE}{SST}$, the residual fraction is $1-0.3=0.7$. Thus $14$ represents $70\%$ of the total sum of squares. Dividing gives $SST=\dfrac{14}{0.7}=20$. The explained component is then $20-14=6$, or $30\%$ of the total as the data require.
