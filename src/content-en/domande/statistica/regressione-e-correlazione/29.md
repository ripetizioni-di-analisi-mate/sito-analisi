A regression with an intercept has slope $b=0.5$, $S_{xx}=40$, and $SST=25$. What is $R^2$?

- $0.8$
- $0.6$
- $0.5$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

The line passes through the point of means, so $\hat y_i-\bar y=b(x_i-\bar x)$ and $SSR=b^2S_{xx}$. The data give $SSR=0.5^2\cdot40=0.25\cdot40=10$. The coefficient of determination is therefore $R^2=\dfrac{SSR}{SST}=\dfrac{10}{25}=0.4$. The value $0.8$ would come from using $b$ instead of $b^2$, that is, $\dfrac{0.5\cdot40}{25}$, and the value $0.6$ is the fraction $\dfrac{SSE}{SST}$ left to the residuals.
