A regression with an intercept gives $SSR=45$ and $SSE=5$. What is $R^2$?

- $0.1$
- $\dfrac89$
- $9$
- $0.9$

## Explanation

With an intercept, the total sum of squares splits into explained and residual components. Here $SST=SSR+SSE=45+5=50$. The fraction explained by the regression is $R^2=\dfrac{45}{50}=0.9$. Dividing by the total shows that the line explains $90\%$ of the observed variability around the mean. The remaining $10\%$ is residual.
