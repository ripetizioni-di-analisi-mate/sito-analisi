In a regression with an intercept, $n=5$, $\bar x=4$, and $S_{xx}=20$. Calculate the leverage of an observation with $x_i=6$.

- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

In simple regression with an intercept, leverage includes a common term, $\dfrac1n$, and a term increasing with the squared distance of the predictor value from its mean. Here the distance is $6-4=2$, so $h_i=\dfrac15+\dfrac{2^2}{20}=\dfrac25$. Leverage depends on the predictor values and measures the potential of a point's location to affect the fit regardless of its response.
