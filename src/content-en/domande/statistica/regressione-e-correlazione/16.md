The pairs are $(1,6)$, $(2,2)$, $(3,4)$, $(4,3)$, and $(5,5)$. How many pairs make a negative contribution to the centered sum $S_{xy}$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The means are $\bar x=\dfrac{1+2+3+4+5}{5}=3$ and $\bar y=\dfrac{6+2+4+3+5}{5}=4$. The deviations of the predictor values are $-2,-1,0,1,2$ and those of the responses are $2,-2,0,-1,1$. The products of the deviations, pair by pair, are $-4$, $2$, $0$, $-1$, $2$. A contribution is negative when the two deviations have opposite signs, which happens for the first and the fourth pair. The number of pairs with a negative contribution is therefore $2$. The third pair contributes zero because its predictor deviation is zero. The sum is $S_{xy}=-4+2+0-1+2=-1$.
