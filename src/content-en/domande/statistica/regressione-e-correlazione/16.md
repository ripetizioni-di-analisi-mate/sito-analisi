For four pairs, the means are $\bar x=3$ and $\bar y=2$, and $S_{xy}=-8$. Find $\sum_i x_i y_i$.

- $-32$
- $24$
- $16$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The centered sum satisfies $S_{xy}=\sum_i x_i y_i-n\bar x\bar y$ because centering subtracts the contribution of the means. To recover the sum of the original cross-products, add $n\bar x\bar y=4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ to the centered value, giving $\sum_i x_i y_i=-8+24=16$. This term can make the signs of the two sums different.
