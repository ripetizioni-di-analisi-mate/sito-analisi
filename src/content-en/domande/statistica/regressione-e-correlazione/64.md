A least-squares line with an intercept uses predictor values $1,2,3$. Which vector could be its residuals?

- $(1,0,-1)$
- $(1,-2,1)$
- $(0,1,-1)$
- $(1,1,1)$

## Explanation

A least-squares line with an intercept satisfies $\sum_i e_i=0$ and $\sum_i x_i e_i=0$. For the vector $(1,-2,1)$, the first sum is $1-2+1=0$. With the given predictor values the second is $1\cdot1+2\cdot(-2)+3\cdot1=0$. The two zero sums are the two normal equations, so the vector satisfies both conditions. The other vectors do not: for $(1,0,-1)$ and $(0,1,-1)$ the sum weighted by the predictor values is $-2$ and $-1$, and for $(1,1,1)$ the residuals sum to $3$.
