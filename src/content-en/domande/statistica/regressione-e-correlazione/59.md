A regression with an intercept fitted to $n=10$ units has $s_e=3$ and, at the predictor value $x_0=\bar x$, the fitted value $\hat y_0=50$. With $t_{8,0.975}=2.306$, what is the $95\%$ prediction interval for a new response at $x_0$?

- $[47.81;\,52.19]$
- $[43.08;\,56.92]$
- $[42.74;\,57.26]$
- $[42.39;\,57.61]$

## Explanation

At $x_0=\bar x$ we have $h_0=\dfrac1n=\dfrac1{10}$. The prediction interval uses $\sqrt{1+h_0}=\sqrt{1.1}\approx1.0488$, so the margin is $2.306\cdot3\cdot1.0488\approx7.26$. The interval is $50\pm7.26$, that is, $[42.74;\,57.26]$. The interval $[47.81;\,52.19]$ uses $\sqrt{h_0}$ and refers to the mean response. The interval $[43.08;\,56.92]$ ignores $h_0$, and $[42.39;\,57.61]$ uses $1+h_0$ without the square root.
