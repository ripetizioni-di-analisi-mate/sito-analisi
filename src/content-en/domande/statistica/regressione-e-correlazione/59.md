At the same confidence level and predictor value, the prediction interval is twice as wide as the mean response interval, which has positive width. What is $h_0$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $\dfrac13$
- $3$

## Explanation

At the same predictor value and confidence level, the ratio of the widths equals the ratio of the standard errors. Thus $\sqrt{\dfrac{1+h_0}{h_0}}=2$. Squaring gives $1+h_0=4h_0$ and hence $h_0=\dfrac13$. Squaring is necessary because the weight enters the variance, while the interval width is proportional to the standard deviation.
