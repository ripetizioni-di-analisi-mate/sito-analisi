The test of $\beta_0=2$ gives $T=-1.5$ and the standard error is $0.4$. Which estimated slope gives this result?

- $2.6$
- $0.5$
- $1.4$
- $-0.6$

## Explanation

The test statistic expresses the difference between the estimated slope and the null slope in standard-error units. Multiplying $T=-1.5$ by $SE(b)=0.4$ gives the difference on the slope scale, $-0.6$. Add this difference to $\beta_0=2$ to obtain $b=2-0.6=1.4$. The negative sign of $T$ indicates an estimate below the hypothesized value.
