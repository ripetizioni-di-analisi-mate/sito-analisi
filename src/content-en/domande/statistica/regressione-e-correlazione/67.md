A line predicting mass in kilograms from age in years has slope $0.7$. What are the units of its slope?

- $\mathrm{year/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg/year}$
- No units.

## Explanation

Slope is a change in the response divided by a change in the predictor. The response here is mass measured in kilograms, and age is measured in years. The slope's units are therefore kilograms per year. The value $0.7$ means that the line assigns an increase of $0.7\,\mathrm{kg}$ in fitted mass to each additional year.
