Two scatterplots both show an increasing trend. In the first the points follow a pronounced curve, while in the second they lie around a straight line. Which feature best distinguishes the cases before a model is fitted?

- The shape of the scatterplot and any curvature.
- Only the positive sign of $S_{xy}$.
- The fact that both sets of predictor values have means.
- Only the value of the intercept.

## Explanation

The positive sign of $S_{xy}$ summarizes an increasing tendency in both plots. The positions of the points show whether the increase has a roughly constant slope or pronounced curvature. In the first plot, the shape suggests that a single slope could leave systematic residuals. In the second, the cloud about the line is consistent with a linear pattern.
