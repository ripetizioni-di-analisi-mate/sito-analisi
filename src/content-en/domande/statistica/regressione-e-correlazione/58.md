At a given predictor value, the weight for the mean response is $h_0=\dfrac14$. For a fixed confidence level, what is the ratio of the individual prediction half-width to the mean response half-width?

- $5$
- $2$
- $\dfrac54$
- $\sqrt5$

## Explanation

At the same level both margins use the same t quantile and residual standard deviation. Their ratio reduces to $\sqrt{\dfrac{1+h_0}{h_0}}$. With $h_0=\dfrac14$ the quantity under the square root is $\dfrac{1+\frac14}{\frac14}=5$. The ratio is $\sqrt5$, greater than one because the prediction includes the variation of the new response.
