In a regression with an intercept, $S_{xx}=12$ and $S_{xy}=-9$. Calculate the slope $b$.

- $\dfrac34$
- $-\dfrac43$
- $-3$
- $-\dfrac34$

## Explanation

For a line with an intercept, divide the sum of centered cross-products by the sum of squared centered predictor values. The given data yield $b=\dfrac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}=\dfrac{-9}{12}=-\dfrac34$. The denominator is positive, so the slope has the sign of $S_{xy}$. A one-unit increase in $x$ corresponds to a three-quarter-unit decrease in the fitted response.
