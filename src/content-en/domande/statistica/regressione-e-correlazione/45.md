In the normal model with an intercept, $n=20$, $b=-1.1$, and $SE(b)=0.25$. Test $\beta=-0.5$ against $\beta<-0.5$ at level $5\%$, with lower critical value $-1.734$. What is the decision?

- Fail to reject $H_0$ because $T=-2.4$ is negative.
- Reject $H_0$ with $T=-2.4$.
- Reject $H_0$ with $T=2.4$.
- Fail to reject $H_0$ with $T=-0.15$.

## Explanation

The test compares the estimated slope with the null value $-0.5$. The difference is $-1.1-(-0.5)=-0.6$, giving $T=\dfrac{-0.6}{0.25}=-2.4$. The alternative specifies a smaller slope, so the rejection region is in the left tail. The statistic falls below the critical value $-1.734$, and the test rejects $H_0$ at the specified level.
