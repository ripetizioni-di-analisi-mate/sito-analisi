For four pairs without ties, the ranks of $x$ are $(1,2,3,4)$ and those of $y$ are $(1,3,2,4)$. What is Spearman's coefficient?

- $0.6$
- $0.8$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

There are no tied ranks, so use the rank-difference formula. Subtracting the ranks of $y$ from those of $x$ gives $(0,-1,1,0)$, with sum of squares $2$. For $n=4$ this yields $r_S=1-\dfrac{6\cdot2}{4(4^2-1)}=0.8$. Only the two middle ranks are interchanged, so only they contribute to the reduction in correlation.
