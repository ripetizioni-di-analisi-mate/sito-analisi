For four pairs without ties, the ranks of $x$ are $(1,2,3,4)$ and those of $y$ are $(1,3,2,4)$. What is Spearman's coefficient?

- $0.6$
- $0.8$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

Spearman's coefficient is Pearson's correlation calculated from the ranks. Both mean ranks equal $\dfrac{1+2+3+4}{4}=2.5$. The deviations from the mean are $(-1.5,-0.5,0.5,1.5)$ for $x$ and $(-1.5,0.5,-0.5,1.5)$ for $y$. Both sums of squares equal $2.25+0.25+0.25+2.25=5$, and the sum of products is $2.25-0.25-0.25+2.25=4$. Therefore $r_S=\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{5\cdot5}}=0.8$. The value is less than $1$ because the two middle ranks of $y$ are interchanged.
