The independent observations $3,4,6,5$ come from a uniform distribution with support $[0,\theta]$ and positive density at the endpoints. What is the maximum likelihood estimate of $\theta$?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The largest observation is $6$, so all the data lie in the support only when $\theta\ge6$. For admissible values the product of the four densities is $\theta^{-4}$, which decreases as $\theta$ increases. The specified endpoint convention also admits $\theta=6$. The maximum is therefore attained at the smallest compatible value.
