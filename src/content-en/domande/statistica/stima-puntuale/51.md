Three independent exponential waiting times with rate $\lambda$ have observed sum $6$. What is the derivative of the log-likelihood at $\lambda=1$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $-6$
- $0$

## Explanation

The product of the three densities contains $\lambda^3$ and the factor $e^{-6\lambda}$ because the observations sum to six. The log-likelihood is therefore $\ell(\lambda)=3\log\lambda-6\lambda$. Its derivative is $\ell'(\lambda)=\dfrac3\lambda-6$ and at $\lambda=1$ it equals $3-6=-3$. The sign means that at the specified point the log-likelihood decreases as the rate increases.
