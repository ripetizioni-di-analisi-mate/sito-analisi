Four independent observations give $s^2=6$. What is the sum of squared deviations from the observed mean?

- $6$
- $2$
- $18$
- $24$

## Explanation

The unbiased sample variance divides the sum of squared deviations by $n-1$. Denote the sum by $D$. Here $6=\dfrac{D}{4-1}$, so multiplication by three gives $D=18$. The required value is the numerator of the observed variance, recovered using the divisor in the definition of $s^2$.
