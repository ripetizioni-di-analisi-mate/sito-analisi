In model $U(0,\theta)$ the second moment is $\dfrac{\theta^2}{3}$. If only this moment is equated to the sample second moment $12$, what is the resulting value of $\theta>0$?

- $36$
- $12$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Equate the theoretical second moment $\dfrac{\theta^2}{3}$ and the sample second moment $12$. Multiplication by three gives $\theta^2=36$. The parameter is the positive endpoint of the support, so the admissible solution is $\theta=\sqrt{36}=6$. Stopping at $36$ would leave the squared parameter. The negative root is outside the specified domain.
