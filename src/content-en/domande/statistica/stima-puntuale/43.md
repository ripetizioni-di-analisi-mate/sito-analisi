Six successes are observed in six independent trials. If the domain is $p\in[0,1]$, what is the maximum likelihood estimate?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Each of the six trials contributes the factor $p$, giving $L(p)=p^6$. The function increases on $[0,1]$ and reaches its highest value at the admissible right endpoint $p=1$. The estimate equals the observed proportion $\dfrac66$. The value $\dfrac56$ would correspond to a sample with one failure.
