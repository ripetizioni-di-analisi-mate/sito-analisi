Two estimators have opposite biases of absolute value $1$. The first has variance $5$ and the second $3$. How do their MSEs compare?

- The second exceeds the first by $2$
- Both MSEs are equal
- The second exceeds the first by $1$
- The first exceeds the second by $2$

## Explanation

The biases have opposite signs and absolute value one, so each contributes $1^2=1$ to the mean squared error. The totals are $5+1=6$ for the first and $3+1=4$ for the second. The first exceeds the second by $6-4=2$. Because the squared biases are equal, only the variances contribute to this difference.
