A Poisson sample contains only zeros. The model allows $\lambda=0$. Where is the likelihood maximized?

- The function is constant
- $\lambda=0$
- $\lambda=n$
- $\lambda=1$

## Explanation

If all $n$ counts are zero, each probability mass is $P_\lambda(X=0)=e^{-\lambda}$ and their product is $L(\lambda)=e^{-n\lambda}$. The function decreases as $\lambda$ increases, so the maximum is at the smallest admissible parameter, $\lambda=0$. At that point all observations are zero with probability one, as in the degenerate Poisson distribution.
