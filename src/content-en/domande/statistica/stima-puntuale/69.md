Given two independent observations $2,4$ from $U(0,\theta)$ with positive density at the endpoints, what is the ratio $\dfrac{L(4)}{L(8)}$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The value $4$ is compatible with the observations because the boundary observation has positive density. The support for $8$ also contains both observations. Independence therefore gives $L(4)=4^{-2}=\dfrac1{16}$ and $L(8)=8^{-2}=\dfrac1{64}$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{64}{16}=4$. Doubling the support length halves each density, reducing the product to one-quarter.
