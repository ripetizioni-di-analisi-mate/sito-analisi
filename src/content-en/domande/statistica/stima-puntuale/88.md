The observations $3,6,8$ are reported as coming from $U(\theta,\theta+4)$ with positive density at the endpoints. What does the support indicate?

- Only $\theta=3$ is compatible with the observations
- Every $\theta\in[3,4]$ is compatible with the observations
- No parameter is compatible with all the observations
- Only $\theta=4$ is compatible with the observations

## Explanation

To contain the minimum $3$, the support's lower endpoint must satisfy $\theta\le3$. To contain the maximum $8$, its upper endpoint must satisfy $\theta+4\ge8$, giving $\theta\ge4$. The conditions are incompatible even with closed endpoints. The sample range is five, greater than the support length of four. Every parameter value gives at least one observation density zero.
