The sequence $1,0,1,0$ is recorded in independent Bernoulli trials. Which function is its exact likelihood?

- $p^4$
- $6p^2(1-p)^2$
- $p^2(1-p)^2$
- $2p(1-p)$

## Explanation

The observation is a particular sequence of four independent outcomes. Their masses multiply to $p(1-p)p(1-p)=p^2(1-p)^2$. The coefficient $\binom42=6$ would count all sequences with two successes if only the count were observed. Because the order is specified, each position contributes the factor for its actual outcome.
