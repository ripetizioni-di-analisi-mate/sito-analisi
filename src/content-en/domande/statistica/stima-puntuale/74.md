For a sequence of estimators the error $T_n-\theta$ equals $n$ with probability $\dfrac1n$ and zero otherwise. Which conclusion holds?

- It is consistent and its MSE is zero
- The sequence is consistent and its MSE diverges
- It is inconsistent because arbitrarily large errors occur
- It is unbiased and its MSE tends to zero

## Explanation

Fix a threshold $\varepsilon>0$. For $n>\varepsilon$ the error exceeds it only when the error equals $n$. This event has probability $\dfrac1n$ tending to zero, so the sequence is consistent. Mean squared error also accounts for the magnitude of the rare error. It is $n^2\cdot\dfrac1n=n$, which diverges because the square grows faster than its probability decreases.
