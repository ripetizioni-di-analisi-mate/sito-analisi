In a normal model, the first two sample moments are $5$ and $34$, respectively. What is the method-of-moments estimate of the variance?

- $9$
- $29$
- $25$
- $34$

## Explanation

For a normal distribution the first theoretical moment is $\mu$ and the second is $\mu^2+\sigma^2$. The first sample moment gives $\widehat\mu=5$. The second then gives $25+\widehat{\sigma^2}=34$ and hence $\widehat{\sigma^2}=34-25=9$. Subtraction removes the location component because the second moment about zero includes the squared mean.
