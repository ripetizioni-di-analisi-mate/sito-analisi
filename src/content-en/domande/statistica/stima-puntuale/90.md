Two procedures give unbiased estimators of the same parameter with variances $v_T(\theta)$ and $v_U(\theta)$. For one value of $\theta$, $v_T<v_U$ holds. Which conclusion is justified?

- $T$ has lower MSE at every admissible parameter value
- $T$ is consistent for every sequence of samples
- $U$ has smaller bias at that parameter value
- $T$ has lower MSE at that parameter value

## Explanation

Both estimators are unbiased, so neither has a bias contribution. At the specified value of $\theta$, we therefore have $MSE_\theta(T)=v_T(\theta)$ and $MSE_\theta(U)=v_U(\theta)$. The given inequality guarantees a smaller mean squared error for $T$ at that point. To establish an advantage over the entire parameter space, compare the two variance functions at every admissible value.
