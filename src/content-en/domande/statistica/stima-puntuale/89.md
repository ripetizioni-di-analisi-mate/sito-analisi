A measurement $X$ has mean $\theta$ and variance $9$. For $\theta=0$ consider $T=\dfrac13X$. By how much does the MSE of $X$ exceed that of $T$?

- $0$
- $6$
- $3$
- $8$

## Explanation

At $\theta=0$ the expectation of $T=\dfrac13X$ is also zero. Both mean squared errors therefore equal the corresponding variances. For $X$ the variance is $9$, and for $T$ it is $\left(\dfrac13\right)^2\cdot9=1$. The required difference is $9-1=8$. This comparison is at the stated parameter value because at other values $T$ would have nonzero bias.
