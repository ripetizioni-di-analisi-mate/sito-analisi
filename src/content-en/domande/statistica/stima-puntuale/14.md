A Bernoulli model allows only $p=\dfrac14$ or $p=\dfrac34$. Three independent trials yield, in order, a success, a failure, and a success. Which value is the maximum likelihood estimate of $p$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the three factors for the observed sequence gives $L(p)=p(1-p)p$. The likelihood values at the two admissible points are $L\!\left(\dfrac14\right)=\dfrac3{64}$ and $L\!\left(\dfrac34\right)=\dfrac9{64}$. The second is larger, so the estimate is $\dfrac34$. The observed proportion $\dfrac23$ would maximize the likelihood on the full domain, but the two-point model excludes it.
