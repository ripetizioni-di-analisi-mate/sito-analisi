A measurement $X$ has expectation $\theta$ and variance $4$. For $\theta=0$, what is the MSE of the estimator $X+1$?

- $5$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

At $\theta=0$ the measurement has expectation zero and $X+1$ has expectation one. The bias relative to the true parameter is therefore $1-0=1$. The variance stays $4$ because adding a constant shifts all observations equally. The decomposition gives $MSE_0(X+1)=4+1^2=5$.
