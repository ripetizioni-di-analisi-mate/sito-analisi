An estimator $T$ has unknown bias $b$ and variance $4$. Adding the constant $2$ to $T$ leaves its mean squared error unchanged. What is $b$?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $-2$
- $0$

## Explanation

Translation leaves variance equal to $4$ and changes bias from $b$ to $b+2$. Equal mean squared errors require $(b+2)^2=b^2$, or $4b+4=0$.
