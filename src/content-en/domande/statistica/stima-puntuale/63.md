The data from $U(0,\theta)$ are $1,2,7$, with the endpoints included in the density. The method-of-moments estimate is $2\bar x$. What is true of the likelihood at this estimate?

- It is maximized because the theoretical and sample moments agree
- It equals zero because $2\bar x<7$
- It is positive because $2\bar x>0$
- It equals $\dfrac1{7^3}$

## Explanation

The three observations have mean $\dfrac{1+2+7}{3}=\dfrac{10}{3}$, giving method-of-moments estimate $\dfrac{20}{3}$. It is positive but below the largest observation, $7$. At this estimate the observation $7$ lies outside the support and has density zero, so the product of the three densities is zero.
