For an independent Poisson sample, $n=5$ and the observed sum is $s=20$. At which value does $\ell'(\lambda)=\dfrac{s}{\lambda}-n$ vanish?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $20$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The stated derivative becomes $\dfrac{20}{\lambda}-5$. For $\lambda>0$, multiply by $\lambda$ and set $20-5\lambda=0$, giving $\lambda=4$. The derivative is positive before four and negative after four, so this point is the maximum. For a Poisson model it equals the mean of the five counts.
