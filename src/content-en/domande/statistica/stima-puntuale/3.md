A sample consists of $1,2,6$. What is the unbiased sample variance $s^2$?

- $7$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{14}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The observed mean is $\bar x=\dfrac{1+2+6}{3}=3$. The deviations are $-2,-1,3$ and their squared sum is $4+1+9=14$. The **unbiased sample variance** uses divisor $n-1=2$, giving $s^2=\dfrac{14}{2}=7$. Dividing by three would give the descriptive variance of the same data.
