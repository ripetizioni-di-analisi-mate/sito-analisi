A log-likelihood has derivative $5-2\theta$ on $\theta\in[0,4]$. Which value is its maximizer?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The stationarity condition is $5-2\theta=0$, or $\theta=\dfrac52$. The point lies inside $[0,4]$. The derivative is positive to its left and negative to its right, so the log-likelihood increases up to it and then decreases. This sign change establishes a maximum on the entire specified interval.
