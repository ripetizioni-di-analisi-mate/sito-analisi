A population has variance $18$. For six independent observations, what is the expectation of $V_6=\dfrac16\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)^2$?

- $15$
- $18$
- $3$
- $21$

## Explanation

The deviations are taken from the sample mean estimated using the same six observations. Their sum of squares has expectation $(6-1)\sigma^2=5\cdot18=90$. The proposed estimator divides this sum by six, so $E[V_6]=\dfrac{90}{6}=15$. Dividing by five would give an estimator whose expectation equals the population variance.
