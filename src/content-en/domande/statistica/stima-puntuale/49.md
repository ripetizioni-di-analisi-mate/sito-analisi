The maximum likelihood estimate of a normal variance is $25$. What is the maximum likelihood estimate of the standard deviation?

- $5$
- $625$
- $25$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The standard deviation is $\sigma=\sqrt{\sigma^2}$. For positive values this is a one-to-one reparameterization, so apply the same function to the maximum likelihood estimate of the variance. Here $\widehat\sigma=\sqrt{25}=5$. The square root also returns the estimate to the units of the observations, whereas variance has squared units.
