Two measurements $X_1,X_2$ each have variance $4$, and their covariance is $2$. What is the variance of $\dfrac{X_1+X_2}{2}$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The measurements are correlated, so add twice their covariance to the sum of the variances, giving $\operatorname{Var}(X_1+X_2)=4+4+2\cdot2=12$. The mean multiplies the sum by $\dfrac12$, giving variance $\dfrac{12}{4}=3$. Omitting the covariance would give $2$, the result for uncorrelated measurements.
