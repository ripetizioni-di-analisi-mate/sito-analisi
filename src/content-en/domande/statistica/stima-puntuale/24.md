A sample of five values gives $\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2=36$. By how much does the unbiased sample variance exceed the variance with divisor $n$?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{36}{5}$

## Explanation

The quantity $36$ is the same sum of squared deviations for both variance measures. With five observations the unbiased sample variance is $\dfrac{36}{4}=9$ and the variance with divisor $n$ is $\dfrac{36}{5}$. The required difference is $9-\dfrac{36}{5}=\dfrac95$. It is positive because the unbiased sample variance divides the same sum by a smaller number.
