Given $b_\theta(T)=2$ and $\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T)=7$, what is the variance of the unbiased estimator $T-2$?

- $11$
- $7$
- $9$
- $5$

## Explanation

Subtracting two offsets the discrepancy between the expectation and the parameter. The center of the distribution also shifts by two, giving $(T-2)-E_\theta[T-2]=T-E_\theta[T]$. Deviations from its own center and their squares stay the same. Hence $\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T-2)=\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T)=7$. The correction shifts the estimator's location and preserves its spread.
