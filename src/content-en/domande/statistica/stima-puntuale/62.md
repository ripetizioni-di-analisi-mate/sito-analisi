In the model $U(0,\theta)$ with $\theta=10$, what is the bias of the maximum of four independent observations?

- $8$
- $-\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

For four uniform observations the distribution function of the maximum is $\left(\dfrac{x}{\theta}\right)^4$ for $0\le x\le\theta$. This gives $E_\theta[M]=\dfrac45\theta$. Substituting $\theta=10$ yields expectation $8$. Subtracting the parameter gives bias $b_{10}(M)=8-10=-2$. The sample maximum tends to underestimate the upper endpoint of the support.
