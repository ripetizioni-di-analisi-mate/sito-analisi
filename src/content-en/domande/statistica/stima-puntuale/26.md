An independent sample of size $4$ comes from a population with known mean $\mu$ and variance $12$. What is $E[\dfrac13\sum_i(X_i-\mu)^2]$?

- $16$
- $4$
- $12$
- $9$

## Explanation

Here the center $\mu$ is known and fixed, so each of the four squares $(X_i-\mu)^2$ has expectation $12$. By linearity their sum has expectation $4\cdot12=48$. The given factor $\dfrac13$ therefore yields $\dfrac{48}{3}=16$. A divisor of three would apply to the unbiased estimator based on deviations from the sample mean.
