In ten independent Bernoulli trials the observed success count is $4$. Which value maximizes the likelihood on $p\in[0.1;0.7]$?

- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

For four successes in ten trials the log-likelihood derivative has the sign of the numerator $4-10p$ for $0<p<1$. The function increases up to $p=\dfrac4{10}=\dfrac25$ and then decreases. Since $0.1<0.4<0.7$, the interior maximum belongs to the constrained interval. The constraint would alter the estimate only if it excluded that point.
