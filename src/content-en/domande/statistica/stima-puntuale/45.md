From ten independent Bernoulli trials, the total number of successes $K=4$ and the order of only three outcomes have been retained. To compare the likelihoods of $p=0.4$ and $p=0.6$, must the order of all ten outcomes be recovered?

- Yes: an early success carries more weight than a late one
- No: the likelihood ratio depends only on $K$ and $n$
- Yes: the position of the last failure is needed
- No: the two parameters always have equal likelihood

## Explanation

Every sequence with four successes has likelihood $p^4(1-p)^6$. Working from the count adds a factor $\binom{10}{4}$, which cancels in the ratio between the two parameter values. The order does not enter the comparison.
