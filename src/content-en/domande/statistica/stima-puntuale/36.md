For a normal distribution with known mean $\mu=2$, the second moment is used to estimate $\sigma^2$. If its sample value is $10$, what estimate is obtained?

- $12$
- $8$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

The second moment is taken about zero and equals $E[X^2]=\mu^2+\sigma^2$. The known mean contributes $\mu^2=2^2=4$. To match the sample moment to $10$ impose $4+\widehat{\sigma^2}=10$, giving $\widehat{\sigma^2}=6$. The second moment equals the variance directly only when the contribution of the mean vanishes.
