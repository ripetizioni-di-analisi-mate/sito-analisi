Under the same model, two unbiased estimators of the same parameter have variances $2$ and $5$. What is the ratio of their MSEs, second to first?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $3$

## Explanation

For each unbiased estimator, the bias is zero and the mean squared error equals the variance. The second has mean squared error $5$ and the first $2$, giving the required ratio $\dfrac52$. Reversing the order would give its reciprocal $\dfrac25$. The second estimator's mean squared error is two and a half times the first's.

[Theory behind this method](/en/statistica/stima-puntuale/teoria#efficienza).
