The size of an independent sample from the same population is multiplied by nine. By what factor does the standard deviation of $\bar X$ change?

- $3$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

For the same population the standard deviation $\sigma$ of an individual observation is constant. The standard error goes from $\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$ to $\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{9n}}=\dfrac{1}{3}\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}$, one-third of its previous value. The variance of the mean is inversely proportional to $n$ and becomes one-ninth of its original value.
