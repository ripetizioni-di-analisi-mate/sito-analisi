An estimator has MSE $0.01$. What upper bound does Markov's inequality give for the probability of an absolute error greater than $0.5$?

- $\dfrac{1}{50}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$

## Explanation

The squared error $Y=(T-\theta)^2$ is nonnegative and has expectation equal to the mean squared error. The event that absolute error exceeds $0.5$ is $Y>0.25$. Applying Markov's inequality at this threshold gives $P(Y>0.25)\le\dfrac{0.01}{0.25}=0.04=\dfrac1{25}$. The threshold is squared because the inequality is being applied to the squared error.
