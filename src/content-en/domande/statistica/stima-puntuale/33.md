For the uniform distribution $U(0,\theta)$, use the first-moment equation. If $\bar x=7$, what is the method-of-moments estimate of $\theta$?

- $49$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

The expectation of the uniform distribution is the midpoint of its support, so $E_\theta[X]=\dfrac\theta2$. The method of moments equates this midpoint to the observed mean. Solving $\dfrac\theta2=7$ gives $\widehat\theta=14$. The target is the upper endpoint of the support. The observed mean therefore supplies half the estimated endpoint.
