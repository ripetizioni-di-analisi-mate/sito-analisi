Independent Bernoulli trials yield eleven successes and four failures. What is the maximum likelihood estimate of $p\in[0,1]$?

- $\dfrac{15}{11}$
- $\dfrac{11}{4}$
- $\dfrac{11}{15}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$

## Explanation

The factors for the fifteen independent trials give a likelihood proportional to $p^{11}(1-p)^4$. The log-likelihood derivative is $\dfrac{11-15p}{p(1-p)}$. Its denominator is positive inside the interval, so the likelihood increases up to $\dfrac{11}{15}$ and then decreases. The estimate is the fraction of successes among all trials, including the four failures.
