A variance estimator uses divisor $n$ with deviations from the sample mean. If $n=8$ and $\sigma^2=16$, what is its bias?

- $-16$
- $14$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

The sum of squared deviations from the sample mean has expectation $(n-1)\sigma^2$. Using divisor $n$ therefore gives expectation $\dfrac{n-1}{n}\sigma^2$. Substituting $n=8$ and $\sigma^2=16$ yields $\dfrac78\cdot16=14$. The bias is $14-16=-2$. Its negative sign means this divisor leads to underestimation of the variance on average.
