The sample maximum $M_n$ of $n$ independent observations from $U(0,\theta)$ is used to estimate $\theta$. What is the smallest $n$ for which $P_\theta(M_n<0.8\,\theta)\le0.1$?

- $10$
- $8$
- $11$
- $5$

## Explanation

The event $\{M_n<0.8\,\theta\}$ occurs exactly when all $n$ observations are less than $0.8\,\theta$. Each one is with probability $\dfrac{0.8\,\theta}{\theta}=0.8$, and by independence the probabilities multiply, so
$$
P_\theta(M_n<0.8\,\theta)=0.8^n .
$$
We have $0.8^{10}\approx0.107$, which exceeds $0.1$, and $0.8^{11}\approx0.086$, which does not. The smallest $n$ is therefore $11$. The probability tends to zero as $n$ grows and does not depend on $\theta$, in agreement with the consistency of the sample maximum.
