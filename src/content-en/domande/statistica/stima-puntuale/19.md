An estimator has mean squared error $19$ and bias $-3$. What is its variance?

- $10$
- $16$
- $22$
- $28$

## Explanation

Mean squared error decomposes into variance plus squared bias. The contribution from the discrepancy in the expectation is $(-3)^2=9$. Subtracting it from the total gives $\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T)=19-9=10$. The sign of the bias describes the direction of the average discrepancy, while its square is used to recover the variance.
