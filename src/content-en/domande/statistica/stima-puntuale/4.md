From a Poisson population with parameter $\lambda$, the observations are $1,2,6,3$. What is the method-of-moments estimate of $\lambda$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

The method of moments equates the first population moment to the observed first moment. For the Poisson distribution, $E_\lambda[X]=\lambda$. The four counts sum to $12$, so $\bar x=\dfrac{12}{4}=3$ and the moment equation is $\lambda=3$. The total counts all four observations together and must be divided by the sample size.
