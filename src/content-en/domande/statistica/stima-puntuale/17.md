If $E_\theta[T]=\dfrac34\theta$ for every $\theta>0$, for which constant $c$ is $cT$ an unbiased estimator?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $4$

## Explanation

Multiplying the estimator by $c$ multiplies its expectation by the same factor, giving $E_\theta[cT]=\dfrac{3c}{4}\theta$. For this to equal $\theta$ for every positive parameter value, the coefficient must equal one. Solving $\dfrac{3c}{4}=1$ gives $c=\dfrac43$. The reciprocal factor offsets the reduction in the original expectation.
