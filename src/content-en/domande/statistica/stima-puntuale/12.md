Five independent Bernoulli trials yield one success. The model requires $p\in[0.4;0.8]$. What is the maximum likelihood estimate under this constraint?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

In the interior the log-likelihood is $\log p+4\log(1-p)$ with derivative $\dfrac{1-5p}{p(1-p)}$. The unconstrained maximum would be $p=\dfrac15$. On the specified interval $p\ge0.4$, so the numerator is always negative and the function decreases. The constrained maximum is the left endpoint $0.4=\dfrac25$, an admissible parameter value.
