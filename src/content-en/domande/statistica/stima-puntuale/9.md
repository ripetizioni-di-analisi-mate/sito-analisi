The independent observations $2,4,6$ come from a normal distribution with both parameters unknown. What is the maximum likelihood estimate of the variance?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

Under the normal model the maximum likelihood estimate of the mean is the observed mean $\bar x=4$. The deviations from $4$ are $-2,0,2$ and their squares sum to $8$. The maximum likelihood estimate of the variance is therefore $\dfrac{8}{3}$. The divisor $n-1$ belongs to the unbiased variance estimator, which uses a different criterion.
