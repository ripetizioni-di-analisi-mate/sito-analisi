The estimator $T_n=X_1$ is used for the mean of a population with positive finite variance, regardless of $n$. Which property does it have?

- It is unbiased and the sequence is consistent
- It is unbiased but the sequence is inconsistent
- It is biased and the sequence is consistent
- Its variance decreases as $\dfrac1n$

## Explanation

The first observation has expectation $\mu$, so $T_n=X_1$ is unbiased at every sample size. The rule always uses only one observation. Positive variance implies there is a threshold $\varepsilon>0$ for which $P(\lvert X_1-\mu\rvert>\varepsilon)>0$. This probability is constant for all $n$, so the sequence lacks the concentration around $\mu$ required for consistency.
