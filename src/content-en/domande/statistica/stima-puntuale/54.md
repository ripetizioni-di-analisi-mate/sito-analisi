The bias of the maximum likelihood estimator of an exponential rate must be at most $20\,\%$ of the rate itself. What is the smallest required sample size $n>1$?

- $5$
- $7$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

For $n>1$ the maximum likelihood estimator of the rate has expectation $\dfrac{n}{n-1}\lambda$, giving relative bias $\dfrac1{n-1}$. The requirement is $\dfrac1{n-1}\le\dfrac15$, or $n-1\ge5$. The smallest admissible sample size is $6$. With five observations the bias would still be one-quarter of the rate, above the specified threshold.
