A sample from a single population is used to estimate $P(X>5)$. Which statistic can be calculated directly for this purpose?

- $P(X>5)$
- $\max_i X_i$
- $\dfrac1n\sum_i X_i$
- $\dfrac1n\sum_i\mathbf 1_{\{X_i>5\}}$

## Explanation

Each indicator $\mathbf 1_{\{X_i>5\}}$ is one if the observation exceeds the threshold and zero otherwise. Their sum counts exceedances, and division by $n$ turns that count into an observable relative frequency. A single indicator has expectation $P(X>5)$, so their mean estimates the required probability.
