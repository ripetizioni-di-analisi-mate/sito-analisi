In a normal model with both parameters unknown, five observations give unbiased sample variance $s^2=15$. What is the maximum likelihood estimate of $\sigma^2$?

- $15$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{75}{4}$
- $12$

## Explanation

The observed unbiased sample variance uses divisor $n-1=4$, so the sum of squared deviations is $D=4\cdot15=60$. In a normal model with unknown mean, the maximum likelihood estimate uses the same deviations from the observed mean and divides by $n=5$. This gives $\widehat{\sigma^2}=\dfrac{60}{5}=12$. The choice of divisor explains the difference from $s^2$.
