Four waiting times from an exponential model with rate $\lambda>0$ are $2,3,7,8$ minutes. What is the numerical value of the method-of-moments estimate of $\lambda$, in $\mathrm{min}^{-1}$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

For an exponential distribution parameterized by its rate, the first moment is $E_\lambda[X]=\dfrac1\lambda$. The observed waiting times sum to $20\,\mathrm{min}$, so the sample first moment is $\dfrac{20}{4}=5\,\mathrm{min}$. The equation $\dfrac1\lambda=5$ gives the estimate $\dfrac15\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$. Five is the observed mean time. Its reciprocal has the units of a rate.
