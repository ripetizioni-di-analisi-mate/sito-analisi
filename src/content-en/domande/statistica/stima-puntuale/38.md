At a fixed parameter value, two independent observations have probability masses $\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac25$. What is their joint contribution to the likelihood?

- $\dfrac{13}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

For fixed parameter, independence allows the two observed masses to be multiplied to obtain the joint probability $\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac25=\dfrac2{20}=\dfrac1{10}$. The likelihood assesses compatibility with both observations simultaneously. Addition would apply to the union of mutually exclusive events. Here both observations must occur.
