At a particular parameter value, $MSE_\theta(T)=16$ and $\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T)=7$. What is the absolute bias?

- $3$
- $23$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

Subtract the variance from the mean squared error to obtain the squared bias. The given values give $b_\theta(T)^2=16-7=9$. The question asks for magnitude, so take $\lvert b_\theta(T)\rvert=\sqrt9=3$. The two given quantities are consistent with either sign of the bias.
