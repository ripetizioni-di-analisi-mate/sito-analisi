The population standard deviation is $12$. What is the standard error of $\bar X$ for an independent sample of $16$ observations?

- $12$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $3$

## Explanation

For independent observations from the same population the variance of the sample mean is $\dfrac{\sigma^2}{n}$. The standard error is its square root. Substituting $\sigma=12$ and $n=16$ gives $\sqrt{\dfrac{12^2}{16}}=\dfrac{12}{4}=3$. The square root of the sample size in the denominator comes from converting variance to standard deviation.
