For $U(0,\theta)$, choose the version of the density positive only for $0<x<\theta$. All observations are positive and their maximum is $m$. What is true of the likelihood?

- Its supremum is approached as $\theta\to m^+$ but is never attained
- It is positive for every $\theta>0$
- The maximum occurs at $m$
- The maximum occurs at $2m$

## Explanation

Since all the data are positive, compare $\theta$ with the maximum $m$ to check the support. With open endpoints, at $\theta\le m$ the density evaluated at the largest observation is zero. For $\theta>m$ each factor is $\dfrac1\theta$, giving the decreasing function $L(\theta)=\theta^{-n}$. The limit $m^{-n}$ as $\theta\to m^+$ is its supremum, while its value at $m$ is zero.
