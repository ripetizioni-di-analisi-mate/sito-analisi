Three independent Bernoulli trials yield two successes and one failure. What is the ratio of the likelihood at $p=\dfrac12$ to the likelihood at $p=\dfrac14$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $2$

## Explanation

For a fixed sequence of two successes and one failure the likelihood is $L(p)=p^2(1-p)$. At the two points it equals $L\!\left(\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac18$ and $L\!\left(\dfrac14\right)=\dfrac3{64}$. The required ratio is $\dfrac18\cdot\dfrac{64}{3}=\dfrac83$. If only the success count is observed, the binomial coefficient is common to both likelihoods and cancels in the ratio.
