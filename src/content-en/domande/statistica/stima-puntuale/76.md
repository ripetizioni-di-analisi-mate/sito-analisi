Two independent unbiased estimators of the same parameter both have variance $8$. What is the variance of their arithmetic mean?

- $2$
- $16$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Independence allows the two estimator variances to be added, giving variance $8+8=16$ for the sum. The arithmetic mean multiplies the sum by $\dfrac12$. Its variance is multiplied by the square of that factor, giving $\dfrac14\cdot16=4$. The mean also preserves the common expectation because the two weights sum to one.
