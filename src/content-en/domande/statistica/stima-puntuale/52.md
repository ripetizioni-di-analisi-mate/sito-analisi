An exponential rate is estimated by $\dfrac{n}{\sum_iX_i}$. If $n=5$ and the true rate is $8$, what is its bias?

- $10$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The sum of $n$ independent exponential waiting times of rate $\lambda$ has a gamma distribution with shape $n$. For $n>1$ its reciprocal has expectation $\dfrac{\lambda}{n-1}$. The estimator multiplies this reciprocal by five, giving expectation $\dfrac54\cdot8=10$. Subtracting the true parameter yields bias $10-8=2$. The estimator overestimates the rate on average.
