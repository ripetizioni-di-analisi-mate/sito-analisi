A sequence $T_n$ has bias $\dfrac1n$ and variance $\dfrac2n$ for every parameter value. Which conclusion follows?

- The MSE converges to one
- It is unbiased for every $n$
- It is consistent because its MSE tends to zero
- Its bias increases with $n$

## Explanation

The decomposition gives $MSE_\theta(T_n)=\dfrac2n+\dfrac1{n^2}$. Both contributions tend to zero. For every fixed threshold $\varepsilon>0$, applying Markov's inequality to $(T_n-\theta)^2$ bounds the probability of error at least $\varepsilon$ by $\dfrac{MSE_\theta(T_n)}{\varepsilon^2}$, which tends to zero. This proves consistency. The bias is still positive at every finite sample size, although it decreases as $n$ grows.
