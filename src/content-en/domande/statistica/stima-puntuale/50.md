The maximum likelihood estimate of an exponential scale is $2.5$ minutes. What is the numerical value of the maximum likelihood estimate of its rate, per minute?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{25}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The scale $\tau$ and rate $\lambda$ satisfy $\lambda=\dfrac1\tau$. This transformation is one-to-one for positive parameters, so it carries the likelihood maximum over to the rate parameterization. An estimated scale of $2.5\,\mathrm{min}$ gives $\widehat\lambda=\dfrac{1}{2.5}=\dfrac25\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$. The units are reciprocal because the rate is measured per unit of time.
