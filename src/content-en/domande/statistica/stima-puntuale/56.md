In a normal model the known mean is $5$. The independent observations are $3,4,5,7$. What is the maximum likelihood estimate of the variance?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{35}{16}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The mean is known, so take deviations from $5$. Their squares are $4,1,0,4$ with sum $D=9$. Writing $v=\sigma^2$, the derivative of the log-likelihood with respect to $v$ has the sign of $D-4v$ for $v>0$. The maximum is attained at $v=\dfrac94$. The mean is fixed in this model, and all deviations are taken from that value directly.
