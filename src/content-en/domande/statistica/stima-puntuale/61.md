For three independent observations from $U(0,8)$, what is the probability that their maximum is at most $6$?

- $\dfrac{27}{64}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$

## Explanation

The maximum is at most six precisely when all three observations are at most six. For one uniform observation on $[0,8]$ the favorable interval has length six out of eight, giving probability $\dfrac68=\dfrac34$. Independence allows us to multiply the three probabilities to obtain $\left(\dfrac34\right)^3=\dfrac{27}{64}$. One observation below the threshold alone would not bound the maximum.
