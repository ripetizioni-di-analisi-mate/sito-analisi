For three independent observations from $U(0,\theta)$, the observed maximum is $9$. What estimate does the unbiased estimator $\dfrac{n+1}{n}M$ give, where $M$ is the sample maximum?

- $18$
- $\dfrac{27}{4}$
- $12$
- $9$

## Explanation

The rule adjusts the sample maximum by a factor greater than one. For $n=3$ that factor is $\dfrac{3+1}{3}=\dfrac43$, giving observed estimate $\dfrac43\cdot9=12$. This corrects for the fact that the maximum of three uniform observations reaches only three-quarters of the population's upper endpoint on average.
