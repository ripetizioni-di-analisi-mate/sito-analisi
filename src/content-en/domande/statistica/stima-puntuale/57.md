All observations from a normal distribution with unknown mean and variance are equal. In the model with $\sigma^2>0$, what follows about joint maximum likelihood estimation?

- The maximum is attained at the sample mean and a strictly positive variance
- The maximum is attained for every positive variance
- The maximum is attained at variance one
- The likelihood grows without bound as the variance approaches zero with the mean equal to the common observation

## Explanation

Setting the mean to the common observed value makes the sum of squared deviations zero. Writing $v=\sigma^2>0$, the likelihood becomes $(2\pi v)^{-\frac n2}$. This grows without bound as $v$ tends to zero from above. Only strictly positive variances are admissible, so the joint likelihood is unbounded and no admissible pair attains a maximum.
