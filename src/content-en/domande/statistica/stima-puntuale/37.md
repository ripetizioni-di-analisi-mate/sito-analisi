For a Bernoulli variable with parameter $p$, the second moment is to be used in place of the first. Which relationship holds?

- $E[X^2]=p$
- $E[X^2]=1-p$
- $E[X^2]=p(1-p)$
- $E[X^2]=p^2$

## Explanation

A Bernoulli variable takes only zero and one, both unchanged by squaring. Thus $X^2=X$ for every outcome and $E[X^2]=0^2(1-p)+1^2p=p$ for every admissible parameter. Subtracting the square of the expectation from the second moment gives $p(1-p)$, the variance.
