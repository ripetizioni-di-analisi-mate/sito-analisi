Two independent instruments provide unbiased estimates of $\theta$ with variances $4$ and $16$. What weight on the first instrument minimizes the variance of $wX_1+(1-w)X_2$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Independence gives zero covariance, so the combined variance is $v(w)=4w^2+16(1-w)^2$. Its derivative is $8w-32(1-w)=40w-32$. Setting it to zero gives $w=\dfrac45$ and the positive second derivative confirms the minimum. The first instrument receives more weight because it has lower variance. Both estimators are unbiased, so weights summing to one preserve the required expectation.
