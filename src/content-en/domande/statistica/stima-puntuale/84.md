A sample is used to estimate $P(X>3)$. The five observations are $2,3,4,7,3$. What estimate is obtained from the sample proportion?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

Count the observations satisfying the condition to estimate the probability. The sign $>$ excludes the two values equal to $3$, leaving just $4$ and $7$. Two observations out of five exceed the threshold, so the sample proportion is $\dfrac25$. Including values equal to the threshold would estimate $P(X\ge3)$.
