Two estimators have the same variance and respective biases $-4$ and $2$. By how much does the first MSE exceed the second?

- $2$
- $6$
- $20$
- $12$

## Explanation

Denote the common variance by $v$. The mean squared errors are $v+(-4)^2=v+16$ and $v+2^2=v+4$, respectively. The term $v$ cancels on subtraction, so the first error exceeds the second by $16-4=12$. The expectations lie on opposite sides of the parameter, but what matters here is their squared distance from it.
