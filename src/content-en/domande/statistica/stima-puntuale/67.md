A sample of size one from $U(0,\theta)$ gives $x=5$. What value does the unbiased estimator of the parameter based on the sample maximum give?

- $10$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$

## Explanation

With one observation the maximum $M$ equals $X$. Under a uniform model on $[0,\theta]$, $E_\theta[X]=\dfrac\theta2$. To obtain expectation $\theta$ use $2M$, which is the rule $\dfrac{n+1}{n}M$ at $n=1$. Applying it to the observed value gives $2\cdot5=10$. The value $5$ is the sample maximum before correction.
