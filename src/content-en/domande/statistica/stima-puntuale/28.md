Both observations in a sample of size two equal $4$. What follows about the unbiased sample variance $s^2$?

- $s^2$ is undefined
- $s^2=8$
- $s^2=4$
- $s^2=0$

## Explanation

Both observations equal four, giving observed mean $\dfrac{4+4}{2}=4$. Both deviations from the mean are zero, so their sum of squares is zero. The unbiased sample variance has divisor $2-1=1$ and is therefore defined, with value $s^2=0$. The common observation value describes location, while the observations have no spread.
