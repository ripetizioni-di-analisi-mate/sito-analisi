The independent observations $7,8,9$ come from a uniform distribution on $[\theta,\theta+4]$ with positive density at the endpoints. What is the set of maximum likelihood estimates of $\theta$?

- $[3,5]$
- $[5,7]$
- $[7,9]$
- $\{5\}$

## Explanation

The model interval must contain the minimum $7$ and maximum $9$. This requires both $\theta\le7$ and $\theta+4\ge9$, giving $5\le\theta\le7$. On that set each of the three densities is $\dfrac14$ and the likelihood is constant at $\dfrac1{64}$. Outside it at least one factor is zero. Every point in $[5,7]$ is therefore a maximizer.
