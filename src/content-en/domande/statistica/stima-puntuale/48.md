Two independent Poisson samples with the same rate contain $2$ and $3$ observations. Their sums are $4$ and $11$. What is the maximum likelihood estimate from the combined data?

- $\dfrac{17}{6}$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The samples share the same rate, so combine their counts. The sample size is $2+3=5$ and the total count is $4+11=15$. The Poisson maximum solves $\dfrac{15}{\lambda}-5=0$, giving $\widehat\lambda=3$, the overall mean. The unweighted mean of the two group means would give the groups equal weight despite their different sample sizes.
