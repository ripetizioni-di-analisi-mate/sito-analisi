For three independent observations from $U(0,8)$, the maximum $M$ has variance $\dfrac{12}{5}$. What is the variance of the unbiased estimator $\dfrac43 M$?

- $\dfrac{16}{5}$
- $\dfrac{48}{5}$
- $\dfrac{64}{15}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$

## Explanation

The correction multiplies the maximum by $\dfrac43$, so its variance is multiplied by the square of that factor, giving $\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\dfrac43M\right)=\dfrac{16}{9}\cdot\dfrac{12}{5}=\dfrac{64}{15}$. The correction scales the expectation and the deviations from the expectation linearly. Squared deviations increase by the squared factor, making the new variance greater than that of the maximum.
