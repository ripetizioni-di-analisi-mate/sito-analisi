A population has variance $40$. What is the smallest $n$ such that the mean of $n$ independent observations has MSE at most $3$?

- $13$
- $12$
- $14$
- $40$

## Explanation

The sample mean is unbiased, so its mean squared error equals its variance $\dfrac{40}{n}$. Achieving $\dfrac{40}{n}\le3$ requires $n\ge\dfrac{40}{3}$. The sample size is an integer, so the first admissible value is $14$. With $13$ the variance is still $\dfrac{40}{13}>3$, while with $14$ it falls below the limit.
