Three instruments $X_1,X_2,X_3$ have expectations $\theta$, $\theta+1$, and $\theta-1$, respectively. Which rule is unbiased for every $\theta\in\mathbb R$?

- $\dfrac{X_1+X_2+X_3}{3}$
- $\dfrac{X_1+X_3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{X_2+X_3}{3}$
- $\dfrac{X_1+X_2}{2}$

## Explanation

The mean of the three instrument readings has expectation $\dfrac{\theta+(\theta+1)+(\theta-1)}{3}=\dfrac{3\theta}{3}=\theta$. Equal weighting makes the opposite biases of the two instruments cancel. Unbiasedness follows by linearity of expectation, so the three expectations suffice for this check. Averaging the first and third instruments alone would give expectation $\theta-\dfrac12$.
