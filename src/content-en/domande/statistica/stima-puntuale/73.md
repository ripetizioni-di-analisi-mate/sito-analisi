For every $n$, use $T_n=\bar X_n+1$ to estimate the mean $\mu$ from an i.i.d. sample with finite variance. To what value does $T_n$ converge in probability?

- $\mu+1$
- $\mu-1$
- $1$ for every $\mu$
- $\mu$

## Explanation

The law of large numbers gives $\bar X_n\to\mu$ in probability. At any positive threshold the deviation of $T_n$ from $\mu+1$ equals the deviation of $\bar X_n$ from $\mu$ because $T_n-(\mu+1)=\bar X_n-\mu$. Consequently $T_n$ converges to $\mu+1$. The added one persists as the sample size grows, preventing consistency for $\mu$.
