Four independent exponential waiting times give $\bar x=10$ minutes. Using the unbiased estimator $\dfrac{n-1}{\sum_i X_i}$, what is the numerical rate estimate per minute?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{40}$
- $\dfrac{1}{40}$

## Explanation

The given rule uses the sum of the times. Four observations with mean $10\,\mathrm{min}$ have sum $4\cdot10=40\,\mathrm{min}$. The numerator is $n-1=3$, so the estimate is $\dfrac3{40}\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$. A numerator of four would give the maximum likelihood estimator, which has positive bias for the rate.
