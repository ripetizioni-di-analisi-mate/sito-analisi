For every $\theta$, an estimator $T$ has expectation $E_\theta[T]=\theta-3$. What is its bias?

- $9$
- $-3$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

Bias is the signed average deviation from the parameter. Here the estimator's expectation is three below every true value of $\theta$. Substitution gives $b_\theta(T)=E_\theta[T]-\theta=(\theta-3)-\theta=-3$. Its sign indicates underestimation on average. The value $9$ is the squared bias used in the mean squared error.
