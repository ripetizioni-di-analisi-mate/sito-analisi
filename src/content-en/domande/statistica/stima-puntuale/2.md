An estimator $T$ has expectation $\theta-2$ and variance $5$. What is $MSE_\theta(T)$?

- $25$
- $3$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

Mean squared error accounts for the variability of the estimator and the distance of its expectation from the parameter. The given expectation gives $b_\theta(T)=(\theta-2)-\theta=-2$. Add the square of this difference to the variance to obtain $MSE_\theta(T)=5+(-2)^2=9$. Even negative bias increases mean squared error because it enters as a square.
