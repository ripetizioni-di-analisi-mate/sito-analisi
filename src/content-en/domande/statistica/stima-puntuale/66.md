For two independent observations from $U(0,6)$, the sample maximum has expectation $4$ and variance $2$. What is its mean squared error as an estimator of the endpoint $6$?

- $10$
- $2$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

The target is the upper endpoint $6$. The maximum has expectation $4$ and hence bias $4-6=-2$. Its mean squared error adds the variance and the square of this bias, giving $MSE_6(M)=2+(-2)^2=6$. The variance alone would give $2$ and account only for dispersion around $4$.
