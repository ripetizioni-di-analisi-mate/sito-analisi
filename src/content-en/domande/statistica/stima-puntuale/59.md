For a normal distribution with known variance, $\mu$ is constrained to $[0,3]$. The independent observations are $2,4,6$. Which mean maximizes the likelihood?

- $4$
- $3$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

With known variance, maximizing the likelihood for the mean is equivalent to minimizing the sum of squared deviations from $\mu$. For the three observations the sum is $8+3(\mu-4)^2$ because $\bar x=4$. The quadratic term is minimized at the admissible point closest to four, $\mu=3$. The unconstrained estimate would be the observed mean, which is excluded here.
