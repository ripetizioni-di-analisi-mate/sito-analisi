For an exponential model parameterized by its scale $\tau=E[X]$, the observed mean is $0.25$. What is the method-of-moments estimate of $\tau$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The parameter $\tau$ is the exponential scale, which equals the theoretical mean. The first-moment equation is thus $\tau=\bar x$. The data give $\widehat\tau=0.25=\dfrac14$. Its reciprocal $4$ estimates the rate and has units reciprocal to the scale units.
