The mean of $n$ independent observations from a population with variance $4$ is used to estimate $\mu$. What is the smallest $n$ for which Chebyshev's upper bound on $P(\lvert\bar X-\mu\rvert\ge1)$ is at most $0.05$?

- $40$
- $80$
- $20$
- $160$

## Explanation

The sample mean has expectation $\mu$ and variance $\dfrac4n$. At threshold $1$, Chebyshev's inequality gives the upper bound $\dfrac{4}{n\cdot1^2}=\dfrac4n$. Requiring $\dfrac4n\le0.05=\dfrac1{20}$ is equivalent to $n\ge80$. Eighty is the first sample size for which this inequality guarantees the specified limit. The actual probability may be smaller than the bound.
