Independent observations $4,5,6$ come from a uniform distribution on $[\theta,8]$ with $\theta<8$ and positive density at the endpoints. Which value maximizes the likelihood?

- $5$
- $6$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The upper endpoint $8$ covers all data. To include the observed minimum, the lower endpoint must satisfy $\theta\le4$. For these parameters the likelihood is $(8-\theta)^{-3}$ because each of the three densities equals the reciprocal of the support length. Increasing $\theta$ shortens the interval and increases the likelihood. The maximum is at $\theta=4$, admissible because the endpoint density is positive.
