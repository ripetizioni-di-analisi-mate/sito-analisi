The observations $4,5,6$ come from a uniform distribution on $[\theta,8]$, using the version of the density positive at the endpoints. The lab also reports a further exact observation below $4$, without giving its value. What is guaranteed about the maximum likelihood estimate for the complete data?

- It remains $4$
- It equals the mean of the three known observations
- It is above $4$
- It is below $4$ but its exact value cannot be determined

## Explanation

For the complete data the likelihood is $(8-\theta)^{-4}$ up to the observed minimum and zero beyond it. It increases on the feasible range and is maximised at the new minimum, the undisclosed observation.
