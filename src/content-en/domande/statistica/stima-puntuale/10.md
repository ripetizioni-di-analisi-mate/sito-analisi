Draw $25$ independent observations from a population with mean $\mu$ and variance $100$. What is the mean squared error of $\bar X$ as an estimator of $\mu$?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $100$
- $4$

## Explanation

By linearity the sample mean has expectation $\mu$, so its bias is zero. Independence allows the observation variances to be added. Dividing the sum of observations by $25$ gives sample mean variance $\dfrac{25\cdot100}{25^2}=4$. This is also its mean squared error. Its square root $2$ is the standard error, in the original units.
