A rigid rod is hinged to the fixed point $O$ and rotates in the plane $Oxy$; a ring $P$, threaded on the rod, slides freely on it. The angle $\vartheta$ that the rod forms with the $x$ axis and the distance $u = |P - O|$ are taken as Lagrangian coordinates. What is the virtual displacement of $P$?

- $\delta u\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $u\,\delta\vartheta\,( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $\delta u\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ) + \delta\vartheta\,( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $\delta u\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ) + u\,\delta\vartheta\,( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$

## Explanation

The position of the ring is written $P - O = u\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$, therefore it depends on the two Lagrangian coordinates and not on time. The virtual displacement is then obtained as
$$
\delta P = \frac{\partial P}{\partial u}\,\delta u + \frac{\partial P}{\partial \vartheta}\,\delta\vartheta ,
$$
with $\delta u$ and $\delta\vartheta$ arbitrary. One computes the two partial derivatives and gets
$$
\delta P = \delta u\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ) + u\,\delta\vartheta\,( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ) ,
$$
where the factor $u$ in the second term comes from the derivative of the product. The first term is directed along the rod and the second is orthogonal to it, therefore the virtual displacements of $P$ form a space of dimension $2$, equal to the number of degrees of freedom.
