A spool is formed by a disk of radius $R$ and a coaxial hub of radius $r = \dfrac{R}{2}$, and it rolls without slipping on a horizontal floor, resting on the disk. An inextensible string wound on the hub leaves horizontally from the lowest point of the hub, as in the figure, and its free end is pulled to the right with speed $u$. What is the velocity of the center of the spool?

- $2u$, to the right
- $\dfrac{2u}{3}$, to the right
- $u$, to the right
- $2u$, to the left

## Explanation

The contact point $H$ has zero velocity, therefore the spool rotates instantaneously about $H$ and each of its points on the vertical through $H$ has a horizontal velocity proportional to the distance from $H$. The point from which the string leaves is at distance $R - r$ from $H$ and the center is at distance $R$, hence, denoting by $\omega$ the magnitude of the angular velocity,
$$
u = \omega\,(R - r) , \qquad v_C = \omega\,R .
$$
Eliminating $\omega$ one obtains $v_C = \dfrac{R}{R - r}\,u = 2u$ for $r = \dfrac{R}{2}$, and the center moves to the right, like the point where the string leaves. The value $\dfrac{2u}{3}$ corresponds to the string leaving from the highest point of the hub, at distance $R + r$ from $H$.
