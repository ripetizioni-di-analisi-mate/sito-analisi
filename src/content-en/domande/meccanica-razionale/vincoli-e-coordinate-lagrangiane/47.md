In the plane $Oxy$, with lengths in meters, the point $P_1$ is constrained to the circle of center $O$ and radius $3$, the point $P_2$ is constrained to the $x$ axis, and the two points are connected by a rigid rod of length $5$. Which of the following pairs is an admissible configuration of the system?

- $P_1 = (0, 3)$ and $P_2 = (4, 0)$
- $P_1 = (3, 0)$ and $P_2 = (5, 0)$
- $P_1 = (0, 3)$ and $P_2 = (5, 0)$
- $P_1 = (0, 4)$ and $P_2 = (3, 0)$

## Explanation

A configuration is admissible when it satisfies all the constraint equations, which here are
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1^2 + y_1^2 = 9 \\[4pt]
y_2 = 0 \\[4pt]
(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 = 25
\end{cases}
$$
The pair $P_1 = (0, 3)$, $P_2 = (4, 0)$ satisfies the three equations, since $0 + 9 = 9$ and $16 + 9 = 25$. The pair with $P_2 = (5, 0)$ and $P_1 = (0, 3)$ gives the distance $\sqrt{34}$, and the one with $P_1 = (3, 0)$ gives the distance $2$, therefore both violate the rigidity of the rod. The pair with $P_1 = (0, 4)$ respects the distance $5$, but $P_1$ does not belong to the circle, since $0 + 16 \neq 9$.
