A smooth straight guide rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed point $O$ with assigned constant angular velocity $\omega$, and a material point $P$ is threaded on the guide. How is the constraint classified?

- Nonholonomic, bilateral and moving
- Holonomic, unilateral and moving
- Holonomic, bilateral and moving
- Holonomic, bilateral and fixed

## Explanation

The belonging of $P$ to the guide is expressed by a relation between the coordinates of $P$ alone and time, $x \sin(\omega t) - y \cos(\omega t) = 0$, therefore the constraint is holonomic. The relation is an equation and not an inequality, hence the constraint is bilateral. Time appears explicitly in the equation, since the law of rotation of the guide is assigned, therefore the constraint is moving. The distinction matters in what follows, since with a moving constraint the virtual displacements do not coincide with the possible displacements and the kinetic energy also contains terms of first degree in the generalized velocities.
