The plane system in the figure is a four-bar linkage: the rods $AB$, $BC$ and $CD$ are hinged to one another at $B$ and at $C$, whereas the rods $AB$ and $CD$ are hinged to the fixed points $A$ and $D$. The constraints are smooth and bilateral. How many degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0.8,2.4;C,3.6,2.8;D,4.6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" titolo="Four-bar linkage with fixed hinges at $A$ and at $D$"}

- $n = 1$
- $n = 2$
- $n = 3$
- $n = 4$

## Explanation

The moving rods are three, therefore the configuration parameters to start from are $3 \cdot 3 = 9$. The two fixed hinges at $A$ and at $D$ impose $2 + 2 = 4$ scalar conditions and the two internal hinges at $B$ and at $C$ impose $4$ more, hence the independent conditions are $8$ and one obtains
$$
n = 9 - 8 = 1 .
$$
As Lagrangian coordinate one takes the angle that the rod $AB$ forms with the $x$ axis, since the position of $B$ determines that of $C$ up to the symmetric configuration of the triangle $BCD$, which is not reached with continuity.
