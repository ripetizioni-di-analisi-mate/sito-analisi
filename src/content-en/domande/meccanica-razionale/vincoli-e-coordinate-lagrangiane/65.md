A ring $P$ is threaded on a smooth, fixed and bilateral circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, placed in the horizontal plane $z = 0$ of the frame $(O; x, y, z)$, with the $z$ axis vertical. How many scalar unknowns does the constraint reaction exerted by the guide on the ring have, and in which plane does it lie?

- One unknown, with the reaction directed along the radius $OP$
- Two unknowns, with the reaction in the horizontal plane $z = 0$
- Three unknowns, with the reaction in any direction
- Two unknowns, with the reaction in the vertical plane that contains $O$ and $P$

## Explanation

The guide is the space curve of equations $f_1 = x^2 + y^2 - R^2 = 0$ and $f_2 = z = 0$, therefore the ring has $3 - 2 = 1$ degree of freedom and its virtual displacements are parallel to the tangent to the guide at $P$, that is, to $\hat{k} \wedge (P - O)$. The constraint is smooth and bilateral, hence it is ideal and the reaction is orthogonal to every virtual displacement, that is, orthogonal to the tangent. The vectors orthogonal to the tangent form the plane spanned by $\operatorname{grad} f_1 = 2\,(P - O)$ and by $\operatorname{grad} f_2 = \hat{k}$, which is the vertical plane through $O$ and $P$, therefore
$$
\vec{\Phi} = \Phi_r\,\frac{P - O}{R} + \Phi_z\,\hat{k} ,
$$
with the two scalar unknowns $\Phi_r$ and $\Phi_z$. The vertical component $\Phi_z$ is the one that supports the ring against its weight, and the reaction directed along the radius neglects it. The plane $z = 0$ instead contains the tangent to the guide, which is the direction of the virtual displacements.
