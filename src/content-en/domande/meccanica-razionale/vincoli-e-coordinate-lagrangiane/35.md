A homogeneous disk of radius $R$ keeps vertical and rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal plane, with the contact point free to move in the plane. How many parameters identify its configuration and how many are its kinematic degrees of freedom?

- Three parameters and two kinematic degrees of freedom
- Four parameters and two kinematic degrees of freedom
- Four parameters and four kinematic degrees of freedom
- Three parameters and three kinematic degrees of freedom

## Explanation

The configuration of the disk is identified by the coordinates $x$ and $y$ of the contact point, by the angle $\psi$ that the plane of the disk forms with the $x$ axis and by the angle of proper rotation $\varphi$, therefore the parameters are four. Pure rolling imposes the two conditions
$$
\begin{cases}
\dot{x} + R\,\dot{\varphi} \cos\psi = 0 \\[4pt]
\dot{y} + R\,\dot{\varphi} \sin\psi = 0
\end{cases}
$$
hence in every configuration the admissible velocities form a subspace of dimension $2$ and the kinematic degrees of freedom are two. The two conditions are nonholonomic, therefore the reachable configurations remain all of them: the disk, with a forward rolling, a rotation of half a turn about the vertical, a second rolling and a second rotation, returns to the initial position with a different value of $\varphi$.
