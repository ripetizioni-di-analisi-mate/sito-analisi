In a slider-crank mechanism the crank $OA$ is hinged to the fixed point $O$ and the connecting rod $AB$ is hinged to the crank at $A$; the end $B$ is constrained to the fixed horizontal guide by a sleeve, which prevents the vertical translation of $B$ and the rotation of the connecting rod. The constraints are smooth and bilateral. How many degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0.9,1.2;B,3.6,0" aste="O-A;A-B" cerniere="A" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,doppio-pendolo,270" linee="-0.6,0 4.6,0" titolo="Slider-crank with a sleeve at $B$ in place of the roller support"}

- $n = 0$
- $n = 1$
- $n = 2$
- $n = 3$

## Explanation

The plane rigid bodies are two, therefore the configuration parameters to start from are $3 \cdot 2 = 6$. The fixed hinge at $O$ imposes $2$ scalar conditions, the internal hinge at $A$ imposes $2$ of them and the sleeve at $B$ imposes $2$ of them, since it leaves free only the translation along the guide, hence the independent conditions are $6$ and one obtains
$$
n = 6 - 6 = 0 .
$$
The system is then locked. The sleeve fixes the direction of the connecting rod, therefore the position of $A$ depends on the abscissa of $B$ alone, and the condition that the distance $|A - O|$ equal the length of the crank determines that abscissa. A roller support in place of the sleeve would leave one degree of freedom, since it would impose a single scalar condition.
