A body is in contact with a fixed rough surface and the static friction coefficient of the pair of materials in contact equals $f_s = 0.75$. What is the friction angle, that is, the half-aperture of the friction cone?

- $36.87^\circ$
- $41.41^\circ$
- $48.59^\circ$
- $53.13^\circ$

## Explanation

The Coulomb-Morin law says that rest is compatible with the contact if and only if $|\vec{\Phi}_t| \leq f_s\,\Phi_n$, and this condition is equivalent to the geometric one that the reaction belong to the cone of vertex the contact point, axis the normal and half-aperture $\rho = \arctan f_s$. One substitutes the assigned value and obtains
$$
\rho = \arctan 0.75 = 36.87^\circ .
$$
The values $48.59^\circ$ and $41.41^\circ$ are the arcsine and the arccosine of $0.75$, therefore neither of the two satisfies the equality $\tan\rho = f_s$. A body resting on a plane inclined with this coefficient stays at rest, for every mass, if and only if the inclination does not exceed $36.87^\circ$.
