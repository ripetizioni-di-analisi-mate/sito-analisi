A material point $P$ rests on the outside of a smooth fixed spherical dome of center $O$ and radius $R$, from which it can detach. How many degrees of freedom does the point have in the ordinary configurations and how many in the boundary ones?

- $2$ in the ordinary configurations and $3$ in the boundary ones
- $3$ in the ordinary configurations and $2$ in the boundary ones
- $2$ in both
- $3$ in both

## Explanation

The point cannot enter the dome, therefore the constraint is the inequality $|P - O| - R \geq 0$ and is unilateral. In the ordinary configurations $|P - O| > R$ holds, the point is detached and the constraint does not restrict its displacements, hence the point has the $3$ degrees of freedom of the free point. In the boundary configurations $|P - O| = R$ holds, and the constraint acts as the bilateral spherical surface, therefore the degrees of freedom are $3 - 1 = 2$, for instance the colatitude and the longitude. The passage from $2$ to $3$ degrees of freedom takes place at detachment, when the normal reaction vanishes.
