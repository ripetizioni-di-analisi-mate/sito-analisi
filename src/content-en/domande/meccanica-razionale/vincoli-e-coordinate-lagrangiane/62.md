A ring $P$ is threaded on a circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R = 0.5\,\mathrm{m}$, placed in a vertical plane that rotates about the vertical diameter with assigned constant angular velocity $\omega = 4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. At a certain instant the ring is at rest relative to the guide in the position where the radius $OP$ forms the angle $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ with the downward vertical. What is the magnitude of the velocity of $P$ at that instant?

- $0$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.73\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With the Lagrangian coordinate $\vartheta$ and the angle $\omega t$ assigned to the plane of the guide, the position of the ring is written
$$
P - O = R\,\big( \sin\vartheta \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta \sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} - \cos\vartheta\,\hat{k} \big) .
$$
The velocity is $\vec{v}_P = \dfrac{\partial P}{\partial \vartheta}\,\dot{\vartheta} + \dfrac{\partial P}{\partial t}$, and at the instant considered $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$, therefore only the term due to the motion of the constraint remains, of magnitude $\omega\,R \sin\vartheta$. One then obtains
$$
|\vec{v}_P| = 4 \cdot 0.5 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 1\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $0$ neglects the term $\dfrac{\partial P}{\partial t}$, which is nonzero with a moving constraint, and the value $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forgets that the radius of the circle described by $P$ about the axis is $R \sin\vartheta$.
