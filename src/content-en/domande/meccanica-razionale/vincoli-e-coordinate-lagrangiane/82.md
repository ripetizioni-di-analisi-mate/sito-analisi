Which of the following constraints is not ideal?

- A smooth internal hinge connecting two rods
- The contact of a block slipping on a rough floor
- A taut inextensible string connecting two material points
- A smooth sleeve constraining a rod to a fixed straight guide

## Explanation

A constraint is ideal when the virtual work of its reactions is zero for every virtual displacement, in the bilateral case. The slipping block receives from the floor a tangential friction force $\vec{\Phi}_t$, and the virtual displacement of the contact point is directed along the floor, therefore $\vec{\Phi}_t \cdot \delta P$ is nonzero. In the internal hinge and in the taut string the two forces on the connected bodies are opposite and do virtual works that cancel out, and the smooth sleeve exerts a force normal to the guide and a reaction moment with virtual displacements $\delta y = 0$ and $\delta\vartheta = 0$. The contact with slipping is hence the only **non-ideal** constraint of the list, and its friction force is treated as an active force.
