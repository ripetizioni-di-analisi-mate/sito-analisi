A rigid rod $AB$ of length $\ell$ has the end $A$ constrained by a roller support to the horizontal guide $y = 0$ and the end $B$ constrained by a roller support to the horizontal guide $y = d$, with $0 < d < \ell$, as in the figure; the end $B$ lies to the right of $A$. Which relation links the abscissas $x_A$ and $x_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,2" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,270;B,carrello,90" linee="-1,0 5,0;-1,2 5,2" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.6;5.3 0,5.3 2,d,-0.4" titolo="Rod with its ends on two parallel horizontal guides at distance $d$"}

- $x_B - x_A = \ell$
- $x_B - x_A = \ell - d$
- $x_B - x_A = \sqrt{\ell^2 - d^2}$
- $x_B^2 - x_A^2 = \ell^2 - d^2$

## Explanation

The two roller supports impose $y_A = 0$ and $y_B = d$, and the rigidity of the rod imposes $|B - A| = \ell$, therefore
$$
(x_B - x_A)^2 + d^2 = \ell^2 .
$$
The end $B$ lies to the right of $A$, hence $x_B - x_A > 0$ and one obtains
$$
x_B - x_A = \sqrt{\ell^2 - d^2} .
$$
The difference of the abscissas is constant, so the rod translates keeping the inclination $\varphi$ with $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{d}{\ell}$, and the system has one degree of freedom with Lagrangian coordinate $x_A$. The relation $x_B^2 - x_A^2 = \ell^2 - d^2$ confuses the square of the difference with the difference of the squares.
