A material point $P = (x, y)$ is constrained to the circle of center $O$ of the plane $Oxy$ whose radius grows in time according to the assigned law $R(t) = R_0 + v\,t$, with $R_0 > 0$ and $v > 0$. Which equation expresses the constraint?

- $x^2 + y^2 = R_0^2$
- $x^2 + y^2 = R_0^2 + v\,t$
- $x^2 + y^2 = (R_0 + v\,t)^2$
- $x^2 + y^2 = R_0^2 + v^2 t^2$

## Explanation

The point belongs to the circle of center $O$ and radius $R(t)$ if and only if its distance from $O$ equals $R(t)$, therefore if and only if $|P - O|^2 = R(t)^2$. Substituting the assigned law one obtains
$$
x^2 + y^2 = (R_0 + v\,t)^2 .
$$
The expression $R_0^2 + v^2 t^2$ differs from the square of $R_0 + v\,t$ by the double product $2 R_0 v\,t$, therefore it does not express the same constraint. Time appears explicitly in the equation, hence the constraint is holonomic, bilateral and moving.
