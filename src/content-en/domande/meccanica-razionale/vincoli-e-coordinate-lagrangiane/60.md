A rigid rod $AB$ rests on a horizontal guide and is constrained by three roller supports, placed at the ends $A$ and $B$ and at the midpoint $M$, as in the figure; the constraints are smooth and bilateral. How many degrees of freedom does the rod have?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;M,2.5,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,270;M,carrello,270;B,carrello,270" linee="-0.8,-0.55 5.8,-0.55" titolo="Rod on a horizontal guide with three roller supports at $A$, $M$ and $B$"}

- $n = 0$
- $n = 2$
- $n = 1$
- $n = 3$

## Explanation

The count $3 - 3 = 0$ would assume that the three constraints are independent. With the center of mass $G = M$ and the angle $\vartheta$ of the rod with the guide, the three roller supports impose $y_G - \dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta = 0$, $y_G = 0$ and $y_G + \dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta = 0$, equations that do not contain $x_G$. The Jacobian matrix with respect to $(x_G, y_G, \vartheta)$ has the first column equal to zero and therefore rank $2$, hence the independent conditions are two and
$$
n = 3 - 2 = 1 .
$$
The three roller supports all prevent the same vertical displacement, therefore the rod remains free to translate along the guide, with Lagrangian coordinate $x_G$.
