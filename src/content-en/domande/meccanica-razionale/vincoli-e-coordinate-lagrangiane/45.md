A body of mass $m = 15\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a rough horizontal plane, with static friction coefficient $f_s = 0.35$, and a horizontal force of magnitude $F$ acts on it. Taking $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the maximum value of $F$ compatible with rest?

- $5.25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $51.50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $147.15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $420.43\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The equilibrium of the body requires the reaction of the plane to be opposite to the resultant of the weight and of the applied force, therefore its components equal $\Phi_n = m g$ and $|\vec{\Phi}_t| = F$. The Coulomb-Morin law then gives the condition $F \leq f_s\,m g$, hence the maximum value is
$$
F_{\max} = f_s\,m\,g = 0.35 \cdot 15 \cdot 9.81 = 51.50\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $147.15\,\mathrm{N}$ is the magnitude of the weight of the body and the value $5.25\,\mathrm{N}$ would be obtained by forgetting the acceleration of gravity, therefore neither of the two satisfies the condition of rest at the limit of sliding.
