A material point $P$ hangs from the fixed point $O$ by a taut inextensible string of length $\ell$, and lies in the position $P - O = \ell\,( \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$. Denoting by $T \geq 0$ the magnitude of the tension, which vector is the reaction that the string exerts on $P$?

- $T\,( \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $T\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $T\,( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $-T\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The string exerts a force directed along the string itself, from the point $P$ toward the attachment point $O$, of magnitude $T \geq 0$. The unit vector that goes from $P$ to $O$ is
$$
\frac{O - P}{\ell} = -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ,
$$
hence the reaction is $T\,( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$. The vector $T\,( \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$ is directed from $O$ toward $P$, that is, it would push the point away from the attachment point, and a flexible string cannot exert a force of that sense. The vector $T\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$ is orthogonal to the string and has the direction of the virtual displacements of $P$ with the string taut.
