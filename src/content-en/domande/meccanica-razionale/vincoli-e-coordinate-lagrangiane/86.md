A homogeneous crate shaped as a rectangular box, with base $b = 0.4\,\mathrm{m}$ along the line of steepest slope and height $h = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$, rests on a plane whose inclination $\alpha$ is slowly increased starting from zero, as in the figure. The static friction coefficient is $f_s = 0.60$. What happens first?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4.2,1.125 4.2,0;1.449,0.388 2.222,0.595 1.704,2.527 0.931,2.320" punti="1.577,1.457,G,baricentro" linee="-0.4,0 4.6,0" etichette="0.75,0.08,α" quote="1.449 0.388,2.222 0.595,b,0.35;2.222 0.595,1.704 2.527,h,0.35" titolo="Crate of base $b$ and height $h$ on a plane of variable inclination"}

- The crate slides at $\alpha = 30.96^\circ$, before tipping over
- The crate tips over at $\alpha = 21.80^\circ$, before sliding
- The crate tips over at $\alpha = 68.20^\circ$, before sliding
- The crate slides at $\alpha = 21.80^\circ$, before tipping over

## Explanation

The only forces are the weight and the reaction of the plane, therefore at equilibrium the reaction is opposite to the weight and has the same line of action, the vertical through $G$. The contact is unilateral, hence the reaction must pass through a point of the base, and the vertical through $G$ falls inside the base as long as $\tan\alpha \leq \dfrac{b}{h} = 0.40$, that is, $\alpha \leq 21.80^\circ$. The Coulomb-Morin law requires in addition $\tan\alpha \leq f_s = 0.60$, that is, $\alpha \leq 30.96^\circ$. The first condition stops holding at a smaller inclination, therefore the crate **tips over** at $\alpha = 21.80^\circ$, when the contact is still far from the limit of slipping.
