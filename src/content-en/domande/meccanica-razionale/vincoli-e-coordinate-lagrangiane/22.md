The planar system in the figure consists of a disk of radius $R$, which rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, and of a point particle $P$ that slides in a smooth straight slot cut along a diameter of the disk. The constraints are bilateral. How many degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{cerchi="2,1.2,1.2" linee="-0.3,0 4.8,0;0.961,0.6 3.039,1.8" punti="2,1.2,G,baricentro;2.606,1.55,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Disk rolling on a horizontal guide, with the point $P$ in the slot along a diameter"}

- $n = 1$
- $n = 2$
- $n = 3$
- $n = 4$

## Explanation

Without constraints the disk would have $3$ configuration parameters and the particle $2$, hence the system would have $5$. The disk rolling without slipping is subject to two conditions, contact with the guide, $y_G = R$, and no slipping, $x_G + R\,\vartheta = \text{constant}$, where $\vartheta$ is the rotation angle of the disk. The slot forces $P$ to lie on the line of the diameter, which depends on the configuration of the disk, and therefore imposes a single scalar condition. The independent conditions are hence $3$, and the number of degrees of freedom equals
$$
n = 5 - 3 = 2 .
$$
As Lagrangian coordinates one takes the angle $\vartheta$ and the abscissa $s$ of $P$ along the slot, measured from the center $G$. The value $n = 1$ is obtained by regarding $P$ as carried along by the disk, while the slot leaves it free to slide along the diameter.
