A particle moves in a plane subject to both $x^2+y^2=a^2$ and $2x^2+2y^2=2a^2$, with $a>0$. How many configurational degrees of freedom remain at points on the circle?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The second equation is twice the first and supplies no independent constraint. Locally a single angle specifies position on the circle.
