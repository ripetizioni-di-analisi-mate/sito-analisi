The plane system in the figure is formed by the rods $AB$ and $BC$, hinged to each other at $B$; the rod $AB$ is hinged to the fixed point $A$ and the end $C$ is free. The constraints are smooth and bilateral. How many degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,2.2;C,4.2,1.2" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera" titolo="Two rods hinged at $B$, with a fixed hinge at $A$ and the end $C$ free"}

- $n = 1$
- $n = 2$
- $n = 3$
- $n = 4$

## Explanation

The system is formed by two plane rigid bodies, hence it starts from $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ configuration parameters. The fixed hinge at $A$ imposes $2$ scalar conditions and the internal hinge at $B$ imposes $2$ more, therefore the independent conditions are $4$ and the number of degrees of freedom equals
$$
n = 6 - 4 = 2 .
$$
As Lagrangian coordinates one takes the angles that the two rods form with the $x$ axis, since to each pair of values there corresponds one and only one admissible configuration. The end $C$ is free, therefore it removes no degree of freedom.
