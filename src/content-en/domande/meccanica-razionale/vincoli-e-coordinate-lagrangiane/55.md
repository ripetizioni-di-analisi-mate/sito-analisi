A small ball, regarded as a material point, moves in the plane $Oxy$ inside a thin straight tube placed along the $x$ axis and closed by two caps at the points of abscissa $0$ and $L$. Which system describes the admissible configurations of the ball?

- $\begin{cases} y = 0 \\ x = L \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} y \geq 0 \\ 0 \leq x \leq L \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} y = 0 \\ 0 \leq x \leq L \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} y = 0 \\ x \geq 0 \end{cases}$

## Explanation

The tube prevents the ball from moving in the transverse direction on both sides, therefore it imposes the bilateral constraint $y = 0$. Each cap instead prevents the displacement in one sense only, hence the caps impose the two unilateral constraints $x \geq 0$ and $L - x \geq 0$. The admissible configurations satisfy all these conditions together, so they are described by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
y = 0 \\[4pt]
0 \leq x \leq L
\end{cases}
$$
The configurations with $0 < x < L$ are ordinary with respect to the caps, whereas $x = 0$ and $x = L$ are boundary configurations, in which the corresponding cap exerts a force directed toward the inside of the tube.
