A homogeneous rod $OA$ of length $\ell$ is hinged to the fixed point $O$ and rotates in a vertical plane; at the depth $d = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\ell$ below $O$ there is a smooth horizontal floor on which the end $A$ can rest. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod forms with the horizontal $x$ axis, what is the interval of the admissible values of $\vartheta$?

- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4} \leq \vartheta \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4} \leq \vartheta \leq \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4} \leq \vartheta \leq \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{3} \leq \vartheta \leq \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The end of the rod satisfies $A - O = \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, whereas the floor lies at the height $y = -d$. The support prevents $A$ from going below the floor, hence it imposes $\ell\sin\vartheta \geq -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\ell$, that is,
$$
\sin\vartheta \geq -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} .
$$
The angles of one turn that satisfy the inequality are those between $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, since the sine equals $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ at both endpoints and stays greater inside. The constraint is unilateral, therefore the two endpoints of the interval are the boundary configurations, in which the floor exerts the vertical reaction $\Phi_n \geq 0$.
