Two gear wheels, regarded as disks of radii $R_1 = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ and $R_2 = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$, rotate about their fixed centers $O_1$ and $O_2$ and are in contact without slipping, as in the figure; the rotation angles $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ are positive counterclockwise. The first wheel has $\dot{\vartheta}_1 = 50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is $\dot{\vartheta}_2$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,0.8;2.8,0,2" punti="0,0,O_1,pieno;2.8,0,O_2,pieno;0.8,0,H,centro" coppie="0 0.35,1,ϑ_1" etichette="-0.4,1.1,R_1;2.8,2.3,R_2" titolo="Two wheels in contact without slipping at the point $H$"}

- $20\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-125\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

With $O_1$ at the origin and $O_2 = (R_1 + R_2, 0)$, the contact point is $H = (R_1, 0)$. The point at $H$ that belongs to the first wheel has velocity $\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\hat{k} \wedge R_1\,\hat{\imath} = R_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\hat{\jmath}$, whereas the one that belongs to the second has velocity $\dot{\vartheta}_2\,\hat{k} \wedge ( -R_2\,\hat{\imath} ) = -R_2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\,\hat{\jmath}$. The absence of slipping imposes that the two velocities coincide, hence
$$
\dot{\vartheta}_2 = -\frac{R_1}{R_2}\,\dot{\vartheta}_1 = -\frac{0.10}{0.25} \cdot 50 = -20\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The second wheel turns clockwise, opposite to the first, and more slowly, because its radius is larger. The relation integrates to $R_1\,\vartheta_1 + R_2\,\vartheta_2 = \text{constant}$, therefore the constraint is holonomic and the system has one degree of freedom.
