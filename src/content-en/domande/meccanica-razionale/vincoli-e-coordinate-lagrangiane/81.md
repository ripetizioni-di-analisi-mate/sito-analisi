The planar system in the figure is formed by the rod $AB$, clamped at $A$ to a wall, and by the rod $BC$, hinged to the first at $B$ and constrained at $C$ by a roller support to a horizontal guide. The constraints are smooth. How many scalar unknowns do the constraint reactions have, including those of the internal hinge?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello,270" titolo="Rod clamped at $A$ connected by a hinge at $B$ to a rod with a roller support at $C$"}

- $4$
- $5$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

The clamped support prevents the two translations of $A$ and the rotation of the rod $AB$, therefore it exerts the components $\Phi_{Ax}$ and $\Phi_{Ay}$ and the reaction moment $\Gamma_A$, that is, $3$ unknowns. The internal hinge at $B$ exerts on the two rods two opposite forces, identified by the $2$ components of one of them, and the roller support exerts a force normal to the guide, that is, $1$ unknown. The reaction unknowns are hence
$$
3 + 2 + 1 = 6 ,
$$
equal to the number $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ of the cardinal equations of the two rods, in agreement with the count of the degrees of freedom, which gives $n = 6 - 6 = 0$. The value $4$ forgets the internal hinge, which has zero virtual work in the whole system but appears when the equations of the single rods are written.
