A body of mass $m$ rests on a plane inclined by $35^\circ$ to the horizontal and the only active forces are the weight and the reaction of the plane. What is the minimum static friction coefficient between body and plane that guarantees equilibrium?

- $f_s = 0.57$
- $f_s = 0.70$
- $f_s = 0.82$
- $f_s = 1.43$

## Explanation

Equilibrium requires the reaction of the plane to be opposite to the weight, therefore its components equal $\Phi_n = m g \cos\alpha$ and $|\vec{\Phi}_t| = m g \sin\alpha$. The Coulomb-Morin law then gives the condition $m g \sin\alpha \leq f_s\,m g \cos\alpha$, that is, $\tan\alpha \leq f_s$, which does not contain the mass. The minimum coefficient therefore equals
$$
f_{s,\min} = \tan 35^\circ = 0.70 .
$$
The values $0.57$ and $0.82$ are the sine and the cosine of $35^\circ$, therefore neither of the two satisfies the equality $f_s = \tan\alpha$.
