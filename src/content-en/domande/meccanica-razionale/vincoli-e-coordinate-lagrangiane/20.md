A slider runs on a fixed straight horizontal guide and a stop, also fixed, prevents it from going beyond the abscissa $x = a$. How is the constraint imposed by the stop classified?

- Nonholonomic and bilateral
- Nonholonomic and unilateral
- Holonomic and bilateral
- Holonomic and unilateral

## Explanation

The stop limits the positions of the slider and not its velocities, since the limitation is written $a - x \geq 0$, a relation between the coordinate $x$ alone. The constraint is therefore holonomic. The relation is an inequality, hence the constraint is unilateral: in the ordinary configurations, with $x < a$, the stop exerts no force, whereas $x = a$ is the boundary configuration, in which the stop exerts a force directed toward the decreasing abscissas.
