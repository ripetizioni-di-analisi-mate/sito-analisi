The plane system in the figure is formed by the three rods $AB$, $BC$ and $CD$, hinged to one another at $B$ and at $C$; the rod $AB$ is hinged to the fixed point $A$ and the end $D$ is constrained by a roller support to the horizontal guide through $A$. The constraints are smooth and bilateral. How many degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.5,2;C,3.5,2.2;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello,270" linee="-0.6,0 6,0" titolo="Chain of three rods with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $D$"}

- $n = 1$
- $n = 2$
- $n = 3$
- $n = 4$

## Explanation

The system is formed by three plane rigid bodies, therefore without constraints it would have $3 \cdot 3 = 9$ configuration parameters. The fixed hinge at $A$ imposes $2$ scalar conditions, each of the two internal hinges at $B$ and at $C$ imposes $2$ of them and the roller support at $D$ imposes $1$, therefore the independent conditions are $2 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 7$. The number of degrees of freedom then equals
$$
n = 9 - 7 = 2 .
$$
As Lagrangian coordinates one takes two of the three angles that the rods form with the horizontal guide, since the third is obtained from the condition that $D$ belong to the guide.
