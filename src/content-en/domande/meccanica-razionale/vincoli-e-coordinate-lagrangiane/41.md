A material point $P$ is threaded on a smooth straight guide that rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed point $O$ with assigned constant angular velocity $\omega$. Which direction does the virtual displacement of $P$ have at the instant considered?

- Zero
- Orthogonal to the guide
- The sum of a term directed along the guide and of a term orthogonal to it
- Directed along the guide

## Explanation

The distance $s = |P - O|$ is taken as Lagrangian coordinate, so that $P - O = s\,\big( \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big)$. The virtual displacement is computed with the constraints regarded as motionless, that is, by differentiating with respect to the Lagrangian coordinate alone, hence
$$
\delta P = \delta s\,\big( \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big) ,
$$
which is a vector directed along the guide. The possible displacement instead also contains the term $s\,\omega\,dt\,\big( -\sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big)$, due to the rotation of the guide, therefore it has a component orthogonal to the guide itself. The guide is smooth, hence its reaction is orthogonal to the virtual displacement and the constraint turns out to be ideal.
