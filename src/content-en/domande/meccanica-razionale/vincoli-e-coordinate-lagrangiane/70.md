A cylinder of radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a board that slides on a horizontal floor, as in the figure. Let $x_T$ be the abscissa of a point of the board, $x_C$ the abscissa of the center of the cylinder and $\vartheta$ the rotation angle of the cylinder, positive counterclockwise. Which condition expresses pure rolling?

::struttura{cerchi="3,1.35,1" poligoni="0.6,0 5.4,0 5.4,0.35 0.6,0.35" linee="-0.3,0 6.3,0" punti="3,1.35,C,baricentro;3,0.35,H,centro" coppie="3 1.35,1,ϑ" frecce="0.9,-0.4,1.9,-0.4,x_T" etichette="3.3,0.95,R" titolo="Cylinder rolling without slipping on a moving board"}

- $\dot{x}_C + R\,\dot{\vartheta} = 0$
- $\dot{x}_C + R\,\dot{\vartheta} = \dot{x}_T$
- $\dot{x}_C - R\,\dot{\vartheta} = \dot{x}_T$
- $\dot{x}_C + R\,\dot{\vartheta} = -\dot{x}_T$

## Explanation

The point $H$ of the cylinder in contact with the board satisfies $H - C = -R\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the fundamental formula of rigid kinematics gives
$$
\vec{v}_H = \dot{x}_C\,\hat{\imath} + \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge ( -R\,\hat{\jmath} ) = \big( \dot{x}_C + R\,\dot{\vartheta} \big)\,\hat{\imath} .
$$
On the moving board pure rolling imposes that the contact point of the cylinder has the same velocity as the board, that is, zero relative velocity, hence
$$
\dot{x}_C + R\,\dot{\vartheta} = \dot{x}_T .
$$
The relation integrates to $x_C + R\,\vartheta - x_T = \text{constant}$, so the constraint is holonomic and the system formed by board and cylinder has $1 + 2 - 1 = 2$ degrees of freedom. With the board at rest one recovers the condition $\dot{x}_C + R\,\dot{\vartheta} = 0$ of rolling on a fixed guide.
