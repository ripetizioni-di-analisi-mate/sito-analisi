A material point $P$ is constrained to the smooth and fixed parabola of equation $y = \dfrac{x^2}{2a}$ of the plane $Oxy$, with $a > 0$, and $q = x$ is taken as Lagrangian coordinate. What is the magnitude of the velocity of $P$?

- $|\dot{x}|\,\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{x^2}{a^2}}$
- $|\dot{x}|\,\Big( 1 + \dfrac{x}{a} \Big)$
- $|\dot{x}|\,\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{x^2}{4a^2}}$
- $\dfrac{|\dot{x}|\,x}{a}$

## Explanation

The position of the point is written $P - O = x\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{x^2}{2a}\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore differentiating with respect to time one obtains
$$
\vec{v}_P = \dot{x}\,\Big( \hat{\imath} + \frac{x}{a}\,\hat{\jmath} \Big) .
$$
The magnitude of this vector then equals
$$
|\vec{v}_P| = |\dot{x}|\,\sqrt{1 + \frac{x^2}{a^2}} .
$$
The velocity is parallel to the vector $\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{x}{a}\,\hat{\jmath}$, which is tangent to the parabola, therefore for a smooth constraint the reaction is orthogonal to it.
