A material point $P$ is threaded on a smooth straight guide that rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed point $O$ with assigned constant angular velocity $\omega = 4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. At the instant considered the point is at distance $s = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$. For the same advance along the guide, what is the magnitude of the difference between the possible displacement and the virtual displacement of $P$, with time measured in seconds and lengths in meters?

- $0$
- $1.5\,dt$
- $6\,dt$
- $12\,dt$

## Explanation

Denoting by $\alpha = \omega t$ the angle of the guide with the $x$ axis, the position of the point is written $P - O = s\,( \cos\alpha\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\alpha\,\hat{\jmath} )$. The virtual displacement is computed with time frozen, therefore it equals $\delta P = \delta s\,( \cos\alpha\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\alpha\,\hat{\jmath} )$, whereas the possible displacement also contains the term due to the motion of the constraint:
$$
dP = ds\,( \cos\alpha\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\alpha\,\hat{\jmath} ) + s\,\omega\,dt\,( -\sin\alpha\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\alpha\,\hat{\jmath} ) .
$$
With $\delta s = ds$ the difference of the two displacements is the second term alone, which is orthogonal to the guide and has magnitude
$$
s\,\omega\,dt = 1.5 \cdot 4\,dt = 6\,dt .
$$
The difference is zero only for a fixed constraint, therefore the value $0$ does not hold for this guide.
