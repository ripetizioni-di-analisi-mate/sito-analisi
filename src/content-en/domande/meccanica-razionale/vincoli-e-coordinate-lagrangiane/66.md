A parcel, regarded as a material point of height $z$, rests on the floor of a freight elevator that rises with assigned constant velocity $u$, so that at the instant $t$ the floor is at height $u\,t$. The parcel can detach upward from the floor. Which relation expresses the constraint and how is it classified?

- $z - u\,t = 0$, holonomic, bilateral and moving
- $z - u\,t \geq 0$, holonomic, unilateral and moving
- $z \geq 0$, holonomic, unilateral and fixed
- $\dot{z} - u \geq 0$, nonholonomic, unilateral and moving

## Explanation

The floor prevents the parcel from going below the height $u\,t$ and leaves it free to rise, therefore the constraint is the inequality
$$
z - u\,t \geq 0 .
$$
The relation contains only the position and time, hence the constraint is holonomic. It is an inequality, therefore the constraint is unilateral, and time appears in it explicitly, so the constraint is moving. In the boundary configurations $z = u\,t$ the parcel is in contact and rises with the floor, whereas in the ordinary ones $z > u\,t$ the parcel is detached. The condition $\dot{z} - u \geq 0$ concerns the velocities and describes a different constraint, since a detached parcel can approach the floor with $\dot{z} < u$.
