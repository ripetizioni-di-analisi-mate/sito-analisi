In a slider-crank mechanism the crank $OA$ has length $r$ and rotates about the fixed hinge $O$, the connecting rod $AB$ has length $\ell = 3r$ and the end $B$ slides on the horizontal guide through $O$. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the crank forms with the guide, what is the abscissa of $B$ for $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$?

- $x_B = 2r$
- $x_B = 2\sqrt{2}\,r$
- $x_B = 3r$
- $x_B = \sqrt{10}\,r$

## Explanation

The crank gives $A - O = r\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + r\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, whereas the end $B$ belongs to the guide, therefore $y_B = 0$. From the condition $|B - A| = \ell$ it follows that $(x_B - r\cos\vartheta)^2 + r^2\sin^2\vartheta = \ell^2$, hence
$$
x_B = r\cos\vartheta + \sqrt{\ell^2 - r^2\sin^2\vartheta} .
$$
For $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ one has $\cos\vartheta = 0$ and $\sin\vartheta = 1$, therefore
$$
x_B = \sqrt{9r^2 - r^2} = 2\sqrt{2}\,r .
$$
The value $3r$ would be obtained by neglecting the term $r^2\sin^2\vartheta$ under the square root, which for $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ equals $r^2$.
