A rod $AB$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ and its end $A$ is required to have velocity parallel to the rod, like the blade of a skate. At the instant when the rod forms the angle $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ with the $x$ axis, which velocity of $A$ is compatible with the constraint?

- $\vec{v}_A = \hat{\imath} + \sqrt{3}\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $\vec{v}_A = -\hat{\imath} + \sqrt{3}\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $\vec{v}_A = \sqrt{3}\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$
- $\vec{v}_A = \hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The constraint of the blade is written $\dot{x} \sin\vartheta - \dot{y} \cos\vartheta = 0$, where $(\dot{x}, \dot{y})$ are the components of $\vec{v}_A$. For $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ one has $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\cos\vartheta = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, hence the condition becomes
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,\dot{x} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\dot{y} = 0 ,
$$
which is satisfied by $\dot{x} = \sqrt{3}$, $\dot{y} = 1$. The velocity $\sqrt{3}\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$ is indeed parallel to the unit vector of the rod $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$. The vector $-\hat{\imath} + \sqrt{3}\,\hat{\jmath}$ is orthogonal to the rod, and $\hat{\imath} + \sqrt{3}\,\hat{\jmath}$ is inclined at $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, therefore neither of them satisfies the condition.
