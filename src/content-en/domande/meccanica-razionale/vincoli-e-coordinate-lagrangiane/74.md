A cart $C$ slides on a horizontal guide and carries a pendulum formed by a rigid rod of length $\ell$, hinged at $C$, with the material point $P$ at the end, as in the figure. The Lagrangian coordinates are the abscissa $x$ of the cart and the angle $\vartheta$ that the rod forms with the downward vertical. What is the virtual displacement of $P$?

::struttura{nodi="C,1.5,0;P,2.46,-1.15" aste="C-P" vincoli="C,carrello,90" linee="-0.5,0.27 4.5,0.27" tratteggi="1.5,0 1.5,-1.7" punti="2.46,-1.15,,pieno" etichette="1.72,-0.6,ϑ;2.15,-0.45,ℓ" frecce="-0.3,-0.4,0.7,-0.4,x" titolo="Cart with pendulum: two degrees of freedom, Lagrangian coordinates $x$ and $\vartheta$"}

- $\ell\,\delta\vartheta\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $\delta x\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\,\delta\vartheta\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $\delta x\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\,\delta\vartheta\,( \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$
- $\delta x\,\hat{\imath} + \delta\vartheta\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$

## Explanation

The position of the point is written $P - O = ( x + \ell \sin\vartheta )\,\hat{\imath} - \ell \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, because the point lies below the cart at distance $\ell$ along the rod. The constraints are fixed, hence the virtual displacement is
$$
\delta P = \frac{\partial P}{\partial x}\,\delta x + \frac{\partial P}{\partial \vartheta}\,\delta\vartheta = \delta x\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\,\delta\vartheta\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} ) .
$$
The first term is the translation of the cart, which the point undergoes entirely, and the second is due to the rotation of the rod and is orthogonal to it. The vector $\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$ is the unit vector of the rod, that is, the direction of the position and not that of its derivative.
