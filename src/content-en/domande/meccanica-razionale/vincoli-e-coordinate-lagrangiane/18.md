A straight guide rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed point $O$ and at the instant $t$ forms the angle $\omega t$ with the $x$ axis, with $\omega$ an assigned constant. Which equation expresses the constraint of a point $P = (x, y)$ threaded on the guide?

- $x^2 + y^2 = \omega^2 t^2$
- $x \sin(\omega t) + y \cos(\omega t) = 0$
- $x \cos(\omega t) - y \sin(\omega t) = 0$
- $x \sin(\omega t) - y \cos(\omega t) = 0$

## Explanation

The unit vector of the guide at the instant $t$ is $\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the point $P$ belongs to the guide if and only if the vector $P - O$ is parallel to that unit vector. The parallelism of two vectors of the plane is equivalent to the vanishing of the determinant of their components, hence the condition is written
$$
x \sin(\omega t) - y \cos(\omega t) = 0 .
$$
The points of the guide are $P - O = s\,\big( \cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} \big)$ with $s$ real, and substituting them into the equation one obtains $s \cos(\omega t) \sin(\omega t) - s \sin(\omega t) \cos(\omega t) = 0$. Substituting the same points into the expression $x \cos(\omega t) - y \sin(\omega t)$ one obtains instead $s \cos(2\omega t)$, which is not zero for every $t$, therefore that relation does not express the constraint.
