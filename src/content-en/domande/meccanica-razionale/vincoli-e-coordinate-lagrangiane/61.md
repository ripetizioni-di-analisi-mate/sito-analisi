The planar system in the figure is a rhombus linkage formed by the four rods $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$, hinged to each other at the vertices. At the vertex $A$ a single fixed hinge connects both rods $AB$ and $DA$ to the ground, whereas the vertex $C$ is constrained by a roller support to the horizontal guide through $A$. The constraints are smooth and bilateral. How many degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,1.5;C,4,0;D,2,-1.5" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" cerniere="B;C;D" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;C,carrello,270" linee="-0.8,0 5.4,0" titolo="Rhombus linkage with a fixed hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $C$"}

- $n = 0$
- $n = 2$
- $n = 3$
- $n = 1$

## Explanation

The rods are four, therefore the starting parameters are $3 \cdot 4 = 12$. The hinge at $A$ connects three bodies, the two rods and the ground, hence it imposes $2 \cdot 2 = 4$ scalar conditions, that is, $2$ for each rod with respect to the ground. The internal hinges at $B$, $C$ and $D$ each connect two rods and impose $2$ conditions each, and the roller support at $C$ imposes $1$, therefore
$$
n = 12 - (4 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1) = 1 .
$$
The Lagrangian coordinate is the abscissa of $C$, from which the positions of $B$ and $D$ follow. Counting the hinge at $A$ with only $2$ conditions one would obtain $n = 3$, a value that does not correspond to the mechanism.
