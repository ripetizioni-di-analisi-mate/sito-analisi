A homogeneous disk of radius $r$ rolls without slipping on the outside of a fixed circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, as in the figure. Let $\varphi$ be the angle that the vector $C - O$ forms with the downward vertical, where $C$ is the center of the disk, and let $\vartheta$ be the rotation angle of the disk; both angles are positive counterclockwise. Which relation links $\dot{\vartheta}$ to $\dot{\varphi}$?

- $\dot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{R}{r}\,\dot{\varphi}$
- $\dot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{R - r}{r}\,\dot{\varphi}$
- $\dot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{R + r}{r}\,\dot{\varphi}$
- $\dot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{r}{R + r}\,\dot{\varphi}$

## Explanation

The center of the disk keeps the distance $R + r$ from $O$, hence, setting $\hat{u} = \sin\varphi\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\varphi\,\hat{\jmath}$, one has $C - O = (R + r)\,\hat{u}$, whereas the contact point satisfies $A - O = R\,\hat{u}$ and therefore $A - C = -r\,\hat{u}$. Differentiating the position of the center one obtains $\vec{v}_C = (R + r)\,\dot{\varphi}\,\hat{w}$, where $\hat{w} = \cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath}$, and the fundamental formula of rigid kinematics gives
$$
\vec{v}_A = \vec{v}_C + \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (A - C) = \big[ (R + r)\,\dot{\varphi} - r\,\dot{\vartheta} \big]\,\hat{w} .
$$
Pure rolling imposes $\vec{v}_A = \vec{0}$, hence
$$
\dot{\vartheta} = \frac{R + r}{r}\,\dot{\varphi} .
$$
The left-hand side is the derivative of $\vartheta$ and the right-hand side is that of $\dfrac{R + r}{r}\,\varphi$, therefore the relation integrates and the constraint is holonomic.
