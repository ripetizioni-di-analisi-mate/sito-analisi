A homogeneous disk of radius $R$ rolls without slipping on the fixed horizontal guide $y = 0$ of the plane $Oxy$, and $\vartheta$ is its rotation angle, positive counterclockwise. What is the velocity of the point of the disk diametrically opposite the contact point?

- $-2R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath}$
- $-R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath}$
- $2R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath}$
- $\vec{0}$

## Explanation

The condition of pure rolling gives $\dot{x}_C + R\,\dot{\vartheta} = 0$, hence the velocity of the center equals $\vec{v}_C = -R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath}$. The point $T$ diametrically opposite the contact satisfies $T - C = R\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the fundamental formula of rigid kinematics gives
$$
\vec{v}_T = \vec{v}_C + \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge R\,\hat{\jmath} = -R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath} - R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath} = -2R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath} ,
$$
where $\hat{k} \wedge \hat{\jmath} = -\hat{\imath}$ has been used. The highest point therefore has velocity of magnitude $2R\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$, twice that of the center, since its distance from the instantaneous center of rotation is twice that of the center itself.
