All the following plane systems have smooth and bilateral constraints. Which of them has two degrees of freedom?

- A slider-crank mechanism with the connecting-rod end constrained by a roller support to a horizontal guide
- A rigid rod with its ends constrained to two fixed orthogonal straight guides
- A material point constrained to a smooth and fixed curve of the plane
- A double pendulum formed by two rods hinged to each other, with the upper rod hinged to a fixed point

## Explanation

The double pendulum is formed by two plane rigid bodies, hence it starts from $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ parameters; the fixed hinge and the internal hinge impose $2 + 2 = 4$ conditions, therefore $n = 6 - 4 = 2$ and the Lagrangian coordinates are the two angles of the rods with the vertical. The slider-crank has $n = 3 \cdot 2 - (2 + 2 + 1) = 1$, since the roller support imposes a single condition. The rod with its ends on the two guides has $n = 3 - 2 = 1$, since the two roller supports impose $x_A = 0$ and $y_B = 0$. The point on a curve of the plane has $n = 2 - 1 = 1$, with a parameter of the curve as Lagrangian coordinate.
