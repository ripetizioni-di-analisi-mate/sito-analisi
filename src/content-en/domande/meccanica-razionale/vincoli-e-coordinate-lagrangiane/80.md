The end $A$ of a rigid rod is constrained by a smooth bilateral roller support to a fixed straight guide inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal and rising to the right, as in the figure. To which unit vector is the reaction exerted by the roller support parallel?

::struttura{nodi="A,2,1.155;B,0.6,2.9" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,300" linee="-0.3,-0.17 4.2,2.42;-0.3,-0.17 1.4,-0.17" etichette="0.75,0.05,30°" titolo="Smooth roller support on a guide inclined at $30^\circ$"}

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $\hat{\jmath}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The roller support leaves the translation along the guide free, therefore the virtual displacements of $A$ are parallel to the unit vector of the guide $\hat{t} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$. The constraint is smooth and bilateral, hence it is ideal and the reaction is orthogonal to every virtual displacement, that is, orthogonal to $\hat{t}$. The unit vector
$$
\hat{n} = -\frac{1}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}
$$
satisfies $\hat{n} \cdot \hat{t} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} = 0$, so the reaction is parallel to $\hat{n}$, with a sense that depends on the sign of its component. The vertical reaction $\hat{\jmath}$ would be that of a roller support on a horizontal guide.
