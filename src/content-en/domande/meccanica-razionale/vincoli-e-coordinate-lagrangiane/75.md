A material point $P$ moves in the vertical plane $Oxy$ and rests on a smooth horizontal floor of equation $y = 0$, from which it can detach upward. In the configuration in which the point lies on the floor, which are the virtual displacements $\delta P = \delta x\,\hat{\imath} + \delta y\,\hat{\jmath}$?

- All those with arbitrary $\delta x$ and $\delta y \geq 0$
- All those with arbitrary $\delta x$ and $\delta y = 0$
- All those with arbitrary $\delta x$ and $\delta y \leq 0$
- All those with $\delta x = 0$ and $\delta y \geq 0$

## Explanation

The support is a unilateral constraint of equation $f(P) = y \geq 0$, and the configuration considered is a boundary one, since $y = 0$ holds. In a boundary configuration the virtual displacements are those that satisfy the linearized inequality $\operatorname{grad} f \cdot \delta P \geq 0$, and with $\operatorname{grad} f = \hat{\jmath}$ the inequality becomes
$$
\delta y \geq 0 ,
$$
while $\delta x$ remains arbitrary. The virtual displacements hence form a half-plane, which contains the slidings along the floor and the detachments upward. The condition $\delta y = 0$ describes the floor as a bilateral constraint, and therefore excludes detachment.
