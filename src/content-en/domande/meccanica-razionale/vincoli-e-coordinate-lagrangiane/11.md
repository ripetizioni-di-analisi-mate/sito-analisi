A rigid rod $AB$ is constrained to a fixed horizontal guide by a smooth sleeve at $A$, which prevents the vertical translation of $A$ and the rotation of the rod and leaves the translation along the guide free. What is the virtual work of the reactions exerted by the sleeve?

- $\Phi_A\,\delta x_A$
- $\Gamma_A\,\delta\vartheta$
- $0$
- $\Phi_A\,\delta x_A + \Gamma_A\,\delta\vartheta$

## Explanation

The sleeve exerts the reaction normal to the guide $\vec{\Phi}_A = \Phi_A\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the reaction moment $\Gamma_A$, since it prevents one translation and the rotation. The system has a single degree of freedom, the abscissa $x_A$, hence the virtual displacements satisfy $\delta A = \delta x_A\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\delta\vartheta = 0$. The virtual work of the reactions then equals
$$
\delta L^{(v)} = \Phi_A\,\hat{\jmath} \cdot \delta x_A\,\hat{\imath} + \Gamma_A\,\delta\vartheta = 0 ,
$$
since $\hat{\jmath} \cdot \hat{\imath} = 0$ and the virtual rotation is zero. The smooth sleeve is therefore an ideal constraint, hence its reactions are eliminated from the equilibrium equations written with virtual work.
