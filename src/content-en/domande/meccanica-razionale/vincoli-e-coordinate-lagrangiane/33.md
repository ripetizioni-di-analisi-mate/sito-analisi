A homogeneous disk of radius $R = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping on the fixed horizontal guide $y = 0$ of the plane $Oxy$, and its center $C$ advances toward the increasing abscissas with velocity $\dot{x}_C = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the rotation angle of the disk, positive counterclockwise, what is $\dot{\vartheta}$?

- $-12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-0.75\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The point of the disk in contact with the guide has zero velocity, therefore the condition of pure rolling is written $\dot{x}_C + R\,\dot{\vartheta} = 0$. One then gets
$$
\dot{\vartheta} = -\frac{\dot{x}_C}{R} = -\frac{3}{0.25} = -12\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The negative sign says that the disk turns clockwise, in agreement with the fact that the center advances toward the increasing abscissas. The value $-0.75\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ would be obtained by multiplying by the radius instead of dividing, therefore it does not even have the dimensions of an angular velocity.
