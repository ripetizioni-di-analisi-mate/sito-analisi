A material point $P$ is constrained to the smooth and fixed cylindrical surface of equation $x^2 + y^2 = R^2$, whose axis coincides with the $z$ axis. Taking as Lagrangian coordinates the angle $\varphi$ about the axis and the height $z$, what is the magnitude of the velocity of $P$?

- $R\,\sqrt{\dot{\varphi}^2 + \dot{z}^2}$
- $R\,\dot{\varphi} + \dot{z}$
- $\sqrt{\dot{\varphi}^2 + \dot{z}^2}$
- $\sqrt{R^2\dot{\varphi}^2 + \dot{z}^2}$

## Explanation

With the chosen coordinates the position of the point is written $P - O = R\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + R\sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} + z\,\hat{k}$, since the radius $R$ is assigned. Differentiating with respect to time one obtains
$$
\vec{v}_P = R\,\dot{\varphi}\,( -\sin\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} ) + \dot{z}\,\hat{k} .
$$
The two terms are orthogonal and have magnitudes $R\,|\dot{\varphi}|$ and $|\dot{z}|$, therefore
$$
|\vec{v}_P| = \sqrt{R^2\dot{\varphi}^2 + \dot{z}^2} .
$$
The sum $R\,\dot{\varphi} + \dot{z}$ would add the magnitudes of two orthogonal vectors, therefore it does not give the magnitude of their sum.
