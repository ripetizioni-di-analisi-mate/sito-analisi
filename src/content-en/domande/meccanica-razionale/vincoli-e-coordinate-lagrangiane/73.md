A disk of radius $R$ moves in a vertical plane keeping in contact with a fixed smooth horizontal guide, on which it can slip. How many degrees of freedom does the disk have and which Lagrangian coordinates can be chosen?

- One, the abscissa $x_C$ of the center, with $\vartheta = -\dfrac{x_C}{R}$
- One, the rotation angle $\vartheta$
- Three, the coordinates $x_C$ and $y_C$ of the center and the angle $\vartheta$
- Two, the abscissa $x_C$ of the center and the rotation angle $\vartheta$

## Explanation

The disk is a planar rigid body, hence it starts from $3$ parameters, for instance $x_C$, $y_C$ and $\vartheta$. The contact with the guide imposes the single condition $y_C = R$, because the smooth guide does not prevent slipping and therefore does not link $\dot{x}_C$ to $\dot{\vartheta}$. One then obtains
$$
n = 3 - 1 = 2 ,
$$
with Lagrangian coordinates $x_C$ and $\vartheta$, independent. The relation $x_C + R\,\vartheta = \text{constant}$ holds only with pure rolling, which adds the second condition and reduces the degrees of freedom to one.
