A rigid rod of a plane system is constrained to the ground at its end $A$ by a smooth clamped support. Which reactions does the clamped support exert?

- A single force normal to the direction of the rod
- A force normal to a guide and a reaction moment
- Two force components and no moment
- Two force components and a reaction moment

## Explanation

A constraint exerts a force component along every direction of translation that it prevents and a reaction moment when it prevents the rotation. The clamped support prevents the two translations of the point $A$ and the rotation of the rod, therefore it exerts the two components $\Phi_{Ax}$ and $\Phi_{Ay}$ and the reaction moment $\Gamma_A$, that is, three scalar unknowns. The fixed hinge, which leaves the rotation free, exerts instead the two force components alone with zero moment, whereas the sleeve on a straight guide exerts a force normal to the guide and a reaction moment.
