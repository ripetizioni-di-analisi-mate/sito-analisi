The system in the figure is formed by a homogeneous disk of radius $R$, hinged at its fixed center $O$, and by a body hanging from an inextensible and flexible string wound on the rim of the disk; the string does not slide on the disk and the free portion is vertical. Let $\vartheta$ be the rotation angle of the disk, positive counterclockwise, and $s$ the descent of the body measured positive downward. Which constraint links $s$ to $\vartheta$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.2" nodi="O,0,0,-;P,1.2,0,-;Q,1.2,-2.2,-" catene="P-Q" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" poligoni="0.7,-2.2 1.7,-2.2 1.7,-2.9 0.7,-2.9" punti="0,0,O,pieno" coppie="-0.55 0.4,1,ϑ" tratteggi="0,0 1.2,0" etichette="0.55,0.2,R" frecce="2.3,-2.2,2.3,-2.9,s" titolo="Pulley of radius $R$ with an inextensible string and a hanging body"}

- $s = R\,\vartheta$
- $s = -2R\,\vartheta$
- $s = -\dfrac{\vartheta}{R}$
- $s = -R\,\vartheta$

## Explanation

The string does not slide on the disk, therefore the point $P$ of the rim from which the string leaves has the same velocity as the free portion. That point satisfies $P - O = R\,\hat{\imath}$, hence the fundamental formula of rigid kinematics gives
$$
\vec{v}_P = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge R\,\hat{\imath} = R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath} ,
$$
that is, a velocity directed upward for $\dot{\vartheta} > 0$. The descent $s$ is measured positive downward, therefore $\dot{s} = -R\,\dot{\vartheta}$ and, integrating with $s = 0$ for $\vartheta = 0$, one obtains $s = -R\,\vartheta$. The constraint is holonomic, bilateral and fixed, as long as the string remains taut.
