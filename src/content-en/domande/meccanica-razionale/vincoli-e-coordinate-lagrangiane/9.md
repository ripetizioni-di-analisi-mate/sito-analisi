The system in the figure is formed by the rods $AB$ and $BC$, both of length $\ell$, hinged to each other at $B$, with a fixed hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $C$ on the horizontal guide through $A$. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod $AB$ forms with the guide, what is the virtual displacement of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,2.6;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello,270" linee="-0.6,0 5.2,0" etichette="0.75,0.33,ϑ" titolo="Two equal rods hinged at $B$, with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $C$"}

- $-2\ell\sin\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\imath}$
- $-\ell\sin\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\imath}$
- $2\ell\cos\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\imath}$
- $-2\ell\sin\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + 2\ell\cos\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The two rods have the same length and the ends $A$ and $C$ lie on the same guide, therefore the triangle $ABC$ is isosceles and the rod $BC$ forms with the guide the angle $\vartheta$ on the opposite side. The abscissa of $C$ then equals $x_C = 2\ell\cos\vartheta$, whereas $y_C = 0$ in every configuration, hence
$$
C - A = 2\ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} .
$$
The system has one degree of freedom with Lagrangian coordinate $\vartheta$ and the constraints are fixed, therefore the virtual displacement is obtained by differentiating with respect to $\vartheta$ and multiplying by $\delta\vartheta$:
$$
\delta C = -2\ell\sin\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} .
$$
The virtual displacement of $C$ is directed along the guide, as the roller support requires.
