A rigid body moves in space with two distinct points $A$ and $B$ of it kept fixed, and the constraints are smooth and bilateral. Which parameter identifies the configuration of the body?

- The three coordinates of the center of mass
- Two Euler angles
- None, because the body is locked
- The rotation angle about the line $AB$

## Explanation

The free body has $6$ degrees of freedom and the fixed point $A$ removes $3$ of them. The point $B$ has an assigned distance from $A$, because the body is rigid, hence the condition that $B$ stays at rest adds only $2$ independent conditions, namely those that fix the direction of the line $AB$. One then obtains
$$
n = 6 - 3 - 2 = 1 .
$$
The admissible configurations are the rotations of the body about the line $AB$, therefore the Lagrangian coordinate is the rotation angle about that line, which becomes a fixed axis.
