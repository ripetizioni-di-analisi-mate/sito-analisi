In the oscillating slotted link in the figure the crank $OA$, of length $r$, rotates about the fixed hinge $O$; the pin $A$ slides in the slot of a rod hinged at the fixed point $Q$, which lies on the horizontal line through $O$ at distance $r$ from $O$, on the side opposite to $A$. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle of the crank and by $\psi$ that of the slotted rod, both measured from the horizontal, what is $\psi$ for $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$?

::struttura{nodi="Q,-2,0;O,0,0;A,1,1.732;L,1.64,2.1,-" aste="O-A;Q-L" vincoli="Q,cerniera;O,cerniera" punti="1,1.732,,pieno" etichette="0.42,0.2,ϑ;-1.35,0.17,ψ;0.35,1.05,r" quote="Q,O,r,-0.6" titolo="Oscillating slotted link: the pin $A$ of the crank slides in the slotted rod hinged at $Q$"}

- $\psi = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\psi = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\psi = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\psi = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The two rods give $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ parameters, the fixed hinges at $O$ and at $Q$ impose $4$ conditions and the pin in the slot imposes $1$, therefore the slotted link has one degree of freedom and $\psi$ depends on $\vartheta$. With $O$ at the origin and $Q = (-r, 0)$ one has $A - Q = r\,(1 + \cos\vartheta)\,\hat{\imath} + r \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, and the slotted rod passes through $A$, hence
$$
\tan\psi = \frac{\sin\vartheta}{1 + \cos\vartheta} = \tan\frac{\vartheta}{2} .
$$
For $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ one obtains $\psi = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$. The result $\psi = \dfrac{\vartheta}{2}$ can also be read on the triangle $QOA$, which is isosceles with $|QO| = |OA| = r$ and has the exterior angle $\vartheta$ at $O$.
