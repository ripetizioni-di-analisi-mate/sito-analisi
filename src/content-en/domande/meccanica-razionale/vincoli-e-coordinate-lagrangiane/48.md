A rigid rod has the end $A$ constrained by a roller support to the horizontal guide $y = 0$ and passes through a swivel ring fixed at the point $Q = (0, h)$, with $h > 0$, as in the figure; the ring lets the rod slide and allows it to rotate. Denoting by $x_A$ the abscissa of $A$ and by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod forms with the $x$ axis, which constraint equation links $x_A$ and $\vartheta$?

::struttura{nodi="A,-2,0;B,1.2,3.2" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,270" linee="-3.5,0 2.2,0;-0.35,2 0.35,2" punti="0,2,Q,centro" etichette="-1.45,0.22,ϑ" quote="0 0,0 2,h,-0.4" titolo="Rod with the end $A$ on a horizontal guide, passing through the fixed swivel ring $Q$"}

- $x_A \cos\vartheta + h \sin\vartheta = 0$
- $x_A \sin\vartheta + h \cos\vartheta = 0$
- $x_A \sin\vartheta - h \cos\vartheta = 0$
- $x_A^2 + h^2 = \ell^2$

## Explanation

The rod passes through $Q$ if and only if the vector $Q - A = -x_A\,\hat{\imath} + h\,\hat{\jmath}$ is parallel to the unit vector of the rod $\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$. Two vectors of the plane are parallel if and only if the determinant of their components vanishes, hence
$$
-x_A \sin\vartheta - h \cos\vartheta = 0 ,
$$
that is, $x_A \sin\vartheta + h \cos\vartheta = 0$. For $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ one obtains $x_A = -h$, and indeed the line through $(-h, 0)$ inclined at $45^\circ$ passes through $(0, h)$. The equation $x_A^2 + h^2 = \ell^2$ would assume that $Q$ is the other end of the rod, whereas in the ring the rod slides and the distance $|Q - A|$ changes.
