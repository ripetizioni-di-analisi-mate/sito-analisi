In the four-bar linkage in the figure the rods $AB$ and $DC$ have the same length $\ell$ and are hinged to the fixed points $A$ and $D$, and the rod $BC$ is as long as the segment $AD$, so that $ABCD$ remains a parallelogram. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod $AB$ forms with the line $AD$, what is the angular velocity of the rod $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3,0;B,1,1.732;C,4,1.732" aste="A-B;B-C;D-C" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" etichette="0.42,0.2,ϑ" titolo="Parallelogram four-bar linkage with fixed hinges at $A$ and at $D$"}

- $\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k}$
- $-\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k}$
- $\vec{0}$
- $2\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The four-bar linkage has one degree of freedom, with Lagrangian coordinate $\vartheta$. With $A$ at the origin and $D = (d, 0)$ one has $B - A = \ell\,( \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$ and, since $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, $C - D = B - A$, hence
$$
C - B = (C - D) + (D - A) - (B - A) = D - A = d\,\hat{\imath} .
$$
The vector $C - B$ is constant, therefore the rod $BC$ keeps the horizontal direction and its angular velocity is **zero**. The rod $BC$ translates, and all its points have the velocity of $B$, that is, $\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\,( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$.
