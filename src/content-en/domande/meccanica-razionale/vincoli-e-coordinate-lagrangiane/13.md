A body of mass $m$ rests on a rough horizontal plane, with static friction coefficient $f_s = 0.30$. A force of magnitude $F$ acts on it, directed downward and inclined by the angle $\gamma$ to the horizontal, which tends to push the body along the plane. What is the minimum value of $\gamma$ for which the body stays in equilibrium whatever the magnitude $F$?

- $\gamma = 16.70^\circ$
- $\gamma = 18.43^\circ$
- $\gamma = 71.57^\circ$
- $\gamma = 73.30^\circ$

## Explanation

The applied force has component $F \cos\gamma$ along the plane and pressing component $F \sin\gamma$, therefore equilibrium requires $\Phi_n = m g + F \sin\gamma$ and $|\vec{\Phi}_t| = F \cos\gamma$. The Coulomb-Morin law then gives the condition
$$
F \cos\gamma \leq f_s\,( m g + F \sin\gamma ) ,
$$
which holds for every $F$ if and only if $\cos\gamma \leq f_s \sin\gamma$, that is, $\tan\gamma \geq \dfrac{1}{f_s}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{f_s} = \dfrac{10}{3}$, the minimum value required is
$$
\gamma = \arctan \frac{10}{3} = 73.30^\circ .
$$
The value $16.70^\circ$ is the friction angle $\rho = \arctan 0.30$, and the condition found says that the angle $90^\circ - \gamma$ between the force and the normal to the plane does not exceed $\rho$.
