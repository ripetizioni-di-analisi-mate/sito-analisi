The point $P_1$ moves on a smooth horizontal table and is connected by an inextensible string of length $\ell$, which passes through a small hole $O$ in the table, to the point $P_2$, which hangs below the hole and moves along the vertical through $O$, as in the figure. With the string taut, which Lagrangian coordinates can be chosen and what is $P_2 - O$?

::struttura{nodi="P_1,-2.4,0;O,0,0,-;P_2,0,-1.4" catene="P_1-O;O-P_2" linee="-3.6,0 -0.12,0;0.12,0 1.4,0" punti="-2.4,0,,pieno;0,-1.4,,pieno" etichette="0.25,0.25,O;-1.2,0.3,r" titolo="Two points connected by a string that passes through the hole $O$ of a horizontal table"}

- The polar coordinates $(r, \varphi)$ of $P_1$ on the table, with $P_2 - O = -(\ell - r)\,\hat{k}$
- The polar coordinates $(r, \varphi)$ of $P_1$ on the table, with $P_2 - O = -r\,\hat{k}$
- The distance $r = |P_1 - O|$ alone, with $P_2 - O = -(\ell - r)\,\hat{k}$
- The coordinates $(r, \varphi)$ of $P_1$ and the height $z_2$ of $P_2$, independent of each other

## Explanation

The point $P_1$ on the table has two coordinates, which are taken polar with center at the hole, $r = |P_1 - O|$ and the angle $\varphi$; the point $P_2$ on the vertical through $O$ has only its height. The taut string imposes that the sum of the two segments equals $\ell$, that is, $r + |P_2 - O| = \ell$, therefore the degrees of freedom are $3 - 1 = 2$. One then takes $r$ and $\varphi$ as Lagrangian coordinates, and the position of $P_2$ follows from the constraint:
$$
P_2 - O = -(\ell - r)\,\hat{k} .
$$
The hanging segment shortens when $P_1$ moves away from the hole, and the same result holds for every value of $\varphi$, since the rotation of $P_1$ about the hole does not change the length of the segment on the table.
