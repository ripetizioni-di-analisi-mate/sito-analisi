## Explanation

The system is formed by two plane rigid bodies, the disk and the rod, therefore it starts from $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ configuration parameters. The pure rolling of the disk on the guide imposes $2$ scalar conditions, that is, the contact and the absence of slipping, the internal hinge at $C$ imposes $2$ of them and the roller support at $B$ imposes $1$, therefore the independent conditions are $5$. The number of degrees of freedom then equals
$$
n = 6 - 5 = 1 ,
$$
and as Lagrangian coordinate one takes the rotation angle of the disk or the abscissa of its center.
