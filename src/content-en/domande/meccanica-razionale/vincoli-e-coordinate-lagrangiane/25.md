A rigid body moves in space with one of its points $\Omega$ kept fixed, and the constraint is smooth and bilateral. How many degrees of freedom does the body have?

- $n = 1$
- $n = 2$
- $n = 3$
- $n = 6$

## Explanation

A free rigid body in space has $6$ degrees of freedom, which are the three coordinates of one of its points and the three Euler angles. The condition that the point $\Omega$ stay at rest assigns its three coordinates, therefore it imposes $3$ independent scalar conditions and one obtains
$$
n = 6 - 3 = 3 .
$$
As Lagrangian coordinates there then remain the three Euler angles $\psi$, $\vartheta$ and $\varphi$, which identify the orientation of the body about the fixed point. The motion of a rigid body with a fixed point is studied with the Euler equations, treated in the topic on the dynamics of the rigid body.
