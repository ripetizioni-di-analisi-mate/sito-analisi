A rigid square plate of side $a$ is hinged at its vertex $O$ to a fixed point and rotates in its own plane, as in the figure; $\alpha$ is the rotation angle of the plate. What magnitude and what direction does the virtual displacement of the vertex $V$ opposite to $O$ have?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;V,1.414,2.449" vincoli="O,cerniera" poligoni="0,0 1.932,0.518 1.414,2.449 -0.518,1.932" tratteggi="0,0 1.414,2.449" quote="0 0,1.932 0.518,a,0.45" titolo="Square plate of side $a$ hinged at the vertex $O$"}

- Magnitude $a\,|\delta\alpha|$, orthogonal to $OV$
- Magnitude $\sqrt{2}\,a\,|\delta\alpha|$, directed along $OV$
- Magnitude $\sqrt{2}\,a\,|\delta\alpha|$, orthogonal to $OV$
- Magnitude $2a\,|\delta\alpha|$, orthogonal to $OV$

## Explanation

The plate has one degree of freedom, because the hinge removes $2$ of the $3$ parameters of the planar rigid body, and its virtual displacements are rotations about $O$. By the fundamental formula of rigid kinematics, with $O$ at rest, the virtual displacement of $V$ is
$$
\delta V = \delta\alpha\,\hat{k} \wedge (V - O) .
$$
The opposite vertex is at a distance from $O$ equal to the diagonal, that is, $|V - O| = \sqrt{2}\,a$, and the cross product with $\hat{k}$ rotates $V - O$ by a right angle in the plane. The virtual displacement hence has magnitude $\sqrt{2}\,a\,|\delta\alpha|$ and is orthogonal to $OV$. The magnitude $a\,|\delta\alpha|$ is that of the two vertices adjacent to $O$.
