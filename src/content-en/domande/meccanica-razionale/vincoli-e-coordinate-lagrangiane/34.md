A cylinder is held between two parallel horizontal belts. The upper belt travels right at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the lower belt left at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The cylinder rolls without slipping at both contacts. What is the velocity of its centre?

::struttura{cerchi="3,1,1" linee="0,0 6,0;0,2 6,2" punti="3,1,C,centro" frecce="4.0,2.20,5.65,2.20,6 m/s;1.85,-0.20,1.05,-0.20,2 m/s" riferimento="0" titolo="The cylinder meets the belts at diametrically opposite points. The arrows indicate the belt velocities."}

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ right
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ left
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ right
- Zero

## Explanation

The contact velocities are $v_C+R\omega$ and $v_C-R\omega$, with a consistent angular sign convention. Their mean cancels rotation, giving $(6-2)/2=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right.
