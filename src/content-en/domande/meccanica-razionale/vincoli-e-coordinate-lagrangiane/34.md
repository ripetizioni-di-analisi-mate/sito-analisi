A cylinder of radius $R$ is squeezed between two parallel horizontal belts, as in the figure: the upper belt moves with velocity $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right and the lower one with velocity $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the left. The cylinder rolls without slipping on both belts. What is the velocity of its center $C$?

::struttura{cerchi="3,1,1" linee="0,0 6,0;0,2 6,2" punti="3,1,C,centro" frecce="4.0,2.2,5.65,2.2,6 m/s;1.85,-0.2,1.05,-0.2,2 m/s" riferimento=0 titolo="Cylinder between two belts moving in opposite directions"}

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the left
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right
- $0$

## Explanation

Rolling without slipping requires each contact point of the cylinder to have the same velocity as the belt it touches. Setting $\vec{v}_C = v_C\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, the fundamental formula gives for the upper contact point, with $R\,\hat{\jmath}$ as position relative to $C$, the velocity $(v_C - \omega R)\,\hat{\imath}$, and for the lower one, with $-\,R\,\hat{\jmath}$, the velocity $(v_C + \omega R)\,\hat{\imath}$. The two rolling conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
v_C - \omega R = 6\,\mathrm{m/s} \\
v_C + \omega R = -\,2\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{cases}
$$
and adding the two equations one obtains $v_C = \dfrac{6 - 2}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, while $\omega R = -\,4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, that is, the cylinder rotates clockwise. The velocity of the center is therefore $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right. The value $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is obtained by averaging the magnitudes of the two velocities without taking their directions into account.
