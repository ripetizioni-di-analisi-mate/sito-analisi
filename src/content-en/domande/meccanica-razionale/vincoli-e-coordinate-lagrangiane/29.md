A double pendulum is formed by the rods $OA$ and $AB$, of lengths $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$, hinged to each other at $A$, with the rod $OA$ hinged to the fixed point $O$. Let $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ be the angles that the two rods form with the downward vertical. What is $B - O$?

- $(\ell_1\sin\vartheta_1 + \ell_2\sin\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\imath} + (\ell_1\cos\vartheta_1 + \ell_2\cos\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $(\ell_1\sin\vartheta_1 + \ell_2\sin\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\imath} - (\ell_1\cos\vartheta_1 + \ell_2\cos\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $(\ell_1\sin\vartheta_1 - \ell_2\sin\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\imath} - (\ell_1\cos\vartheta_1 - \ell_2\cos\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $\ell_2\sin\vartheta_2\,\hat{\imath} - \ell_2\cos\vartheta_2\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The angle $\vartheta_1$ is measured from the downward vertical, therefore the first rod gives $A - O = \ell_1\,( \sin\vartheta_1\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta_1\,\hat{\jmath} )$, with negative ordinate for $\vartheta_1$ small. By the same reasoning the second rod gives $B - A = \ell_2\,( \sin\vartheta_2\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta_2\,\hat{\jmath} )$. Adding the two vectors one obtains
$$
B - O = (\ell_1\sin\vartheta_1 + \ell_2\sin\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\imath} - (\ell_1\cos\vartheta_1 + \ell_2\cos\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
For $\vartheta_1 = \vartheta_2 = 0$ the formula gives $B - O = -(\ell_1 + \ell_2)\,\hat{\jmath}$, that is, the two rods aligned along the downward vertical, as it must be.
