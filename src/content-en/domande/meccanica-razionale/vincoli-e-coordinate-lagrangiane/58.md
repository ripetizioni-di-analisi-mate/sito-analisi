A material point $P$ is constrained to lie on the spherical surface of center $O$ and radius $R$ and on the plane $z = h$, with $0 < h < R$. Taking the longitude $\varphi$ as Lagrangian coordinate, what is $P - O$?

- $R\,( \cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} ) + h\,\hat{k}$
- $\sqrt{R^2 - h^2}\,( \cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} ) + h\,\hat{k}$
- $\sqrt{R^2 + h^2}\,( \cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} ) + h\,\hat{k}$
- $\sqrt{R^2 - h^2}\,( \cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} )$

## Explanation

The constraint equations are $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = R^2$ and $z = h$, and their Jacobian matrix has rank $2$ for $0 < h < R$, hence the point has $n = 3 - 2 = 1$ degree of freedom. Substituting $z = h$ into the first equation one obtains $x^2 + y^2 = R^2 - h^2$, therefore the point moves along the circle of radius $\sqrt{R^2 - h^2}$ with center on the $z$ axis at height $h$. One then gets
$$
P - O = \sqrt{R^2 - h^2}\,( \cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} ) + h\,\hat{k} .
$$
The expression with the radius $R$ gives $|P - O|^2 = R^2 + h^2$, so it describes points outside the sphere.
