In a slider-crank mechanism the crank $OA$ has length $r$, the connecting rod $AB$ has length $\ell = 2r$ and the end $B$ slides on the horizontal guide through $O$. Let $\vartheta$ be the angle that the crank forms with the guide and let $\varphi$ be the acute angle that the connecting rod forms with the guide. What is $\varphi$ for $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$?

- $\varphi = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\varphi = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\varphi = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\varphi = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The end $B$ belongs to the guide, therefore $y_B = 0$, whereas the ordinate of $A$ equals $y_A = r\sin\vartheta$. The point $A$ is the other end of the connecting rod, hence its ordinate is also written $y_A = \ell\sin\varphi$, and equating the two expressions one obtains
$$
\sin\varphi = \frac{r}{\ell}\,\sin\vartheta .
$$
With $\ell = 2r$ and $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ one gets $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and the acute angle that satisfies this equality is $\varphi = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$. The angle of the connecting rod is therefore not an independent Lagrangian coordinate, since the relation expresses it as a function of $\vartheta$.
