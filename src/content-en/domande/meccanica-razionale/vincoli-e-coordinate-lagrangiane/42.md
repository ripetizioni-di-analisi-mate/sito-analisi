A smooth moving guide constrains a point to $x=ut$, with constant $u$. The reaction has component $R_x$ and no $y$ component. What are its actual power and its work in an admissible virtual displacement at fixed time, respectively?

- $R_xu,\;0$
- $0,\;0$ for all $R_xu$
- $0,\;R_x\delta y$
- $R_xu,\;R_x\delta y$

## Explanation

In actual motion $\dot x=u$, so the reaction power is $R_xu$. During a virtual displacement time is fixed and $\delta x=0$, hence the normal reaction does no virtual work.
