A material point $P$ is constrained to the smooth, fixed and bilateral surface of equation $z = x^2 + y^2$. To which vector is the constraint reaction parallel at the point $P = (1, 0, 1)$ of the surface?

- $-2\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{k}$
- $2\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{k}$
- $\hat{\imath} + 2\,\hat{k}$
- $\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The constraint is smooth and bilateral, therefore the theorem on the reaction of a smooth constraint applies and guarantees that the reaction is parallel to $\operatorname{grad} f$, where $f(x, y, z) = z - x^2 - y^2$. One computes the gradient and obtains
$$
\operatorname{grad} f = -2x\,\hat{\imath} - 2y\,\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k} .
$$
The point $P = (1, 0, 1)$ satisfies the equation of the surface, since $1 = 1^2 + 0^2$, and at it the gradient equals $-2\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{k}$, hence the reaction is parallel to this vector. The unit vector $\hat{k}$ is normal to the surface only at the vertex $(0, 0, 0)$, therefore it does not give the direction sought at $P$.
