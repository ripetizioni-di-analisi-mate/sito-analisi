A holonomic system is subject to bilateral and fixed constraints, that is, to constraints whose equations do not contain time explicitly. Which relation holds between the possible displacements and the virtual displacements of the system?

- The virtual displacements are a proper subset of the possible displacements
- The two sets coincide, up to the factor $dt$
- The possible displacements are a proper subset of the virtual displacements
- The two sets have no element in common

## Explanation

The possible displacement equals $dP_i = \sum_h \dfrac{\partial P_i}{\partial q_h}\,dq_h + \dfrac{\partial P_i}{\partial t}\,dt$, whereas the virtual displacement equals $\delta P_i = \sum_h \dfrac{\partial P_i}{\partial q_h}\,\delta q_h$. The constraints are fixed, therefore the functions $P_i(q)$ do not depend explicitly on time and the term $\dfrac{\partial P_i}{\partial t}\,dt$ is zero. The possible displacement is then the virtual displacement obtained by taking $\delta q_h = dq_h$, hence the two sets coincide up to the factor $dt$. With at least one moving constraint the term $\dfrac{\partial P_i}{\partial t}\,dt$ is not zero and is not of the form $\sum_h \dfrac{\partial P_i}{\partial q_h}\,\delta q_h$, therefore in that case the two sets are different.
