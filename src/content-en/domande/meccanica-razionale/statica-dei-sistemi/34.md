A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ lies horizontally in a vertical plane, with a fixed hinge at $A$ and, at $B$, a roller support that slides on a smooth guide inclined at the angle $\beta$ to the horizontal, as in the diagram in the figure. The weight is the only active force. What is the magnitude of the reaction of the roller support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,300" tratteggi="6,0 7.6,0" forze="3 0,0,-1.1,mg" quote="A,B,ℓ,1.4" etichette="6.8,-0.45,β" titolo="Horizontal rod with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support on a guide inclined at $\beta$ at $B$"}

- $\dfrac{m g}{2}$
- $\dfrac{m g \cos\beta}{2}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2\sin\beta}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2\cos\beta}$

## Explanation

The roller support slides on a smooth guide, therefore its reaction is orthogonal to the guide and, given that the guide is inclined at $\beta$ to the horizontal, the reaction makes the angle $\beta$ with the vertical and has components $-\,\Phi_B\sin\beta$ and $\Phi_B\cos\beta$. With the pole at $A$ the reaction of the hinge has zero moment and the moment of the reaction of the roller support, applied at $B - A = \ell\,\hat{\imath}$, equals $\ell\,\Phi_B\cos\beta$, therefore the moment equation is
$$
\ell\,\Phi_B\cos\beta - \frac{\ell}{2}\,m g = 0 .
$$
From it follows $\Phi_B = \dfrac{m g}{2\cos\beta}$, which for $\beta = 0$ reduces to the value $\dfrac{m g}{2}$ of the roller support on a horizontal guide. The value $\dfrac{m g \cos\beta}{2}$ would be obtained by multiplying by the cosine instead of dividing.
