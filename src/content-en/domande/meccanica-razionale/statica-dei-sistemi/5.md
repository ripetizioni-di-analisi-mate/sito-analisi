A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at $A$ to a fixed point, in a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge. An ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length connects the end $B$ to the fixed point $D$, lying on the horizontal through $A$ at distance $\ell$ from $A$, as in the diagram in the figure. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod makes with the horizontal, measured below it, which condition identifies the equilibrium configuration?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.25,-2.68;D,3.5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 4.1,0" linee="2.25,-2.68 2.48,-2.20 2.75,-1.98 2.60,-1.56 3.02,-1.41 2.86,-0.99 3.29,-0.84 3.27,-0.48 3.50,0.00" forze="1.13 -1.34,0,-1,mg" quote="A,D,ℓ,-0.9" etichette="0.72,-0.5,ϑ;3.5,-1.5,k" titolo="Rod hinged at $A$ with the spring between $B$ and the fixed point $D$"}

- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{4\,k\,\ell}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{2\,m g}{k\,\ell}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{k\,\ell}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{2\,k\,\ell}$

## Explanation

The moment equation with respect to $A$ does not contain the reaction of the hinge, therefore it is the equation that determines the equilibrium configuration. With $B - A = \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $D - A = \ell\,\hat{\imath}$, the spring of zero natural length exerts on $B$ the force $-\,k\,(B - D) = k\,\ell\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)\,\hat{\imath} + k\,\ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, whose moment with respect to $A$ equals $k\,\ell^2\sin\vartheta$. The weight, applied at the midpoint, gives the moment $-\,\dfrac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$, therefore the equation is
$$
k\,\ell^2\sin\vartheta - \frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta = 0 .
$$
Therefore $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{2\,k\,\ell}$. The condition $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{k\,\ell}$ is obtained by applying the weight at the end $B$ instead of at the midpoint of the rod.
