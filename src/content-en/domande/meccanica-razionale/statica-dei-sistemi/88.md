A particle $P$ of mass $m$ hangs in a vertical plane from two ideal strings, fixed to the ceiling at the points $A$ and $B$; each string makes the angle $45^\circ$ with the vertical, and $B$ lies to the right of $P$. A horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$ directed to the right, with $F > 0$, is applied to the particle, as in the diagram in the figure. Beyond which value of $F$ does the string $PB$ go slack?

::struttura{nodi="A,-2.2,2.2;B,2.2,2.2;P,0,0" catene="A-P;B-P" linee="-2.9,2.2 2.9,2.2" tratteggi="0,0 0,2.2" punti="0,0" forze="P,1.1,0,F;0 -1.2,0,-1.1," etichette="-0.5,1.35,45°;0.55,1.35,45°;0.4,-0.85,mg" titolo="Particle hanging from the strings $PA$ and $PB$, inclined at $45^\circ$ to the vertical, with the horizontal force $\vec{F}$"}

- $\sqrt{2}\,m g$
- $m g$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,m g$

## Explanation

As long as both strings are taut, the particle remains in the position in the figure and the tensions $T_A$ and $T_B$ are obtained from the equilibrium of the particle. The horizontal and vertical components of the resultant give
$$
\begin{cases}
-\,T_A\sin 45^\circ + T_B\sin 45^\circ + F = 0 \\[4pt]
T_A\cos 45^\circ + T_B\cos 45^\circ - m g = 0
\end{cases}
$$
that is $T_A - T_B = \sqrt{2}\,F$ and $T_A + T_B = \sqrt{2}\,m g$, therefore $T_B = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,(m g - F)$. The string is a unilateral constraint and requires $T_B \ge 0$, hence the string $PB$ **goes slack** for $F > m g$. The value $\sqrt{2}\,m g$ is the tension of the string $PA$ in that condition.
