Two homogeneous rods $AB$ and $BC$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $B$; the end $A$ is hinged to the ground and the end $C$ is constrained by a roller support that slides on a smooth horizontal guide through $A$. An ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length connects the roller support $C$ to the fixed point $A$, as in the diagram in the figure. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that each rod makes with the horizontal, which condition identifies the equilibrium configuration with $\vartheta \in \left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.6,1.5;C,5.2,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" cerniere="B" linee="-0.8,0 6,0;0.00,0.00 0.94,0.00 1.49,-0.28 2.05,0.28 2.60,-0.28 3.15,0.28 3.71,-0.28 4.26,0.00 5.20,0.00" forze="1.3 0.75,0,-1,mg;3.9 0.75,0,-1,mg" etichette="0.85,0.3,ϑ;2.6,0.55,k" titolo="Two rods hinged at $B$, with the spring between the roller support $C$ and the fixed point $A$"}

- $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{2\,k\,\ell}$
- $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{4\,k\,\ell}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{4\,k\,\ell}$
- $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{8\,k\,\ell}$

## Explanation

The moment equation of the whole system with respect to $A$ contains the reaction of the roller support alone, since the spring is horizontal and applied at $A$ and the reaction of the hinge passes through the pole, and it gives $\Phi_C = m g$. The system is then taken apart and the moment equation of the rod $BC$ with respect to $B$, where the internal reaction acts, is written: at $C$ the reaction of the roller support $m g\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the elastic force $-\,2\,k\,\ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath}$ act, whereas the weight acts at the midpoint of $BC$, therefore
$$
-\,\frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta + m g \ell\cos\vartheta - 2\,k\,\ell^2\sin\vartheta\cos\vartheta = 0 .
$$
Since $\cos\vartheta \neq 0$ for $\vartheta \in \left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, one divides by $\ell\cos\vartheta$ and obtains $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{4\,k\,\ell}$. The condition with $8\,k\,\ell$ in the denominator corresponds to taking into account the weight of one rod alone.
