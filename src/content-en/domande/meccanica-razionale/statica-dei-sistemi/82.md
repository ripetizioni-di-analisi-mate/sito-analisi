A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ rests, in a vertical plane, inside a groove formed by two smooth planes inclined at $45^\circ$ and at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. The end $A$ lies on the plane inclined at $45^\circ$ and the end $B$ on the plane inclined at $30^\circ$, as in the diagram in the figure, and the weight is the only active force. What angle does the rod make with the horizontal at equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="A,-2.48,2.48;B,1.05,0.61" aste="A-B" linee="-3.3,3.3 0,0 4,2.31" tratteggi="-1.6,0 1.6,0;1.05,0.61 -0.6,0.61" forze="-0.72 1.54,0,-1,mg" etichette="-0.75,0.28,45°;1.75,0.32,30°;0.2,0.78,ϑ" titolo="Rod resting with its ends on the smooth planes inclined at $45^\circ$ and at $30^\circ$, drawn in a generic configuration"}

- $36.21^\circ$
- $11.93^\circ$
- $15.00^\circ$
- $20.10^\circ$

## Explanation

The reactions of the smooth planes are normal to the planes, therefore $\vec{\Phi}_A$ makes the angle $45^\circ$ with the vertical and points toward the $30^\circ$ plane, while $\vec{\Phi}_B$ makes the angle $30^\circ$ with the vertical and points toward the $45^\circ$ plane. The horizontal component of the first cardinal equation gives $\Phi_A \sin 45^\circ = \Phi_B \sin 30^\circ$, that is, $\Phi_B = \sqrt{2}\,\Phi_A$. Let $\vartheta$ be the angle that the rod makes with the horizontal, positive when $B$ lies lower than $A$; then $\vec{\Phi}_A$ makes the angle $45^\circ + \vartheta$ with the rod and $\vec{\Phi}_B$ the angle $60^\circ - \vartheta$. The moment equation with respect to the center of mass $G$ does not contain the weight, and the moments of the two reactions have opposite senses, therefore
$$
\Phi_A\,\frac{\ell}{2}\sin(45^\circ + \vartheta) = \Phi_B\,\frac{\ell}{2}\sin(60^\circ - \vartheta) .
$$
Substituting $\Phi_B = \sqrt{2}\,\Phi_A$ and expanding the sines one obtains $\sin\vartheta + \cos\vartheta = \sqrt{3}\cos\vartheta - \sin\vartheta$, hence
$$
\tan\vartheta = \frac{\sqrt{3} - 1}{2} ,
\qquad
\vartheta = 20.10^\circ ,
$$
and the lower end is the one resting on the less steep plane. The value $36.21^\circ$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $2$ in the denominator.
