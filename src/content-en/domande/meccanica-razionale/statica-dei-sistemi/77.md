A beam is formed by two rigid rods of negligible mass, $AB$ of length $a$ and $BC$ of length $b$, horizontally aligned and connected by an internal hinge at $B$. At $A$ there is a clamped support and at $C$ a roller support on a smooth horizontal guide; at the midpoint of $BC$ a vertical force $\vec{F}$ directed downward acts, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the reactive moment $\Gamma_A$ of the clamped support, measured counterclockwise?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" forze="4.5 0,0,-1.1,F" quote="A,B,a,0.8;B,C,b,0.8" titolo="Beam with a clamped support at $A$, an internal hinge at $B$ and a roller support at $C$, loaded at the midpoint of $BC$"}

- $F\left(a + \dfrac{b}{2}\right)$
- $\dfrac{F a}{2}$
- $F a$
- $\dfrac{F b}{2}$

## Explanation

The system is taken apart at $B$. The rod $BC$ is subject to the force $\vec{F}$, to the vertical reaction $\Phi_C$ of the roller support and to the action of $AB$ at $B$; the moment equation with respect to $B$ gives $\Phi_C\,b = F\,\dfrac{b}{2}$, therefore $\Phi_C = \dfrac{F}{2}$, and the first cardinal equation gives for the action of $AB$ on $BC$ the vertical component $\dfrac{F}{2}$, upward. By the action-reaction principle the rod $BC$ exerts on $AB$, at $B$, the force $\dfrac{F}{2}$ downward. The moment equation of the rod $AB$ with respect to $A$ is then
$$
\Gamma_A - \frac{F}{2}\,a = 0 ,
\qquad\text{therefore}\qquad
\Gamma_A = \frac{F a}{2} .
$$
The value $F\left(a + \dfrac{b}{2}\right)$ is the reactive moment of a single cantilever from $A$ to $C$, without internal hinge and without roller support.
