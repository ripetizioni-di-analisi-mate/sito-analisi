A homogeneous door of mass $m = 20\,\mathrm{kg}$ and width $b = 0.8\,\mathrm{m}$ is supported, in a vertical plane, by two hinges placed on its vertical edge at distance $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$ from each other. The lower hinge $A$ is modeled as a fixed hinge and the upper one $B$ as a roller support on a smooth vertical guide, which exerts a horizontal reaction, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the magnitude of the reaction of the upper hinge?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0.5;B,0,4.5" poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,5 0,5" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;B,carrello,180" forze="1 2.5,0,-1.1,mg" quote="A,B,1.6 m,-0.9;0 0,2 0,0.8 m,0.6" titolo="Door supported by the hinge at $A$ and by the roller support on a vertical guide at $B$"}

- $98.10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $196.20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24.53\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49.05\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The door is a plane rigid body with three unknown reaction components, the two components at $A$ and the horizontal reaction $\Phi_B$ at $B$. The moment equation with respect to $A$ does not contain the reaction of the hinge. The weight, applied at the center of the door, has lever arm $\dfrac{b}{2} = 0.4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the reaction at $B$ has lever arm $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore
$$
\Phi_B \cdot 1.6\,\mathrm{m} = 20 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.4\,\mathrm{m} ,
\qquad\text{whence}\qquad
\Phi_B = 49.05\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The upper hinge pulls the door toward the side of the hinges, the horizontal component of the reaction at $A$ is opposite, and the lower hinge supports the whole weight. The value $98.10\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by taking the whole width $b$ as the lever arm of the weight.
