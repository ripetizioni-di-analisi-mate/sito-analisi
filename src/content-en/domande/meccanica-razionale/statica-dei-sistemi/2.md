A particle $P$ of mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs in a vertical plane from two ideal strings $PA$ and $PB$, fixed to two points $A$ and $B$ lying at the same height, as in the diagram in the figure. The string $PA$ makes the angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ with the horizontal and the string $PB$ the angle $\beta = 45^\circ$. What is the tension of the string $PA$?

::struttura{nodi="A,-3.2,3;B,5,3;P,2,0" catene="A-P;B-P" linee="-3.9,3 5.7,3" tratteggi="0.4,0 3.6,0" forze="P,0,-1.1,mg" etichette="1.1,0.22,α;2.55,0.3,β" titolo="Particle hanging from two strings that make the angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ with the horizontal"}

- $25.39\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35.91\,\mathrm{N}$
- $43.98\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49.05\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The particle is free and subject to the weight and to the two tensions, therefore equilibrium requires that the resultant vanish. Projecting onto the horizontal and onto the vertical, with $T_A$ directed from $P$ toward $A$ and $T_B$ from $P$ toward $B$, one obtains
$$
\begin{cases}
-\,T_A\cos\alpha + T_B\cos\beta = 0 \\[4pt]
T_A\sin\alpha + T_B\sin\beta - m g = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Solving the system one finds $T_A = \dfrac{m g \cos\beta}{\sin(\alpha + \beta)}$, therefore the tension of the string $PA$ equals $35.91\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $43.98\,\mathrm{N}$ is the tension of the other string, which is obtained by interchanging the two cosines.
