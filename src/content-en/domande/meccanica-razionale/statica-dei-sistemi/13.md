Two homogeneous rods $AB$ and $BC$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $B$ and hinged to the ground at $A$ and at $C$, in a vertical plane; the points $A$ and $C$ lie at the same height and each rod makes the angle $\vartheta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal, as in the diagram in the figure. All the hinges are smooth and the weights are the only active forces. What is the horizontal component of the reaction of the hinge at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.5,2.6;C,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,cerniera" cerniere="B" forze="0.75 1.3,0,-0.9,mg;2.25 1.3,0,-0.9,mg" etichette="0.5,0.33,ϑ" titolo="Arch formed by two rods hinged to each other at $B$ and to the ground at $A$ and at $C$"}

- $\dfrac{m g}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{12}\,m g$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,m g$

## Explanation

The system is symmetric with respect to the vertical through $B$, therefore the two reactions at the ground have the same vertical component, and the equilibrium of the whole system under vertical translation gives $\Phi_{Ay} = \Phi_{Cy} = m g$. To obtain the horizontal component the system is taken apart and the moment equation of the rod $AB$ with respect to $B$, where the internal reaction acts, is written, with $A - B = -\,\dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\hat{\imath} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\,\hat{\jmath}$:
$$
-\,\frac{\ell}{2}\,m g + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\,\Phi_{Ax} + \frac{\ell}{4}\,m g = 0 .
$$
From it follows $\Phi_{Ax} = \dfrac{m g}{2\tan\vartheta} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,m g$. The value $\dfrac{m g}{2}$ would be obtained by neglecting the inclination of the rod, that is, by setting $\tan\vartheta = 1$.
