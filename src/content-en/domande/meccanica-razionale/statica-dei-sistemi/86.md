A horizontal rod $AB$ of negligible mass and length $\ell$ has a fixed hinge at $A$ and, at $B$, a bilateral roller support on a smooth horizontal guide. A couple of moment $\mathcal{M} > 0$, counterclockwise, is applied to the rod at an arbitrary point $P$ of it. What is the vertical reaction $\Phi_B$ of the roller support, positive upward?

- $\Phi_B = \dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{\ell}$, whatever $P$ is
- $\Phi_B = -\,\dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{AP}$, depending on the position of $P$
- $\Phi_B = 0$, since the couple has zero resultant
- $\Phi_B = -\,\dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{\ell}$, whatever $P$ is

## Explanation

The moment equation with respect to $A$ does not contain the reaction of the hinge. The moment of a couple is the same with respect to every pole, therefore it does not depend on the point where the couple is applied, and the equation is
$$
\ell\,\Phi_B + \mathcal{M} = 0 ,
\qquad\text{whence}\qquad
\Phi_B = -\,\frac{\mathcal{M}}{\ell} .
$$
The roller support therefore pulls the rod downward, and the vertical component of the first cardinal equation gives $\Phi_{Ay} = \dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{\ell}$. The two vertical reactions thus form a clockwise couple of moment $\mathcal{M}$, which balances the applied couple.
