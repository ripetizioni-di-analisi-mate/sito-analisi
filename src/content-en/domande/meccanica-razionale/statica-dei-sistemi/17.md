A particle of mass $m$ is constrained to the smooth fixed curve of equation $y = x^2$, lying in a vertical plane with the $y$ axis vertical and pointing upward, and the weight is the only active force. What are the equilibrium configurations?

- The point of abscissa $x = 0$ alone
- Every point of the curve
- The two points of abscissa $x = \pm 1$
- No point of the curve

## Explanation

The constraint is smooth, therefore the reaction is orthogonal to the curve and the equilibrium configurations are those in which the active force has zero tangential component. A vector tangent to the curve at the point of abscissa $x$ is $\vec{t} = \hat{\imath} + 2 x\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the active force is $-\,m g\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the equilibrium condition is written as
$$
-\,2\,m g\,x = 0 .
$$
The only solution is $x = 0$, therefore the only equilibrium configuration is the vertex of the curve. At that point the tangent is horizontal and the weight is orthogonal to the curve, therefore the constraint balances it by itself.
