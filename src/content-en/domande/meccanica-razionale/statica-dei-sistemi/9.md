A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $4a$ lies horizontally in a vertical plane and rests on two smooth unilateral supports, one at $A$ and one at the point $C$ with $AC = 3a$. A particle of mass $m_1$ hangs from the end $B$ by a vertical string, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the reaction of the support at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4.5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;C,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-1.1,mg;B,0,-1.3,m_1g" quote="A,C,3a,1.3;C,B,a,1.3" titolo="Rod resting at $A$ and at $C$, with the weight $m_1 g$ hanging at $B$"}

- $\dfrac{g\,(m + m_1)}{3}$
- $\dfrac{g\,(m - m_1)}{4}$
- $\dfrac{g\,(m - 2\,m_1)}{3}$
- $\dfrac{g\,(m - m_1)}{3}$

## Explanation

The rod is a rigid body and the two smooth supports exert vertical reactions, therefore it is convenient to write the moment equation with respect to $C$, through which the line of action of the reaction of the support at $C$ passes. With respect to $C$ the support at $A$ has moment arm $3a$, the weight of the rod, applied at the midpoint, has moment arm $a$ and the weight hanging at $B$ has moment arm $a$ on the opposite side, therefore
$$
-\,3a\,\Phi_A + a\,m g - a\,m_1 g = 0 .
$$
Therefore $\Phi_A = \dfrac{g\,(m - m_1)}{3}$, directed upward, and it vanishes for $m_1 = m$, a value beyond which the rod lifts off at $A$. The form with the sum $m + m_1$ would be obtained by assigning to the hanging weight the same direction of rotation as the weight of the rod, which instead lies on the other side of $C$.
