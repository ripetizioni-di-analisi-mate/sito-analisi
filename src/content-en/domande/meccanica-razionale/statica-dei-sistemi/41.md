Two rods $AB$ and $CB$ of negligible mass and equal length are connected by the internal hinge $B$ and hinged to the ground at $A$ and at $C$, at the same height, in a vertical plane; each rod forms the angle $\vartheta$ with the horizontal. At the hinge $B$ the vertical load $P$ directed downward is applied, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the axial force in the rod $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,1.5;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,cerniera" cerniere="B" forze="B,0,-1.1,P" etichette="0.6,0.17,ϑ" titolo="Two unloaded rods hinged at $B$, with the load $P$ applied at the hinge"}

- $\dfrac{P}{2\cos\vartheta}$, compressive
- $\dfrac{P}{2}$, compressive
- $\dfrac{P}{2\sin\vartheta}$, compressive
- $\dfrac{P}{2\sin\vartheta}$, tensile

## Explanation

On each rod, of negligible mass, only the forces of the two hinges at the ends act, and rotational equilibrium requires these two forces to have the same line of action, that is, the line of the rod. The two rods therefore exert on the pin $B$ forces directed along $AB$ and along $CB$, and by the symmetry of the structure and of the load they have the same magnitude $N$. Translational equilibrium of the pin in the vertical direction gives
$$
2\,N\sin\vartheta - P = 0 ,
$$
therefore $N = \dfrac{P}{2\sin\vartheta}$. The rods push the pin upward, hence they are **compressed**. The value $\dfrac{P}{2}$ is obtained by neglecting the inclination of the rods, and $\dfrac{P}{2\cos\vartheta}$ by projecting the axial force on the horizontal direction instead of the vertical one.
