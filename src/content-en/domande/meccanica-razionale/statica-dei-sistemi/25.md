A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ lies horizontally in a vertical plane, with a fixed hinge at $A$ and a roller support on a smooth horizontal guide at $B$. At the point $P$ with $AP = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$ the vertical force $\vec{F}$, directed downward, acts, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the reaction of the roller support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="1.5,0,P" forze="3 0,0,-1.1,mg;1.5 0,0,-1.1,F" quote="A,B,ℓ,1.4" titolo="Horizontal rod with a hinge at $A$, a roller support at $B$ and the force $\vec{F}$ applied at $P$"}

- $\dfrac{m g}{2} - \dfrac{F}{4}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2} + \dfrac{F}{2}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2} + \dfrac{3 F}{4}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2} + \dfrac{F}{4}$

## Explanation

The rod is a rigid body, therefore the three cardinal equations determine its three reaction components, and the pole is taken at $A$, through which the line of action of the reaction of the hinge passes. The roller support slides on a horizontal guide, therefore its reaction is vertical and has moment arm $\ell$, whereas the weight has moment arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ and the force $\vec{F}$ has moment arm $\dfrac{\ell}{4}$, and the moment equation is
$$
\ell\,\Phi_B - \frac{\ell}{2}\,m g - \frac{\ell}{4}\,F = 0 .
$$
Therefore $\Phi_B = \dfrac{m g}{2} + \dfrac{F}{4}$, directed upward. The term $\dfrac{3 F}{4}$ corresponds to the distance of $P$ from the roller support, which is the moment arm of the force with respect to $B$ and not with respect to $A$.
