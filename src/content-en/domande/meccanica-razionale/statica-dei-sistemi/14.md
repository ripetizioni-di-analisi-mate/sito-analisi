A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in a vertical plane. A particle of mass $m_1$ is welded on the rim of the disk and at the highest point $H$ of the disk the horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$, with $F > 0$, is applied, as in the diagram in the figure. Denoting by $\varphi$ the angle, measured in the clockwise direction, through which the disk has rotated starting from the configuration in which the welded particle touches the guide, which condition identifies the equilibrium configurations?

::struttura{linee="-0.6,0 8.2,0" cerchi="4,1.5,1.5" tratteggi="4,1.5 3.14,0.27;4,1.5 4,0" punti="4,1.5,O,baricentro;4,0,C,centro;3.14,0.27,P;4,3,H" forze="4 3,1.2,0,F;4 1.5,0,-0.85,mg;3.14 0.27,0,-0.8,m_1g" etichette="3.85,0.72,φ" titolo="Disk rolling without slipping with the point $P$ welded on the rim and the force $\vec{F}$ applied at the highest point $H$"}

- $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{F}{m_1 g}$
- $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{2 F}{m_1 g}$
- $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{F}{2\,m_1 g}$
- $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{2 F}{(m + m_1)\,g}$

## Explanation

The disk with the welded particle is a rigid body with one degree of freedom and at the contact point $C$ the normal reaction and the friction component act, therefore the moment equation with respect to $C$ does not contain unknown reactions. The weight of the disk is applied at the center $O$, which lies on the vertical through $C$, therefore its moment is zero; the welded particle is located at $P - C = -\,R\sin\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + R\,(1 - \cos\varphi)\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the force $\vec{F}$ acts at $H$, at height $2R$ above $C$. The moment equation is then
$$
m_1 g R \sin\varphi - 2 R F = 0 .
$$
Therefore $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{2 F}{m_1 g}$, and the equilibrium configurations exist only if $2 F \le m_1 g$. The moment arm of the force equals $2R$, since $\vec{F}$ is applied at the point diametrically opposite the contact, and the value $\dfrac{F}{m_1 g}$ corresponds to the moment arm $R$.
