Two equal homogeneous cylinders, of mass $m$ and radius $R$, are placed with their axes horizontal in a box with vertical walls and horizontal bottom, all smooth. The lower cylinder touches the bottom and the left wall; the upper one rests on the lower cylinder and touches the right wall, and the line of the centers makes the angle $60^\circ$ with the horizontal, as in the cross-section in the figure. What is the magnitude of the reaction of the right wall?

::struttura{cerchi="1,1,1;2.00,2.73,1" linee="0,4.6 0,0 3,0 3,4.6" punti="1,1,,baricentro;2.00,2.73,,baricentro" tratteggi="1,1 2.00,2.73;1,1 2.3,1" forze="1 1,0,-0.8,mg;2.00 2.73,0,-0.8,mg" etichette="1.75,1.22,60°" titolo="Cross-section of the two stacked cylinders in the box: the line of the centers makes $60^\circ$ with the horizontal"}

- $\sqrt{3}\,m g$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g$

## Explanation

The system is taken apart and the upper cylinder is considered, subject to the weight, to the horizontal reaction $\Phi_D$ of the right wall and to the contact force $\vec{N}$ of the lower cylinder. The surfaces are smooth, therefore $\vec{N}$ is directed along the line of the centers, inclined at $60^\circ$, and the three forces pass through the center of the upper cylinder. The two components of the first cardinal equation give
$$
\begin{cases}
N\cos 60^\circ - \Phi_D = 0 \\[4pt]
N\sin 60^\circ - m g = 0
\end{cases}
$$
hence $N = \dfrac{2\,m g}{\sqrt{3}}$ and $\Phi_D = \dfrac{m g}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g$. The value $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g$ is the magnitude of the contact force between the two cylinders, and $\sqrt{3}\,m g$ is obtained by exchanging sine and cosine.
