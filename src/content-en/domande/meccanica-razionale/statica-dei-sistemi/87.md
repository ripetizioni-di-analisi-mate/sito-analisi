An ideal pulley of center $O$ can rotate about a fixed horizontal axis through $O$. An ideal string passes over the pulley; one branch is horizontal and fixed to a vertical wall, the other goes down vertically and supports a particle of mass $m$, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the magnitude of the reaction that the axis exerts on the pulley?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;W,-4,1,-;T,0,1,-;S,1,0,-;Q,1,-2.6,-" cerchi="0,0,1" vincoli="O,cerniera" catene="T-W;S-Q" linee="-4,-0.6 -4,2.2" punti="1,-2.6" forze="Q,0,-0.9,mg" titolo="Pulley with a fixed axis at $O$: one branch of the string is fixed to the wall, the other supports the weight $m g$"}

- $2\,m g$
- $m g$
- $\sqrt{2}\,m g$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,m g$

## Explanation

The pulley is ideal, therefore the tension has the same value in the two branches, and the equilibrium of the hanging particle gives $T = m g$. The system formed by the pulley and the portion of string wound on it is acted on by the two tensions, one horizontal toward the wall and one vertical downward, and by the reaction of the axis. The first cardinal equation gives
$$
\vec{\Phi}_O - T\,\hat{\imath} - T\,\hat{\jmath} = \vec{0} ,
\qquad\text{therefore}\qquad
\vec{\Phi}_O = m g\,\hat{\imath} + m g\,\hat{\jmath} ,
$$
of magnitude $\sqrt{2}\,m g$ and inclined at $45^\circ$. The value $2\,m g$ is obtained by adding the magnitudes of the two tensions as if the branches were parallel.
