A plane rigid rod is constrained at $A$ by a clamped support. Which reactions does the constraint exert?

- A force of two components
- A force of two components and a couple
- A force orthogonal to the wall and a couple
- A couple alone

## Explanation

The clamped support prevents both translations of the point $A$ and also prevents the rotation of the rod, therefore it exerts one reaction for each prevented movement. To the two prevented translations there correspond the two components $\Phi_{Ax}$ and $\Phi_{Ay}$ of a force applied at $A$, and to the prevented rotation there corresponds a couple of moment $\Gamma_A$, therefore the reaction unknowns are three. A plane rigid body built in at one point then has zero residual degrees of freedom and three reaction components, and the three cardinal equations determine them.
