A ring of mass $m$, which can be modeled as a particle, is threaded on a smooth fixed circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, lying in a vertical plane. An ideal spring of constant $k$ and natural length $\ell_0$ connects the ring to the center $O$. What are the equilibrium configurations?

- Only the lowest point of the guide
- The lowest point and the highest point of the guide
- Every point of the guide
- The two points of the guide at the height of $O$

## Explanation

The ring is constrained to a smooth curve, therefore it is in equilibrium if and only if the tangential component of the active force is zero. The spring connects the ring to the center, hence the elastic force, of magnitude $k\,|R - \ell_0|$, is directed along the radius and is orthogonal to the guide at every point. The equilibrium condition then contains only the weight, whose tangential component is $-\,m g\sin\vartheta$, with $\vartheta$ measured from the downward vertical, and it vanishes for $\vartheta = 0$ and $\vartheta = \pi$. The constraint is bilateral, therefore **both** configurations are equilibrium configurations; the spring changes only the reaction of the guide.
