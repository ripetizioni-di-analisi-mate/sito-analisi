- $1.70\,\mathrm{kg} \le m_2 \le 4.30\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{kg} \le m_2 \le 4.50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{kg} \le m_2 \le 3.75\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.70\,\mathrm{kg} \le m_2 \le 3.00\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The particle $P_2$ hangs vertically, therefore its equilibrium gives $T = m_2 g$, and the pulley is ideal, therefore the same tension acts on $P_1$ along the inclined plane. For $P_1$ equilibrium along the plane requires that the friction component equal $m_1 g \sin\alpha - m_2 g$ and equilibrium in the normal direction gives $\Phi_n = m_1 g \cos\alpha$, therefore the Coulomb-Morin law is written as
$$
|m_1 g \sin\alpha - m_2 g| \le f\,m_1 g \cos\alpha .
$$
From it follows $m_1\,(\sin\alpha - f\cos\alpha) \le m_2 \le m_1\,(\sin\alpha + f\cos\alpha)$, that is, the interval between $1.70\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $4.30\,\mathrm{kg}$. The interval between $1.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $4.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ is obtained by multiplying the coefficient of friction by the whole weight instead of by its normal component.
