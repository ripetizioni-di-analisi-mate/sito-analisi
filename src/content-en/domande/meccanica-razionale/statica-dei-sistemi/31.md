A rigid rod is constrained in a plane by a fixed hinge at $A$ and by a roller support on a smooth guide at $B$. How many residual degrees of freedom does the rod have and how many unknown reaction components does the problem present?

- No residual degree of freedom and three unknown reaction components
- One residual degree of freedom and two unknown reaction components
- No residual degree of freedom and four unknown reaction components
- Two residual degrees of freedom and one unknown reaction component

## Explanation

A free plane rigid body has three degrees of freedom, the fixed hinge prevents both translations of the point $A$ and the roller support prevents the translation orthogonal to the guide, therefore the residual degrees of freedom are zero and the configuration is assigned. The hinge exerts a force of two components and the roller support a force orthogonal to the guide, therefore the unknown reaction components are three. The count $3 = n + r$ is satisfied with $n = 0$ and $r = 3$, therefore the three cardinal equations determine the three reactions and the problem is well posed.
