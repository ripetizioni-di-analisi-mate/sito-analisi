A particle of mass $m = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a smooth inclined plane, which makes the angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ with the horizontal, and is held by an ideal string parallel to the plane and fixed to the upper vertex, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the tension of the string?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,2.31 4,0 0,0" nodi="K,3.9,2.48,-;P,2.15,1.47,-" catene="P-K" punti="2.15,1.47,P" forze="2.15 1.47,0,-1,mg" etichette="0.95,0.2,α" titolo="Particle resting on the smooth inclined plane and held by the string parallel to the plane"}

- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16.99\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19.62\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11.33\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The plane is smooth, therefore the constraint reaction is orthogonal to the plane and equilibrium along the plane involves only the tension of the string and the component of the weight parallel to the plane. That component equals $m g \sin\alpha$ and is directed downward, therefore the equilibrium condition along the plane is written as
$$
T - m g \sin\alpha = 0 .
$$
Therefore $T = m g \sin\alpha = 9.81\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $16.99\,\mathrm{N}$ is the component of the weight orthogonal to the plane, that is, the magnitude of the constraint reaction, and not the tension of the string.
