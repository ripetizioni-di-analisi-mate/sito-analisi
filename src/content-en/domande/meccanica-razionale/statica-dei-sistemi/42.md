Two plane rigid bodies are connected by a smooth internal hinge at the point $B$ and can rotate with respect to each other. What do the two bodies transmit to each other through the hinge?

- A couple and no force
- A force of two components and a couple
- A force directed along the line joining the two free ends
- A force of two components, without a couple

## Explanation

The internal hinge prevents the relative translation of the two bodies at the point $B$ and allows the relative rotation, therefore it transmits one reaction for each prevented movement and no reaction for the allowed movement. To the two prevented translational movements there correspond the two components $X_B$ and $Y_B$ of a force applied at $B$, whereas to the allowed relative rotation there corresponds no couple. The body that lies on one side therefore receives the force of components $X_B$ and $Y_B$ and the other the opposite force, applied at the same point, as the principle of action and reaction requires.
