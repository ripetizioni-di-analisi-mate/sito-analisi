A rigid rod $AB$ lies in a vertical plane with the end $A$ constrained by a roller support sliding on a smooth vertical guide and the end $B$ by a roller support sliding on a smooth horizontal guide; the two guides meet at the point $O$. With respect to which pole does the moment equation contain neither of the two constraint reactions?

- The intersection of the horizontal line through $A$ with the vertical line through $B$
- The point $O$ where the two guides meet
- The midpoint of the rod
- The end $A$

## Explanation

A reaction has zero moment with respect to every pole lying on its line of action. The roller support on the smooth vertical guide exerts at $A$ a horizontal reaction, whose line of action is the horizontal through $A$; the roller support on the smooth horizontal guide exerts at $B$ a vertical reaction, whose line of action is the vertical through $B$. The intersection of the two lines belongs to both, therefore with respect to it the moment equation contains only the active forces and is the equation of the equilibrium configuration. The point $O$ lies on the two guides, while the lines of action of the reactions are orthogonal to the guides and pass through $A$ and through $B$.
