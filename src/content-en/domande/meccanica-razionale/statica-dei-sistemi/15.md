A particle of mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is constrained to a smooth fixed straight guide, lying in a vertical plane and inclined at $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal. The weight and a horizontal force $\vec{F}$, directed toward the upper part of the guide, are applied to the particle, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the magnitude of $\vec{F}$ at equilibrium?

::struttura{linee="0,0 4,2.31" tratteggi="0,0 1.9,0" punti="2.6,1.5,P" forze="2.6 1.5,0,-1,mg;2.6 1.5,0.9,0,F" etichette="1.15,0.2,α" titolo="Particle on a smooth straight guide inclined at $\alpha$, subject to the weight and to the horizontal force $\vec{F}$"}

- $19.62\,\mathrm{N}$
- $33.98\,\mathrm{N}$
- $22.66\,\mathrm{N}$
- $39.24\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The guide is smooth, therefore the constraint reaction is orthogonal to the guide and equilibrium requires that the tangential component of the active forces vanish. The unit tangent vector to the guide, oriented upward, equals $\hat{t} = \cos\alpha\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\alpha\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the horizontal force has tangential component $F\cos\alpha$ and the weight $-\,m g \sin\alpha$, and the equilibrium condition is
$$
F\cos\alpha - m g \sin\alpha = 0 .
$$
From it follows $F = m g \tan\alpha$, therefore with the assigned values the magnitude equals $22.66\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $19.62\,\mathrm{N}$ is the component of the weight along the guide, which balances a force parallel to the guide and not a horizontal force.
