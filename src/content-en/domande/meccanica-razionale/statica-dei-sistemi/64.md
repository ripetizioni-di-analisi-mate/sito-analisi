A plane rigid body of negligible mass has the shape of a square $ABC$, with the horizontal part $AB$ of length $b$ and the vertical part $BC$ of length $h$, pointing upward. The body is constrained by a fixed hinge at $A$ and by a roller support on a smooth horizontal guide at $B$, and at the end $C$ the horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$, with $F > 0$, acts, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the vertical component $\Phi_{Ay}$ of the reaction of the hinge, with $\hat{\jmath}$ upward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,3,2" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.2,0,F" quote="A,B,b,1.4;B,C,h,0.6" titolo="Square with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, loaded by the horizontal force $\vec{F}$ at the end $C$"}

- $-\,\dfrac{F h}{b}$
- $\dfrac{F h}{b}$
- $-\,\dfrac{F b}{h}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The unknowns are $\Phi_{Ax}$, $\Phi_{Ay}$ and the vertical reaction $\Phi_B$ of the roller support, and the body has no residual degrees of freedom. The moment equation with respect to $A$ contains only $\Phi_B$ and $\vec{F}$; with $B - A = b\,\hat{\imath}$ and $C - A = b\,\hat{\imath} + h\,\hat{\jmath}$ one has
$$
b\,\Phi_B - h\,F = 0 ,
\qquad\text{therefore}\qquad
\Phi_B = \frac{F h}{b} .
$$
The vertical component of the first cardinal equation gives $\Phi_{Ay} + \Phi_B = 0$, hence $\Phi_{Ay} = -\,\dfrac{F h}{b}$, and the hinge pulls the body **downward**. The two vertical reactions, opposite and at distance $b$, form the couple that balances the moment $F h$ of the force; finally, the horizontal component gives $\Phi_{Ax} = -\,F$.
