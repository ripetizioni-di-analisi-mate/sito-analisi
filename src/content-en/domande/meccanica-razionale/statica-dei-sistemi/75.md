Two homogeneous rods $AB$ and $BC$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $B$; the end $A$ is hinged to a fixed point and the end $C$ is free, in a vertical plane. At $C$ the horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$, with $F > 0$, acts. Denoting by $\varphi_1$ and $\varphi_2$ the angles that $AB$ and $BC$ make with the downward vertical, as in the diagram in the figure, which condition identifies the inclination of the rod $AB$ at equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.88,-3.53;C,5.03,-5.99" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-3.2;1.88,-3.53 1.88,-6.53" forze="C,1.1,0,F;0.94 -1.77,0,-1,mg;3.45 -4.76,0,-1,mg" etichette="0.3,-1.4,φ_1;2.33,-4.83,φ_2" titolo="Two rods hinged at $B$, hanging from $A$, with the horizontal force $\vec{F}$ at the free end $C$"}

- $\tan\varphi_1 = \dfrac{2F}{3\,m g}$
- $\tan\varphi_1 = \dfrac{2F}{m g}$
- $\tan\varphi_1 = \dfrac{F}{m g}$
- $\tan\varphi_1 = \dfrac{F}{3\,m g}$

## Explanation

The system has two degrees of freedom, and the equations of the configurations are obtained from the moments with respect to the two hinges. For the rod $BC$ the moments with respect to $B$ give $F\,\ell\cos\varphi_2 = m g\,\dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\varphi_2$, therefore $\tan\varphi_2 = \dfrac{2F}{m g}$. For the whole system the moments with respect to $A$ give
$$
F\,\ell\,(\cos\varphi_1 + \cos\varphi_2) = m g\,\frac{\ell}{2}\sin\varphi_1 + m g\,\ell\left(\sin\varphi_1 + \frac{1}{2}\sin\varphi_2\right) .
$$
Subtracting the first relation one obtains $F\,\ell\cos\varphi_1 = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m g\,\ell\sin\varphi_1$, hence $\tan\varphi_1 = \dfrac{2F}{3\,m g}$. The factor $3$ appears because the rod $AB$ is acted on by its own weight, at the midpoint, and by the weight of the rod $BC$, transmitted at $B$. The value $\dfrac{2F}{m g}$ is the inclination of the lower rod.
