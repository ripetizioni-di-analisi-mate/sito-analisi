A mass on a smooth horizontal track is attached to a wall by an ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and natural length $\ell_0$. An inextensible string from the same wall limits the distance to $L>\ell_0$. An outward force $F$ pulls the mass. Under what condition does equilibrium require positive string tension?

- $F<k(L-\ell_0)$
- $F>k(L-\ell_0)$
- $F=k\ell_0$ for every $L$
- Any $F>0$

## Explanation

Before the string becomes taut, the spring can supply up to $k(L-\ell_0)$. At distance $L$ the string tension is $F-k(L-\ell_0)$, positive only above the threshold.
