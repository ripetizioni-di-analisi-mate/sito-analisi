A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m = 6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at $A$ to a vertical wall, in a vertical plane, and makes the angle $\alpha = 45^\circ$ with the horizontal. The end $B$ is connected to the wall by a horizontal ideal string, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the tension of the string?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.54,3.54;C,0,3.54" aste="A-B" catene="B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180" linee="0,-0.7 0,4.3" tratteggi="0,0 1.7,0" forze="1.77 1.77,0,-1.1,mg" etichette="0.82,0.26,α" titolo="Rod hinged to the wall at $A$ and held by the horizontal string $BC$"}

- $29.43\,\mathrm{N}$
- $41.62\,\mathrm{N}$
- $58.86\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20.81\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The rod is a rigid body, therefore the cardinal equations suffice to solve the problem, and the pole is taken at $A$, through which the line of action of the reaction of the hinge passes. The string is horizontal and pulls $B$ toward the wall, therefore its moment with respect to $A$ has moment arm $\ell\sin\alpha$, whereas the weight, applied at the midpoint, has moment arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\alpha$; the moment equation is then
$$
T\,\ell\sin\alpha - \frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\alpha = 0 .
$$
From it follows $T = \dfrac{m g}{2\tan\alpha}$, therefore with the assigned values the tension equals $29.43\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $58.86\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by forgetting that the weight acts at the midpoint of the rod and not at the end $B$.
