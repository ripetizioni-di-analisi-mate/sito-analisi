A particle $P$ of mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ is constrained to a smooth fixed straight guide, lying in a vertical plane and inclined at $\alpha = 45^\circ$ to the horizontal. An ideal spring of constant $k = 150\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and zero natural length connects $P$ to the upper end $O$ of the guide, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the distance $s$ of $P$ from $O$ at equilibrium?

::struttura{linee="0,3.2 3.2,0;0.00,3.20 0.28,2.92 0.30,2.61 0.75,2.73 0.64,2.28 1.09,2.40 0.97,1.95 1.28,1.92 1.56,1.64" tratteggi="2.0,0 3.2,0" punti="0,3.2,O;1.56,1.64,P" forze="1.56 1.64,0,-1,mg" quote="0 3.2,1.56 1.64,s,-1.1" etichette="0.95,2.6,k;2.75,0.17,α" titolo="Particle on a smooth straight guide inclined at $\alpha$, connected to $O$ by the spring"}

- $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.28\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The guide is smooth, therefore the constraint reaction is orthogonal to the guide and equilibrium requires that the tangential component of the active forces vanish. Orienting the guide downward, the weight has tangential component $m g \sin\alpha$ and the elastic force, which has zero natural length, has tangential component $-\,k\,s$, therefore the equilibrium condition is
$$
m g \sin\alpha - k\,s = 0 .
$$
From it follows $s = \dfrac{m g \sin\alpha}{k}$, and with the assigned values the equilibrium distance equals $0.14\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by balancing with the spring the whole weight instead of its component along the guide alone.
