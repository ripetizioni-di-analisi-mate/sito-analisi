Consider the system formed by two rods hinged to each other at $B$, and take as internal forces the actions that the two rods exchange in the hinge. What can be said of the resultant and of the resultant moment of the internal forces?

- The resultant is zero and the resultant moment in general is not
- The resultant and the resultant moment are zero with respect to every pole
- The resultant and the resultant moment are zero only with respect to the center of mass
- The resultant moment is zero and the resultant in general is not

## Explanation

By the principle of action and reaction the two actions that the rods exchange at $B$ are opposite, therefore their sum is zero and the resultant of the internal forces equals $\vec{0}$. The two forces are moreover applied at the same point $B$, therefore they have the same line of action and their moments with respect to any pole $O$ are opposite, so that $\vec{M}_O^{(i)} = \vec{0}$ as well. The result holds for every material system and for every pole, and for this reason the cardinal equations of the whole system contain only the external forces. The actions in the hinge reappear when the cardinal equations of one of the two rods alone are written, for which they are external.
