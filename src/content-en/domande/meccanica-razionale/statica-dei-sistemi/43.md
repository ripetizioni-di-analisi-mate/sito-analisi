A homogeneous rod $OA$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at $O$ to a fixed point, in a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and a couple of moment $\mathcal{M} > 0$ in the counterclockwise direction is applied to it. For which values of $\mathcal{M}$ do equilibrium configurations exist?

- $\mathcal{M} \le \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$
- $\mathcal{M} \le \dfrac{m g \ell}{4}$
- $\mathcal{M} \le m g \ell$
- $\mathcal{M} \le 2\,m g \ell$

## Explanation

The rod is a rigid body with one degree of freedom and the moment equation with respect to $O$ does not contain the reaction of the hinge. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that $OA$ makes with the downward vertical, the weight acts at the midpoint with moment arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta$ and the couple enters with its own moment, therefore
$$
\mathcal{M} - \frac{m g \ell}{2}\sin\vartheta = 0 .
$$
From it follows $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{2\,\mathcal{M}}{m g \ell}$, an equation solvable if and only if the right-hand side does not exceed $1$, that is, if $\mathcal{M} \le \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$ is the largest moment that the weight of the rod can balance, and it is attained in the horizontal configuration.
