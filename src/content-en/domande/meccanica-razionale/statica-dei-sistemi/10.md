A particle $P$ of mass $m$ rests on the concave side of a smooth circular guide of radius $R$ and center $O$, lying in a vertical plane and limited to the arc $|\vartheta| \le \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, where $\vartheta$ is the angle measured from the downward vertical through $O$ in the counterclockwise direction. The constraint is unilateral and can only push $P$ toward the center. The horizontal force $\vec{F}_1 = F_1\,\hat{\imath}$ and the vertical force $\vec{F}_2 = F_2\,\hat{\jmath}$ are applied to the particle, as in the diagram in the figure. Which condition identifies the equilibrium configuration?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="2*sin(t),-2*cos(t),-0.785,0.785" segmenti="0,0,0,-2;0,0,1,-1.73;0,0,1.41,-1.41" vettori="1,-1.73,1,-2.83,mg;1,-1.73,2.2,-1.73,F₁;1,-1.73,1,-0.63,F₂" punti="1,-1.73,pieno,P;0,0,pieno,O" etichette="0.28,-1.3,ϑ;0.85,-0.3,45°" xmin=-2.4 xmax=2.9 ymin=-3.2 ymax=0.8 titolo="Particle resting on the concave side of a circular guide limited to the arc $|\vartheta| \le \dfrac{\pi}{4}$"}

- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{F_1}{m g - F_2}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{F_1}{m g + F_2}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{F_1}{m g}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g - F_2}{F_1}$

## Explanation

The guide is smooth, therefore the reaction is orthogonal to the guide and equilibrium requires that the tangential component of the active forces vanish. The resultant of the active forces equals $F_1\,\hat{\imath} + (F_2 - m g)\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the unit tangent vector, oriented in the direction of increasing $\vartheta$, equals $\hat{t} = \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the equilibrium condition is written as
$$
F_1\cos\vartheta + (F_2 - m g)\sin\vartheta = 0 .
$$
Dividing by $\cos\vartheta$, which is positive on the arc considered, one obtains $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{F_1}{m g - F_2}$. The force $\vec{F}_2$ is directed upward, therefore the difference $m g - F_2$ appears in the denominator and not the sum.
