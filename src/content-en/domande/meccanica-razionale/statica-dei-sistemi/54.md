A homogeneous crate shaped as a rectangular box, of mass $m = 20\,\mathrm{kg}$, has the base side $b = 0.4\,\mathrm{m}$ in the plane of the drawing and the height $h = 1\,\mathrm{m}$, and rests on a horizontal floor. A horizontal force $\vec{F}$ directed to the right acts on the upper left edge, as in the diagram in the figure, and the friction of the floor is sufficient to prevent sliding. Beyond what magnitude of $\vec{F}$ does the crate tip over?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 1.2,0 1.2,3 0,3" linee="-1.6,0 2.6,0" forze="0 3,1.1,0,F;0.6 1.5,0,-1,mg" quote="0 0,1.2 0,b,0.5;1.2 0,1.2 3,h,0.5" titolo="Crate of base $b$ and height $h$ pushed by the horizontal force $\vec{F}$ on the upper edge"}

- $39.24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78.48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $245.25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19.62\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The floor is a unilateral constraint, and its reactions on the base have a resultant applied at a point of the base. As $F$ increases this point moves toward the lower right edge $S$, and at the tipping limit it coincides with $S$. The moment equation with respect to $S$ then does not contain the reactions of the floor, therefore the limit condition is
$$
F\,h = m g\,\frac{b}{2} ,
\qquad\text{that is}\qquad
F = \frac{m g\,b}{2h} = \frac{20 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.4}{2 \cdot 1}\,\mathrm{N} = 39.24\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
For larger values the reaction would have to fall outside the base, therefore the crate rotates about $S$. The value $78.48\,\mathrm{N}$ corresponds to the force applied at mid-height, with lever arm $\dfrac{h}{2}$.
