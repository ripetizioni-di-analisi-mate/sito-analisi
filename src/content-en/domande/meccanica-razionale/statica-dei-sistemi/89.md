A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ lies in a vertical plane with the lower end $A$ resting against a smooth vertical wall, and rests on a smooth peg $P$ placed at horizontal distance $a$ from the wall, with $2a < \ell$. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod makes with the horizontal, as in the diagram in the figure, which condition identifies the equilibrium configuration?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4.91,3.44" aste="A-B" linee="0,-0.8 0,4.2" cerchi="1.87,1.16,0.12" punti="1.80,1.26,P" tratteggi="0,0 1.5,0;1.80,1.01 1.80,-0.55" forze="2.46 1.72,0,-1,mg" quote="0 -0.55,1.80 -0.55,a,0.45" etichette="1.0,0.27,ϑ" titolo="Rod resting against the smooth wall at $A$ and on the smooth peg $P$, at distance $a$ from the wall"}

- $\cos^3\vartheta = \dfrac{2a}{\ell}$
- $\cos^3\vartheta = \dfrac{a}{\ell}$
- $\cos^2\vartheta = \dfrac{2a}{\ell}$
- $\cos\vartheta = \dfrac{2a}{\ell}$

## Explanation

The forces acting on the rod are the weight, the horizontal reaction $\Phi_A$ of the wall and the reaction $\Phi_P$ of the peg, orthogonal to the rod because the peg is smooth. The distance of $P$ from $A$ measured along the rod is $\dfrac{a}{\cos\vartheta}$. The vertical component of the first cardinal equation contains only the weight and the vertical component $\Phi_P\cos\vartheta$ of the reaction of the peg, therefore $\Phi_P = \dfrac{m g}{\cos\vartheta}$. The moment equation with respect to $A$ does not contain $\Phi_A$ and gives
$$
\Phi_P\,\frac{a}{\cos\vartheta} = m g\,\frac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta ,
\qquad\text{that is}\qquad
\cos^3\vartheta = \frac{2a}{\ell} .
$$
The condition $\cos^2\vartheta = \dfrac{2a}{\ell}$ is obtained by taking $a$ as the distance of $P$ from $A$ measured along the rod.
