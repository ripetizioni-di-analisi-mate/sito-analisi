A particle of mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a smooth horizontal plane, which can only push. A force $\vec{F}$ that makes the angle $\beta = 30^\circ$ with the plane and is directed upward is applied to the particle. Beyond which magnitude of $\vec{F}$ does the particle detach from the plane?

- $24.53\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49.05\,\mathrm{N}$
- $56.64\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The support is unilateral and smooth, therefore it exerts a normal reaction $\Phi\,\hat{\jmath}$ with $\Phi \ge 0$, and the detachment occurs when the reaction required by equilibrium turns out to be negative. Equilibrium in the vertical direction is written as
$$
\Phi + F\sin\beta - m g = 0 ,
$$
therefore $\Phi = m g - F\sin\beta$, and that value is negative for $F > \dfrac{m g}{\sin\beta}$. With the assigned data the particle detaches for $F > 98.10\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $49.05\,\mathrm{N}$ is the weight of the particle, which would suffice only if the force were vertical.
