A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $6a$ lies horizontally in a vertical plane and rests on two smooth unilateral supports, one at $A$ and one at the point $C$ with $AC = 4a$. At the end $B$ the vertical force $\vec{F}$, directed downward, acts, as in the diagram in the figure. For which values of $F$ does the rod lift off at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;C,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-1.1,mg;B,0,-1.1,F" quote="A,C,4a,1.3;C,B,2a,1.3" titolo="Rod resting at $A$ and at $C$, with the force $\vec{F}$ applied at the end $B$"}

- $F > \dfrac{m g}{4}$
- $F > m g$
- $F > \dfrac{m g}{2}$
- $F > 2\,m g$

## Explanation

The supports are smooth and unilateral, therefore they exert vertical reactions with $\Phi_A \ge 0$ and $\Phi_C \ge 0$, and the rod lifts off at $A$ when the reaction required at that point turns out to be negative. The moment equation with respect to $C$ does not contain $\Phi_C$ and, with the weight applied at the midpoint at distance $a$ from $C$ and the force $\vec{F}$ at distance $2a$ on the opposite side, it gives
$$
-\,4a\,\Phi_A + a\,m g - 2a\,F = 0 .
$$
From it follows $\Phi_A = \dfrac{m g - 2 F}{4}$, therefore the reaction is negative for $F > \dfrac{m g}{2}$ and the detachment at $A$ occurs beyond that value. The limiting value $m g$ would be obtained by assigning to the force the same moment arm as the weight.
