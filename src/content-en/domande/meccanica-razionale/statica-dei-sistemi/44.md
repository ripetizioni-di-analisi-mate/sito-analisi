A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rests in a vertical plane on a rough plane inclined at the angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. At the point $D$ of the rim diametrically opposite the contact point $C$ a force $\vec{F}$ parallel to the inclined plane and directed upward is applied, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the magnitude of $\vec{F}$ at equilibrium?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 7,2.55 7,0 0,0" cerchi="3.09,2.4,1.2" punti="3.09,2.4,O,baricentro;3.5,1.27,C,centro;2.68,3.53,D" forze="2.68 3.53,1.05,0.38,F;3.09 2.4,0,-1.2,mg" etichette="1,0.2,α" titolo="Disk resting on a rough inclined plane, with the force $\vec{F}$ parallel to the plane applied at $D$"}

- $m g \sin\alpha$
- $\dfrac{m g \cos\alpha}{2}$
- $\dfrac{m g \sin\alpha}{2}$
- $2\,m g \sin\alpha$

## Explanation

At the contact point $C$ the normal reaction and the friction component act, therefore the moment equation with respect to $C$ does not contain unknown reactions. The weight is applied at the center $O$, which lies at distance $R$ from $C$ along the normal to the plane, and its component parallel to the plane, of magnitude $m g \sin\alpha$, therefore has moment arm $R$; the force $\vec{F}$ is parallel to the plane and applied at $D$, at distance $2R$ from $C$ along the same normal, therefore
$$
m g R \sin\alpha - 2 R F = 0 .
$$
From it follows $F = \dfrac{m g \sin\alpha}{2}$. The component of the weight orthogonal to the plane has its line of action passing through $C$, therefore it does not enter the moment equation.
