A particle $P$ is constrained to a smooth fixed horizontal straight guide, lying in a vertical plane, and is connected by an ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length to the fixed point $D$, which lies at distance $h$ above the guide. What are the equilibrium configurations?

- The point of the guide lying on the vertical through $D$ alone
- Every point of the guide
- The two points of the guide at distance $h$ from the vertical through $D$
- No point of the guide

## Explanation

The guide is smooth and horizontal, therefore the constraint reaction is vertical and balances the weight, and equilibrium requires that the horizontal component of the active forces vanish. Taking the origin at the foot of the perpendicular from $D$ to the guide and denoting by $x$ the abscissa of $P$, the spring of zero natural length exerts on $P$ the force $-\,k\,(P - D)$, whose component along the guide equals $-\,k\,x$, therefore the equilibrium condition is written as
$$
-\,k\,x = 0 .
$$
The only solution is $x = 0$, therefore the only equilibrium configuration is the point of the guide lying on the vertical through $D$. At that point the spring is directed as the perpendicular to the guide and its force has no component along it.
