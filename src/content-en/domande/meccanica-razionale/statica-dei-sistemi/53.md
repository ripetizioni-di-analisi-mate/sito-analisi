A wheel, which can be modeled as a homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$, rests in a vertical plane on a smooth horizontal floor and against the smooth edge $E$ of a step of height $h = \dfrac{R}{2}$. At the center $O$ of the wheel a horizontal force $\vec{F}$ acts, directed toward the step, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the minimum magnitude of $\vec{F}$ for which the wheel leaves the floor?

::struttura{linee="-3.6,0 0,0 0,0.75 1.6,0.75" cerchi="-1.30,1.5,1.5" punti="-1.30,1.5,O,baricentro;0,0.75,E" forze="-1.30 1.5,1.3,0,F;-1.30 1.5,0,-1.1,mg" quote="0 0,0 0.75,h,-0.5" etichette="-0.55,2.45,R" tratteggi="-1.30,1.5 -0.33,2.65" titolo="Wheel pushed at the center $O$ against the edge $E$ of the step of height $h = \dfrac{R}{2}$"}

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g$
- $m g$
- $\sqrt{3}\,m g$

## Explanation

When the wheel leaves the floor, the reaction of the floor, which is a unilateral constraint, vanishes, and the weight, the force $\vec{F}$ and the reaction of the edge act on the wheel. The moment equation with respect to $E$ does not contain the reaction of the edge. The center $O$ lies at height $R - h = \dfrac{R}{2}$ above $E$ and at horizontal distance
$$
d = \sqrt{R^2 - (R - h)^2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R
$$
from $E$, therefore the moment of $\vec{F}$ with respect to $E$ has lever arm $R - h$ and that of the weight lever arm $d$. The wheel leaves the floor if $F\,(R - h) \ge m g\,d$, that is, for $F \ge \dfrac{m g\,d}{R - h} = \sqrt{3}\,m g$. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g$ is obtained by exchanging the two lever arms.
