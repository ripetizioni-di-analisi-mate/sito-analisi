A book of mass $m = 1\,\mathrm{kg}$ is held at rest, with its covers vertical, between two hands that press on it with two opposite horizontal forces of equal magnitude $N$. The coefficient of static friction between each hand and the book is $f = 0.5$. What is the minimum value of $N$ for which the book remains in equilibrium?

- $19.62\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two horizontal forces are opposite, therefore the horizontal component of the first cardinal equation is satisfied and each hand exerts on the book the normal reaction $N$. In the vertical direction the weight is balanced by the two friction forces, one per cover, whose sum must equal $m g$. The Coulomb-Morin law limits each of them to $f\,N$, therefore equilibrium requires $2 f N \ge m g$, that is
$$
N \ge \frac{m g}{2 f} = \frac{9.81}{2 \cdot 0.5}\,\mathrm{N} = 9.81\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $19.62\,\mathrm{N}$ takes into account the friction on one cover only.
