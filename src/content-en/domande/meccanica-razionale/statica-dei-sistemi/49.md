A homogeneous cylinder of mass $m$ rests, with its axis horizontal, in a V-shaped groove formed by two smooth planes, inclined at $60^\circ$ and at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal, as in the cross-section in the figure. The weight is the only active force. What is the magnitude of the reaction of the plane inclined at $60^\circ$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1" linee="-1.67,0.90 -0.37,-1.37 3.56,0.90" tratteggi="-1.77,-1.37 1.43,-1.37" punti="0,0,O,baricentro" forze="0 0,0,-0.9,mg" etichette="-0.99,-1.15,60°;0.68,-1.17,30°" titolo="Cross-section of the cylinder resting in the V-shaped groove formed by the smooth planes inclined at $60^\circ$ and at $30^\circ$"}

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g$
- $m g$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2}$

## Explanation

The two reactions are normal to the planes and pass through the center $O$ of the cross-section, like the weight, therefore the moment equation with respect to $O$ is identically satisfied and the two components of the first cardinal equation remain. The normal to the plane inclined at $60^\circ$ makes the angle $60^\circ$ with the vertical and the normal to the plane inclined at $30^\circ$ the angle $30^\circ$; denoting by $\Phi_1$ and $\Phi_2$ the magnitudes of the two reactions, the horizontal and vertical components give
$$
\begin{cases}
\Phi_1 \sin 60^\circ - \Phi_2 \sin 30^\circ = 0 \\[4pt]
\Phi_1 \cos 60^\circ + \Phi_2 \cos 30^\circ - m g = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first equation gives $\Phi_2 = \sqrt{3}\,\Phi_1$, and substituting into the second one obtains $2\,\Phi_1 = m g$, therefore $\Phi_1 = \dfrac{m g}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g$ is the reaction of the plane inclined at $30^\circ$, that is, of the less steep plane.
