A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ lies horizontally in a vertical plane and is built in at $A$ to a wall. A couple of moment $\mathcal{M}$ in the counterclockwise direction is applied to the rod, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the reaction moment of the clamped support, measured in the counterclockwise direction?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" coppie="4.5 0,1,ℳ" forze="3 0,0,-1.1,mg" quote="A,B,ℓ,1.3" titolo="Cantilever built in at $A$, with its own weight and the counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$"}

- $\dfrac{m g \ell}{2} + \mathcal{M}$
- $\mathcal{M} - \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$
- $\dfrac{m g \ell}{2} - \mathcal{M}$
- $m g \ell - \mathcal{M}$

## Explanation

The clamped support exerts a force of two components applied at $A$ and a couple of moment $\Gamma_A$, therefore with the pole at $A$ the two force components have zero moment and the moment equation contains only $\Gamma_A$, the moment of the weight and the applied couple. The weight acts at the midpoint, at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from $A$, and its moment is clockwise, whereas the couple enters with its own moment at whatever point it is applied, therefore
$$
\Gamma_A + \mathcal{M} - \frac{m g \ell}{2} = 0 .
$$
Therefore $\Gamma_A = \dfrac{m g \ell}{2} - \mathcal{M}$, and the reaction moment is counterclockwise as long as $\mathcal{M} < \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$. The sum $\dfrac{m g \ell}{2} + \mathcal{M}$ would be obtained by taking the couple in the clockwise direction.
