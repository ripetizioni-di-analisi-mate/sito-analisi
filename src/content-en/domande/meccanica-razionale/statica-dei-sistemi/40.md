Two homogeneous rods $AB$ and $BC$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $B$; the end $A$ is hinged to the ground and the end $C$ is constrained by a roller support that slides on a smooth horizontal guide through $A$. Each rod makes the angle $\vartheta$ with the horizontal and the weights are the only active forces, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the vertical component of the internal reaction at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.6,1.5;C,5.2,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" cerniere="B" linee="-0.8,0 6,0" forze="1.3 0.75,0,-1,mg;3.9 0.75,0,-1,mg" etichette="0.85,0.3,ϑ" titolo="Two rods hinged at $B$, with the hinge to the ground at $A$ and the roller support at $C$"}

- $\dfrac{m g}{2}$
- $0$
- $m g$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2\tan\vartheta}$

## Explanation

The moment equation of the whole system with respect to $A$ contains the reaction of the roller support alone, since the reaction of the hinge passes through the pole; with the two weights applied at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$ and $\dfrac{3\,\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$ from $A$ and the roller support at distance $2\,\ell\cos\vartheta$ one obtains $\Phi_C = m g$. The system is then taken apart and the equilibrium of the rod $BC$ under vertical translation is written, on which its own weight, the reaction of the roller support and the internal reaction at $B$ act:
$$
Y_B + \Phi_C - m g = 0 .
$$
Therefore $Y_B = 0$, that is, the internal reaction at $B$ is horizontal. The roller support supports by itself the weight of the rod $BC$, therefore through the internal hinge the two rods exchange a horizontal push alone.
