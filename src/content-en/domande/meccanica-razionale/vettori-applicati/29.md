The applied vector $(P, \vec{v})$ has $P - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath} + a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{v} = -F\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$, and is transferred to the origin $O$. What is the moment of the transfer couple?

- $2\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,3\,a F\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the transfer of an applied vector, the system formed by $(P, \vec{v})$ is equivalent to the system formed by $(O, \vec{v})$ and by the couple of moment $\mathcal{\vec{M}}_t = (P - O) \wedge \vec{v}$.

The vector is vertical, therefore its arm about $O$ is the abscissa $2a$ of the point of application, and one obtains
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = x_P\,v_y - y_P\,v_x = 2a \cdot (-F) - a \cdot 0 = -\,2\,a F .
$$
The transfer couple therefore has moment $-\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}$, clockwise. The ordinate $a$ of the point of application does not enter the computation, since the horizontal component of $\vec{v}$ vanishes.
