A system of applied vectors has resultant $\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$ and resultant moment $\vec{M}_O = a F\,\hat{k}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. About which poles $O'$ is the resultant moment equal to $\vec{M}_O$?

- Only the points of the central axis
- The points of the line through $O$ parallel to $\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$
- The points of the line through $O$ parallel to $\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath}$
- All the points of the plane $Oxy$

## Explanation

By the change of pole formula $\vec{M}_{O'} = \vec{M}_O + (O - O') \wedge \vec{R}$, hence the two moments coincide if and only if $(O - O') \wedge \vec{R} = \vec{0}$.

Since $\vec{R} \neq \vec{0}$, the cross product vanishes if and only if $O - O'$ is parallel to $\vec{R}$, that is if and only if $O'$ belongs to the line through $O$ parallel to $\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$.

For a pole on the line parallel to $\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath}$, for example $O' - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$, one obtains instead $(O - O') \wedge \vec{R} = -\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}$, and the moment becomes $-\,a F\,\hat{k}$. About the points of the central axis the moment equals $\dfrac{I}{|\vec{R}|^2}\,\vec{R} = \vec{0}$, since $I = 0$, hence it differs from $\vec{M}_O$.
