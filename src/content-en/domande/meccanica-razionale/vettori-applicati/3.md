On the rigid rectangular plate in the figure, with sides $3a$ and $2a$, with $O$ at a vertex and the axes along the sides, there act $\vec{v}_1 = 2F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $O$, $\vec{v}_2 = -F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point $B$ of coordinates $(3a, 0)$ and $\vec{v}_3 = F\,(-\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$ at the point $C$ of coordinates $(3a, 2a)$. What is the resultant moment about $O$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" nodi="O,0,0;B,3,0;C,3,2;E,0,2" forze="O,1.1,0,v_1;B,0,-1,v_2;C,-0.55,1.1,v_3" quote="O,B,3a,-3.2;B,C,2a,1" titolo="The plate and the three applied vectors"}

- $3\,a F$
- $a F$
- $-\,3\,a F$
- $5\,a F$

## Explanation

The resultant moment about $O$ is the sum of the moments of the three vectors, and each one is computed with the formula $M = x\,v_y - y\,v_x$.

The vector $\vec{v}_1$ is applied at $O$, therefore it has zero moment about $O$; the vector $\vec{v}_2$ gives $3a \cdot (-F) = -\,3\,a F$; the vector $\vec{v}_3$ gives $3a \cdot 2F - 2a \cdot (-F) = 8\,a F$. Summing the three contributions one obtains
$$
M_O = 0 - 3\,a F + 8\,a F = 5\,a F .
$$
The value $3\,a F$ is obtained by omitting the moment of the horizontal component of $\vec{v}_3$, which by **Varignon's theorem** must be added to that of the vertical component.
