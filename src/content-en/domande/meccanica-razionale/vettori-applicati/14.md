In the plane $Oxy$ the applied vectors $(O, 2F\,\hat{\imath})$ and $(D, -\,3F\,\hat{\jmath})$, with $D - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$, act on a rigid body, as the diagram in the figure shows. To the system are added the applied vector $(B, -\,2F\,\hat{\imath} + 3F\,\hat{\jmath})$, with $B - O = 4a\,\hat{\imath}$, and a couple of moment $\mathcal{M}\,\hat{k}$. For which value of $\mathcal{M}$ is the complete system balanced?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;D,2,0;B,4,0" aste="O-B" forze="O,1.1,0,2F;D,0,-1.6,3F" quote="O,D,2a,1.2;D,B,2a,1.2" titolo="The two vectors applied at $O$ and at $D$, and the point $B$"}

- $\mathcal{M} = 6\,a F$
- $\mathcal{M} = -\,12\,a F$
- $\mathcal{M} = -\,6\,a F$
- $\mathcal{M} = -\,18\,a F$

## Explanation

A system is balanced when the resultant and the resultant moment about a pole both vanish.

The resultant of the three vectors equals $2F\,\hat{\imath} - 3F\,\hat{\jmath} - 2F\,\hat{\imath} + 3F\,\hat{\jmath} = \vec{0}$, therefore the first condition is satisfied and the resultant moment is the same about every pole. Taking $O$ as the pole one obtains
$$
M_O = 0 + 2a \cdot (-\,3F) + 4a \cdot 3F + \mathcal{M} = 6\,a F + \mathcal{M} ,
$$
since the horizontal component of the vector applied at $B$ has zero arm about $O$. Requiring $M_O = 0$ one finds $\mathcal{M} = -\,6\,a F$, that is a clockwise couple of magnitude $6\,a F$.
