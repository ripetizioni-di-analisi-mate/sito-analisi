In the plane $Oxy$ the applied vector $(P, \vec{v})$ has $P - O = a\,(3\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$ and $\vec{v} = v\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$, with $a > 0$ and $v > 0$. What is the arm of the line of action about $O$?

- $4a$
- $2\sqrt{2}\,a$
- $\sqrt{2}\,a$
- $4\sqrt{2}\,a$

## Explanation

By the **theorem on the moment and the arm** one has $|\vec{M}_O| = |\vec{v}|\,b$, therefore the arm is obtained by dividing the magnitude of the moment by the magnitude of the vector.

The moment equals
$$
M_O = x_P\,v_y - y_P\,v_x = 3a \cdot v - (-a) \cdot v = 4\,a v ,
$$
whereas the magnitude of the vector equals $|\vec{v}| = v\sqrt{2}$. Therefore
$$
b = \frac{4\,a v}{v\sqrt{2}} = 2\sqrt{2}\,a .
$$
The value $4a$ would be obtained by dividing by $v$ instead of by $v\sqrt{2}$, that is by treating $\vec{v}$ as if it had a single component.
