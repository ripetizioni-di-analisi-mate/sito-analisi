In the plane $Oxy$ the applied vector $(P, \vec{v})$ has $P - O = a\,(4\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$ and $\vec{v} = v\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$, with $a > 0$ and $v > 0$. What is the moment about $O$?

- $2\,a v\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,7\,a v\,\hat{k}$
- $9\,a v\,\hat{k}$
- $7\,a v\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

In the plane $Oxy$ the moment of an applied vector is parallel to $\hat{k}$ and its component equals $M_O = x_P\,v_y - y_P\,v_x$.

Substituting the components one obtains
$$
M_O = 4a \cdot 2v - a \cdot v = 8\,a v - a v = 7\,a v ,
$$
therefore $\vec{M}_O = 7\,a v\,\hat{k}$. The positive sign says that the vector tends to produce a counterclockwise rotation about $O$. The value $9\,a v$ is obtained by adding the two products instead of subtracting them, whereas $2\,a v$ is obtained by multiplying together the components with the same name, that is by computing $x_P\,v_x - y_P\,v_y$, which is not the moment.
