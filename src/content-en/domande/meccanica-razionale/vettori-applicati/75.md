A vertical pole $4\,\mathrm{m}$ high is clamped in the ground at the point $O$. A taut cable goes from the top of the pole to an anchor on the ground $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the base, as in the figure, and pulls the top with a force of magnitude $500\,\mathrm{N}$ directed along the cable. What is the magnitude of the moment of this force about $O$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;T,0,4;K,3,0" aste="O-T" catene="T-K" vincoli="O,incastro,270" linee="-1,0 4,0" forze="0.9 2.8,0.9,-1.2," etichette="1.3,3.05,500 N" quote="O,T,4 m,-0.8;O,K,3 m,0.8" titolo="The clamped pole and the anchor cable"}

- $2000\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The cable is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$, hence it is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long, and the force has horizontal component $500 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = 300\,\mathrm{N}$ and vertical component $500 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} = 400\,\mathrm{N}$.

By **Varignon's theorem** the moment about $O$ is the sum of the moments of the two components applied at the top. The vertical component has its line of action along the pole, which passes through $O$, therefore only the horizontal component contributes, with arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and one obtains
$$
|M_O| = 300 \cdot 4 = 1200\,\mathrm{N\,m} .
$$
The same value is the product of $500\,\mathrm{N}$ and the distance of $O$ from the line of the cable, which equals $\dfrac{4 \cdot 3}{5} = 2.4\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $2000\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ uses the height of the pole as the arm of the whole force, and $1600\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ multiplies by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ the vertical component, which has zero arm.
