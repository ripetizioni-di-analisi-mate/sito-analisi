A couple acting on a rigid body has moment $\vec{M} = a F\,(2\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} + 2\hat{k})$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. Which of the following vectors can be one of the two vectors forming the couple?

- $F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$
- $F\,(2\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} + 2\hat{k})$
- $F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$
- $F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$

## Explanation

The moment of a couple formed by $(A, \vec{v})$ and $(B, -\vec{v})$ equals $(A - B) \wedge \vec{v}$, hence it is orthogonal to $\vec{v}$. The vectors of the couple are therefore orthogonal to $\vec{M}$, and the condition is checked with the dot product. For $\vec{v} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$ one obtains
$$
\vec{v} \cdot \vec{M} = a F^2\,\big( 1 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot (-1) + 0 \cdot 2 \big) = 0 .
$$
The vectors $F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$ and $F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$ give the dot product $a F^2 \neq 0$, and the vector parallel to $\vec{M}$ gives $9\,a F^2$, hence none of the three is orthogonal to the moment.

With $\vec{v} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$ the couple is realized by two lines of action parallel to $\vec{v}$, contained in a plane orthogonal to $\vec{M}$ and at distance
$$
d = \frac{|\vec{M}|}{|\vec{v}|} = \frac{3\,a F}{\sqrt{5}\,F} = \frac{3\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a .
$$
