The lines of action of three applied vectors all pass through the point $K$ with $K - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$, and the resultant of the three vectors is $\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the resultant moment about $O$?

- $\vec{0}$
- $-\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $2\sqrt{2}\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $2\,a F\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The three vectors are concurrent at $K$, therefore by **Varignon's theorem** the resultant moment coincides with the moment of the resultant applied at $K$.

Therefore
$$
\vec{M}_O = (K - O) \wedge \vec{R} = 2a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) = 2\,a F\,\hat{k} .
$$
The moment about $K$, on the other hand, vanishes, since $K$ belongs to all three lines of action, and the system is equivalent to the single applied vector $(K, \vec{R})$.
