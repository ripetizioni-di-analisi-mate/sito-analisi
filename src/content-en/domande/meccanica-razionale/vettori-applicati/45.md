In the plane $Oxy$ the applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ and $(A, -\,2F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $A - O = 3a\,\hat{\imath}$ act, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. At which point of the $x$ axis must the vector $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ be applied for the complete system to be balanced?

- At the point of abscissa $3a$
- At the point of abscissa $6a$
- At the point of abscissa $-\,6a$
- At the point of abscissa $\dfrac{3a}{2}$

## Explanation

The resultant of the three vectors equals $F - 2F + F = 0$ whatever the point of application of the third one, therefore the first condition of equilibrium is satisfied and the resultant moment does not depend on the pole.

Denoting by $x$ the required abscissa, the moment about $O$ equals
$$
M_O = 0 + 3a \cdot (-\,2F) + x \cdot F = -\,6\,a F + x F .
$$
Requiring $M_O = 0$ one finds $x = 6a$. The point therefore lies outside the segment joining the two given points of application, on the opposite side with respect to the vector with negative coefficient.
