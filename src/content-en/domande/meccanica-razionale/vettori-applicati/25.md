The applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{k})$ and $(A, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $A - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$ are given, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the scalar invariant of the system?

- $0$
- $2\,a F^2$
- $-\,2\,a F^2$
- $4\,a F^2$

## Explanation

The resultant equals $\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$, whereas the moment about $O$ receives the contribution of the vector applied at $A$ alone:
$$
\vec{M}_O = 2a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath} = 2\,a F\,\hat{k} .
$$
The scalar invariant therefore equals $I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = F\,(0, 1, 1) \cdot (0, 0, 2\,a F) = 2\,a F^2$.

Since $I \neq 0$, the two lines of action are skew and the system is not equivalent to a single applied vector.
