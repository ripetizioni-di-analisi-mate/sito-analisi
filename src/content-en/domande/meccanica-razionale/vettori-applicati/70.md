About all the points of the $z$ axis the resultant moment of a system of applied vectors equals $C\,\hat{k}$, and the resultant of the system is $\vec{R} = F\,\hat{k}$, with $F > 0$; the $z$ axis is therefore the central axis. What is the resultant moment about the point $P$ with $P - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$, where $a > 0$?

- $C\,\hat{k} - a F\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $C\,\hat{k}$
- $C\,\hat{k} + a F\,\hat{\imath}$
- $C\,\hat{k} + a F\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The origin $O$ belongs to the central axis, hence $\vec{M}_O = C\,\hat{k}$. By the change of pole formula, since $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{k} = -\,\hat{\jmath}$, one obtains
$$
\vec{M}_P = \vec{M}_O + (O - P) \wedge \vec{R} = C\,\hat{k} - a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{k} = C\,\hat{k} + a F\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The component along $\vec{R}$ remains $C$, as the invariance of $I$ requires, while a component orthogonal to $\vec{R}$ of magnitude $a F$ is added, proportional to the distance of $P$ from the central axis.

The vector $C\,\hat{k}$ is the moment about the points of the central axis only, and $C\,\hat{k} - a F\,\hat{\jmath}$ is obtained by using $P - O$ in place of $O - P$.
