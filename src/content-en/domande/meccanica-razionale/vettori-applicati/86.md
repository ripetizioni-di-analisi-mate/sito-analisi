The homogeneous rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$ and weight $m g$, is hinged to the ground at $A$ and rests at $B$ against a smooth vertical wall, which exerts a horizontal reaction on it; the rod forms the angle $60^\circ$ with the floor and the weight is applied at the midpoint $G$. Taking $A$ as the origin, through which point does the line of action of the reaction of the hinge at $A$ pass?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.5,2.598" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" linee="1.75,-0.3 1.75,3.3;-0.8,0 1.75,0" punti="0.75,1.299,G,baricentro" forze="0.75 0.3,0,-1," etichette="0.98,0.45,mg;0.5,0.17,60°" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.6" titolo="The rod hinged at $A$ and resting against the smooth wall at $B$"}

- $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{4},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,\ell \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{2},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{4},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell \right)$
- $\left( 0,\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell \right)$

## Explanation

Three non-parallel forces act on the rod, namely the weight, the reaction of the wall and the reaction of the hinge, and their system is balanced. By the **theorem on balanced systems of three vectors** the three lines of action pass through the same point $K$.

The point $B$ has coordinates $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{2},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell \right)$ and $G$ has coordinates $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{4},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,\ell \right)$. The line of action of the weight is the vertical line $x = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$ and that of the reaction of the wall is the horizontal line $y = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell$, hence $K$ is the point of coordinates $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{4},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell \right)$ and the reaction at $A$ is directed along $AK$.

The point $G$ belongs only to the line of action of the weight, and the point $B$ only to that of the reaction of the wall.
