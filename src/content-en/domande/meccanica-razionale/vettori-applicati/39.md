In the plane $Oxy$ there act the vector $2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the origin $O$ and the vector $-\,F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the point $P$ with $P - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$, where $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. At which point of the $x$ axis does the line of action of the resultant pass?

- At the point with abscissa $a$
- At the point with abscissa $-\,\dfrac{a}{3}$
- At the point with abscissa $\dfrac{a}{3}$
- At the point with abscissa $-\,a$

## Explanation

The two vectors are parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ and the sum of the coefficients equals $2F - F = F \neq 0$, hence the system is equivalent to the resultant $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the center of the parallel vectors. In the formula for the center the coefficients enter with their own sign, therefore
$$
x_C = \frac{2F \cdot 0 + (-F) \cdot a}{2F - F} = -\,a .
$$
The same value follows from the moment about $O$, which equals $-\,a F$ and is equal to the moment $x_C\,F$ of the resultant applied at $x_C$. One of the coefficients is negative, hence the center falls outside the segment $OP$, on the side of the vector of larger magnitude. The value $-\,\dfrac{a}{3}$ is obtained by dividing by the sum of the magnitudes $3F$ instead of by the algebraic sum.
