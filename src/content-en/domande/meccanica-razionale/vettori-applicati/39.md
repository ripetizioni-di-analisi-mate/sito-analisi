On the $x$ axis three vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ are applied, with coefficients $3F$, $-F$ and $F$, at the points of abscissa $0$, $a$ and $2a$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the abscissa of the center of the system?

- $\dfrac{a}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3a}{5}$
- $a$
- $\dfrac{a}{3}$

## Explanation

In the formula for the center the coefficients enter with their own sign, therefore their sum equals $3F - F + F = 3F$.

One then obtains
$$
x_C = \frac{3F \cdot 0 - F \cdot a + F \cdot 2a}{3F} = \frac{a}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{a}{5}$ would be obtained by dividing by the sum of the magnitudes of the coefficients, that is by $5F$, instead of by their algebraic sum. Since one coefficient is negative, the center could also fall outside the segment containing the three points, whereas in this case it belongs to it.
