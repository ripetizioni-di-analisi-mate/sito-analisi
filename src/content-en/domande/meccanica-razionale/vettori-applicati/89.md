The horizontal top of a table, of negligible weight, is supported by three vertical legs that touch it at the points $O$, $A$ and $B$ of coordinates $(0, 0)$, $(2a, 0)$ and $(0, 2a)$, as in the view from above in the figure, with $a > 0$. An object of weight $P$ is placed on the top at the point of coordinates $\left( \dfrac{a}{2},\ \dfrac{a}{2} \right)$, and each leg exerts on the top a vertical force directed upward. What is the intensity $N_O$ of the force of the leg at $O$?

::struttura{tratteggi="0,0 4,0 0,4 0,0" punti="0,0,O;4,0,A;0,4,B;1,1" etichette="1.3,1.25,P" quote="0 0,4 0,2a,0.6;0 4,0 0,2a,0.6" titolo="View from above: the legs at $O$, $A$, $B$ and the point where the object is placed"}

- $\dfrac{P}{3}$
- $\dfrac{P}{2}$
- $\dfrac{P}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3P}{4}$

## Explanation

The forces are all parallel to $\hat{k}$, hence the nontrivial equilibrium conditions are the vertical component of the resultant and the components of the moment along $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$. About $O$, with $\hat{k}$ pointing upward, the force $N\,\hat{k}$ applied at the point $(x, y, 0)$ has moment $N\,(y\,\hat{\imath} - x\,\hat{\jmath})$, therefore the three conditions form the system
$$
\begin{cases}
N_O + N_A + N_B - P = 0 \\[4pt]
2a\,N_B - \dfrac{a}{2}\,P = 0 \\[4pt]
-\,2a\,N_A + \dfrac{a}{2}\,P = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The second and third equations, the components of the moment along $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$, give $N_B = N_A = \dfrac{P}{4}$; the first, the vertical component of the resultant, gives $N_O = \dfrac{P}{2}$.

The leg at $O$ carries the largest load because the object is closest to it. The uniform distribution $\dfrac{P}{3}$ would occur for an object placed at the centroid of the triangle $OAB$, of coordinates $\left( \dfrac{2a}{3},\ \dfrac{2a}{3} \right)$.
