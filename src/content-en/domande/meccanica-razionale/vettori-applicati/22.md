A system of applied vectors has resultant $\vec{R} = F\,(2\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$ and resultant moment $\vec{M}_O = 3\,a F\,\hat{k}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the resultant moment about the pole $O'$ with $O' - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$?

- $3\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $2\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $4\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,4\,a F\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

By the **change of pole formula** one has $\vec{M}_{O'} = \vec{M}_O + (O - O') \wedge \vec{R}$, where the difference of points goes from the new pole to the old one.

One has $O - O' = -\,a\,\hat{\imath}$, therefore
$$
(O - O') \wedge \vec{R} = -\,a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,(2\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath}) = a F\,\hat{k} ,
$$
hence $\vec{M}_{O'} = 3\,a F\,\hat{k} + a F\,\hat{k} = 4\,a F\,\hat{k}$.

Reversing the order of the difference of points one would obtain $2\,a F\,\hat{k}$, since the cross product changes sign when the factors are interchanged.
