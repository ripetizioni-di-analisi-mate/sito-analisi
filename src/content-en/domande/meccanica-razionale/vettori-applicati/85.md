A ring is pulled in the plane $Oxy$ by two ropes, with the forces $F\,(2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$ and $F\,(-\,\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$, where $F > 0$; the lines of action of the two forces pass through the center $K$ of the ring. Which third force makes the system balanced?

- $F\,(\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath})$ applied at $K$
- $-F\,(\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath})$ applied at $K$
- $-F\,(\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath})$ applied at any point of the plane
- $-F\,(3\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$ applied at $K$

## Explanation

The condition $\vec{R} = \vec{0}$ requires the third force to be the opposite of the sum of the two given ones, that is
$$
-\,\big[ F\,(2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) + F\,(-\,\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath}) \big] = -F\,(\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath}) .
$$
The two given forces have zero moment about $K$, hence the third one must also have zero moment about $K$, and by the theorem on the moment and the arm its line of action must pass through $K$.

With a line of action not passing through $K$, the third force would form a **couple** with the resultant of the first two, and the system would not be balanced. The result is a case of the theorem on balanced systems of three vectors, according to which the three lines of action pass through the same point.
