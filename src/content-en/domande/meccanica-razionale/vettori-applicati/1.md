In the plane $Oxy$ the applied vector $(P, \vec{v})$ is given, with $P - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath})$ and $\vec{v} = v\,(2\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$, where $a > 0$ and $v > 0$. What is the moment about the pole $O'$ with $O' - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$?

- $-\,7\,a v\,\hat{k}$
- $4\,a v\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,4\,a v\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,10\,a v\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The moment about a pole is built on the vector going from the pole to the point of application, therefore one writes $P - O' = (P - O) - (O' - O) = 2a\,\hat{\jmath}$.

The system is planar, hence the moment is parallel to $\hat{k}$ and reduces to the number
$$
M_{O'} = (x_P - x_{O'})\,v_y - (y_P - y_{O'})\,v_x = 0 \cdot (-v) - 2a \cdot 2v = -\,4\,a v .
$$
The same value is obtained from the **change of pole formula**, since $\vec{M}_O = -\,7\,a v\,\hat{k}$ and $(O - O') \wedge \vec{v} = 3\,a v\,\hat{k}$, whose sum equals $-\,4\,a v\,\hat{k}$. The negative sign says that the vector tends to produce a clockwise rotation about $O'$.
