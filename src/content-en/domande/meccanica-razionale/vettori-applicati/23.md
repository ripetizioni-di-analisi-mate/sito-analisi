In the plane $Oxy$ there act $\vec{v}_1 = 2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point $P_1$ with $P_1 - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$, $\vec{v}_2 = -F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point $P_2$ with $P_2 - O = 3a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{v}_3 = F\,\hat{\imath}$ at the point $P_3$ with $P_3 - O = 2a\,\hat{\jmath}$, where $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the resultant moment about $O$?

- $-\,a F$
- $a F$
- $3\,a F$
- $-\,3\,a F$

## Explanation

The resultant moment is the sum of the moments of the three vectors, each computed with the formula $M = x\,v_y - y\,v_x$.

The three contributions equal $a \cdot 2F = 2\,a F$, then $3a \cdot (-F) = -\,3\,a F$ and finally $-\,2a \cdot F = -\,2\,a F$, therefore
$$
M_O = 2\,a F - 3\,a F - 2\,a F = -\,3\,a F .
$$
The value $-\,a F$ is obtained by neglecting the moment of $\vec{v}_3$, which is horizontal but has arm $2a$ about $O$, therefore its moment differs from zero.
