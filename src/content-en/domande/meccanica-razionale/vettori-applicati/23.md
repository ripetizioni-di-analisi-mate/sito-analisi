A sensor at $O$ measures a resultant $\vec R=(0,4,0)\,\mathrm N$ and a moment $\vec M_O=(2,0,6)\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. It is moved along the $x$ axis. Which coordinate makes just the $z$ component of the moment vanish?

- $-1.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Moving the reference point subtracts $(x,0,0)\times(0,4,0)$, along $z$. The $z$ component becomes $6-4x$, while the $x$ component remains $2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
