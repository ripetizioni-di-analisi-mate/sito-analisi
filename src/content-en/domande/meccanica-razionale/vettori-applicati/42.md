The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, carries the distributed load of intensity $q(s) = q_0\,\left( 1 - \dfrac{s}{\ell} \right)$ for $0 \leq s \leq \ell$, directed along $-\hat{\jmath}$, with $\ell > 0$ and $q_0 > 0$. Through which abscissa does the line of action of the resultant pass?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,1.2,0,q_0" quote="A,B,ℓ,1.6" riferimento=0 titolo="The triangular load, of intensity $q_0$ at $A$ and zero at $B$"}

- $s_C = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$
- $s_C = \dfrac{2\ell}{3}$
- $s_C = \dfrac{\ell}{3}$
- $s_C = \dfrac{\ell}{6}$

## Explanation

The intensity decreases linearly from $q_0$ at $A$ down to zero at $B$, therefore the load is triangular.

The two integrals of the theorem on the resultant of a distributed load equal
$$
R = q_0 \int_0^\ell \left( 1 - \frac{s}{\ell} \right) ds = \frac{q_0\,\ell}{2} ,
\qquad
\int_0^\ell s\,q(s)\,ds = q_0\,\left( \frac{\ell^2}{2} - \frac{\ell^2}{3} \right) = \frac{q_0\,\ell^2}{6} ,
$$
hence
$$
s_C = \frac{q_0\,\ell^2}{6} \cdot \frac{2}{q_0\,\ell} = \frac{\ell}{3} .
$$
The center falls at one third of the base from the side on which the intensity is greatest, that is near $A$; the value $\dfrac{2\ell}{3}$ corresponds to the increasing triangular load, with maximum intensity at $B$.
