The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $2a$ and lying along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, carries on the segment $AM$, where $M$ is the midpoint, a uniform load of intensity $q$ directed along $\hat{\jmath}$, and on the segment $MB$ a uniform load of the same intensity directed along $-\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $q > 0$. To which elementary system is the load equivalent?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;M,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-M;M-B" carichi="A,M,0.75,0.75,q,90;M,B,0.75,0.75,q,270" quote="A,M,a,1.5;M,B,a,1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Two segments of length $a$ with uniform loads of equal intensity and opposite direction"}

- To a force of magnitude $2\,q\,a$ applied at $M$
- To the null system
- To a couple of moment of magnitude $q\,a^2$
- To a couple of moment of magnitude $2\,q\,a^2$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the resultant of a distributed load, the load on the segment $AM$ is equivalent to the force $q\,a\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the point with abscissa $\dfrac{a}{2}$, and that on the segment $MB$ to the force $-\,q\,a\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the point with abscissa $\dfrac{3a}{2}$. The resultant of the system is hence zero. The resultant moment about $A$ equals
$$
\frac{a}{2}\,q\,a - \frac{3a}{2}\,q\,a = -\,q\,a^2 ,
$$
and since the resultant is zero it does not depend on the pole, therefore the load is equivalent to a couple of moment $-\,q\,a^2\,\hat{k}$, clockwise and of magnitude $q\,a^2$. The value $2\,q\,a^2$ is obtained by taking as arm the length $2a$ of the rod instead of the distance $a$ between the two lines of action.
