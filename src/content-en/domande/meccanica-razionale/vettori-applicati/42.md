A beam of length $2a$ carries a constant transverse load $+q$ on its first half and $-q$ on its second half, with $q>0$. Which system represents the reduced loading in the plane?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;M,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-M;M-B" carichi="A,M,0.75,0.75,+q,90;M,B,0.75,0.75,−q,270" quote="A,M,a,1.50;M,B,a,1.50" riferimento="0" titolo="The beam has two spans of length $a$ carrying uniform transverse loads of equal magnitude and opposite direction."}

- A force of magnitude $2qa$ at midspan.
- Zero force and zero moment.
- A couple of magnitude $qa^2$.
- A couple of magnitude $2qa^2$.

## Explanation

Each half is equivalent to a force of magnitude $qa$ at its midpoint. The two opposite forces have lines of action a distance $a$ apart, giving a couple of magnitude $qa^2$.
