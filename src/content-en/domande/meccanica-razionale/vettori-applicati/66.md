The applied vector $(P, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ has $P - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. Which of the following systems is equivalent to it?

- The vector $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at $O$ and a couple of moment $-\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- The vector $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the point of coordinates $(5a, 0)$
- The vector $2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the point of coordinates $(a, 0)$
- The vector $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the point of coordinates $(2a, 5a)$

## Explanation

The point of coordinates $(2a, 5a)$ belongs to the line of action of the given vector, which is the vertical line $x = 2a$, hence by the **theorem on sliding** the vector can be applied at that point and the system remains equivalent.

The given system has $\vec{R} = F\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{M}_O = 2\,a F\,\hat{k}$, and the comparison with these two quantities rules out the other systems. The couple of moment $-\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}$ has the opposite sign of the transfer couple, which equals $(P - O) \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath} = 2\,a F\,\hat{k}$. The vector applied at $(5a, 0)$ has moment $5\,a F\,\hat{k}$, while $2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at $(a, 0)$ has the same moment about $O$ but twice the resultant.
