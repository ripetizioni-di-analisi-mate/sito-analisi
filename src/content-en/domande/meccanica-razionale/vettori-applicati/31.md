In the plane $Oxy$ the system formed by the single applied vector $(O, 2F\,\hat{\jmath})$ and the system formed by the applied vector $(A, 2F\,\hat{\jmath})$, with $A - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$, and by a couple of moment $\mathcal{M}\,\hat{k}$ are equivalent, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the value of $\mathcal{M}$?

- $0$
- $2\,a F$
- $-\,4\,a F$
- $-\,2\,a F$

## Explanation

Two systems are equivalent when they have the same resultant and the same resultant moment about a pole.

The two resultants coincide, since they both equal $2F\,\hat{\jmath}$. The first system has zero moment about $O$, whereas the second has moment
$$
M_O = a \cdot 2F + \mathcal{M} = 2\,a F + \mathcal{M} .
$$
Requiring the equality of the two moments one finds $\mathcal{M} = -\,2\,a F$. The result is the transfer couple with the sign changed, since moving the vector from $O$ to $A$ introduces the moment $2\,a F$ which the couple must compensate.
