On a rigid body two couples act, of moments $\vec{M}_1 = 2\,a F\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{M}_2 = -\,2\,a F\,\hat{\imath}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. Which statement about the complete system is true?

- The system is balanced
- The system is equivalent to a couple of moment $4\,a F\,\hat{\imath}$
- The system has resultant different from the zero vector
- The resultant moment of the system depends on the pole

## Explanation

Every couple has zero resultant, therefore the resultant of the complete system vanishes and the resultant moment is the same about every pole.

Resultant and resultant moment are additive, hence the moment of the system equals
$$
\vec{M} = \vec{M}_1 + \vec{M}_2 = 2\,a F\,\hat{\imath} - 2\,a F\,\hat{\imath} = \vec{0} .
$$
The two conditions $\vec{R} = \vec{0}$ and $\vec{M}_O = \vec{0}$ are both satisfied, therefore the system is balanced and a rigid body initially at rest, subject to these actions alone, stays in equilibrium.
