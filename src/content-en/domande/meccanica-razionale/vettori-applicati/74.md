On the rigid triangular plate in the figure, with vertices $O$, $A$ and $B$ of coordinates $(0, 0)$, $(4a, 0)$ and $(0, 3a)$, the vector $F\,(-\,4\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath})$ applied at $A$, directed along the side $AB$, and the vector $2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at $O$, directed along the side $OB$, act, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. At which point does the line of action of the resultant meet the $x$ axis?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 0,3" nodi="O,0,0;A,4,0;B,0,3" forze="2.8 0.9,-1.2,0.9,;0 1.2,0,1.2," etichette="2.8,1.3,5F;0.3,0.9,2F" quote="O,A,4a,0.8;B,O,3a,0.8" titolo="The triangular plate with the vectors along the sides $AB$ and $OB$"}

- At the point of abscissa $4a$
- At the point of abscissa $\dfrac{12a}{5}$
- At the point of abscissa $3a$
- At the point of abscissa $-\dfrac{12a}{5}$

## Explanation

The lines of action of the two vectors are the lines of the sides $AB$ and $OB$, which meet at $B$. By **Varignon's theorem** the system is therefore equivalent to the resultant alone applied at $B$.

The resultant equals $\vec{R} = F\,(-\,4\hat{\imath} + 5\hat{\jmath})$, hence its line of action passes through $B$ and is parallel to $-\,4\hat{\imath} + 5\hat{\jmath}$; its points have coordinates $(-\,4t,\ 3a + 5t)$ with $t$ real. The ordinate vanishes for $t = -\dfrac{3a}{5}$, which corresponds to the abscissa $\dfrac{12a}{5}$.

The same value follows from the equation $x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_O$, with $M_O = 4a \cdot 3F = 12\,a F$, which for $y = 0$ gives $5F\,x = 12\,a F$. The value $3a$ is obtained by dividing $M_O$ by $|R_x|$ instead of by $R_y$.
