On a horizontal plate, in the plane $Oxy$, vertical loads parallel to $-\,\hat{k}$ act, with resultant $-\,3F\,\hat{k}$ and center at the point $C$ with $C - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath} + a\,\hat{\jmath}$, where $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the resultant moment of the loads about $O$?

- $a F\,(-\,3\hat{\imath} + 6\hat{\jmath})$
- $a F\,(3\hat{\imath} - 6\hat{\jmath})$
- $a F\,(-\,6\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath})$
- $-\,9\,a F\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

By the **theorem on the center of a system of parallel vectors** the loads are equivalent to the resultant alone applied at the center, hence their moment about $O$ is $(C - O) \wedge \vec{R}$.

Since $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{k} = -\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{k} = \hat{\imath}$, one obtains
$$
\vec{M}_O = (2a\,\hat{\imath} + a\,\hat{\jmath}) \wedge (-\,3F\,\hat{k}) = 6\,a F\,\hat{\jmath} - 3\,a F\,\hat{\imath} .
$$
The moment is horizontal, as for every system of vertical forces; its component along $\hat{\imath}$ depends on the ordinate of $C$ and the one along $\hat{\jmath}$ on the abscissa. The vector $a F\,(-\,6\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath})$ swaps the two roles, and the vector $-\,9\,a F\,\hat{k}$ treats the moment as if it were parallel to the forces.
