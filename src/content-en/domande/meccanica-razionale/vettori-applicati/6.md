The rigid bracket in the figure is formed by the vertical post $AB$ of length $3\ell$ and by the horizontal arm $BC$ of length $2\ell$, with $A$ at the origin. At $C$ the vector $\vec{v} = F\,(\hat{\imath} - 2\hat{\jmath})$ acts. Reducing the system at $A$, what is the moment of the transfer couple?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,2,3" aste="A-B;B-C" forze="C,0.7,-1.4,v" quote="A,B,3ℓ,0.9;B,C,2ℓ,0.9" titolo="The bracket with the vector applied at $C$"}

- $-\,4F\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,F\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,7F\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $7F\ell\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

By the **theorem on the transfer of an applied vector** the transfer couple at $A$ has moment $\mathcal{\vec{M}}_t = (C - A) \wedge \vec{v}$, that is the moment of the vector about the arrival point.

The point $C$ has coordinates $(2\ell, 3\ell)$, therefore
$$
\mathcal{M}_t = x_C\,v_y - y_C\,v_x = 2\ell \cdot (-\,2F) - 3\ell \cdot F = -\,7F\ell .
$$
The moment is therefore $-\,7F\ell\,\hat{k}$, clockwise. The value $-\,4F\ell$ takes into account only the vertical component of $\vec{v}$, whereas the horizontal component too has arm $3\ell$ about $A$ and gives the contribution $-\,3F\ell$.
