A system of applied vectors has resultant $\vec{R} = F\,\hat{k}$, and its central axis is the line parallel to the $z$ axis passing through the point of coordinates $(a, 0, 0)$; the moment about the points of the central axis equals $2\,a F\,\hat{k}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. A couple of moment $-\,2\,a F\,\hat{k}$ is added to the system. To which system is the total system equivalent?

- To the empty system, that is the total system is balanced
- To the single vector $F\,\hat{k}$ applied at a point of the line through $(a, 0, 0)$ parallel to the $z$ axis
- To the single vector $F\,\hat{k}$ applied at a point of the $z$ axis
- To the vector $F\,\hat{k}$ applied on the central axis and a couple of moment $2\,a F\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The added couple has zero resultant, hence the resultant remains $F\,\hat{k}$ and the total system is not balanced.

The moment of the couple is the same about every pole, therefore about every point $Q$ of the line through $(a, 0, 0)$ parallel to $\hat{k}$ the total moment equals $2\,a F\,\hat{k} - 2\,a F\,\hat{k} = \vec{0}$. By the **reduction theorem** the total system is therefore equivalent to the single vector $F\,\hat{k}$ applied on that line.

A couple parallel to the resultant changes the minimum moment and leaves the central axis where it was, since the product $\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O$, which appears in the equation of the axis, does not change when a vector parallel to $\vec{R}$ is added to $\vec{M}_O$.
