The rigid L-shaped frame in the figure is formed by the horizontal rod $OA$, of length $2\ell$ and mass $m$, and by the vertical rod $AB$, of length $\ell$ and mass $2m$, both homogeneous, with $O$ at the origin. The weights of the two rods are vectors parallel to $-\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at the midpoints $G_1$ and $G_2$. What are the coordinates of the center of the two weights?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,4,0;B,4,2" aste="O-A;A-B" punti="2,0,G_1,baricentro;4,1,G_2,baricentro" etichette="2,-0.5,m;4.55,1.3,2m" quote="O,A,2ℓ,1.1;A,B,ℓ,1.1" titolo="The L-shaped frame, with the masses of the two rods and their midpoints"}

- $\left( \dfrac{3\ell}{2},\ \dfrac{\ell}{4} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{4\ell}{3},\ \dfrac{\ell}{6} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5\ell}{3},\ \dfrac{\ell}{3} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5\ell}{3},\ \dfrac{2\ell}{3} \right)$

## Explanation

The midpoints of the two rods have coordinates $G_1 = (\ell, 0)$ and $G_2 = \left( 2\ell,\ \dfrac{\ell}{2} \right)$. The coefficients of the two parallel vectors are $m g$ and $2 m g$, with sum $3 m g$, hence by the theorem on the center of a system of parallel vectors
$$
x_C = \frac{m g \cdot \ell + 2 m g \cdot 2\ell}{3 m g} = \frac{5\ell}{3} ,
\qquad
y_C = \frac{m g \cdot 0 + 2 m g \cdot \dfrac{\ell}{2}}{3 m g} = \frac{\ell}{3} .
$$
The point $\left( \dfrac{3\ell}{2},\ \dfrac{\ell}{4} \right)$ is the unweighted mean of the two midpoints, and $\left( \dfrac{4\ell}{3},\ \dfrac{\ell}{6} \right)$ is obtained by swapping the two masses. The point $\left( \dfrac{5\ell}{3},\ \dfrac{2\ell}{3} \right)$ is obtained by applying the weight of $AB$ at the end $B$ instead of at its midpoint.
