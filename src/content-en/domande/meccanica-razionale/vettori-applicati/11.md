In the plane $Oxy$ three vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ are given, with coefficients $2F$, $-F$ and $\mu F$ where $\mu$ is a real parameter, applied at the points $P_1 = O$, $P_2$ and $P_3$ with $P_2 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $P_3 - O = 5a\,\hat{\imath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. For which value of $\mu$ is the center of the system the point of abscissa $7a$?

::struttura{nodi="P_1,0,0;P_2,2,0;P_3,5,0" aste="P_1-P_3" forze="0 1.3,0,1.3,;2 -0.7,0,-0.7,;5 1.6,0,1.6," etichette="0.22,1.34,2F;2.22,-0.72,F;5.22,1.62,μF" quote="P_1,P_2,2a,1.6;P_2,P_3,3a,1.6" titolo="The three parallel vectors, with the coefficient $\mu$ of the third as a parameter"}

- $\mu = -\,1$
- $\mu = -\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\mu = \dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\mu = -\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The three points of application lie on the $x$ axis, therefore the center lies on that axis as well and its abscissa equals
$$
x_C = \frac{2F \cdot 0 - F \cdot 2a + \mu F \cdot 5a}{2F - F + \mu F} = \frac{(5\mu - 2)\,a}{\mu + 1} .
$$
Requiring $x_C = 7a$ one obtains $5\mu - 2 = 7\mu + 7$, therefore $\mu = -\dfrac{9}{2}$.

The value $\mu = -\,1$ makes the sum of the coefficients vanish, therefore for that value the center does not exist and the system is equivalent to a couple of moment $-\,7\,a F\,\hat{k}$. A negative coefficient allows the center to fall outside the segment containing the points of application, as happens here.
