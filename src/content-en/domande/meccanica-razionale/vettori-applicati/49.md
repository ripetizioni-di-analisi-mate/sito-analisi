A door rotates about the vertical axis of its hinges, which coincides with the $z$ axis. In the position shown in the figure, seen from above, the door lies in the plane $Oxz$ and the handle is at the point $P$ with $P - O = b\,\hat{\imath} + h\,\hat{k}$. The horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,(\cos 60^\circ\,\hat{\imath} + \sin 60^\circ\,\hat{\jmath})$, which forms the angle $60^\circ$ with the plane of the door, acts on the handle, with $b > 0$, $h > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the axial moment of the force about the $z$ axis?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;P,3,0" aste="O-P" forze="3.6 1.04,0.6,1.04," tratteggi="3,0 4.3,0" etichette="3.85,1.15,F;3.65,0.2,60°" titolo="View from above: the door $OP$, with the hinges at $O$, and the force on the handle"}

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,b F$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,F\sqrt{b^2 + h^2}$
- $b F$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,b F$

## Explanation

The point $O$ belongs to the $z$ axis, hence the axial moment is the third component of $\vec{M}_O$, which equals $M_z = x_P\,F_y - y_P\,F_x$. The height $h$ of the handle enters only the components of $\vec{M}_O$ along $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$, therefore it does not contribute.

One obtains
$$
M_z = b \cdot F\sin 60^\circ - 0 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,b F .
$$
The value $\dfrac{1}{2}\,b F$ is obtained with the component of the force parallel to the door, whose line of action meets the axis and which therefore has zero axial moment. The value with $\sqrt{b^2 + h^2}$ uses the distance of the handle from $O$, while the arm with respect to the axis is the distance $b$ of the handle from the axis.
