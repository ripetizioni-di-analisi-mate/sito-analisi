The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $2\ell$, is placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin. At $B$ the vector $\vec{v} = F\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$ acts, with $\ell > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the moment about $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" forze="B,0.7,-0.7,v" quote="A,B,2ℓ,0.8" titolo="The rod $AB$ with the vector applied at $B$"}

- $-\,F\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,2F\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $2F\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,2\sqrt{2}\,F\ell\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

By Varignon's theorem the moment of the vector is the sum of the moments of its two components applied at $B$.

The horizontal component $F\,\hat{\imath}$ has a line of action passing through $A$, therefore it has zero moment about $A$; the vertical component $-F\,\hat{\jmath}$ has arm $2\ell$ and tends to rotate the rod clockwise. Therefore
$$
M_A = x_B\,v_y - y_B\,v_x = 2\ell \cdot (-F) - 0 = -\,2F\ell .
$$
The value $-\,2\sqrt{2}\,F\ell$ would be obtained by multiplying the magnitude $F\sqrt{2}$ of the vector by the length $2\ell$, which is not the arm, since the arm is the distance of $A$ from the line of action and equals $\sqrt{2}\,\ell$.
