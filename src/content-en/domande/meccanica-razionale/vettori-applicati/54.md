A system of applied vectors has resultant $\vec{R} = F\,\hat{\imath}$ and resultant moment $\vec{M}_O = a F\,(2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. Which of the following vectors can be the resultant moment of the system about another pole $O'$?

- $a F\,(3\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$
- $a F\,(-\,2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$
- $a F\,(2\hat{\imath} - 3\hat{\jmath} + 4\hat{k})$
- $a F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath} + 2\hat{k})$

## Explanation

By the **theorem on the scalar invariant** the product $\vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_{O'}$ is the same for every pole, and here it equals $I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = 2\,a F^2$. Since $\vec{R}$ is parallel to $\hat{\imath}$, the component along $\hat{\imath}$ of the resultant moment therefore equals $2\,a F$ about every pole.

Among the proposed vectors only $a F\,(2\hat{\imath} - 3\hat{\jmath} + 4\hat{k})$ satisfies this condition; for example $a F\,(3\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$ would give $I = 3\,a F^2$.

The vector found is indeed the moment about some pole. The difference $a F\,(-\,4\hat{\jmath} + 4\hat{k})$ is orthogonal to $\vec{R}$, and by the change of pole formula every vector orthogonal to $\vec{R}$ can be written as $(O - O') \wedge \vec{R}$ for a suitable pole $O'$.
