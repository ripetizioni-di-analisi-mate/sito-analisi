The crank $OP$ of a bicycle, of length $0.17\,\mathrm{m}$, rotates about the axis of the bottom bracket, which passes through $O$ and is parallel to $\hat{k}$. At the instant shown in the figure the crank forms the angle $30^\circ$ with the horizontal $x$ axis, and the foot pushes on the pedal $P$ with the vertical force $-\,400\,\mathrm{N}\,\hat{\jmath}$. The moment of the force about $O$ is $M_O\,\hat{k}$: what is $M_O$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;P,1.732,1" aste="O-P" forze="P,0,-1.2,400 N" tratteggi="0,0 2.6,0" etichette="0.75,0.17,30°" quote="O,P,0.17 m,-0.5" titolo="The crank $OP$ and the force of the foot on the pedal"}

- $-\,34.00\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $58.89\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-\,58.89\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-\,68.00\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The pedal is at the point $P$ with $P - O = 0.17\,\mathrm{m}\,(\cos 30^\circ\,\hat{\imath} + \sin 30^\circ\,\hat{\jmath})$, and the force has only the vertical component. The formula for the moment in the plane gives
$$
M_O = x_P\,v_y - y_P\,v_x = 0.17 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot (-\,400) - 0 = -\,58.89\,\mathrm{N\,m} .
$$
The arm of the vertical force is the horizontal distance between the axis and the pedal, that is $0.17\cos 30^\circ\,\mathrm{m}$, and the negative sign shows that the force tends to rotate the crank clockwise. The value $-\,34.00\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ is obtained by using $\sin 30^\circ$, that is the vertical distance, which would be the arm of a horizontal force; the value $-\,68.00\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ uses the whole length of the crank as the arm.
