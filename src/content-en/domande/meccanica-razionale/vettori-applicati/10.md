In the plane $Oxy$ four vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ are given, with coefficients $F$, $3F$, $2F$ and $2F$, applied respectively at the points $P_1 = O$, $P_2$, $P_3$ and $P_4$ of the rectangle in the figure, with $P_2 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$, $P_3 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath} + 3a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $P_4 - O = 3a\,\hat{\jmath}$, where $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What are the coordinates of the center of the system?

::struttura{tratteggi="0,0 2,0 2,3 0,3 0,0" punti="0,0;2,0;2,3;0,3" forze="0 0.5,0,0.5,;2 1.5,0,1.5,;2 4,0,1,;0 4,0,1," etichette="0.2,0.52,F;2.2,1.52,3F;2.2,4.02,2F;0.2,4.02,2F;-0.5,-0.3,P_1;2.2,-0.3,P_2;2.25,2.7,P_3;-0.55,2.7,P_4" titolo="The four parallel vectors applied at the vertices of the rectangle"}

- $\left( a,\ \dfrac{3a}{2} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5a}{4},\ 0 \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5a}{4},\ \dfrac{3a}{2} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3a}{2},\ \dfrac{5a}{4} \right)$

## Explanation

By the **theorem on the center of a system of parallel vectors** the center is the average of the points of application weighted with the coefficients, and the sum of the coefficients equals $F + 3F + 2F + 2F = 8F$.

The two coordinates are computed separately:
$$
x_C = \frac{0 + 3 \cdot 2a + 2 \cdot 2a + 0}{8} = \frac{5a}{4} ,
\qquad
y_C = \frac{0 + 0 + 2 \cdot 3a + 2 \cdot 3a}{8} = \frac{3a}{2} .
$$
The simple arithmetic mean of the abscissas would give $a$, therefore the coefficients must be used as weights. The point of coordinates $\left( \dfrac{5a}{4},\ 0 \right)$ belongs to the line of action of the resultant, which is the vertical line of abscissa $\dfrac{5a}{4}$, but the center is the only point of that line about which the line of action rotates when all the vectors rotate by the same angle.
