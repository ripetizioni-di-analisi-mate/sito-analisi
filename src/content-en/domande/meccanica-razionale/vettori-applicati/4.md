On the rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $4\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, there act the vector $-\,2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point of abscissa $\ell$, the vector $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point of abscissa $3\ell$ and a couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = -\,2F\ell\,\hat{k}$. The system is equivalent to a single applied vector. At which point does its line of action meet the rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" forze="1 0,0,-1.6,2F;3 1,0,1," etichette="3.22,1.02,F" coppie="3.6 0,-1,ℳ" quote="A,1 0,ℓ,1.3;1 0,3 0,2ℓ,1.3;3 0,B,ℓ,1.3" titolo="The rod with the two vertical vectors and the clockwise couple"}

- At the point of abscissa $3\ell$
- At the point of abscissa $\ell$
- At the point of abscissa $-\,\ell$
- At the point of abscissa $-\,3\ell$

## Explanation

The resultant equals $\vec{R} = (-\,2F + F)\,\hat{\jmath} = -F\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence it differs from the zero vector, and the system is planar, therefore the scalar invariant vanishes and the reduction to a single applied vector is possible.

The resultant moment about $A$ collects the moments of the two vectors and the moment of the couple, which is the same about every pole:
$$
M_A = \ell \cdot (-\,2F) + 3\ell \cdot F - 2F\ell = -F\ell .
$$
The line of action of the resultant has equation $x\,R_y = M_A$, therefore $x = \dfrac{-F\ell}{-F} = \ell$. Neglecting the couple one would obtain $M_A = F\ell$ and the abscissa $-\,\ell$, which falls outside the rod.
