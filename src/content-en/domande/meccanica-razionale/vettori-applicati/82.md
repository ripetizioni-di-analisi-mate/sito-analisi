A vertical gate of height $h$ is hinged along its lower edge $B$ and holds back on one side water of density $\rho$, which reaches the upper edge $T$. Per unit width of the gate, the water pressure is a horizontal distributed load of intensity $q(s) = \rho\,g\,s$, where $s$ is the depth measured from $T$. What is the magnitude of the moment of this load about the hinge $B$?

::struttura{nodi="T,0,3;B,0,0" aste="T-B" vincoli="B,cerniera" carichi="T,B,0,1.2,,0" linee="-2.2,3 0,3" etichette="-1.6,3.25,water;-1.75,0.6,ρgh" quote="T,B,h,-0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="The gate hinged at $B$ and the triangular load due to the water pressure"}

- $\dfrac{\rho\,g\,h^3}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\rho\,g\,h^3}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\rho\,g\,h^3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\rho\,g\,h^3}{4}$

## Explanation

The load is triangular, zero at $T$ and maximum at $B$, hence the resultant equals
$$
R = \int_0^h \rho\,g\,s\,ds = \frac{\rho\,g\,h^2}{2} .
$$
The center of a triangular load lies at one third of the base from the side where the intensity is maximum, that is at depth $\dfrac{2h}{3}$, at distance $\dfrac{h}{3}$ from the hinge. Therefore
$$
|M_B| = \frac{\rho\,g\,h^2}{2} \cdot \frac{h}{3} = \frac{\rho\,g\,h^3}{6} ,
$$
as is also obtained from the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^h \rho\,g\,s\,(h - s)\,ds$. The value $\dfrac{\rho\,g\,h^3}{3}$ uses as the arm the depth $\dfrac{2h}{3}$ of the center, which is its distance from $T$ and not from the hinge; the value $\dfrac{\rho\,g\,h^3}{4}$ applies the resultant at mid-height, as for a uniform load.
