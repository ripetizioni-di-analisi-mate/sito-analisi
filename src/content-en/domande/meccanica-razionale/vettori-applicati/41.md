The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $3\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, carries a uniform load of intensity $q_0$ directed along $-\hat{\jmath}$ on the part $\ell \leq s \leq 3\ell$, with $\ell > 0$ and $q_0 > 0$. Through which abscissa does the line of action of the resultant pass?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="2 0,B,0.9,0.9,q_0" quote="A,2 0,ℓ,1.3;2 0,B,2ℓ,1.3" riferimento=0 titolo="The uniform load on the part from $s = \ell$ to $s = 3\ell$"}

- $s_C = \dfrac{3\ell}{2}$
- $s_C = 2\ell$
- $s_C = \ell$
- $s_C = \dfrac{5\ell}{2}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the resultant of a distributed load the resultant equals $R = \displaystyle\int_\ell^{3\ell} q_0\,ds = 2\,q_0\,\ell$ and passes through the point of abscissa $s_C = \dfrac{1}{R}\displaystyle\int_\ell^{3\ell} s\,q_0\,ds$.

The second integral equals
$$
q_0 \int_\ell^{3\ell} s\,ds = q_0\,\frac{9\ell^2 - \ell^2}{2} = 4\,q_0\,\ell^2 ,
$$
therefore
$$
s_C = \frac{4\,q_0\,\ell^2}{2\,q_0\,\ell} = 2\ell .
$$
The load is uniform, therefore its center is the midpoint of the loaded part, which goes from $\ell$ to $3\ell$; the value $\dfrac{3\ell}{2}$ would be the midpoint of the whole rod, which is not the loaded part.
