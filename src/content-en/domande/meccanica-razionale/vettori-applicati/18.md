The rigid rod $OA$ in the figure has its end $O$ at the origin and its end $A$ with $A - O = \ell\,\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} \right)$. At $A$ the weight $-\,m g\,\hat{\jmath}$ acts. What is the moment about $O$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.732,1" aste="O-A" forze="A,0,-0.8,mg" tratteggi="0,0 2.2,0" etichette="0.62,0.12,ϑ" titolo="The rod $OA$ and the weight applied at $A$, with $\vartheta$ the angle with the $x$ axis"}

- $-\,m g \ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,m g \ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g \ell\,\hat{k}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g \ell\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The weight has a vertical line of action, therefore its arm about $O$ is the horizontal distance between $O$ and the point $A$, that is $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell$.

From the formula for the moment in the plane one obtains
$$
M_O = x_A\,v_y - y_A\,v_x = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell \cdot (-\,m g) - \frac{\ell}{2} \cdot 0 = -\,\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g \ell .
$$
The negative sign says that the weight tends to rotate the rod clockwise about $O$. The value $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,m g \ell$ would be obtained by using the ordinate of $A$ as the arm, whereas the arm of a vertical force is the horizontal distance between the pole and the line of action, that is the abscissa of $A$.
