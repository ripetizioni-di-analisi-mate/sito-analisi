The rigid rod $AB$, of length $3\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, carries over its whole length a uniform load of intensity $q_0$ directed along $-\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $\ell > 0$ and $q_0 > 0$. What is the moment of the load about the point $D$ of abscissa $\ell$?

- $-\,2\,q_0\,\ell^2\,\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{9}{2}\,q_0\,\ell^2\,\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}\,q_0\,\ell^2\,\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{2}\,q_0\,\ell^2\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The uniform load has resultant $3\,q_0\,\ell$ applied at the midpoint of the rod, of abscissa $\dfrac{3\ell}{2}$, which lies at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ to the right of $D$. The resultant points downward, hence it tends to produce a clockwise rotation about $D$ and
$$
M_D = \left( \frac{3\ell}{2} - \ell \right) \cdot (-\,3\,q_0\,\ell) = -\,\frac{3}{2}\,q_0\,\ell^2 .
$$
Splitting the load into the two parts gives the same value: the part to the left of $D$ gives $\dfrac{q_0\,\ell^2}{2}$, counterclockwise, and the one to the right gives $-\,2\,q_0\,\ell^2$, clockwise. The value $-\dfrac{5}{2}\,q_0\,\ell^2$ is obtained by adding the two contributions with the same sign, and $-\dfrac{9}{2}\,q_0\,\ell^2$ is the moment about $A$.
