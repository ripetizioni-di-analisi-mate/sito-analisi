The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $2\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, carries a uniform load of intensity $q_0$ directed along $-\hat{\jmath}$ on the part $0 \leq s \leq \ell$ and a uniform load of the same intensity directed along $\hat{\jmath}$ on the part $\ell \leq s \leq 2\ell$, with $\ell > 0$ and $q_0 > 0$. To which system is the complete load equivalent?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,3 0,0.9,0.9,q_0;3 0,B,0.9,0.9,q_0,90" quote="A,3 0,ℓ,-1.6;3 0,B,ℓ,1.6" riferimento=0 titolo="The downward load on the first half of the rod and the upward load on the second"}

- To a force of magnitude $2\,q_0\,\ell$ applied at the point of abscissa $\ell$
- To the empty system
- To a couple of moment $-\,q_0\,\ell^2\,\hat{k}$
- To a couple of moment $q_0\,\ell^2\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The two parts carry loads of equal intensity and opposite orientation, therefore the two resultants equal $q_0\,\ell$ with opposite orientations and the complete resultant vanishes.

The moment about $A$ collects the contributions of the two resultants, applied at the midpoints of the respective parts:
$$
M_A = \frac{\ell}{2} \cdot (-\,q_0\,\ell) + \frac{3\ell}{2} \cdot q_0\,\ell = q_0\,\ell^2 .
$$
Since the resultant vanishes and the resultant moment differs from the zero vector, by the **reduction theorem** the load is equivalent to a couple of moment $q_0\,\ell^2\,\hat{k}$, counterclockwise and the same about every pole.
