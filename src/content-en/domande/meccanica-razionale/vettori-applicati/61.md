A couple is formed by the applied vectors $(A, F\,\hat{k})$ and $(B, -F\,\hat{k})$ with $B - A = 2a\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the axial moment of the couple about the line $r$ passing through the point $C$ with $C - O = a\,(5\hat{\imath} + 7\hat{\jmath})$ and oriented along $\hat{\imath}$?

- $2\,a F$
- $0$
- $-\,2\,a F$
- $-\,4\,a F$

## Explanation

The axial moment of the couple is the component along $\hat{\imath}$ of its moment about a point of the axis, and by the **theorem on the moment of a couple** this moment is the same about every pole. One obtains
$$
\vec{M} = (A - B) \wedge F\,\hat{k} = -\,2a\,\hat{\jmath} \wedge F\,\hat{k} = -\,2\,a F\,\hat{\imath} ,
$$
hence $M_r = \hat{\imath} \cdot \vec{M} = -\,2\,a F$.

The result holds for every line parallel to $\hat{\imath}$, whatever its distance from the two forces, and the value $0$ would be obtained only for an axis orthogonal to $\vec{M}$. The value $-\,4\,a F$ is obtained by assigning the arm $2a$ to each of the two forces, while $2a$ is the arm of the couple as a whole.
