A system of applied vectors has resultant $\vec{R} = 3F\,\hat{k}$ and resultant moment $\vec{M}_O = a F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{k})$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the magnitude of the minimum moment?

- $\sqrt{5}\,a F$
- $2\,a F$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,a F$
- $a F$

## Explanation

The minimum moment is the resultant moment about the points of the central axis, and by the theorem on the equation of the central axis its magnitude equals $\dfrac{|I|}{|\vec{R}|}$.

The scalar invariant equals
$$
I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = 3F\,\hat{k} \cdot a F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{k}) = 6\,a F^2 ,
$$
therefore
$$
|\vec{M}_Q| = \frac{6\,a F^2}{3F} = 2\,a F .
$$
The value $\sqrt{5}\,a F$ is the magnitude of $\vec{M}_O$, which is greater because about a pole outside the central axis the moment acquires a component orthogonal to $\vec{R}$.
