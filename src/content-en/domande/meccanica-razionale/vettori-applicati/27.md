A couple has moment $\mathcal{\vec{M}} = 12\,a F\,\hat{k}$ and is formed by two applied vectors of magnitude $3F$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the arm of the couple?

- $2a$
- $36\,a$
- $\dfrac{a}{4}$
- $4a$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the moment of a couple the magnitude of the moment equals $|\vec{v}|\,d$, where $d$ is the distance between the two lines of action.

From the equality $12\,a F = 3F \cdot d$ one finds
$$
d = \frac{12\,a F}{3F} = 4a .
$$
The value $2a$ would be obtained by dividing by the sum of the two magnitudes, whereas the magnitude of only one of the two vectors appears in the formula, since the moment has already been computed as the product of that magnitude by the arm.
