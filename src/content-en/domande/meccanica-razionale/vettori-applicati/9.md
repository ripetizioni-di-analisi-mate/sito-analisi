The applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\imath})$, $(A, \lambda F\,\hat{\imath})$ with $A - O = a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $(B, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $B - O = a\,\hat{k}$ are given, where $\lambda$ is a real parameter, as the diagram in the figure shows, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. For which value of $\lambda$ is the system equivalent to a single applied vector?

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="0,0;1.4,0,A;0,1.4,B" forze="-0.55 -0.396,-0.55,-0.396,;0.85 -0.396,-0.55,-0.396,;1.1 1.4,1.1,0," etichette="0.2,-0.3,O;-0.85,-0.24,F;0.58,-0.26,λF;1.3,1.46,F" riferimento=0 titolo="The three applied vectors, with the coefficient $\lambda$ of the second as a parameter"}

- $\lambda = -\,1$
- $\lambda = 1$
- $\lambda = 0$
- $\lambda = 2$

## Explanation

A system whose resultant differs from the zero vector is equivalent to a single applied vector if and only if the scalar invariant vanishes.

One obtains
$$
\vec{R} = F\,\big[ (1 + \lambda)\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} \big] ,
\qquad
\vec{M}_O = -\,a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \lambda\,\hat{k}) ,
$$
therefore $I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = -\,a F^2\,(1 + \lambda)$. The condition $I = 0$ is satisfied for $\lambda = -\,1$, and for that value $\vec{R} = F\,\hat{\jmath}$ still differs from the zero vector, hence the reduction to a single applied vector is possible and the vector lies on the central axis.
