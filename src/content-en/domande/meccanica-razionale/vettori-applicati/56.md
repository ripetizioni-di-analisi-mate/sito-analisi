On the rigid triangular plate in the figure, with vertices $O$, $A$ and $B$ of coordinates $(0, 0)$, $(3a, 0)$ and $(0, 3a)$, the vectors $F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $O$, $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $A$ and $-F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $B$ act, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the resultant moment about the centroid $G$ of the plate?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 0,3" nodi="O,0,0;A,3,0;B,0,3" forze="O,1,0,F;A,0,1,F;B,-1,0,F" punti="1,1,G,baricentro" quote="O,A,3a,1.4;B,O,3a,1.4" titolo="The triangular plate with the three vectors and the centroid $G$"}

- $6\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $4\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $a F\,\hat{k}$
- $5\,a F\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The centroid of a triangle is the mean of its three vertices, hence $G$ has coordinates $(a, a)$.

About $O$ the three vectors give $0$, then $3a \cdot F = 3\,a F$ and finally $-\,3a \cdot (-F) = 3\,a F$, therefore $M_O = 6\,a F$; the resultant equals $\vec{R} = F\,\hat{\jmath}$. By the **change of pole formula** one obtains
$$
\vec{M}_G = \vec{M}_O + (O - G) \wedge \vec{R} = 6\,a F\,\hat{k} + (-\,a\,\hat{\imath} - a\,\hat{\jmath}) \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath} = 5\,a F\,\hat{k} .
$$
The value $6\,a F\,\hat{k}$ is the moment about $O$. The value $4\,a F\,\hat{k}$ is obtained by neglecting the vector applied at $O$, which has zero moment about $O$ but arm $a$ about $G$.
