On the rectangular cross-section of the retaining wall in the figure, of base $b$ and height $h$, with $O$ at the upstream corner of the base and $A$ at the downstream corner, of coordinates $(b, 0)$, act the weight $-\,W\,\hat{\jmath}$, applied at the centroid of the section, and the earth thrust $H\,\hat{\imath}$, applied on the upstream face at height $\dfrac{h}{3}$, with $W > 0$ and $H > 0$. For which value of $H$ does the line of action of the resultant pass through the corner $A$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,4 0,4" nodi="O,0,0;A,2,0" punti="1,2,G,baricentro" linee="-2.5,0 3.5,0;-2.5,4 0,4" forze="1 0.8,0,-1.2,;0 1.333,1.2,0," etichette="1.35,1.1,W;-0.7,1.6,H;-1.6,3.6,soil" quote="O,A,b,0.8;A,2 4,h,0.8" titolo="The cross-section of the wall, the weight applied at the centroid and the earth thrust"}

- $H = \dfrac{3\,W b}{2h}$
- $H = \dfrac{W b}{h}$
- $H = \dfrac{3\,W b}{h}$
- $H = \dfrac{W b}{2h}$

## Explanation

The system is planar and has resultant $H\,\hat{\imath} - W\,\hat{\jmath} \neq \vec{0}$, hence it is equivalent to the resultant alone applied at any point of its line of action. A point belongs to this line if and only if the resultant moment about that point vanishes, therefore one imposes $M_A = 0$.

The centroid has abscissa $\dfrac{b}{2}$, hence the weight has moment $\left( \dfrac{b}{2} - b \right)(-\,W) = \dfrac{W b}{2}$ about $A$. The thrust is applied at the point of coordinates $\left( 0,\ \dfrac{h}{3} \right)$, hence it has moment $-\dfrac{h}{3}\,H$. Therefore
$$
M_A = \frac{W b}{2} - \frac{H h}{3} = 0 ,
$$
which gives $H = \dfrac{3\,W b}{2h}$.

For larger values of $H$ the line of action of the resultant meets the line of the base beyond the corner $A$, that is outside the wall. The value $\dfrac{W b}{h}$ is obtained by applying the thrust at mid-height, $\dfrac{W b}{2h}$ by applying it at the top, and $\dfrac{3\,W b}{h}$ by applying the weight at $O$ instead of at the centroid.
