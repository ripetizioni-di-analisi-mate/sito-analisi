A system of applied vectors has resultant $\vec{R} = 2F\,\hat{\imath}$ and resultant moment $\vec{M}_O = 4\,a F\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the resultant moment about the points of the central axis?

- $\vec{0}$
- $4\,a F\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $2\,a F\,\hat{\imath}$
- $4\,a F\,\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The scalar invariant equals $I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = 2F\,\hat{\imath} \cdot 4\,a F\,\hat{\jmath} = 0$, since the two vectors are orthogonal.

By the theorem on the equation of the central axis the moment about the points of the central axis equals $\vec{M}_Q = \dfrac{I}{|\vec{R}|^2}\,\vec{R}$, therefore it is the zero vector.

The system is therefore equivalent to the single applied vector $(Q, \vec{R})$ with $Q$ on the central axis, and about every point of that axis the resultant moment vanishes.
