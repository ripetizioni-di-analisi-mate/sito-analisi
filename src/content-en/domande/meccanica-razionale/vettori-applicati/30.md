A system of applied vectors has resultant $\vec{R} = \vec{0}$ and resultant moment $\vec{M}_O = 3\,a F\,\hat{k}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. To which system is it equivalent?

- To the empty system
- To the single applied vector $(O, 3\,a F\,\hat{k})$
- To a couple of moment $3\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- To a vector applied at $O$ and to a couple whose moment is parallel to $\vec{R}$

## Explanation

Since the resultant vanishes, by the change of pole formula the resultant moment is the same about every pole, and by hypothesis it equals $3\,a F\,\hat{k} \neq \vec{0}$.

A couple is completely determined by its moment, therefore the system has the same zero resultant and the same moment as a couple of moment $3\,a F\,\hat{k}$; by the **reduction theorem** the two systems are therefore equivalent.

The system is not equivalent to the empty system, since a balanced system has zero resultant moment as well.
