A system of forces all parallel to $\hat{k}$, applied at arbitrary points of space, acts on a rigid body. Of the six scalar equations contained in the conditions $\vec{R} = \vec{0}$ and $\vec{M}_O = \vec{0}$, how many are not identically satisfied, that is impose an actual condition on the forces?

- $3$
- $6$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Every force has the form $c_i\,\hat{k}$, hence the resultant has components $R_x = R_y = 0$ whatever the coefficients, and of the three scalar equations of $\vec{R} = \vec{0}$ only $R_z = 0$ remains.

Every moment $(P_i - O) \wedge c_i\,\hat{k}$ is orthogonal to $\hat{k}$, therefore so is $\vec{M}_O$, and its component $M_z$ is zero for every system of this kind.

The three equations $R_z = 0$, $M_x = 0$ and $M_y = 0$ therefore remain. For this reason a body resting on three non-collinear vertical supports has its three reactions determined by the equilibrium conditions alone.
