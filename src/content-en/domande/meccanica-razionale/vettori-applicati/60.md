On the rigid square plate $OBCD$ in the figure, of side $a$, with $O$ at the origin and sides along the axes, four vectors of magnitude $F$ directed along the sides act: $F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $O$, $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $B$, $-F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $C$ and $-F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $D$. What is the resultant moment about the center $K$ of the square?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,2 0,2" nodi="O,0,0;B,2,0;C,2,2;D,0,2" forze="O,1,0,F;B,0,1,F;C,-1,0,F;D,0,-1,F" punti="1,1,K,centro" quote="O,B,a,1.4" titolo="The square plate with the four vectors along the sides"}

- $4\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $2\,a F\,\hat{k}$
- $a F\,\hat{k}$
- $\vec{0}$

## Explanation

The resultant equals $F\,\hat{\imath} + F\,\hat{\jmath} - F\,\hat{\imath} - F\,\hat{\jmath} = \vec{0}$, hence the system is equivalent to a **couple** and its moment is the same about every pole.

About the center $K$ every vector has its line of action along a side, at distance $\dfrac{a}{2}$ from $K$, and all four tend to produce a counterclockwise rotation, therefore
$$
M_K = 4 \cdot F \cdot \frac{a}{2} = 2\,a F .
$$
The same value is obtained about $O$, where only $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at $B$ and $-F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $C$ contribute, with $a F$ each. The value $4\,a F$ is obtained by using the side $a$ as the arm; the zero vector is obtained by confusing the zero resultant with equilibrium, which also requires a zero moment.
