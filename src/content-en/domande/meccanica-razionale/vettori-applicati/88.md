The beam $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$, has a hinge at $A$ and a roller at $B$, which exerts the vertical reaction $\Phi_B\,\hat{\jmath}$. A uniform load of intensity $q_0$ directed downward acts on the left half of the beam, with $q_0 > 0$. What is $\Phi_B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,3 0,0.8,0.8,q_0" tratteggi="3,-0.3 3,1.3" quote="A,B,ℓ,1.3" titolo="The simply supported beam with the uniform load on the left half"}

- $\dfrac{3\,q_0\,\ell}{8}$
- $\dfrac{q_0\,\ell}{4}$
- $\dfrac{q_0\,\ell}{2}$
- $\dfrac{q_0\,\ell}{8}$

## Explanation

The system formed by the load and the constraint reactions is balanced, hence its resultant moment about $A$ is zero. The pole $A$ is chosen because the reaction of the hinge has its line of action through $A$ and does not appear in the equation.

The load has resultant $\dfrac{q_0\,\ell}{2}$ applied at the midpoint of the loaded part, of abscissa $\dfrac{\ell}{4}$, therefore
$$
M_A = -\,\frac{q_0\,\ell}{2} \cdot \frac{\ell}{4} + \Phi_B\,\ell = 0 ,
$$
from which $\Phi_B = \dfrac{q_0\,\ell}{8}$.

The vertical reaction at $A$ then equals $\dfrac{q_0\,\ell}{2} - \dfrac{q_0\,\ell}{8} = \dfrac{3\,q_0\,\ell}{8}$, larger because the load is closer to $A$; the value $\dfrac{3\,q_0\,\ell}{8}$ for $\Phi_B$ is obtained by swapping the two reactions.
