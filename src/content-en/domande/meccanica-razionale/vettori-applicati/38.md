On the $x$ axis three vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ are applied, with coefficients $2F$, $F$ and $3F$, at the points of abscissa $0$, $2a$ and $4a$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the abscissa of the center of the system?

- $2a$
- $\dfrac{7a}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7a}{3}$
- $\dfrac{14a}{3}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the center of a system of parallel vectors the abscissa of the center is the average of the abscissas of the points of application weighted with the coefficients.

The sum of the coefficients equals $2F + F + 3F = 6F$, therefore
$$
x_C = \frac{2F \cdot 0 + F \cdot 2a + 3F \cdot 4a}{6F} = \frac{14a}{6} = \frac{7a}{3} .
$$
All the coefficients are positive, therefore the center falls between the first and the last abscissa, as the value found confirms. The simple arithmetic mean would give $2a$, which does not take the weights into account.
