An L-shaped bracket is clamped at the point $O$ of a wall and is formed by two horizontal arms of length $\ell$: the first goes from $O$ to the point $A$ with $A - O = \ell\,\hat{\imath}$, the second goes from $A$ to the point $B$ with $B - A = \ell\,\hat{\jmath}$. The $z$ axis is vertical and points upward, and a load of weight $-P\,\hat{k}$ hangs at $B$, with $P > 0$. Reducing the load at $O$, what is the moment of the transfer couple?

- $P\ell\,(-\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$
- $P\ell\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$
- $P\ell\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $P\ell\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$

## Explanation

The transfer couple at $O$ has as its moment the moment of the load about $O$, that is $(B - O) \wedge (-P\,\hat{k})$ with $B - O = \ell\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$. Since $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{k} = -\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{k} = \hat{\imath}$, one obtains
$$
\ell\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) \wedge (-P\,\hat{k}) = P\ell\,\hat{\jmath} - P\ell\,\hat{\imath} = P\ell\,(-\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}) .
$$
For the arm $OA$ the component along $\hat{\jmath}$, orthogonal to its axis, is a bending moment, while the component along $\hat{\imath}$, directed along its axis, is a twisting moment. The vector $P\ell\,\hat{\jmath}$ is the moment that would be obtained with the load applied at $A$, that is by neglecting the second arm.
