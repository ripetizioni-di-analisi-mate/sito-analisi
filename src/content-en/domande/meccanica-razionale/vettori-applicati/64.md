The tower crane in the figure has the vertical tower clamped at the base $O$. The suspended load, of weight $m g$, has a vertical line of action at distance $d$ to the right of the axis of the tower, and the wind exerts on the tower the horizontal force $H\,\hat{\imath}$ at height $h$, with $d > 0$, $h > 0$ and $H > 0$. Reducing the two forces at $O$, what is the moment of the total transfer couple?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;T,0,5,-;J,-1.5,5,-;E,3.5,5,-;L,3.5,3,-" aste="O-T;J-E" catene="E-L" vincoli="O,incastro,270" forze="3.5 1.8,0,-1.2,;0 2.5,1.2,0," etichette="3.85,2.2,mg;-0.7,2.85,H" quote="T,E,d,-0.6;O,0 2.5,h,1" titolo="The tower crane with the suspended load and the wind force"}

- $(H h - m g d)\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,(m g d + H h)\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,m g d\,\hat{k}$
- $(m g d + H h)\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

By the **theorem on the transfer of an applied vector** every force moved to $O$ must be accompanied by the couple whose moment is the moment of the force about $O$, and the couples add up.

The weight points vertically downward and has its line of action to the right of $O$, hence it has moment $d \cdot (-\,m g) = -\,m g d$. The wind force is horizontal, points to the right and is applied at height $h$, hence it has moment $-\,h \cdot H = -\,H h$. Therefore the total transfer couple has moment
$$
\mathcal{\vec{M}}_t = -\,(m g d + H h)\,\hat{k} ,
$$
and both forces tend to rotate the tower clockwise. The system reduced at $O$ is formed by this couple and by the vector $H\,\hat{\imath} - m g\,\hat{\jmath}$ applied at $O$. The value $(H h - m g d)\,\hat{k}$ is obtained with the wrong sign for the moment of the wind, and $-\,m g d\,\hat{k}$ by neglecting the wind.
