At the six vertices of a regular hexagon with center $O$, inscribed in a circle of radius $R$ in the plane $Oxy$, vectors parallel to $\hat{k}$ are applied: at the vertex $V_1$ with $V_1 - O = R\,\hat{\imath}$ the vector equals $2F\,\hat{k}$, at the other five vertices it equals $F\,\hat{k}$, with $F > 0$. Where is the center of the system?

::struttura{tratteggi="2,0 1,1.732 -1,1.732 -2,0 -1,-1.732 1,-1.732 2,0" punti="0,0,O,centro;2,0,V_1;1,1.732;-1,1.732;-2,0;-1,-1.732;1,-1.732" etichette="2.35,0.35,2F;1.3,2.05,F;-1.3,2.05,F;-2.4,0.35,F;-1.3,-2.05,F;1.3,-2.05,F" quote="0 0,2 0,R,0.5" titolo="View from above: the coefficients of the vectors parallel to $\hat{k}$ applied at the vertices of the hexagon"}

- At the point $O$
- At the point $C$ with $C - O = \dfrac{R}{7}\,\hat{\imath}$
- At the point $C$ with $C - O = \dfrac{2R}{7}\,\hat{\imath}$
- At the point $C$ with $C - O = \dfrac{R}{6}\,\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The system is split into two groups: six vectors $F\,\hat{k}$, one per vertex, and an additional vector $F\,\hat{k}$ at $V_1$. The first group has equal coefficients at points symmetric with respect to $O$, hence by the symmetry property its center is $O$, with total coefficient $6F$.

By additivity the center of the complete system is the center of the two vectors $6F\,\hat{k}$ at $O$ and $F\,\hat{k}$ at $V_1$, hence
$$
C - O = \frac{6F \cdot \vec{0} + F \cdot R\,\hat{\imath}}{7F} = \frac{R}{7}\,\hat{\imath} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{R}{6}$ is obtained by dividing by $6F$ instead of by the total coefficient $7F$. The value $\dfrac{2R}{7}$ is obtained by neglecting the other five vertices, whose weighted sum $F\,(V_2 - O) + \dots + F\,(V_6 - O) = -\,F R\,\hat{\imath}$ is different from the zero vector.
