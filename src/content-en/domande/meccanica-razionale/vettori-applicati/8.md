The applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\imath})$, $(A, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $A - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $(B, F\,\hat{\imath})$ with $B - O = a\,\hat{k}$ are given, as the diagram in the figure shows, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the scalar invariant of the system?

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="0,0;-0.7,-0.504,A;0,1.4,B" forze="-0.55 -0.396,-0.55,-0.396,;0.4 -0.504,1.1,0,;-0.55 1.004,-0.55,-0.396," etichette="0.2,-0.3,O;-0.85,-0.24,F;0.55,-0.92,F;-0.9,1.12,F" riferimento=0 titolo="The three vectors applied at $O$, $A$ and $B$"}

- $0$
- $2\,a F^2$
- $-\,a F^2$
- $a F^2$

## Explanation

The resultant equals $\vec{R} = F\,(2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$, since the vectors applied at $O$ and at $B$ are both directed along $\hat{\imath}$.

The vector applied at $O$ has zero moment about $O$, whereas the other two give $a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{\jmath} = a F\,\hat{k}$ and $a\,\hat{k} \wedge F\,\hat{\imath} = a F\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore $\vec{M}_O = a F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$. The scalar invariant therefore equals
$$
I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = F\,(2, 1, 0) \cdot a F\,(0, 1, 1) = a F^2 .
$$
Since $I \neq 0$ and $\vec{R} \neq \vec{0}$, the system is not equivalent to a single applied vector, but to a vector applied at a point of the central axis and to a couple whose moment is parallel to $\vec{R}$.
