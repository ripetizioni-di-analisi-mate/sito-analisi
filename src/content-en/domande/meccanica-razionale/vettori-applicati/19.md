In the plane $Oxy$ the applied vector $(P_1, \vec{v}_1)$ with $P_1 - O = 3a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{v}_1 = F\,\hat{\imath}$ acts, together with the applied vector $(P_2, \vec{v}_2)$ with $P_2 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{v}_2 = 2F\,\hat{\jmath}$, where $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the distance from $O$ of the line of action of the resultant?

- $\dfrac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a$
- $\dfrac{a}{5}$
- $\dfrac{a}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a$

## Explanation

The resultant equals $\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$, of magnitude $F\sqrt{5}$, and differs from the zero vector.

The resultant moment about $O$ is the sum of the moments of the two vectors, each computed with the formula $M = x\,v_y - y\,v_x$, therefore
$$
M_O = \big( 0 - 3a \cdot F \big) + \big( 2a \cdot 2F - 0 \big) = a F .
$$
The system is planar, hence the scalar invariant vanishes and the system is equivalent to the resultant alone applied on its own line of action, whose distance from $O$ is $\dfrac{|M_O|}{|\vec{R}|}$, that is
$$
d = \frac{a F}{F\sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a .
$$
The vector $\vec{v}_1$ is horizontal, but it has arm $3a$ about $O$, therefore its moment differs from zero; neglecting it one would obtain $\dfrac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a$.
