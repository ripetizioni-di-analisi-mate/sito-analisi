A planar system of applied vectors has resultant $\vec{R} = 2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ and resultant moment $\vec{M}_O = 6\,a F\,\hat{k}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. Which point belongs to the central axis?

- The point of coordinates $(0, 3a)$
- The point of coordinates $(-\,3a, 0)$
- The point of coordinates $(3a, 0)$
- The point of coordinates $(6a, 0)$

## Explanation

The system is planar, therefore the scalar invariant vanishes and the central axis is the line of action of the resultant, of equation $x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_O$.

Substituting the components one obtains $2F\,x = 6\,a F$, therefore $x = 3a$, hence the central axis is the vertical line of abscissa $3a$, parallel to $\vec{R}$. Among the proposed points, the point of coordinates $(3a, 0)$ belongs to that line.

The same result follows from the formula $Q - O = \dfrac{\vec{R} \wedge \vec{M}_O}{|\vec{R}|^2}$, which gives $Q - O = \dfrac{12\,a F^2\,\hat{\imath}}{4F^2} = 3a\,\hat{\imath}$.
