The applied vector $(P, \vec{v})$ has $P - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{v} = F\,\hat{k}$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the axial moment about the line $r$ through $O$ with unit vector $\hat{u} = \dfrac{\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}}{\sqrt{2}}$?

- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,a F$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,a F$
- $-\,a F$
- $0$

## Explanation

The axial moment is computed by taking as pole a point of the line, and the origin $O$ belongs to $r$.

The moment about $O$ equals $\vec{M}_O = a\,\hat{\imath} \wedge F\,\hat{k} = -\,a F\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore
$$
M_r = \hat{u} \cdot \vec{M}_O = \frac{(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) \cdot (-\,a F\,\hat{\jmath})}{\sqrt{2}} = -\,\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,a F .
$$
The value $-\,a F$ is obtained by using the vector $\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}$ without dividing it by its magnitude, whereas the unit vector of the axis appears in the definition of axial moment.
