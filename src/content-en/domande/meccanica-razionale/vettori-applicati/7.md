The applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\imath})$ and $(P, 2F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $P - O = 2a\,\hat{k}$ are given, as the diagram in the figure shows, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the magnitude of the resultant moment about the points of the central axis?

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="0,0;0,1.6,P" forze="-0.5 -0.36,-0.5,-0.36,;1.4 1.6,1.4,0," etichette="0.2,-0.3,O;-0.8,-0.22,F;1.6,1.66,2F" riferimento=0 titolo="The two applied vectors, with skew lines of action"}

- $4\,a F$
- $\dfrac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a F$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,a F$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}\,a F$

## Explanation

The resultant equals $\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$ and the resultant moment about $O$ equals $\vec{M}_O = 2a\,\hat{k} \wedge 2F\,\hat{\jmath} = -\,4\,a F\,\hat{\imath}$, since the first vector is applied at $O$.

The scalar invariant is therefore $I = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}_O = -\,4\,a F^2$, different from zero, hence the system is not equivalent to a single applied vector. By the **theorem on the equation of the central axis** the moment about the points of the central axis has magnitude
$$
|\vec{M}_Q| = \frac{|I|}{|\vec{R}|} = \frac{4\,a F^2}{F\sqrt{5}} = \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a F .
$$
The value $4\,a F$ is the magnitude of $\vec{M}_O$, which is greater because the moment is minimum precisely at the points of the central axis.
