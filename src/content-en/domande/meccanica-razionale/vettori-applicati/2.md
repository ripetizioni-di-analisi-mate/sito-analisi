The applied vector $(P, \vec{v})$ has $P - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$ and $\vec{v} = F\,(2\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} - \hat{k})$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. What is the axial moment about the $z$ axis?

- $-\,3\,a F$
- $3\,a F$
- $a F$
- $0$

## Explanation

The point $O$ belongs to the $z$ axis, therefore the axial moment about that axis is the third component of $\vec{M}_O$.

From the cross product in components one obtains
$$
M_z = x_P\,v_y - y_P\,v_x = a\,(-F) - a\,(2F) = -\,3\,a F .
$$
The complete moment equals $\vec{M}_O = 3\,a F\,(\hat{\jmath} - \hat{k})$, therefore it differs from the zero vector. The value $0$ would be obtained only for an axis coplanar with the line of action, as happens for the line through $O$ with unit vector $\dfrac{\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}}{\sqrt{3}}$, which contains the point $P$.
