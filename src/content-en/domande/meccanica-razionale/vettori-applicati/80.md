A wheel with center $O$, radius $R$ and weight $W$ rests on the horizontal ground against a step of height $\dfrac{R}{2}$, which touches it at the edge $E$, as in the figure. The horizontal force $H\,\hat{\imath}$, directed towards the step, is applied at the center, with $W > 0$ and $H > 0$. The wheel is about to lift off the ground when the force of the ground vanishes and the system formed by the weight, the force $H\,\hat{\imath}$ and the force of the edge is balanced. For which value of $H$ does this happen?

::struttura{cerchi="0,1,1" linee="-1.8,0 0.866,0 0.866,0.5 2.2,0.5" punti="0,1,O;0.866,0.5,E" forze="0 0.2,0,-0.8,;0 1,0.8,0," etichette="0.25,0.35,W;-0.55,0.82,H" quote="0 1,-1 1,R,0.3" titolo="The wheel against the step, with the weight and the horizontal force applied at the center"}

- $H = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,W$
- $H = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,W$
- $H = 2W$
- $H = \sqrt{3}\,W$

## Explanation

The system formed by the weight, the force $H\,\hat{\imath}$ and the force of the edge is balanced, hence its resultant moment about $E$ vanishes. The pole $E$ is chosen because the force of the edge, whose direction is unknown, has zero moment about $E$.

The edge lies $\dfrac{R}{2}$ below the center and at distance $R$ from it, hence its horizontal distance from the center equals $\sqrt{R^2 - \dfrac{R^2}{4}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R$ and $O - E = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{R}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$. The weight and the force $H\,\hat{\imath}$ are applied at $O$, therefore
$$
M_E = \left( -\,\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R \right)(-\,W) - \frac{R}{2}\,H = 0 ,
$$
which gives $H = \sqrt{3}\,W$.

The arm of the weight about $E$ is the horizontal distance $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R$ and the arm of $H$ is the difference in height $\dfrac{R}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,W$ is obtained by swapping the two arms, $2W$ by using the radius as the arm of the weight and $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,W$ by using the radius as the arm of $H$.
