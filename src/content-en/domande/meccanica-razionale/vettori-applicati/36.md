A planar system of applied vectors, with its points and its vectors in the plane $Oxy$, has resultant $\vec{R} = -\,4F\,\hat{\jmath}$ and resultant moment $M_O = 8\,a F$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. At which abscissa does the line of action of the resultant meet the $x$ axis?

- $x = -\,2a$
- $x = 2a$
- $x = -\dfrac{a}{2}$
- $x = 32\,a$

## Explanation

The line of action of the resultant has equation $x\,R_y - y\,R_x = M_O$, and for vertical loads, that is with $R_x = 0$, it reduces to the vertical line of abscissa $x = \dfrac{M_O}{R_y}$.

Substituting the data one obtains
$$
x = \frac{8\,a F}{-\,4F} = -\,2a ,
$$
therefore the line of action lies to the left of the origin. The sign is consistent with the moment, since a resultant directed downwards and applied to the left of $O$ produces a counterclockwise moment about $O$.
