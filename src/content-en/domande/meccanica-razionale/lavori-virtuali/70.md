The Roberval balance in the figure is an articulated parallelogram: the two horizontal arms, of length $2a$, are hinged at their midpoints to a fixed vertical column, and their ends are connected by two vertical posts with smooth hinges. A horizontal pan is welded to each post. A body of mass $m$ is placed on the left pan at distance $d$ from the post, and a body of mass $M$ on the right pan at distance $e$ from its post; rods and pans have negligible mass. For which value of $M$ is the balance in equilibrium?

- $M = \dfrac{m\,d}{e}$
- $M = m$
- $M = \dfrac{m\,(a + d)}{a + e}$
- $M = \dfrac{m\,a}{a + d}$

## Explanation

The parallelogram has one degree of freedom, the rotation $\delta\varphi$ of the two arms about the hinges of the column. The posts stay parallel to the column, hence they translate vertically together with the welded pans, and every point of the left pan goes up by $a\,\delta\varphi$ while every point of the right pan goes down by $a\,\delta\varphi$, whatever its distance from the post. The virtual work of the two weights is therefore
$$
\delta L^{(a)} = -m g\,a\,\delta\varphi + M g\,a\,\delta\varphi ,
$$
and equilibrium requires $\delta L^{(a)} = 0$ for every $\delta\varphi$, hence $M = m$. The distances $d$ and $e$ do not appear, and for this reason the Roberval balance gives the same reading wherever the load is placed on the pan. The value $\dfrac{m\,d}{e}$ is obtained by treating the balance as a lever with arms $d$ and $e$.
