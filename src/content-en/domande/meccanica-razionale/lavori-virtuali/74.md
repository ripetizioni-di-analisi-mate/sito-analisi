A material point $P$ of mass $m$ slides along a smooth circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, placed in a vertical plane; $\vartheta$ is the angle between $P - O$ and the downward vertical, measured counterclockwise. The point is acted upon by the weight and by a constant horizontal force $F\,\hat{\imath}$, with $F > 0$, as in the figure. Which equilibrium configuration lies in the lower half of the guide?

- $\vartheta = \arctan\dfrac{m g}{F}$
- $\vartheta = \arctan\dfrac{F}{m g}$
- $\vartheta = \arcsin\dfrac{F}{m g}$
- $\vartheta = -\arctan\dfrac{F}{m g}$

## Explanation

The position of the point is $P - O = R\,(\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath})$, and the constant force $F\,\hat{\imath}$ has potential $F\,x$, hence the potential of the force system is
$$
U(\vartheta) = m g R\cos\vartheta + F R\sin\vartheta .
$$
The guide is smooth, fixed and bilateral, therefore equilibrium is equivalent to $U'(\vartheta) = -m g R\sin\vartheta + F R\cos\vartheta = 0$, that is, $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{F}{m g}$. The solutions in $[0, 2\pi)$ are $\vartheta = \arctan\dfrac{F}{m g}$, in the lower half and shifted in the direction of the force, and the configuration opposite with respect to $O$, which is obtained by adding $\pi$ and lies in the upper half. The value $-\arctan\dfrac{F}{m g}$ moves the point to the side opposite to the one toward which the force pushes.
