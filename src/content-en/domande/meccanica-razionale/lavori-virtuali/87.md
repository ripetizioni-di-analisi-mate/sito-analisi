A rod $OA$ is hinged at $O$ and rotates in the vertical plane. A fixed support placed under the rod prevents it from going below the horizontal position and allows it to rise. In which configuration are the virtual displacements of the rod not all reversible?

- In the horizontal configuration, in which the rod touches the support
- In all the configurations with the rod raised
- In no configuration, since the hinge is a bilateral constraint
- In the vertical configuration, with the rod pointing upward

## Explanation

The support is a unilateral constraint, expressed by the inequality $\vartheta \geq 0$ on the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the horizontal. In the configurations with $\vartheta > 0$ the inequality is satisfied strictly, hence they are **ordinary** and every small rotation in either direction is virtual. In the horizontal configuration $\vartheta = 0$ the inequality holds with equality, therefore the configuration is a **boundary** one, the rotation that lifts the rod is virtual and the opposite one, which would bring it below the support, is not. In this configuration equilibrium is studied with the inequality $\delta L^{(a)} \leq 0$ for the rotations with $\delta\vartheta \geq 0$ only.
