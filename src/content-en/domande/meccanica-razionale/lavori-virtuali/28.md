A plane rigid body is acted upon by a system of forces with resultant $\vec{R} = R\,\hat{\imath}$ and resultant moment $\vec{M}_\Omega = \mathcal{M}\,\hat{k}$ with respect to one of its points $\Omega$. The rigid body undergoes the virtual displacement identified by $\delta\Omega = \delta a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\delta\vec{\vartheta} = \delta\vartheta\,\hat{k}$. What is the virtual work of the system of forces?

- $R\,\delta a + \mathcal{M}\,\delta\vartheta$
- $R\,\delta a$
- $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\vartheta$
- $0$

## Explanation

The virtual work of a system of forces on a rigid body depends on the forces only through the resultant and the resultant moment, and is
$$
\delta L = \vec{R} \cdot \delta\Omega + \vec{M}_\Omega \cdot \delta\vec{\vartheta} .
$$
The resultant is directed along $\hat{\imath}$ whereas the virtual translation is directed along $\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the first scalar product is zero because the two vectors are orthogonal. The resultant moment and the virtual rotation are both directed along $\hat{k}$, hence the second scalar product is $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\vartheta$ and
$$
\delta L = \mathcal{M}\,\delta\vartheta .
$$
