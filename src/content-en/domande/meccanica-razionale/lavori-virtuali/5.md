Two homogeneous rods $OA$ and $AB$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $A$ and to the ground at $O$ with smooth hinges, and move in the vertical plane. A constant couple of moment $\mathcal{M} > 0$ in the counterclockwise direction acts on the second rod, as in the figure, and the angles $\vartheta$ and $\varphi$ are those that the two rods form with the downward vertical. Which condition on the data is necessary and sufficient for the system to admit equilibrium configurations?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.1,-2.2;B,2.9,-3.1" aste="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" cerniere="A" punti="0.55,-1.1,G_1,baricentro;2,-2.65,G_2,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.6;1.1,-2.2 1.1,-4" coppie="2.6 -2,1,ℳ" etichette="0.3,-1.1,ϑ;1.5,-3.3,φ" titolo="Double pendulum with a constant couple applied to the second rod"}

- $\mathcal{M} \leq m g \ell$
- $2\,\mathcal{M} \leq 3\,m g \ell$
- $\mathcal{M} \leq 2\,m g \ell$
- $2\,\mathcal{M} \leq m g \ell$

## Explanation

The heights of the two centers of mass are $y_{G_1} = -\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$ and $y_{G_2} = -\ell\cos\vartheta - \dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\varphi$, whereas the constant couple contributes to the potential with the term $\mathcal{M}\,\varphi$, hence
$$
U(\vartheta, \varphi) = \frac{3}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta + \frac{1}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\varphi + \mathcal{M}\,\varphi .
$$
The constraints are holonomic, fixed, ideal and bilateral, therefore equilibrium is equivalent to the stationarity of the potential and the system of the two conditions is
$$
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac{3}{2}\,m g \ell\sin\vartheta = 0 \\[6pt]
\mathcal{M} - \dfrac{1}{2}\,m g \ell\sin\varphi = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The second equation gives $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{2\,\mathcal{M}}{m g \ell}$, which admits a solution if and only if $\dfrac{2\,\mathcal{M}}{m g \ell} \leq 1$, that is, $2\,\mathcal{M} \leq m g \ell$. The first equation, on the other hand, is satisfied by $\vartheta = 0$ and by $\vartheta = \pi$ for every value of the data, therefore it imposes no condition.
