A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $3\ell$, is placed horizontally in the vertical plane, with a smooth hinge at $A$ and a smooth roller on a horizontal plane at $B$. A constant couple of moment $\mathcal{M}$ in the counterclockwise direction acts on the rod, as in the figure. What is the reaction of the roller?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4.2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="2.1,0,G,baricentro" forze="2.1 -1.4,0,-0.6,mg" coppie="3.2 0.55,1,ℳ" etichette="3.2,1.1, " quote="A,B,3 ℓ,1.9" titolo="Supported rod with a constant couple applied"}

- $\dfrac{m g}{2} + \dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{3\,\ell}$
- $m g - \dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{3\,\ell}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2} - \dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2} - \dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{3\,\ell}$

## Explanation

The roller is removed and the reaction $\Phi_B\,\hat{\jmath}$ is applied at $B$ as an active force; the rod so released can rotate about the hinge $A$ and the system has one degree of freedom. For the virtual rotation $\delta\varphi$ about $A$ one has $\delta y_B = 3\ell\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta y_G = \dfrac{3\ell}{2}\,\delta\varphi$, therefore the virtual work of the active forces and of the reaction is
$$
\delta L = \Phi_B\,3\ell\,\delta\varphi - m g\,\frac{3\ell}{2}\,\delta\varphi + \mathcal{M}\,\delta\varphi = 0 .
$$
The variation $\delta\varphi$ is arbitrary, hence one obtains
$$
\Phi_B = \frac{m g}{2} - \frac{\mathcal{M}}{3\,\ell} ,
$$
directed upwards if $\mathcal{M} < \dfrac{3\,m g \ell}{2}$. The minus sign in front of the term with $\mathcal{M}$ follows from the fact that the couple is counterclockwise and the work of the reaction of the roller for the same rotation is positive.
