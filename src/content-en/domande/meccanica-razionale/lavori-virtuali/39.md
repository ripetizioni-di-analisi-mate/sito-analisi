A particle of mass $m$ slides without friction along a fixed vertical guide and is connected to the fixed point $O$ of the guide by an ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length, as in the figure. At equilibrium, what is the height $y$ of the particle, measured from $O$ upwards?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;P,0,-1.8" linee="-0.45,0 0.45,0;0.00,0.00 0.00,-0.25 0.24,-0.38 -0.24,-0.64 0.24,-0.90 -0.24,-1.16 0.24,-1.42 0.00,-1.55 0.00,-1.80" tratteggi="0,0.3 0,-2.7" punti="0,-1.8" forze="0 -2.55,0,-0.55,mg" etichette="0.7,-0.9,k;-0.65,-1.8,y" titolo="Particle on a vertical guide, pulled back towards $O$ by a spring of constant $k$"}

- $-\dfrac{m g}{k}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{k}$
- $-\dfrac{2\,m g}{k}$
- $-\dfrac{m g}{2\,k}$

## Explanation

The spring has zero natural length and one end at the fixed point $O$, therefore its length is $|y|$ and its potential is $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,y^2$; the potential of the weight is $-m g\,y$. The potential of the system is hence
$$
U(y) = -\frac{1}{2}\,k\,y^2 - m g\,y .
$$
The constraint is fixed, ideal and bilateral, therefore equilibrium is equivalent to $U'(y) = 0$, that is, $-k\,y - m g = 0$, whence
$$
y = -\frac{m g}{k} .
$$
The value is negative, therefore the equilibrium position lies below $O$, where the spring pulls upwards with intensity equal to the weight.
