The beam in the figure consists of the rod $AB$, of length $\ell$, clamped at $A$, and of the rod $BC$, of length $2\ell$, connected to the first one by the internal hinge $B$ and resting at $C$ on a roller with vertical reaction; the rods are horizontal and have negligible mass. The vertical load $P$ directed downward acts at the midpoint of $BC$. What is the reaction moment $\Gamma_A$ of the clamp?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" cerniere="B" forze="4 0,0,-0.9,P" quote="A,B,ℓ,1;B,C,2 ℓ,1" titolo="Beam with a clamp at $A$, an internal hinge at $B$ and a roller at $C$, loaded at the midpoint of $BC$"}

- $P\,\ell$, counterclockwise
- $2\,P\,\ell$, counterclockwise
- $\dfrac{P\,\ell}{2}$, clockwise
- $\dfrac{P\,\ell}{2}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The clamp is replaced by a hinge at $A$ and the unknown couple $\Gamma_A$, positive counterclockwise, is applied to the rod $AB$; the released system has one degree of freedom. In the counterclockwise virtual rotation $\delta\varphi$ of $AB$ about $A$, the hinge $B$ goes up by $\ell\,\delta\varphi$, while the rod $BC$ rotates about $C$, which can only slide horizontally, therefore its midpoint goes up by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\delta\varphi$. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
\Gamma_A\,\delta\varphi - P\,\frac{\ell}{2}\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
$$
hence $\Gamma_A = \dfrac{P\,\ell}{2}$, in the **counterclockwise** direction. The cardinal equations confirm the result, since the supported rod $BC$ transmits at $B$ the force $\dfrac{P}{2}$, which on the rod $AB$ has lever arm $\ell$ about $A$. The value $2\,P\,\ell$ is obtained by neglecting the internal hinge and treating the beam as a single cantilever.
