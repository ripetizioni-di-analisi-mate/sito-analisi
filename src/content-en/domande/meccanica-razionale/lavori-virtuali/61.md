A spool consists of two outer disks of radius $R$ and a coaxial hub of radius $r < R$, welded together, and it rolls without slipping on a horizontal plane, resting on the outer disks. A string wound on the hub leaves it from its lower side and is pulled horizontally with the constant force $F\,\hat{\imath}$, as in the figure. Taking the abscissa $x$ of the center as the Lagrangian coordinate, what is the Lagrangian component $Q_x$ of the force?

- $F\left( 1 + \dfrac{r}{R} \right)$
- $F$
- $F\left( 1 - \dfrac{r}{R} \right)$
- $\dfrac{F\,r}{R}$

## Explanation

Pure rolling makes the contact point $H$ the instantaneous center of rotation, therefore the virtual displacements of the points of the spool are those of a rotation about $H$, with magnitude proportional to the distance from $H$. The center is at distance $R$ from $H$ and moves by $\delta x$, while the point $D$ of the hub from which the string leaves lies on the same vertical, at distance $R - r$ from $H$. The virtual displacement of $D$ is therefore horizontal and equals
$$
\delta D = \frac{R - r}{R}\,\delta x\,\hat{\imath} .
$$
The force of the string is applied at $D$, hence $Q_x = F\,\hat{\imath} \cdot \dfrac{\partial D}{\partial x} = F\left( 1 - \dfrac{r}{R} \right)$, which is positive, and the virtual work of the force is positive when the spool advances in the direction of the string. The value $F\left( 1 + \dfrac{r}{R} \right)$ corresponds to the string leaving the upper side of the hub, at distance $R + r$ from $H$.
