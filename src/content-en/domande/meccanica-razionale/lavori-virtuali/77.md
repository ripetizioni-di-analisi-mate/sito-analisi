A material point $P$ of mass $m$ moves freely in the vertical plane $Oxy$ and is connected by two ideal springs of zero natural length to the fixed points $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (2\ell, 0)$; the spring attached at $A$ has constant $k$, the one attached at $B$ has constant $3k$, as in the figure. What is the equilibrium position of $P$?

- $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{2},\ -\dfrac{m g}{4\,k} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3\,\ell}{2},\ -\dfrac{m g}{4\,k} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{3\,\ell}{2},\ -\dfrac{m g}{3\,k} \right)$
- $\left( \ell,\ -\dfrac{m g}{4\,k} \right)$

## Explanation

The point is free in the plane, hence it has two degrees of freedom, with Lagrangian coordinates $x$ and $y$. The potential is
$$
U(x, y) = -\frac{1}{2}\,k\,(x^2 + y^2) - \frac{3}{2}\,k\,\big( (x - 2\ell)^2 + y^2 \big) - m g\,y ,
$$
and equilibrium is equivalent to the vanishing of the two partial derivatives, that is, to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
-k\,x - 3\,k\,(x - 2\ell) = 0 \\[4pt]
-4\,k\,y - m g = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first equation, which comes from the derivative with respect to $x$, gives $x = \dfrac{3\,\ell}{2}$, closer to $B$, where the stiffer spring is attached; the second gives $y = -\dfrac{m g}{4\,k}$. The abscissa $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ is obtained by exchanging the constants of the two springs.
