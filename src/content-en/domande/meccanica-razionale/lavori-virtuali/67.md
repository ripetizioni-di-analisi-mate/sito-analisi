Two material points $P_1 = (0, 0)$ and $P_2 = (\ell, 0)$ are connected by a rigid rod of negligible mass, hinged to the two points. Which pair of virtual displacements is compatible with the constraint, for arbitrary $\varepsilon$ and $\eta$?

- $\delta P_1 = \varepsilon\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\delta P_2 = 2\,\varepsilon\,\hat{\imath}$
- $\delta P_1 = \varepsilon\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\delta P_2 = \varepsilon\,\hat{\imath}$
- $\delta P_1 = \varepsilon\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\delta P_2 = -\varepsilon\,\hat{\imath}$
- $\delta P_1 = \varepsilon\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\delta P_2 = \varepsilon\,\hat{\imath} + \eta\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The constraint requires the distance between the two points to stay constant, that is, $|P_2 - P_1|^2 = \ell^2$, and differentiating one obtains the condition on the virtual displacements
$$
(P_2 - P_1) \cdot (\delta P_2 - \delta P_1) = 0 .
$$
Since $P_2 - P_1 = \ell\,\hat{\imath}$, the condition requires the two points to have the same horizontal component of the displacement, while the vertical components are free. The pair $\delta P_1 = \varepsilon\,\hat{\imath}$, $\delta P_2 = \varepsilon\,\hat{\imath} + \eta\,\hat{\jmath}$ satisfies the condition, and corresponds to a horizontal translation combined with a rotation of the rod about $P_1$. The same condition makes zero the virtual work of the two opposite forces, directed along the rod, that the rod exerts on the two points, therefore the rigidity constraint is ideal.
