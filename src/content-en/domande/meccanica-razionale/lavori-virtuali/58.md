A straight rod rotates in the horizontal plane about its fixed end $O$ with an assigned constant angular velocity $\omega$, so that at time $t$ it has the direction of the unit vector $\hat{u}(t) = \cos\omega t\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\omega t\,\hat{\jmath}$; let $\hat{n}(t) = -\sin\omega t\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\omega t\,\hat{\jmath}$ be the unit vector orthogonal to it. A ring $P$ slides without friction along the rod, and the Lagrangian coordinate is the distance $s = |P - O|$. What is the virtual displacement of the ring starting from the configuration occupied at time $t$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;E,3.4,1.7,-" aste="O-E" vincoli="O,cerniera" punti="2,1,P" quote="O,2 1,s,-0.5" coppie="0.9 1.3,1,ω" frecce="2,1,2.67,1.34,u;2,1,1.66,1.67,n" titolo="Ring on a rod that rotates in the horizontal plane with assigned angular velocity $\omega$"}

- $\delta P = \delta s\,\hat{u} + s\,\omega\,\delta t\,\hat{n}$
- $\delta P = \delta s\,\hat{u}$
- $\delta P = s\,\omega\,\delta t\,\hat{n}$
- $\delta P = \delta s\,\hat{n}$

## Explanation

The position of the ring is $P - O = s\,\hat{u}(t)$, and the constraint is moving, because the direction of the rod depends explicitly on time. The virtual displacement is obtained by varying the Lagrangian coordinate with time held fixed, hence
$$
\delta P = \frac{\partial P}{\partial s}\,\delta s = \delta s\,\hat{u} .
$$
The possible displacement in an interval $dt$ also contains the term $\dfrac{\partial P}{\partial t}\,dt = s\,\omega\,dt\,\hat{n}$, due to the rotation of the rod, therefore with this moving constraint the possible displacements and the virtual ones are different. The virtual displacement is parallel to the rod, and for this reason the reaction of the smooth rod, which is orthogonal to the rod, does zero virtual work.
