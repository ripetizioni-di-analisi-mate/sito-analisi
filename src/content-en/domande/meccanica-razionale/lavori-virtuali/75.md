A rigid rod $AB$, of negligible mass and length $R\sqrt{2}$, has its ends constrained to slide on a smooth circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, placed in a vertical plane. A material point of mass $m$ is fixed at the end $A$ and one of mass $3m$ at the end $B$, as in the figure. Denoting by $\beta$ the angle between the rod and the horizontal, which equilibrium configuration does the system take with the rod below the center $O$?

- $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$, with $A$ lower than $B$
- $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{3}$, with $B$ lower than $A$
- $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{4}$, with $B$ lower than $A$
- $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$, with $B$ lower than $A$

## Explanation

The active force system consists of the two weights, hence the potential is $U = -4\,m g\,y_G$, where $G$ is the center of mass of the two points, and equilibrium requires $y_G$ to be stationary. The rod has length $R\sqrt{2}$, therefore it subtends a right angle and its midpoint $H$ is at distance $\dfrac{R}{\sqrt{2}}$ from $O$, while $G$ lies on the rod at distance $\dfrac{3}{4}\,R\sqrt{2}$ from $A$, that is, at distance $\dfrac{R\sqrt{2}}{4}$ from $H$ toward $B$. The system moves by rotating rigidly about $O$ and $|G - O|$ is constant, so $y_G$ is stationary when $G - O$ is vertical, and with the rod below $O$ the point $G$ lies on the downward vertical. In that configuration $OH$, perpendicular to the rod, forms the angle $\beta$ with the vertical, so that
$$
\tan\beta = \frac{|G - H|}{|H - O|} = \frac{R\sqrt{2}}{4} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{R} = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
The point $G$ is below $O$ and lies on the side of $B$ with respect to $H$, therefore $B$, which carries the larger mass, is lower than $A$.
