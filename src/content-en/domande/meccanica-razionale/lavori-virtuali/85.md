A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is horizontal in the vertical plane. At $A$ it is constrained by a double-pendulum support that allows the end only vertical translation and prevents the rotation of the rod; at $B$ it rests on a roller with vertical reaction, as in the figure. What is the reaction moment $\Gamma_A$ of the double-pendulum support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" punti="1.6,0,G,baricentro" forze="1.6 -0.3,0,-0.7,mg" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.8" titolo="Rod with a double-pendulum support at $A$, which allows only vertical translation, and a roller at $B$"}

- $\dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$, counterclockwise
- $\dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$, clockwise
- $m g \ell$, clockwise
- $m g \ell$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

One removes the part of the constraint that prevents rotation and applies to the rod the unknown couple $\Gamma_A$, positive counterclockwise. In the released system $A$ can move only vertically and $B$ only horizontally, therefore the instantaneous center of rotation of the horizontal rod is the intersection of the horizontal line through $A$ with the vertical through $B$, that is, the point $B$. In the counterclockwise virtual rotation $\delta\varphi$ about $B$, the center of mass, which is at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from $B$ on the side of $A$, goes down by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\delta\varphi$, hence
$$
\Gamma_A\,\delta\varphi + m g\,\frac{\ell}{2}\,\delta\varphi = 0 .
$$
It follows that $\Gamma_A = -\dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$, that is, a moment of magnitude $\dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$ in the **clockwise** direction. The cardinal equations confirm the result, since the double-pendulum support exerts no vertical reaction, the roller carries the whole weight and the rotational equilibrium about $A$ is $\Gamma_A + m g \ell - \dfrac{m g \ell}{2} = 0$.
