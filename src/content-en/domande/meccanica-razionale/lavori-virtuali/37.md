A plane system is made of three rigid bodies connected to each other and to the ground with holonomic, fixed, ideal and bilateral constraints, and has one degree of freedom. How many scalar equilibrium equations does the principle of virtual work give?

- $3$
- $6$
- $9$
- $1$

## Explanation

The principle of virtual work requires the vanishing of the virtual work of the active force system for every virtual displacement. Writing the virtual work with the Lagrangian components gives $\delta L^{(a)} = \sum_h Q_h\,\delta q_h$, and the variations $\delta q_h$ are arbitrary and independent because the constraints are bilateral, therefore one has one scalar equation $Q_h = 0$ for each degree of freedom. The system has one degree of freedom, hence there is a single equation. The cardinal equations of statics, applied to the three bodies, would instead give nine equations, in which the constraint reactions would also appear.
