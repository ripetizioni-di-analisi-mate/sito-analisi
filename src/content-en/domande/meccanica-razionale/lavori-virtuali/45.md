A system with one degree of freedom, with ideal constraints, generalized coordinate $q$ and conservative active force system of potential $U(q)$ of class $C^1$, is subject to the unilateral constraint $q \geq 0$ and is in the boundary configuration $q = 0$. Which condition characterizes equilibrium in that configuration?

- $U'(0) = 0$
- $U'(0) \geq 0$
- $U''(0) \leq 0$
- $U'(0) \leq 0$

## Explanation

In the boundary configuration $q = 0$ the unilateral constraint admits only the virtual displacements with $\delta q \geq 0$, since negative variations would violate the inequality $q \geq 0$. By the theorem on equilibrium with unilateral constraints the configuration is an equilibrium configuration if and only if
$$
U'(0)\,\delta q \leq 0
\qquad
\text{for every } \delta q \geq 0 ,
$$
and this condition is equivalent to $U'(0) \leq 0$. The equality $U'(0) = 0$ is sufficient but not necessary, because a potential decreasing in the admissible direction keeps the system at rest against the constraint. In the ordinary configurations, in which the virtual displacements are reversible, the inequality becomes instead the equality to zero.
