- $F \leq \dfrac{m g}{4}$
- $F \leq \dfrac{m g}{2}$
- $F \leq m g$
- $F \leq \dfrac{3\,m g}{2}$

## Explanation

The supports are unilateral, therefore starting from the horizontal configuration the virtual displacements are the rotations about $D$ that lift $A$, identified by a parameter $\varepsilon \geq 0$. The center of mass is at distance $\dfrac{3\ell}{2}$ from $A$, that is, at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from $D$ on the side of $A$, whereas $B$ is at distance $\ell$ from $D$ on the opposite side, hence $\delta y_G = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\varepsilon$ and $\delta y_B = -\ell\,\varepsilon$. The virtual work of the active force system is then
$$
\delta L^{(a)} = -m g\,\frac{\ell}{2}\,\varepsilon + F\,\ell\,\varepsilon = \frac{\ell\,\varepsilon}{2}\,(2\,F - m g) .
$$
The configuration is a boundary one, therefore equilibrium is equivalent to $\delta L^{(a)} \leq 0$ for every $\varepsilon \geq 0$, that is, $F \leq \dfrac{m g}{2}$. For $F = \dfrac{m g}{2}$ the reaction of the support at $A$ vanishes and the rod is on the point of rotating about $D$.
