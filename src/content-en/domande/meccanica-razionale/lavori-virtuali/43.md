A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $4\ell$, is placed horizontally in the vertical plane, with a smooth hinge at $A$ and a smooth roller at $B$. At the point $P$, at distance $\ell$ from $A$, a particle of mass $2m$ is attached, as in the figure. What is the vertical component of the reaction of the hinge?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4.8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="1.2,0,P;2.4,0,G,baricentro" forze="1.2 -1.1,0,-0.55,2mg;2.4 -1.9,0,-0.55,mg" quote="A,1.2 0,ℓ,1.3;A,B,4 ℓ,2.6" titolo="Rod with a hinge at $A$, a roller at $B$ and a particle of mass $2m$ at $P$"}

- $2\,m g$
- $\dfrac{3\,m g}{2}$
- $m g$
- $3\,m g$

## Explanation

The hinge is removed and the reaction $\Phi_{Ay}\,\hat{\jmath}$ is applied at $A$ as an active force; the rod so released rotates about the roller $B$, which remains as the only effective constraint for this displacement. In the virtual rotation $\delta\varphi$ about $B$ one has $\delta y_A = -4\ell\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta y_G = -2\ell\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta y_P = -3\ell\,\delta\varphi$, because the three points are at distance $4\ell$, $2\ell$ and $3\ell$ from $B$ respectively. The virtual work vanishes, therefore
$$
-\Phi_{Ay}\,4\ell\,\delta\varphi + m g\,2\ell\,\delta\varphi + 2\,m g\,3\ell\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
$$
whence $\Phi_{Ay} = 2\,m g$, directed upwards. The total weight is $3\,m g$, therefore the reaction $m g$ remains for the roller, as the equation of equilibrium for the vertical translation confirms.
