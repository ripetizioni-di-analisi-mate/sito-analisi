In a holonomic system with fixed constraints the generalized coordinate $q_1$ is an angle and the generalized coordinate $q_2$ is a length. What dimensions do the Lagrangian components $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ of the force system have?

- $Q_1$ has the dimensions of a force and $Q_2$ those of a moment
- Both have the dimensions of a force
- Both have the dimensions of a moment
- $Q_1$ has the dimensions of a moment and $Q_2$ those of a force

## Explanation

The virtual work is written as $\delta L = Q_1\,\delta q_1 + Q_2\,\delta q_2$ and has the dimensions of an energy, that is, of a force times a length. The variation $\delta q_1$ is an angle, therefore it is dimensionless, hence $Q_1$ must have the dimensions of an energy, which are those of a moment. The variation $\delta q_2$ is a length, therefore $Q_2$ must have the dimensions of a force. The dimensional check of $Q_h$ is a quick way of verifying a computation, since to every angular coordinate there corresponds a moment and to every length coordinate a force.
