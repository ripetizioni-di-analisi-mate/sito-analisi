The cantilever $AB$ in the figure, of negligible mass and length $\ell$, is horizontal in the vertical plane, clamped at the end $A$ to a wall, and carries over its whole length a uniform load of intensity $q$ directed downward. What is the reactive moment $\Gamma_A$ of the clamp?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.8,q" quote="A,B,ℓ,1.3" titolo="Cantilever clamped at $A$ with a uniform load over its whole length"}

- $\dfrac{q\,\ell^2}{2}$, clockwise
- $\dfrac{q\,\ell^2}{2}$, counterclockwise
- $q\,\ell^2$, counterclockwise
- $\dfrac{q\,\ell^2}{8}$, counterclockwise

## Explanation

The clamp is replaced by a hinge at $A$ and the unknown couple $\Gamma_A$, positive counterclockwise, is applied to the cantilever; the cantilever thus released can rotate about $A$ and has one degree of freedom. In the counterclockwise virtual rotation $\delta\varphi$ the point of the cantilever at distance $s$ from $A$ rises by $s\,\delta\varphi$, hence the principle of virtual work gives
$$
\delta L = \Gamma_A\,\delta\varphi - \int_0^\ell q\,s\,\delta\varphi\,ds = \left( \Gamma_A - \frac{q\,\ell^2}{2} \right) \delta\varphi = 0 .
$$
The rotation $\delta\varphi$ is arbitrary, therefore $\Gamma_A = \dfrac{q\,\ell^2}{2}$, positive and hence counterclockwise. The value $q\,\ell^2$ is obtained by applying the resultant $q\,\ell$ at the end $B$ instead of at the midpoint, and $\dfrac{q\,\ell^2}{8}$ is the maximum bending moment of the simply supported beam.
