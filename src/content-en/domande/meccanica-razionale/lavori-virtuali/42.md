A vertical support reaction is sought by virtual work. A first trial virtual displacement leaves the support point fixed. Which change allows the reaction to be determined?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4.8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" riferimento="0" titolo="A beam illustrates the constraint. The roller at B allows horizontal motion."}

- Keep the constraint and increase the magnitude of the same displacement.
- Replace the vertical constraint by its reaction and choose a displacement allowed by the remaining constraints that moves the point vertically.
- Move the point virtually in the horizontal direction only.
- Remove all applied loads from the balance.

## Explanation

In the first displacement the reaction has zero coefficient in the virtual work. Releasing its direction and choosing $\delta y\ne0$ introduces $R\delta y$, which is balanced against the virtual work of the known loads.
