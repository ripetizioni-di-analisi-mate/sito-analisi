A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $4\ell$, is placed horizontally in the vertical plane, with a smooth hinge at $A$ and a smooth roller at $D$, at distance $3\ell$ from $A$. The vertical force $F$ directed downwards acts at the end $B$, as in the figure. What is the reaction of the roller?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,3.6,0;B,4.8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" punti="2.4,0,G,baricentro" forze="B,0,-1,F;2.4 -1.3,0,-0.6,mg" quote="A,D,3 ℓ,1.6;D,B,ℓ,1.6" titolo="Rod with a hinge at $A$, a roller at $D$ and a vertical load at the overhanging end"}

- $\dfrac{2\,m g - 4\,F}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\,m g + 4\,F}{3}$
- $\dfrac{m g + 4\,F}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\,m g + 4\,F}{4}$

## Explanation

The roller is removed and the reaction $\Phi_D\,\hat{\jmath}$ is applied at $D$ as an active force; the rod so released rotates about the hinge $A$ and the system has one degree of freedom. For the virtual rotation $\delta\varphi$ about $A$ one has $\delta y_D = 3\ell\,\delta\varphi$, $\delta y_G = 2\ell\,\delta\varphi$ and $\delta y_B = 4\ell\,\delta\varphi$, since the center of mass is the midpoint of the rod. The virtual work of the active forces and of the reaction is
$$
\delta L = \Phi_D\,3\ell\,\delta\varphi - m g\,2\ell\,\delta\varphi - F\,4\ell\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
$$
and since $\delta\varphi$ is arbitrary one obtains
$$
\Phi_D = \frac{2\,m g + 4\,F}{3} ,
$$
directed upwards. The two loads lie on the same side with respect to $A$, therefore their contributions add up.
