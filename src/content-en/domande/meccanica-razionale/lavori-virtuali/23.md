An ideal spring of constant $k$ and natural length $\ell_0 = \ell$ has one end at the fixed point $O$ and the other at the point $P = (3\ell, 4\ell)$. What is the potential of the elastic force in this configuration?

- $-\dfrac{25}{2}\,k\,\ell^2$
- $-16\,k\,\ell^2$
- $-8\,k\,\ell^2$
- $8\,k\,\ell^2$

## Explanation

The potential of an ideal spring depends on the distance between its ends alone and is $U = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(\ell_{OP} - \ell_0)^2$. The distance between $O$ and $P$ is
$$
\ell_{OP} = \sqrt{(3\ell)^2 + (4\ell)^2} = 5\ell ,
$$
therefore the elongation with respect to the natural length is $5\ell - \ell = 4\ell$ and
$$
U = -\frac{1}{2}\,k\,(4\ell)^2 = -8\,k\,\ell^2 .
$$
The value $-\dfrac{25}{2}\,k\,\ell^2$ is obtained by neglecting the natural length, whereas $-16\,k\,\ell^2$ comes from having omitted the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
