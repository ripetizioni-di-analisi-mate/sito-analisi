A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rotates in the vertical plane about its own center $O$, which is fixed, with a smooth hinge. An ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length connects the point $P$ of the rim to the fixed point $Q = (2R, 0)$. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle between $P - O$ and the $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise, as in the figure, what is the potential of the system, up to an additive constant?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.2" punti="0,0,O,centro;0.75,0.94,P;2.4,0,Q" linee="0.75,0.94 0.91,0.85 1.08,0.90 1.18,0.55 1.53,0.65 1.62,0.29 1.97,0.39 2.07,0.04 2.24,0.09 2.40,0.00" tratteggi="0,0 2.9,0;0,0 0.75,0.94" etichette="0.5,0.2,ϑ;1.75,0.75,k" titolo="Disk with the center $O$ fixed and the spring between the point $P$ of the rim and the fixed point $Q = (2R, 0)$"}

- $-2\,k\,R^2\cos\vartheta$
- $4\,k\,R^2\cos\vartheta$
- $2\,k\,R^2\cos\vartheta$
- $2\,k\,R^2\cos\vartheta - m g R\sin\vartheta$

## Explanation

The center of mass of the homogeneous disk is the center $O$, which is fixed, therefore the potential of the weight is constant and is discarded. The point of the rim is $P - O = R\,(\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath})$, hence the square of the length of the spring is
$$
|P - Q|^2 = (R\cos\vartheta - 2R)^2 + R^2\sin^2\vartheta = 5\,R^2 - 4\,R^2\cos\vartheta .
$$
The potential of the spring is $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,|P - Q|^2$, that is, $2\,k\,R^2\cos\vartheta$ up to the constant $-\dfrac{5}{2}\,k\,R^2$. The expression with $-m g R\sin\vartheta$ assigns the weight to the point $P$ of the rim, while the weight of the disk is applied at the center of mass $O$.
