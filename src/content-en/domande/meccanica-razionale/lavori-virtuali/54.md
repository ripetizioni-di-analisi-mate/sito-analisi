A homogeneous plate shaped as an equilateral triangle, of mass $m$ and side $\ell$, is hinged at the vertex $O$ with a smooth hinge and rotates in its own plane, which is vertical. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle between the altitude from $O$ and the downward vertical, as in the figure, what is the potential of the weight of the plate?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" poligoni="0,0 1.91,-1.45 -0.30,-2.38" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.7;0,0 0.81,-1.91" punti="0.54,-1.28,G,baricentro" forze="0.54 -2.1,0,-0.6,mg" etichette="0.2,-0.85,ϑ" titolo="Equilateral triangular plate hinged at the vertex $O$; $\vartheta$ is the angle between the altitude from $O$ and the vertical"}

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$

## Explanation

The center of mass of the homogeneous plate is the centroid of the triangle, which lies on the altitude from $O$ at two thirds of its length starting from the vertex. The altitude measures $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell$, hence $|G - O| = \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,\ell$ and the height of the center of mass is $y_G = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,\ell\cos\vartheta$. By the theorem on the potential of the weight one therefore obtains
$$
U = -m g\,y_G = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta .
$$
The coefficient $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ is obtained by placing the center of mass at the foot of the altitude, and the coefficient $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ by placing it at the midpoint of the altitude.
