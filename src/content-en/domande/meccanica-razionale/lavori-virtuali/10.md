- $M = m\sin\alpha$
- $M = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m\sin\alpha$
- $M = m\tan\alpha$

## Explanation

The system has one degree of freedom, because pure rolling relates the rotation of the disk to the displacement of the center and the inextensible string relates the displacement of the hanging body to that of the center. Take as virtual displacement the one in which the center $C$ moves up the plane by $\delta\xi > 0$, so that the hanging body descends by $\delta\xi$. The center of mass of the disk rises by $\delta\xi\sin\alpha$, therefore the virtual work of the active force system is
$$
\delta L^{(a)} = -m g\sin\alpha\,\delta\xi + M g\,\delta\xi .
$$
The constraints are ideal and bilateral, hence equilibrium is equivalent to $\delta L^{(a)} = 0$ for every $\delta\xi$, whence $M = m\sin\alpha$. The reactions of pure rolling and of the pulley do not appear, since their virtual work is zero.
