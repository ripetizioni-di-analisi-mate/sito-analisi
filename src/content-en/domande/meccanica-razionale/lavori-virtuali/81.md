A homogeneous rod $OA$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at $O$ to a vertical wall and is in equilibrium in a horizontal position, supported by a taut string that connects the end $A$ to a point of the wall above $O$; the string forms the angle $\alpha$ with the rod, as in the figure. What is the tension of the string?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,3,0;C,0,2.05,-" aste="O-A" catene="A-C" vincoli="O,cerniera,180" linee="0,-0.8 0,2.5" punti="1.5,0,G,baricentro" forze="1.5 -0.3,0,-0.6,mg" etichette="2.4,0.17,α" titolo="Horizontal rod hinged to the wall and supported by a string inclined at the angle $\alpha$ to the rod"}

- $\dfrac{m g}{2\cos\alpha}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2\sin\alpha}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{\sin\alpha}$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2\tan\alpha}$

## Explanation

The string is removed and its tension $\tau$ is applied at $A$ as an active force, directed from $A$ toward the attachment point on the wall; the released rod rotates about the hinge $O$ and has one degree of freedom. In the counterclockwise virtual rotation $\delta\varphi$ the end $A$ goes up by $\ell\,\delta\varphi$ and the center of mass goes up by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\delta\varphi$, while the tension has vertical component $\tau\sin\alpha$. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
\tau\sin\alpha\,\ell\,\delta\varphi - m g\,\frac{\ell}{2}\,\delta\varphi = 0 ,
$$
hence $\tau = \dfrac{m g}{2\sin\alpha}$. The horizontal component of the tension does no work, because the virtual displacement of $A$ is vertical. The value $\dfrac{m g}{\sin\alpha}$ is obtained by applying the weight at the end $A$ instead of at the midpoint.
