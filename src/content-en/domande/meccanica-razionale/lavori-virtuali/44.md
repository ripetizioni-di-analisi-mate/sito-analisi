A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, is hinged at $A$ with a smooth hinge and rests at $B$ on a horizontal plane that can only push it upwards; the rod is horizontal. The vertical force $F$ directed upwards is applied at the midpoint $G$, as in the figure. For which value of $F$ does the support at $B$ detach?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" linee="3.05,-0.16 4.15,-0.16" punti="1.8,0,G,baricentro" forze="1.8 1.1,0,0.6,F;1.8 -1.1,0,-0.6,mg" frecce="3.6,0,3.6,0.7,δy_B" quote="A,B,2 ℓ,2" titolo="Rod hinged at $A$ and resting at $B$, with the force $F$ directed upwards at the midpoint"}

- $F = \dfrac{m g}{2}$
- $F = \dfrac{m g}{4}$
- $F = m g$
- $F = 2\,m g$

## Explanation

The support at $B$ is unilateral, therefore starting from the horizontal configuration the virtual displacements are the rotations about $A$ that lift $B$, identified by $\delta\vartheta \geq 0$. The center of mass is at distance $\ell$ from $A$, hence $\delta y_G = \ell\,\delta\vartheta$, and the virtual work of the active force system is
$$
\delta L^{(a)} = -m g\,\ell\,\delta\vartheta + F\,\ell\,\delta\vartheta = \ell\,(F - m g)\,\delta\vartheta .
$$
The configuration is a boundary one, therefore equilibrium is equivalent to $\delta L^{(a)} \leq 0$ for every $\delta\vartheta \geq 0$, that is, $F \leq m g$. For $F = m g$ the inequality becomes an equality and the reaction of the support vanishes, hence that is the value at which the support detaches.
