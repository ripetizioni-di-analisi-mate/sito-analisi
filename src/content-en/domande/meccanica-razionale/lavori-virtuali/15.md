A point is subject to $\vec F=(-cy,cx)$, with $c>0$. On the circle $x^2+y^2=R^2$ the force is tangential and points anticlockwise. What work is done on an anticlockwise semicircle followed by a clockwise return along exactly the same arc?

- $2\pi cR^2$
- $\pi cR^2$
- $0$
- $-2\pi cR^2$

## Explanation

Reversing an oriented curve changes the sign of its line integral. The outward and return contributions cancel even for this field, which would do nonzero work on a full lap in one direction.
