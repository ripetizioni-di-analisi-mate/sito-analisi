A particle is acted upon by the constant force $\vec{F} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\,\hat{\jmath})$, with $F > 0$. The particle travels along the semicircle of center $O$ and radius $\ell$ that goes from the position $(\ell, 0)$ to the position $(-\ell, 0)$. What is the work of the force?

- $0$
- $2\,F\,\ell$
- $-2\,F\,\ell$
- $-2\pi\,F\,\ell$

## Explanation

The force is constant, therefore it is conservative with potential $U = F\,(x + 2\,y)$ and its work depends only on the initial and final positions. The overall displacement of the point of application is $(-\ell, 0) - (\ell, 0) = -2\ell\,\hat{\imath}$, hence
$$
L = \vec{F} \cdot (-2\ell\,\hat{\imath}) = -2\,F\,\ell .
$$
The value $-2\pi\,F\,\ell$ is obtained by multiplying a component of the force by the length $\pi\ell$ of the arc, whereas the work of a constant force follows from the overall displacement of the point of application alone, therefore the length of the path traveled does not enter the computation.
