A material point of mass $m$ rests on a smooth inclined plane, which forms the angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal, and is kept in equilibrium by a horizontal force $F$ directed toward the plane, as in the figure. What is the magnitude of the reaction of the plane?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,2.31" punti="2.5,1.44,P" forze="2.5 1.44,0.9,0,;2.5 0.64,0,-0.8," etichette="0.75,0.15,α;1.75,1.7,F;2.85,0.75,mg" titolo="Material point on a smooth inclined plane, kept in equilibrium by a horizontal force"}

- $\dfrac{m g}{\cos\alpha}$
- $m g\cos\alpha$
- $m g$
- $\dfrac{m g}{\sin\alpha}$

## Explanation

The virtual displacement $\delta s$ up along the plane gives the equation $F\cos\alpha\,\delta s - m g\sin\alpha\,\delta s = 0$, hence $F = m g\tan\alpha$. To compute the reaction one removes the constraint and applies the reaction $\Phi$, normal to the plane, as an active force, so that the point can also undergo the virtual displacement $\delta n$ normal to the plane and directed outward. In that displacement the weight does the work $-m g\cos\alpha\,\delta n$ and the horizontal force, which pushes toward the plane, the work $-F\sin\alpha\,\delta n$, therefore
$$
\Phi\,\delta n - m g\cos\alpha\,\delta n - F\sin\alpha\,\delta n = 0 .
$$
It follows that $\Phi = m g\cos\alpha + m g\tan\alpha\sin\alpha = \dfrac{m g}{\cos\alpha}$, larger than the weight. The value $m g\cos\alpha$ neglects the normal component of the horizontal force.
