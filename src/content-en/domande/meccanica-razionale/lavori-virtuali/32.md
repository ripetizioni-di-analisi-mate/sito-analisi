A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, has its midpoint $G$ hinged to the ground with a smooth hinge and rotates in the vertical plane; the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the horizontal is measured counterclockwise. A particle of mass $2m$ is attached at the end $B$ and a constant couple of moment $\mathcal{M}$ in the counterclockwise direction acts on the rod, as in the figure. What is the Lagrangian component $Q_\vartheta$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0.3,0.3;B,3.7,2.1;G,2,1.2,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="G,cerniera" punti="2,1.2,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0.3,1.2 3.6,1.2" forze="3.7 1.1,0,-0.5,2mg" coppie="1.1 1.5,1,ℳ" etichette="2.75,1.35,ϑ" titolo="Rod hinged at the midpoint, with a particle at $B$ and a constant couple"}

- $\mathcal{M} - 2\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$
- $\mathcal{M} + 2\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$
- $\mathcal{M} - 2\,m g \ell\sin\vartheta$
- $\mathcal{M} - m g \ell\cos\vartheta$

## Explanation

The hinge is at the midpoint, therefore the center of mass of the rod stays fixed and the weight of the rod has constant potential. The end $B$ is given by $B - G = \ell\,(\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath})$, hence $y_B = \ell\sin\vartheta$ up to a constant and the potential of the weight of the particle is $-2\,m g \ell\sin\vartheta$. The constant counterclockwise couple contributes to the potential with the term $\mathcal{M}\,\vartheta$, therefore
$$
U(\vartheta) = -2\,m g \ell\sin\vartheta + \mathcal{M}\,\vartheta .
$$
The force system is conservative and the constraints are fixed, therefore $Q_\vartheta = \dfrac{\partial U}{\partial \vartheta}$ and differentiating one obtains $Q_\vartheta = \mathcal{M} - 2\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$. The contribution $m g \ell\cos\vartheta$ is obtained by assigning to the particle the mass $m$ instead of $2m$.
