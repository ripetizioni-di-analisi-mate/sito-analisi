A homogeneous rectangular plate, of mass $m$, base $b$ and height $h$, stands in a vertical plane on a smooth horizontal floor that can only push it upward; the lower right corner $D$ rests against a fixed stop that prevents the plate from sliding to the right. The horizontal force $F$ directed to the right acts at the upper left corner, as in the figure. What is the largest value of $F$ for which the plate stays in equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="D,2,0" poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,3 0,3;2,0 2.3,0 2.3,0.4 2,0.4" linee="-0.6,0 3.2,0" punti="1,1.5,G,baricentro" forze="0 3,0.8,0,F;1 1.2,0,-0.7,mg" quote="0 0,D,b,0.5;2.3 0,2.3 3,h,0.5" titolo="Rectangular plate with the corner $D$ resting against a stop, with the force $F$ at the upper left corner"}

- $\dfrac{m g\,h}{2\,b}$
- $\dfrac{m g\,b}{2\,h}$
- $\dfrac{m g\,b}{h}$
- $\dfrac{m g\,b}{4\,h}$

## Explanation

The plate touches the floor and the stop, hence the configuration is a boundary one, and among the virtual displacements there is the clockwise rotation about $D$, of amplitude $\varepsilon \geq 0$, which lifts the lower left corner. In that rotation the upper left corner, which is at height $h$ above $D$, moves to the right by $h\,\varepsilon$, while the center of mass, at horizontal distance $\dfrac{b}{2}$ from $D$, rises by $\dfrac{b}{2}\,\varepsilon$. The virtual work of the active force system is therefore
$$
\delta L^{(a)} = F\,h\,\varepsilon - m g\,\frac{b}{2}\,\varepsilon .
$$
Equilibrium requires $\delta L^{(a)} \leq 0$ for every $\varepsilon \geq 0$, hence $F \leq \dfrac{m g\,b}{2\,h}$; for larger values the plate tips over about $D$. The other virtual displacements, such as the translation to the left or the lifting, give negative or zero work and add no conditions.
