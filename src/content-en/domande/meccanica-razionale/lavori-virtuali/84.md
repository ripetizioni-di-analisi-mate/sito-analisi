A rod $AB$ is constrained by a hinge at $A$ and by a roller at $B$ with vertical reaction, and is in equilibrium under given loads. To compute with the principle of virtual work the horizontal component $\Phi_{Ax}$ of the reaction of the hinge, which system is studied?

- The rod with a roller at $A$ that allows vertical translation, with $\Phi_{Ax}$ applied as an active force
- The rod with a roller at $A$ that allows horizontal translation, with no forces applied at $A$
- The rod with a roller at $A$ that allows horizontal translation, with $\Phi_{Ax}$ applied as an active force
- The rod with a clamp at $A$, with $\Phi_{Ax}$ applied as an active force

## Explanation

To compute a reaction one removes the constraint that exerts it, keeping the others, and applies the reaction as an active force. For the component $\Phi_{Ax}$ alone one removes the part of the hinge that prevents the horizontal translation of $A$, that is, one replaces it with a roller that allows that translation. With the roller at $B$ the released rod can then translate horizontally, and the principle of virtual work for the displacement $\delta x$ gives $\Phi_{Ax}\,\delta x + R_x^{(a)}\,\delta x = 0$, where $R_x^{(a)}$ is the horizontal component of the resultant of the loads, hence $\Phi_{Ax} = -R_x^{(a)}$. Without applying $\Phi_{Ax}$ the released system would in general not be in equilibrium, while the roller that allows vertical translation serves to compute the component $\Phi_{Ay}$.
