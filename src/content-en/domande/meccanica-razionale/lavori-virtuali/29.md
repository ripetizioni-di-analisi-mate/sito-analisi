Two particles $P_1$ and $P_2$, of mass $m$ each, slide without friction on a fixed horizontal guide and are connected by an ideal spring of constant $k$ and natural length $\ell_0$. Taking as generalized coordinates the abscissas $x_1$ and $x_2$ of the two particles, with $x_2 - x_1 > 0$, what is the Lagrangian component $Q_{x_1}$?

::struttura{nodi="P_1,1.2,0;P_2,3.8,0" tratteggi="-0.4,0 5.2,0" linee="1.20,0.00 1.56,0.00 1.72,0.24 2.03,-0.24 2.34,0.24 2.66,-0.24 2.97,0.24 3.28,-0.24 3.44,0.00 3.80,0.00" punti="0,0,O;1.2,0;3.8,0" quote="0 0,P_1,x_1,0.8;0 0,P_2,x_2,1.5" etichette="2.5,0.62,k" titolo="Two particles on a horizontal guide connected by a spring of constant $k$"}

- $-k\,(x_2 - x_1 - \ell_0)$
- $k\,(x_2 - x_1 - \ell_0)$
- $k\,(x_2 - x_1)$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(x_2 - x_1 - \ell_0)$

## Explanation

The guide is horizontal, therefore the weight of the two particles does no work and the only active force system that contributes is that of the spring. The distance between the ends of the spring is $x_2 - x_1$, hence the potential is
$$
U(x_1, x_2) = -\frac{1}{2}\,k\,(x_2 - x_1 - \ell_0)^2 .
$$
The force system is conservative and the constraints are fixed, therefore $Q_{x_1} = \dfrac{\partial U}{\partial x_1}$ and differentiating one obtains
$$
Q_{x_1} = -k\,(x_2 - x_1 - \ell_0) \cdot (-1) = k\,(x_2 - x_1 - \ell_0) .
$$
With the spring stretched the result is positive, since the spring pulls $P_1$ towards $P_2$, that is, in the direction of increasing $x$.
