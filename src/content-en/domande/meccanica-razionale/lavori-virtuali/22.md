A homogeneous rod $OA$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the origin $O$ and moves in the vertical plane; the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical is measured counterclockwise. A particle of mass $2m$ is attached at the end $A$. What is the potential of the weight of the system?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$
- $3\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$
- $-\dfrac{5}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$

## Explanation

The end of the rod is $A - O = \ell\,(\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath})$, therefore $y_A = -\ell\cos\vartheta$, whereas the center of mass of the rod is its midpoint and has height $y_G = -\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$. The potential of the weight of a body of mass $M$ is $-M g\,y_G$, hence the two contributions are
$$
U_1 = \frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta ,
\qquad
U_2 = 2\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta .
$$
The potentials of the forces of a system add up, therefore
$$
U = \frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta + 2\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta = \frac{5}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta .
$$
The value $3\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta$ is obtained by placing the center of mass of the rod at the end $A$ instead of at the midpoint.
