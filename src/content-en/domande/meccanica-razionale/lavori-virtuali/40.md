A holonomic system with fixed, ideal and bilateral constraints has one degree of freedom, generalized coordinate $\vartheta \in [0, 2\pi)$ and conservative active force system of potential $U(\vartheta) = k\,\ell^2\,(\cos 2\vartheta + 2\cos\vartheta)$. How many equilibrium configurations does the system admit?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The equilibrium of a conservative system with fixed, ideal and bilateral constraints is equivalent to the stationarity of the potential, therefore one has to solve $U'(\vartheta) = 0$. Differentiating and factoring one obtains
$$
U'(\vartheta) = -2\,k\,\ell^2\,\big( \sin 2\vartheta + \sin\vartheta \big) = -2\,k\,\ell^2\,\sin\vartheta\,\big( 2\cos\vartheta + 1 \big) ,
$$
where the double-angle formula $\sin 2\vartheta = 2\sin\vartheta\cos\vartheta$ has been used. The product is zero when $\sin\vartheta = 0$, that is, for $\vartheta = 0$ and $\vartheta = \pi$, or when $\cos\vartheta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, that is, for $\vartheta = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ and $\vartheta = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$. The equilibrium configurations in $[0, 2\pi)$ are therefore four.
