In the slider-crank mechanism in the figure the crank $OA$ and the connecting rod $AB$ have the same length $\ell$ and negligible mass; $O$ is a fixed hinge, $A$ an internal hinge, and the piston $B$ slides on a smooth horizontal guide through $O$. The horizontal force $F$ directed toward $O$ acts on the piston, and $\vartheta$ is the angle between the crank and the guide. Which couple, applied to the crank, keeps the system in equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.53,1.29;B,3.06,0" aste="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera" cerniere="A;B" linee="-0.4,-0.25 4.6,-0.25;-0.4,0.25 0,0.25;2.7,0.25 4.6,0.25" poligoni="2.76,-0.2 3.36,-0.2 3.36,0.2 2.76,0.2" forze="3.36 0,-0.8,0,F" etichette="0.55,0.2,ϑ" titolo="Slider-crank with crank and connecting rod of length $\ell$ and force $F$ on the piston"}

- $2\,F\,\ell\sin\vartheta$, clockwise
- $2\,F\,\ell\sin\vartheta$, counterclockwise
- $F\,\ell\sin\vartheta$, clockwise
- $2\,F\,\ell\cos\vartheta$, clockwise

## Explanation

The system has one degree of freedom, described by $\vartheta$, and the triangle $OAB$ is isosceles, hence the abscissa of the piston is $x_B = 2\ell\cos\vartheta$ and $\delta x_B = -2\ell\sin\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta$. Let $C$ be the moment of the couple applied to the crank, positive counterclockwise. The force on the piston is $-F\,\hat{\imath}$, therefore the virtual work of the active force system is
$$
\delta L^{(a)} = C\,\delta\vartheta - F\,\delta x_B = \big( C + 2\,F\,\ell\sin\vartheta \big)\,\delta\vartheta .
$$
The constraints are ideal and bilateral, so equilibrium is equivalent to $\delta L^{(a)} = 0$ for every $\delta\vartheta$, hence $C = -2\,F\,\ell\sin\vartheta$, that is, a couple of magnitude $2\,F\,\ell\sin\vartheta$ in the **clockwise** direction. The force pushes the piston toward $O$ and tends to increase $\vartheta$, therefore the balancing couple opposes this rotation.
