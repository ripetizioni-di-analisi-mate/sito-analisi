A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rests, in a vertical plane, on a horizontal floor and against the edge $S$ of a step of height $h = \dfrac{R}{2}$; both contacts are smooth and unilateral. The horizontal force $F$ directed toward the step acts at the center of the disk, as in the figure. For which value of $F$ does the disk leave the floor?

::struttura{cerchi="-0.3,1.5,1.5" poligoni="1,0 3,0 3,0.75 1,0.75" linee="-2.2,0 3,0" punti="-0.3,1.5,C,baricentro;1,0.75,S" forze="0.5 1.5,0.8,0,;-0.3 0.8,0,-0.7," etichette="0.6,1.75,F;0,0.95,mg" quote="3 0,3 0.75,h,-0.5" titolo="Disk resting on the floor and on the edge $S$ of a step of height $h = \dfrac{R}{2}$"}

- $\dfrac{m g}{\sqrt{3}}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g$
- $\sqrt{3}\,m g$
- $2\,m g$

## Explanation

The disk touches the floor and the edge $S$, hence the configuration is a boundary one, and the virtual displacement that lifts it from the floor is the rotation of the center about $S$, with the disk staying in contact with the edge. The center is at height $R - h = \dfrac{R}{2}$ above $S$ and at horizontal distance $\sqrt{R^2 - \dfrac{R^2}{4}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R$ from $S$, therefore in a rotation of amplitude $\varepsilon \geq 0$ toward the step the center advances by $\dfrac{R}{2}\,\varepsilon$ and rises by $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R\,\varepsilon$. The force and the weight are applied at the center, so
$$
\delta L^{(a)} = F\,\frac{R}{2}\,\varepsilon - m g\,\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R\,\varepsilon .
$$
Equilibrium requires $\delta L^{(a)} \leq 0$ for every $\varepsilon \geq 0$, that is, $F \leq \sqrt{3}\,m g$, and for $F = \sqrt{3}\,m g$ the reaction of the floor vanishes and the disk leaves it. The value $\dfrac{m g}{\sqrt{3}}$ is obtained by exchanging the horizontal displacement of the center with the vertical one.
