The three-hinged arch in the figure consists of two rods $AC$ and $CB$ of negligible mass and length $\ell$, hinged to the ground at $A$ and at $B$, at the same height, and connected to each other by the hinge $C$; each rod forms the angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal. The vertical load $P$ directed downward acts at $C$. What is the magnitude of the horizontal component of the reaction of the hinge $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,1.2;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" forze="C,0,-0.9,P" tratteggi="0,0 4,0" etichette="0.6,0.14,α" titolo="Three-hinged arch with the load $P$ at the crown hinge $C$"}

- $\dfrac{P\tan\alpha}{2}$
- $\dfrac{P}{\tan\alpha}$
- $\dfrac{P}{2\tan\alpha}$
- $\dfrac{P}{2\sin\alpha}$

## Explanation

The hinge $B$ is replaced by a roller that lets $B$ slide horizontally, and the horizontal component of the reaction, $-H\,\hat{\imath}$, directed toward $A$, is applied at $B$. The released system has one degree of freedom, the angle $\vartheta$ of the rods to the horizontal, with $y_C = \ell\sin\vartheta$ and $x_B = 2\ell\cos\vartheta$. The principle of virtual work gives
$$
-P\,\ell\cos\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta - H\,(-2\ell\sin\vartheta)\,\delta\vartheta = 0 ,
$$
and since $\delta\vartheta$ is arbitrary, in the configuration $\vartheta = \alpha$ one obtains $H = \dfrac{P}{2\tan\alpha}$. The positive sign confirms that the hinge pushes $B$ toward $A$, and the thrust grows when the arch is flatter, because $\tan\alpha$ decreases. The value $\dfrac{P}{2\sin\alpha}$ is the compressive force in each rod, of which the thrust is the horizontal component.
