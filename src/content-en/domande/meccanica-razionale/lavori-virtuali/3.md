A particle in the plane is acted upon by the position-dependent force $\vec{F} = c\,(y\,\hat{\imath} - x\,\hat{\jmath})$, with $c > 0$ constant. What is the work of the force along the quarter circle of center $O$ and radius $\ell$ traveled from $(\ell, 0)$ to $(0, \ell)$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{\pi\,c\,\ell^2}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi\,c\,\ell^2}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi\,c\,\ell^2}{4}$

## Explanation

The arc is parametrized by $P(u) = \ell\,(\cos u\,\hat{\imath} + \sin u\,\hat{\jmath})$ for $u \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$, whence $\dfrac{dP}{du} = \ell\,(-\sin u\,\hat{\imath} + \cos u\,\hat{\jmath})$. On the curve the force is $\vec{F} = c\,\ell\,(\sin u\,\hat{\imath} - \cos u\,\hat{\jmath})$, hence the integrand is
$$
\vec{F} \cdot \frac{dP}{du} = c\,\ell^2\,(-\sin^2 u - \cos^2 u) = -c\,\ell^2 ,
$$
which is constant. Integrating between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ then gives $L = -\dfrac{\pi\,c\,\ell^2}{2}$. The work is negative since the force is at every point tangent to the circle and opposite to the direction of travel.
