A homogeneous rod $AB$ of length $\ell$ has the end $A$ constrained by a smooth sleeve to slide along a fixed horizontal guide and can rotate about $A$ in the horizontal plane, as in the figure. A force of constant magnitude $F$, always perpendicular to the rod, is applied at the midpoint $G$; the weight is orthogonal to the plane of motion and does no work. Taking as generalized coordinates the abscissa $s$ of $A$ and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the guide, what is the Lagrangian component $Q_\vartheta$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.2,0;B,3.33,1.49" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello" linee="-0.2,0 4.6,0" punti="2.27,0.75,G,baricentro" forze="2.27 0.75,-0.57,0.82,F" etichette="1.95,0.22,ϑ" quote="O,A,s,0.8" titolo="Rod on a horizontal guide with a force always perpendicular to it applied at the midpoint"}

- $F\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{F\,\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$
- $-\dfrac{F\,\ell}{2}$
- $\dfrac{F\,\ell}{2}$

## Explanation

Setting $\hat{u} = \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$ as the unit vector of the rod, the unit vector orthogonal to it in the direction of the force is $\hat{n} = -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{n}$ and the point of application is $G - O = s\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\hat{u}$. Differentiating with respect to $\vartheta$ one obtains $\dfrac{\partial G}{\partial \vartheta} = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\hat{n}$, because the derivative of $\hat{u}$ with respect to $\vartheta$ is precisely $\hat{n}$. Hence
$$
Q_\vartheta = \vec{F} \cdot \frac{\partial G}{\partial \vartheta} = F\,\frac{\ell}{2}\,\hat{n} \cdot \hat{n} = \frac{F\,\ell}{2} ,
$$
since $\hat{n}$ is a unit vector. The Lagrangian component is therefore constant, and it coincides with the moment of the force with respect to $A$, which has moment arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ in every configuration. The value $F\,\ell$ is obtained by applying the force at the end $B$ instead of at the midpoint.
