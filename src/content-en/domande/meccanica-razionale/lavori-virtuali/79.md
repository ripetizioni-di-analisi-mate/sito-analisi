A homogeneous square plate, of mass $m$ and side $\ell$, is hinged at the vertex $O$ with a smooth hinge and rotates in its own plane, which is vertical. A material point of mass $m$ is fixed at the vertex $B$, adjacent to $O$, as in the figure. Denoting by $\beta$ the angle between the side $OB$ and the downward vertical, what value does $\tan\beta$ take in the equilibrium configuration in which the plate lies below the hinge?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;B,1.42,-1.68" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" poligoni="0,0 1.42,-1.68 -0.27,-3.10 -1.68,-1.42" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.6" punti="1.42,-1.68,,pieno" etichette="0.22,-0.75,β;1.85,-1.75,m" titolo="Square plate hinged at the vertex $O$, with a material point of mass $m$ at the vertex $B$"}

- $\tan\beta = 1$
- $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The force system consists of the weights of the plate and of the point, hence the potential is $U = -2\,m g\,y_{G'}$, where $G'$ is the center of mass of the system, and equilibrium with the plate below the hinge requires $G'$ to lie on the downward vertical through $O$. In a body frame, with the $\xi$ axis along $OB$ and the $\eta$ axis along the other side from $O$, the center of the plate has coordinates $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{2}, \dfrac{\ell}{2} \right)$ and the point $B$ has coordinates $(\ell, 0)$. The two masses are equal, therefore by the additivity of the center of mass
$$
G' = \left( \frac{3\,\ell}{4},\ \frac{\ell}{4} \right) ,
$$
and the angle between $OB$ and $OG'$ has tangent $\dfrac{1}{3}$, the ratio between the two coordinates. At equilibrium $OG'$ is the downward vertical, hence $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The value $\tan\beta = 1$ corresponds to the plate alone, hanging with its diagonal vertical, and neglects the material point.
