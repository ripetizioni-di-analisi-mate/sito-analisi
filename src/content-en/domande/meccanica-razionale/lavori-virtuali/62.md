A cart of mass $M$ slides on a smooth horizontal guide, and its position is given by the abscissa $x$. A rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$ is hinged at the point $C$ of the cart and carries at its other end a material point $P$ of mass $m$; $\varphi$ is the angle between the rod and the downward vertical, measured counterclockwise. The constant horizontal force $F\,\hat{\imath}$ acts on the cart, as in the figure. What is the Lagrangian component $Q_\varphi$ of the force system formed by the weights and by the force $F\,\hat{\imath}$?

::struttura{nodi="C,1.8,0;P,2.66,-1.58" aste="C-P" cerniere="C" linee="-0.4,-0.22 1.55,-0.22;2.1,-0.22 4.4,-0.22" cerchi="1.3,0,0.22;2.3,0,0.22" poligoni="1,0.2 2.6,0.2 2.6,0.8 1,0.8" tratteggi="1.8,0 1.8,-2" punti="2.66,-1.58,,pieno" forze="1 0.5,0.8,0,F;2.66 -2.4,0,-0.8," etichette="2.02,-0.75,φ;1.8,0.5,M;3.05,-2.1,mg" titolo="Cart on a horizontal guide with a pendulum hinged at the point $C$"}

- $F\,\ell\cos\varphi - m g \ell\sin\varphi$
- $-m g \ell\sin\varphi$
- $-(M + m)\,g \ell\sin\varphi$
- $-m g \ell\cos\varphi$

## Explanation

With $C - O = x\,\hat{\imath}$ one has $P - O = (x + \ell\sin\varphi)\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\varphi\,\hat{\jmath}$, while the points of the cart do not depend on $\varphi$. The force $F\,\hat{\imath}$ and the weight of the cart are applied to the cart, hence the derivatives with respect to $\varphi$ of their points of application are zero and the two contributions to $Q_\varphi$ vanish. The weight of $P$ remains, with $\dfrac{\partial P}{\partial \varphi} = \ell\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore
$$
Q_\varphi = -m g\,\hat{\jmath} \cdot \frac{\partial P}{\partial \varphi} = -m g \ell\sin\varphi .
$$
The expression with $F\,\ell\cos\varphi$ is obtained by applying the force to the point $P$ instead of the cart, while $-(M + m)\,g \ell\sin\varphi$ assigns to the point $P$ the mass of the cart as well.
