A particle of mass $m$ slides along the horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide in the figure. It is connected to the wall by two ideal springs placed one after the other, of constants $k$ and $2k$ and of negligible mass, joined at the point $B$. What is the equivalent elastic constant of the connection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.1,0;P,6.2,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180" linee="0,0 0.25,0.35 0.75,-0.35 1.25,0.35 1.75,-0.35 2.25,0.35 2.75,-0.35 3.1,0;3.1,0 3.35,0.35 3.85,-0.35 4.35,0.35 4.85,-0.35 5.35,0.35 5.85,-0.35 6.2,0;6.2,0 8.4,0" punti="6.2,0;3.1,0" etichette="1.5,0.75,k;4.7,0.75,2k" riferimento=0 titolo="Two springs in series between the wall and the particle $P$"}

- $\dfrac{2k}{3}$
- $3k$
- $\dfrac{3k}{2}$
- $\dfrac{k}{3}$

## Explanation

The point $B$ has negligible mass, therefore the same force passes through the two springs, while their elongations add up. Calling $F$ that force, the total elongation equals
$$
\frac{F}{k} + \frac{F}{2k} = \frac{3F}{2k} ,
$$
therefore the equivalent constant, defined by $F = k_{\text{eq}}\,\Delta\ell$, is $k_{\text{eq}} = \dfrac{2k}{3}$. The value obtained is smaller than the smaller of the two constants, as happens for every connection in series.
