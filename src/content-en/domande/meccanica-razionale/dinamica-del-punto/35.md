A particle of mass $m$ is constrained to a smooth and fixed curve and is subject to the weight and to the constraint reaction. Which of the intrinsic equations determines the law of motion $s(t)$?

- The projection onto the tangent unit vector
- The projection onto the principal normal unit vector
- The projection onto the binormal unit vector
- The system of all three, because the reaction is unknown

## Explanation

The curve is smooth, therefore the constraint reaction is orthogonal to it and its tangential component is zero. The projection of the fundamental equation onto the tangent unit vector is then
$$
m\,\ddot{s} = \vec{F} \cdot \hat{t} ,
$$
and it contains the single unknown $s$, therefore it is the pure equation of motion. The other two projections contain the components of the constraint reaction, hence they determine it once the law of motion is known.
