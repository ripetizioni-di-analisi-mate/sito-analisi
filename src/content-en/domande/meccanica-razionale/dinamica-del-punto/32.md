A particle is confined to $x\ge0$ by a wall. For $x>0$ its potential energy is $V=V_0+cx$ with $c>0$. At contact the wall can exert a positive normal reaction along $+x$. Where can equilibrium occur?

- At every interior point.
- Only at $x=V_0/c$.
- Nowhere because $V'(x)$ never vanishes.
- At $x=0$, with reaction of magnitude $c$.

## Explanation

The interior force is $-c$ and cannot balance there. At the boundary the wall provides a reaction $+c$, in the direction allowed by the unilateral constraint.
