A particle of mass $m$ slides along the horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide in the figure, connected to the wall by an ideal spring of constant $k$. The abscissa $x$ is measured from the position in which the spring has its rest length. At the instant $t = 0$ the particle is at $x = 0$ with velocity $v_0\,\hat{\imath}$. What is the amplitude of the oscillations?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,3.6,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180" linee="0,0 0.3,0.35 0.9,-0.35 1.5,0.35 2.1,-0.35 2.7,0.35 3.3,-0.35 3.6,0;3.6,0 6.2,0" punti="3.6,0" frecce="3.6,0.6,5,0.6,v_0" etichette="1.8,0.75,k" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle on a smooth guide pulled back by a spring of constant $k$, launched from the rest position"}

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,v_0\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$
- $v_0\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\dfrac{m\,v_0}{k}$
- $v_0\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$

## Explanation

Along the horizontal and smooth guide the elastic force $-k\,x$ alone acts, therefore the motion is harmonic with angular frequency $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$. The amplitude is obtained from the initial data by the formula $A = \sqrt{x_0^2 + \dfrac{v_0^2}{\omega_0^2}}$ and in the case at hand $x_0 = 0$, therefore there remains $A = \dfrac{v_0}{\omega_0} = v_0\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$. The same result follows from the conservation of mechanical energy, since in the rest position the energy is entirely kinetic and equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_0^2$, while at the point of maximum elongation it is entirely elastic and equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,A^2$.
