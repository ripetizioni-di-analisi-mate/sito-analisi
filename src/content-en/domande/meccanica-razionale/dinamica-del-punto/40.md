A simple pendulum is formed by a particle of mass $m$ hanging from an inextensible string of length $\ell$, and performs small oscillations in the vertical plane. On which quantities does the period depend?

- On $\ell$, on $g$ and on $m$
- On $\ell$, on $g$ and on the amplitude
- On $\ell$ and on the amplitude
- Only on $\ell$ and on $g$

## Explanation

The pure equation of motion of the pendulum is $\ddot{\vartheta} + \dfrac{g}{\ell}\sin\vartheta = 0$ and, for $|\vartheta|$ small, the Taylor expansion $\sin\vartheta = \vartheta + o(\vartheta^2)$ reduces it to $\ddot{\vartheta} + \dfrac{g}{\ell}\,\vartheta = 0$. This is the equation of the harmonic oscillator of angular frequency $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\ell}}$, therefore the period equals
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ell}{g}} .
$$
The mass does not appear, since it cancels out in the pure equation of motion, and the amplitude does not appear, since the linearized motion is harmonic.
