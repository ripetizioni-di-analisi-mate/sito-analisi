A particle of mass $m$ is at rest at $O$ at the instant $t = 0$ and slides along a horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide, subject to the force $b\,t\,\hat{\imath}$, with $b > 0$, besides the weight. What is the abscissa of the particle at the instant $t$?

- $\dfrac{b\,t^2}{2m}$
- $\dfrac{b\,t^3}{2m}$
- $\dfrac{b\,t^3}{3m}$
- $\dfrac{b\,t^3}{6m}$

## Explanation

The guide is horizontal and smooth, therefore along the $x$ axis the assigned force alone acts and the fundamental equation is $m\,\ddot{x} = b\,t$. A first integration with $\dot{x}(0) = 0$ gives $\dot{x}(t) = \dfrac{b\,t^2}{2m}$. A second integration with $x(0) = 0$ gives
$$
x(t) = \frac{b\,t^3}{6m} ,
$$
since a primitive of $t^2$ is $\dfrac{t^3}{3}$.
