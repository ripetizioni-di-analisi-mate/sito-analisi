A smooth inclined plane, forming the angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal, is fixed to a cart that moves on a horizontal track with constant acceleration of magnitude $a$, directed as in the figure. A particle of mass $m$ rests on the plane. For which value of $a$ does the particle remain at rest with respect to the cart?

::struttura{linee="0,0 0,3 4,0 0,0;-1,-0.5 5.5,-0.5" cerchi="0.6,-0.25,0.25;3.4,-0.25,0.25" punti="2,1.5,P" frecce="1.2,-1.1,2.8,-1.1,a" etichette="3.05,0.25,α" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle resting on a smooth inclined plane fixed to an accelerating cart"}

- $g\sin\alpha$
- $\dfrac{g}{\tan\alpha}$
- $g\tan\alpha$
- $g\cos\alpha$

## Explanation

In the frame of the cart, which translates with acceleration $\vec{a}$, the transport force $-m\,\vec{a}$, horizontal and opposite to the acceleration, is added to the real forces; the Coriolis force is zero, because the frame does not rotate. The reaction of the smooth plane is normal to the plane, therefore the particle is in relative equilibrium if the sum of the weight and the transport force has no component along the plane. Along the line of steepest slope the weight has component $m g\sin\alpha$ downwards and the transport force has component $m a\cos\alpha$ upwards, so equilibrium requires $m a\cos\alpha = m g\sin\alpha$, that is $a = g\tan\alpha$. With a smaller acceleration the particle slides down the plane, with a larger one it moves up.
