A particle of mass $m$ moves on a smooth and fixed inclined plane, which forms the angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal, and is subject to its weight. At the initial instant the particle has a horizontal velocity $\vec{v}_0 \neq \vec{0}$, parallel to the plane. Which trajectory does it describe on the plane?

- A segment of the line of steepest slope
- A parabola with horizontal axis
- A parabola with axis parallel to the line of steepest slope
- An arc of a circle

## Explanation

The reaction of the smooth plane is normal to the plane and balances the normal component of the weight, of magnitude $m g\cos\alpha$, since the particle remains on the plane. The resultant force is therefore the component of the weight parallel to the plane, which is constant, has magnitude $m g\sin\alpha$ and is directed downwards along the line of steepest slope. The particle hence moves under a constant force, with initial velocity orthogonal to it, and the trajectory is a parabola with axis parallel to the force. The motion on the plane is that of a body launched horizontally, with $g\sin\alpha$ in place of $g$.
