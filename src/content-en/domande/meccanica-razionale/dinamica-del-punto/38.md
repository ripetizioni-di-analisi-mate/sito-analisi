A particle of mass $m$ hangs at $O$ from an inextensible string of negligible mass and length $\ell$, and moves in the vertical plane. The particle is released from rest with the string taut and horizontal, as in the figure. What is the tension of the string when the particle passes through the lowest position?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,2.6;P,2.6,2.6" catene="O-P" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,2.6 0,-0.2" punti="2.6,2.6" quote="O,P,ℓ,-0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle hanging at $O$ from a string of length $\ell$, released from rest with the string horizontal"}

- $m g$
- $2 m g$
- $3 m g$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m g$

## Explanation

The particle starts from rest with $\vartheta_0 = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, therefore the conservation of mechanical energy gives $\ell^2\dot{\vartheta}^2 = 2 g \ell\,( \cos\vartheta - \cos\vartheta_0 ) = 2 g \ell\cos\vartheta$. The tension of the string equals $\Phi = m g\cos\vartheta + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$, therefore substituting one obtains
$$
\Phi = m g\,( 3\cos\vartheta - 2\cos\vartheta_0 ) = 3 m g\cos\vartheta .
$$
In the lowest position one has $\vartheta = 0$, hence $\Phi = 3 m g$, that is, three times the weight.
