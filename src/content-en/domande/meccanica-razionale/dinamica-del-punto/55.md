A particle of mass $m$ slides along a smooth and fixed horizontal rectilinear guide, subject to the elastic force $-k\,x\,\hat{\imath}$, and one sets $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$. At the instant $t = 0$ the particle is at $x = d$, with $d > 0$, and has velocity $-\omega_0\,d\,\hat{\imath}$. At what instant does it pass through the origin for the first time?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2\,\omega_0}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4\,\omega_0}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\omega_0}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4\,\omega_0}$

## Explanation

The general integral of the equation $\ddot{x} + \omega_0^2\,x = 0$ is $x(t) = c_1\cos(\omega_0 t) + c_2\sin(\omega_0 t)$, and the initial data give $c_1 = d$ and $c_2 = \dfrac{v_0}{\omega_0} = -d$. Therefore
$$
x(t) = d\,\big( \cos(\omega_0 t) - \sin(\omega_0 t) \big) = \sqrt{2}\,d\cos\left( \omega_0 t + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) .
$$
The cosine vanishes for the first time when its argument equals $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so $\omega_0 t = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and the instant sought is $\dfrac{\pi}{4\,\omega_0}$. The particle reaches the origin before a quarter of a period, because it starts already moving towards it.
