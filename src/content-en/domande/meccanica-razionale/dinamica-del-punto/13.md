A smooth circular guide of radius $R$ is placed in a vertical plane and rotates with constant angular velocity $\omega$ about its own vertical diameter, as in the figure; a bead of mass $m$ is threaded on it. Setting $\omega^2 = \dfrac{3 g}{2 R}$ and denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle between the radius that locates the bead and the downward vertical, which are the configurations of relative equilibrium with $\sin\vartheta \neq 0$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2,foro" nodi="C,0,0" punti="0,0;1.491,-1.333" tratteggi="0,2.9 0,-2.9;0,0 1.491,-1.333" coppie="0 2.5,1,ω" etichette="0.42,-1.1,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Circular guide rotating about its own vertical diameter"}

- $\vartheta = \pm 41.81^\circ$
- $\vartheta = \pm 48.19^\circ$
- $\vartheta = \pm 60^\circ$
- $\vartheta = \pm 70.53^\circ$

## Explanation

In the frame attached to the guide there act the weight and the centrifugal force, both conservative, with total potential
$$
U(\vartheta) = m g R\cos\vartheta + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\omega^2R^2\sin^2\vartheta ,
$$
because the height of the bead is $-R\cos\vartheta$ and its distance from the axis of rotation is $R\sin\vartheta$. Differentiating one obtains $U'(\vartheta) = m R\sin\vartheta\,( \omega^2R\cos\vartheta - g )$, therefore the configurations with $\sin\vartheta \neq 0$ satisfy $\cos\vartheta = \dfrac{g}{\omega^2 R}$. With $\omega^2 = \dfrac{3 g}{2 R}$ it turns out that $\cos\vartheta = \dfrac{2}{3}$, therefore $\vartheta = \pm\arccos\dfrac{2}{3} \approx \pm 48.19^\circ$.
