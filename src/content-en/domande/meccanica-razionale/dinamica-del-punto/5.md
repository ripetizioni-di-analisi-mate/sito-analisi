An ideal spring of constant $k$ and rest length $\ell_0$ hangs from the ceiling at $A$; to its other end a particle of mass $m$ is attached, which moves along the vertical. The particle is brought to the distance $a$ below the equilibrium position and released from rest. What is the maximum tension of the spring during the motion?

- $k\,a$
- $m g + k\,a$
- $m g$
- $2 m g + k\,a$

## Explanation

In the equilibrium position the elastic force balances the weight, therefore the static elongation equals $\delta = \dfrac{m g}{k}$. The particle starts from rest at the distance $a$ below the equilibrium and the motion is harmonic about the equilibrium with amplitude $a$, therefore the maximum elongation of the spring is $\delta + a$. The tension is proportional to the elongation, hence its maximum value equals
$$
k\,( \delta + a ) = m g + k\,a ,
$$
and it is reached at the lowest position of the trajectory.
