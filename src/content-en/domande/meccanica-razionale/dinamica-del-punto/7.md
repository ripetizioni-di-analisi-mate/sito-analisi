A particle of mass $m$ slides along the horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide in the figure, connected to the wall by an ideal spring of constant $k$ and by a damper that exerts the force $-h\,\dot{x}\,\hat{\imath}$, with $h = \sqrt{3 m k}$. What is the pseudo angular frequency of the motion?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,3.6,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180" linee="0,0 0.3,0.35 0.9,-0.35 1.5,0.35 2.1,-0.35 2.7,0.35 3.3,-0.35 3.6,0;3.6,0 6.2,0;3.6,0 3.6,-0.8;0,-0.8 0.6,-0.8;1.2,-0.8 3.6,-0.8;0.6,-1.15 1.8,-1.15 1.8,-0.45 0.6,-0.45 0.6,-1.15;1.2,-1.05 1.2,-0.55" punti="3.6,0" etichette="1.8,0.75,k;1.2,-1.5,h" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle on a smooth guide with a spring of constant $k$ and a damper of constant $h = \sqrt{3 m k}$"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$

## Explanation

The equation of motion is $\ddot{x} + 2\gamma\,\dot{x} + \omega_0^2\,x = 0$, with $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$ and $\gamma = \dfrac{h}{2m} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$. Since $\gamma < \omega_0$, the damping is weak and the pseudo angular frequency equals $\omega_s = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \gamma^2}$. Substituting one obtains
$$
\omega_s = \omega_0\sqrt{1 - \frac{3}{4}} = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} ,
$$
therefore the pseudo-period is twice the period that one would have without the damper.
