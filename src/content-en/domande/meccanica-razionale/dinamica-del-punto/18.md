A particle of mass $m$ is launched from the origin in the vertical plane $Oxy$ with velocity of magnitude $v_0$ inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal, subject to the weight alone. At which abscissa does the particle return to the starting height?

- $\dfrac{v_0^2}{2g}$
- $\dfrac{v_0^2}{g}$
- $\dfrac{2 v_0^2}{g}$
- $\dfrac{v_0^2}{\sqrt{2}\,g}$

## Explanation

The force is constant, therefore $x(t) = v_0\cos 45^\circ\,t$ and $y(t) = v_0\sin 45^\circ\,t - \dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2$. The starting height is reached again when $y = 0$ with $t > 0$, that is, at the instant $t_1 = \dfrac{2 v_0\sin 45^\circ}{g}$. Substituting into the abscissa one obtains
$$
x(t_1) = \frac{2 v_0^2\sin 45^\circ\cos 45^\circ}{g} = \frac{v_0^2\sin 90^\circ}{g} ,
$$
therefore the range equals $\dfrac{v_0^2}{g}$, which is the largest possible value for a given initial velocity.
