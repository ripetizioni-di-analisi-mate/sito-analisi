A particle of mass $m = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides along a horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide, connected to the wall by an ideal spring of constant $k = 50\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and by a damper that exerts the force $-h\,\dot{x}\,\hat{\imath}$. For which value of $h$ is the damping critical?

- $10\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

The equation of motion is $\ddot{x} + 2\gamma\,\dot{x} + \omega_0^2\,x = 0$, with $\gamma = \dfrac{h}{2m}$ and $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$. The damping is critical when the two roots of the characteristic equation coincide, that is, when $\gamma = \omega_0$, a condition equivalent to
$$
h = 2\sqrt{m k} .
$$
With the assigned data $m k = 100\,\mathrm{kg^2/s^2}$, therefore $h = 20\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. For this value the particle returns to the rest position in the shortest time without oscillating.
