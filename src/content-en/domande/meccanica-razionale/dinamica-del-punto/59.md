A particle $P$ of mass $m$ slides along the smooth and fixed horizontal rectilinear guide in the figure, and is connected to the fixed point $A$, at the distance $d$ from the guide, by an ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero rest length. The particle is released from rest at an arbitrary position on the guide. Which statement about the motion is true?

- It is periodic but not harmonic, because the spring is oblique
- It is harmonic, with a period that grows with $d$
- It is harmonic with period $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$
- It is harmonic with period $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$, whatever $d$ is

## Explanation

The origin $O$ is taken at the foot of the perpendicular drawn from $A$ to the guide, so that $A - O = d\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $P - O = x\,\hat{\imath}$. The spring has zero rest length, therefore its force is
$$
-k\,(P - A) = -k\,x\,\hat{\imath} + k\,d\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The component orthogonal to the guide, together with the weight, is balanced by the constraint reaction, and the guide is smooth, so along the guide only the component $-k\,x$ acts. The equation of motion is thus $m\,\ddot{x} = -k\,x$, that of the harmonic oscillator with angular frequency $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$, and the period $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$ does not depend on $d$. With a nonzero rest length the component along the guide would no longer be proportional to $x$.
