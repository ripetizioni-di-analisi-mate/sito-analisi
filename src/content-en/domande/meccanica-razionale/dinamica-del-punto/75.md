A particle of mass $m$ moves on a smooth and fixed horizontal plane $Oxy$ and is subject, in addition to the weight and the reaction of the plane, to the force
$$
-k\,( x\,\hat{\imath} + 4y\,\hat{\jmath} ) ,
\qquad
k > 0 .
$$
At the initial instant the particle is at rest at the position $(x_0, y_0)$, with $x_0 \neq 0$ and $y_0 \neq 0$. The motion is periodic; what is its period?

- $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$
- $4\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{5k}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$

## Explanation

The weight and the reaction of the smooth plane are vertical and balance each other, therefore in the plane only the given force acts, and the components of the fundamental equation are decoupled:
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,\ddot{x} = -k\,x \\[4pt]
m\,\ddot{y} = -4k\,y
\end{cases}
$$
Setting $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$, with zero initial velocity one obtains $x(t) = x_0\cos(\omega t)$ and $y(t) = y_0\cos(2\omega t)$. The first has period $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ and the second $\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$, which is half the first, so both return to their initial values after $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$. Before that instant $x(t) \neq x_0$, because $x_0 \neq 0$, therefore the period is $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$. Since $\cos(2\omega t) = 2\cos^2(\omega t) - 1$, the trajectory is the arc of the parabola $y = y_0\left( \dfrac{2x^2}{x_0^2} - 1 \right)$ with $|x| \le |x_0|$, traversed back and forth.
