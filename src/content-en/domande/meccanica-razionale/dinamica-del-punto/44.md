A smooth rectilinear guide is placed in a horizontal plane and rotates with constant angular velocity $\omega$ about the vertical axis through its point $O$. A particle of mass $m$, threaded on the guide, is connected to $O$ by an ideal spring of constant $k$, with $k > m\,\omega^2$, and zero rest length. Denoting by $s$ the abscissa of the particle on the guide, measured from $O$, what is the angular frequency of the motion relative to the guide?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m} + \omega^2}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m} - 2\omega^2}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m} - \omega^2}$

## Explanation

In the frame attached to the guide there act the elastic force $-k\,s$, the centrifugal force $m\,\omega^2 s$, directed outwards, and the Coriolis force, which is orthogonal to the guide and is balanced by the constraint reaction. The equation of the relative motion along the guide is therefore
$$
m\,\ddot{s} = -k\,s + m\,\omega^2 s ,
\qquad\text{that is}\qquad
\ddot{s} + \left( \frac{k}{m} - \omega^2 \right)s = 0 .
$$
Since $k > m\,\omega^2$ the coefficient is positive, therefore the relative motion is harmonic with angular frequency $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m} - \omega^2}$, smaller than the angular frequency that one would have with the guide at rest.
