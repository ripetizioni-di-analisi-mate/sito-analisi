A simple pendulum of length $\ell$ performs small oscillations with period $T$. Which length must a simple pendulum have in order to perform small oscillations with period $2T$?

- $2\ell$
- $4\ell$
- $\sqrt{2}\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{\ell}{4}$

## Explanation

The period of the small oscillations of a simple pendulum equals $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{g}}$, therefore it is proportional to the square root of the length. Calling $\ell'$ the length sought, the condition
$$
2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ell'}{g}} = 2 \cdot 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ell}{g}}
$$
gives $\sqrt{\ell'} = 2\sqrt{\ell}$. Squaring one obtains $\ell' = 4\ell$; the mass does not appear, since the period does not depend on it.
