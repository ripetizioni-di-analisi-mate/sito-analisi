A particle of mass $m$ slides along a smooth and fixed horizontal rectilinear guide and is subject to a force with potential energy
$$
V(x) = V_0\left( e^{-\frac{2x}{a}} - 2\,e^{-\frac{x}{a}} \right) ,
\qquad
V_0 > 0 ,
\qquad
a > 0 .
$$
For which values of the mechanical energy $E$ is the motion periodic?

- $-V_0 < E < 0$
- $E < V_0$
- $-V_0 < E < V_0$
- $E > -V_0$

## Explanation

The derivative of the potential energy is
$$
V'(x) = \frac{2V_0}{a}\,e^{-\frac{2x}{a}}\left( e^{\frac{x}{a}} - 1 \right) ,
$$
which is negative for $x < 0$ and positive for $x > 0$, therefore $x = 0$ is the only minimum point of $V$, with $V(0) = -V_0$. Moreover $V(x) \to +\infty$ for $x \to -\infty$, while for $x > 0$ the function is increasing and negative and tends to $0$ for $x \to +\infty$. If $-V_0 < E < 0$, the set where $V(x) \le E$ is a bounded interval at whose endpoints $V' \neq 0$, so the motion is periodic between two turning points. If $E \ge 0$ one has $V(x) < E$ for every $x > 0$, therefore the particle moves indefinitely towards increasing $x$; if $E = -V_0$ the motion is rest at $x = 0$.
