A particle of mass $m$ slides along the horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide in the figure, and is connected to the two opposite walls by two ideal springs of constants $k$ and $3k$. In the configuration of the drawing both springs have their rest length. What is the period of the oscillations?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;P,4,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" linee="0,0 0.25,0.35 0.75,-0.35 1.25,0.35 1.75,-0.35 2.25,0.35 2.75,-0.35 3.2,0;4.8,0 5.2,0.35 5.7,-0.35 6.2,0.35 6.7,-0.35 7.2,0.35 7.6,-0.35 8,0;3.2,0 4.8,0" punti="4,0" etichette="1.6,0.75,k;6.4,0.75,3k" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle on a smooth horizontal guide connected to the two walls by two springs of constants $k$ and $3k$"}

- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3m}{4k}}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{\sqrt{3}}\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$
- $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$

## Explanation

Displacing the particle by $x$ towards the right, one spring stretches by $x$ and the other shortens by the same amount, therefore both pull it back towards the initial configuration and the two forces add up. The resultant force is $-( k + 3k )\,x = -4k\,x$, therefore the equivalent constant equals $k_{\text{eq}} = 4k$ and the motion is harmonic with angular frequency $\sqrt{\dfrac{4k}{m}}$. It follows that
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{4k}} = \pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}} ,
$$
that is, half of the period that one would have with the spring of constant $k$ alone.
