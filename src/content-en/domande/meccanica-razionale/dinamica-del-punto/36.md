A particle of mass $m$ rests at the vertex $S$ of the smooth and fixed spherical cap of radius $R$ in the figure, with the center $C$ on the vertical, and slides from rest along a meridian. The constraint is unilateral, because the cap can only push the particle outwards. At which height above $C$ does the detachment occur?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" nodi="C,0,0;S,0,2" linee="-2.6,-2 2.6,-2" punti="0,2;0,0" tratteggi="0,0 0,2.55" quote="C,S,R,-0.55" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle at rest at the vertex $S$ of the smooth spherical cap of radius $R$"}

- $\dfrac{R}{3}$
- $\dfrac{R}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3R}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2R}{3}$

## Explanation

Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle between the upward vertical and the segment joining $C$ with the particle, the conservation of mechanical energy gives $v^2 = 2 g R\,( 1 - \cos\vartheta )$, since the particle starts from rest and the reaction of the smooth constraint has zero power. The second intrinsic equation, with the normal directed towards $C$, is
$$
m\,\frac{v^2}{R} = m g\cos\vartheta - \Phi ,
$$
and the detachment occurs when $\Phi = 0$, that is, when $v^2 = g R\cos\vartheta$. Equating the two expressions of $v^2$ one obtains $\cos\vartheta_d = \dfrac{2}{3}$, therefore the height of the particle above $C$ equals $R\cos\vartheta_d = \dfrac{2R}{3}$.
