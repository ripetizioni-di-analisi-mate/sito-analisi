A particle of mass $m$ hangs at $O$ from an inextensible massless string of length $\ell$, and moves in the vertical plane. The particle is launched from the lowest position with horizontal velocity of magnitude $v_0$, as in the figure. What is the minimum value of $v_0$ for which the particle travels the whole circle with the string always taut?

- $\sqrt{5 g\ell}$
- $2\sqrt{g\ell}$
- $\sqrt{3 g\ell}$
- $\sqrt{g\ell}$

## Explanation

As long as the string is taut, the conservation of mechanical energy gives $\ell^2\dot{\vartheta}^2 = v_0^2 - 2 g\ell\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)$, with $\vartheta$ measured from the downward vertical. The tension is $\Phi = m g\cos\vartheta + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$, therefore
$$
\Phi = \frac{m\,v_0^2}{\ell} - 2 m g + 3 m g\cos\vartheta ,
$$
which is minimum at the highest point, $\vartheta = \pi$, where it equals $\dfrac{m\,v_0^2}{\ell} - 5 m g$. The string remains taut along the whole circle if and only if this value is nonnegative, so $v_0 \ge \sqrt{5 g\ell}$. The value $2\sqrt{g\ell}$ would suffice for a particle attached to a rigid rod, which can also push it outwards, while the string can only pull it towards $O$.
