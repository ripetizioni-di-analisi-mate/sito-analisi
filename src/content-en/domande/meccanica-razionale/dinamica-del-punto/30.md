Two one-dimensional oscillators have equal mass and potentials $V_1(x)=ax^2$ and $V_2(x)=ax^2+bx^4$, with $a,b>0$. Can a measurement of the period only in the limit of vanishing amplitude distinguish the models?

- The second period is always twice as long.
- No period difference in that limit, because the curvatures at zero agree.
- The second period tends to zero.
- The first model is unstable at zero.

## Explanation

Both potentials have second derivative $2a$ at zero. The linearised angular frequency is therefore $\sqrt{2a/m}$ in either case. The quartic term affects motion beyond the linear approximation.
