A particle of mass $m$ slides along a horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide, and is subject to a force of potential energy
$$
V(x) = V_0\left( \frac{x^4}{a^4} - 2\,\frac{x^2}{a^2} \right) ,
\qquad
V_0 > 0 ,
\qquad
a > 0 .
$$
What is the angular frequency of the small oscillations about $x = a$?

- $\dfrac{1}{a}\sqrt{\dfrac{2 V_0}{m}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{a}\sqrt{\dfrac{2 V_0}{m}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{a}\sqrt{\dfrac{V_0}{m}}$
- $\dfrac{4}{a}\sqrt{\dfrac{V_0}{m}}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the potential energy is
$$
V'(x) = \frac{4 V_0\,x}{a^2}\left( \frac{x^2}{a^2} - 1 \right) ,
$$
therefore the equilibrium configurations are $x = 0$ and $x = \pm a$. The second derivative is $V''(x) = V_0\left( \dfrac{12 x^2}{a^4} - \dfrac{4}{a^2} \right)$, therefore $V''(a) = \dfrac{8 V_0}{a^2} > 0$ and $x = a$ is a point of minimum of $V$. The angular frequency of the small oscillations then equals $\sqrt{\dfrac{V''(a)}{m}} = \dfrac{2}{a}\sqrt{\dfrac{2 V_0}{m}}$.
