A simple pendulum of length $\ell$ oscillates in the vertical plane with amplitude $\vartheta_0 = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, measured from the downward vertical. What is the magnitude of the acceleration of the particle at the extreme position $\vartheta = \vartheta_0$?

- $0$
- $g$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,g$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g$

## Explanation

At the extreme position the velocity is zero, therefore the normal component of the acceleration, $\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$, is zero. The tangential component is obtained from the pure equation of motion $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{g}{\ell}\sin\vartheta$, so its magnitude is $\ell\,|\ddot{\vartheta}| = g\sin\vartheta_0$. With $\vartheta_0 = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ the magnitude of the acceleration is thus $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,g$, and the acceleration is tangent to the trajectory and directed towards the equilibrium position. At that instant the tension of the string equals $m g\cos\vartheta_0$ and balances the normal component of the weight.
