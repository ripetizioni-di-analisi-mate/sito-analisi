A particle not subject to forces moves with uniform rectilinear motion, with velocity of magnitude $v$, along a straight line at distance $d$ from the fixed point $O$, as in the figure. What is its areal velocity with respect to $O$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;H,0,2" linee="-2,2 5,2" tratteggi="0,0 0,2" punti="0,0;2.5,2,P" frecce="2.5,2.4,3.9,2.4,v" quote="O,H,d,-0.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle in uniform rectilinear motion along a straight line at distance $d$ from $O$"}

- $v\,d$
- $0$
- $2\,v\,d$
- $\dfrac{v\,d}{2}$

## Explanation

The force is zero, therefore it is parallel to $P - O$ at every instant and the motion is central with center $O$; hence the angular momentum $\vec{K}_O$ and the areal velocity are constant. At the instant when the particle passes through the foot $H$ of the perpendicular drawn from $O$ to the line, the velocity is orthogonal to the position vector $H - O$, of length $d$, therefore $|\vec{K}_O| = m\,d\,v$. Since $|\vec{K}_O| = m\,|c|$, it follows that $|c| = v\,d$ and the areal velocity equals $\dfrac{v\,d}{2}$. The same value is obtained from the area of the triangle swept by the position vector in the time $\Delta t$, which has base $v\,\Delta t$ on the line and height $d$.
