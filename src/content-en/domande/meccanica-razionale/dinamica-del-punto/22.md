A free oscillator of angular frequency $\omega$ is at $x=A$ with zero velocity. At that instant the spring anchor is rapidly shifted by $A/2$ in the same direction without imparting an impulse to the mass. What is the amplitude relative to the new equilibrium?

- $A$
- $\dfrac A2$
- $\dfrac{3A}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Equilibrium shifts by $A/2$, but the mass retains its instantaneous position and velocity. It is therefore at a turning point a distance $A-A/2$ from the new equilibrium.
