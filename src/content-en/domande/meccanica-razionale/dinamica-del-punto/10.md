A particle of mass $m$ slides along the half-line $r > 0$ of a smooth and fixed rectilinear guide, and is subject to a force of potential energy
$$
V(r) = -\frac{A}{r} + \frac{B}{r^2} ,
\qquad
A > 0 ,
\qquad
B > 0 .
$$
At which abscissa is the equilibrium configuration?

- $\dfrac{B}{A}$
- $\dfrac{2B}{A}$
- $\dfrac{A}{2B}$
- $\dfrac{B}{2A}$

## Explanation

The equilibrium configuration is a stationary point of the potential energy, therefore
$$
V'(r) = \frac{A}{r^2} - \frac{2B}{r^3}
$$
vanishes. Multiplying by $r^3$, which is positive on the half-line, one obtains $A\,r - 2B = 0$, therefore $r_e = \dfrac{2B}{A}$. Since $V''(r) = -\dfrac{2A}{r^3} + \dfrac{6B}{r^4}$ takes at $r_e$ the value $\dfrac{A^4}{8B^3} > 0$, the abscissa found is a point of minimum of $V$ and the equilibrium is stable.
