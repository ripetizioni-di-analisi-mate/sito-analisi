A particle of mass $m$ is at the vertex $S$ of the smooth and fixed cylindrical surface of radius $R$ in the figure, with horizontal axis, and is launched with velocity $v_0$ in a plane orthogonal to the axis. The constraint is unilateral, because the surface can only push the particle outwards. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle measured from the upward vertical, what is the cosine of the angle of detachment?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" nodi="C,0,0;S,0,2" linee="-2.6,-2 2.6,-2" punti="0,2;0,0" frecce="0.2,2.3,1.6,2.3,v_0" tratteggi="0,0 0,2.55" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle launched from the vertex of a smooth cylindrical surface of radius $R$"}

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{v_0^2}{g R}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{v_0^2}{3 g R}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{v_0^2}{3 g R}$

## Explanation

As long as the particle remains in contact it moves on the circle of radius $R$ and the reaction of the smooth constraint has zero power, therefore the conservation of mechanical energy gives $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2 g R\,( 1 - \cos\vartheta )$. The second intrinsic equation, with the normal directed towards the center, is
$$
m\,\frac{v^2}{R} = m g\cos\vartheta - \Phi ,
$$
where $\Phi \geq 0$ is the thrust of the surface. The detachment occurs when $\Phi = 0$, therefore $v^2 = g R\cos\vartheta$; equating the two expressions of $v^2$ and solving for the cosine one obtains $\cos\vartheta_d = \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{v_0^2}{3 g R}$.
