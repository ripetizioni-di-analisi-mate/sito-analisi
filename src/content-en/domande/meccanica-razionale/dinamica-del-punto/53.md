A small ring of mass $m$ is threaded on a smooth and fixed guide, placed in a vertical plane, which has the shape of the catenary
$$
y = a\left( \cosh\frac{x}{a} - 1 \right) ,
\qquad
a > 0 ,
$$
with the $y$ axis vertical and pointing upward. What is the period of the small oscillations of the ring about the lowest point of the guide?

- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{2g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2a}{g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{g}}$
- $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{g}}$

## Explanation

The guide is smooth and fixed, therefore the motion is determined by the pure equation $m\,\ddot{s} = -m g\sin\beta$, where $s$ is the arc length measured from the lowest point $O$ and $\beta$ is the angle that the tangent forms with the horizontal. One has $\tan\beta = y'(x) = \sinh\dfrac{x}{a}$, and near $O$ one has $\sin\beta \approx \tan\beta \approx \dfrac{x}{a}$ and $s \approx x$ up to higher order infinitesimals. The linearized equation is therefore
$$
\ddot{s} + \frac{g}{a}\,s = 0 ,
$$
that of the harmonic oscillator with angular frequency $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{a}}$, and the period is $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{g}}$. This value coincides with the period of the small oscillations of a simple pendulum of length $a$, which is the radius of curvature of the catenary at its lowest point.
