A homogeneous solid sphere rolls without slipping on a plane inclined by $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal. What is the minimum coefficient of static friction compatible with pure rolling?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{9} \simeq 0.19$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{15} \simeq 0.23$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{21} \simeq 0.16$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \simeq 0.58$

## Explanation

The homogeneous solid sphere has $I_G = \dfrac{2}{5}\,m\,R^2$, hence $\beta = \dfrac{2}{5}$. By the theorem on rolling without slipping on an inclined plane, pure rolling is possible as long as
$$
\tan\alpha \le \frac{1 + \beta}{\beta}\,f_s ,
$$
a condition that is rewritten as $f_s \ge \dfrac{\beta}{1+\beta}\,\tan\alpha = \dfrac{2}{7}\,\tan\alpha$. With $\alpha = 30^\circ$ one has $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$, therefore the minimum coefficient is $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{21} \simeq 0.16$. The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{9} \simeq 0.19$ is obtained with $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$, that is for the solid disk, and the value $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \simeq 0.58$ coincides with $\tan\alpha$, that is with the condition of a body that does not rotate.
