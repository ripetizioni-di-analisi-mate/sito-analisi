Two friction wheels, modeled as homogeneous disks cut from the same metal sheet, have radii $R$ and $2R$ and rotate about fixed, parallel, smooth axes through their centers. The small wheel turns with angular velocity $\omega_0$ and the large one is at rest; the two wheels are brought into contact at the rim, as in the diagram in the figure, and after a short slipping they roll on each other without slipping. What is the final angular velocity of the small wheel?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1;3,0,2" punti="0,0,O_1,centro;3,0,O_2,centro" quote="0 0,0 1,R,-0.35;3 0,3 -2,2R,0.4" coppie="-0.55 0.55,1,ω_0" riferimento=0 titolo="Two friction wheels of radii $R$ and $2R$ brought into contact at the rim"}

- $\dfrac{\omega_0}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\omega_0}{9}$
- $\dfrac{\omega_0}{\sqrt{5}}$
- $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2}$

## Explanation

The sheet is the same, hence the mass is proportional to the area and, if $m$ is the mass of the small wheel, the large one has mass $4m$. The moments of inertia are $I_1 = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$ and $I_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}\,(4m)(2R)^2 = 16\,I_1$. During the slipping the friction force at the rim has the same magnitude on the two wheels, with arms $R$ and $2R$ with respect to the respective axes; denoting by $J$ its impulse, the equation of motion about each fixed axis, integrated in time, and the final rolling condition give
$$
\begin{cases}
I_1\,(\omega_0 - \omega_1) = J\,R \\[4pt]
I_2\,\omega_2 = 2\,J\,R \\[4pt]
\omega_1\,R = \omega_2\,(2R)
\end{cases}
$$
Substituting $J\,R$ from the first equation and $\omega_2 = \dfrac{\omega_1}{2}$ into the second one obtains $8\,I_1\,\omega_1 = 2\,I_1\,(\omega_0 - \omega_1)$, whence $\omega_1 = \dfrac{\omega_0}{5}$. The value $\dfrac{\omega_0}{9}$ is obtained by imposing conservation of the total angular momentum, which does not hold because the reactions of the two axes have nonzero resultant moment; $\dfrac{\omega_0}{\sqrt{5}}$ imposes conservation of energy, which does not hold because friction dissipates energy during the slipping, and $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2}$ treats the two wheels as equal.
