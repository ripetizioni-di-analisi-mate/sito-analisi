A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is welded by its midpoint $G$ to a vertical shaft, with which it forms the constant angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$; the point $G$ lies on the axis of rotation, halfway between the two bearings, which are at distance $h$ from each other, as in the diagram in the figure. The shaft has negligible mass and turns with constant angular velocity $\omega$. What is the magnitude of the transverse reactions of the bearings?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;B,0,4;D,1,3.73,-;E,-1,0.27,-" aste="O-B;D-E" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,cerniera,90" punti="0,2,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,2 0,3.7" etichette="0.27,3,α" quote="O,B,h,-0.95" titolo="Rod welded by its midpoint to a vertical shaft, inclined by $\alpha = 30^\circ$"}

- $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{48\,h}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{24\,h}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{48\,h}$

## Explanation

The center of mass lies on the axis, hence $\vec{a}_G = \vec{0}$ and the first cardinal equation produces no reactions; the terms due to the products of inertia remain. In the body-fixed frame with $\xi$ in the plane of the rod and of the shaft one has
$$
I_{\xi\zeta} = \frac{m}{\ell}\int_{-\ell/2}^{\ell/2} s^2 \sin\alpha \cos\alpha \,ds = \frac{m\,\ell^2}{24}\,\sin 2\alpha ,
$$
while $I_{\eta\zeta} = 0$ by symmetry. Since $\omega$ is constant one has $\ddot{\vartheta} = 0$, therefore by the theorem on the reactions of a fixed axis the two transverse reactions are equal and opposite and their magnitude equals $\dfrac{I_{\xi\zeta}\,\omega^2}{h} = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2\,\sin 2\alpha}{24\,h}$. With $\alpha = 30^\circ$ one has $\sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, hence the magnitude is $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{48\,h}$. The answer $0$ would correspond to a system balanced dynamically as well, a condition that here is not satisfied because $I_{\xi\zeta} \neq 0$.
