A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ carries a point mass $m$ welded at the end $B$, and the system oscillates about a fixed smooth horizontal axis through the end $A$ and perpendicular to the rod, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the reduced length?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,-3.2" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" cerchi="0,-3.2,0.3" punti="0,-1.6,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 0,-4" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.9" titolo="Rod with a point mass welded at the end $B$"}

- $\dfrac{8\,\ell}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3\,\ell}{4}$
- $\ell$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia is additive, hence $I_A = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3} + m\,\ell^2 = \dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$, where the second term is the contribution of the point mass placed at distance $\ell$ from the axis. The total mass is $2m$ and the center of mass of the system is at distance
$$
d = \frac{m\,\dfrac{\ell}{2} + m\,\ell}{2m} = \frac{3\,\ell}{4}
$$
from the axis. The reduced length therefore equals $\ell_r = \dfrac{I_A}{2m\,d} = \dfrac{4\,m\,\ell^2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3\,m\,\ell} = \dfrac{8\,\ell}{9}$. The value $\dfrac{3\,\ell}{4}$ is the distance of the center of mass from the axis, which for a compound pendulum is always smaller than the reduced length, while $\dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$ is the reduced length of the rod alone and $\ell$ that of the point mass alone.
