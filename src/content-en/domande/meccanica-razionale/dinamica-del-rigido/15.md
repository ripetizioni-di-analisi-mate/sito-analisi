A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ turns with angular velocity $\omega$ about a fixed horizontal axis through its end $A$ and perpendicular to the rod. What is the kinetic energy?

- $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{24}$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{6}$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{2}$

## Explanation

The rod has a fixed axis, therefore its state of motion is rotational and the kinetic energy equals $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_a\,\omega^2$, where $I_a$ is the moment of inertia with respect to the axis of rotation. The moment of inertia of a homogeneous rod with respect to the axis through one end, perpendicular to the rod, is $I_a = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$. Substituting one obtains
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{m\,\ell^2}{3}\,\omega^2 = \frac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{6} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{24}$ is obtained by using the moment of inertia about the center of mass $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{12}$, which is the one with respect to a parallel axis through the midpoint, while $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{3}$ comes from forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
