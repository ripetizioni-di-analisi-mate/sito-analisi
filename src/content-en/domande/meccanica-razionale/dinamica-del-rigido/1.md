A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a plane inclined by $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal, as the diagram in the figure shows, and starts from rest. What is the friction force at the contact point?

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,3.46 6,0 0,0" cerchi="2.57,2.48,0.86" punti="2.57,2.48,G,baricentro;3,1.73,C,centro" etichette="1.05,0.26,α;1.75,3.3,m, R" titolo="Homogeneous disk on the plane inclined by $\alpha = 30^\circ$"}

- $\dfrac{m\,g}{4}$
- $\dfrac{m\,g}{6}$
- $\dfrac{m\,g}{2}$
- $\dfrac{m\,g}{7}$

## Explanation

The homogeneous disk has moment of inertia about the center of mass $I_G = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$, hence in the form $I_G = \beta\,m\,R^2$ one has $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$. By the theorem on **rolling without slipping on an inclined plane** the friction force equals
$$
F_a = \frac{\beta}{1 + \beta}\,m\,g\,\sin\alpha = \frac{1}{3}\,m\,g\,\sin\alpha ,
$$
therefore with $\alpha = 30^\circ$ one obtains $F_a = \dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{m\,g}{2} = \dfrac{m\,g}{6}$, directed up along the plane. The value $\dfrac{m\,g}{2}$ is the component of the weight along the plane, which would balance the friction only if the center did not accelerate. The value $\dfrac{m\,g}{7}$ is obtained with $\beta = \dfrac{2}{5}$, that is for the solid sphere, and the value $\dfrac{m\,g}{4}$ with $\beta = 1$, that is for the thin ring.
