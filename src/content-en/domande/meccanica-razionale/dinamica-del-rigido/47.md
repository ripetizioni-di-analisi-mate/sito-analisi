A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is at rest on a smooth horizontal plane. At time $t = 0$ a horizontal force $\vec{F}$, perpendicular to the rod, is applied at the end $A$, as in the diagram in the figure seen from above. What is the acceleration of the end $B$ at the initial instant?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" punti="0,2,G,baricentro" forze="0 0,1.2,0," etichette="-0.7,-0.4,F" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Free rod on a smooth horizontal plane, seen from above, with the force $\vec{F}$ applied at the end $A$"}

- $\dfrac{F}{m}$, in the direction of $\vec{F}$
- $\dfrac{4\,F}{m}$, in the direction of $\vec{F}$
- $\dfrac{F}{4\,m}$, in the direction of $\vec{F}$
- $\dfrac{2\,F}{m}$, opposite to $\vec{F}$

## Explanation

The weight and the reaction of the plane are vertical and balance each other, hence in the horizontal plane only $\vec{F}$ acts and the rod is a free rigid body in planar motion. The first cardinal equation gives $a_G = \dfrac{F}{m}$ in the direction of $\vec{F}$, and the second one with pole at $G$ gives
$$
\frac{m\,\ell^2}{12}\,\ddot{\vartheta} = F\,\frac{\ell}{2} ,
\qquad
\ddot{\vartheta} = \frac{6\,F}{m\,\ell} .
$$
At the initial instant the rod is at rest, therefore the acceleration of $B$ is the sum of $\vec{a}_G$ and of the term $\ddot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (B - G)$, which has magnitude $\ddot{\vartheta}\,\dfrac{\ell}{2} = \dfrac{3\,F}{m}$. The rotation produced by $\vec{F}$ moves $A$ in the direction of the force and $B$ in the opposite direction, hence $a_B = \dfrac{F}{m} - \dfrac{3\,F}{m} = -\dfrac{2\,F}{m}$, that is $\dfrac{2\,F}{m}$ opposite to $\vec{F}$. The value $\dfrac{F}{m}$ neglects the rotation, $\dfrac{4\,F}{m}$ is the acceleration of the end $A$, and $\dfrac{F}{4\,m}$ uses in the second cardinal equation the moment of inertia about an end, $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$, instead of the centroidal one.
