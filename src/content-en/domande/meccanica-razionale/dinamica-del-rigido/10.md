A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ rests with the end $A$ on a smooth horizontal plane and moves in a vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. At the initial instant the rod forms the angle $\vartheta_0 = 60^\circ$ with the plane and is at rest. What is the angular velocity at the instant when the rod reaches the plane?

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,0" nodi="A,1.2,0;B,3,3.12" aste="A-B" punti="2.1,1.56,G,baricentro" tratteggi="1.2,0 3.9,0" etichette="2,0.33,ϑ_0" titolo="Rod resting with one end on a smooth horizontal plane"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}\,g}{2\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}\,g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{6\sqrt{3}\,g}{\ell}}$

## Explanation

The plane is smooth, hence the external forces are all vertical and the horizontal resultant is zero; since the rod starts from rest, the abscissa of the center of mass stays constant and $G$ descends along a vertical line with $y_G = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta$. The kinetic energy is then
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\left( \frac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta} \right)^{\!2} + \frac{1}{2}\,\frac{m\,\ell^2}{12}\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 ,
$$
which for $\vartheta = 0$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$. The constraint is smooth and fixed, therefore the mechanical energy is conserved and the descent of the center of mass is $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,\ell}{4}$, whence
$$
\frac{m\,\ell^2}{6}\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = m\,g\,\frac{\sqrt{3}\,\ell}{4} .
$$
One therefore obtains $\dot{\vartheta} = \sqrt{\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}\,g}{2\,\ell}} \simeq 1.61\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\ell}}$. The value $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$ corresponds to a descent of $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, that is to the rod initially vertical, while $\sqrt{\dfrac{6\sqrt{3}\,g}{\ell}}$ is obtained by using the moment of inertia about the center of mass alone.
