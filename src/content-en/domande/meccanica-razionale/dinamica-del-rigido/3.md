Two bodies of masses $3m$ and $m$ hang from the two ends of an inextensible string of negligible mass that passes without slipping over a homogeneous pulley of mass $4m$ and radius $R$, with a fixed smooth horizontal axis, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the acceleration of the bodies?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.4" nodi="O,0,0;A,-1.4,0,-;B,1.4,0,-;C,-1.4,-2.4,-;D,1.4,-1.5,-" poligoni="-1.75,-2.4 -1.05,-2.4 -1.05,-3 -1.75,-3;1.05,-1.5 1.75,-1.5 1.75,-2.1 1.05,-2.1" catene="A-C;B-D" vincoli="O,cerniera" etichette="-2.3,-2.7,3m;2.1,-1.8,m;-1.6,1.2,4m, R" titolo="Pulley of mass $4m$ with two hanging bodies"}

- $\dfrac{g}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2\,g}{3}$
- $\dfrac{g}{4}$
- $\dfrac{g}{3}$

## Explanation

The pulley has $I_O = \dfrac{1}{2}\,(4m)\,R^2 = 2\,m\,R^2$, hence in the balance of the system it enters as an additional mass $\dfrac{I_O}{R^2} = 2m$. Denoting by $S_1$ and $S_2$ the tensions in the two branches and by $a$ the common acceleration, the equations are
$$
\begin{cases}
3\,m\,g - S_1 = 3\,m\,a \\[4pt]
S_2 - m\,g = m\,a \\[4pt]
2\,m\,R^2\,\dfrac{a}{R} = (S_1 - S_2)\,R
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the three equations the tensions cancel and one obtains $2\,m\,g = 6\,m\,a$, therefore $a = \dfrac{g}{3}$ and the body of mass $3m$ descends. The value $\dfrac{g}{2}$ is obtained by neglecting the inertia of the pulley and the value $\dfrac{g}{4}$ by assigning to it the moment of inertia $4\,m\,R^2$ of the ring, while $\dfrac{2\,g}{3}$ comes from putting in the numerator the sum of the two weights instead of their difference.
