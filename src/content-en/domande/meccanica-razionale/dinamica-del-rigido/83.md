A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at its upper end $A$ to a fixed smooth horizontal axis. An ideal spring of constant $k$ is attached to the end $B$; it is horizontal, has its other end fastened to a wall, and has its rest length when the rod is vertical, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the angular frequency of small oscillations about the vertical position?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,-3.6" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" punti="0,-1.8,G,baricentro" linee="0,-3.6 0.5,-3.6 0.7,-3.4 1.1,-3.8 1.5,-3.4 1.9,-3.8 2.3,-3.4 2.5,-3.6 3,-3.6;3,-2.9 3,-4.3" etichette="1.5,-3.05,k" forze="0 -1.8,0,-0.9,mg" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.6" titolo="Rod hinged at $A$ with a horizontal spring at the end $B$"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell} + \dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell} + \dfrac{3\,k}{4\,m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell} + \dfrac{3\,k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell} + \dfrac{3\,k}{m}}$

## Explanation

Let $\vartheta$ be the angle of the rod with the vertical; for small $\vartheta$ the end $B$ moves horizontally by $\ell\,\vartheta$, hence the spring, which to first order stays horizontal, exerts the force $-k\,\ell\,\vartheta$ with arm $\ell$ with respect to $A$. The weight has arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta \simeq \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\vartheta$, therefore the linearized equation of motion about a fixed axis is
$$
\frac{m\,\ell^2}{3}\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -\left( m\,g\,\frac{\ell}{2} + k\,\ell^2 \right)\vartheta .
$$
Dividing by $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$ one obtains the angular frequency $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell} + \dfrac{3\,k}{m}}$. The value with $\dfrac{k}{m}$ treats the spring as acting on a point mass, the one with $\dfrac{3\,k}{4\,m}$ gives the elastic force the arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, and the one with $\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}$ gives the weight the arm $\ell$.
