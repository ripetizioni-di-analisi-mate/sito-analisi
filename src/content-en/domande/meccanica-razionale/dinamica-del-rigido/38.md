A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ rests with the end $A$ on a smooth horizontal plane, moves in a vertical plane and is released from rest in an inclined position, as in the diagram in the figure. Which trajectory does the center of mass describe?

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,0" nodi="A,1.2,0;B,4.2,2.6" aste="A-B" punti="2.7,1.3,G,baricentro" forze="2.7 1.3,0,-1,mg" tratteggi="1.2,0 4.5,0;2.7,1.3 2.7,-0.6" etichette="2.05,0.3,ϑ" titolo="Rod resting with the end $A$ on a smooth horizontal plane"}

- A circle of center $A$ and radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$
- A vertical segment, because the resultant of the horizontal external forces is zero
- An arc of ellipse, because the end $A$ slides on the plane while the rod rotates
- An arc of parabola, because the rod is falling

## Explanation

The external forces are the weight, which is vertical, and the reaction of the plane, which is smooth and hence exerts a normal reaction, vertical as well. The resultant of the horizontal external forces is therefore zero and the first cardinal equation gives $m\,\ddot{x}_G = 0$. The rod starts from rest, hence $\dot{x}_G = 0$ at every instant and the abscissa of the center of mass stays constant, so that $G$ travels along a vertical segment. The end $A$ slides on the plane, because the plane is smooth, and the end $B$ describes an arc of ellipse, while the center of mass stays on the same vertical line.
