A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the end $A$ to a fixed smooth horizontal axis, perpendicular to the vertical plane of the motion, as in the diagram in the figure. The rod is released from rest in the horizontal position. What is the initial angular acceleration?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" punti="1.6,0,G,baricentro" forze="1.6 0,0,-1,mg" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.9" coppie="0.7 0,-1,ϑ" titolo="Rod hinged at $A$, initially horizontal and at rest"}

- $\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}$
- $\dfrac{g}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{6\,g}{\ell}$

## Explanation

The rod turns about a fixed axis with ideal constraints, hence $I_A\,\ddot{\vartheta} = M_A^{(e)}$ holds, where the moment is that of the weight alone. The moment of inertia of a homogeneous rod with respect to the axis through one end is $I_A = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$ and the weight is applied at the center of mass, which in the horizontal position has arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$. Therefore
$$
\frac{m\,\ell^2}{3}\,\ddot{\vartheta} = m\,g\,\frac{\ell}{2} ,
$$
whence $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}$. The value $\dfrac{6\,g}{\ell}$ is obtained by using the moment of inertia about the center of mass $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{12}$, which is not the one with respect to the axis of rotation, while $\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}$ comes from taking $\ell$ as the arm of the weight instead of $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$.
