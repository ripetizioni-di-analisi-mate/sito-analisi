A homogeneous disk of mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R = 15\,\mathrm{cm}$ is keyed on a small shaft of negligible mass pivoted at $O$ and turns about its own axis of figure with constant spin velocity $\dot{\varphi} = 200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The axis of figure is horizontal and the center of the disk is at distance $d = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from $O$, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the precession velocity of the axis about the vertical line through $O$? Assume $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;B,3.4,0,-" aste="O-B" cerchi="3.4,0,1.1" vincoli="O,cerniera" frecce="0,-1.2,0,3,precession axis;0,0,4.9,0,ζ" punti="3.4,0,G,baricentro" forze="3.4 0,0,-1.1,mg" coppie="0 2.5,1,Ω;4.6 0,1,φ̇" quote="O,3.4 0,d,1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Gyroscope with horizontal axis of figure pivoted at $O$"}

- $0.87\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.44\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4.36\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The disk has gyroscopic structure with respect to $O$ and its principal moment with respect to the axis of figure equals $C = \dfrac{m\,R^2}{2} = \dfrac{4 \cdot 0.15^2}{2} = 0.045\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. With the axis of figure horizontal, that is with nutation $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, the condition for the regular precession of the heavy gyroscope reduces to $C\,\Omega\,\dot{\varphi} = m\,g\,d$, therefore
$$
\Omega = \frac{m\,g\,d}{C\,\dot{\varphi}} = \frac{4 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.20}{0.045 \cdot 200} \simeq 0.87\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $0.44\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is obtained by using $C = m\,R^2$, that is the moment of inertia of the ring, and the value $1.15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ by inverting the ratio between the moment of the weight and $C\,\dot{\varphi}$.
