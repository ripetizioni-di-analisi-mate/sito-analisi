A spool of mass $m$, outer radius $R$ and centroidal moment of inertia $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$ rests on a rough horizontal plane. A string is wound on the hub, of radius $r = \dfrac{R}{2}$; it leaves from the lower side of the hub and is pulled horizontally to the right with a constant force $F$, as in the diagram in the figure. The spool, initially at rest, rolls without slipping. In which direction does the center move, and with what acceleration?

::struttura{linee="-0.5,0 6.4,0" cerchi="2,1.4,1.4;2,1.4,0.7,foro" nodi="N,2,0.7,-;E,5,0.7,-" catene="N-E" forze="5.9 0.7,0.9,0,F" punti="2,1.4,G,baricentro;2,0,C,centro" titolo="Spool of outer radius $R$ pulled by a string leaving from the lower side of the hub, of radius $r = \dfrac{R}{2}$"}

- To the left, with acceleration $\dfrac{F}{3\,m}$
- To the right, with acceleration $\dfrac{2\,F}{3\,m}$
- To the right, with acceleration $\dfrac{F}{3\,m}$
- To the right, with acceleration $\dfrac{F}{m}$

## Explanation

The spool rolls on a straight guide, hence the second cardinal equation can be written with the pole at the contact point $C$, in the form $I_C\,\ddot{\vartheta} = M_{Cz}^{(e)}$, with $I_C = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2$. The weight, the normal reaction and friction have zero moment with respect to $C$, while the horizontal string lies at height $R - r = \dfrac{R}{2}$ above $C$, therefore the force has a clockwise moment of magnitude $\dfrac{1}{2}\,F\,R$. The spool therefore rotates clockwise, and in pure rolling a clockwise rotation moves the center to the right with
$$
a_G = R\,|\ddot{\vartheta}| = R\cdot\frac{\frac{1}{2}\,F\,R}{\frac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2} = \frac{F}{3\,m} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{2\,F}{3\,m}$ corresponds to a force applied at the center, at height $R$ above $C$, and $\dfrac{F}{m}$ neglects the rotation of the spool. Motion to the left would require a counterclockwise moment with respect to $C$, while the horizontal force, applied above $C$ and directed to the right, has a clockwise moment.
