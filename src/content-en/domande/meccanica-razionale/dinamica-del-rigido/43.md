A rigid body with one fixed point has principal moments $I_1<I_2<I_3$. The magnitude $H$ of angular momentum about the fixed point is given. Which rotation minimises kinetic energy among rotations with that $H$?

- Rotation about the axis of moment $I_3$.
- Rotation about the axis of moment $I_1$.
- Rotation about the axis of moment $I_2$.
- Every direction gives the same energy.

## Explanation

In principal axes $T=\dfrac12\sum H_i^2/I_i$. With $\sum H_i^2=H^2$ fixed, the minimum is obtained by placing all angular momentum along the direction with the largest denominator, $I_3$.
