A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is keyed on a light axle pivoted at the point $O$, with its center at distance $R$ from $O$ on the axis of figure. The axis of figure is horizontal and precesses about the vertical through $O$ with constant angular velocity $\Omega$; the disk spins about it with constant spin velocity equal to the precession velocity, $\dot{\varphi} = \Omega$, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the kinetic energy of the disk?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;B,2.2,0,-" aste="O-B" poligoni="2.08,-2.2 2.32,-2.2 2.32,2.2 2.08,2.2" vincoli="O,cerniera" frecce="0,-1.2,0,3.4,axis of precession;0,0,5.2,0,ζ" punti="2.2,0,G,baricentro" coppie="0 2.9,1,Ω;4.6 0,1,φ̇" quote="O,2.2 0,R,0.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Disk keyed at distance $R$ from the pivot $O$, with horizontal axis of figure in precession"}

- $\dfrac{3}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2$
- $\dfrac{9}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2$

## Explanation

The point $O$ is fixed and lies on the axis of figure, hence $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\vec{\omega} \cdot \mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{\omega}$, where the principal moments at $O$ are the axial one $C = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$ and the transverse one, which by the Huygens-Steiner theorem equals $A = \dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \dfrac{5}{4}\,m\,R^2$. The angular velocity is $\vec{\omega} = \Omega\,\hat{k} + \Omega\,\hat{e}_\zeta$ and, with the axis of figure horizontal, $\hat{k}$ is perpendicular to $\hat{e}_\zeta$; therefore the axial and the transverse components of $\vec{\omega}$ both have magnitude $\Omega$. One obtains
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{5}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2 \right) = \frac{7}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2 .
$$
The value $\dfrac{3}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2$ omits the transport from $G$ to $O$ in the transverse moment, $\dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2$ neglects the precession, and $\dfrac{9}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\Omega^2$ uses $m\,R^2$ as axial moment, which is that of the ring.
