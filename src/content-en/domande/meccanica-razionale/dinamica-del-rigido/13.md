A homogeneous rectangular lamina of mass $m$ and sides $2\ell$ and $\ell$ moves torque-free about its own center of mass $G$, which is kept fixed, as in the diagram in the figure. The permanent rotation about the intermediate axis $\eta$ is unstable. Perturbing the initial datum with $q_0$ small, what is the growth coefficient $\lambda$ of the perturbation?

::struttura{poligoni="-2,-1 2,-1 2,1 -2,1" nodi="G,0,0,-" frecce="0,0,3,0,ξ;0,0,0,2,η" punti="0,0,G,baricentro" etichette="0.45,-0.3,ζ out of page" quote="-2 -1,2 -1,2ℓ,0.8;2 -1,2 1,ℓ,0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Homogeneous rectangular lamina with fixed center of mass"}

- $q_0\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}}$
- $q_0\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{5}}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,q_0$
- $q_0\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{20}}$

## Explanation

The principal moments of the lamina at $G$ equal $A = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{12}$ with respect to the axis $\xi$ parallel to the long side, $B = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$ with respect to $\eta$ and $C = A + B = \dfrac{5\,m\,\ell^2}{12}$ with respect to the axis $\zeta$ perpendicular to the lamina. The intermediate axis is therefore $\eta$ and the linearization of the Euler equations about the rotation $\vec{\omega} = q_0\,\hat{e}_\eta$ gives for the perturbed components the equation
$$
\ddot{p} = \frac{(A - B)(B - C)}{C\,A}\,q_0^2\,p ,
$$
with positive coefficient, therefore the solution contains the growing exponential $e^{\lambda t}$. Substituting the three moments one obtains $\dfrac{(A-B)(B-C)}{C\,A} = \dfrac{3}{5}$, hence $\lambda = q_0\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{5}} \simeq 0.775\,q_0$. The value $\dfrac{3}{5}\,q_0$ comes from forgetting the square root and $q_0\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}}$ from inverting the ratio.
