A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is crossed by a fixed smooth horizontal axis at the point $P$, at distance $a = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$ from the end $A$, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the moment of inertia of the rod with respect to that axis?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;P,1,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="P,cerniera" punti="2,0,G,baricentro" quote="A,P,a,0.9;A,B,ℓ,-1.1" titolo="Rod crossed by the axis at the point $P$, at distance $a = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$ from the end $A$"}

- $\dfrac{7}{48}\,m\,\ell^2$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{12}$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{16}$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$

## Explanation

The center of mass of the homogeneous rod lies at the midpoint, hence its distance from $P$ equals $d = \dfrac{\ell}{2} - \dfrac{\ell}{4} = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$. The moment of inertia about the center of mass with respect to an axis perpendicular to the rod is $I_G = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{12}$, therefore by the **Huygens-Steiner theorem**
$$
I_P = \frac{m\,\ell^2}{12} + m\left( \frac{\ell}{4} \right)^{\!2} = \frac{m\,\ell^2}{12} + \frac{m\,\ell^2}{16} = \frac{7}{48}\,m\,\ell^2 .
$$
The value $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{12}$ omits the transport term and the value $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{16}$ keeps the transport term alone. The value $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$ is the moment of inertia with respect to the axis through one end, that is for $a = 0$.
