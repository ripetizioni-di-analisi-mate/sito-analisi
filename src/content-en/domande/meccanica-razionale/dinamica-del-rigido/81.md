A solid homogeneous cylinder of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rests on a horizontal board. The board is pulled with constant horizontal acceleration $a$, and the cylinder, initially at rest, rolls without slipping on the board. What is the acceleration of the center of the cylinder with respect to the ground?

- $a$, in the direction of motion of the board
- $\dfrac{2\,a}{3}$, opposite to the motion of the board
- $\dfrac{a}{3}$, in the direction of motion of the board
- $\dfrac{a}{2}$, in the direction of motion of the board

## Explanation

The cylinder is acted upon by the weight, the normal reaction and the static friction force $F_a$ at the contact point, horizontal and directed in the direction of motion of the board. The horizontal first cardinal equation and the second one with pole at $G$ give
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,a_G = F_a \\[4pt]
\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = F_a\,R
\end{cases}
$$
where $\vartheta$ is measured in the sense in which friction turns the cylinder, that is the one that moves the contact point forward with respect to $G$. The cylinder rolls without slipping on the board, hence the contact point has the acceleration of the board and $a_G + R\,\ddot{\vartheta} = a$. From the two equations one obtains $R\,\ddot{\vartheta} = 2\,a_G$, therefore $3\,a_G = a$ and $a_G = \dfrac{a}{3}$, in the direction of motion of the board. With respect to the board the cylinder moves backward with acceleration $\dfrac{2\,a}{3}$, which is the value given as opposite; the value $a$ neglects the rotation, as if the cylinder were fixed to the board, and $\dfrac{a}{2}$ uses the moment of inertia of the ring $m\,R^2$.
