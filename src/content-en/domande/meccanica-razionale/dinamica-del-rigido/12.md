A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal guide. An inextensible string of negligible mass is wound on its circumference, leaves horizontally from the highest point, passes over a small pulley of negligible mass and supports a body of mass $m$, as in the diagram in the figure. The system starts from rest. What is the acceleration of the center of the disk?

::struttura{linee="0,0 7,0" cerchi="1.8,1.2,1.2;7.6,1.8,0.6" nodi="O,7.6,1.8;P,1.8,2.4,-;H,7.6,2.4,-;K,8.2,1.8,-;L,8.2,-1.2,-" catene="P-H;K-L" poligoni="7.85,-1.2 8.55,-1.2 8.55,-1.9 7.85,-1.9" vincoli="O,cerniera" punti="1.8,1.2,G,baricentro;1.8,0,C,centro;1.8,2.4,P" etichette="0.5,2.6,m, R;8.9,-1.5,m" titolo="String wound on the disk, leaving from the highest point, and hanging body"}

- $\dfrac{8\,g}{11}$
- $\dfrac{4\,g}{7}$
- $\dfrac{2\,g}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4\,g}{11}$

## Explanation

The contact point $C$ is the instantaneous center of rotation, hence the highest point of the disk has velocity $2\,v_G$ and the hanging body descends with acceleration $2\,a_G$. Taking the contact point as pole one has $I_C = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2$ and the tension has arm $2R$, therefore
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,g - S = m\,(2\,a_G) \\[4pt]
\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dfrac{a_G}{R} = S\,(2R)
\end{cases}
$$
From the second equation it follows that $S = \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,a_G$, hence substituting into the first one obtains $m\,g = \dfrac{11}{4}\,m\,a_G$, whence $a_G = \dfrac{4\,g}{11}$. The value $\dfrac{8\,g}{11}$ is the acceleration of the hanging body, while $\dfrac{4\,g}{7}$ is obtained by setting the acceleration of the body equal to that of the center, that is by forgetting the factor $2$ of the kinematic condition.
