A book, modeled as a homogeneous rectangular box with edges $a > b > c$, is thrown into the air spinning about one of the axes through the center of mass parallel to the edges; air resistance is negligible. About which axis is the rotation unstable?

- The axis parallel to the longest edge $a$
- The axis parallel to the shortest edge $c$
- The axis parallel to the intermediate edge $b$
- None, because the book is homogeneous and symmetric

## Explanation

During the flight the weight is applied at the center of mass and has zero moment with respect to $G$, hence the motion about $G$ is a Poinsot motion; the axes through $G$ parallel to the edges are principal axes of inertia by symmetry. The moment of inertia about the axis parallel to an edge contains the squares of the other two edges, therefore
$$
J_a = \frac{m\,(b^2 + c^2)}{12} ,
\qquad
J_b = \frac{m\,(a^2 + c^2)}{12} ,
\qquad
J_c = \frac{m\,(a^2 + b^2)}{12} .
$$
Since $a > b > c$, one has $J_a < J_b < J_c$, therefore the intermediate moment is $J_b$. By the theorem on permanent rotations the rotation about the axis of intermediate moment is unstable, that is the one about the axis parallel to the edge $b$, while the other two are stable. The symmetry of the book makes the three axes principal, but the three moments remain distinct.
