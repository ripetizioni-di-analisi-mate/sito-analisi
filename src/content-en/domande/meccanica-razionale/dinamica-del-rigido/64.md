A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $r$ rolls without slipping inside a fixed circular guide of radius $R > r$, lying in a vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. Let $\varphi$ be the angle that the segment from the center $O$ of the guide to the center $G$ of the disk forms with the vertical. What is the period of small oscillations about $\varphi = 0$?

- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,(R - r)}{2\,g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{R - r}{g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,R}{2\,g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,(R - r)}{3\,g}}$

## Explanation

The center $G$ describes a circle of radius $R - r$, hence $v_G = (R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}$, and the condition of pure rolling on the guide gives the disk an angular velocity of magnitude $\dfrac{v_G}{r}$. By König's theorem the kinetic energy is
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v_G^2 + \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\,m\,r^2\,\frac{v_G^2}{r^2} = \frac{3}{4}\,m\,(R - r)^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 ,
$$
and the potential energy of the weight is $V = -m\,g\,(R - r)\cos\varphi$. Friction acts at the contact point, which has zero velocity, hence the mechanical energy is conserved; differentiating it with respect to time and replacing $\sin\varphi$ with $\varphi$ one obtains
$$
\frac{3}{2}\,(R - r)\,\ddot{\varphi} + g\,\varphi = 0 ,
$$
with angular frequency $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g}{3\,(R - r)}}$. The value $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{R - r}{g}}$ neglects the rotation of the disk, as for a point sliding without friction, $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,R}{2\,g}}$ takes $R$ as the radius of the path of $G$, and $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,(R - r)}{3\,g}}$ inverts the factor $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
