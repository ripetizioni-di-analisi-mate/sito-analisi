A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ lies in a horizontal plane and can rotate about a fixed smooth vertical axis passing through the point $O$, at distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from the center $G$, as in the diagram in the figure seen from above. The disk is at rest and at time $t = 0$ a constant driving couple of moment $\Gamma$ is applied to it. At the initial instant, what is the magnitude of the horizontal component of the reaction of the axis?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" nodi="O,-1,0,-" punti="-1,0,O,centro;0,0,G,baricentro" quote="0 0,2 0,R,-0.5" coppie="-1 0,1,Γ" riferimento=0 titolo="Disk seen from above: the fixed vertical axis passes through $O$, at distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from the center"}

- $\dfrac{\Gamma}{R}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{4\,\Gamma}{3\,R}$
- $\dfrac{2\,\Gamma}{3\,R}$

## Explanation

By the Huygens-Steiner theorem the moment of inertia about the axis through $O$ is $I_O = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,\dfrac{R^2}{4} = \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2$, hence the equation of motion about a fixed axis gives $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{\Gamma}{I_O} = \dfrac{4\,\Gamma}{3\,m\,R^2}$. At the initial instant $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$, therefore the acceleration of $G$ is only tangential, perpendicular to $OG$, of magnitude $\dfrac{R}{2}\,\ddot{\vartheta}$. The weight is vertical and the couple has zero resultant, hence only the reaction of the axis appears in the horizontal component of the first cardinal equation, and
$$
\Phi_{\text{hor}} = m\,\frac{R}{2}\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \frac{2\,\Gamma}{3\,R} .
$$
The answer $0$ neglects the part of the reaction proportional to $\ddot{\vartheta}$, the value $\dfrac{\Gamma}{R}$ uses the centroidal moment $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$ instead of $I_O$, and $\dfrac{4\,\Gamma}{3\,R}$ takes $R$ as the distance of $G$ from the axis.
