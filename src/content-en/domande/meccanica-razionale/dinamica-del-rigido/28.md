A homogeneous rod of length $\ell$ is suspended from a fixed smooth horizontal axis through one of its ends and perpendicular to the rod, and oscillates under the action of the weight alone. What is the period of the small oscillations?

- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,\ell}{3\,g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{2\,g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,\ell}{2\,g}}$

## Explanation

The system is a compound pendulum with $I_a = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$ and with the center of mass at distance $d = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from the axis. The reduced length therefore equals
$$
\ell_r = \frac{I_a}{m\,d} = \frac{m\,\ell^2}{3}\cdot\frac{2}{m\,\ell} = \frac{2\,\ell}{3} ,
$$
hence the rod oscillates like a simple pendulum two thirds of the rod long and the period is $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell_r}{g}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,\ell}{3\,g}}$. The value $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{g}}$ is the period of a simple pendulum of length $\ell$, that is of a point mass placed at the free end, while $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{2\,g}}$ corresponds to a point mass concentrated at the center of mass.
