A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is suspended by the end $A$ from a fixed smooth horizontal axis and is at rest in a vertical position. A horizontal blow, perpendicular to the axis, applies to the rod an impulse $J$ at the point $P$ at distance $x$ from $A$, as in the diagram in the figure. For which value of $x$ does the axis exert no impulse on the rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,-4;P,0,-3.1,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" punti="0,-2,G,baricentro;0,-3.1,P" forze="0 -3.1,1,0," etichette="-1.25,-2.75,J" quote="A,P,x,-0.8" titolo="Rod suspended at $A$ and struck by a horizontal impulse at distance $x$ from the axis"}

- $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$
- $\ell$
- $\dfrac{\ell}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$

## Explanation

During the impact, which is very short, the finite forces produce no appreciable change of linear momentum and angular momentum, hence the cardinal equations integrated over the duration of the blow contain only the impulse $J$ and the impulse $J_A$ of the axis. The rod starts from rest and immediately afterwards rotates about $A$ with angular velocity $\omega$, therefore $v_G = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\omega$ and
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,\dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\omega = J + J_A \\[6pt]
\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}\,\omega = J\,x
\end{cases}
$$
where the second equation is written with the pole at the fixed point $A$, with respect to which $J_A$ has zero moment. Setting $J_A = 0$ and dividing the second equation by the first one obtains $x = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell$. This value coincides with the reduced length $\ell_r = \dfrac{I_A}{m\,d}$ of the rod, hence the point sought is the **center of oscillation**. For $x = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$ one finds $J_A = -\dfrac{J}{4}$, for $x = \ell$ one finds $J_A = \dfrac{J}{2}$ and for $x = \dfrac{\ell}{3}$ one finds $J_A = -\dfrac{J}{2}$, therefore in these cases the axis exerts an impulse.
