A rigid system is formed by a homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ and by a homogeneous disk of mass $2m$ and radius $R = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$, welded to the rod with its center at $B$ and lying in the same vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. The system oscillates about a fixed smooth horizontal axis through $A$ and perpendicular to the plane. What is the moment of inertia with respect to that axis?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" cerchi="4,0,1" vincoli="A,cerniera" punti="3.33,0,G,baricentro" quote="A,B,ℓ,1.6;4 0,5 0,R,-1.3" etichette="5.35,1.15,2m;1.5,0.75,m" titolo="Rod with a disk welded at the end, oscillating about the axis through $A$"}

- $\dfrac{19}{48}\,m\,\ell^2$
- $\dfrac{103}{48}\,m\,\ell^2$
- $\dfrac{115}{48}\,m\,\ell^2$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia is additive, hence the contribution of the rod is added to that of the disk. The rod gives $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$, while for the disk one applies the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** with distance $\ell$ between the center $B$ and the axis through $A$, therefore its contribution is
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,R^2 + 2m\,\ell^2 = \frac{m\,\ell^2}{16} + 2\,m\,\ell^2 ,
$$
where $R = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$ has been substituted. Adding the two contributions one obtains
$$
I_A = m\,\ell^2\left( \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{16} + 2 \right) = \frac{115}{48}\,m\,\ell^2 .
$$
The value $\dfrac{19}{48}\,m\,\ell^2$ is obtained by omitting the transport term $2m\,\ell^2$ and the value $\dfrac{7}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$ by treating the disk as a point mass at $B$.
