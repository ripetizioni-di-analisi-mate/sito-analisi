On a vertical shaft that turns with constant angular velocity $\omega = 100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, supported by the bearings $A$ at the height $\zeta = 0$ and $B$ at the height $\zeta = h = 60\,\mathrm{cm}$, a point mass $m = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ is keyed at distance $e = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis, at the height $\zeta = c = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the magnitude of the dynamic reaction of the bearing $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;P,1.3,1.33,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera,90" cerchi="1.3,1.33,0.3" punti="0,1.33,C" quote="A,0 1.33,c,-1.1;A,B,h,1.75;0 1.33,1.3 1.33,e,-0.75" etichette="2.1,1.85,m" titolo="Shaft with an unbalancing mass at the height $c$"}

- $1000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$
- $667\,\mathrm{N}$
- $333\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The point mass describes a circle of radius $e$ with constant angular velocity, hence its acceleration is centripetal and of magnitude $e\,\omega^2$, therefore the inertia force to be balanced has magnitude $m\,e\,\omega^2 = 2 \cdot 0.05 \cdot 100^2 = 1000\,\mathrm{N}$. The two reactions of the bearings are directed towards the axis and their sum and their moment with respect to $B$ give
$$
\begin{cases}
|\vec{\Phi}_A| + |\vec{\Phi}_B| = m\,e\,\omega^2 \\[4pt]
|\vec{\Phi}_A|\,h = m\,e\,\omega^2\,(h - c)
\end{cases}
$$
From the second equation it follows that $|\vec{\Phi}_A| = m\,e\,\omega^2\,\dfrac{h-c}{h} = 1000 \cdot \dfrac{0.4}{0.6} \simeq 667\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $333\,\mathrm{N}$ is the reaction at $B$, which is obtained with the ratio $\dfrac{c}{h}$, while $1000\,\mathrm{N}$ is the whole force, which would be carried by a single bearing.
