A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ carries a circular groove of radius $r = \dfrac{R}{3}$, of negligible mass, cut around the center. An inextensible vertical string, fastened above, is wound on this groove, as in the diagram in the figure. The disk is released from rest and descends unwinding the string, keeping its axis horizontal. What is the acceleration of the center?

::struttura{linee="-1.7,3.4 1.7,3.4" cerchi="0,0,1.5" nodi="K,-0.5,0;B,-0.5,3.4,-" catene="K-B" punti="0,0,G,baricentro" forze="0 0,0,-1.1,mg" quote="0 0,-0.5 0,r,-0.5;0 0,1.5 0,R,0.5" titolo="Disk with a string wound on a groove of radius $r = \dfrac{R}{3}$"}

- $\dfrac{2\,g}{3}$
- $\dfrac{g}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2\,g}{11}$
- $\dfrac{2\,g}{5}$

## Explanation

The string unwinds from the groove of radius $r$, hence the kinematic condition is $a = r\,\ddot{\vartheta}$ and the moment of the tension with respect to $G$ has arm $r$. The two equations of the planar rigid motion give
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,g - S = m\,a \\[4pt]
\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dfrac{a}{r} = S\,r
\end{cases}
$$
From the second equation it follows that $S = \dfrac{m\,a\,R^2}{2\,r^2}$ and, since $r = \dfrac{R}{3}$, one has $\dfrac{R^2}{r^2} = 9$, therefore $S = \dfrac{9}{2}\,m\,a$. Substituting into the first equation one obtains $m\,g = \dfrac{11}{2}\,m\,a$, hence $a = \dfrac{2\,g}{11}$. The value $\dfrac{2\,g}{3}$ is obtained by winding the string on the circle of radius $R$ and the value $\dfrac{g}{10}$ by forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the moment of inertia of the disk.
