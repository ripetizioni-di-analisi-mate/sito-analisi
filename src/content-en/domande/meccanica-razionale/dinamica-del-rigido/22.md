A homogeneous pulley of mass $M = 6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R = 0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ turns about its own fixed smooth horizontal axis and is initially at rest. A constant driving couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = 3\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ acts on it. What is the angular acceleration?

- $12.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The pulley is modeled as a homogeneous disk, hence its moment of inertia with respect to the axis through the center equals
$$
I_a = \frac{1}{2}\,M\,R^2 = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 6 \cdot 0.04 = 0.12\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2} .
$$
The axis is fixed and the constraint is ideal, therefore the equation of motion is $I_a\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \mathcal{M}$ and one obtains $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3}{0.12} = 25\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. The value $12.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ is obtained by using $I_a = M\,R^2$, that is the moment of inertia of the thin ring, and the value $37.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ by using $I_a = \dfrac{1}{3}\,M\,R^2$.
