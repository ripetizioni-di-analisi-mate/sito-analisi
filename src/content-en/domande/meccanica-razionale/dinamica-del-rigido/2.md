A homogeneous pulley, modeled as a disk of mass $2m$ and radius $R$, turns about a fixed smooth horizontal axis through its center $O$. An inextensible string of negligible mass is wound on it, and a body of mass $m$ hangs from its end, as in the diagram in the figure. The system starts from rest. What is the tension of the string?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.4" nodi="O,0,0;P,1.4,0,-;Q,1.4,-2.6,-" poligoni="1.05,-2.6 1.75,-2.6 1.75,-3.2 1.05,-3.2" catene="P-Q" forze="1.4 -3.2,0,-0.9,mg" vincoli="O,cerniera" etichette="-1.5,1.1,2m, R;2.1,-2.9,m" titolo="Pulley of mass $2m$ with a hanging body of mass $m$"}

- $\dfrac{m\,g}{2}$
- $m\,g$
- $\dfrac{m\,g}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\,m\,g}{3}$

## Explanation

The pulley has $I_O = \dfrac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,R^2 = m\,R^2$ and the string does not slip, therefore the acceleration $a$ of the body and the angular acceleration $\ddot{\vartheta}$ of the pulley are linked by $a = R\,\ddot{\vartheta}$. The equations of the hanging body and of the pulley with a fixed axis are
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,g - S = m\,a \\[4pt]
m\,R^2\,\dfrac{a}{R} = S\,R
\end{cases}
$$
From the second equation it follows that $S = m\,a$, hence substituting into the first one obtains $m\,g = 2\,m\,a$, whence $a = \dfrac{g}{2}$ and $S = \dfrac{m\,g}{2}$. The value $m\,g$ is the static tension, which one would have only with the body at rest, while $\dfrac{m\,g}{3}$ is obtained by using for the pulley the moment of inertia $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$ instead of $m\,R^2$.
