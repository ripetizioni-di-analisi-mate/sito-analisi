A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is keyed on a shaft so that its center $G$ lies on the axis of the shaft, but with the axis of figure inclined by $\gamma = 30^\circ$ to the shaft, as in the diagram in the figure. The shaft rotates with constant angular velocity $\omega$ in fixed bearings. What is the magnitude of the moment that the bearings must exert on the disk?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;D,1.21,2.7,-;E,-1.21,1.3,-" aste="A-B;D-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera,90" punti="0,2,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,2 -0.75,3.3;0,2 0,3.6" etichette="-0.32,3.15,γ" coppie="0 4.6,1,ω" titolo="Disk keyed with its axis of figure inclined by $\gamma = 30^\circ$ to the shaft (disk seen edge-on)"}

- $0$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{16}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$

## Explanation

The point $G$ is fixed, hence one uses the Euler equations with pole $G$ and with the principal frame formed by the axis of figure $\zeta$, of moment $C = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$, by a diameter $\xi$ in the plane containing the shaft and $\zeta$, and by a perpendicular diameter $\eta$, with $A = B = \dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2$. The shaft is fixed to the disk, therefore the components of $\vec{\omega}$ on the frame are constant, $p = \omega\sin\gamma$, $q = 0$, $r = \omega\cos\gamma$, and their derivatives vanish. The Euler equations then give $M_\xi = M_\zeta = 0$ and
$$
M_\eta = (A - C)\,r\,p = -\frac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2\sin\gamma\cos\gamma ,
$$
which for $\gamma = 30^\circ$ has magnitude $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2 = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{16}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$. The weight has zero moment with respect to $G$, hence this moment is exerted by the bearings. The answer $0$ accounts only for static balancing, $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$ uses $C$ instead of $C - A$, and $\dfrac{1}{16}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$ replaces $\sin\gamma\cos\gamma$ with $\sin^2\gamma$.
