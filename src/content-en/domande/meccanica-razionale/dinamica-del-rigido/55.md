A rigid body has the point $O$ fixed and at the instant considered its inertia tensor at $O$, written in the fixed frame $Oxyz$, is
$$
\mathbb{I}_O =
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & -1 & 0 \\
-1 & 3 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
\mathrm{kg\,m^2} ,
$$
that is the product of inertia is $I_{xy} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. The angular velocity is $\vec{\omega} = 2\,\hat{\imath} + 2\,\hat{\jmath}$, in $\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the kinetic energy?

- $14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The point $O$ is fixed, hence $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\vec{\omega} \cdot \mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{\omega}$. The product of the matrix and the vector is $\mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{\omega} = (4 \cdot 2 - 1 \cdot 2,\ -1 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 2,\ 0) = (6,\ 4,\ 0)$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,(2 \cdot 6 + 2 \cdot 4) = 10\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
In the quadratic form $\dfrac{1}{2}\big( I_{xx}\,\omega_x^2 + I_{yy}\,\omega_y^2 - 2\,I_{xy}\,\omega_x\,\omega_y \big)$ the product of inertia gives the contribution $-I_{xy}\,\omega_x\,\omega_y = -4\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $14\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by neglecting the product of inertia, $18\,\mathrm{J}$ by adding it with the wrong sign, and $20\,\mathrm{J}$ by forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
