A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged to a fixed smooth horizontal axis through a point $O$ of its rim and perpendicular to the plane of the disk, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the reduced length of the compound pendulum so obtained?

::struttura{cerchi="0,-1.5,1.5" nodi="O,0,0" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" punti="0,-1.5,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 0,-3.3" quote="O,0 -1.5,R,0.8" titolo="Disk hinged at a point $O$ of the rim"}

- $\dfrac{R}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,R$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,R$
- $2\,R$

## Explanation

The distance of the center of mass from the axis is $d = R$ and, by the **Huygens-Steiner theorem**, the moment of inertia with respect to the axis through $O$ equals
$$
I_O = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2 .
$$
The reduced length is defined by $\ell_r = \dfrac{I_O}{m\,d}$, hence $\ell_r = \dfrac{3\,m\,R^2}{2\,m\,R} = \dfrac{3}{2}\,R$. The same result follows from $\ell_r = d + \dfrac{\rho^2}{d}$ with $\rho^2 = \dfrac{R^2}{2}$, therefore $\ell_r = R + \dfrac{R}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{R}{2}$ is obtained by using the moment of inertia about the center of mass alone, without the transport term.
