A physical pendulum can hang from either of two pivots on the same line, on opposite sides of the centre of mass at unequal distances $d_1,d_2$. Small oscillations have the same period. What is $I_G/m$, where $I_G$ is the central moment about the axis parallel to the pivots?

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,0" punti="0.5,0,P₁,centro;2.5,0,G,baricentro;5.5,0,P₂,centro" quote="0.5 0,2.5 0,d₁,0.8;2.5 0,5.5 0,d₂,0.8" riferimento="0" titolo="Locations of the two alternative pivots relative to the pendulum’s centre of mass G."}

- $(d_1+d_2)^2$
- $d_1^2+d_2^2$
- $d_1d_2$
- $\dfrac{(d_1-d_2)^2}4$

## Explanation

With $k^2=I_G/m$, the equivalent length is $d+k^2/d$. Equal periods require $d_1+k^2/d_1=d_2+k^2/d_2$. Since the distances differ, dividing by $d_1-d_2$ yields $k^2=d_1d_2$.
