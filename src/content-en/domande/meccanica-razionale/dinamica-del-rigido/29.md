A homogeneous rod of length $\ell$ oscillates about a fixed smooth horizontal axis through one of its ends and perpendicular to the rod. A homogeneous disk of radius $R$ oscillates about a fixed smooth horizontal axis through a point of its rim and perpendicular to the plane of the disk. For which value of $R$ do the small oscillations of the two compound pendulums have the same period?

- $R = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$
- $R = \dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$
- $R = \dfrac{4\,\ell}{9}$
- $R = \dfrac{\ell}{3}$

## Explanation

The period of the small oscillations of a compound pendulum depends only on the equivalent length $\ell_r = \dfrac{I_a}{M\,d}$, hence the two periods are equal when the two equivalent lengths coincide, whatever the masses. For the rod, calling $M$ its mass, one has $I_a = \dfrac{1}{3}\,M\,\ell^2$ and $d = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$, therefore $\ell_r = \dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$. For the disk, again calling $M$ its mass, the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** gives $I_a = \dfrac{1}{2}\,M R^2 + M R^2 = \dfrac{3}{2}\,M R^2$, and with $d = R$ one obtains $\ell_r = \dfrac{3R}{2}$. The equality $\dfrac{3R}{2} = \dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$ then gives $R = \dfrac{4\,\ell}{9}$. The value $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ is obtained by equating the distances of the centers of mass from the axes, and $\dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$ by taking $R$ as the equivalent length of the disk.
