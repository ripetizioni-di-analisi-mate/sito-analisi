A homogeneous thin ring of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a plane inclined by $\alpha$ to the horizontal, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the acceleration of the center?

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,3.46 6,0 0,0" cerchi="2.57,2.48,0.86;2.57,2.48,0.74,foro" punti="2.57,2.48,G,baricentro;3,1.73,C,centro" etichette="1.05,0.26,α;1.6,3.4,m, R" titolo="Thin ring rolling without slipping on the inclined plane"}

- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,g\,\sin\alpha$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,\sin\alpha$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}\,g\,\sin\alpha$
- $g\,\sin\alpha$

## Explanation

The thin ring has all its mass at distance $R$ from the center, hence $I_G = m\,R^2$ and in the form $I_G = \beta\,m\,R^2$ one has $\beta = 1$. By the theorem on **rolling without slipping on an inclined plane** the acceleration of the center equals
$$
a_G = \frac{g\,\sin\alpha}{1 + \beta} ,
$$
therefore with $\beta = 1$ one obtains $a_G = \dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,\sin\alpha$. The ring is the body that descends most slowly among those of assigned radius, since it has the largest value of $\beta$. The value $\dfrac{2}{3}\,g\,\sin\alpha$ corresponds to the solid disk, with $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$, the value $\dfrac{5}{7}\,g\,\sin\alpha$ to the solid sphere, with $\beta = \dfrac{2}{5}$, and the value $g\,\sin\alpha$ to a body that slides without rotating on a smooth plane.
