A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ hangs in a horizontal position from two vertical inextensible strings fastened to its ends, as in the diagram in the figure. At a certain instant the string at $B$ is cut. What is the tension of the string at $A$ immediately after the cut?

::struttura{linee="-0.6,2.2 4.6,2.2" nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;P,0,2.2,-;Q,4,2.2,-" aste="A-B" catene="P-A;Q-B" punti="2,0,G,baricentro" forze="2 0,0,-0.9,mg" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.7" etichette="4.75,1.1,cut" titolo="Rod hanging from two vertical strings: the string at $B$ is cut"}

- $\dfrac{m\,g}{4}$
- $\dfrac{m\,g}{2}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{4\,m\,g}{7}$

## Explanation

Immediately after the cut the rod is still at rest, hence $\omega = 0$; the end $A$ moves on a circle centered at the suspension point, therefore its acceleration along the string, that is the centripetal one, is zero. The forces are all vertical, hence $G$ has no horizontal acceleration, and with $A$ having no vertical acceleration one has $a_G = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\ddot{\vartheta}$ downward. The first cardinal equation in the vertical direction and the second one with pole $G$ give
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,g - S = m\,\dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\ddot{\vartheta} \\[6pt]
S\,\dfrac{\ell}{2} = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{12}\,\ddot{\vartheta}
\end{cases}
$$
From the second equation one gets $S = \dfrac{m\,\ell}{6}\,\ddot{\vartheta}$; substituting into the first one obtains $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}$, hence $S = \dfrac{m\,g}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{m\,g}{2}$ is the tension before the cut, and $\dfrac{4\,m\,g}{7}$ is obtained by using with pole $G$ the moment of inertia about an end, $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$.
