A rigid body of mass $M$ has radius of gyration about the center of mass $\rho$ with respect to a fixed direction. Among all the horizontal axes parallel to that direction, which distance $d$ of the center of mass from the axis makes the period of the small oscillations minimum?

- $d = 2\,\rho$
- $d = \dfrac{\rho}{2}$
- $d = \rho\sqrt{2}$
- $d = \rho$

## Explanation

The period equals $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell_r}{g}}$ with $\ell_r = d + \dfrac{\rho^2}{d}$, hence the period is minimum where the reduced length is minimum. The derivative of $\ell_r$ with respect to $d$ equals $1 - \dfrac{\rho^2}{d^2}$ and vanishes for $d = \rho$, where the second derivative $\dfrac{2\,\rho^2}{d^3}$ is positive, therefore that point is a minimum. The minimum value of the reduced length is
$$
\ell_r = \rho + \frac{\rho^2}{\rho} = 2\,\rho .
$$
The distances $2\,\rho$ and $\dfrac{\rho}{2}$ both give $\ell_r = \dfrac{5}{2}\,\rho$, hence a larger period, and the distance $\rho\sqrt{2}$ gives $\ell_r = \dfrac{3\,\rho}{\sqrt{2}} \simeq 2.12\,\rho$.
