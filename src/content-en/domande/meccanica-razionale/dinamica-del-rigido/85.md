A plate, modeled as a thin homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$, is thrown into the air spinning and wobbling. Its motion about the center of mass is a regular precession of the axis of figure about the fixed direction of the angular momentum $\vec{K}_G$, with nutation angle $\vartheta = 60^\circ$, and the component of the angular velocity along the axis of figure is $r$. What is the precession velocity $\Omega$?

- $2\,r$
- $\dfrac{4\,r}{\sqrt{3}}$
- $4\,r$
- $r$

## Explanation

The weight has zero moment with respect to the center of mass, hence the motion about $G$ is a motion by inertia and in the condition of regular precession the term $M\,g\,d$ is replaced by zero, that is
$$
C\,\Omega\,r - A\,\Omega^2\cos\vartheta = 0 .
$$
For $\Omega \neq 0$ one gets $\Omega = \dfrac{C\,r}{A\cos\vartheta}$. The thin disk has axial moment $C = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$ and moment about a diameter $A = \dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2$, hence $\dfrac{C}{A} = 2$, and with $\cos 60^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$ one obtains $\Omega = 4\,r$. The value $2\,r$ neglects the factor $\cos\vartheta$, $\dfrac{4\,r}{\sqrt{3}}$ uses $\sin\vartheta$ instead of $\cos\vartheta$, and $r$ swaps $A$ and $C$.
