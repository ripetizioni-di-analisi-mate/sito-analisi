A homogeneous rigid solid cube has mass $m$ and edge $a$. What is the moment of inertia about one of its diagonals, that is the line joining two opposite vertices through the center?

- $\dfrac{ma^2}{4}$
- $\dfrac{ma^2}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2\,ma^2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{ma^2}{12}$

## Explanation

The three planes through the center parallel to the faces are planes of material symmetry, so the lines through the center parallel to the edges form a central principal frame. By the symmetry of the cube the three principal moments are equal, and each equals that of the rectangular box about a central axis parallel to an edge:
$$
A = B = C = \frac{m\,(a^2 + a^2)}{12} = \frac{ma^2}{6} .
$$
For every unit vector $\vec{u}$ one then has $I_u = A\,u_\xi^2 + B\,u_\eta^2 + C\,u_\zeta^2 = \dfrac{ma^2}{6}\,(u_\xi^2 + u_\eta^2 + u_\zeta^2) = \dfrac{ma^2}{6}$, hence the moment about the diagonal also equals $\dfrac{ma^2}{6}$. The value $\dfrac{ma^2}{4}$ is the moment about the center, $\dfrac{2\,ma^2}{3}$ is the moment about an edge and $\dfrac{ma^2}{12}$ counts only one term of the sum $a^2 + a^2$.
