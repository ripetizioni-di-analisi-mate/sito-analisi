- $G = \left( \dfrac{3}{4}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{\ell}{2} \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{5}{6}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{\ell}{3} \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{\ell}{2},\ \ell \right)$

## Explanation

Each rod is homogeneous, therefore its center of mass is the midpoint: the rod $OA$ has center of mass $G_1 = \left( \dfrac{\ell}{2},\ 0 \right)$ and mass $m$, the rod $AB$ has center of mass $G_2 = (\ell,\ \ell)$ and mass $2m$. The system consists of two disjoint parts, hence the **theorem on the center of mass of a composite system** applies, which treats the two rods as two material points placed at $G_1$ and at $G_2$. Since the total mass is $3m$, one obtains
$$
x_G = \frac{m \cdot \dfrac{\ell}{2} + 2m \cdot \ell}{3m} = \frac{5}{6}\,\ell ,
\qquad
y_G = \frac{m \cdot 0 + 2m \cdot \ell}{3m} = \frac{2}{3}\,\ell .
$$
The point $\left( \dfrac{3}{4}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{\ell}{2} \right)$ is the midpoint of the segment $G_1 G_2$, that is the average of the two positions without the weights of the masses, whereas $\left( \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{\ell}{3} \right)$ is obtained by interchanging the masses of the two rods.
