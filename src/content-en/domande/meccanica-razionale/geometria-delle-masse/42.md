A rigid homogeneous solid cylinder has mass $m$, radius $R$ and height $h = 2R$. What is the moment of inertia about the central axis orthogonal to its own axis?

- $\dfrac{7\,mR^2}{12}$
- $\dfrac{mR^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5\,mR^2}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7\,mR^2}{3}$

## Explanation

For the homogeneous solid cylinder of radius $R$ and height $h$ the moment of inertia about the central axis orthogonal to its own axis equals
$$
I = \frac{m\,(3R^2 + h^2)}{12} .
$$
With $h = 2R$ one has $h^2 = 4R^2$, hence
$$
I = \frac{m\,(3R^2 + 4R^2)}{12} = \frac{7\,mR^2}{12} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{mR^2}{2}$ is the moment of inertia about its own axis, the one about which the cylinder is a body of revolution, and $\dfrac{5\,mR^2}{12}$ is obtained by writing $R^2 + h^2$ in place of $3R^2 + h^2$.
