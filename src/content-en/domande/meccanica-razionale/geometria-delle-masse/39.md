A plane material system contained in the plane $Oxy$ has, relative to the pole $O$, the values $I_{xx} = I_{yy} = 2\,m\ell^2$ and $I_{xy} = -m\ell^2$. What is the direction of the principal axis of inertia of minimum moment among those contained in the plane?

- The bisector of the first and third quadrants
- The $x$ axis
- The bisector of the second and fourth quadrants
- The $y$ axis

## Explanation

In the matrix of the tensor the products of inertia appear with the sign changed, therefore the block relative to the plane is
$$
m\ell^2 \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
whose eigenvalues are $m\ell^2$ and $3\,m\ell^2$. The eigenvector relative to the eigenvalue $m\ell^2$ satisfies $2v_x + v_y = v_x$, that is $v_y = -v_x$, hence the direction sought is that of the vector $(1, -1)$, which is the bisector of the second and fourth quadrants. The result agrees with the sign of the product of inertia, since $I_{xy} = \displaystyle\int xy\,dm$ is negative, the mass is distributed predominantly where $xy < 0$, that is around the line $y = -x$, and the moment of inertia about that line is the smallest one. The bisector of the first and third quadrants is the other principal axis, the one of moment $3\,m\ell^2$.
