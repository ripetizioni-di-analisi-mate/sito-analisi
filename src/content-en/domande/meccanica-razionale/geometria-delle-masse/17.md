Three material points of masses $m$, $2m$ and $3m$ are placed respectively at $(0, 0)$, $(2\ell, 0)$ and $(0, 4\ell)$ of the plane $Oxy$. Where is the center of mass of the system located?

- $G = \left( \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell,\ 2\ell \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{4}{3}\,\ell \right)$
- $G = \left( \ell,\ \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{4}{3}\,\ell,\ 4\ell \right)$

## Explanation

The total mass of the system is $M = m + 2m + 3m = 6m$. The coordinates of the center of mass are obtained from the definition, weighting each position with the mass of the corresponding point, therefore
$$
x_G = \frac{m \cdot 0 + 2m \cdot 2\ell + 3m \cdot 0}{6m} = \frac{2}{3}\,\ell ,
\qquad
y_G = \frac{m \cdot 0 + 2m \cdot 0 + 3m \cdot 4\ell}{6m} = 2\ell .
$$
The center of mass is displaced toward the point of mass $3m$, which is the heaviest one. The point $\left( \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{4}{3}\,\ell \right)$ is the arithmetic mean of the three positions, that is the center of mass one would have with three equal masses, whereas $\left( \dfrac{4}{3}\,\ell,\ 4\ell \right)$ is obtained by dividing by $3m$ instead of by the total mass.
