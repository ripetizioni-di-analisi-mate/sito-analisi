A plane material system contained in the plane $Oxy$ has, relative to the pole $O$, the values $I_{xx} = 4\,m\ell^2$, $I_{yy} = 2\,m\ell^2$ and $I_{xy} = m\ell^2$. What is the moment of inertia about the line through $O$ that bisects the first and third quadrants?

- $I_u = 3\,m\ell^2$
- $I_u = 6\,m\ell^2$
- $I_u = 4\,m\ell^2$
- $I_u = 2\,m\ell^2$

## Explanation

The bisector of the first and third quadrants has unit vector $\vec{u} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$, therefore $u_x = u_y = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ and $u_z = 0$. For the moment of inertia about an axis through the pole one has
$$
I_u = I_{xx}u_x^2 + I_{yy}u_y^2 + I_{zz}u_z^2 - 2\,I_{xy}u_xu_y - 2\,I_{xz}u_xu_z - 2\,I_{yz}u_yu_z ,
$$
and since the system is plane it results $I_{xz} = I_{yz} = 0$. Substituting $u_x^2 = u_y^2 = u_xu_y = \dfrac{1}{2}$ one obtains
$$
I_u = \frac{4\,m\ell^2}{2} + \frac{2\,m\ell^2}{2} - 2\,m\ell^2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 3\,m\ell^2 - m\ell^2 = 2\,m\ell^2 .
$$
The value $3\,m\ell^2$ is obtained by omitting the term with the product of inertia and $4\,m\ell^2$ by giving it the plus sign, whereas $6\,m\ell^2$ is the sum $I_{xx} + I_{yy}$, that is $I_{zz}$.
