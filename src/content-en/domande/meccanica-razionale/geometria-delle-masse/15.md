The plate that occupies the rectangle $[0, a] \times [0, b]$ of the plane $Oxy$ has surface density
$$
\sigma(x, y) = \sigma_0\,\frac{x}{a} ,
$$
where $\sigma_0$ is a positive constant. What is the abscissa of its center of mass?

- $x_G = \dfrac{a}{2}$
- $x_G = \dfrac{a}{3}$
- $x_G = \dfrac{2}{3}\,a$
- $x_G = \dfrac{3}{4}\,a$

## Explanation

The density grows with $x$ and does not depend on $y$, therefore the mass of the plate is
$$
M = \int_0^b \int_0^a \sigma_0\,\frac{x}{a}\,dx\,dy = \frac{\sigma_0 b}{a} \cdot \frac{a^2}{2} = \frac{\sigma_0\,a\,b}{2} .
$$
The numerator of the definition of center of mass equals
$$
\int_0^b \int_0^a x\,\sigma_0\,\frac{x}{a}\,dx\,dy = \frac{\sigma_0 b}{a} \cdot \frac{a^3}{3} = \frac{\sigma_0\,a^2\,b}{3} ,
$$
hence $x_G = \dfrac{\sigma_0\,a^2\,b}{3} \cdot \dfrac{2}{\sigma_0\,a\,b} = \dfrac{2}{3}\,a$. The center of mass moves toward the side $x = a$, where the density is larger. The value $\dfrac{a}{2}$ is the center of mass of the homogeneous plate and $\dfrac{a}{3}$ is obtained by dividing by $\sigma_0\,a\,b$, that is by using the mass that the plate would have with constant density $\sigma_0$.
