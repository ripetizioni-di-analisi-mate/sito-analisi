In the plane $Oxy$ two material points of mass $m$ are placed, the first at $(\ell, 2\ell)$ and the second at $(-\ell, \ell)$, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the product of inertia $I_{xy}$ relative to the pole $O$?

::struttura{punti="2,4,P_1;-2,2,P_2" etichette="2.9,4.15,m;-2.9,2.1,m;-0.35,-0.45,O" frecce="-3.4,0,3.4,0,x;0,-1,0,5.2,y" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,-0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Two material points of mass $m$ in the plane $Oxy$"}

- $I_{xy} = 3\,m\ell^2$
- $I_{xy} = -m\ell^2$
- $I_{xy} = m\ell^2$
- $I_{xy} = 0$

## Explanation

For a discrete system the product of inertia relative to the pole $O$ is $I_{xy} = \displaystyle\sum_i m_i\,x_i\,y_i$, therefore each point contributes with the product of its own coordinates, with the sign that this product has. The first point lies in the first quadrant and gives $m \cdot \ell \cdot 2\ell = 2\,m\ell^2$, the second lies in the second quadrant and gives $m \cdot (-\ell) \cdot \ell = -m\ell^2$, hence
$$
I_{xy} = 2\,m\ell^2 - m\ell^2 = m\ell^2 .
$$
The value $3\,m\ell^2$ is obtained by adding the absolute values of the two contributions, that is by neglecting the sign of the contribution of the point that has negative abscissa. The product of inertia would vanish if the two contributions were opposite, as happens when the $x$ axis or the $y$ axis is an axis of material symmetry.
