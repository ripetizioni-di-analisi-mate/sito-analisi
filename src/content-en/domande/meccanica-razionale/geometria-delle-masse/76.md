A homogeneous rigid rectangular plate has mass $m$ and sides $a$ and $b$. What value does the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the plate through the center of mass tend to as $b$ tends to $0$, with $m$ and $a$ fixed?

- $\dfrac{ma^2}{12}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{ma^2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{ma^2}{6}$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia of the plate about the axis orthogonal to it through the center of mass equals $\dfrac{m\,(a^2 + b^2)}{12}$, hence as $b \to 0$ it tends to
$$
\lim_{b \to 0} \frac{m\,(a^2 + b^2)}{12} = \frac{ma^2}{12} .
$$
As $b$ tends to $0$ the plate reduces to a rod of length $a$, and the limit coincides with the moment of the homogeneous rod about the orthogonal axis through the midpoint. The value $0$ is the moment about the line containing the rod, $\dfrac{ma^2}{3}$ refers to the axis through an end and $\dfrac{ma^2}{6}$ is the value of the square plate of side $a$.
