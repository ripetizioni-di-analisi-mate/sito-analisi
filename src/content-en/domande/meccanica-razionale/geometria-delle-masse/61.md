Which of the following triples cannot be the triple of principal moments of inertia $(A,\ B,\ C)$ of a rigid body relative to a pole $O$?

- $(m\ell^2,\ 2\,m\ell^2,\ 3\,m\ell^2)$
- $(2\,m\ell^2,\ 2\,m\ell^2,\ 2\,m\ell^2)$
- $(m\ell^2,\ 2\,m\ell^2,\ 4\,m\ell^2)$
- $(3\,m\ell^2,\ 4\,m\ell^2,\ 5\,m\ell^2)$

## Explanation

In the principal frame $(O; \xi, \eta, \zeta)$ the principal moments are
$$
A = \int (\eta^2 + \zeta^2)\,dm ,
\qquad
B = \int (\xi^2 + \zeta^2)\,dm ,
\qquad
C = \int (\xi^2 + \eta^2)\,dm ,
$$
so $A + B - C = 2 \displaystyle\int \zeta^2\,dm \ge 0$. Therefore each principal moment is less than or equal to the sum of the other two. The triple $(m\ell^2,\ 2\,m\ell^2,\ 4\,m\ell^2)$ does not satisfy this condition, since $4\,m\ell^2 > m\ell^2 + 2\,m\ell^2$, and hence it corresponds to no body. The triple with $C = A + B$ is that of a plane plate, for which $\displaystyle\int \zeta^2\,dm = 0$; the triple of three equal values is that of a homogeneous sphere or cube about the center, and the triple $(3,\ 4,\ 5)$ satisfies the three inequalities and is that of a homogeneous rectangular box about the center.
