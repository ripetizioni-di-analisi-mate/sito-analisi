A homogeneous rigid rod has mass $m$ and length $\ell$. What is the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the rod through the point $Q$ that is at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{3}$ from one end?

- $I_Q = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$
- $I_Q = \dfrac{7\,m\ell^2}{36}$
- $I_Q = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{9}$
- $I_Q = \dfrac{4\,m\ell^2}{9}$

## Explanation

The center of mass of the homogeneous rod is its midpoint, therefore $Q$ is at a distance
$$
d = \frac{\ell}{2} - \frac{\ell}{3} = \frac{\ell}{6}
$$
from the center of mass. The axis through $Q$ and the central axis are parallel, hence the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** applies starting from the central value $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$, and one obtains
$$
I_Q = \frac{m\ell^2}{12} + m\,\frac{\ell^2}{36} = \frac{3\,m\ell^2 + m\ell^2}{36} = \frac{m\ell^2}{9} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{4\,m\ell^2}{9}$ is obtained by transporting from the axis through the end, which is not central, and the value $\dfrac{7\,m\ell^2}{36}$ by using $d = \dfrac{\ell}{3}$ in place of the distance from the center of mass.
