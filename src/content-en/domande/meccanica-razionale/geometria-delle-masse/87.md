A homogeneous rigid disk of mass $m$, center $C$ and radius $R$ carries a material point of mass $m$ welded on its rim. What is the moment of inertia of the system about the axis orthogonal to the disk through the center of mass $G$ of the system?

- $\dfrac{3\,mR^2}{2}$
- $mR^2$
- $\dfrac{3\,mR^2}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7\,mR^2}{4}$

## Explanation

The disk and the point have equal masses, so by the **theorem on the center of mass of a composite system** $G$ is the midpoint between $C$ and the material point and is at distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from both. The disk has moment $\dfrac{mR^2}{2}$ about the axis through $C$, therefore by the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** about the axis through $G$ it equals
$$
\frac{mR^2}{2} + m\left( \frac{R}{2} \right)^2 = \frac{3\,mR^2}{4} ,
$$
while the material point contributes $m\left( \dfrac{R}{2} \right)^2 = \dfrac{mR^2}{4}$. By additivity the system has $I_G = mR^2$. The value $\dfrac{3\,mR^2}{2}$ is the moment about the axis through the center $C$, $\dfrac{3\,mR^2}{4}$ neglects the transport of the disk and $\dfrac{7\,mR^2}{4}$ uses for the material point the distance $R$ from $C$ instead of the distance from $G$.
