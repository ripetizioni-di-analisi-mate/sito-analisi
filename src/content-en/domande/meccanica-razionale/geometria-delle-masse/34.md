A homogeneous plate of mass $m$ has the shape of the half-disk with center $O$ and radius $R$ contained in the half-plane $y \geq 0$ of the plane $Oxy$. What is the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the plate through its center of mass $G$?

- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{16}{9\pi^2} \right) m R^2$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{16}{9\pi^2} \right) m R^2$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{4}{\pi^2} \right) m R^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m R^2$

## Explanation

Each element of the half-disk is at the same distance from the axis through $O$ as in the full disk, and the full disk of mass $2m$ has moment $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,R^2$, hence about the axis through $O$ one has $I_O = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m R^2$. The center of mass lies on the $y$ axis by material symmetry, at distance $y_G = \dfrac{4R}{3\pi}$ from $O$. The axis through $O$ and the axis through $G$ are parallel, therefore the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** applies in the form $I_G = I_O - m\,y_G^2$, and one obtains
$$
I_G = \frac{1}{2}\,m R^2 - m\,\frac{16 R^2}{9\pi^2} = \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{16}{9\pi^2} \right) m R^2 .
$$
The plus sign is obtained by adding the transport term, which must instead be subtracted because the axis through the center of mass has the smallest moment; the value with $\dfrac{4}{\pi^2}$ uses the distance $\dfrac{2R}{\pi}$, which is that of the center of mass of the semicircular arc.
