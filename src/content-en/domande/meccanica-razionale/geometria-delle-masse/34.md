A homogeneous rigid disk has mass $m$ and radius $R$. What is the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the plane of the disk through a point of the boundary?

- $I = \dfrac{3\,mR^2}{2}$
- $I = \dfrac{5\,mR^2}{4}$
- $I = 2\,mR^2$
- $I = \dfrac{mR^2}{2}$

## Explanation

The center of the disk is its center of mass by material symmetry, and about the central axis orthogonal to the plane $I_G = \dfrac{mR^2}{2}$ holds. The axis through a point of the boundary is parallel to the central one and is at distance $R$ from it, therefore the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** applies:
$$
I = \frac{mR^2}{2} + mR^2 = \frac{3\,mR^2}{2} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{5\,mR^2}{4}$ is obtained by starting from $\dfrac{mR^2}{4}$, which is the moment of inertia of the disk about a diameter and therefore refers to an axis contained in the plane, whereas $2\,mR^2$ follows from using as central value $mR^2$, which is the one of the ring.
