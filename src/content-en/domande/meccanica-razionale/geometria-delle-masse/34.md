A laboratory measures the inertia of a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ body about parallel axes at coordinates $x=-1,0,+1\,\mathrm m$ along a straight line perpendicular to them. The two outer readings are respectively $9$ and $5\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. What is the coordinate of the centre of mass projected onto that line?

- $+0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $+1\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

All terms independent of $x$ cancel in the difference of the two inertias. With coordinates in metres, $9-5=2[(-1-x_G)^2-(1-x_G)^2]=8x_G$, so $x_G=0.5$.
