The homogeneous triangular plate in the figure occupies the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(3\ell, 0)$ and $(0, 6\ell)$ of the plane $Oxy$. Where is its center of mass located?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 0,6" quote="0 0,3 0,3ℓ,0.6;0 6,0 0,6ℓ,0.6" frecce="-0.8,0,4,0,x;0,-0.8,0,7,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Homogeneous triangular plate with the legs of lengths $3\ell$ and $6\ell$ on the axes"}

- $G = \left( \dfrac{3}{2}\,\ell,\ 3\ell \right)$
- $G = (\ell,\ 2\ell)$
- $G = (2\ell,\ 4\ell)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{3}{4}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{3}{2}\,\ell \right)$

## Explanation

For the homogeneous triangular plate the center of mass is the point where the medians meet, and for the triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(b, 0)$ and $(0, h)$ it results $G = \left( \dfrac{b}{3},\ \dfrac{h}{3} \right)$, as is obtained by integrating by strips. Here $b = 3\ell$ and $h = 6\ell$, therefore
$$
x_G = \frac{3\ell}{3} = \ell ,
\qquad
y_G = \frac{6\ell}{3} = 2\ell .
$$
The same point is the arithmetic mean of the three vertices, because the center of mass of the homogeneous triangle coincides with that of its vertices endowed with equal masses. The point $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}\,\ell,\ 3\ell \right)$ is the midpoint of the hypotenuse and $(2\ell,\ 4\ell)$ is obtained by taking two thirds of the legs in place of one third.
