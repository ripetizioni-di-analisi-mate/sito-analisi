The homogeneous circular sector in the figure has center $O$, radius $R$ and half-angle $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, with the bisector on the $x$ axis. What is the abscissa of its center of mass?

- $x_G = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{\pi}\,R \approx 0.551\,R$
- $x_G = \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2\pi}\,R \approx 0.827\,R$
- $x_G = \dfrac{2}{3}\,R$
- $x_G = \dfrac{R}{2}$

## Explanation

The bisector of the sector is an axis of material symmetry, therefore the center of mass lies on the $x$ axis and $y_G = 0$. For the homogeneous circular sector of radius $R$ and half-angle $\alpha$, with the vertex at the origin and the bisector on the $x$ axis, one has
$$
x_G = \frac{2R\sin\alpha}{3\alpha} ,
$$
a result obtained by integrating in polar coordinates over the area $A = \alpha R^2$. With $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ one has $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, hence
$$
x_G = \frac{2R \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}}{3 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\pi}\,R \approx 0.551\,R .
$$
The value $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2\pi}\,R$ is obtained by applying the formula $\dfrac{R\sin\alpha}{\alpha}$, which holds for the circular arc and not for the sector, where part of the mass lies near the vertex.
