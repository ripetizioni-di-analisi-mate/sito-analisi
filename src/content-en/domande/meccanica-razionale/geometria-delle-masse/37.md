A homogeneous plate has the shape of an isosceles triangle with the base on the $x$ axis and the axis of symmetry coinciding with the $y$ axis, as the diagram in the figure shows, and the pole $O$ is the midpoint of the base. Which statement is true?

::struttura{poligoni="-2,0 2,0 0,3" punti="0,0,O" frecce="-3,0,3.2,0,x;0,-0.9,0,4,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Isosceles triangular plate with the $y$ axis as axis of material symmetry"}

- The axes $x$, $y$ and $z$ are principal axes of inertia relative to $O$
- Only the $z$ axis is a principal axis of inertia relative to $O$
- None of the coordinate axes is a principal axis of inertia relative to $O$
- The $x$ axis is a principal axis of inertia relative to $O$ and the $y$ axis is not

## Explanation

The plate lies in the plane $Oxy$, therefore $I_{xz} = I_{yz} = 0$ and the $z$ axis is principal with respect to every point of the plane, in particular with respect to $O$. The plane $x = 0$, which contains the axes $y$ and $z$, is a plane of material symmetry of the plate and passes through $O$, hence by the **theorem on the principal axes from material symmetry** the line through $O$ orthogonal to that plane, that is the $x$ axis, is principal relative to $O$. The direct computation confirms $I_{xy} = 0$, since to every point $(x, y)$ there corresponds the point $(-x, y)$ with the same mass and the two contributions $xy$ are opposite. The matrix of the tensor relative to $O$ is then diagonal in the frame $x$, $y$, $z$, therefore all three coordinate axes are principal.
