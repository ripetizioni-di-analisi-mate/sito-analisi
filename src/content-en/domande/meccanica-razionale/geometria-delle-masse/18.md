A homogeneous rigid rod $OA$ of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$ is placed on the $x$ axis with the end $O$ at the origin, and at the end $A$ a material point of mass $3m$ is welded. At what distance from $O$ is the center of mass of the system located?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}\,\ell$
- $2\ell$

## Explanation

The rod is homogeneous, therefore its center of mass is the midpoint, which is at distance $\ell$ from $O$; the material point lies at $A$, which is at distance $2\ell$ from $O$. The system consists of two disjoint parts, hence the **theorem on the center of mass of a composite system** applies with total mass $m + 3m = 4m$:
$$
x_G = \frac{m \cdot \ell + 3m \cdot 2\ell}{4m} = \frac{7}{4}\,\ell .
$$
The center of mass lies between the midpoint of the rod and $A$, closer to $A$ since the material point has three times the mass of the rod. The value $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\ell$ is the average of the two positions without the weights of the masses and $\dfrac{5}{4}\,\ell$ is obtained by interchanging the two masses.
