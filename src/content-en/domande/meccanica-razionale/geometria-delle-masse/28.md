A material system is contained in the plane $Oxy$ and has, relative to the pole $O$, the axial moments of inertia $I_{xx} = 3\,m\ell^2$ and $I_{yy} = 5\,m\ell^2$. What is the moment of inertia about the point $O$?

- $I_O = 4\,m\ell^2$
- $I_O = 2\,m\ell^2$
- $I_O = 16\,m\ell^2$
- $I_O = 8\,m\ell^2$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia about the point $O$ is $I_O = \displaystyle\int (x^2 + y^2 + z^2)\,dm$ and the relation $I_{xx} + I_{yy} + I_{zz} = 2\,I_O$ holds. The system lies in the plane $Oxy$, therefore $z = 0$ for every point and hence $I_{zz} = I_{xx} + I_{yy} = 8\,m\ell^2$. Substituting one obtains
$$
I_O = \frac{I_{xx} + I_{yy} + I_{zz}}{2} = \frac{3\,m\ell^2 + 5\,m\ell^2 + 8\,m\ell^2}{2} = 8\,m\ell^2 .
$$
For a plane system the moment of inertia about $O$ therefore coincides with $I_{zz}$, since $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = x^2 + y^2$ throughout the system. The value $4\,m\ell^2$ is obtained by halving the sum of the two given moments alone, without adding $I_{zz}$, and $16\,m\ell^2$ by forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
