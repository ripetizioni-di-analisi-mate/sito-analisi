A homogeneous circular arc has center $O$, radius $R$ and half-angle $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, with the bisector on the $x$ axis. What is the abscissa of its center of mass?

- $x_G = \dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{3\pi}\,R \approx 0.600\,R$
- $x_G = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,R \approx 0.707\,R$
- $x_G = R$
- $x_G = \dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{\pi}\,R \approx 0.900\,R$

## Explanation

The bisector is an axis of material symmetry of the arc, therefore $y_G = 0$. The arc has length $2\alpha R$ and the line element is $ds = R\,d\vartheta$, hence
$$
x_G = \frac{1}{2\alpha R} \int_{-\alpha}^{\alpha} R\cos\vartheta \cdot R\,d\vartheta = \frac{R\sin\alpha}{\alpha} .
$$
With $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ one has $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, therefore
$$
x_G = \frac{R\,\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{\dfrac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{\pi}\,R \approx 0.900\,R .
$$
All the points of the arc are at distance $R$ from $O$, hence the center of mass lies inside the circumference but near the arc. The value $\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{3\pi}\,R$ is obtained by applying the formula $\dfrac{2R\sin\alpha}{3\alpha}$, which holds for the circular sector, and $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,R$ is the abscissa of the midpoint of the chord.
