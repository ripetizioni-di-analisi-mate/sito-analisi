A rigid homogeneous square plate has mass $m$ and side $a$. What is the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the plate through one vertex?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3" punti="1.5,1.5,G,baricentro;0,0,O" quote="0 0,3 0,a,0.6;3 0,3 3,a,0.6" tratteggi="0,0 1.5,1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate of side $a$ with the center of mass $G$ and the vertex $O$"}

- $I_O = \dfrac{5\,ma^2}{12}$
- $I_O = \dfrac{2\,ma^2}{3}$
- $I_O = \dfrac{ma^2}{6}$
- $I_O = \dfrac{7\,ma^2}{6}$

## Explanation

About the central axis orthogonal to the plate the homogeneous square of side $a$ has moment of inertia
$$
I_G = \frac{m\,(a^2 + a^2)}{12} = \frac{ma^2}{6} ,
$$
which is the value of the rectangular plate with both sides equal to $a$. The vertex is at a distance from the center of mass equal to half the diagonal, therefore $d = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,a$ and $d^2 = \dfrac{a^2}{2}$. The two axes are parallel and one of them is central, hence the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** applies:
$$
I_O = \frac{ma^2}{6} + m\,\frac{a^2}{2} = \frac{ma^2 + 3\,ma^2}{6} = \frac{2\,ma^2}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{5\,ma^2}{12}$ is obtained by using $d = \dfrac{a}{2}$, that is the distance from the side instead of the one from the vertex, and $\dfrac{7\,ma^2}{6}$ by using $d = a$.
