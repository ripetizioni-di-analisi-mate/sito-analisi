The homogeneous plate in the figure has the shape of the right trapezoid with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(2a, 0)$, $(a, a)$ and $(0, a)$. Where is its center of mass?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 2,2 0,2" tratteggi="2,0 2,2" quote="0 0,4 0,2a,0.6;0 2,0 0,a,0.6;0 2,2 2,a,-0.5" frecce="-0.8,0,5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,3,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Plate shaped as a right trapezoid, with the dashed line $x = a$ dividing it into a square and a triangle"}

- $G = \left( \dfrac{11}{12}\,a,\ \dfrac{5}{12}\,a \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{3}{4}\,a,\ \dfrac{a}{2} \right)$
- $G = \left( a,\ \dfrac{a}{2} \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{7}{9}\,a,\ \dfrac{4}{9}\,a \right)$

## Explanation

The line $x = a$ divides the trapezoid into the square $[0, a] \times [0, a]$, of area $a^2$ and center of mass $G_1 = \left( \dfrac{a}{2},\ \dfrac{a}{2} \right)$, and the triangle with vertices $(a, 0)$, $(2a, 0)$, $(a, a)$, of area $\dfrac{a^2}{2}$. The center of mass of the triangle is the mean of its vertices, so $G_2 = \left( \dfrac{4}{3}\,a,\ \dfrac{a}{3} \right)$. The plate is homogeneous, therefore the areas replace the masses in the **theorem on the center of mass of a composite system**, and with the total area $\dfrac{3a^2}{2}$ one obtains
$$
x_G = \frac{a^2 \cdot \dfrac{a}{2} + \dfrac{a^2}{2} \cdot \dfrac{4a}{3}}{\dfrac{3a^2}{2}} = \frac{7}{9}\,a ,
\qquad
y_G = \frac{a^2 \cdot \dfrac{a}{2} + \dfrac{a^2}{2} \cdot \dfrac{a}{3}}{\dfrac{3a^2}{2}} = \frac{4}{9}\,a .
$$
The point $\left( \dfrac{11}{12}\,a,\ \dfrac{5}{12}\,a \right)$ is the mean of $G_1$ and $G_2$ with equal weights, $\left( \dfrac{3}{4}\,a,\ \dfrac{a}{2} \right)$ is the mean of the four vertices and $\left( a,\ \dfrac{a}{2} \right)$ is the center of the rectangle $[0, 2a] \times [0, a]$ containing the trapezoid.
