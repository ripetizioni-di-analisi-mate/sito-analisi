A homogeneous rigid disk of mass $m$, center $O$ and radius $R$ rotates with constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega}$ about one of its diameters, which is fixed. What is its kinetic energy $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\vec{\omega} \cdot \mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{\omega}$?

- $T = \dfrac{mR^2\omega^2}{4}$
- $T = \dfrac{mR^2\omega^2}{8}$
- $T = \dfrac{5\,mR^2\omega^2}{8}$
- $T = \dfrac{3\,mR^2\omega^2}{4}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity is directed along the diameter, so $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\vec{u}$ with $\vec{u}$ unit vector of the diameter, and $\vec{\omega} \cdot \mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{\omega} = \omega^2\,\vec{u} \cdot \mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{u}$ is $\omega^2$ times the moment of inertia about the diameter. The disk is a plane system with two orthogonal diameters that are axes of material symmetry, so the two moments about the diameters are equal and their sum is $\dfrac{mR^2}{2}$; each of them therefore equals $\dfrac{mR^2}{4}$. It follows that
$$
T = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{mR^2}{4} \cdot \omega^2 = \frac{mR^2\omega^2}{8} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{mR^2\omega^2}{4}$ uses the moment $\dfrac{mR^2}{2}$ about the axis orthogonal to the disk, $\dfrac{5\,mR^2\omega^2}{8}$ uses the line tangent to the rim in the plane of the disk and $\dfrac{3\,mR^2\omega^2}{4}$ the axis orthogonal to the disk through a point of the rim.
