A rigid shaft of negligible mass coincides with the $x$ axis and rotates about it with angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{\imath}$. Two material points of mass $m$ are fixed to the shaft by arms of negligible mass, at the positions $(\ell, \ell, 0)$ and $(-\ell, -\ell, 0)$ at the instant considered, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the angular momentum $\vec{K}_O = \mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{\omega}$ at that instant?

::struttura{linee="-3.2,0 3.2,0" punti="2,2,P_1;-2,-2,P_2;0,0,O" tratteggi="2,0 2,2;-2,0 -2,-2" frecce="3.2,0,4,0,x;0,-2.6,0,2.8,y" etichette="2.45,2.35,m;-2.5,-2.3,m;3.4,0.45,ω" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,-0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Shaft along the $x$ axis with two eccentric masses in opposite positions with respect to $O$"}

- $\vec{K}_O = 2\,m\ell^2\omega\,(\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$
- $\vec{K}_O = 2\,m\ell^2\omega\,\hat{\imath}$
- $\vec{K}_O = 2\,m\ell^2\omega\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$
- $\vec{K}_O = 4\,m\ell^2\omega\,\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

Since $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{\imath}$, the vector $\mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{\omega}$ is $\omega$ times the first column of the tensor, that is $\omega\,(I_{xx},\ -I_{xy},\ -I_{xz})$. The definitions give
$$
I_{xx} = \sum_i m_i\,(y_i^2 + z_i^2) = 2\,m\ell^2 ,
\qquad
I_{xy} = \sum_i m_i\,x_i\,y_i = m\ell^2 + m\ell^2 = 2\,m\ell^2 ,
\qquad
I_{xz} = 0 ,
$$
hence $\vec{K}_O = 2\,m\ell^2\omega\,\hat{\imath} - 2\,m\ell^2\omega\,\hat{\jmath}$. The angular momentum is not parallel to $\vec{\omega}$ because the rotation axis is not a principal axis of inertia, given that $I_{xy} \ne 0$, therefore the rotor is dynamically unbalanced even though its center of mass lies on the axis. The value $2\,m\ell^2\omega\,\hat{\imath}$ neglects the product of inertia, $2\,m\ell^2\omega\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$ forgets the minus sign with which the product appears in the tensor and $4\,m\ell^2\omega\,\hat{\imath}$ uses the distances $\sqrt{2}\,\ell$ of the points from $O$ instead of the distances $\ell$ from the axis.
