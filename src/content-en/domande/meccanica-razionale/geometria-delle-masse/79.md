The homogeneous plate in the figure has mass $m$ and is the square $[0, 2a] \times [0, 2a]$ without the square $[a, 2a] \times [a, 2a]$. What is the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the plate through the origin $O$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,2 2,2 2,4 0,4" punti="0,0,O" tratteggi="2,0 2,2 0,2" quote="0 0,4 0,2a,0.6;0 4,0 0,2a,0.6;2 4,4 4,a,-0.4" frecce="4.3,0,5,0,x;0,4.3,0,5,y" riferimento=0 titolo="L-shaped plate made of three squares of side $a$"}

- $\dfrac{7\,ma^2}{2}$
- $2\,ma^2$
- $\dfrac{32\,ma^2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{10\,ma^2}{3}$

## Explanation

The plate is made of three squares of side $a$ with the same area, so each has mass $\dfrac{m}{3}$; their centers are $\left( \dfrac{a}{2},\ \dfrac{a}{2} \right)$, $\left( \dfrac{3a}{2},\ \dfrac{a}{2} \right)$ and $\left( \dfrac{a}{2},\ \dfrac{3a}{2} \right)$, at distances from the axis through $O$ whose squares are $\dfrac{a^2}{2}$, $\dfrac{5a^2}{2}$ and $\dfrac{5a^2}{2}$. Each square has moment $\dfrac{m}{3} \cdot \dfrac{a^2 + a^2}{12} = \dfrac{ma^2}{18}$ about the orthogonal axis through its own center, hence by the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** and additivity
$$
I_O = 3 \cdot \frac{ma^2}{18} + \frac{m}{3}\left( \frac{a^2}{2} + \frac{5a^2}{2} + \frac{5a^2}{2} \right) = \frac{ma^2}{6} + \frac{11\,ma^2}{6} = 2\,ma^2 .
$$
Treating the plate as a difference, the full square has mass $\dfrac{4m}{3}$ and moment $\dfrac{32\,ma^2}{9}$, which is the value obtained by neglecting the missing square; $\dfrac{7\,ma^2}{2}$ subtracts the moment of the missing square without transporting it from its center to $O$, and $\dfrac{10\,ma^2}{3}$ subtracts it computed about its vertex $(a, a)$.
