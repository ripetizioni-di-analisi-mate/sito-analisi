A homogeneous rigid wire of mass $m$ is bent into a semicircle with center $O$ and radius $R$, contained in the half-plane $y \ge 0$. A material point of mass $\mu$ is connected to the ends of the wire by two light rods of negligible mass and occupies the point $(0, -R)$, as the diagram in the figure shows. What must $\mu$ be for the center of mass of the system to coincide with $O$?

::struttura{nodi="P0,2.000,0.000,-;P1,1.932,0.518,-;P2,1.732,1.000,-;P3,1.414,1.414,-;P4,1.000,1.732,-;P5,0.518,1.932,-;P6,0.000,2.000,-;P7,-0.518,1.932,-;P8,-1.000,1.732,-;P9,-1.414,1.414,-;P10,-1.732,1.000,-;P11,-1.932,0.518,-;P12,-2.000,0.000,-;Q,0,-2,-" aste="P0-P1;P1-P2;P2-P3;P3-P4;P4-P5;P5-P6;P6-P7;P7-P8;P8-P9;P9-P10;P10-P11;P11-P12" catene="P0-Q;P12-Q" punti="0,0,O;0,-2,,pieno" etichette="0.4,-2.3,μ;0.7,1.35,m" quote="0 0,2 0,R,0.5" frecce="-2.8,0,2.9,0,x;0,-2.8,0,2.9,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Semicircular wire of mass $m$ and counterweight of mass $\mu$ at the point $(0, -R)$"}

- $\mu = \dfrac{4m}{3\pi}$
- $\mu = \dfrac{2m}{\pi}$
- $\mu = \dfrac{\pi m}{2}$
- $\mu = m$

## Explanation

The $y$ axis is an axis of material symmetry of the system, so $x_G = 0$ and it remains to impose $y_G = 0$. The wire is an arc of circle of half-angle $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ with the bisector on the $y$ axis, therefore its center of mass is at distance
$$
\frac{R\sin\alpha}{\alpha} = \frac{2R}{\pi}
$$
from $O$. By the **theorem on the center of mass of a composite system** the ordinate of $G$ vanishes when
$$
m \cdot \frac{2R}{\pi} + \mu \cdot (-R) = 0 ,
$$
hence $\mu = \dfrac{2m}{\pi} \approx 0.64\,m$. The value $\dfrac{4m}{3\pi}$ is obtained by using the center of mass $\dfrac{4R}{3\pi}$ of the semicircular plate in place of that of the wire, while $\dfrac{\pi m}{2}$ inverts the ratio between the distances.
