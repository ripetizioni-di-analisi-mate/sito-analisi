A homogeneous rigid square plate of mass $m$ occupies the square $[0, a] \times [0, a]$ of the plane $Oxy$. What is the moment of inertia about the line through $O$ with unit vector $\vec{u} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(1, 0, 1)$, which does not lie in the plane of the plate?

- $I_u = ma^2$
- $I_u = \dfrac{ma^2}{3}$
- $I_u = \dfrac{ma^2}{2}$
- $I_u = \dfrac{ma^2}{8}$

## Explanation

Relative to the vertex $O$ the plate has $I_{xx} = I_{yy} = \dfrac{ma^2}{3}$ and $I_{xy} = \dfrac{ma^2}{4}$; since it lies in the plane $Oxy$, moreover, $I_{xz} = I_{yz} = 0$ and $I_{zz} = I_{xx} + I_{yy} = \dfrac{2\,ma^2}{3}$. The unit vector has $u_x = u_z = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ and $u_y = 0$, so in the formula $I_u = \vec{u} \cdot \mathbb{I}_O\,\vec{u}$ only the terms in $u_x^2$, $u_z^2$ and $u_x u_z$ remain:
$$
I_u = I_{xx}\,\frac{1}{2} + I_{zz}\,\frac{1}{2} - 2\,I_{xz}\,\frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{ma^2}{3} + \frac{2\,ma^2}{3} \right) = \frac{ma^2}{2} .
$$
The product $I_{xy}$ does not contribute because $u_y = 0$. The value $ma^2$ is obtained without the factors $u_x^2 = u_z^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{ma^2}{3}$ by setting $I_{zz} = I_{xx}$ and $\dfrac{ma^2}{8}$ by using the central moments $\dfrac{ma^2}{12}$ and $\dfrac{ma^2}{6}$, which refer to the center of the plate and not to the vertex.
