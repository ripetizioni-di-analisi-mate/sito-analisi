A homogeneous plate has the shape of an equilateral triangle with center of mass $G$. Consider the lines through $G$ contained in the plane of the plate. Which statement about the moment of inertia of the plate about these lines is true?

- It is maximum about the three medians
- It is minimum about the three medians
- It has the same value for all the lines
- It depends on the line, but it is the same for two mutually orthogonal lines

## Explanation

Each median is an axis of material symmetry of the plate, so by the **theorem on the principal axes from material symmetry** it is a principal axis of inertia relative to $G$. The rotation by $120^\circ$ about $G$ maps the plate onto itself and maps each median onto another one, therefore the three medians have the same moment of inertia. If the two principal moments in the plane were different, the principal axes in the plane would be only two orthogonal lines, while the medians form angles of $60^\circ$ with each other. It follows that the $2 \times 2$ block of the tensor relative to the plane is a multiple of the identity matrix, hence every line through $G$ in the plane is principal and the moment of inertia has the same value for all of them.
