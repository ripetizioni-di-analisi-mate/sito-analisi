A rigid wheel is made of a rim, modeled as a homogeneous ring of mass $3m$ and radius $R$, and of four spokes, each a homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $R$ going from the center $O$ to the rim, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the moment of inertia of the wheel about the axis orthogonal to its plane through $O$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2.2;0,0,2.0,foro" nodi="O,0,0;A,2,0,-;B,0,2,-;C,-2,0,-;D,0,-2,-" aste="O-A;O-B;O-C;O-D" quote="0 0,2.2 0,R,-2.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Wheel made of a rim of mass $3m$ and four spokes of mass $m$"}

- $\dfrac{13\,mR^2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{10\,mR^2}{3}$
- $7\,mR^2$
- $\dfrac{17\,mR^2}{6}$

## Explanation

All the points of the rim are at distance $R$ from the axis, so its moment equals $3m \cdot R^2 = 3\,mR^2$. Each spoke has one end on the axis and is orthogonal to it, therefore its moment is that of the rod about the orthogonal axis through an end, $\dfrac{mR^2}{3}$. By the additivity of the moment of inertia
$$
I_O = 3\,mR^2 + 4 \cdot \frac{mR^2}{3} = \frac{13\,mR^2}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{10\,mR^2}{3}$ uses for the spokes the central moment $\dfrac{mR^2}{12}$ without the transport, $7\,mR^2$ concentrates the mass of the spokes on the rim and $\dfrac{17\,mR^2}{6}$ treats the rim as a solid disk.
