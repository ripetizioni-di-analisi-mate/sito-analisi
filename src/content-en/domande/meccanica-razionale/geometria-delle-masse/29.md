A homogeneous plate is contained in the plane $Oxy$ and has, relative to the pole $O$, the moments of inertia $I_{xx} = 2\,m\ell^2$ and $I_{zz} = 5\,m\ell^2$. What is the value of $I_{yy}$?

- $I_{yy} = 7\,m\ell^2$
- $I_{yy} = 3\,m\ell^2$
- $I_{yy} = \dfrac{5}{2}\,m\ell^2$
- $I_{yy} = 2\,m\ell^2$

## Explanation

All the points of the plate have $z = 0$, therefore $I_{xx} = \displaystyle\int y^2\,dm$ and $I_{yy} = \displaystyle\int x^2\,dm$, whereas $I_{zz} = \displaystyle\int (x^2 + y^2)\,dm$. Adding the first two expressions one obtains the relation of plane systems
$$
I_{zz} = I_{xx} + I_{yy} ,
$$
hence $I_{yy} = I_{zz} - I_{xx} = 5\,m\ell^2 - 2\,m\ell^2 = 3\,m\ell^2$. The value $7\,m\ell^2$ is obtained by adding the two data instead of subtracting them, and $2\,m\ell^2$ would assume $I_{xx} = I_{yy}$, since with that value the relation of plane systems would give $I_{zz} = 4\,m\ell^2$, in contrast with the given datum.
