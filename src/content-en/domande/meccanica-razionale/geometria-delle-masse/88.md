A homogeneous triangular plate of mass $m$ has a vertex at the point $O$, and the opposite side, of length $b$, is parallel to a line $a$ through $O$ at distance $h$ from it, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the moment of inertia of the plate about the line $a$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 -1,3 2,3" punti="0,0,O" tratteggi="-2,0 3,0" etichette="3.3,0,a" quote="-1 3,2 3,b,-0.6;2.6 0,2.6 3,h,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Triangular plate with the vertex $O$ on the line $a$ and the opposite side parallel to $a$"}

- $\dfrac{mh^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{mh^2}{6}$
- $\dfrac{mh^2}{18}$
- $\dfrac{mh^2}{4}$

## Explanation

The plate has area $\dfrac{bh}{2}$, so its density is $\sigma = \dfrac{2m}{bh}$. The plate is divided into strips parallel to $a$: by similar triangles, the strip at distance $y$ from $a$ has length $\dfrac{b\,y}{h}$, and all its points are at distance $y$ from $a$. Therefore
$$
I_a = \sigma \int_0^h y^2\,\frac{b\,y}{h}\,dy = \frac{2m}{bh} \cdot \frac{b\,h^3}{4} = \frac{mh^2}{2} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{mh^2}{6}$ is the moment about the side opposite to $O$, $\dfrac{mh^2}{18}$ that about the line parallel to $a$ through the center of mass, and $\dfrac{mh^2}{4}$ is obtained by using the density $\dfrac{m}{bh}$, that is by forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the area of the triangle.
