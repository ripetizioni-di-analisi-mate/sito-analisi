Four equal homogeneous rigid rods, each of mass $m$ and length $a$, are welded to form the square frame in the figure. What is the moment of inertia of the frame about the axis orthogonal to its plane through the center $O$ of the square?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,3,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" punti="1.5,1.5,O" tratteggi="1.5,1.5 1.5,0" quote="A,B,a,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Square frame of four equal rods with center $O$"}

- $\dfrac{ma^2}{3}$
- $ma^2$
- $\dfrac{4\,ma^2}{3}$
- $2\,ma^2$

## Explanation

The midpoint of each rod is at distance $\dfrac{a}{2}$ from $O$, and the axis through $O$ is parallel to the axis orthogonal to the plane through that midpoint. By the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** each rod therefore contributes
$$
\frac{ma^2}{12} + m\left( \frac{a}{2} \right)^2 = \frac{ma^2}{3} ,
$$
and by additivity the frame has $I_O = 4 \cdot \dfrac{ma^2}{3} = \dfrac{4\,ma^2}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{ma^2}{3}$ is the contribution of a single rod, $ma^2$ concentrates the mass of each rod at its midpoint and $2\,ma^2$ concentrates the masses at the vertices, at distance $\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$ from $O$.
