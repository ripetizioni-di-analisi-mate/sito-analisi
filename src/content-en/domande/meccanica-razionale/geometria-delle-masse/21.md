A homogeneous half-disk plate has radius $R$, the diameter on the $x$ axis with its midpoint at the origin, and is contained in the half-plane $y \ge 0$. What is the ordinate of its center of mass?

- $y_G = \dfrac{4R}{3\pi} \approx 0.424\,R$
- $y_G = \dfrac{2R}{\pi} \approx 0.637\,R$
- $y_G = \dfrac{3R}{8}$
- $y_G = \dfrac{R}{2}$

## Explanation

The $y$ axis is an axis of material symmetry, therefore $x_G = 0$. The half-disk is the circular sector of half-angle $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ with the bisector on the $y$ axis, hence from the value $\dfrac{2R\sin\alpha}{3\alpha}$ of the sector one obtains
$$
y_G = \frac{2R \sin\dfrac{\pi}{2}}{3 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{2}} = \frac{4R}{3\pi} \approx 0.424\,R .
$$
The same value follows by integrating in polar coordinates over the area $\dfrac{\pi R^2}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{2R}{\pi}$ is the ordinate of the center of mass of the material half-circumference, where the whole mass lies at distance $R$ from the center, and $\dfrac{3R}{8}$ is the height of the center of mass of the solid half-sphere.
