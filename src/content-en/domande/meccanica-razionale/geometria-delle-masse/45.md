A homogeneous plate is the square $[0, 2a] \times [0, 2a]$ with the square $[a, 2a] \times [a, 2a]$ removed, as the diagram in the figure shows. Where is its center of mass located?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,2 2,2 2,4 0,4" quote="0 0,4 0,2a,0.6;0 4,0 0,2a,0.6" frecce="-0.9,0,5,0,x;0,-0.9,0,5,y" tratteggi="0,0 4,4" riferimento=0 titolo="Square of side $2a$ with the square of side $a$ removed at the corner opposite the origin"}

- $G = (a,\ a)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{11}{10}\,a,\ \dfrac{11}{10}\,a \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{5}{6}\,a,\ \dfrac{5}{6}\,a \right)$
- $G = \left( \dfrac{4}{3}\,a,\ \dfrac{4}{3}\,a \right)$

## Explanation

The diagonal issuing from the origin is an axis of material symmetry of the plate, therefore $x_G = y_G$ and it suffices to compute one coordinate. The plate is the difference between the solid square, of area $4a^2$ and center of mass at $(a, a)$, and the missing square, of area $a^2$ and center of mass at $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}\,a,\ \dfrac{3}{2}\,a \right)$. The plate is homogeneous, hence the areas replace the masses in the formula for the center of mass of the composite system and
$$
x_G = \frac{4a^2 \cdot a - a^2 \cdot \dfrac{3}{2}\,a}{4a^2 - a^2} = \frac{\dfrac{5}{2}\,a^3}{3a^2} = \frac{5}{6}\,a .
$$
The center of mass moves toward the origin, that is to the side opposite the hole. The point $\left( \dfrac{11}{10}\,a,\ \dfrac{11}{10}\,a \right)$ is obtained by adding the missing square instead of subtracting it, and $\left( \dfrac{4}{3}\,a,\ \dfrac{4}{3}\,a \right)$ by removing its area from the denominator without removing its contribution from the numerator.
