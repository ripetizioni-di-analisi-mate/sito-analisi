The homogeneous rigid rod in the figure has mass $m$ and length $\ell$, has one end at the origin $O$ and forms an angle of $60^\circ$ with the $x$ axis, in the plane $Oxy$. What is the moment of inertia about the line through $O$ that forms an angle of $30^\circ$ with the $x$ axis?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.5,2.598" aste="O-A" frecce="-0.7,0,3.4,0,x;0,-0.7,0,3.2,y;0,0,2.6,1.5,u" etichette="0.95,0.3,30°;0.75,0.95,30°" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod inclined at $60^\circ$ and line of unit vector $\vec{u}$ inclined at $30^\circ$"}

- $I_u = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$
- $I_u = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{48}$
- $I_u = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{4}$
- $I_u = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$

## Explanation

The line of unit vector $\vec{u}$ and the rod form between them the angle $\beta = 60^\circ - 30^\circ = 30^\circ$. The point of the rod at distance $t$ from $O$ is at distance $t \sin\beta$ from the line, therefore, with linear density $\lambda = \dfrac{m}{\ell}$,
$$
I_u = \int_0^{\ell} \frac{m}{\ell}\,(t \sin\beta)^2\,dt = \frac{m\ell^2}{3}\,\sin^2\beta .
$$
Since $\sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$, it results
$$
I_u = \frac{m\ell^2}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{m\ell^2}{12} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$ is the moment of inertia about the axis through $O$ orthogonal to the rod, that is the case $\beta = 90^\circ$, whereas $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{48}$ is obtained by starting from the central value $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$, which refers to an axis through the midpoint and not through $O$.
