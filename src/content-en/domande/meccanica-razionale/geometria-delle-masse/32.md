A homogeneous plate has the shape of the right triangle with vertices $O = (0, 0)$, $(a, 0)$ and $(0, b)$ of the plane $Oxy$, with $a > 0$ and $b > 0$. Which statement about the inertia tensor relative to the pole $O$ is true?

- All the products of inertia vanish, since the plate is plane
- It results $I_{xy} < 0$, whereas $I_{xz} = I_{yz} = 0$
- It results $I_{xy} > 0$, whereas $I_{xz} = I_{yz} = 0$
- It results $I_{zz} = I_{yy} - I_{xx}$

## Explanation

All the points of the plate have $z = 0$, therefore the integrals $\displaystyle\int xz\,dm$ and $\displaystyle\int yz\,dm$ vanish and hence $I_{xz} = I_{yz} = 0$, from which it follows that the $z$ axis is principal. The product of inertia in the plane is instead $I_{xy} = \displaystyle\int xy\,dm$, and the plate lies in the first quadrant, where $x > 0$ and $y > 0$ at all interior points, therefore the integrand is positive and $I_{xy} > 0$. The explicit computation gives $I_{xy} = \dfrac{mab}{12}$. For plane systems $I_{zz} = I_{xx} + I_{yy}$ also holds, therefore the equality with the difference of the two moments is false.
