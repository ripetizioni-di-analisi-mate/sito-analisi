A rigid plate of mass $m$ lies in the plane $Oxy$. About the axes orthogonal to the plate through the points $P_1$ and $P_2$, which are $2\ell$ apart, the moments of inertia equal $I_1 = 6\,m\ell^2$ and $I_2 = 4\,m\ell^2$, and the center of mass $G$ of the plate lies on the line $P_1 P_2$, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the plate through $G$?

::struttura{poligoni="-1.2,-1.1 1.6,-1.6 4.4,-1.2 5.3,0.6 3.6,1.9 0.6,1.7 -1.0,0.8" punti="0,0,P_1,pieno;4,0,P_2,pieno" tratteggi="-1.6,0 5.8,0" quote="0 0,4 0,2ℓ,-1" riferimento=0 titolo="Plate with the points $P_1$ and $P_2$ on the line containing the center of mass"}

- $5\,m\ell^2$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}\,m\ell^2$
- $3\,m\ell^2$
- $2\,m\ell^2$

## Explanation

Let $d$ be the distance of $G$ from $P_1$ measured along the line, so that the distance from $P_2$ equals $|2\ell - d|$. Applying the **Huygens-Steiner theorem** to the two axes gives the system
$$
\begin{cases}
6\,m\ell^2 = I_G + m\,d^2 \\[4pt]
4\,m\ell^2 = I_G + m\,(2\ell - d)^2
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting the second equation from the first and dividing by $m$ gives $2\ell^2 = d^2 - (2\ell - d)^2 = 4\ell d - 4\ell^2$, hence $d = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\ell$ and
$$
I_G = 6\,m\ell^2 - \frac{9}{4}\,m\ell^2 = \frac{15}{4}\,m\ell^2 .
$$
The center of mass is closer to $P_2$, consistently with $I_2 < I_1$. The value $5\,m\ell^2$ is the mean of the two moments, $3\,m\ell^2$ is obtained by assuming $G$ at the midpoint of $P_1 P_2$ and transporting from $P_2$, and $2\,m\ell^2$ by transporting from $P_1$ with the distance $2\ell$ between the two axes.
