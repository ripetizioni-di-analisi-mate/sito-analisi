In the frame $(O; x, y, z)$ a material point of mass $2m$ is placed at $(\ell, \ell, 0)$ and a point of mass $3m$ at $(\ell, 0, 0)$, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the smallest of the principal moments of inertia relative to the pole $O$?

::struttura{punti="2,2,P_1;2,0,P_2;0,0," tratteggi="0,0 2,2;0,0 2,0" etichette="2.2,2.3,2m;2.2,0.35,3m;-0.3,-0.35,O" frecce="-0.7,0,3.2,0,x;0,-0.7,0,3.2,y" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Two material points of masses $2m$ and $3m$ in the plane $Oxy$"}

- $2\,m\ell^2$
- $m\ell^2$
- $6\,m\ell^2$
- $7\,m\ell^2$

## Explanation

From the definitions one obtains $I_{xx} = 2\,m\ell^2$, $I_{yy} = 5\,m\ell^2$ and $I_{xy} = 2\,m\ell^2$, and since the two points lie in the plane $Oxy$ it results $I_{zz} = 7\,m\ell^2$ with $I_{xz} = I_{yz} = 0$. The $z$ axis is then principal and the two remaining principal moments are the eigenvalues of the block
$$
m\ell^2 \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ -2 & 5 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
whose characteristic equation is $\lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 6 = 0$, since the trace equals $7$ and the determinant equals $10 - 4 = 6$. The roots are $\lambda = 1$ and $\lambda = 6$, therefore the principal moments are $m\ell^2$, $6\,m\ell^2$ and $7\,m\ell^2$ and the smallest one equals $m\ell^2$. The values $2\,m\ell^2$ and $7\,m\ell^2$ are entries of the diagonal of the tensor, which coincide with the principal moments only when the product of inertia vanishes.
