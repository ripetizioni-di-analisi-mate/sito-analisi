A homogeneous rigid rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, with negligible cross section, is parallel to the line $a$ at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from it, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the moment of inertia of the rod about the line $a$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,1,-;B,4,1,-" aste="A-B" tratteggi="-0.6,-1 4.6,-1" quote="4 -1,4 1,0.5ℓ,-0.6;A,B,ℓ,0.6" etichette="4.9,-1,a" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod parallel to the line $a$, at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$"}

- $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$
- $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7\,m\ell^2}{12}$

## Explanation

The cross section of the rod is negligible and the rod is parallel to $a$, so each of its points is at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from the line. The definition of moment of inertia gives
$$
I_a = \int \left( \frac{\ell}{2} \right)^2 dm = \frac{m\ell^2}{4} .
$$
The same value follows from the **Huygens-Steiner theorem**, since the line parallel to $a$ through the center of mass is the line containing the rod, about which the moment is zero, and the transport term equals $m\left( \dfrac{\ell}{2} \right)^2$. The value $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$ is obtained by adding the moment $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$, which refers to the axis orthogonal to the rod and not to the line of the rod, and $\dfrac{7\,m\ell^2}{12}$ by adding the moment $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$ about the orthogonal axis through an end.
