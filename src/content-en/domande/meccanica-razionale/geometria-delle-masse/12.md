- $\mathbb{I}_O = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{6} \begin{pmatrix} 4 & -1 & 0 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\mathbb{I}_O = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{6} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 4 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\mathbb{I}_O = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{6} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ -1 & 4 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\mathbb{I}_O = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{6} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ -1 & 4 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The plate has area $\ell^2$, therefore its surface density is $\sigma = \dfrac{m}{\ell^2}$, and at height $y$ the horizontal strip goes from $x = 0$ to $x = 2\,(\ell - y)$. Integrating one obtains
$$
I_{xx} = \sigma \int_0^{\ell} 2\,(\ell - y)\,y^2\,dy = \frac{m\ell^2}{6} ,
\qquad
I_{yy} = \sigma \int_0^{2\ell} \left( \ell - \frac{x}{2} \right) x^2\,dx = \frac{2\,m\ell^2}{3} ,
$$
and since the plate lies in the plane $Oxy$ it results $I_{zz} = I_{xx} + I_{yy} = \dfrac{5\,m\ell^2}{6}$. The product of inertia equals
$$
I_{xy} = \sigma \int_0^{\ell} y\,\frac{\left[ 2\,(\ell - y) \right]^2}{2}\,dy = \frac{m\ell^2}{6} ,
$$
and it is positive because the plate lies in the first quadrant, where $xy > 0$; in the matrix it therefore appears with the minus sign. The moment $I_{yy}$ is four times $I_{xx}$ since the leg on the $x$ axis is twice as long as the one on the $y$ axis, hence the matrix with the two values interchanged is wrong.
