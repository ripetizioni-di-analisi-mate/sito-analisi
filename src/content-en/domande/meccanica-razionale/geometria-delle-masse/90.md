From a homogeneous disk with center $O$ and radius $R$ a disk of radius $\dfrac{R}{4}$ is removed, entirely contained in the original disk, with its center at distance $d$ from $O$. For which value of $d$ is the moment of inertia of the plate with the hole about the axis through $O$ orthogonal to the plane minimum?

- $d = 0$
- $d = \dfrac{R}{4}$
- $d = \dfrac{R}{2}$
- $d = \dfrac{3R}{4}$

## Explanation

The density $\sigma$ of the plate is fixed, so the removed disk has mass $m_f = \sigma\pi\,\dfrac{R^2}{16}$, which does not depend on $d$. The moment of the removed disk about the axis through $O$ is obtained by the **Huygens-Steiner theorem**, therefore the moment of the plate with the hole equals
$$
I_O(d) = \frac{\sigma\pi R^4}{2} - m_f \left( \frac{R^2}{32} + d^2 \right) ,
$$
which decreases as $d$ increases. The hole is contained in the disk when $d + \dfrac{R}{4} \le R$, that is for $d \le \dfrac{3R}{4}$, hence the minimum is reached for $d = \dfrac{3R}{4}$, with the hole internally tangent to the rim. Removing mass far from the axis reduces the moment more than removing it close to the axis, and for $d = 0$ the moment of the plate with the hole is maximum.
