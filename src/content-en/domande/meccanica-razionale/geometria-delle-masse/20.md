Two material points of masses $m_1 = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2$ are placed at distance $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ from each other, and the center of mass of the system is at distance $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the point of mass $m_1$. What is the value of $m_2$?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

For two material points the center of mass lies on the segment joining them and divides it into parts inversely proportional to the masses, therefore, denoting by $d_1$ and $d_2$ the distances of $G$ from the two points, $m_1 d_1 = m_2 d_2$ holds. Since $d_1 = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $d_2 = 30 - 10 = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$, one obtains
$$
m_2 = m_1\,\frac{d_1}{d_2} = 2 \cdot \frac{10}{20}\,\mathrm{kg} = 1\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
The center of mass is closer to the point of larger mass, hence $m_2$ turns out to be smaller than $m_1$. The value $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is obtained by inverting the ratio of the distances, whereas $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ comes from using the distance between the two points in place of $d_2$.
