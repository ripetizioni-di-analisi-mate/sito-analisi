A compass is made of two homogeneous rigid rods $OA$ and $OB$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, hinged at the common end $O$ and free to open in the plane $Oxy$ forming an angle $\gamma$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Which statement about the moment of inertia of the compass about the axis through $O$ orthogonal to the plane is true?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,3.5,0;B,2.2,2.7" aste="O-A;O-B" cerniere="O" etichette="0.85,0.45,γ" quote="O,A,ℓ,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Compass of two rods hinged at $O$ forming the angle $\gamma$"}

- It equals $\dfrac{2\,m\ell^2}{3}$ for every value of $\gamma$
- It is maximum when the rods are aligned, that is for $\gamma = \pi$
- It is maximum when the rods are orthogonal, that is for $\gamma = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- It equals $\dfrac{2\,m\ell^2}{3}$ only for $\gamma = \pi$

## Explanation

Each rod has one end on the axis and lies in a plane orthogonal to the axis, so its point at distance $t$ from $O$ is at distance $t$ from the axis, whatever the direction of the rod in the plane. Therefore each rod has moment
$$
\int_0^{\ell} \frac{m}{\ell}\,t^2\,dt = \frac{m\ell^2}{3} ,
$$
and by additivity the compass has moment $\dfrac{2\,m\ell^2}{3}$ for every opening $\gamma$. The moment about a line of the plane through $O$, for example the bisector of the angle $\gamma$, depends instead on the opening, because the distances of the points from that line change with $\gamma$.
