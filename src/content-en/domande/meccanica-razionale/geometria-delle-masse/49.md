The homogeneous rigid rectangular plate in the figure has mass $m$ and sides $2\ell$ and $\ell$. What is the moment of inertia about one of its diagonals?

::struttura{poligoni="-2,-1 2,-1 2,1 -2,1" tratteggi="-2,-1 2,1" punti="0,0,G" quote="-2 -1,2 -1,2ℓ,0.6;2 -1,2 1,ℓ,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular plate with sides $2\ell$ and $\ell$ and a dashed diagonal"}

- $\dfrac{17\,m\ell^2}{60}$
- $\dfrac{5\,m\ell^2}{24}$
- $\dfrac{2\,m\ell^2}{15}$
- $\dfrac{5\,m\ell^2}{12}$

## Explanation

The pole is chosen at the center of mass $G$, with the $x$ axis parallel to the side $2\ell$ and the $y$ axis parallel to the side $\ell$. The $x$ and $y$ axes are axes of material symmetry of the plate, so by the **theorem on the principal axes from material symmetry** they are principal and $I_{xy} = 0$. The moments about the two axes equal
$$
I_{xx} = \frac{m\ell^2}{12} ,
\qquad
I_{yy} = \frac{m\,(2\ell)^2}{12} = \frac{m\ell^2}{3} .
$$
The diagonal passes through $G$ and has the direction of the vector $(2\ell,\ \ell,\ 0)$, therefore its unit vector is $\vec{u} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\,(2,\ 1,\ 0)$. Since the product of inertia is zero, the formula $I_u = \vec{u} \cdot \mathbb{I}_G\,\vec{u}$ gives
$$
I_u = I_{xx}\,u_x^2 + I_{yy}\,u_y^2 = \frac{m\ell^2}{12} \cdot \frac{4}{5} + \frac{m\ell^2}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{5} = \frac{2\,m\ell^2}{15} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{17\,m\ell^2}{60}$ is obtained by exchanging the squares of the components of the unit vector, $\dfrac{5\,m\ell^2}{24}$ is the mean of $I_{xx}$ and $I_{yy}$, which holds for the line through $G$ inclined at $45^\circ$ and not for the diagonal, and $\dfrac{5\,m\ell^2}{12}$ is the moment about the axis orthogonal to the plate through $G$.
