A rigid homogeneous rectangular plate of mass $m$ occupies the rectangle $[0, a] \times [0, b]$ of the plane $Oxy$. What is the product of inertia $I_{xy}$ relative to the pole $O$?

- $I_{xy} = 0$
- $I_{xy} = \dfrac{mab}{2}$
- $I_{xy} = \dfrac{mab}{12}$
- $I_{xy} = \dfrac{mab}{4}$

## Explanation

The surface density is $\sigma = \dfrac{m}{ab}$, therefore from the definition of product of inertia
$$
I_{xy} = \sigma \int_0^a \int_0^b xy\,dy\,dx = \frac{m}{ab} \cdot \frac{a^2}{2} \cdot \frac{b^2}{2} = \frac{mab}{4} .
$$
The same value is found again with the theorem on the transport of the products of inertia: in the central axes parallel to the sides it results $I_{xy}^{(G)} = 0$ by material symmetry, and since $G = \left( \dfrac{a}{2},\ \dfrac{b}{2} \right)$ one obtains $I_{xy}^{(O)} = m \cdot \dfrac{a}{2} \cdot \dfrac{b}{2} = \dfrac{mab}{4}$. The value zero holds for the central axes and not for the axes that start from the vertex, whereas $\dfrac{mab}{12}$ is the product of inertia of the triangular plate with the legs on the axes.
