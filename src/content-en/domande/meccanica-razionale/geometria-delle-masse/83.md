A homogeneous plate of mass $m$ occupies the ellipse
$$
\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} \le 1
$$
of the plane $Oxy$, with $a > b$. Which statement about the principal axes of inertia relative to the center $O$ is true?

- The $x$ and $y$ axes are not principal, since the ellipse has no rotational symmetry
- The $x$, $y$, $z$ axes are principal and the smallest moment is the one about the $x$ axis
- The $x$, $y$, $z$ axes are principal and the smallest moment is the one about the $y$ axis
- The $x$, $y$, $z$ axes are principal and the moments about $x$ and $y$ are equal

## Explanation

The $x$ and $y$ axes are axes of material symmetry of the plane plate, so by the **theorem on the principal axes from material symmetry** they are principal relative to $O$; the $z$ axis is principal because the plate lies in the plane $Oxy$. With the substitution $x = a\,u$, $y = b\,v$ the ellipse becomes the unit disk and $dm = \dfrac{m}{\pi}\,du\,dv$, therefore
$$
I_{xx} = \int y^2\,dm = \frac{mb^2}{4} ,
\qquad
I_{yy} = \int x^2\,dm = \frac{ma^2}{4} .
$$
Since $b < a$, one has $I_{xx} < I_{yy}$, hence the smallest moment is the one about the $x$ axis, which contains the major axis, because the mass is on average closer to that axis. The two moments would be equal only for $a = b$, that is for the disk.
