A door is modeled as a homogeneous rigid rectangular plate of mass $m$, height $a$ and width $b$, which rotates about the vertical axis of the hinges, coinciding with a vertical side, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the moment of inertia of the door about the axis of the hinges?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,4 0,4" tratteggi="0,-0.7 0,4.7" punti="0,0.6,,pieno;0,3.4,,pieno" quote="0 0,2 0,b,0.6;2 0,2 4,a,0.6" etichette="-0.6,4.5,axis" riferimento=0 titolo="Door of height $a$ and width $b$ with the axis of the hinges on the vertical side"}

- $\dfrac{mb^2}{12}$
- $\dfrac{ma^2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{mb^2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{m\,(a^2 + b^2)}{3}$

## Explanation

The door is divided into vertical strips of width $dx$: the strip at distance $x$ from the axis of the hinges has mass $\dfrac{m}{b}\,dx$ and all its points are at distance $x$ from the axis. Hence
$$
I = \int_0^b x^2\,\frac{m}{b}\,dx = \frac{mb^2}{3} ,
$$
and the height $a$ does not appear, because moving the mass parallel to the axis does not change its distance from the axis. The same value is obtained from the **Huygens-Steiner theorem**, adding to the moment $\dfrac{mb^2}{12}$ about the vertical axis through the center of mass the term $m\left( \dfrac{b}{2} \right)^2$. The value $\dfrac{mb^2}{12}$ is precisely the central moment, $\dfrac{ma^2}{3}$ exchanges the height with the width and $\dfrac{m\,(a^2 + b^2)}{3}$ is the moment about the axis orthogonal to the door through a vertex.
