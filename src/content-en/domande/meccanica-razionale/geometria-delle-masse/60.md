A material system is contained in the plane $Oxy$ and, relative to the pole $O$, has moment of inertia $I_{xx} = 0$. Which statement is true?

- All the mass lies on the $x$ axis, hence $I_{xy} = 0$ and $I_{yy} = I_{zz}$
- The system does not exist, since a moment of inertia is always positive
- Also $I_{yy} = 0$
- All the mass lies on the $y$ axis, hence $I_{yy} = 0$

## Explanation

By definition $I_{xx} = \displaystyle\int (y^2 + z^2)\,dm$, and the integrand is never negative. The integral is therefore zero only if $y = z = 0$ at every point carrying mass, that is if all the mass lies on the $x$ axis. In that case the product $xy$ is zero at every point, therefore $I_{xy} = 0$, and
$$
I_{yy} = \int (x^2 + z^2)\,dm = \int x^2\,dm = I_{zz} .
$$
A rod placed on the $x$ axis is an example: it has $I_{xx} = 0$ and $I_{yy} = I_{zz} > 0$. A moment of inertia is positive for a system not contained in the axis and zero for a system contained in the axis, while $I_{yy} = 0$ would require all the mass concentrated at $O$.
