- $x_G = y_G = \dfrac{(32 + \pi)\,\ell}{2\,(16 + \pi)} \approx 0.918\,\ell$
- $x_G = y_G = \ell$
- $x_G = y_G = \dfrac{(32 - \pi)\,\ell}{2\,(16 - \pi)} \approx 1.122\,\ell$
- $x_G = y_G = \dfrac{16\,\ell}{16 - \pi} \approx 1.244\,\ell$

## Explanation

The diagonal issuing from the origin contains the center of the square and the center of the hole, therefore it is an axis of material symmetry of the plate and hence $x_G = y_G$. The plate with the hole is treated as the difference between the solid square, of area $4\ell^2$ and center of mass at $(\ell, \ell)$, and the missing disk, of area $\dfrac{\pi \ell^2}{4}$ and center of mass at $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{2},\ \dfrac{\ell}{2} \right)$. Since the density is the same for the two bodies, the areas replace the masses in the formula for the center of mass of the composite system, therefore
$$
x_G = \frac{4\ell^2 \cdot \ell - \dfrac{\pi \ell^2}{4} \cdot \dfrac{\ell}{2}}{4\ell^2 - \dfrac{\pi \ell^2}{4}} = \frac{(32 - \pi)\,\ell}{2\,(16 - \pi)} \approx 1.122\,\ell .
$$
The center of mass moves to the side opposite the hole, hence beyond the center of the square. The value $\dfrac{16\,\ell}{16 - \pi}$ is obtained by removing the area of the hole from the denominator and forgetting its contribution to the numerator, whereas $\dfrac{(32 + \pi)\,\ell}{2\,(16 + \pi)}$ comes from adding the disk instead of subtracting it.
