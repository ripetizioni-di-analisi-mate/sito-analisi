A solid right circular cone, rigid and homogeneous, has mass $m$, base radius $R$ and height $h$. What is the moment of inertia about its axis of symmetry?

- $\dfrac{mR^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\,mR^2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{mR^2}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3\,mR^2}{10}$

## Explanation

With the base in the plane $z = 0$ and the vertex at $z = h$, the cone is divided into thin disks: the disk at height $z$ has radius $r(z) = R\left(1 - \dfrac{z}{h}\right)$ and mass $dm = \rho\,\pi\,r(z)^2\,dz$. The moment of each disk about the axis equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,dm\,r(z)^2$, hence
$$
I = \frac{\rho\pi R^4}{2} \int_0^h \left(1 - \frac{z}{h}\right)^4 dz = \frac{\rho\pi R^4 h}{10} .
$$
Since $m = \dfrac{\rho\pi R^2 h}{3}$, one has $\rho\pi = \dfrac{3m}{R^2 h}$ and therefore $I = \dfrac{3\,mR^2}{10}$. The value $\dfrac{mR^2}{2}$ is that of the cylinder, $\dfrac{3\,mR^2}{5}$ assigns to each slice the moment $dm\,r(z)^2$ of a ring instead of that of a disk, and $\dfrac{mR^2}{8}$ treats the cone as a disk with radius equal to the mean radius $\dfrac{R}{2}$.
