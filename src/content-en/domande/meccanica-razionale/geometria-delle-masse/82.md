A homogeneous rigid wire is bent to form the boundary of a half disk: the semicircle with center $O$ and radius $R$ contained in the half-plane $y \ge 0$ and the diameter joining its ends on the $x$ axis, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the ordinate of the center of mass of the wire?

- $y_G = \dfrac{2R}{\pi}$
- $y_G = \dfrac{4R}{3\pi}$
- $y_G = \dfrac{R}{\pi}$
- $y_G = \dfrac{2R}{\pi + 2}$

## Explanation

The $y$ axis is an axis of material symmetry, so $x_G = 0$. The wire is homogeneous, therefore the masses of the two pieces are proportional to the lengths, $\pi R$ for the semicircle and $2R$ for the diameter. The center of mass of the semicircle has ordinate $\dfrac{2R}{\pi}$, value of the arc of half-angle $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, while that of the diameter is $O$. By the **theorem on the center of mass of a composite system**
$$
y_G = \frac{\pi R \cdot \dfrac{2R}{\pi} + 2R \cdot 0}{\pi R + 2R} = \frac{2R}{\pi + 2} \approx 0.39\,R .
$$
The value $\dfrac{2R}{\pi}$ is the center of mass of the semicircle alone, $\dfrac{4R}{3\pi}$ is that of the semicircular plate and $\dfrac{R}{\pi}$ gives the two pieces equal masses.
