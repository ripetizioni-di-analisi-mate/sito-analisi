- $x_G = 0$ and $y_G = \dfrac{2R}{3\pi + 12} \approx 0.093\,R$
- $x_G = 0$ and $y_G = -\dfrac{R}{2}$

## Explanation

The $y$ axis is an axis of material symmetry of the plate, therefore $x_G = 0$ and the ordinate remains to be computed. The rectangle has area $2R^2$ and center of mass at $y_1 = -\dfrac{R}{2}$, whereas the half-disk has area $\dfrac{\pi R^2}{2}$ and center of mass at $y_2 = \dfrac{4R}{3\pi}$, as follows from the known value for the circular sector of half-angle $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The plate is homogeneous, hence the areas replace the masses in the formula for the center of mass of the composite system and
$$
y_G = \frac{2R^2 \cdot \left( -\dfrac{R}{2} \right) + \dfrac{\pi R^2}{2} \cdot \dfrac{4R}{3\pi}}{2R^2 + \dfrac{\pi R^2}{2}} = \frac{-R^3 + \dfrac{2R^3}{3}}{R^2\,\dfrac{4 + \pi}{2}} = -\frac{2R}{3\pi + 12} \approx -0.093\,R .
$$
The negative sign says that the center of mass lies slightly below the diameter, since the rectangle has a larger area than the half-disk. The value $-\dfrac{R}{2}$ is the center of mass of the rectangle alone, and $y_G = 0$ would be obtained if the two parts had centers of mass symmetric with respect to the diameter.
