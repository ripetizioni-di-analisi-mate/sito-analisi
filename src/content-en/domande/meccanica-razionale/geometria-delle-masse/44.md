A homogeneous plate has mass $m$ and the shape of the annulus with center $O$, inner radius $\dfrac{R}{2}$ and outer radius $R$, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the plane of the plate through $O$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2.4;0,0,1.2,foro" punti="0,0,O" quote="0 0,2.4 0,R,-2.7;0 0,0 1.2,0.5 R,0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Annulus of outer radius $R$ and inner radius $\dfrac{R}{2}$"}

- $\dfrac{mR^2}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\,mR^2}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2\,mR^2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5\,mR^2}{8}$

## Explanation

The annulus is treated as the difference between the solid disk of radius $R$ and the missing disk of radius $\dfrac{R}{2}$, both with the density of the given plate. The area of the annulus is $\pi R^2 - \dfrac{\pi R^2}{4} = \dfrac{3\pi R^2}{4}$, therefore $\sigma = \dfrac{4m}{3\pi R^2}$ and the masses of the two fictitious disks equal
$$
m_1 = \sigma\,\pi R^2 = \frac{4m}{3} ,
\qquad
m_2 = \sigma\,\pi\,\frac{R^2}{4} = \frac{m}{3} .
$$
The two disks have the same center $O$, hence no transport is necessary and the moment of inertia is the difference of the two contributions:
$$
I_O = \frac{1}{2}\,m_1 R^2 - \frac{1}{2}\,m_2\,\frac{R^2}{4} = \frac{2\,mR^2}{3} - \frac{mR^2}{24} = \frac{5\,mR^2}{8} .
$$
The same value is obtained from the formula $I_O = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\left( R^2 + \dfrac{R^2}{4} \right)$ of the annulus. The value $\dfrac{2\,mR^2}{3}$ is the contribution of the solid disk alone, that is it stops before removing the hole.
