The material point $P$ of mass $m$ slides on a smooth fixed straight guide, inclined at $30°$ to the horizontal. The reaction of the guide has magnitude $\Phi$ and is orthogonal to the guide. What is the virtual work of the reaction in the virtual displacement $\delta s$ of $P$ along the guide?

- $\Phi\,\delta s$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\Phi\,\delta s$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\Phi\,\delta s$
- $0$

## Explanation

The constraint is fixed and bilateral, therefore the virtual displacements of $P$ are all tangent to the guide and have the direction of the guide itself. The guide is smooth, hence the reaction $\vec{\Phi}$ is orthogonal to the guide and the scalar product $\vec{\Phi} \cdot \delta P$ is zero for every virtual displacement. The virtual work of the reaction is therefore $0$, whatever the magnitude $\Phi$ and the inclination of the guide may be. This is precisely the condition that makes the constraint ideal and that makes it possible to eliminate the reaction from the symbolic relation of dynamics.
