The ring $P$ of mass $m$ slides without friction on a straight rod which rotates in a vertical plane about the fixed point $O$, with the law $\varphi = \omega\,t$ and $\omega$ constant, as in the diagram in the figure. Taking the distance $r$ of $P$ from $O$ as Lagrangian coordinate, what is the equation of motion?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,3.03,1.75" aste="O-A" cerchi="0,0,0.1" tratteggi="0,0 3.6,0" punti="1.732,1.0,P" forze="1.732 1,0,-0.7,mg" etichette="1.0,0.2,φ = ωt;2.1,0.82,m" quote="O,1.732 1,r,0.62" titolo="Rod rotating in the vertical plane with the ring $P$ sliding on it"}

- $m\,\ddot{r} = -m\,\omega^2\,r - m\,g\sin(\omega\,t)$
- $m\,\ddot{r} = -m\,g\sin(\omega\,t)$
- $m\,\ddot{r} = m\,\omega^2\,r - m\,g\sin(\omega\,t)$
- $m\,\ddot{r} = m\,\omega^2\,r - m\,g\cos(\omega\,t)$

## Explanation

The position of the ring is $P - O = r\cos(\omega\,t)\,\hat{\imath} + r\sin(\omega\,t)\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the velocity has the component $\dot{r}$ along the rod and the component $\omega\,r$ orthogonal to it and the kinetic energy is
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{r}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\omega^2\,r^2 .
$$
The constraint is moving, hence the term $T_0 = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\omega^2\,r^2$ depends on $r$ and the derivative $\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial r} = m\,\omega^2\,r$ appears in the equation. The potential of the weight is $U = -m\,g\,r\sin(\omega\,t)$, hence $Q_r = -m\,g\sin(\omega\,t)$. Lagrange's equation then gives $m\,\ddot{r} - m\,\omega^2\,r = -m\,g\sin(\omega\,t)$, where the term $m\,\omega^2\,r$ is interpreted in the frame moving with the rod as a centrifugal force.
