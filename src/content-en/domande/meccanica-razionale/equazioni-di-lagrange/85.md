Two gear wheels mesh with each other and rotate about two fixed parallel axes: the first has pitch radius $R_1$ and moment of inertia $I_1$ about its own axis, the second has pitch radius $R_2$ and moment of inertia $I_2$ about its own axis. The pitch circles roll on each other without slipping. With the rotation angle $\vartheta_1$ of the first wheel as Lagrangian coordinate, what is the kinetic energy of the system?

- $\dfrac{1}{2} \left( I_1 + I_2\,\dfrac{R_1^2}{R_2^2} \right) \dot{\vartheta}_1^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} \left( I_1 + I_2\,\dfrac{R_2^2}{R_1^2} \right) \dot{\vartheta}_1^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\big( I_1 + I_2 \big)\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} \left( I_1 + I_2\,\dfrac{R_1}{R_2} \right) \dot{\vartheta}_1^2$

## Explanation

At the contact point of the pitch circles the two wheels have the same velocity, therefore $R_1\,|\dot{\vartheta}_1| = R_2\,|\dot{\vartheta}_2|$ and the two rotations have opposite senses. The constraint integrates into the relation $R_1\,\vartheta_1 + R_2\,\vartheta_2 = \text{constant}$, hence it is holonomic and the system has a single degree of freedom, with $|\dot{\vartheta}_2| = \dfrac{R_1}{R_2}\,|\dot{\vartheta}_1|$. Each wheel rotates about a fixed axis, so
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,I_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,I_2\,\frac{R_1^2}{R_2^2}\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 = \frac{1}{2} \left( I_1 + I_2\,\frac{R_1^2}{R_2^2} \right) \dot{\vartheta}_1^2 .
$$
The second wheel contributes with the reduced moment of inertia $I_2\,\dfrac{R_1^2}{R_2^2}$, which contains the square of the ratio of the radii.
