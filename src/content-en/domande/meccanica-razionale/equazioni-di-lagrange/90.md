The homogeneous disk of center $C$, mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal conveyor belt, which moves with assigned velocity $u(t)\,\hat{\imath}$, as in the diagram in the figure. No horizontal active forces act on the disk. If the belt accelerates with constant and positive $\dot{u} = a_0$, what is the acceleration of $C$?

::struttura{poligoni="-0.5,-0.35 6,-0.35 6,0 -0.5,0" cerchi="-0.5,-0.175,0.175;6,-0.175,0.175;2.8,0.9,0.9" punti="2.8,0.9,C,baricentro" frecce="1,-0.75,2.6,-0.75,u(t)" etichette="3.95,1.5,m" titolo="Disk rolling without slipping on a moving conveyor belt"}

- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,a_0$, in the direction of motion of the belt
- $a_0$, in the direction of motion of the belt
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,a_0$, opposite to the motion of the belt
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,a_0$, in the direction of motion of the belt

## Explanation

The abscissa $x$ of $C$ in a fixed frame is taken as Lagrangian coordinate and $\omega$ denotes the component of the angular velocity of the disk along $\hat{k}$. The point of the disk in contact with the belt has velocity $(\dot{x} + \omega\,R)\,\hat{\imath}$, and pure rolling requires it to equal $u\,\hat{\imath}$, therefore $\omega = \dfrac{u - \dot{x}}{R}$; the constraint is holonomic and moving. By König's theorem
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{4}\,m\,\big( u - \dot{x} \big)^2 ,
$$
and the weight and the reaction of the belt do no virtual work. Lagrange's equation is
$$
\frac{d}{dt} \left[ m\,\dot{x} - \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( u - \dot{x} \big) \right] = 0 ,
\qquad \text{that is} \qquad
\frac{3}{2}\,m\,\ddot{x} = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{u} ,
$$
hence $\ddot{x} = \dfrac{1}{3}\,a_0$. The acceleration is positive, therefore the center accelerates in the direction of motion of the belt, but with one third of the acceleration of the belt.
