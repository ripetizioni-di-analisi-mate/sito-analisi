The material point $P$ of mass $m$ moves in the smooth horizontal plane subject to the elastic force alone of a spring of constant $k$ and zero rest length, which connects it to the fixed point $O$. The polar coordinates $r$ and $\varphi$ with center $O$ are taken as Lagrangian coordinates. What is the kinetic momentum conjugate to the cyclic coordinate?

- $m\,\dot{\varphi}$
- $m\,r\,\dot{\varphi}$
- $m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}$
- $m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy in polar coordinates is $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{r}^2 + r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 \big)$ and the potential of the spring is $U = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,r^2$, therefore the Lagrangian depends on $r$, $\dot{r}$ and $\dot{\varphi}$ but not on $\varphi$. The coordinate $\varphi$ is hence cyclic and the conjugate kinetic momentum
$$
p_\varphi = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \dot{\varphi}} = m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}
$$
remains constant along every motion. This constant is the angular momentum of the point with respect to $O$, and its conservation expresses the fact that the elastic force is central, hence it has zero moment with respect to $O$.
