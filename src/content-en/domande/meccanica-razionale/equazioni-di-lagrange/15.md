Which of the constraints listed is not ideal?

- The smooth fixed hinge that connects the endpoint of a rigid rod to a fixed point
- The contact between a disk that slips on a fixed rough horizontal guide
- The pure rolling of a disk on a fixed horizontal guide
- The inextensible string of negligible mass that connects two material points

## Explanation

A constraint is ideal when the reactions that it exerts do zero virtual work in every virtual displacement of the configuration occupied. The smooth fixed hinge applies the reaction at a point that does not move, therefore its virtual work is zero; the inextensible string applies opposite reactions at the two endpoints of a stretch of constant length, and the sum of the two contributions vanishes; in the pure rolling the reaction is applied at the contact point, which in the act of motion has zero velocity. When instead the disk slips on a rough guide the contact point has velocity different from zero and the kinetic friction has component opposite to that velocity, hence its virtual work is negative and the constraint is not ideal. In that case friction is treated as an active force and its Lagrangian component is computed from the virtual work.
