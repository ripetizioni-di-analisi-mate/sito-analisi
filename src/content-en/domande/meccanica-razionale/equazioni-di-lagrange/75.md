The ring $P$ of mass $m$ slides without friction on a guide shaped as the parabola of equation $z = \dfrac{x^2}{2\,a}$, with $a > 0$, lying in a vertical plane with the $z$ axis pointing vertically upward. The guide rotates with constant angular velocity $\omega$, imposed from outside, about the $z$ axis, and the Lagrangian coordinate is the abscissa $x$ of $P$ in the plane of the guide. For which value of $\omega$ is every position of the ring on the guide a position of relative equilibrium?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{a}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g}{a}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{2\,a}}$
- $2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{a}}$

## Explanation

In the plane of the guide the velocity of $P$ has the components $\dot{x}$ and $\dot{z} = \dfrac{x\,\dot{x}}{a}$, while the rotation adds the component $\omega\,x$ orthogonal to the plane, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 \left( 1 + \frac{x^2}{a^2} \right) + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\omega^2\,x^2 ,
\qquad
U = -m\,g\,\frac{x^2}{2\,a} .
$$
Lagrange's equation is
$$
m \left( 1 + \frac{x^2}{a^2} \right) \ddot{x} + \frac{m\,x\,\dot{x}^2}{a^2} - m\,\omega^2\,x + \frac{m\,g\,x}{a} = 0 .
$$
A position $x_0$ is of relative equilibrium when $x = x_0$ with $\dot{x} = 0$ and $\ddot{x} = 0$ satisfies the equation, that is when $x_0 \left( \dfrac{g}{a} - \omega^2 \right) = 0$. For $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{g}{a}}$ this condition is satisfied by every $x_0$, because the centrifugal force balances at every point the component of the weight along the guide; for the other values of $\omega$ the only position of relative equilibrium is $x_0 = 0$.
