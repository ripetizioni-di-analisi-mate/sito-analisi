The double pendulum in the figure consists of two rigid rods of negligible mass and length $\ell$: the first one is hinged at the fixed point $O$ and carries at the endpoint $A$ a material point of mass $m$, the second one is hinged at $A$ and carries at the endpoint $B$ a material point of mass $m$. The hinges are smooth, the system moves in the vertical plane and the Lagrangian coordinates are the angles $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ that the two rods form with the downward vertical. What is the kinetic energy of the system?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0.803,-1.722;B,2.359,-2.812" catene="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" cerniere="A" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.0;0.803,-1.722 0.803,-3.2" etichette="0.17,-1.02,ϑ_1;1.02,-2.45,ϑ_2;0.28,-2.05,m;2.62,-2.9,m" titolo="Double pendulum with two material points of equal mass"}

- $m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2 + m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2$
- $m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2$
- $m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2 + m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\cos(\vartheta_1 + \vartheta_2)$

## Explanation

The positions of the two material points are
$$
A - O = \ell\sin\vartheta_1\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\vartheta_1\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
B - O = (\ell\sin\vartheta_1 + \ell\sin\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\imath} - (\ell\cos\vartheta_1 + \ell\cos\vartheta_2)\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
Differentiating gives $v_A^2 = \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2$ and, expanding the squares and collecting with the subtraction formula for the cosine,
$$
v_B^2 = \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2 + 2\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2) .
$$
Since the rods have negligible mass, the kinetic energy is the sum of the two terms $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_A^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_B^2$, therefore it is $m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2 + m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}_1\,\dot{\vartheta}_2\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)$. The mixed term depends on the configuration and is what couples the two Lagrange equations to each other.
