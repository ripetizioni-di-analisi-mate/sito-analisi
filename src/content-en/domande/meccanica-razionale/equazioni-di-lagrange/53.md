The homogeneous square plate of mass $m$ and side $a$ is hinged at the vertex $O$ with a smooth fixed hinge and moves in its own plane, which is vertical, as in the diagram in the figure. The angle $\vartheta$ between the diagonal $OG$ and the downward vertical is taken as Lagrangian coordinate. What is the equation of motion?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0" poligoni="0,0 1.813,-0.845 0.968,-2.658 -0.845,-1.813" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-3;0,0 0.968,-2.658" punti="0.484,-1.329,G,baricentro" etichette="0.14,-0.85,ϑ;1.75,-1.9,m" quote="O,1.813 -0.845,a,0.5" titolo="Square plate hinged at a vertex, in the vertical plane"}

- $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}\,g}{a}\sin\vartheta$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}\,g}{4\,a}\sin\vartheta$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}\,g}{2\,a}\sin\vartheta$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{3\,g}{4\,a}\sin\vartheta$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia of the plate about the axis through $G$ orthogonal to the plane is $I_G = \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,\big( a^2 + a^2 \big) = \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,a^2$, and the distance $OG$ is half the diagonal, that is $\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$. By the Huygens-Steiner theorem $I_O = \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,a^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,a^2 = \dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,a^2$, and the act of motion is rotational about the axis through $O$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{3}\,m\,a^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 ,
\qquad
U = m\,g\,\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}\cos\vartheta .
$$
Lagrange's equation gives $\dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,a^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,m\,g\,a\sin\vartheta$, hence $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}\,g}{4\,a}\sin\vartheta$.
