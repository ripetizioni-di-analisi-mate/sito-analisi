The material point $P$ of mass $m$ is constrained to the smooth circle of center $O$ and radius $R$, which rotates with constant angular velocity $\omega$, imposed from outside, about its own vertical diameter. With $\vartheta$ the angle between $OP$ and the downward vertical, what is the Jacobi function?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2\sin^2\vartheta - m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2\sin^2\vartheta - m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2\sin^2\vartheta + m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2\cos^2\vartheta - m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$

## Explanation

The point is at distance $R\sin\vartheta$ from the axis of rotation, therefore its velocity has the component $R\,\dot{\vartheta}$ in the plane of the circle and the component $\omega\,R\sin\vartheta$ orthogonal to that plane, and the kinetic energy splits into
$$
T_2 = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 ,
\qquad
T_0 = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2\sin^2\vartheta .
$$
The height of $P$ is $-R\cos\vartheta$, hence the potential energy is $V = -m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$. The constraint is moving, therefore the expression $\mathcal{H} = T_2 - T_0 + V$ is used, which here gives $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2\sin^2\vartheta - m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$. The Lagrangian does not depend explicitly on time, since $\omega$ is constant, hence this function is a first integral, whereas the mechanical energy $T + V$ is not conserved.
