- $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{g}{2\,\ell}\,\big( \sin\vartheta + \cos\vartheta \big)$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{g}{2\,\ell}\,\big( \sin\vartheta + 2\cos\vartheta \big)$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{g}{2\,\ell}\,\big( 2\sin\vartheta + \cos\vartheta \big)$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{2\,g}{\ell}\,\big( \sin\vartheta + 2\cos\vartheta \big)$

## Explanation

The two rods are welded, hence the bracket is a single rigid body with one degree of freedom and its moment of inertia with respect to the axis through $O$ is $I_O = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 + \dfrac{1}{3}\,(2m)\,\ell^2 = m\,\ell^2$, therefore $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$. Since $OB$ is obtained from $OA$ by a rotation of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ in the counterclockwise sense, the two centers of mass are at the heights $y_{G_1} = -\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$ and $y_{G_2} = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta$, hence the potential of the weight is
$$
U = -m\,g\,y_{G_1} - 2\,m\,g\,y_{G_2} = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta - m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta .
$$
Differentiating with respect to $\vartheta$ gives $Q_\vartheta = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta - m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta$, therefore Lagrange's equation is $m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\,\big( \sin\vartheta + 2\cos\vartheta \big)$, that is $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{g}{2\,\ell}\,\big( \sin\vartheta + 2\cos\vartheta \big)$.
