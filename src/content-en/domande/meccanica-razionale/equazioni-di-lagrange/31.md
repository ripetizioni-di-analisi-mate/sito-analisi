The homogeneous rod $OA$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the smooth fixed point $O$ and performs small oscillations in the vertical plane about the configuration with the rod vertical and downward. What is the angular frequency of the small oscillations?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g}{3\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$

## Explanation

With $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the downward vertical one has $I_O = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$, hence $T = \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ and $U = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta$, therefore Lagrange's equation is
$$
\frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -\frac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta .
$$
For small values of $\vartheta$ one replaces $\sin\vartheta$ with $\vartheta$, since the Taylor expansion of the sine at $0$ has $\vartheta$ as its first-degree term, and $\ddot{\vartheta} + \dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}\,\vartheta = 0$ is obtained. The equation is that of the harmonic oscillator, hence the angular frequency is $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}}$, which is the angular frequency of a simple pendulum of length $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell$.
