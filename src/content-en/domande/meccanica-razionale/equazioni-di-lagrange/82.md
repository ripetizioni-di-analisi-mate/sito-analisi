A door is a homogeneous rectangular plate of mass $m$ and width $b$, hinged along a vertical side to a smooth fixed axis. The door closer is modeled as a torsional spring, which exerts on the door a torque of moment $-\kappa\,\vartheta$ about the axis, with $\kappa > 0$ and $\vartheta$ the opening angle measured from the closed position. What is the period of the oscillations of the door, in the absence of friction?

- $2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{m\,b^2}{12\,\kappa}}$
- $2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{m\,b^2}{\kappa}}$
- $2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{m\,b^2}{2\,\kappa}}$
- $2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{m\,b^2}{3\,\kappa}}$

## Explanation

The door splits into strips parallel to the axis, whose distances from the axis range from $0$ to $b$, as for a rod of length $b$ rotating about an endpoint; therefore the moment of inertia about the axis of the hinge is $I = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,b^2$ and $T = \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,b^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$. The center of mass stays at the same height, hence the weight does not contribute, while the elastic torque has potential $U = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\kappa\,\vartheta^2$. Lagrange's equation is
$$
\frac{1}{3}\,m\,b^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -\kappa\,\vartheta ,
$$
which describes a harmonic motion of angular frequency $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,\kappa}{m\,b^2}}$, therefore the period is $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{m\,b^2}{3\,\kappa}}$. The value with $12\,\kappa$ corresponds to the moment of inertia about the vertical axis through the center of the door.
