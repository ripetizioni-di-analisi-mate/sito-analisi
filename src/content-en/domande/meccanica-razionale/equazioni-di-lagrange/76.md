The material point $P$ of mass $m$ is constrained to the surface of a smooth fixed circular cylinder of radius $R$ with vertical axis; the constraint is bilateral and the angle $\varphi$ about the axis and the height $z$ are taken as Lagrangian coordinates, with the $z$ axis pointing vertically upward. Besides the reaction, only the weight acts on the point. Which statement about the motion is true?

- $\dot{\varphi}$ increases while the point descends
- $\dot{\varphi}$ is constant and the height $z$ is constant
- $\dot{\varphi}$ is constant and $\ddot{z} = -g$
- $\ddot{z} = -g$ and $\dot{\varphi}$ decreases while the point descends

## Explanation

The velocity of the point has the horizontal component $R\,\dot{\varphi}$ tangent to the cross-section of the cylinder and the vertical component $\dot{z}$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 + \dot{z}^2 \big) ,
\qquad
U = -m\,g\,z .
$$
The Lagrangian does not depend on $\varphi$, hence $\varphi$ is cyclic and the kinetic momentum $p_\varphi = m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}$ is constant; since $R$ is constant, $\dot{\varphi}$ is constant too. Lagrange's equation for $z$ gives $m\,\ddot{z} = -m\,g$. The point therefore rotates about the axis with constant angular velocity, while its height has uniformly accelerated motion with acceleration $g$ downward.
