The material point $P$ of mass $m$ slides on a smooth fixed horizontal straight guide and is connected by a spring of elastic constant $k$ and rest length $\ell_0$ to the fixed point $O$ of the guide. With $x > 0$ the abscissa of $P$ measured from $O$, what is the Lagrangian component of the elastic force?

- $-k\,x$
- $k\,(x - \ell_0)$
- $-k\,(x - \ell_0)$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(x - \ell_0)$

## Explanation

The spring has length $x$ in the configuration identified by the Lagrangian coordinate, therefore its potential is $U = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(x - \ell_0)^2$, with the convention $\vec{F} = \operatorname{grad} U$ used throughout the subject. The load is conservative, hence the Lagrangian component is obtained by differentiating the potential with respect to the coordinate:
$$
Q_x = \frac{\partial U}{\partial x} = -k\,(x - \ell_0) .
$$
The sign is negative for $x > \ell_0$, since the stretched spring pulls $P$ back towards $O$, and positive for $x < \ell_0$, since the compressed spring pushes $P$ away from $O$. The component vanishes for $x = \ell_0$, which is the equilibrium configuration of the point on the horizontal guide.
