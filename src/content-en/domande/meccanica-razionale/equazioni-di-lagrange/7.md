The material point $A$ of mass $m$ slides on a smooth fixed horizontal guide; a rigid rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$ is hinged to it, which carries at its other endpoint the material point $P$ of mass $m$, as in the diagram in the figure. The system moves in the vertical plane and the abscissa $x$ of $A$ and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical are taken as Lagrangian coordinates. Which function remains constant along the motion?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0,-;A,2.4,0;P,3.662,-1.802" catene="A-P" vincoli="A,carrello,90" linee="-0.6,0 6,0" tratteggi="2.4,0 2.4,-2.3" etichette="2.68,-1.3,ϑ;1.75,0.45,m;4.05,-2.2,m" quote="0 0,A,x,0.75" titolo="Slider on a horizontal guide with the pendulum hinged at $A$"}

- $2\,m\,\dot{x}$
- $m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta$
- $2\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta$
- $2\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}$

## Explanation

The position of the two points is $A - O = x\,\hat{\imath}$ and $P - O = (x + \ell\sin\vartheta)\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the kinetic energy is
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{x}^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + 2\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta \big)
= m\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta .
$$
The guide is horizontal and the heights of the two points do not depend on $x$, hence the potential of the weight does not depend on $x$ and the coordinate $x$ is cyclic. By the theorem on the conservation of the kinetic momentum the conjugate momentum $p_x = \dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{x}}$ is constant along every motion, and from the expression of $T$ one obtains $p_x = 2\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta$. This function is the horizontal component of the linear momentum of the system.
