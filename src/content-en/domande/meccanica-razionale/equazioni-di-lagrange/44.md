In the vertical plane the material points $A$ and $B$, both of mass $m$, are connected by an inextensible string of negligible mass which passes over an ideal fixed pulley of negligible mass: $A$ hangs along the vertical and $B$ rests on a smooth fixed inclined plane of angle $\alpha$, as in the diagram in the figure. The string remains taut during the motion. What is the acceleration with which $A$ descends?

::struttura{nodi="A,-0.25,0.9;B,1.398,1.464;Q,-0.25,2.75,-" poligoni="0,0 3.2,0 0,2.6" catene="B-Q;Q-A" cerchi="-0.25,2.75,0.22" etichette="2.6,0.22,α;-0.78,0.95,m;1.72,1.4,m" titolo="Two material points connected by the string passing over the fixed pulley"}

- $g\,\big( 1 - \sin\alpha \big)$
- $\dfrac{g\,\big( 1 - \cos\alpha \big)}{2}$
- $\dfrac{g\,\big( 1 + \sin\alpha \big)}{2}$
- $\dfrac{g\,\big( 1 - \sin\alpha \big)}{2}$

## Explanation

The string is inextensible, therefore the displacement of $A$ downwards is equal to the displacement of $B$ along the plane upwards and the system has a single degree of freedom; that displacement $s$ is taken as Lagrangian coordinate. The two velocities have the same magnitude $|\dot{s}|$, hence
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{s}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{s}^2 = m\,\dot{s}^2 .
$$
The point $A$ descends by the height $s$ and the point $B$ rises by the height $s\sin\alpha$, therefore the potential of the weight is $U = m\,g\,s - m\,g\,s\sin\alpha$ and $Q_s = m\,g\,\big( 1 - \sin\alpha \big)$. Lagrange's equation gives $2\,m\,\ddot{s} = m\,g\,\big( 1 - \sin\alpha \big)$, that is $\ddot{s} = \dfrac{g\,\big( 1 - \sin\alpha \big)}{2}$, positive for $0 < \alpha < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, therefore $A$ does descend.
