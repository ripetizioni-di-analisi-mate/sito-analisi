The material point $P$ of mass $m$ is constrained to the smooth fixed guide of equation $y = \dfrac{x^2}{2\,a}$ in the vertical plane $Oxy$, with $a > 0$ and the $y$ axis vertical and upward. The abscissa $x$ of $P$ is taken as Lagrangian coordinate. What is the equation of motion?

- $m\,\ddot{x}\left( 1 + \dfrac{x^2}{a^2} \right) + \dfrac{m\,x\,\dot{x}^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{m\,g\,x}{a} = 0$
- $m\,\ddot{x}\left( 1 + \dfrac{x^2}{a^2} \right) + \dfrac{2\,m\,x\,\dot{x}^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{m\,g\,x}{a} = 0$
- $m\,\ddot{x}\left( 1 + \dfrac{x^2}{a^2} \right) + \dfrac{m\,g\,x}{a} = 0$
- $m\,\ddot{x} + \dfrac{m\,g\,x}{a} = 0$

## Explanation

The position of the point is $P - O = x\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{x^2}{2\,a}\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore $\vec{v}_P = \dot{x}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{x\,\dot{x}}{a}\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the kinetic energy is $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2\left( 1 + \dfrac{x^2}{a^2} \right)$. The potential of the weight is $U = -m\,g\,\dfrac{x^2}{2\,a}$, hence $Q_x = -\dfrac{m\,g\,x}{a}$. The two derivatives that appear in the equation are
$$
\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{x}} = m\,\ddot{x}\left( 1 + \frac{x^2}{a^2} \right) + \frac{2\,m\,x\,\dot{x}^2}{a^2} ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial T}{\partial x} = \frac{m\,x\,\dot{x}^2}{a^2} ,
$$
and their difference leaves a single term in $\dot{x}^2$. Lagrange's equation is therefore $m\,\ddot{x}\left( 1 + \dfrac{x^2}{a^2} \right) + \dfrac{m\,x\,\dot{x}^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{m\,g\,x}{a} = 0$.
