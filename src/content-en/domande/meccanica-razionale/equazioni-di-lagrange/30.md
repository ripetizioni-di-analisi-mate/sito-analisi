The homogeneous disk of center $C$, mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged by a smooth fixed hinge at the point $A$ of its rim and rotates in the vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. The angle $\vartheta$ between the radius $AC$ and the downward vertical is taken as Lagrangian coordinate. What is the Lagrangian of the system?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0,-" cerchi="0.75,-1.299,1.5" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.9;0,0 0.75,-1.299" punti="0,0,,pieno;0.75,-1.299,C,baricentro" etichette="0.26,-1.02,ϑ;2.35,-0.4,m;-0.34,0.1,A" titolo="Homogeneous disk hinged at the point $A$ of its rim"}

- $\mathcal{L} = \dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$
- $\mathcal{L} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$
- $\mathcal{L} = \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 - m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$
- $\mathcal{L} = \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$

## Explanation

The act of motion is a rotation about the fixed axis through $A$, therefore $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_A\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$. The moment of inertia of the disk with respect to the axis through the center is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$ and the Huygens-Steiner theorem, applied with the distance $R$ between the two axes, gives
$$
I_A = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2 ,
$$
hence $T = \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$. The center of mass coincides with $C$ and is at height $y_C = -R\cos\vartheta$, hence the potential of the weight is $U = m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$ and the Lagrangian $\mathcal{L} = T + U$ turns out to be $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$.
