The material point $P$ of mass $m$ moves on a smooth horizontal table and is tied to an inextensible string of negligible mass that passes through a small hole $O$ in the table and supports, on the other side, the material point $Q$ of mass $M$, which moves along the vertical through $O$, as in the diagram in the figure. The string stays taut. The polar coordinates $r$ and $\varphi$ of $P$ on the table, centered at $O$, are taken as Lagrangian coordinates, and $p_\varphi$ denotes the constant value of the kinetic momentum conjugate to $\varphi$. Which equation does $r$ satisfy?

::struttura{nodi="P,-2.2,0;O,0,0,-;Q,0,-1.8" linee="-3.2,0 -0.12,0;0.12,0 1.4,0" catene="P-O;O-Q" punti="-2.2,0,,pieno;0,-1.8,,pieno" etichette="-2.2,0.35,m;0.35,-1.85,M;0,0.35,O" quote="P,0 0,r,0.45" titolo="Point on the table tied to the hanging point $Q$ by a string that passes through the hole $O$ (side view)"}

- $m\,\ddot{r} = \dfrac{p_\varphi^2}{m\,r^3} - M\,g$
- $(m + M)\,\ddot{r} = \dfrac{p_\varphi^2}{m\,r^3} - M\,g$
- $(m + M)\,\ddot{r} = \dfrac{p_\varphi^2}{m\,r^3} + M\,g$
- $(m + M)\,\ddot{r} = \dfrac{p_\varphi^2}{m\,r^2} - M\,g$

## Explanation

The string is inextensible, therefore when $r$ increases $Q$ rises by the same amount and its speed is $|\dot{r}|$; it follows that
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,(m + M)\,\dot{r}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 ,
\qquad
U = -M\,g\,r ,
$$
up to an additive constant. The coordinate $\varphi$ is cyclic, hence $p_\varphi = m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}$ is constant and $\dot{\varphi} = \dfrac{p_\varphi}{m\,r^2}$. Lagrange's equation for $r$ is $(m + M)\,\ddot{r} - m\,r\,\dot{\varphi}^2 = -M\,g$, and substituting $\dot{\varphi}$ gives
$$
(m + M)\,\ddot{r} = \frac{p_\varphi^2}{m\,r^3} - M\,g .
$$
The equation contains $r$ alone, and the circular orbit of radius $r_0$ corresponds to $\dfrac{p_\varphi^2}{m\,r_0^3} = M\,g$.
