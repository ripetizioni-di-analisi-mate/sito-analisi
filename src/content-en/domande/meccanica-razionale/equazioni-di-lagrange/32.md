The homogeneous rod $OA$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the smooth fixed point $O$, carries at the endpoint $A$ a welded material point of mass $m$ and rotates in the vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the reduced length of the compound pendulum thus obtained?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.2,-2.078" aste="O-A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.4" punti="0.6,-1.039,G,baricentro" etichette="0.3,-1.1,ϑ;1.5,-2.2,m" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.65" titolo="Rod hinged at $O$ with the material point of mass $m$ welded at $A$"}

- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{9}{8}\,\ell$

## Explanation

The overall moment of inertia with respect to the axis through $O$ is $I_O = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 + m\,\ell^2 = \dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$, the total mass is $2\,m$ and the distance of the center of mass from $O$ is
$$
d = \frac{m\,\dfrac{\ell}{2} + m\,\ell}{2\,m} = \frac{3}{4}\,\ell .
$$
The equation of motion is $I_O\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -2\,m\,g\,d\sin\vartheta$, therefore $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{2\,m\,g\,d}{I_O}\sin\vartheta$. The comparison with the equation of the simple pendulum $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{g}{L}\sin\vartheta$ gives the reduced length
$$
L = \frac{I_O}{2\,m\,d} = \frac{\dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2}{\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,\ell} = \frac{8}{9}\,\ell .
$$
