In the vertical plane the homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $R$, has its endpoints constrained to a smooth fixed circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, as in the diagram in the figure. The angle $\vartheta$ between the segment $OG$, with $G$ the midpoint of the rod, and the downward vertical is taken as Lagrangian coordinate. What is the kinetic energy of the rod?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,-0.174,-1.993;B,1.638,-1.147" cerchi="0,0,2,foro" aste="A-B" tratteggi="0,2.4 0,-2.6;0,0 0.732,-1.570;0,0 1.638,-1.147" punti="0.732,-1.570,G,baricentro" etichette="0.17,-0.62,ϑ;1.05,-0.38,R;2.3,1.2,fixed guide" quote="A,B,R,-0.45" titolo="Rod of length $R$ with its endpoints on a smooth circular guide of radius $R$"}

- $\dfrac{1}{24}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$

## Explanation

The triangle $OAB$ has all three sides of length $R$, hence it is equilateral and the distance of $O$ from the midpoint of the rod is $OG = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R$. The endpoints stay on the circle and the rod is rigid, therefore every configuration is obtained from another by a rotation about $O$, and the act of motion is rotational about the axis through $O$ orthogonal to the plane even though the rod does not pass through $O$. By the Huygens-Steiner theorem
$$
I_O = \frac{1}{12}\,m\,R^2 + m \left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R \right)^2 = \frac{5}{6}\,m\,R^2 ,
$$
so the kinetic energy is $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_O\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \dfrac{5}{12}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$.
