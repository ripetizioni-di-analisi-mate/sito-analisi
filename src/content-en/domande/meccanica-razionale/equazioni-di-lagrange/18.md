The homogeneous disk rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide. The component of the reaction tangent to the guide at the contact point is different from zero, and nevertheless the pure rolling constraint is ideal. For what reason?

- The contact point has zero velocity in the act of motion, hence the virtual work of the reaction is zero
- The tangential component is orthogonal to every virtual displacement of the points of the disk
- The reaction is applied to the guide and not to the disk, hence it does no work on the disk
- The tangential component is balanced by the normal component of the reaction

## Explanation

The reaction of the pure rolling constraint is applied at the contact point between the disk and the guide. The condition of pure rolling requires that point of the disk to have zero velocity, therefore it is the instantaneous center of rotation and its virtual displacement is the zero vector. The virtual work of the reaction is the scalar product between the reaction and the virtual displacement of the point of application, hence it is $0$ even when the tangential component is different from zero. The constraint is therefore ideal and the reaction does not appear in Lagrange's equation of the disk, as is seen in the disk rolling on an inclined plane, where the acceleration of the center is obtained without computing the reaction.
