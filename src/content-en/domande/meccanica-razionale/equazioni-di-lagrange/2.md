The homogeneous disk of center $C$, mass $2m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in the vertical plane; a spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero rest length connects $C$ to the point $O$ of the line described by $C$, as in the diagram in the figure. What is the period of the oscillations of the disk?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,1;C,3.5,1,-" cerchi="3.5,1,1" linee="-0.5,0 6.5,0;0,1 0.4,1.25 0.95,0.75 1.5,1.25 2.05,0.75 2.45,1 2.5,1" punti="3.5,1,C,baricentro;3.5,0,H,centro" etichette="1.2,1.55,k;4.6,1.5,2m" quote="0 0,3.5 0,x,0.75" titolo="Disk of mass $2m$ rolling, pulled back by the spring of constant $k$"}

- $2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,m}{k}}$
- $4\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$
- $2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,m}{2\,k}}$
- $2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,m}{k}}$

## Explanation

The pure rolling constraint links the angular velocity to the velocity of the center by $|\dot{\varphi}| = \dfrac{\dot{x}}{R}$, therefore the system has a single degree of freedom. The moment of inertia of the disk with respect to the axis through $C$ orthogonal to the plane of the motion is $I_C = \dfrac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,R^2 = m\,R^2$, hence by König's theorem
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\frac{\dot{x}^2}{R^2} = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 .
$$
The potential of the spring is $U = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,x^2$, hence $Q_x = -k\,x$ and Lagrange's equation is $3\,m\,\ddot{x} = -k\,x$. The motion is harmonic with $\omega^2 = \dfrac{k}{3\,m}$, therefore the period is $\mathcal{T} = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega} = 2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,m}{k}}$.
