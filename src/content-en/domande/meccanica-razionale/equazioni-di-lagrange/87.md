A rigid wheel rotates about a fixed axis and has moment of inertia $I$ about that axis; the Lagrangian coordinate is the rotation angle $\vartheta$. Which physical quantity is the kinetic momentum $p_\vartheta$ conjugate to $\vartheta$?

- The kinetic energy of the wheel
- The momentum of the wheel
- The angular momentum of the wheel about the axis of rotation
- The moment of the active forces about the axis of rotation

## Explanation

The act of motion is rotational about the fixed axis, therefore $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ and $p_\vartheta = \dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{\vartheta}} = I\,\dot{\vartheta}$. For a rigid body rotating about a fixed axis, $I\,\dot{\vartheta}$ is the component along the axis of the angular momentum about a pole on the axis, that is, the **axial angular momentum**. Lagrange's equation $\dfrac{d p_\vartheta}{dt} = Q_\vartheta$ thus coincides with the second cardinal equation projected on the axis, in which $Q_\vartheta$ is the axial moment of the active forces.
