The material point $P$ of mass $m$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ and its position is identified by the polar coordinates $r$ and $\varphi$, taken as Lagrangian coordinates in the order $q_1 = r$, $q_2 = \varphi$. What is the element $a_{22}$ of the kinetic matrix?

- $m$
- $m\,r$
- $m\,r^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,r^2$

## Explanation

The position of the point is $P - O = r\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + r\sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the velocity has the radial component $\dot{r}$ and the transverse component $r\,\dot{\varphi}$ and the kinetic energy is
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{r}^2 + r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 \big) .
$$
The constraints are fixed, hence $T = T_2$ and the kinetic matrix is read from the coefficients of the quadratic form $T_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle\sum_{h,k} a_{hk}\,\dot{q}_h\,\dot{q}_k$. The coefficient of $\dot{\varphi}^2$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,r^2$, hence $a_{22} = m\,r^2$, while $a_{11} = m$ and $a_{12} = 0$. The determinant is $m^2\,r^2$, which is positive for $r > 0$, in agreement with the theorem on the kinetic matrix.
