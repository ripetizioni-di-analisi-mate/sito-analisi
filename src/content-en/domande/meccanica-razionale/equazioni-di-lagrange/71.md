In each of the listed systems a ring of mass $m$ slides without friction on a fixed circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, and the Lagrangian coordinate is the angle $\varphi$ giving the position of the ring on the guide. In which system is $\varphi$ a cyclic coordinate?

- The guide lies in a horizontal plane and only the weight acts on the ring
- The guide lies in a vertical plane and only the weight acts on the ring
- The guide lies in a horizontal plane and a spring connects the ring to a fixed point of the plane other than $O$
- The guide lies in a vertical plane and a spring of zero rest length connects the ring to $O$

## Explanation

In all four systems the kinetic energy is $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$ and does not depend on $\varphi$, therefore $\varphi$ is cyclic if and only if the potential does not depend on $\varphi$. With the horizontal guide the height of the ring is constant, hence the potential of the weight is constant and $\varphi$ is **cyclic**; the kinetic momentum $p_\varphi = m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}$ is conserved and the ring runs along the guide with uniform motion. With the vertical guide the height of the ring depends on $\varphi$, and with the spring toward a point other than $O$ the length of the spring depends on $\varphi$. In the last system the spring has constant length $R$, but the weight still depends on $\varphi$.
