The material point $P$ of mass $m$ slides on a smooth fixed guide shaped as a cylindrical helix, with equations $x = R\cos\varphi$, $y = R\sin\varphi$, $z = c\,\varphi$, with $c > 0$ and the $z$ axis pointing vertically upward. The angle $\varphi$ is taken as Lagrangian coordinate. What is the magnitude of the vertical acceleration $\ddot{z}$ of the point?

- $\dfrac{g\,c}{\sqrt{R^2 + c^2}}$
- $\dfrac{g\,c^2}{R^2 + c^2}$
- $g$
- $\dfrac{g\,R^2}{R^2 + c^2}$

## Explanation

Differentiating the equations of the helix gives $v_P^2 = \big( R^2 + c^2 \big)\,\dot{\varphi}^2$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( R^2 + c^2 \big)\,\dot{\varphi}^2 ,
\qquad
U = -m\,g\,z = -m\,g\,c\,\varphi .
$$
Lagrange's equation gives $m\,\big( R^2 + c^2 \big)\,\ddot{\varphi} = -m\,g\,c$, hence $\ddot{\varphi} = -\dfrac{g\,c}{R^2 + c^2}$. Since $z = c\,\varphi$, one has $\ddot{z} = c\,\ddot{\varphi} = -\dfrac{g\,c^2}{R^2 + c^2}$, and its magnitude is $\dfrac{g\,c^2}{R^2 + c^2}$. The value $\dfrac{g\,c}{\sqrt{R^2 + c^2}}$ is the magnitude of the acceleration along the helix, whose projection on the vertical is obtained by multiplying once more by $\dfrac{c}{\sqrt{R^2 + c^2}}$.
