The homogeneous rod $OA$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the fixed point $O$ and rotates in the vertical plane; the angle $\vartheta$ is the one between the rod and the downward vertical. With the convention $\vec{F} = \operatorname{grad} U$, what is the potential of the weight?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta$
- $m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta$

## Explanation

The rod is homogeneous, therefore its center of mass $G$ is the midpoint and is at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from the hinge. With the $y$ axis vertical and upward and the origin at $O$ the height of the center of mass is $y_G = -\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$. The potential of the weight of a body of mass $m$ is $U = -m\,g\,y_G$, hence here it is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta$. The potential is maximum for $\vartheta = 0$, that is in the configuration with the rod vertical and downward, which is the stable equilibrium configuration of the rod.
