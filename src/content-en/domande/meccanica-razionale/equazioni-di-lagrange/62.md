The material point $P$ of mass $m$ slides on a smooth fixed horizontal guide and is connected by a spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero rest length to the point $A$ of the guide, which is moved along the guide with the assigned law $x_A = a\sin(\Omega\,t)$. With the abscissa $x$ of $P$, measured from the same fixed point of the guide from which $x_A$ is measured, as Lagrangian coordinate, what is the equation of motion?

- $m\,\ddot{x} + k\,x = 0$
- $m\,\ddot{x} + k\,x = -k\,a\sin(\Omega\,t)$
- $m\,\ddot{x} + k\,x = m\,a\,\Omega^2\sin(\Omega\,t)$
- $m\,\ddot{x} + k\,x = k\,a\sin(\Omega\,t)$

## Explanation

The spring has length $|x - x_A|$, therefore its potential depends explicitly on time,
$$
U = -\frac{1}{2}\,k\,\big( x - a\sin(\Omega\,t) \big)^2 ,
$$
and at every fixed instant it is still a potential of the elastic force. The Lagrangian component is $Q_x = \dfrac{\partial U}{\partial x} = -k\,\big( x - a\sin(\Omega\,t) \big)$, while $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2$ and the weight does no virtual work on the horizontal guide. Lagrange's equation thus gives $m\,\ddot{x} = -k\,x + k\,a\sin(\Omega\,t)$, that is $m\,\ddot{x} + k\,x = k\,a\sin(\Omega\,t)$. The motion imposed on the end of the spring therefore acts on the point as a **sinusoidal forcing** of amplitude $k\,a$.
