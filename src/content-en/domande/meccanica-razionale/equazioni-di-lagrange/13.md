- $m\,R^2\,\big( 5 - 2\cos\vartheta \big)\,\ddot{\vartheta} + m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta = 0$
- $m\,R^2\,\big( 5 - 2\cos\vartheta \big)\,\ddot{\vartheta} + 2\,m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta = 0$
- $m\,R^2\,\big( 4 - 2\cos\vartheta \big)\,\ddot{\vartheta} + m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta = 0$
- $m\,R^2\,\big( 5 - 2\cos\vartheta \big)\,\ddot{\vartheta} + m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 - m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta = 0$

## Explanation

The pure rolling gives $v_C = R\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$, while the welded point is at distance $2\,R\left| \sin\dfrac{\vartheta}{2} \right|$ from the contact point, therefore $v_P^2 = 2\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)$. Adding the term of the center, the rotational term of the disk with $I_C = \dfrac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,R^2 = m\,R^2$ and that of the material point one obtains
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\big( 5 - 2\cos\vartheta \big)\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 .
$$
The point $P$ is at height $R\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)$, hence the potential of the weight is $U = -m\,g\,R\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)$ and $Q_\vartheta = -m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta$. In the derivative $\dfrac{d}{dt}\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{\vartheta}}$ the term $2\,m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ appears, from which $\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \vartheta} = m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ is subtracted, therefore Lagrange's equation is
$$
m\,R^2\,\big( 5 - 2\cos\vartheta \big)\,\ddot{\vartheta} + m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta = 0 .
$$
