The material point $P$ of mass $m$ hangs from an inextensible string that passes through a small fixed hole $O$; on the other side of the hole the string is pulled so that the length of the segment $OP$ is an assigned function $\ell(t)$, always positive. The string stays taut and $P$ moves in the vertical plane. With $\vartheta$ the angle between $OP$ and the downward vertical, what is the equation of motion?

- $\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta} + \dot{\ell}\,\dot{\vartheta} + g\sin\vartheta = 0$
- $\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta} + g\sin\vartheta = 0$
- $\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta} - 2\,\dot{\ell}\,\dot{\vartheta} + g\sin\vartheta = 0$
- $\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta} + 2\,\dot{\ell}\,\dot{\vartheta} + g\sin\vartheta = 0$

## Explanation

The constraint $|P - O| = \ell(t)$ is holonomic and moving, and the system has the single degree of freedom $\vartheta$. In polar coordinates centered at $O$ the velocity of $P$ has the radial component $\dot{\ell}$ and the transverse component $\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{\ell}^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 \big) ,
\qquad
U = m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta .
$$
One has $\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{\vartheta}} = m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}$, whose time derivative is $m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} + 2\,m\,\ell\,\dot{\ell}\,\dot{\vartheta}$, while $\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \vartheta} = 0$ and $Q_\vartheta = -m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta$. Dividing by $m\,\ell$ gives $\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta} + 2\,\dot{\ell}\,\dot{\vartheta} + g\sin\vartheta = 0$, where the term $2\,\dot{\ell}\,\dot{\vartheta}$ is the part of the transverse acceleration due to the shortening of the string.
