In the vertical plane the material point $A$ of mass $2m$ slides on a smooth fixed horizontal guide; a rigid rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$ is hinged at $A$, which carries at its other endpoint the material point $P$ of mass $m$, as in the diagram in the figure. The abscissa $x$ of $A$ and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical are taken as Lagrangian coordinates, in the order $q_1 = x$, $q_2 = \vartheta$. What is the element $a_{12}$ of the kinetic matrix?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0,-;A,2.4,0;P,3.662,-1.802" catene="A-P" vincoli="A,carrello,90" linee="-0.6,0 6,0" tratteggi="2.4,0 2.4,-2.3" etichette="2.68,-1.3,ϑ;1.7,0.45,2m;4.05,-2.2,m" quote="0 0,A,x,0.75" titolo="Material point $A$ on a horizontal guide with the rod and the point $P$ hinged at $A$"}

- $m\,\ell\cos\vartheta$
- $2\,m\,\ell\cos\vartheta$
- $3\,m\,\ell\cos\vartheta$
- $m\,\ell$

## Explanation

The position of the two points is $A - O = x\,\hat{\imath}$ and $P - O = (x + \ell\sin\vartheta)\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore $v_A^2 = \dot{x}^2$ and $v_P^2 = \dot{x}^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + 2\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta$. The kinetic energy is then
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v_P^2
= \frac{3}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,\ell\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta .
$$
In the quadratic form $T_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle\sum_{h,k} a_{hk}\,\dot{q}_h\,\dot{q}_k$ the mixed term appears twice, since $a_{12} = a_{21}$, and it contributes with $a_{12}\,\dot{x}\,\dot{\vartheta}$. From the comparison with the mixed term of $T$ it follows that $a_{12} = m\,\ell\cos\vartheta$, while $a_{11} = 3\,m$ and $a_{22} = m\,\ell^2$.
