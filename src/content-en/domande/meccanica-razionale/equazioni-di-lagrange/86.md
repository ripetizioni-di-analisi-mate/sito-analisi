The robotic arm in the figure moves in a horizontal plane and consists of two rods: $OA$ is hinged at the fixed point $O$ and $AB$ is hinged at $A$ to the rod $OA$. A motor placed at the elbow $A$ exerts on the rod $AB$ the torque $\mathcal{M}\,\hat{k}$ and on the rod $OA$ the opposite torque $-\mathcal{M}\,\hat{k}$. The angles $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ that the rods $OA$ and $AB$ form with the fixed $x$ axis, positive counterclockwise, are taken as Lagrangian coordinates. What are the Lagrangian components of the two torques of the motor?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,2,1;B,3.8,0.3" aste="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera" cerniere="A" tratteggi="0,0 1.6,0;2,1 3.6,1" coppie="A,1,M" etichette="0.75,0.15,ϑ_1;3.2,0.78,ϑ_2" titolo="Two-link robotic arm with the motor at the elbow $A$ (top view)"}

- $Q_1 = 0$, $Q_2 = \mathcal{M}$
- $Q_1 = \mathcal{M}$, $Q_2 = \mathcal{M}$
- $Q_1 = \mathcal{M}$, $Q_2 = -\mathcal{M}$
- $Q_1 = -\mathcal{M}$, $Q_2 = \mathcal{M}$

## Explanation

The virtual work of a torque applied to a rigid body is the product of its moment and the virtual rotation of the body. The rod $OA$ rotates by $\delta\vartheta_1$ and the rod $AB$ rotates by $\delta\vartheta_2$, because both angles are measured from the fixed direction of the $x$ axis, therefore
$$
\delta L = -\mathcal{M}\,\delta\vartheta_1 + \mathcal{M}\,\delta\vartheta_2 .
$$
The coefficients of the increments give $Q_1 = -\mathcal{M}$ and $Q_2 = \mathcal{M}$. The work of the two torques depends only on the relative rotation $\delta\vartheta_2 - \delta\vartheta_1$, as for every internal torque; the plane is horizontal, hence the weight does not contribute.
