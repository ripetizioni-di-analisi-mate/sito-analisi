The homogeneous disk of center $O$, mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged at the center to a smooth fixed horizontal axis and moves in the vertical plane; a material point of mass $m$ is welded at the point $P$ of its rim, as in the diagram in the figure. The disk starts from rest in the configuration $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, where $\vartheta$ is the angle between the radius $OP$ and the downward vertical. What is the value of $|\dot{\vartheta}|$ as it passes through the configuration $\vartheta = 0$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.6" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" nodi="O,0,0,-" tratteggi="0,0 0,-1.6;0,0 1.226,-1.028" punti="0,0,O,centro;1.226,-1.028,P" etichette="0.3,-0.95,ϑ;1.3,-1.42,m" titolo="Disk with a fixed axis and material point welded at the point $P$ of the rim"}

- $2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{3\,R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g}{3\,R}}$
- $2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{R}}$

## Explanation

The act of motion is a rotation about the fixed axis through $O$ and the overall moment of inertia is $I_O = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2$, therefore $T = \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$. The center of the disk stays at rest, hence the only weight that does work is that of the welded point, which is at height $-R\cos\vartheta$, and the potential is $U = m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$. The constraints are fixed and the load is conservative, therefore the mechanical energy $E = T - U$ is conserved. In the initial configuration one has $T = 0$ and $U = 0$, hence $E = 0$, and in the configuration $\vartheta = 0$ one has
$$
\frac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 - m\,g\,R = 0 .
$$
From this equality it follows that $\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \dfrac{4\,g}{3\,R}$, hence $|\dot{\vartheta}| = 2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{3\,R}}$.
