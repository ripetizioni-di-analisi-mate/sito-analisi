The material points $P_1$ and $P_2$, of masses $m$ and $2m$, slide on one and the same smooth fixed horizontal straight guide and are connected to each other by an ideal spring of elastic constant $k$ and rest length $\ell_0$. With $x_1$ and $x_2$ their abscissas, which function remains constant along the motion?

- $m\,\dot{x}_1 - 2\,m\,\dot{x}_2$
- $m\,\dot{x}_1 + 2\,m\,\dot{x}_2$
- $m\,\big( \dot{x}_1 + \dot{x}_2 \big)$
- $2\,m\,\dot{x}_1 + m\,\dot{x}_2$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}_1^2 + m\,\dot{x}_2^2$ and the potential of the spring is $U = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(x_2 - x_1 - \ell_0)^2$, therefore the potential depends on the two abscissas only through the difference $x_2 - x_1$. Adding the two Lagrange equations the derivatives of the potential cancel, since $\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial x_1} = -\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial x_2}$, and there remains
$$
\frac{d}{dt}\big( m\,\dot{x}_1 + 2\,m\,\dot{x}_2 \big) = 0 .
$$
The function $m\,\dot{x}_1 + 2\,m\,\dot{x}_2$ is hence a first integral and coincides with the component of the linear momentum along the guide. The same result is read in the change of coordinates that takes the abscissa of the center of mass and the distance between the two points, since the abscissa of the center of mass is a cyclic coordinate.
