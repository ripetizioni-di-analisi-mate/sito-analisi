- $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{2\,\mathcal{M} - 5\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta}{5\,m\,\ell^2}$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3\,\big( 2\,\mathcal{M} - 5\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta \big)}{14\,m\,\ell^2}$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{2\,\mathcal{M} - 5\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta}{4\,m\,\ell^2}$
- $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3\,\big( 2\,\mathcal{M} - 3\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta \big)}{14\,m\,\ell^2}$

## Explanation

The system has one degree of freedom and the act of motion is a rotation about the fixed axis through $O$, therefore the kinetic energy is $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_O\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$. The moment of inertia of the rod with respect to the axis through $O$ is $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$ and that of the material point welded at $A$ is $2\,m\,\ell^2$, hence $I_O = \dfrac{7}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$. The center of mass of the rod is at height $-\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta$ and the material point at height $-\ell\cos\vartheta$, hence the potential of the weight is $U = \dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta$ and its derivative with respect to $\vartheta$ is $-\dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta$. The constant counterclockwise couple does the virtual work $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\vartheta$, therefore it adds the term $\mathcal{M}$ to $Q_\vartheta$. Lagrange's equation then becomes
$$
\frac{7}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \mathcal{M} - \frac{5}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta ,
$$
that is $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3\,\big( 2\,\mathcal{M} - 5\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta \big)}{14\,m\,\ell^2}$.
