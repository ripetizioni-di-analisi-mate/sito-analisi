A cart consists of a body of mass $M$ and four wheels, each a homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $r$, mounted on smooth pins attached to the body. The wheels roll without slipping on a fixed horizontal track. With the abscissa $x$ of the body as Lagrangian coordinate, what is the kinetic energy of the cart?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(M + 4\,m)\,\dot{x}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(M + 6\,m)\,\dot{x}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(M + 8\,m)\,\dot{x}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(M + 2\,m)\,\dot{x}^2$

## Explanation

The body translates, therefore its kinetic energy is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,M\,\dot{x}^2$. The center of each wheel moves with the body and has velocity $\dot{x}$, while pure rolling gives the wheel an angular velocity of magnitude $\dfrac{\dot{x}}{r}$; by König's theorem the kinetic energy of one wheel is thus
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\,m\,r^2 \cdot \frac{\dot{x}^2}{r^2} = \frac{3}{4}\,m\,\dot{x}^2 .
$$
Adding the body and the four wheels gives $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,M\,\dot{x}^2 + 3\,m\,\dot{x}^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}\,(M + 6\,m)\,\dot{x}^2$. The quantity $M + 6\,m$ is the **equivalent mass** of the cart, since the wheels have mass $4\,m$ but because of the rotation they count as $6\,m$.
