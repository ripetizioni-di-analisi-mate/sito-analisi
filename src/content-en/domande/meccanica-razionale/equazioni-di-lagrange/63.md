Two equal homogeneous disks of radius $R$ roll without slipping on the same fixed horizontal guide, in the vertical plane; a rigid rod is hinged with smooth hinges to the centers $C_1$ and $C_2$ of the two disks, as in the diagram in the figure. How many Lagrange equations describe the motion of the system?

::struttura{nodi="C1,1,0.8,-;C2,4,0.8,-" cerchi="1,0.8,0.8;4,0.8,0.8" aste="C1-C2" cerniere="C1;C2" linee="-0.5,0 5.5,0" etichette="1,1.85,C_1;4,1.85,C_2;0.2,1.75,R" titolo="Two disks rolling on the same guide, connected by a rod hinged at their centers"}

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The number of Lagrange equations equals the number of degrees of freedom, therefore one counts the independent coordinates needed to determine the configuration. The centers of the two disks stay at height $R$ and the rod keeps their distance $\ell$ constant, hence the abscissa $x_1$ of $C_1$ also determines $C_2$, with $x_2 = x_1 + \ell$. Pure rolling links the rotation angle of each disk to the abscissa of its center, and the rod translates while staying horizontal. The system therefore has **a single** degree of freedom and its motion is described by a single Lagrange equation. The value $4$ is obtained by counting the abscissas and the angles of the two disks without imposing the rolling constraints and the constraint of the rod.
