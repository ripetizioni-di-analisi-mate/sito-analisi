The homogeneous disk of center $C$, mass $m$ and radius $R$ has an inextensible string of negligible mass wound around its rim; the free end of the string is fixed to a ceiling and the unwound segment stays vertical, as in the diagram in the figure. The disk descends in the vertical plane unwinding the string, which does not slip on the rim. What is the acceleration of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="Q,-1,3,-;K,-1,0,-" linee="-1.8,3 1.4,3" catene="Q-K" cerchi="0,0,1" punti="0,0,C,baricentro" frecce="1.6,0.6,1.6,-0.6,y" etichette="0.75,-1.15,m" titolo="Disk descending while unwinding the string fixed to the ceiling"}

- $g$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,g$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,g$

## Explanation

The string is fixed, inextensible and does not slip, therefore the point of the disk in contact with the vertical segment of the string has zero velocity and is the instantaneous center of rotation. With $y$ the height of $C$ measured downward one thus has $|\dot{\varphi}| = \dfrac{\dot{y}}{R}$, and the system has one degree of freedom. By König's theorem
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{y}^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 \cdot \frac{\dot{y}^2}{R^2} = \frac{3}{4}\,m\,\dot{y}^2 ,
\qquad
U = m\,g\,y .
$$
The tension of the string is applied at a point of zero velocity and does no virtual work. Lagrange's equation gives $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,\ddot{y} = m\,g$, hence the acceleration of $C$ is $\dfrac{2}{3}\,g$, directed downward.
