In a coordinate $q$, a system has Lagrangian $L=\dfrac12(m+bq^2)\dot q^2$, with $m,b>0$ and no potential. What acceleration follows from Lagrange's equation when $q>0$ and $\dot q\ne0$?

- $\ddot q=0$
- $\ddot q=+\dfrac{bq\dot q^2}{m+bq^2}$
- $\ddot q=-\dfrac{2bq\dot q^2}{m+bq^2}$
- $\ddot q=-\dfrac{bq\dot q^2}{m+bq^2}$

## Explanation

The conjugate momentum is $(m+bq^2)\dot q$. Its derivative contains $2bq\dot q^2$, while $\partial L/\partial q=bq\dot q^2$. Their difference leaves $(m+bq^2)\ddot q+bq\dot q^2=0$.
