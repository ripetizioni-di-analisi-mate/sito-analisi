The material point $P$ of mass $m$ is constrained to the smooth fixed circle of center $O$ and radius $R$, lying in the vertical plane, and is connected by a spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero rest length to the highest point $A$ of the circle. With $\vartheta$ the angle between $OP$ and the downward vertical, what is the potential of the system, up to an additive constant?

- $\big( m\,g\,R - k\,R^2 \big)\cos\vartheta$
- $\big( m\,g\,R + k\,R^2 \big)\cos\vartheta$
- $\big( m\,g\,R - 2\,k\,R^2 \big)\cos\vartheta$
- $\big( k\,R^2 - m\,g\,R \big)\cos\vartheta$

## Explanation

With the origin at $O$ one has $P - O = R\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} - R\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $A - O = R\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the length of the spring satisfies
$$
|AP|^2 = R^2\sin^2\vartheta + R^2\,(1 + \cos\vartheta)^2 = 2\,R^2\,(1 + \cos\vartheta) .
$$
The potential of the weight is $-m\,g\,y_P = m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$ and that of the spring is $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,|AP|^2 = -k\,R^2\,(1 + \cos\vartheta)$, hence the sum is $m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta - k\,R^2\cos\vartheta - k\,R^2$. The term $-k\,R^2$ is constant and can be omitted, since the potential enters the equations only through its derivatives, therefore the potential of the system is $\big( m\,g\,R - k\,R^2 \big)\cos\vartheta$. For $k\,R > m\,g$ the coefficient is negative and the configuration with $P$ at the lowest point becomes one of unstable equilibrium.
