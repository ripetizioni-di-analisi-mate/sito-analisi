The material points $P_1$ and $P_2$, both of mass $m$, slide on a smooth fixed horizontal guide: a spring of elastic constant $k$ connects a fixed wall to $P_1$ and a second spring, equal to the first, connects $P_1$ to $P_2$, as in the diagram in the figure. The displacements $x_1$ and $x_2$ of the two points from the configuration in which both springs have their rest length are taken as Lagrangian coordinates. What is Lagrange's equation for $x_1$?

::struttura{poligoni="-0.3,-0.3 0,-0.3 0,1.1 -0.3,1.1;1.8,0.1 2.4,0.1 2.4,0.7 1.8,0.7;4.2,0.1 4.8,0.1 4.8,0.7 4.2,0.7" linee="0,0.1 5.8,0.1;0.000,0.400 0.250,0.400 0.358,0.580 0.575,0.220 0.792,0.580 1.008,0.220 1.225,0.580 1.442,0.220 1.550,0.400 1.800,0.400;2.400,0.400 2.650,0.400 2.758,0.580 2.975,0.220 3.192,0.580 3.408,0.220 3.625,0.580 3.842,0.220 3.950,0.400 4.200,0.400" frecce="2.1,1.0,2.9,1.0,x_1;4.5,1.0,5.3,1.0,x_2" etichette="0.9,0.85,k;3.3,0.85,k;2.1,-0.25,P_1;4.5,-0.25,P_2" riferimento=0 titolo="Two material points of mass $m$ connected to the wall and to each other by two equal springs"}

- $m\,\ddot{x}_1 = -k\,x_1 + k\,x_2$
- $m\,\ddot{x}_1 = -2\,k\,x_1 - k\,x_2$
- $m\,\ddot{x}_1 = -2\,k\,x_1 + k\,x_2$
- $m\,\ddot{x}_1 = -k\,x_1$

## Explanation

The elongation of the first spring is $x_1$ and that of the second is $x_2 - x_1$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{x}_1^2 + \dot{x}_2^2 \big) ,
\qquad
U = -\frac{1}{2}\,k\,x_1^2 - \frac{1}{2}\,k\,(x_2 - x_1)^2 .
$$
The coordinate $x_1$ appears in both terms of the potential, hence $Q_1 = \dfrac{\partial U}{\partial x_1} = -k\,x_1 + k\,(x_2 - x_1) = -2\,k\,x_1 + k\,x_2$. Lagrange's equation for $x_1$ is thus $m\,\ddot{x}_1 = -2\,k\,x_1 + k\,x_2$, and the one for $x_2$ is $m\,\ddot{x}_2 = k\,x_1 - k\,x_2$. The term $k\,x_2$ couples the two equations, because the second spring acts on both points.
