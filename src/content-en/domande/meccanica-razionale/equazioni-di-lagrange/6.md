- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \big( m\,g\,\ell + 4\,k\,\ell^2\cos\vartheta \big)\sin\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \big( m\,g\,\ell + 4\,k\,\ell^2\cos\vartheta \big)\sin\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \big( m\,g\,\ell - 4\,k\,\ell^2\cos\vartheta \big)\sin\vartheta$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \big( m\,g\,\ell + 2\,k\,\ell^2\cos\vartheta \big)\sin\vartheta$

## Explanation

With $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the vertical guide one has $A - O = 2\,\ell\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath}$ and $B - O = 2\,\ell\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence the center of mass $G$, the midpoint of the rod, has coordinates $(\ell\sin\vartheta,\ \ell\cos\vartheta)$ and velocity of magnitude $v_G = \ell\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$. The moment of inertia of the rod with respect to the axis through $G$ orthogonal to the plane of the motion is $I_G = \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,(2\ell)^2 = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$, therefore by König's theorem
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 .
$$
The spring has length $OB = 2\,\ell\cos\vartheta$, hence the overall potential is $U = -m\,g\,\ell\cos\vartheta - 2\,k\,\ell^2\cos^2\vartheta$ and its derivative with respect to $\vartheta$ is $\big( m\,g\,\ell + 4\,k\,\ell^2\cos\vartheta \big)\sin\vartheta$. Lagrange's equation is therefore $\dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \big( m\,g\,\ell + 4\,k\,\ell^2\cos\vartheta \big)\sin\vartheta$.
