- $3\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $2\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The angular momentum about $O$ is the sum of the moments of the vectors $m_i\,\vec{v}_i$, hence the two cross products are computed. For the first point it equals
$$
(2\ell\,\hat{\imath}) \wedge (m\,v\,\hat{\jmath}) = 2\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k} ,
$$
whereas for the second one it equals $(\ell\,\hat{\jmath}) \wedge (m\,v\,\hat{\imath}) = -\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$, since $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{\imath} = -\hat{k}$. Adding the two contributions one obtains $\vec{K}_O = m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$. The component along $\hat{k}$ is positive, hence the system turns about $O$ counterclockwise.
