A cart of mass $5m$ slides without friction on a horizontal guide and carries on its upper surface, smooth as well, a material point of mass $m$, as in the figure. The system starts from rest and the point moves a distance $d$ to the right relative to the cart. By how much does the cart move relative to the guide?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0.3 4,0.3 4,1.3 0,1.3" cerchi="0.9,0.3,0.3;3.1,0.3,0.3" linee="-1,0 5.6,0" punti="1,1.3,P,pieno" frecce="1,1.75,2.8,1.75" etichette="2.2,0.78,5m;0.55,1.8,m;1.9,2.05,d" titolo="The point moves a distance $d$ relative to the cart"}

- $\dfrac{5\,d}{6}$ to the left
- $\dfrac{d}{6}$ to the left
- $\dfrac{d}{5}$ to the left
- $\dfrac{d}{6}$ to the right

## Explanation

The horizontal external forces vanish and the system starts from rest, hence the horizontal component of the linear momentum stays zero and the center of mass does not move. Denoting by $x_c$ the displacement of the cart and by $x_p$ the displacement of the point, the immobility of the center of mass is written $m\,x_p + 5m\,x_c = 0$. The displacement of the point relative to the cart equals $x_p - x_c = d$, therefore substituting $x_p = d + x_c$ one obtains $6\,x_c = -d$, that is $x_c = -\dfrac{d}{6}$. The negative sign says that the cart moves a distance $\dfrac{d}{6}$ in the direction opposite to the one in which the point moves, hence to the left.
