A cart of mass $2m$ has a smooth upper profile, which starts at height $h$ and ends horizontal at the base, and it slides without friction on a horizontal guide, as in the figure. A material point of mass $m$ is released at rest at the highest point of the profile and slides down to the base. What magnitude does the velocity of the point have at the instant at which it leaves the cart?

::struttura{poligoni="1.3,2 1.4,1.42 1.7,0.91 2.15,0.53 2.71,0.33 3,0.3 4.2,0.3 4.2,0 1.3,0" linee="-0.4,0 5.6,0" punti="1.3,2,P,pieno" quote="1.3 0.3,1.3 2,h,-0.7" etichette="3.1,1,2m;0.95,2.25,m" titolo="The point slides along the smooth profile of the cart"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,h}{3}}$
- $\sqrt{2\,g\,h}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g\,h}{3}}$
- $2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,h}{3}}$

## Explanation

The profile and the guide are smooth, hence in the horizontal direction no external forces act and the horizontal linear momentum stays zero, that is $m\,v_1 = 2m\,v_2$ for the magnitudes of the two velocities. The constraints are smooth and the weight is conservative, therefore the mechanical energy is conserved and it equals
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,m\,v_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,v_2^2 = m\,g\,h .
$$
Substituting $v_2 = \dfrac{v_1}{2}$ one obtains $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,v_1^2 = m\,g\,h$, hence $v_1 = 2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,h}{3}}$. With the cart locked the velocity of the point would be $\sqrt{2\,g\,h}$, whereas here the cart recoils and part of the energy goes into its motion.
