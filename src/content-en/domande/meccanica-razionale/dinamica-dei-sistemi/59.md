A thin homogeneous ring of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed inclined plane of angle $\alpha$, as in the figure. What is the acceleration of its center?

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,3 6,0 0,0" cerchi="3.64,2.72,0.8;3.64,2.72,0.68,foro" punti="3.64,2.72,G,baricentro" etichette="0.95,0.2,α" titolo="Ring rolling without slipping on an inclined plane"}

- $g\,\sin\alpha$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,\sin\alpha$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,g\,\sin\alpha$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,g\,\sin\alpha$

## Explanation

The pole is chosen at the geometric point $C$ of contact between the ring and the plane. The point $C$ moves parallel to the plane with the same velocity as $G$, hence the term $\vec{v}_C \wedge m\,\vec{v}_G$ vanishes and the second cardinal equation holds in the form $\dot{K}_C = M_C^{(e)}$. The reaction of the plane, friction included, is applied at $C$ and has zero moment, whereas the weight has lever arm $R\,\sin\alpha$ about $C$. The moment of inertia of the ring about the axis through $C$ equals $m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = 2\,m\,R^2$ and the pure rolling condition gives $\dot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{v_G}{R}$, therefore
$$
2\,m\,R^2\,\frac{a}{R} = m\,g\,R\,\sin\alpha , \qquad a = \frac{1}{2}\,g\,\sin\alpha .
$$
