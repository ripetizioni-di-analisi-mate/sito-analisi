A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping to the right on a fixed horizontal guide, with $\vec{v}_G = v\,\hat{\imath}$. The pole is chosen at the point $P$ of the disk that at the given instant lies in front of the center, $P = G + R\,\hat{\imath}$, as in the figure. What is the term $\vec{v}_P \wedge m\,\vec{v}_G$ of the second cardinal equation with a moving pole?

::struttura{cerchi="3,1.5,1.5" linee="0,0 7,0" punti="3,1.5,G,baricentro;4.5,1.5,P,pieno;3,0,C,centro" frecce="2.2,3.4,3.8,3.4,v_G" titolo="Rolling disk: the pole $P$ is the point of the rim in front of the center"}

- $\vec{0}$
- $-\,m\,v^2\,\hat{k}$
- $2\,m\,v^2\,\hat{k}$
- $m\,v^2\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The disk rolls to the right, hence the pure rolling condition gives $\vec{\omega} = -\dfrac{v}{R}\,\hat{k}$. By the fundamental formula of rigid kinematics the velocity of $P$ equals
$$
\vec{v}_P = v\,\hat{\imath} + \Big( -\frac{v}{R}\,\hat{k} \Big) \wedge R\,\hat{\imath} = v\,\hat{\imath} - v\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The required term is therefore $(v\,\hat{\imath} - v\,\hat{\jmath}) \wedge m\,v\,\hat{\imath} = -\,m\,v^2\,(\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{\imath}) = m\,v^2\,\hat{k}$. The velocity of $P$ is not parallel to $\vec{v}_G$, hence with this pole the second cardinal equation contains the additional term, which instead vanishes with pole at $G$ or at the geometric contact point.
