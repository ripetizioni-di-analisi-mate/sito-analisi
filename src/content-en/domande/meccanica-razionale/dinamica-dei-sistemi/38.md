A material point has velocity $\vec{v} = 3v\,\hat{\imath} - 4v\,\hat{\jmath}$ and is subject to the force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath} + 2F\,\hat{\jmath}$. What is the power of the force at the instant considered?

- $5\,F\,v$
- $-5\,F\,v$
- $11\,F\,v$
- $-\,F\,v$

## Explanation

The power of a force applied to a point equals $\Pi = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$, hence one adds the products of the corresponding components:
$$
\Pi = (F)(3v) + (2F)(-4v) = 3\,F\,v - 8\,F\,v = -5\,F\,v .
$$
The power is negative, therefore the force takes kinetic energy away from the point. The value $11\,F\,v$ would be obtained by adding the magnitudes of the two products, that is by neglecting the sign of the component $-4v$.
