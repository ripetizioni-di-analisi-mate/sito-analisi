The rods $AB$ and $BC$ in the figure are hinged to each other at $B$ and constrained to the ground at $A$ and at $C$. The system formed by the two rods is considered. How is the reaction that the rod $BC$ exerts on $AB$ at the hinge $B$ classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,2;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" titolo="Two rods hinged to each other at $B$"}

- An external force for the system of the two rods and an internal one for the single rod $AB$
- An external force in both cases
- An internal force for the system of the two rods and an external one for the single rod $AB$
- An internal force in both cases

## Explanation

A force is internal to a system when it is exerted by a point that belongs to the system itself. The rod $BC$ belongs to the system of the two rods, hence the reaction that it exerts on $AB$ is a force internal to that system and, by the theorem on the resultant and on the resultant moment of the internal forces, it does not appear in its cardinal equations. If instead the rod $AB$ alone is considered, the rod $BC$ is a body external to it, therefore the same reaction is an external force and it appears as an unknown in the cardinal equations of $AB$. The classification therefore depends on the system that is chosen, and on this choice rests the procedure of taking the system apart with which the internal reactions are computed.
