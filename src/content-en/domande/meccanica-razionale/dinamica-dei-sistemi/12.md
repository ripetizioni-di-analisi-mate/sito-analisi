- $\sqrt{g\,h}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,h}{2}}$
- $\sqrt{2\,g\,h}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g\,h}{3}}$

## Explanation

The string is inextensible and does not slip on the pulley, hence the two bodies have the same velocity $v$ and the pulley turns with angular velocity $\dfrac{v}{R}$. The moment of inertia of the pulley about its axis equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(4m)\,R^2 = 2\,m\,R^2$, therefore the kinetic energy of the system is
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,(m + 3m)\,v^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,(2\,m\,R^2)\,\frac{v^2}{R^2} = 3\,m\,v^2 .
$$
The constraints are fixed and ideal and the weight is conservative, hence the mechanical energy is conserved. While the body of mass $3m$ descends by $h$ the other one rises by $h$, therefore the work of the weight equals $3\,m\,g\,h - m\,g\,h = 2\,m\,g\,h$, and from the equality $3\,m\,v^2 = 2\,m\,g\,h$ it follows that $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g\,h}{3}}$.
