A homogeneous rod of mass $4m$ and length $\ell$ is at rest on a smooth horizontal plane. A material point of mass $m$, sliding on the plane with velocity $v_0$ orthogonal to the rod, hits an endpoint of the rod elastically and immediately after the impact is at rest. What angular velocity does the rod have after the impact?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" punti="-2,3,P,pieno;0,1.5,G,baricentro" frecce="-1.8,3,-0.35,3" etichette="-1.1,3.3,v_0;-2.3,2.65,m;-0.5,0.8,4m" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.8" titolo="View from above: the point hits the endpoint of the free rod"}

- $\dfrac{v_0}{2\,\ell}$
- $\dfrac{2\,v_0}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{3\,v_0}{2\,\ell}$
- $\dfrac{3\,v_0}{4\,\ell}$

## Explanation

No external impulsive forces act in the plane, hence in the impact the linear momentum and the angular momentum about a fixed point are conserved; the fixed point is chosen at the position occupied by $G$ at the instant of the impact. The point is at rest after the impact, therefore the conservation of the linear momentum gives $m\,v_0 = 4m\,v_G$, that is $v_G = \dfrac{v_0}{4}$. Before the impact the point has lever arm $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ about that pole, and after the impact the angular momentum of the rod about it coincides with $I_G\,\omega$, since $\vec{v}_G$ passes through the pole; hence
$$
m\,v_0\,\frac{\ell}{2} = \frac{1}{12}\,(4m)\,\ell^2\,\omega , \qquad \omega = \frac{3\,v_0}{2\,\ell} .
$$
The energy balance confirms that the impact is elastic, since $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(4m)\,\dfrac{v_0^2}{16} + \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 \cdot \dfrac{9\,v_0^2}{4\,\ell^2} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_0^2$.
