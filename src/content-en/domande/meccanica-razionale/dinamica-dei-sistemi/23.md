A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide and its center has velocity of magnitude $v$. What magnitude does the angular momentum of the disk about the contact point $C$ have?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R\,v$
- $m\,R\,v$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R\,v$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R\,v$

## Explanation

The velocity of the contact point vanishes, hence the state of motion is rotatory about $C$ and the angular momentum about $C$ equals $K_C = I_C\,\omega$. By the Huygens-Steiner theorem we have $I_C = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2$, and the pure rolling condition gives $\omega = \dfrac{v}{R}$. It follows that
$$
K_C = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2 \cdot \frac{v}{R} = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,R\,v .
$$
The value $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R\,v$ would be obtained with the moment of inertia about the center alone, that is neglecting the Huygens-Steiner transport term.
