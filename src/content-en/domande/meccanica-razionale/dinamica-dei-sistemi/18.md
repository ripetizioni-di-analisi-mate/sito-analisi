Two material points of masses $m$ and $4m$ occupy the positions $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (5\,\ell, 0)$ of the fixed frame $(O; x, y)$. What abscissa does the center of mass of the system have?

- $\ell$
- $4\,\ell$
- $\dfrac{5\,\ell}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5\,\ell}{4}$

## Explanation

The center of mass is defined by the relation $M\,(G - O) = \sum_i m_i\,(P_i - O)$, where $M = 5m$ is the total mass. Projecting onto the $x$ axis one obtains
$$
x_G = \frac{m \cdot 0 + 4m \cdot 5\,\ell}{5m} = 4\,\ell .
$$
The center of mass therefore lies on the segment $AB$, at distance $4\,\ell$ from $A$ and $\ell$ from $B$, that is closer to the point of larger mass. The value $\dfrac{5\,\ell}{2}$ is the abscissa of the midpoint, which would be the center of mass only with equal masses.
