A vehicle is formed by a body of mass $4m$ and by two wheels, each of which can be treated as a homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $r$, rolling without slipping on a horizontal road; the body translates with velocity $v$, as in the figure. What is the kinetic energy of the vehicle?

::struttura{poligoni="0,1 5,1 5,2.1 0,2.1" cerchi="1,0.5,0.5;4,0.5,0.5" punti="1,0.5,,centro;4,0.5,,centro" linee="-0.6,0 5.8,0" frecce="1.8,2.6,3.4,2.6,v" etichette="2.5,1.55,4m;1,-0.35,m;4,-0.35,m" titolo="Vehicle with two wheels rolling without slipping"}

- $\dfrac{7}{2}\,m\,v^2$
- $3\,m\,v^2$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,m\,v^2$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,v^2$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy of the vehicle is the sum of those of the three rigid bodies. The body translates, hence it contributes $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(4m)\,v^2 = 2\,m\,v^2$. The center of each wheel moves with the body, and the pure rolling condition gives the angular velocity $\dfrac{v}{r}$, therefore each wheel has kinetic energy
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\,m\,r^2 \cdot \frac{v^2}{r^2} = \frac{3}{4}\,m\,v^2 .
$$
Adding up the contributions one obtains $T = 2\,m\,v^2 + 2 \cdot \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,v^2 = \dfrac{7}{2}\,m\,v^2$. The value $3\,m\,v^2$ corresponds to wheels that translate without rotating, that is, to the translational energy of the total mass $6m$ alone.
