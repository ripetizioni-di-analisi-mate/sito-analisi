A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ rotates in a plane about the fixed hinge placed at one of its endpoints with angular velocity $\dot{\vartheta}$. What magnitude does the linear momentum of the rod have?

- $m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}}{2}$

## Explanation

For an arbitrary system we have $\vec{Q} = M\,\vec{v}_G$, hence the velocity of the center of mass is enough. The center of mass of a homogeneous rod lies at the midpoint, therefore it is at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from the hinge and, since the state of motion is rotatory about it, it has velocity of magnitude $v_G = \dfrac{\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}}{2}$. It follows that $Q = \dfrac{m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}}{2}$. The value $m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}$ would be obtained with the velocity of the free endpoint, which is twice that of the center of mass.
