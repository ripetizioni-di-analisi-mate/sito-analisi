A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed inclined plane, as in the figure, and starts from rest. What magnitude does the velocity of the center have when the center has descended a height $h$?

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,3 6,0 0,0" cerchi="2.64,2.22,0.8" punti="2.64,2.22,G,baricentro" etichette="0.95,0.2,α" titolo="Disk rolling without slipping on an inclined plane"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g\,h}{3}}$
- $\sqrt{2\,g\,h}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{10\,g\,h}{7}}$
- $\sqrt{g\,h}$

## Explanation

The reaction of the plane is applied at the contact point, which has zero velocity, hence its power vanishes and the only force that does work is the weight. The mechanical energy is therefore conserved and, with $\omega = \dfrac{v}{R}$, the kinetic energy of the disk equals $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,v^2$. The kinetic energy theorem between the initial configuration and the final one then gives
$$
\frac{3}{4}\,m\,v^2 = m\,g\,h ,
$$
hence $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g\,h}{3}}$. The value $\sqrt{2\,g\,h}$ would be obtained for a body that slides without rotating, since in that case all the energy would go into the translation.
