A homogeneous, flexible and inextensible chain of mass $m$ and length $L$ rests on a smooth horizontal table, orthogonal to the edge, with a part of length $\dfrac{L}{3}$ hanging vertically beyond the edge, as in the figure. The edge is rounded and smooth. The chain is released at rest. What is the magnitude of its velocity at the instant when the last link leaves the table?

::struttura{nodi="P,-2,0.06,-;E,0.08,0.06,-;F,0.08,-1,-" aste="P-E;E-F" poligoni="-4,0 0,0 0,-0.25 -4,-0.25" linee="-3.6,-0.25 -3.6,-2.2;-0.5,-0.25 -0.5,-2.2" etichette="-1,0.4,m" titolo="Chain on a smooth table with a part hanging beyond the edge"}

- $2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,L}{3}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\sqrt{2\,g\,L}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\sqrt{g\,L}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g\,L}{3}}$

## Explanation

The table and the edge are smooth and fixed, hence their reactions do no work; the chain is inextensible, therefore the internal forces also do zero total work and the mechanical energy is conserved. All the elements of the chain have velocities of the same magnitude $v$, hence the kinetic energy equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2$. The work of the weight depends only on the lowering of the center of mass. With heights measured from the plane of the table, at the beginning the part on the table is at height $0$ and the hanging part, of mass $\dfrac{m}{3}$, has its center of mass at height $-\dfrac{L}{6}$, therefore the center of mass of the chain is at height
$$
y_G = \frac{1}{m} \cdot \frac{m}{3} \cdot \Big( -\frac{L}{6} \Big) = -\frac{L}{18} .
$$
When the last link leaves the table the chain is entirely vertical and $y_G = -\dfrac{L}{2}$, hence $G$ is lowered by $\dfrac{L}{2} - \dfrac{L}{18} = \dfrac{4\,L}{9}$ and
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 = m\,g\,\frac{4\,L}{9} , \qquad v = \sqrt{\frac{8\,g\,L}{9}} = \frac{2}{3}\,\sqrt{2\,g\,L} .
$$
