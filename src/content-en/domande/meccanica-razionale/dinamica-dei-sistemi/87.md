Two homogeneous rods $AB$ and $BC$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $B$; the endpoint $A$ is a fixed hinge and the endpoint $C$ slides on a smooth horizontal guide passing through $A$, in a vertical plane, as in the figure. The system starts from rest with the rods inclined by $\vartheta = 30^\circ$ on the guide and stretches out under the action of the weight. What angular velocity does the rod $AB$ have when the two rods lie flat on the guide?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.73,1;C,3.46,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" linee="-0.8,0 4.6,0" etichette="0.62,0.14,ϑ" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.5;B,C,ℓ,-0.5" titolo="Two hinged rods stretching out on the guide"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{4\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{2\,\ell}}$

## Explanation

When the rods lie flat the velocity of $B$ is vertical, because $B$ rotates about $A$, and the velocity of $C$ is horizontal. The normals to the two velocities through $B$ and through $C$ are the guide and the vertical through $C$, which meet at $C$; the instantaneous center of rotation of $BC$ is therefore $C$, and at that instant $BC$ also has a rotatory state of motion, about $C$, with angular velocity of the same magnitude $\dot{\vartheta}$. The kinetic energy therefore equals
$$
T = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 .
$$
The constraints are smooth and fixed, hence the mechanical energy is conserved; the center of mass of each rod descends by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$, and the work of the weight equals $\dfrac{m\,g\,\ell}{2}$. From the equality $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \dfrac{m\,g\,\ell}{2}$ it follows that $\dot{\vartheta} = \sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}}$.
