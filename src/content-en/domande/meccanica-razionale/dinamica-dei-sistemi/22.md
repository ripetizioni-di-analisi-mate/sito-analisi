A material point of mass $m$ occupies the position $P = (3\,\ell, 2\,\ell)$ of the fixed frame $(O; x, y)$ and has velocity $\vec{v} = v\,\hat{\imath}$. What is its angular momentum about the pole $O$?

- $2\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-2\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $3\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$
- $-3\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The angular momentum of a point about $O$ equals $\vec{K}_O = (P - O) \wedge m\,\vec{v}$, hence one computes the cross product
$$
(3\,\ell\,\hat{\imath} + 2\,\ell\,\hat{\jmath}) \wedge (m\,v\,\hat{\imath}) = 2\,\ell\,m\,v\,(\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{\imath}) = -2\,m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k} ,
$$
since $\hat{\imath} \wedge \hat{\imath} = \vec{0}$ and $\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{\imath} = -\hat{k}$. The abscissa of the point does not enter the result, because the velocity is parallel to the $x$ axis and only the arm $2\,\ell$ measured orthogonally to the velocity counts. The component along $\hat{k}$ is negative, hence the point turns about $O$ clockwise.
