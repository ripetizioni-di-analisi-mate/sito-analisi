A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\sqrt{2}\,R$ has its endpoints constrained to a fixed circular guide with center $O$ and radius $R$, in a plane, as in the figure. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle of rotation of the rod, what is its kinetic energy?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2.15;0,0,2,foro" nodi="A,-1.41,-1.41;B,1.41,-1.41" aste="A-B" punti="0,0,O,centro;0,-1.41,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 0,-1.41;0,0 1.41,-1.41" etichette="0.95,-0.4,R" titolo="Rod with its endpoints on a circular guide"}

- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$

## Explanation

The endpoints of the rod move on the circle, hence their velocities are orthogonal to the radii $OA$ and $OB$, and the normals to the two velocities meet at $O$. The instantaneous center of rotation is therefore the fixed point $O$, and the state of motion is rotatory about $O$ with angular velocity $\dot{\vartheta}$. The distance of $G$ from $O$ satisfies $|G - O|^2 = R^2 - \dfrac{R^2}{2} = \dfrac{R^2}{2}$, and $I_G = \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,(\sqrt{2}\,R)^2 = \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,R^2$, hence by the Huygens-Steiner theorem
$$
I_O = \frac{1}{6}\,m\,R^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 = \frac{2}{3}\,m\,R^2 .
$$
It follows that $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_O\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$.
