A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ hangs by the endpoint $A$ from a carriage of negligible mass that slides on a smooth horizontal guide, in a vertical plane. At the given instant the rod is vertical with $B$ below, $A$ has velocity $u\,\hat{\imath}$ and the rod rotates with angular velocity $\omega\,\hat{k}$, as in the figure. What is the kinetic energy of the rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,2,0;B,2,-3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,90" linee="-0.3,0 4.8,0" punti="2,-1.5,G,baricentro" frecce="2.4,0.8,3.8,0.8,u" coppie="2 -2.4,1,ω" titolo="Rod hanging from a carriage that slides on a horizontal guide"}

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,u^2 + \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,u^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell\,u\,\omega + \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,u^2 + \dfrac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,u^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell\,u\,\omega + \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$

## Explanation

The velocity of the center of mass is obtained from the fundamental formula of rigid kinematics with pole at $A$:
$$
\vec{v}_G = u\,\hat{\imath} + \omega\,\hat{k} \wedge \Big( -\frac{\ell}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} \Big) = \Big( u + \frac{\omega\,\ell}{2} \Big)\,\hat{\imath} .
$$
By **König's theorem** the kinetic energy is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_G^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_G\,\omega^2$ with $I_G = \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2$, hence
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\Big( u^2 + u\,\omega\,\ell + \frac{\omega^2\,\ell^2}{4} \Big) + \frac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,u^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell\,u\,\omega + \frac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 .
$$
The mixed term is positive, since with the counterclockwise rotation and $B$ below, the contribution of the rotation to the velocity of $G$ has the same direction as $u\,\hat{\imath}$.
