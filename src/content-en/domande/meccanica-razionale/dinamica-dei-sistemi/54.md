A box of mass $M$ rests on a scale and contains a small ball of mass $m$ which, having detached from the lid, falls freely inside without touching the walls. What vertical force does the scale exert on the box while the ball is falling?

- $M\,g$
- $(M + m)\,g$
- $(M - m)\,g$
- $(M + 2m)\,g$

## Explanation

Consider the system formed by the box and the ball. The external forces are the total weight $(M + m)\,g$ downward and the reaction $N$ of the scale upward, hence the first cardinal equation projected onto the vertical reads
$$
\dot{Q}_y = N - (M + m)\,g .
$$
The box is at rest and the ball falls with acceleration $-g$, therefore $\dot{Q}_y = -\,m\,g$ and one obtains $N = M\,g$. During the fall the scale therefore reads only the weight of the box, because the ball, which does not touch the walls, exchanges no forces with it.
