A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ moves in a plane. At the given instant its center of mass occupies the point $G = (d, 0)$ of the fixed frame $(O; x, y)$ and has velocity $v\,\hat{\jmath}$, and the rod rotates with angular velocity $\omega\,\hat{k}$, as in the figure. What is the angular momentum of the rod about $O$?

::struttura{nodi="A,2,-0.9,-;B,4,0.9,-" aste="A-B" punti="0,0,O,centro;3,0,G,baricentro" frecce="3,0,3,1.4,v" coppie="3 0,1,ω" quote="0 0,3 0,d,-0.9" titolo="Rod translating and rotating in the plane"}

- $m\,v\,d\,\hat{k}$
- $\Big( m\,v\,d + \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega \Big)\,\hat{k}$
- $\Big( m\,v\,d + \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega \Big)\,\hat{k}$
- $\Big( -\,m\,v\,d + \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega \Big)\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

By **König's theorem** the angular momentum about the fixed pole $O$ is the sum of $\vec{K}_G$ and of the moment of the linear momentum thought of as applied at $G$. The motion relative to $G$ is a rotation, hence $\vec{K}_G = \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$, whatever the inclination of the rod. The second term equals
$$
(G - O) \wedge m\,\vec{v}_G = (d\,\hat{\imath}) \wedge (m\,v\,\hat{\jmath}) = m\,v\,d\,\hat{k} .
$$
Adding up one obtains $\vec{K}_O = \Big( m\,v\,d + \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega \Big)\,\hat{k}$. The moment of inertia about an endpoint, $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$, does not enter, because König's theorem refers the rotational motion to the center of mass.
