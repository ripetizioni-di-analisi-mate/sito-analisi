In a horizontal plane a homogeneous rod $OA$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ rotates about the fixed hinge $O$ with angular velocity of magnitude $\Omega$. A small ring, which can be treated as a material point of mass $m$, slides along the rod; at the given instant it is at distance $r$ from $O$ and moves away from $O$ with relative velocity of magnitude $\dot{r}$, as in the figure. What is the kinetic energy of the system?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,4,0" aste="O-A" vincoli="O,cerniera" punti="2.6,0,P,pieno" frecce="2.6,0.45,3.6,0.45" coppie="O,1,Ω" quote="O,2.6 0,r,-0.6" titolo="View from above: the small ring $P$ slides along the rotating rod"}

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{r}^2 + r^2\,\Omega^2 \big) + \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\Omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{r}^2 + r^2\,\Omega^2 \big) + \dfrac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\Omega^2$
- $m\,\big( \dot{r}^2 + r^2\,\Omega^2 \big) + \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\Omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{r}^2 + r^2\,\Omega^2 \big) + \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\Omega^2$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is the sum of that of the rod and that of the small ring. The rod rotates about the fixed point $O$, hence its kinetic energy equals $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\Omega^2 = \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\Omega^2$. The velocity of the ring is the sum of the relative velocity, directed along the rod and of magnitude $\dot{r}$, and of the transport velocity, orthogonal to the rod and of magnitude $r\,\Omega$. The two components are orthogonal, therefore $v_P^2 = \dot{r}^2 + r^2\,\Omega^2$ and
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{r}^2 + r^2\,\Omega^2 \big) + \frac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\Omega^2 .
$$
With the moment of inertia of the rod about $G$, $\dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2$, one would neglect the kinetic energy due to the motion of the center of mass of the rod.
