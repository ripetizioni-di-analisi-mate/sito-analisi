A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and is released at rest in the horizontal position. What angular velocity does the rod have as it passes through the vertical position?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{6\,g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{12\,g}{\ell}}$

## Explanation

The hinge is fixed and smooth, hence its reaction does no work and the mechanical energy is conserved. The state of motion is rotatory about $A$, therefore $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_A\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ with $I_A = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$. In passing from the horizontal position to the vertical one the center of mass descends by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, hence the work of the weight equals $m\,g\,\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ and the conservation of the energy gives
$$
\frac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = m\,g\,\frac{\ell}{2} .
$$
From this equality it follows that $\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}$, therefore $\dot{\vartheta} = \sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$.
