A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point of the ground, in a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge. An ideal spring of constant $k = \dfrac{m\,g}{4\,\ell}$ and zero rest length connects $B$ to the fixed point $D$ that lies on the vertical through $A$ at height $\ell$, as in the figure. The rod starts at rest from the vertical position and, after a small perturbation, falls. What angular velocity does it have when it reaches the horizontal position?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.93,2.3;D,0,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera,90" linee="-1,0 4,0;1.93,2.3 1.56,2.27 1.4,2.65 1.04,2.46 0.89,2.84 0.52,2.65 0.37,3.03 0,3" tratteggi="0,0 0,3" punti="0.96,1.15,G,baricentro" etichette="0.25,1.1,ϑ;0.83,3.2,k" quote="A,D,ℓ,-0.7" titolo="Rod hinged to the ground with a spring toward the point $D$"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{9\,g}{2\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{9\,g}{4\,\ell}}$

## Explanation

The hinge is fixed and smooth, the weight and the spring are conservative, hence the mechanical energy is conserved. In the vertical position $B$ coincides with $D$ and the spring has zero length; in the horizontal position the length of the spring is $|B - D| = \sqrt{\ell^2 + \ell^2} = \sqrt{2}\,\ell$, hence its potential energy equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,k \cdot 2\,\ell^2 = k\,\ell^2 = \dfrac{m\,g\,\ell}{4}$. The center of mass descends by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ and the state of motion is rotatory about $A$, therefore
$$
\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = m\,g\,\frac{\ell}{2} - \frac{m\,g\,\ell}{4} = \frac{m\,g\,\ell}{4} .
$$
It follows that $\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}$. The value $\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$ is that of the rod without the spring; here the spring stretches during the fall and absorbs half of the work of the weight.
