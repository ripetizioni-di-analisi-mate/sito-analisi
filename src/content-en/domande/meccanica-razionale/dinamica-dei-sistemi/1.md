Three material points of masses $m$, $2m$ and $m$ occupy the positions $P_1 = (0, 0)$, $P_2 = (\ell, 0)$ and $P_3 = (0, 2\ell)$ of the fixed frame $(O; x, y)$, as in the figure, and at the instant considered they have velocities $\vec{v}_1 = \vec{0}$, $\vec{v}_2 = v\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{v}_3 = -2v\,\hat{\imath}$. What is the linear momentum of the system?

::struttura{punti="0,0,P_1,pieno;2,0,P_2,pieno;0,4,P_3,pieno" frecce="2,0,2,1.6;0,4,-1.6,4" etichette="2.5,0.9,v;-1.1,4.3,2v;-0.4,-0.35,m;2.5,-0.35,2m;0.45,4.4,m" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,0.6;0 0,0 4,2ℓ,0.6" titolo="The three points with their velocities"}

- $-2\,m\,v\,\hat{\imath} + 2\,m\,v\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $2\,m\,v\,\hat{\imath} + 2\,m\,v\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $-2\,m\,v\,\hat{\imath} + m\,v\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $-\dfrac{m\,v}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{m\,v}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The linear momentum of a system of material points is the sum of the vectors $m_i\,\vec{v}_i$, therefore the three contributions are added. The first point is at rest, hence it gives no contribution, whereas the second one gives $2m\,v\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the third one gives $-2m\,v\,\hat{\imath}$. The sum is therefore
$$
\vec{Q} = -2\,m\,v\,\hat{\imath} + 2\,m\,v\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The positions of the three points do not enter the computation, since the linear momentum depends on the masses and on the velocities alone. The vector $-\dfrac{m\,v}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{m\,v}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$ is instead the velocity of the center of mass, which is obtained by dividing $\vec{Q}$ by the total mass $4m$.
