A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ rotates in a plane about the fixed hinge placed at one of its endpoints with angular velocity $\dot{\vartheta}$. What is the kinetic energy of the rod?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$

## Explanation

The hinge is fixed, hence the state of motion is rotatory and we have $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_A\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$, where $I_A$ is the moment of inertia about the axis through the hinge orthogonal to the plane. For a homogeneous rod that moment of inertia equals $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 .
$$
The same value is obtained from König's theorem by adding $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_G^2 = \dfrac{1}{8}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}\,I_G\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \dfrac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$. The value $\dfrac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ on its own is the contribution of the rotation about $G$ alone, hence it neglects the term $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_G^2$.
