A material point strikes the endpoint $B$ of a rod hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point and sticks to it. What quantity is conserved during the impact?

- The linear momentum of the system, because the impact is instantaneous
- The kinetic energy, because the hinge is smooth
- The angular momentum about the center of mass of the rod
- The angular momentum about $A$, because the impulsive reaction is applied at $A$

## Explanation

During the impact the hinge exerts an impulsive reaction, which is an impulsive external force, hence the linear momentum of the system is not conserved. That reaction is applied at $A$, therefore its moment about $A$ vanishes, and integrating the second cardinal equation with pole at $A$ one obtains $\vec{K}_A^+ = \vec{K}_A^-$. The kinetic energy is not conserved, since the point sticks to the rod and the impact is perfectly inelastic. The center of mass of the rod is not a suitable pole, since with respect to it the moment of the impulsive reaction is different from zero.
