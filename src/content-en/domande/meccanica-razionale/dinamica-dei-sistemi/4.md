A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $2m$ and length $2\ell$ rotates in a plane about the fixed hinge $A$ with angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. What is the angular momentum of the rod about $A$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$
- $2\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$
- $4\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The point $A$ is fixed, hence the state of motion is rotatory about $A$ and the angular momentum equals $\vec{K}_A = I_A\,\omega\,\hat{k}$, where $I_A$ is the moment of inertia about the axis through $A$ orthogonal to the plane. For a homogeneous rod of mass $M$ and length $L$ that moment of inertia equals $\dfrac{1}{3}\,M\,L^2$, therefore with $M = 2m$ and $L = 2\ell$ one obtains
$$
I_A = \frac{1}{3}\,(2m)\,(2\ell)^2 = \frac{8}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 .
$$
It follows that $\vec{K}_A = \dfrac{8}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$. The same value is recovered with **König's theorem**, since $\vec{K}_G = \dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$ and the transport term $(G - A) \wedge 2m\,\vec{v}_G$ equals $2\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$.
