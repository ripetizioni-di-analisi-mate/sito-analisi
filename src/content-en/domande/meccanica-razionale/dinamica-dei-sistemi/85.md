A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal table. A horizontal inextensible string of negligible mass is attached to its center, passes over an ideal pulley fixed at the edge of the table and holds a body of mass $m$, as in the figure. The system starts from rest. What is the magnitude of the velocity of the hanging body when it has descended a distance $h$?

::struttura{cerchi="1.5,1,1;5.6,0.7,0.3" linee="-0.2,0 5.3,0;5.3,0 5.3,-2.6;1.5,1 5.6,1;5.9,0.7 5.9,-1.4;5.3,0 5.6,0.7" poligoni="5.6,-2 6.2,-2 6.2,-1.4 5.6,-1.4" punti="1.5,1,G,baricentro" etichette="1.5,-0.35,m;6.6,-1.7,m" titolo="Disk rolling on a table, pulled by a hanging body"}

- $\sqrt{g\,h}$
- $\sqrt{2\,g\,h}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g\,h}{3}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g\,h}{5}}$

## Explanation

The string is inextensible, hence the center of the disk and the hanging body have velocities of the same magnitude $v$. The constraints are ideal and fixed, and in particular the reaction of the table does no work because it is applied at the contact point, which has zero velocity; the weight is conservative, therefore the mechanical energy is conserved. The kinetic energy of the rolling disk equals $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,v^2$ and that of the hanging body $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2$, while only the weight of the hanging body does work, hence
$$
\frac{3}{4}\,m\,v^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 = m\,g\,h , \qquad \frac{5}{4}\,m\,v^2 = m\,g\,h .
$$
It follows that $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g\,h}{5}}$.
