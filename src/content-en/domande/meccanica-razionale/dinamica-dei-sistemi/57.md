A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged at a point $A$ of its rim to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and is released at rest with the segment $AG$ horizontal, as in the figure. What is the angular acceleration of the disk at the initial instant?

::struttura{cerchi="1.5,0,1.5" nodi="A,0,0" vincoli="A,cerniera,180" punti="1.5,0,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 1.5,0" etichette="0.75,0.3,R" titolo="Disk hinged at a point of its rim, released with $AG$ horizontal"}

- $\dfrac{g}{R}$
- $\dfrac{2\,g}{R}$
- $\dfrac{3\,g}{2\,R}$
- $\dfrac{2\,g}{3\,R}$

## Explanation

The point $A$ is fixed, hence one writes the second cardinal equation with pole at $A$, in which the reaction of the hinge does not appear because its moment about $A$ vanishes. The state of motion is rotatory about $A$, therefore $K_A = I_A\,\dot{\vartheta}$, and by the Huygens-Steiner theorem
$$
I_A = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2 .
$$
With $AG$ horizontal the lever arm of the weight about $A$ is $R$, hence the moment of the weight has magnitude $m\,g\,R$ and the second cardinal equation gives $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = m\,g\,R$. It follows that the angular acceleration has magnitude $\dfrac{2\,g}{3\,R}$.
