A homogeneous square plate of mass $m$ and side $a$ rotates in its plane about the fixed axis orthogonal to the plate and passing through the vertex $A$, as in the figure, with angular velocity of magnitude $\omega$. What is the kinetic energy of the plate?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3" nodi="A,0,0" vincoli="A,cerniera" punti="1.5,1.5,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 1.5,1.5" quote="0 0,3 0,a,0.6" titolo="Square plate rotating about the axis through the vertex $A$"}

- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,a^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,a^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{5}{24}\,m\,a^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,a^2\,\omega^2$

## Explanation

The vertex $A$ is fixed, hence the state of motion is rotatory and $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_A\,\omega^2$. The moment of inertia of the plate about the axis through $G$ orthogonal to the plane equals $I_G = \dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,(a^2 + a^2) = \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,a^2$, and the distance of $G$ from $A$ is half the diagonal, whose square equals $\dfrac{a^2}{2}$. By the Huygens-Steiner theorem
$$
I_A = \frac{1}{6}\,m\,a^2 + m\,\frac{a^2}{2} = \frac{2}{3}\,m\,a^2 ,
$$
therefore $T = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,a^2\,\omega^2$. The value $\dfrac{5}{24}\,m\,a^2\,\omega^2$ would be obtained by transporting $I_G$ to a distance $\dfrac{a}{2}$, that is, to the midpoint of a side instead of the vertex.
