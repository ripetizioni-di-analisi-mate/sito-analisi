A planar rigid body has laboratory kinetic energy $30\,\mathrm J$, mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and centre-of-mass speed $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What kinetic energy is measured at that instant in a frame translating with the centre of mass without rotating?

- $18\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $30\,\mathrm J$
- $48\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

König's decomposition separates the centre-of-mass translational energy from the relative kinetic energy. The former is $\dfrac12\cdot4\cdot3^2=18\,\mathrm J$, leaving $30-18=12\,\mathrm J$.
