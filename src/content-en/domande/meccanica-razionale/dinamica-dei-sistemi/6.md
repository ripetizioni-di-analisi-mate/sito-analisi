- $m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2\,\Big( \dfrac{3}{4} + \cos\varphi \Big)$
- $m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2\,\Big( \dfrac{7}{4} - \cos\varphi \Big)$
- $m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2\,\Big( \dfrac{7}{4} + \cos\varphi \Big)$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy of the system is the sum of the kinetic energy of the disk and that of the material point. The disk rolls without slipping, hence $v_G = R\,\dot{\varphi}$ and, with $I_G = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$, its kinetic energy equals $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$. The point welded to the rim is at distance $2R\,\Big|\sin\dfrac{\varphi}{2}\Big|$ from the contact point $C$, which has zero velocity, therefore its velocity has magnitude $2R\,\dot{\varphi}\,\Big|\sin\dfrac{\varphi}{2}\Big|$ and its kinetic energy equals
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,m\,4R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2\,\sin^2\frac{\varphi}{2} = m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2\,(1 - \cos\varphi) ,
$$
since $2\sin^2\dfrac{\varphi}{2} = 1 - \cos\varphi$. Adding the two contributions one obtains $T = m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2\,\Big( \dfrac{7}{4} - \cos\varphi \Big)$, which for $\varphi = 0$ reduces to the kinetic energy of the disk alone, as it must, since in that configuration the point occupies the contact.
