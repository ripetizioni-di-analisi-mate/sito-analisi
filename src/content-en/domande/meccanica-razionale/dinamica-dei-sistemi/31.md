A homogeneous solid sphere of mass $m$ and radius $R$, whose moment of inertia about a diameter equals $\dfrac{2}{5}\,m\,R^2$, rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide and its center has velocity of magnitude $v$. What is the kinetic energy of the sphere?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,v^2$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}\,m\,v^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,m\,v^2$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy of a rigid body equals $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_G^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_G\,\omega^2$, and the pure rolling condition gives $\omega = \dfrac{v}{R}$. Substituting $I_G = \dfrac{2}{5}\,m\,R^2$ one obtains
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{5}\,m\,R^2 \cdot \frac{v^2}{R^2} = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 + \frac{1}{5}\,m\,v^2 = \frac{7}{10}\,m\,v^2 .
$$
The value $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,v^2$ holds for the disk, whose moment of inertia about the axis through the center is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2$. At equal velocity of the center the sphere has smaller kinetic energy than the disk, since at equal mass and radius its moment of inertia is smaller.
