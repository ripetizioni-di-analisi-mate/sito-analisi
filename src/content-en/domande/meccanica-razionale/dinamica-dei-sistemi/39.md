A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged at its center $O$ to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and carries a material point of mass $m$ welded to its rim, as in the figure. The system starts from rest with the material point just displaced from the highest position. What angular velocity does the disk have when the material point passes through the lowest position?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.5" nodi="O,0,0" vincoli="O,cerniera" punti="0,1.5,P,pieno" tratteggi="0,0 0,1.5" etichette="0.3,0.75,R;-0.5,1.75,m" titolo="Disk hinged at its center with a material point welded to the rim"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g}{3\,R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g}{R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{8\,g}{3\,R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g}{R}}$

## Explanation

The hinge is fixed and smooth, hence its reaction does no work and the mechanical energy is conserved. The system rotates about the fixed axis through $O$ with moment of inertia
$$
I_O = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2 ,
$$
where the second summand is the contribution of the material point, which is at distance $R$ from the axis. The center of the disk stays at rest, therefore the only lowering is that of the material point, which passes from the height $R$ to the height $-R$ and hence descends by $2R$. The conservation of the energy gives $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2 = 2\,m\,g\,R$, hence $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{8\,g}{3\,R}}$.
