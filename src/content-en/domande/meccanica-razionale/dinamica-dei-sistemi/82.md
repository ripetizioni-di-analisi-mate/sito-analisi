A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is placed in a vertical position with the endpoint $A$ resting on a smooth horizontal plane, as in the figure, and is let fall from rest after a small perturbation. What is the magnitude of the velocity of the center of mass at the instant when the rod lies flat on the plane?

::struttura{nodi="A,2,0;B,2,4" aste="A-B" linee="-0.4,0 4.4,0" punti="2,2,G,baricentro" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.6" titolo="Rod standing on a smooth plane, let fall"}

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3\,g\,\ell}}{2}$
- $\sqrt{g\,\ell}$
- $\sqrt{3\,g\,\ell}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,\ell}{3}}$

## Explanation

The plane is smooth, hence the external forces are vertical, the horizontal component of the linear momentum stays zero and $G$ descends along the vertical. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the plane, $y_G = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\sin\vartheta$, therefore $v_G = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\cos\vartheta\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$, which with the rod lying flat equals $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$. The constraint is smooth and fixed and the weight is conservative, hence the mechanical energy is conserved and, since $G$ descends by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$,
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,m\,v_G^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2\,\frac{4\,v_G^2}{\ell^2} = m\,g\,\frac{\ell}{2} , \qquad \frac{2}{3}\,m\,v_G^2 = m\,g\,\frac{\ell}{2} .
$$
It follows that $v_G^2 = \dfrac{3\,g\,\ell}{4}$, that is $v_G = \dfrac{\sqrt{3\,g\,\ell}}{2}$.
