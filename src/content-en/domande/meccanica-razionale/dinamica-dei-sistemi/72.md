In the slider-crank mechanism in the figure the crank $OA$, a homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, rotates about the fixed hinge $O$ with angular velocity of magnitude $\omega$; the connecting rod $AB$, a homogeneous rod of mass $2m$ and length $2\ell$, is hinged at $A$ and has the endpoint $B$ on a horizontal guide passing through $O$. What is the kinetic energy of the system at the instant when the crank is vertical?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0,2;B,3.46,0" aste="O-A;A-B" cerniere="A" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello" linee="-0.8,0 4.6,0" coppie="O,1,ω" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.6;A,B,2ℓ,0.5" titolo="Slider-crank mechanism with the crank vertical"}

- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{7}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$

## Explanation

With the crank vertical the velocity of $A$ is horizontal and has magnitude $\omega\,\ell$, and the velocity of $B$ is horizontal too, because $B$ slides on the guide. The normals to the two velocities drawn through $A$ and through $B$ are two distinct vertical lines, hence the instantaneous center of rotation of the connecting rod is at infinity and the connecting rod has a translational state of motion. All the points of the connecting rod therefore have velocity of magnitude $\omega\,\ell$, and
$$
T = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,\omega^2\,\ell^2 = \frac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 + m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 = \frac{7}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 .
$$
The first term is the kinetic energy of the crank in the rotatory state of motion about $O$, the second that of the translating connecting rod.
