A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rotates in a vertical plane about the fixed axis orthogonal to the disk and passing through a point $A$ of its rim, as in the figure, with angular velocity of magnitude $\omega$. What is the kinetic energy of the disk?

::struttura{cerchi="1.5,0,1.5" nodi="A,0,0" vincoli="A,cerniera,180" punti="1.5,0,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 1.5,0" etichette="0.75,0.3,R" titolo="Disk hinged at the point $A$ of its rim"}

- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$

## Explanation

The point $A$ is fixed, hence the state of motion is rotatory about $A$ and we have $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_A\,\omega^2$. The moment of inertia about the axis through $A$ is obtained from the Huygens-Steiner theorem, since $A$ is at distance $R$ from the center of mass:
$$
I_A = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = \frac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2 .
$$
Therefore $T = \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}\,m\,R^2\,\omega^2$ would be obtained with the moment of inertia about the center alone, hence without the transport term.
