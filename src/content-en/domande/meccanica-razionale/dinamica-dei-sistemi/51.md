Two bodies of masses $m$ and $3m$, which can be treated as material points, hang from the ends of an inextensible string of negligible mass that passes over an ideal pulley, of negligible mass and friction, supported by a fixed support. The system is released. What acceleration does the center of mass of the two bodies have?

- $\dfrac{g}{2}$, directed downward
- $\dfrac{g}{4}$, directed downward
- $\dfrac{g}{4}$, directed upward
- $0$

## Explanation

The Newton equations of the two bodies, with the same tension and equal and opposite accelerations, give the body of mass $3m$ the acceleration $a = \dfrac{3m - m}{3m + m}\,g = \dfrac{g}{2}$ downward, and the body of mass $m$ the same acceleration upward. The center of mass has the mass-weighted average acceleration, hence, with the vertical axis oriented downward,
$$
a_G = \frac{3m \cdot \dfrac{g}{2} - m \cdot \dfrac{g}{2}}{4\,m} = \frac{g}{4} .
$$
The result is positive, therefore $G$ accelerates downward. The same value can be read from the first cardinal equation: the support exerts on the pulley a force smaller than the total weight $4\,m\,g$, and the difference gives $G$ the acceleration $\dfrac{g}{4}$ downward.
