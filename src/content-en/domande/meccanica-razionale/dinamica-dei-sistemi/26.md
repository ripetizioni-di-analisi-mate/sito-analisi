A homogeneous circular platform of mass $4m$ and radius $R$ rotates without friction about the fixed vertical axis through its center, in a horizontal plane, with angular velocity $\omega_0$. A material point of mass $m$ is placed on the rim and, because of the friction with the platform, ends up staying at rest relative to it. What angular velocity does the platform have from that instant on?

- $\dfrac{2\,\omega_0}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4\,\omega_0}{5}$
- $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2}$
- $\omega_0$

## Explanation

The axis is vertical, fixed and smooth, hence the weight and the reaction of the axis have vanishing axial moment and the axial angular momentum of the system is conserved, even though the friction between the point and the platform dissipates energy. The moment of inertia of the platform about its axis equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(4m)\,R^2 = 2\,m\,R^2$, therefore the initial angular momentum equals $2\,m\,R^2\,\omega_0$, since the point is at rest when it is placed. In the final configuration the moment of inertia equals $I_f = 2\,m\,R^2 + m\,R^2 = 3\,m\,R^2$, hence
$$
\omega_f = \frac{2\,m\,R^2\,\omega_0}{3\,m\,R^2} = \frac{2\,\omega_0}{3} .
$$
The moment of inertia has increased, therefore the angular velocity decreases.
