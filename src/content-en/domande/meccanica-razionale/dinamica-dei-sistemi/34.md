A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ moves in a plane: its center of mass has velocity of magnitude $v$ and the rod rotates with angular velocity of magnitude $\omega$. What is the kinetic energy of the rod?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 + \dfrac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 + \dfrac{1}{6}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2$

## Explanation

The state of motion is roto-translational, hence the formula for the kinetic energy of a rigid body applies, $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,v_G^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_G\,\omega^2$. The moment of inertia of a homogeneous rod about the axis through the center of mass orthogonal to the plane equals $\dfrac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{12}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,v^2 + \frac{1}{24}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 .
$$
The value $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2$ is the moment of inertia about an endpoint, which is not used here, since the pole of the rotatory term must be the center of mass.
