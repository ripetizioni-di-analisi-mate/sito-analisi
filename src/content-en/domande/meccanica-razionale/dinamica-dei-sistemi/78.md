A person climbs a staircase at constant speed. The person is considered as the system. Which forces do the positive work that balances the negative work of the weight?

- The internal forces exerted by the muscles, since the reaction of the steps is applied at points of the foot that have zero velocity
- The reaction of the steps, since it is directed upward like the displacement of the center of mass
- None, since the kinetic energy is constant and therefore the work of each force vanishes
- The weight itself, since the person goes up

## Explanation

By the kinetic energy theorem the sum of the works of the external and internal forces equals the change of the kinetic energy, which here vanishes. The weight does negative work, because the center of mass rises. The reaction of a step acts on the foot resting on it, which is at rest while in contact, hence its power $\vec{\Phi} \cdot \vec{v}$ vanishes. The positive work that balances that of the weight is therefore done by the internal muscular forces, which have nonzero power because the person is not a rigid body.
