A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and is released, at rest, in the horizontal position. What value does the vertical component of the reaction of the hinge have at the initial instant?

- $\dfrac{m\,g}{4}$, directed upward
- $m\,g$, directed upward
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,g$, directed upward
- $\dfrac{m\,g}{2}$, directed upward

## Explanation

The second cardinal equation with pole at the hinge $A$ does not contain the reaction, since the moment of the latter about $A$ vanishes, and it gives $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = m\,g\,\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, hence $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}$. At the initial instant the angular velocity vanishes, therefore the acceleration of the center of mass has the tangential component alone, directed downward and with magnitude
$$
a_G = \frac{\ell}{2}\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \frac{3\,g}{4} .
$$
The first cardinal equation projected onto the vertical then gives $-\,m\,\dfrac{3\,g}{4} = \Phi_{Ay} - m\,g$, hence $\Phi_{Ay} = \dfrac{m\,g}{4}$. The reaction is therefore directed upward and equals one quarter of the weight of the rod, since the center of mass is descending with acceleration.
