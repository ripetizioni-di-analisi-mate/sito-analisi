A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $2m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and is at rest in the vertical position, as in the figure. A material point of mass $m$, moving horizontally with velocity $v_0$, strikes the endpoint $B$ and sticks to it. What angular velocity does the system have immediately after the impact?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;B,0,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" punti="-1.8,0,P,pieno;0,1.5,G,baricentro" frecce="-1.8,0,-0.35,0" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.6" etichette="-2.1,0.35,m;-0.75,2.2,2m;-1.05,0.3,v_0" titolo="The point strikes the endpoint $B$ and sticks to it"}

- $\dfrac{v_0}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{3\,v_0}{5\,\ell}$
- $\dfrac{3\,v_0}{2\,\ell}$
- $\dfrac{v_0}{2\,\ell}$

## Explanation

The only impulsive external force is the reaction of the hinge at $A$, whose moment about $A$ vanishes, hence in the impact the angular momentum about $A$ is conserved. Before the impact the velocity of the point is orthogonal to $AB$, therefore $K_A^- = m\,v_0\,\ell$. After the impact the system rotates about $A$ with moment of inertia
$$
I_A = \frac{1}{3}\,(2m)\,\ell^2 + m\,\ell^2 = \frac{5}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 ,
$$
hence $K_A^+ = \dfrac{5}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega$. From the equality of the two values it follows that $\omega = \dfrac{3\,v_0}{5\,\ell}$.
