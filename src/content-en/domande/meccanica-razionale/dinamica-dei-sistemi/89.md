A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point, with a smooth hinge, and is at rest in a vertical position with $B$ below. A horizontal blow transmits to it an impulse $\vec{J}$ at a point at distance $x$ from $A$, as in the figure. For which value of $x$ does the hinge exert no impulse on the rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;B,0,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" punti="0,1.5,G,baricentro" frecce="-1.4,1.2,-0.08,1.2" etichette="-0.8,1.5,J" quote="A,0 1.2,x,0.6" titolo="Hinged rod hit by a horizontal impulse"}

- $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$
- $\ell$
- $\dfrac{3\,\ell}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$

## Explanation

The angular momentum about $A$ changes only by the moment of the impulse $\vec{J}$, because the impulse of the hinge has zero moment about $A$; hence $\dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega = J\,x$. The linear momentum, instead, changes because of the impulse $\vec{J}$ and of the impulse $J_A$ of the hinge, and after the blow the center of mass has velocity $\omega\,\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, therefore
$$
m\,\omega\,\frac{\ell}{2} = J + J_A .
$$
Substituting $\omega = \dfrac{3\,J\,x}{m\,\ell^2}$ one obtains $J_A = J\,\Big( \dfrac{3\,x}{2\,\ell} - 1 \Big)$, which vanishes for $x = \dfrac{2\,\ell}{3}$. This point is the **center of percussion** of the rod: a bat held at the end and hit at that point transmits no impulse to the hand.
