- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $m\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $4\,m\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy is conserved, hence in passing through the vertical position we have $\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}$. In that configuration the moment of the weight about $A$ vanishes, therefore the second cardinal equation gives $\ddot{\vartheta} = 0$ and the acceleration of the center of mass is only centripetal, directed from $G$ toward $A$, with magnitude
$$
\frac{\ell}{2}\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{3\,g}{2} .
$$
The first cardinal equation projected onto the vertical then gives $m\,\dfrac{3\,g}{2} = \Phi_{Ay} - m\,g$, hence $\Phi_{Ay} = \dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,g$. The reaction of the hinge is therefore directed upward and its magnitude is two and a half times the weight of the rod.
