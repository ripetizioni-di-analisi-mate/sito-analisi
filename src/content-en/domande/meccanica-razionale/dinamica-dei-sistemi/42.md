Two material points of masses $m$ and $2m$ are welded to the endpoints of a rigid rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$, hinged at its midpoint to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, as in the figure. The system starts from rest with the rod horizontal. What angular velocity does the rod have as it passes through the vertical position?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0,-;O,2,0,-;B,4,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera" punti="0,0,m,pieno;4,0,2m,pieno" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.9" titolo="Two material points at the endpoints of a rod of negligible mass, hinged at its midpoint"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{8\,g}{3\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{3\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g}{3\,\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g}{\ell}}$

## Explanation

The rod has negligible mass, hence the moment of inertia about the axis through the hinge is due to the two points alone, which are at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from it:
$$
I_O = m\,\Big(\frac{\ell}{2}\Big)^2 + 2m\,\Big(\frac{\ell}{2}\Big)^2 = \frac{3}{4}\,m\,\ell^2 .
$$
In passing from the horizontal position to the vertical one the point of mass $2m$ descends by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ and the one of mass $m$ rises by $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, therefore the work of the weight equals $2m\,g\,\dfrac{\ell}{2} - m\,g\,\dfrac{\ell}{2} = \dfrac{m\,g\,\ell}{2}$. The hinge is smooth and fixed, hence the mechanical energy is conserved and we have $\dfrac{3}{8}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2 = \dfrac{m\,g\,\ell}{2}$, from which $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g}{3\,\ell}}$. The value $\sqrt{\dfrac{8\,g}{3\,\ell}}$ would be obtained by neglecting the rise of the point of mass $m$.
