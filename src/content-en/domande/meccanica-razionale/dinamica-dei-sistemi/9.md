A homogeneous circular platform of mass $2m$ and radius $R$ rotates without friction about a fixed vertical axis through its center, in a horizontal plane, with angular velocity $\omega_0$, as in the figure, seen from above. Two material points of mass $m$ each are rigidly attached to the platform, diametrically opposite on the rim, and are brought to a distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from the axis, where they stay at rest relative to the platform. What angular velocity does the platform have in the final configuration?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" punti="0,0,O,centro;2,0,P_1,pieno;-2,0,P_2,pieno;1,0,,pieno;-1,0,,pieno" frecce="1.8,0,1.2,0;-1.8,0,-1.2,0" coppie="-1.2 1.2,1,ω_0" etichette="-1.3,-1.2,2m" titolo="View from above: the two points are brought to a distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from the axis"}

- $2\,\omega_0$
- $4\,\omega_0$
- $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2}$
- $\dfrac{8\,\omega_0}{5}$

## Explanation

The axis is vertical, fixed and smooth, hence the weight and the reaction of the axis have vanishing axial moment and the axial angular momentum is conserved. In the initial configuration the moment of inertia about the axis equals
$$
I_i = \frac{1}{2}\,(2m)\,R^2 + 2\,m\,R^2 = 3\,m\,R^2 ,
$$
whereas in the final configuration, with the two points at distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$, it equals $I_f = m\,R^2 + 2\,m\,\Big(\dfrac{R}{2}\Big)^2 = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2$. From the equality $I_i\,\omega_0 = I_f\,\omega_f$ it therefore follows that $\omega_f = 2\,\omega_0$. The moment of inertia has halved, hence the angular velocity doubles.
