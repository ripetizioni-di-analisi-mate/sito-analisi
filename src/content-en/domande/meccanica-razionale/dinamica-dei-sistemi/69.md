- $2\,m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}\,m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$
- $\dfrac{27}{4}\,m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$
- $3\,m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$

## Explanation

The center of the disk travels along a circle with center $O$ and radius $3r - r = 2r$, hence $v_G = 2\,r\,|\dot{\varphi}|$. The contact point has zero velocity, therefore the disk rotates about it with angular velocity of magnitude $\omega = \dfrac{v_G}{r} = 2\,|\dot{\varphi}|$. By the theorem on the kinetic energy of a rigid body, with $I_G = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,r^2$,
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,(2\,r\,\dot{\varphi})^2 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\,m\,r^2\,(2\,\dot{\varphi})^2 = 2\,m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 + m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 = 3\,m\,r^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 .
$$
The angular velocity of the disk is twice $\dot{\varphi}$, because the center moves on a radius $2r$ while the disk has radius $r$.
