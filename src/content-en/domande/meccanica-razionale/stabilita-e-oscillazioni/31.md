A conservative holonomic system with two degrees of freedom has potential energy $V(q_1, q_2) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,q_1^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,q_2^2 - \alpha\,q_1 q_2$, with $k > 0$ and $\alpha > 0$. For which values of $\alpha$ is the origin a stable equilibrium configuration?

- $\alpha > k$
- $\alpha < k$
- $\alpha < 2k$
- $\alpha > \dfrac{k}{2}$

## Explanation

The gradient of $V$ is zero at the origin for every value of $\alpha$, therefore $(0, 0)$ is an equilibrium configuration. The Hessian matrix equals
$$
\mathbb{V} =
\begin{bmatrix}
k & -\alpha \\
-\alpha & k
\end{bmatrix} ,
$$
with $\det\mathbb{V} = k^2 - \alpha^2$ and $V_{11} = k > 0$. By the Sylvester criterion the matrix is positive definite if and only if $k^2 - \alpha^2 > 0$, that is, since $\alpha > 0$, if and only if $\alpha < k$; in that case the origin is a strict minimum of $V$ and the equilibrium is stable.
