The uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, has the end $A$ on a smooth vertical guide and the end $B$ on a smooth horizontal guide; the two guides meet at the point $O$ and the system moves in a vertical plane. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and zero natural length connects $B$ to $O$, and we assume $2k\ell > m g$, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the square of the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the configuration with the rod vertical?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2.6;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,180;B,carrello,270" linee="0,0 4,0;0,0 0,3.2;0,0 0.4,0.3 0.9,0 1.4,0.3 1.9,0 2.4,0.3 2.7,0.15 3,0" punti="0,0,O;1.5,1.3,G,baricentro" etichette="2.45,0.3,ϑ;1.2,0.6,k" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.5" titolo="The rod with the end $A$ on the vertical guide, the end $B$ on the horizontal one and the spring between $B$ and $O$"}

- $\dfrac{3k}{m} + \dfrac{3g}{2\ell}$
- $\dfrac{3k}{m} - \dfrac{3g}{2\ell}$
- $\dfrac{k}{m} - \dfrac{g}{2\ell}$
- $\dfrac{12k}{m} - \dfrac{6g}{\ell}$

## Explanation

We take as coordinate the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the horizontal guide, so that, with respect to $O$, the end $B$ has abscissa $\ell\cos\vartheta$ and the center of mass is $G = \left( \dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta,\ \dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta \right)$. The potential energy is
$$
V(\vartheta) = \frac{1}{2}\,k\,\ell^2\cos^2\vartheta + \frac{m g \ell}{2}\sin\vartheta ,
$$
with $V'\!\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = 0$ and $V''\!\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right) = k\ell^2 - \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$, positive by the hypothesis $2k\ell > m g$. The center of mass lies at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from $O$ in every configuration, hence $v_G = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$, and by König's theorem
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\frac{\ell^2}{4}\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,\frac{m\ell^2}{12}\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = \frac{1}{2}\,\frac{m\ell^2}{3}\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 .
$$
The inertia coefficient therefore equals $\dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}$, and
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{k\ell^2 - \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}}{\dfrac{m\ell^2}{3}} = \frac{3k}{m} - \frac{3g}{2\ell} .
$$
