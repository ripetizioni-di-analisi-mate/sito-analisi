An overhead crane is modeled as a trolley of mass $M$ that slides without friction on a fixed horizontal guide; a rigid rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$ is hinged to the trolley, carries at its end a point load of mass $m$ and oscillates in the vertical plane of the guide, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the square of the nonzero natural angular frequency of the small oscillations about the configuration with the rod vertical?

::struttura{linee="-3,3 3,3" poligoni="-0.8,2.4 0.8,2.4 0.8,2.9 -0.8,2.9" nodi="O,0,2.4;P,0.684,0.521,-" catene="O-P" cerniere="O" tratteggi="0,2.4 0,0.3" punti="0.684,0.521,m,pieno" frecce="-0.4,3.4,0.6,3.4,x" etichette="-1.2,2.65,M" quote="O,P,ℓ,-0.4" titolo="The trolley of mass $M$ on the horizontal guide with the load hung from the rod of length $\ell$"}

- $\dfrac{g}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{(M + m)\,g}{m\ell}$
- $\dfrac{M g}{(M + m)\,\ell}$
- $\dfrac{(M + m)\,g}{M\ell}$

## Explanation

With $x$ the abscissa of the trolley and $\varphi$ the angle of the rod with the downward vertical, the load has velocity $\big( \dot{x} + \ell\cos\varphi\,\dot{\varphi},\ \ell\sin\varphi\,\dot{\varphi} \big)$ and height $-\ell\cos\varphi$. At $\varphi = 0$ the two matrices equal
$$
\mathbb{A} =
\begin{bmatrix}
M + m & m\ell \\
m\ell & m\ell^2
\end{bmatrix} ,
\qquad
\mathbb{V} =
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 0 \\
0 & m g \ell
\end{bmatrix} ,
$$
and the characteristic equation is
$$
\det(\mathbb{V} - \omega^2\mathbb{A}) = -\,\omega^2\,m\ell\,\big[ (M + m)\,g - \omega^2 M\ell \big] = 0 .
$$
The root $\omega^2 = 0$ corresponds to the uniform translation of the whole system, since $V$ does not depend on $x$. From the vanishing of the factor $(M + m)\,g - \omega^2 M\ell$ it follows that
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{(M + m)\,g}{M\ell} ,
$$
larger than the value $\dfrac{g}{\ell}$ obtained with a fixed suspension point.
