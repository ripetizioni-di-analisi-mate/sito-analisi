A point particle of mass $m = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides without friction on a horizontal guide, connected to an ideal spring of stiffness $k = 50\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and to a viscous damper. What is the critical damping constant?

- $10\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}$

## Explanation

The equation of motion $m\,\ddot{x} + h\,\dot{x} + k\,x = 0$ is written in the form $\ddot{x} + 2\beta\,\dot{x} + \omega_0^2\,x = 0$ with $\beta = \dfrac{h}{2m}$ and $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$. The critical damping separates the oscillatory motion from the aperiodic one and corresponds to $\beta = \omega_0$, therefore it equals $h_c = 2 m \omega_0 = 2\sqrt{k m}$. Substituting the data one obtains $h_c = 2\sqrt{50 \cdot 2} = 20\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}$.
