A uniform rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $a$ is suspended from two parallel rigid rods $O_1A$ and $O_2B$, of negligible mass and length $L$, hinged at the fixed points $O_1$ and $O_2$, placed at the same height at distance $a$ from each other. All the hinges are smooth and the system moves in a vertical plane, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the square of the angular frequency of the small oscillations?

::struttura{nodi="O_1,0,3;O_2,3,3;A,0.773,0.622;B,3.773,0.622" catene="O_1-A;O_2-B" aste="A-B" vincoli="O_1,cerniera,90;O_2,cerniera,90" cerniere="A;B" tratteggi="0,3 0,0.4" punti="2.273,0.622,G,baricentro" quote="O_1,A,L,-0.4;A,B,a,0.5" titolo="The rod $AB$ suspended from the two light parallel rods $O_1A$ and $O_2B$"}

- $\dfrac{3g}{2L}$
- $\dfrac{g}{2L}$
- $\dfrac{2g}{L}$
- $\dfrac{g}{L}$

## Explanation

The quadrilateral $O_1ABO_2$ has the opposite sides $O_1O_2$ and $AB$ equal, and also $O_1A$ and $O_2B$ equal, hence it is a parallelogram; therefore $AB$ stays parallel to $O_1O_2$ and the rod translates without rotating. With $\varphi$ the angle between $O_1A$ and the vertical, all the points of the rod have the velocity of $A$, of magnitude $L\,|\dot{\varphi}|$, hence $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m L^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$. The center of mass has height $-L\cos\varphi$ with respect to the line $O_1O_2$, therefore $V = -m g L\cos\varphi$ and $V''(0) = m g L$. It follows that
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{m g L}{m L^2} = \frac{g}{L} ,
$$
independent of the length of the rod, as for a simple pendulum of length $L$.
