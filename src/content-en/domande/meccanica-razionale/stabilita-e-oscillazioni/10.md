The uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the fixed end $A$ and moves in a vertical plane. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and zero natural length connects the end $B$ to the fixed point $D$, placed at the same height as $A$ and at distance $\ell$ from it, as the diagram in the figure shows. Calling $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the downward vertical, which condition identifies the stable equilibrium configuration?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;B,1.72,0.54;D,3,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" linee="1.72,0.54 1.78,1.20 2.33,1.16 1.98,1.59 2.53,1.56 2.19,1.98 2.74,1.95 2.39,2.38 2.94,2.34 3.00,3.00" tratteggi="0,3 0,0.8;0,3 3,3" punti="0.86,1.77,G,baricentro" forze="0.86 1.77,0,-0.8,mg" etichette="0.4,2,ϑ;2.75,1.45,k" titolo="The rod hinged at $A$ with the spring between $B$ and the fixed point $D$"}

- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{k \ell}{m g}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{2 k \ell}{m g}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{2 k \ell}$
- $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{k \ell}{2 m g}$

## Explanation

With the origin at $A$ we have $B - A = \ell\,(\sin\vartheta,\ -\cos\vartheta)$ and $D - A = (\ell,\ 0)$, hence $\overline{BD}^2 = 2\ell^2\,(1 - \sin\vartheta)$ and the potential of the system is
$$
U(\vartheta) = \frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta + k \ell^2 \sin\vartheta - k \ell^2 .
$$
From the condition $U'(\vartheta) = -\dfrac{m g \ell}{2}\sin\vartheta + k \ell^2\cos\vartheta = 0$ it follows that $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{2 k \ell}{m g}$, which identifies the two configurations $\vartheta_1 \in \left( 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$ and $\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1 + \pi$. Since $V''(\vartheta_1) = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\sqrt{m^2 g^2 + 4 k^2 \ell^2} > 0$, the configuration $\vartheta_1$ is a strict minimum of the potential energy and it is stable, while $\vartheta_2$ is unstable.
