A point particle of mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides without friction on a horizontal guide, connected to an ideal spring of stiffness $k = 36\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and to a viscous damper of constant $h = 12\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}$. What is the pseudo-period of the oscillations?

- $2.09\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.87\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.42\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.19\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The equation of motion $m\,\ddot{x} + h\,\dot{x} + k\,x = 0$ is written in the form $\ddot{x} + 2\beta\,\dot{x} + \omega_0^2\,x = 0$ with $\beta = \dfrac{h}{2m} = 1.5\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ and $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Since $\beta < \omega_0$, the motion is a damped oscillation and the pseudo angular frequency equals
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \beta^2} = \sqrt{6.75} \simeq 2.60\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The pseudo-period is therefore $\mathcal{T} = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega} \simeq 2.42\,\mathrm{s}$, greater than the period $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega_0} \simeq 2.09\,\mathrm{s}$ that the system would have without the damper.
