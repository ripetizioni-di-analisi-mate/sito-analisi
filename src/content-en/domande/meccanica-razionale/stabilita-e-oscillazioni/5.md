A uniform disk of mass $M$ and radius $R$ rotates without friction about its fixed center $O$, in a vertical plane. A point particle of mass $m$ is welded to the boundary of the disk, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the period of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2,foro" punti="0,0,O,baricentro;1.41,-1.41,P,pieno" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2;0,0 1.41,-1.41" etichette="0.3,-1.15,ϑ;1.7,-1.65,m" titolo="The disk hinged at $O$ with the point particle welded at the point $P$ of the boundary"}

- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{R}{g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{R\,(M + 2m)}{m g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{R\,(M + m)}{2 m g}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{R\,(M + 2m)}{2 m g}}$

## Explanation

Calling $\vartheta$ the angle between $OP$ and the downward vertical, the center of mass of the disk stays at $O$, hence the potential energy comes from the point particle alone and equals $V(\vartheta) = -m g R\cos\vartheta$, with $V''(0) = m g R > 0$. The moment of inertia of the system with respect to the axis through $O$ is the sum of that of the disk and that of the particle, therefore
$$
I_O = \frac{1}{2}\,M R^2 + m R^2 = \frac{R^2\,(M + 2m)}{2} .
$$
From $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_O\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ it follows that $\omega^2 = \dfrac{m g R}{I_O} = \dfrac{2 m g}{R\,(M + 2m)}$, hence the period is
$$
\mathcal{T} = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{R\,(M + 2m)}{2 m g}} .
$$
