The potential energy of a conservative holonomic system with one degree of freedom, with fixed and ideal constraints, is $V(q) = q^3$. How is the equilibrium configuration $q = 0$ classified?

- Stable, since $V''(0) = 0$
- Unstable, since $q = 0$ is not a minimum of $V$
- Stable, since $V(0) = 0$
- Of neutral equilibrium

## Explanation

From $V'(q) = 3q^2$ it follows that $V'(0) = 0$, therefore $q = 0$ is an equilibrium configuration, and from $V''(q) = 6q$ it follows that $V''(0) = 0$, hence the second derivative criterion does not apply. For $q < 0$ we have $V(q) < 0 = V(0)$, therefore in every neighborhood of $0$ the potential energy takes values smaller than $V(0)$ and the configuration is not a minimum. The hypothesis of the Lagrange-Dirichlet theorem is not satisfied, and by the Lyapunov instability theorems the equilibrium is unstable.
