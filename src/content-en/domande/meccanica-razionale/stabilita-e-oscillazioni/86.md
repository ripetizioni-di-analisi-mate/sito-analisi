A conservative holonomic system with two degrees of freedom has, in the equilibrium configuration, kinetic matrix and Hessian matrix of the potential energy
$$
\mathbb{A} = m
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 0 \\
0 & 1
\end{bmatrix} ,
\qquad
\mathbb{V} = k
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & -1 \\
-1 & 2
\end{bmatrix} ,
$$
and the vector $u = (1, 1)$ is a normal mode. What is the square of the natural angular frequency of that mode?

- $\dfrac{3k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{5k}{2m}$
- $\dfrac{k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{5k}{3m}$

## Explanation

Since $u$ is a normal mode, $(\mathbb{V} - \omega^2\mathbb{A})\,u = 0$ holds; taking the scalar product with $u$ one therefore obtains
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{u \cdot \mathbb{V}\,u}{u \cdot \mathbb{A}\,u} .
$$
We have $u \cdot \mathbb{V}\,u = k\,(3 - 1 - 1 + 2) = 3k$ and $u \cdot \mathbb{A}\,u = 3m$, therefore $\omega^2 = \dfrac{k}{m}$. Indeed $\mathbb{V}\,u = k\,(2, 1)$ and $\mathbb{A}\,u = m\,(2, 1)$ are proportional with factor $\dfrac{k}{m}$. The other natural angular frequency, given by the characteristic equation, equals $\dfrac{5k}{2m}$ and belongs to the second mode.
