A uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the fixed end $A$ and moves in a vertical plane. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and zero natural length connects the midpoint $M$ of the rod to the fixed point $D$, placed on the vertical through $A$ at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ below $A$, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the square of the angular frequency of the small oscillations about $\vartheta = 0$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;B,1.99,1.33" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" linee="0,1.7 0.13,1.76 0.11,1.91 0.32,1.7 0.3,2.0 0.51,1.79 0.48,2.08 0.69,1.87 0.67,2.17 0.88,1.96 0.86,2.11 0.99,2.17" tratteggi="0,3 0,1.2" punti="0,1.7,D;0.99,2.17,M,baricentro" etichette="0.4,1.35,ϑ;0.3,2.4,k" titolo="The rod hinged at $A$ with the spring between the midpoint $M$ and the fixed point $D$"}

- $\dfrac{3g}{2\ell} + \dfrac{3k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{3g}{2\ell} + \dfrac{3k}{2m}$
- $\dfrac{g}{\ell} + \dfrac{k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{3g}{2\ell} + \dfrac{3k}{4m}$

## Explanation

With $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the downward vertical we have $M - D = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,(\sin\vartheta,\ 1 - \cos\vartheta)$, hence $\overline{DM}^2 = \dfrac{\ell^2}{2}\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)$ and the potential is
$$
U(\vartheta) = \left( \frac{m g \ell}{2} + \frac{k \ell^2}{4} \right)\cos\vartheta + \text{constant} .
$$
It follows that $V''(0) = \dfrac{m g \ell}{2} + \dfrac{k \ell^2}{4} > 0$, therefore $\vartheta = 0$ is one of stable equilibrium. Since $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{m \ell^2}{3}\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$, the inertia coefficient equals $\dfrac{m \ell^2}{3}$ and we obtain
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{\dfrac{m g \ell}{2} + \dfrac{k \ell^2}{4}}{\dfrac{m \ell^2}{3}} = \frac{3g}{2\ell} + \frac{3k}{4m} .
$$
