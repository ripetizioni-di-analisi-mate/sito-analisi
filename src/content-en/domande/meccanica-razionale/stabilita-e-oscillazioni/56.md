A holonomic system with two degrees of freedom, with fixed, bilateral and ideal constraints, is subject to a conservative loading of potential energy $V(q_1, q_2) = k\,\big( q_1^2 + q_2^4 \big)$, with $k > 0$. How is the equilibrium configuration $(0, 0)$ classified?

- Stable, since $(0, 0)$ is a strict minimum of $V$
- Unstable, since the Hessian matrix of $V$ is singular
- The Lagrange-Dirichlet theorem does not apply, since the Hessian matrix is not positive definite
- Stable only for the motions with $q_2 = 0$

## Explanation

The gradient $\big( 2k q_1,\ 4k q_2^3 \big)$ vanishes at $(0, 0)$, hence the origin is an equilibrium configuration. The Hessian matrix equals
$$
\mathbb{V} =
\begin{bmatrix}
2k & 0 \\
0 & 0
\end{bmatrix} ,
$$
which is semidefinite and singular, therefore the Hessian criterion does not allow a conclusion. The Lagrange-Dirichlet theorem, however, requires only a strict minimum, and $V(q_1, q_2) > 0 = V(0, 0)$ for every $(q_1, q_2) \neq (0, 0)$. The hypothesis of the theorem is therefore satisfied and the equilibrium is **stable**.
