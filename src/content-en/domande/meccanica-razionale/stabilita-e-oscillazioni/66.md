A uniform semicircular plate of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rests with its curved edge on a fixed horizontal plane and can rock by rolling without slipping, in a vertical plane, as the diagram in the figure shows. The center of mass $G$ lies on the radius of symmetry at distance $\dfrac{4R}{3\pi}$ from the center $O$. What is the square of the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration?

::struttura{poligoni="-1.500,1.500 -1.449,1.112 -1.299,0.750 -1.061,0.439 -0.750,0.201 -0.388,0.051 0,0 0.388,0.051 0.750,0.201 1.061,0.439 1.299,0.750 1.449,1.112 1.500,1.500" linee="-2.6,0 2.6,0" punti="0,1.5,O;0,0.863,G,baricentro;0,0,H,centro" quote="0 1.5,1.5 1.5,R,-0.35" titolo="The semicircular plate resting on the plane with its curved edge"}

- $\dfrac{8\,g}{3\pi R}$
- $\dfrac{8\,g}{(9\pi - 16)\,R}$
- $\dfrac{8\,g}{9\pi R}$
- $\dfrac{4\,g}{3\pi R}$

## Explanation

Let $d = \dfrac{4R}{3\pi}$. In the equilibrium configuration the diameter is horizontal and the contact point $H$ lies on the vertical through $O$ and $G$. During rolling the center $O$ stays at height $R$, hence the height of $G$ is $R - d\cos\vartheta$, with $\vartheta$ the rotation angle of the plate, and $V''(0) = m g d$. At $\vartheta = 0$ the act of motion is a rotation about $H$, therefore the inertia coefficient is the moment of inertia about $H$. The moment about $O$ equals $\dfrac{m R^2}{2}$, hence by the Huygens-Steiner theorem $I_G = \dfrac{m R^2}{2} - m d^2$ and
$$
I_H = I_G + m\,(R - d)^2 = m\left( \frac{3R^2}{2} - 2Rd \right) .
$$
It follows that
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{g d}{\dfrac{3R^2}{2} - 2Rd} = \frac{8\,g}{(9\pi - 16)\,R} .
$$
