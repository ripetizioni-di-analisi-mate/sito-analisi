A uniform disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged at a point $A$ of its boundary and oscillates in a vertical plane about the horizontal axis through $A$. What is the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2g}{R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2g}{3R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{2R}}$

## Explanation

The axis of rotation through $A$ is parallel to the axis through the center of mass, therefore the **Huygens-Steiner** theorem applies and gives
$$
I_A = \frac{1}{2}\,m R^2 + m R^2 = \frac{3}{2}\,m R^2 .
$$
Calling $\vartheta$ the angle between $AG$ and the downward vertical, the potential energy is $V(\vartheta) = -m g R\cos\vartheta$, hence $V''(0) = m g R > 0$ and the configuration with the center below $A$ is one of stable equilibrium. The kinetic energy equals $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_A\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$, therefore
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{m g R}{\dfrac{3}{2}\,m R^2} = \frac{2g}{3R} .
$$
