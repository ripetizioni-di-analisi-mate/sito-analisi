A uniform rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the point $A$, placed at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{4}$ from the center of mass, and oscillates in a vertical plane about the horizontal axis through $A$. What is the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3 g}{\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{12 g}{7\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{3 g}{2\ell}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{4 g}{7\ell}}$

## Explanation

The axis of rotation through $A$ is parallel to the axis through the center of mass, therefore the **Huygens-Steiner** theorem applies and gives
$$
I_A = \frac{m \ell^2}{12} + m\left( \frac{\ell}{4} \right)^2 = \frac{7}{48}\,m \ell^2 .
$$
Calling $\vartheta$ the angle between $AG$ and the downward vertical, the potential energy is $V(\vartheta) = -\dfrac{m g \ell}{4}\cos\vartheta$, hence $V''(0) = \dfrac{m g \ell}{4} > 0$ and the configuration with the center of mass below $A$ is stable. From $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I_A\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$ it follows that
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{\dfrac{m g \ell}{4}}{\dfrac{7}{48}\,m \ell^2} = \frac{12 g}{7\ell} .
$$
