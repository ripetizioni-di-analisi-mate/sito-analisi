A physical pendulum of mass $m$ and pivot inertia $I_O$ has its centre of mass a distance $d$ from the pivot. A torsional spring of stiffness $k_t$ is relaxed when the centre is **above** the pivot. Under what condition does linearisation about this upright position give oscillations?

- $0<k_t<mgd$
- $k_t>mgd$
- $k_t=mgd$
- Any $k_t>0$

## Explanation

Near the upright position gravity increases the angle while the spring restores it. The linear equation is $I_O\ddot\theta+(k_t-mgd)\theta=0$, giving a real, nonzero oscillation frequency when the net stiffness is positive.
