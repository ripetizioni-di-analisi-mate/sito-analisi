A conservative holonomic system with two degrees of freedom has, in the equilibrium configuration, kinetic matrix and Hessian matrix of the potential energy
$$
\mathbb{A} = m
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
0 & 4
\end{bmatrix} ,
\qquad
\mathbb{V} = k
\begin{bmatrix}
5 & 0 \\
0 & 8
\end{bmatrix} .
$$
What is the smaller natural angular frequency?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{5k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{8k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{2m}}$

## Explanation

Since the two matrices are diagonal, the characteristic equation is
$$
\det\big( \mathbb{V} - \omega^2\mathbb{A} \big) = \big( 5k - \omega^2 m \big)\big( 8k - 4\omega^2 m \big) = 0 .
$$
Its roots are $\omega^2 = \dfrac{5k}{m}$ and $\omega^2 = \dfrac{2k}{m}$, both positive because $\mathbb{A}$ and $\mathbb{V}$ are positive definite. The smaller natural angular frequency is therefore $\sqrt{\dfrac{2k}{m}}$, and it corresponds to the second coordinate, which has inertia four times greater than the first one and stiffness less than twice as great.
