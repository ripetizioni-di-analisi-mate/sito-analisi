A point particle of mass $m$ hangs from the fixed point $O$ by an ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and natural length $\ell_0$, and moves in a vertical plane (elastic pendulum). The distance $r$ from $O$ and the angle $\varphi$ between the spring and the downward vertical are taken as coordinates. What is the square of the natural angular frequency of the pendulum mode, in which only $\varphi$ varies?

- $\dfrac{g k}{k\ell_0 + m g}$
- $\dfrac{g}{\ell_0}$
- $\dfrac{k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{g}{\ell_0} + \dfrac{k}{m}$

## Explanation

The potential energy is $V = \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(r - \ell_0)^2 - m g r\cos\varphi$, whose gradient vanishes at $\varphi = 0$ and $r_0 = \ell_0 + \dfrac{m g}{k}$. The kinetic energy is $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\big( \dot{r}^2 + r^2\dot{\varphi}^2 \big)$, hence in the equilibrium configuration
$$
\mathbb{A} =
\begin{bmatrix}
m & 0 \\
0 & m r_0^2
\end{bmatrix} ,
\qquad
\mathbb{V} =
\begin{bmatrix}
k & 0 \\
0 & m g r_0
\end{bmatrix} .
$$
The two matrices are diagonal, therefore $r$ and $\varphi$ are already normal coordinates, with $\omega_1^2 = \dfrac{k}{m}$ for the vibration of the spring and
$$
\omega_2^2 = \frac{m g r_0}{m r_0^2} = \frac{g}{r_0} = \frac{g k}{k\ell_0 + m g}
$$
for the pendulum oscillation, as for a simple pendulum as long as the spring stretched by the weight.
