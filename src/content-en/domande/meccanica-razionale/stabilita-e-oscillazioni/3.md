A uniform disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a plane inclined at the angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$, parallel to the inclined plane, connects the center $C$ of the disk to a fixed point of the plane placed higher up, and it is unloaded in the initial position of $C$, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the angular frequency of the small oscillations?

::struttura{cerchi="2.087,1.826,0.7,foro" linee="0,0 5.4,2.7;0,0 5.4,0;5.4,0 5.4,2.7;2.09,1.83 2.24,2.15 2.69,1.88 2.74,2.40 3.19,2.13 3.24,2.65 3.69,2.38 3.74,2.90 4.09,2.83" punti="4.087,2.826,D;2.087,1.826,C,baricentro;2.4,1.2,H,centro" etichette="0.75,0.12,α;2.86,2.77,k" titolo="The disk rolling on the inclined plane, connected by the spring to the fixed point $D$"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2k}{3m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{2m}}$

## Explanation

With $s$ the abscissa of $C$ along the plane, positive downward and measured from the position with the spring unloaded, the potential is $U(s) = m g s \sin\alpha - \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,s^2$, hence the equilibrium falls at $s_0 = \dfrac{m g \sin\alpha}{k}$ and $V''(s) = k$. The condition of pure rolling ties the angular velocity of the disk to $\dot{s}$ according to $\dot{\varphi} = \dfrac{\dot{s}}{R}$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{s}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\,m R^2 \cdot \frac{\dot{s}^2}{R^2} = \frac{3}{4}\,m\,\dot{s}^2
$$
and the inertia coefficient equals $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m$. Then $\omega^2 = \dfrac{k}{\dfrac{3}{2}\,m} = \dfrac{2k}{3m}$, hence the angular frequency is $\sqrt{\dfrac{2k}{3m}}$ and it does not depend on the inclination of the plane.
