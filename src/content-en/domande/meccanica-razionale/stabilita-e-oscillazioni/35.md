A conservative holonomic system with one degree of freedom has potential energy $V(\vartheta) = -m g \ell\cos\vartheta + \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,\ell^2\sin^2\vartheta$, with $\vartheta \in (-\pi, \pi]$. Which condition on $k$ is necessary and sufficient for equilibrium configurations other than $\vartheta = 0$ and $\vartheta = \pi$ to exist?

- $k < \dfrac{m g}{\ell}$
- $k > \dfrac{2 m g}{\ell}$
- $k > \dfrac{m g}{2\ell}$
- $k > \dfrac{m g}{\ell}$

## Explanation

From $V'(\vartheta) = \ell\,\sin\vartheta\,(m g + k \ell\cos\vartheta)$ it follows that the equilibrium configurations are $\vartheta = 0$, $\vartheta = \pi$ and those that satisfy $\cos\vartheta = -\dfrac{m g}{k \ell}$. The last equation admits solutions other than $0$ and $\pi$ when $\dfrac{m g}{k \ell} < 1$, that is, when $k > \dfrac{m g}{\ell}$, and in that case the solutions are two, symmetric with respect to the vertical. The value $k = \dfrac{m g}{\ell}$ is therefore a bifurcation value, since crossing it the number of the equilibrium configurations changes.
