A uniform rigid plank of thickness $2h$ rests horizontally on top of a fixed cylinder of radius $R$ with horizontal axis, with its center of mass on the vertical through the contact point, and it can roll without slipping on the cylinder while staying in a vertical plane, as the diagram in the figure shows. For which values of $h$ is the configuration one of stable equilibrium?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.5" poligoni="-2.6,1.5 2.6,1.5 2.6,1.9 -2.6,1.9" punti="0,0,O;0,1.7,G,baricentro" quote="2.6 1.5,2.6 1.9,2h,0.4;0 0,-1.06 -1.06,R,0.3" titolo="The plank of thickness $2h$ resting on the fixed cylinder of radius $R$"}

- $h > R$
- $h < \dfrac{R}{2}$
- $h < 2R$
- $h < R$

## Explanation

We take as coordinate the angle $\vartheta$ by which the plank rotates. The contact point moves on the cylinder up to the angle $\vartheta$ from the vertical and, by pure rolling, the center of mass lies at distance $R\vartheta$ from the new contact point along the plank; its height with respect to the center of the cylinder therefore equals
$$
y_G(\vartheta) = (R + h)\cos\vartheta + R\,\vartheta\sin\vartheta .
$$
We have $y_G'(\vartheta) = -h\sin\vartheta + R\,\vartheta\cos\vartheta$, which vanishes at $\vartheta = 0$, and
$$
y_G''(0) = R - h .
$$
The potential energy is $V = m g\,y_G$, hence $V''(0) = m g\,(R - h)$ and the equilibrium is **stable** for $h < R$.
