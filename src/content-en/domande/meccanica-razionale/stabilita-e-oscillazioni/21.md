A conservative holonomic system starts from the equilibrium configuration $q_0$ with a large initial generalized velocity, and in the subsequent motion it moves far away from $q_0$. What is concluded about the stability of $q_0$?

- That $q_0$ is unstable
- That $q_0$ is not an equilibrium configuration
- That $q_0$ is stable, since the motion starts from an equilibrium configuration
- Nothing, since the definition concerns initial data that are small in the velocities as well

## Explanation

The definition of stability in the sense of Lyapunov imposes a condition only on the motions whose initial data satisfy $|q(0) - q_0| < \delta$ and $|\dot{q}(0)| < \delta$, therefore it concerns displacements that are small both in the coordinates and in the velocities. A motion that starts from $q_0$ with a large velocity does not belong to that family, hence its behavior does not contradict the definition. To conclude instability one must exhibit motions that move away although their initial data are arbitrarily close to $q_0$ and their initial velocities are arbitrarily small.
