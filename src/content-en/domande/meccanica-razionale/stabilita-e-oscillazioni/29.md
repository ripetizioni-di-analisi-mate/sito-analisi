In an equilibrium configuration of a conservative holonomic system with two degrees of freedom the Hessian matrix of the potential energy equals
$$
\mathbb{V} =
\begin{bmatrix}
3k & -2k \\
-2k & k
\end{bmatrix} ,
$$
with $k > 0$. How is the configuration classified?

- Stable, since $V_{11} > 0$
- Stable, since the trace of $\mathbb{V}$ is positive
- The Hessian criterion does not allow a conclusion
- Unstable, since $\det\mathbb{V} < 0$

## Explanation

We have $\det\mathbb{V} = 3k^2 - 4k^2 = -k^2 < 0$, and the determinant of a symmetric matrix is the product of its eigenvalues, therefore the two eigenvalues have opposite signs. The matrix then admits a negative eigenvalue and the configuration is not a minimum of the potential energy. The Hessian criterion hence ensures that the equilibrium is unstable, and the sign of the first entry or of the trace does not change the conclusion.
