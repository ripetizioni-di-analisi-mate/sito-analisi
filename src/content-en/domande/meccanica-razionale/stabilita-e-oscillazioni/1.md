A collar $P$ of mass $m$ slides without friction on a fixed straight guide, inclined at the angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and natural length $\ell_0$ connects $P$ to the fixed point $O$ of the guide, placed higher up, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the equilibrium configuration?

::struttura{nodi="O,3.4,1.7,-;P,1,0.5,-" linee="0,0 5,2.5;1.00,0.50 1.43,0.96 1.82,0.66 1.81,1.15 2.20,0.86 2.20,1.34 2.59,1.05 2.58,1.54 2.97,1.24 3.40,1.70" punti="3.4,1.7,O;1,0.5,P,pieno" forze="1 0.5,0,-0.8,mg" etichette="0.75,0.1,α;2.6,1.75,k" titolo="The collar $P$ on the inclined guide, drawn by the spring toward the fixed point $O$"}

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k\sin\alpha}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2k}{3m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g\sin\alpha}{\ell_0}}$

## Explanation

With $s$ the distance of $P$ from $O$ along the guide, measured downward, the potential of the weight is $m g s \sin\alpha$ and that of the spring is $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(s - \ell_0)^2$, therefore $U(s) = m g s \sin\alpha - \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(s - \ell_0)^2$. From the condition $U'(s) = 0$ it follows that $s_0 = \ell_0 + \dfrac{m g \sin\alpha}{k}$, and since $V''(s) = k > 0$ the configuration is a strict minimum of the potential energy, hence it is stable. The kinetic energy is $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{s}^2$, therefore the inertia coefficient equals $m$ and we obtain $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$. The weight produces only a shift of the equilibrium configuration, hence the inclination of the guide does not appear in the angular frequency.
