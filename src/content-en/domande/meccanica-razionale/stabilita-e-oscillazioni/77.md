A point particle $P$ of mass $m$ slides on a smooth fixed circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, placed in a horizontal plane. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and zero natural length connects $P$ to the fixed point $D$ of the plane, inside the guide, at distance $d$ from $O$. What is the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k d}{m R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k\,(R - d)}{m R}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k R}{m d}}$

## Explanation

The weight is perpendicular to the plane of the guide and does no work. With $\vartheta$ the angle between $OP$ and $OD$, the law of cosines gives $\overline{PD}^2 = R^2 + d^2 - 2Rd\cos\vartheta$, hence
$$
V(\vartheta) = \frac{1}{2}\,k\,\big( R^2 + d^2 - 2Rd\cos\vartheta \big) ,
$$
which has a minimum at $\vartheta = 0$, with $P$ at the point of the guide closest to $D$, and $V''(0) = kRd$. The inertia coefficient equals $m R^2$, therefore
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k R d}{m R^2}} = \sqrt{\frac{k d}{m R}} .
$$
