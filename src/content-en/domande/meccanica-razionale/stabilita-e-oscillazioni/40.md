In the equations of the small oscillations $\mathbb{A}\,\ddot{x} + \mathbb{V}\,x = 0$, written for the motions near an equilibrium configuration $q_0$, in which configuration is the kinetic matrix $\mathbb{A}$ computed?

- In any configuration, since the kinetic matrix is constant
- At $q_0$, since the terms that depend on the displacement are of higher order
- In the initial configuration of the motion
- In the configuration in which the potential energy is maximum

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is the quadratic form $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\dot{x} \cdot \mathbb{A}(q)\,\dot{x}$ and already contains a factor of the second order in the velocities. Substituting $\mathbb{A}(q) = \mathbb{A}(q_0) + O(|x|)$, the additional terms turn out to be of order higher than the second in the variables $x$ and $\dot{x}$, therefore they are neglected in the linearization. The kinetic matrix is hence computed in the equilibrium configuration $q_0$, and in the same configuration the Hessian matrix of the potential energy is computed.
