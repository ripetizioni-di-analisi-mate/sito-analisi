A holonomic system with one degree of freedom, with fixed, bilateral and ideal constraints, is subject to a conservative force system of potential energy $V(q) = m g \ell\,(q^3 - 3q)$, with $q \in \mathbb{R}$. What are the equilibrium configurations?

- $q = 1$ and $q = -1$
- $q = 0$
- $q = 0$ and $q = \pm\sqrt{3}$
- $q = \sqrt{3}$ and $q = -\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The equilibrium configurations of a conservative holonomic system with fixed and ideal constraints are the stationary points of the potential energy, hence they are found by solving $V'(q) = 0$. From $V'(q) = 3 m g \ell\,(q^2 - 1)$ it follows that the solutions are $q = 1$ and $q = -1$, therefore the system admits two equilibrium configurations. Since $V''(q) = 6 m g \ell\,q$, at $q = 1$ we have $V'' > 0$ and the equilibrium is stable, while at $q = -1$ we have $V'' < 0$ and the equilibrium is unstable.
