A point particle $P$ of mass $m$ slides without friction on a fixed vertical straight guide. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and zero natural length connects $P$ to the fixed point $O$ of the guide. Calling $y$ the abscissa of $P$ along the guide, measured from $O$ upward, what is the equilibrium configuration?

- $y = -\dfrac{m g}{2 k}$
- $y = -\dfrac{m g}{k}$
- $y = -\dfrac{2 m g}{k}$
- $y = 0$

## Explanation

The potential of the weight is $-m g y$ and that of the spring, whose length is $|y|$, is $-\dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,y^2$, therefore $U(y) = -m g y - \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,y^2$. From the condition $U'(y) = -m g - k y = 0$ it follows that $y = -\dfrac{m g}{k}$, hence the particle lies below $O$ at distance $\dfrac{m g}{k}$, equal to the static elongation of the spring. Since $V''(y) = k > 0$, the configuration is a strict minimum of the potential energy and the Lagrange-Dirichlet theorem ensures that the equilibrium is stable.
