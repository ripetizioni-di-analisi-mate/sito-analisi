A conservative holonomic system with one degree of freedom, with fixed, bilateral and ideal constraints, has potential energy $V(q) = q^4$. How is the equilibrium configuration $q = 0$ classified?

- Unstable, since $V''(0) = 0$
- The Lagrange-Dirichlet theorem does not allow a conclusion, since $V''(0) = 0$
- Stable only in the presence of a dissipation
- Stable, since $q = 0$ is a strict minimum of $V$

## Explanation

From $V'(q) = 4q^3$ it follows that $V'(0) = 0$, therefore $q = 0$ is an equilibrium configuration. Since $V(q) = q^4 > 0 = V(0)$ for every $q \neq 0$, the configuration is a strict relative minimum of the potential energy. The hypothesis of the Lagrange-Dirichlet theorem is therefore satisfied and the equilibrium is stable, even though the second derivative is zero and the second derivative criterion does not apply.
