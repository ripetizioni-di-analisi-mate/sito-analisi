A uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $R$, has its ends constrained to a smooth fixed circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, placed in a vertical plane, so that the rod is a chord of the guide, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the square of the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2,foro" nodi="A,-0.347,-1.970;B,1.532,-1.286" aste="A-B" punti="0,0,O;0.593,-1.628,G,baricentro" quote="A,B,R,0.35;0 0,-1.414 1.414,R,0.25" titolo="The rod $AB$, as long as the radius, with its ends on the circular guide"}

- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}\,g}{3R}$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}\,g}{5R}$
- $\dfrac{6\sqrt{3}\,g}{13R}$
- $\dfrac{12\,g}{13R}$

## Explanation

The triangle $OAB$ is equilateral, hence the distance of $O$ from the center of mass $G$, midpoint of $AB$, equals $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,R$ and stays constant. The rod therefore rotates about the fixed axis through $O$, and with $\vartheta$ the angle between $OG$ and the downward vertical we have $V(\vartheta) = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g R\cos\vartheta$, with minimum at $\vartheta = 0$ and $V''(0) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g R$. By the Huygens-Steiner theorem
$$
I_O = \frac{m R^2}{12} + m\,\frac{3R^2}{4} = \frac{5}{6}\,m R^2 ,
$$
hence
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,m g R}{\dfrac{5}{6}\,m R^2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}\,g}{5R} .
$$
