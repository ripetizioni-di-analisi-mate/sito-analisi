The linearised restoring force on a mass $m$ is $F=-(k-\alpha)x$, where a control parameter $\alpha$ approaches $k$ from below. What happens to the small-oscillation period and what limit of the model does this reveal?

- The period tends to zero and the linear stiffness grows.
- The period stays equal to the one based on $k$.
- The period grows without bound and at $\alpha=k$ the linear stiffness vanishes.
- The period is imaginary for every $\alpha<k$.

## Explanation

For $\alpha<k$, $\omega^2=(k-\alpha)/m>0$. As angular frequency tends to zero, $T=2\pi/\omega$ diverges. At the limit linearisation alone does not determine the effect of higher-order terms.
