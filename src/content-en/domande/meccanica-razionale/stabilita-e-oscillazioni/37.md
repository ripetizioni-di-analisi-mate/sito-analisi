A point particle of mass $m$ hangs from the fixed point $O$ by means of an ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and natural length $\ell_0$, and it moves along the vertical. What is the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the equilibrium configuration?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\ell_0}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m} + \dfrac{g}{\ell_0}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\ell_0 + \dfrac{m g}{k}}}$

## Explanation

Calling $s$ the distance of the particle from $O$, measured downward, the potential energy is $V(s) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(s - \ell_0)^2 - m g s$. From $V'(s) = k\,(s - \ell_0) - m g = 0$ it follows that $s_0 = \ell_0 + \dfrac{m g}{k}$, and the second derivative equals $V''(s) = k$ in every configuration, therefore the equilibrium is stable. Since $T = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{s}^2$, the inertia coefficient equals $m$ and we obtain $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$. The weight hence shifts the equilibrium configuration and does not modify the angular frequency, whatever the static elongation of the spring.
