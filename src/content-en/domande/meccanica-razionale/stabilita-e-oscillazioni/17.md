A uniform rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the fixed end $A$ and moves in a vertical plane; besides the weight, a constant couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = \dfrac{m g \ell}{4}$ acts, in the counterclockwise direction. Calling $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the downward vertical, measured counterclockwise, which equilibrium configuration falls between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$?

- $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{8}$
- $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$

## Explanation

A constant couple of moment $\mathcal{M}$ does the work $\mathcal{M}\,\delta\vartheta$ in a virtual displacement, therefore it admits the potential $\mathcal{M}\,\vartheta$. Adding the potential of the weight one obtains
$$
U(\vartheta) = \frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta + \frac{m g \ell}{4}\,\vartheta ,
$$
and from the condition $U'(\vartheta) = \dfrac{m g \ell}{4}\,(1 - 2\sin\vartheta) = 0$ it follows that $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The equilibrium configurations are hence $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\vartheta = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, and the only one between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ is $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
