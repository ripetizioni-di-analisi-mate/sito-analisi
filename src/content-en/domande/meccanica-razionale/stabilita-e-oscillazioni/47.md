Two uniform rods $AB$ and $BC$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $B$ and move in a vertical plane. The end $A$ is hinged to a fixed point and the end $C$ is a roller that slides without friction on the horizontal guide through $A$; an ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and zero natural length connects $A$ and $C$, as the diagram in the figure shows. With $\vartheta$ the angle between each rod and the guide, with $B$ above the guide, and assuming $4k\ell > m g$, which condition identifies the equilibrium configuration with $0 < \vartheta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.5,2;C,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" linee="-0.6,0 4,0;0.2,0 0.4,0.15 0.7,-0.15 1.0,0.15 1.3,-0.15 1.6,0.15 1.9,-0.15 2.2,0.15 2.5,-0.15 2.7,0 2.85,0" punti="0.75,1,G_1,baricentro;2.25,1,G_2,baricentro" etichette="0.75,0.35,ϑ;1.5,-0.5,k" titolo="The linkage with the spring between the hinge $A$ and the roller $C$"}

- $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{2 k \ell}$
- $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{k \ell}$
- $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{8 k \ell}$
- $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{4 k \ell}$

## Explanation

The triangle $ABC$ is isosceles, hence the centers of mass of the two rods are at height $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta$ and the distance between $A$ and $C$ equals $2\ell\cos\vartheta$. The potential is therefore
$$
U(\vartheta) = -m g \ell \sin\vartheta - 2k\ell^2\cos^2\vartheta ,
$$
and its derivative equals
$$
U'(\vartheta) = \ell\cos\vartheta\,\big( 4k\ell\sin\vartheta - m g \big) .
$$
For $0 < \vartheta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ we have $\cos\vartheta \neq 0$, hence the configuration is one of equilibrium if and only if $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{4 k \ell}$, a value smaller than $1$ by the hypothesis $4k\ell > m g$.
