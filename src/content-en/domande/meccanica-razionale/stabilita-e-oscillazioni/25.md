A conservative holonomic system with one degree of freedom, with fixed and ideal constraints, has potential energy $V(\vartheta) = m g \ell\,(\cos 2\vartheta - 2\cos\vartheta)$, with $\vartheta \in [0, \pi]$. Which equilibrium configuration is stable?

- $\vartheta = 0$
- $\vartheta = \pi$
- $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\vartheta = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

From $V'(\vartheta) = 2 m g \ell\,(\sin\vartheta - \sin 2\vartheta) = 2 m g \ell\,\sin\vartheta\,(1 - 2\cos\vartheta)$ it follows that the equilibrium configurations in $[0, \pi]$ are $\vartheta = 0$, $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $\vartheta = \pi$. The second derivative equals $V''(\vartheta) = 2 m g \ell\,(\cos\vartheta - 2\cos 2\vartheta)$, hence
$$
V''(0) = -2 m g \ell ,
\qquad
V''\!\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = 3 m g \ell ,
\qquad
V''(\pi) = -6 m g \ell .
$$
The only configuration with positive second derivative is $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, therefore by the second derivative criterion it is the only one of stable equilibrium, while the other two are of unstable equilibrium.
