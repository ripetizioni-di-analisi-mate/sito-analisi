Two uniform rods form a double pendulum in a vertical plane: the rod $OA$, of mass $2m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the fixed point $O$; the rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the end $A$ of the first one, as the diagram in the figure shows. The hinges are smooth. How many unstable equilibrium configurations does the system admit?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,4.4;A,1.37,2.5;B,3.58,1.71" aste="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" cerniere="A" tratteggi="0,4.4 0,2.6;1.37,2.5 1.37,1.1" punti="0.685,3.45,G_1,baricentro;2.47,2.11,G_2,baricentro" etichette="0.3,3.5,ϑ_1;1.65,1.7,ϑ_2;0.95,3.75,2m;2.6,2.45,m" titolo="The double pendulum formed by the rod $OA$ of mass $2m$ and the rod $AB$ of mass $m$"}

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

With $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ the angles of the two rods with respect to the downward vertical, the potential energy is
$$
V(\vartheta_1, \vartheta_2) = -2 m g \ell\cos\vartheta_1 - \frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta_2 ,
$$
therefore the gradient vanishes in the four configurations with $\vartheta_1, \vartheta_2 \in \{0, \pi\}$. The Hessian matrix is diagonal and equals
$$
\mathbb{V} = m g \ell
\begin{bmatrix}
2\cos\vartheta_1 & 0 \\[4pt]
0 & \dfrac{1}{2}\cos\vartheta_2
\end{bmatrix} ,
$$
hence its eigenvalues are both positive only at $(0, 0)$, which is the only stable configuration. In the other three configurations at least one eigenvalue is negative, therefore the unstable equilibrium configurations are three.
