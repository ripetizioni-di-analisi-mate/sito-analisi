A uniform square plate of mass $m$ and side $a$ is hung by a vertex $O$ from a fixed hinge and oscillates in its own plane, which is vertical. What is the square of the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration?

- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}\,g}{a}$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}\,g}{2a}$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}\,g}{4a}$
- $\dfrac{3\,g}{2a}$

## Explanation

The center of mass $G$ is the center of the square, at distance $\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$ from the vertex $O$, hence with $\vartheta$ the angle between $OG$ and the downward vertical we have $V''(0) = m g\,\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$. The moment of inertia of the plate about the axis through $G$ perpendicular to the plane is $\dfrac{m a^2}{6}$, therefore by the Huygens-Steiner theorem
$$
I_O = \frac{m a^2}{6} + m\,\frac{a^2}{2} = \frac{2}{3}\,m a^2 .
$$
It follows that
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{m g\,\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}}{\dfrac{2}{3}\,m a^2} = \frac{3\sqrt{2}\,g}{4a} .
$$
