A uniform disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged at a point $A$ at distance $b$ from the center, with $0 < b \le R$, and oscillates in a vertical plane about the horizontal axis through $A$. For which value of $b$ is the period of the small oscillations a minimum?

- $\dfrac{R}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $\dfrac{R}{2}$
- $R$
- $\dfrac{R}{\sqrt{3}}$

## Explanation

By the Huygens-Steiner theorem the moment of inertia about $A$ equals $\dfrac{m R^2}{2} + m b^2$, and the center of mass lies at distance $b$ from $A$, hence $V''(0) = m g b$ and
$$
\omega^2(b) = \frac{g b}{\dfrac{R^2}{2} + b^2} .
$$
The period is a minimum where $\omega^2$ is a maximum. The derivative
$$
\frac{d\,\omega^2}{d b} = g\,\frac{\dfrac{R^2}{2} - b^2}{\left( \dfrac{R^2}{2} + b^2 \right)^2}
$$
is positive for $b < \dfrac{R}{\sqrt{2}}$ and negative for $b > \dfrac{R}{\sqrt{2}}$, therefore the period is a minimum for $b = \dfrac{R}{\sqrt{2}}$.
