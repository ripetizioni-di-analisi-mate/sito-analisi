A conservative holonomic system with one degree of freedom has potential energy $V(q) = m g \ell\left( \dfrac{q^4}{4} + \dfrac{\lambda\,q^2}{2} \right)$, where $\lambda$ is a dimensionless parameter. For $\lambda < 0$, which are the configurations of stable equilibrium?

- Only $q = 0$
- $q = 0$ and $q = \pm\sqrt{-\lambda}$
- $q = \pm\sqrt{-\lambda}$
- No configuration

## Explanation

The derivative $V'(q) = m g \ell\,q\,(q^2 + \lambda)$ vanishes at $q = 0$ and, for $\lambda < 0$, also at $q = \pm\sqrt{-\lambda}$. The second derivative is $V''(q) = m g \ell\,(3q^2 + \lambda)$, hence $V''(0) = m g \ell\,\lambda < 0$ and $V''\big( \pm\sqrt{-\lambda} \big) = -2\,m g \ell\,\lambda > 0$. Therefore the **stable** configurations are $q = \pm\sqrt{-\lambda}$, while $q = 0$ is unstable. For $\lambda > 0$ the only equilibrium configuration is $q = 0$, which is stable, and $\lambda = 0$ is a bifurcation value.
