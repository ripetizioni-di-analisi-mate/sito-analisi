Two equal uniform disks, of mass $m$ and radius $R$, roll without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide. The center of the first is connected to the left wall by a spring of stiffness $k$, the center of the second to the right wall by an equal spring, and the two centers are connected to each other by a third equal spring, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the square of the smaller natural angular frequency?

- $\dfrac{k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{2k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{4k}{3m}$
- $\dfrac{2k}{3m}$

## Explanation

Calling $x_1$ and $x_2$ the abscissas of the two centers measured from the equilibrium positions, the condition of pure rolling gives $T_i = \dfrac{3}{4}\,m\,\dot{x}_i^2$ for each disk, hence $\mathbb{A} = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,\mathbb{I}$. The elastic potential energy
$$
V = \frac{1}{2}\,k\,x_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,k\,x_2^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,k\,(x_1 - x_2)^2
$$
has Hessian matrix $\mathbb{V} = k\begin{bmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$, whose eigenvalues are $k$ and $3k$. The roots of the characteristic equation $\det\big( \mathbb{V} - \omega^2\mathbb{A} \big) = 0$ are therefore $\omega_1^2 = \dfrac{k}{\dfrac{3}{2}\,m} = \dfrac{2k}{3m}$ and $\omega_2^2 = \dfrac{3k}{\dfrac{3}{2}\,m} = \dfrac{2k}{m}$, and the smaller one is $\dfrac{2k}{3m}$, associated with the mode $(1,1)$ in which the central spring stays unloaded.
