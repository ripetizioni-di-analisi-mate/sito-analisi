Two equal uniform rods $AB$ and $CD$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to the ground at the ends $A$ and $C$, placed at the same height at distance $d$, and move in the vertical plane that contains them. The upper ends $B$ and $D$ are connected by an ideal spring of stiffness $k$, unloaded when the two rods are vertical, as the diagram in the figure shows. How is the equilibrium configuration with both rods vertical, with $B$ and $D$ at the top, classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,3,0;D,3,3" aste="A-B;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,cerniera" linee="-0.8,0 3.8,0;0,3 0.4,3 0.6,3.2 0.9,2.8 1.2,3.2 1.5,2.8 1.8,3.2 2.1,2.8 2.4,3.2 2.6,3 3,3" punti="0,1.5,G_1,baricentro;3,1.5,G_2,baricentro" etichette="1.5,3.55,k" quote="A,C,d,0.7;A,B,ℓ,-0.6" titolo="The two vertical rods hinged to the ground and the spring between the upper ends"}

- Stable if $k > \dfrac{m g}{4\ell}$
- Stable if $k > \dfrac{m g}{2\ell}$
- Unstable for every value of $k$
- Stable for every value of $k$

## Explanation

We take as coordinates the angles $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ that the two rods form with the upward vertical, positive in the same sense. The centers of mass have heights $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta_1$ and $\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta_2$, and to first order the distance between $B$ and $D$ equals $d + \ell\,(\vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1)$, hence the expansion of the potential energy up to second order is
$$
V = m g \ell - \frac{m g \ell}{4}\,\big( \vartheta_1^2 + \vartheta_2^2 \big) + \frac{1}{2}\,k\,\ell^2\,(\vartheta_2 - \vartheta_1)^2 + \cdots
$$
The first-degree term is zero, therefore the configuration is one of equilibrium, and the Hessian matrix equals
$$
\mathbb{V} =
\begin{bmatrix}
k\ell^2 - \dfrac{m g \ell}{2} & -k\ell^2 \\[8pt]
-k\ell^2 & k\ell^2 - \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}
\end{bmatrix} .
$$
The vector $u = (1, 1)$, which corresponds to the two rods rotated by the same angle with the spring undeformed, is an eigenvector of $\mathbb{V}$ with eigenvalue $-\dfrac{m g \ell}{2} < 0$. By the Hessian criterion the configuration is therefore **unstable** for every value of $k$. The spring stiffens only the mode $(1, -1)$, whose eigenvalue $2k\ell^2 - \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}$ is positive for $k > \dfrac{m g}{4\ell}$.
