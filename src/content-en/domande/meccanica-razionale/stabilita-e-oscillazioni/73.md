A uniform ring of mass $m$ and radius $R$, of negligible thickness, rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in a vertical plane. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$, horizontal and unloaded in the equilibrium configuration, connects to a fixed wall the point of the ring that is highest in that configuration. What is the square of the angular frequency of the small oscillations?

- $\dfrac{2k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{k}{2m}$
- $\dfrac{4k}{m}$
- $\dfrac{8k}{3m}$

## Explanation

Let $s$ be the displacement of the center from the equilibrium configuration. By pure rolling the contact point has zero velocity, hence the point of the ring attached to the spring, which lies at distance $2R$ from the contact point, moves horizontally by $2s$ to first order; therefore $V = \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,(2s)^2 + \cdots$ and $V''(0) = 4k$. The ring has moment of inertia $m R^2$ about its center and angular velocity of magnitude $\dfrac{|\dot{s}|}{R}$, hence
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{s}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,m R^2\,\frac{\dot{s}^2}{R^2} = m\,\dot{s}^2 ,
$$
and the inertia coefficient equals $2m$. It follows that
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{4k}{2m} = \frac{2k}{m} .
$$
