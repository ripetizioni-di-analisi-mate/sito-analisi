A uniform disk rotates without friction about its fixed center $O$, in a vertical plane, subject to the weight alone. Which configurations are equilibrium configurations?

- Only the configuration in which the center of mass is lowest
- All configurations, since the potential is constant
- No configuration
- Only the two configurations in which a given diameter is vertical

## Explanation

The center of mass of a uniform disk coincides with the center, which is fixed, therefore the height of the center of mass does not depend on the angle of rotation. The potential of the weight $U = -m g\,y_G$ is then constant and its derivative with respect to the Lagrangian coordinate is zero in every configuration. From the theorem on the stationarity of the potential it follows that every configuration is an equilibrium configuration, and an equilibrium of this kind is called neutral.
