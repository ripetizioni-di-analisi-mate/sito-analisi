A string of negligible mass, of length $3a$, is stretched between two fixed points at the same height with tension $S$, which is considered constant. Two point particles of mass $m$ are fixed on the string at the points that divide it into three equal parts and undergo small transverse displacements $y_1$ and $y_2$ in a vertical plane; the weight is negligible with respect to the tension, as the diagram in the figure shows. What is the square of the larger natural angular frequency?

::struttura{nodi="S1,0,1,-;S2,6,1,-" vincoli="S1,incastro,180;S2,incastro,0" linee="0,1 2,1.6 4,0.6 6,1" tratteggi="0,1 6,1" punti="2,1.6,m,pieno;4,0.6,m,pieno" frecce="2,1,2,1.55,;4,1,4,0.65," etichette="2.35,1.3,y_1;4.45,0.95,y_2;0.7,1.55,S" quote="0 1,2 1,a,0.9;2 1,4 1,a,0.9;4 1,6 1,a,0.9" titolo="The taut string with the two point particles displaced transversely"}

- $\dfrac{3S}{m a}$
- $\dfrac{S}{m a}$
- $\dfrac{2S}{m a}$
- $\dfrac{3S}{2 m a}$

## Explanation

A segment of string of length $a$, whose ends have transverse displacements differing by $\Delta$, is stretched by $\sqrt{a^2 + \Delta^2} - a = \dfrac{\Delta^2}{2a} + \cdots$, and the constant tension stores in it the energy $S\,\dfrac{\Delta^2}{2a}$. Summing over the three segments one obtains
$$
V = \frac{S}{2a}\,\big( y_1^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + y_2^2 \big) ,
$$
which coincides with the energy of three springs of stiffness $\dfrac{S}{a}$. With
$$
\mathbb{A} = m
\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} ,
\qquad
\mathbb{V} = \frac{S}{a}
\begin{bmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} ,
$$
the characteristic equation $\left( \dfrac{2S}{a} - m\omega^2 \right)^2 - \dfrac{S^2}{a^2} = 0$ gives $\omega_1^2 = \dfrac{S}{m a}$ and
$$
\omega_2^2 = \frac{3S}{m a} ,
$$
which belongs to the mode $(1, -1)$, in which the two particles move to opposite sides.
