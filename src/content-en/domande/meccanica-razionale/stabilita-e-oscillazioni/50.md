The uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the fixed end $A$ and moves in a vertical plane. An inextensible string of negligible mass is attached at $B$, passes over a smooth pulley of negligible size placed at the point $C$, on the vertical through $A$ at distance $\ell$ above $A$, and at its other end holds a body of mass $M < m$ that hangs vertically, as the diagram in the figure shows. With $\vartheta$ the angle between $AB$ and $AC$, which condition identifies the equilibrium configuration with $0 < \vartheta < \pi$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.82,1.03;C,0,3;K,-0.12,3,-;J,-0.12,1.9,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,cerniera,90" catene="B-C;K-J" poligoni="-0.42,1.3 0.18,1.3 0.18,1.9 -0.42,1.9" tratteggi="0,0 0,1.25" punti="1.41,0.515,G,baricentro" quote="A,C,ℓ,-0.9;A,B,ℓ,0.5" etichette="0.3,0.85,ϑ;0.45,1.6,M" titolo="The rod hinged at $A$, the string passing over the pulley $C$ and the hanging body of mass $M$"}

- $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{M}{m}$
- $\sin\dfrac{\vartheta}{2} = \dfrac{M}{m}$
- $\sin\dfrac{\vartheta}{2} = \dfrac{2M}{m}$
- $\cos\dfrac{\vartheta}{2} = \dfrac{M}{m}$

## Explanation

The triangle $ABC$ is isosceles with $\overline{AB} = \overline{AC} = \ell$, hence $\overline{BC} = 2\ell\sin\dfrac{\vartheta}{2}$. The part of the string hanging from $C$ is as long as the string minus $\overline{BC}$, therefore the height of the hanging body equals $\overline{BC}$ plus a constant, and the potential of the system is
$$
U(\vartheta) = -\frac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta - 2 M g \ell \sin\frac{\vartheta}{2} + \text{constant} .
$$
Since $\sin\vartheta = 2\sin\dfrac{\vartheta}{2}\cos\dfrac{\vartheta}{2}$, the derivative equals
$$
U'(\vartheta) = g\ell\cos\frac{\vartheta}{2}\left( m\sin\frac{\vartheta}{2} - M \right) .
$$
For $0 < \vartheta < \pi$ the factor $\cos\dfrac{\vartheta}{2}$ is positive, hence the configuration is one of equilibrium if and only if $\sin\dfrac{\vartheta}{2} = \dfrac{M}{m}$, a value smaller than $1$ since $M < m$.
