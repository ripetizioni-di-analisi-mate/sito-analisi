A conservative holonomic system with one degree of freedom has potential energy $V(x) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,x^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}\,b\,x^4$, with $k > 0$ and $b > 0$. How many unstable equilibrium configurations does the system admit?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

From $V'(x) = k x - b x^3 = -x\,(b x^2 - k)$ it follows that the equilibrium configurations are $x = 0$ and $x = \pm\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{b}}$, hence three in all. The second derivative equals $V''(x) = k - 3 b x^2$, therefore $V''(0) = k > 0$ while
$$
V''\!\left( \pm\sqrt{\frac{k}{b}} \right) = k - 3k = -2k < 0 .
$$
The equilibrium is then stable at the origin and unstable in the two symmetric configurations, which are two.
