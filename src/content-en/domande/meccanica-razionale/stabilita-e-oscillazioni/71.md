A point particle of mass $m$ moves without friction on a fixed inclined plane, which forms the angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal, and it is connected to the fixed point $O$ of the plane by a rigid rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$, hinged at $O$ and lying on the plane. What is the square of the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration?

- $\dfrac{g}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{g\sin\alpha}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{g\cos\alpha}{\ell}$
- $\dfrac{g\tan\alpha}{\ell}$

## Explanation

The particle stays on the plane, and the reaction of the plane, normal to the plane, does no work. With $\varphi$ the angle between the rod and the line of steepest descent pointing downward, the particle lies at distance $\ell\cos\varphi$ from $O$ along that line, hence its height with respect to $O$ equals $-\ell\sin\alpha\cos\varphi$. Therefore $V(\varphi) = -m g \ell\sin\alpha\cos\varphi$, with minimum at $\varphi = 0$ and $V''(0) = m g \ell\sin\alpha$. The inertia coefficient equals $m\ell^2$, hence
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{g\sin\alpha}{\ell} ,
$$
as for a simple pendulum in a gravitational field of magnitude $g\sin\alpha$.
