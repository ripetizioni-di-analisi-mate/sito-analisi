A slider passes through the origin along the $x$-axis with $x(t)=ut$ and $u>0$. Polar coordinates use $r\geq0$ and measure the angle from the positive $x$-axis. Which description agrees with the constant Cartesian velocity?

- $r=ut$ and the angle is always zero.
- $r=|ut|$ and the angle is always zero.
- $r=|ut|$ and the angle changes from $\pi$ to $0$, being undefined at the origin.
- $r=|ut|$ and the Cartesian velocity vanishes when the angle changes.

## Explanation

Before the crossing the slider lies on the negative half-axis and afterwards on the positive one. The radial direction reverses at the pole, but $dx/dt=u$ throughout the motion.
