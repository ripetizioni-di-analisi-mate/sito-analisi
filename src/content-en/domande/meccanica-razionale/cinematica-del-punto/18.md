A particle $P$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ with
$$
P - O = \ell\,e^{\lambda t}\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\,e^{-\lambda t}\,\hat{\jmath} ,
$$
where $\ell > 0$ and $\lambda > 0$ are constants. What is the minimum value of the magnitude of the velocity?

- $\ell\,\lambda$
- $2\,\ell\,\lambda$
- The magnitude of the velocity has no minimum
- $\ell\,\lambda\,\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Differentiating one obtains $\vec{v}_P = \ell\,\lambda\,e^{\lambda t}\,\hat{\imath} - \ell\,\lambda\,e^{-\lambda t}\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence
$$
|\vec{v}_P|^2 = \ell^2\lambda^2\left( e^{2\lambda t} + e^{-2\lambda t} \right) .
$$
The two summands have constant product equal to $1$, therefore their sum is minimum when they are equal, that is for $t = 0$, where it equals $2$. The minimum value of the magnitude of the velocity is then $\ell\,\lambda\,\sqrt{2}$ and it is attained at the point of coordinates $(\ell,\,\ell)$.
