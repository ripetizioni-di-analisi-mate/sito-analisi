A particle moves in the plane $Oxy$ with the polar coordinates
$$
r(t) = r_0\,e^{\omega t} ,
\qquad
\vartheta(t) = \omega t ,
$$
where $r_0 > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ are constants. What angle does the velocity form with the radial unit vector?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- The angle grows in time

## Explanation

The velocity in polar coordinates equals $\vec{v}_P = \dot{r}\,\hat{u}_r + r\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{u}_\vartheta$. From the given laws there follow $\dot{r} = \omega\,r_0e^{\omega t} = \omega\,r$ and $r\,\dot{\vartheta} = \omega\,r$, hence the two components are equal at every instant. The angle $\psi$ between the velocity and the radial unit vector then satisfies $\tan\psi = 1$, therefore it equals $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ at every point of the spiral.
