A particle $P$ describes the arc of cycloid of equations
$$
x(t) = R\,(\omega t - \sin\omega t) ,
\qquad
y(t) = R\,(1 - \cos\omega t) ,
\qquad 0 \le \omega t \le 2\pi ,
$$
with $R > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ constants. What is the radius of curvature at the instant $\omega t = \pi$?

- $4R$
- $R$
- $2R$
- $8R$

## Explanation

Differentiating one obtains $\dot{x} = R\,\omega\,(1 - \cos\omega t)$ and $\dot{y} = R\,\omega\sin\omega t$, hence at the instant $\omega t = \pi$ the velocity equals $2R\omega\,\hat{\imath}$. Differentiating a second time one gets $\ddot{x} = R\,\omega^2\sin\omega t$ and $\ddot{y} = R\,\omega^2\cos\omega t$, therefore at that instant the acceleration equals $-\,R\omega^2\,\hat{\jmath}$ and it is orthogonal to the velocity. Then $a_t = 0$ and $a_n = R\,\omega^2$, and from the relation $a_n = \dfrac{\dot{s}^2}{\rho}$ there follows
$$
\rho = \frac{4R^2\omega^2}{R\,\omega^2} = 4R .
$$
