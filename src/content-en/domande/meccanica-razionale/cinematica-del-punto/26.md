A particle moves in the plane $Oxy$ with $r(t) > 0$ and the transverse component of its acceleration is zero at every instant. Which statement is true?

- $\dot{\vartheta}$ is constant, hence the motion is uniform circular motion
- The velocity is entirely radial, hence the trajectory is a straight line through $O$
- The acceleration is zero, hence the motion is uniform rectilinear motion
- The quantity $r^2\,\dot{\vartheta}$ is constant, hence the areal velocity with respect to $O$ is constant

## Explanation

The transverse component of the acceleration is rewritten as
$$
r\,\ddot{\vartheta} + 2\,\dot{r}\,\dot{\vartheta} = \frac{1}{r}\,\frac{d}{dt}\big( r^2\,\dot{\vartheta} \big) ,
$$
as one checks by differentiating the product on the right. Since $r > 0$, the vanishing of the transverse component at every instant is equivalent to the constancy of $r^2\,\dot{\vartheta}$. The areal velocity with respect to $O$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,r^2\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$, therefore it is constant.
