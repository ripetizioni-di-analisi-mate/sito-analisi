A river with parallel banks flows with uniform velocity $V\,\hat{\imath}$ with respect to the banks. A boat leaves the point $A$ on one bank and moves with respect to the water with velocity of constant magnitude $u > V$; its motion with respect to the banks must follow the segment $AB$ orthogonal to the banks, as in the figure. What angle $\beta$ must the velocity of the boat with respect to the water form with the segment $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,2.5,0;B,2.5,2.4" linee="0,0 5,0;0,2.4 5,2.4" tratteggi="2.5,0 2.5,2.4" punti="2.5,0;2.5,2.4" frecce="0.4,1.2,1.5,1.2,V" riferimento=0 titolo="River with current of velocity $V$: the boat must go from $A$ to $B$ along the direction orthogonal to the banks"}

- $\tan\beta = \dfrac{V}{u}$, upstream
- $\sin\beta = \dfrac{V}{u}$, upstream
- $\cos\beta = \dfrac{V}{u}$, upstream
- $\sin\beta = \dfrac{V}{u}$, downstream

## Explanation

One takes the moving frame attached to the water, which translates with velocity $V\,\hat{\imath}$; its angular velocity is zero, hence $\vec{v}_a = V\,\hat{\imath} + \vec{v}_r$. Writing the relative velocity as $\vec{v}_r = u\,\big( -\sin\beta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\beta\,\hat{\jmath} \big)$, with $\beta$ measured from the direction $AB$ toward upstream, the component of the absolute velocity along $\hat{\imath}$ equals $V - u\sin\beta$. The absolute motion must be orthogonal to the banks, therefore this component is zero and
$$
\sin\beta = \frac{V}{u} ,
$$
which has a solution because $u > V$. The boat thus points upstream, and it crosses with velocity of magnitude $\sqrt{u^2 - V^2}$.
