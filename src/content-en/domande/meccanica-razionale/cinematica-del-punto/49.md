A particle $P$ moves in space with
$$
P - O = \ell\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} + \ell\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{k} ,
$$
where $\ell > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ are constants. Which statement about the trajectory is true?

- It is contained in the plane $z = 0$
- It is contained in the plane $z = x$
- It is contained in the plane $z = y$
- It is not contained in any plane, since $z$ varies with time

## Explanation

The equations of motion give $z(t) = \ell\cos(\omega t) = x(t)$ at every instant, hence every position of the particle satisfies the equation $z - x = 0$. Therefore the trajectory is contained in the plane $z = x$, which contains the $y$ axis and is inclined at $45^\circ$ to the plane $Oxy$. The coordinates $x$ and $y$ also satisfy $x^2 + y^2 = \ell^2$, hence the trajectory is the intersection of that plane with the cylinder of radius $\ell$ and axis $z$, that is, an ellipse.
