A disk of radius $R$ rolls without slipping on the $x$ axis, and its center $C$ advances with constant velocity $V\,\hat{\imath}$, with $V > 0$. What is the velocity of the point $P$ of the rim that is at the height of the center, ahead of it, as in the figure?

::struttura{cerchi="2,1,1" punti="2,1,C,centro;3,1,P" linee="-0.4,0 4.6,0" frecce="1.6,2.3,2.4,2.3,V" riferimento=0 titolo="Disk rolling without slipping with the center $C$ advancing with velocity $V$"}

- $V\,\hat{\imath} + V\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $V\,\hat{\imath} - V\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $2\,V\,\hat{\imath}$
- $V\,\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The moving frame with origin at $C$ and unit vectors parallel to the fixed ones translates with the center, hence $\vec{v}_P = V\,\hat{\imath} + \vec{v}_r$. With respect to this frame the point $P$ rotates about $C$; the rolling-without-slipping condition requires the contact point to have zero velocity, therefore the angular velocity of the disk is $-\,\dfrac{V}{R}\,\hat{k}$, clockwise. With $P - C = R\,\hat{\imath}$ one obtains
$$
\vec{v}_r = -\,\frac{V}{R}\,\hat{k} \wedge R\,\hat{\imath} = -\,V\,\hat{\jmath} ,
$$
hence $\vec{v}_P = V\,\hat{\imath} - V\,\hat{\jmath}$, of magnitude $V\sqrt{2}$, orthogonal to the segment joining $P$ with the contact point.
