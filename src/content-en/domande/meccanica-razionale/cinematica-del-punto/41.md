A platform rotates about the fixed $z$ axis with constant angular velocity of magnitude $\omega = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. A particle runs on a radial guide attached to the platform, in the plane $Oxy$, with relative velocity of magnitude $u = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed outward. What is the magnitude of the Coriolis acceleration?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The Coriolis acceleration equals $\vec{a}_c = 2\,\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_r$. The angular velocity is directed along $\hat{k}$ and the relative velocity belongs to the plane $Oxy$, hence the two vectors are orthogonal and the magnitude of their cross product equals $\omega\,u$. Therefore
$$
|\vec{a}_c| = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 12\ \mathrm{m/s^2} ,
$$
with the orientation of the transverse unit vector $\hat{u}_\vartheta$, since the particle moves away from the center.
