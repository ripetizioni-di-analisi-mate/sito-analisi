A rod rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about its fixed end $O$ with angular velocity of magnitude $\omega > 0$, and a bead $P$ slides along the rod moving away from $O$, as in the figure. At the instant at which $|P - O| = d$ the absolute velocity of $P$ forms an angle of $45^\circ$ with the rod. What is at that instant the magnitude of the velocity of $P$ relative to the rod?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;B,2.6,1.95" aste="O-B" vincoli="O,cerniera" punti="1.44,1.08,P" frecce="1.44,1.08,1.98,1.49,u" coppie="0.5 1.6,1,ω" quote="O,1.44 1.08,d" titolo="Bead sliding along a rod rotating about the fixed end $O$"}

- $\dfrac{\omega\,d}{2}$
- $\omega\,d$
- $\omega\,d\,\sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{\omega\,d}{\sqrt{2}}$

## Explanation

In the frame attached to the rod the relative velocity is directed along the rod and has magnitude $u$, while the transport velocity is orthogonal to the rod and has magnitude $\omega\,d$. The angle $\psi$ between the absolute velocity and the rod therefore satisfies $\tan\psi = \dfrac{\omega\,d}{u}$. Imposing $\psi = 45^\circ$, that is $\tan\psi = 1$, one obtains $u = \omega\,d$.
