A cylinder rotates about its own axis $z$, which is fixed, with constant angular velocity $\omega$. A particle $P$ moves up along a vertical guide attached to the cylinder, at distance $d$ from the axis, with constant relative velocity $u\,\hat{k}$, where $u > 0$. What is the trajectory of $P$ in the fixed frame?

- A vertical straight line
- The circle of radius $d$ with center on the axis
- A circular helix of radius $d$ and pitch $\dfrac{u}{\omega}$
- A circular helix of radius $d$ and pitch $\dfrac{2\pi u}{\omega}$

## Explanation

In the fixed frame the projection of $P$ on the plane $Oxy$ rotates about the axis with angular velocity $\omega$ staying at distance $d$, while the height grows with velocity $u$; hence
$$
P - O = d\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + d\sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} + \big( z_0 + u\,t \big)\,\hat{k} .
$$
This is a circular helix of radius $d$. The particle completes one turn in the time $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ and in that time it rises by $u\,\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$, therefore the pitch equals $\dfrac{2\pi u}{\omega}$. The absolute velocity is the sum of the vertical relative velocity $u\,\hat{k}$ and of the horizontal transport velocity of magnitude $\omega\,d$.
