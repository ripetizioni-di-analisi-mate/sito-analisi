A particle $P$ travels along a given trajectory with scalar velocity proportional to the arc length coordinate, $\dot{s} = \lambda\,s$, where $\lambda > 0$ is constant, and at the initial instant $s(0) = s_0 > 0$. At which instant does the arc length coordinate equal $2\,s_0$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\lambda}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\lambda}$
- $\dfrac{\ln 2}{\lambda}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2\,\lambda}$

## Explanation

The equation $\dot{s} = \lambda\,s$ is linear of the first order with constant coefficients, hence with the initial datum $s(0) = s_0$ the time law is
$$
s(t) = s_0\,e^{\lambda t} .
$$
Imposing $s_0\,e^{\lambda t} = 2\,s_0$ one obtains $e^{\lambda t} = 2$, that is $\lambda t = \ln 2$. Therefore the required instant is $t = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\lambda}$, which does not depend on $s_0$.
