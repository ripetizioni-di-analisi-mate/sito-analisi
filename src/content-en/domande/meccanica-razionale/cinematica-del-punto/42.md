A platform rotates about the fixed $z$ axis with constant angular velocity of magnitude $\omega$ and a particle $P$ is at rest with respect to the platform, at distance $d$ from the axis. What is the absolute acceleration of $P$?

- Zero, since the particle is at rest with respect to the platform
- Of magnitude $2\,\omega^2 d$, directed toward the axis
- Of magnitude $\omega^2 d$, directed toward the axis
- Of magnitude $\omega\,d$, directed toward the axis

## Explanation

The particle is at relative rest, hence $\vec{v}_r = \vec{0}$ and $\vec{a}_r = \vec{0}$, and the Coriolis acceleration $2\,\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_r$ is zero as well. There remains the transport acceleration alone, and choosing the moving origin $\Omega$ on the axis in the plane of the motion of $P$ the vector $P - \Omega$ is orthogonal to $\vec{\omega}$ and has magnitude $d$, therefore
$$
\vec{a}_\tau = \vec{\omega} \wedge \big( \vec{\omega} \wedge (P - \Omega) \big) = -\,\omega^2\,(P - \Omega) .
$$
The absolute acceleration then has magnitude $\omega^2 d$ and it is directed toward the axis of rotation.
