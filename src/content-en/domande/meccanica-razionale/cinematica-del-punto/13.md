A platform rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed center $O$ with constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. A slider $P$ runs on a radial guide attached to the platform, as in the figure, starting from $O$ with zero relative velocity and constant relative acceleration of magnitude $a_0$, so that $|P - O| = \dfrac{1}{2}\,a_0 t^2$. Denoting by $\hat{e}_1$ the unit vector of the guide, at what instant does the component of the absolute acceleration along $\hat{e}_1$ vanish?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" nodi="O,0,0;P,1.22,0.44" nomi=0 punti="0,0,O,centro;1.22,0.44,P" linee="0,0 1.88,0.68" frecce="1.22,0.44,1.78,0.64,v_r;1.22,0.44,1.0,1.05,a_c" coppie="-1.0 -0.9,1,ω" titolo="Slider running with constant relative acceleration on a radial guide of a rotating platform"}

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\omega}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\omega}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\omega}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2\,\omega}$

## Explanation

The relative acceleration equals $a_0\,\hat{e}_1$, while the transport acceleration, with $\vec{\omega}$ constant and $O$ fixed, equals $-\,\omega^2\,(P - O) = -\,\dfrac{1}{2}\,\omega^2a_0t^2\,\hat{e}_1$. The Coriolis acceleration equals $2\,\omega\,a_0t\,\hat{e}_2$, hence it is orthogonal to $\hat{e}_1$ and does not contribute to the component required. The component along $\hat{e}_1$ of the absolute acceleration is therefore
$$
a_0\left( 1 - \frac{\omega^2t^2}{2} \right) ,
$$
which vanishes at the instant $t^{*} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\omega}$.
