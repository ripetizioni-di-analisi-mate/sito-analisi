A particle moves with simple harmonic motion along the $x$ axis with amplitude $A$ and period $T$. What is the maximum value of the magnitude of the acceleration?

- $\dfrac{2\pi A}{T}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi^2 A}{T}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi^2 A}{T^2}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi^2 A}{T^2}$

## Explanation

In simple harmonic motion one has $\ddot{x} = -\,\omega^2 x$, hence the magnitude of the acceleration is $\omega^2\,|x|$ and it is maximum at the endpoints of the oscillation, where $|x| = A$. The period and the angular frequency are linked by $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$, therefore $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}$. Substituting one obtains the maximum value
$$
A\,\omega^2 = \frac{4\pi^2 A}{T^2} .
$$
