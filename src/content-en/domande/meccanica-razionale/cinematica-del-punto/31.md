A cart translates along the $x$ axis with constant acceleration of magnitude $a_0 > 0$. A particle $P$ moves with respect to the cart along a vertical guide attached to it, with constant relative acceleration of magnitude $a_0$. What is the magnitude of the absolute acceleration of $P$?

- $a_0$
- $a_0\,\sqrt{2}$
- $2\,a_0$
- $0$

## Explanation

The frame attached to the cart moves with translational motion, hence its angular velocity is zero and the Coriolis acceleration $2\,\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_r$ vanishes. The theorem on the composition of accelerations therefore reduces to $\vec{a}_a = \vec{a}_\Omega + \vec{a}_r$, since in a translational motion the transport acceleration coincides with that of the moving origin. The acceleration of the origin equals $a_0\,\hat{\imath}$ and the relative one equals $\pm\,a_0\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence the two vectors are orthogonal and one has
$$
|\vec{a}_a| = \sqrt{a_0^2 + a_0^2} = a_0\,\sqrt{2} .
$$
