A platform rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed center $O$ with constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. A slider $P$ runs on a radial guide attached to the platform, as in the figure, moving away from $O$ with relative velocity of constant magnitude $u > 0$. At the instant at which $|P - O| = d$, what is the magnitude of the absolute velocity of $P$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2.2" punti="0,0,O,centro;1.3,0.5,P" linee="0,0 2.05,0.79" frecce="1.3,0.5,1.78,0.68,u" coppie="-1.2 -1.1,1,ω" quote="0 0,1.3 0.5,d" titolo="Slider on a radial guide of a rotating platform"}

- $u + \omega\,d$
- $\omega\,d$
- $\sqrt{u^2 + \omega^2 d^2}$
- $u$

## Explanation

The theorem on the composition of velocities gives $\vec{v}_a = \vec{v}_\tau + \vec{v}_r$, where $\vec{v}_\tau = \vec{\omega} \wedge (P - O)$ since the center $O$ is fixed. The relative velocity is directed along the guide and has magnitude $u$, while the transport velocity is orthogonal to the guide and has magnitude $\omega\,d$. The two vectors are orthogonal, therefore
$$
|\vec{v}_a| = \sqrt{u^2 + \omega^2 d^2} .
$$
