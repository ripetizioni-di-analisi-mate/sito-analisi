A particle $P$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ with
$$
x(t) = \ell\,\omega t ,
\qquad
y(t) = \ell\,\omega^2 t^2 ,
$$
where $\ell > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ are constants. What is the trajectory of the particle?

- $y = \dfrac{x^2}{\ell}$
- $y = \dfrac{x^2}{2\,\ell}$
- $y = \dfrac{x^2}{\ell^2}$
- $y = \ell\,\omega\,x$

## Explanation

The trajectory is obtained by eliminating time between the two equations of motion. From the first one there follows $t = \dfrac{x}{\ell\,\omega}$, and substituting in the second one gets
$$
y = \ell\,\omega^2\,\frac{x^2}{\ell^2\omega^2} = \frac{x^2}{\ell} .
$$
Since $x$ grows from zero as time grows, the particle travels along the branch of the parabola $y = \dfrac{x^2}{\ell}$ with $x \ge 0$.
