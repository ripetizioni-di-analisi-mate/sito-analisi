A particle travels along the circle of radius $R = 0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ with constant angular velocity of magnitude $\omega = 4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity is constant, hence $\ddot{\vartheta} = 0$ and the tangential acceleration $R\,\ddot{\vartheta}$ is zero. The acceleration is therefore entirely normal and equals $a_n = R\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = R\,\omega^2$. Substituting the data one obtains $0.5 \cdot 16 = 8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with the acceleration directed toward the center of the circle.
