A particle moves on the $x$ axis with constant acceleration $a_0 > 0$, starting from the origin with velocity $v_0 > 0$. At which position does the scalar velocity equal $2\,v_0$?

- $\dfrac{3\,v_0^2}{2\,a_0}$
- $\dfrac{v_0^2}{2\,a_0}$
- $\dfrac{3\,v_0^2}{a_0}$
- $\dfrac{2\,v_0^2}{a_0}$

## Explanation

In uniformly accelerated rectilinear motion the relation $\dot{x}^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,a_0\,(x - x_0)$ holds, which here becomes $\dot{x}^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,a_0\,x$ since $x_0 = 0$. Imposing $\dot{x} = 2\,v_0$ one obtains $4\,v_0^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,a_0\,x$. Solving with respect to $x$ there follows
$$
x = \frac{3\,v_0^2}{2\,a_0} .
$$
