A particle moves in uniform rectilinear motion along the line $y = d$, with $d > 0$, and its velocity equals $u\,\hat{\imath}$, with $u > 0$. What is the areal velocity with respect to the pole $O$?

- $0$, since the motion is rectilinear
- $u\,d$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,u\,d$, constant
- It depends on time, since the distance from $O$ varies

## Explanation

The position of the particle is $P - O = x(t)\,\hat{\imath} + d\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the velocity is $u\,\hat{\imath}$, hence
$$
(P - O) \wedge \vec{v}_P = d\,u\,(\hat{\jmath} \wedge \hat{\imath}) = -\,d\,u\,\hat{k} .
$$
The areal velocity therefore equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,|(P - O) \wedge \vec{v}_P| = \dfrac{1}{2}\,u\,d$, which does not depend on time. Geometrically, in an interval $\Delta t$ the segment $OP$ sweeps a triangle with base $u\,\Delta t$ on the line and height $d$, whose area is always the same.
