At the instant considered a particle $P$ moving in the plane $Oxy$ has position $P - O = 3\,\hat{\imath} + 4\,\hat{\jmath}$, in $\mathrm{m}$, and velocity $\vec{v}_P = 2\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$, in $\mathrm{m/s}$. What is at that instant the derivative $\dot{\vartheta}$ of the polar angle?

- $-\,0.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-\,1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-\,5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

One has $r = |P - O| = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ and the transverse unit vector is $\hat{u}_\vartheta = \dfrac{1}{5}\,(-4\,\hat{\imath} + 3\,\hat{\jmath})$, obtained by rotating $\hat{u}_r = \dfrac{1}{5}\,(3\,\hat{\imath} + 4\,\hat{\jmath})$ by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ counterclockwise. The transverse velocity therefore equals
$$
r\,\dot{\vartheta} = \vec{v}_P \cdot \hat{u}_\vartheta = \frac{-8 + 3}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s} = -\,1\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Dividing by $r = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ one obtains $\dot{\vartheta} = -\,0.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The negative sign indicates that at that instant the polar angle decreases, that is, the position vector rotates clockwise.
