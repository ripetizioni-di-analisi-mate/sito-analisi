A cart translates along the $x$ axis with constant velocity $V > 0$. At the point $\Omega$ of the cart a rod of length $\ell$ is hinged, which rotates with respect to the cart with constant angular velocity $\omega > 0$, so that the angle it forms with the $x$ axis equals $\vartheta = \omega t$; the system is in the figure. For which value of $V$ does there exist an instant at which the absolute velocity of the free end $A$ of the rod is zero?

::struttura{nodi="Ω,1.6,0;A,2.63,1.23" aste="Ω-A" vincoli="Ω,carrello,270" linee="-0.3,0 4.6,0;2.15,0.0 2.15,0.06 2.14,0.12 2.12,0.18 2.1,0.23 2.07,0.29 2.04,0.33 2.0,0.38 1.95,0.42" punti="2.63,1.23" frecce="1.6,-0.55,2.6,-0.55,V;0,0,0,1.5,y" coppie="0.9 1.0,1,ω" etichette="2.3,0.12,ϑ;2.0,0.75,ℓ" titolo="Cart in uniform translation with a hinged rod that rotates with respect to the cart"}

- $V = \dfrac{\ell\,\omega}{2}$
- $V = \ell\,\omega$
- $V = 2\,\ell\,\omega$
- For no value of $V$

## Explanation

The frame attached to the cart moves with translational motion, hence the theorem on the composition of velocities gives $\vec{v}_A = V\,\hat{\imath} + \omega\,\hat{k} \wedge (A - \Omega)$, that is
$$
\vec{v}_A = \big( V - \ell\,\omega\sin(\omega t) \big)\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\,\omega\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The component along $\hat{\jmath}$ vanishes only at the instants with $\cos(\omega t) = 0$, and at them $\sin(\omega t) = \pm 1$. Since $V > 0$, the component along $\hat{\imath}$ vanishes at those instants only if $V = \ell\,\omega$, with the rod orthogonal to the direction of the motion of the cart.
