At the instant considered a particle $P$ has velocity $\vec{v}_P = 6\,\hat{\imath} - 8\,\hat{\jmath}$, in $\mathrm{m/s}$, and acceleration $\vec{a}_P = 3\,\hat{\imath} + 4\,\hat{\jmath}$, in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the tangential acceleration $a_t = \ddot{s}$, with the arc length coordinate increasing in the direction of the motion?

- $-\,1.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the velocity equals $|\vec{v}_P| = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the dot product of velocity and acceleration equals $\vec{v}_P \cdot \vec{a}_P = 18 - 32 = -\,14\,\mathrm{m^2/s^3}$. From the formula $a_t = \dfrac{\vec{v}_P \cdot \vec{a}_P}{|\vec{v}_P|}$ there follows $a_t = -\,1.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The negative sign says that at that instant the magnitude of the velocity is decreasing.
