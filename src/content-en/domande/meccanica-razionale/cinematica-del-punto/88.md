Two balls are thrown at the same instant from the same point, with different initial velocities $\vec{v}_1$ and $\vec{v}_2$, and during the flight both have acceleration $-\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$. What motion does the second ball describe for an observer attached to the frame with origin at the first ball and constant unit vectors?

- A parabolic motion with acceleration $-\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$
- A uniform rectilinear motion with velocity $\vec{v}_2 - \vec{v}_1$
- Rest, since the two balls have the same acceleration
- A uniform rectilinear motion with velocity $\vec{v}_2 + \vec{v}_1$

## Explanation

The frame has constant unit vectors, hence it is in translational motion with $\vec{\omega} = \vec{0}$, and its origin, the first ball, has acceleration $\vec{a}_\Omega = -\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$. By the theorem on the composition of accelerations in a translating frame $\vec{a}_a = \vec{a}_\Omega + \vec{a}_r$, and the second ball has $\vec{a}_a = -\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore $\vec{a}_r = \vec{0}$. The relative velocity is thus constant and equal to its initial value, which by the composition of velocities equals $\vec{v}_2 - \vec{v}_1$. The two balls start from the same point, hence the second one moves away from the first along a straight line, with constant velocity $\vec{v}_2 - \vec{v}_1$.
