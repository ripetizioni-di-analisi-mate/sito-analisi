A particle $P$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ with
$$
x(t) = \ell\cos(\omega t) ,
\qquad
y(t) = 2\,\ell\sin(\omega t) ,
$$
where $\ell > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ are constants. What is the maximum value of the magnitude of the velocity?

- $\ell\,\omega$
- $2\,\ell\,\omega$
- $\sqrt{5}\,\ell\,\omega$
- $3\,\ell\,\omega$

## Explanation

Differentiating the equations of motion one obtains $\vec{v}_P = -\,\ell\omega\sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + 2\,\ell\omega\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath}$. The square of the magnitude equals
$$
|\vec{v}_P|^2 = \ell^2\omega^2\sin^2(\omega t) + 4\,\ell^2\omega^2\cos^2(\omega t) = \ell^2\omega^2\big( 1 + 3\cos^2(\omega t) \big) ,
$$
where the identity $\sin^2(\omega t) + \cos^2(\omega t) = 1$ has been used. This expression is maximum when $\cos^2(\omega t) = 1$, therefore the maximum value of the magnitude of the velocity is $2\,\ell\,\omega$. It is attained at the points $(\pm\,\ell,\,0)$, that is at the endpoints of the minor semiaxis of the ellipse.
