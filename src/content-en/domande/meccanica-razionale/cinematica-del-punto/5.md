A particle $P$ travels along the circle with center $O$ and radius $R$ starting from rest, with the central angle given by
$$
\vartheta(t) = \frac{1}{2}\,\alpha t^2 ,
\qquad \alpha > 0 .
$$
At what instant does the acceleration form an angle of $30^\circ$ with the radius?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha}}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{\alpha}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[4]{3}\,\sqrt{\alpha}}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{3}}{\sqrt{\alpha}}$

## Explanation

In circular motion the intrinsic components of the acceleration equal $a_t = R\,\ddot{\vartheta} = R\,\alpha$ and $a_n = R\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 = R\,\alpha^2t^2$. The normal acceleration is directed along the radius toward the center and the tangential one is orthogonal to the radius, therefore the angle $\beta$ between the acceleration and the radius satisfies
$$
\tan\beta = \frac{a_t}{a_n} = \frac{1}{\alpha t^2} .
$$
Imposing $\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ one obtains $\alpha t^2 = \sqrt{3}$, hence the instant sought is $t^{*} = \dfrac{\sqrt[4]{3}}{\sqrt{\alpha}}$.
