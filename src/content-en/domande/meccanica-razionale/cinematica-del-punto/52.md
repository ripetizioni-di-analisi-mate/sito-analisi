A particle travels along a circle of radius $R = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. At a certain instant the magnitude of the velocity equals $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnitude of the acceleration equals $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is at that instant the magnitude of the tangential acceleration?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The radius of curvature of a circle equals its radius, hence the normal acceleration equals
$$
a_n = \frac{\dot{s}^2}{\rho} = \frac{16}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 8\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The tangential and normal components are orthogonal, therefore $|\vec{a}_P|^2 = a_t^2 + a_n^2$. It follows that $|a_t| = \sqrt{100 - 64}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
