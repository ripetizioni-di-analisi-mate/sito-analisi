A particle moves in the plane $Oxy$ with constant areal velocity with respect to the pole $O$. At one instant it is at distance $r = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$ with $\dot{\vartheta} = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is $\dot{\vartheta}$ at the instant at which it is at distance $r = 3\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\dfrac{27}{4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The areal velocity with respect to $O$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\,r^2\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$, hence its constancy is equivalent to the constancy of the product $r^2\,\dot{\vartheta}$. At the first instant this product equals $4 \cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. Imposing the same value with $r = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ one obtains
$$
\dot{\vartheta} = \frac{12}{9} = \frac{4}{3}\ \mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
