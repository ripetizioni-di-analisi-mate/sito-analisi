At the instant considered a particle $P$ has velocity $\vec{v}_P = 3\,\hat{\imath} + 4\,\hat{\jmath}$, in $\mathrm{m/s}$, and acceleration $\vec{a}_P = -\,8\,\hat{\imath} + 6\,\hat{\jmath}$, in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the radius of curvature of the trajectory at that instant?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the velocity equals $|\vec{v}_P| = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The cross product of the two vectors equals
$$
\vec{v}_P \wedge \vec{a}_P = \big( 3 \cdot 6 - 4 \cdot (-8) \big)\,\hat{k} = 50\,\hat{k} ,
$$
measured in $\mathrm{m^2/s^3}$. From the formula $\rho = \dfrac{|\vec{v}_P|^3}{|\vec{v}_P \wedge \vec{a}_P|}$ there follows $\rho = \dfrac{125}{50} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
