A platform rotates about the fixed center $O$ with constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. A straight guide is fixed on it at distance $d$ from $O$, and a slider $P$ travels along it with constant relative velocity $u\,\hat{e}_1$, where $\hat{e}_1$ is the unit vector of the guide oriented as in the figure. For which value of $u$ is the absolute acceleration of $P$ zero at the instant when the slider passes through the foot $H$ of the perpendicular drawn from $O$ to the guide?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" nodi="O,0,0;H,0,0.9" nomi=0 punti="0,0,O,centro;0,0.9,H" linee="-1.78,0.9 1.78,0.9" tratteggi="0,0 0,0.9" frecce="0,0.9,0.7,0.9,e_1" coppie="-1.0 -1.0,1,ω" quote="O,H,d,-0.45" titolo="Straight guide at distance $d$ from the center of a rotating platform"}

- $u = \omega\,d$
- $u = \dfrac{\omega\,d}{2}$
- $u = 2\,\omega\,d$
- For no value of $u$

## Explanation

The relative motion is uniform rectilinear, hence $\vec{a}_r = \vec{0}$. At the point $H$ one has $P - O = d\,\hat{e}_2$, with $\hat{e}_2 = \hat{k} \wedge \hat{e}_1$ directed from $O$ toward the guide, therefore the transport acceleration equals $\vec{a}_\tau = -\,\omega^2 d\,\hat{e}_2$. The Coriolis acceleration equals $2\,\omega\,\hat{k} \wedge u\,\hat{e}_1 = 2\,\omega\,u\,\hat{e}_2$. Adding up one obtains
$$
\vec{a}_a = \big( 2\,\omega\,u - \omega^2 d \big)\,\hat{e}_2 ,
$$
which is zero for $u = \dfrac{\omega\,d}{2}$. At that instant the Coriolis acceleration exactly balances the centripetal transport acceleration.
