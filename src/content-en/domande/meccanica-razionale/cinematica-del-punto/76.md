A cart translates along a horizontal guide with velocity $V\,\hat{\imath}$. At the point $\Omega$ of the cart a pendulum of length $\ell$ is hinged, with the particle $P$ at its end, and $\varphi$ is the angle that the pendulum forms with the downward vertical, positive counterclockwise, as in the figure. What is the square of the magnitude of the velocity of $P$?

::struttura{nodi="Ω,2,3;P,3,1.27" aste="Ω-P" vincoli="Ω,carrello,270" linee="0,3 4.6,3;2,2.4 2.1,2.41 2.21,2.44 2.3,2.48" tratteggi="2,3 2,0.9" punti="3,1.27" frecce="0.6,3.6,1.6,3.6,V" etichette="2.18,2.12,φ;2.78,2.25,ℓ" titolo="Translating cart with a pendulum of length $\ell$ hinged at $\Omega$"}

- $V^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2$
- $\big( V + \ell\,\dot{\varphi} \big)^2$
- $V^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 + 2\,V\,\ell\,\dot{\varphi}\cos\varphi$
- $V^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 + 2\,V\,\ell\,\dot{\varphi}\sin\varphi$

## Explanation

One takes the moving frame attached to the cart, which translates with velocity $V\,\hat{\imath}$, hence $\vec{v}_P = V\,\hat{\imath} + \vec{v}_r$. With respect to the cart the particle describes a circle with center $\Omega$, with $P - \Omega = \ell\,(\sin\varphi\,\hat{\imath} - \cos\varphi\,\hat{\jmath})$, therefore
$$
\vec{v}_r = \ell\,\dot{\varphi}\,(\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath}) .
$$
The scalar product $V\,\hat{\imath} \cdot \vec{v}_r$ equals $V\,\ell\,\dot{\varphi}\cos\varphi$, hence
$$
|\vec{v}_P|^2 = V^2 + \ell^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2 + 2\,V\,\ell\,\dot{\varphi}\cos\varphi .
$$
For $\dot{\varphi} \neq 0$ the mixed term vanishes only when the relative velocity is vertical, that is, for $\varphi = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
