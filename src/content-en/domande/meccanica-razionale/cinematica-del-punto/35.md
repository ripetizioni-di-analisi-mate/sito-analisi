A moving frame rotates about the fixed $z$ axis and its unit vectors $\hat{e}_1$, $\hat{e}_2$ belong to the plane $Oxy$, with $\hat{e}_3 = \hat{k}$; the moving triad is orthonormal and right-handed and the angle of rotation is $\vartheta(t)$. What is $\dot{\hat{e}}_1$?

- $-\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{e}_2$
- $\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{e}_2$
- $\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{e}_1$
- $\vec{0}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity of a frame that rotates about the $z$ axis equals $\vec{\omega} = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{e}_3$. By Poisson's formulas one has $\dot{\hat{e}}_1 = \vec{\omega} \wedge \hat{e}_1$, hence $\dot{\hat{e}}_1 = \dot{\vartheta}\;\hat{e}_3 \wedge \hat{e}_1$. Since the triad is orthonormal and right-handed, one has $\hat{e}_3 \wedge \hat{e}_1 = \hat{e}_2$, therefore $\dot{\hat{e}}_1 = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{e}_2$.
