A circular platform of radius $R$ rotates about the fixed center $O$ with constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. A particle $P$ travels along the rim of the platform with relative velocity of constant magnitude $u$ counterclockwise, in the same direction as the rotation, as in the figure. What is the magnitude of the absolute acceleration of $P$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" punti="0,0,O,centro;1.41,1.41,P" frecce="1.41,1.41,0.85,1.97,u" coppie="-1.2 -1.1,1,ω" quote="0 0,1.41 1.41,R,-0.45" titolo="Particle traveling along the rim of a rotating platform in the direction of rotation"}

- $\omega^2 R + \dfrac{u^2}{R}$
- $\dfrac{(\omega R - u)^2}{R}$
- $\dfrac{(\omega R + u)^2}{R}$
- $\omega^2 R + \dfrac{u^2}{R} + \omega\,u$

## Explanation

In the frame of the platform the particle moves in uniform circular motion of radius $R$, hence $\vec{a}_r = -\,\dfrac{u^2}{R}\,\hat{u}_r$. The center is at rest and $\vec{\omega}$ is constant, therefore $\vec{a}_\tau = -\,\omega^2 R\,\hat{u}_r$. The relative velocity is $u\,\hat{u}_\vartheta$, hence $\vec{a}_c = 2\,\omega\,u\,(\hat{k} \wedge \hat{u}_\vartheta) = -\,2\,\omega\,u\,\hat{u}_r$. The three terms all point toward the center, therefore their magnitudes add up and
$$
|\vec{a}_a| = \omega^2 R + 2\,\omega\,u + \frac{u^2}{R} = \frac{(\omega R + u)^2}{R} .
$$
The result is the centripetal acceleration of a uniform circular motion of radius $R$ with absolute velocity of magnitude $\omega R + u$.
