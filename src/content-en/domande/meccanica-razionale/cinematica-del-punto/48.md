A ladder, modeled as a rigid rod $AB$ of length $\ell$, has the end $A$ sliding on the $x$ axis and the end $B$ sliding on the $y$ axis, as in the figure. Which curve does the midpoint $M$ of the ladder describe?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,2.4,0;B,0,1.8;M,1.2,0.9" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,270;B,carrello,180" punti="1.2,0.9" frecce="0,0,3.6,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" quote="A,B,ℓ" riferimento=0 titolo="Ladder of length $\ell$ with the end $A$ on the $x$ axis and the end $B$ on the $y$ axis"}

- An arc of the circle with center $O$ and radius $\ell$
- An arc of an ellipse with semi-axes $\ell$ and $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$
- A segment of the line $x + y = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$
- An arc of the circle with center $O$ and radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$

## Explanation

Let $A = (x_A,\,0)$ and $B = (0,\,y_B)$. The length of the rod is constant, hence $x_A^2 + y_B^2 = \ell^2$ in every configuration. The midpoint has coordinates $x_M = \dfrac{x_A}{2}$ and $y_M = \dfrac{y_B}{2}$, therefore
$$
x_M^2 + y_M^2 = \frac{x_A^2 + y_B^2}{4} = \frac{\ell^2}{4} .
$$
The point $M$ thus stays at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from $O$, and with the ends on the positive semi-axes it describes the arc of that circle contained in the first quadrant.
