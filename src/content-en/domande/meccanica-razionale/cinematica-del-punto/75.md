A particle $P$ moves in the fixed frame along the $x$ axis with $P - O = u\,t\,\hat{\imath}$, where $u > 0$. A moving frame has origin at $O$ and rotates about the $z$ axis with constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. What is at the instant $t$ the magnitude of the velocity of $P$ with respect to the moving frame?

- $u$
- $\omega\,u\,t$
- $u\,(1 + \omega t)$
- $u\,\sqrt{1 + \omega^2 t^2}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the composition of velocities $\vec{v}_r = \vec{v}_a - \vec{v}_\tau$. The absolute velocity is $u\,\hat{\imath}$; the moving origin is at rest, hence the transport velocity equals
$$
\vec{v}_\tau = \vec{\omega} \wedge (P - O) = \omega\,\hat{k} \wedge u\,t\,\hat{\imath} = \omega\,u\,t\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
Therefore $\vec{v}_r = u\,\hat{\imath} - \omega\,u\,t\,\hat{\jmath}$, and the two terms are orthogonal; the magnitude equals $u\,\sqrt{1 + \omega^2 t^2}$ and grows with time, even though in the fixed frame the motion is uniform.
