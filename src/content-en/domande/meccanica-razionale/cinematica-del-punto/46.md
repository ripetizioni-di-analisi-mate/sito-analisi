An inextensible thread is wound on a fixed spool of center $O$ and radius $\ell$ and unwinds while staying taut in the plane $Oxy$. Its end $P$ moves according to
$$
x(t) = \ell\,\big( \cos(\omega t) + \omega t\sin(\omega t) \big) ,
\qquad
y(t) = \ell\,\big( \sin(\omega t) - \omega t\cos(\omega t) \big) ,
$$
where $\omega > 0$ is constant. What is the length of the arc of trajectory described by $P$ in the interval $0 \le t \le \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$?

- $\pi^2\,\ell$
- $4\,\pi^2\,\ell$
- $2\,\pi^2\,\ell$
- $2\,\pi\,\ell$

## Explanation

Differentiating the equations of motion, in $\dot{x}$ the two terms $\pm\,\ell\,\omega\sin(\omega t)$ cancel and in $\dot{y}$ the two terms $\pm\,\ell\,\omega\cos(\omega t)$ cancel, hence
$$
\dot{x} = \ell\,\omega^2 t\cos(\omega t) ,
\qquad
\dot{y} = \ell\,\omega^2 t\sin(\omega t) ,
$$
and for $t \ge 0$ the magnitude of the velocity is $|\vec{v}_P| = \ell\,\omega^2 t$. The arc length is the integral of the magnitude of the velocity, therefore
$$
L = \int_0^{\frac{2\pi}{\omega}} \ell\,\omega^2 t\,dt = \frac{1}{2}\,\ell\,\omega^2 \left( \frac{2\pi}{\omega} \right)^2 = 2\,\pi^2\,\ell .
$$
The trajectory is the involute of the circle. The length $2\pi\ell$ is instead that of the piece of thread unwound in one turn, that is, the distance of $P$ from the point where the thread leaves the spool.
