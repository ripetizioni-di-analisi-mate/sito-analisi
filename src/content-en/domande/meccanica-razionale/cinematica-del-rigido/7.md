A homogeneous disk of radius $r$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping while staying in contact with the inner wall of a fixed circular guide of radius $R > r$ and center $O$. Through what angle, with respect to the fixed frame, does the disk turn about itself while $G$ makes one complete revolution about $O$?

- $\dfrac{R}{r}\,2\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{R - r}{r}\,2\pi$
- $\dfrac{R + r}{r}\,2\pi$

## Explanation

The center $G$ stays at the distance $R - r$ from $O$, therefore it describes the circle of radius $R - r$ and one has $|\vec{v}_G| = (R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}$, where $\varphi$ is the angle that $OG$ forms with the $x$ axis. The contact takes place without slipping on a fixed guide, hence the contact point is the instantaneous center of rotation of the disk and lies at the distance $r$ from $G$; then $|\vec{\omega}| = \dfrac{(R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}}{r}$. Integrating over one revolution, that is, for a variation of $\varphi$ equal to $2\pi$, the disk turns through $\dfrac{R - r}{r}\,2\pi$. For $R = 2r$ the disk therefore makes a single revolution about itself.
