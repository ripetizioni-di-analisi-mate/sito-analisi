The disk in the figure, of radius $R$, rolls without slipping on a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal, and its center $G$ descends along the plane with velocity of magnitude $v > 0$. What is the magnitude of the angular velocity of the disk?

::struttura{linee="0,0 4.5,2.598;0,0 4.5,0;4.5,0 4.5,2.598" cerchi="1.665,2.116,1" punti="1.665,2.116,G,baricentro;2.165,1.25,C,centro" frecce="1.665,2.116,0.886,1.666,v_G" etichette="0.95,0.22,30°" riferimento=0 titolo="Disk of radius $R$ rolling without slipping on the inclined plane"}

- $\dfrac{v}{2R}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\dfrac{v}{R}$
- $\dfrac{v}{R}$
- $\dfrac{2v}{R}$

## Explanation

The inclined plane is fixed and the contact takes place without slipping, therefore the contact point $C$ has zero velocity and is the instantaneous center of rotation of the disk. The center $G$ lies at the distance $R$ from $C$, hence $|\vec{v}_G| = |\vec{\omega}|\,R$. Then $|\vec{\omega}| = \dfrac{v}{R}$, and the result does not depend on the inclination of the plane. The inclination enters in the direction of the velocity of the center, which is parallel to the plane, whereas the relation between the magnitudes remains the one of rolling on a straight guide.
