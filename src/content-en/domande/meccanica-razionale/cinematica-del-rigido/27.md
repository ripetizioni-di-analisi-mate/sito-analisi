A rigid body has angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = 2\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$ and its point $\Omega = (0,\,0,\,0)$ has velocity $\vec{v}_\Omega = (0,\,4,\,1)\;\mathrm{m/s}$. Which is the point of the Mozzi axis that belongs to the plane $z = 0$?

- $(2,\,0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(-4,\,0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(0,\,-2,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(-2,\,0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Mozzi's theorem assigns to the axis the point $P^* = \Omega + \dfrac{\vec{\omega} \wedge \vec{v}_\Omega}{|\vec{\omega}|^2}$. The cross product equals $(0,\,0,\,2) \wedge (0,\,4,\,1) = (-8,\,0,\,0)$ and $|\vec{\omega}|^2 = 4\;\mathrm{rad^2/s^2}$, therefore $P^* - \Omega = (-2,\,0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$. The vector $P^* - \Omega$ is orthogonal to $\vec{\omega}$, hence $P^*$ has zero height and is the point sought. The Mozzi axis is therefore the line through $(-2,\,0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ parallel to $\hat{k}$.
