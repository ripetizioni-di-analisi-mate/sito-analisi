The rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$, has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and the endpoint $B$ on a smooth vertical guide, the two guides meeting at $O$. At the instant considered the rod forms the angle $\vartheta = 30^\circ$ with the horizontal and one has $\dot{\vartheta} = \omega_0 > 0$. What is the magnitude of the velocity of the midpoint $M$ of the rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,2.6,0;B,0,1.5" aste="A-B" linee="-0.4,0 3.4,0;0,-0.4 0,2.4" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,180" punti="2.6,0;0,1.5;1.3,0.75,M;0,0,O" etichette="2.1,0.2,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod with its endpoints on the two guides, inclined at $\vartheta$ to the horizontal"}

- $\dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\omega_0$
- $\ell\omega_0$
- $\dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{2}$

## Explanation

The instantaneous center of rotation is the intersection of the vertical line through $A$ with the horizontal line through $B$, therefore $C - O = \ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}$. The midpoint of the rod satisfies $M - O = \dfrac{\ell\cos\vartheta}{2}\;\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\ell\sin\vartheta}{2}\;\hat{\jmath}$, hence $|M - C| = \dfrac{\ell}{2}$ for every value of $\vartheta$. Since $|\vec{v}_M| = |\dot{\vartheta}|\,|M - C|$, one obtains $|\vec{v}_M| = \dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{2}$. The midpoint therefore moves on the circle of center $O$ and radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$, and the magnitude of its velocity does not depend on the inclination of the rod.
