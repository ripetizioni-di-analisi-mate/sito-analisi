A homogeneous disk of radius $R$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in the vertical plane. The center advances to the right with constant velocity $\vec{v}_G = v\,\hat{\imath}$, with $v > 0$. What is the acceleration of the point $C$ of the disk in contact with the guide?

- $\dfrac{v^2}{R}\;\hat{\jmath}$
- $\vec{0}$
- $-\dfrac{v^2}{R}\;\hat{\jmath}$
- $\dfrac{2v^2}{R}\;\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The condition of rolling without slipping on a fixed guide gives $\vec{\omega} = -\dfrac{v}{R}\;\hat{k}$, constant because $v$ is constant, therefore $\dot{\vec{\omega}} = \vec{0}$. The center moves with uniform rectilinear motion, hence $\vec{a}_G = \vec{0}$. In the acceleration formula for the plane case, with $C - G = -R\,\hat{\jmath}$, only the centripetal term remains:
$$
\vec{a}_C = -\,\dot{\vartheta}^{\,2}\,(C - G) = \frac{v^2}{R}\;\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The acceleration of the contact point is therefore directed from $C$ toward the center $G$ and has magnitude $\dfrac{v^2}{R}$, whereas its velocity is zero.
