A block $A$ slides along a horizontal guide and is connected to the endpoint $B$ of a rod by an inextensible and taut string. At the instant considered the string forms the angle of $60^\circ$ with the guide and the velocity of the block has magnitude $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the component of the velocity of $B$ along the string?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The string is inextensible and taut, therefore the distance between its endpoints stays constant and the components of the velocities of $A$ and of $B$ along the string are equal. The velocity of the block is directed along the guide, which forms the angle of $60^\circ$ with the string, hence the magnitude of its component along the string equals $4\cos 60^\circ\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since $\cos 60^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$, that value is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Then the component of the velocity of $B$ along the string has magnitude $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ as well, whereas the component orthogonal to the string remains undetermined.
