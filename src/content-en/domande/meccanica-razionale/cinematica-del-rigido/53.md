A rigid plate moves in the plane $Oxy$ and contains the points $A = (0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $B = (2,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$. At the instant considered their accelerations are $\vec{a}_A = (1,\,0)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $\vec{a}_B = (-7,\,6)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of the angular velocity of the plate?

- $2\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $5\;\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The acceleration formula in the plane case, with $B - A = 2\,\hat{\imath}\;\mathrm{m}$, gives
$$
\vec{a}_B - \vec{a}_A = \ddot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge 2\,\hat{\imath} - 2\,\dot{\vartheta}^{\,2}\,\hat{\imath} = -2\,\dot{\vartheta}^{\,2}\,\hat{\imath} + 2\,\ddot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
One has $\vec{a}_B - \vec{a}_A = (-8,\,6)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, therefore the component along $\hat{\imath}$ gives $\dot{\vartheta}^{\,2} = 4\,\mathrm{rad^2/s^2}$ and the one along $\hat{\jmath}$ gives $\ddot{\vartheta} = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Then $|\dot{\vartheta}| = 2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The accelerations determine the magnitude of the angular velocity, while the sense of rotation remains **undetermined**, because $\dot{\vartheta}$ appears only squared.
