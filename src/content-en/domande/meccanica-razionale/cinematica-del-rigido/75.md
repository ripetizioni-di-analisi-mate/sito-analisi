A sphere of radius $R = 0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping on the fixed horizontal plane $z = 0$. At the instant considered its angular velocity is $\vec{\omega} = (1,\,2,\,3)\;\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the velocity of the center $G$?

- $(-1,\; 0.5,\; 0)\;\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(1,\; -0.5,\; 0)\;\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(0.5,\; -1,\; 0)\;\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(1,\; -0.5,\; 1.5)\;\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The contact point $C$ has zero velocity, because the plane is fixed, therefore the fundamental formula with pole $C$ gives $\vec{v}_G = \vec{\omega} \wedge (G - C)$, with $G - C = 0.5\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{m}$. The cross product equals
$$
(1,\; 2,\; 3) \wedge (0,\; 0,\; 0.5) = (1,\; -0.5,\; 0)\;\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The component $\omega_z$ of the angular velocity does not contribute, because it is parallel to $G - C$, and it corresponds to a rotation of the sphere about the vertical through the contact point. The velocity of the center is horizontal, as the contact with the plane requires.
