A rod $OA$ of length $\ell$ is hinged to the ground at the endpoint $O$ and turns in the plane. At the instant considered one has $\dot{\vartheta} = \omega_0$ and $\ddot{\vartheta} = \alpha$, with $\omega_0 > 0$ and $\alpha > 0$. What is the magnitude of the acceleration of the endpoint $A$?

- $\ell\,\big( \alpha + \omega_0^2 \big)$
- $\ell\,\sqrt{\alpha^2 + \omega_0^4}$
- $\ell\,\sqrt{\alpha^2 + \omega_0^2}$
- $\ell\,\omega_0^2$

## Explanation

The point $O$ is fixed, therefore the acceleration formula in the plane case gives $\vec{a}_A = \ddot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (A - O) - \dot{\vartheta}^{\,2}\,(A - O)$. The first summand is orthogonal to the rod and has magnitude $\alpha\ell$, whereas the second is directed from $A$ toward $O$ and has magnitude $\omega_0^2\,\ell$. The two summands are orthogonal to each other, hence the magnitude of the acceleration is obtained by the Pythagorean theorem. Then $|\vec{a}_A| = \ell\sqrt{\alpha^2 + \omega_0^4}$, the sum in quadrature of the tangential component and the centripetal one.
