A rigid body moves in space. At the instant considered its point $A$ has velocity $\vec{v}_A = v\,\hat{\imath}$ and the angular velocity of the body is $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\big( \hat{\imath} + 2\,\hat{\jmath} + 2\,\hat{k} \big)$, with $v > 0$ and $\omega > 0$. What is the pitch of the state of motion?

- $\dfrac{v}{3\omega}$
- $\dfrac{v}{9\omega}$
- $\dfrac{2v}{9\omega}$
- $\dfrac{v}{\omega}$

## Explanation

The kinematic invariant equals $I = \vec{\omega} \cdot \vec{v}_A = \omega v$, therefore it is different from zero and the state of motion is helical. The magnitude of the angular velocity is $|\vec{\omega}| = \omega\sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = 3\omega$. Mozzi's theorem assigns to the state of motion the pitch $p = \dfrac{I}{|\vec{\omega}|^2}$, hence
$$
p = \frac{\omega v}{9\,\omega^2} = \frac{v}{9\omega} .
$$
The pitch measures the advance of the body along the Mozzi axis for each radian of rotation.
