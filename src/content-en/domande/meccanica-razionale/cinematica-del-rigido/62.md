A screw has a thread of pitch $h$, that is, it advances by $h$ along its own axis at every complete turn, and it is screwed in with constant angular speed $\omega$. What is the speed at which the screw advances?

- $h\,\omega$
- $\dfrac{h\,\omega}{2\pi}$
- $\dfrac{h\,\omega}{\pi}$
- $2\pi\,h\,\omega$

## Explanation

The state of motion of the screw is helical, with the Mozzi axis coinciding with the axis of the screw. The pitch $p$ of the state of motion is the advance per radian of rotation, and a complete turn corresponds to $2\pi$ radians, therefore $p = \dfrac{h}{2\pi}$. The points of the axis have speed $|\vec{v}_{\min}| = p\,|\vec{\omega}|$, hence the screw advances with speed $\dfrac{h\,\omega}{2\pi}$. The pitch of the thread and the pitch of the state of motion therefore differ by the factor $2\pi$.
