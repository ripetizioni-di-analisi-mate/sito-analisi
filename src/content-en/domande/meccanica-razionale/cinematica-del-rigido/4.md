The rod in the figure has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and runs inside a fixed sleeve hinged at $D$, placed at the height $h$ above the guide. At the instant considered the rod forms the angle $\vartheta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal and one has $\dot{\vartheta} = \omega_0 > 0$. What is the velocity of the endpoint $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,-1.73,0;B,0.77,4.33" aste="A-B" linee="-3,0 1.6,0;0.03,3.45 0.37,3.25 -0.03,2.55 -0.37,2.75 0.03,3.45" punti="-1.73,0;0.77,4.33;0,3,D;0,0,O" vincoli="A,carrello" tratteggi="0,0 0,3" quote="0 0,0 3,h,0.5" etichette="-1.05,0.3,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod in the fixed sleeve $D$, with the endpoint $A$ on the horizontal guide"}

- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,h\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,h\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$
- $4\,h\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,h\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The rod always passes through the fixed point $D$, therefore its line of action is identified by the angle $\vartheta$ and the abscissa of the endpoint $A$ equals $x_A = -\dfrac{h}{\tan\vartheta}$. Differentiating with respect to time one obtains
$$
\dot{x}_A = \frac{h}{\sin^2\vartheta}\;\dot{\vartheta} .
$$
Since $\sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, it follows that $\sin^2 60^\circ = \dfrac{3}{4}$ and hence $\dot{x}_A = \dfrac{4}{3}\,h\omega_0$. The velocity of the endpoint $A$ is therefore $\dfrac{4}{3}\,h\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$, directed to the right.
