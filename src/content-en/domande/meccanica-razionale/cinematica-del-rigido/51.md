A triangular plate $ABC$ moves in the plane $Oxy$, and at the instant considered the vertices $A$ and $B$ have zero velocity. What can be concluded about the state of motion of the plate?

- The state of motion is rotational about $A$
- All points of the plate have zero velocity
- The state of motion is rotational about the midpoint of $AB$
- The state of motion is rotational, with the instantaneous center of rotation on the line $AB$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the instantaneous center of rotation, if $\dot{\vartheta} \neq 0$ there exists one and only one point of the plane with zero velocity. The points at rest are instead two, $A$ and $B$, therefore $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$. With $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$ the fundamental formula gives $\vec{v}_P = \vec{v}_A = \vec{0}$ for every point $P$, hence the plate is **at rest** at the instant considered. The same result follows from $\vec{v}_B = \vec{v}_A + \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (B - A)$, which with $\vec{v}_A = \vec{v}_B = \vec{0}$ and $B \neq A$ forces $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$.
