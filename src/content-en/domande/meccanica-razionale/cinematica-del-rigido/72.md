The train wheel in the figure rolls without slipping on the rail with the circle of radius $R$, and its flange extends below the level of the rail up to the distance $R + d$ from the center $G$. The center moves with velocity $v\,\hat{\imath}$, with $v > 0$. What is the velocity of the lowest point $F$ of the flange?

- $\vec{0}$
- $-\dfrac{v\,d}{R}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $\dfrac{v\,d}{R}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $-\dfrac{v\,(R + d)}{R}\;\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The contact point with the rail has zero velocity, therefore the angular velocity of the wheel is $\vec{\omega} = -\dfrac{v}{R}\;\hat{k}$. The point $F$ satisfies $F - G = -(R + d)\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence
$$
\vec{v}_F = v\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{v}{R}\;\hat{k} \wedge \big( -(R + d)\,\hat{\jmath} \big) = v\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{v\,(R + d)}{R}\;\hat{\imath} = -\frac{v\,d}{R}\;\hat{\imath} .
$$
The point $F$ lies below the instantaneous center of rotation, therefore it moves **opposite** to the train. The points of the flange below the level of the rail have velocity with horizontal component opposite to that of the center.
