In the oscillating slotted-link mechanism in the figure the crank $OA$, of length $r$, is hinged to the ground at $O$ and rotates with angular velocity $\Omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\Omega > 0$. The pin $A$ of the crank slides in a straight slot of the rod $DE$, hinged to the ground at the point $D$ located at distance $\sqrt{3}\,r$ below $O$. At the instant when the crank is horizontal, with $A$ to the right of $O$, what is the angular velocity of the rod $DE$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.5,0;D,0,-2.6;E,2.03,0.91" aste="O-A;D-E" vincoli="O,cerniera,90;D,cerniera" punti="1.5,0,,pieno" coppie="O,1,Ω" quote="O,A,r,-0.45;O,D,√3 r,0.6" titolo="Oscillating slotted link: the pin $A$ of the crank $OA$ slides in the slot of the rod $DE$"}

- $\dfrac{\Omega}{2}\;\hat{k}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,\Omega\;\hat{k}$
- $\dfrac{\Omega}{4}\;\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{\Omega}{4}\;\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The pin $A$ belongs to the crank, which rotates about $O$ with $A - O = r\,\hat{\imath}$, therefore its absolute velocity is $\vec{v}_A = \Omega\,\hat{k} \wedge r\,\hat{\imath} = \Omega r\,\hat{\jmath}$. With respect to the rod $DE$ the pin slides along the slot, hence, by the composition of velocities, $\vec{v}_A = \vec{v}_\tau + \vec{v}_r$, where $\vec{v}_r$ is parallel to the line $DA$ and $\vec{v}_\tau = \omega\,\hat{k} \wedge (A - D)$ is the velocity of the point of the rod occupying the position $A$. One has $A - D = r\,\hat{\imath} + \sqrt{3}\,r\,\hat{\jmath}$, of magnitude $2r$, therefore the unit vector orthogonal to the slot is
$$
\hat{n} = \frac{\hat{k} \wedge (A - D)}{2r} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\;\hat{\imath} + \frac{1}{2}\;\hat{\jmath} .
$$
Taking the dot product of the composition of velocities with $\hat{n}$, the term $\vec{v}_r$ vanishes and one obtains $\dfrac{\Omega r}{2} = 2r\,\omega$. Then $\omega = \dfrac{\Omega}{4}$, and the rod rotates counterclockwise with angular velocity $\dfrac{\Omega}{4}\;\hat{k}$.
