The system in the figure is formed by the rods $OA$ and $AB$, both of length $\ell$, hinged to each other at $A$; the rod $OA$ is hinged to the ground at $O$ and the endpoint $B$ runs on a slider along the horizontal guide passing through $O$. At the instant considered the rod $OA$ forms the angle $\vartheta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal and one has $\dot{\vartheta} = \omega_0 > 0$. What is the velocity of the endpoint $B$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.1,1.9;B,2.2,0" aste="O-A;A-B" linee="-0.5,0 3,0" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="0,0;1.1,1.9;2.2,0" etichette="0.55,0.25,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Two equal rods hinged at $A$, with $B$ on a slider on the guide through $O$"}

- $-2\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$
- $\sqrt{3}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$
- $-\sqrt{3}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The two rods have the same length and the points $O$ and $B$ belong to the same horizontal guide, therefore the triangle $OAB$ is isosceles and $B - O = 2\ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath}$ holds. Differentiating with respect to time one obtains $\vec{v}_B = -\,2\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath}$. Since $\sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, it follows that $\vec{v}_B = -\sqrt{3}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$. The endpoint $B$ therefore moves to the left, that is, it approaches $O$ while the rod $OA$ rises.
