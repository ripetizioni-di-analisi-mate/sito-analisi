A rod $AB$ of length $\ell = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ has the endpoint $A$ on a horizontal guide and the endpoint $B$ on a vertical guide, the two guides meeting at $O$. What is the radius of the moving centrode of the motion of the rod?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The instantaneous center of rotation is the fourth vertex of the rectangle having the two guides as sides, therefore it lies at the distance $\ell$ from the origin $O$ and at the distance $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ from the midpoint of the rod. In the plane attached to the rod the center therefore describes the circle of diameter $\ell$ having its own center at the midpoint of the rod, and the moving centrode has radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$. With $\ell = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ the radius of the moving centrode equals $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The fixed centrode is instead the circle of center $O$ and radius $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
