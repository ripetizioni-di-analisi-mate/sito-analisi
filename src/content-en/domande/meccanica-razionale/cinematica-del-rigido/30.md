A rigid plate moves in the plane $Oxy$ with angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = 2\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$, and its point $A = (1,\,1)\,\mathrm{m}$ has velocity $\vec{v}_A = (4,\,0)\;\mathrm{m/s}$. Where is the instantaneous center of rotation?

- $(1,\,3)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(1,\,-1)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(1,\,5)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(3,\,1)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The instantaneous center of rotation is the point $C = A + \dfrac{\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_A}{\dot{\vartheta}}$, with $\dot{\vartheta} = 2\;\mathrm{rad/s}$. One has $\hat{k} \wedge \vec{v}_A = (0,\,4,\,0)\;\mathrm{m/s}$, therefore $C - A = (0,\,2,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$. Then $C = (1,\,3)\,\mathrm{m}$. The center lies on the line through $A$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_A$, that is, on the vertical line of equation $x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$, as the geometric construction requires.
