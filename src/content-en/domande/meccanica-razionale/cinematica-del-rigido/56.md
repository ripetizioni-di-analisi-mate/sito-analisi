A disk of radius $r$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping inside a fixed circular guide of radius $R > r$ and center $O$, and the radius $OG$ rotates with constant angular speed $\dot{\varphi}$. What is the magnitude of the acceleration of the point of the disk in contact with the guide?

- $(R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}^{\,2}$
- $\dfrac{(R - r)^2\,\dot{\varphi}^{\,2}}{r}$
- $\dfrac{R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^{\,2}}{r}$
- $\dfrac{R\,(R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}^{\,2}}{r}$

## Explanation

The center $G$ moves in uniform circular motion of radius $R - r$, therefore $\vec{a}_G$ has magnitude $(R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}^{\,2}$ and is directed toward $O$. The contact point $C$ is the instantaneous center of rotation, hence the disk has angular velocity of constant magnitude $\dfrac{(R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}}{r}$ and zero angular acceleration. The acceleration formula in the plane case gives $\vec{a}_C = \vec{a}_G - \dot{\vartheta}^{\,2}\,(C - G)$, where $C - G$ has magnitude $r$ and is directed from $O$ toward $C$, opposite to $\vec{a}_G$. The two terms therefore have the same sense, both directed toward $O$, and
$$
|\vec{a}_C| = (R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}^{\,2} + \frac{(R - r)^2\,\dot{\varphi}^{\,2}}{r^2}\;r = \frac{R\,(R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}^{\,2}}{r} .
$$
