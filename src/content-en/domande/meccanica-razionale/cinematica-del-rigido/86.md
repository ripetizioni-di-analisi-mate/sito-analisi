The four-bar linkage in the figure consists of the crank $OA$, the coupler $AB$ and the rocker $DB$, with $O$ and $D$ fixed hinges. At the instant considered $O = (0,\,0)$, $A = (0,\,2\ell)$, $B = (2\ell,\,2\ell)$ and $D = (4\ell,\,0)$, and the crank has angular velocity $\Omega\,\hat{k}$. What is the angular velocity of the coupler $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0,2;B,2,2;D,4,0" aste="O-A;A-B;D-B" vincoli="O,cerniera;D,cerniera" coppie="O,1,Ω" quote="O,A,2ℓ,-0.6;A,B,2ℓ,-0.5;O,D,4ℓ,0.8" titolo="Four-bar linkage $OABD$ with the fixed hinges $O$ and $D$"}

- $-\,\Omega\,\hat{k}$
- $\Omega\,\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{\Omega}{2}\;\hat{k}$
- $\vec{0}$

## Explanation

The point $A$ rotates about $O$, therefore its velocity is orthogonal to $OA$ and the instantaneous center of rotation of the coupler lies on the line $OA$, that is, on the $y$ axis. The point $B$ rotates about $D$, hence the center also lies on the line $DB$, with equation $y = 4\ell - x$. The two lines meet at the point $C = (0,\,4\ell)$. One has $\vec{v}_A = \Omega\,\hat{k} \wedge 2\ell\,\hat{\jmath} = -2\,\Omega\ell\,\hat{\imath}$, and with $A - C = -2\ell\,\hat{\jmath}$ the relation $\vec{v}_A = \omega_{AB}\,\hat{k} \wedge (A - C) = 2\ell\,\omega_{AB}\,\hat{\imath}$ gives $\omega_{AB} = -\,\Omega$. The coupler therefore rotates clockwise with angular velocity $-\,\Omega\,\hat{k}$.
