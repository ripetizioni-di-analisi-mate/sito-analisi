The slider-crank system in the figure has the crank $OA$, of length $r$, hinged to the ground at $O$ and turning with constant angular velocity $\Omega\,\hat{k}$, and the connecting rod $AB$, of length $3r$, with $B$ on a slider on the horizontal guide passing through $O$. What is the angular velocity of the connecting rod at the instant at which the crank is aligned with the guide and directed opposite to $B$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,-1.4,0;B,2.8,0" aste="O-A;A-B" linee="-2.2,0 3.6,0" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="0,0;-1.4,0;2.8,0" quote="A,O,r,0.7;A,B,3r,-0.7" coppie="-0.7 0.7,1,Ω" titolo="Slider-crank at dead center: $OA = r$ aligned with the guide, $AB = 3r$"}

- $\Omega\,\hat{k}$
- $\dfrac{\Omega}{3}\,\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{\Omega}{3}\,\hat{k}$
- $3\,\Omega\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

In that configuration one has $A - O = -r\,\hat{\imath}$, therefore $\vec{v}_A = \Omega\,\hat{k} \wedge (A - O) = -\,\Omega r\,\hat{\jmath}$. The slider forces the velocity of $B$ to be parallel to the guide, hence the component of $\vec{v}_B$ along $\hat{\jmath}$ vanishes. Since $B - A = 3r\,\hat{\imath}$, the fundamental formula applied to the connecting rod gives
$$
\vec{v}_B = -\,\Omega r\,\hat{\jmath} + \omega_{AB}\,\hat{k} \wedge 3r\,\hat{\imath} = \big( 3r\,\omega_{AB} - \Omega r \big)\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
Setting this component equal to zero one gets $\omega_{AB} = \dfrac{\Omega}{3}$, hence the connecting rod turns in the counterclockwise sense with angular velocity $\dfrac{\Omega}{3}\,\hat{k}$ and the endpoint $B$ is at rest at the instant considered.
