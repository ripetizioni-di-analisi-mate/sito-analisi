A rigid body has angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = 3\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$ and one of its points $P$ has velocity $\vec{v}_P = (1,\,2,\,0)\;\mathrm{m/s}$. How is the state of motion classified?

- Translational
- Rotational, with axis of instantaneous rotation parallel to $\hat{k}$
- Helical, with pitch different from zero
- Rotational, with axis of instantaneous rotation contained in the plane $Oxy$

## Explanation

The kinematic invariant equals $I = \vec{\omega} \cdot \vec{v}_P = 0$, since $\vec{v}_P$ is orthogonal to $\hat{k}$. The angular velocity is different from the zero vector and the invariant is zero, therefore the state of motion is rotational and the Mozzi axis is an axis of instantaneous rotation. By Mozzi's theorem the axis is parallel to $\vec{\omega}$, hence it is parallel to $\hat{k}$. The points of that axis have zero velocity at the instant considered.
