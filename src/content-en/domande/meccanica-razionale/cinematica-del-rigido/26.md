A rigid body moves in space with angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = (1,\,2,\,2)\;\mathrm{rad/s}$, and its point $A$ has velocity $\vec{v}_A = (3,\,0,\,0)\;\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the velocity of the points of the Mozzi axis?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The points of the Mozzi axis have velocity $\vec{v}_{\min} = \dfrac{I}{|\vec{\omega}|^2}\;\vec{\omega}$, where $I = \vec{\omega} \cdot \vec{v}_A$ is the kinematic invariant. One has $I = 3$ and $|\vec{\omega}| = \sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = 3\;\mathrm{rad/s}$, therefore
$$
|\vec{v}_{\min}| = \frac{|I|}{|\vec{\omega}|} = 1\;\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The invariant is different from zero, hence the state of motion is helical and no point of the body is at rest. The value found is the smallest of the magnitudes of the velocities of the points of the body.
