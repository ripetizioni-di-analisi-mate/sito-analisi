The spool in the figure consists of an outer disk of radius $R$, which rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal plane, and of a coaxial hub of radius $r < R$, on which a string is wound. The string unwinds from the lowest point $H$ of the hub, stays horizontal, and its free end is pulled to the right with speed $u$. What is the velocity of the center $G$?

- $\dfrac{uR}{R + r}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $-\dfrac{uR}{R - r}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $\dfrac{u\,r}{R}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $\dfrac{uR}{R - r}\;\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The contact point with the plane is the instantaneous center of rotation, therefore, setting $\vec{v}_G = v_G\,\hat{\imath}$, the angular velocity is $\vec{\omega} = -\dfrac{v_G}{R}\;\hat{k}$. The point $H$ satisfies $H - G = -r\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence
$$
\vec{v}_H = v_G\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{v_G}{R}\;\hat{k} \wedge (-r\,\hat{\jmath}) = v_G\,\frac{R - r}{R}\;\hat{\imath} .
$$
The string is inextensible and taut, therefore the component of $\vec{v}_H$ along the string equals the speed $u$ of the free end. Then $v_G\,\dfrac{R - r}{R} = u$, and the center moves to the right with $\vec{v}_G = \dfrac{uR}{R - r}\;\hat{\imath}$, faster than the string.
