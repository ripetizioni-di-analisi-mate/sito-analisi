A rigid plate moves in the plane $Oxy$. The point $A = (0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ has velocity $\vec{v}_A = (0,\,2)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the point $B = (2,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ has velocity $\vec{v}_B = (0,\,-4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the angular velocity of the plate?

- $3\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-6\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-3\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $-1\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

One sets $\vec{\omega} = \omega_z\,\hat{k}$ and applies the fundamental formula to the two points, with $B - A = 2\,\hat{\imath}\;\mathrm{m}$. The cross product equals $\omega_z\,\hat{k} \wedge 2\,\hat{\imath} = 2\,\omega_z\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the component of $\vec{v}_B$ along $\hat{\jmath}$ satisfies $-4 = 2 + 2\,\omega_z$. Then $\omega_z = -3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the plate turns in the clockwise sense. The components of the two velocities along $\hat{\imath}$ are both zero, hence equiprojectivity is satisfied.
