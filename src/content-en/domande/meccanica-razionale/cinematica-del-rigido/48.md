The rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$, has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and the endpoint $B$ on a second smooth straight guide, which passes through $O$ and forms the angle of $60^\circ$ with the first one. At the instant considered the triangle $OAB$ is equilateral and the endpoint $A$ has velocity $\vec{v}_A = v\,\hat{\imath}$, with $v > 0$. What is the magnitude of the velocity of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,-2,0;B,-1,1.732" aste="A-B" linee="-3,0 0.8,0;0.35,-0.606 -1.5,2.598" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,30" punti="0,0,O" frecce="-2,0,-1.1,0,v_A" etichette="-0.45,0.22,60°" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod with its endpoints on two guides that form the angle of $60^\circ$"}

- $v$
- $2v$
- $\dfrac{v}{2}$
- $\sqrt{3}\,v$

## Explanation

The endpoint $B$ slides on the inclined guide, hence $\vec{v}_B$ is parallel to that guide. By **equiprojectivity** the velocities of $A$ and of $B$ have the same component along the line $AB$. The triangle $OAB$ is equilateral, therefore the line $AB$ forms the angle of $60^\circ$ both with the horizontal guide and with the guide of $B$. The equiprojectivity condition then becomes $v\cos 60^\circ = |\vec{v}_B|\cos 60^\circ$, hence $|\vec{v}_B| = v$.
