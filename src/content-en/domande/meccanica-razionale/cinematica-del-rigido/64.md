The rectangular plate $ABCD$ in the figure has sides $AB = 2a$ and $AD = a$ and moves in the plane. At the instant considered the instantaneous center of rotation coincides with the vertex $A$ and the angular velocity has magnitude $\omega$. What is the largest of the speeds of the vertices?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,2 0,2" punti="0,0,A,centro;4,0,B;4,2,C;0,2,D" quote="0 0,4 0,2a,0.5;0 0,0 2,a,-0.5" titolo="Rectangular plate with the instantaneous center of rotation at the vertex $A$"}

- $2\,\omega a$
- $\omega a$
- $3\,\omega a$
- $\sqrt{5}\;\omega a$

## Explanation

The vertex $A$ is the instantaneous center of rotation, therefore every point $P$ of the plate has speed $\omega\,|P - A|$. The largest speed hence belongs to the vertex farthest from $A$, which is the opposite vertex $C$. By the Pythagorean theorem $|C - A| = \sqrt{(2a)^2 + a^2} = \sqrt{5}\,a$, therefore $|\vec{v}_C| = \sqrt{5}\;\omega a$. The other two vertices have speeds $2\,\omega a$, the vertex $B$, and $\omega a$, the vertex $D$.
