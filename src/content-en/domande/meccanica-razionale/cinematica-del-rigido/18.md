A rigid rod $AB$ moves in the plane and at the instant considered the endpoint $A$ is at rest. Which statement about the velocity of the endpoint $B$ is true?

- $\vec{v}_B$ is parallel to $AB$
- $\vec{v}_B$ is zero
- $\vec{v}_B$ is orthogonal to $AB$
- $\vec{v}_B$ forms with $AB$ an angle that depends on the length of the rod

## Explanation

The point $A$ has zero velocity, therefore it is the instantaneous center of rotation of the rod. For every point $P$ of the rod one then has $\vec{v}_P = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (P - A)$, and the cross product is orthogonal to $P - A$. Applying this relation to $P = B$ one obtains that $\vec{v}_B$ is orthogonal to the segment $AB$ and has magnitude $|\dot{\vartheta}|\,|B - A|$. The velocity of $B$ vanishes only for $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$, the case in which the whole rod is at rest.
