In the system in the figure a disk of radius $R$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, and at the center $G$ a rod $GB$ of length $\ell < R$ is hinged, free to turn in the vertical plane. At the instant considered the rod is vertical and points downward, the center has velocity $\vec{v}_G = v\,\hat{\imath}$ and the rod has angular velocity $\vec{\omega}_{GB} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $v > 0$ and $\omega > 0$. What is the velocity of the endpoint $B$?

::struttura{cerchi="2,1.2,1.2" linee="-0.3,0 4.8,0" aste="G-B" nodi="G,2,1.2,-;B,2,0.3,-" punti="2,1.2,G,baricentro;2,0.3,B;2,0,C,centro" frecce="2,1.2,2.9,1.2,v_G" quote="G,B,ℓ,0.5" titolo="Disk rolling with the rod $GB$ hinged at the center, in the vertical configuration"}

- $v\,\hat{\imath}$
- $(v - \ell\omega)\,\hat{\imath}$
- $(v + \ell\omega)\,\hat{\imath}$
- $v\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\omega\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The point $G$ belongs both to the disk and to the rod, therefore the fundamental formula applied to the rod with pole $G$ gives $\vec{v}_B = \vec{v}_G + \vec{\omega}_{GB} \wedge (B - G)$. At the instant considered one has $B - G = -\,\ell\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence $\omega\,\hat{k} \wedge \big( -\,\ell\,\hat{\jmath} \big) = \ell\omega\,\hat{\imath}$. Adding the two contributions one obtains $\vec{v}_B = (v + \ell\omega)\,\hat{\imath}$. The velocity of $B$ is therefore horizontal, since the contribution of the rotation of the rod is orthogonal to the rod itself.
