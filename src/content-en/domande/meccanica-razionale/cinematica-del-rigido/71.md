A bowling ball of radius $R$ and center $G$ moves in plane motion and slips on a fixed horizontal lane. At the instant considered $\vec{v}_G = v\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{\omega} = -\dfrac{v}{2R}\;\hat{k}$, with $v > 0$. What is the velocity of the point of the ball in contact with the lane?

- $\dfrac{v}{2}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $\vec{0}$
- $\dfrac{3v}{2}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $-\dfrac{v}{2}\;\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The contact point $C$ satisfies $C - G = -R\,\hat{\jmath}$, therefore the fundamental formula gives
$$
\vec{v}_C = v\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{v}{2R}\;\hat{k} \wedge (-R\,\hat{\jmath}) = v\,\hat{\imath} - \frac{v}{2}\;\hat{\imath} = \frac{v}{2}\;\hat{\imath} .
$$
The velocity of the contact point is different from the zero vector, therefore the ball **slips** on the lane in the direction of motion. Rolling without slipping would require $\vec{\omega} = -\dfrac{v}{R}\;\hat{k}$, that is, a rotation twice as fast.
