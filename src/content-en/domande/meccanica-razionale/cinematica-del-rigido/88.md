In the system in the figure an inextensible string has one end fixed to the ceiling, goes down vertically, passes under the movable pulley of radius $R$ and center $G$ and goes back up vertically. The string does not slip on the pulley, and its free end is pulled upward with speed $u$. What is the angular velocity of the pulley?

::struttura{cerchi="0,1,0.5" linee="-1.4,3.5 0.1,3.5;-0.5,3.5 -0.5,1;0.5,1 0.5,3" punti="0,1,G,baricentro;-0.5,1,L;0.5,1,Q" frecce="0.5,3,0.5,3.7,u" riferimento=0 titolo="Movable pulley of radius $R$ supported by a string fixed to the ceiling"}

- $\dfrac{u}{R}\;\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{u}{2R}\;\hat{k}$
- $\dfrac{u}{2R}\;\hat{k}$
- $\dfrac{2u}{R}\;\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The left portion of the string is at rest, because it starts from the ceiling, and the string does not slip, therefore the point $L$ of the pulley in contact with that portion has zero velocity and is the instantaneous center of rotation. The point $Q$ in contact with the right portion has the velocity of the string, $u\,\hat{\jmath}$, and one has $Q - L = 2R\,\hat{\imath}$. The relation $\vec{v}_Q = \omega\,\hat{k} \wedge (Q - L) = 2R\,\omega\,\hat{\jmath}$ then gives $\omega = \dfrac{u}{2R}$. The pulley therefore rotates counterclockwise with angular velocity $\dfrac{u}{2R}\;\hat{k}$, and its center rises with speed $\dfrac{u}{2}$.
