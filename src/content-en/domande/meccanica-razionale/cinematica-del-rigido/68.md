A rod $OA$ is hinged to the ground at the endpoint $O$ and rotates in the plane with $\dot{\vartheta} \neq 0$. What are the fixed centrode and the moving centrode of its motion?

- The fixed centrode is the circle described by $A$, the moving centrode is the rod $OA$
- The fixed centrode and the moving centrode both reduce to the point $O$
- The fixed centrode is the point $O$, the moving centrode is the circle with center $O$ and radius $OA$
- The fixed centrode and the moving centrode are not defined, because the motion has no points at rest

## Explanation

The point $O$ of the rod is at rest at every instant, therefore it is the instantaneous center of rotation for every value of $t$. In the fixed plane the center always occupies the position $O$, hence the fixed centrode reduces to the point $O$. In the plane attached to the rod the center always coincides with the same material point, the endpoint $O$, therefore the moving centrode also reduces to a point. The rolling of the moving centrode on the fixed centrode then reduces to the rotation about a fixed point.
