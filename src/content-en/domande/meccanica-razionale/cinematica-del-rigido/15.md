A rigid plate moves in the plane $Oxy$ and contains the points $A = (0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $B = (3,\,4)\,\mathrm{m}$. The velocity of $A$ is $\vec{v}_A = (5,\,0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the first component of the velocity of $B$ equals $v_{Bx} = 1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the second component $v_{By}$?

- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The differential form of the rigidity constraint requires $(\vec{v}_B - \vec{v}_A) \cdot (B - A) = 0$. One has $B - A = (3,\,4)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\vec{v}_B - \vec{v}_A = (1 - 5,\;v_{By})\,\mathrm{m/s}$, therefore the condition becomes $-12 + 4\,v_{By} = 0$. Then $v_{By} = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With this value the relative velocity of the two points turns out to be orthogonal to the line joining them, as the rigidity constraint requires.
