A rod $OA$ of length $\ell$ is hinged to the ground at the endpoint $O$ and turns in the plane $Oxy$ with angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k}$, where $\vartheta$ is the angle between the rod and the $x$ axis. At the instant at which $\vartheta = 30^\circ$ one has $\dot{\vartheta} = \omega_0 > 0$. What is the velocity of the endpoint $A$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{2}\;\hat{\jmath}$
- $-\dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{2}\;\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\jmath}$
- $\dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{2}\;\hat{\imath} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\jmath}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{2}\;\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The endpoint $O$ is fixed, therefore the fundamental formula gives $\vec{v}_A = \vec{\omega} \wedge (A - O)$. From the geometry it follows that $A - O = \ell\cos\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \ell\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath}$, hence
$$
\vec{v}_A = \ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\big( -\sin\vartheta\;\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\;\hat{\jmath} \big) .
$$
Substituting $\vartheta = 30^\circ$ one obtains $\vec{v}_A = -\dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{2}\;\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\jmath}$. The velocity turns out to be orthogonal to the rod and of magnitude $\ell\omega_0$, as it must be in a rotational state of motion about $O$.
