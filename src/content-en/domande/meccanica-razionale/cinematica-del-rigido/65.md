The rod in the figure has the endpoint $A$ on a fixed horizontal plane and leans, without losing contact, on a fixed half-cylinder of radius $R$, whose axis passes through the point $O$ of the plane. At the instant considered the rod forms the angle $\vartheta = 30^\circ$ with the horizontal and one has $|\dot{\vartheta}| = \omega_0$. What is the speed of $A$?

- $2R\,\omega_0$
- $2\sqrt{3}\,R\,\omega_0$
- $\sqrt{3}\,R\,\omega_0$
- $4R\,\omega_0$

## Explanation

The velocity of $A$ is horizontal, therefore the instantaneous center of rotation $C$ lies on the vertical through $A$. The point of the rod in contact with the half-cylinder has velocity directed along the rod, because the rod stays tangent to the surface, hence $C$ also lies on the line through the contact point orthogonal to the rod, which passes through $O$. The radius drawn to the contact point is orthogonal to the rod, therefore $|A - O| = \dfrac{R}{\sin\vartheta} = 2R$. The line through $O$ orthogonal to the rod forms the angle $\vartheta$ with the vertical, hence it meets the vertical through $A$ at the height
$$
|C - A| = |A - O|\,\frac{\cos\vartheta}{\sin\vartheta} = 2\sqrt{3}\,R .
$$
Then $|\vec{v}_A| = \omega_0\,|C - A| = 2\sqrt{3}\,R\,\omega_0$.
