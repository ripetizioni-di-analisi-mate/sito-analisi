The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure has the end $A$ on the horizontal guide $y = 0$ and stays in contact with the fixed peg $P = (0, h)$, with $h > 0$, on which it slides. What is the fixed centrode of the motion of the rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,2.4,0;B,-1,1.7" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello" linee="-1.8,0 3.4,0" tratteggi="0,-0.3 0,2.3" frecce="3.4,0,3.9,0,x;0,2.3,0,2.8,y" punti="0,1.2,P,pieno;0,0,O" quote="0 0,0 1.2,h,-0.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod with the end $A$ on the horizontal guide, resting on the fixed peg $P$"}

- The parabola with equation $y = \dfrac{x^2}{h}$
- The parabola with equation $y = h - \dfrac{x^2}{h}$
- The parabola with equation $y = h + \dfrac{x^2}{2h}$
- The parabola with equation $y = h + \dfrac{x^2}{h}$

## Explanation

The velocity of $A$ is horizontal, hence the instantaneous center of rotation $C$ lies on the vertical line through $A$. The point of the rod that is on the peg has velocity directed along the rod, because the rod slides on the peg without leaving it, therefore $C$ also lies on the line through $P$ perpendicular to the rod. Calling $x_A$ the abscissa of $A$, the rod has the direction of $P - A = -\,x_A\,\hat{\imath} + h\,\hat{\jmath}$, and the point $C = (x_A, y_C)$ satisfies
$$
(C - P) \cdot (P - A) = -\,x_A^2 + h\,(y_C - h) = 0 ,
$$
from which $y_C = h + \dfrac{x_A^2}{h}$. As $x_A$ varies, the center therefore describes in the fixed plane the parabola $y = h + \dfrac{x^2}{h}$, which is the fixed centrode. The parabola $y = \dfrac{x^2}{h}$ is obtained by measuring the height of $C$ from the peg instead of from the guide.
