A disk of radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed straight horizontal guide. What are the fixed centrode and the moving centrode of the motion of the disk?

- The fixed centrode and the moving centrode are both the straight guide
- The fixed centrode is the circle of radius $R$ of the rim of the disk, the moving centrode is the straight guide
- The fixed centrode is the line parallel to the guide that passes through the center, the moving centrode is the circle of radius $R$
- The fixed centrode is the straight guide, the moving centrode is the circle of radius $R$ of the rim of the disk

## Explanation

The contact point has zero velocity, therefore it is the instantaneous center of rotation of the disk. In the fixed plane that point always lies on the guide, hence the fixed centrode is the straight guide. In the plane attached to the disk that point always lies on the rim, at the distance $R$ from the center, hence the moving centrode is the circle of radius $R$. The motion of the disk is therefore obtained by rolling the circle of the rim without slipping on the guide.
