The rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and the endpoint $B$ on a smooth vertical guide, the two guides meeting at $O$. At the instant considered the rod forms the angle $\vartheta = 45^\circ$ with the horizontal and the endpoint $A$ has velocity of magnitude $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the angular velocity of the rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,1.41,0;B,0,1.41" aste="A-B" linee="-0.4,0 2.3,0;0,-0.4 0,2.3" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,180" punti="1.41,0;0,1.41;0,0,O" etichette="1.0,0.2,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod of length $\ell$ with its endpoints on the two guides, inclined at $45^\circ$"}

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3\sqrt{2}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The instantaneous center of rotation is the intersection of the vertical line through $A$ with the horizontal line through $B$, therefore it lies at the same distance from $A$ as the height of $B$, that is, $\ell\sin\vartheta$. From the relation $|\vec{v}_A| = |\dot{\vartheta}|\,\ell\sin\vartheta$ one gets $|\dot{\vartheta}| = \dfrac{|\vec{v}_A|}{\ell\sin\vartheta}$. Substituting $\ell = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\sin 45^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ one obtains $|\dot{\vartheta}| = \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{2}}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$. The magnitude of the angular velocity therefore equals $\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$, that is, about $2.12\;\mathrm{rad/s}$.
