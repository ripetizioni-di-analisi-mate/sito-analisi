A plate moves in the plane $Oxy$ and at the instant considered it rotates with angular speed $2\;\mathrm{rad/s}$ about its instantaneous center of rotation $C$. Which points of the plane attached to the plate have, at that instant, speed $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- The points of the circle with center $C$ and radius $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- The points of the circle with center $C$ and radius $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- The points of the circle with center $C$ and radius $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- The points of a line through $C$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the instantaneous center of rotation every point $P$ has velocity $\vec{v}_P = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (P - C)$, of magnitude $|\dot{\vartheta}|\,|P - C|$. The condition $|\vec{v}_P| = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with $|\dot{\vartheta}| = 2\;\mathrm{rad/s}$, is therefore equivalent to $|P - C| = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. The points sought hence form the **circle** with center $C$ and radius $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Their velocities are tangent to that circle, therefore they have the same magnitude and different directions.
