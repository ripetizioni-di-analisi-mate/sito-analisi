A rigid rod $AB$ moves in the plane with $\dot{\vartheta} \neq 0$, and at the instant considered the velocity of $A$ is nonzero and parallel to $AB$. Where is the instantaneous center of rotation?

- On the line $AB$
- At $A$
- On the line through $A$ orthogonal to $AB$
- On the line through $B$ parallel to $AB$

## Explanation

From the relation $\vec{v}_A = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (A - C)$ it follows that $\vec{v}_A$ is orthogonal to $A - C$, therefore the center belongs to the line through $A$ orthogonal to $\vec{v}_A$. The velocity of $A$ is parallel to $AB$, hence that line is the perpendicular to $AB$ drawn through $A$. The center does not coincide with $A$, since $\vec{v}_A$ is different from the zero vector. The point $A$ therefore moves along the direction of the rod, as happens to a rod that runs inside a sleeve.
