The two gear wheels in the figure, of radii $R_1$ and $R_2$, rotate about fixed axes through their centers $O_1$ and $O_2$ and mesh externally at the point $C$, without slipping. Their angular velocities are $\omega_1\,\hat{k}$ and $\omega_2\,\hat{k}$. Which relation holds?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.5;2.5,0,1" nodi="O_1,0,0;O_2,2.5,0" vincoli="O_1,cerniera;O_2,cerniera" punti="1.5,0,C" quote="O_1,1.5 0,R_1,-1.9;1.5 0,O_2,R_2,-1.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Two gear wheels in external contact at the point $C$"}

- $R_1\,\omega_1 = R_2\,\omega_2$
- $R_2\,\omega_1 = -\,R_1\,\omega_2$
- $R_1\,\omega_1 = -\,R_2\,\omega_2$
- $\omega_1 = -\,\omega_2$

## Explanation

The contact without slipping requires that the points of the two wheels occupying the position $C$ have the same velocity. The centers are at rest and $C - O_1 = R_1\,\hat{\imath}$, $C - O_2 = -\,R_2\,\hat{\imath}$, therefore the fundamental formula gives
$$
\vec{v}_C^{\,(1)} = \omega_1\,\hat{k} \wedge R_1\,\hat{\imath} = R_1\,\omega_1\,\hat{\jmath} ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_C^{\,(2)} = \omega_2\,\hat{k} \wedge (-\,R_2\,\hat{\imath}) = -\,R_2\,\omega_2\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
Equating the two velocities one obtains $R_1\,\omega_1 = -\,R_2\,\omega_2$. The two wheels therefore turn in **opposite senses**, and the wheel of smaller radius turns faster.
