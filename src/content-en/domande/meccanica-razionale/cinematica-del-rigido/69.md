The rod in the figure has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide, taken as the $x$ axis, and slides inside a fixed sleeve hinged at the point $D = (0,\,h)$. What is the equation of the fixed centrode of the motion of the rod?

::struttura{nodi="A,-1.73,0;B,0.77,4.33" aste="A-B" linee="-3,0 1.6,0;0.03,3.45 0.37,3.25 -0.03,2.55 -0.37,2.75 0.03,3.45" punti="-1.73,0;0.77,4.33;0,3,D;0,0,O" vincoli="A,carrello" tratteggi="0,0 0,3" quote="0 0,0 3,h,0.5" etichette="-1.05,0.3,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod in the fixed sleeve $D$, with the endpoint $A$ on the horizontal guide"}

- $y = \dfrac{x^2}{h}$
- $y = h + \dfrac{x^2}{2h}$
- $y = h + \dfrac{x^2}{h}$
- $x^2 + (y - h)^2 = h^2$

## Explanation

The velocity of $A$ is horizontal, therefore the instantaneous center of rotation $C$ lies on the vertical through $A$ and has abscissa $x_C = x_A$. The point of the rod passing through $D$ has velocity directed along the rod, because the rod slides in the sleeve, hence $C$ also lies on the line through $D$ orthogonal to the rod. If $\vartheta$ is the angle between the rod and the horizontal, one has $|x_A| = \dfrac{h\cos\vartheta}{\sin\vartheta}$, and the line through $D$ orthogonal to the rod, which forms the angle $\vartheta$ with the vertical, meets the vertical through $A$ at the height
$$
y_C = h + |x_A|\,\frac{\cos\vartheta}{\sin\vartheta} = h + \frac{x_A^2}{h} .
$$
Eliminating $\vartheta$ one obtains the fixed centrode $y = h + \dfrac{x^2}{h}$, a **parabola** with its vertex at the point $D$.
