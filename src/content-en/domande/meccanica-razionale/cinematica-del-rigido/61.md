A helical rigid state of motion has angular velocity $\vec{\omega}$ and pitch $p > 0$. Where are the points of the body whose velocity forms the angle of $45^\circ$ with $\vec{\omega}$?

- On the cylindrical surface of radius $p$ whose axis is the Mozzi axis
- On the cylindrical surface of radius $\sqrt{2}\,p$ whose axis is the Mozzi axis
- On the cylindrical surface of radius $p\,|\vec{\omega}|$ whose axis is the Mozzi axis
- On the Mozzi axis

## Explanation

By Mozzi's theorem the velocity of a point $P$ is $\vec{v}_P = \vec{v}_{\min} + \vec{\omega} \wedge (P - P^*)$, where $\vec{v}_{\min} = p\,\vec{\omega}$ is parallel to $\vec{\omega}$ and $P^*$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to the axis. If $d$ is the distance of $P$ from the Mozzi axis, the second term is orthogonal to $\vec{\omega}$ and has magnitude $|\vec{\omega}|\,d$. The angle between $\vec{v}_P$ and $\vec{\omega}$ equals $45^\circ$ when the two components have the same magnitude, that is, when $p\,|\vec{\omega}| = |\vec{\omega}|\,d$, therefore $d = p$. The points sought hence form the **cylindrical surface of radius $p$** coaxial with the Mozzi axis, whereas on the axis the velocity is parallel to $\vec{\omega}$.
