In the system in the figure a horizontal board rests on two equal cylindrical rollers of radius $R$, which in turn rest on the fixed floor, and all contacts take place without slipping. The rollers rotate with angular velocity $-\,\omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. What is the velocity of the board?

::struttura{cerchi="1,0.5,0.5;4,0.5,0.5" linee="-0.6,0 5.6,0" poligoni="-0.3,1 5.3,1 5.3,1.25 -0.3,1.25" punti="1,0.5,,baricentro;4,0.5,,baricentro" coppie="1 0.5,-1,ω;4 0.5,-1,ω" riferimento=0 titolo="Board on two rollers of radius $R$ rolling on the floor"}

- $\omega R\;\hat{\imath}$
- $-2\,\omega R\;\hat{\imath}$
- $\dfrac{\omega R}{2}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $2\,\omega R\;\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

Each roller rolls without slipping on the fixed floor, therefore its contact point with the floor is the instantaneous center of rotation. The highest point of the roller is at distance $2R$ from that center, hence its velocity is $-\,\omega\,\hat{k} \wedge 2R\,\hat{\jmath} = 2\,\omega R\;\hat{\imath}$. The board stays horizontal, therefore it has a translational state of motion, and the contact with the rollers takes place without slipping, hence its velocity is $2\,\omega R\;\hat{\imath}$. The centers of the rollers instead move with velocity $\omega R\;\hat{\imath}$, therefore the board moves **twice** as far as the rollers.
