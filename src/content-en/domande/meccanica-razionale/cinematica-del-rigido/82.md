A rigid body has a fixed point $O$, and at the instant considered the unit vector of the attached axis $\zeta$ equals $\hat{e}_3 = \left( \dfrac{1}{2},\; -\dfrac{1}{2},\; \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$ in the fixed frame. What are the nutation angle $\vartheta$ and the precession angle $\psi$?

- $\vartheta = 45^\circ$ and $\psi = 45^\circ$
- $\vartheta = 45^\circ$ and $\psi = 135^\circ$
- $\vartheta = 45^\circ$ and $\psi = 225^\circ$
- $\vartheta = 135^\circ$ and $\psi = 45^\circ$

## Explanation

The nutation is the angle between $\hat{k}$ and $\hat{e}_3$, therefore $\cos\vartheta = \hat{k} \cdot \hat{e}_3 = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ and, since $0 \le \vartheta \le \pi$, one has $\vartheta = 45^\circ$. The line of nodes has unit vector $\hat{N} = \dfrac{\hat{k} \wedge \hat{e}_3}{|\hat{k} \wedge \hat{e}_3|}$, and one has $\hat{k} \wedge \hat{e}_3 = \left( \dfrac{1}{2},\; \dfrac{1}{2},\; 0 \right)$, of magnitude $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. Then $\hat{N} = \left( \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\; \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\; 0 \right)$, which forms the angle $\psi = 45^\circ$ with $\hat{\imath}$. The proper rotation $\varphi$ remains undetermined, because it depends on the position of $\hat{e}_1$, which the data do not assign.
