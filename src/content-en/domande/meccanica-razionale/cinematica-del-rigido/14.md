The two homogeneous disks in the figure, of different radii $R_1$ and $R_2$, roll without slipping on the same fixed horizontal guide and are connected by a rigid rod hinged at their centers $G_1$ and $G_2$. The center $G_1$ has velocity $\vec{v}_{G_1} = v\,\hat{\imath}$, with $v > 0$. What is the angular velocity of the disk of radius $R_2$?

::struttura{cerchi="1.2,1.2,1.2;4.2,0.7,0.7" linee="-0.3,0 5.6,0" aste="G1-G2" nodi="G1,1.2,1.2,-;G2,4.2,0.7,-" punti="1.2,1.2,G_1,baricentro;4.2,0.7,G_2,baricentro;1.2,0,C_1,centro;4.2,0,C_2,centro" titolo="Two disks of different radii connected by a rod hinged at their centers"}

- $-\dfrac{v}{R_2}\;\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{v}{R_1}\;\hat{k}$
- $\dfrac{v}{R_2}\;\hat{k}$
- $-\dfrac{2v}{R_2}\;\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The two centers stay at the constant heights $R_1$ and $R_2$, and their distance does not change, therefore the rod has a translational state of motion and $\vec{v}_{G_2} = \vec{v}_{G_1} = v\,\hat{\imath}$ holds. The second disk rolls without slipping on the fixed guide, hence its contact point $C_2$ is the instantaneous center of rotation and lies at the distance $R_2$ from the center $G_2$. From the condition $\vec{v}_{C_2} = \vec{0}$ it follows that $\vec{\omega}_2 = -\dfrac{v}{R_2}\;\hat{k}$, that is, a rotation in the clockwise sense. The disk of smaller radius therefore turns more rapidly, even though its center has the same velocity as the other one.
