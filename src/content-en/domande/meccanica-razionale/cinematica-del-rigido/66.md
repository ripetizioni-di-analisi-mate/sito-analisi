In the system in the figure the slider $H$ runs on a fixed horizontal guide and carries a rod $HB$ of length $\ell$, hinged at $H$ and free to turn in the vertical plane. The angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical is positive counterclockwise. At the instant considered $\vec{v}_H = v\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\dot{\vartheta} > 0$, with $v > 0$. Where is the instantaneous center of rotation $C$ of the rod?

::struttura{nodi="H,0,0;B,1,-1.732" aste="H-B" linee="-2,0 3,0" vincoli="H,carrello,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2" etichette="0.2,-0.75,ϑ" frecce="0.6,-0.3,1.6,-0.3,v_H" riferimento=0 titolo="Slider on a horizontal guide with the rod $HB$ hinged at $H$"}

- $C - H = -\dfrac{v}{\dot{\vartheta}}\;\hat{\jmath}$
- $C - H = \dfrac{v}{\dot{\vartheta}}\;\hat{\imath}$
- $C - H = \dfrac{v}{\dot{\vartheta}}\;\hat{\jmath}$
- $C - H = -\dfrac{v}{\dot{\vartheta}}\;\hat{\imath}$

## Explanation

The rod has angular velocity $\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k}$ and its point $H$ has velocity $v\,\hat{\imath}$, therefore the instantaneous center of rotation is
$$
C = H + \frac{\hat{k} \wedge v\,\hat{\imath}}{\dot{\vartheta}} = H + \frac{v}{\dot{\vartheta}}\;\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The center therefore lies on the vertical through $H$, on the side opposite to the rod, at distance $\dfrac{v}{\dot{\vartheta}}$ from $H$, and its position does not depend on the angle $\vartheta$. The vertical through $H$ is the line orthogonal to $\vec{v}_H$, as the geometric construction of the center requires. The sense is checked with the relation $\vec{v}_H = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge (H - C) = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge \left( -\dfrac{v}{\dot{\vartheta}}\,\hat{\jmath} \right) = v\,\hat{\imath}$.
