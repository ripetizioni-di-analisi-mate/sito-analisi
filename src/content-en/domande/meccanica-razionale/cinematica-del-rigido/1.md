A rigid rod $AB$ of length $\ell$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ and forms the angle of $45^\circ$ with the $x$ axis. At the instant considered the endpoint $A$ has zero acceleration and the rod has constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. What is the acceleration of the endpoint $B$?

- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\omega\ell\,\big( \hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} \big)$
- $\vec{0}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\omega^2\ell\,\big( \hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} \big)$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\omega^2\ell\,\big( \hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} \big)$

## Explanation

The acceleration field of a rigid body is given by $\vec{a}_B = \vec{a}_A + \dot{\vec{\omega}} \wedge (B - A) + \vec{\omega} \wedge \big( \vec{\omega} \wedge (B - A) \big)$. The angular velocity is constant, therefore $\dot{\vec{\omega}} = \vec{0}$ and the second summand vanishes. The motion is plane, hence $\vec{\omega}$ is orthogonal to $B - A$ and the third summand equals $-\,\omega^2\,(B - A)$. Since $B - A = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\ell\,\big( \hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} \big)$ and $\vec{a}_A = \vec{0}$, one obtains
$$
\vec{a}_B = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\omega^2\ell\,\big( \hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} \big) ,
$$
directed from $B$ toward $A$ and of magnitude $\omega^2\ell$.
