A rigid body with a fixed point moves with a regular precession with constant nutation angle $\vartheta = 45^\circ$ and with $\dot{\psi} = \dot{\varphi} = \Omega > 0$. What is the magnitude of the angular velocity?

- $\sqrt{2}\;\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$
- $\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}}\;\Omega$
- $\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}\;\Omega$

## Explanation

For a rigid body with a fixed point the magnitude of the angular velocity in terms of the Euler angles satisfies
$$
|\vec{\omega}|^2 = \dot{\psi}^{\,2} + \dot{\vartheta}^{\,2} + \dot{\varphi}^{\,2} + 2\,\dot{\psi}\dot{\varphi}\cos\vartheta .
$$
The precession is regular, therefore the nutation is constant and $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$. Substituting $\dot{\psi} = \dot{\varphi} = \Omega$ and $\cos 45^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ one obtains $|\vec{\omega}|^2 = \big( 2 + \sqrt{2} \big)\,\Omega^2$. The magnitude of the angular velocity therefore equals $\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}\;\Omega$, that is, about $1.85\,\Omega$.
