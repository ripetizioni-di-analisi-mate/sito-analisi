The double pendulum in the figure consists of the rod $OA$, hinged to the ground at $O$, and of the rod $AB$, hinged to the first one at $A$, both of length $\ell$ and free to turn in the vertical plane. The angles $\vartheta$ and $\psi$ that the two rods form with the downward vertical are positive counterclockwise. At the instant considered $OA$ is horizontal, with $A$ to the right of $O$, and $AB$ is vertical and points downward. What is the velocity of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,2,0;B,2,-2" aste="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2" etichette="0.5,-0.5,ϑ" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.5;A,B,ℓ,-0.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Double pendulum with $OA$ horizontal and $AB$ vertical"}

- $\ell\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\dot{\psi}\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $\ell\dot{\psi}\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $-\,\ell\dot{\psi}\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $\ell\,(\dot{\vartheta} + \dot{\psi})\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The angles are measured from a fixed direction counterclockwise, therefore the angular velocities of the two rods are $\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k}$ and $\dot{\psi}\,\hat{k}$. The rod $OA$ rotates about the fixed point $O$ and $A - O = \ell\,\hat{\imath}$, hence $\vec{v}_A = \dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{k} \wedge \ell\,\hat{\imath} = \ell\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath}$. The fundamental formula for the rod $AB$ with pole $A$, with $B - A = -\ell\,\hat{\jmath}$, gives
$$
\vec{v}_B = \ell\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath} + \dot{\psi}\,\hat{k} \wedge (-\ell\,\hat{\jmath}) = \ell\dot{\psi}\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The contribution of each rod is orthogonal to the rod itself, hence in the configuration considered the two contributions are orthogonal to each other.
