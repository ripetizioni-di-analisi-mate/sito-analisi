A disk of radius $R$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, with $\vec{v}_G = v\,\hat{\imath}$ and $v > 0$. What is the magnitude of the velocity of the point $P$ of the rim that at the instant considered is at the same height as the center?

- $v$
- $\sqrt{2}\,v$
- $2v$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,v$

## Explanation

The contact point $C$ is the instantaneous center of rotation, therefore for every point $Q$ of the disk one has $|\vec{v}_Q| = |\vec{\omega}|\,|Q - C|$, with $|\vec{\omega}| = \dfrac{v}{R}$. The point $P$ lies on the rim at the height of the center, hence $P - C$ has a horizontal component of magnitude $R$ and a vertical component of magnitude $R$, and therefore $|P - C| = \sqrt{2}\,R$. Then $|\vec{v}_P| = \dfrac{v}{R}\,\sqrt{2}\,R = \sqrt{2}\,v$. The magnitude of the velocity of the points of the rim therefore varies between $0$ at the contact and $2v$ at the diametrically opposite point.
