The right circular cone in the figure, of half-angle $\alpha$, has its vertex fixed at $O$ and rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal plane, on which it rests along a generator. Its axis rotates about the vertical through $O$ with constant angular speed $\Omega$. What is the magnitude of the angular velocity of the cone?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 2.57,3.06" linee="-0.6,0 4.8,0" tratteggi="0,0 3.29,1.53;0,0 0,3.4" punti="0,0,O" etichette="1.45,0.28,α;0.25,3.3,Ω" riferimento=0 titolo="Cone of half-angle $\alpha$ with the fixed vertex $O$, resting on the plane along a generator"}

- $\dfrac{\Omega}{\sin\alpha}$
- $\dfrac{\Omega\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}$
- $\Omega\cos\alpha$
- $\dfrac{\Omega\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$

## Explanation

The points of the contact generator have zero velocity, because the cone rolls without slipping on a fixed plane, therefore the angular velocity $\vec{\omega}$ is directed along that generator. A point $Q$ of the axis at distance $d$ from $O$ is at distance $d\sin\alpha$ from the contact generator, hence $|\vec{v}_Q| = |\vec{\omega}|\,d\sin\alpha$. The same point describes a horizontal circle of radius $d\cos\alpha$ about the vertical through $O$, therefore $|\vec{v}_Q| = \Omega\,d\cos\alpha$. Equating the two expressions one obtains
$$
|\vec{\omega}| = \frac{\Omega\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha} .
$$
The motion of the cone is a regular precession, with axis of instantaneous rotation along the contact generator.
