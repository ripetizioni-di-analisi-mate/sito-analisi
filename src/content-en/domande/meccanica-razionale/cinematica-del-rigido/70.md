Over a given interval the instantaneous centre of a planar mechanism traces a fixed-centrode arc of length $18\,\mathrm{mm}$. Contact between the fixed and moving centrodes is regular. What length of moving-centrode arc has passed through the contact in the same interval?

- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mm}$
- The fixed centrode's radius of curvature is required.
- $18\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The two centrodes roll on one another without slipping. The developed arc lengths are therefore equal even if their curvatures differ.
