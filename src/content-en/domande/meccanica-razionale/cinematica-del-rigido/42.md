A rigid body with a fixed point moves with constant nutation angle $\vartheta = 60^\circ$ and with $\dot{\psi} = \Omega$ and $\dot{\varphi} = 2\,\Omega$, where $\Omega > 0$. What is the component $\omega_\zeta$ of the angular velocity along the attached axis $\zeta$?

- $3\,\Omega$
- $\dfrac{\Omega}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\Omega$
- $\left( 2 + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)\Omega$

## Explanation

The component of the angular velocity along the attached axis $\zeta$ equals $\omega_\zeta = \dot{\psi}\cos\vartheta + \dot{\varphi}$. The nutation angle is constant, therefore $\dot{\vartheta} = 0$ and the term of the line of nodes does not appear, since $\hat{N}$ is orthogonal to $\hat{e}_3$. Substituting $\cos 60^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dot{\psi} = \Omega$ and $\dot{\varphi} = 2\,\Omega$ one obtains $\omega_\zeta = \dfrac{\Omega}{2} + 2\,\Omega$. Then $\omega_\zeta = \dfrac{5}{2}\,\Omega$.
