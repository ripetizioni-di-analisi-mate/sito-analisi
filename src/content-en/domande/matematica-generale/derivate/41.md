The function $f$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$ and has a local minimum at $x_0 = 2$. Which statement is necessarily true?

- $f''(2) > 0$
- $f'(2) = 0$
- $f(2) = 0$
- $f'(2) > 0$

## Explanation

The point $2$ is interior to the domain $\mathbb{R}$ and $f$ is differentiable at $2$, so by **Fermat's theorem** one has $f'(2) = 0$. The condition $f''(2) > 0$ may fail to hold. For example $f(x) = (x - 2)^4$ has a minimum at $2$, but its second derivative $12\,(x - 2)^2$ vanishes at $2$. The value of the minimum depends on the function, and for example $(x - 2)^2 + 1$ has a minimum at $2$ and equals $1$ at that point.
