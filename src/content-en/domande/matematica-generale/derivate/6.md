- $f'(x) = 4\,(x^2 - 3x)^3$
- $f'(x) = 4\,(2x - 3)^3$
- $f'(x) = 4\,(x^2 - 3x)^3\,(2x - 3)$
- $f'(x) = 3\,(x^2 - 3x)^3\,(2x - 3)$

## Explanation

The outer function is the power $t^4$, and the inner function is $g(x) = x^2 - 3x$. The derivative of the outer function is $4t^3$ and the derivative of the inner function is $g'(x) = 2x - 3$. By the **chain rule**, we evaluate $4t^3$ at $t = x^2 - 3x$ and multiply by $g'(x)$, so
$$
f'(x) = 4\,(x^2 - 3x)^3\,(2x - 3) .
$$
