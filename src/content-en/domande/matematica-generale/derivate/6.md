- $f'(x) = 4\,(x^2 - 3x)^3$
- $f'(x) = 4\,(2x - 3)^3$
- $f'(x) = 4\,(x^2 - 3x)^3\,(2x - 3)$
- $f'(x) = 3\,(x^2 - 3x)^3\,(2x - 3)$

## Explanation

The function is a composite function, with the power $t^4$ as outer function and $g(x) = x^2 - 3x$ as inner function. The derivative of the outer function is $4t^3$ and the derivative of the inner function is $g'(x) = 2x - 3$. By the **composite function rule** one evaluates $4t^3$ at $t = x^2 - 3x$ and multiplies by $g'(x)$, so
$$
f'(x) = 4\,(x^2 - 3x)^3\,(2x - 3) .
$$
