The demand function for a good is $q(p) = 1000\,e^{-0{,}05\,p}$. What is the price elasticity of demand at $p = 40$?

- $-0{,}05$
- $2$
- $-0{,}00125$
- $-2$

## Explanation

By the chain rule $q'(p) = -0{,}05 \cdot 1000\,e^{-0{,}05\,p} = -0{,}05\,q(p)$. The **price elasticity of demand** is therefore
$$
\varepsilon(p) = \frac{p}{q(p)} \cdot \big( -0{,}05\,q(p) \big) = -0{,}05\,p ,
$$
and at a price of $40$ it equals $-2$. Since $|\varepsilon(40)| > 1$, demand is elastic at that price.
