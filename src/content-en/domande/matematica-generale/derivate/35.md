The price of a good rises from $10$ to $10.5$ euros and the quantity demanded falls from $200$ to $188$ units. What estimate of the elasticity of demand at the price $10$ is obtained from the ratio of the percentage changes?

- $1.2$
- $-24$
- $-0.83$
- $-1.2$

## Explanation

The percentage change in quantity is $\dfrac{\Delta q}{q} = \dfrac{-12}{200} = -0.06$, that is, $-6\%$.

The percentage change in price is $\dfrac{\Delta p}{p} = \dfrac{0.5}{10} = 0.05$, that is, $+5\%$.

Since for small changes $\dfrac{\Delta q}{q} \approx \varepsilon\,\dfrac{\Delta p}{p}$, the elasticity is approximately
$$
\varepsilon \approx \frac{-0.06}{0.05} = -1.2 .
$$
The value $-24$ is the ratio $\dfrac{\Delta q}{\Delta p}$, which depends on the units of measurement and is not an elasticity.
