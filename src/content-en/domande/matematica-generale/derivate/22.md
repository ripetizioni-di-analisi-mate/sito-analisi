Two sensors report $g(t)$ and $y(t)=e^{g(t)}$. At the same instant the readings are $y=4$ and $y'=6$. At what rate is $g$ changing at that instant?

- $24$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$
- $2$

## Explanation

The chain rule gives $y'=e^g g'=yg'$. Dividing the measured rate by the level of the second sensor recovers the rate for the first, $g'=\dfrac64=\dfrac32$.
