What is the derivative of $f(x) = e^{x^2 - 2x}$?

- $f'(x) = e^{x^2 - 2x}$
- $f'(x) = (2x - 2)\,e^{x^2 - 2x}$
- $f'(x) = (x^2 - 2x)\,e^{x^2 - 2x - 1}$
- $f'(x) = (2x - 2)\,e^{2x - 2}$

## Explanation

The function is a composition, with outer function $e^{t}$ and inner function $g(x) = x^2 - 2x$. The derivative of $e^{t}$ is $e^{t}$, and the derivative of the inner function is $g'(x) = 2x - 2$. By the **chain rule**, we evaluate $e^{t}$ at $t = x^2 - 2x$ and multiply by $g'(x)$, so $f'(x) = (2x - 2)\,e^{x^2 - 2x}$.
