At the current output level two departments account for $75\%$ and $25\%$ of total cost, respectively. Their cost elasticities with respect to the same output are $1$ and $3$. What is total cost elasticity?

- $2$
- $1.5$
- $2.5$
- $4$

## Explanation

Since $C=C_1+C_2$, the ratio $\dfrac{qC'}{C}$ is a weighted average of the departments’ elasticities, with their shares of cost as weights. This gives $0.75\cdot1+0.25\cdot3=1.5$.
