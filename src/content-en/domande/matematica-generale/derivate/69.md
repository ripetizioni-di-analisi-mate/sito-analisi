The production cost is $C(q) = 100 + 5q^{2}$. What is the elasticity of the cost with respect to the quantity, $\varepsilon_C(q) = \dfrac{q}{C(q)}\,C'(q)$, at $q = 10$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $100$

## Explanation

One has $C'(q) = 10q$, so $C'(10) = 100$, and $C(10) = 100 + 500 = 600$. Therefore
$$
\varepsilon_C(10) = \frac{10}{600} \cdot 100 = \frac{5}{3} .
$$
The elasticity is also the ratio between the marginal cost $C'(10) = 100$ and the average cost $\bar{C}(10) = 60$. Since it is greater than $1$, the marginal cost exceeds the average cost, and the average cost is increasing at $q = 10$.
