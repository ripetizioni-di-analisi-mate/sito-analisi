What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{- 3 x + e^{3 x} - 1}{x^{2}}$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $9$

## Explanation

One application of l’Hôpital’s rule gives another $\dfrac00$ form. The second gives the quotient of $a^2e^{ax}$ and $2$, with $a$ the stated coefficient.
