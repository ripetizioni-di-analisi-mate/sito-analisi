What is the derivative of $\dfrac{4 x + 7}{x + 9}$?

- $\dfrac{29}{\left(x + 9\right)^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{29}{\left(x + 9\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{43}{\left(x + 9\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{29}{x + 9}$

## Explanation

The quotient rule gives numerator $4(x+9)-(4x+7)=29$. Square the denominator.
