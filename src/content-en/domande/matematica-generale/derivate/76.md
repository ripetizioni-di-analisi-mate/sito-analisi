What is the derivative of $\ln{\left(4 x^{2} + 7 \right)}$?

- $\dfrac{2 x}{4 x^{2} + 7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4 x^{2} + 7}$
- $8 x$
- $\dfrac{8 x}{4 x^{2} + 7}$

## Explanation

Differentiate the argument to get $8x$, then divide by the argument itself. The denominator is positive for every $x$.
