At the current price the elasticity of demand for a good is $-1.5$. By approximately how much does the quantity demanded change if the price increases by $2\%$?

- $+3\%$
- $-3\%$
- $-0.75\%$
- $-1.5\%$

## Explanation

For a small change in the price one has $\dfrac{\Delta q}{q} \approx \varepsilon\,\dfrac{\Delta p}{p}$. With $\varepsilon = -1.5$ and $\dfrac{\Delta p}{p} = 2\%$ one gets $\dfrac{\Delta q}{q} \approx -1.5 \cdot 2\% = -3\%$. So the quantity demanded **decreases by about $3\%$**. The sign is negative because demand decreases as the price increases.
