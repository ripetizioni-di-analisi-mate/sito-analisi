The function $f$ is differentiable and positive, and at the point $x_0 > 0$ it has elasticity $\varepsilon_f(x_0) = 0.4$. What is the elasticity of the function $g(x) = f(x)^3$ at $x_0$?

- $0.4$
- $1.2$
- $0.064$
- $3.4$

## Explanation

By the chain rule $g'(x) = 3 f(x)^2 f'(x)$. Hence
$$
\varepsilon_g(x) = \frac{x}{f(x)^3} \cdot 3 f(x)^2 f'(x) = 3\,\frac{x}{f(x)}\,f'(x) = 3\,\varepsilon_f(x) .
$$
Therefore $\varepsilon_g(x_0) = 3 \cdot 0.4 = 1.2$. In percentage terms, if $f$ grows by about $0.4\%$, its cube grows by about three times as much, that is, by $1.2\%$.
