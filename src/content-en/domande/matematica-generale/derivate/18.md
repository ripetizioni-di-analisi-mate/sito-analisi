For $f(x) = x^3 - x$, what is the slope of the secant line through the points of the graph with abscissas $0$ and $2$?

- $11$
- $6$
- $-1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The slope of the secant is the difference quotient of $f$ between $0$ and $2$. One has $f(0) = 0$ and $f(2) = 8 - 2 = 6$, so
$$
\frac{f(2) - f(0)}{2 - 0} = \frac{6}{2} = 3 .
$$
The values $f'(2) = 11$ and $f'(0) = -1$ are the slopes of the tangent lines at the two points, and $6$ is the change in $f$ before dividing by the change in the variable.
