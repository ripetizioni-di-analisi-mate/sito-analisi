The functions $f$ and $g$ are differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, with $g(1) = 2$, $g'(1) = 3$, $f'(1) = 4$ and $f'(2) = 5$. What is the derivative of $h(x) = f(g(x))$ at $x = 1$?

- $12$
- $15$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

By the **chain rule** $h'(x) = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$. At $x = 1$ the derivative of the outer function is evaluated at the point $g(1) = 2$, so $h'(1) = f'(2) \cdot g'(1) = 5 \cdot 3 = 15$. The value $12 = f'(1) \cdot g'(1)$ is obtained by evaluating $f'$ at the point $1$ instead of at $g(1)$, and the value $5$ is obtained without the factor $g'(1)$.
