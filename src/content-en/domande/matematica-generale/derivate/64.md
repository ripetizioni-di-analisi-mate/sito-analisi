A point moves along a line with position $s(t) = t^{3} - 6t^{2} + 9t$, in meters, where $t \geq 0$ is the time in seconds. The velocity is $s'(t)$ and the acceleration is $s''(t)$. What is the acceleration at the first instant $t > 0$ at which the velocity is zero?

- $6$ m/s²
- $0$ m/s²
- $4$ m/s²
- $-6$ m/s²

## Explanation

The velocity is $s'(t) = 3t^{2} - 12t + 9 = 3\,(t - 1)(t - 3)$, so it vanishes at $t = 1$ and at $t = 3$. The first instant is $t = 1$. The acceleration is the second derivative $s''(t) = 6t - 12$, hence $s''(1) = -6$ m/s². The value $4$ is the position $s(1)$, and the value $6$ is the acceleration at the instant $t = 3$.
