What is the derivative in the interior of the domain of $\sqrt{3 x + 5}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{3 x + 5}}$
- $3 \sqrt{3 x + 5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2 \sqrt{3 x + 5}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 \sqrt{3 x + 5}}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the square root of $u$ is $\dfrac1{2\sqrt u}$. The chain rule multiplies this by $u\prime=3$.
