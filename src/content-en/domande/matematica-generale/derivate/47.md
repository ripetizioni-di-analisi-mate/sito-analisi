What is the derivative of $\left(x - 2\right) e^{- 3 x}$?

- $\left(6 - 3 x\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $\left(7 - 3 x\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $\left(3 x - 5\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $e^{- 3 x}$

## Explanation

Apply the product rule. Differentiating the exponential produces a factor of $-3$, and the common factor $e^{-3x}$ can be taken out.
