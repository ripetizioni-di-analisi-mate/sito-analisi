What is the derivative of $\left(x - 2\right) e^{- 3 x}$?

- $\left(6 - 3 x\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $\left(7 - 3 x\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $\left(3 x - 5\right) e^{- 3 x}$
- $e^{- 3 x}$

## Explanation

The product rule applies. Differentiating the exponential gives a factor of $-3$. Then factor out $e^{-3x}$.
