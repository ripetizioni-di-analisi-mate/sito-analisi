The supply of a good is $q_s(p) = 2p - 10$, for $p > 5$. For which prices is supply elastic, that is, its elasticity $\varepsilon(p) = \dfrac{p}{q_s(p)}\,q_s'(p)$ has absolute value greater than $1$?

- For every $p > 5$
- Only for $p > 10$
- Only for $5 < p < 10$
- For no price $p > 5$

## Explanation

Since $q_s'(p) = 2$, the elasticity is
$$
\varepsilon(p) = \frac{2p}{2p - 10} = \frac{p}{p - 5} .
$$
For $p > 5$ numerator and denominator are positive and $p > p - 5$, hence $\varepsilon(p) > 1$.

Therefore supply is elastic at every price $p > 5$. For example at $p = 10$ the elasticity is $2$ and at $p = 6$ it is $6$.
