The demand function for a good is $q(p) = 120 - 3p$, for $0 < p < 40$. At what price is demand unit elastic?

- $p = 20$
- $p = 40$
- $p = 30$
- $p = 10$

## Explanation

The price elasticity of demand is $\varepsilon(p) = \dfrac{p}{q(p)}\,q'(p) = \dfrac{-3p}{120 - 3p}$. Demand is unit elastic when $|\varepsilon(p)| = 1$, that is, when $3p = 120 - 3p$, so for $p = 20$. At a price of $30$ the elasticity equals $-3$ and demand is elastic, while at a price of $10$ it equals $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ and demand is inelastic. At a price of $40$ the quantity demanded is zero, hence the elasticity is not defined.
