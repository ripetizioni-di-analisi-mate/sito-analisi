What is the tangent line to the graph of $f(x) = x^3 - 2x$ at the point with abscissa $1$?

- $y = x$
- $y = 3x - 4$
- $y = x - 2$
- $y = x - 1$

## Explanation

One has $f(1) = 1 - 2 = -1$. The derivative is $f'(x) = 3x^2 - 2$, so the slope of the tangent is $f'(1) = 3 - 2 = 1$. By the **tangent line** formula $y = f(1) + f'(1)\,(x - 1)$ one gets $y = -1 + (x - 1)$, that is, $y = x - 2$.
