Which line through the origin is tangent to the graph of $f(x) = e^{x}$?

- $y = x$
- $y = x + 1$
- $y = e\,x$
- $y = \dfrac{x}{e}$

## Explanation

The tangent at the point with $x$-coordinate $x_0$ is $y = e^{x_0} + e^{x_0}(x - x_0)$, because $f'(x_0) = e^{x_0}$. This line passes through the origin if $0 = e^{x_0} - x_0\,e^{x_0} = e^{x_0}(1 - x_0)$. Since $e^{x_0} > 0$, this gives $x_0 = 1$. Therefore the required tangent is $y = e + e\,(x - 1) = e\,x$. The line $y = x + 1$ is the tangent at the point with $x$-coordinate $0$, which does not pass through the origin.
