What is the derivative in the interior of the domain of $\sqrt{2 x + 3}$?

- $2 \sqrt{2 x + 3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 \sqrt{2 x + 3}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2 x + 3}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{2 x + 3}}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the square root of $u$ is $\dfrac1{2\sqrt u}$. By the chain rule, this is multiplied by $u\prime=2$.
