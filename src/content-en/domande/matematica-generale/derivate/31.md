A firm sells its product at the fixed price of $40$ euros and has cost $C(q) = 1200 + 10q + 0.05\,q^2$. For which quantity is the marginal profit zero?

- $q = 400$
- $q = 600$
- $q = 300$
- $q = 150$

## Explanation

The revenue is $R(q) = 40q$, so the marginal revenue is $R'(q) = 40$. The marginal cost is $C'(q) = 10 + 0.1\,q$, hence the **marginal profit** is
$$
\pi'(q) = R'(q) - C'(q) = 30 - 0.1\,q .
$$
The marginal profit is zero when $0.1\,q = 30$, that is, for $q = 300$.
