What is the third derivative of $f(x) = x^4 - 2x^3 + 5x$?

- $f'''(x) = 12x^2 - 12x$
- $f'''(x) = 24x - 6$
- $f'''(x) = 24$
- $f'''(x) = 24x - 12$

## Explanation

Differentiating once gives $f'(x) = 4x^3 - 6x^2 + 5$, and differentiating again gives the second derivative $f''(x) = 12x^2 - 12x$. The **third derivative** is the derivative of $f''$, so $f'''(x) = 24x - 12$. The term $5x$ has zero second derivative, hence it appears neither in $f''$ nor in $f'''$.
