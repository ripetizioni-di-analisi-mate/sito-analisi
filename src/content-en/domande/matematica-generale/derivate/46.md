What is $\displaystyle\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{(2 + h)^5 - 32}{h}$?

- $32$
- $80$
- $160$
- $0$

## Explanation

Since $2^5 = 32$, the limit is the limit of the difference quotient of $f(x) = x^5$ between $2$ and $2 + h$. Therefore the limit is the **derivative** $f'(2)$. By the power rule $f'(x) = 5x^4$, so $f'(2) = 5 \cdot 16 = 80$. The value $32$ is $f(2)$, and the value $160$ comes from using $5 \cdot 2^5$ instead of $5 \cdot 2^4$.
