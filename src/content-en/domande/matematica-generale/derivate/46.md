What is the derivative of $\ln{\left(2 x^{2} + 3 \right)}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2 x^{2} + 3}$
- $\dfrac{4 x}{2 x^{2} + 3}$
- $4 x$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{2 x^{2} + 3}$

## Explanation

Differentiate the argument to get $4x$, then divide by the argument itself. The denominator is positive for every $x$.
