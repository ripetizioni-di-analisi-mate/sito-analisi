For $f(x) = x\,e^{2x}$, what is the second derivative $f''(0)$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

By the chain rule $(e^{2x})' = 2e^{2x}$, so by the product rule $f'(x) = e^{2x} + 2x\,e^{2x} = (1 + 2x)\,e^{2x}$. Differentiating again with the product rule gives
$$
f''(x) = 2e^{2x} + (1 + 2x) \cdot 2e^{2x} = (4 + 4x)\,e^{2x} .
$$
Hence $f''(0) = 4$. The value $1$ is the first derivative $f'(0)$.
