Demand is $q_d(p)=30 - 2 p$. What is the magnitude of point price elasticity at $p=3$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the elasticity is the product of $|q_d\prime(p)|=2$ and $p/q_d(p)$. At $p=3$, the quantity demanded is $24$.
