What is the derivative of $\dfrac{3 x + 5}{x + 7}$?

- $- \dfrac{16}{\left(x + 7\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{26}{\left(x + 7\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{16}{x + 7}$
- $\dfrac{16}{\left(x + 7\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

The quotient rule gives numerator $3(x+7)-(3x+5)=16$. Square the denominator.
