Demand is $q_d(p)=105 - 3 p$. What is the magnitude of point price elasticity at $p=5$?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the elasticity is the product of $|q_d\prime(p)|=3$ and $p/q_d(p)$. At $p=5$, the quantity demanded is $90$.
