A firm produces $q = 40$ units. At this level the marginal revenue is $R'(40) = 35$ euros and the marginal cost is $C'(40) = 42$ euros. Which statement about the profit $\pi(q) = R(q) - C(q)$ is true?

- A small decrease in output decreases the profit
- A small change in output leaves the profit unchanged
- A small increase in output increases the profit
- A small decrease in output increases the profit

## Explanation

The **marginal profit** is $\pi'(40) = R'(40) - C'(40) = 35 - 42 = -7$ euros. Since $\pi'(40) < 0$, the profit is decreasing near $q = 40$. Hence a small increase in output decreases the profit, and a small decrease increases it. By the linear approximation, one unit less increases the profit by about $7$ euros.
