What is the derivative of $f(x) = \ln(3x^2 + 1)$?

- $f'(x) = \dfrac{6x}{3x^2 + 1}$
- $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{3x^2 + 1}$
- $f'(x) = \dfrac{6x}{(3x^2 + 1)^2}$
- $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{6x}$

## Explanation

Since $3x^2 + 1 > 0$ for every $x$, the function is defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$. It is a composition, with outer function $\ln t$, whose derivative is $\dfrac{1}{t}$, and inner function $3x^2 + 1$, whose derivative is $6x$. By the **chain rule** we therefore have
$$
f'(x) = \frac{1}{3x^2 + 1} \cdot 6x = \frac{6x}{3x^2 + 1} .
$$
