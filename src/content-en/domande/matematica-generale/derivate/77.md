What is the derivative of $\left(x - 4\right) e^{- 7 x}$?

- $\left(29 - 7 x\right) e^{- 7 x}$
- $\left(28 - 7 x\right) e^{- 7 x}$
- $e^{- 7 x}$
- $\left(7 x - 27\right) e^{- 7 x}$

## Explanation

Apply the product rule. Differentiating the exponential produces a factor of $-7$, and the common factor $e^{-7x}$ can be taken out.
