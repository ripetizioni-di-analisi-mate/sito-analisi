What is the derivative of $\ln{\left(3 x^{2} + 5 \right)}$?

- $\dfrac{6 x}{3 x^{2} + 5}$
- $6 x$
- $\dfrac{1}{3 x^{2} + 5}$
- $\dfrac{2 x}{3 x^{2} + 5}$

## Explanation

Divide the derivative of the argument, $6x$, by the argument itself. The denominator is positive for every $x$.
