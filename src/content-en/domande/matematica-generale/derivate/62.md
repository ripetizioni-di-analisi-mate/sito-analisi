What is the derivative of $\left(x - 3\right) e^{- 5 x}$?

- $\left(16 - 5 x\right) e^{- 5 x}$
- $e^{- 5 x}$
- $\left(15 - 5 x\right) e^{- 5 x}$
- $\left(5 x - 14\right) e^{- 5 x}$

## Explanation

Apply the product rule. Differentiating the exponential produces a factor of $-5$, and the common factor $e^{-5x}$ can be taken out.
