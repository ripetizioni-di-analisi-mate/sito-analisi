- $-400\,p^{-1{,}8}$

## Explanation

By the power rule, $q'(p) = 500 \cdot (-0{,}8)\,p^{-1{,}8} = -400\,p^{-1{,}8}$. The price elasticity of demand is therefore
$$
\varepsilon(p) = \frac{p}{500\,p^{-0{,}8}} \cdot \left( -400\,p^{-1{,}8} \right) = -\frac{400}{500}\,p^{1 + 0{,}8 - 1{,}8} = -0{,}8 .
$$
The result does not depend on $p$, so the elasticity is constant and equals $-0{,}8$ at every price. Since $|-0{,}8| < 1$, demand is **inelastic** at every price. In general, for a demand $q(p) = k\,p^{a}$ the elasticity equals the exponent $a$.
