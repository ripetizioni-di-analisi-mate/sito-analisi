The production cost, in euros, is $C(q) = 500 + 8q + 0.002\,q^3$. What is the marginal cost $C'(20)$, that is, the derivative of the cost at $q = 20$?

- $10.4$ euros
- $8.8$ euros
- $10.522$ euros
- $33.8$ euros

## Explanation

The **marginal cost** is the derivative of the cost. Since the derivative of $0.002\,q^3$ is $0.006\,q^2$, one has $C'(q) = 8 + 0.006\,q^2$, so $C'(20) = 8 + 0.006 \cdot 400 = 10.4$ euros. This value approximates the actual cost of the twenty-first unit, $C(21) - C(20) = 10.522$ euros, which is a different quantity. The average cost $\bar{C}(20) = \dfrac{676}{20} = 33.8$ euros is the cost per unit of the whole output.
