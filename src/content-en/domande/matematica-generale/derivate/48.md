What is the derivative of $\dfrac{2 x + 3}{x + 5}$?

- $- \dfrac{7}{\left(x + 5\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{7}{\left(x + 5\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{13}{\left(x + 5\right)^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{7}{x + 5}$

## Explanation

The quotient rule gives numerator $2(x+5)-(2x+3)=7$. Square the denominator.
