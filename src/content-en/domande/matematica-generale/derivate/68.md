The production cost, in euros, is $C(q) = 0.02\,q^{3} - 0.6\,q^{2} + 8q + 20$, drawn in the figure with the points $P$, $R$, $S$, $T$ with $q$-coordinates $4$, $10$, $16$, $22$. At which of the four points is the marginal cost smallest?

::grafico{f="0.02*x^3-0.6*x^2+8*x+20" punti="4,43.68,pieno,P;10,60,pieno,R;16,76.32,pieno,S;22,118.56,pieno,T" xmin=-4 xmax=28 ymin=-15 ymax=135 nomi="q,C" titolo="The graph of the cost $C(q)$ with the points $P$, $R$, $S$, $T$ with $q$-coordinates $4$, $10$, $16$, $22$"}

- At $P$
- At $S$
- At $R$
- At $T$

## Explanation

The marginal cost $C'(q)$ is the slope of the tangent to the graph of the cost, and in the figure the curve is least steep at $R$. The computation confirms it. One has $C'(q) = 0.06\,q^{2} - 1.2\,q + 8$, which is a parabola with vertex at $q = \dfrac{1.2}{0.12} = 10$. Hence the marginal cost is smallest at $q = 10$, where $C'(10) = 2$ euros. At the points $P$ and $S$ it equals $4.16$ euros and at the point $T$ it equals $10.64$ euros. The point $P$ has the lowest cost of the four, but the slope at $P$ is larger.
