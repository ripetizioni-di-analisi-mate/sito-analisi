- $y = x$
- $y = x - 1$
- $y = x + 1$
- $y = 1$

## Explanation

The tangent line at the point with x-coordinate $x_0 = 0$ has equation $y = f(0) + f'(0)\,x$. We have $f(0) = e^{0} = 1$. Since the derivative of $e^{x}$ is $e^{x}$, we also have $f'(0) = e^{0} = 1$. Therefore the tangent is $y = 1 + 1 \cdot x$, that is, $y = x + 1$.
