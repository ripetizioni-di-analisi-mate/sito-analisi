What is the derivative in the interior of the domain of $\sqrt{4 x + 7}$?

- $\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{4 x + 7}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2 \sqrt{4 x + 7}}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{4 x + 7}}$
- $4 \sqrt{4 x + 7}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the square root of $u$ is $\dfrac1{2\sqrt u}$. By the chain rule, this is multiplied by $u\prime=4$.
