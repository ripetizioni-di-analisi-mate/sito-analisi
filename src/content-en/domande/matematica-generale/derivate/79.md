The function $f(x) = x^{4} - 4x^{3}$ has two stationary points. Which of them is neither a local maximum point nor a local minimum point?

- $x = 3$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 0$
- Both are local extremum points

## Explanation

One has $f'(x) = 4x^{3} - 12x^{2} = 4x^{2}(x - 3)$, so the stationary points are $0$ and $3$. The factor $4x^{2}$ is never negative, so the sign of $f'$ is that of $x - 3$ for $x \neq 0$. Hence $f'$ is negative to the left and to the right of $0$, and $f$ is decreasing near $0$. Therefore $0$ **is not an extremum point**, while at $3$ the derivative changes from negative to positive and $f$ has a local minimum. The point $x = 4$ is a zero of $f$, but it is not stationary.
