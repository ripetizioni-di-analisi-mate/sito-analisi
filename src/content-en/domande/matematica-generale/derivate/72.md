For the logarithmic demand $q(p) = 100 - 20 \ln p$, with $1 \leq p < e^{5}$, which elasticity is obtained at the price $p = e$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{20}{e}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

One has $q'(p) = -\dfrac{20}{p}$, so $p\,q'(p) = -20$ at every price. At the price $e$ the quantity is $q(e) = 100 - 20 = 80$. Therefore $\varepsilon(e) = \dfrac{-20}{80} = -\dfrac{1}{4}$, and demand is inelastic. The value $-\dfrac{20}{e}$ is the derivative $q'(e)$, and the value $-\dfrac{1}{5}$ comes from dividing by $100$ instead of $q(e)$.
