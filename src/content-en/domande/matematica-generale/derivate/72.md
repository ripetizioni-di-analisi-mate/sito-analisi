The price in the same demand model is recorded first in euros and then in cents. The slope with respect to the numerical price becomes one hundredth of its former value. What happens to point price elasticity?

- It is multiplied by $100$.
- It is divided by $100$.
- Its sign reverses.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The numerical price becomes $100p$, while the derivative with respect to cents is $\dfrac{q'(p)}{100}$. The factors cancel in the product of price and slope. Demand itself is unchanged.
