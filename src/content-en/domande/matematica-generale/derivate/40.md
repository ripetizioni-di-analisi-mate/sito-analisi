What are the intervals of monotonicity of $f(x) = \ln x - x$?

- Decreasing on $(0, 1]$, increasing on $[1, +\infty)$
- Increasing on $(-\infty, 1]$, decreasing on $[1, +\infty)$
- Increasing on $(0, 1]$, decreasing on $[1, +\infty)$
- Increasing on $(0, e]$, decreasing on $[e, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The function is defined for $x > 0$, and its derivative is $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{x} - 1 = \dfrac{1 - x}{x}$. Since $x > 0$, the sign of $f'(x)$ is the sign of $1 - x$, which is positive for $0 < x < 1$ and negative for $x > 1$. Hence, by the sign of the derivative, $f$ is **increasing** on $(0, 1]$ and **decreasing** on $[1, +\infty)$.
