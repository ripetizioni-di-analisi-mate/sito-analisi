A differentiable cost function $C(q)$ satisfies $C(10) = 500$ and has marginal cost $C'(10) = 30$. What is the derivative of the average cost $\bar{C}(q) = \dfrac{C(q)}{q}$ at $q = 10$?

- $2$
- $-20$
- $-0{,}2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

By the quotient rule
$$
\bar{C}'(q) = \frac{C'(q)\,q - C(q)}{q^2} ,
$$
so $\bar{C}'(10) = \dfrac{30 \cdot 10 - 500}{100} = -2$. The result is negative because the marginal cost $30$ is smaller than the average cost $\bar{C}(10) = 50$, and a unit that costs less than the average lowers the average.
