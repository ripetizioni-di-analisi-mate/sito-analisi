The demand for a good is $q(p) = 400 - 8p$. What is the price elasticity of demand at the price $p = 30$?

- $-1.5$
- $1.5$
- $-0.05$
- $-240$

## Explanation

The price elasticity of demand is $\varepsilon(p) = \dfrac{p}{q(p)}\,q'(p)$. At the price $30$ the quantity is $q(30) = 400 - 240 = 160$, and the derivative is $q'(p) = -8$ for every $p$. Hence
$$
\varepsilon(30) = \frac{30}{160} \cdot (-8) = -\frac{240}{160} = -1.5 .
$$
The elasticity is negative because demand decreases as the price increases. Since $|\varepsilon(30)| > 1$, demand is **elastic**, and a price increase lowers revenue.
