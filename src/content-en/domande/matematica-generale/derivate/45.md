Which approximate value of $\sqrt[3]{8{,}12}$ does the linear approximation of $f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x}$ at $x_0 = 8$ give?

- $2{,}04$
- $2{,}01$
- $2{,}12$
- $2{,}005$

## Explanation

By the power rule $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{3}\,x^{-\frac{2}{3}}$, so $f'(8) = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{12}$. By the **linear approximation** with $h = 0{,}12$ one has
$$
\sqrt[3]{8{,}12} \approx f(8) + f'(8) \cdot 0{,}12 = 2 + \frac{0{,}12}{12} = 2{,}01 .
$$
The value $2{,}04$ is obtained without the factor $8^{-\frac{2}{3}} = \dfrac{1}{4}$ in the derivative.
