Consider the product of three factors $f(x) = x\,e^{x} \ln x$, for $x > 0$. What is the value of $f'(1)$?

- $2e$
- $e$
- $0$
- $1$

## Explanation

We apply the **product rule** to the factors $x\,e^{x}$ and $\ln x$. The derivative of $x\,e^{x}$ is $(1 + x)\,e^{x}$, so
$$
f'(x) = (1 + x)\,e^{x} \ln x + x\,e^{x} \cdot \frac{1}{x} = (1 + x)\,e^{x} \ln x + e^{x} .
$$
Since $\ln 1 = 0$, the first term vanishes at $1$, hence $f'(1) = e$. The value $0$ is $f(1)$.
