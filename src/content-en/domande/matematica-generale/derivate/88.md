Demand is $q_d(p)=252 - 4 p$. What is the magnitude of point price elasticity at $p=7$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{8}$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the elasticity is the product of $|q_d\prime(p)|=4$ and $p/q_d(p)$. At $p=7$, the quantity demanded is $224$.
