The area $A(r)=\pi r^2$ is approximated by its tangent line at $r=10$ cm. Compare the absolute errors for increases in radius of $0.2$ cm and $0.1$ cm. What is the ratio of the first error to the second?

- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

In the expansion $\pi(10+h)^2=100\pi+20\pi h+\pi h^2$, the tangent line retains the first two terms. The error is therefore $\pi h^2$. Doubling $h$ multiplies the error by four.
