A differentiable cost $C(q)$ has $C(200) = 9000$ euros and marginal cost $C'(200) = 35$ euros. Which estimate of $C(204)$ does the linear approximation give?

- $9035$ euros
- $9140$ euros
- $8860$ euros
- $16{,}140$ euros

## Explanation

By the **linear approximation** $C(q + \Delta q) \approx C(q) + C'(q)\,\Delta q$. With $q = 200$ and $\Delta q = 4$ one gets $C(204) \approx 9000 + 35 \cdot 4 = 9140$ euros. The value $9035$ euros is the estimate of the cost of $201$ units, and $16{,}140$ euros is obtained by multiplying the marginal cost by $204$ instead of by the increment $\Delta q = 4$.
