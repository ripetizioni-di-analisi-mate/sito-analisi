The function $f$ equals $x^2$ for $x \leq 1$ and $ax + b$ for $x > 1$. For which values of $a$ and $b$ is the function differentiable at $x_0 = 1$?

- $a = 1$, $b = 0$
- $a = 2$, $b = -1$
- $a = 2$, $b = 1$
- $a = -2$, $b = 3$

## Explanation

A function differentiable at $1$ is continuous at $1$, so the values of the two pieces at $1$ coincide, that is, $a + b = 1$. Moreover, the slopes from the left and from the right coincide. The slope from the left is the derivative of $x^2$ at $1$, which is $2$, and the slope from the right is $a$. Therefore $f$ is differentiable at $1$ if
$$
\begin{cases} a + b = 1 \\ a = 2 \end{cases}
$$
that is, for $a = 2$ and $b = -1$. With $a = 1$, $b = 0$ and with $a = -2$, $b = 3$ the function is continuous but has a corner at $1$.
