For $a \neq 1$ and $a \neq -1$, what is the solution $(x, y)$ of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a x + y = 1 \\[4pt]
x + a y = 1
\end{cases}
$$

- $\left( \dfrac{1}{a - 1}, \dfrac{1}{a - 1} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{a^{2} - 1}, \dfrac{1}{a^{2} - 1} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{a + 1}, \dfrac{a}{a + 1} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{a + 1}, \dfrac{1}{a + 1} \right)$

## Explanation

The determinant of the coefficient matrix is $\begin{vmatrix} a & 1 \\ 1 & a \end{vmatrix} = a^{2} - 1$, which is nonzero for $a \neq \pm 1$. Hence **Cramer's rule** applies and
$$
x = \frac{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & a \end{vmatrix}}{a^{2} - 1} = \frac{a - 1}{(a - 1)(a + 1)} = \frac{1}{a + 1},
\qquad
y = \frac{\begin{vmatrix} a & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix}}{a^{2} - 1} = \frac{a - 1}{(a - 1)(a + 1)} = \frac{1}{a + 1} ,
$$
where the factor $a - 1 \neq 0$ has been cancelled. The two unknowns are equal, as can also be seen by subtracting the two equations, which gives $(a - 1)(x - y) = 0$.
