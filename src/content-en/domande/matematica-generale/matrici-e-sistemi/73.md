Cramer’s rule is used on $3x+y=26$ and $x+5y=32$. What is the determinant in the numerator of the formula for $x$?

- $-98$
- $14$
- $98$
- $70$

## Explanation

Replace the first column with the right-hand-side column and keep the second column. Compute the determinant of the resulting matrix.
