What is the value of the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 3 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & -1 \\ 4 & 5 & 2 \end{vmatrix} ?
$$

- $-25$
- $-5$
- $5$
- $25$

## Explanation

The second column has only one nonzero entry, $a_{32} = 5$, so the **Laplace expansion** along the second column is convenient. Since $3 + 2 = 5$ is odd, the sign of position $(3, 2)$ is $-1$. Deleting the third row and the second column gives the complementary minor
$$
M_{32} = \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = -3 - 2 = -5 .
$$
Therefore the determinant equals $5 \cdot (-1) \cdot (-5) = 25$.
