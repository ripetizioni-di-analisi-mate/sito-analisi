In the Leontief model $X=AX+d$, $A$ = $\begin{pmatrix}\frac{1}{4} & 0\\\frac{1}{7} & 0\end{pmatrix}$ and $d$ = $\begin{pmatrix}300\\300\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first sector’s output?

- $700$
- $300$
- $1200$
- $400$

## Explanation

The first row gives $x_1=\dfrac{x_1}{4}+300$. Move the term involving $x_1$ to the left to obtain the output.
