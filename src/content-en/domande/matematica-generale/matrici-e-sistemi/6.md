For the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix},
$$
which number is the entry in position $(1, 2)$ of the inverse $A^{-1}$?

- $-1$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Expanding along the first row gives $\det A = 1 \cdot (2 - 1) - 0 + 2 \cdot (0 - 1) = -1$, so $A$ is invertible. The entry in position $(i, j)$ of the inverse is $\dfrac{A_{ji}}{\det A}$, so for position $(1, 2)$ one needs the cofactor $A_{21}$, with the indices swapped. Deleting the second row and the first column gives the complementary minor
$$
M_{21} = \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = -2 .
$$
Since $2 + 1$ is odd, the cofactor is $A_{21} = -M_{21} = 2$. Hence the required entry is $\dfrac{2}{-1} = -2$.
