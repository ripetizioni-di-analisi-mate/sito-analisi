What is the area of the parallelogram whose sides are the vectors $u = (3, 1)$ and $v = (1, 2)$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,3,1,u;0,0,1,2,v" segmenti="3,1,4,3;1,2,4,3" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="The parallelogram with sides $u = (3, 1)$ and $v = (1, 2)$"}

- $7$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The area of the parallelogram whose sides are the row vectors $(a, b)$ and $(c, d)$ is the absolute value of the determinant of the matrix with those vectors as rows. With $u = (3, 1)$ and $v = (1, 2)$,
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 3 \cdot 2 - 1 \cdot 1 = 5 ,
$$
so the area is $5$. The number $\dfrac{5}{2}$ is the area of the triangle with sides $u$ and $v$, that is, half of the parallelogram.
