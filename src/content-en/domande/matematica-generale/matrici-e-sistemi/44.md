The demand and supply functions of two complementary goods are
$$
q_{d1} = 20 - 2p_1 - p_2, \quad q_{s1} = p_1 + 1,
\qquad
q_{d2} = 30 - p_1 - 3p_2, \quad q_{s2} = 2p_2 + 5 .
$$
What is the equilibrium quantity of the second good?

- $4$
- $6$
- $8$
- $13$

## Explanation

At equilibrium, in both markets the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied. Setting $20 - 2p_1 - p_2 = p_1 + 1$ and $30 - p_1 - 3p_2 = 2p_2 + 5$ gives the system
$$
\begin{cases}
3p_1 + p_2 = 19 \\[4pt]
p_1 + 5p_2 = 25
\end{cases}
$$
with determinant $15 - 1 = 14$. By **Cramer's rule** $p_1 = \dfrac{19 \cdot 5 - 1 \cdot 25}{14} = \dfrac{70}{14} = 5$ and $p_2 = \dfrac{3 \cdot 25 - 1 \cdot 19}{14} = \dfrac{56}{14} = 4$. Therefore the equilibrium quantity of the second good is $q_2 = 2 \cdot 4 + 5 = 13$; as a check, $30 - 5 - 12 = 13$.
