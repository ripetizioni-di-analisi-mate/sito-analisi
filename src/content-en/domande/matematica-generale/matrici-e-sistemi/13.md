For an economy with two sectors, the technical coefficient matrix and final demand vector are
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0{,}3 & 0{,}2 \\ 0{,}1 & 0{,}4 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
d = \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 40 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What output levels $x_1$ and $x_2$ meet final demand in the Leontief model?

- $x_1 = 20$, $x_2 = 30$
- $x_1 = 50$, $x_2 = 75$
- $x_1 = 10$, $x_2 = 65$
- $x_1 = 40$, $x_2 = 80$

## Explanation

The production levels satisfy $(I_2 - A)\, x = d$, with
$$
I_2 - A = \begin{pmatrix} 0{,}7 & -0{,}2 \\ -0{,}1 & 0{,}6 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det(I_2 - A) = 0{,}42 - 0{,}02 = 0{,}4 .
$$
Since the determinant is nonzero, $I_2 - A$ is invertible. The inverse formula for matrices of order $2$ swaps the main diagonal entries and changes the signs of the other two, so $-0{,}2$ and $-0{,}1$ become $0{,}2$ and $0{,}1$. Therefore
$$
x = \frac{1}{0{,}4} \begin{pmatrix} 0{,}6 & 0{,}2 \\ 0{,}1 & 0{,}7 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 40 \end{pmatrix}
= \frac{1}{0{,}4} \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 30 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 50 \\ 75 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
and the first sector produces $50$ units, the second $75$.
