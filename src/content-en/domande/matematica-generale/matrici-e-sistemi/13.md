An economy with two sectors has technology matrix and final demand
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0.3 & 0.2 \\ 0.1 & 0.4 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
d = \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 40 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Which production levels $x_1$ and $x_2$ of the two sectors meet the final demand in the Leontief model?

- $x_1 = 20$, $x_2 = 30$
- $x_1 = 50$, $x_2 = 75$
- $x_1 = 10$, $x_2 = 65$
- $x_1 = 40$, $x_2 = 80$

## Explanation

The production levels satisfy $(I_2 - A)\, x = d$, with
$$
I_2 - A = \begin{pmatrix} 0.7 & -0.2 \\ -0.1 & 0.6 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det(I_2 - A) = 0.42 - 0.02 = 0.4 .
$$
Since the determinant is different from zero, $I_2 - A$ is invertible. In the inverse of order $2$ the entries of the main diagonal are swapped and the other two change sign, so $-0.2$ and $-0.1$ become $0.2$ and $0.1$. Therefore
$$
x = \frac{1}{0.4} \begin{pmatrix} 0.6 & 0.2 \\ 0.1 & 0.7 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 40 \end{pmatrix}
= \frac{1}{0.4} \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 30 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 50 \\ 75 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
and the first sector produces $50$ units, the second $75$.
