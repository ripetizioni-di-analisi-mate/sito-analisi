The following matrix is in row echelon form and has a zero first column. What is its rank?
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$

- $3$
- $1$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The pivot of the first row is $2$, in the second column, and the pivot of the second row is $5$, in the fourth column, strictly to the right of the previous one. The third row is zero. The rank of a matrix in row echelon form is the number of nonzero rows, so $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$. The minor formed by the first two rows and the pivot columns confirms it:
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 5 \end{vmatrix} = 10 \neq 0 .
$$
