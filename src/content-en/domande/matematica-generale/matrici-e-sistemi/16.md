Which matrix is the square $A^2 = AA$ of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $\begin{pmatrix} 7 & 0 \\ 0 & 7 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 9 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 7 & 0 \\ 0 & -7 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 1 \\ 1 & 10 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The power $A^2$ is the row-by-column product of $A$ with itself. Multiplying each row of $A$ by each column of $A$ gives
$$
A^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 + 6 & 2 - 2 \\ 3 - 3 & 6 + 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 & 0 \\ 0 & 7 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
In the entry in position $(2, 2)$ the product $(-1) \cdot (-1)$ equals $+1$, so the entry is $6 + 1 = 7$. Therefore $A^2 = 7 I_2$.
