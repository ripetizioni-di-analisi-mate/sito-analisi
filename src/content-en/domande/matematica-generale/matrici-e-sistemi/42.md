What are the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y - z = 1 \\[4pt]
2x + 5y + z = 4
\end{cases}
$$
when $z = t$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $(-3 + 7t,\ 2 - 3t,\ t)$
- $(-3 - 5t,\ 2 - 3t,\ t)$
- $(-3 + 5t,\ 2 - 3t,\ t)$
- $(-3 - 5t,\ 2 + 3t,\ t)$

## Explanation

Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $y + 3z = 2$. Hence the coefficient matrix and the augmented matrix have rank $2$, and there are $3$ unknowns, therefore by the Rouché–Capelli theorem there are $\infty^{1}$ solutions. Setting $z = t$, the equation $y + 3t = 2$ gives $y = 2 - 3t$. The first equation gives
$$
x = 1 - 2y + z = 1 - 2\,(2 - 3t) + t = 1 - 4 + 6t + t = -3 + 7t .
$$
Therefore the solutions are $(-3 + 7t,\ 2 - 3t,\ t)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
