A saver splits $10{,}000$ euros between two funds. The first yields $3\%$ per year, the second $5\%$ per year, and after one year the total return is $420$ euros. How many euros were invested in the second fund?

- $6000$
- $4000$
- $8400$
- $14{,}000$

## Explanation

Let $x$ and $y$ be the euros invested in the first and in the second fund. The amount invested and the return give the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y = 10{,}000 \\[4pt]
0.03\,x + 0.05\,y = 420
\end{cases}
$$
From the first equation, $x = 10{,}000 - y$. Substituting into the second gives $300 - 0.03\,y + 0.05\,y = 420$, that is, $0.02\,y = 120$. Hence $y = 6000$ euros and $x = 4000$ euros. As a check, $0.03 \cdot 4000 + 0.05 \cdot 6000 = 120 + 300 = 420$.
