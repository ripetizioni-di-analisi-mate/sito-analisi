Which system is written in matrix form $A x = b$ with
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -2 \\ 3 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
x = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
b = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $\begin{cases} x - 2y = 4 \\ 3x + y + z = -1 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x - 2z = -1 \\ 3x + y + z = 4 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x - 2z = 4 \\ 3x + z = -1 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x - 2z = 4 \\ 3x + y + z = -1 \end{cases}$

## Explanation

Row $i$ of $A$ times the column $x$ gives the left side of equation $i$, and the right-hand side is entry $i$ of $b$. The first row $(1, 0, -2)$ gives $1 \cdot x + 0 \cdot y - 2 \cdot z = x - 2z$, with right-hand side $4$. The second row $(3, 1, 1)$ gives $3x + y + z$, with right-hand side $-1$. Hence the system consists of $x - 2z = 4$ and $3x + y + z = -1$. The coefficient $0$ in the first row lies in the column of $y$, so the unknown $y$ does not appear in the first equation.
