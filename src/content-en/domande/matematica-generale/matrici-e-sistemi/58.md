For the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 3 & -1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix},
$$
with $A_{ij}$ denoting the cofactors, what is the value of the sum $a_{11} A_{21} + a_{12} A_{22} + a_{13} A_{23}$?

- $0$
- $-22$
- $22$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The sum multiplies the entries of the first row by the cofactors of the second row. By the **Laplace expansion**, the sum of the entries of one row times the cofactors of a different row is zero, because it is the expansion along the second row of the matrix that has the first row repeated in place of the second, and that matrix has two equal rows. Hence the sum is $0$. A direct computation confirms it: $A_{21} = -(8 - 0) = -8$, $A_{22} = 4 - 0 = 4$, $A_{23} = -(1 - 4) = 3$, and
$$
1 \cdot (-8) + 2 \cdot 4 + 0 \cdot 3 = 0 .
$$
The number $-22$ is instead $\det A$, that is, the sum of the entries of the second row times their own cofactors.
