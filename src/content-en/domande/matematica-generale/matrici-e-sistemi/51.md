What is the entry in row one, column two of the inverse of $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3\\0 & 5\end{pmatrix}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{10}$
- $0$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The determinant is $10$. The inverse formula for a $2\times2$ matrix negates the off-diagonal entry and divides by the determinant.
