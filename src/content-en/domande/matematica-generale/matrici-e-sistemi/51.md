Which matrix is the product $B B^{T}$, where
$$
B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ -1 & 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 1 \\ 1 & 11 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 & 1 \\ -3 & 9 & 3 \\ 1 & 3 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 5 & -1 \\ 1 & 11 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 5 & 3 \\ 3 & 11 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The matrix $B$ is $2 \times 3$ and the transpose $B^{T}$ is $3 \times 2$, so $B B^{T}$ is a $2 \times 2$ matrix. The entry in position $(i, j)$ is the product of row $i$ of $B$ and column $j$ of $B^{T}$, which is row $j$ of $B$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(B B^{T})_{11} &= 1 + 0 + 4 = 5, \\[4pt]
(B B^{T})_{12} &= -1 + 0 + 2 = 1, \\[4pt]
(B B^{T})_{22} &= 1 + 9 + 1 = 11 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $(B B^{T})^{T} = B B^{T}$, the matrix is symmetric, so $(B B^{T})_{21} = 1$ and $B B^{T} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 1 \\ 1 & 11 \end{pmatrix}$. The $3 \times 3$ matrix is instead $B^{T} B$.
