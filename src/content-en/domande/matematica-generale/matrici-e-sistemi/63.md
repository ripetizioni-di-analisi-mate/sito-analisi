The matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ satisfies the equality $A^{2} - 3A + I_2 = O$. Which expression is the inverse $A^{-1}$?

- $3 I_2 - A$
- $A - 3 I_2$
- $3A - I_2$
- $I_2 - 3A$

## Explanation

From the equality $A^{2} - 3A + I_2 = O$ we get $3A - A^{2} = I_2$. Factoring $A$ out on the left gives
$$
A\,(3 I_2 - A) = I_2 ,
$$
and in the same way, factoring $A$ out on the right, $(3 I_2 - A)\,A = I_2$. Hence $3 I_2 - A$ satisfies the definition of inverse and
$$
A^{-1} = 3 I_2 - A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The formula for the inverse of order $2$ gives the same result, because $\det A = 1$.
