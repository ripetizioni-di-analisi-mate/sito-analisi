Which expression is the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} a & b & c \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} ?
$$

- $a - c$
- $c - a$
- $a + c$
- $a - b + c$

## Explanation

The second row has a single nonzero entry, $a_{22} = 1$, whose sign is $(-1)^{2 + 2} = +1$. The **Laplace expansion** along the second row therefore gives
$$
\det A = 1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} a & c \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = a \cdot 1 - c \cdot 1 = a - c ,
$$
where the minor is obtained by deleting the second row and the second column. The entry $b$ lies in the deleted column, so it does not appear in the result.
