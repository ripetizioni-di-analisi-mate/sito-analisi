Two square matrices $A$ and $B$ have identical corresponding rows except for their second rows. Their determinants are $6$ and $-2$. A third matrix keeps the common rows and uses the average of the two second rows as its second row. What is its determinant?

- $2$
- $4$
- $-12$
- $-4$

## Explanation

With every other row fixed, the determinant is linear in the second row. Averaging the rows therefore gives the average determinant, $\dfrac{6+(-2)}2=2$.
