The production cost of a firm has the form $C(q) = a q^{2} + b q + c$. It is known that $C(0) = 10$, $C(1) = 13$ and $C(2) = 20$, in thousands of euros. What is the value of $C(3)$?

- $27$
- $19$
- $37$
- $31$

## Explanation

The three conditions give a linear system in the unknowns $a$, $b$, $c$:
$$
\begin{cases}
c = 10 \\[4pt]
a + b + c = 13 \\[4pt]
4a + 2b + c = 20
\end{cases}
$$
Substituting $c = 10$ gives $a + b = 3$ and $4a + 2b = 10$, that is, $2a + b = 5$. Subtracting the first of these from the second gives $a = 2$, hence $b = 1$. Therefore $C(q) = 2q^{2} + q + 10$ and
$$
C(3) = 2 \cdot 9 + 3 + 10 = 31 ,
$$
that is, $31$ thousand euros.
