Let $A$ and $B$ be square matrices of order $2$ with $\det A = 4$ and $\det B = -3$. What is $\det\left( A^{-1} B^{2} \right)$?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $36$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $\det A \neq 0$, the matrix $A$ is invertible and $\det A^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{\det A} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. By **Binet's theorem** the determinant of a product is the product of the determinants, so $\det B^{2} = (\det B)^{2} = 9$. Therefore
$$
\det\left( A^{-1} B^{2} \right) = \frac{1}{4} \cdot 9 = \frac{9}{4} .
$$
The square of $-3$ is positive, so the result is positive.
