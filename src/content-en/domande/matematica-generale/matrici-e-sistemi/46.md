Let $A$ = $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 3\\1 & 5\end{pmatrix}$ and $B$ = $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1\\5 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. What is the entry in row one, column two of $AB$?

- $19$
- $13$
- $8$
- $11$

## Explanation

Multiply the first row of $A$ by the second column of $B$. Their dot product is $2\cdot1+3\cdot2$.
