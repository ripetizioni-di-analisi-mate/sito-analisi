An economy with two sectors has technical coefficient matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0.2 & 0.3 \\ 0.1 & 0.4 \end{pmatrix}
$$
and outputs $x_1 = 50$ and $x_2 = 40$. How many units of the product of the first sector are absorbed by intermediate uses?

- $22$
- $28$
- $26$
- $15$

## Explanation

The coefficient $a_{1j}$ is the amount of product of the first sector needed for one unit of sector $j$. Hence sector $1$ uses $a_{11} x_1$ units of its own product and sector $2$ uses $a_{12} x_2$ of them. The intermediate uses of the product of the first sector are therefore
$$
a_{11} x_1 + a_{12} x_2 = 0.2 \cdot 50 + 0.3 \cdot 40 = 10 + 12 = 22 ,
$$
that is, the first entry of the vector $A x$. The remaining $50 - 22 = 28$ units are the final demand of the first sector.
