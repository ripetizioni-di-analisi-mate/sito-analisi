Let $A$ = $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 5\\1 & 7\end{pmatrix}$ and $B$ = $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1\\7 & 3\end{pmatrix}$. What is the entry in row one, column two of $AB$?

- $41$
- $18$
- $17$
- $25$

## Explanation

Multiply the first row of $A$ by the second column of $B$. Their dot product is $3\cdot1+5\cdot3$.
