The coefficient matrix of the homogeneous system $A x = 0$ in the unknowns $x_1$, $x_2$, $x_3$, $x_4$ is reduced to row echelon form, where
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & -2 & 5 & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
In which columns are the pivots of the echelon matrix, and on how many parameters do the solutions of the system depend?

- Columns $1$ and $2$; the solutions depend on $2$ parameters
- Columns $1$, $3$ and $4$; the solutions depend on $1$ parameter
- Columns $1$ and $3$; the solutions depend on $2$ parameters
- Columns $1$ and $3$; the solutions depend on $1$ parameter

## Explanation

Subtract twice the first row from the second row and add the first row to the third:
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Subtracting then the second row from the third gives the row echelon matrix
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
In the second row the first nonzero entry is in the third column, so the pivots are in columns $1$ and $3$. There are two nonzero rows, hence $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$. The system is homogeneous and has $4$ unknowns, so by the **Rouché–Capelli theorem** there are $\infty^{4 - 2} = \infty^{2}$ solutions: they depend on $2$ parameters, namely the unknowns $x_2$ and $x_4$ of the columns without a pivot.
