Let $A$ be a square matrix of order $3$ with $\det A = -2$. What is $\det\left( 3A^{T} \right)$?

- $-6$
- $54$
- $-18$
- $-54$

## Explanation

The determinant of the transpose equals that of the matrix, so $\det A^{T} = -2$. Multiplying the matrix by $3$ multiplies each of its $3$ rows by $3$, and each row multiplied by $3$ multiplies the determinant by $3$. Therefore
$$
\det\left( 3A^{T} \right) = 3^{3} \det A^{T} = 27 \cdot (-2) = -54 .
$$
