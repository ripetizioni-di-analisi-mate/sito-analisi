For which values of the real parameter $k$ does the solution of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + k y = 3 \\[4pt]
k x - y = 1
\end{cases}
$$
have $x = 1$?

- Only for $k = 2$
- Only for $k = -1$
- For $k = 1$ and for $k = -2$
- For $k = 2$ and for $k = -1$

## Explanation

The determinant of the coefficient matrix is $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & k \\ k & -1 \end{vmatrix} = -1 - k^{2}$, which is negative for every $k$. Hence the system has a unique solution for every value of $k$. Substitute $x = 1$. The second equation gives $y = k - 1$, and the first becomes
$$
1 + k\,(k - 1) = 3
\iff
k^{2} - k - 2 = 0
\iff
(k - 2)(k + 1) = 0 .
$$
Therefore $x = 1$ for $k = 2$, with solution $(1, 1)$, and for $k = -1$, with solution $(1, -2)$.
