For the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 1 & 0 & 6 \end{pmatrix},
$$
what is the cofactor $A_{23}$ of the entry in position $(2, 3)$?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-5$
- $5$

## Explanation

The complementary minor $M_{23}$ is obtained by deleting the second row and the third column, so
$$
M_{23} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - 2 = -2 .
$$
The cofactor is $A_{23} = (-1)^{2 + 3} M_{23}$. Since $2 + 3$ is odd, the sign is $-1$, therefore $A_{23} = -(-2) = 2$.
