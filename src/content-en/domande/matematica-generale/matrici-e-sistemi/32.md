The matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -2 & 2 \\ 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}
\quad \text{has inverse} \quad
A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the solution $(x_1, x_2, x_3)$ of the system $A x = b$ with $b = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$?

- $(7, -1, 3)$
- $(1, 2, 0)$
- $(4, 5, 0)$
- $(-7, 1, -3)$

## Explanation

Since $A$ is invertible, both sides of $A x = b$ are multiplied on the left by $A^{-1}$. This gives $A^{-1} A x = A^{-1} b$, that is, $x = A^{-1} b$. The row-by-column product gives
$$
x = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 1 + 6 \\ 2 - 3 \\ 1 + 2 \end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ -1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The vector $(1, 2, 0)$ is the product $A b$, which is obtained by using $A$ instead of its inverse.
