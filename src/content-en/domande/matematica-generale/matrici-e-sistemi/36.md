For which value of the real parameter $a$ does the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 3 & a \\ 1 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
have rank $2$?

- $a = -2$
- $a = 1$
- $a = 2$
- For no value of $a$

## Explanation

With the reduction to echelon form one subtracts twice the first row from the second and the first row from the third, and obtains the rows $(0, -1, -1, a - 2)$ and $(0, -1, -1, 0)$. Subtracting the new second row from the third gives
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & -1 & a - 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 2 - a \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The rank is the number of nonzero rows of the echelon matrix. For $a \neq 2$ the third row is nonzero and the rank is $3$; for $a = 2$ the third row is zero, so the rank is $2$.
