Which of the following matrices has zero determinant?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 & 10 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 3 & 3 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

In the second matrix the third row $(3, 3, 3)$ is the sum of the first row $(1, 2, 3)$ and the second $(2, 1, 0)$. Subtracting from the third row first the first row and then the second does not change the determinant, and it gives a matrix with a zero row, so the determinant of the second matrix is $0$. For the other three matrices Sarrus's rule gives the values $-3$, $-9$ and $1$. In particular, in the fourth matrix the first column $(2, 1, 0)$ and the second $(4, 2, 1)$ are not proportional, because of the third entry.
