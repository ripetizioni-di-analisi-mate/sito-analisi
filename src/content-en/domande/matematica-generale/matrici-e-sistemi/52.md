Let $A$ and $B$ be symmetric matrices of order $2$. Which of the following matrices is symmetric for every choice of $A$ and $B$?

- $AB$
- $AB - BA$
- $A B^{2}$
- $A + 2B$

## Explanation

By the properties of the transpose, $(A + 2B)^{T} = A^{T} + 2B^{T} = A + 2B$, so $A + 2B$ is always symmetric. For the product, instead, $(AB)^{T} = B^{T} A^{T} = BA$, so $AB$ is symmetric only when $AB = BA$. With
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
one gets
$$
AB = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A B^{2} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
AB - BA = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
and none of the three is symmetric. In general $(AB - BA)^{T} = BA - AB$ is the negative of $AB - BA$.
