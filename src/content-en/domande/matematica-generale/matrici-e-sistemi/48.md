Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix} k & 2 \\ -1 & -1 \end{pmatrix},
$$
for which value of the real parameter $k$ is the product $AB$ the zero matrix?

- $k = 2$
- $k = -2$
- $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- For no value of $k$, because $A$ and $B$ are not zero

## Explanation

Matrix multiplication gives
$$
AB = \begin{pmatrix} k - 2 & 2 - 2 \\ 2k - 4 & 4 - 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} k - 2 & 0 \\ 2k - 4 & 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The second column is zero for every $k$, and the first column is zero if and only if $k - 2 = 0$. Hence $AB = O$ for $k = 2$. For $k = 2$ neither matrix is zero, so the product of two nonzero matrices can be the zero matrix.
