Let $A$ = $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 5\\1 & 7\end{pmatrix}$ and $B$ = $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1\\7 & 3\end{pmatrix}$. What is the entry in row two, column one of $A^T-B$?

- $-2$
- $-6$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

Transposition gives the requested entry of $A^T$ from the first row and second column of $A$. Then subtract $B_{21}=7$.
