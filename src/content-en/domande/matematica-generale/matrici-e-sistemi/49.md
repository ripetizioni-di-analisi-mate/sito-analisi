Let
$$
u = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
w = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What is the value of the product $u\,A\,w$?

- $5$
- $-4$
- $4$
- $12$

## Explanation

The vector $u$ is $1 \times 3$, the matrix $A$ is $3 \times 3$ and the vector $w$ is $3 \times 1$, so $u\,A\,w$ is a $1 \times 1$ matrix, that is, a number. By the associative property, $A w$ can be computed first:
$$
A w = \begin{pmatrix} 4 + 1 + 0 \\ 2 + 3 + 0 \\ 0 + 2 + 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 5 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Then
$$
u\,(A w) = 1 \cdot 5 + (-1) \cdot 5 + 2 \cdot 2 = 4 .
$$
