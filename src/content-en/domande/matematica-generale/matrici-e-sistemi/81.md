For a parameter $t$ the system is $x+y=t$ and $x-y=2-t$. Which coordinate of the solution can be reported before $t$ is known?

- $y=1$
- $x=1$
- $x=0$
- $y=0$

## Explanation

Adding the equations cancels both $y$ and $t$, leaving $2x=2$. Substituting $x=1$ gives $y=t-1$. Thus only the first coordinate is independent of the parameter.
