What is the entry in row one, column two of the inverse of $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 7\\0 & 9\end{pmatrix}$?

- $- \dfrac{7}{36}$
- $\dfrac{7}{36}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{4}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The determinant is $36$. The inverse formula for a $2\times2$ matrix negates the off-diagonal entry and divides by the determinant.
