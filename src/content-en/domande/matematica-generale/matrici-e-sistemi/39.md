Which statement is true for the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y = 3 \\[4pt]
2x + 4y = 6
\end{cases}
$$

- The system is inconsistent, because the two lines are parallel
- The system has the unique solution $(1, 1)$
- The system has infinitely many solutions, denoted by $\infty^{1}$
- The system has infinitely many solutions, denoted by $\infty^{2}$

## Explanation

The second equation is the first multiplied by $2$, including the constant term, so the equations represent the same line. The coefficient matrix and augmented matrix both have rank $1$, and there are $2$ unknowns. By the **Rouché–Capelli theorem**, the system has **infinitely many solutions**, denoted by $\infty^{2 - 1} = \infty^{1}$, given by $(3 - 2t,\ t)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$. The pair $(1, 1)$ is one of them, obtained at $t = 1$. The lines would be parallel and distinct if the constant terms did not have the same ratio as the coefficients.
