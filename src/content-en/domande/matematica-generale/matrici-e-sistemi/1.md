Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix},
$$
which matrix is the product $AB$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 8 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 8 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 8 \\ 3 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 8 & 10 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The matrix $A$ is of type $2 \times 3$ and $B$ is of type $3 \times 2$, so the product $AB$ is defined and is a $2 \times 2$ matrix. The entry in position $(i, j)$ is obtained by multiplying row $i$ of $A$ by column $j$ of $B$, entry by entry, and adding the products. Therefore
$$
AB = \begin{pmatrix} 2 + 0 + 0 & 1 + 2 + 0 \\ 6 + 0 + 2 & 3 - 1 + 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 8 & 8 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
In the entry in position $(1, 2)$ the product $(-2) \cdot (-1)$ equals $+2$, and in the entry in position $(2, 2)$ the product $1 \cdot (-1)$ equals $-1$. The product $BA$, instead, is a $3 \times 3$ matrix, so $AB \neq BA$.
