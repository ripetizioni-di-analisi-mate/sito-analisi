What is the solution $(x, y)$ of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
3x - 2y = 4 \\[4pt]
5x + 4y = 3
\end{cases}
$$

- $\left( 1, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$
- $\left( 11, -\dfrac{11}{2} \right)$
- $\left( -\dfrac{1}{2}, 1 \right)$
- $\left( 1, -\dfrac{1}{2} \right)$

## Explanation

The determinant of the coefficient matrix is $\begin{vmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 12 - (-10) = 22$, so it is different from zero and **Cramer's rule** applies. Replacing the first and the second column with the right-hand-side column gives
$$
x = \frac{\begin{vmatrix} 4 & -2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{vmatrix}}{22} = \frac{16 + 6}{22} = 1,
\qquad
y = \frac{\begin{vmatrix} 3 & 4 \\ 5 & 3 \end{vmatrix}}{22} = \frac{9 - 20}{22} = -\frac{1}{2} .
$$
As a check, $3 \cdot 1 - 2 \cdot \left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) = 4$ and $5 \cdot 1 + 4 \cdot \left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) = 3$.
