In Gaussian elimination, two elementary row operations turn the augmented matrix
$$
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 & 4 \\ -1 & 3 & 2 & 1 \end{array} \right)
\quad \text{into} \quad
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 0 & -3 & 3 & -2 \\ 0 & 5 & 1 & 4 \end{array} \right) .
$$
Which operations were performed?

- Twice the first row was added to the second row, and the first row was subtracted from the third
- Twice the first row was subtracted from the second row, and the first row was subtracted from the third
- Twice the first row was subtracted from the second row, and the first row was added to the third
- The second row was subtracted from twice the first, and the first row was added to the third

## Explanation

The first row does not change, so the operations involve the second and third rows. In the second row, the $0$ in place of $2$ is obtained by subtracting twice the first row:
$$
(2, 1, 1 \mid 4) - 2\,(1, 2, -1 \mid 3) = (0, -3, 3 \mid -2) .
$$
In the third row, the $0$ in place of $-1$ is obtained by adding the first row:
$$
(-1, 3, 2 \mid 1) + (1, 2, -1 \mid 3) = (0, 5, 1 \mid 4) .
$$
Subtracting the second row from twice the first would instead give $(0, 3, -3 \mid 2)$, which is the opposite of the row shown.
