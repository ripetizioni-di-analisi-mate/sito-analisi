What is the entry in row one, column two of the inverse of $\begin{pmatrix}3 & 5\\0 & 7\end{pmatrix}$?

- $0$
- $- \dfrac{5}{21}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{21}$

## Explanation

The determinant is $21$. The inverse formula for a $2\times2$ matrix negates the off-diagonal entry and divides by the determinant.
