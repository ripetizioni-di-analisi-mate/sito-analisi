## Explanation

The second row has a single nonzero entry, $a_{22} = 1$, with sign $(-1)^{2 + 2} = +1$. The **Laplace expansion** along the second row therefore gives
$$
\det A = 1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} ,
$$
where the minor is obtained by deleting the second row and the second column. In the minor the third row has $1$ as its only nonzero entry, in position $(3, 3)$ with sign $+1$, so
$$
\det A = 1 \cdot \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 4 - 0 = 4 .
$$
