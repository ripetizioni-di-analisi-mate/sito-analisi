What is the rank of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & k & 2 \end{pmatrix}
$$
as the real parameter $k$ varies?

- The rank is $2$ for every value of $k$
- The rank is $2$ for $k \neq 2$ and $1$ for $k = 2$
- The rank is $1$ for $k \neq 4$ and $2$ for $k = 4$
- The rank is $2$ for $k \neq 4$ and $1$ for $k = 4$

## Explanation

The matrix has two rows, so its rank is at most $2$, and it is at least $1$ because $A$ has nonzero entries. The minor formed by the first and the third column is $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 0$ for every $k$, while the minor formed by the first two columns is
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & k \end{vmatrix} = k - 4 .
$$
For $k \neq 4$ this minor is nonzero, so $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$. For $k = 4$ the second row $(2, 4, 2)$ is twice the first, therefore all minors of order $2$ are zero and $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$.
