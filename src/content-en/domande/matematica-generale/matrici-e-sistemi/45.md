In the Leontief model of an economy with two sectors the technology matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0.2 & 0.5 \\ 0.6 & 0.3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What does the coefficient $a_{21} = 0.6$ represent?

- To produce one unit of sector $2$, $0.6$ units of the output of sector $1$ are needed
- To produce one unit of sector $1$, $0.6$ units of the output of sector $2$ are needed
- Sector $2$ allocates $0.6$ units of each unit produced to final demand
- To produce $0.6$ units of sector $1$, one unit of the output of sector $2$ is needed

## Explanation

The technical coefficient $a_{ij}$ is the quantity of output of sector $i$ needed to produce one unit of sector $j$. In the coefficient $a_{21}$ the row index is $2$ and the column index is $1$, so $a_{21} = 0.6$ is the quantity of output of sector $2$ needed to produce one unit of sector $1$. Column $j$ of the technology matrix collects the inputs of one unit of sector $j$, therefore the first column says that one unit of sector $1$ requires $0.2$ units of sector $1$ and $0.6$ units of sector $2$. Final demand appears in the vector $d$ of the equation $(I_2 - A)\, x = d$ and not in the technology matrix.
