In the Leontief model of an economy with two sectors the technical coefficient matrix is
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0{,}2 & 0{,}5 \\ 0{,}6 & 0{,}3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
What does the coefficient $a_{21} = 0{,}6$ represent?

- Producing one unit of output in sector $2$ requires $0{,}6$ units of output from sector $1$
- Producing one unit of output in sector $1$ requires $0{,}6$ units of output from sector $2$
- Sector $2$ allocates $0{,}6$ units of each unit produced to final demand
- Producing $0{,}6$ units of output in sector $1$ requires one unit of output from sector $2$

## Explanation

The technical coefficient $a_{ij}$ is the amount of output from sector $i$ needed to produce one unit of output in sector $j$. In the coefficient $a_{21}$, the row index is $2$ and the column index is $1$, so $a_{21} = 0{,}6$ is the amount of output from sector $2$ needed to produce one unit of output in sector $1$. Column $j$ of the technical coefficient matrix lists the inputs needed for one unit of output in sector $j$. The first column therefore shows that producing one unit of output in sector $1$ requires $0{,}2$ units from sector $1$ and $0{,}6$ units from sector $2$. Final demand appears in the vector $d$ of the equation $(I_2 - A)\, x = d$ and not in the technical coefficient matrix.
