How many minors of order $2$ does a $3 \times 4$ matrix have?

- $18$
- $6$
- $12$
- $9$

## Explanation

A minor of order $2$ is obtained by choosing $2$ rows and $2$ columns of the matrix. There are $3$ pairs of rows that can be chosen out of $3$: the first and second, the first and third, the second and third. There are $\dfrac{4 \cdot 3}{2} = 6$ pairs of columns that can be chosen out of $4$. Each pair of rows combines with each pair of columns, so there are $3 \cdot 6 = 18$ minors of order $2$. The number $6$ counts only the pairs of columns.
