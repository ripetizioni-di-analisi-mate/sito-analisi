The matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$
swaps the two components of the column vector $v$ in the product $A v$. Which matrix is the power $A^{7}$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 7 \\ 7 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Matrix multiplication gives
$$
A^{2} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = I_2 .
$$
Since $A^{7} = (A^{2})^{3} A$ and $A^{2} = I_2$, we have $A^{7} = I_2^{3} A = A$. Hence the odd powers of $A$ are equal to $A$, and the even powers are equal to the identity.
