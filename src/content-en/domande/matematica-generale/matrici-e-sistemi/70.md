Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix},
$$
which matrix is the difference $(A + B)^{2} - \left( A^{2} + 2AB + B^{2} \right)$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ -1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -4 & 0 \\ 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

By the distributive properties of the product,
$$
(A + B)^{2} = (A + B)(A + B) = A^{2} + AB + BA + B^{2} ,
$$
so the difference equals $AB + BA - 2AB = BA - AB$. The row-by-column product gives
$$
AB = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
BA = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
therefore
$$
BA - AB = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
The difference would be the zero matrix if $A$ and $B$ commuted, but here $AB \neq BA$, so the formula for the square of a binomial of real numbers does not hold for these matrices.
