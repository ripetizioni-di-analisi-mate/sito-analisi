Let $A$ = $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 7\\1 & 9\end{pmatrix}$ and $B$ = $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1\\9 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. What is the entry in row one, column two of $AB$?

- $23$
- $41$
- $71$
- $32$

## Explanation

Multiply the first row of $A$ by the second column of $B$. Their dot product is $4\cdot1+7\cdot4$.
