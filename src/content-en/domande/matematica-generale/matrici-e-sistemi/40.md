How many solutions does the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 1 \\[4pt]
x + 2y + 3z = 2 \\[4pt]
2x + 3y + 4z = 4
\end{cases}
$$
have?

- Exactly one solution
- No solution
- $\infty^{1}$ solutions
- $\infty^{2}$ solutions

## Explanation

With the **Gaussian elimination method** one subtracts the first row from the second and twice the first from the third, then subtracts the second row from the third
$$
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 & 4 \end{array} \right)
\longrightarrow
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 2 \end{array} \right)
\longrightarrow
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) .
$$
The last row corresponds to the equation $0 = 1$. Hence $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ and $\operatorname{rk}(A \mid b) = 3$, and by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the system is **inconsistent**.
