What are the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - y + 2z = 1 \\[4pt]
2x + y + z = 5 \\[4pt]
3x + 3z = 6
\end{cases}
$$
setting $z = t$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $(t,\ 1 + t,\ t)$
- $(-t,\ -1 + t,\ t)$
- $(2 - t,\ 1 + t,\ t)$
- $(2 + 3t,\ 1 + t,\ t)$

## Explanation

With **Gauss's method** we subtract twice the first row from the second row and three times the first row from the third, then we subtract the second row from the third, and we obtain
$$
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 & 5 \\ 3 & 0 & 3 & 6 \end{array} \right)
\longrightarrow
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 & -3 & 3 \\ 0 & 3 & -3 & 3 \end{array} \right)
\longrightarrow
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 & -3 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right) .
$$
The echelon matrix has two nonzero rows, so $\operatorname{rk} A = \operatorname{rk}(A \mid b) = 2 < 3$ and by the **Rouché-Capelli theorem** the solutions are $\infty^{1}$. From the second equation, $3y - 3z = 3$, we get $y = 1 + t$. From the first we get $x = 1 + y - 2z = 1 + (1 + t) - 2t = 2 - t$. Therefore the solutions are $(2 - t,\ 1 + t,\ t)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
