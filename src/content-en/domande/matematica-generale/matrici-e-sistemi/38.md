In the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 6 \\[4pt]
2x - y + z = 3 \\[4pt]
x + 2y - z = 2
\end{cases}
$$
what is the value of the unknown $z$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $21$

## Explanation

With the expansion along the first row $\det A = 1 \cdot (1 - 2) - 1 \cdot (-2 - 1) + 1 \cdot (4 + 1) = 7$, so **Cramer's rule** applies. For the third unknown one replaces the third column of $A$ with the column of constant terms
$$
\det A_3 = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 & 6 \\ 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 1 & 2 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 1 \cdot (-2 - 6) - 1 \cdot (4 - 3) + 6 \cdot (4 + 1) = 21 .
$$
Therefore $z = \dfrac{\det A_3}{\det A} = \dfrac{21}{7} = 3$. As a check, the complete solution is $(1, 2, 3)$ and it satisfies all three equations.
