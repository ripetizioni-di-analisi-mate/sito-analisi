What is the value of the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 7 & -3 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 4 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 & 8 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \frac{1}{2} \end{vmatrix} ?
$$

- $3$
- $-3$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The entries below the main diagonal are all zero, so the matrix is upper triangular. The determinant of a triangular matrix is the product of the entries on the main diagonal, and the entries above the diagonal play no role. Therefore the determinant equals
$$
2 \cdot (-1) \cdot 3 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = -3 .
$$
