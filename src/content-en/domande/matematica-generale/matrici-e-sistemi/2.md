Two customers buy three goods. The first buys $2$, $1$, and $3$ units of the three goods, and the second buys $0$, $4$, and $1$ units. The unit prices are $5$, $3$, and $2$ euros. With the quantity matrix $Q$, one row per customer, and the price column vector $p$, what is $Qp$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 22 \\ 17 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 13 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 19 \\ 14 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 19 \\ 23 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The quantity matrix and the price vector are
$$
Q = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
p = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Since $Q$ has size $2 \times 3$ and $p$ has size $3 \times 1$, $Qp$ is a column vector with two entries, one per customer. To find each entry, take the dot product of a customer’s row and the price column, adding up the quantities multiplied by their respective prices.
$$
Qp = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \cdot 5 + 1 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot 2 \\ 0 \cdot 5 + 4 \cdot 3 + 1 \cdot 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 19 \\ 14 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Therefore the first customer spends $19$ euros and the second $14$ euros.
