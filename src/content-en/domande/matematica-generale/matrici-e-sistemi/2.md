Two customers buy three goods. The first buys $2$, $1$ and $3$ units of the three goods, the second $0$, $4$ and $1$ units; the unit prices are $5$, $3$ and $2$ euros. With the matrix $Q$ of the quantities (one row per customer) and the column vector $p$ of the prices, which vector is $Qp$?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 22 \\ 17 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 13 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 19 \\ 14 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 19 \\ 23 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The matrix of the quantities and the vector of the prices are
$$
Q = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
p = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Since $Q$ is of type $2 \times 3$ and $p$ is of type $3 \times 1$, the product $Qp$ is a column vector with two components, one per customer. Each component is obtained by multiplying the row of a customer by the column of the prices, so it is the sum of the products of the quantities by the corresponding prices
$$
Qp = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \cdot 5 + 1 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot 2 \\ 0 \cdot 5 + 4 \cdot 3 + 1 \cdot 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 19 \\ 14 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Therefore the first customer spends $19$ euros and the second $14$ euros.
