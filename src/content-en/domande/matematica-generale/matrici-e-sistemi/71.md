The two lines in the figure represent the two equations of a linear system in the unknowns $x$ and $y$. Which system?

::grafico{f="3-x;x-1" xmin=-2 xmax=6 ymin=-3 ymax=5 uguale=1 legenda=0 titolo="Two lines in the $xy$-plane"}

- $\begin{cases} x + y = 3 \\ x - y = -1 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x + y = 3 \\ x - y = 1 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} x - y = 3 \\ x + y = 1 \end{cases}$
- $\begin{cases} 2x + y = 3 \\ x - y = 1 \end{cases}$

## Explanation

The decreasing line passes through the points $(0, 3)$ and $(3, 0)$, which both satisfy the equation $x + y = 3$. The increasing line passes through the points $(1, 0)$ and $(0, -1)$, which both satisfy the equation $x - y = 1$. Hence the system consists of $x + y = 3$ and $x - y = 1$. The two lines meet at the point $(2, 1)$, which is the solution of the system. The line $x - y = -1$ passes instead through $(0, 1)$, and the line $2x + y = 3$ passes through $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, 0 \right)$.
