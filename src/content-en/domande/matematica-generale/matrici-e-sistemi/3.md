## Explanation

With **Sarrus's rule** one adds the products along the three diagonals descending to the right and subtracts those along the three diagonals ascending to the right. The products along the descending diagonals are $2 \cdot 0 \cdot (-1) = 0$, $(-1) \cdot 2 \cdot 4 = -8$ and $3 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 3$, with sum $-5$. The products along the ascending diagonals are $3 \cdot 0 \cdot 4 = 0$, $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 4$ and $(-1) \cdot 1 \cdot (-1) = 1$, with sum $5$. Therefore the determinant equals $-5 - 5 = -10$.

The same result is obtained with the Laplace expansion along the second row. The cofactors are $A_{21} = -\begin{vmatrix} -1 & 3 \\ 1 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = 2$ and $A_{23} = -\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 4 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -6$, so the determinant equals $1 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot (-6) = -10$.
