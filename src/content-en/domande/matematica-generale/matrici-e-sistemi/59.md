In the Leontief model $X=AX+d$, $A$ = $\begin{pmatrix}\frac{1}{2} & 0\\\frac{1}{3} & 0\end{pmatrix}$ and $d$ = $\begin{pmatrix}100\\100\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first sector’s output?

- $300$
- $100$
- $500$
- $200$

## Explanation

The first row gives $x_1=\dfrac{x_1}{2}+100$. Move the term involving $x_1$ to the left to obtain the output.
