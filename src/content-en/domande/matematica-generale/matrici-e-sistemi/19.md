Which matrix is the power $D^4$ of the diagonal matrix
$$
D = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix} ?
$$

- $\begin{pmatrix} 8 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -4 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 12 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 16 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 81 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 16 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 81 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 8 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 27 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The product of two diagonal matrices is diagonal and is obtained by multiplying the corresponding diagonal entries. Hence $D^4$ is the diagonal matrix whose diagonal entries are the fourth powers of the entries of $D$, that is $2^4 = 16$, $(-1)^4 = 1$ and $3^4 = 81$. The exponent $4$ is even, so the entry $-1$ becomes $+1$. The power $D^4$ is different from $4D$, which would be obtained by multiplying each entry by $4$.
