- The rank is $3$ for $k \neq 2$ and $2$ for $k = 2$
- The rank is $3$ for $k \neq \pm 2$ and $2$ for $k = \pm 2$
- The rank is $3$ for every value of $k$
- The rank is $3$ for $k \neq 2$ and $1$ for $k = 2$

## Explanation

With the Laplace expansion along the first row
$$
\det A = (k^2 + 4k - 12) - (2k - 4) + 2\,(6 - 3k) = k^2 - 4k + 4 = (k - 2)^2 .
$$
For $k \neq 2$ the determinant is nonzero, so $\operatorname{rk} A = 3$. The square $(k - 2)^2$ vanishes for $k = 2$, and for this value the rows are $(1, 1, 2)$, $(2, 2, 4)$ and $(3, 3, 6)$. The second and the third row are multiples of the first, so every minor of order $2$ is zero. Since $A$ has nonzero entries, for $k = 2$ the rank is $1$.
