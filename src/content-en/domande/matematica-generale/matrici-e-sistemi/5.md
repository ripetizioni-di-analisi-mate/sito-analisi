- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 \\ -3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -4 & 3 \\ 3 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 3 \\ 3 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & -3 \\ -3 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The determinant is $\det A = 4 \cdot 2 - 3 \cdot 3 = -1$, so $\det A \neq 0$ and $A$ is invertible. By the formula for the inverse of order $2$ the entries of the main diagonal are swapped, the other two change sign and everything is divided by $\det A$, so
$$
A^{-1} = \frac{1}{-1} \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 \\ -3 & 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 3 \\ 3 & -4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Since $\det A = -1$, the division by the determinant changes the sign of all the entries of the matrix.
