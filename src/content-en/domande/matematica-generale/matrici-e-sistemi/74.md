In the Leontief model $X=AX+d$, $A$ = $\begin{pmatrix}\frac{1}{3} & 0\\\frac{1}{5} & 0\end{pmatrix}$ and $d$ = $\begin{pmatrix}200\\200\end{pmatrix}$. What is the first sector’s output?

- $300$
- $600$
- $500$
- $200$

## Explanation

Row one gives $x_1=\dfrac{x_1}{3}+200$. Move the term in $x_1$ to the left and solve.
