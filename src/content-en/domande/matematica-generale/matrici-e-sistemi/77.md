What are the solutions of the system in row echelon form
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y - z + w = 2 \\[4pt]
y + 2z - w = 1 \\[4pt]
z + w = 3
\end{cases}
$$
setting $w = t$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$?

- $(10 - 4t,\ -5 + 2t,\ 3 - t,\ t)$
- $(4 - 3t,\ -5 + 3t,\ 3 - t,\ t)$
- $(10 - 5t,\ -5 + 3t,\ 3 - t,\ t)$
- $(10 + t,\ -5 - t,\ 3 + t,\ t)$

## Explanation

The system is already in row echelon form, with pivot unknowns $x$, $y$, $z$, and the unknown $w$ is free. Back substitution starts from the last equation. Setting $w = t$, the third equation gives $z = 3 - t$. The second equation gives
$$
y = 1 - 2z + w = 1 - 6 + 2t + t = -5 + 3t .
$$
The first equation gives
$$
x = 2 - y + z - w = 2 + 5 - 3t + 3 - t - t = 10 - 5t .
$$
Hence the solutions are $(10 - 5t,\ -5 + 3t,\ 3 - t,\ t)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$, that is, $\infty^{1}$ solutions.
