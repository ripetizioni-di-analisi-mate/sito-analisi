Let $A$ = $\begin{pmatrix}4 & 7\\1 & 9\end{pmatrix}$ and $B$ = $\begin{pmatrix}2 & 1\\9 & 4\end{pmatrix}$. What is the entry in row two, column one of $A^T-B$?

- $-8$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $7$

## Explanation

Transposition gives the requested entry of $A^T$ from the first row and second column of $A$. Then subtract $B_{21}=9$.
