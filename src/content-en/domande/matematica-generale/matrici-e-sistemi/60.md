Let $A$ be a square matrix of order $3$ such that $A A^{T} = I_3$. Which values can $\det A$ take?

- Only $1$
- Only $1$ or $-1$
- Any nonzero value
- Only $0$ or $1$

## Explanation

By **Binet's theorem** $\det(A A^{T}) = \det A \cdot \det A^{T}$, and since $\det A^{T} = \det A$ we get $\det(A A^{T}) = (\det A)^{2}$. On the other hand $\det I_3 = 1$, so $(\det A)^{2} = 1$ and $\det A = 1$ or $\det A = -1$. Both values occur: for $A = I_3$ the determinant is $1$, and for
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
we still have $A A^{T} = I_3$, with $\det A = -1$.
