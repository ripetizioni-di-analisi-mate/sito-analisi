Consider the system in the unknowns $x$, $y$, $z$ with augmented matrix
$$
\left( \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & h - 2 & h \end{array} \right),
$$
where $h$ is a real parameter. Which statement is true?

- For $h = 2$ the system has infinitely many solutions, for $h \neq 2$ it has a unique solution
- For $h = 2$ the system is inconsistent, for $h \neq 2$ it has a unique solution
- For $h = 0$ the system has infinitely many solutions, for $h = 2$ it is inconsistent, otherwise it has a unique solution
- The system has a unique solution for every value of $h$

## Explanation

The matrix is in row echelon form for every $h \neq 2$, with the three pivots $1$, $1$ and $h - 2$. In that case $\operatorname{rk} A = \operatorname{rk}(A \mid b) = 3$, equal to the number of unknowns, so the system has a unique solution. This also holds for $h = 0$, where the last equation is $-2z = 0$ and gives $z = 0$. For $h = 2$ the last row becomes $(0 \ \ 0 \ \ 0 \mid 2)$, that is, the equation $0 = 2$. Therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$ and $\operatorname{rk}(A \mid b) = 3$, and by the **Rouché–Capelli theorem** the system is inconsistent.
