In the Leontief model with two sectors, if every column of the technical coefficient matrix $A$ has sum less than $1$, the matrix $(I_2 - A)^{-1}$ exists and has nonnegative entries. Which of the following technical coefficient matrices satisfies the condition on the column sums?

- $\begin{pmatrix} 0.4 & 0.5 \\ 0.7 & 0.3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0.6 & 0.2 \\ 0.4 & 0.5 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0.5 & 0.6 \\ 0.3 & 0.3 \end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0.2 & 0.9 \\ 0.3 & 0.4 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

Column $j$ of the technical coefficient matrix contains the amounts of the products of the two sectors needed to produce one unit of sector $j$. For the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 0.5 & 0.6 \\ 0.3 & 0.3 \end{pmatrix}$ the column sums are $0.5 + 0.3 = 0.8$ and $0.6 + 0.3 = 0.9$, both less than $1$, so the condition is satisfied. Its first row has sum $1.1$, but the condition concerns the columns. In the other matrices one column has sum greater than or equal to $1$: $0.4 + 0.7 = 1.1$ in the first column of the first matrix, $0.6 + 0.4 = 1$ in the first column of the second, $0.9 + 0.4 = 1.3$ in the second column of the last.
