- $(-1, -2, -1)$
- $(1, 2, 1)$
- $(1, -2, 1)$
- $\left( 1, \dfrac{1}{2}, 1 \right)$

## Explanation

With Sarrus's rule $\det A = (-2 + 6 - 2) - (3 + 8 + 1) = -10$, so $\det A \neq 0$ and **Cramer's rule** applies. Replacing the first, the second and the third column of $A$ with the right-hand-side column gives $\det A_1 = -10$, $\det A_2 = -20$ and $\det A_3 = -10$. Therefore
$$
x = \frac{-10}{-10} = 1,
\qquad
y = \frac{-20}{-10} = 2,
\qquad
z = \frac{-10}{-10} = 1 .
$$
In each ratio $\det A_j$ is in the numerator and $\det A$ in the denominator, so the two negative signs cancel and the solution is $(1, 2, 1)$.
