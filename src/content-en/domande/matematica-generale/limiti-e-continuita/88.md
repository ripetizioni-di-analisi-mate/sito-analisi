On which interval does the intermediate value theorem guarantee a zero of $x + \left(x - 7\right)^{3} - 11$ using the endpoint signs?

- $\left(5, 6\right)$
- $\left(6, 7\right)$
- $\left(7, 9\right)$
- $\left(9, 10\right)$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous. Its values at $7$ and $9$ are $-4$ and $6$, so they have opposite signs.
