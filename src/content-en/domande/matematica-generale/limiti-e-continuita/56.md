What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\sqrt{x} + 2x}{3x - \sqrt{x}}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $+\infty$

## Explanation

Numerator and denominator tend to $+\infty$, so the limit has the form $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$. In both the highest power is $x$, because $\sqrt{x} = x^{\frac{1}{2}}$ grows more slowly than $x$. We factor out $x$ above and below:
$$
\frac{\sqrt{x} + 2x}{3x - \sqrt{x}} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}} + 2}{3 - \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \to 0$, the quotient tends to $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
