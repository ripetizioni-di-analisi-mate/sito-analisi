Two cost models are $C(q)=3q+\sqrt q$ and $D(q)=3q-\sqrt q$ for $q>0$. A manager argues that a ratio tending to $1$ makes the difference in euros tend to zero. What happens to the difference?

- $C(q)-D(q)\to0$
- $C(q)-D(q)\to1$
- $C(q)-D(q)\to+\infty$
- $C(q)-D(q)\to3$

## Explanation

Subtracting cancels the linear terms and leaves $2\sqrt q$, which grows without bound. Relative to $3q$ this gap becomes negligible even though its absolute size grows.
