The unit production cost of a company, $t$ months after a new line is started, is $c(t) = \dfrac{40t + 90}{2t + 3}$ euros. What value does $c(t)$ tend to as $t \to +\infty$?

- $30$
- $40$
- $20$
- $0$

## Explanation

As $t \to +\infty$ the numerator and the denominator diverge, so the limit is in the indeterminate form $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$. The numerator and the denominator have the same degree, therefore the limit is the ratio of the coefficients of $t$, that is, $\dfrac{40}{2} = 20$. The unit cost therefore tends to $20$ euros. The value $30$ is the initial cost $c(0) = \dfrac{90}{3}$.
