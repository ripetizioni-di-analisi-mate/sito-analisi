What are the horizontal asymptotes of the function $f(x) = \dfrac{2e^{x} + 3}{e^{x} + 1}$?

- $y = 3$ on the right and $y = 2$ on the left
- $y = 2$ on the right and $y = 3$ on the left
- $y = 2$ on the right and on the left
- $y = 2$ on the right and none on the left

## Explanation

As $x \to +\infty$ we divide the numerator and the denominator by $e^{x}$ and obtain $\dfrac{2 + 3e^{-x}}{1 + e^{-x}}$. Since $e^{-x} \to 0$, the limit is $2$. As $x \to -\infty$ we have $e^{x} \to 0$, so $f(x) \to \dfrac{0 + 3}{0 + 1} = 3$. Therefore the line $y = 2$ is a **horizontal asymptote on the right** and the line $y = 3$ is a **horizontal asymptote on the left**.
