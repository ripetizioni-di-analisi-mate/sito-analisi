The unit price is $p(q)=\dfrac{4 q + 7}{q + 9}$ for $q\geq0$. What value does the price approach as the quantity grows without bound?

- $4$
- $9$
- $0$
- $7$

## Explanation

Both the numerator and denominator are divided by $q$. The constant terms divided by $q$ tend to zero.
