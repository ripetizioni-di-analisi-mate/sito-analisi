- $a = 3$
- $a = 2$
- $a = 1$
- $a = 5$

## Explanation

For $x < 1$ and for $x > 1$ the function coincides with a polynomial, so it is continuous; it remains to impose continuity at $x = 1$. The left-hand limit and the value at $1$ are both equal to $a \cdot 1^2 = a$, while the right-hand limit is $2 \cdot 1 + 3 = 5$. The function is continuous at $1$ if and only if the two values coincide, that is, if $a = 5$.
