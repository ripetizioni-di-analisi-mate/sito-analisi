What is the right-hand limit minus the left-hand limit of $4 + \dfrac{\left|{x - 7}\right|}{x - 7}$ at $x=7$?

- $0$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The quotient containing the absolute value is $1$ on the right and $-1$ on the left. The constant cancels when the limits are subtracted.
