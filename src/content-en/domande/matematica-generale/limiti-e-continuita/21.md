The cumulative sales of a product, $t$ months after its launch, are $V(t) = \dfrac{8000}{1 + 3e^{-\frac{t}{2}}}$ units. What value does $V(t)$ tend to as $t \to +\infty$?

- $2000$
- $8000$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{8000}{3}$

## Explanation

As $t \to +\infty$ the exponent $-\dfrac{t}{2}$ tends to $-\infty$, so $e^{-\frac{t}{2}} \to 0$. The denominator therefore tends to $1 + 3 \cdot 0 = 1$, and $V(t)$ tends to $\dfrac{8000}{1} = 8000$. The value $2000$ is the number of units at the initial time, because $V(0) = \dfrac{8000}{1 + 3} = 2000$.
