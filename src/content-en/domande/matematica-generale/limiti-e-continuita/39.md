The shipping cost for a parcel weighing $x$ kg is $C(x) = 8 + x$ euros for $0 < x \leq 10$ and $C(x) = 20 + \dfrac{x}{2}$ euros for $x > 10$. How large is the jump in $C$ at $x = 10$?

- $18$
- $12$
- $7$
- $25$

## Explanation

The left-hand limit at $x = 10$ coincides with the value $C(10) = 8 + 10 = 18$, because for $x \leq 10$ the first formula applies. The second formula gives the right-hand limit $20 + \dfrac{10}{2} = 25$. The two limits are finite and different, so the discontinuity is a jump and its size is the difference, $25 - 18 = 7$ euros. The value $12$ is just the difference between the constant terms of the two formulas.
