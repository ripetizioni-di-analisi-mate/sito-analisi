- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

At $x = -1$ the numerator and the denominator both equal $0$, so the limit is in the indeterminate form $\dfrac{0}{0}$. The numerator is a sum of cubes, therefore $x^3 + 1 = (x + 1)(x^2 - x + 1)$. For $x \neq -1$ the factor $x + 1$ cancels and what remains is the polynomial $x^2 - x + 1$, which is continuous. Hence the limit equals $(-1)^2 - (-1) + 1 = 3$.
