- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

As $x \to +\infty$ the numerator and the denominator diverge, so the limit is in the indeterminate form $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$. Factoring out $x^2$ in the numerator and in the denominator we obtain
$$
\frac{4x^2 - 3x}{2x^2 + 7} = \frac{4 - \dfrac{3}{x}}{2 + \dfrac{7}{x^2}} .
$$
Since $\dfrac{3}{x} \to 0$ and $\dfrac{7}{x^2} \to 0$, the limit is the ratio of the leading coefficients, that is, $\dfrac{4}{2} = 2$.
