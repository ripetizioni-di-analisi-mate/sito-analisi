The unit price is $p(q)=\dfrac{3 q + 5}{q + 7}$ for $q\geq0$. What value does the price approach as the quantity grows without bound?

- $7$
- $0$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

Both the numerator and denominator are divided by $q$. The constant terms divided by $q$ tend to zero.
