## Explanation

At $x = 3$ the numerator and denominator both equal $0$, so the limit has the indeterminate form $\dfrac{0}{0}$. Since $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3)$ and $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$, for $x \neq 3$ we have
$$
\frac{x^2 - 5x + 6}{x^2 - 9} = \frac{x - 2}{x + 3} .
$$
The function $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 3}$ is continuous at $x = 3$. Substitution therefore gives the limit $\dfrac{3 - 2}{3 + 3} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.
