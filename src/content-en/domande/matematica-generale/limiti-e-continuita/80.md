What are the minimum and the maximum of the function $f(x) = x^2$ on the interval $[-1, 2]$?

- Minimum $1$, maximum $4$
- Minimum $0$, maximum $1$
- Minimum $0$, maximum $4$
- Minimum $-1$, maximum $4$

## Explanation

The function is continuous on the closed and bounded interval $[-1, 2]$, so by the **extreme value theorem** it has a maximum and a minimum. Since $x^2 \geq 0$ for every $x$ and $f(0) = 0$, with $0 \in [-1, 2]$, the minimum is $0$. For $x \in [-1, 2]$ we have $|x| \leq 2$, so $x^2 \leq 4$, and the value $4$ is attained at $x = 2$. Therefore the maximum is $4$.
