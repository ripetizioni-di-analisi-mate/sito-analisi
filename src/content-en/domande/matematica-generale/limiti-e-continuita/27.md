The total cost of producing $q$ units is $C(q) = q^2 + 5q$. What is $\displaystyle\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{C(10 + h) - C(10)}{h}$?

- $150$
- $15$
- $20$
- $25$

## Explanation

As $h \to 0$ the numerator and the denominator tend to $0$, so the limit is in the indeterminate form $\dfrac{0}{0}$. We have $C(10) = 150$ and
$$
C(10 + h) = (10 + h)^2 + 5(10 + h) = 150 + 25h + h^2 ,
$$
so for $h \neq 0$ the quotient equals $\dfrac{25h + h^2}{h} = 25 + h$. Therefore the limit equals $25$, which is the marginal cost at $q = 10$. The value $15$ is the average cost $\dfrac{C(10)}{10}$.
