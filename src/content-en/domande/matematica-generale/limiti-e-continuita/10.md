- Right-hand $+\infty$, left-hand $-\infty$
- Right-hand $-\infty$, left-hand $+\infty$
- Right-hand and left-hand $+\infty$
- Right-hand and left-hand $-2$

## Explanation

As $x \to 3$ the numerator $2x$ tends to $6$, which is positive, and the denominator $3 - x$ tends to $0$. For $x > 3$ we have $3 - x < 0$, so the denominator tends to $0^{-}$ and the right-hand limit equals $-\infty$. For $x < 3$ we have $3 - x > 0$, so the denominator tends to $0^{+}$ and the left-hand limit equals $+\infty$. The value $-2$ is the limit as $x \to \pm\infty$, given by the ratio of the coefficients of $x$.
