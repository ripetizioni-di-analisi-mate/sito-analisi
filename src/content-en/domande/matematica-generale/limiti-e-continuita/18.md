What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 3} \frac{x - 5}{(x - 3)^2}$?

- $+\infty$
- $0$
- The limit does not exist
- $-\infty$

## Explanation

As $x \to 3$ the numerator tends to $-2$, which is negative. The denominator $(x - 3)^2$ tends to $0$ and is positive both for $x < 3$ and for $x > 3$, so it tends to $0^{+}$ from both sides. Therefore the right-hand limit and the left-hand limit are both equal to $-\infty$, so the two-sided limit is $-\infty$.
