Which of the following functions tends to $0$ as $x \to +\infty$?

- $\dfrac{x^{100}}{(1{,}01)^{x}}$
- $\dfrac{(1{,}01)^{x}}{x^{100}}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{(\ln x)^{20}}$
- $\dfrac{x}{\ln x}$

## Explanation

By the **hierarchy of growth rates**, every exponential with base greater than $1$ grows faster than every power. The base $1{,}01$ is greater than $1$, so $\dfrac{x^{100}}{(1{,}01)^{x}} \to 0$, and its reciprocal $\dfrac{(1{,}01)^{x}}{x^{100}}$ tends to $+\infty$. Every power with a positive exponent grows faster than every power of the logarithm, so $\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{(\ln x)^{20}}$ and $\dfrac{x}{\ln x}$ tend to $+\infty$.
