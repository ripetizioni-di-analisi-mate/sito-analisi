A supplier charges $10q$ euros for $q$ units of goods when $0 < q \leq 100$ and $8q + k$ euros when $q > 100$. For which value of $k$ is the total price a continuous function of $q$?

- $k = 2$
- $k = 1000$
- $k = -200$
- $k = 200$

## Explanation

For $q < 100$ and for $q > 100$ the price is a polynomial of degree one, so it is continuous; it remains to impose continuity at $q = 100$. The left-hand limit and the value at $100$ are both equal to $10 \cdot 100 = 1000$, while the right-hand limit is $8 \cdot 100 + k = 800 + k$. The function is continuous at $100$ if and only if $800 + k = 1000$, that is, if $k = 200$.
