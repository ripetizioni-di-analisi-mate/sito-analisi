For which interval do the endpoint values of $x + \left(x - 5\right)^{3} - 8$ have opposite signs, so that Bolzano’s theorem guarantees a zero?

- $\left(3, 4\right)$
- $\left(5, 7\right)$
- $\left(7, 8\right)$
- $\left(4, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous. Its values at $5$ and $7$ are $-3$ and $7$, so they have opposite signs.
