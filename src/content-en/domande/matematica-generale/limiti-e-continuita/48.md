We have $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{3}{x} = 0$. For $\varepsilon = 0.01$, what is the smallest number $N > 0$ such that $\left| \dfrac{3}{x} \right| < \varepsilon$ for every $x > N$?

- $30$
- $3000$
- $300$
- $100$

## Explanation

For $x > 0$ we have $\left| \dfrac{3}{x} \right| = \dfrac{3}{x}$. The inequality $\dfrac{3}{x} < 0.01$ is equivalent to $x > \dfrac{3}{0.01} = 300$. So the condition holds for every $x > 300$, while for $N < 300$ the values of $x$ between $N$ and $300$ do not satisfy it. Therefore the smallest $N$ is $300$.
