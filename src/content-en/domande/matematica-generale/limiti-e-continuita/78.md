Which of the following equations certainly has at least one real solution?

- $x^5 - 3x^4 + 7 = 0$
- $x^4 + 3x^2 + 1 = 0$
- $x^2 - x + 1 = 0$
- $e^{x} + x^2 = 0$

## Explanation

The function $f(x) = x^5 - 3x^4 + 7$ is a polynomial, so it is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$. We have $f(-2) = -32 - 48 + 7 = -73 < 0$ and $f(-1) = -1 - 3 + 7 = 3 > 0$, therefore by the **Bolzano's theorem** the equation has a solution in $(-2, -1)$. The other equations have no real solutions. Indeed $x^4 + 3x^2 + 1 \geq 1$, the trinomial $x^2 - x + 1$ has discriminant $1 - 4 = -3 < 0$, and $e^{x} + x^2 > 0$ because $e^{x} > 0$ and $x^2 \geq 0$.
