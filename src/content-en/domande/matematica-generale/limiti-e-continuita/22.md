What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^3 - 8}{x^2 - x - 2}$?

- $12$
- $3$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

At $x = 2$ the numerator and the denominator equal $0$, so the limit is in the indeterminate form $\dfrac{0}{0}$. The numerator is a difference of cubes, therefore $x^3 - 8 = (x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4)$; the denominator factors as $(x - 2)(x + 1)$. For $x \neq 2$ we cancel the factor $x - 2$ and obtain $\dfrac{x^2 + 2x + 4}{x + 1}$, which is continuous at $x = 2$. The limit therefore equals $\dfrac{4 + 4 + 4}{3} = 4$.
