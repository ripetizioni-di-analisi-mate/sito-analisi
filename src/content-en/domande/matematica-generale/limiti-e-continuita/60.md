The unit price is $p(q)=\dfrac{2 q + 3}{q + 5}$ for $q\geq0$. What value does the price approach as the quantity grows without bound?

- $5$
- $3$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

Both the numerator and denominator are divided by $q$. The constant terms divided by $q$ tend to zero.
