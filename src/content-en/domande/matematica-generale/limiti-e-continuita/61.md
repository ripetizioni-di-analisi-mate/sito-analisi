An index experiences two consecutive relative changes, $+h$ and $-h$, with $0<h<1$. The first-order changes cancel. What does the total logarithmic return divided by $h^2$ approach as $h\to0^+$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $-2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The overall factor is $(1+h)(1-h)=1-h^2$, giving a logarithmic return of $\ln(1-h^2)$. With $u=-h^2$ the ratio becomes $-\dfrac{\ln(1+u)}u$, whose limit is $-1$.
