What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to -2} \frac{x^2 + x - 2}{x^2 + 4x + 4}$?

- The limit does not exist
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $0$
- $-\infty$

## Explanation

At $x = -2$ numerator and denominator equal $0$, and we have $x^2 + x - 2 = (x + 2)(x - 1)$ and $x^2 + 4x + 4 = (x + 2)^2$. For $x \neq -2$ we cancel the factor $x + 2$, and the function becomes $\dfrac{x - 1}{x + 2}$. The numerator tends to $-3$ and the denominator tends to $0$, positive as $x \to -2^+$ and negative as $x \to -2^-$. So the right-hand limit is $-\infty$ and the left-hand limit is $+\infty$. Since the two limits are different, the limit **does not exist**.
