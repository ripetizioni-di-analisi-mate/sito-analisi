For $x>0$, approximate $\sqrt{x^2+1}$ by $x$. Let $E=\sqrt{x^2+1}-x$ be the error. Which pair of bounds holds for every $x>0$?

- $\dfrac1{2x}<E<\dfrac1x$
- $0<E<\dfrac1{2x+1}$
- $\dfrac1{2x+1}<E<\dfrac1{2x}$
- $-\dfrac1{2x}<E<0$

## Explanation

Rationalising gives $E=\dfrac1{\sqrt{x^2+1}+x}$. Since $x<\sqrt{x^2+1}<x+1$, the denominator lies between $2x$ and $2x+1$. Taking positive reciprocals reverses the inequalities.
