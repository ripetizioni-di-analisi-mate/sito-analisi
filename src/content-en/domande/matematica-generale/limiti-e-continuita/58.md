For which interval do the endpoint values of $x + \left(x - 3\right)^{3} - 5$ have opposite signs, so that Bolzano’s theorem guarantees a zero?

- $\left(2, 3\right)$
- $\left(1, 2\right)$
- $\left(5, 6\right)$
- $\left(3, 5\right)$

## Explanation

The polynomial is continuous. Its values at $3$ and $5$ are $-2$ and $8$, so they have opposite signs.
