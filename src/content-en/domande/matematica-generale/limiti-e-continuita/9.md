- $+\infty$
- $3$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

As $x \to +\infty$ the numerator and the denominator diverge, so the limit is in the indeterminate form $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$. Dividing the numerator and denominator by the faster-growing term $2^{x}$ gives
$$
\frac{2^{x} + x^{10}}{3 \cdot 2^{x}} = \frac{1 + \dfrac{x^{10}}{2^{x}}}{3} .
$$
By the **hierarchy of growth rates**, every exponential with base greater than $1$ grows faster than every power, hence $\dfrac{x^{10}}{2^{x}} \to 0$. Therefore the limit equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
