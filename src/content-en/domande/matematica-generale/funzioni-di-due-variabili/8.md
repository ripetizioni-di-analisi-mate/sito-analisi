- $z = 3x - 3y$
- $z = 3x - y - 3$
- $z = -3x + 3y - 1$
- $z = 3x - 3y - 1$

## Explanation

The value of the function at the point is $f(1, 1) = 1 - 2 = -1$. The partial derivatives are $f_x = 3x^2 y$ and $f_y = x^3 - 4y$, so $f_x(1, 1) = 3$ and $f_y(1, 1) = 1 - 4 = -3$. The function is a polynomial, therefore it is differentiable and the tangent plane has equation
$$
z = -1 + 3\,(x - 1) - 3\,(y - 1) .
$$
Expanding the products gives $z = 3x - 3y - 1$.
