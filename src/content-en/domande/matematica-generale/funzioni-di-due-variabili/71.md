For which of the following functions is the Hessian matrix the same at every point of the plane?

- $f(x, y) = 3x^2 - 4xy + y^2$
- $f(x, y) = x^3 + y^2$
- $f(x, y) = e^{x + y}$
- $f(x, y) = xy^2$

## Explanation

The Hessian matrix is the same at every point if and only if the four second derivatives are constant. For $3x^2 - 4xy + y^2$ we have $f_{xx} = 6$, $f_{xy} = f_{yx} = -4$ and $f_{yy} = 2$, so
$$
H(x, y) = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & -4 \\ -4 & 2 \end{pmatrix}
$$
at every point. For $x^3 + y^2$ we have $f_{xx} = 6x$, for $e^{x + y}$ all second derivatives equal $e^{x + y}$, and for $xy^2$ we have $f_{yy} = 2x$; in these three cases the Hessian matrix depends on the point. The answer is therefore **$3x^2 - 4xy + y^2$**.
