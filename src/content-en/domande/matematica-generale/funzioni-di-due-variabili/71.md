For $3 x^{2} + 5 y^{2}$, what is the differential at $(1,1)$ for increments $dx=\dfrac1{6}$ and $dy=-\dfrac1{20}$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives at the point are $6$ and $10$. The differential is the sum of their products with the corresponding increments.
