Let $f$ be a function defined on the whole plane, with continuous second derivatives, such that $f_x(x, y) = 3x^2 y + 2y$. What is $f_{yx}(1, 2)$?

- $5$
- $12$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The mixed derivatives are continuous on the plane, which is an open set, so the hypotheses of **Schwarz's theorem** are satisfied and $f_{yx} = f_{xy}$. The derivative $f_{xy}$ is obtained by differentiating $f_x$ with respect to $y$, and it equals $3x^2 + 2$. Therefore $f_{yx}(1, 2) = f_{xy}(1, 2) = 3 + 2 = 5$. The result does not depend on $y$, because $f_x$ is of first degree in $y$.
