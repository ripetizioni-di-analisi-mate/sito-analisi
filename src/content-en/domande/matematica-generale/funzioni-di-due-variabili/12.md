For the production function $Q(K, L) = 5\,K^{\frac{2}{5}} L^{\frac{3}{5}}$, what are the marginal products for $K = 32$ and $L = 32$?

- $Q_K = 3, \quad Q_L = 2$
- $Q_K = 64, \quad Q_L = 96$
- $Q_K = \dfrac{2}{5}, \quad Q_L = \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $Q_K = 2, \quad Q_L = 3$

## Explanation

Since $32^{\frac{2}{5}} = 4$ and $32^{\frac{3}{5}} = 8$, the output is $Q(32, 32) = 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 8 = 160$. For the Cobb-Douglas function the **marginal products** are $Q_K = \dfrac{a\,Q}{K}$ and $Q_L = \dfrac{b\,Q}{L}$, with $a = \dfrac{2}{5}$ and $b = \dfrac{3}{5}$. Hence $Q_K = \dfrac{2}{5} \cdot \dfrac{160}{32} = 2$ and $Q_L = \dfrac{3}{5} \cdot \dfrac{160}{32} = 3$.
