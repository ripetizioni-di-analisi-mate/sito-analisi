Which of the following utility functions, defined for $x > 0$ and $y > 0$, has at every bundle the same marginal rate of substitution $\dfrac{U_x}{U_y}$ as the function $U(x, y) = xy$?

- $V(x, y) = x + y$
- $V(x, y) = \ln x + \ln y$
- $V(x, y) = x^2 y$
- $V(x, y) = x y^2$

## Explanation

For $U(x, y) = xy$ we have $U_x = y$ and $U_y = x$, so the marginal rate of substitution is $\dfrac{y}{x}$. For $V(x, y) = \ln x + \ln y$ we have $V_x = \dfrac{1}{x}$ and $V_y = \dfrac{1}{y}$, so
$$
\frac{V_x}{V_y} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{x}}{\dfrac{1}{y}} = \frac{y}{x} .
$$
The two rates coincide at every bundle, because $\ln x + \ln y = \ln(xy)$ and the two functions have the same indifference curves. For the other functions the rate is $1$, $\dfrac{2y}{x}$ and $\dfrac{y}{2x}$.
