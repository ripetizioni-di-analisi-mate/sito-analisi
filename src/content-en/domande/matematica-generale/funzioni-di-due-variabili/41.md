A consumer has utility function $U(x, y) = \ln x + 2 \ln y$, with $x > 0$ and $y > 0$. What is the marginal rate of substitution $\dfrac{U_x}{U_y}$ at the bundle $(3, 4)$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

The marginal utilities are $U_x = \dfrac{1}{x}$ and $U_y = \dfrac{2}{y}$. The **marginal rate of substitution** is therefore
$$
\frac{U_x}{U_y} = \frac{1}{x} \cdot \frac{y}{2} = \frac{y}{2x} .
$$
At the bundle $(3, 4)$ we get $\dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{2}{3}$.
