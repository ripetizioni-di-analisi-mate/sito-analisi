A consumer has a utility function $U(x, y) = 3 \ln x + \ln y$, with $x > 0$ and $y > 0$. At the bundle $(2, 4)$, what is the marginal rate of substitution $\dfrac{U_x}{U_y}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $12$

## Explanation

The marginal utilities are $U_x = \dfrac{3}{x}$ and $U_y = \dfrac{1}{y}$. The **marginal rate of substitution** is therefore
$$
\frac{U_x}{U_y} = \frac{3}{x} \cdot y = \frac{3y}{x} .
$$
At the bundle $(2, 4)$ we get $\dfrac{12}{2} = 6$.
