What is the limit of $\dfrac{x y}{x^{2} + 2 y^{2}}$ at the origin along the line $y=3x$?

- $\dfrac{3}{19}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $0$

## Explanation

After substituting $y=3x$, the numerator and denominator both contain the factor $x^2$. Since $x\ne0$, that factor cancels and the ratio is constant.
