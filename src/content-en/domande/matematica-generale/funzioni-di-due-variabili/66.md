Which of the following functions has partial derivatives $f_x(x, y) = 2xy$ and $f_y(x, y) = x^2 + 1$?

- $f(x, y) = x^2 y + x$
- $f(x, y) = 2xy + y$
- $f(x, y) = x^2 y + y$
- $f(x, y) = x^2 y + y^2$

## Explanation

For $f(x, y) = x^2 y + y$, treating $y$ as a constant gives $f_x = 2xy$, and treating $x$ as a constant gives $f_y = x^2 + 1$; so **$x^2 y + y$** has the required derivatives. For $x^2 y + x$ we have $f_x = 2xy + 1$, which differs from $2xy$. For $2xy + y$ we have $f_x = 2y$. For $x^2 y + y^2$ we have $f_y = x^2 + 2y$, which differs from $x^2 + 1$.
