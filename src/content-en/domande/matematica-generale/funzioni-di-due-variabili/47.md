Which of the following points belongs to the open disk $B((2, -1), 3)$ with center $(2, -1)$ and radius $3$?

- $(4, 1)$
- $(5, -1)$
- $(-1, 0)$
- $(2, 2)$

## Explanation

A point $P$ belongs to $B((2, -1), 3)$ if its distance from the center is *strictly* less than $3$. The point $(4, 1)$ is at distance $\sqrt{2^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{8}$ from the center, and $\sqrt{8} < 3$ because $8 < 9$, so **$(4, 1)$ belongs to the disk**. The points $(5, -1)$ and $(2, 2)$ are at distance exactly $3$ from the center, therefore they lie on the boundary circle and do not belong to the disk. The point $(-1, 0)$ is at distance $\sqrt{9 + 1} = \sqrt{10} > 3$, so it lies outside.
