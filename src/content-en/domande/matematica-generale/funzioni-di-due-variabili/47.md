Which set must be excluded from the natural domain of $\dfrac{1}{2 x + 3 y - 5}$?

- $2 x - 3 y = 5$
- $2 x + 3 y = -5$
- Only the origin
- $2 x + 3 y = 5$

## Explanation

A rational function is defined where its denominator is nonzero. The zeros of this denominator form a line.
