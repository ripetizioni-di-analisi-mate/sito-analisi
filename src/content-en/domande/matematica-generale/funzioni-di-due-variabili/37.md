A function $f$, defined for $x > 0$ and $y > 0$, is homogeneous of degree $2$ and satisfies $f(1, 2) = 3$. What is $f(3, 6)$?

- $9$
- $27$
- $81$
- $12$

## Explanation

The point $(3, 6)$ is obtained from $(1, 2)$ by multiplying both coordinates by $t = 3$. Since $f$ is homogeneous of degree $2$, $f(tx, ty) = t^{2} f(x, y)$, so
$$
f(3, 6) = 3^2 \cdot f(1, 2) = 9 \cdot 3 = 27 .
$$
The value $9$ corresponds to a function homogeneous of degree $1$, and the value $81$ to one of degree $3$.
