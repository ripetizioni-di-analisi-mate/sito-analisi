What is the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \sqrt{xy}$?

- The first and third quadrants, axes excluded
- The first and third quadrants, axes included
- The first quadrant, axes included
- The second and fourth quadrants, axes included

## Explanation

The square root requires $xy \geq 0$. The product $xy$ is positive when $x$ and $y$ have the same sign, that is, in the first and third quadrants, and it is zero when $x = 0$ or $y = 0$, that is, on the axes. The domain is therefore the union of the first and third quadrants, **axes included**. In the second and fourth quadrants the coordinates have opposite signs, so $xy < 0$ and the square root is not defined.
