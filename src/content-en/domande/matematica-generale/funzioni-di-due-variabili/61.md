Which condition describes the entire domain of $\sqrt{3 x - 5 y + 7}$?

- $3 x - 5 y + 7 > 0$
- $3 x - 5 y + 7 \geq 0$
- $3 x - 5 y + 7 \leq 0$
- $3 x - 5 y + 7 = 0$

## Explanation

A real square root requires a nonnegative radicand. Points on the line where the radicand vanishes are included.
