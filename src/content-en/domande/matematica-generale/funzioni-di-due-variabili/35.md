What is the equation of the tangent plane to the graph of $f(x, y) = e^{x - 2y}$ at the point with coordinates $x = 2$, $y = 1$?

- $z = x - 2y$
- $z = x + 2y - 3$
- $z = x - y$
- $z = x - 2y + 1$

## Explanation

The value of the function at the point is $f(2, 1) = e^{0} = 1$. By the chain rule $f_x = e^{x - 2y}$ and $f_y = -2e^{x - 2y}$, because the derivative of $x - 2y$ with respect to $y$ is $-2$; hence $f_x(2, 1) = 1$ and $f_y(2, 1) = -2$. The partial derivatives are continuous on the plane, so $f$ is differentiable and the **tangent plane** has equation
$$
z = 1 + (x - 2) - 2\,(y - 1) .
$$
Expanding gives $z = x - 2y + 1$.
