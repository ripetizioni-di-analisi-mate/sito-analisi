- $f_x = 3x^2 - 8xy^2, \quad f_y = -8x^2 y + 2$
- $f_x = 3x^2 - 8xy^2 + 2, \quad f_y = -8x^2 y + 2$
- $f_x = 3x^2 - 16xy, \quad f_y = -16xy + 2$
- $f_x = 3x^2 - 8x^2 y, \quad f_y = -8xy^2 + 2$

## Explanation

The derivative $f_x$ is taken with respect to $x$, holding $y$ constant. The derivative of $x^3$ is $3x^2$, that of $-4x^2 y^2$ is $-8xy^2$, because $-4y^2$ is a constant factor, and that of $2y$ is $0$, because $2y$ does not depend on $x$. The derivative $f_y$ is taken with respect to $y$, holding $x$ constant. In this case $x^3$ has derivative $0$, $-4x^2 y^2$ has derivative $-8x^2 y$ and $2y$ has derivative $2$, therefore $f_y = -8x^2 y + 2$.
