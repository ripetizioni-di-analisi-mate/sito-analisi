For utility $U(x,y)=3 \ln{\left(x \right)} + 5 \ln{\left(y \right)}$, what is the ratio $U_x/U_y$ at the bundle $(1,2)$?

- $- \dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

The marginal utilities are $\dfrac{3}x$ and $\dfrac{5}y$. At the stated bundle divide $3$ by $\dfrac{5}2$.
