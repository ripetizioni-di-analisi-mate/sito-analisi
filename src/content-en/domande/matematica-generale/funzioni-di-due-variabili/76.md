Which condition describes the entire domain of $\sqrt{4 x - 7 y + 9}$?

- $4 x - 7 y + 9 \geq 0$
- $4 x - 7 y + 9 > 0$
- $4 x - 7 y + 9 \leq 0$
- $4 x - 7 y + 9 = 0$

## Explanation

A real square root requires a nonnegative radicand. Points on the line where the radicand vanishes are included.
