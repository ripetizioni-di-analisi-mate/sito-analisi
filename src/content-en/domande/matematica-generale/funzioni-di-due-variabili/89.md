For the utility function $U(x,y)=4 \ln{\left(x \right)} + 7 \ln{\left(y \right)}$, what is the ratio $U_x/U_y$ at the bundle $(1,2)$?

- $\dfrac{8}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{8}{7}$

## Explanation

The marginal utilities are $\dfrac{4}x$ and $\dfrac{7}y$. At the given bundle, the ratio is $4$ divided by $\dfrac{7}2$.
