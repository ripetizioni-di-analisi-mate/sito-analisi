At which points $(x, y)$ of the plane is the function $f(x, y) = \sqrt{\ln(x + y)}$ defined?

- At the points with $x + y > 0$
- At the points with $x + y \geq 1$
- At the points with $0 < x + y \leq 1$
- At the points with $x + y \geq 0$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires $x + y > 0$, and the square root requires its argument to be greater than or equal to zero, that is, $\ln(x + y) \geq 0$. The two conditions must hold together:
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y > 0 \\[4pt]
\ln(x + y) \geq 0
\end{cases}
$$
Since the logarithm is increasing and $\ln 1 = 0$, the second condition is equivalent to $x + y \geq 1$, which implies the first. Therefore the domain is **$x + y \geq 1$**, the half-plane above the line $y = 1 - x$, line included.
