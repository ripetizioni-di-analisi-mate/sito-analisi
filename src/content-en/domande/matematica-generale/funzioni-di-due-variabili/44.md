What type of returns to scale does the production function $Q(K, L) = \dfrac{KL}{K + L}$, with $K > 0$ and $L > 0$, have?

- Increasing returns to scale
- Decreasing returns to scale
- Constant returns to scale
- No type of returns to scale, because the function is not homogeneous

## Explanation

Multiplying capital and labor by $t > 0$ gives
$$
Q(tK, tL) = \frac{tK \cdot tL}{tK + tL} = \frac{t^2 KL}{t\,(K + L)} = t\,Q(K, L) .
$$
Hence the function is homogeneous of degree $1$, even though it is not a Cobb-Douglas function. Since the degree equals $1$, the function has **constant returns to scale**, and doubling the inputs doubles the output.
