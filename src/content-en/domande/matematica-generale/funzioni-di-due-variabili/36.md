The sides of a rectangle measure $x = 10$ cm and $y = 5$ cm, each with a measurement error of at most $0.1$ cm. Using the differential of the area $A(x, y) = xy$, what is the estimated maximum error in the area?

- $1$ cm²
- $0.5$ cm²
- $1.5$ cm²
- $0.01$ cm²

## Explanation

The partial derivatives of the area are $A_x = y = 5$ and $A_y = x = 10$. The error is largest when both sides are overestimated, or both underestimated, so we take $dx = dy = 0.1$. Therefore
$$
dA = 5 \cdot 0.1 + 10 \cdot 0.1 = 1.5 .
$$
The estimated maximum error in the area is thus $1.5$ cm², compared with the exact change $10.1 \cdot 5.1 - 50 = 1.51$ cm². The value $0.01$ cm² is the product $dx \cdot dy$ of the two errors, which the differential neglects.
