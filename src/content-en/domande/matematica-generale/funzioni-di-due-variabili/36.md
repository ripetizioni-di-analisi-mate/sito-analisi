For the function $f(x, y) = x y^2$, what is the differential $df$ at the point $(2, 1)$ with increments $dx = 0{,}1$ and $dy = 0{,}05$?

- $0{,}2$
- $0{,}45$
- $0{,}3$
- $2{,}3$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = y^2$ and $f_y = 2xy$, so $f_x(2, 1) = 1$ and $f_y(2, 1) = 4$. The **differential** is therefore
$$
df = f_x(2, 1)\,dx + f_y(2, 1)\,dy = 1 \cdot 0{,}1 + 4 \cdot 0{,}05 = 0{,}3 .
$$
The number $df$ approximates the change in $f$, while the approximate value of the function is $f(2{,}1;\ 1{,}05) \approx f(2, 1) + df = 2{,}3$.
