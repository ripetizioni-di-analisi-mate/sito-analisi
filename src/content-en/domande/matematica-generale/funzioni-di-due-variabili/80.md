What is the limit of $\dfrac{x y}{x^{2} + 4 y^{2}}$ at the origin along the line $y=7x$?

- $\dfrac{7}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{28}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{7}{197}$

## Explanation

Substitute $y=7x$. Both numerator and denominator contain $x^2$, so cancelling it for $x\ne0$ leaves a constant.
