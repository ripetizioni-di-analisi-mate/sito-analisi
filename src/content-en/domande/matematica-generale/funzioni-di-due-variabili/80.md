What is the limit of $\dfrac{x y}{x^{2} + 4 y^{2}}$ at the origin along the line $y=7x$?

- $\dfrac{7}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{28}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{7}{197}$

## Explanation

After substituting $y=7x$, the numerator and denominator both contain the factor $x^2$. Since $x\ne0$, that factor cancels and the ratio is constant.
