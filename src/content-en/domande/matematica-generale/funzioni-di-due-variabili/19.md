Which of the following points belongs to the natural domain of the function $f(x, y) = \sqrt{y - x} + \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{4 - x^2 - y^2}}$?

- $(1, 0)$
- $(0, 2)$
- $(0, 1)$
- $(-2, -1)$

## Explanation

The first square root requires $y - x \geq 0$. The second square root is in the denominator, so its argument must be strictly positive. The conditions to be satisfied together are
$$
\begin{cases}
y \geq x \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 < 4
\end{cases}
$$
The point $(0, 1)$ satisfies both, because $1 \geq 0$ and $0 + 1 < 4$. The point $(1, 0)$ does not satisfy the first one, because $0 < 1$, while the points $(0, 2)$ and $(-2, -1)$ do not satisfy the second one, because they give $x^2 + y^2$ equal to $4$ and $5$.
