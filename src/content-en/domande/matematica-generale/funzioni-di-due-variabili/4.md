For the function $f(x, y) = x^2 - y$, which level curve passes through the point $(2, 1)$?

- $y = x^2 + 3$
- $y = x^2 - 3$
- $y = x^2 - 5$
- $y = 3 - x^2$

## Explanation

The level curve through $(2, 1)$ has level $k = f(2, 1)$, and $f(2, 1) = 4 - 1 = 3$. Its points therefore satisfy the equation $x^2 - y = 3$. Solving for $y$ gives $y = x^2 - 3$, a parabola with vertex at $(0, -3)$. The point $(2, 1)$ belongs to the curve, because $4 - 3 = 1$.
