Output is $Q(x,y)=2 x + 3 y$. By how much must $y$ change if $x$ rises by one unit while output remains constant?

- $- \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The change in output is $2\,\Delta x+3\,\Delta y$. Setting the change in output to zero with $\Delta x=1$ determines the change in $y$.
