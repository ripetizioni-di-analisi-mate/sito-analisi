What is the radius of the level curve for $25$ of $\left(x - 2\right)^{2} + \left(y + 3\right)^{2}$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $25$
- $3$

## Explanation

The function gives the squared distance from $(2,-3)$. On the level curve the distance is therefore the square root of $25$.
