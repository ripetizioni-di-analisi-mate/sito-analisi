What is the boundary of the set $A = \{ (x, y) : 0 < x^2 + y^2 \leq 1 \}$?

- The circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ alone
- The circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and the origin
- The disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$
- The origin alone

## Explanation

The set $A$ is the closed disk of radius $1$ with the origin removed. Every neighborhood of a point of the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ contains points with $x^2 + y^2 < 1$, which lie in $A$, and points with $x^2 + y^2 > 1$, which do not, so the circle is part of the boundary. Every neighborhood of the origin contains the origin, which is not in $A$, and points near the origin other than the origin itself, which are in $A$; therefore the origin is a boundary point as well. The remaining points with $0 < x^2 + y^2 < 1$ are interior points, and those with $x^2 + y^2 > 1$ are exterior points. Hence the boundary consists of **the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and the origin**.
