Which of the following points is a boundary point of the set $A = \{ (x, y) : 1 < x^2 + y^2 < 9 \}$?

- $(0, 3)$
- $(2, 0)$
- $(0, 0)$
- $(2, 2)$

## Explanation

The set $A$ is the annulus between the circles centered at the origin with radii $1$ and $3$, both excluded, and its boundary consists of these two circles. The point $(0, 3)$ satisfies $0^2 + 3^2 = 9$, so it lies on the outer circle, and every neighborhood of it contains points of $A$ and points not in $A$. Therefore $(0, 3)$ is a **boundary point**. The points $(2, 0)$ and $(2, 2)$ give $x^2 + y^2$ equal to $4$ and $8$, values strictly between $1$ and $9$, so they are interior points. The origin is an exterior point, because the disk centered at $(0, 0)$ with radius $1$ contains no points of $A$.
