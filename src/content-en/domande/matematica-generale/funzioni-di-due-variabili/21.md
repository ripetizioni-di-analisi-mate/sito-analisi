For the function $f(x, y) = y\,e^{-x}$, which level curve passes through the point $(0, 3)$?

- $y = 3e^{-x}$
- $y = e^{3x}$
- $y = e^{x} + 2$
- $y = 3e^{x}$

## Explanation

The level curve through $(0, 3)$ has level $k = f(0, 3) = 3 \cdot e^{0} = 3$. Its points therefore satisfy the equation $y\,e^{-x} = 3$. Multiplying both sides by $e^{x}$ gives $y = 3e^{x}$. The point $(0, 3)$ belongs to the curve, because $3e^{0} = 3$.
