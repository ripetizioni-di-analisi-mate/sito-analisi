A sensor records a differentiable signal $F(x,y)$ along $\gamma(t)=(3+t,1)$. At $t=0$ the signal changes at a rate of $12$. A second sensor follows the same line according to $\eta(t)=(3+t^2,1)$. What are $F_x(3,1)$ and the rate of change along $\eta$ at $t=0$, respectively?

- $(12,12)$
- $(0,12)$
- $(12,24)$
- $(12,0)$

## Explanation

The velocity on the first path is $(1,0)$, so its reading equals $F_x(3,1)=12$. The second path has velocity $(2t,0)$ and the sensor is momentarily at rest when $t=0$. The chain rule gives a zero rate of change even though it follows the same line.
