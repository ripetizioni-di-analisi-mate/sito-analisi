For the function $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 2x$, for which values of $k$ is the level curve $k$ the empty set?

- $k < 0$
- $k < -1$
- $k \leq -1$
- $k < 1$

## Explanation

Completing the square gives $x^2 - 2x = (x - 1)^2 - 1$, so
$$
f(x, y) = (x - 1)^2 + y^2 - 1 .
$$
The level curve $k$ therefore has equation $(x - 1)^2 + y^2 = k + 1$. The left-hand side is greater than or equal to zero, so for $k + 1 < 0$ the equation has no solutions. For $k = -1$ the curve reduces to the point $(1, 0)$, and for $k > -1$ it is the circle centered at $(1, 0)$ with radius $\sqrt{k + 1}$. The level curve is empty for **$k < -1$**.
