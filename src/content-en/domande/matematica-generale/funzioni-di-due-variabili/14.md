For the production function $Q(K, L) = 3\,K^{\frac{1}{2}} L^{\frac{3}{4}}$, by what number is the output multiplied when capital and labor both double?

- $2^{\frac{3}{8}}$
- $2^{\frac{5}{4}}$
- $3 \cdot 2^{\frac{5}{4}}$
- $2^{\frac{3}{4}}$

## Explanation

Doubling both factors gives
$$
Q(2K, 2L) = 3\,(2K)^{\frac{1}{2}} (2L)^{\frac{3}{4}} = 2^{\frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{4}} \cdot 3\,K^{\frac{1}{2}} L^{\frac{3}{4}} = 2^{\frac{5}{4}}\,Q(K, L) .
$$
The constant $3$ already appears in $Q(K, L)$, so the multiplicative factor is $2^{\frac{5}{4}} \approx 2.38$. The same computation with a number $t > 0$ in place of $2$ gives $Q(tK, tL) = t^{\frac{5}{4}}\,Q(K, L)$, so the function is homogeneous of degree $\dfrac{5}{4}$. Since $\dfrac{5}{4} > 1$, the function has **increasing returns to scale**.
