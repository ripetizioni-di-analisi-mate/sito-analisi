A consumer has utility function $U(x, y) = x + \ln y$, with $y > 0$. At which bundles does the marginal rate of substitution $\dfrac{U_x}{U_y}$ equal $5$?

- At all bundles with $x = 5$
- At all bundles with $y = 5$
- Only at the bundle $(5, 5)$
- At all bundles with $y = \dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The marginal utilities are $U_x = 1$ and $U_y = \dfrac{1}{y}$. The marginal rate of substitution is therefore
$$
\frac{U_x}{U_y} = \frac{1}{\dfrac{1}{y}} = y ,
$$
which does not depend on $x$. Therefore the rate equals $5$ **at all bundles with $y = 5$**, whatever the quantity $x$ of the first good.
