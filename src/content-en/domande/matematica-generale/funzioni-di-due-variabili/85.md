At an interior bundle $U_x=6$ and $U_y=2$. The consumer loses $0.3$ units of good $x$. How many units of good $y$ compensate for that loss to first order?

- $0.1$
- $0.9$
- $1.8$
- $3$

## Explanation

To keep utility constant to first order we need $6\,\Delta x+2\,\Delta y=0$. Setting $\Delta x=-0.3$ gives $2\,\Delta y=1.8$, so $0.9$ units of $y$ must be added.
