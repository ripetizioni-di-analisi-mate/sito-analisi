What is the constant term in $z=ux+vy+w$ for the tangent plane to the graph of $x^{2} + 4 x y + 7 y$ at the point above $(1,1)$?

- $-4$
- $-5$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The height of the plane is $12$ at $(1,1)$. Its slopes are $6$ and $11$, so the constant term is the height at that point minus both slopes.
