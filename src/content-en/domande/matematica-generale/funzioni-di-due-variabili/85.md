What is the constant term in $z=ux+vy+w$ for the tangent plane to the graph of $x^{2} + 4 x y + 7 y$ above $(1,1)$?

- $-4$
- $-5$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The plane has value $12$ at $(1,1)$. Its slopes are $6$ and $11$, so subtract both from the plane’s value at the point.
