Which of the following functions has mixed derivative $f_{xy}(x, y) = 0$ at every point of the plane?

- $f(x, y) = e^{x + y}$
- $f(x, y) = e^{x} + \sin y$
- $f(x, y) = x^2 + xy$
- $f(x, y) = x^2 y$

## Explanation

For $f(x, y) = e^{x} + \sin y$ we have $f_x = e^{x}$, which does not depend on $y$, so $f_{xy} = 0$ at every point. In general the mixed derivative is zero when the function is the sum of a function of $x$ alone and a function of $y$ alone. For the other functions $f_{xy} = e^{x + y}$, $f_{xy} = 1$ and $f_{xy} = 2x$, which are not zero on the whole plane.
