For the function $f(x, y) = x^3 y^2 - 2xy$, what is the mixed derivative $f_{xy}(1, 1)$?

- $6$
- $4$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Differentiating with respect to $x$ with $y$ constant gives $f_x = 3x^2 y^2 - 2y$. The derivative $f_{xy}$ is obtained by differentiating $f_x$ with respect to $y$ with $x$ constant, so $f_{xy} = 6x^2 y - 2$. At the point $(1, 1)$ we have $f_{xy}(1, 1) = 6 - 2 = 4$. The computation in the other order gives $f_y = 2x^3 y - 2x$ and $f_{yx} = 6x^2 y - 2$, the same result, as **Schwarz's theorem** guarantees.
