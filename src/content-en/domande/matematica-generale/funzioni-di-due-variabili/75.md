Output is $Q(x,y)=3 x + 5 y$. By how much must $y$ change if $x$ rises by one unit while output remains constant?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The change in output is $3\,\Delta x+5\,\Delta y$. Setting the change in output to zero with $\Delta x=1$ determines the change in $y$.
