Which set must be excluded from the natural domain of $\dfrac{1}{4 x + 7 y - 9}$?

- Only the origin
- $4 x + 7 y = -9$
- $4 x + 7 y = 9$
- $4 x - 7 y = 9$

## Explanation

A rational function is defined where its denominator is nonzero. The zeros of this denominator form a line.
