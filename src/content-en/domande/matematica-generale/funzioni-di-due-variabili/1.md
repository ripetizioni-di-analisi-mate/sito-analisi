What is the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \sqrt{x - y^2}$?

- $D = \{ (x, y) : x > y^2 \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : x \leq y^2 \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : x \geq y^2 \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : y \geq x^2 \}$

## Explanation

The argument of a square root must be greater than or equal to zero, so the domain consists of the points with $x - y^2 \geq 0$. Moving $y^2$ to the right-hand side gives $x \geq y^2$. The inequality is non-strict, because the square root of $0$ exists; therefore the points of the parabola $x = y^2$ belong to the domain. The domain is therefore the region to the right of the parabola $x = y^2$, including the parabola.
