What is the natural domain of the function $f(x, y) = \dfrac{\sqrt{4 - x^2 - y^2}}{x - y}$?

- $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 < 4, \ y \neq x \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \geq 4, \ y \neq x \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 4, \ y > x \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 4, \ y \neq x \}$

## Explanation

The square root requires its argument to be greater than or equal to zero and the denominator must be nonzero, so the conditions to be satisfied simultaneously are
$$
\begin{cases}
4 - x^2 - y^2 \geq 0 \\[4pt]
x - y \neq 0
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 \leq 4 \\[4pt]
y \neq x
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition describes the disk centered at the origin with radius $2$, boundary circle included, because the inequality is non-strict. The second excludes only the points of the line $y = x$, and leaves in the domain the points lying both above and below the line. The domain is therefore the disk of radius $2$ with the diameter lying on the line $y = x$ removed.
