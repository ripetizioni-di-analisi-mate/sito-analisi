Fixing $x = 1$ in the function $f(x, y) = e^{xy^2}$ gives the one-variable function $g(y) = f(1, y)$. What is $g''(1)$, that is, $f_{yy}(1, 1)$?

- $2e$
- $4e$
- $6e$
- $8e$

## Explanation

Treating $x$ as a constant, the derivative of the exponent $xy^2$ with respect to $y$ is $2xy$, so
$$
f_y(x, y) = 2xy\,e^{xy^2} .
$$
Differentiating the product with respect to $y$ gives
$$
f_{yy}(x, y) = 2x\,e^{xy^2} + 2xy \cdot 2xy\,e^{xy^2} = (2x + 4x^2 y^2)\,e^{xy^2} .
$$
Therefore at the point $(1, 1)$ we have $f_{yy}(1, 1) = (2 + 4)\,e = 6e$.
