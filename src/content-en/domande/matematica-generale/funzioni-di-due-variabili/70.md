What is the constant term in $z=ux+vy+w$ for the tangent plane to the graph of $x^{2} + 3 x y + 5 y$ at the point above $(1,1)$?

- $-3$
- $-4$
- $9$
- $4$

## Explanation

The height of the plane is $9$ at $(1,1)$. Its slopes are $5$ and $8$, so the constant term is the height at that point minus both slopes.
