What kind of set is $A = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 4, \ y > 0 \}$?

- It is open and bounded
- It is closed and bounded
- It is bounded and neither open nor closed
- It is closed and unbounded

## Explanation

The set $A$ is the upper half of the disk centered at the origin with radius $2$. Since $A$ is contained in the disk of radius $2$, the set is **bounded**. The points of the arc $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ with $y > 0$ are boundary points and belong to $A$, so $A$ is not open. The points of the segment $y = 0$, $-2 \leq x \leq 2$ are boundary points as well, but they do not belong to $A$, because they do not satisfy $y > 0$; therefore $A$ is not closed.
