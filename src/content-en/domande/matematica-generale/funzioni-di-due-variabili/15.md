What is the equation of the tangent line at the point $(1, 1)$ to the level curve of $f(x, y) = x^2 + 2y^2$ through that point?

- $2x + y = 3$
- $2x - y = 1$
- $x + 2y = 3$
- $x - 2y = -1$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = 2x$ and $f_y = 4y$, so $\nabla f(1, 1) = (2, 4)$. The gradient is perpendicular to the level curve through the point, therefore the tangent line is the line through $(1, 1)$ perpendicular to the vector $(2, 4)$:
$$
2\,(x - 1) + 4\,(y - 1) = 0 .
$$
Dividing by $2$ gives $x - 1 + 2y - 2 = 0$, that is, $x + 2y = 3$. The line $2x - y = 1$ also passes through $(1, 1)$, but it has the direction of the gradient and is therefore perpendicular to the level curve.
