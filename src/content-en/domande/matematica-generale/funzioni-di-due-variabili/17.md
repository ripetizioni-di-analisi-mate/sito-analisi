What is the natural domain of the function $f(x, y) = \ln(2x - y + 4)$?

- $D = \{ (x, y) : y > 2x + 4 \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : y \leq 2x + 4 \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : y < -2x + 4 \}$
- $D = \{ (x, y) : y < 2x + 4 \}$

## Explanation

The argument of the logarithm must be strictly positive, so the domain consists of the points with $2x - y + 4 > 0$. Moving $y$ to the right-hand side gives $y < 2x + 4$. The domain is therefore the half-plane below the line $y = 2x + 4$, with the line excluded, because at its points the argument equals $0$. The origin belongs to the domain, as the substitution $2 \cdot 0 - 0 + 4 = 4 > 0$ confirms.
