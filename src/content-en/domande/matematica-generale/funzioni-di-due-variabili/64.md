What is the expression of the partial derivative $f_y(x, y)$ of the function $f(x, y) = x \sin(xy)$?

- $x \cos(xy)$
- $-x^2 \cos(xy)$
- $\sin(xy) + xy \cos(xy)$
- $x^2 \cos(xy)$

## Explanation

In computing $f_y$ the variable $x$ is a constant, so the factor $x$ stays in front. The derivative of $\sin(xy)$ with respect to $y$ is $\cos(xy)$ multiplied by the derivative of the inner function, which with respect to $y$ is $x$, by the chain rule. Therefore
$$
f_y(x, y) = x \cdot x \cos(xy) = x^2 \cos(xy) .
$$
The expression $\sin(xy) + xy \cos(xy)$ is instead the derivative with respect to $x$, obtained with the product rule.
