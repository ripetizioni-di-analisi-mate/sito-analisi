A consumer has utility function $U(x, y) = x y^2$, with $x > 0$ and $y > 0$. What is the marginal rate of substitution $\dfrac{U_x}{U_y}$ at the bundle $(4, 2)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $1$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The marginal utilities are $U_x = y^2$, because $y^2$ is constant with respect to $x$, and $U_y = 2xy$. At the bundle $(4, 2)$ they are $U_x = 4$ and $U_y = 2 \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = 16$. The **marginal rate of substitution** is therefore $\dfrac{U_x}{U_y} = \dfrac{4}{16} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. The number is positive because it is the slope of the indifference curve $x y^2 = 16$ with its sign changed, and this slope at $(4, 2)$ equals $-\dfrac{1}{4}$.
