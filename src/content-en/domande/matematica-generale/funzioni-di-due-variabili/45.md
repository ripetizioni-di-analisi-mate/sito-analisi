A production function $Q(K, L)$, defined for $K > 0$ and $L > 0$, is differentiable and homogeneous of degree $1$. At the point $K = 10$, $L = 20$ the marginal productivities are $Q_K = 3$ and $Q_L = 2$. What is $Q(10, 20)$?

- $80$
- $70$
- $5$
- $140$

## Explanation

The hypotheses of **Euler's theorem** are satisfied with degree $r = 1$, so at every point $K\,Q_K + L\,Q_L = Q$. At the point $(10, 20)$ we get
$$
Q(10, 20) = 10 \cdot 3 + 20 \cdot 2 = 30 + 40 = 70 .
$$
The payments to the two inputs according to their marginal productivities, $30$ and $40$, therefore exhaust the output exactly.
