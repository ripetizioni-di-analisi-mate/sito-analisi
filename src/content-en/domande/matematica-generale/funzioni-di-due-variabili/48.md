Let $A = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 < 100 \}$. What is the largest radius $r$ for which the open disk $B((3, 4), r)$ is contained in $A$?

- $10$
- $7$
- $15$
- $5$

## Explanation

The set $A$ is the open disk centered at the origin with radius $10$. The point $(3, 4)$ is at distance $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$ from the origin, so it is at distance $10 - 5 = 5$ from the boundary circle. An open disk centered at $(3, 4)$ with radius $r \leq 5$ is contained in $A$, because each of its points is at distance less than $5 + r \leq 10$ from the origin. For $r > 5$ the disk contains points of the ray from the origin through $(3, 4)$ at distance greater than $10$ from the origin. Therefore the largest radius is **$r = 5$**.
