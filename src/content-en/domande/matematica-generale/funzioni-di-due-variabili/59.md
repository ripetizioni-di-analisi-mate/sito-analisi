For utility $U(x,y)=2 \ln{\left(x \right)} + 3 \ln{\left(y \right)}$, what is the ratio $U_x/U_y$ at the bundle $(1,2)$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $- \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The marginal utilities are $\dfrac{2}x$ and $\dfrac{3}y$. At the stated bundle divide $2$ by $\dfrac{3}2$.
