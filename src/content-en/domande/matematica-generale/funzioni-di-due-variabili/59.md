What is the graph of the function $f(x, y) = \sqrt{9 - x^2 - y^2}$?

- The hemisphere centered at the origin with radius $3$ lying in the half-space $z \geq 0$
- The sphere centered at the origin with radius $3$
- A paraboloid with vertex at $(0, 0, 9)$
- A cone with vertex at $(0, 0, 3)$

## Explanation

The graph is the set of points $(x, y, z)$ with $z = \sqrt{9 - x^2 - y^2}$. Squaring gives $z^2 = 9 - x^2 - y^2$, that is, $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 9$, which is the equation of the sphere centered at the origin with radius $3$. The square root is greater than or equal to zero, so the points of the graph have $z \geq 0$. Therefore the graph is the **upper hemisphere** of radius $3$; the whole sphere is not the graph of a function, because above each point inside the disk it would have two heights.
