What is the limit of $\dfrac{x y}{x^{2} + 3 y^{2}}$ at the origin along the line $y=5x$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{5}{76}$

## Explanation

Substitute $y=5x$. Both numerator and denominator contain $x^2$, so cancelling it for $x\ne0$ leaves a constant.
