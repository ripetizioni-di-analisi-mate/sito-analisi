What is the limit of $\dfrac{x y}{x^{2} + 3 y^{2}}$ at the origin along the line $y=5x$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{5}{76}$

## Explanation

After substituting $y=5x$, the numerator and denominator both contain the factor $x^2$. Since $x\ne0$, that factor cancels and the ratio is constant.
