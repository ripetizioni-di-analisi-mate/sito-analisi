A firm has production function $Q(K, L) = K L^2$, with $K > 0$ and $L > 0$, and uses $K = 2$ and $L = 4$. If capital rises to $K = 8$, what amount of labor keeps output unchanged?

- $L = 2$
- $L = 1$
- $L = 8$
- $L = 2\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The initial output is $Q(2, 4) = 2 \cdot 16 = 32$. To keep it, the firm stays on the isoquant $K L^2 = 32$, so with $K = 8$ we need $8 L^2 = 32$. Hence $L^2 = 4$ and, since $L > 0$, $L = 2$. The value $L = 1$ would come from treating the isoquant as $K L = 8$, that is, from forgetting that labor appears squared.
