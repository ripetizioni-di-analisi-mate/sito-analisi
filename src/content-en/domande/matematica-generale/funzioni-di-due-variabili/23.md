For the production function $Q(K, L) = 2\,K^{\frac{1}{2}} L$, with $K > 0$ and $L > 0$, what is the equation of the isoquant through $(K, L) = (4, 3)$?

- $K = \dfrac{36}{L^2}$
- $K = \dfrac{36}{L}$
- $K = \dfrac{6}{L}$
- $K = \dfrac{144}{L^2}$

## Explanation

At the point $(4, 3)$ the output is $Q(4, 3) = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 = 12$, so the **isoquant** has equation $2\,K^{\frac{1}{2}} L = 12$. Dividing by $2L$ gives $K^{\frac{1}{2}} = \dfrac{6}{L}$. Squaring both sides, which are positive, gives $K = \dfrac{36}{L^2}$. For $L = 3$ we recover $K = 4$.
