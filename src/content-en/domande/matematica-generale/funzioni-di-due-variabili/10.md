## Explanation

The partial derivatives of $f$ are $f_x = y^2$ and $f_y = 2xy$, and the derivatives of the components of the curve are $x'(t) = 2t$ and $y'(t) = -1$. Since $f$ is a polynomial, hence differentiable, and the components of the curve are differentiable, by the formula for the **derivative along a curve** we have
$$
g'(t) = y(t)^2 \cdot 2t + 2\,x(t)\,y(t) \cdot (-1) = 2t\,(3 - t)^2 - 2t^2 (3 - t) .
$$
For $t = 1$ the point of the curve is $(1, 2)$, so $g'(1) = 2 \cdot 4 - 2 \cdot 2 = 8 - 4 = 4$.
