For $4 x^{2} + 7 y^{2}$, what is the differential at $(1,1)$ for increments $dx=\dfrac1{8}$ and $dy=-\dfrac1{28}$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

At the point the partial derivatives are $8$ and $14$. The differential is the sum of their products with the corresponding increments.
