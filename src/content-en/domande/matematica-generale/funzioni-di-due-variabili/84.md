The figure shows, in the $(L, K)$ plane, an isoquant of a production function $Q(K, L)$ and its tangent line at the point $(L, K) = (4, 4)$. What is the marginal rate of technical substitution $\dfrac{Q_L}{Q_K}$ at that point?

::grafico{f="16/x+0*sqrt(x);8-x" punti="4,4,pieno;8,0,pieno;0,8,pieno" xmin=-2 xmax=12 ymin=-2 ymax=12 nomi="L,K" legenda="K = \dfrac{16}{L};K = 8 - L" titolo="The isoquant $K = \dfrac{16}{L}$ and its tangent at the point $(L, K) = (4, 4)$, which passes through $(8, 0)$ and $(0, 8)$"}

- $-1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The marginal rate of technical substitution is the slope of the isoquant with the opposite sign, because along the isoquant $K'(L) = -\dfrac{Q_L}{Q_K}$. The tangent line passes through $(8, 0)$ and $(0, 8)$, so it has slope $\dfrac{8 - 0}{0 - 8} = -1$. The same value is obtained by differentiating $K = \dfrac{16}{L}$, which gives $K'(L) = -\dfrac{16}{L^2}$ and $K'(4) = -1$. Therefore the marginal rate of technical substitution equals **$1$**.
