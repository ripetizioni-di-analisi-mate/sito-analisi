Output is $Q(x,y)=4 x + 7 y$. By how much must $y$ change if $x$ rises by one unit while output remains constant?

- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{4}$
- $-4$
- $- \dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

The change in output is $4\,\Delta x+7\,\Delta y$. Set it equal to zero with $\Delta x=1$ and solve for the change in $y$.
