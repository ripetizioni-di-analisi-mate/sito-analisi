For the function $f(x, y) = x^2 - y^2$, what is the level curve $k = 0$?

- Only the line $y = x$
- The two lines $y = x$ and $y = -x$
- Only the point $(0, 0)$
- The empty set

## Explanation

The level curve $0$ consists of the points with $x^2 - y^2 = 0$. Since $x^2 - y^2 = (x - y)(x + y)$, the product is zero when $x - y = 0$ or $x + y = 0$. The level curve is therefore the union of the **two lines** $y = x$ and $y = -x$, which meet at the origin. For example, the point $(1, -1)$ belongs to it, because $1 - 1 = 0$.
