What is the limit of $\dfrac{4 x^{2} y}{x^{2} + y^{2}}$ as $(x,y)\to(0,0)$?

- $1$
- The limit does not exist
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The factor $\dfrac{x^2}{x^2+y^2}$ lies between zero and one. The absolute value of the ratio is therefore at most $4|y|$ and tends to zero.
