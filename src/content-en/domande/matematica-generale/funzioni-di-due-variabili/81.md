A firm has production function $Q(K, L) = 10\,K^{\frac{1}{3}} L^{\frac{2}{3}}$. Using the differential, by approximately what percentage does output change if capital increases by $3\%$ and labor decreases by $3\%$?

- $1\%$
- $-1\%$
- $0\%$
- $-3\%$

## Explanation

For the Cobb-Douglas function we have $Q_K = \dfrac{Q}{3K}$ and $Q_L = \dfrac{2Q}{3L}$. The differential divided by $Q$ is therefore
$$
\frac{dQ}{Q} = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{dK}{K} + \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{dL}{L} .
$$
With $\dfrac{dK}{K} = 0.03$ and $\dfrac{dL}{L} = -0.03$ we get $\dfrac{dQ}{Q} = 0.01 - 0.02 = -0.01$. So output decreases by approximately **$1\%$**, whatever the initial values of $K$ and $L$.
