The point $(a, 1)$, with $a > 0$, is at distance $5$ from the point $(1, -2)$. What is $a$?

- $4$
- $-3$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

By the distance formula,
$$
d\big((a, 1), (1, -2)\big) = \sqrt{(a - 1)^2 + (1 + 2)^2} = \sqrt{(a - 1)^2 + 9} .
$$
Setting the distance equal to $5$ and squaring gives $(a - 1)^2 + 9 = 25$, that is, $(a - 1)^2 = 16$. Therefore $a - 1 = 4$ or $a - 1 = -4$, that is, $a = 5$ or $a = -3$. Since $a > 0$, we get **$a = 5$**.
