Which condition describes the entire domain of $\sqrt{2 x - 3 y + 5}$?

- $2 x - 3 y + 5 > 0$
- $2 x - 3 y + 5 = 0$
- $2 x - 3 y + 5 \leq 0$
- $2 x - 3 y + 5 \geq 0$

## Explanation

A real square root requires a nonnegative radicand. Points on the line where the radicand vanishes are included.
