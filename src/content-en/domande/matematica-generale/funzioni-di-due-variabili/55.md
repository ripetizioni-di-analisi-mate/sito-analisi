What is the constant term in $z=ux+vy+w$ for the tangent plane to the graph of $x^{2} + 2 x y + 3 y$ at the point above $(1,1)$?

- $6$
- $2$
- $-2$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The height of the plane is $6$ at $(1,1)$. Its slopes are $4$ and $5$, so the constant term is the height at that point minus both slopes.
