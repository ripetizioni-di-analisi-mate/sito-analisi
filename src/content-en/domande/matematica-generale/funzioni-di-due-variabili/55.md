What is the area of the natural domain of the function $f(x, y) = \sqrt{9 - x^2 - y^2} + \sqrt{y}$?

- $\dfrac{9\pi}{2}$
- $9\pi$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The two square roots require their arguments to be greater than or equal to zero:
$$
\begin{cases}
9 - x^2 - y^2 \geq 0 \\[4pt]
y \geq 0
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 \leq 9 \\[4pt]
y \geq 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first condition gives the disk centered at the origin with radius $3$, the second gives the upper half-plane. So the domain is the upper half of the disk of radius $3$. The disk of radius $3$ has area $9\pi$, therefore the area of the domain is **$\dfrac{9\pi}{2}$**.
