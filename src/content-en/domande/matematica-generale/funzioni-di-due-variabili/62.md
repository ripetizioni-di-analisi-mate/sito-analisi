Which set must be excluded from the natural domain of $\dfrac{1}{3 x + 5 y - 7}$?

- $3 x + 5 y = 7$
- $3 x - 5 y = 7$
- Only the origin
- $3 x + 5 y = -7$

## Explanation

A rational function is defined where its denominator is nonzero. The zeros of this denominator form a line.
