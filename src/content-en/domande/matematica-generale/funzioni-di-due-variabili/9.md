- $Q(102, 396) \approx 2030$
- $Q(102, 396) \approx 1965$
- $Q(102, 396) \approx 2020$

## Explanation

At the starting point we have $Q(100, 400) = 10 \cdot 10 \cdot 20 = 2000$. The marginal products are
$$
Q_K = 5\,K^{-\frac{1}{2}} L^{\frac{1}{2}} = 5 \cdot \frac{20}{10} = 10,
\qquad
Q_L = 5\,K^{\frac{1}{2}} L^{-\frac{1}{2}} = 5 \cdot \frac{10}{20} = \frac{5}{2} .
$$
The increments are $dK = 2$ and $dL = -4$, because labor decreases, so the **differential** is $dQ = 10 \cdot 2 + \dfrac{5}{2} \cdot (-4) = 20 - 10 = 10$. Since $Q$ is differentiable at the points with $K > 0$ and $L > 0$, the linear approximation gives $Q(102, 396) \approx 2000 + 10 = 2010$.
