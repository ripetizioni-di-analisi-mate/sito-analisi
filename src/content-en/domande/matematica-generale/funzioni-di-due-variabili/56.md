For $2 x^{2} + 3 y^{2}$, what is the differential at $(1,1)$ for increments $dx=\dfrac1{4}$ and $dy=-\dfrac1{12}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

At the point the partial derivatives are $4$ and $6$. The differential is the sum of their products with the corresponding increments.
