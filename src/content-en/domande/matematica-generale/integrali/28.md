Let $I = \displaystyle\int_0^4 \sqrt{x^2 + 9}\,dx$. What bounds on $I$ follow by comparing the integrand with its minimum and maximum on $[0, 4]$?

- $3 \leq I \leq 5$
- $36 \leq I \leq 100$
- $6 \leq I \leq 10$
- $12 \leq I \leq 20$

## Explanation

For $x \geq 0$, $x^2 + 9$ is increasing, so $\sqrt{x^2 + 9}$ is also increasing on $[0, 4]$. Its minimum is $m = \sqrt{9} = 3$ at $x = 0$, and its maximum is $M = \sqrt{25} = 5$ at $x = 4$. By **comparison with constants**, $m\,(b - a) \leq I \leq M\,(b - a)$ with $b - a = 4$, so
$$
12 \leq I \leq 20 .
$$
