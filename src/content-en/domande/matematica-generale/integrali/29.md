What is the area of the region between the graph of $f(x) = x^2 - 4$ and the $x$-axis for $0 \leq x \leq 3$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{23}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

The function is negative for $0 \leq x < 2$ and positive for $2 < x \leq 3$, so we split the interval at $x = 2$ and change the sign of the integral of the negative part. We have
$$
\int_0^2 \left( 4 - x^2 \right) dx = \left[ 4x - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^2 = \frac{16}{3},
\qquad
\int_2^3 \left( x^2 - 4 \right) dx = \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} - 4x \right]_2^3 = -3 + \frac{16}{3} = \frac{7}{3} .
$$
Therefore the area is $\dfrac{16}{3} + \dfrac{7}{3} = \dfrac{23}{3}$, while the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^3 \left( x^2 - 4 \right) dx$ equals $\dfrac{7}{3} - \dfrac{16}{3} = -3$.
