The marginal cost of a firm is $C'(q) = 2q + 5$ and the fixed cost is $300$ euros. Which quantity is equal to $\displaystyle\int_{10}^{20} (2q + 5)\,dq$?

- $C(20) - C(10)$
- $C(20)$
- $C'(20) - C'(10)$
- $\dfrac{C(20) - C(10)}{10}$

## Explanation

The total cost $C$ is an antiderivative of the marginal cost $C'$, so by the fundamental formula of calculus $\displaystyle\int_{10}^{20} C'(q)\,dq = C(20) - C(10)$. This number is the **additional cost** of raising production from $10$ to $20$ units, and the fixed cost cancels in the difference. With $C(q) = 300 + 5q + q^2$ we have $C(20) - C(10) = 800 - 450 = 350$, while $C(20) = 800$, $C'(20) - C'(10) = 20$ and $\dfrac{C(20) - C(10)}{10} = 35$.
