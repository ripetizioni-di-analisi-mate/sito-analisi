What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{3}\left(\dfrac{2}{x + 3}\right)\,dx$?

- $\ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $1$
- $2 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $2 \ln{\left(6 \right)}$

## Explanation

An antiderivative is $2\ln(x+3)$. The difference of logarithms is the logarithm of the ratio of their arguments, which is $2$.
