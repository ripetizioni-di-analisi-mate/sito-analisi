The marginal cost of a firm is $C'(q) = 4q + 6$ and the fixed cost is $100$ euros. What is the total cost of $10$ units?

- $260$ euros
- $360$ euros
- $146$ euros
- $560$ euros

## Explanation

Since the total cost $C$ is an antiderivative of the marginal cost, we have $C(q) = C(0) + \displaystyle\int_0^{q} C'(x)\,dx$, where $C(0) = 100$ is the fixed cost. Therefore
$$
C(10) = 100 + \int_0^{10} (4q + 6)\,dq = 100 + \Big[ 2q^2 + 6q \Big]_0^{10} = 100 + 260 = 360 ,
$$
that is, the total cost is $360$ euros.
