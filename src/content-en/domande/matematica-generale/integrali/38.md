An investment generates a continuous cash flow of $1000$ euros per year for $10$ years, and each receipt is reinvested under continuous compounding at a force of interest $\delta = 0.05$. One euro received at time $t$ is therefore worth $e^{\delta\,(10 - t)}$ euros at the end of the tenth year, and the value of the cash flow at the end of the tenth year is
$$
M = \int_0^{10} 1000\,e^{0.05\,(10 - t)}\,dt .
$$
What is the value of $M$?

- $10\,000$ euros
- About $12\,974.43$ euros
- About $7869.39$ euros
- About $32\,974.43$ euros

## Explanation

Write $e^{0.05\,(10 - t)} = e^{0.5}\,e^{-0.05\,t}$, so
$$
M = 1000\,e^{0.5} \int_0^{10} e^{-0.05\,t}\,dt = 1000\,e^{0.5} \left[ -\frac{e^{-0.05\,t}}{0.05} \right]_0^{10} = 20\,000\,e^{0.5} \left( 1 - e^{-0.5} \right) .
$$
Therefore $M = 20\,000 \left( e^{0.5} - 1 \right) \approx 12\,974.43$ euros. This is more than the $10\,000$ euros received in total, because each receipt earns interest until the end of the tenth year. The number $20\,000 \left( 1 - e^{-0.5} \right) \approx 7869.39$ is instead the present value of the cash flow, and $M$ equals the present value multiplied by $e^{0.5}$.
