What is the area of the region bounded by the graphs of $y = x^3$ and $y = x$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The abscissas of the intersection points are the solutions of $x^3 = x$, that is, of $x\,(x - 1)(x + 1) = 0$, so $x = -1$, $x = 0$ and $x = 1$. On $[-1, 0]$ we have $x^3 \geq x$, while on $[0, 1]$ we have $x \geq x^3$; therefore we split the interval at the crossing point $x = 0$. Hence
$$
A = \int_{-1}^{0} \left( x^3 - x \right) dx + \int_0^1 \left( x - x^3 \right) dx = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
The integral of $x - x^3$ over the whole of $[-1, 1]$ equals $0$ instead, because the two parts have opposite signs.
