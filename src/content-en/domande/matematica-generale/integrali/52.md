Suppose $F$ is an antiderivative of $f$. A clock reads the signal time backwards according to $s=5-2t$. Which function of $t$ is an antiderivative of $f(5-2t)$?

- $-2F(5-2t)$
- $\dfrac12F(5-2t)$
- $F(5-2t)$
- $-\dfrac12F(5-2t)$

## Explanation

The change of variable gives $ds=-2\,dt$, hence $dt=-\dfrac12\,ds$. The coefficient accounts for both the reversal of direction and the clock’s different rate.
