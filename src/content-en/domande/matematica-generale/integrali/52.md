What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\left(\dfrac{2 x}{x^{2} + 3}\right)\,dx$?

- $- \ln{\left(\dfrac{4}{3} \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(4 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(\dfrac{4}{3} \right)}$
- $2 \ln{\left(\dfrac{4}{3} \right)}$

## Explanation

With $u=x^2+3$, $du=2x\,dx$. The new endpoints are $3$ and $4$.
