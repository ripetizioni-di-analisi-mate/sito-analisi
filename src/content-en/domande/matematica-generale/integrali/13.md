The inverse demand for a good is $p_d(q) = 52 - q^2$ and the inverse supply is $p_s(q) = 12 + 6q$, with $q \geq 0$. What is the consumer surplus?

- $\dfrac{128}{3}$ euros
- $32$ euros
- $48$ euros
- $\dfrac{560}{3}$ euros

## Explanation

At equilibrium $52 - q^2 = 12 + 6q$, that is, $q^2 + 6q - 40 = 0$, whose solutions are $q = 4$ and $q = -10$. The solution with $q \geq 0$ is $q^* = 4$, so the equilibrium price is $p^* = 12 + 24 = 36$ euros. The consumer surplus is the area between the demand curve and the line $p = 36$; therefore
$$
S_C = \int_0^4 \left( 52 - q^2 - 36 \right) dq = \left[ 16q - \frac{q^3}{3} \right]_0^4 = 64 - \frac{64}{3} = \frac{128}{3} ,
$$
in euros.
