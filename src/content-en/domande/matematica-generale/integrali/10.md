- $\dfrac{125}{6}$

## Explanation

The abscissas of the intersection points are the solutions of $x^2 = 6 - x$, that is, of $x^2 + x - 6 = 0$, so $x = -3$ and $x = 2$. At the intermediate point $x = 0$ the line equals $6$ and the parabola equals $0$; therefore between $-3$ and $2$ the line lies above the parabola. Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
A
&= \int_{-3}^{2} \left( 6 - x - x^2 \right) dx
= \left[ 6x - \frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_{-3}^{2} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{22}{3} - \left( -\frac{27}{2} \right)
= \frac{125}{6} .
\end{aligned}
$$
