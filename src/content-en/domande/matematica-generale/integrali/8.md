## Explanation

The average price is the average value of $p(t)$ over $[0, 4]$, given by
$$
\bar{p} = \frac{1}{4} \int_0^4 \left( 20 + 3\sqrt{t} \right) dt .
$$
We write $\sqrt{t} = t^{\frac{1}{2}}$, so an antiderivative of $3\sqrt{t}$ is $3 \cdot \dfrac{2}{3}\,t^{\frac{3}{2}} = 2\,t^{\frac{3}{2}}$. Therefore the integral equals $\Big[ 20t + 2\,t^{\frac{3}{2}} \Big]_0^4 = 80 + 16 = 96$, and the average price is $\dfrac{96}{4} = 24$ euros.
