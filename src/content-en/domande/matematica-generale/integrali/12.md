The marginal cost of a firm is $C'(q) = 3q^2 - 24q + 60$ and the fixed cost is $200$ euros. What is the additional cost of raising production from $4$ to $10$ units?

- $488$ euros
- $600$ euros
- $288$ euros
- $108$ euros

## Explanation

The additional cost is $C(10) - C(4)$, and by the fundamental formula of calculus it equals the integral of the marginal cost from $4$ to $10$. The fixed cost appears both in $C(10)$ and in $C(4)$, so it cancels in the difference. Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
C(10) - C(4)
&= \int_4^{10} \left( 3q^2 - 24q + 60 \right) dq
= \Big[ q^3 - 12q^2 + 60q \Big]_4^{10} \\[6pt]
&= 400 - 112
= 288 ,
\end{aligned}
$$
that is, the additional cost is $288$ euros.
