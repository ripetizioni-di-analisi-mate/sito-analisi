Let $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_0^x \left( 4 - t^2 \right) dt$. What is the relative maximum of $F$?

- $-\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}$
- $8$

## Explanation

The integrand $4 - t^2$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$, so by the **fundamental theorem of integral calculus** we have $F'(x) = 4 - x^2$. The derivative is positive for $-2 < x < 2$ and negative for $x < -2$ and for $x > 2$; therefore $F$ is increasing on $[-2, 2]$ and decreasing on $[2, +\infty)$. Hence $x = 2$ is a relative maximum point, and the relative maximum is
$$
F(2) = \int_0^2 \left( 4 - t^2 \right) dt = \left[ 4t - \frac{t^3}{3} \right]_0^2 = 8 - \frac{8}{3} = \frac{16}{3} .
$$
