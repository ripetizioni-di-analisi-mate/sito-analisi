What is the area between the graph of $x - 3$ and the $x$-axis for $0\leq x\leq 8$?

- $17$
- $32$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}$
- $8$

## Explanation

The function changes sign at $x=3$. Add the areas of the two triangles. The first has base and height $3$, and the second has base and height $5$.
