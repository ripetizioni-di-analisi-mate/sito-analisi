Inverse demand is $p=22-3q$. At market price $p=7$, what is consumer surplus?

- $\dfrac{75}{2}$
- $75$
- $35$
- $\dfrac{35}{2}$

## Explanation

Quantity purchased is $5$. Surplus is the area between inverse demand and the market price from $0$ to $5$, a triangle of height $15$.
