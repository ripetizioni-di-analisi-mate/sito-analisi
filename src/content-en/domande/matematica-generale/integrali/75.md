The inverse demand is $p=22-3q$. At the market price $p=7$, what is the consumer surplus?

- $\dfrac{75}{2}$
- $75$
- $35$
- $\dfrac{35}{2}$

## Explanation

The quantity purchased is $5$. The consumer surplus is the area between the demand curve and the market price from $0$ to $5$, a triangle of height $15$.
