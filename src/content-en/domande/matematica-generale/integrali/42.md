The inverse demand for a good is $p_d(q) = 36 - q^2$, with $q \geq 0$, and the good sells at the market price of $11$ euros. What is the consumer surplus?

- $\dfrac{125}{3}$ euros
- $\dfrac{250}{3}$ euros
- $\dfrac{125}{2}$ euros
- $125$ euros

## Explanation

At a price of $11$ euros, the quantity traded solves $36 - q^2 = 11$ with $q \geq 0$, so it is $q = 5$. The **consumer surplus** is the area between the demand curve and the line $p = 11$. Therefore
$$
S_C = \int_0^5 \left( 36 - q^2 - 11 \right) dq = \left[ 25q - \frac{q^3}{3} \right]_0^5 = 125 - \frac{125}{3} = \frac{250}{3} ,
$$
in euros.
