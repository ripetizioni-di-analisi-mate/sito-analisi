An investment produces, with no end, a continuous cash flow of intensity $f(t) = 1000\,e^{-0.02\,t}$ euros per year. With instantaneous intensity of interest $\delta = 0.03$, what is the present value of the flow?

- $100{,}000$ euros
- About $33{,}333.33$ euros
- $50{,}000$ euros
- $20{,}000$ euros

## Explanation

The present value is the improper integral of the flow multiplied by the discount factor $e^{-0.03\,t}$. Since $e^{-0.02\,t}\,e^{-0.03\,t} = e^{-0.05\,t}$, by the improper integral of the exponential with $k = 0.05 > 0$ we have
$$
V = \int_0^{+\infty} 1000\,e^{-0.05\,t}\,dt = \frac{1000}{0.05} = 20{,}000 .
$$
The present value is therefore $20{,}000$ euros.
