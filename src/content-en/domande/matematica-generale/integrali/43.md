An investment generates a perpetual continuous cash flow at a rate of $f(t) = 1000\,e^{-0{,}02\,t}$ euros per year. At a force of interest $\delta = 0{,}03$, what is the present value of the cash flow?

- $100\,000$ euros
- About $33\,333{,}33$ euros
- $50\,000$ euros
- $20\,000$ euros

## Explanation

The present value is the improper integral of the cash flow rate multiplied by the discount factor $e^{-0{,}03\,t}$. Since $e^{-0{,}02\,t}\,e^{-0{,}03\,t} = e^{-0{,}05\,t}$, the improper integral of the exponential with $k = 0{,}05 > 0$ gives
$$
V = \int_0^{+\infty} 1000\,e^{-0{,}05\,t}\,dt = \frac{1000}{0{,}05} = 20\,000 .
$$
The present value is therefore $20\,000$ euros.
