The figure shows the graph of a function $f$ continuous on $[0, 5]$, made of three segments: from $(0, 2)$ to $(2, 2)$, from $(2, 2)$ to $(4, -2)$ and from $(4, -2)$ to $(5, -2)$. What is $\displaystyle\int_0^5 f(x)\,dx$?

::grafico{f="min(2,max(-2,6-2*x))+0*sqrt(x*(5-x))" punti="0,2,pieno;5,-2,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-3 ymax=3 legenda="y = f(x)" titolo="The graph of $f$ on $[0, 5]$"}

- $8$
- $6$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The integral counts areas above the $x$-axis with a plus sign and areas below it with a minus sign. On $[0, 2]$ the region is a rectangle with base $2$ and height $2$, with area $4$ above the axis. On $[2, 4]$ the segment crosses the axis at $x = 3$, so there are two triangles with base $1$ and height $2$, one above and one below, each of area $1$, which cancel in the integral. On $[4, 5]$ there is a rectangle with base $1$ and height $2$ below the axis. Therefore
$$
\int_0^5 f(x)\,dx = 4 + 1 - 1 - 2 = 2 .
$$
The value $8$ is the total area of the region, which adds all the parts with a plus sign.
