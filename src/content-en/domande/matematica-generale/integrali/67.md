What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\left(\dfrac{3 x}{x^{2} + 5}\right)\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{3 \ln{\left(\dfrac{6}{5} \right)}}{2}$
- $- \dfrac{3 \ln{\left(\dfrac{6}{5} \right)}}{2}$
- $3 \ln{\left(\dfrac{6}{5} \right)}$
- $\dfrac{3 \ln{\left(6 \right)}}{2}$

## Explanation

With $u=x^2+5$, $du=2x\,dx$. The new endpoints are $5$ and $6$.
