A meter accumulates the signal $1+\sin t$ from an arbitrary initial reading. By how much does the reading change over a full interval of length $2\pi$, regardless of the start time?

- $0$
- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $2$

## Explanation

An antiderivative is $t-\cos t$. After $2\pi$ the cosine has the same value, while the term $t$ has increased by $2\pi$. The starting constant also cancels in the difference between readings.
