Which function is an antiderivative of $x e^{3 x}$?

- $\left(\dfrac{x}{3} - \dfrac{1}{9}\right) e^{3 x}$
- $\left(x - \dfrac{1}{9}\right) e^{3 x}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{1}{9}\right) e^{3 x}$
- $\dfrac{x e^{3 x}}{3}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating $x$ and integrating the exponential. Subtract the integral of $\dfrac{e^{3x}}{3}$.
