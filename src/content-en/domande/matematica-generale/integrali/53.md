What is the derivative of $G(x)=\displaystyle\int_{3}^{x^2} (t^{2} + 2)\,dt$?

- $x \left(2 x^{2} + 4\right)$
- $x \left(- 2 x^{4} - 4\right)$
- $x^{4} + 2$
- $x \left(2 x^{4} + 4\right)$

## Explanation

The fundamental theorem of calculus evaluates the integrand at $t=x^2$. Then multiply by the derivative of $x^2$.
