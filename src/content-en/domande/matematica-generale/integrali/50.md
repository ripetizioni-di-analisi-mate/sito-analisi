For $x>0$ write $\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 2\right) \left(x + 3\right)}$ as $\dfrac A{x+2}+\dfrac B{x+3}$. What is $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Multiply by the common denominator to get a polynomial identity. In that identity put $x=-2$ to isolate $A(3-2)=1$.
