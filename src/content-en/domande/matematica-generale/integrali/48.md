Which function is an antiderivative of $x e^{2 x}$?

- $\left(x - \dfrac{1}{4}\right) e^{2 x}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{1}{4}\right) e^{2 x}$
- $\dfrac{x e^{2 x}}{2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4}\right) e^{2 x}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating $x$ and integrating the exponential. Subtract the integral of $\dfrac{e^{2x}}{2}$.
