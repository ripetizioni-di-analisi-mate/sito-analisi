Let $I = \displaystyle\int_0^1 e^{x^2}\,dx$. Which inequality is true?

- $I \leq e - 1$
- $I \geq e - 1$
- $I \leq 1$
- $I \geq e$

## Explanation

For $0 \leq x \leq 1$ we have $x^2 \leq x$, and since the exponential function is increasing it follows that $e^{x^2} \leq e^{x}$ on $[0, 1]$. By the **monotonicity** of the definite integral
$$
I \leq \int_0^1 e^{x}\,dx = e - 1 .
$$
Moreover $e^{x^2} \geq 1$ on $[0, 1]$, with equality only at $x = 0$, so $I > 1$. Finally, $e^{x^2} \leq e$ gives $I \leq e$, and the numerical value is $I \approx 1.46$.
