An investment generates a continuous cash flow of $5000$ euros per year for $4$ years. At a force of interest $\delta = 0{,}05$, what is its present value?

- $20\,000$ euros
- About $18\,126{,}92$ euros
- About $81\,873{,}08$ euros
- About $906{,}35$ euros

## Explanation

To find the present value of a constant continuous cash flow, integrate the annual cash flow multiplied by the discount factor $e^{-0{,}05\,t}$ from $0$ to $4$. An antiderivative of $e^{-0{,}05\,t}$ is $-\dfrac{e^{-0{,}05\,t}}{0{,}05}$, so
$$
V = 5000 \int_0^4 e^{-0{,}05\,t}\,dt = \frac{5000}{0{,}05} \left( 1 - e^{-0{,}2} \right) = 100\,000 \left( 1 - e^{-0{,}2} \right) .
$$
Since $e^{-0{,}2} \approx 0{,}8187308$, the present value is about $18\,126{,}92$ euros, less than the $20\,000$ euros received in total, because the future receipts are discounted.
