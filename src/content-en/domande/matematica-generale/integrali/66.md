What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{5}\left(\dfrac{3}{x + 5}\right)\,dx$?

- $3 \ln{\left(10 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(2 \right)}$

## Explanation

An antiderivative is $3\ln(x+5)$. The difference of logarithms is the logarithm of the ratio of their arguments, which is $2$.
