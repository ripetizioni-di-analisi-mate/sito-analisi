For $x>0$ write $\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 3\right) \left(x + 5\right)}$ as $\dfrac A{x+3}+\dfrac B{x+5}$. What is $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the common denominator gives a polynomial identity. Substituting $x=-3$ into that identity gives $A(5-3)=1$.
