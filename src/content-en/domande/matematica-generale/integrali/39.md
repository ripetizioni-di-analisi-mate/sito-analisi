The marginal revenue of a firm is $R'(q) = 200 - 4q$, with $R(0) = 0$. What is the maximum revenue?

- $10{,}000$ euros
- $7500$ euros
- $3750$ euros
- $5000$ euros

## Explanation

The revenue is the antiderivative of $R'$ that equals $0$ at $q = 0$, so
$$
R(q) = \int_0^{q} (200 - 4x)\,dx = 200q - 2q^2 .
$$
The derivative $R'(q) = 200 - 4q$ is positive for $q < 50$ and negative for $q > 50$; therefore the revenue is maximum at $q = 50$. The maximum revenue is $R(50) = 10{,}000 - 5000 = 5000$ euros.
