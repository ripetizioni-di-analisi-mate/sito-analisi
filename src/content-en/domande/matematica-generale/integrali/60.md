The inverse demand is $p=11-2q$. At the market price $p=5$, what is the consumer surplus?

- $15$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $18$
- $9$

## Explanation

The quantity purchased is $3$. The consumer surplus is the area between the demand curve and the market price from $0$ to $3$, a triangle of height $6$.
