What is the derivative of $G(x)=\displaystyle\int_{7}^{x^2} (t^{2} + 4)\,dt$?

- $x \left(- 2 x^{4} - 8\right)$
- $x \left(2 x^{4} + 8\right)$
- $x^{4} + 4$
- $x \left(2 x^{2} + 8\right)$

## Explanation

By the fundamental theorem of calculus, evaluate the integrand at $t=x^2$. Then multiply by the derivative of $x^2$.
