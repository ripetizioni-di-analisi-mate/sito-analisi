The marginal cost is $C\prime(q)=3 q + 5$. How much does total cost increase as output rises from $q=5$ to $q=12$?

- $\dfrac{427}{2}$
- $287$
- $140$
- $276$

## Explanation

Integrate the marginal cost from $5$ to $12$. The fixed cost cancels in the difference between the total costs.
