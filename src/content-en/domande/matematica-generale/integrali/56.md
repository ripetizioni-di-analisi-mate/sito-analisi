Compute $\displaystyle\int x^2 e^{x}\,dx$.

- $\left( x^2 - 2x \right) e^{x} + c$
- $\left( x^2 + 2x + 2 \right) e^{x} + c$
- $\left( x^2 - 2x - 2 \right) e^{x} + c$
- $\left( x^2 - 2x + 2 \right) e^{x} + c$

## Explanation

We apply **integration by parts** with $x^2$ as the factor to differentiate and $e^{x}$ as the factor to integrate, because the derivative of $x^2$ has lower degree:
$$
\int x^2 e^{x}\,dx = x^2 e^{x} - \int 2x\,e^{x}\,dx .
$$
The remaining integral is again computed by parts, with $2x$ as the factor to differentiate:
$$
\int 2x\,e^{x}\,dx = 2x\,e^{x} - \int 2\,e^{x}\,dx = 2x\,e^{x} - 2\,e^{x} + c .
$$
Substituting and changing the sign of both terms gives $x^2 e^{x} - 2x\,e^{x} + 2\,e^{x} + c = \left( x^2 - 2x + 2 \right) e^{x} + c$.
