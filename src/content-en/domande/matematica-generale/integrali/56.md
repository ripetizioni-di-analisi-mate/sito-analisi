What is the area between the graph of $x - 2$ and the $x$-axis for $0\leq x\leq 5$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}$

## Explanation

The function changes sign at $x=2$. Add the areas of the two triangles. The first has base and height $2$, and the second has base and height $3$.
