Which function is an antiderivative on $x>0$ of $\ln{\left(2 x \right)}$?

- $x \ln{\left(2 x \right)}$
- $\dfrac{\ln{\left(2 x \right)}}{x}$
- $x \ln{\left(2 x \right)} - x$
- $x \ln{\left(2 x \right)} + x$

## Explanation

Use integration by parts, differentiating the logarithm and integrating $1$. The product left in the integral is $x\cdot\dfrac1x=1$.
