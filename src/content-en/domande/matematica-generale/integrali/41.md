The inverse demand for a good is $p_d(q) = 120 - 3q$ and the inverse supply is $p_s(q) = 20 + 2q$. What is the producer surplus?

- $400$ euros
- $600$ euros
- $800$ euros
- $1000$ euros

## Explanation

At equilibrium $120 - 3q = 20 + 2q$, so $q^* = 20$ and the equilibrium price is $p^* = 20 + 40 = 60$ euros. The **producer surplus** is the area between the line $p = 60$ and the supply curve; therefore
$$
S_P = \int_0^{20} \big( 60 - (20 + 2q) \big)\,dq = \Big[ 40q - q^2 \Big]_0^{20} = 800 - 400 = 400 ,
$$
in euros.
