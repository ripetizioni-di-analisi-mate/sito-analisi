Willingness to pay minus marginal cost is $8-2q$ euros per unit. A constraint limits output to $3$ units although unconstrained output would be $4$. How much surplus is lost when output falls from $4$ to $3$?

- €1
- €2
- €3
- €9

## Explanation

Only the net benefit of units between $3$ and $4$ is lost. The gap falls linearly from $2$ to $0$, so the triangular area is $\dfrac{1\cdot2}{2}=1$ euro.
