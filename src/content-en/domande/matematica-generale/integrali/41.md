The inverse demand for a good is $p_d(q) = 14 - q$ and the marginal cost of production is $C'(q) = 6 + q$, in euros. Without constraints $4$ units are produced, the quantity at which $p_d(q) = C'(q)$. A capacity limit reduces production to $3$ units. By how much does the total surplus decrease, that is the area between the demand curve and the marginal cost curve?

- $1$ euro
- $2$ euros
- $10.5$ euros
- $15$ euros

## Explanation

With a production of $Q$ units the total surplus is $\displaystyle\int_0^{Q} \big( p_d(q) - C'(q) \big)\,dq$, and the integrand is $p_d(q) - C'(q) = 8 - 2q$. Going from $4$ to $3$ units one therefore loses the integral between $3$ and $4$, that is
$$
\int_3^4 (8 - 2q)\,dq = \Big[ 8q - q^2 \Big]_3^4 = 16 - 15 = 1 ,
$$
in euros. The result is the area of the triangle with base $1$ and height $8 - 2 \cdot 3 = 2$. The value $2$ euros comes from forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the area of the triangle, $15$ euros is the total surplus that remains with $3$ units, and $10.5$ euros is the area under the demand curve alone between $3$ and $4$.
