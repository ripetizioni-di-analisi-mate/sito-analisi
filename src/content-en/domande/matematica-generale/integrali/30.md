For $f(x) = 3x^2$ on $[0, 2]$, what point $c$ satisfies the mean value theorem for integrals?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{6}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The average value of $f$ on $[0, 2]$ is
$$
\frac{1}{2} \int_0^2 3x^2\,dx = \frac{1}{2} \Big[ x^3 \Big]_0^2 = 4 .
$$
The point $c$ satisfies $f(c) = 4$, or $3c^2 = 4$, so $c^2 = \dfrac{4}{3}$. Since $c$ lies in $[0, 2]$, we have $c = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
