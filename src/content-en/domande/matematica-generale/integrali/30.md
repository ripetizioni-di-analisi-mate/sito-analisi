For $f(x) = 3x^2$ on the interval $[0, 2]$, what is the point $c$ of the integral mean value theorem?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{6}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The integral mean of $f$ on $[0, 2]$ is
$$
\frac{1}{2} \int_0^2 3x^2\,dx = \frac{1}{2} \Big[ x^3 \Big]_0^2 = 4 .
$$
The point $c$ satisfies $f(c) = 4$, that is, $3c^2 = 4$, so $c^2 = \dfrac{4}{3}$. Since $c$ belongs to $[0, 2]$, we have $c = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
