The inverse demand is $p=37-4q$. At the market price $p=9$, what is the consumer surplus?

- $196$
- $63$
- $\dfrac{63}{2}$
- $98$

## Explanation

The quantity purchased is $7$. The consumer surplus is the area between the demand curve and the market price from $0$ to $7$, a triangle of height $28$.
