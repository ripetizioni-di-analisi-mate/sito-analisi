For $x>0$ write $\dfrac{1}{\left(x + 4\right) \left(x + 7\right)}$ as $\dfrac A{x+4}+\dfrac B{x+7}$. What is $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Multiply by the common denominator to get a polynomial identity. In that identity put $x=-4$ to isolate $A(7-4)=1$.
