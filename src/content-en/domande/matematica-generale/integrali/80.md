A program cancels $x-1$ in $\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}$ and writes the antiderivative $\dfrac{x^2}{2}+x+C$. What choice is available when describing all antiderivatives on the original domain?

- A constant fixed by the value of the integrand at $1$.
- Only the zero constant.
- A different constant for each integer.
- Two independent constants, one on either side of $1$.

## Explanation

The original domain consists of two separate intervals. On each the integrand equals $x+1$ and the integration constant is arbitrary. The excluded point supplies no condition linking the two constants.
