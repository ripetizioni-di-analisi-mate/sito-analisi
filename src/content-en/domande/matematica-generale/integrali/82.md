What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\left(\dfrac{4 x}{x^{2} + 7}\right)\,dx$?

- $- 2 \ln{\left(\dfrac{8}{7} \right)}$
- $2 \ln{\left(\dfrac{8}{7} \right)}$
- $4 \ln{\left(\dfrac{8}{7} \right)}$
- $2 \ln{\left(8 \right)}$

## Explanation

With $u=x^2+7$, $du=2x\,dx$. The new endpoints are $7$ and $8$.
