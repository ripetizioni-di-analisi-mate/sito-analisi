What is the area between the graph of $x - 4$ and the x-axis for $0\leq x\leq 11$?

- $\dfrac{49}{2}$
- $\dfrac{33}{2}$
- $\dfrac{65}{2}$
- $\dfrac{121}{2}$

## Explanation

The graph changes sign at $x=4$. Add the areas of the two triangles, whose bases and heights are respectively $4$ and $7$.
