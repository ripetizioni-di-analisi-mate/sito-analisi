What is the area between the graph of $x - 4$ and the $x$-axis for $0\leq x\leq 11$?

- $\dfrac{49}{2}$
- $\dfrac{33}{2}$
- $\dfrac{65}{2}$
- $\dfrac{121}{2}$

## Explanation

The function changes sign at $x=4$. Add the areas of the two triangles. The first has base and height $4$, and the second has base and height $7$.
