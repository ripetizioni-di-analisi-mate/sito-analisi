What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{7}\left(\dfrac{4}{x + 7}\right)\,dx$?

- $4 \ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $\ln{\left(2 \right)}$
- $4 \ln{\left(14 \right)}$
- $2$

## Explanation

An antiderivative is $4\ln(x+7)$. The difference of logarithms is the logarithm of the ratio of their arguments, which is $2$.
