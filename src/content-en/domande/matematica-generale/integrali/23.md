Compute $\displaystyle\int_0^1 x\,e^{x}\,dx$.

- $-1$
- $1$
- $e - 1$
- $2e - 1$

## Explanation

Apply **integration by parts**, differentiating $x$ and integrating $e^{x}$. This gives
$$
\int x\,e^{x}\,dx = x\,e^{x} - \int e^{x}\,dx = (x - 1)\,e^{x} + c .
$$
By the definite integral evaluation formula the integral equals $\Big[ (x - 1)\,e^{x} \Big]_0^1 = 0 - (-1) = 1$.
