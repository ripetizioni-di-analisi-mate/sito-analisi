What is the area of the region bounded by the graphs of $y = e^{x}$ and $y = e^{-x}$ and by the line $x = 1$?

- $e - \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $e + \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $e - 1$
- $e + \dfrac{1}{e} - 2$

## Explanation

The two graphs intersect where $e^{x} = e^{-x}$, that is, at $x = 0$. For $0 \leq x \leq 1$ we have $x \geq -x$, so $e^{x} \geq e^{-x}$, and the region lies between $x = 0$ and $x = 1$. An antiderivative of $e^{-x}$ is $-e^{-x}$; therefore
$$
A = \int_0^1 \left( e^{x} - e^{-x} \right) dx = \Big[ e^{x} + e^{-x} \Big]_0^1 = e + \frac{1}{e} - 2 .
$$
