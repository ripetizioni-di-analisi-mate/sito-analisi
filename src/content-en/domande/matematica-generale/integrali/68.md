Let $f$ be a continuous odd function on $\mathbb{R}$, with $\displaystyle\int_0^3 f(x)\,dx = 5$. What is $\displaystyle\int_{-3}^{3} \big( f(x) + 2 \big)\,dx$?

- $22$
- $2$
- $17$
- $12$

## Explanation

Since $f$ is odd, its graph is symmetric with respect to the origin, so the region on $[-3, 0]$ is the mirror image of the one on $[0, 3]$ with the opposite sign, and $\displaystyle\int_{-3}^{0} f(x)\,dx = -5$. By **additivity with respect to the interval** we therefore have $\displaystyle\int_{-3}^{3} f(x)\,dx = -5 + 5 = 0$. By **linearity**
$$
\int_{-3}^{3} \big( f(x) + 2 \big)\,dx = 0 + 2 \cdot 6 = 12 .
$$
The value $22$ would result if $f$ were even, because in that case the two integrals over $[-3, 0]$ and over $[0, 3]$ would be equal.
