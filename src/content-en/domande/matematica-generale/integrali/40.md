The marginal cost of a firm is $C'(q) = 10 + 0{,}3\,q^2$ euros. What is the average cost per additional unit when production rises from $10$ to $20$ units?

- $85$ euros
- $130$ euros
- $80$ euros
- $800$ euros

## Explanation

The average cost of the additional units is the average value of the marginal cost over the interval $[10, 20]$, that is, $\dfrac{C(20) - C(10)}{20 - 10}$. Since the total cost $C$ is an antiderivative of $C'$, by the definite integral evaluation formula
$$
C(20) - C(10) = \int_{10}^{20} \left( 10 + 0{,}3\,q^2 \right) dq = \Big[ 10q + 0{,}1\,q^3 \Big]_{10}^{20} = 1000 - 200 = 800 .
$$
Dividing the extra cost among the $10$ additional units gives an average of $\dfrac{800}{10} = 80$ euros.
