The price of a good in its first $10$ months on sale is $p(t) = 10 + 0.3\,t^2$ euros, with $t$ in months. What is the average price over the interval $[0, 10]$?

- $25$ euros
- $40$ euros
- $20$ euros
- $200$ euros

## Explanation

The average price is the integral mean of $p(t)$ over the interval $[0, 10]$, that is,
$$
\bar{p} = \frac{1}{10} \int_0^{10} \left( 10 + 0.3\,t^2 \right) dt = \frac{1}{10} \Big[ 10t + 0.1\,t^3 \Big]_0^{10} .
$$
The integral equals $100 + 100 = 200$, so the average price is $\dfrac{200}{10} = 20$ euros.
