What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} \sqrt{4 - x^2}\,dx$?

- $4\pi$
- $\pi$
- $8$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

For $-2 \leq x \leq 2$ the equation $y = \sqrt{4 - x^2}$ is equivalent to $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ with $y \geq 0$, so the graph of the integrand is the upper semicircle with center $(0, 0)$ and radius $2$. Since the integrand is nonnegative, the integral is the area of the region between the graph and the $x$-axis, that is, the area of the half disk of radius $2$. Therefore
$$
\int_{-2}^{2} \sqrt{4 - x^2}\,dx = \frac{\pi \cdot 2^2}{2} = 2\pi .
$$
