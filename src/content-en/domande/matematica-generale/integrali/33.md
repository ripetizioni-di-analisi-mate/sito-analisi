Let $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_2^x \dfrac{t - 3}{t^2 + 1}\,dt$. Which statement is true?

- $x = 2$ is a relative minimum point of $F$
- $x = 3$ is a relative maximum point of $F$
- $x = 3$ is a relative minimum point of $F$
- $F$ has no relative extremum points

## Explanation

The denominator $t^2 + 1$ never vanishes, so the integrand is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$. By the **fundamental theorem of integral calculus** we therefore have $F'(x) = \dfrac{x - 3}{x^2 + 1}$. Since the denominator is positive, $F'(x) < 0$ for $x < 3$ and $F'(x) > 0$ for $x > 3$. Hence $F$ is decreasing on $(-\infty, 3]$ and increasing on $[3, +\infty)$, and $x = 3$ is a **relative minimum point**. The point $x = 2$, where $F$ equals $0$, lies in the interval on which $F$ is decreasing.
