Which function is an antiderivative of $x e^{4 x}$?

- $\left(\dfrac{x}{4} - \dfrac{1}{16}\right) e^{4 x}$
- $\left(\dfrac{x}{4} + \dfrac{1}{16}\right) e^{4 x}$
- $\dfrac{x e^{4 x}}{4}$
- $\left(x - \dfrac{1}{16}\right) e^{4 x}$

## Explanation

Integrate by parts, differentiating $x$ and integrating the exponential. Subtract the integral of $\dfrac{e^{4x}}{4}$.
