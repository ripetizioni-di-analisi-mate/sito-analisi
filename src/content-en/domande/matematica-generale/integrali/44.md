Let $f$ be the function defined on $[0, 2]$ by
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
x^2 & \text{if } 0 \leq x \leq 1 \\[4pt]
2x - 1 & \text{if } 1 < x \leq 2
\end{cases}
$$
What is the value of $\displaystyle\int_0^{2} f(x)\,dx$?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$

## Explanation

Both expressions equal $1$ at $x = 1$, so $f$ is continuous on $[0, 2]$. By **additivity with respect to the interval** the integral splits at $x = 1$, and on each part we integrate the corresponding expression:
$$
\int_0^{2} f(x)\,dx = \int_0^{1} x^2\,dx + \int_1^{2} (2x - 1)\,dx = \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^{1} + \Big[ x^2 - x \Big]_1^{2} = \frac{1}{3} + 2 = \frac{7}{3} .
$$
