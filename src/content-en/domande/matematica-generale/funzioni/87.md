Cost is $C(q)=4 q + 7$ and the unit price is $13$ euros. For which real quantities $q\geq0$ is profit strictly positive?

- $\left(\dfrac{7}{9}, \infty\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{7}{9}, \infty\right)$
- $\left[0, \dfrac{7}{9}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{7}{13}, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Profit is revenue minus cost, so it is $9q-7$. Require this difference to be strictly positive.
