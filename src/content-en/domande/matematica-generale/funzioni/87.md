In which of the following markets is there no equilibrium with positive price and positive quantity traded?

- $q_d(p) = 40 - 2p$, $\quad q_s(p) = p - 5$
- $q_d(p) = 20 - p$, $\quad q_s(p) = 2p - 50$
- $q_d(p) = 60 - 3p$, $\quad q_s(p) = 2p$
- $q_d(p) = 30 - p$, $\quad q_s(p) = 3p - 10$

## Explanation

In the market with $q_d(p) = 20 - p$ and $q_s(p) = 2p - 50$ demand is positive only for $p < 20$, that is, the consumers' maximum price is $20$ euros, while supply is positive only for $p > 25$. Therefore there is no price at which demand and supply are both positive, and indeed the equation $20 - p = 2p - 50$ gives $p = \dfrac{70}{3}$, which corresponds to the negative quantity $q_d = -\dfrac{10}{3}$. In the other markets the equilibrium has positive price and quantity. The market with $q_s(p) = p - 5$ has $p^* = 15$ and $q^* = 10$, the one with $q_s(p) = 2p$ has $p^* = 12$ and $q^* = 24$, the one with $q_s(p) = 3p - 10$ has $p^* = 10$ and $q^* = 20$.
