The linear function $f(x) = m x + 4$ satisfies $f(3) = -2$. Which statement is true?

- $f$ is increasing and vanishes at $x = 2$
- $f$ is decreasing and vanishes at $x = 2$
- $f$ is decreasing and vanishes at $x = -2$
- $f$ is decreasing and vanishes at $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The condition $f(3) = -2$ becomes $3m + 4 = -2$, so $m = -2$. Since the slope is negative, the function $f(x) = -2x + 4$ is decreasing. The equation $-2x + 4 = 0$ gives $x = 2$, therefore $f$ vanishes at $x = 2$.
