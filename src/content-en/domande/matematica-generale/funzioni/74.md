Inverse demand is $p=105-3q$ for $0\leq q\leq 35$. What quantity maximizes revenue?

- $\dfrac{35}{2}$
- $70$
- $\dfrac{35}{4}$
- $35$

## Explanation

Revenue is $q(105-3q)$. This is a downward-opening parabola with zeros at $0$ and $35$, so the vertex lies halfway between them.
