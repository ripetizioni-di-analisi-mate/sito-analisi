What are the solutions of the equation $4^{x} - 3 \cdot 2^{x} - 4 = 0$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -1$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

Since $4^{x} = (2^{2})^{x} = (2^{x})^{2}$, setting $t = 2^{x}$ turns the equation into $t^2 - 3t - 4 = 0$. We have $t^2 - 3t - 4 = (t - 4)(t + 1)$, so $t = 4$ or $t = -1$. An exponential is always positive, therefore $2^{x} = -1$ has no solutions. The equation $2^{x} = 4 = 2^{2}$ gives $x = 2$. Hence the only solution is $x = 2$, while $4$ is the value of $t$.
