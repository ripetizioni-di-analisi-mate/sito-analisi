What is the natural domain of $f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x + 3}}{x - 2}$?

- $[-3, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$
- $(-3, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$
- $[-3, +\infty)$
- $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{2\}$

## Explanation

The root requires $x + 3 \geq 0$ and the denominator requires $x - 2 \neq 0$. The two conditions must hold together:
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 3 \geq 0 \\[4pt]
x - 2 \neq 0
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x \geq -3 \\[4pt]
x \neq 2
\end{cases}
$$
The endpoint $-3$ belongs to the domain, because $f(-3) = \dfrac{0}{-5} = 0$. Hence the domain is $[-3, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$.
