At which point do the lines $y = 3x - 2$ and $y = -x + 6$ intersect?

- $(2, 4)$
- $(4, 2)$
- $(4, 10)$
- $(1, 1)$

## Explanation

At the intersection point the two lines have the same ordinate, so $3x - 2 = -x + 6$. Moving the terms with $x$ to the left we get $4x = 8$, therefore $x = 2$. The ordinate is $y = 3 \cdot 2 - 2 = 4$, and indeed also $-2 + 6 = 4$. Hence the lines intersect at the point $(2, 4)$.
