What is the exact range of $5 - \left(x - 3\right)^{2}$ over $\mathbb R$?

- $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right)$
- $\left[5, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The square is nonnegative and vanishes at $3$. The function therefore reaches $5$ and takes every smaller value.
