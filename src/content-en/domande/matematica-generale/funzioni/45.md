A firm sells at the constant price of $30$ euros, with total cost $C(q) = 2400 + 18q$. Which statement is true?

- With $150$ units the firm makes a profit
- The break-even point is $q_0 = 80$
- The average cost for $q = 100$ is $18$ euros
- With $250$ units the profit is $600$ euros

## Explanation

The profit is $\pi(q) = 30q - 2400 - 18q = 12q - 2400$, so $\pi(250) = 3000 - 2400 = 600$ euros. The other statements are false. We have $\pi(150) = 1800 - 2400 = -600$, therefore with $150$ units the firm makes a loss. The **break-even point** is $q_0 = \dfrac{2400}{30 - 18} = 200$, and the average cost for $q = 100$ is $\dfrac{2400}{100} + 18 = 42$ euros.
