Which of the following pairs can represent the demand function $q_d(p)$ and the supply function $q_s(p)$ of the same good?

- $q_d(p) = 2p + 10$ and $q_s(p) = 50 - p$
- $q_d(p) = 40 - 3p$ and $q_s(p) = 20 - p$
- $q_d(p) = 60 - 2p$ and $q_s(p) = 3p - 15$
- $q_d(p) = 5p$ and $q_s(p) = 4p + 2$

## Explanation

As the price increases consumers buy less and producers offer more for sale, so demand is decreasing and supply is increasing. For linear functions this means that demand has a negative slope and supply has a positive slope. In the pair $q_d(p) = 60 - 2p$ and $q_s(p) = 3p - 15$ the slopes are $-2$ and $3$, therefore it is the only suitable pair. In the pair with $q_d(p) = 2p + 10$ demand is increasing, in the pair with $q_s(p) = 20 - p$ supply is decreasing, and in the pair with $q_d(p) = 5p$ demand is increasing.
