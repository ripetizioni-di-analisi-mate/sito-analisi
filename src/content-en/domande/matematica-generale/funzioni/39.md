The inverse demand and supply functions for a good are $p = 120 - 3q$ and $p = 20 + 2q$, with $q \geq 0$. What are the equilibrium quantity and price?

- $q^* = 60$, $p^* = 20$ euros
- $q^* = 20$, $p^* = 80$ euros
- $q^* = 20$, $p^* = 60$ euros
- $q^* = 25$, $p^* = 45$ euros

## Explanation

At **market equilibrium** the price is the same on the two curves, so $120 - 3q = 20 + 2q$. Moving the terms with $q$ to the right gives $100 = 5q$. Therefore $q^* = 20$. The equilibrium price is $p^* = 120 - 3 \cdot 20 = 60$ euros. The supply curve gives the same result because $20 + 2 \cdot 20 = 60$.
