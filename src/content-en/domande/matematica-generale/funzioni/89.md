An order for $20$ units brings in €500 of additional revenue. Variable cost is €18 per unit and the order alone requires a €90 inspection. The monthly rent is already committed. By how much does profit change if the order is accepted?

- +€140
- −€50
- +€410
- +€50

## Explanation

Accepting the order adds $20\cdot18+90=450$ euros to costs. Rent is the same under both choices. The difference in profit is therefore $500-450=50$ euros.
