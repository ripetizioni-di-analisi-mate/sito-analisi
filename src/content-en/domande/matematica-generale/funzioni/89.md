Inverse demand is $p=252-4q$ for $0\leq q\leq 63$. What quantity maximizes revenue?

- $63$
- $\dfrac{63}{4}$
- $\dfrac{315}{2}$
- $\dfrac{63}{2}$

## Explanation

Revenue is $q(252-4q)$. This is a downward-opening parabola with zeros at $0$ and $63$, so the vertex lies halfway between them.
