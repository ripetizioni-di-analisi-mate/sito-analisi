The average cost of a firm is $\bar{C}(q) = 30 + \dfrac{2000}{q}$, and each unit sells for $50$ euros. What is the profit when $300$ units are sold?

- $6000$ euros
- $11000$ euros
- $2000$ euros
- $4000$ euros

## Explanation

The **average cost** is total cost divided by quantity, so total cost is $C(q) = q\,\bar{C}(q) = 30q + 2000$. With $300$ units the cost is $C(300) = 9000 + 2000 = 11000$ euros and the revenue is $R(300) = 50 \cdot 300 = 15000$ euros. Therefore the profit is $15000 - 11000 = 4000$ euros. The value $6000$ euros is obtained by forgetting the fixed cost of $2000$ euros.
