A firm records costs of €2600 for $80$ units and €3400 for $120$ units. Its observed cost for $100$ units is €3100. How far above the straight line through the first two observations is this cost?

- €50
- €100
- €200
- €400

## Explanation

One hundred units is halfway between $80$ and $120$. On a straight line the cost is also halfway between the two costs, so the prediction is $\dfrac{2600+3400}{2}=3000$ euros. The observed cost exceeds it by $3100-3000=100$ euros.
