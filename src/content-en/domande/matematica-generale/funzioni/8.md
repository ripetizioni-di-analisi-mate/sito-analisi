- Fixed cost of $0$ euros and variable cost of $32{,}5$ euros per unit
- Fixed cost of $1000$ euros and variable cost of $20$ euros per unit
- Fixed cost of $1800$ euros and variable cost of $10$ euros per unit
- Fixed cost of $4200$ euros and variable cost of $20$ euros per unit

## Explanation

The cost is linear, so it is written as $C(q) = F + c\,q$, with $F$ the fixed cost and $c$ the variable cost per unit. The variable cost per unit is the slope of the line through the points $(80, 2600)$ and $(120, 3400)$:
$$
c = \frac{3400 - 2600}{120 - 80} = \frac{800}{40} = 20 .
$$
Since $C(80) = 2600$, we have $F = 2600 - 20 \cdot 80 = 1000$. Hence the fixed cost is $1000$ euros and the variable cost per unit is $20$ euros.
