What is the natural domain of $\ln{\left(\dfrac{x - 4}{x + 7} \right)}$?

- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right] \cup \left[4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-7, 4\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires a positive argument. The numerator and denominator have the same sign on the two outer intervals.
