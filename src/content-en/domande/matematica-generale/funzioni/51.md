What is the solution set of $\left(x - 2\right) \left(x + 5\right) < 0$?

- $\left[-5, 2\right]$
- $\left(-2, 5\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, 2\right)$

## Explanation

The zeros are $-5$ and $2$. The product is negative only when the factors have opposite signs, between the zeros.
