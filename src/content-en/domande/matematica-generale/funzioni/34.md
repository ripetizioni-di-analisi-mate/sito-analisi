What is the value of $2 \log_2 6 - \log_2 9$?

- $2$
- $\log_2 \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\log_2 27$
- $4$

## Explanation

By the property $\log_a x^{k} = k \log_a x$ we have $2 \log_2 6 = \log_2 6^2 = \log_2 36$. By the property of the logarithm of a quotient we then get
$$
\log_2 36 - \log_2 9 = \log_2 \frac{36}{9} = \log_2 4 .
$$
Since $2^2 = 4$, the result is $2$.
