Let $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$ and $g(x) = \ln x$. What is the domain of the composite function $f \circ g$?

- $(0, +\infty)$
- $[0, +\infty)$
- $[1, +\infty)$
- $(1, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The composite is $(f \circ g)(x) = \sqrt{\ln x}$. The logarithm requires $x > 0$, and the root requires that $g(x) = \ln x$ lie in the domain of $f$, that is, $\ln x \geq 0$. The two conditions must hold together:
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\[4pt]
\ln x \geq 0
\end{cases}
\iff x \geq 1 ,
$$
because the natural logarithm is increasing and $\ln 1 = 0$. Hence the domain of $f \circ g$ is $[1, +\infty)$; the endpoint $1$ belongs to the domain, because $\sqrt{\ln 1} = \sqrt{0} = 0$.
