A firm has total cost $C(q) = 900 + 15q$. Which statement about the average cost $\bar{C}(q)$, for $q > 0$, is true?

- The average cost increases as $q$ increases
- The average cost decreases as $q$ increases and equals $15$ euros for $q$ large enough
- The average cost decreases as $q$ increases and always remains greater than $15$ euros
- The average cost is constant and equals $15$ euros

## Explanation

The average cost is
$$
\bar{C}(q) = \frac{900 + 15q}{q} = \frac{900}{q} + 15 .
$$
As $q$ increases the fraction $\dfrac{900}{q}$ decreases, so the average cost decreases. Since $\dfrac{900}{q} > 0$ for every $q > 0$, we have $\bar{C}(q) > 15$ for every quantity. Therefore the average cost approaches the unit variable cost of $15$ euros without reaching it; for example it equals $105$ euros for $q = 10$ and $15.9$ euros for $q = 1000$.
