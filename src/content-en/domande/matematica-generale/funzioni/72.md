Cost is $C(q)=3 q + 5$ and the unit price is $10$ euros. For which real quantities $q\geq0$ is profit strictly positive?

- $\left[\dfrac{5}{7}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{7}, \infty\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}, \infty\right)$
- $\left[0, \dfrac{5}{7}\right)$

## Explanation

Profit is revenue minus cost, so it is $7q-5$. Require this difference to be strictly positive.
