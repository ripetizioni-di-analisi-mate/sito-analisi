What is the natural domain of $\sqrt{\left(3 - x\right) \left(x - 2\right)}$?

- $\left(2, 3\right)$
- $\left[2, 3\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right] \cup \left[3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative. The product is positive between the roots and zero at the roots.
