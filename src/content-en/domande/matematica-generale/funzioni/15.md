Let $A = [-2, 5)$ and $B = (1, 7]$. What is the intersection $A \cap B$?

- $[1, 5]$
- $(1, 5)$
- $[-2, 7]$
- $(1, 5]$

## Explanation

The **intersection** $A \cap B$ consists of the numbers that belong both to $A$ and to $B$, so the following must hold together:
$$
\begin{cases}
-2 \leq x < 5 \\[4pt]
1 < x \leq 7
\end{cases}
$$
The strictest lower bound is $x > 1$ and the strictest upper bound is $x < 5$. The endpoint $1$ does not belong to $B$ and the endpoint $5$ does not belong to $A$, therefore neither of them lies in the intersection. Hence $A \cap B = (1, 5)$.
