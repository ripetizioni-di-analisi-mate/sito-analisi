Demand and supply of a good are $q_d(p) = 300 - 6p$ and $q_s(p) = 4p - 40$. What is the maximum price that consumers are willing to pay?

- $50$ euros
- $300$ euros
- $10$ euros
- $34$ euros

## Explanation

The maximum price that consumers are willing to pay is the one at which the quantity demanded becomes zero. The equation $300 - 6p = 0$ gives $p = 50$, so at every price greater than $50$ euros demand is zero. The producers' minimum price is $10$ euros because $q_s(10) = 0$. The equilibrium price of $34$ euros solves $300 - 6p = 4p - 40$.
