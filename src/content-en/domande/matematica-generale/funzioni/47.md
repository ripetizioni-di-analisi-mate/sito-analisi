What is the natural domain of $\ln{\left(\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 3} \right)}$?

- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right] \cup \left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, 2\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires a positive argument. The numerator and denominator have the same sign on the two outer intervals.
