What is the solution set of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - 1 > 3 \\[4pt]
x^2 \leq 9
\end{cases}
$$

- $(2, 3)$
- $(2, 3]$
- $[2, 3]$
- $(2, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The first inequality is equivalent to $2x > 4$, that is, to $x > 2$. The second one holds for $-3 \leq x \leq 3$, because $x^2 - 9$ vanishes at $\pm 3$ and is negative between these two values. The solutions of the system must satisfy both inequalities, so they form the **intersection** of the two solution sets. Therefore the required set is $(2, 3]$. The endpoint $2$ is excluded, because $2 \cdot 2 - 1 = 3$ is not greater than $3$, while the endpoint $3$ is included, because $3^2 = 9$.
