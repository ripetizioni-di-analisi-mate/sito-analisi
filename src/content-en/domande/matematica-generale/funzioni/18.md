What is the image of the function $f(x) = 3 - x^2$, defined on $\mathbb{R}$?

- $[3, +\infty)$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $(-\infty, 3]$
- $(-\infty, 3)$

## Explanation

Since $x^2 \geq 0$ for every $x$, we have $3 - x^2 \leq 3$, and the value $3$ is attained at $x = 0$. Conversely, for every $y \leq 3$ the number $x = \sqrt{3 - y}$ satisfies $f(x) = 3 - (3 - y) = y$, so every $y \leq 3$ is an image. Therefore the image of $f$ is $(-\infty, 3]$.
