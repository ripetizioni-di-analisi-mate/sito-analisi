Let $f(x) = 2x - 1$ and $g(x) = x^2 + 1$. What is $(f \circ g)(2)$?

- $10$
- $15$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

The composite $f \circ g$ applies $g$ first and then $f$, so $(f \circ g)(2) = f(g(2))$. We have $g(2) = 4 + 1 = 5$, therefore $(f \circ g)(2) = f(5) = 10 - 1 = 9$. The value $10$ is instead $(g \circ f)(2) = g(3)$, which confirms that the order of composition matters.
