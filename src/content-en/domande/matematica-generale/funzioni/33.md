What are the solutions of the equation $\log_2 (x + 1) + \log_2 (x - 1) = 3$?

- $x = -3$ and $x = 3$
- $x = 4$
- $x = \sqrt{7}$
- $x = 3$

## Explanation

The arguments of the logarithms must be positive, so $x + 1 > 0$ and $x - 1 > 0$ must hold together, that is, $x > 1$. By the property of the logarithm of a product the equation becomes $\log_2 \big((x + 1)(x - 1)\big) = 3$, which by the definition of **logarithm** is equivalent to $x^2 - 1 = 2^3 = 8$. Therefore $x^2 = 9$ and $x = \pm 3$. The value $-3$ does not satisfy the condition $x > 1$, so the only solution is $x = 3$.
