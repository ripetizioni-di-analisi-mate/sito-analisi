What is the linear function whose graph passes through the points $(-1, 4)$ and $(3, 12)$?

- $f(x) = 2x + 2$
- $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,x + \dfrac{9}{2}$
- $f(x) = 4x + 8$
- $f(x) = 2x + 6$

## Explanation

By the formula for the **line through two points**, the slope is the ratio between the change in $y$ and the change in $x$:
$$
m = \frac{12 - 4}{3 - (-1)} = \frac{8}{4} = 2 .
$$
The line with slope $2$ through $(-1, 4)$ is $f(x) = 4 + 2\,(x + 1) = 2x + 6$. Hence the slope is $2$ and the intercept is $f(0) = 6$.
