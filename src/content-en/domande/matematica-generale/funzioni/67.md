With $60$ meters of fencing we want to enclose a rectangular area against a straight wall, which forms the fourth side and needs no fence. What is the largest area that can be enclosed?

- $225\ \mathrm{m}^2$
- $450\ \mathrm{m}^2$
- $900\ \mathrm{m}^2$
- $400\ \mathrm{m}^2$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the length, in meters, of each of the two sides perpendicular to the wall. The fence covers these two sides and the side parallel to the wall, so the parallel side measures $60 - 2x$, with $0 < x < 30$. The area is
$$
A(x) = x\,(60 - 2x) = -2x^2 + 60x ,
$$
a quadratic function with $a = -2 < 0$, therefore its maximum value occurs at the vertex $x = -\dfrac{60}{2 \cdot (-2)} = 15$. Hence the largest area is $A(15) = 15 \cdot 30 = 450\ \mathrm{m}^2$. The value $225\ \mathrm{m}^2$ is the area of the square with side $15$ m, which is obtained by fencing the fourth side as well.
