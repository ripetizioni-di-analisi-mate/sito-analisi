An investment of $1000$ euros can be invested for $10$ years in two ways: A, simple interest at $6\%$ per year; B, compound interest at $5\%$ per year. Which statement is true?

- B is worth about $1629$ euros and exceeds A
- A is worth $1600$ euros and exceeds B
- B is worth $1500$ euros and A exceeds B
- A and B are worth the same amount

## Explanation

With simple interest the interest of each year is computed on the initial $1000$ euros, so A is worth $1000 \cdot (1 + 10 \cdot 0.06) = 1600$ euros. With compound interest the capital is multiplied by $1.05$ every year, so B is worth $1000 \cdot 1.05^{10} \approx 1628.89$ euros. Therefore B exceeds A by about $29$ euros, despite the lower rate. Exponential growth overtakes linear growth from some time on, and here that time has already passed after $10$ years. The value $1500$ euros is the value of B computed by mistake with simple interest.
