What is the exact range of $7 - \left(x - 4\right)^{2}$ over $\mathbb R$?

- $\left(-\infty, 4\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right)$
- $\left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right]$

## Explanation

The square is nonnegative and vanishes at $4$. The function therefore reaches $7$ and takes every smaller value.
