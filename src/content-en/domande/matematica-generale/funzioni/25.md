What are the slope and the intercept of the line $2x + 3y - 6 = 0$?

- Slope $2$, intercept $-6$
- Slope $\dfrac{2}{3}$, intercept $2$
- Slope $-\dfrac{2}{3}$, intercept $2$
- Slope $-\dfrac{2}{3}$, intercept $3$

## Explanation

To read the slope and the intercept we write the line in the form $y = m x + k$. Solving for $y$ we get $3y = -2x + 6$, so
$$
y = -\frac{2}{3}\,x + 2 .
$$
Therefore the slope is $m = -\dfrac{2}{3}$ and the intercept is $k = 2$, that is, the ordinate of the point where the line crosses the $y$-axis. The number $3$ is instead the abscissa of the point where the line crosses the $x$-axis.
