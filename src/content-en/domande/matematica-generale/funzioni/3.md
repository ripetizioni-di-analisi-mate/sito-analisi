- $D = [-1, 3)$
- $D = (-\infty, -1) \cup (3, +\infty)$
- $D = (-1, +\infty) \setminus \{3\}$
- $D = (-1, 3)$

## Explanation

The argument of the logarithm must be strictly positive, so we impose $\dfrac{x + 1}{3 - x} > 0$, with $x \neq 3$. The numerator is positive for $x > -1$ and the denominator is positive for $x < 3$. The quotient is positive when the numerator and the denominator have the same sign. They are both positive when
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 1 > 0 \\[4pt]
3 - x > 0
\end{cases}
\iff -1 < x < 3 ,
$$
while they are never both negative, because $x < -1$ and $x > 3$ would have to hold together. Therefore the domain is $D = (-1, 3)$.
