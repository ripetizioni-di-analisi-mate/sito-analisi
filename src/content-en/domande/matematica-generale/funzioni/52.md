What is the range of the function $f(x) = 2 + e^{-x}$, defined on $[0, +\infty)$?

- $[2, 3]$
- $(2, +\infty)$
- $(2, 3]$
- $[3, +\infty)$

## Explanation

We have $e^{-x} = \left( \dfrac{1}{e} \right)^{x}$, an exponential with base between $0$ and $1$, so $f$ is decreasing. Therefore the largest value of $f$ on $[0, +\infty)$ is $f(0) = 2 + 1 = 3$. An exponential is always positive, so $f(x) > 2$ for every $x$, and the value $2$ is not attained. Conversely, every $y$ with $2 < y \leq 3$ is the image of $x = -\ln(y - 2)$, which is greater than or equal to $0$ because $0 < y - 2 \leq 1$. Hence the range of $f$ is $(2, 3]$.
