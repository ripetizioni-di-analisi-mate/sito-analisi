- $D = \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-2, -3\}$
- $D = \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0, 2, 3\}$
- $D = \mathbb{R} \setminus \{2, 3\}$
- $D = (-\infty, 2) \cup (3, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The function is a quotient of polynomials, so the only condition for the **natural domain** is that the denominator be different from zero. Since $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3)$, the denominator vanishes for $x = 2$ and for $x = 3$. The numerator $x$ imposes no conditions, therefore $x = 0$ belongs to the domain. Hence the domain is $D = \mathbb{R} \setminus \{2, 3\}$.
