What is the natural domain of $f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x - 4} + \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$?

- $(4, +\infty)$
- $[4, +\infty)$
- $[0, +\infty)$
- $(0, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The cube root has odd index, so $\sqrt[3]{x - 4}$ is defined for every real $x$ and imposes no conditions. The term $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$ requires $x \geq 0$ for the root and $\sqrt{x} \neq 0$ for the denominator, that is, $x > 0$. Therefore the domain is $(0, +\infty)$. For example, $x = 1$ belongs to the domain, because $f(1) = \sqrt[3]{-3} + 1$ is a real number.
