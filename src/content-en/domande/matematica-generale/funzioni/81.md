What is the solution set of $\left(x - 4\right) \left(x + 9\right) < 0$?

- $\left(-\infty, -9\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-4, 9\right)$
- $\left(-9, 4\right)$
- $\left[-9, 4\right]$

## Explanation

The zeros are $-9$ and $4$. The product is negative only when the factors have opposite signs, between the zeros.
