Inverse demand is $p=30-2q$ for $0\leq q\leq 15$. What quantity maximizes revenue?

- $\dfrac{45}{2}$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $15$

## Explanation

Revenue is $q(30-2q)$. This is a downward-opening parabola with zeros at $0$ and $15$, so the vertex lies halfway between them.
