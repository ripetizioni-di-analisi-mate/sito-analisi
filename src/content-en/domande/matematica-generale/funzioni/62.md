What is the natural domain of $\ln{\left(\dfrac{x - 3}{x + 5} \right)}$?

- $\left(-\infty, -5\right] \cup \left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-5, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The logarithm requires a positive argument. The numerator and denominator have the same sign on the two outer intervals.
