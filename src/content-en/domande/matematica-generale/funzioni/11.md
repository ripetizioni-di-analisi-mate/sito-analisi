## Explanation

Each unit sold contributes a margin of $50 - 35 = 15$ euros, that is, the difference between the price and the variable cost per unit. The **break-even point** is reached when the sum of the margins covers the fixed cost, so
$$
q_0 = \frac{12000}{50 - 35} = \frac{12000}{15} = 800 .
$$
