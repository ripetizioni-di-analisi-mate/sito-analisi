Which of the following curves is the graph of a function $y = f(x)$?

- The curve $x = y^2$
- The curve $y = |x - 1|$
- The circle $x^2 + y^2 = 9$
- The line $x = 2$

## Explanation

A curve is the graph of a function *if and only if* every vertical line meets it in at most one point. The line $x = 1$ meets the curve $x = y^2$ at the points $(1, 1)$ and $(1, -1)$, and the line $x = 0$ meets the circle at the points $(0, 3)$ and $(0, -3)$. The line $x = 2$ contains infinitely many points with the same horizontal coordinate $2$, so none of these three curves is the graph of a function. The formula $y = |x - 1|$, instead, assigns a single value to each $x$, so its curve is the graph of a function.
