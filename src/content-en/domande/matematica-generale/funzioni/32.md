What is the solution of the equation $2^{x + 1} = 8^{x - 1}$?

- $x = 1$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 2$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $8 = 2^3$, we have $8^{x - 1} = 2^{3(x - 1)} = 2^{3x - 3}$, and the equation becomes $2^{x + 1} = 2^{3x - 3}$. The exponential with base $2$ is increasing, hence injective, and two powers with base $2$ are equal only if they have the same exponent. Therefore $x + 1 = 3x - 3$, that is, $2x = 4$, and the solution is $x = 2$.
