The figure shows the complete graph of a function $f$, which goes from the point $(-1, 0)$ to the point $(8, 3)$; the two filled points belong to the graph. What are the domain and the range of $f$?

::grafico{f="sqrt(x+1)+0*sqrt(8-x)" punti="-1,0,pieno;8,3,pieno" xmin=-2 xmax=10 ymin=-1 ymax=4 legenda=0}

- Domain $[-1, 8]$, range $[0, 3]$
- Domain $[0, 3]$, range $[-1, 8]$
- Domain $(-1, 8)$, range $(0, 3)$
- Domain $[-1, +\infty)$, range $[0, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The **domain** is the set of the $x$-coordinates of the points of the graph, the **range** is the set of their $y$-coordinates. The graph is an unbroken curve that rises from the point $(-1, 0)$ to the point $(8, 3)$, so the $x$-coordinates of its points fill the interval from $-1$ to $8$ and the $y$-coordinates fill the interval from $0$ to $3$. The two endpoints are filled points, so they belong to the graph and the endpoints of the intervals are included. Hence the domain is $[-1, 8]$ and the range is $[0, 3]$.
