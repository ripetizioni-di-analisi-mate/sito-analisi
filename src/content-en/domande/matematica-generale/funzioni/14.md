An initial investment of $3000$ euros earns $4\%$ per year with compound interest. What is it worth after $5$ years?

- $3649{,}96$ euros
- $3600{,}00$ euros
- $3509{,}58$ euros
- $3795{,}96$ euros

## Explanation

The rate of $4\%$ corresponds to $0{,}04$, so at the end of each year the balance is multiplied by $1 + 0{,}04 = 1{,}04$. After $5$ years the balance has been multiplied $5$ times by $1{,}04$, therefore it is worth
$$
3000 \cdot 1{,}04^{5} \approx 3649{,}96 \text{ euros} .
$$
With simple interest each year’s interest would be calculated only on the initial $3000$ euros. The balance would then be $3000 \cdot (1 + 5 \cdot 0{,}04) = 3600$ euros.
