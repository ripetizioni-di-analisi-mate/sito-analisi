A capital of $3000$ euros is invested at $4\%$ per year with compound interest. What is it worth after $5$ years?

- $3649.96$ euros
- $3600.00$ euros
- $3509.58$ euros
- $3795.96$ euros

## Explanation

The rate of $4\%$ corresponds to $0.04$, so at the end of each year the capital is multiplied by $1 + 0.04 = 1.04$. After $5$ years the capital has been multiplied $5$ times by $1.04$, therefore it is worth
$$
3000 \cdot 1.04^{5} \approx 3649.96 \text{ euros} .
$$
With simple interest the interest of each year would be computed only on the initial $3000$ euros, and the capital would be worth $3000 \cdot (1 + 5 \cdot 0.04) = 3600$ euros.
