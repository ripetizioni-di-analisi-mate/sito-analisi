An exponential function $f(x) = k \cdot a^{x}$, with $k > 0$, $a > 0$ and $a \neq 1$, satisfies $f(1) = 6$ and $f(3) = 54$. What is $f(0)$?

- $3$
- $2$
- $-18$
- $18$

## Explanation

The ratio of the two known values is
$$
\frac{f(3)}{f(1)} = \frac{k\,a^{3}}{k\,a} = a^{2} ,
$$
so $a^{2} = \dfrac{54}{6} = 9$. Since $a > 0$, we have $a = 3$. The condition $f(1) = 3k = 6$ gives $k = 2$, therefore $f(0) = k \cdot a^{0} = 2$. Indeed $f(3) = 2 \cdot 27 = 54$. The value $-18$ is that of the linear function whose graph passes through $(1, 6)$ and $(3, 54)$, which has slope $24$, and the value $18$ is $f(2)$.
