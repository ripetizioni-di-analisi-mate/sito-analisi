A firm has inverse demand $p = 60 - 2q$ and total cost $C(q) = 100 + 12q$. Which quantity maximizes the profit?

- $q = 15$
- $q = 24$
- $q = 6$
- $q = 12$

## Explanation

The revenue is $R(q) = (60 - 2q)\,q = 60q - 2q^2$, so the profit is
$$
\pi(q) = 60q - 2q^2 - 100 - 12q = -2q^2 + 48q - 100 .
$$
The profit is a quadratic function with $a = -2 < 0$, therefore its maximum value is attained at the vertex $q = -\dfrac{48}{2 \cdot (-2)} = 12$. The quantity $15$ is the vertex of the revenue, that is, the quantity that maximizes the revenue without taking costs into account.
