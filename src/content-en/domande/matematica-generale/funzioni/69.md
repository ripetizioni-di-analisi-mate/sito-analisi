A stone thrown upward is, after $t$ seconds, at height $h(t) = 20t - 5t^2$ meters. For how many seconds is the height of the stone greater than $15$ meters?

- $3$ seconds
- $4$ seconds
- $1$ second
- $2$ seconds

## Explanation

The height exceeds $15$ meters when $20t - 5t^2 > 15$, that is, when $5t^2 - 20t + 15 < 0$. Dividing by $5$ gives $t^2 - 4t + 3 < 0$, and the polynomial $t^2 - 4t + 3 = (t - 1)(t - 3)$ has zeros $1$ and $3$. The coefficient of $t^2$ is positive, so the polynomial is negative between the zeros, that is, for $1 < t < 3$. Therefore the stone stays above $15$ meters for $3 - 1 = 2$ seconds. The value $4$ seconds is instead the duration of the whole flight, because $h(4) = 0$.
