Demand and supply of a good are $q_d(p) = 90 - 2p$ and $q_s(p) = 4p - 30$. What happens in the market at the price $p = 15$ euros?

- There is an excess supply of $30$ units
- The market is in equilibrium
- There is an excess demand of $90$ units
- There is an excess demand of $30$ units

## Explanation

At the price $p = 15$ the quantity demanded is $q_d(15) = 90 - 30 = 60$ and the quantity supplied is $q_s(15) = 60 - 30 = 30$. Since demand exceeds supply, there is an **excess demand** of $60 - 30 = 30$ units. The same result follows from the equilibrium price. The equation $90 - 2p = 4p - 30$ gives $p^* = 20$, and for a price lower than $p^*$ demand exceeds supply.
