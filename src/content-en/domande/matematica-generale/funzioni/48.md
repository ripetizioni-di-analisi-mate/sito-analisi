What is the exact range of $3 - \left(x - 2\right)^{2}$ over $\mathbb R$?

- $\left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$

## Explanation

The square is nonnegative and vanishes at $2$. The function therefore reaches $3$ and takes every smaller value.
