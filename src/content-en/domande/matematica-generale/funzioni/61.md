What is the natural domain of $\sqrt{\left(5 - x\right) \left(x - 3\right)}$?

- $\left(3, 5\right)$
- $\left[3, 5\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right] \cup \left[5, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 5\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative. The product is positive between the roots and zero at the roots.
