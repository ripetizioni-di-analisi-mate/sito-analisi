The amount of a drug in the blood, in milligrams, is $Q(t) = 80 \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{4}}$, with $t$ in hours. After how many hours is the amount $10$ milligrams?

- $8$ hours
- $32$ hours
- $12$ hours
- $3$ hours

## Explanation

The condition $Q(t) = 10$ becomes $80 \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{4}} = 10$, that is, $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{4}} = \dfrac{1}{8}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{8} = \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{3}$ and the exponential is injective, the exponents are equal, so $\dfrac{t}{4} = 3$. Therefore $t = 12$ hours. In other words, the amount halves every $4$ hours, and three halvings are needed to go from $80$ to $10$ milligrams.
