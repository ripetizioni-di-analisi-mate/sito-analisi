- $p^* = 30$ euros and $q^* = 90$
- $p^* = 50$ euros and $q^* = 30$

## Explanation

At **market equilibrium** the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied, so $200 - 5p = 3p - 40$. Moving the terms in $p$ to the right-hand side we obtain $240 = 8p$, therefore $p^* = 30$ euros. The equilibrium quantity is $q^* = q_d(30) = 200 - 150 = 50$.
