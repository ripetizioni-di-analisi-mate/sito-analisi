Cost is $C(q)=2 q + 3$ and the unit price is $7$ euros. For which real quantities $q\geq0$ is profit strictly positive?

- $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \infty\right)$
- $\left[\dfrac{3}{5}, \infty\right)$
- $\left[0, \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{7}, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

Profit is revenue minus cost, so it is $5q-3$. Require this difference to be strictly positive.
