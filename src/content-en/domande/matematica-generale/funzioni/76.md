What is the natural domain of $\sqrt{\left(7 - x\right) \left(x - 4\right)}$?

- $\left(-\infty, 4\right] \cup \left[7, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, 7\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 7\right]$
- $\left[4, 7\right]$

## Explanation

The radicand must be nonnegative. The product is positive between the roots and zero at the roots.
