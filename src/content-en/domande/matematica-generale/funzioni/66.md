What is the solution set of $\left(x - 3\right) \left(x + 7\right) < 0$?

- $\left[-7, 3\right]$
- $\left(-7, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right) \cup \left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, 7\right)$

## Explanation

The zeros are $-7$ and $3$. The product is negative only when the factors have opposite signs, between the zeros.
