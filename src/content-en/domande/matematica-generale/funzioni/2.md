- $D = [-3, 3]$
- $D = (-3, 3)$
- $D = (-\infty, -3] \cup [3, +\infty)$
- $D = (-\infty, 3]$

## Explanation

The radicand of a root with an even index must be greater than or equal to zero, so we impose $9 - x^2 \geq 0$, that is, $x^2 \leq 9$. The inequality $x^2 \leq 9$ is satisfied for $-3 \leq x \leq 3$, because the polynomial $9 - x^2$ has zeros $\pm 3$ and is positive between them. The endpoints belong to the domain, because at $x = \pm 3$ we have $\sqrt{0} = 0$. Hence the domain is $D = [-3, 3]$.
