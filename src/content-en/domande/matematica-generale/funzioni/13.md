A firm has cost $C(q) = 500 + 20q$ and inverse demand $p = 100 - q$. What is the maximum profit?

- $1600$ euros
- $1000$ euros
- $1100$ euros
- $2100$ euros

## Explanation

The revenue is $R(q) = (100 - q)\,q = 100q - q^2$, so the profit is
$$
\pi(q) = 100q - q^2 - 500 - 20q = -q^2 + 80q - 500 .
$$
The profit is a quadratic function with $a = -1 < 0$, therefore its maximum value is attained at the vertex $q = -\dfrac{80}{2 \cdot (-1)} = 40$. Hence the maximum profit is $\pi(40) = -1600 + 3200 - 500 = 1100$ euros.
