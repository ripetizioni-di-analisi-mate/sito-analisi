A firm sells each unit at $6$ euros and has a variable cost per unit of $4$ euros. The fixed cost is $800$ euros up to $500$ units produced and rises to $1400$ euros above $500$ units, because a second warehouse is needed. For which quantities is the profit positive?

- $q > 400$
- $400 < q < 700$
- $400 < q \leq 500$ or $q > 700$
- $q > 700$

## Explanation

Revenue is $R(q) = 6q$ and cost is the piecewise-defined function $C(q) = 800 + 4q$ for $0 \leq q \leq 500$ and $C(q) = 1400 + 4q$ for $q > 500$. Hence profit is
$$
\pi(q) =
\begin{cases}
2q - 800 & \text{if } 0 \leq q \leq 500 \\[4pt]
2q - 1400 & \text{if } q > 500
\end{cases}
$$
For $0 \leq q \leq 500$ profit is positive when $2q > 800$, that is, for $400 < q \leq 500$. For $q > 500$ profit is positive when $2q > 1400$, that is, for $q > 700$. For $500 < q \leq 700$ profit is negative or zero; for example $\pi(600) = 1200 - 1400 = -200$ euros. Therefore profit is positive for $400 < q \leq 500$ and for $q > 700$. The set $q > 400$ is obtained by neglecting the increase in the fixed cost.
