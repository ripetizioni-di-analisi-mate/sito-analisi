A firm sells at $16$ euros per unit and incurs $7$ euros in variable cost per unit. If it breaks even at $11$ units, what is its fixed cost?

- $77$
- $176$
- $99$
- $9$

## Explanation

The contribution margin is $16-7=9$ euros per unit. At the break-even point, the total contribution margin equals the fixed cost.
