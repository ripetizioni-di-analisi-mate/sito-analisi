The parabola $f(x) = a x^2 + b x + 1$ has its vertex at the point $(1, -1)$. What are $a$ and $b$?

- $a = 2$, $b = -4$
- $a = -2$, $b = 4$
- $a = 2$, $b = 4$
- $a = 1$, $b = -2$

## Explanation

The abscissa of the vertex is $x_V = -\dfrac{b}{2a}$, so the condition $x_V = 1$ gives $b = -2a$. The ordinate of the vertex is $f(1) = a + b + 1$, therefore $a + b + 1 = -1$ must hold. Substituting $b = -2a$ we get $-a + 1 = -1$, so $a = 2$ and $b = -4$. Indeed $f(x) = 2x^2 - 4x + 1$ has $f(1) = 2 - 4 + 1 = -1$.
