A machine bought for $24000$ euros loses $2500$ euros of value every year. After how many years is its value one quarter of the purchase price?

- $2.4$ years
- $9.6$ years
- $4.8$ years
- $7.2$ years

## Explanation

The value decreases by the same amount every year, so after $t$ years it is given by the linear function $V(t) = 24000 - 2500\,t$, whose intercept is the purchase price and whose slope is $-2500$. One quarter of the purchase price is $\dfrac{24000}{4} = 6000$ euros. The condition $24000 - 2500\,t = 6000$ becomes $2500\,t = 18000$. Therefore $t = \dfrac{18000}{2500} = 7.2$ years.
