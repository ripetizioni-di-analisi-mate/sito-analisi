For which value of $k$ does the point $(2, 5)$ belong to the graph of $f(x) = k x^2 - 3$?

- $k = 2$
- $k = 4$
- $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $k = \dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The point $(2, 5)$ belongs to the graph of $f$ *if and only if* $f(2) = 5$. Since $f(2) = 4k - 3$, the condition becomes $4k - 3 = 5$, that is, $4k = 8$. Hence $k = 2$, and indeed with $f(x) = 2x^2 - 3$ we have $f(2) = 8 - 3 = 5$.
