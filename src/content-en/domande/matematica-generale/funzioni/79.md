A warehouse records $x$ as the number of whole boxes taken from a stock of eight. The cost per box taken is $c(x)=\dfrac{12+x}{x}$. For which values of $x$ can the warehouse record this cost?

- $[1,8]$
- $\{1,2,\ldots,8\}$
- $\{0,1,\ldots,8\}$
- $(0,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The denominator rules out taking zero boxes. Counting whole boxes requires integers, and the stock limits the count to eight. For example, the formula can be evaluated at $x=1.5$, although that value cannot represent a withdrawal.
