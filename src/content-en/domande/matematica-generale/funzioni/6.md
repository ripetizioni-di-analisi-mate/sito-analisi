Let $f(x) = x^2 + 1$ and $g(x) = \sqrt{x - 2}$. What is the domain of the composite function $g \circ f$?

- $(-\infty, -1] \cup [1, +\infty)$
- $[2, +\infty)$
- $[-1, 1]$
- $[1, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The composite function applies $f$ first and then $g$, so $(g \circ f)(x) = \sqrt{(x^2 + 1) - 2} = \sqrt{x^2 - 1}$. The domain consists of the values of $x$ for which $f(x)$ lies in the domain of $g$, that is, $x^2 + 1 \geq 2$. This condition is equivalent to $x^2 \geq 1$, which is satisfied for $x \leq -1$ and for $x \geq 1$. Therefore the domain of $g \circ f$ is $(-\infty, -1] \cup [1, +\infty)$.
