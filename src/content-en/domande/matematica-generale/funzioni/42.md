A firm has total cost $C(q) = 1500 + 8q$. For which quantity is the average cost $11$ euros?

- $q \approx 136$
- $q = 500$
- $q \approx 79$
- $q = 187.5$

## Explanation

The **average cost** is $\bar{C}(q) = \dfrac{C(q)}{q} = \dfrac{1500}{q} + 8$. The condition $\bar{C}(q) = 11$ becomes $\dfrac{1500}{q} = 3$, so $q = \dfrac{1500}{3} = 500$. Indeed $C(500) = 1500 + 4000 = 5500$ and $\dfrac{5500}{500} = 11$.
