What is the range of the function $f(x) = x^2 - 4x$ restricted to the interval $[3, 6]$?

- $[-4, 12]$
- $[-3, 12]$
- $[0, 12]$
- $[-4, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The vertex of the parabola has $x$-coordinate $x_V = -\dfrac{-4}{2} = 2$, and the parabola opens upward, so $f$ is increasing on $[2, +\infty)$. The interval $[3, 6]$ is contained in $[2, +\infty)$, therefore $f$ is increasing on it. The smallest value is then $f(3) = 9 - 12 = -3$ and the largest is $f(6) = 36 - 24 = 12$. Since $f$ takes all the values between $-3$ and $12$, the range is $[-3, 12]$. The value $-4 = f(2)$ is not attained, because $2$ does not belong to $[3, 6]$.
