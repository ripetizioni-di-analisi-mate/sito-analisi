A firm sells at $12$ euros per unit and incurs $5$ euros in variable cost per unit. If it breaks even at $8$ units, what is its fixed cost?

- $96$
- $40$
- $7$
- $56$

## Explanation

The contribution margin is $12-5=7$ euros per unit. At break-even total contribution exactly covers the fixed cost.
