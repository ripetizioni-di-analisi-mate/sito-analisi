A firm sells a product at the constant price of $40$ euros, with total cost $C(q) = 6000 + 25q$. For which quantity is the profit $3000$ euros?

- $q = 400$
- $q = 200$
- $q = 600$
- $q = 360$

## Explanation

The revenue is $R(q) = 40q$, so the profit is $\pi(q) = 40q - 6000 - 25q = 15q - 6000$. The condition $\pi(q) = 3000$ becomes $15q = 9000$, therefore $q = 600$. The quantity $400$ is the break-even point, where the profit is $0$.
