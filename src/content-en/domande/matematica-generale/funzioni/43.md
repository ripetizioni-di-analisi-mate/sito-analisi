A firm has total cost $C(q) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,q^2 + 10q + 300$. What is the variable cost for $q = 20$?

- $700$ euros
- $300$ euros
- $400$ euros
- $35$ euros

## Explanation

The fixed cost is the cost for $q = 0$, that is, $C(0) = 300$ euros. The total cost for $q = 20$ is
$$
C(20) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 400 + 200 + 300 = 700 .
$$
The variable cost is the difference between total cost and fixed cost, so it equals $C(20) - C(0) = 700 - 300 = 400$ euros. The value $35$ euros is instead the average cost $\dfrac{700}{20}$.
