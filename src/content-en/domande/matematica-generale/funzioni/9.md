- $y = -3x + 7$
- $y = \dfrac{1}{3}\,x - \dfrac{5}{3}$
- $y = -3x + 5$
- $y = -3x - 7$

## Explanation

Two parallel lines have the same slope, so the required line has slope $-3$. The line with slope $-3$ through $(2, -1)$ is
$$
y - (-1) = -3\,(x - 2) ,
$$
that is, $y + 1 = -3x + 6$. Therefore the required line is $y = -3x + 5$.
