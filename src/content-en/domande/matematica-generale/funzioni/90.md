A firm produces $q(L) = 2\sqrt{L}$ units using $L$ hours of labor, and the cost of producing $q$ units is $C(q) = 100 + 5q$. Which function gives the cost as a function of the hours of labor $L$?

- $100 + 10\sqrt{L}$
- $2\sqrt{100 + 5L}$
- $100 + 5\sqrt{L}$
- $200 + 10\sqrt{L}$

## Explanation

The cost depends on the quantity $q$, and the quantity depends on the hours $L$, so the cost as a function of $L$ is the **composite function** $C \circ q$, which applies $q$ first and then $C$. Therefore
$$
(C \circ q)(L) = C\big(2\sqrt{L}\,\big) = 100 + 5 \cdot 2\sqrt{L} = 100 + 10\sqrt{L} .
$$
For example, with $L = 25$ hours the firm produces $2 \cdot 5 = 10$ units, which cost $100 + 50 = 150$ euros, and indeed $100 + 10 \cdot 5 = 150$. The function $2\sqrt{100 + 5L}$ is the composite in the opposite order, $q \circ C$, and the function $100 + 5\sqrt{L}$ is obtained by neglecting the factor $2$ of the production function.
