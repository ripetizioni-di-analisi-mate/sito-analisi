A firm sells at $8$ euros per unit and incurs $3$ euros in variable cost per unit. If it breaks even at $5$ units, what is its fixed cost?

- $15$
- $25$
- $40$
- $5$

## Explanation

The contribution margin is $8-3=5$ euros per unit. At break-even total contribution exactly covers the fixed cost.
