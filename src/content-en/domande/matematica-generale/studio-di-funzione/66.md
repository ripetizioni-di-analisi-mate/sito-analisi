A differentiable function satisfies $f'(1)=1$, $f'(2)=4$, and $f'(3)=2$. What can these readings establish about convexity over the whole interval $[1,3]$?

- The function is convex over the entire interval.
- The function is concave over the entire interval.
- The function can be neither convex nor concave over the entire interval.
- The function is affine over the entire interval.

## Explanation

The derivative of a convex function is nondecreasing, yet here it falls from $4$ to $2$. The derivative of a concave function is nonincreasing, yet here it rises from $1$ to $4$. Together the observations rule out both global properties.
