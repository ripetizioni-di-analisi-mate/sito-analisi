Profit is $\pi(q)=- 2 q^{2} + 12 q - 5$, with real output $0\leq q\leq 2$. What output maximizes profit?

- $2$
- $0$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The vertex of the parabola is at $q=3$. The derivative is positive throughout the feasible interval, so the maximum is at the right endpoint.
