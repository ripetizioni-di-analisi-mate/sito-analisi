The profit of a firm is $\pi(q) = -q^3 + 9q^2 - 24q - 10$ euros, with $q \geq 0$. Which quantity maximizes profit on $[0, +\infty)$?

- $q = 4$
- $q = 0$
- $q = 2$
- $q = 3$

## Explanation

The derivative is $\pi'(q) = -3q^2 + 18q - 24 = -3(q - 2)(q - 4)$, so $\pi$ decreases on $[0, 2]$, increases on $[2, 4]$ and decreases on $[4, +\infty)$. The point $q = 4$ is a relative maximum point, and the maximum on $[0, +\infty)$ is the larger of $\pi(0)$ and $\pi(4)$. We have $\pi(0) = -10$ euros and $\pi(4) = -64 + 144 - 96 - 10 = -26$ euros. Since $-10 > -26$, the **maximum profit** is obtained with $q = 0$, that is, by not producing, with a loss of $10$ euros. The point $q = 2$ is a relative minimum point and $q = 3$ is the inflection point.
