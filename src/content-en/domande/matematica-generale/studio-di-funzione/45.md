By studying the monotonicity of $f(x) = \dfrac{\ln x}{x}$, for $x > 0$, one can determine which of the following numbers is the largest. Which one?

- $\sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt[3]{3}$
- $\sqrt[4]{4}$
- $\sqrt[5]{5}$

## Explanation

For every integer $n \geq 2$ we have $\sqrt[n]{n} = e^{\frac{\ln n}{n}} = e^{f(n)}$, and the exponential is strictly increasing, so the largest number is the one with the largest value of $f(n)$. The derivative
$$
f'(x) = \frac{1 - \ln x}{x^2}
$$
is negative for $x > e$, therefore $f$ is strictly decreasing on $[e, +\infty)$. Since $e < 3 < 4 < 5$, we have $f(3) > f(4) > f(5)$. Moreover $f(4) = \dfrac{2\ln 2}{4} = \dfrac{\ln 2}{2} = f(2)$, so $\sqrt[4]{4} = \sqrt{2}$. Therefore the largest number is $\sqrt[3]{3}$.
