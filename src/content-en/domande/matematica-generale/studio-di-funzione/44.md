For a firm with revenue $R(q)$ and cost $C(q)$ we have, at $q_0 = 5$, $R'(5) = C'(5) = 40$, $R''(5) = -2$ and $C''(5) = 3$. Which statement is true?

- $q = 5$ is a relative maximum point of profit
- $q = 5$ is a relative minimum point of profit
- The data do not allow us to classify $q = 5$
- $q = 5$ is a maximum point of revenue

## Explanation

Profit is $\pi(q) = R(q) - C(q)$, so $\pi'(5) = R'(5) - C'(5) = 0$ and the first-order condition is satisfied. Moreover $\pi''(5) = R''(5) - C''(5) = -2 - 3 = -5 < 0$, that is, $R''(5) < C''(5)$, therefore the second-order condition is satisfied as well. By the theorem on the conditions for maximum profit, $q = 5$ is therefore a **relative maximum point** of profit. The point is not a maximum point of revenue, because $R'(5) = 40 \neq 0$.
