For the graph of $- 9 x + \left(x - 4\right)^{4} + 36$, how is the point with x-coordinate $4$ classified?

- Neither a local extremum nor an inflection point
- Local minimum
- Local maximum
- Inflection point

## Explanation

The first derivative there is $-9$, so there is no local extremum. The second derivative is $12(x-4)^2$ and has the same sign on both sides.
