A firm in perfect competition sells at the price $p = 60$ euros and has cost $C(q) = q^3 - 15q^2 + 87q + 43$ euros. The condition $p = C'(q)$ is satisfied at $q = 1$ and at $q = 9$. At which of the two points is the second-order condition also satisfied?

- Only at $q = 1$
- At both points
- Only at $q = 9$
- At neither point

## Explanation

In perfect competition $R''(q) = 0$, so the second-order condition $R''(q_0) < C''(q_0)$ becomes $C''(q_0) > 0$. The second derivative of cost is $C''(q) = 6q - 30$. We have $C''(1) = -24 < 0$, so at $q = 1$ the condition fails and profit has a local minimum at $q = 1$. We have instead $C''(9) = 24 > 0$, therefore the **second-order condition** is satisfied only at $q = 9$, which is a point of local maximum of profit, with $\pi(9) = 540 - 340 = 200$ euros.
