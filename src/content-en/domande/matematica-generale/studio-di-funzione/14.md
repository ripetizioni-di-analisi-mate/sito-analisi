The demand for a good is $q(p) = 108 - p^2$, with $0 \leq p \leq \sqrt{108}$ and $p$ in euros. Which price maximizes revenue?

- $6$ euros
- $3\sqrt{6}$ euros
- $6\sqrt{3}$ euros
- $3\sqrt{3}$ euros

## Explanation

Revenue as a function of price and its derivative are
$$
R(p) = p\,q(p) = 108p - p^3,
\qquad
R'(p) = 108 - 3p^2 = 3\,(6 - p)(6 + p) .
$$
On the interval $[0, \sqrt{108}]$ the derivative is positive for $p < 6$ and negative for $p > 6$, so revenue increases up to $p = 6$ and then decreases. Therefore the **maximum revenue** is obtained at the price $p = 6$ euros, and it equals $R(6) = 648 - 216 = 432$ euros. At that price the elasticity of demand is $\varepsilon(6) = \dfrac{-2 \cdot 6 \cdot 6}{108 - 36} = -1$, in agreement with the condition of unit elasticity.
