As $x \to +\infty$ the graph of $f(x) = \sqrt{x^2 + 4x}$ approaches a line. What is the equation of this line?

- $y = x + 2$
- $y = x$
- $y = x + 4$
- $y = x - 2$

## Explanation

For $x > 0$ we have $\sqrt{x^2 + 4x} = x\sqrt{1 + \dfrac{4}{x}}$, so $\dfrac{f(x)}{x} \to 1$ and the slope of the asymptote is $m = 1$. For the intercept we rationalize:
$$
f(x) - x = \frac{x^2 + 4x - x^2}{\sqrt{x^2 + 4x} + x} = \frac{4x}{\sqrt{x^2 + 4x} + x} .
$$
Dividing numerator and denominator by $x$ gives $\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{1 + \frac{4}{x}} + 1} \to \dfrac{4}{2} = 2$. Therefore the **oblique asymptote** as $x \to +\infty$ is $y = x + 2$.
