What is the exact set of absolute minimizers of $\left|{x - 7}\right| + \left|{x - 3}\right|$?

- $\left(3, 7\right)$
- $\left[3, 7\right]$
- $\left\{5\right\}$
- $\left\{3, 7\right\}$

## Explanation

The function is the sum of distances from $3$ and $7$. That sum equals the distance between the points on $[3,7]$ and is larger outside it.
