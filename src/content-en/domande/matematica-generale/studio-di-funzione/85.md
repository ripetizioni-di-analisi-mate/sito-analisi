What is $f\prime(x)$ for $f(x)=x + \dfrac{7}{x - 4}$?

- $1 - \dfrac{7}{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}$
- $1 + \dfrac{7}{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}$
- $1 - \dfrac{7}{x - 4}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{\left(x - 4\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

Write the second term as $7(x-4)^{-1}$. Its derivative is $-\dfrac{7}{(x-4)^2}$, to which the derivative of $x$ is added.
