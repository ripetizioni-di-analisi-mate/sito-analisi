For the function $f(x) = x^4 - 18x^2 + 5$, what are all the points of local minimum?

- $x = 0$
- $x = -\sqrt{3}$ and $x = \sqrt{3}$
- $x = -3\sqrt{2}$ and $x = 3\sqrt{2}$
- $x = -3$ and $x = 3$

## Explanation

The derivative is $f'(x) = 4x^3 - 36x = 4x(x - 3)(x + 3)$, which vanishes at $x = -3$, $x = 0$ and $x = 3$. From the sign chart of the three factors, $f'$ is negative for $x < -3$, positive for $-3 < x < 0$, negative for $0 < x < 3$ and positive for $x > 3$. By the **first derivative test**, $x = -3$ and $x = 3$ are therefore points of local minimum, with $f(\pm 3) = 81 - 162 + 5 = -76$, while $x = 0$ is a point of local maximum. The points $x = \pm\sqrt{3}$ are the zeros of $f''(x) = 12x^2 - 36$, that is, the inflection points.
