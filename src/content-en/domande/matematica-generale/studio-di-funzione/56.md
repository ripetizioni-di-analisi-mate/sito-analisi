Where does $f\prime\prime(x)<0$ hold for $f(x)=\left(x - 2\right)^{3} + 3$?

- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -2\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 2\right]$

## Explanation

The second derivative is $6(x-2)$. It is strictly negative only for x-coordinates below $2$.
