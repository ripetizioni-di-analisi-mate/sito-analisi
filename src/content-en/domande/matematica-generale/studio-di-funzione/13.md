The production cost is $C(q) = 2q^2 + 8q + 200$ euros, with $q > 0$. What is the minimum average cost?

- $28$ euros
- $48$ euros
- $40$ euros
- $480$ euros

## Explanation

The average cost and its derivative are
$$
\bar{C}(q) = \frac{C(q)}{q} = 2q + 8 + \frac{200}{q},
\qquad
\bar{C}'(q) = 2 - \frac{200}{q^2} = \frac{2\,(q - 10)(q + 10)}{q^2} .
$$
The derivative is negative for $0 < q < 10$ and positive for $q > 10$, so by the theorem on functions with a single stationary point the absolute minimum is at $q = 10$. The **minimum average cost** is therefore $\bar{C}(10) = 20 + 8 + 20 = 48$ euros. In agreement with the theorem on minimum average cost, at the same point marginal cost also equals $C'(10) = 4 \cdot 10 + 8 = 48$ euros.
