Among the right circular cones with slant height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the height of the cone with maximum volume?

- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

If $h$ is the height, the base radius satisfies $r^2 = 36 - h^2$ by the Pythagorean theorem, so the volume is
$$
V(h) = \frac{\pi}{3}\,(36 - h^2)\,h = \frac{\pi}{3}\,(36h - h^3), \qquad 0 \leq h \leq 6 .
$$
The derivative $V'(h) = \dfrac{\pi}{3}\,(36 - 3h^2)$ vanishes in $(0, 6)$ only for $h^2 = 12$, that is $h = 2\sqrt{3}$. Since $V(0) = V(6) = 0$ and $V(2\sqrt{3}) > 0$, the **maximum volume** is obtained with height $2\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $2\sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the base radius of this cone, because $r^2 = 36 - 12 = 24$.
