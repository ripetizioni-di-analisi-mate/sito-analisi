For the function $f(x) = (x + 1)\,e^{-x}$, what is the x-coordinate of the inflection point?

- $x = 0$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 2$
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

By the product rule we have $f'(x) = e^{-x} - (x + 1)\,e^{-x} = -x\,e^{-x}$, and differentiating again
$$
f''(x) = -e^{-x} + x\,e^{-x} = (x - 1)\,e^{-x} .
$$
Since $e^{-x} > 0$, the sign of $f''$ is that of $x - 1$, negative for $x < 1$ and positive for $x > 1$. Hence $f''$ changes sign at $x = 1$, and by the sufficient condition for inflection points $x = 1$ is the **inflection point**, with $f(1) = \dfrac{2}{e}$. The point $x = 0$, instead, is the maximum point, where $f'$ vanishes, and $x = -1$ is the zero of $f$.
