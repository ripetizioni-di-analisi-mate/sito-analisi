Two positive numbers $x$ and $y$ have sum $12$. What is the maximum value of the product $x\,y^2$?

- $216$
- $128$
- $256$
- $243$

## Explanation

From the constraint we get $x = 12 - y$, so we must maximize
$$
P(y) = (12 - y)\,y^2 = 12y^2 - y^3, \qquad 0 \leq y \leq 12 .
$$
The derivative is $P'(y) = 24y - 3y^2 = 3y\,(8 - y)$, which on $(0, 12)$ vanishes only at $y = 8$. With the closed interval method we compare $P(0) = 0$, $P(12) = 0$ and $P(8) = 4 \cdot 64 = 256$. The **maximum product** is therefore $256$, with $x = 4$ and $y = 8$. The value $216$ corresponds to $x = y = 6$, and $128$ to $x = 8$, $y = 4$.
