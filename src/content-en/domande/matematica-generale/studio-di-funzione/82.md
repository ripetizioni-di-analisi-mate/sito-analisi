On what set is $\dfrac{x - 4}{x + 7}$ strictly positive?

- $\left(-7, 4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right) \cup \left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -7\right) \cup \left[4, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The two factors vanish at $4$ and $-7$. The quotient is positive where their signs agree, outside the interval between these values.
