What is the exact set of absolute minimizers of $\left|{x - 5}\right| + \left|{x - 2}\right|$?

- $\left\{2, 5\right\}$
- $\left(2, 5\right)$
- $\left[2, 5\right]$
- $\left\{\dfrac{7}{2}\right\}$

## Explanation

The function is the sum of distances from $2$ and $5$. That sum equals the distance between the points for every $x\in[2,5]$ and increases outside that segment.
