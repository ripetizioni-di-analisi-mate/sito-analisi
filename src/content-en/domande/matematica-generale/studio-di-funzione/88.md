Profit is $\pi(q)=- 4 q^{2} + 56 q - 9$, where the output is a real number satisfying $0\leq q\leq 6$. What output maximizes profit?

- $6$
- $7$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

The vertex of the parabola is at $q=7$. The derivative is positive throughout the feasible interval, so the maximum is at the right endpoint.
