For a firm with differentiable cost $C(q)$, at $q = 4$ marginal cost equals $C'(4) = 10$ euros and average cost equals $\bar{C}(4) = 15$ euros. Which statement is true?

- Average cost is increasing at $q = 4$
- Average cost has its minimum at $q = 4$
- Average cost is decreasing at $q = 4$
- Total cost is decreasing at $q = 4$

## Explanation

The derivative of average cost $\bar{C}(q) = \dfrac{C(q)}{q}$ is
$$
\bar{C}'(q) = \frac{1}{q}\left( C'(q) - \bar{C}(q) \right) ,
$$
so $\bar{C}'(4) = \dfrac{10 - 15}{4} = -\dfrac{5}{4} < 0$. Therefore **average cost is decreasing** at $q = 4$. When marginal cost is less than average cost, the additional unit costs less than the average and lowers it. The minimum of average cost requires $C'(q) = \bar{C}(q)$, which does not hold at $q = 4$, and total cost is increasing because $C'(4) > 0$.
