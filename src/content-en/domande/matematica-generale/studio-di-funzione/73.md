Profit is $\pi(q)=- 3 q^{2} + 30 q - 7$, with real output $0\leq q\leq 4$. What output maximizes profit?

- $3$
- $0$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The vertex of the parabola is at $q=5$. The derivative is positive throughout the feasible interval, so the maximum is at the right endpoint.
