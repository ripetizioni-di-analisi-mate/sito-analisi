What is $f\prime(x)$ for $f(x)=x + \dfrac{3}{x - 2}$?

- $1 - \dfrac{3}{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}$
- $1 - \dfrac{3}{x - 2}$
- $1 + \dfrac{3}{\left(x - 2\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

The second term can be written as $3(x-2)^{-1}$. Its derivative is $-\dfrac{3}{(x-2)^2}$, to which the derivative of $x$ is added.
