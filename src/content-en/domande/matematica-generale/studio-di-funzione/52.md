On what set is $\dfrac{x - 2}{x + 3}$ strictly positive?

- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -3\right) \cup \left[2, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-3, 2\right)$

## Explanation

The two factors vanish at $2$ and $-3$. The quotient is positive where their signs agree, outside the interval between these values.
