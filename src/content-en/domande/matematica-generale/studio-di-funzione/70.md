What is $f\prime(x)$ for $f(x)=x + \dfrac{5}{x - 3}$?

- $1 - \dfrac{5}{x - 3}$
- $1 - \dfrac{5}{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}$
- $- \dfrac{5}{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}$
- $1 + \dfrac{5}{\left(x - 3\right)^{2}}$

## Explanation

The second term can be written as $5(x-3)^{-1}$. Its derivative is $-\dfrac{5}{(x-3)^2}$, to which the derivative of $x$ is added.
