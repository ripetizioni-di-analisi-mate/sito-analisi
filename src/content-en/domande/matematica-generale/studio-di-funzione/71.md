Where does $f\prime\prime(x)<0$ hold for $f(x)=\left(x - 3\right)^{3} + 5$?

- $\left(-\infty, -3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right]$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 3\right)$

## Explanation

The second derivative is $6(x-3)$. It is strictly negative only for x-coordinates below $3$.
