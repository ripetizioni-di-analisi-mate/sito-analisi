A collection point can be placed at kilometre $x$ on a road, with $0\leq x\leq 8$. The two users are at kilometres $0$ and $8$. Where should the point be placed to minimise the longer trip?

- $x=0$
- $x=4$
- $x=8$
- Every $x$ from $0$ to $8$.

## Explanation

The two trips have lengths $x$ and $8-x$. Up to kilometre $4$, the longer distance is $8-x$ and decreases. Beyond kilometre $4$, it is $x$ and increases. The longer trip is therefore shortest when the lengths are equal.
