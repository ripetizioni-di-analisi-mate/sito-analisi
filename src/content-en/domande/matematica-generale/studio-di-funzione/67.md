On what set is $\dfrac{x - 3}{x + 5}$ strictly positive?

- $\left(-5, 3\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left(3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, -5\right) \cup \left[3, \infty\right)$
- $\left(3, \infty\right)$

## Explanation

The two factors vanish at $3$ and $-5$. The quotient is positive where their signs agree, outside the interval between these values.
