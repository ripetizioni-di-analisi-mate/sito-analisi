The production cost is $C(q) = q^3 - 6q^2 + 20q$ euros, with $q > 0$. What is the minimum average cost?

- $8$ euros
- $11$ euros
- $20$ euros
- $12$ euros

## Explanation

The average cost is
$$
\bar{C}(q) = \frac{C(q)}{q} = q^2 - 6q + 20,
\qquad
\bar{C}'(q) = 2q - 6 = 2\,(q - 3) .
$$
The derivative is negative for $0 < q < 3$ and positive for $q > 3$, so by the theorem on functions with a single stationary point the absolute minimum is at $q = 3$. The **minimum average cost** equals $\bar{C}(3) = 9 - 18 + 20 = 11$ euros, and in agreement with the theorem on minimum average cost $C'(3) = 27 - 36 + 20 = 11$ euros. The value $8$ euros is the minimum of marginal cost $C'(q) = 3q^2 - 12q + 20$, attained at $q = 2$.
