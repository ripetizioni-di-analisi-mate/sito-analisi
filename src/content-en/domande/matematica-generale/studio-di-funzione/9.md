The profit of a firm is $\pi(q) = -q^3 + 9q^2 - 15q - 10$ euros, and the production capacity allows $0 \leq q \leq 4$. Which quantity maximizes the profit on the admissible interval?

- $q = 4$
- $q = 5$
- $q = 1$
- $q = 0$

## Explanation

The derivative is $\pi'(q) = -3q^2 + 18q - 15 = -3(q - 1)(q - 5)$, which vanishes at $q = 1$ and at $q = 5$. The point $q = 5$ does not belong to the interval $[0, 4]$, so it is not a solution of the problem. With the closed-interval method one compares the profits at the endpoints and at the interior stationary point, that is, $\pi(0) = -10$ euros, $\pi(1) = -17$ euros and $\pi(4) = 10$ euros. The largest value is $\pi(4)$, therefore the **maximum profit** is obtained with $q = 4$ and equals $10$ euros.
