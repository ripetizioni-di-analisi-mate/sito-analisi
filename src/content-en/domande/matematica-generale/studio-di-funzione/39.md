A rectangle has its base on the $x$-axis and its two upper vertices on the parabola $y = 12 - x^2$. What is the maximum area of the rectangle?

- $32$
- $16$
- $64$
- $24$

## Explanation

By the symmetry of the parabola the upper vertices are $(-x, 12 - x^2)$ and $(x, 12 - x^2)$, with $0 \leq x \leq 2\sqrt{3}$. The base measures $2x$ and the height $12 - x^2$, so the area is
$$
A(x) = 2x\,(12 - x^2) = 24x - 2x^3,
\qquad
A'(x) = 24 - 6x^2 = 6\,(2 - x)(2 + x) .
$$
On the interval $(0, 2\sqrt{3})$ the derivative vanishes only at $x = 2$. With the closed-interval method we compare $A(0) = 0$, $A(2\sqrt{3}) = 0$ and $A(2) = 4 \cdot 8 = 32$, therefore the **maximum area** is $32$. The value $16$ is obtained by taking $x$ as the base instead of $2x$.
