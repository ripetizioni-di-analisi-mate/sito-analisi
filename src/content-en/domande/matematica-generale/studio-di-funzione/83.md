A rectangular panel has perimeter $22$ cm. Its first side must be a whole multiple of $2$ cm and its second a whole multiple of $1$ cm. Which ordered pair maximises the area among the allowable designs?

- $(6,5)$ cm
- $(4,7)$ cm
- $(8,3)$ cm
- $(2,9)$ cm

## Explanation

For a first side of $x$ the area is $x(11-x)$. The vertex is at $5.5$. The nearest admissible even integer is $6$, giving a second side of $5$ and an area of $30$ square centimetres.
