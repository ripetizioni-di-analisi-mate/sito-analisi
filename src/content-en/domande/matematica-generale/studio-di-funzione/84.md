What is the exact set of absolute minimizers of $\left|{x - 9}\right| + \left|{x - 4}\right|$?

- $\left\{\dfrac{13}{2}\right\}$
- $\left[4, 9\right]$
- $\left\{4, 9\right\}$
- $\left(4, 9\right)$

## Explanation

The function is the sum of distances from $4$ and $9$. That sum equals the distance between the points for every $x\in[4,9]$ and increases outside that segment.
