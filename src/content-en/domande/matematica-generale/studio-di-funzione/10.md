A rectangular enclosure of area $50$ square meters has one side against a wall and the other three sides made of fencing. What is the minimum length of the fencing?

- $20\sqrt{2}$ meters
- $20$ meters
- $25$ meters
- $15$ meters

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the length in meters of each of the two sides perpendicular to the wall. The side parallel to the wall measures $\dfrac{50}{x}$, so the length of the fencing is
$$
L(x) = 2x + \frac{50}{x}, \qquad x > 0,
\qquad
L'(x) = 2 - \frac{50}{x^2} = \frac{2\,(x - 5)(x + 5)}{x^2} .
$$
The derivative is negative for $0 < x < 5$ and positive for $x > 5$, therefore by the theorem on functions with a single stationary point the absolute minimum is at $x = 5$. The parallel side then measures $10$ meters, and the **minimum fencing** is $L(5) = 10 + 10 = 20$ meters long.
