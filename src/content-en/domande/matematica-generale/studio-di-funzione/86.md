Where does $f\prime\prime(x)<0$ hold for $f(x)=\left(x - 4\right)^{3} + 7$?

- $\left(4, \infty\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right]$
- $\left(-\infty, -4\right)$
- $\left(-\infty, 4\right)$

## Explanation

The second derivative is $6(x-4)$. It is strictly negative only for x-coordinates below $4$.
