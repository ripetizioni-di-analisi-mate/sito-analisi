What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty\left(\dfrac1{n+3}-\dfrac1{n+4}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $0$

## Explanation

In the partial sum through $N$, the intermediate terms cancel, leaving $\dfrac1{4}-\dfrac1{N+4}$. Its second term tends to zero.
