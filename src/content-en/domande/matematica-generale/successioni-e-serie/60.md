A geometric progression has first term $4$ and a positive common ratio, and the sum of its first three terms is $52$. What is the common ratio?

- $4$
- $2\sqrt{3}$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

The first three terms are $4$, $4q$, $4q^2$, so the condition is $4 + 4q + 4q^2 = 52$. Dividing by $4$ gives $q^2 + q - 12 = 0$, that is, $(q + 4)(q - 3) = 0$.

The solutions are $q = -4$ and $q = 3$. Since the common ratio is positive, $q = 3$, and indeed $4 + 12 + 36 = 52$.

The value $2\sqrt{3}$ comes from forgetting the term $4q$, that is, from solving $4 + 4q^2 = 52$.
