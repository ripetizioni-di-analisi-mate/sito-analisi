How is the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{2 n + 5}{3 n + 2}$ classified?

- Diverges to $+\infty$
- Diverges to $-\infty$
- Converges to a finite sum
- Has bounded, oscillating partial sums

## Explanation

The general term tends to $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Since this limit is positive, the positive-term series diverges to $+\infty$.
