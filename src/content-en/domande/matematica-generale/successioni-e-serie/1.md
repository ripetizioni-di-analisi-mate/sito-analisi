An arithmetic progression has first term $4$ and common difference $7$. What is the sum of the first fifteen terms?

- $1590$
- $795$
- $847{,}5$
- $1530$

## Explanation

The fifteenth term is obtained by adding the common difference $14$ times to the first term, so $a_{15} = 4 + 14 \cdot 7 = 102$.

The sum of the first $n$ terms of an arithmetic progression is the number of terms times the mean of the first and last terms. Thus
$$
S_{15} = \frac{15\,(4 + 102)}{2} = \frac{15 \cdot 106}{2} = 795 .
$$
Consequently, the sum of the first fifteen terms is $795$.
