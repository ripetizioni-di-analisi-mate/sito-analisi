What is the sum of the first four terms of $a_n=9 \cdot 4^{1 - n}$ for $n\geq1$?

- $\dfrac{189}{16}$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{765}{256}$
- $\dfrac{765}{64}$

## Explanation

The first term is $9$ and the common ratio is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Sum powers of the ratio from zero through three and multiply by the first term.
