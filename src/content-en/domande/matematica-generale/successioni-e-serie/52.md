The points in the figure are the first eight terms of a sequence. Which general term matches the points?

::grafico{punti="1,0,pieno;2,1.5,pieno;3,0.6667,pieno;4,1.25,pieno;5,0.8,pieno;6,1.1667,pieno;7,0.8571,pieno;8,1.125,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=9 ymin=-1 ymax=2 nomi="n,aₙ"}

- $a_n = 1 - \dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}$
- $a_n = (-1)^n + \dfrac{1}{n}$
- $a_n = 1 + \dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}$
- $a_n = 1 + \dfrac{1}{n}$

## Explanation

From the figure $a_1 = 0$, $a_2 = \dfrac{3}{2}$, $a_3 = \dfrac{2}{3}$ and $a_4 = \dfrac{5}{4}$, so the terms lie alternately below and above $1$, at distance $\dfrac{1}{n}$ from $1$.

The formula $a_n = 1 + \dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}$ gives $1 - 1 = 0$, $1 + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, $1 - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, $1 + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{4}$, so it matches the points.

The formula $(-1)^n + \dfrac{1}{n}$ gives the same first two terms, but $a_3 = -1 + \dfrac{1}{3} = -\dfrac{2}{3}$, which is negative, while in the figure $a_3$ is positive. The formulas $1 - \dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}$ and $1 + \dfrac{1}{n}$ give $a_1 = 2$.
