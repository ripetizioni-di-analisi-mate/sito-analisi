A saver puts aside $50$ euros in the first month and every month increases the amount by the same sum. In $20$ months the saver accumulates $2900$ euros in total. By how much does the amount increase each month?

- $10$ euros
- $9.50$ euros
- $5$ euros
- $12$ euros

## Explanation

The monthly amounts form an arithmetic progression with $a_1 = 50$, and the total is the sum of the first $20$ terms.

By the formula for the sum of the terms of an arithmetic progression, $\dfrac{20\,(50 + a_{20})}{2} = 2900$, so $50 + a_{20} = 290$ and $a_{20} = 240$.

Since $a_{20} = a_1 + 19\,d$, one obtains $19\,d = 190$, therefore the amount increases by $d = 10$ euros per month.
