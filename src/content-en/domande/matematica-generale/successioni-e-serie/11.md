In the series $\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}(\dfrac13)^k$, terms after index $N$ are omitted. What is the smallest $N$ for which their sum is less than $\dfrac1{100}$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

The tail starts at $k=N+1$ and has ratio $\dfrac13$. Its sum is $\dfrac{1}{2\cdot3^N}$. This gives $\dfrac1{54}$ for $N=3$ and $\dfrac1{162}$ for $N=4$. Since the tail decreases, $4$ is the first admissible index.
