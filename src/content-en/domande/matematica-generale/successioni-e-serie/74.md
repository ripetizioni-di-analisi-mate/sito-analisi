For which of the following values of $x$ does the geometric series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty\left(\dfrac{x-5}{3}\right)^n$ converge?

- $9$
- $2$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

The series converges when the absolute value of the common ratio is less than one, so $2<x<8$. Both endpoints are excluded.
