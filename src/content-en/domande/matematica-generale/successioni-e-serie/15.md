A sequence is defined recursively by $a_1 = 3$ and $a_{n+1} = 2\,a_n - 4$. What is $a_4$?

- $0$
- $-12$
- $-4$
- $-32$

## Explanation

The terms are computed one after the other with the rule $a_{n+1} = 2\,a_n - 4$.

Starting with $a_1 = 3$ gives $a_2 = 2 \cdot 3 - 4 = 2$, then $a_3 = 2 \cdot 2 - 4 = 0$, and finally $a_4 = 2 \cdot 0 - 4 = -4$.

Therefore $a_4 = -4$, while $0$ is the third term.
