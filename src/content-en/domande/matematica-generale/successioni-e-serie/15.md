Which term of the sequence $a_n = \dfrac{n}{n + 2}$, with $n \geq 1$, equals $\dfrac{9}{10}$?

- $a_9$
- $a_8$
- $a_{18}$
- $a_{20}$

## Explanation

We look for the index $n$ such that $\dfrac{n}{n + 2} = \dfrac{9}{10}$.

Since $n + 2 > 0$, both sides can be multiplied by $10\,(n + 2)$, which gives $10n = 9n + 18$. Therefore $n = 18$.

Indeed $a_{18} = \dfrac{18}{20} = \dfrac{9}{10}$. The term $a_9 = \dfrac{9}{11}$ has numerator $9$ but denominator $11$, and the term $a_8 = \dfrac{8}{10}$ has denominator $10$ but numerator $8$.
