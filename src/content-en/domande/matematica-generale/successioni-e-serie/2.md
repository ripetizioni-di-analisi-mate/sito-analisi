In an arithmetic progression $a_5 = 20$ and $a_{12} = 48$. What is the sum of the first ten terms?

- $180$
- $440$
- $240$
- $220$

## Explanation

To go from $a_5$ to $a_{12}$ the common difference is added $7$ times, so $a_{12} - a_5 = 7\,d$. Since $a_{12} - a_5 = 28$, the common difference is $d = 4$.

By the formula for the general term, $a_5 = a_1 + 4\,d$, therefore $a_1 = 20 - 16 = 4$. Consequently, the tenth term is $a_{10} = a_1 + 9\,d = 40$.

By the formula for the sum of the terms of an arithmetic progression,
$$
S_{10} = \frac{10\,(4 + 40)}{2} = 220 .
$$
