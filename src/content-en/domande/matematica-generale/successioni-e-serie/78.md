The sequence $(b_k)$ converges to $3$ and has first term $b_1 = 7$. What is the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left( b_k - b_{k+1} \right)$?

- $4$
- $-4$
- $10$
- $7$

## Explanation

In the partial sum each term $-b_{k+1}$ cancels with the term $b_{k+1}$ of the next addend, so
$$
S_n = (b_1 - b_2) + (b_2 - b_3) + \dots + (b_n - b_{n+1}) = b_1 - b_{n+1} = 7 - b_{n+1} .
$$
The sequence $(b_{n+1})$ is the sequence $(b_n)$ shifted by one place, therefore it also tends to $3$.

Hence $S_n \to 7 - 3 = 4$, and the series converges with sum $4$. The value $-4$ comes from swapping the signs, that is, from adding $b_{k+1} - b_k$, and $7$ from neglecting the last term $-b_{n+1}$ of the partial sum.
