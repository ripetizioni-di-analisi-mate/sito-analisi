A company sells $1200$ units in the first year, and every year sales increase by $10\%$ compared with the previous year. How many units does it sell in total over the first three years?

- $3960$
- $1452$
- $3972$
- $2520$

## Explanation

Increasing by $10\%$ means multiplying by $1{,}1$, so the annual sales form a geometric progression with first term $1200$ and common ratio $1{,}1$. Sales over the three years are $1200$, $1320$ and $1452$.

By the formula for the sum of the terms of a geometric progression, the total is
$$
S_3 = 1200 \cdot \frac{1{,}1^3 - 1}{1{,}1 - 1} = 1200 \cdot \frac{0{,}331}{0{,}1} = 3972 .
$$
The value $3960$ would be obtained with a fixed increase of $120$ units per year, while in the third year the increase is $10\%$ of $1320$, that is, $132$ units.
