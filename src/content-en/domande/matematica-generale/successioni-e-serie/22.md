In an arithmetic progression $a_1 = 50$ and the common difference is $d = -3$. What is the first index $n$ for which $a_n < 0$?

- $n = 17$
- $n = 16$
- $n = 18$
- $n = 19$

## Explanation

The general term is $a_n = 50 - 3\,(n - 1)$.

The condition $a_n < 0$ is equivalent to $3\,(n - 1) > 50$, that is, to $n - 1 > \dfrac{50}{3} \approx 16.7$. The smallest integer that satisfies it is $n - 1 = 17$, so $n = 18$.

Indeed $a_{17} = 50 - 48 = 2$ is still positive, while $a_{18} = 50 - 51 = -1$.
