As $n\to\infty$, what is the limit of $\left(1 + \dfrac{4}{7 n}\right)^{9 n}$?

- $e^{\dfrac{4}{7}}$
- $e^{\dfrac{36}{7}}$
- $e^{\dfrac{9}{7}}$
- $e^{36}$

## Explanation

The logarithm of the sequence term is the exponent times the logarithm of the base. Applying $\ln(1+u)\sim u$ and then exponentiating gives the limit.
