The government spends $100$ million euros. The recipients spend $80\%$ of that amount, the recipients in the second round spend $80\%$ of what they receive, and so on for infinitely many rounds. What is the total spending, including the initial amount?

- $180$ million euros
- $125$ million euros
- $500$ million euros
- $400$ million euros

## Explanation

The successive amounts spent, in millions of euros, are $100$, $100 \cdot 0{,}8$, $100 \cdot 0{,}8^2$ and so on. The total spending is therefore the sum of the geometric series with first term $100$ and common ratio $q = 0{,}8$.

Since $-1 < q < 1$, the series converges and its sum is
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} 100 \cdot 0{,}8^{k} = \frac{100}{1 - 0{,}8} = \frac{100}{0{,}2} = 500 .
$$
Therefore the total spending is $500$ million euros, five times the initial amount.
