In a sequence of spending rounds, the same fraction $r$ of each receipt is spent in the next round, with $0<r<1$. The first two expenditures together total $150$ and all expenditures, including the first, total $200$. What is $r$?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

After two rounds the remaining terms form a copy of the entire series scaled by $r^2$. The remaining expenditure is $200-150=50$, so $200r^2=50$. Taking the positive root gives $\dfrac12$.
