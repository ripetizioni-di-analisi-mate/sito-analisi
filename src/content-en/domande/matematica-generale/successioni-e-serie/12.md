## Explanation

The repeating block $45$ recurs shifted by two digits each time, so
$$
0.\overline{45} = \frac{45}{100} + \frac{45}{10\,000} + \frac{45}{1\,000\,000} + \dots
$$
This is a **geometric series** with first term $a = \dfrac{45}{100}$ and common ratio $q = \dfrac{1}{100}$, which converges because $-1 < q < 1$.

Its sum is
$$
\frac{\dfrac{45}{100}}{1 - \dfrac{1}{100}} = \frac{45}{100} \cdot \frac{100}{99} = \frac{45}{99} = \frac{5}{11} ,
$$
where the last equality is obtained by dividing the numerator and the denominator by $9$.
