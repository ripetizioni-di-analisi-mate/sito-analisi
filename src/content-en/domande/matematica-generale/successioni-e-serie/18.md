For the sequence $a_n = \dfrac{n}{2^n}$, with $n \geq 1$, which expression is equal to the ratio $\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}$?

- $\dfrac{2\,(n + 1)}{n}$
- $\dfrac{n + 1}{2n}$
- $\dfrac{n + 1}{2^n\,n}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The next term is $a_{n+1} = \dfrac{n + 1}{2^{n+1}}$. Dividing by $a_n$ gives
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{n + 1}{2^{n+1}} \cdot \frac{2^n}{n} = \frac{n + 1}{2n} ,
$$
because $\dfrac{2^n}{2^{n+1}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Since $n + 1 \leq 2n$ for every $n \geq 1$, the ratio is less than or equal to $1$, so the sequence is non-increasing. Equality holds only for $n = 1$, and indeed $a_1 = a_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
