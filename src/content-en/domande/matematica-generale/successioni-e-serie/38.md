The partial sums of a series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k$ are $S_n = \dfrac{3n}{n + 1}$. What is the term $a_4$?

- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $3$
- $-\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

Each term is the difference between two consecutive partial sums, so $a_4 = S_4 - S_3$.

Since $S_4 = \dfrac{12}{5}$ and $S_3 = \dfrac{9}{4}$, one obtains
$$
a_4 = \frac{12}{5} - \frac{9}{4} = \frac{48 - 45}{20} = \frac{3}{20} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{12}{5}$ is the sum of the first four terms, while $3$ is the limit of $S_n$, that is, the sum of the series.
