A capital of $1000$ euros is invested at an annual rate of $10\%$ with compound interest, so after $t$ years it is worth $1000 \cdot 1.1^{t}$ euros. After how many whole years does the capital exceed $2000$ euros for the first time?

- $7$
- $11$
- $20$
- $8$

## Explanation

The values of the capital at the end of each year form a geometric progression with common ratio $1.1$, and the condition $1000 \cdot 1.1^{t} > 2000$ is equivalent to $1.1^{t} > 2$.

Since the logarithm is increasing, the inequality is equivalent to $t \ln 1.1 > \ln 2$, that is,
$$
t > \frac{\ln 2}{\ln 1.1} \approx \frac{0.6931}{0.0953} \approx 7.27 .
$$
Therefore the first integer is $t = 8$. Indeed after $7$ years the capital is worth about $1948.72$ euros and after $8$ years about $2143.59$ euros. The value $11$ corresponds to simple interest, under which the capital grows by $100$ euros per year.
