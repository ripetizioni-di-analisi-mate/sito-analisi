For the sequence $a_n = \dfrac{3n - 1}{n + 2}$, which expression is equal to the difference $a_{n+1} - a_n$?

- $\dfrac{1}{(n + 2)(n + 3)}$
- $-\dfrac{7}{(n + 2)(n + 3)}$
- $\dfrac{7}{(n + 2)^2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{(n + 2)(n + 3)}$

## Explanation

The next term is obtained by substituting $n + 1$ for $n$, so $a_{n+1} = \dfrac{3n + 2}{n + 3}$.

Bringing the fractions to a common denominator gives
$$
a_{n+1} - a_n = \frac{(3n + 2)(n + 2) - (3n - 1)(n + 3)}{(n + 2)(n + 3)} .
$$
The numerator is $(3n^2 + 8n + 4) - (3n^2 + 8n - 3) = 7$, so $a_{n+1} - a_n = \dfrac{7}{(n + 2)(n + 3)}$.

The difference is positive for every $n$, so the sequence is **increasing**. Its limit is $3$, the ratio of the coefficients of $n$.
