What is the sum of the first four terms of $a_n=5 \cdot 2^{1 - n}$ for $n\geq1$?

- $\dfrac{75}{8}$
- $\dfrac{75}{16}$
- $\dfrac{35}{4}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The first term is $5$ and the common ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The sum is the first term times the sum of the powers of the ratio with exponents zero through three.
