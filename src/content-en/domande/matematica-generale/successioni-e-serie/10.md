## Explanation

The series is a **geometric series** with common ratio $q = \dfrac{3}{4}$. The index starts at $k = 0$, so the first term is $2 \left( \dfrac{3}{4} \right)^{0} = 2$.

Since $-1 < q < 1$, the series converges. Its sum is the first term divided by $1 - q$, which gives
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} 2 \left( \frac{3}{4} \right)^{k} = \frac{2}{1 - \dfrac{3}{4}} = \frac{2}{\dfrac{1}{4}} = 8 .
$$
