What is the sum of the first four terms of $a_n=7 \cdot 3^{1 - n}$ for $n\geq1$?

- $\dfrac{21}{2}$
- $\dfrac{280}{81}$
- $\dfrac{91}{9}$
- $\dfrac{280}{27}$

## Explanation

The first term is $7$ and the common ratio is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The sum is the first term times the sum of the powers of the ratio with exponents zero through three.
