For which of the following values of $x$ does the geometric series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty\left(\dfrac{x-7}{4}\right)^n$ converge?

- $12$
- $11$
- $8$
- $3$

## Explanation

The series converges when the absolute value of the common ratio is less than one, so $3<x<11$. Both endpoints are excluded.
