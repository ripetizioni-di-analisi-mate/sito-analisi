What is the value of the sum $7 + 11 + 15 + \dots + 203$?

- $5250$
- $5145$
- $5355$
- $10\,500$

## Explanation

The addends form an arithmetic progression with first term $7$ and common difference $4$.

From the condition $a_n = 7 + 4\,(n - 1) = 203$ it follows that $n - 1 = 49$, so the sum contains $50$ terms.

The formula for the sum of an arithmetic progression gives
$$
S_{50} = \frac{50\,(7 + 203)}{2} = 25 \cdot 210 = 5250 .
$$
