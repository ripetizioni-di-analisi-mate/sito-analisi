Four numbers are inserted between $3$ and $23$ so that the six numbers, in order, form an arithmetic progression. What is the third of the inserted numbers?

- $15$
- $18$
- $13$
- $11$

## Explanation

The six numbers form an arithmetic progression with $a_1 = 3$ and $a_6 = 23$. Since $a_6 = a_1 + 5d$, we get $5d = 20$, so the common difference is $d = 4$.

The inserted numbers are therefore $7$, $11$, $15$, $19$, and the third one is $a_4 = 3 + 3 \cdot 4 = 15$.

The value $18$ comes from the common difference $5$, that is, from dividing $20$ by the number of inserted terms instead of the number of gaps between the six terms, and computing $3 + 3 \cdot 5$. The value $13$ is the mean of $3$ and $23$, which is not a term of the progression, because the number of terms is even.
