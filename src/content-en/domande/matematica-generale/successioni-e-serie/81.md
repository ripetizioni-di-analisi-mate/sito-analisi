For $a_n=7+\dfrac{9}{n+4}$ with $n\geq1$, what is the infimum of the terms?

- $0$
- $7$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac{44}{5}$

## Explanation

The fraction is positive and tends to zero. Every term exceeds $7$, and the terms can be made arbitrarily close to this bound by taking $n$ large enough.
