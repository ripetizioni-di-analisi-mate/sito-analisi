The series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k$ converges with sum $10$, and $a_1 = 3$, $a_2 = 2$. What is $\displaystyle\sum_{k=3}^{\infty} a_k$?

- $8$
- $7$
- $15$
- $5$

## Explanation

For $n \geq 3$ the partial sum of the series can be written as
$$
S_n = a_1 + a_2 + \sum_{k=3}^{n} a_k = 5 + \sum_{k=3}^{n} a_k .
$$
Hence the partial sums of the series starting at $k = 3$ are $S_n - 5$.

Since $S_n \to 10$, these partial sums tend to $10 - 5 = 5$, so $\displaystyle\sum_{k=3}^{\infty} a_k = 5$. Removing finitely many terms leaves the behavior of the series unchanged, and the sum decreases by the sum of the removed terms.
