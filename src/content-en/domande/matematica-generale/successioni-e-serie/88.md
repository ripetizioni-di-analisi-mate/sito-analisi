What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty\left(\dfrac1{n+4}-\dfrac1{n+5}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

In the partial sum through $N$, the intermediate terms cancel, leaving $\dfrac1{5}-\dfrac1{N+5}$. Its second term tends to zero.
