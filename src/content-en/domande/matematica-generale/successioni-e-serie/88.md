What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty\left(\dfrac1{n+4}-\dfrac1{n+5}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

In the sum through $N$ the intermediate terms cancel. What remains is $\dfrac1{5}-\dfrac1{N+5}$, whose second term tends to zero.
