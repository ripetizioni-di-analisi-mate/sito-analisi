In a series with terms $1,r,r^2,r^3,\ldots$, where $0<r<1$, the second, fourth and all other even-positioned terms are removed. What fraction of the original sum remains?

- $\dfrac{r}{1+r}$
- $1-r$
- $\dfrac{1}{1-r^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{1+r}$

## Explanation

If $A$ is the sum of the retained terms, the omitted terms sum to $rA$ because each is $r$ times the preceding retained term. The original sum is $(1+r)A$, giving the fraction $\dfrac{1}{1+r}$.
