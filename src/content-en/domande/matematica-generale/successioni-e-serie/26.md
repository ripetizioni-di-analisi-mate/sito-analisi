Which statement about the geometric progression with first term $a_1 = -5$ and common ratio $q = \dfrac{1}{3}$ is true?

- It is decreasing
- It is not monotonic
- It diverges to $-\infty$
- It is increasing

## Explanation

The first terms are $-5$, $-\dfrac{5}{3}$, $-\dfrac{5}{9}$, $-\dfrac{5}{27}$.

The difference between two consecutive terms is $a_{n+1} - a_n = a_n\,(q - 1) = -\dfrac{2}{3}\,a_n$. Since $a_n < 0$ for every $n$, the difference is positive, so the progression is **increasing**.

The condition $0 < q < 1$ gives a decreasing progression only when $a_1 > 0$. Moreover $-1 < q < 1$, therefore the terms tend to $0$.
