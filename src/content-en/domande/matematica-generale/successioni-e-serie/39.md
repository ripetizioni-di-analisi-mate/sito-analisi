For which of the following series does the necessary condition for convergence rule out convergence?

- $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k}$
- $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{k}$
- $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k\,(k + 1)}$
- $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{k}{2k + 1}$

## Explanation

The necessary condition states that if a series converges, its general term tends to $0$.

For the series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{k}{2k + 1}$ the general term tends to $\dfrac{1}{2} \neq 0$, so the series **does not converge**.

For the other three series, the general term tends to $0$. The necessary condition holds in all three cases, so it does not determine whether any of them converges. The harmonic series diverges, whereas the other two converge.
