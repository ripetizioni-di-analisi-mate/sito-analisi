An arithmetic progression starts at $5$ with common difference $3$. What is the sum of the first $7$ terms?

- $98$
- $35$
- $161$
- $\dfrac{217}{2}$

## Explanation

The last term is $5+(7-1)3$. Multiplying the mean of the first and last terms by the number of terms gives the sum.
