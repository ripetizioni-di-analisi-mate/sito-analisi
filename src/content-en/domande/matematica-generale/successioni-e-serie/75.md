How is the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{3 n + 7}{5 n + 3}$ classified?

- Has bounded, oscillating partial sums
- Diverges to $+\infty$
- Diverges to $-\infty$
- Converges to a finite sum

## Explanation

The general term tends to $\dfrac{3}{5}$. Since this limit is positive, the positive-term series diverges to $+\infty$.
