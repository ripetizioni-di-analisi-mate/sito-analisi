What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty 9\left(-\dfrac1{7}\right)^n$?

- $- \dfrac{9}{8}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$
- $\dfrac{63}{8}$

## Explanation

The common ratio is $-\dfrac1{7}$, and its absolute value is less than one. The sum is the first term $9$ divided by one minus the ratio.
