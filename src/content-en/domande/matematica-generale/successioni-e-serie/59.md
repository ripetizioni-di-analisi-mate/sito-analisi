For which of the following values of $x$ does the geometric series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty\left(\dfrac{x-3}{2}\right)^n$ converge?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

The series converges when the absolute value of the common ratio is less than one, so $1<x<5$. Both endpoints are excluded.
