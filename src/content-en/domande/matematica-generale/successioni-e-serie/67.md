Which statement about the following sequence is true?
$$
a_n =
\begin{cases}
n & \text{if } n \text{ is even} \\[4pt]
\dfrac{1}{n} & \text{if } n \text{ is odd}
\end{cases}
$$

- It diverges to $+\infty$
- It is oscillatory
- It converges to $0$
- It is bounded

## Explanation

The terms with even index $2, 4, 6, \dots$ exceed any threshold, so the sequence is not bounded and does not converge.

The terms with odd index $1, \dfrac{1}{3}, \dfrac{1}{5}, \dots$ are less than or equal to $1$. Therefore, with the threshold $M = 1$, beyond any index $N$ there is a term with odd index that does not exceed $M$, and the sequence does not diverge to $+\infty$.

Moreover the terms are positive, so the sequence does not diverge to $-\infty$. Since it neither converges nor diverges, the sequence is **oscillatory**.
