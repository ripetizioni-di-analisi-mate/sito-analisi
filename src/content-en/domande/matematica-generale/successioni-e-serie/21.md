A worker earns $24\,000$ euros in the first year and receives a fixed raise of $600$ euros every year. How much does the worker earn in total over the first $10$ years?

- $270\,000$ euros
- $534\,000$ euros
- $29\,400$ euros
- $267\,000$ euros

## Explanation

The annual salaries form an arithmetic progression with first term $24\,000$ and common difference $600$.

The tenth salary contains the raise $9$ times, so $a_{10} = 24\,000 + 9 \cdot 600 = 29\,400$ euros.

The total earnings are the sum of the first ten terms, therefore
$$
S_{10} = \frac{10\,(24\,000 + 29\,400)}{2} = 5 \cdot 53\,400 = 267\,000 \text{ euros} .
$$
