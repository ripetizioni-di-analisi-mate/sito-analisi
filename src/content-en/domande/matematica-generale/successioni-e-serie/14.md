At the end of each year, $300$ euros are deposited for $10$ years into an account at $2\%$ compound interest. What is the balance immediately after the last deposit?

- $3650.61$ euros
- $2694.78$ euros
- $3350.61$ euros
- $3284.92$ euros

## Explanation

The deposit made at the end of year $k$ remains in the account for $10 - k$ years, so at the time of the last deposit it is worth $300 \cdot 1.02^{\,10 - k}$ euros.

The last deposit earns no interest and the first earns interest for $9$ years, therefore the balance is the sum of the first ten terms of the geometric progression with first term $300$ and common ratio $1.02$, that is,
$$
300 + 300 \cdot 1.02 + \dots + 300 \cdot 1.02^{9} = 300 \cdot \frac{1 - 1.02^{10}}{1 - 1.02} = 300 \cdot \frac{1.02^{10} - 1}{0.02} .
$$
Since $1.02^{10} \approx 1.218994$, the balance is $300 \cdot 10.94972 \approx 3284.92$ euros.
