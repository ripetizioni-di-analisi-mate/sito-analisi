A geometric progression with positive terms has $a_2 = 6$ and $a_4 = 54$. What is $a_7$?

- $486$
- $324$
- $1458$
- $4374$

## Explanation

Going from $a_2$ to $a_4$ requires multiplying twice by the common ratio, so $q^2 = \dfrac{54}{6} = 9$.

The terms are positive, so the common ratio is $q = 3$.

Going from $a_4$ to $a_7$ requires multiplying three times by $q$, so $a_7 = 54 \cdot 3^3 = 54 \cdot 27 = 1458$.
