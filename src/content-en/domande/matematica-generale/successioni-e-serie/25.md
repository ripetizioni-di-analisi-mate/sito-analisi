In a geometric progression the fifth term is $a_5 = 6$. What is the product $a_2 \cdot a_8$?

- $12$
- $72$
- $36$
- $6$

## Explanation

The general term is $a_n = a_1\,q^{\,n-1}$, so $a_2 = a_1\,q$ and $a_8 = a_1\,q^{7}$.

The product is therefore
$$
a_2 \cdot a_8 = a_1^{2}\,q^{8} = \left( a_1\,q^{4} \right)^{2} = a_5^{2} ,
$$
because $a_5 = a_1\,q^{4}$. Hence $a_2 \cdot a_8 = 6^2 = 36$, whatever the common ratio.

The value $12$ is $a_5 + a_5$, that is, the property of equally spaced terms of arithmetic progressions, $a_2 + a_8 = 2\,a_5$, which does not hold for geometric progressions.
