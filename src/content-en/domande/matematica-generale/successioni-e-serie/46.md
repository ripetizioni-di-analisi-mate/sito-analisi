A sequence has $a_1 = 2$ and $a_2 = 3$, and every term from the third onward is the sum of the two terms before it, that is, $a_{n+2} = a_{n+1} + a_n$. What is $a_6$?

- $21$
- $13$
- $34$
- $55$

## Explanation

The rule says that every term from the third onward is the sum of the two terms preceding it. Therefore $a_3 = 3 + 2 = 5$ and $a_4 = 5 + 3 = 8$.

In the same way $a_5 = 8 + 5 = 13$, so $a_6 = 13 + 8 = 21$.

The value $13$ is the fifth term, while $34$ and $55$ are the seventh and the eighth: they come from miscounting the positions.
