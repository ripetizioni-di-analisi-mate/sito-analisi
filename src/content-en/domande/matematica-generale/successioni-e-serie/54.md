The side lengths of a right triangle, in centimeters, are consecutive terms of an arithmetic progression, and the perimeter is $36$ centimeters. How long is the hypotenuse?

- $15$ centimeters
- $12$ centimeters
- $18$ centimeters
- $9$ centimeters

## Explanation

Denote the three sides by $a - d$, $a$, $a + d$, with $d > 0$, so the hypotenuse, which is the longest side, measures $a + d$. The sum of the three sides is $3a$, therefore $3a = 36$ and $a = 12$.

By the Pythagorean theorem $(12 - d)^2 + 12^2 = (12 + d)^2$. Expanding the squares, the terms $144$ and $d^2$ cancel, so $144 = 48\,d$ and $d = 3$.

Therefore the sides measure $9$, $12$ and $15$ centimeters, and the hypotenuse measures $15$ centimeters. The value $12$ is the middle term, $9$ is the shorter leg and $18$ is half the perimeter.
