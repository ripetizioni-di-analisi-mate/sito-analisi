As $n\to\infty$, what is the limit of $\left(1 + \dfrac{2}{3 n}\right)^{5 n}$?

- $e^{\dfrac{2}{3}}$
- $e^{10}$
- $e^{\dfrac{10}{3}}$
- $e^{\dfrac{5}{3}}$

## Explanation

Take the logarithm, which is the exponent times the logarithm of the base. Use $\ln(1+u)\sim u$ and then exponentiate.
