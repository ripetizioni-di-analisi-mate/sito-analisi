How is the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty \dfrac{4 n + 9}{7 n + 4}$ classified?

- Converges to a finite sum
- Has bounded, oscillating partial sums
- Diverges to $-\infty$
- Diverges to $+\infty$

## Explanation

The general term tends to $\dfrac{4}{7}$. Since this limit is positive, the positive-term series diverges to $+\infty$.
