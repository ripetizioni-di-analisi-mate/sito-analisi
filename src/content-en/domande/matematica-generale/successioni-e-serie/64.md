The sequence $a_n = \dfrac{2n + 3}{n}$ tends to $2$. For which indices $n$ does $|a_n - 2| < \dfrac{1}{100}$ hold?

- $n > 100$
- $n > 30$
- $n > 300$
- $n > 3$

## Explanation

Over a common denominator,
$$
a_n - 2 = \frac{2n + 3 - 2n}{n} = \frac{3}{n} ,
$$
which is positive, so $|a_n - 2| = \dfrac{3}{n}$.

The inequality $\dfrac{3}{n} < \dfrac{1}{100}$ is equivalent to $n > 300$. Therefore, with the tolerance $\varepsilon = \dfrac{1}{100}$ in the definition of limit, one can take $N = 300$, because all terms with $n > 300$ are at a distance from $2$ of less than $\dfrac{1}{100}$.

The value $100$ comes from forgetting the $3$ in the numerator of $a_n - 2$.
