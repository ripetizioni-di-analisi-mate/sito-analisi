The sequence defined by $a_1 = 1$ and $a_{n+1} = \dfrac{a_n}{2} + 1$ is increasing and satisfies $a_n < 2$ for every $n$. Which statement is true?

- It diverges to $+\infty$
- It converges to $1$
- It converges to $2$
- It is oscillating

## Explanation

Since the sequence is increasing and $a_1 = 1$, we have $1 \leq a_n < 2$ for every $n$, so the sequence is bounded. By the **monotone sequence theorem** an increasing bounded sequence converges, therefore there is a real number $L$ such that $a_n \to L$.

The sequence $a_{n+1}$ also tends to $L$, because it is the same sequence shifted by one place. Passing to the limit on both sides of the recurrence relation, by the rules for computing limits one obtains $L = \dfrac{L}{2} + 1$.

From this equation one finds $\dfrac{L}{2} = 1$, so $L = 2$, and indeed the first terms $1$, $\dfrac{3}{2}$, $\dfrac{7}{4}$, $\dfrac{15}{8}$ approach $2$.
