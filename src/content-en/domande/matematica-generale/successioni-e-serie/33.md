The sequence defined by $a_1 = 1$ and $a_{n+1} = \dfrac{a_n}{2} + 1$ is increasing and satisfies $a_n < 2$ for every $n$. Which statement is true?

- It diverges to $+\infty$
- It converges to $1$
- It converges to $2$
- It is oscillating

## Explanation

The sequence is increasing and bounded, because $1 \leq a_n < 2$ for every $n$. By the **monotone sequence theorem** the sequence converges to a number $L$.

The sequence $a_{n+1}$ also tends to $L$, because it is the same sequence shifted by one place, so passing to the limit in the recurrence relation gives $L = \dfrac{L}{2} + 1$.

Therefore $L = 2$, and indeed the first terms $1$, $\dfrac{3}{2}$, $\dfrac{7}{4}$, $\dfrac{15}{8}$ approach $2$.
