What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty\left(\dfrac1{n+2}-\dfrac1{n+3}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $0$

## Explanation

In the sum through $N$ the intermediate terms cancel. What remains is $\dfrac1{3}-\dfrac1{N+3}$, whose second term tends to zero.
