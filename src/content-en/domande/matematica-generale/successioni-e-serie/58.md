What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty\left(\dfrac1{n+2}-\dfrac1{n+3}\right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $0$

## Explanation

In the partial sum through $N$, the intermediate terms cancel, leaving $\dfrac1{3}-\dfrac1{N+3}$. Its second term tends to zero.
