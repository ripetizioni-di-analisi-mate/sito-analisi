In the long division for $\dfrac1{13}$, the starting remainder first reappears after six digits. What is the smallest integer $k>0$ for which $\dfrac{10^k-1}{13}$ is an integer?

- $13$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

At each digit the previous remainder is multiplied by $10$ and reduced modulo $13$. Returning to the starting remainder $1$ means $10^k-1$ is divisible by $13$. The first return occurs at $k=6$.
