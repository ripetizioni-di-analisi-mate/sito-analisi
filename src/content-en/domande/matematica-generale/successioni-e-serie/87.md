Which fraction equals the repeating decimal $0.1\overline{6} = 0.1666\ldots$?

- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{23}{30}$
- $\dfrac{8}{75}$

## Explanation

The number can be written as $\dfrac{1}{10} + \dfrac{6}{100} + \dfrac{6}{1000} + \dots$, where the digit $1$ does not repeat and the digits $6$ form a geometric series.

The series $\dfrac{6}{100} + \dfrac{6}{1000} + \dots$ has first term $\dfrac{6}{100}$ and common ratio $\dfrac{1}{10}$, so its sum is
$$
\frac{\frac{6}{100}}{1 - \frac{1}{10}} = \frac{6}{100} \cdot \frac{10}{9} = \frac{1}{15} .
$$
Therefore $0.1\overline{6} = \dfrac{1}{10} + \dfrac{1}{15} = \dfrac{3 + 2}{30} = \dfrac{1}{6}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{15}$ leaves out the digit $1$, and $\dfrac{23}{30}$ starts the digits $6$ at the first decimal place.
