What is the tail sum $\displaystyle\sum_{n=3}^\infty \left(\dfrac{4}{7}\right)^n$?

- $\dfrac{256}{1029}$
- $\dfrac{64}{147}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{16}{21}$

## Explanation

Factor out the first term, which is the cube of the ratio. The remaining geometric series starts at the zeroth power.
