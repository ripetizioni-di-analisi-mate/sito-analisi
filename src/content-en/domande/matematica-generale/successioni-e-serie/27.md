A car is worth $20\,000$ euros today and every year it loses $15\%$ of the value it had the previous year. What is it worth after $4$ years?

- $8000$ euros
- $10\,440.13$ euros
- $8874.11$ euros
- $12\,282.50$ euros

## Explanation

Losing $15\%$ of the value means multiplying it by $1 - 0.15 = 0.85$, so the values year after year form a geometric progression with common ratio $0.85$.

After $4$ years the value has been multiplied four times by $0.85$, therefore it is $20\,000 \cdot 0.85^4$.

Since $0.85^2 = 0.7225$ and $0.7225^2 = 0.52200625$, the value is $20\,000 \cdot 0.52200625 \approx 10\,440.13$ euros. The value $8000$ euros corresponds to a fixed loss of $3000$ euros per year, that is, to an arithmetic progression.
