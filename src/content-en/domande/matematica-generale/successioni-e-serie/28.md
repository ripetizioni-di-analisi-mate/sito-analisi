What is the value of the sum $1 - 2 + 4 - 8 + \dots + 256$?

- $171$
- $511$
- $-85$
- $-341$

## Explanation

The addends are the terms of the geometric progression with first term $1$ and common ratio $q = -2$.

Since $256 = (-2)^8$, the last addend is the term in position $9$, so there are $9$ addends.

By the formula for the sum of the terms of a geometric progression,
$$
S_9 = \frac{1 - (-2)^9}{1 - (-2)} = \frac{1 + 512}{3} = 171 .
$$
