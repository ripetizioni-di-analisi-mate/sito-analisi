All that is known about an arithmetic progression is that its third and fifteenth terms add up to $40$. What is $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_{17}$?

- $680$
- $170$
- $340$
- $20$

## Explanation

By the formula for the general term $a_3 = a_1 + 2d$ and $a_{15} = a_1 + 14d$, so $a_3 + a_{15} = 2a_1 + 16d$.

Also $a_1 + a_{17} = a_1 + (a_1 + 16d) = 2a_1 + 16d$, so $a_1 + a_{17} = 40$, without needing to know $a_1$ and $d$ separately.

By the sum formula
$$
S_{17} = \frac{17\,(a_1 + a_{17})}{2} = \frac{17 \cdot 40}{2} = 340 .
$$
The value $680$ comes from forgetting to divide by $2$, and $20$ is the middle term $a_9$.
