What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty 5\left(-\dfrac1{3}\right)^n$?

- $5$
- $- \dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}$

## Explanation

The common ratio is $-\dfrac1{3}$, and its absolute value is less than one. The sum is the first term $5$ divided by one minus the ratio.
