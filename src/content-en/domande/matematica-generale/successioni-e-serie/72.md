What is the tail sum $\displaystyle\sum_{n=3}^\infty \left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^n$?

- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{27}{50}$
- $\dfrac{81}{250}$

## Explanation

Factor out the first term, which is the cube of the ratio. The remaining geometric series starts at the zeroth power.
