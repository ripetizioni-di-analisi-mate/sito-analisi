What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty 7\left(-\dfrac1{5}\right)^n$?

- $\dfrac{35}{6}$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{35}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{7}{6}$

## Explanation

The common ratio is $-\dfrac1{5}$, and its absolute value is less than one. The sum is the first term $7$ divided by one minus the ratio.
