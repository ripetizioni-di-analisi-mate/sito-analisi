A debt of $12\,000$ euros is repaid over $6$ years. At the end of each year one pays a fixed installment of $2000$ euros plus $5\%$ interest on the debt still outstanding at the beginning of that year. How much interest is paid in total?

- $3600$ euros
- $1500$ euros
- $4200$ euros
- $2100$ euros

## Explanation

At the beginning of the six years the debt is $12\,000$, $10\,000$, $\dots$, $2000$ euros, because each year it decreases by the fixed installment of $2000$ euros. Therefore the interest payments, equal to $5\%$ of these values, are $600$, $500$, $400$, $300$, $200$, $100$ euros, that is, an arithmetic progression with common difference $-100$.

By the sum formula the total interest is
$$
S_6 = \frac{6\,(600 + 100)}{2} = 2100 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $3600$ euros comes from always computing the interest on the initial debt, and $1500$ euros from computing it on the debt at the end of each year.
