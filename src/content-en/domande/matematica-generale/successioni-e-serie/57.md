What is the tail sum $\displaystyle\sum_{n=3}^\infty \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^n$?

- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\dfrac{16}{27}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

Factoring out the first term, which is the cube of the common ratio, leaves a geometric series that starts at the zeroth power.
