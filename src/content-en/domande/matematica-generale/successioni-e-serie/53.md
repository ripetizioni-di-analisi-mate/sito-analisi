The points in the figure are the first five terms of an arithmetic progression. What is $a_{10}$?

::grafico{punti="1,11,pieno;2,8,pieno;3,5,pieno;4,2,pieno;5,-1,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-4 ymax=14 nomi="n,aₙ"}

- $38$
- $-19$
- $-13$
- $-16$

## Explanation

From the figure $a_1 = 11$ and $a_2 = 8$, so the common difference is $d = 8 - 11 = -3$; indeed each point lies $3$ units below the previous one.

By the formula for the general term $a_n = a_1 + (n - 1)\,d$,
$$
a_{10} = 11 + 9 \cdot (-3) = 11 - 27 = -16 .
$$
The value $-19$ comes from multiplying the common difference by $10$ instead of $9$, the value $-13$ is $a_9$, and $38$ comes from the common difference $+3$.
