For $a_n=3+\dfrac{5}{n+2}$ with $n\geq1$, what is the infimum of the terms?

- $3$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $\dfrac{14}{3}$

## Explanation

The fraction is positive and tends to zero. Every term exceeds $3$, and the terms can be made arbitrarily close to this bound by taking $n$ large enough.
