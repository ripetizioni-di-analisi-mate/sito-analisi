As $n\to\infty$, what is the limit of $\left(1 + \dfrac{3}{5 n}\right)^{7 n}$?

- $e^{\dfrac{3}{5}}$
- $e^{21}$
- $e^{\dfrac{7}{5}}$
- $e^{\dfrac{21}{5}}$

## Explanation

Take the logarithm, which is the exponent times the logarithm of the base. Use $\ln(1+u)\sim u$ and then exponentiate.
