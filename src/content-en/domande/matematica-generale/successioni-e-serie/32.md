What is $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{2^n - 5^{n+1}}{3 \cdot 5^n + 4^n}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Since $5^{n+1} = 5 \cdot 5^n$, the power that grows fastest is $5^n$, both in the numerator and in the denominator. Factoring out $5^n$ gives
$$
\frac{2^n - 5 \cdot 5^n}{3 \cdot 5^n + 4^n} = \frac{\left( \dfrac{2}{5} \right)^{n} - 5}{3 + \left( \dfrac{4}{5} \right)^{n}} .
$$
The ratios $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\dfrac{4}{5}$ lie between $-1$ and $1$. By the limit law for geometric sequences, $\left( \dfrac{2}{5} \right)^{n}$ and $\left( \dfrac{4}{5} \right)^{n}$ tend to $0$.

Therefore the limit is $-\dfrac{5}{3}$.
