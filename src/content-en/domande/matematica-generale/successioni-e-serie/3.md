- $10{,}000$
- $20{,}000$
- $9801$
- $19{,}900$

## Explanation

The odd numbers form an arithmetic progression with first term $1$ and common difference $2$.

The hundredth term is $a_{100} = 1 + 99 \cdot 2 = 199$, so the sum $1 + 3 + \dots + 199$ contains exactly $100$ terms.

By the formula for the sum of the terms of an arithmetic progression, one obtains
$$
S_{100} = \frac{100\,(1 + 199)}{2} = \frac{100 \cdot 200}{2} = 10{,}000 .
$$
The result is $100^2$; in general, the sum of the first $n$ odd numbers is $n^2$.
