A geometric progression has first term $3$ and common ratio $2$. What is the sum of the first ten terms?

- $1023$
- $6141$
- $3069$
- $3072$

## Explanation

Since the common ratio is $q = 2 \neq 1$, the formula for the sum of the terms of a geometric progression applies with $a_1 = 3$ and $n = 10$.

The exponent of $q$ in the formula is the number of terms, so
$$
S_{10} = 3 \cdot \frac{1 - 2^{10}}{1 - 2} = 3 \cdot \frac{1 - 1024}{-1} = 3 \cdot 1023 = 3069 .
$$
Therefore the sum of the first ten terms is $3069$, while the last term added is $a_{10} = 3 \cdot 2^{9} = 1536$.
