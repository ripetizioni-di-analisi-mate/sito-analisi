A firm considers shifting a small amount of output from market A to market B. Total capacity is already in use. At current quantities the marginal profits are €5 per unit in A and €8 in B. What is the first-order profit gain per unit shifted?

- €13
- €8
- €5
- €3

## Explanation

Shifting an amount $h$ loses about $5h$ in A and gains about $8h$ in B. The total first-order change is $3h$, giving a gain of €3 per unit shifted.
