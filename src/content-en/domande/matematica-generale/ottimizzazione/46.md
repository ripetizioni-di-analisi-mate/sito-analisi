Where does $\left(x - 2\right)^{2} + 5 \left(y - 3\right)^{2}$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( -2, \  -3\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  2\right)$

## Explanation

Both squares are nonnegative. The minimum value is zero, attained when both squares vanish.
