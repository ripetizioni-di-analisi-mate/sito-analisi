Which point on the line $x+y=15$ minimizes $\left(x - 2\right)^{2} + \left(y - 3\right)^{2}$?

- $\left( 7, \  8\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 2, \  13\right)$
- $\left( 12, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

Substitute $y=15-x$ to get an upward-opening parabola. Its derivative vanishes when both deviations from $2$ and $3$ equal $5$.
