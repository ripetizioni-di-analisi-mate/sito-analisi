A consumer maximizes $U(x,y)=xy$ subject to $4x+7y=504$ and $x,y\geq0$. What quantity $x$ is chosen?

- $9$
- $126$
- $63$
- $36$

## Explanation

Substituting the budget constraint gives a downward-opening parabola with zeros at $0$ and $126$. The vertex’s x-coordinate is halfway between the zeros.
