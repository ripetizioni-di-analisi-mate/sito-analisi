A firm splits an advertising budget of $500$ euros between two channels. Spending $x$ euros on the first and $y$ euros on the second, the sales are $S(x, y) = 10\sqrt{x} + 20\sqrt{y}$. Which split maximizes sales?

- $x = 250$, $y = 250$
- $x = 400$, $y = 100$
- $x = 100$, $y = 400$
- $x = \dfrac{500}{3}$, $y = \dfrac{1000}{3}$

## Explanation

The Lagrange system with the constraint $x + y = 500$ gives $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{x}} = \lambda$ and $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt{y}} = \lambda$. Equating the two expressions of $\lambda$ we obtain $\sqrt{y} = 2\sqrt{x}$, that is, $y = 4x$. The constraint becomes $5x = 500$, so $x = 100$ and $y = 400$, with sales $S = 100 + 400 = 500$. On the constraint the function $\varphi(x) = 10\sqrt{x} + 20\sqrt{500 - x}$ has derivative $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{x}} - \dfrac{10}{\sqrt{500 - x}}$, positive for $x < 100$ and negative for $x > 100$, so the point found is the **maximum**. In the optimal split the last euro spent increases sales by the same amount in both channels.
