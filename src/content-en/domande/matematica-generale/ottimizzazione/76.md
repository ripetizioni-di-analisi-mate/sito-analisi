Where does $\left(x - 4\right)^{2} + 9 \left(y - 7\right)^{2}$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $\left( 7, \  4\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  7\right)$
- $\left( -4, \  -7\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

Both squares are nonnegative. The minimum value is zero, attained when both squares vanish.
