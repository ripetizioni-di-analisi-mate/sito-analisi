From the filled square shown, given by $|x|+|y|\leq1$, only the vertex $(1,0)$ is removed. Consider $f(x,y)=x$ on the remaining set. Which conclusion is correct?

::grafico{poligoni="1,0 0,1 -1,0 0,-1" punti="1,0,vuoto" xmin=-1.6 xmax=1.6 ymin=-1.6 ymax=1.6 uguale=1 legenda=0 titolo="The open circle marks the excluded vertex."}

- The maximum is $1$.
- The maximum is $0$.
- The function is unbounded above.
- The supremum is $1$ and is not attained.

## Explanation

The excluded vertex is the square’s only point with $x=1$. For every integer $n\geq2$, the point $(1-\dfrac1n,0)$ still belongs to the set. Its values approach $1$ arbitrarily closely without attaining it.
