For $f(x,y)=3x+5y$ on $x^2+y^2=49$, which equation follows from the Lagrange multiplier condition $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$ with $g=x^2+y^2$?

- $3 x = 5 y$
- $5 x = - 3 y$
- $5 x = 3 y$
- $x = y$

## Explanation

The gradients are $(3,5)$ and $(2x,2y)$. Eliminating the multiplier gives $5x=3y$.
