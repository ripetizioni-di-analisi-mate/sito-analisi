For the function $f(x, y) = 2x^2 - 2xy + y^2 - 2x$, what is the value of $f$ at its minimum point?

- $1$
- $0$
- $-1$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = 4x - 2y - 2$ and $f_y = -2x + 2y$. The condition $f_y = 0$ gives $y = x$, and substituting into $f_x = 0$ gives $2x - 2 = 0$, so the only stationary point is $(1, 1)$. The second derivatives are $f_{xx} = 4$, $f_{xy} = -2$ and $f_{yy} = 2$, therefore $\det H = 8 - 4 = 4 > 0$. Since moreover $f_{xx} = 4 > 0$, by the **Hessian test** the point $(1, 1)$ is a minimum point. The minimum value is therefore
$$
f(1, 1) = 2 - 2 + 1 - 2 = -1 .
$$
