Let $f$ be a function with continuous second partial derivatives and let $P_0$ be a stationary point of $f$, with $f_{xx}(P_0) = 3$, $f_{xy}(P_0) = 4$ and $f_{yy}(P_0) = 5$. What can be concluded about $P_0$?

- $P_0$ is a relative minimum point
- $P_0$ is a relative maximum point
- The Hessian test does not allow a conclusion
- $P_0$ is a saddle point

## Explanation

The determinant of the Hessian matrix at $P_0$ is
$$
\det H(P_0) = f_{xx}(P_0)\,f_{yy}(P_0) - f_{xy}(P_0)^2 = 15 - 16 = -1 .
$$
Since $\det H(P_0) < 0$, by the **Hessian test** $P_0$ is a **saddle point**. The positive sign of $f_{xx}(P_0)$ and of $f_{yy}(P_0)$ is not enough for a minimum, because the test also requires $\det H(P_0) > 0$.
