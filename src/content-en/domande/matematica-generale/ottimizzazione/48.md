Which of the following points is an absolute maximum point of $f(x, y) = \sin x + \cos y$?

- $\left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}, 0\right)$
- $(0, 0)$

## Explanation

Since $\sin x \leq 1$ and $\cos y \leq 1$ for every $x$ and $y$, we have $f(x, y) \leq 2$ at every point of the plane. At the point $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}, 0\right)$ we have $\sin \dfrac{\pi}{2} = 1$ and $\cos 0 = 1$, so $f = 2$. The value $2$ is therefore the **absolute maximum**, and $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}, 0\right)$ is an absolute maximum point. At the other three points $f$ equals $0$, $0$ and $1$, all smaller than $2$.
