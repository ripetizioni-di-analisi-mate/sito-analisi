A firm sells a good at the price of $12$ euros and produces it in two plants, with costs $C_1(q_1) = q_1^2$ and $C_2(q_2) = 2q_2^2$. How many units does it produce in the second plant to maximize profit?

- $6$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The profit is $\pi(q_1, q_2) = 12\,(q_1 + q_2) - q_1^2 - 2q_2^2$. The first-order conditions are $12 - 2q_1 = 0$ and $12 - 4q_2 = 0$, that is, in each plant the marginal cost equals the price. Hence $q_1 = 6$ and $q_2 = 3$. The Hessian is $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 0 \\ 0 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$, with determinant $8 > 0$ and $\pi_{q_1 q_1} = -2 < 0$, so the point is a **maximum** point. The second plant, which has the higher marginal cost $4q_2$, produces $3$ units.
