Which point on the line $x+y=22$ minimizes $\left(x - 3\right)^{2} + \left(y - 5\right)^{2}$?

- $\left( 3, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 17, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  19\right)$
- $\left( 10, \  12\right)$

## Explanation

Substitute $y=22-x$ to get an upward-opening parabola. Its derivative vanishes when both deviations from $3$ and $5$ equal $7$.
