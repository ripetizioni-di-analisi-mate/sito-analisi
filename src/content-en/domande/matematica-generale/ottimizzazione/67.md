For which of the following functions does the Hessian test fail to classify the stationary point $(0, 0)$?

- $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$
- $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^4$
- $f(x, y) = x^2 - y^2$
- $f(x, y) = x^2 + xy + y^2$

## Explanation

For $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^4$ the second derivatives at the origin are $f_{xx} = 2$, $f_{xy} = 0$ and $f_{yy} = 12y^2 = 0$, so $\det H(0, 0) = 0$ and the test **is inconclusive**. For the other three functions the determinant equals $2 \cdot 2 = 4$, $2 \cdot (-2) = -4$ and $2 \cdot 2 - 1^2 = 3$, always different from $0$. For $x^2 + y^4$ the origin is classified directly, because $f(x, y) > 0 = f(0, 0)$ for every $(x, y) \neq (0, 0)$, so it is an absolute minimum point.
