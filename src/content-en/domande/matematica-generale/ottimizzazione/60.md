For real quantities $x,y\geq0$, profit is $\pi(x,y)=- 2 x^{2} + 12 x - y^{2} + 10 y$. Which pair maximizes it?

- $\left( 3, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  10\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  3\right)$

## Explanation

Both squared terms have negative coefficients and there is no cross term. Setting both partial derivatives to zero gives $x=3$ and $y=5$.
