How is the stationary point $(4,7)$ of $\left(x - 4\right)^{2} - 9 \left(y - 7\right)^{2}$ classified?

- Strict local minimum
- Absolute minimum
- Saddle point
- Strict local maximum

## Explanation

Along the horizontal line through the point the change is a positive square. Along the vertical line it is a negative multiple of a square, so the point is a saddle.
