For $f(x,y)=4x+7y$ on $x^2+y^2=81$, which equation follows from the Lagrange multiplier condition $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$ with $g=x^2+y^2$?

- $7 x = - 4 y$
- $x = y$
- $4 x = 7 y$
- $7 x = 4 y$

## Explanation

The gradients are $(4,7)$ and $(2x,2y)$. Eliminating the multiplier gives $7x=4y$.
