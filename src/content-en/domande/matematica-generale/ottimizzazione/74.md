A bundle satisfies $x+y=7$ and $x,y\geq0$. If cost is $3x+5y$, which bundle minimizes cost?

- $\left( \dfrac{7}{2}, \  \dfrac{7}{2}\right)$
- $\left( 7, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 6, \  1\right)$

## Explanation

After substituting $y=7-x$, the coefficient of $x$ in the cost is $-2$. Cost falls as $x$ increases, so the minimum is at the endpoint with the most units of the first good.
