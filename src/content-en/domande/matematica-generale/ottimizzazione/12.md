A firm in perfect competition sells two goods at prices of $32$ and $22$ euros, with cost $C(q_1, q_2) = q_1^2 + q_1 q_2 + q_2^2$. Which quantities maximize the profit?

- $q_1 = 16$, $q_2 = 11$
- $q_1 = 10$, $q_2 = 12$
- $q_1 = 4$, $q_2 = 14$
- $q_1 = 14$, $q_2 = 4$

## Explanation

The profit is $\pi(q_1, q_2) = 32q_1 + 22q_2 - q_1^2 - q_1 q_2 - q_2^2$, and the first-order conditions are
$$
\begin{cases}
\pi_{q_1} = 32 - 2q_1 - q_2 = 0 \\[4pt]
\pi_{q_2} = 22 - q_1 - 2q_2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
From the first equation we obtain $q_2 = 32 - 2q_1$, and substituting into the second gives $3q_1 - 42 = 0$, so $q_1 = 14$ and $q_2 = 4$. The Hessian is $\begin{pmatrix} -2 & -1 \\ -1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$, with $\det H = 3 > 0$ and $\pi_{q_1 q_1} = -2 < 0$, therefore by the **Hessian test** the point $(14, 4)$ is a maximum point. The maximum profit is $268$ euros.
