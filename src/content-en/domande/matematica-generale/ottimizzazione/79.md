How is $(4,7)$ classified for $\left(x - 4\right)^{4} + \left(y - 7\right)^{2}$?

- Strict local maximum
- Strict absolute minimum
- Nonstationary point
- Saddle point

## Explanation

The Hessian determinant vanishes at the point. The function is a sum of two even powers and equals zero only at that point.
