An open box (without a lid) has a square base with side $x$ and height $h$, measured in decimeters, and its volume is $32$ cubic decimeters. How many square decimeters is the minimum surface area of the box, that is, the sum of the areas of the base and of the four lateral faces?

- $80$
- $48$
- $64$
- $32$

## Explanation

We look for the minimum of $S(x, h) = x^2 + 4xh$ subject to the constraint $x^2 h = 32$, with $x, h > 0$. From the constraint we get $h = \dfrac{32}{x^2}$, so
$$
\varphi(x) = x^2 + \frac{128}{x},
\qquad
\varphi'(x) = 2x - \frac{128}{x^2} .
$$
The derivative vanishes for $x^3 = 64$, that is, $x = 4$; it is negative for $0 < x < 4$ and positive for $x > 4$, therefore $x = 4$ is the absolute minimum point of $\varphi$. Then $h = \dfrac{32}{16} = 2$, and the minimum surface area is $\varphi(4) = 16 + 32 = 48$ square decimeters.
