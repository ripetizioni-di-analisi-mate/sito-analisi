A bundle satisfies $x+y=5$ and $x,y\geq0$. If cost is $2x+3y$, which bundle minimizes cost?

- $\left( 5, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  1\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{5}{2}, \  \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  5\right)$

## Explanation

After substituting $y=5-x$, the coefficient of $x$ in the cost is $-1$. Cost falls as $x$ increases, so the minimum is at the endpoint with the most units of the first good.
