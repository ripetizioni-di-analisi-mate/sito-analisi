A bundle satisfies $x+y=9$ and $x,y\geq0$. If cost is $4x+7y$, which bundle minimizes cost?

- $\left( 8, \  1\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  9\right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{9}{2}, \  \dfrac{9}{2}\right)$
- $\left( 9, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

After substituting $y=9-x$, the coefficient of $x$ in the cost is $-3$. Cost falls as $x$ increases, so the minimum is at the endpoint with the most units of the first good.
