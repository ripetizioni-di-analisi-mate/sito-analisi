A firm sells its product at a price of $4$ euros, has production function $Q = L^{\frac{1}{2}} K^{\frac{1}{4}}$ and pays $1$ euro for each unit of labor and of capital. The profit $\pi(L, K) = 4\,L^{\frac{1}{2}} K^{\frac{1}{4}} - L - K$, with $L, K > 0$, has a single stationary point. Which is it, and of what type?

- $L = 4$, $K = 8$, local maximum point
- $L = 8$, $K = 4$, saddle point
- $L = 16$, $K = 8$, local maximum point
- $L = 8$, $K = 4$, local maximum point

## Explanation

The first-order conditions are
$$
\begin{cases}
\pi_L = 2\,L^{-\frac{1}{2}} K^{\frac{1}{4}} - 1 = 0 \\[6pt]
\pi_K = L^{\frac{1}{2}} K^{-\frac{3}{4}} - 1 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
that is, $L^{\frac{1}{2}} = 2K^{\frac{1}{4}}$ and $L^{\frac{1}{2}} = K^{\frac{3}{4}}$. Setting the two expressions equal gives $K^{\frac{1}{2}} = 2$, so $K = 4$ and $L^{\frac{1}{2}} = 4^{\frac{3}{4}} = 2\sqrt{2}$, that is, $L = 8$. The second derivatives at $(8, 4)$ are $\pi_{LL} = -L^{-\frac{3}{2}} K^{\frac{1}{4}} = -\dfrac{1}{16}$, $\pi_{KK} = -\dfrac{3}{4}\,L^{\frac{1}{2}} K^{-\frac{7}{4}} = -\dfrac{3}{16}$ and $\pi_{LK} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,L^{-\frac{1}{2}} K^{-\frac{3}{4}} = \dfrac{1}{16}$. Therefore $\det H = \dfrac{3}{256} - \dfrac{1}{256} = \dfrac{1}{128} > 0$ and $\pi_{LL} < 0$, and the point $(8, 4)$ is a **local maximum**, with $Q = 4$ and profit $\pi = 16 - 8 - 4 = 4$ euros.
