Which of the stationary points of $f(x, y) = xy\,(3 - x - y)$ is a local maximum point?

- $(1, 1)$
- $(0, 0)$
- $(3, 0)$
- $(0, 3)$

## Explanation

We have $f = 3xy - x^2 y - xy^2$, so $f_x = y\,(3 - 2x - y)$ and $f_y = x\,(3 - x - 2y)$. The system $\nabla f = (0, 0)$ has the solutions $(0, 0)$, $(3, 0)$, $(0, 3)$ and $(1, 1)$, the last one obtained from the system $2x + y = 3$, $x + 2y = 3$. The second derivatives are $f_{xx} = -2y$, $f_{yy} = -2x$ and $f_{xy} = 3 - 2x - 2y$. At $(1, 1)$ we have $\det H = (-2)(-2) - (-1)^2 = 3 > 0$ and $f_{xx} = -2 < 0$, so $(1, 1)$ is a **local maximum** point, with $f(1, 1) = 1$. At the other three points $\det H = -9 < 0$, so they are saddle points.
