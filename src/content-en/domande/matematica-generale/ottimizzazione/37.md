A firm has production function $Q = LK$ and pays $2$ euros per unit of labor and $1$ euro per unit of capital. What is the minimum cost of producing $Q = 8$, with $L, K > 0$?

- $8$ euros
- $10$ euros
- $6\sqrt{2}$ euros
- $16$ euros

## Explanation

One minimizes the cost $C = 2L + K$ subject to the constraint $LK - 8 = 0$. The Lagrange system is
$$
\begin{cases}
2 = \lambda K \\[4pt]
1 = \lambda L \\[4pt]
LK = 8
\end{cases}
$$
Dividing the first equation by the second we obtain $\dfrac{K}{L} = 2$, that is, $K = 2L$, and the constraint becomes $2L^2 = 8$. Therefore $L = 2$, $K = 4$ and the cost is $C = 4 + 4 = 8$ euros. Substituting $K = \dfrac{8}{L}$ we have $C(L) = 2L + \dfrac{8}{L}$, which tends to $+\infty$ as $L \to 0^+$ and as $L \to +\infty$, so the only candidate $(2, 4)$ is the **minimum** point and the minimum cost is $8$ euros.
