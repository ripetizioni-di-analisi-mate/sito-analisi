What is the maximum value of $4 x^{2} + 7 y^{2}$ on the rectangle $0\leq x\leq 4$, $0\leq y\leq 9$?

- $567$
- $97$
- $631$
- $64$

## Explanation

Each square increases with its coordinate on this rectangle. The maximum is at the vertex with both coordinates at their upper endpoints.
