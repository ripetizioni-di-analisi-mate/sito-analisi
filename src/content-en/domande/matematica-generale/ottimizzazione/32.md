What is the maximum of $f(x, y) = xy$ on the line $2x + y = 8$, with $x > 0$ and $y > 0$?

- $16$
- $\dfrac{64}{9}$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

From the constraint we get $y = 8 - 2x$, and the conditions $x > 0$, $y > 0$ give $0 < x < 4$. With the **substitution method** one studies
$$
\varphi(x) = x\,(8 - 2x) = 8x - 2x^2, \qquad \varphi'(x) = 8 - 4x .
$$
The derivative vanishes for $x = 2$, it is positive before and negative after, so $x = 2$ is the maximum point of $\varphi$ on the interval $(0, 4)$. For $x = 2$ the constraint gives $y = 4$, therefore the **constrained maximum** is $f(2, 4) = 8$.
