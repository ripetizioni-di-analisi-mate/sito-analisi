We look for the stationary point of $f(x, y) = xy + \dfrac{8}{x} + \dfrac{8}{y}$ with $x > 0$ and $y > 0$ and classify it with the Hessian test. What value of $f$ is found at the minimum point?

- $12$
- $8$
- $16$
- $6$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = y - \dfrac{8}{x^2}$ and $f_y = x - \dfrac{8}{y^2}$. From the first equation we get $y = \dfrac{8}{x^2}$, and substituting into the second we obtain $x = \dfrac{x^4}{8}$, that is, $x^3 = 8$ for $x > 0$. Hence $x = 2$ and $y = 2$. The second derivatives at $(2, 2)$ are $f_{xx} = \dfrac{16}{x^3} = 2$, $f_{yy} = \dfrac{16}{y^3} = 2$ and $f_{xy} = 1$, so $\det H = 4 - 1 = 3 > 0$ and $f_{xx} > 0$. The point $(2, 2)$ is a **local minimum** point, with $f(2, 2) = 4 + 4 + 4 = 12$.
