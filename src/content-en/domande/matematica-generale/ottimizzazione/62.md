How is the stationary point $(3,5)$ of $\left(x - 3\right)^{2} - 7 \left(y - 5\right)^{2}$ classified?

- Strict local maximum
- Strict local minimum
- Absolute minimum
- Saddle point

## Explanation

Along the horizontal line through the point the change is a positive square. Along the vertical line it is a negative multiple of a square, so the point is a saddle point.
