For real quantities $x,y\geq0$, profit is $\pi(x,y)=- 4 x^{2} + 56 x - y^{2} + 18 y$. Which pair maximizes it?

- $\left( 14, \  18\right)$
- $\left( 9, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( 7, \  9\right)$

## Explanation

Both squared terms have negative coefficients and there is no cross term. Setting both partial derivatives to zero gives $x=7$ and $y=9$.
