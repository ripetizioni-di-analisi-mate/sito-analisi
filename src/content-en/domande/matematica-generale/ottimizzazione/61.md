Where does $\left(x - 3\right)^{2} + 7 \left(y - 5\right)^{2}$ attain its absolute minimum?

- $\left( 5, \  3\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$
- $\left( -3, \  -5\right)$
- $\left( 3, \  5\right)$

## Explanation

Both squares are nonnegative. The minimum value is zero, attained when both squares vanish.
