A firm in perfect competition sells two goods at prices of $24$ and $18$ euros, with cost $C(q_1, q_2) = 2q_1^2 + 2q_1 q_2 + q_2^2$. What is the maximum profit?

- $180$ euros
- $153$ euros
- $45$ euros
- $90$ euros

## Explanation

The profit is $\pi(q_1, q_2) = 24q_1 + 18q_2 - 2q_1^2 - 2q_1 q_2 - q_2^2$, and the first-order conditions are
$$
\begin{cases}
\pi_{q_1} = 24 - 4q_1 - 2q_2 = 0 \\[4pt]
\pi_{q_2} = 18 - 2q_1 - 2q_2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting the second equation from the first we obtain $6 - 2q_1 = 0$, so $q_1 = 3$, and the second equation then gives $q_2 = 6$. The Hessian is $\begin{pmatrix} -4 & -2 \\ -2 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$, with $\det H = 4 > 0$ and $\pi_{q_1 q_1} = -4 < 0$, therefore by the **Hessian test** the point $(3, 6)$ is a maximum point. The maximum profit is
$$
\pi(3, 6) = 72 + 108 - 18 - 36 - 36 = 90 \text{ euros} .
$$
