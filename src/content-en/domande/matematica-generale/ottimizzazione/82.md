Which point on the line $x+y=29$ minimizes $\left(x - 4\right)^{2} + \left(y - 7\right)^{2}$?

- $\left( 22, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  25\right)$
- $\left( 4, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 13, \  16\right)$

## Explanation

Substitute $y=29-x$ to get an upward-opening parabola. Its derivative vanishes when both deviations from $4$ and $7$ equal $9$.
