How many solutions $(x, y, \lambda)$ does the Lagrange system have for the extrema of $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$ with the constraint $x^4 + y^4 = 2$?

- $8$
- $4$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

With $g(x, y) = x^4 + y^4 - 2$ the first two equations are $2x = 4\lambda x^3$ and $2y = 4\lambda y^3$, that is, $x\,(1 - 2\lambda x^2) = 0$ and $y\,(1 - 2\lambda y^2) = 0$. The coordinates $x$ and $y$ are not both zero, because the origin is not on the constraint. If $x = 0$, the constraint gives $y^4 = 2$, hence the two points $\left(0, \pm \sqrt[4]{2}\right)$, and in the same way $y = 0$ gives the two points $\left(\pm \sqrt[4]{2}, 0\right)$. If $x$ and $y$ are both different from $0$, we have $x^2 = y^2 = \dfrac{1}{2\lambda}$, and the constraint $2x^4 = 2$ gives $x^2 = y^2 = 1$, that is, the four points $(\pm 1, \pm 1)$. Each point corresponds to a single value of $\lambda$, so the system has **eight solutions**. At the points $(\pm 1, \pm 1)$ the function equals $2$, which is the maximum, and at the other four it equals $\sqrt{2}$, which is the minimum.
