A consumer maximizes $U(x,y)=xy$ subject to $3x+5y=210$ and $x,y\geq0$. What quantity $x$ is chosen?

- $35$
- $70$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

Substituting the budget constraint gives a downward-opening parabola with zeros at $0$ and $70$. The vertex’s x-coordinate is halfway between the zeros.
