What is the value of $f(x, y) = 4xy - x^4 - y^4$ at its local maximum points?

- $0$
- $4$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = 4y - 4x^3$ and $f_y = 4x - 4y^3$, so the stationary points satisfy $y = x^3$ and $x = y^3$. Substituting the first equality into the second we obtain $x = x^9$, that is, $x\,(x^8 - 1) = 0$, therefore the stationary points are $(0, 0)$, $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, -1)$. The second derivatives are $f_{xx} = -12x^2$, $f_{xy} = 4$ and $f_{yy} = -12y^2$, so $\det H = 144x^2y^2 - 16$. At $(1, 1)$ and at $(-1, -1)$ we have $\det H = 128 > 0$ and $f_{xx} = -12 < 0$, therefore by the **Hessian test** the two points are local maximum points; at the origin $\det H = -16 < 0$ and the point is a saddle point. The value at the maximum points is $f(1, 1) = f(-1, -1) = 4 - 1 - 1 = 2$.
