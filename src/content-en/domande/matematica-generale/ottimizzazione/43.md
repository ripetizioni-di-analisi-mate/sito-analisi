A consumer maximises strictly concave utility subject to $p_x x+p_y y\leq B$, with nonnegative quantities and positive prices. Both prices and income $B$ double together. How does the optimal bundle change?

- Both quantities double.
- It stays the same.
- Only $x$ is halved.
- Both quantities are halved.

## Explanation

Dividing the new constraint by two recovers the original inequality. The affordable bundles and preferences are identical, so the same bundle maximises utility. Strict concavity makes the optimum unique whenever it exists.
