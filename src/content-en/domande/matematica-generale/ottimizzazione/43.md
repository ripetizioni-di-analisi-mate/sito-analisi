A consumer has utility $U(x, y) = x y^3$ and the prices of the two goods are $p_1 = 2$ and $p_2 = 3$ euros. Which relation between $x$ and $y$ holds at the optimal bundle, with $x, y > 0$?

- $y = \dfrac{9}{2}\,x$
- $y = 2x$
- $y = \dfrac{2}{3}\,x$
- $x = 2y$

## Explanation

The derivatives of the utility are $U_x = y^3$ and $U_y = 3xy^2$, so the marginal rate of substitution is
$$
\frac{U_x}{U_y} = \frac{y^3}{3xy^2} = \frac{y}{3x} .
$$
At the optimal choice the **marginal rate of substitution** equals the price ratio $\dfrac{p_1}{p_2} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, so $\dfrac{y}{3x} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. Therefore at the optimal bundle $y = 2x$. The relation does not depend on income, which is then used to find the bundle through the budget constraint.
