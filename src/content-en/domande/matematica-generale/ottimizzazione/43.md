A consumer chooses the bundle $(x, y)$, with $x \geq 0$ and $y \geq 0$, that maximises their utility subject to the budget constraint $p_1 x + p_2 y = B$, and the optimal bundle is unique. The prices of the two goods and the income $B$ double. What happens to the optimal bundle?

- Both quantities double
- The optimal bundle stays the same
- Only the quantity of the more expensive good is halved
- Both quantities are halved

## Explanation

After the doubling the budget constraint is $2p_1 x + 2p_2 y = 2B$. Dividing both sides by $2$ gives $p_1 x + p_2 y = B$, so the set of affordable bundles does not change. The utility function is also the same, therefore the maximisation problem coincides with the original one and the **optimal bundle stays the same**. For example, with $U(x, y) = xy$, prices of $1$ and $4$ euros and an income of $40$ euros the optimal bundle is $(20, 5)$, and with prices of $2$ and $8$ euros and an income of $80$ euros it is still $(20, 5)$.
