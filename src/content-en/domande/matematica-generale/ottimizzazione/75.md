For real quantities $x,y\geq0$, profit is $\pi(x,y)=- 3 x^{2} + 30 x - y^{2} + 14 y$. Which pair maximizes it?

- $\left( 7, \  5\right)$
- $\left( 5, \  7\right)$
- $\left( 10, \  14\right)$
- $\left( 0, \  0\right)$

## Explanation

Both squared terms have negative coefficients and there is no cross term. Setting both partial derivatives to zero gives $x=5$ and $y=7$.
