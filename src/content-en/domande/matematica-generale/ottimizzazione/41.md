A consumer has utility $U(x, y) = x^2 y$ and an income of $90$ euros, with prices $p_1 = 3$ and $p_2 = 3$ euros. What is the optimal bundle, with $x, y > 0$?

- $(20, 10)$
- $(10, 20)$
- $(15, 15)$
- $(25, 5)$

## Explanation

The derivatives of the utility are $U_x = 2xy$ and $U_y = x^2$. At the optimal choice the marginal rate of substitution equals the price ratio, so $\dfrac{2xy}{x^2} = \dfrac{3}{3}$, and since $x > 0$ we obtain $\dfrac{2y}{x} = 1$, that is, $x = 2y$. The budget constraint $3x + 3y = 90$ becomes $9y = 90$, therefore $y = 10$ and $x = 20$. On the constraint we have $y = 30 - x$, and the utility $\varphi(x) = x^2\,(30 - x)$ has derivative $\varphi'(x) = 3x\,(20 - x)$, positive for $0 < x < 20$ and negative for $20 < x < 30$. So the **optimal bundle** is $(20, 10)$.
