For $f(x,y)=2x+3y$ on $x^2+y^2=25$, which equation follows from the Lagrange multiplier condition $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$ with $g=x^2+y^2$?

- $2 x = 3 y$
- $x = y$
- $3 x = 2 y$
- $3 x = - 2 y$

## Explanation

The gradients are $(2,3)$ and $(2x,2y)$. Eliminating the multiplier gives $3x=2y$.
