How many saddle points does the function $f(x, y) = x^3 - 3x + y^3 - 3y$ have?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = 3x^2 - 3$ and $f_y = 3y^2 - 3$, so the stationary points satisfy $x^2 = 1$ and $y^2 = 1$. There are therefore four stationary points, $(1, 1)$, $(1, -1)$, $(-1, 1)$ and $(-1, -1)$. The second derivatives are $f_{xx} = 6x$, $f_{xy} = 0$ and $f_{yy} = 6y$, so $\det H = 36xy$. The determinant is negative when $x$ and $y$ have opposite signs, that is, at $(1, -1)$ and at $(-1, 1)$, which by the **Hessian test** are saddle points. At the other two points $\det H = 36 > 0$, and $(1, 1)$ is a minimum while $(-1, -1)$ is a maximum; so there are **two** saddle points.
