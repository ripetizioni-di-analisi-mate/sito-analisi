How is the origin classified for $f(x, y) = e^{xy}$?

- Local minimum point, with $\det H(0, 0) = 1$
- Saddle point, with $\det H(0, 0) = -1$
- Local maximum point, with $\det H(0, 0) = 1$
- The test is inconclusive, because $\det H(0, 0) = 0$

## Explanation

The partial derivatives are $f_x = y\,e^{xy}$ and $f_y = x\,e^{xy}$, which equal $0$ at the origin, so the origin is stationary. The second derivatives are $f_{xx} = y^2 e^{xy}$, $f_{yy} = x^2 e^{xy}$ and $f_{xy} = (1 + xy)\,e^{xy}$, which at the origin equal $0$, $0$ and $1$. Therefore $\det H(0, 0) = 0 \cdot 0 - 1^2 = -1 < 0$, and the origin is a **saddle point**. Indeed $e^{xy} > 1 = f(0, 0)$ at the points with $xy > 0$ and $e^{xy} < 1$ at the points with $xy < 0$, and every disk centered at the origin contains points of both kinds.
