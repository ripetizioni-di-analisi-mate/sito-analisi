The origin is a stationary point of $f(x, y) = x^2 - 3xy + 2y^2$. Along which line through the origin is the function negative at every point other than the origin?

- $y = 0$
- $y = \dfrac{3}{4}\,x$
- $y = 2x$
- $y = -x$

## Explanation

On the line $y = mx$ the function equals $f(x, mx) = x^2\,(1 - 3m + 2m^2) = x^2\,(1 - m)(1 - 2m)$. For $m = \dfrac{3}{4}$ we have $(1 - m)(1 - 2m) = \dfrac{1}{4} \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -\dfrac{1}{8}$, so $f\left(x, \dfrac{3}{4}\,x\right) = -\dfrac{1}{8}\,x^2 < 0$ for $x \neq 0$. For $m = 0$, $m = 2$ and $m = -1$ the coefficient equals $1$, $3$ and $6$, so along those lines $f$ is positive. Since the function is positive along the $x$-axis and negative along the line $y = \dfrac{3}{4}\,x$, the origin is a **saddle point**.
