A loan of $12\,000$ euros is repaid in $4$ annual installments in arrears at the rate of $5\%$, by French amortization or by Italian amortization. Which statement about the two schedules is true?

- The first principal portions coincide
- The first installment of the French schedule is larger than that of the Italian schedule
- The first interest portions coincide
- The total interest is the same in the two schedules

## Explanation

In both schedules the first interest portion is computed on the initial debt, $I_1 = i\,S = 0.05 \cdot 12\,000 = 600$ euros, so the first interest portions **coincide**. In the Italian schedule the first installment is $3000 + 600 = 3600$ euros, while in the French schedule the installment is $R = \dfrac{12\,000}{a_{\overline{4}|0.05}} \approx 3384.14$ euros, which is smaller. The first principal portions are therefore $3000$ euros in the Italian schedule and $3384.14 - 600 = 2784.14$ euros in the French schedule. The total interest is $1500$ euros in the Italian schedule and $4R - 12\,000 \approx 1536.57$ euros in the French schedule.
