A loan of $21000$ euros is repaid in $3$ end-of-year payments under Italian amortization at an annual rate of $5$ percent. What is the first payment?

- $1050$
- $8050.0$
- $7000.0$
- $7700.0$

## Explanation

The constant principal component equals the loan divided by the number of payments. Interest at the first due date is calculated on the full opening balance.
