A payment of $2000$ euros is made at the end of each year into an account at the annual rate of $4\%$ under compound interest. What is the minimum number of payments for which the accumulated value, at the time of the last payment, exceeds $20\,000$ euros?

- $9$
- $10$
- $8$
- $14$

## Explanation

The accumulated value after $n$ payments is $2000\,s_{\overline{n}|0.04} = 2000 \cdot \dfrac{1.04^{n} - 1}{0.04}$. The condition $2000\,s_{\overline{n}|0.04} > 20\,000$ is equivalent to $1.04^{n} - 1 > 0.4$, that is, $1.04^{n} > 1.4$. Taking logarithms gives $n > \dfrac{\ln 1.4}{\ln 1.04} \approx 8.58$, so $9$ payments are needed. Indeed the accumulated value is about $18\,428.45$ euros after $8$ payments and about $21\,165.59$ euros after $9$. The value $10$ is the number of payments that, without interest, add up to $20\,000$ euros, and $14$ imposes the condition on the present value of the payments instead of on the accumulated value.
