- $7$ months and $10$ days
- $8$ months
- $6$ months and $15$ days
- $12$ months

## Explanation

With commercial discount the average due date is the arithmetic mean of the times weighted by the amounts. The sum of the debts is $1000 + 3000 + 2000 = 6000$ euros, so
$$
t^* = \frac{1000 \cdot 3 + 3000 \cdot 7 + 2000 \cdot 12}{6000} = \frac{48\,000}{6000} = 8 .
$$
Hence the $6000$ euros can be paid in a single payment in $8$ months. The unweighted mean of the times, $\dfrac{3 + 7 + 12}{3} = \dfrac{22}{3}$ months, does not take the amounts into account, whereas the debt of $3000$ euros has a greater weight in the mean than the other two.
