A principal of $3000$ euros is invested for $6$ years at the annual rate of $4.5\%$ under compound interest. How much interest does it earn?

- $810$ euros
- $738.55$ euros
- $3906.78$ euros
- $906.78$ euros

## Explanation

Under compound interest the accumulated value is $M = C\,(1 + i)^{t}$, so after $6$ years
$$
M = 3000 \cdot 1.045^{6} \approx 3906.78 \text{ euros} .
$$
The interest is the difference between the accumulated value and the principal, $I = M - C$. Hence the interest is $3906.78 - 3000 = 906.78$ euros. Under simple interest one would obtain $3000 \cdot 0.045 \cdot 6 = 810$ euros, a smaller sum because the interest earned does not itself earn interest.
