In the Italian amortization of a loan of $10\,000$ euros with $5$ annual installments paid at the end of each year at the rate of $8\%$, each installment differs from the previous one by the same amount. What is the difference $R_{k+1} - R_k$?

- $160$ euros
- $-800$ euros
- $-160$ euros
- $-2000$ euros

## Explanation

The principal portion is constant, $Q = \dfrac{10\,000}{5} = 2000$ euros, so the outstanding balance decreases by $2000$ euros with each installment. The interest portion is $8\%$ of the outstanding balance, therefore it decreases each year by $0.08 \cdot 2000 = 160$ euros. Since $R_k = Q + I_k$ with $Q$ constant, the installment also decreases by $160$ euros per year, so $R_{k+1} - R_k = -160$ euros. Indeed the first installment is $2000 + 800 = 2800$ euros and the second is $2000 + 640 = 2640$ euros.
