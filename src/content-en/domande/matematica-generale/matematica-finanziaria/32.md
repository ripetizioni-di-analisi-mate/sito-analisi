At the annual rate of $5\%$ under compound interest, compare payment A of $2000$ euros due in one year and payment B of $2205$ euros due in three years. Which statement is true?

- A and B are equivalent only at date $0$
- A and B are equivalent at every valuation date
- A and B are equivalent only at date $3$
- B has a larger value than A at every date

## Explanation

At date $3$ the amount A is worth $2000 \cdot 1{,}05^{2} = 2000 \cdot 1{,}1025 = 2205$ euros, that is, as much as B, so the two amounts have the same value at date $3$. By the **independence of the valuation date**, under compound interest two amounts that have the same value at one date have the same value at any other date. Hence A and B are equivalent at every date. For example at date $0$ one has $2000 \cdot 1{,}05^{-1} \approx 1904{,}76$ euros and $2205 \cdot 1{,}05^{-3} \approx 1904{,}76$ euros.
