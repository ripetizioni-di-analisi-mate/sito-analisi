The effective semiannual rate is $5$ percent. What is the effective annual rate under compound interest?

- $2\cdot 5\%$
- $(5\%)^2$
- $(1+5\%)^2-1$
- $(1+5\%)^2$

## Explanation

The annual accumulation factor is obtained by applying the semiannual factor twice. Subtracting one from the annual factor gives the effective annual rate.
