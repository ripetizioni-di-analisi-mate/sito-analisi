A capital of $5000$ euros is invested for $2$ years at the annual rate of $6\%$. By how much does the accumulated value under compound interest exceed the accumulated value under simple interest?

- $36$ euros
- $600$ euros
- $618$ euros
- $18$ euros

## Explanation

Under simple interest the accumulated value is $5000 \cdot (1 + 0.06 \cdot 2) = 5600$ euros, while under compound interest it is $5000 \cdot 1.06^{2} = 5618$ euros, so the difference is $18$ euros. In general
$$
C\,(1 + i)^{2} - C\,(1 + 2i) = C\,i^{2} ,
$$
and with the data this gives $5000 \cdot 0.06^{2} = 18$ euros. It is the interest earned in the second year by the interest of the first year, that is, $0.06 \cdot 300$ euros. The value $618$ euros is the compound interest over the two years, and $600$ euros is the simple interest.
