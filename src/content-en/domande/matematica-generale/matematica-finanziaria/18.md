A principal of $5000$ euros is invested for $2$ years and $6$ months at the annual rate of $4\%$ under compound interest. What is the accumulated value? (result rounded to the cent)

- $5500$ euros
- $5624.32$ euros
- $5408$ euros
- $5515.10$ euros

## Explanation

Under compound interest the accumulated value is $M = C\,(1 + i)^{t}$ for every time $t \geq 0$, including non-integer times. The duration of $2$ years and $6$ months corresponds to $t = 2.5$, so
$$
M = 5000 \cdot 1.04^{2.5} \approx 5515.10 .
$$
Hence the accumulated value is $5515.10$ euros. The values $5408$ euros and $5624.32$ euros are the accumulated values after $2$ and after $3$ years, and $5500$ euros is the accumulated value under simple interest.
