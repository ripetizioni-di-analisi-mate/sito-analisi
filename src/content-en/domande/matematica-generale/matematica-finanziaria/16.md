A principal of $1500$ euros, invested for $9$ months under simple interest, has an accumulated value of $1545$ euros. What is the annual interest rate? (percentage rounded to two decimal places)

- $4\%$
- $3\%$
- $2{,}25\%$
- $3{,}88\%$

## Explanation

The interest earned is $I = M - C = 1545 - 1500 = 45$ euros. Under simple interest $I = C\,i\,t$, with $t = \dfrac{9}{12} = \dfrac{3}{4}$ of a year, so
$$
i = \frac{I}{C\,t} = \frac{45}{1500 \cdot \frac{3}{4}} = \frac{45}{1125} = 0{,}04 .
$$
Hence the annual rate is $4\%$. The value $3\%$ is the ratio $\dfrac{45}{1500}$, that is, the interest earned by $1$ euro in $9$ months, which is the rate for the $9$-month period.
