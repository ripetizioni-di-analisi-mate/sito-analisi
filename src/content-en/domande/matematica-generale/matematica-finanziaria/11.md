A mortgage of $60\,000$ euros is repaid by French amortization in $15$ annual installments in arrears at the rate of $4\%$. What is the installment?

- $5396.47$ euros
- $5188.91$ euros
- $6400$ euros
- $4000$ euros

## Explanation

In French amortization the installments are constant and form an annuity immediate whose present value is the debt $S$. Therefore $S = R\,a_{\overline{n}|i}$, and the installment is $R = \dfrac{S}{a_{\overline{n}|i}}$. With $n = 15$ and $i = 0.04$ one has $a_{\overline{15}|0.04} \approx 11.118387$, hence
$$
R = \frac{60\,000}{11.118387} \approx 5396.47 .
$$
The installment is $5396.47$ euros. The value $6400$ euros is the first installment of the Italian schedule, in which the constant principal portion is $4000$ euros and the interest for the first year is $2400$ euros.
