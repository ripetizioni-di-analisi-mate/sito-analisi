A perpetuity pays $900$ euros every year, starting one year from today. What is its present value at $7$ percent compounded annually?

- $\dfrac{90000}{7}$
- $\dfrac{96300}{7}$
- $63$
- $\dfrac{90000}{107}$

## Explanation

The discounted payments form a geometric series. Its first term is the payment divided by one plus the rate, and its ratio is the reciprocal of that factor.
