A loan of $50\,000$ euros is repaid by French amortization in $10$ year-end installments at the rate of $5\%$. What is the outstanding balance after the fourth installment is paid? (result rounded to the cent)

- $30\,000$ euros
- $22\,960{,}84$ euros
- $38\,851{,}37$ euros
- $32\,866{,}27$ euros

## Explanation

In French amortization the installment is $R = \dfrac{S}{a_{\overline{10}|0{,}05}}$, and the outstanding balance after $k$ installments is the value of the $n - k$ remaining installments, $S_k = R\,a_{\overline{n-k}|i}$. With $n - k = 6$ one obtains
$$
S_4 = 50\,000 \cdot \frac{a_{\overline{6}|0{,}05}}{a_{\overline{10}|0{,}05}} \approx 50\,000 \cdot \frac{5{,}075692}{7{,}721735} \approx 32\,866{,}27 .
$$
Hence the outstanding balance is $32\,866{,}27$ euros, and the installment is $R \approx 6475{,}23$ euros. The value $30\,000$ euros is the outstanding balance of the Italian schedule, and $22\,960{,}84$ euros is obtained with $a_{\overline{4}|0{,}05}$, that is, with the number of installments paid instead of the number of installments remaining.
