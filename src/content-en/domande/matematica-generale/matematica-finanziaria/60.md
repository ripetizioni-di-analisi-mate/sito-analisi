At $3$ percent compounded annually, a receipt due in $2$ years is worth $V$ today. What is its equivalent value in $1$ years?

- $V(1+3\%)^{1}$
- $V$
- $V(1+3\%)^2$
- $\dfrac V{(1+3\%)^{1}}$

## Explanation

Move $V$ forward from today by $1$ years. Apply the annual factor exactly $1$ times.
