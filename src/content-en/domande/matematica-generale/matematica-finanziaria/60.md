An investment produces a continuous cash flow of $1200$ euros per year, spread uniformly over time for $5$ years. What is the present value of the flow under continuous compounding with force of interest $\delta = 5\%$? (result rounded to the cent)

- $6000$ euros
- $4672.80$ euros
- $5308.78$ euros
- $6816.61$ euros

## Explanation

In the time interval between $t$ and $t + dt$ the flow provides $1200\,dt$ euros, which are worth $1200\,e^{-0.05\,t}\,dt$ today. The present value is therefore the integral
$$
V = \int_{0}^{5} 1200\,e^{-0.05\,t}\,dt = 1200 \left[ -\frac{e^{-0.05\,t}}{0.05} \right]_{0}^{5} = 24\,000 \left( 1 - e^{-0.25} \right) \approx 5308.78 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $6000$ euros is the sum of the payments without discounting, and $4672.80$ euros discounts all $6000$ euros as if they were paid at time $5$. The value $6816.61$ euros, that is, $24\,000\,(e^{0.25} - 1)$, is the value of the flow at time $5$.
