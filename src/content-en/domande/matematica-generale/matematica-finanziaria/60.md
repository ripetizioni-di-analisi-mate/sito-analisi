At $3$ percent compounded annually, a receipt due in $2$ years is worth $V$ today. What is its equivalent value in $1$ year?

- $V(1+3\%)^{1}$
- $V$
- $V(1+3\%)^2$
- $\dfrac V{(1+3\%)^{1}}$

## Explanation

The value $V$ is compounded from today for $1$ year. This applies the annual accumulation factor exactly $1$ time.
