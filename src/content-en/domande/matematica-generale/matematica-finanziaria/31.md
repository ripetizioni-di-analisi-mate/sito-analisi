A debt requires the payment of $1000$ euros in one year and of $2000$ euros in three years. At the annual rate of $5\%$ under compound interest, which single payment in two years is equivalent to the two payments? (result rounded to the cent)

- $2954.76$ euros
- $3000$ euros
- $3052.38$ euros
- $2680.06$ euros

## Explanation

The valuation date is chosen at time $2$, that is, the date of the single payment. The payment of $1000$ euros at time $1$ is accumulated for one year, while that of $2000$ euros at time $3$ is discounted by one year, so
$$
1000 \cdot 1.05 + \frac{2000}{1.05} \approx 1050 + 1904.76 = 2954.76 .
$$
Hence the single payment in two years is $2954.76$ euros. The value $2680.06$ euros is the present value of the two payments, referred to time $0$, and $3052.38$ euros is obtained by swapping the dates of the two amounts.
