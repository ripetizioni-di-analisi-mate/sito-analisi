Let $i > 0$ and let $n \geq 2$ be an integer. Which relation holds between $a_{\overline{n}|i}$, $s_{\overline{n}|i}$ and $n$?

- $s_{\overline{n}|i} < n < a_{\overline{n}|i}$
- $a_{\overline{n}|i} < s_{\overline{n}|i} < n$
- $a_{\overline{n}|i} < n < s_{\overline{n}|i}$
- $n < a_{\overline{n}|i} < s_{\overline{n}|i}$

## Explanation

The number $a_{\overline{n}|i}$ is the sum of the present values of $n$ payments equal to $1$, that is, $v + v^{2} + \dots + v^{n}$ with $v = (1 + i)^{-1}$. Since $0 < v < 1$, every term is less than $1$, so $a_{\overline{n}|i} < n$. The number $s_{\overline{n}|i}$ is the sum of the values of the payments at time $n$, that is, $1 + (1 + i) + \dots + (1 + i)^{n-1}$. Since $n \geq 2$, at least one term is greater than $1$ and none is less, so $s_{\overline{n}|i} > n$. For $n = 5$ and $i = 5\%$ one has $a_{\overline{5}|0.05} \approx 4.33$ and $s_{\overline{5}|0.05} \approx 5.53$.
