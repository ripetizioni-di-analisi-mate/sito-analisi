Two payments of $500$ euros are received at the ends of years one and two. Which expression gives their value at the end of year two at $3$ percent compounded annually?

- $500\left(1+\dfrac1{1+3\%}\right)$
- $500\big((1+3\%)^2+1\big)$
- $2\cdot 500(1+3\%)$
- $500(2+3\%)$

## Explanation

The first payment earns one year of interest. The second is made at the valuation date and is added at face value.
