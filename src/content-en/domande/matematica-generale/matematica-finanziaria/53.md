An amount of $10\,000$ euros is due in $3$ years. Under compound interest the annual discount rate is $d = 20\%$. What is the present value of the amount? (result rounded to the cent)

- $5787.04$ euros
- $4000$ euros
- $6250$ euros
- $5120$ euros

## Explanation

The discount rate is $d = 1 - v$, where $v$ is the annual discount factor, so $v = 1 - d = 0.8$. Under compound interest the present value is $V = M\,v^{t}$, therefore
$$
V = 10\,000 \cdot 0.8^{3} = 10\,000 \cdot 0.512 = 5120 \text{ euros} .
$$
The same result is obtained with the corresponding interest rate, $i = \dfrac{d}{1 - d} = \dfrac{0.2}{0.8} = 25\%$, since $10\,000 \cdot 1.25^{-3} = 5120$ euros. The value $5787.04$ euros uses $20\%$ as the interest rate, $10\,000 \cdot 1.2^{-3}$. The value $4000$ euros applies commercial discount, $10\,000\,(1 - 0.2 \cdot 3)$, and $6250$ euros applies rational discount with $i = 20\%$, $\dfrac{10\,000}{1 + 0.2 \cdot 3}$.
