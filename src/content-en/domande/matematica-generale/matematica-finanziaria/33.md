A series of $8$ year-end payments of $500$ euros earns interest at the annual rate of $3\%$ under compound interest. What is the accumulated value at the time of the last payment? (result rounded to the cent)

- $4000$ euros
- $3509{,}85$ euros
- $4579{,}55$ euros
- $4446{,}17$ euros

## Explanation

The accumulated value of a nondeferred ordinary annuity with $n$ payments $R$ is $M = R\,s_{\overline{n}|i}$, with $s_{\overline{n}|i} = \dfrac{(1 + i)^{n} - 1}{i}$. With $n = 8$ and $i = 0{,}03$ one has
$$
s_{\overline{8}|0{,}03} = \frac{1{,}03^{8} - 1}{0{,}03} \approx 8{,}892336 .
$$
Therefore $M = 500 \cdot 8{,}892336 \approx 4446{,}17$ euros. The value $3509{,}85$ euros is the present value $500\,a_{\overline{8}|0{,}03}$, referred to time $0$, and $4579{,}55$ euros is the accumulated value of the payments made in advance.
