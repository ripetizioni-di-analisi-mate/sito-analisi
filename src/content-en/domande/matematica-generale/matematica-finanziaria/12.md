A loan of $24\,000$ euros is repaid by Italian amortization in $6$ end-of-year installments at the annual rate of $5\%$. What is the fourth installment?

- $4400$ euros
- $5200$ euros
- $4600$ euros
- $4800$ euros

## Explanation

In Italian amortization the principal portion is constant, $Q = \dfrac{24\,000}{6} = 4000$ euros. The interest portion of the fourth year is computed on the outstanding balance after three installments, which is $S_3 = 24\,000 - 3 \cdot 4000 = 12\,000$ euros. Therefore
$$
I_4 = 0.05 \cdot 12\,000 = 600 \text{ euros} .
$$
Hence the fourth installment is $R_4 = 4000 + 600 = 4600$ euros. Computing the interest on the debt after four installments, $8000$ euros, would give the fifth installment, $4400$ euros.
