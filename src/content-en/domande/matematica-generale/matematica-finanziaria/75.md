At $5$ percent compounded annually, a receipt due in $3$ years is worth $V$ today. What is its equivalent value in $2$ years?

- $V$
- $\dfrac V{(1+5\%)^{2}}$
- $V(1+5\%)^3$
- $V(1+5\%)^{2}$

## Explanation

Move $V$ forward from today by $2$ years. Apply the annual factor exactly $2$ times.
