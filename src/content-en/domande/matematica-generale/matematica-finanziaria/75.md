An asset worth $10\,000$ euros is acquired through a lease with $5$ equal annual payments made in advance, the first paid today, at the annual rate of $10\%$ under compound interest. What is the payment? (result rounded to the cent)

- $2398.16$ euros
- $2637.97$ euros
- $2000$ euros
- $2901.77$ euros

## Explanation

The present value of the payments must equal the price of the asset. The annuity is a nondeferred annuity due, so $10\,000 = R \cdot 1.1 \cdot a_{\overline{5}|0.1}$, with $a_{\overline{5}|0.1} = \dfrac{1 - 1.1^{-5}}{0.1} \approx 3.790787$. Therefore
$$
R = \frac{10\,000}{1.1 \cdot 3.790787} \approx 2398.16 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $2637.97$ euros is the payment of the ordinary annuity, which is larger because each payment is made one year later, and $2901.77$ euros multiplies the ordinary annuity payment by $1.1$ instead of dividing it.
