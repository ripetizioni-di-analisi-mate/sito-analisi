An annuity pays $900$ euros today and the same amount in one year. Which expression gives its present value at $7$ percent compounded annually?

- $900+\dfrac{900}{1+7\%}$
- $1800$
- $\dfrac{900}{1+7\%}+\dfrac{900}{(1+7\%)^2}$
- $\dfrac{1800}{1+7\%}$

## Explanation

Today’s payment is already at the valuation date. Only the payment due next year is discounted.
