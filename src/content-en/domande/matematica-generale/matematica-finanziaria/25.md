A loan has a nominal annual rate of $9\%$ convertible monthly. What is the equivalent quarterly rate under compound interest? (percentage rounded to two decimal places)

- $2.27\%$
- $2.25\%$
- $2.35\%$
- $9.38\%$

## Explanation

The nominal rate $j_{12} = 9\%$ corresponds to the monthly rate $i_{12} = \dfrac{0.09}{12} = 0.0075$. A quarter contains $3$ months, therefore the equivalent quarterly rate $i_4$ satisfies $1 + i_4 = (1 + i_{12})^{3}$. Therefore
$$
i_4 = 1.0075^{3} - 1 \approx 0.02267 ,
$$
that is, $2.27\%$. The value $2.25\%$ is the proportional quarterly rate $\dfrac{9\%}{4}$, and $9.38\%$ is the effective annual rate $1.0075^{12} - 1$.
