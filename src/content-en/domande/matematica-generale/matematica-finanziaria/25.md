One contract credits $1\%$ each month. A second promises the same result by dividing the year into $24$ periods and crediting $0.5\%$ per period. For the same initial balance, how do the amounts compare after one month?

- The second amount is slightly greater.
- The two amounts are equal.
- The first is greater because it credits interest only once.
- The comparison depends on the size of the positive initial balance.

## Explanation

One month contains two of the new periods. The second factor is $1.005^2=1.010025$, while the first is $1.01$. The difference comes from interest on the interest earned in the first period.
