## Explanation

Under compound interest the time $t$ satisfies $4000 \cdot 1.02^{t} = 5000$, so $1.02^{t} = \dfrac{5000}{4000} = 1.25$. Taking the natural logarithm of both sides gives $t \ln 1.02 = \ln 1.25$. Hence
$$
t = \frac{\ln 1.25}{\ln 1.02} \approx \frac{0.22314}{0.01980} \approx 11.27 ,
$$
and the principal reaches $5000$ euros after $11.27$ years, that is, about $11$ years and $3$ months. Under simple interest it would take $\dfrac{0.25}{0.02} = 12.5$ years, a longer time.
