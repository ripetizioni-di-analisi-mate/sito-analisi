An investment costs $10\,000$ euros today and returns $2500$ euros at the end of each of the next $5$ years. In which interval does its internal rate of return $i^*$ lie?

- $0 < i^* < 4\%$
- $4\% < i^* < 6\%$
- $8\% < i^* < 10\%$
- $6\% < i^* < 8\%$

## Explanation

The NPV is $\operatorname{VAN}(i) = -10\,000 + 2500\,a_{\overline{5}|i}$. The initial cash flow is negative, the others are positive and the sum of the cash flows is $2500 > 0$, so by the theorem on the existence and uniqueness of the IRR the NPV is continuous and strictly decreasing and has a single zero. At $6\%$ one has $a_{\overline{5}|0.06} \approx 4.212364$, so $\operatorname{VAN}(0.06) \approx 530.91 > 0$. At $8\%$ one has $a_{\overline{5}|0.08} \approx 3.992710$, so $\operatorname{VAN}(0.08) \approx -18.22 < 0$. By the intermediate value theorem the IRR therefore lies between $6\%$ and $8\%$, and it is about $7.93\%$.
