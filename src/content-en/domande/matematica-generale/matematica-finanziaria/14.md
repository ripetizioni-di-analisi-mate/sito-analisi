An investment requires $1000$ euros today and returns $600$ euros in one year and $720$ euros in two years. What is the NPV at the rate of $10\%$?

- $140{,}50$ euros
- $145{,}45$ euros
- $320$ euros
- $200$ euros

## Explanation

The NPV is the sum of the cash flows discounted to the present, with outflows negative and inflows positive. The inflow at time $k$ is divided by $1{,}1^{k}$, so
$$
\operatorname{VAN}(0{,}1) = -1000 + \frac{600}{1{,}1} + \frac{720}{1{,}1^{2}} \approx -1000 + 545{,}45 + 595{,}04 = 140{,}50 .
$$
Hence the NPV is $140{,}50$ euros. Since the NPV is positive, at the rate of $10\%$ the investment is worthwhile.
