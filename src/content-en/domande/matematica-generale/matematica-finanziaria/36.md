Which expression gives, for every annual rate $i > 0$, the present value of $5$ annual payments of $1000$ euros made in advance, the first paid today?

- $1000\,(1 + i)^{-1}\,a_{\overline{5}|i}$
- $1000\,(1 + i)^{5}\,a_{\overline{5}|i}$
- $1000 + 1000\,a_{\overline{4}|i}$
- $1000\,a_{\overline{6}|i} - 1000$

## Explanation

The payments in advance are made at times $0, 1, 2, 3, 4$. The payment at time $0$ is worth $1000$ euros today, and the four payments at times $1, 2, 3, 4$ form an annuity immediate of $4$ payments, which is worth $1000\,a_{\overline{4}|i}$. Hence the present value is $1000 + 1000\,a_{\overline{4}|i}$, which coincides with $1000\,(1 + i)\,a_{\overline{5}|i}$. The expression $1000\,(1 + i)^{5}\,a_{\overline{5}|i}$ is the accumulated value of the annuity immediate, and $1000\,(1 + i)^{-1}\,a_{\overline{5}|i}$ is the present value of the annuity immediate deferred by one period, with the payments at times $2$ to $6$.
