An annuity consists of $10$ annual payments of $1000$ euros made at the end of each year, the first one year from now, at the annual rate of $5\%$ under compound interest. What is the value of the set of ten payments at date $4$, right after the fourth payment? (result rounded to the cent)

- $7721.73$ euros
- $12\,577.89$ euros
- $9385.82$ euros
- $5075.69$ euros

## Explanation

By the independence of the valuation date, the value at date $4$ is obtained by moving the present value forward by $4$ years. The present value is $1000\,a_{\overline{10}|0.05} \approx 7721.73$ euros, so the value at date $4$ is $7721.73 \cdot 1.05^{4} \approx 9385.82$ euros. The same value is obtained by adding the accumulated value of the four payments already made, $1000\,s_{\overline{4}|0.05} \approx 4310.13$ euros, and the value of the six future payments, $1000\,a_{\overline{6}|0.05} \approx 5075.69$ euros. The value $12\,577.89$ euros is the accumulated value of the ten payments at date $10$.
