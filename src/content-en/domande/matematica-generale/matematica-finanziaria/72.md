A debt of $7000$ euros at $5$ percent per year has a first payment of $3500$ euros at the end of year one. What balance remains immediately after the payment?

- $3500$
- $3675$
- $3850$
- $7350$

## Explanation

Before the payment, the debt includes interest for the first year. Subtract the full payment from that amount.
