An investment with an initial outflow followed only by inflows has its IRR between $5\%$ and $15\%$, and the IRR is approximated with the bisection method. The NPV is $120$ euros at the rate of $5\%$, $-80$ euros at the rate of $15\%$ and $15$ euros at the midpoint, at the rate of $10\%$. In which interval does the IRR lie after this step?

- $[10\%;\ 15\%]$
- $[5\%;\ 10\%]$
- $[5\%;\ 15\%]$
- $[7.5\%;\ 10\%]$

## Explanation

At the midpoint the NPV is $15 > 0$, while at the rate of $15\%$ it is $-80 < 0$. The NPV is continuous, so by the intermediate value theorem it vanishes at a point of the interval $[10\%;\ 15\%]$, and the step keeps the right half. By the theorem on the existence and uniqueness of the IRR the NPV is strictly decreasing, therefore on the interval $[5\%;\ 10\%]$ it is positive and does not vanish. The interval $[5\%;\ 15\%]$ is the starting one, which is halved at each step, and $[7.5\%;\ 10\%]$ is the result of a further step on the wrong half.
