$7000$ euros are invested for $3$ years with continuous compounding at annual force $\delta=5\%$. Which expression gives the accumulated value?

- $7000\,e^{3\cdot 5\%}$
- $7000(1+5\%)^3$
- $7000(1+3\cdot 5\%)$
- $7000\,e^{-3\cdot 5\%}$

## Explanation

With continuous compounding, the accumulation factor is the exponential of the force times elapsed time. Here time is measured in years.
