An annuity pays $700$ euros today and the same amount in one year. Which expression gives its present value at $5$ percent compounded annually?

- $\dfrac{700}{1+5\%}+\dfrac{700}{(1+5\%)^2}$
- $\dfrac{1400}{1+5\%}$
- $700+\dfrac{700}{1+5\%}$
- $1400$

## Explanation

Today’s payment is already at the valuation date. Discount only the payment due next year.
