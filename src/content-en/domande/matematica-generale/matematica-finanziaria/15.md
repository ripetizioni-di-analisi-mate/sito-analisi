A principal of $3000$ euros is invested at the annual rate of $2\%$ and a principal of $2500$ euros at the annual rate of $6\%$, both under simple interest and starting at the same time. After how long are the two accumulated values equal? (years rounded to two decimal places)

- $2.38$ years
- $8.33$ years
- $5.56$ years
- $3.33$ years

## Explanation

Under simple interest the accumulated value is $M = C\,(1 + i\,t)$, so the two accumulated values are $3000 + 60\,t$ and $2500 + 150\,t$. They are two linear functions of time, with slopes $60$ and $150$ euros per year. The accumulated values are equal when
$$
3000 + 60\,t = 2500 + 150\,t ,
$$
that is, when $90\,t = 500$. Therefore $t = \dfrac{500}{90} \approx 5.56$ years. The value $2.38$ years is obtained by adding the slopes instead of subtracting them.
