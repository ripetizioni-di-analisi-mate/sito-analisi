What principal, invested for $8$ months at the annual rate of $3\%$ under simple interest, earns interest of $50$ euros? (result rounded to the cent)

- $2083.33$ euros
- $1666.67$ euros
- $2500$ euros
- $208.33$ euros

## Explanation

Under simple interest the interest is $I = C\,i\,t$, with the time $t$ measured in years. Since $8$ months are $\dfrac{8}{12} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ of a year, one sets $t = \dfrac{2}{3}$, and the rate is $i = 0.03$. Solving the interest formula for $C$ gives
$$
C = \frac{I}{i\,t} = \frac{50}{0.03 \cdot \frac{2}{3}} = \frac{50}{0.02} = 2500 .
$$
Hence the principal is $2500$ euros. The value $2083.33$ euros corresponds to $t = 0.8$, that is, to the $8$ months written as tenths of a year, and $1666.67$ euros is obtained by ignoring the time.
