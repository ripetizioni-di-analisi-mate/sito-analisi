## Explanation

A perpetuity with end-of-period payments of $R$ at the rate $i$ has present value $\dfrac{R}{i}$. Payments made at the beginning of each year form a perpetuity due. Since each payment is made one year earlier than in the perpetuity with year-end payments, the present value is that of the perpetuity with year-end payments multiplied by $1 + i$. With $R = 1800$ euros and $i = 0{,}03$ one obtains
$$
V = \frac{1800 \cdot 1{,}03}{0{,}03} = 61\,800 .
$$
Hence the perpetuity is worth $61\,800$ euros today, that is, $1800$ euros more than the perpetuity with year-end payments, which is worth $60\,000$ euros.
