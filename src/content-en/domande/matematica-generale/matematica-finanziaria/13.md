## Explanation

The perpetuity immediate with payment $R$ at the rate $i$ has present value $\dfrac{R}{i}$. Payments made at the beginning of each year form a perpetuity due. Since each payment is made one year earlier than in the perpetuity immediate, the present value is that of the perpetuity immediate multiplied by $1 + i$. With $R = 1800$ euros and $i = 0.03$ one obtains
$$
V = \frac{1800 \cdot 1.03}{0.03} = 61\,800 .
$$
Hence the perpetuity is worth $61\,800$ euros today, that is, $1800$ euros more than the perpetuity immediate, which is worth $60\,000$ euros.
