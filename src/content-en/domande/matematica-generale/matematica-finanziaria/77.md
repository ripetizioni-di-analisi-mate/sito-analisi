$9000$ euros are invested at $7$ percent compounded annually for two years. Which expression gives the accumulated value in euros?

- $9000(1+7\%)$
- $9000(1+7\%)^2$
- $9000(1+2\cdot7\%)$
- $\dfrac{9000}{(1+7\%)^2}$

## Explanation

After the first year, the principal has been multiplied by one plus the rate. The same factor applies to the accumulated value during the second year.
