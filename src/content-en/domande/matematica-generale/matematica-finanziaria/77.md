A payment of $2000$ euros is made at the end of each of the next $20$ years into an account at the annual rate of $3\%$ under compound interest. At the time of the last payment the accumulated value is converted into an ordinary perpetuity at the same rate. What is the annual payment of the perpetuity? (result rounded to the cent)

- $2000$ euros
- $1612.22$ euros
- $1200$ euros
- $53\,740.75$ euros

## Explanation

The accumulated value of the payments is $2000\,s_{\overline{20}|0.03} = 2000 \cdot \dfrac{1.03^{20} - 1}{0.03} \approx 53\,740.75$ euros. This amount is the value, at date $20$, of the ordinary perpetuity, so the payment $P$ satisfies $\dfrac{P}{0.03} = 53\,740.75$. Therefore $P = 0.03 \cdot 53\,740.75 \approx 1612.22$ euros, that is, the annual interest earned by the accumulated value. The value $1200$ euros is obtained by applying $3\%$ to the $40\,000$ euros paid in, without the interest earned.
