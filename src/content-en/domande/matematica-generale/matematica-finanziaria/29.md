A principal of $1000$ euros is invested for one year at the nominal annual rate of $10\%$. Let $M_1$ be the accumulated value with annual compounding, $M_{12}$ the accumulated value with monthly compounding and $M_c$ the accumulated value under continuous compounding with force of interest $\delta = 10\%$. Which relation is true?

- $M_1 < M_{12} < M_c$
- $M_c < M_{12} < M_1$
- $M_1 < M_c < M_{12}$
- $M_1 = M_{12} = M_c$

## Explanation

With annual compounding one obtains $M_1 = 1000 \cdot 1{,}1 = 1100$ euros. With monthly compounding the rate applied each month is $\dfrac{0{,}1}{12}$, so $M_{12} = 1000 \left( 1 + \dfrac{0{,}1}{12} \right)^{12} \approx 1104{,}71$ euros. Under continuous compounding one has $M_c = 1000\,e^{0{,}1} \approx 1105{,}17$ euros, which is the limit of the accumulated values $1000 \left( 1 + \dfrac{0{,}1}{k} \right)^{k}$ as $k \to +\infty$. Hence $M_1 < M_{12} < M_c$ holds, and for the same nominal rate the accumulated value increases with the number $k$ of compounding periods per year.
