A project costs $10000$ euros today and pays $12500$ euros in one year. Which expression is its NPV at $7$ percent?

- $\dfrac{12500}{1+7\%}-10000$
- $12500-10000$
- $12500(1+7\%)-10000$
- $\dfrac{12500-10000}{1+7\%}$

## Explanation

The initial cost is already stated at the valuation date. Divide the receipt by one plus the rate, then subtract the cost.
