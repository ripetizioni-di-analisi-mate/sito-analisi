A perpetuity pays $700$ euros every year, starting one year from today. What is its present value at $5$ percent compounded annually?

- $14000$
- $35$
- $\dfrac{2000}{3}$
- $14700$

## Explanation

The discounted payments form a geometric series. Its first term is the payment divided by one plus the rate, and its ratio is the reciprocal of that factor.
