A bond has a face value of $1000$ euros, pays a coupon of $50$ euros at the end of each of the next $3$ years and repays the face value at the end of the third year. What is its fair price at the annual rate of $6\%$ under compound interest? (result rounded to the cent)

- $1150$ euros
- $973.27$ euros
- $839.62$ euros
- $133.65$ euros

## Explanation

The fair price is the present value of all the payments. The coupons form a nondeferred ordinary annuity of $3$ payments, with present value $50\,a_{\overline{3}|0.06} \approx 50 \cdot 2.673012 \approx 133.65$ euros. The repayment is worth $1000 \cdot 1.06^{-3} \approx 839.62$ euros today. Hence the price is $133.65 + 839.62 = 973.27$ euros. The price is lower than the face value because the coupon, equal to $5\%$ of the face value, is lower than the valuation rate of $6\%$.
