A debt of $10\,000$ euros is due in $5$ years. The debtor pays $4000$ euros today and agrees to settle the rest with a single payment in $10$ years. At the annual rate of $6\%$ under compound interest, what is the final payment? (result rounded to the cent)

- $3472.58$ euros
- $4647.10$ euros
- $8029.35$ euros
- $6218.86$ euros

## Explanation

The new plan must be equivalent to the debt, that is, have the same present value. The debt is worth $10\,000 \cdot 1.06^{-5} \approx 7472.58$ euros today, so after the payment of $4000$ euros a present value of $3472.58$ euros remains to be covered. The payment in $10$ years is the accumulated value of this amount, therefore
$$
X = 3472.58 \cdot 1.06^{10} \approx 6218.86 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $4647.10$ euros accumulates the difference for $5$ years instead of $10$, and $8029.35$ euros accumulates the difference $10\,000 - 4000$ for $5$ years without bringing the debt back to today.
