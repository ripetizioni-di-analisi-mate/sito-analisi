What is the monthly rate equivalent to the annual rate of $7\%$ under compound interest?

- $0.5833\%$
- $0.5654\%$
- $1.7059\%$
- $125.2192\%$

## Explanation

The monthly rate $i_{12}$ is equivalent to the annual rate $i$ if $(1 + i_{12})^{12} = 1 + i$, so $i_{12} = (1 + i)^{\frac{1}{12}} - 1$. With $i = 0.07$ one obtains
$$
i_{12} = 1.07^{\frac{1}{12}} - 1 \approx 0.005654 ,
$$
that is, $0.5654\%$. The proportional rate $\dfrac{7\%}{12} \approx 0.5833\%$ is equivalent to $7\%$ per year only under simple interest; under compound interest the equivalent rate is smaller, because the interest of each month earns interest in the following months.
