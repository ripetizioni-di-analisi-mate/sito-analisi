In a French amortization at the annual rate of $4\%$ the principal portion of the first installment is $1500$ euros. What is the principal portion of the third installment? (result rounded to the cent)

- $1560$ euros
- $1620$ euros
- $1687.30$ euros
- $1622.40$ euros

## Explanation

In French amortization the principal portions form a geometric progression with common ratio $1 + i$, that is, $Q_{k+1} = Q_k\,(1 + i)$. From the first to the third installment the factor $1 + i$ is applied twice, so
$$
Q_3 = Q_1\,(1 + i)^{2} = 1500 \cdot 1.04^{2} = 1500 \cdot 1.0816 = 1622.40 .
$$
Hence the third principal portion is $1622.40$ euros. The value $1620$ euros corresponds to a constant increase of $60$ euros with each installment, that is, to an arithmetic progression, and $1687.30$ euros is the fourth principal portion.
