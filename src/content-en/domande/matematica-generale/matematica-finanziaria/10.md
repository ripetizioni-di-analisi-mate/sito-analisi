## Explanation

The present value of an annuity immediate of $n$ payments $R$ at the rate $i$ is $V = R\,a_{\overline{n}|i}$, with $a_{\overline{n}|i} = \dfrac{1 - (1 + i)^{-n}}{i}$. With $n = 15$ and $i = 0.035$ one has
$$
a_{\overline{15}|0.035} = \frac{1 - 1.035^{-15}}{0.035} \approx 11.517411 .
$$
Therefore $V = 1200 \cdot 11.517411 \approx 13\,820.89$ euros. If the annuity were an annuity due, the present value would have to be multiplied by $1.035$, because each payment would be made one year earlier.
