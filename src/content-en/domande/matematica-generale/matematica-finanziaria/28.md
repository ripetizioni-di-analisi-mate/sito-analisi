What is the annual interest rate equivalent to the force of interest $\delta = 6\%$? (percentage rounded to two decimal places)

- $5.83\%$
- $6\%$
- $6.09\%$
- $6.18\%$

## Explanation

Continuous compounding with force of interest $\delta$ is equivalent to compound interest at the annual rate $i$ when $e^{\delta} = 1 + i$. So $i = e^{\delta} - 1$, and with $\delta = 0.06$ one obtains
$$
i = e^{0.06} - 1 \approx 0.06184 ,
$$
that is, $6.18\%$. The value $5.83\%$ is $\ln 1.06$, that is, the force of interest equivalent to the annual rate of $6\%$. The value $6.09\%$ is the effective rate corresponding to $6\%$ nominal convertible twice a year, which is smaller than that of continuous compounding.
