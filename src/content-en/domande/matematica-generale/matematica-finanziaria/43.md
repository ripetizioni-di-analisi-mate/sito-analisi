An investment requires $2000$ euros today and returns $1100$ euros in one year and $1210$ euros in two years. What is its internal rate of return? (percentage rounded to two decimal places)

- $10\%$
- $15{,}5\%$
- $7{,}75\%$
- $9{,}09\%$

## Explanation

The IRR $i^*$ makes the NPV vanish, and with $v = (1 + i^*)^{-1}$ the equation is
$$
-2000 + 1100\,v + 1210\,v^{2} = 0 .
$$
The discriminant is $1100^{2} + 4 \cdot 1210 \cdot 2000 = 10\,890\,000 = 3300^{2}$, so the positive solution is $v = \dfrac{-1100 + 3300}{2420} = \dfrac{10}{11}$. Since $1 + i^* = \dfrac{1}{v} = \dfrac{11}{10}$, the IRR is $i^* = 10\%$. The value $9{,}09\%$ is $1 - v$, that is, the discount rate corresponding to $10\%$, and $15{,}5\%$ is the ratio $\dfrac{310}{2000}$ between total gain and cost, computed without discounting.
