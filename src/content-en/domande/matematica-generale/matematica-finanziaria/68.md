Two payments of $700$ euros are received at the end of the first and second years. Which expression gives their accumulated value at the end of the second year at $5$ percent compounded annually?

- $700\big((1+5\%)^2+1\big)$
- $700\left(1+\dfrac1{1+5\%}\right)$
- $700(2+5\%)$
- $2\cdot 700(1+5\%)$

## Explanation

The first payment earns one year of interest. The second falls on the valuation date, so it is added at its original amount.
