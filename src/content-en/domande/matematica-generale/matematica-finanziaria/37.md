A debt of $10\,000$ euros at the annual rate of $6\%$ is repaid with end-of-year payments of amount $R$. The final payment can be reduced to the amount still due. For which values of $R$ is the debt paid off with a finite number of payments?

- $R \geq 600$
- $R > 566.04$
- $R > 600$
- For every $R > 0$

## Explanation

Interest in the first year is $10\,000\cdot0.06=600$ euros. If $R\leq600$, the payment does not reduce the balance, so the loan is never paid off. If $R>600$, the balance falls and interest in later periods is at most $600$ euros. Each full payment therefore reduces the balance by at least $R-600$ euros. After finitely many years a final payment of the amount still due settles the debt.
