A debt of $10\,000$ euros at the annual rate of $6\%$ is repaid with constant annual payments in arrears of amount $R$. For which values of $R$ is the debt paid off with a finite number of payments?

- $R \geq 600$
- $R > 566.04$
- $R > 600$
- For every $R > 0$

## Explanation

From the relation $V = R\,a_{\overline{n}|i}$ one obtains $1 - (1 + i)^{-n} = \dfrac{V\,i}{R}$. The left-hand side lies between $0$ and $1$ for every $n > 0$, so the equation has a solution $n$ if and only if $\dfrac{V\,i}{R} < 1$, that is, $R > V\,i$. Here $V\,i = 10\,000 \cdot 0.06 = 600$ euros, which is the interest of the first year, therefore the debt is paid off for $R > 600$. With $R = 600$ each payment covers only the interest and the debt stays at $10\,000$ euros, while with $R < 600$ the debt grows.
