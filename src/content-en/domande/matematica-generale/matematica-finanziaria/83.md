Two payments of $900$ euros are received at the end of the first and second years. Which expression gives their accumulated value at the end of the second year at $7$ percent compounded annually?

- $900\big((1+7\%)^2+1\big)$
- $900\left(1+\dfrac1{1+7\%}\right)$
- $900(2+7\%)$
- $2\cdot 900(1+7\%)$

## Explanation

The first payment earns one year of interest. The second falls on the valuation date, so it is added at its original amount.
