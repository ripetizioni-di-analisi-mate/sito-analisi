A debt of $10\,000$ euros at the annual rate of $6\%$ under compound interest is repaid with two installments: $5000$ euros after one year and a second installment $X$ after two years, which pays off the debt. What is $X$?

- $5000$ euros
- $5300$ euros
- $5936$ euros
- $4700$ euros

## Explanation

After one year the debt, with interest, is $10\,000 \cdot 1.06 = 10\,600$ euros, and the payment of $5000$ euros reduces it to the outstanding balance $S_1 = 5600$ euros. The second installment must pay this outstanding balance with the interest of the second year, so $X = 5600 \cdot 1.06 = 5936$ euros. The same value is obtained from the equivalence between the loan and the installments, $10\,000 = 5000 \cdot 1.06^{-1} + X \cdot 1.06^{-2}$. The value $4700$ euros subtracts the accumulated value of the first installment from the debt, $10\,000 - 5000 \cdot 1.06$, without the interest on the debt.
