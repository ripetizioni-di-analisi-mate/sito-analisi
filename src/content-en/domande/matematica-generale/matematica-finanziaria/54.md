An annuity pays $500$ euros today and the same amount in one year. Which expression gives its present value at $3$ percent compounded annually?

- $1000$
- $500+\dfrac{500}{1+3\%}$
- $\dfrac{500}{1+3\%}+\dfrac{500}{(1+3\%)^2}$
- $\dfrac{1000}{1+3\%}$

## Explanation

Today’s payment is already at the valuation date. Discount only the payment due next year.
