A principal of $1000$ euros is invested under compound interest at the annual rate of $10\%$ for the first $4$ years and at the annual rate of $20\%$ for the following $2$ years. What is the accumulated value after $6$ years? (result rounded to the cent)

- $1800$ euros
- $2985.98$ euros
- $2108.30$ euros
- $2509.06$ euros

## Explanation

Under compound interest the accumulated value is multiplied each year by $1$ plus the rate of that year. After the first $4$ years the principal has become $1000 \cdot 1.1^{4} = 1464.10$ euros. This accumulated value is the principal invested in the following $2$ years, so the accumulated value after $6$ years is
$$
1464.10 \cdot 1.2^{2} = 1464.10 \cdot 1.44 \approx 2108.30 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $1800$ euros adds the simple interest amounts $1000 \cdot 0.1 \cdot 4$ and $1000 \cdot 0.2 \cdot 2$, without the interest earned on interest. The value $2509.06$ euros applies $20\%$ to the first four years and $10\%$ to the last two, and $2985.98$ euros applies $20\%$ to all six years.
