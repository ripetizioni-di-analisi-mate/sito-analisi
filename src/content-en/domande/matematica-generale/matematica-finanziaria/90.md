At $7$ percent compounded annually, a receipt due in $4$ years is worth $V$ today. What is its equivalent value in $3$ years?

- $\dfrac V{(1+7\%)^{3}}$
- $V(1+7\%)^{3}$
- $V(1+7\%)^4$
- $V$

## Explanation

The value $V$ is compounded from today for $3$ years. This applies the annual accumulation factor exactly $3$ times.
