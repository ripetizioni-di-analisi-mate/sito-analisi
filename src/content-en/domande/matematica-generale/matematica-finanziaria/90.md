A receivables advance gives $950$ euros today against a receivable of $1000$ euros due in $6$ months. What is the effective annual rate of the transaction under compound interest? (percentage rounded to two decimal places)

- $5.26\%$
- $5\%$
- $10.80\%$
- $2.60\%$

## Explanation

The semiannual rate $i_2$ satisfies $950\,(1 + i_2) = 1000$, so $1 + i_2 = \dfrac{1000}{950} \approx 1.052632$. The equivalent annual rate is $i = (1 + i_2)^{2} - 1 \approx 1.108033 - 1$, that is, about $10.80\%$. The value $5.26\%$ is the semiannual rate, and $5\%$ is the discount rate for the half-year, $\dfrac{50}{1000}$. The value $2.60\%$ takes the square root instead of squaring.
