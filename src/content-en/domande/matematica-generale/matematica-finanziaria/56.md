An account applies the nominal annual rate of $6\%$, compounded $k$ times a year. Among those listed, what is the lowest compounding frequency for which the effective annual rate exceeds $6.15\%$?

- Semiannual ($k = 2$)
- Quarterly ($k = 4$)
- Monthly ($k = 12$)
- No frequency brings the effective rate above $6.15\%$

## Explanation

The effective rate is $i = \left( 1 + \dfrac{0.06}{k} \right)^{k} - 1$, and it increases with $k$. For $k = 2$ it equals $1.03^{2} - 1 = 6.09\%$, for $k = 4$ it equals $1.015^{4} - 1 \approx 6.136\%$ and for $k = 12$ it equals $1.005^{12} - 1 \approx 6.168\%$. Hence the lowest frequency is **monthly**. As $k$ increases the effective rate tends to $e^{0.06} - 1 \approx 6.18\%$, the rate of continuous compounding.
