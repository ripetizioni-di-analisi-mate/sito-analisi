A loan of $10000$ euros is repaid in $2$ end-of-year payments under Italian amortization at an annual rate of $3$ percent. What is the first payment?

- $5000.0$
- $5300.0$
- $300$
- $5150.0$

## Explanation

The constant principal component equals the loan divided by the number of payments. Interest at the first due date is calculated on the full opening balance.
