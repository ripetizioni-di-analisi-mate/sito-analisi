At a certain annual rate $i$ one has $a_{\overline{10}|i} = 7.3601$ and $(1 + i)^{10} = 1.7908$. What is $s_{\overline{10}|i}$, the accumulated value of the nondeferred ordinary annuity of $10$ payments equal to $1$? (result rounded to four decimal places)

- $13.1805$
- $4.1100$
- $9.1509$
- $17.9080$

## Explanation

The accumulated value of an annuity is its present value carried to the date of the last payment, that is, multiplied by the accumulation factor $(1 + i)^{10}$. Hence the relation $s_{\overline{10}|i} = (1 + i)^{10}\,a_{\overline{10}|i}$ holds, and
$$
s_{\overline{10}|i} = 1.7908 \cdot 7.3601 \approx 13.1805 .
$$
The value $4.1100$ divides $a_{\overline{10}|i}$ by the accumulation factor instead of multiplying it, and $9.1509$ adds the two numbers. The value $17.9080$, that is, $10 \cdot 1.7908$, is the accumulated value of ten payments all made today.
