A perpetuity pays $500$ euros every year, starting one year from today. What is its present value at $3$ percent compounded annually?

- $\dfrac{51500}{3}$
- $\dfrac{50000}{3}$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{50000}{103}$

## Explanation

The discounted payments form a geometric series. Its first term is the payment divided by one plus the rate, and its ratio is the reciprocal of that factor.
