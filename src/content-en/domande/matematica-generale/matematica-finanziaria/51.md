At the annual interest rate of $8\%$ under compound interest, what is the corresponding annual discount rate $d$? (percentage rounded to two decimal places)

- $8.70\%$
- $7.41\%$
- $92.59\%$
- $108\%$

## Explanation

The discount rate is the discount on $1$ euro due in one year, that is, $d = 1 - v$, where $v = \dfrac{1}{1 + i}$ is the discount factor. Hence
$$
d = \frac{i}{1 + i} = \frac{0.08}{1.08} \approx 0.0741 ,
$$
that is, about $7.41\%$. The discount rate is lower than the interest rate, because the discount refers to the amount due at maturity and the interest to the initial principal, which is smaller. The value $8.70\%$ is $\dfrac{0.08}{0.92}$, that is, the interest rate corresponding to a discount rate of $8\%$, and $92.59\%$ is the discount factor $v$.
