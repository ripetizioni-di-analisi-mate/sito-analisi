A deposit of $5000$ euros is made into an account that earns $6\%$ per year under compound interest, with no withdrawals. By how much does the balance of the account grow during the fourth year? (result rounded to the cent)

- $357.30$ euros
- $300$ euros
- $1312.38$ euros
- $955.08$ euros

## Explanation

The interest of the fourth year is computed on the accumulated value at the beginning of that year, that is, at the end of the third year, which is $5000 \cdot 1.06^{3} \approx 5955.08$ euros. In one year this amount earns the interest $5955.08 \cdot 0.06 \approx 357.30$ euros. Since the interest already earned also earns interest, the annual interest exceeds the $300$ euros of simple interest. The value $1312.38$ euros is the total interest of the first four years, and $955.08$ euros is that of the first three.
