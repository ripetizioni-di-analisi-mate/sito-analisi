$5000$ euros are invested at $3$ percent compounded annually for two years. Which expression gives the accumulated value in euros?

- $5000(1+3\%)$
- $5000(1+2\cdot3\%)$
- $5000(1+3\%)^2$
- $\dfrac{5000}{(1+3\%)^2}$

## Explanation

After year one, principal has been multiplied by one plus the rate. The same factor applies to that accumulated value in year two.
