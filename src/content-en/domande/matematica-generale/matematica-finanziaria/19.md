A principal of $2000$ euros grows to $2662$ euros in $3$ years under compound interest. What is the annual interest rate? (percentage rounded to two decimal places)

- $11.03\%$
- $33.10\%$
- $10\%$
- $15.37\%$

## Explanation

Under compound interest the rate linking $C$, $M$ and $t$ is $i = \left( \dfrac{M}{C} \right)^{\frac{1}{t}} - 1$. Since $\dfrac{2662}{2000} = 1.331$ and $1.331 = 1.1^{3}$, one obtains
$$
i = 1.331^{\frac{1}{3}} - 1 = 1.1 - 1 = 0.1 .
$$
Hence the annual rate is $10\%$. The value $11.03\%$ is obtained by dividing the ratio $\dfrac{662}{2000} = 33.1\%$ by $3$, as under simple interest, and $15.37\%$ is obtained with the square root instead of the cube root.
