A perpetuity pays $600$ euros per year, with the first payment $4$ years from now. What is its present value at the annual rate of $12\%$ under compound interest? (result rounded to the cent)

- $3558.90$ euros
- $5000$ euros
- $3177.59$ euros
- $3985.97$ euros

## Explanation

The first payment falls at time $4$, so at time $3$ the annuity is a perpetuity with end-of-period payments. At that date it is worth $\dfrac{600}{0.12} = 5000$ euros. The factor $1.12^{-3}$ discounts this value to time $0$, so
$$
V = 5000 \cdot 1.12^{-3} \approx 3558.90 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $3177.59$ euros is obtained by discounting for $4$ years, that is, by treating the annuity as if the first payment were at time $5$. The value $3985.97$ euros treats the annuity, at time $3$, as a perpetuity due.
