A bill of exchange, discounted with commercial discount at the annual rate $d = 6\%$ five months before maturity, has a present value of $4875$ euros. What is the amount at maturity? (result rounded to the cent)

- $5000$ euros
- $4996{,}88$ euros
- $5186{,}17$ euros
- $4753{,}13$ euros

## Explanation

Under commercial discount $V = M\,(1 - d\,t)$, with $t$ in years. Since $5$ months are $\dfrac{5}{12}$ of a year, one has $1 - d\,t = 1 - 0{,}06 \cdot \dfrac{5}{12} = 1 - 0{,}025 = 0{,}975$. Therefore
$$
M = \frac{V}{1 - d\,t} = \frac{4875}{0{,}975} = 5000 .
$$
Hence the amount at maturity is $5000$ euros. The value $4996{,}88$ euros is obtained by multiplying $4875$ by $1{,}025$, that is, by computing the discount on the present value, whereas under commercial discount the discount $D = M\,d\,t$ is proportional to the amount at maturity.
