To which of the following investments, described by the cash flows at times $0, 1, 2, \dots$, does the theorem on the existence and uniqueness of the IRR apply?

- $(-1000,\ 300,\ 300,\ 300)$
- $(-1000,\ 2600,\ -1680)$
- $(800,\ -300,\ -600)$
- $(-1000,\ 300,\ 400,\ 500)$

## Explanation

The theorem requires a negative initial cash flow, subsequent cash flows greater than or equal to zero and not all zero, and a positive sum of the cash flows. The investment $(-1000,\ 300,\ 400,\ 500)$ satisfies all the hypotheses, because $-1000 + 300 + 400 + 500 = 200 > 0$, so it has **one and only one IRR**, which is positive. For $(-1000,\ 300,\ 300,\ 300)$ the sum of the cash flows is $-100$, therefore the last hypothesis is not satisfied. The transactions $(-1000,\ 2600,\ -1680)$ and $(800,\ -300,\ -600)$ have respectively a negative subsequent cash flow and a positive initial cash flow, so the theorem does not apply.
