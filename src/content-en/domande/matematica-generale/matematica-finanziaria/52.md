$5000$ euros are invested for $2$ years with continuous compounding at an annual force of interest $\delta=3\%$. Which expression gives the accumulated value?

- $5000(1+2\cdot 3\%)$
- $5000(1+3\%)^2$
- $5000\,e^{2\cdot 3\%}$
- $5000\,e^{-2\cdot 3\%}$

## Explanation

With continuous compounding, the accumulation factor is the exponential of the product of the force of interest and elapsed time. Here time is measured in years.
