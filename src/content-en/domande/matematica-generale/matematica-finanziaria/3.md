## Explanation

Under compound interest the present value of an amount $M$ available in $t$ years is $V = M\,(1 + i)^{-t}$. With $M = 10\,000$ euros, $i = 0.03$ and $t = 7$ one obtains
$$
V = 10\,000 \cdot 1.03^{-7} = \frac{10\,000}{1.03^{7}} \approx \frac{10\,000}{1.229874} \approx 8130.92 .
$$
Therefore the present value is $8130.92$ euros. Multiplying by $1.03^{7}$ instead of dividing would give the accumulated value of $10\,000$ euros after $7$ years, which is greater than the starting amount.
