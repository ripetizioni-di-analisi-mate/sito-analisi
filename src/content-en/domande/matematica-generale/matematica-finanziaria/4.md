A bill of exchange of $2000$ euros matures in $4$ months. What is its present value under commercial discount at the annual rate $d = 4.5\%$?

- $1640$ euros
- $2030$ euros
- $1970$ euros
- $1910$ euros

## Explanation

Under commercial discount the discount is $D = M\,d\,t$, with $t$ in years, and the present value is $V = M\,(1 - d\,t)$. Since $4$ months are $\dfrac{4}{12} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ of a year, the discount equals
$$
D = 2000 \cdot 0.045 \cdot \frac{1}{3} = 30 \text{ euros} .
$$
Hence the present value is $2000 - 30 = 1970$ euros.
