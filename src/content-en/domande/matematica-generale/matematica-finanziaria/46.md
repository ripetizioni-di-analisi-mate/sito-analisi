The figure shows the accumulated value $M$ of a principal as a function of the time $t$, measured in years. The graph is a straight line segment that starts at the point $(0, 800)$ and passes through the point $(5, 1000)$. What is the annual interest rate?

::grafico{f="800+40*x+0*sqrt(x)" punti="0,800,pieno;5,1000,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=9 ymin=-200 ymax=1800 nomi="t,M" titolo="Accumulated value of a principal as a function of time"}

- $4\%$
- $5\%$
- $25\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The accumulated value is a linear function of time, so the regime is simple interest, with $M = C\,(1 + i\,t)$. The value at $t = 0$ is the principal, therefore $C = 800$ euros. The slope of the line is $\dfrac{1000 - 800}{5} = 40$ euros per year, and it equals the annual interest $C\,i$. Hence $i = \dfrac{40}{800} = 0.05$, that is, the rate is $5\%$. The value $4\%$ is obtained by dividing the annual interest by the accumulated value of $1000$ euros instead of by the principal.
