In an amortization schedule at the annual rate of $10\%$ the outstanding balance after installment $k - 1$ is $S_{k-1} = 8000$ euros, and installment $k$ is $R_k = 2500$ euros. What is the outstanding balance $S_k$ after installment $k$ is paid?

- $5500$ euros
- $4700$ euros
- $7200$ euros
- $6300$ euros

## Explanation

The interest portion of installment $k$ is $I_k = 0.1 \cdot 8000 = 800$ euros, so the principal portion is $Q_k = 2500 - 800 = 1700$ euros. The debt decreases only by the principal portion, therefore
$$
S_k = 8000 - 1700 = 6300 \text{ euros} .
$$
This is the relation $S_k = (1 + i)\,S_{k-1} - R_k = 8800 - 2500$. The value $5500$ euros subtracts the whole installment, as if it contained no interest. The value $4700$ euros adds the interest portion to the installment instead of subtracting it.
