Account A pays a nominal annual rate of $4\%$ with quarterly compounding. Account B pays an effective annual rate of $4{,}05\%$. Both accounts use compound interest. Which statement is true?

- Account B yields more
- The two accounts yield the same return
- The comparison depends on the duration of the investment
- Account A yields more

## Explanation

Account A applies the rate $i_4 = \dfrac{0{,}04}{4} = 0{,}01$ every quarter, so its effective annual rate is
$$
i = 1{,}01^{4} - 1 \approx 0{,}040604 ,
$$
that is, about $4{,}0604\%$, larger than the $4{,}05\%$ of account B. Under compound interest the accumulated value after $t$ years is $C\,(1 + i)^{t}$, where $i$ is the effective annual rate. Since the effective rate of A is larger, for every duration $t > 0$ the accumulated value of account A is larger, therefore **account A yields more**.
