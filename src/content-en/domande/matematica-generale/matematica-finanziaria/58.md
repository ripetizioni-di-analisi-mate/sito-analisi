A project costs $10000$ euros today and pays $11300$ euros in one year. Which expression is its NPV at $3$ percent?

- $\dfrac{11300-10000}{1+3\%}$
- $11300(1+3\%)-10000$
- $\dfrac{11300}{1+3\%}-10000$
- $11300-10000$

## Explanation

The initial cost is already stated at the valuation date. Divide the receipt by one plus the rate, then subtract the cost.
