An annuity consists of $5$ annual payments of $1000$ euros, the first in $4$ years. What is its present value at the annual rate of $4\%$ under compound interest? (result rounded to the cent)

- $3805{,}44$ euros
- $3957{,}65$ euros
- $4451{,}82$ euros
- $3659{,}07$ euros

## Explanation

The first payment is at time $4 = p + 1$, so this is an ordinary annuity deferred by $p = 3$ periods. Its present value is $V = R\,(1 + i)^{-p}\,a_{\overline{n}|i}$, with $a_{\overline{5}|0{,}04} = \dfrac{1 - 1{,}04^{-5}}{0{,}04} \approx 4{,}451822$. Therefore
$$
V = 1000 \cdot 1{,}04^{-3} \cdot 4{,}451822 \approx 3957{,}65 .
$$
The present value is $3957{,}65$ euros. With the deferment $p = 4$ one would obtain $3805{,}44$ euros, which is the present value of an annuity with the first payment at time $5$.
