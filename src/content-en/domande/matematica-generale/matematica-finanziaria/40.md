A loan of $20\,000$ euros is repaid by Italian amortization in $5$ end-of-year installments at the annual rate of $4\%$. What is the total interest paid?

- $4000$ euros
- $2400$ euros
- $1600$ euros
- $2000$ euros

## Explanation

In Italian amortization the principal portion is constant, $Q = \dfrac{20\,000}{5} = 4000$ euros, so the debts on which the interest of the five years is computed are $20\,000$, $16\,000$, $12\,000$, $8000$ and $4000$ euros. The interest portion of each year is $4\%$ of the debt at the beginning of the year, therefore the interest amounts are $800$, $640$, $480$, $320$ and $160$ euros. The total is
$$
800 + 640 + 480 + 320 + 160 = 2400 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $4000$ euros corresponds to the interest computed every year on the initial debt, whereas the debt decreases by $4000$ euros with each installment.
