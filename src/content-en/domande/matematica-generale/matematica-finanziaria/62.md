A loan of $6000$ euros is repaid in $6$ annual installments paid at the end of each year at the rate of $8\%$, with French amortization or with Italian amortization. The figure shows the outstanding balance $S$ of the two schedules as a function of the number $k$ of installments paid. Which schedule involves the larger total interest?

::grafico{f="6000*(1-1.08^(-(6-x)))/(1-1.08^(-6))+0*sqrt(x*(6-x));6000*(1-x/6)+0*sqrt(x*(6-x))" xmin=-1 xmax=7.5 ymin=-800 ymax=7200 nomi="k,S" legenda="\text{French};\text{Italian}" titolo="Outstanding balance of a loan of $6000$ euros in $6$ installments at $8\%$"}

- The Italian schedule, because its first installments are higher
- The French schedule, because its outstanding balance is never smaller than that of the Italian schedule
- The two schedules involve the same interest, because they repay the same debt at the same rate
- The Italian schedule, because its outstanding balance decreases faster

## Explanation

The interest portion of period $k$ is $I_k = i\,S_{k-1}$, so the total interest is $i\,(S_0 + S_1 + \dots + S_5)$. In the figure the outstanding balance of the French schedule is larger than that of the Italian schedule for $0 < k < 6$, and the two coincide for $k = 0$ and for $k = 6$. Therefore the sum of the outstanding balances, and with it the total interest, is larger in the **French schedule**. With the data the Italian schedule pays $0.08 \cdot (6000 + 5000 + 4000 + 3000 + 2000 + 1000) = 1680$ euros of interest, while the French schedule, with installment $R \approx 1297.89$ euros, pays $6\,R - 6000 \approx 1787.35$ euros.
