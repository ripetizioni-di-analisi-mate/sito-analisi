$7000$ euros are invested at $5$ percent compounded annually for two years. Which expression gives the accumulated value in euros?

- $7000(1+2\cdot5\%)$
- $7000(1+5\%)$
- $\dfrac{7000}{(1+5\%)^2}$
- $7000(1+5\%)^2$

## Explanation

After the first year, the principal has been multiplied by one plus the rate. The same factor applies to the accumulated value during the second year.
