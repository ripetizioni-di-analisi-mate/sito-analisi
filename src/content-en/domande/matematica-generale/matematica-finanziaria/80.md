A loan of $20\,000$ euros at the annual rate of $5\%$ requires, in the first $2$ years, the payment of interest only at the end of each year. From the third year the debt is repaid with Italian amortization in $4$ annual installments paid at the end of each year. What is the installment paid at the end of the third year?

- $1000$ euros
- $5000$ euros
- $4333.33$ euros
- $6000$ euros

## Explanation

In the first two years the principal portion is zero, so the debt remains $20\,000$ euros until the end of the second year. From the third year the four principal portions are constant and repay the whole debt, therefore each one is $\dfrac{20\,000}{4} = 5000$ euros. The interest portion of the third year is computed on the debt still to be repaid at the beginning of the year, $0.05 \cdot 20\,000 = 1000$ euros. Hence the installment of the third year is $5000 + 1000 = 6000$ euros. The value $4333.33$ euros spreads the debt over all $6$ years, $\dfrac{20\,000}{6} + 1000$, although no principal is repaid in the first two.
