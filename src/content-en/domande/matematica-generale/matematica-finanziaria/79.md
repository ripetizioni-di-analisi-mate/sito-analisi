A mortgage with French amortization at the annual rate of $10\%$ is repaid with constant installments of $1000$ euros. Which part of the last installment reduces the debt, that is, what is its principal portion? (result rounded to the cent)

- $909.09$ euros
- $1000$ euros
- $90.91$ euros
- $1100$ euros

## Explanation

After the last installment the outstanding balance is zero, so the principal portion of the last installment equals the outstanding balance before that installment, $Q_n = S_{n-1}$. By the prospective method $S_{n-1}$ is the value, one year earlier, of the last installment, that is, $S_{n-1} = 1000 \cdot 1.1^{-1} \approx 909.09$ euros. Therefore $Q_n \approx 909.09$ euros, and the interest portion of the last installment is $1000 - 909.09 = 90.91$ euros. The result depends neither on the number of installments nor on the initial debt.
