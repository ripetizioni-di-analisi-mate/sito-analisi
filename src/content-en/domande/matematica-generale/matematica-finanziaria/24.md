What is the annual rate equivalent to the semiannual rate of $3\%$ under compound interest? (percentage rounded to two decimal places)

- $6\%$
- $6{,}09\%$
- $1{,}49\%$
- $9{,}27\%$

## Explanation

The semiannual rate $i_2$ and the annual rate $i$ are equivalent if $(1 + i_2)^{2} = 1 + i$, so $i = (1 + i_2)^{2} - 1$. With $i_2 = 0{,}03$ one obtains
$$
i = 1{,}03^{2} - 1 = 1{,}0609 - 1 = 0{,}0609 ,
$$
that is, $6{,}09\%$. The rate is larger than $2 \cdot 3\% = 6\%$, because the interest of the first half-year earns interest in the second. The value $1{,}49\%$ is the semiannual rate equivalent to $3\%$ per year, that is, the result of the formula applied in the opposite direction.
