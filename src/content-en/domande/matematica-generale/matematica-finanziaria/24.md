In a French amortization with $3$ annual installments paid at the end of each year at the rate of $20\%$, the principal portion of the first installment is $Q_1 = 1000$ euros. What is the initial debt $S$?

- $3000$ euros
- $3640$ euros
- $4368$ euros
- $2106.48$ euros

## Explanation

In the French schedule the principal portions form a geometric progression with ratio $1 + i$, so $Q_2 = 1000 \cdot 1.2 = 1200$ euros and $Q_3 = 1000 \cdot 1.2^{2} = 1440$ euros. Since the sum of the principal portions equals the debt,
$$
S = 1000 + 1200 + 1440 = 3640 \text{ euros} ,
$$
that is, $S = Q_1\,s_{\overline{3}|0.2}$. The installment is therefore $R = Q_1 + 0.2 \cdot 3640 = 1728$ euros. The value $3000$ euros treats the principal portions as constant, as in the Italian schedule, and $2106.48$ euros is $Q_1\,a_{\overline{3}|0.2}$, the present value of three installments equal to $Q_1$.
