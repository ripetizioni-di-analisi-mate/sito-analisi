For the investment $(-1000,\ 400,\ 400,\ 400)$, with the cash flows at times $0, 1, 2, 3$, what is the limit of $\operatorname{VAN}(i)$ as $i \to +\infty$?

- $0$
- $-1000$
- $200$
- $-\infty$

## Explanation

One has
$$
\operatorname{VAN}(i) = -1000 + \frac{400}{1 + i} + \frac{400}{(1 + i)^{2}} + \frac{400}{(1 + i)^{3}} .
$$
As $i \to +\infty$ each denominator tends to $+\infty$, so the three inflow terms tend to $0$. Therefore $\operatorname{VAN}(i) \to -1000$, that is, the NPV tends to the initial cash flow. The value $200$ is $\operatorname{VAN}(0)$, the sum of the cash flows. The negative limit and the value $\operatorname{VAN}(0) > 0$ are the two facts that allow the intermediate value theorem to be applied to prove the existence of the IRR.
