A debt of $9000$ euros at $7$ percent per year has a first payment of $4500$ euros at the end of year one. What balance remains immediately after the payment?

- $9630$
- $5130$
- $4815$
- $4500$

## Explanation

Before the payment, the debt includes interest for the first year. Subtract the full payment from that amount.
