A mortgage of $100\,000$ euros is repaid by French amortization over $20$ years, with semiannual installments in arrears, at the effective annual rate of $4\%$. What is the semiannual installment? (result rounded to the cent)

- $3655.57$ euros
- $3679.09$ euros
- $3643.01$ euros
- $5052.35$ euros

## Explanation

The installments are semiannual, so one needs the semiannual rate equivalent to $4\%$ per year, $i_2 = \sqrt{1.04} - 1 \approx 0.019804$. In $20$ years there are $n = 40$ installments, and in French amortization the installment is $R = \dfrac{S}{a_{\overline{40}|i_2}}$. Since $a_{\overline{40}|i_2} \approx 27.449794$, one obtains
$$
R \approx \frac{100\,000}{27.449794} \approx 3643.01 .
$$
The semiannual installment is $3643.01$ euros. The value $3655.57$ euros corresponds to the semiannual rate of $2\%$, which is equivalent to $4\%$ per year only under simple interest.
