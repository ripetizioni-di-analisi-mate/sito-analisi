A loan of $10\,000$ euros is repaid with French amortization in $20$ annual installments paid at the end of each year at the rate of $6\%$, with installment $R \approx 871.85$ euros. From which installment on is the principal portion greater than the interest portion?

- From installment $11$
- From installment $9$
- From installment $10$
- From installment $8$

## Explanation

The first principal portion is $Q_1 = R - 0.06 \cdot 10\,000 \approx 271.85$ euros, and in the French schedule $Q_k = Q_1 \cdot 1.06^{\,k-1}$. Since $Q_k + I_k = R$, the principal portion exceeds the interest portion when $Q_k > \dfrac{R}{2} \approx 435.92$ euros, that is, when
$$
1.06^{\,k-1} > \frac{435.92}{271.85} \approx 1.6035 .
$$
One has $1.06^{8} \approx 1.5938$ and $1.06^{9} \approx 1.6895$, so the condition holds for $k - 1 \geq 9$. Therefore the principal portion exceeds the interest portion from installment $10$ on, with $Q_{10} \approx 459.28$ euros and $I_{10} \approx 412.57$ euros.
