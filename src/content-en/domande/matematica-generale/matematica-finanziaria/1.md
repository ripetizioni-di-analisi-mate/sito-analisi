## Explanation

Under simple interest the accumulated value is $M = C\,(1 + i\,t)$, with the time $t$ measured in years. Since $10$ months are $\dfrac{10}{12}$ of a year, one sets $t = \dfrac{10}{12}$, and the rate of $2.5\%$ is written $i = 0.025$. Therefore
$$
M = 2400 \left( 1 + 0.025 \cdot \frac{10}{12} \right) = 2400 + 50 = 2450 .
$$
Hence the accumulated value is $2450$ euros, of which $50$ euros are interest.
