A debt of $5000$ euros at $3$ percent per year has a first payment of $2500$ euros at the end of year one. What balance remains immediately after the payment?

- $5150$
- $2575$
- $2500$
- $2650$

## Explanation

Before the payment, the debt includes interest for the first year. Subtract the full payment from that amount.
