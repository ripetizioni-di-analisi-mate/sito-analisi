An amount of $1000$ euros is invested for one year at the annual rate of $5\%$ under simple interest; at the end of the year the whole accumulated value is reinvested for another year, at the same rate and under the same model. Which statement about the final accumulated value is true?

- It equals the accumulated value under simple interest at $5\%$ for $2$ years
- It is less than the accumulated value under simple interest at $5\%$ for $2$ years
- It equals the accumulated value under compound interest at $5\%$ for $2$ years
- It depends on the principal invested

## Explanation

After the first year the accumulated value is $1000 \cdot 1.05 = 1050$ euros. This amount is reinvested for one year, so the final accumulated value is
$$
1050 \cdot 1.05 = 1000 \cdot 1.05^{2} = 1102.50 \text{ euros} .
$$
This is the accumulated value under compound interest at $5\%$ for $2$ years, because the interest of the first year also earns interest in the second. Under simple interest for $2$ years the accumulated value would be $1000 \cdot (1 + 0.05 \cdot 2) = 1100$ euros, less than $1102.50$ euros.
