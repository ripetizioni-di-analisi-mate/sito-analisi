At the annual rate of $4\%$ under compound interest, which amount available in $3$ years is financially equivalent to $2000$ euros available in one year? (result rounded to the cent)

- $2160$ euros
- $2249.73$ euros
- $2163.20$ euros
- $1849.11$ euros

## Explanation

Two amounts are equivalent if they have the same value at a valuation date, and under compound interest the date can be chosen freely. At date $3$ the amount of $2000$ euros available at time $1$ is accumulated for $2$ years, so it is worth
$$
2000 \cdot 1.04^{2} = 2000 \cdot 1.0816 = 2163.20 \text{ euros} .
$$
Hence the equivalent amount in $3$ years is $2163.20$ euros. The value $2249.73$ euros is obtained by accumulating for $3$ years, as if the $2000$ euros were available today, and $2160$ euros is obtained under simple interest.
