A loan of $36000$ euros is repaid in $4$ end-of-year payments under Italian amortization at an annual rate of $7$ percent. What is the first payment?

- $2520$
- $9000.0$
- $10890.0$
- $11520.0$

## Explanation

The constant principal component equals the loan divided by the number of payments. Interest at the first due date is calculated on the full opening balance.
