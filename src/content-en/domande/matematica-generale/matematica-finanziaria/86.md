A plot of land is bought today for $20\,000$ euros and is leased forever at $1500$ euros per year, paid at the end of each year. What is the internal rate of return of the purchase?

- $7.5\%$
- $13.33\%$
- $75\%$
- $3.75\%$

## Explanation

The rents form an ordinary perpetuity, which at the rate $i > 0$ is worth $\dfrac{1500}{i}$. The NPV is therefore $\operatorname{VAN}(i) = -20\,000 + \dfrac{1500}{i}$, which vanishes for $i = \dfrac{1500}{20\,000} = 0.075$. Hence the IRR is $7.5\%$, that is, the ratio of the annual income to the cost. The value $13.33\%$ comes from the inverse ratio $\dfrac{20\,000}{1500} \approx 13.33$.
