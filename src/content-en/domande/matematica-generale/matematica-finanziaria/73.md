A project costs $10000$ euros today and pays $11900$ euros in one year. Which expression is its NPV at $5$ percent?

- $\dfrac{11900-10000}{1+5\%}$
- $11900-10000$
- $11900(1+5\%)-10000$
- $\dfrac{11900}{1+5\%}-10000$

## Explanation

The initial cost is already stated at the valuation date. Divide the receipt by one plus the rate, then subtract the cost.
