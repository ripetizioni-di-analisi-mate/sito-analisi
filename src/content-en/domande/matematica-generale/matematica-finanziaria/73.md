A perpetuity pays $300$ euros at the end of each half-year. The effective annual rate is $6.09\%$ under compound interest. What is the present value of the perpetuity? (result rounded to the cent)

- $4926.11$ euros
- $5000$ euros
- $20\,000$ euros
- $10\,000$ euros

## Explanation

The payments are semiannual, so the equivalent semiannual rate is needed, $i_2 = \sqrt{1.0609} - 1 = 1.03 - 1 = 3\%$. The present value of an ordinary perpetuity is the payment divided by the rate per period, therefore $V = \dfrac{300}{0.03} = 10\,000$ euros. The value $4926.11$ euros divides the semiannual payment by the annual rate, and $5000$ euros divides it by $6\%$. The value $20\,000$ euros divides the annual total of the payments, $600$ euros, by the semiannual rate.
