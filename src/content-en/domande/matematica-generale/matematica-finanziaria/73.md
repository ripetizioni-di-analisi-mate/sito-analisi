A project costs $10000$ euros today and generates $11900$ euros in one year. Which expression is its NPV at $5$ percent?

- $\dfrac{11900-10000}{1+5\%}$
- $11900-10000$
- $11900(1+5\%)-10000$
- $\dfrac{11900}{1+5\%}-10000$

## Explanation

The initial cost is already stated at the valuation date. The present value of the receipt is the receipt divided by one plus the rate. The initial cost is then subtracted.
