A foundation pays €600 at the end of every year in perpetuity. Only the fourth payment will be reduced to €400. At an effective annual rate $i>0$, by how much does the present value fall relative to the original perpetuity?

- $\dfrac{200}{i}$ euros
- $200(1+i)^4$ euros
- $\dfrac{600}{(1+i)^4}$ euros
- $\dfrac{200}{(1+i)^4}$ euros

## Explanation

Subtracting the two payment streams cancels every payment except that in year four. The only future loss is €200 on that date. Discounting it for four years gives the reduction in present value.
