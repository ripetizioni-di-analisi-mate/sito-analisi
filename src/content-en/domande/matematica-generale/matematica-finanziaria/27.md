A loan of $6000$ euros at the annual rate of $10\%$ is repaid with three annual installments paid at the end of each year, with principal portions $Q_1 = 3000$, $Q_2 = 1000$ and $Q_3 = 2000$ euros. What is the second installment?

- $1600$ euros
- $1300$ euros
- $1100$ euros
- $1000$ euros

## Explanation

After the first installment the outstanding balance is $S_1 = 6000 - 3000 = 3000$ euros. The interest portion of the second installment is computed on this balance, so $I_2 = 0.1 \cdot 3000 = 300$ euros. Therefore the second installment is
$$
R_2 = Q_2 + I_2 = 1000 + 300 = 1300 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $1600$ euros computes the interest on the initial debt of $6000$ euros. The value $1100$ euros computes it on the principal portion $Q_2$ alone.
