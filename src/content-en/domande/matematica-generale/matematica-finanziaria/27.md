A principal of $2000$ euros is invested for $3$ years under continuous compounding with force of interest $\delta = 4\%$. What is the accumulated value? (result rounded to the cent)

- $2249{,}73$ euros
- $2254{,}99$ euros
- $2240$ euros
- $2081{,}62$ euros

## Explanation

Under continuous compounding the accumulated value is $M = C\,e^{\delta t}$. With $\delta = 0{,}04$ and $t = 3$ the exponent is $\delta t = 0{,}12$, so
$$
M = 2000\,e^{0{,}12} \approx 2000 \cdot 1{,}127497 \approx 2254{,}99 .
$$
Hence the accumulated value is $2254{,}99$ euros. The value $2249{,}73$ euros is the accumulated value under compound interest at the annual rate of $4\%$, which is smaller because the annual rate equivalent to the force of interest $\delta = 4\%$ is $e^{0{,}04} - 1 \approx 4{,}08\%$.
