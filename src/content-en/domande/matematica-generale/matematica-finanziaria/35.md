What year-end payment, made each year for $10$ years at the annual rate of $3\%$ under compound interest, produces an accumulated value of $20\,000$ euros at the time of the last payment? (result rounded to the cent)

- $1744{,}61$ euros
- $2344{,}61$ euros
- $2000$ euros
- $1693{,}80$ euros

## Explanation

The accumulated value of the $10$ year-end payments is $M = R\,s_{\overline{10}|0{,}03}$, so the payment is $R = \dfrac{M}{s_{\overline{10}|0{,}03}}$. One has
$$
s_{\overline{10}|0{,}03} = \frac{1{,}03^{10} - 1}{0{,}03} \approx 11{,}463879 ,
$$
therefore $R = \dfrac{20\,000}{11{,}463879} \approx 1744{,}61$ euros. The value $2344{,}61$ euros is obtained by dividing by $a_{\overline{10}|0{,}03}$, that is, by treating $20\,000$ euros as a present value, and $2000$ euros is the payment one would have without interest.
