$9000$ euros are invested for $4$ years with continuous compounding at an annual force of interest $\delta=7\%$. Which expression gives the accumulated value?

- $9000(1+4\cdot 7\%)$
- $9000\,e^{-4\cdot 7\%}$
- $9000\,e^{4\cdot 7\%}$
- $9000(1+7\%)^4$

## Explanation

With continuous compounding, the accumulation factor is the exponential of the product of the force of interest and elapsed time. Here time is measured in years.
