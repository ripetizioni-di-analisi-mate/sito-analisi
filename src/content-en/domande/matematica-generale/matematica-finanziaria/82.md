A loan of $10\,000$ euros is repaid with French amortization in $4$ annual installments paid at the end of each year at the rate of $5\%$, with installment $R \approx 2820.12$ euros. Right after the second installment the rate becomes $8\%$, and the two remaining installments are recomputed, equal to each other, on the outstanding balance. What is the new installment? (result rounded to the cent)

- $2940.54$ euros
- $2621.88$ euros
- $2500$ euros
- $5663.26$ euros

## Explanation

By the prospective method the outstanding balance after the second installment is the value of the two remaining installments at the original rate, $S_2 = 2820.12 \cdot a_{\overline{2}|0.05} \approx 2820.12 \cdot 1.859410 \approx 5243.76$ euros. The new plan is a French amortization of $S_2$ in $2$ installments at $8\%$, so the new installment is
$$
R' = \frac{5243.76}{a_{\overline{2}|0.08}} \approx \frac{5243.76}{1.783265} \approx 2940.54 \text{ euros} .
$$
The new installment is larger than the original one because interest is computed at a higher rate. The value $2621.88$ euros divides the outstanding balance by $2$ without interest, and $5663.26$ euros is the outstanding balance with one year of interest at $8\%$.
