In a loan with level annual payments, the balance immediately after payment three is €1800. The borrower immediately repays an extra €200 of principal. Future payments keep their original dates and the annual rate is $5\%$. By how much does just the interest component of payment four decrease?

- €10
- €200
- €80
- €90

## Explanation

Interest in the fourth payment accrues on the balance between the third and fourth dates. The extra repayment reduces that balance by €200 for a full year. The decrease in interest is $200\cdot0.05=10$ euros.
