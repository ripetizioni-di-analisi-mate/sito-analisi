What is the present value of $3090$ euros available in $6$ months with rational discount, that is, discounting under simple interest, at the annual rate of $6\%$? (result rounded to the cent)

- $2997.30$ euros
- $3000$ euros
- $2915.09$ euros
- $3001.27$ euros

## Explanation

Under simple interest the present value of an amount $M$ available in $t$ years is $V = \dfrac{M}{1 + i\,t}$. With $t = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $i = 0.06$ one has $1 + i\,t = 1.03$, so
$$
V = \frac{3090}{1.03} = 3000 .
$$
Hence the present value is $3000$ euros. With commercial discount at the rate $d = 6\%$ one would obtain $3090\,(1 - 0.03) = 2997.30$ euros, and with discounting under compound interest $3090 \cdot 1.06^{-\frac{1}{2}} \approx 3001.27$ euros.
