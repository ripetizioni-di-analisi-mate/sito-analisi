$10000$ euros are paid today and $10700$ euros are received in one year. What is the internal rate of return, as a percentage?

- $5$
- $10$
- $107$
- $7$

## Explanation

With a single future receipt, NPV is zero when its present value equals the cost. The rate equals the receipt divided by the cost, minus one.
