A loan is repaid with Italian amortization in annual installments paid at the end of each year. The first two rows of the amortization schedule are the following (amounts in euros).

| $k$ | $R_k$ | $Q_k$ | $I_k$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $1$ | $2600$ | $2000$ | $600$ |
| $2$ | $2480$ | $2000$ | $480$ |

What is the annual rate of the loan?

- $30\%$
- $6\%$
- $23.08\%$
- $4.8\%$

## Explanation

Between the first and the second installment the outstanding balance decreases by the principal portion, $2000$ euros, and the interest portion decreases by $600 - 480 = 120$ euros. Since $I_k = i\,S_{k-1}$, the decrease of the interest portion is $i \cdot 2000$, so $i = \dfrac{120}{2000} = 6\%$. The initial debt is therefore $S = \dfrac{I_1}{i} = \dfrac{600}{0.06} = 10\,000$ euros, repaid in $\dfrac{10\,000}{2000} = 5$ installments. The value $30\%$ divides the first interest portion by the principal portion instead of by the initial debt, and $23.08\%$ divides it by the first installment.
