What is the force of interest equivalent to the annual rate of $3\%$?

- $2{,}956\%$
- $3{,}045\%$
- $1{,}284\%$
- $138{,}629\%$

## Explanation

Continuous compounding with force $\delta$ is equivalent to compound interest at the annual rate $i$ when $e^{\delta} = 1 + i$. Therefore $\delta = \ln(1 + i)$, with the natural logarithm and the rate written as a decimal number, $i = 0{,}03$. One obtains
$$
\delta = \ln 1{,}03 \approx 0{,}02956 ,
$$
that is, $2{,}956\%$. The number $e^{0{,}03} - 1 \approx 3{,}045\%$ is instead the annual rate equivalent to the force $\delta = 3\%$.
