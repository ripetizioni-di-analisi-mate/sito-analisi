An archive lists $30$ passes among $40$ students in course A and $30$ passes among $60$ students in course B. A passing student is chosen at random. What is the probability that the student came from A?

- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac3{10}$

## Explanation

After conditioning on a pass, the reference group consists of the $60$ passing students. Of these, $30$ came from A, so the probability is $\dfrac{30}{60}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
