A school has $200$ students, including $120$ girls. There are $80$ students who play a sport, of whom $30$ are girls. A student who plays a sport is selected at random. What is the probability that the student is a girl?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

The condition is “the student plays a sport,” so the possible outcomes are the $80$ students who play a sport. Of these, $30$ are girls, so the probability is $\dfrac{30}{80} = \dfrac{3}{8}$. The value $\dfrac{30}{120} = \dfrac{1}{4}$ is the reverse conditional probability, that is, the probability that a girl plays a sport.
