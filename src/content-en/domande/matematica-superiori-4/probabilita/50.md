In course A, $30$ of the $40$ enrolled students passed; in course B, $30$ of $60$. One of the students who passed in the two courses is chosen at random. What is the probability that the student comes from course A?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

The choice is made among the students who passed, so the possible outcomes are the $30+30=60$ students who passed in the two courses. The favourable outcomes are the $30$ students who passed in course A. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{30}{60}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is instead the probability that a student of course A passes, that is, the conditional probability in the opposite direction.
