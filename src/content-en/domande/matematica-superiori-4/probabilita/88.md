A fair die is rolled $5$ times. What is the probability that exactly $2$ of the rolls show a number less than or equal to $2$?

- $\dfrac{80}{243}$
- $\dfrac{8}{243}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{32}{243}$

## Explanation

On each roll rolling $1$ or $2$ counts as a success and has probability $p = \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, and the rolls are independent. By the Bernoulli model with $n = 5$ and $k = 2$,
$$
P = \binom{5}{2} \left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^2 \left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^3 = 10 \cdot \frac{1}{9} \cdot \frac{8}{27} = \frac{80}{243} .
$$
Omitting the binomial coefficient gives $\dfrac{8}{243}$, the probability of just one specified sequence.
