A card is drawn from a deck of $40$ Neapolitan cards. Let $A$ be “drawing an ace” and $B$ be “drawing a face card.” Which statement is true?

- $B$ is the complement of $A$
- $A \cup B$ is the certain event
- $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive
- $A \cap B$ contains $4$ cards

## Explanation

In each suit the face cards are the jack, knight, and king. The ace is not a face card. Therefore no card belongs to both events: $A \cap B = \varnothing$ and hence $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive. The union contains $4 + 12 = 16$ cards out of $40$ and therefore is not the certain event. For the same reason, $B$ is not the complement of $A$.
