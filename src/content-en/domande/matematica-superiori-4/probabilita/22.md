An event $A$ is contained in $B$. What is $(A\cup B)\setminus(A\cap B)$?

- $A$
- $A\cup B$
- $B\setminus A$
- $\varnothing$ in all cases

## Explanation

Since $A\subseteq B$, the union is $B$ and the intersection is $A$. The difference consists of the outcomes in $B$ that do not belong to $A$.
