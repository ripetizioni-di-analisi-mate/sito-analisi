A coin is tossed $4$ times. How many elements does the sample space contain?

- $8$
- $16$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each outcome is an ordered sequence of four results, each heads or tails. Each toss has $2$ possible results, so by the fundamental counting principle the number of sequences is $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 2^4 = 16$. For example, $TTCT$ and $CTTT$ are distinct outcomes even though they contain the same number of heads.
