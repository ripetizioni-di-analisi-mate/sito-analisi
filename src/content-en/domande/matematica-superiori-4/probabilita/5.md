An urn contains $7$ white balls and $3$ black balls. Two balls are drawn with replacement. What is the probability that their colors match?

- $\dfrac{49}{100}$
- $\dfrac{21}{50}$
- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $\dfrac{29}{50}$

## Explanation

With replacement, the composition of the urn stays the same, so the two draws are independent. The probability of drawing two white balls is $\dfrac{7}{10} \cdot \dfrac{7}{10} = \dfrac{49}{100}$ and that of drawing two black balls is $\dfrac{3}{10} \cdot \dfrac{3}{10} = \dfrac{9}{100}$. The two cases are mutually exclusive, so
$$
P = \frac{49}{100} + \frac{9}{100} = \frac{58}{100} = \frac{29}{50} .
$$
