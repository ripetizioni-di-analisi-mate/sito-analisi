Three suppliers $A$, $B$, and $C$ supply $50\%$, $30\%$, and $20\%$ of the parts in a warehouse, respectively. The percentages of defective parts are $1\%$ for $A$, $2\%$ for $B$, and $3\%$ for $C$. What is the probability that a randomly selected part is defective?

- $0.02$
- $0.06$
- $0.017$
- $0.006$

## Explanation

The three suppliers form a partition, since each part is supplied by exactly one of them. By the law of total probability,
$$
P(D) = 0.5 \cdot 0.01 + 0.3 \cdot 0.02 + 0.2 \cdot 0.03 = 0.005 + 0.006 + 0.006 = 0.017 .
$$
Each defect percentage is multiplied by the corresponding supplier’s share of the parts, so the result differs from the simple average $0.02$.
