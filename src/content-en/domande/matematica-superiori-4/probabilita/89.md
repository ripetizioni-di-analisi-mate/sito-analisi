For three independent trials with success probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, at least one success is known to have occurred. What is the probability that all three were successes?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac17$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Of the eight equally likely outcomes only the all-failure outcome is excluded. Exactly one of the remaining seven contains three successes, giving conditional probability $\dfrac{1}{7}$.
