A basketball player makes a free throw with probability $0.8$, and the shots are independent. The player takes $3$ shots. What is the probability of making at least $2$ shots?

- $0.384$
- $0.64$
- $0.896$
- $0.992$

## Explanation

Making at least $2$ shots means making exactly $2$ or exactly $3$, and the cases are mutually exclusive. By the Bernoulli model,
$$
P(2) = \binom{3}{2} \cdot 0.8^2 \cdot 0.2 = 3 \cdot 0.64 \cdot 0.2 = 0.384 ,
\qquad
P(3) = 0.8^3 = 0.512 .
$$
The required probability is therefore $0.384 + 0.512 = 0.896$.
