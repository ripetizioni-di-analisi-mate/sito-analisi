Four distinct students are assigned random positions in a row. What is the probability that Ada stands before Bruno, without requiring them to be adjacent?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

Swapping Ada and Bruno pairs every favorable arrangement with an unfavorable one. None stays fixed, so the two classes have equal probability.
