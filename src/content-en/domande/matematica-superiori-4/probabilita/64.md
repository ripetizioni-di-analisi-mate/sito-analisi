A fair coin is tossed until the first heads. What is the probability that the game ends on exactly the fourth toss?

- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac{15}{16}$

## Explanation

Ending on the fourth toss requires three tails and then a head in that exact order. Independence gives $(\dfrac{1}{2})^4=\dfrac{1}{16}$ for this single sequence.
