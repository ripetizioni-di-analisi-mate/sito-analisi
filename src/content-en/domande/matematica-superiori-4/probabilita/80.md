A filter flags one in ten legitimate messages. If a message is flagged, can this fact alone establish a $90\%$ chance that it is spam?

- Yes, simply subtract $0.1$ from one.
- Yes, if there are many messages.
- No, the spam rate and the flag rate for spam are also needed.
- No, the probability is certainly $0.1$.

## Explanation

The given rate is $P(\text{flagged}\mid\text{legitimate})$. Finding the share of spam among flagged messages requires the contributions of flagged messages from both categories.
