## Explanation

Drawing $3$ balls at the same time means choosing a group of $3$ balls from $12$, so there are $\dbinom{12}{3} = 220$ possible outcomes. To obtain a favorable outcome, choose one red ball from $5$, one blue ball from $4$, and one green ball from $3$. By the fundamental counting principle this gives $5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 = 60$ favorable outcomes. Therefore $P = \dfrac{60}{220} = \dfrac{3}{11}$.
