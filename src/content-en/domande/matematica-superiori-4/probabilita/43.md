A card is drawn from a deck of $40$ Neapolitan cards. What is the probability that it is a face card or belongs to the suit of cups?

- $\dfrac{11}{20}$
- $\dfrac{19}{40}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{40}$

## Explanation

There are $12$ face cards, $10$ cards in the suit of cups, and $3$ face cards in that suit, namely the jack, knight, and king of cups. By the addition rule,
$$
P = \frac{12}{40} + \frac{10}{40} - \frac{3}{40} = \frac{19}{40} .
$$
The three face cards in the suit of cups belong to both events, so without the subtraction they would be counted twice.
