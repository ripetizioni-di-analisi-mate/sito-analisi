In a class, $60\%$ speak English and $50\%$ speak Spanish. Everyone speaks at least one of these two languages. What proportion speak exactly one?

- $10\%$
- $90\%$
- $50\%$
- $100\%$

## Explanation

The proportions sum to $110\%$, with the extra $10\%$ counting bilingual students twice. As everyone speaks at least one language, the remaining $90\%$ speak exactly one.
