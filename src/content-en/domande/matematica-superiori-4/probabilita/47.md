Two events $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive, with $P(A) = 0.3$ and $P(B) = 0.5$. Which statement is true?

- $P(A \cup B) = 0.65$
- $P(A \cap B) = 0.15$
- $A$ and $B$ are independent
- $P(A \cup B) = 0.8$

## Explanation

The events are mutually exclusive, so $A \cap B = \varnothing$ and $P(A \cap B) = 0$. By the addition rule for mutually exclusive events, $P(A \cup B) = 0.3 + 0.5 = 0.8$. The events are dependent, since $P(A) \cdot P(B) = 0.15$ differs from $P(A \cap B) = 0$.
