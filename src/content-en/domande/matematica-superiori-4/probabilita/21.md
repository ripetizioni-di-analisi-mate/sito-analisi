On a roll of a die, which of these pairs consists of mutually exclusive events?

- “Rolling an even number” and “rolling a multiple of $3$”
- “Rolling a prime number” and “rolling an even number”
- “Rolling a number greater than $4$” and “rolling an odd number”
- “Rolling an even number” and “rolling $3$”

## Explanation

Two events are mutually exclusive if their intersection is empty. The event “rolling an even number” is $\{ 2, 4, 6 \}$ and does not contain $3$, so the two events are mutually exclusive. In the other pairs there is a common outcome: $6$ among the even numbers and the multiples of $3$, $2$ among the primes and the even numbers, and $5$ among the numbers greater than $4$ and the odd numbers.
