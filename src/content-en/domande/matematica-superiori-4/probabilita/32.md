Which of the following probabilities can be calculated using the classical definition?

- The probability that a tossed thumbtack lands point up
- The probability that a team wins its next game
- The probability of drawing a king from a deck of $40$ cards
- The probability that a loaded die shows $6$

## Explanation

The classical definition applies when the sample space is finite and the outcomes are equally likely. All $40$ cards in a well-shuffled deck are equally likely to be drawn, so $P(\text{kings}) = \dfrac{4}{40}$. The outcomes of the thumbtack and the loaded die are not equally likely, and a win in one particular game is an event that cannot be repeated, so the classical definition does not apply in those cases.
