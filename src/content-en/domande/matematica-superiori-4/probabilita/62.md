Two events $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive, with $P(A) = 0.2$ and $P(B) = 0.5$. Which statement is true?

- $A$ and $B$ are dependent
- $A$ and $B$ are independent
- $P(A \cap B) = 0.1$
- $P(A \mid B) = 0.2$

## Explanation

The events are mutually exclusive, so $P(A \cap B) = 0$. The product $P(A) \cdot P(B) = 0.1$ differs from $0$, so the independence condition is not satisfied and the events are dependent. Indeed, $P(A \mid B) = \dfrac{0}{0.5} = 0$, while $P(A) = 0.2$.
