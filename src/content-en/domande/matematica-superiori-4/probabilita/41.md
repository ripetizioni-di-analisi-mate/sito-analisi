For two events $A$ and $B$, we know that $P(A) = 0.5$, $P(B) = 0.3$, and $P(A \cap B) = 0.1$. What is the value of $P(A \cup B)$?

- $0.8$
- $0.6$
- $0.15$
- $0.7$

## Explanation

The events are not mutually exclusive, since $P(A \cap B) = 0.1$ differs from $0$. By the addition rule,
$$
P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B) = 0.5 + 0.3 - 0.1 = 0.7 .
$$
Subtract the probability of the intersection because the sum $P(A) + P(B)$ counts the outcomes common to both events twice.
