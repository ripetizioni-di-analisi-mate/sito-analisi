Two fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that at least one of the two dice shows $6$ or that the sum of the two numbers is $8$?

- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{7}{18}$

## Explanation

Let $A$ be the event "at least one die shows $6$" and $B$ the event "the sum is $8$". Out of $36$ equally likely pairs, $11$ contain at least one $6$ and $5$ have sum $8$, namely $(2, 6)$, $(3, 5)$, $(4, 4)$, $(5, 3)$, $(6, 2)$. The pairs $(2, 6)$ and $(6, 2)$ belong to both events, so the events are not mutually exclusive and $P(A \cap B) = \dfrac{2}{36}$. By the addition rule
$$
P(A \cup B) = \frac{11}{36} + \frac{5}{36} - \frac{2}{36} = \frac{14}{36} = \frac{7}{18} .
$$
