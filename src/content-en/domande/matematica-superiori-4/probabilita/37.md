A fair coin is tossed $4$ times. What is the probability of obtaining at least one tail?

- $\dfrac{15}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$

## Explanation

Four tosses give $2^4 = 16$ equally likely sequences. The complement of “at least one tail” is “four heads,” which contains only the sequence $TTTT$. By the complement rule, $P = 1 - \dfrac{1}{16} = \dfrac{15}{16}$.
