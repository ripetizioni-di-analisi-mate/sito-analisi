Two real numbers are chosen independently and uniformly between zero and one. What is the probability that they are **exactly** equal?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The outcomes are the points of a unit square. Equality describes its diagonal, whose area is zero even though it contains infinitely many points.
