Two dice are rolled. Let $A$ be “the sum of the two numbers is even” and $B$ be “the two numbers are equal.” Which statement is true?

- $B$ is a subset of $A$
- $A$ is a subset of $B$
- $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive
- $A \cup B$ is the certain event

## Explanation

If the two numbers are equal, their sum is twice one of them and is therefore even. Thus every outcome in $B$ belongs to $A$, so $B \subseteq A$, and the two events can occur together. The reverse inclusion is false, since the pair $(1, 3)$ has an even sum and distinct numbers. Finally, the pair $(1, 2)$ has an odd sum and distinct numbers, so $A \cup B$ is not the certain event.
