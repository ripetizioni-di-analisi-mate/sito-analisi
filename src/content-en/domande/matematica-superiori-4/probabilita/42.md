A player chooses one of three equally likely doors, only one of which wins. The host knows the prize location, always opens an unchosen losing door and offers a switch to the sole other closed door. What is the win probability **when always switching**?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Switching wins exactly when the original choice was wrong. That happens for two of the three prize locations, and the host's informed action does not change the initial event.
