For two events $A$ and $B$, we know that $P(A) = 0.4$ and $P(B) = 0.5$. Which value is impossible for $P(A \cup B)$?

- $0.5$
- $0.7$
- $0.9$
- $0.95$

## Explanation

By the addition rule, $P(A \cup B) = 0.9 - P(A \cap B)$, and $P(A \cap B) \geq 0$ by the first axiom. Thus $P(A \cup B) \leq 0.9$, ruling out $0.95$. The value $0.9$ occurs when the events are mutually exclusive and the value $0.5$ when $A \subseteq B$, since in that case $A \cup B = B$.
