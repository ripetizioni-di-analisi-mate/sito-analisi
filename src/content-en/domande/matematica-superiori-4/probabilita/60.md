Two independent events $A$ and $B$ have probabilities $P(A) = 0.3$ and $P(B) = 0.6$. What is the value of $P(A \cup B)$?

- $0.9$
- $0.72$
- $0.18$
- $0.82$

## Explanation

The events are independent, so $P(A \cap B) = 0.3 \cdot 0.6 = 0.18$. By the addition rule,
$$
P(A \cup B) = 0.3 + 0.6 - 0.18 = 0.72 .
$$
The complement gives the same value, since $\overline{A}$ and $\overline{B}$ are also independent and $1 - 0.7 \cdot 0.4 = 0.72$.
