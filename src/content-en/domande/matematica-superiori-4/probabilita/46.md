Two dice are rolled. What is the probability that their sum is at least $9$?

- $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- $\dfrac{4}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The sum is at least $9$ if it equals $9$, $10$, $11$, or $12$, and these four cases are mutually exclusive. The number of favorable pairs is $4$ for the sum $9$, $3$ for the sum $10$, $2$ for the sum $11$, and $1$ for the sum $12$. By the addition rule for mutually exclusive events,
$$
P = \frac{4 + 3 + 2 + 1}{36} = \frac{10}{36} = \frac{5}{18} .
$$
The sums from $2$ to $12$ are not equally likely, so the ratio $\dfrac{4}{11}$ of favorable sums to possible sums is incorrect.
