A fair six-sided die and another die with unknown probabilities are rolled independently. What is the probability of an even sum, regardless of the second die's distribution?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- It cannot be determined.

## Explanation

For any fixed outcome of the second die, exactly three faces of the fair die match its parity. The conditional probability is always one half, and therefore so is the overall probability.
