A rare event has probability $p>0$. It has not occurred in $100$ independent trials. Which conclusion still holds?

- This proves $p=0$.
- The observed absence is consistent with $0<p<1$.
- It must occur on the next trial.
- Its probability on the next trial is exactly $\dfrac{1}{100}$.

## Explanation

For $0<p<1$ the probability of a hundred nonoccurrences is $(1-p)^{100}>0$. A finite sequence with no successes is therefore possible without forcing the parameter to zero.
