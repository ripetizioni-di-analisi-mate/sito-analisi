A fair coin is tossed $5$ times and shows heads every time. What is the probability of heads on the sixth toss?

- $\dfrac{1}{64}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{32}$
- $\dfrac{63}{64}$

## Explanation

Coin tosses are independent trials, so the results of the first five tosses do not change the probability on the sixth. For a fair coin the probability of heads is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ on each toss. The value $\dfrac{1}{64} = \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^6$ is the probability of obtaining six heads in six tosses, calculated before the first toss.
