A die is loaded so that the face $6$ has probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$, while the other five faces are equally likely. What is the probability of rolling an odd number?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{15}$

## Explanation

By the second axiom the probabilities of the six faces add up to $1$, so the faces from $1$ to $5$ have total probability $1 - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. Each therefore has probability $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{2}{15}$. The odd faces are $1$, $3$, and $5$, so $P(\text{odd}) = 3 \cdot \dfrac{2}{15} = \dfrac{2}{5}$.
