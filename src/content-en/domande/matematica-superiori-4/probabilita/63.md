A service has two independent lines with availability probabilities $0.9$ and $0.8$. If both must be available for transmission, what is the probability of an interruption?

- $0.28$
- $0.02$
- $0.72$
- $0.30$

## Explanation

Transmission succeeds with probability $0.9\cdot0.8=0.72$. An interruption is the complement of success, so its probability is $1-0.72=0.28$.
