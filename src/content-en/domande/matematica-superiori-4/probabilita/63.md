Two students take an exam, with independent results. The first passes with probability $0.8$ and the second with probability $0.7$. What is the probability that neither passes?

- $0.06$
- $0.44$
- $0.5$
- $0.56$

## Explanation

By the complement rule the probabilities of failing the exam are $1 - 0.8 = 0.2$ and $1 - 0.7 = 0.3$. The events “the first student passes” and “the second student passes” are independent, so their complements are independent as well. Therefore the probability that neither student passes is $0.2 \cdot 0.3 = 0.06$.
