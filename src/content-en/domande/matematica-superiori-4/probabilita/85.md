Urn $A$ contains $2$ red balls and $3$ blue balls; urn $B$ contains $1$ red ball and $1$ blue balls. A ball selected at random is transferred from $A$ to $B$, and then a ball is drawn from $B$. What is the probability that it is red?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $\dfrac{7}{15}$

## Explanation

The events “the transferred ball is red” and “the transferred ball is blue” form a partition, with probabilities $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\dfrac{3}{5}$. In the first case urn $B$ contains $2$ red balls out of $3$ balls, and in the second $1$ red ball out of $3$. By the law of total probability,
$$
P(R) = \frac{2}{5} \cdot \frac{2}{3} + \frac{3}{5} \cdot \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{15} + \frac{3}{15} = \frac{7}{15} .
$$
