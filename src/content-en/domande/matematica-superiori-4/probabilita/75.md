Five people, including Anna and Bruno, sit at random on five chairs in a row. What is the probability that Anna and Bruno sit next to each other?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{4}{25}$

## Explanation

The $5$ people can be arranged in a row in $5! = 120$ equally likely ways. Treating Anna and Bruno as a single block leaves $4$ objects to arrange, namely the block and the other three people, and they can be arranged in $4! = 24$ ways. The two people within the block can switch places, so there are $2 \cdot 24 = 48$ favorable outcomes. Therefore $P = \dfrac{48}{120} = \dfrac{2}{5}$.
