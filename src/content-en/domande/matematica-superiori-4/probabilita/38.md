A card is drawn uniformly at random from a $40$-card deck with four suits of ten cards each. Which piece of information leaves all four suits equally likely, given that each suit has exactly one ace?

- The card belongs to the first suit.
- The card does not belong to the second suit.
- The card belongs to either of the first two suits.
- The card is an ace.

## Explanation

The four aces contain exactly one representative of each suit. Conditioning on the ace rank therefore preserves the original suit proportions.
