Two dice are rolled. What is the probability that the two numbers are different?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{25}{36}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$

## Explanation

The complement is “the two numbers are equal,” which contains the $6$ pairs $(1, 1), (2, 2), \ldots, (6, 6)$ out of $36$. By the complement rule,
$$
P = 1 - \frac{6}{36} = \frac{30}{36} = \frac{5}{6} .
$$
There are $30$ pairs with distinct numbers, since each unordered pair, such as $\{ 2, 5 \}$, corresponds to two ordered outcomes, $(2, 5)$ and $(5, 2)$.
