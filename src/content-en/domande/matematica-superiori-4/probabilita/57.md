An urn contains $5$ red balls and $3$ blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. Given that the first is blue, what is the probability that the second is red?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{15}{56}$

## Explanation

If the first ball drawn is blue, $5$ red balls and $2$ blue balls remain in the urn, for a total of $7$ balls. The second draw is made from this urn, so the probability that the second ball is red is $\dfrac{5}{7}$. The value $\dfrac{15}{56} = \dfrac{3}{8} \cdot \dfrac{5}{7}$ is the probability of the intersection “first blue and second red,” which differs from the conditional probability.
