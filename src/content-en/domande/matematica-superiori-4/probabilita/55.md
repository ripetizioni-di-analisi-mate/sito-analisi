Out of $100$ tickets, $20$ are red and $5$ are both red and winners. What are the respective probabilities of red and winning, and of winning given red?

- $\dfrac{5}{20}$ and $\dfrac{5}{100}$
- $\dfrac{5}{100}$ and $\dfrac{5}{20}$
- $\dfrac{20}{100}$ and $\dfrac{5}{100}$
- $\dfrac{5}{20}$ in both cases

## Explanation

The favorable outcomes are identical, but the denominator changes. The intersection uses all one hundred tickets, whereas conditioning leaves only the twenty red ones.
