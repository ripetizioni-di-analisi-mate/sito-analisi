At a café, $30\%$ of customers buy coffee and, among them, $40\%$ also buy a croissant. What is the probability that a randomly selected customer buys coffee and a croissant?

- $0.7$
- $0.12$
- $0.75$
- $0.28$

## Explanation

Let $C$ be “the customer buys coffee” and $K$ be “the customer buys a croissant.” The data give $P(C) = 0.3$ and $P(K \mid C) = 0.4$, since $40\%$ refers only to customers who buy coffee. By the multiplication rule, $P(C \cap K) = 0.3 \cdot 0.4 = 0.12$.
