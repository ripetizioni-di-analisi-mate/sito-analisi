A coin is tossed and a die is rolled. What is the probability of getting heads and a number greater than $4$?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

The coin and the die do not affect one another, so “getting heads” and “rolling a number greater than $4$” are independent events. Their probabilities are $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, so $P = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{6}$. The same value follows by counting the favorable pairs $(T, 5)$ and $(T, 6)$ among the $12$ equally likely outcomes.
