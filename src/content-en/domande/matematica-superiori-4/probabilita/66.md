Four equally likely tickets show $(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)$. What is the probability that the product of the two numbers equals their sum?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$
- $1$

## Explanation

Product and sum are both zero only at $(0,0)$. Mixed pairs give zero and one, while the last pair gives one and two. One of the four outcomes qualifies.
