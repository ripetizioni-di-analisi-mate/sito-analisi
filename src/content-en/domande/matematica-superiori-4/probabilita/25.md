Two dice are rolled. What is the probability that their product is even?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The product is odd only when both numbers are odd, which occurs for $3 \cdot 3 = 9$ pairs out of $36$. By the complement rule,
$$
P(\text{even product}) = 1 - \frac{9}{36} = \frac{27}{36} = \frac{3}{4} .
$$
The value exceeds $\dfrac{1}{2}$ because a single even number is sufficient to make the product even.
