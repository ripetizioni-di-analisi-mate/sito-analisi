In the word MATEMATICA one may choose uniformly among the positions or uniformly among the **distinct** letters. Which comparison holds for the letter A?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$ in both cases.
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$ in both cases.
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$ when choosing a position, $\dfrac{1}{3}$ when choosing a type.
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$ when choosing a position, $\dfrac{1}{6}$ when choosing a distinct letter.

## Explanation

The ten positions include three As. The distinct letters are M, A, T, E, I and C, so in the second experiment A is one of six equally likely outcomes.
