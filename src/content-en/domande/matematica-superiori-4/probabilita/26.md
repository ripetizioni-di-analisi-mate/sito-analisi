A letter of the word MATEMATICA is chosen at random. What is the probability that it is an A?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

The word contains $10$ letters, each equally likely to be selected. The letter A appears $3$ times, in the second, sixth, and tenth positions. There are therefore $3$ favorable outcomes, and the probability is $\dfrac{3}{10}$. The $6$ distinct letters M, A, T, E, I, C are not equally likely outcomes, since they occur different numbers of times.
