An urn contains $5$ red balls and $3$ blue balls. Draw $2$ balls at the same time. What is the probability that both are red?

- $\dfrac{25}{64}$
- $\dfrac{5}{28}$
- $\dfrac{15}{28}$
- $\dfrac{5}{14}$

## Explanation

Drawing two balls together amounts to choosing a group of $2$ balls from $8$, so there are $\dbinom{8}{2} = 28$ possible outcomes. The favorable outcomes are groups of $2$ red balls, numbering $\dbinom{5}{2} = 10$. Therefore $P = \dfrac{10}{28} = \dfrac{5}{14}$.
