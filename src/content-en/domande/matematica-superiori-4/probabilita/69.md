Two fair dice are rolled once and their sum is reported to be $7$. What is the probability that one die shows $1$?

- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac1{36}$
- $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

Six equally likely ordered pairs have sum $7$. The pairs $(1,6)$ and $(6,1)$ satisfy the requirement, with probability $\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
