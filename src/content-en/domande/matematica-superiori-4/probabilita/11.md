Roll a die. If it shows $1$ or $2$, draw a ball from urn $A$, which contains $2$ red balls and $3$ blue balls. Otherwise draw from urn $B$, which contains $4$ red balls and $1$ blue balls. Given that the ball drawn is red, what is the probability that it came from urn $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The prior probabilities of the urns are $P(A) = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $P(B) = \dfrac{2}{3}$. By the law of total probability,
$$
P(R) = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{2}{5} + \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{2}{15} + \frac{8}{15} = \frac{2}{3} .
$$
By Bayes’ theorem,
$$
P(A \mid R) = \frac{\frac{2}{15}}{\frac{2}{3}} = \frac{1}{5} .
$$
