Paolo assigns probability $0.25$ to rain tomorrow. He pays $5$ euros in a coherent bet. How much must he receive if it rains tomorrow?

- $1.25$ euros
- $20$ euros
- $6.25$ euros
- $15$ euros

## Explanation

In the subjective definition, $P = \dfrac{S}{V}$, where $S$ is the amount paid and $V$ the amount received if the event occurs. Solving for $V$ gives
$$
V = \frac{S}{P} = \frac{5}{0.25} = 20 \text{ euros} .
$$
The value $1.25$ euros is the product $5 \cdot 0.25$, obtained by incorrectly solving the formula for $V$.
