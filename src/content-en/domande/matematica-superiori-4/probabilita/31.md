Two complementary outcomes for one match, “win” and “no win”, are assigned probabilities $0.55$ and $0.60$. Which check exposes the inconsistency?

- Both probabilities are positive.
- Their sum exceeds $1$.
- The probabilities differ.
- The spectator count is missing.

## Explanation

The events are disjoint and exhaust all possibilities. Their probabilities must sum to one, but $0.55+0.60=1.15$.
