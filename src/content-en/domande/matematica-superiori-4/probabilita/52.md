A calculation proposes $P(A\mid B)=0.6$ and $P(A^c\mid B)=0.5$, with $P(B)>0$. Can these numbers describe the same experiment?

- Yes, because both are below one.
- Yes, if $A$ and $B$ are independent.
- Only if $P(B)=0.1$.
- No, because within $B$ the two probabilities must sum to one.

## Explanation

Conditioning on $B$ leaves $A$ and $A^c$ complementary. The second probability should equal $1-0.6=0.4$, not $0.5$.
