For two events $A$ and $B$, we know that $P(A \cap B) = 0.12$ and $P(B) = 0.4$. What is the value of $P(A \mid B)$?

- $0.048$
- $0.28$
- $0.52$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

The conditional probability is defined since $P(B) = 0.4$ is positive. By definition,
$$
P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} = \frac{0.12}{0.4} = 0.3 .
$$
In other words, the intersection accounts for $30\%$ of the probability of $B$.
