A group of $10$ people consists of $6$ girls and $4$ boys. Choose $4$ people at random. What is the probability of choosing exactly $2$ girls?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{216}{625}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}$

## Explanation

There are $4$ people per group and $\dbinom{10}{4} = 210$ groups. Groups with $2$ girls and $2$ boys are formed by choosing $2$ girls from $6$ and $2$ boys from $4$, giving $\dbinom{6}{2} \dbinom{4}{2} = 15 \cdot 6 = 90$ groups. Therefore $P = \dfrac{90}{210} = \dfrac{3}{7}$.
