An urn contains $n$ white balls and $2$ black balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. For what value of $n$ is the probability of drawing one white ball and one black ball, in either order, equal to $\dfrac{8}{15}$?

- $n = 2$
- $n = 3$
- $n = 6$
- $n = 4$

## Explanation

By the multiplication rule, the sequences white–black and black–white both have probability $\dfrac{n}{n + 2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{n + 1}$, so the required probability is $\dfrac{4n}{(n + 2)(n + 1)}$. Setting this equal to $\dfrac{8}{15}$ gives $60 n = 8 (n^2 + 3n + 2)$, or $2n^2 - 9n + 4 = 0$. The equation has solutions $n = 4$ and $n = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The number of balls is an integer, so $n = 4$.
