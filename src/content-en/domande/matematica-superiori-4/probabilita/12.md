## Explanation

By the law of total probability, the probability that a part is defective is
$$
P(D) = 0.25 \cdot 0.04 + 0.35 \cdot 0.02 + 0.40 \cdot 0.01 = 0.010 + 0.007 + 0.004 = 0.021 .
$$
By Bayes’ theorem,
$$
P(L_1 \mid D) = \frac{0.010}{0.021} = \frac{10}{21} .
$$
This value exceeds the prior probability $0.25$, since line $L_1$ has the highest percentage of defective parts.
