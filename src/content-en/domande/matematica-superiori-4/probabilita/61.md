Three independent switches each work with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. A lamp turns on if an odd number of switches work. What is the probability that it turns on?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac18$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac78$

## Explanation

Of the $2^3=8$ equally likely outcomes, three have exactly one working switch and one has all three working. Four outcomes turn on the lamp.
