Two independent events $A$ and $B$ satisfy $P(A) = 0.4$ and $P(A \cup B) = 0.7$. What is the value of $P(B)$?

- $0.5$
- $0.3$
- $0.75$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

Set $P(B) = p$. Since the events are independent, $P(A \cap B) = 0.4 p$, and by the addition rule we have $0.4 + p - 0.4 p = 0.7$. Therefore $0.6 p = 0.3$, or $p = 0.5$.
