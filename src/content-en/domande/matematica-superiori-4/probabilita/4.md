A fair coin is tossed three times. What is the probability of obtaining at least two tails?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The outcomes are the $2^3 = 8$ sequences of three tosses, all equally likely. The sequences with exactly two tails are $CCT$, $CTC$, and $TCC$, and the sequence with three tails is $CCC$. Therefore there are $4$ favorable outcomes, and the probability is $\dfrac{4}{8} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
