An urn contains $2$ red balls and $1$ blue. Draw a ball, return it and add another ball of that color. What is the probability that the first two draws have different colors?

- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

The two paths are red-blue, of probability $(\dfrac{2}{3})(\dfrac{1}{4})$, and blue-red, of probability $(\dfrac{1}{3})(\dfrac{2}{4})$. They are disjoint, so their probabilities add to $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
