Toss a coin. On heads, roll a die and win if it shows $6$. On tails, roll a die and win if it shows an even number. Given a win, what is the probability that the coin showed heads?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

By the law of total probability, the probability of winning is
$$
P(V) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
By Bayes’ theorem,
$$
P(T \mid V) = \frac{\frac{1}{12}}{\frac{1}{3}} = \frac{1}{4} .
$$
The prior probability of heads is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and its posterior probability is lower because a win is less likely on heads than on tails.
