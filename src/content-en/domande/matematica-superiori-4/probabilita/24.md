An integer is chosen at random from $1$ to $50$. What is the probability that it is a perfect square?

- $\dfrac{3}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{50}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

The $50$ numbers are equally likely, so the classical definition applies. The perfect squares between $1$ and $50$ are $1$, $4$, $9$, $16$, $25$, $36$, and $49$, giving $7$ numbers. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{7}{50}$.
