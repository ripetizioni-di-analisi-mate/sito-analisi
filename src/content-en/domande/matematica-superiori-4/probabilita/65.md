Anna and Bruno try to solve a problem independently. Anna solves it with probability $0.7$ and Bruno with probability $0.4$. What is the probability that exactly one of them solves the problem?

- $0.28$
- $0.82$
- $0.18$
- $0.54$

## Explanation

Exactly one person solves the problem when only Anna solves it or only Bruno solves it. These cases are mutually exclusive. By independence of the complements, the first case has probability $0.7 \cdot 0.6 = 0.42$ and the second $0.3 \cdot 0.4 = 0.12$. The required probability is therefore $0.42 + 0.12 = 0.54$.
