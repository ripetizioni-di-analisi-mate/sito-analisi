Anna and Bruno solve a problem with probabilities $0.7$ and $0.6$. Their dependence is unknown. What is the smallest possible probability that both solve it?

- $0.42$
- $0$
- $0.6$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

The addition rule gives $P(A\cap B)=1.3-P(A\cup B)$. The union can cover the whole sample space, leaving a minimum intersection probability of $0.3$.
