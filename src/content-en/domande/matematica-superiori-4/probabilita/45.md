An integer is chosen at random from $1$ to $20$. What is the probability that it is divisible by $2$, $3$, or $5$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Use the addition rule for three events. The numbers of multiples of $2$, $3$, and $5$ from $1$ to $20$ are $10$, $6$, and $4$, respectively. The numbers of multiples of $6$, $10$, and $15$ are $3$, $2$, and $1$. No number from $1$ to $20$ is a multiple of $30$. Therefore
$$
P = \frac{10 + 6 + 4 - 3 - 2 - 1 + 0}{20} = \frac{14}{20} = \frac{7}{10} .
$$
