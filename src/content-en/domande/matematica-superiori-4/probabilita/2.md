- $\dfrac{4}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{11}{36}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

There are $36$ equally likely ordered pairs. The sum is a multiple of $3$ when it equals $3$, $6$, $9$, or $12$. These cases occur for $2$, $5$, $4$, and $1$ pairs, respectively. The four cases are mutually exclusive, so there are $2 + 5 + 4 + 1 = 12$ favorable outcomes. The probability is therefore $\dfrac{12}{36} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
