Marta considers $12$ euros a **fair price** for a ticket that pays $20$ euros on a win and zero otherwise. In the model where the fair price equals the expected gross payout, which probability does she assign to a win?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

If her subjective probability is $p$, the expected payout is $20p$. Equating it to the fair price gives $20p=12$, hence $p=\dfrac{3}{5}$.
