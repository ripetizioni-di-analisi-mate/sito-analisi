Marta is willing to pay $12$ euros in exchange for $20$ euros if her team wins its next game. What probability does Marta assign to a win?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

In the subjective definition, probability is the ratio of the amount $S$ that a person is willing to pay to the amount $V$ received if the event occurs. Here $S = 12$ and $V = 20$, so $P = \dfrac{12}{20} = \dfrac{3}{5}$. The reciprocal $\dfrac{20}{12}$ is greater than $1$ and therefore cannot be a probability.
