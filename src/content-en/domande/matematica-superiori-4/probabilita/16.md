A die is rolled and a coin is tossed. How many elements does the sample space contain?

- $8$
- $12$
- $36$
- $64$

## Explanation

Each outcome is a pair consisting of the number on the die and the result of the coin toss, for example $(3, T)$. There are $6$ choices for the number and $2$ for the coin, so by the fundamental counting principle the number of outcomes is $6 \cdot 2 = 12$. The sum $6 + 2 = 8$ would count the outcomes of an experiment in which only the die is rolled or only the coin is tossed.
