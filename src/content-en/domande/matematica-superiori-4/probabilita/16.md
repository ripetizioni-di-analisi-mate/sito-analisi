A fair coin is tossed until the first heads, with at most two tosses. Which list describes its terminal outcomes, using $H$ for heads and $T$ for tails?

- $HH,HT,TH,TT$
- $H,TH,TT$
- $H,T$
- $HH,TT$

## Explanation

A head on the first toss stops the experiment immediately. A first tail leads to one more toss and the other two outcomes. The three terminal probabilities are $\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{4},\dfrac{1}{4}$.
