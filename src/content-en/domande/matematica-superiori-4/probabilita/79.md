A survey finds success in $9$ cases out of $10$ in a small group and $48$ out of $60$ in a large group. What is the overall success frequency?

- $\dfrac{57}{70}$
- $\dfrac{17}{20}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$

## Explanation

Add the successes and totals separately. This gives $9+48=57$ successes out of $10+60=70$ observations. A simple average of the percentages would assign equal weight to groups of different sizes.
