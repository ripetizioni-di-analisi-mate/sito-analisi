Toss a coin. On heads, draw a ball from urn $U_1$, which contains $3$ red balls and $2$ blue balls. On tails, draw from urn $U_2$, which contains $1$ red ball and $4$ blue balls. What is the probability of drawing a red ball?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{25}$

## Explanation

The events “getting heads” and “getting tails” form a partition, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. By the law of total probability,
$$
P(R) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{3}{5} + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{5} = \frac{3}{10} + \frac{1}{10} = \frac{2}{5} .
$$
In a tree diagram the result is the sum of the probabilities of the two paths ending in a red ball.
