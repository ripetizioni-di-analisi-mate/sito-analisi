An urn contains $3$ red balls and $7$ blue balls. Make $4$ draws with replacement. What is the probability that all four balls drawn are blue?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{49}{100}$
- $\dfrac{2401}{10000}$

## Explanation

With replacement the composition of the urn stays the same, so the four draws are independent. On each draw the probability of a blue ball is $\dfrac{7}{10}$. Therefore
$$
P = \left( \frac{7}{10} \right)^4 = \frac{2401}{10000} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{1}{6}$ is the probability without replacement, namely $\dfrac{\binom{7}{4}}{\binom{10}{4}} = \dfrac{35}{210}$.
