A bag contains a fair coin and a double-headed coin. A coin is chosen uniformly and tossed twice. What is the probability that the two results differ?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac38$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Different results are possible only if the fair coin is chosen, with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. For that coin the results differ with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, giving $\dfrac{1}{4}$ overall.
