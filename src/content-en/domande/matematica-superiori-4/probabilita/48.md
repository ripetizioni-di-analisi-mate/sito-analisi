For two events $A$ and $B$, we know that $P(A) = 0.6$ and $P(B) = 0.7$. Which value is possible for $P(A \cap B)$?

- $0.4$
- $0$
- $0.2$
- $0.7$

## Explanation

By the addition rule, $P(A \cap B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cup B) = 1.3 - P(A \cup B)$. Since $P(A \cup B) \leq 1$, we have $P(A \cap B) \geq 0.3$. Also $A \cap B \subseteq A$, so $P(A \cap B) \leq P(A) = 0.6$. Of the proposed values, only $0.4$ lies between $0.3$ and $0.6$.
