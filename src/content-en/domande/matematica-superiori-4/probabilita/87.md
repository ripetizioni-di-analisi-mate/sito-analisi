Four independent tosses of a coin with head probability $p$ can produce two heads in six different orders. What is the probability of the one sequence heads, tails, heads, tails?

- $p^2(1-p)^2$
- $6p^2(1-p)^2$
- $p^4$
- $2p(1-p)$

## Explanation

The order is specified. Multiply the four factors $p,1-p,p,1-p$ without including the coefficient that would count all possible placements of the heads.
