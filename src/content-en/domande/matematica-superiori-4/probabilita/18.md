When two dice are rolled, how many outcomes belong to the event “the sum is $5$”?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The outcomes are the ordered pairs $(a, b)$, where $a$ is the number on the first die and $b$ the number on the second. The sum is $5$ for the pairs $(1, 4)$, $(2, 3)$, $(3, 2)$, and $(4, 1)$. The pairs $(1, 4)$ and $(4, 1)$ are distinct outcomes, since the $4$ appears on different dice, so the event contains $4$ outcomes.
