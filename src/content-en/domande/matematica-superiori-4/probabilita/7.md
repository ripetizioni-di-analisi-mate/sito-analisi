- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

The number of groups of $3$ people is $\dbinom{10}{3} = 120$, and all the groups are equally likely. A group containing Anna and Bruno is determined by the third member, chosen from the other $8$ people. There are therefore $8$ favorable outcomes, and $P = \dfrac{8}{120} = \dfrac{1}{15}$.
