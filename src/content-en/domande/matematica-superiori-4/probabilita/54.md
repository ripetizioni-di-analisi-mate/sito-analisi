A bus runs exactly every ten minutes. A passenger arrives at a time uniformly distributed between two buses. The total wait is known to exceed three minutes. What is the probability that it also exceeds five?

- $\dfrac57$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac25$

## Explanation

Conditioning on a wait longer than three leaves the uniform interval from three to ten, of length seven minutes. Waits longer than five occupy five of those minutes, so the ratio is $\dfrac{5}{7}$.
