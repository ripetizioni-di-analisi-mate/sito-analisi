An urn contains $4$ red balls and $6$ blue balls. Three balls are drawn one after the other without replacement. What is the probability that all three are red?

- $\dfrac{1}{30}$
- $\dfrac{8}{125}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{120}$

## Explanation

In the multiplication rule for three events, each factor is the probability of drawing a red ball given that all preceding draws produced red balls. After each draw both the number of red balls and the total decrease by $1$, so
$$
P = \frac{4}{10} \cdot \frac{3}{9} \cdot \frac{2}{8} = \frac{24}{720} = \frac{1}{30} .
$$
Combinations give the same value, since $\dfrac{\binom{4}{3}}{\binom{10}{3}} = \dfrac{4}{120}$.
