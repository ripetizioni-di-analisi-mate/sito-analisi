On a roll of a die, let $A = \{ 1, 2, 3, 4 \}$ and $B = \{ 3, 4, 5 \}$. Which set is the complement of $A \cup B$?

- $\{ 1, 2, 5, 6 \}$
- $\{ 6 \}$
- $\{ 5, 6 \}$
- $\{ 1, 2 \}$

## Explanation

The union contains the outcomes belonging to at least one of the two events, so $A \cup B = \{ 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 \}$. Its complement contains the outcomes in $\Omega = \{ 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 \}$ that do not belong to the union, so $\overline{A \cup B} = \{ 6 \}$. The set $\{ 1, 2, 5, 6 \}$ is the complement of the intersection $A \cap B = \{ 3, 4 \}$.
