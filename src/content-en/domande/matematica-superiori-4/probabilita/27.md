Two dice are rolled. What is the probability that the larger number minus the smaller one equals $2$?

- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{18}$

## Explanation

There are $36$ equally likely ordered pairs. The difference equals $2$ for the pairs $(1, 3)$, $(2, 4)$, $(3, 5)$, $(4, 6)$ and for the four pairs with the same numbers in reverse order. There are therefore $8$ favorable outcomes, and the probability is $\dfrac{8}{36} = \dfrac{2}{9}$.
