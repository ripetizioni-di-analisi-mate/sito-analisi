Two fair dice are rolled. A player treats the sums $2,3,\ldots,12$ as eleven equally likely results. Which comparison disproves this model?

- A sum of $7$ has six favorable ordered pairs, whereas a sum of $2$ has one.
- All sums lie between $2$ and $12$.
- The two dice have the same number of faces.
- There are six even sums.

## Explanation

The equally likely elementary outcomes are the $36$ ordered pairs. Grouping them by sum gives groups of different sizes, so the sums have different probabilities.
