A die is loaded so that the probability of each face is proportional to the number on that face. What is the probability of rolling an even number?

- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$

## Explanation

Set $P(k) = c \, k$ for $k = 1, \ldots, 6$. By the second axiom, $c \, (1 + 2 + \ldots + 6) = 21 c = 1$, so $c = \dfrac{1}{21}$. The probability of an event is the sum of the probabilities of its outcomes, so
$$
P(\text{even}) = \frac{2 + 4 + 6}{21} = \frac{12}{21} = \frac{4}{7} .
$$
