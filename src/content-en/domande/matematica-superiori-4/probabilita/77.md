Two bags contain respectively $1$ red marble out of $2$ and $1$ red out of $8$. What are the red probabilities when choosing a bag uniformly and then a marble, and when pooling all marbles?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\dfrac{5}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$ in both cases
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$ in both cases
- $\dfrac{5}{16}$ and $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Choosing a bag gives the average of the two fractions, $(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{8})/2=\dfrac{5}{16}$. When pooled, the two red marbles are part of a total of ten, giving probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
