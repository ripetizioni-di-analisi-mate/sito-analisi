An urn contains $6$ red balls and $4$ blue balls. Draw two balls with replacement. What is the probability of drawing one red ball and one blue ball in either order?

- $\dfrac{6}{25}$
- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $\dfrac{13}{25}$
- $\dfrac{12}{25}$

## Explanation

With replacement the two draws are independent, and on each draw the probability of red is $\dfrac{6}{10}$ and the probability of blue is $\dfrac{4}{10}$. The sequences $RB$ and $BR$ both have probability $\dfrac{6}{10} \cdot \dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{6}{25}$ and are mutually exclusive. Therefore $P = 2 \cdot \dfrac{6}{25} = \dfrac{12}{25}$.
