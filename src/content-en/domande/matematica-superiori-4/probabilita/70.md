Three people have independent birthdays, each uniform on $365$ days. Which expression gives the probability that **at least two** share a birthday?

- $\dfrac{1}{365^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{365}$
- $1-\dfrac{364^2}{365^2}$
- $1-\dfrac{364\cdot 363}{365^2}$

## Explanation

Count the complementary event. The first birthday is unrestricted, the second must avoid the first date, and the third must avoid both dates already taken. The probability of three distinct days is $(364/365)(363/365)$.
