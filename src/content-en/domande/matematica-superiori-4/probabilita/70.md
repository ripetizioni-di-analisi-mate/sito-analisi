An urn contains $5$ red balls and $5$ blue balls. Draw $3$ balls at the same time. What is the probability that exactly one is red?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{36}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$

## Explanation

The possible outcomes are groups of $3$ balls chosen from $10$, numbering $\dbinom{10}{3} = 120$. To obtain a favorable outcome, choose $1$ red ball from $5$ and $2$ blue balls from $5$, giving $\dbinom{5}{1} \dbinom{5}{2} = 5 \cdot 10 = 50$ outcomes. By the formula for draws without replacement, $P = \dfrac{50}{120} = \dfrac{5}{12}$.
