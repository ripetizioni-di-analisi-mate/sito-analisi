A company tests $800$ light bulbs and finds $28$ defective bulbs. What is the estimated probability that a bulb produced by the company is defective?

- $0.035$
- $0.028$
- $0.35$
- $0.965$

## Explanation

The probability is estimated from the relative frequency of the event over many trials. There are $28$ defective bulbs out of $800$, so
$$
P(\text{defective}) \approx \frac{28}{800} = 0.035 .
$$
By the empirical law of large numbers, the estimate is usually more reliable when more bulbs are tested.
