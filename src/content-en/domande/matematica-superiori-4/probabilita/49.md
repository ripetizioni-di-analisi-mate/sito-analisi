A fair die is rolled and the result is known to be greater than $3$. What is the probability that the result is $6$?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{18}$

## Explanation

Given that the result is greater than $3$, the possible outcomes are reduced to $4$, $5$, and $6$, all equally likely. The only favorable outcome is $6$, so the probability is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The definition of conditional probability gives the same value,
$$
P(6 \mid \text{greater than } 3) = \frac{\frac{1}{6}}{\frac{3}{6}} = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
