An integer is chosen at random from $1$ to $100$. What is the probability that it is not a multiple of $7$?

- $\dfrac{6}{7}$
- $\dfrac{43}{50}$
- $\dfrac{7}{50}$
- $\dfrac{93}{100}$

## Explanation

The multiples of $7$ from $1$ to $100$ are $7, 14, \ldots, 98$, giving $14$ numbers, since $98 = 7 \cdot 14$. The probability of choosing a multiple of $7$ is therefore $\dfrac{14}{100}$. By the complement rule,
$$
P = 1 - \frac{14}{100} = \frac{86}{100} = \frac{43}{50} .
$$
