A point is selected uniformly on a segment $10$ cm long. What is the probability that it is within $2$ cm of at least one endpoint?

- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

The two ends contribute disjoint intervals of length $2$ cm each. The favorable length is $4$ cm out of $10$. Including the endpoints does not affect the probability.
