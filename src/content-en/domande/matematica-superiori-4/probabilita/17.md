Two dice are rolled. Which of the following is the impossible event?

- The sum of the two numbers is $1$
- The sum of the two numbers is $12$
- The two numbers are equal
- The product of the two numbers is $36$

## Explanation

The impossible event contains no outcomes. Each die shows at least $1$, so the sum is at least $2$ and the event “the sum is $1$” is the empty set. The sum $12$ and the product $36$ both occur for the pair $(6, 6)$, and the two numbers are equal in the $6$ pairs $(1, 1), \ldots, (6, 6)$, so each of the other events contains at least one outcome.
