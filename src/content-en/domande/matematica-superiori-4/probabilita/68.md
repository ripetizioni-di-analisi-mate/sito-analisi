For two events $A$ and $B$, we know that $P(A) = 0.5$, $P(B) = 0.4$, and $P(A \cup B) = 0.8$. Which statement is true?

- $P(A \mid B) = 0.25$
- $P(A \mid B) = 0.5$
- $A$ and $B$ are independent
- $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive

## Explanation

By the addition rule, $P(A \cap B) = 0.5 + 0.4 - 0.8 = 0.1$. Hence $P(A \mid B) = \dfrac{0.1}{0.4} = 0.25$. The events are not mutually exclusive, since $P(A \cap B) > 0$, and they are dependent, since $P(A) \cdot P(B) = 0.2$ differs from $0.1$.
