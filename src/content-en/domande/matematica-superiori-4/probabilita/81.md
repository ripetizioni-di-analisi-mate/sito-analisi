In a store, $40\%$ of customers are women. An item on sale is bought by $30\%$ of the women and $20\%$ of the men. What is the probability that a randomly selected customer buys the item on sale?

- $0.5$
- $0.25$
- $0.24$
- $0.12$

## Explanation

The events “the customer is a woman” and “the customer is a man” form a partition, with probabilities $0.4$ and $0.6$. By the law of total probability,
$$
P = 0.4 \cdot 0.3 + 0.6 \cdot 0.2 = 0.12 + 0.12 = 0.24 .
$$
The simple average $0.25$ of the two percentages would be correct only if there were equal numbers of women and men.
