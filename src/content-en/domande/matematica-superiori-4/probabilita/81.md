In a survey, $70\%$ of respondents choose A, but only half the invited people respond. With no assumptions about nonrespondents, what are the possible bounds on A's share among all invitees?

- From $50\%$ to $70\%$.
- Exactly $70\%$.
- From $35\%$ to $85\%$.
- From $20\%$ to $50\%$.

## Explanation

The known votes for A account for $0.5\cdot0.7=0.35$ of all invitees. Among nonrespondents, anyone from none to all could favor A, adding between zero and $0.5$ of the total.
