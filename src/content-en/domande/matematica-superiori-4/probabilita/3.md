- $\dfrac{19}{40}$
- $\dfrac{21}{40}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$

## Explanation

There are $12$ face cards, $10$ cards in the suit of swords, and $3$ face cards in that suit. By the addition rule, the probability of drawing a face card or a sword is
$$
\frac{12 + 10 - 3}{40} = \frac{19}{40} .
$$
The required event is the complement of this union, so by the complement rule $P = 1 - \dfrac{19}{40} = \dfrac{21}{40}$.
