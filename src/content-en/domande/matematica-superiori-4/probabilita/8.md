For two events $A$ and $B$, we know that $P(A) = \dfrac{1}{3}$, $P(B) = \dfrac{1}{4}$, and $P(A \cup B) = \dfrac{1}{2}$. What is the value of $P(A \mid B)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{48}$

## Explanation

The addition rule gives
$$
P(A \cap B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cup B) = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{12} .
$$
By the definition of conditional probability,
$$
P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} = \frac{\frac{1}{12}}{\frac{1}{4}} = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
The value equals $P(A)$, so $A$ and $B$ are independent.
