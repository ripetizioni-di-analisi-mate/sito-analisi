For two events $A$ and $B$ with $A \subseteq B$, we know that $P(B) = 0.4$. Which of these could be the value of $P(A)$?

- $0.45$
- $0.5$
- $0.3$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

One consequence of the axioms is that if $A \subseteq B$, then $P(A) \leq P(B)$. Thus $P(A)$ is at most $0.4$. Of the proposed values, only $0.3$ satisfies this condition; the others exceed $0.4$.
