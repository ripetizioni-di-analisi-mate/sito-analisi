A fair coin is tossed $10$ times and shows heads $7$ times. Which statement is true?

- The probability of heads is $0.7$
- The relative frequency of heads is $0.7$ and the probability of heads is $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- Getting tails is more likely than getting heads on the next toss
- The relative frequency of heads is $7$

## Explanation

The relative frequency is the ratio of the number of heads to the number of tosses, so it equals $\dfrac{7}{10} = 0.7$. The coin is fair, so the probability of heads is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ on each toss, regardless of the preceding results. The empirical law of large numbers states that the relative frequency is close to the probability when there are many trials, and $10$ tosses is a small number.
