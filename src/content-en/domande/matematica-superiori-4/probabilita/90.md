A fair coin is tossed $n$ times. What is the smallest value of $n$ for which the probability of obtaining at least one head exceeds $0.95$?

- $n = 4$
- $n = 10$
- $n = 5$
- $n = 20$

## Explanation

The probability of no heads in $n$ tosses is $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^n$, so the probability of at least one head is $1 - \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^n$. The condition $1 - \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^n > 0.95$ is equivalent to $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^n < 0.05$, or $2^n > 20$. Since $2^4 = 16$ and $2^5 = 32$, the smallest value is $n = 5$.
