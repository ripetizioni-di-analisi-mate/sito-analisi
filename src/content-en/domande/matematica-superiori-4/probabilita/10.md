Three shooters hit a target independently of one another with probabilities $0.6$, $0.5$, and $0.4$. Each takes one shot. What is the probability that the target is hit exactly once?

- $0.88$
- $0.38$
- $0.36$
- $0.12$

## Explanation

The target is hit exactly once if exactly one of the three shooters hits it. The three cases are mutually exclusive. The complements of independent events are independent, so the probability of each case is the product
$$
0.6 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.6 = 0.18 ,
\qquad
0.4 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.6 = 0.12 ,
\qquad
0.4 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.4 = 0.08 .
$$
By the addition rule for mutually exclusive events the probability is $0.18 + 0.12 + 0.08 = 0.38$.
