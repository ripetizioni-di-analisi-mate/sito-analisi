An integer is chosen at random from $1$ to $60$. What is the probability that it is divisible by $4$ or $6$?

- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

The number of multiples of $4$ from $1$ to $60$ is $15$, and the number of multiples of $6$ is $10$. A number is divisible by both $4$ and $6$ if and only if it is a multiple of their least common multiple $12$, and the number of multiples of $12$ is $5$. By the addition rule,
$$
P = \frac{15 + 10 - 5}{60} = \frac{20}{60} = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
