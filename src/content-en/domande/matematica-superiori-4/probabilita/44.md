A dataset gives only $P(A)=0.8$ and $P(B)=0.7$. What is the smallest possible value of $P(A\cap B)$?

- $0$
- $0.5$
- $0.56$
- $0.7$

## Explanation

Since $P(A\cup B)\leq1$, the addition rule gives $P(A\cap B)\geq0.8+0.7-1=0.5$. This bound can be reached when the union covers the whole space.
