## Explanation

The number of pairs in which at least one die shows $6$ is $6 + 6 - 1 = 11$, since the pair $(6, 6)$ belongs to both groups of six. Of these pairs, the sum is at least $10$ for $(6, 4)$, $(4, 6)$, $(6, 5)$, $(5, 6)$, and $(6, 6)$, giving $5$ pairs. The outcomes are equally likely, so the conditional probability is the ratio of the counts, namely $\dfrac{5}{11}$.
