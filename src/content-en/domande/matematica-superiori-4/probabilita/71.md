From an urn with the numbers $1$ through $90$, draw $5$ numbers without replacement. What is the probability that $27$ is among the numbers drawn?

- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{1}{90}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{45}$

## Explanation

The possible outcomes are groups of $5$ numbers chosen from $90$, giving $\dbinom{90}{5}$ groups. A group containing $27$ is determined by its other $4$ numbers, chosen from the $89$ remaining, so there are $\dbinom{89}{4}$ favorable outcomes. Since $\dbinom{90}{5} = \dfrac{90}{5} \dbinom{89}{4}$, we have
$$
P = \frac{\binom{89}{4}}{\binom{90}{5}} = \frac{5}{90} = \frac{1}{18} .
$$
