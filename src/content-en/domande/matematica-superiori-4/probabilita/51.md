Two independent fair coins are tossed. What is the probability of two heads if the first coin is shown and it landed heads?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The first result has been specified, leaving only the second unknown. The two possible results of the second toss are still equally likely.
