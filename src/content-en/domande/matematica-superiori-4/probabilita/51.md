A red die and a blue die are rolled. Given that the red die shows an even number, what is the probability that their sum is $8$?

- $\dfrac{5}{36}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

There are $3 \cdot 6 = 18$ pairs in which the red die shows an even number. Among these, the sum is $8$ for $(2, 6)$, $(4, 4)$, and $(6, 2)$. The outcomes are equally likely, so the conditional probability is the ratio of the counts, $\dfrac{3}{18} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.
