On a roll of a die, let $A$ be the event “rolling an even number.” Which of the following events is independent of $A$?

- $\{ 1, 2, 3 \}$
- $\{ 4, 5, 6 \}$
- $\{ 1, 3, 5 \}$
- $\{ 1, 2, 3, 4 \}$

## Explanation

With $A = \{ 2, 4, 6 \}$ and $B = \{ 1, 2, 3, 4 \}$, we have $A \cap B = \{ 2, 4 \}$, so $P(A \cap B) = \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The product of the probabilities is $P(A) \cdot P(B) = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, equal to $P(A \cap B)$, so the events are independent. With $\{ 1, 2, 3 \}$ the intersection is $\{ 2 \}$, which has probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$, whereas the product is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. With $\{ 4, 5, 6 \}$ the intersection has probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and the product is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and $\{ 1, 3, 5 \}$ is mutually exclusive with $A$.
