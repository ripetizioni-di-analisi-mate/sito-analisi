For two events $A$ and $B$, we know that $P(A) = 0.5$, $P(B) = 0.2$, and $P(A \mid B) = 0.6$. What is the value of $P(B \mid A)$?

- $0.24$
- $0.6$
- $0.12$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

By the multiplication rule, $P(A \cap B) = P(B) \cdot P(A \mid B) = 0.2 \cdot 0.6 = 0.12$. By the definition of conditional probability,
$$
P(B \mid A) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(A)} = \frac{0.12}{0.5} = 0.24 .
$$
The two conditional probabilities differ, since in one case the divisor is $P(B)$ and in the other it is $P(A)$.
