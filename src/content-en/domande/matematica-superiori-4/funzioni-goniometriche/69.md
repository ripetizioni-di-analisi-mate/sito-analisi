Shift the graph of $\tan x$ to the right by $\pi/4$. Which family of lines now contains its vertical asymptotes?

- $x=\pi/4+k\pi$
- $x=3\pi/4+k\pi$
- $x=k\pi$
- $x=3\pi/4+2k\pi$ only

## Explanation

The original asymptotes are at $\pi/2+k\pi$. The shift adds $\pi/4$ to each horizontal coordinate while preserving the spacing $\pi$ between consecutive asymptotes.
