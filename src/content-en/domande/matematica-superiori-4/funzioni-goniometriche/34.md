Suppose that $\tan\alpha > 0$ and $\sin\alpha < 0$. In which quadrant is the point corresponding to the angle $\alpha$?

- In the first quadrant
- In the second quadrant
- In the fourth quadrant
- In the third quadrant

## Explanation

Tangent is positive when sine and cosine have the same sign. Since $\sin\alpha < 0$, the value of $\cos\alpha$ is also negative. The corresponding point therefore has two negative coordinates and lies in the third quadrant.
