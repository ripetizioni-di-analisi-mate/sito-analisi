An oscillator completes exactly three cycles in two seconds. If the clock is recalibrated to measure time in milliseconds, how is the same frequency expressed?

- $1.5$ cycles per millisecond
- $0.0015$ cycles per millisecond
- $1500$ cycles per millisecond
- $0.015$ cycles per millisecond

## Explanation

The three cycles take $2000$ milliseconds. Dividing cycle count by time in the new unit gives $\dfrac{3}{2000}=0.0015$ cycles per millisecond.
