A point moves according to $x(t) = 5\cos(4\pi t)$, with $x$ in centimeters and $t$ in seconds. What is the frequency of the motion?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\ \text{Hz}$
- $2\ \text{Hz}$
- $4\pi\ \text{Hz}$
- $4\ \text{Hz}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega = 4\pi\ \text{rad/s}$. The frequency is $f = \dfrac{\omega}{2\pi}$, so $f = \dfrac{4\pi}{2\pi} = 2\ \text{Hz}$. Therefore the point completes two full oscillations per second, and its period is $T = \dfrac{1}{f} = \dfrac{1}{2}\ \text{s}$.
