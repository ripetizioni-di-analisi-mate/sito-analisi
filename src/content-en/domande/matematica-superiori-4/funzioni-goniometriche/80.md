A point $Q$ moves around a circle of radius $2\ \text{m}$ at constant angular velocity $3\ \text{rad/s}$. What is the period of the simple harmonic motion of the projection of $Q$ onto the $x$-axis?

- $6\pi\ \text{s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2\pi}\ \text{s}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\ \text{s}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\ \text{s}$

## Explanation

The projection of $Q$ onto the $x$-axis moves according to $x(t) = 2\cos(3t + \varphi)$, where $\varphi$ is the angle of the starting position. Therefore the angular frequency of the simple harmonic motion equals the angular velocity, $\omega = 3\ \text{rad/s}$. The period is $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega} = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}\ \text{s}$, the time $Q$ takes to complete a full turn. The radius of the circle is the amplitude of the motion and does not affect the period.
