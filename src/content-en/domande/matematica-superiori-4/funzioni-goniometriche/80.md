Two motions are $s_1(t)=\cos t$ and $s_2(t)=\cos (2t)$. How many cycles does the second complete in one full cycle of the first?

- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

Over the same interval the second phase advances twice as far. When the first advances $2\pi$, the second advances $4\pi$, which is two cycles.
