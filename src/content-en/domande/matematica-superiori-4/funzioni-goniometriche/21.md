A circular sector has radius $4\ \text{cm}$ and area $24\ \text{cm}^2$. What is the radian measure of its central angle?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The sector area formula $A = \dfrac{1}{2}\,r^2\alpha$ gives $\alpha = \dfrac{2A}{r^2}$. Therefore $\alpha = \dfrac{2 \cdot 24}{16} = 3$. Since $3 < \pi$, the sector is slightly smaller than a semicircle.
