A circular sector has radius $r$ and arc length $s$. Which expression gives its area without finding the angle?

- $\dfrac{rs}{2}$
- $rs$
- $\dfrac{s^2}{2r}$
- $\pi rs$

## Explanation

The arc formula gives $\theta=\dfrac{s}{r}$. Substitution in $A=\dfrac{r^2 \theta}{2}$ eliminates the angle and leaves $\dfrac{rs}{2}$.
