A program computes a direction using $\arctan (y/x)$. Why do the vectors $(1,1)$ and $(-1,-1)$ give the same result?

- Both have positive components.
- They have the same component ratio and opposite directions.
- The two vectors are identical.
- Tangent has period $2\pi$ and no shorter period.

## Explanation

In both cases the ratio is one. Arctangent returns its principal value. The signs of the individual components distinguish the quadrants.
