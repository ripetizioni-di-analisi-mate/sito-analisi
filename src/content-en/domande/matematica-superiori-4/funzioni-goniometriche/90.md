What is $\arccos\left( \cos\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{3} \right) \right)$?

- $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

By the negative-angle identity, $\cos\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{3} \right) = \cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The arccosine of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the angle in $[0, \pi]$ with cosine $\dfrac{1}{2}$, that is, $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. The result differs from $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ because $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ does not belong to $[0, \pi]$.
