The function $f(x)=\sin x+\sin (2x)$ satisfies $f(\dfrac{\pi}{2})=1$. Which observation rules out $\pi$ as a period?

- $f(\dfrac{3\pi}{2})=-1$
- $f(0)=f(\pi)=0$
- $f(2\pi)=0$
- $f(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi)=1$

## Explanation

A period must preserve the function value at every input. The two points a distance $\pi$ apart here have opposite nonzero values.
