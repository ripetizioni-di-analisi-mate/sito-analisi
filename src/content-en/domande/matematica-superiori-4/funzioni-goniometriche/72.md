For which value of $\omega > 0$ does the function $y = \sin(\omega x)$ have period $4$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $8\pi$
- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The period of the function $y = \sin(\omega x)$ is $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$. Setting $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega} = 4$ gives $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The number $8\pi$ results from multiplying $2\pi$ by the period instead of dividing.
