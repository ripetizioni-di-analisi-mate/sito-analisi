What is $\sin\dfrac{31\pi}{3}$?

- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Write $\dfrac{31\pi}{3} = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 10\pi = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 5 \cdot 2\pi$. Angles that differ by a multiple of $2\pi$ have the same sine, so $\sin\dfrac{31\pi}{3} = \sin\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. The point corresponding to the angle therefore lies in the first quadrant, where sine is positive.
