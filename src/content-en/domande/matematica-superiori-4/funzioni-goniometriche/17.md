Which angle $\beta$, with $0^\circ \leq \beta < 360^\circ$, has the same terminal side as the directed angle $-150^\circ$?

- $150^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $210^\circ$
- $330^\circ$

## Explanation

Two angles that differ by a whole number of turns have the same terminal side. Adding one turn to the given angle gives $-150^\circ + 360^\circ = 210^\circ$, which belongs to the required interval. The angle $-150^\circ$ is a clockwise rotation, so its terminal side differs from that of $150^\circ$, a rotation through the same angle counterclockwise.
