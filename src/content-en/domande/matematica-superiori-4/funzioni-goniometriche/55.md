Given $\sin\alpha = 0.6$, what is $\sin(\pi + \alpha)$?

- $-0.6$
- $0.6$
- $-0.8$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

An additional rotation through $\pi$ takes the corresponding point to its diametrically opposite point, so both coordinates change sign. The identity for angles that differ by $\pi$ therefore gives $\sin(\pi + \alpha) = -\sin\alpha = -0.6$. The quadrant of $\alpha$ is not needed for this result.
