Is there an interval of width $2\pi$ on which sine is strictly increasing?

- No, a full turn includes increasing and decreasing arcs.
- Yes, just centre it at zero.
- Yes, just exclude both endpoints.
- Only if negative radians are used.

## Explanation

Sine completes one oscillation in an interval one turn wide. Its height both rises and falls within the interval even if the endpoints are excluded.
