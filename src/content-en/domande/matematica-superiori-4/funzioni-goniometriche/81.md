A point moves according to $x(t) = 4\cos\left( \pi t + \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)$, with $x$ in centimeters and $t$ in seconds. What is its position at time $t = 0$?

- $2\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}$
- $4\ \text{cm}$
- $2\ \text{cm}$
- $-2\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

At time $t = 0$ the argument of the cosine equals the initial phase $\varphi = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$. Therefore $x(0) = 4\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = 4 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 2\ \text{cm}$. The number $4$ is the amplitude, which equals the initial position only when $\varphi = 0$.
