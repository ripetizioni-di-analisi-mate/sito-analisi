A sinusoid crosses its midline at $t=0$ and is rising. Which model fits, with $A>0$ and $\omega>0$?

- $-A \sin (\omega t)$
- $A \cos (\omega t)$
- $A \sin (\omega t)$
- $-A \cos (\omega t)$

## Explanation

Both cosine models start at an extreme. Of the sine models, the one with a positive coefficient rises immediately after zero as required.
