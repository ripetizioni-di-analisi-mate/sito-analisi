In which quadrant is the point on the unit circle corresponding to the angle of $5$ radians?

- In the first quadrant
- In the second quadrant
- In the third quadrant
- In the fourth quadrant

## Explanation

Compare $5$ with multiples of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ using $\pi \approx 3.14$. We have $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} \approx 4.71$ and $2\pi \approx 6.28$, so $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < 5 < 2\pi$. Therefore the corresponding point lies in the fourth quadrant, where sine is negative and cosine is positive.
