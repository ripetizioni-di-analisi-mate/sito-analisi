Which expression equals $(\sin x + \cos x)^2 + (\sin x - \cos x)^2$ for every real number $x$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0$
- $4\sin x\cos x$

## Explanation

Expanding the squares gives
$$
\sin^2 x + 2\sin x\cos x + \cos^2 x + \sin^2 x - 2\sin x\cos x + \cos^2 x .
$$
The two cross terms are opposites and cancel, leaving $2\left( \sin^2 x + \cos^2 x \right)$. By the first Pythagorean identity, the expression therefore equals $2$.
