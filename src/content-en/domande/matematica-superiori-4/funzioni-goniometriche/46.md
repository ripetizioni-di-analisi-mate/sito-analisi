The function $f(x)=\cos x+b$, with $b$ constant, equals $0.7$ at a point $x_0$ and $-0.3$ at the point $x_0+\pi$. What is the value of $b$?

- $0.4$
- $0.5$
- $1$
- $0.2$

## Explanation

Since $\cos(x_0+\pi)=-\cos x_0$, the two values give $\cos x_0+b=0.7$ and $-\cos x_0+b=-0.3$. Adding the two equalities eliminates the cosine and gives $2b=0.4$. Hence $b=0.2$, and consequently $\cos x_0=0.5$.
