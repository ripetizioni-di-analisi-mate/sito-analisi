A sensor should measure $\cos x$ but adds the same constant $b$ to every value. It reads $0.7$ at angle $x$ and $-0.3$ at the opposite angle $x+\pi$. What is the offset $b$?

- $0.4$
- $0.5$
- $1$
- $0.2$

## Explanation

The readings are $\cos x+b$ and $-\cos x+b$. Their sum cancels the cosine and gives $2b=0.7-0.3=0.4$, so $b=0.2$.
