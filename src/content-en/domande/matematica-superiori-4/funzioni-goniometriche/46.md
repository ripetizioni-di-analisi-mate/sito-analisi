What is $4\sin^2\dfrac{\pi}{3} - 3\tan^2\dfrac{\pi}{6} + \cos 0$?

- $-5$
- $1$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The special-angle table gives $\sin^2\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{3}{4}$, $\tan^2\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, and $\cos 0 = 1$. Therefore
$$
4 \cdot \frac{3}{4} - 3 \cdot \frac{1}{3} + 1 = 3 - 1 + 1 = 3 .
$$
The number $-5$ results from interchanging $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.
