An angle $x$ lies in the first quadrant below $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. How do $\sin x$, $\cos x$ and one compare?

- $0<\cos x<\sin x<1$
- $0<\sin x<1<\cos x$
- $0<\sin x<\cos x<1$
- $0<\cos x=\sin x<1$

## Explanation

The point lies below the first-quadrant bisector, so its vertical coordinate is smaller than its horizontal one. Both are positive and strictly less than one.
