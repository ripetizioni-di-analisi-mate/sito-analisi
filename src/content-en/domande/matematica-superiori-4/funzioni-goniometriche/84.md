What is $\arctan\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$

## Explanation

The arctangent is the angle in $\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$ with the given tangent. The special-angle table gives $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$, so $\arctan\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$. The angle $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$ also has tangent $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ because tangent has period $\pi$, but it lies outside the interval.
