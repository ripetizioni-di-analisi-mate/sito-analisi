What is the least positive period of $\lvert \sin x\rvert$?

- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

A shift of $\pi$ reverses sine and preserves its absolute value. Consecutive zeros are exactly $\pi$ apart, so no shorter period can take a zero to another zero.
