What is the period of the function $y = 3\sin(4x - \pi)$?

- $8\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

For a sinusoidal function $y = A\sin(\omega x + \varphi) + b$ the period is $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$. Here $\omega = 4$, so $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The amplitude $3$ and the phase $-\pi$ do not affect the period.
