Which of the following inequalities is true? (The angles are measured in radians.)

- $\cos 1 < \sin 1 < \tan 1$
- $\sin 1 < \cos 1 < \tan 1$
- $\tan 1 < \sin 1 < \cos 1$
- $\cos 1 < \tan 1 < \sin 1$

## Explanation

The angle of $1$ radian lies between $\dfrac{\pi}{4} \approx 0.79$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2} \approx 1.57$. In the first quadrant, as the angle increases, sine increases and cosine decreases, and the two values coincide at $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Therefore $\sin 1 > \cos 1$. Moreover, $0 < \cos 1 < 1$, so $\tan 1 = \dfrac{\sin 1}{\cos 1}$ is greater than $\sin 1$. It follows that $\cos 1 < \sin 1 < \tan 1$.
