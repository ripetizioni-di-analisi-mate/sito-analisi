Why does $\tan x$ grow without bound as $x$ approaches $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ from the left?

- Sine tends to zero and cosine to one.
- Its numerator tends to one and its denominator to zero from above.
- Both numerator and denominator tend to zero.
- Cosine becomes arbitrarily large.

## Explanation

Read tangent as $\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}$. In the first quadrant the divisor stays positive and becomes small while the numerator remains near one.
