What is $\cot\dfrac{\pi}{6}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

Cotangent is the ratio of cosine to sine. Since $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, we have
$$
\cot\frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot 2 = \sqrt{3} .
$$
The number $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ is the tangent of $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, the reciprocal of the result.
