In a circle of radius $12\ \text{cm}$, a central angle measures $75^\circ$. What is the area of the corresponding sector?

- $60\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $15\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $30\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $5\pi\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The angle measures $\dfrac{\pi}{180} \cdot 75 = \dfrac{5\pi}{12}$ in radians. The area of a circular sector of radius $r$ and angle $\alpha$ in radians is $A = \dfrac{1}{2}\,r^2\alpha$, so
$$
A = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 144 \cdot \frac{5\pi}{12} = 30\pi\ \text{cm}^2 .
$$
The number $5\pi$ is the length of the arc in centimeters, since $r\alpha = 12 \cdot \dfrac{5\pi}{12} = 5\pi$.
