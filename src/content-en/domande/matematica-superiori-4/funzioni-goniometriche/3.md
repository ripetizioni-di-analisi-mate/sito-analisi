- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} + \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The special-angle table gives $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \sqrt{3}$. Therefore $\cos^2\dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\tan^2\dfrac{\pi}{3} = 3$. Substitution into the expression gives
$$
2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2} + 3 = \frac{7}{2} .
$$
