Which of the following numbers can be the cosine of an angle?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\sqrt{2}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The cosine is the horizontal coordinate of a point on the unit circle, so it lies between $-1$ and $1$. We have $\dfrac{\pi}{4} \approx 0.785$, which belongs to the interval $[-1, 1]$. Therefore there is an angle whose cosine is $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. By contrast, $\dfrac{\pi}{2} \approx 1.57$, $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.41$, and $-\dfrac{4}{3}$ lie outside this interval.
