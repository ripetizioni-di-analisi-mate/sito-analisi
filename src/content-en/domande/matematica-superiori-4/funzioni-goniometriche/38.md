For $x \neq k\,\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, which expression equals $\dfrac{1 - \cos^2 x}{\sin x \cos x}$?

- $\cot x$
- $\tan x$
- $\sin x$
- $1$

## Explanation

By the first Pythagorean identity, $1 - \cos^2 x = \sin^2 x$. The fraction therefore becomes $\dfrac{\sin^2 x}{\sin x \cos x}$, which can be simplified by canceling $\sin x$ since it differs from $0$ by assumption. This gives $\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x} = \tan x$.
