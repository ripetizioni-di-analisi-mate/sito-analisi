How many solutions does the equation $\sin x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ have in the interval $[0, 4\pi]$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $8$

## Explanation

In one period $[0, 2\pi)$, sine takes the value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ at two points, $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, because supplementary angles have the same sine. The interval $[0, 4\pi]$ contains two periods of sine, so there are four solutions, $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, $\dfrac{13\pi}{6}$, and $\dfrac{17\pi}{6}$. On the graph, the line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}$ crosses the sine wave twice in every period.
