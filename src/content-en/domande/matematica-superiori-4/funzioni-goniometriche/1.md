What is the radian measure of the angle $300^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The conversion formula gives the radian measure by multiplying the degree measure by $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$. Therefore $300^\circ$ corresponds to
$$
\alpha = \frac{\pi}{180} \cdot 300 = \frac{300\pi}{180} = \frac{5\pi}{3} .
$$
The same result follows by observing that $300^\circ = 360^\circ - 60^\circ$, that is, a full turn minus $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, and that $2\pi - \dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{5\pi}{3}$.
