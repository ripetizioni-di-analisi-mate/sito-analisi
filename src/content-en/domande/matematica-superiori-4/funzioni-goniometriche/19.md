A rigid disk rolls without slipping along a straight line. If its contact position advances by one full circumference, through how many radians does the disk rotate?

- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $1$
- It depends on the radius.

## Explanation

With no slipping, the distance traveled equals the length of rim that has touched the line. The equation $r\theta=2\pi r$ gives $\theta=2\pi$ regardless of the radius.
