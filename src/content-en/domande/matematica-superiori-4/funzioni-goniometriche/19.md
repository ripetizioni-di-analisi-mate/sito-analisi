In a circle of radius $6\ \text{cm}$, a central angle intercepts an arc of length $15\ \text{cm}$. What is the radian measure of the angle?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $90$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The radian measure of a central angle is the ratio of the arc length to the radius, $\alpha = \dfrac{s}{r}$. Therefore $\alpha = \dfrac{15}{6} = \dfrac{5}{2}$. The result is dimensionless because it is the ratio of two lengths expressed in the same unit.
