For $x \neq k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, which expression equals $\dfrac{\sin(\pi + x)}{\cos\left( \frac{\pi}{2} - x \right)}$?

- $1$
- $-1$
- $-\cot x$
- $\tan x$

## Explanation

The identity for angles that differ by $\pi$ gives $\sin(\pi + x) = -\sin x$. By the complementary-angle identity, $\cos\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} - x \right) = \sin x$. Since $x \neq k\pi$, the sine differs from $0$, so the fraction equals $\dfrac{-\sin x}{\sin x} = -1$.
