What is the degree measure of an angle of $\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$ radians?

- $67.5^\circ$
- $135^\circ$
- $22.5^\circ$
- $75^\circ$

## Explanation

The degree measure is obtained by multiplying the radian measure by $\dfrac{180}{\pi}$. Therefore
$$
\frac{180}{\pi} \cdot \frac{3\pi}{8} = \frac{540}{8} = 67.5 ,
$$
and the angle measures $67.5^\circ$. This agrees with the fact that $\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$ is less than $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, that is, less than $90^\circ$.
