A calculator returns about $0{,}0274$ for $\sin (\dfrac{\pi}{2})$. Which setting explains the result?

- It interpreted the input number as degrees.
- It calculated cosine in radians.
- It used tangent in radians.
- It interpreted the angle as one whole turn.

## Explanation

The number $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ is about $1{,}57$. The sine of roughly one and a half degrees is small, whereas the sine of a quarter turn in radians is one.
