What are the coordinates of the point on the unit circle corresponding to the angle $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$?

- $(-1, 0)$
- $(0, -1)$
- $(0, 1)$
- $(1, 0)$

## Explanation

The angle $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ corresponds to three-quarters of a turn counterclockwise from $A(1, 0)$. A quarter turn takes us to $(0, 1)$, half a turn to $(-1, 0)$, and three-quarters of a turn to $(0, -1)$. Therefore $\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = 0$ and $\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = -1$.
