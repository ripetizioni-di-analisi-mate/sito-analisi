Which axis does a point cross going counterclockwise from the second to the third quadrant, and which function changes sign?

- The positive vertical axis, and cosine changes sign.
- The positive horizontal axis, and cosine changes sign.
- The negative vertical axis, and sine changes sign.
- The negative horizontal axis, and sine changes sign.

## Explanation

The crossing is at $(-1,0)$. The horizontal coordinate stays negative while the vertical coordinate passes through zero and becomes negative.
