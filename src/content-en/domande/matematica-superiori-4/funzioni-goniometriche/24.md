Which statement about the signs of $\sin 2$ and $\cos 2$ is true? (The angle is measured in radians.)

- $\sin 2 > 0$ and $\cos 2 > 0$
- $\sin 2 < 0$ and $\cos 2 < 0$
- $\sin 2 < 0$ and $\cos 2 > 0$
- $\sin 2 > 0$ and $\cos 2 < 0$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{\pi}{2} \approx 1.57$ and $\pi \approx 3.14$, the angle of $2$ radians is between $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\pi$. The corresponding point therefore lies in the second quadrant, where the vertical coordinate is positive and the horizontal coordinate is negative. Hence $\sin 2 > 0$ and $\cos 2 < 0$.
