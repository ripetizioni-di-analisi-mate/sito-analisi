- $\sqrt{3} - 1$

## Explanation

Use related angles to reduce each angle to the first quadrant. We have $\sin\dfrac{4\pi}{3} = \sin\left( \pi + \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right) = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and $\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{6} = \cos\left( \pi - \dfrac{\pi}{6} \right) = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. Moreover, $\dfrac{7\pi}{4} = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2\pi$, so $\tan\dfrac{7\pi}{4} = \tan\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{4} \right) = -1$. The expression therefore equals
$$
-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - 1 = -1 .
$$
