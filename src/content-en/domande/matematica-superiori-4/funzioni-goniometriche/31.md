What is the slope of the line through the origin and the point on the unit circle corresponding to the angle $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$?

- $-\sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

By the geometric interpretation of the tangent, the line $OP$ has slope $\tan\alpha$. The angle $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ is supplementary to $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, so $\tan\dfrac{2\pi}{3} = -\tan\dfrac{\pi}{3} = -\sqrt{3}$. Indeed, the corresponding point is $P\left( -\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)$, and the ratio of its vertical to its horizontal coordinate is $-\sqrt{3}$.
