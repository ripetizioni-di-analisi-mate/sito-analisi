For the same geometric angle on the graph of $y=\tan x$, changing from degrees to radians changes which scale?

- Only the vertical scale.
- Both axes by the same factor.
- The horizontal scale changes while the vertical coordinate stays the same.
- No numerical value on either axis.

## Explanation

The two units assign different numbers to the same angle. The ratio of the circle point's coordinates, its tangent, depends only on the geometric angle.
