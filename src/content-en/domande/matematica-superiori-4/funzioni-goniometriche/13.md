What is the domain of the function $f(x) = \arccos\left( 1 - \dfrac{x}{2} \right)$?

- $[-4, 0]$
- $[0, 2]$
- $[0, 4]$
- $[-4, 4]$

## Explanation

Arccosine is defined for arguments in $[-1, 1]$, so we must have $-1 \leq 1 - \dfrac{x}{2} \leq 1$. Subtracting $1$ throughout gives $-2 \leq -\dfrac{x}{2} \leq 0$. Multiplying by $-2$ reverses both inequalities, giving $0 \leq x \leq 4$. Therefore the domain is $[0, 4]$.
