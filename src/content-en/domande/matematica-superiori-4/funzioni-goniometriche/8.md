Which of the following expressions equals $\sin^4 x - \cos^4 x$ for every real number $x$?

- $\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x$
- $1$
- $1 - 2\sin^2 x$
- $(\sin x - \cos x)^2$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is a difference of squares, so
$$
\sin^4 x - \cos^4 x = \left( \sin^2 x - \cos^2 x \right)\left( \sin^2 x + \cos^2 x \right) .
$$
By the first Pythagorean identity, the second factor equals $1$. Therefore the expression equals $\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x$. The expression $1 - 2\sin^2 x$ equals $\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x$, the negative of the result.
