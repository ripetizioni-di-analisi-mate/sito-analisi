For which values of $\alpha$ in the open interval $(0, 2\pi)$ is the cotangent undefined?

- At $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\alpha = \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- Only at $\alpha = \pi$
- Only at $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- At no value

## Explanation

Cotangent equals $\cot\alpha = \dfrac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$, so it is undefined where sine is zero, that is, at $\alpha = k\pi$. In the open interval $(0, 2\pi)$, the only multiple of $\pi$ is $\pi$ because the endpoints $0$ and $2\pi$ are excluded. At $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ the cosine is zero, so the cotangent there is $0$.
