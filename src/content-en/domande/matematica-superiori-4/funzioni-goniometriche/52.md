What is $\cos\dfrac{11\pi}{6}$?

- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Write $\dfrac{11\pi}{6} = 2\pi - \dfrac{\pi}{6}$, so by periodicity $\cos\dfrac{11\pi}{6} = \cos\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{6} \right)$. The negative-angle identity leaves the cosine unchanged, hence $\cos\dfrac{11\pi}{6} = \cos\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. The value is positive, as expected for an angle in the fourth quadrant.
