Consider the values of $\cos x$ at $u-h$ and $u+h$, with $u=\pi$. How do their heights compare?

- They are always opposites.
- The right-hand height is always greater.
- Both are always zero.
- They are equal for every $h$.

## Explanation

Symmetry gives $\cos (\pi-h)=-\cos h=\cos (\pi+h)$. The vertical line through the minimum is an axis of symmetry of the graph.
