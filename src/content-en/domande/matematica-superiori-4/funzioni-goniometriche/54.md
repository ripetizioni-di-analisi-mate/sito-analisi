For every real number $x$, which expression equals $\cos(-x) + \cos(\pi - x) + \sin\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} - x \right)$?

- $3\cos x$
- $-\cos x$
- $\sin x$
- $\cos x$

## Explanation

By the negative-angle identity, $\cos(-x) = \cos x$, and by the supplementary-angle identity, $\cos(\pi - x) = -\cos x$. By the complementary-angle identity, $\sin\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} - x \right) = \cos x$. Therefore the expression equals $\cos x - \cos x + \cos x = \cos x$.
