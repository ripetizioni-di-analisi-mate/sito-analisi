Which of the following lines is a vertical asymptote of the graph of $y = \tan x$?

- $x = \pi$
- $x = 2\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The vertical asymptotes of the tangent are the lines $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, where the cosine is zero and the tangent is undefined. For $k = 1$ we obtain the line $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. At $\pi$ and $2\pi$ the tangent equals $0$, and at $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ it equals $1$.
