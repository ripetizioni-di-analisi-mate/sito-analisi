In a purely sinusoidal tide, high tide is at hour $3$ and the next low tide at hour $9$. When is the next high tide?

- At hour $12$.
- At hour $15$.
- At hour $18$.
- At hour $21$.

## Explanation

The gap from a maximum to the next minimum is half a period, here six hours. After another six hours the tide returns to a maximum.
