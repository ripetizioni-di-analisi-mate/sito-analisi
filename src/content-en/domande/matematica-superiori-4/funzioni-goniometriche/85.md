What is $\arcsin\left( \sin\dfrac{4\pi}{5} \right)$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{4\pi}{5}$

## Explanation

The angle $\dfrac{4\pi}{5}$ is greater than $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so the arcsine of its sine differs from $\dfrac{4\pi}{5}$. By the supplementary-angle identity, $\sin\dfrac{4\pi}{5} = \sin\left( \pi - \dfrac{4\pi}{5} \right) = \sin\dfrac{\pi}{5}$. The angle $\dfrac{\pi}{5}$ belongs to $\left[ -\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$, so $\arcsin\left( \sin\dfrac{4\pi}{5} \right) = \dfrac{\pi}{5}$.
