On which of the following intervals is the function $y = \cos x$ increasing?

- $[0, \pi]$
- $\left[ -\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$
- $[\pi, 2\pi]$
- $\left[ \dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{3\pi}{2} \right]$

## Explanation

As $x$ goes from $\pi$ to $2\pi$, the corresponding point traces the lower half of the unit circle from $(-1, 0)$ to $(1, 0)$. Its horizontal coordinate increases from $-1$ to $1$, so cosine is increasing on $[\pi, 2\pi]$. On $[0, \pi]$ cosine decreases from $1$ to $-1$, and each of the other two intervals contains both an increasing and a decreasing section.
