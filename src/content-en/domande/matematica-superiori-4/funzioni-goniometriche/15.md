Two wheels sweep arcs of equal length. The second has twice the radius. How do their swept angles in radians compare?

- The second is twice the first.
- The two angles are equal.
- The second is half the first.
- The second is four times the first.

## Explanation

In radians we have $\theta=\dfrac{s}{r}$. At fixed arc length, doubling the radius halves the angle.
