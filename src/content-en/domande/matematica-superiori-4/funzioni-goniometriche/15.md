What is the radian measure of the angle $210^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{6\pi}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{12}$

## Explanation

The radian measure is obtained by multiplying the degree measure by $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$. Therefore $210^\circ$ corresponds to $\dfrac{210\pi}{180} = \dfrac{7\pi}{6}$. Equivalently, $210^\circ = 180^\circ + 30^\circ$, and $\pi + \dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{7\pi}{6}$. The angle $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, on the other hand, corresponds to $150^\circ$.
