What is $\cos\dfrac{7\pi}{3} - \sin\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{6} \right)$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3} + 1}{2}$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Write $\dfrac{7\pi}{3} = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2\pi$, so $\cos\dfrac{7\pi}{3} = \cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. By the negative-angle identity, $\sin\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{6} \right) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the expression equals $\dfrac{1}{2} - \left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) = 1$.
