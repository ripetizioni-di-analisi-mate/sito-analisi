Why does reflecting the entire sine graph across the diagonal fail to produce an **inverse function** on the full range?

- Several inputs have the same sine.
- Sine takes negative values.
- The domain excludes zero.
- Sine is positive in two quadrants, so no inverse could exist even on a restriction.

## Explanation

For example $\sin 0=\sin \pi=0$. After the axes are interchanged, the same input zero would have two different outputs.
