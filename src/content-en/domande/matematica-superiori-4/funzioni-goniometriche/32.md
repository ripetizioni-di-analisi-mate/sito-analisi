Let $P$ be the point on the unit circle corresponding to the angle $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$. The line $OP$ intersects the line $x = 1$ at the point $T$. What are the coordinates of $T$?

- $\left( 1, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$
- $\left( 1, \sqrt{3} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$
- $\left( 1, \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \right)$

## Explanation

The line $OP$ passes through the origin and has slope $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$, so its equation is $y = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,x$. Substituting $x = 1$ gives $y = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$. Therefore $T = \left( 1, \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \right)$, in agreement with the geometric interpretation of the tangent. The point $\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$, on the other hand, is $P$.
