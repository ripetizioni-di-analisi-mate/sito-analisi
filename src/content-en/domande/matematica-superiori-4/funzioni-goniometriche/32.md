On the Cartesian graph of $y=\tan x$ with $x$ in radians, what is the slope of the secant through the points with horizontal coordinates $-\pi/4$ and $\pi/4$?

- $1$
- $\pi/4$
- $2/\pi$
- $4/\pi$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinates are $-1$ and $1$, giving a vertical difference of two. The horizontal separation is $\pi/2$, so their ratio is $2/(\pi/2)=4/\pi$.
