Given that $\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{8}{17}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < \alpha < \pi$, what is $\cot\alpha$?

- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $-\dfrac{15}{8}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{17}$

## Explanation

By the first Pythagorean identity, $\sin^2\alpha = 1 - \dfrac{64}{289} = \dfrac{225}{289}$. In the second quadrant the sine is positive, so $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{15}{17}$. Cotangent is the ratio of cosine to sine, so $\cot\alpha = -\dfrac{8}{17} \cdot \dfrac{17}{15} = -\dfrac{8}{15}$. The number $-\dfrac{15}{8}$ is the tangent of $\alpha$.
