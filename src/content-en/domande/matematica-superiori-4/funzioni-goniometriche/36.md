Two distinct points on the unit circle have the same defined tangent. If one is in the first quadrant, where must the other be?

- In the third quadrant.
- In the first quadrant.
- In the second quadrant.
- In the fourth quadrant.

## Explanation

Tangent is the slope of the line from the point to the origin. This line meets the circle at two opposite points. The opposite of a first-quadrant point lies in the third.
