Given that $\sin\alpha = -\dfrac{12}{13}$ and $\pi < \alpha < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, what is $\tan\alpha$?

- $\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{12}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{12}$

## Explanation

By the first Pythagorean identity, $\cos^2\alpha = 1 - \dfrac{144}{169} = \dfrac{25}{169}$. In the third quadrant the cosine is negative, so $\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{5}{13}$. Therefore
$$
\tan\alpha = \frac{-\frac{12}{13}}{-\frac{5}{13}} = \frac{12}{5} ,
$$
which is positive, as it must be in the third quadrant.
