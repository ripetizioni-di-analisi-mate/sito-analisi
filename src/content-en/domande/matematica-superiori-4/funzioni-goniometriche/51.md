What is $\tan\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $-\sqrt{3}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Write $\dfrac{5\pi}{6} = \pi - \dfrac{\pi}{6}$. By the supplementary-angle identity, $\tan(\pi - \alpha) = -\tan\alpha$, so $\tan\dfrac{5\pi}{6} = -\tan\dfrac{\pi}{6} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$. The number $-\sqrt{3}$, on the other hand, is the tangent of $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.
