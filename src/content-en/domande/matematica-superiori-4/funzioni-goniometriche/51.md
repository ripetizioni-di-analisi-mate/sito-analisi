Which angle represents the point obtained by reflecting $(\cos x,\sin x)$ across the bisector $v=u$?

- $-x$
- $\pi+x$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x$
- $\pi-x$

## Explanation

Reflection interchanges the coordinates. The coordinates of the complementary angle are $\cos (\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x)=\sin x$ and $\sin (\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x)=\cos x$.
