A wheel makes three counterclockwise turns followed by a quarter-turn clockwise. What is the **total signed angle** without reducing modulo a turn?

- $\dfrac{13\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{11\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

Three turns contribute $6\pi$ and the return contributes $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Adding the signed displacements gives $\dfrac{11\pi}{2}$.
