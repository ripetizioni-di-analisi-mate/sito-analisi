Which angle $\beta$, with $0 \leq \beta < 2\pi$, corresponds to the same point on the unit circle as the angle $\dfrac{17\pi}{4}$?

- $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

Write $\dfrac{17\pi}{4} = \dfrac{16\pi}{4} + \dfrac{\pi}{4} = 2 \cdot 2\pi + \dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Angles that differ by a multiple of $2\pi$ determine the same point, so $\beta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$. The angle $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$ differs from the given angle by $3\pi$, that is, by three half-turns, and determines the diametrically opposite point.
