How many zeros does the function $y = 2\sin(3x)$ have in the interval $[0, \pi]$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The function is zero when $\sin(3x) = 0$, that is, when $3x = k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. Therefore its zeros are $x = k\,\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. The interval $[0, \pi]$ contains $0$, $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, and $\pi$, giving four zeros.
