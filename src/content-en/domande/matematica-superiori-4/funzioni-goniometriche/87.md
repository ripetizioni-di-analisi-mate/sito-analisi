What is the range of the function $y = 2\arctan x$?

- $\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$
- $[-\pi, \pi]$
- $(-\pi, \pi)$
- $(-2, 2)$

## Explanation

Arctangent takes every value in the open interval $\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$. It never reaches the endpoints because the lines $y = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ are horizontal asymptotes. Multiplying by $2$ gives every value in $(-\pi, \pi)$. The endpoints $-\pi$ and $\pi$ are again excluded, so the interval is open.
