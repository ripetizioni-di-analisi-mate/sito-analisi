For every real number $x$, which expression equals $\sin(\pi - x) + \cos\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} + x \right)$?

- $2\sin x$
- $-2\sin x$
- $0$
- $\sin x + \cos x$

## Explanation

By the supplementary-angle identity, $\sin(\pi - x) = \sin x$. The complementary-angle identities give $\cos\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} + x \right) = -\sin x$. Therefore the expression equals $\sin x - \sin x = 0$ for every $x$.
