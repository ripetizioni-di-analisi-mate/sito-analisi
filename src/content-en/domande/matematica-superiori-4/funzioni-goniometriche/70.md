What are the horizontal coordinates of the maximum points of the function $y = \sin\left( x - \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right)$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

Sine equals $1$ when its argument is $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. Therefore we must have $x - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, that is, $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$. Two consecutive maxima are one period apart, which is $2\pi$. For this reason the points $\dfrac{3\pi}{4} + k\pi$ with odd $k$ are minimum points.
