A graph is given by $f(x)=\sin x+2$. What is its vertical distance from the point with the same input on $-\sin x+2$ when $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The two heights are $3$ and $1$, symmetric about the midline of height two. Their difference is the vertical distance.
