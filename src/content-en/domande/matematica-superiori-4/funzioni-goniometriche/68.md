A temperature follows a sinusoid between $12$ and $20$ degrees. Which pair gives its midline and amplitude?

- $(16,4)$
- $(16,8)$
- $(4,16)$
- $(8,4)$

## Explanation

The midline is the average $\dfrac{20+12}{2}=16$. The amplitude is the distance from the midline to either extreme, which is four degrees.
