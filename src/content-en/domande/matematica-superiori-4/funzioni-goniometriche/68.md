What is the range of the function $y = 5\cos(3x) - 2$?

- $[-7, 3]$
- $[-3, 7]$
- $[-5, 5]$
- $[-7, 7]$

## Explanation

Cosine takes every value in $[-1, 1]$, so $5\cos(3x)$ takes every value in $[-5, 5]$. Subtracting $2$ yields the interval $[-5 - 2, 5 - 2] = [-7, 3]$. The coefficient $3$ of $x$ affects only the period, which is $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.
