A unit square has a diagonal inclined at $45^\circ$. How is $\cos 45^\circ$ read from the triangle formed by two adjacent sides and the diagonal?

- As the ratio $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.
- As the ratio $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{1}$.
- As the ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- As the diagonal divided by the perimeter.

## Explanation

The adjacent leg has length one and the hypotenuse has length $\sqrt{2}$ by Pythagoras. Their ratio gives the required cosine.
