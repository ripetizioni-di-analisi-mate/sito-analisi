A signal $a(t)=\sin t$ is sampled only at $t=k\pi$ for integer $k$. Which conclusion is justified?

- The signal is identically zero.
- The signal has zero amplitude.
- Every sample is zero even though the signal oscillates between samples.
- Its fundamental period is zero.

## Explanation

The sample times coincide exactly with the zeros of the sinusoid. At the intermediate time $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, for example, the signal equals one.
