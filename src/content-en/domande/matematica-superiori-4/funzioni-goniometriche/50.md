Over a turn a function has its maximum at $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, its minimum at $x=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ and value zero at the origin. Which function fits all three observations?

- $\cos x$
- $\sin x$
- $-\sin x$
- $-\cos x$

## Explanation

The proposed cosine functions equal one or minus one at the origin. Of the two sine functions only positive sine reaches a maximum after a quarter turn.
