What is $\sin\dfrac{19\pi}{2} + \cos(10\pi)$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $-2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Write $\dfrac{19\pi}{2} = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 8\pi = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 4 \cdot 2\pi$, so $\sin\dfrac{19\pi}{2} = \sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = -1$. Similarly, $10\pi = 5 \cdot 2\pi$, hence $\cos(10\pi) = \cos 0 = 1$. The sum is $-1 + 1 = 0$.
