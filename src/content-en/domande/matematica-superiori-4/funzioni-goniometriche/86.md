What is $\cos\left( \arcsin\left( -\dfrac{4}{5} \right) \right)$?

- $-\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

Set $y = \arcsin\left( -\dfrac{4}{5} \right)$, so $\sin y = -\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $y \in \left[ -\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$. Cosine is nonnegative on this interval. The first Pythagorean identity therefore gives
$$
\cos y = \sqrt{1 - \frac{16}{25}} = \frac{3}{5} .
$$
