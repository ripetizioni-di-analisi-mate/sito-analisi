Two functions are $f(x)=\sin x$ and $g(x)=\sin x+3$. Which coordinates of their extrema match when they are plotted on the same scales?

- Only the vertical coordinates of the maxima.
- Only the vertical coordinates of the minima.
- The horizontal coordinates of the maxima and minima.
- The horizontal-axis intercepts.

## Explanation

Adding a constant lifts every point by the same amount. The extrema therefore occur at the same angles.
