A point undergoes simple harmonic motion with amplitude $3\ \text{cm}$ and period $0.5\ \text{s}$ and at time $t = 0$ it is at $x = 3\ \text{cm}$. What is the equation of motion, with $x$ in centimeters and $t$ in seconds?

- $x(t) = 3\cos(4\pi t)$
- $x(t) = 3\cos(2t)$
- $x(t) = 3\cos(\pi t)$
- $x(t) = 3\sin(4\pi t)$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T} = \dfrac{2\pi}{0.5} = 4\pi\ \text{rad/s}$. In the equation $x(t) = A\cos(\omega t + \varphi)$ the initial position is $x(0) = 3\cos\varphi = 3$, so $\cos\varphi = 1$ and we can choose $\varphi = 0$. The equation of motion is therefore $x(t) = 3\cos(4\pi t)$. By contrast, the equation $x(t) = 3\sin(4\pi t)$ gives $x(0) = 0$.
