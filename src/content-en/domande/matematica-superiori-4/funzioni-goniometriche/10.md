What is the range of the function $y = 4\sin\dfrac{x}{3} - 1$?

- $[-4, 4]$
- $[-3, 5]$
- $[-5, 5]$
- $[-5, 3]$

## Explanation

The sine takes every value in the interval $[-1, 1]$, regardless of its argument. Multiplying by $4$ gives every value in $[-4, 4]$, and subtracting $1$ gives those in $[-5, 3]$. The coefficient $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of $x$ changes the period to $6\pi$ and leaves the range unchanged.
