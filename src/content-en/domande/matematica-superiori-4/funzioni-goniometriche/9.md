- $-1$
- $\cos x$
- $-\sin x$
- $1$

## Explanation

The related-angle identities give $\cos(\pi - x) = -\cos x$, $\tan(\pi + x) = \tan x$, and $\sin(-x) = -\sin x$. The numerator becomes $-\cos x \cdot \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x} = -\sin x$ because $\cos x \neq 0$. Therefore
$$
\frac{\cos(\pi - x)\tan(\pi + x)}{\sin(-x)} = \frac{-\sin x}{-\sin x} = 1
$$
for every $x \neq k\,\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, for which we also have $\sin x \neq 0$.
