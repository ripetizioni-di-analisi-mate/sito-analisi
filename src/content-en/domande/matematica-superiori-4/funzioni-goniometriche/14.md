A mass attached to a spring moves according to $x(t) = 8\cos\left( \dfrac{\pi}{3}\,t \right)$, with $x$ in centimeters and $t$ in seconds. What is the period of the motion?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}\ \text{s}$
- $3\ \text{s}$
- $6\ \text{s}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi^2}{3}\ \text{s}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is the coefficient of $t$ in the argument of the cosine, that is, $\omega = \dfrac{\pi}{3}\ \text{rad/s}$. The period of simple harmonic motion is $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$, so
$$
T = \frac{2\pi}{\frac{\pi}{3}} = 6\ \text{s} .
$$
The number $\dfrac{1}{6}$ is the frequency in hertz, that is, the number of oscillations per second.
