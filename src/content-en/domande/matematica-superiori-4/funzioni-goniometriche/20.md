A disk of radius $r$ rotates through $\theta>0$. What changes when tracking the point halfway along a radius instead of the point at its end?

- Both the angle and the arc length halve.
- The angle doubles and the arc length stays the same.
- The angle is the same and the arc length halves.
- Both arc and angle stay the same.

## Explanation

Points on a rigid disk rotate through the same angle. Arc length $s=r\theta$ is proportional to distance from the axis.
