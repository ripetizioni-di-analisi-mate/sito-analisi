What is the area of the circular sector with radius $4\ \text{cm}$ and central angle $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$?

- $12\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $3\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $6\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The area of a sector of radius $r$ and angle $\alpha$ in radians is $A = \dfrac{1}{2}\,r^2\alpha$. Therefore
$$
A = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 16 \cdot \frac{3\pi}{4} = 6\pi\ \text{cm}^2 .
$$
The number $3\pi$ is the length of the arc in centimeters, since $r\alpha = 4 \cdot \dfrac{3\pi}{4} = 3\pi$.
