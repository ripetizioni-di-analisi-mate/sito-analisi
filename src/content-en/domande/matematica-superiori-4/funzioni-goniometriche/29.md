What is the domain of the function $y = \tan(2x)$?

- $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x \neq k\,\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$
- $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\,\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

The tangent is defined when its argument differs from $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. We must therefore have $2x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$. Dividing both sides by $2$ gives $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\,\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The term $k\pi$ must also be divided by $2$, so the excluded points are $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ apart.
