Software normalizes the vector $(8,-6)$ by dividing it by its length. What is the sine of its direction angle?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The vector has length $\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=10$. Sine is the vertical component of the unit vector, namely $-\dfrac{6}{10}=-\dfrac{3}{5}$.
