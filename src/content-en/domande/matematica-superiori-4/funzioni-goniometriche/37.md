Let $\alpha$ be the angle, measured anticlockwise, that the vector $(8,-6)$ makes with the positive $x$-axis. What is $\sin\alpha$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The vector has length $\sqrt{8^2+(-6)^2}=\sqrt{100}=10$. The unit vector with the same direction and orientation is $\left(\dfrac{8}{10},-\dfrac{6}{10}\right)$ and corresponds to a point of the unit circle, so its components are $\cos\alpha$ and $\sin\alpha$. Therefore $\sin\alpha=-\dfrac{6}{10}=-\dfrac{3}{5}$.
