The point $P\left( -\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$ on the unit circle corresponds to the angle $\alpha$. What is $\tan\alpha$?

- $-\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

The cosine of $\alpha$ is the horizontal coordinate of $P$ and the sine is its vertical coordinate, so $\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$. Tangent is the ratio of sine to cosine, hence
$$
\tan\alpha = \frac{\frac{4}{5}}{-\frac{3}{5}} = -\frac{4}{3} .
$$
The negative sign is the sign of the tangent in the second quadrant, where $P$ lies.
