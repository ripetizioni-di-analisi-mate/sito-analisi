A slope increases without bound. Its angle to the horizontal is $\arctan m$ with $m>0$. Which angle does it approach?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ from below
- $\pi$ from below
- $0$ from above
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ from above

## Explanation

A line with increasing positive slope approaches vertical. The arctangent of any finite slope remains less than a right angle.
