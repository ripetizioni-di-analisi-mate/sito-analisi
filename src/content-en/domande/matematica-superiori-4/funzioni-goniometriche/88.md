What is $\tan\left( \arccos\dfrac{1}{3} \right)$?

- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $-2\sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$

## Explanation

Set $y = \arccos\dfrac{1}{3}$, so $\cos y = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $y \in [0, \pi]$, where sine is nonnegative. By the first Pythagorean identity, $\sin y = \sqrt{1 - \dfrac{1}{9}} = \dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$. Therefore $\tan y = \dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3} \cdot 3 = 2\sqrt{2}$. The number $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$ is the reciprocal of the result, that is, the cotangent of $y$.
