A sinusoid of the form $y = A\sin(\omega x) + b$, with $A > 0$ and $\omega > 0$, has maximum $5$, minimum $1$, and period $\pi$. What is its equation?

- $y = 3\sin(2x) + 2$
- $y = 2\sin\dfrac{x}{2} + 3$
- $y = 4\sin(2x) + 3$
- $y = 2\sin(2x) + 3$

## Explanation

The midline of the graph is halfway between the maximum and minimum, so $b = \dfrac{5 + 1}{2} = 3$. The amplitude is half the difference between the maximum and minimum, that is, $A = \dfrac{5 - 1}{2} = 2$. The period is $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega} = \pi$, so $\omega = 2$ and the function is $y = 2\sin(2x) + 3$.
