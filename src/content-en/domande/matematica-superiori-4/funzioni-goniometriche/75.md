The function $f(x)=A\sin(\omega x)$ is known only through $f(0)=0$ and $f(\pi)=0$, with $A,\omega>0$. Which restriction follows from the data?

- $A$ must equal $1$.
- $\omega$ must equal $1$.
- $A$ must be a positive integer.
- $\omega$ must be a positive integer.

## Explanation

The first condition is automatic. Since $A>0$, the second requires $\omega\pi=k\pi$. Thus $\omega$ is a positive integer, while amplitude remains free.
