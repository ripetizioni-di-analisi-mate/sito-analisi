Which of the following functions has period $\pi$ and range $[-3, 3]$?

- $y = 3\sin\dfrac{x}{2}$
- $y = \dfrac{3}{2}\sin(2x)$
- $y = 6\cos(2x)$
- $y = 3\cos(2x)$

## Explanation

The function $y = 3\cos(2x)$ has amplitude $3$, so its range is $[-3, 3]$. Its angular frequency is $2$, so its period is $\dfrac{2\pi}{2} = \pi$. The function $3\sin\dfrac{x}{2}$ has the same range and period $4\pi$. The functions $\dfrac{3}{2}\sin(2x)$ and $6\cos(2x)$ have period $\pi$ and ranges $\left[ -\dfrac{3}{2}, \dfrac{3}{2} \right]$ and $[-6, 6]$, respectively.
