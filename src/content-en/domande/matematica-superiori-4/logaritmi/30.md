For which values of $x$ does the equality $\ln \left( x^2 \right) = 2 \ln x$ hold?

- For every real $x$
- For $x > 0$
- For every $x \neq 0$
- For $x \geq 1$

## Explanation

The left-hand side $\ln \left( x^2 \right)$ is defined for every $x \neq 0$ because $x^2 > 0$. The right-hand side $2 \ln x$ is defined only for $x > 0$.

For $x > 0$ the power rule for logarithms applies, so the equality holds. For $x < 0$ the right-hand side is undefined, and the correct equality is $\ln \left( x^2 \right) = 2 \ln |x|$.

Therefore the equality holds *only* for $x > 0$.
