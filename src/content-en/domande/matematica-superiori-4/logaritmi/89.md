The pH of a solution is $\mathrm{pH} = -\log_{10} \left[ \mathrm{H}^+ \right]$. What is the approximate pH of a solution with $\left[ \mathrm{H}^+ \right] = 5 \cdot 10^{-4}$ mol/L?

- $4.70$
- $3.70$
- $3.30$
- $-3.30$

## Explanation

By the product rule for logarithms,
$$
\log_{10} \left( 5 \cdot 10^{-4} \right) = \log_{10} 5 + \log_{10} 10^{-4} = \log_{10} 5 - 4 .
$$
Since $\log_{10} 5 \approx 0.699$, we obtain $\mathrm{pH} = 4 - \log_{10} 5 \approx 3.30$. The value is less than $7$, so the solution is acidic.
