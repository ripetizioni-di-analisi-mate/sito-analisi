In the equation $5^{2x}-5^x-6=0$, the substitution $t=5^x$ gives the only admissible root $t=3$. A calculator has only the $\ln$ key. Which calculation and checking interval complete the solution?

- $x=\dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 5}$ with $0<x<1$.
- $x=\dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 3}$ with $0<x<1$.
- $x=\ln (3/5)$ with $x<0$.
- $x=\ln (15)$ with $x>1$.

## Explanation

Taking the natural logarithm of $5^x=3$ gives $x \ln 5=\ln 3$. The stated interval follows from $5^0<3<5^1$ and the fact that the exponential function is increasing.
