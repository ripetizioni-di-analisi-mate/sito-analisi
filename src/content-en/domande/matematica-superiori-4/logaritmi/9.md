- $x = \log_5 3$
- $x = \log_3 5$
- $x = 3$
- $x = \log_5 2$

## Explanation

Since $5^{2x} = \left( 5^x \right)^2$, set $t = 5^x$, with $t > 0$, to obtain $t^2 - t - 6 = 0$. The trinomial factors as $(t - 3)(t + 2)$, so the solutions are $t = 3$ and $t = -2$.

The value $t = -2$ does not satisfy $t > 0$ because an exponential is always positive. This leaves $5^x = 3$, and by the definition of a logarithm the unique solution is $x = \log_5 3$.
