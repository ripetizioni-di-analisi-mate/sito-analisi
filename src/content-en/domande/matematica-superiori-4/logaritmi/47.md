The line $y = 5$ intersects the graph of $y = \log_2 x$ at one point. What is its horizontal coordinate?

- $10$
- $32$
- $25$
- $\log_2 5$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate of the intersection solves the equation $\log_2 x = 5$.

By the definition of a logarithm, $x = 2^5 = 32$.

The function $\log_2 x$ takes every real value exactly once because it is strictly increasing with range $\mathbb{R}$, so the two graphs intersect at $(32, 5)$.
