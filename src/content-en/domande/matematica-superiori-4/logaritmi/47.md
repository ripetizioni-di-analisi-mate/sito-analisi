Which function agrees with $\exp(\ln|x|)$ everywhere the expression is defined?

- $x$ for all real numbers
- $|x|$ for $x\ne0$
- $-x$ for $x\ne0$
- $1$ for $x>0$

## Explanation

The argument $|x|$ must be positive, so $x\ne0$. On this domain the exponential undoes the logarithm and returns $|x|$ itself.
