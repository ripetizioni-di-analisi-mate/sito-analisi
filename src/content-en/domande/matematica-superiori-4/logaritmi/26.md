A spreadsheet returns only $\log_{10}$ values. Which expression gives $\ln x$ for every $x>0$?

- $\log_{10}x+\log_{10}e$
- $\dfrac{\log_{10}x}{\log_{10}e}$
- $\dfrac{\log_{10}e}{\log_{10}x}$
- $\log_{10}(ex)$

## Explanation

If $y=\ln x$, then $e^y=x$. Taking common logarithms gives $y\log_{10}e=\log_{10}x$, yielding the stated quotient.
