What is the value of $3 \log_2 6 - \log_2 27$?

- $\log_2 \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $3$
- $1$
- $9$

## Explanation

By the power rule for logarithms, $3 \log_2 6 = \log_2 6^3 = \log_2 216$.

By the quotient rule for logarithms, we obtain
$$
\log_2 216 - \log_2 27 = \log_2 \frac{216}{27} = \log_2 8 .
$$
Therefore the expression equals $3$.
