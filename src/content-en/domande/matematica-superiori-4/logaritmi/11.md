What are the solutions of the inequality $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (2x - 3) > -1$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2} < x < \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x > \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x < \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2} < x < \dfrac{7}{4}$

## Explanation

Since $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{-1} = 2$, write $-1 = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 2$. The base $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is less than $1$, so the direction reverses when comparing the arguments. Include the domain condition in the system:
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - 3 > 0 \\[4pt]
2x - 3 < 2
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x > \dfrac{3}{2} \\[4pt]
x < \dfrac{5}{2} .
\end{cases}
$$
Therefore the solutions are $\dfrac{3}{2} < x < \dfrac{5}{2}$.
