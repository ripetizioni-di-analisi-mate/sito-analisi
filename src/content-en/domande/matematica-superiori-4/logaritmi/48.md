As $x$ approaches $0$ from the right, what does $\log_{\frac{1}{5}} x$ approach?

- It approaches $-\infty$
- It approaches $0$
- It approaches $1$
- It approaches $+\infty$

## Explanation

The function $\log_a x$ has the $y$-axis as a vertical asymptote. For $0 < a < 1$ the function is decreasing, so as $x$ approaches $0$ from the right its values increase without bound.

For example, $\log_{\frac{1}{5}} \dfrac{1}{125} = 3$ and $\log_{\frac{1}{5}} \dfrac{1}{5^{10}} = 10$.

Therefore $\log_{\frac{1}{5}} x$ tends to $+\infty$.
