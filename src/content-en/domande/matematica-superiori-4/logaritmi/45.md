What is the inverse function of $y = 2^{x - 1} + 3$?

- $y = \log_2 (x - 3) - 1$
- $y = \log_2 (x + 1) - 3$
- $y = 1 + \log_2 (x - 3)$
- $y = 3 + \log_2 (x - 1)$

## Explanation

Solve for $x$ in terms of $y$. The equality $y = 2^{x - 1} + 3$ gives $2^{x - 1} = y - 3$, which is defined for $y > 3$.

By the definition of a logarithm, $x - 1 = \log_2 (y - 3)$, so $x = 1 + \log_2 (y - 3)$.

Interchanging the names of the variables gives the inverse function $y = 1 + \log_2 (x - 3)$, defined for $x > 3$.
