A substance decays according to the law $N(t) = N_0 e^{-k t}$ and its half-life is $10$ years. What is the approximate value of the constant $k$, in years$^{-1}$?

- $0.0500$
- $0.0301$
- $14.4$
- $0.0693$

## Explanation

After one half-life $T = 10$, we have $N(T) = \dfrac{N_0}{2}$, so $e^{-10 k} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

Taking the natural logarithm gives $-10 k = \ln \dfrac{1}{2} = -\ln 2$.

Therefore $k = \dfrac{\ln 2}{10} \approx 0.0693$.
