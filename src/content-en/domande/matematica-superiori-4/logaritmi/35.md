Why can $\log _2 3$ not be a fraction of positive integers $p/q$?

- It would imply $2^p=3^q$, which is impossible by prime factorization.
- All logarithms of integers are integers.
- Any number between one and two must be irrational.
- The base and argument must both be even.

## Explanation

Assuming $2^{p/q}=3$ and raising to the power $q$ would give two integers with different prime factorizations and no common prime factor. They cannot be equal.
