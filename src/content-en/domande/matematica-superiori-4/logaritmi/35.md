What is the solution of the equation $\log_5 x - \log_{25} x = 1$?

- $x = 25$
- $x = 5$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$
- $x = 625$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x > 0$. The change-of-base formula gives $\log_{25} x = \dfrac{\log_5 x}{\log_5 25} = \dfrac{1}{2} \log_5 x$.

The equation becomes $\log_5 x - \dfrac{1}{2} \log_5 x = 1$, that is, $\dfrac{1}{2} \log_5 x = 1$, so $\log_5 x = 2$.

Therefore $x = 5^2 = 25$, which satisfies $x > 0$.
