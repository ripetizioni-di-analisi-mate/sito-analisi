For which values of $x$ does the function $y = \left| \log_3 x \right|$ equal $2$?

- $x = 9$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{9}$
- $x = 9$
- $x = 9$ and $x = -9$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The equality $\left| \log_3 x \right| = 2$ holds when $\log_3 x = 2$ or $\log_3 x = -2$.

The first case gives $x = 3^2 = 9$ and the second gives $x = 3^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{9}$.

Both values are positive and hence belong to the domain. On the graph of $\left| \log_3 x \right|$ the value $\dfrac{1}{9}$ corresponds to the part of $\log_3 x$ reflected upward across the $x$-axis.
