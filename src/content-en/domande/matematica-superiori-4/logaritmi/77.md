How many binary digits are needed to write $2^{12}-1$ without leading zeros?

- $11$
- $13$
- $24$
- $12$

## Explanation

A positive integer uses $m$ binary digits when it is between $2^{m-1}$ and $2^m-1$. This integer is the largest representable with $12$ digits, all of them ones.
