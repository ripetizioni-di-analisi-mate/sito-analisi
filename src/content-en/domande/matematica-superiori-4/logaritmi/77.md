What is the solution of the equation $3^{2x} = 2^{x + 3}$?

- $x = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln \frac{9}{2}} \approx 0.461$
- $x = \dfrac{3 \ln 2}{\ln \frac{3}{2}} \approx 5.129$
- $x = \dfrac{3 \ln 2}{\ln \frac{2}{9}} \approx -1.383$
- $x = \dfrac{3 \ln 2}{\ln \frac{9}{2}} \approx 1.383$

## Explanation

Both sides are positive, so take the natural logarithm of both. The power rule for logarithms gives $2x \ln 3 = (x + 3) \ln 2$.

Factor out $x$ to obtain $x \left( 2 \ln 3 - \ln 2 \right) = 3 \ln 2$. Since $2 \ln 3 - \ln 2 = \ln \dfrac{9}{2}$ is positive, we can divide to obtain
$$
x = \frac{3 \ln 2}{\ln \frac{9}{2}} \approx 1.383 .
$$
