What is the value of $\log_{12} 3 + \log_{12} 48$?

- $1$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The arguments are positive, so the product rule for logarithms applies:
$$
\log_{12} 3 + \log_{12} 48 = \log_{12} (3 \cdot 48) = \log_{12} 144 .
$$
Since $144 = 12^2$, the sum equals $2$.
