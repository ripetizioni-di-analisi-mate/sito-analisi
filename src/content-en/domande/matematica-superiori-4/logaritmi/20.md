A positive quantity $x$ is measured with a multiplicative error $k>1$. How much does its natural logarithm change from $x$ to $kx$?

- $k\ln x$
- $\ln(k-1)$
- $k-1$
- $\ln k$

## Explanation

The change is the difference $\ln(kx)-\ln x$. The product rule cancels the two $\ln x$ terms, leaving $\ln k$ independently of the original reading.
