What is the value of $\log_3 \sqrt[4]{27}$?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Write the argument as a power of $3$. Since $27 = 3^3$, we have $\sqrt[4]{27} = \left( 3^3 \right)^{\frac{1}{4}} = 3^{\frac{3}{4}}$.

The identity $\log_a \left( a^x \right) = x$ therefore gives $\log_3 \sqrt[4]{27} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
