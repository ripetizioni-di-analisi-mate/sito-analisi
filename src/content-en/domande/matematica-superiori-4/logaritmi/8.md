- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = -1$
- $x = e$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{e}}$
- $x = \sqrt{e}$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{e}$
- $x = \sqrt{e}$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x > 0$. Setting $t = \ln x$ gives $2t^2 + t - 1 = 0$, with solutions $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $t = -1$.

Return to the original unknown $x$. From $\ln x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ we obtain $x = e^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{e}$, and from $\ln x = -1$ we obtain $x = e^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{e}$.

Both values are positive, so the solutions are $x = \sqrt{e}$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{e}$.
