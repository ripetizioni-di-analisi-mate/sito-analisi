What is the solution of the equation $\log_2 (x + 1) = \log_2 (5 - x) + 1$?

- $x = \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{9}{2}$
- $x = 3$
- $x = 4$

## Explanation

The domain conditions $x + 1 > 0$ and $5 - x > 0$ give $-1 < x < 5$.

Write $1 = \log_2 2$ and apply the product rule for logarithms to the right-hand side to obtain $\log_2 \big( 2(5 - x) \big)$. Equating the arguments gives $x + 1 = 10 - 2x$, so $3x = 9$.

Therefore $x = 3$, which satisfies $-1 < x < 5$.
