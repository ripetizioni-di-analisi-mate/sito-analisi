A reader displays $L=\log_2 x$ rounded **down** to an integer. If the display shows $5$, what interval gives exactly the possible values of $x$?

- $(32,64]$
- $[16,32)$
- $[32,64)$
- $[25,36)$

## Explanation

A display of five means $5\le\log_2 x<6$. Since the base is greater than one, exponentiation preserves the inequalities, giving $2^5\le x<2^6$.
