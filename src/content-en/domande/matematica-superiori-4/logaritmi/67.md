What are the solutions of the equation $\left( \log_3 x \right)^2 = \log_3 x^2$?

- $x = 9$
- $x = 1$ and $x = 9$
- $x = 3$ and $x = 9$
- $x = 0$ and $x = 9$

## Explanation

The left-hand side requires $x > 0$, so apply the power rule for logarithms to the right-hand side, giving $2 \log_3 x$.

Set $t = \log_3 x$ to obtain $t^2 = 2t$, that is, $t(t - 2) = 0$, with solutions $t = 0$ and $t = 2$.

From $\log_3 x = 0$ we obtain $x = 1$, and from $\log_3 x = 2$ we obtain $x = 9$. Both values are positive, so both are solutions.
