- $x = 4$
- $x = -1$ and $x = 4$
- $x = -1$
- $x = -4$

## Explanation

The domain conditions are $x > 0$ and $3x + 4 > 0$, so $x > 0$.

By the power rule for logarithms, $2 \log_5 x = \log_5 x^2$ and the equation becomes $\log_5 x^2 = \log_5 (3x + 4)$. Two logarithms with the same base are equal only if their arguments are equal, so $x^2 - 3x - 4 = 0$, with solutions $x = 4$ and $x = -1$.

The value $x = -1$ does not satisfy $x > 0$, so the only solution is $x = 4$.
