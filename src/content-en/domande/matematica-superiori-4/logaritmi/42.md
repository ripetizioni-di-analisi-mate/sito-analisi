Two positive quantities satisfy $\ln x+\ln y=\ln 16$. What is the least possible value of their sum?

- $4$
- $8$
- $16$
- $2$

## Explanation

The logarithm law turns the given relation into $xy=16$. The arithmetic mean is at least the geometric mean, so $x+y\ge 2\sqrt{xy}=8$, with equality at $x=y=4$.
