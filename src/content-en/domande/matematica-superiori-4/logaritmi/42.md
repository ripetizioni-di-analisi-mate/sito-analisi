Which of the following numbers is positive?

- $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} 5$
- $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\log_3 \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\ln 0.9$

## Explanation

If the base exceeds $1$, the logarithm is positive for arguments greater than $1$ and negative for arguments between $0$ and $1$. If the base is between $0$ and $1$, the signs are reversed.

The numbers $\log_3 \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\ln 0.9$ have bases greater than $1$ and arguments less than $1$, so they are negative. The number $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} 5$ has base less than $1$ and argument greater than $1$, so it is negative.

The base and argument of $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} \dfrac{1}{2}$ are both between $0$ and $1$, so the number **is positive**. Indeed, it equals $\log_3 2$.
