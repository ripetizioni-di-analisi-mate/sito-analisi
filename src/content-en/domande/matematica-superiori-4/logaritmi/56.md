What is the domain of the function $f(x) = \log_2 \left( x^2 - 5x + 6 \right)$?

- $(2, 3)$
- $(3, +\infty)$
- $(-\infty, 2) \cup (3, +\infty)$
- $(-\infty, 2] \cup [3, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The argument of the logarithm must be strictly positive, so solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 > 0$.

The trinomial factors as $(x - 2)(x - 3)$ and has a positive leading coefficient. Thus it is positive outside the interval between the roots, that is, for $x < 2$ or $x > 3$.

The values $x = 2$ and $x = 3$ make the argument zero and are excluded. Therefore the domain is $(-\infty, 2) \cup (3, +\infty)$.
