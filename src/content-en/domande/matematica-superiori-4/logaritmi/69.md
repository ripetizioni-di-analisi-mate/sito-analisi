What is the solution of the equation $\log_2 \left( \log_3 x \right) = 1$?

- $x = 6$
- $x = 9$
- $x = 8$
- $x = \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Applying the definition of a logarithm to the outer logarithm shows that the equation is equivalent to $\log_3 x = 2^1 = 2$.

Applying the definition again gives $x = 3^2 = 9$.

For $x = 9$ we have $\log_3 9 = 2 > 0$, so the argument of the outer logarithm is positive and the solution is admissible.
