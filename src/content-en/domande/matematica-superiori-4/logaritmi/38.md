Which change of coordinates turns the graph $y=Ax^k$, with $A,x>0$, into a straight line of slope $k$?

- $(X,Y)=(\ln x,\ln y)$
- $(X,Y)=(x,\ln y)$
- $(X,Y)=(\ln x,y)$
- $(X,Y)=(x^2,y^2)$

## Explanation

Taking logarithms gives $\ln y=\ln A+k\ln x$. In the proposed coordinates the equation becomes $Y=kX+\ln A$.
