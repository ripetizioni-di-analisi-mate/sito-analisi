Given $\ln 20 \approx 2.996$ and $\ln 3 \approx 1.099$, what is the approximate value of $\log_3 20$?

- $2.73$
- $0.37$
- $1.90$
- $6.67$

## Explanation

The change-of-base formula with natural logarithms gives
$$
\log_3 20 = \frac{\ln 20}{\ln 3} \approx \frac{2.996}{1.099} \approx 2.73 .
$$
This value is plausible because $3^2 = 9 < 20 < 27 = 3^3$, so $\log_3 20$ lies between $2$ and $3$.
