A principal earns compound interest at an annual rate of $5\%$, so after $t$ years its value is $C_0 \cdot 1.05^t$. After approximately how many years does it triple?

- $60$
- $40$
- $22.5$
- $14.2$

## Explanation

The principal triples when $C_0 \cdot 1.05^t = 3 C_0$, that is, $1.05^t = 3$.

Taking the natural logarithm of both sides gives $t \ln 1.05 = \ln 3$.

Therefore
$$
t = \frac{\ln 3}{\ln 1.05} \approx \frac{1.0986}{0.0488} \approx 22.5 \text{ years} .
$$
