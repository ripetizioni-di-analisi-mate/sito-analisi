What is the value of $\log_{\frac{1}{4}} 32$?

- $-\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Find the exponent $x$ such that $\left( \dfrac{1}{4} \right)^x = 32$. Since $\dfrac{1}{4} = 2^{-2}$ and $32 = 2^5$, the equation becomes $2^{-2x} = 2^5$.

Equating the exponents gives $-2x = 5$, so $\log_{\frac{1}{4}} 32 = -\dfrac{5}{2}$. The sign is negative because the base is less than $1$ and the argument is greater than $1$.
