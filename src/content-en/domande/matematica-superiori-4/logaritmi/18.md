A program compares models by the score $P=p_1p_2\cdots p_n$, where $0<p_i\le 1$, preferring higher scores. To avoid multiplying many small numbers it uses a cost to **minimize**. Which cost gives the same ranking of preferences?

- $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ln p_i$
- $-\prod_{i=1}^{n}\ln p_i$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_i$
- $-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ln p_i$

## Explanation

Because the logarithm is strictly increasing, maximizing $P$ is equivalent to maximizing $\ln P=\sum_i \ln p_i$. The minus sign reverses the order and turns maximization into minimization.
