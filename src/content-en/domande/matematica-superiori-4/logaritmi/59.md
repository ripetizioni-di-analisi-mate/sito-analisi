A sensor computes $S(x)=1/\ln x$. As $x$ increases through $1$, how do readings behave immediately before and after that value?

- They tend to $+\infty$ from both sides.
- They tend to $-\infty$ from the left and to $+\infty$ from the right.
- They tend to zero from both sides.
- They tend to $+\infty$ from the left and to $-\infty$ from the right.

## Explanation

Near one the logarithm approaches zero. It is negative for $0<x<1$ and positive for $x>1$. Its reciprocal grows without bound in magnitude and retains the sign of the denominator.
