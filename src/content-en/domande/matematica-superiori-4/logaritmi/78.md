Two independent sounds each have intensity $I$. Intensities add, and $L=10\log_{10}(I/I_0)$. What is the increase over a single sound?

- $10\log_{10}2$ decibels
- $2$ decibels
- $20$ decibels
- $L$ decibels for every $I$.

## Explanation

The intensity ratio is $2I/I=2$. The level difference is therefore $10\log_{10}2$, about three decibels, regardless of the initial intensity.
