What are the solutions of the equation $e^{2x} - 5 e^x + 6 = 0$?

- $x = \ln 2$ and $x = \ln 3$
- $x = 2$ and $x = 3$
- $x = \ln 2$
- $x = -\ln 2$ and $x = -\ln 3$

## Explanation

Since $e^{2x} = \left( e^x \right)^2$, set $t = e^x$, with $t > 0$, to obtain $t^2 - 5t + 6 = 0$, that is, $(t - 2)(t - 3) = 0$.

The values $t = 2$ and $t = 3$ are both positive, so both are admissible.

From $e^x = 2$ we obtain $x = \ln 2$ and from $e^x = 3$ we obtain $x = \ln 3$.
