What is the solution of the equation $\ln (x + 3) - \ln (x - 1) = \ln 3$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = -3$
- $x = 3$
- No solution

## Explanation

The domain conditions $x + 3 > 0$ and $x - 1 > 0$ give $x > 1$.

For $x > 1$ the quotient rule for logarithms applies and the equation becomes $\ln \dfrac{x + 3}{x - 1} = \ln 3$. Two logarithms with the same base are equal only if their arguments are equal, so $x + 3 = 3(x - 1)$.

This gives $2x = 6$, that is, $x = 3$, which satisfies $x > 1$.
