Which of the following functions is strictly decreasing on its domain?

- $y = \log_{\frac{2}{3}} x$
- $y = \log_{\frac{3}{2}} x$
- $y = -\log_{\frac{1}{3}} x$
- $y = \log_2 x^3$

## Explanation

The function $\log_a x$ is strictly decreasing when $0 < a < 1$ and strictly increasing when $a > 1$.

The base $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is less than $1$, so $\log_{\frac{2}{3}} x$ **is decreasing**. The base $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is greater than $1$, so $\log_{\frac{3}{2}} x$ is increasing.

Since $-\log_{\frac{1}{3}} x = \log_3 x$, this function is also increasing. Finally, $\log_2 x^3$ has domain $x > 0$ and equals $3 \log_2 x$, which is increasing.
