What is the vertical asymptote of the graph of $y = \log_3 (x + 2) - 4$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = -4$
- $y = -4$
- $x = -2$

## Explanation

The graph is obtained from the graph of $\log_3 x$ by shifting it $2$ units to the left and $4$ units down.

The vertical shift leaves the vertical asymptote unchanged, whereas the horizontal shift moves it. The asymptote of $\log_3 x$ is $x = 0$, so that of the shifted function is $x = -2$.

Indeed, the domain is $x + 2 > 0$, that is, $x > -2$.
