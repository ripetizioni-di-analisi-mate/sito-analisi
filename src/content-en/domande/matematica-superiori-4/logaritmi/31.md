Two measurements have approximate common logarithms $2.1$ and $2.4$. Given $10^{0.3}\simeq2$, what can you conclude?

- The second is roughly twice the first.
- The second exceeds the first by $0.3$ units.
- The first is roughly twice the second.
- The second is roughly ten times the first.

## Explanation

The logarithms differ by $0.3$, which is the logarithm of the second measurement divided by the first. Their ratio is therefore about $2$.
