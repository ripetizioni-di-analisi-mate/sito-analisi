Given $\log_{10} 2 \approx 0.301$ and $\log_{10} 3 \approx 0.477$, what is the approximate value of $\log_{10} 18$?

- $1.255$
- $1.079$
- $0.778$
- $0.954$

## Explanation

Write the argument as a product of prime factors, $18 = 2 \cdot 3^2$.

By the product and power rules for logarithms, $\log_{10} 18 = \log_{10} 2 + 2 \log_{10} 3$.

Therefore $\log_{10} 18 \approx 0.301 + 2 \cdot 0.477 = 1.255$.
