At what point does the graph of $y = \log_2 (8 - x) + 1$ meet the $y$-axis?

- At the point with vertical coordinate $3$
- At the point with vertical coordinate $2$
- At the point with vertical coordinate $\log_2 9$
- At the point with vertical coordinate $4$

## Explanation

The intersection with the $y$-axis has horizontal coordinate $0$, which belongs to the domain $x < 8$.

Substituting $x = 0$ gives $y = \log_2 8 + 1 = 3 + 1 = 4$.

Therefore the graph meets the $y$-axis at $(0, 4)$.
