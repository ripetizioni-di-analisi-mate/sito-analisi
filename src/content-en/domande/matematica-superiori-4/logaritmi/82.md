A transducer records $y=\ln (1+t^2)$, where the position $t$ may be positive or negative. For which positions does the inverse readout formula $t=\sqrt{e^y-1}$ always return the original position?

- For every real position.
- Only for $t\le 0$.
- Only for $t=0$.
- For $t\ge 0$.

## Explanation

Substituting the recorded value gives $\sqrt{e^{\ln (1+t^2)}-1}=\sqrt{t^2}=|t|$. The result recovers the original position exactly on the nonnegative half-line.
