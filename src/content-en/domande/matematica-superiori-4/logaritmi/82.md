What are the solutions of the inequality $\log_3 (x - 2) < 2$?

- $x < 11$
- $2 < x < 8$
- $x > 11$
- $2 < x < 11$

## Explanation

Write $2 = \log_3 9$. The base $3$ is greater than $1$, so the direction is preserved. Include the domain condition in the system:
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 2 > 0 \\[4pt]
x - 2 < 9
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x > 2 \\[4pt]
x < 11 .
\end{cases}
$$
Therefore the solutions are $2 < x < 11$.
