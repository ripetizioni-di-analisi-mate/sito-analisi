Which transformation produces the graph of $y = \log_2 \dfrac{x}{8}$ from the graph of $y = \log_2 x$?

- A shift to the right by $8$ units
- A shift upward by $3$ units
- A shift downward by $3$ units
- A shift downward by $8$ units

## Explanation

For $x > 0$, apply the quotient rule for logarithms:
$$
\log_2 \frac{x}{8} = \log_2 x - \log_2 8 = \log_2 x - 3 .
$$
Subtracting $3$ from every function value shifts the graph down by $3$ units. The domain $x > 0$ and asymptote $x = 0$ stay the same.
