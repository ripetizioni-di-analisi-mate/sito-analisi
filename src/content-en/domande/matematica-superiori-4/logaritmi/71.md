What is the solution of the equation $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x - 1) = -3$?

- $x = \dfrac{9}{8}$
- $x = 7$
- $x = -7$
- $x = 9$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, the equation is equivalent to
$$
x - 1 = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{-3} = 2^3 = 8 .
$$
Therefore $x = 9$. The argument equals $8 > 0$, so the domain condition is satisfied.
