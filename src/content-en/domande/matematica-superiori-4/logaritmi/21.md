Which of the following expressions is undefined over the real numbers?

- $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 3$
- $\log_3 (-9)$
- $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\log_{10} 0.5$

## Explanation

The logarithm $\log_a b$ is defined when the base satisfies $a > 0$ and $a \neq 1$ and the argument satisfies $b > 0$.

The expressions $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 3$, $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\log_{10} 0.5$ have positive bases different from $1$ and positive arguments, so they are defined. The last two are negative numbers.

In the expression $\log_3 (-9)$, the argument is negative. Since $3^x > 0$ for every $x$, no power of $3$ equals $-9$, so $\log_3 (-9)$ **is undefined**.
