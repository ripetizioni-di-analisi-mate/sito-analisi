What is the value of $\log_{27} 9$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Find the exponent $x$ such that $27^x = 9$. Writing the two powers with base $3$ gives $3^{3x} = 3^2$, so $3x = 2$.

Therefore $\log_{27} 9 = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The value is less than $1$, as expected, because the argument $9$ is less than the base $27$.
