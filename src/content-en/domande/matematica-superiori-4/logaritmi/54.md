Which transformation maps the graph of $y = \log_2 x$ onto the graph of $y = -\log_2 (-x)$?

- A reflection through the origin
- A reflection across the $x$-axis
- A reflection across the $y$-axis
- A reflection across the line $y = x$

## Explanation

The graph of $\log_2 (-x)$ is the reflection of the graph of $\log_2 x$ across the $y$-axis, with domain $x < 0$.

The minus sign in front of the logarithm adds a reflection across the $x$-axis.

The composition of these two reflections maps $(p, q)$ to $(-p, -q)$, so it is reflection through the origin. For example, the point $(2, 1)$ on the graph of $\log_2 x$ corresponds to $(-2, -1)$.
