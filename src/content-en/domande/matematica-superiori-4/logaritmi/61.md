What is the domain of the function $f(x) = \ln (\ln x)$?

- $(0, +\infty)$
- $(1, +\infty)$
- $(e, +\infty)$
- $(0, 1)$

## Explanation

The inner logarithm requires $x > 0$, and the outer logarithm requires its argument to be positive, that is, $\ln x > 0$.

Since $\ln 1 = 0$ and the natural logarithm is increasing, $\ln x > 0$ is equivalent to $x > 1$.

The values $x > 1$ also satisfy $x > 0$, so the domain is $(1, +\infty)$.
