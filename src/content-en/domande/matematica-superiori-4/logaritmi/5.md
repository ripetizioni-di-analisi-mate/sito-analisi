- $(-\infty, -1]$
- $(-3, +\infty)$
- $(-3, -1]$

## Explanation

The domain conditions require a positive logarithm argument and a nonnegative radicand:
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 3 > 0 \\[4pt]
1 - \log_2 (x + 3) \geq 0
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x > -3 \\[4pt]
\log_2 (x + 3) \leq 1 .
\end{cases}
$$
Since $1 = \log_2 2$ and the base $2$ exceeds $1$, the second condition is equivalent to $x + 3 \leq 2$, that is, $x \leq -1$. The value $x = -1$ makes the radicand zero and is therefore admissible. Hence $D = (-3, -1]$.
