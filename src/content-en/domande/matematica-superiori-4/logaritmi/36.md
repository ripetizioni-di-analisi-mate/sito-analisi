What is the value of $\log_4 2 + \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 4$?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $4^{\frac{1}{2}} = 2$, we have $\log_4 2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

For the reciprocal base we have $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} 4 = -\log_2 4 = -2$.

Therefore the sum equals $\dfrac{1}{2} - 2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}$.
