A value of $\log_2 x$ is printed as $3.0$ after rounding to the nearest tenth. Which interval must contain $x$ regardless of how ties are rounded?

- $[2.95,3.05]$
- $[2^{2.95},2^{3.05}]$
- $[8.95,9.05]$
- $[2^{3.1},2^{3.2}]$

## Explanation

Before rounding the exponent differs from $3$ by at most $0.05$. The function $2^t$ is increasing, so the stated endpoints bound the possible values of $x$.
