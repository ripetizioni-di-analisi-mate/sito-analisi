Given $\log_2 3 = a$, what is $\log_6 3$ in terms of $a$?

- $\dfrac{1}{1 + a}$
- $\dfrac{a}{1 + a}$
- $\dfrac{a}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1 + a}{a}$

## Explanation

Using the change-of-base formula with base $2$ gives
$$
\log_6 3 = \frac{\log_2 3}{\log_2 6} .
$$
The product rule for logarithms gives $\log_2 6 = \log_2 2 + \log_2 3 = 1 + a$. Therefore $\log_6 3 = \dfrac{a}{1 + a}$.
