What is the solution of the equation $\log_x 16 = 2$?

- $x = 4$
- $x = \pm 4$
- $x = 8$
- $x = 256$

## Explanation

The unknown is in the base, so the conditions are $x > 0$ and $x \neq 1$.

By the definition of a logarithm, the equation is equivalent to $x^2 = 16$, with solutions $x = 4$ and $x = -4$.

The value $x = -4$ does not satisfy $x > 0$, so the only solution is $x = 4$.
