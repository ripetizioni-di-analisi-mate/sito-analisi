- $x = \dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 2} \approx 1.585$
- $x = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln \frac{2}{3}} \approx -1.710$
- $x = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln \frac{3}{2}} \approx 1.710$
- $x = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 6} \approx 0.387$

## Explanation

Both sides are positive, so take the natural logarithm of both. The power rule for logarithms gives $(x + 1) \ln 2 = x \ln 3$, that is, $x \left( \ln 3 - \ln 2 \right) = \ln 2$.

Since $\ln 3 - \ln 2 = \ln \dfrac{3}{2}$ differs from $0$, we can divide to obtain
$$
x = \frac{\ln 2}{\ln \frac{3}{2}} \approx \frac{0.6931}{0.4055} \approx 1.710 .
$$
