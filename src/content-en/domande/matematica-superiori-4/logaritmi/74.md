Why does $\ln(-3)=\dfrac12\ln9$ fail to give a real logarithm of $-3$?

- $\exp(\dfrac12\ln9)=3$.
- $\ln9=0$.
- $\exp(\dfrac12\ln9)=-9$.
- The logarithm of $9$ does not exist.

## Explanation

Exponentiating the right-hand side returns the principal square root of $9$, namely $3$. Every real exponential is positive, so no real exponent produces $-3$.
