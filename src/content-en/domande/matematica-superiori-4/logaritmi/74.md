What is the solution of the equation $4^{x - 2} = 3$?

- $x = 2 + \log_4 3$
- $x = \log_4 3 - 2$
- $x = \log_4 5$
- $x = 2 \log_4 3$

## Explanation

The right-hand side is positive, so by the definition of a logarithm the equation is equivalent to $x - 2 = \log_4 3$.

Therefore $x = 2 + \log_4 3$. A calculator gives $\log_4 3 = \dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 4} \approx 0.792$, so $x \approx 2.792$.
