What is the value of $\log_2 \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{16}$?

- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $-2$
- $-\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

By the quotient rule for logarithms, $\log_2 \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{16} = \log_2 \sqrt{2} - \log_2 16$.

Since $\sqrt{2} = 2^{\frac{1}{2}}$ and $16 = 2^4$, the two logarithms equal $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $4$.

Therefore the result is $\dfrac{1}{2} - 4 = -\dfrac{7}{2}$.
