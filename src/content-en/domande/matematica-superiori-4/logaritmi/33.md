Compare $\ln(1+u)$ with $0$ for $-1<u<0$. Which statement is correct?

- $\ln(1+u)>0$
- $\ln(1+u)=0$
- The logarithm is undefined.
- $\ln(1+u)<0$

## Explanation

The assumption gives $0<1+u<1$. The natural logarithm is increasing and equals zero at $1$, so it is negative on that interval.
