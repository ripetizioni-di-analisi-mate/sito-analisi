The graph of the function $y = \log_a x$ passes through $(9, -2)$. What is the value of its base $a$?

- $a = 3$
- $a = \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $a = \dfrac{1}{9}$
- $a = \dfrac{1}{81}$

## Explanation

The point lies on the graph, so $\log_a 9 = -2$. By the definition of a logarithm, this means $a^{-2} = 9$, that is, $\dfrac{1}{a^2} = 9$.

This gives $a^2 = \dfrac{1}{9}$. The base must be positive, so $a = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
