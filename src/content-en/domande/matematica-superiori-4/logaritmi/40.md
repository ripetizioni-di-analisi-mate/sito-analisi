For $a,b>1$ and $a\ne b$, what is the horizontal coordinate of the only point common to the graphs $y=\log_a x$ and $y=\log_b x$?

- $x=0$
- $x=ab$
- $x=\sqrt{ab}$
- $x=1$

## Explanation

Changing base gives $\ln x(1/\ln a-1/\ln b)=0$. The difference is nonzero, so $\ln x=0$ and $x=1$.
