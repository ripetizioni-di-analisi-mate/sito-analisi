What is the value of the product $\log_{\sqrt{3}} 9 \cdot \log_9 \sqrt{3}$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $\left( \sqrt{3} \right)^4 = 9$, we have $\log_{\sqrt{3}} 9 = 4$.

Since $9^{\frac{1}{4}} = 3^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{3}$, we have $\log_9 \sqrt{3} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.

Therefore the product equals $4 \cdot \dfrac{1}{4} = 1$. The result also follows from the identity $\log_a b = \dfrac{1}{\log_b a}$, a consequence of the change-of-base formula.
