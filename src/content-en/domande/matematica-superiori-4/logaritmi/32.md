If $u=\log_2 x$ and $v=\log_2 y$, which relation between $u$ and $v$ expresses $xy=8$ for $x,y>0$?

- $u+v=3$
- $uv=3$
- $u+v=8$
- $u-v=3$

## Explanation

Taking base-two logarithms gives $\log_2x+\log_2y=\log_28=3$. Thus a constant-product curve becomes a straight line in the new coordinates.
