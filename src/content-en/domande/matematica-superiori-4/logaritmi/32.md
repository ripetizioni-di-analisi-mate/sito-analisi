Given $\log_2 3 = b$, what is $\log_2 \dfrac{9}{8}$ in terms of $b$?

- $2b - 3$
- $2b + 3$
- $b - 3$
- $3 - 2b$

## Explanation

By the quotient rule for logarithms, $\log_2 \dfrac{9}{8} = \log_2 9 - \log_2 8$.

Since $9 = 3^2$, the power rule for logarithms gives $\log_2 9 = 2 \log_2 3 = 2b$. Also, $\log_2 8 = 3$.

Therefore $\log_2 \dfrac{9}{8} = 2b - 3$.
