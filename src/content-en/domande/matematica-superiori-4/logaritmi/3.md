Given $\log_3 2 = a$, what is $\log_3 72$ in terms of $a$?

- $3a + 2$
- $2a + 3$
- $3a$
- $a^3 + 2$

## Explanation

Write the argument as a product of prime factors, $72 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2$. By the product and power rules for logarithms,
$$
\log_3 72 = 3 \log_3 2 + 2 \log_3 3 .
$$
Since $\log_3 2 = a$ and $\log_3 3 = 1$, we obtain $\log_3 72 = 3a + 2$.
