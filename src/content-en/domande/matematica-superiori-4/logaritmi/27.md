For $x > 0$ and $y > 0$, which expression equals $\ln \dfrac{x^3}{e \sqrt{y}}$?

- $3 \ln x - 1 - \dfrac{1}{2} \ln y$
- $3 \ln x + 1 - \dfrac{1}{2} \ln y$
- $3 \ln x - 1 - 2 \ln y$
- $\ln 3x - 1 - \dfrac{1}{2} \ln y$

## Explanation

By the quotient rule for logarithms, subtract the logarithm of the denominator:
$$
\ln \frac{x^3}{e \sqrt{y}} = \ln x^3 - \left( \ln e + \ln y^{\frac{1}{2}} \right) .
$$
By the power rule for logarithms, $\ln x^3 = 3 \ln x$ and $\ln y^{\frac{1}{2}} = \dfrac{1}{2} \ln y$, whereas $\ln e = 1$.

Therefore the expression equals $3 \ln x - 1 - \dfrac{1}{2} \ln y$.
