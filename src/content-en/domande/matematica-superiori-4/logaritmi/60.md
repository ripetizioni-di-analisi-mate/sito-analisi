For which $k$ does $f(x)=\ln(x^2+k)$ have domain exactly $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$?

- $k=0$
- $k=1$
- $k=-1$
- Every $k<0$.

## Explanation

For $k=0$ the argument $x^2$ is positive everywhere except at zero. If $k>0$, zero is included. If $k<0$, a whole interval around zero is excluded.
