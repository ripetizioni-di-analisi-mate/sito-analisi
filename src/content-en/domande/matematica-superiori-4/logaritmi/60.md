What is the domain of the function $f(x) = \sqrt{\log_{\frac{1}{2}} x}$?

- $(0, 1]$
- $[1, +\infty)$
- $(0, 1)$
- $\left( 0, \dfrac{1}{2} \right]$

## Explanation

The conditions are $x > 0$ for the logarithm and $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} x \geq 0$ for the radical.

Since $0 = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} 1$ and the base is less than $1$, the second condition is equivalent to $x \leq 1$, with the direction reversed.

The value $x = 1$ makes the radicand zero and is admissible. Therefore the domain is $(0, 1]$.
