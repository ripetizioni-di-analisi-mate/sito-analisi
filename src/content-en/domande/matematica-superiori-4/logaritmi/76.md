For which values of the parameter $k$ does the equation $2^x = k - 3$ have a solution?

- For $k \geq 3$
- For $k > 3$
- For $k > 0$
- For every real $k$

## Explanation

The left-hand side $2^x$ is positive for every $x$ and takes all positive values.

Therefore the equation has a solution if and only if $k - 3 > 0$, in which case the solution is $x = \log_2 (k - 3)$.

For $k = 3$ the equation becomes $2^x = 0$, which has no solutions. Therefore the equation has a solution for $k > 3$.
