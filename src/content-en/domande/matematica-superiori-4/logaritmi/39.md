What is $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{99}\log_{10}\dfrac{k+1}{k}$?

- $99$
- $1$
- $\log_{10}99$
- $2$

## Explanation

The sum is the logarithm of the product $(\dfrac{2}{1})(\dfrac{3}{2})\cdots(\dfrac{100}{99})$. Each intermediate numerator cancels the next denominator. This leaves $100$, whose common logarithm is $2$.
