What is the value of $5^{\log_5 7 + 1}$?

- $8$
- $12$
- $35$
- $7$

## Explanation

By the product rule for powers, write
$$
5^{\log_5 7 + 1} = 5^{\log_5 7} \cdot 5^1 .
$$
By the identity $a^{\log_a b} = b$, the first factor equals $7$. Therefore the expression equals $7 \cdot 5 = 35$.
