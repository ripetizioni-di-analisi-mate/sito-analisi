What is the base $a$ if the graph of $y=\log_a x$ is the reflection of $y=\log_5 x$ in the horizontal axis?

- $-5$
- $\dfrac15$
- $25$
- $\sqrt5$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinate of the reflected point is $-\ln x/\ln5=\ln x/\ln(\dfrac{1}{5})$. The right-hand side is the logarithm to base $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
