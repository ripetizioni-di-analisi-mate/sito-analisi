What is the solution of the equation $3 e^{2x} = 12$?

- $x = \ln 4$
- $x = \ln 2$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2} \ln 12$
- $x = \ln 3$

## Explanation

Dividing by $3$ gives $e^{2x} = 4$. By the definition of the natural logarithm, $2x = \ln 4$.

By the power rule for logarithms, $\ln 4 = 2 \ln 2$, so $x = \ln 2$.
