A programmer wants to compare $8^{100}$ and $10^{90}$ using logarithms. Using $\log _{10}2\approx 0{,}3010$, rounded to four decimal places, which quantity is greater?

- $10^{90}$
- $8^{100}$
- They are equal.
- It depends on a choice of unit.

## Explanation

The first common logarithm is $100\log _{10}8=300\log _{10}2\approx 90{,}30$. The second is $90$. The logarithm is increasing and preserves their order.
