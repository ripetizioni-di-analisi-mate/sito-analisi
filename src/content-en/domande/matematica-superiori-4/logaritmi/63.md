Given that $\log_{10} 2\approx 0.3010$, which of the two numbers $8^{100}$ and $10^{90}$ is greater?

- $10^{90}$
- $8^{100}$
- The two numbers are equal.
- The data are not enough to decide.

## Explanation

Since $8=2^3$, the power rule for logarithms gives $\log_{10} 8^{100}=300\log_{10} 2\approx 90.30$, while the common logarithm of $10^{90}$ is $90$. The function $\log_{10}$ is strictly increasing, so the greater of the two numbers is the one with the greater logarithm. Therefore $8^{100}>10^{90}$. The difference between the two logarithms is about $0.30$, while rounding $\log_{10} 2$ to four decimal places, multiplied by $300$, shifts the first logarithm by less than $0.02$; hence the comparison does not depend on the rounding.
