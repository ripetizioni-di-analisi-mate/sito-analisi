What are the solutions of the equation $\log_3 \left( x^2 - 1 \right) = 1$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = \pm 2$
- $x = \pm \sqrt{2}$
- $x = \pm \sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, the equation is equivalent to $x^2 - 1 = 3^1 = 3$, so $x^2 = 4$ and $x = \pm 2$.

The domain condition is $x^2 - 1 > 0$. At both values the argument is $3$, which is positive.

Therefore the solutions are $x = 2$ and $x = -2$.
