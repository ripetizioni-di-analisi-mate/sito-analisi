What are the solutions of the equation $\log_2 x + \log_x 2 = \dfrac{5}{2}$?

- $x = 2$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 4$ and $x = \sqrt{2}$
- $x = 4$ and $x = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$

## Explanation

The conditions are $x > 0$ and $x \neq 1$, imposed by the base of the second logarithm. The change-of-base formula gives $\log_x 2 = \dfrac{1}{\log_2 x}$.

Set $t = \log_2 x$, with $t \neq 0$, to obtain $t + \dfrac{1}{t} = \dfrac{5}{2}$, that is, $2t^2 - 5t + 2 = 0$, with solutions $t = 2$ and $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

From $\log_2 x = 2$ we obtain $x = 4$, and from $\log_2 x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ we obtain $x = \sqrt{2}$. Both values satisfy the conditions.
