- $x = -3$ and $x = 3$
- $x = \sqrt{7}$
- $x = 3$
- $x = -3$

## Explanation

The domain conditions $x - 1 > 0$ and $x + 1 > 0$ give $x > 1$.

For $x > 1$ the product rule for logarithms applies, giving $3 = \log_2 8$. The equation becomes $\log_2 (x^2 - 1) = \log_2 8$, so $x^2 - 1 = 8$ and $x = \pm 3$.

The value $x = -3$ does not satisfy $x > 1$, so the equation has the unique solution $x = 3$.
