## Explanation

By the quotient rule for logarithms, $\log_5 50 - \log_5 2 = \log_5 \dfrac{50}{2} = \log_5 25 = 2$.

By the power rule for logarithms, $2 \log_5 \sqrt{5} = 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \log_5 5 = 1$ because $\sqrt{5} = 5^{\frac{1}{2}}$.

Therefore the expression equals $2 + 1 = 3$.
