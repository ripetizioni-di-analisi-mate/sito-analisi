The graphs of $y=\log _2 x$ and $y=x-1$ agree at inputs $1$ and $2$. Why is checking just these two points insufficient to establish equality of the functions?

- At $x=1$ the outputs differ.
- The base-two logarithm is decreasing.
- Their domains have no points in common.
- At $x=4$ the outputs are $2$ and $3$.

## Explanation

Equality on the common domain would require equal outputs at every input. This counterexample rules it out.
