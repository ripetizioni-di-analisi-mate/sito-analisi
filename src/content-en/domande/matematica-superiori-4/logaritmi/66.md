What are the solutions of the equation $\log_2 \left( x^2 - 2x \right) = 3$?

- $x = 4$
- $x = -2$
- $x = 1 \pm \sqrt{7}$
- $x = 4$ and $x = -2$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, the equation is equivalent to $x^2 - 2x = 2^3 = 8$, that is, $x^2 - 2x - 8 = 0$, whose solutions are $x = 4$ and $x = -2$.

The equation has the form $\log_a f(x) = c$, so every solution satisfies the domain condition $f(x) > 0$ because $f(x) = 8$.

Therefore both values are solutions. Indeed, at $x = -2$ we have $(-2)^2 - 2 \cdot (-2) = 8$.
