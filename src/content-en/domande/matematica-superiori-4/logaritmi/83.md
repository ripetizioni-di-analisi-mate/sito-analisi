What are the solutions of the inequality $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} x < 3$?

- $0 < x < \dfrac{1}{8}$
- $x > \dfrac{1}{8}$
- $x > 8$
- $0 < x < 8$

## Explanation

Write $3 = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} \dfrac{1}{8}$ because $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^3 = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

The base is less than $1$, so the direction reverses when comparing the arguments, giving $x > \dfrac{1}{8}$.

These values satisfy the domain condition $x > 0$, so the solutions are $x > \dfrac{1}{8}$.
