Two positive variables satisfy $\ln x<\ln y<0$. What follows from the order of the three quantities?

- $0<y<x<1$
- $0<x<y<1$
- $1<x<y$
- $x<y<0$

## Explanation

Comparing with $\ln1=0$ gives $y<1$. Strict monotonicity preserves the order of $x$ and $y$, and both are positive by assumption.
