What is the value of $\log_{\sqrt{2}} 8$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-6$
- $6$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, find the exponent $x$ such that $\left( \sqrt{2} \right)^x = 8$. Since $\sqrt{2} = 2^{\frac{1}{2}}$ and $8 = 2^3$, the equation becomes $2^{\frac{x}{2}} = 2^3$.

Two powers with the same base $2$ are equal only if their exponents are equal, so $\dfrac{x}{2} = 3$. Therefore $\log_{\sqrt{2}} 8 = 6$.
