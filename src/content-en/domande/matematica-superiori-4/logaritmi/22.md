Estimate $\log _{1{,}001}2$ without a calculator. Given $1{,}001^{100}<2$, what follows?

- The logarithm lies between $0$ and $1$.
- The logarithm exceeds $100$.
- The logarithm is negative.
- The logarithm is exactly $100$.

## Explanation

The base is just above one and its powers increase. A hundred factors still give less than two, so reaching two requires an exponent greater than one hundred.
