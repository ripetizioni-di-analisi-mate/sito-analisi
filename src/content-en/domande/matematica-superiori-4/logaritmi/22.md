Given that $1.001^{100}<2$, what can be said about $\log_{1.001} 2$?

- It lies between $0$ and $1$.
- It is greater than $100$.
- It is negative.
- It equals $100$.

## Explanation

The base $1.001$ is greater than $1$, so the function $1.001^{y}$ is strictly increasing. By definition, $\log_{1.001} 2$ is the exponent $y$ for which $1.001^{y}=2$. Since $1.001^{100}<2=1.001^{y}$ and the function is increasing, the exponent satisfies $y>100$. Therefore $\log_{1.001} 2$ is greater than $100$.
