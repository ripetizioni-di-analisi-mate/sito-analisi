Between which consecutive integers does $\log_2 50$ lie?

- $4$ and $5$
- $5$ and $6$
- $6$ and $7$
- $25$ and $26$

## Explanation

Compare the argument with powers of $2$. Since $2^5 = 32$ and $2^6 = 64$, we have $32 < 50 < 64$.

The function $\log_2 x$ is strictly increasing because its base exceeds $1$. Applying it to the inequality gives $\log_2 32 < \log_2 50 < \log_2 64$, that is, $5 < \log_2 50 < 6$.
