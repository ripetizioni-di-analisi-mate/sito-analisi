At $x=3$ a student writes $\ln(x+1)=\ln x+\ln1$. Which numerical check reveals the error?

- Both sides give $4$ after exponentiation.
- $\ln1=1$.
- $\ln3=0$.
- Exponentiating both sides compares $4$ and $3$.

## Explanation

The right-hand side is $\ln3$ because $\ln1=0$, while the left-hand side is $\ln4$. A logarithm turns a product into a sum. Here the argument is a sum.
