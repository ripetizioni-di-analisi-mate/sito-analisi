What is the value of $\log_2 40 - \log_2 5 + \log_2 \dfrac{1}{4}$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

By the quotient rule for logarithms, $\log_2 40 - \log_2 5 = \log_2 8 = 3$.

Since $\dfrac{1}{4} = 2^{-2}$, we have $\log_2 \dfrac{1}{4} = -2$.

Therefore the expression equals $3 - 2 = 1$.
