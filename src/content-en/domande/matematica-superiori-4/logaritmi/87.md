What are the solutions of the inequality $(x - 3) \ln x < 0$?

- $1 < x < 3$
- $0 < x < 1$
- $x > 3$
- $0 < x < 3$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x > 0$. Examine the signs of the two factors on this set.

The factor $\ln x$ is positive for $x > 1$ and negative for $0 < x < 1$. The factor $x - 3$ is positive for $x > 3$ and negative for $x < 3$.

The product is negative when the two factors have opposite signs. This happens only for $1 < x < 3$, where $\ln x > 0$ and $x - 3 < 0$. For $0 < x < 1$ both factors are negative and their product is positive.
