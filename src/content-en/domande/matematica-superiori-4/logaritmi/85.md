What are the solutions of the inequality $\left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{x - 1} > 2$?

- $x > 1 - \log_3 2$
- $x < 1 + \log_3 2$
- $x > 1 + \log_3 2$
- $x < 1 - \log_3 2$

## Explanation

Both sides are positive and the base $\dfrac{1}{3}$ is less than $1$, so taking logarithms to base $\dfrac{1}{3}$ reverses the direction:
$$
x - 1 < \log_{\frac{1}{3}} 2 .
$$
For the reciprocal base, $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} 2 = -\log_3 2$, so $x - 1 < -\log_3 2$. Therefore the solutions are $x < 1 - \log_3 2$.
