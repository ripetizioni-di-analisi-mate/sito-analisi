For $0<x<1$, which comparison between $\ln(x^2)$ and $\ln x$ is correct?

- $\ln(x^2)>\ln x$
- $\ln(x^2)=\ln x$
- The comparison depends on the unit of measurement.
- $\ln(x^2)<\ln x$

## Explanation

On the given interval $0<x^2<x$. Apply the increasing function $\ln$ to get the inequality. Equivalently, $2\ln x$ is below $\ln x$ because the latter is negative.
