For $p,q>0$, which expression measures the distance between $\ln p$ and $\ln q$ regardless of the common unit used for $p$ and $q$?

- $\ln(p+q)$
- $\ln(pq)$
- $|\ln p|+|\ln q|$
- $\left|\ln\dfrac pq\right|$

## Explanation

The distance is $|\ln p-\ln q|=|\ln(p/q)|$. Changing units multiplies both measurements by the same positive factor, which cancels in the ratio.
