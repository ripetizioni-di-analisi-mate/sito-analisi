What is the value of $\dfrac{\log_2 64}{\log_2 4}$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\log_2 60$
- $3$

## Explanation

Evaluate the two logarithms separately. Since $64 = 2^6$ and $4 = 2^2$, we have $\log_2 64 = 6$ and $\log_2 4 = 2$.

Therefore the quotient equals $\dfrac{6}{2} = 3$. The quotient of two logarithms differs from the logarithm of the quotient, which equals $\log_2 16 = 4$.
