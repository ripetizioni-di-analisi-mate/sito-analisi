- $x = 4$
- $x = 16$
- $x = 8$
- $x = 64$

## Explanation

The domain condition is $x > 0$. By the change-of-base formula,
$$
\log_4 x = \frac{\log_2 x}{\log_2 4} = \frac{1}{2} \log_2 x .
$$
The equation becomes $\dfrac{3}{2} \log_2 x = 6$, so $\log_2 x = 4$. Therefore $x = 2^4 = 16$.
