What is the value of the product $\log_2 3 \cdot \log_3 8$?

- $1$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Using the change-of-base formula with base $2$ gives
$$
\log_3 8 = \frac{\log_2 8}{\log_2 3} = \frac{3}{\log_2 3} .
$$
The factor $\log_2 3$ cancels, so the product equals $3$.
