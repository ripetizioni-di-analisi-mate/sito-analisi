For which $a>0$ does the graph of $f(x)=\ln(x+a)-\ln a$, defined for $x>-a$, pass through $(a,\ln2)$?

- Only $a=1$.
- Only $a=2$.
- No $a>0$.
- Every $a>0$.

## Explanation

Substituting $x=a$ and using the quotient rule gives $f(a)=\ln(2a/a)=\ln2$. Since $a>0$, all the logarithms are defined.
