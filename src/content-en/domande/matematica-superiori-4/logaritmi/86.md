For which values of $x$ does the inequality $3^x > -2$ hold?

- For no value of $x$
- For $x > \log_3 2$
- For $x > -\log_3 2$
- For every real $x$

## Explanation

The power $3^x$ is defined for every real $x$ and is always positive.

Every positive number is greater than $-2$, so the inequality $3^x > -2$ holds for every $x$.

The logarithm cannot be taken on the right because $-2$ is negative and $\log_3 (-2)$ is undefined.
