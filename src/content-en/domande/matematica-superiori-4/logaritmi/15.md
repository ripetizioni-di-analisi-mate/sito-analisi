A calculator displays $-2<\log_a 9<-1$. Which base is possible?

- $\dfrac15$
- $3$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac1{10}$

## Explanation

The negative sign requires $0<a<1$. For $a=\dfrac{1}{5}$, the powers with exponents $-1$ and $-2$ are $5$ and $25$, with $9$ strictly between them. The other bases fail at least one strict bound.
