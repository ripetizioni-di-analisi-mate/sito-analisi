What is the value of $\log_4 \dfrac{1}{8}$?

- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Find the exponent $x$ such that $4^x = \dfrac{1}{8}$. Since $4 = 2^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{8} = 2^{-3}$, the equation becomes $2^{2x} = 2^{-3}$.

Two powers with the same base are equal only if their exponents are equal, so $2x = -3$. Therefore $\log_4 \dfrac{1}{8} = -\dfrac{3}{2}$.
