For which of the following bases $a$ does $-2<\log_a 9<-1$ hold?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

The logarithm of $9>1$ is negative only if the base satisfies $0<a<1$, so the base $3$ is ruled out. For $0<a<1$ the function $a^y$ is decreasing, therefore the condition $-2<\log_a 9<-1$ is equivalent to $a^{-1}<9<a^{-2}$. With $a=\dfrac{1}{5}$ we have $a^{-1}=5$ and $a^{-2}=25$, and $9$ lies between the two values. With $a=\dfrac{1}{3}$ we have $a^{-2}=9$, so $\log_{\frac{1}{3}} 9=-2$ and the strict inequality does not hold. With $a=\dfrac{1}{10}$ we have $a^{-1}=10>9$, so $\log_{\frac{1}{10}} 9>-1$.
