The sound intensity level is $L = 10 \log_{10} \dfrac{I}{I_0}$, in decibels. If the level rises from $50$ dB to $80$ dB, by what factor is the intensity $I$ multiplied?

- By a factor of $30$
- By a factor of $1000$
- By a factor of $300$
- By a factor of $1.6$

## Explanation

By the quotient rule for logarithms, the difference between two levels is
$$
L_2 - L_1 = 10 \log_{10} \frac{I_2}{I_1} .
$$
This gives $10 \log_{10} \dfrac{I_2}{I_1} = 30$, so $\log_{10} \dfrac{I_2}{I_1} = 3$. Therefore $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1} = 10^3$ and the intensity is multiplied by $1000$.
