Two sound levels are rounded to the nearest whole decibel. If their displayed readings agree, what can we conclude about the underlying intensities?

- They must be identical because the logarithm is injective.
- Different intensities may give the same rounded reading.
- One must be double the other.
- Their ratio is necessarily ten.

## Explanation

Distinct levels such as $40{,}1$ and $40{,}4$ decibels can round to the same integer. Before rounding they correspond to different intensities because the logarithm is strictly increasing.
