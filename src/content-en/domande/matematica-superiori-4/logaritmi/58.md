What is the domain of the function $f(x) = \log_{x - 1} 4$?

- $(1, +\infty)$
- $(2, +\infty)$
- $(1, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$
- $(0, 1) \cup (1, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The variable appears in the base, which must be positive and different from $1$:
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 1 > 0 \\[4pt]
x - 1 \neq 1
\end{cases}
\iff
\begin{cases}
x > 1 \\[4pt]
x \neq 2 .
\end{cases}
$$
The argument $4$ is positive for every $x$. Therefore the domain is $(1, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$.
