What is the solution of the equation $2^x + 2^{x + 2} = 15$?

- $x = \log_2 \dfrac{11}{2}$
- $x = \log_2 3$
- $x = \log_2 \dfrac{15}{4}$
- $x = \log_3 2$

## Explanation

By the product rule for powers, $2^{x + 2} = 4 \cdot 2^x$.

Factoring out $2^x$ gives $5 \cdot 2^x = 15$, so $2^x = 3$.

By the definition of a logarithm, the solution is $x = \log_2 3$.
