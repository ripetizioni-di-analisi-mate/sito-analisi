Which real value is missing from the range of $f(x)=\dfrac{\ln x+2}{\ln x-1}$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $-2$
- $4$

## Explanation

With $t=\ln x$, we have $y=(t+2)/(t-1)$. Setting $y=1$ would give $t+2=t-1$, which is impossible. For $y\ne1$ we obtain $t=(y+2)/(y-1)$, which never equals $1$, so $x=e^t$ is admissible.
