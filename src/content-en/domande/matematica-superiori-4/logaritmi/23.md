Given $\log_{10} 3 \approx 0.4771$, how many digits does $3^{50}$ have?

- $24$
- $23$
- $25$
- $50$

## Explanation

By the power rule for logarithms, $\log_{10} 3^{50} = 50 \log_{10} 3 \approx 23.86$.

A natural number $N$ with $k$ digits satisfies $k - 1 \leq \log_{10} N < k$. Since $23 \leq 23.86 < 24$, we have $k = 24$.

Therefore $3^{50}$ has $24$ digits.
