What can be said about the solutions of the equation $\log_3 x = 4 - x$?

- It has the unique solution $x = 3$
- It has the solutions $x = 1$ and $x = 3$
- It has the unique solution $x = 1$
- It has no solutions

## Explanation

The solutions are the horizontal coordinates of the intersections of the graphs of $y = \log_3 x$ and $y = 4 - x$.

The first graph is strictly increasing and the second is strictly decreasing, so they meet at most once.

For $x = 3$ we have $\log_3 3 = 1$ and $4 - 3 = 1$, so $x = 3$ is a solution and therefore the only one. At $x = 1$, however, we have $\log_3 1 = 0$ and $4 - 1 = 3$.
