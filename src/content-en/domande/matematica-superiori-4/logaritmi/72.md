What are the solutions of the equation $\log_4 (x + 2) = \log_2 x$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 2$ and $x = -1$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 4$

## Explanation

The domain conditions $x + 2 > 0$ and $x > 0$ give $x > 0$.

The change-of-base formula gives $\log_4 (x + 2) = \dfrac{1}{2} \log_2 (x + 2)$, so the equation becomes $\log_2 (x + 2) = 2 \log_2 x = \log_2 x^2$. Equating the arguments gives $x^2 - x - 2 = 0$, with solutions $x = 2$ and $x = -1$.

The value $x = -1$ does not satisfy $x > 0$, so the only solution is $x = 2$.
