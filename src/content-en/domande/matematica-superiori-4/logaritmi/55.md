For which values of $x$ does the graph of $y = \log_2 (x - 3)$ lie above the $x$-axis?

- $x > 3$
- $3 < x < 4$
- $x > 1$
- $x > 4$

## Explanation

The graph lies above the $x$-axis where $\log_2 (x - 3) > 0 = \log_2 1$.

The base $2$ exceeds $1$, so the direction is preserved and we obtain $x - 3 > 1$, that is, $x > 4$. These values satisfy the domain condition $x > 3$.

The graph is the graph of $\log_2 x$ shifted $3$ units to the right, so it meets the $x$-axis at $(4, 0)$.
