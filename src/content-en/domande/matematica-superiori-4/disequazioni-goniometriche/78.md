A sign chart is used to study the sign of the product $\tan x\,(2\cos x - 1)$. What is the smallest common period of its two factors?

- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $4\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The factor $\tan x$ has period $\pi$, and the factor $2\cos x - 1$ has period $2\pi$. A common period must be an integer multiple of both periods, and the smallest is $2\pi$. A shift of $\pi$ is not a period of the second factor because $2\cos(x + \pi) - 1 = -2\cos x - 1$, which differs from $2\cos x - 1$. Therefore the sign chart is drawn on an interval of length $2\pi$, for example $[0, 2\pi)$.
