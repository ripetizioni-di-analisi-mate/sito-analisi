What are the solutions of the inequality $\sin 2x > \sin x$ in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$?

- $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ or $\dfrac{5\pi}{3} < x < 2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3} < x < \pi$ or $\dfrac{5\pi}{3} < x < 2\pi$
- $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ or $\pi < x < \dfrac{5\pi}{3}$
- $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

By the double-angle identity, the inequality becomes $2\sin x\cos x - \sin x > 0$, that is, $\sin x\,(2\cos x - 1) > 0$. In $[0, 2\pi)$, the factor $\sin x$ is positive for $0 < x < \pi$ and negative for $\pi < x < 2\pi$. The factor $2\cos x - 1$ is positive for $0 \leq x < \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and for $\dfrac{5\pi}{3} < x < 2\pi$ and negative for $\dfrac{\pi}{3} < x < \dfrac{5\pi}{3}$. The product is positive where the two factors have the same sign, that is, for $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and for $\pi < x < \dfrac{5\pi}{3}$.
