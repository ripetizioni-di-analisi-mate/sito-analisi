What is the largest $c$ such that every angle $x$ satisfies at least one of $|\sin x|\ge c$ and $|\cos x|\ge c$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\sqrt2$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$

## Explanation

The squares of sine and cosine add to one, so at least one square must be at least $\dfrac{1}{2}$. At $x=\pi/4$ both magnitudes equal $\sqrt2/2$, ruling out any larger threshold.
