On which of the following intervals is the product $\sin x\,(2\cos x + 1)$ positive at every point?

- $\left( \dfrac{2\pi}{3}, \pi \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{4\pi}{3}, 2\pi \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2}, \pi \right)$
- $\left( 0, \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \right)$

## Explanation

In the interval $\left( 0, \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \right)$, we have $\sin x > 0$ and $\cos x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$, so both factors are positive and the product is positive. For $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} < x < \pi$, the sine is positive and $2\cos x + 1$ is negative, so the product is negative. For $\dfrac{4\pi}{3} < x < 2\pi$, the sine is negative and $2\cos x + 1$ is positive, so the product is again negative. The interval $\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2}, \pi \right)$ contains points where the product is negative, such as $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$.
