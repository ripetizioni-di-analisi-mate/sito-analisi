In $\dfrac{\sin x-a}{\sin x-b}>0$ the parameters satisfy $a=b$. How does this simplify the solution?

- Take only angles with $\sin x=a$.
- There are no such angles.
- Require $\sin x>a$ even after cancelling.
- Take all angles with $\sin x\ne a$.

## Explanation

Numerator and denominator are identical, so the quotient equals one wherever it is defined. Only any zeros of the denominator must be removed.
