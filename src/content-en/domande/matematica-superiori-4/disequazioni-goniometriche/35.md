One multiplies $\tan x>1$ by $\cos x$ without knowing its sign. Which case requires reversing the inequality?

- $\cos x>0$
- $\cos x<0$
- $\sin x<0$ in every case.
- $\cos x=0$

## Explanation

The factor multiplying the two sides determines the direction. A negative factor reverses it, while a zero factor makes the original tangent undefined.
