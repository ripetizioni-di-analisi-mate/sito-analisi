An angle is chosen uniformly on $[0,2\pi)$. What is the probability that it satisfies $\sin x=1$ or $\sin x>1$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The second condition is impossible and the first identifies just one angle. A single point has zero length, so its probability under a uniform choice is also zero.
