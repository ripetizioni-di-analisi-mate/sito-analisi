Which of the following numbers belongs to the domain of the function $f(x) = \ln(\tan x)$?

- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

The function is defined when tangent is defined and positive. For $x = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$, we have $\tan x = \tan\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \sqrt{3} > 0$, so $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ belongs to the domain. For $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, the tangent equals $-1$, for $x = \pi$ it equals $0$, and at $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ it is undefined. The logarithm is undefined at all three points.
