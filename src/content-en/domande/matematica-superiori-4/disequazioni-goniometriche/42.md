A periodic solution is written $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}+2k\pi<x<\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2k\pi$. How wide is the excluded gap **between** two successive intervals?

- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

Each allowed interval has width $\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Subtracting this width from the period $2\pi$ gives the excluded gap.
