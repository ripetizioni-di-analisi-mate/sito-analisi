What is the largest real constant $C$ for which $\sin^2x+4\cos^2x\ge C|\sin x\cos x|$ holds for every angle $x$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $5$
- There is no upper bound.

## Explanation

The square $(|\sin x|-2|\cos x|)^2$ is nonnegative. Expanding it gives the inequality with $C=4$. At $x=\arctan 2$ the square is zero and $|\sin x\cos x|>0$, so every larger constant would make the inequality fail at that angle.
