A system requires $\sin x>\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\cos x>\dfrac{3}{4}$. Why has it no solution?

- Sine and cosine always have opposite signs.
- Each condition is impossible on its own.
- The sum of their squares would exceed one.
- In the first quadrant at least one coordinate vanishes.

## Explanation

Both squares would be greater than $\dfrac{9}{16}$. Their sum would exceed $\dfrac{18}{16}>1$, contrary to the Pythagorean identity.
