Does $\dfrac{1}{1-\cos x}>0$ exclude any angles, even though the denominator is never negative?

- Yes, the angles $x=2k\pi$.
- Yes, all the angles $x=k\pi$.
- Yes, the angles $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+k\pi$.
- No angles.

## Explanation

We have $1-\cos x\ge 0$. The fraction is positive wherever its denominator is strictly positive and undefined when $\cos x=1$.
