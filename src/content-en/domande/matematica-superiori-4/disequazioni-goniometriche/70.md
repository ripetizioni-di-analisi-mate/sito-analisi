Which of the following inequalities is homogeneous and quadratic in sine and cosine?

- $\sin^2 x + \sin x > 0$
- $\sin x + \cos x > 1$
- $\sin^2 x + \cos x > 0$
- $\sin^2 x + \sin x\cos x > 0$

## Explanation

An inequality is homogeneous of degree two when every term has total degree $2$ in $\sin x$ and $\cos x$. In $\sin^2 x + \sin x\cos x$, both terms have degree $2$, so the inequality is homogeneous with $a = 1$, $b = 1$, and $c = 0$. In $\sin^2 x + \sin x$ and in $\sin^2 x + \cos x$, there is a term of degree $1$. The inequality $\sin x + \cos x > 1$ is linear.
