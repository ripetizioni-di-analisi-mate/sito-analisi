A rectangle is inscribed in a circle of radius $1$, and its diagonal makes an angle $x$ with its base, where $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. For which values of $x$ is the area of the rectangle greater than or equal to $1$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \leq x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{12} \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{12}$
- No value of $x$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{12} \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The diagonal of the rectangle is a diameter of the circle, so its length is $2$. The base and height are the legs of the right triangle with the diagonal as its hypotenuse, so their lengths are $2\cos x$ and $2\sin x$. The area is $4\sin x\cos x = 2\sin 2x$, and the condition $2\sin 2x \geq 1$ is equivalent to $\sin 2x \geq \dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $0 < 2x < \pi$, we have $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \leq 2x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, that is, $\dfrac{\pi}{12} \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{12}$.
