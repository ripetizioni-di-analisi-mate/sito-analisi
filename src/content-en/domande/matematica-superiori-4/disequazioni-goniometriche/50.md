What are the solutions of the inequality $\cos 2x + 3\sin x - 2 \geq 0$?

- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{13\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

With $\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x$, the inequality becomes $-2\sin^2 x + 3\sin x - 1 \geq 0$, that is, $2\sin^2 x - 3\sin x + 1 \leq 0$. With $t = \sin x$, the trinomial factors as $(2t - 1)(t - 1)$, which is less than or equal to $0$ for $\dfrac{1}{2} \leq t \leq 1$. The condition $\sin x \leq 1$ holds for every $x$, so this leaves $\sin x \geq \dfrac{1}{2}$. The solutions are $\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$.
