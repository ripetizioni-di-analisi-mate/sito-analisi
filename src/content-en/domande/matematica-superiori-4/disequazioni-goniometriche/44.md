Someone proposes $\tan x>0$ as the only condition needed for $\sin x>0$. Which angle shows that it is insufficient?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

In the third quadrant sine and cosine are both negative, making tangent positive. At $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$ the proposed condition holds while sine is negative.
