A device places $x$ in one of the intervals $[0,\pi/4]$ and $[\pi/4,\pi/2]$. In which interval is $\cos x\geq\sin x$ guaranteed for **every** angle?

- Only in the first.
- Only in the second.
- In both.
- In neither.

## Explanation

In the first quadrant sine increases and cosine decreases. They agree at $\pi/4$, so cosine is at least as large before that point and smaller after it.
