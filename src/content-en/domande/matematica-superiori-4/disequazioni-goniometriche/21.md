In which of the two intervals $\left[0,\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right]$ and $\left[\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ does the inequality $\cos x\geq\sin x$ hold for **every** $x$?

- Only in the first.
- Only in the second.
- In both.
- In neither.

## Explanation

On the interval $\left[0,\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ sine is increasing and cosine is decreasing. The two functions take the same value $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ at $x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, so $\cos x\geq\sin x$ for $0\leq x\leq\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. For $\dfrac{\pi}{4}<x\leq\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, instead, $\cos x<\sin x$, so in the second interval the inequality holds only at the endpoint $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Therefore the inequality holds for every $x$ **only in the first** interval.
