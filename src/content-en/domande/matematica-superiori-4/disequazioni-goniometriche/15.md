The line $v=\dfrac{1}{2}$ is drawn on the unit circle. Which portion represents $\sin x>\dfrac{1}{2}$?

- The horizontal chord joining the two points.
- The disk above the line.
- Only the two intersection points.
- The open upper arc between the two intersection points.

## Explanation

Sine is the vertical coordinate of the point representing the angle. Take points on the circle above the line, excluding the boundary where equality holds.
