A point has coordinates $(\cos x,\sin x)$. In which half-plane must it lie to satisfy $\cos x+\sin x<0$?

- Above the line $v=-u$.
- Below the line $v=-u$.
- To the right of $u=0$.
- Between the axes in the first quadrant.

## Explanation

With $u=\cos x$ and $v=\sin x$ the condition becomes $v<-u$. The boundary is excluded because the inequality is strict.
