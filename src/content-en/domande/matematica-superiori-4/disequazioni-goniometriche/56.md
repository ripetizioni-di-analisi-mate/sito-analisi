A unit-circle point is required to satisfy $3\cos x+4\sin x>5$. What happens if $>$ is replaced with $\ge$?

- A whole semicircle becomes admissible.
- Exactly one circle point becomes admissible.
- There are four points.
- Every point remains excluded.

## Explanation

The sum reaches at most $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$, in the direction of the vector $(3,4)$. Including equality adds only the point attaining that maximum.
