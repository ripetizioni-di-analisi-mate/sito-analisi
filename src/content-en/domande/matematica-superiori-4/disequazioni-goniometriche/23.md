For every $x$ in the open interval $(0,\pi/2)$ we have $\tan x>0$. Is there a constant $m>0$ with $\tan x\geq m$ for every such $x$?

- Yes, choose $m=1$.
- Yes, choose $m=\dfrac{1}{2}$ because the interval of angles is bounded.
- No, because tangent values come arbitrarily close to zero.
- No, because tangent changes sign on the interval.

## Explanation

For positive $x$ approaching zero, tangent stays positive but tends to zero. Some values therefore fall below any positive proposed threshold.
