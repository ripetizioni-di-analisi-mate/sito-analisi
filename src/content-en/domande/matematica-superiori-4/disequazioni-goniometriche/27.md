Which set is the complement, in the reals, of the solutions of $\sin x<0$?

- The solutions of $\sin x>0$.
- Only the zeros of sine.
- The solutions of $\sin x\ge 0$.
- The solutions of $\cos x\ge 0$.

## Explanation

The complement contains every point that fails the strict inequality. It therefore includes both the points with positive sine and those with zero sine.
