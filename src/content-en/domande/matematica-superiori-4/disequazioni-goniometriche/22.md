A cabin has height $h(t)=10+8\sin t$ metres. What fraction of a full period does it spend at least $14$ metres high?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The threshold is equivalent to $\sin t\ge \dfrac{1}{2}$. The arc from $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ to $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$ has length $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. Dividing by $2\pi$ gives one third.
