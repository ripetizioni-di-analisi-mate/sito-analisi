What are the solutions of the inequality $2\sin x + 1 > 0$?

- $-\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi < x < \dfrac{7\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{6} + 2k\pi < x < \dfrac{11\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi < x < \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi < x < \dfrac{4\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

Isolating the sine gives $\sin x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $\arcsin\left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) = -\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, the line $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ intersects the unit circle at the points corresponding to $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\pi + \dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{7\pi}{6}$. The points above this line form the arc from $-\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ to $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$ through $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The solutions are therefore $-\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi < x < \dfrac{7\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$.
