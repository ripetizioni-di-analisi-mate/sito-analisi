When solving $\sin x\,\cos x<0$ over one turn, which symmetry takes the second quadrant to the fourth and preserves the sign of the product?

- Reverse the vertical coordinate only.
- Add $\pi$ to the angle.
- Reverse the horizontal coordinate only.
- Add $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ to the angle.

## Explanation

A half-turn reverses the signs of both coordinates. Their product therefore keeps its sign as the point moves to the opposite quadrant.
