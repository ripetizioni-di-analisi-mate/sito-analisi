On $[0,2\pi)$ we require $\cos x\ge 0$ **or** $\sin x\ge 0$. Which part is excluded?

- The open first quadrant.
- All axes.
- The second and fourth quadrants.
- The open third quadrant.

## Explanation

For both conditions to fail, sine and cosine must both be strictly negative. This happens only inside the third quadrant.
