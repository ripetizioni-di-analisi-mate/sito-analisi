A homogeneous inequality reduces to $(\tan x-1)^2>0$ where $\cos x\ne 0$. What happens on crossing a point with $\tan x=1$?

- The point is excluded and the square stays positive on both nearby sides.
- The sign changes from negative to positive.
- The sign changes from positive to negative.
- A vertical asymptote occurs.

## Explanation

The factor has multiplicity two. Tangent is continuous where it equals one, and the square is positive at all nearby points other than that zero.
