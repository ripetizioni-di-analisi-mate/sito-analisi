With $u=\cos x$ and $v=\sin x$, can $u+v>1$ hold when $u\le 0$?

- No, because $v\le 1$.
- Yes, at every point in the second quadrant.
- Yes, at $(-1,0)$.
- Yes, exactly when $v=0$.

## Explanation

If $u\le 0$, then $u+v\le v$. The vertical coordinate of a unit-circle point never exceeds one, so the strict inequality is impossible.
