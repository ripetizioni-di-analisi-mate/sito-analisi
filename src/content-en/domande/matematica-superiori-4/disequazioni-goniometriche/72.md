What are the solutions of the inequality $\sin x\cos x < 0$ in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < x < \pi$ or $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < x < 2\pi$
- $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ or $\pi < x < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < x < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\pi < x < 2\pi$

## Explanation

The product is negative when its two factors have opposite signs. In the second quadrant, sine is positive and cosine is negative. In the fourth quadrant, sine is negative and cosine is positive. In the first and third quadrants, the two factors have the same sign. Thus, in $[0, 2\pi)$ the solutions are $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < x < \pi$ or $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < x < 2\pi$, excluding the zeros of the factors.
