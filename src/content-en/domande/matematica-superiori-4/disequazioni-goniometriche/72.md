At which point does $\dfrac{\sin^2 x}{\sin x}$ have a hole, although it has the same sign as $\sin x$ wherever it is defined?

- $x=\pi$
- $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $x=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

Cancellation requires $\sin x\ne 0$. Among the given points only $\pi$ makes the denominator zero, so it is excluded even in a non-strict inequality.
