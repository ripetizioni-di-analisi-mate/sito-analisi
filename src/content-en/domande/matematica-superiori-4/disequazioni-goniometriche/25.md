An angle satisfies $x\in [\dfrac{\pi}{6},\dfrac{\pi}{3}]$. Which bound is guaranteed over the entire interval?

- $\sin x\ge \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $\sin x\ge \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\sin x\le \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\sin x<0$

## Explanation

The interval lies in the first quadrant, where sine is increasing. Its minimum there is attained at the left endpoint, $\sin (\dfrac{\pi}{6})=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
