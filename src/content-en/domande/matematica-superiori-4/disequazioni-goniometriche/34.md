An encoder records only the eight angles $x=\dfrac{k\pi}{4}$ with $k=0,\ldots,7$. How many readings satisfy $\tan x>0$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

Tangent is positive in the first and third quadrants, excluding the axes. Of the eight angles, only $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$ remain.
