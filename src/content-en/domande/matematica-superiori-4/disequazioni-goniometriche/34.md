Which of the following numbers is a solution of the inequality $\tan x \geq 1$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

For $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, we have $\tan x = \tan\dfrac{\pi}{4} = 1$ because tangent has period $\pi$, and $1 \geq 1$ is true. The number $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ does not belong to the domain of tangent and is therefore not a solution. For $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and for $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, the tangent equals $-1$. The solutions are $\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi \leq x < \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$ is the left endpoint of the interval for $k = 1$.
