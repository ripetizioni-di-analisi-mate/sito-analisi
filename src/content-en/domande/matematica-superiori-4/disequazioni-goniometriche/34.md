Among the eight angles $x=\dfrac{k\pi}{4}$, with $k=0,1,\ldots,7$, how many satisfy the inequality $\tan x>0$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The eight angles are $0$, $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, $\pi$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$. At $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ the tangent is not defined, while at $0$ and $\pi$ it equals $0$, so these four angles do not satisfy the inequality. The tangent is positive in the first and third quadrants and negative in the second and fourth, so among the remaining angles only $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$ satisfy it, where $\tan x=1$. The angles that satisfy the inequality are therefore **two**.
