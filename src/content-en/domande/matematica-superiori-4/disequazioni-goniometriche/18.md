What is the smallest positive period of the solution set of the inequality $\sin 3x > 0$?

- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $6\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

With $t = 3x$, the inequality $\sin t > 0$ has solutions $2k\pi < t < \pi + 2k\pi$, so $\dfrac{2k\pi}{3} < x < \dfrac{\pi}{3} + \dfrac{2k\pi}{3}$. The intervals repeat at increments of $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, the period of $\sin 3x$. Shifting by $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ takes the interval $\left( 0, \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)$ to the interval $\left( \dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \right)$, where $\sin 3x < 0$, so $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ is not a period of the solution set.
