Can the constraints $\sin x+\cos x>1$ and $\sin x-\cos x>1$ hold simultaneously?

- Yes, at $x=\pi/2$.
- No, adding them would give $\sin x>1$.
- Yes, at $x=\pi/4$.
- Yes, for all angles in the first quadrant.

## Explanation

If both inequalities held, their sum would be $2\sin x>2$. Sine cannot exceed one. At $x=\pi/2$ both constraints reach equality only.
