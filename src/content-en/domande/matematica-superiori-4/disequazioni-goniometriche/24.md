A student claims $\sin x\ge \sin a$ if and only if $x\ge a$ for arbitrary angles. With $a=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, which value of $x$ disproves the claim?

- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

For $x=\pi$ the comparison of inputs holds, while the comparison of their sines would require $0\ge 1$. Sine is not increasing over the entire real line.
