On the unit circle, why does $\{x:|\tan x|<1\}$ correspond to points closer to the horizontal axis than to the vertical axis?

- Both distances are $1$.
- Tangent is the sum of the two distances.
- The distances are $|\sin x|$ and $|\cos x|$, and their ratio is $|\tan x|$.
- Points on the vertical axis are always included.

## Explanation

The point has coordinates $(\cos x,\sin x)$. It is closer to the horizontal axis when $|\sin x|<|\cos x|$. The denominator is then positive, so division gives exactly $|\tan x|<1$.
