What are the solutions of the inequality $\sin^2 x + 2\sin x - 3 < 0$?

- Every real $x$
- Every real $x$ with $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$
- No value of $x$
- Every real $x$ with $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$

## Explanation

Setting $t = \sin x$ gives $t^2 + 2t - 3 < 0$, that is, $(t + 3)(t - 1) < 0$, which holds for $-3 < t < 1$. The condition $\sin x > -3$ holds for every $x$, since $\sin x \geq -1$. The condition $\sin x < 1$ fails only where $\sin x = 1$, that is, for $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$. Thus the inequality holds for every real $x$ with $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$.
