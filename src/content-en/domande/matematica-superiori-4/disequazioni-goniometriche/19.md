What are the solutions of the inequality $\sin x \geq 1$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$
- No value of $x$
- Every real $x$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$

## Explanation

The range of sine is $[-1, 1]$, so $\sin x > 1$ holds for no value of $x$. Therefore $\sin x \geq 1$ is equivalent to the equation $\sin x = 1$. The solutions of this equation are $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, that is, the angles corresponding to the point $(0, 1)$ on the unit circle. For $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, however, we have $\sin x = -1$, so the expression with period $\pi$ includes values that are not solutions.
