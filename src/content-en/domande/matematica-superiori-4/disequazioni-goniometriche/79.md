A program tests only $k\pi/3$ for $k=0,\ldots,5$ and finds no solution to $\sin x>0.9$. Can it conclude that the inequality has no solutions on $[0,2\pi)$?

- Yes, six angles represent all sine values.
- Yes, because sine is periodic.
- No, every sample satisfies the inequality.
- No, between samples lies $\pi/2$, where sine is one.

## Explanation

The largest sampled value is $\sqrt3/2$, below $0.9$. The maximum of the continuous function is missed and equals $1$. A discrete search can therefore miss an entire allowed arc.
