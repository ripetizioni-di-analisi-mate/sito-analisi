For which values of $t$ is it enough to draw the sign chart after substituting $t=\sin x$ in a polynomial inequality?

- All positive reals.
- $-1\le t\le 1$
- $0\le t\le 2\pi$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\le t\le \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The new variable is a vertical coordinate on the unit circle. Every value in $[-1,1]$ is attainable and no other value corresponds to an angle.
