For which of the following values of $m$ does the inequality $\sin x \geq m$ have no solutions?

- $m = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $m = 1$
- $m = -1$
- $m = -\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Sine takes every value in the interval $[-1, 1]$ and no value greater than $1$. For $m = \dfrac{3}{2}$, the inequality becomes $\sin x \geq \dfrac{3}{2}$, and since $\dfrac{3}{2} > 1$, it **has no solutions**. For $m = 1$, the inequality has solutions $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$. For $m = -1$ and for $m = -\dfrac{3}{2}$, the inequality holds for every real $x$, since $\sin x \geq -1$ for every $x$.
