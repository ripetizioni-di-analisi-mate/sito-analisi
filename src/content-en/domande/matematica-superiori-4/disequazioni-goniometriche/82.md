What condition on $a$ guarantees that $\sin x\ge a$ and $\sin x\le-a$ have at least one common solution?

- $a\ge 0$
- $a>1$
- $a<-1$ only
- $a\le 0$

## Explanation

For $a\le \sin x\le-a$ one needs $a\le-a$. If $a\le 0$, choosing $x=0$ gives a sine value within the required interval.
