Which of the following numbers is a solution of this system?
$$
\begin{cases}
\sin x < 0 \\[4pt]
\tan x < 1
\end{cases}
$$

- $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

For $x = \dfrac{7\pi}{4}$, we have $\sin x = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} < 0$ and $\tan x = -1 < 1$, so both inequalities are satisfied. For $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, the sine is negative, but $\tan x = 1$ is not less than $1$. For $x = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$, we have $\tan x = \sqrt{3} > 1$, and for $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ the sine is positive. Therefore $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ is the only one of the four numbers that belongs to the intersection of the solution sets.
