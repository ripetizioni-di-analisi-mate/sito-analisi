The graphs of $\sin x$ and $\cos x$ have the same height at $x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. For $x$ just to the right of that point in the first quadrant, which graph is higher?

- Cosine.
- Sine.
- The two curves still coincide.
- It depends on whether degrees or radians are used.

## Explanation

Throughout the first quadrant sine rises while cosine falls. Moving right from their intersection makes the first vertical coordinate larger.
