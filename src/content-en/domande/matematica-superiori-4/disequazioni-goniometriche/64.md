Let $F(x)=\sin^2 x+2\cos^2 x$. Why does $F(x)>0$ hold for every real input?

- $F(x)=\cos^2 x-\sin^2 x$.
- $F(x)=\sin (2x)$.
- $F(x)=2\sin x \cos x$.
- $F(x)=1+\cos^2x$.

## Explanation

Grouping $\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x$ gives one. The remaining term is a square, so the left side is at least one.
