An algorithm solves $P(\sin x)>0$ and finds that $P(t)>0$ only on $(-2,-1)\cup(1,3)$. Can any angle be accepted?

- No, neither interval intersects $[-1,1]$.
- Yes, all angles with sine $1$.
- Yes, all angles with sine $-1$.
- Yes, because the intervals contain infinitely many reals.

## Explanation

The substitution $t=\sin x$ yields only values between $-1$ and $1$ inclusive. The endpoints $-1$ and $1$ are excluded from the intervals where the polynomial is positive.
