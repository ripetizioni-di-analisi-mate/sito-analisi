For $0<x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, which relation between $\sin x$ and $\tan x$ holds throughout the interval?

- $\tan x<\sin x$
- $\tan x=\sin x$
- $\tan x>\sin x$
- $\tan x=-\sin x$

## Explanation

On this interval $\sin x>0$ and $0<\cos x<1$. Dividing a positive quantity by such a cosine increases it, so $\tan x=\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}>\sin x$.
