What are the solutions of the inequality $\cos x \leq \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$?

- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{7\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

Since $\arccos\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, the vertical line with horizontal coordinate $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ intersects the unit circle at the points corresponding to $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $2\pi - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{7\pi}{4}$. The points with horizontal coordinate less than or equal to $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ lie to the left of this line or on the line itself and form the arc from $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ to $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ through $\pi$. The solutions are therefore $\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{7\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, including the endpoints.
