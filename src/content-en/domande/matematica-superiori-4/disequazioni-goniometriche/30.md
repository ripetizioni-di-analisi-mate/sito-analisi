After setting $u=\cos x$, one obtains $u^2<4$. For which real inputs does the original inequality $\cos^2 x<4$ hold?

- Only $-2<x<2$.
- Only $-1<x<1$.
- The entire real line.
- No real number.

## Explanation

The constraint $-2<u<2$ contains the entire range of cosine. It therefore imposes no restriction on $x$.
