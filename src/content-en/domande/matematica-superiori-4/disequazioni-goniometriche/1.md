- $\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{6} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{11\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{3} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{10\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

Isolating the sine gives $\sin x \leq -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. The angles in $[0, 2\pi)$ for which $\sin x = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ are $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$, in the third and fourth quadrants, with reference angle $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. On the unit circle, the points with vertical coordinate less than or equal to $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ form the arc from $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ to $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$ through $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. The endpoints are included because the inequality allows equality, and the period is $2\pi$, the period of sine.
