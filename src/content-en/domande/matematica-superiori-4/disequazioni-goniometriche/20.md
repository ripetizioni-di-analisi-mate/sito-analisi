A student wants to solve $\sin x<\cos x$ by squaring both sides. In which open quadrant does replacing it by $\sin^2 x<\cos^2 x$ always preserve equivalence?

- The first.
- The second.
- The third.
- The fourth.

## Explanation

In the first quadrant sine and cosine are positive, and squaring is increasing on positive numbers. In the third both are negative and the order reverses. Where the signs differ, a comparison of magnitudes alone is insufficient.
