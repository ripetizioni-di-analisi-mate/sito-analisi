What are the solutions of the inequality $\cos x > -1$?

- Every real $x$ with $x \neq \pi + 2k\pi$
- Every real $x$
- Every real $x$ with $x \neq 2k\pi$
- Every real $x$ with $x \neq k\pi$

## Explanation

The range of cosine is $[-1, 1]$, and cosine equals $-1$ only at angles corresponding to the point $(-1, 0)$, that is, for $x = \pi + 2k\pi$. At all other points, $\cos x > -1$. Thus the inequality holds for every real $x$ with $x \neq \pi + 2k\pi$. At the points $x = 2k\pi$, the cosine equals $1$, which is greater than $-1$.
