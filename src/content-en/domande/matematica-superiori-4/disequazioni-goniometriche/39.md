What are the solutions of the inequality $\cos 3x \leq 0$ in the interval $[0, \pi]$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ or $\dfrac{5\pi}{6} \leq x \leq \pi$
- $0 \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ or $\dfrac{\pi}{2} \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ or $\dfrac{5\pi}{6} \leq x \leq \dfrac{7\pi}{6}$

## Explanation

Set $t = 3x$. The solutions of the inequality $\cos t \leq 0$ are $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi \leq t \leq \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, so dividing by $3$ gives $\dfrac{\pi}{6} + \dfrac{2k\pi}{3} \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + \dfrac{2k\pi}{3}$. Setting $k = 0$ gives the interval $\left[ \dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$. Setting $k = 1$ gives $\left[ \dfrac{5\pi}{6}, \dfrac{7\pi}{6} \right]$, whose intersection with $[0, \pi]$ is $\left[ \dfrac{5\pi}{6}, \pi \right]$. The other values of $k$ give intervals with no points in common with $[0, \pi]$.
