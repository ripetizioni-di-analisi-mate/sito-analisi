For how many hours each day does $\cos (\dfrac{\pi t}{12})<0$ hold when $t$ is measured in hours from midnight?

- $6$
- $12$
- $18$
- $24$

## Explanation

During one turn cosine is negative for half a period. The factor $\dfrac{\pi}{12}$ makes a full turn last $24$ hours, so the duration is half of that.
