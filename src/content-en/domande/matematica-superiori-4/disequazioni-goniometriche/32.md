The open interval $(-\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{\pi}{2})$ is one branch of tangent. How many solutions of $\tan x>100$ does it contain?

- None.
- One.
- Infinitely many.
- Two.

## Explanation

Tangent increases without bound as it approaches its right asymptote. The entire open interval from $\arctan 100$ to $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ satisfies the condition.
