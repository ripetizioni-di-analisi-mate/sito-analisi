On $[0,\pi]$, the condition is $\cos x\le a$ with $-1<a<1$. What comparison with $\arccos a$ follows?

- $x\ge \arccos a$
- $x\le \arccos a$
- $x\ge \arcsin a$
- $x\le-\arccos a$

## Explanation

Cosine is strictly decreasing on this interval. Values at most $a$ occur to the right of $\arccos a$, including the boundary input.
