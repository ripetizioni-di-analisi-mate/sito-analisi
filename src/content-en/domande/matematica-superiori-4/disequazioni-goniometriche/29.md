What are the solutions of the inequality $2\cos x + 1 \geq 0$?

- $-\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{4\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $-\dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

Isolating the cosine gives $\cos x \geq -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $\arccos\left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, the points on the circle with horizontal coordinate $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ correspond to the angles $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. The points with horizontal coordinate greater than or equal to $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ form the arc between these two angles through $(1, 0)$. The solutions are therefore $-\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$.
