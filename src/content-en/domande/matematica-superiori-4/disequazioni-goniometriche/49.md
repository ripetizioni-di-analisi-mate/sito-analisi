A periodic solution is written $-\pi/4+2k\pi\leq x\leq\pi/4+2k\pi$. If independent integers $k$ on the left and $h$ on the right are used by mistake, what set results as both vary?

- The original periodic set.
- Only the interval $[-\pi/4,\pi/4]$.
- The empty set.
- The entire real line.

## Explanation

Given any real number, choose $k$ small enough to put the left endpoint below it and $h$ large enough to put the right endpoint above it. In the correct expression one integer translates both endpoints together.
