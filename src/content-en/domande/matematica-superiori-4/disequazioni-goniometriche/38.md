A calculation gives $0<\pi-2x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Over what interval do these bounds place $x$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}<x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $0<x<\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}<x<\pi$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}<x<-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

From $\pi-2x>0$ we get $x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The second condition gives $2x>\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, hence $x>\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Intersect the two bounds.
