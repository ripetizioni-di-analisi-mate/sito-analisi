In $\sin (x+\varphi)>0$ the value of $\varphi$ is increased by a small positive amount $\delta$. Which way does each solution interval shift?

- Right by $\delta$.
- Left by $2\delta$.
- Left by $\delta$.
- Its length doubles.

## Explanation

To keep the phase fixed as $\varphi$ grows by $\delta$, $x$ must decrease by the same amount. Both endpoints shift without changing the length.
