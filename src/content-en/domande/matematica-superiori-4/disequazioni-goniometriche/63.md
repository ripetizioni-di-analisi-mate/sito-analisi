Lighting is adequate when $\cos x\geq0$. A control offers the two orientations $x$ and $x+\pi$. How many of them meet the constraint if $\cos x\ne0$?

- Always both.
- Neither.
- Exactly one.
- It depends on the magnitude of $\sin x$.

## Explanation

The two angles have opposite cosines. Since neither cosine is zero, one is positive and the other negative, giving exactly one acceptable orientation.
