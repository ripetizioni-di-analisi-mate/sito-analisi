An arithmetic progression has common difference $d$. Keep only the terms with indices $2,5,8,11,\ldots$. What is the common difference of the resulting sequence?

- $d+3$
- $3d$
- $d^3$
- $d/3$

## Explanation

There are three steps of the original sequence between consecutive retained terms. Each adds $d$, so the total increment is $3d$.
