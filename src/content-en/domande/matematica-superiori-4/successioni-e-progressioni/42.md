In an arithmetic progression, $a_3 = 11$ and $a_{10} = 39$. What is the first term?

- $7$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $4$

## Explanation

There are $7$ steps from the third to the tenth term, so $39 = 11 + 7d$ and the common difference is $d = 4$. The first term follows from $a_3 = a_1 + 2d$, that is, $11 = a_1 + 8$. Thus $a_1 = 3$.
