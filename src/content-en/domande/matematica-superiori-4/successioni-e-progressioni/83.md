A geometric series has terms $1,\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{4},\ldots$. After adding through the term $\dfrac{1}{8}$ inclusive, how much remains to reach the infinite sum?

- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The first omitted term is $\dfrac{1}{16}$. The tail has ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and sums to $(\dfrac{1}{16})/(1-\dfrac{1}{2})=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Using only its first term would omit all the following ones.
