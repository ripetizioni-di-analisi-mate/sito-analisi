What is the sum $1 + \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{9} + \dfrac{1}{27} + \dots$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The summands are the terms of the geometric progression with $a_1 = 1$ and $q = \dfrac{1}{3}$. Since $|q| < 1$, the sum is
$$
\frac{1}{1 - \frac{1}{3}} = \frac{1}{\frac{2}{3}} = \frac{3}{2} .
$$
The partial sums $1$, $\dfrac{4}{3}$, $\dfrac{13}{9}$, and $\dfrac{40}{27}$ approach $\dfrac{3}{2}$ while remaining below $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
