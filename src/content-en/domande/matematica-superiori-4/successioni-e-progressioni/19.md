An integer sequence satisfies $a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_n$. Two consecutive terms $a_n,a_{n+1}$ are odd. What are the parities of the next three terms, in order?

- Even, even, odd
- Odd, even, odd
- Even, odd, odd
- Even, odd, even

## Explanation

The sum of two odd integers is even. The following term adds odd and even and is odd. The third again adds even and odd and is odd, returning to a pair of consecutive odd terms.
