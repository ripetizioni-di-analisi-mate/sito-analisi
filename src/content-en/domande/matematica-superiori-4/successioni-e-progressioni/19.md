A sequence of integers satisfies $a_{n+2} = a_{n+1} + a_n$ for every $n \geq 1$. Two consecutive terms $a_k$ and $a_{k+1}$ are odd. What are, in order, the parities of the three terms $a_{k+2}$, $a_{k+3}$, $a_{k+4}$?

- Even, even, odd
- Odd, even, odd
- Even, odd, odd
- Even, odd, even

## Explanation

The term $a_{k+2} = a_{k+1} + a_k$ is the sum of two odd numbers, so it is even. The term $a_{k+3} = a_{k+2} + a_{k+1}$ is the sum of an even number and an odd number, so it is odd. The term $a_{k+4} = a_{k+3} + a_{k+2}$ is again the sum of an odd number and an even number, hence it is odd. The parities are therefore even, odd, odd. Since $a_{k+3}$ and $a_{k+4}$ are again two consecutive odd terms, the pattern of parities repeats every three terms.
