A sequence is defined recursively by
$$
\begin{cases}
a_1 = 2 \\[4pt]
a_2 = 1 \\[4pt]
a_{n+2} = a_{n+1} + a_n
\end{cases}
$$
What is the value of $a_7$?

- $11$
- $29$
- $18$
- $21$

## Explanation

Each term is the sum of the two preceding terms, as in the Fibonacci sequence but with different initial terms. This gives $a_3 = 1 + 2 = 3$, $a_4 = 3 + 1 = 4$, $a_5 = 4 + 3 = 7$, and $a_6 = 7 + 4 = 11$. Thus $a_7 = 11 + 7 = 18$.
