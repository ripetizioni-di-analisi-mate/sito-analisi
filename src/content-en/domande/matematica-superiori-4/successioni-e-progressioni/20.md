What is the general term of the sequence $3, 7, 11, 15, \ldots$ when the index starts at $1$?

- $a_n = 4n + 3$
- $a_n = 3n + 4$
- $a_n = 4n - 3$
- $a_n = 4n - 1$

## Explanation

Each term exceeds the preceding term by $4$, so the sequence is an arithmetic progression with $a_1 = 3$ and $d = 4$. The formula for its general term gives $a_n = 3 + 4(n - 1) = 4n - 1$. Indeed, setting $n = 1$ gives $3$ and setting $n = 2$ gives $7$.
