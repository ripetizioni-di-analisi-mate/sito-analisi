A sequence starts with $1,2,4$. Without an additional rule, what can be concluded about its fourth term?

- It must be $8$.
- It must be $7$.
- It must exceed $4$.
- The three data points do not determine it.

## Explanation

Doubling would give $8$. Adding the successive increments $1,2,3$ instead gives $7$. Both rules reproduce the three given terms.
