In a geometric progression of six positive terms, the product of the first and last terms is $10$. What is the product of all six terms?

- $1000000$
- $1000$
- $60$
- $100$

## Explanation

The formula for the product of the first $n$ terms gives $P_6^2 = (a_1 \cdot a_6)^6 = 10^6$. The terms are positive, so $P_6 = \sqrt{10^6} = 10^3 = 1000$. This can also be seen by pairing terms equidistant from the ends, because the three products $a_1 a_6$, $a_2 a_5$, and $a_3 a_4$ all equal $10$.
