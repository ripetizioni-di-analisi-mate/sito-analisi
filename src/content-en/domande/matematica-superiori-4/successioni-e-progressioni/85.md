Which values occur in the partial sums of $1-1+1-1+\cdots$?

- It is constantly $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- It tends to $+\infty$.
- It alternates between $1$ and $0$.
- It is constantly $0$.

## Explanation

After an even number of terms each pair cancels. After an odd number a single $1$ remains. The two subsequences have different values, so the usual infinite sum does not exist.
