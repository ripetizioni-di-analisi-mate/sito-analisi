What is the sum $8 - 4 + 2 - 1 + \dfrac{1}{2} - \dots$?

- $16$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

Each term is obtained by multiplying the preceding one by $-\dfrac{1}{2}$, so this is the sum of the geometric progression with $a_1 = 8$ and $q = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $|q| < 1$, the sum is
$$
\frac{8}{1 - \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right)} = \frac{8}{\frac{3}{2}} = \frac{16}{3} .
$$
The partial sums $8$, $4$, $6$, and $5$ lie alternately above and below $\dfrac{16}{3}$ and approach that value.
