A design repeatedly applies a transformation scaling every length by $\dfrac{2}{3}$. What is the common ratio of the volumes of the resulting similar solids?

- $\dfrac{8}{27}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Volume receives a factor $\dfrac{2}{3}$ from each of the three dimensions. The ratio of consecutive volumes is therefore $(\dfrac{2}{3})^3=\dfrac{8}{27}$.
