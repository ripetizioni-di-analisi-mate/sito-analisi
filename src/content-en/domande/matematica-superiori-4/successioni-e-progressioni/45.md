An arithmetic progression with $12$ terms satisfies $a_3 + a_{10} = 40$. What is the sum of the $12$ terms?

- $240$
- $480$
- $120$
- $200$

## Explanation

The terms $a_3$ and $a_{10}$ are equidistant from the ends because $3 + 10 = 12 + 1$. By the property of terms equidistant from the ends, $a_1 + a_{12} = a_3 + a_{10} = 40$. Thus $S_{12} = \dfrac{12 \cdot 40}{2} = 240$.
