Which of the following infinite sums has a finite value?

- $2 - 3 + \dfrac{9}{2} - \dfrac{27}{4} + \dots$
- $1 + 0.9 + 0.81 + 0.729 + \dots$
- $1 - 1 + 1 - 1 + \dots$
- $1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + \dots$

## Explanation

The four sums are sums of geometric progressions with common ratios $0.9$, $-\dfrac{3}{2}$, $-1$, and $2$. The formula $\dfrac{a_1}{1 - q}$ holds only for $|q| < 1$, and of the four ratios only $0.9$ satisfies this condition. Thus $1 + 0.9 + 0.81 + \dots = \dfrac{1}{1 - 0.9} = 10$. The partial sums of $1 - 1 + 1 - \dots$ alternate between $1$ and $0$, and those of the other two sums grow without bound in absolute value.
