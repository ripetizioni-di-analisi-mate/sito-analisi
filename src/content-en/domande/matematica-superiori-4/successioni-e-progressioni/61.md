A geometric progression has first term $5$ and common ratio $-\dfrac{1}{3}$. Which statement is true?

- The progression is strictly decreasing
- The terms alternate in sign and the progression is not monotone
- The progression is strictly increasing
- The terms are all positive and approach $0$

## Explanation

The common ratio is negative, so each term has the sign opposite to that of the preceding one. The first few terms are $5, -\dfrac{5}{3}, \dfrac{5}{9}, -\dfrac{5}{27}, \ldots$, and the difference $a_{n+1} - a_n$ changes sign at each step. Thus the progression is not monotone. The absolute values of the terms approach $0$ because $|q| < 1$.
