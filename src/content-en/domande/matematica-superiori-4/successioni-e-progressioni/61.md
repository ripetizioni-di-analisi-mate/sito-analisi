The recurrence $a_1=5$, $a_{n+1}=0\cdot a_n$ produces $5,0,0,\ldots$. Why can't the constant-ratio test be applied to every consecutive pair?

- The first ratio equals five.
- Starting with the second term, one would need to calculate $0/0$.
- Every ratio equals one.
- The sequence has no first term.

## Explanation

The multiplication rule defines every term. The first ratio is $0/5=0$, but the next would be $0/0$, which is undefined.
