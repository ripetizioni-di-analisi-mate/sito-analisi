Two notebooks give the same rule $a_{n+1}=2a_n+1$, but only the first gives $a_1$. Can you determine whether they describe the same sequence?

- Yes, the rule forces $a_1=1$.
- Yes, all such sequences agree from the second term onward.
- No, the rule allows different initial terms.
- No, because the rule allows no initial term.

## Explanation

For example, $a_1=0$ gives $a_2=1$, whereas $a_1=1$ gives $a_2=3$. The recurrence determines later terms once the initial value is specified.
