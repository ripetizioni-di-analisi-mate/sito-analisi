A sequence is defined recursively by
$$
\begin{cases}
a_1 = 2 \\[4pt]
a_{n+1} = 3a_n - 2
\end{cases}
$$
What is the value of $a_4$?

- $10$
- $82$
- $28$
- $54$

## Explanation

Apply the rule three times starting from $a_1 = 2$. This gives $a_2 = 3 \cdot 2 - 2 = 4$, then $a_3 = 3 \cdot 4 - 2 = 10$, and finally $a_4 = 3 \cdot 10 - 2 = 28$. Finding $a_4$ therefore requires all its preceding terms.
