An arithmetic progression has first term $50$ and common difference $-4$. What is the smallest index $n$ for which $a_n < 0$?

- $13$
- $14$
- $12$
- $15$

## Explanation

The general term is $a_n = 50 - 4(n - 1) = 54 - 4n$. The inequality $54 - 4n < 0$ is equivalent to $n > \dfrac{27}{2}$, so the smallest index is $n = 14$. Indeed, $a_{13} = 2$ and $a_{14} = -2$.
