Three consecutive values of an arithmetic progression are listed in an unknown order as $2,10,x$. If $2$ and $10$ are its extreme values, which number must be in the middle?

- $4$
- $8$
- $\sqrt{20}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The middle term is equally far from the two extremes. Their arithmetic mean is $(2+10)/2=6$. Both steps have magnitude four.
