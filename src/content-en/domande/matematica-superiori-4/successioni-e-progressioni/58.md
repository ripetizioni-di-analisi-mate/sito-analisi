In a geometric progression, $a_2 = 5$ and $a_4 = 45$. What values can the common ratio take?

- Only $q = 3$
- Only $q = 9$
- $q = 3$ or $q = -3$
- $q = \sqrt{40}$ or $q = -\sqrt{40}$

## Explanation

The formula $a_4 = a_2\,q^2$ gives $45 = 5\,q^2$, so $q^2 = 9$. The equation has two real solutions, $q = 3$ and $q = -3$, because the exponent is even. The two common ratios give two different progressions, $\dfrac{5}{3}, 5, 15, 45$ and $-\dfrac{5}{3}, 5, -15, 45$.
