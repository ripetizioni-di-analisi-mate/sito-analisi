## Explanation

The sum of the first $n$ terms is $S_n = \dfrac{n\,\big( 2 \cdot 2 + (n - 1) \cdot 3 \big)}{2} = \dfrac{n\,(3n + 1)}{2}$. Setting $S_n = 155$ gives the equation $3n^2 + n - 310 = 0$. The discriminant is $1 + 3720 = 3721 = 61^2$, so the solutions are $n = \dfrac{-1 \pm 61}{6}$, namely $n = 10$ and $n = -\dfrac{31}{3}$. The number of terms is a natural number, so $n = 10$.
