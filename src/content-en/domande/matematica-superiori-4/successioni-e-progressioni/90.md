Which statement describes the powers $(-1.2)^n$ for large $n$?

- They approach $0$
- They are positive and grow without bound
- They alternate in sign and their absolute values grow without bound
- They stay between $-1$ and $1$

## Explanation

The base is negative, so even powers are positive and odd powers are negative. The absolute value is $|-1.2|^n = 1.2^n$. Since $1.2 > 1$, these powers exceed any prescribed number for sufficiently large $n$. Thus the powers alternate in sign and their absolute values grow without bound.
