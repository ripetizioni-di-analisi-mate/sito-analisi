For a sequence $a_n$, the even-indexed terms approach $1$ and the odd-indexed terms approach $2$. What follows for the entire sequence?

- It approaches the average of the two values.
- It approaches the limit of the even-indexed terms.
- It cannot approach a single limit.
- Its terms exceed every upper bound.

## Explanation

If the entire sequence approached one value, both subsequences would approach it as well. Here they approach two different values, $1$ and $2$.
