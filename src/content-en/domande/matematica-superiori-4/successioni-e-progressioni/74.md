An investment earns compound interest at an annual rate of $10\%$. After how many years does the accumulated amount first reach at least twice the initial principal?

- $7$ years
- $10$ years
- $9$ years
- $8$ years

## Explanation

We require $C \cdot 1.1^n \geq 2C$, that is, after dividing by $C > 0$, $1.1^n \geq 2$. The natural logarithm is increasing and $\ln 1.1 > 0$, so the inequality is equivalent to $n \geq \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 1.1} \approx 7.27$. The number of years is an integer, so the investment doubles after $8$ years. Indeed, $1.1^7 \approx 1.95$ and $1.1^8 \approx 2.14$.
