Two successive years yield $+25\%$ and $-20\%$. What constant annual return produces the same final value after two years?

- $2.5\%$
- $5\%$
- $-2.5\%$
- $0\%$

## Explanation

The overall factor is $1.25\cdot0.80=1$. The positive constant factor must have square one, so it is one, giving a zero equivalent annual return.
