Which set is the graph of the first three terms of the sequence $a_n = 2n - 3$?

- The isolated points $(0, -3)$, $(1, -1)$, and $(2, 1)$
- The isolated points $(1, -1)$, $(2, 1)$, and $(3, 3)$
- The isolated points $(-1, 1)$, $(1, 2)$, and $(3, 3)$
- The segment of the line $y = 2x - 3$ with $1 \leq x \leq 3$

## Explanation

The graph of a sequence consists of points $(n, a_n)$, with the index as the horizontal coordinate and the term as the vertical coordinate. The first three terms are $a_1 = -1$, $a_2 = 1$, and $a_3 = 3$. The indices are natural numbers, so the graph consists of three isolated points. The points between them on the line segment do not belong to the graph.
