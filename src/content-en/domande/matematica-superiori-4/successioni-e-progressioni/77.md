The same principal is invested at the same annual rate, once at simple interest and once at compound interest. Which statement holds for the two accumulated amounts after $1$ year?

- They are equal
- The accumulated amount with compound interest is larger
- The accumulated amount with simple interest is larger
- The comparison depends on the principal

## Explanation

With simple interest, the accumulated amount after $n$ years is $C\,(1 + n\,i)$. With compound interest it is $C\,(1 + i)^n$. For $n = 1$, both formulas give $C\,(1 + i)$, so the two accumulated amounts are equal. A difference appears in the second year because the first year's interest itself earns interest only under compound interest.
