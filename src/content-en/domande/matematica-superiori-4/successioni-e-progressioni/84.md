Which fraction equals the repeating decimal $0.\overline{45}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{5}{11}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{111}$

## Explanation

The number can be written as $\dfrac{45}{100} + \dfrac{45}{10000} + \dots$, that is, as the sum of the geometric progression with $a_1 = \dfrac{45}{100}$ and $q = \dfrac{1}{100}$. Since $|q| < 1$,
$$
0.\overline{45} = \frac{\frac{45}{100}}{1 - \frac{1}{100}} = \frac{45}{99} = \frac{5}{11} .
$$
The fraction $\dfrac{9}{20}$, however, corresponds to the terminating decimal $0.45$.
