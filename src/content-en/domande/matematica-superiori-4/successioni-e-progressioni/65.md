What is the sum $64 + 32 + 16 + \dots + 1$?

- $128$
- $63$
- $126$
- $127$

## Explanation

The summands form a geometric progression with $a_1 = 64$ and $q = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and $64 \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{n-1} = 1$ gives $n = 7$. By the sum formula,
$$
S_7 = 64 \cdot \frac{1 - \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^7}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 64 \cdot \frac{127}{128} \cdot 2 = 127 .
$$
The same result is obtained by reading the sum in reverse order as $1 + 2 + 4 + \dots + 64 = 2^7 - 1$.
