Starting with the finite sum $S=1+q+\cdots+q^7$, which product gives exactly $1-q^8$ for every real $q$ without division?

- $(1+q)S$
- $qS$
- $(q-1)S$
- $(1-q)S$

## Explanation

In $S-qS$, the terms of degrees one through seven occur with opposite signs and cancel. This leaves $1$ and $-q^8$, even when $q=1$.
