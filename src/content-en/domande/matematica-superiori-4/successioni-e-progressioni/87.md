A spreadsheet rounds each term to the nearest integer **before** computing the next. Starting from $4$, it multiplies by $0{,}8$ each time. What is the first value that stays unchanged from one step to the next?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The successive values are $4$, then $3$ from $3{,}2$, then $2$ from $2{,}4$. Starting from $2$ gives $1{,}6$, which rounds back to $2$.
