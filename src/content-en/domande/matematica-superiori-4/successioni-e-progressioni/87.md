What is the smallest natural number $n$ for which $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^n < 0.001$?

- $9$
- $11$
- $10$
- $3$

## Explanation

Taking reciprocals of these positive numbers gives the equivalent inequality $2^n > 1000$. Since $2^9 = 512$ and $2^{10} = 1024$, the smallest $n$ is $10$. This agrees with the fact that the powers $q^n$ with $|q| < 1$ approach $0$ and shows how large $n$ must be for $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^n$ to be less than one thousandth.
