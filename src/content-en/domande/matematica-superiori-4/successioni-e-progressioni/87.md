The sequence $(a_n)$ is defined by $a_1=4$ and, for every $n\ge 1$, $a_{n+1}$ is the integer nearest to $0.8\,a_n$. What is the value of the first term equal to the next term?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The terms are computed one after another. Since $0.8\cdot 4=3.2$, we have $a_2=3$; since $0.8\cdot 3=2.4$, we have $a_3=2$. Since $0.8\cdot 2=1.6$ and the integer nearest to $1.6$ is $2$, we have $a_4=2=a_3$. Therefore the first term equal to the next one is $a_3=2$, and from that term on the sequence is constant.
