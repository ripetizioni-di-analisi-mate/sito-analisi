Four positive numbers are inserted between $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $81$ so that all six numbers form a geometric progression. What is its common ratio?

- $\sqrt[4]{243}$
- $3$
- $\sqrt[5]{81}$
- $\sqrt[6]{243}$

## Explanation

With four numbers inserted, $81$ becomes the sixth term of the progression with first term $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The formula for the general term gives $81 = \dfrac{1}{3}\,q^5$, so $q^5 = 243$. Since $243 = 3^5$, the common ratio is $q = 3$, and the inserted numbers are $1, 3, 9, 27$.
