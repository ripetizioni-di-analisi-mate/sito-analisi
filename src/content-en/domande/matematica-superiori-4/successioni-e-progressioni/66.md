What is the product of the first five terms of the geometric progression with first term $2$ and common ratio $3$?

- $324^5$
- $6^{10}$
- $18^5$
- $2^5 \cdot 3^{15}$

## Explanation

The fifth term is $a_5 = 2 \cdot 3^4 = 162$, so $a_1 \cdot a_5 = 324$. The terms are positive, so the product formula gives $P_5 = \sqrt{324^5} = \left( \sqrt{324} \right)^5 = 18^5$. Direct multiplication gives the same result: $2 \cdot 6 \cdot 18 \cdot 54 \cdot 162 = 2^5 \cdot 3^{10}$ equals $18^5$.
