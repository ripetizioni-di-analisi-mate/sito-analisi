A battery retains four fifths of its charge each cycle. A device works only if at least $50$ units remain. What is the **minimum initial** charge that will power it after two cycles, allowing real values?

- $72$
- $\dfrac{125}{2}$
- $\dfrac{625}{8}$
- $32$

## Explanation

If the initial charge is $C$, the charge after two cycles is $16C/25$ units. The threshold requires $16C/25\ge 50$, and the minimum is $C=50\cdot 25/16=625/8$.
