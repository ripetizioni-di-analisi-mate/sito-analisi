A battery keeps, in each cycle of use, four fifths of the charge it had at the start of the cycle. What is the minimum initial charge such that at least $50$ units remain after two cycles?

- $\dfrac{3125}{32}$
- $\dfrac{125}{2}$
- $\dfrac{625}{8}$
- $32$

## Explanation

The charges at the end of each cycle form a geometric progression with ratio $\dfrac{4}{5}$, so starting from an initial charge $C$ the charge after two cycles is $C\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^2=\dfrac{16}{25}C$. The required condition is $\dfrac{16}{25}C\ge 50$. Multiplying by $\dfrac{25}{16}$ gives $C\ge\dfrac{1250}{16}=\dfrac{625}{8}$. Therefore the minimum initial charge is $\dfrac{625}{8}=78.125$ units.
