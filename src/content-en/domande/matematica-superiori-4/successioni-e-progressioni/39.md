Which of the following numbers divides $7^n - 1$ for every $n \geq 1$?

- $6$
- $4$
- $12$
- $7$

## Explanation

For $n = 1$, the number equals $6$, so a common divisor of all values must divide $6$, ruling out $4$, $12$, and $7$. We prove by induction that $6$ divides $7^n - 1$. The base case is $7 - 1 = 6$. For the inductive step, write $7^{n+1} - 1 = 7\left( 7^n - 1 \right) + 6$. If $7^n - 1$ is a multiple of $6$, so is the right-hand side. Thus $6$ divides $7^{n+1} - 1$.
