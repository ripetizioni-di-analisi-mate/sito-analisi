The identity $1+2+\cdots+n=n(n+1)/2$ holds for $n\geq1$. For $1\leq m<n$, which sum remains after subtracting the formula for $m$ from that for $n$?

- $(m+1)+(m+2)+\cdots+n$
- $m+(m+1)+\cdots+n$
- $1+2+\cdots+(n-m)$
- $1+2+\cdots+m$

## Explanation

The terms from one through $m$ appear in both sums and cancel. The first remaining term therefore has index $m+1$, and the last has index $n$.
