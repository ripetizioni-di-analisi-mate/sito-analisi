Which sequence is both an arithmetic and a geometric progression with all terms positive?

- Any increasing sequence.
- Any progression with common ratio $2$.
- Only the sequence of positive integers.
- A positive constant sequence.

## Explanation

Write the first three terms as $a,aq,aq^2$. Equal differences give $a(q-1)=aq(q-1)$ and hence $a(q-1)^2=0$. Since $a>0$, we obtain $q=1$.
