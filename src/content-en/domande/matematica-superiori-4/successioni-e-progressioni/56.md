A geometric progression has first term $2$ and common ratio $3$. What is the sixth term?

- $1458$
- $162$
- $7776$
- $486$

## Explanation

The formula for the general term $a_n = a_1\,q^{n-1}$ gives $a_6 = 2 \cdot 3^5$. Since $3^5 = 243$, the sixth term equals $486$. The exponent is $5$, the number of multiplications by $3$ from the first term to the sixth.
