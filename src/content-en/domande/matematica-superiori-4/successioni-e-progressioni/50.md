In an arithmetic progression, the sum of the first $n$ terms is $S_n = n^2 + 4n$ for every $n \geq 1$. What is $a_{10}$?

- $140$
- $23$
- $14$
- $24$

## Explanation

The term $a_{10}$ is the difference between the sum of the first ten terms and the sum of the first nine. We have $S_{10} = 100 + 40 = 140$ and $S_9 = 81 + 36 = 117$, so $a_{10} = 140 - 117 = 23$.
