How many terms of the geometric progression $5, 15, 45, \ldots$ must be added, starting with the first, to obtain $1820$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $364$
- $6$

## Explanation

The sum formula gives $S_n = 5 \cdot \dfrac{1 - 3^n}{1 - 3} = \dfrac{5\left( 3^n - 1 \right)}{2}$. Setting $\dfrac{5\left( 3^n - 1 \right)}{2} = 1820$ gives $3^n - 1 = 728$ and $3^n = 729$. Since $729 = 3^6$, the required number of terms is $6$.
