A table gives the partial sums $S_0=0,S_1=3,S_2=9,S_3=21$. Which check determines whether the three observed terms follow a geometric progression?

- Check that consecutive nonzero partial sums have the same ratio.
- Use the differences between the table indices as the terms.
- Check only whether the partial sums are positive.
- Compute the differences $3,6,12$ and compare their successive ratios.

## Explanation

The terms are $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$. The data give $3,6,12$, whose successive ratios both equal $2$. This verifies the three observed terms.
