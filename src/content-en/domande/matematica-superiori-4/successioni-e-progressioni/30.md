Which number is an upper bound of $a_n=1-1/n$ for $n\geq1$ but is not a term of the sequence?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Since $1/n$ is always positive, every term is strictly below one. The values $0$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$ are reached and then exceeded by later terms.
