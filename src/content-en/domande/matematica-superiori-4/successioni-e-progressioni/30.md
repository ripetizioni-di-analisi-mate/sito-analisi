What is the minimum of the sequence $a_n = 5 - \dfrac{2}{n}$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The fraction $\dfrac{2}{n}$ decreases as $n$ increases, so $a_n$ increases and the sequence is strictly increasing. Its minimum is therefore its first term $a_1 = 5 - 2 = 3$. The number $5$ is greater than every term and is never attained because $\dfrac{2}{n} > 0$.
