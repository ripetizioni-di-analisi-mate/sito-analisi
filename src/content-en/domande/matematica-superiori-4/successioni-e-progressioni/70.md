In a geometric progression with first term $96$ and common ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$, what is the position of the term equal to $3$?

- Sixth
- Fifth
- Thirty-second
- Seventh

## Explanation

The formula for the general term gives $a_n = 96 \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{n-1}$. Setting $a_n = 3$ gives $2^{n-1} = \dfrac{96}{3} = 32 = 2^5$. Thus $n - 1 = 5$, and the term equal to $3$ is the sixth. Indeed, the terms are $96, 48, 24, 12, 6, 3$.
