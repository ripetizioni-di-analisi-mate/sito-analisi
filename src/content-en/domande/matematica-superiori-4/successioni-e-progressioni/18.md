The recurrence $a_1=2$, $a_{n+1}=a_n/(a_n+1)$ involves a fraction. What progression results after setting $b_n=1/a_n$?

- Geometric with common ratio $2$.
- Constant and equal to $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- Arithmetic with common difference $-1$.
- Arithmetic with common difference $1$.

## Explanation

All terms are positive by induction, so reciprocals are valid. Then $b_{n+1}=(a_n+1)/a_n=1+b_n$, giving a constant increment.
