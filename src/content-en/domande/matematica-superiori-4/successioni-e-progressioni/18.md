A sequence is defined recursively by
$$
\begin{cases}
a_1 = 1 \\[4pt]
a_{n+1} = \dfrac{a_n}{1 + a_n}
\end{cases}
$$
What is the value of $a_4$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

From $a_1 = 1$ we obtain $a_2 = \dfrac{1}{1 + 1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Then $a_3 = \dfrac{\frac{1}{2}}{1 + \frac{1}{2}} = \dfrac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and, in the same way, $a_4 = \dfrac{\frac{1}{3}}{\frac{4}{3}} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. Thus the terms are the reciprocals of the natural numbers, $a_n = \dfrac{1}{n}$.
