What is the sum $3 + 6 + 12 + \dots + 384$?

- $765$
- $768$
- $381$
- $1533$

## Explanation

The summands are terms of a geometric progression with $a_1 = 3$ and $q = 2$. From $384 = 3 \cdot 2^{n-1}$ we obtain $2^{n-1} = 128 = 2^7$, so there are $n = 8$ summands. The sum formula gives $S_8 = 3 \cdot \dfrac{1 - 2^8}{1 - 2} = 3 \cdot 255 = 765$.
