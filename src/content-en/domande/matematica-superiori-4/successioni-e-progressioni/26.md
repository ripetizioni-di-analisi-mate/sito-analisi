What is the minimum of the sequence $a_n = n^2 - 8n$?

- $-15$
- $-12$
- $-16$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The difference between consecutive terms is $a_{n+1} - a_n = (n + 1)^2 - 8(n + 1) - n^2 + 8n = 2n - 7$. It is negative for $n \leq 3$ and positive for $n \geq 4$, so the sequence decreases up to $a_4$ and then increases. Its minimum is therefore $a_4 = 16 - 32 = -16$.
