The recurrence $a_{n+1}=3a_n-4$ has a fixed point $2$. Which transformation produces a geometric progression?

- $b_n=a_n+2$
- $b_n=3a_n$
- $b_n=a_n+4$
- $b_n=a_n-2$

## Explanation

Subtract the fixed point to obtain $a_{n+1}-2=3a_n-6=3(a_n-2)$. The signed differences from $2$ therefore follow a progression with ratio three.
