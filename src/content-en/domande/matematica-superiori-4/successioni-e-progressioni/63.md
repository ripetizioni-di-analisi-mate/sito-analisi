For what value of $x$ are $x - 2$, $x + 1$, and $x + 7$, in that order, consecutive terms of a geometric progression?

- $x = -5$
- $x = 3$
- $x = 1$
- $x = 5$

## Explanation

The square of the middle term must equal the product of the other two, so $(x + 1)^2 = (x - 2)(x + 7)$. Expanding gives $x^2 + 2x + 1 = x^2 + 5x - 14$, that is, $3x = 15$. Therefore $x = 5$, and the terms are $3, 6, 12$, with common ratio $2$.
