A geometric progression has first term $-3$ and common ratio $2$. Which statement is true?

- It is strictly decreasing
- It is strictly increasing
- It is not monotone
- It is constant

## Explanation

The terms are $-3, -6, -12, -24, \ldots$, all negative. The difference between two consecutive terms is $a_{n+1} - a_n = a_n\,(q - 1) = a_n$, which is negative because $a_n < 0$. Thus the progression is **strictly decreasing**, while the absolute values of its terms increase.
