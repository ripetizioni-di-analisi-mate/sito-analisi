If $a_n=3(-2)^{n-1}$, which statement describes the odd-index subsequence $a_1,a_3,a_5,\ldots$?

- It is positive and geometric with common ratio $4$.
- It alternates with common ratio $-2$.
- It is constant.
- It is negative and geometric with common ratio $4$.

## Explanation

Moving from one odd index to the next applies the factor $-2$ twice, producing $4$. The initial term is positive, so every selected term is positive.
