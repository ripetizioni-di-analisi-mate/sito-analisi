A principal of $1500$ euros is deposited at an annual simple interest rate of $2\%$. After how many years does the accumulated amount equal $1800$ euros?

- $9$ years
- $11$ years
- $10$ years
- $20$ years

## Explanation

With simple interest, the annual interest is always $1500 \cdot 0.02 = 30$ euros. The total interest required is $1800 - 1500 = 300$ euros. Thus it takes $\dfrac{300}{30} = 10$ years, and indeed $1500 \cdot (1 + 10 \cdot 0.02) = 1800$.
