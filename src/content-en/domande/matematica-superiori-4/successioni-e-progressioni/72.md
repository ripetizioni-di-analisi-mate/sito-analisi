A loan starts at balance $D_0$ with annual rate $r$. Interest is added at year-end and a fixed payment $R$ is made immediately afterwards. Which recurrence gives the balance after payment?

- $D_{n+1}=(1+r)(D_n-R)$
- $D_{n+1}=D_n+r-R$
- $D_{n+1}=(1+r)D_n-R$
- $D_{n+1}=rD_n+R$

## Explanation

Interest $rD_n$ accrues first, increasing the balance to $(1+r)D_n$. The payment is then subtracted, so it does not earn interest during the year that has just ended.
