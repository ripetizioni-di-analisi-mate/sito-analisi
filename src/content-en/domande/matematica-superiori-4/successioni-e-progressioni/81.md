At every step a figure is replaced by three disjoint copies, each with one quarter of its parent's area. What fraction of the original area remains after $n$ steps?

- $(\dfrac{1}{4})^n$
- $3^n$
- $(\dfrac{1}{12})^n$
- $(\dfrac{3}{4})^n$

## Explanation

The number of copies triples while their individual areas are quartered. The total area in each generation is therefore $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of the previous one, giving $(\dfrac{3}{4})^n$ after $n$ steps.
