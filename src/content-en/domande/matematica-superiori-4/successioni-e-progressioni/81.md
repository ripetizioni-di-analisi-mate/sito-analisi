Consider infinitely many circles. The first has radius $4$, and each subsequent circle has half the radius of the preceding circle. What is the sum of all their circumferences?

- $8\pi$
- $32\pi$
- $\dfrac{64\pi}{3}$
- $16\pi$

## Explanation

The first circle has circumference $2\pi \cdot 4 = 8\pi$. Circumference is proportional to radius, so each circumference is half the preceding one and the circumferences form a geometric progression with $a_1 = 8\pi$ and $q = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $|q| < 1$, the sum is $\dfrac{8\pi}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 16\pi$.
