At the end of each year for $3$ years, $500$ euros are deposited in an account earning compound interest at an annual rate of $10\%$. What is the account balance immediately after the third deposit?

- $1655$ euros
- $1500$ euros
- $1820.50$ euros
- $1650$ euros

## Explanation

The third deposit is worth $500$ euros. The second has been in the account for one year and is worth $500 \cdot 1.1 = 550$ euros, and the first has been there for two years and is worth $500 \cdot 1.1^2 = 605$ euros. The account balance is therefore the sum of the geometric progression with first term $500$ and common ratio $1.1$, namely
$$
V = 500 \cdot \frac{1.1^3 - 1}{0.1} = 1655 \text{ euros} .
$$
