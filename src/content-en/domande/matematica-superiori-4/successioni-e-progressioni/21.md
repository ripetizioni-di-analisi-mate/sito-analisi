A sequence changes sign at each step, starts with $a_1=\dfrac{1}{2}$, and satisfies $|a_n|=n/(n+1)$. Which formula fits both conditions?

- $(-1)^{n-1}\dfrac n{n+1}$
- $(-1)^n\dfrac n{n+1}$
- $(-1)^{n-1}\dfrac{n+1}n$
- $\dfrac n{n+1}$

## Explanation

The absolute value fixes the fraction. The factor $(-1)^{n-1}$ is one at $n=1$ and changes sign whenever the index increases by one, as required.
