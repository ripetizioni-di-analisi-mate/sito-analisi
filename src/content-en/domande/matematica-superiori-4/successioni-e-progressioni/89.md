A geometric progression with common ratio $\dfrac{3}{5}$ has sum $10$. What is its first term?

- $6$
- $4$
- $16$
- $\dfrac{50}{3}$

## Explanation

Since $|q| < 1$, the sum of the progression is $\dfrac{a_1}{1 - q}$. Setting $\dfrac{a_1}{1 - \frac{3}{5}} = 10$ gives $\dfrac{a_1}{\frac{2}{5}} = 10$. Thus $a_1 = 10 \cdot \dfrac{2}{5} = 4$.
