Two convergent geometric series have the same ratio $q$ and respective sums $6$ and $10$. What is the sum of the series formed by subtracting the first termwise from the second?

- $16$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- It depends on $q$.

## Explanation

For each finite partial sum, termwise subtraction produces the difference between the two partial sums. Taking limits gives $10-6=4$ because both limits exist.
