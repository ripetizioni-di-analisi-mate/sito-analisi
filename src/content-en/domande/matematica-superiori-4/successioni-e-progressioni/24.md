A machine generates a real sequence using $a_{n+1}=a_n^2-1$ but has stored only the second term $a_2=8$. Which starting values are compatible?

- Only $a_1=3$.
- Only $a_1=7$.
- $a_1=-7$ or $a_1=7$.
- $a_1=-3$ or $a_1=3$.

## Explanation

Stepping back requires solving $a_1^2-1=8$, or $a_1^2=9$. Both roots generate the same second term, so knowing how to step forward does not guarantee a unique predecessor.
