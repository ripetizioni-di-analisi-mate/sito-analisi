An investment earns compound interest at an annual rate of $3\%$. What sequence do the accumulated amounts $M_0, M_1, M_2, \ldots$ form at the end of each year?

- A geometric progression with common ratio $0.03$
- A geometric progression with common ratio $1.03$
- An arithmetic progression with common difference $0.03\,C$
- An arithmetic progression with common difference $1.03$

## Explanation

With compound interest, each year's interest is added to the principal and itself earns interest the following year. At the end of each year the accumulated amount becomes $M_{n+1} = M_n + 0.03\,M_n = 1.03\,M_n$. The ratio of consecutive accumulated amounts is therefore always $1.03$, so the amounts form a geometric progression with common ratio $1.03$. An arithmetic progression with common difference $0.03\,C$ corresponds to simple interest.
