How many terms does the arithmetic progression $5, 9, 13, \ldots, 201$ have?

- $49$
- $51$
- $48$
- $50$

## Explanation

The common difference is $d = 4$ and the last term is $201$. The formula $a_n = a_1 + (n - 1)\,d$ gives $201 = 5 + 4(n - 1)$, so $n - 1 = 49$. Thus there are $n = 50$ terms.
