A kiosk's daily profits follow the sequence $9,5,1,-3,-7,\ldots$. At the end of which day is the cumulative profit highest, counting from day one?

- Day one.
- Day two.
- Day four.
- Day three.

## Explanation

Each positive profit adds to the previous total. The third is the final positive one, and every subsequent term is negative. The first four totals are $9,14,15,12$.
