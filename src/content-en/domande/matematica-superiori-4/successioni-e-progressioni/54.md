A hall has ten rows with $10,13,16,\ldots,37$ chairs. Can the same chairs be redistributed so that all ten rows have the same whole number of chairs?

- Yes: every row can have $23$ chairs with none left over.
- Yes: every row can have $24$ chairs without adding any.
- No: because the ten rows have a total of $47$ chairs.
- No: the mean is $23{,}5$ chairs per row.

## Explanation

In an arithmetic progression the mean equals the mean of the endpoints, $(10+37)/2=23{,}5$. The $235$ chairs leave five over at $23$ per row, while $24$ per row would require five extra chairs.
