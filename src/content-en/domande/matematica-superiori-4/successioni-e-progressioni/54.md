What is the sum of the odd numbers between $100$ and $200$?

- $7350$
- $15000$
- $7650$
- $7500$

## Explanation

The odd numbers between $100$ and $200$ are $101, 103, \ldots, 199$ and form an arithmetic progression with common difference $2$. From $199 = 101 + 2(n - 1)$, there are $n = 50$ terms. The sum formula gives $S_{50} = \dfrac{50\,(101 + 199)}{2} = 7500$.
