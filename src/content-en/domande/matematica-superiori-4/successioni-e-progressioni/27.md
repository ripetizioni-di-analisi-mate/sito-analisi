Which inequality holds for every term of the sequence $a_n = \dfrac{3n + 5}{n + 1}$, with $n \geq 1$?

- $3 \leq a_n < 4$
- $4 < a_n \leq 5$
- $3 < a_n \leq 4$
- $0 < a_n \leq 3$

## Explanation

Dividing the numerator by the denominator gives $a_n = 3 + \dfrac{2}{n + 1}$. The fraction $\dfrac{2}{n + 1}$ is positive and is at most $1$, a value attained when $n = 1$. Thus $3 < a_n \leq 4$ for every $n$, and the sequence is **bounded**.
