An investment of $5000$ euros earns compound interest at an annual rate of $2\%$. After how many years does the accumulated amount first exceed $6000$ euros?

- $10$ years
- $9$ years
- $11$ years
- $12$ years

## Explanation

The accumulated amount after $n$ years is $M_n = 5000 \cdot 1.02^n$, so we solve $5000 \cdot 1.02^n > 6000$, that is, $1.02^n > 1.2$. The natural logarithm is increasing and $\ln 1.02 > 0$, so the inequality is equivalent to $n > \dfrac{\ln 1.2}{\ln 1.02} \approx 9.2$. The first integer that satisfies it is $n = 10$. Indeed, $M_9 \approx 5975.46$ euros and $M_{10} \approx 6094.97$ euros.
