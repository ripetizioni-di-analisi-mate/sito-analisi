What is the sum of the first $25$ terms of the arithmetic progression with first term $4$ and common difference $2$?

- $700$
- $725$
- $1400$
- $650$

## Explanation

The twenty-fifth term is $a_{25} = 4 + 24 \cdot 2 = 52$. The sum formula gives $S_{25} = \dfrac{25\,(4 + 52)}{2} = \dfrac{25 \cdot 56}{2} = 700$. The second form of the same formula gives the same value, $\dfrac{25\,(2 \cdot 4 + 24 \cdot 2)}{2} = \dfrac{25 \cdot 56}{2}$.
