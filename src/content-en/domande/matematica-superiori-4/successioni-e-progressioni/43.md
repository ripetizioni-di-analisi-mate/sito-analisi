Two arithmetic progressions $a_n$ and $b_n$ have common differences $3$ and $-1$. What is the common difference of $c_n=2a_n-b_n$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $4$
- $-7$

## Explanation

Subtracting consecutive terms gives $c_{n+1}-c_n=2(a_{n+1}-a_n)-(b_{n+1}-b_n)$. Thus the difference is $2\cdot3-(-1)=7$.
