- $15150$

- $30300$
- $45150$

## Explanation

The sum of the numbers from $1$ to $300$ is $\dfrac{300 \cdot 301}{2} = 45150$. The multiples of $3$ up to $300$ are $3, 6, \ldots, 300$, forming $100$ terms of an arithmetic progression with common difference $3$. Their sum is $\dfrac{100\,(3 + 300)}{2} = 15150$. The required sum is the difference $45150 - 15150 = 30000$.
