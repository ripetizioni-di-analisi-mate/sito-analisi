An account credits $5\%$ interest at year-end. With everything else fixed, how much larger is the year-end balance if $100$ euros are deposited at the beginning of the year rather than just after the interest credit?

- $100$ euros
- $105$ euros
- $0$ euros
- $5$ euros

## Explanation

The early deposit earns interest and contributes $105$ euros. The late deposit contributes $100$ euros to the balance, giving a difference of $5$ euros.
