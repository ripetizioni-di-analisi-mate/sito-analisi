An investment of $1000$ euros earns compound interest at an annual rate of $4\%$. What is the accumulated amount after $3$ years, rounded to the nearest cent?

- $1120.00$ euros
- $1169.86$ euros
- $1081.60$ euros
- $1124.86$ euros

## Explanation

With compound interest, the accumulated amount after $n$ years is $M_n = C\,(1 + i)^n$. With $C = 1000$, $i = 0.04$, and $n = 3$, we have $M_3 = 1000 \cdot 1.04^3$. Since $1.04^3 = 1.124864$, the accumulated amount rounded to the nearest cent is $1124.86$ euros. With simple interest at the same rate, the accumulated amount would be $1120$ euros.
