Which statement holds for the sequence $a_n = (-2)^n$?

- It is bounded
- It is bounded below and unbounded above
- It is strictly increasing
- It is unbounded both above and below

## Explanation

The terms are $-2, 4, -8, 16, -32, \ldots$ and alternate in sign, so the sequence is not monotone. For even $n = 2m$, the term is $4^m$, which exceeds any prescribed number, so the sequence is not bounded above. For odd $n = 2m + 1$, the term is $-2 \cdot 4^m$, which falls below any prescribed number, so the sequence is not bounded below either.
