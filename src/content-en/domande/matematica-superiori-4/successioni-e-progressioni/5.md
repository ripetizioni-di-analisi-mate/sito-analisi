In an arithmetic progression, $a_4 = 10$ and $a_9 = 25$. What is the sum of the first thirty terms?

- $1380$
- $2670$
- $1335$
- $1290$

## Explanation

There are $5$ steps between the fourth and ninth terms, so $25 = 10 + 5d$ and the common difference is $d = 3$. The first term follows from $a_4 = a_1 + 3d$, that is, $10 = a_1 + 9$, so $a_1 = 1$. The thirtieth term is $a_{30} = 1 + 29 \cdot 3 = 88$. Therefore
$$
S_{30} = \frac{30\,(1 + 88)}{2} = 1335 .
$$
