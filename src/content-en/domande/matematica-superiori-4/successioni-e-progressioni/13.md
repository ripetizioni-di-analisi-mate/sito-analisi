What is the sum $6 + 2 + \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{2}{9} + \dots$?

- $18$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

Each term is obtained by multiplying the preceding one by $\dfrac{1}{3}$, so this is the sum of the geometric progression with $a_1 = 6$ and $q = \dfrac{1}{3}$. Since $|q| < 1$, the sum is
$$
S = \frac{6}{1 - \frac{1}{3}} = \frac{6}{\frac{2}{3}} = 9 .
$$
The partial sums $6$, $8$, $\dfrac{26}{3}$, and $\dfrac{80}{9}$ approach $9$ while remaining below $9$.
