Which of the following sequences consists of the first terms of a geometric progression?

- $1, 4, 9, 16$
- $2, 4, 8, 14$
- $2, -6, 18, -54$
- $3, 6, 9, 12$

## Explanation

In the sequence $2, -6, 18, -54$, the ratios of consecutive terms are all $-3$, so it is a geometric progression with common ratio $-3$. In the sequence $2, 4, 8, 14$, the ratios are $2$, $2$, and $\dfrac{7}{4}$, which are not all equal. The sequences $1, 4, 9, 16$ and $3, 6, 9, 12$ also have unequal ratios. The second of these is an arithmetic progression with common difference $3$.
