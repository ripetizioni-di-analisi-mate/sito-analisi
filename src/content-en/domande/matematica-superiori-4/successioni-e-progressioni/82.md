The interior angles of a triangle form an arithmetic progression, and the smallest angle measures $40^\circ$. What is the measure of the largest?

- $60^\circ$
- $100^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $70^\circ$

## Explanation

Write the angles as $\alpha - d$, $\alpha$, and $\alpha + d$. Their sum is $3\alpha = 180^\circ$, so the middle angle is $\alpha = 60^\circ$. From the smallest angle $60^\circ - d = 40^\circ$ we obtain $d = 20^\circ$, so the largest measures $60^\circ + 20^\circ = 80^\circ$.
