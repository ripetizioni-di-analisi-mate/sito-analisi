A principal of $2000$ euros is deposited at an annual simple interest rate of $3\%$. What is the accumulated amount after $6$ years?

- $2388.10$ euros
- $2360$ euros
- $2060$ euros
- $2180$ euros

## Explanation

With simple interest, each year's interest is calculated on the initial principal and equals $2000 \cdot 0.03 = 60$ euros. After $6$ years, the total interest is $6 \cdot 60 = 360$ euros. Therefore $M_6 = 2000 \cdot (1 + 6 \cdot 0.03) = 2360$ euros.
