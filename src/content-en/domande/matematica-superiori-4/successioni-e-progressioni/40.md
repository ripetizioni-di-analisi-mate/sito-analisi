Which formula gives, for every $n \geq 1$, the sum $2 + 4 + 6 + \dots + 2n$?

- $n^2$
- $n(n + 1)$
- $\dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2}$
- $2n^2$

## Explanation

Factoring out $2$ gives $2\,(1 + 2 + \dots + n)$. By the formula for the sum of the first $n$ natural numbers, $1 + 2 + \dots + n = \dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2}$. Thus the sum is $2 \cdot \dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2} = n(n + 1)$. For example, $2 + 4 + 6 = 12 = 3 \cdot 4$.
