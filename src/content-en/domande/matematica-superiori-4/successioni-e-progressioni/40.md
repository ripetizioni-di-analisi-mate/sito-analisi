To sum $1/(k(k+1))$ from $k=1$ to $N$, write each term as $1/k-1/(k+1)$. What remains after cancellation?

- $1-\dfrac{1}{N}$
- $1-\dfrac{1}{N+1}$
- $\dfrac{1}{N+1}$
- $\dfrac{N+1}{2}$

## Explanation

The negative contribution $-1/2$ cancels the next $1/2$, and so on. The first positive contribution $1$ and the last negative contribution $-1/(N+1)$ remain.
