Four numbers are inserted between $5$ and $30$ so that all six numbers form an arithmetic progression. What is its common difference?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $\dfrac{25}{6}$
- $6$

## Explanation

With four numbers inserted, $30$ becomes the sixth term of the progression starting at $5$. The formula for the general term gives $30 = 5 + 5d$, so $d = \dfrac{30 - 5}{5} = 5$. The progression is $5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30$.
