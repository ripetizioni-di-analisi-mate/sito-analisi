One sensor records at minutes $4,8,12,\ldots$ and another at minutes $6,12,18,\ldots$. Which sequence lists the times when both record?

- $12,24,36,\ldots$
- $2,4,6,\ldots$
- $24,48,72,\ldots$
- $10,20,30,\ldots$

## Explanation

A shared time must be a multiple of both $4$ and $6$. Their least common multiple is $12$, so the shared times are exactly the positive multiples of twelve.
