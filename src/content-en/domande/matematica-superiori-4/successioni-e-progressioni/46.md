Which terms are common to the arithmetic progressions $4,8,12,\ldots$ and $6,12,18,\ldots$?

- $12,24,36,\ldots$
- $2,4,6,\ldots$
- $24,48,72,\ldots$
- $10,20,30,\ldots$

## Explanation

The terms of the first progression are the positive multiples of $4$, and those of the second are the positive multiples of $6$. A common term is therefore a common multiple of $4$ and $6$, that is, a multiple of their least common multiple, which is $12$. Therefore the common terms form the arithmetic progression $12,24,36,\ldots$, with first term $12$ and common difference $12$.
