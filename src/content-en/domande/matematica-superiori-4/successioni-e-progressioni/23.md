For the sequence $a_n = n^2 - 5n$, which indices $n$ satisfy $a_n = 14$?

- Only $n = 7$
- $n = 7$ and $n = -2$
- Only $n = 2$
- No index

## Explanation

Solve $n^2 - 5n = 14$, that is, $n^2 - 5n - 14 = 0$. The trinomial factors as $(n - 7)(n + 2)$, so the solutions of the equation are $7$ and $-2$. The index of a sequence is a natural number, so only $n = 7$ is admissible, and indeed $a_7 = 49 - 35 = 14$.
