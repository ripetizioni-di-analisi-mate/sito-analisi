The side lengths of a right triangle form an arithmetic progression, and the shorter leg has length $9$. What is the length of the hypotenuse?

- $12$
- $15$
- $18$
- $9\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Write the sides as $a - d$, $a$, and $a + d$. The Pythagorean theorem $(a - d)^2 + a^2 = (a + d)^2$ gives $a^2 = 4ad$, so $a = 4d$. The side lengths are therefore $3d$, $4d$, and $5d$. Since the shorter leg has length $3d = 9$, we have $d = 3$ and the hypotenuse has length $5d = 15$.
