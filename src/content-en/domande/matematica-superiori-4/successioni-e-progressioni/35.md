What is the sum $1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 + \dots + 20^2$?

- $2800$
- $2870$
- $8610$
- $1435$

## Explanation

By the formula for the sum of the squares of the first $n$ natural numbers, with $n = 20$, we have
$$
1^2 + 2^2 + \dots + 20^2 = \frac{20 \cdot 21 \cdot 41}{6} ,
$$
where the factor $2n + 1$ equals $41$. Canceling $\dfrac{20 \cdot 21}{6} = 70$ gives the sum $70 \cdot 41 = 2870$.
