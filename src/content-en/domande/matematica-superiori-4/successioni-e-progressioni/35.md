A student assumes $P(n+1)$ directly when proving the step from $P(n)$ to $P(n+1)$. Why is the induction step incomplete?

- The student must assume $P(n)$ is false.
- The conclusion to be derived is being assumed.
- Induction applies only to numerical formulas.
- The base case always replaces the step.

## Explanation

The induction hypothesis is $P(n)$, from which $P(n+1)$ must be deduced. Assuming the latter assertion makes the reasoning circular.
