An arithmetic progression has first term $3$ and common difference $-2$. On which line do the points $(n, a_n)$ of its graph lie?

- $y = -2x + 3$
- $y = 3x - 2$
- $y = 2x + 5$
- $y = -2x + 5$

## Explanation

The general term is $a_n = 3 + (n - 1)(-2) = -2n + 5$, so every point $(n, a_n)$ satisfies the equation $y = -2x + 5$. The slope of the line is the common difference $-2$, and the vertical intercept is $a_1 - d = 5$.
