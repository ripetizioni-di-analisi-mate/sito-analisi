An investment of $1000$ euros earns compound interest and after $2$ years has an accumulated amount of $1210$ euros. What is the annual interest rate?

- $10\%$
- $10.5\%$
- $21\%$
- $11\%$

## Explanation

The accumulated amount after $2$ years is $M_2 = 1000\,(1 + i)^2$, so $(1 + i)^2 = 1.21$. The number $1 + i$ is positive, so $1 + i = 1.1$ and $i = 0.1$, that is, $10\%$. The value $10.5\%$ would be obtained by spreading the total interest of $210$ euros evenly over the two years, as with simple interest.
