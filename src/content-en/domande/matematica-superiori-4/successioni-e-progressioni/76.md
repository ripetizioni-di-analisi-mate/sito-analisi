A geometric progression has positive terms with $a_3=4$ and $a_4=6$. The terms from $a_4$ to $a_8$ sum to $S$. What is the sum from $a_5$ to $a_9$ in terms of $S$?

- $\dfrac32S$
- $S+2$
- $S+10$
- $\dfrac23S$

## Explanation

The common ratio is $\dfrac64=\dfrac32$. Increasing every index by one multiplies every summand, and hence the whole sum, by $\dfrac32$.
