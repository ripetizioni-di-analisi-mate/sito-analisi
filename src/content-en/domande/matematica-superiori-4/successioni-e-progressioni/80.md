A ball drops from $4$ meters and each bounce reaches half the previous height. What is the total distance traveled over all idealized bounces?

- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $16\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The initial drop of $4$ meters is counted once. The bounce heights sum to $2+1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\cdots=4$ meters and are covered both going up and coming down. The total is $4+2\cdot4=12$ meters.
