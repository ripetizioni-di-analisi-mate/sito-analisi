Starting with a square of side length $6$, construct infinitely many squares, each with its vertices at the midpoints of the sides of the preceding square. What is the sum of the areas of all the squares?

- $48$
- $144$
- $36$
- $72$

## Explanation

The first square has area $36$. The side length of each new square equals the preceding side length divided by $\sqrt{2}$, so its area is half the preceding area. Thus the areas form a geometric progression with $a_1 = 36$ and $q = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $|q| < 1$, their sum is $\dfrac{36}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 72$.
