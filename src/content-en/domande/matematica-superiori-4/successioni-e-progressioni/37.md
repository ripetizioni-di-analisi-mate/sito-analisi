Which inequality follows from Bernoulli's inequality?

- $1.02^{50} \leq 2$
- $1.02^{50} \geq 3$
- $0.98^{50} \geq 1$
- $1.02^{50} \geq 2$

## Explanation

Bernoulli's inequality states that $(1 + x)^n \geq 1 + nx$ for every $x \geq -1$ and every $n \geq 1$. With $x = 0.02$ and $n = 50$ it gives $1.02^{50} \geq 1 + 50 \cdot 0.02 = 2$. The value of $1.02^{50}$ is about $2.69$, so it is greater than $2$ and less than $3$. With $x = -0.02$, the same inequality gives only $0.98^{50} \geq 0$, and $0.98^{50}$ is less than $1$ because $0 < 0.98 < 1$.
