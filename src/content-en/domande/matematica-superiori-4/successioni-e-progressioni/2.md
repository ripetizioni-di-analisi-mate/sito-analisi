A sequence is defined recursively by
$$
\begin{cases}
a_1 = 1 \\[4pt]
a_{n+1} = a_n + 2n + 1
\end{cases}
$$
What is the value of $a_5$?

- $16$
- $36$
- $25$
- $21$

## Explanation

Apply the rule with $n = 1, 2, 3, 4$, starting from $a_1 = 1$. This gives $a_2 = 1 + 3 = 4$, then $a_3 = 4 + 5 = 9$, $a_4 = 9 + 7 = 16$, and $a_5 = 16 + 9 = 25$. At each step we add the odd number $2n + 1$ calculated using the index of the preceding term, and the terms are the squares $n^2$.
