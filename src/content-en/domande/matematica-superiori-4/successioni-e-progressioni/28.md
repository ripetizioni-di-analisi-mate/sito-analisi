For which indices does $a_{n+1} > a_n$ hold in the sequence $a_n = n^2 - 12n$?

- For every $n \geq 5$
- For every $n \geq 6$
- For every $n \geq 7$
- For every $n \geq 12$

## Explanation

The difference is $a_{n+1} - a_n = (n + 1)^2 - 12(n + 1) - n^2 + 12n = 2n - 11$. The inequality $2n - 11 > 0$ is equivalent to $n > \dfrac{11}{2}$, that is, to $n \geq 6$ among the natural numbers. Thus the sequence is strictly increasing from $a_6$ onward, and its minimum is $a_6 = -36$.
