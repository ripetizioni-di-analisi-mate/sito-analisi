What is the geometric mean of $2$ and $50$?

- $26$
- $10$
- $100$
- $25$

## Explanation

The geometric mean of two positive numbers $a$ and $c$ is $\sqrt{ac}$. We have $\sqrt{2 \cdot 50} = \sqrt{100} = 10$. Thus $2, 10, 50$ are consecutive terms of a geometric progression with common ratio $5$.
