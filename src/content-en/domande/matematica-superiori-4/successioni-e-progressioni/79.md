The side lengths of a right triangle form an arithmetic progression, and its perimeter is $36$. What is the area of the triangle?

- $108$
- $54$
- $90$
- $\dfrac{135}{2}$

## Explanation

Applying the Pythagorean theorem to the side lengths $a - d$, $a$, and $a + d$ gives $a = 4d$, so the side lengths are $3d$, $4d$, and $5d$. The perimeter is $12d = 36$, so $d = 3$ and the side lengths are $9$, $12$, and $15$. The area is half the product of the legs, $\dfrac{9 \cdot 12}{2} = 54$.
