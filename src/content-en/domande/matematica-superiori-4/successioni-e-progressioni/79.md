A staircase of unit squares has $1$ square in the first row, $3$ in the second, $5$ in the third and so on. What is the side of a square containing as many unit squares as the first $n$ rows?

- $2n-1$
- $n$
- $n+1$
- $n^2$

## Explanation

Going from a square of side $n-1$ to one of side $n$ adds $n^2-(n-1)^2=2n-1$ cells. The first $n$ rows thus correspond to a square of side $n$.
