A positive geometric progression has common ratio $q$. What kind of sequence is $\ln a_n$?

- Geometric with common ratio $\ln q$.
- Always constant.
- Arithmetic with common difference $\ln q$.
- Always decreasing.

## Explanation

Since $a_{n+1}/a_n=q$, the difference $\ln a_{n+1}-\ln a_n$ is the logarithm of their ratio, namely $\ln q$. The difference is thus constant.
