In a geometric progression, $a_3 = 18$ and $a_6 = 486$. What is the first term?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{2}{81}$

## Explanation

The formula $a_6 = a_3\,q^3$ gives $486 = 18\,q^3$, so $q^3 = 27$ and $q = 3$. The first term follows from $a_3 = a_1\,q^2$, that is, $18 = 9\,a_1$. Thus $a_1 = 2$.
