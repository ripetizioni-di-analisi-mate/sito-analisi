What is the sum of the odd numbers $1 + 3 + 5 + \dots + 49$?

- $576$
- $625$
- $1225$
- $2401$

## Explanation

The odd numbers from $1$ to $49$ form an arithmetic progression with common difference $2$, and $49 = 2n - 1$ shows that there are $n = 25$ terms. The sum formula gives $S_{25} = \dfrac{25\,(1 + 49)}{2} = 625$. The result is $25^2$, in agreement with the formula $1 + 3 + \dots + (2n - 1) = n^2$, which can be proved by induction.
