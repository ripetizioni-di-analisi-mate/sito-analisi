An induction proves $P(1)$ and proves that $P(n)$ implies $P(n+2)$ for every $n\geq1$. Which indices do these facts cover?

- All positive indices.
- All positive odd indices.
- Only even indices.
- Only $1$ and $3$.

## Explanation

Starting at one and adding two gives $1,3,5,\ldots$. Reaching the even indices with the same step would require an even starting case, for example $P(2)$.
