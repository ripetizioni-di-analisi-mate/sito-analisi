In a geometric progression, $a_2 = 12$ and $a_5 = -96$. What is the sum of the first six terms?

- $-126$
- $-66$
- $378$
- $126$

## Explanation

By the formula $a_5 = a_2\,q^3$, we have $-96 = 12\,q^3$, so $q^3 = -8$ and $q = -2$. The first term is $a_1 = \dfrac{a_2}{q} = -6$. Therefore
$$
S_6 = -6 \cdot \frac{1 - (-2)^6}{1 - (-2)} = -6 \cdot \frac{-63}{3} = 126 .
$$
Indeed, the terms are $-6, 12, -24, 48, -96, 192$.
