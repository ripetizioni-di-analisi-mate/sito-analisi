How many terms of the sequence $a_n = \dfrac{n + 4}{n}$ are greater than $2$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

Write $a_n = 1 + \dfrac{4}{n}$. Then $a_n > 2$ is equivalent to $\dfrac{4}{n} > 1$. Since $n > 0$, the inequality is equivalent to $n < 4$. The terms greater than $2$ are therefore $a_1 = 5$, $a_2 = 3$, and $a_3 = \dfrac{7}{3}$, whereas $a_4 = 2$ equals $2$.
