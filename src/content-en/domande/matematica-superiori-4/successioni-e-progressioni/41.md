An arithmetic progression has first term $7$ and common difference $-3$. What is its fifteenth term?

- $-38$
- $49$
- $-35$
- $-32$

## Explanation

The formula for the general term $a_n = a_1 + (n - 1)\,d$, with $n = 15$, gives $a_{15} = 7 + 14 \cdot (-3)$. The product is $-42$, so $a_{15} = 7 - 42 = -35$. The factor $14$ is the number of steps from the first to the fifteenth term.
