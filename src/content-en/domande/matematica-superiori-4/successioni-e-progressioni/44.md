What is the sum of the multiples of $7$ between $1$ and $200$?

- $2842$
- $3045$
- $5684$
- $2646$

## Explanation

The largest multiple of $7$ less than $200$ is $196 = 7 \cdot 28$, so the multiples are $7, 14, \ldots, 196$, forming $28$ terms of an arithmetic progression with common difference $7$. The sum formula gives $S_{28} = \dfrac{28\,(7 + 196)}{2} = 14 \cdot 203 = 2842$. The same number is obtained as $7\,(1 + 2 + \dots + 28) = 7 \cdot 406$.
