If $a_n=n^2$ for $n\geq1$, what kind of sequence do the first differences $a_{n+1}-a_n$ form?

- An arithmetic progression with common difference $2$.
- A geometric progression with common ratio $2$.
- A constant sequence.
- An alternating sequence.

## Explanation

The first difference is $(n+1)^2-n^2=2n+1$. Each time $n$ increases by one, this expression increases by two.
