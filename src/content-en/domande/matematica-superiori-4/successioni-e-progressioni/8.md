A geometric progression has first term $5$ and common ratio $2$. What is the sum of the first eight terms?

- $1275$
- $1280$
- $635$
- $2555$

## Explanation

By the formula for the sum of the first $n$ terms of a geometric progression with $q \neq 1$, we have
$$
S_8 = 5 \cdot \frac{1 - 2^8}{1 - 2} = 5 \cdot \frac{-255}{-1} = 1275 .
$$
The exponent in the numerator is $8$, the number of terms being added. The eighth term itself is $a_8 = 5 \cdot 2^7 = 640$, with exponent $7$.
