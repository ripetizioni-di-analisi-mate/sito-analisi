A mistake replaces $\sum_{k=0}^{3}2^k$ with $2^{0+1+2+3}$. Which operation would actually produce the second expression?

- Their arithmetic mean.
- The first term minus the last.
- The product of the four terms.
- The square of their sum.

## Explanation

Exponents add when powers of the same base are multiplied. The original sum is $1+2+4+8=15$, whereas the product is $2^6=64$.
