What is the sum $1 - 2 + 4 - 8 + 16 - 32$?

- $21$
- $-63$
- $-21$
- $63$

## Explanation

The summands are the first six terms of the geometric progression with $a_1 = 1$ and $q = -2$. By the sum formula,
$$
S_6 = \frac{1 - (-2)^6}{1 - (-2)} = \frac{1 - 64}{3} = -21 .
$$
The number $(-2)^6$ is positive because the exponent is even.
