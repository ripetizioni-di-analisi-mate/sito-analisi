A sequence $(a_n)$ is strictly decreasing and all its terms are positive. Which statement is certainly true?

- It is bounded above, but not necessarily bounded below
- It is bounded below, but not bounded above
- It has a minimum term
- It is bounded

## Explanation

Since the sequence is strictly decreasing, every term is less than or equal to the first one, so $a_1$ is an upper bound and the sequence is bounded above. Since all terms are positive, $a_n > 0$ for every $n$, therefore the sequence is bounded below by $0$. Both properties hold together, so $0 < a_n \leq a_1$ for every $n$ and the sequence is **bounded**. A minimum term, on the other hand, may not exist. The sequence $a_n = \dfrac{1}{n}$ is strictly decreasing and positive, but every term is greater than the next one, so no term is the minimum.
