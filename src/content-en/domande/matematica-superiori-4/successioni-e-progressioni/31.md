Which statement holds for the sequence $a_n = n + (-1)^n$?

- It is strictly increasing
- It is bounded
- It is nonincreasing from some index onward
- It is not monotone and is bounded below

## Explanation

The first terms are $0, 3, 2, 5, 4, 7, \ldots$, so the difference $a_{n+1} - a_n$ alternates between $3$ and $-1$ and the sequence is not monotone. Every term is greater than or equal to $n - 1$, which is greater than or equal to $0$, so the sequence is bounded below by $0$. The even-indexed terms equal $n + 1$ and exceed any prescribed number, so the sequence is not bounded above.
