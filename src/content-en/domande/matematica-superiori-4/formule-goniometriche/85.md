Which statement about the function $f(x) = \sin x\cos x$ is true?

- It has least positive period $2\pi$ and maximum $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- It has least positive period $\pi$ and maximum $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- It has least positive period $\pi$ and maximum $1$
- It has least positive period $2\pi$ and maximum $1$

## Explanation

The sine **double-angle formula** gives $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{2}\sin 2x$. The function $\sin 2x$ has least positive period $\pi$ and takes every value between $-1$ and $1$. Therefore $f$ has least positive period $\pi$, and its maximum is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, attained at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$. The maximum $1$ would require $\sin x$ and $\cos x$ to equal $1$ at the same point.
