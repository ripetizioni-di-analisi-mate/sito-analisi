The formula $\cos (3x)=4\cos^3x-3\cos x$ can be rearranged for $\cos^3x$. What is the coefficient of $\cos x$ in the resulting expression?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $-3$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Move the linear term to the other side and divide by four to get $\cos^3x=\dfrac{\cos (3x)+3\cos x}{4}$. The required coefficient is three quarters.
