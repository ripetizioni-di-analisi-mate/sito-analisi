In the parametrization $u=\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}$, $v=\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}$, which symmetry results when $t$ is replaced by $-t$?

- Reflection across the vertical axis.
- A half-turn.
- Interchange of the coordinates.
- Reflection across the horizontal axis.

## Explanation

The first coordinate contains only even powers and stays the same. The second changes sign, reflecting the point across the horizontal axis.
