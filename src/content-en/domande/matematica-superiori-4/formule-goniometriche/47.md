Given $\tan\dfrac{x}{2} = 3$, what is $\tan x$?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The **tangent half-angle substitution** with $t = 3$ gives
$$
\tan x = \frac{2t}{1 - t^2} = \frac{6}{1 - 9} = -\frac{3}{4} .
$$
The same result follows from $\sin x = \dfrac{6}{10} = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\cos x = \dfrac{1 - 9}{10} = -\dfrac{4}{5}$, whose quotient is $-\dfrac{3}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{5}$ is $\sin x$, and $-\dfrac{4}{3}$ is the reciprocal of $\tan x$.
