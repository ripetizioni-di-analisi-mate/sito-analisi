What is $\cos 20^\circ\cos 40^\circ\cos 80^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

Multiply and divide the product $P$ by $\sin 20^\circ$, which differs from $0$. Applying the sine **double-angle formula** three times gives
$$
\sin 20^\circ \cdot P = \frac{1}{2}\sin 40^\circ\cos 40^\circ\cos 80^\circ = \frac{1}{4}\sin 80^\circ\cos 80^\circ = \frac{1}{8}\sin 160^\circ .
$$
Since $\sin 160^\circ = \sin(180^\circ - 20^\circ) = \sin 20^\circ$, dividing by $\sin 20^\circ$ gives $P = \dfrac{1}{8}$. The values $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{16}$ result from applying the double-angle formula one time too few or too many.
