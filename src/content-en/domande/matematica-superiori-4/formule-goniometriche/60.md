What is $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{8}\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2 + \sqrt{2}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$

## Explanation

By the second of **Werner's formulas**, with $\alpha = \dfrac{3\pi}{8}$ and $\beta = \dfrac{\pi}{8}$ we have $\alpha + \beta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\alpha - \beta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, so
$$
\cos\frac{3\pi}{8}\cos\frac{\pi}{8} = \frac{1}{2}\left( \cos\frac{\pi}{2} + \cos\frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{2}\left( 0 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ results from omitting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the formula.
