For every real $x$, which sum equals $2\sin 3x\sin x$?

- $\cos 4x - \cos 2x$
- $\cos 2x + \cos 4x$
- $\cos 2x - \cos 4x$
- $\sin 4x + \sin 2x$

## Explanation

By the third of **Werner's formulas**, $2\sin\alpha\sin\beta = \cos(\alpha - \beta) - \cos(\alpha + \beta)$. With $\alpha = 3x$ and $\beta = x$, we have $\alpha - \beta = 2x$ and $\alpha + \beta = 4x$, so
$$
2\sin 3x\sin x = \cos 2x - \cos 4x .
$$
The expression $\cos 4x - \cos 2x$ has the opposite sign. For instance, at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ the product equals $2 \cdot (-1) \cdot 1 = -2$, and we have $\cos\pi - \cos 2\pi = -2$.
