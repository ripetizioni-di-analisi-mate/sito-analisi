For an acute angle, which of the forms $\dfrac{1-\cos x}{\sin x}$ and $\dfrac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}$ avoids subtracting nearly equal numbers when $x$ is very small?

- The first.
- Both contain the same subtraction.
- Neither equals the half-angle tangent.
- The second.

## Explanation

Both forms give $\tan (\dfrac{x}{2})$. At an angle close to zero, cosine is close to one, so the first form subtracts two nearly equal numbers.
