In the sum $S=\cos (2x)+\cos (4x)$, $x$ is shifted by $\pi$. Why is $S$ unchanged without converting the sum to a product?

- Cosine is constant on any interval of width $\pi$.
- The two terms always cancel.
- Both arguments increase by whole multiples of $2\pi$.
- Cosine functions have arbitrary periods.

## Explanation

The two phase increments are $2\pi$ and $4\pi$. Each term thus returns to its own value and their sum stays fixed.
