Can $2\sin x+3\cos (2x)$ be identically equal to $R \sin (x+\varphi)$ for some real constants?

- Yes, with $R=5$.
- Yes, with $R=1$.
- Yes, for any phase.
- No, its values at $0$ and $\pi$ are both $3$.

## Explanation

A single first harmonic changes sign when $\pi$ is added to the argument. Here the $\cos (2x)$ term keeps its sign, as the two sample values show.
