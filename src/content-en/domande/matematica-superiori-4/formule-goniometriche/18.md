Which expression equals $\sin\left( x + \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right) + \sin\left( x - \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)$ for every real $x$?

- $2\sin x$
- $\sqrt{3}\sin x$
- $\sqrt{3}\cos x$
- $\sin x$

## Explanation

The **sine sum and difference** formulas give
$$
\sin\left( x \pm \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \sin x\cos\frac{\pi}{3} \pm \cos x\sin\frac{\pi}{3} .
$$
Adding the two expressions cancels the terms containing $\cos x\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, leaving $2\sin x\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}\sin x = \sin x$. The result $2\sin x$ comes from omitting the factor $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
