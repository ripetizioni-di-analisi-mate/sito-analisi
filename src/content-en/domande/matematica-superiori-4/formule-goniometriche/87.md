Which addition formula results from comparing real parts in $e^{ia}e^{ib}=e^{i(a+b)}$?

- $\cos(a+b)=\cos a\cos b+\sin a\sin b$
- $\cos(a+b)=\sin a\cos b+\cos a\sin b$
- $\cos(a+b)=\cos a+\cos b$
- $\cos(a+b)=\cos a\cos b-\sin a\sin b$

## Explanation

Multiply $(\cos a+i\sin a)$ by $(\cos b+i\sin b)$. The real terms are $\cos a\cos b$ and $i^2\sin a\sin b=-\sin a\sin b$. Their sum is the real part of $e^{i(a+b)}$.
