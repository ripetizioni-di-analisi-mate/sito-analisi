For $0<x<\pi$, can $\tan (\dfrac{x}{2})<0$?

- Yes, whenever $\cos x<0$.
- No, the half-angle lies in the first quadrant.
- Yes, for every obtuse angle.
- Yes, because tangent changes sign at $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

## Explanation

Halving the bounds gives $0<\dfrac{x}{2}<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Both coordinates are positive in that interval, so tangent is positive.
