For which of the following values of $\alpha$ does the formula $\tan 2\alpha = \dfrac{2\tan\alpha}{1 - \tan^2\alpha}$ fail to apply?

- $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{8}$

## Explanation

The formula requires that $\tan\alpha$ and $\tan 2\alpha$ be defined, that is, $\alpha \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ and $\alpha \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. For $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ we have $\tan\alpha = 1$, so the denominator $1 - \tan^2\alpha$ is zero and the tangent of $2\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ is undefined. For $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ the formula applies and gives $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{1 - 3} = -\sqrt{3}$, which is the value of $\tan\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. Both conditions are also satisfied for $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$.
