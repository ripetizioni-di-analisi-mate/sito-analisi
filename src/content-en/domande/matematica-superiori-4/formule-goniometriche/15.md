A rotation through $\alpha$ sends $(1,0)$ to $(c,s)$. Where does it send the perpendicular vector $(0,1)$?

- $(s,c)$
- $(c,-s)$
- $(-s,c)$
- $(-c,-s)$

## Explanation

The second vector is the first turned through an additional right angle. The addition formulas give $\cos (\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{2})=-\sin \alpha$ and $\sin (\alpha+\dfrac{\pi}{2})=\cos \alpha$.
