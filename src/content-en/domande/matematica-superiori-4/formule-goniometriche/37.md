For values of $x$ with $\cos x \neq \sin x$, which expression equals $\dfrac{\cos 2x}{\cos x - \sin x}$?

- $\cos x - \sin x$
- $1$
- $\sin x - \cos x$
- $\cos x + \sin x$

## Explanation

The **double-angle formula** gives $\cos 2x = \cos^2 x - \sin^2 x$, and the right-hand side is a difference of squares:
$$
\cos 2x = (\cos x - \sin x)(\cos x + \sin x) .
$$
Since $\cos x - \sin x \neq 0$, the numerator and denominator can be divided by this factor, giving $\cos x + \sin x$. The result $\cos x - \sin x$ comes from interchanging the two factors in the factorization.
