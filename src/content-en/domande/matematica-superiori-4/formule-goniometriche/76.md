What is $\tan 15^\circ + \tan 75^\circ$?

- $2\sqrt{3}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

By the tangent difference formula, $\tan 15^\circ = \tan(45^\circ - 30^\circ) = 2 - \sqrt{3}$. The angle $75^\circ$ is complementary to $15^\circ$, so its tangent is the reciprocal:
$$
\tan 75^\circ = \frac{1}{2 - \sqrt{3}} = \frac{2 + \sqrt{3}}{4 - 3} = 2 + \sqrt{3} .
$$
Therefore $\tan 15^\circ + \tan 75^\circ = 2 - \sqrt{3} + 2 + \sqrt{3} = 4$. The value $1$ is the product $\tan 15^\circ \cdot \tan 75^\circ$.
