A signal gives readings $s(t)=\cos t$ and $s(t+h)=\cos(t+h)$. Which expression gives their **mean** exactly?

- $\cos\left(t+\dfrac h2\right)$
- $\cos\left(t+\dfrac h2\right)\cos\dfrac h2$
- $\sin\left(t+\dfrac h2\right)\cos\dfrac h2$
- $2\cos(t+h)\cos t$

## Explanation

The sum-of-cosines formula uses the half-sum $t+\dfrac h2$ and half-difference $\dfrac h2$. Dividing by two cancels the factor $2$ in the formula.
