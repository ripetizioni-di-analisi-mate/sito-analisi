We write $a \sin x+b \cos x=R \sin (x+\varphi)$ with $R>0$. Which relation connects the coefficients **before** the phase is determined?

- $R=a+b$
- $R^2=a^2-b^2$
- $R^2=a^2+b^2$
- $R=ab$

## Explanation

Comparing coefficients gives $a=R \cos \varphi$ and $b=R \sin \varphi$. Squaring and adding eliminates the phase.
