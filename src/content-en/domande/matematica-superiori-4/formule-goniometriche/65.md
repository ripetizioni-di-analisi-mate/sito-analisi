What is the range of the function $f(x) = 3\sin x - 4\cos x + 2$?

- $[-5, 5]$
- $[-5, 9]$
- $[-1, 5]$
- $[-3, 7]$

## Explanation

By the **auxiliary-angle formula**, $3\sin x - 4\cos x = r\sin(x + \varphi)$ with $r = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$. The expression $5\sin(x + \varphi)$ takes every value in the interval $[-5, 5]$, so $f$ takes every value in
$$
[-5 + 2, 5 + 2] = [-3, 7] .
$$
The interval $[-5, 5]$ overlooks the constant term $2$, and $[-1, 5]$ uses the amplitude $3$ in place of $5$.
