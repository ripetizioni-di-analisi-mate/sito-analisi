A signal is $f(x)=a \sin x+b \cos x$. The measured values are $f(0)=3$ and $f(\dfrac{\pi}{2})=4$. Which expression fits them?

- $3\sin x+4\cos x$
- $4\sin x-3\cos x$
- $3\sin x-4\cos x$
- $4\sin x+3\cos x$

## Explanation

At zero the sine term vanishes, giving $b=3$. A quarter turn later the cosine term vanishes, giving $a=4$.
