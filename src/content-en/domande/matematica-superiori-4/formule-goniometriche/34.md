For which values of $x$ does the equality $\sin 2x = 2\sin x$ hold?

- $x = 2k\pi$
- $x = k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$
- For every real $x$

## Explanation

By the **double-angle formula**, the equality becomes $2\sin x\cos x = 2\sin x$, that is, $2\sin x\,(\cos x - 1) = 0$. The product is zero when $\sin x = 0$ or $\cos x = 1$. The first condition gives $x = k\pi$, and the second gives $x = 2k\pi$, values already included in the first set. Therefore the equality holds only for $x = k\pi$ and is an equation rather than an identity. For example, at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ the left-hand side equals $0$ and the right-hand side equals $2$.
