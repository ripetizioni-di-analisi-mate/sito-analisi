For acute angles $a,b$, suppose $\tan a\,\tan b<1$. What can be concluded about $a+b$ without finding its value?

- It is a right angle.
- It is acute.
- It is obtuse.
- It is greater than $\pi$.

## Explanation

The cosine of the sum is $\cos a\cos b(1-\tan a\tan b)$. All factors are positive. Since $0<a+b<\pi$, the positive cosine places the sum in the first quadrant.
