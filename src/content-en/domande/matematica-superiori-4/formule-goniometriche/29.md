Two angles $\alpha$ and $\pi-\alpha$ have equal sines. How do the sines of their doubled angles compare?

- They always both equal one.
- They are negatives of each other.
- The second is twice the first.
- Their product is always positive.

## Explanation

The two angles have the same sine but opposite cosines. The formula $\sin2t=2\sin t\cos t$ therefore changes sign, including the case where both sides are zero.
