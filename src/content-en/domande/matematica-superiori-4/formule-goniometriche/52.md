For $t=\tan(x/2)$ and $-\pi<x<\pi$, which condition on $t$ places the point $(\cos x,\sin x)$ strictly to the left of the vertical axis?

- $|t|>1$
- $|t|<1$
- $t>0$
- $t<0$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate is $(1-t^2)/(1+t^2)$. The denominator is always positive, so the coordinate is negative when $t^2>1$. The sign of $t$ alone distinguishes the upper and lower half-planes instead.
