Which of the measurements $f(0)$, $f(\pi/2)$, $f(\pi)$ and $f(3\pi/2)$ distinguishes $f(x)=\cos x$ from $f(x)=\cos(2x)$ using only a positive or negative sign while avoiding zero?

- $f(0)$
- $f(\pi/2)$
- $f(\pi)$
- $f(3\pi/2)$

## Explanation

At $\pi$ the values are $\cos\pi=-1$ and $\cos(2\pi)=1$, giving opposite signs. At zero the values agree, and at the two other positions one reading is zero.
