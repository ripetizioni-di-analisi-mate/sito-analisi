To build a sine table at equal steps $h$, you know $\sin x$, $\sin(x-h)$ and $\cos h$. Which rule gives the next reading?

- $\sin(x+h)=2\cos h\sin x-\sin(x-h)$
- $\sin(x+h)=2\cos h\sin x+\sin(x-h)$
- $\sin(x+h)=\sin x+\sin h$
- $\sin(x+h)=\cos h-\sin(x-h)$

## Explanation

Adding the sine addition and subtraction formulas gives $\sin(x+h)+\sin(x-h)=2\sin x\cos h$. Isolating the first term produces the required recurrence.
