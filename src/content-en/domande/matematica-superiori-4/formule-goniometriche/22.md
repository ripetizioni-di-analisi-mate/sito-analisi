Given $\tan\alpha = 2$ and $\tan\beta = -3$, what is $\tan(\alpha - \beta)$?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{7}$

## Explanation

By the **tangent difference** formula,
$$
\tan(\alpha - \beta) = \frac{2 - (-3)}{1 + 2 \cdot (-3)} = \frac{5}{-5} = -1 .
$$
The value $\dfrac{5}{7}$ results from giving the product $2 \cdot (-3)$ in the denominator the wrong sign. The value $-\dfrac{1}{7}$ is the tangent of the sum, $\dfrac{2 - 3}{1 + 6}$.
