What is $\cos^2(\alpha+\beta)+\sin^2(\alpha+\beta)$ if neither angle is known?

- $1$
- $\cos (2\alpha+2\beta)$
- $2$
- $\sin (\alpha+\beta)$

## Explanation

Treat $\alpha+\beta$ as a single angle. The Pythagorean identity holds for every argument, so there is no need to expand the squares.
