Two tones are described by $\sin (11t)+\sin (9t)$. Which factor multiplies $\sin (10t)$ in the product form?

- $2\cos t$
- $2\sin t$
- $\cos (20t)$
- $2\cos (2t)$

## Explanation

The half-sum of the arguments is $10t$ and their half-difference is $t$. The sum-to-product formula gives $2\sin (10t)\cos t$.
