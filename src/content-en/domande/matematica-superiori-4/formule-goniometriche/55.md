For every real $x$, which product equals $\sin 5x + \sin 3x$?

- $2\sin 4x\cos x$
- $2\cos 4x\sin x$
- $2\sin 4x\sin x$
- $2\sin 8x\cos 2x$

## Explanation

In the **sum-to-product formula** for a sum of sines with $p = 5x$ and $q = 3x$, half the sum is $\dfrac{5x + 3x}{2} = 4x$ and half the difference is $\dfrac{5x - 3x}{2} = x$. Therefore
$$
\sin 5x + \sin 3x = 2\sin 4x\cos x .
$$
The product $2\sin 8x\cos 2x$ results from using the sum and difference of the angles without dividing them by $2$.
