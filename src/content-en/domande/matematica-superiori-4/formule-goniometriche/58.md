Why is $\sin (x+h)-\sin (x-h)$ an even function of $x$ for fixed $h$?

- It equals $2\cos x \sin h$.
- It equals $2\sin x \cos h$.
- Sine is even.
- Every difference of odd functions is even.

## Explanation

The sine difference formula leaves $\cos x$ as the only factor depending on $x$. Cosine is unchanged when its argument changes sign.
