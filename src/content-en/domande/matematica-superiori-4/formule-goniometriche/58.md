What is $\cos 165^\circ + \cos 75^\circ$?

- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

In the **sum-to-product formula** for a sum of cosines, half the sum of the angles is $120^\circ$ and half their difference is $45^\circ$, so
$$
\cos 165^\circ + \cos 75^\circ = 2\cos 120^\circ\cos 45^\circ = 2 \cdot \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ results from taking $\cos 120^\circ$ to be positive and thus overlooking that $120^\circ$ lies in the second quadrant.
