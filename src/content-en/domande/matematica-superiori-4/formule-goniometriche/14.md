A rectangle is inscribed in a circle of radius $2$, and $x$ is the angle a diagonal makes with its base, with $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. What is the maximum perimeter of the rectangle?

- $16$
- $8$
- $8\sqrt{2}$
- $4\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The diagonal of the rectangle is a diameter, so its length is $4$, and the sides have lengths $4\cos x$ and $4\sin x$. The perimeter is $p(x) = 8\sin x + 8\cos x$ and, using the **auxiliary angle** $\varphi = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, can be written as
$$
p(x) = 8\sqrt{2}\sin\left( x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) .
$$
The sine equals $1$ when $x + \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, that is, at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, which belongs to the interval. Therefore the maximum perimeter is $8\sqrt{2}$, and the corresponding rectangle is a square of side length $2\sqrt{2}$.
