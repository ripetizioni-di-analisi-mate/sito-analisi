The line through $A(-1, 0)$ with slope $t = \dfrac{1}{3}$ intersects the unit circle at the point $A$ and at a second point $P$. What are the coordinates of $P$?

- $\left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$
- $\left( -\dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$
- $\left( \dfrac{4}{5}, -\dfrac{3}{5} \right)$

## Explanation

The slope of the line $AP$ is $\tan\dfrac{x}{2}$, where $x$ is the angle corresponding to $P$. Therefore $P = (\cos x, \sin x)$ with $t = \tan\dfrac{x}{2} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, and by the **tangent half-angle substitution**,
$$
\cos x = \frac{1 - \dfrac{1}{9}}{1 + \dfrac{1}{9}} = \frac{4}{5} , \qquad \sin x = \frac{\dfrac{2}{3}}{\dfrac{10}{9}} = \frac{3}{5} .
$$
Therefore $P = \left( \dfrac{4}{5}, \dfrac{3}{5} \right)$. The point $\left( \dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$ corresponds to $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$ instead.
