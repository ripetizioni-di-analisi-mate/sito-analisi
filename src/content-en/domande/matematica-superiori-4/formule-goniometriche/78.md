A program simplifies $\sin(2x)/(1+\cos(2x))$ to $\tan x$. At which points are both sides undefined?

- $x=k\pi$
- $x=k\pi/2$
- None.
- $x=\pi/2+k\pi$

## Explanation

The original denominator is $2\cos^2x$. It vanishes exactly where $\cos x=0$, which are also the undefined points of tangent. The simplification preserves the whole domain in this case.
