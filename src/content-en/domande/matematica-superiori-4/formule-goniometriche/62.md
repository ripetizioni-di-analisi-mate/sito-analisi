For values of $x$ where it is defined, which expression equals $\dfrac{\sin 3x + \sin x}{\cos 3x + \cos x}$?

- $\tan x$
- $\tan 4x$
- $\tan 2x$
- $\dfrac{1}{\tan 2x}$

## Explanation

By the **sum-to-product formulas**, with half-sum $2x$ and half-difference $x$, the numerator is $2\sin 2x\cos x$ and the denominator is $2\cos 2x\cos x$. Wherever the fraction is defined, its denominator differs from $0$, so $\cos x \neq 0$ and the factor $2\cos x$ cancels:
$$
\frac{\sin 3x + \sin x}{\cos 3x + \cos x} = \frac{2\sin 2x\cos x}{2\cos 2x\cos x} = \frac{\sin 2x}{\cos 2x} = \tan 2x .
$$
The result $\tan 4x$ comes from using the sum of the angles in place of half their sum.
