For every real $x$, which product equals $\sin 3x - \sin x$?

- $2\sin 2x\cos x$
- $2\cos 2x\cos x$
- $2\cos 2x\sin x$
- $-2\cos 2x\sin x$

## Explanation

In the **sum-to-product formula** $\sin p - \sin q = 2\cos\dfrac{p + q}{2}\sin\dfrac{p - q}{2}$ with $p = 3x$ and $q = x$, half the sum is $2x$ and half the difference is $x$. Therefore
$$
\sin 3x - \sin x = 2\cos 2x\sin x .
$$
The product $2\sin 2x\cos x$, on the other hand, equals the sum $\sin 3x + \sin x$.
