If $t=\tan (\dfrac{x}{2})$, which point on the circle is approached as $t$ becomes large and positive?

- $(1,0)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(-1,0)$
- $(0,-1)$

## Explanation

In the rational parametrization the first coordinate is $\dfrac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}$, which approaches minus one. The second is $\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}$ and tends to zero.
