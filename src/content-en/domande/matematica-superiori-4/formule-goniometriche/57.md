In the product $\cos (5t)\cos (2t)$, which coefficients of $t$ occur after conversion to a sum of cosines?

- $10$ and $3$
- $7$ and $10$
- $7$ and $3$
- $5$ and $2$

## Explanation

The product-to-sum formula uses the sum and difference of the arguments. Here they are $5t+2t=7t$ and $5t-2t=3t$.
