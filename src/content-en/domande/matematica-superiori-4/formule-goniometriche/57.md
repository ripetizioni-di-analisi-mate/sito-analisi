What is $\sin 75^\circ\cos 15^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{2 - \sqrt{3}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2 + \sqrt{3}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1 + \sqrt{3}}{4}$

## Explanation

By the first of **Werner's formulas**, with $\alpha = 75^\circ$ and $\beta = 15^\circ$,
$$
\sin 75^\circ\cos 15^\circ = \frac{1}{2}\left( \sin 90^\circ + \sin 60^\circ \right) = \frac{1}{2}\left( 1 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right) = \frac{2 + \sqrt{3}}{4} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{2 - \sqrt{3}}{4}$ results from using $\sin 90^\circ - \sin 60^\circ$, and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ is the value of $\sin 15^\circ\cos 15^\circ$.
