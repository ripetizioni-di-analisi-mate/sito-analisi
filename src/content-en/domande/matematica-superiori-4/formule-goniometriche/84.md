In a right triangle, an acute angle $\alpha$ satisfies $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{3}{4}$. What is $\sin 2\alpha$?

- $\dfrac{24}{25}$
- $\dfrac{7}{25}$
- $\dfrac{12}{25}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The angle $\alpha$ is acute and $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{3}{4}$, so the legs are in the ratio $3 : 4$ and the hypotenuse is proportional to $5$. Therefore $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{4}{5}$, and by the **double-angle formula**,
$$
\sin 2\alpha = 2 \cdot \frac{3}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{24}{25} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{7}{25}$ is $\cos 2\alpha$, and $\dfrac{12}{25}$ is the product $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha$ without the factor $2$.
