What is the value of $\tan 75^\circ$ when calculated as $\tan(45^\circ + 30^\circ)$?

- $2 - \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{3 + \sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $2 + \sqrt{3}$
- $-2 - \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Since $\tan 45^\circ = 1$ and $\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$, the **tangent sum** formula gives
$$
\tan 75^\circ = \frac{1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}}{1 - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}} = \frac{3 + \sqrt{3}}{3 - \sqrt{3}} .
$$
Multiplying the numerator and denominator by $3 + \sqrt{3}$ gives $\dfrac{12 + 6\sqrt{3}}{6} = 2 + \sqrt{3}$. The value $\dfrac{3 + \sqrt{3}}{3}$ is the sum $\tan 45^\circ + \tan 30^\circ$, and $2 - \sqrt{3}$ is the tangent of $15^\circ$.
