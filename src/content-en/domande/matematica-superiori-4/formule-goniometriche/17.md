A student claims $\cos (2x)=2\cos x$ for every angle. Which test suffices to disprove the identity?

- At $x=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ both sides vanish.
- One supporting example proves the identity.
- At $x=0$ the two sides are $1$ and $2$.
- Cosine is periodic, so the identity holds.

## Explanation

An identity must hold at every angle. The origin already gives a counterexample because $\cos 0=1$.
