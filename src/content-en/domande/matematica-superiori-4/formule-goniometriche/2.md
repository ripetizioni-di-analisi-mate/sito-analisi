- $2 + \sqrt{3}$
- $-2 - \sqrt{3}$
- $1$
- $\sqrt{3} - 2$

## Explanation

Since $\tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}$ and $\tan 45^\circ = 1$, the **tangent sum** formula gives
$$
\tan 105^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3} + 1}{1 - \sqrt{3} \cdot 1} = \frac{\sqrt{3} + 1}{1 - \sqrt{3}} .
$$
Multiplying the numerator and denominator by $1 + \sqrt{3}$ gives $\dfrac{\left( 1 + \sqrt{3} \right)^2}{1 - 3} = \dfrac{4 + 2\sqrt{3}}{-2} = -2 - \sqrt{3}$. The result is negative, as it must be for an angle in the second quadrant. The value $1$ results from writing $1 + \sqrt{3}$ in the denominator, which uses the sign from the difference formula.
