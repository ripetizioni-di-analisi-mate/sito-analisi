What is the least positive period of the function $f(x) = \cos^2 x$?

- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $4\pi$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

By the **power-reduction formula**,
$$
\cos^2 x = \frac{1 + \cos 2x}{2} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\cos 2x .
$$
The function $\cos 2x$ has least positive period $\dfrac{2\pi}{2} = \pi$, and multiplying it by $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and adding $\dfrac{1}{2}$ does not change the period. Therefore the least positive period of $\cos^2 x$ is $\pi$. The number $2\pi$ is a period, but there is a smaller positive one.
