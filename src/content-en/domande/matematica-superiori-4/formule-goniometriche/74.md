For $x \neq k\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, which expression equals $\tan x + \dfrac{1}{\tan x}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sin 2x}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\sin 2x}$
- $\dfrac{2}{\cos 2x}$
- $2\sin 2x$

## Explanation

Write the tangent as a ratio and use a common denominator:
$$
\frac{\sin x}{\cos x} + \frac{\cos x}{\sin x} = \frac{\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x}{\sin x\cos x} = \frac{1}{\sin x\cos x} .
$$
The **double-angle formula** gives $\sin x\cos x = \dfrac{1}{2}\sin 2x$, so the expression equals $\dfrac{2}{\sin 2x}$. The result $\dfrac{1}{\sin 2x}$ comes from confusing $\sin x\cos x$ with $\sin 2x$.
