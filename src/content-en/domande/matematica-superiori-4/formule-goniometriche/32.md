For every real $x$, which expression equals $(\sin x + \cos x)^2$?

- $1$
- $1 + \sin 2x$
- $1 + 2\sin 2x$
- $1 + \cos 2x$

## Explanation

Expanding the square of the binomial gives $\sin^2 x + 2\sin x\cos x + \cos^2 x$. The Pythagorean identity gives $\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1$, and the sine **double-angle formula** gives $2\sin x\cos x = \sin 2x$. Therefore
$$
(\sin x + \cos x)^2 = 1 + \sin 2x .
$$
The result $1$ comes from omitting the cross term, and $1 + 2\sin 2x$ comes from counting the factor $2$ twice.
