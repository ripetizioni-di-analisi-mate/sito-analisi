What additional information is needed to recover the signed value of the half-angle sine from the cosine of the full angle?

- The length unit of the axes.
- The sign of the sine of the half-angle.
- The radius of the circle, which is always unknown.
- The frequency of an external signal.

## Explanation

The half-angle formula fixes $\sin^2(\dfrac{\alpha}{2})=\dfrac{1-\cos \alpha}{2}$. Choosing the square root requires knowing which semicircle contains the half-angle.
