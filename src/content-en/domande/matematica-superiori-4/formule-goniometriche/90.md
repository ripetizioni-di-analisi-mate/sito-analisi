A triangle has angles $A,B,C$. Which identity replaces $\sin (A+B)$ with an expression in the remaining angle?

- $\sin (A+B)=-\sin C$
- $\sin (A+B)=\sin C$
- $\sin (A+B)=\cos C$
- $\sin (A+B)=-\cos C$

## Explanation

The interior-angle sum gives $A+B=\pi-C$. Supplementary angles have the same sine, so the result is $\sin C$.
