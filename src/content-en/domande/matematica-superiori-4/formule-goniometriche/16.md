What is the dot product of the unit vectors $(\cos \alpha,\sin \alpha)$ and $(\cos \beta,\sin \beta)$?

- $\sin (\alpha-\beta)$
- $\cos (\alpha+\beta)$
- $\cos (\alpha-\beta)$
- $\sin (\alpha+\beta)$

## Explanation

Add the products of corresponding coordinates. The result is $\cos \alpha \cos \beta+\sin \alpha \sin \beta$, the expansion of the cosine of the difference.
