When reducing $\sin^4 x+\cos^4 x$, which expression contains **only** a constant and $\cos (4x)$?

- $\dfrac{3+\cos (4x)}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1+\cos (4x)}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3-\cos (4x)}{4}$
- $1+\cos (4x)$

## Explanation

Write $1-2\sin^2x \cos^2x=1-\dfrac{1}{2}\sin^2(2x)$. Reducing the square with the double-angle formula yields the required expression.
