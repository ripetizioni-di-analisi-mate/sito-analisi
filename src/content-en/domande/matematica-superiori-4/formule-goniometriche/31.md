An expression contains $\cos^2 x$ and must be rewritten using the double angle. Which substitution removes the square?

- $\dfrac{1+\cos (2x)}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1-\cos (2x)}{2}$
- $1+\cos (2x)$
- $\dfrac{\cos (2x)}{2}$

## Explanation

Isolate the square in $\cos (2x)=2\cos^2 x-1$. The constant term is also needed to get the correct value at zero.
