For every real $x$, which expression equals $\cos\left( x + \dfrac{\pi}{6} \right) - \cos\left( x - \dfrac{\pi}{6} \right)$?

- $\sin x$
- $-\sqrt{3}\sin x$
- $-\sin x$
- $\sqrt{3}\cos x$

## Explanation

By the **cosine sum and difference** formulas,
$$
\cos\left( x \pm \frac{\pi}{6} \right) = \cos x\cos\frac{\pi}{6} \mp \sin x\sin\frac{\pi}{6} .
$$
On subtraction, the terms containing $\cos x$ cancel, leaving $-2\sin x\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} = -2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}\sin x = -\sin x$. The result $-\sqrt{3}\sin x$ comes from using $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ in place of $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
