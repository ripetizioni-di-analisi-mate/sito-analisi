An average of two readings should cancel the $\sin x$ term and preserve the $\cos x$ term. Which combination of $f(x)=a \sin x+b \cos x$ does this?

- $\dfrac{f(x)-f(-x)}{2}$
- $f(x)+f(x+\pi)$
- $\dfrac{f(x)+f(-x)}{2}$
- $\dfrac{f(x)+f(x)}{2}$

## Explanation

Replacing $x$ by $-x$ reverses sine and preserves cosine. Taking the average cancels the odd component and leaves $b \cos x$.
