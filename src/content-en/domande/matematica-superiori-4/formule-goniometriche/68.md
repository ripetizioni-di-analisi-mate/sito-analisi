A phase is sought in $3\cos x+4\sin x=5\cos (x-\varphi)$. In which quadrant does $\varphi$ lie when chosen between $0$ and $2\pi$?

- Second.
- Third.
- Fourth.
- First.

## Explanation

The cosine difference formula requires $\cos \varphi=\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\sin \varphi=\dfrac{4}{5}$. Both coordinates are positive, fixing the quadrant.
