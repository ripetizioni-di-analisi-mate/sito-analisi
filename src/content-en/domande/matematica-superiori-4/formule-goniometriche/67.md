A pair of signals is $u=\cos x+\sin x$ and $v=\cos x-\sin x$. What is their product in terms of a single double angle?

- $\cos (2x)$
- $\sin (2x)$
- $1$
- $2\cos (2x)$

## Explanation

The product is a difference of squares, $uv=\cos^2x-\sin^2x$. The double-angle formula identifies it with the cosine of the double angle.
