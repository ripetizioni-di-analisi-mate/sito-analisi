At which points does the function $f(x) = \sin x - \cos x$ attain its maximum?

- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{7\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

With $a = 1$ and $b = -1$ we have $r = \sqrt{2}$ and the **auxiliary angle** is $\varphi = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, so
$$
f(x) = \sqrt{2}\sin\left( x - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) .
$$
The maximum $\sqrt{2}$ occurs when $x - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, that is, at $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$. At the points $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, by contrast, we have $f(x) = 0$.
