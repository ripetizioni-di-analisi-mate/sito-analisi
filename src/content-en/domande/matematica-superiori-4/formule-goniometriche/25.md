Which term cancels when computing $\cos (a+b)-\cos (a-b)$?

- $\sin a \sin b$
- $\cos a \cos b$
- $2\sin a \sin b$
- $\sin (a+b)$

## Explanation

The product of the cosines is positive in both expansions. It cancels on subtraction, while the sine products give $-2\sin a \sin b$.
