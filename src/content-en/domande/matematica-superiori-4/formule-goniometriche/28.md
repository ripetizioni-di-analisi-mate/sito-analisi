What is $\dfrac{\tan 25^\circ + \tan 20^\circ}{1 - \tan 25^\circ\tan 20^\circ}$?

- $\sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

The expression is the right-hand side of the **tangent sum** formula with $\alpha = 25^\circ$ and $\beta = 20^\circ$. Tangent is defined at $25^\circ$, $20^\circ$, and $45^\circ$, so the formula applies and the expression equals
$$
\tan(25^\circ + 20^\circ) = \tan 45^\circ = 1 .
$$
The values $\sqrt{3}$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ are the tangents of $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$, respectively.
