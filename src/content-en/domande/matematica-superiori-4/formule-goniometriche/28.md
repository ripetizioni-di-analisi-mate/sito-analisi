Lines of slopes $m$ and $-m$, with $0<m<1$, are symmetric about the horizontal axis. What is the tangent of their acute angle?

- $\dfrac{2m}{1+m^2}$
- $m^2$
- $\dfrac{1-m^2}{2m}$
- $\dfrac{2m}{1-m^2}$

## Explanation

The positive inclination is $\alpha=\arctan m$. The angle between the lines is $2\alpha$, acute since $m<1$. The double-angle formula gives the required ratio.
