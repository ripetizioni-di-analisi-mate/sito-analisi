A rotation takes a unit vector from direction $a$ to direction $b$. The determinant of the two columns is $\cos a \sin b-\sin a \cos b$. What does it measure?

- $\cos (b-a)$
- $\sin (b+a)$
- $\sin (b-a)$
- $\cos (b+a)$

## Explanation

Reordering the products as in the sine difference formula gives $\sin b \cos a-\cos b \sin a$. Its sign depends on the orientation of the rotation.
