What is $\tan\dfrac{7\pi}{8}$ when calculated using the formula $\tan\dfrac{\alpha}{2} = \dfrac{1 - \cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$ and $\alpha = \dfrac{7\pi}{4}$?

- $1 - \sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{2} - 1$
- $-1 - \sqrt{2}$
- $1 + \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The angle $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ lies in the fourth quadrant, so $\cos\dfrac{7\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ and $\sin\dfrac{7\pi}{4} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. By the **tangent half-angle** formula,
$$
\tan\frac{7\pi}{8} = \frac{1 - \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} = -\frac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}} = -\left( \sqrt{2} - 1 \right) = 1 - \sqrt{2} .
$$
The result is negative, as expected since $\dfrac{7\pi}{8}$ lies in the second quadrant. The value $\sqrt{2} - 1$ results from taking $\sin\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ to be positive.
