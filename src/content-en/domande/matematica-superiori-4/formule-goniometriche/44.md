What is the distance between the unit-circle points $(1,0)$ and $(\cos x,\sin x)$ if $0\le x\le 2\pi$?

- $2\sin (\dfrac{x}{2})$
- $2\cos (\dfrac{x}{2})$
- $\sin x$
- $1-\cos x$

## Explanation

The squared distance is $(1-\cos x)^2+\sin^2x=2(1-\cos x)=4\sin^2(\dfrac{x}{2})$. On the stated domain the half-angle sine is nonnegative, so taking the square root yields the given distance.
