Given $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$, what is $\tan\left( \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \alpha \right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

Since $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{4} = 1$, the **tangent sum** formula gives
$$
\tan\left( \frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha \right) = \frac{1 + \dfrac{1}{2}}{1 - \dfrac{1}{2}} = \frac{\dfrac{3}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{2}} = 3 .
$$
The value $\dfrac{1}{3}$ is the tangent of $\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \alpha$, and $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is just the numerator in the formula.
