When finding $\tan (a+b)$ we obtain $\tan a\,\tan b=1$, with both tangents defined. What follows for $\cos (a+b)$?

- It equals one.
- It equals minus one.
- It is zero.
- It must be positive.

## Explanation

Factoring gives $\cos (a+b)=\cos a \cos b(1-\tan a \tan b)$. The last factor vanishes, which is why the tangent of the sum is undefined.
