What is the horizontal asymptote of the graph of $y = \left| 3^x - 9 \right|$?

- $y = -9$
- $y = 0$
- $y = 2$
- $y = 9$

## Explanation

The graph of $y = 3^x - 9$ has asymptote $y = -9$ to the left and is negative for $x < 2$ because $3^2 = 9$. The absolute value reflects the part below the $x$-axis with $x < 2$ upward, so the asymptote $y = -9$ becomes the line $y = 9$. To the right the graph increases without bound, so its only horizontal asymptote is $y = 9$.
