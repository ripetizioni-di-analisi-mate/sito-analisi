What is the graph of $y=\ln(e^{-|x|})$ after simplification, for every real $x$?

- A parabola with its vertex at the origin.
- The line $y=x$ on the whole domain.
- The constant $y=1$.
- Two rays meeting at the origin with nonpositive values.

## Explanation

The exponential is always positive and its natural logarithm recovers its exponent. This leaves $y=-\lvert x\rvert$, which has its maximum of zero at the origin.
