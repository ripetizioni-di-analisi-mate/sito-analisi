What is the solution of the equation $2^{x + 2} + 2^{x} = 40$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 3$
- $x = \log_2 10$

## Explanation

Write $2^{x + 2} = 4 \cdot 2^x$ and factor out $2^x$, so the equation becomes $2^x (4 + 1) = 40$. It follows that $2^x = 8 = 2^3$, and hence $x = 3$. Substitution gives $2^5 + 2^3 = 32 + 8 = 40$.
