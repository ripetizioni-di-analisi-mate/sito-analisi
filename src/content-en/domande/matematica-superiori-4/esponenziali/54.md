A table gives $f(0)=3$, $f(1)=6$ and $f(2)=11$. Which observation rules out a model $f(x)=Ca^x$?

- All the readings are positive.
- The first input is zero.
- The ratios of consecutive readings differ.
- The second reading is even.

## Explanation

For steps of one the ratio should always be $a$. The first pair gives $2$, predicting a third reading of $12$ whereas the observed value is $11$.
