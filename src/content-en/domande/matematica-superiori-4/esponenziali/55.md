Without expanding the powers, how can you determine the sign of $7^{x+1}-8\cdot7^x$?

- It is negative for every $x$.
- It is positive for every $x$.
- It changes sign at $x=0$.
- It vanishes for every $x$.

## Explanation

Factor out the positive term $7^x$. The remaining factor is $7-8=-1$, so the product is always negative.
