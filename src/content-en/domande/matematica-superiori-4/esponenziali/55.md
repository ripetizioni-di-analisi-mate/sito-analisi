What is the solution of the equation $3^{x + 1} - 3^{x} - 3^{x - 1} = 45$?

- $x = 3$
- $x = 2$
- $x = 4$
- $x = 1$

## Explanation

Write each power as a multiple of $3^x$ and factor it out:
$$
3^x \left( 3 - 1 - \frac{1}{3} \right) = 45
\iff
3^x \cdot \frac{5}{3} = 45
\iff
3^x = 27 .
$$
Since $27 = 3^3$, the solution is $x = 3$. Substitution gives $81 - 27 - 9 = 45$.
