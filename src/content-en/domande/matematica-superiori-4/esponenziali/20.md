Let $a > 0$. Which expression equals $\dfrac{a^{\frac{1}{2}} \cdot a^{\frac{2}{3}}}{a^{\frac{1}{6}}}$?

- $a$
- $a^2$
- $a^{\frac{4}{3}}$
- $a^{\frac{2}{3}}$

## Explanation

With a positive base, the exponent laws also hold for rational exponents. Add the exponents in the numerator and subtract the exponent in the denominator. The resulting exponent is therefore
$$
\frac{1}{2} + \frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3 + 4 - 1}{6} = 1 .
$$
Hence the expression equals $a^1 = a$.
