The function $y=2^x$ is plotted in the plane with coordinates $(x,Y)$, where $Y=\log_2 y$. What is obtained?

- A parabola.
- A hyperbola.
- A horizontal line.
- A line of slope $1$.

## Explanation

The base-$2$ logarithm is the inverse function of $2^x$, so for every point of the graph $Y=\log_2 y=\log_2\left(2^x\right)=x$. The plotted points therefore have coordinates $(x,x)$. Hence one obtains the line $Y=x$, which has **slope** $1$.
