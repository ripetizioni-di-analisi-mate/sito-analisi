Under the shift taking the graph of $y = 5^x$ to that of $y = 5^{x - 2} + 3$, where does the point $(0, 1)$ move?

- $(-2, 4)$
- $(2, 1)$
- $(-2, -2)$
- $(2, 4)$

## Explanation

The graph of $y = 5^{x - 2} + 3$ is obtained from that of $5^x$ by shifting $2$ units to the right and $3$ units up. Therefore $(0, 1)$ moves to $(0 + 2, 1 + 3) = (2, 4)$. Indeed, for $x = 2$ we have $5^0 + 3 = 4$.
