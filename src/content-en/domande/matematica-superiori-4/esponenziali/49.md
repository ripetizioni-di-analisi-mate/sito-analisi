A camera records $y=2^x$ with equally spaced powers of two on the vertical scale. What shape does the trace have in coordinates $(x,\log_2 y)$?

- A parabola.
- A hyperbola.
- A horizontal line.
- A straight line of slope $1$.

## Explanation

The value plotted vertically is $\log_2(2^x)=x$. The recorded coordinates are therefore $(x,x)$ and lie on the line with slope one through the origin.
