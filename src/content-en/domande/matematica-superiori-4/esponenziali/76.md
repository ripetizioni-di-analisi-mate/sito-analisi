A model $T(t)=18+42\,(\dfrac{1}{2})^{t/4}$ describes an object's temperature in a room. Which quantity halves every four hours?

- $T(t)$
- $18-T(0)$
- $T(t)+18$
- $T(t)-18$

## Explanation

Room temperature is the constant level $18$. The difference $T(t)-18$ contains only the exponential term, which is multiplied by $\dfrac{1}{2}$ over four hours.
