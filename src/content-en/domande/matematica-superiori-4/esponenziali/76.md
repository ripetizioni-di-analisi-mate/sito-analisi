What is the solution of the equation $4^x = 8$, that is, the number $\log_4 8$?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The number $8$ is a known power of $2$, as is $4$, so the logarithm is rational. Since $4^x = 2^{2x}$ and $8 = 2^3$, the equation becomes $2x = 3$. Therefore $\log_4 8 = \dfrac{3}{2}$, and indeed $4^{\frac{3}{2}} = \left( \sqrt{4} \right)^3 = 8$.
