A population grows according to the law $N(t) = N_0 \, e^{0.05 t}$, where $t$ is measured in years. What is the approximate doubling time?

- $40$ years
- $13.9$ years
- $20$ years
- $0.035$ years

## Explanation

The doubling time is the time $T$ for which $e^{0.05 T} = 2$. By the definition of the natural logarithm, $0.05 \, T = \ln 2$. Therefore
$$
T = \frac{\ln 2}{0.05} \approx \frac{0.693}{0.05} \approx 13.9 \text{ years} .
$$
