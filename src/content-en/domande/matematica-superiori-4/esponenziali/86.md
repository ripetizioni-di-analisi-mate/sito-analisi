An image loses $10\%$ of its brightness with each copy. To return to the initial brightness after one copy, by what percentage of the remaining brightness must it be increased?

- $10\%$
- $9\%$
- $\dfrac{100}{9}\%$
- $90\%$

## Explanation

An initial $100$ becomes $90$. Recovering $10$ units relative to $90$ requires the fraction $\dfrac{10}{90}=\dfrac{1}{9}$, or $\dfrac{100}{9}$ percent.
