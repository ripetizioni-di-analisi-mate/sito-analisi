A population of $5000$ individuals decreases by $20\%$ each year. How many individuals are there after $2$ years?

- $3000$
- $4000$
- $3200$
- $7200$

## Explanation

A decrease of $20\%$ corresponds to a factor of $q = 1 - \dfrac{20}{100} = 0.8$, so after $n$ years the population is $N_n = 5000 \cdot 0.8^n$. For $n = 2$ this gives $5000 \cdot 0.64 = 3200$ individuals. Each year the percentage applies to the previous year's population, so the second decrease of $800$ individuals is smaller than the first decrease of $1000$ individuals.
