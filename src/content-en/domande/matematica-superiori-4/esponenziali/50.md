What is the solution of the equation $8^{x} = \dfrac{1}{16}$?

- $x = -\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $x = \dfrac{4}{3}$
- $x = -2$
- $x = -\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

Write both sides as powers with base $2$. Since $8 = 2^3$ and $\dfrac{1}{16} = 2^{-4}$, the equation becomes $2^{3x} = 2^{-4}$. Equating the exponents gives $3x = -4$, so $x = -\dfrac{4}{3}$.
