Which values can $2^{1/x}$ take for real nonzero $x$?

- $(0,+\infty)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$
- $(0,1)\cup(1,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The reciprocal $1/x$ takes every nonzero real value. The exponential takes every positive value except $1$, which would require a zero exponent.
