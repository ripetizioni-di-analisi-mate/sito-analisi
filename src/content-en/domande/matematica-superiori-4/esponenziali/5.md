- $x = 1$
- $x = 2$
- $x = 3$
- $x = \log_5 30$

## Explanation

Write $5^{x + 1} = 5 \cdot 5^x$ and $5^{x - 1} = \dfrac{5^x}{5}$, then factor out $5^x$:
$$
5^x \left( 5 - \frac{1}{5} \right) = 120
\iff
5^x \cdot \frac{24}{5} = 120
\iff
5^x = 25 .
$$
Since $25 = 5^2$, the equation has the solution $x = 2$. Substitution gives $5^3 - 5^1 = 125 - 5 = 120$.
