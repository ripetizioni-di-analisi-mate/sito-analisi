Given $2^{\sqrt{2}} \approx 2.665$, what is the approximate value of $4^{\sqrt{2}}$?

- $5.33$
- $4.665$
- $1.63$
- $7.10$

## Explanation

Since $4 = 2^2$, the exponent laws for real exponents give $4^{\sqrt{2}} = \left( 2^2 \right)^{\sqrt{2}} = 2^{2\sqrt{2}} = \left( 2^{\sqrt{2}} \right)^2$. Therefore $4^{\sqrt{2}} \approx 2.665^2 \approx 7.10$. Doubling $2.665$ would give $2 \cdot 2^{\sqrt{2}} = 2^{1 + \sqrt{2}}$, which is a different number.
