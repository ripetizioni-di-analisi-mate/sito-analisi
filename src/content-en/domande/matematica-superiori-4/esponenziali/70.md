What is the solution of the equation $2^{x + 1} = 7$?

- $x = \log_2 7 + 1$
- $x = \log_7 2 - 1$
- $x = \log_2 7 - 1$
- $x = \dfrac{\log_2 7}{2}$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, $2^{x + 1} = 7$ is equivalent to $x + 1 = \log_2 7$. Therefore $x = \log_2 7 - 1$. Since $4 < 7 < 8$, we have $2 < \log_2 7 < 3$, so the solution lies between $1$ and $2$.
