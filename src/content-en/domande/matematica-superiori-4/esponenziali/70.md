Two cultures start at the same time with $90$ and $10$ units. Each hour the first doubles and the second grows by a factor of $6$. After how many hours are their sizes equal?

- $3$
- $6$
- $2$
- $9$

## Explanation

Equate $90\cdot2^t$ and $10\cdot6^t$ and divide by $10\cdot2^t$ to obtain $3^t=9$. The starting gap is closed in two hours.
