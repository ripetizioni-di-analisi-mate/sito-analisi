A bisection method looks for a zero of $g(x)=2^x+x-4$ on $[1,2]$. Given $\sqrt 8>2.5$, which interval is retained after the first step?

- $[1.5,2]$
- $[1,1.5]$
- $[0,1]$
- $[2,3]$

## Explanation

We have $g(1)=-1$ and $g(1.5)=\sqrt8-2.5>0$. Continuity gives a zero between these two points, and the increasing function has only one.
