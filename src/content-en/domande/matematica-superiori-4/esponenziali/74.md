Which interval contains the solution of the equation $3^x = 5 - x$?

- $(0, 1)$
- $(1, 2)$
- $(2, 3)$
- $(-1, 0)$

## Explanation

The function $3^x$ is increasing and $5 - x$ is decreasing, so the equation has at most one solution. For $x = 1$ we have $3^1 = 3 < 4 = 5 - 1$, whereas for $x = 2$ we have $3^2 = 9 > 3 = 5 - 2$. The difference $3^x - (5 - x)$ changes sign between $1$ and $2$, so the solution lies in the interval $(1, 2)$.
