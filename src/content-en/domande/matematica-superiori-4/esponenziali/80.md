What are the solutions of the inequality $3^{x^2} \leq 81$?

- $x \leq 2$
- $x \leq -2$ or $x \geq 2$
- $-4 \leq x \leq 4$
- $-2 \leq x \leq 2$

## Explanation

Since $81 = 3^4$, the inequality can be written $3^{x^2} \leq 3^4$. The base $3$ exceeds $1$, so the direction is preserved and we obtain $x^2 \leq 4$. Therefore the solutions are $-2 \leq x \leq 2$.
