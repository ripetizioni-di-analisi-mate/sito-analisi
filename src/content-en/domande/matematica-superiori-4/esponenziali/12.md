- $0 < k < 1$

## Explanation

With $t = 2^x$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t^2 - 2t + k = 0$. Each positive solution in $t$ gives exactly one solution in $x$, so we need two distinct positive solutions in $t$. The conditions that must hold simultaneously are
$$
\begin{cases}
\Delta = 4 - 4k > 0 \\
t_1 \, t_2 = k > 0 \\
t_1 + t_2 = 2 > 0
\end{cases}
$$
The third condition always holds, so the equation has two distinct real solutions for $0 < k < 1$.
