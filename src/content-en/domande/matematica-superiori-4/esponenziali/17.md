A worksheet gives $16^{\dfrac{3}{4}}=\dfrac{16^3}{4}$. Which number can be raised to the fourth power to check the correct answer directly?

- $12$
- $48$
- $8$
- $64$

## Explanation

The denominator of the exponent specifies a fourth root. The required positive number has fourth power $16^3=2^{12}$, so it is $2^3=8$.
