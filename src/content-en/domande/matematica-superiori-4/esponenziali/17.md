What is the value of $\left( \dfrac{4}{9} \right)^{-\frac{3}{2}}$?

- $\dfrac{8}{27}$
- $-\dfrac{27}{8}$
- $\dfrac{27}{8}$
- $\dfrac{81}{16}$

## Explanation

Taking the reciprocal of the base removes the negative sign from the exponent, so $\left( \dfrac{4}{9} \right)^{-\frac{3}{2}} = \left( \dfrac{9}{4} \right)^{\frac{3}{2}}$. The square root of $\dfrac{9}{4}$ is $\dfrac{3}{2}$. Therefore the value is $\left( \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^3 = \dfrac{27}{8}$.
