- $x = 3$
- $x = 6$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 9$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is $\left( 3^{x + 3} \right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = 3^{\frac{x + 3}{2}}$. Since $9^x = 3^{2x}$ and $27 = 3^3$, the right-hand side is $3^{2x - 3}$. Equating the exponents gives $\dfrac{x + 3}{2} = 2x - 3$, that is, $x + 3 = 4x - 6$, so $x = 3$. At $x = 3$ both sides equal $27$.
