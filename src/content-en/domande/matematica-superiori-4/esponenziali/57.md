Which of the following equations has no real solutions?

- $2^{x} - 3 = 1$
- $2^{-x} = 3$
- $2^{x} + 3 = 1$
- $3^{x} = \dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The elementary equation $a^x = b$ has no solution if $b \leq 0$ because $a^x > 0$ for every $x$. The equation $2^x + 3 = 1$ is equivalent to $2^x = -2$, so it **has no solutions**. The other three have positive right-hand sides: $2^x = 4$ gives $x = 2$, $2^{-x} = 3$ gives $x = -\log_2 3$, and $3^x = \dfrac{1}{9}$ gives $x = -2$.
