What is the solution of the equation $4^{x} \cdot 8^{x - 1} = 2^{7}$?

- $x = 2$
- $x = \dfrac{4}{5}$
- $x = 1$
- $x = \dfrac{7}{5}$

## Explanation

Since $4 = 2^2$ and $8 = 2^3$, the left-hand side is $2^{2x} \cdot 2^{3x - 3} = 2^{5x - 3}$. The equation becomes $2^{5x - 3} = 2^7$, so $5x - 3 = 7$. Therefore $5x = 10$, that is, $x = 2$.
