A device uses repeated squaring and stores $a^2,a^4,a^8$. Which stored values should be multiplied to obtain $a^{12}$ for every $a>0$?

- $a^4$ and $a^8$
- $a^2$ and $a^8$
- $a^2$ and $a^4$
- All three once each.

## Explanation

With the same base, multiplication adds exponents. The decomposition $12=8+4$ uses two stored results and produces the required power exactly.
