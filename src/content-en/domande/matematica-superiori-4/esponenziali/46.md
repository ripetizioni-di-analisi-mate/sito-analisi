Which transformation produces the graph of $y = 2^{x + 3} - 1$ from the graph of $y = 2^x$?

- A shift of $3$ units to the right and $1$ unit down
- A shift of $3$ units to the left and $1$ unit down
- A shift of $3$ units to the left and $1$ unit up
- A shift of $1$ unit to the left and $3$ units down

## Explanation

The function can be written in the form $y = 2^{x - h} + k$ with $h = -3$ and $k = -1$. Its graph is therefore obtained by shifting the graph of $2^x$ by $h = -3$ units horizontally, that is, $3$ units to the left, and $k = -1$ units vertically, that is, $1$ unit down. Indeed, the point $(0, 1)$ on the graph of $2^x$ moves to $(-3, 0)$, and $2^{-3 + 3} - 1 = 0$.
