Which of the following numbers cannot be written in the form $2^n$ with $n$ an integer?

- $\sqrt2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac18$
- $16$

## Explanation

We have $1=2^0$, $\dfrac{1}{8}=2^{-3}$ and $16=2^4$, so these three numbers are powers of $2$ with integer exponent. On the other hand $\sqrt2=2^{\frac{1}{2}}$. Since the function $2^x$ is injective, the only exponent giving $\sqrt2$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, which is not an integer; therefore $\sqrt2$ is **not** of the form $2^n$ with $n$ an integer.
