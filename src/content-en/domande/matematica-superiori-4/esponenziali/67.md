An instrument records $S(x)=2^x$ only for integer $x$, including zero and negative integers. Which positive reading cannot appear in the log?

- $\sqrt2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac18$
- $16$

## Explanation

Possible readings are powers of two with integer exponents. Since $\sqrt2=2^{\dfrac{1}{2}}$ and the exponential is one-to-one, no other exponent gives this value.
