What is the solution of the equation $\sqrt[3]{5^{x}} = 25^{x - 5}$?

- $x = \dfrac{15}{2}$
- $x = 6$
- $x = -6$
- $x = 2$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is $\left( 5^x \right)^{\frac{1}{3}} = 5^{\frac{x}{3}}$ and the right-hand side is $\left( 5^2 \right)^{x - 5} = 5^{2x - 10}$. Equating the exponents gives $\dfrac{x}{3} = 2x - 10$, that is, $x = 6x - 30$. Hence $5x = 30$, so $x = 6$. At $x = 6$ both sides equal $25$.
