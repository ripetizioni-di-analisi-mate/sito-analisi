The equation $16^x=2^{3x+1}$ leads to $4x=3x+1$. Which fact justifies equating the exponents?

- The exponents are integers.
- The function $t\mapsto2^t$ is one-to-one.
- Every power has a base larger than its exponent.
- The square of a number is positive.

## Explanation

Rewriting $16^x$ as $2^{4x}$ gives the same base on both sides. Strict monotonicity means distinct inputs give different outputs.
