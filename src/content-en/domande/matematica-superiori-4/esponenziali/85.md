A greenhouse records alternating monthly increases of $20\%$ and decreases of $20\%$. Which factor applies after each pair of months?

- $1$
- $0.96$
- $0.8$
- $1.04$

## Explanation

The second change acts on the new value. The factor for the two months is $1.2\cdot0.8=0.96$, equivalent to a $4\%$ decrease.
