A principal of $1000$ euros is invested at an annual compound interest rate of $2\%$. What is the accumulated amount after $2$ years?

- $1040.00$ euros
- $1040.40$ euros
- $1020.00$ euros
- $1004.00$ euros

## Explanation

With compound interest, the accumulated amount after $n$ years is $M = C \, (1 + i)^n$. Here $C = 1000$, $i = 0.02$ and $n = 2$, so the balance is $M = 1000 \cdot 1.02^2 = 1000 \cdot 1.0404 = 1040.40$ euros. The value $1040$ euros corresponds to simple interest, which does not take account of the first year's interest.
