Which of the following numbers is the smallest?

- $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{1.2}$
- $4^{-0.7}$
- $2^{-1.5}$
- $8^{-0.4}$

## Explanation

Write the numbers as powers with base $2$. We have $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{1.2} = 2^{-1.2}$, $4^{-0.7} = 2^{-1.4}$ and $8^{-0.4} = 2^{-1.2}$, so the exponents are $-1.5$, $-1.2$, $-1.4$ and $-1.2$. The base $2$ is greater than $1$, so the smallest number has the smallest exponent, namely $2^{-1.5}$.
