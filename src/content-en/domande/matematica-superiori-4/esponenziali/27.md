You know that $a^{1.41}<a^{\sqrt2}<a^{1.42}$. Why can more digits of $\sqrt2$ give a narrower interval when $a>1$?

- Powers with irrational exponents are integers.
- Each extra digit multiplies the power by $10$.
- The function $x\mapsto a^x$ is increasing and continuous.
- We have $a^x=ax$ for irrational $x$.

## Explanation

Monotonicity preserves the order of the rational bounds. Continuity ensures that the two powers approach the same value as their exponents approach $\sqrt2$.
