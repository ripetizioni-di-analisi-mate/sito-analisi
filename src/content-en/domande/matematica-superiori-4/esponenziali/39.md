What is the equation of the tangent line to the graph of $y = e^x$ at $(0, 1)$?

- $y = x + 1$
- $y = e x + 1$
- $y = x$
- $y = e x$

## Explanation

The function $e^x$ is the only exponential function whose tangent line at $(0, 1)$ has slope $1$. The line passes through $(0, 1)$, so its vertical intercept is $1$. Therefore the tangent line is $y = x + 1$.
