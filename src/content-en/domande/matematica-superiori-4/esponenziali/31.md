Which property distinguishes $g(x)=2^x$ from $h(x)=x^2$ on the positive real numbers?

- Increasing $x$ by one always doubles $g$.
- Both functions are decreasing.
- Only $g$ takes the value $4$.
- Only $h$ is positive.

## Explanation

For every $x$, $2^{x+1}/2^x=2$. For the power function the ratio is $(x+1)^2/x^2$, which is $4$ at $x=1$ and $\dfrac{9}{4}$ at $x=2$.
