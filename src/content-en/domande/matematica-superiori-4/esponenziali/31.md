For which values of the real parameter $k$ is $f(x) = (2k - 1)^x$ a decreasing exponential function?

- $\dfrac{1}{2} < k < 1$
- $k < 1$
- $0 < k < 1$
- $k < \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

An exponential function is decreasing when its base is between $0$ and $1$. The conditions that must hold simultaneously are
$$
\begin{cases}
2k - 1 > 0 \\
2k - 1 < 1
\end{cases}
$$
The first gives $k > \dfrac{1}{2}$ and the second gives $k < 1$, so the function is decreasing for $\dfrac{1}{2} < k < 1$.
