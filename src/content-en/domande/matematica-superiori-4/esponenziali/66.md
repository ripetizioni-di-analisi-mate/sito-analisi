In the equation $4^x - 2^x - 2 = 0$ the substitution $t = 2^x$ gives $t = 2$ and $t = -1$. What are the solutions of the original equation?

- $x = 1$ or $x = -1$
- $x = 0$ or $x = 1$
- Only $x = 1$
- The equation has no solutions

## Explanation

For each solution in $t$, return to the elementary equation $2^x = t$. The value $t = 2$ gives $2^x = 2$, so $x = 1$. The value $t = -1$ gives $2^x = -1$, which has no solution because $2^x > 0$ for every $x$. Therefore the equation has the unique solution $x = 1$.
