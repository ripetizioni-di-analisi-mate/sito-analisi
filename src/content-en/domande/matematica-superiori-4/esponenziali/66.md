A table gives $f(0)=5$, $f(1)=7$ and $f(2)=11$. Which model with a horizontal asymptote matches all three readings?

- $f(x)=2+3\cdot2^x$
- $f(x)=3+2^x$
- $f(x)=3+2\cdot2^x$
- $f(x)=5\cdot2^x$

## Explanation

The successive increments are $2$ and $4$, so they double. Subtract the level $3$ to obtain the geometric sequence $2,4,8$, given by $2\cdot2^x$.
