What is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $10^{-n}<0.005$?

- $2$
- $1$
- No positive integer.
- $3$

## Explanation

We have $10^{-1}=0.1$ and $10^{-2}=0.01$, and both are greater than $0.005$. On the other hand $10^{-3}=0.001<0.005$. The function $10^{-n}=\left(\dfrac{1}{10}\right)^n$ is decreasing, because the base lies between $0$ and $1$, so the inequality holds for every $n\geq3$. The smallest integer is therefore $n=3$.
