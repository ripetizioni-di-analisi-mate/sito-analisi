An instrument displays two decimal places and rounds to the nearest hundredth. For $f(n)=10^{-n}$ with positive integer $n$, what is the first $n$ for which the display shows zero even though $f(n)>0$?

- $2$
- $1$
- It never happens.
- $3$

## Explanation

For $n=2$ the display still reads $0{,}01$. At the next step the exact value is $0{,}001$, which rounds to $0{,}00$. The display has lost the distinction between a small positive value and zero.
