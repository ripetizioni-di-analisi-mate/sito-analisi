How many real solutions does the equation $2^x = -x^2 - 1$ have?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is positive for every $x$ because the range of the exponential function is $(0, +\infty)$. The right-hand side satisfies $-x^2 - 1 \leq -1$ for every $x$ and is therefore always negative. The two graphs lie on opposite sides of the $x$-axis and do not intersect. Hence the equation **has no solutions**.
