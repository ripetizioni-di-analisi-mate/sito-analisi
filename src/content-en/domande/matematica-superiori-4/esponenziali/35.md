At times $0,1,2,3$ the exact readings are $1,2,4,8$. Is that enough to conclude that an unknown law equals $2^x$ at every real time?

- Yes, four points determine every function.
- No, no exponential can take those values.
- Yes, continuity alone is enough.
- No, different laws can agree at those four points.

## Explanation

For example, $2^x+x(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)$ fits the readings but differs between them. If a model of the form $Ca^x$ is assumed, the first two readings force $C=1$ and $a=2$.
