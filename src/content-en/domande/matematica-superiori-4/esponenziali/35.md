For which of the following values of $x$ does $3^x < 2^x$ hold?

- $x = 0$
- $x = 1$
- $x = 2$
- $x = -1$

## Explanation

Let $1 < a < b$. For $x > 0$ we have $a^x < b^x$, whereas for $x < 0$ the inequality is reversed. With $a = 2$ and $b = 3$, therefore, $3^x < 2^x$ holds only for $x < 0$. Indeed, $3^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{3} < \dfrac{1}{2} = 2^{-1}$, whereas for $x = 0$ both powers equal $1$.
