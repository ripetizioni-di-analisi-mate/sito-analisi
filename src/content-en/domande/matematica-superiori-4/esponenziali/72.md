What is the solution of the equation $2^x = 5^{x - 1}$?

- $x = \dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 2 - \ln 5}$
- $x = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 5 - \ln 2}$
- $x = \dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 5 - \ln 2}$
- $x = \dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 5 + \ln 2}$

## Explanation

The bases $2$ and $5$ cannot be written as powers of a common base, so take the natural logarithm of both sides. Applying the rule $\ln a^x = x \ln a$ gives $x \ln 2 = (x - 1) \ln 5$. Moving the terms involving $x$ to the left-hand side gives $x \left( \ln 5 - \ln 2 \right) = \ln 5$, so
$$
x = \frac{\ln 5}{\ln 5 - \ln 2} \approx 1.76 .
$$
