What are the solutions of the equation $4^x - 3 \cdot 6^x + 2 \cdot 9^x = 0$?

- $x = 1$ or $x = 2$
- Only $x = 0$
- $x = 0$ or $x = \log_{\frac{3}{2}} 2$
- $x = 0$ or $x = \log_{\frac{2}{3}} 2$

## Explanation

Divide both sides by the positive quantity $9^x$. Since $\dfrac{4^x}{9^x} = \left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{2x}$ and $\dfrac{6^x}{9^x} = \left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{x}$, setting $t = \left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^x$ gives the equation $t^2 - 3t + 2 = 0$. The solutions are $t = 1$ and $t = 2$, both positive. From $\left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^x = 1$ we obtain $x = 0$ and from $\left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^x = 2$ we obtain $x = \log_{\frac{2}{3}} 2$.
