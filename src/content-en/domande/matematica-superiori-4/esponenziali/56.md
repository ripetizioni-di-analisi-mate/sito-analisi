What is the solution of the equation $3^{2x - 5} = 7^{2x - 5}$?

- $x = \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $x = 0$
- $x = \dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x = 5$

## Explanation

Divide both sides by the positive quantity $7^{2x - 5}$ to obtain $\left( \dfrac{3}{7} \right)^{2x - 5} = 1 = \left( \dfrac{3}{7} \right)^{0}$. The base $\dfrac{3}{7}$ is different from $1$, so the equation is equivalent to $2x - 5 = 0$. Therefore the solution is $x = \dfrac{5}{2}$, at which both sides equal $1$.
