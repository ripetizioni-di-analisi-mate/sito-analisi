Both sides of the inequality $2^x<3$ are multiplied by $-2^x$. Which inequality is obtained?

- $-4^x>-3\cdot2^x$
- $-4^x<-3\cdot2^x$
- $-4^x=-3\cdot2^x$
- It depends on the sign of $x$.

## Explanation

For every real $x$ we have $2^x>0$, so $-2^x<0$. When both sides of an inequality are multiplied by a negative number the inequality is reversed, so one obtains $-2^x\cdot2^x>-3\cdot2^x$. Since $2^x\cdot2^x=(2\cdot2)^x=4^x$, the inequality becomes $-4^x>-3\cdot2^x$.
