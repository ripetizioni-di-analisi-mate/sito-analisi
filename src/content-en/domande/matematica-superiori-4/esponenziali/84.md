Software multiplies $2^x<3$ by $-2^x$ and reports $-4^x<-3\cdot2^x$. Which sign is correct after the multiplication?

- $-4^x>-3\cdot2^x$
- $-4^x<-3\cdot2^x$
- $-4^x=-3\cdot2^x$
- It depends on the sign of $x$.

## Explanation

We have $2^x>0$ for every real input, so the multiplier is always negative. The inequality reverses, and $2^x2^x=4^x$.
