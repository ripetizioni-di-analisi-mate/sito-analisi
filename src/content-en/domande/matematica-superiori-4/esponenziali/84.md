What are the solutions of the inequality $4^x - 2^{x + 1} - 8 > 0$?

- $x > 2$
- $x < 2$
- $x > 4$
- $x < -1$ or $x > 2$

## Explanation

With $t = 2^x$, $t > 0$, we have $2^{x + 1} = 2t$ and the inequality becomes $t^2 - 2t - 8 > 0$, that is, $(t - 4)(t + 2) > 0$. It holds for $t < -2$ or $t > 4$. Imposing the condition $t > 0$ leaves only $t > 4$, that is, $2^x > 2^2$, so $x > 2$.
