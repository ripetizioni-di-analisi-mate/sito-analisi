What are the solutions of the equation $9^x - 4 \cdot 3^x + 3 = 0$?

- $x = 1$ or $x = 3$
- Only $x = 1$
- $x = 0$ or $x = 3$
- $x = 0$ or $x = 1$

## Explanation

With the substitution $t = 3^x$, $t > 0$, we have $9^x = t^2$ and the equation becomes $t^2 - 4t + 3 = 0$, that is, $(t - 1)(t - 3) = 0$. Both solutions $t = 1$ and $t = 3$ are positive. From $3^x = 1$ we obtain $x = 0$ and from $3^x = 3$ we obtain $x = 1$.
