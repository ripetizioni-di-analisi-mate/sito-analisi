Between which consecutive integers does the solution of the equation $3^x = 20$ lie?

- Between $3$ and $4$
- Between $6$ and $7$
- Between $1$ and $2$
- Between $2$ and $3$

## Explanation

The equation has the unique solution $x = \log_3 20$ because $20 > 0$ and the function $3^x$ is one-to-one. Since $3^2 = 9 < 20 < 27 = 3^3$ and the function $3^x$ is increasing, the solution lies between $2$ and $3$. Its approximate value is $\log_3 20 \approx 2.73$.
