- $x = 1$ or $x = 8$
- $x = 0$ or $x = 3$
- $x = 1$ or $x = 3$
- Only $x = 3$

## Explanation

With the substitution $t = 2^x$, where $t > 0$, we have $4^x = t^2$ and the equation becomes $t^2 - 9t + 8 = 0$, that is, $(t - 1)(t - 8) = 0$. Both solutions $t = 1$ and $t = 8$ are positive, so both are admissible. From $2^x = 1 = 2^0$ we obtain $x = 0$, and from $2^x = 8 = 2^3$ we obtain $x = 3$.
