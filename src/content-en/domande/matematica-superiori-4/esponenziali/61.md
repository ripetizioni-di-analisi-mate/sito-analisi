In the model $N(t)=N_0 e^{kt}$ with $N_0,k>0$, time $t$ is measured in hours. If a sensor records minutes $s$ and uses the form $N_0 e^{hs}$ to describe the same quantities, what must $h$ equal?

- $60k$
- $k$
- $k/60$
- $k/3600$

## Explanation

A duration of $s$ minutes is $s/60$ hours. Substituting in the exponent gives $kt=k(s/60)=(k/60)s$, preserving the predicted value.
