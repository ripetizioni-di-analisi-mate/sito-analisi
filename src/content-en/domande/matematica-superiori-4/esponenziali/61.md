What are the solutions of the equation $4^x + 2^x - 6 = 0$?

- Only $x = 2$
- $x = 1$ or $x = \log_2 3$
- Only $x = 1$
- Only $x = -1$

## Explanation

With $t = 2^x$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t^2 + t - 6 = 0$, that is, $(t + 3)(t - 2) = 0$. The solution $t = -3$ is inadmissible because $2^x$ is positive for every $x$. This leaves $2^x = 2$, so the equation has the unique solution $x = 1$.
