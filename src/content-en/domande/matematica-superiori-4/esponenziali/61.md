In the model $N(t)=N_0e^{kt}$, with $N_0,k>0$, time $t$ is measured in hours. If time is measured in minutes, with $s=60t$, the model is written $N=N_0e^{hs}$. What is the value of $h$?

- $60k$
- $k$
- $\dfrac{k}{60}$
- $\dfrac{k}{3600}$

## Explanation

Since $s=60t$, a time of $s$ minutes corresponds to $t=\dfrac{s}{60}$ hours. Substituting into the exponent gives $kt=k\cdot\dfrac{s}{60}=\dfrac{k}{60}\,s$. The model therefore becomes $N=N_0e^{\frac{k}{60}s}$, so $h=\dfrac{k}{60}$.
