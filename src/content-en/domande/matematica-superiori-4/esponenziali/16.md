What is the value of $32^{\frac{3}{5}}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{96}{5}$
- $8$
- $6$

## Explanation

By the definition of a power with a rational exponent, $32^{\frac{3}{5}} = \left( \sqrt[5]{32} \right)^3$. Since $2^5 = 32$, the fifth root of $32$ is $2$. Therefore $32^{\frac{3}{5}} = 2^3 = 8$.
