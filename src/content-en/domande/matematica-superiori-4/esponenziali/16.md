To compare $7^{\frac{2}{3}}$ and $5^{\frac{3}{4}}$ without decimals, which comparison of integer powers is equivalent?

- $7^2$ with $5^3$
- $7^3$ with $5^4$
- $7^8$ with $5^9$
- $7^6$ with $5^{12}$

## Explanation

Both quantities are positive, so raising them to the common denominator $12$ preserves their order. The exponents become $12\cdot \dfrac{2}{3}=8$ and $12\cdot \dfrac{3}{4}=9$.
