Which of the following numbers is the largest?

- $\left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{-\sqrt{5}}$
- $9^{1.2}$
- $27^{0.7}$
- $3^{2}$

## Explanation

Write the numbers as powers with base $3$. We have $\left( \dfrac{1}{3} \right)^{-\sqrt{5}} = 3^{\sqrt{5}}$, $9^{1.2} = 3^{2.4}$ and $27^{0.7} = 3^{2.1}$. The exponents are $\sqrt{5} \approx 2.236$, $2.4$, $2.1$ and $2$. Since the base $3$ is greater than $1$, the largest number has the greatest exponent, namely $9^{1.2}$.
