An exponent $x$ is rounded to $x+0.01$. When estimating $10^x$, which quantity is independent of $x$?

- The difference between the estimate and the exact value.
- The ratio of the estimate to the exact value.
- The sum of the two values.
- The exact value divided by $x+1$ wherever defined.

## Explanation

The estimate is $10^x10^{0.01}$. Dividing by the exact value gives the constant $10^{0.01}$, while the difference is $10^x(10^{0.01}-1)$.
