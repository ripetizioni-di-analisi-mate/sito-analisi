To calculate $2^{1.37}$, you already know $2^{1.3}=u$ and $2^{1.4}=v$. Which expression is exact?

- $\dfrac{3u+7v}{10}$
- $u^{\dfrac{7}{10}}v^{\dfrac{3}{10}}$
- $u^{\dfrac{3}{10}}v^{\dfrac{7}{10}}$
- $uv$

## Explanation

The exponent $1.37$ equals $0.3\cdot1.3+0.7\cdot1.4$. Adding exponents corresponds to multiplying powers, giving $u^{\dfrac{3}{10}}v^{\dfrac{7}{10}}$.
