What are the solutions of the inequality $3^{2x - 1} < 27$?

- $x < 2$
- $x > 2$
- $x < 1$
- $x < \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $27 = 3^3$, the inequality can be written $3^{2x - 1} < 3^3$. The base $3$ exceeds $1$, so the direction is preserved and we obtain $2x - 1 < 3$. Therefore $2x < 4$, that is, $x < 2$.
