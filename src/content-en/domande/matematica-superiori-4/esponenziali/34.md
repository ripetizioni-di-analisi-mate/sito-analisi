The point $(3, 8)$ lies on the graph of $y = 2^x$. Which point lies on the graph of $y = \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^x$?

- $(8, 3)$
- $(-3, 8)$
- $(3, -8)$
- $\left( -3, \dfrac{1}{8} \right)$

## Explanation

By the exponent laws, $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^x = 2^{-x}$. The value of $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^x$ at $-3$ therefore equals the value of $2^x$ at $3$, namely $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{-3} = 2^3 = 8$. Hence $(-3, 8)$ lies on the graph of $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^x$, which is the reflection of the graph of $2^x$ across the $y$-axis.
