Two graphs $y=a^x$ and $y=b^x$ with $1<a<b$ intersect at $x=0$. How does their order change across this point?

- $a^x>b^x$ for $x<0$, then $a^x<b^x$ for $x>0$.
- $a^x<b^x$ on both sides.
- $a^x>b^x$ on both sides.
- The curves coincide for $x<0$.

## Explanation

The quotient $b^x/a^x=(b/a)^x$ has base greater than one. It is below one to the left of zero and above one to the right.
