A population of $2000$ individuals grows by $10\%$ every year. After how many whole years does it first exceed $3000$ individuals?

- After $4$ years
- After $5$ years
- After $6$ years
- After $3$ years

## Explanation

The number of individuals is multiplied by $1.1$ each year, so after $n$ years it is $N(n) = 2000 \cdot 1.1^n$. We seek the first integer $n$ for which $1.1^n > \dfrac{3000}{2000} = 1.5$. Since $1.1^4 = 1.4641$ and $1.1^5 = 1.61051$, after $4$ years the population is about $2928$, and after $5$ years it is about $3221$. Therefore the population first exceeds $3000$ individuals after $5$ years.
