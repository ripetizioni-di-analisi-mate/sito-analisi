## Explanation

Write all the factors as powers with base $3$. Since $27 = 3^3$, we have $27^{\frac{2}{3}} = 3^{2} = 9$. Since $9 = 3^2$, we have $9^{-\frac{1}{2}} = 3^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{3}$. Therefore the expression equals
$$
\frac{3^2 \cdot 3^{-1}}{3^{-1}} = 3^{2 - 1 + 1} = 3^2 = 9 .
$$
