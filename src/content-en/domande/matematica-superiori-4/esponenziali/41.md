What is the horizontal asymptote of the graph of $y = 5 - 2^{x - 1}$?

- $y = -5$
- $y = 5$
- $y = 1$
- $y = 0$

## Explanation

The graph of $y = 2^{x - 1}$ is the graph of $2^x$ shifted $1$ unit to the right, and its asymptote is the $x$-axis. The graph of $y = -2^{x - 1}$ is its reflection across the $x$-axis and has the same asymptote $y = 0$. Adding $5$ shifts the graph $5$ units upward, so the horizontal asymptote is the line $y = 5$.
