What are the solutions of the inequality $9^x - 10 \cdot 3^x + 9 < 0$?

- $1 < x < 9$
- $x < 0$ or $x > 2$
- $0 < x < 2$
- $0 < x < 1$

## Explanation

With $t = 3^x$, $t > 0$, the inequality becomes $t^2 - 10t + 9 < 0$, that is, $(t - 1)(t - 9) < 0$, which holds for $1 < t < 9$. These solutions are positive, so the condition $t > 0$ is satisfied. We have $3^0 < 3^x < 3^2$ and the base is greater than $1$, so $0 < x < 2$.
