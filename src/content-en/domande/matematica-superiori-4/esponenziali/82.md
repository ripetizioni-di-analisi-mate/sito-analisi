A graph represents $f(x)=\dfrac{2^x-1}{2^x+1}$. Which argument shows that its values lie strictly between $-1$ and $1$?

- We have $2^x<1$ for every $x$.
- The numerator is always positive.
- For $t=2^x>0$ we have $|t-1|<t+1$.
- The denominator minus the numerator is zero.

## Explanation

For $t\geq1$ compare $t-1$ with $t+1$. For $0<t<1$ compare $1-t$ with $1+t$. In both cases the numerator's absolute value is below the positive denominator.
