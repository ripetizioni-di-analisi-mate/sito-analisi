What are the solutions of the inequality $\dfrac{3^x - 9}{x - 1} \geq 0$?

- $x < 1$ or $x \geq 2$
- $1 < x \leq 2$
- $x \geq 2$
- $x < 1$ or $x > 2$

## Explanation

The denominator vanishes at $x = 1$, which must be excluded. The numerator is positive for $3^x > 9 = 3^2$, that is, for $x > 2$, and vanishes at $x = 2$. The denominator is positive for $x > 1$. The quotient is positive when numerator and denominator have the same sign, that is, for $x < 1$ and for $x > 2$. Including the point $x = 2$, where the quotient equals $0$, gives the solution $x < 1$ or $x \geq 2$.
