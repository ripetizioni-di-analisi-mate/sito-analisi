- $x = \log_5 4$
- $x = \log_4 5$
- $x = \log_5 3$
- $x = \log_5 4$ or $x = \log_5 3$

## Explanation

With $t = 5^x$, $t > 0$, we have $25^x = t^2$ and the equation becomes $t^2 - t - 12 = 0$, that is, $(t - 4)(t + 3) = 0$. The solution $t = -3$ is inadmissible because $5^x$ is positive for every $x$. This leaves $5^x = 4$, and by the definition of a logarithm the solution is $x = \log_5 4$.
