Two substances independently halve every $2$ and $4$ hours respectively. They start at $8$ and $4$ grams. What is their combined mass after four hours?

- $3\,\mathrm g$
- $6\,\mathrm g$
- $8\,\mathrm g$
- $4\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The first substance undergoes two halvings and falls from $8$ to $2$ grams. The second undergoes one and falls from $4$ to $2$ grams. Adding them gives $4$ grams.
