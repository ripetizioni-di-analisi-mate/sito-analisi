A radioactive substance has a half-life of $6$ days. What fraction of the original nuclei remains after $18$ days?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The number of nuclei after a time $t$ is $N(t) = N_0 \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{T}}$. With $t = 18$ days and $T = 6$ days, the number of half-lives elapsed is $\dfrac{18}{6} = 3$. Therefore the fraction of the original nuclei remaining is $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^3 = \dfrac{1}{8}$.
