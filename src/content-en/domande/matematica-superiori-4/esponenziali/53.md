What is the solution of the equation $5^{\frac{x + 1}{x}} = 25$?

- $x = -1$
- $x = 1$
- $x = 2$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The exponent $\dfrac{x + 1}{x}$ is defined for $x \neq 0$. Since $25 = 5^2$, the equation is equivalent to $\dfrac{x + 1}{x} = 2$, that is, $x + 1 = 2x$. Thus $x = 1$, which satisfies the condition $x \neq 0$. Substitution gives $5^2 = 25$.
