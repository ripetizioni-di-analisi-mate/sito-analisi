The graph of $f(x)=2^x+c$ passes through $(0,4)$. Which point must lie on its horizontal asymptote?

- $(0,3)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(3,0)$

## Explanation

The condition $1+c=4$ gives $c=3$. As $x$ tends to $-\infty$, the term $2^x$ tends to zero and the asymptote is the line $y=3$.
