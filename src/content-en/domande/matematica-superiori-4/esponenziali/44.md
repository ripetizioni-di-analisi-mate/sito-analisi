At what point does the graph of $y = 4^{x} - 8$ intersect the $x$-axis?

- $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, 0 \right)$
- $(2, 0)$
- $\left( \dfrac{2}{3}, 0 \right)$
- $(0, -7)$

## Explanation

Points on the $x$-axis have vertical coordinate $0$, so solve $4^x = 8$. Since $4 = 2^2$ and $8 = 2^3$, the equation becomes $2^{2x} = 2^3$, that is, $2x = 3$. Therefore the graph intersects the $x$-axis at $\left( \dfrac{3}{2}, 0 \right)$.
