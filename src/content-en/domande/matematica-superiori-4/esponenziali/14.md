The concentration of a drug in the blood is halved every $4$ hours. Immediately after administration it is $80$ mg/l. After how many hours does the concentration fall to $5$ mg/l?

- $4$ hours
- $64$ hours
- $16$ hours
- $12$ hours

## Explanation

The concentration after $t$ hours is $c(t) = 80 \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{4}}$. The equation $c(t) = 5$ is equivalent to
$$
\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{4}} = \frac{5}{80} = \frac{1}{16} = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{4} .
$$
Equating the exponents gives $\dfrac{t}{4} = 4$, so the time is $t = 16$ hours. In $16$ hours the concentration is halved four times, taking the values $80$, $40$, $20$, $10$ and $5$ mg/l.
