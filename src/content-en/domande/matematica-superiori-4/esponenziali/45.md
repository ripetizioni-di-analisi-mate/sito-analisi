Which of the following functions has a graph symmetric about the $y$-axis?

- $y = 3^{-x}$
- $y = |3^x - 1|$
- $y = -3^x$
- $y = 3^{|x|}$

## Explanation

The graph of $y = 3^{|x|}$ coincides with that of $3^x$ for $x \geq 0$, and for $x < 0$ it is the reflection of this part across the $y$-axis. Indeed, $3^{|-x|} = 3^{|x|}$ for every $x$. By contrast, the graph of $3^{-x}$ is the reflection of the graph of $3^x$ across the $y$-axis, rather than its own reflection. For example, its value is $3$ at $x = -1$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ at $x = 1$.
