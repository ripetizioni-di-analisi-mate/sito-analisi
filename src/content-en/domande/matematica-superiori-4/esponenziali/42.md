What is the range of the function $f(x) = 3^{x + 2} - 6$?

- $(-4, +\infty)$
- $(0, +\infty)$
- $(-\infty, -6)$
- $(-6, +\infty)$

## Explanation

The graph of $y = 3^{x + 2} - 6$ is obtained from that of $y = 3^x$ by a shift of $2$ units to the left and $6$ units down. The horizontal shift leaves the range $(0, +\infty)$ unchanged, while the vertical shift moves it down by $6$. Therefore the range is $(-6, +\infty)$.
