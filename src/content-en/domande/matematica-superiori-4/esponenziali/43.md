At what point does the graph of $y = 2^{x - 3} + 1$ intersect the $y$-axis?

- $(0, 2)$
- $\left( 0, \dfrac{1}{8} \right)$
- $\left( 0, \dfrac{9}{8} \right)$
- $(0, 9)$

## Explanation

Points on the $y$-axis have horizontal coordinate $0$, so evaluate the function at $x = 0$. This gives $y = 2^{-3} + 1 = \dfrac{1}{8} + 1 = \dfrac{9}{8}$. Therefore the graph intersects the $y$-axis at $\left( 0, \dfrac{9}{8} \right)$.
