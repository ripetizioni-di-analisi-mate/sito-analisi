Two instruments display $A(t)=3\cdot 2^t$ and $B(t)=12\cdot 2^t$. To make the entire first curve coincide with the second, the first instrument is read at $t+h$. What constant value of $h$ works for every $t$?

- $4$
- $9$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

We need $3\cdot 2^{t+h}=12\cdot 2^t$. Dividing by $3\cdot 2^t$, which is positive at every time, leaves $2^h=4$. The required shift is therefore two time units.
