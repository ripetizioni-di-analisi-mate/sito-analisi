- $x < 0$ or $x > 2$
- $0 < x < 2$
- $x < 1$ or $x > 4$
- $x > 2$

## Explanation

With $t = 2^x$, $t > 0$, the inequality becomes $t^2 - 5t + 4 > 0$, that is, $(t - 1)(t - 4) > 0$, which holds for $t < 1$ or $t > 4$. Imposing the condition $t > 0$ gives $0 < 2^x < 1$ or $2^x > 4$. The base $2$ is greater than $1$, so $2^x < 1 = 2^0$ is equivalent to $x < 0$ and $2^x > 4 = 2^2$ is equivalent to $x > 2$.
