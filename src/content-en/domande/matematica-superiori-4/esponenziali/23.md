Which number equals $7^{2 + \sqrt{2}} \cdot 7^{2 - \sqrt{2}}$?

- $7^2$
- $7^4$
- $7^{2\sqrt{2}}$
- $49^4$

## Explanation

When powers with the same base are multiplied, their exponents add. The sum of the exponents is $(2 + \sqrt{2}) + (2 - \sqrt{2}) = 4$ because the terms $\sqrt{2}$ and $-\sqrt{2}$ cancel. Therefore the product equals $7^4 = 2401$.
