Which of the following inequalities holds for every real number $x$?

- $7^{x} > 1$
- $7^{-x} < 0$
- $7^{x} > -1$
- $\left( \dfrac{1}{7} \right)^{x} < 1$

## Explanation

Since $7^x > 0$ for every $x$, the inequality $7^x > -1$, whose right-hand side is negative, holds for every real $x$. The inequality $7^{-x} < 0$, however, has no solutions. The inequality $7^x > 1$ holds only for $x > 0$, as does $\left( \dfrac{1}{7} \right)^x < 1$, because $\left( \dfrac{1}{7} \right)^x = 7^{-x}$.
