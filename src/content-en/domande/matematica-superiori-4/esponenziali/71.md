A model grows by $21\%$ every two months with a constant monthly factor. Which monthly increase agrees with it?

- $10.5\%$
- $21\%$
- $5\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

If $r$ is the monthly rate, the factors for the two months multiply. From $(1+r)^2=1.21$ we get $1+r=1.1$, hence $r=0.1$.
