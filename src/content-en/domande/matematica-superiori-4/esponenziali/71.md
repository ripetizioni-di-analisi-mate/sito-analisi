What is the solution of the equation $5^{2x} = 3$?

- $x = 2 \log_5 3$
- $x = \log_5 \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2} \log_3 5$
- $x = \dfrac{1}{2} \log_5 3$

## Explanation

By the definition of a logarithm, $5^{2x} = 3$ is equivalent to $2x = \log_5 3$. Dividing by $2$ gives $x = \dfrac{1}{2} \log_5 3$. The value is positive and less than $\dfrac{1}{2}$ because $1 < 3 < 5$ implies $0 < \log_5 3 < 1$.
