A culture starts at $5$ units and doubles hourly for the first two hours. From then on it triples hourly. Assuming exponential growth within each phase, which formula for $t\ge 2$ joins the two phases correctly?

- $20\cdot 3^{t-2}$
- $5\cdot 3^t$
- $20\cdot 3^t$
- $5\cdot 2^t\cdot 3^{t-2}$

## Explanation

At the changeover the value is $5\cdot 2^2=20$. Since then $t-2$ hours have elapsed, and only the factor of three applies to each of those hours.
