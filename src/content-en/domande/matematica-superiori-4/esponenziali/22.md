What is the value of $\left( 5^{\sqrt{3}} \right)^{\sqrt{3}}$?

- $125$
- $25$
- $15$
- $5^{2\sqrt{3}}$

## Explanation

By the rule $\left( a^u \right)^v = a^{uv}$, valid for any two real exponents with a positive base, the exponents are multiplied. Since $\sqrt{3} \cdot \sqrt{3} = 3$, we have
$$
\left( 5^{\sqrt{3}} \right)^{\sqrt{3}} = 5^{3} = 125 .
$$
