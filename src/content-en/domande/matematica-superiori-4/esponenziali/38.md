What is the value of $\left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{4} \right)^4$, the final balance on $1$ euro invested at an annual rate of $100\%$ with interest paid in four installments?

- $\dfrac{625}{256}$
- $2$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{25}{16}$

## Explanation

We have $1 + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{4}$, so
$$
\left( \frac{5}{4} \right)^4 = \frac{5^4}{4^4} = \frac{625}{256} \approx 2.44 .
$$
The value exceeds $2$, the balance with a single annual interest payment, and is less than the number $e \approx 2.718$.
