A quantity follows the law $N(t) = N_0 \, e^{-0.05 t}$. Which statement is true?

- The quantity increases because the base $e$ is greater than $1$
- In each unit of time the quantity decreases by $0.05$
- The quantity vanishes at $t = 20$
- In each unit of time the quantity is multiplied by $e^{-0.05}$

## Explanation

We have $N(t + 1) = N_0 \, e^{-0.05 t} \cdot e^{-0.05} = N(t) \cdot e^{-0.05}$, so in each unit of time the quantity is multiplied by $e^{-0.05} \approx 0.951$. The factor is less than $1$ because $k = -0.05 < 0$, so the model describes **decay**. The quantity remains positive for every $t$ because $e^{-0.05 t} > 0$.
