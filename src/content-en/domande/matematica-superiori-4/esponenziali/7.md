- $x = 2$ or $x = 4$
- $x = 1$ or $x = 2$
- $x = 0$ or $x = 3$
- Only $x = 2$

## Explanation

The second term is $2^{3 - x} = \dfrac{8}{2^x}$. With $t = 2^x$, which is positive, the equation becomes $t + \dfrac{8}{t} = 6$, and multiplying by $t$ gives $t^2 - 6t + 8 = 0$, that is, $(t - 2)(t - 4) = 0$. From $2^x = 2$ we obtain $x = 1$ and from $2^x = 4$ we obtain $x = 2$. Both values solve the equation.
