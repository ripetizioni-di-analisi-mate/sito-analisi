What are the solutions of the inequality $\left( \dfrac{1}{5} \right)^{x} > 25$?

- $x > -2$
- $x < 2$
- $x > 2$
- $x < -2$

## Explanation

Since $25 = \left( \dfrac{1}{5} \right)^{-2}$, the inequality can be written $\left( \dfrac{1}{5} \right)^{x} > \left( \dfrac{1}{5} \right)^{-2}$. The base $\dfrac{1}{5}$ is between $0$ and $1$, so the direction **reverses**. This gives $x < -2$.
