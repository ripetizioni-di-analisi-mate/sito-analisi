A population grows exponentially and has $160$ individuals after $3$ hours and $320$ after $5$ hours. How many individuals did it have after one hour?

- $20$
- $120$
- $80$
- $40$

## Explanation

In exponential growth the number of individuals is multiplied by the same factor over equal time intervals. From $3$ to $5$ hours the population goes from $160$ to $320$, so it doubles in two hours. From $1$ to $3$ hours two hours also elapse, so after one hour there were $\dfrac{160}{2}=80$ individuals.
