A counter loses its initial reading but records $160$ units after $3$ hours and $320$ after $5$ hours. Assuming exponential growth, how many units were present after one hour?

- $0$
- $120$
- $80$
- $40$

## Explanation

The population doubles in two hours. Going back from hour three to hour one crosses one doubling interval in reverse, so divide $160$ by two.
