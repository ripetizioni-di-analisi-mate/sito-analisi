A colony of $200$ bacteria triples every $30$ minutes. How many bacteria are there after $2$ hours?

- $2400$
- $1800$
- $16\,200$
- $48\,600$

## Explanation

After $t$ minutes the colony has tripled $\dfrac{t}{30}$ times, so the number of bacteria is $N(t) = 200 \cdot 3^{\frac{t}{30}}$. In $2$ hours, that is, $120$ minutes, it has tripled $4$ times. Therefore there are $200 \cdot 3^4 = 200 \cdot 81 = 16\,200$ bacteria.
