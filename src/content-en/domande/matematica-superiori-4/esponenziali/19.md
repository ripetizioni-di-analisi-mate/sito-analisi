Which power of $3$ equals $\sqrt{3} \cdot \sqrt[4]{3}$?

- $3^{\frac{3}{4}}$
- $3^{\frac{1}{8}}$
- $3^{\frac{1}{6}}$
- $9^{\frac{3}{4}}$

## Explanation

Write the radicals as powers with rational exponents, $\sqrt{3} = 3^{\frac{1}{2}}$ and $\sqrt[4]{3} = 3^{\frac{1}{4}}$. When powers with the same base are multiplied, their exponents add, so
$$
3^{\frac{1}{2}} \cdot 3^{\frac{1}{4}} = 3^{\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4}} = 3^{\frac{3}{4}} .
$$
