For $u,v>0$ let $S=u^{\frac{2}{3}}v^{\frac{1}{3}}$. If $u$ and $v$ are both doubled, how does $S$ change?

- It becomes $2S$.
- It becomes $4S$.
- It becomes $2^{\frac{2}{3}}S$.
- It stays $S$.

## Explanation

Replacing $u$ and $v$ with $2u$ and $2v$ gives
$$
(2u)^{\frac{2}{3}}(2v)^{\frac{1}{3}}=2^{\frac{2}{3}}\,2^{\frac{1}{3}}\,u^{\frac{2}{3}}v^{\frac{1}{3}}=2^{\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{3}}\,S .
$$
Since $\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{1}{3}=1$, the factor equals $2^1=2$. So the new value is $2S$.
