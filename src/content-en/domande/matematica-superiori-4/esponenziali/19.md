For $u,v>0$ a sensor gives $S=u^{\dfrac{2}{3}}v^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$. If both readings double, what happens to $S$?

- It becomes $2S$.
- It becomes $4S$.
- It becomes $2^{\dfrac{2}{3}}S$.
- It stays at $S$.

## Explanation

The extra factors are $2^{\dfrac{2}{3}}$ and $2^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$. Their product is $2^{\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{1}{3}}=2$, so the sensor doubles its output.
