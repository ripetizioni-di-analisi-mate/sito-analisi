What is the value of $\dfrac{3^4 \cdot 9^{-1}}{27^{2}}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{27}$
- $81$
- $\dfrac{1}{81}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

Write each factor as a power with base $3$. Since $9 = 3^2$ and $27 = 3^3$, we have $9^{-1} = 3^{-2}$ and $27^2 = 3^6$. By the exponent laws, the expression equals
$$
\frac{3^4 \cdot 3^{-2}}{3^6} = 3^{4 - 2 - 6} = 3^{-4} = \frac{1}{81} .
$$
