Which expression is positive for every integer $x$, including zero and negative integers?

- $-(2^{2x})$
- $(-2^2)^x$
- $(-2)^{2x}$
- $(-2)^x$

## Explanation

The exponent $2x$ is even. Including the minus sign in the base makes the power positive, including at zero and for negative exponents. The base of $(-2^2)^x$ is $-4$, so at $x=1$ it gives a negative value.
