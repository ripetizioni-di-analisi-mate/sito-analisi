What are the solutions of the equation $2^{2x + 1} - 5 \cdot 2^x + 2 = 0$?

- $x = 2$ or $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- Only $x = 1$
- $x = -1$ or $x = 1$
- $x = 0$ or $x = 1$

## Explanation

Write $2^{2x + 1} = 2 \cdot \left( 2^x \right)^2$. With $t = 2^x$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $2t^2 - 5t + 2 = 0$, with solutions $t = 2$ and $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$, both positive. From $2^x = 2$ we obtain $x = 1$ and from $2^x = \dfrac{1}{2} = 2^{-1}$ we obtain $x = -1$.
