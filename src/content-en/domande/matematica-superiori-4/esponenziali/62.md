If $x_1,x_2$ are the two solutions of $4^x-5\cdot2^x+4=0$, what is $x_1+x_2$ using the product of the roots?

- $4$
- $5$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The positive roots of $t^2-5t+4=0$ are $t_1=2^{x_1}$ and $t_2=2^{x_2}$. Their product is $4=2^{x_1+x_2}$, so the exponents add up to $2$.
