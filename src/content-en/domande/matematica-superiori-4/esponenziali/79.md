What are the solutions of the inequality $2^{-x} \geq \dfrac{1}{8}$?

- $x \leq 3$
- $x \geq 3$
- $x \leq -3$
- $x \geq -3$

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{1}{8} = 2^{-3}$, the inequality can be written $2^{-x} \geq 2^{-3}$. The base $2$ exceeds $1$, so the direction is preserved and we obtain $-x \geq -3$. Multiplying both sides by $-1$ reverses the direction, so $x \leq 3$.
