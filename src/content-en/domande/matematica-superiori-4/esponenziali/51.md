What are the solutions of the equation $3^{x^2 - 1} = 27$?

- $x = -2$ or $x = 2$
- Only $x = 2$
- $x = -\sqrt{2}$ or $x = \sqrt{2}$
- $x = -\sqrt{3}$ or $x = \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Since $27 = 3^3$, the equation can be written $3^{x^2 - 1} = 3^3$. Two powers with the same base are equal only when they have the same exponent, so $x^2 - 1 = 3$, that is, $x^2 = 4$. Therefore the solutions are $x = -2$ and $x = 2$.
