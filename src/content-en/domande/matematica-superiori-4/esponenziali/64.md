To solve $2^x+4^{-x}=3$, let $t=2^x$. Which equivalent polynomial equation follows for $t>0$?

- $t^2-3t+1=0$
- $t^3-3t^2+1=0$
- $t^3-3t+1=0$
- $2t^2-3t=0$

## Explanation

The term $4^{-x}$ equals $t^{-2}$. Multiply $t+t^{-2}=3$ by the positive number $t^2$ to get $t^3+1=3t^2$.
