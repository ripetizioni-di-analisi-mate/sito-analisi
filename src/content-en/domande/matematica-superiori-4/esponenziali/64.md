What is the solution of the equation $5^x - 5^{1 - x} = 4$?

- $x = 5$
- $x = 1$
- $x = -1$
- $x = 0$

## Explanation

The second term is $5^{1 - x} = \dfrac{5}{5^x}$. With $t = 5^x$, $t > 0$, the equation becomes $t - \dfrac{5}{t} = 4$, that is, $t^2 - 4t - 5 = 0$, which factors as $(t - 5)(t + 1) = 0$. The solution $t = -1$ is inadmissible because it is negative. This leaves $5^x = 5$, so $x = 1$.
