A fund loses the same fraction each year. After two years it retains $\dfrac{16}{25}$ of its initial value. Which fraction of the **initial** amount is lost during the third year alone?

- $\dfrac{16}{125}$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac{64}{125}$
- $\dfrac9{25}$

## Explanation

The positive annual factor is $\sqrt{\dfrac{16}{25}}=\dfrac45$. The third-year loss is one fifth of the surviving sixteen twenty-fifths, or $\dfrac{16}{125}$ of the initial amount.
