What are the solutions of the equation $3^x + 3^{2 - x} = 10$?

- $x = 1$ or $x = 9$
- $x = 1$ or $x = 2$
- $x = 0$ or $x = 2$
- Only $x = 2$

## Explanation

The second term is $3^{2 - x} = \dfrac{9}{3^x}$. With $t = 3^x$, which is positive, the equation becomes $t + \dfrac{9}{t} = 10$, and multiplying by $t$ gives $t^2 - 10t + 9 = 0$. The solutions are $t = 1$ and $t = 9$. From $3^x = 1$ we obtain $x = 0$ and from $3^x = 9$ we obtain $x = 2$.
