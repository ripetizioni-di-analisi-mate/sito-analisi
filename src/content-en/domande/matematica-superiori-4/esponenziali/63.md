Draw the chord joining $(0,1)$ to $(2,4)$ on the graph of $2^x$. At $x=1$ is it above or below the curve, and by how much?

- Below by $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- Above by one unit.
- Above by $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- They coincide.

## Explanation

The input is halfway between the endpoints, so the height on the line is $\dfrac{1+4}{2}=\dfrac{5}{2}$. The curve has height $2^1=2$ and the difference is one half.
