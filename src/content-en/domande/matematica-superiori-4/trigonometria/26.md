What is the inradius of the right triangle with legs $6$ and $8$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $2$

## Explanation

The hypotenuse is $10$, the area is $24$ and the semiperimeter is $12$. Splitting the triangle into three triangles with altitude equal to the inradius gives $r=\dfrac{24}{12}=2$.
