Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$, hypotenuse $a = 13$, and leg $c = 5$. What is $\tan\beta$?

- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{12}{13}$
- $\dfrac{5}{13}$
- $\dfrac{12}{5}$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean theorem, the other leg is $b = \sqrt{169 - 25} = 12$. Leg $b$ is opposite $\beta$ and leg $c$ is adjacent, so by the second right-triangle theorem, $\tan\beta = \dfrac{b}{c}$. Therefore $\tan\beta = \dfrac{12}{5}$.
