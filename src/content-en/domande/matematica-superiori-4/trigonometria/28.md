The diagonal of a rectangle has length $12$ and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the longer side. What is the area of the rectangle?

- $72 \sqrt{3}$
- $36$
- $18 \sqrt{3}$
- $36 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The diagonal divides the rectangle into two right triangles with hypotenuse $12$. By the first right-triangle theorem, the side adjacent to the $30^\circ$ angle measures $12\cos 30^\circ = 6\sqrt{3}$ and the opposite side measures $12\sin 30^\circ = 6$. The area is therefore $6\sqrt{3} \cdot 6 = 36\sqrt{3}$.
