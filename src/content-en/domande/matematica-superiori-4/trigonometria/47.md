In triangle $ABC$, we have $a = 8$, $\alpha = 30^\circ$, and $\beta = 45^\circ$. What is the length of side $b$?

- $8 \sqrt{2}$
- $4 \sqrt{2}$
- $16$
- $8 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Side $a$ and the opposite angle are known, so we apply the law of sines, $\dfrac{b}{\sin\beta} = \dfrac{a}{\sin\alpha}$. The ratio $\dfrac{a}{\sin\alpha}$ equals $\dfrac{8}{\dfrac{1}{2}} = 16$. Consequently, $b = 16\sin 45^\circ = 16 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = 8\sqrt{2}$. The value $16$ is the diameter of the circumcircle.
