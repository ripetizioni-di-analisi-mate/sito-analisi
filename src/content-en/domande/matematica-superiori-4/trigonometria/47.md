The sine rule is $\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}$. Which pairing would invalidate its use?

- Pairing a side with an adjacent angle instead of its opposite angle.
- Using two acute angles.
- Using lengths both expressed in metres.
- Using unequal side lengths.

## Explanation

The common ratio is the circumdiameter. It is obtained by pairing each chord with the angle that subtends it.
