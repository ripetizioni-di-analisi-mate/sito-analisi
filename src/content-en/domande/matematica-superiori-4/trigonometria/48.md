In triangle $ABC$, we have $a = 9$ and $\alpha = 60^\circ$. What is the radius of the circumcircle?

- $6 \sqrt{3}$
- $3 \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{9 \sqrt{3}}{4}$
- $9$

## Explanation

By the law of sines, $\dfrac{a}{\sin\alpha} = 2R$, so $R = \dfrac{a}{2\sin\alpha}$. Substitution gives $R = \dfrac{9}{2 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} = \dfrac{9}{\sqrt{3}}$. Rationalizing the denominator gives $R = 3\sqrt{3}$. The value $6\sqrt{3}$ is the diameter $2R$.
