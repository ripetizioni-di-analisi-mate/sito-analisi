For which values of $x$ are the numbers $x$, $5$, and $8$ the side lengths of a triangle?

- $3 < x < 13$
- $0 < x < 13$
- $x > 3$
- $5 < x < 8$

## Explanation

By the triangle inequality, each side is between the difference and the sum of the other two. For side $x$ this gives $|8 - 5| < x < 8 + 5$, that is, $3 < x < 13$. For these values the other two inequalities also hold, since $8 < x + 5$ is equivalent to $x > 3$ and $5 < x + 8$ holds for every $x > 0$.
