A triangle has two fixed sides of lengths $5$ and $7$, and the included angle can vary. What is the greatest possible area?

- $35$
- $\dfrac{35}{2}$
- $\dfrac{35 \sqrt{3}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{35}{4}$

## Explanation

If $x$ is the included angle, the area is $S(x) = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 7 \cdot \sin x = \dfrac{35}{2}\sin x$, with $0 < x < \pi$. Since $\sin x \leq 1$ and the value $1$ is attained at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, the maximum area is $\dfrac{35}{2}$. The triangle with maximum area is therefore the right triangle whose legs are the two fixed sides.
