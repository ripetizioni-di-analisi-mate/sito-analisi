A convex quadrilateral has diagonals $6$ and $10$ meeting at $30^\circ$. What is its area?

- $15$
- $30$
- $60$
- $15\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The diagonals divide the quadrilateral into four triangles. Adding their areas gives $\dfrac{1}{2}d_1d_2 \sin \theta$ because supplementary angles have equal sines. Here this gives $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot 6\cdot 10\cdot \dfrac{1}{2}=15$.
