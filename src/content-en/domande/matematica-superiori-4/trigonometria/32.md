A parallelogram has sides $8$ and $5$ and an angle of $120^\circ$. What is its area?

- $20 \sqrt{3}$
- $10 \sqrt{3}$
- $20$
- $40 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

A diagonal divides the parallelogram into two congruent triangles, each with sides $8$ and $5$ and an included angle of $120^\circ$, so the area of the parallelogram is $S = 8 \cdot 5 \cdot \sin 120^\circ$. Since $\sin 120^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, we obtain $S = 40 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 20\sqrt{3}$. The value $10\sqrt{3}$ is the area of just one of the two triangles.
