Two triangles each have sides of lengths $5$ and $8$. The included angle is $60^\circ$ in the first and $120^\circ$ in the second. How do their areas compare?

- The second is twice the first
- They are equal
- The first is greater than the second
- The second is greater than the first

## Explanation

By the area formula using two sides and the included angle, the two areas are $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 8 \cdot \sin 60^\circ$ and $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 8 \cdot \sin 120^\circ$. Angles $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ are supplementary, so they have the same sine, $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. Therefore the areas **are equal**, each measuring $10\sqrt{3}$, even though the two triangles are not congruent.
