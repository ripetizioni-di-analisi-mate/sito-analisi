In the triangle with $a = 7$, $b = 9$, and $c = 5$, what is the order of the angles?

- $\gamma > \alpha > \beta$
- $\alpha > \beta > \gamma$
- $\beta > \gamma > \alpha$
- $\beta > \alpha > \gamma$

## Explanation

The larger angle lies opposite the longer side. The sides are ordered as $b > a > c$ since $9 > 7 > 5$. The angles opposite these sides are $\beta$, $\alpha$, and $\gamma$ respectively, so $\beta > \alpha > \gamma$.
