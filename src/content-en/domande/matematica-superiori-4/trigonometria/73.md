In triangle $ABC$, we have $\alpha = 30^\circ$ and $b = 12$. For which lengths of side $a$ do two triangles exist?

- $6 < a < 12$
- $a > 6$
- $0 < a < 6$
- $6 \leq a \leq 12$

## Explanation

The distance from $C$ to the line containing side $c$ is $h = b\sin\alpha = 12 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 6$. For acute $\alpha$, two triangles exist if and only if $h < a < b$. Therefore two triangles exist for $6 < a < 12$. For $a = 6$ there is exactly one triangle, with a right angle at $B$, and for $a \geq 12$ there is again just one.
