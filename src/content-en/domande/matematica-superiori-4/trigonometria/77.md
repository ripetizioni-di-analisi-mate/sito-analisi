From the top of a lighthouse of height $30\,\mathrm{m}$, a boat is seen at an angle of depression of $30^\circ$. How far is the boat from the base of the lighthouse?

- $10 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The line of sight to the boat makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the horizontal through the top, so it makes the same angle with sea level because these are alternate interior angles for parallel lines. In the right triangle whose legs are the lighthouse and the distance $d$, the height of the lighthouse is the leg opposite this angle. By the second right-triangle theorem, $30 = d\tan 30^\circ$, so $d = \dfrac{30}{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}} = 30\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 51.96\,\mathrm{m}$.
