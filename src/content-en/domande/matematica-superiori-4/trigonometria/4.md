A regular octagon is inscribed in a circle of radius $4$. What is its area?

- $64 \sqrt{2}$
- $16 \sqrt{2}$
- $32$
- $32 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Joining the center to the eight vertices divides the octagon into eight congruent isosceles triangles. Each has two sides equal to the radius $4$ and an included angle of $\dfrac{360^\circ}{8} = 45^\circ$. By the area formula using two sides and the included angle, each triangle has area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 4 \cdot \sin 45^\circ = 4\sqrt{2}$. The area of the octagon is therefore $8 \cdot 4\sqrt{2} = 32\sqrt{2}$.
