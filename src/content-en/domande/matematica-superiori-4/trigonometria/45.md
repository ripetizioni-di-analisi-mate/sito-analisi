In a circle of radius $r$, chord $AB$ has length $r$. What is the measure of the central angle that intercepts the minor arc $AB$?

- $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

By the chord length theorem, $r = 2r\sin\alpha$, so $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and the inscribed angle with its vertex on the major arc is $30^\circ$. The central angle is twice the inscribed angle that intercepts the same arc, hence it measures $60^\circ$. This agrees with the fact that triangle $AOB$ has three sides of length $r$ and is equilateral.
