How many triangles $ABC$ exist with $a = 7$, $b = 5$, and $\alpha = 40^\circ$?

- Two
- Exactly one
- None
- Exactly one, with a right angle at $B$

## Explanation

This is the ambiguous case with acute $\alpha$, and side $a$ opposite the known angle is longer than $b$. For $a \geq b$, exactly one triangle exists. Indeed, the law of sines gives $\sin\beta = \dfrac{5\sin 40^\circ}{7} \approx 0.46$, and the obtuse angle with this sine is inadmissible because $\beta$ is opposite the side $b < a$ and is therefore smaller than $\alpha$.
