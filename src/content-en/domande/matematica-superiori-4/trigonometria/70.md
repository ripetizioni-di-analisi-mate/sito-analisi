Given sides $a,b$ opposite angles $A,B$ with $A$ acute, the sine rule gives $\sin B>1$. Which inconsistency can explain this result?

- Both lengths are in metres rather than centimetres.
- Side $a$ is shorter than the perpendicular distance $b \sin A$.
- The triangle is right-angled.
- Angle $B$ is obtuse.

## Explanation

The data give $\sin B=\dfrac{b \sin A}{a}>1$. A circle of radius $a$ cannot reach the line that must contain the third vertex.
