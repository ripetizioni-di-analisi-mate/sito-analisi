A quadrilateral is cyclic. Two opposite angles are $2x$ and $x+30^\circ$. What is $x$?

- $30^\circ$
- $50^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $75^\circ$

## Explanation

The arcs subtended by opposite angles cover the entire circle. Halving their measures gives $2x+x+30^\circ=180^\circ$ and hence $x=50^\circ$.
