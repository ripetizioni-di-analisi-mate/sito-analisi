An equilateral triangle is inscribed in a circle of radius $6$. What is its side length?

- $3 \sqrt{3}$
- $6 \sqrt{3}$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

Each side of the equilateral triangle is a chord, and the opposite angle, which is an inscribed angle, measures $60^\circ$. By the chord length theorem, the side length is $\ell = 2 \cdot 6 \cdot \sin 60^\circ = 12 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 6\sqrt{3}$. The same result follows from the law of sines, since the diameter of the circumcircle is $2R = 12$.
