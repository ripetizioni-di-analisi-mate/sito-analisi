In a triangle, side $c$ is the longest, $c^2 > a^2 + b^2$, and $\sin\gamma = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. What is the measure of $\gamma$?

- $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $150^\circ$
- $60^\circ$ or $120^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ with sine $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ are $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$, so the sine alone does not determine $\gamma$. By the law of cosines, the angle opposite $c$ is obtuse if and only if $c^2 > a^2 + b^2$. This condition holds, so $\gamma = 120^\circ$.
