An isosceles triangle has equal sides of length $a$ and vertex angle $2x$ with $0<x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Which expression gives its **area from its base and height**?

- $2a^2 \sin x \cos x$
- $a^2 \sin x \cos x$
- $\dfrac{a^2}{2}\cos^2x$
- $a^2\sin^2x$

## Explanation

The altitude bisects the angle and the base. The base is $2a \sin x$ and the height is $a \cos x$, so half their product yields the required expression.
