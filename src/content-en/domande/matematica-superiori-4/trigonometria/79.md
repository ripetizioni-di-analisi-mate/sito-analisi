From two points $A$ and $B$ on a straight riverbank, with $AB = 60\,\mathrm{m}$, a point $P$ on the other bank is observed and the angles $\widehat{PAB} = 30^\circ$ and $\widehat{PBA} = 120^\circ$ are measured. How far is $P$ from line $AB$?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The angle at $P$ equals $180^\circ - 30^\circ - 120^\circ = 30^\circ$, the same as the angle at $A$, so triangle $ABP$ is isosceles with $BP = AB = 60\,\mathrm{m}$. Segment $BP$ makes the supplementary angle with the extension of $AB$ beyond $B$, and this angle equals $180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ$. By the first right-triangle theorem, the distance from $P$ to line $AB$ is therefore $60\sin 60^\circ = 30\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 51.96\,\mathrm{m}$.
