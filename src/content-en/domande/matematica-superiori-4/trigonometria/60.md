In the triangle with sides $4$, $5$, and $6$, what is the cosine of the smallest angle?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The smallest angle lies opposite the shortest side, of length $4$. By the law of cosines, its cosine is $\dfrac{5^2 + 6^2 - 4^2}{2 \cdot 5 \cdot 6} = \dfrac{25 + 36 - 16}{60} = \dfrac{45}{60}$. Therefore the cosine of the smallest angle is $\dfrac{3}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{8}$ is the cosine of the largest angle, opposite the side of length $6$.
