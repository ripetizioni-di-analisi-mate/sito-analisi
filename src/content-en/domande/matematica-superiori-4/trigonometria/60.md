In triangle $ABC$ the point $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$. How does the projection of the directed median from A to M compare with the projection of the sum of the vectors from A to B and from A to C on the same oriented line?

- They are equal in every triangle.
- They are always opposite.
- The projection of the median is half the projection of the sum.
- The projection of the median is twice as large.

## Explanation

The midpoint coordinates are the averages of the endpoint coordinates. Therefore $\overrightarrow{AM}=\dfrac{\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{AC}}{2}$, and the same relation holds for the components along any axis.
