What is the largest angle in the triangle with sides $7$, $8$, and $13$?

- $60^\circ$
- $150^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $135^\circ$

## Explanation

The largest angle is opposite the longest side, of length $13$. By the law of cosines, its cosine is $\dfrac{49 + 64 - 169}{2 \cdot 7 \cdot 8} = -\dfrac{56}{112} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The angle between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ with cosine $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ is $120^\circ$.
