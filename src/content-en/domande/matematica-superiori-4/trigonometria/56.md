Two sides measure $3$ and $4$. The third side grows while the first two are held fixed. How does their included angle change?

- It decreases.
- It stays fixed.
- It increases.
- It decreases and then increases.

## Explanation

By the cosine rule $\cos C=\dfrac{3^2+4^2-c^2}{2\cdot 3\cdot 4}$. As $c$ grows the cosine decreases and the interior angle increases.
