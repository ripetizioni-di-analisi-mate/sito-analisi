The bisector of the right angle in a triangle with legs $3$ and $4$ splits the hypotenuse into two segments. How long is the shorter one?

- $\dfrac{20}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{15}{7}$

## Explanation

By Pythagoras the hypotenuse is $5$. The angle bisector theorem gives segment lengths in the ratio $3:4$, so the shorter segment is $\dfrac{3}{7}$ of the hypotenuse, namely $\dfrac{15}{7}$.
