Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$, leg $b = 6$, and $\beta = 60^\circ$. What is the length of the hypotenuse $a$?

- $12$
- $3 \sqrt{3}$
- $2 \sqrt{3}$
- $4 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Leg $b$ is opposite $\beta$, so the first right-triangle theorem gives $b = a\sin\beta$. It follows that $a = \dfrac{b}{\sin\beta} = \dfrac{6}{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} = \dfrac{12}{\sqrt{3}}$. Rationalizing the denominator gives $a = 4\sqrt{3}$. The value $12$ comes from dividing by $\cos\beta$, treating $b$ as the leg adjacent to $\beta$.
