To check an area written as $K=\dfrac{abc}{4R}$, what units should result if $a,b,c,R$ are all measured in meters?

- $\mathrm{m}^2$
- $\mathrm m$
- $\mathrm{m}^3$
- Dimensionless.

## Explanation

The numerator is a product of three lengths and has cubic-meter units. The denominator has meter units, so the quotient gives square meters, as required for an area.
