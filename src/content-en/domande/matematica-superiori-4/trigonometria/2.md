Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$ and legs $b = 8$ and $c = 15$. What is the measure of angle $\beta$ to the nearest hundredth of a degree?

- $61.93^\circ$
- $32.23^\circ$
- $28.07^\circ$
- $57.77^\circ$

## Explanation

Leg $b$ is opposite $\beta$ and leg $c$ is adjacent. By the second right-triangle theorem, $b = c\tan\beta$, so $\tan\beta = \dfrac{8}{15}$. Angle $\beta$ is acute, hence $\beta = \arctan\dfrac{8}{15} \approx 28.07^\circ$. The value $61.93^\circ$ is the complementary angle $\gamma$, opposite leg $c$.
