Two interior angles of a triangle have the same sine. What follows about their opposite sides?

- One is twice the other.
- They have equal lengths.
- Both are diameters of the circumcircle.
- Their sum equals the third side.

## Explanation

The sine rule gives $\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}$. Equal positive sines imply equal corresponding sides.
