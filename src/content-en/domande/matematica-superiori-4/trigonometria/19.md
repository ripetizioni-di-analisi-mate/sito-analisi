In a triangle, $\alpha = 50^\circ$ and $\beta = 70^\circ$. Which side is longest?

- Side $c$
- Side $b$
- Side $a$
- It cannot be determined without knowing a side length

## Explanation

The third angle is $\gamma = 180^\circ - 50^\circ - 70^\circ = 60^\circ$, so the largest angle is $\beta = 70^\circ$. Since the longer side is opposite the larger angle, the longest side is $b$. The comparison depends only on the angles and therefore does not require any side length.
