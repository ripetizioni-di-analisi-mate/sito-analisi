All three side lengths are known, but no angles. Which calculation prepares the use of the sine rule without guessing a supplementary angle?

- Immediately take the arcsine of the longest side.
- Assume all angles are acute.
- Replace every side length by its sine.
- Find the cosine of the largest angle first.

## Explanation

Cosine determines a unique angle in $[0,\pi]$. Once the largest angle is known, the other two are acute and the sine rule can be used without an acute-obtuse ambiguity.
