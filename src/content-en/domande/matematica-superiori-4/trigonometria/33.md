A triangle has sides of lengths $6$ and $8$ and area $12$. What values can the included angle take?

- $30^\circ$ only
- $30^\circ$ or $150^\circ$
- $60^\circ$ or $120^\circ$
- $150^\circ$ only

## Explanation

By the area formula using two sides and the included angle, $12 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 8 \cdot \sin\gamma = 24\sin\gamma$, so $\sin\gamma = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The angles between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ with sine $\dfrac{1}{2}$ are $30^\circ$ and its supplement $150^\circ$. Both are admissible because two sides and any included angle between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ determine a triangle.
