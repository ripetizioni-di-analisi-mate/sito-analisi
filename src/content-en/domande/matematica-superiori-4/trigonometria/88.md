A rectangle is inscribed in a circle of radius $r$, and $x$, with $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, is the angle a diagonal makes with a side. What is the maximum perimeter of the rectangle?

- $8 r$
- $4 r$
- $4 \sqrt{2} r$
- $2 \sqrt{2} r$

## Explanation

The diagonal is a diameter, so by the first right-triangle theorem the sides of the rectangle are $2r\cos x$ and $2r\sin x$. Its perimeter is $p(x) = 4r\left( \sin x + \cos x \right)$. The auxiliary angle method gives $\sin x + \cos x = \sqrt{2}\sin\left( x + \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right)$, which reaches its maximum of $\sqrt{2}$ when $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Therefore the maximum perimeter is $4\sqrt{2}\,r$ and is attained when the rectangle is a square.
