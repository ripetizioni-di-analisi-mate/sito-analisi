A triangle has a fixed base $AB$ and vertex $C$ moving along a line parallel to that base. What stays fixed as the angle at $A$ varies?

- Its perimeter.
- The length of $AC$.
- The triangle's area.
- The angle at $B$.

## Explanation

The distance between parallel lines is constant, so the height above the fixed base does not change. Half the base times the height therefore gives a constant area.
