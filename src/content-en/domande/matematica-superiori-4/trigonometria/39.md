Two chords of one circle subtend central angles of $50^\circ$ and $100^\circ$. Which chord is longer?

- The one subtending $50^\circ$.
- The one subtending $100^\circ$.
- Their lengths are equal.
- The radius is needed to compare lengths.

## Explanation

The chord lengths are $2R \sin (25^\circ)$ and $2R \sin (50^\circ)$. Both half-angles are in the first quadrant, where sine increases.
