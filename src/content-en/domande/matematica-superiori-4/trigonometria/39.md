In a circle of radius $6$, chord $AB$ subtends an angle of $45^\circ$ at a point on the circle. How long is $AB$?

- $3 \sqrt{2}$
- $6 \sqrt{2}$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

By the chord length theorem, the length of a chord equals the diameter times the sine of an inscribed angle subtended by that chord. The diameter is $12$, so $AB = 12\sin 45^\circ = 12 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = 6\sqrt{2}$. The value $3\sqrt{2}$ comes from using the radius in place of the diameter.
