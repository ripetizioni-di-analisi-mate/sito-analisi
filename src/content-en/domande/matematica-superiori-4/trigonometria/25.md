In a right triangle $\sin A=\dfrac{7}{25}$ with acute $A$. A scale drawing doubles all sides. What happens to $\tan A$?

- It doubles.
- It halves.
- It is multiplied by four.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

Tangent is a ratio of two corresponding sides. The scale factor of two appears in both numerator and denominator and cancels.
