Two stations at the ends of a known horizontal baseline measure the **interior** angles of a triangle leading to a tree. If the data do not say on which side of the baseline the tree lies, how many plan positions fit a nondegenerate triangle?

- Exactly one in all cases.
- Infinitely many on the circle with the baseline as diameter.
- Two, reflected across the baseline.
- Three, one per vertex.

## Explanation

The base and the two angles fix the triangle up to congruence. Reflecting the far vertex across the baseline preserves the data and gives a distinct second position. In each half-plane the two rays determine one point.
