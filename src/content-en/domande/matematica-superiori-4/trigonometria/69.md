An observer $P$ is $7$ meters from the center $O$ of a circle of radius $4$ meters. A ray from $P$ meets the circle at two distinct points at distances $d_1,d_2$ from $P$. What is the product $d_1d_2$ for every rotation of the ray that preserves two intersections?

- $d_1d_2=33\,\mathrm{m}^2$
- $d_1d_2=9\,\mathrm{m}^2$
- $d_1d_2=28\,\mathrm{m}^2$
- $d_1d_2=65\,\mathrm{m}^2$

## Explanation

Let $A$ be the angle between the ray and $PO$. For each intersection at distance $d$, the law of cosines gives $4^2=7^2+d^2-14d\cos A$. The two distances are the roots of $d^2-14\cos A\,d+33=0$. Their product is the constant term $33$, independent of $A$.
