In the triangle with sides $a = 5$, $b = 7$, and $c = 8$, what is the measure of angle $\beta$?

- $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $45^\circ$

## Explanation

The law of cosines gives $\cos\beta = \dfrac{a^2 + c^2 - b^2}{2ac}$. Substituting yields $\cos\beta = \dfrac{25 + 64 - 49}{80} = \dfrac{40}{80} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $0^\circ < \beta < 180^\circ$, the cosine determines a unique angle, so $\beta = 60^\circ$.
