The cosine rule gives $\cos A=-\dfrac{1}{4}$ in a triangle. Without calculating the angle, where is the foot of the altitude from $B$ to line $AC$?

- Between $A$ and $C$.
- Beyond $A$, on the side away from $C$.
- At $C$.
- At $A$.

## Explanation

A negative cosine means the angle at $A$ is obtuse. The projection of $AB$ along $AC$ is negative, so the foot lies on the extension beyond the vertex.
