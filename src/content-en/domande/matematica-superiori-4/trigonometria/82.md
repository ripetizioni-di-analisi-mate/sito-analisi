A trail makes an angle of $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Two maps report respectively a trail length of $100$ meters and a horizontal projection of $100$ meters. At the same angle, which gives the larger rise?

- The first, because sine exceeds tangent.
- They are equal because the same number appears.
- The second gives zero rise.
- The second, because the rise is $100\tan30^\circ$ instead of $100\sin30^\circ$.

## Explanation

The first map measures the hypotenuse. The second measures the adjacent leg. For an acute angle $\tan\theta=\sin\theta/\cos\theta>\sin\theta$, so the second rise is larger.
