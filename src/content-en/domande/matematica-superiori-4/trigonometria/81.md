A path climbs at $30^\circ$ then descends at the same angle over the same distance. If travel is always forward, which displacement components cancel?

- The horizontal components.
- Both components.
- Neither component.
- The vertical components.

## Explanation

For equal lengths the vertical projections have magnitude $L \sin (30^\circ)$ and opposite signs. Both horizontal projections point forward.
