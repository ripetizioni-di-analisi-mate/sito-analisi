In the side-side-angle case, two triangles are suggested with the same base $b$, the same side $a$ opposite the same acute angle $A$. If both exist, what do the two candidate missing vertices represent geometrically?

- The two intersections of a fixed ray with a circle of radius $a$.
- The two foci of one ellipse.
- The midpoints of the known sides.
- The endpoints of a diameter of any circle.

## Explanation

The side of length $b$ fixes two vertices, and angle $A$ determines the direction of the side still unknown. The third vertex must also be distance $a$ from the other endpoint of the base, so one intersects the ray with a circle.
