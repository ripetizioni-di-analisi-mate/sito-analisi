How many triangles $ABC$ exist with $a = 5$, $b = 7$, and $\alpha = 120^\circ$?

- None
- Exactly one
- Two
- Exactly one, with acute $\beta$

## Explanation

Angle $\alpha$ is obtuse, so it is the largest angle of the triangle. Since the longer side is opposite the larger angle, side $a$ would have to be longer than $b$. Here $a = 5 < b = 7$, so a triangle **does not exist**. The law of sines confirms the result since it would give $\sin\beta = \dfrac{7\sin 120^\circ}{5} = \dfrac{7\sqrt{3}}{10} > 1$.
