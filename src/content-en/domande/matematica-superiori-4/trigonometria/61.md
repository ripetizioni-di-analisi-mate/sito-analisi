A boat travels $3$ km, changes heading by $60^\circ$, then travels $4$ km. Which angle between the known sides belongs in the head-to-tail route triangle?

- $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

At the turn the first side of the triangle points back to the start. The interior angle is supplementary to the change in heading, hence $180^\circ-60^\circ=120^\circ$.
