Two ships leave the same port on straight courses at an angle of $60^\circ$ to each other. After one hour, the first has traveled $30\,\mathrm{km}$ and the second $40\,\mathrm{km}$. How far apart are the ships?

- $10 \sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $10 \sqrt{37}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $50\,\mathrm{km}$
- $10\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The port and the positions of the two ships are the vertices of a triangle with sides $30\,\mathrm{km}$ and $40\,\mathrm{km}$ and an included angle of $60^\circ$. By the law of cosines, the square of the distance is $900 + 1600 - 2 \cdot 30 \cdot 40 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 2500 - 1200 = 1300$. Therefore the ships are $\sqrt{1300}\,\mathrm{km} = 10\sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{km} \approx 36.06\,\mathrm{km}$ apart.
