A horizontal force is resolved parallel and perpendicular to an inclined plane. As the plane becomes horizontal, where is the entire force?

- In the normal component.
- The two components become equal.
- The force vanishes.
- In the component parallel to the plane.

## Explanation

The angle between the force and the plane tends to zero. The parallel component tends to $F \cos 0=F$ and the normal component tends to zero.
