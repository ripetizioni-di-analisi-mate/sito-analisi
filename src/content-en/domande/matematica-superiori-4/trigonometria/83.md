An object of weight $80\,\mathrm{N}$ rests on a frictionless inclined plane. A force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$ parallel to the plane is needed to hold it at rest. What is the angle of inclination?

- $60^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $15^\circ$
- $30^\circ$

## Explanation

The force holding the object at rest balances the component of its weight parallel to the plane, which is $P_\parallel = P\sin\alpha$. Thus $80\sin\alpha = 40$, that is, $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The inclination is an acute angle, so $\alpha = 30^\circ$. The value $60^\circ$ comes from confusing the parallel component with the perpendicular component, $P\cos\alpha$.
