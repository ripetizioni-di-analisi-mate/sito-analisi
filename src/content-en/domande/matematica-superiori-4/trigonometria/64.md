In the triangle with sides $2$, $3$, and $4$, what is the cosine of the largest angle?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{4}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{11}{16}$

## Explanation

The largest angle lies opposite the longest side, of length $4$. By the law of cosines, its cosine is $\dfrac{2^2 + 3^2 - 4^2}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3} = \dfrac{4 + 9 - 16}{12} = -\dfrac{3}{12} = -\dfrac{1}{4}$. The cosine is negative, so the angle is obtuse and the triangle is obtuse.
