From $a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc \cos A$, which relation follows in the isosceles case $b=c$?

- $a^2=2b^2(1+\cos A)$
- $a^2=2b^2(1-\cos A)$
- $a^2=b^2(1-\cos A)$
- $a^2=2b^2\cos A$

## Explanation

Setting $c=b$ makes the first two squares add to $2b^2$ and the product become $2b^2\cos A$. Factoring out the common term gives the relation.
