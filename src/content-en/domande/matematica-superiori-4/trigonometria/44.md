Which of the following cannot be a chord length in a circle of radius $3$?

- $7$
- $6$
- $3\sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

By the chord length theorem, each chord has length $2r\sin\alpha = 6\sin\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is an inscribed angle between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$. Since $0 < \sin\alpha \leq 1$, a chord has length at most $6$, the diameter. Therefore $7$ cannot be a chord length. The values $6$, $3\sqrt{3}$, and $\sqrt{2}$ are obtained when $\sin\alpha$ equals $1$, $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, and $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{6}$, respectively.
