In triangle $ABC$, we have $a = 12$, $\alpha = 60^\circ$, and $\beta = 45^\circ$. What is the length of side $b$?

- $4 \sqrt{6}$
- $6 \sqrt{6}$
- $6 \sqrt{2}$
- $8 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Side $a$ and its opposite angle $\alpha$ are known, so we apply the law of sines, $\dfrac{a}{\sin\alpha} = \dfrac{b}{\sin\beta}$. It follows that
$$
b = \frac{a\sin\beta}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{12 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} = \frac{12\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}} .
$$
Rationalizing the denominator gives $b = 4\sqrt{6}$. The value $6\sqrt{6}$ comes from interchanging $\sin\alpha$ and $\sin\beta$ in the formula.
