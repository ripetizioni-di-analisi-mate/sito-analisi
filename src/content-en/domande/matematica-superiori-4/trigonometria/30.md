In triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at $A$, we have $\beta = 40^\circ$. Which relationship between the legs is true?

- $b > c$
- $b = c$
- $c > b$
- It depends on the length of the hypotenuse

## Explanation

The acute angles of a right triangle are complementary, so $\gamma = 90^\circ - 40^\circ = 50^\circ$. Leg $c$ is opposite $\gamma$ and leg $b$ is opposite $\beta$. Since $\gamma > \beta$ and a longer side is opposite a larger angle, we have $c > b$. The same result follows from the second theorem, since $\dfrac{b}{c} = \tan 40^\circ < \tan 45^\circ = 1$.
