An inscribed angle subtends a diameter. What information about its vertex is sufficient to know it is a right angle?

- The vertex lies on the circle away from the diameter endpoints.
- The vertex is at the centre.
- The vertex lies anywhere inside the disk.
- The vertex is the midpoint of the diameter.

## Explanation

The subtended arc is a semicircle of $180^\circ$. The inscribed angle is half that measure, thus $90^\circ$.
