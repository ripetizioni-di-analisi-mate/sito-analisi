In a circle of radius $5$, chord $AB$ corresponds to a central angle of $90^\circ$. How long is $AB$?

- $5 \sqrt{2}$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $5 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The inscribed angle that intercepts arc $AB$ is half the central angle, so it equals $45^\circ$. By the chord length theorem, $AB = 2r\sin 45^\circ = 10 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = 5\sqrt{2}$. The same result follows from the Pythagorean theorem in triangle $AOB$, which has a right angle at $O$ and legs of length $5$. The value $10$ comes from using the central angle in place of the inscribed angle.
