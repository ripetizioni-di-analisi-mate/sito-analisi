A force of $10$ newtons acts at $60^\circ$ to a displacement of $3$ metres. How much work does it do?

- $30\,\mathrm J$
- $15\,\mathrm J$
- $15\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Only the force projection along the displacement contributes to work. It is $10\cos (60^\circ)=5$ newtons, so multiplying by three metres gives fifteen joules.
