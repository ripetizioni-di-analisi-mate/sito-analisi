Two forces, each of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N}$, act at the same point and make an angle of $120^\circ$. What is the magnitude of their resultant?

- $10 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By the parallelogram rule, $R^2 = F_1^2 + F_2^2 + 2F_1F_2\cos\theta$. With $\theta = 120^\circ$, we have $\cos\theta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $R^2 = 100 + 100 - 100 = 100$. Therefore $R = 10\,\mathrm{N}$. Using the opposite sign before the cross term gives the value $10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
