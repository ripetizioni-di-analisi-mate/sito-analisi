In triangle $ABC$, we have $b = 4$, $c = 6$, and $\alpha = 120^\circ$. What is the length of side $a$?

- $2 \sqrt{7}$
- $2 \sqrt{19}$
- $2 \sqrt{13}$
- $10$

## Explanation

Two sides and the included angle are known, so we apply the law of cosines, $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos\alpha$. Since $\cos 120^\circ = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, the term $-2bc\cos\alpha$ equals $-2 \cdot 4 \cdot 6 \cdot \left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) = 24$. Hence $a^2 = 16 + 36 + 24 = 76$ and $a = \sqrt{76} = 2\sqrt{19}$. Side $a$ is opposite the obtuse angle, so it is the longest of the three, consistent with $2\sqrt{19} > 6$.
