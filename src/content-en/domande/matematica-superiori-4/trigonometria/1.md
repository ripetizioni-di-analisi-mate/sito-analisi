Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$, hypotenuse $a = 16$, and $\beta = 60^\circ$. What is the length of leg $b$?

- $8$
- $16 \sqrt{3}$
- $8 \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{32 \sqrt{3}}{3}$

## Explanation

Leg $b$ is opposite angle $\beta$. By the first right-triangle theorem, a leg equals the hypotenuse times the sine of the opposite angle, so $b = a\sin\beta$. Since $\sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, we obtain $b = 16 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 8\sqrt{3}$. The value $8$ is instead the length of leg $c = a\cos\beta$, adjacent to $\beta$.
