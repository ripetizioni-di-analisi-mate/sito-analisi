The sides of a triangle satisfy $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 + bc$. What is the measure of angle $\alpha$?

- $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $150^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

By the law of cosines, $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos\alpha$. Comparison with the given relation yields $-2bc\cos\alpha = bc$, so $\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, since $bc \neq 0$. The angle between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ with cosine $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ is $\alpha = 120^\circ$.
