Two triangles have the same sides $b,c$ enclosing angles $A$ and $\pi-A$ respectively. What is the square of the opposite side in the second triangle minus that in the first?

- $2bc\cos A$
- $4bc\sin A$
- $0$
- $4bc\cos A$

## Explanation

The cosine rule gives $b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A$ for the first square. In the second triangle the cosine reverses sign, so subtraction gives $4bc\cos A$.
