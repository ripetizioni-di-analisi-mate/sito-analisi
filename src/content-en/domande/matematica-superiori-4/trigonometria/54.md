In triangle $ABC$, we have $b = 3$, $c = 8$, and $\alpha = 60^\circ$. What is the length of side $a$?

- $\sqrt{97}$
- $\sqrt{73}$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

Two sides and the included angle are known, so we apply the law of cosines. We have $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos\alpha = 9 + 64 - 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 8 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 73 - 24 = 49$. Therefore $a = 7$. Using a $+$ sign before the cross term would give $\sqrt{97}$, the length of the side opposite a $120^\circ$ angle.
