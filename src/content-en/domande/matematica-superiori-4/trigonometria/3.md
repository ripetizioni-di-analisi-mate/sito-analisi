What is the area of triangle $ABC$ with $a = 10$, $b = 6$, and $\gamma = 135^\circ$?

- $30 \sqrt{2}$
- $15$
- $15 \sqrt{2}$
- $15 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Angle $\gamma$ is included between sides $a$ and $b$, so the area is $S = \dfrac{1}{2}\,ab\sin\gamma$. The identity for supplementary angles gives $\sin 135^\circ = \sin 45^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. Therefore $S = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot 6 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = 15\sqrt{2}$. The formula also holds for an obtuse angle because its sine is positive.
