A triangle has angles $\alpha = 30^\circ$, $\beta = 60^\circ$, and $\gamma = 90^\circ$. What is the ratio of the side lengths $a$, $b$, and $c$?

- $1 : 2 : 3$
- $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$
- $1 : \sqrt{2} : \sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{3} : 1 : 2$

## Explanation

By the law of sines, the sides are proportional to the sines of their opposite angles, so $a : b : c = \sin 30^\circ : \sin 60^\circ : \sin 90^\circ$. These sines equal $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, and $1$. Multiplying by $2$ gives $a : b : c = 1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$. The ratio $1 : 2 : 3$ is the ratio of the angles, which differs from the ratio of the sides.
