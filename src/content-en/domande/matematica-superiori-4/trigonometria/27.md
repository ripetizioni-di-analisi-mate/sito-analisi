In triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at $A$, which equality is true?

- $b = a\sin\gamma$
- $b = c\tan\gamma$
- $c = b\tan\beta$
- $b = a\cos\gamma$

## Explanation

Leg $b = CA$ is opposite angle $\beta$ and adjacent to angle $\gamma$. By the first right-triangle theorem, $b = a\sin\beta = a\cos\gamma$, so $b = a\cos\gamma$ is true. The equalities $b = a\sin\gamma$ and $b = c\tan\gamma$ treat $\gamma$ as if it were opposite $b$. The second theorem gives $c = b\tan\gamma = b\cot\beta$, so $c = b\tan\beta$ is also false in general.
