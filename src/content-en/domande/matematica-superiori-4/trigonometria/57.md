What type of triangle has sides $5$, $12$, and $14$?

- Acute
- Obtuse
- Right
- The triangle does not exist

## Explanation

The triangle exists since $14 < 5 + 12$. Its type depends on the angle opposite the longest side $14$. By the law of cosines, that angle is obtuse if and only if $14^2 > 5^2 + 12^2$. Since $196 > 25 + 144 = 169$, the condition holds and the triangle is **obtuse**.
