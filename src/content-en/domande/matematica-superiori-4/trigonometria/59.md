A parallelogram has sides $3$ and $5$ and an angle of $60^\circ$. How long is its shorter diagonal?

- $7$
- $\sqrt{34}$
- $\sqrt{19}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The shorter diagonal is opposite the $60^\circ$ angle in the triangle whose other sides are sides of the parallelogram. By the law of cosines, its square is $3^2 + 5^2 - 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot \cos 60^\circ = 34 - 15 = 19$. Therefore the shorter diagonal has length $\sqrt{19}$. The longer diagonal is opposite the $120^\circ$ angle and has length $\sqrt{34 + 15} = 7$.
