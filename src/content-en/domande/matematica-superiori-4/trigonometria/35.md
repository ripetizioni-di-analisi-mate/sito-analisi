A triangle has area $18$, side $b = 6$, and angle $\alpha = 45^\circ$. How long is side $c$?

- $12$
- $3 \sqrt{2}$
- $6 \sqrt{2}$
- $12 \sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

Angle $\alpha$ is included between sides $b$ and $c$, so $S = \dfrac{1}{2}\,bc\sin\alpha$. Substitution gives $18 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot c \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \dfrac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\,c$. Therefore $c = \dfrac{36}{3\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{12}{\sqrt{2}} = 6\sqrt{2}$.
