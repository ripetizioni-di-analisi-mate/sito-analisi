A median is drawn in a triangle. What is the ratio of the two resulting areas, even if the apex angle is not bisected?

- $1:2$
- $2:3$
- $1:1$
- It depends on the cosine of the apex angle.

## Explanation

The two bases on the same line are equal by the definition of a median. They share the same altitude from the same vertex.
