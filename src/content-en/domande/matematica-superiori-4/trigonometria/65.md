In a triangle, sides $a = 5$ and $b = 7$ and the included angle $\gamma = 40^\circ$ are known. Which theorem gives an unknown measurement directly?

- The law of sines, which gives angle $\alpha$
- The law of sines, which gives side $c$
- The first right-triangle theorem, which gives the altitude
- The law of cosines, which gives side $c$

## Explanation

Two sides and the included angle form the second case for solving triangles. The law of sines requires a side and its opposite angle. No such pair is known here, since $\gamma$ is opposite the unknown side $c$. The law of cosines gives $c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos\gamma$, in which all quantities except $c$ are known. Therefore the first unknown is found using the law of cosines.
