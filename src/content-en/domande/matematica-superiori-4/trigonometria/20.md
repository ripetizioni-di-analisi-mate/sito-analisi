A diagram has vertical height $3$ and horizontal run $4$. Which ratio gives the tangent of the sloping segment's angle **with the vertical**?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

For the angle measured from the vertical, the horizontal segment is opposite. The adjacent leg is the height, giving $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
