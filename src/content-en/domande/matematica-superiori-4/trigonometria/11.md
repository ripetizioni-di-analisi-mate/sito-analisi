- $25\left( \sqrt{3} - 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50\left( \sqrt{3} + 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\left( \sqrt{3} + 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Let $h$ be the height and $d$ the distance from the base to the nearer point. By the second right-triangle theorem, $h = d\tan 45^\circ = d$ and $h = (d + 50)\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{d + 50}{\sqrt{3}}$. Equating the two expressions gives $\sqrt{3}\,h = h + 50$, hence $h = \dfrac{50}{\sqrt{3} - 1}$. Rationalizing the denominator gives $h = 25\left( \sqrt{3} + 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m} \approx 68.30\,\mathrm{m}$.
