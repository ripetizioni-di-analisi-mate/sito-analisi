Which of the following triples can be the side lengths of a triangle?

- $3$, $5$, $9$
- $4$, $4$, $9$
- $5$, $6$, $10$
- $2$, $7$, $9$

## Explanation

By the triangle inequality, three positive numbers are the side lengths of a triangle if and only if the largest is less than the sum of the other two. For the triple $5$, $6$, $10$, we have $10 < 5 + 6 = 11$, so the condition holds. For $3$, $5$, $9$ and for $4$, $4$, $9$, the largest number exceeds the sum of the other two. For $2$, $7$, $9$, we have $9 = 2 + 7$, and the three vertices would be collinear.
