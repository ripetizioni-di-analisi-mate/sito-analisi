What is the largest angle of the triangle with sides $6$, $8$, and $11$, to the nearest hundredth of a degree?

- $77.36^\circ$
- $115.94^\circ$
- $102.64^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

The largest angle lies opposite the longest side, of length $11$. By the law of cosines,
$$
\cos\gamma = \frac{6^2 + 8^2 - 11^2}{2 \cdot 6 \cdot 8} = \frac{36 + 64 - 121}{96} = -\frac{21}{96} = -\frac{7}{32} .
$$
The cosine is negative, so the angle is obtuse. Since $0^\circ < \gamma < 180^\circ$, we have $\gamma = \arccos\left( -\dfrac{7}{32} \right) \approx 102.64^\circ$.
