To find the distances from a point $P$ to points $A$ and $B$, with $AB = 80\,\mathrm{m}$, the angles $\widehat{PAB} = 60^\circ$ and $\widehat{PBA} = 75^\circ$ are measured. What is $AP$?

- $40\left( \sqrt{3} + 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\left( \sqrt{3} - 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The angle at $P$ is $180^\circ - 60^\circ - 75^\circ = 45^\circ$. Side $AP$ is opposite the angle at $B$, so the law of sines gives $AP = \dfrac{AB\sin 75^\circ}{\sin 45^\circ}$. Since $\sin 75^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$, we obtain
$$
AP = 80 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4} \cdot \frac{2}{\sqrt{2}} = 40\left( \sqrt{3} + 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $40\sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{m}$ is instead $BP$, opposite the $60^\circ$ angle.
