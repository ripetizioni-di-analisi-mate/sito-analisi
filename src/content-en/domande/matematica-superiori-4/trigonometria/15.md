The angles of a triangle are proportional to the numbers $2$, $3$, and $4$. How large is the largest angle?

- $40^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $100^\circ$

## Explanation

Write the angles as $2k$, $3k$, and $4k$ with $k > 0$. The sum of the angles of a triangle is $180^\circ$, so $9k = 180^\circ$ and $k = 20^\circ$. Thus the largest angle is $4k = 80^\circ$ and the other two are $40^\circ$ and $60^\circ$.
