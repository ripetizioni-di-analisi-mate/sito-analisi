In triangle $ABC$, we have $\alpha = 60^\circ$, $b = 8$, and $a = 7$. What values can side $c$ take?

- $5$ only
- $1$ or $15$
- $3$ or $5$
- $3$ only

## Explanation

By the law of cosines, $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos\alpha$, that is, $49 = 64 + c^2 - 8c$. This yields the equation $c^2 - 8c + 15 = 0$ with solutions $c = 3$ and $c = 5$. Both are positive, so two triangles exist. The result agrees with the ambiguous case, since $h = 8\sin 60^\circ = 4\sqrt{3} \approx 6.93$ and $h < 7 < 8$.
