With $A=30^\circ$ and $a=b$ in a triangle, why can the candidate $B=150^\circ$ from the sine calculation be rejected without adding the angles?

- An angle of $150^\circ$ is impossible in every triangle.
- Every isosceles triangle is right-angled.
- Equal sides have equal opposite angles.
- The sine of $150^\circ$ is negative.

## Explanation

Equal sides have equal opposite angles. Therefore $B=A=30^\circ$ and the supplementary candidate is inconsistent with the isosceles condition.
