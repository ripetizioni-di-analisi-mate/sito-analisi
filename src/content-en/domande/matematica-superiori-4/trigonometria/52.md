In triangle $ABC$, we have $a = 10$, $\beta = 45^\circ$, and $\gamma = 105^\circ$. What is the length of side $b$?

- $5 \sqrt{2}$
- $20$
- $10 \sqrt{2}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The third angle is $\alpha = 180^\circ - 45^\circ - 105^\circ = 30^\circ$ and lies opposite the known side $a$. By the law of sines, $b = \dfrac{a\sin\beta}{\sin\alpha} = \dfrac{10 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{2}}$. Therefore $b = 10\sqrt{2}$. The value $20$ is the diameter $2R = \dfrac{a}{\sin\alpha}$ of the circumcircle.
