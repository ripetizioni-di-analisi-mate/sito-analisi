A triangle has side lengths in the ratio $1:1:\sqrt{2}$. Which angle is opposite the longest side?

- $45^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $120^\circ$

## Explanation

Their squared lengths are in the ratio $1:1:2$. The largest square equals the sum of the other two, so the converse of Pythagoras applies.
