A chord $AB$ of length $10$ subtends an angle of $30^\circ$ at a point on the circle. What is the radius of the circle?

- $5$
- $20$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{10 \sqrt{3}}{3}$

## Explanation

By the chord length theorem, $AB = 2r\sin\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is the inscribed angle. Substitution gives $10 = 2r \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = r$. Therefore the radius is $r = 10$, and the chord has the same length as the radius.
