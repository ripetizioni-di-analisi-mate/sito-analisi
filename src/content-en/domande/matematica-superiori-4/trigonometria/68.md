The sides of nondegenerate triangles approach $2$, $5$ and $7$. What does the angle opposite the longest side approach?

- $0^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $60^\circ$

## Explanation

The longest side approaches the sum of the other two. In the cosine formula the limiting value is $\dfrac{2^2+5^2-7^2}{2\cdot 2\cdot 5}=-1$, corresponding to a straight angle.
