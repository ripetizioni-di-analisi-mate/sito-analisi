How many triangles have side lengths $4$, $6$, and $10$?

- Exactly one
- Exactly one, an obtuse triangle
- None
- Two

## Explanation

By the triangle inequality, the longest side must be shorter than the sum of the other two. Here $10 = 4 + 6$, so the inequality $10 < 4 + 6$ does not hold. Therefore a triangle with these sides **does not exist**, and the three points would be collinear.
