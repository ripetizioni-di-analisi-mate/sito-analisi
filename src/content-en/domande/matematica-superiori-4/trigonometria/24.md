A technician uses $\arctan(b/c)$ for the angle opposite leg $b$ of a right triangle. How can the other acute angle be obtained from the same reading without another trigonometric calculation?

- Subtract it from $180^\circ$.
- Subtract the reading from $90^\circ$ if it is in degrees.
- Divide the reading by two.
- Take its negative.

## Explanation

The two acute angles sum to a right angle. If the first is $\beta$, the second is $90^\circ-\beta$ in degrees or $\pi/2-\beta$ in radians.
