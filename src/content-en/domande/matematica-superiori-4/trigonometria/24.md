Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$ and legs $b = 20$ and $c = 21$. What is the measure of angle $\gamma$ to the nearest hundredth of a degree?

- $43.60^\circ$
- $46.40^\circ$
- $35.91^\circ$
- $72.25^\circ$

## Explanation

The leg opposite $\gamma$ is $c$ and the adjacent leg is $b$. The second right-triangle theorem gives $c = b\tan\gamma$, so $\tan\gamma = \dfrac{21}{20}$. Since $\gamma$ is acute, $\gamma = \arctan\dfrac{21}{20} \approx 46.40^\circ$. The result is greater than $45^\circ$, as expected since the opposite leg $c$ is the longer of the two.
