A regular dodecagon is inscribed in a circle of radius $3$. What is its area?

- $54$
- $27 \sqrt{3}$
- $9 \sqrt{3}$
- $27$

## Explanation

Joining the center to the twelve vertices gives twelve congruent isosceles triangles, with two sides equal to the radius $3$ and an included angle of $\dfrac{360^\circ}{12} = 30^\circ$. Each triangle has area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 3 \cdot \sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{9}{4}$. The area of the dodecagon is therefore $12 \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} = 27$.
