Joining the centre of a regular hexagon to its vertices gives six triangles. What makes these triangles equilateral?

- Every inscribed triangle is equilateral.
- Every interior angle of the hexagon is $60^\circ$.
- The radii are perpendicular to the opposite side at its endpoints.
- They have two equal radii enclosing $60^\circ$.

## Explanation

A turn is split into six central angles of $60^\circ$. The two base angles of each isosceles triangle therefore also equal $60^\circ$.
