A road has a $12\%$ grade, defined as rise divided by horizontal run. Which equation gives its angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal?

- $\sin \alpha=0{,}12$
- $\tan \alpha=0{,}12$
- $\cos \alpha=0{,}12$
- $\alpha=12^\circ$

## Explanation

Percent grade is one hundred times the ratio of the legs. The angle is obtained by taking the arctangent of that ratio.
