Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$, leg $c = 9$, and $\beta = 30^\circ$. What is the length of leg $b$?

- $9 \sqrt{3}$
- $3 \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $6 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Leg $b$ is opposite angle $\beta$ and leg $c$ is adjacent. By the second right-triangle theorem, $b = c\tan\beta$. Since $\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$, we obtain $b = 9 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = 3\sqrt{3}$. The value $9\sqrt{3}$ comes from multiplying by $\tan 60^\circ$, that is, from using angle $\gamma$ in place of $\beta$.
