Two forces of $5\,\mathrm{N}$ and $8\,\mathrm{N}$ act at the same point and make an angle of $60^\circ$. What is the magnitude of their resultant?

- $\sqrt{129}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\sqrt{89}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By the parallelogram rule, the magnitude of the resultant is given by $R^2 = F_1^2 + F_2^2 + 2F_1F_2\cos\theta$. With $\theta = 60^\circ$, the cross term equals $2 \cdot 5 \cdot 8 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 40$, so $R^2 = 25 + 64 + 40 = 129$. Therefore $R = \sqrt{129}\,\mathrm{N} \approx 11.36\,\mathrm{N}$. The $+$ sign in front of the cross term follows from the law of cosines applied to the triangle in which the angle opposite $R$ is $180^\circ - \theta$.
