How many triangles have angles of $50^\circ$, $60^\circ$, and $70^\circ$?

- Infinitely many, all similar to one another
- Exactly one
- None
- Two

## Explanation

The angles add up to $180^\circ$, so at least one triangle with these angles exists. Three angles determine only the shape of a triangle, since similar triangles have the same angles and proportional sides. Therefore **infinitely many** triangles have these angles, all similar to one another. A side length is also needed to determine the triangle.
