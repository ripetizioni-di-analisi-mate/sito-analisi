In triangle $ABC$, we have $\alpha = 30^\circ$, $b = 10$, and $a = 6$. What values can angle $\beta$ take, to the nearest hundredth of a degree?

- $56.44^\circ$ or $123.56^\circ$
- $56.44^\circ$ only
- $33.56^\circ$ or $146.44^\circ$
- $123.56^\circ$ only

## Explanation

The distance from $C$ to the line containing side $c$ is $h = 10\sin 30^\circ = 5$, and $5 < 6 < 10$, so two triangles exist. The law of sines gives $\sin\beta = \dfrac{b\sin\alpha}{a} = \dfrac{5}{6}$. The two values are the acute angle $\beta_1 = \arcsin\dfrac{5}{6} \approx 56.44^\circ$ and its supplement $\beta_2 \approx 123.56^\circ$. Both are admissible since $30^\circ + 123.56^\circ < 180^\circ$.
