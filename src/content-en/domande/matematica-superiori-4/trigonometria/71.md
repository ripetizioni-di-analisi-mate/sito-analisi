Which candidate for angle $B$ must be discarded if $A=65^\circ$ and the sine calculation for $B$ yields $40^\circ$ or $140^\circ$?

- $140^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- Both.
- Neither.

## Explanation

Adding $65^\circ+140^\circ$ exceeds a straight angle and leaves no positive third angle. The acute candidate leaves $75^\circ$.
