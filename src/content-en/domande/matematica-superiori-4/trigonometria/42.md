A chord moves farther from the center of a fixed circle. What happens to its length up to the limiting case of tangency?

- It increases to the diameter.
- It stays equal to the radius.
- It decreases to the radius and then increases.
- It decreases to zero.

## Explanation

If $d$ is the distance from the center and $R$ the radius, half the chord is $\sqrt{R^2-d^2}$. As $d$ increases to $R$, this quantity falls to zero and the endpoints merge.
