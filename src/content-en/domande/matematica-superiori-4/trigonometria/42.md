In a circle of radius $4$, chord $AB$ has length $4\sqrt{3}$. What are the measures of the inscribed angles subtended by $AB$?

- $60^\circ$ only
- $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$
- $120^\circ$ only
- $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$

## Explanation

By the chord length theorem, $4\sqrt{3} = 8\sin\alpha$, so $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. The inscribed angles with vertices on one of the two arcs are all equal, and those with vertices on the other arc are their supplements. Since the angles between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ with sine $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ are $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$, the inscribed angles measure $60^\circ$ on one arc and $120^\circ$ on the other.
