Why can the longest side of triangle $ABC$ with circumradius $R$ never exceed $2R$?

- Every side is a chord of the circumcircle.
- All sides are radii.
- The triangle must be acute.
- The diameter is the shortest chord.

## Explanation

By the sine rule each side takes the form $2R \sin A$ using its opposite angle. Sine never exceeds one and equality is attained for a right angle.
