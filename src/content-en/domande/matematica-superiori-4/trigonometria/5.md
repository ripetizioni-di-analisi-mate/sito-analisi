In a circle of radius $8$, chord $AB$ corresponds to a central angle of $60^\circ$. What are the measures of the inscribed angles subtended by $AB$?

- $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$
- $30^\circ$ only
- $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$

## Explanation

The central angle is twice the inscribed angle that intercepts the same arc, so angles with vertices on the major arc measure $\dfrac{60^\circ}{2} = 30^\circ$. The angles with vertices on the other arc are opposite these in an inscribed quadrilateral, so they are supplementary and measure $150^\circ$. The two angles have the same sine, and the chord length theorem gives $AB = 16\sin 30^\circ = 8$ in either case.
