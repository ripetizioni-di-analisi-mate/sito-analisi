A ladder of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ leans against a vertical wall, making an angle of $60^\circ$ with the horizontal floor. At what height does it touch the wall?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{10 \sqrt{3}}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The ladder, wall, and floor form a right triangle whose hypotenuse is the ladder. The required height is the leg opposite the $60^\circ$ angle, so by the first right-triangle theorem it equals $5\sin 60^\circ$. Therefore the height is $\dfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 4.33\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance from the foot of the ladder to the wall.
