A ladder of length $L$ leans against a vertical wall. As the contact height approaches $L$, what happens to the distance of its foot from the wall?

- It approaches $L$.
- It approaches zero.
- It exceeds $L$.
- It approaches $2L$.

## Explanation

The horizontal leg is $\sqrt{L^2-h^2}$. As the height approaches the ladder's length, the expression under the root tends to zero.
