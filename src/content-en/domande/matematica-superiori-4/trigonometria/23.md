Parallel sun rays cast a pole's and a tower's shadows on level ground at the same time. Given the pole's height and shadow, which extra measurement suffices to find the tower's height?

- The tower's shadow length.
- The distance between pole and tower.
- The width of the tower.
- The time expressed in minutes.

## Explanation

The triangles share the solar angle and the same height-to-shadow ratio, the tangent of the elevation. Multiplying the known ratio by the tower's shadow length gives its height.
