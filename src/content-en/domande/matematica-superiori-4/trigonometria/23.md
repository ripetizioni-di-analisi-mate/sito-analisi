Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$, hypotenuse $a = 25$, and leg $b = 7$. What is the measure of angle $\beta$ to the nearest hundredth of a degree?

- $16.26^\circ$
- $73.74^\circ$
- $15.64^\circ$
- $16.96^\circ$

## Explanation

Leg $b$ is opposite $\beta$, so the first right-triangle theorem gives $\sin\beta = \dfrac{b}{a} = \dfrac{7}{25}$. Angle $\beta$ is acute, hence $\beta = \arcsin\dfrac{7}{25} \approx 16.26^\circ$. The value $73.74^\circ$ is the complementary angle $\gamma$, for which $\cos\gamma = \dfrac{7}{25}$.
