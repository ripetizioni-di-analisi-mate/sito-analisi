Chord $AB$ is seen from a point $P$ on the circle at an angle of $100^\circ$. What angle does it subtend at point $Q$ on the other arc with endpoints $A$ and $B$?

- $100^\circ$
- $50^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $40^\circ$

## Explanation

The points $A$, $P$, $B$, and $Q$ are the vertices of an inscribed quadrilateral in which the angles at $P$ and $Q$ are opposite. Opposite angles of an inscribed quadrilateral are supplementary, so the angle at $Q$ is $180^\circ - 100^\circ = 80^\circ$. The two angles have the same sine, in agreement with the chord length theorem.
