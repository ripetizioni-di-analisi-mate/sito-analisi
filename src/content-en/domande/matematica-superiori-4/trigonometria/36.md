A triangle has sides $5$ and $7$ enclosing $120^\circ$. What is the **signed projection** of the side of length $5$ onto the direction of the other?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The projection is $5\cos (120^\circ)$ and is negative because the angle is obtuse. The cosine is $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.
