In the triangle with $a = 12$, $b = 8$, and $\gamma = 60^\circ$, how long is the altitude to side $a$?

- $6 \sqrt{3}$
- $4 \sqrt{3}$
- $4$
- $24 \sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Let $H$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$ to line $BC$. Triangle $AHC$ has a right angle at $H$, hypotenuse $AC = b$, and an angle at $C$ equal to $\gamma$, which is acute. By the first right-triangle theorem, the altitude is $h = b\sin\gamma = 8 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 4\sqrt{3}$. The same value follows from the area $S = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 12 \cdot 8 \cdot \sin 60^\circ = 24\sqrt{3}$ by dividing $2S$ by $a = 12$.
