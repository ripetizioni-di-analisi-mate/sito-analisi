A camera stands $2$ metres above level ground. It sees the top of a wall $10$ metres away horizontally at elevation $45^\circ$. What correction converts the measurement to height above ground?

- Subtract the horizontal distance.
- Subtract the camera height from the rise.
- Halve the angle.
- Add the camera height to the measured rise.

## Explanation

Tangent gives the rise from the camera, $10\tan (45^\circ)=10$ metres. The height above ground is therefore $2+10=12$ metres.
