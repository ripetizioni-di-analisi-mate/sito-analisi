A triangle has sides of lengths $6$ and $10$ and included angle $x$, with $0 < x < \pi$. For which value of $x$ does the third side have length $14$?

- $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

By the law of cosines, the square of the third side is $36 + 100 - 120\cos x = 136 - 120\cos x$. The condition $136 - 120\cos x = 196$ is equivalent to $\cos x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. On the interval $0 < x < \pi$, the cosine determines a unique angle, so $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.
