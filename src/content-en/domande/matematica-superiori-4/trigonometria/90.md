Two sides of a triangle are $3$ and $5$ and the included angle is $60^\circ$. Which expression gives the length of the internal angle bisector from that angle?

- $\dfrac{2\cdot 3\cdot 5\cos (30^\circ)}{3+5}$
- $\dfrac{2\cdot 3\cdot 5\sin (30^\circ)}{3+5}$
- $\dfrac{3+5}{2\cos (30^\circ)}$
- $\dfrac{3\cdot 5\cos (60^\circ)}{3+5}$

## Explanation

If the bisector has length $d$, the two subtriangle areas add to $\dfrac{1}{2}d(3+5)\sin (30^\circ)$. Equating this to $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot 3\cdot 5\sin (60^\circ)$ and using the double-angle sine gives the length.
