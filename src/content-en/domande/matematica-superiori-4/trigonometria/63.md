After rounding the measured side lengths, a calculation returns $1.0003$ as the cosine of an interior angle. Why does the calculator report an error before even assessing whether the triangle is acute?

- $\arccos$ accepts only integers.
- The value is outside the real domain of $\arccos$.
- A positive cosine always means the triangle is impossible.
- Converting to degrees would change the domain.

## Explanation

Real cosine takes values in $[-1,1]$ regardless of the angular unit. A value above one flags inconsistent data or a numerical error and gives no real angle.
