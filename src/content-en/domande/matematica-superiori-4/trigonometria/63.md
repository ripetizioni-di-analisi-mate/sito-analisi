In triangle $ABC$, we have $a = 7$, $b = 5$, and $\alpha = 60^\circ$. What is the length of side $c$?

- $3$
- $8$
- $2 \sqrt{6}$
- $5$

## Explanation

By the law of cosines, $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos\alpha$, that is, $49 = 25 + c^2 - 5c$. This gives the equation $c^2 - 5c - 24 = 0$ with solutions $c = 8$ and $c = -3$. A side length is positive, so $c = 8$. The triangle is unique, consistent with the ambiguous case, because $\alpha$ is acute and $a \geq b$.
