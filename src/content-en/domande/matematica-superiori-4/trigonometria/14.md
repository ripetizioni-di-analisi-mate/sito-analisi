Rectangle $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle of radius $r$, and $x = \widehat{CAB}$ is the angle between diagonal $AC$ and side $AB$. For which values of $x$ does the rectangle have area $\sqrt{3}\,r^2$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ only
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ or $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{12}$ or $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{12}$

## Explanation

Diagonal $AC$ is a diameter, so $AC = 2r$ and the restrictions are $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. In triangle $ABC$, with a right angle at $B$, the first right-triangle theorem gives $AB = 2r\cos x$ and $BC = 2r\sin x$. The area is $4r^2\sin x\cos x = 2r^2\sin 2x$, so the condition becomes $\sin 2x = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. Since $0 < 2x < \pi$, we have $2x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ or $2x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, that is, $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.
