What is the radius of the circumcircle of the triangle with sides $5$, $5$, and $6$?

- $\dfrac{25}{8}$
- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The triangle is isosceles, and the altitude to the base of length $6$ meets it at its midpoint. By the Pythagorean theorem, the altitude has length $\sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$, so the area is $S = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 4 = 12$. The circumradius formula gives $R = \dfrac{abc}{4S} = \dfrac{5 \cdot 5 \cdot 6}{48} = \dfrac{25}{8}$.
