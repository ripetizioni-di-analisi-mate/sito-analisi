In triangle $ABC$, we have $\alpha = 30^\circ$, $a = 6$, and $b = 6\sqrt{3}$. What values can angle $\gamma$ take?

- $90^\circ$ only
- $90^\circ$ or $30^\circ$
- $60^\circ$ or $120^\circ$
- $30^\circ$ only

## Explanation

The distance from $C$ to the line containing side $c$ is $h = 6\sqrt{3} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 3\sqrt{3} \approx 5.20$, and $h < 6 < 6\sqrt{3}$, so there are two triangles. The law of sines gives $\sin\beta = \dfrac{6\sqrt{3} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, so $\beta = 60^\circ$ or $\beta = 120^\circ$. The sum of the angles gives $\gamma = 90^\circ$ in the first triangle and $\gamma = 30^\circ$ in the second.
