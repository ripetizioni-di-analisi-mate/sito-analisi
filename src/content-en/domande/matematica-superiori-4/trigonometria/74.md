Two triangles fit the same side-side-opposite-angle data and have supplementary possible angles $B$. What can be said about their circumradii?

- One is twice the other.
- They are equal.
- One must be zero.
- They are equal only for right triangles.

## Explanation

Both triangles share the given side $a$ and its opposite angle $A$. The sine rule gives $R=\dfrac{a}{2\sin A}$ in both cases.
