A student turns $\cot x=0$ into $\tan x=\dfrac{1}{0}$ and concludes that the equation has no solution. What is the error?

- Cotangent is always positive.
- Sine must be zero.
- Cotangent has an infinite period.
- Cotangent vanishes where cosine is zero and sine is nonzero.

## Explanation

From the definition $\cot x=\dfrac{\cos x}{\sin x}$, the numerator must vanish and the denominator must be defined and nonzero. The values $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+k\pi$ meet these conditions.
