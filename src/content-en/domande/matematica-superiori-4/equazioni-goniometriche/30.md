What are the solutions of the equation $\cos (2x) = -1$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$
- $x = k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$

## Explanation

By the special-case formula for cosine, the solutions of the equation $\cos t = -1$ are $t = \pi + 2k\pi$. Setting $t = 2x$ gives $2x = \pi + 2k\pi$. Dividing both terms by $2$ gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, so the period of the solutions is $\pi$.
