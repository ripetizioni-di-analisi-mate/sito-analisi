What are the solutions of the equation $2\sin^2 x = 1$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $\sin^2 x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\sin x = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ or $\sin x = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. The first equation gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$, and the second gives $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{7\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$. The angles $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ are spaced $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ apart, so the four families combine into $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$.
