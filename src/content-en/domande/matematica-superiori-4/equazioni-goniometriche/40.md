At which of the following points is the left-hand side of the equation $\tan (2x) = 1$ undefined?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\pi$

## Explanation

The tangent of $2x$ is defined for $2x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, that is, for $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$. The point $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ is obtained for $k = 0$, so the left-hand side is undefined there. At $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\pi$, we have $2x = \pi$ and $2x = 2\pi$, where the tangent equals $0$. At $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$, we have $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{4} = 1$, so $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$ is a solution.
