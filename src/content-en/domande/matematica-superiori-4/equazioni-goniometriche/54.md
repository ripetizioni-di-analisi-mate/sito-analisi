Which points on the unit circle can solve $\sin^4 x+\cos^4 x=1$?

- Only points in the first quadrant.
- All points on the line $y=x$.
- The entire circle.
- The four points on the axes.

## Explanation

From $(\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x)^2=1$ we obtain $\sin^4 x+\cos^4 x=1-2\sin^2 x \cos^2 x$. Equality requires at least one coordinate to vanish.
