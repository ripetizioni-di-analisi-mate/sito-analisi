For which values of the real parameter $m$ does the equation $\sin x = 3m + 1$ have solutions?

- $-1 \leq m \leq 1$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3} \leq m \leq 0$
- $0 \leq m \leq \dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3} < m < 0$

## Explanation

The basic sine equation has solutions if and only if the right-hand side lies between $-1$ and $1$, so we require $-1 \leq 3m + 1 \leq 1$. Subtracting $1$ from all three parts gives $-2 \leq 3m \leq 0$. Dividing by $3$, which is positive, gives $-\dfrac{2}{3} \leq m \leq 0$.
