For how many ordered pairs $(x,y)$ in $[0,2\pi)\times[0,2\pi)$ do $\cos x=1$ and $\sin y=\cos x$ both hold?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first equation fixes $x=0$. Substitution in the second gives $\sin y=1$, hence $y=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Both coordinates of the pair are fixed.
