A program searches for zeros of $\tan x$ by bisection whenever two successive readings have opposite signs. Why would that conclusion fail on $[\dfrac{\pi}{3},\dfrac{2\pi}{3}]$?

- Tangent is always negative.
- The interval contains two zeros.
- Both endpoints are zeros.
- There is a discontinuity between the endpoints.

## Explanation

Tangent changes from positive to negative values across the asymptote $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. This interval contains no multiple of $\pi$, so it contains no zero.
