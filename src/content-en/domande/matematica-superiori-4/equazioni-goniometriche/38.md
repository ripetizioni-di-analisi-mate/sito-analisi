For which values of the real parameter $m$ does the equation $\cos x = m - 2$ have solutions?

- $-1 \leq m \leq 1$
- $-3 \leq m \leq -1$
- $1 \leq m \leq 3$
- $1 < m < 3$

## Explanation

The basic cosine equation has solutions if and only if the right-hand side lies between $-1$ and $1$. We therefore require $-1 \leq m - 2 \leq 1$. Adding $2$ to all three parts gives $1 \leq m \leq 3$. The endpoints are included because for $m = 1$ and for $m = 3$ the equation becomes $\cos x = -1$ and $\cos x = 1$, respectively, and these equations have solutions.
