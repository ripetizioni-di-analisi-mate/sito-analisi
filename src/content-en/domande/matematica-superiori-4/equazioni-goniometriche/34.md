For which $x$ in $[0,2\pi)$ do $\sin x=0$ and $\cos x=0$ hold simultaneously?

- $\{0,\pi \}$
- $\{\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{0,\dfrac{\pi}{2},\pi,\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\}$

## Explanation

If both coordinates vanished, we would have $\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x=0$. The Pythagorean identity requires the value $1$.
