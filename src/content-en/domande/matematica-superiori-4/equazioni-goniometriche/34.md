What are the solutions of the equation $\tan (3x) = 1$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{12} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 3k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{12} + \dfrac{k\pi}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{12} + \dfrac{2k\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

Since $\arctan 1 = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, we have $3x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$. Dividing both terms by $3$ gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{12} + \dfrac{k\pi}{3}$. The period of the solutions is therefore $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, that is, $\dfrac{\pi}{|a|}$ with $a = 3$.
