Which solutions of the equation $\tan x = 1$ lie in the interval $[-\pi, \pi]$?

- Only the value $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The solutions are $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$. The condition $-\pi \leq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi \leq \pi$ is equivalent to $-\dfrac{5\pi}{4} \leq k\pi \leq \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, and dividing by $\pi$ gives $-\dfrac{5}{4} \leq k \leq \dfrac{3}{4}$. The integers satisfying this condition are $k = -1$ and $k = 0$. This therefore gives $-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
