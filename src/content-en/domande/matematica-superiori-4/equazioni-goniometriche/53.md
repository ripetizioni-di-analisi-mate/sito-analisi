What are the solutions of the equation $\cos^2 x + 2\cos x - 3 = 0$?

- $x = 2k\pi$
- $x = k\pi$
- $x = \pi + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$

## Explanation

Setting $t = \cos x$ gives $t^2 + 2t - 3 = 0$, that is, $(t + 3)(t - 1) = 0$. The value $t = -3$ is inadmissible because the cosine cannot be less than $-1$. This leaves $\cos x = 1$, which by the special-case formula for cosine gives $x = 2k\pi$.
