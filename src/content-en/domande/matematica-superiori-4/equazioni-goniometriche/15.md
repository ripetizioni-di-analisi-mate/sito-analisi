Which of the following numbers is a solution of the equation $\tan x = -1$?

- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

Since $\arctan(-1) = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, the solutions are $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$. Setting $k = 1$ gives $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, so $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ is a solution. The numbers $-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$ differ from $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ by a multiple of $\pi$, so the tangent equals $1$ at those points.
