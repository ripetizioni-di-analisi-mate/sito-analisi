How many distinct values does $\sin(n\pi)$ take as $n$ ranges over the integers?

- $2$
- $3$
- Infinitely many
- $1$

## Explanation

The angles $n\pi$, with $n$ an integer, correspond on the unit circle to the point $(1,0)$, for even $n$, and to the point $(-1,0)$, for odd $n$. Both points have ordinate $0$, so $\sin(n\pi)=0$ for every integer $n$. The expression therefore takes **a single value**, namely $0$.
