A sensor records $s(t)=\sin (\pi t)$ at integer times $t=0,1,2,\ldots$. How many distinct readings appear?

- $2$
- $3$
- Infinitely many
- $1$

## Explanation

For every integer $n$ we have $\sin (n\pi)=0$. Although the signal oscillates between readings, every sampling time is a zero.
