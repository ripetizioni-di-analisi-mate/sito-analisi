What are the solutions of the equation $\tan\left(x - \dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) = \sqrt{3}$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$

## Explanation

Since $\arctan\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, we have $x - \dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$. Adding $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$. The domain restrictions require $x - \dfrac{\pi}{6} \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, that is, $x \neq \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + k\pi$, and the solutions found satisfy them. At $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, the tangent of $x$ is undefined, but the equation contains the tangent of $x - \dfrac{\pi}{6}$, which equals $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \sqrt{3}$ at that point.
