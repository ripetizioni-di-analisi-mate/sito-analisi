Does $\sin x=\dfrac{1}{\cos x}$ have real solutions?

- Yes, at $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
- Yes, at $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
- No, the required product exceeds its maximum.
- Yes, whenever $\sin x=\cos x$.

## Explanation

On the domain we would need $\sin x \cos x=1$. Since $\sin x \cos x=\dfrac{1}{2}\sin (2x)$, the absolute value is at most $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
