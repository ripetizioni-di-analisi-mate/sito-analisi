Which of the following numbers is not a solution of the equation $\sin^2 x = \sin x$?

- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $\sin x\,(\sin x - 1) = 0$, so the solutions are the values satisfying $\sin x = 0$ or $\sin x = 1$. At $\pi$ and $2\pi$, the sine equals $0$, and at $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ it equals $1$, so these three numbers are solutions. At $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, the sine equals $-1$, so the left-hand side equals $1$ and the right-hand side equals $-1$. Therefore $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ is not a solution.
