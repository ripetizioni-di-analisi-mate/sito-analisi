Two angles $x,y$ have the same sine and the same cosine. What condition on their difference is necessary and sufficient?

- $x-y=k\pi$, $k\in \mathbb Z$
- $x-y=2k\pi$, $k\in \mathbb Z$
- $x+y=\pi$
- $x-y=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The sine and cosine determine both coordinates of the point on the circle. To reach the same point, the angles must differ by a whole number of turns.
