Let $x$ be an angle such that $\sin x=2\cos x$. What is the value of $\dfrac{\cos x+3\sin x}{2\cos x+\sin x}$?

- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac74$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

If $\cos x=0$, the condition would also give $\sin x=0$, which is impossible because $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$; so $\cos x\neq0$. Substituting $\sin x=2\cos x$, the numerator becomes $\cos x+6\cos x=7\cos x$ and the denominator becomes $2\cos x+2\cos x=4\cos x$. Since $\cos x\neq0$, one can cancel $\cos x$, so the fraction equals $\dfrac{7}{4}$.
