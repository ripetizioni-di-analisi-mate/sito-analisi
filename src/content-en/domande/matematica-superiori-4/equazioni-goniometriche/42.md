A robot must face a direction $x$ satisfying $\sin x=2\cos x$. What is $\dfrac{\cos x+3\sin x}{2\cos x+\sin x}$ without selecting a particular angle?

- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac74$
- $\dfrac53$
- $2$

## Explanation

If $\cos x=0$, the condition would also force the sine to be zero, which is impossible. Replace the sine by $2\cos x$ to get $7\cos x$ over $4\cos x$ and cancel the common factor.
