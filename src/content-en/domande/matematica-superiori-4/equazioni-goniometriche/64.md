What is the sum of the roots of $\sin x+\cos x=0$ in $[0,2\pi)$?

- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The intersections with the line $y=-x$ have angles $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$. Adding them gives $\dfrac{10\pi}{4}=\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$.
