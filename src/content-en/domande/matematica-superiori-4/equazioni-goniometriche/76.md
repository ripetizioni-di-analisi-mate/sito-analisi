What are the solutions of the equation $\sin^2 x + \sin x\cos x = 0$?

- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ or $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = k\pi$ or $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $\sin^2 x$ is $1 \neq 0$, so points with $\cos x = 0$ are not solutions and we can divide by $\cos^2 x$. This gives $\tan^2 x + \tan x = 0$, that is, $\tan x\,(\tan x + 1) = 0$. Therefore $\tan x = 0$ or $\tan x = -1$. The first equation gives $x = k\pi$, and the second gives $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$.
