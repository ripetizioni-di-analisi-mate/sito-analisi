In $\sin x=\sqrt{1-\cos^2 x}$, what condition remains after using the Pythagorean identity?

- $\sin x\le 0$
- $\cos x\ge 0$
- $\cos x=0$
- $\sin x\ge 0$

## Explanation

The right side becomes $\sqrt{\sin^2 x}=\lvert \sin x\rvert$. A number equals its absolute value exactly when it is nonnegative.
