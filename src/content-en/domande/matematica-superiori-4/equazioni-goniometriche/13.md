A point moves along a line with position given by $s(t) = 3\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{4}$, where $s$ is measured in centimeters and $t$ in seconds. At what times in the interval $[0, 8]$ is the point at $s = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $t = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = \dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = \dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = \dfrac{22}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = \dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The condition $3\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ is equivalent to $\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\dfrac{\pi t}{4} = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$. Multiplying by $\dfrac{4}{\pi}$ gives $t = \pm\dfrac{4}{3} + 8k$. In the interval $[0, 8]$, the family with the plus sign gives $t = \dfrac{4}{3}$ for $k = 0$. The family with the minus sign gives $t = -\dfrac{4}{3} + 8 = \dfrac{20}{3}$ for $k = 1$.
