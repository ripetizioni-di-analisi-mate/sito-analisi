What are the solutions of the equation $\sin^2 x - 2\sqrt{3}\sin x\cos x + 3\cos^2 x = 0$?

- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $\sin^2 x$ is $1 \neq 0$, so divide by $\cos^2 x$ to obtain $\tan^2 x - 2\sqrt{3}\tan x + 3 = 0$. The left-hand side is the square $\left(\tan x - \sqrt{3}\right)^2$, so the equation is equivalent to $\tan x = \sqrt{3}$. The solutions are therefore $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$.
