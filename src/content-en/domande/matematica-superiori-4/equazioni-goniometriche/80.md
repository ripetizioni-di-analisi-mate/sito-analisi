Consider $\sin x+\cos x=c$. Which listed value of $c$ produces **exactly one** solution in $[0,2\pi)$?

- $0$
- $\sqrt2$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is $\sqrt2\sin(x+\dfrac\pi4)$. Its maximum $\sqrt2$ occurs once per period. The listed interior levels are crossed twice, whereas $2$ is unreachable.
