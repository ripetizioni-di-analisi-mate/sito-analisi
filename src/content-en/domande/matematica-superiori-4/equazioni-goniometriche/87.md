Two signals have phases $2t$ and $3t$. What is the first $t>0$ for which both phases are integer multiples of $2\pi$?

- $\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The first phase completes a whole number of turns if $t=k\pi$. For $3k\pi$ to be a multiple of $2\pi$ as well, $k$ must be even. The first positive integer that works is two.
