In the graphical method, let $X = \cos x$ and $Y = \sin x$. Which points on the unit circle correspond to the solutions of the equation $\sin x - \cos x = 1$?

- $(1, 0)$ and $(0, 1)$
- $(0, -1)$ and $(1, 0)$
- $(0, 1)$ and $(-1, 0)$
- Only the point $(0, 1)$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
-X + Y = 1 \\[4pt]
X^2 + Y^2 = 1
\end{cases}
$$
Substituting $Y = 1 + X$ into the second equation gives $X^2 + 1 + 2X + X^2 = 1$, that is, $2X\,(X + 1) = 0$. For $X = 0$ we have $Y = 1$, and for $X = -1$ we have $Y = 0$. The points are therefore $(0, 1)$ and $(-1, 0)$, corresponding to the angles $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$ and $\pi + 2k\pi$.
