What are the solutions of the equation $\sin^2 x + \sin x\cos x = 1$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ or $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$

## Explanation

Write the constant term as $1 = \sin^2 x + \cos^2 x$, which gives the equation $\sin x\cos x - \cos^2 x = 0$. The coefficient of $\sin^2 x$ is now $0$, so factor out $\cos x$ to obtain $\cos x\,(\sin x - \cos x) = 0$. The first factor gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$. The second gives $\tan x = 1$, that is, $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$.
