In $a \sin^2 x+b \cos^2 x=0$ the coefficients satisfy $a>0$ and $b>0$. How many real solutions are there?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

Both terms are nonnegative. For their sum to vanish both would have to be zero, which $\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x=1$ rules out.
