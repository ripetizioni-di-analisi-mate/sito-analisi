Can one and the same angle $x$ satisfy $\cos x=\cos(x+2\pi/3)=\cos(x+4\pi/3)$?

- No, a vertical line intersects the unit circle in at most two points.
- Yes, for every $x$.
- Yes, take $x=0$.
- Yes, because the three angles add up to one turn.

## Explanation

The angles describe three distinct points on the circle separated by one third of a turn. Equal cosines would put all three on one vertical line, and a vertical line meets the circle in at most two points.
