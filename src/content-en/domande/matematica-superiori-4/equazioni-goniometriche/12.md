For which values of the real parameter $m$ does the equation $\cos x = 2m - 1$ have exactly two solutions in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$?

- $0 \leq m \leq 1$
- $-1 < m < 1$
- $0 < m \leq 1$
- $0 < m < 1$

## Explanation

In $[0, 2\pi)$, the horizontal line $y = h$ intersects the graph of $y = \cos x$ at two points for $-1 < h < 1$, whereas for $h = 1$ and for $h = -1$ it intersects the graph at only one point, $x = 0$ and $x = \pi$, respectively. There are therefore two solutions for $-1 < 2m - 1 < 1$. Adding $1$ to all three parts gives $0 < 2m < 2$. Dividing by $2$ gives $0 < m < 1$.
