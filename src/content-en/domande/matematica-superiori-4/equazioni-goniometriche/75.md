Taking account of its domain, which roots does $\tan x=\sin x$ have in $[0,2\pi)$?

- $\{0,\pi \}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\}$
- $\{0,\dfrac{\pi}{2},\pi \}$

## Explanation

Where $\cos x\ne 0$, multiplication by cosine gives $\sin x(1-\cos x)=0$. The roots of the second factor are already among those of the first.
