What are the solutions of the equation $\sin^2 x - 3\cos^2 x = 0$?

- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $\sin^2 x$ is $1 \neq 0$, so points with $\cos x = 0$ are not solutions and we can divide by $\cos^2 x$. This gives $\tan^2 x = 3$, that is, $\tan x = \sqrt{3}$ or $\tan x = -\sqrt{3}$. The solutions are therefore $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$ or $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$, which can be written together as $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$.
