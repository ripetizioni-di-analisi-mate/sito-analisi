What are the solutions of the equation $\cot (2x) = 0$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$

## Explanation

The cotangent of $2x$ is $\dfrac{\cos(2x)}{\sin(2x)}$, so it vanishes where $\cos(2x) = 0$ and $\sin(2x) \neq 0$. The equation $\cos(2x) = 0$ gives $2x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, that is, $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$. At these points $\sin(2x) = \pm 1$, so the condition $\sin(2x) \neq 0$ is satisfied and all the solutions found are admissible.
