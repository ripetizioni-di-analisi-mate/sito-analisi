The system $\cos x=a$, $\sin x=b$ has a solution. If $a=\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $b<0$, what is $b$?

- $-\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $-\dfrac{16}{25}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The unit-circle identity gives $b^2=1-\dfrac{9}{25}=\dfrac{16}{25}$. The sign condition selects $b=-\dfrac{4}{5}$.
