The two points satisfying $\sin x=a$, with $0<a<1$, are marked on the unit circle. What is the sum of their two angles in $[0,\pi]$?

- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\pi$
- $2\arcsin a$

## Explanation

The points have the same vertical coordinate and are reflections across the vertical axis. If the first angle is $\alpha$, the second is $\pi-\alpha$, so the sum is $\pi$.
