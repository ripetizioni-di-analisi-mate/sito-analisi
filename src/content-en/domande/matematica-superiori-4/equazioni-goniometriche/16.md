Which of the following numbers is not a solution of the equation $\sin x = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$?

- $\dfrac{9\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $-\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The solutions are $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$. The number $\dfrac{9\pi}{4}$ comes from the first family with $k = 1$, and $-\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$ comes from the second with $k = -1$. The number $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ belongs to the second family with $k = 0$. In contrast, the number $-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ belongs to neither family, and indeed $\sin\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
