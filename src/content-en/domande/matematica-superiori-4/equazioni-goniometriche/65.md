What condition on $\alpha$ makes $\sin (x+\alpha)=\cos x$ true for **every** real $x$?

- $\alpha=-\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi$
- $\alpha=k\pi$
- $\alpha=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi$
- $\alpha=\dfrac{\pi}{4}+2k\pi$

## Explanation

Setting $x=0$ requires $\sin \alpha=1$. For $\alpha=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi$ the addition formula gives exactly $\cos x$ for every $x$.
