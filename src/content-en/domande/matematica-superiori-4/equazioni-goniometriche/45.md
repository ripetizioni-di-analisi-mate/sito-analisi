Can the equations $\sin x=\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\sin(3x)=1$ hold at the same angle, given that $\sin(3x)=3\sin x-4\sin^3x$?

- Yes, because $3\cdot(\dfrac{1}{3})=1$.
- No, the first forces $\sin(3x)=\dfrac{23}{27}$.
- Yes, at any first-quadrant angle.
- No, because sine cannot equal $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

## Explanation

Substituting into the triple-angle formula gives $1-\dfrac{4}{27}=\dfrac{23}{27}$. The cubic term prevents replacing the sine of the triple angle by three times the sine.
