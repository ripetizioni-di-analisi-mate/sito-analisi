The equation $\cos^2x-\sin^2x=\cos x+\sin x$ contains the factor $\cos x+\sin x$. If that factor is nonzero, which condition remains?

- $\cos x-\sin x=1$
- $\cos x+\sin x=1$
- $\cos x-\sin x=0$
- $\cos x \sin x=1$

## Explanation

The left side is a difference of squares equal to $(\cos x+\sin x)(\cos x-\sin x)$. Under the stated condition, cancelling the common factor leaves one on the right.
