What are the solutions of the equation $2\cos^2 x + \cos x = 0$?

- $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ or $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ or $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

Factoring out $\cos x$ gives $\cos x\,(2\cos x + 1) = 0$. The zero-product property gives $\cos x = 0$ or $\cos x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The first equation gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$. The second gives $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$ because $\arccos\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.
