The equation $1-\cos x=0$ has the solution $x=0$. What is the sign of the function $f(x)=1-\cos x$ for $x$ close to $0$ and different from $0$?

- Negative to the left of $0$ and positive to the right.
- Positive to the left of $0$ and negative to the right.
- Positive on both sides of $0$.
- Negative on both sides of $0$.

## Explanation

Cosine equals $1$ only at the angles $x=2k\pi$, and among these the only one with $|x|<2\pi$ is $x=0$. So for $0<|x|<2\pi$ we have $\cos x<1$, that is, $f(x)=1-\cos x>0$. The function is therefore **positive on both sides** of $0$, and its zero $x=0$ is not a point where it changes sign.
