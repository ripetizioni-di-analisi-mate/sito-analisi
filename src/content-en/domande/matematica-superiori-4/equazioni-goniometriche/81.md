Which of the following equations is homogeneous and quadratic in sine and cosine?

- $\sin^2 x + \cos x = 0$
- $\sin x\cos x + \cos x = 0$
- $\sin^2 x - \cos^2 x = \sin x$
- $2\sin^2 x - \sin x\cos x = 0$

## Explanation

A homogeneous quadratic equation has the form $a\sin^2 x + b\sin x\cos x + c\cos^2 x = 0$, in which every term has total degree $2$ in $\sin x$ and $\cos x$. The equation $2\sin^2 x - \sin x\cos x = 0$ has this form, with $a = 2$, $b = -1$, and $c = 0$. The other three equations contain terms of degree $2$ together with a term of degree $1$, namely $\cos x$ or $\sin x$.
