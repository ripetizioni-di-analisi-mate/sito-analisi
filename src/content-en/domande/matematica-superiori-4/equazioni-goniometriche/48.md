Both $\sin x=a$ and $\sin x=b$ have solutions. When these equations are combined as $(\sin x-a)(\sin x-b)=0$, when should their shared roots be counted only once?

- When $a=-b$ and $a\ne 0$.
- When $a+b=1$.
- When $a=b$.
- Whenever $a$ and $b$ have opposite signs.

## Explanation

Every angle has a single sine value. An angle can therefore make both factors vanish exactly when the two levels coincide.
