How many distinct solutions does $(\sin x-1)^2=0$ have on the closed interval $[0,2\pi]$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The square vanishes only when $\sin x=1$. In the interval this happens at $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The power repeats the factor without creating a second angle.
