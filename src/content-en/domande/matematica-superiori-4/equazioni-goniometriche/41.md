What are the solutions of the equation $\cos\dfrac{x}{3} = 0$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + \dfrac{k\pi}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 6k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 3k\pi$

## Explanation

By the special-case formula for cosine, $\dfrac{x}{3} = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$. Multiplying both terms by $3$ gives $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 3k\pi$. The term $k\pi$ is also multiplied by $3$, so the period of the solutions is $3\pi$.
