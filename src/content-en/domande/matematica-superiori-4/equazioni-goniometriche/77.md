The system $\sin (x+y)=0$, $x-y=0$ is considered with $0\le x,y\le \pi$. Which pair is the solution in the interior of the square?

- $(0,0)$
- $(\pi,\pi)$
- $(\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{\pi}{2})$
- $(\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{\pi}{4})$

## Explanation

The second equation forces $x=y$. Inside the square we must therefore have $0<2x<2\pi$ and $\sin (2x)=0$, giving $2x=\pi$.
