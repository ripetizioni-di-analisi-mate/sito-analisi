Which solutions of the equation $\cos (2x) = \cos x$ lie in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$?

- $0$, $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, $\pi$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$
- $0$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $0$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$, $2\pi$
- $0$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

By the theorem for angles with equal cosines, $2x = x + 2k\pi$ or $2x = -x + 2k\pi$. The first equation gives $x = 2k\pi$, and the second gives $3x = 2k\pi$, that is, $x = \dfrac{2k\pi}{3}$. The first family is contained in the second. In $[0, 2\pi)$, the second family gives $0$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ for $k = 0, 1, 2$, whereas for $k = 3$ it gives $2\pi$, which does not belong to the interval.
