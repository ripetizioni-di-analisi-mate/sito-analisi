A calculator returns one value for $\arcsin$. Why is adding $2\pi$ to the displayed number insufficient to find **all** the roots of $\sin x=-0.2$ on $[0,2\pi)$?

- Sine has period $\pi$.
- The point reflected across the vertical axis is missing.
- The calculator returns a positive value.
- Sine is negative only in the fourth quadrant.

## Explanation

The principal value is negative, and adding a turn gives its fourth-quadrant representative. However, the horizontal line at height $-0.2$ also intersects the circle in the third quadrant.
