- $x = \pi + 2k\pi$ or $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = 2k\pi$ or $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = 2k\pi$ or $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

Setting $t = \cos x$ gives $2t^2 - t - 1 = 0$, which has $\Delta = 1 + 8 = 9$, so $t = \dfrac{1 \pm 3}{4}$. The solutions are $t = 1$ and $t = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, both between $-1$ and $1$. The equation $\cos x = 1$ gives $x = 2k\pi$. Since $\arccos\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, the equation $\cos x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$.
