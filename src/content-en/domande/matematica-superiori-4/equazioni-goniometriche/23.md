What are the solutions of the equation $\sin (2x) = -1$?

- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

By the special-case formula for sine, the solutions of the equation $\sin t = -1$ are $t = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$. Setting $t = 2x$ gives $2x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$. Dividing both terms by $2$ gives $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$, so the period of the solutions is $\pi$.
