Can $\sin x=a$ have exactly three solutions on a closed interval of length $2\pi$, with $-1<a<1$? When does it happen?

- When the endpoints are both solutions.
- For every choice of endpoints.
- Only when sine has a maximum in the interior.
- Only when $a=1$.

## Explanation

A half-open interval spanning one turn contains two solutions. Closing the interval adds a third input exactly if the excluded endpoint satisfies the equation, as the other endpoint then does by periodicity.
