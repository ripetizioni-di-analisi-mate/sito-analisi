What are the domain restrictions for the equation $\dfrac{1}{\sin x} + \tan x = 2$?

- $x \neq \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$
- $x \neq k\pi$
- $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$
- $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The fraction $\dfrac{1}{\sin x}$ requires $\sin x \neq 0$, and the tangent requires $\cos x \neq 0$. Both conditions must hold simultaneously, so we write the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x \neq k\pi \\[4pt]
x \neq \frac{\pi}{2} + k\pi
\end{cases}
$$
Together, the integer multiples of $\pi$ and the numbers $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ give all integer multiples of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The domain restrictions are therefore $x \neq \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$.
