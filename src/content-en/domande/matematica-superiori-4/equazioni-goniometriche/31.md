How many solutions does the equation $3\cos x = 2$ have in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$, and in which quadrants do they lie?

- Two, in the first and fourth quadrants
- Two, in the first and second quadrants
- One, in the first quadrant
- Two, in the second and third quadrants

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $\cos x = \dfrac{2}{3}$, and $\dfrac{2}{3}$ lies between $-1$ and $1$. Let $\beta = \arccos\dfrac{2}{3}$. The solutions are $x = \pm\beta + 2k\pi$. Since $\dfrac{2}{3} > 0$, the angle $\beta$ lies in $\left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$. In $[0, 2\pi)$, the solutions are therefore $\beta$ in the first quadrant and $2\pi - \beta$ in the fourth, the two quadrants where cosine is positive.
