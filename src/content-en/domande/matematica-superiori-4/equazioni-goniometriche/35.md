Which solutions of the equation $\tan x = 2$ lie in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$?

- Only the value $\arctan 2$
- $\arctan 2$ and $\pi + \arctan 2$
- $\arctan 2$ and $\pi - \arctan 2$
- $\arctan 2$ and $2\pi - \arctan 2$

## Explanation

The solutions are $x = \arctan 2 + k\pi$. The number $\arctan 2$ belongs to $\left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, so $k = 0$ gives a solution in the first quadrant. Setting $k = 1$ gives $\pi + \arctan 2$ in the third quadrant, whereas setting $k = 2$ gives a value greater than $2\pi$. There are therefore two solutions in $[0, 2\pi)$.
