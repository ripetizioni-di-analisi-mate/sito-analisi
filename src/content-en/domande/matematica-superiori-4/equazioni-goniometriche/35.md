Let $\alpha=\arctan 2$. Which value is the first root of $\tan x=2$ strictly greater than $\pi$?

- $\pi-\alpha$
- $\pi+\alpha$
- $2\pi-\alpha$
- $2\pi+\alpha$

## Explanation

The roots are $\alpha+k\pi$ with $0<\alpha<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The first integer that takes the angle past $\pi$ is $k=1$.
