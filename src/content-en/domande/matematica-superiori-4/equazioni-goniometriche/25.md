What are the solutions of the equation $\sin\dfrac{x}{2} = 1$?

- $x = \pi + 4k\pi$
- $x = \pi + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 4k\pi$

## Explanation

By the special-case formula for sine, $\dfrac{x}{2} = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$. Multiplying both terms by $2$ gives $x = \pi + 4k\pi$. The period of the solutions is therefore $4\pi$, that is, $\dfrac{2\pi}{|a|}$ with $a = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
