- $x = 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \pi + 2k\pi$
- $x = 2k\pi$ or $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

Dividing by $\sqrt{2}$ gives $\cos\left(x - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. Since $\arccos\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, the basic cosine equation gives $x - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$. Add $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ to both sides for each family. The plus sign gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, and the minus sign gives $x = 2k\pi$.
