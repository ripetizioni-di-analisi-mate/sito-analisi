Is there an angle $x$ in the third quadrant such that $\sin x=0.8$?

- No, because sine is negative in the third quadrant.
- Yes, it is $x=\pi+\arcsin 0.8$.
- Yes, it is $x=\pi-\arcsin 0.8$.
- Yes, provided the cosine is positive.

## Explanation

In the third quadrant, that is, for $\pi<x<\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, the points of the unit circle have negative ordinate, so $\sin x<0$. Since $0.8>0$, **no angle** in the third quadrant satisfies the condition. The angles with $\sin x=0.8$ are $\arcsin 0.8$, in the first quadrant, and $\pi-\arcsin 0.8$, in the second, up to multiples of $2\pi$.
