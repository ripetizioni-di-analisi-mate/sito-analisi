An app reports $\sin x=0{,}80$ and assigns $x$ to the third quadrant. What can be concluded immediately?

- The two readings are incompatible.
- The angle is $\arcsin (0{,}80)+\pi$.
- The angle is a right angle.
- Cosine must be positive.

## Explanation

In the third quadrant every point has a negative vertical coordinate. A positive sine indicates the first or second quadrant, so its sign alone exposes the inconsistency.
