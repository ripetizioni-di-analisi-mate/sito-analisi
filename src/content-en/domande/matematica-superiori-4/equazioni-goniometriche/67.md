A unit-circle point satisfies $3\cos x+4\sin x=5$. What are its coordinates $(\cos x,\sin x)$?

- $(\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5})$
- $(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$
- $(-\dfrac{3}{5},-\dfrac{4}{5})$
- $(\dfrac{3}{4},1)$

## Explanation

The dot product with $(3,4)$ is maximized in that vector's direction. The corresponding unit vector is $(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$.
