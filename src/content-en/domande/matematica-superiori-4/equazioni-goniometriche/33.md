A hand starts at twelve o'clock and makes a clockwise turn in $60$ seconds. How many seconds have passed when its rightward and upward projections first have equal positive values?

- $15$
- $22{,}5$
- $30$
- $7{,}5$

## Explanation

In the first quadrant the projections agree on the angle bisector. The hand travels one eighth of a turn, so the time is $\dfrac{60}{8}=7{,}5$ seconds.
