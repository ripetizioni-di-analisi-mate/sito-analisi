What are the solutions of the equation $\tan x + \sqrt{3} = 0$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $\tan x = -\sqrt{3}$. Since $\arctan\left(-\sqrt{3}\right) = -\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, the basic tangent equation gives the solutions $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$. A single family suffices because tangent has period $\pi$. The points $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, where tangent is undefined, do not belong to this family.
