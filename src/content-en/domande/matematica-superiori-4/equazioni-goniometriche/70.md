Without introducing tangent, which line in the plane $(u,v)=(\cos x,\sin x)$ identifies the solutions of $2\sin x-\cos x=0$?

- $v=\dfrac{u}{2}$
- $v=2u$
- $v=-2u$
- $v=2-u$

## Explanation

Substitution turns the equation into $2v-u=0$. The line passes through the origin and meets the circle at two diametrically opposite points.
