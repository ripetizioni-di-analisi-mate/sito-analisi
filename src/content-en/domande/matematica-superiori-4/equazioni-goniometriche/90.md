The solution set of $\sin (2x)=0$ is periodic. What is its least positive period, the gap between consecutive zeros?

- $\pi$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The zeros satisfy $2x=k\pi$, so $x=\dfrac{k\pi}{2}$. Increasing the integer by one reaches the next zero, a step of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
