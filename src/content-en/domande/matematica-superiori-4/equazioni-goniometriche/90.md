Which solutions of the equation $\tan (2x) = -1$ lie in the interval $[0, \pi)$?

- Only the value $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- Only the value $\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{8}$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{8}$

## Explanation

Since $\arctan(-1) = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, we have $2x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$, that is, $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{8} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$. Setting $k = 0$ gives $-\dfrac{\pi}{8}$, which does not belong to the interval. Setting $k = 1$ and $k = 2$ gives $\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$ and $\dfrac{7\pi}{8}$, whereas setting $k = 3$ gives $\dfrac{11\pi}{8}$, which is greater than $\pi$.
