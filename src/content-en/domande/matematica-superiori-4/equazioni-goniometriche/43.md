What are the solutions of the equation $\cos (4x) = \cos (2x)$?

- $x = k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{k\pi}{3}$
- $x = k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + \dfrac{k\pi}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

By the theorem for angles with equal cosines, $4x = 2x + 2k\pi$ or $4x = -2x + 2k\pi$. The first equation gives $2x = 2k\pi$, that is, $x = k\pi$. The second gives $6x = 2k\pi$, that is, $x = \dfrac{k\pi}{3}$. Every integer multiple of $\pi$ is also an integer multiple of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, so the first family is contained in the second, and the solutions are $x = \dfrac{k\pi}{3}$.
