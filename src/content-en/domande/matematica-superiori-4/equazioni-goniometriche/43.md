A family of solutions is $x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}+k\pi$. What is the sum of its smallest positive member and its largest negative member?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $-\pi$
- $0$

## Explanation

The two integers needed are $k=0$ and $k=-1$. The angles are $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, whose sum is $-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.
