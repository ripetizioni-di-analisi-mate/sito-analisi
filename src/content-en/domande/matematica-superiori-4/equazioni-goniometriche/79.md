How many distinct points in one turn are represented by the union of the families $x=k\pi$ and $x=\dfrac{k\pi}{2}$, for integer $k$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

The first family corresponds to the even indices of the second. This leaves the four points of the second family in $[0,2\pi)$.
