A wheel starts at its highest point. After a rotation of $x$ radians its height relative to the centre is $R \cos x$. At what positive angle does it first regain its initial height?

- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $2\pi$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The initial height is $R$. With $R>0$ the equation becomes $\cos x=1$, which requires a whole turn. The first return is at $2\pi$.
