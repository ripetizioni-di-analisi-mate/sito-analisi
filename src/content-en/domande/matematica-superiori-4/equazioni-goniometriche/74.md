For which values of $a$ do the lines $v=a$ and $u=a$ share a point on the circle $u^2+v^2=1$?

- $a=\pm 1$
- $a=0$
- $a=\pm \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $-1\le a\le 1$

## Explanation

Their only common point in the plane is $(a,a)$. It lies on the circle if $2a^2=1$, giving the two stated values.
