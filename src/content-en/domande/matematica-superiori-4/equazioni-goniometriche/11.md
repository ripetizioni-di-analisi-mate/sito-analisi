- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$ or $x = \arctan 3 + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \arctan\dfrac{1}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$ or $x = \arctan\dfrac{1}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$ or $x = -\arctan\dfrac{1}{3} + k\pi$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $\sin^2 x$ is $3 \neq 0$, so points with $\cos x = 0$ are not solutions and we can divide by $\cos^2 x$. This gives $3\tan^2 x - 4\tan x + 1 = 0$, which has $\Delta = 16 - 12 = 4$, so $\tan x = \dfrac{4 \pm 2}{6}$. The roots are $\tan x = 1$ and $\tan x = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The first gives $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$, and the second gives $x = \arctan\dfrac{1}{3} + k\pi$.
