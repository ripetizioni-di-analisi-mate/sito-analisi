The equation $\sin x=\sin y$ allows two branches. If $x$ and $y$ both lie in $[-\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{\pi}{2}]$, which conclusion always holds?

- $x=-y$
- $x+y=\pi$
- $x=y$
- $x-y=\pi$

## Explanation

Sine is strictly increasing on the stated interval. Distinct inputs would have distinct outputs, so equality forces $x=y$.
