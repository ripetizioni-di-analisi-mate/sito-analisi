For which values of the real parameter $m$ does the equation $\sin x = m$ have exactly two solutions in the interval $[0, \pi]$?

- $0 < m < 1$
- $-1 < m < 1$
- $0 \leq m < 1$
- $0 \leq m \leq 1$

## Explanation

In $[0, \pi]$, the graph of $y = \sin x$ starts at $0$, rises to $1$ at $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, and returns to $0$ at $\pi$. A line $y = m$ with $0 \leq m < 1$ therefore intersects the graph at two points symmetric about $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, and for $m = 0$ these points are $0$ and $\pi$. For $m = 1$, the only solution is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. For $m < 0$ or $m > 1$, there are no solutions because in $[0, \pi]$ the sine takes only values between $0$ and $1$.
