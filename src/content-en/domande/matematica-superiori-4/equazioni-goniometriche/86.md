Solutions of $\tan x=3$ are to be expressed in degrees. If $\alpha$ is the principal calculator answer in radians, which description uses consistent units?

- $x=(\alpha+180k)^\circ$
- $x=(180\alpha/\pi+360k)^\circ$
- $x=(180\alpha/\pi+180k)^\circ$
- $x=(\pi\alpha/180+180k)^\circ$

## Explanation

Convert the principal value from radians to degrees by multiplying by $180/\pi$. Tangent repeats every half-turn, or $180^\circ$, so the period must also be given in degrees.
