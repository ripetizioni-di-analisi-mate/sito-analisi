Let $\alpha=\arctan 3$, expressed in radians. How are all the solutions of the equation $\tan x=3$ written in degrees, with $k\in\mathbb{Z}$?

- $x=(\alpha+180k)^\circ$
- $x=\left(\dfrac{180\alpha}{\pi}+360k\right)^\circ$
- $x=\left(\dfrac{180\alpha}{\pi}+180k\right)^\circ$
- $x=\left(\dfrac{\pi\alpha}{180}+180k\right)^\circ$

## Explanation

Since $\pi$ radians correspond to $180^\circ$, an angle of $\alpha$ radians measures $\dfrac{180\alpha}{\pi}$ degrees. The tangent has period $\pi$, that is, $180^\circ$, so the solutions are obtained by adding integer multiples of $180^\circ$ to the principal value. Therefore the solutions are $x=\left(\dfrac{180\alpha}{\pi}+180k\right)^\circ$, with $k\in\mathbb{Z}$.
