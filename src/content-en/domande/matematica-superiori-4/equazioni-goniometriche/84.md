How many solutions does the equation $\cos (3x) = \dfrac{1}{2}$ have in the interval $[0, 2\pi]$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $2$
- $9$

## Explanation

We have $3x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$, so $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{9} + \dfrac{2k\pi}{3}$. In $[0, 2\pi]$, the family with the plus sign gives $\dfrac{\pi}{9}$, $\dfrac{7\pi}{9}$, $\dfrac{13\pi}{9}$ for $k = 0, 1, 2$. The family with the minus sign gives $\dfrac{5\pi}{9}$, $\dfrac{11\pi}{9}$, $\dfrac{17\pi}{9}$ for $k = 1, 2, 3$. There are therefore six solutions, in agreement with the fact that $\cos(3x)$ has period $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ and the interval contains three periods.
