We want the first positive zero of $\cos (3x)$ after $x=0$. On which interval of its argument is it enough to track cosine to confirm that the zero is the first?

- $0<3x\le \dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\pi \le 3x\le 2\pi$
- $-\pi \le 3x<0$
- $2\pi<3x\le 3\pi$

## Explanation

The phase $3x$ increases from zero. Cosine stays positive until $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and vanishes at the endpoint, so the first zero is $x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
