How many families of solutions does the equation $3\sin x + 4\cos x = 5$ have?

- Two families
- None, because the equation has no solutions
- One family
- Four families

## Explanation

We have $r = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$, so the equation becomes $5\sin(x + \varphi) = 5$, that is, $\sin(x + \varphi) = 1$. By the special-case formula for sine, the equation $\sin t = 1$ has only one family of solutions, $t = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$. In the graphical method, the line $4X + 3Y = 5$ is at a distance $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{16 + 9}} = 1$ from the origin, so it is tangent to the unit circle.
