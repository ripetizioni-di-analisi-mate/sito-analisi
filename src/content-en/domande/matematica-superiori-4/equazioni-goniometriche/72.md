If $x$ solves $a \sin x+b \cos x=c$ and $x+\pi$ is also a solution, what condition on $c$ is necessary?

- $c=1$
- $c=a+b$
- $c=0$
- $c^2=a^2+b^2$

## Explanation

Adding $\pi$ changes the signs of both functions, so the right side must equal both $c$ and $-c$. This gives $c=0$.
