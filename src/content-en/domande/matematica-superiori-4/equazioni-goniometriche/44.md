What are the solutions of the equation $\tan (2x) = \tan\left(x + \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$?

- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{9} + \dfrac{k\pi}{3}$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

By the theorem for angles with equal tangents, $2x = x + \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$, that is, $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$. Both tangents must be defined, so the domain restrictions are
$$
\begin{cases}
2x \neq \frac{\pi}{2} + k\pi \\[4pt]
x + \frac{\pi}{3} \neq \frac{\pi}{2} + k\pi
\end{cases}
$$
For $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$, both tangents equal $\tan\dfrac{2\pi}{3} = -\sqrt{3}$, so the conditions are satisfied and all the solutions found are admissible.
