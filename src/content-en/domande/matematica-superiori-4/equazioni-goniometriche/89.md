How many pairs $(x, y)$, with $x$ and $y$ in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$, solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2\sin x = 1 \\[4pt]
\tan y = 1
\end{cases}
$$

- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $8$

## Explanation

The first equation gives $\sin x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, whose solutions in $[0, 2\pi)$ are $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$. The second gives $y = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$, which in $[0, 2\pi)$ gives $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$. The two equations involve different unknowns, so each value of $x$ can be paired with every value of $y$. There are therefore $2 \cdot 2 = 4$ pairs.
