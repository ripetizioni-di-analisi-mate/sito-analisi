- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{4\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$
- $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{7\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$

## Explanation

Isolating the sine gives $\sin x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, a value between $-1$ and $1$. We have $\alpha = \arcsin\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, so the first family is $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$. The second family for the sine equation is $x = \pi - \alpha + 2k\pi$, that is, $x = \pi + \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi = \dfrac{7\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$. The two angles lie in the fourth and third quadrants, where sine is negative.
