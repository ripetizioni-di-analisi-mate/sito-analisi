What are the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
\cos x + \cos y = 1 \\[4pt]
\cos x - \cos y = 0
\end{cases}
$$
with $x$ and $y$ in the interval $[0, \pi]$?

- Only the pair $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$
- Only the pair $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)$
- Only the pair $\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)$
- The pairs $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)$ and $\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}, \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$

## Explanation

Adding the two equations gives $2\cos x = 1$, and subtracting them gives $2\cos y = 1$. Hence $\cos x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\cos y = \dfrac{1}{2}$. In $[0, \pi]$, cosine takes each value between $-1$ and $1$ exactly once, so $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $y = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$. The only solution is the pair $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}, \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$.
