How many solutions does the equation $\sin x = 0.3$ have in the interval $[0, 4\pi)$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $3$

## Explanation

The number $0.3$ lies between $-1$ and $1$ and is different from $\pm 1$, so the equation has two distinct families of solutions, $x = \alpha + 2k\pi$ and $x = \pi - \alpha + 2k\pi$, with $\alpha = \arcsin 0.3$. Each family has only one point in every interval of length $2\pi$, so there are two solutions in $[0, 2\pi)$. The interval $[0, 4\pi)$ contains two periods, so there are four solutions.
