What are the solutions of the equation $4\cos^2 x - 3 = 0$?

- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $\cos^2 x = \dfrac{3}{4}$, so $\cos x = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ or $\cos x = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. The first equation gives $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$, and the second gives $x = \pm\dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$. Since $\dfrac{5\pi}{6} = -\dfrac{\pi}{6} + \pi$ and $-\dfrac{5\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\pi}{6} - \pi$, the four families combine into $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$.
