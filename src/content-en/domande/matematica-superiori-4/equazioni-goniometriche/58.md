A table gives rounded values $\sin x=0.60$ and $\cos x=0.60$ to two decimal places for an acute angle $x$. Could both come from the same angle?

- Yes, equal values are always compatible.
- Yes, their sum exceeds one.
- No, the cosine of an acute angle is negative.
- No, even the largest possible sum of squares is below one.

## Explanation

Both true values are at most $0.605$. This would give $\sin^2x+\cos^2x\leq2(0.605)^2<1$, contradicting the Pythagorean identity.
