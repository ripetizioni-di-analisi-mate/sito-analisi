What are the solutions of the equation $\sin (2x) = 2\sin x$?

- $x = 2k\pi$
- $x = \pi + 2k\pi$
- $x = \dfrac{k\pi}{2}$
- $x = k\pi$

## Explanation

By the double-angle identity, the equation becomes $2\sin x\cos x - 2\sin x = 0$, that is, $2\sin x\,(\cos x - 1) = 0$. The factor $\sin x$ vanishes for $x = k\pi$, and the factor $\cos x - 1$ for $x = 2k\pi$. The second family is contained in the first, so the solutions are $x = k\pi$.
