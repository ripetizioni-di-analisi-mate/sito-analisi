The letters $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$ are used to form one- or two-letter codes, with repetition allowed. How many codes are there?

- $30$
- $25$
- $10$
- $50$

## Explanation

The one-letter and two-letter codes form disjoint groups, so count them separately. There are $5$ one-letter codes. Each position in a two-letter code can be filled in $5$ ways, so there are $5^2 = 25$ such codes. By the addition principle the total is $5 + 25 = 30$.
