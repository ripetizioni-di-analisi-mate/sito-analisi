Which of the following is an ordered selection with repetition from $4$ objects taken $6$ at a time and is therefore counted by $4^6$?

- The responses to a test with $6$ questions and $4$ options per question
- The responses to a test with $4$ questions and $6$ options per question
- Groups of $4$ questions chosen from $6$
- The orders in which $6$ questions can be presented

## Explanation

In an ordered selection with repetition from $4$ objects taken $6$ at a time, there are $6$ positions. Each is filled by one of the $4$ objects, including objects already used. On a test with $6$ questions and $4$ options, the positions represent the questions and the objects are the options, so there are $4^6$ possible sets of responses. A test with $4$ questions and $6$ options instead gives $6^4$ possible sets of responses. Groups of questions are counted by $\dbinom{6}{4}$ and the orders of the questions by $6!$.
