Six identical tokens are divided among three **unlabelled** nonempty boxes. Configurations differing only by a permutation of the boxes are identified. How many configurations are there?

- $3$
- $10$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

We can list the three quantities in nondecreasing order. The only triples are $(1,1,4)$, $(1,2,3)$ and $(2,2,2)$. Swapping two entries does not create a new configuration.
