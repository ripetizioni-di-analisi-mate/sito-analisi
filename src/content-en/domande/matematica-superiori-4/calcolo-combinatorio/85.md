How many terms are in the expansion of $(a + b)^{12}$, where $a$ and $b$ are variables?

- $13$
- $12$
- $24$
- $4096$

## Explanation

By the binomial theorem the expansion is $\sum_{k = 0}^{12} \dbinom{12}{k} a^{12 - k} b^k$, with one term for each value of $k$ from $0$ to $12$. The terms have distinct exponents of $b$, so they do not combine. The expansion therefore has $12 + 1 = 13$ terms.
