For which natural number $n \geq 5$ does $\dbinom{n}{3} = \dbinom{n}{5}$ hold?

- $n = 15$
- $n = 6$
- $n = 10$
- $n = 8$

## Explanation

In row $n$ of Pascal’s triangle, entries increase up to the center and then decrease symmetrically, so two coefficients with different lower indices are equal only if those indices are complementary. Therefore $\dbinom{n}{3} = \dbinom{n}{5}$ when $n - 5 = 3$, that is, for $n = 8$. Indeed, $\dbinom{8}{3} = \dbinom{8}{5} = 56$.
