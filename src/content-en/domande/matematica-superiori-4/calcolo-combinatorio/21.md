In a tree diagram, $4$ branches leave the root. At the end of each, $3$ more branches begin, and from the end of each of these, $2$ branches lead to leaves. How many leaves are there?

- $24$
- $9$
- $12$
- $40$

## Explanation

Each leaf corresponds to a path from the root, that is, a complete choice in three stages. After the first stage there are $4$ nodes, after the second $4 \cdot 3 = 12$ nodes, and after the third $12 \cdot 2 = 24$ nodes. The number of leaves is therefore $24$. The number $4 + 12 + 24 = 40$ counts all nodes other than the root, including the intermediate ones.
