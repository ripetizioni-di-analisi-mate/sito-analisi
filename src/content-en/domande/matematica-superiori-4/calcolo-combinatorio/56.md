At a party there are $12$ people, and each shakes hands with every other person exactly once. How many handshakes take place?

- $132$
- $66$
- $144$
- $24$

## Explanation

A handshake is determined by the pair of people who exchange it, and their order does not matter. The number of handshakes is therefore
$$
\binom{12}{2} = \frac{12 \cdot 11}{2} = 66 .
$$
The number $12 \cdot 11 = 132$ counts each handshake twice, once for each person involved.
