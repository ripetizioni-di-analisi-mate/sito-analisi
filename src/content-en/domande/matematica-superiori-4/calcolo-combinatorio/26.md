Which of the following is counted by the number of ordered selections without repetition $D_{10,4}$?

- Groups of $4$ students chosen from $10$
- The finishing orders of the top $4$ among $10$ runners
- Codes consisting of $4$ digits from $0$ to $9$
- Anagrams of a word with $10$ distinct letters

## Explanation

For $10$ objects and a selection size of $4$, an ordered selection without repetition is a sequence of $4$ distinct objects chosen from $10$. In the ranking of the first $4$ runners, order matters and each runner appears only once, so there are $D_{10,4} = 5040$ rankings. In groups of students order does not matter, so use combinations, $\dbinom{10}{4}$. Digit codes allow repetition and number $10^4$, whereas anagrams arrange all $10$ letters and number $10!$.
