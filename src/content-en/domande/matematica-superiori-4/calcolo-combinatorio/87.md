What is the value of the sum $\sum_{k = 0}^{6} \dbinom{6}{k} \, 2^k$?

- $64$
- $128$
- $729$
- $4096$

## Explanation

By the binomial theorem with $a = 1$ and $b = 2$ we have $(1 + 2)^6 = \sum_{k = 0}^{6} \dbinom{6}{k} \, 1^{6 - k} \, 2^k$. Every power of $1$ equals $1$, so the given sum equals $(1 + 2)^6$. Therefore the sum is $3^6 = 729$.
