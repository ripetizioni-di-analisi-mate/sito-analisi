How many entries in row $10$ of Pascal’s triangle are greater than $100$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The first entries in row $10$ are $\dbinom{10}{0} = 1$, $\dbinom{10}{1} = 10$, $\dbinom{10}{2} = 45$, $\dbinom{10}{3} = 120$, $\dbinom{10}{4} = 210$, and $\dbinom{10}{5} = 252$. By the symmetry of binomial coefficients the row is symmetric, so it is $1, 10, 45, 120, 210, 252, 210, 120, 45, 10, 1$. The entries greater than $100$ are $120$, $210$, $252$, $210$, $120$, giving $5$ entries.
