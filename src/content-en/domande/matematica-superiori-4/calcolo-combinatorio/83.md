What is the term independent of $x$ in the expansion of $\left( x - \dfrac{1}{x} \right)^6$, with $x \neq 0$?

- $-20$
- $20$
- $-15$
- $15$

## Explanation

With $a = x$ and $b = -x^{-1}$, the general term of the expansion is
$$
T_{k + 1} = \binom{6}{k} x^{6 - k} \left( -x^{-1} \right)^k = \binom{6}{k} (-1)^k x^{6 - 2k} .
$$
The term is independent of $x$ when $6 - 2k = 0$, that is, for $k = 3$. The required term is therefore $\dbinom{6}{3} (-1)^3 = -20$.
