How many trailing zeros does the decimal representation of $25!$ have?

- $5$
- $6$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

Each trailing zero requires a factor $10=2\cdot5$. Factors of two are more plentiful. Each multiple of five supplies one factor of five, and $25$ supplies one extra, giving $5+1=6$.
