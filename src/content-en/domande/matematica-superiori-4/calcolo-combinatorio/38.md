What is the value of $\dfrac{10!}{8!}$?

- $45$
- $90$
- $80$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

Applying the relation $n! = n \cdot (n - 1)!$ twice gives $10! = 10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8!$. The factor $8!$ cancels, so
$$
\frac{10!}{8!} = \frac{10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8!}{8!} = 10 \cdot 9 = 90 .
$$
The number $45$ is $\dfrac{10!}{8! \cdot 2!} = \dbinom{10}{2}$, which also has the factor $2!$ in its denominator.
