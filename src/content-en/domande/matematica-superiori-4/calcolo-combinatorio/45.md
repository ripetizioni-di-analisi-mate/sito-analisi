Which equality is true for every natural number $n \geq 1$?

- $n! = n \cdot (n - 1)!$
- $(2n)! = 2 \cdot n!$
- $(n + 1)! = n! + 1$
- $\dfrac{n!}{(n - 1)!} = n - 1$

## Explanation

By definition $n! = n \cdot (n - 1) \cdot \ldots \cdot 1$, and the product of the factors from $n - 1$ to $1$ is $(n - 1)!$. Therefore $n! = n \cdot (n - 1)!$ for every $n \geq 1$, using the convention for $n = 1$ that $0! = 1$. The other equalities already fail for $n = 2$, since $4! = 24$ and $2 \cdot 2! = 4$, then $3! = 6$ and $2! + 1 = 3$, and finally $\dfrac{2!}{1!} = 2$ and $n - 1 = 1$.
