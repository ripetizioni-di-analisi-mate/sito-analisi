A teacher counts the ways to choose a leader and a three-person committee containing that leader in two orders. With $n\ge3$ people, which identity follows?

- $3\binom n3=n\binom{n-1}2$
- $\binom n3=n\binom{n-1}2$
- $3\binom n3=n\binom n2$
- $n\binom n3=3\binom{n-1}2$

## Explanation

Choosing the committee first gives $\binom n3$ triples and three possible leaders per triple. Choosing the leader first gives $n$ choices and then $\binom{n-1}2$ pairs to complete the group.
