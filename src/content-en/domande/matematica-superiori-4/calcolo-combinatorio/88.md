In the expansion of $(2x + y)^5$ in descending powers of $x$, what is the third term?

- $80x^3 y^2$
- $20x^3 y^2$
- $40x^2 y^3$
- $80x^2 y^3$

## Explanation

The term in position $k + 1$ is $T_{k + 1} = \dbinom{5}{k} (2x)^{5 - k} y^k$, so the third term occurs at $k = 2$. This gives
$$
T_3 = \binom{5}{2} (2x)^3 y^2 = 10 \cdot 8x^3 \, y^2 = 80x^3 y^2 .
$$
The exponent $3$ also applies to the coefficient $2$, so the factor is $2^3 = 8$. Writing $2x^3$ in place of $(2x)^3$ would give $20x^3 y^2$.
