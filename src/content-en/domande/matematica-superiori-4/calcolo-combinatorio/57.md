How many diagonals does a convex polygon with $12$ sides have?

- $66$
- $108$
- $54$
- $60$

## Explanation

The number of segments joining pairs of the $12$ vertices is $\dbinom{12}{2} = 66$. Of these segments, $12$ are sides, so there are $66 - 12 = 54$ diagonals. The formula $\dfrac{n \, (n - 3)}{2} = \dfrac{12 \cdot 9}{2} = 54$ gives the same count.
