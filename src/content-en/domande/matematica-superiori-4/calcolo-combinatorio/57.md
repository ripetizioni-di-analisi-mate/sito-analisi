Six distinct points are placed in the plane so that four are on one line and no other triple is collinear. How many nondegenerate triangles do they determine?

- $20$
- $12$
- $16$
- $18$

## Explanation

There are $\binom63=20$ triples. The $\binom43=4$ triples on the line have zero area and must be discarded. The other $16$ are triangles.
