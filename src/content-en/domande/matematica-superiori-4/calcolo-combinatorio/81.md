What is the coefficient of $x^2$ in the expansion of $(x + 3)^5$?

- $90$
- $10$
- $270$
- $27$

## Explanation

The general term of the expansion is $\dbinom{5}{k} x^{5 - k} \, 3^k$. The exponent of $x$ equals $2$ when $5 - k = 2$, that is, for $k = 3$. The coefficient is therefore $\dbinom{5}{3} \cdot 3^3 = 10 \cdot 27 = 270$. The value $90 = \dbinom{5}{2} \cdot 3^2$ is the coefficient of $x^3$.
