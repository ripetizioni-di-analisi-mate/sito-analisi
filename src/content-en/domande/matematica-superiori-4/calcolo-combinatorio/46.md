How many anagrams of the word $CANTO$, including arrangements that are not words, have $C$ and $O$ at the two ends in either order?

- $6$
- $24$
- $120$
- $12$

## Explanation

The letters $C$ and $O$ fill the first and last positions in $2! = 2$ ways, with either $C$ first or $O$ first. The $3$ letters $A$, $N$, $T$ can fill the middle positions in $3! = 6$ ways. By the fundamental counting principle the number of anagrams is $2 \cdot 6 = 12$.
