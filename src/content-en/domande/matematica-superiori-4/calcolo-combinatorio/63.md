Four friends each give and receive one gift. Nobody may keep their own gift. If Ada and Bruno exchange with one another, how many complete assignments are possible?

- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

Ada and Bruno have already received gifts. The remaining two recipients are the other two friends, who must exchange gifts with each other to avoid giving to themselves.
