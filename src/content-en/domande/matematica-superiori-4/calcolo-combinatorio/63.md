In a plane, $8$ lines are drawn. No two are parallel and no three pass through the same point. How many intersection points are there?

- $56$
- $64$
- $16$
- $28$

## Explanation

Two nonparallel lines meet at a point, so every pair of lines gives an intersection point. Since no three lines pass through one point, different pairs give different points. There are therefore as many points as pairs of lines, namely $\dbinom{8}{2} = \dfrac{8 \cdot 7}{2} = 28$.
