How many anagrams of the word $NUMERO$, including arrangements that are not words, begin with a vowel?

- $720$
- $360$
- $120$
- $240$

## Explanation

The word $NUMERO$ has $6$ distinct letters, including the $3$ vowels $U$, $E$, $O$. The first letter can be chosen from the vowels in $3$ ways. The $5$ remaining letters can be arranged in the other positions in $5! = 120$ ways, regardless of the vowel chosen. By the fundamental counting principle the number of anagrams is $3 \cdot 120 = 360$.
