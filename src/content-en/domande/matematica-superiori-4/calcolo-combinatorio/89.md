In how many ways can a row of four cells be tiled with pieces of length $1$ or $2$, without overlaps and with identical pieces of each length?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

There is one tiling using only single tiles. With one double tile there are three positions for it. With two double tiles there is one tiling. The total is $1+3+1=5$.
