How many anagrams of the word $SCUOLA$, including arrangements that are not words, have the three vowels $U$, $O$, $A$ all together?

- $24$
- $144$
- $36$
- $720$

## Explanation

The three vowels form a block, so the objects to arrange are the block and the consonants $S$, $C$, $L$, giving $4$ objects. They can be arranged in $4! = 24$ ways. Within the block the vowels can be ordered in $3! = 6$ ways regardless of the block’s position. By the fundamental counting principle the number of anagrams is $24 \cdot 6 = 144$.
