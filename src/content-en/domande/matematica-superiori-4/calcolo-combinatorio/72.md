The labelled vertices of a square are coloured using three colours, with adjacent vertices different. Why does assigning the same colour to opposite vertices $1$ and $3$ affect the count for the other two?

- Each remaining vertex independently has two colours to choose from.
- The other two must have the same colour.
- All three colours remain available to each.
- No colouring of the square is then possible.

## Explanation

The remaining vertices are not adjacent to one another, and both neighbours of each use the same colour. The fixed colour is the only forbidden one.
