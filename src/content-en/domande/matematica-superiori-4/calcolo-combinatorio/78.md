Row $6$ of Pascal’s triangle is $1, 6, 15, 20, 15, 6, 1$. What is the fourth entry in row $7$?

- $35$
- $20$
- $21$
- $56$

## Explanation

By Pascal’s identity, each interior entry in row $7$ is the sum of the two entries in row $6$ directly above it. The fourth entry in row $7$ is $\dbinom{7}{3}$, and the entries above it are $\dbinom{6}{2} = 15$ and $\dbinom{6}{3} = 20$. The fourth entry is therefore $15 + 20 = 35$. The number $21 = 6 + 15$ is the third entry in row $7$.
