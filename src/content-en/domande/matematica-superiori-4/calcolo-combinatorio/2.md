## Explanation

Between $1$ and $100$, the number of multiples of $3$ is $33$, since $3 \cdot 33 = 99$, and the number of multiples of $5$ is $20$. Numbers divisible by both are multiples of $15$, of which there are $6$, since $15 \cdot 6 = 90$ and $15 \cdot 7 = 105$. In the sum $33 + 20$, these $6$ numbers are counted twice, so they are subtracted once. The required count is therefore $33 + 20 - 6 = 47$.
