In how many ways can $4$ books, chosen from $6$ distinct books, be placed in $4$ positions on a shelf?

- $360$
- $15$
- $1296$
- $24$

## Explanation

Both the books chosen and their positions matter, so each arrangement is an ordered selection without repetition from $6$ objects taken $4$ at a time. The factorial form of the formula for ordered selections gives
$$
D_{6,4} = \frac{6!}{(6 - 4)!} = \frac{720}{2} = 360 .
$$
The number $\dbinom{6}{4} = 15$ counts just the choices of books, and each choice can be arranged in $4! = 24$ ways. Indeed, $15 \cdot 24 = 360$.
