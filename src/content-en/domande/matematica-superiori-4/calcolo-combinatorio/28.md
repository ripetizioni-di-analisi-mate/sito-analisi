How many length-three strings over $\{A,B,C\}$ use exactly two different letters?

- $18$
- $24$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

Choose the pair of letters in three ways. For a fixed pair there are $2^3$ strings, of which the two constant strings are excluded. The total is $3(8-2)=18$.
