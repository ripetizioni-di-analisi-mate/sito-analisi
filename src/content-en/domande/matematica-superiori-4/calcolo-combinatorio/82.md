What is the coefficient of $x^3$ in the expansion of $(1 - x)^7$?

- $-35$
- $35$
- $-21$
- $21$

## Explanation

With $a = 1$ and $b = -x$, the general term of the expansion is $\dbinom{7}{k} (-x)^k = \dbinom{7}{k} (-1)^k x^k$. The term containing $x^3$ occurs at $k = 3$, so its coefficient is $\dbinom{7}{3} (-1)^3 = -35$. The sign is negative because the exponent $3$ is odd.
