## Explanation

The pastries are chosen from $5$ types, a type may repeat, and order on the tray does not matter. Each choice is therefore a **combination with repetition** from $5$ objects taken $3$ at a time, and the number of such choices is
$$
C'_{5,3} = \binom{5 + 3 - 1}{3} = \binom{7}{3} = \frac{7 \cdot 6 \cdot 5}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 35 .
$$
The number $\dbinom{5}{3} = 10$ counts only trays with three different types.
