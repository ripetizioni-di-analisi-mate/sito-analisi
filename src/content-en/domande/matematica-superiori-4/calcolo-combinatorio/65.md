Two identical dice are rolled and cannot be distinguished. How many outcomes are possible if only the numbers showing matter?

- $36$
- $21$
- $15$
- $12$

## Explanation

An outcome is a pair of numbers from $1$ to $6$. A number may appear twice, and order does not matter. Each outcome is therefore a combination with repetition from $6$ objects taken $2$ at a time. The number of outcomes is
$$
C'_{6,2} = \binom{6 + 2 - 1}{2} = \binom{7}{2} = 21 .
$$
The same count is the sum of the $\dbinom{6}{2} = 15$ pairs of distinct numbers and the $6$ outcomes with matching numbers.
