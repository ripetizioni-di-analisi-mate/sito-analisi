Subsets of $\{a,b,c,d\}$ are recorded with four bits, one per element in that order. Which operation on the bits represents taking a complement?

- Read the digits in reverse.
- Clear only the first bit.
- Swap $0$ and $1$ in every position.
- Add one to the binary number.

## Explanation

A one indicates that the element belongs to the subset. Taking the complement reverses membership for every element, so all four bits are toggled.
