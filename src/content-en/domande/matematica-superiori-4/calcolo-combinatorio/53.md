How many $6$-digit numbers can be formed by using each of the digits $1$, $2$, $3$ exactly twice?

- $720$
- $180$
- $90$
- $729$

## Explanation

Every number is a sequence of $6$ digits with two occurrences of $1$, two of $2$, and two of $3$, so it is a permutation with repeated elements. The number of such permutations is
$$
\frac{6!}{2! \cdot 2! \cdot 2!} = \frac{720}{8} = 90 .
$$
None of the digits is $0$, so every sequence is a $6$-digit number.
