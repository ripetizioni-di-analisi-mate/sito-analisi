A keypad uses the digits $0$ and $1$. How many four-digit codes have at most one change between consecutive digits?

- $8$
- $6$
- $4$
- $10$

## Explanation

There are two constant codes. For exactly one change, choose the first symbol in two ways and the change position in any of the three gaps. The total is $2+2\cdot3=8$.
