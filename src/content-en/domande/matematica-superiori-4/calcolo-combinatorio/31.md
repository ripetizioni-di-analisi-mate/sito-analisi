An Italian soccer pool ticket lists $13$ matches. For each match, choose one of the three symbols $1$, $X$, or $2$. How many different tickets are possible?

- $3^{13}$
- $13^3$
- $3 \cdot 13$
- $2^{13}$

## Explanation

A ticket is an ordered sequence of $13$ symbols, and a symbol may appear more than once. It is therefore an ordered selection with repetition from $3$ objects taken $13$ at a time. By the counting formula for such selections, the number of tickets is $D'_{3,13} = 3^{13}$. In this formula the base is the number of possible symbols per match and the exponent is the number of matches.
