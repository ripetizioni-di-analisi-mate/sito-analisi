## Explanation

The word $BANANA$ has $6$ letters, with $3$ occurrences of $A$, $2$ occurrences of $N$, and one $B$. Interchanging two identical letters does not produce a new anagram, so we use the formula for **permutations with repeated elements**:
$$
\frac{6!}{3! \cdot 2! \cdot 1!} = \frac{720}{12} = 60 .
$$
The number $6! = 720$ counts the anagrams of a word with six distinct letters.
