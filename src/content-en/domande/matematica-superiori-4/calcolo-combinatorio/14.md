A class has $5$ boys and $6$ girls. How many committees of $4$ students contain at least one boy?

- $325$
- $330$
- $315$
- $600$

## Explanation

The number of committees of $4$ students chosen from $11$ is $\dbinom{11}{4} = 330$. A committee contains no boys if it consists of $4$ of the $6$ girls; there are $\dbinom{6}{4} = 15$ such committees. The number with at least one boy is therefore $330 - 15 = 315$. The product $5 \cdot \dbinom{10}{3} = 600$ counts the same committee more than once, since a committee with several boys can be formed starting with any of them.
