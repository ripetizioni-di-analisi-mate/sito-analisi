From $5$ men and $4$ women, a committee of $3$ people with at least $2$ women is chosen. How many such committees are there?

- $34$
- $30$
- $42$
- $84$

## Explanation

Split the count into two disjoint cases according to the number of women. Committees with $2$ women and $1$ man number $\dbinom{4}{2} \cdot \dbinom{5}{1} = 6 \cdot 5 = 30$. The number of committees with $3$ women is $\dbinom{4}{3} = 4$. By the addition principle the total is $30 + 4 = 34$. The product $\dbinom{4}{2} \cdot 7 = 42$ counts committees consisting of three women more than once.
