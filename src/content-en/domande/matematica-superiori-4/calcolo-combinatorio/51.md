On a grid, travel from $(0, 0)$ to $(3, 3)$ in steps of length $1$ to the right or up. How many paths pass through $(1, 1)$?

- $20$
- $8$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

Each path consists of two stretches. From $(0, 0)$ to $(1, 1)$ it takes $1$ step to the right and $1$ up, so there are $\dfrac{2!}{1! \cdot 1!} = 2$ paths. From $(1, 1)$ to $(3, 3)$ it takes $2$ steps to the right and $2$ up, so there are $\dfrac{4!}{2! \cdot 2!} = 6$ paths. By the fundamental counting principle the number of paths through $(1, 1)$ is $2 \cdot 6 = 12$.
