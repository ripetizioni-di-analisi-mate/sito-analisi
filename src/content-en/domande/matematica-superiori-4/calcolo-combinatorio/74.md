How many subsets with at most $2$ elements does a set of $7$ elements have?

- $28$
- $21$
- $29$
- $49$

## Explanation

Subsets with at most $2$ elements have $0$, $1$, or $2$ elements, and these three groups are disjoint. By the addition principle their number is
$$
\binom{7}{0} + \binom{7}{1} + \binom{7}{2} = 1 + 7 + 21 = 29 .
$$
The value $28$ results from omitting the empty set, counted by $\dbinom{7}{0} = 1$.
