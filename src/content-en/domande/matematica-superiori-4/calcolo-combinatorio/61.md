In a tournament every team plays every other team exactly once, for a total of $15$ games. How many teams are there?

- $5$
- $6$
- $7$
- $15$

## Explanation

A game is determined by the pair of teams that play it, regardless of order, so with $n$ teams the number of games is $\dbinom{n}{2} = \dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2}$. The equation $\dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2} = 15$ is equivalent to $n^2 - n - 30 = 0$, whose solutions are $n = 6$ and $n = -5$. The number of teams is a natural number, so there are $6$ teams.
