For $n \geq 0$, which expression equals $\dfrac{(n + 2)!}{n!}$?

- $n + 2$
- $(n + 2)(n + 1)$
- $2$
- $(n + 2) \, n$

## Explanation

Applying the relation $m! = m \cdot (m - 1)!$ twice gives $(n + 2)! = (n + 2)(n + 1) \cdot n!$. The factor $n!$ differs from $0$ and cancels. Therefore $\dfrac{(n + 2)!}{n!} = (n + 2)(n + 1)$. For $n = 1$ this gives $\dfrac{3!}{1!} = 6 = 3 \cdot 2$.
