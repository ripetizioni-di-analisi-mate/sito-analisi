How many permutations of $A,B,C,D,E$ place $A$ before $B$ and $B$ before $C$, even if they are not consecutive?

- $60$
- $20$
- $24$
- $10$

## Explanation

The three designated symbols have $3!$ relative orders, equally frequent by symmetry. Only one qualifies, so the answer is $5!/3!=20$.
