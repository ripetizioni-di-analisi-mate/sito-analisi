In how many ways can $6$ people, including Anna and Bruno, line up with Anna and Bruno next to each other?

- $240$
- $720$
- $120$
- $480$

## Explanation

Treat Anna and Bruno as a single block. The objects to arrange are the block and the other $4$ people, giving $5$ objects. They can be arranged in $5! = 120$ ways. Within the block, Anna and Bruno can be arranged in $2! = 2$ ways regardless of the block’s position. By the fundamental counting principle the number of lineups is $120 \cdot 2 = 240$.
