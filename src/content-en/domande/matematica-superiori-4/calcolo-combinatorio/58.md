How many four-card hands from a deck with four suits of ten cards each contain exactly one card from each suit?

- $4^{10}$
- $\binom{10}{4}$
- $10^4$
- $4!\,10^4$

## Explanation

Choose one of ten cards for each of the four suits. The choices are not then permuted because a hand does not retain draw order.
