In the Italian lottery, numbers are drawn from $1$ to $90$. How many triples are there, that is, groups of $3$ distinct numbers without regard to order?

- $704\,880$
- $729\,000$
- $117\,480$
- $4005$

## Explanation

A triple is a subset of $3$ elements of the set of numbers from $1$ to $90$, so the number of triples is
$$
\binom{90}{3} = \frac{90 \cdot 89 \cdot 88}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = \frac{704\,880}{6} = 117\,480 .
$$
The numerator $704\,880 = D_{90,3}$ counts the ordered sequences, and each triple corresponds to $3! = 6$ sequences. The number $90^3 = 729\,000$ counts sequences in which numbers may repeat.
