How many integers from $1$ through $100$ are divisible by exactly one of $2$ and $5$?

- $60$
- $70$
- $50$
- $40$

## Explanation

There are $50$ multiples of two and $20$ multiples of five. The $10$ multiples of ten were counted twice and must be removed both times, giving $50+20-2\cdot10=50$.
