How many natural numbers from $1$ to $60$ inclusive are divisible by $4$ or $6$?

- $25$
- $23$
- $20$
- $15$

## Explanation

Between $1$ and $60$, the number of multiples of $4$ is $15$ and the number of multiples of $6$ is $10$. A number divisible by both $4$ and $6$ is a multiple of their least common multiple $12$. The number of multiples of $12$ up to $60$ is $5$. The sum $15 + 10$ counts these numbers twice, so subtract them once. The required count is therefore $15 + 10 - 5 = 20$.
