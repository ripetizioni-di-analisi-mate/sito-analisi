On a grid from $(0,0)$ to $(2,2)$, take steps right or up and stay in the region $y\leq x$ throughout. How many paths are possible?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The first step must go right. Using $R$ for right and $U$ for up, the only sequences that never rise above the diagonal are $RRUU$ and $RURU$.
