A coin is tossed $6$ times. How many sequences of outcomes contain exactly $2$ heads?

- $30$
- $15$
- $64$
- $12$

## Explanation

A sequence with exactly $2$ heads contains $2$ occurrences of $T$ and $4$ occurrences of $C$. The sequences are therefore the permutations with repeated elements of these $6$ letters, and their number is
$$
\frac{6!}{2! \cdot 4!} = \frac{6 \cdot 5}{2} = 15 .
$$
The number $30 = D_{6,2}$ distinguishes the two heads from each other, although they are identical.
