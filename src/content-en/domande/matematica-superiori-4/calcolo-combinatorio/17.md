In a class of $28$ students, $15$ take an English course, $12$ play soccer, and $5$ do both. How many students do neither?

- $1$
- $11$
- $6$
- $22$

## Explanation

The sum $15 + 12$ counts students who do both activities twice. Therefore $15 + 12 - 5 = 22$ students do at least one activity. The remaining $28 - 22 = 6$ students do neither.
