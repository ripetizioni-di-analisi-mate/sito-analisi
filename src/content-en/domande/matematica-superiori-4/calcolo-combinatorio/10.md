- $n = 6$
- $n = 5$
- $n = 7$

## Explanation

By definition, $\dbinom{n}{2} = \dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2}$, so the equation becomes $\dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2} = 3n$. Since $n \geq 2$, both sides can be divided by $n$ to give $\dfrac{n - 1}{2} = 3$. Therefore $n - 1 = 6$, or $n = 7$. Indeed, $\dbinom{7}{2} = 21 = 3 \cdot 7$.
