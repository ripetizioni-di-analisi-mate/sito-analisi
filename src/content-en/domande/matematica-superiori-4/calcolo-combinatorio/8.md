## Explanation

A hand is a group of $4$ distinct cards whose order of arrival does not matter, so it is a combination without repetition from $40$ objects taken $4$ at a time. The number of hands is therefore
$$
\binom{40}{4} = \frac{40 \cdot 39 \cdot 38 \cdot 37}{4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = \frac{2\,193\,360}{24} = 91\,390 .
$$
The numerator $2\,193\,360 = D_{40,4}$ counts ordered sequences of $4$ cards, and each hand corresponds to $4! = 24$ sequences.
