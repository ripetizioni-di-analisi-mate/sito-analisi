A coach selects $2$ forwards from $5$ and $3$ defenders from $7$. In how many ways can the selection be made?

- $45$
- $792$
- $70$
- $350$

## Explanation

The forwards can be chosen in $\dbinom{5}{2} = 10$ ways and the defenders in $\dbinom{7}{3} = 35$ ways, regardless of the forwards chosen. By the fundamental counting principle the number of selections is $10 \cdot 35 = 350$. The sum $10 + 35 = 45$ counts the ways to choose only the forwards or only the defenders.
