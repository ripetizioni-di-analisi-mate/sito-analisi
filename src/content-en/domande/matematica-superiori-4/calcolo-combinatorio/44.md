Arrange $3$ mathematics books and $4$ physics books, all distinct, on a shelf so that books on the same subject are together. In how many ways can this be done?

- $144$
- $5040$
- $288$
- $48$

## Explanation

The mathematics books form one block and the physics books another. The two blocks can be ordered in $2! = 2$ ways. The books in the first block can be ordered in $3! = 6$ ways and those in the second in $4! = 24$ ways, regardless of the order of the blocks. By the fundamental counting principle the number of arrangements is $2 \cdot 6 \cdot 24 = 288$.
