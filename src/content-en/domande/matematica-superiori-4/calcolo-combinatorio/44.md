Two ordered lists of three and four names are merged. All names are distinct. How many merged orders preserve the internal ordering of each list?

- $144$
- $210$
- $35$
- $5040$

## Explanation

Choose three of the seven positions for names from the first list. Both internal orders are fixed, so the count is $\binom{7}{3}=35$.
