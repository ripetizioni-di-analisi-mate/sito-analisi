How many distinct arrangements of $A,A,B,B$ have the two $A$s nonadjacent?

- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The two $B$s leave one gap between them and one at each end. Separate the $A$s by selecting two different gaps out of three. Their order does not matter, giving three choices.
