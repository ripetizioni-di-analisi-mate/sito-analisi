How many anagrams does the word $MAMMA$ have, including arrangements that are not words?

- $120$
- $20$
- $60$
- $10$

## Explanation

The word $MAMMA$ has $5$ letters, with $3$ occurrences of $M$ and $2$ occurrences of $A$. By the formula for permutations with repeated elements, the number of anagrams is
$$
\frac{5!}{3! \cdot 2!} = \frac{120}{12} = 10 .
$$
The number $5! = 120$ counts the same anagram more than once, since interchanging two identical letters leaves the word unchanged.
