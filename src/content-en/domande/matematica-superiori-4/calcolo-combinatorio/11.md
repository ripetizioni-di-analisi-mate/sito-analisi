For a natural number $n \geq 2$, which expression equals $\dbinom{n}{2} + \dbinom{n + 1}{2}$?

- $n^2$
- $n^2 + n$
- $n^2 - 1$
- $2n^2$

## Explanation

By the definition of a binomial coefficient, $\dbinom{n}{2} = \dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2}$ and $\dbinom{n + 1}{2} = \dfrac{(n + 1) \, n}{2}$. The two fractions have the same denominator, so add the numerators and factor out $n$:
$$
\frac{n \, (n - 1) + n \, (n + 1)}{2} = \frac{n \, (n - 1 + n + 1)}{2} = \frac{n \cdot 2n}{2} = n^2 .
$$
Thus the sum equals $n^2$ for every $n \geq 2$.
