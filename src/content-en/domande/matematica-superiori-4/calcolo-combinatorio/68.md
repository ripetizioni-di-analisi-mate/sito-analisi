What is the value of $\dbinom{15}{13}$?

- $105$
- $210$
- $15$
- $455$

## Explanation

By the **symmetry of binomial coefficients**, $\dbinom{15}{13} = \dbinom{15}{2}$. The second coefficient can be calculated with just two factors in the numerator:
$$
\binom{15}{2} = \frac{15 \cdot 14}{2} = 105 .
$$
The number $210 = 15 \cdot 14$ results from omitting the denominator $2!$.
