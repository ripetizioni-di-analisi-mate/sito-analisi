## Explanation

The choice has three stages: choose a T-shirt in $4$ ways, pants in $3$ ways, and shoes in $2$ ways, regardless of the preceding choices. By the **fundamental counting principle**, the number of outfits is the product of the numbers of choices at the individual stages, namely $4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 24$. The sum $4 + 3 + 2 = 9$ counts the ways to choose just one item from all those available.
