On a street grid, travel from intersection $O$ to intersection $P$, which is $5$ blocks to the right and $2$ blocks up, moving only right and up. How many shortest paths are there?

- $10$
- $21$
- $35$
- $128$

## Explanation

Each path is a sequence of $7$ steps, with $5$ steps to the right and $2$ up. Conversely, every such sequence describes a path from $O$ to $P$. The paths are therefore counted by the permutations with repeated elements:
$$
\frac{7!}{5! \cdot 2!} = \frac{7 \cdot 6}{2} = 21 .
$$
