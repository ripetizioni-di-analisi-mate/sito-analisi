On a grid, move from $(0,0)$ to $(4,2)$ using only unit steps right or up. The junction $(2,1)$ is closed. How many shortest routes remain?

- $9$
- $6$
- $12$
- $15$

## Explanation

There are $\binom62=15$ sequences in all. Routes through the closed junction combine three approaches with three departures. Hence $15-3\cdot3=6$ routes remain.
