Two red and two blue beads, indistinguishable within each colour, are arranged in a circle. Only rotations are identified. How many necklaces are there?

- $6$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

There are just two configurations. The red beads are adjacent or opposite, in which case the colours alternate. Rotation preserves this distinction.
