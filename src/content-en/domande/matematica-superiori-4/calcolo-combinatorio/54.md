How many anagrams of the word $ROSSO$, including arrangements that are not words, start with the letter $R$?

- $24$
- $12$
- $30$
- $6$

## Explanation

Fix $R$ in the first position. The other $4$ positions contain $2$ occurrences of $O$ and $2$ occurrences of $S$. The number of ways to arrange them is
$$
\frac{4!}{2! \cdot 2!} = \frac{24}{4} = 6 .
$$
The number $30 = \dfrac{5!}{2! \cdot 2!}$ counts all the anagrams of $ROSSO$.
