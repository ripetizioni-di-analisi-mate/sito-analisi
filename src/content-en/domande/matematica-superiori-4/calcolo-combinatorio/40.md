For which natural number $n \geq 1$ does $\dfrac{(n + 1)!}{(n - 1)!} = 30$ hold?

- $n = 6$
- $n = 4$
- $n = 15$
- $n = 5$

## Explanation

Since $(n + 1)! = (n + 1) \cdot n \cdot (n - 1)!$, the left-hand side equals $(n + 1) \, n$. The equation therefore becomes $n^2 + n - 30 = 0$, whose solutions are $n = 5$ and $n = -6$. Since $n \geq 1$, we have $n = 5$, and indeed $6 \cdot 5 = 30$.
