## Explanation

The even digits are $0$, $2$, $4$, $6$, $8$, giving $5$ digits. The first digit of a four-digit number must differ from $0$, so it is chosen from $2$, $4$, $6$, $8$ in $4$ ways. Each of the other three digits is chosen from all $5$ even digits, since repetition is allowed. By the fundamental counting principle the total is $4 \cdot 5^3 = 4 \cdot 125 = 500$.
