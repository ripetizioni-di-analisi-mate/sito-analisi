A code consists of $2$ letters chosen from $26$, followed by $3$ digits. Letters and digits may repeat. How many codes are there?

- $468\,000$
- $676\,000$
- $650\,000$
- $67\,600$

## Explanation

The letters form an ordered selection with repetition from $26$ objects taken $2$ at a time, so there are $26^2 = 676$ choices. The digits form an ordered selection with repetition from $10$ objects taken $3$ at a time, so there are $10^3 = 1000$ choices. By the fundamental counting principle the number of codes is $676 \cdot 1000 = 676\,000$.
