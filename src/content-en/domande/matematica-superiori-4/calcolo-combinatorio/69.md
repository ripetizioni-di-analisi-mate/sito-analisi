What is the value of $\dbinom{9}{4} + \dbinom{9}{5}$?

- $126$
- $252$
- $210$
- $120$

## Explanation

By **Pascal’s identity** with $n = 10$ and $k = 5$, we have $\dbinom{9}{4} + \dbinom{9}{5} = \dbinom{10}{5}$. The sum is therefore
$$
\binom{10}{5} = \frac{10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6}{5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 252 .
$$
The same value follows from $\dbinom{9}{4} = \dbinom{9}{5} = 126$, since $126 + 126 = 252$.
