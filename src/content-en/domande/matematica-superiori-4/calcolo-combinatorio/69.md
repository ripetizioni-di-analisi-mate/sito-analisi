One delegate is chosen from each of two classes of $3$ and $4$ students. Why does $\binom72$ overcount the possible delegations?

- It counts every valid pair twice.
- It also counts pairs from the same class.
- It leaves out one class.
- It allows choosing the same person twice.

## Explanation

The binomial coefficient counts any pair of distinct students. There are $3\cdot4=12$ allowed pairs. The other $\binom32+\binom42=9$ come from a single class.
