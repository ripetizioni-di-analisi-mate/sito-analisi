You want to count one-way tickets between three distinct stations. Why is selecting an unordered pair of stations insufficient?

- Every ticket involves all three stations.
- The departure and arrival stations may coincide.
- Each pair determines two directions of travel.
- Each pair determines three tickets.

## Explanation

A ticket from $A$ to $B$ differs from one from $B$ to $A$. The three unordered pairs therefore generate $2\cdot3=6$ tickets.
