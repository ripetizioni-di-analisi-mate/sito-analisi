A code consists of a vowel chosen from $A$, $E$, $I$, $O$, $U$ followed by a digit from $0$ to $9$. How many codes are possible?

- $15$
- $25$
- $50$
- $100$

## Explanation

The code is formed in two stages: first choose the vowel in $5$ ways, then the digit in $10$ ways regardless of the vowel. By the fundamental counting principle the number of codes is $5 \cdot 10 = 50$. The sum $5 + 10 = 15$ counts codes made of a single symbol, either a vowel or a digit.
