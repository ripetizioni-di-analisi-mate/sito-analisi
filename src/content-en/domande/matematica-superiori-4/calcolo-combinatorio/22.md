How many three-digit natural numbers are odd? Digits may repeat.

- $500$
- $450$
- $405$
- $125$

## Explanation

The hundreds digit differs from $0$, so it can be chosen in $9$ ways. The tens digit is chosen from all $10$ digits. A number is odd when its units digit is odd, so the last digit is chosen from $1$, $3$, $5$, $7$, $9$ in $5$ ways. By the fundamental counting principle the total is $9 \cdot 10 \cdot 5 = 450$.
