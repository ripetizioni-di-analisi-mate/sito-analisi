In the expansion of $(a - 2b)^5$, what is the coefficient of $a^2 b^3$?

- $80$
- $-40$
- $-10$
- $-80$

## Explanation

The general term in the expansion of $(a - 2b)^5$ is $\dbinom{5}{k} a^{5 - k} (-2b)^k$. The term containing $a^2 b^3$ occurs when $k = 3$ and equals
$$
\binom{5}{3} a^2 (-2b)^3 = 10 \cdot (-8) \, a^2 b^3 = -80 \, a^2 b^3 .
$$
The exponent $3$ applies to both the coefficient $-2$ and $b$, so the factor is $(-2)^3 = -8$. The coefficient is therefore $-80$.
