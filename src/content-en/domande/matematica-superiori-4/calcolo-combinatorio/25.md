For which natural number $n \geq 2$ does $D_{n,2} = 56$ hold?

- $n = 8$
- $n = 7$
- $n = 28$
- $n = 56$

## Explanation

By the formula for counting ordered selections without repetition, $D_{n,2} = n \, (n - 1)$, so the equation is $n \, (n - 1) = 56$, or $n^2 - n - 56 = 0$. The solutions are $n = 8$ and $n = -7$. Since $n$ is a natural number, $n = 8$, and indeed $8 \cdot 7 = 56$.
