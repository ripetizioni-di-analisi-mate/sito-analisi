In how many ways can $5$ people, including Luca and Sara, sit around a round table with Luca and Sara next to each other? Arrangements differing by a rotation are considered the same.

- $24$
- $48$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

Treat Luca and Sara as one block, so there are $4$ objects to arrange around the table. The number of circular permutations of $4$ objects is $(4 - 1)! = 6$. Within the block, Luca and Sara can be arranged in $2$ ways regardless of the seating around the table. By the fundamental counting principle the number of arrangements is $6 \cdot 2 = 12$.
