Four beads of distinct colors form a bracelet. Rotations and flipping the bracelet give equivalent arrangements. How many different bracelets are there?

- $6$
- $12$
- $3$
- $24$

## Explanation

Fixing one bead removes rotations and leaves $3!=6$ orders. Each order has a distinct mirror image because all colors differ, so divide by two.
