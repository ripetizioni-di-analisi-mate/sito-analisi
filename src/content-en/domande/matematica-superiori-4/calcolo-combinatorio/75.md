From $10$ people, one of whom is Anna, a group of $4$ is chosen. How many groups contain Anna?

- $126$
- $210$
- $40$
- $84$

## Explanation

A group containing Anna is determined by its other $3$ members, chosen from the $9$ people other than Anna. There are therefore $\dbinom{9}{3} = \dfrac{9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7}{6} = 84$ groups. The number of groups that do not contain Anna is $\dbinom{9}{4} = 126$, and their sum $84 + 126 = 210 = \dbinom{10}{4}$ is an instance of Pascal’s identity.
