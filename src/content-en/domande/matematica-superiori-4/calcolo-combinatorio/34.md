From a five-element set select an ordered pair of subsets $(A,B)$ with $A\cap B=\varnothing$. How many pairs are possible if empty subsets are allowed?

- $32$
- $120$
- $1024$
- $243$

## Explanation

Each element may lie in $A$ only, in $B$ only, or in neither. The choices are independent, giving $3^5=243$.
