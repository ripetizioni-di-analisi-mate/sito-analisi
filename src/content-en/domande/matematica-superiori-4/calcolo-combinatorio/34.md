A coin is tossed $5$ times. How many sequences of outcomes contain at least one head?

- $32$
- $5$
- $25$
- $31$

## Explanation

Sequences of $5$ tosses are ordered selections with repetition from $2$ objects taken $5$ at a time, so there are $2^5 = 32$ sequences. Only one contains no heads, namely the sequence of five tails. The number containing at least one head is therefore $32 - 1 = 31$.
