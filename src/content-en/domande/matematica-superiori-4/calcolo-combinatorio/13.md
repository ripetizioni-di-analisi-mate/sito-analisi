What is the term independent of $x$ in the expansion of $\left( 3x + \dfrac{2}{x} \right)^6$, with $x \neq 0$?

- $20$
- $216$
- $4320$
- $4860$

## Explanation

With $a = 3x$ and $b = \dfrac{2}{x} = 2x^{-1}$, the general term of the expansion is
$$
T_{k + 1} = \binom{6}{k} (3x)^{6 - k} \left( 2x^{-1} \right)^k = \binom{6}{k} \, 3^{6 - k} \, 2^k \, x^{6 - 2k} .
$$
The term is independent of $x$ when $6 - 2k = 0$, that is, for $k = 3$. The required term is therefore $\dbinom{6}{3} \cdot 3^3 \cdot 2^3 = 20 \cdot 27 \cdot 8 = 4320$.
