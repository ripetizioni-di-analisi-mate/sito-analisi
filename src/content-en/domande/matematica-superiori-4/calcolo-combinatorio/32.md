How many sequences of $8$ digits, each equal to $0$ or $1$, start with $1$?

- $256$
- $64$
- $16$
- $128$

## Explanation

The first digit is fixed at $1$. Each of the other $7$ digits can be chosen in $2$ ways regardless of the other digits. By the fundamental counting principle the number of sequences is $2^7 = 128$.
