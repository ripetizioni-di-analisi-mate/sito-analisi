## Explanation

A finishing order for the first three horses is an ordered triple of distinct horses, so it is an ordered selection without repetition from $12$ objects taken $3$ at a time. The first horse is chosen from $12$ horses, the second from the $11$ remaining, and the third from the $10$ still remaining. The number of finishing orders is therefore $D_{12,3} = 12 \cdot 11 \cdot 10 = 1320$. The number $\dbinom{12}{3} = 220$ counts groups of three finishers without regard to order.
