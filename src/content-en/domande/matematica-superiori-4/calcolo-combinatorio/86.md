What is the coefficient of $x^6$ in the expansion of $\left( x^2 + \dfrac{1}{x} \right)^9$, with $x \neq 0$?

- $84$
- $36$
- $126$
- $9$

## Explanation

With $a = x^2$ and $b = x^{-1}$, the general term of the expansion is
$$
T_{k + 1} = \binom{9}{k} \left( x^2 \right)^{9 - k} \left( x^{-1} \right)^k = \binom{9}{k} x^{18 - 3k} .
$$
The exponent equals $6$ when $18 - 3k = 6$, that is, for $k = 4$. The coefficient is therefore $\dbinom{9}{4} = 126$.
