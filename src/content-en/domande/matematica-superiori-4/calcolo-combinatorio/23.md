A captain and a deputy are chosen from $6$ people. Two candidates, Ada and Leo, may not hold the two posts together. How many ordered choices remain?

- $29$
- $26$
- $24$
- $28$

## Explanation

Without the restriction there are $6\cdot5=30$ choices. Two ordered pairs are forbidden, Ada as captain with Leo as deputy and the reverse. This leaves $28$ assignments.
