Roll a die. If the result is even, toss a coin; if it is odd, roll the die again. How many outcomes, that is, ordered pairs of results, does the experiment have?

- $24$
- $12$
- $36$
- $48$

## Explanation

The number of outcomes at the second stage depends on the first result, so the fundamental counting principle does not apply to the whole experiment. Split it into two disjoint cases. If one of the $3$ even numbers comes up, the coin has $2$ outcomes, giving $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ pairs. If one of the $3$ odd numbers comes up, the second roll has $6$ outcomes, giving $3 \cdot 6 = 18$ pairs. By the **addition principle** the number of outcomes is $6 + 18 = 24$.
