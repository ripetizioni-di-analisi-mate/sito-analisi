What is the sum of the coefficients of the polynomial obtained by expanding $(2x - 1)^9$?

- $1$
- $512$
- $0$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The sum of the coefficients of a polynomial $p(x)$ is the value $p(1)$, since for $x = 1$ every power of $x$ equals $1$. For $x = 1$ we obtain $(2 \cdot 1 - 1)^9 = 1^9 = 1$. Therefore the sum of the coefficients is $1$. The value $512 = 2^9$ is the sum of the binomial coefficients $\dbinom{9}{k}$ without the factors $2^{9 - k}$ and $(-1)^k$.
