What is the maximum value of $\dbinom{10}{k}$ as $k$ varies from $0$ to $10$?

- $210$
- $252$
- $1024$
- $120$

## Explanation

Row $10$ of Pascal’s triangle is $1, 10, 45, 120, 210, 252, 210, 120, 45, 10, 1$. Its entries increase up to the center and then decrease, and the row is symmetric, so the maximum occurs at $k = 5$. The maximum value is
$$
\binom{10}{5} = \frac{10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6}{5!} = 252 .
$$
The number $1024 = 2^{10}$ is the sum of all the entries in the row.
