Expand $(1+x)^8$ and reduce the coefficients modulo two. Given that $(1+x)^2$ reduces to $1+x^2$ modulo two, how many monomials retain an odd coefficient?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $9$

## Explanation

Repeated squaring gives $1+x^4$ and then $1+x^8$ modulo two. Only the constant and the degree-eight monomial remain.
