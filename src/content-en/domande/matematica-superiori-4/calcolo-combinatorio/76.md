What is the third entry in row $8$ of Pascal’s triangle if rows are numbered starting at $n = 0$?

- $56$
- $21$
- $28$
- $8$

## Explanation

Row $n$ contains the coefficients $\dbinom{n}{0}$, $\dbinom{n}{1}$, $\dbinom{n}{2}$, and so on in order, so the third entry is $\dbinom{n}{2}$. For $n = 8$ we have
$$
\binom{8}{2} = \frac{8 \cdot 7}{2} = 28 .
$$
The number $21 = \dbinom{7}{2}$ is the third entry in row $7$.
