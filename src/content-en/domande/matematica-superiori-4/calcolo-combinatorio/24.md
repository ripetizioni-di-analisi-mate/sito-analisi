How many four-digit natural numbers have distinct digits?

- $5040$
- $3024$
- $6561$
- $4536$

## Explanation

The first digit differs from $0$, so it can be chosen in $9$ ways. The second differs from the first and may be $0$, so there are $9$ choices. The third and fourth are chosen first from the $8$ unused digits and then from the $7$ still unused. By the fundamental counting principle the total is $9 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 = 4536$. The number $D_{10,4} = 5040$ also includes sequences that start with $0$.
