## Explanation

A convex polygon with $n$ sides has $\dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2}$ segments joining two vertices, of which $n$ are sides. The number of diagonals is therefore $\dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2} - n = \dfrac{n \, (n - 3)}{2}$. The equation $\dfrac{n \, (n - 3)}{2} = 20$ is equivalent to $n^2 - 3n - 40 = 0$, whose solutions are $n = 8$ and $n = -5$. Since $n$ is a natural number, the polygon has $8$ sides and is an octagon.
