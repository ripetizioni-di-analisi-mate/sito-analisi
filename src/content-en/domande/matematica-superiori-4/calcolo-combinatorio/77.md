What is the sum of the entries in row $9$ of Pascal’s triangle?

- $256$
- $512$
- $1024$
- $81$

## Explanation

Row $9$ contains the coefficients $\dbinom{9}{0}, \dbinom{9}{1}, \ldots, \dbinom{9}{9}$. By the identity for the sum of the binomial coefficients, their sum is $2^9$. The sum therefore equals $512$. The value $1024 = 2^{10}$ is the sum of row $10$, which contains $11$ entries.
