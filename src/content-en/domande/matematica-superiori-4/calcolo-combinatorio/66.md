Using $4$ distinct letters, form all length-three words in strictly increasing alphabetical order. Why are there only four?

- The three letters are always consecutive in the alphabet.
- Each selection of three letters has exactly one increasing order.
- Only the first letter is chosen.
- Each word allows three repetitions.

## Explanation

Exclude one of the four letters and arrange the other three in their unique increasing order. The four possible excluded letters give four different words.
