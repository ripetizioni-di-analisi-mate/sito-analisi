Which of the following is counted by the binomial coefficient $\dbinom{10}{3}$?

- The finishing orders of the top $3$ among $10$ runners
- Groups of $3$ students to be called on, chosen from $10$
- Codes of $3$ digits, each ranging from $0$ to $9$
- Cups with $3$ scoops chosen from $10$ flavors, allowing repeated flavors

## Explanation

The coefficient $\dbinom{10}{3}$ counts subsets of $3$ elements of a set with $10$ elements, that is, selections of $3$ distinct objects without regard to order. A group of students to be called on has these properties, so there are $\dbinom{10}{3} = 120$ groups. Finishing orders are ordered selections without repetition, $D_{10,3} = 720$, and codes are ordered selections with repetition, $10^3 = 1000$. Ice cream cups allowing repeated flavors are combinations with repetition, and there are $\dbinom{12}{3} = 220$ of them.
