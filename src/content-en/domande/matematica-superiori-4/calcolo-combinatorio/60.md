Choose three vertices of a labeled hexagon so that no two are consecutive, counting the first and last as consecutive. How many choices are there?

- $6$
- $10$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The three unselected vertices must separate the three selected ones individually, or two selected ones would be adjacent. The two possibilities are to take all odd vertices or all even vertices.
