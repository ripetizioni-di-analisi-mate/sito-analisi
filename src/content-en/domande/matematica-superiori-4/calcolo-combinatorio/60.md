There are $8$ distinct points on a circle. How many triangles have vertices among these points?

- $336$
- $24$
- $56$
- $28$

## Explanation

Three distinct points on a circle are never collinear, so each set of $3$ points determines a triangle. The order of the vertices does not affect the triangle, so the number of triangles is
$$
\binom{8}{3} = \frac{8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 56 .
$$
The number $D_{8,3} = 336$ counts each triangle $3! = 6$ times.
