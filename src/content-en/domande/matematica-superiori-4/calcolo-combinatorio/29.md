How many three-digit even numbers with distinct digits can be formed from $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, $6$?

- $120$
- $108$
- $40$
- $60$

## Explanation

A number is even when its units digit is even, so the last digit is chosen from $2$, $4$, $6$ in $3$ ways. The hundreds and tens digits must be distinct and are chosen from the $5$ remaining digits in $D_{5,2} = 5 \cdot 4 = 20$ ways. By the fundamental counting principle the total is $3 \cdot 20 = 60$.
