A four-symbol code uses only $A$ and $B$. If it must read the same forwards and backwards, how many codes are possible?

- $8$
- $16$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Once the first two symbols are chosen, the third equals the second and the fourth equals the first. The two free positions each have two choices, giving $2^2=4$.
