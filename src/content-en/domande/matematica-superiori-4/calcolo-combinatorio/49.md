How many anagrams does the word $PAPPAGALLO$ have, including arrangements that are not words?

- $50\,400$
- $100\,800$
- $302\,400$
- $3\,628\,800$

## Explanation

The word has $10$ letters: $3$ occurrences of $P$, $3$ occurrences of $A$, $2$ occurrences of $L$, and one occurrence each of $G$ and $O$. Letters occurring once contribute factors of $1! = 1$ to the denominator, so the number of anagrams is
$$
\frac{10!}{3! \cdot 3! \cdot 2!} = \frac{3\,628\,800}{72} = 50\,400 .
$$
The value $100\,800$ results from leaving out the factor $2!$ for the two occurrences of $L$.
