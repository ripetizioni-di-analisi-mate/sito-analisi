Arrange the letters of $AABC$ into four-letter strings. How many begin and end with different letters?

- $10$
- $8$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

There are $4!/2!=12$ arrangements in total. Only $A$ is repeated, so equal endpoints force the two $A$s there and leave two orders for $B,C$ in the middle. This leaves $12-2=10$.
