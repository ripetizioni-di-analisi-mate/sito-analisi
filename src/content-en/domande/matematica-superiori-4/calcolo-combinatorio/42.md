In how many ways can $6$ people sit around a round table if arrangements differing by a rotation are considered the same?

- $720$
- $60$
- $120$
- $36$

## Explanation

Fix the seat of one of the $6$ people. The other $5$ people can occupy the remaining seats in $5! = 120$ ways, and each seating around the table occurs exactly once this way. The number of **circular permutations** of $6$ objects is therefore $(6 - 1)! = 120$. The same count is $\dfrac{6!}{6}$, since every seating around the table corresponds to the $6$ linear arrangements obtained from its rotations.
