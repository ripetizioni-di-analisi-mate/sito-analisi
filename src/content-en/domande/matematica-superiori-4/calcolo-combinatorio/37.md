A row of $4$ squares is colored using $3$ colors, with adjacent squares required to have different colors. How many colorings are there?

- $81$
- $6$
- $24$
- $48$

## Explanation

The first square can be colored in $3$ ways. Each subsequent square must differ in color from the preceding square, so it can be colored in $2$ ways regardless of the colors already used. By the fundamental counting principle the number of colorings is $3 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 24$. The number $3^4 = 81$ counts colorings without the restriction on adjacent squares.
