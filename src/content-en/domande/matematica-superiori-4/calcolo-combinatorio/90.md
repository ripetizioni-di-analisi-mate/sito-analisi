In a league with $10$ teams, each team plays every other team twice, once at home and once away. How many games are played?

- $45$
- $90$
- $100$
- $20$

## Explanation

A game is determined by its home team and its away team, so it is an ordered pair of distinct teams. The number of games is therefore the number of ordered selections without repetition $D_{10,2} = 10 \cdot 9 = 90$. The number $\dbinom{10}{2} = 45$ counts pairs of teams without distinguishing between the home and away fixtures.
