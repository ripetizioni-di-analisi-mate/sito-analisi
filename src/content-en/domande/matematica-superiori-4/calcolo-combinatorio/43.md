What is the smallest natural number $n$ for which $n! > 1000$?

- $n = 6$
- $n = 5$
- $n = 10$
- $n = 7$

## Explanation

Successive factorials are found using $n! = n \cdot (n - 1)!$. This gives $5! = 120$, then $6! = 6 \cdot 120 = 720$ and $7! = 7 \cdot 720 = 5040$. Since $6! = 720$ is less than $1000$ and $7! = 5040$ is greater than $1000$, the required number is $n = 7$.
