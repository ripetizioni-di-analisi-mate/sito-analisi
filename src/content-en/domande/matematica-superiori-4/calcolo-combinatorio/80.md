The sum of the entries in a row of Pascal’s triangle is $128$. How many entries does the row contain?

- $7$
- $64$
- $9$
- $8$

## Explanation

The sum of the entries in row $n$ is $2^n$, and $128 = 2^7$, so this is row $7$. Row $n$ contains the coefficients from $\dbinom{n}{0}$ to $\dbinom{n}{n}$, giving $n + 1$ entries. The row therefore contains $7 + 1 = 8$ entries.
