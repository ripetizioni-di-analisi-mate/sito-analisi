In cube $ABCDEFGH$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, how are the planes of faces $ABFE$ and $DCGH$ positioned relative to each other?

- Parallel
- Intersect along a line
- Perpendicular
- Coincident

## Explanation

Edge $AD$ is perpendicular to lines $AB$ and $AE$ because the faces of the cube are squares. Thus, by the perpendicularity criterion, it is perpendicular to plane $ABFE$. Similarly, edge $DA$ is perpendicular to plane $DCGH$. Two distinct planes perpendicular to the same line have no points in common, so the planes of the two faces are **parallel** and the distance between them is $AD$.
