A cone has base radius $4$ and slant height $12$. Its lateral surface, when unrolled onto a plane, is a circular sector. What is the central angle of the sector?

- $60°$
- $240°$
- $30°$
- $120°$

## Explanation

The radius of the sector is the slant height, $12$, and its arc length is the circumference of the base, $2\pi \cdot 4 = 8\pi$. The central angle in radians is the ratio of arc length to radius, so it equals $\dfrac{8\pi}{12} = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. Therefore the angle measures $120°$.
