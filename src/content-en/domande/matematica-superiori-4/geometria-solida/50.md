A convex polyhedron has $10$ faces, all triangular. How many edges $S$ and vertices $V$ does it have?

- $S = 30$, $V = 22$
- $S = 15$, $V = 27$
- $S = 10$, $V = 2$
- $S = 15$, $V = 7$

## Explanation

The $10$ triangular faces have a total of $30$ sides, and each edge is a side of exactly two faces. Thus the number of edges is $S = \dfrac{30}{2} = 15$. By Euler's formula, $V = 2 - F + S = 2 - 10 + 15 = 7$. The value $S = 30$ comes from counting each edge twice.
