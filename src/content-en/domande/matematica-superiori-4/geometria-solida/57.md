The lateral surface of a cylinder of radius $3$ and height $5$ is cut along a generator and unrolled onto a plane. What are the dimensions of the resulting rectangle?

- $3\pi$ and $5$
- $9\pi$ and $5$
- $6\pi$ and $5$
- $6$ and $5$

## Explanation

One side of the rectangle is the generator, whose length is the height of the cylinder, or $5$. The other side is the circumference of the base unrolled onto a straight line, so its length is $2\pi r = 6\pi$. Thus the rectangle has sides $6\pi$ and $5$, and its area $30\pi$ is the lateral surface area of the cylinder. The value $9\pi$ is the area of the base disk.
