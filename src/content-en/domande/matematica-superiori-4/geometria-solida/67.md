A solid ball of radius $2$ is melted into eight equal balls with no loss. What is the radius of each?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

Each new ball has one eighth of the original volume. The radius is multiplied by the cube root of $\dfrac{1}{8}$, so it halves.
