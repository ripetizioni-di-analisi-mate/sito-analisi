A sphere has surface area $100\pi$. What is its volume?

- $\dfrac{500}{3}\,\pi$
- $500\pi$
- $\dfrac{4000}{3}\,\pi$
- $125\pi$

## Explanation

From the formula $4\pi R^2 = 100\pi$ we get $R^2 = 25$, so $R = 5$. The volume of the sphere is $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\pi R^3 = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\pi \cdot 125 = \dfrac{500}{3}\,\pi$. The value $\dfrac{4000}{3}\,\pi$ comes from writing the surface area as $\pi R^2$, which gives $R = 10$.
