A right prism of height $5$ has a regular hexagonal base of side length $2$. What is its volume?

- $10\sqrt{3}$
- $30\sqrt{3}$
- $15\sqrt{3}$
- $60\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

A regular hexagon of side length $2$ can be divided into $6$ equilateral triangles of side length $2$, each of area $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot 4 = \sqrt{3}$. Thus the base area is $B = 6\sqrt{3}$. The volume of the prism is $V = B \cdot h = 6\sqrt{3} \cdot 5 = 30\sqrt{3}$. The value $10\sqrt{3}$ comes from incorrectly using the factor $\dfrac{1}{3}$ for a pyramid.
