Rectangular prism $ABCDEFGH$ has base $ABCD$ with $AB = 3$ and $BC = 4$, and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$ of length $12$. What is the distance between skew lines $AB$ and $CG$?

- $3$
- $12$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The distance between two skew lines is the length of their common perpendicular segment. Edge $BC$ is perpendicular to $AB$ because the base is a rectangle, and it is perpendicular to $CG$ because face $BCGF$ is a rectangle. Also, $BC$ has one endpoint on each of the two lines, so it is their common perpendicular segment. Therefore the distance is $BC = 4$.
