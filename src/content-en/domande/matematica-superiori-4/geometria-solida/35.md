A right prism of height $9$ has a rhombus with diagonals $6$ and $8$ as its base. What is its lateral surface area?

- $90$
- $228$
- $252$
- $180$

## Explanation

The diagonals of the rhombus are perpendicular and bisect each other, so its side length is $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$ and the base perimeter is $2p = 20$. The lateral surface area of a right prism is the base perimeter times the height, so $A_l = 20 \cdot 9 = 180$. The value $228$ is the total surface area, which includes the two bases, each of area $24$.
