A cone of height $9$ is inscribed in a sphere of radius $5$. What is the base radius of the cone?

- $4$
- $\sqrt{41}$
- $\sqrt{7}$
- $3$

## Explanation

In the axial section, the center $O$ of the sphere lies on the altitude of the cone, at a distance of $9 - 5 = 4$ from the center $K$ of the base. A point $P$ on the rim of the base is at a distance of $5$ from $O$, and triangle $OKP$ has a right angle at $K$. Thus the base radius is $KP = \sqrt{25 - 16} = 3$. The value $4$ is the distance from $O$ to the base, and $\sqrt{41}$ comes from adding the squares instead of subtracting them.
