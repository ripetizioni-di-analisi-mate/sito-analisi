A cylindrical hole of radius $r<a/2$ is drilled through a cube of side $a$, with its axis through the centers of two opposite faces. Which expression gives the remaining surface area including the inside wall?

- $6a^2-\pi r^2a$
- $6a^2-2\pi r^2$
- $6a^2+2\pi r^2+2\pi ra$
- $6a^2-2\pi r^2+2\pi ra$

## Explanation

Two disks of radius $r$ are removed from the entry and exit faces. The inner wall adds the lateral area of a cylinder of height $a$, equal to $2\pi ra$.
