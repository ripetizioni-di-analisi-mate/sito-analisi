Point $P$ is at a distance of $5$ from plane $\alpha$, and $H$ is its orthogonal projection onto $\alpha$. The points $A$ in $\alpha$ such that $PA = 13$ form a circle centered at $H$. What is its radius?

- $8$
- $12$
- $\sqrt{194}$
- $18$

## Explanation

For each point $A$ in $\alpha$, triangle $PHA$ has a right angle at $H$ because $PH$ is perpendicular to the plane and hence to line $HA$. By the Pythagorean theorem, $HA^2 = PA^2 - PH^2 = 169 - 25 = 144$. Therefore every point satisfying $PA = 13$ is at a distance of $12$ from $H$, and the circle has radius $12$. The value $8$ comes from subtracting the lengths instead of their squares.
