The orthogonal projection of an **entire segment** onto a plane is a single point. What direction does the nonzero segment have?

- It is parallel to the plane.
- It is perpendicular to the plane.
- It lies in the plane.
- It necessarily makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the plane.

## Explanation

The endpoints share one projection. They therefore lie on the same projecting line, which by construction is perpendicular to the plane.
