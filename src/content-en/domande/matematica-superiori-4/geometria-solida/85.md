In cube $ABCDEFGH$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, face diagonal $AF$ is perpendicular to edge $AD$. Which statement about line $AF$ is true?

- $AF$ is perpendicular to the plane of the base
- $AF$ makes an angle of $45°$ with the plane of the base
- $AF$ is parallel to the plane of the base
- $AF$ makes an angle of $\arctan\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ with the plane of the base

## Explanation

The perpendicularity criterion requires the line to be perpendicular to *two* distinct lines in the plane through the point of intersection. Line $AF$ is perpendicular to $AD$ and its angle with $AB$ is $45°$ because $AF$ is a diagonal of square $ABFE$, so $AF$ is not perpendicular to the base. Edge $FB$ is perpendicular to the base, so the projection of $AF$ onto the base is $AB$. Thus the angle between $AF$ and the base is $\widehat{FAB} = 45°$.
