A cylinder of height $8$ has lateral surface area $48\pi$. What is its volume?

- $24\pi$
- $72\pi$
- $288\pi$
- $66\pi$

## Explanation

The lateral surface area of the cylinder is $2\pi r h$, so $2\pi r \cdot 8 = 48\pi$ and $r = 3$. The volume is the base area times the height, so $V = \pi \cdot 3^2 \cdot 8 = 72\pi$. The value $288\pi$ comes from writing the lateral surface area as $\pi r h$, which gives $r = 6$.
