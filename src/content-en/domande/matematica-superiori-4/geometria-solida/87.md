A rectangular prism has a square base of side length $4$, and its height is $4\sqrt{6}$. What angle does its space diagonal make with the plane of the base?

- $30°$
- $\arctan\sqrt{6}$
- $45°$
- $60°$

## Explanation

The projection of the diagonal onto the plane of the base is the diagonal of the square, whose length is $4\sqrt{2}$. The space diagonal of the prism, the base diagonal, and the lateral edge form a right triangle. Thus the angle $\theta$ with the base satisfies $\tan\theta = \dfrac{4\sqrt{6}}{4\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{3}$, so $\theta = 60°$. The value $\arctan\sqrt{6}$ comes from dividing the height by the side length of the base instead of by the base diagonal.
