The internal dimensions of a rectangular box are $2,3,6$. Can a straight rod of length $7$ and negligible thickness fit completely inside, allowing contact with the walls?

- No, because no side is $7$ long.
- Yes, on any face.
- Yes, with at least one unit of length to spare.
- Yes, exactly along a space diagonal.

## Explanation

The largest distance between two points of the box is its space diagonal, $\sqrt{2^2+3^2+6^2}=7$. The rod can therefore touch two opposite vertices with its endpoints.
