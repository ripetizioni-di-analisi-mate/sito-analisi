A sphere is inscribed in a cylinder of volume $54\pi$. What is the volume of the sphere?

- $18\pi$
- $36\pi$
- $27\pi$
- $72\pi$

## Explanation

A cylinder circumscribed about a sphere of radius $R$ has radius $R$ and height $2R$, so its volume is $2\pi R^3$. The volume of the sphere, $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\pi R^3$, is two-thirds of this value. Therefore the volume of the sphere is $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot 54\pi = 36\pi$. Indeed, $2\pi R^3 = 54\pi$ gives $R = 3$, and $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\pi \cdot 27 = 36\pi$.
