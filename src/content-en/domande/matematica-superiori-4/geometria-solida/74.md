A ball of radius $r$ is lowered into a brimful cylindrical vessel and remains fully submerged. What volume of water spills out?

- $4\pi r^2$
- $\dfrac{4\pi r^3}{3}$
- $\pi r^2$
- $\dfrac{2\pi r^3}{3}$

## Explanation

A fully submerged object displaces a volume of liquid equal to the volume it occupies. For a solid ball that volume is $\dfrac{4\pi r^3}{3}$.
