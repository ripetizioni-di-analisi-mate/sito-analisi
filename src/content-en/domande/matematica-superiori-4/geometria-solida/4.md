A regular square pyramid has a base edge of length $12$ and slant height $10$. What is its total surface area?

- $384$
- $240$
- $624$
- $264$

## Explanation

The semiperimeter of the base is $p = 24$, so the lateral surface area is $A_l = p \cdot a = 24 \cdot 10 = 240$. The base area is $12^2 = 144$. Therefore the total surface area is
$$
A_t = A_l + B = 240 + 144 = 384 .
$$
The value $624$ comes from using the full perimeter $2p = 48$ in place of the semiperimeter.
