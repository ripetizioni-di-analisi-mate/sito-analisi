In cube $ABCDEFGH$ of edge length $6$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, what is the distance from vertex $B$ to plane $ACGE$?

- $6\sqrt{2}$
- $6$
- $3\sqrt{2}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Let $O$ be the center of the base. Diagonal $BD$ is perpendicular to diagonal $AC$ because the diagonals of a square are perpendicular, and it is perpendicular to $AE$ because $AE$ is perpendicular to the plane of the base. By the **perpendicularity criterion**, line $BD$ is perpendicular to plane $ACGE$, which it meets at $O$. Thus the distance from $B$ to the plane is $BO$, half the base diagonal, or $\dfrac{6\sqrt{2}}{2} = 3\sqrt{2}$.
