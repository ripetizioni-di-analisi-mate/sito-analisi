A sphere is inscribed in a cube of edge length $6$. What is the ratio of the volume of the sphere to that of the cube?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The inscribed sphere is tangent to all six faces, so its diameter equals the edge length and its radius is $3$. The volume of the sphere is $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\pi \cdot 27 = 36\pi$ and the volume of the cube is $216$. Therefore the ratio is $\dfrac{36\pi}{216} = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$. The value $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is the ratio of the volume of the sphere to that of its circumscribed cylinder.
