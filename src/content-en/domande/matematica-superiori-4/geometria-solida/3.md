A regular triangular prism has a base edge of length $6$ and height $5$. What is its volume?

- $45\sqrt{3}$
- $15\sqrt{3}$
- $90\sqrt{3}$
- $180$

## Explanation

The base is an equilateral triangle with side length $6$ and height $3\sqrt{3}$, so its area is $B = \dfrac{6 \cdot 3\sqrt{3}}{2} = 9\sqrt{3}$. The volume of a prism is the product of its base area and its height. Therefore $V = 9\sqrt{3} \cdot 5 = 45\sqrt{3}$. The value $15\sqrt{3}$ comes from incorrectly using the pyramid formula with the factor $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
