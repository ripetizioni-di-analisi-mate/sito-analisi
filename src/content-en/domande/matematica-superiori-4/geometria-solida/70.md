A cube has volume $0.008\,\mathrm{m}^3$. What is its edge length in centimeters?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $80\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{m}^3 = 10^6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, the volume is $0.008 \cdot 10^6 = 8000\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. The edge length is the cube root of the volume, so $l = \sqrt[3]{8000} = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Alternatively, $\sqrt[3]{0.008} = 0.2\,\mathrm{m}$, which is equivalent to $20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
