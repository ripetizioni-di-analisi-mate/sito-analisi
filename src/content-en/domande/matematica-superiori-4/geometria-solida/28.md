In cube $ABCDEFGH$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, which angle gives the inclination of diagonal $AG$ to the plane of the base?

- $\widehat{GAB}$
- $\widehat{GAE}$
- $\widehat{AGC}$
- $\widehat{GAC}$

## Explanation

The angle between a line and a plane is the angle the line makes with its orthogonal projection onto the plane. Edge $GC$ is perpendicular to the plane of the base, so the projection of $G$ onto the base is $C$. The projection of diagonal $AG$ is therefore base diagonal $AC$, and the required angle is $\widehat{GAC}$. Angle $\widehat{AGC}$ is the other acute angle of triangle $ACG$ and gives the inclination of $AG$ to edge $CG$.
