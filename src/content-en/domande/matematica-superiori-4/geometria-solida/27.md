A rectangle of area $12$ is orthogonally projected onto a plane making an angle of $60^\circ$ with its plane. What is the projected area?

- $12$
- $6\sqrt{3}$
- $6$
- $24$

## Explanation

Lengths along the intersection of the planes are unchanged. Lengths perpendicular to that line in the original plane are scaled by $\cos (60^\circ)=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the area halves.
