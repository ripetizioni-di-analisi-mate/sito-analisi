Segment $PA$, with $A$ in plane $\alpha$, has length $8$, and its orthogonal projection $HA$ onto $\alpha$ has length $4$. What angle does $PA$ make with plane $\alpha$?

- $30°$
- $45°$
- $60°$
- $\arctan 2$

## Explanation

The angle between line $PA$ and the plane is angle $\theta$ between $PA$ and its projection $HA$. Triangle $PHA$ has a right angle at $H$, so $\cos\theta = \dfrac{HA}{PA} = \dfrac{4}{8} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $\theta = 60°$. The value $30°$ comes from using the sine in place of the cosine.
