A sphere has volume $288\pi$. How far from the center is a plane that cuts the sphere in a disk of area $20\pi$?

- $2\sqrt{14}$
- $16$
- $4$
- $2\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

From $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\pi R^3 = 288\pi$ we obtain $R^3 = 216$, so $R = 6$. The cross section is a disk of area $\pi r^2 = 20\pi$, so $r^2 = 20$. For a plane section of a sphere, $r^2 = R^2 - d^2$, so $d^2 = 36 - 20 = 16$ and $d = 4$. The value $2\sqrt{5}$ is the radius of the cross section.
