Two congruent right cones stand between the same horizontal planes, one apex-up and one apex-down. What do the horizontal sections near the lower plane demonstrate?

- The sections are equal at every height.
- The apex-down cone has zero volume.
- Equal volumes do not force equal cross-sectional areas at every height.
- Congruence depends on the direction of the vertical axis.

## Explanation

The volumes agree because the cones are congruent. Near the lower plane the first has a section almost as large as its base and the second has a small section near its apex. Cavalieri's cross-section hypothesis is sufficient for equal volumes, but it is not necessary.
