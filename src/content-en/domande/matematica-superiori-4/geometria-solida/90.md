The surface area of one sphere is $4$ times that of another sphere. How many times as large is its volume?

- $4$
- $8$
- $16$
- $2$

## Explanation

The ratio of the surface areas of two spheres is the square of the ratio of their radii, so $k^2 = 4$ and the ratio of the radii is $k = 2$. The ratio of the volumes is the cube of the ratio of the radii, so the volume is $k^3 = 8$ times as large. The value $16$ comes from squaring the ratio of the areas.
