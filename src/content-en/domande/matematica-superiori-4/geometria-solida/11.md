A conical frustum has base radii $4$ and $1$ and height $4$. What is its volume?

- $84\pi$
- $28\pi$
- $\dfrac{68}{3}\,\pi$
- $34\pi$

## Explanation

The volume of a conical frustum with radii $R$ and $r$ and height $h$ is $\dfrac{\pi h}{3}\,(R^2 + Rr + r^2)$. With $R = 4$, $r = 1$, and $h = 4$, we have $R^2 + Rr + r^2 = 16 + 4 + 1 = 21$. Therefore
$$
V = \frac{4\pi}{3} \cdot 21 = 28\pi .
$$
The value $\dfrac{68}{3}\,\pi$ comes from omitting the term $Rr$.
