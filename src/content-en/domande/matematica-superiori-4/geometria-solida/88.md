What is the height of a regular tetrahedron with edge length $3$?

- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{6}$
- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}$

## Explanation

The foot of the altitude is the center of the base, whose distance from each vertex of the base is two-thirds of the height of the equilateral triangle. The height of the face is $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$, so this distance is $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} = \sqrt{3}$. The altitude of the tetrahedron, this segment, and a lateral edge form a right triangle with hypotenuse $3$. Therefore the height is $\sqrt{9 - 3} = \sqrt{6}$.
