Four noncoplanar vertices form a tetrahedron. If any of its four faces is chosen as the base, which quantity must stay fixed?

- The altitude alone.
- The base area alone.
- The base area times its corresponding altitude.
- The base perimeter alone.

## Explanation

The tetrahedron's volume does not depend on the chosen face. In every case $V=\dfrac{Bh}{3}$ and the product $Bh$ is always three times that same volume.
