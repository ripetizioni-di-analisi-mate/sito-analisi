An equilateral cylinder has base radius $3$. What is its total surface area?

- $36\pi$
- $45\pi$
- $54\pi$
- $72\pi$

## Explanation

The cylinder is equilateral, so its height is twice its radius, or $h = 6$. The total surface area is $A_t = 2\pi r\,(h + r)$, so
$$
A_t = 2\pi \cdot 3 \cdot (6 + 3) = 54\pi .
$$
The value $36\pi$ is the lateral surface area alone, and $45\pi$ comes from adding only one base.
