A convex polyhedron has $10$ faces and $16$ vertices. How many edges does it have?

- $28$
- $24$
- $26$
- $8$

## Explanation

By Euler's formula, $F + V - S = 2$ holds for every convex polyhedron. With $F = 10$ and $V = 16$, we have $26 - S = 2$, so $S = 24$. An octagonal prism has exactly $10$ faces, $16$ vertices, and $24$ edges.
