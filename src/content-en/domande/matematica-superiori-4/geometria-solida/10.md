A cone has base radius $5$ and volume $100\pi$. What is its total surface area?

- $90\pi$
- $65\pi$
- $85\pi$
- $155\pi$

## Explanation

The volume formula $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\pi \cdot 25 \cdot h = 100\pi$ gives $h = 12$. The slant height is $a = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = 13$. Therefore the total surface area is
$$
A_t = \pi r a + \pi r^2 = 65\pi + 25\pi = 90\pi .
$$
The value $85\pi$ comes from using the height in place of the slant height in the lateral surface area formula.
