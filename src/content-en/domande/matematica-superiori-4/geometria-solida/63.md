A right cone of height $h$ and radius $r$ has volume $V$. Another cone must have the same volume and radius $2r$. What height does it need?

- $\dfrac{h}{2}$
- $2h$
- $\dfrac{h}{4}$
- $4h$

## Explanation

Volume is proportional to radius squared and to height. Doubling the radius quadruples the base area, so the height must be reduced to a quarter.
