A conical frustum has base radii $3$ and $1$ and height $6$. What is its volume?

- $20\pi$
- $78\pi$
- $26\pi$
- $60\pi$

## Explanation

The volume of a conical frustum is $\dfrac{\pi h}{3}\,(R^2 + Rr + r^2)$. With $R = 3$, $r = 1$, and $h = 6$, we have $R^2 + Rr + r^2 = 9 + 3 + 1 = 13$. Therefore $V = 2\pi \cdot 13 = 26\pi$. The value $20\pi$ comes from omitting the term $Rr$, and $78\pi$ comes from omitting the factor $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
