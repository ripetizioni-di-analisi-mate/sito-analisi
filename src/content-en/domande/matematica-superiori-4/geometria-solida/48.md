A pyramidal frustum has bases of areas $36$ and $9$ and height $6$. What is its volume?

- $90$
- $270$
- $126$
- $378$

## Explanation

The volume of a pyramidal frustum is $\dfrac{h}{3}\left( B + B' + \sqrt{B B'} \right)$. Here $\sqrt{B B'} = \sqrt{324} = 18$, so $B + B' + \sqrt{B B'} = 36 + 9 + 18 = 63$. Therefore $V = 2 \cdot 63 = 126$. The value $90$ comes from omitting the term $\sqrt{B B'}$.
