A sphere of radius $5$ has an inscribed cylinder of base radius $3$. What is the volume of the cylinder?

- $36\pi$
- $24\pi$
- $72\pi$
- $90\pi$

## Explanation

In the axial section, the cylinder forms a rectangle inscribed in a great circle, and its diagonal is a diameter of the sphere. Half the height, the base radius, and the radius of the sphere form a right triangle, so $\dfrac{h}{2} = \sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$ and $h = 8$. Therefore $V = \pi \cdot 9 \cdot 8 = 72\pi$. The value $36\pi$ comes from taking $4$ as the full height.
