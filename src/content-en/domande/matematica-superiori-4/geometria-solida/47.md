The frustum of a regular pyramid has square bases of side lengths $8$ and $2$, and its height is $4$. What is its lateral surface area?

- $200$
- $40\sqrt{13}$
- $20\sqrt{34}$
- $100$

## Explanation

Cut the frustum with the plane that passes through the centers of the bases and is perpendicular to two opposite sides. The cross section is an isosceles trapezoid of height $4$ with bases $8$ and $2$, and its legs are slant heights of the frustum. The difference between the half-lengths of the bases is $4 - 1 = 3$, so the slant height is $a = \sqrt{16 + 9} = 5$. The semiperimeters of the bases are $16$ and $4$. Therefore $A_l = (16 + 4) \cdot 5 = 100$.
