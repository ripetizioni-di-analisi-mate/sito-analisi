A regular pyramid has a hexagonal base of side length $4$, and its slant height is $9$. What is its lateral surface area?

- $216$
- $54$
- $72$
- $108$

## Explanation

The semiperimeter of the base is $p = \dfrac{6 \cdot 4}{2} = 12$. The lateral surface area of the right pyramid is $A_l = p \cdot a$ because the six faces are triangles of base $4$ and height $9$. Thus $A_l = 12 \cdot 9 = 108$. The value $216$ comes from multiplying the full perimeter by the slant height.
