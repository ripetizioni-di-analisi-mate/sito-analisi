A regular pyramid has a square base of side length $4$, and each lateral edge has length $6$. What is its height?

- $2\sqrt{7}$
- $4\sqrt{2}$
- $2\sqrt{11}$
- $2\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The foot $H$ of the altitude is the center of the square, whose distance from each vertex is half the diagonal, or $2\sqrt{2}$. The altitude $VH$, segment $HA$, and lateral edge $VA$ form a right triangle with a right angle at $H$. Thus $VH^2 = 36 - 8 = 28$ and $VH = 2\sqrt{7}$. The value $4\sqrt{2}$ comes from using half the side length in place of half the diagonal.
