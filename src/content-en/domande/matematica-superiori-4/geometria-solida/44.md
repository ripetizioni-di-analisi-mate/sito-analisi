A tetrahedron has volume $24$. A median divides one triangular face in two. What are the volumes of the two tetrahedra on these half-faces with the same opposite apex?

- $12$ and $12$
- $8$ and $16$
- $6$ and $18$
- They depend on the shape of the face.

## Explanation

The bases have equal area since the median halves the triangular face. The heights agree, so the volumes are equal and each is half the total.
