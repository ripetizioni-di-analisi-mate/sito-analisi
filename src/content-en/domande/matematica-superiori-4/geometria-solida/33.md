A prism has two heptagonal bases. How many faces $F$, edges $S$, and vertices $V$ does it have?

- $F = 9$, $S = 21$, $V = 14$
- $F = 9$, $S = 14$, $V = 21$
- $F = 7$, $S = 21$, $V = 14$
- $F = 9$, $S = 18$, $V = 14$

## Explanation

The prism has $2$ bases and one lateral face for each side of the base, so $F = 2 + 7 = 9$. Each base has $7$ vertices, so $V = 14$. The edges consist of the $7$ sides of each base and the $7$ lateral edges, so $S = 21$. Euler's formula holds because $9 + 14 - 21 = 2$.
