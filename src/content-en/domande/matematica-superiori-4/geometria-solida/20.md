A thread joins opposite vertices of a unit cube while remaining on its surface. How long is the shortest path?

- $\sqrt{5}$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Unfolding two adjacent faces gives a rectangle of sides one and two. Its shortest path is the diagonal of length $\sqrt{1^2+2^2}=\sqrt{5}$. Equivalent unfoldings give the same distance.
