A cube has a space diagonal of length $9$. What is its edge length?

- $3\sqrt{3}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{9\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $9\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The space diagonal of a cube of edge length $l$ has length $l\sqrt{3}$ by the formula for the space diagonal of a rectangular prism. Thus $l\sqrt{3} = 9$ and
$$
l = \frac{9}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{9\sqrt{3}}{3} = 3\sqrt{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{9\sqrt{2}}{2}$ comes from confusing the space diagonal of the cube with a face diagonal.
