A cone has base radius $6$ and height $8$. What is the radius of the sphere inscribed in the cone?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The axial section is an isosceles triangle with base $12$ and legs equal to the slant height, $\sqrt{64 + 36} = 10$. The inscribed sphere appears in the cross section as the disk inscribed in the triangle. The triangle has area $\dfrac{12 \cdot 8}{2} = 48$ and semiperimeter $16$, and the radius of the inscribed disk is the area divided by the semiperimeter. Therefore the radius of the sphere is $\dfrac{48}{16} = 3$.
