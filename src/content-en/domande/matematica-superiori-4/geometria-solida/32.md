In a regular tetrahedron of edge length $6$, consider the dihedral angle between two faces. What is the cosine of this angle?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Let $M$ be the midpoint of the edge shared by the two faces. The altitudes of the two faces from the opposite vertices meet the edge at $M$ and are perpendicular to it, so they form the normal section of the dihedral angle. These altitudes have length $3\sqrt{3}$, and the distance between their other endpoints is $6$ because they are two vertices of the tetrahedron. By the law of cosines,
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{27 + 27 - 36}{2 \cdot 27} = \frac{18}{54} = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
