A regular icosahedron has $20$ triangular faces, and $5$ faces meet at each vertex. How many vertices does it have?

- $12$
- $60$
- $30$
- $10$

## Explanation

The $20$ faces have a total of $20 \cdot 3 = 60$ vertices when counted one face at a time. Each vertex of the polyhedron is counted $5$ times, once for each face that meets there. Thus the number of vertices is $V = \dfrac{60}{5} = 12$. Euler's formula holds with $S = 30$ because $20 + 12 - 30 = 2$.
