Two tetrahedra share one triangular face, with their opposite vertices on opposite sides of its plane. How many triangular faces remain on the outside if adjacent outer faces are not coplanar?

- $6$
- $7$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

Both glued faces become internal. Three faces from each tetrahedron remain, giving six in all.
