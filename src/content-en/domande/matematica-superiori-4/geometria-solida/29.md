A regular pyramid has a square base of side length $8$, and its height is $4$. What is the measure of the dihedral angle between the base and a lateral face?

- $\arctan\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
- $45°$
- $\arctan 2$
- $60°$

## Explanation

Let $H$ be the center of the base and $M$ the midpoint of a base edge, so that $HM = 4$. Segment $HM$ is perpendicular to the edge, and by the **three perpendiculars theorem**, the slant height $VM$ is also perpendicular to the edge. Thus angle $\widehat{VMH}$ is the normal section of the dihedral angle. In triangle $VHM$, with a right angle at $H$, we have $\tan\widehat{VMH} = \dfrac{4}{4} = 1$, so the dihedral angle measures $45°$. The value $\arctan\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ gives the inclination of a lateral edge to the base.
