A right cone of radius $3$ and height $4$ is capped with a hemisphere of the same radius, joined along the base disk. What is the **external** surface area?

- $42\pi$
- $51\pi$
- $33\pi$
- $27\pi$

## Explanation

The cone's slant height is $5$ and its lateral area is $15\pi$. The curved part of the hemisphere adds $2\pi \cdot 3^2=18\pi$. The shared disk is internal.
