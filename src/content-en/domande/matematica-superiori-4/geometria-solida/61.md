A cone has base radius $5$ and lateral surface area $65\pi$. What is its height?

- $13$
- $\sqrt{194}$
- $12$
- $8$

## Explanation

From the lateral surface area $\pi r a = 65\pi$ with $r = 5$, we get the slant height $a = 13$. The height, radius, and slant height form a right triangle whose hypotenuse is the slant height. Thus $h = \sqrt{169 - 25} = 12$. The value $13$ is the slant height.
