In cube $ABCDEFGH$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, how are lines $AE$ and $BD$ positioned relative to each other?

- Intersecting
- Parallel
- Skew
- Both lie in the plane of the base

## Explanation

Line $BD$ lies in the plane of the base and does not pass through $A$. Line $AE$ meets the plane of the base only at $A$, so the two lines have no points in common. If they lay in the same plane, that plane would contain $A$, $B$, and $D$, so it would be the plane of the base. However, point $E$ does not lie in the plane of the base. Therefore lines $AE$ and $BD$ are **skew**.
