Two lines in space do not meet. What additional fact guarantees that they are parallel?

- Each meets a third line.
- Each is parallel to one of two parallel planes.
- They lie in the same plane.
- There is a segment joining a point on one to a point on the other.

## Explanation

Distinct disjoint lines in space may be parallel or skew. Coplanarity rules out the second case.
