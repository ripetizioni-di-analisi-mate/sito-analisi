Where can the centres of spheres through two distinct points $A$ and $B$ lie?

- Only on the segment $AB$.
- On the line through $A$ and $B$, excluding both.
- On one circle of radius $AB$.
- On the plane perpendicular to $AB$ through its midpoint.

## Explanation

A centre must be equally distant from the two points. In space this locus is the perpendicular bisector plane, and the common distance at each of its points defines a sphere.
