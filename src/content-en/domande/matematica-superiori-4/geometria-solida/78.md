A cube of edge length $2\sqrt{3}$ is inscribed in a sphere. What is the radius of the sphere?

- $\sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{6}$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The vertices of the cube lie on the surface of the sphere, and the center of the sphere is the center of the cube. Two opposite vertices are the endpoints of a space diagonal of the cube, which is therefore a diameter of the sphere. The diagonal has length $2\sqrt{3} \cdot \sqrt{3} = 6$, so the radius is $3$. The value $\sqrt{3}$ is the radius of the inscribed sphere, half the edge length, and $\sqrt{6}$ is half a face diagonal.
