In cube $ABCDEFGH$ of edge length $6$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, what is the volume of tetrahedron $ABDE$?

- $108$
- $72$
- $12$
- $36$

## Explanation

Tetrahedron $ABDE$ can be viewed as a pyramid with triangular base $ABD$ and apex $E$. The base is half of the square face, so its area is $\dfrac{36}{2} = 18$. The height is $AE = 6$ because $AE$ is perpendicular to the plane of the base. Therefore $V = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot 18 \cdot 6 = 36$. The value $108$ comes from omitting the factor $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
