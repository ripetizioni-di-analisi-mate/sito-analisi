A convex polyhedron has $12$ edges and $6$ vertices. How many faces does it have?

- $4$
- $16$
- $20$
- $8$

## Explanation

By Euler's formula, $F + V - S = 2$, so $F = 2 - V + S = 2 - 6 + 12 = 8$. The regular octahedron has exactly $8$ faces, $6$ vertices, and $12$ edges. The value $16$ comes from using the wrong sign for the term $2$.
