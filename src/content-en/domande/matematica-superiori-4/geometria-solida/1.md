A cube has total surface area $150\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the length of its space diagonal?

- $5\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\sqrt{5}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The surface of a cube consists of six congruent squares of side length $l$, so $6l^2 = 150$ and $l^2 = 25$. Therefore the edge length is $l = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The space diagonal of a cube of edge length $l$ is $d = \sqrt{l^2 + l^2 + l^2} = l\sqrt{3}$ by the formula for the space diagonal of a rectangular prism. Thus the diagonal has length $5\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $5\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the length of a face diagonal.
