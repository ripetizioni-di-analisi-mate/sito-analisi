A regular polyhedron has equilateral triangles as its faces, and $4$ faces meet at each vertex. Which polyhedron is it?

- Octahedron
- Tetrahedron
- Icosahedron
- Cube

## Explanation

The regular polyhedra with triangular faces are the tetrahedron, octahedron, and icosahedron, with $3$, $4$, and $5$ faces meeting at each vertex, respectively. With $4$ faces at a vertex, the sum of the angles is $4 \cdot 60° = 240°$, less than $360°$. The polyhedron is therefore the **octahedron**, which has $8$ faces, $6$ vertices, and $12$ edges. A cube has square faces.
