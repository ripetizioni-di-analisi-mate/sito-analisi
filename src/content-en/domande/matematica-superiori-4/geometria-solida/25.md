In cube $ABCDEFGH$ of edge length $4$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, what is the distance from vertex $E$ to line $BC$?

- $4$
- $4\sqrt{2}$
- $4\sqrt{3}$
- $2\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

Segment $EA$ is perpendicular to the plane of the base, and segment $AB$ is perpendicular to line $BC$. By the **three perpendiculars theorem**, $EB$ is also perpendicular to $BC$, so the distance from $E$ to line $BC$ is $EB$. Triangle $EAB$ has a right angle at $A$, so
$$
EB = \sqrt{4^2 + 4^2} = 4\sqrt{2} .
$$
