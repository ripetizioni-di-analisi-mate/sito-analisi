A pyramid of height $15$ has a base of area $75$. A plane parallel to the base cuts the pyramid at a distance of $6$ from the base. What is the area of the cross section?

- $12$
- $45$
- $30$
- $27$

## Explanation

The plane is at a distance of $6$ from the base, so its distance from the apex is $15 - 6 = 9$. The cross section is similar to the base with scale factor $\dfrac{9}{15} = \dfrac{3}{5}$, and the ratio of the areas is the square of this factor. Therefore
$$
B' = 75 \cdot \left( \frac{3}{5} \right)^2 = 75 \cdot \frac{9}{25} = 27 .
$$
The value $12$ comes from measuring the distance from the base instead of from the apex.
