Two congruent cubes of edge $a$ are glued together along an entire face. By how much does the external surface area decrease compared with the sum of the original areas?

- $2a^2$
- $a^2$
- $4a^2$
- $0$

## Explanation

Both touching faces become internal and no longer contribute to surface area. Each has area $a^2$, while the volume remains the sum of the two original volumes.
