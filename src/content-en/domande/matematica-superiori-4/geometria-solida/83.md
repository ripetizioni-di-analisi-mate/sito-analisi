A rectangular prism with dimensions $1$, $4$, and $8$ is inscribed in a sphere. What is the radius of the sphere?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{13}{2}$
- $4$

## Explanation

All eight vertices lie on the surface of the sphere, and two opposite vertices are the endpoints of a space diagonal of the prism, which is a diameter of the sphere. The diagonal has length $\sqrt{1 + 16 + 64} = \sqrt{81} = 9$. Therefore the radius of the sphere is $\dfrac{9}{2}$. The value $9$ is the diameter.
