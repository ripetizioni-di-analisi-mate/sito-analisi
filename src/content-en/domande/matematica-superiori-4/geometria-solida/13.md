A cone is inscribed in a sphere of radius $13$, the radius of its base is $12$, and the center of the sphere lies inside the cone. What is the volume of the cone?

- $2592\pi$
- $864\pi$
- $384\pi$
- $1014\pi$

## Explanation

In the axial section, the center $O$ of the sphere lies on the altitude of the cone, and a point on the rim of the base is at a distance of $13$ from $O$. The distance from $O$ to the center $K$ of the base is therefore $\sqrt{13^2 - 12^2} = 5$. The center lies inside the cone, so the height is $h = 13 + 5 = 18$. The volume is
$$
V = \frac{1}{3}\,\pi \cdot 144 \cdot 18 = 864\pi .
$$
The value $384\pi$ corresponds to a height of $13 - 5 = 8$, that is, to the cone for which the center of the sphere lies outside the cone.
