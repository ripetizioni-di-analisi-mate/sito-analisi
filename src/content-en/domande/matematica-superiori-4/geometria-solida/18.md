In cube $ABCDEFGH$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, how is line $EG$ positioned relative to the plane of base $ABCD$?

- Intersects the plane at one point
- Lies in the plane
- Perpendicular to the plane
- Parallel to the plane

## Explanation

Quadrilateral $ACGE$ is a rectangle, so line $EG$ is parallel to line $AC$. Line $AC$ runs along a base diagonal and lies in the plane of the base. Line $EG$ does not lie in the plane of the base because $E$ does not lie in that plane. A line outside a plane and parallel to a line in that plane is **parallel to the plane**.
