Do three distinct points always determine a unique plane?

- Yes, even when they lie on one line.
- Only when all three distances are equal.
- Only when one distance is twice another.
- Yes, only if they are not collinear.

## Explanation

A line and a point outside it determine a unique plane. If the points are collinear, every plane containing their common line contains all three.
