Which of these triples giving the numbers of faces $F$, vertices $V$, and edges $S$ could describe a convex polyhedron?

- $F = 7$, $V = 10$, $S = 15$
- $F = 6$, $V = 6$, $S = 12$
- $F = 8$, $V = 8$, $S = 12$
- $F = 5$, $V = 6$, $S = 10$

## Explanation

For every convex polyhedron, **Euler's formula** gives $F + V - S = 2$. For the triple $F = 7$, $V = 10$, $S = 15$, we have $7 + 10 - 15 = 2$, and a pentagonal prism has exactly these counts. The other triples give $0$, $4$, and $1$, so they do not satisfy the formula and none can describe a convex polyhedron.
