A convex polyhedron has a quadrilateral face. Drawing one diagonal across that face lets it be counted as two triangular faces. How does $V-E+F$ change in the subdivided count?

- It does not change.
- It increases by one.
- It decreases by one.
- It increases by two.

## Explanation

The subdivision adds no vertices, adds one edge, and increases the number of faces by one. The changes $-1$ and $+1$ cancel in the expression.
