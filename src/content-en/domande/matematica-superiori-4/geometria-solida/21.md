Two oblique segments start at the same point above a plane and end on the plane. If their lengths are equal, how do their orthogonal projection lengths compare?

- One is twice the other.
- Both vanish.
- They depend only on the orientation chosen on the plane.
- They are equal.

## Explanation

The right triangles have equal hypotenuses and the same height above the plane. Pythagoras therefore gives equal projected legs.
