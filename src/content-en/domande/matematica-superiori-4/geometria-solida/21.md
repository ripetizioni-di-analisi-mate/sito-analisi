Point $P$ is at a distance of $15$ from plane $\alpha$, and the distance from $P$ to line $r$ in $\alpha$ is $17$. What is the distance from line $r$ to the orthogonal projection $H$ of $P$ onto $\alpha$?

- $2$
- $\sqrt{514}$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

Let $A$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $H$ to line $r$. By the **three perpendiculars theorem**, segment $PA$ is perpendicular to $r$, so $PA = 17$ is the distance from $P$ to $r$. Triangle $PHA$ has a right angle at $H$ because $PH$ is perpendicular to the plane. Therefore
$$
HA = \sqrt{17^2 - 15^2} = \sqrt{64} = 8 .
$$
