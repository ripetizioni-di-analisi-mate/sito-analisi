A right triangle with legs $6$ and $8$ makes one full turn about the leg of length $8$. What is the lateral surface area of the resulting cone?

- $60\pi$
- $80\pi$
- $96\pi$
- $48\pi$

## Explanation

The leg about which the triangle rotates is the height of the cone, so $h = 8$, and the other leg is the base radius, $r = 6$. The hypotenuse sweeps out the lateral surface and is the slant height, $a = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$. Therefore $A_l = \pi r a = 60\pi$. The value $48\pi$ comes from using the height in place of the slant height.
