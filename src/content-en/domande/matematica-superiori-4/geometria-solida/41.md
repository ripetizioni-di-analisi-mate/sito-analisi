A regular pyramid has a square base of side length $10$, and its height is $12$. What is its slant height?

- $\sqrt{194}$
- $13$
- $17$
- $\sqrt{119}$

## Explanation

The radius of the circle inscribed in the square is half its side length, or $5$. By the slant height formula for a right pyramid, $a = \sqrt{h^2 + r^2}$, so $a = \sqrt{144 + 25} = 13$. The value $\sqrt{194}$ comes from using half the diagonal, $5\sqrt{2}$, and is the lateral edge length.
