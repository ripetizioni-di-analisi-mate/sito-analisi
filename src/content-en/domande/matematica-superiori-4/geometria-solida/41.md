A square-based pyramid has its apex off the base centre. Are the base perimeter and just one slant height enough to find lateral area?

- Yes, every pyramid has a common slant height.
- Generally no, because the four slant heights can differ.
- Yes, lateral area is perimeter squared.
- No, an oblique pyramid has no lateral area.

## Explanation

Add the areas of the lateral triangles. The formula $\dfrac{Pa}{2}$ assumes a common slant height $a$, which the given information does not guarantee.
