A pyramid has height $10$. How far from the apex is the plane parallel to the base that cuts the pyramid in a cross section with one-fourth the area of the base?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $5\sqrt{2}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$

## Explanation

At a distance of $x$ from the apex, the cross section has area $B \left( \dfrac{x}{10} \right)^2$. The condition is $\left( \dfrac{x}{10} \right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{4}$, so $\dfrac{x}{10} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = 5$. The value $\dfrac{5}{2}$ comes from taking the ratio of the lengths to be equal to the ratio of the areas.
