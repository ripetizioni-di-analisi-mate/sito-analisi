A pyramid is cut halfway up by a plane parallel to its base. What fraction of the original volume is in the frustum?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The small top pyramid is similar with linear factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Its volume is $\dfrac{1}{8}$ of the total and the frustum is the remainder.
