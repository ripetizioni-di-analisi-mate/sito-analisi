Segment $PA$ has length $10$, with $A$ in plane $\alpha$, and makes an angle of $30°$ with the plane. How far is $P$ from $\alpha$?

- $5$
- $5\sqrt{3}$
- $20$
- $\dfrac{10\sqrt{3}}{3}$

## Explanation

Let $H$ be the orthogonal projection of $P$ onto $\alpha$. The angle between $PA$ and the plane is angle $\theta$ at $A$ in triangle $PHA$, which has a right angle at $H$. Leg $PH$ is opposite $\theta$, so $PH = PA \sin\theta = 10 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 5$. The value $5\sqrt{3}$ is the length of the projection $HA = PA\cos\theta$.
