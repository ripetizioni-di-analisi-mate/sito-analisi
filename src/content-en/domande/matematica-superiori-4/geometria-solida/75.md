A right triangle with legs $3$ and $4$ makes one full turn about its hypotenuse. What solid does it generate?

- A cone of radius $3$ and height $4$
- A conical frustum
- A cylinder with a cone removed
- Two cones joined base to base

## Explanation

The altitude to the hypotenuse divides the triangle into two right triangles, each with one leg on the hypotenuse. When rotated about that leg, each triangle generates a cone. The shared base of the cones is the disk swept out by the altitude to the hypotenuse, of radius $\dfrac{3 \cdot 4}{5} = \dfrac{12}{5}$. A cone of radius $3$ and height $4$ is obtained by rotating the triangle about the leg of length $4$ instead.
