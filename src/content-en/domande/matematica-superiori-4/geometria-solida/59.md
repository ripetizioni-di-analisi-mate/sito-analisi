A cone has slant height $10$ and base radius $6$. What is its volume?

- $120\pi$
- $96\pi$
- $288\pi$
- $128\pi$

## Explanation

The height, radius, and slant height form a right triangle, so $h = \sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$. The volume of a cone is one-third of the base area times the height. Therefore
$$
V = \frac{1}{3}\,\pi \cdot 36 \cdot 8 = 96\pi .
$$
The value $120\pi$ comes from using the slant height in place of the height.
