A solid cone is cut by a plane containing its axis. Which calculation gives the area of the cut if the radius is $r$ and the height is $h$?

- $\pi rh$
- $rh$
- $rh/2$
- $\pi r^2$

## Explanation

The axial section is an isosceles triangle with base equal to the diameter $2r$ and height $h$. Its area is $(2r)h/2=rh$.
