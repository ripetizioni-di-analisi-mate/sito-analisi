A right prism has a regular hexagonal base. What is the measure of the dihedral angle between two adjacent lateral faces?

- $60°$
- $120°$
- $90°$
- $135°$

## Explanation

The edge shared by the two faces is a lateral edge, which in a right prism is perpendicular to the plane of the base. The plane of the base is therefore perpendicular to the edge, and the normal section of the dihedral angle is the angle between two consecutive sides of the base. Thus the dihedral angle has the same measure as an interior angle of the regular hexagon, namely $\dfrac{4 \cdot 180°}{6} = 120°$. The value $60°$ is the central angle of the hexagon.
