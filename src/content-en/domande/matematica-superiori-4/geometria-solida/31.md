In cube $ABCDEFGH$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, what is the normal section of the dihedral angle along edge $BC$ between faces $ABCD$ and $BCGF$?

- $\widehat{ABF}$
- $\widehat{CBF}$
- $\widehat{ABC}$
- $\widehat{DBG}$

## Explanation

The normal section is the angle formed by two rays, one in each face, perpendicular to the edge at the same point. At point $B$, ray $BA$ lies in the base and is perpendicular to $BC$, and ray $BF$ lies in face $BCGF$ and is perpendicular to $BC$. Therefore the normal section is $\widehat{ABF}$, a right angle. Angles $\widehat{CBF}$ and $\widehat{ABC}$ each have a side along the edge itself.
