A cylinder of height $6$ is inscribed in a sphere of radius $5$. What is the base radius of the cylinder?

- $4$
- $\sqrt{19}$
- $\sqrt{34}$
- $3$

## Explanation

In the axial section, the cylinder forms a rectangle inscribed in a great circle of radius $5$. The center of the sphere is the center of the rectangle, so half the height, the base radius, and the radius of the sphere form a right triangle. Therefore the base radius is $\sqrt{25 - 3^2} = 4$. The value $\sqrt{19}$ comes from subtracting the entire height instead of the square of half the height.
