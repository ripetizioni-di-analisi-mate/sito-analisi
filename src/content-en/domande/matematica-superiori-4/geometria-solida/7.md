## Explanation

The projection of the diagonal onto the plane of the base is the base diagonal, whose length is $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$. The space diagonal of the prism, the base diagonal, and the lateral edge form a right triangle whose vertical leg has length $12$. The angle $\theta$ with the plane of the base is opposite the lateral edge, so
$$
\tan\theta = \frac{12}{5}, \qquad \theta = \arctan\frac{12}{5} \approx 67.4° .
$$
