In cube $ABCDEFGH$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, how are lines $AD$ and $FG$ positioned relative to each other?

- Skew
- Intersecting
- Parallel
- Coincident

## Explanation

Rectangle $ADGF$ is a cross section of the cube, as is rectangle $ACGE$, so lines $AD$ and $FG$ lie in the same plane. Line $AD$ lies in the plane of the base and line $FG$ lies in the plane of the top face. These two planes are parallel and have no points in common, so the two lines have no points in common. Two coplanar lines with no points in common are **parallel**.
