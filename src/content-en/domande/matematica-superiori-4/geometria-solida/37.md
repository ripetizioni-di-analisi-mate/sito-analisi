What is the length of the space diagonal of a rectangular prism with dimensions $2$, $3$, and $6$?

- $11$
- $49$
- $2\sqrt{10}$
- $7$

## Explanation

The space diagonal of a rectangular prism with dimensions $a$, $b$, and $c$ is $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}$, because the Pythagorean theorem applies twice. Here $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = 4 + 9 + 36 = 49$, so the diagonal has length $7$. The value $11$ is the sum of the dimensions.
