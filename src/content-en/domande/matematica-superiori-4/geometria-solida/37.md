Only the height of a rectangular box is doubled, with its base unchanged. Which contribution to total surface area stays fixed?

- The area of all vertical faces.
- The total area of the six faces.
- No area stays fixed.
- The combined area of the two bases.

## Explanation

Total surface area is $2B+Ph$, with $B$ the base area and $P$ its perimeter. Changing $h$ affects only the second term.
