## Explanation

Let $A$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $H$ to line $r$, so that $HA = 5$. By the **three perpendiculars theorem**, $PA$ is also perpendicular to $r$, so $PA$ is the distance from $P$ to $r$. Triangle $PHA$ has a right angle at $H$ because $PH$ is perpendicular to the plane. Therefore
$$
PA = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{169} = 13 .
$$
