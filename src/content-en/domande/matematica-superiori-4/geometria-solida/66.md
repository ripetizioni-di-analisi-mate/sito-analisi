A sphere has center $O$ and radius $4$, and plane $\alpha$ is at a distance of $5$ from $O$. What do the sphere and the plane have in common?

- A disk of radius $3$
- A single point
- No points
- A disk of radius $\sqrt{41}$

## Explanation

Every point in the plane is at least as far from $O$ as the foot of the perpendicular from $O$ to $\alpha$, that is, at least $5$. The sphere consists of the points whose distance from $O$ is at most $4$. Since $5 > 4$, no point in the plane belongs to the sphere, and the plane is **exterior** to it. A disk of radius $3$ would be the cross section for $R = 5$ and $d = 4$.
