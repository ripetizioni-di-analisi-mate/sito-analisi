Which of these solids is a regular polyhedron?

- The right prism with equilateral triangular bases and square lateral faces
- The square pyramid with equilateral triangular lateral faces
- The tetrahedron with four congruent equilateral triangular faces
- The rectangular prism with dimensions $1$, $1$, and $2$

## Explanation

A convex polyhedron is regular if its faces are congruent regular polygons and the same number of faces meet at each vertex. The prism with square lateral faces and the square pyramid each have two different types of faces, so they are not regular. The rectangular prism with dimensions $1$, $1$, and $2$ has rectangular faces that are not squares. The tetrahedron with four congruent equilateral triangles has three faces meeting at each vertex, so it is **regular**.
