A cone has base radius $6$ and height $9$. A cylinder of radius $2$ is inscribed in the cone, with one base on the base of the cone. What is the height of the cylinder?

- $3$
- $7$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

In the axial section, the rim of the upper base of the cylinder lies on the lateral surface of the cone. The radius of a cross section of the cone is proportional to its distance from the apex, so the radius is $2$ at a distance of $9 \cdot \dfrac{2}{6} = 3$ from the apex. The height of the cylinder is the distance from this cross section to the base, or $9 - 3 = 6$. The value $3$ is the distance from the apex.
