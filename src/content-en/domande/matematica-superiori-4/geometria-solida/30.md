A regular pyramid has a square base of side length $6$, and the dihedral angle between the base and each lateral face measures $60°$. What is the height of the pyramid?

- $\sqrt{3}$
- $3\sqrt{3}$
- $6$
- $3\sqrt{6}$

## Explanation

Let $H$ be the center of the base and $M$ the midpoint of a base edge, so that $HM = 3$. By the three perpendiculars theorem, angle $\widehat{VMH}$ is the normal section of the dihedral angle, so it measures $60°$. In triangle $VHM$, with a right angle at $H$, we have $VH = HM \tan 60° = 3\sqrt{3}$. The value $\sqrt{3}$ comes from dividing by $\tan 60°$ instead of multiplying.
