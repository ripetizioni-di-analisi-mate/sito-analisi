An open book forms two faces of a dihedral angle. Where must one measure to obtain the dihedral angle?

- Between any two segments on the pages.
- In a cross-section perpendicular to the spine, between the traces of the two faces.
- Along the spine between two parts of it.
- Between the two vertices farthest from the spine.

## Explanation

A dihedral angle is measured in its normal cross-section. An oblique cross-section can give a different angle.
