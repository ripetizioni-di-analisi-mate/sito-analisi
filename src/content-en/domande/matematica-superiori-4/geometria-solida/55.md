A rectangle with sides $4$ and $7$ makes one full turn about its side of length $4$. What is the volume of the resulting cylinder?

- $196\pi$
- $112\pi$
- $56\pi$
- $\dfrac{196}{3}\,\pi$

## Explanation

The side about which the rectangle rotates is the axis and becomes the height, so $h = 4$. The other side sweeps out the base, so the radius is $r = 7$. Therefore $V = \pi r^2 h = \pi \cdot 49 \cdot 4 = 196\pi$. The value $112\pi$ corresponds to rotation about the side of length $7$.
