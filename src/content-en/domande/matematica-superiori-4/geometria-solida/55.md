A disk of radius $3$ rotates around one of its diameters. What solid does it sweep out?

- A ball of radius $3$.
- A cylinder of radius $3$.
- A cone of height $6$.
- A ball of radius $6$.

## Explanation

Every disk point lies at most one radius from its centre. Rotation around the diameter reaches every spatial direction and fills the ball.
