Two distinct parallel planes cut a sphere in congruent nondegenerate circles. Where is the center of the sphere relative to the two planes?

- In either one of the planes.
- In both planes.
- Beyond both, at unequal distances.
- In the midplane equidistant from both.

## Explanation

For sphere radius $R$ and center-to-plane distance $d$, the section radius is $\sqrt{R^2-d^2}$. Equal radii imply equal distances. Two distinct parallel planes have a unique equidistant midplane.
