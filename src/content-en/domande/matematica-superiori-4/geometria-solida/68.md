A sphere has center $O$ and radius $5$. Which plane cuts it in a disk bounded by a great circle?

- Any plane at a distance of $5$ from $O$
- Any plane at a distance of $\dfrac{5}{2}$ from $O$
- Any plane at a distance of less than $5$ from $O$
- Any plane through $O$

## Explanation

A plane at a distance of $d$ from the center, with $0 \leq d < 5$, cuts the sphere in a disk of radius $\sqrt{25 - d^2}$. The radius equals $5$, its maximum value, only for $d = 0$, so the plane must pass through $O$. A plane at a distance of $5$ is tangent and has a single point in common with the sphere. At a distance of $\dfrac{5}{2}$, the cross section has radius $\dfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}$, which is less than $5$.
