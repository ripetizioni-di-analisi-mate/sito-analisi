An oblique prism has base area $12$, height $5$, and lateral edge length $13$. What is its volume?

- $156$
- $20$
- $130$
- $60$

## Explanation

Consider the oblique prism and a right prism with a base of equal area in the same plane and height $5$. Every plane parallel to the bases cuts each prism in a cross section congruent to its base. By **Cavalieri's principle**, the two prisms have equal volumes, so the volume is $V = B \cdot h = 12 \cdot 5 = 60$. The lateral edge is oblique to the bases, so it is longer than the height and does not appear in the volume formula. The value $156$ comes from multiplying the base area by the lateral edge length.
