A prism is deformed by sliding its layers sideways, preserving their heights and cross-sectional areas. Which principle guarantees unchanged volume?

- Euler's relation.
- The angle-bisector theorem.
- The triangle inequality.
- Cavalieri's principle.

## Explanation

Sections parallel to the bases have equal areas at every height. Cavalieri's principle therefore compares the volumes even when the horizontal positions of the sections change.
