A pyramid has a rectangular base with sides $4$ and $6$. Which statement is true?

- The pyramid is right if the foot of its altitude is the center of the rectangle
- The pyramid is regular if its lateral edges are congruent
- The pyramid cannot be right because no circle can be inscribed in the rectangle
- The pyramid is right if its height is $5$

## Explanation

A pyramid is right if a circle can be inscribed in its base and the foot of its altitude is the center of that circle. A circle inscribed in the rectangle would have to be tangent to two opposite sides a distance of $4$ apart and to two opposite sides a distance of $6$ apart, so its diameter would have to equal both $4$ and $6$. Therefore no circle can be inscribed in the rectangle of sides $4$ and $6$, and the pyramid **is not right** regardless of the position of its apex. If the foot of the altitude is the center of the rectangle, the lateral edges are congruent and the lateral faces have different heights.
