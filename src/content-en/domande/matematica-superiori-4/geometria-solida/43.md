A pyramid has a rectangular base of sides $4$ and $6$, and its apex lies on the perpendicular through the base center. What shape is the section by the plane through the apex and one base diagonal?

- A rectangle.
- A necessarily equilateral triangle.
- An isosceles triangle.
- A trapezium with unequal bases.

## Explanation

The section joins the endpoints of the diagonal to the apex. Both endpoints are equally far from the base center and hence, by Pythagoras, from the apex above it.
