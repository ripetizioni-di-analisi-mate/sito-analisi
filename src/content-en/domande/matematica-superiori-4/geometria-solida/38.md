A rectangular prism has a space diagonal of length $15$ and base dimensions $5$ and $10$. What is its height?

- $10$
- $5\sqrt{14}$
- $5\sqrt{5}$
- $100$

## Explanation

The formula for the diagonal gives $d^2 = a^2 + b^2 + c^2$, so $c^2 = 225 - 25 - 100 = 100$. Therefore the height is $c = 10$. The value $5\sqrt{14}$ comes from adding the squares of the base dimensions instead of subtracting them, and $5\sqrt{5}$ is the length of the base diagonal.
