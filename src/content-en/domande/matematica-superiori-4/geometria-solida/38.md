In a rectangular box three mutually adjacent faces have areas $6$, $10$ and $15$. What is the volume?

- $30$
- $31$
- $60$
- $900$

## Explanation

With edge lengths $a,b,c$, the product of the face areas is $(ab)(bc)(ca)=(abc)^2$. Take the square root of $6\cdot 10\cdot 15=900$.
