A sphere of radius $10$ is cut by a plane at a distance of $6$ from the center. What is the area of the cross section?

- $16\pi$
- $64\pi$
- $136\pi$
- $36\pi$

## Explanation

For a plane section of a sphere, the radius of the cross section is $r = \sqrt{R^2 - d^2}$. Here $r = \sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$, so the area of the cross section is $\pi \cdot 64 = 64\pi$. The value $16\pi$ comes from taking the difference $10 - 6 = 4$ as the radius.
