A cylindrical pipe has outer radius $5$, inner radius $3$ and length $10$. How much material does it contain in volume units?

- $40\pi$
- $250\pi$
- $160\pi$
- $90\pi$

## Explanation

The material cross-section is an annulus of area $\pi (5^2-3^2)=16\pi$. The cross-section is constant, so multiply by the length.
