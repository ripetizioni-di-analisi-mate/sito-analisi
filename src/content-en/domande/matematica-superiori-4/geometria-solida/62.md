A conical frustum has base radii $7$ and $4$ and height $4$. What is its lateral surface area?

- $110\pi$
- $44\pi$
- $55\pi$
- $15\pi$

## Explanation

The slant height of the frustum is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose legs are the height and the difference between the radii, so $a = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5$. The lateral surface area of a conical frustum is $\pi\,(R + r)\,a$. Therefore $A_l = \pi \cdot 11 \cdot 5 = 55\pi$. The value $44\pi$ comes from using the height in place of the slant height.
