A right prism of height $4$ has a right triangle with legs $5$ and $12$ as its base. What is its total surface area?

- $150$
- $120$
- $240$
- $180$

## Explanation

The hypotenuse of the base is $\sqrt{25 + 144} = 13$, so the base perimeter is $2p = 30$ and the lateral surface area is $A_l = 30 \cdot 4 = 120$. The base area is $B = \dfrac{5 \cdot 12}{2} = 30$. Therefore the total surface area is $A_t = 120 + 2 \cdot 30 = 180$. The value $150$ comes from counting only one base.
