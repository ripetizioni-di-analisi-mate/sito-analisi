How many edges does a pyramid with a hexagonal base have?

- $7$
- $12$
- $18$
- $6$

## Explanation

The edges of a pyramid consist of the sides of the base and the lateral edges that join the apex to the vertices of the base. The hexagonal base has $6$ sides and $6$ vertices, so the number of edges is $6 + 6 = 12$. The pyramid has $7$ faces and $7$ vertices, and Euler's formula holds because $7 + 7 - 12 = 2$.
