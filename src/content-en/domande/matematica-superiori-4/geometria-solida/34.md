If one corner of a cube is sliced off by a plane meeting only the three incident edges, how does the number of faces change?

- It goes from $6$ to $5$.
- It goes from $6$ to $7$.
- It goes from $6$ to $9$.
- It stays at $6$.

## Explanation

All original cube faces remain, although three lose a corner. The cutting plane adds one triangular face.
