The dimensions of a rectangular prism are proportional to $1$, $2$, and $2$, and its space diagonal has length $12$. What is its total surface area?

- $128$
- $256$
- $192$
- $320$

## Explanation

Write the dimensions as $k$, $2k$, and $2k$, where $k > 0$. The formula for the diagonal gives $\sqrt{k^2 + 4k^2 + 4k^2} = 3k = 12$, so $k = 4$ and the dimensions are $4$, $8$, and $8$. The faces form three pairs of congruent rectangles. Therefore
$$
A_t = 2\,(4 \cdot 8 + 4 \cdot 8 + 8 \cdot 8) = 2 \cdot 128 = 256 .
$$
