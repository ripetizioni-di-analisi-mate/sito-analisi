A regular pyramid has a square base of side length $10$, and its height is $12$. What is the radius of its inscribed sphere?

- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $\dfrac{10}{9}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}$

## Explanation

The slant height is $\sqrt{144 + 25} = 13$, so the total surface area is $A_t = 20 \cdot 13 + 100 = 360$, and the volume is $V = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot 100 \cdot 12 = 400$. The radius of a sphere inscribed in a polyhedron is $r = \dfrac{3V}{A_t}$. Therefore
$$
r = \frac{3 \cdot 400}{360} = \frac{10}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{10}{9}$ comes from omitting the factor $3$.
