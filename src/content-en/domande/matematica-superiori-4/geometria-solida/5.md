A regular tetrahedron has edge length $6$. Given that the foot of its altitude is the centroid of its base, what is the volume of the tetrahedron?

- $54\sqrt{2}$
- $18\sqrt{2}$
- $36\sqrt{2}$
- $18\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The base is an equilateral triangle of side length $6$, height $3\sqrt{3}$, and area $B = 9\sqrt{3}$. The distance from the centroid to a vertex is two-thirds of the height of the face, or $2\sqrt{3}$. The lateral edge, the altitude of the tetrahedron, and this segment form a right triangle, so the height is $h = \sqrt{36 - 12} = 2\sqrt{6}$. Therefore
$$
V = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 9\sqrt{3} \cdot 2\sqrt{6} = 6\sqrt{18} = 18\sqrt{2} .
$$
