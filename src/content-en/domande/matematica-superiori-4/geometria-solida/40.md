In cube $ABCDEFGH$, with base $ABCD$ and lateral edges $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, which pair of edges lies on skew lines?

- $AB$ and $GH$
- $AB$ and $DH$
- $AB$ and $BF$
- $AB$ and $DC$

## Explanation

Edges $AB$ and $GH$ lie in rectangle $ABGH$ and are parallel. Edges $AB$ and $DC$ are also parallel because they are opposite sides of the base. Edges $AB$ and $BF$ intersect at $B$. Line $DH$ meets the plane of the base only at $D$, which does not lie on line $AB$, so $AB$ and $DH$ have no points in common. A plane containing them both would contain $A$, $B$, and $D$, so it would be the plane of the base, which does not contain $H$. Therefore $AB$ and $DH$ are **skew**.
