A plane parallel to the base cuts an oblique prism halfway up. What is the ratio of cross-sectional area to base area?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- It depends on the inclination.

## Explanation

Cross-sections of a prism parallel to its base are translates of that base. They preserve its shape and area even when the lateral edges are oblique.
