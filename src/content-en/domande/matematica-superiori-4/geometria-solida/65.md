A plane moves parallel to itself through a ball, from the lower pole to the upper pole. How does the cross-sectional area vary?

- It grows throughout.
- It remains constant.
- It decreases throughout.
- It grows up to the centre, then decreases symmetrically.

## Explanation

At distance $d$ from the centre the section area is $\pi (R^2-d^2)$. As the plane approaches the centre, $d$ decreases, then grows again once the centre is passed.
