A plane cuts a sphere of radius $13$ in a disk of radius $5$. What is the distance from the plane to the center of the sphere?

- $8$
- $\sqrt{194}$
- $18$
- $12$

## Explanation

Let $O'$ be the projection of the center $O$ onto the plane, and let $P$ be a point on the boundary circle of the cross section. Triangle $OO'P$ has a right angle at $O'$, with hypotenuse $OP = 13$ and leg $O'P = 5$. Hence the distance is $OO' = \sqrt{169 - 25} = 12$. The value $8$ comes from subtracting the radii instead of their squares.
