- $x = 3$ and $y = 1$
- $x = 1$ and $y = 3$
- $x = 1$ and $y = -3$
- $x = 3$ and $y = 3$

## Explanation

The number $x + yi$ is obtained by dividing $5 + 5i$ by $2 - i$, which is different from $0$. Multiplying the numerator and denominator by the conjugate $2 + i$ gives
$$
x + yi = \frac{(5 + 5i)(2 + i)}{(2 - i)(2 + i)} = \frac{10 + 5i + 10i + 5i^2}{5} = \frac{5 + 15i}{5} = 1 + 3i .
$$
Since the two complex numbers are equal, their real parts are equal and their imaginary parts are equal, so $x = 1$ and $y = 3$.
