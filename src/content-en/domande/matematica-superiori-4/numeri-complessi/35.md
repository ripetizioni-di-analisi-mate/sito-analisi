What is the solution of the equation $(1 - i)\,z = 4$?

- $z = 2 - 2i$
- $z = 4 + 4i$
- $z = -2 - 2i$
- $z = 2 + 2i$

## Explanation

The coefficient $1 - i$ is different from $0$, so divide both sides by $1 - i$. To calculate the quotient, multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate $1 + i$. The denominator becomes $(1 - i)(1 + i) = 2$. Thus
$$
z = \frac{4}{1 - i} = \frac{4\,(1 + i)}{2} = 2 + 2i .
$$
