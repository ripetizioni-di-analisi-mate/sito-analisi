Which operation inverts the affine transformation $w=(1+i)z+2$?

- $z=\dfrac{w}{1+i}-2$
- $z=(w-2)(1+i)$
- $z=\dfrac{w+2}{1-i}$
- $z=\dfrac{w-2}{1+i}$

## Explanation

To recover the starting point, first undo the translation by subtracting $2$. Then undo the multiplication by dividing by the nonzero number $1+i$.
