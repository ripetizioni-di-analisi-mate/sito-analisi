What is the trigonometric form of the number $-5i$?

- $5\left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} \right)$
- $5\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$
- $25\left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} \right)$
- $5\,(\cos\pi + i\sin\pi)$

## Explanation

The number $-5i$ is represented by the point $(0, -5)$, which lies on the negative imaginary axis. Thus its modulus is $5$ and its argument is $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. Hence $-5i = 5\left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} \right)$, since $\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = 0$ and $\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = -1$.
