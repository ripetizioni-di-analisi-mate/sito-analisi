Which sector contains exactly the nonzero points with $0<\arg z<\pi/4$, taking arguments in $[0,2\pi)$?

- $0<\operatorname{Im}z<\operatorname{Re}z$
- $0<\operatorname{Re}z<\operatorname{Im}z$
- $\operatorname{Re}z<0<\operatorname{Im}z$
- $\operatorname{Re}z=\operatorname{Im}z>0$

## Explanation

The boundary rays are the positive real axis and the line $y=x$ in the first quadrant. Strictly between them the coordinates satisfy $0<y<x$.
