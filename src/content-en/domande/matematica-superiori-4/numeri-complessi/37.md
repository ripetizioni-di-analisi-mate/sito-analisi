Which complex numbers $z$ satisfy the system
$$
\begin{cases}
z \cdot \bar z = 25 \\
\operatorname{Re} z = 3
\end{cases}
$$

- $z = 4 \pm 3i$
- $z = 3 \pm 16i$
- $z = 3 \pm \sqrt{34}\,i$
- $z = 3 \pm 4i$

## Explanation

Set $z = 3 + bi$ with $b$ real, because the real part equals $3$. Since $z \cdot \bar z$ is the sum of the squares of the real and imaginary parts, the first equation becomes $9 + b^2 = 25$, that is, $b^2 = 16$. Thus $b = 4$ or $b = -4$, and the required numbers are $z = 3 \pm 4i$.
