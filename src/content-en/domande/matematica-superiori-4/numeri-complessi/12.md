What is the area of the polygon whose vertices are the solutions in $\mathbb{C}$ of the equation $z^4 + 4 = 0$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $16$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $z^4 = -4$, and $-4 = 4\,(\cos\pi + i\sin\pi)$. The fourth roots have modulus $\sqrt[4]{4} = \sqrt{2}$ and arguments $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, and $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$, so they are $1 + i$, $-1 + i$, $-1 - i$, and $1 - i$. These points are the vertices of a square with side length $2$, because consecutive vertices such as $1 + i$ and $-1 + i$ are a distance $|2| = 2$ apart. Thus the area is $2^2 = 4$.
