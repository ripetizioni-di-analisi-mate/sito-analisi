What is the algebraic form of the conjugate of $z = 6\left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{4} + i\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{4} \right)$?

- $-3\sqrt{2} - 3\sqrt{2}\,i$
- $-3\sqrt{2} + 3\sqrt{2}\,i$
- $3\sqrt{2} - 3\sqrt{2}\,i$
- $3\sqrt{2} + 3\sqrt{2}\,i$

## Explanation

Since $\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{4} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ and $\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, we have $z = 6\left( -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,i \right) = -3\sqrt{2} + 3\sqrt{2}\,i$. The conjugate has the same real part and the opposite imaginary part. Thus $\bar z = -3\sqrt{2} - 3\sqrt{2}\,i$, the reflection of $z$ across the real axis.
