Given $z = 6\left( \cos\dfrac{5\pi}{12} + i\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{12} \right)$ and $w = 2\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{12} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{12} \right)$, what is the algebraic form of the quotient $\dfrac{z}{w}$?

- $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{3}{2}\,i$
- $12i$
- $\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$
- $\dfrac{3}{2} - \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$

## Explanation

The modulus of the quotient is the quotient of the moduli, $\dfrac{6}{2} = 3$, and an argument is the difference of the arguments, $\dfrac{5\pi}{12} - \dfrac{\pi}{12} = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$. Thus $\dfrac{z}{w} = 3\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)$. Since $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, we obtain $\dfrac{z}{w} = \dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$.
