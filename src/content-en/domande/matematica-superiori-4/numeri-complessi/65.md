Why does $z\mapsto z^2$ take the semicircle $z=e^{i\theta}$, $0\leq\theta\leq\pi$, onto the whole unit circle?

- The modulus doubles.
- The argument is unchanged.
- The modulus stays one and the argument runs over $[0,2\pi]$.
- Every point maps to $1$.

## Explanation

By De Moivre's formula, $z^2=e^{2i\theta}$. As $\theta$ makes half a turn, $2\theta$ makes a full turn.
