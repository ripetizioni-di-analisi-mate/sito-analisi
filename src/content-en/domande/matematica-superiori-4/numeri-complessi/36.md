What is the value of $\overline{(2 - i)(1 + 3i)}$?

- $5 + 5i$
- $5 - 5i$
- $-1 - 5i$
- $-5 - 5i$

## Explanation

The product is $(2 - i)(1 + 3i) = 2 + 6i - i - 3i^2 = 5 + 5i$. Its conjugate has the same real part and the opposite imaginary part, so it is $5 - 5i$. The product of the conjugates gives the same result, $(2 + i)(1 - 3i) = 2 - 6i + i - 3i^2 = 5 - 5i$, because the conjugate of a product is the product of the conjugates.
