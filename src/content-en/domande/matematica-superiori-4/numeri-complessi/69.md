What is the smallest integer $n \geq 1$ for which $(1 + i)^n$ is a negative real number?

- $8$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The number $1 + i$ has modulus $\sqrt{2}$ and argument $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, so by De Moivre's formula, $(1 + i)^n$ has argument $\dfrac{n\pi}{4}$. A nonzero number is real and negative when its argument is $\pi$ plus a multiple of $2\pi$. The condition $\dfrac{n\pi}{4} = \pi + 2k\pi$ gives $n = 4 + 8k$, and the smallest positive value is $n = 4$. For this value, $(1 + i)^4 = (2i)^2 = -4$.
