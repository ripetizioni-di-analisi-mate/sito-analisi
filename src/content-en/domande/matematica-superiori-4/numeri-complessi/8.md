What set in the complex plane is formed by the complex numbers $z$ satisfying $|z - 1 + 2i| = 3$?

- The circle with center $(-1, 2)$ and radius $3$
- The circle with center $(1, -2)$ and radius $9$
- The circle with center $(1, 2)$ and radius $3$
- The circle with center $(1, -2)$ and radius $3$

## Explanation

Write $z - 1 + 2i = z - (1 - 2i)$. The condition then states that the distance from $z$ to the point representing $1 - 2i$ is $3$. The points at distance $3$ from the point $(1, -2)$ form the circle with center $(1, -2)$ and radius $3$. With $z = x + yi$, the equation becomes $\sqrt{(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2} = 3$, that is, $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 9$, where $9$ is the square of the radius.
