Which of the following numbers is a sixth root of $-64$?

- $2$
- $2i$
- $1 + \sqrt{3}\,i$
- $1 + i$

## Explanation

A number $w$ is a sixth root of $-64$ if $w^6 = -64$. For $w = 2i$ we have $w^6 = 2^6\, i^6 = 64 \cdot (-1) = -64$, because $6 = 4 + 2$ and hence $i^6 = i^2$. For the other options, $2^6 = 64$, $\left( 1 + \sqrt{3}\,i \right)^6 = \left( 2\,e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}} \right)^6 = 64\,e^{2\pi i} = 64$, and $(1 + i)^6 = (2i)^3 = -8i$. Thus the only sixth root of $-64$ among the given numbers is $2i$.
