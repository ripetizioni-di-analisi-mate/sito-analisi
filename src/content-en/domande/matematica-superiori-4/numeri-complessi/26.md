Where does $z^2$ lie if $z$ is purely imaginary and nonzero?

- On the positive real axis.
- On the negative imaginary axis.
- Always at the origin.
- On the negative real axis.

## Explanation

Write $z=it$ with real nonzero $t$. Then $z^2=-t^2<0$, independent of the sign of $t$.
