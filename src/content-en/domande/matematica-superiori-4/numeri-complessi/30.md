In the distance formula, which expression gives the real part of $z\bar w$ for $z=x+iy$ and $w=u+iv$?

- $xu+yv$
- $xu-yv$
- $xv+yu$
- $xv-yu$

## Explanation

In $(x+iy)(u-iv)$ the real contributions are $xu$ and $-i^2yv=yv$. Their sum is the dot product of the coordinate vectors.
