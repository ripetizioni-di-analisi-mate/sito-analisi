What is the value of $(3 + 2i)(3 - 2i) + (1 + i)^2$?

- $13 + 2i$
- $5 + 2i$
- $13$
- $15 + 2i$

## Explanation

The difference-of-squares identity applies to the first product, so $(3 + 2i)(3 - 2i) = 9 - 4i^2 = 9 + 4 = 13$. The square is $(1 + i)^2 = 1 + 2i + i^2 = 2i$. Thus the expression equals $13 + 2i$.
