What is the algebraic form of the quotient $\dfrac{7 + i}{1 - i}$?

- $6 + 8i$
- $4 + 4i$
- $3 - 4i$
- $3 + 4i$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by $1 + i$, the conjugate of the denominator. The denominator becomes $(1 - i)(1 + i) = 1 - i^2 = 2$. The numerator becomes $(7 + i)(1 + i) = 7 + 7i + i + i^2 = 6 + 8i$. Thus the quotient is $\dfrac{6 + 8i}{2} = 3 + 4i$.
