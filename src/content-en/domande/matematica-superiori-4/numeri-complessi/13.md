- $z = 1 \pm 3i$
- $z = -1 \pm 3i$
- $z = 2 \pm 6i$
- $z = 1 \pm 9i$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $\Delta = 4 - 40 = -36$, which is negative. Thus the solutions are complex conjugates, and we obtain them using $\sqrt{-\Delta} = 6$:
$$
z_{1,2} = \frac{2 \pm 6i}{2} = 1 \pm 3i .
$$
The two solutions are reflections of one another across the real axis because they are conjugates.
