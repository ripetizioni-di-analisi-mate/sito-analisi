For which values of $\theta$ in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$ does the number $z = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta$ have equal real and imaginary parts?

- $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\theta = \dfrac{5\pi}{4}$
- Only $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\theta = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\theta = \dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and $\theta = \dfrac{7\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The real part is $\cos\theta$ and the imaginary part is $\sin\theta$, so we solve $\cos\theta = \sin\theta$. When $\cos\theta = 0$, we have $\sin\theta = \pm 1$, so the solutions satisfy $\cos\theta \neq 0$ and we can divide by $\cos\theta$, obtaining $\tan\theta = 1$. In $[0, 2\pi)$, the solutions are $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{4} + \pi = \dfrac{5\pi}{4}$. They correspond to the points $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,i$ and $-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,i$, which lie on the angle bisector $y = x$.
