What are the solutions in $\mathbb{C}$ of the equation $z^2 + 25 = 0$?

- $z = \pm 5i$
- $z = \pm 5$
- $z = \pm 25i$
- $z = \pm \sqrt{5}\,i$

## Explanation

The equation is equivalent to $z^2 = -25$. Since $(5i)^2 = 25\,i^2 = -25$ and $(-5i)^2 = 25\,i^2 = -25$, the numbers $5i$ and $-5i$ are solutions. There are no others because $z^2 + 25 = (z - 5i)(z + 5i)$ and the zero-product property holds. Thus the solutions are $z = \pm 5i$.
