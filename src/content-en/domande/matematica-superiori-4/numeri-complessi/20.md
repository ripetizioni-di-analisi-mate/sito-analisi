Which identity shows that the product of two nonzero complex numbers cannot be zero?

- $|zw|=|z|\,|w|$
- $|z+w|=|z|+|w|$ always
- $\overline{zw}=\bar z+w$
- $|zw|=|z|-|w|$

## Explanation

The moduli of nonzero $z$ and $w$ are positive. Their product is positive, so $zw$ itself is nonzero.
