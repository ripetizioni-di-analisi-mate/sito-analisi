Which of the following expressions gives a complex number in trigonometric form?

- $2\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{5} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{5} \right)$
- $2\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{5} - i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{5} \right)$
- $-2\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{5} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{5} \right)$
- $2\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{5} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)$

## Explanation

Trigonometric form is $\rho\,(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)$, with $\rho > 0$, the same angle in the cosine and sine, and a plus sign between the two terms. The expression with $-2$ in front has a negative coefficient, so $-2$ is not the modulus. The expression with the minus sign corresponds to the argument $-\dfrac{\pi}{5}$ because $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{5} - i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{5} = \cos\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{5} \right) + i\sin\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{5} \right)$, and the expression with two different angles does not specify a single argument. Thus only $2\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{5} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{5} \right)$ is in trigonometric form, with modulus $2$ and argument $\dfrac{\pi}{5}$.
