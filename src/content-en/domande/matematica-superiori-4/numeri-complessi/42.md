The number $z = 2 - 5i$ is represented in the complex plane by the point $(2, -5)$. Which point represents the number $-\bar z$?

- $(-2, -5)$
- $(2, 5)$
- $(-2, 5)$
- $(5, -2)$

## Explanation

The conjugate of $z$ is $\bar z = 2 + 5i$, and its negative is $-\bar z = -2 - 5i$. Thus $-\bar z$ is represented by the point $(-2, -5)$. This point is the reflection of $(2, -5)$ across the imaginary axis because it has the same vertical coordinate and the opposite horizontal coordinate.
