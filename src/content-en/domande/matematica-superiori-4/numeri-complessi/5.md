What is the exponential form of $z = -\sqrt{3} + i$?

- $2\,e^{i\frac{\pi}{6}}$
- $2\,e^{-i\frac{\pi}{6}}$
- $2\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{6}}$
- $4\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{6}}$

## Explanation

The modulus is $|z| = \sqrt{3 + 1} = 2$. The argument $\theta$ satisfies $\cos\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and $\sin\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so the point $\left( -\sqrt{3}, 1 \right)$ is in the second quadrant. The second-quadrant angle with reference angle $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ is $\pi - \dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$. Thus $z = 2\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{6}}$.
