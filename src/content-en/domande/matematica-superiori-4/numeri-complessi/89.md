The polynomial $p(z) = z^3 - z^2 + 4z - 4$ has the root $2i$. What are its other two roots?

- $-2i$ and $-1$
- $2$ and $1$
- $-2i$ and $1$
- $-2$ and $-1$

## Explanation

The polynomial has real coefficients, so the conjugate $-2i$ is also a root. Factoring by grouping gives $p(z) = z^2(z - 1) + 4(z - 1) = (z - 1)\left( z^2 + 4 \right)$. The factor $z^2 + 4$ vanishes at $z = \pm 2i$ and the factor $z - 1$ at $z = 1$. Thus the other two roots are $-2i$ and $1$.
