What is the sum of the three cube roots of unity?

- $1$
- $0$
- $3$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The cube roots of $1$ have modulus $1$ and arguments $0$, $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, and $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$, so they are $1$, $-\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$, and $-\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$. The two nonreal roots are conjugates, so their sum is twice their real part, that is, $-1$. Thus the sum of the three roots is $1 + (-1) = 0$.
