Given $z = 2 - 5i$, which of the following numbers is real?

- $z - \bar z$
- $z^2$
- $z + \bar z$
- $i z$

## Explanation

The conjugate is $\bar z = 2 + 5i$. The sum $z + \bar z = 4$ is real because it is twice the real part of $z$. The difference $z - \bar z = -10i$, however, is purely imaginary. Also, $z^2 = 4 - 20i + 25i^2 = -21 - 20i$ and $iz = 2i - 5i^2 = 5 + 2i$, both of which have imaginary parts different from $0$.
