If $z$ and $w$ have positive real parts, must their product have a positive real part?

- Yes, because the real parts multiply with no other terms.
- Yes, because the product has positive modulus.
- No. For $z=w=1+2i$ the real part of the product is $-3$.
- No. For $z=w=1$ the real part is negative.

## Explanation

The real part of $(a+bi)(c+di)$ is $ac-bd$. In the counterexample the second product equals four, exceeding the first, which equals one.
