What is the algebraic form of the reciprocal $\dfrac{1}{z}$ of the number $z = 2\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,i$
- $1 - \sqrt{3}\,i$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,i$
- $\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,i$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of a number with modulus $\rho$ and argument $\theta$ has modulus $\dfrac{1}{\rho}$ and argument $-\theta$. Thus $\dfrac{1}{z}$ has modulus $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and argument $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. Since $\cos\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{3} \right) = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\sin\left( -\dfrac{\pi}{3} \right) = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, we obtain $\dfrac{1}{z} = \dfrac{1}{2}\left( \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i \right) = \dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,i$.
