The number $e^{i\alpha}+e^{i\beta}$ is zero. What does this tell you about the two phases?

- They differ by $\pi$ modulo $2\pi$.
- They agree modulo $2\pi$.
- Their sum is always zero.
- They differ by $\pi/2$ modulo $2\pi$.

## Explanation

Divide by $e^{i\beta}$ to obtain $e^{i(\alpha-\beta)}=-1$. The arguments of $-1$ are the odd multiples of $\pi$.
