Let $z = 3\,e^{i\frac{\pi}{5}}$ and $w = 6\,e^{i\frac{7\pi}{10}}$. What is the quotient $\dfrac{w}{z}$?

- $2i$
- $-2i$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,i$
- $2$

## Explanation

In exponential form, the modulus of the quotient is the quotient of the moduli, and an argument is the difference of the arguments. Thus
$$
\frac{w}{z} = \frac{6}{3}\,e^{i\left( \frac{7\pi}{10} - \frac{\pi}{5} \right)} = 2\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{10}} = 2\,e^{i\frac{\pi}{2}} .
$$
Since $e^{i\frac{\pi}{2}} = \cos\dfrac{\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} = i$, the quotient equals $2i$.
