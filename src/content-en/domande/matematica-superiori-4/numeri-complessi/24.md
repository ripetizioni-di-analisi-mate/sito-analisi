What is the sum $i + i^2 + i^3 + \dots + i^{10}$?

- $0$
- $i$
- $-1 + i$
- $-1 - i$

## Explanation

Four consecutive powers of $i$ sum to zero because $i + i^2 + i^3 + i^4 = i - 1 - i + 1 = 0$ and the powers repeat in cycles of four. Thus the first eight terms, from $i$ to $i^8$, sum to $0$. The remaining terms are $i^9 = i$ and $i^{10} = i^2 = -1$, so the sum is $-1 + i$.
