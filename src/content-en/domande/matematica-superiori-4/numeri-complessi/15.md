Which statement about the number $z = -7i$ is true?

- It is purely imaginary, with imaginary part $-7i$
- It is real, with real part $-7$
- It is purely imaginary, with imaginary part $-7$
- It has real part $0$ and imaginary part $7$

## Explanation

In algebraic form the number is $z = 0 + (-7)\,i$, so $\operatorname{Re} z = 0$ and $\operatorname{Im} z = -7$. The imaginary part is the coefficient of $i$, that is, the real number $-7$, without the $i$. Since the real part is zero and the imaginary part is different from $0$, the number is **purely imaginary**.
