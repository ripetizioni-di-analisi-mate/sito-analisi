What is the value of $i^{2027}$?

- $-i$
- $i$
- $-1$
- $1$

## Explanation

The powers of $i$ repeat in cycles of four because $i^4 = 1$. Thus we divide the exponent by $4$, and since $2027 = 4 \cdot 506 + 3$, the remainder is $3$. Therefore $i^{2027} = i^3 = i^2 \cdot i = -i$.
