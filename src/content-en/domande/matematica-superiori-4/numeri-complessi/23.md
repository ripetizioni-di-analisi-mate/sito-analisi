For which complex $z$ does the real part of $iz$ equal the real part of $z$?

- On the line $y=-x$ when $z=x+iy$.
- On the real axis.
- On the line $y=x$.
- On the unit circle.

## Explanation

The product $i(x+iy)=-y+ix$ has real part $-y$. Equality with the original real part requires $-y=x$.
