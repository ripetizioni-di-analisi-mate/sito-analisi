What is the algebraic form of $\dfrac{5i}{2 - i}$?

- $1 + 2i$
- $-1 - 2i$
- $-1 + 2i$
- $\dfrac{-5 + 10i}{3}$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by $2 + i$, the conjugate of the denominator. The denominator becomes $(2 - i)(2 + i) = 4 + 1 = 5$. The numerator becomes $5i\,(2 + i) = 10i + 5i^2 = -5 + 10i$. Thus the quotient is $\dfrac{-5 + 10i}{5} = -1 + 2i$.
