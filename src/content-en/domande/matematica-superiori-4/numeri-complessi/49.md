What is the argument in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$ of the number $z = 1 - i$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$

## Explanation

The modulus is $|z| = \sqrt{2}$, so the argument satisfies $\cos\theta = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ and $\sin\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. The cosine is positive and the sine is negative, so $\theta$ lies in the fourth quadrant. The fourth-quadrant angle with reference angle $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ is $2\pi - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{7\pi}{4}$.
