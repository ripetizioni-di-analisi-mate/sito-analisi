In the complex plane, what is the distance between the points representing $z = 2 + 3i$ and $w = -1 - i$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $\sqrt{5}$
- $25$

## Explanation

The distance between the points representing $z$ and $w$ is the modulus of their difference $|z - w|$. We have $z - w = (2 + 1) + (3 + 1)\,i = 3 + 4i$. Thus the distance is $|3 + 4i| = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.
