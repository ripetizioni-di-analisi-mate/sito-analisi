What is the value of $\left( \sqrt{2}\,e^{i\frac{3\pi}{8}} \right)^4$?

- $4i$
- $-4$
- $-4i$
- $-16i$

## Explanation

In exponential form we calculate the power using the rule $\left( \rho\,e^{i\theta} \right)^n = \rho^n e^{in\theta}$. With $\rho = \sqrt{2}$, $\theta = \dfrac{3\pi}{8}$, and $n = 4$, we obtain modulus $\left( \sqrt{2} \right)^4 = 4$ and argument $4 \cdot \dfrac{3\pi}{8} = \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. Since $e^{i\frac{3\pi}{2}} = \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = -i$, the power equals $-4i$.
