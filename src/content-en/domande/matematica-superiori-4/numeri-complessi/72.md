Let $u,v$ be the distinct complex roots of $z^2=-16i$. What is their distance without calculating their coordinates?

- It is $4$
- It is $16$
- It is $8$
- It depends on the chosen argument of $-16i$

## Explanation

The roots are opposites and both have modulus $\sqrt{16}=4$. They are endpoints of a diameter, so their distance is $2\cdot4=8$.
