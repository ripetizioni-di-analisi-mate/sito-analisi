What are the square roots of $-16i$?

- $\pm\left( 2\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{2}\,i \right)$
- $\pm(8 - 8i)$
- $\pm\left( 2\sqrt{2} - 2\sqrt{2}\,i \right)$
- $\pm(2 - 2i)$

## Explanation

Write $-16i = 16\left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} \right)$. The square roots have modulus $\sqrt{16} = 4$ and arguments $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3\pi}{4} + \pi = \dfrac{7\pi}{4}$. For $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ we obtain $4\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,i \right) = 2\sqrt{2} - 2\sqrt{2}\,i$, and for $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ we obtain its negative. Thus the roots are $\pm\left( 2\sqrt{2} - 2\sqrt{2}\,i \right)$.
