Why can no complex number satisfy both $z+\bar z=2+i$ and $|z|>0$?

- The modulus is always zero.
- The conjugate is always the negative of $z$.
- The sum with the conjugate is always positive.
- The sum with the conjugate is always real.

## Explanation

Writing $z=x+iy$ gives $z+\bar z=2x$. Its imaginary part is zero, whereas that of $2+i$ is one, so the first condition alone is impossible.
