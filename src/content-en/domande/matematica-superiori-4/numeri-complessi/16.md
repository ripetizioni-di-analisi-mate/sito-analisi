For which real numbers $x$ and $y$ does the equality $(2x - 1) + (y + 3)\,i = 5 - i$ hold?

- $x = 2$ and $y = -4$
- $x = 3$ and $y = 2$
- $x = 3$ and $y = -1$
- $x = 3$ and $y = -4$

## Explanation

Two complex numbers are equal when they have the same real part and the same imaginary part. The imaginary parts are the coefficients of $i$, namely $y + 3$ and $-1$. Thus we must have
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - 1 = 5 \\
y + 3 = -1
\end{cases}
$$
and therefore $x = 3$ and $y = -4$.
