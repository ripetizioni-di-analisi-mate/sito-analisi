What are the solutions in $\mathbb{C}$ of the equation $4z^2 - 4z + 5 = 0$?

- $z = -\dfrac{1}{2} \pm i$
- $z = \dfrac{1}{2} \pm i$
- $z = 1 \pm 2i$
- $z = \dfrac{1}{2} \pm 2i$

## Explanation

The discriminant is $\Delta = 16 - 80 = -64$, which is negative, so $\sqrt{-\Delta} = 8$. The denominator in the quadratic formula is $2a = 8$ because the coefficient of $z^2$ is $4$. Therefore
$$
z_{1,2} = \frac{4 \pm 8i}{8} = \frac{1}{2} \pm i .
$$
