The equation $z^2+(1-i)z-i=0$ has the root $i$. Which operation gives the other root without using the discriminant?

- Necessarily take the conjugate of $i$.
- Divide the constant term $-i$ by $i$.
- Add $i$ to the constant term.
- Reverse the sign of the constant term.

## Explanation

The polynomial is monic, so its roots have product $-i$. The other root is $(-i)/i=-1$. The conjugate-root rule requires real coefficients, which this example does not have.
