Which of the following inequalities is meaningful and true?

- $1 + i < 2i$
- $i > 0$
- $3 - i > 2$
- $|1 + i| < |2i|$

## Explanation

There is no ordering of $\mathbb{C}$ compatible with arithmetic, so an inequality is meaningful only between real numbers. The expressions $1 + i < 2i$, $i > 0$, and $3 - i > 2$ compare nonreal numbers and therefore have no meaning. Moduli, however, are real numbers, with $|1 + i| = \sqrt{2}$ and $|2i| = 2$. Since $\sqrt{2} < 2$, the inequality $|1 + i| < |2i|$ is true.
