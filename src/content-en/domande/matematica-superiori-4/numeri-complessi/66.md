Which information determines whether $(re^{i\theta})^n$ is real for $r>0$ and positive integer $n$?

- Whether $r^n$ is an integer.
- Whether $r=1$.
- Whether $\cos\theta=0$ for every $n$.
- Whether $n\theta$ is an integer multiple of $\pi$.

## Explanation

The imaginary part of the power is $r^n\sin(n\theta)$. Since $r^n$ is positive, the sine must vanish, which happens at $n\theta=k\pi$.
