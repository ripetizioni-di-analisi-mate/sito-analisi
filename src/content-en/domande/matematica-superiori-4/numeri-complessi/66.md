What is the value of $(1 - i)^{10}$?

- $32i$
- $-32$
- $32$
- $-32i$

## Explanation

The number $1 - i$ has modulus $\sqrt{2}$ and argument $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ because it lies in the fourth quadrant on the angle bisector $y = -x$. By De Moivre's formula, the tenth power has modulus $\left( \sqrt{2} \right)^{10} = 32$ and argument $10 \cdot \dfrac{7\pi}{4} = \dfrac{35\pi}{2}$. Subtracting $16\pi$, a multiple of $2\pi$, gives the argument $\dfrac{35\pi}{2} - 16\pi = \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, with $\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = 0$ and $\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{2} = -1$. Thus $(1 - i)^{10} = -32i$.
