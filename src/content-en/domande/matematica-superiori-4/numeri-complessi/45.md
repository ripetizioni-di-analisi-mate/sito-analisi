What set in the complex plane is formed by the numbers $z$ satisfying $|z + 1| \leq 1$?

- The disk with center $(1, 0)$ and radius $1$, including its boundary
- The circle with center $(-1, 0)$ and radius $1$
- The disk with center $(-1, 0)$ and radius $1$, including its boundary
- The points outside the circle with center $(-1, 0)$ and radius $1$

## Explanation

Write $|z + 1| = |z - (-1)|$, which is the distance from $z$ to the point $(-1, 0)$. Thus the condition requires this distance to be less than or equal to $1$. Points at a distance less than $1$ from the center are inside the circle of radius $1$, and those at a distance equal to $1$ are on the circle. Thus the set is the disk with center $(-1, 0)$ and radius $1$, including its boundary.
