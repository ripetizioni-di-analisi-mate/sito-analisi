Let $\omega$ be a cube root of $1$ other than $1$. Which quadratic polynomial must vanish at $\omega$?

- $z^2+z+1$
- $z^2-z+1$
- $z^2+1$
- $z^2-1$

## Explanation

The factorization $z^3-1=(z-1)(z^2+z+1)$ separates the root $1$. Since $\omega-1\ne0$, the second factor must vanish.
