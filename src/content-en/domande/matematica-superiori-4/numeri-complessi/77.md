The cube roots of $8i$ are the vertices of an equilateral triangle. What is its side length?

- $2\sqrt{3}$
- $2$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The cube roots of $8i$ have modulus $\sqrt[3]{8} = 2$, so the triangle is inscribed in the circle of radius $2$. The argument of $8i$ is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so two roots have arguments $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{6} + \dfrac{2\pi}{3} = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, that is, they are $\sqrt{3} + i$ and $-\sqrt{3} + i$. Their distance is $\left| 2\sqrt{3} \right| = 2\sqrt{3}$, so the side length is $2\sqrt{3}$.
