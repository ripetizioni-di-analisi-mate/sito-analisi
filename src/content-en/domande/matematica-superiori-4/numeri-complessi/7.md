- $z = 2 - 2i$
- $z = 2 + 2i$
- $z = 2 + i$
- $z = -2 + 2i$

## Explanation

Set $z = x + yi$, with $x$ and $y$ real, so $\bar z = x - yi$. The left-hand side becomes $x + yi + 3x - 3yi = 4x - 2y\,i$. Since the two complex numbers are equal, we must have
$$
\begin{cases}
4x = 8 \\
-2y = -4
\end{cases}
$$
so $x = 2$ and $y = 2$. Thus the solution is $z = 2 + 2i$.
