Two complex numbers satisfy $z^3=w^3$ and $w\ne0$. What can $z/w$ be?

- Any cube root of $1$.
- Only $1$.
- Any cube root of $-1$.
- Any point on the unit circle.

## Explanation

Dividing by $w^3$ gives $(z/w)^3=1$. All three roots occur, since for any $w\ne0$ we can multiply it by the chosen root.
