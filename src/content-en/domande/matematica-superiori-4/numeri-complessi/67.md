What is the value of $\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{9} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{9} \right)^{12}$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$
- $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2}\,i$

## Explanation

The number has modulus $1$ and argument $\dfrac{\pi}{9}$. By De Moivre's formula, its twelfth power has modulus $1^{12} = 1$ and argument $12 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{9} = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$. Since $\cos\dfrac{4\pi}{3} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\sin\dfrac{4\pi}{3} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, the power is $-\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$.
