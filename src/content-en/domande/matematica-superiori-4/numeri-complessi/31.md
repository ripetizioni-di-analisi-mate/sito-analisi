What is the algebraic form of $\dfrac{1}{1 + 2i}$?

- $1 - \dfrac{1}{2}\,i$
- $\dfrac{1}{5} - \dfrac{2}{5}\,i$
- $\dfrac{1}{5} + \dfrac{2}{5}\,i$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{2}{3}\,i$

## Explanation

Multiply the numerator and denominator by $1 - 2i$, the conjugate of the denominator. The denominator becomes $(1 + 2i)(1 - 2i) = 1 + 4 = 5$, a real number. Thus
$$
\frac{1}{1 + 2i} = \frac{1 - 2i}{5} = \frac{1}{5} - \frac{2}{5}\,i .
$$
