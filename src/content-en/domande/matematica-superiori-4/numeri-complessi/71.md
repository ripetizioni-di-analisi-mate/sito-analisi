Expand $(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^3$ using the formula for the cube of a binomial and compare it with De Moivre's formula. What expression do you obtain for $\cos(3\theta)$?

- $\cos^3\theta - 3\cos\theta\sin^2\theta$
- $\cos^3\theta + 3\cos\theta\sin^2\theta$
- $3\cos^2\theta\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta$
- $\cos^3\theta - \sin^3\theta$

## Explanation

The formula for the cube of a binomial gives
$$
(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^3 = \cos^3\theta + 3i\cos^2\theta\sin\theta + 3i^2\cos\theta\sin^2\theta + i^3\sin^3\theta .
$$
Since $i^2 = -1$ and $i^3 = -i$, the real part is $\cos^3\theta - 3\cos\theta\sin^2\theta$ and the imaginary part is $3\cos^2\theta\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta$. By De Moivre's formula, the same number equals $\cos(3\theta) + i\sin(3\theta)$. Equating real parts gives $\cos(3\theta) = \cos^3\theta - 3\cos\theta\sin^2\theta$.
