A nonzero point $z$ is specified only by its argument and the condition $\operatorname{Re}z=0$. Which statement holds?

- The modulus is always $1$.
- The argument must be zero.
- The point is uniquely determined.
- If the data are consistent, the modulus can be any positive number.

## Explanation

A zero real coordinate places the point on the imaginary axis. Its argument selects one of the two directions along that axis. The distance from the origin is still free.
