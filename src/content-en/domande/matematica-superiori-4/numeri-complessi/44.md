The numbers $z$ satisfying $|2z - 4 + 6i| = 10$ form a circle in the complex plane. What is its radius?

- $10$
- $20$
- $\sqrt{10}$
- $5$

## Explanation

Factor out $2$ in the expression $2z - 4 + 6i$ to obtain $2\,(z - 2 + 3i)$. The modulus of a product is the product of the moduli, so the condition becomes $2\,|z - (2 - 3i)| = 10$, that is, $|z - (2 - 3i)| = 5$. Thus the circle has center $(2, -3)$ and radius $5$.
