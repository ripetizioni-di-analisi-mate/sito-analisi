How is the locus $1<|z-i|\leq3$ described?

- A closed disk centered at the origin.
- A line through $i$.
- Just two circles.
- An annulus centered at $i$, including its outer but not its inner boundary.

## Explanation

The modulus $|z-i|$ is the distance from $i$. Every distance greater than $1$ and at most $3$ is allowed, including points on the outer boundary.
