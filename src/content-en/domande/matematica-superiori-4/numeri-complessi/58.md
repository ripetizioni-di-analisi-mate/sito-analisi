For $z,w\ne0$, which condition means their vectors point in the same direction and sense?

- $z+w$ is real.
- $|z|=|w|$.
- $z/w$ is positive real.
- $z/w$ is purely imaginary.

## Explanation

Pointing in the same direction means $z=tw$ for some $t>0$. Dividing by $w$ gives the stated condition and still allows different lengths.
