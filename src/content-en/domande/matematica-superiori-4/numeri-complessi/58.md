Given $z = 3\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{8} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{8} \right)$ and $w = 2\left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{8} + i\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{8} \right)$, what is the product $z w$?

- $5i$
- $6$
- $6i$
- $-6i$

## Explanation

The modulus of the product is the product of the moduli, $3 \cdot 2 = 6$, and an argument is the sum of the arguments, $\dfrac{\pi}{8} + \dfrac{3\pi}{8} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Thus $z w = 6\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$. Since $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 0$ and $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 1$, we obtain $z w = 6i$.
