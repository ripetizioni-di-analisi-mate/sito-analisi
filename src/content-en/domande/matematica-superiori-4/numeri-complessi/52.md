What is the algebraic form of the number $4\left( \cos\dfrac{5\pi}{3} + i\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{3} \right)$?

- $2 + 2\sqrt{3}\,i$
- $-2 - 2\sqrt{3}\,i$
- $2 - 2\sqrt{3}\,i$
- $2\sqrt{3} - 2i$

## Explanation

The angle $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$ lies in the fourth quadrant and has reference angle $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, so $\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{3} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. Substitution gives $4\left( \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i \right) = 2 - 2\sqrt{3}\,i$. The result has positive real part and negative imaginary part, as expected for a point in the fourth quadrant.
