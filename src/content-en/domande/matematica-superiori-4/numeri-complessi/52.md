In the complex plane the point $P$ is at distance $6$ from the origin, and the ray $OP$ makes an angle of $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ with the positive real semi-axis, measured counterclockwise. Which complex number is represented by $P$?

- $3 - 3\sqrt{3}\,i$
- $-3\sqrt{3} - 3i$
- $-3 - 3\sqrt{3}\,i$
- $-3 + 3\sqrt{3}\,i$

## Explanation

The distance of $P$ from the origin is the modulus $\rho = 6$ and the angle is an argument $\theta = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$, so the number is $z = 6\left( \cos\dfrac{4\pi}{3} + i\sin\dfrac{4\pi}{3} \right)$. The angle $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ lies in the third quadrant and has reference angle $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, therefore $\cos\dfrac{4\pi}{3} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\sin\dfrac{4\pi}{3} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. Substitution gives $z = 6\left( -\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i \right) = -3 - 3\sqrt{3}\,i$. The point has both coordinates negative, as every point of the third quadrant does.
