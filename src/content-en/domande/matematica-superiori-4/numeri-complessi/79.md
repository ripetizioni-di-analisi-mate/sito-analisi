How many of the eight eighth roots of $1$ are real numbers?

- $1$
- $4$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

The eighth roots of unity have modulus $1$ and arguments $\dfrac{2k\pi}{8} = \dfrac{k\pi}{4}$, with $k = 0, 1, \dots, 7$. A number with modulus $1$ is real when its argument is $0$ or $\pi$. This happens for $k = 0$ and $k = 4$, giving the roots $1$ and $-1$. Thus two roots are real.
