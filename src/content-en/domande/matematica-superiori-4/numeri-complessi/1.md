## Explanation

Expand both products using the distributive property, replacing $i^2$ with $-1$. The first product is $(3 - 2i)(1 + 4i) = 3 + 12i - 2i - 8i^2 = 11 + 10i$. The second is $2i\,(5 - i) = 10i - 2i^2 = 2 + 10i$. Thus the difference is $(11 + 10i) - (2 + 10i) = 9$, a real number.
