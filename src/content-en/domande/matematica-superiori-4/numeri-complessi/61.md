A point $z$ is rotated counterclockwise by $\pi/2$ about $c=2+i$. Which formula keeps the center fixed?

- $c+i(z-c)$
- $iz+c$
- $i(z+c)-c$
- $z+ic$

## Explanation

The vector from the center to the point is $z-c$. Rotation sends it to $i(z-c)$. Adding the center restores the original coordinates. Substitution of $z=c$ gives $c$.
