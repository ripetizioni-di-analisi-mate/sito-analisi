What is the value of $e^{i\pi} + e^{i\frac{\pi}{2}} + e^{2\pi i}$?

- $-1 + i$
- $2 + i$
- $-2 + i$
- $i$

## Explanation

By Euler's formula, $e^{i\pi} = \cos\pi + i\sin\pi = -1$. Similarly, $e^{i\frac{\pi}{2}} = \cos\dfrac{\pi}{2} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} = i$ and $e^{2\pi i} = \cos 2\pi + i\sin 2\pi = 1$. Thus the sum is $-1 + i + 1 = i$.
