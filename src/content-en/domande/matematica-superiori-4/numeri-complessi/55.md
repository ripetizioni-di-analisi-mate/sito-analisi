The number $z$ has argument $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. In which quadrant of the complex plane is the point representing its conjugate $\bar z$?

- In the second quadrant
- In the third quadrant
- In the fourth quadrant
- In the first quadrant

## Explanation

The angle $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ lies between $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\pi$, so $z$ lies in the second quadrant, with negative real part and positive imaginary part. The conjugate has the same real part and the opposite imaginary part, so both its parts are negative. Thus $\bar z$ lies in the third quadrant with argument $2\pi - \dfrac{2\pi}{3} = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.
