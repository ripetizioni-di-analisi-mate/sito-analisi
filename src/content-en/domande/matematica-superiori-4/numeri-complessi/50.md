What is the argument in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$ of the number $z = -2\sqrt{3} - 2i$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{11\pi}{6}$

## Explanation

The modulus is $|z| = \sqrt{12 + 4} = 4$, so $\cos\theta = -\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{4} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and $\sin\theta = -\dfrac{2}{4} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. The cosine and sine are negative, so $\theta$ lies in the third quadrant. The third-quadrant angle with reference angle $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ is $\pi + \dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{7\pi}{6}$.
