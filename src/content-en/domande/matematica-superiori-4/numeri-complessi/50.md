Two principal arguments are $179^\circ$ and $-179^\circ$. What is the **smaller** angle between their rays in the complex plane?

- $358^\circ$
- $2^\circ$
- $179^\circ$
- $0^\circ$

## Explanation

The second angle is equivalent to $181^\circ$ modulo a full turn. Along the short arc across the negative real axis their separation is therefore $181^\circ-179^\circ=2^\circ$.
