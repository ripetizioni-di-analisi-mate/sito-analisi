For nonzero $z$, why does $\dfrac{\bar z}{z}$ always lie on the unit circle even when $z$ is outside that circle?

- Numerator and denominator are always equal.
- Numerator and denominator have the same modulus.
- The quotient is always real.
- The conjugate always has modulus one.

## Explanation

The modulus of the quotient is $\dfrac{\lvert\bar z\rvert}{\lvert z\rvert}$. Conjugation preserves distance from the origin, so the ratio is one.
