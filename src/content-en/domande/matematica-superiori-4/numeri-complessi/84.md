What is the conjugate of the number $5\,e^{i\frac{2\pi}{7}}$?

- $-5\,e^{i\frac{2\pi}{7}}$
- $5\,e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{7}}$
- $5\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{7}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{7}}$

## Explanation

The conjugate is represented by the reflection of the point across the real axis. This point is at the same distance from the origin and has the opposite argument. Thus the conjugate of $5\,e^{i\frac{2\pi}{7}}$ is $5\,e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{7}}$. The number $\dfrac{1}{5}\,e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{7}}$ is the reciprocal, and $5\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{7}}$ is the reflection across the imaginary axis.
