For what value of the real parameter $k$ is the number $1 + 2i$ a solution of the equation $z^2 + kz + 5 = 0$?

- $k = 2$
- $k = -2$
- $k = -4$
- $k = -1$

## Explanation

Substitute $z = 1 + 2i$ into the equation. Since $(1 + 2i)^2 = 1 + 4i + 4i^2 = -3 + 4i$, this gives $-3 + 4i + k + 2k\,i + 5 = 0$, that is, $(2 + k) + (4 + 2k)\,i = 0$. A complex number is zero if and only if its real and imaginary parts are both zero, so we must have $2 + k = 0$ and $4 + 2k = 0$. Both conditions give $k = -2$.
