For what real value of $k$ is the product $(2 + i)(k - 3i)$ a real number?

- $k = -\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $k = -6$
- $k = 6$
- $k = \dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The distributive property gives $(2 + i)(k - 3i) = 2k - 6i + ki - 3i^2 = (2k + 3) + (k - 6)\,i$. The product is real when its imaginary part is zero, that is, when $k - 6 = 0$. Therefore $k = 6$, and for this value the product is $15$.
