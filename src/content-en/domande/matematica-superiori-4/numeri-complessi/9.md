What is the value of $(-2 - 2i)^8$?

- $-4096$
- $4096$
- $4096\,i$
- $256$

## Explanation

The number $-2 - 2i$ has modulus $\sqrt{4 + 4} = 2\sqrt{2}$ and lies in the third quadrant, with $\cos\theta = \sin\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, so its argument is $\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$. By De Moivre's formula, the eighth power has modulus $\left( 2\sqrt{2} \right)^8 = 8^4 = 4096$ and argument $8 \cdot \dfrac{5\pi}{4} = 10\pi$. Since $10\pi$ is a multiple of $2\pi$, we have $\cos 10\pi = 1$ and $\sin 10\pi = 0$. Thus $(-2 - 2i)^8 = 4096$.
