In the complex plane, the points representing $1 + 2i$, $0$, and $3 - i$ are three consecutive vertices of a parallelogram. What is the fourth vertex?

- $(-2, 3)$
- $(4, 1)$
- $(2, -3)$
- $\left( 2, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$

## Explanation

The vectors from the origin to the points $z = 1 + 2i$ and $w = 3 - i$ form two adjacent sides of the parallelogram, with common vertex $0$. By the parallelogram rule, the vertex opposite the origin represents the sum $z + w$. Since $z + w = 4 + i$, the fourth vertex is the point $(4, 1)$.
