What is the modulus of $z = (2 - i)(3 + 4i)$?

- $5\sqrt{5}$
- $25$
- $5$
- $5 + \sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The modulus of a product is the product of the moduli, so there is no need to carry out the multiplication. We have $|2 - i| = \sqrt{4 + 1} = \sqrt{5}$ and $|3 + 4i| = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$. Thus $|z| = 5\sqrt{5}$.
