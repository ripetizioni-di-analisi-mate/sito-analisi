What is the value of $\left| (1 + i)^{10} \right|$?

- $32$
- $1024$
- $10\sqrt{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The modulus of a product is the product of the moduli, so the modulus of a power is the corresponding power of the modulus. We have $|1 + i| = \sqrt{2}$. Therefore $\left| (1 + i)^{10} \right| = \left( \sqrt{2} \right)^{10} = 2^5 = 32$.
