In the complex plane a repeater at $z$ must be positioned to **minimize the larger** of its distances from $1+2i$ and $5+2i$. What is the smallest possible value of this larger distance?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The two points are four units apart. The triangle inequality makes the sum of their distances to the repeater at least four, so at least one distance is at least two. At the midpoint $3+2i$ both distances are two and the minimum is attained.
