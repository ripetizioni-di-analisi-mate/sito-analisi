Which of the following equations with real coefficients has $3 - i$ as a solution?

- $z^2 - 6z + 10 = 0$
- $z^2 + 6z + 10 = 0$
- $z^2 - 6z + 8 = 0$
- $z^2 - 3z + 10 = 0$

## Explanation

An equation with real coefficients that has the nonreal solution $3 - i$ also has its conjugate $3 + i$ as a solution. The sum of the two solutions is $6$ and their product is $(3 - i)(3 + i) = 9 + 1 = 10$. A quadratic equation whose solutions have sum $s$ and product $p$ can be written as $z^2 - s z + p = 0$, so the equation is $z^2 - 6z + 10 = 0$.
