Given $z = 8\left( \cos\dfrac{7\pi}{6} + i\sin\dfrac{7\pi}{6} \right)$ and $w = 4\left( \cos\dfrac{\pi}{6} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} \right)$, what is the quotient $\dfrac{z}{w}$?

- $2$
- $-2i$
- $-4$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The modulus of the quotient is the quotient of the moduli, $\dfrac{8}{4} = 2$, and an argument is the difference of the arguments, $\dfrac{7\pi}{6} - \dfrac{\pi}{6} = \pi$. Thus $\dfrac{z}{w} = 2\,(\cos\pi + i\sin\pi)$. Since $\cos\pi = -1$ and $\sin\pi = 0$, we obtain $\dfrac{z}{w} = -2$.
