A calculation gives $(x+iy)^2=x^2-y^2$ for real $x,y$. When does the omitted term actually vanish?

- $x=y$ always
- $xy=0$
- $x=-y$ always
- $x^2+y^2=1$

## Explanation

The complete expansion includes the cross term $2xyi$. It vanishes when at least one coordinate is zero, meaning the point lies on a coordinate axis.
