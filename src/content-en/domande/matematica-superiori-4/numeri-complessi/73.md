What are the square roots of $5 - 12i$?

- $\pm(3 + 2i)$
- $\pm(2 - 3i)$
- $\pm\left( \sqrt{5} - 2\sqrt{3}\,i \right)$
- $\pm(3 - 2i)$

## Explanation

We seek the roots in the form $w = x + yi$, with $x$ and $y$ real. Since $w^2 = x^2 - y^2 + 2xy\,i$, equality of the complex numbers requires
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 - y^2 = 5 \\
2xy = -12
\end{cases}
$$
The second equation gives $y = -\dfrac{6}{x}$, and substituting into the first gives $x^4 - 5x^2 - 36 = 0$, yielding $x^2 = 9$ or $x^2 = -4$. We discard the second possibility because $x$ is real. Thus $x = 3$ and $y = -2$, or $x = -3$ and $y = 2$. Therefore the roots are $\pm(3 - 2i)$.
