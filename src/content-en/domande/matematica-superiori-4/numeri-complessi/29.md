What is the value of $i^{100} - i^{33}$?

- $1 + i$
- $-1 - i$
- $0$
- $1 - i$

## Explanation

The powers of $i$ repeat in cycles of four, so the value of the power depends on the remainder when the exponent is divided by $4$. Since $100 = 4 \cdot 25$, the remainder is $0$ and $i^{100} = 1$. Since $33 = 4 \cdot 8 + 1$, the remainder is $1$ and $i^{33} = i$. Thus $i^{100} - i^{33} = 1 - i$.
