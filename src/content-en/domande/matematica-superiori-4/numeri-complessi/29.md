What goes wrong when $\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt a\sqrt b$ is applied to the principal complex square roots for $a=b=-1$?

- Both sides equal $i$.
- The product $ab$ is negative.
- We have $i^2=1$.
- The left side is $1$ and the right side is $-1$.

## Explanation

The product $ab$ is $1$, whose principal square root is $1$. Both factors have principal square root $i$, and their product is $i^2=-1$.
