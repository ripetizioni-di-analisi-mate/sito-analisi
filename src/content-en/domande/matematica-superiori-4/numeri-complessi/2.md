- $-1 - 2i$
- $-1$
- $1 - 2i$
- $-1 + 2i$

## Explanation

The powers of $i$ repeat in cycles of four, so we use the remainder when the exponent is divided by $4$. Since $47 = 4 \cdot 11 + 3$, we have $i^{47} = i^3 = -i$. Since $50 = 4 \cdot 12 + 2$, we have $i^{50} = i^2 = -1$. Also, $i^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{i} = -i$ because $i \cdot (-i) = -i^2 = 1$. Thus the sum is $-i - 1 - i = -1 - 2i$.
