What is the algebraic form of the number $6\,e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{3}}$?

- $-3 + 3\sqrt{3}\,i$
- $3 - 3\sqrt{3}\,i$
- $-3\sqrt{3} - 3i$
- $-3 - 3\sqrt{3}\,i$

## Explanation

By Euler's formula, $e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{3}} = \cos\left( -\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \right) + i\sin\left( -\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \right)$. The cosine is even and the sine is odd, so $\cos\left( -\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \right) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\sin\left( -\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \right) = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. Therefore $6\,e^{-i\frac{2\pi}{3}} = 6\left( -\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i \right) = -3 - 3\sqrt{3}\,i$.
