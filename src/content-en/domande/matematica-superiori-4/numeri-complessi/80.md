Which identity expresses a real sine using two complex exponentials?

- $\sin t=\dfrac{e^{it}+e^{-it}}{2i}$
- $\sin t=\dfrac{e^{it}-e^{-it}}2$
- $\sin t=e^{it}e^{-it}$
- $\sin t=\dfrac{e^{it}-e^{-it}}{2i}$

## Explanation

Euler's formulas at $t$ and $-t$ have equal real parts and opposite imaginary parts. Subtraction gives $2i\sin t=e^{it}-e^{-it}$.
