For which real values of $k$ is the number $z = (k - 1) + (k^2 - 9)\,i$ real?

- $k = 3$
- $k = \pm 3$
- $k = 1$
- $k = 9$

## Explanation

A complex number is real when its imaginary part is zero. Thus we must have $k^2 - 9 = 0$, that is, $k = 3$ or $k = -3$. The real part $k - 1$ can take any value, so it imposes no restrictions. Therefore $z$ is real for $k = \pm 3$.
