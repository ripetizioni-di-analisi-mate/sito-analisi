The map $T(z)=\bar z$ is applied twice to the same point. What is the overall effect?

- The point rotates through $\pi$.
- The point returns to its starting position.
- The point projects onto the real axis.
- The coordinates are interchanged.

## Explanation

The first conjugation sends $(x,y)$ to $(x,-y)$. The second reverses the sign of the second coordinate once more, giving $(x,y)$.
