The argument $\theta$ of a complex number $z$ satisfies $\tan\theta = \sqrt{3}$, and the real part of $z$ is negative. What is $\theta$ in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$

## Explanation

The tangent has period $\pi$, so in $[0, 2\pi)$ the condition $\tan\theta = \sqrt{3}$ is satisfied by $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{3} + \pi = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$. Since $\operatorname{Re} z = |z|\cos\theta$, the real part is negative when $\cos\theta < 0$. The cosine of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ is positive and that of $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ is negative, so $\theta = \dfrac{4\pi}{3}$.
