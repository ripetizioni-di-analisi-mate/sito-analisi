Two complex numbers have moduli $3$ and $5$. Which value is impossible for the modulus of their sum?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $8$

## Explanation

The triangle inequality and its reverse give $|5-3|\leq|z+w|\leq5+3$. Parallel vectors attain the bounds, and intermediate directions provide all values between $2$ and $8$.
