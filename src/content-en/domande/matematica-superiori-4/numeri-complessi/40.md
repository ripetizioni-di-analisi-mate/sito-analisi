What is the value of $\left| \dfrac{1 + 3i}{3 - i} \right|$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $10$
- $1$
- $\sqrt{10}$

## Explanation

The modulus of a quotient is the quotient of the moduli. We have $|1 + 3i| = \sqrt{1 + 9} = \sqrt{10}$ and $|3 - i| = \sqrt{9 + 1} = \sqrt{10}$, so the required modulus is $\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{\sqrt{10}} = 1$.
