What figure is traced by $w=z+\bar z$ as $z$ runs over the entire circle $|z|=1$?

- The circle $|w|=2$.
- The real segment $[-2,2]$.
- The whole real axis.
- Only the point $0$.

## Explanation

If $z=x+iy$, then $w=2x$. On the unit circle $x$ takes every value from $-1$ to $1$, so $w$ runs over the entire stated segment.
