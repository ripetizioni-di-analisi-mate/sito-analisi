What is the solution in $\mathbb{C}$ of the equation $2z - \bar z = 3 + 6i$?

- $z = 3 + 6i$
- $z = 3 + 2i$
- $z = 3 - 2i$
- $z = 1 + 2i$

## Explanation

Set $z = x + yi$, with $x$ and $y$ real, so $\bar z = x - yi$. The left-hand side becomes $2x + 2yi - x + yi = x + 3y\,i$. Since the two complex numbers are equal, we must have
$$
\begin{cases}
x = 3 \\
3y = 6
\end{cases}
$$
so $y = 2$ and the solution is $z = 3 + 2i$.
