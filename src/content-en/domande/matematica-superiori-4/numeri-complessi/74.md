A program computes fourth roots using $\theta/4+k\pi/4$ as angles for $k=0,1,2,3$. What angular step should it use?

- $\pi/4$
- $\pi$
- $\pi/2$
- $2\pi$

## Explanation

The original number's arguments are $\theta+2k\pi$. Dividing the entire expression by four gives $\theta/4+k\pi/2$, so consecutive roots are a quarter turn apart.
