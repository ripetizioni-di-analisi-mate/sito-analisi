Which of the following numbers is a fourth root of $16i$?

- $2\,e^{i\frac{3\pi}{8}}$
- $4\,e^{i\frac{\pi}{8}}$
- $2\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{8}}$
- $2\,e^{i\frac{\pi}{4}}$

## Explanation

Write $16i = 16\,e^{i\frac{\pi}{2}}$. The fourth roots have modulus $\sqrt[4]{16} = 2$. Their arguments are obtained starting from $\dfrac{1}{4} \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{2} = \dfrac{\pi}{8}$ and adding $\dfrac{2\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ at each step, giving $\dfrac{\pi}{8}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{8}$, $\dfrac{9\pi}{8}$, and $\dfrac{13\pi}{8}$. The only option with modulus $2$ and one of these arguments is $2\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{8}}$. The number $2\,e^{i\frac{3\pi}{8}}$, by contrast, has fourth power $16\,e^{i\frac{3\pi}{2}} = -16i$.
