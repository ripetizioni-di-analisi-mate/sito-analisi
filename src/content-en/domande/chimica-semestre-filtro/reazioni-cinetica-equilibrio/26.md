For $v=k[A][B]$, with v in $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$, which expression gives the correct dimensions for k?

- $\dfrac{v}{[A][B]}$, with units $\mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$.
- $v[A][B]$, with units $\mathrm{mol^3\,L^{-3}\,s^{-1}}$.
- $\dfrac{v}{[A]}$, with units $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
- $\dfrac{[A][B]}{v}$, with units $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,s}$.

## Explanation

Isolating k requires dividing rate by both concentration factors. This leaves inverse concentration multiplied by inverse time. Dividing by only one concentration omits B and describes a different coefficient, potentially observed only if constant B were incorporated into it. Taking the complete reciprocal instead gives inverse k dimensions.
