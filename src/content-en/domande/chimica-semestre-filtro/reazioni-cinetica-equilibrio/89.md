In a biological-fluid model, free activities give $Q_{\mathrm{ps}}>K_{\mathrm{ps}}$ for a specified solid phase. Which conclusion is directly supported?

- Crystallisation rate is numerically determined by the Q-to-K ratio alone.
- Every other possible solid phase has the same saturation degree.
- Formation of that phase is thermodynamically favoured under the considered conditions.
- A macroscopic stone must already be present when measured.

## Explanation

The comparison concerns thermodynamic tendency relative to the selected solid. Nucleation, growth, residence time and components inhibiting or promoting crystallisation affect the observed outcome. Other solids have other equilibrium relations. Saturation data therefore inform about the specified phase but do not alone determine macroscopic aggregate size or timing.
