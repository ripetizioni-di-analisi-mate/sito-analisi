For $\mathrm{N_2}+3\mathrm{H_2}\rightleftharpoons2\mathrm{NH_3}$, all ideal gases, partial pressures are instantly doubled by compressing an equilibrium mixture at constant temperature. What is new Q relative to K?

- $Q'=2K$.
- $Q'=\dfrac{K}{4}$.
- $Q'=4K$.
- $Q'=K$.

## Explanation

The numerator is multiplied by $2^2$, while the denominator receives $2^{1+3}$. The overall factor is therefore $2^{-2}=\dfrac14$. Q falls below K and ammonia synthesis is initially favoured. The result follows from gas-exponent comparison; K remains unchanged because temperature does not change.
