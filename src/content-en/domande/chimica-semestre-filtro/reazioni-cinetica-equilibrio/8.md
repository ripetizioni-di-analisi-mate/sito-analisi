A cell maintains a constant metabolite concentration through continuous production and equal net consumption supplied from outside. Which description is justified?

- Chemical equilibrium established by constant concentration alone.
- A steady state sustained by fluxes.
- Constant net metabolite accumulation over time.
- Cessation of both production and consumption.

## Explanation

The concentration balance is zero because input and output match while flux continues. This defines a steady state. Chemical equilibrium would require no net progress of the relevant reactions; stable concentration alone does not establish it. Net accumulation would instead raise concentration in the volume considered.
