A student reverses a reaction with constant K and writes the new constant as $-K$. Which correction is appropriate?

- The new constant is $\dfrac1K$ because its logarithm changes sign.
- The new constant is $K^2$ because two sides are exchanged.
- The new constant remains K because the atoms are the same.
- The new constant is $1-K$ to preserve the activity balance.

## Explanation

Reversal exchanges numerator and denominator of the quotient, making the constant reciprocal. Energetically, $\Delta_rG^\circ$ changes sign and therefore ln K changes sign. The reciprocal keeps K positive; assigning a negative value is incompatible with positive activity products. Atom balancing does not preserve thermodynamic reaction direction.
