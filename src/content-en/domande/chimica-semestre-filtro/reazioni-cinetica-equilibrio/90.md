After MX precipitation, an ideal solution remains in equilibrium with the solid at constant temperature and $K_{\mathrm{ps}}>0$. Which constraint do residual free ions satisfy?

- Concentrations must be equal even when one ion was in excess.
- Both activities must be exactly zero.
- Their activity product equals $K_{\mathrm{ps}}$.
- Their activity product must equal the reciprocal of $K_{\mathrm{ps}}$.

## Explanation

Solid presence and equilibrium impose the solubility product for the written dissolution. Extensive precipitation may leave small concentrations, but both zero would give zero product, incompatible with positive K. An initially excess ion can remain more concentrated. The reciprocal would describe the reversed reaction's constant with its quotient also inverted.
