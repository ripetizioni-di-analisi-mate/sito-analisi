For the overall reaction $2\mathrm A+\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm P$, only the balanced equation is known. What can be deduced without kinetic or mechanistic data?

- The overall law must be $v=k[A]^2[B]$.
- A half-life is independent of $[A]_0$.
- The reaction must occur in one termolecular event.
- The ratio of consumed A to B moles is two to one.

## Explanation

Coefficients impose matter balance and therefore consumption and production ratios. Orders instead depend on the observed mechanism; an overall equation may sum several steps. Molecularity belongs to an elementary step, and half-life requires a rate law. None of those additional facts follows from atom counting alone.
