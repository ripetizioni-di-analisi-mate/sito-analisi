An ideal saturated MX solution contacts excess solid. Solvent is added at constant temperature. What is the immediate tendency before the new equilibrium?

- Precipitation is favoured because concentrations decrease.
- The ion product decreases and some solid can dissolve.
- K decreases in the same proportion as dilution.
- Concentrations remain instantly unchanged because excess solid is present.

## Explanation

Dilution immediately lowers ionic concentrations and thus Q. Solid then supplies more formula units that can dissolve until renewed saturation, provided sufficient solid remains. Unchanged temperature keeps K constant. The immediate solvent-addition change must be distinguished from the subsequent chemical response restoring saturation activities.
