In an ideal MX model, X is added from a soluble salt at constant temperature without complexation. Which comparison is correct before and after common-ion addition?

- Solubility and $K_{\mathrm{ps}}$ decrease in the same proportion.
- Solubility remains equal because the equilibrium constant is unchanged.
- MX solubility decreases while $K_{\mathrm{ps}}$ remains unchanged.
- Solubility increases because more ions are available.

## Explanation

Higher X activity reaches the same product with less M supplied by dissolution. The equilibrium constraint is unchanged, but the composition satisfying it changes. Solubility and solubility product are therefore distinct quantities. No complexation and ideality exclude additional effects that could modify a real comparison.
