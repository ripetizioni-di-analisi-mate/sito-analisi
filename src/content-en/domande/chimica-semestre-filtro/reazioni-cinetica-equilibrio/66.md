In an open system supplied with reactant, a product is continuously removed. Composition may become nearly constant while net conversion continues. Which interpretation is correct?

- Removing product necessarily changes the thermodynamic constant at the same temperature.
- Net flux proves that matter balance does not apply.
- An external flux can maintain a steady state away from equilibrium.
- Constant composition requires Q equal to K for every reaction.

## Explanation

The balance includes input and output: concentration can stay constant through balanced flows despite net conversion. Product removal can maintain a quotient favouring the forward direction. The same reaction's constant at the same temperature stays unchanged. An open-system balance is therefore required, distinct from equal opposing rates in a closed system.
