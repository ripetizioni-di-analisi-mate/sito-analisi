A synthesis is exothermic and its forward pathway has positive activation energy with approximately constant parameters. Which paired effects can accompany heating?

- Both constants unchanged because coefficients do not change.
- Increased forward rate constant and decreased synthesis equilibrium constant.
- Obligatory increase in both constants for the same reason.
- Decreased forward rate constant and increased K as a necessary consequence of the assumptions.

## Explanation

Arrhenius with positive activation energy predicts an increased rate constant. K instead depends on reaction enthalpy, negative in the exothermic direction, and is predicted to decrease. A synthesis can therefore approach a less product-favoured equilibrium faster. Unchanged stoichiometry does not imply temperature-independent kinetic or thermodynamic quantities.
