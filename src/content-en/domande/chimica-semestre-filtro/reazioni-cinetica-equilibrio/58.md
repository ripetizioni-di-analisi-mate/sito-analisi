In an ideal mixture, a species has partial pressure $2.0\,\mathrm{bar}$ and total pressure is $4.0\,\mathrm{bar}$. With standard $p^\circ=1.0\,\mathrm{bar}$, which activity enters the reaction quotient?

- $0.50$.
- $2.0$.
- $4.0$.
- $0.25$.

## Explanation

Ideal-gas activity relative to the stated standard is $\dfrac{p_i}{p^\circ}=2$. The value 0.50 is mole fraction, obtained by dividing by total pressure; it alone does not equal activity when total pressure differs from the standard. The quotient requires participating species' partial pressures, not total pressure in their place.
