For $\mathrm A\rightleftharpoons\mathrm B$ in a closed system at equilibrium, which equality is required?

- A concentration equal to B concentration.
- Forward rate equal to reverse rate.
- Forward rate constant equal to reverse rate constant in every model.
- Initial A amount equal to initial B amount.

## Explanation

No net progress requires balanced rates, not equal concentrations. In an elementary model with $v_+=k_+[A]$ and $v_-=k_-[B]$, different constants can be balanced by different concentrations. Initial amounts affect the available balance, but equilibrium can be approached from very different initial ratios.
