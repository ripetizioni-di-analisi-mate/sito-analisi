Two processes have parallel plots of $\ln k$ against $\dfrac1T$ but different intercepts over the interval considered. Which comparison follows from the Arrhenius model?

- The same rate constant at every temperature.
- Equal activation energies and different pre-exponential factors.
- The same equilibrium constant because slopes match.
- Different activation energies and the same pre-exponential factor.

## Explanation

Slope is $-\dfrac{E_a}{R}$, so parallel lines give the same activation energy in the model. The intercept is $\ln A$, so different intercepts indicate different prefactors and separated rate constants. These lines describe rates; without thermodynamic information they establish neither equality nor difference of equilibrium constants.
