At the same temperature, $\mathrm A\rightleftharpoons\mathrm B$ has $K_1=4$ and $\mathrm B\rightleftharpoons\mathrm C$ has $K_2=9$, with consistent standard states. What is K for $\mathrm C\rightleftharpoons\mathrm A$?

- $\dfrac94$.
- $36$.
- $\dfrac{1}{13}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$.

## Explanation

Adding reactions in the A-to-C direction gives $K_1K_2=36$. The requested direction is opposite, so the constant becomes reciprocal. Adding four and nine confuses the rule for constants with the rule for standard free energies. Nine fourths would represent another equation combination and does not cancel B in the required way.
