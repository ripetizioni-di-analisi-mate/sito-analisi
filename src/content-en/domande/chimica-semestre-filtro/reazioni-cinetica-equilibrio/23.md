A plot of $[A]$ decreases and becomes progressively less steep at constant volume. Which quantity describes instantaneous A disappearance rate at time $t$?

- The negative of the tangent slope at $t$.
- The total initial-to-final concentration change over the experiment.
- The value of $[A]$ at $t$, without division by time.
- The negative secant slope from the origin to each point, independently of interval.

## Explanation

The derivative measures local concentration change; the minus sign makes disappearance positive. A secant gives an interval average and can differ from the tangent when slope changes. Concentration or total change without time also has different units from rate. The described graph indicates slowing disappearance.
