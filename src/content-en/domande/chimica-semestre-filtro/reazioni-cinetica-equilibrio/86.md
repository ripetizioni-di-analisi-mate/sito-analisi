A saturated MX solution contacts the solid. A ligand selectively binds free M without forming other solids at constant temperature. Which initial effect favours further dissolution?

- Ligand binding necessarily increases the original phase's $K_{\mathrm{ps}}$.
- Every formed complex equals a newly precipitated solid unit.
- Decreased free-M activity lowers the ion product.
- Total M concentration automatically replaces free concentration in K.

## Explanation

The complex removes M from the free form required by dissolution equilibrium. Immediately after binding, Q can fall below K and more solid can dissolve. Total dissolved M may increase while the free fraction remains controlled by simultaneous equilibria. Quantification also requires the complex formation constant and matter balances.
