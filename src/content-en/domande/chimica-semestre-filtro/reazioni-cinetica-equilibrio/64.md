A reaction has $K=10^{12}$ at fixed temperature. Which conclusion requires information beyond this value?

- That it reaches equilibrium within one second.
- That the reverse reaction's constant is $10^{-12}$.
- That $\Delta_rG^\circ$ is negative.
- That the natural logarithm of K is positive.

## Explanation

A large K determines the standard-change sign and equilibrium activity ratio. It contains no information about barriers or rate constants, which are needed to predict time. The reverse reaction has the reciprocal constant at the same temperature. Even a strongly favoured standard-reference transformation can be very slow experimentally.
