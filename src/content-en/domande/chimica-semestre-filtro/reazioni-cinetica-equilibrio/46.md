Two pathways connect the same reactants and products at the same temperature, but one has a lower free-energy barrier. Which quantity is common to both pathways?

- The free-energy difference between initial and final states.
- The rate at which each pathway is followed.
- Every intermediate barrier value.
- The required number of elementary steps.

## Explanation

Free energy is a state function, so its difference depends on the specified endpoints. Pathways may differ in barriers, intermediates and rates. This explains why catalysis changes kinetics while preserving the same transformation's equilibrium condition. A different sequence of steps does not justify changing final-state energy without changing products.
