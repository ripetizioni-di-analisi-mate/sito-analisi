For complete combustion $\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}+a\mathrm{O_2}\longrightarrow2\mathrm{CO_2}+3\mathrm{H_2O}$, which value of $a$ conserves atoms?

- $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
- $2$.
- $3$.
- $6$.

## Explanation

Products contain seven oxygen atoms for the written reaction amount. One is already present in ethanol; the remaining six come from three oxygen molecules. Seven halves would ignore oxygen in the fuel, whereas six would count oxygen atoms as O2 molecules. Carbon and hydrogen are already balanced.
