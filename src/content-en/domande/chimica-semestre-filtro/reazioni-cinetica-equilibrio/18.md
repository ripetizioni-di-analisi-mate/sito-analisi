An exothermic reaction transfers heat to a thermal bath. Which description respects energy conservation for system plus surroundings?

- Energy transferred to the surroundings corresponds to a system energy change, including any work.
- Balancing atoms requires exchanged heat to be zero.
- System energy alone must stay constant even while it loses heat and does no work.
- Released heat is created without any energy change in the substances.

## Explanation

A reaction redistributes energy among substances, surroundings and any exchanged work. Conservation applies to the complete balance, while the chemical subsystem's energy may change. Balancing atoms and charges does not determine heat: balanced reactions can have different enthalpies. Total conservation must be distinguished from constant energy in one part.
