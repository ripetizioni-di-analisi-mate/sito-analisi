In the mechanism $\mathrm A+\mathrm C\longrightarrow\mathrm I$, followed by $\mathrm I+\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm P+\mathrm C$, which role assignment is consistent?

- I is the regenerated catalyst; C is the net final product.
- C is consumed overall; I must appear among overall products.
- C is regenerated as a catalyst; I is an intermediate.
- C and I are both consumed as net stoichiometric reactants.

## Explanation

C enters the first step and is returned in the second, allowing another cycle. I is formed after the start and then consumed. Both cancel from the sum, but the sequence distinguishes their roles. The overall reaction is A plus B to P, while the mechanism shows the catalyst's actual participation.
