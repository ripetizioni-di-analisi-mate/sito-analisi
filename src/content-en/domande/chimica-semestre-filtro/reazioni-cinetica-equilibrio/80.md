Two salts MX and $\mathrm{NY_2}$ both have $K_{\mathrm{ps}}=10^{-12}$ at the same temperature. In ideal pure water with each solid phase present and without other equilibria, which molar-solubility comparison is correct?

- $\mathrm{NY_2}$ is more soluble in the model because it obeys $4s^3=K_{\mathrm{ps}}$.
- Solubilities are equal because the constants have the same value.
- Comparison remains impossible even with all the stated ideal assumptions.
- MX is more soluble because it produces fewer ions per formula unit.

## Explanation

MX gives $s=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. $\mathrm{NY_2}$ gives $s=\left(\dfrac{10^{-12}}4\right)^{\dfrac{1}{3}}\simeq6.3\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. Different stoichiometry changes the relationship between K and solubility. Numerically equal constants therefore do not establish equal dissolved salt amounts.
