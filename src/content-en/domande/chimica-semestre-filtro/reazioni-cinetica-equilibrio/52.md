An inhibitor reduces a catalyst's active fraction without binding reactants or products or changing temperature. Which effect is directly consistent?

- Slower approach to the same reaction equilibrium.
- An obligatory shift of K towards reactants.
- Equilibrium product concentration decreases in the same proportion as active fraction.
- Reversal of $\Delta_rG^\circ$ sign solely through reduced catalytic activity.

## Explanation

The model changes access to the catalysed pathway and thus the time required for composition to relax. State energies and temperature remain unchanged, so K and standard Gibbs-energy change stay the same. Excluding binding to reactants and products avoids confusing a kinetic effect with altered available activities.
