Over an interval, an Arrhenius law has $E_a=0$ and constant A. What does it predict for k as T changes?

- $k=AT$, directly proportional to temperature.
- $k=A$, independent of T within the model assumptions.
- $k=Ae^{-T}$, decreasing exponentially.
- $k=0$ at every temperature.

## Explanation

The exponent $-\dfrac{E_a}{RT}$ is zero, making the exponential factor one. The constant equals the prefactor. Zero activation energy in the model does not mean no reaction or zero rate constant. The conclusion is limited to unchanged A and does not extend to every real process with apparently weak temperature dependence.
