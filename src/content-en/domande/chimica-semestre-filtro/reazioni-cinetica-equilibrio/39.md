For $2\mathrm A\longrightarrow\mathrm P$ at constant volume, the normalised rate is $v=-\dfrac12\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}=k[A]$. What is A half-life in this model?

- $\dfrac{\ln2}{k}$.
- $\dfrac{[A]_0}{2k}$.
- $\dfrac{2\ln2}{k}$.
- $\dfrac{\ln2}{2k}$.

## Explanation

Multiplying by two gives the species disappearance law $-\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}=2k[A]$. The coefficient in A's exponential is therefore 2k. Applying the first-order formula directly to normalised k without this step would double the time. The formula $\dfrac{[A]_0}{2k}$ belongs to zero order and is inconsistent with linear dependence on A.
