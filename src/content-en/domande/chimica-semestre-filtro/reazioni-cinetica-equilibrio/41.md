A sample is heated from $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ to $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Which values enter reciprocal temperatures in the Arrhenius relation?

- $10$ and $35\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $273.15$ and $10\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $25$ and $35$ as absolute values.
- $298.15$ and $308.15\,\mathrm{K}$.

## Explanation

The exponent contains thermal energy RT and therefore requires absolute temperature. Add 273.15 to each Celsius temperature before taking reciprocals. A ten-degree difference has the same numerical value in kelvin, but reciprocal temperatures do not depend on that difference alone. Using 25 and 35 would greatly distort the predicted ratio.
