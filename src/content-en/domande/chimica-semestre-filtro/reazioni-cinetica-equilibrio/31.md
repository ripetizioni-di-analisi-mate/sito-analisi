A mechanism contains $\mathrm A\longrightarrow\mathrm I$ and $\mathrm I+\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm P$. Which equation follows by adding the steps?

- $\mathrm A+\mathrm I+\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm P$.
- $\mathrm A\longrightarrow\mathrm B+\mathrm P$.
- $\mathrm A+\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm P$.
- $\mathrm A+\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm I+\mathrm P$.

## Explanation

Intermediate I is produced in the first step and consumed with the same coefficient in the second, so it cancels from the sum. A and B are consumed and P is formed. Keeping I on only one side gives an incomplete balance. Its cancellation from the overall equation does not eliminate its possible role in the observed rate law.
