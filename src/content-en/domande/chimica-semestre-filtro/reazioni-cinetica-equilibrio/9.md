For $\mathrm{MX_2(s)}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{M^{2+}}+2\mathrm{X^-}$ with pure solid present, which thermodynamic expression describes the solubility product?

- $K_{\mathrm{ps}}=2a_{M^{2+}}a_{X^-}$.
- $K_{\mathrm{ps}}=a_{M^{2+}}^2a_{X^-}$.
- $K_{\mathrm{ps}}=a_{M^{2+}}a_{X^-}^2$.
- $K_{\mathrm{ps}}=\dfrac{a_{M^{2+}}}{a_{X^-}^2}$.

## Explanation

Stoichiometric coefficients become activity exponents in the equilibrium expression. X has coefficient two and M one; ionic charge does not replace the coefficient. Both are dissolution products and therefore appear in the numerator. Pure solid has unit activity in the model and introduces no variable concentration in the denominator.
