A closed system reaches stable chemical equilibrium at constant temperature and pressure. What does $\Delta_rG=0$ mean for the reaction considered?

- The G change for an admissible infinitesimal reaction extent is zero at equilibrium.
- Standard change $\Delta_rG^\circ$ must be zero for every reaction.
- Every individual microscopic process must stop.
- The system's total G value must be zero.

## Explanation

Reaction Gibbs energy is the derivative of G with respect to extent, not the function's absolute value. At stable equilibrium, no admissible infinitesimal direction lowers G through that reaction. Standard change may be nonzero and balanced by RT ln Q. Microscopic processes can continue in both directions without net progress.
