Mixing silver nitrate and sodium chloride solutions produces AgCl solid. Which net ionic equation represents precipitation in the dissociated-electrolyte model?

- $\mathrm{Ag^+}+\mathrm{NO_3^-}\longrightarrow\mathrm{AgNO_3(s)}$.
- $\mathrm{Ag^+}+2\mathrm{Cl^-}\longrightarrow\mathrm{AgCl(s)}$.
- $\mathrm{Na^+}+\mathrm{NO_3^-}\longrightarrow\mathrm{NaNO_3(s)}$.
- $\mathrm{Ag^+}+\mathrm{Cl^-}\longrightarrow\mathrm{AgCl(s)}$.

## Explanation

Sodium and nitrate remain in the aqueous phase unchanged in the specified process, so they cancel from both sides. Silver and chloride enter the solid one to one, also conserving zero total charge. Nitrate alternatives describe different solids; doubling chloride changes both atom count and net charge.
