For a solute, $a=\gamma\dfrac{c}{c^\circ}$ with $\gamma=0.50$, $c=0.020\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and $c^\circ=1\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. What is a?

- $0.020\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.
- $0.040$, dimensionless.
- $0.010$, dimensionless.
- $0.50\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

The ratio of c to its standard cancels units, then multiplication by the activity coefficient gives $0.50\cdot0.020=0.010$. Dividing by gamma gives 0.040 and reverses the assigned definition. Setting activity equal to concentration ignores both correction and normalisation. The example shows why free-ion concentration and activity are related but distinct.
