At unchanged temperature and other concentrations, doubling inhibitor I halves rate in the studied range. For a power law, which order in I describes the observation?

- $1$.
- $\dfrac12$.
- $-1$.
- $0$.

## Explanation

The rate ratio satisfies $\dfrac12=2^\alpha$, so $\alpha=-1$. A negative order is allowed in an experimental law and describes inhibition within its validity range. It does not mean negative molecularity, which counts entities in one elementary step. Zero order would instead leave rate unchanged when I changes.
