A reaction profile shows two energy maxima separated by a local minimum I, distinct from reactants and products. What does I represent?

- The first step's transition state.
- The second step's transition state.
- The overall reactant–product energy difference.
- A mechanism intermediate.

## Explanation

The local minimum is relatively stable compared with neighbouring regions and can host an intermediate with finite lifetime. Maxima instead identify transition regions along the steps. The overall difference compares initial and final levels, rather than identifying an intermediate species. The profile suggests multiple steps but does not alone determine all rate constants.
