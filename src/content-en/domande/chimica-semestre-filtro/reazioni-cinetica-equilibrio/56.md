In $\Delta_rG^\circ=-RT\ln K$, which K must be used?

- The dimensionless constant built from activities referenced to consistent standard states.
- A concentration product expressed in arbitrary units without normalisation.
- The numerical concentration of the most abundant product.
- The forward rate constant k, even if it has inverse-time units.

## Explanation

A logarithm requires a dimensionless argument. Activities introduce a standard-state ratio and preserve the constant's meaning when measurement units change. A form using unnormalised concentrations requires care. A rate constant instead describes kinetics and does not replace the thermodynamic constant.
