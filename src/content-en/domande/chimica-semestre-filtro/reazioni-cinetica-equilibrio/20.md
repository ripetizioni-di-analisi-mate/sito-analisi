An isolated product mass corresponds to an apparent yield of $108\%$ relative to the pure product's stoichiometric maximum. Which check is most directly relevant?

- Attribute exceeding the maximum to a catalyst.
- Check residual solvent and impurities in the weighed sample.
- Use the product coefficient to correct the already calculated percentage.
- Conclude that the limiting reactant produced more moles than the balanced ratio.

## Explanation

The theoretical maximum is the pure product amount compatible with the stated reactants and reaction. Additional water, solvent or other substances increase measured mass without increasing product moles. A catalyst may improve conversion or recovery within available time, but does not change stoichiometric ratios. Initial data and product identity should also be checked.
