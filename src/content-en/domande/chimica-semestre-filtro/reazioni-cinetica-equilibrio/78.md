For $\mathrm A\rightleftharpoons2\mathrm B$ at constant volume and temperature, initially $[A]_0=0.60\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and B is absent. In the ideal model $K=0.40$, with standard $1\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. Which pair satisfies both balance and equilibrium?

- $[A]=0.20$ and $[B]=0.20\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.
- $[A]=0.20$ and $[B]=0.40\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.
- $[A]=0.40$ and $[B]=0.40\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.
- $[A]=0.40$ and $[B]=0.20\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Consuming 0.20 moles per litre of A produces 0.40 moles per litre of B, leaving A at 0.40. The normalised quotient is $\dfrac{0.40^2}{0.40}=0.40$. Both conditions must be checked: quantities giving a suitable ratio but violating $[A]+\dfrac{[B]}2=[A]_0$ would not describe the specified starting mixture.
