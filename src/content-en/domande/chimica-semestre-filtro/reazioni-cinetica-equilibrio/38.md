For a simple disappearance, data form an increasing straight line in a plot of $\dfrac1{[A]}$ against time at constant volume and temperature. Which law is consistent?

- $-\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}=k$.
- $\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}=k$ with constant production.
- $-\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}=k[A]$.
- $-\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}=k[A]^2$.

## Explanation

Integrating second order gives $\dfrac1{[A]}=\dfrac1{[A]_0}+kt$, exactly a straight line of positive slope k. Concentration itself is linear for zero order, while its logarithm is linear for first order. An increasing reciprocal means decreasing A, so constant production would behave differently.
