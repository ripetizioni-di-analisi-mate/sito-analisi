To identify the limiting reactant in $a\mathrm A+b\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm P$, initial masses and molar masses are known. Which comparison is correct?

- Compare $m_A$ directly with $m_B$.
- Compare $M_A$ with $M_B$ without available masses.
- Compare $m_Aa$ with $m_Bb$ without molar masses.
- Compare $\dfrac{m_A}{M_Aa}$ with $\dfrac{m_B}{M_Bb}$.

## Explanation

Mass divided by molar mass gives moles. Dividing again by the coefficient gives the extent each supply would permit. The minimum constrains the reaction. Masses alone cannot establish stoichiometric availability when substances and coefficients differ; molar masses alone do not specify how much substance is actually present.
