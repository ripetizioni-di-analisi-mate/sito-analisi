Adding a small crystal of the same macroscopic phase to a supersaturated solution shortens the precipitation delay at unchanged temperature. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

- The seed lowers K because it adds solid mass to the denominator.
- The seed must dissolve completely before growth can begin.
- The seed facilitates growth without requiring a change in solubility product.
- The seed necessarily reverses the Gibbs-energy sign for precipitation.

## Explanation

An existing crystal surface may permit growth without waiting for spontaneous formation of a new nucleus. The solution was already supersaturated, so the thermodynamically favoured direction need not change. Phase and temperature are unchanged, preserving the solubility product. The phenomenon shows changed observed timing at the same initial thermodynamic condition.
