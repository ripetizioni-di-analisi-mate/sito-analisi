A non-perturbing isotope label moves from reactants to products in a mixture of constant overall composition. Which conclusion is supported?

- Labelling proves equal concentrations of all species.
- Label appearance in products alone proves a net increase in total product amount.
- Microscopic transformations continue, consistently with dynamic equilibrium.
- Every reaction has stopped because composition is constant.

## Explanation

Label transfer shows that chemical entities continue to transform. If opposing processes balance amounts, overall composition can remain unchanged while tracer distribution changes. The finding is therefore consistent with microscopic dynamics without requiring net product accumulation or equal concentrations. The non-perturbing assumption separates observation from an equilibrium shift.
