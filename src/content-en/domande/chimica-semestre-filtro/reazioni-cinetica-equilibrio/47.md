In a schematic profile, reactants, maximum and products lie at $0$, $50$ and $-30\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. What is the barrier from the product side?

- $20\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $-30\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

The reverse process starts at minus thirty and must reach fifty, so the difference is $50-(-30)=80$. Fifty measures the barrier from the reactant side; minus thirty is the product–reactant difference. Subtracting thirty without its sign would measure a separation between levels not present in the profile.
