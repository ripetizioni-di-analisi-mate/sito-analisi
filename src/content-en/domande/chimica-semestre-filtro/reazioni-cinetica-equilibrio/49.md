Two samples have equal temperature, the same medium composition and saturating substrate. The second has twice the active enzyme, with independent sites and equal turnover. What initial effect does the model predict?

- Rate remains unchanged because substrate is already saturating.
- Rate halves because each site receives half the available substrate.
- Initial catalytic capacity doubles while the reaction's K stays unchanged.
- K doubles while catalytic capacity remains unchanged.

## Explanation

Saturation limits the effect of adding substrate, not functioning catalytic sites. With equal turnover per site, twice the sites permit twice the initial rate in the model. Temperature and reaction are unchanged, so equilibrium constant stays the same. The saturating-substrate assumption ensures that the additional sites can also operate at the same capacity.
