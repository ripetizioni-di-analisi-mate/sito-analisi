A mixture has $\Delta_rG<0$ for the forward direction but shows very little conversion during the experiment. Which explanation is consistent with both findings?

- Every $\Delta_rG<0$ requires instantaneous conversion.
- The equilibrium constant directly measures the seconds required for reaction.
- A small rate proves that $\Delta_rG$ must be positive.
- The accessible pathway has a high kinetic barrier.

## Explanation

The Gibbs-energy sign describes thermodynamic direction under current conditions. Time also depends on steps crossing the barrier; a high barrier can make transformation rare while leaving it favoured. A catalyst could accelerate it while preserving energetic endpoints. Observed rate and spontaneity are complementary information and cannot substitute for each other.

[Theory: Favourable but slow](/en/chimica-semestre-filtro/reazioni-cinetica-equilibrio/teoria#arrhenius).
