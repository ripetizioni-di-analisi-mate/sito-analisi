A reaction has $K=1$ at a given temperature, while a mixture has $Q=2$. Which pair of statements is correct?

- $\Delta_rG^\circ=0$ and $\Delta_rG=0$.
- $\Delta_rG^\circ>0$ and $\Delta_rG=0$.
- $\Delta_rG^\circ=0$ and $\Delta_rG>0$.
- $\Delta_rG^\circ<0$ and $\Delta_rG<0$.

## Explanation

The logarithm of K equal to one is zero, so standard change vanishes. The current mixture also requires the composition term: $\Delta_rG=RT\ln2>0$. The standard reference does not automatically coincide with every actual composition. Reaction equilibrium would require Q equal to one, not merely K equal to one.
