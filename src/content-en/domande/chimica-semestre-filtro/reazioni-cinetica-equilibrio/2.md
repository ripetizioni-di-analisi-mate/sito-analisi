Complete reaction $2\mathrm{H_2}+\mathrm{O_2}\longrightarrow2\mathrm{H_2O}$ starts with $5\,\mathrm{mol}$ of hydrogen and $3\,\mathrm{mol}$ of oxygen, without side reactions. Which result is correct?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{mol}$ of water forms and $2.5\,\mathrm{mol}$ of hydrogen remains.
- $3\,\mathrm{mol}$ of water forms and $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ of hydrogen remains.
- $6\,\mathrm{mol}$ of water forms and all oxygen is consumed.
- $5\,\mathrm{mol}$ of water forms and $0.5\,\mathrm{mol}$ of oxygen remains.

## Explanation

Available extents are $\dfrac52$ and $3\,\mathrm{mol}$; hydrogen sets the minimum. Maximum extent is therefore $2.5\,\mathrm{mol}$, producing twice as many water moles and consuming $2.5\,\mathrm{mol}$ of oxygen. Confusing extent with product moles loses coefficient two; consuming all oxygen would require more hydrogen than is available.
