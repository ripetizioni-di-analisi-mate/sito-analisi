An equilibrium equation is rewritten with every coefficient doubled at unchanged temperature. How do $\Delta_rG^\circ$ and K for the new equation change?

- Both standard change and K double.
- Standard change halves and K becomes $\sqrt K$.
- Both remain equal because the physical mixture is the same.
- Standard change doubles and the constant becomes $K^2$.

## Explanation

One new mole of extent corresponds to twice the previous transformation, so standard free energy doubles. Every activity exponent also doubles, squaring the ratio. The physical equilibrium composition remains consistent with both notations; quantities referenced to equation normalisation change, not the mixture itself.
