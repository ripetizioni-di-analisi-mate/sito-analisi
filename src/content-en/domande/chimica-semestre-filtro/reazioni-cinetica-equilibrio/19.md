For $2\mathrm A+3\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm C$, $0.40\,\mathrm{mol}$ of A is consumed without other reactions. Which pair gives extent and B moles consumed?

- $\xi=0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$; B consumed $0.60\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $\xi=0.80\,\mathrm{mol}$; B consumed $0.60\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $\xi=0.40\,\mathrm{mol}$; B consumed $1.20\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $\xi=0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$; B consumed $0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$.

## Explanation

A consumption equals $2\xi$, so extent is half the measured consumption. B consumption is then $3\xi$. Extent is a shared variable, but each species' change includes its coefficient. C formation would be $0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$, also identifying which amount equals extent.
