A rapid pre-equilibrium $\mathrm A+\mathrm B\rightleftharpoons\mathrm I$ maintains $[I]=K_c[A][B]$. The slow elementary step $\mathrm I\longrightarrow\mathrm P$ follows with rate $k_2[I]$. Which overall law follows in this approximation?

- $v=k_2([A]+[B])$.
- $v=\dfrac{k_2}{K_c}[A][B]$.
- $v=k_2K_c[A][B]$.
- $v=k_2[A]$ because the slow step is unimolecular.

## Explanation

Substitute intermediate concentration into the P-forming step's law. Dependence on A and B arises from the pre-equilibrium supplying I, even though the slow step involves only one I entity. Overall order is therefore two in this model. The conclusion requires the pre-equilibrium approximation to remain valid as I is consumed. Dividing by $K_c$ would invert the assigned relation for I.
