In $-\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}=k$, concentrations are in $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and time in seconds. Which units must k have?

- $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
- $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,s}$.
- $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$.
- $\mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

In a zero-order law, the constant has concentration-rate dimensions: amount per volume per time. Inverse seconds alone belong to a first-order constant; litres per mole instead appear for second order. Multiplication by seconds would be incompatible with the derivative on the left.
