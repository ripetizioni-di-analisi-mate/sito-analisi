Measured activities give a solution $Q_{\mathrm{ps}}=3K_{\mathrm{ps}}$, yet it remains clear for several minutes. Which explanation is consistent?

- K must rise over time to reach the ion product at unchanged temperature.
- Salt dissolution is favoured because Q exceeds K.
- Precipitation is favoured but nucleation may be slow.
- No visible crystals necessarily proves $Q_{\mathrm{ps}}\le K_{\mathrm{ps}}$.

## Explanation

An ion product above the constant describes a thermodynamic tendency to form solid. Initiating a crystal nevertheless requires a sufficiently stable nucleus, and this barrier may cause a delay. Clarity therefore does not negate activity data. Nucleation time and saturation condition are different information.
