Over equal intervals, a concentration changes from $100$ to $50$, then $25$ and finally $12.5$ in the same units. At constant volume without input, which simple model describes these data?

- Zero-order disappearance with constant k.
- Net production at constant rate.
- Second-order disappearance with constant half-lives in every interval.
- First-order disappearance with constant k.

## Explanation

Each interval retains half the preceding concentration. First order gives this constant ratio with half-life independent of initial value. Zero order would give equal decrements, whereas decrements here decrease. In simple second order, halving instead takes progressively longer as concentration decreases.
