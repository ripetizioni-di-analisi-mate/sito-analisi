During a CO2-releasing reaction, the mass of an open vessel and its contents decreases. Which measurement would directly test overall chemical mass conservation?

- Use liquid volume as a substitute for total mass.
- Compare only solid masses before and after.
- Include gas that left the vessel in the balance.
- Treat the entire mass decrease as destroyed reactant mass.

## Explanation

The chosen boundary excludes gaseous matter leaving the vessel. Collecting and measuring it permits comparison of all initial and final substances. Considering only solids excludes liquid and gas; volume does not equal mass without composition and density. Loss from the vessel is therefore compatible with conservation in the complete material system.
