The model explicitly specifies $2\mathrm A+\mathrm B\longrightarrow\mathrm P$ as one elementary step. Which pair is consistent with this assumption?

- Molecularity three; dependence $v=k([A]+[B])$.
- Molecularity one; dependence $v=k[P]$.
- Molecularity two; dependence $v=k[A][B]$.
- Molecularity three; dependence $v=k[A]^2[B]$.

## Explanation

The stated event involves two A entities and one B, three in total. Under this elementary-step assumption, encounter probability gives the concentration product with corresponding multiplicities. Counting only the names A and B confuses distinct species with entity number. The conclusion depends on the explicit elementary-step assumption and does not extend to every identical overall equation.
