The same solid mass reacts faster when powdered at equal temperature and liquid composition. Which explanation is most direct?

- K of the same reaction necessarily increases because mass is subdivided.
- Molar mass decreases in proportion to grain size.
- Accessible surface area for heterogeneous reaction increases.
- Powdering necessarily lowers the intrinsic activation energy of every reactive site.

## Explanation

Powdering exposes more solid–liquid interface, making more sites available for surface steps. Total mass, chemical formula and molar mass can remain unchanged. For the same macroscopic phase and temperature, this rate effect does not imply a different equilibrium constant. Increasing the number of accessible sites does not require changing the intrinsic barrier at each site. This distinguishes sample geometry from molecular and thermodynamic properties.
