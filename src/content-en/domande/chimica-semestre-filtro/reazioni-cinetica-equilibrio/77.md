Two genuinely coupled transformations have standard Gibbs changes of $+20$ and $-35\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ for compatible reaction amounts at the same temperature. What is the overall standard change?

- $-15\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $+15\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $+55\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $-55\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Energies add with their signs: $20+(-35)=-15$. The favourable contribution exceeds the unfavourable one in the combined transformation's standard reference. Adding magnitudes would lose each reaction's directional meaning. Direction in an actual mixture also requires activities and its Q; the question specifically concerns standard change.
