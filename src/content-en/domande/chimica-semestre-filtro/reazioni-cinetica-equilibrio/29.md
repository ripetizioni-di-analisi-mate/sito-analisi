An elementary step is $\mathrm A\longrightarrow\mathrm B+\mathrm C$. What is its molecularity?

- Unimolecular because one reacting entity participates.
- Termolecular because three species appear in the equation.
- Bimolecular because two product entities form.
- Zero order by definition because there is no second reactant.

## Explanation

Molecularity counts entities participating in the event on the reactant side, not product number or total written species. The stated step involves one A entity. Having one reactant does not remove concentration dependence; an elementary unimolecular model uses a first-order law.
