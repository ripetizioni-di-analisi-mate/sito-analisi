With approximately constant standard enthalpy, a plot of $\ln K$ against $\dfrac1T$ has negative slope. What does this indicate for the reaction direction defining K?

- $\Delta_rH^\circ>0$, hence an endothermic direction.
- No temperature dependence of K.
- $\Delta_rH^\circ<0$, hence an exothermic direction.
- Negative activation energy for the forward reaction.

## Explanation

The integrated van't Hoff relation gives slope $-\dfrac{\Delta_rH^\circ}{R}$ against reciprocal temperature. A negative slope therefore implies positive enthalpy. Activation energy instead comes from a plot of kinetic ln k, not thermodynamic ln K. A zero slope would indicate absence of the considered dependence within the approximation.
