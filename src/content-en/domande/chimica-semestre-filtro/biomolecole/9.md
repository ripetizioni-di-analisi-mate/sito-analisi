In lactose the glucose anomeric carbon remains free; in sucrose both anomeric carbons form the bond. Which comparison applies before hydrolysis?

- Both nonreducing because they are disaccharides.
- Lactose nonreducing; sucrose reducing.
- Lactose reducing; sucrose nonreducing.
- Both reducing because they have free OH groups.

## Explanation

The free hemiacetal in lactose can open to the aldehyde form, making the disaccharide reducing. Sucrose locks both anomeric centres as acetal and ketal; hydrolysis is needed to release the reducing monosaccharides. The other OH groups alone do not allow opening to the anomeric carbonyl.
