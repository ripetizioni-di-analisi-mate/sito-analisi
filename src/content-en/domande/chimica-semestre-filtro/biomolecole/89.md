In a solution containing only GSH and a compatible oxidant, GSH consumption is measured even without proteins. What does the result directly demonstrate?

- A nonenzymatic reaction is possible for that reagent pair.
- The reaction occurs at the same rate against every oxidant.
- Glutathione peroxidases can never use GSH.
- The uncatalysed reaction must have the same rate as the enzymatic route.

## Explanation

The protein-free system shows that this transformation can occur directly between reagents. The observation establishes neither a universal rate nor exclusion of catalysed routes in other systems. GSH is a small thiol molecule that can participate directly or as an enzyme substrate: both possibilities are compatible.
