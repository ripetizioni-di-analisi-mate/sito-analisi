Arginine’s side chain ends in guanidinium. Which representation best describes its charge at pH 7?

- Positive charge rigidly localised on the nitrogen bearing + in one resonance contributor.
- A neutral group identical to the asparagine amide.
- Positive charge delocalised across the three-nitrogen group.
- Neutral guanidinium because pH 7 is below its pKa.

## Explanation

Arginine guanidinium is mainly protonated at pH 7, and resonance distributes its charge within the nitrogen-containing group. Being below its pKa favours the protonated form, not the neutral one. This favours interactions with anions such as phosphates and carboxylates. A single drawing with one double bond represents a resonance contributor; it does not confine the entire charge rigidly to one nitrogen.
