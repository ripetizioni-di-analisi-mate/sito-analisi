Initial vitamin D₃ production from 7-dehydrocholesterol involves photochemical opening of a ring. Which structural inference is justified?

- Opening necessarily preserves the number of rings.
- The derivative retains its sterol origin but not all four intact rings.
- The transformation saturates every double bond without changing the skeleton.
- The transformation consists only of esterifying the C3 OH.

## Explanation

The transformation opens one ring of the polycyclic skeleton, followed by further rearrangements. A sterol origin therefore does not imply retention of all four closed rings. Simple OH esterification or double-bond hydrogenation would retain ring count and does not describe the specified change.
