A Tyr → Phe substitution removes the phenolic OH while retaining the aromatic ring. Which direct interaction is removed from the side chain?

- A permanent negative phenol charge at pH 7.
- Hydrogen-bond donation through the phenolic OH.
- Every interaction of the ring with other aromatic systems.
- All side-chain van der Waals interactions.

## Explanation

Tyrosine has an O–H able to donate a hydrogen bond; phenylalanine has H instead. The ring remains and retains aromatic and dispersion interactions. Phenol is mainly neutral near pH 7, so no permanent negative charge is removed. The overall folding effect depends on context.
