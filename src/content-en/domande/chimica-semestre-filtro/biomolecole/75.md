A canonical DNA duplex contains 100 base pairs, 42 G–C and the remainder A–T. How many hydrogen bonds join the paired bases in the model?

- $258$.
- $200$.
- $242$.
- $300$.

## Explanation

There are $100-42=58$ A–T pairs. Counting three bonds per G–C and two per A–T gives $3(42)+2(58)=242$. The value 258 swaps the contributions of the two classes. The calculation counts only interbase bonds; overall stability also includes stacking and medium conditions.
