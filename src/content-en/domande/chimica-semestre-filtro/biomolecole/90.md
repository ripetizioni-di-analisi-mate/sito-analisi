After enzyme-free incubation, a protein covalently acquires a glucose-derived unit and a lysine loses reactivity towards a free-amine marker. Which mechanism is consistent with both observations?

- Lysine N-acetylation without incorporation of a sugar portion.
- Simple reversible ionic association without amine modification.
- Glycation of the side-chain amino function.
- O-glycosylation of serine alone while leaving the lysine amine intact.

## Explanation

A glucose carbonyl can react with the lysine side-chain amine, initially forming a Schiff base and subsequent products. The amine is modified and the protein gains a sugar-derived portion. Ionic association alone does not explain a stable covalent amine modification; the data are consistent with glycation without identifying every final product.
