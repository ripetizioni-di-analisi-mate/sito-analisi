The half-reaction $2\mathrm{RSH}\to\mathrm{RSSR}+2\mathrm{H^+}+2e^-$ forms one disulfide. If $3\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of bridges form, what amount of electrons must the oxidant accept?

- $3\,\mathrm{mmol}$.
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mmol}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{mmol}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{mmol}$.

## Explanation

The half-reaction loses two electrons for each bridge formed, so $n(e^-)=2(3)=6\,\mathrm{mmol}$. The coefficient two before the thiols expresses the two units required per bridge; it must not be multiplied again by the electron count. The complete reaction requires a reduction consuming these electrons.
