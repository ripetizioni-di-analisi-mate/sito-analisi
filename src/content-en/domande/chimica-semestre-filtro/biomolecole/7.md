In the conventional Haworth projection of D-glucopyranose, $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ and the C1 OH are both above the ring. Which assignment is correct?

- α-D-glucopyranose, with both groups on the same side.
- β-D-glucopyranose, with both groups on the same side.
- β-L-glucopyranose, because the anomeric OH points up.
- α-L-glucopyranose, because cyclisation reverses the series.

## Explanation

The β configuration of D-glucopyranose places the anomeric OH and terminal $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ on the same side. The α anomer places them on opposite sides. Ring closure generates C1 configuration but retains C5 configuration, which determines the D/L series: changing anomer does not change series.
