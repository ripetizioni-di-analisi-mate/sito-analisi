Under the same conditions, a transformation has $\Delta G=+22\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ and hydrolysis of one ATP has $\Delta G=-31\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. If coupled one to one, what is the net balance?

- $-53\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $+53\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $-9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $+9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Free-energy changes add using the coupling coefficients: $\Delta G_{\mathrm{net}}=22-31=-9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. The negative sign makes the overall process favourable under the stated conditions. It does not determine rate and requires a mechanism actually linking the processes, not merely ATP being present in solution.
