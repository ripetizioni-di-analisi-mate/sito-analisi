A lipid contains sphingosine with an amide-linked fatty acid, but no sugar or phosphocholine head. Which molecule does this describe?

- Cerebroside.
- Ceramide.
- Sphingomyelin.
- Phosphatidylcholine.

## Explanation

Ceramide is the backbone formed by sphingosine and an amide-linked fatty acid. Sphingomyelin requires a phosphocholine head, whereas a cerebroside adds a single sugar to ceramide. Phosphatidylcholine instead uses glycerol with two acyl residues: backbone identity distinguishes these classes.
