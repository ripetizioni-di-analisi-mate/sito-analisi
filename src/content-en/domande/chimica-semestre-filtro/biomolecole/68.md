In the N-glycosidic bond of a canonical nucleoside, which base atom attaches to pentose C1′?

- N1 in purines; N3 in pyrimidines.
- N1 in purines; N9 in pyrimidines.
- N7 in purines; N3 in pyrimidines.
- N9 in purines; N1 in pyrimidines.

## Explanation

The canonical bond uses sugar C1′ and a ring nitrogen: N9 for adenine and guanine, N1 for cytosine, thymine and uracil. Other nitrogens have different positions and roles in the ring. The numbers belong to the base, while the prime in C1′ distinguishes pentose numbering.
