In aminolysis of an amino acid methyl ester, the $\mathrm{-OCH_3}$ group is replaced by a second amino acid amino group. Which small molecule accompanies amide formation after proton transfers?

- Methylamine.
- Methanol.
- Water.
- Formic acid.

## Explanation

The leaving group contains the ester oxygen and methyl group; after protonation it gives $\mathrm{CH_3OH}$. Substitution at the carbonyl creates the amide C–N bond. Water appears in the formal balance from two free amino acids, whereas the specified substrate is already esterified: the starting balances differ.
