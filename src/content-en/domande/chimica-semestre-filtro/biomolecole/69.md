In the ribose–Pα–Pβ–Pγ segment of ATP, how are the links to the three phosphates classified?

- Three phosphoesters, one for each phosphate.
- One phosphoester to the sugar and two phosphoanhydrides between phosphates.
- Three phosphoanhydrides, including ribose–Pα.
- Two phosphoesters and one phosphoanhydride.

## Explanation

The α phosphate is esterified to a ribose hydroxyl. The P–O–P bridges between α and β and between β and γ are phosphoanhydrides. The N-glycosidic bond instead joins ribose and adenine. Identifying the oxygen bridge’s partners avoids confusing nucleic-acid phosphodiesters with ATP anhydrides.
