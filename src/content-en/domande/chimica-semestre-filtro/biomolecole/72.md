Which transfer to the nicotinamide group describes NAD⁺ → NADH reduction?

- A proton without electrons.
- A hydride, equivalent to two electrons and one proton.
- Two protons without electron transfer.
- A hydrogen atom with only one electron.

## Explanation

NAD⁺ receives hydride at the nicotinamide centre, giving the balance $\mathrm{NAD^++H^++2e^-\to NADH}$. A proton alone changes protonation without supplying reduction electrons. An H atom has one electron, whereas hydride has two: this distinction determines the redox balance.
