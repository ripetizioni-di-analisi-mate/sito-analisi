One chain contains eight cysteines, all paired into internal disulfides. Only the disulfides are reduced, without peptide-bond hydrolysis. Which result is certain?

- Eight bridges are broken and residue order is unchanged.
- Four bridges are broken and conformation necessarily stays unchanged.
- Four bridges are broken and five peptide chains form.
- Four bridges are broken and residue order is unchanged.

## Explanation

Each disulfide joins two cysteines, so there are $\dfrac{8}{2}=4$ bridges. Reduction affects side-chain S–S links while leaving the peptide backbone continuous. The sequence stays the same, whereas folding may change because covalent constraints are removed; conserving sequence does not guarantee conserving shape.
