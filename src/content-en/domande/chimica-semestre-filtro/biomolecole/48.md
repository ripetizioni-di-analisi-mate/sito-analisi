A hyaluronan fragment contains four disaccharide units, each with one carboxylate and no sulfate. Assuming all other groups are neutral, what is its net charge?

- $-8$.
- $+4$.
- $0$.
- $-4$.

## Explanation

Each unit contributes charge $-1$ from glucuronate, so the total is $4(-1)=-4$. N-Acetylglucosamine is neutral under the assumptions given. Absence of sulfate does not imply absence of charge: carboxylates alone contribute to hydration and interaction with cations.
