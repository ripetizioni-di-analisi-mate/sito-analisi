The C1 aldehyde of D-glucose is selectively oxidised while the C6 primary alcohol remains unchanged. Which product forms?

- D-Sorbitol.
- D-Glucuronic acid.
- D-Glucaric acid.
- D-Gluconic acid.

## Explanation

The aldehyde becomes a carboxyl group at C1, producing gluconic acid. Oxidising only C6 would give glucuronic acid; oxidising both ends would give glucaric acid, an aldaric acid. Sorbitol instead comes from reducing the carbonyl to an alcohol. The stated selectivity distinguishes the three oxidised products.
