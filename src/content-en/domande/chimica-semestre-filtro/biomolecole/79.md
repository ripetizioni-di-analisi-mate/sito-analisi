Only one hydrogen bond between two bases in a duplex is broken, while covalent backbones remain intact. Which statement is correct?

- The number of strand 3′-OH ends increases by one.
- A phosphodiester link is necessarily hydrolysed.
- Nucleotide order in the strands remains unchanged.
- The strand necessarily loses a terminal nucleotide.

## Explanation

Hydrogen bonds contribute to base pairing and are distinct from covalent links along a strand. Breaking one can locally alter pairing without changing sequence, generating a new end or releasing a nucleotide. These latter outcomes would require covalent backbone cleavage, excluded by the question.
