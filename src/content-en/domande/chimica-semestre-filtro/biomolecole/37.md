In a model containing only α and β anomers, their specific rotations under the same conditions are $112$ and $19$ in the same unit. The mixture gives $56.2$, and rotation is additive. What is the α fraction?

- $0.50$.
- $0.20$.
- $0.40$.
- $0.60$.

## Explanation

If $x$ is the α fraction, the β fraction is $1-x$. The weighted average obeys $112x+19(1-x)=56.2$, so $x=\dfrac{56.2-19}{112-19}=0.40$. The fraction $0.60$ belongs to β. The model neglects the small open-chain population and requires identical measurement conditions.
