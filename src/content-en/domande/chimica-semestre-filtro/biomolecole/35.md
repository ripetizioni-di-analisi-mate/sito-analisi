An open-chain aldohexose has four independent stereocentres and no internal symmetry making different configurations identical. How many stereoisomers are possible in this model?

- $4$.
- $16$.
- $8$.
- $32$.

## Explanation

Each stereocentre allows two configurations. Four independent centres give $2^4=16$ combinations; the absence of symmetry equivalences allows all to be counted. The open-chain carbonyl is planar and does not add a fifth stereocentre: the anomeric centre appears only after cyclisation.
