In a regular α-helix, the carbonyl of residue 8 forms the typical hydrogen bond with which group?

- Backbone N–H of residue 12.
- The side chain of residue 12, whatever its identity.
- Backbone carbonyl of residue 4.
- Backbone N–H of residue 9.

## Explanation

The local α-helix pattern connects residue $i$ C=O to residue $i+4$ N–H, so $8+4=12$. The carbonyl oxygen is the acceptor and N–H the donor. Two carbonyls are both acceptors in this model, and the typical bond does not require a particular side chain.
