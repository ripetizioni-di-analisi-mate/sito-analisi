The thiols of $0.040\,\mathrm{mol}$ of GSH are completely oxidised, with GSSG as the only sulfur-containing product. How many moles of GSSG form?

- $0.020\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.040\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.010\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.080\,\mathrm{mol}$.

## Explanation

One GSSG contains two glutathione units joined by a disulfide. Conserving these units requires $n(\mathrm{GSSG})=\dfrac{0.040}{2}=0.020\,\mathrm{mol}$. There are two sulfur atoms per disulfide, so the moles of sulfur atoms are conserved even though the number of molecules is halved.
