In the organisation of the main extracellular-matrix GAGs, which is synthesised without covalent attachment to a core protein?

- Dermatan sulfate.
- Chondroitin sulfate.
- Heparan sulfate.
- Hyaluronan.

## Explanation

Many GAGs form proteoglycans through covalent attachment to a core protein. Hyaluronan is an exception: it can organise aggregates through interactions with proteins without covalent attachment to a core protein. The distinction concerns the protein linkage, not the general ability to associate with other molecules.
