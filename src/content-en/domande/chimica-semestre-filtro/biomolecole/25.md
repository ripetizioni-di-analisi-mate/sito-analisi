Leucine and isoleucine have the same molecular formula, but their $R$ groups are respectively $\mathrm{CH_2CH(CH_3)_2}$ and $\mathrm{CH(CH_3)CH_2CH_3}$. What is their relationship?

- Epimers differing only at Cα.
- Constitutional isomers with different branching.
- Enantiomers with identical connectivity.
- Conformers interconverted by a single rotation.

## Explanation

The branched carbon occupies a different position relative to Cα: which atom is connected to which changes. This connectivity difference defines constitutional isomers. Rotation preserves all bonds between the same atoms; enantiomers and epimers also require identical connectivity, a condition not met here.
