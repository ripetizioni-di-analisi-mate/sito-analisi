Two amino acids have $pI_A=3.0$ and $pI_B=9.0$. In electrophoresis at pH 6, neglecting solvent flow, how do they initially migrate?

- A towards the positive electrode; B towards the negative one.
- Both stationary because pH 6 lies between the values.
- Both towards the positive electrode.
- A towards the negative electrode; B towards the positive one.

## Explanation

For A, pH exceeds $pI$, so mean charge is negative and attraction is towards the positive electrode. For B, pH is below $pI$, so charge is positive and migration is opposite. Being between two isoelectric points does not separately cancel the charge of either species.
