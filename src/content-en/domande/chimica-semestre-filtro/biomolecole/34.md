An entire Fischer projection is rotated by $180^\circ$ in the page plane, without exchanging individual groups. What happens to the represented molecular configuration?

- All stereocentres necessarily invert.
- Only the centre furthest from the carbonyl changes.
- The molecule becomes a constitutional isomer.
- It remains the same, although its page arrangement changes.

## Explanation

A $180^\circ$ rotation retains the horizontal or vertical nature of each bond: bonds towards the observer remain horizontal. Configuration therefore does not change. To apply the D/L rule directly, the Fischer projection can be returned to the conventional carbonyl-at-top orientation; page position alone does not change the molecule.
