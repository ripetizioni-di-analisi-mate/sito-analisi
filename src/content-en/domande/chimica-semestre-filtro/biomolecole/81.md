Which product pair results from deamination of cytosine and 5-methylcytosine, respectively?

- Thymine and uracil.
- Uracil and thymine.
- Uracil and uracil.
- Cytosine and thymine.

## Explanation

Deamination replaces the exocyclic amino function with a carbonyl. Cytosine gives uracil; when a C5 methyl is already present, it remains and the product is 5-methyluracil, or thymine. The process retains the pyrimidine ring and pre-existing methyl without converting the base into a purine.
