What minimum stoichiometric amount of NaOH is consumed in complete saponification of $0.060\,\mathrm{mol}$ of a pure triacylglycerol?

- $0.180\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.060\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.120\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.020\,\mathrm{mol}$.

## Explanation

The molecule has three ester bonds, each consuming one hydroxide equivalent to give a carboxylate. Thus $n(\mathrm{NaOH})=3(0.060)=0.180\,\mathrm{mol}$. A one-to-one ratio would count the glycerol backbone rather than the hydrolysable groups; the requested amount is that consumed, distinct from any excess used.
