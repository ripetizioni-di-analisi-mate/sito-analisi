Two glycosphingolipids share the same ceramide. One has a single glucose; the other has an oligosaccharide containing sialic acid. Which comparison is correct?

- The first is a cerebroside; the second a ganglioside.
- Both are cerebrosides because every sugar head defines that class.
- Both are sphingomyelins because they share ceramide.
- The first is a ganglioside; the second a cerebroside.

## Explanation

A ceramide with a single sugar is a cerebroside. An oligosaccharide containing sialic acid identifies a ganglioside, whose head can carry negative carboxylate charge. The shared backbone does not make the molecules identical: head composition distinguishes glycosphingolipid subclasses.
