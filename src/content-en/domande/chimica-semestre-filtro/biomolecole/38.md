Methyl α-D-glucopyranoside retains the nonanomeric OH groups but has OCH₃ at C1. In water without glycosidic-bond hydrolysis, which prediction is correct?

- It immediately becomes β by simple C1–O bond rotation.
- It mutarotates because every OH can generate a C1 aldehyde.
- It does not show mutarotation through free-glucose ring opening.
- It changes to the L series because C1 is substituted.

## Explanation

The anomeric centre is an acetal, with the ring O and OCH₃: it lacks the free hemiacetal needed for ordinary opening to the aldehyde form. Hydrolysis can restore glucose capable of mutarotation. Conformational rotation around a single bond does not invert C1 configuration or change the D series.
