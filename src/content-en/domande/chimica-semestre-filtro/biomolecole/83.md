Which step propagates peroxidation by regenerating a lipid radical? Here $\mathrm{LH}$ denotes lipid, $\mathrm{LOO^\bullet}$ peroxyl and $\mathrm{TOH}$ a phenolic antioxidant.

- $\mathrm{LOO^\bullet+TOH\to LOOH+TO^\bullet}$.
- $\mathrm{L^\bullet+LOO^\bullet\to LOOL}$.
- $\mathrm{L^\bullet+L^\bullet\to L{-}L}$.
- $\mathrm{LOO^\bullet+LH\to LOOH+L^\bullet}$.

## Explanation

Peroxyl abstracts H from a new lipid, forming a hydroperoxide and a lipid radical that can react with O₂. This propagates the chain. Joining two radicals into nonradical products terminates it; H donation from TOH instead intercepts peroxyl and forms an antioxidant radical without regenerating a lipid radical.
