A cysteine Fischer projection has COOH at the top, $\mathrm{CH_2SH}$ at the bottom, $\mathrm{NH_2}$ on the left and H on the right. Which designation correctly describes the α-carbon?

- D series and S configuration.
- L series and S configuration.
- L series and R configuration.
- D series and R configuration.

## Explanation

The amino group on the left identifies L in the specified Fischer orientation. The CIP priorities are amino group, sulfur-containing chain, carboxyl group and hydrogen. Sulfur outranks oxygen when comparing the chains starting with carbon; accounting for the hydrogen pointing towards the observer gives R. L does not always imply S.
