A student explains favourable ATP hydrolysis by saying that “breaking the bond releases energy.” Which correction is appropriate?

- The reaction is favourable only if new-bond formation is ignored.
- An enzyme makes the hydrolysis free-energy change negative.
- Breaking the bond requires energy; the favourable balance concerns the complete reactants and products.
- Hydrolysis free energy is independent of ATP, ADP and phosphate.

## Explanation

Breaking a bond requires energy input. Hydrolysis also forms bonds and changes stabilisation, solvation and entropy; their overall balance can give $\Delta G<0$. Concentrations and conditions also affect the actual value. “High-energy bond” summarises a property of the transformation, not of cleavage alone.
