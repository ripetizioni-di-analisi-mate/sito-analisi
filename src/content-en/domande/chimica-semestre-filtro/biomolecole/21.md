A healthy adult synthesises tyrosine from phenylalanine but does not produce enough phenylalanine. Which conclusion follows?

- Both are always nonessential because they share a pathway.
- Tyrosine is essential because it contains a phenolic group.
- Phenylalanine is essential; tyrosine synthesis depends on its precursor.
- Phenylalanine is nonessential because it occurs in proteins.

## Explanation

Essential means endogenous synthesis does not meet requirements. Phenylalanine must therefore be available from the diet; tyrosine formation also requires this substrate. Aromatic or phenolic character describes reactivity and polarity, whereas essentiality concerns metabolic capacity and the organism’s conditions.
