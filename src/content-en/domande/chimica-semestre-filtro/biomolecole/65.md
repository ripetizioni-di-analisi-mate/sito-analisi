For a monomeric fatty acid in dilute solution, assume $pK_a=4.8$. At pH 7, which form is favoured? Exclude aggregates that alter apparent $pK_a$.

- The forms are equal because pH 7 is near water neutrality.
- Carboxylate, because pH exceeds $pK_a$.
- Undissociated acid, because every tail is hydrophobic.
- The predominant species has two negative charges per carboxyl group.

## Explanation

In the model, $\dfrac{[\mathrm{RCOO^-}]}{[\mathrm{RCOOH}]}=10^{7-4.8}=10^{2.2}$ is much greater than one, so carboxylate predominates. The forms would be equimolar at pH equal to $pK_a$. Tail hydrophobicity does not cancel carboxyl equilibrium; this is why aggregate-free conditions are specified.
