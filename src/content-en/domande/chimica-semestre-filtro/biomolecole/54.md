Two fatty acids have eighteen carbons and one double bond at C9, one cis and one trans. Other conditions being equal, which qualitative comparison is expected?

- The trans form must melt lower because it is less bent.
- Melting temperatures match because the molecular formula is identical.
- The cis form has a straighter chain and necessarily melts higher.
- The cis form tends to pack less regularly and melt at a lower temperature.

## Explanation

Cis geometry places both chain portions on the same side of the double bond, producing a bend that hinders packing. The trans form can align more regularly. The same formula preserves composition and mass but does not guarantee identical solid-state interactions or melting temperature.
