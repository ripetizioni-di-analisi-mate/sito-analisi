A DNA cytosine becomes uracil by deamination and escapes repair. When that strand is copied, which canonical base tends to be inserted opposite uracil?

- Adenine.
- Cytosine.
- Thymine.
- Guanine.

## Explanation

The initial cytosine paired with G; deamination changes its recognition groups, and U tends to pair with A. Copying can therefore fix a G–C → A–T transition in one descendant lineage after further replication. Inserting G as before would ignore the template base’s chemical change.
