Glucosamine undergoes N-acetylation. Which group correctly describes the product at C2?

- $\mathrm{-NH{-}C(=O){-}CH_3}$.
- $\mathrm{-COO^-}$.
- Unchanged $\mathrm{-NH_3^+}$.
- $\mathrm{-O{-}C(=O){-}CH_3}$.

## Explanation

Acetyl attaches to the amino nitrogen, giving an amide with an NH–CO–CH₃ fragment. Attachment through oxygen would describe O-acetylation of a hydroxyl. The new amide has different acid–base properties from the free amine; sugar C2 and the other centres do not change connectivity through this reaction alone.
