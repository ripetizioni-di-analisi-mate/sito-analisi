A nucleotide contains uracil, ribose with C2′ OH and a C5′ phosphate. Which name is appropriate?

- Uridine 5′-monophosphate, UMP.
- Deoxyuridine 5′-monophosphate, dUMP.
- Uridine, a nucleoside without phosphate.
- Thymidine 5′-monophosphate, TMP.

## Explanation

Uracil plus ribose forms uridine; the C5′ phosphate adds the 5′-monophosphate designation. C2′ OH excludes the deoxy derivative. Thymine has an additional methyl group compared with uracil, so TMP does not match the specified base. Each component contributes part of the name.
