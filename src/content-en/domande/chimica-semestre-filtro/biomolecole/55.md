A fatty acid is $18{:}2\,\Delta^{9,12}$. Which ω series does it belong to?

- ω-12.
- ω-6.
- ω-2.
- ω-9.

## Explanation

From the methyl end, the first double bond encountered has the highest Δ position, 12. The distance is $18-12=6$, so the series is ω-6. Subtracting 9 would count the other double bond, further from the methyl end; 2 indicates the number of double bonds, not their position.
