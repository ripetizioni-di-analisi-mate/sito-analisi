A phosphatidylcholine has charge $-1$ on its phosphodiester and $+1$ on choline, with neutral tails. Which description is correct?

- Net charge $+1$ because phosphate does not contribute.
- Net charge $-1$ because choline nitrogen is treated as neutral.
- Zero net charge, therefore a completely nonpolar molecule.
- Zero net charge, a polar head and an amphipathic molecule.

## Explanation

The charge sum is $-1+1=0$, but the head retains separated ionic groups that interact with water. Choline contains a quaternary ammonium whose positive charge must be included in the balance. The tails remain hydrophobic, so the molecule is amphipathic. Zero net charge and absence of polarity are different properties: a zwitterionic head can still be strongly hydrated.
