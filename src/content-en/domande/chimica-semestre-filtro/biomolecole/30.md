For linear peptide Asp-Lys-Ala at pH 7, assume N-terminus $+1$, C-terminus $-1$, Asp side chain $-1$ and Lys side chain $+1$. Other groups are neutral. What is the model’s net charge?

- $-1$.
- $+2$.
- $0$.
- $+1$.

## Explanation

Only the specified ionised groups are summed: $q=(+1)+(-1)+(-1)+(+1)=0$. Internal α-amino and α-carboxyl groups form amides, so they are not counted as free ends of three separate amino acids. Zero net charge still leaves four charged groups in this model.
