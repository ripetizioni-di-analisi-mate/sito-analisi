In open-chain fructose, the C5 OH attacks the C2 carbonyl. Which description of the resulting ring is correct?

- Five-membered hemiketal; anomeric C2.
- Six-membered hemiketal; anomeric C5.
- Six-membered hemiacetal; anomeric C1.
- Five-membered acetal without an anomeric OH.

## Explanation

The ring path includes C2, C3, C4, C5 and the oxygen of the attacking OH: five atoms. The starting carbonyl is a ketone, so the product is a hemiketal and C2 becomes anomeric. C1 remains outside the ring; no second external alcohol has converted the hemiketal to a ketal.
