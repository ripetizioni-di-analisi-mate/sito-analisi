An open-chain monosaccharide has five carbons and structure $\mathrm{CH_2OH{-}CO{-}(CHOH)_2{-}CH_2OH}$. How is it classified?

- Aldopentose.
- Aldohexose.
- Ketopentose.
- Ketohexose.

## Explanation

The carbonyl is internal and bonded to two carbons, so it is a ketone. The count is five carbons: one in each terminal CH₂OH, one in the carbonyl and two in CHOH groups. It is therefore a ketopentose. The class name combines carbonyl type with total carbon count.
