A triacylglycerol contains two palmitoyl residues and one oleoyl residue. Complete saponification of $0.10\,\mathrm{mol}$ forms which carboxylate amounts?

- $0.30\,\mathrm{mol}$ of each carboxylate.
- $0.10\,\mathrm{mol}$ of each carboxylate.
- $0.10\,\mathrm{mol}$ palmitate and $0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$ oleate.
- $0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$ palmitate and $0.10\,\mathrm{mol}$ oleate.

## Explanation

Hydrolysis preserves each acyl-chain identity. Each mole of lipid releases two moles of palmitate and one of oleate, so the amounts are $2(0.10)=0.20$ and $0.10\,\mathrm{mol}$. The total $0.30\,\mathrm{mol}$ corresponds to three esters per molecule and checks the count.
