In γ-glutamyl-cysteinyl-glycine glutathione, which combination describes the glutamate–cysteine linkage?

- Side-chain γ carboxyl; amide bond.
- α carboxyl; amide bond.
- Side-chain γ carboxyl; ester bond.
- α carboxyl; ester bond.

## Explanation

The γ prefix indicates use of glutamate’s side-chain carboxyl, while its α carboxyl remains available. The linkage is still an amide. Cysteine’s side-chain thiol has a separate redox role: GSSG formation joins two already assembled molecules through their sulfur atoms.
