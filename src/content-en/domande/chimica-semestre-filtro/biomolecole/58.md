After saponification, the medium is acidified well below the fatty-acid $pK_a$. Which transformation predominates for the tail products?

- The carboxyl group is reduced to a primary alcohol.
- Carboxylates are protonated to carboxylic acids.
- Carboxylates necessarily become glycerol esters.
- Carboxylates lose CO₂ and shorten every tail by one carbon.

## Explanation

Acidification shifts $\mathrm{RCOO^-+H^+\rightleftharpoons RCOOH}$ towards the protonated form. Acid–base state changes while the carbon chain remains the same. Ester re-formation, decarboxylation and carboxyl reduction require further transformations: they do not follow from carboxylate protonation alone.
