Reduction of the fructose carbonyl can yield sorbitol and mannitol. Why are two stereoisomeric products possible?

- Rotation around an already tetrahedral C2 generates two new stable conformers.
- Reduction of C1 to CH₂OH creates two configurations at that carbon.
- Reduction necessarily changes every existing stereocentre.
- Planar carbonyl C2 becomes a new stereocentre.

## Explanation

The C2 carbonyl is planar; reduction makes it a carbon bearing OH, H and two different chain portions. The two possible configurations at this new centre give the two polyols while retaining the other centres. C1 is already CH₂OH and, with two identical hydrogens, does not become a stereocentre. Simple conformational rotation does not interconvert the two configurations at the new C2.
