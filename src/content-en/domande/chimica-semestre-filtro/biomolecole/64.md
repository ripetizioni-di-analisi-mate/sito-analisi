In two membranes with different compositions and temperatures, cholesterol has different effects on tail mobility. Which conclusion avoids an unjustified generalisation?

- Cholesterol always increases every membrane’s fluidity.
- Temperature is irrelevant whenever a sterol is present.
- The effect depends on membrane state and conditions.
- Cholesterol always decreases every membrane’s fluidity.

## Explanation

Sterol rings can restrict some motions, while insertion among tails can hinder ordered packing under other conditions. The net effect depends on temperature and composition, so the results do not support a universal direction. A mobility measurement must be interpreted with the examined membrane’s physical state.
