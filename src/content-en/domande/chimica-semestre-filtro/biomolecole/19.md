An internal proline replaces alanine in a peptide segment. Which local property changes directly because of proline structure?

- The ring makes the amide C–N bond freely rotatable.
- Cα becomes achiral because the two ring paths are identical.
- The internal nitrogen retains an extra donor H compared with alanine.
- The side chain closes onto nitrogen and restricts its geometry.

## Explanation

Proline has a ring including the backbone nitrogen, so it constrains the available angles. When internal, its amide nitrogen lacks the usual donor H. The chain retains amide bonds and a stereogenic Cα: substitution changes conformational and hydrogen-bonding possibilities without changing the bond class.
