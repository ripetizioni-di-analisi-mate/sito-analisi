At pH 7, a lysine side chain changes from ammonium to a mainly neutral N-acetyl amide. What local charge change is expected?

- Formation of a carboxylate with charge $-1$.
- No change, because nitrogen remains present.
- Loss of one positive charge unit.
- Gain of one positive charge unit.

## Explanation

The protonated lysine amine carries charge $+1$. In the amide, the nitrogen lone pair is delocalised towards the carbonyl and the group is mainly neutral under the stated conditions. Thus the change is $0-(+1)=-1$. Acetylation adds an acyl group to nitrogen; it does not create a free carboxylate.
