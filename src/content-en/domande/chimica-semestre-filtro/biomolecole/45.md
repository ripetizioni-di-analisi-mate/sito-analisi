In the open-chain Amadori product derived from an aldose, the initial segment is $\mathrm{R^{\prime}{-}NH{-}CH_2{-}C(=O){-}R}$. What change from the Schiff base has occurred?

- C1 becomes carboxylic and C2 retains OH.
- C1 loses nitrogen and returns to the initial aldehyde.
- C1 remains aldehydic and the amine moves to C2.
- C1 bears the amine and C2 has become ketonic.

## Explanation

The rearrangement converts the aldimine into a ketoamine. The first carbon, initially aldehydic, remains attached to nitrogen and becomes CH₂; the carbonyl appears at the second carbon. The process retains the carbon skeleton and attachment to the amino group. Regenerating the free aldehyde would instead describe Schiff-base hydrolysis.
