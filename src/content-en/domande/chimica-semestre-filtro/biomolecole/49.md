A GAG contains galactose and sulfated N-acetylglucosamine, without uronic acid. Which conclusion is correct?

- It is compatible with keratan sulfate.
- It is compatible with hyaluronan.
- It is compatible with heparan sulfate.
- It is compatible with chondroitin sulfate.

## Explanation

Keratan sulfate contains galactose and sulfated N-acetylglucosamine, without the uronic acid typical of many other GAGs. Hyaluronan, chondroitin sulfate and heparan sulfate instead include uronic units. Hyaluronan is also unsulfated. Composition and substitutions therefore distinguish these GAGs without treating uronate as universal.
