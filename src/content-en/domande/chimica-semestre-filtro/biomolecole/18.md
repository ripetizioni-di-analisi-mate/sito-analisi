A $120\,\mathrm{kDa}$ complex dissociates into four intact $30\,\mathrm{kDa}$ chains without changing their sequences. Which level is certainly lost upon complete dissociation?

- Quaternary association.
- All tertiary structure of the chains.
- All secondary-structure segments.
- The primary sequence of each chain.

## Explanation

The data show separation of four subunits: $4(30)=120\,\mathrm{kDa}$. Quaternary association is therefore lost. Sequences remain intact by assumption; dissociation alone does not establish whether individual chains retain all or some of their helices and folding. Loss of the other levels is not required by the data.
