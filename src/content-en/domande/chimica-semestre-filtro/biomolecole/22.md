For an isolated imidazolium group, assume $pK_a=6.0$. At pH 7 in dilute solution, what is the protonated fraction according to Henderson–Hasselbalch?

- $\dfrac{1}{11}$, about $9.1\%$.
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$, or $1\%$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$, or $50\%$.
- $\dfrac{10}{11}$, about $90.9\%$.

## Explanation

The base/acid ratio is $10^{7-6}=10$. Out of eleven total units, ten are deprotonated and one protonated, so the protonated fraction is $\dfrac{1}{1+10}=\dfrac{1}{11}$. The ratio $\dfrac{1}{10}$ compares two forms; a fraction instead uses the total of both forms in the denominator.
