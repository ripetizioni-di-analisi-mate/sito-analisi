To regenerate two GSH from one GSSG, which half-reaction is balanced for atoms and charge?

- $\mathrm{GSSG+2H^++2e^-\to2GSH}$.
- $\mathrm{GSSG+H^++e^-\to2GSH}$.
- $\mathrm{GSSG\to2GSH+2H^++2e^-}$.
- $\mathrm{GSSG+2e^-\to2GSH}$.

## Explanation

The disulfide must gain two hydrogens to restore two thiols. Two protons and two electrons supply these atoms without changing the half-reaction’s overall charge: $+2-2=0$. The correctly reversed half-reaction is $\mathrm{2GSH\to GSSG+2H^++2e^-}$, an oxidation. Keeping GSSG on the left while moving protons and electrons to the right does not correctly reverse the reaction and does not conserve hydrogen.
