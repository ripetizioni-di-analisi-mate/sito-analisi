What is the molar mass of linear tripeptide Ala-Ala-Ala if $M(\mathrm{Ala})=89.09\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ and $M(\mathrm{H_2O})=18.02\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$?

- $213.21\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $231.23\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $249.25\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $267.27\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Three residues in a linear chain require two peptide bonds. The composition balance is therefore $M=3(89.09)-2(18.02)=231.23\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. Subtracting three waters would describe an additional cyclic closure; subtracting one would count only one link. This formal subtraction does not specify the synthesis mechanism.
