A hydroxyl radical abstracts H from DNA deoxyribose. Which consequence is chemically plausible after subsequent reactions?

- Sugar damage with possible backbone cleavage.
- Only reversible loss of a hydrogen bond, with unchanged sugar.
- Selective cytosine deamination as the only possible outcome.
- Obligatory conversion of deoxyribose into intact ribose.

## Explanation

H abstraction leaves a sugar radical; reactions with oxygen and rearrangements can destabilise the residue and cause strand breaks. Oxidative DNA damage can affect sugars and bases with different outcomes. The specified attack does not determine one obligatory base substitution.
