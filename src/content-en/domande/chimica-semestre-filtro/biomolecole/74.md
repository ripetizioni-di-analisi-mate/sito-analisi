A system transfers $40\,\mathrm{nmol}$ of electrons exclusively to NAD⁺, with complete conversion to NADH and no other reductions. How many nanomoles of NADH form?

- $80\,\mathrm{nmol}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{nmol}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{nmol}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{nmol}$.

## Explanation

Each NADH incorporates two electrons relative to NAD⁺. Thus $n(\mathrm{NADH})=\dfrac{40}{2}=20\,\mathrm{nmol}$. This does not divide by two protons, since the NAD half-reaction contains one; the amount of reduced coenzyme is determined by two electrons per molecule.
