A purified polymer contains only glucose, linear chains and β$(1\to4)$ bonds. Which identification fits all the data?

- Amylose, a homopolysaccharide.
- Hyaluronan, a heteropolysaccharide.
- Glycogen, a homopolysaccharide.
- Cellulose, a homopolysaccharide.

## Explanation

Glucose-only composition makes the polymer a homopolysaccharide. The β$(1\to4)$ bond identifies cellulose: amylose uses α$(1\to4)$, and glycogen adds α$(1\to6)$ branches. Hyaluronan contains two types of modified units. Monomer identity, stereochemistry and branching must therefore be considered together.
