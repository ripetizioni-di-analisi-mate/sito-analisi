A dipeptide with neutral side chains has a protonated N-terminus and a carboxylate C-terminus at pH 7. After esterifying only the C-terminus, what predominant net charge does the model predict?

- $-1$.
- $0$.
- $+1$.
- $+2$.

## Explanation

The C-terminal ester is neutral under the stated conditions, so the carboxylate contribution of $-1$ is removed. The N-terminal group remains at $+1$. The peptide bond is not cleaved by the specified modification, and its amide is not counted as a new protonated amino group.
