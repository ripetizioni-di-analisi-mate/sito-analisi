Complete hydrolysis of compound X releases only adenine and ribose, without phosphate. Which identification is consistent?

- Deoxyadenosine, a deoxynucleoside.
- AMP, a monophosphate nucleotide.
- ATP, a triphosphate nucleotide.
- Adenosine, a nucleoside.

## Explanation

Base plus ribose without phosphate describes a ribonucleoside; with adenine it is adenosine. AMP and ATP contain phosphates, which would appear upon complete hydrolysis. Deoxyadenosine would release deoxyribose, so sugar identity also distinguishes the nucleoside type.
