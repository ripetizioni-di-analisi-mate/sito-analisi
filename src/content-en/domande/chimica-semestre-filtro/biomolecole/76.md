A 20-nucleotide strand is initially linear, then covalently closed into one unbranched ring. How does its number of phosphodiester links between nucleotides change?

- From $19$ to $20$.
- It stays $19$.
- From $38$ to $40$.
- From $20$ to $19$.

## Explanation

The linear strand has $20-1=19$ links between consecutive residues. Closure joins the two ends with one additional link, bringing the total to 20. Each phosphodiester bridge contains two ester bonds of the same phosphate; counting them separately would measure a different quantity from that requested.
