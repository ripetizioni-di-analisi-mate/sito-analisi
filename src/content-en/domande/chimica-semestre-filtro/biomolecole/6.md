Open-chain D-glucose and D-galactose have the same connectivity and differ only in C4 configuration. How are they classified?

- Tautomers with different carbonyl positions.
- Enantiomers with opposite configurations at all centres.
- C4 epimers and diastereomers.
- Anomers with different configurations at C1.

## Explanation

Only one stereocentre differs, so the molecules are epimers. Since the other centres retain their configurations, they are diastereomers rather than complete mirror images. C4 is not the glucose anomeric carbon; the carbonyl remains at C1 in both open-chain forms.
