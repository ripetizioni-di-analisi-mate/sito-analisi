A lipid has sphingosine, an amide-linked fatty acid and a phosphocholine head. Which classification is correct?

- Phosphatidylcholine: a glycerophospholipid.
- Sphingomyelin: both a sphingolipid and a phospholipid.
- Simple ceramide without an additional head.
- Cerebroside: a glycosphingolipid without phosphate.

## Explanation

The sphingosine backbone and amide identify the sphingolipid family; the phosphocholine head identifies sphingomyelin and also justifies including it among phospholipids. These categories describe different features and can overlap. Phosphatidylcholine instead has a glycerol backbone, whereas a cerebroside has a sugar head.
