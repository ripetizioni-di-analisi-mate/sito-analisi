A cholesterol derivative retains the steroid skeleton but has additional polar groups, including a carboxyl. Which bile-acid property is consistent with this structure?

- Absence of hydrophobic portions because a carboxyl group is present.
- The same properties as free cholesterol, independent of the new groups.
- Amphipathicity favouring interaction between aqueous phase and lipids.
- Behaviour as a neutral glycerol triester.

## Explanation

The steroid rings retain a hydrophobic surface, while additional polar groups allow interactions with water and ions. This combination gives bile acids amphipathic character and favours lipid organisation in an aqueous medium. Adding a carboxyl changes properties without removing the molecule’s hydrocarbon portion.
