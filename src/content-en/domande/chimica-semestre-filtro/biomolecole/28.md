Two residues are sought that can directly form a disulfide by oxidation of their thiols. Which pair meets this requirement?

- Cys–Cys.
- Ser–Ser.
- Cys–Met.
- Met–Met.

## Explanation

Cysteine has $\mathrm{-SH}$, so two cysteines can give $\mathrm{-S-S-}$. Methionine contains a thioether whose sulfur is already bonded to two carbons and lacks the required thiol. Serine has OH. The presence of sulfur alone is insufficient: its functional group must be identified.
