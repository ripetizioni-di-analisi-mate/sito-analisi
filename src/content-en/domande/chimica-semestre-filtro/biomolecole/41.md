A glucose transformation oxidises both C1 and C6 to carboxyl groups. Which product class results?

- Polyol with both carbons reduced.
- Aldaric acid.
- Aldonic acid with only C1 oxidised.
- Uronic acid with only C6 oxidised.

## Explanation

Oxidation of both ends creates a dicarboxylic acid in the aldaric-acid class. For glucose this is glucaric acid. Aldonic and uronic acids instead distinguish selective oxidation of only one end. Counting carboxyl groups and locating them identifies the product.
