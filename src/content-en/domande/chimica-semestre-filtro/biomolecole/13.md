Which is the DNA strand complementary to 5′-ACGTA-3′, written from 5′ to 3′?

- 5′-UACGU-3′.
- 5′-TACGT-3′.
- 5′-ATGCA-3′.
- 5′-TGCAT-3′.

## Explanation

A pairs with T and C with G. The aligned complement is 3′-TGCAT-5′; reversing the order to use the requested direction gives 5′-TACGT-3′. TGCAT retains alignment but assigns the wrong ends; ATGCA reverses without complementing. Uracil belongs to the corresponding RNA sequence.
