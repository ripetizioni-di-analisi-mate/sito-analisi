Serine has $R=\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ and threonine $R=\mathrm{CH(OH)CH_3}$. How many stereocentres does each free amino acid have?

- Serine two; threonine one.
- Both one.
- Both two.
- Serine one; threonine two.

## Explanation

Both have four different substituents at Cα. Serine’s side-chain carbon has two H atoms and is not stereogenic. Threonine’s bears OH, H, methyl and the remainder of the amino acid, four different groups, so it adds a second centre. The number of hydroxyl groups alone does not count stereocentres.
