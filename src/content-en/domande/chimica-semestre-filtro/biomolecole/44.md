In a glucose glycation model, accessible primary amines of a protein are chemically blocked while other groups remain unchanged. Which initial event is directly limited?

- Mutarotation of free glucose in water.
- Condensation between sugar carbonyl and protein amine.
- Formation of an O-glycosidic bond at a protein hydroxyl.
- Selective oxidation of glucose to gluconic acid.

## Explanation

Initial glycation requires a nucleophilic amino function and a sugar carbonyl form. Blocking amines reduces the sites available for Schiff-base formation in this model. Free-glucose anomeric equilibrium is a separate process; the result does not establish general inhibition of enzymatic glycosylation.
