A basic amino acid has successive $pK_a$ values $2.20$, $9.00$ and $10.50$, with successive charges $+2,+1,0,-1$. What is the approximate $pI$ using the two equilibria adjacent to the neutral form in dilute solution?

- $9.00$.
- $9.75$.
- $5.60$.
- $6.35$.

## Explanation

The neutral form is bracketed by dissociations with $pK_a=9.00$ and $10.50$, so $pI=\dfrac{9.00+10.50}{2}=9.75$. Averaging the first value with another would include an equilibrium between two positive forms. The approximation neglects the highly protonated form, whose contribution is minimal near this $pI$.
