A nucleophile attacks an ester and a tetrahedral intermediate forms. Which step characterizes a completed nucleophilic acyl substitution?

- Expulsion of group R as a carbanion while retaining the original OR group.
- Restoration of C=O with elimination of a leaving group.
- C=C formation by dehydration at the alpha carbon without restoring C=O.
- Protonation into a stable addition product without loss of the original group.

## Explanation

Addition forms the intermediate, but substitution requires its collapse: O restores the carbonyl and the displaced group leaves. The carbon substituent R normally remains. Stopping at a protonated tetrahedral center would describe addition; alpha-beta dehydration instead belongs to aldol condensation, not the outcome requested here.
