In the enzymatic sequence ethanol → acetaldehyde → acetate, which cofactor description is consistent with substrate oxidation?

- The cofactor only changes pH without participating in redox balance.
- NAD+ is reduced because it donates electrons to the substrate.
- NAD+ accepts reducing equivalents and is reduced to NADH.
- NADH must be oxidized to oxidize that same substrate.

## Explanation

Substrate oxidation must be coupled to reduction of an acceptor. In the indicated reactions, NAD+ receives reducing equivalents and forms NADH: reduction means gaining electrons, not donating them. The cofactor therefore participates in redox balance rather than merely controlling pH.
