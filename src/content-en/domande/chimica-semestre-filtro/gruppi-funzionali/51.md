A simple aldehyde is oxidized without cleaving C–C bonds; the product is then placed in acidic medium. Which group replaces CHO?

- COOH.
- CH(OR)2.
- CH2OH.
- An internal ketone group formed by relocating the carbonyl.

## Explanation

Aldehyde oxidation increases the terminal carbon's oxidation level and produces the carboxyl group. Acid work-up specifies COOH rather than its carboxylate salt. CH2OH would instead be the reduction product; an acetal would require alcohol and acetalization conditions, not the same redox transformation.
