Acetic acid and methylamine are mixed in water at room temperature without activating or dehydrating agents. Which immediate transformation is most reasonable?

- Neutralization that necessarily leaves both reactants uncharged.
- Fischer esterification with nitrogen replacing alcohol oxygen.
- Immediate quantitative formation of neutral amide by mixing alone.
- Proton transfer with predominant formation of an ammonium carboxylate salt.

## Explanation

The acid can protonate the amine, forming acetate and methylammonium. This does not require overcoming the acyl-substitution barriers leading to an amide. Efficient amide formation needs suitable conditions, often carboxyl activation or water removal; the mere presence of both reagents is insufficient.
