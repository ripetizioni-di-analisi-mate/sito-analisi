Acetoacetic acid $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_2COOH}$ is heated under beta-keto acid decarboxylation conditions. Which final products are expected after tautomerization?

- Propanal and CO2.
- Ethanol and two CO2.
- Propanone and CO2.
- Propanoic acid and CO.

## Explanation

The carboxyl carbon is lost as CO2. The remaining three carbons initially form an enol that tautomerizes to the ketone, propanone. The position of the remaining carbonyl determines this outcome. Four carbons are conserved overall, but counting alone would not distinguish propanal from propanone without tracking structure.
