Neutral triethylamine contains no OH. Which hydrogen-bonding description is correct?

- Accepts: no; donates through N–H: no.
- Accepts: no; donates through N–H: yes.
- Accepts: yes; donates through N–H: yes.
- Accepts: yes; donates through N–H: no.

## Explanation

Triethylamine has three C–N bonds and a lone pair, allowing it to accept. With no N–H, it cannot donate through that bond. Protonation would produce another species, triethylammonium, with different charge and properties; the neutral base should not be assigned its conjugate acid's structure.
