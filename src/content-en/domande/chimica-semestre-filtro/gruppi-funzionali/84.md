In Fischer esterification of acetic acid with ethanol $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2{}^{18}OH}$, track the labeled alcohol oxygen while excluding secondary isotope exchange. Where is it found in the ester formed?

- In the ester's carbonyl oxygen.
- In the ester oxygen bonded to the ethyl group.
- In the eliminated water, with no label in the ester.
- Equally distributed between both ester oxygens.

## Explanation

Alcohol oxygen attacks the acyl carbon while retaining its bond to ethyl. The labeled product is $\mathrm{CH_3C(=O){-}^{18}OCH_2CH_3}$. Proton transfers alone do not exchange oxygen nuclei. Under the stated assumption, the label distinguishes substitution at acyl carbon from cleavage of the ethyl–O bond.
