In reversible acid-catalyzed acetalization at constant temperature, which intervention favors the acetal at equilibrium?

- Increase only catalyst amount to change K.
- Omit the alcohol, retaining only aldehyde and water.
- Remove water as the transformation proceeds.
- Add abundant water while keeping everything else unchanged.

## Explanation

Water is a product of acetal formation. Removing it lowers the reaction quotient and favors further conversion toward products. Acid speeds attainment of equilibrium without changing its constant at the same temperature. Adding water instead favors the hydrolysis direction.
