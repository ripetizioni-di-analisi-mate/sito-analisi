For B/BH+ in dilute water at 25 °C, $pK_a(\mathrm{BH^+})=9$ and $pH=8$. Approximating activities by concentrations, what fraction of the total is BH+?

- $\dfrac{10}{11}$.
- Exactly $\dfrac9{10}$.
- $\dfrac1{11}$.
- $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

The B/BH+ ratio is $10^{8-9}=0.1$. For ten parts BH+ there is one part B, so the protonated fraction is ten out of eleven, approximately 90.9%. A ratio between the species is not a fraction of the total; 90% is close but is not the exact value.
