The C5 OH of open-chain glucose attacks the C1 aldehyde. How many atoms belong to the resulting hemiacetal ring?

- Six: five carbons and one oxygen.
- Five: all carbons.
- Seven: six carbons and one oxygen.
- Six: all carbons.

## Explanation

The ring path includes C1, C2, C3, C4, C5 and the attacking OH oxygen. C6 remains outside as part of the side chain. Carbon 1 is the new anomeric center; counting ring atoms is not the same as counting every carbon in the molecule.
