In the balance $\mathrm Q+2\,\mathrm{H^+}+n\,e^-\rightarrow\mathrm{QH_2}$, what is n for complete reduction of quinone to quinol?

- $4$.
- $2$.
- $1$.
- $0$.

## Explanation

The overall balance requires two protons and two electrons. One electron can lead to a semiquinone state, rather than the fully reduced form shown. This count concerns the complete transformation and does not require both electrons to be transferred in the same elementary step.
