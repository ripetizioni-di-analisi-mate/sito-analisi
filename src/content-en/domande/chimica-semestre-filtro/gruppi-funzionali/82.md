N-Methylethanamide $\mathrm{CH_3CONHCH_3}$ is completely hydrolyzed in strongly acidic water with heating. Which product forms are expected in the acidic medium?

- Acetate and methylamine as necessarily predominant forms.
- Acetaldehyde and dimethylamine.
- Methanol and ethanamide.
- Acetic acid and methylammonium.

## Explanation

Hydrolysis cleaves the acyl C–N bond: CH3CO becomes acetic acid and the N-methyl group remains on the released amine. In strongly acidic medium that amine is protonated, giving methylammonium. The treatment neither reduces the carbonyl to an aldehyde nor moves methyl from N to O.
