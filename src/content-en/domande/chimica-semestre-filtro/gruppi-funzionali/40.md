Which groups are present in choline, $\mathrm{HOCH_2CH_2N^+(CH_3)_3}$?

- A secondary alcohol and quaternary ammonium.
- A primary alcohol and a neutral tertiary amine.
- A primary alcohol and trialkylammonium with N–H.
- A primary alcohol and quaternary ammonium.

## Explanation

The OH-bearing CH2 carbon is bonded to one other carbon, making the alcohol primary. N is bonded to hydroxyethyl and three methyls: four carbon groups, with a positive charge. It bears no N–H and is not trialkylammonium obtained merely by protonating a tertiary amine.
