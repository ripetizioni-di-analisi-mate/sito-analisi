1,2-Epoxypropane reacts with methoxide in methanol, followed by protonation. What is the product of attack at the less hindered carbon?

- $\mathrm{CH_3OCH_2CH(OH)CH_3}$.
- $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OCH_3}$.
- $\mathrm{HOCH_2CH(OH)CH_3}$ as the sole product with these reagents.
- $\mathrm{HOCH_2CH(OCH_3)CH_3}$.

## Explanation

Methoxide attacks the ring CH2 through an SN2-type mechanism. The bond between that carbon and the ring oxygen breaks; oxygen remains on the other carbon and becomes OH after protonation. The product therefore has a terminal methoxy group and an alcohol on the middle carbon, retaining both oxygens.
