A protein disulfide bridge is selectively reduced while retaining C–S bonds, and the products are protonated. Which groups are regenerated?

- Two thiol groups, $\mathrm{RSH}$.
- Two sulfenic acid groups, $\mathrm{RSOH}$.
- Two sulfinic acid groups, $\mathrm{RSO_2H}$.
- Two sulfonic acid groups, $\mathrm{RSO_3H}$.

## Explanation

Reduction cleaves S–S while keeping each sulfur attached to its original carbon group. Protonation gives two S–H bonds. The S–O-containing alternatives instead represent oxidized sulfur states: they do not result from this selective reduction. Both transformation type and sulfur count must be tracked.
