A nitrosamine is preincubated with active or inactivated enzyme. After protein removal, products from the first experiment form more DNA adducts. pH, concentrations and times are identical. Which interpretation is supported by the comparison?

- Adducts require the enzyme to remain bound to DNA during measurement.
- Enzymatic conversion decreases product reactivity toward DNA.
- The result depends only on a different pH between the experiments.
- Metabolism can generate products capable of covalently modifying DNA.

## Explanation

Greater activity persists after enzyme removal, consistent with reactive products forming during preincubation. pH is controlled and the protein need not remain on DNA. The comparison alone identifies neither every intermediate nor the behavior of every nitrosamine; it supports metabolic activation of the compound studied.
