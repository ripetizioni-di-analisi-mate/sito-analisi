A carbonyl is to remain protected as an acetal during a basic step and be regenerated afterwards. Which final condition is normally suitable?

- Mild base without acid as a general deprotection rule.
- Further water removal in excess alcohol.
- Excess anhydrous alkoxide without water.
- Water with acid catalysis.

## Explanation

Acetals are relatively stable to bases, whereas protonation facilitates the bond cleavage needed to regenerate the carbonyl in water. Removing water and using excess alcohol would instead favor acetal formation. This distinguishes acid's kinetic role from shifting equilibrium through reagent availability.
