Propanone and primary methylamine react under conditions suitable for imine formation and water removal. Which organic product is expected?

- $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHNHCH_3}$ without additional reduction.
- $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C(OCH_3)_2}$.
- $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C{=}NCH_3}$.
- $\mathrm{CH_3CONHCH_3}$.

## Explanation

Nitrogen adds to the carbonyl and subsequent water loss forms C=N. The methyl group originally on the amine remains attached to N. A saturated amine would require further imine reduction; an amide or acetal would arise from different transformations and reagents.
