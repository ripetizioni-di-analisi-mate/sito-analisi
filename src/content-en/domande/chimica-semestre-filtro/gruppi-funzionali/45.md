Which functional groups occur in $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_2CHO}$?

- Two ketones.
- Two aldehydes.
- One ketone and one aldehyde.
- One ester and one alcohol.

## Explanation

The internal carbonyl is bonded to CH3 and CH2, making it a ketone. The terminal CHO carbonyl instead bears H and is an aldehyde. Classifying each carbonyl requires inspecting its immediate bonds; the presence of C=O in both does not put them in the same class.
