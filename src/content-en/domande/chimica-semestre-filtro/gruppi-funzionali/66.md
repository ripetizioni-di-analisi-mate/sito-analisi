Why can tautomerization of the enol product contribute to the favorable balance of phosphoenolpyruvate hydrolysis?

- It allows formation of the relatively more stable keto form of pyruvate.
- The enol only changes resonance contributor without any additional stabilization.
- Breaking any bond by itself always releases energy.
- The enol must remain the only form after hydrolysis.

## Explanation

The energetic balance compares complete reactants and products. Access to a more stable keto product contributes to favoring the transformation. Bond breaking requires energy; the net change also depends on bonds and interactions formed, rather than merely labeling a bond as high-energy.
