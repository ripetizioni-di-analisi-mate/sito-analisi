How is the alcohol $(\mathrm{CH_3})_3\mathrm{COH}$ classified from its OH-bearing carbon?

- Tertiary.
- Primary.
- A triol.
- Secondary.

## Explanation

The OH-bearing carbon is attached to three methyl groups and therefore three carbons: the alcohol is tertiary. Count that carbon's neighbors, not all the carbons in the molecule. There is only one OH, so the molecule is not a triol despite its three methyl groups.
