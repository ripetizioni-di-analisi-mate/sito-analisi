N-Methylethanamide is completely hydrolyzed with excess aqueous base and heating, without final acidification. Which products are represented?

- Acetic acid and methylammonium.
- Ethanol and methylamine.
- Acetate and methylamine.
- Methyl ethanoate and ammonia.

## Explanation

Acyl cleavage preserves group allocation: CH3CO supplies the carboxylate and N–CH3 the amine. Excess base keeps the carboxyl group deprotonated and favors methylamine over its ammonium form. Without final acidification, the acid should not be presented directly as the predominant form.
