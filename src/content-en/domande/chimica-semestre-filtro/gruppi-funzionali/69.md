One donor ethanal molecule is tracked conceptually through an aldol reaction: its methyl carbon is labeled. In 3-hydroxybutanal, numbered from CHO, where does that carbon end up?

- At position 1, as the aldehyde carbon.
- At position 2, alpha to the retained aldehyde.
- At position 4, as the terminal methyl.
- At position 3, as the OH-bearing carbon.

## Explanation

The donor methyl is deprotonated and becomes the enolate's nucleophilic carbon. It forms the new bond to the acceptor carbonyl while remaining next to its own CHO. Numbered from that CHO, it is C2. The acceptor carbonyl instead becomes OH-bearing C3, and its methyl becomes C4.
