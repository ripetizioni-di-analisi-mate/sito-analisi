What principal structural role does ubiquinone's long isoprenoid chain play in the mitochondrial membrane?

- It covalently anchors Q to one complex, preventing lateral diffusion.
- It favors retention in the lipid environment.
- It is the site where carbonyl conversion occurs during the Q/QH2 cycle.
- It favors distribution into the aqueous phase rather than the bilayer.

## Explanation

The isoprenoid chain is predominantly hydrocarbon and lipophilic, favoring distribution within the membrane. The redox system is associated with the quinone portion. Distinguishing these parts explains how one molecule can remain in the bilayer and carry reducing equivalents between protein partners.
