Butan-2-one is treated with NaBH4 under customary mild conditions, then protonated. Which organic product forms?

- Butanoic acid.
- Butan-1-ol.
- But-2-ene.
- Butan-2-ol.

## Explanation

Hydride adds to the carbonyl carbon and subsequent protonation gives OH. The affected carbon remains at position 2 and retains its bonds to two carbon groups: the product is a secondary alcohol. Neither relocation to position 1 nor oxidation to an acid occurs.
