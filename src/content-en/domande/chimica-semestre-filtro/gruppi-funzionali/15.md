A compound has structure $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2OH}$. How is its OH-containing group classified?

- Phenol.
- A primary benzyl alcohol.
- An enol.
- A secondary alcohol.

## Explanation

OH is attached to a saturated CH2 carbon bonded to only one other carbon. That CH2 separates the aromatic ring from the hydroxyl. In a phenol, the O–H oxygen would be directly attached to the ring; in an enol, the hydroxyl-bearing carbon would participate in C=C.
