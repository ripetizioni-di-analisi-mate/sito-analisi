3-Hydroxybutanal is dehydrated under conditions favoring the conjugated system. Which product corresponds to aldol condensation?

- But-2-enal.
- Butanoic acid.
- Butan-2-one.
- Butanal without a double bond.

## Explanation

Beta OH and an alpha H are removed overall as water. A double bond remains between the alpha and beta carbons, conjugated with the aldehyde: the product is but-2-enal. Saturating that segment would require another transformation; the carbonyl is not relocated to form a ketone either.
