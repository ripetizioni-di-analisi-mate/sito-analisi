A monocarboxylic acid HA is in equilibrium with A− in dilute solution. If $pH=pK_a$ and activities are approximated by concentrations, which relation holds?

- All acid is undissociated.
- All acid is necessarily dissociated.
- $[\mathrm{A^-}]=10[\mathrm{HA}]$.
- $[\mathrm{HA}]=[\mathrm{A^-}]$.

## Explanation

The A−/HA ratio is $10^{pH-pK_a}=1$. The two forms are therefore equimolar and each accounts for half their combined concentration. One pH unit above pKa would instead give a ratio of ten; equality means neither absence of equilibrium nor complete dissociation.
