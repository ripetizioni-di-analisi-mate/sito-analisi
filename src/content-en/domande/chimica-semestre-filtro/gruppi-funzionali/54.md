Which structure shows an aldehyde-derived acetal rather than a hemiacetal?

- $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OH)OCH_3}$.
- $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OCH_3)_2}$.
- $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OH)_2}$.
- $\mathrm{CH_3COOCH_3}$.

## Explanation

The acetal center bears two OR groups, alongside H and the carbon group of the former aldehyde. OH plus OR would give a hemiacetal; two OH groups would give a geminal hydrate. An ester instead has C=O and different connectivity despite also containing two oxygens.
