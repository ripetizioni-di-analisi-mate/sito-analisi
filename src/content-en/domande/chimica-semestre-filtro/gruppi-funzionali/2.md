In water under the same conditions, phenol has $pK_a=10$ and ethanol $pK_a=16$. Which interpretation is correct?

- Phenol is less acidic because the ring prevents deprotonation.
- Phenol is more acidic and its anion is stabilized by delocalization.
- Ethanol is more acidic because its pKa is higher.
- The compounds have equal acidity because both contain OH.

## Explanation

A lower pKa corresponds to a larger acid dissociation constant. Phenoxide can delocalize charge into the aromatic system, a stabilization absent from an ordinary aliphatic alkoxide. OH identifies a functional group but does not make the acid-base equilibria of all molecules containing it identical.
