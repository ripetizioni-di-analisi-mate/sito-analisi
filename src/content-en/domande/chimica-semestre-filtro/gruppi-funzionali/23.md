Four moles of RSH groups are completely oxidized to RSSR disulfides. How many moles of electrons are released in the balance?

- $1\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{mol}$.

## Explanation

Two RSH groups form one RSSR while releasing two protons and two electrons. Four moles of RSH therefore produce two moles of disulfide and release four moles of electrons. Confusing the number of disulfides with the number of electrons halves the result despite the same stoichiometry.
