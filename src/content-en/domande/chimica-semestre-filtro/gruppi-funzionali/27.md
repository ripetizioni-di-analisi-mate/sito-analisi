In alcohol dehydration to an alkene, besides OH activation, which structural feature is needed to form the adjacent double bond?

- An H on the hydroxyl-bearing carbon instead of the adjacent carbon.
- A necessarily tertiary hydroxyl-bearing carbon.
- A hydrogen on a beta carbon relative to the hydroxyl-bearing carbon.
- An H only on oxygen, with no H on neighboring carbons.

## Explanation

Elimination forms C=C between the carbon losing the leaving group and an adjacent carbon losing H. That hydrogen is called beta relative to the initial site. Acid activation of OH facilitates water departure but does not replace the requirement for a suitable arrangement of the eliminated atoms.
