Propan-2-amine has structure $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHNH_2}$. How is the amine classified?

- Secondary, because the carbon next to nitrogen is secondary.
- Quaternary, because nitrogen forms four bonds overall.
- Primary, because nitrogen is bonded to one carbon group.
- Tertiary, because the molecule contains three carbons.

## Explanation

For amines, count the carbon groups directly bonded to nitrogen. Here N bears an isopropyl group and two hydrogens, so the amine is primary. The carbon attached to N is secondary, but alcohol or carbon classification should not automatically be transferred to nitrogen.
