Two representations differ because a proton moved from an alpha carbon to oxygen and C=O became C=C–OH. How is this change described?

- A reduction requiring two external electrons.
- Keto-enol tautomerism.
- Deprotonation alone with net loss of H+.
- Two resonance contributors of the same species.

## Explanation

Tautomerism changes the position of a hydrogen nucleus and the bonds while preserving molecular formula. Resonance changes only electron distribution with nuclei fixed. Here the proton remains within the molecule, so this is also different from simply forming an anion by deprotonation.
