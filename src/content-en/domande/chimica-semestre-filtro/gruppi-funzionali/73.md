A quinone receives one electron in the first step of a redox sequence. Which intermediate state is compatible, with protonation dependent on the medium?

- Semiquinone.
- Quinone further oxidized by losing one electron.
- Quinol already fully reduced by two electrons.
- Quinone necessarily unchanged in oxidation state.

## Explanation

A semiquinone state connects oxidized quinone to its two-electron-reduced form. One transfer does not necessarily complete Q + 2 e− + 2 H+ → QH2. Intermediate protonation must be specified according to the environment; it cannot be inferred from the one-electron count alone.
