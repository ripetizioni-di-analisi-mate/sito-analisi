A monoester, $0.12\,\mathrm{mol}$, is saponified with $0.08\,\mathrm{mol}$ of OH−. Assuming quantitative base consumption and no side reactions, how many moles of carboxylate form?

- $0.04\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.12\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.08\,\mathrm{mol}$.

## Explanation

Saponifying a monoester consumes one equivalent of OH− for each carboxylate formed. Base is limiting, so 0.08 mol of ester reacts and 0.04 mol remains. The latter amount describes leftover reagent, not product; base is not merely a catalyst in this balance.
