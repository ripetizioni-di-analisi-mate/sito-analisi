A mixture contains propanone and methyl ethanoate. With NaBH4 under customary mild conditions and no special activation, which transformation is favored?

- Reduction of both ketone and ester to alcohols with equal ease.
- No reduction, because NaBH4 would act only on carboxylates.
- Reduction of propanone to propan-2-ol.
- Selective ester reduction while leaving the ketone intact.

## Explanation

NaBH4 is commonly used to reduce aldehydes and ketones under conditions leaving many esters unchanged. Specifying conditions limits the prediction: other reducing agents or activation can alter selectivity. Propanone receives hydride equivalents and becomes a secondary alcohol, not an oxidation product.
