Which linkage identifies an acyl phosphate, a biologically relevant mixed anhydride?

- C(=O)–P without an intervening oxygen.
- C(=O)–S–P.
- C–O–P without an acyl carbonyl.
- C(=O)–O–P.

## Explanation

An acyl phosphate connects an acyl group to a phosphate oxygen. It is therefore distinct both from a C–P phosphonate and from an ordinary phosphate ester of an alcohol lacking a carbonyl. Carbonyl position is decisive for recognizing the derivative and its possible transfer pathways.
