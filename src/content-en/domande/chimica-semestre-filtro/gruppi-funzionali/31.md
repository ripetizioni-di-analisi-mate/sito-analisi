2,2-Dimethyloxirane reacts with HCl in ether. Greater carbocation character at the tertiary carbon favors which major product?

- $\mathrm{HOCH_2C(OH)(CH_3)_2}$.
- $\mathrm{ClCH_2C(OH)(CH_3)_2}$.
- $\mathrm{ClCH_2CH(CH_3)_2}$.
- $\mathrm{HOCH_2C(Cl)(CH_3)_2}$.

## Explanation

Protonation activates the epoxide and, in this substrate, chloride attack is favored at the tertiary carbon. Oxygen remains on CH2 and gives OH. The other chlorohydrin represents opposite regiochemistry; the diol would require introduction of OH from water instead of the chloride attack considered here.
