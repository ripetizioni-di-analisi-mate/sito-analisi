Why are para-benzoquinone and hydroquinone not simply two tautomers with the same formula?

- Cyclic molecules cannot exhibit tautomerism.
- Conversion requires overall addition of two protons and two electrons.
- Conversion moves only a proton and preserves the same molecular formula.
- Tautomers must always have the same functional group.

## Explanation

Q/QH2 reduction changes hydrogen content and oxidation state. Tautomerization instead preserves molecular formula while redistributing a proton and bonds. A ring's presence does not distinguish the processes; the overall balance of atoms, charges and electrons does.
