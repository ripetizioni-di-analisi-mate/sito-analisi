Before nucleophilic addition, which geometry describes the carbonyl carbon of a simple ketone?

- Tetrahedral with four single bonds.
- Approximately trigonal planar.
- Linear with only two sigma bonds.
- Pyramidal like a neutral amine.

## Explanation

Carbonyl carbon has three sigma-bond directions and participates in a pi bond to O, consistent with approximately planar geometry. Addition changes connectivity and gives a tetrahedral center. Confusing reactant geometry with intermediate geometry obscures the role of breaking the pi bond.
