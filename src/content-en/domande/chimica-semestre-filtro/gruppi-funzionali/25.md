In methionine's side-chain fragment $\mathrm{{-}CH_2CH_2SCH_3}$, which sulfur functional group is present?

- Thioester.
- Disulfide.
- Thiol.
- Thioether.

## Explanation

Sulfur is bonded to two carbon groups, with no H on S, no adjacent second sulfur and no carbonyl directly bonded to S. These observations distinguish the thioether from a thiol, disulfide and thioester respectively. The presence of sulfur alone does not make these functional groups equivalent.
