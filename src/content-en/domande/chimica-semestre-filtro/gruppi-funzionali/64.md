Uric acid loses H+ and forms urate with one additional negative charge. How is this particular step classified?

- Tautomerism alone at constant formula and charge.
- Resonance alone with fixed nuclei.
- Acid-base deprotonation.
- Reduction with obligatory uptake of two electrons.

## Explanation

The species loses a proton overall and its charge changes, identifying deprotonation. Urate can itself have tautomers, but that does not make net H+ transfer to solvent tautomerism. Resonance, furthermore, does not move even a single nucleus.
