A secondary R2NH is converted into an N-nitrosamine R2N–N=O by a nitrosating species. Which new bond identifies the transformation?

- A C=O directly bonded to the original nitrogen.
- Only one additional N–H.
- N–N.
- C–C.

## Explanation

The product contains the amine nitrogen bonded to the nitroso group's nitrogen: an N–N bond forms. Simple protonation would instead add H+ to the lone pair and give ammonium without introducing a second nitrogen. A nitrosamine must also be distinguished from an amide, which has C=O beside N.
