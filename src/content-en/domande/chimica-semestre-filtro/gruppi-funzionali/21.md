For phenol with $pK_a=10$, at $pH=12$ in dilute water where the Henderson ratio applies, what is phenoxide/phenol?

- $0.01$.
- $100$.
- $2$.
- $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

The conjugate-base/acid ratio is $10^{pH-pK_a}=10^2=100$. A pH above pKa favors the deprotonated form. The two-unit difference enters as an exponent: it is not itself the ratio and does not justify a result of two.
