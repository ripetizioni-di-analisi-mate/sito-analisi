An unhindered primary alcohol reacts with HBr under substitution-favoring conditions. After OH protonation, which description matches an SN2 pathway?

- The C–O bond breaks homolytically before bromide attacks.
- A long-lived free primary carbocation forms first.
- Bromide approaches from the same side occupied by the departing C–O bond.
- Bromide attacks while the C–O bond breaks and releases water.

## Explanation

In an SN2 pathway, C–Br formation and C–O cleavage are concerted. An unhindered primary carbon is accessible to backside attack, whereas a free primary carbocation would be poorly stabilized. Preparatory protonation makes water the leaving group without automatically turning the reaction into SN1.
