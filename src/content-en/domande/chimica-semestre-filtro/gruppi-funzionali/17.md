Comparing ethanol and octan-1-ol in water at the same temperature, which trend is expected and why?

- Ethanol is less soluble because it forms fewer C–C bonds.
- Octan-1-ol is more soluble because it contains more carbons.
- Octan-1-ol is less soluble because of its larger hydrocarbon portion.
- Equal solubility because both have one OH.

## Explanation

Both can interact with water through OH, but octan-1-ol has a much larger nonpolar portion. The hydroxyl's relative contribution does not compensate equally for inserting the chain into the solvent. Solubility follows the whole structure and its interactions, rather than functional-group counting alone.
