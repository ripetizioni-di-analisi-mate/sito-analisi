In substitution of an alcohol with HBr, what initial role does oxygen protonation play?

- It imposes formation of a free carbocation for every primary alcohol.
- It converts OH into a group that can depart as water.
- It converts bromide into the alcohol's leaving group.
- It makes OH a more nucleophilic alkoxide.

## Explanation

OH− would be an unfavorable leaving group. After protonation, C–O cleavage can release neutral water, making substitution accessible. Bromide instead acts as the nucleophile under suitable conditions; whether SN1 or SN2 dominates depends mainly on the substrate and conditions.
