A ketone with alpha hydrogens reacts with a secondary amine under suitable condensation conditions. Which neutral product is typically possible after dehydration and deprotonation?

- An enamine.
- An oxime with a C=N–OH group.
- A saturated tertiary amine obtained without a reducing agent.
- A neutral imine with N still bonded to both original alkyl groups.

## Explanation

Addition and water loss form an iminium species. Removing an alpha H creates C=C beside N and gives a neutral enamine. N bearing C=N and two other carbon bonds would remain positively charged, not form the proposed neutral imine. A saturated amine would require reduction; an oxime would require hydroxylamine as reagent.
