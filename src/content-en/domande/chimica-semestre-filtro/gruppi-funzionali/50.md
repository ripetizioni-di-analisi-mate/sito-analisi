A hydride equivalent adds to a neutral ketone. Before protonation, what group forms at the oxygen originally in the carbonyl?

- An O− alkoxide.
- An O+ oxonium without proton addition.
- An already complete neutral OH without a proton source.
- A C=O carbonyl remaining intact despite the new C–H bond.

## Explanation

The new C–H pair forms while the carbonyl pi pair moves to oxygen. Oxygen is therefore anionic, rather than already protonated. A later step with a proton source gives the alcohol. Tracking electron and proton balances separately avoids skipping the intermediate.
