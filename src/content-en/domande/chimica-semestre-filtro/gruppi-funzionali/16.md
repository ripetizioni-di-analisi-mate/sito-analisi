What is the name of $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OH)CH(CH_3)CH_3}$ using the longest chain containing OH and the lowest hydroxyl locant?

- 2-Methylbutan-3-ol.
- 3-Methylbutan-2-ol.
- Pentan-2-ol.
- 3-Methylbutan-1-ol.

## Explanation

The longest continuous chain containing the hydroxyl-bearing carbon has four atoms, not five: the fifth carbon is a branch. Numbering from the end nearer OH places the hydroxyl at position 2 and methyl at position 3. Reversing the numbering would unnecessarily give OH the higher locant.
