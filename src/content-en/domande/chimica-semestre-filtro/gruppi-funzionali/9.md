Two ethanal molecules undergo aldol addition with dilute base; the transformation is stopped before dehydration. Which product is considered?

- Ethyl ethanoate.
- But-2-enal.
- Butane-1,3-diol.
- 3-Hydroxybutanal.

## Explanation

One ethanal enolate attacks the other carbonyl. The donor carbonyl remains an aldehyde while the attacked one becomes OH, giving a four-carbon beta-hydroxyaldehyde. But-2-enal would require the subsequent dehydration step; a diol would additionally require reduction of the remaining aldehyde.
