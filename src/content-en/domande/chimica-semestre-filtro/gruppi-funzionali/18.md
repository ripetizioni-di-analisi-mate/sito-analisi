Pure dimethyl ether contains no other functional groups. Which statement about its hydrogen bonding is correct?

- It cannot accept them because oxygen has two bonds.
- It donates them through ordinary C–H bonds as if they were O–H.
- It can accept them from water but cannot donate them through an O–H bond.
- It can donate and accept them like an alcohol.

## Explanation

Ether oxygen retains lone pairs that can accept a hydrogen bond from water. It does not, however, possess a hydrogen bonded to O. Ordinary C–H bonds do not give it an alcohol's donor behavior in the usual model of the interactions being compared here.
