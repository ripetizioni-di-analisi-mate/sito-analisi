Comparing ethanal and propanone toward the same nucleophile under identical conditions, which factor contributes to ethanal's typical greater reactivity?

- Greater alkyl electron donation to ethanal's carbonyl carbon.
- Less steric hindrance and only one alkyl group on the carbonyl carbon.
- Greater steric stabilization of the initial state by ethanal's two alkyl groups.
- A greater partial negative charge on ethanal's carbonyl carbon.

## Explanation

Ethanal bears H and CH3 at the carbonyl, whereas propanone bears two methyls. Nucleophilic approach is generally less hindered for the aldehyde, which also receives less alkyl electron donation. This compares similar substrates under common conditions rather than imposing an absolute order for every substituent.
