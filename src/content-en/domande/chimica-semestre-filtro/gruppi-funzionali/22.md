A thiol has $pK_a=8.3$. At $pH=7.3$, in dilute solution with activities approximated by concentrations, what fraction is thiolate?

- $\dfrac12$.
- $\dfrac1{100}$.
- $\dfrac1{11}$.
- $\dfrac{10}{11}$.

## Explanation

The RS−/RSH ratio is $10^{-1}=0.1$. Of eleven total parts, one is deprotonated and ten are protonated, giving a thiolate fraction of 1/11. This distinguishes a species ratio from a fraction of the total and shows that some nucleophilic species exists even below pKa.
