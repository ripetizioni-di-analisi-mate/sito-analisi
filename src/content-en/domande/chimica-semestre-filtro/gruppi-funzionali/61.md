Two enolate formulas show negative charge on O and on the alpha carbon respectively, without moving nuclei. How are the formulas related?

- They necessarily have different total electron numbers.
- They are resonance contributors of the same species.
- They are two tautomers separated by H transfer.
- They are two enantiomers with identical connectivity.

## Explanation

Resonance preserves the skeleton, nuclear positions and total charge while changing how electrons are drawn. The actual enolate has delocalized density, rather than a charge jumping between two isolated molecules. Tautomerism would instead require proton movement accompanied by bond changes.
