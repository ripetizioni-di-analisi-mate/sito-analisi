Ethanol and dimethyl ether have formula $\mathrm{C_2H_6O}$. Which feature explains ethanol's higher boiling point at the same pressure?

- Dimethyl ether forms a more extensive hydrogen-bond network between its own molecules.
- Ethanol can form hydrogen bonds between its own molecules through O–H.
- Boiling requires cleavage of ethanol's covalent O–H bonds.
- Ethanol has a larger molecular mass.

## Explanation

The identical molecular formula implies identical molecular mass, so mass cannot explain the difference. Ethanol forms intermolecular networks with donors and acceptors; dimethyl ether lacks O–H. Boiling separates molecules without requiring widespread cleavage of their covalent bonds.
