In enzymatic conversion of a ketone to an alcohol, which transfer from NADH to the substrate describes the reducing equivalents?

- A hydride equivalent, containing two electrons, followed by the necessary proton transfers.
- Only one electron-free H+ as the entire redox event.
- A hydroxide to carbon, leaving NADH's oxidation state unchanged.
- One neutral hydrogen atom with one electron as the entire reducing transfer.

## Explanation

NADH can supply a hydride equivalent to electrophilic carbon. This transfers an electron pair and forms C–H; oxygen protonation completes the alcohol. Adding only H+ can activate the carbonyl but does not supply the electrons required for reduction.
