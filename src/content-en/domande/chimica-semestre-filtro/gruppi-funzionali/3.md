Propan-1-ol is oxidized using PCC under customary anhydrous conditions that stop at the carbonyl. Which product is expected?

- Propanal.
- Propene as the oxidation product.
- Propanone.
- Propanoic acid as the obligatory product.

## Explanation

The hydroxyl-bearing carbon of propan-1-ol is terminal: oxidation without changing the skeleton gives an aldehyde. The stated anhydrous conditions allow stopping at propanal. Propanone would require a secondary alcohol, whereas propene belongs to dehydration. The acid requires conditions permitting further oxidation.
