4-Hydroxybutanoic acid forms its lactone by internal esterification. How many atoms belong to the ring?

- Three.
- Five.
- Four.
- Six.

## Explanation

The ring contains the three CH2 carbons, the carboxyl carbon and the terminal OH oxygen. That makes five atoms despite starting with a four-carbon acid. The carbonyl oxygen remains outside and does not add a sixth member; counting only carbons would instead give four.
