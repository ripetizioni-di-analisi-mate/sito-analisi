In $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OH)OCH_3}$, the middle carbon bears both OH and OCH3. Which functional group is present?

- Hemiacetal.
- Ester.
- Acetal.
- Ketone.

## Explanation

The identification depends on the groups attached to the same carbon: one OH and one OR define a hemiacetal. An acetal has two OR groups and no OH at that center. An ester or ketone would instead require a C=O bond, absent from the stated structure.
