Ethyl ethanoate reacts with excess methanol and suitable transesterification catalysis while excluding water. Which product pair describes the exchange?

- Ethyl methanoate and ethanol.
- Methyl ethanoate and ethanol.
- Methyl propanoate and methanol.
- Acetic acid and ethanol.

## Explanation

The CH3CO acyl portion remains unchanged while ethoxy is replaced by methoxy. The departing alcohol is therefore ethanol. The acid chain is not lengthened and the carbonyl is not reduced. Specifying alcohol and absence of water distinguishes this exchange from aqueous saponification.
