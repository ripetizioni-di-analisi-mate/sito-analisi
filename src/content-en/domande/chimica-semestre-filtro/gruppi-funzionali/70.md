In an intramolecular aldol model for hexane-2,5-dione, $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_2CH_2COCH_3}$, number the chain C1 to C6. The enolate at C1 attacks carbonyl C5. How many carbons belong to the resulting ring?

- $4$ carbons.
- $6$ carbons.
- $7$ carbons.
- $5$ carbons.

## Explanation

The new C1–C5 bond closes the path C1–C2–C3–C4–C5, giving five ring carbons. C6 remains an external methyl, and the attacked carbonyl oxygen becomes an OH outside the ring. Count the closed bond path, not every carbon in the molecule. The specified closure is to be tracked without predicting the entire reaction mixture.
