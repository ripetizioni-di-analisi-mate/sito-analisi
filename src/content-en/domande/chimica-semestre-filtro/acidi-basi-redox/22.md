A solution's pH changes from $7.2$ to $6.8$. How does proton activity change?

- It increases by $0.4\%$.
- It decreases by a factor of approximately $2.51$.
- It increases by a factor of approximately $2.51$.
- It becomes $0.4$ times its initial value.

## Explanation

The final-to-initial activity ratio is $10^{7.2-6.8}=10^{0.4}\simeq2.51$. pH decreases while activity increases. A pH difference is a difference of logarithms, representing a multiplicative ratio rather than a percentage read directly from the decimal difference.
