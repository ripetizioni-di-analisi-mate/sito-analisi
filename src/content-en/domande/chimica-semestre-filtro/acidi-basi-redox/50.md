In an educational bicarbonate-buffer model, $\mathrm{pH}=6.1+\log_{10}\dfrac{[\mathrm{HCO_3^-}]}{\alpha p_{\mathrm{CO_2}}}$. With bicarbonate and $\alpha$ fixed, CO₂ partial pressure doubles. How does pH change?

- It remains unchanged because bicarbonate is fixed.
- It halves.
- It increases by about $0.30$.
- It decreases by about $0.30$.

## Explanation

Doubling the denominator halves the ratio. The logarithmic change is $\log_{10}(\dfrac{1}{2})\simeq-0.301$; pH itself does not halve. This result applies to the quantities artificially held fixed in the problem and does not by itself describe all possible compensations in an organism.
