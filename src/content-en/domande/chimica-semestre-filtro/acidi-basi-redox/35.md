An ideal $\mathrm{HIn/In^-}$ indicator has $pK_a=5.0$. At pH $4.0$, which description is correct?

- The forms have equal abundance.
- HIn must be completely absent.
- HIn is about ten times as abundant as $\mathrm{In^-}$.
- $\mathrm{In^-}$ is about ten times as abundant.

## Explanation

The base-to-acid ratio is $10^{4.0-5.0}=0.10$, so HIn predominates. Colour changes through an equilibrium, not the instant disappearance of one form at a threshold. Equal abundances occur when pH equals pK in the ideal model.
