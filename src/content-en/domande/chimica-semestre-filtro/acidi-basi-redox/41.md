An ideal buffer contains $20\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of HA and $30\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of A⁻. Which addition gives approximately $\mathrm{pH}=pK_a$, assuming that amounts after neutralisation may be used in the buffer formula and neglecting dilution and water autoionisation?

- $10\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of HCl.
- $5\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of NaOH.
- $10\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of NaOH.
- $5\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of HCl.

## Explanation

Having $\mathrm{pH}=pK_a$ requires equal amounts of the two forms in the same volume. Strong acid converts A⁻ into HA: $30-x=20+x$, hence $x=5\,\mathrm{mmol}$. Adding base would move the ratio in the opposite direction. Ten millimoles are unnecessary because each mole transferred changes both amounts.
