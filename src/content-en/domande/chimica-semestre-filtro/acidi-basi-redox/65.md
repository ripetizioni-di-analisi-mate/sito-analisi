A student writes $\mathrm{MnO_4^-+8H^++Fe^{2+}\rightarrow Mn^{2+}+4H_2O+Fe^{3+}}$. Atoms are balanced. Which check exposes the error, and what correction is needed?

- Oxygen is missing on the right; water must be doubled.
- Hydrogen is missing on the left; H⁺ ions must be doubled.
- Charges are $+9$ and $+5$; both iron species need coefficient $5$.
- Charges are equal; no correction is needed.

## Explanation

Left-hand charge is $-1+8+2=+9$ and right-hand charge is $2+3=+5$. Manganese gains five electrons, while each Fe²⁺ loses one: five iron ions are needed per permanganate. With both iron coefficients equal to five, charges become $+17$ on both sides and atoms remain balanced.
