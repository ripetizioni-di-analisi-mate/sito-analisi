For an ideal monoprotic acid, $C=0.010\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and $K_a=10^{-3}$. The quadratic solution gives $x/C\simeq0.27$. Is $C-x\simeq C$ justified?

- No: acid consumption is too large for that approximation.
- Yes: classifying the acid as weak is sufficient.
- No: complete dissociation must be assumed instead.
- Yes: any $K_a$ below one makes consumption zero.

## Explanation

A reacted fraction of about twenty-seven percent substantially changes the equilibrium denominator. Retain $C-x$ and use the quadratic rather than switching to the opposite assumption of complete dissociation. The word “weak” does not replace a quantitative check of the approximation.
