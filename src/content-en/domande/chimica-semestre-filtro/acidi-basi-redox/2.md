In $\mathrm{BF_3+NH_3\rightarrow F_3B\!\leftarrow\!NH_3}$, which species is the Lewis base, and why?

- $\mathrm{BF_3}$, because it donates an electron pair to nitrogen.
- $\mathrm{NH_3}$, because it donates an electron pair to boron.
- $\mathrm{NH_3}$, because it donates a proton to boron.
- $\mathrm{BF_3}$, because accepting a pair makes it a base.

## Explanation

Lewis defines the base as the electron-pair donor. The nitrogen lone pair in ammonia forms the new bond to boron, so $\mathrm{NH_3}$ is the base. $\mathrm{BF_3}$ accepts the pair and is the acid. No proton transfer is needed: the criterion here is the source of the pair forming the bond.
