Which mixture can directly form a buffer when appreciable amounts of its components are present?

- Acetic acid and sodium acetate.
- Only water and NaCl.
- Stoichiometrically equivalent amounts of HCl and NaOH.
- HCl and NaCl under the same dilute aqueous conditions.

## Explanation

Acetic acid and acetate provide a weak conjugate pair able to consume small additions of both base and acid. Chloride is not sufficiently reactive as a base in water to buffer HCl in this model. Exactly neutralising a strong acid with a strong base does not create a buffer pair.
