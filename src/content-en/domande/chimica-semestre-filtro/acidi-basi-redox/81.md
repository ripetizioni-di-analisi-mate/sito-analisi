At $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ a cell follows $E=E^\circ-\dfrac{0.05916}{n}\log_{10}Q$. The graph of $E$ against $\log_{10}Q$ has slope $-0.01972\,\mathrm{V}$. What is $n$ for the written reaction?

- $6$.
- $3$.
- $1$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

The slope magnitude is $\dfrac{0.05916}{n}$, so $n=\dfrac{0.05916}{0.01972}=3$. The negative sign means that increasing the quotient lowers potential in the selected direction. The result refers to the particular balanced equation used to define $Q$.
