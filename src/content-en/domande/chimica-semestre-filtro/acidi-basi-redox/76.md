Standard reduction potentials are $E^\circ_{\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}}=+0.80\,\mathrm{V}$ and $E^\circ_{\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}}=-0.44\,\mathrm{V}$. What is $E^\circ$ for $\mathrm{Fe+2Ag^+\rightarrow Fe^{2+}+2Ag}$?

- $1.24\,\mathrm{V}$.
- $-1.24\,\mathrm{V}$.
- $2.04\,\mathrm{V}$.
- $0.36\,\mathrm{V}$.

## Explanation

Silver is reduced at the cathode and iron oxidised at the anode. Using both values as reduction potentials, subtract the anode value: $0.80-(-0.44)=1.24\,\mathrm{V}$. The coefficient two before silver balances electrons but does not double its potential.
