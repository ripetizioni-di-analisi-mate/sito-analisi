In superoxide ion $\mathrm{O_2^-}$, what is oxygen's mean oxidation number?

- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $-2$.
- $0$.
- $-1$.

## Explanation

The sum of oxidation numbers must equal the overall charge $-1$. Dividing between the two oxygens gives a mean of $-1/2$. Assigning the entire charge to each atom would give an incorrect sum. The fractional mean does not indicate an isolated half-electron.
