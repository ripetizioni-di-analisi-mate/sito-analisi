In the radical model $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}+H_2O_2\rightarrow Fe^{3+}+OH^-+{}^\bullet OH}$, identify the iron change and the radical produced.

- Iron(II) is oxidised and ${}^\bullet\mathrm{OH}$ is produced.
- Iron(II) is reduced and ${}^\bullet\mathrm{OH}$ is produced.
- Iron(II) is oxidised and $\mathrm{OH^-}$ is the radical produced.
- Iron(II) is oxidised and the radical product is $\mathrm{HO_2^\bullet}$.

## Explanation

Iron charge changes from $+2$ to $+3$, so iron loses an electron and is oxidised. The products include hydroxyl radical ${}^\bullet\mathrm{OH}$. Hydroxide $\mathrm{OH^-}$ is a different, negatively charged species, while the stated radical is neutral. Its unpaired-electron dot and the hydroxide charge are not interchangeable notation; the hydroperoxyl radical is not a product in the assigned equation.
