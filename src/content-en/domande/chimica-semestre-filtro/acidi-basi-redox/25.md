At $25\,^\circ\mathrm C$ a conjugate pair has $pK_a=9.25$ and $pK_w=14.00$. What is $pK_b$?

- Approximately $1.51$.
- $9.25$.
- $4.75$.
- $23.25$.

## Explanation

The product of conjugate constants gives $pK_a+pK_b=pK_w$. Therefore $pK_b=14.00-9.25=4.75$. Subtract the logarithmic values: dividing the two pK values or treating them as nonlogarithmic constants would not respect the equilibrium relation.
