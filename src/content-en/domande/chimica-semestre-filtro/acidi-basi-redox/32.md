For an amphiprotic $\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}$ solution, the conditions for averaging the pK values hold. With $pK_{a1}=2.15$ and $pK_{a2}=7.20$, what pH is estimated?

- Approximately $4.68$.
- Approximately $2.53$.
- Approximately $9.35$.
- $7.20$.

## Explanation

The intermediate species connects the two adjacent steps, giving $\mathrm{pH}\simeq(2.15+7.20)/2=4.675$. The estimate is acidic even though the species is amphiprotic. Using only one pK, adding without dividing, or halving their difference does not apply the assigned model.
