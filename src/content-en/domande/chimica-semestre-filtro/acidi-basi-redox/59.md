For the ideal salt $\mathrm{CaA(s)\rightleftharpoons Ca^{2+}+A^{2-}}$, compare two solutions in equilibrium with the solid at the same temperature. Free A²⁻ activity is ten times greater in the second. What is its free Ca²⁺ activity?

- One hundred times smaller.
- Unchanged.
- Ten times smaller.
- Ten times greater.

## Explanation

While the same solid phase coexists at equilibrium, $a_{\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}}a_{\mathrm{A^{2-}}}=K_{sp}$. If one factor rises tenfold, the other must fall tenfold. This comparison concerns free activities, not necessarily total concentrations when protonation or complexes are present.
