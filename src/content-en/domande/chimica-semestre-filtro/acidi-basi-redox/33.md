For the second step $\mathrm{HA^-\rightleftharpoons H^++A^{2-}}$, $K_{a2}=10^{-8}$ and proton activity is $10^{-3}$. What is $a_{\mathrm{A^{2-}}}/a_{\mathrm{HA^-}}$?

- $10^{-11}$.
- $10^{-3}$.
- $10^{-5}$.
- $10^{5}$.

## Explanation

Rearranging the constant gives the ratio $K_{a2}/a_{\mathrm{H^+}}=10^{-8}/10^{-3}=10^{-5}$. The further-deprotonated form is scarce relative to the intermediate. Hydronium already present suppresses the second dissociation; the two stages are not simply independent contributions to add.
