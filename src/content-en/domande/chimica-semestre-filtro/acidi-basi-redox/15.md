Two monoprotic acids under the same conditions have $pK_a=3$ and $pK_a=5$. How do their acid constants compare?

- The first has a $K_a$ twice as large.
- The second has a $K_a$ one hundred times larger.
- The constants coincide if initial concentrations are equal.
- The first has a $K_a$ one hundred times larger.

## Explanation

The relation is $K_a=10^{-pK_a}$: the values are $10^{-3}$ and $10^{-5}$, with ratio $100$. A difference of two logarithmic units is not a factor of two. The constant characterises equilibrium under the given conditions; choosing equal analytical concentrations does not make the constants equal.
