Which distinction between reactive oxygen species and radicals is correct?

- The hydroxyl radical contains no unpaired electron.
- Superoxide is excluded from reactive species because it is charged.
- H₂O₂ is a reactive oxygen species but is not a radical in its ordinary form.
- All reactive oxygen species must be radicals.

## Explanation

A radical has unpaired electrons, whereas reactive oxygen species includes several categories. Ordinary hydrogen peroxide is nonradical; hydroxyl radical and superoxide have radical character. Electric charge does not rule out an unpaired electron.
