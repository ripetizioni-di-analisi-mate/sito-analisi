Identify the conjugate base and conjugate acid of $\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}$, in that order.

- $\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}$ and $\mathrm{H_4PO_4^+}$.
- $\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}$ and $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$.
- $\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}$ and $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$.
- $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$ and $\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}$.

## Explanation

The conjugate base is obtained by removing one proton, lowering the charge by one unit and giving $\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}$. The conjugate acid is obtained by adding one proton, giving $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$. Each conjugation step involves just one proton. Removing two protons would skip a step; adding a proton must also change the charge correctly.
