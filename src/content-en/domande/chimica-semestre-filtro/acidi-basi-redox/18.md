When water coordinates to a metal ion, oxygen supplies a lone pair to the metal. What are their Lewis roles?

- Neither: Lewis definitions require proton transfer.
- Water is the base and the metal ion the acid.
- Water is the acid and the metal ion the base.
- Both are acids because the bond involves a cation.

## Explanation

The pair donor is the Lewis base, so water has that role in this event. The metal ion accepts the pair and is the acid. This classification concerns the bond being formed; any subsequent proton transfer by coordinated water is another equilibrium to analyse separately.
