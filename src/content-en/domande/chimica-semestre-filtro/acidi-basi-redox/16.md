At the same temperature, HA is a stronger acid than HB. Which comparison of their conjugate bases is correct?

- $\mathrm{A^-}$ is a stronger base than $\mathrm{B^-}$.
- The bases have equal strength because they have the same charge.
- The conjugate base of the stronger acid must always be strong.
- $\mathrm{A^-}$ is a weaker base than $\mathrm{B^-}$.

## Explanation

Each conjugate pair satisfies $K_aK_b=K_w$. At fixed temperature, a larger acid constant means a smaller basic constant for that pair. Equal charge does not imply equal willingness to accept protons: the stability and equilibrium of each species matter.
