In $\mathrm{2Fe^{3+}+2I^-\rightarrow2Fe^{2+}+I_2}$, which species acts as the oxidising agent?

- $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$.
- $\mathrm{I_2}$.
- $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$.
- $\mathrm{I^-}$.

## Explanation

The oxidising agent is the reactant that accepts electrons and is reduced. Fe³⁺ becomes Fe²⁺, while iodide loses electrons to form molecular iodine. Fe²⁺ and I₂ are products in the stated direction; a reactant's role must not be confused with the species produced after transfer.
