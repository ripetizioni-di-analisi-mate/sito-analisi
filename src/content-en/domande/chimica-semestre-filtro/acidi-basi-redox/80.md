A reaction has $E^\circ>0$, but the actual composition gives $Q>K$. Which conclusion is correct?

- The equilibrium constant becomes negative.
- The system must be at equilibrium.
- Actual potential must remain positive because standard potential is positive.
- The reverse direction is favoured under actual conditions despite positive standard potential.

## Explanation

The sign of $E^\circ$ describes standard conditions. In the actual mixture, $Q>K$ gives $\Delta_rG>0$ for the written direction and thus $E<0$. The reverse transformation is favoured as composition approaches equilibrium. The constant remains positive; the actual composition relative to equilibrium has changed.
