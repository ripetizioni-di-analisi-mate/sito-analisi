Which property contributes to the buffering function of many proteins?

- Exclusively peptide bonds with no other groups.
- Ionisable groups whose constants can depend on their local environment.
- Side chains being unable to exchange protons with water.
- An identical $pK_a$ for every residue, independent of structure.

## Explanation

Ionisable terminal groups and side chains can accept or donate protons. Their constants depend on electrostatic environment and structure, so one value cannot describe every group in every protein. Effective capacity depends on pH and the amount of groups available near their respective titration regions.
