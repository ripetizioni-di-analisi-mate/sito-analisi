A cell reaction transfers $n=2$ electrons and has reversible electromotive force $E=0.150\,\mathrm{V}$ under the stated conditions. With $F=96485\,\mathrm{C\,mol^{-1}}$, what is $\Delta_rG$ per mole of reaction?

- About $-28945\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- About $+28.9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- About $-28.9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
- About $-14.5\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Use $\Delta_rG=-nFE=-2\cdot96485\cdot0.150=-28945.5\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}$. Divide by one thousand to convert to kilojoules. The negative sign agrees with positive potential in the written direction; omitting $n$ would incorrectly halve the energy associated with one mole of reaction.
