In acidic medium, $10.0\,\mathrm{mL}$ of $0.0200\,\mathrm{M}$ permanganate exactly oxidises the Fe²⁺ in a $25.0\,\mathrm{mL}$ sample. The stoichiometric ratio is $1$ MnO₄⁻ to $5$ Fe²⁺. What is the initial Fe²⁺ concentration?

- $0.100\,\mathrm{M}$.
- $0.00160\,\mathrm{M}$.
- $0.00800\,\mathrm{M}$.
- $0.0400\,\mathrm{M}$.

## Explanation

Permanganate amount is $0.0200\cdot0.0100=2.00\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{mol}$. Iron is five times this, or $1.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol}$. Initial concentration uses the sample volume, $0.0250\,\mathrm{L}$, giving $0.0400\,\mathrm{M}$, rather than the final titration-mixture volume.
