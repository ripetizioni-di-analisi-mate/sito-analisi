A weak monoprotic acid is moderately diluted in the regime $x\simeq\sqrt{K_aC}$ with negligible water contribution. How do hydronium concentration and dissociated fraction change?

- Hydronium decreases while the dissociated fraction increases.
- Both increase.
- Hydronium stays constant because $K_a$ is unchanged.
- Both decrease in the same proportion.

## Explanation

The reacted concentration follows $x\simeq\sqrt{K_aC}$, so it falls when $C$ falls. The fraction is instead $\dfrac{x}{C}\simeq\sqrt{\dfrac{K_a}{C}}$ and increases. A greater dissociation percentage can coexist with less hydronium per litre: absolute amount and fraction are different quantities.
