At a stated temperature, $pK_w=13.00$. In the ideal model, a solution has equal hydronium and hydroxide activities. What is its pH?

- $6.50$.
- $0.50$.
- $7.00$.
- $13.00$.

## Explanation

Equal activities imply equal pH and pOH. Their sum is $pK_w$, so each is $\dfrac{13.00}{2}=6.50$. The solution is neutral under the assigned conditions. Calling it acidic merely because the number is below seven would incorrectly import the reference from another temperature.
