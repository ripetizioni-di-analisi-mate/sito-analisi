Which half-reaction for reducing nitrate to nitrite is balanced in basic medium?

- $\mathrm{NO_3^-+H_2O+2e^-\rightarrow NO_2^-+2OH^-}$.
- $\mathrm{NO_3^-+2OH^-+2e^-\rightarrow NO_2^-+H_2O}$.
- $\mathrm{NO_3^-+H_2O\rightarrow NO_2^-+2OH^-+2e^-}$.
- $\mathrm{NO_3^-+H_2O+e^-\rightarrow NO_2^-+2OH^-}$.

## Explanation

In the correct form, each side contains one nitrogen, four oxygens, two hydrogens and total charge $-3$. Nitrogen changes from $+5$ to $+3$ and must gain two electrons. Electrons among the products would indicate oxidation; reversing water and hydroxide also breaks atom balance.
