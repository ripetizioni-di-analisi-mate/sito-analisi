For $\mathrm{O_2+4H^++4e^-\rightarrow2H_2O}$ at $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, assume ideal gas and unit water activity. At fixed pH, O₂ partial pressure increases by factor $100$. How much does reduction potential rise?

- About $0.118\,\mathrm{V}$.
- It does not rise: it falls by $0.0296\,\mathrm{V}$.
- About $0.0592\,\mathrm{V}$.
- About $0.0296\,\mathrm{V}$.

## Explanation

At fixed pH, the pressure contribution is $(\dfrac{0.05916}{4})\log_{10}(\dfrac{p_{\mathrm{O_2}}}{p^\circ})$. A hundredfold rise adds $(\dfrac{0.05916}{4})\cdot2=0.02958\,\mathrm{V}$. The factor four comes from the half-reaction's electrons; the sign is positive because oxygen is a reduction reactant.
