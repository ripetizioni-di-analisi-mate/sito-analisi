Which definition identifies the anode in both a galvanic cell and an electrolytic cell?

- It is always the negative electrode.
- It is the electrode where reduction occurs.
- It is the electrode where oxidation occurs.
- It is always the positive electrode.

## Explanation

Anode and cathode are defined by processes: oxidation at the former and reduction at the latter. The anode is negative in a discharging galvanic cell and positive in the usual externally powered electrolytic cell. Memorising only a sign without specifying cell type therefore gives an incomplete rule.
