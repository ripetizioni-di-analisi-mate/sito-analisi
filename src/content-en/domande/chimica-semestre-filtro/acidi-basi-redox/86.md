In the educational cycle linking superoxide and Fenton, which step regenerates iron in the state required for $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}+H_2O_2\rightarrow Fe^{3+}+OH^-+{}^\bullet OH}$?

- $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+H^+\rightarrow Fe^{2+}+H_2}$.
- $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}+O_2\rightarrow Fe^{3+}+O_2^{\bullet-}}$.
- $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+O_2^{\bullet-}\rightarrow Fe^{2+}+O_2}$.
- $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}+H_2O_2\rightarrow Fe^{3+}+OH^-+{}^\bullet OH}$.

## Explanation

Fenton requires Fe²⁺ and converts it to Fe³⁺. Regenerating the reactant therefore requires Fe³⁺ reduction; in this cycle, superoxide donates the electron and forms O₂. Fenton itself does not regenerate Fe²⁺. The actual cycle's rate and effectiveness also depend on metal coordination.
