The reaction $\mathrm{2H_2O_2\rightarrow2H_2O+O_2}$ is disproportionation. Why?

- No oxidation number changes.
- All oxygen atoms gain electrons.
- Oxygen initially at $-1$ partly becomes $-2$ and partly $0$.
- Hydrogen changes from $+1$ to $0$ in both products.

## Explanation

Oxygen is at $-2$ in water and $0$ in elemental O₂. Starting from $-1$, some is reduced and some oxidised: the same starting species participates in both roles. Hydrogen retains oxidation number $+1$ in this reaction.
