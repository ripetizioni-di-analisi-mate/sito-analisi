Ideal HCl has analytical concentration $C=10^{-8}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ at $25\,^\circ\mathrm C$. Including $K_w=10^{-14}$, what is its approximate pH?

- $8.00$.
- $6.98$.
- $6.00$.
- $7.30$.

## Explanation

Electroneutrality requires $h=C+\dfrac{K_w}{h}$, giving $h=\dfrac{C+\sqrt{C^2+4K_w}}{2}\simeq1.051\cdot10^{-7}$ in the molar convention. The resulting pH is about $6.98$. Using only $-\log_{10}C$ would incorrectly predict a basic solution: autoprotolysis is not negligible here.
