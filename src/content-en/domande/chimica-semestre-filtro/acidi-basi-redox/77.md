A concentration cell uses two identical copper electrodes at equal temperature but different Cu²⁺ activities. Neglect junction potential. Which statement is correct?

- It works only when both activities are equal.
- It must have $E=0$ because the metals are identical.
- The side with lower Cu²⁺ activity always has the higher reduction potential.
- It can have $E\ne0$ even though the standard-potential difference is zero.

## Explanation

The standard terms of the half-cells cancel, but their Nernst terms depend on activities. For Cu²⁺/Cu, reduction potential increases with ion activity: the more concentrated side tends to be the cathode in the ideal model. Available energy comes from the composition difference rather than different metals.
