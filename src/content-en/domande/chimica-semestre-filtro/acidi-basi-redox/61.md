What is the oxidation number of each oxygen in H₂O₂?

- $-1$.
- $+1$.
- $-2$.
- $0$.

## Explanation

Hydrogen bonded to this nonmetal has oxidation number $+1$. The molecule is neutral, so $2(+1)+2x=0$ and $x=-1$. Peroxide is an exception to the common assignment of $-2$ for oxygen. Oxidation number and the molecule's net charge are not the same quantity.
