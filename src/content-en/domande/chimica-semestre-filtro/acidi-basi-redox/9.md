An acetate buffer contains $0.0200\,\mathrm{mol}$ acetate and $0.0100\,\mathrm{mol}$ acetic acid in the same solution. Calculate pH with $pK_a=4.76$, assuming that the approximations of concentration-based Henderson–Hasselbalch apply.

- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq4.46$.
- $\mathrm{pH}=4.76$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq5.06$.
- $\mathrm{pH}=9.52$.

## Explanation

The concentration ratio equals the mole ratio because volume is common. Conjugate base is present in twice the acid amount, so $\mathrm{pH}=pK_a+\log_{10}2=5.06103\simeq5.06$. Reversing the ratio gives the lower-pH distractor. The excess of conjugate base is consistent with pH above the pair's logarithmic acid constant.
