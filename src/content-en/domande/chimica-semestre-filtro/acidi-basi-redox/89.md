A reactant pair has a redox reaction with $\Delta_rG<0$, yet the observed transformation is very slow. Which explanation is consistent?

- Negative $\Delta_rG$ means equilibrium has already been reached.
- Positive potential makes every collision effective.
- A high activation barrier limits rate despite favourable reaction energetics.
- A slow reaction must have $\Delta_rG>0$.

## Explanation

Reaction Gibbs energy compares reactants and products; rate depends on the pathway and barriers to cross. A favourable process can therefore be slow without a catalyst or accessible route. At equilibrium, reaction Gibbs energy would be zero, not merely negative.
