An ideal monoprotic acid with no other solutes and negligible autoprotolysis has $C=0.010\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and is $50\%$ dissociated. What $K_a$ follows using concentrations reduced to the molar standard?

- $1.0\cdot10^{-2}$.
- $5.0\cdot10^{-3}$.
- $0.50$.
- $2.5\cdot10^{-5}$.

## Explanation

At equilibrium $x=0.0050$ and $C-x=0.0050$ in the numerical molar convention. Thus $K_a=\dfrac{x^2}{C-x}=0.0050$. The dissociated fraction is not the constant; the product-concentration square must be divided by remaining acid.
