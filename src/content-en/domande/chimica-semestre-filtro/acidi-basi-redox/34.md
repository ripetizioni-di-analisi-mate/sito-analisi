An ideal phosphate system has successive pK values $2.15$, $7.20$ and $12.35$. At pH $9.0$, which form predominates?

- $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$.
- $\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}$.
- $\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}$.
- $\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}$.

## Explanation

pH is well above the second pK but well below the third. Loss of the second proton is therefore favoured, while the third remains limited: $\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}$ predominates. The most negative charge does not automatically identify the dominant species; locate pH relative to successive equilibria.
