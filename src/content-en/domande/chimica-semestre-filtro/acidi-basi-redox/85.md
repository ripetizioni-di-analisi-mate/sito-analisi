Superoxide reduction of Fe³⁺ is added to the Fenton reaction. Why can iron be absent from the cycle's net equation?

- Fe³⁺ and Fe²⁺ are consumed and regenerated in the two steps and cancel in the sum.
- Iron participates in no electron transfer.
- Iron accumulates only as Fe³⁺ and is omitted because its initial concentration is low.
- Fe³⁺ is a spectator and changes oxidation state in neither step.

## Explanation

An intermediate or catalyst can be essential in individual steps yet absent from their sum. Superoxide can regenerate Fe²⁺ from Fe³⁺; Fenton reoxidises Fe²⁺ using peroxide. Cancelling iron in the bookkeeping shows a possible cycle, not its absence from the mechanism or a rate independent of its availability.
