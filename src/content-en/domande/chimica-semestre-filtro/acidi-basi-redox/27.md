Two ideal solutions have the same analytical concentration of monoprotic acid and negligible water contribution. The first acid is fully dissociated; the second only partly dissociated. Which comparison is correct?

- Their pH values coincide because analytical concentrations coincide.
- The second must be basic because it is a weak acid.
- The first has more hydronium and lower pH.
- The second has more hydronium because it retains undissociated acid.

## Explanation

In the first case every acid unit supplies hydronium; in the second only a fraction reacts. At equal analytical concentration, hydronium is therefore greater for the fully dissociated acid and its negative logarithm is smaller. This comparison needs the stated assumptions and does not rank acids at arbitrary concentrations.
