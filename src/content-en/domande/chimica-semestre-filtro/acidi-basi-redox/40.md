In $1.00\,\mathrm L$ there are $0.010\,\mathrm{mol}$ HA and the same amount of $\mathrm{A^-}$. Add $0.015\,\mathrm{mol}$ HCl without changing volume. Neglecting the weak-acid contribution after reaction, what is pH?

- Approximately $1.82$, using all added HCl.
- Undefined, because the buffer ratio would contain a negative amount.
- Equal to the initial $pK_a$.
- Approximately $2.30$.

## Explanation

Conjugate base consumes $0.010\,\mathrm{mol}$ HCl, leaving $0.0050\,\mathrm{mol}$ strong-acid excess. In the given volume, $h=0.0050$ in the molar convention and $\mathrm{pH}\simeq2.30$. The buffer is exhausted: a negative amount in its formula signals the wrong model, not a physically undefined pH.
