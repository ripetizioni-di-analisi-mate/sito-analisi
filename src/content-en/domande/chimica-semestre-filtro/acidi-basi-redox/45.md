For a closed ideal buffer to be prepared at $\mathrm{pH}=7.40$, which pair is preferable at equal total concentration and otherwise equal conditions if a ratio of forms near unity is desired?

- A pair with $pK_a=6.10$.
- A pair with $pK_a=7.20$.
- A pair with $pK_a=4.76$.
- A pair with $pK_a=9.25$.

## Explanation

The Henderson–Hasselbalch relation gives a ratio of $10^{\mathrm{pH}-pK_a}$. The value nearest the target pH produces a ratio nearer one, avoiding a scarce form. Specifying a closed buffer distinguishes this comparison from bicarbonate physiology, where CO₂ exchange and regulation of the components are essential.
