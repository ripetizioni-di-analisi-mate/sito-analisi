A species can both donate and accept protons. What can be concluded without knowing its constants?

- Its solution must have exactly neutral pH.
- It is amphiprotic, but its solution pH is not yet determined.
- Its two reactions with water must have equal constants.
- It cannot form conjugate acid–base pairs.

## Explanation

Amphiprotic describes the ability to react in both roles. It does not require the tendencies to cancel or the equilibria to have equal constants. Predicting pH requires the relevant constants, concentration and system conditions; bicarbonate illustrates how basic behaviour can predominate.
