In the external metal wire of a discharging galvanic cell, how do net electron motion and conventional current compare?

- They are opposite: electrons travel from anode to cathode.
- They coincide: both travel from anode to cathode.
- They coincide: both travel from cathode to anode.
- They are opposite: electrons travel from cathode to anode.

## Explanation

Oxidation at the anode supplies electrons, while reduction at the cathode consumes them. Their net motion through the wire is therefore from anode to cathode. Conventional current is defined in the direction of positive-charge motion and is opposite to the motion of negatively charged electrons.
