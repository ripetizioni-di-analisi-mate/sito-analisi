Manganese in MnO₄⁻ is reduced to Mn²⁺ in one case and MnO₂ in another. How many electrons does it gain per manganese, respectively?

- $7$ and $4$.
- $5$ and $3$.
- $3$ and $5$.
- $5$ and $5$.

## Explanation

Manganese is at $+7$ in permanganate. Reaching $+2$ requires five electrons; manganese is at $+4$ in MnO₂ and requires three. Recognising the reactant is insufficient to set electron count: the product must be known, and it can depend on reaction conditions.
