For $\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-/HPO_4^{2-}}$, assume $pK_a=7.20$ under the stated conditions. What $\dfrac{[\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}]}{[\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}]}$ ratio corresponds to pH $7.40$ in the ideal buffer model?

- Approximately $0.63$.
- Approximately $1.58$.
- $0.20$.
- $10.0$.

## Explanation

Henderson–Hasselbalch gives $\log_{10}r=\mathrm{pH}-pK_a=0.20$, where $r$ is the base-to-acid ratio. Thus $r=10^{0.20}=1.58489\simeq1.58$. A ratio greater than one agrees with pH above $pK_a$. The pH difference is the logarithm of the ratio, not the ratio itself.
