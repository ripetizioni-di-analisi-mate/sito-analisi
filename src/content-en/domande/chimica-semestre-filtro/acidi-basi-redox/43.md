An ideal buffer is moderately diluted with water. Considering only the buffer pair's contribution and neglecting water autoionisation, what happens to capacity per litre and total moles available in the entire vessel?

- Both remain unchanged because the A⁻/HA ratio is unchanged.
- Capacity per litre decreases; total moles of the pair remain unchanged.
- Both increase in proportion to volume.
- Capacity per litre is unchanged; total moles decrease.

## Explanation

Dilution lowers both concentrations without removing solute. Less buffer pair is available per litre, while total moles in the vessel are conserved. Under the stated model, the ratio and thus approximately the pH remain unchanged. A quantity expressed per unit volume must be distinguished from the total reserve in the sample.
