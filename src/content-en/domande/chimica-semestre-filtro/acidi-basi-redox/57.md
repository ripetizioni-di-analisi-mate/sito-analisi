At fixed temperature, more pure solid of the same salt is added to an already saturated solution, keeping solution volume and solid phase unchanged. What happens to $K_{sp}$?

- It doubles if solid mass doubles.
- It remains unchanged.
- It becomes equal to total ion concentration because the solution is saturated.
- It decreases through the common-ion effect, even without net dissolution.

## Explanation

The constant depends on the equilibrium and temperature, not on the amount of an already present pure phase. Pure-solid activity is incorporated into the constant's convention. Increasing its mass can extend the phase's availability, but does not change $K_{sp}$ under the stated conditions.
