In a constant-volume sample of $0.100\,\mathrm{L}$, adding $0.0020\,\mathrm{mol}$ of strong base raises pH by $0.20$. What is the mean buffer capacity $\overline{\beta}=\dfrac{\Delta n}{V\,\Delta\mathrm{pH}}$ over this interval?

- $0.0040\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ per pH unit.
- $0.100\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ per pH unit.
- $0.010\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ per pH unit.
- $1.00\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ per pH unit.

## Explanation

Divide the added amount by both volume and pH change: $0.0020/(0.100\cdot0.20)=0.100$. This is a mean capacity over the interval, not the differential value at one point. Moles alone cannot compare samples with different volumes.
