For $\mathrm{Zn(s)+Cu^{2+}\rightleftharpoons Zn^{2+}+Cu(s)}$, which quotient enters the Nernst relation when both pure metals are present?

- $Q=\dfrac{a_{\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}}}{a_{\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}}}$.
- $Q=\dfrac{a_{\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}}}{a_{\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}}}$.
- $Q=a_{\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}}+a_{\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}}$.
- $Q=\dfrac{m_{\mathrm{Cu}}\,a_{\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}}}{m_{\mathrm{Zn}}\,a_{\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}}}$.

## Explanation

Products are divided by reactants with stoichiometric exponents. Pure-solid activities, for the phases present, are unity under the adopted convention and are not replaced by electrode masses. Their amounts can limit how long the cell operates, but do not appear in this quotient.
