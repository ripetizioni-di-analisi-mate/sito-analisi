Which transformation is a neutralisation without changes in oxidation numbers?

- $\mathrm{Zn+Cu^{2+}\rightarrow Zn^{2+}+Cu}$.
- $\mathrm{2Fe^{2+}+Cl_2\rightarrow2Fe^{3+}+2Cl^-}$.
- $\mathrm{HCl+NaOH\rightarrow NaCl+H_2O}$.
- $\mathrm{2H_2O_2\rightarrow2H_2O+O_2}$.

## Explanation

In neutralisation, the net event is acid–base transfer forming water; oxidation numbers are unchanged. The other transformations change those of zinc and copper, iron and chlorine, or oxygen. Ions or spontaneity alone do not establish that a process is redox.
