Two ideal buffers of the same pair have equal volumes and equal A⁻/HA ratios. Both concentrations are ten times higher in the second. Which comparison is correct, away from extreme dilution?

- Their pH values are approximately equal, but the second better resists the same small acid addition.
- The second has pH one unit lower and equal resistance to acid.
- The second has pH one unit higher and equal resistance to acid.
- Equal pH necessarily means equal resistance to acid.

## Explanation

The pH depends on the ratio of conjugate forms, which is unchanged. Buffer capacity also depends on the available amount: at equal volume, the second contains more A⁻ to protonate and more HA. The same small addition therefore changes its ratio less. Equal initial pH does not imply an equal response to a disturbance.
