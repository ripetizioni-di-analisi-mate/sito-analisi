A monoprotic acid HA has analytical concentration $0.0400\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and $K_a=1.00\cdot10^{-5}$ at $25\,^\circ\mathrm C$. Calculate pH in the ideal dilute model, neglecting water autoprotolysis.

- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq3.20$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq1.40$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq10.80$.
- $\mathrm{pH}=5.00$.

## Explanation

Writing $x=[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]$, equilibrium requires $\dfrac{x^2}{0.0400-x}=10^{-5}$. The positive root is $x=6.275\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, giving $\mathrm{pH}=3.2024\simeq3.20$. The dissociated fraction is about $0.0157$, consistent with small dissociation. The constant alone is not hydronium concentration; total acid concentration also enters the balance.
