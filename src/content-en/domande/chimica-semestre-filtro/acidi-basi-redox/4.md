A $\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2}$ solution has concentration $2.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ at $25\,^\circ\mathrm C$. Calculate pH assuming complete dissociation, ideal dilute behaviour and $pK_w=14.00$.

- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq11.60$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq12.00$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq2.40$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq11.30$.

## Explanation

Each formula unit releases two hydroxides, so $[\mathrm{OH^-}]=4.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. This gives $\mathrm{pOH}=2.39794$ and $\mathrm{pH}=14.00-2.39794=11.60206$. Using solute concentration directly would miss the stoichiometric factor. The pOH is the acidic-looking number among the options; it is not the requested pH.
