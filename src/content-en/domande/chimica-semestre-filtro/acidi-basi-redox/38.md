A dilute NaCl solution, initially neutral in a model without dissolved gases, absorbs $\mathrm{CO_2}$ from air at fixed temperature and its pH falls. Which explanation is consistent?

- Chloride has become the conjugate base of a weak acid.
- The new carbonic equilibrium can produce hydronium; significant acidic chloride hydrolysis is not needed.
- Merely containing a salt necessarily imposes pH below seven.
- The gas necessarily changes the water ion product at the same temperature.

## Explanation

Gas absorption changes composition, introducing equilibria absent from the initial model. The change does not show that NaCl ions have acquired a new acid–base strength. This case illustrates why closed-system assumptions or the absence of reactive gases must be explicit.
