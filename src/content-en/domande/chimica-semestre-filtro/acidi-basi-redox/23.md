Equal volumes of two ideal HCl solutions are mixed, one at pH $2$ and the other at pH $4$. Assuming additive volumes and negligible water contribution, what is the final pH?

- Approximately $2.30$.
- Approximately $2.00$.
- $3.00$.
- $6.00$.

## Explanation

For equal volumes, average hydronium concentration, not pH: $h=\dfrac{10^{-2}+10^{-4}}{2}=5.05\cdot10^{-3}$ in the numerical molar convention. Thus $-\log_{10}h\simeq2.30$. Both contributions are acidic; no neutralisation occurs and their mole amounts are not subtracted.
