Does $\mathrm{pH}=-\log_{10}a_{\mathrm{H^+}}$ mathematically rule out negative pH?

- No: proton activity greater than one gives negative pH.
- Yes: pH is a concentration and cannot be negative.
- No, but only if proton activity is negative.
- Yes: the logarithm of every positive number is negative.

## Explanation

The logarithm of a number greater than one is positive, so the minus sign gives negative pH. Activity remains positive and dimensionless. The familiar interval for many dilute aqueous solutions is not a limit of the definition; in concentrated solutions, indiscriminately replacing activity by concentration is especially inappropriate.
