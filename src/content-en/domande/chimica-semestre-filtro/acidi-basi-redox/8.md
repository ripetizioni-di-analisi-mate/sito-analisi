A titration has a steep pH rise containing its equivalence point at pH $8.8$. Which proposed indicator range is most suitable if it must include that point?

- From pH $6.0$ to pH $7.0$.
- From pH $8.2$ to pH $10.0$.
- From pH $3.1$ to pH $4.4$.
- From pH $1.0$ to pH $2.0$.

## Explanation

The given equivalence pH, $8.8$, lies in $8.2$–$10.0$. The other ranges end at lower pH. Indicator selection concerns its colour-change range relative to the steep titration region. This question explicitly requires inclusion of equivalence; in an actual titration, check the complete curve rather than assuming that equivalence is always neutral.
