In a controlled experiment, a chelator reduces catalytically active iron availability and substrate oxidation rate without interfering with the measurement. Which conclusion is justified by these data alone?

- The lower rate necessarily proves a smaller equilibrium constant for the overall reaction.
- Hydroxyl radical is proved to be the only intermediate in every step.
- Every chelator is proved to prevent all oxidative damage.
- The result supports a role for available iron but does not by itself identify every mechanistic intermediate.

## Explanation

The control links metal availability to the observed rate, consistent with an iron-mediated pathway. Identifying intermediates still requires further measurements and specific controls. Chelators can behave differently with complex and conditions; one experiment establishes neither universal protection nor, by itself, a change in the equilibrium constant.
