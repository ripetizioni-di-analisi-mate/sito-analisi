$10\,\mathrm{mL}$ of $0.100\,\mathrm{M}$ HA is mixed with $5\,\mathrm{mL}$ of $0.200\,\mathrm{M}$ NaA. In the ideal model, at concentrations suitable for the buffer approximation, which relation holds?

- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq pK_a-0.301$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq \dfrac{pK_a}{2}$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq pK_a+0.301$.
- $\mathrm{pH}\simeq pK_a$.

## Explanation

Calculate moles before forming the ratio: each solution supplies $1.00\,\mathrm{mmol}$. The concentrations of both forms are therefore equal in the common final volume, making the logarithmic term zero. Using the initial concentration ratio directly would ignore the different volumes added.
