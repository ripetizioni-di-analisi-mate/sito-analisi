During bromine addition to an alkene through a bromonium intermediate, which stereochemical outcome is typical of opening?

- An obligatory 1:1 syn/anti mixture because bromonium loses all stereochemical information.
- Anti addition through attack opposite the bridge.
- Nonstereospecific addition through a freely rotating acyclic carbocation.
- Exclusively syn addition because both Br atoms attack from the same face.

## Explanation

The bromonium bridge obstructs same-face attack and is opened from the opposite face, typically giving anti addition of the two bromines. This intermediate retains a stereochemical constraint and is not equivalent to a freely rotating acyclic carbocation. An equimolar syn/anti mixture therefore does not follow; absolute configurations are assigned from the complete product.
