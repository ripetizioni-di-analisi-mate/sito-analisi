In butan-2-ol, what makes the OH-bearing carbon stereogenic?

- Rotation around C–O alone generates two configurations.
- It is tetrahedral and bears H, OH, methyl and ethyl.
- It is planar and bears three different groups.
- Bearing OH makes it stereogenic even with two identical methyl groups.

## Explanation

The four carbon substituents are distinct. Ethyl and methyl both begin with carbon but differ at the next part of the chain. The corresponding carbon in propan-2-ol instead has two identical methyl groups. Ordinary single-bond rotation changes a conformation without exchanging two configurations of the centre.
