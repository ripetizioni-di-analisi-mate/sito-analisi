Which criterion places the locant series $2,3,5$ before $2,4,4$ in ordinary substituent nomenclature?

- The last locant, which takes precedence over earlier ones.
- The first locant, which is lower in the first series.
- The first point of difference, where 3 precedes 4.
- The sum of locants, which is lower in the first sequence.

## Explanation

The first values are equal, so the second values are compared: 3 is lower than 4. Both sums equal ten and do not resolve the choice. The criterion compares the ordered series at the first point of difference, without giving precedence to the final locant or treating equal initial locants as different.
