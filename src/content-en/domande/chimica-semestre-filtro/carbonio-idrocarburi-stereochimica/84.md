In planar cyclopentadienyl anion, two double bonds supply four pi electrons. Which contribution completes the aromatic system?

- The charged carbon’s pair in a p orbital brings the total to six.
- All five ring sigma bonds are added to the pi count.
- Negative charge removes two electrons, giving two.
- The charged carbon supplies only one electron, giving five.

## Explanation

In the stated planar model, the pair occupies a p orbital completing ring conjugation. Four electrons from the double bonds plus two from the pair give six, consistent with Hückel. Sigma bonding and the pi system are counted separately; negative charge is not an electron subtraction.
