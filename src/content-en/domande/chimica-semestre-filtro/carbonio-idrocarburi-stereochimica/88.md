Compared with benzene, chlorobenzene is deactivated towards many electrophilic substitutions. Which relative orientation is nevertheless typical?

- Ortho and para.
- No orientation, because induction and resonance remove all differences.
- Meta and para, because both ortho positions would always be inaccessible.
- Only meta, because every deactivator is meta-directing.

## Explanation

Halogens withdraw inductively and slow reaction relative to benzene. Resonance donation nevertheless relatively favours ortho and para intermediates. Overall rate and positional selection are distinct properties, so this exception prevents automatic identification of deactivation with meta direction.
