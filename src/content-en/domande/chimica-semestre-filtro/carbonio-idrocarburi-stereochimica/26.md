A molecule has two stereogenic centres and symmetry making its mirror image superposable. How is it classified?

- As a meso species, overall achiral.
- As a racemate even though every molecule is identical.
- As chiral because counting centres is sufficient.
- As an obligatory RR/SS mixture.

## Explanation

Chirality concerns the whole structure. Superposability with its mirror excludes distinct enantiomers for the described species despite its stereogenic centres. Meso describes this combination. A racemate instead denotes an equimolar mixture of two enantiomeric species, not a single symmetrical molecule.
