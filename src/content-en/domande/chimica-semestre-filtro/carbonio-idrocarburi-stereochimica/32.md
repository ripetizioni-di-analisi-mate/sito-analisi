Why does CHO outrank $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ in glyceraldehyde CIP priorities?

- Any group with fewer hydrogens outranks every other group.
- The CHO carbon has a greater atomic number than the other carbon.
- The carbonyl compares as O,O,H, whereas the other group compares as O,H,H.
- Priority follows from CHO being at the top of the Fischer projection.

## Explanation

Both directly attached atoms are carbons, so the initial comparison is tied. C=O is handled using a formal oxygen duplicate. Comparing the ordered lists, the aldehyde group’s second O outranks H. Position on the page does not alter these structural priorities.
