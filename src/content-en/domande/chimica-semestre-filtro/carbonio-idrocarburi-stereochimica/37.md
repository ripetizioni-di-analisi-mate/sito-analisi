Pure R has $[\alpha]=-18\,\mathrm{{}^\circ mL\,g^{-1}\,dm^{-1}}$. What numerical value, in the same units, does pure S have in the same achiral solvent at the same concentration, temperature and wavelength?

- $0$.
- $+18$.
- $+36$.
- $-18$.

## Explanation

An enantiomeric pair has opposite specific rotations under identical achiral conditions. Because R’s value is experimentally supplied here, S’s value follows by reversing the sign. This does not mean that S is always dextrorotatory: the conclusion uses the datum for this particular pair.
