2-Bromobutane undergoes elimination with an unhindered base under conditions favouring Zaitsev orientation. Which alkene family is generally favoured?

- But-2-ene, the more substituted alkene.
- Buta-1,3-diene through loss of one HBr.
- 2-Methylpropene without any skeletal rearrangement.
- But-1-ene, because every elimination must give a terminal double bond.

## Explanation

Elimination from the two possible beta carbons gives a terminal or internal double bond. In the stated Zaitsev comparison, the more substituted internal alkene is favoured. This identifies a constitutional family rather than requiring one exclusive stereoisomer; conditions and geometry also influence E/Z distribution.
