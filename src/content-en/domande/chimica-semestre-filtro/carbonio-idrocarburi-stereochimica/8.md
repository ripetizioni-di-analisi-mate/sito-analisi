How many distinct configurational stereoisomers does 2,3-dichlorobutane have when internal symmetry is considered?

- $4$.
- $2$.
- $3$.
- $1$.

## Explanation

Two centres suggest at most four assignments. RR and SS form an enantiomeric pair, while RS and SR describe the same meso form because of symmetry. The total is therefore three. Automatically using the power of two would count an identical structure twice.
