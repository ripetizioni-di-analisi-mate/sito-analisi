In the usual alphabetical ordering of simple substituents, which entry comes first: ethyl or dimethyl?

- Dimethyl, because two substituents always precede one.
- It depends only on the two groups’ total mass.
- Dimethyl, because di- is included in alphabetical ordering.
- Ethyl, because ethyl and methyl are compared.

## Explanation

The multiplying prefix di- indicates two methyl groups and is disregarded in ordinary alphabetical ordering of simple substituents. Ethyl is therefore compared with methyl and comes first. Name ordering is neither CIP priority nor a ranking based on the number of groups.
