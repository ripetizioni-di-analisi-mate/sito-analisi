Which feature identifies a heterocycle?

- An OH group is attached outside an all-carbon ring.
- At least one heteroatom belongs to the ring skeleton.
- The ring contains at least one double bond.
- Two ring carbons bear different substituents.

## Explanation

Classification depends on the atoms forming the cycle. Replacing a ring carbon with nitrogen or oxygen produces a heterocycle; adding an external oxygen does not change an all-carbon ring skeleton. Aromaticity, unsaturation and substitution are separate classifications rather than the required criterion.
