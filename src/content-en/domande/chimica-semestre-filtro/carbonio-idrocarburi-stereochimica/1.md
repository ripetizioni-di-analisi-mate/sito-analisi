How many $\sigma$ and $\pi$ bonds are present in an ethyne molecule, $\mathrm{HC{\equiv}CH}$?

- One $\sigma$ and two $\pi$.
- Five $\sigma$ and no $\pi$.
- Three $\sigma$ and two $\pi$.
- Two $\sigma$ and three $\pi$.

## Explanation

The two C–H bonds are sigma bonds. The triple bond contains one sigma and two pi bonds, giving three sigma and two pi overall. Counting the triple bond as three sigma bonds confuses multiplicity with overlap type; counting only C–C omits the two bonds to hydrogen.
