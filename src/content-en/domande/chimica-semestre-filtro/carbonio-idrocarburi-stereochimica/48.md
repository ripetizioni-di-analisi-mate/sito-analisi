Which reasoning is appropriate when assessing carbanion stability?

- Use steric hindrance alone without electronic effects.
- Consider resonance, withdrawing groups and the orbital containing the pair.
- Always apply the carbocation tertiary > secondary > primary order.
- Ignore delocalization because it concerns only positive charges.

## Explanation

A carbanion contains additional electron density and can be stabilized by effects that distribute or attract it. Hybridization and context also matter. Stabilizing trends for an electron-deficient centre cannot automatically be copied: opposite charge and different orbitals change the interpretation.
