Benzene and some nucleic-acid bases are aromatic but have very different biological roles and toxicological profiles. What follows?

- An aromatic system always prevents bioactivation.
- Aromaticity alone does not determine toxicity, which also depends on structure, metabolism and exposure.
- Every aromatic substance forms the same metabolites as benzene.
- Natural origin makes the toxicological profile independent of dose and metabolic transformations.

## Explanation

Aromaticity describes electronic and structural properties, while functional groups and metabolic pathways may differ greatly. Benzene and some polycyclic hydrocarbons illustrate harmful metabolite formation; nitrogenous bases show that shared aromaticity does not imply shared biological reactivity or toxicity.
