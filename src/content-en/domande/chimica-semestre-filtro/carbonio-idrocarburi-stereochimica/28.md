The (2R,3R) and meso-(2R,3S) forms of 2,3-dichlorobutane are compared. What is their relationship?

- Conformations of the same configuration.
- Diastereoisomers.
- Enantiomers.
- Constitutional isomers.

## Explanation

The forms have identical connectivity and different configurations, but the meso form is achiral and is not the nonsuperposable mirror image of RR. The mirror of RR is SS. The proposed pair is therefore diastereomeric; the comparison must use whole structures and the meso form’s symmetry.
