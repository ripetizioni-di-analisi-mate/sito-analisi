For an ideal SN2 reaction at unchanged temperature and solvent, $[\mathrm{RX}]$ doubles and $[\mathrm{Nu^-}]$ triples. By what factor does the initial rate change?

- $2$.
- $6$.
- $3$.
- $5$.

## Explanation

The bimolecular law is $v=k[\mathrm{RX}][\mathrm{Nu^-}]$. With constant k, the two concentration factors multiply: $2\cdot3=6$. Adding them does not follow the law. Using only one factor would omit one reactant participating in the model’s concerted step.
