Enantiomeric excess is to be inferred from mixture rotation using the pure-enantiomer value. Which additional condition is required by the simple model?

- Several chiral compounds, provided each has one centre.
- Additive contributions and no other optically active species.
- Unknown total concentration, because the ratio always removes every difference.
- Any solvent, provided the rotation sign agrees.

## Explanation

The comparison requires matching conditions and an additive response from the pair. Other active species can contribute rotation without being either enantiomer being quantified. Concentration and path length must also be controlled or normalized. Without these conditions, reduced rotation does not directly measure a particular enantiomeric excess.
