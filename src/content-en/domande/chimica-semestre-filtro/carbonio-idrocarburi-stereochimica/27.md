Two compounds with identical connectivity and no symmetry identifying different centres are $(2R,3R,4S)$ and $(2R,3S,4S)$. Which relationship is most specific?

- C3 epimers.
- Constitutional isomers.
- The same configuration after bond rotation.
- Enantiomers.

## Explanation

Only C3 changes configuration; C2 and C4 remain unchanged. They are epimers, a particular type of diastereoisomer. The mirror pair would invert every centre in this model without identifying symmetries. Conformational rotation does not exchange R and S at a centre while retaining connectivity.
