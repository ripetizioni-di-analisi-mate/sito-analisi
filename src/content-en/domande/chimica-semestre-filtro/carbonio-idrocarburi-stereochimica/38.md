A solution has zero optical rotation. What conclusion is justified without further information about substance and composition?

- The substance cannot contain stereogenic centres.
- The datum alone does not distinguish an achiral substance from a racemate.
- Every molecule must be achiral.
- The solution must contain an equimolar R/S pair.

## Explanation

Cancellation of two enantiomeric contributions can give zero, but an achiral species can produce the same result. A meso form provides an example with stereogenic centres but no overall chirality. The measurement therefore permits several explanations, requiring structural or compositional data to distinguish them.
