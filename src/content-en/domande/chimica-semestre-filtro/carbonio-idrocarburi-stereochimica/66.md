In neopentyl bromide, $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CCH_2Br}$, which requirement for ordinary E2 loss of HBr across the bond adjacent to C–Br is missing?

- A hydrogen on the quaternary beta carbon.
- A beta carbon attached to the bromine-bearing carbon.
- A leaving group.
- A hydrogen on the bromine-bearing carbon.

## Explanation

The alpha carbon is CH₂Br and has hydrogens, but the beta carbon is attached to three methyls and the alpha carbon, leaving no H. E2 requires beta-hydrogen removal to create the double bond between those carbons. Increasing base strength does not supply the missing structural hydrogen.
