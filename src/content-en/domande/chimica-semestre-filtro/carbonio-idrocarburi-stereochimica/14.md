How many $\pi$ electrons participate in benzene’s aromatic system?

- $12$.
- $3$.
- $6$.
- $4$.

## Explanation

Each of the six sp² carbons supplies one electron to the p-orbital system. The three pairs represented by double bonds in a Kekulé formula therefore give six pi electrons. Three counts pairs instead of electrons; twelve adds sigma contributions that do not belong to the pi system.
