In a glyceraldehyde Fischer projection, CHO is above, $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ below, OH right and H left. What is the centre’s absolute configuration?

- Achiral because the Fischer drawing is planar.
- $S$.
- $R$.
- Unassignable without knowing optical rotation.

## Explanation

Priorities are OH, CHO, $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$, H: the carbon groups compare as O,O,H against O,H,H. The path on the page is anticlockwise, but horizontal H points forwards. Reversing the sense gives R. Optical measurement is not part of CIP assignment.
