In pyrimidine, how are the two pyridine-like nitrogen lone pairs treated in aromatic counting?

- Nitrogens supply no p electrons, so only the four carbon pi electrons are counted.
- They are not added as pairs to the six-pi-electron system.
- Both are added, necessarily bringing the total to ten.
- Only one pair is added, giving eight, because the two nitrogens would play different roles.

## Explanation

Each pyridine-like nitrogen supplies one electron through its p orbital, while its lone pair occupies an orbital in the ring plane. Four carbon contributions plus two nitrogen contributions give six pi electrons. Adding one or both lone pairs would count electrons outside pyrimidine’s aromatic circuit.
