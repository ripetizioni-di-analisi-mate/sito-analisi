Which comparison of methyl and nitro substituents on benzene matches their usual electrophilic-substitution effects?

- Both activate and direct exclusively para.
- Methyl deactivates and directs meta; nitro activates and directs ortho/para.
- Methyl activates and directs ortho/para; nitro deactivates and directs meta.
- Both deactivate and direct ortho/para through the same electronic effect.

## Explanation

Methyl helps stabilize ortho/para attack intermediates through donating effects, while nitro withdraws density and particularly disfavors certain intermediates, making meta relatively preferred. Direction is a tendency under suitable conditions, not a guarantee of one product regardless of steric effects or other substituents.
