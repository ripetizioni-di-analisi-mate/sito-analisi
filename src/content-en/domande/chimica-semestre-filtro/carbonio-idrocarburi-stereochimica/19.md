At equal carbon count, how does hydrogen count change from a saturated acyclic alkane to a saturated monocyclic cycloalkane?

- It remains unchanged.
- It increases by two.
- It decreases by two.
- It decreases by four.

## Explanation

Ring closure adds a C–C connection replacing two C–H bonds. The formula changes from $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n+2}}$ to $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n}}$. This comparison involves one ring and no multiple bonds; two independent rings or a triple bond would require a different hydrogen deficit.
