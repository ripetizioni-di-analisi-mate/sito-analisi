In Hückel’s simple monocyclic model, which set of conditions supports aromaticity?

- An open chain with six $\pi$ electrons and no cyclic closure.
- A planar ring, continuous conjugation and $4n+2$ $\pi$ electrons.
- Any ring with $4n+2$ atoms, even if all are $sp^3$.
- A planar conjugated ring with $4n$ $\pi$ electrons.

## Explanation

Counting concerns pi electrons in a conjugated circuit rather than total atom count. Cyclic structure, planarity and orbital continuity are part of the model. A planar conjugated four-n system is antiaromatic; an open chain may be conjugated without being aromatic.
