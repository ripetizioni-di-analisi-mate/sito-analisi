In complete combustion of $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ of butane, how many moles of $\mathrm{O_2}$ are stoichiometrically required?

- $10\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $13\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $26\,\mathrm{mol}$.

## Explanation

The balance is $2\mathrm{C_4H_{10}}+13\mathrm{O_2}\longrightarrow8\mathrm{CO_2}+10\mathrm{H_2O}$. The products contain twenty-six oxygen atoms in the written equation, supplied by thirteen oxygen molecules. The coefficient also applies to moles; confusing O atoms with O₂ molecules doubles the requirement.
