A centre is directly bonded to O, N, C and H. Which CIP comparison is made first?

- Partial charge calculated over the whole group.
- Total masses of the four substituents.
- Atomic numbers of the directly attached atoms.
- Overall lengths of the four chains.

## Explanation

The first difference already occurs among directly attached atoms, so O outranks N, followed by C and H. Total mass and group length need not be summed. Later layers matter only when the initial comparison is tied, as for two groups beginning with carbon.
