What is the molecular formula of $\mathrm{CH_3CH(CH_3)CH_2CH_2CH_3}$?

- $\mathrm{C_5H_{12}}$.
- $\mathrm{C_6H_{12}}$.
- $\mathrm{C_7H_{16}}$.
- $\mathrm{C_6H_{14}}$.

## Explanation

The written chain contains five carbons and the parenthesized group adds one. Hydrogens total $3+1+3+2+2+3=14$. This agrees with the saturated acyclic alkane formula. Branching changes connectivity but does not remove two hydrogens as ring closure would.
