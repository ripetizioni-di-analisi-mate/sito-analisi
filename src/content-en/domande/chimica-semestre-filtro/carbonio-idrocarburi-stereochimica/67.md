In a cyclohexane chair with Br as leaving group, which pair can satisfy trans-diaxial E2 geometry?

- Equatorial Br and an axial H on a nonadjacent carbon.
- Axial Br and an H on the same carbon.
- Equatorial Br and equatorial beta H regardless of dihedral angle.
- Axial Br and an opposite axial beta H on an adjacent carbon.

## Explanation

The chair constrains bond directions. A trans-diaxial pair on adjacent carbons supplies the antiperiplanar alignment of the ordinary model. A chair flip may therefore be needed to make the leaving group axial. The appropriate beta position must also actually contain hydrogen.
