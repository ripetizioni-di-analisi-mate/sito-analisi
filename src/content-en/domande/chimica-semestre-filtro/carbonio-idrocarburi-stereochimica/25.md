What relationship is shown by staggered and eclipsed Newman projections of ethane?

- Resonance contributors with different pi-electron locations.
- Configurational enantiomers stably separable at ordinary temperature.
- Constitutional isomers with different C–H connectivity.
- Conformations interconverted by C–C rotation.

## Explanation

Ethane connectivity remains unchanged while the dihedral angle around C–C varies. The torsional barrier gives the conformations different energies but allows rapid interconversion under ordinary conditions. Ethane has no pi system for these two projections to represent as resonance contributors.
