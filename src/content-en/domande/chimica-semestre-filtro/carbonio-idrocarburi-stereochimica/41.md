During $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ homolysis, how are the two bonding electrons distributed?

- A pair remains shared between the atoms, retaining the sigma bond.
- One to each atom, forming two Cl⁻ anions.
- Both to one atom, forming Cl⁻ and Cl⁺.
- One to each atom, forming two radicals.

## Explanation

Homolysis means symmetrical division of the pair: each chlorine retains an unpaired electron. Heterolysis would instead give the pair to one fragment and produce opposite charges in this case. Electrons are conserved; their different partitioning determines different reactive species.
