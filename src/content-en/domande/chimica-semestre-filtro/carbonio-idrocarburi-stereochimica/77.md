Anti-Markovnikov orientation is observed for HBr addition with peroxides under suitable radical conditions. Which extension is justified?

- Peroxides prove that the ionic pathway’s same carbocation forms.
- The result depends on the radical pathway and does not automatically extend to HCl and HI.
- Peroxides turn every addition into SN2 at sp² carbon.
- All hydrogen halides necessarily show the same peroxide effect.

## Explanation

Mechanism and chain-step energetics depend on the halogen. The peroxide effect is the classical HBr case and does not justify a blanket rule for all HX. Orientation must be interpreted through radical intermediates, distinguished from ionic-protonation intermediates.
