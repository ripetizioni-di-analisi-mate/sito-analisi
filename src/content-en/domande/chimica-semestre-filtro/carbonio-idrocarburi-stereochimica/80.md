In 1,4 HBr addition to buta-1,3-diene, H attaches to C1 and Br to C4. Where does the double bond remain, retaining the original numbering?

- Between C3 and C4.
- No double bond remains after one HBr addition.
- Between C1 and C2.
- Between C2 and C3.

## Explanation

The allylic intermediate allows delocalization and attack at the opposite terminal carbon. The 1,4 product retains a double bond between the internal carbons. Adding one HBr molecule consumes only one diene unsaturation unit; saturating both double bonds would require an additional transformation.
