A hydrocarbon has formula $\mathrm{C_6H_{10}}$. Which combination is incompatible with its degree of unsaturation, assuming ordinary valences?

- Two double bonds and no ring.
- One ring and one double bond.
- One triple bond and no ring.
- One ring and one triple bond.

## Explanation

The count gives $I=\dfrac{2\cdot6+2-10}{2}=2$. Two double bonds, a ring plus a double bond, or one triple bond each use two units. A ring plus a triple bond requires three and is incompatible. The calculation excludes a combination without uniquely identifying the remaining structures.
