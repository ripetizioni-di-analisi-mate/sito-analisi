Why does cyclopropane have substantial angle strain?

- Ring geometry imposes angles far from the tetrahedral ideal.
- Angle strain depends exclusively on C–H eclipsing.
- The ring attains every tetrahedral angle exactly without distortion.
- Ring carbons must be sp², so the only ideal reference angle is 120°.

## Explanation

The three-membered ring imposes C–C connection angles near 60°, far from the roughly 109.5° tetrahedral reference. Bonds accommodate the distortion, but ideal tetrahedral geometry cannot be assumed. Eclipsing adds torsional strain: that contribution is distinct from the angle deviation asked about here.
