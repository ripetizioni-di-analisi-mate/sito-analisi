Hexane and 2-methylpentane have the same molecular formula. What relationship connects them?

- Resonance contributors.
- Enantiomerism.
- Constitutional isomerism.
- Conformations of the same connectivity.

## Explanation

In hexane the six carbons form a continuous chain; in 2-methylpentane a five-carbon chain bears a branch. Nuclear connectivity changes, making the relationship constitutional. Bond rotation does not alter those connections, while resonance redistributes electrons without moving atoms.
