A mixture contains only two enantiomers, with mole fractions $x_R=0.75$ and $x_S=0.25$. What is its enantiomeric excess?

- $100\%$ because the fractions sum to one.
- $75\%$ in favour of R.
- $50\%$ in favour of R.
- $25\%$ in favour of S.

## Explanation

The fraction difference is $0.75-0.25=0.50$. Multiplying by one hundred gives fifty percent excess in favour of R. The majority component’s proportion is not the excess: part of R is matched by an equal amount of S in the enantiomeric comparison.
