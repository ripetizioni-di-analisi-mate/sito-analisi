Which relative arrangement of the beta C–H and C–Br bonds favours an ordinary E2 reaction in an accessible conformation?

- Antiperiplanar, with a dihedral angle near 180°.
- Any arrangement, provided the carbon bears Br.
- Perpendicular, with a dihedral angle near 90°.
- Both on the same carbon, without a beta carbon.

## Explanation

Antiperiplanar alignment allows orbital overlap during proton removal, double-bond formation and bromide departure. The requirement concerns hydrogen on the adjacent carbon. Br alone does not guarantee E2, because a beta hydrogen and suitable geometry are needed.
