A benzylic carbocation is compared with an unconjugated primary alkyl carbocation. Which additional contribution stabilizes the benzylic species?

- Tetrahedral sp³ geometry at the positive carbon without an available p orbital.
- Interruption of overlap between the centre’s orbital and ring orbitals.
- Hyperconjugation from three methyls directly attached to the positive centre.
- Charge delocalization in the system conjugated with the ring.

## Explanation

The benzylic centre’s p orbital can interact with the adjacent pi system and distribute charge across several atoms in contributing structures. Three methyls attached to the positive centre are not required. Orbital alignment supports delocalization: disrupting it or imposing a tetrahedral centre without an available p orbital would remove the contribution being sought.
