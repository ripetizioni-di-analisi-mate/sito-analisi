In radical methane chlorination, which choice tends to limit further substitution of chloromethane without guaranteeing absolute purity?

- Initially increase chloromethane relative to methane.
- Use a large excess of methane over chlorine.
- Continue chlorination until nearly all methane is consumed.
- Use a large excess of chlorine over methane.

## Explanation

Chloromethane retains C–H bonds and competes with methane for further substitution. Keeping abundant methane available tends to favour encounters producing more monosubstituted product, while excess chlorine and very high conversion favour further chlorination. This controls product distribution without guaranteeing a single pure product.
