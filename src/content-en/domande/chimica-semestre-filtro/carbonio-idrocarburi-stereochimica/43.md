Which step represents termination in radical methane chlorination?

- $2\mathrm{CH_3}^{\boldsymbol{\cdot}}\longrightarrow\mathrm{C_2H_6}$.
- $\mathrm{CH_3}^{\boldsymbol{\cdot}}+\mathrm{Cl_2}\longrightarrow\mathrm{CH_3Cl}+\mathrm{Cl}^{\boldsymbol{\cdot}}$.
- $\mathrm{Cl_2}\xrightarrow{h\nu}2\mathrm{Cl}^{\boldsymbol{\cdot}}$.
- $\mathrm{Cl}^{\boldsymbol{\cdot}}+\mathrm{CH_4}\longrightarrow\mathrm{HCl}+\mathrm{CH_3}^{\boldsymbol{\cdot}}$.

## Explanation

Combining two methyl radicals consumes two radical centres without generating others. Steps with one radical on each side propagate the chain; chlorine photolysis initiates it. Classification therefore depends on radical balance as well as atom balance.
