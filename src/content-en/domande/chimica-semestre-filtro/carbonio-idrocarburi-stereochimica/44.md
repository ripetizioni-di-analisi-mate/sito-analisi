During photochemical initiation of methane chlorination, which transformation is promoted by light absorbed by chlorine?

- Cl–Cl bond homolysis.
- Heterolysis of Cl–Cl into Cl⁺ and Cl⁻.
- Termination through recombination of two chlorine radicals.
- Direct C–Cl formation without a radical step.

## Explanation

Absorbed energy enables homolytic cleavage, generating chlorine radicals. These begin propagation by abstracting hydrogen from methane; C–Cl forms in a later step. Heterolysis would instead produce ions. Recombination consumes radicals and is a termination step, performing the opposite role to their initial generation.
