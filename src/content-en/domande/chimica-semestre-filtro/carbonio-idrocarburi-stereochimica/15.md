In $\mathrm{CH_3CH{=}CH_2}$, which sequence describes the carbons’ hybridizations in the written order?

- $sp^3,sp^2,sp^2$.
- $sp^2,sp^2,sp^2$.
- $sp^3,sp^3,sp^2$.
- $sp^3,sp,sp$.

## Explanation

The methyl carbon has four sigma directions and tetrahedral geometry. Each double-bond carbon has three sigma directions and a p orbital participating in the pi bond, making it sp². Hybridization is local: a double bond does not make every carbon in the molecule planar.
