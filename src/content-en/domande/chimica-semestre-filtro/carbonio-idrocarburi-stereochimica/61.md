An elementary substitution energy profile has one maximum and no intermediate minimum between reactants and products. Which reading is consistent with SN2?

- One concerted step crosses a single transition state.
- Two steps are separated by a stable intermediate.
- The maximum is an isolable carbocation waiting for the nucleophile.
- The reaction has no activation barrier because it is concerted.

## Explanation

An energy maximum represents a transition state; an intermediate would occupy a minimum between maxima. The described profile is therefore consistent with bond formation and cleavage in one step. Concerted does not mean barrier-free: the system must still reach the maximum.
