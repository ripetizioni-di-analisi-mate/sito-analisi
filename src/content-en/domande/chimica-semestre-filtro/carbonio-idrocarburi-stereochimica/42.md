The C–Br bond of $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CBr}$ breaks heterolytically, with the pair remaining on bromine. Which species form in the model?

- $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3C^+}$ and $\mathrm{Br^-}$.
- $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3C^-}$ and $\mathrm{Br^+}$.
- Two neutral closed-shell fragments.
- A carbon radical and a bromine radical.

## Explanation

Carbon loses the pair contributing to the bond and becomes a carbocation; bromine receives it and becomes bromide. Net charge remains zero, matching the neutral reactant. Two radicals would describe homolysis, while reversing the charges contradicts the specified electron destination.
