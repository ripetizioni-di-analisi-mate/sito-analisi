A monosaccharide is designated D and its measured rotation is negative. How are the two labels interpreted?

- D expresses a configurational relationship; the negative sign is a measured property.
- The negative sign requires every centre to be S.
- The measurement must be wrong because D always means dextrorotatory.
- D automatically assigns R to every stereogenic centre in the molecule.

## Explanation

D/L compares a configuration with the reference specified for that biomolecular class. Rotation sign depends on the structure’s optical response under the measurement conditions. D and levorotatory are therefore compatible; R/S also supplies no automatic conversion to optical sign.
