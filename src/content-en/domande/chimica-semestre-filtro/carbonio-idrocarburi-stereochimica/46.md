Among simple alkyl carbocations without resonance stabilization, which stability order is generally expected?

- Equal stability because formal charge is identical.
- Primary > secondary > tertiary.
- Tertiary > secondary > primary.
- Secondary > primary > tertiary.

## Explanation

Alkyl groups can stabilize an electron-deficient centre through hyperconjugation and inductive donation. Greater substitution therefore favours the tertiary species in this comparison. Excluding resonance matters: an allylic or benzylic intermediate requires comparison that includes delocalization.
