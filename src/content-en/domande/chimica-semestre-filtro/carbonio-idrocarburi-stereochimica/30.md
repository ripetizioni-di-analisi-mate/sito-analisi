How are the hydrogen isotopes $^3\mathrm H$, $^2\mathrm H$ and $^1\mathrm H$ ranked by CIP when directly attached to a centre?

- Priority depends on the compound’s optical-rotation sign.
- $^3\mathrm H> {}^2\mathrm H> {}^1\mathrm H$.
- They always have equal priority because atomic number is equal.
- $^1\mathrm H> {}^2\mathrm H> {}^3\mathrm H$.

## Explanation

Isotopes of the same element share atomic number, so the isotopic-mass criterion applies: the heavier isotope outranks the lighter one. This distinguishes isotopic groups even when the element symbol is identical. Optical-rotation sign is not part of CIP rules.
