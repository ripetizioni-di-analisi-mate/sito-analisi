In a stereochemical drawing, a hashed wedge starts at a tetrahedral centre. What orientation does it represent?

- Directed towards the observer.
- Directed behind the page plane.
- Always contained in the page plane.
- A fractional-order bond independent of orientation.

## Explanation

A hashed wedge encodes a spatial direction away from the observer. A solid wedge indicates the opposite direction, while an ordinary line denotes an in-plane bond. These conventions concern geometry; they do not convert a single bond into a partial bond used to depict a reaction stage.
