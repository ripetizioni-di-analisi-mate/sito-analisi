An axial methyl group pointing above the mean cyclohexane ring plane undergoes a chair flip. What orientation results?

- Configuration changes through C–methyl bond cleavage.
- Axial, but pointing below the ring.
- Equatorial, still pointing above the ring.
- Equatorial, necessarily pointing below the ring.

## Explanation

A chair flip exchanges axial and equatorial positions while preserving bond identity and the substituent’s above/below orientation. It therefore does not convert cis into trans merely through conformational change. Confusing axial with up would incorrectly imply configurational inversion.
