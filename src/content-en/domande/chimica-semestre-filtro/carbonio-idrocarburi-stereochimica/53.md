When drawing nucleophilic attack on an electrophile, where does a full-headed curved arrow showing pair movement start?

- From the bond yet to form towards the initial lone pair.
- From the lone pair or bond supplying the electrons.
- From positive charge towards the nucleophile regardless of electrons.
- Always from the carbon nucleus.

## Explanation

The arrow follows electrons, starting at their initial location and ending at their new location, often a new bond. Starting from positive charge or the bond yet to form would reverse nucleophilic donation. The nucleus does not supply the pair: an occupied lone pair or bond must be identified and the resulting charges checked.
