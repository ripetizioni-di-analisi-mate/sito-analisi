Two pure enantiomers are compared under identical achiral conditions. Which behaviour is expected?

- Specific rotations equal in both magnitude and sign.
- Different boiling points because mirror reflection changes connectivity.
- Identical interaction with every chiral receptor.
- The same properties such as boiling point, but opposite optical rotations.

## Explanation

Under achiral conditions the interactions determining many physical properties are equivalent for the enantiomers. Optical rotations have equal magnitude and opposite signs under matching conditions. A chiral environment, such as a protein site, may interact differently with them, explaining the biological relevance of stereochemistry.
