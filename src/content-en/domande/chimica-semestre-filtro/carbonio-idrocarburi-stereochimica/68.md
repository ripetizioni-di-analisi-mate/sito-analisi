Only the name “2-bromobutane” is supplied, and the task asks whether it reacts by SN1, SN2, E1 or E2. What information is missing for a reasoned prediction?

- Only optical-rotation sign, which selects SN1 or SN2.
- Only molecular formula, which alone determines mechanism.
- None: every secondary halide necessarily follows E1.
- Reagent, solvent, temperature and system conditions.

## Explanation

A secondary substrate can participate in competing pathways. Base or nucleophile strength and bulk, solvent and temperature alter relative rates. Structure constrains possibilities without selecting a unique answer by itself. Prediction requires conditions or experimental observations distinguishing the pathways.
