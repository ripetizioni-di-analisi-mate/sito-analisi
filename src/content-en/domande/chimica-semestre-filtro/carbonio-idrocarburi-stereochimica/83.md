Cyclooctatetraene has eight pi electrons and a nonplanar geometry. How is it classified in its ordinary geometry?

- Aromatic because it has four double bonds.
- Nonaromatic.
- Antiaromatic solely because eight is divisible by four.
- Antiaromatic because nonplanarity satisfies the geometric requirement of Hückel’s model.

## Explanation

Antiaromatic classification would also require a planar continuously conjugated circuit. Cyclooctatetraene avoids that geometry and cannot be classified using the number eight alone. The case shows why conditions must be checked before electron counting and why nonaromatic and antiaromatic have different meanings.
