In diene addition, A forms faster while B is more stable. The system is subsequently held under conditions permitting equilibration. Which criterion favours B?

- Kinetic control determined only by the lower initial barrier.
- Thermodynamic control linked to relative product stability.
- Obligatory preservation of whichever product appeared first.
- Equivalence between formation rate and final stability.

## Explanation

If pathways are reversible under the stated conditions, distribution can evolve towards that favoured by product stability. The faster product instead depends on its formation barrier. The question explicitly separates rate and final energy, so equilibration is not merely persistence of the kinetic product.
