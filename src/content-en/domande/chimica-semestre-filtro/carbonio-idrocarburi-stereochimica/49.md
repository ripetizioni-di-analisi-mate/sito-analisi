Which change is allowed between resonance contributors of the same species?

- Break a sigma connection and create one between different nuclei.
- Move a proton from one atom to another.
- Move electrons while preserving nuclei and total charge.
- Change the species’ total electron count.

## Explanation

Contributors describe one delocalized electronic structure and preserve the nuclear arrangement. Moving a proton may describe a reaction or tautomerism, while changing sigma connections alters connectivity. Overall electron count and charge remain the same.
