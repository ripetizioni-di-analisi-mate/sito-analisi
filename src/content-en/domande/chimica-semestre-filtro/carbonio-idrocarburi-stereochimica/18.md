Butan-2-ol is shown as a condensed formula without R/S descriptors or wedges. What follows about the sample’s enantiomeric composition?

- The sample must be racemic.
- The sample contains only R.
- The compound is achiral because wedges are absent.
- The formula does not specify enantiomeric composition.

## Explanation

The butan-2-ol connectivity contains a stereogenic centre even when its orientation is omitted. Omission can mean unspecified information and does not select an equimolar mixture. Establishing composition requires a stereochemical specification or experimental data rather than an inference from absent symbols.
