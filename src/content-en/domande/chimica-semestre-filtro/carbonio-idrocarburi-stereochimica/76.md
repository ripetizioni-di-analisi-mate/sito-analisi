Two lipid chains have equal length and a double bond at the same position, one cis and one trans. Which geometric difference is expected?

- The trans form introduces the greater bend and always hinders packing more than cis.
- Free double-bond rotation rapidly makes the two geometries indistinguishable.
- The cis form introduces a bend that can hinder packing relative to trans.
- Only chain length can affect packing; configuration is irrelevant.

## Explanation

Configuration changes the orientation of chain portions without changing connectivity or atom count. Cis geometry can make chain alignment less efficient and alter collective membrane properties. Holding length and unsaturation position fixed makes this a comparison of spatial arrangement.
