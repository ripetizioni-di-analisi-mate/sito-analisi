In a specified solvent, a sample has $\alpha=+2.4^\circ$, $\ell=2\,\mathrm{dm}$ and $c=0.040\,\mathrm{g/mL}$. What is its specific rotation under this convention, at fixed temperature and wavelength?

- $+0.192\,\mathrm{{}^\circ mL\,g^{-1}\,dm^{-1}}$.
- $+120\,\mathrm{{}^\circ mL\,g^{-1}\,dm^{-1}}$.
- $+30\,\mathrm{{}^\circ mL\,g^{-1}\,dm^{-1}}$.
- $+3\,\mathrm{{}^\circ mL\,g^{-1}\,dm^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Divide rotation by path length times concentration: $[\alpha]=\dfrac{2.4}{2\cdot0.040}=30$. The sign remains positive. Concentration is already in grams per millilitre and path length in decimetres; introducing a factor of one hundred or converting the path again would incorrectly change the stated convention.
