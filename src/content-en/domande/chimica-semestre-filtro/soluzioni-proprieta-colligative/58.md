When comparing a solution's boiling temperature with that of pure solvent, which external condition must match?

- Total masses of both samples.
- External pressure.
- Stirring rate as the sole determining variable.
- Exact initial volumes of both samples.

## Explanation

Boiling occurs when vapour pressure equals external pressure. Samples must therefore be compared at the same pressure to isolate the solute effect. An ideal dilute solution containing non-volatile solute requires a higher temperature to reach that pressure. Changing pressure and composition together would confound the effects.
