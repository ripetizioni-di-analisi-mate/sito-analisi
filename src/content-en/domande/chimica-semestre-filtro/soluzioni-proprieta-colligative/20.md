In a cation's first hydration shell, which water orientation is electrostatically favoured?

- Water molecules remain randomly oriented because the whole solution is electrically neutral.
- The partially positive hydrogens face the cation.
- Both water ends are equally attracted because the molecule's total charge is zero.
- The partially negative oxygen faces the cation.

## Explanation

Water's asymmetric charge distribution allows ion–dipole interactions. The electron-rich oxygen region is attracted to a cation; an anion instead favours nearby hydrogens. This is a dynamic average orientation. Overall neutrality neither removes the local charge distribution nor makes both molecular ends equivalent.
