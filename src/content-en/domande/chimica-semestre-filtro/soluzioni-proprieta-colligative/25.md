A solution containing $10.0\%$ solute by mass has density $1.20\,\mathrm{g\,mL^{-1}}$. How many grams of solute are in one litre?

- $12.0\,\mathrm g$.
- $100\,\mathrm g$.
- $1200\,\mathrm g$.
- $120\,\mathrm g$.

## Explanation

One litre of solution has mass $1.20\cdot1000=1200\,\mathrm g$. Solute is ten percent of this: $0.100\cdot1200=120\,\mathrm g$. Treating mass percentage as mass–volume percentage would incorrectly give one hundred grams. Calculating molarity would additionally require solute molar mass.
