An ideal liquid mixture has $x_A=0.25$, $p_A^\ast=80\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $p_B^\ast=40\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Both components are volatile. What is total pressure?

- $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$.

## Explanation

B has fraction $x_B=0.75$. Contributions are $p_A=0.25\cdot80=20\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $p_B=0.75\cdot40=30\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Their sum is $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Pure-component pressures are not added directly; each must be weighted by the component's liquid mole fraction.
