Prepare $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ of a $0.120\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ solution from a $0.800\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ stock without reactions or losses. Find the stock volume required.

- $66.7\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- $12.0\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- $80.0\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- $15.0\,\mathrm{mL}$.

## Explanation

Solute amount is conserved: $V_1=\dfrac{c_2V_2}{c_1}=\dfrac{0.120\cdot0.100}{0.800}\,\mathrm L=0.0150\,\mathrm L$. This is $15.0\,\mathrm{mL}$ of stock. The aliquot is brought to the specified final volume, rather than mixed with that entire volume of solvent. The lower final concentration requires a stock aliquot smaller than the final volume.
