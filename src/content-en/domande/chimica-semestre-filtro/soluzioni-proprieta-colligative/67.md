A freezing-point osmometer measures $\Delta T_f=0.558\,\mathrm K$ in water. In the ideal dilute model, with $K_f=1.86\,\mathrm{K\,kg\,mol^{-1}}$, what osmolality is inferred?

- $0.300\,\mathrm{Osm\,kg^{-1}}$.
- $0.150\,\mathrm{Osm\,kg^{-1}}$.
- $1.04\,\mathrm{Osm\,kg^{-1}}$.
- $0.558\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Cryoscopy measures the combined particle effect: $b_{\mathrm{osm}}=\Delta T_f/K_f=0.558/1.86=0.300\,\mathrm{Osm\,kg^{-1}}$. No further division by a dissociation factor is needed when total osmolality is requested. The result alone identifies neither the solutes nor their membrane permeabilities.
