In an ideal gas mixture, the oxygen mole fraction refers to dry gases, but the sample contains water vapour. Which pressure should multiply that fraction?

- Total pressure minus water partial pressure.
- Water partial pressure alone.
- Total pressure plus water partial pressure.
- Total pressure without correction.

## Explanation

The fraction is normalised to dry-gas moles alone, so its reference pressure must cover only that portion of the mixture. Dalton's law allows subtraction of water's contribution. If oxygen fraction in the entire humid mixture were supplied instead, total pressure would be used directly.
