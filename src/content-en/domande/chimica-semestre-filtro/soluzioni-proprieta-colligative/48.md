CO₂ enters water and reacts to form other species. To which quantity does the Henry relation for molecular CO₂ directly apply?

- To molecular CO₂ plus bicarbonate concentration, provided the same partial pressure is used.
- Only to bicarbonate concentration under every condition.
- To the sum of all carbon-containing species without considering equilibria.
- To the concentration of the physically dissolved molecular species specified by the constant.

## Explanation

The constant must be associated with the stated species and convention. If the gas reacts, total content also includes products that are not the same molecular species. Calculating the total requires additional equilibria and balances. Applying one Henry relation to CO₂ and all its products would confuse physical dissolution with chemical transformation.
