A solution causes a rapid cell-volume change followed by recovery while its solute enters gradually. What information is essential for interpreting tonicity?

- Initial osmolarity alone, regardless of later permeation.
- The presence of a non-electrolyte alone, which guarantees impermeability.
- Only the maximum volume change, because final volume is independent of permeating solutes.
- Observation time and permeability of the membrane–solute system.

## Explanation

A solute can behave as effectively retained over short times and later distribute across the membrane. Initial response can therefore differ from the post-equilibration response. Tonicity must be related to membrane and timescale; total osmolarity alone does not describe volume-change kinetics.
