$c_{\mathrm{osm}}$, total particle concentration in the ideal model, is already known. Which osmotic-pressure formula avoids double counting?

- $\pi=i c_{\mathrm{osm}}RT$ with an additional dissociation factor.
- $\pi=\dfrac{c_{\mathrm{osm}}R}{T}$.
- $\pi=\dfrac{RT}{c_{\mathrm{osm}}}$.
- $\pi=c_{\mathrm{osm}}RT$.

## Explanation

Osmotic concentration already includes the number of particles produced by each solute. Multiplying again by a dissociation factor would count the same effect twice. The factor is needed when starting from formula-unit concentration, not when particle accounting is already complete.
