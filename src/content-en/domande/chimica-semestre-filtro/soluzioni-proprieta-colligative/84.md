A saline solution is approximately isotonic to a cell. What does NOT follow?

- That its ionic and protein composition is identical to plasma.
- That the comparison concerns solutes effectively retained by the membrane.
- That it may approximately maintain cell volume under the stated conditions.
- That membrane permeability matters for interpretation.

## Explanation

Isotonicity describes a volume effect under stated conditions, not complete chemical identity between fluids. A solution containing only salt and water does not reproduce all plasma ions, proteins and other solutes. Separating osmotic behaviour from composition avoids giving the term physiological a broader meaning than warranted.
