For a monovalent ion, what relation holds between mmol and charge mEq?

- mEq depend on molar mass rather than charge.
- An anion must have a negative mEq amount.
- mEq are always twice mmol.
- They have the same numerical value.

## Explanation

Ionic equivalent counting uses charge magnitude. For sodium, potassium or chloride that magnitude is one, so the numerical value equals millimoles. Charge sign is handled separately when imposing electroneutrality, rather than turning the amount of anions into a negative amount.
