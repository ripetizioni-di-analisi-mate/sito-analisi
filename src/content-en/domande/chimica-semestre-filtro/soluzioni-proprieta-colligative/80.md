Red blood cells are placed in a hypotonic medium containing retained solutes. Which sequence describes possible osmotic injury?

- Water entry, volume increase, possible haemolysis.
- Water exit, volume decrease, shrinkage.
- No net water flow and stable volume whenever the medium contains any solute.
- Water entry and volume increase, but no possibility of membrane rupture however large the imbalance.

## Explanation

Under the stated conditions a hypotonic medium promotes net water entry. Increased volume may exceed membrane capacity and cause rupture. Shrinkage instead accompanies water loss in a hypertonic medium. Injury depends on the size and duration of the imbalance.
