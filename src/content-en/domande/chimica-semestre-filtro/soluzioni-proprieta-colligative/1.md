Dissolve $12.0\,\mathrm g$ of solute in $188\,\mathrm g$ of water without losses. Calculate the mass percentage.

- $12.0\%$ by mass.
- $6.38\%$ by mass.
- $6.00\%$ by mass.
- $0.0600\%$ by mass.

## Explanation

The solution mass is $12.0+188=200\,\mathrm g$, so $100\dfrac{12.0}{200}=6.00\%$. The denominator includes the solute mass. Dividing by water mass alone would give the solute-to-solvent ratio, not the requested mass fraction. Multiplying by 100 converts the fraction into a percentage.
