A closed container is heated: no reactions or phase changes occur and solution volume increases. Which pair of changes is correct?

- Molarity stays constant; molality increases.
- Molarity decreases; molality stays constant.
- Molarity and molality increase because temperature rises.
- Molarity and molality decrease by the same proportion.

## Explanation

Solute amount and solvent mass are conserved under the stated conditions. Molarity uses the expanding volume and therefore decreases; molality uses unchanged solvent mass. Temperature itself does not appear directly in these definitions: the relevant issue is which denominator changes.
