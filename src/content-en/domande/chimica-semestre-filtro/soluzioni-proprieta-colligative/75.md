In a uniform ideal solution without fields or currents, concentration gradient is zero. Which description of diffusion is correct?

- The diffusion coefficient must be exactly zero.
- Molecular motion continues but net diffusive flux is zero.
- A constant net flux persists because the diffusion coefficient is positive.
- Every molecule is immobile.

## Explanation

Net flux measures the difference between transfers in opposite directions. With no gradient, those contributions balance in the model while thermal motion continues. The diffusion coefficient describes mobility and can remain positive at equilibrium. Zero net flux does not mean absence of microscopic dynamics.
