A solution undergoes two successive dilutions: first to five times the aliquot volume, then to ten times a new aliquot volume. Without losses, what is $\dfrac{c_f}{c_0}$?

- $50$.
- $\dfrac12$.
- $\dfrac1{50}$.
- $\dfrac1{15}$.

## Explanation

Each step conserves solute in its own aliquot and reduces concentration by the corresponding volume factor. The factors multiply: $\dfrac{c_f}{c_0}=(\dfrac{1}{5})(\dfrac{1}{10})=\dfrac{1}{50}$. Adding them would confuse sequential dilutions with a single volume increase. The new aliquot retains the concentration reached after the first step.
