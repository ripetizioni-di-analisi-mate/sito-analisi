To calculate molality, a student divides solute moles by kilograms of the whole solution. What error does this introduce?

- The denominator is too large and molality is underestimated.
- It directly gives molarity without knowing volume.
- This gives exact molality at any concentration.
- The denominator is too small and molality is overestimated.

## Explanation

Solution mass includes both solvent and solute. Molality instead requires solvent alone: including solute mass increases the denominator without changing the numerator. The error can be small in a very dilute solution, but it does not disappear by definition and does not turn the ratio into molarity.
