In a hypothetical experiment a membrane passes water but fully retains both urea and internal solute. At equal temperature and pressure, what initial water flux occurs between ideal isoosmotic solutions?

- Obligatory water exit because urea is a non-electrolyte.
- No initial net flow.
- Obligatory water entry because urea is always permeating.
- Flow determined only by the solutes' molar masses.

## Explanation

Permeability belongs to the membrane–solute system and must be read from the question. Urea is retained here by assumption, so total and effective particles coincide for this comparison. At equal temperature, pressure and osmotic concentration there is no initial net driving force. Conclusions for permeable red blood cells do not transfer to this different membrane.
