Total vapour pressure is to be identified with the solvent's Raoult contribution alone. Which solute condition makes this appropriate?

- Solute and solvent have equal molar masses.
- Their mole fractions are equal.
- The solute is non-volatile under the stated conditions.
- The solute is more volatile than the solvent.

## Explanation

Total pressure sums contributions from components in the vapour. If the solute is non-volatile, its contribution is negligible and only the solvent remains. A volatile solute must instead be included. Liquid ideality is also needed for the simple mole-fraction form of Raoult's law.
