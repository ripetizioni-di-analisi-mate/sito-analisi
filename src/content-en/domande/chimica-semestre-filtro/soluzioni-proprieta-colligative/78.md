An ideal cell contains a fixed amount of non-permeating solutes initially at $300\,\mathrm{mOsm\,L^{-1}}$. It is placed in a large reservoir of non-permeating solutes at $400\,\mathrm{mOsm\,L^{-1}}$. Neglecting non-osmotic volume and hydrostatic differences, what is equilibrium $\dfrac{V_f}{V_0}$?

- $0.75$.
- $1.33$.
- $1.00$.
- $0.25$.

## Explanation

Internal amount is conserved and effective concentrations match at equilibrium: $300V_0=400V_f$. Thus $\dfrac{V_f}{V_0}=\dfrac{300}{400}=0.75$. The cell loses water and falls to seventy-five percent of initial volume. The inverse ratio would predict swelling in a hypertonic medium, contradicting osmotic direction.
