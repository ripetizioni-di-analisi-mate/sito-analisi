In $\Delta T_f=iK_fb$, with $b$ in $\mathrm{mol\,kg^{-1}}$, what units must $K_f$ have?

- $\mathrm{K\,L\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $\mathrm{K\,kg\,mol^{-1}}$.
- $\mathrm{K\,mol\,kg^{-1}}$.
- $\mathrm{mol\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

The van’t Hoff factor is dimensionless. Constant times molality must leave only temperature units, so kilograms and moles must cancel. A constant containing litres would match another concentration measure, not the specified molality. Dimensional checking helps identify use of the wrong denominator.
