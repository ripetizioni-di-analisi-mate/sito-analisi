Only solvent is added to a binary mixture, without losses or reactions. How do solute mole fraction and the sum of fractions change?

- Solute fraction decreases; the sum remains one.
- Solute fraction remains unchanged; the sum exceeds one.
- Both increase because total moles increase.
- Both decrease because the solution is diluted.

## Explanation

Solute moles stay constant while total moles increase, so their ratio decreases. Solvent fraction increases correspondingly. Fractions always sum to one because they share the same total denominator; dilution does not change this balance identity.
