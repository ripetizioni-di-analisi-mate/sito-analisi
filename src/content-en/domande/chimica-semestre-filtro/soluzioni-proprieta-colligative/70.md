At equal temperature and pressure, two ideal compartments have different non-permeating solute concentrations and are separated by a water-permeable membrane. What initial direction is expected?

- Water towards the compartment with fewer retained particles per volume.
- Solute through the membrane while water does not pass.
- Water towards the compartment with more retained particles per volume.
- No flow because initial pressures match.

## Explanation

At equal pressure, retained solutes lower water chemical potential on the more concentrated side. Initial flow is towards that side. Equal pressures remove an initial mechanical contribution but not the osmotic difference. Volumes and pressures can change during the process, so initial direction alone does not specify the final state.
