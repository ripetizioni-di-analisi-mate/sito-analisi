A solution contains $4.00\,\mathrm g$ of solute in a final volume of $160\,\mathrm{mL}$. Calculate its mass–volume percentage.

- $2.50\%$ mass–volume.
- $0.0250\%$ mass–volume.
- $40.0\%$ mass–volume.
- $4.00\%$ mass–volume.

## Explanation

Express the mass contained in a final volume of $100\,\mathrm{mL}$. The sample contains $4.00\,\mathrm g$ in $160\,\mathrm{mL}$, giving $4.00\dfrac{100}{160}=2.50\,\mathrm g$ per $100\,\mathrm{mL}$. This convention refers to solution volume, not solvent mass. The same number cannot be labelled a mass percentage without further density information.
