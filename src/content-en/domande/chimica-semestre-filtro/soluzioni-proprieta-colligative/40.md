Why does the activity formula divide concentration by a standard concentration?

- To convert molarity automatically into molality.
- To eliminate activity's dependence on composition.
- To force the activity coefficient to equal one in every solution.
- To obtain a dimensionless ratio relative to the chosen standard state.

## Explanation

Activity is a relative, dimensionless quantity. Standard concentration makes this definition consistent without converting volumes into masses or making the solution ideal. Changing convention requires consistent changes to standard state and coefficients; isolated values cannot be compared without knowing the reference used.
