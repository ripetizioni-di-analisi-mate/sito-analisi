Two non-volatile, non-associating non-electrolytes have different molar masses. In separate ideal dilute solutions at equal molality in the same solvent, how do their freezing-point depressions compare?

- They are equal in the stated model.
- They are inversely proportional to molar masses.
- They are equal only if dissolved masses are also equal.
- The higher-molar-mass solute produces the greater depression.

## Explanation

The colligative property depends on particle molality, equal for the two undissociated solutes. Molar mass is needed when starting from grams, but moles per kilogram are already specified here. Equal dissolved masses would instead give different particle counts when molar masses differ.
