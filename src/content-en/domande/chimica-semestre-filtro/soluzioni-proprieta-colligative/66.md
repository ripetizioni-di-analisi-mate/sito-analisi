A solution's same osmole amount is divided either by litres of solution or by kilograms of solvent. Which distinction is correct?

- Both always give the same numerical value.
- These give osmolarity and osmolality respectively.
- The first is molality and the second molarity.
- The denominator does not matter because osmoles represent particles.

## Explanation

The numerator describes osmotic amount, but the denominator defines concentration. Volume belongs to the whole solution, whereas osmolality uses solvent mass. Values may be close in dilute aqueous solutions without being identical by definition. Conversion requires information about mass, volume and composition.
