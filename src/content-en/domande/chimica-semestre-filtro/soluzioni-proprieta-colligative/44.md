Two non-reactive gases in the same solvent at the same temperature have $k_{H,A}>k_{H,B}$ in the convention $c=k_Hp$. At equal partial pressure, which comparison holds in the dilute limit?

- The comparison requires equal gas molar masses.
- Dissolved B concentration is higher.
- Dissolved A concentration is higher.
- The concentrations are necessarily equal.

## Explanation

In the stated convention the constant measures solubility: at equal pressure a larger coefficient gives higher concentration. A constant defined as pressure divided by concentration would have the opposite ordering. Molar mass would convert amount concentration to mass concentration, but is not needed for this molar comparison.
