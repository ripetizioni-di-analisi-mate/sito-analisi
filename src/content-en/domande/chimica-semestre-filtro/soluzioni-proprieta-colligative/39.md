At the same temperature, two samples have equal solute concentration but different activity coefficients in the same convention. Which conclusion is correct?

- The difference necessarily proves an error in mole counting.
- Activities must match because concentrations match.
- Activities can differ because the medium composition differs.
- The coefficient must be one in every aqueous solution.

## Explanation

The coefficient describes departure from ideal behaviour in the chosen convention. Interactions with other solutes can change it without changing the species' analytical concentration. Equal concentrations therefore do not guarantee equal activities or chemical-potential contributions. Water's presence does not automatically eliminate particle interactions.
