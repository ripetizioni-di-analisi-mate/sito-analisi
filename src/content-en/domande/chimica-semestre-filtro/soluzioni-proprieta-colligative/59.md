A pure solvent freezes at $5.0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. A solution has freezing-point depression $\Delta T_f=1.2\,\mathrm K$. What is its freezing temperature?

- $-1.2\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $3.8\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $1.2\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $6.2\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

The depression is a positive difference: $T_f=5.0-1.2=3.8\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. It is not the final temperature and does not require that temperature to be negative. Temperature intervals have equal numerical values in kelvin and Celsius; the reference zero differs, not the size of the degree.
