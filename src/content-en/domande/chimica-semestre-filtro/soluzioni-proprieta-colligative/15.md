A clear saline solution contains two uniformly dispersed ionic species. How many liquid phases are described?

- Three phases, including water.
- One phase.
- No phase, because ionic species are completely dispersed.
- Two phases, one for each ion.

## Explanation

A phase is a region with uniform intensive properties, not a chemical species. The ions share one liquid medium and do not form regions separated by interfaces. Counting cations, anions and water separately confuses components with phases. Changing sample amount does not change this classification.
