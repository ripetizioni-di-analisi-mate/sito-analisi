In an ideal model, every pair of solute molecules associates completely into a dimer. Relative to initially added molecular units, what colligative factor is expected?

- $i=2$.
- $i=4$.
- $i=1$.
- $i=0.5$.

## Explanation

Association reduces independent particle number: two initial molecules form one dimer particle. Effective number is therefore half the number of added units. The factor can be below one; it does not describe only particle increases from dissociation. Total solute mass remains conserved.
