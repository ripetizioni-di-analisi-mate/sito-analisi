In fog, which pair correctly identifies dispersed phase and continuous medium?

- Gas molecules uniformly dissolved in water.
- Gas bubbles in a liquid.
- Liquid droplets in a gas.
- Solid particles in a liquid.

## Explanation

Fog contains water droplets dispersed in air and is an aerosol. Reversing the phases would describe a foam rather than fog. Gas dissolution in water instead gives a molecular distribution in a liquid solvent. Identifying the continuous medium distinguishes dispersions containing the same physical states.
