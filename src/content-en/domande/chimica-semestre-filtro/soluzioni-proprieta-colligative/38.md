With $a=\dfrac{\gamma c}{c^\circ}$, $c=0.080\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, $c^\circ=1\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and $\gamma=0.75$, what is the activity?

- $0.060$, dimensionless.
- $0.080\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.
- $0.75$, dimensionless.
- $0.107$, dimensionless.

## Explanation

Multiply relative concentration by the coefficient: $a=0.75\cdot0.080=0.060$. Dividing by standard concentration removes the units. Dividing by the coefficient would reverse the stated definition. The coefficient alone is not activity because it lacks information about solute concentration.
