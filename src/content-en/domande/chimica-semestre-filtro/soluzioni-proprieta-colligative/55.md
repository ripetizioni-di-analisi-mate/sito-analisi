At equilibrium with a liquid mixture, ideal vapour has $p_A=30\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $p_B=10\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is vapour mole fraction $y_A$?

- $0.25$.
- $3.0$.
- $0.75$.
- $0.50$.

## Explanation

Total pressure is $30+10=40\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and Dalton gives $y_A=\dfrac{p_A}{p_{\mathrm{tot}}}=\dfrac{30}{40}=0.75$. Dividing by B's pressure alone gives a component ratio, not a fraction. This result describes vapour and cannot automatically be assigned to the composition of the equilibrium liquid.
