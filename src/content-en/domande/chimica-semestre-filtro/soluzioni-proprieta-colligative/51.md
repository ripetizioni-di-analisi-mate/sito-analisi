Compared with inspired air, which qualitative change normally characterises expired air after respiratory exchange?

- Identical composition in every expiratory phase.
- More oxygen and less carbon dioxide, without vapour.
- Less oxygen and more carbon dioxide, with humidification.
- Complete absence of oxygen because it is all absorbed.

## Explanation

Exchange with blood lowers oxygen and raises carbon dioxide; airways humidify the gas. Expired air still contains oxygen, and composition varies during a breath partly because of dead-space contributions. This is a qualitative comparison and does not assign universal percentages to every sample.
