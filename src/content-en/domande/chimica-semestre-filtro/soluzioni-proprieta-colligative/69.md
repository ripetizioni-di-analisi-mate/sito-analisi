Two compartments separated by a membrane with positive water permeability that retains their solutes have different concentrations but no net water flow. Which situation can explain equilibrium?

- A hydrostatic pressure difference balances the osmotic contribution.
- Osmosis can stop only when concentrations are identical.
- Water molecules have stopped all motion.
- Lower water permeability, which by itself stops flow completely even with a non-zero driving force.

## Explanation

Water responds to the total chemical-potential difference, which also includes pressure. Higher pressure on the more concentrated side can balance the osmotic tendency. Equilibrium concerns net flow, not cessation of molecular motion. Lower permeability may slow flow but does not by itself remove the driving force. Concentration comparison alone is sufficient only when other contributions are controlled.
