A Henry constant measured at one temperature is applied at another without correction. Which limitation is ignored?

- The constant depends on temperature as well as gas and solvent.
- Changing temperature always causes the same percentage solubility change for every gas.
- The same constant applies at every temperature if pressure uses the same units.
- Partial pressure can always be replaced by total pressure.

## Explanation

The linear relation refers to specified thermodynamic conditions. Heating lowers aqueous solubility for many gases, but a quantitative prediction requires the constant's specific temperature dependence. Keeping partial pressure unchanged is not sufficient to ensure unchanged concentration when temperature changes.
