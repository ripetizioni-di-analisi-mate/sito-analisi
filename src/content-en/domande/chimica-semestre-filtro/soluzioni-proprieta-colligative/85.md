A $5\%$ mass–volume solution contains anhydrous glucose with $M=180\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. Assuming ideality and no dissociation, what initial osmolarity is calculated?

- Approximately $0.0278\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- Approximately $0.278\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- Approximately $0.556\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- $5.00\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Five grams per hundred millilitres correspond to $50\,\mathrm{g\,L^{-1}}$. Thus $c=50/180\simeq0.278\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and, with $i=1$, the same numerical osmotic concentration. This initial measure does not ensure sustained tonicity: glucose transport and metabolism can change its effect over time.
