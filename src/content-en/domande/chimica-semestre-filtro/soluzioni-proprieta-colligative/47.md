Why does Henry's law alone not calculate all oxygen carried by blood?

- The Henry constant automatically includes saturation of haemoglobin binding sites.
- It describes only haemoglobin-bound oxygen.
- Partial pressure counts the total number of bound molecules.
- It describes physically dissolved oxygen; haemoglobin binding requires an additional model.

## Explanation

The concentration directly related to partial pressure is free dissolved gas within Henry's validity range. Haemoglobin binds an additional fraction governed by binding equilibria and protein availability. Total carried concentration and physically dissolved concentration are therefore distinct, though related, quantities.
