At constant temperature, a graph of concentration $c$ against partial pressure $p$ shows Henry’s law as a straight line through the origin. What does its slope represent in the convention $c=k_Hp$?

- $k_H$, with concentration-per-pressure units.
- $1/k_H$, with pressure-per-concentration units.
- $k_Hp$, the concentration value at one particular point.
- Zero, because the line passes through the origin.

## Explanation

Slope is the change in the vertical variable divided by the change in the horizontal variable. Here it equals the stated solubility constant. Swapping the axes would give a reciprocal slope without changing the physical relation. Linearity is a dilute-regime model and does not justify unlimited pressure extrapolation.
