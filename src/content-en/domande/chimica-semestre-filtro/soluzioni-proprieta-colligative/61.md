Electrolyte AB produces two ions when dissociated. In the ideal model its dissociation fraction is $\alpha=0.40$. What is $i$?

- $0.40$.
- $0.80$.
- $2.00$.
- $1.40$.

## Explanation

One initial mole leaves $0.60\,\mathrm{mol}$ undissociated and produces $2\cdot0.40=0.80\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ions. Total particles are $1.40\,\mathrm{mol}$, so $i=1.40$. Counting ions alone would omit undissociated units, which still contribute to colligative properties.
