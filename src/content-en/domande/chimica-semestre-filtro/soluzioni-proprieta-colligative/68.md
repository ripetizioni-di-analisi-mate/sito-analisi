A calculation of $\pi=c_{\mathrm{osm}}RT$ uses numerical temperature in degrees Celsius. What correction is needed?

- Convert temperature to kelvin and use consistent concentration and R units.
- Use degrees Celsius directly because R automatically corrects the scale zero.
- Multiply the Celsius value by 273.15 instead of adding the offset between scale zeros.
- Use the absolute value of degrees Celsius without changing the scale.

## Explanation

The law uses absolute thermodynamic temperature, not a temperature interval. Zero Celsius does not imply zero thermal energy or zero osmotic pressure. The gas constant's units must also match litres or cubic metres used. Taking the absolute Celsius value does not correct the scale zero; conversion requires adding the offset between zeros, not multiplying by it.
