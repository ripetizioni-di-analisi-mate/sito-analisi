An ideal mixture contains $0.100\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ NaCl with $i=2$ and $0.050\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ glucose with $i=1$, both referred to final volume. What is its osmolarity?

- $0.300\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- $0.150\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- $0.200\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- $0.250\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Add contributions with their own factors: $c_{\mathrm{osm}}=2\cdot0.100+1\cdot0.050=0.250\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$. Multiplying the whole sum by two would incorrectly dissociate glucose too. Concentrations already refer to final volume, so no further dilution calculation is needed.
