One text defines Henry’s law as $c=k_sp$; another uses $p=k_vc$, with the same pressure and concentration units. How are the constants related?

- $k_v=1/k_s$.
- $k_v=-k_s$.
- $k_v=k_s$.
- $k_v=k_s^2$.

## Explanation

Solving the first relation for pressure gives $p=c/k_s$, hence $k_v=1/k_s$. The units also invert. Specifying matching units avoids confusing reciprocal conventions with conversions among atmospheres, bar or pascal. A Henry constant's numerical value is unusable without its definition.
