A $0.9\%$ mass–volume NaCl solution contains $9.0\,\mathrm{g\,L^{-1}}$. With $M=58.5\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ and $i=2$, what is its approximate ideal osmolarity?

- $0.900\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- $0.0308\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- $0.308\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.
- $0.154\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Formal concentration is $c=\dfrac{9.0}{58.5}\simeq0.154\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. Two ideal particles per formula unit give $c_{\mathrm{osm}}\simeq2\cdot0.154=0.308\,\mathrm{Osm\,L^{-1}}$. This is the stated model's prediction: ionic interactions modify real behaviour and the name physiological saline does not imply composition identical to plasma.
