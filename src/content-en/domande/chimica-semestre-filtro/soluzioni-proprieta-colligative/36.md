In the reduction $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+e^-\longrightarrow Fe^{2+}}$, how many redox equivalents correspond to one mole of iron(III)?

- $2\,\mathrm{Eq}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{Eq}$.
- $\dfrac13\,\mathrm{Eq}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{Eq}$.

## Explanation

Each ion gains one electron in the stated transformation. One mole of ions therefore accepts one mole of electrons, or one redox equivalent. Using the initial charge of three would confuse ionic-charge equivalents with equivalents for this reaction. The final charge is not the number of transferred electrons either.
