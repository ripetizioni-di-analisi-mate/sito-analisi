A solution of a solid is heated and its new solubility is to be predicted. What information is needed?

- Only the number of ions in the solute formula.
- The temperature dependence of solubility for that system.
- Solubility measured at just one temperature, without further data.
- A universal rule that all solids become more soluble.

## Explanation

Dissolution depends on the thermodynamic balance of interactions and on temperature. No increasing-solubility rule holds for every solid; system-specific data or a model are needed. A single solubility value and stoichiometric ion count do not alone determine the solubility curve.
