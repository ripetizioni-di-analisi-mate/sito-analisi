Only solvent evaporates from a solution containing non-volatile solute. All solute remains dissolved and final volume halves. What molarity change is expected?

- It halves.
- It increases fourfold.
- It doubles.
- It remains constant.

## Explanation

Solute moles are conserved while solution volume halves. The amount-to-volume ratio doubles: this concentrates the solution, opposite to dilution. The assumption that all solute remains dissolved is essential; precipitation would remove solute from the liquid phase and require a different balance.
