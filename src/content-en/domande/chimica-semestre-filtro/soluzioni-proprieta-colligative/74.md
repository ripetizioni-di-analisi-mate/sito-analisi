With diffusive flux per unit area unchanged, available area doubles. What happens to total molar transfer per unit time?

- It halves.
- It stays unchanged because flux does not change.
- It quadruples.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

Total transfer is $\dot n=JA$. If flux per unit area stays the same, doubling area doubles transfer rate. Flux and total transfer are also distinguished by units: the former contains an area denominator, the latter does not. The diffusion coefficient is not being changed.
