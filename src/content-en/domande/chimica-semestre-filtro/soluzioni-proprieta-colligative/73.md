For a neutral solute, $J=-D\,\dfrac{\mathrm dc}{\mathrm dx}$ with $D>0$. If concentration increases towards larger $x$, what sign does J have?

- Zero: a constant gradient does not produce diffusion.
- The sign depends only on the crossed area.
- Negative: net flow is towards smaller x.
- Positive: net flow always follows the gradient.

## Explanation

The stated gradient and diffusion coefficient are positive. The law's minus sign makes flux negative, towards lower concentrations. This does not mean a negative amount of matter; it specifies direction relative to the chosen axis. Area changes total transfer rate, not this directional convention.
