A mixture contains $0.500\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ethanol and $4.50\,\mathrm{mol}$ of water. Calculate the ethanol mole fraction.

- $0.500$.
- $0.900$.
- $0.100$.
- $0.111$.

## Explanation

Total amount is $0.500+4.50=5.00\,\mathrm{mol}$. Ethanol mole fraction is therefore $x=\dfrac{0.500}{5.00}=0.100$, a dimensionless number. Dividing by water alone would give a molar ratio between components. The complementary water fraction makes the sum of both fractions equal to one.
