In a potential-energy curve versus internuclear separation, what does the minimum represent?

- Equilibrium bond distance.
- Complete separation of the atoms.
- A distance where both attraction and repulsion are absent.
- The distance where the net attractive force has its greatest magnitude.

## Explanation

At the minimum, the energy derivative with respect to separation is zero, so net force vanishes. Attractions and repulsions balance without individually disappearing. Separating atoms from the minimum requires energy towards dissociation; excessive compression rapidly increases repulsion. The minimum therefore identifies a stable model separation.
