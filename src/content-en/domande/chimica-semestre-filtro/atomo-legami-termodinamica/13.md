A closed system absorbs $500\,\mathrm J$ and performs $200\,\mathrm J$ of work on its surroundings. Neglecting macroscopic kinetic and potential energies, what is $\Delta U$ when received work has $w>0$?

- $+300\,\mathrm J$.
- $-700\,\mathrm J$.
- $-300\,\mathrm J$.
- $+700\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

Absorbed heat gives $q=+500\,\mathrm J$, while work delivered to the surroundings gives $w=-200\,\mathrm J$. Thus $\Delta U=q+w=+300\,\mathrm J$. Work’s sign follows the stated convention; adding two positive values would assign the system energy it has just released. The system remains closed to matter.
