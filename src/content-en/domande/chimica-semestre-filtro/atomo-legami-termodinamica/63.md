Why is $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ classified as a ternary compound?

- Its molecular formula contains three independent ions.
- It contains exactly three atoms in total.
- It contains three different elements: H, S and O.
- Sulfur has oxidation state three.

## Explanation

Binary and ternary count different elements, not total atoms. H₂SO₄ contains hydrogen, sulfur and oxygen, giving three elements and seven atoms in its molecular formula. This classification alone assigns neither an oxidation state nor a number of ions produced in solution.
