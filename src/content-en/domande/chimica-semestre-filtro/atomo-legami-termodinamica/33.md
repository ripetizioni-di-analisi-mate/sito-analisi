Relative molecular mass is defined as molecular mass divided by the atomic mass unit. What unit does $M_r$ have?

- Inverse moles.
- It is dimensionless.
- Kilograms.
- Grams per mole.

## Explanation

The ratio divides one mass by another, so units cancel. The relative quantity differs from molecular mass, which has mass units, and molar mass, which has mass per amount-of-substance units. Numerical equality at a given precision does not make the three quantities identical.
