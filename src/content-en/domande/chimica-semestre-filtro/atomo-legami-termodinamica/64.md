Which solid–structure pairing is correct?

- Ice: molecular; diamond: covalent network.
- NaCl: molecular; copper: neutral-molecule network.
- Ice: ionic; diamond: molecular.
- Copper: ionic; diamond: metallic.

## Explanation

Ice retains identifiable water molecules connected by intermolecular interactions, especially hydrogen bonds. Diamond’s covalent bonds extend throughout the crystal. NaCl is an ionic lattice and copper is metallic. Classification depends on structural units and the interactions connecting them.
