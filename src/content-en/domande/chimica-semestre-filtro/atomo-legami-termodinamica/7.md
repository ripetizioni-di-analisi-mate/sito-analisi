During hydration of $\mathrm{Na^+}$, which end of water preferentially points towards the ion, and which interaction describes this orientation?

- Hydrogen; hydrogen bonding between water and the cation.
- Oxygen; interaction between two permanent dipoles.
- Oxygen; ion–dipole interaction.
- Hydrogen; ion–dipole interaction.

## Explanation

Oxygen carries a partial negative charge and therefore points towards the positive cation. Sodium is an ion, whereas water is a polar molecule, giving an ion–dipole interaction. Preferred average orientation does not imply motionless molecules. Calling it dipole–dipole interaction would overlook sodium’s net charge.
