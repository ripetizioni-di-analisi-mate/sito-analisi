Stable nuclei with $I=0$ are compared with nuclei having $I=\dfrac12$. Which group can provide conventional NMR signals based on nuclear magnetic moments?

- Those with $I=\dfrac12$, without requiring radioactivity.
- Both necessarily give the same signal.
- Only those with $I=0$, because they are stable.
- Neither, because stability prevents resonance.

## Explanation

Nonzero nuclear spin allows a magnetic moment and levels separated by an applied field. Spin-zero nuclei do not provide conventional magnetic NMR signals. Stability concerns nuclear decay, whereas resonance concerns transitions between spin states. These are different properties, as illustrated by the stable proton.
