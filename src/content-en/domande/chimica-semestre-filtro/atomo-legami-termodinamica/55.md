For carbon in $\mathrm{O=C=O}$, which pair gives formal charge and oxidation state, in that order?

- $+4$ and $0$.
- $-4$ and $+4$.
- $0$ and $+4$.
- $0$ and $0$.

## Explanation

Formal charge divides each bond’s electrons equally, assigning carbon four electrons, equal to its neutral valence count, so formal charge is zero. Oxidation state instead assigns C-O electrons to more electronegative oxygen, giving carbon plus four. The conventions perform different electron-counting operations.
