Neutral phosphorus has configuration $[\mathrm{Ne}]\,3s^2\,3p^3$. How many electrons belong to its outer principal level?

- $3$.
- $15$.
- $2$.
- $5$.

## Explanation

The outer level has principal number three and includes both 3s and 3p. Adding two and three gives five electrons. Counting only p would give three, while fifteen includes the inner electrons represented by Ne. Distinguishing total electrons from valence electrons supports Lewis-structure construction.
