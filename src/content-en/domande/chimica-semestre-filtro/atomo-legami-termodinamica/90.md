Two processes have $\Delta G_A=+12\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ and $\Delta G_B=-20\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ under the same conditions. What makes a $-8\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ balance relevant for driving A?

- A must proceed twice for every B progression.
- Simply placing A and B in the same vessel always suffices.
- A mechanism links A and B progress in a 1:1 ratio.
- B must be faster than A, even if their mechanisms remain independent.

## Explanation

The balance $12-20=-8$ corresponds to one progression of each process. Mechanistic coupling makes this sum relevant to the overall transformation. With two A progressions per B, it would instead be $24-20=+4$. Spatial coexistence or a faster B process does not guarantee the required mechanistic link.
