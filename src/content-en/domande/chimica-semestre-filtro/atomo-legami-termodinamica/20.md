For the same nucleus and chemical environment, spin-level separation is proportional to the field: $\Delta E=h\nu\propto B_0$. How does resonance frequency change when $B_0$ doubles?

- It doubles.
- It quadruples.
- It halves.
- It remains unchanged.

## Explanation

Doubling the field doubles $\Delta E$. Planck’s constant is unchanged, so $\nu=\dfrac{\Delta E}{h}$ also doubles. This prediction compares the same nucleus in the same chemical conditions. Different nuclei have different gyromagnetic ratios and cannot be compared using field strength alone.
