An interface has constant surface tension $\gamma=0.0700\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$. What reversible work increases total area by $2.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, using $W=\gamma\Delta A$?

- $2.80\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm J$.
- $1.40\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm J$.
- $3.50\cdot10^{1}\,\mathrm J$.
- $1.40\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

The product is $0.0700\cdot2.00\cdot10^{-3}=1.40\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm J$. Force per length multiplied by area gives energy. Total interfacial area is already specified, so no factor of two for unspecified faces is added. Work is positive because area increases.
