A species has four bonding and two antibonding electrons. Using $b=\dfrac{n_b-n_a}{2}$, what is its bond order?

- $6$.
- $3$.
- $1$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

The net contribution is $4-2=2$ electrons, equivalent to one bonding pair. Division by two gives $b=1$. Adding all occupancies would ignore antibonding destabilisation; omitting division by two would confuse electrons and pairs. The result belongs to the specified orbital model.
