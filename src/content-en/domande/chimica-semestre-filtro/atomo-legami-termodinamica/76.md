Which comparison distinguishes evaporation from boiling of a pure liquid?

- Evaporation involves no energy exchange.
- Boiling occurs only at the surface without internal bubbles.
- Evaporation always requires a temperature above boiling.
- Evaporation occurs from the surface even below boiling; boiling involves bubbles within the liquid.

## Explanation

Some molecules can leave the surface before the liquid reaches boiling temperature. Boiling instead requires conditions allowing vapour bubbles within the bulk. Both liquid-to-gas changes require energy under usual conditions. Evaporation can therefore contribute to cooling below the boiling point.
