Which combination describes $\mathrm{CH_4}$ in the localised model: geometry, C hybridisation and bond components?

- Tetrahedral; $sp^3$; three $\sigma$ and one $\pi$.
- Tetrahedral; $sp^3$; four $\sigma$ and no $\pi$.
- Trigonal pyramidal; $sp^3$; four $\sigma$ and no $\pi$.
- Square planar; $sp^2$; four $\sigma$ and no $\pi$.

## Explanation

Carbon forms four single bonds and retains no lone pairs. Four domains give a tetrahedral geometry, described using four sp³ hybrid orbitals. Each C-H bond contains one sigma component. A pyramidal geometry would instead require only three bonded atoms and a lone pair, as in ammonia.
