Which set of quantum numbers describes an allowed electron state?

- $n=3, l=1, m_l=0, m_s=+1$.
- $n=2, l=2, m_l=0, m_s=+\dfrac12$.
- $n=2, l=1, m_l=-1, m_s=+\dfrac12$.
- $n=1, l=0, m_l=1, m_s=-\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

For $n=2$, $l$ can be 0 or 1; for $l=1$, $m_l$ can be minus one, zero or plus one. Electron spin projection is $\pm\dfrac12$. Each other set violates at least one constraint: the l range, m range or spin projection. All four constraints must hold together.
