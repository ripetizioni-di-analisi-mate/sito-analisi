Determine protons, neutrons and electrons in ${}^{40}_{20}\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}$.

- $18$ protons, $22$ neutrons and $20$ electrons.
- $20$ protons, $40$ neutrons and $18$ electrons.
- $20$ protons, $20$ neutrons and $18$ electrons.
- $20$ protons, $20$ neutrons and $22$ electrons.

## Explanation

Atomic number counts protons, giving 20. Neutrons number $A-Z=40-20=20$. The double positive charge results from losing two electrons: $20-2=18$. Ion formation leaves the nucleus unchanged. Taking 40 as the neutron count would count protons again, even though mass number already includes them.
