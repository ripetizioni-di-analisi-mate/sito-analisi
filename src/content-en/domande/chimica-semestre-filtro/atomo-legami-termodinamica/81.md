Two closed-system paths connect the same states, without changes in macroscopic kinetic or potential energy. On the first, $q=100\,\mathrm J$ and $w=-40\,\mathrm J$. On the second, $q=20\,\mathrm J$. What must received work be on the second path?

- $+120\,\mathrm J$.
- $+40\,\mathrm J$.
- $+80\,\mathrm J$.
- $-40\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

Internal energy is a state function, so $\Delta U=100-40=60\,\mathrm J$ on both paths. On the second, $60=20+w$, giving $w=40\,\mathrm J$. Heat and work can change separately while their sum remains the same between identical states. Positive work denotes energy received.
