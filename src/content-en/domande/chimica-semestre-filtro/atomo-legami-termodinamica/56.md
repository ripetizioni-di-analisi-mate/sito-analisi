In $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$, with H at $+1$, what is each O oxidation state?

- $-1$.
- $-2$.
- $0$.
- $+1$.

## Explanation

Neutrality requires $2(+1)+2x=0$, giving $x=-1$. The O-O group identifies a peroxide and distinguishes it from ordinary oxides. Assigning minus two to both oxygens would give a negative sum inconsistent with the neutral formula. Oxygen’s Lewis formal charge can nevertheless be zero.
