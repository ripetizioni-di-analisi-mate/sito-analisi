In $\mathrm{R-C\equiv N}$, what R-C-N angle and carbon hybridisation are expected in the localised model?

- Approximately $90^\circ$; $sp^2$.
- Approximately $109.5^\circ$; $sp^3$.
- Approximately $180^\circ$; $sp$.
- Approximately $120^\circ$; $sp^2$.

## Explanation

Carbon has two bond directions, towards R and N. The triple bond occupies one domain, so the two domains arrange linearly. The sp model supplies two directional orbitals and leaves two p orbitals for the triple bond’s pi components. Domain number differs from the number of shared pairs.
