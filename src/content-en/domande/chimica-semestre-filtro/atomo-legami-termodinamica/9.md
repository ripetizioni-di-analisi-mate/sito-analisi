A fixed amount of ideal gas at constant temperature expands from $2.00\,\mathrm L$ to $3.00\,\mathrm L$, starting at an absolute pressure of $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the final pressure?

- $80.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $40.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $180\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$.

## Explanation

Boyle’s law gives $p_1V_1=p_2V_2$, so $p_2=\dfrac{120\cdot2.00}{3.00}=80.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Volume increases by a factor of 1.5 and pressure decreases by that factor. Reversing the ratio would incorrectly predict increased pressure during isothermal expansion. Both constant temperature and constant amount are required.
