Potassium has $Z=19$. What is the ground-state electron configuration of $\mathrm{K^+}$?

- $[\mathrm{Ar}]$.
- $[\mathrm{Ne}]\,3s^1$.
- $[\mathrm{Ar}]\,4s^2$.
- $[\mathrm{Ar}]\,4s^1$.

## Explanation

Neutral potassium has 19 electrons and configuration $[\mathrm{Ar}]\,4s^1$. Positive charge requires removal of the outer 4s electron, leaving 18 electrons. The Ar configuration accounts for all of them. Adding a second 4s electron would instead represent electron gain, inconsistent with the requested cation.
