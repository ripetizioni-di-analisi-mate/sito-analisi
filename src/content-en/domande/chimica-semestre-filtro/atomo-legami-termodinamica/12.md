Near ordinary conditions, which substance has a negatively sloped melting curve, and what transition occurs when solid CO₂ is heated at one bar?

- Water; CO₂ melts into stable liquid.
- Water; CO₂ sublimes.
- CO₂; CO₂ melts into stable liquid.
- CO₂; CO₂ sublimes.

## Explanation

Ordinary ice is less dense than liquid water: pressure favours liquid and lowers melting temperature. CO₂ has a triple-point pressure above one bar, so its liquid region is not reached along that isobar. Heating crosses the solid–gas boundary. Melting-curve slope and triple-point pressure are two distinct pieces of diagram information.
