Electron attachment to a gaseous atom releases $349\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. With IUPAC electron affinity defined as released energy, what are the signs of affinity and process $\Delta E$?

- Negative affinity; positive $\Delta E$.
- Affinity and $\Delta E$ both positive.
- Affinity and $\Delta E$ both negative.
- Positive affinity; negative $\Delta E$.

## Explanation

The system loses energy during attachment, so its energy change is negative. Affinity defined as released energy instead uses the positive magnitude of that release. These are not opposite processes but quantities using different conventions. Stating the definition prevents errors when comparing tables.
