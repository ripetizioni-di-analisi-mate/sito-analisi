In a transformation, $\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}=-10\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ and $\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}=+15\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. Is the balance compatible with the second law?

- Yes, because the total change is $+5\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- Yes, and this proves the process is reversible.
- No, because the total change is $-25\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- No, because every system’s entropy must always increase.

## Explanation

System and surroundings together give $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=-10+15=+5\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. The second law is satisfied despite the local decrease. A positive total increase is consistent with irreversibility; a reversible process would have zero total change. The subscripts identify the studied system and its surroundings.
