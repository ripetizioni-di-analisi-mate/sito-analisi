A solid metal conducts current without macroscopic translation of its lattice. Which description fits metallic bonding?

- Delocalised electrons can carry charge.
- Lattice ions must migrate as in a molten salt.
- Lattice vibrations alone, without net charge movement, constitute the current.
- All valence electrons remain permanently confined to individual local bonds.

## Explanation

In the metallic model, some electrons are delocalised and respond to an electric field. Nuclei and inner electrons remain around lattice positions while vibrating. Current requires net charge transport, which vibration around a fixed position alone does not provide. This electronic conduction differs from ion migration in a molten salt.
