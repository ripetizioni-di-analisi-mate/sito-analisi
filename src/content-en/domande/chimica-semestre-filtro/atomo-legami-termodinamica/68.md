A closed ideal gas at constant volume is heated from $27\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ to $54\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. A student says pressure doubles. What is the correction?

- Pressure stays constant because the container is rigid.
- The ratio is $\dfrac{54}{27}=2$.
- The ratio is $\dfrac{327.15}{300.15}\simeq1.09$.
- Pressure halves because the gas is heated.

## Explanation

Pressure–temperature proportionality requires kelvin. Absolute temperatures are 300.15 and 327.15 K, giving an increase of approximately 9%. A rigid container fixes volume, not pressure. A ratio of Celsius temperatures lacks the physical zero required by the gas law.
