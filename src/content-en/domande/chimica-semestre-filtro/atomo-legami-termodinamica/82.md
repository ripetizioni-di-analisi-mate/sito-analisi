For which closed-system process can $q_p=\Delta H$ be used directly?

- Constant pressure with additional unaccounted electrical work.
- Any path, because heat is a state function.
- Constant pressure and only expansion work.
- Constant volume with any pressure change.

## Explanation

The equality follows from the first law combined with H=U+pV under the stated conditions. Work other than expansion adds a contribution preventing automatic identification of heat with enthalpy. At constant volume with no work, heat instead measures the internal-energy change. Heat is path-dependent.
