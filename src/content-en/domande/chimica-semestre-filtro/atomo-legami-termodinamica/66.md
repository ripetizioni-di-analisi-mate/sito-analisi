Diamond and graphite both consist of carbon but have different properties. Which explanation is appropriate?

- They differ only in carbon isotope abundances.
- Identical composition always requires equal conductivity and hardness.
- They differ in structure and the arrangement of bonds and electrons.
- Both are molecular solids held together only by dispersion.

## Explanation

Diamond has a three-dimensional network, whereas graphite has layers and different electron delocalisation. These structures produce different hardness and conductivity while retaining the same elemental identity. They are allotropes. Atomic number remains carbon’s, with no isotope change required.
