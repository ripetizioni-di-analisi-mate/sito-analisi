A metabolite maintains constant concentration while being continuously produced and consumed, with nutrients entering and products leaving. Which conclusion is justified?

- The system must be isolated from matter and energy.
- Every internal reaction must have zero Gibbs change.
- A steady state with nonzero flows can exist.
- Constant concentration alone proves thermodynamic equilibrium.

## Explanation

Concentration remains constant when production, consumption and transport balance. Such a balance can sustain continuous flows in an open system. Thermodynamic equilibrium instead requires no net driving force for the processes considered. Constancy of one macroscopic quantity alone does not distinguish these situations.
