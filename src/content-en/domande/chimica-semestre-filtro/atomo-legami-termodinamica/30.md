In a C-O bond, electron density is displaced towards O. Which property directly compares the bonded atoms’ tendency to attract shared electrons?

- Electronegativity.
- Electron affinity of the free atom.
- First ionisation energy of the free atom.
- Atomic radius.

## Explanation

Electronegativity concerns bonded atoms and the distribution of shared electrons. Ionisation energy and electron affinity instead concern removing or attaching an electron to free gaseous species. Radius expresses size. Oxygen’s greater electronegativity directly describes the displacement of bonding density away from carbon.
