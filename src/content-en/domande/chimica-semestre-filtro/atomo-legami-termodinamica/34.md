Which entity is a polyatomic ion?

- $\mathrm{NH_4^+}$.
- $\mathrm{Na^+}$.
- $\mathrm{NaCl}$ as a neutral formula unit.
- $\mathrm{NH_3}$.

## Explanation

Ammonium contains several bonded atoms and a net positive charge, satisfying both requirements. Na⁺ is monatomic, while NH₃ is a neutral molecule. Sodium chloride’s formula unit describes an electrically balanced ratio of cations and anions rather than one polyatomic ion.
