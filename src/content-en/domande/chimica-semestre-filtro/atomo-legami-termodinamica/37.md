NH₃ donates its lone pair to H⁺, forming NH₄⁺. Which description of the final ion’s four N-H bonds is correct?

- One remains electron-free because H⁺ contributed none.
- They are equivalent; coordination describes the pair’s initial origin.
- One must be longer because it contains only one electron.
- Three are covalent and the fourth remains purely ionic attraction.

## Explanation

Nitrogen’s pair supplies both electrons of the new bond during formation. The product nevertheless has four equivalent covalent bonds in its ideal tetrahedral structure. Electron origin does not identify an electron-free bond or a permanently different physical bond category. The overall positive charge is included in Lewis counting.
