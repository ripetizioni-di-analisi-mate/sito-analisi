With all other occupancies unchanged, one electron is added to an antibonding orbital. How does bond order change in the molecular orbital model?

- It decreases by $1$.
- It remains unchanged.
- It increases by $\dfrac12$.
- It decreases by $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

In $b=\dfrac{n_b-n_a}{2}$, only $n_a$ increases by one. The numerator decreases by one, so the result decreases by one half. An antibonding electron opposes the net bonding contribution. Other occupancies are explicitly fixed, excluding additional electronic rearrangements.
