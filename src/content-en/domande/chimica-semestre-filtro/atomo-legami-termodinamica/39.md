For $\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}$, C contributes 4 valence electrons and each O contributes 6. How many electrons must be distributed in its Lewis structure?

- $22$.
- $26$.
- $20$.
- $24$.

## Explanation

The neutral-atom sum is $4+3\cdot6=22$. The double negative charge adds two electrons, giving 24. Subtracting two would incorrectly treat the anion as a cation. Charge belongs to the whole ion, so the correction is applied once rather than to each oxygen.
