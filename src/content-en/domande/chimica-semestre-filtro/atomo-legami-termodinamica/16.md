A magnesium atom forms $\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}$ by losing two electrons without nuclear transformation. Which quantity changes?

- Atomic number Z.
- Neutron number.
- Electron number.
- Mass number A.

## Explanation

The stated ionisation changes the electron cloud. Protons and neutrons remain unchanged, preserving Z, A and isotopic identity. The new imbalance between protons and electrons produces positive charge. Actual mass decreases slightly with electron loss, but mass number counts only nucleons.
