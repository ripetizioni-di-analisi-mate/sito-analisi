How many moles of chloride ions are present in the solid composition of a $0.200\,\mathrm{mol}$ sample of $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$?

- $0.400\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.100\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.600\,\mathrm{mol}$.
- $0.200\,\mathrm{mol}$.

## Explanation

Each formula unit contains two chloride ions, so $n(\mathrm{Cl^-})=2\cdot0.200=0.400\,\mathrm{mol}$. The value 0.200 instead describes calcium, occurring once per formula unit. The value 0.600 would count all ions together. This composition count requires no assumptions about dissociation in solution.
