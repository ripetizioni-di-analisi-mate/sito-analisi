What is N’s oxidation state in $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$, assuming O is $-2$?

- $+5$.
- $+3$.
- $+6$.
- $-1$.

## Explanation

The sum must equal ionic charge: $x+3(-2)=-1$, so $x=+5$. Setting the sum to zero would incorrectly give plus six. Minus one belongs to the whole ion, not automatically to nitrogen. This count distinguishes nitrate from nitrite, which have different oxygen counts.
