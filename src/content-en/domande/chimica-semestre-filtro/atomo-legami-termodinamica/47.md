Two species have four domains around the central atom. One has four bonded atoms; the other has three bonded atoms and a lone pair. Which VSEPR comparison is correct?

- Same tetrahedral domain arrangement, different molecular shapes.
- The atoms form the same tetrahedral shape in both.
- The second species has three electron domains.
- Square planar geometry in the first and linear in the second.

## Explanation

Domains include bonding pairs and lone pairs, so both species retain four. Molecular shape considers only atoms: tetrahedral in the first and trigonal pyramidal in the second. A lone pair can also change bond angles. Separating these descriptions avoids treating a lone pair as an atom.
