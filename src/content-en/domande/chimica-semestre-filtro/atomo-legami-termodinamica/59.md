Which naming distinguishes gaseous HCl from its aqueous solution?

- Hydrogen hypochlorite for both.
- Chloric acid for the gas; hydrogen chloride for the solution.
- Hydrogen chlorate for the gas; hydrochloric acid for the solution.
- Hydrogen chloride for the gas; hydrochloric acid for the solution.

## Explanation

HCl is a binary hydrogen–chlorine compound. Hydrogen chloride names the molecular species, while hydrochloric acid names its aqueous solution. Chloric acid and chlorate species also involve oxygen. Naming must therefore match both composition and physical context.
