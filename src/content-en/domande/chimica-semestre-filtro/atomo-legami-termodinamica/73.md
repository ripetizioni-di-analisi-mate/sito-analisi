In a normalised Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution, the area between two speeds is 0.15. What does this mean?

- Density f(v) equals 0.15 at every point in the interval.
- 15% of particles have exactly the interval’s midpoint speed.
- 15% of particles have speeds outside the interval.
- 15% of particles have speeds in that interval.

## Explanation

Normalisation assigns total area one. Area over an interval represents a fraction, here 0.15 or 15%; the remaining 85% lies outside. Height f(v) is a density and can vary within the interval. The datum does not concentrate particles at the midpoint speed: it sums contributions across the entire interval.
