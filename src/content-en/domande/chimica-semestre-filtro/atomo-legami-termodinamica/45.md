A carbon has a double bond to O and two single bonds to other atoms, with no lone pairs. Which local description is appropriate?

- Four domains, tetrahedral geometry, $sp^3$.
- Three domains, trigonal planar geometry, $sp^2$.
- Three domains, trigonal pyramidal geometry, $sp^3$.
- Two domains, linear geometry, $sp$.

## Explanation

The C=O double bond counts as one domain, alongside the two single bonds. Three domains and no carbon lone pair give trigonal planar geometry in the localised model. Counting sigma and pi as separate domains would incorrectly give four. This describes carbon within the molecule rather than an isolated carbon atom.
