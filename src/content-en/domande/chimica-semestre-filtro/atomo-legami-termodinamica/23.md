Three electrons occupy the three degenerate orbitals of a p subshell. Which arrangement describes the ground state according to Hund?

- Three electrons in the same orbital.
- One per orbital, with parallel spins.
- One per orbital, with one spin opposite to the other two.
- One pair and one single electron, leaving the third orbital empty.

## Explanation

Hund’s rule favours separate occupancy of degenerate orbitals with parallel spins before pairing. A pair plus a single electron can satisfy Pauli but is not the requested ground-state arrangement. Three electrons in one orbital would also violate Pauli. The question therefore distinguishes an allowed configuration from the lowest-energy one.
