A transformation has $\Delta H<0$ and $\Delta S<0$, treated as constant. How does increasing T affect $\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$?

- It necessarily makes ΔH positive.
- It leaves ΔG unchanged.
- It makes ΔG less negative or more positive.
- It makes ΔG increasingly negative.

## Explanation

With negative entropy change, minus T times delta S is positive and grows with temperature, increasingly opposing negative enthalpy. A transformation favoured at low temperature can therefore become unfavoured at high temperature. The model holds enthalpy and entropy changes constant rather than changing the sign of $\Delta H$.
