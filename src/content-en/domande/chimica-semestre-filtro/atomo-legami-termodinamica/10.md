A fixed amount of ideal gas occupies $3.00\,\mathrm L$ at $300\,\mathrm K$. What volume does it occupy at $400\,\mathrm K$ if pressure remains constant?

- $4.00\,\mathrm L$.
- $3.00\,\mathrm L$.
- $12.0\,\mathrm L$.
- $2.25\,\mathrm L$.

## Explanation

At constant pressure and amount, $\dfrac VT$ is constant. Thus $V_2=3.00\cdot\dfrac{400}{300}=4.00\,\mathrm L$. Temperature increases by one third and volume by the same factor. Temperatures are already absolute; using temperature differences or the inverse ratio would not express Charles’s law.
