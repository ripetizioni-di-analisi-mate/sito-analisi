A rigid vessel slowly loses ideal gas through a valve. Temperature remains constant and pressure decreases. What follows from $pV=nRT$?

- pV must remain constant because T is constant.
- Only mean speed decreases, while the amount of gas remains fixed.
- The amount of gas present decreases.
- The gas constant R must decrease.

## Explanation

With V, R and T unchanged, pressure is proportional to n. Its decrease therefore indicates less gas, consistent with outflow through the valve. Boyle’s law also requires fixed n and does not apply to the whole open process. R is a universal constant, not an adjustable loss parameter.
