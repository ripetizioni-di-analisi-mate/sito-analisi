In a gas-phase reaction, energy released by forming product bonds exceeds energy required to break reactant bonds. What is the sign of enthalpy estimated from bond energies?

- Positive.
- Determined only by molecule count.
- Always zero.
- Negative.

## Explanation

The estimate subtracts formed-bond energy from broken-bond energy. If released energy is greater, the system releases net energy and the change is negative. Breaking bonds remains a positive energy cost even in an exothermic reaction. Molecule count alone does not quantify the bond energies involved.
