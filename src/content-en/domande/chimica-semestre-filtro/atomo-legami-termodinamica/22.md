Which occupancy directly violates the Pauli exclusion principle?

- Two electrons in different orbitals with parallel spins.
- Two electrons in the same orbital with parallel spins.
- Two electrons in the same orbital with opposite spins.
- An empty p orbital beside an occupied p orbital.

## Explanation

Two electrons in the same orbital share n, l and magnetic quantum number. Pauli therefore requires different spin projections. Parallel spins would make all four quantum numbers identical. Empty orbitals can be compatible with Pauli; Hund’s rule adds criteria for the lowest-energy state.
