For $n=3$ and $l=1$, which $m_l$ values, orbital count and maximum electron count are allowed?

- $m_l=-1,0,+1$; three orbitals and three electrons.
- $m_l=-2,-1,0,+1,+2$; five orbitals and ten electrons.
- $m_l=0$; one orbital and two electrons.
- $m_l=-1,0,+1$; three orbitals and six electrons.

## Explanation

The magnetic quantum number takes integer values from $-l$ to $+l$, giving three values for $l=1$. Each orbital can contain two electrons with opposite spins, so capacity is six. Hund’s rule describes initial separate occupancy; it does not restrict a p subshell’s maximum electron count to three.
