Which pair correctly names $\mathrm{SO_3^{2-}}$ and $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$, in that order?

- Hydrogensulfite and hydrogensulfate.
- Sulfide and sulfate.
- Sulfate and sulfite.
- Sulfite and sulfate.

## Explanation

The species are sulfur oxoanions with three and four oxygen atoms, named sulfite and sulfate. Sulfide contains no oxygen; the prefix hydrogen would indicate an H in the formula. Equal overall charge does not make the names interchangeable.
