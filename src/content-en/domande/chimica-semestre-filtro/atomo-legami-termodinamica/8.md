In $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$, Cl has oxidation state $-1$. What are iron’s oxidation state and the Stock name?

- Fe $+1$; iron(I) chloride.
- Fe $-3$; iron(III) chloride.
- Fe $+3$; iron(III) chloride.
- Fe $+2$; iron(II) chloride.

## Explanation

The formula is neutral, so $x+3(-1)=0$ and $x=+3$. The Roman numeral specifies iron’s oxidation state. Chlorine’s subscript instead contributes three chloride charges to the balance. The name neither assigns iron a negative oxidation state nor counts three iron atoms.
