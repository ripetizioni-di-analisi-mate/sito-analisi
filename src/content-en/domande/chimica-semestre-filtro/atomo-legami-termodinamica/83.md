At constant pressure, which ordinary phase changes absorb heat?

- Freezing and vaporisation.
- Freezing and condensation.
- Melting and condensation.
- Melting and vaporisation.

## Explanation

Melting and vaporisation have positive enthalpy under ordinary conditions and require heat. Reverse processes release heat with negative enthalpy. This compares enthalpy signs, not Gibbs signs at arbitrary temperature. At coexistence temperature, one phase can reversibly become the other with zero transformation Gibbs energy.
