Which pair consists of isotopes of the same element?

- ${}^{40}_{18}\mathrm{Ar}$ and ${}^{40}_{20}\mathrm{Ca}$.
- ${}^{12}_{6}\mathrm C$ and ${}^{13}_{6}\mathrm C$.
- ${}^{14}_{6}\mathrm C$ and ${}^{14}_{7}\mathrm N$.
- ${}^{23}_{11}\mathrm{Na}$ and ${}^{24}_{12}\mathrm{Mg}$.

## Explanation

Isotopes share atomic number, hence proton number, but differ in neutrons and mass number. Both carbon nuclei have six protons. Pairs with the same mass number and different Z instead belong to different elements: matching A does not identify the element.
