Which bond is nonpolar covalent because the two atoms are equivalent?

- The H-Cl bond in hydrogen chloride.
- The bond in $\mathrm{H_2}$.
- The C-O bond in carbon monoxide.
- The O-H bond in water.

## Explanation

The two hydrogen atoms have equal electronegativity and share bonding density symmetrically in the isolated molecule. The listed heteronuclear bonds join atoms with different electronegativities. Nonpolar here describes the individual bond; whole-molecule polarity with several bonds also requires geometry.
