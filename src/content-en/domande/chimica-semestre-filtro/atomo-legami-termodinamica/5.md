In VSEPR, S in $\mathrm{SO_2}$ has two bonding domains and one lone pair. Which domain arrangement, molecular shape and polarity follow?

- Linear domains; linear, nonpolar molecule.
- Tetrahedral domains; bent, polar molecule.
- Trigonal planar domains; bent, polar molecule.
- Trigonal planar domains; linear, nonpolar molecule.

## Explanation

Three domains have an approximately trigonal planar arrangement. One is a lone pair rather than a bonded atom, so the molecule’s three atoms form a bent shape. The polar S-O contributions are not opposite and do not cancel. Counting separate components of a multiple bond as separate domains would give an incorrect domain geometry.
