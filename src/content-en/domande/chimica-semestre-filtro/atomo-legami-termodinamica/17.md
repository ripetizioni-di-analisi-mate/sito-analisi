A sample contains 80% of an isotope of mass $20.0\,\mathrm u$ and 20% of one of mass $22.0\,\mathrm u$. The percentages are by number of atoms. What is its mean atomic mass?

- $20.4\,\mathrm u$.
- $21.6\,\mathrm u$.
- $42.0\,\mathrm u$.
- $21.0\,\mathrm u$.

## Explanation

The weighted mean is $0.80\cdot20.0+0.20\cdot22.0=20.4\,\mathrm u$. It must lie between the masses and closer to the more abundant isotope. The arithmetic mean 21.0 would assume equal abundances; 21.6 reverses them. Simply adding the masses describes two atoms rather than the mean per atom.
