For a main-group element, the jump from the second to the third ionisation energy is much greater than that from the first to the second. Which outer configuration best fits this observation?

- $ns^2np^5$.
- $ns^2$.
- $ns^1$.
- $ns^2np^1$.

## Explanation

Two relatively less costly removals followed by a large jump suggest two valence electrons. The third removal then involves a more strongly bound inner level. An ns² configuration fits this pattern. The criterion uses the relative jump between successive energies: all ionisations require energy, and the first two are not assumed free.
