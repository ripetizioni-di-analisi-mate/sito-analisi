Using rounded masses, one water molecule has mass approximately $18\,\mathrm u$. Which statement is consistent?

- One mole of molecules has mass approximately $18\,\mathrm g$.
- One mole contains approximately $18$ molecules.
- Molar mass is approximately $18\,\mathrm{u\,mol^{-1}}$.
- One molecule has mass approximately $18\,\mathrm g$.

## Explanation

Mass in u concerns one entity, whereas molar mass concerns an Avogadro collection of entities. At ordinary precision, the numerical mass in u matches the molar mass in grams per mole. Molecules per mole are fixed by Avogadro’s constant, not by the value 18.
