How many oxygen atoms correspond to one formula unit of $\mathrm{Ca_3(PO_4)_2}$?

- $4$.
- $6$.
- $12$.
- $8$.

## Explanation

The subscript 2 outside the parentheses multiplies the entire PO₄ group, giving two phosphorus atoms and eight oxygens. Calcium’s subscript 3 applies only to Ca and does not multiply phosphate. Reading grouped formulas correctly also supports molar-mass and amount-ratio calculations.
