For electron counting in the oxide ion $\mathrm{O^{2-}}$, with $Z=8$, what ground-state configuration and unpaired-electron count follow in the orbital model?

- $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^2$; two unpaired.
- $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6$; zero unpaired.
- $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^4$; two unpaired.
- $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6$; six unpaired.

## Explanation

The double negative charge adds two electrons to the neutral atom’s eight. Ten electrons fill 1s, 2s and 2p. In a filled p subshell, each of the three orbitals holds an opposite-spin pair, leaving no unpaired electrons. Neutral oxygen’s count cannot be transferred to the anion without updating occupancy.
