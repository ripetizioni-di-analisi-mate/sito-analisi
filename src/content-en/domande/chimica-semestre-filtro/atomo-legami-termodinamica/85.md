A substance melts reversibly at $300\,\mathrm K$, with $\Delta H_{\mathrm{fus}}=6.00\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. What is $\Delta S_{\mathrm{fus}}$?

- $20.0\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- $-20.0\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- $0.0200\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- $1800\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

At coexistence temperature, $\Delta S_{\mathrm{fus}}=\dfrac{\Delta H_{\mathrm{fus}}}{T}=\dfrac{6000}{300}=20.0\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$. Kilojoules must be converted to joules for the requested units. The positive sign is consistent with melting. Multiplying by T would also give units incompatible with entropy.
