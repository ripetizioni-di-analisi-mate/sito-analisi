Ideal helium and oxygen are at the same temperature. Which particle comparison is correct in the classical regime?

- Equal characteristic speed; greater energy for He.
- Equal mean translational energy; higher characteristic speed for He.
- Greater mean translational energy for He because it is faster on average.
- Greater mean translational energy for O₂ because it is heavier.

## Explanation

Mean translational energy per particle is $\dfrac32k_BT$ and depends only on temperature. At equal energy, the lighter particle has a greater root-mean-square speed. This concerns translational energy and does not equate the total internal energies of monatomic and diatomic gases.
