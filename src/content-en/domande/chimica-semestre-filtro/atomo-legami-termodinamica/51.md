A gas of nonpolar molecules can condense. Which interaction explains why lacking permanent dipoles does not remove all attractions?

- Ionic attraction between necessarily pre-existing cations and anions.
- London dispersion.
- Only hydrogen bonding.
- Only permanent-dipole interactions.

## Explanation

Even a molecule with an average symmetrical distribution has instantaneous electron-density fluctuations. These can induce and correlate dipoles in neighbouring molecules. The resulting attractions contribute to condensation. Zero average permanent dipole therefore does not mean zero intermolecular interaction.
