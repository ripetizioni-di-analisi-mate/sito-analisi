A transformation has $\Delta H=18.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ and $\Delta S=60.0\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$, constant over the range. What is $\Delta G$ at $350\,\mathrm K$, and at what temperature is it zero?

- $+39.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$; $300\,\mathrm K$.
- $+3.00\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$; $300\,\mathrm K$.
- $-3.00\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$; $300\,\mathrm K$.
- $-21.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$; $350\,\mathrm K$.

## Explanation

Converting entropy gives $\Delta S=0.0600\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$. Thus $\Delta G=18.0-350\cdot0.0600=-3.00\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. The threshold is $T_*=\dfrac{18000}{60.0}=300\,\mathrm K$. Above it, the entropy term dominates: a process can be both endothermic and exergonic. This threshold uses the constant-change approximation.
