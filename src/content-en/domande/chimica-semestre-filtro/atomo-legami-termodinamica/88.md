For a reversible reaction at equilibrium, Gibbs change per extent is zero. What does this imply for forward and reverse transformations?

- Both transformations must cease completely.
- Forward and reverse rates balance, with no net progress.
- Forward rate must remain greater than reverse rate.
- Reactants and products must have equal concentrations.

## Explanation

Chemical equilibrium is dynamic: both transformations may continue while their net contributions cancel. The criterion does not require equal concentrations, which depend on the reaction and conditions. A larger forward rate would produce net composition change, inconsistent with the specified equilibrium.
