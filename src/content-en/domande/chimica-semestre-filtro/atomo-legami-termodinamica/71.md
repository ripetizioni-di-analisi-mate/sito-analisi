For the same ideal gas, absolute temperature quadruples. How does $v_{\mathrm{rms}}$ change?

- It increases sixteenfold.
- It halves.
- It quadruples.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

From $v_{\mathrm{rms}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3RT}{M}}$, with fixed M, the speed ratio is $\sqrt4=2$. Mean translational kinetic energy quadruples, but speed enters energy quadratically. This difference prevents transferring the factor four directly to speed.
