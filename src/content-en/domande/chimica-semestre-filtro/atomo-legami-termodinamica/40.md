In the Lewis structure of BF₃, B forms three single bonds and has no lone pairs. Which conclusion is correct?

- B necessarily has an unwritten lone pair completing its octet.
- B is surrounded by six electrons and has an incomplete octet.
- B completes its own octet because each of the three F atoms completes its octet.
- The formula must be neutral BF₄ because octets are always compulsory.

## Explanation

Three bonding pairs give six electrons around boron. This is an electron-deficient species whose central atom does not satisfy the octet rule. Arbitrarily adding a lone pair or atom would change electron counting and the species. The octet rule is a useful model with a limited scope.
