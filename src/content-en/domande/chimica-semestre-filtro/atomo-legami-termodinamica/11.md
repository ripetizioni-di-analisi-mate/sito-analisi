What volume is occupied by $0.250\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ideal gas at $300\,\mathrm K$ and absolute pressure $1.00\,\mathrm{atm}$? Use $R=0.082057\,\mathrm{L\,atm\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.

- $61.5\,\mathrm L$.
- $6.15\,\mathrm L$.
- $24.6\,\mathrm L$.
- $0.615\,\mathrm L$.

## Explanation

The equation of state gives $V=\dfrac{nRT}{p}=\dfrac{0.250\cdot0.082057\cdot300}{1.00}\simeq6.15\,\mathrm L$. R uses units consistent with the data. One mole under these conditions would occupy approximately 24.6 L; the sample contains one quarter of a mole. Molar volume must not be borrowed from different standard conditions.
