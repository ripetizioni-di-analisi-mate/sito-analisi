An element has a noninteger mean atomic mass. Which interpretation is correct?

- Atomic number must equal the rounded mean mass.
- Every nucleus must contain a fraction of a neutron.
- The sample may contain isotopes in different proportions.
- Every atom must contain a fraction of a proton.

## Explanation

A sample’s atomic mass is an abundance-weighted mean of isotope masses. Individual nuclei have integer proton and neutron counts. Actual isotope masses also do not exactly equal mass numbers expressed in u. Z remains a proton count, distinct from the mean of the masses.
