In iron(III) oxide, what does III express?

- The number of iron atoms per formula unit.
- The compound’s total atom count.
- The charge of the entire neutral formula unit.
- Iron’s oxidation state.

## Explanation

Stock nomenclature identifies an element’s oxidation state with a Roman numeral. This is not its formula subscript: Fe₂O₃ contains two iron atoms, each at plus three. Oxygens at minus two balance the assigned iron charges, leaving a neutral formula unit.
