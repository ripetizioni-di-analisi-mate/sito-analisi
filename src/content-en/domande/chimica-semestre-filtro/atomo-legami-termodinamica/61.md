Which neutral formula uses the smallest ratios of $\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}$ and $\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}$?

- $\mathrm{Ca_3(PO_4)_2}$.
- $\mathrm{CaPO_4}$.
- $\mathrm{Ca_2(PO_4)_3}$.
- $\mathrm{Ca_3PO_4}$.

## Explanation

Three calcium ions provide $3\cdot(+2)=+6$ and two phosphates $2\cdot(-3)=-6$. Three to two is the smallest integer ratio cancelling charge. Reversing the subscripts fails neutrality. Parentheses show that the outer subscript multiplies the entire phosphate anion without altering its internal composition.
