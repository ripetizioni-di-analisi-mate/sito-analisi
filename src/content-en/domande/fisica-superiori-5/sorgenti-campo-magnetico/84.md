A long coaxial cable carries $5.0\,\mathrm A$ in its central conductor. Outside the sheath at $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis, a tangential field of $10\,\mu\mathrm T$ is measured in the direction produced by the central current. With cylindrical symmetry and zero background, what current returns in the sheath? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $3.0\,\mathrm A$
- $2.0\,\mathrm A$
- $7.0\,\mathrm A$
- $5.0\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The external field measures the net current $I_{\rm net}=\dfrac{2\pi rB}{\mu_0}=2.0\,\mathrm A$ in the central-current direction. To reduce the central current to that net value the sheath must return $5.0-2.0=3.0\,\mathrm A$.
