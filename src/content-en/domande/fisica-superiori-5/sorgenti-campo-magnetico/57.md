An ideal clamp meter surrounds both the outgoing and return wires of a two-wire circuit carrying 2 A. What net current is enclosed?

- $0\,\mathrm A$
- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $1\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The currents cross the clamp surface with opposite signs. Their algebraic sum is $2-2=0$ even though both wires carry current.
