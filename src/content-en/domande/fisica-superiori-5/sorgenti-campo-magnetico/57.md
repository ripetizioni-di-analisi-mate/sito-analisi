A circular loop of radius $4$ m carries current $9$ A. What is $B/\mu_0$ at the center, in amperes per meter?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$

## Explanation

At the center of the loop, the magnitude is $B=\mu_0 I/(2R)$. Divide the current by twice the radius to obtain $\dfrac{9}{8}$.
