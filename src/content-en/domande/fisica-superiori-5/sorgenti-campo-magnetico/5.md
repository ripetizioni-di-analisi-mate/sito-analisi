A circular loop of radius $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries current $3.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Calculate the magnetic field at its center. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

- $4.7\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $94\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $15\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $47\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

At the centre each current element contributes along the axis. Adding around the circumference gives the field of a circular loop. $B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2R} \approx 4.7 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$.
