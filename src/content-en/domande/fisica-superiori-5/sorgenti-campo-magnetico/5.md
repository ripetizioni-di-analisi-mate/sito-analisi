Two infinitely long, straight, parallel wires are $0.1$ m apart and carry currents of $2$ A and $1$ A. What is the magnitude of the force per unit length, in micronewtons per meter? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}$ T m/A.

- $4\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu N/m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $\dfrac{F}{\ell}=\dfrac{\mu_0 I_1I_2}{2\pi d}$. Substituting the data gives $4\cdot10^{-6}$ newtons per meter. The required value is therefore $4$ micronewtons per meter.
