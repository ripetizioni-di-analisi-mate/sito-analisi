A closed path in the plane of the page, traversed counterclockwise, encloses three wires perpendicular to the page, carrying currents $6.0\,\mathrm{A}$ out of the page, $2.0\,\mathrm{A}$ into the page, and $9.0\,\mathrm{A}$ into the page. Calculate the circulation of the magnetic field around the path. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

- $0\,\mathrm{T\,m}$
- $+6.3\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T\,m}$
- $-6.3\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T\,m}$
- $-2.1\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T\,m}$

## Explanation

Counterclockwise traversal chooses the outward normal. The algebraic current is negative and Ampère's law gives negative circulation. $\Gamma(\vec{B}) = \mu_0\,(6.0 - 2.0 - 9.0)\,\mathrm{A} \approx -6.3 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m}$.
