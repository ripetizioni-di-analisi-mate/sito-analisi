At a distance of $4$ cm from a long straight wire, the field has magnitude $54$ µT. What is its magnitude in microteslas at a distance of $12$ cm?

- $6$
- $54$
- $18$
- $162$

## Explanation

For a long straight wire carrying constant current, the field is inversely proportional to distance. Tripling the distance reduces the field to one-third of its original value, giving $18$.
