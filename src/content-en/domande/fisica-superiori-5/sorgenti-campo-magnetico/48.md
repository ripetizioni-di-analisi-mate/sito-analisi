A circular loop is immersed in a nonuniform magnetic field. The flux of the field through the flat disk bounded by the loop is $3.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$. What is the flux through a bag-shaped surface bounded by the same loop, oriented consistently with the same direction of traversal of the loop?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $-3.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The disk and the bag have the same boundary, so together they form a closed surface.

On this surface the outward normal coincides with the normal of one of the two surfaces and with the opposite of the normal of the other, therefore the outward flux is the difference between the fluxes through the disk and through the bag.

By Gauss's law for the magnetic field the outward flux through a closed surface is zero, so the two fluxes are equal. The flux through the bag is therefore $3.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$, whatever its shape.
