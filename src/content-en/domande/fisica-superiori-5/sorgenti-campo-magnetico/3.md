Two infinitely long, straight, parallel wires are $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart and carry currents $I_1 = 3.0\,\mathrm{A}$ and $I_2 = 9.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in the same direction. Find the point on the line joining the wires where the magnetic field is zero.

- Between the wires, $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the $3.0\,\mathrm A$ wire
- Between the wires, $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the $3.0\,\mathrm A$ wire
- Outside the pair, near the $9.0\,\mathrm A$ wire
- Halfway between the wires

## Explanation

Between currents in the same direction the two fields oppose. Equal magnitudes require $3/x=9/(8-x)$ with distances in centimetres. Outside the segment the fields add.
