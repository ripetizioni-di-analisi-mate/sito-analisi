An experimental $B(I)$ curve for a core flattens at higher currents. Why does doubling the current there fail to double the field?

- Magnetization approaches saturation
- The circuit must become open
- The turn count halves
- The current always reverses

## Explanation

Many domains are already aligned. Effective permeability is not constant, so the initial linear relation no longer describes the whole interval.
