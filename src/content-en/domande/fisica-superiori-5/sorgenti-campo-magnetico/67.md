Two parallel wires $2$ cm apart exert a force per meter of $20$ µN/m. One current doubles and the separation quadruples. What is the new force per meter, in µN/m?

- $20$
- $10$
- $40$
- $5$

## Explanation

The force per unit length is directly proportional to the product of the currents and inversely proportional to the separation. The overall factor is one-half, giving $10$.
