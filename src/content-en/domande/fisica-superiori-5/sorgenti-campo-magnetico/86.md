A bundle of closely spaced straight wires carries steady currents. Far away compared with their spacing, which arrangement most reduces the field relative to a single outgoing wire?

- Place equal outgoing and return currents together
- Remove all nearby return wires
- Increase all currents in the same direction
- Bundle only outgoing currents

## Explanation

From far away the distances to corresponding portions of the two wires are nearly equal. Their opposite currents largely cancel each other's fields.
