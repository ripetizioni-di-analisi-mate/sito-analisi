A square loop of side $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ is placed in a uniform magnetic field of $0.25\,\mathrm{T}$. The normal to the loop makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. Calculate the magnetic flux through the loop.

- $3.1\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{mWb}$

## Explanation

The angle is measured from the normal, so use its cosine. The loop area is $(0.12\,\mathrm m)^2$. $\Phi(\vec{B}) = B S \cos 60^\circ = 1.8 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
