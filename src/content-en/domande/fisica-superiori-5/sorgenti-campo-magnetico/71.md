A passive magnetic shield for a compass uses a high-permeability enclosure. What is its main effect?

- It offers a preferred flux path around the cavity
- It always doubles the uniform field in the cavity
- It only insulates static electric current
- It absorbs and destroys north poles

## Explanation

The material magnetizes readily and guides part of the flux through its walls. The field inside the cavity can thereby decrease.
