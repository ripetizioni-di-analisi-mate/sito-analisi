The outward magnetic flux through part of a closed surface is $7$ mWb. What is the signed flux through the remaining part, in milliwebers?

- $14$
- $7$
- $-7$
- $0$

## Explanation

The total magnetic flux through a closed surface is zero. The flux through the remaining part must cancel the outward flux and therefore has the opposite sign, giving $-7$.
