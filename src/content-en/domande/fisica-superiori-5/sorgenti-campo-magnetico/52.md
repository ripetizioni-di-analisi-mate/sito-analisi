A wire along the $z$-axis carries $5$ A in the $+z$ direction. In which direction does the field point at $(2,0,0)$?

- Toward $-y$.
- Toward $+z$.
- Toward $+x$.
- Toward $+y$.

## Explanation

Curl the fingers of your right hand around the wire with your thumb pointing along the current. On the positive x-axis, the tangent to the circular field lines points toward $+y$.
