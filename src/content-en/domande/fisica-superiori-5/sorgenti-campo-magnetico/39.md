Two currents pass through a surface bounded by an Amperian loop. The first is $8$ A in the positive direction. The circulation divided by $\mu_0$ is $3$ A. What is the signed value of the second current, in amperes?

- $-11$
- $5$
- $-5$
- $11$

## Explanation

The circulation divided by $\mu_0$ gives the algebraic sum of the enclosed currents. Subtracting the first current’s contribution from that sum gives $-5$.
