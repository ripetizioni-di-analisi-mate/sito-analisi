A toroid has $1200$ turns. The magnetic field at a distance $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis, inside the ring, is to have magnitude $4.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$. Determine the current the turns must carry. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

- $0.40\,\mathrm A$
- $0.25\,\mathrm A$
- $2.5\,\mathrm A$
- $30\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

For an air-core toroid each turn crosses the Amperian surface, so the enclosed current is $NI$. $I = \dfrac{2\pi r B}{\mu_0 N} = 2.5\,\mathrm{A}$.
