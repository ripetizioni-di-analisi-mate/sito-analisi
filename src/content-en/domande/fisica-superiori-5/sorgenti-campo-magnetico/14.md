A long solenoid with 800 turns per metre carries $0.50\,\mathrm A$. Without the core its central field is about $0.50\,\mathrm{mT}$. If the iron inserted has relative permeability 2000 under the test conditions, what field is predicted in the core?

- $1000\,\mathrm T$
- $1.0\,\mathrm T$
- $0.25\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{mT}$

## Explanation

For a long solenoid the fixed current maintains $H=nI$. The iron-to-vacuum field ratio is $\mu_r$, so $B=2000\,(0.50\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm T)$.
