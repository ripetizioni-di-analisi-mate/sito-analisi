The field of an infinitely long straight wire is $28$ microteslas at a distance $r$. What is its magnitude at a distance $2r$ if the current remains constant?

- $14\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $56\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $28\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $7\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

For an infinitely long wire, the field is inversely proportional to distance. Doubling the distance halves the field. Its new magnitude is therefore $14$ microteslas.
