Three branches pierce a surface. A current of 7 A enters while 2 A and 5 A leave. Why can a point on the surface still have a nonzero field?

- The field must be parallel to every edge
- The surface becomes a magnetic insulator
- The algebraic sum fixes circulation around the boundary
- Each wire stops producing a field

## Explanation

Ampère's law gives a sum along a closed curve. A zero total can contain nonzero local contributions.
