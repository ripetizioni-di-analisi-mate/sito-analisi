The outward magnetic flux through part of a closed surface is $5$ mWb. What is the signed flux through the remaining part, in milliwebers?

- $5$
- $0$
- $-5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The total magnetic flux through a closed surface is zero. The flux through the remaining part must cancel the outward flux and therefore has the opposite sign, giving $-5$.
