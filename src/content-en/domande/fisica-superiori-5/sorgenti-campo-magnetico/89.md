A cylindrical wire of radius $6$ mm carries a uniformly distributed current. The surface field is $14$ mT. What is the field in milliteslas at a distance of $3$ mm from the axis?

- $28$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

Inside the wire, the enclosed current increases with the square of the radius. Dividing the circulation by the circumference gives a field proportional to the radius, yielding $7$.
