One loop has zero flux in a nonzero uniform field. An equal-area second loop lies in a perpendicular plane. Is maximum flux through the second loop guaranteed?

- Yes, because total flux is zero
- Yes, for any perpendicular pair of planes
- No, the field direction within the first plane is also needed
- No, because both fluxes must vanish

## Explanation

The field lies in the first plane. It might align with the second normal, but it could also lie along the line where the two planes intersect.
