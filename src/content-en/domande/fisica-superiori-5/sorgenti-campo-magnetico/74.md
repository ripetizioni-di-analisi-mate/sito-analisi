A long solenoid has halves wound with 400 and 600 turns per metre in the same sense. Far from the ends and the junction, where is the field stronger at the same current?

- It is equal because the wire is continuous
- In the 600-turns-per-metre half
- It is zero in both
- In the 400-turns-per-metre half

## Explanation

In the long-solenoid approximation $B=\mu nI$. With material and current fixed the local turns per metre set the field.
