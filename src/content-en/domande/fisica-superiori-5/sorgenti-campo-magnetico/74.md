An Amperian loop encloses currents of $7$ A and $3$ A in opposite directions. The first flows in the positive direction. What is the circulation of the field in units of $\mu_0$?

- $4$
- $-4$
- $10$
- $0$

## Explanation

Ampère’s law uses the algebraic sum of the enclosed currents. Subtract the current in the opposite direction from the positive contribution to obtain $4$.
