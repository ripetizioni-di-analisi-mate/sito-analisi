A long solenoid has $400$ turns per meter and carries $9$ A. How many turns per meter would maintain the same field if the current fell to $9/2$ A?

- $1600$
- $400$
- $200$
- $800$

## Explanation

In a long solenoid, the field is proportional to the number of turns per unit length times the current. Halving the current requires doubling the number of turns per unit length, giving $800$.
