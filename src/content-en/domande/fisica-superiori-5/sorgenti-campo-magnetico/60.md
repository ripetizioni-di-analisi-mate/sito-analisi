On a straight $0.20\,\mathrm m$ segment a uniform $3\,\mathrm{mT}$ field makes $60^\circ$ with the direction of travel. What is the contribution to circulation?

- $0.30\,\mathrm{mT\,m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{mT\,m}$
- $0.52\,\mathrm{mT\,m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mT\,m}$

## Explanation

Only the component parallel to displacement contributes. It gives $B\ell\cos60^\circ=3\cdot0.20/2=0.30\,\mathrm{mT\,m}$.
