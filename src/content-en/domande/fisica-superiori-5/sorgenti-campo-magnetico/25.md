Four infinite straight wires, perpendicular to the page, pass through the vertices of a square of side $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and carry the same current $10\,\mathrm{A}$, out of the page in the two wires of the upper side and into the page in the two wires of the lower side. What is the magnitude of the magnetic field at the center $O$ of the square?

- $0$
- $1.1 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $5.7 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $8.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

Each wire is $\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 7.07\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center, so it produces at $O$ a field of magnitude $B_1 = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi d} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 10^{-7} \cdot 10}{0.0707}\,\mathrm{T} \approx 2.83 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$, perpendicular to the half-diagonal.

By the right-hand rule the field lines of a wire carrying current out of the page circulate counterclockwise and those of a wire carrying current into the page circulate clockwise. The center lies below the upper wires and above the lower ones, so all four fields have at $O$ a horizontal component pointing to the right, while the vertical components cancel in pairs.

Each horizontal component equals $\dfrac{B_1}{\sqrt{2}} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$, therefore $B = 4 \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T} = 8.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$.
