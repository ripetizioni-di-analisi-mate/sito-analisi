The outward magnetic flux through part of a closed surface is $9$ mWb. What is the signed flux through the remaining part, in milliwebers?

- $-9$
- $18$
- $9$
- $0$

## Explanation

The total magnetic flux through a closed surface is zero. The flux through the remaining part must cancel the outward flux and therefore has the opposite sign, giving $-9$.
