A linear sample has magnetic susceptibility $\chi=0.002$ and retains no magnetization when the field is removed. What is its relative permeability?

- $0.998$
- $500$
- $0.002$
- $1.002$

## Explanation

For a linear medium $\mu_r=1+\chi$. A small positive susceptibility therefore gives a relative permeability just above one.
