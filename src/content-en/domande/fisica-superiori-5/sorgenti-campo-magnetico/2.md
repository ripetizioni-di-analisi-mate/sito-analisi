A field magnitude of $5.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm T$ is measured $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a very long straight wire to infer the current. Which additional information would also determine the current direction?

- The wire's resistance per unit length
- The field magnitude at twice the distance
- The diameter of a second circle centred on the wire
- The tangential field direction at a point of known position relative to the wire

## Explanation

The data already give the magnitude $I=2\pi rB/\mu_0=15\,\mathrm A$. Reversing the current preserves the magnitude but reverses the field direction by the right-hand rule.
