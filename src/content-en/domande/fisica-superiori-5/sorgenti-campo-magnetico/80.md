Two parallel wires $10$ cm apart carry currents in the same direction, $3$ A in the first wire and $7$ A in the second. How many centimeters from the first wire, on the segment between the wires, is the magnetic field zero?

- $10$
- $5$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

Between the wires, the fields point in opposite directions. For equal magnitudes, the distances to the wires must be in the same ratio as the currents and must add up to the total separation, giving $3$.
