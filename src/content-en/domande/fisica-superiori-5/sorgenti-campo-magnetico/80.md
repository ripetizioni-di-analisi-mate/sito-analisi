A narrow air gap is cut in a toroidal core of constant cross-sectional area. Leakage is negligible and the flux crosses the gap normally. If the iron operates in its linear regime with $\mu_r=200$, what is the ratio $\dfrac{H_{\rm air}}{H_{\rm iron}}$?

- $200^2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{200}$
- $200$

## Explanation

With equal areas, the same flux gives equal $B$ in the iron and in the air. Since $H=\dfrac{B}{\mu}$ and air permeability is nearly $\mu_0$, a magnetizing field two hundred times greater is required in the gap.
