A winding produces an internal field from left to right. Viewed from the right end of the solenoid, how does the winding current circulate?

- Clockwise
- Counterclockwise
- Alternating between adjacent turns
- Toward the observer at every point

## Explanation

From the right the field points toward the observer. With the right thumb in that direction the fingers curl counterclockwise.
