A probe at distances $r$, $2r$, $3r$ from a wire reads 15, 9 and 7 µT, all in the same direction. What constant background fits $B(r)=B_0+k/r$?

- $9\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $6\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $0\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $3\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The difference $15-9=6$ is half the wire contribution at $r$. That contribution is 12, leaving $15-12=3$ µT background, consistent with the third reading.
