A circular loop of radius $2$ m carries current $5$ A. What is $B/\mu_0$ at the center, in amperes per meter?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

At the center of the loop, the magnitude is $B=\mu_0 I/(2R)$. Divide the current by twice the radius to obtain $\dfrac{5}{4}$.
