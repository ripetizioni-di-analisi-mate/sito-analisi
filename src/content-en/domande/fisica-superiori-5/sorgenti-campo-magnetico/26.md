The magnetic permeability of vacuum $\mu_0$ is measured in $\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$. Which unit is it equivalent to?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/(A\cdot s)}$
- $\mathrm{N/A}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot m/A^2}$
- $\mathrm{N/A^2}$

## Explanation

A straight wire of length $\ell$, carrying the current $I$ and perpendicular to a uniform field, experiences a force of magnitude $F = I \ell B$, so $1\,\mathrm{T} = 1\,\mathrm{N/(A\cdot m)}$.

Multiplying by $\mathrm{m/A}$ gives $1\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A} = 1\,\mathrm{N/A^2}$.

The same result follows from the force per unit length between parallel wires, $\dfrac{F}{\ell} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d}$. Since $\dfrac{F}{\ell}$ is measured in $\mathrm{N/m}$ and $\dfrac{I_1 I_2}{d}$ in $\mathrm{A^2/m}$, the constant $\mu_0$ is measured in $\mathrm{N/A^2}$.
