A long straight wire carries $9$ A. What is the magnetic field $40$ cm from the wire? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $45\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

Use $B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$. After the distance is converted to meters, the factor $2\cdot10^{-7}$ becomes $0.2$ when multiplying the current-to-distance ratio to obtain the field in microteslas. The result is $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$.
