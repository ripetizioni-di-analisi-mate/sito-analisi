An Amperian loop encloses currents of $9$ A and $4$ A in opposite directions. The first flows in the positive direction. What is the circulation of the field in units of $\mu_0$?

- $-5$
- $0$
- $5$
- $13$

## Explanation

Ampère’s law uses the algebraic sum of the enclosed currents. Subtract the current in the opposite direction from the positive contribution to obtain $5$.
