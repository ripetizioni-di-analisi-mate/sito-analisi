An electromagnet has opposing windings on the same linear core, $100$ turns at $0.30\,\mathrm A$ and $50$ turns at $0.80\,\mathrm A$. Which winding sets the resultant field direction?

- It depends only on wire diameter
- The 50-turn winding
- The 100-turn winding
- Neither, the field is zero

## Explanation

The contributions are proportional to ampere-turns. The opposing contributions are 30 and 40 ampere-turns, so the second winding dominates.
