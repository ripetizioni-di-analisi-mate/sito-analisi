An infinitely long straight wire carries $9$ A. What is the magnitude of the magnetic field, in microteslas, at a distance of $0.1$ m from the wire? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}$ T m/A.

- $9\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $180\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $18\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

For an infinitely long wire, $B=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$. Substituting $r=0.1$ m gives $B=18\cdot10^{-6}$ T. The field is therefore $18$ microteslas.
