A very long straight wire carries current $I = 10\,\mathrm{A}$. Calculate the magnitude of the magnetic field at a point $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the wire. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

- $800\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $40\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $80\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

For a long wire the field has the same magnitude all along a circle centred on the wire. The Amperian path length is $2\pi r$. $B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} = 8.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$.
