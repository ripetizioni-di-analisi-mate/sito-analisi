A cylindrical wire of radius $8$ mm carries a uniformly distributed current. The surface field is $18$ mT. What is the field in milliteslas at a distance of $4$ mm from the axis?

- $36$
- $18$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

Inside the wire, the enclosed current increases with the square of the radius. Dividing the circulation by the circumference gives a field proportional to the radius, yielding $9$.
