A closed cylindrical surface is placed in a nonuniform magnetic field. With the normal pointing outward, the flux through one base is $-3.2 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ and the flux through the other is $1.1 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$. Find the flux through the curved surface.

- $+0.21\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $-0.21\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $+0.43\,\mathrm{mWb}$

## Explanation

The net flux through all parts of the closed surface is zero. Add the two end fluxes with their signs and then reverse the sign of the sum. $\Phi_{\text{lat}} = 3.2 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb} - 1.1 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb} = 2.1 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ (outward), because the total flux is zero.
