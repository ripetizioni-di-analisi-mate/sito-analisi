A cylindrical wire of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a uniformly distributed $25\,\mathrm A$. To calculate the field first at $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and then at $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis, which enclosed currents belong in Ampère's law, respectively?

- $(4.0\,\mathrm A,0\,\mathrm A)$
- $(4.0\,\mathrm A,25\,\mathrm A)$
- $(10\,\mathrm A,25\,\mathrm A)$
- $(25\,\mathrm A,25\,\mathrm A)$

## Explanation

Inside, uniform current density makes enclosed current proportional to area, giving $25(2/5)^2=4\,\mathrm A$. The outer circle encloses the entire cross section.
