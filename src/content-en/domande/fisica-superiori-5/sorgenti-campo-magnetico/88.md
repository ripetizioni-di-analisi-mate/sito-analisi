A cylindrical wire of radius $4$ mm carries a uniformly distributed current. The surface field is $10$ mT. What is the field in milliteslas at a distance of $2$ mm from the axis?

- $10$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $20$

## Explanation

Inside the wire, the enclosed current increases with the square of the radius. Dividing the circulation by the circumference gives a field proportional to the radius, yielding $5$.
