A long ideal solenoid has $1000$ turns per meter and carries $8$ A. What is $B/\mu_0$ inside?

- $125\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $800\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{A/m}$

## Explanation

For a long solenoid in air, $B=\mu_0nI$. Dividing by $\mu_0$ leaves $nI$. The result is therefore $\dfrac{B}{\mu_0}=1000\cdot8=8000$ amperes per meter.
