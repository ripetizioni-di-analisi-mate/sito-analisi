A long straight wire carries $7$ A. What is the magnetic field $30$ cm from the wire? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $\dfrac{140}{3}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

Use $B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$. After the distance is converted to meters, the factor $2\cdot10^{-7}$ becomes $0.2$ when multiplying the current-to-distance ratio to obtain the field in microteslas. The result is $\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{\mu T}$.
