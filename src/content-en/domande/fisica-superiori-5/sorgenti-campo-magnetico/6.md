A solenoid of length $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ has $1000$ turns carrying current $4.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Calculate the magnetic field inside it. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

- $1.0\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $100\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{mT}$

## Explanation

For a long air-core solenoid away from its ends the relevant quantity is turns per unit length. Here there are $1000/0.50=2000$ turns per metre. $B = \mu_0\,\dfrac{N}{\ell}\,I \approx 1.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{T}$.
