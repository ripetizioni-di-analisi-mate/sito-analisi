An infinitely long straight wire carries $3$ A. What is the magnitude of the magnetic field, in microteslas, at a distance of $0.1$ m from the wire? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}$ T m/A.

- $3\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $60\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

For an infinitely long wire, $B=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$. Substituting $r=0.1$ m gives $B=6\cdot10^{-6}$ T. The field is therefore $6$ microteslas.
