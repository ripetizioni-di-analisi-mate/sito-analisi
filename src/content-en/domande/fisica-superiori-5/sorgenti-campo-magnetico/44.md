Two very long wires carry equal currents in the same direction. What is the magnetic field at the midpoint between them?

- A time-varying field even when the currents are constant.
- A field directed along the currents.
- A field twice as strong as that of a single wire.
- The total field is zero.

## Explanation

The midpoint is equally far from both wires, so the magnitudes are equal. The right-hand rule gives opposite field directions between the wires.
