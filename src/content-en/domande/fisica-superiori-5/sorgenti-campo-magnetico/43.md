A circular loop of radius $0.2$ m carries a current of $10$ A. What is $B/\mu_0$ at its center?

- $50\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{A/m}$

## Explanation

At the center of a circular loop, $B=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2r}$. Dividing by $\mu_0$ gives $\dfrac{I}{2r}$. The value is therefore $\dfrac{10}{0.4}=25$ amperes per meter.
