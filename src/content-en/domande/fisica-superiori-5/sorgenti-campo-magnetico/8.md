Two long wires $0.20\,\mathrm m$ apart carry currents of the same magnitude $I$ and repel with force per metre $f=4.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Which procedure gives the current magnitude and relative direction?

- Use $I=\sqrt{2\pi d f/\mu_0}$, with currents in opposite directions
- Use $I=2\pi d f/\mu_0$, with currents in opposite directions
- Use $I=\sqrt{2\pi d f/\mu_0}$, with currents in the same direction
- Use $I=2\pi d f/\mu_0$, with currents in the same direction

## Explanation

The force depends on the product of the currents. Equal magnitudes give $I^2$, so taking a square root gives $I\simeq6.7\,\mathrm A$. Repulsion identifies opposite currents.
