Two identical solenoids are connected in parallel to a battery. A linear core is inserted into one only. At DC steady state, assuming equal fixed resistances, which quantity remains equal in both windings?

- Core magnetization
- The current
- The internal magnetic field
- The flux per turn

## Explanation

At steady state each current equals $V/R$. The core changes the magnetic response at that current, while the resistive voltage drop stays the same.
