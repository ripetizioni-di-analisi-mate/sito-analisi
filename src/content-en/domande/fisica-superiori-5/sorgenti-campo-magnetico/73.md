An Amperian loop encloses currents of $5$ A and $2$ A in opposite directions. The first flows in the positive direction. What is the circulation of the field in units of $\mu_0$?

- $7$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $0$

## Explanation

Ampère’s law uses the algebraic sum of the enclosed currents. Subtract the current in the opposite direction from the positive contribution to obtain $3$.
