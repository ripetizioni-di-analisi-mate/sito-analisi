A circular loop of radius $3$ m carries current $7$ A. What is $B/\mu_0$ at the center, in amperes per meter?

- $\dfrac{3}{14}$
- $\dfrac{14}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

At the center of the loop, the magnitude is $B=\mu_0 I/(2R)$. Divide the current by twice the radius to obtain $\dfrac{7}{6}$.
