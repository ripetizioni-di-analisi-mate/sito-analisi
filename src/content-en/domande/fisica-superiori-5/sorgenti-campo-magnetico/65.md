A square sheet of side $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ sits normal to a uniform $0.5\,\mathrm T$ field. A student gets $2\,\mathrm{Wb}$ by writing the area as $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What result corrects the conversion?

- $2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $2\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $2\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$

## Explanation

Both dimensions must be converted. Area is $(0.02)^2=4\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the flux in webers is half that value.
