The same wire length can form one circular turn of radius $R$ or two overlapping turns of radius $R/2$. At the same current, what central field does the second arrangement give compared with the first?

- Half the field
- Four times the field
- The same field
- Twice the field

## Explanation

Central field is proportional to $N/R$. The number of turns doubles and the radius halves, giving a combined factor of four.
