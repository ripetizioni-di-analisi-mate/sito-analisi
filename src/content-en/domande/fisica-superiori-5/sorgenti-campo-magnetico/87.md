In an ideal toroid, the current and number of turns are fixed. The field is $27$ mT at a distance of $4$ cm from the axis. What is its magnitude in milliteslas at $12$ cm, still inside the toroid?

- $27$
- $81$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The circulation around a circle of radius $r$ is $2\pi rB$. The enclosed current is the same for both circles, so the field is inversely proportional to the radius, giving $9$.
