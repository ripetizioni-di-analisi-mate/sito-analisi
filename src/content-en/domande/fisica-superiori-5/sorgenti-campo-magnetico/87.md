In a long cylindrical wire all current flows uniformly in a very thin outer coating. What profile is measured inside the coating, far from the ends?

- A field increasing as $r^2$
- Zero field throughout the current-free interior
- A constant field equal to the external surface field
- A $1/r$ field all the way to the axis

## Explanation

An interior circular path encloses no current. Symmetry gives constant tangential field along it, so Ampère's law requires $B=0$.
