A circular loop of radius $0.2$ m carries a current of $9$ A. What is $B/\mu_0$ at its center?

- $45\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $11.25\,\mathrm{A/m}$

## Explanation

At the center of a circular loop, $B=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2r}$. Dividing by $\mu_0$ gives $\dfrac{I}{2r}$. The value is therefore $\dfrac{9}{0.4}=22.5$ amperes per meter.
