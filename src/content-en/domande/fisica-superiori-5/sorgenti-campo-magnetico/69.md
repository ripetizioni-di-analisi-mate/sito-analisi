After positive magnetization a negative external field must be applied to bring flux density back to zero. Which coordinate on the loop is used?

- The intercept on the magnetizing-field axis
- The geometrical area of the sample
- The intercept on the flux-density axis
- The positive maximum of $B$

## Explanation

On the return branch select the point where $B=0$. Its horizontal coordinate gives the coercive field on that branch.
