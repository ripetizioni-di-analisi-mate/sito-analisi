Two parallel wires $4$ cm apart exert a force per meter of $36$ µN/m. One current doubles and the separation quadruples. What is the new force per meter, in µN/m?

- $72$
- $9$
- $36$
- $18$

## Explanation

The force per unit length is directly proportional to the product of the currents and inversely proportional to the separation. The overall factor is one-half, giving $18$.
