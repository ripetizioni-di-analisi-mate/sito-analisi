An Amperian loop surrounds a wire carrying steady current. It is deformed without crossing the wire or reversing orientation. What remains unchanged?

- The circulation of $\mathbf B$
- The field at each point on the loop
- The loop length
- The enclosed area

## Explanation

The algebraic linked current stays fixed. Ampère's law therefore gives the same circulation even though the path changes.
