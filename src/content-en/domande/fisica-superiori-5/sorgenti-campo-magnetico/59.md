A long solenoid has $300$ turns per meter and carries $7$ A. How many turns per meter would maintain the same field if the current fell to $7/2$ A?

- $150$
- $300$
- $1200$
- $600$

## Explanation

In a long solenoid, the field is proportional to the number of turns per unit length times the current. Halving the current requires doubling the number of turns per unit length, giving $600$.
