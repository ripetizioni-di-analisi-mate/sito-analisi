Two parallel wires carry $4$ A and $9$ A in opposite directions. What is observed?

- The wires repel each other.
- The forces are parallel to the wires.
- The forces are zero.
- The wires attract each other.

## Explanation

At the second wire, the field of the first is tangent to a circle centered on the first wire. The cross product of the second wire’s current element with this field gives a force directed away from the first wire.
