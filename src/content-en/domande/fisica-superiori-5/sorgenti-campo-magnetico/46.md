At a distance of $2$ cm from a long straight wire, the field has magnitude $30$ µT. What is its magnitude in microteslas at a distance of $6$ cm?

- $10$
- $30$
- $90$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$

## Explanation

For a long straight wire carrying constant current, the field is inversely proportional to distance. Tripling the distance reduces the field to one-third of its original value, giving $10$.
