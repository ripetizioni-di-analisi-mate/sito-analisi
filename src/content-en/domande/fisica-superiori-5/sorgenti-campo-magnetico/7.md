A long ideal solenoid has $1000$ turns per meter and carries $3$ A. What is $B/\mu_0$ inside?

- $\dfrac{1000}{3}\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{A/m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{A/m}$

## Explanation

For a long solenoid in air, $B=\mu_0nI$. Dividing by $\mu_0$ leaves $nI$. The result is therefore $\dfrac{B}{\mu_0}=1000\cdot3=3000$ amperes per meter.
