To size the supports of two long parallel wires $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart, one needs the magnetic force on each metre of wire. Currents of $15\,\mathrm A$ and $10\,\mathrm A$ flow in the same direction. What is the force magnitude per metre, and do the wires attract or repel? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $0.10\,\mathrm{mN/m}$, attractive
- $1.0\,\mathrm{mN/m}$, repulsive
- $10\,\mathrm{mN/m}$, repulsive
- $1.0\,\mathrm{mN/m}$, attractive

## Explanation

The first wire creates the field that exerts a force on the second. The right-hand rule gives attraction for the two currents in the same direction. $\dfrac{F}{\ell} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d} = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N/m}$, attractive.
