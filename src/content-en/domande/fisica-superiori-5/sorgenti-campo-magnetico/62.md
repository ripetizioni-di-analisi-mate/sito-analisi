A flat surface is divided into two coplanar patches with the original normal. How is the flux of the full surface recovered?

- Multiply the fluxes
- Always subtract the second flux
- Average the fluxes
- Add the signed fluxes

## Explanation

Flux sums contributions of normal field across the area. The patches cover disjoint parts, so their fluxes add.
