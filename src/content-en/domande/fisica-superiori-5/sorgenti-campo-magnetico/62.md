An ideal solenoid of length $3$ m has $700$ turns. How many turns must a solenoid of length $9$ m have to produce the same field at the same current?

- $700$
- $2100$
- $\dfrac{700}{3}$
- $6300$

## Explanation

At a fixed current, producing the same field requires the same number of turns per meter. Tripling the length therefore triples the number of turns, giving $2100$.
