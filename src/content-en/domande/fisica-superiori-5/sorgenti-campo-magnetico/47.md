At a distance of $3$ cm from a long straight wire, the field has magnitude $42$ µT. What is its magnitude in microteslas at a distance of $9$ cm?

- $14$
- $42$
- $\dfrac{14}{3}$
- $126$

## Explanation

For a long straight wire carrying constant current, the field is inversely proportional to distance. Tripling the distance reduces the field to one-third of its original value, giving $14$.
