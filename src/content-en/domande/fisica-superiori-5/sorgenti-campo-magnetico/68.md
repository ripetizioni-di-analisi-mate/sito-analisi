Two parallel wires $3$ cm apart exert a force per meter of $28$ µN/m. One current doubles and the separation quadruples. What is the new force per meter, in µN/m?

- $14$
- $56$
- $7$
- $28$

## Explanation

The force per unit length is directly proportional to the product of the currents and inversely proportional to the separation. The overall factor is one-half, giving $14$.
