Two materials have hysteresis loops drawn on the same axes. Which is better for an electromagnet core that should lose almost all magnetization when switched off?

- The heavier one regardless of its loop
- The one with a longer winding wire
- The one with maximum remanent flux density
- The one with small remanent flux density

## Explanation

The residual field at zero current indicates how much magnetization persists. Nearly switching off the electromagnet requires a small residual value.
