Two parallel wires $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart carry equal $5.0\,\mathrm A$ currents in opposite directions. At the midpoint each wire produces about $17\,\mu\mathrm T$. How do the two contributions combine?

- They oppose and cancel
- They are perpendicular and give about $24\,\mu\mathrm T$
- They point the same way and give about $33\,\mu\mathrm T$
- Only the inward current contributes and $17\,\mu\mathrm T$ remains

## Explanation

The midpoint lies on opposite sides of the two wires. Applying the right-hand rule to opposite currents therefore gives two parallel fields pointing in the same direction there.
