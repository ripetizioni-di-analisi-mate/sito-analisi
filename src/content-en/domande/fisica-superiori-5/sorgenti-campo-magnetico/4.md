The field of an infinitely long straight wire is $8$ microteslas at a distance $r$. What is its magnitude at a distance $2r$ if the current remains constant?

- $16\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\mu T}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu T}$

## Explanation

For an infinitely long wire, the field is inversely proportional to distance. Doubling the distance halves the field. Its new magnitude is therefore $4$ microteslas.
