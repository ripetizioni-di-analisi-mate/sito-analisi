Two infinite straight wires lie in the plane of the page: one along the $x$ axis, carrying the current $6.0\,\mathrm{A}$ toward increasing $x$, the other along the $y$ axis, carrying the current $6.0\,\mathrm{A}$ toward increasing $y$. The wires are insulated at the crossing point. What is the magnitude of the magnetic field at the point $P = (3.0,\ 4.0)\,\mathrm{cm}$?

::grafico{nomi="x,y" uguale=1 vettori="1,0,2.5,0,I;0,1,0,2.5,I" punti="3,4,pieno,P" segmenti="3,0,3,4;0,4,3,4" xmin=-1 xmax=5.5 ymin=-1 ymax=5.5 titolo="Wires along the $x$ and $y$ axes (coordinates in centimeters), carrying the current $I$ toward increasing coordinates, and the point $P$"}

- $4.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $7.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$
- $1.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The wire along the $x$ axis is $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from $P$ and produces there a field of magnitude $B_1 = \dfrac{2 \cdot 10^{-7} \cdot 6.0}{0.040}\,\mathrm{T} = 3.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$. By the right-hand rule, above a wire carrying current toward increasing $x$ the field points out of the page.

The wire along the $y$ axis is $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from $P$ and produces there $B_2 = \dfrac{2 \cdot 10^{-7} \cdot 6.0}{0.030}\,\mathrm{T} = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$; to the right of a wire carrying current toward increasing $y$ the field points into the page.

The two fields are perpendicular to the page with opposite directions, so $B = B_2 - B_1 = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$, into the page.
