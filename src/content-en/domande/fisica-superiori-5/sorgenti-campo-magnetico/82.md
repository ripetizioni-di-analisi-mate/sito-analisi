A core has a relative permeability of $500$ under the measurement conditions. Which description is consistent with this value?

- A vacuum.
- A ferromagnetic core.
- A weakly diamagnetic material.
- A material with a relative permeability of one.

## Explanation

A relative permeability much greater than one is consistent with a ferromagnetic material under the stated conditions. The field inside the core can be much stronger than the field in a vacuum.
