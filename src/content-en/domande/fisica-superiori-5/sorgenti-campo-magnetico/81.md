A model proposes $B=k/r^2$ outside a wire. After background subtraction, which experimental plot directly tests the correct $B\propto1/r$ relation at fixed current?

- Plot $B$ against $1/r$ and look for a straight line through the origin
- Plot $B$ against $r$ and look for an increasing straight line
- Plot $B^2$ against $r$ and look for a constant
- Plot $B$ against time and ignore $r$

## Explanation

If $B=k/r$, choosing $x=1/r$ gives $B=kx$. The proposed inverse-square law would give $B=kx^2$ instead.
