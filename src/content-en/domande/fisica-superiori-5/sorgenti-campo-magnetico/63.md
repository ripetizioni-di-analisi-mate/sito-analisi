An ideal solenoid of length $4$ m has $900$ turns. How many turns must a solenoid of length $12$ m have to produce the same field at the same current?

- $2700$
- $900$
- $8100$
- $300$

## Explanation

At a fixed current, producing the same field requires the same number of turns per meter. Tripling the length therefore triples the number of turns, giving $2700$.
