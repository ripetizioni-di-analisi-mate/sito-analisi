In an ideal toroid, the current and number of turns are fixed. The field is $15$ mT at a distance of $2$ cm from the axis. What is its magnitude in milliteslas at $6$ cm, still inside the toroid?

- $15$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $45$

## Explanation

The circulation around a circle of radius $r$ is $2\pi rB$. The enclosed current is the same for both circles, so the field is inversely proportional to the radius, giving $5$.
