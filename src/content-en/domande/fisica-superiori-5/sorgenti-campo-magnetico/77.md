A closed path encloses two currents of $3$ A flowing in opposite directions. A third wire carrying $7$ A lies outside the path. What is the circulation in units of $\mu_0$?

- $6$
- $0$
- $13$
- $7$

## Explanation

Only the enclosed currents enter the sum in Ampère’s law. The first two cancel because they have equal magnitudes and opposite directions, giving $0$.
