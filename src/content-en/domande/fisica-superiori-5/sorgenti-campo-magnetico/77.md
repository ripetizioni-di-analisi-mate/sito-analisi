A probe measures the axial field at three points near the centre of a long solenoid. Which trend fits the ideal model?

- An alternating sign at every turn
- A halving every centimetre
- Three equal readings within uncertainty
- A field vanishing only on the axis

## Explanation

The ideal model neglects edge effects and treats the winding as continuous. The internal field is uniform and axial.
