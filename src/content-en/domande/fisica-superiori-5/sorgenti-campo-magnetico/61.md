A rectangular loop has opposite sides parallel to a uniform field. A student adds both $B\ell$ contributions as positive. What correction is needed?

- Replace length by area
- Also add four terms $B$ without length
- Double both contributions
- Reverse the sign of one contribution

## Explanation

Travel around the rectangle traverses the opposite sides in opposite directions. The dot products of field and displacement cancel.
