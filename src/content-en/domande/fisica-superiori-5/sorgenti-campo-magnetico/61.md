An ideal solenoid of length $2$ m has $500$ turns. How many turns must a solenoid of length $6$ m have to produce the same field at the same current?

- $4500$
- $500$
- $\dfrac{500}{3}$
- $1500$

## Explanation

At a fixed current, producing the same field requires the same number of turns per meter. Tripling the length therefore triples the number of turns, giving $1500$.
