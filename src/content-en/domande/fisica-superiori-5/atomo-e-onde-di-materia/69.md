Two nonrelativistic particles have the same kinetic energy. The second has $16$ times the mass of the first, whose de Broglie wavelength is $9$ nm. What is the wavelength of the second in nanometers?

- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $144$

## Explanation

At fixed energy, momentum is proportional to the square root of mass. Wavelength is inversely proportional to the magnitude of the momentum, giving $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
