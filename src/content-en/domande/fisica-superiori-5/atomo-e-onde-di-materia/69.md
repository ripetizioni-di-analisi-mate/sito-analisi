An entire normalized wave function is multiplied by $-1$. What change is observed when a position measurement is repeated on the new state?

- The position distribution stays the same
- All maxima turn into nodes
- Total probability becomes negative
- Every measured position changes sign

## Explanation

An overall sign disappears in the squared magnitude. At every point $|-\psi(x)|^2=|\psi(x)|^2$, so each interval retains the same probability.
