In the Bohr model $r_n=n^2a_0$ and $v_n=\dfrac{v_1}{n}$. Which pair describes the $n=4$ orbit relative to the ground-state orbit?

- $(4a_0,\dfrac{v_1}{4})$
- $(16a_0,4v_1)$
- $(4a_0,\dfrac{v_1}{16})$
- $(16a_0,\dfrac{v_1}{4})$

## Explanation

The radius grows as the square of the index, giving $4^2a_0$. The speed varies inversely with the index and falls to one quarter of $v_1$.
