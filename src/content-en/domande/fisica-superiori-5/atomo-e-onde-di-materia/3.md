A particle has momentum $2\cdot10^{-24}$ kg m/s. Taking $h=6.6\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, what is its de Broglie wavelength in nanometers?

- $1.32\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.33\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.033\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation is $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$. Substitution gives $\lambda=\dfrac{6.6}{2}\cdot10^{-10}$ meters. Converting to nanometers gives $\lambda=\dfrac{0.66}{2}=\dfrac{33}{100}$ nm.
