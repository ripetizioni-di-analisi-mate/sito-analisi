To test $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$, the measured wavelengths are plotted vertically. Which horizontal axis gives a straight line through the origin with slope $h$?

- $p$
- $\dfrac{1}{p}$
- $\sqrt p$
- $p^2$

## Explanation

Setting $x=\dfrac{1}{p}$ gives $\lambda=hx$, a linear relation. Using $p$ directly would instead give a hyperbola.
