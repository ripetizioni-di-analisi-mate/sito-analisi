The gold-foil experiment uses a very thin foil to interpret rare large deflections. What ambiguity would a much thicker foil introduce?

- Gold would acquire a different atomic number
- Several successive deflections could contribute to the observed angle
- The Coulomb force would become attractive
- The alpha particle's charge would reverse at every encounter

## Explanation

In a thin foil a large angle can more plausibly be attributed to one close encounter. Increasing the thickness makes multiple encounters along the same path more likely.
