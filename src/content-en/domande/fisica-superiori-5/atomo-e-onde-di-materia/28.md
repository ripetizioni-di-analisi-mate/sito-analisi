A particle has momentum $7\cdot10^{-24}$ kg m/s. Taking $h=6.6\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, what is its de Broglie wavelength in nanometers?

- $4.62\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $\dfrac{33}{35}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $\dfrac{33}{3500}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $\dfrac{33}{350}\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation is $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$. Substitution gives $\lambda=\dfrac{6.6}{7}\cdot10^{-10}$ meters. Converting to nanometers gives $\lambda=\dfrac{0.66}{7}=\dfrac{33}{350}$ nm.
