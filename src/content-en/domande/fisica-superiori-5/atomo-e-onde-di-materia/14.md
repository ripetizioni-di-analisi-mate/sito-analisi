A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{4}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $32\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $16\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=4$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{4}=16\cdot2=32$ electronvolts.
