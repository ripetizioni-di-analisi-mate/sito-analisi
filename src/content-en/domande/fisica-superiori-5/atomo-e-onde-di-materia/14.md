The $n=3$ Bohr orbit has radius $9a_0$. What is the electron’s de Broglie wavelength along the orbit?

- $54\pi a_0$
- $3\pi a_0$
- $6\pi a_0$
- $18\pi a_0$

## Explanation

The wave must return in phase after one turn, so the circumference contains three wavelengths. Dividing $2\pi(9a_0)$ by $3$ gives $6\pi a_0$.
