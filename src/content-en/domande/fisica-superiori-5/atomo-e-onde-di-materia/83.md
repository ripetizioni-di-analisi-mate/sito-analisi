When an infinite wall is replaced by a finite wall higher than the energy of a bound state, a small density is found just beyond the edge. Which description is consistent with this state?

- The observation proves that the total energy exceeds the wall
- The density becomes negative outside the edge
- Every state must remain identically zero beyond the edge
- The wave function can extend into the classically forbidden region and decay

## Explanation

A finite wall requires matching to a decaying solution in the region where $U>E$. Hence even a bound state can have a nonzero penetration probability.
