The ground-state energy in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls is $7$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=3$, in eV?

- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $63$

## Explanation

The energy levels in an infinitely deep well increase with the square of the quantum number. Multiplying the ground-state energy by that square gives $63$.
