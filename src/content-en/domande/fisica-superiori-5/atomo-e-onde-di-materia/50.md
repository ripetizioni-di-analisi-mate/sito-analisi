A free electron initially travels to the right. After elastic reflection it travels left at the same speed. What happens to its de Broglie plane wave?

- The wavelength stays the same and the wave vector reverses
- The wavelength becomes negative
- The momentum magnitude changes
- The wave vector magnitude becomes zero

## Explanation

Reflection preserves $|p|$ but reverses the momentum direction. Since $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{|p|}$ and the wave vector is parallel to momentum, only the propagation direction changes.
