The ground-state energy of hydrogen is $-E_0$, with $E_0>0$. What is the energy of level $n=3$ in units of $E_0$?

- $- \dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-9$

## Explanation

In the Bohr model, the energy of a bound state is negative. Its magnitude decreases as the reciprocal of the square of the quantum number, giving $- \dfrac{1}{9}$.
