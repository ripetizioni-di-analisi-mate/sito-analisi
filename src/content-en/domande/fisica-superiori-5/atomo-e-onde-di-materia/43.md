A particle has momentum $10\cdot10^{-24}$ kg m/s. Taking $h=6.6\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, what is its de Broglie wavelength in nanometers?

- $6.6\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.66\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.066\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.0066\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation is $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$. Substitution gives $\lambda=\dfrac{6.6}{10}\cdot10^{-10}$ meters. Converting to nanometers gives $\lambda=\dfrac{0.66}{10}=\dfrac{33}{500}$ nm.
