A sample emits photons of energy $3.0\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$ on a spectral line. The energies of the photons registered on the line sum to $6.0\cdot10^{-13}\,\mathrm J$. The detector registers half of the incident photons. How many photons arrived?

- $4.0\cdot10^6$
- $1.0\cdot10^6$
- $4.0\cdot10^{-6}$
- $2.0\cdot10^6$

## Explanation

Dividing the registered energy by the energy per photon gives $2.0\cdot10^6$ counts. The efficiency is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so twice that many photons arrived.
