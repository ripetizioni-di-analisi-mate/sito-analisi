A nonrelativistic particle A and a particle B of four times the mass have the same kinetic energy. For A the measured wavelength is $\lambda_A=5\,\mathrm{nm}$. What wavelength is predicted for B?

- $10\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

Since $p=\sqrt{2mK}$, quadrupling the mass at fixed kinetic energy doubles the momentum. The relation $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$ therefore halves the wavelength.
