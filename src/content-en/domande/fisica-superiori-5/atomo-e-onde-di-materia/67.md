Two nonrelativistic particles have the same kinetic energy. The second has $4$ times the mass of the first, whose de Broglie wavelength is $5$ nm. What is the wavelength of the second in nanometers?

- $20$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

At fixed energy, momentum is proportional to the square root of mass. Wavelength is inversely proportional to the magnitude of the momentum, giving $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
