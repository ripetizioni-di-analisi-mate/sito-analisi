A one-dimensional well has infinite walls at $x=0$ and $x=L$. Which proposed function, with $A\ne0$, satisfies the boundary conditions and can represent a nonzero stationary state inside?

- $A\cos(\dfrac{\pi x}{L})$
- $A\sin(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L})$
- $A$
- $A\sin(\dfrac{\pi x}{2L})$

## Explanation

The wave function must vanish at both walls. The sine with argument $\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}$ is zero at $0$ and at $L$ and takes nonzero values between the walls.
