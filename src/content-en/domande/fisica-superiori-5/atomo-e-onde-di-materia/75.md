For a particle, $\Delta x=4$ nm. What is the lower bound on $\Delta p$ in units of $\hbar/\mathrm{nm}$?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The uncertainty inequality is $\Delta x\,\Delta p\geq\hbar/2$. Dividing the lower bound on the product by the position uncertainty gives $\dfrac{1}{8}$.
