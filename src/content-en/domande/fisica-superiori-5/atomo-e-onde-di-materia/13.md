For an electron in a one-dimensional infinite well of width $1.0\,\mathrm{nm}$, $\dfrac{h^2}{8m_eL^2}=0.376\,\mathrm{eV}$. Which level contains an electron of energy $3.38\,\mathrm{eV}$?

- $n=2$
- $n=3$
- $n=9$
- $n=4$

## Explanation

In the well the energy is $n^2E_1$. The ratio $\dfrac{3.38}{0.376}$ is about $9$, so the index is its positive square root, $3$.
