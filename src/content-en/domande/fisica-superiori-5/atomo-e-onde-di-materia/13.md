A particle has momentum $4\cdot10^{-24}$ kg m/s. Taking $h=6.6\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, what is its de Broglie wavelength in nanometers?

- $0.165\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $2.64\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $1.65\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.0165\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation is $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$. Substitution gives $\lambda=\dfrac{6.6}{4}\cdot10^{-10}$ meters. Converting to nanometers gives $\lambda=\dfrac{0.66}{4}=\dfrac{33}{200}$ nm.
