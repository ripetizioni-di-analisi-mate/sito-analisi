A particle has momentum $3\cdot10^{-24}$ kg m/s. Taking $h=6.6\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, what is its de Broglie wavelength in nanometers?

- $1.98\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.022\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.22\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation is $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$. Substitution gives $\lambda=\dfrac{6.6}{3}\cdot10^{-10}$ meters. Converting to nanometers gives $\lambda=\dfrac{0.66}{3}=\dfrac{11}{50}$ nm.
