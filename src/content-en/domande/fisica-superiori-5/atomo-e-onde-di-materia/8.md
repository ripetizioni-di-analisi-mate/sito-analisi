A proton of mass $1.67\cdot10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels at $2.0\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Given $h=6.63\cdot10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$, what is its de Broglie wavelength?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $200\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The speed is far below the speed of light, so $p=mv=3.34\cdot10^{-24}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ applies. This gives $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}\simeq2.0\cdot10^{-10}\,\mathrm m$.
