An atom goes from an energy level of $-4$ eV to one of $-6$ eV. What is the energy of the emitted photon?

- $-2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $10\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $4\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

In emission, the atom goes to a lower energy level. The photon’s energy is the difference $E_i-E_f$. Here it is $(-4)-(-6)=2$ electronvolts.
