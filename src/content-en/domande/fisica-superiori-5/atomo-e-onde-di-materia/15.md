A cool, dilute gas is placed in front of a continuous lamp. Dark lines appear in the transmitted beam. What does the position of each line measure?

- The absolute temperature of one atom
- The total energy of all the electrons
- The speed of each absorbed photon
- The energy difference between two accessible levels

## Explanation

The gas removes photons from the direct beam when their frequency matches a transition. The wavelength of the line thus determines the energy gap through $\Delta E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$.
