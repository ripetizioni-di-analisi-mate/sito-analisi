A proposed orbit in the Bohr model has angular momentum $L=2.5\hbar$. Why is it absent from the allowed sequence?

- $L$ and $\hbar$ have different units
- Every orbit has $L=\hbar$
- Angular momentum must be negative
- The ratio $\dfrac{L}{\hbar}$ must be a positive integer

## Explanation

The Bohr model requires $mvr=n\hbar$ with $n=1,2,\ldots$. The proposed value lies between those for $n=2$ and $n=3$.
