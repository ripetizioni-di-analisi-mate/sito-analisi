A calibrated scanning tunneling microscope follows $I(s)=I_0e^{-2\kappa s}$, where $s$ is the tip–surface separation. How can $\kappa$ be obtained from the slope $b$ of a plot of $\ln(\dfrac{I}{I_0})$ against $s$?

- $\kappa=\dfrac{1}{b}$
- $\kappa=\dfrac{b}{2}$
- $\kappa=-\dfrac{b}{2}$
- $\kappa=-2b$

## Explanation

Taking the logarithm turns the exponential decay into $\ln(\dfrac{I}{I_0})=-2\kappa s$. The coefficient of $s$ is $b=-2\kappa$, which determines the attenuation constant.
