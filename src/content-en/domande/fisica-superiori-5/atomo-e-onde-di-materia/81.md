A program returns a wave function with $\int|\psi|^2\,dx=9$. By what positive factor should $\psi$ be multiplied before probabilities are calculated?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $9$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the amplitude by $c$ changes the integral of the squared magnitude to $9c^2$. Setting $9c^2=1$ and taking the positive factor gives $c=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
