For an electron whose kinetic energy is comparable to $m_ec^2$, which procedure correctly gives its de Broglie wavelength?

- Impose the photon relation $E=pc$ on the electron
- Use relativistic momentum in $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$
- Always use $p=\sqrt{2m_eK}$
- Set $\lambda=0$ as soon as $K=m_ec^2$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation still holds, but the classical approximation for $p$ loses accuracy. Momentum follows from $p^2c^2=(K+m_ec^2)^2-m_e^2c^4$.
