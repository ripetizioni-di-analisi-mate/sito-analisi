A hydrogen atom goes from $n=3$ to $n=1$. What is the photon energy in units of $|E_1|$?

- $\dfrac{10}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The photon energy is the initial energy minus the final energy. The energy levels are negative and vary inversely with the square of the quantum number, giving $\dfrac{8}{9}$.
