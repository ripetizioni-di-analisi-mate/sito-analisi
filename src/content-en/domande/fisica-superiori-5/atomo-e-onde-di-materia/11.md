In an electronic device the spread in the transverse position of an electron is $\Delta x=1.0\,\mathrm{nm}$. Use $\hbar=1.05\cdot10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$ and $m_e=9.11\cdot10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the lower bound on the spread of its transverse velocity?

- $1.2\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.6\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.8\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.8\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Along this direction $\Delta x\,m_e\Delta v_x\geq\dfrac{\hbar}{2}$. Dividing $\hbar$ by $2m_e\Delta x$ gives $\Delta v_x\geq5.8\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
