In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=4$?

- $-3.4\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.85\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-0.85\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-217.6\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=4$ in the given formula gives $E_{4}=-\dfrac{13.6}{16}=- \dfrac{17}{20}$ electronvolts.
