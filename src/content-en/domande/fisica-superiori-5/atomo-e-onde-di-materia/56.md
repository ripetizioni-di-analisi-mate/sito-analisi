A hydrogen atom goes from $n=4$ to $n=1$. What is the photon energy in units of $|E_1|$?

- $\dfrac{15}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{17}{16}$

## Explanation

The photon energy is the initial energy minus the final energy. The energy levels are negative and vary inversely with the square of the quantum number, giving $\dfrac{15}{16}$.
