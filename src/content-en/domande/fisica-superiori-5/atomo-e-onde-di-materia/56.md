In repeated identical preparations $p_x$ is measured once per particle. Its mean is zero and its spread is large. What can be concluded from the zero mean?

- The spread must actually be zero
- Every position measurement gives the same result
- Every particle has $p_x=0$
- Positive and negative contributions cancel in the mean

## Explanation

The mean takes the signs of the individual results into account. A distribution containing opposite values can have zero mean and still have a large spread.
