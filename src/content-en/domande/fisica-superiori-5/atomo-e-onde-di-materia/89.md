A particle with kinetic energy $2\,\mathrm{eV}$ tunnels through a static $3\,\mathrm{eV}$ barrier. The potentials on both sides are equal and the interaction is elastic. What kinetic energy is measured far beyond the barrier for a transmitted particle?

- $1\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $3\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

A static barrier conserves total energy. Since the final potential matches the initial potential, a transmitted particle emerges with the same kinetic energy.
