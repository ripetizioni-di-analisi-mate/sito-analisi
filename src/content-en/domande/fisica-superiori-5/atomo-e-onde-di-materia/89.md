A particle encounters a finite rectangular barrier higher than its energy. How does the tunneling transmission probability change if only the width increases, from $3$ to $7$ nm?

- The transmission probability increases.
- The transmission probability stays constant.
- The transmission probability becomes exactly one.
- The transmission probability decreases.

## Explanation

The wave function decays inside the barrier. For the same barrier height and particle energy, a wider barrier gives a smaller transmitted amplitude.
