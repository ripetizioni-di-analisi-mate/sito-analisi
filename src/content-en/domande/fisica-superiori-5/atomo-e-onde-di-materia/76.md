The graph shows a stationary wave in an infinite well. The walls are at $x=0$ and $x=L$. How many half-wavelengths fit between them?

::grafico{f="x<0||x>1?NaN:sin(3*pi*x)" linee="0,-1.2 0,1.2;1,-1.2 1,1.2" xmin=-0.12 xmax=1.15 ymin=-1.4 ymax=1.4 nomi="x/L,ψ" legenda=0 titolo="Wave amplitude against position as a fraction of the well width."}

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The four nodes bound three intervals between successive nodes. Each interval is half a wavelength, so this is the $n=3$ state.
