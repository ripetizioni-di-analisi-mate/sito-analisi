The position uncertainty is reduced from $10$ to $5$ nm. By what factor does the lower bound on $\Delta p$ change?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The lower bound on momentum uncertainty is inversely proportional to position uncertainty. Reducing the position uncertainty by a factor increases the bound by the same factor, giving $2$.
