Two wave packets have the same $\Delta x$. The first is measured to have $\Delta x\,\Delta p_x=\dfrac{\hbar}{2}$. What follows about the second from this information alone?

- It can have $\Delta p_x=0$ with that same finite $\Delta x$
- Its $\Delta p_x$ must be twice as large
- Its $\Delta p_x$ is at least that of the first
- It must have the same $\Delta p_x$

## Explanation

The first packet attains the minimum allowed at the given $\Delta x$. The same inequality holds for the second packet, but its product may exceed the minimum.
