A particle has de Broglie wavelength $7$ nm. What is its new wavelength in nanometers if its momentum increases by a factor of $3$?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $7$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie wavelength is Planck’s constant divided by the momentum. Multiplying the momentum by a factor therefore divides the wavelength by the same factor, giving $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
