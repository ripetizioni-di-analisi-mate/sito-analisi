A particle can occupy only three disjoint regions. The probabilities of the first two are $0.18$ and $0.37$. What probability must the third have for the state to be normalized?

- $0.45$
- $0.55$
- $0.19$
- $0.76$

## Explanation

A measurement must find the particle in one of the three regions. Their probabilities sum to one, so the third is $1-0.18-0.37=0.45$.
