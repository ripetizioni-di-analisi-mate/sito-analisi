At one point on a screen the amplitudes of two coherent paths, in common units, are $1$ and $i$. What is the relative intensity there?

- $\sqrt2$
- $4$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

First add the amplitudes to obtain $1+i$. The intensity is the squared magnitude, $(1-i)(1+i)=2$.
