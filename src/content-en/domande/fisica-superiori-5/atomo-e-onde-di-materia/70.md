A beam of nonrelativistic electrons has de Broglie wavelength $5$ nm. The accelerating voltage increases by a factor of $4$, with negligible initial speed. What is the new wavelength in nanometers?

- $10$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy is proportional to accelerating voltage. Momentum grows as the square root of energy, and the wavelength decreases by the corresponding factor, giving $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
