A discharge prepares $100$ atoms in level C. Exactly $70\%$ decay directly to A with one photon, and the remainder decay along C–B–A with two photons per atom. How many photons are emitted in all?

- $200$
- $100$
- $130$
- $170$

## Explanation

The direct branch produces $70$ photons. The other $30$ atoms produce $2\cdot30=60$ photons, bringing the total to $130$.
