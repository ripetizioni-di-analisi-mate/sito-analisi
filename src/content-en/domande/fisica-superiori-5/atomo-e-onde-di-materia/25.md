An atom goes from an energy level of $-6$ eV to one of $-8$ eV. What is the energy of the emitted photon?

- $2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $14\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $6\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

In emission, the atom goes to a lower energy level. The photon’s energy is the difference $E_i-E_f$. Here it is $(-6)-(-8)=2$ electronvolts.
