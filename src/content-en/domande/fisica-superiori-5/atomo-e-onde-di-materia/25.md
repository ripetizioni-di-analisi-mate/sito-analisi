In a model with levels at $0$, $2$, and $5\,\mathrm{eV}$, an incident $3\,\mathrm{eV}$ electron excites a ground-state atom. Neglecting recoil, what kinetic energy does the electron retain?

- $3\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The only accessible excitation costs $2\,\mathrm{eV}$. In the collision the electron can transfer that part of its energy and continue with $3-2=1\,\mathrm{eV}$.
