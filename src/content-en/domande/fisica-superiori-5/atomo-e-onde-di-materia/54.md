Hydrogen has energy levels $E_n=-\dfrac{13.6}{n^2}$ eV. How much energy in eV is needed to ionize it from state $n=4$?

- $- \dfrac{17}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1088}{5}$
- $\dfrac{17}{5}$
- $\dfrac{17}{20}$

## Explanation

At the ionization threshold, the energy increases from the bound-state value to zero. The required energy is therefore the magnitude of the initial energy, giving $\dfrac{17}{20}$.
