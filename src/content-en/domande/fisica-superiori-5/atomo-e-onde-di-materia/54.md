A device records with certainty which of two slits each electron uses. Both slits remain open. How does the screen distribution change when the measurement makes the paths fully distinguishable?

- The interference term between paths disappears
- Every electron lands at the centre
- The fringe count always doubles
- Momentum becomes exactly zero

## Explanation

Fully distinguishable paths contribute probabilities that add. The cross term that generates fringes for coherent, indistinguishable paths is lost.
