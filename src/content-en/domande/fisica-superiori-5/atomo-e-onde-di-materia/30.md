An alpha particle changes direction in a static Coulomb field and returns to a point far from the fixed nucleus. Neglect radiative losses. Which quantity is equal before and after scattering?

- Its velocity component along the initial direction for every angle
- Its momentum vector
- The magnitude of its momentum
- Its distance from the nucleus at all times

## Explanation

Far from the nucleus the incoming and outgoing potential energies have the same value. Conservation of mechanical energy therefore gives equal kinetic energies and so are the momentum magnitudes, even though the directions differ.
