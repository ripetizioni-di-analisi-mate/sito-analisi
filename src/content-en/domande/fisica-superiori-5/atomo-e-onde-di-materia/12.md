To estimate the energy width of a state lasting $\tau=2.0\,\mathrm{ns}$, use $\Delta E\simeq\dfrac{\hbar}{2\tau}$. Take $\hbar=6.58\cdot10^{-16}\,\mathrm{eV\,s}$. What is the order of magnitude?

- $10^{7}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $10^{-16}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $10^{-7}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The lifetime is $2.0\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm s$. The quotient is about $1.6\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{eV}$ and therefore has the stated order of magnitude.
