An electron beam is repeatedly prepared in the same state. Detector resolution is improved until instrumental error is negligible. What does the width of the recorded position distribution still measure?

- Necessarily the geometric size of an electron
- Only a residual detector defect
- The interval between two clock readings
- The intrinsic position spread of the state

## Explanation

The state assigns probabilities to different positions. Even an ideal instrument samples that distribution and can therefore record a nonzero spread.
