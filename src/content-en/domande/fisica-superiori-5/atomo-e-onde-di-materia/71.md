Probability density is constant in each of two windows. It is $4\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$ across $0.01\,\mathrm{nm}$ in A and $1\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$ across $0.10\,\mathrm{nm}$ in B. In which window is a detection more likely?

- The probabilities are equal
- In A, with probability $0.40$
- In B, with probability $0.10$
- In A, with probability $1$

## Explanation

The area under the density matters. Density times width gives $0.04$ in A and $0.10$ in B, so the wider window collects more events.
