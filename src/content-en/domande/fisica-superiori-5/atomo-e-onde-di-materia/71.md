A beam of nonrelativistic electrons has de Broglie wavelength $7$ nm. The accelerating voltage increases by a factor of $9$, with negligible initial speed. What is the new wavelength in nanometers?

- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $21$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy is proportional to accelerating voltage. Momentum grows as the square root of energy, and the wavelength decreases by the corresponding factor, giving $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
