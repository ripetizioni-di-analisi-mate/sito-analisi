A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{5}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $25\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $50\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=5$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{5}=25\cdot2=50$ electronvolts.
