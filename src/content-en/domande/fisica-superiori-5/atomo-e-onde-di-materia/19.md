An atom can emit light of frequency $f_1$ from C to B and of frequency $f_2$ from B to A. If a direct transition from C to A is also allowed, what frequency does its photon have?

- $\dfrac{f_1+f_2}{2}$
- $f_1+f_2$
- $|f_1-f_2|$
- $\dfrac{f_1f_2}{f_1+f_2}$

## Explanation

The direct transition spans the two consecutive energy gaps. Since $E_C-E_A=hf_1+hf_2$, dividing by $h$ gives $f_1+f_2$.
