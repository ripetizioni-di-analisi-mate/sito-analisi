In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=7$?

- $- \dfrac{68}{35}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $- \dfrac{68}{245}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{68}{245}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-666.4\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=7$ in the given formula gives $E_{7}=-\dfrac{13.6}{49}=- \dfrac{68}{245}$ electronvolts.
