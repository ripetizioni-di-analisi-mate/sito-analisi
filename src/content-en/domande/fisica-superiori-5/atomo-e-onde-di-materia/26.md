In the Thomson model positive charge is spread uniformly throughout a sphere. By symmetry, what electric force does that distribution alone exert on a test particle exactly at the centre?

- Infinite force toward the surface
- Constant force toward the incoming beam
- Zero force
- The force of the entire charge concentrated on the test particle

## Explanation

Each portion of charge has an identical counterpart on the opposite side. At the centre their fields have equal magnitudes and opposite directions, so their sum is zero.
