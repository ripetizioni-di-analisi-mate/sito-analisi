For a particle, $\Delta x=3$ nm. What is the lower bound on $\Delta p$ in units of $\hbar/\mathrm{nm}$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The uncertainty inequality is $\Delta x\,\Delta p\geq\hbar/2$. Dividing the lower bound on the product by the position uncertainty gives $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
