A particle is confined between $0$ and $L$ in a state whose probability density is symmetric about $\dfrac{L}{2}$. Which result follows without knowing the detailed shape of the density?

- The probability of finding it in the left half is $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- The density is constant everywhere
- The maximum must be at the centre
- The particle must have the ground-state energy

## Explanation

By symmetry every interval on the left has an equally probable mirror interval on the right. The two half-box probabilities are thus equal and add to one.
