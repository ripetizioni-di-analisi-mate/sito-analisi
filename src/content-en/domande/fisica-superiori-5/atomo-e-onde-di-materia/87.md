A particle in a box with infinitely high walls has energy $9$ eV in a given level. The width is tripled and the quantum number is unchanged. What is the new energy in eV?

- $3$
- $1$
- $27$
- $81$

## Explanation

At a fixed quantum number, energy is inversely proportional to the square of the width. Tripling the width divides the energy by nine, giving $1$.
