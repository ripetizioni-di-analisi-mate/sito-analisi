A particle encounters a barrier with no absorption. In $500$ identical preparations, $35$ particles are expected to be transmitted. Which reflection coefficient corresponds to this prediction?

- $0.35$
- $0.07$
- $0.65$
- $0.93$

## Explanation

The transmission probability is $T=\dfrac{35}{500}=0.07$. With no absorption probability is conserved, so $R=1-T=0.93$.
