In the ground state of a box $|\psi_1(x)|^2$ is constant in time but varies with $x$. Which statement correctly distinguishes these two properties?

- A fixed interval retains its probability over time, which can differ from that of another interval
- Each particle keeps a definite position at the centre
- The kinetic energy is zero
- All intervals of equal width have the same probability

## Explanation

A stationary state preserves the shape of its distribution over time. Its spatial profile is still proportional to $\sin^2(\dfrac{\pi x}{L})$, so probabilities differ near the centre and near the walls.
