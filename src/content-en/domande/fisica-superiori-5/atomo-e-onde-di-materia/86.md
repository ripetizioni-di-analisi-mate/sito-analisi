A particle in a box with infinitely high walls has energy $7$ eV in a given level. The width is tripled and the quantum number is unchanged. What is the new energy in eV?

- $63$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

At a fixed quantum number, energy is inversely proportional to the square of the width. Tripling the width divides the energy by nine, giving $\dfrac{7}{9}$.
