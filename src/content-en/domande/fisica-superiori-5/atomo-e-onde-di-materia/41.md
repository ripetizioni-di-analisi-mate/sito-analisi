In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=10$?

- $-0.136\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-1.36\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.136\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-1360\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=10$ in the given formula gives $E_{10}=-\dfrac{13.6}{100}=- \dfrac{17}{125}$ electronvolts.
