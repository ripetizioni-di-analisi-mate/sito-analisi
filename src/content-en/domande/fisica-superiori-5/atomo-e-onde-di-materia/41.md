An isolated atom initially at rest emits a photon to the right. What momentum vector must the atom have immediately afterwards if the initial total momentum was zero?

- Zero because the photon has no rest mass
- Equal in magnitude and opposite to the photon's momentum
- Perpendicular to the photon's momentum
- Equal in both magnitude and direction to the photon's momentum

## Explanation

The photon carries momentum $\dfrac{h}{\lambda}$ to the right. To keep the total momentum zero, the atom recoils to the left with the same momentum magnitude.
