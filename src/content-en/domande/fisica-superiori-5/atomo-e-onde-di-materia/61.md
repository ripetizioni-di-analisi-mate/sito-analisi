In a thin foil nearly all alpha particles continue forward, while a few turn back. Which atomic structure accounts for both observations?

- An atom uniformly filled with impenetrable matter
- A diffuse positive sphere producing only slight deflections
- A massive positive nucleus concentrated in a very small volume
- An outer electron shell containing nearly all the atomic mass

## Explanation

The rare backscattering events require a strong field in a close encounter. If the positive charge is concentrated in a small nucleus, most particles pass far from it.
