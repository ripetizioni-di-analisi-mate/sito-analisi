A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{6}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $36\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{1}{18}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $72\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=6$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{6}=36\cdot2=72$ electronvolts.
