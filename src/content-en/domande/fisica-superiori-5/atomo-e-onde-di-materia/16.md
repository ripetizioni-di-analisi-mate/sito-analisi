In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=5$?

- $-2.72\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.544\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-340\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-0.544\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=5$ in the given formula gives $E_{5}=-\dfrac{13.6}{25}=- \dfrac{68}{125}$ electronvolts.
