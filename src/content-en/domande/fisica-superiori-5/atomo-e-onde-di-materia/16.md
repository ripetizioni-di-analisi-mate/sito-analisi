A gas has lines at $450$, $510$, and $620\,\mathrm{nm}$. A catalogue assigns lines at $450$ and $620\,\mathrm{nm}$ to element X and a line at $510\,\mathrm{nm}$ to element Y. With only X and Y as candidates, which sample explains all three lines?

- A mixture of X and Y
- X alone
- Y alone
- A sample containing neither X nor Y

## Explanation

The two outer lines match X. The middle line also requires Y, since the emission spectra of the species present are superimposed.
