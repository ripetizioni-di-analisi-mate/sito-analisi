In region A, the magnitude of the wave function is $2$ times its magnitude in region B. What is the ratio of the probability densities, A to B?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Probability density is the square of the magnitude of the wave function. Squaring the ratio of the magnitudes gives $4$.
