At points A and B in the same state, $|\psi(A)|=2|\psi(B)|$ and $|\psi(B)|>0$. What is the probability-density ratio $\dfrac{\rho(A)}{\rho(B)}$?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Born's rule gives $\rho=|\psi|^2$. The magnitude ratio is two, so the density ratio is its square, $2^2=4$.
