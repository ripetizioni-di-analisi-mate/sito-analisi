The position uncertainty is reduced from $21$ to $7$ nm. By what factor does the lower bound on $\Delta p$ change?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The lower bound on momentum uncertainty is inversely proportional to position uncertainty. Reducing the position uncertainty by a factor increases the bound by the same factor, giving $3$.
