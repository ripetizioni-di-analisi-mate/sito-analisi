Why must $n=0$ be excluded from the family $\psi_n(x)=A\sin(\dfrac{n\pi x}{L})$ in the infinite well?

- The wave would have a node only at the right wall
- The function would vanish everywhere and could not be normalized
- Its energy would be negative and unbounded
- Its total probability would be infinite

## Explanation

For $n=0$ the sine's argument vanishes everywhere. The integral of $|\psi|^2$ would be zero for every $A$, incompatible with a particle whose total probability is one.
