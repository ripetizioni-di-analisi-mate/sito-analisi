Hydrogen has energy levels $E_n=-\dfrac{13.6}{n^2}$ eV. How much energy in eV is needed to ionize it from state $n=2$?

- $\dfrac{34}{5}$
- $\dfrac{17}{5}$
- $\dfrac{272}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{17}{5}$

## Explanation

At the ionization threshold, the energy increases from the bound-state value to zero. The required energy is therefore the magnitude of the initial energy, giving $\dfrac{17}{5}$.
