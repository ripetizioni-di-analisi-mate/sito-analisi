A hydrogen energy diagram is redrawn by adding $5\,\mathrm{eV}$ to every level and to the continuum threshold. Which observable stays the same?

- The numerical value assigned to the threshold
- The numerical label next to each level
- The frequency of every spectral line
- The sum of the labels of two levels

## Explanation

Changing the zero shifts the initial and final energies by the same amount. The difference entering $hf=|E_f-E_i|$ stays the same.
