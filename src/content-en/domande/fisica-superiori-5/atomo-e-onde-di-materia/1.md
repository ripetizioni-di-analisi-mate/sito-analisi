In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=2$?

- $-3.4\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-6.8\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $3.4\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-54.4\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=2$ in the given formula gives $E_{2}=-\dfrac{13.6}{4}=- \dfrac{17}{5}$ electronvolts.
