Hydrogen emits a Balmer line in the $6\to2$ transition. Using $R_H=1.097\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and $\lambda^{-1}=R_H(n_f^{-2}-n_i^{-2})$, what wavelength do you obtain?

- $365\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $410\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $821\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $3.28\,\mathrm{\mu m}$

## Explanation

The Balmer series ends at $n_f=2$. The difference between the reciprocal squares is $\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{36}=\dfrac{2}{9}$, so $\lambda=\dfrac{9}{2R_H}=4.10\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$.
