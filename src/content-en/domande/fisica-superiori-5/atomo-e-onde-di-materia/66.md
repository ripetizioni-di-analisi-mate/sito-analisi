A particle has de Broglie wavelength $9$ nm. What is its new wavelength in nanometers if its momentum increases by a factor of $4$?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $9$

## Explanation

The de Broglie wavelength is Planck’s constant divided by the momentum. Multiplying the momentum by a factor therefore divides the wavelength by the same factor, giving $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
