A student writes $|\psi(x)|^2=2\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$ in a small region and objects that the value exceeds one. How can a normalization problem actually be checked?

- By adding only the positions of the maxima
- By reversing the sign of the density there
- By integrating the density over all space
- By requiring every numerical density to be below one in every unit system

## Explanation

A one-dimensional density has units of inverse length. Its integral over space is a dimensionless probability and must equal one for a normalized state.
