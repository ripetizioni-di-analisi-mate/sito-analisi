The probability density is uniform at $3\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$ in a region $0.04\,\mathrm{nm}$ wide. In $500$ identical preparations, how many detections in this region are expected on average?

- $150$
- $375$
- $12$
- $60$

## Explanation

The probability of the region is its density times its width, or $3\cdot0.04=0.12$. The expected detection count is therefore $500\cdot0.12=60$.
