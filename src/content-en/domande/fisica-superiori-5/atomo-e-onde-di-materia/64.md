A particle has de Broglie wavelength $5$ nm. What is its new wavelength in nanometers if its momentum increases by a factor of $2$?

- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$

## Explanation

The de Broglie wavelength is Planck’s constant divided by the momentum. Multiplying the momentum by a factor therefore divides the wavelength by the same factor, giving $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
