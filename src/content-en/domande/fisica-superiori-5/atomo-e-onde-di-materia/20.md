In the Balmer formula, $y=\dfrac{1}{\lambda}$ is plotted against $x=\dfrac{1}{n_i^2}$, with $n_f=2$. What are the slope and intercept?

- $\dfrac{R_H}{4},\ 0$
- $-\dfrac{R_H}{4},\ R_H$
- $-R_H,\ \dfrac{R_H}{4}$
- $R_H,\ \dfrac{R_H}{4}$

## Explanation

Substituting $n_f=2$ into the Rydberg formula gives $y=\dfrac{R_H}{4}-R_Hx$. The slope is the coefficient of $x$, while the intercept is the value at $x=0$.
