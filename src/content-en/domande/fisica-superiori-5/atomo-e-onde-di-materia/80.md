In region A, the magnitude of the wave function is $3$ times its magnitude in region B. What is the ratio of the probability densities, A to B?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Probability density is the square of the magnitude of the wave function. Squaring the ratio of the magnitudes gives $9$.
