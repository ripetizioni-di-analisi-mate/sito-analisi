For identically prepared particles in a box, energy measurements give $E_1$ three quarters of the time and $4E_1$ otherwise. What average is observed over many measurements?

- $1.75E_1$
- $3.25E_1$
- $2.5E_1$
- $4E_1$

## Explanation

Each value contributes in proportion to how often it occurs. The mean is $0.75E_1+0.25(4E_1)=1.75E_1$, even though no individual measurement yields that value.
