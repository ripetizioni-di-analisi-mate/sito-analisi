Why would a classical electron in a circular planetary orbit lose energy even if its speed is constant?

- The centripetal force does constant positive work
- Its velocity changes direction and an accelerating charge radiates
- The nucleus absorbs electric charge on every orbit
- The electron mass increases on each orbit

## Explanation

Velocity changes direction in circular motion, producing centripetal acceleration. Classical electromagnetism predicts radiation, which carries energy away from the orbit.
