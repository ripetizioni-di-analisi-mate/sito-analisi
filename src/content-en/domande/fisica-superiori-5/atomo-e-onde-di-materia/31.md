In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=8$?

- $-1.7\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.2125\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-0.2125\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-870.4\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=8$ in the given formula gives $E_{8}=-\dfrac{13.6}{64}=- \dfrac{17}{80}$ electronvolts.
