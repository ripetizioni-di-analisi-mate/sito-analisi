All Paschen lines end at $n_f=3$. Given $R_H=1.097\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, in which region is their series limit as the initial level increases without bound?

- Near infrared, about $820\,\mathrm{nm}$
- Ultraviolet, about $91\,\mathrm{nm}$
- Violet, about $410\,\mathrm{nm}$
- Infrared, about $7.4\,\mathrm{\mu m}$

## Explanation

In the Rydberg formula the term $\dfrac{1}{n_i^2}$ tends to zero. This leaves $\dfrac{1}{\lambda}=\dfrac{R_H}{9}$, so the limit is at $820\,\mathrm{nm}$, beyond visible red.
