A particle has momentum $8\cdot10^{-24}$ kg m/s. Taking $h=6.6\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, what is its de Broglie wavelength in nanometers?

- $0.0825\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $5.28\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.825\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.00825\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation is $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$. Substitution gives $\lambda=\dfrac{6.6}{8}\cdot10^{-10}$ meters. Converting to nanometers gives $\lambda=\dfrac{0.66}{8}=\dfrac{33}{400}$ nm.
