An electron at rest infinitely far from the proton defines zero energy. How should an atomic level at $-3.4\,\mathrm{eV}$ be interpreted?

- Its kinetic energy is negative
- The electron is already free with $3.4\,\mathrm{eV}$ of kinetic energy
- The state is bound and reaching the threshold requires $3.4\,\mathrm{eV}$
- The photon occupying it has negative energy

## Explanation

The total energy is below that of the free electron at rest. The negative value thus measures binding relative to the chosen zero, while the kinetic energy remains positive.
