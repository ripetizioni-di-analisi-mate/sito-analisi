A hydrogen atom in its ground state absorbs a photon of $15$ eV. The ionization threshold is $13.6$ eV. Neglecting recoil, what happens?

- The atom is ionized and the electron escapes with zero kinetic energy.
- The atom moves to a bound level.
- The freed electron has $15$ eV of kinetic energy.
- The atom is ionized and the electron has $1.4$ eV of kinetic energy.

## Explanation

The photon energy exceeds the threshold. The excess becomes kinetic energy of the freed electron.
