A ground-state hydrogen atom absorbs a $15.0\,\mathrm{eV}$ photon. Its ionization threshold is $13.6\,\mathrm{eV}$. Neglecting recoil, with what kinetic energy does the electron emerge?

- $15.0\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $13.6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $28.6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Part of the photon energy separates the electron from the proton. The remaining energy is kinetic and equals $15.0-13.6=1.4\,\mathrm{eV}$.
