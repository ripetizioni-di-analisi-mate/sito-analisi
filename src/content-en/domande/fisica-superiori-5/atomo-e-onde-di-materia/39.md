A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{9}$?

- $18\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $81\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $162\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{2}{81}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=9$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{9}=81\cdot2=162$ electronvolts.
