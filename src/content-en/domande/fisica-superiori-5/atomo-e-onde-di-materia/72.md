From $x=0$ to $x=2\,\mathrm{nm}$ the density graph rises linearly from zero to a height $H$. The density vanishes outside this interval. What value of $H$ normalizes the distribution?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$
- $1\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$
- $4\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$
- $2\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The area under the triangular graph is half its base times its height. Setting $\dfrac{(2\,\mathrm{nm})H}{2}=1$ gives $H=1\,\mathrm{nm^{-1}}$.
