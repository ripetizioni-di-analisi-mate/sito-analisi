A beam of nonrelativistic electrons has de Broglie wavelength $9$ nm. The accelerating voltage increases by a factor of $16$, with negligible initial speed. What is the new wavelength in nanometers?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $36$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy is proportional to accelerating voltage. Momentum grows as the square root of energy, and the wavelength decreases by the corresponding factor, giving $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
