Hydrogen has energy levels $E_n=-\dfrac{13.6}{n^2}$ eV. How much energy in eV is needed to ionize it from state $n=3$?

- $\dfrac{68}{45}$
- $- \dfrac{68}{45}$
- $\dfrac{612}{5}$
- $\dfrac{68}{15}$

## Explanation

At the ionization threshold, the energy increases from the bound-state value to zero. The required energy is therefore the magnitude of the initial energy, giving $\dfrac{68}{45}$.
