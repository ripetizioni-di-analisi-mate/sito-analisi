In a double-slit experiment electrons arrive one at a time. After many hours an interference pattern appears. What is recorded on the screen for each individual electron?

- The entire pattern in one event
- A continuous line joining the maxima
- One localized hit
- Two simultaneous impacts, one per slit

## Explanation

Each detection is localized. When the preparation is repeated, the distribution of many hits follows the probability density of the state passing through the two slits.
