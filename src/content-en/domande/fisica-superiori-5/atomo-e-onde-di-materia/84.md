The ground-state energy in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls is $9$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=4$, in eV?

- $36$
- $144$
- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

The energy levels in an infinitely deep well increase with the square of the quantum number. Multiplying the ground-state energy by that square gives $144$.
