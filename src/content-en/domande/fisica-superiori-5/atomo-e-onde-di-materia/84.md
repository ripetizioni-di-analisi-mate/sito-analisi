An electron in a box can absorb a photon of energy $8E_1$. Its initial level is $n=1$. Which level is energetically accessible according to $E_n=n^2E_1$, considering energy conservation alone?

- $n=8$
- $n=9$
- $n=3$
- $n=2$

## Explanation

The required final energy is $E_1+8E_1=9E_1$. This is the $n=3$ level. Only the energy condition is being tested here.
