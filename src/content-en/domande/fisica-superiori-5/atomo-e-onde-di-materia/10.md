Electrons with wavelength $0.123\,\mathrm{nm}$ strike a grating of spacing $0.215\,\mathrm{nm}$ at normal incidence. Given $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$, at what angle from the normal is the first side maximum?

- $70^\circ$
- $35^\circ$
- $55^\circ$
- $17^\circ$

## Explanation

The first side maximum corresponds to $m=1$. Thus $\sin\theta=\dfrac{0.123}{0.215}\simeq0.572$, which gives $\theta\simeq35^\circ$.
