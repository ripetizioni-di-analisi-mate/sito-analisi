In the Bohr model, the ground-state orbital radius is $a_0$. What is the radius for level $n=2$ in units of $a_0$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The allowed radii are proportional to the square of the principal quantum number. Squaring the given number gives $4$.
