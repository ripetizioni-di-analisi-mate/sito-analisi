A photon and a free electron have the same de Broglie wavelength in vacuum. Which quantity must be equal?

- Rest mass
- Kinetic energy
- Speed
- Momentum magnitude

## Explanation

Both obey $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$, so equal wavelengths imply equal momentum magnitudes. Energy also depends on the energy–momentum relation, which differs for a photon and an electron.
