In the hydrogen transition from $n=5$ to $n=3$, the level energies are $-0.544$ and $-1.511\,\mathrm{eV}$. What is the wavelength of the emitted photon? Use $hc=1240\,\mathrm{eV\,nm}$.

- $0.603\,\mathrm{\mu m}$
- $0.821\,\mathrm{\mu m}$
- $1.28\,\mathrm{\mu m}$
- $2.28\,\mathrm{\mu m}$

## Explanation

The atom loses $(-0.544)-(-1.511)=0.967\,\mathrm{eV}$. This is the photon energy, giving $\lambda=\dfrac{1240}{0.967}\,\mathrm{nm}\simeq1282\,\mathrm{nm}$.
