Three preparations are proposed with $\Delta x\,\Delta p_x$ equal to $0.30\hbar$, $0.50\hbar$, and $0.90\hbar$. How many are compatible with the uncertainty principle?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The product of the standard deviations must be at least $\dfrac{\hbar}{2}$. The value $0.50\hbar$ reaches the bound, $0.90\hbar$ exceeds it, and $0.30\hbar$ is too small.
