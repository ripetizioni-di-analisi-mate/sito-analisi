A hydrogen atom goes from $n=5$ to $n=1$. What is the photon energy in units of $|E_1|$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{24}{25}$
- $\dfrac{26}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$

## Explanation

The photon energy is the initial energy minus the final energy. The energy levels are negative and vary inversely with the square of the quantum number, giving $\dfrac{24}{25}$.
