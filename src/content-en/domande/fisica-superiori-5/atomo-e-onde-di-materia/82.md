The ground-state energy in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls is $5$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=2$, in eV?

- $20$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

The energy levels in an infinitely deep well increase with the square of the quantum number. Multiplying the ground-state energy by that square gives $20$.
