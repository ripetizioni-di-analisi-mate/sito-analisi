A detector counts red photons at $660\,\mathrm{nm}$ and blue photons at $440\,\mathrm{nm}$. If the two lines carry the same total energy, how many red photons are needed for every two blue photons?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Photon energy is inversely proportional to wavelength. Each blue photon carries $\dfrac{660}{440}=1.5$ times the energy of a red photon, so two blue photons have the energy of three red photons.
