A particle encounters a finite rectangular barrier with $E<U$. The height is held fixed and only the width is increased. What happens to the transmission probability?

- It becomes exactly one for every finite width
- It decreases
- It always increases in proportion to width
- It is independent of width

## Explanation

The amplitude is attenuated in the classically forbidden region. For a wider barrier a smaller amplitude reaches the second interface, so transmission decreases.
