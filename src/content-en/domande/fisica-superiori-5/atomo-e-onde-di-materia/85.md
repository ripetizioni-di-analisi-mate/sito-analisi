Two infinite wells contain particles of the same mass at the same level $n$. The second well is three times wider. If the energy in the first is $9\,\mathrm{eV}$, what is it in the second?

- $27\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $3\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $81\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

At fixed mass and level, $E_n=\dfrac{n^2h^2}{8mL^2}$ is inversely proportional to $L^2$. Tripling the width divides the energy by nine.
