To resolve features of about $0.10\,\mathrm{nm}$, a beam with wavelength no greater than that scale is sought. Which beam meets the wavelength requirement alone?

- Electrons of wavelength $5\,\mathrm{nm}$
- Green light of wavelength $500\,\mathrm{nm}$
- Electrons of wavelength $0.05\,\mathrm{nm}$
- Infrared radiation of wavelength $2\,\mathrm{\mu m}$

## Explanation

Compare each wavelength with the target separation. Only $0.05\,\mathrm{nm}$ is shorter than $0.10\,\mathrm{nm}$. In practice the quality of the lenses also affects resolution.
