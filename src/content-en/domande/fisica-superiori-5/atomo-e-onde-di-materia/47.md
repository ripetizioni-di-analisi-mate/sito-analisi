In the Bohr model, the ground-state orbital radius is $a_0$. What is the radius for level $n=3$ in units of $a_0$?

- $9$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The allowed radii are proportional to the square of the principal quantum number. Squaring the given number gives $9$.
