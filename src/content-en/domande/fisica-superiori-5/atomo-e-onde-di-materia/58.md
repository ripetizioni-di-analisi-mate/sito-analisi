A beam passes through a slit made increasingly narrow in the horizontal $x$ direction. Which momentum component enters the Heisenberg bound directly associated with this localization?

- Only $p_y$
- $p_x$
- Only $p_z$
- The product $p_yp_z$

## Explanation

The localization is in $x$, so the conjugate pair is $(x,p_x)$. The relation $\Delta x\,\Delta p_x\geq\dfrac{\hbar}{2}$ concerns the transverse spread in that same direction.
