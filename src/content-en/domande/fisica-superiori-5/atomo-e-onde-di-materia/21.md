An atom has four levels with energies $0$, $1$, $4$, and $10\,\mathrm{eV}$. All are populated in the sample and every downward transition is allowed. How many different emission energies are possible?

- $6$
- $4$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

Each pair of levels contributes one downward transition. The six differences are $1$, $3$, $4$, $6$, $9$, and $10\,\mathrm{eV}$, all distinct.
