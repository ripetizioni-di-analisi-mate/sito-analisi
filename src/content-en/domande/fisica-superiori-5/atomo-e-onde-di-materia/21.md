In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=6$?

- $- \dfrac{17}{45}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $- \dfrac{34}{15}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{17}{45}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-489.6\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=6$ in the given formula gives $E_{6}=-\dfrac{13.6}{36}=- \dfrac{17}{45}$ electronvolts.
