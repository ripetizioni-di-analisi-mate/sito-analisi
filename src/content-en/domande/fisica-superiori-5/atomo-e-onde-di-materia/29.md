A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{7}$?

- $98\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $14\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $49\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{2}{49}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=7$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{7}=49\cdot2=98$ electronvolts.
