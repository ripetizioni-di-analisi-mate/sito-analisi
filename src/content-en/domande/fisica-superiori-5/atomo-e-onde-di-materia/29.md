Scattering data agree with a point charge down to the smallest distance probed, $30\,\mathrm{fm}$. What do the data imply about the size of the charge distribution?

- A size below about $30\,\mathrm{fm}$ is consistent with the data
- The diameter must be exactly $60\,\mathrm{fm}$
- The distribution fills the entire atom
- The radius is exactly $30\,\mathrm{fm}$

## Explanation

The particles have probed the field down to that distance. As long as it looks pointlike the data constrain the resolvable size without yielding an exact radius.
