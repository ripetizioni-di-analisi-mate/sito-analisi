Two alpha particles of equal energy pass the same heavy nucleus. One approaches on a line with a larger lateral offset from the nuclear centre. Which particle generally undergoes the smaller deflection?

- The one with the larger offset
- The one with the smaller offset
- Both reverse direction
- Both turn through the same angle

## Explanation

The initial lateral offset is the impact parameter. A larger one keeps the particle farther from the nucleus, reducing its deflection by Coulomb repulsion.
