Hydrogen levels obey $E_n=-\dfrac{E_0}{n^2}$. At large $n$, how do the energy gaps between consecutive levels behave?

- They alternate in sign as $n$ increases
- They get smaller and smaller towards the threshold
- They grow without bound
- They remain equal to $E_0$

## Explanation

As $n$ increases, the levels approach zero from below. The difference $E_0(\dfrac{1}{n^2}-\dfrac{1}{(n+1)^2})$ is positive and tends to zero.
