A particle has momentum $9\cdot10^{-24}$ kg m/s. Taking $h=6.6\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, what is its de Broglie wavelength in nanometers?

- $5.94\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $\dfrac{11}{150}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $\dfrac{11}{15}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $\dfrac{11}{1500}\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation is $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$. Substitution gives $\lambda=\dfrac{6.6}{9}\cdot10^{-10}$ meters. Converting to nanometers gives $\lambda=\dfrac{0.66}{9}=\dfrac{11}{150}$ nm.
