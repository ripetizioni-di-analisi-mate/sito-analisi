In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=9$?

- $- \dfrac{68}{45}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{68}{405}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-1101.6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $- \dfrac{68}{405}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=9$ in the given formula gives $E_{9}=-\dfrac{13.6}{81}=- \dfrac{68}{405}$ electronvolts.
