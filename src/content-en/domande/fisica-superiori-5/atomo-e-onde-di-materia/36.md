To which of the following systems does the one-electron Bohr model apply directly? The atomic numbers of helium and lithium are $2$ and $3$.

- $\mathrm{H}^{-}$
- $\mathrm{He}^{+}$
- $\mathrm{Li}^{+}$
- $\mathrm{He}$

## Explanation

Neutral helium has two electrons. Removing one leaves a single electron in the nuclear field, whereas each of the other systems listed still has two electrons.
