A hydrogen atom is already in the $n=3$ level at $-1.51\,\mathrm{eV}$. What is the lowest photon frequency that can free the electron? Use $h=4.14\cdot10^{-15}\,\mathrm{eV\,s}$.

- $6.25\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3.29\cdot10^{15}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3.65\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $8.22\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The threshold corresponds to a free electron with zero kinetic energy. Starting from this level requires $1.51\,\mathrm{eV}$, so $f_{\min}=\dfrac{1.51\,\mathrm{eV}}{h}=3.65\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
