A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{2}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $9\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $8\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=2$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{2}=4\cdot2=8$ electronvolts.
