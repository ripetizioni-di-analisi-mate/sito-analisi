Which sequence gives the first three gaps between adjacent levels of an infinite well with $E_n=n^2E_1$, in the order $1\to2$, $2\to3$, $3\to4$?

- $E_1,\ 2E_1,\ 3E_1$
- $4E_1,\ 9E_1,\ 16E_1$
- $3E_1,\ 3E_1,\ 3E_1$
- $3E_1,\ 5E_1,\ 7E_1$

## Explanation

Subtract consecutive squares to obtain $4-1$, $9-4$, and $16-9$. The gaps grow according to $E_{n+1}-E_n=(2n+1)E_1$.
