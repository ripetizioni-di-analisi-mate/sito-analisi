For slow electrons accelerated from rest, $\lambda=\dfrac{1.23\,\mathrm{nm}}{\sqrt{\dfrac{V}{1\,\mathrm{V}}}}$. What voltage setting gives $\lambda=0.050\,\mathrm{nm}$?

- $605\,\mathrm V$
- $2460\,\mathrm V$
- $121\,\mathrm V$
- $24.6\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{1.23}{0.050}=24.6$ gives the square root of the numerical voltage in volts. Squaring this result gives about $605\,\mathrm V$.
