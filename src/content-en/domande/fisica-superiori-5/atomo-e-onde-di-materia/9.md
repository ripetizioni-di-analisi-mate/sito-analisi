A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{3}$?

- $18\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $9\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=3$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{3}=9\cdot2=18$ electronvolts.
