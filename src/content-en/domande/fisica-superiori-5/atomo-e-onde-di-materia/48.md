Electrons of wavelength $0.12\,\mathrm{nm}$ strike a grating of spacing $d=0.30\,\mathrm{nm}$ at normal incidence. What is the highest integer diffraction order possible on either side? Use $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$.

- $3$
- $2$
- $1$
- $5$

## Explanation

The sine cannot exceed $1$, so $m\leq \dfrac{d}{\lambda}=2.5$. The largest allowed integer is $2$; the third order would require a sine greater than one.
