A source emits two lines at $500$ and $600\,\mathrm{nm}$. Their wavelengths change on entering glass. Which property of the light should be used directly in $\Delta E=h f$ to identify the same atomic transitions?

- The period squared
- The phase speed multiplied by $h$
- The wavelength in glass alone substituted for $f$
- Frequency, which stays the same at the interface

## Explanation

At a stationary interface light retains its frequency. Phase speed and wavelength change in glass, but photon energy is still tied to the same frequency.
