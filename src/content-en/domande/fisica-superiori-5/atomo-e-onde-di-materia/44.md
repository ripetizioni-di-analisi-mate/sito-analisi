A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{10}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $100\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $200\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=10$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{10}=100\cdot2=200$ electronvolts.
