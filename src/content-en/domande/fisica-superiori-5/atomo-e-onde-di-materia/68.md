Two nonrelativistic particles have the same kinetic energy. The second has $9$ times the mass of the first, whose de Broglie wavelength is $7$ nm. What is the wavelength of the second in nanometers?

- $63$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $21$

## Explanation

At fixed energy, momentum is proportional to the square root of mass. Wavelength is inversely proportional to the magnitude of the momentum, giving $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
