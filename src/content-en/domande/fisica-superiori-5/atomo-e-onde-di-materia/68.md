At one point the wave function is $-3i\,\mathrm{m^{-\frac{1}{2}}}$, where $i^2=-1$. What is the probability density there?

- $-9\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Density is $\psi^*\psi$. Complex conjugation reverses the sign of $i$, so the product $(3i)(-3i)$ equals $9$.
