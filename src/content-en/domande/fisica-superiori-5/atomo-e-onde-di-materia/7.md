In a Franck–Hertz experiment successive current dips are $6.7\,\mathrm V$ apart. If an excited atom returns directly to its initial state, what wavelength does it emit? Use $hc=1240\,\mathrm{eV\,nm}$.

- $8308\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $370\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $93\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $185\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

An electron accelerated through $6.7\,\mathrm V$ gains $6.7\,\mathrm{eV}$. The voltage spacing measures the energy transferred in an excitation collision. The returning atom therefore emits a photon with $\lambda=\dfrac{1240}{6.7}\,\mathrm{nm}\simeq185\,\mathrm{nm}$.
