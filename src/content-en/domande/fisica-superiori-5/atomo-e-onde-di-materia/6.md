In the Bohr model of hydrogen, $E_n=-13.6/n^2$ eV. What is the energy of level $n=3$?

- $- \dfrac{68}{15}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $- \dfrac{68}{45}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{68}{45}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-122.4\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The energy varies inversely with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=3$ in the given formula gives $E_{3}=-\dfrac{13.6}{9}=- \dfrac{68}{45}$ electronvolts.
