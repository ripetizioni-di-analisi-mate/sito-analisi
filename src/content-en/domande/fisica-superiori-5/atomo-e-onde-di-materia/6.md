A $7.7\,\mathrm{MeV}$ alpha particle approaches a gold nucleus whose recoil is neglected head-on. The potential energy is $U(r)=\dfrac{228\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}}{r}$, where $r$ is a length. At what distance does the motion reverse if the initial $U$ is negligible?

- $15\,\mathrm{fm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{fm}$
- $1.8\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{fm}$
- $59\,\mathrm{fm}$

## Explanation

At the turning point the speed is zero. All the initial energy is potential, so $r=\dfrac{228\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}}{7.7\,\mathrm{MeV}}\simeq30\,\mathrm{fm}$.
