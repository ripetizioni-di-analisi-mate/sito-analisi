For Bohr orbits $r_n=n^2a_0$ and $v_n=\dfrac{v_1}{n}$. How does the orbital period change when the index goes from $1$ to $2$?

- It increases by a factor of eight
- It doubles
- It quadruples
- It halves

## Explanation

The period is the ratio $\dfrac{2\pi r_n}{v_n}$. The circumference quadruples while the speed halves, so the time required increases by a factor of $8$.
