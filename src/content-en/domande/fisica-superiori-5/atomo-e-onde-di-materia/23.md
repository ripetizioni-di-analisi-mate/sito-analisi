Two stellar lines have wavelengths that are both $1\%$ longer than their laboratory wavelengths. Assuming radial motion causes the shift, what does this measurement indicate?

- The source is at rest relative to the observer
- Its motion is purely transverse in the classical limit
- The source is approaching
- The source is receding

## Explanation

Both lines show the same fractional redshift. For a radial Doppler shift, recession increases the received wavelength.
