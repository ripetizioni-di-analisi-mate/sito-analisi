A particle has momentum $6\cdot10^{-24}$ kg m/s. Taking $h=6.6\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, what is its de Broglie wavelength in nanometers?

- $3.96\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.11\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.011\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The de Broglie relation is $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$. Substitution gives $\lambda=\dfrac{6.6}{6}\cdot10^{-10}$ meters. Converting to nanometers gives $\lambda=\dfrac{0.66}{6}=\dfrac{11}{100}$ nm.
