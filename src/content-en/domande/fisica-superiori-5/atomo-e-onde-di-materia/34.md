A particle in a one-dimensional box with infinitely high walls obeys $E_n=n^2E_1$. If $E_1=2$ eV, what is $E_{8}$?

- $16\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $128\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $64\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.03125\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Energy increases with the square of the quantum number. Substituting $n=8$ and $E_1=2$ eV gives $E_{8}=64\cdot2=128$ electronvolts.
