For a circular Coulomb orbit $U=-2K$. If the total energy is $-1.5\,\mathrm{eV}$, which pair $(K,U)$ is consistent with the orbit?

- $(3,-4.5)\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $(-1.5,0)\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $(0,-1.5)\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $(1.5,-3)\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Combining the two conditions gives $E=K+U=K-2K=-K$. Hence $K=1.5\,\mathrm{eV}$ and $U=-3\,\mathrm{eV}$.
