A particle has a de Broglie wavelength of $8$ nm. Its momentum drops to one-quarter of its initial value. What is its new wavelength in nanometers?

- $32$
- $8$
- $2$
- $16$

## Explanation

The wavelength is inversely proportional to the magnitude of the momentum. Reducing the momentum to one-quarter of its original value quadruples the wavelength, giving $32$.
