An atomic lamp emits a green line. More atoms are prepared in the same excited level without changing their energies. With the same observation conditions and negligible reabsorption, what change is expected?

- The line becomes stronger at the same frequency
- The frequency increases in proportion to the atom count
- Each photon carries more energy
- The line necessarily shifts into the infrared

## Explanation

More atoms available for the transition produce more photons. Their energy is fixed by the level spacing, which is unchanged here.
