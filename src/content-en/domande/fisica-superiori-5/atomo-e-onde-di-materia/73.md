For a particle, $\Delta x=2$ nm. What is the lower bound on $\Delta p$ in units of $\hbar/\mathrm{nm}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The uncertainty inequality is $\Delta x\,\Delta p\geq\hbar/2$. Dividing the lower bound on the product by the position uncertainty gives $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
