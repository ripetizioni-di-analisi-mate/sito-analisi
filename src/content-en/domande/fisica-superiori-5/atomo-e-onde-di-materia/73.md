The centre of a box is a node of a particle's wave function. A sensor covers a small interval of finite width around the centre, and the density is positive nearby. What can it record?

- All particles because the sensor is central
- No events for any sensor width
- Negative probability near the node
- Detections with positive probability on each preparation

## Explanation

The density vanishes at the central point. The sensor measures probability integrated over its whole interval, which also includes points with positive density.
