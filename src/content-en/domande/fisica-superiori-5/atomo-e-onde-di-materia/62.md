Two lines are emitted to the same stable final level. The initial state for the first line has a lifetime one hundred times shorter than the second. Other conditions being equal, which line has the greater natural frequency width?

- The first
- Both widths are always equal
- Both widths must be zero
- The second

## Explanation

A shorter lifetime gives a greater natural energy width. Since photon energy is $hf$, the first line also has the greater frequency width.
