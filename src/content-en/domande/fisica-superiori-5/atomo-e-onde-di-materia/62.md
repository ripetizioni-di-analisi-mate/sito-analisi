A beam of alpha particles passes through a gold foil, and a few particles are scattered backward. Which distribution of positive charge is consistent with this observation?

- The electrons occupy the nucleus and the positive charge is outside it.
- The positive charge is uniformly distributed throughout the atom.
- The atom contains only neutral particles.
- The positive charge is concentrated in a small nucleus.

## Explanation

A large deflection requires a strong interaction at short range. This is consistent with positive charge concentrated in a small region of the atom.
