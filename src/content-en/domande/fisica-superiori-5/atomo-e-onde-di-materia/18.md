Two nearby lines occur at $486.0$ and $486.6\,\mathrm{nm}$. A spectrometer resolves them when its resolving power $R$ is at least the required value $\dfrac{\lambda}{\Delta\lambda}$. Which is the smallest adequate value listed?

- $1000$
- $500$
- $700$
- $100$

## Explanation

The separation is $0.6\,\mathrm{nm}$ and the central wavelength is about $486\,\mathrm{nm}$. The ratio is about $810$, so only $1000$ among the choices resolves the pair.
