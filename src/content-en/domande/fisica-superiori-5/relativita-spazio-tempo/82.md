For two events, $\Delta t=5$ µs and $\Delta x=2c\,\mathrm{\mu s}$, with zero transverse separation. What is the proper time between the events in microseconds?

- $\sqrt{21}$
- $5$
- $\sqrt{29}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Use the invariant interval. In the frame in which the events occur at the same point, the proper time is the square root of $\Delta t^2-(\Delta x/c)^2$. The result is $\sqrt{21}$.
