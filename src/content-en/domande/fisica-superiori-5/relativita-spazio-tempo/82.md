When origins align, a craft moves at $0.6c$ and a nearby probe moves transversely in the craft frame at $0.5c$. For a launch with $u'_x=0$ we have $u_y=\dfrac{u'_y}{\gamma}$. What is the magnitude of the probe's Earth-frame velocity if $\gamma=1.25$?

- $0.1c$
- $1.1c$
- $0.5c$
- $\sqrt{0.52}\,c$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is the craft velocity $0.6c$. The transverse component is $\dfrac{0.5c}{1.25}=0.4c$. Combining these perpendicular components gives $c\sqrt{0.6^2+0.4^2}$.
