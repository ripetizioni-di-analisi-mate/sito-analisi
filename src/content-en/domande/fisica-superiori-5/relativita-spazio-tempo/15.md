Two events occur at the same place in an inertial frame. Which clock measures the proper time between them?

- A clock at rest at the location of both events.
- A clock moving relative to that location.
- Every inertial clock measures the same interval.
- Two moving clocks located far apart.

## Explanation

Proper time is measured by a single clock present at both events. A clock at rest where both events occur meets this condition.
