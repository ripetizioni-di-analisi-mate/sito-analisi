In a spacetime diagram, two events have $c\Delta t=20$ m and $\Delta x=12$ m, with $\Delta y=\Delta z=0$. What is the invariant interval $s^2=(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$?

- $544\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $64\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $256\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the squares of the temporal and spatial separations separately. This gives $s^2=(20)^2-(12)^2$. Thus $s^2=256$ square meters, and the interval is timelike.
