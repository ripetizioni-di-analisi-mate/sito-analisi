A clock moves at $0.6c$ relative to a laboratory. If the clock measures a proper time interval of $8$ s, what time interval does the laboratory measure between the same events?

- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The Lorentz factor is $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.6^2}}=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Time dilation gives $\Delta t=\gamma\Delta\tau$, so the laboratory measures $\Delta t=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot8=10$ seconds.
