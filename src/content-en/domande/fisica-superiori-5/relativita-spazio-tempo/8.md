Two protons travel at $+0.70c$ and $-0.70c$ in the laboratory. What is the speed of the second measured in the first proton's frame?

- $0.49c$
- $1.40c$
- $0.70c$
- $0.94c$

## Explanation

On transforming to the first proton's frame the numerator is $-0.70c-0.70c$ and the denominator is $1-(-0.70)(0.70)=1.49$. The sign gives the direction, so the speed is $\dfrac{1.40c}{1.49}$.
