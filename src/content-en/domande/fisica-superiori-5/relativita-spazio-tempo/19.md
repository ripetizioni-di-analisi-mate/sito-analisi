A vehicle travels at $\dfrac{2c}{5}$ relative to Earth. It launches a probe backward at the same speed relative to the vehicle. What is the probe’s velocity relative to Earth, in units of $c$?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{5}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The two velocities have opposite signs and the same magnitude. The numerator in the relativistic formula is zero, while the denominator remains positive. The result is $0$.
