A rod with proper length $50$ m moves parallel to its length at $0.6c$. What length does the laboratory measure when the positions of the two ends are recorded simultaneously?

- $62.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For $v=0.6c$, we have $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Length contraction gives $L=\dfrac{L_0}{\gamma}$, so the measured length is $L=50\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=40$ meters.
