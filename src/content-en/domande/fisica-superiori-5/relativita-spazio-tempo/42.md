A spacecraft carries a transparent block. In its frame a pulse crosses the block at $0.5c$. The craft travels in the same direction at $0.5c$ relative to Earth. What is the speed of the pulse relative to Earth?

- $0.5c$
- $c$
- $0.8c$
- $0.25c$

## Explanation

In the frame of the craft the pulse has speed $u'=0.5c$, and the craft moves relative to Earth at $v=0.5c$ in the same direction. Since both speeds are comparable with $c$, the speed of the pulse relative to Earth is found with the relativistic velocity addition, so
$$
u=\frac{u'+v}{1+\dfrac{u'v}{c^2}}=\frac{0.5c+0.5c}{1+0.25}=0.8c.
$$
The value $c$ comes from the Galilean addition $u=u'+v$, which holds only for speeds small compared with $c$, and the value $0.5c$ ignores the motion of the craft.
