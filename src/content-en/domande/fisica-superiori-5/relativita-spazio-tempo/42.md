Two stationary laboratory sensors are one light-second apart. A probe passes them in succession. The second sensor's clock consistently reads $0.4\,\mathrm s$ behind laboratory time. Its recorded time minus the first reading is $0.8\,\mathrm s$, and the first clock is synchronised. What is the probe's speed?

- $\dfrac54c$
- $\dfrac25c$
- $\dfrac56c$
- $\dfrac65c$

## Explanation

The second clock's lag must be added back, giving a laboratory travel time of $0.8+0.4=1.2\,\mathrm s$. One light-second divided by this duration gives $\dfrac{1}{1.2}c=\dfrac56c$.
