A spacecraft carries a transparent block. In its frame a pulse crosses the block at $0.5c$. The craft travels in the same direction at $0.5c$ relative to Earth. What is the Earth-frame speed of the pulse?

- $0.5c$
- $c$
- $0.8c$
- $0.25c$

## Explanation

In the material the signal speed is below $c$, so use relativistic velocity addition. This gives $\dfrac{(0.5+0.5)c}{1+0.25}=0.8c$.
