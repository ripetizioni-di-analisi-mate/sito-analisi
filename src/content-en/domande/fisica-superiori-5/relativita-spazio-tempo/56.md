An inertial station moves with constant velocity. Its radar transmits at $t=4\,\mathrm s$ and receives the reflection at $t=10\,\mathrm s$ on the same clock. What radar distance is assigned to the reflection event?

- $7$ light-seconds
- $14$ light-seconds
- $6$ light-seconds
- $3$ light-seconds

## Explanation

The round-trip time is $6\,\mathrm s$. Radar distance is half the round-trip time times the speed of light, giving three light-seconds.
