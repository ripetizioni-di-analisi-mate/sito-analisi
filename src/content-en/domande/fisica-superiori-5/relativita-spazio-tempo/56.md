A laboratory measures $21$ µs between the production and decay of a particle. The Lorentz factor is $3$. What is the proper time interval in microseconds?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $21$
- $7$
- $63$

## Explanation

The two events occur at the same point in the particle’s frame. Its proper time interval is the laboratory interval divided by the Lorentz factor. This gives $7$.
