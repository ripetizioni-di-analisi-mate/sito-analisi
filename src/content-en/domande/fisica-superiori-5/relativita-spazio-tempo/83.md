Two time zones assign different clock labels to the same comet crossing. Does that difference demonstrate relativity of simultaneity?

- No, every event is absolutely simultaneous with all others.
- No, it is a different conventional offset for one event.
- Yes, the reading difference directly measures the Lorentz factor.
- Yes, every numerical difference is a Lorentz transformation.

## Explanation

A uniform offset of the time origin leaves time differences unchanged. Relative simultaneity concerns pairs of separated events compared in relatively moving frames.
