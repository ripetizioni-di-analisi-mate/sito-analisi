For two events, $\Delta t=7$ µs and $\Delta x=3c\,\mathrm{\mu s}$, with zero transverse separation. What is the proper time between the events in microseconds?

- $7$
- $2 \sqrt{10}$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{58}$

## Explanation

Use the invariant interval. In the frame in which the events occur at the same point, the proper time is the square root of $\Delta t^2-(\Delta x/c)^2$. The result is $2 \sqrt{10}$.
