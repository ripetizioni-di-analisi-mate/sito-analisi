On a spacetime diagram a line labelled $t'=3\,\mathrm s$ intersects several worldlines. What do these intersections provide?

- Positions at a single point of $S'$.
- Zero velocities for all bodies.
- Equal proper times on every body.
- The bodies' positions at a single instant of $S'$.

## Explanation

A constant-coordinate-time line is a simultaneity line of the frame. Each intersection selects the corresponding event on a different trajectory.
