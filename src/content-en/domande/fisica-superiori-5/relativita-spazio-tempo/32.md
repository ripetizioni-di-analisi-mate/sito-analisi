Two beams of identical particles traverse the same tube at different speeds. Their proper mean lifetime is $\tau$. What should be compared with $\tau$ to predict the surviving fraction?

- $\dfrac{\gamma L}{v}$
- $\dfrac{L}{\gamma v}$
- $\dfrac{L}{c}$
- $\dfrac{\gamma L}{c}$

## Explanation

The lab transit time is $\dfrac{L}{v}$. A clock moving with a particle measures that time divided by $\gamma$.
