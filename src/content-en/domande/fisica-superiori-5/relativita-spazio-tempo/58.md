A craft recedes at $0.6c$ with $\gamma=1.25$ and emits two flashes separated by $4\,\mathrm s$ on its own clock. What is their arrival-time separation on Earth?

- $8\,\mathrm s$
- $3.2\,\mathrm s$
- $5\,\mathrm s$
- $2\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The emissions are separated by $5\,\mathrm s$ in the Earth frame. The source recedes by three light-seconds during that interval, so the second flash takes an extra three seconds to arrive.
