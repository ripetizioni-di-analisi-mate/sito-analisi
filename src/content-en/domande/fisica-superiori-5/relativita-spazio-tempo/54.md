Two lab events have $x=0$ and $x=10$ light-seconds at $t=0$ and $t=6\,\mathrm s$ respectively. What velocity would a courier need to travel from the first to the second?

- $c$, possible for the courier.
- $\dfrac{3c}{5}$, possible.
- $\dfrac{5c}{3}$, impossible for the courier.
- $\dfrac{c}{3}$, possible.

## Explanation

Ten light-seconds in six seconds requires mean velocity $\dfrac{10c}{6}$. The separation is spacelike and no material courier can connect the events.
