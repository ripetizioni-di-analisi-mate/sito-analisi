A spacecraft travels at $3c/5$ relative to Earth and releases an object forward at $3c/5$ relative to itself. What is the object’s velocity relative to Earth, in units of $c$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{15}{8}$
- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{15}{17}$

## Explanation

Relativistic addition divides the sum of the velocities by one plus their product expressed in units of $c^2$. Both velocities point in the same direction. The result is $\dfrac{15}{17}$.
