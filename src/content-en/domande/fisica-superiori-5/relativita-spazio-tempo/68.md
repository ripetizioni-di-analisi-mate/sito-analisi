A probe emits a flash at A, a mirror reflects it at B and the probe receives it at C on its own clock. Both AB and BC are lightlike. What type of separation is AC?

- Spacelike.
- Timelike.
- Lightlike because the zero proper times add.
- Zero in both time and space.

## Explanation

A and C occur on the same probe at different proper times. The endpoint interval depends on the endpoints; it is not the sum of the squared intervals along the two different legs.
