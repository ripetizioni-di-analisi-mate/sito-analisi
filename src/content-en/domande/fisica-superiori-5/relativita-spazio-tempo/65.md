Two bodies travel along the same straight line in space. On the spacetime diagram A follows a straight worldline and B a curved one, both timelike. Which conclusion about B follows from the shape of the graph?

::grafico{f="x<0?NaN:2*x;x<0?NaN:2*x+0.48*x^2" xmin=-0.22 xmax=2.3 ymin=-0.4 ymax=6.2 nomi="x,ct" legenda="A;B" titolo="Worldlines of A and B in the same inertial frame. The spatial and time coordinates are both expressed in metres."}

- It is travelling at light speed.
- It always has constant acceleration.
- Its coordinate velocity varies in the plotted frame.
- Its rest mass must increase.

## Explanation

Velocity is $\dfrac{dx}{dt}$. A curved path changes slope and therefore velocity; an arbitrary curvature does not imply constant acceleration.
