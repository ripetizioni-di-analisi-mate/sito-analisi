Between meetings A and B one traveller follows the straight inertial path while another changes velocity on a detour. In flat spacetime which clock accumulates more proper time?

- The readings must agree.
- Reunited clocks cannot be compared.
- The one on the straight inertial path.
- The one that changes velocity more often.

## Explanation

The first traveller stays at rest in their own frame. On the second traveller's moving segments each $d\tau=dt\sqrt{1-\dfrac{v^2}{c^2}}$ is less than $dt$.
