On a Minkowski chart the lines $x'=0$ and $x'=2$ are parallel. What does the second represent when $x'$ is measured in light-seconds?

- A point at rest in the moving frame two light-seconds from its origin.
- A point at rest in the other frame for every boost speed.
- A single instantaneous event.
- An arbitrary light ray.

## Explanation

Each constant $x'$ identifies a fixed position in the moving frame. Varying $t'$ traces all events on its worldline.
