Two events are simultaneous on Earth, and the second is $7$ m farther along $+x$. In a frame moving along $+x$ at $3c/5$, which event happens first?

- Their order depends on the transverse distance.
- The second event.
- The first event.
- They remain simultaneous.

## Explanation

For events simultaneous in Earth’s frame, the transformed time difference is proportional to $-v\Delta x$. The time of the second event minus the time of the first is negative.
