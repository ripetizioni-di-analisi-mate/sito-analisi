In a spacetime diagram, two events have $c\Delta t=50$ m and $\Delta x=30$ m, with $\Delta y=\Delta z=0$. What is the invariant interval $s^2=(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$?

- $1600\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3400\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the squares of the temporal and spatial separations separately. This gives $s^2=(50)^2-(30)^2$. Thus $s^2=1600$ square meters, and the interval is timelike.
