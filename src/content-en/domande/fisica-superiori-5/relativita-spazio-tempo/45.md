A craft travels at $0.8c$. It overtakes a probe travelling in the same direction at $0.5c$ in Earth's frame. In which direction does the probe move as seen on board?

- Towards the rear.
- It remains stationary.
- Perpendicular to the flight path.
- Towards the front.

## Explanation

In $u'=\dfrac{u-v}{1-\dfrac{uv}{c^2}}$ the numerator is negative and the denominator is positive. The probe thus moves backwards in the craft frame.
