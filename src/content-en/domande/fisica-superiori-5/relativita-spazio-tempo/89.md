A body moves horizontally at constant velocity. A point on an $(x,ct)$ chart marks its encounter with a stationary buoy. Which additional datum determines the body's worldline?

- The body's name alone.
- The buoy's colour.
- The body's mass alone.
- The body's signed velocity in the plotted frame.

## Explanation

Uniform straight-line motion has a straight worldline. One point and the slope set by $v$ determine its equation, including the case $v=0$.
