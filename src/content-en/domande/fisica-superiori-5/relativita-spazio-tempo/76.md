Two flashes, one from B and one from A, reach a sensor in that order. The source positions are fixed and their distances from the sensor are known. How can the emission order in the sensor frame be recovered?

- Subtract the shorter travel time from both.
- Always add both distances and divide by $c$.
- Subtract the corresponding travel time $\dfrac{d}{c}$ from each reception time.
- Always keep the reception order.

## Explanation

In the chosen frame arrival time equals emission time plus travel time. The correction must use the distance travelled by each signal.
