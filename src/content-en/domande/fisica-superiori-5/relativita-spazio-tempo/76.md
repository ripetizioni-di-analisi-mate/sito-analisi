The separation of two events is measured as $c\Delta t=5$ m and $\Delta x=2$ m, with zero transverse separation. What is $(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$ in square meters?

- $9$
- $-21$
- $21$
- $29$

## Explanation

With the stated convention, the interval is the square of the temporal separation expressed in meters minus the square of the spatial separation. Subtracting the two squares gives $21$.
