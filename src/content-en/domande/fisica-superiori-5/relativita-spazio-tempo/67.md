Two observers classify events A and B as spacelike. One puts A earlier than B and the other later. Which measurement can they still agree on numerically?

- The first observer's velocity relative both to themself and to the second.
- $(\Delta x)^2-c^2(\Delta t)^2$ in one spatial dimension
- Signed time $\Delta t$.
- The absolute position of A.

## Explanation

For spacelike events the stated combination is the negative of the interval in the time-positive convention. It is invariant even when the sign of $\Delta t$ changes.
