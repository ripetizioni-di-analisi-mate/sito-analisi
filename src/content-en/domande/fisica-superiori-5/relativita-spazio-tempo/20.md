In a spacetime diagram, two events have $c\Delta t=25$ m and $\Delta x=15$ m, with $\Delta y=\Delta z=0$. What is the invariant interval $s^2=(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$?

- $850\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the squares of the temporal and spatial separations separately. This gives $s^2=(25)^2-(15)^2$. Thus $s^2=400$ square meters, and the interval is timelike.
