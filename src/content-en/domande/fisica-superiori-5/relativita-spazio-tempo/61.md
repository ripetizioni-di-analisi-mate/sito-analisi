A spacecraft is $5$ m wide in a direction perpendicular to its motion. Its Lorentz factor is $2$. What is its width in Earth’s frame, in meters?

- $7$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The Lorentz transformation leaves the transverse coordinates unchanged. Length contraction applies only to the dimension parallel to the motion. The result is $5$.
