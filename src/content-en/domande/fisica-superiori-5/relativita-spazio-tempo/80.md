During an infinitesimal lab time an accelerated clock has instantaneous velocity $v(t)$. Which expression is integrated to find its elapsed proper time?

- $\dfrac{dt}{\sqrt{1-\dfrac{v(t)^2}{c^2}}}$
- $(1-\dfrac{v(t)}{c})\,dt$
- $\sqrt{1-\dfrac{v(t)^2}{c^2}}\,dt$
- $\dfrac{v(t)\,dt}{c}$

## Explanation

On an infinitesimal segment the momentarily comoving frame gives $d\tau=\dfrac{dt}{\gamma(t)}$. Adding these contributions yields the clock reading.
