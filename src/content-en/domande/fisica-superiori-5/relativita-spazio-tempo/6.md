A beam of muons travels at $0.99c$ in a laboratory. Their mean rest lifetime is $2.2\,\mu\mathrm s$ and $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What mean distance do they travel in the laboratory before decay?

- $0.65\,\mathrm{km}$
- $0.093\,\mathrm{km}$
- $4.6\,\mathrm{km}$
- $46\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The laboratory lifetime is dilated by $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.99^2}}\simeq7.09$, giving about $15.6\,\mu\mathrm s$. Multiplying it by the muon speed gives about $4.6\,\mathrm{km}$.
