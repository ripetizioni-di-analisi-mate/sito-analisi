A clock moves at $0.6c$ relative to a laboratory. If the clock measures a proper time interval of $12$ s, what time interval does the laboratory measure between the same events?

- $9.6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The Lorentz factor is $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.6^2}}=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Time dilation gives $\Delta t=\gamma\Delta\tau$, so the laboratory measures $\Delta t=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot12=15$ seconds.
