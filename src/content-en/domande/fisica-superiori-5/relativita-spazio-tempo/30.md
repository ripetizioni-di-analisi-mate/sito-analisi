A light clock has two vertically separated mirrors. As seen from a lab moving horizontally relative to the clock, what path does the pulse take between successive reflections?

- The same vertical segment.
- A parabola caused solely by uniform horizontal motion.
- A horizontal segment with no vertical component.
- A diagonal segment longer than the vertical spacing.

## Explanation

As the light travels between the mirrors, the second mirror moves horizontally. Light takes the diagonal at $c$, so the travel time increases.
