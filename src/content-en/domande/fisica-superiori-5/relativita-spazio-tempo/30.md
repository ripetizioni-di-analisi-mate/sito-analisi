In a spacetime diagram, two events have $c\Delta t=35$ m and $\Delta x=21$ m, with $\Delta y=\Delta z=0$. What is the invariant interval $s^2=(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$?

- $1666\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $784\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $196\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the squares of the temporal and spatial separations separately. This gives $s^2=(35)^2-(21)^2$. Thus $s^2=784$ square meters, and the interval is timelike.
