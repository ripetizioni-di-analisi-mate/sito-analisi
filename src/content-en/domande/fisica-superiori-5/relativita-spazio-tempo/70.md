For an observer at velocity $v$, $\gamma(v)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-\dfrac{v^2}{c^2}}}$. A vehicle reverses direction with equal speed before and after the manoeuvre. How do the values of $\gamma$ compare on its two uniform legs?

- Both are equal and positive.
- The second equals one.
- The second is negative.
- They are reciprocals.

## Explanation

The formula contains $v^2$. Reversing velocity leaves unchanged the factor relating proper time to coordinate time.
