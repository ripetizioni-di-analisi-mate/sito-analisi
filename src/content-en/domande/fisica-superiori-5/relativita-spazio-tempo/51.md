A source on a vehicle traveling at $4/10$ of the speed of light emits a light pulse forward in a vacuum. What speed does an observer on the ground measure for the pulse?

- $\left(1+\dfrac{4}{10}\right)c$.
- $\left(1-\dfrac{4}{10}\right)c$.
- $c$.
- $\dfrac{4}{10}c$.

## Explanation

The second postulate gives the same speed of light in a vacuum for all inertial observers. The motion of the source does not alter the result.
