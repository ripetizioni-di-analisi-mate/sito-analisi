A camera photographs a bridge in relative motion using light reaching its lens at one instant. Why might the uncorrected image fail to give the contracted length?

- Light travels more slowly from the far end.
- The bridge stops contracting when lit.
- A length cannot be measured in space.
- Photons from the endpoints may have left at different times.

## Explanation

The definition uses endpoint positions at the same coordinate time. Simultaneous arrival at the lens does not guarantee simultaneous emission.
