A longitudinal Lorentz transformation multiplies the coordinates of a ray travelling along $+x$ by the same factor, $ct'=a\,ct$ and $x'=a\,x$. What does equality of those factors express?

- All distances are equal.
- All times remain identical.
- The light-ray slope remains $\dfrac{x'}{ct'}=1$.
- Its period stays the same in every frame.

## Explanation

Since $x=ct$ on the ray, multiplying both sides by the same factor preserves $x'=ct'$. The inferred speed remains $c$.
