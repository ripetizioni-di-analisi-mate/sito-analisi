A clock moves at $0.6c$ relative to a laboratory. If the clock measures a proper time interval of $28$ s, what time interval does the laboratory measure between the same events?

- $22.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $35\,\mathrm{s}$
- $28\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8.75\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The Lorentz factor is $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.6^2}}=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Time dilation gives $\Delta t=\gamma\Delta\tau$, so the laboratory measures $\Delta t=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot28=35$ seconds.
