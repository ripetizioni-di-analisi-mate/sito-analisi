Clock A sends a signal at $2\,\mu\mathrm s$ and receives its echo at $10\,\mu\mathrm s$. Clock B, at rest relative to A, reflects it without delay. What time should B display at reflection under Einstein synchronization?

- $4\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $6\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $10\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The outgoing and return distances are equal in the common rest frame. The reflection is assigned the midpoint $\dfrac{2+10}{2}=6\,\mu\mathrm s$.
