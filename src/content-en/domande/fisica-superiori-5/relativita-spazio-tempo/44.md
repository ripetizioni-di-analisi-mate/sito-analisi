A technician uses $\dfrac{u+v}{1+\dfrac{uv}{c^2}}$ with $u=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v=30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which number justifies the Galilean approximation?

- $\dfrac{u}{v}\simeq0.67$
- $u+v=50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on its own
- $\dfrac{uv}{c^2}\simeq7\cdot10^{-15}$
- $v-u=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on its own

## Explanation

The relative correction in the denominator is $\dfrac{uv}{c^2}$. With $c\simeq3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ it is negligible compared with one.
