The separation of two events is measured as $c\Delta t=9$ m and $\Delta x=4$ m, with zero transverse separation. What is $(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$ in square meters?

- $25$
- $-65$
- $97$
- $65$

## Explanation

With the stated convention, the interval is the square of the temporal separation expressed in meters minus the square of the spatial separation. Subtracting the two squares gives $65$.
