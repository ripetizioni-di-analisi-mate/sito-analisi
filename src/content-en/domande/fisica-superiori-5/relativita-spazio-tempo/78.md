A spacecraft travels at constant velocity relative to Earth, with $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{3}$. A single onboard detector records the arrivals of light pulses from two stars, $3\,\mathrm s$ apart on the detector's clock. What further information is needed to find the Earth-frame time between these same two arrivals?

- The craft's proper length.
- None: both events are recorded by the same spacecraft clock.
- The distances of both stars.
- The colours of the stars.

## Explanation

Both recordings occur at the same detector. The lab time between the recordings is $\gamma\Delta\tau=5\,\mathrm s$, independent of the light's earlier travel history.
