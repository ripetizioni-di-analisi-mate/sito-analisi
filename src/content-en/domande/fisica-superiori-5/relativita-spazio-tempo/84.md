Two flashes simultaneous in the lab are separated by $600\,\mathrm m$ along the $x$ axis. In another inertial frame moving along that axis, can the spatial separation of those same two events be less than $600\,\mathrm m$?

- No, because $\Delta x'^2=600^2+c^2\Delta t'^2$ in SI units.
- Yes, down to zero by treating them as timelike.
- Yes, because the endpoints must be stationary in every frame.
- Yes, multiply by $\dfrac{1}{\gamma}$.

## Explanation

The same event pair is tracked without replacing it by a new simultaneous pair. The spacelike invariant is $600^2\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the new time term is nonnegative.
