A spacecraft travels at $0.6c$ relative to Earth and launches a probe forward at $\dfrac{5}{12}c$ relative to itself. What is $u/c$ for the probe in Earth’s frame?

- $\dfrac{61}{60}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{61}{75}$
- $\dfrac{61}{45}$

## Explanation

For collinear velocities in the same direction, use $u=\dfrac{u^\prime+v}{1+u^\prime v/c^2}$. Dividing by $c$ and substituting gives $\dfrac{u}{c}=\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{12}+\dfrac{3}{5}}{1+\dfrac{5}{12}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}}$. The result is $\dfrac{61}{75}$, less than $1$.
