Two events have $c\Delta t=4$ m and a spatial separation of $9$ m. How is their interval classified?

- Spacelike.
- Lightlike.
- Timelike.
- Zero, because the two events are simultaneous.

## Explanation

The spatial separation exceeds the distance light travels in the given time. With the time-positive sign convention, the squared interval is negative.
