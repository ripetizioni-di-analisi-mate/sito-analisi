A lab and a particle record two events with $\Delta x=0$ in the lab and $\Delta x'\ne0$ in the particle frame. Which pair of events fits?

- One event at the coincident origins.
- One flash counted twice without delay.
- The creation and decay of the uniformly moving particle.
- Two ticks of one clock fixed in the lab.

## Explanation

The ticks share a lab coordinate. In the particle frame the lab clock moves between ticks.
