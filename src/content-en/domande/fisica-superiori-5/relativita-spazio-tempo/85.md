An astronaut receives news of a distant explosion while turning for the journey home. Which statement distinguishes reception from the time assigned to the explosion?

- The explosion physically moves when the frame changes.
- Reception is local; assigning an explosion time requires distance and a frame.
- Reception cancels light travel time.
- The turn causes the explosion.

## Explanation

Reception is an event on the astronaut's trajectory. The assigned time of the remote event depends on the frame and on correcting for propagation.
