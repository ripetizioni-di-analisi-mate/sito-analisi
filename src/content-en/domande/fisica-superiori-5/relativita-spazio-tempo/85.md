A traveler makes a round trip with a constant Lorentz factor $2$ on both legs. The total time on Earth is $10$ years, and the turnaround takes a negligible time. How many years does the traveler measure?

- $5$
- $20$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$

## Explanation

On each leg, the proper time is the Earth time divided by the Lorentz factor. The same factor applies to the sum of the times for the two legs. The result is $5$.
