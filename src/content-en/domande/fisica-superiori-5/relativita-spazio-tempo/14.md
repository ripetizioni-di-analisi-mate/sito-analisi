In frame $S$, two events have $\Delta t=20$ s and $\Delta x=0$. Frame $S^\prime$ moves at $0.6c$ along $x$. What is $\Delta t^\prime$?

- $16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The Lorentz transformation gives $\Delta t^\prime=\gamma(\Delta t-v\Delta x/c^2)$. Here $\Delta x=0$, leaving $\gamma\Delta t$. With $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$, this gives $\Delta t^\prime=25$ seconds.
