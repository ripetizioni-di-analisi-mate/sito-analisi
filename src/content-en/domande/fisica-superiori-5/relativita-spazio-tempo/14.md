A star lies $8.0$ light-years from Earth in their common inertial frame. One twin flies there at $0.80c$ and returns immediately at the same speed. Neglecting the turnaround time, how many years elapse for the Earth twin and the traveller, respectively?

- $20,\ 16$
- $12,\ 20$
- $10,\ 6$
- $20,\ 12$

## Explanation

In Earth's frame the journey covers $16$ light-years at $0.80c$, taking $20$ years. On each inertial leg the traveller's proper time is smaller by the factor $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{3}$, giving a total of $12$ years.
