A student defines $u_{\rm p}=\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta\tau}$ for uniform particle motion. At $v=0.8c$ and $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{3}$ the result is $u_{\rm p}=\dfrac{4c}{3}$. Which quantity exceeds $c$ in this calculation?

- Lab displacement per unit particle proper time.
- Signal speed relative to the particle.
- Velocity measured with synchronized lab clocks.
- The speed of light measured in the lab.

## Explanation

Since $\Delta t=\gamma\Delta\tau$, we have $\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta\tau}=\gamma v$. The causal speed limit applies to spatial displacement and coordinate time measured in a single frame.
