A spacecraft travels at $0.6c$ relative to Earth and launches a probe forward at $\dfrac{1}{4}c$ relative to itself. What is $u/c$ for the probe in Earth’s frame?

- $\dfrac{17}{23}$
- $\dfrac{17}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $1$

## Explanation

For collinear velocities in the same direction, use $u=\dfrac{u^\prime+v}{1+u^\prime v/c^2}$. Dividing by $c$ and substituting gives $\dfrac{u}{c}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{5}}{1+\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}}$. The result is $\dfrac{17}{23}$, less than $1$.
