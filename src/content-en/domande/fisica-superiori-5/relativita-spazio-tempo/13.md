Frame $S'$ moves relative to $S$ at velocity $v = 0.75\,c$. In a Minkowski diagram of $S$ drawn with the same scale on both axes, calculate the angle between the $ct'$-axis and the $ct$-axis.

- $45.0^\circ$
- $48.6^\circ$
- $53.1^\circ$
- $36.9^\circ$

## Explanation

The moving time axis follows the origin $x'=0$, whose equation in $S$ is $x=vt$. With equal axis scales, the angle measured from the vertical satisfies $\tan\alpha=\dfrac{x}{ct}=0.75$.
