A rod is at rest along $x'$ in a moving lab. Its ends are located at one Earth-frame time $t$. Can their Earth separation be multiplied by $\gamma$ to find the rest length even though $t'_1\ne t'_2$?

- No, because contraction affects time only.
- Yes, because all times are simultaneous.
- No, proper length is undefined.
- Yes, the endpoints' coordinates are constant in the rod frame.

## Explanation

Transforming simultaneous Earth events gives $\Delta x'=\gamma\Delta x$. Stationary endpoints keep their coordinates at every time in the rest frame.
