A clock moves at $0.6c$ relative to a laboratory. If the clock measures a proper time interval of $36$ s, what time interval does the laboratory measure between the same events?

- $28.8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{s}$
- $11.25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The Lorentz factor is $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.6^2}}=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Time dilation gives $\Delta t=\gamma\Delta\tau$, so the laboratory measures $\Delta t=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot36=45$ seconds.
