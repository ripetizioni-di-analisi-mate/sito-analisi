In its rest frame a tilted rod has components $4\,\mathrm m$ along the motion and $3\,\mathrm m$ perpendicular to it. In the lab $\gamma=2$. What is the simultaneously measured endpoint separation?

- $\sqrt{13}\,\mathrm m$
- $\sqrt{10}\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The longitudinal component becomes $2\,\mathrm m$ and the transverse component remains $3\,\mathrm m$. Pythagoras gives $\sqrt{2^2+3^2}\,\mathrm m$.
