On a Minkowski diagram with vertical $ct$, two events on the same $ct$ axis are connected by a vertical segment. Which quantity is unchanged by translating the coordinate origin?

- Their spacetime interval.
- The position coordinate of each event.
- The time coordinate of each event.
- The coordinates of the previous origin.

## Explanation

A translation adds the same constants to both events' coordinates. The differences used in the interval are therefore unchanged.
