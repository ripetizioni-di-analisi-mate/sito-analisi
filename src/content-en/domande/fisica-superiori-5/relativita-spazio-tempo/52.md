A subluminal signal connects emission A to reception B. Can an ordinary inertial-frame transformation make B precede A?

- No, the order of causally connected events is preserved.
- Yes, if the observer sees B first.
- Only if A and B have different spatial coordinates.
- Yes, choosing $\gamma>2$ suffices.

## Explanation

The signal obeys $\lvert\Delta x\rvert<c\Delta t$ with $\Delta t>0$. For any observer with $\lvert v\rvert<c$, the term $\Delta t-\dfrac{v\Delta x}{c^2}$ stays positive.
