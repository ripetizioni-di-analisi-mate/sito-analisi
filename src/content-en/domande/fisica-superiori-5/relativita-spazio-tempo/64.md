An object moves at $2/5$ of the speed of light. What is its Lorentz factor?

- $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{21}}{21}$
- $\dfrac{25}{21}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{21}}{5}$

## Explanation

Substitute the ratio of the speed to the speed of light into $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. Take the positive square root because the Lorentz factor is positive. The result is $\dfrac{5 \sqrt{21}}{21}$.
