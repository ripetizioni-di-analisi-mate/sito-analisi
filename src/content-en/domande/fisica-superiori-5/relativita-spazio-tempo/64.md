All future events at proper time $2\,\mathrm s$ from the origin along inertial paths through it are plotted. Which curve appears in the $(x,ct)$ plane?

- The future branch of the hyperbola $(ct)^2-x^2=(2c\,\mathrm s)^2$.
- The line $ct=x$.
- The circle $(ct)^2+x^2=(2c\,\mathrm s)^2$.
- The entire $x$ axis.

## Explanation

For each observer the interval equals $c^2\tau^2$. Fixing this difference of squares gives a hyperbola, and the future condition selects the branch with $t>0$.
