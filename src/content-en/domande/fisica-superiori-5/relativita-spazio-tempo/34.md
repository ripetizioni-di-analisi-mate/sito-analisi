An astronaut remains in one inertial frame after passing Earth. Can the astronaut meet inertial Earth again without changing trajectory or velocity in ordinary unbounded space, assuming nonzero relative velocity?

- No, two distinct straight worldlines intersect at most once.
- Yes, when both clocks display the same time.
- Yes, by the symmetry of time dilation.
- Yes, provided the speed is close to $c$.

## Explanation

After crossing, the two straight worldlines diverge. A second encounter requires changing at least one trajectory, as in the twin journey.
