A laboratory measures $10$ µs between the production and decay of a particle. The Lorentz factor is $2$. What is the proper time interval in microseconds?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$
- $5$
- $20$

## Explanation

The two events occur at the same point in the particle’s frame. Its proper time interval is the laboratory interval divided by the Lorentz factor. This gives $5$.
