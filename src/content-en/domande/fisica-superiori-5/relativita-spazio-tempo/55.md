A signal leaves the origin at $t=0$ and travels at $c$. A station initially $1$ light-second ahead recedes at $0.5c$. After what Earth-frame time does the signal reach it?

- $1\,\mathrm s$
- $1.5\,\mathrm s$
- $0.5\,\mathrm s$
- $2\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

In the Earth frame the positions are $ct$ and $1\,\text{ls}+0.5ct$. Setting them equal gives $0.5ct=1\,\text{ls}$, so $t=2\,\mathrm s$.
