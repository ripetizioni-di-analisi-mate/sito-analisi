A light signal connects two distinct events. To find a supposed rest frame for light one sets $\Delta x'=0$. What value of $v$ would be required in magnitude?

- $c$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{c}{\sqrt2}$
- $\dfrac{c}{2}$

## Explanation

Setting the separation to zero requires $v=\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$. Its magnitude on a light ray is $c$, which is not the speed of a material inertial frame.
