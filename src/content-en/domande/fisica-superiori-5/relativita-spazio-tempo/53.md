A spacecraft has Lorentz factor $3$. The same clock on board measures $7$ s between the emission of two signals. How much time elapses in Earth’s frame, in seconds?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $10$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

The signals are emitted at the same location on board, so the stated interval is the proper time. In Earth’s frame the interval is multiplied by the Lorentz factor. This gives $21$.
