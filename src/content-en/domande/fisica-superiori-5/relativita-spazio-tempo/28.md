Two probes cross at the coincident origins of two inertial frames, and one flash occurs at the crossing. Which fact holds in both frames?

- The probes have the same velocities in both.
- All distances measured by the two frames agree.
- The flash and the crossing occur at the same event.
- Every other simultaneous flash is simultaneous in both.

## Explanation

A local coincidence has $\Delta x=\Delta t=0$. Lorentz transformations map both zero differences to zero differences.
