A spacecraft travels at $0.6c$ relative to Earth and launches a probe forward at $\dfrac{1}{2}c$ relative to itself. What is $u/c$ for the probe in Earth’s frame?

- $\dfrac{11}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{11}{7}$
- $\dfrac{11}{13}$

## Explanation

For collinear velocities in the same direction, use $u=\dfrac{u^\prime+v}{1+u^\prime v/c^2}$. Dividing by $c$ and substituting gives $\dfrac{u}{c}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{3}{5}}{1+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}}$. The result is $\dfrac{11}{13}$, less than $1$.
