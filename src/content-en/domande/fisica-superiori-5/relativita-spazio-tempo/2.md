On board a spacecraft, the same loudspeaker starts emitting and stops $30\,\mathrm s$ later on its onboard clock. If the spacecraft travels at $0.60c$ relative to Earth, how far apart in time are the events in Earth's frame?

- $37.5\,\mathrm s$
- $50\,\mathrm s$
- $24\,\mathrm s$
- $30\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The start and end of emission occur at the same place in the spacecraft frame. The proper duration is $30\,\mathrm s$, so Earth measures $\gamma\Delta\tau=\dfrac{30}{\sqrt{1-0.60^2}}\,\mathrm s$.
