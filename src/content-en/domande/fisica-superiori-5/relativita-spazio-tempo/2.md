A rod with proper length $10$ m moves parallel to its length at $0.6c$. What length does the laboratory measure when the positions of the two ends are recorded simultaneously?

- $12.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For $v=0.6c$, we have $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Length contraction gives $L=\dfrac{L_0}{\gamma}$, so the measured length is $L=10\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=8$ meters.
