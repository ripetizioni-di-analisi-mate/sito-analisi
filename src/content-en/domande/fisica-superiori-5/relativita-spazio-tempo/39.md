A moving pole briefly fits inside a barn shorter than its proper length. In the barn frame both doors can close and reopen together for an instant without touching it. How are the two closures described in the pole frame?

- The barn expands to the pole's proper length.
- They occur at different times.
- Both occur at one place.
- The doors stop the pole without touching it.

## Explanation

The closures are separate events simultaneous in the barn frame. Changing frames along their separation introduces a time difference.
