A light signal travels in a vacuum from one event to another in $4$ µs. What is the spatial separation in units of $c\,\mathrm{\mu s}$?

- $2$
- $16$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

Light in a vacuum travels a distance equal to its speed $c$ times the time interval. In the requested units, this leaves the numerical value of the time. The result is $4$.
