A row of Earth clocks is synchronized in Earth's frame. A vehicle moves along $+x$. At one vehicle-frame instant, which Earth clock displays the later Earth time?

- The one farther back along $x$.
- All always show the same time in the vehicle frame.
- The one nearest the row's midpoint.
- The one farther along $+x$.

## Explanation

For simultaneous vehicle-frame events $\Delta t'=0$, so $\Delta t=\dfrac{v\Delta x}{c^2}$. If $\Delta x>0$ and $v>0$, the Earth-time difference is positive too.
