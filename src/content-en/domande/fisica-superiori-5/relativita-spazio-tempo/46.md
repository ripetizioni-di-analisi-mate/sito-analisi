An observer stays at the origin of $S'$. In $S$ the events $(t,x)=(1,2)$ and $(4,8)$ in seconds and metres lie on the observer's path. What is the velocity of $S'$ relative to $S$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The origin's velocity is the slope of its position–time graph, $\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{8-2}{4-1}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
