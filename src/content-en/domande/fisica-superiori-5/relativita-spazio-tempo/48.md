In $ct'=\gamma(ct-\beta x)$ the sign of the second term is accidentally reversed. Which test detects this when $S'$ moves along $+x$?

- Check only that $\gamma>0$.
- Count the terms.
- Check that events simultaneous in $S'$ satisfy $ct=\beta x$ when one is at the origin.
- Substitute only $x=t=0$.

## Explanation

The moving spatial axis has $t'=0$, hence $ct=\beta x$. The wrong sign tilts the axis in the opposite direction.
