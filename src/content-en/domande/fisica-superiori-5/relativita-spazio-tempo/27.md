Two people stand at the ends of a platform. To measure one-way light speed they divide the distance by the difference between the reception and emission readings. What preparation is essential?

- Choose very high-frequency light.
- Put both clocks at the reception point.
- Reduce their separation to zero.
- Synchronize both clocks in the platform frame.

## Explanation

Measurements at different positions compare separate clocks. Any offset between them would enter the measured travel time directly.
