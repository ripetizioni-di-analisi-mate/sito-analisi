An event occurs at $x'=0$ at $t'=4\,\mathrm s$. The lab sees the origin of $S'$ moving at $0.6c$ with $\gamma=1.25$; the origins coincided at time zero. What are the lab coordinates?

- $t=3.2\,\mathrm s,\ x=0$
- $t=5\,\mathrm s,\ x=3$ light-seconds
- $t=4\,\mathrm s,\ x=2.4$ light-seconds
- $t=5\,\mathrm s,\ x=-3$ light-seconds

## Explanation

The event is on the moving clock, so $t=\gamma t'=5\,\mathrm s$. The moving origin has then travelled $x=vt=3$ light-seconds.
