A train travels at $70$ m/s. A passenger walks at $3$ m/s toward the rear. In the Galilean limit, what is the passenger’s velocity relative to the ground, in meters per second, taking the train’s direction as positive?

- $73$
- $70$
- $67$
- $-67$

## Explanation

In the Galilean limit, add the signed velocities. The passenger’s velocity relative to the train is negative when the direction of travel is taken as positive. The result is $67$.
