A chart uses $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ per light-second horizontally and $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ per second vertically. What drawn slope does a rightward light ray have?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

In one second light advances one light-second. On this sheet that is two vertical centimetres for one horizontal centimetre.
