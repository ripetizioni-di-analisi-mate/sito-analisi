A spacecraft is $7$ m wide in a direction perpendicular to its motion. Its Lorentz factor is $3$. What is its width in Earth’s frame, in meters?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $7$
- $21$
- $10$

## Explanation

The Lorentz transformation leaves the transverse coordinates unchanged. Length contraction applies only to the dimension parallel to the motion. The result is $7$.
