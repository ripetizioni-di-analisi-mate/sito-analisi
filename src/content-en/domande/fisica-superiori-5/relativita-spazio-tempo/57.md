A laboratory measures $36$ µs between the production and decay of a particle. The Lorentz factor is $4$. What is the proper time interval in microseconds?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $36$
- $144$
- $9$

## Explanation

The two events occur at the same point in the particle’s frame. Its proper time interval is the laboratory interval divided by the Lorentz factor. This gives $9$.
