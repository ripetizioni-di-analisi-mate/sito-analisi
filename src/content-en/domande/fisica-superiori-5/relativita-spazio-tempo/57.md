A receding source emits one flash per second of its own clock. A stationary receiver wants to measure time dilation. Why is the arrival interval generally different from $\gamma$ seconds?

- The second flash travels more slowly.
- The receiver clock changes speed.
- The source frequency changes in its own frame.
- The second flash also has farther to travel.

## Explanation

Time dilation compares the emission times in the receiver frame. The changing distance travelled by the light contributes to arrival intervals as well.
