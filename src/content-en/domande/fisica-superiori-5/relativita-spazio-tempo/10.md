Two flashes A and B are simultaneous in the track frame, with B $600\,\mathrm m$ farther along $+x$. A rocket moves at $0.80c$ along $+x$. For $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is $t'_B-t'_A$ in the rocket frame?

- $-1.6\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $-2.7\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $0\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $+2.7\,\mu\mathrm s$

## Explanation

With $\Delta t=0$ the only remaining term is $\Delta t'=-\dfrac{\gamma v\Delta x}{c^2}$. The negative sign means B happens first in the rocket frame, and $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{3}$ gives the stated magnitude.
