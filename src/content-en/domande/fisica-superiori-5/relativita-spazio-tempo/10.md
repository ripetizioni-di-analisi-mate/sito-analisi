In a spacetime diagram, two events have $c\Delta t=15$ m and $\Delta x=9$ m, with $\Delta y=\Delta z=0$. What is the invariant interval $s^2=(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$?

- $306\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $144\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the squares of the temporal and spatial separations separately. This gives $s^2=(15)^2-(9)^2$. Thus $s^2=144$ square meters, and the interval is timelike.
