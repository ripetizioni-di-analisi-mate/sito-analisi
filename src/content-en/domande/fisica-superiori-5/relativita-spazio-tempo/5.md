A station simultaneously records the bow and stern of a ship moving uniformly along its length. It measures $45\,\mathrm m$ against a proper length of $75\,\mathrm m$. What is the relative speed?

- $0.64c$
- $0.60c$
- $0.40c$
- $0.80c$

## Explanation

Length contraction gives $\dfrac{L}{L_0}=\sqrt{1-\beta^2}=\dfrac{45}{75}=0.60$. Squaring and solving yields $\beta=\sqrt{1-0.60^2}=0.80$.
