In a spacetime diagram, two events have $c\Delta t=10$ m and $\Delta x=6$ m, with $\Delta y=\Delta z=0$. What is the invariant interval $s^2=(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$?

- $64\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $136\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the squares of the temporal and spatial separations separately. This gives $s^2=(10)^2-(6)^2$. Thus $s^2=64$ square meters, and the interval is timelike.
