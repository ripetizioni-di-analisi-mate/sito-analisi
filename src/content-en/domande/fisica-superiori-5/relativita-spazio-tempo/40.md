One end of a long rod is pushed. The other end is proposed as a way to announce the push before light could reach it. What limits this idea?

- The elastic disturbance travels at a finite speed below $c$.
- The far end moves before the push.
- Momentum is not conserved for rods.
- Simultaneity at the ends is absolute.

## Explanation

Neighbouring atoms pass the deformation along progressively. Perfect rigidity with instantaneous force transmission is incompatible with relativistic causality.
