In a spacetime diagram, two events have $c\Delta t=45$ m and $\Delta x=27$ m, with $\Delta y=\Delta z=0$. What is the invariant interval $s^2=(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$?

- $2754\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $324\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $1296\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the squares of the temporal and spatial separations separately. This gives $s^2=(45)^2-(27)^2$. Thus $s^2=1296$ square meters, and the interval is timelike.
