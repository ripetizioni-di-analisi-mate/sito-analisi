A moving clock advances one second while two synchronized laboratory clocks record two seconds between the same events. What relative speed gives this ratio?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,c}{2}$
- $\dfrac{c}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3c}{4}$
- $\dfrac{c}{4}$

## Explanation

The ratio of lab time to proper time is $\gamma=2$. From $1-\beta^2=\dfrac{1}{\gamma^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ it follows that $\beta=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
