In a spacecraft frame a flash travels vertically. The craft moves horizontally at $0.6c$ in the lab. If the flash has horizontal component $0.6c$ in the lab, what is its vertical component in magnitude?

- $c$
- $0.8c$
- $1.6c$
- $0.4c$

## Explanation

The lab-frame light speed remains $c$. The components are perpendicular, giving $u_y=c\sqrt{1-0.6^2}=0.8c$.
