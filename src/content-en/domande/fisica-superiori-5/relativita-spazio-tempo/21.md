A closed carriage moves at constant velocity. A ball is released from rest relative to the carriage. Where does it land in the carriage frame, ignoring air resistance?

- At a distance set by the speed relative to the stars.
- Ahead of the release point.
- Behind the release point.
- Directly below the release point.

## Explanation

The ball retains the same horizontal ground-frame velocity as the carriage. Its horizontal velocity in the carriage frame remains zero.
