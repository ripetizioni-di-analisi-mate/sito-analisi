A clock moves at $0.6c$ relative to a laboratory. If the clock measures a proper time interval of $24$ s, what time interval does the laboratory measure between the same events?

- $30\,\mathrm{s}$
- $19.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The Lorentz factor is $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.6^2}}=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Time dilation gives $\Delta t=\gamma\Delta\tau$, so the laboratory measures $\Delta t=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot24=30$ seconds.
