A radar assigns $ct=5$ and $x=3$ in metres to a future event. Which second pair $(ct',x')$ could describe it in another frame with the same origin?

- $(5,4)$
- $(0,4)$
- $(3,5)$
- $(4,0)$

## Explanation

The invariant is $25-9=16\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The pair with positive time and zero position has $4^2-0^2=16$ and describes the frame that passes through the event.
