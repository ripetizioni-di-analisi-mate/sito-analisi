A spacecraft is $9$ m wide in a direction perpendicular to its motion. Its Lorentz factor is $4$. What is its width in Earth’s frame, in meters?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $13$
- $9$
- $36$

## Explanation

The Lorentz transformation leaves the transverse coordinates unchanged. Length contraction applies only to the dimension parallel to the motion. The result is $9$.
