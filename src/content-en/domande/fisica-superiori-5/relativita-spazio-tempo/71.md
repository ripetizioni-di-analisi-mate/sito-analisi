A vehicle travels at $3c/5$ relative to Earth. It launches a probe backward at the same speed relative to the vehicle. What is the probe’s velocity relative to Earth, in units of $c$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $0$
- $- \dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

The two velocities have opposite signs and the same magnitude. The numerator in the relativistic formula is zero, while the denominator remains positive. The result is $0$.
