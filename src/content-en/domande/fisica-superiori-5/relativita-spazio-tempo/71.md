A craft has a transverse deck $6\,\mathrm m$ long in its own frame. In Earth's frame its ends meet two light signals at the same time and the same $x$ position. Under a boost along $x$ are the meetings still simultaneous?

- Only if the deck is much shorter.
- No, every pair of separated events loses simultaneity.
- Yes, because $\Delta x=0$ along the relative-motion direction.
- Only if the craft is at rest.

## Explanation

Only the separation parallel to the boost enters the transformation of $\Delta t$. Both it and $\Delta t$ vanish, so $\Delta t'$ vanishes too.
