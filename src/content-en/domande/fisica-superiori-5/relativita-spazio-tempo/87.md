A speed estimate divides a distance contracted in the craft frame by a duration from two Earth clocks. Why is the quotient generally neither an Earth-frame nor an onboard velocity?

- A craft cannot measure distances.
- Contraction applies only to light signals.
- Numerator and denominator are measured in different frames.
- All distance-to-time ratios are invariant.

## Explanation

A coordinate velocity compares two positions and corresponding times assigned in the same frame. Mixing frames does not meet that definition.
