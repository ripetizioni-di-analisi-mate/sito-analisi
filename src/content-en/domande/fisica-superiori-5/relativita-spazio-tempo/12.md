Two events have separations $\Delta x=900\,\mathrm m$ and $c\Delta t=1500\,\mathrm m$. How much time elapses on a clock moving uniformly from the first event to the second? Use $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $5.0\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $2.0\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $4.0\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $3.0\,\mu\mathrm s$

## Explanation

For a timelike interval $c\Delta\tau=\sqrt{1500^2-900^2}\,\mathrm m=1200\,\mathrm m$. Dividing by $c$ gives the proper time on the clock.
