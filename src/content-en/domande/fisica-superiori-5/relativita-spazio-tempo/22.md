A transparent lift accelerates upward. Which observation can distinguish this from uniform straight-line motion in the same gravitational field?

- A scale supporting a passenger stationary on the floor reads more than the passenger's gravitational weight.
- The cabin passes windows.
- An object attached to the cabin stays fixed relative to it.
- Light travels locally at $c$.

## Explanation

The floor force must balance gravity and supply acceleration, hence $N=m(g+a)$. In uniform motion the reading would be $mg$.
