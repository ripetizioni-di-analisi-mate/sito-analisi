Two pulses leave the ends of a stationary rod simultaneously towards its centre and meet in a single event. In a frame where the rod moves, is the meeting at the rod's midpoint at that time?

- Only if $v=0$.
- Yes, meeting the midpoint mark is a local coincidence.
- No, it must be at the rear end.
- No, the pulses no longer meet.

## Explanation

In the rest frame both pulses and the central mark share one point and time. A transformation changes that event's coordinates while preserving the three-way coincidence.
