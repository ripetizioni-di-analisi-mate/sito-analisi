On an equal-scale diagram a path runs from $x=0$, $ct=0$ to $x=8\,\mathrm m$ at $ct=4\,\mathrm m$. What could it represent?

- A message sent between the two points.
- One photon in vacuum.
- The intersection spot of two beams moving across a screen without carrying information between those points.
- An electron's worldline.

## Explanation

The apparent speed is $2c$. A sequence of geometric spots can move this way, whereas a material object or a causal signal cannot traverse that path.
