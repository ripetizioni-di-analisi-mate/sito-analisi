An object moves at $4/5$ of the speed of light. What is its Lorentz factor?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{25}{9}$
- $5$

## Explanation

Substitute the ratio of the speed to the speed of light into $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. Take the positive square root because the Lorentz factor is positive. The result is $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
