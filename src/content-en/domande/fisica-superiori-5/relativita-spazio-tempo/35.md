A clock stays on a lab bench for $3$ hours, then travels for $4$ lab hours with $\gamma=2$. How far behind the bench clock does it fall?

- $5$ hours
- $2$ hours
- $3$ hours
- $7$ hours

## Explanation

Both clocks accumulate the first three hours equally. During the four travelling hours the moving clock accumulates two, so it falls two hours behind.
