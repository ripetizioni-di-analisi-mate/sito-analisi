In a light clock the mirror spacing is $3\,\mathrm m$. In the lab the upper mirror moves $4\,\mathrm m$ during one upward transit. What is the lab-to-onboard transit-time ratio?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The light paths are the $5\,\mathrm m$ hypotenuse and the $3\,\mathrm m$ vertical leg. The speed is the same, so the time ratio is $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
