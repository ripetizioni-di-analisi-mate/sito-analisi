Two synchronized station clocks send their local times as a spacecraft passes them in uniform straight-line motion. Their readings are $5\,\mathrm s$ and $9\,\mathrm s$, while the onboard clock advances $3.2\,\mathrm s$. How far apart are the station clocks?

- $0.8$ light-seconds
- $3.2$ light-seconds
- $4$ light-seconds
- $2.4$ light-seconds

## Explanation

The station time between passages is $4\,\mathrm s$. The interval invariant gives $\dfrac{\Delta x}{c}=\sqrt{4^2-3.2^2}\,\mathrm s=2.4\,\mathrm s$.
