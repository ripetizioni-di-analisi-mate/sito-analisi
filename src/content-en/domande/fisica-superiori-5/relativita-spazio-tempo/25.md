Two flashes from stationary stars reach an astronomer together. One star is four light-years away and the other six light-years away. Which emission occurred first in the astronomer's frame?

- The farther star's emission, two years earlier.
- The farther star's emission, ten years earlier.
- They are simultaneous because their signals arrive together.
- The nearer star's emission, two years earlier.

## Explanation

Travel times are six years for the far star and four for the near one. Subtracting from the same reception time places the far emission two years earlier.
