In a spacetime diagram, two events have $c\Delta t=30$ m and $\Delta x=18$ m, with $\Delta y=\Delta z=0$. What is the invariant interval $s^2=(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$?

- $576\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $1224\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $144\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the squares of the temporal and spatial separations separately. This gives $s^2=(30)^2-(18)^2$. Thus $s^2=576$ square meters, and the interval is timelike.
