A rod has proper length $21$ m and moves parallel to its length with Lorentz factor $3$. What is its length in the laboratory, in meters?

- $21$
- $63$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $7$

## Explanation

The positions of the ends are recorded simultaneously in the laboratory. The length along the direction of motion is the proper length divided by the Lorentz factor. The result is $7$.
