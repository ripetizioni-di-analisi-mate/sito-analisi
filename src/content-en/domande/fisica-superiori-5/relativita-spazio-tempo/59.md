A ship emits a line of proper frequency $f_0$. It is measured at $2f_0$ on approach. At the same recession speed, the longitudinal Doppler factor is the reciprocal. What frequency is then measured?

- $\dfrac{f_0}{2}$
- $2f_0$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{f_0}{4}$

## Explanation

The approach factor is $2$. Its reciprocal on recession is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the received frequency is half the emitted frequency.
