Two spacecraft approach a station from opposite sides at $0.8c$ each. In the station frame, at what rate does the distance between them decrease?

- $0.4c$
- $0.976c$
- $0.8c$
- $1.6c$

## Explanation

The positions compared are simultaneous in the station frame. Each covers $0.8c\Delta t$ in the time interval. The closing rate is the sum, distinct from one ship's speed in the other's frame.
