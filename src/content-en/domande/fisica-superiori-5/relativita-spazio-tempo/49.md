An event has coordinates $ct=5\,\mathrm m$, $x=3\,\mathrm m$ and $y=4\,\mathrm m$ relative to a flash emitted at the origin, with $z=0$. Can the flash reach it?

- No, because all coordinates would have to coincide.
- Yes, it lies on the light cone.
- No, because $x+y>ct$.
- Only if light travels faster than $c$.

## Explanation

The spatial distance is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\,\mathrm m$. It equals $ct$, so the signal arrives exactly at the event.
