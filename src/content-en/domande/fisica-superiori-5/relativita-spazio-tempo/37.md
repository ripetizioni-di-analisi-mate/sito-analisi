A cube of proper side $a$ moves parallel to one edge with factor $\gamma$. What number density does the lab measure for beads fixed inside it if the onboard density is $n_0$?

- $\dfrac{n_0}{\gamma}$
- $\gamma n_0$
- $n_0$
- $\gamma^3 n_0$

## Explanation

Only the edge parallel to the motion contracts, so simultaneous volume is $\dfrac{a^3}{\gamma}$. The bead count is unchanged and the density grows by $\gamma$.
