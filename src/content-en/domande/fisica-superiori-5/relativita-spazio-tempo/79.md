Two identical probes pass a station with equal speeds in opposite directions. The station records different lengths using simultaneous endpoints. Which modelling assumption should be checked first?

- That light has different speeds in the two directions.
- That the leftward probe has negative proper time.
- That the positive axis points right.
- That they have the same orientation to motion and the same proper length.

## Explanation

For equal orientation, proper length and speed, the contraction is the same. The sign of $v$ does not affect $\gamma$.
