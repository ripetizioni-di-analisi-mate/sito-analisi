A pulse reflects from a receding mirror. Its frequency Doppler factor from lab to mirror is $0.8$ and the factor from mirror back to lab is again $0.8$. What fraction of the initial frequency returns to the lab?

- $1$
- $1.6$
- $0.64$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

Ideal reflection preserves frequency in the mirror frame. The two transformations therefore multiply the frequency by $0.8^2=0.64$.
