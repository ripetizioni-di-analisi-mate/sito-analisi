In frame $S$, two events have $\Delta t=15$ s and $\Delta x=0$. Frame $S^\prime$ moves at $0.6c$ along $x$. What is $\Delta t^\prime$?

- $18.75\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The Lorentz transformation gives $\Delta t^\prime=\gamma(\Delta t-v\Delta x/c^2)$. Here $\Delta x=0$, leaving $\gamma\Delta t$. With $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$, this gives $\Delta t^\prime=18.75$ seconds.
