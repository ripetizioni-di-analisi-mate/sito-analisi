An event has $x=1200\,\mathrm m$ and $t=5.0\,\mu\mathrm s$ in the laboratory. The two frame origins coincide at time zero, and the second frame moves along $+x$ at $0.60c$. What is the spatial coordinate of the event in that frame? Use $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $300\,\mathrm m$
- $240\,\mathrm m$
- $375\,\mathrm m$
- $2625\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The moving origin covers $vt=900\,\mathrm m$ in the laboratory time. The transformation also needs the Lorentz factor: $x'=\gamma(x-vt)=1.25(1200-900)\,\mathrm m$.
