A sample has proper mean lifetime $2\,\mu\mathrm s$. One particle decays after $5\,\mu\mathrm s$ in its rest frame. Does this contradict relativity?

- Yes, every particle must decay at exactly $2\,\mu\mathrm s$.
- No, the lifetime is a statistical mean.
- No, because $\gamma>1$ in the rest frame.
- Yes, a proper time cannot exceed the mean.

## Explanation

The proper-time distribution allows decays both before and after the mean. Time dilation compares the same events as observed in different frames.
