A rod of proper length $2.0\,\mathrm m$ travels along its axis at $0.60c$ through a laboratory. The laboratory records the positions of both ends simultaneously. How far apart are the recorded positions?

- $2.0\,\mathrm m$
- $1.6\,\mathrm m$
- $1.2\,\mathrm m$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

For simultaneous measurements in the laboratory, longitudinal contraction gives $L=L_0\sqrt{1-\dfrac{v^2}{c^2}}$. The multiplicative factor is $0.80$.
