On a constant-speed trip a navigator uses $\beta=\dfrac{3}{5}$. If the navigator incorrectly calculates $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{1-\beta}$, which comparison with the correct value reveals the error?

- The result is $2.5$ instead of $1.25$.
- Both expressions give $0.8$.
- Both expressions give $1$.
- The result is $1.25$ instead of $2.5$.

## Explanation

The correct expression is $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-\dfrac{9}{25}}}=\dfrac{5}{4}$. The other gives $\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{3}{5}}=\dfrac{5}{2}$ and overestimates time dilation.
