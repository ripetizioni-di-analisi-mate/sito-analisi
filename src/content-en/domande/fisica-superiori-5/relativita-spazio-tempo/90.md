A light pulse travels along $+x$ in a vacuum for $9$ µs. What is its squared interval $(c\Delta t)^2-(\Delta x)^2$ in square meters?

- $81$
- $162$
- $0$
- $-81$

## Explanation

For the pulse the distance is exactly $c\Delta t$. The two terms in the squared interval are equal and cancel. The result is $0$.
