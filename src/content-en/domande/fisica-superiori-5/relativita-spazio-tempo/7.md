A spacecraft moves away from Earth at speed $0.60\,c$. It launches a shuttle forward at speed $0.60\,c$ relative to the spacecraft. Calculate the shuttle’s speed relative to Earth.

- $0.60c$
- $0.36c$
- $0.88c$
- $1.20c$

## Explanation

The shuttle moves in the same direction as the ship. Relativistic addition gives $u=\dfrac{(0.60+0.60)c}{1+0.60^2}\simeq0.88c$. The denominator keeps the resulting speed below that of light.
