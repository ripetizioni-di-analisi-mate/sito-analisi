A rod with proper length $15$ m moves parallel to its length at $0.6c$. What length does the laboratory measure when the positions of the two ends are recorded simultaneously?

- $18.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For $v=0.6c$, we have $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Length contraction gives $L=\dfrac{L_0}{\gamma}$, so the measured length is $L=15\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=12$ meters.
