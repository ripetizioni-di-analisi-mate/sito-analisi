Two events have $\Delta x=1500\,\mathrm m$ and $\Delta t=4.0\,\mu\mathrm s$ in a laboratory. At what speed along $+x$ must an inertial observer move to judge them simultaneous? Use $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $0.40c$
- $0.60c$
- $0.80c$
- $1.25c$

## Explanation

Simultaneity requires $\Delta t'=\gamma(\Delta t-\dfrac{v\Delta x}{c^2})=0$. This gives $\dfrac{v}{c}=\dfrac{c\Delta t}{\Delta x}=\dfrac{1200}{1500}=0.80$. Such a physical frame exists because the separation is spacelike.
