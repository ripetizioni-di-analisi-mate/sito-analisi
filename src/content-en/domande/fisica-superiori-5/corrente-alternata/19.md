A series RLC circuit has $R=15$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=20$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $35\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $25\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $125\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(15)^2+(20)^2}=25$ ohms.
