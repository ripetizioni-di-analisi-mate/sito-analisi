A sinusoidal voltage has a peak value of $2\sqrt2$ V. What is its rms value?

- $2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $1\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For a sinusoid the rms value is the amplitude divided by $\sqrt2$. Calculating $V_\mathrm{eff}=\dfrac{2\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}$ gives an rms value of $2$ volts.
