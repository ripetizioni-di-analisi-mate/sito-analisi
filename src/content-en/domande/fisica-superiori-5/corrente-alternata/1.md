An 80-turn coil, each turn of area $0.030\,\mathrm{m^2}$, rotates at 25 revolutions per second about a diameter perpendicular to a uniform $0.10\,\mathrm T$ field. What is the peak emf?

- $38\,\mathrm V$
- $27\,\mathrm V$
- $6.0\,\mathrm V$
- $0.48\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Emf is the negative time derivative of flux linkage. The linkage amplitude is $NBA$ and differentiation introduces $\omega=2\pi f$, giving $\mathcal E_0=NBA\,2\pi f$.
