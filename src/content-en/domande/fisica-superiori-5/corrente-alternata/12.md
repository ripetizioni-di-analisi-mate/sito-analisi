An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=400$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $4000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.025\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $400\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $40\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=400\cdot0.1=40$ ohms.
