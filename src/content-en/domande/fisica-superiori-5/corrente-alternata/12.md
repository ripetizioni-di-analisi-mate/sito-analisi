A washing machine connected to the grid at $230\,\mathrm{V}$ draws an rms current of $3.0\,\mathrm{A}$ and has a power factor of $0.85$. Calculate the average power it draws.

- $587\,\mathrm W$
- $690\,\mathrm W$
- $499\,\mathrm W$
- $812\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The product of rms voltage and rms current is apparent power. Apply the power factor once to obtain the active power drawn by the washing machine. $P = V_{\text{eff}} I_{\text{eff}} \cos\varphi \approx 587\,\mathrm{W}$.
