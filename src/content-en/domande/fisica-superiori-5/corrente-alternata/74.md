A tuning circuit offers two capacitors, $C$ and $9C$. Starting with $9C$, which connection tunes to a station at three times the frequency without changing inductance?

- Use both in parallel
- Keep $9C$ alone
- Short the capacitor
- Use $C$ alone

## Explanation

Natural frequency is inversely proportional to the square root of capacitance. Going from $9C$ to $C$ gives a factor of $\sqrt9=3$.
