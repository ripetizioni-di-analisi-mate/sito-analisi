A series RLC circuit at resonance has a resistance of $3$ Ω. The source rms voltage is $21$ V. What is the rms current in amperes?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

At resonance the two reactances cancel. The magnitude of the impedance therefore equals the resistance, and applying Ohm’s law gives $7$.
