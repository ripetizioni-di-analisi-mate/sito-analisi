An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=500$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $500\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=500\cdot0.1=50$ ohms.
