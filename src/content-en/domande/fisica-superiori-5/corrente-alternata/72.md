A tweeter is modeled as a resistor in series with a capacitor. Which part of the signal is more strongly attenuated in the tweeter current?

- Only frequencies with zero initial phase
- Low frequencies
- High frequencies
- All frequencies equally

## Explanation

At low frequency $X_C=1/(\omega C)$ is large. For a given voltage the greater impedance reduces current through the tweeter.
