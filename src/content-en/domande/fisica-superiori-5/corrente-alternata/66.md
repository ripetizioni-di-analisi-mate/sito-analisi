A capacitor has a reactance of $27$ Ω at $400$ Hz. What is its reactance at $1200$ Hz, in ohms?

- $81$
- $9$
- $3$
- $27$

## Explanation

Capacitive reactance is the reciprocal of $2\pi fC$. Tripling the frequency reduces the reactance to one third of its initial value, giving $9$.
