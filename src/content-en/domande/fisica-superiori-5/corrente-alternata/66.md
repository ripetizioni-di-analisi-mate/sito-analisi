A meter reads total RMS voltage. A signal has a $3\,\mathrm V$ DC component and a zero-mean sinusoidal component of $4\,\mathrm V$ RMS. What does the meter read?

- $5\,\mathrm V$
- $7\,\mathrm V$
- $1\,\mathrm V$
- $3.5\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The cross term vanishes in the mean square because the AC component has zero mean. The RMS value is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\,\mathrm V$.
