An inductor has a reactance of $9$ Ω at a frequency of $400$ Hz. What is its reactance at $1200$ Hz, in ohms?

- $27$
- $3$
- $81$
- $9$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $2\pi fL$. At constant inductance, it increases in direct proportion to the frequency, giving $27$.
