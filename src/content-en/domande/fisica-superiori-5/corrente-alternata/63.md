An alternator drives a resistor with sinusoidal current of zero mean. What net charge crosses a wire section in the chosen positive direction in one period?

- Zero
- The charge from the positive half-cycle alone
- RMS current times the period
- Twice the positive half-cycle charge

## Explanation

Net charge is the signed area under the current–time graph. The two half-cycles have equal and opposite areas, so they cancel.
