A resistor, an inductor and a capacitor are connected in parallel across an alternating voltage source. The rms currents in the branches are $3.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in the resistor, $6.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in the inductor and $2.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in the capacitor. What is the rms current delivered by the source?

- $8.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $11\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The three branches have the same voltage across them, whose phasor is taken as reference. The current in the resistor is in phase with the voltage, the one in the inductor lags by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and the one in the capacitor leads by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

By the junction rule applied to phasors, the source current has an in-phase component of $3.0\,\mathrm{A}$ and a lagging component of $(6.0 - 2.0)\,\mathrm{A} = 4.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Therefore its rms value is $\sqrt{3.0^2 + 4.0^2}\,\mathrm{A} = 5.0\,\mathrm{A}$.
