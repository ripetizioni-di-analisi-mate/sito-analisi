A series RLC circuit has an impedance of $13$ Ω and a resistance of $5$ Ω. What is the magnitude of its net reactance, in ohms?

- $\sqrt{194}$
- $18$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The square of the impedance magnitude is the sum of the squares of the resistance and the net reactance. Subtract the square of the resistance and take the square root to obtain the magnitude of the net reactance, $12$.
