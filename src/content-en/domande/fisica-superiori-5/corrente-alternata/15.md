An ideal transformer has $N_2/N_1=4$ and a primary rms voltage of $10$ V. What is its secondary rms voltage?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $40\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For an ideal transformer, $\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}=\dfrac{N_2}{N_1}$. The voltage is therefore multiplied by $4$, giving $V_2=40$ volts.
