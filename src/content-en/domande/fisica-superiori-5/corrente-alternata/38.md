A resistor of resistance $100\,\Omega$ and a capacitor of reactance $100\,\Omega$ are connected in parallel. What is the magnitude of the impedance of the combination?

- $50\,\Omega$
- $141\,\Omega$
- $200\,\Omega$
- $71\,\Omega$

## Explanation

For components in parallel the inverses of the complex impedances add, with $Z_R = 100\,\Omega$ and $Z_C = -100j\,\Omega$. Since $\dfrac{1}{-j} = j$, we have
$$
\frac{1}{Z} = \frac{1}{100\,\Omega} + \frac{j}{100\,\Omega} = \frac{1 + j}{100\,\Omega} .
$$
The number $1 + j$ has magnitude $\sqrt{2}$, therefore $|Z| = \dfrac{100}{\sqrt{2}}\,\Omega \approx 71\,\Omega$.
