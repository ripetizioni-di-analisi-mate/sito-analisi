A pure sinusoidal voltage ranges from $-12$ to $+12\,\mathrm V$. What peak-to-peak voltage does an oscilloscope display?

- $24\,\mathrm V$
- $6\,\mathrm V$
- $12/\sqrt2\,\mathrm V$
- $12\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Peak-to-peak voltage is the difference between maximum and minimum, giving $12-(-12)=24\,\mathrm V$.
