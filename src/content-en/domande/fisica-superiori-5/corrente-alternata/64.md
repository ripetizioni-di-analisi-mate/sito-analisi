A capacitor has a reactance of $15$ Ω at $200$ Hz. What is its reactance at $600$ Hz, in ohms?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $15$
- $45$
- $5$

## Explanation

Capacitive reactance is the reciprocal of $2\pi fC$. Tripling the frequency reduces the reactance to one third of its initial value, giving $5$.
