A sinusoidal voltage has a peak value of $7\sqrt2$ V. What is its rms value?

- $7 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $7\,\mathrm{V}$
- $14\,\mathrm{V}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For a sinusoid the rms value is the amplitude divided by $\sqrt2$. Calculating $V_\mathrm{eff}=\dfrac{7\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}$ gives an rms value of $7$ volts.
