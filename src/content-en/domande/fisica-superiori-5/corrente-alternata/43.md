A resistor, an inductor and a capacitor are connected in series to an AC voltage source. Let $V$ be the rms voltage of the source and $V_R$, $V_L$, $V_C$ the rms voltages across the three components. Which relation holds for any values of $R$, $L$, $C$ and of the frequency?

- $V = V_R + V_L + V_C$
- $V_L \leq V$
- $V_R \leq V$
- $V = V_R + |V_L - V_C|$

## Explanation

By the loop rule applied to phasors, the phasor of the source voltage is the sum of the phasors of the three voltages. The phasor of $V_R$ is perpendicular to the phasors of $V_L$ and $V_C$, which are opposite to each other, so
$$
V = \sqrt{V_R^2 + (V_L - V_C)^2} .
$$
Since $(V_L - V_C)^2 \geq 0$, it follows that $V_R \leq V$ in every case. The other relations do not hold in general; for example, at resonance $V_L = Q\,V$, which exceeds $V$ when $Q > 1$.
