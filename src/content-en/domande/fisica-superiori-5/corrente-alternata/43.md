A capacitor of $C=100$ microfarads operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=1000$ rad/s. What is its capacitive reactance?

- $1000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $1\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{\omega C}$. The capacitance is $100\cdot10^{-6}=10^{-4}$ farads, so the reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{1000\cdot10^{-4}}=\dfrac{100}{10}$ ohms.
