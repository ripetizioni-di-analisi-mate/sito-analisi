A series $RLC$ circuit is supplied at the resonant angular frequency. What is the phase of the voltage across the capacitor relative to the source voltage?

- It is in antiphase
- It lags by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- It leads by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- It is in phase

## Explanation

At resonance $\varphi = 0$, so the current is in phase with the source voltage.

In a capacitor the current leads the voltage by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so the voltage across the capacitor lags the current by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Therefore the voltage across the capacitor also **lags** the source voltage by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The voltages across the capacitor and across the inductor are in antiphase with each other.
