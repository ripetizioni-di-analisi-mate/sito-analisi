An ideal transformer has $N_2/N_1=2$ and a primary rms voltage of $10$ V. What is its secondary rms voltage?

- $20\,\mathrm{V}$
- $5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For an ideal transformer, $\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}=\dfrac{N_2}{N_1}$. The voltage is therefore multiplied by $2$, giving $V_2=20$ volts.
