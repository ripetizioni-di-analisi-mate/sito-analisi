An ideal $0.20\,\mathrm H$ inductor is connected to a $230\,\mathrm V$ rms, $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ supply. Which pair gives reactance and rms current?

- $(62.8\,\Omega,5.18\,\mathrm A)$
- $(62.8\,\Omega,3.66\,\mathrm A)$
- $(314\,\Omega,0.73\,\mathrm A)$
- $(10\,\Omega,23\,\mathrm A)$

## Explanation

Inductive reactance is $X_L=2\pi fL$. Use rms voltage to obtain rms current, $230/X_L$.
