A series RLC circuit has $R=12$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=16$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $28\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $20\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $100\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(12)^2+(16)^2}=20$ ohms.
