A $0.10\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor initially charged to $20\,\mathrm V$ is connected to a $10\,\mathrm{mH}$ inductor with negligible resistance. What peak current does the circuit reach?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $63\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $6.3\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $126\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

At peak current the capacitor is discharged and its initial energy $CV_0^2/2$ is stored in the magnetic field. Equating this to $LI_0^2/2$ gives $I_0=V_0\sqrt{C/L}$.
