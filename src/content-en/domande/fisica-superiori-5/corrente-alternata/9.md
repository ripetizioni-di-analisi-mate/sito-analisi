A series RLC circuit has $R=9$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=12$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $15\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $21\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $3\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $75\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(9)^2+(12)^2}=15$ ohms.
