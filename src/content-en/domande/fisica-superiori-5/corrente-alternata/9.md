At $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ a series RLC has $R=100\,\Omega$, $L=0.50\,\mathrm H$ and $C=40\,\mu\mathrm F$. The reactances are about $157\,\Omega$ and $80\,\Omega$. What term is added to $R^2$ when calculating $\lvert Z\rvert^2$, and what is the circuit's character?

- $(157-80)^2\,\Omega^2$, inductive
- $(157+80)^2\,\Omega^2$, inductive
- $(157-80)^2\,\Omega^2$, capacitive
- $(157^2+80^2)\,\Omega^2$, purely resistive

## Explanation

Inductive and capacitive reactance contribute with opposite signs to the phasor sum. Their positive difference makes voltage lead current and gives $\lvert Z\rvert\simeq1.3\cdot10^2\,\Omega$.
