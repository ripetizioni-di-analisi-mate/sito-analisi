In a series circuit a $60\,\Omega$ resistor and an inductor of $80\,\Omega$ reactance are supplied at $50\,\mathrm V$ rms. By what phase angle does the current lag the supply voltage?

- $0^\circ$
- About $53^\circ$
- About $37^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

Taking current as the reference, voltage has an in-phase component $IR$ and a quadrature component $IX_L$. The angle is $\arctan(80/60)\simeq53^\circ$.
