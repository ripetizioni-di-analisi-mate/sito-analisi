An AC voltage is given by $v(t) = 325 \sin(314\,t)$, with voltage in volts and time in seconds. Determine its rms value and frequency.

- $230\,\mathrm V$ and $314\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $230\,\mathrm V$ and $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $325\,\mathrm V$ and $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $325\,\mathrm V$ and $314\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The amplitude in front of the sine and the time coefficient represent different quantities. The first gives peak voltage and the second angular frequency in radians per second. $V_{\text{eff}} = \dfrac{325}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{V} \approx 230\,\mathrm{V}$, $f = \dfrac{314}{2\pi}\,\mathrm{Hz} \approx 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
