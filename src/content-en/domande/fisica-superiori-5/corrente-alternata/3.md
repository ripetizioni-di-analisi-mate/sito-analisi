A capacitor of $C=100$ microfarads operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=200$ rad/s. What is its capacitive reactance?

- $200\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{\omega C}$. The capacitance is $100\cdot10^{-6}=10^{-4}$ farads, so the reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{200\cdot10^{-4}}=\dfrac{100}{2}$ ohms.
