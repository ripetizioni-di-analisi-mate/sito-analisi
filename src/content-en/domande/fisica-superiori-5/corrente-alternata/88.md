In an ideal LC circuit, the capacitor charge reaches its maximum of $5$ µC. How is the energy distributed at that instant?

- All of it is magnetic energy in the inductor.
- The total energy is zero.
- Half is electric and half is magnetic.
- All of it is electric energy in the capacitor.

## Explanation

When the charge reaches its maximum, its time derivative is zero, so the current is also zero. The inductor stores no magnetic energy at that instant.
