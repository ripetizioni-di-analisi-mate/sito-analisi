A wattmeter and two RMS meters measure voltage, current, and active power for a two-terminal load in sinusoidal operation. The power factor is $0.80$. What information is still needed to distinguish an inductive from a capacitive load?

- The active power in kilowatts
- The period in milliseconds instead of seconds
- The product of the two RMS readings already taken
- Whether the current leads or lags the voltage

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{P}{V_{\mathrm{rms}}I_{\mathrm{rms}}}$ gives $\cos\varphi$. The angles $+\varphi$ and $-\varphi$ have the same cosine, so a measurement that distinguishes lead from lag is needed.
