A line of total resistance $2.0\,\Omega$ transmits $1.0\,\mathrm{MW}$ at a sending-end rms voltage of $50\,\mathrm{kV}$ and unity power factor. How much power becomes heat in the line?

- $400\,\mathrm W$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{MW}$
- $40\,\mathrm W$
- $800\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The rms current is $P/V=20\,\mathrm A$. The losses depend on the square of this current and on the line resistance alone, giving $2\cdot20^2\,\mathrm W$.
