An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=300$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $3000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{1}{30}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $30\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $300\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=300\cdot0.1=30$ ohms.
