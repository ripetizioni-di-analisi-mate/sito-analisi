A resistor at operating temperature is rated for $2\,\mathrm W$ and has resistance $8\,\Omega$. What is the largest sinusoidal peak current consistent with the mean power rating?

- $0.5\,\mathrm A$
- $1\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $1/\sqrt2\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

From $P=RI_{\rm rms}^2$ one obtains $I_{\rm rms}=0.5\,\mathrm A$. Peak current is $\sqrt2$ times the RMS value, namely $1/\sqrt2\,\mathrm A$.
