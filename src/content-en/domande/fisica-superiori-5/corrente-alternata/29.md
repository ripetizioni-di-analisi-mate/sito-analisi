A series RLC circuit has $R=21$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=28$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $35\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $49\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $7\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $175\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(21)^2+(28)^2}=35$ ohms.
