An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=600$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $6000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $60\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{1}{60}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $600\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=600\cdot0.1=60$ ohms.
