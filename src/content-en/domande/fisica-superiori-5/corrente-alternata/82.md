The primary of an ideal transformer has $200$ turns and the secondary has $600$. The primary rms voltage is $50$ V. What is the secondary rms voltage, in volts?

- $150$
- $450$
- $50$
- $\dfrac{50}{3}$

## Explanation

The ratio of secondary to primary voltage equals the ratio of secondary to primary turns. The secondary has three times as many turns, giving $150$.
