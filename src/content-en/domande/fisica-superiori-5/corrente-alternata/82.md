A real transformer receives $50\,\mathrm W$ and is 80% efficient. It supplies a resistor at $10\,\mathrm V$ RMS. What is its secondary current?

- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $0.8\,\mathrm A$
- $6.25\,\mathrm A$
- $5\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

Output power is $0.80\cdot50=40\,\mathrm W$. A resistive load has unit power factor, so $I=40/10=4\,\mathrm A$.
