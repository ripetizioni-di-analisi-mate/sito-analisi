An ideal $33\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor is connected to a sinusoidal $230\,\mathrm V$ rms supply at $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What should a series rms ammeter read?

- $0.38\,\mathrm A$
- $0.42\,\mathrm A$
- $3.37\,\mathrm A$
- $2.38\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

Charge equals $Cv$ and current is its derivative, so sinusoidal amplitudes and rms values satisfy $I_{\rm rms}=2\pi f C V_{\rm rms}$. The corresponding reactance is about $96.5\,\Omega$.
