A sinusoidal voltage has a peak value of $3\sqrt2$ V. What is its rms value?

- $3 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $3\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For a sinusoid the rms value is the amplitude divided by $\sqrt2$. Calculating $V_\mathrm{eff}=\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}$ gives an rms value of $3$ volts.
