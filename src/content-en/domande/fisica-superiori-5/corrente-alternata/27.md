An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=700$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $7000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{1}{70}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $70\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $700\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=700\cdot0.1=70$ ohms.
