A capacitor of $C=100$ microfarads operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=400$ rad/s. What is its capacitive reactance?

- $25\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $400\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{\omega C}$. The capacitance is $100\cdot10^{-6}=10^{-4}$ farads, so the reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{400\cdot10^{-4}}=\dfrac{100}{4}$ ohms.
