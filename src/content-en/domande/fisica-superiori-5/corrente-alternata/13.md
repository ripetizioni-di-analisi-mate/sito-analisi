An ideal transformer with 500 primary turns and 25 secondary turns is connected to $230\,\mathrm V$ rms on its primary. The load draws $4.0\,\mathrm A$ rms on its secondary. What do a secondary voltmeter and a primary ammeter read, respectively?

- $(230\,\mathrm V,4.0\,\mathrm A)$
- $(11.5\,\mathrm V,80\,\mathrm A)$
- $(4600\,\mathrm V,0.20\,\mathrm A)$
- $(11.5\,\mathrm V,0.20\,\mathrm A)$

## Explanation

The secondary voltage is $230(25/500)\,\mathrm V$. In an ideal transformer primary current is reduced relative to secondary current by the same ratio as secondary voltage relative to primary voltage.
