Two appliances each receive $100\,\mathrm V$ RMS and draw equal real power. Their power factors are one and $0.5$. Which causes greater heating in their separate identical supply leads?

- The unity load, with twice the loss
- The losses are equal
- The $0.5$ load, with half the loss
- The $0.5$ load, with four times the loss

## Explanation

From $P=VI\cos\varphi$ the second load draws twice the current. Cable loss is proportional to $I^2$, so it quadruples.
