A circuit operates at $70$ V and draws $3$ A, both rms values. Its power factor is $0.5$. What is the average power in watts?

- $210$
- $\dfrac{35}{3}$
- $105$
- $420$

## Explanation

The average power is the product of the rms voltage, the rms current, and the power factor. Here the factor halves the product of the first two values, giving $105$.
