A $4$ Ω resistor is supplied with a sinusoidal voltage of rms value $36$ V. What is the average power in watts?

- $162$
- $324$
- $81$
- $648$

## Explanation

The average power in the resistor is the square of the rms voltage divided by the resistance. Canceling one factor of the resistance gives $324$.
