A series RLC circuit has $R=18$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=24$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $42\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $150\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $30\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(18)^2+(24)^2}=30$ ohms.
