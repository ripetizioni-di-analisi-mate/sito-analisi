An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=900$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $90\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $9000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{1}{90}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $900\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=900\cdot0.1=90$ ohms.
