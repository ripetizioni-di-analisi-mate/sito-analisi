What resistance must the filament of a $100\,\mathrm W$ incandescent lamp have at operating temperature on a sinusoidal $230\,\mathrm V$ rms supply, treating the filament as a resistor?

- $1058\,\Omega$
- $529\,\Omega$
- $2.30\,\Omega$
- $230\,\Omega$

## Explanation

For a resistive filament $P=V_{\rm rms}^2/R$. The stated voltage is already an rms value, giving $R=230^2/100\,\Omega$.
