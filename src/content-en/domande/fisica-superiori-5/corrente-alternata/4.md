A series RLC circuit has $R=6$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=8$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $14\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(6)^2+(8)^2}=10$ ohms.
