An ideal transformer has $N_2/N_1=6$ and a primary rms voltage of $10$ V. What is its secondary rms voltage?

- $60\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For an ideal transformer, $\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}=\dfrac{N_2}{N_1}$. The voltage is therefore multiplied by $6$, giving $V_2=60$ volts.
