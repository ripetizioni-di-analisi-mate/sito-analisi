A circuit operates at $50$ V and draws $2$ A, both rms values. Its power factor is $0.5$. What is the average power in watts?

- $100$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}$
- $200$
- $50$

## Explanation

The average power is the product of the rms voltage, the rms current, and the power factor. Here the factor halves the product of the first two values, giving $50$.
