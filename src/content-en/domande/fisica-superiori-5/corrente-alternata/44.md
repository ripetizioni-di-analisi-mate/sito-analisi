A series RLC circuit has $R=30$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=40$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $70\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $250\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(30)^2+(40)^2}=50$ ohms.
