The figure shows the rms current in a series $RLC$ circuit as a function of the frequency of the source, whose rms voltage is $10\,\mathrm{V}$. What is the resistance of the circuit?

::grafico{f="20/sqrt(1+25*(x/5-5/x)^2)+0*sqrt(x)" punti="5,20,pieno" segmenti="5,0,5,20;0,20,5,20" xmin=-0.5 xmax=10.5 ymin=-1 ymax=22 nomi="f,I" legenda=0 titolo="Rms current $I_{\text{eff}}$, in units of $10\,\mathrm{mA}$, as a function of the frequency $f$, in hundreds of hertz"}

- $100\,\Omega$
- $50\,\Omega$
- $35\,\Omega$
- $71\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The maximum of the curve is at $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and equals $0.20\,\mathrm{A}$, so that is the resonant frequency.

At resonance $X_L = X_C$ and the impedance reduces to the resistance, $Z = R$. Therefore $R = \dfrac{V_{\text{eff}}}{I_{\text{eff}}} = \dfrac{10}{0.20}\,\Omega = 50\,\Omega$.
