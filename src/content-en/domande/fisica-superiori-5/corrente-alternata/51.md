A sinusoidal current has amplitude $9\sqrt 2$ A. What is its rms value in amperes?

- $18$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $9 \sqrt{2}$
- $9$

## Explanation

The mean square of a sinusoid of unit amplitude is one half. Taking the square root of the mean square of the current gives the amplitude divided by $\sqrt 2$, yielding $9$.
