A sinusoidal current has amplitude $5\sqrt 2$ A. What is its rms value in amperes?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5 \sqrt{2}$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The mean square of a sinusoid of unit amplitude is one half. Taking the square root of the mean square of the current gives the amplitude divided by $\sqrt 2$, yielding $5$.
