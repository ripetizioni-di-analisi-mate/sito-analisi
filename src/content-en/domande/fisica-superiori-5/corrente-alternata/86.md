A search coil measures sinusoidal transformer flux. Frequency is to be halved while keeping peak flux and turns fixed. How must ideal primary voltage amplitude change?

- It must be doubled
- It must stay unchanged
- It must be halved
- It must quadruple

## Explanation

For sinusoidal flux $V_0=N\omega\Phi_0$. With $N$ and $\Phi_0$ fixed, voltage amplitude is proportional to frequency.
