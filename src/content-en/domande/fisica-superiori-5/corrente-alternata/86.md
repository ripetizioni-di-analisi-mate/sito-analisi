An ideal transformer triples the rms voltage and supplies a resistive load. If the primary rms current is $21$ A, what is the secondary rms current in amperes?

- $7$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $63$

## Explanation

In an ideal transformer, primary and secondary powers are equal. Tripling the voltage reduces the current to one third, giving $7$.
