A DC-coupled oscilloscope shows $v(t)=2+3\sin(\omega t)$ in volts. What does a meter that reads only the mean over whole cycles indicate?

- $5\,\mathrm V$
- $0\,\mathrm V$
- $2\,\mathrm V$
- $3\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The sinusoid averages to zero over a full period. The remaining DC component is therefore $2\,\mathrm V$.
