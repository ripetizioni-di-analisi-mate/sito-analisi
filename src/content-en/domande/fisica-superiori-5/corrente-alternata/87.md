An ideal transformer triples the rms voltage and supplies a resistive load. If the primary rms current is $27$ A, what is the secondary rms current in amperes?

- $9$
- $3$
- $81$
- $27$

## Explanation

In an ideal transformer, primary and secondary powers are equal. Tripling the voltage reduces the current to one third, giving $9$.
