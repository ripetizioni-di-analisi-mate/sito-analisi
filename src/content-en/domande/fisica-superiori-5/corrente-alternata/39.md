A series RLC circuit has $R=27$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=36$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $63\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $9\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $45\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $225\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(27)^2+(36)^2}=45$ ohms.
