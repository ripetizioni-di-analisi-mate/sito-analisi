An ideal $2$ µF capacitor is supplied at $5$ Hz. How do the phases of the current and voltage compare?

- They are in phase.
- The current leads the voltage by ninety degrees.
- They are in opposite phase.
- The current lags the voltage by ninety degrees.

## Explanation

The current is proportional to the derivative of the voltage. Differentiating a sinusoid advances its phase by ninety degrees.
