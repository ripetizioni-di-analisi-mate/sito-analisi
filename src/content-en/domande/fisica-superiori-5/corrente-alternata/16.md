A sinusoidal voltage has a peak value of $5\sqrt2$ V. What is its rms value?

- $5 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $5\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For a sinusoid the rms value is the amplitude divided by $\sqrt2$. Calculating $V_\mathrm{eff}=\dfrac{5\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}$ gives an rms value of $5$ volts.
