An ideal transformer triples the rms voltage and supplies a resistive load. If the primary rms current is $15$ A, what is the secondary rms current in amperes?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $15$
- $5$
- $45$

## Explanation

In an ideal transformer, primary and secondary powers are equal. Tripling the voltage reduces the current to one third, giving $5$.
