A $3$ Ω resistor is supplied with a sinusoidal voltage of rms value $21$ V. What is the average power in watts?

- $294$
- $147$
- $49$
- $\dfrac{147}{2}$

## Explanation

The average power in the resistor is the square of the rms voltage divided by the resistance. Canceling one factor of the resistance gives $147$.
