A capacitor has a reactance of $21$ Ω at $300$ Hz. What is its reactance at $900$ Hz, in ohms?

- $63$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $21$
- $7$

## Explanation

Capacitive reactance is the reciprocal of $2\pi fC$. Tripling the frequency reduces the reactance to one third of its initial value, giving $7$.
