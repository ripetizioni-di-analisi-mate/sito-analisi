The mean current in a resistor is zero over each cycle, yet the resistor warms up. Which average determines its heating?

- The mean of $i^2$
- The square of the mean current
- Only the mean of the positive peaks
- The mean of the derivative of $i$

## Explanation

Instantaneous power is $Ri^2$. Opposite currents cancel in the mean current but give positive contributions when squared.
