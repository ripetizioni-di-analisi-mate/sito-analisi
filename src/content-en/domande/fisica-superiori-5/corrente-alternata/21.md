A sinusoidal voltage has a peak value of $6\sqrt2$ V. What is its rms value?

- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $3\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For a sinusoid the rms value is the amplitude divided by $\sqrt2$. Calculating $V_\mathrm{eff}=\dfrac{6\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}$ gives an rms value of $6$ volts.
