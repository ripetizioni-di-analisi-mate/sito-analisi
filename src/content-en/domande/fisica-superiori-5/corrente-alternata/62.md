An alternator with two pole pairs produces two electrical cycles per revolution. What frequency is measured at $1500$ revolutions per minute?

- $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $100\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $25\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The rotor makes $1500/60=25$ revolutions per second. Two cycles per revolution give 50 cycles per second.
