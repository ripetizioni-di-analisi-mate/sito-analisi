An inductor has a reactance of $7$ Ω at a frequency of $300$ Hz. What is its reactance at $900$ Hz, in ohms?

- $63$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $7$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $2\pi fL$. At constant inductance, it increases in direct proportion to the frequency, giving $21$.
