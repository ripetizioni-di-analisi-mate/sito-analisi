A sinusoidal current has amplitude $7\sqrt 2$ A. What is its rms value in amperes?

- $7 \sqrt{2}$
- $7$
- $14$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The mean square of a sinusoid of unit amplitude is one half. Taking the square root of the mean square of the current gives the amplitude divided by $\sqrt 2$, yielding $7$.
