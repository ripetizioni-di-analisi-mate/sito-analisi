An ideal source maintains $6\,\mathrm V$ RMS across a resistor. An ideal voltmeter is added across it. What happens to the resistor current?

- It falls to zero
- It halves
- It stays unchanged
- It doubles

## Explanation

An ideal voltmeter has infinite impedance. It leaves the resistor voltage and hence the current $V/R$ unchanged.
