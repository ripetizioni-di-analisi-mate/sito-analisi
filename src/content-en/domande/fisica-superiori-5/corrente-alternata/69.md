A series RLC circuit has $R=12$ Ω, $X_L=25$ Ω, and $X_C=9$ Ω. What is the magnitude of its impedance, in ohms?

- $20$
- $28$
- $16$
- $12$

## Explanation

The reactances contribute with opposite signs, so the net reactance is ${4*a}$ Ω. Combining it in quadrature with the resistance gives $20$.
