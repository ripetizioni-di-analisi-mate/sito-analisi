A rotating 60-turn coil of turn area $5.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ gives a sinusoidal emf of peak $12\,\mathrm V$ at $40\,\mathrm{Hz}$. A technician estimates the field using the meter's rms reading in the formula for peak emf. How should the resulting value of $B$ be corrected?

- Divide it by $\sqrt2$
- Leave it unchanged
- Multiply it by $\sqrt2$
- Multiply it by $2\pi$

## Explanation

For an axis in the coil plane and perpendicular to the field, $B=\mathcal E_0/(NA\,2\pi f)$. The rms reading is smaller than the peak by a factor of $\sqrt2$, underestimating the field by that same factor.
