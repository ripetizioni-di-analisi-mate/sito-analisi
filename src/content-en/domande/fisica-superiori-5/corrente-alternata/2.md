An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=200$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $2000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $20\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $200\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=200\cdot0.1=20$ ohms.
