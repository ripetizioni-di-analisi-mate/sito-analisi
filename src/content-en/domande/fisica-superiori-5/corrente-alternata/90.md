An ideal alternator drives a resistor at fixed angular speed. A second identical resistor is connected in parallel. What must happen to the mean mechanical input power?

- It doubles
- It quadruples
- It stays zero
- It halves

## Explanation

At unchanged speed the ideal generator maintains the same RMS voltage. Each resistor receives the original power, so energy conservation requires double the mechanical input.
