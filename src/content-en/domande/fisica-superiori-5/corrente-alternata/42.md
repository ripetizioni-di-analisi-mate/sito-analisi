An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=1000$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $10000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $100\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=1000\cdot0.1=100$ ohms.
