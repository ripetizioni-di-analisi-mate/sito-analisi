The capacitor in an ideal LC oscillator has maximum positive charge at $t=0$. After what fraction of a period is its charge first maximally negative?

- $T/8$
- $T/2$
- $T$
- $T/4$

## Explanation

Charge follows $q=Q\cos(2\pi t/T)$. Its first minimum occurs at phase $\pi$, half a period later.
