An ideal LC circuit oscillates at $700$ Hz. The capacitance is quadrupled while the inductance stays the same. What is the new frequency in hertz?

- $175$
- $1400$
- $350$
- $2800$

## Explanation

The frequency is inversely proportional to the square root of the product $LC$. Quadrupling the capacitance doubles the denominator, giving $350$.
