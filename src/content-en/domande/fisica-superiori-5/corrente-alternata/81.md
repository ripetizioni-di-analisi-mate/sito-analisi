A circuit operates at $90$ V and draws $4$ A, both rms values. Its power factor is $0.5$. What is the average power in watts?

- $180$
- $720$
- $\dfrac{45}{4}$
- $360$

## Explanation

The average power is the product of the rms voltage, the rms current, and the power factor. Here the factor halves the product of the first two values, giving $180$.
