A transformer has two identical secondary windings producing $6\,\mathrm V$ RMS each. In series they deliver almost zero with no load. What does this measurement indicate?

- RMS voltages can never add
- The secondaries both have double frequency
- The open-circuit current is $12\,\mathrm A$
- The two voltages are in phase opposition

## Explanation

The two sinusoids have equal frequency and amplitude. With opposing winding polarities their instantaneous sum vanishes.
