The primary of an ideal transformer has $300$ turns and the secondary has $900$. The primary rms voltage is $70$ V. What is the secondary rms voltage, in volts?

- $630$
- $70$
- $210$
- $\dfrac{70}{3}$

## Explanation

The ratio of secondary to primary voltage equals the ratio of secondary to primary turns. The secondary has three times as many turns, giving $210$.
