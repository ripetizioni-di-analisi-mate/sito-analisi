A series RLC circuit has $R=9$ Ω, $X_L=19$ Ω, and $X_C=7$ Ω. What is the magnitude of its impedance, in ohms?

- $15$
- $21$
- $9$
- $12$

## Explanation

The reactances contribute with opposite signs, so the net reactance is ${4*a}$ Ω. Combining it in quadrature with the resistance gives $15$.
