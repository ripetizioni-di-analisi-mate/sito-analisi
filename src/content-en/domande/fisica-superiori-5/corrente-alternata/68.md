A graph shows sinusoidal voltage and current at different frequencies. Can two phasors separated by a constant angle represent them?

- Only if both have positive mean values
- Yes, for every pair of sinusoids
- Yes, if the amplitudes are equal
- No, their phase difference varies with time

## Explanation

Phasors rotate at their respective angular frequencies. Different frequencies therefore make the relative angle change continuously.
