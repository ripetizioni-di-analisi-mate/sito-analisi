In a weakly damped oscillating circuit the peak capacitor voltage is measured at the same phase. After each period its amplitude is $80\%$ of the preceding value. What fraction of the initial peak electric energy remains after two periods?

- $0.64$
- $0.20$
- $0.4096$
- $0.80$

## Explanation

After two periods the ratio of the peak voltages is $0.8^2=0.64$. Since $U_C=\dfrac12 CV^2$, the ratio of peak energies is $0.64^2=0.4096$.
