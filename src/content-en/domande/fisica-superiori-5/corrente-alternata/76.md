An ideal LC circuit oscillates at $500$ Hz. The capacitance is quadrupled while the inductance stays the same. What is the new frequency in hertz?

- $1000$
- $2000$
- $250$
- $125$

## Explanation

The frequency is inversely proportional to the square root of the product $LC$. Quadrupling the capacitance doubles the denominator, giving $250$.
