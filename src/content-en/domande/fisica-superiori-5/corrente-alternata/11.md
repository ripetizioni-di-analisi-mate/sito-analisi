A $0.80\,\mathrm H$ inductor must resonate in series at $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. This requires about $13\,\mu\mathrm F$. Only ideal $26\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitors are available. Which arrangement tunes the circuit?

- A single capacitor
- Four capacitors in parallel
- Two capacitors in series
- Two capacitors in parallel

## Explanation

Resonance requires $C=1/(4\pi^2f^2L)\simeq13\,\mu\mathrm F$. Two equal series capacitors have equivalent capacitance $C_1/2$, the required value.
