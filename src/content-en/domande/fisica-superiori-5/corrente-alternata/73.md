A series RLC circuit at resonance has a resistance of $2$ Ω. The source rms voltage is $10$ V. What is the rms current in amperes?

- $10$
- $0$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

At resonance the two reactances cancel. The magnitude of the impedance therefore equals the resistance, and applying Ohm’s law gives $5$.
