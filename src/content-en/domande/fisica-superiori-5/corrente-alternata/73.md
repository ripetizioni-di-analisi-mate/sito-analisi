During a frequency sweep of a series RLC circuit, resistor power falls to half its peak at $480$ and $520\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The peak is near $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the quality factor, defined as the peak frequency divided by the half-power bandwidth?

- $2$
- $25$
- $0.08$
- $12.5$

## Explanation

The bandwidth is the separation of the two half-power frequencies, $520-480=40\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The ratio $\dfrac{500}{40}=12.5$ is dimensionless.
