An ideal transformer has fixed sinusoidal primary voltage. When its secondary load is disconnected, which secondary quantity is still fixed by the turns ratio?

- The resistance of the removed load
- Open-circuit voltage
- The previous delivered active power
- The former load current

## Explanation

For an ideal transformer $V_2/V_1=N_2/N_1$ even at no load. The open secondary carries zero current and delivers zero power.
