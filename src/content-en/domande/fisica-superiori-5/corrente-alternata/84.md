The primary of an ideal transformer has $400$ turns and the secondary has $1200$. The primary rms voltage is $90$ V. What is the secondary rms voltage, in volts?

- $810$
- $270$
- $90$
- $30$

## Explanation

The ratio of secondary to primary voltage equals the ratio of secondary to primary turns. The secondary has three times as many turns, giving $270$.
