A $2$ Ω resistor is supplied with a sinusoidal voltage of rms value $10$ V. What is the average power in watts?

- $50$
- $-50$
- $25$
- $100$

## Explanation

The average power in the resistor is the square of the rms voltage divided by the resistance. Canceling one factor of the resistance gives $50$.
