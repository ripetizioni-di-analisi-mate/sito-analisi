A series RLC circuit has $R=24$ ohms and $X_L-X_C=32$ ohms. What is the magnitude of its impedance?

- $56\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $40\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $200\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$. The two terms are the legs of a Pythagorean triple, giving $|Z|=\sqrt{(24)^2+(32)^2}=40$ ohms.
