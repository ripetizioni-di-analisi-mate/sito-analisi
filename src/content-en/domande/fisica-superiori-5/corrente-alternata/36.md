A sinusoidal voltage has a peak value of $9\sqrt2$ V. What is its rms value?

- $9 \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $18\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $9\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For a sinusoid the rms value is the amplitude divided by $\sqrt2$. Calculating $V_\mathrm{eff}=\dfrac{9\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}$ gives an rms value of $9$ volts.
