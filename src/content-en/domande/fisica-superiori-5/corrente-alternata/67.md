Three sinusoidal currents with equal frequency and amplitude reach a node. Their phasors are spaced by $120^\circ$. What current flows in the common return wire?

- Equal to one phase current
- Zero only on average, with three times the peak current
- Three times one phase current
- Zero at every instant

## Explanation

The three phasors have equal lengths and form a closed equilateral triangle when added tip to tail. Their resultant is zero. By the current law at a junction, the sum of the three instantaneous currents, and hence the return current, is zero.
