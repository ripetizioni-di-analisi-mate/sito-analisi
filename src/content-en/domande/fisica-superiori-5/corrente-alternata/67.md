A series RLC circuit has $R=6$ Ω, $X_L=13$ Ω, and $X_C=5$ Ω. What is the magnitude of its impedance, in ohms?

- $8$
- $6$
- $14$
- $10$

## Explanation

The reactances contribute with opposite signs, so the net reactance is ${4*a}$ Ω. Combining it in quadrature with the resistance gives $10$.
