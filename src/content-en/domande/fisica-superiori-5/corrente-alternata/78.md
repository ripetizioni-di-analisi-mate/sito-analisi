An ideal LC circuit oscillates at $900$ Hz. The capacitance is quadrupled while the inductance stays the same. What is the new frequency in hertz?

- $3600$
- $450$
- $1800$
- $225$

## Explanation

The frequency is inversely proportional to the square root of the product $LC$. Quadrupling the capacitance doubles the denominator, giving $450$.
