An energy meter records $0.30\,\mathrm{kWh}$ in 15 minutes while an appliance runs steadily. What is its mean active power?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $0.075\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

Fifteen minutes is $0.25$ hours. Energy divided by time gives $0.30/0.25=1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$.
