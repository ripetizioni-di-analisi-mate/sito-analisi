A logger samples a $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ sinusoidal voltage exactly every $20\,\mathrm{ms}$. Every sample reads $4\,\mathrm V$. Why is this record compatible with an alternating voltage?

- The rms value equals every instantaneous value.
- Every sample is taken at the same phase of the cycle.
- The source undergoes only half a cycle per interval.
- An alternating signal with zero DC component can give only zero-valued samples.

## Explanation

The period is $1/50\,\mathrm s=20\,\mathrm{ms}$, exactly the sampling interval. Readings at other phases, for example at a higher sampling rate, are needed to reveal the oscillation.
