Measurements on a series RLC circuit give $X_L=8$ Ω and $X_C=5$ Ω. How does the circuit behave?

- It is capacitive.
- It is purely resistive.
- It is at resonance.
- It is inductive.

## Explanation

The net reactance is the inductive reactance minus the capacitive reactance. Here the difference is positive, so the inductive contribution dominates.
