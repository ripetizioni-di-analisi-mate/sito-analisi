A sinusoidal voltage at $5$ Hz is applied to an ideal $2$ H inductor. How is the current shifted in phase relative to the voltage?

- The current leads by a quarter of a period.
- The current lags by half a period.
- The current is in phase.
- The current lags by a quarter of a period.

## Explanation

The voltage across the inductor is proportional to the derivative of the current. Differentiation advances a sinusoid by a quarter of a period.
