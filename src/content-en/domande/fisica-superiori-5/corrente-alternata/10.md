A series $RLC$ circuit has $L = 0.15\,\mathrm{H}$ and $C = 2.2\,\mu\mathrm{F}$. Calculate the resonant frequency.

- $44\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2.77\,\mathrm{kHz}$
- $1.74\,\mathrm{kHz}$
- $277\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

At resonance the opposite reactances cancel. From $\omega L=1/(\omega C)$ obtain angular frequency, then divide by $2\pi$ to get hertz. $f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} \approx 277\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
