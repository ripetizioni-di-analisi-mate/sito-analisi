An inductor has a reactance of $5$ Ω at a frequency of $200$ Hz. What is its reactance at $600$ Hz, in ohms?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $15$
- $45$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $2\pi fL$. At constant inductance, it increases in direct proportion to the frequency, giving $15$.
