Two series RLC circuits are tested separately at the same voltage with identical $L,C$. The second has twice the internal resistance. How do their current peaks compare?

- Same frequency and double the current in the second
- Double frequency and equal current
- Half the frequency and double the current
- Same frequency, half the current in the second

## Explanation

The maximum occurs when $X_L=X_C$, independently of $R$. At that point $I=V/R$, so doubling $R$ halves the current.
