A series RLC circuit at resonance has a resistance of $4$ Ω. The source rms voltage is $36$ V. What is the rms current in amperes?

- $0$
- $36$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

At resonance the two reactances cancel. The magnitude of the impedance therefore equals the resistance, and applying Ohm’s law gives $9$.
