A capacitor of $C=100$ microfarads operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=700$ rad/s. What is its capacitive reactance?

- $700\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.07\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{10}{7}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{100}{7}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The capacitive reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{\omega C}$. The capacitance is $100\cdot10^{-6}=10^{-4}$ farads, so the reactance is $X_C=\dfrac{1}{700\cdot10^{-4}}=\dfrac{100}{7}$ ohms.
