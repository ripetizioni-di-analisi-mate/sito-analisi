An ideal inductor of $L=0.1$ H operates at an angular frequency of $\omega=800$ rad/s. What is its inductive reactance?

- $8000\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.0125\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $800\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $80\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

The inductive reactance is $X_L=\omega L$. Multiplying the angular frequency by the inductance gives $X_L=800\cdot0.1=80$ ohms.
