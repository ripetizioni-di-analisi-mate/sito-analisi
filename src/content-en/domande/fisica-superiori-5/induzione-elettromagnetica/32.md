The flux through a single loop changes by $8$ Wb in $0.5$ s. What is the magnitude of the average emf?

- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$
- $32\,\mathrm{V}$
- $16\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Faraday’s law gives the magnitude of the average emf as $|\mathcal E|=\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_B|}{\Delta t}$. The given change is for one loop. The result is $|\mathcal E|=\dfrac{8}{0.5}=16$ volts.
