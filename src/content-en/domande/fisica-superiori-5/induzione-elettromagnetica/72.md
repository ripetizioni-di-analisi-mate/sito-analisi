A moving-rod generator produces a current of $4$ A with an emf of $9$ V. At a constant speed of $5$ m/s, what is the magnitude in newtons of the external force required if all the power is converted to heat?

- $36$
- $\dfrac{36}{5}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $180$

## Explanation

The mechanical power supplied by the external force equals the electrical power dissipated, $\mathcal E I$. Divide by the rod’s speed. The result is $\dfrac{36}{5}$.
