The base of an induction pan remains stationary on the hob. What change produces the currents that heat it?

- A required change of pan position on every cycle
- A shrinking pan perimeter
- Rotation of the normal by gravity
- The time variation of the field from the coil below

## Explanation

The alternating field changes the flux through closed paths in the metal. Induced currents dissipate energy by Joule heating.
