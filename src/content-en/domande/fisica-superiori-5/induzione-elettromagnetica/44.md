A rod of length $0.5$ m moves at $10$ m/s in a field of $B=0.2$ T. The rod, its velocity, and the field are mutually perpendicular. What is the magnitude of the motional emf?

- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $100\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $1\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For this orientation, the magnitude of the motional emf is $|\mathcal E|=B\ell v$. Multiply $0.2$, $0.5$, and $10$. The magnitude in volts is therefore $1$.
