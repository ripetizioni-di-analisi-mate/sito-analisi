The flux linkage of a coil increases from $1$ Wb to $7$ Wb in three seconds. What final flux would double the average emf if the time, the initial flux, and the sign of the flux change all remained the same?

- $14$
- $8$
- $13$
- $12$

## Explanation

For a fixed time interval, the change in flux must double. The original change is six webers, so add twelve webers to the initial flux. The result is $13$.
