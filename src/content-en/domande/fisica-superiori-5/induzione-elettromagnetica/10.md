A rod of length $0.50\,\mathrm m$ moves at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along rails in a normal field of $0.30\,\mathrm T$. The circuit resistance is $1.8\,\Omega$. Neglect friction and self-inductance. How much mechanical power is needed to maintain its speed?

- $1.8\,\mathrm W$
- $0.90\,\mathrm W$
- $0.45\,\mathrm W$
- $0.075\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The motional emf is $B\ell v=0.90\,\mathrm V$ and the current is $0.50\,\mathrm A$. The external work per second replaces the heat dissipated, $P=I^2R=0.45\,\mathrm W$.
