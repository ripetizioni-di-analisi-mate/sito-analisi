A square loop rotates one quarter turn from its normal aligned with the field, then another quarter turn in the same direction until it is anti-aligned. How do the signed flux changes compare?

- The first is zero and the second $-2BA$
- Both equal $+BA$
- They are equal and opposite
- Both equal $-BA$

## Explanation

The three successive fluxes are $+BA$, zero and $-BA$. Subtracting each initial value from the corresponding final one gives $-BA$ both times.
