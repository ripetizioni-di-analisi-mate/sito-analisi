The magnetic field increases uniformly at $7$ T/s. Two loops perpendicular to the field have radii of $3$ cm and $9$ cm. What is the ratio of the emf magnitudes, larger loop to smaller loop?

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $3$
- $9$
- $1$

## Explanation

The change in flux is proportional to the area of each loop. The ratio of the radii is three, so the ratio of the areas is nine. The result is $9$.
