The flux linkage of a coil changes by $10$ mWb. The total resistance is $5$ Ω and self-induction is negligible. What magnitude of charge passes through the circuit, in millicoulombs?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $2$
- $10$
- $50$

## Explanation

Integrate the current over time. Ohm’s law and Faraday’s law give the magnitude of the change in flux linkage divided by the resistance. The result is $2$.
