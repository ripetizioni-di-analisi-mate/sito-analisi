The flux through a loop is $\Phi(t)=2t^2+5t$ in webers. What is the signed emf at $t=3$ s, in volts?

- $-17$
- $17$
- $-12$
- $-11$

## Explanation

The signed emf is the negative of the time derivative of the flux. Differentiate the polynomial and then substitute the specified time. The result is $-17$.
