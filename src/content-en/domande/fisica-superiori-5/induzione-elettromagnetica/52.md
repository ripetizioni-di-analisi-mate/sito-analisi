A coil in series with an ideal diode forms a circuit of finite resistance and negligible inductance. A magnet entering the coil induces an emf in the diode's forward direction. What happens when the same magnet is withdrawn along the same path?

- The emf reverses and the diode blocks current
- The emf and current keep their initial direction
- Current reverses with the same magnitude as during entry
- The emf vanishes because the diode is open

## Explanation

Emf direction follows the change of flux and reverses upon withdrawal. The reverse-biased diode prevents current even though an emf is present.
