The total change in the flux through a loop of resistance $R=2$ ohms has magnitude $10$ Wb. Neglecting self-induction, what is the magnitude of the charge that passes through a cross section of the loop?

- $5\,\mathrm{C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{C}$
- $10\,\mathrm{C}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Integrating Ohm’s law together with Faraday’s law gives $|q|=\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_B|}{R}$. The total change in flux is given, so the magnitude of the charge is $\dfrac{10}{2}=5$ coulombs.
