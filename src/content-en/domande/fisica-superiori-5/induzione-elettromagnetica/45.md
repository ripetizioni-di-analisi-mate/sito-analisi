Two flux traces are parallel throughout an interval, with the second always 4 mWb above the first. How do the emfs compare?

- They are equal at every instant
- They always have opposite signs
- The second exceeds the first by 4 mV
- The second is always zero

## Explanation

Adding a constant to flux leaves its derivative unchanged. Faraday's law thus gives both traces the same emf.
