Along a circle of radius $3$ m, the induced electric field is tangential and has uniform magnitude. If the emf has magnitude $42\pi$ V, what is the field magnitude in volts per meter?

- $14 \pi$
- $14$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

The circulation is the field magnitude multiplied by the circumference. Dividing the given emf by $2\pi r$ gives the field magnitude, $7$.
