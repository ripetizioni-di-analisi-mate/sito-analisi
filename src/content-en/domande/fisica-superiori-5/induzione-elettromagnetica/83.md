A copper loop is replaced by an identical-shape aluminium loop. Both see the same varying flux with $R_{\rm Al}>R_{\rm Cu}$. Which dissipates more power at equal emf, with negligible self-inductance?

- Equal shape forces equal power
- The copper loop
- Both powers are always zero
- The aluminium loop

## Explanation

At imposed emf the dissipated power is $\mathcal E^2/R$. Copper's lower resistance gives greater power.
