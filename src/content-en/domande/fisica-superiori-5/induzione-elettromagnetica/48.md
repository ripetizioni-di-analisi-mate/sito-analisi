A loop of area $90$ cm² is in a uniform field of $4$ T. The normal makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. What is the flux in milliwebers?

- $36$
- $18 \sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{9}{500}$
- $18$

## Explanation

The flux is $BA\cos\theta$. To find the area in square meters, divide the given area by ten thousand. The cosine of sixty degrees is one-half. The result is $18$.
