During one cycle flux linkage rises and then returns exactly to its starting value. For a purely resistive circuit, which pair describes net charge and dissipated heat?

- Both zero
- Zero net charge, positive heat
- Positive charge, zero heat
- Negative heat, negative charge

## Explanation

Net charge is proportional to overall flux change and therefore vanishes. Heat integrates $RI^2$ and remains positive even if the current reverses.
