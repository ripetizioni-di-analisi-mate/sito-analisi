The flux linkage of a circuit of resistance $3.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ increases uniformly from $0$ to $12\,\mathrm{mWb}$ between $t = 0$ and $t = 0.40\,\mathrm{s}$, remains constant until $t = 0.60\,\mathrm{s}$, and then decreases uniformly to $-2.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$ at $t = 0.80\,\mathrm{s}$. Neglecting self-inductance, in which interval is current magnitude largest, and approximately what is it?

- In the middle, $12\,\mathrm{mA}$
- In the first, $30\,\mathrm{mA}$
- In the last, $7\,\mathrm{mA}$
- In the last, $23\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The largest current magnitude occurs where flux has the steepest absolute slope. Neglecting self-inductance, $i=-(1/R)\,d\Phi/dt$ gives $-10\,\mathrm{mA}$, zero and about $+23\,\mathrm{mA}$ in order.
