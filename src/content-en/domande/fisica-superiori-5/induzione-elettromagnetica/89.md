A search coil is to drive a voltmeter responding from $1\,\mathrm{mV}$ upward. One turn gives $0.15\,\mathrm{mV}$ in the test. What is the smallest number of identical aiding series turns that exceeds threshold?

- $7$
- $10$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

Aiding turn voltages add. Six give $0.90\,\mathrm{mV}$, whereas seven give $1.05\,\mathrm{mV}$ and exceed the threshold.
