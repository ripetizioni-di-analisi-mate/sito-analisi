A rod slides at $3$ m/s along two rails $4$ m apart in a perpendicular field of $7$ T. The circuit resistance is $28$ Ω. What is the current magnitude in amperes?

- $\dfrac{3}{28}$
- $28$
- $3$
- $84$

## Explanation

First find the motional emf by multiplying the field magnitude, the length, and the speed. Then divide by the circuit resistance. The result is $3$.
