A magnet passes through equal-sized copper and plastic tubes without touching them. Which tube provides greater eddy-current braking and why?

- Plastic, because it insulates charge
- The less conductive tube
- They are equal for equal lengths
- Copper, where currents can circulate around the tube

## Explanation

The passing magnet changes flux through rings of material. A conductor supports currents opposing motion; in the insulator such currents are negligible.
