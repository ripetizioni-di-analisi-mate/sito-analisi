Along a circle of radius $2$ m, the induced electric field is tangential and has uniform magnitude. If the emf has magnitude $20\pi$ V, what is the field magnitude in volts per meter?

- $5$
- $10$
- $-5$
- $10 \pi$

## Explanation

The circulation is the field magnitude multiplied by the circumference. Dividing the given emf by $2\pi r$ gives the field magnitude, $5$.
