Induced voltage is sampled every $1\,\mathrm{ms}$. A very short pulse rises and falls entirely between two samples. What follows?

- Flux must have been constant
- Current is infinite at sampling times
- Two zero readings do not rule out an intervening flux change
- The coil must have zero resistance

## Explanation

Samples describe only the measured instants. A missed pulse can have nonzero area and correspond to a flux change.
