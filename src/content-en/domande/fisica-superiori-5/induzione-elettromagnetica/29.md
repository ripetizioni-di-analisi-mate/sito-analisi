A coil of $50$ turns of area $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ rotates with constant angular velocity $100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about an axis perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of $0.40\,\mathrm{T}$. What is the magnitude of the induced emf at the instant when the normal to the turns makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the field?

- $20\,\mathrm{V}$
- $40\,\mathrm{V}$
- $35\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

If $\theta$ is the angle between the normal and the field, the flux linkage is $N B A\cos\theta$ with $\theta = \omega t$. By the **Faraday-Neumann-Lenz law** the emf is $N B A \omega \sin\theta$, therefore it is proportional to the sine of the angle. The maximum value is $50 \cdot 0.40 \cdot 0.020 \cdot 100\,\mathrm{V} = 40\,\mathrm{V}$, and with $\sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$ one obtains
$$
|\mathcal{E}| = 40 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{V} = 20\,\mathrm{V} .
$$
The value $35\,\mathrm{V}$ is obtained with the cosine, which describes the flux and not the emf.
