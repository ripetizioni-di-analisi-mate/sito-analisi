The flux through a single loop changes by $3$ Wb in $0.5$ s. What is the magnitude of the average emf?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $3\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Faraday’s law gives the magnitude of the average emf as $|\mathcal E|=\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_B|}{\Delta t}$. The given change is for one loop. The result is $|\mathcal E|=\dfrac{3}{0.5}=6$ volts.
