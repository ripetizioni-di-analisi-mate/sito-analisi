A circular loop of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ is in a uniform magnetic field of $0.30\,\mathrm{T}$. The normal to the loop makes an angle of $40^\circ$ with the field. Calculate the flux linkage of the loop.

- $1.5\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $18\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{mWb}$

## Explanation

Compute the circular area using the radius in metres. Only the component of the field along the oriented normal contributes to flux. $\Phi_B = B\,\pi r^2 \cos 40^\circ \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
