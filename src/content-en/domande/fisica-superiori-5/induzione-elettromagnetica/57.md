A stationary conducting ring has a small gap and a changing field links its contour. What is expected compared with an otherwise identical closed ring?

- Faraday's law stops applying
- The current doubles
- A gap voltage can appear without a sustained conduction current around the ring
- The induced electric field vanishes everywhere

## Explanation

Electric-field circulation still follows the flux change. The gap interrupts conduction and allows charge to gather on its edges.
