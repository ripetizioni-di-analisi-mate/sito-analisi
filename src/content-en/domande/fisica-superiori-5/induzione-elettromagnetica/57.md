The flux linkage of a coil changes by $36$ mWb. The total resistance is $9$ Ω and self-induction is negligible. What magnitude of charge passes through the circuit, in millicoulombs?

- $324$
- $4$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

Integrate the current over time. Ohm’s law and Faraday’s law give the magnitude of the change in flux linkage divided by the resistance. The result is $4$.
