A coil in a spatially uniform changing field is accidentally deformed. To distinguish field changes from area changes, a rigid coil of known area and turn count is placed alongside it with the same orientation. The field is normal to both. Which measurement isolates the field change?

- The resistance of the deformable coil alone
- The sum of the instantaneous coil currents for any external circuit
- The integrated voltage of the rigid coil divided by its turn count and area, with the Faraday sign
- The difference between the final areas alone

## Explanation

For the rigid coil normal to the field, $N$ and $A$ are constant. Integrating $\mathcal E=-NA\,dB/dt$ gives $\Delta B=-\dfrac{1}{NA}\int\mathcal E\,dt$. The deformation of the other coil does not enter this measurement.
