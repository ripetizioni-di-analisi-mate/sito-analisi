A magnet and ring translate together slowly with fixed relative distance and orientation. Other fields are negligible. What does the flux rule predict for the ring?

- Current proportional to distance travelled
- A positive emf regardless of relative motion
- Continually growing net charge
- No net emf

## Explanation

The field distribution relative to the ring does not change. Its linked flux stays constant throughout their common motion.
