The flux linkage of a coil changes by $21$ mWb. The total resistance is $7$ Ω and self-induction is negligible. What magnitude of charge passes through the circuit, in millicoulombs?

- $21$
- $3$
- $147$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

Integrate the current over time. Ohm’s law and Faraday’s law give the magnitude of the change in flux linkage divided by the resistance. The result is $3$.
