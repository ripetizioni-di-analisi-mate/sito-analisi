The flux through a single loop changes by $9$ Wb in $0.5$ s. What is the magnitude of the average emf?

- $18\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $9\,\mathrm{V}$
- $36\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Faraday’s law gives the magnitude of the average emf as $|\mathcal E|=\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_B|}{\Delta t}$. The given change is for one loop. The result is $|\mathcal E|=\dfrac{9}{0.5}=18$ volts.
