A rigid circuit moves at $5$ m/s without rotating and remains entirely within a uniform, constant field of $2$ T. What is the net emf in volts?

- $10$
- $2$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

The area, orientation, and field remain the same throughout the motion. The flux linkage is constant and the net emf is zero. The result is $0$.
