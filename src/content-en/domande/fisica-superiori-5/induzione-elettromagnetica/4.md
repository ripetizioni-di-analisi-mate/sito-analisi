A magnet's north pole moves away along the axis of a closed conducting ring initially carrying no current. Which pole does the near face show to the magnet during withdrawal, and what mechanical effect follows?

- No pole, because the distance is increasing
- South pole, attracting the magnet
- South pole, repelling the magnet
- North pole, repelling the magnet

## Explanation

The magnet's flux through the ring decreases. The induced current tends to maintain it. Seen from the magnet it is clockwise, giving a south pole facing the magnet's north pole.
