A rod of length $0.5$ m moves at $2$ m/s in a field of $B=0.2$ T. The rod, its velocity, and the field are mutually perpendicular. What is the magnitude of the motional emf?

- $2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $20\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For this orientation, the magnitude of the motional emf is $|\mathcal E|=B\ell v$. Multiply $0.2$, $0.5$, and $2$. The magnitude in volts is therefore $0.2$.
