A magnetized metal string vibrates near a guitar pickup. For small displacements the flux varies linearly with position. When is the sinusoidal signal magnitude greatest?

- Equally at every instant
- At equilibrium crossing, where speed is largest
- At the turning points, where speed is zero
- Only with the string stationary

## Explanation

For flux linear in displacement, its derivative is proportional to string velocity. Speed is greatest at the centre of the oscillation.
