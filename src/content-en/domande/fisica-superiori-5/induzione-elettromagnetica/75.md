A rigid circuit moves at $9$ m/s without rotating and remains entirely within a uniform, constant field of $4$ T. What is the net emf in volts?

- $0$
- $36$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

The area, orientation, and field remain the same throughout the motion. The flux linkage is constant and the net emf is zero. The result is $0$.
