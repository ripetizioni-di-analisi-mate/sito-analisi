A loop has area $0.1$ square meters, and its normal points in the same direction as a uniform magnetic field of $B=5$ T. What is the signed flux?

- $50\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $5\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0\,\mathrm{Wb}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{Wb}$

## Explanation

The flux is $\Phi_B=BA\cos\theta$. The normal and field point in the same direction, so $\cos\theta=1$. The flux is $\Phi_B=5\cdot0.1=0.5$ webers.
