The flux through a single loop changes by $2$ Wb in $0.5$ s. What is the magnitude of the average emf?

- $1\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Faraday’s law gives the magnitude of the average emf as $|\mathcal E|=\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_B|}{\Delta t}$. The given change is for one loop. The result is $|\mathcal E|=\dfrac{2}{0.5}=4$ volts.
