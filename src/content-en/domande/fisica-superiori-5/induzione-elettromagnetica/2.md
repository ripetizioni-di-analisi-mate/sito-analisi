A coil of $80$ turns, each of area $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, is perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field. The field increases uniformly from $0$ to $0.60\,\mathrm{T}$ in $50\,\mathrm{ms}$. Calculate the magnitude of the emf induced in the coil.

- $0.12\,\mathrm V$
- $2.4\,\mathrm V$
- $24\,\mathrm V$
- $0.030\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The voltages of the 80 turns in the same sense add. For a linear field ramp the derivative equals total change divided by duration. $|\mathcal{E}| = \dfrac{N S\,\Delta B}{\Delta t} = 2.4\,\mathrm{V}$.
