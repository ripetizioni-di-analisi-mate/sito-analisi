A moving-rod generator produces a current of $2$ A with an emf of $5$ V. At a constant speed of $3$ m/s, what is the magnitude in newtons of the external force required if all the power is converted to heat?

- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $30$

## Explanation

The mechanical power supplied by the external force equals the electrical power dissipated, $\mathcal E I$. Divide by the rod’s speed. The result is $\dfrac{10}{3}$.
