The flux through each turn of a coil with $20$ turns changes from $5$ to $-5$ mWb in $3$ ms. What is the magnitude of the average emf in volts?

- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{200}{3}$
- $\dfrac{100}{3}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the change per turn is twice the initial flux. Multiply by the number of turns and divide by the time. The flux and time are expressed with the same metric prefix. The result is $\dfrac{200}{3}$.
