Two rectangular emf pulses give identical flux changes in a resistor. The second lasts twice as long as the first. What is the ratio of heat dissipated, second to first?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1/2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Equal areas require half the amplitude for the second emf. Heat scales with squared amplitude times duration, so the ratio is $(1/2)^2\cdot2=1/2$.
