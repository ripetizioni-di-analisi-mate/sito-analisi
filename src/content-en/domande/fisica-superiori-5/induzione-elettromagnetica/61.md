The same magnet enters two coils with identical geometry and turns but resistances of 2 Ω and 8 Ω. Path and speed are imposed identically. How do their emfs compare at corresponding instants?

- The second is one quarter
- The second is four times larger
- They are equal
- The first is zero

## Explanation

The emf due to the magnet depends on external flux history, identical for both coils. Resistance changes the resulting current.
