A square loop of side $0.20\,\mathrm m$ enters a region of normal field $0.30\,\mathrm T$ at constant speed. It carries $0.90\,\mathrm A$ while entering. What net magnetic force along the motion acts when only its leading side lies entirely inside the field?

- $54\,\mathrm{mN}$ along the motion
- Zero force
- $54\,\mathrm{mN}$ against the motion
- $108\,\mathrm{mN}$ against the motion

## Explanation

The lateral sections experience opposing transverse forces. The longitudinal force originates on the leading side and has magnitude $I\ell B$. Lenz's law makes it oppose entry.
