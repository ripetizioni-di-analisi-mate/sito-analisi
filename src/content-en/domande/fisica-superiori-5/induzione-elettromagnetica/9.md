A rod of length $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ slides along two rails connected by a $0.50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ resistor in a uniform magnetic field of $0.40\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to the plane of the rails. The rod moves away from the resistor throughout. Neglect the resistance of the rails and rod and the circuit's self-inductance. A charge of $0.80\,\mathrm{C}$ passes through the resistor. Determine the distance traveled by the rod.

- $0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $10\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The flux increases by $B\ell x$. The total charge gives this increase divided by resistance, independently of the speed history. $x = \dfrac{q R}{B \ell} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
