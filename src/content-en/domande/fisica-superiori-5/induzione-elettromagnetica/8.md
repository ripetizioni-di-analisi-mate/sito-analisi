A 50-turn coil of radius $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and resistance $5.0\,\Omega$ turns through $180^\circ$ in a $0.25\,\mathrm T$ field initially along its normal. Why is the total charge magnitude about $6.3\,\mathrm{mC}$ instead of $3.1\,\mathrm{mC}$? Neglect self-inductance.

- Rotation doubles the area
- Flux linkage goes from $+NBA$ to $-NBA$
- Each turn makes two complete revolutions
- Resistance halves during rotation

## Explanation

Oriented flux reverses sign. Final minus initial linkage is $-2NBA$, so the charge magnitude is $2NBA/R$.
