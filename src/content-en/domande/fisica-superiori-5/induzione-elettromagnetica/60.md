A search coil of known area is removed completely from a uniform normal field. To estimate the initial field with a ballistic galvanometer, is peak current or total charge needed?

- Only the pointer's fall time
- Final charge on the magnet
- Peak current alone with no other data
- Total charge, also knowing resistance and turn count

## Explanation

The current integral measures the flux-linkage change divided by resistance. The peak also depends on withdrawal speed.
