A galvanometer briefly deflects as a magnet enters a coil. Holding the magnet still inside makes the pointer return to zero. Why?

- The coil has become permanently charged
- Resistance has become infinite
- Flux remains nonzero but stops changing
- The magnet necessarily loses its poles

## Explanation

With the magnet still, the configuration is stationary. After the transient, flux derivative vanishes and no emf sustains current.
