A loop of area $0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field that decreases according to $B(t) = 0.80\,e^{-\frac{t}{2}}$, with $B$ in teslas and $t$ in seconds. What is the magnitude of the induced emf after two seconds?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{mV}$

## Explanation

At constant area the emf depends on the derivative of the field. Differentiating the exponential brings in the reciprocal of the time constant. $\mathcal{E}(t) = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,e^{-\frac{t}{2}}$ (in volts), $\mathcal{E}(2.0\,\mathrm{s}) \approx 1.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{V}$.
