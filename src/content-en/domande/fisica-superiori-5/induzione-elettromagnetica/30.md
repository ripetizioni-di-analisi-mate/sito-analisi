The total change in the flux through a loop of resistance $R=2$ ohms has magnitude $7$ Wb. Neglecting self-induction, what is the magnitude of the charge that passes through a cross section of the loop?

- $14\,\mathrm{C}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{C}$
- $7\,\mathrm{C}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Integrating Ohm’s law together with Faraday’s law gives $|q|=\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_B|}{R}$. The total change in flux is given, so the magnitude of the charge is $\dfrac{7}{2}=3.5$ coulombs.
