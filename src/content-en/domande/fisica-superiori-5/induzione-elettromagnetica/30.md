A coil of $20$ turns of area $0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$ rotates with constant angular velocity $50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in a uniform magnetic field of $0.50\,\mathrm{T}$, perpendicular to the axis of rotation. The coil is closed on a total resistance of $2.0\,\Omega$, and self-induction is negligible. What is the maximum value of the torque of the magnetic forces opposing the rotation?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $0.13\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$

## Explanation

With $\theta = \omega t$ the angle between the normal and the field, the emf is $N B A \omega \sin\theta$ and the current is $i = \dfrac{N B A \omega \sin\theta}{R}$. The coil carrying current experiences a torque of magnitude $N i A B \sin\theta$, so
$$
M = \frac{(N B A)^2 \omega}{R} \sin^2\theta .
$$
The torque is maximum for $\sin\theta = 1$, that is, when the plane of the turns is parallel to the field. Since $N B A = 20 \cdot 0.50 \cdot 0.010\,\mathrm{Wb} = 0.10\,\mathrm{Wb}$, one obtains $M_{\max} = \dfrac{0.10^2 \cdot 50}{2.0}\,\mathrm{N \cdot m} = 0.25\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$. The value $0.13\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$ is the average torque over a period.
