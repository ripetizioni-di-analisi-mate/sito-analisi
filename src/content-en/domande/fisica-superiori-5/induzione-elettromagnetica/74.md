A dynamic microphone attaches a coil to a moving diaphragm. During a note the coil oscillates in a magnet gap. At the same position, what reverses if the diaphragm reverses direction?

- The permanent magnet polarity
- The polarity of the induced voltage
- Coil resistance
- Turn count

## Explanation

Passing through the same configuration in the opposite direction reverses velocity and the flux derivative. The emf therefore reverses sign.
