A rigid circuit moves at $7$ m/s without rotating and remains entirely within a uniform, constant field of $3$ T. What is the net emf in volts?

- $0$
- $7$
- $21$
- $3$

## Explanation

The area, orientation, and field remain the same throughout the motion. The flux linkage is constant and the net emf is zero. The result is $0$.
