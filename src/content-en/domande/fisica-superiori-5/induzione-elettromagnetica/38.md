Two identical windings are rigidly fixed at right angles and rotate together, with both normals and the uniform field in the plane of rotation. The first produces a sinusoidal emf. Which observation of the second winding reveals reversal of rotation at unchanged speed magnitude?

- Both frequencies double.
- The second voltage becomes constant.
- The second switches from leading the first by a quarter period to lagging by a quarter period, or vice versa.
- The rms voltage of the first winding goes to zero.

## Explanation

For windings in quadrature a peak in the second occurs one quarter-turn before or after a peak in the first. Reversing rotation reverses the time order of the peaks.
