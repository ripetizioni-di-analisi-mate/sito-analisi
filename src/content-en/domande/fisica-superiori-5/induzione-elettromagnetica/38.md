A closed loop enters a region of uniform magnetic field at constant speed. The field points out of the page. What net magnetic force acts along the line of motion as the loop enters the region?

- A force in the direction of motion.
- A force opposite to the direction of motion.
- Zero force because the velocity is constant.
- A force directed out of the page.

## Explanation

The area of the loop inside the field increases, inducing a current. The force along the line of motion opposes entry, in accordance with Lenz’s law.
