A closed superconducting ring of constant inductance $L$ and zero resistance initially has zero current and no external flux. A positive external flux $\Phi$ is slowly applied through its hole. In the ideal circuit model, what persistent current forms, taking positive current in the sense associated with positive flux?

- $-\Phi/L$
- $0$
- $+\Phi/L$
- $-2\Phi/L$

## Explanation

Zero resistance gives $L\,di/dt=-d\Phi_{\rm ext}/dt$. Total flux $Li+\Phi_{\rm ext}$ retains its initial zero value even after the external flux stops changing.
