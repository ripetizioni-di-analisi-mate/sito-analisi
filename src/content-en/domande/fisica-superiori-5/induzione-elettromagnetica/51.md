Each of the first 8 turns links a 3 mWb change, while each of the other 2 links only 1 mWb. All are oriented alike. What is the total flux-linkage change?

- $4\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $26\,\mathrm{mWb}$

## Explanation

With nonuniform field the first turn's flux cannot be applied to every turn. The sum is $8\cdot3+2\cdot1=26\,\mathrm{mWb}$.
