A rod of length $3$ m slides along rails at $7$ m/s in a field of $4$ T. The rod, its velocity, and the field are mutually perpendicular. What is the emf in volts?

- $\dfrac{28}{3}$
- $\dfrac{12}{7}$
- $21$
- $84$

## Explanation

The motional emf is $B\ell v$ when the field, the rod, and the velocity are mutually perpendicular. Multiply the three values in SI units. The result is $84$.
