Two parallel rods close a rectangle on two rails. They move in the same direction at the same speed in a uniform normal field. What determines the net current direction?

- There is no current because the two rod emfs cancel
- Current is always counterclockwise
- Only the trailing rod matters
- Only the leading rod matters

## Explanation

The rods stay equally far apart and their enclosed area is constant. Along the circuit the equal motional contributions have opposite signs.
