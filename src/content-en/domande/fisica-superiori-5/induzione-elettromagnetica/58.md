A uniform field directed out of the page passes through a loop. Its magnitude changes from $5$ to $7$ mT. In which direction does the induced current flow as seen by an observer facing the page?

- The current remains zero.
- Counterclockwise.
- The direction alternates periodically.
- Clockwise.

## Explanation

The field out of the page is increasing. By Lenz’s law, the current produces a field into the page, which corresponds to a clockwise current.
