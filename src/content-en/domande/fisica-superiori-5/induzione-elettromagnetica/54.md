The flux through a loop is $\Phi(t)=4t^2+9t$ in webers. What is the signed emf at $t=5$ s, in volts?

- $-29$
- $49$
- $-49$
- $-40$

## Explanation

The signed emf is the negative of the time derivative of the flux. Differentiate the polynomial and then substitute the specified time. The result is $-49$.
