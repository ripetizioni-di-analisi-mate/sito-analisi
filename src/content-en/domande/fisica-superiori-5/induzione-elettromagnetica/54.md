A flat loop expands in a decreasing normal field. Its area doubles while $BA$ stays constant at every instant. What total emf is measured?

- Zero
- An emf proportional only to perimeter
- A constant positive emf
- A constant negative emf

## Explanation

Area growth and field decrease cancel in the flux. Since the derivative of the product vanishes, the total emf vanishes too.
