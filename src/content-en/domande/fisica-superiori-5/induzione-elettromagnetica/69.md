A rod slides at $4$ m/s along two rails $5$ m apart in a perpendicular field of $9$ T. The circuit resistance is $45$ Ω. What is the current magnitude in amperes?

- $\dfrac{4}{45}$
- $4$
- $180$
- $45$

## Explanation

First find the motional emf by multiplying the field magnitude, the length, and the speed. Then divide by the circuit resistance. The result is $4$.
