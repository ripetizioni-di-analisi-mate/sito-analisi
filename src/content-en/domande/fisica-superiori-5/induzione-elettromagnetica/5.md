In a stationary 120-turn coil of turn area $15\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, the normal field varies as $B(t)=0.050t^2$, with $B$ in teslas and $t$ in seconds. Taking the normal along the field, which law describes the emf in volts?

- $-0.009t^2$
- $+0.018t$
- $-0.018t$
- $-0.018$

## Explanation

The flux linkage is $NAB(t)$. Differentiating and reversing the sign gives $\mathcal E=-NA\,(0.100t)$, whose magnitude grows linearly in time.
