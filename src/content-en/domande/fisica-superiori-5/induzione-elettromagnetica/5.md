The total change in the flux through a loop of resistance $R=2$ ohms has magnitude $2$ Wb. Neglecting self-induction, what is the magnitude of the charge that passes through a cross section of the loop?

- $1\,\mathrm{C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Integrating Ohm’s law together with Faraday’s law gives $|q|=\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_B|}{R}$. The total change in flux is given, so the magnitude of the charge is $\dfrac{2}{2}=1$ coulombs.
