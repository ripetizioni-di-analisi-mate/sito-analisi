The flux through each turn of a coil with $10$ turns increases by $\dfrac{5}{100}$ Wb in $1$ s. What is the magnitude of the average emf?

- $0.05\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $50\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For $N$ identical turns the flux linkage changes by $N\Delta\Phi_1$. Faraday’s law gives $|\mathcal E|=N\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_1|}{\Delta t}$, so the magnitude is $|\mathcal E|=10\cdot\dfrac{5}{100}=0.5$ volts.
