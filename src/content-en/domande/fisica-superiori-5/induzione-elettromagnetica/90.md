A coil has $4$ turns, each of area $9$ m². A perpendicular field changes from zero to $5$ T in $5$ s. What is the magnitude of the average emf in volts?

- $180$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{36}{5}$
- $36$

## Explanation

The field changes at an average rate of one tesla per second. Multiply the rate of change of the field by the area of one turn and the number of turns. The result is $36$.
