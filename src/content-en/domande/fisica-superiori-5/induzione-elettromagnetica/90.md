In a Faraday experiment only the voltmeter leads are swapped, leaving coil orientation and motion unchanged. Which quantity reverses sign?

- Dissipated heat
- The magnet's field
- Physical flux with respect to the same normal
- The signed meter reading

## Explanation

The meter displays the first terminal's voltage relative to the second. Swapping them reverses its sign convention while the physical process remains the same.
