A vertical rod moves horizontally in a horizontal field parallel to its velocity. What charge separation along the rod is produced by the motion alone?

- Alternately at either end
- Positive charge at the top
- Positive charge at the bottom
- None

## Explanation

The magnetic contribution on carriers is proportional to $\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$. Parallel vectors give zero, so motion does not separate charges.
