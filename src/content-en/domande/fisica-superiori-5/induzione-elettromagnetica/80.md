A conducting plate oscillates in a field of $3$ T and is slowed by eddy currents. Which change reduces the braking effect if all other conditions remain the same?

- Increasing the speed at which the plate enters the field.
- Replacing it with a solid plate of lower resistivity.
- Increasing the magnetic field.
- Cutting slots that interrupt the current paths.

## Explanation

The slots interrupt the large closed paths that currents follow in the plate. The remaining loops are smaller and the eddy currents decrease.
