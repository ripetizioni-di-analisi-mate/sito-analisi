A rod slides at $2$ m/s along two rails $3$ m apart in a perpendicular field of $5$ T. The circuit resistance is $15$ Ω. What is the current magnitude in amperes?

- $15$
- $30$
- $\dfrac{2}{15}$
- $2$

## Explanation

First find the motional emf by multiplying the field magnitude, the length, and the speed. Then divide by the circuit resistance. The result is $2$.
