A circuit emf is $+3\,\mathrm V$ for $2\,\mathrm{ms}$, then $-1\,\mathrm V$ for $4\,\mathrm{ms}$. What is the total change in flux linkage?

- $+10\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $+2\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{mWb}$

## Explanation

The signed area under the emf is $3\cdot2-1\cdot4=2\,\mathrm{V\,ms}$. Flux change has the opposite sign, giving $-2\,\mathrm{mWb}$.
