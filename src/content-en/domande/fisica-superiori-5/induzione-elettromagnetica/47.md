A loop of area $70$ cm² is in a uniform field of $3$ T. The normal makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. What is the flux in milliwebers?

- $\dfrac{21}{2}$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{21 \sqrt{3}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2000}$

## Explanation

The flux is $BA\cos\theta$. To find the area in square meters, divide the given area by ten thousand. The cosine of sixty degrees is one-half. The result is $\dfrac{21}{2}$.
