A rod of mass $15\,\mathrm{g}$ and length $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ slides without friction on two vertical rails connected at the top by a $0.25\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ resistor. The rails are in a uniform horizontal magnetic field of $0.50\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to their plane. The resistances of the rails and rod are negligible. Calculate the terminal speed of the rod. Use $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At terminal speed magnetic braking balances weight. Since current is $B\ell v/R$, the braking force is $B^2\ell^2 v/R$. $v_{\text{lim}} = \dfrac{m g R}{B^2 \ell^2} \approx 1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
