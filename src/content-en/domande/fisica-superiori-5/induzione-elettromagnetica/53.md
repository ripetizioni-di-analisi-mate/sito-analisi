A flexible thin loop is squeezed to a smaller area without changing its perimeter. In a constant normal field, which quantity explains the transient current?

- A $180^\circ$ turn of the normal
- The change in enclosed area
- The change in wire length
- The change in total metal charge

## Explanation

Flux is $BA$ and changes with area even at fixed field and orientation. Faraday's law predicts an emf during deformation.
