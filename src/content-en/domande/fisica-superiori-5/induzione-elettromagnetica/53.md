The flux through a loop is $\Phi(t)=3t^2+7t$ in webers. What is the signed emf at $t=4$ s, in volts?

- $-19$
- $-24$
- $31$
- $-31$

## Explanation

The signed emf is the negative of the time derivative of the flux. Differentiate the polynomial and then substitute the specified time. The result is $-31$.
