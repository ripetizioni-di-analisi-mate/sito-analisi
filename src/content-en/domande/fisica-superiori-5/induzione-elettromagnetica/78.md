A stationary coil has its normal along $x$. The field has constant $B_x$ and varying $B_y$. Can its flux change?

- No, only the normal component $B_x$ contributes
- Yes, for every change in $B_y$
- Only if the coil is square
- Yes, if $B_y$ exceeds $B_x$

## Explanation

The dot product of uniform field and area selects $B_x$. The in-plane component can change without changing flux.
