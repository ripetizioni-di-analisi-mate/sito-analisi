A loop is at rest in a uniform field of $4$ T. Which change generates an emf?

- Rotating it about its normal.
- Heating it while keeping its area and orientation constant.
- Moving it without rotating it within the uniform field.
- Rotating it to change the angle between the normal and the field.

## Explanation

For a fixed field, the flux changes if the projection of the loop’s area onto a plane perpendicular to the field changes. Rotating the loop so that its normal makes a different angle with the field changes this projection.
