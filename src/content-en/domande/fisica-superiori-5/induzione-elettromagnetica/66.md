A solid copper disk crosses a magnetic-field boundary and warms up. Where does its thermal energy come from if there is no electrical source?

- From a necessary increase in magnet mass
- From disappearing magnetic poles
- From mechanical work or the disk's kinetic energy
- From charge creation in copper

## Explanation

Eddy currents dissipate Joule heat and create drag. If motion is maintained, external work supplies the dissipated energy.
