A rod of length $4$ m slides along rails at $9$ m/s in a field of $5$ T. The rod, its velocity, and the field are mutually perpendicular. What is the emf in volts?

- $\dfrac{45}{4}$
- $\dfrac{20}{9}$
- $180$
- $36$

## Explanation

The motional emf is $B\ell v$ when the field, the rod, and the velocity are mutually perpendicular. Multiply the three values in SI units. The result is $180$.
