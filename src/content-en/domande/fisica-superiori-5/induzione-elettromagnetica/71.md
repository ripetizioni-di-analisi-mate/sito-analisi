Two identical conducting disks brake in the same field. One is hot and has larger resistivity; speed and field are equal. In the weak-current limit what happens to its damping?

- It is always identical
- It becomes forward thrust
- It increases
- It decreases

## Explanation

Motional emf is set by the field and motion. Higher resistance reduces induced currents and thus the magnetic braking force.
