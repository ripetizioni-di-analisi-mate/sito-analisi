A sliding rod in a uniform field supplies two equal resistors in parallel. If the ideal circuit generates 6 V and each resistor is 3 Ω, what current flows through the rod?

- $4\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $6\,\mathrm A$
- $1\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

Each branch receives 6 V and carries $6/3=2\,\mathrm A$. The two branch currents add in the rod.
