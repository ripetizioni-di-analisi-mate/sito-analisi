A rod of length $2$ m slides along rails at $5$ m/s in a field of $3$ T. The rod, its velocity, and the field are mutually perpendicular. What is the emf in volts?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $30$

## Explanation

The motional emf is $B\ell v$ when the field, the rod, and the velocity are mutually perpendicular. Multiply the three values in SI units. The result is $30$.
