A wheel with metal spokes rotates in an axial field. Two contacts measure voltage from hub to rim. Why do identical spokes not add their emfs like series windings?

- The hub cancels the field
- All connect the same two nodes in parallel
- Spokes always intersect zero flux
- The wheel has infinite resistance

## Explanation

Each spoke connects hub and rim directly and generates the same potential difference. Multiple parallel branches do not multiply voltage between those nodes.
