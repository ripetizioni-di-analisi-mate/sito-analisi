A magnet approaches a closed loop. When it is withdrawn along the same path at equal speed, which applied force is needed to maintain withdrawal, neglecting self-inductance?

- No force because the magnet is permanent
- A force toward the loop to oppose repulsion
- A force in the withdrawal direction to oppose attraction
- A force always at right angles to the path

## Explanation

As the magnet withdraws the flux decreases and current tends to maintain it. The interaction becomes attractive and opposes the imposed motion.
