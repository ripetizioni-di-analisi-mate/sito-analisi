A uniform field through a loop points into the page and decreases from $9$ to $4$ mT. In which direction does the induced current flow as seen by an observer facing the page?

- Clockwise.
- The current remains zero.
- Counterclockwise.
- The direction changes halfway through the decrease.

## Explanation

The decrease in flux into the page induces a field that also points into the page. The right-hand rule associates this field with a clockwise current.
