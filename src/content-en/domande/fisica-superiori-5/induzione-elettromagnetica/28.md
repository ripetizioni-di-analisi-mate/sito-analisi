The flux through each turn of a coil with $10$ turns increases by $\dfrac{7}{100}$ Wb in $1$ s. What is the magnitude of the average emf?

- $0.07\,\mathrm{V}$
- $7\,\mathrm{V}$
- $70\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For $N$ identical turns the flux linkage changes by $N\Delta\Phi_1$. Faraday’s law gives $|\mathcal E|=N\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi_1|}{\Delta t}$, so the magnitude is $|\mathcal E|=10\cdot\dfrac{7}{100}=0.7$ volts.
