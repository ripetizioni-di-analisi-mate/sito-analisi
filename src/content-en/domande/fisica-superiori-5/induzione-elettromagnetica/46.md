A loop of area $50$ cm² is in a uniform field of $2$ T. The normal makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. What is the flux in milliwebers?

- $5$
- $5 \sqrt{3}$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{1}{200}$

## Explanation

The flux is $BA\cos\theta$. To find the area in square meters, divide the given area by ten thousand. The cosine of sixty degrees is one-half. The result is $5$.
