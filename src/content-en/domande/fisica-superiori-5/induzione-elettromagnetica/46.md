A rectangular loop is $0.30\,\mathrm m$ long and $0.20\,\mathrm m$ wide. The field is normal to the loop and increases linearly from zero to $0.40\,\mathrm T$ across its width. What is the flux through the loop?

- $0\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mWb}$
- $24\,\mathrm{mWb}$

## Explanation

Strips of equal width have equal areas. Since the field rises linearly, its area average is $0.20\,\mathrm T$. The flux is therefore $0.20\cdot0.30\cdot0.20=0.012\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
