The induced emf in a coil is known at every instant of a time interval. What other quantity is needed to determine the magnetic flux linked with the coil at every instant of the interval?

- The resistance of the coil
- The area of each turn
- The induced current at the initial instant
- The linked flux at the initial instant

## Explanation

By **Faraday-Neumann's law** the induced emf is the opposite of the rate of change of the linked flux. From the emf at every instant one therefore obtains only the change in flux from the initial instant. To obtain the flux at every instant this change must therefore be added to the **linked flux at the initial instant**.
