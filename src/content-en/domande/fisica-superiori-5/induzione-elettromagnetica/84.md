Along a circle of radius $4$ m, the induced electric field is tangential and has uniform magnitude. If the emf has magnitude $72\pi$ V, what is the field magnitude in volts per meter?

- $18$
- $36$
- $18 \pi$
- $9$

## Explanation

The circulation is the field magnitude multiplied by the circumference. Dividing the given emf by $2\pi r$ gives the field magnitude, $9$.
