A triangular loop enters a uniform-field region vertex first at constant speed. Why does the emf magnitude initially grow instead of staying constant?

- The width cut by the boundary increases
- The field grows at every fixed point
- The vertex speed increases
- Resistance must increase

## Explanation

The entering area grows with an increasingly wide instantaneous cross-section. Flux change equals $B$ times area change, so emf magnitude increases.
