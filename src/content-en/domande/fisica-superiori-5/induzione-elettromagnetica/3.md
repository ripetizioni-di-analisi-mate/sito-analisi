A square loop of side $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and resistance $0.10\,\Omega$ is completely pulled out of a uniform $0.20\,\mathrm T$ field initially normal to its plane. Neglecting self-inductance, what charge magnitude passes a cross section during removal?

- $0.45\,\mathrm{mC}$
- $45\,\mathrm{mC}$
- $300\,\mathrm{mC}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{mC}$

## Explanation

Integrate current over time. Faraday's and Ohm's laws give charge magnitude $\lvert\Delta\Phi\rvert/R=B\ell^2/R$, so the duration of the motion is unnecessary.
