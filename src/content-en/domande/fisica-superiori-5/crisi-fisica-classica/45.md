A photocell is connected to an ammeter. Increasing the positive collection voltage makes the current reach a plateau. What does this mean under steady ideal illumination?

- The work function is increasing without bound.
- Nearly all emitted electrons are now collected.
- All emitted electrons have zero initial energy.
- The voltage has made incident light monochromatic.

## Explanation

Above a certain voltage the collection rate is limited by the emission rate. Increasing voltage further changes arrival speeds without increasing the collected charge count.
