In Compton scattering, a photon’s wavelength increases by $2.4$ pm at an angle of $90^\circ$. What is its final wavelength if its initial wavelength is $10$ pm?

- $12.4\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $7.6\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{pm}$

## Explanation

In Compton scattering the final wavelength is the initial wavelength plus the given shift. The stated shift already accounts for the scattering angle. The result is $\lambda^\prime=10+2.4=12.4$ picometers.
