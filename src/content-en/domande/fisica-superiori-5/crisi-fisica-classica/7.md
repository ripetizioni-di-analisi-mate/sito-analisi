In Planck's convention $E_n=nhf$, an oscillator has $f=6.0\cdot10^{13}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. If $h=6.63\cdot10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$, what energy belongs to the state with $n=3$?

- $1.33\cdot10^{-20}\,\mathrm J$
- $7.96\cdot10^{-20}\,\mathrm J$
- $1.19\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$
- $3.98\cdot10^{-20}\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

One quantum has energy $hf=3.98\cdot10^{-20}\,\mathrm J$. The specified state contains three such quanta and therefore has energy $3hf$.
