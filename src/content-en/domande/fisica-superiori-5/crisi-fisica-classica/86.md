A material is illuminated in an intense regime allowing one electron to absorb two $1.8\,\mathrm{eV}$ photons in the same process. For $W=3.0\,\mathrm{eV}$, what kinematic upper limit follows for electrons emitted by that process?

- $3.6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-1.2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Here the absorbed energy is $2\cdot1.8=3.6\,\mathrm{eV}$. Subtracting the work function gives $0.6\,\mathrm{eV}$ even though either photon alone is below threshold.
