A photon of frequency $f$ has energy $5$ eV. What is the energy in eV of a photon of frequency $2f$?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$
- $20$

## Explanation

By Planck’s relation, the energy of a photon is Planck’s constant times its frequency. The ratio of the energies therefore equals the ratio of the frequencies, giving $10$.
