A voltmeter adds $+0.2\,\mathrm V$ to every stopping-potential reading. Which estimate stays unchanged when using the difference between two frequencies and their corresponding voltages?

- The vertical intercept.
- The horizontal intercept.
- The slope yielding $\dfrac{h}{e}$.
- Every individual value of $K_{\max}$ inferred as $eV_s$.

## Explanation

A constant additive error cancels in $\Delta V_s$. The line shifts vertically while $\dfrac{\Delta V_s}{\Delta f}$ remains correct.
