The emission spectrum of a blackbody peaks at $900$ nm. Use Wien’s constant $b=2.9\cdot10^{-3}$ m K. What is the temperature?

- $261000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{29000}{9}\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{2900}{9}\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{290000}{9}\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Wien’s law is $\lambda_{\max} T=b$. The given wavelength is $9\cdot10^{-7}$ meters, so the temperature is $T=\dfrac{2.9\cdot10^{-3}}{9\cdot10^{-7}}=\dfrac{29000}{9}$ kelvins.
