A lamp emits yellow light at optical power $2\,\mathrm W$; another emits blue light at the same power. Which emits more photons per second if blue photons have higher energy?

- Their photon rates must be equal.
- The yellow lamp.
- Neither emits photons because power is continuous.
- The blue lamp.

## Explanation

Photon rate is $\dfrac{P}{hf}$. At fixed power each lower-energy photon carries a smaller fraction of the energy emitted per second.
