A classical prescription assigns mean energy $kT$ to each field mode. Why is the problem particularly severe at high frequencies?

- The number of modes falls to zero.
- The number of modes per frequency interval grows without sufficient thermal suppression.
- A quantum becomes frequency independent in quantum theory.
- Temperature automatically rises as observation frequency rises.

## Explanation

Summing a fixed contribution over ever more modes predicts too much energy. Quantization makes high-frequency excitations costly.
