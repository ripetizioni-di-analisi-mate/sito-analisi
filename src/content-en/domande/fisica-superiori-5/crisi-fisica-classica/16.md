A photon has a wavelength of $500$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $62\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1.24\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $24.8\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $2.48\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{500}=\dfrac{62}{25}$ electronvolts.
