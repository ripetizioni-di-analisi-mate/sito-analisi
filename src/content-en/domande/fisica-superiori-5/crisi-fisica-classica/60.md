A photocell is illuminated at its ideal threshold frequency. Neglecting temperature and source linewidth, which maximum kinetic energy does the model assign to electrons just released?

- $2hf_0$
- Zero.
- $hf_0$
- $-hf_0$

## Explanation

At threshold the photon energy exactly equals the work function. No kinetic energy remains after escape from the surface.
