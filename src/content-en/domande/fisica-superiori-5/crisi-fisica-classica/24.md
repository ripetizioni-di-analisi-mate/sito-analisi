The absolute temperature of a blackbody changes from $T$ to $6T$, while its surface area stays the same. By what factor does the total emitted power increase?

- $6$
- $36$
- $24$
- $1296$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives $P=\sigma AT^4$. The area and the constant are unchanged. Multiplying the temperature by $6$ multiplies the power by $6^4=1296$.
