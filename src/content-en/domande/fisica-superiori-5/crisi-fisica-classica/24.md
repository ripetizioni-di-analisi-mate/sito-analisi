In a plot of emission per unit wavelength, which operation gives the power per unit surface emitted between $400$ and $700\,\mathrm{nm}$?

- Multiply temperature by the shortest wavelength.
- Take the slope at the peak.
- Read only the peak height.
- Find the area under the curve between those limits.

## Explanation

The ordinate is a density with respect to wavelength. Band emission is obtained by adding contributions from the small widths $d\lambda$.
