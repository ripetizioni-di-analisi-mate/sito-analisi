The emission spectrum of a blackbody peaks at $200$ nm. Use Wien’s constant $b=2.9\cdot10^{-3}$ m K. What is the temperature?

- $58000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $1450\,\mathrm{K}$
- $14500\,\mathrm{K}$
- $145000\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Wien’s law is $\lambda_{\max} T=b$. The given wavelength is $2\cdot10^{-7}$ meters, so the temperature is $T=\dfrac{2.9\cdot10^{-3}}{2\cdot10^{-7}}=\dfrac{29000}{2}$ kelvins.
