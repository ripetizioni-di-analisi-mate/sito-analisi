A blackbody of fixed area goes from $300\,\mathrm K$ to $600\,\mathrm K$. What are the final-to-initial ratios of emitted power and peak wavelength per unit wavelength, respectively?

- $2,\ \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $16,\ \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $16,\ 2$
- $8,\ \dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Power grows as $T^4$, whereas the peak wavelength is inversely proportional to $T$. Doubling temperature therefore produces factors of $2^4$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
