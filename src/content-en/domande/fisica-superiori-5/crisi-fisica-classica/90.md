For single-photon photoemission the measured values are $hf=(3.00\pm0.05)\,\mathrm{eV}$ and $W=(3.02\pm0.05)\,\mathrm{eV}$. What follows from these uncertainties alone, treated as possible ranges?

- The ranges overlap and do not establish with certainty which side of threshold applies.
- Planck's constant must be zero.
- Emission is certainly allowed for every permitted value.
- Emission is certainly forbidden for every allowed value.

## Explanation

Photon energy can reach $3.05\,\mathrm{eV}$ and work function can fall to $2.97\,\mathrm{eV}$. The opposite ordering is also possible, so the sign of the difference is uncertain.
