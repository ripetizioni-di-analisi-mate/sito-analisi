An oscillator in Planck’s model changes from level $n=4$ to level $n=9$. How many energy quanta $hf$ does it absorb in total?

- $9$
- $5$
- $4$
- $10$

## Explanation

The energy difference between adjacent levels is $hf$. The number of quanta equals the difference between the level indices, giving $5$.
