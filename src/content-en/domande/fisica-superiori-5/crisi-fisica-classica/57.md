A photon of frequency $f$ has energy $9$ eV. What is the energy in eV of a photon of frequency $4f$?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$
- $36$
- $144$

## Explanation

By Planck’s relation, the energy of a photon is Planck’s constant times its frequency. The ratio of the energies therefore equals the ratio of the frequencies, giving $36$.
