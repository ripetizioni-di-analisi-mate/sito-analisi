Frequency is swept until the first electrons are detected. The lamp also emits a small ultraviolet component in addition to the selected light. How can this affect the threshold estimate for the selected light?

- It always prevents emission from any metal.
- It may make photons negatively charged.
- It may make emission appear to begin at too low a selected frequency.
- It may lower the universal value of $h$.

## Explanation

Ultraviolet photons may exceed threshold while the selected band remains below it. The counter does not identify which frequency caused each emission.
