The absolute temperature of a blackbody changes from $T$ to $5T$, while its surface area stays the same. By what factor does the total emitted power increase?

- $5$
- $25$
- $625$
- $20$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives $P=\sigma AT^4$. The area and the constant are unchanged. Multiplying the temperature by $5$ multiplies the power by $5^4=625$.
