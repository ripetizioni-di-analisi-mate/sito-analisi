A room-temperature ideal blackbody in a dark room is invisible to the eye. What does a thermal infrared detector register?

- No radiation at any frequency.
- Infrared emission due to the body's temperature.
- Only reflected visible light.
- Only ultraviolet radiation.

## Explanation

An ideal blackbody both absorbs and radiates. At room temperature its spectrum peaks in the infrared and visible emission is extremely weak.
