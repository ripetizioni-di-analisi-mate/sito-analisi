A blackbody has a peak wavelength of $14$ µm. At what wavelength, in micrometers, does its spectrum peak if its absolute temperature doubles?

- $14$
- $28$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$

## Explanation

By Wien’s law, the product of absolute temperature and peak wavelength is constant. Doubling the temperature halves the wavelength, giving $7$.
