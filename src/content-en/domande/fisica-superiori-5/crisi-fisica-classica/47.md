Two photocells receive the same above-threshold photon rate from the same source. The second has twice the quantum efficiency and all emitted electrons are collected. Which reading doubles?

- Saturation current.
- Maximum kinetic energy for the same metal.
- Frequency of each photon.
- Stopping potential for the same metal.

## Explanation

Saturation current is $e$ times the emission rate. Twice the efficiency gives twice as many emitted electrons for the same illumination.
