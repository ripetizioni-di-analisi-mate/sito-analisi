A metal with a work function of $2$ eV is illuminated by photons of energy $8$ eV. What is the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons?

- $6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $10\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $8\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $16\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Einstein’s photoelectric equation is $K_{\max}=E_\gamma-W_0$. The photon energy exceeds the work function, giving $K_{\max}=8-2=6$ electronvolts.
