After each count a photon detector is insensitive for $10\,\mu\mathrm s$. Under very intense monochromatic light its reading approaches $10^5$ counts per second even when the power is increased. Which test distinguishes detector dead time from a physical limit of the source?

- Repeat under identical conditions with the same detector and retain only the highest reading.
- Apply known attenuation and check whether the rate corrected for attenuation exceeds the plateau.
- Lengthen the observation under unchanged conditions and compare only total counts.
- Switch the displayed units from counts per second to counts per minute.

## Explanation

A dead time of $10\,\mu\mathrm s$ limits the instrument to roughly $10^5$ events per second. Sufficient attenuation spaces out the arrivals, so the reconstructed flux reveals whether the source exceeded the instrument limit.
