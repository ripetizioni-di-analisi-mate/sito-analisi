Two blackbodies have the same temperature of $300$ K. The first emits $7$ W, and the second has three times its area. What power does the second emit, in watts?

- $63$
- $7$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives the same emitted power per unit area for both bodies. The total power therefore increases in proportion to the area, giving $21$.
