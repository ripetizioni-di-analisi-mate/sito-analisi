A metal's plot of $V_s$ against $f$ crosses the frequency axis at $6\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What physical quantity is obtained by multiplying that intercept by $h$?

::grafico{f="x<6?NaN:0.414*(x-6)" xmin=0 xmax=11.7 ymin=-0.5 ymax=2.6 nomi="f (10¹⁴ Hz),Vₛ (V)" punti="6,0,pieno" legenda=0 titolo="Stopping potential measured above threshold, with the model threshold indicated."}

- The elementary charge.
- The saturation current.
- The lamp intensity.
- The work function.

## Explanation

At the intercept $V_s=0$, so even the most energetic electron has just reached the escape threshold. Einstein's equation reduces to $hf_0=W$.
