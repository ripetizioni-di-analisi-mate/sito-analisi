In Compton scattering from a stationary free electron a photon is observed exactly along its original direction. What is the ideal wavelength shift?

- $-\lambda_C$
- Zero.
- $\lambda_C$
- $2\lambda_C$

## Explanation

At $\theta=0$ we have $\cos\theta=1$. The factor $1-\cos\theta$ vanishes and there is no energy transfer in the forward limit.
