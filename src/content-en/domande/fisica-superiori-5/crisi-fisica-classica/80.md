An attenuated laser illuminates two slits with less than one photon on average in the apparatus. After many detections an interference pattern builds up. What is observed for an individual event on an ideal screen?

- Zero energy at all points.
- One point for each slit in the same event.
- One localized detection.
- The entire continuous pattern in one event.

## Explanation

Arrival probabilities reflect interference between paths. An individual photon is absorbed locally; many events build up the distribution.
