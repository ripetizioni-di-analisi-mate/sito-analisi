The Compton wavelength of the electron is $\lambda_C$. An incident photon of wavelength $7\lambda_C$ is scattered through $90^\circ$. What is its final wavelength in units of $\lambda_C$?

- $7$
- $6$
- $9$
- $8$

## Explanation

The Compton shift is $\lambda_C(1-\cos\theta)$. At ninety degrees the cosine is zero, so one Compton wavelength is added to the initial wavelength, giving $8$.
