In Compton scattering, a photon’s wavelength increases by $2.4$ pm at an angle of $90^\circ$. What is its final wavelength if its initial wavelength is $2$ pm?

- $4.4\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $-0.4\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{pm}$

## Explanation

In Compton scattering the final wavelength is the initial wavelength plus the given shift. The stated shift already accounts for the scattering angle. The result is $\lambda^\prime=2+2.4=4.4$ picometers.
