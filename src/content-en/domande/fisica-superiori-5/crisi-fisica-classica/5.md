A blue photon has wavelength $450\,\mathrm{nm}$. Use $hc=1.989\cdot10^{-25}\,\mathrm{J\,m}$ and $1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$. Which pair expresses its energy in both units?

- $7.07\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J,\ 4.42\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $4.42\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J,\ 0.362\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $4.42\cdot10^{-22}\,\mathrm J,\ 2.76\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $4.42\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J,\ 2.76\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Divide $hc$ by the wavelength in metres, obtaining $4.42\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$. Dividing once more by the number of joules per electronvolt yields $2.76\,\mathrm{eV}$.
