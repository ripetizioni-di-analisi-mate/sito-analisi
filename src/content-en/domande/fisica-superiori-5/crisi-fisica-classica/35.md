In Compton scattering, a photon’s wavelength increases by $2.4$ pm at an angle of $90^\circ$. What is its final wavelength if its initial wavelength is $8$ pm?

- $5.6\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $19.2\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $10.4\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{pm}$

## Explanation

In Compton scattering the final wavelength is the initial wavelength plus the given shift. The stated shift already accounts for the scattering angle. The result is $\lambda^\prime=8+2.4=10.4$ picometers.
