An ultraviolet photon has wavelength $250\,\mathrm{nm}$. With $h=6.63\cdot10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$, what is its momentum magnitude?

- $7.96\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $2.65\cdot10^{-30}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $1.66\cdot10^{-40}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $2.65\cdot10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

For light $p=\dfrac{h}{\lambda}$. First convert the wavelength to $2.50\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$, then divide Planck's constant by that length.
