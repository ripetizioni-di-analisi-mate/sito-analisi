A metal with a work function of $2$ eV is illuminated by photons of energy $7$ eV. What is the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons?

- $9\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $7\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $14\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $5\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Einstein’s photoelectric equation is $K_{\max}=E_\gamma-W_0$. The photon energy exceeds the work function, giving $K_{\max}=7-2=5$ electronvolts.
