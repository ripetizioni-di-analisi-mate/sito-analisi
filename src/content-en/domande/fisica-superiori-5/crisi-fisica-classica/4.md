The cosmic background is approximated by a blackbody at $2.7\,\mathrm K$. With $b=2.9\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\,K}$, which detector suits the peak per unit wavelength?

- An ultraviolet spectrometer.
- An X-ray telescope.
- A millimetre-wave receiver.
- A camera sensitive only to green.

## Explanation

Wien's law gives $\lambda_{\max}=\dfrac{b}{T}\simeq1.1\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$. The peak lies in the millimetre band.
