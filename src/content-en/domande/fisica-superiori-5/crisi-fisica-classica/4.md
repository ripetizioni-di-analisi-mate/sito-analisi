The absolute temperature of a blackbody changes from $T$ to $2T$, while its surface area stays the same. By what factor does the total emitted power increase?

- $2$
- $4$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives $P=\sigma AT^4$. The area and the constant are unchanged. Multiplying the temperature by $2$ multiplies the power by $2^4=16$.
