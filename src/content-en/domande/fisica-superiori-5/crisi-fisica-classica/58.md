A photocell shows a small current even with the lamp off. What experimental control is needed to estimate the current due to light?

- Attribute the background to a single photon's energy.
- Always add the background twice.
- Measure the dark background at the same bias and subtract it from the illuminated reading.
- Set the incident frequency to zero in calculations.

## Explanation

The illuminated reading can include a contribution independent of light. A dark measurement under otherwise identical conditions estimates it.
