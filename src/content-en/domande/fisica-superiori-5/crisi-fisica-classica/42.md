Light increases the current in a semiconductor while no electrons leave the material for the surrounding vacuum. Which microscopic process can increase its conductivity?

- Light absorbed only by lattice ions produces a direct current of those ions towards the contacts.
- Light transfers electrons only between localized states without creating mobile carriers.
- Light makes electron–hole pairs recombine, reducing the number of free carriers.
- A photon promotes an electron into the conduction band and leaves a mobile hole.

## Explanation

In the internal photoelectric effect, electrons and holes can conduct within the solid. The electrical signal does not require a charge to escape into the vacuum.
