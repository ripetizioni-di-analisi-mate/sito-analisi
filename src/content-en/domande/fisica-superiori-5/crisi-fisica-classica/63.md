Two photons of equal energy arrive from opposite directions and are absorbed by the same initially stationary small object. Which balance applies?

- Impulse doubles and energy vanishes.
- Energy is negative because one photon travels backwards.
- Both absorbed energy and impulse vanish.
- Absorbed energy is positive and net impulse is zero.

## Explanation

The scalar photon energies add. Their momenta are opposite vectors and sum to zero.
