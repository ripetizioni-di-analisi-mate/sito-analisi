A photon scattered through a large angle is detected with a shorter wavelength than the incident one. The target was modelled as a stationary free electron. Which assumption should be re-examined?

- The photon has negative energy.
- A shorter-wavelength photon has lower energy.
- Planck's constant changes sign in the collision.
- The electron may have had initial kinetic energy.

## Explanation

A photon that shortens has gained energy. An initially stationary electron cannot supply kinetic energy, whereas a moving electron can under suitable conditions.
