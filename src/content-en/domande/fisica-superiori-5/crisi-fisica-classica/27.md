The classical blackbody curve grows without bound at high frequency if every mode receives the same mean energy. Which observation contradicts this prediction?

- Photons can have only one universal frequency.
- A blackbody never emits visible radiation at any temperature.
- The measured spectrum falls after a peak and the total energy is finite.
- All bodies peak at the same frequency.

## Explanation

At high frequency a quantum costs $hf$. When this greatly exceeds the typical thermal energy those modes are sparsely populated and the measured tail falls.
