A sphere of radius $r = 5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ that can be treated as a blackbody is kept at temperature $T = 500\,\mathrm{K}$. Calculate the power it radiates. Use $\sigma = 5.67 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{W/(m^2\cdot K^4)}$.

- $1.1\cdot10^2\,\mathrm W$
- $1.1\cdot10^4\,\mathrm W$
- $2.8\cdot10^1\,\mathrm W$
- $3.5\cdot10^3\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The whole sphere radiates, with area $4\pi(0.050\,\mathrm m)^2$. Stefan–Boltzmann gives the power per square metre as $\sigma T^4$, which must be multiplied by this area.
