The absolute temperature of a blackbody changes from $T$ to $3T$, while its surface area stays the same. By what factor does the total emitted power increase?

- $81$
- $3$
- $9$
- $12$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives $P=\sigma AT^4$. The area and the constant are unchanged. Multiplying the temperature by $3$ multiplies the power by $3^4=81$.
