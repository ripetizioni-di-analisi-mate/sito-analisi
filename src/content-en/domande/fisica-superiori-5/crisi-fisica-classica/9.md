Copper has work function $4.70\,\mathrm{eV}$. What is the longest wavelength for which the single-photon photoelectric model permits emission? Use $hc=1240\,\mathrm{eV\,nm}$.

- $5.83\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $529\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $264\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $132\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

At threshold the photon energy just equals the work function: $\dfrac{hc}{\lambda_0}=W$. This gives $\lambda_0=\dfrac{1240}{4.70}\,\mathrm{nm}\simeq264\,\mathrm{nm}$.
