After a collision a photon changes from $\lambda$ to $1.25\lambda$. What fraction of its initial energy remains in the photon?

- $1.25$
- $0.25$
- $0.80$
- $0.75$

## Explanation

Energy obeys $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The final-to-initial ratio is $\dfrac{1}{1.25}=0.80$, while the transferred fraction is $0.20$.
