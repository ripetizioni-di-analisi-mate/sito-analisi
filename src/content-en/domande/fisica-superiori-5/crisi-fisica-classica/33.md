The emission spectrum of a blackbody peaks at $800$ nm. Use Wien’s constant $b=2.9\cdot10^{-3}$ m K. What is the temperature?

- $3625\,\mathrm{K}$
- $232000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $362.5\,\mathrm{K}$
- $36250\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Wien’s law is $\lambda_{\max} T=b$. The given wavelength is $8\cdot10^{-7}$ meters, so the temperature is $T=\dfrac{2.9\cdot10^{-3}}{8\cdot10^{-7}}=\dfrac{29000}{8}$ kelvins.
