A crystal converts two identical photons of frequency $f$ into one photon with no other energy changes. What vacuum wavelength does the resulting photon have relative to the original?

- Half.
- Four times the original.
- Twice the original.
- The same.

## Explanation

Energy conservation gives $hf_{\rm nuovo}=2hf$. Frequency doubles and vacuum wavelength halves.
