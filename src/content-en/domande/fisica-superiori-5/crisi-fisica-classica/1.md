A star is treated as a blackbody. Its spectrum per unit wavelength peaks at $\lambda_{\max} = 480\,\mathrm{nm}$. Determine the star’s surface temperature. Wien’s constant is $2.90 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\cdot K}$.

- $1.39\cdot10^3\,\mathrm K$
- $6.04\cdot10^4\,\mathrm K$
- $6.04\cdot10^2\,\mathrm K$
- $6.04\cdot10^3\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

For the peak per unit wavelength, $\lambda_{\max}T=b$. Convert the wavelength to $4.80\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$ and divide $b$ by that length.
