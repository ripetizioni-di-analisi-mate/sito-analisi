A photon has a wavelength of $200$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $6.2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $24.8\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $62\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{200}=\dfrac{31}{5}$ electronvolts.
