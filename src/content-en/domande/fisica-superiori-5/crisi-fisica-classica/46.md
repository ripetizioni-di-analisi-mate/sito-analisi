A blackbody has a peak wavelength of $10$ µm. At what wavelength, in micrometers, does its spectrum peak if its absolute temperature doubles?

- $5$
- $10$
- $20$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

By Wien’s law, the product of absolute temperature and peak wavelength is constant. Doubling the temperature halves the wavelength, giving $5$.
