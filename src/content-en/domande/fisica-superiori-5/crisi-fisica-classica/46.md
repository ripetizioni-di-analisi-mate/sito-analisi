A saturated photocell supplies $3.2\,\mathrm{nA}$. How many electrons are collected per second, using $e=1.6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$?

- $2.0\cdot10^{10}$
- $2.0\cdot10^{7}$
- $5.1\cdot10^{-28}$
- $5.0\cdot10^9$

## Explanation

Current is charge per unit time. Dividing $3.2\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/s}$ by the elementary charge gives $2.0\cdot10^{10}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
