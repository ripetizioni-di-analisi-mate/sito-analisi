A photon of frequency $f$ has energy $7$ eV. What is the energy in eV of a photon of frequency $3f$?

- $21$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $63$

## Explanation

By Planck’s relation, the energy of a photon is Planck’s constant times its frequency. The ratio of the energies therefore equals the ratio of the frequencies, giving $21$.
