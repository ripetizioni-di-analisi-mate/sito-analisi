Stopping potential $V_s$ is plotted against wavelength $\lambda$ instead of frequency. Which horizontal-axis transformation makes the above-threshold relation linear?

- $\sqrt{\lambda}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\lambda}$
- $\ln\lambda$
- $\lambda^2$

## Explanation

Substitution of $f=\dfrac{c}{\lambda}$ gives $V_s=(\dfrac{hc}{e})(\dfrac{1}{\lambda})-\dfrac{W}{e}$. Inverse wavelength is therefore the linear variable.
