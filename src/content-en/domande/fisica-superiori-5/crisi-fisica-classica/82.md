In a balanced interferometer two coherent paths reach one detector with equal amplitudes and phase difference $\pi$. Why does the ideal absence of counts at that output respect energy conservation?

- Photons lose all their energy through universal cancellation.
- Interference redistributes probabilities and the other output can receive the photons.
- The detector always absorbs two opposite photons at once.
- A dark fringe means the source is off.

## Explanation

Amplitudes cancel at one output and can add at the other. The total detection probability of an ideal apparatus remains one.
