Three metals under the same above-threshold light give stopping-potential magnitudes $0.4$, $1.1$ and $0.8\,\mathrm V$. Which cathode has the greatest work function?

- The $0.4\,\mathrm V$ cathode.
- All three work functions must be equal.
- The $0.8\,\mathrm V$ cathode.
- The $1.1\,\mathrm V$ cathode.

## Explanation

Since $W=hf-eV_s$, at common frequency the smallest stopping potential corresponds to the largest extraction cost.
