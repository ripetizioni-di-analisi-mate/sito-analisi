A black surface emits $5$ W at $200$ K. How much power does it emit, in watts, if the absolute temperature doubles and the area stays the same?

- $20$
- $10$
- $40$
- $80$

## Explanation

By the Stefan–Boltzmann law, the emitted power is proportional to the fourth power of absolute temperature. Doubling that temperature multiplies the power by sixteen, giving $80$.
