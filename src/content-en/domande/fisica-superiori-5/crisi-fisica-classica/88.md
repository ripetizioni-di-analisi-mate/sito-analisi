An oscillator in Planck’s model changes from level $n=2$ to level $n=5$. How many energy quanta $hf$ does it absorb in total?

- $5$
- $3$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The energy difference between adjacent levels is $hf$. The number of quanta equals the difference between the level indices, giving $3$.
