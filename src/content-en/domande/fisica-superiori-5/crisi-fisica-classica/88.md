A modern oscillator has levels $(n+\dfrac{1}{2})hf$ instead of $nhf$. Which measurement from Planck's model remains unchanged under this constant shift?

- The energy of an adjacent-level transition.
- The sum of two given level energies.
- The absolute ground-state energy.
- The absolute energy of level $n=2$.

## Explanation

Taking the difference between two levels cancels the contribution added to both. The adjacent-level gap remains $hf$.
