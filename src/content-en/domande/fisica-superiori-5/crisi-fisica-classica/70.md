Two beams with very different incident wavelengths scatter through the same angle from stationary free electrons. What is equal in their spectra?

- The final wavelength.
- The percentage wavelength increase.
- The absolute wavelength increase.
- The kinetic energy transferred in both cases.

## Explanation

For the same mass and angle $\lambda_C(1-\cos\theta)$ is identical. The same absolute increase represents different fractions of the two initial wavelengths.
