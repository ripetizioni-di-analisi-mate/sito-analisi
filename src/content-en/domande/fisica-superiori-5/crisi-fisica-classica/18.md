The emission spectrum of a blackbody peaks at $500$ nm. Use Wien’s constant $b=2.9\cdot10^{-3}$ m K. What is the temperature?

- $145000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $5800\,\mathrm{K}$
- $580\,\mathrm{K}$
- $58000\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Wien’s law is $\lambda_{\max} T=b$. The given wavelength is $5\cdot10^{-7}$ meters, so the temperature is $T=\dfrac{2.9\cdot10^{-3}}{5\cdot10^{-7}}=\dfrac{29000}{5}$ kelvins.
