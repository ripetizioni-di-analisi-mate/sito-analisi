A small hole in an opaque cavity looks dark even when the inner walls are not perfectly black. What makes the aperture a good absorber?

- Entering light is reflected many times and mostly absorbed.
- A hole always reflects all light towards the source.
- Light stops travelling in the empty cavity.
- Each photon's frequency becomes zero on entry.

## Explanation

The walls absorb a fraction of the energy at each encounter. With a small opening it is unlikely that light escapes before many encounters.
