The absolute temperature of a blackbody changes from $T$ to $8T$, while its surface area stays the same. By what factor does the total emitted power increase?

- $8$
- $4096$
- $64$
- $32$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives $P=\sigma AT^4$. The area and the constant are unchanged. Multiplying the temperature by $8$ multiplies the power by $8^4=4096$.
