At equal total luminosity one blackbody star is twice as hot as another. How does its radius compare?

- It is one sixteenth as large.
- It is half as large.
- It is four times larger.
- It is one quarter as large.

## Explanation

For a sphere $L=4\pi R^2\sigma T^4$. Keeping $L$ fixed when $T$ doubles requires sixteen times less area, hence a fourfold smaller radius.
