A photon has a wavelength of $900$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $111.6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{31}{45}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{124}{9}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{62}{45}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{900}=\dfrac{62}{45}$ electronvolts.
