A thermal camera receives both emission and reflected radiation from polished metal. Why can a nearby hot object affect the metal's apparent temperature?

- Infrared radiation always gives the reflecting spot's temperature.
- Some detected radiation can be reflected from the hot object.
- Reflection necessarily changes the metal temperature instantaneously.
- Polished metal is a perfect blackbody.

## Explanation

The camera infers temperature from received flux. A reflective surface can add an external contribution unrelated to its own emission.
