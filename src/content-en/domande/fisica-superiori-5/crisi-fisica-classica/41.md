A detector counts $600$ pulses in one second with $30\%$ efficiency and negligible background. What incident photon flux is estimated?

- $180\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $300\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $600\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The measured rate is efficiency times incident flux. Dividing $600$ by $0.30$ gives an estimate of $2000$ photons per second.
