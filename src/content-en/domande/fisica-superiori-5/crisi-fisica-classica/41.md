A photon has a wavelength of $1000$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $1.24\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $124\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.62\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $12.4\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{1000}=\dfrac{31}{25}$ electronvolts.
