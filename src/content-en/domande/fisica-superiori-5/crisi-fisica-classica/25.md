Two blackbody stars have the same temperature. B has twice A's radius but is four times farther from the observer. What is the received flux from B relative to A?

- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

Emitted power scales as $R^2$ and is four times larger. Received flux falls as $\dfrac{1}{d^2}$, giving the ratio $\dfrac{4}{16}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.
