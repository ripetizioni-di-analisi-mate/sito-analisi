A black surface emits $7$ W at $300$ K. How much power does it emit, in watts, if the absolute temperature doubles and the area stays the same?

- $14$
- $112$
- $28$
- $56$

## Explanation

By the Stefan–Boltzmann law, the emitted power is proportional to the fourth power of absolute temperature. Doubling that temperature multiplies the power by sixteen, giving $112$.
