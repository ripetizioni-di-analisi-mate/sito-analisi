A metal surface oxidizes. Under the same above-threshold light the stopping-potential magnitude falls by $0.6\,\mathrm V$. What is the change in effective work function?

- It decreases by $0.6\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- It increases by $0.6\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- It increases by $1.2\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- It must stay unchanged.

## Explanation

The photon supplies the same energy before and after. If maximum kinetic energy falls by $0.6\,\mathrm{eV}$, the extraction cost has risen by the same amount.
