An X-ray photon of wavelength $20.0\,\mathrm{pm}$ scatters at $60^\circ$ from a free electron initially at rest. If the Compton wavelength is $2.43\,\mathrm{pm}$, what is the outgoing wavelength?

- $21.2\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $22.4\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $18.8\,\mathrm{pm}$
- $1.22\,\mathrm{pm}$

## Explanation

The shift is $\Delta\lambda=\lambda_C(1-\cos60^\circ)=1.215\,\mathrm{pm}$. The photon loses energy, so the shift is added to the initial wavelength.
