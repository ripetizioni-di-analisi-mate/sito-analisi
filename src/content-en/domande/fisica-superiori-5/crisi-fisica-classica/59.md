Why does changing the metal of an ideal photocell alter the threshold frequency even with the same lamp?

- The minimum energy needed to free an electron from the surface changes.
- Planck's constant changes.
- Vacuum light speed changes.
- Each photon takes on the metal's mass.

## Explanation

Threshold satisfies $hf_0=W$. The value of $W$ depends on the surface, whereas $h$ is common to all metals.
