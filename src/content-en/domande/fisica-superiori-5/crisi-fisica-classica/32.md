An oven is held at constant temperature. Only the area of the small aperture used to observe its cavity radiation is doubled. As long as the apertures remain small, what changes in the radiation emerging from the hole?

- Total power halves.
- Total power doubles and the peak position stays fixed.
- The peak shifts to twice the wavelength.
- The colour temperature doubles.

## Explanation

Temperature fixes the spectral shape per unit area. Doubling area doubles the power in every band without shifting the peak.
