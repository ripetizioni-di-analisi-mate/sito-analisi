In the Compton model the target electron is replaced by a stationary free particle of ten times its mass. At the same angle, how does the wavelength shift change?

- It becomes one hundred times larger.
- It stays identical.
- It becomes ten times larger.
- It becomes one tenth as large.

## Explanation

The characteristic wavelength is $\lambda_C=\dfrac{h}{mc}$. A tenfold greater mass makes the length multiplying the angular factor ten times smaller.
