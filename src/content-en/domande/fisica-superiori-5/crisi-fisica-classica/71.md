The stopping potential in a photoelectric experiment has magnitude $7$ V. What is the maximum kinetic energy in eV?

- $7$
- $49$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $14$

## Explanation

The retarding field removes kinetic energy equal to the elementary charge times the potential difference. In electronvolts the energy has the same numerical value as the voltage in volts, giving $7$.
