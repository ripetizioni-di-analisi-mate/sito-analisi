Two spectrometers have fractional resolution $1\%$, one at $100\,\mathrm{pm}$ in X-rays and one at $500\,\mathrm{nm}$ in visible light. Which can resolve a Compton shift of $2.4\,\mathrm{pm}$ better?

- Neither, because the shift is always invisible.
- Both equally, because their percentage resolutions are equal.
- The X-ray spectrometer.
- The visible-light spectrometer.

## Explanation

The shift is $2.4\%$ of $100\,\mathrm{pm}$, exceeding the resolution. The same shift is a much smaller fraction of $500\,\mathrm{nm}$.
