A photon has a wavelength of $700$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $86.8\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{62}{35}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{31}{35}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{124}{7}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{700}=\dfrac{62}{35}$ electronvolts.
