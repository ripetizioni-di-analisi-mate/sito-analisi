For a stationary free electron a Compton shift exactly equal to $\lambda_C$ is measured. What geometry produces it?

- The scattered photon is perpendicular to the incident photon.
- The angle must be $60^\circ$.
- The photon reverses.
- The photon travels straight ahead.

## Explanation

From $\dfrac{\Delta\lambda}{\lambda_C}=1-\cos\theta$ a unit ratio requires $\cos\theta=0$ and hence $\theta=90^\circ$.
