On one metal only some emitted electrons reach $K_{\max}$. Why does $K_{\max}=hf-W$ describe a maximum rather than the mean?

- All monochromatic photons have random unequal energies.
- Some electrons lose energy in the material before escaping.
- The stopping potential selects only the mean.
- All electrons always leave at the same speed.

## Explanation

Electrons escaping from the most favourable energetic conditions without loss reach the limit. Different paths and initial states can yield lower energies.
