On a graph of stopping potential versus frequency, what quantity does the positive slope give?

- The work function.
- $e/h$.
- The threshold frequency.
- $h/e$.

## Explanation

The photoelectric equation written for the stopping potential is $V_s=(h/e)f-W/e$. The coefficient of frequency is the ratio of Planck’s constant to the elementary charge.
