A black cube and its surroundings are both at $300\,\mathrm K$ in thermal equilibrium. Which statement is correct?

- The cube continues to cool indefinitely.
- The cube emits and absorbs the same mean power.
- The cube emits without absorbing.
- The cube emits no photons.

## Explanation

A positive temperature entails thermal emission. At equilibrium absorbed energy replaces emitted energy, so the net power is zero.
