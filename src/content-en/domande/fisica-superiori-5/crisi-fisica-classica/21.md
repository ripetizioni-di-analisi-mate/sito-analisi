A photon has a wavelength of $600$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $\dfrac{31}{15}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $74.4\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{31}{30}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{62}{3}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{600}=\dfrac{31}{15}$ electronvolts.
