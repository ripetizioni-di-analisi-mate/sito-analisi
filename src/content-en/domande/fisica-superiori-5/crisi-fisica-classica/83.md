One photon enters an ideal $50{:}50$ beam splitter with two perfect detectors and no background. What happens in one trial?

- Both always record a whole photon.
- Neither detector records anything.
- Both always record half a photon.
- One detector receives the whole photon, with equal probability for each output.

## Explanation

The splitter divides probability amplitudes between the outputs. For a lossless single-photon input absorption transfers one whole quantum.
