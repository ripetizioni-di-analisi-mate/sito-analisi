The scattered wavelength $\lambda'$ is plotted against $x=1-\cos\theta$ for a monochromatic beam. Which pair gives the intercept and slope?

- $0,\ \lambda_{\rm i}$
- $\lambda_C,\ \lambda_{\rm i}$
- $\lambda_{\rm i},\ \lambda_C$
- $\lambda_{\rm i},\ -\lambda_C$

## Explanation

The equation is $\lambda'=\lambda_{\rm i}+\lambda_Cx$. The intercept is the incident wavelength and the slope measures the target's Compton wavelength.
