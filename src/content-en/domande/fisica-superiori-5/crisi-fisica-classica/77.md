A photon has a momentum of $7$ in a chosen unit. What momentum, in the same unit, does a photon of three times the wavelength have?

- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $63$
- $21$

## Explanation

For a photon the magnitude of the momentum is $h/\lambda$. Tripling the wavelength divides that magnitude by three, giving $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
