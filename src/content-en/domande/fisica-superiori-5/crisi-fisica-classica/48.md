A blackbody has a peak wavelength of $18$ µm. At what wavelength, in micrometers, does its spectrum peak if its absolute temperature doubles?

- $18$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $36$

## Explanation

By Wien’s law, the product of absolute temperature and peak wavelength is constant. Doubling the temperature halves the wavelength, giving $9$.
