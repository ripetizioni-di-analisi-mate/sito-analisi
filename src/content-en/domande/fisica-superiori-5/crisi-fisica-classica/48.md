A beam contains equal numbers of photons at $2$ and $5\,\mathrm{eV}$. It illuminates a metal with $W=3\,\mathrm{eV}$ in the single-photon regime. What fraction of incident photons has enough energy to release an electron?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Compare each component's photon energy with the work function rather than averaging. Only the $5\,\mathrm{eV}$ photons exceed threshold and they are half the total.
