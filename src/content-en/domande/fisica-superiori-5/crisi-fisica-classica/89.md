An oscillator in Planck’s model changes from level $n=3$ to level $n=7$. How many energy quanta $hf$ does it absorb in total?

- $7$
- $4$
- $3$
- $8$

## Explanation

The energy difference between adjacent levels is $hf$. The number of quanta equals the difference between the level indices, giving $4$.
