Two sources show nearly identical continuous visible spectra; a third has a few narrow lines. Which observation distinguishes a monochromatic line from a broad blackbody peak experimentally?

- Measure emission in several narrow bands on both sides of the apparent peak.
- Check only whether the source is visible to the eye.
- Measure only total unfiltered power.
- Count only the source's operating hours.

## Explanation

A thermal peak is surrounded by a wavelength continuum. Resolving neighbouring bands distinguishes that shape from an isolated line.
