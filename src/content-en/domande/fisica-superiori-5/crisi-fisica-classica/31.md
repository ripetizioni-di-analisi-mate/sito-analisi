At fixed temperature, quanta of a mode have energy $hf$ much larger than $kT$. How does this affect thermal emission in that mode?

- Each quantum loses energy until it equals $kT$.
- Its frequency vanishes.
- Every mode necessarily receives infinitely many quanta.
- Thermal excitation of the mode is rare.

## Explanation

Typical thermal energies are small relative to the first required jump. Consequently the excited high-frequency modes have low populations.
