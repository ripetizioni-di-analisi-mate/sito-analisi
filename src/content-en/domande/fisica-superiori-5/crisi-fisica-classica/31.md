A photon has a wavelength of $800$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $99.2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0.775\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1.55\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $15.5\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{800}=\dfrac{31}{20}$ electronvolts.
