A photon has a momentum of $5$ in a chosen unit. What momentum, in the same unit, does a photon of three times the wavelength have?

- $5$
- $45$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $15$

## Explanation

For a photon the magnitude of the momentum is $h/\lambda$. Tripling the wavelength divides that magnitude by three, giving $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
