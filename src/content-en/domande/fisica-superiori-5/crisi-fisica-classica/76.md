A material's scattered X-ray spectrum contains an almost unshifted line as well as a shifted Compton line. Which possible process accounts for the nearly unchanged line?

- Coherent scattering with recoil of the whole, much more massive atom.
- An unshifted line always comes from zero-energy photons.
- Every photon must shift by the same amount even on targets of different mass.
- The photon is completely annihilated.

## Explanation

For a given momentum transfer a heavy target takes little recoil energy. The resulting shift can be too small to resolve.
