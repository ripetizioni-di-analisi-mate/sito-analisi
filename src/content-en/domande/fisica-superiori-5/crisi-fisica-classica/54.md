The stopping-potential line is extrapolated to zero frequency and gives $-2.3\,\mathrm V$ there. What does the negative value mean?

- At zero frequency electrons are emitted with negative kinetic energy.
- The work function is negative.
- The extrapolated intercept is $-\dfrac{W}{e}$; below threshold no photoelectrons are measured.
- A negative saturation current of two amperes is measured.

## Explanation

The line describes above-threshold emission. Its extrapolation estimates $W$ but does not describe a physical emission below threshold.
