Two blackbodies have the same temperature of $400$ K. The first emits $9$ W, and the second has three times its area. What power does the second emit, in watts?

- $3$
- $9$
- $81$
- $27$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives the same emitted power per unit area for both bodies. The total power therefore increases in proportion to the area, giving $27$.
