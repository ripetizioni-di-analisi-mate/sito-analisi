Two Planck oscillators have frequencies $f$ and $3f$. What is the smallest positive energy that each oscillator could exchange through allowed jumps, with integer numbers of quanta?

- $3hf$
- $\dfrac{hf}{3}$
- $hf$
- $2hf$

## Explanation

The first can exchange multiples of $hf$ and the second multiples of $3hf$. Their smallest common positive multiple is $3hf$.
