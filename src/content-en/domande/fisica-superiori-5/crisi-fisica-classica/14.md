A gamma photon of wavelength $5.00\,\mathrm{pm}$ is backscattered by a free electron initially at rest. Use $\lambda_C=2.43\,\mathrm{pm}$ and $hc=1240\,\mathrm{keV\,pm}$. How much kinetic energy does the electron acquire?

- $62\,\mathrm{keV}$
- $248\,\mathrm{keV}$
- $374\,\mathrm{keV}$
- $122\,\mathrm{keV}$

## Explanation

Backscattering gives $1-\cos180^\circ=2$, hence $\lambda'=5.00+2(2.43)=9.86\,\mathrm{pm}$. The recoil takes up the photon's energy loss: $\dfrac{1240}{5.00}-\dfrac{1240}{9.86}\simeq122\,\mathrm{keV}$.
