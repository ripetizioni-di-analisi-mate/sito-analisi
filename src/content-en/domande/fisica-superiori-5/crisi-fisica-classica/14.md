The absolute temperature of a blackbody changes from $T$ to $4T$, while its surface area stays the same. By what factor does the total emitted power increase?

- $4$
- $256$
- $16$
- $257$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives $P=\sigma AT^4$. The area and the constant are unchanged. Multiplying the temperature by $4$ multiplies the power by $4^4=256$.
