A black surface emits $9$ W at $400$ K. How much power does it emit, in watts, if the absolute temperature doubles and the area stays the same?

- $36$
- $144$
- $72$
- $18$

## Explanation

By the Stefan–Boltzmann law, the emitted power is proportional to the fourth power of absolute temperature. Doubling that temperature multiplies the power by sixteen, giving $144$.
