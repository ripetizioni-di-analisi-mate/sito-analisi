The absolute temperature of a blackbody changes from $T$ to $7T$, while its surface area stays the same. By what factor does the total emitted power increase?

- $2401$
- $7$
- $49$
- $28$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives $P=\sigma AT^4$. The area and the constant are unchanged. Multiplying the temperature by $7$ multiplies the power by $7^4=2401$.
