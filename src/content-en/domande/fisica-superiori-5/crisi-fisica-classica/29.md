An ideal oscillator with levels $E_n=nhf$ is proposed to exchange a net $2.5hf$ at fixed frequency between two allowed states. Is it possible?

- No, the index difference must be an integer.
- No, every exchange larger than $hf$ is forbidden.
- Yes, every energy is allowed when the oscillator is hot.
- Yes, because $2.5$ is positive.

## Explanation

Every difference is $(n_f-n_i)hf$ with both indices integers. A noninteger multiple cannot connect two levels of this model.
