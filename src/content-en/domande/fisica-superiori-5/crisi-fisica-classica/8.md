The emission spectrum of a blackbody peaks at $300$ nm. Use Wien’s constant $b=2.9\cdot10^{-3}$ m K. What is the temperature?

- $87000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{2900}{3}\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{290000}{3}\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{29000}{3}\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Wien’s law is $\lambda_{\max} T=b$. The given wavelength is $3\cdot10^{-7}$ meters, so the temperature is $T=\dfrac{2.9\cdot10^{-3}}{3\cdot10^{-7}}=\dfrac{29000}{3}$ kelvins.
