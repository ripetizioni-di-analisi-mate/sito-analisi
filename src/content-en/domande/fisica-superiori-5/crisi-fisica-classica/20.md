A grey surface of constant emissivity $0.4$ has the same area and temperature as a blackbody. What power does it emit if the blackbody emits $50\,\mathrm W$?

- $20\,\mathrm W$
- $30\,\mathrm W$
- $125\,\mathrm W$
- $50\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

A grey body obeys $P=\varepsilon\sigma A T^4$. For equal area and temperature multiply $50\,\mathrm W$ by $0.4$.
