A photon has a momentum of $9$ in a chosen unit. What momentum, in the same unit, does a photon of three times the wavelength have?

- $9$
- $3$
- $81$
- $27$

## Explanation

For a photon the magnitude of the momentum is $h/\lambda$. Tripling the wavelength divides that magnitude by three, giving $3$.
