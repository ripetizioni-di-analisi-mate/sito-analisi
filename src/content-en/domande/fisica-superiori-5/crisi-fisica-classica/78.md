After scattering through $90^\circ$ a photon has momentum magnitude $3$ in arbitrary units, while the incoming photon had $4$. What recoil momentum magnitude follows from conservation for an initially stationary electron?

- $\sqrt7$
- $7$
- $1$
- $5$

## Explanation

Recoil is the vector difference between the incoming and outgoing photon momenta. Perpendicular vectors give magnitude $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5$.
