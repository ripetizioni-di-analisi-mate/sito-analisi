A metal has a work function of $3$ eV. It is illuminated by photons of energy $10$ eV. What is the maximum kinetic energy of the emitted electrons, in eV?

- $7$
- $13$
- $3$
- $10$

## Explanation

Subtract the work required to free an electron from the energy of each photon. The remainder is the maximum kinetic energy, $7$.
