A plate normally absorbs $10^{12}$ photons per second, each with momentum $2\cdot10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$. What is the mean force?

- $4\cdot10^{-15}\,\mathrm N$
- $2\cdot10^{-27}\,\mathrm N$
- $2\cdot10^{-39}\,\mathrm N$
- $2\cdot10^{-15}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Force is the momentum transferred per unit time. Each photon is absorbed, so $F=\dot Np=10^{12}\cdot2\cdot10^{-27}\,\mathrm N$.
