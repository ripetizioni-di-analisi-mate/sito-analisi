A photon of energy $E$ travels right and is elastically reflected backwards by a very massive mirror. Taking right as positive, what impulse component does the mirror receive?

- $+\dfrac{2E}{c}$
- $+\dfrac{E}{c}$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{2E}{c}$

## Explanation

The photon goes from $+\dfrac{E}{c}$ to $-\dfrac{E}{c}$. Momentum conservation gives the mirror the opposite of the photon change, namely $+\dfrac{2E}{c}$.
