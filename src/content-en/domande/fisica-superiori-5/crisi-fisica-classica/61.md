A metal has a work function of $2$ eV. It is illuminated by photons of energy $7$ eV. What is the maximum kinetic energy of the emitted electrons, in eV?

- $5$
- $7$
- $2$
- $9$

## Explanation

Subtract the work required to free an electron from the energy of each photon. The remainder is the maximum kinetic energy, $5$.
