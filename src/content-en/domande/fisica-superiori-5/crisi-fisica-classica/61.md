Two pulses of equal total energy have wavelengths $500$ and $250\,\mathrm{nm}$. Both are fully absorbed at normal incidence. Which pulse transfers more total momentum?

- Neither transfers momentum because photons have zero rest mass.
- They transfer the same total momentum.
- The $500\,\mathrm{nm}$ pulse transfers twice as much.
- The $250\,\mathrm{nm}$ pulse transfers twice as much.

## Explanation

For a unidirectional beam $p_{\rm tot}=\dfrac{U}{c}$. The shorter-wavelength photons individually carry more momentum, but fewer photons are present.
