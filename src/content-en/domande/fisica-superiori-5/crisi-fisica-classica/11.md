A photon has a wavelength of $400$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $49.6\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1.55\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $31\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{400}=\dfrac{31}{10}$ electronvolts.
