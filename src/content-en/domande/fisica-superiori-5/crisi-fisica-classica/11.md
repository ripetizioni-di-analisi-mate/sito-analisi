For the same cathode the stopping potentials are $0.30\,\mathrm V$ and $1.13\,\mathrm V$ at frequencies $5.5\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $7.5\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$, respectively. What value of $h$ follows in $\mathrm{J\,s}$? Use $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$.

- $2.66\cdot10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$
- $4.15\cdot10^{-15}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$
- $1.33\cdot10^{-33}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$
- $6.64\cdot10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the two photoelectric equations eliminates the work function: $h=\dfrac{e(V_2-V_1)}{f_2-f_1}$. Substitution gives $\dfrac{1.60\cdot10^{-19}\times0.83}{2.0\cdot10^{14}}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$.
