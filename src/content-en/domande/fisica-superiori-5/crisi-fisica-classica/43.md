A photocell is illuminated by a few photons per second, each above threshold. Some emissions follow absorption promptly even at very low intensity. Which model accounts for the absence of a long accumulation time?

- All electrons must share a single photon's energy equally.
- Elementary charge decreases at low intensity.
- One electron can receive the necessary energy from one photon.
- Every electron must store hours of energy before emission.

## Explanation

Lowering intensity increases the mean wait between photons without reducing $hf$. One above-threshold absorption can release an electron.
