A lab observes an ejected electron and a correlated scattered photon with reduced energy. Which elementary mechanism best matches these two products?

- Spontaneous blackbody emission without a collision.
- Complete photoelectric absorption of the incident photon.
- Simple transmission without interaction.
- Compton scattering.

## Explanation

In a Compton collision the photon gives up part of its energy and momentum and continues as a scattered photon. In the elementary photoelectric process the incident photon is absorbed.
