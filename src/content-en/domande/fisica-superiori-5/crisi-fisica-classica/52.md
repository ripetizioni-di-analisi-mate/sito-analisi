A $5\,\mathrm{eV}$ photon strikes a cathode with $W=2\,\mathrm{eV}$. The retarding potential is $2\,\mathrm V$. What energy can an electron reach the collector with if it is emitted at maximum energy directly towards the collector?

- $5\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $0\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $3\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Initial kinetic energy is $5-2=3\,\mathrm{eV}$. Climbing the electric barrier costs another $2\,\mathrm{eV}$, leaving one electronvolt at the collector.
