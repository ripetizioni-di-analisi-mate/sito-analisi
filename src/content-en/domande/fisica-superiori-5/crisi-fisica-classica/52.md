Two blackbodies have the same temperature of $200$ K. The first emits $5$ W, and the second has three times its area. What power does the second emit, in watts?

- $45$
- $5$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$

## Explanation

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives the same emitted power per unit area for both bodies. The total power therefore increases in proportion to the area, giving $15$.
