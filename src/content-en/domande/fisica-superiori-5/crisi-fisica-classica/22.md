A metal with a work function of $2$ eV is illuminated by photons of energy $9$ eV. What is the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons?

- $11\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $7\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $9\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $18\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Einstein’s photoelectric equation is $K_{\max}=E_\gamma-W_0$. The photon energy exceeds the work function, giving $K_{\max}=9-2=7$ electronvolts.
