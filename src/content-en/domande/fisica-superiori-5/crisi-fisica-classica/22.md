A black radiator at $400\,\mathrm K$ is completely surrounded by black walls at $300\,\mathrm K$. Which expression gives the net outward radiative power per unit area of the radiator?

- $\sigma(400^4+300^4)$
- $\sigma(400-300)^4$
- $\sigma(400^2-300^2)$
- $\sigma(400^4-300^4)$

## Explanation

Subtract radiation absorbed from the surroundings from the radiator's emission. The fourth power applies separately to each absolute temperature.
