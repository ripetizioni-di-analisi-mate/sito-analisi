A photon has a wavelength of $300$ nm. Use $hc=1240$ eV nm. What is its energy?

- $37.2\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{62}{15}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{31}{15}\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $\dfrac{124}{3}\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

For a photon, $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$. The nanometers cancel when the given constant is used, leaving $E=\dfrac{1240}{300}=\dfrac{62}{15}$ electronvolts.
