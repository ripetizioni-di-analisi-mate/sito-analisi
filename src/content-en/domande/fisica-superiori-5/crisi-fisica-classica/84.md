A photon counter records independent Poisson arrivals. One measurement yields $N=400$ events. With $\sigma_N\simeq\sqrt N$, what is the relative statistical uncertainty?

- $20\%$
- $0.25\%$
- $5\%$
- $50\%$

## Explanation

The count standard deviation is $20$. Dividing by the $400$ events gives $\dfrac{20}{400}=0.05$.
