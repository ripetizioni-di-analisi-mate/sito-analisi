A photon of wavelength $4\lambda_C$ is backscattered at $180^\circ$ by an electron initially at rest. What is its final wavelength in units of $\lambda_C$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

In the Compton shift formula the cosine of a hundred eighty degrees is minus one. The wavelength therefore increases by two Compton wavelengths, giving $6$.
