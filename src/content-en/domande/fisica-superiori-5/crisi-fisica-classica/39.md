A metal has a photoelectric threshold frequency of $f_0$. What is the maximum kinetic energy in units of the work function when the incident frequency is $3f_0$?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

At the threshold the work function equals $hf_0$. At three times the threshold frequency, each photon supplies three times the work function, one part of which is needed to free the electron. The result is $2$.
