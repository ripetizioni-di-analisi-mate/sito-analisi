A monochromatic $1\,\mathrm{mW}$ laser is attenuated by a neutral filter transmitting $10\%$ of the power. What happens to an individual transmitted photon?

- It leaves with one tenth of $hf$.
- It leaves at ten times the frequency.
- It retains energy $hf$.
- It gains rest mass.

## Explanation

Attenuation transmits a smaller fraction of the incident photons. In a stationary neutral filter without frequency conversion the surviving photons retain the original frequency and energy.
