A photon undergoes two successive $90^\circ$ Compton scatterings, each off a different stationary free electron. What is its final wavelength?

- $\lambda_{\rm i}$
- $\lambda_{\rm i}+2\lambda_C$
- $2\lambda_{\rm i}$
- $\lambda_{\rm i}+\dfrac{\lambda_C}{2}$

## Explanation

Each right-angle collision adds $\lambda_C$ independently of that collision's incident wavelength. The increments therefore add.
