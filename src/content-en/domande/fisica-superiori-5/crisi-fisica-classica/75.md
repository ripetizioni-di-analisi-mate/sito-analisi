For a given metal the stopping potential increases from $4$ V to $13$ V. By how much has the photon energy increased, in eV?

- $18$
- $13$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

The work function of the metal is the same in both measurements. The increase in photon energy therefore equals the increase in maximum kinetic energy, giving $9$.
