An incident photon moves along $+x$. After scattering the outgoing photon has momentum component toward $+y$ and the electron was initially at rest. What sign must $p_{e,y}$ have?

- Positive.
- The same as a hypothetical photon charge.
- Negative.
- Zero at every angle.

## Explanation

Initial total transverse momentum is zero. The electron recoil must balance the positive transverse component of the outgoing photon.
