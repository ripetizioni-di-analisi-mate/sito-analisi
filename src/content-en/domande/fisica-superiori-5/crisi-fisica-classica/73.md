For a given metal the stopping potential increases from $2$ V to $7$ V. By how much has the photon energy increased, in eV?

- $5$
- $7$
- $2$
- $10$

## Explanation

The work function of the metal is the same in both measurements. The increase in photon energy therefore equals the increase in maximum kinetic energy, giving $5$.
