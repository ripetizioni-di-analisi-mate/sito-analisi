A photon with an initial wavelength of $5\lambda_C$ undergoes Compton scattering through a nonzero angle. How does its energy change?

- It increases.
- It stays constant.
- It falls to zero for every nonzero angle.
- It decreases.

## Explanation

The Compton shift is positive for a nonzero scattering angle. Since the photon energy is inversely proportional to wavelength, the energy decreases.
