In an X-ray tube electrons accelerated from rest through $20\,\mathrm{kV}$ brake on a target. Neglecting losses before impact, what is the lower limit of the emitted photon wavelength? Use $hc=1.24\,\mathrm{keV\,nm}$.

- $16\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.062\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $25\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $0.62\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The most energetic photon can receive at most the full kinetic energy of one electron, $20\,\mathrm{keV}$. From $\lambda_{\min}=\dfrac{hc}{E_{\max}}$ the minimum is $\dfrac{1.24}{20}\,\mathrm{nm}$.
