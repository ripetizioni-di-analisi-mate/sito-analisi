The electron Compton wavelength is $2.43\,\mathrm{pm}$. A group reports a $7\,\mathrm{pm}$ shift in one collision with a stationary free electron. Why is this incompatible with the model?

- The largest increase is $2\lambda_C=4.86\,\mathrm{pm}$.
- The smallest increase is $7\,\mathrm{pm}$.
- The maximum depends only on incident intensity.
- Wavelength can never increase.

## Explanation

The factor $1-\cos\theta$ ranges from zero to two at every physical angle. Thus no angle gives a shift larger than two Compton wavelengths.
