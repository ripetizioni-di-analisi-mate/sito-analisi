Photons of energy $10$ eV strike a metal of work function $3$ eV. What happens to the maximum kinetic energy if only the intensity is increased in the single-photon regime?

- It is halved if the intensity doubles.
- It doubles if the intensity doubles.
- It stays the same.
- It becomes zero.

## Explanation

The maximum kinetic energy depends on the energy of each photon and on the work function. Neither changes when only the intensity increases.
