In a double-slit experiment information becomes available distinguishing the two paths perfectly, while both slits remain open. What change is expected for the photons?

- Each photon doubles its energy.
- The interference term disappears from the overall distribution.
- The light frequency vanishes.
- Photons cease being detected locally.

## Explanation

Distinguishable paths contribute probabilities that add. Fringe contrast requires coherent, indistinguishable alternatives.
