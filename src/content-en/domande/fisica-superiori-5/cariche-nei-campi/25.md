In the nonrelativistic regime, a particle with $\dfrac{|q|}{m}=6$ C/kg moves perpendicular to a magnetic field of $B=2$ T. What is its cyclotron angular frequency?

- $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency of the circular motion is $\omega_c=\dfrac{|q|B}{m}$. Multiply the charge-to-mass ratio by the field strength. This gives $\omega_c=12$ radians per second.
