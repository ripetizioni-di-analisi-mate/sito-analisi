A positive charge of $4$ µC moves from $14$ V to $9$ V. How much work does the electric field do?

- $5\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The work done by the field is the charge multiplied by the drop in potential. The difference between the two potentials, taken in the order given, is positive. The result is $20\,\mathrm{\mu J}$.
