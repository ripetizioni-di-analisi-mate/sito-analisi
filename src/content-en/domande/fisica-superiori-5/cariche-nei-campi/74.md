Two oppositely charged ions enter a velocity selector at the same speed of $7000$ m/s. The selector is set for that speed. What happens?

- Both pass through the region without deflection.
- Only the positive ion passes through.
- Both are deflected in the same direction.
- Only the negative ion passes through.

## Explanation

Reversing the sign of the charge reverses both the electric and the magnetic force. At the selected speed, the forces are still equal in magnitude and opposite in direction.
