A proton traverses successive opposite magnetic sectors, each turning its velocity through $30^\circ$ in opposite senses. What is the exit direction relative to the entry direction?

- Reversed
- Parallel and forward
- Tilted by $60^\circ$
- Perpendicular

## Explanation

The planar rotations are equal and opposite, so their angles cancel. The path may still acquire a sideways displacement.
