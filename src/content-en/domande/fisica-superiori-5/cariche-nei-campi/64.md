The period of a particle in a magnetic field is $5$ µs. In the nonrelativistic regime, what is the period in microseconds if the perpendicular speed triples while the field and the particle stay the same?

- $5$
- $15$
- $45$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$

## Explanation

The circumference increases in the same proportion as the speed because the radius is proportional to the speed. In the ratio of distance to speed, the two factors cancel. The result is $5$.
