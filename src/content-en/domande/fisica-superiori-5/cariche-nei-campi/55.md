A particle enters the space between two plates with a horizontal initial velocity. If its transit time is $2$ ms and its vertical acceleration is $5000$ m/s², what is its vertical displacement in millimeters?

- $-10$
- $20$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$

## Explanation

The initial vertical velocity is zero. Use one-half the acceleration times the square of the time, converting the time to seconds and the result to millimeters. This gives $10$.
