An electron enters a uniform magnetic field $B = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$ at a speed $v = 2.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a direction that makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the field. Which pair gives the radius and then the pitch of the helix? ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

- $(5.7\,\mathrm{mm},6.2\,\mathrm{cm})$
- $(11.4\,\mathrm{mm},6.2\,\mathrm{cm})$
- $(5.7\,\mathrm{mm},3.1\,\mathrm{cm})$
- $(6.2\,\mathrm{cm},5.7\,\mathrm{mm})$

## Explanation

The transverse component sets the radius, while the parallel component sets the distance along the field in one turn. $r = \dfrac{m_e v \sin 30^\circ}{e B} \approx 5.7\,\mathrm{mm}$, $p = \dfrac{2\pi m_e v \cos 30^\circ}{e B} \approx 6.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
