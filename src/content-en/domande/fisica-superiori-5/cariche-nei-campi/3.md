Two plates $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart have a potential difference of $120\,\mathrm V$. Neglecting edge effects, which statement describes an electron's acceleration between them if $e/m_e=1.76\cdot10^{11}\,\mathrm{C/kg}$?

- $2.4\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ towards the negative plate
- $4.2\cdot10^{15}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ towards the negative plate
- $4.2\cdot10^{15}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ towards the positive plate
- $2.1\cdot10^{13}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ towards the positive plate

## Explanation

The field is $E=120/0.005=24000\,\mathrm{V/m}$. The acceleration magnitude is $(e/m_e)E$. Negative charge makes the acceleration point against the field, towards the positive plate.
