A particle enters the space between two plates with a horizontal initial velocity. If its transit time is $4$ ms and its vertical acceleration is $9000$ m/s², what is its vertical displacement in millimeters?

- $72$
- $\dfrac{9}{125}$
- $36$
- $144$

## Explanation

The initial vertical velocity is zero. Use one-half the acceleration times the square of the time, converting the time to seconds and the result to millimeters. This gives $72$.
