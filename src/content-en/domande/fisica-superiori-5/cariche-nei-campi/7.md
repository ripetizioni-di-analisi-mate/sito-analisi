An electron enters a $0.10\,\mathrm T$ field perpendicularly. Another electron enters at twice its speed, still in the nonrelativistic regime. How do the second electron's orbit radius and cyclotron frequency compare with the first?

- Twice the radius, same frequency
- Same radius, twice the frequency
- Twice the radius, half the frequency
- Half the radius, same frequency

## Explanation

The radius $r=m_ev/(eB)$ grows with $v$. Circumference and speed double together, leaving the time per turn equal to $2\pi m_e/(eB)$.
