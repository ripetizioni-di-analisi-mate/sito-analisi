In the nonrelativistic regime, a particle with $\dfrac{|q|}{m}=4$ C/kg moves perpendicular to a magnetic field of $B=2$ T. What is its cyclotron angular frequency?

- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency of the circular motion is $\omega_c=\dfrac{|q|B}{m}$. Multiply the charge-to-mass ratio by the field strength. This gives $\omega_c=8$ radians per second.
