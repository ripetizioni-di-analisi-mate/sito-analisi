In a velocity selector, plates $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart have a potential difference of $30\,\mathrm V$. What magnetic field magnitude transmits ions moving at $3.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to both fields without deflection?

- $200\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{mT}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{mT}$

## Explanation

The electric and magnetic forces must have equal magnitudes and opposite directions. The electric field is $6000\,\mathrm{V/m}$ and $B=E/v=0.020\,\mathrm T$.
