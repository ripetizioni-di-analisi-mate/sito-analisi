In a velocity selector the crossed fields have magnitudes $7000$ V/m and $3$ T. At what speed do the opposing forces have equal magnitudes?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{7000}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7000}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $21000\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Set $|q|E=|q|vB$. The magnitude of the charge cancels, and the speed is the ratio of the field strengths. The result is $\dfrac{7000}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
