A planar circular track appears elliptical in an oblique photograph. In an orthogonal projection with no magnification, which ellipse axis preserves the circle's diameter?

- The major axis
- The minor axis
- The mean of the axes
- The product of the axes

## Explanation

Projection shortens the tilted dimension. The direction parallel to both the circle plane and the image plane retains its original length.
