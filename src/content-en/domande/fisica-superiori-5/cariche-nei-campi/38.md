A small positive charge is released from rest at a point where the field line through it is curved. Only the electric force is considered. Which statement about its trajectory is true?

- It coincides with the field line at all its points
- At the starting point it is tangent to the field line, then in general it departs from it
- It is perpendicular to the field line at the starting point
- It is an arc of a parabola with its axis parallel to the field at the starting point

## Explanation

At the initial instant the velocity is zero and the acceleration is $\dfrac{q\,\vec{E}}{m}$, tangent to the field line, so the charge starts along the tangent to the line.

Immediately afterward the charge has a velocity along the old tangent, while the curved line changes direction. The force determines the acceleration and not the velocity, therefore the velocity does not remain tangent to the line and the trajectory **departs** from the field line.

Field line and trajectory coincide only in special cases, for example when the field line is straight.
