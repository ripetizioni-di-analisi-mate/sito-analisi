A particle enters a magnetic field perpendicular to its velocity. The radius of its path is $7$ cm. Its speed doubles and the magnetic field strength triples. What is the new radius in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{14}{3}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{6}$
- $42$

## Explanation

For circular motion, $r=mv/(|q|B)$. With fixed mass and charge, the ratio of the new radius to the original radius is two-thirds. This gives $\dfrac{14}{3}$.
