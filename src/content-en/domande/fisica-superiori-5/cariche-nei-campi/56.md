A particle enters the space between two plates with a horizontal initial velocity. If its transit time is $3$ ms and its vertical acceleration is $7000$ m/s², what is its vertical displacement in millimeters?

- $21$
- $\dfrac{63}{2}$
- $63$
- $\dfrac{63}{2000}$

## Explanation

The initial vertical velocity is zero. Use one-half the acceleration times the square of the time, converting the time to seconds and the result to millimeters. This gives $\dfrac{63}{2}$.
