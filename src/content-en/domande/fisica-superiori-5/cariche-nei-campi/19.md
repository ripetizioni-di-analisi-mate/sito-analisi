A particle follows a helix with a velocity component of $5$ m/s parallel to the field and a period of $0.2$ s. What is the pitch of the helix?

- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic field does not change the velocity component parallel to it. In one period the particle travels a distance $p=v_\parallel T$ along the field. The pitch is therefore $5\cdot0.2=1$ meters.
