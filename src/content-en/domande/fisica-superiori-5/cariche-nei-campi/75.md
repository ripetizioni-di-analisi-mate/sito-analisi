A helical track makes five turns in $10\,\mu\mathrm s$ while advancing $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ along the field. What is the longitudinal velocity component?

- $3\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.5\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Divide total axial advance by total time because magnetic force has no longitudinal component. This gives $0.15/10^{-5}=15000\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
