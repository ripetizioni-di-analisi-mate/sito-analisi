A deuteron, of charge $e$ and mass $3.34 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, enters with speed $v = 2.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, perpendicular to the edges, a strip of width $d = 9.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ containing a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.40\,\mathrm{T}$ into the page. By what angle is the deuteron's velocity deflected when it leaves the strip?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,-1.6 0,2.4;2.4,-1.6 2.4,2.4" segmenti="0,-1.9,2.4,-1.9" vettori="-1.8,0,-0.4,0,v" punti="-1.8,0,pieno" etichette="1.1,-2.3,d;0.4,-1.0,⊗;0.4,0.5,⊗;0.4,2.0,⊗;1.6,-1.0,⊗;1.6,0.5,⊗;1.6,2.0,⊗" xmin=-2.4 xmax=3.2 ymin=-2.6 ymax=2.6 titolo="The deuteron enters perpendicular to the edges the strip of width $d$, where the field is uniform and into the page"}

- About $60^\circ$
- About $49^\circ$
- About $30^\circ$
- About $90^\circ$

## Explanation

In the strip the deuteron moves along a circular arc of radius
$$
r = \frac{m v}{e\,B} = \frac{3.34 \cdot 10^{-27} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^6}{1.60 \cdot 10^{-19} \cdot 0.40}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 0.104\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The center of the arc lies on the entry edge, because the initial velocity is perpendicular to the edge. Since $r > d$, the deuteron reaches the opposite edge and leaves.

When the radius has rotated by the angle $\theta$, the distance from the entry edge is $r\sin\theta$, and the velocity, perpendicular to the radius, has rotated by the same angle. At the exit $r\sin\theta = d$, so $\sin\theta = \dfrac{9.0}{10.4} \approx 0.87$ and $\theta \approx 60^\circ$. The value $49^\circ$ corresponds to $\dfrac{d}{r} \approx 0.87\,\mathrm{rad}$, used in place of the sine.
