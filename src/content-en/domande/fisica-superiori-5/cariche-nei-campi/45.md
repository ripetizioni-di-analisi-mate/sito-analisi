In a spectrometer, two ions with the same charge and speed enter a uniform magnetic field perpendicular to their velocity. The radius of the first ion’s path is twice that of the second. How does its mass compare with that of the second ion?

- The same.
- Twice as large.
- Half as large.
- Four times as large.

## Explanation

At fixed speed, charge, and field strength, the radius is directly proportional to the mass. The ratio of the radii is therefore also the ratio of the masses.
