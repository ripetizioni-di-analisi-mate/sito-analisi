A strip uses longitudinal current leads and transverse Hall-voltage contacts. Why should the transverse voltmeter draw negligible current?

- To make the magnetic field vanish
- To reverse the longitudinal current
- To avoid substantially discharging the lateral charge buildup
- To increase gravity on the carriers

## Explanation

Hall voltage comes from transverse charge separation. A low-resistance path across the edges disturbs the voltage being measured.
