An electron is launched with speed $v_0 = 3.0 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal, in a region where there is a uniform vertical field $E = 200\,\mathrm{N/C}$ pointing upward. At what horizontal distance does it return to the starting height?

- $22\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $44\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The force on the electron is $-e\vec{E}$, pointing downward, so the electron has a constant downward acceleration of magnitude $a = \dfrac{eE}{m_e} \approx 3.51 \cdot 10^{13}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and moves like a projectile.

The range of a projectile with acceleration $a$ is $D = \dfrac{v_0^2 \sin 2\theta}{a}$, so
$$
D = \frac{\left( 3.0 \cdot 10^{6} \right)^2 \cdot \sin 60^\circ}{3.51 \cdot 10^{13}}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 0.22\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Therefore the electron returns to the starting height at about $22\,\mathrm{cm}$.
