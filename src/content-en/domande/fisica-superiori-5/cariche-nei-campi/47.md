A particle of mass $3$ g and charge $7$ µC starts from rest in a uniform electric field of $4000$ N/C. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $\dfrac{28}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{750}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{12}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{28000}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the force is $qE$. Express the charge and mass in SI units and divide the force by the mass. The result is $\dfrac{28}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
