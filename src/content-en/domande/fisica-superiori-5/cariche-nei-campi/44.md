A particle follows a helix with a velocity component of $10$ m/s parallel to the field and a period of $0.2$ s. What is the pitch of the helix?

- $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic field does not change the velocity component parallel to it. In one period the particle travels a distance $p=v_\parallel T$ along the field. The pitch is therefore $10\cdot0.2=2$ meters.
