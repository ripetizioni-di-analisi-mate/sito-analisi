A particle of mass $2$ g and charge $5$ µC starts from rest in a uniform electric field of $3000$ N/C. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7500\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{400}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the force is $qE$. Express the charge and mass in SI units and divide the force by the mass. The result is $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
