An electron starts from rest and is accelerated through a potential difference of magnitude $500$ V. What is its final kinetic energy in electronvolts?

- $1000$
- $500$
- $5$
- $5000$

## Explanation

An electronvolt is the energy gained by a particle with one elementary charge in magnitude when accelerated through one volt. The final energy in electronvolts is numerically equal to the accelerating voltage in volts. The result is $500$.
