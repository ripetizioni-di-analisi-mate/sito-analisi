A photograph shows a circular arc left by a particle in a uniform magnetic field perpendicular to the photograph. The field direction is known and only the magnetic force acts. What additional information determines the charge sign?

- The particle mass alone.
- The total length of the photographed arc.
- A more precise measurement of the radius alone.
- The time order of two successive points on the track.

## Explanation

The centre of curvature gives the force direction, but a track without an arrow admits either direction of travel. Two time-ordered positions determine the tangential velocity direction and hence the sign of $q$ in the Lorentz force.
