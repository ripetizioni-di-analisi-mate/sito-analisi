A particle enters a magnetic field perpendicular to its velocity. The radius of its path is $5$ cm. Its speed doubles and the magnetic field strength triples. What is the new radius in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $30$

## Explanation

For circular motion, $r=mv/(|q|B)$. With fixed mass and charge, the ratio of the new radius to the original radius is two-thirds. This gives $\dfrac{10}{3}$.
