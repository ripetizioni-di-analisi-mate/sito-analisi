An electron enters with speed $v_0 = 2.0 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ a region where there is a uniform field $E = 500\,\mathrm{N/C}$ parallel to the velocity and in the same direction. What distance does it travel before stopping?

- $0.23\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The force on the electron is $-e\vec{E}$, opposite to the velocity, so the electron decelerates with $a = \dfrac{eE}{m_e}$.

From the relation $0 = v_0^2 - 2a\,s$ we get
$$
s = \frac{m_e v_0^2}{2eE} = \frac{9.11 \cdot 10^{-31} \cdot \left( 2.0 \cdot 10^{6} \right)^2}{2 \cdot 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19} \cdot 500}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2.3 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Therefore the electron stops after about $2.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and then turns back.
