A velocity selector has crossed fields of magnitudes $E=1000$ V/m and $B=0.1$ T. What speed does it select?

- $100\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For the particle to pass through without deflection, the electric and magnetic forces must cancel. Their magnitudes satisfy $|q|E=|q|vB$, so $v=\dfrac{E}{B}$. This gives $v=10000$ meters per second.
