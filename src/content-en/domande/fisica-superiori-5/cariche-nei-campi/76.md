In a uniform field a helix has decreasing pitch while its radius stays constant. Which additional force is consistent with this observation?

- A force instantly reversing charge
- A force increasing only transverse speed
- An axial force slowing the longitudinal motion
- None, this always happens in a uniform field

## Explanation

Constant radius indicates constant transverse speed. For constant period a smaller pitch requires a smaller longitudinal velocity.
