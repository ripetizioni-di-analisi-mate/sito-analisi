Two ions have the same velocity perpendicular to the same magnetic field. The first has charge $e$ and an orbital radius of $5$ cm. The second ion has three times the mass of the first and carries charge $2e$. What is the second ion’s orbital radius in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $30$

## Explanation

The radius is proportional to the mass and inversely proportional to the magnitude of the charge. The combined factor is three-halves. This gives $\dfrac{15}{2}$.
