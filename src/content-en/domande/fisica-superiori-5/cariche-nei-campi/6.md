An electron moves in a circle of radius $r = 4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 2.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to its velocity. What is the electron's kinetic energy in electronvolts? ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

- $1760\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $88\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $880\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $440\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The field bends the path and the radius determines speed, which enters the kinetic energy quadratically. $v = \dfrac{e B r}{m_e} \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $K \approx 8.8 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{eV}$.
