A proton travels at speed $v = 5.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.80\,\mathrm{T}$. Calculate the radius of its path. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

- $6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $65\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.65\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Magnetic force provides the centripetal acceleration. Equating $evB$ and $m_pv^2/r$ cancels one factor of speed. $r = \dfrac{m_p v}{e B} \approx 6.5 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$.
