A particle enters horizontally at $v_x=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ into a region of length $2\,\mathrm m$, where only a vertical electric field acts. How long does it take to cross the region?

- $0.05\,\mathrm s$
- $0.4\,\mathrm s$
- $0.2\,\mathrm s$
- $0.1\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The vertical electric field does not change the horizontal velocity. The crossing time is therefore $t=\dfrac{L}{v_x}$. The result is $0.1\,\mathrm s$.
