In the nonrelativistic regime a cyclotron operates at $9$ MHz for a particular particle. What frequency, in megahertz, is required if the magnetic field is reduced to one-third of its original strength?

- $27$
- $9$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

The frequency is $|q|B/(2\pi m)$. With charge and mass fixed, reducing the magnetic field to one-third of its original strength reduces the frequency in the same proportion. The result is $3$.
