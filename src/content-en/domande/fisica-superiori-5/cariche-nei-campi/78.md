A selector transmits particles of speed $v_0$ undeflected. A neutral atom enters in the same direction at $v_0/2$. Neglecting polarization and magnetic moment, what is expected?

- The atom stops at the centre
- The atom is always repelled
- The atom also travels straight
- The atom accelerates to $v_0$

## Explanation

Both selector forces are proportional to charge. For a neutral atom they vanish separately, independently of speed.
