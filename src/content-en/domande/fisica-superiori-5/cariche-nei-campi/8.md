An electron moves to the right in the plane of the page in a uniform magnetic field pointing out of the page. Determine the direction of the Lorentz force on the electron at this instant and the direction in which the electron travels around its circular path.

- Upward force and counterclockwise rotation
- Upward force and clockwise rotation
- Downward force and clockwise rotation
- Rightward force and a straight path

## Explanation

For positive charge, rightward velocity crossed with an outward field would give a downward force. The negative charge reverses it. A particle initially moving right curves upwards, producing counterclockwise motion.
