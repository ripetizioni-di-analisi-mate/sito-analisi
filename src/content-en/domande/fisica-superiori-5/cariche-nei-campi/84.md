In an ideal cyclotron a particle crosses the accelerating gap twice per turn. Each crossing adds $3\,\mathrm{keV}$. How much energy is gained in five full turns?

- $6\,\mathrm{keV}$
- $15\,\mathrm{keV}$
- $60\,\mathrm{keV}$
- $30\,\mathrm{keV}$

## Explanation

The magnetic field bends the track without doing work. Ten electric-gap crossings give $10\cdot3=30\,\mathrm{keV}$.
