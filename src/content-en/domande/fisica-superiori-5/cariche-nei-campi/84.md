In a Thomson experiment, the ratio $|q|/m$ is found from $v/(Br)$. The radius was recorded as $9$ cm instead of $18$ cm. By what factor does this multiply the estimated ratio?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

At fixed speed and field strength, the estimate is inversely proportional to the recorded radius. Entering half the actual radius doubles the estimate. The factor is $2$.
