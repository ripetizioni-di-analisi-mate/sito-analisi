A classical cyclotron with $B=1.5\,\mathrm T$ accelerates alpha particles with charge $2e$ and mass $4m_p$. A proton makes 23 million revolutions per second in the same field. What fundamental accelerating frequency should be used for the alpha particles?

- About $46\,\mathrm{MHz}$
- About $11.5\,\mathrm{MHz}$
- About $23\,\mathrm{MHz}$
- About $5.8\,\mathrm{MHz}$

## Explanation

The frequency is $qB/(2\pi m)$. An alpha particle has half the proton's charge-to-mass ratio. The voltage must reverse every half-turn, completing one cycle per revolution.
