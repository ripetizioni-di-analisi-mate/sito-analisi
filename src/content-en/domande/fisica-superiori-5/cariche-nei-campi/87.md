The Hall field in a sample is $27$ V/m. At fixed current density and carrier concentration, the magnetic field is reduced to one-third of its original strength. What is the new magnitude of the Hall field in volts per meter?

- $27$
- $81$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

At transverse equilibrium, the Hall field balances the magnetic force per unit charge. At fixed drift speed, it is proportional to the magnetic field strength. The result is $9$.
