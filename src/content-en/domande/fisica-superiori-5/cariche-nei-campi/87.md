A Hall probe has a fixed zero offset. At one point it reads $+8\,\mathrm{mV}$, and after reversing only the field it reads $-4\,\mathrm{mV}$. What is the magnitude of the Hall signal alone?

- $2\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mV}$

## Explanation

The Hall contribution reverses while the offset stays fixed. Half the reading difference isolates the signal, $[8-(-4)]/2=6\,\mathrm{mV}$.
