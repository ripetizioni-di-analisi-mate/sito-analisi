A small sphere of mass $m = 2.0\,\mathrm{g}$ and charge $q = +4.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ is released from rest in a region with a uniform horizontal electric field of magnitude $E = 2.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Only its weight, with $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the electric force act on it. What path does the sphere follow?

- A straight segment inclined at $22^\circ$ to the vertical
- An arc of a parabola with a horizontal axis
- A straight segment inclined at $68^\circ$ to the vertical
- An arc of a parabola with a vertical axis

## Explanation

The weight $m\vec{g}$ and the electric force $q\vec{E}$ are constant, so the net force is constant and the sphere has a constant acceleration.

The sphere starts from rest, therefore it moves along the line of the acceleration and the path is a straight segment, with velocity and acceleration always parallel.

The angle $\alpha$ of the line with the vertical satisfies
$$
\tan\alpha = \frac{qE}{mg} = \frac{4.0 \cdot 10^{-6} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 10^{3}}{2.0 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 9.81} \approx 0.41 ,
$$
so $\alpha \approx 22^\circ$. A parabola would occur only with an initial velocity not parallel to the net force.
