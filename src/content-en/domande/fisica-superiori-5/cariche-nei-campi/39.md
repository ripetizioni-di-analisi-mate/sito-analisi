A particle follows a helix with a velocity component of $9$ m/s parallel to the field and a period of $0.2$ s. What is the pitch of the helix?

- $45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic field does not change the velocity component parallel to it. In one period the particle travels a distance $p=v_\parallel T$ along the field. The pitch is therefore $9\cdot0.2=1.8$ meters.
