A particle with $\dfrac{m}{|q|}=0.02$ kg/C enters a magnetic field of $B=0.5$ T at a speed of $7$ m/s perpendicular to the field. What is the radius of its path?

- $0.07\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $175\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force supplies the centripetal force, so $r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}$. Substituting the given ratio yields $r=\dfrac{0.02\cdot7}{0.5}$. Therefore, $r=0.28$ meters.
