A particle follows a helix with a velocity component of $6$ m/s parallel to the field and a period of $0.2$ s. What is the pitch of the helix?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic field does not change the velocity component parallel to it. In one period the particle travels a distance $p=v_\parallel T$ along the field. The pitch is therefore $6\cdot0.2=1.2$ meters.
