A proton travels at speed $v = 1.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What potential difference in magnitude would accelerate the proton from rest to the stated speed? ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

- $0.52\,\mathrm{kV}$
- $5.2\,\mathrm{kV}$
- $2.6\,\mathrm{kV}$
- $10.4\,\mathrm{kV}$

## Explanation

Work by the electric field supplies the final kinetic energy, so potential difference in magnitude is energy divided by charge. $K \approx 8.4 \cdot 10^{-16}\,\mathrm{J} \approx 5.2 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{eV}$, so $\Delta V \approx 5.2\,\mathrm{kV}$.
