In a velocity selector the crossed fields have magnitudes $9000$ V/m and $4$ T. At what speed do the opposing forces have equal magnitudes?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2250\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2250}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Set $|q|E=|q|vB$. The magnitude of the charge cancels, and the speed is the ratio of the field strengths. The result is $2250\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
