A circular detector region has a field normal to its plane. A particle reaches its edge tangentially with initial magnetic force directed outward. Which description applies immediately after this grazing contact?

- It must complete a full circle inside
- It leaves the region and cannot complete a circular orbit inside it
- It stays at rest on the edge
- It follows the edge at any field strength

## Explanation

The curvature implied by the force points outward. The uniform-field circular solution applies only while the particle remains in the magnetic region.
