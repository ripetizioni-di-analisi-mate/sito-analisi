A particle enters a uniform magnetic field of $2$ T with its velocity parallel to the field. Neglect all other forces. What path does it follow?

- A helix with nonzero pitch.
- A parabola with constant acceleration.
- A circle of constant radius.
- A straight line at constant speed.

## Explanation

The cross product of the velocity and the magnetic field is zero. The force is therefore zero, and the particle continues at its constant initial velocity.
