A selector has a finite-width exit slit. Why can some ions slightly away from $E/B$ still reach the slit?

- Selection depends only on beam colour
- Their charge vanishes inside the slit
- Their deflection may fit inside the available half-width
- Each ion experiences a different value of the set fields

## Explanation

A speed mismatch produces a residual transverse force. If the accumulated displacement is small, the ion can still pass through the opening.
