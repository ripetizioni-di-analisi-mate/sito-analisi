In the nonrelativistic regime, particles in a beam follow semicircles of radius $5$ cm. Their momentum doubles while the magnetic field and charge remain unchanged. What is the new diameter in centimeters?

- $10$
- $20$
- $5$
- $40$

## Explanation

The radius is $p/(|q|B)$. Doubling the momentum doubles the radius. Multiply the new radius by two to obtain the diameter. This gives $20$.
