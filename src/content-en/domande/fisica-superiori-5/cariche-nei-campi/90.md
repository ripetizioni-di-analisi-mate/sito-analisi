A cloud-chamber track bends more tightly as it progresses. The field and charge state stay fixed and the motion is transverse. Which quantity is decreasing?

- Charge magnitude
- Applied field magnitude
- Transverse momentum
- Separation of the magnet poles

## Explanation

Since $p_\perp=\lvert q\rvert Br$, the radius is proportional to transverse momentum. A smaller radius signals its loss.
