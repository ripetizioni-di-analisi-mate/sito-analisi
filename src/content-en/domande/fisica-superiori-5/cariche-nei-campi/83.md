Two peak centres on a plate are $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart. Each beam leaves a strip $1.6\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide about its centre. Do the strips overlap?

- Yes, by $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- They just touch
- Yes, by $0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- No, there is a $0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$ gap

## Explanation

The inward half-widths total $0.8+0.8=1.6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Subtracting centre spacing leaves a $0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$ overlap.
