An initially stationary electron is released in a uniform electric field pointing upward. Neglecting its weight, in which direction does it accelerate?

- Upward
- It remains at rest
- Downward
- Horizontally

## Explanation

The electron's charge is negative. In the relation $\vec a=\dfrac{q\vec E}{m}$, the coefficient of the field is therefore negative. The acceleration is opposite to the field.
