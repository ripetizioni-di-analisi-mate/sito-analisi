A beam leaves deflection plates with $v_y/v_x=0.04$ and enters a field-free region. A screen is $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ farther along $x$. What additional vertical displacement occurs there?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the force-free region the path is straight with slope $v_y/v_x$. The vertical change is $0.04\cdot25=1\,\mathrm{cm}$.
