A particle of mass $4$ g and charge $9$ µC starts from rest in a uniform electric field of $5000$ N/C. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $11250\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{20}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{800}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{45}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the force is $qE$. Express the charge and mass in SI units and divide the force by the mass. The result is $\dfrac{45}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
