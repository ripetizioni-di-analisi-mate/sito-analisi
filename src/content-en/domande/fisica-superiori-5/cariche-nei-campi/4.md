An electron enters parallel to two plates of length $L=6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ with zero initial transverse velocity. The field between the plates is uniform and transverse. Its sideways displacement at exit is $y=1.4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What angle does the exit velocity make with the initial direction?

- $1.3^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $2.7^\circ$
- $5.4^\circ$

## Explanation

Transverse velocity increases uniformly from zero, so its average is half its final value. From $y=v_{y,f}t/2$ and $L=v_x t$ it follows that $\tan\theta=2y/L\simeq0.047$.
