Why does a highly relativistic particle lose synchronism with a fixed-frequency classical cyclotron?

- The magnetic field necessarily becomes electric
- Electric charge increases at each turn
- The revolution period lengthens as energy rises
- The accelerating gap changes position after each turn

## Explanation

The relativistic period is $2\pi\gamma m/(\lvert q\rvert B)$. The factor $\gamma$ grows while the source period remains fixed.
