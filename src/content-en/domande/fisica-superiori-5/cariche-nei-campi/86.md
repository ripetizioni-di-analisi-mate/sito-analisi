The Hall field in a sample is $21$ V/m. At fixed current density and carrier concentration, the magnetic field is reduced to one-third of its original strength. What is the new magnitude of the Hall field in volts per meter?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $21$
- $63$
- $7$

## Explanation

At transverse equilibrium, the Hall field balances the magnetic force per unit charge. At fixed drift speed, it is proportional to the magnetic field strength. The result is $7$.
