A particle with $\dfrac{|q|}{m}=8$ C/kg is in a uniform electric field of $4$ N/C. What is the magnitude of its acceleration due to the electric force alone?

- $32\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the electric force is $F=|q|E$. Dividing by the mass gives $a=\dfrac{|q|}{m}E$. The acceleration therefore has magnitude $8\cdot4=32$ meters per second squared.
