A particle follows a helical path in a magnetic field with a period of $2$ µs. Its velocity component parallel to the field is $5000$ m/s. What is the pitch in millimeters?

- $10000$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The pitch is the displacement along the field in one revolution. Multiply the parallel velocity component by the period, converting the time from microseconds and the result to millimeters. This gives $10$.
