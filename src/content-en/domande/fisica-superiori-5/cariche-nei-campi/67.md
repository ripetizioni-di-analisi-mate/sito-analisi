A beam enters horizontal deflection plates that give constant vertical acceleration. Why does doubling entry speed at fixed length and field reduce exit displacement to a quarter?

- Time halves and vertical displacement is proportional to its square
- The electric field halves
- Mass doubles in the nonrelativistic model
- The charge drops by a factor of four

## Explanation

Horizontal speed stays constant, so $t=L/v_x$. Starting with zero vertical speed gives displacement $a_y t^2/2$.
