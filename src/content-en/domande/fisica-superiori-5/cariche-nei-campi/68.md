An ion beam crosses two short vertical electric fields giving equal opposite impulses, separated by a field-free gap. After both zones, which statement is certain?

- The charge changes sign
- Kinetic energy becomes zero
- Vertical momentum returns to its initial value
- Vertical position returns to its initial value

## Explanation

The impulses cancel in the change of vertical momentum. During the intervening drift, vertical velocity can still produce a displacement.
