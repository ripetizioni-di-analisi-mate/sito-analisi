A particle enters a magnetic field perpendicular to its velocity. The radius of its path is $9$ cm. Its speed doubles and the magnetic field strength triples. What is the new radius in centimeters?

- $\dfrac{27}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $54$
- $6$

## Explanation

For circular motion, $r=mv/(|q|B)$. With fixed mass and charge, the ratio of the new radius to the original radius is two-thirds. This gives $6$.
