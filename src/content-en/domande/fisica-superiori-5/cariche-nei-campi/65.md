An electron enters horizontally halfway between plates separated by $8\,\mathrm{mm}$. An unlimited-plate calculation predicts a $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ exit displacement. What actually happens for plates of that length?

- It automatically returns to the axis
- It hits a plate before exiting
- It exits 5 mm off axis without a collision
- It stops in the middle

## Explanation

Only $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ separates the centre from a plate. The growing displacement reaches that distance before the predicted exit.
