An electric impulse parallel to a magnetic field reverses only the longitudinal velocity of a particle on a helix. What happens immediately afterwards?

- Axial travel reverses with the same radius
- The radius doubles
- The particle comes to rest
- Rotation seen along the field reverses

## Explanation

The impulse leaves the transverse component, which determines radius and rotational sense, unchanged. The axial component reverses sign.
