A particle starts from rest and travels $4$ m along a uniform electric field in $2$ s. What is the magnitude of its acceleration in meters per second squared?

- $4$
- $1$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

In the uniform field the acceleration is constant. With zero initial velocity, the position equation gives twice the distance traveled divided by the square of the time. The result is $2$.
