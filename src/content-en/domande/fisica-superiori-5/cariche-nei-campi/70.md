Two particles trace the same circular arc in the same direction in a uniform magnetic field at different speeds. Which magnitude must match if they enter perpendicular to the field?

- Kinetic energy
- Revolution period
- Mass
- Momentum per unit charge

## Explanation

Radius measures magnetic rigidity, $p/\lvert q\rvert=Br$. The same path and field fix this ratio even when speeds and masses differ.
