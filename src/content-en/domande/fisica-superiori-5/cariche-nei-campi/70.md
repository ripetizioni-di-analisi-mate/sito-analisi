In a velocity selector the crossed fields have magnitudes $5000$ V/m and $2$ T. At what speed do the opposing forces have equal magnitudes?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2500\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2500}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Set $|q|E=|q|vB$. The magnitude of the charge cancels, and the speed is the ratio of the field strengths. The result is $2500\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
