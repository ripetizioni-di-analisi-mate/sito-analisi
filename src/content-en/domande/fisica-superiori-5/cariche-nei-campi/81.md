In the nonrelativistic regime, particles in a beam follow semicircles of radius $9$ cm. Their momentum doubles while the magnetic field and charge remain unchanged. What is the new diameter in centimeters?

- $9$
- $18$
- $36$
- $72$

## Explanation

The radius is $p/(|q|B)$. Doubling the momentum doubles the radius. Multiply the new radius by two to obtain the diameter. This gives $36$.
