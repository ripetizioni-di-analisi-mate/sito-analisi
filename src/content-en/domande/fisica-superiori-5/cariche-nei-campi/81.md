A Thomson measurement uses $e/m=E/(B^2r)$. The field $B$ has a small relative uncertainty of 3%, with other uncertainties negligible. Approximately what relative uncertainty does this contribute to $e/m$?

- $1.5\%$
- $3\%$
- $9\%$
- $6\%$

## Explanation

For small changes the magnitude of the exponent multiplies the relative variation. Dependence on $B^{-2}$ gives roughly $2\cdot3\%=6\%$.
