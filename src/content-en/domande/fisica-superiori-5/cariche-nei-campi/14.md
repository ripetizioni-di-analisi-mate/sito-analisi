A particle follows a helix with a velocity component of $4$ m/s parallel to the field and a period of $0.2$ s. What is the pitch of the helix?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnetic field does not change the velocity component parallel to it. In one period the particle travels a distance $p=v_\parallel T$ along the field. The pitch is therefore $4\cdot0.2=0.8$ meters.
