A particle of charge $+2e$ has $600\,\mathrm{eV}$ of kinetic energy. It crosses a region with a $200\,\mathrm V$ rise in potential. How much kinetic energy remains?

- $200\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $400\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $800\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Potential energy rises by $2e\cdot200\,\mathrm V=400\,\mathrm{eV}$. The remaining kinetic energy is therefore $600-400=200\,\mathrm{eV}$.
