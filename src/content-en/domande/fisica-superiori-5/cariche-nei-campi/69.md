A nonrelativistic calculation for an electron accelerated from rest gives $v=1.2c$. Which conclusion is warranted?

- The electron has reached $1.2c$
- The classical energy–speed relation must be replaced
- The field always does zero work
- Elementary charge changed during the motion

## Explanation

Electrical work still determines the energy change. At these energies $K=mv^2/2$ is outside its validity range and the relativistic relation is required.
