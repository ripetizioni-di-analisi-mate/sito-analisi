A snapshot of a pulse in vacuum shows the peak at $x=2\,\mathrm m$ at $t=0$. The profile translates without distortion according to $f(x-ct)$ with $c=0.30\,\mathrm{m/ns}$. Where should the peak be marked on the snapshot taken $10\,\mathrm{ns}$ later?

::grafico{f="exp(-2*(x-2)^2)" xmin=-1.5 xmax=7.5 ymin=-0.25 ymax=1.3 nomi="x,E" punti="2,1,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,1" titolo="Profile of the pulse at the instant $t = 0$, with $x$ in meters and $E$ in units of the maximum value"}

- At $x = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $x = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $x = -1.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The graph of $f(x - ct)$ at the instant $t$ is the graph of $f$ translated by the distance $ct$ in the positive direction of the $x$ axis, so the profile moves without changing shape.
In $10\,\mathrm{ns}$ the pulse travels $ct = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8} \cdot 1.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
Therefore the maximum is at $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m} + 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
