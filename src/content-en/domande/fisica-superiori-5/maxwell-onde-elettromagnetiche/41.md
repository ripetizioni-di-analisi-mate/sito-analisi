An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $10$ MHz. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its wavelength?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{30}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum, $\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}$. The frequency is $10\cdot10^6$ Hz. The wavelength is therefore $\lambda=\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{10\cdot10^6}=\dfrac{300}{10}$ m.
