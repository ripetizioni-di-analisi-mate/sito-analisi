An experiment measures peak electric field $E_0$. An analysis incorrectly uses $c\varepsilon_0 E_0^2$ as the mean intensity of a sinusoidal wave. Which field measurement would make $I=c\varepsilon_0 E^2$ correct without an extra factor?

- The signed mean over a period.
- The rms value.
- The peak-to-peak value.
- The field sampled only at zero crossings.

## Explanation

The rms value is defined by $E_{\rm rms}^2=\langle E^2\rangle$. Substituting it gives the time-averaged intensity directly.
