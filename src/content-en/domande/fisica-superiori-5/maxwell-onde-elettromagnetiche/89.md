A plane wave in a vacuum travels along $+z$. Its electric field has amplitude $7$ V/m and is polarized along $x$. Along which axis does the magnetic field oscillate?

- Along the $y$ axis.
- Along the $z$ axis.
- Along the $x$ axis.
- Along the bisector between $x$ and $z$.

## Explanation

The fields are perpendicular to each other and to the propagation direction. The axis perpendicular to both $x$ and $z$ is $y$.
