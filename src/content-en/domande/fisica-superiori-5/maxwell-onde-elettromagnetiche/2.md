A plane wave in a vacuum has a magnetic field amplitude of $2$ nanoteslas. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its electric field amplitude?

- $6\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The amplitudes satisfy $E_0=cB_0$. One nanotesla equals $10^{-9}$ T. The result is therefore $E_0=3\cdot10^8\cdot2\cdot10^{-9}=0.6$ V/m.
