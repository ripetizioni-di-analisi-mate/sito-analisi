An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $4$ MHz. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its wavelength?

- $1200\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{75}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum, $\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}$. The frequency is $4\cdot10^6$ Hz. The wavelength is therefore $\lambda=\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{4\cdot10^6}=\dfrac{300}{4}$ m.
