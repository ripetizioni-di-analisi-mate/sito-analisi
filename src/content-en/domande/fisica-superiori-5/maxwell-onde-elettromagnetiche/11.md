A laser beam of power $P = 20\,\mathrm{W}$ strikes a surface at normal incidence and is completely absorbed. Calculate the force exerted by the beam on the surface ($c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$).

- $6.7\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm N$
- $6.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm N$
- $1.3\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm N$
- $3.3\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Each joule of absorbed radiation transfers momentum $\dfrac{1}{c}$ in SI units. For a fully intercepted beam at normal incidence the force is $\dfrac{P}{c}$, without the doubling that reflection would produce.
