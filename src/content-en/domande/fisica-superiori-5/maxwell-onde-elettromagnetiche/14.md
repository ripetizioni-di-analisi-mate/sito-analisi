A plane wave in vacuum has mean energy density $4.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$. At what average rate does energy cross a unit area perpendicular to the beam? Use $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $1.3\cdot10^{-14}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $6.0\cdot10^2\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $1.2\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $2.4\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

In one second the wave crosses a slab of thickness $c\cdot1\,\mathrm s$. Multiplying mean energy density by speed gives $I=c\bar u=1.2\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
