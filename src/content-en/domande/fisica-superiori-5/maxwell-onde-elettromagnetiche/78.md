An aperture sends a beam onto a disk of radius $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. A lossless lens focuses all the same power onto a disk of radius $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. By what factor does the mean intensity over the disk increase?

- $4$
- $8$
- $16$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

Mean intensity is power divided by area. Reducing the radius fourfold reduces the area sixteenfold, so mean intensity increases sixteenfold.
