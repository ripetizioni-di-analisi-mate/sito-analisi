An unpolarized beam of intensity $I$ passes through two crossed ideal polarizers. A third filter is inserted between them, at $45^\circ$ to each. What intensity emerges?

- $\dfrac{I}{2}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{I}{8}$
- $\dfrac{I}{4}$

## Explanation

The first filter leaves $\dfrac{I}{2}$. At each of the two $45^\circ$ steps half the incoming intensity passes, leaving $\dfrac{I}{8}$.
