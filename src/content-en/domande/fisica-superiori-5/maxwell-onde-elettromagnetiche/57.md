An analyser splits orthogonal components and sends $3\,\mathrm{mW}$ and $1\,\mathrm{mW}$ to its two detectors. The incident beam is linearly polarized and there are no losses. What acute angle does it make with the brighter detector's axis?

- $60^\circ$
- $15^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $45^\circ$

## Explanation

The weaker-to-stronger power ratio is $\tan^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{3}$. The acute angle to the brighter axis is therefore $30^\circ$.
