An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $4$ GHz. What is its wavelength in centimeters? Use $c=3\cdot 10^8$ m/s.

- $120$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $75$

## Explanation

Wavelength is speed divided by frequency. One gigahertz is one billion hertz, and one meter is one hundred centimeters. This gives $\dfrac{15}{2}$.
