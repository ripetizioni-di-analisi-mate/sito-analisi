In a slightly conducting dielectric, a surface carries $3\,\mathrm A$ of conduction current and $2\,\mathrm A$ of displacement current in the same direction. What current follows from dividing the circulation of $B$ by $\mu_0$, assuming the medium has permeability $\mu_0$?

- $5\,\mathrm A$
- $6\,\mathrm A$
- $3\,\mathrm A$
- $1\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The complete law adds the conduction current and displacement current. Since they have the same direction, both values are added with a positive sign. The result is $5\,\mathrm A$.
