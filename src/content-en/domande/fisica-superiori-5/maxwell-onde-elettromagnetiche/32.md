A plane wave in a vacuum has a magnetic field amplitude of $8$ nanoteslas. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its electric field amplitude?

- $24\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $240\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The amplitudes satisfy $E_0=cB_0$. One nanotesla equals $10^{-9}$ T. The result is therefore $E_0=3\cdot10^8\cdot8\cdot10^{-9}=2.4$ V/m.
