An electromagnetic wave of wavelength $9$ m enters a transparent medium in which its speed is one third of its initial speed. What is the new wavelength, in meters?

- $9$
- $27$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

The frequency stays the same at the interface. Wavelength is the ratio of speed to frequency, so it decreases in the same proportion as the speed. This gives $3$.
