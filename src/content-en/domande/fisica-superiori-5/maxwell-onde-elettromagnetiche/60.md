A surface spanning an Amperian loop cuts a capacitor lead. It is deformed to pass between the plates without moving its boundary. Which quantity in the Ampère–Maxwell law stays the same?

- Displacement current alone.
- Conduction current alone.
- The area of the surface.
- The oriented sum of conduction and displacement currents.

## Explanation

Conduction current supplies the term in the wire, whereas changing electric flux supplies it across the gap. Their sum gives the same circulation around the fixed boundary.
