A pulsed laser illuminates a surface that absorbs all the energy it receives. Each pulse carries an energy of $3\,\mathrm{mJ}$, and in $2\,\mathrm s$ the surface absorbs $200$ pulses. What is the mean power absorbed by the surface?

- $0.30\,\mathrm W$
- $0.003\,\mathrm W$
- $0.60\,\mathrm W$
- $1.2\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The energy absorbed in $2\,\mathrm s$ is $200\cdot3\,\mathrm{mJ}=600\,\mathrm{mJ}=0.60\,\mathrm J$. The mean power is the absorbed energy divided by the time, so $P=\dfrac{0.60\,\mathrm J}{2\,\mathrm s}=0.30\,\mathrm W$.
