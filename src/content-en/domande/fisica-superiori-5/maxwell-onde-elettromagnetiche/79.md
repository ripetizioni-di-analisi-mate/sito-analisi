An unpolarized beam of intensity $20$ W/m² passes through two ideal polarizers whose axes make an angle of $45^\circ$. What intensity emerges, in watts per square meter?

- $0$
- $20$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

The first polarizer halves the intensity of the unpolarized beam. The second multiplies this intensity by the square of the cosine of forty-five degrees, which is one half. This gives $5$.
