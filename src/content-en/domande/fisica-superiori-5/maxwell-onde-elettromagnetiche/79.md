A detector absorbs $3\,\mathrm{mJ}$ each time it registers a light packet. It registers $200$ identical packets in $2\,\mathrm s$. What mean power does it receive?

- $0.30\,\mathrm W$
- $0.003\,\mathrm W$
- $0.60\,\mathrm W$
- $1.2\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The total energy is $200\cdot0.003=0.6\,\mathrm J$. Dividing by the two-second observation time gives $0.30\,\mathrm W$.
