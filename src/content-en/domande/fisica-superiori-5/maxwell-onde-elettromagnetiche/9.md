A sinusoidal plane wave travelling in vacuum has peak electric field $200\,\mathrm{V/m}$. Using $c\varepsilon_0=2.66\cdot10^{-3}$ in SI units, what is its mean intensity in W/m²?

- $53$
- $266$
- $0.53$
- $106$

## Explanation

Instantaneous intensity is proportional to the field squared. The average of $\sin^2$ over a period is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $I=\dfrac{c\varepsilon_0 E_0^2}{2}\simeq53\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
