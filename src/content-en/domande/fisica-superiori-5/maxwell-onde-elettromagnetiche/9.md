A plane wave in a vacuum has an average energy density of $3$ microjoules per cubic meter. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is the average intensity?

- $900\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $9\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $9000\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The average intensity of a plane wave is $I=c\overline u$. The given density is $3\cdot10^{-6}$ joules per cubic meter. The intensity is therefore $I=3\cdot10^8\cdot3\cdot10^{-6}=900$ watts per square meter.
