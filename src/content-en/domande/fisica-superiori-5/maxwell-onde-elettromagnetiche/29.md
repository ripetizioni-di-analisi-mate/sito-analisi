Two coherent waves have the same frequency and polarization. At the detector their amplitudes are $3E$ and $E$ and their phases differ by half a period. What is the intensity in units of that of one wave of amplitude $E$?

- $10$
- $2$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

The fields add with their signs, giving resultant amplitude $3E-E=2E$. Intensity scales with its square, giving four units.
