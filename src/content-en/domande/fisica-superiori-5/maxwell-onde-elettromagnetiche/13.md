Two ideal filters transmit $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of the initially unpolarized light. What acute angle separates their axes?

- $75^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $30^\circ$

## Explanation

The first filter transmits half the intensity. The transmitted fraction after the second is $\dfrac{\cos^2\theta}{2}=\dfrac{3}{8}$, giving $\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\theta=30^\circ$.
