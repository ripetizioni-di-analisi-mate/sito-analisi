Two parallel conducting mirrors are $0.30\,\mathrm m$ apart in vacuum. The tangential electric field has nodes at the surfaces. What is the lowest frequency of a standing wave along the normal to the mirrors, with $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $1\,\mathrm{GHz}$
- $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$
- $100\,\mathrm{MHz}$
- $500\,\mathrm{MHz}$

## Explanation

The lowest mode fits half a wavelength between the walls. Thus $\lambda=2L=0.60\,\mathrm m$ and $f=\dfrac{c}{\lambda}=500\,\mathrm{MHz}$.
