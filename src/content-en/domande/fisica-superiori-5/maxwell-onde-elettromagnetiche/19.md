A plane wave in a vacuum has an average energy density of $5$ microjoules per cubic meter. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is the average intensity?

- $15\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $15000\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{60}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The average intensity of a plane wave is $I=c\overline u$. The given density is $5\cdot10^{-6}$ joules per cubic meter. The intensity is therefore $I=3\cdot10^8\cdot5\cdot10^{-6}=1500$ watts per square meter.
