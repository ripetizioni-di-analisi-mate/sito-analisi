Two antennas separated by a wall opaque to visible light communicate by radio. Which observation suffices to rule out identical absorption in all bands, if reflection and scattering are negligible in both bands?

- The antennas are less than a kilometre apart.
- The source frequencies differ.
- Light and radio waves have different periods.
- The measured transmitted fractions differ at equal incident intensity.

## Explanation

With negligible reflection and scattering, the transmitted and absorbed fractions sum to one. Different transmissions then imply different absorptions.
