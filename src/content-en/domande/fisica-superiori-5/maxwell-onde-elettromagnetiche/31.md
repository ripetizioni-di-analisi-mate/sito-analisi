An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $8$ MHz. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its wavelength?

- $2400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{2}{75}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum, $\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}$. The frequency is $8\cdot10^6$ Hz. The wavelength is therefore $\lambda=\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{8\cdot10^6}=\dfrac{300}{8}$ m.
