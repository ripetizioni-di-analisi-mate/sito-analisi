Three independent layers each transmit $80\%$ of their incident intensity. Ignoring multiple reflections, what percentage of the original intensity emerges from the third?

- $80\%$
- $40\%$
- $60\%$
- $51.2\%$

## Explanation

Each layer acts on the light emerging from the previous one. The surviving fraction is therefore $0.8^3=0.512$.
