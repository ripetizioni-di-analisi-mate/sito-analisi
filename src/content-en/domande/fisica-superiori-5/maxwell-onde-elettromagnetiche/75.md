A beam strikes two surfaces at normal incidence. One absorbs the beam and the other reflects it perfectly. The pressure on the first is $9$ nPa. What is the pressure on the second in nanopascals?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $18$
- $36$

## Explanation

Absorption transfers the incident momentum. At normal incidence, reflection reverses the momentum, doubling the momentum transfer. This gives $18$.
