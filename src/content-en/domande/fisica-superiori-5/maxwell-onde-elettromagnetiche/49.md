A current of $5$ mA flows through the wires of a charging capacitor. What is the displacement current through the entire cross section between the plates, in milliamperes?

- $0$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The change in electric flux between the plates is related to the charge accumulated. Multiplying its rate of change by $\varepsilon_0$ gives the same current that arrives through the wire. This gives $5$.
