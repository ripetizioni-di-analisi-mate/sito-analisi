An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $7$ MHz. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its wavelength?

- $2100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{300}{7}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{7}{300}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{30}{7}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum, $\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}$. The frequency is $7\cdot10^6$ Hz. The wavelength is therefore $\lambda=\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{7\cdot10^6}=\dfrac{300}{7}$ m.
