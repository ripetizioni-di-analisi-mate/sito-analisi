Two antennas receive the same plane wave in vacuum. The second is $\dfrac{3\lambda}{4}$ farther along the propagation direction. What is the shortest positive delay for the same crest to reach the second antenna?

- $3T$
- $\dfrac{T}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3T}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4T}{3}$

## Explanation

A crest travels one wavelength per period. Covering three quarters of a wavelength therefore takes $\dfrac{3T}{4}$.
