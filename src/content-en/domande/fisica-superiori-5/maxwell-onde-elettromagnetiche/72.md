A probe in vacuum measures the electric and magnetic field vectors at the same point and at the same instant. Both are nonzero and they are parallel. What can be concluded about the hypothesis that the fields belong to a single travelling plane wave?

- The hypothesis is incompatible with the measurement.
- The hypothesis becomes valid by increasing frequency.
- This is a property of all radio waves.
- The wave must be circularly polarized.

## Explanation

In a single travelling plane wave the fields are perpendicular. Polarization changes how the transverse electric field oscillates while preserving that orthogonality.
