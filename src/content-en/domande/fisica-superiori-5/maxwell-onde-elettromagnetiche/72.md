A point source radiates uniformly in all directions. At $4$ m the intensity is $36$ W/m². What is the intensity at $8$ m, in watts per square meter?

- $36$
- $18$
- $9$
- $144$

## Explanation

The same power spreads over the surface of a sphere. Doubling the radius quadruples the area. This gives $9$.
