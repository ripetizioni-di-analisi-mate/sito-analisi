A $1\,\mathrm{GHz}$ wave enters a line where signals travel at $2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Two probes are $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart along the line. What phase difference do they observe at the same instant?

- $2\pi$
- $\pi$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

## Explanation

The wavelength in the line is $\dfrac{v}{f}=0.20\,\mathrm m$. The probe spacing is half a wavelength, so the fields are in antiphase.
