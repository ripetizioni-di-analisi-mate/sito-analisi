Magnetic flux through a fixed loop is $2t^2\,\mathrm{mWb}$ with $t$ in seconds. What is the oriented electric field circulation at $t=3\,\mathrm s$, using the positive orientation associated with the flux?

- $-18\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $+12\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{mV}$

## Explanation

Faraday’s law gives minus the instantaneous rate of change of flux. The derivative of $2t^2$ is $4t$, which at the specified time is $12\,\mathrm{mWb/s}$.
