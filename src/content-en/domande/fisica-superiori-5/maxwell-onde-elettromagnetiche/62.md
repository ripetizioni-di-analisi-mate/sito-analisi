The magnetic field amplitude of a plane wave in a vacuum is $7$ nT. What is its electric field amplitude in volts per meter? Use $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s.

- $210$
- $\dfrac{21}{10}$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

For a plane wave in a vacuum, $E_0=cB_0$. After the magnetic field is converted from nanoteslas, the product of the powers of ten is one tenth. This gives $\dfrac{21}{10}$.
