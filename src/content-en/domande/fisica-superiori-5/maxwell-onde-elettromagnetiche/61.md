A charge is moved from the centre of a sphere to a point inside it close to the surface. How does the outward electric flux through the entire sphere change?

- It is unchanged, while the surface field becomes nonuniform.
- It decreases with distance from the centre.
- It vanishes when the charge leaves the centre.
- It increases because the field is stronger on the nearer side.

## Explanation

Gauss's law links total flux to the enclosed charge. Moving the charge changes the local field distribution while leaving the enclosed charge unchanged.
