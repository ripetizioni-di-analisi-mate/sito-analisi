A plane electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has average intensity $7$ W/m². Its electric field amplitude triples. What is its new intensity in watts per square meter?

- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $63$
- $7$

## Explanation

The average intensity is proportional to the square of the electric field amplitude. Tripling the amplitude multiplies the intensity by nine. This gives $63$.
