A monochromatic wave is normally incident on a stationary interface between two transparent linear media. Which quantity has the same value for the incident and transmitted waves?

- Transmitted and incident intensity in all cases.
- Frequency.
- Phase speed.
- Wavelength.

## Explanation

The same oscillation sets the time dependence on both sides. A change of speed shows up in the separation between crests.
