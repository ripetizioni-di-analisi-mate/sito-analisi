Light reflected by a lake is mostly horizontally polarized. How should the transmission axis of polarizing glasses be oriented to suppress it most strongly?

- Vertical.
- Horizontal.
- At $30^\circ$ from horizontal.
- At $45^\circ$ from horizontal.

## Explanation

A polarizer transmits the field parallel to its axis. A vertical axis blocks the horizontal component of the reflection.
