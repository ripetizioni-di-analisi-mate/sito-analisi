An electromagnetic wave of wavelength $5$ m enters a transparent medium in which its speed is one third of its initial speed. What is the new wavelength, in meters?

- $5$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$

## Explanation

The frequency stays the same at the interface. Wavelength is the ratio of speed to frequency, so it decreases in the same proportion as the speed. This gives $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
