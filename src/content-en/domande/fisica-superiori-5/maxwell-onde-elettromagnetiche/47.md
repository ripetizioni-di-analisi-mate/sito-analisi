The magnetic flux through a surface increases at a constant rate of $7$ Wb/s. What is the circulation of the electric field, in volts, using the orientation corresponding to the normal chosen for the flux?

- $7$
- $-7$
- $14$
- $0$

## Explanation

Faraday's law relates the circulation to the time derivative of the magnetic flux. The circulation has the opposite sign to the change in flux. This gives $-7$.
