A parallel-plate capacitor has capacitance $2$ nF, and the voltage across it increases at $10^6$ V/s. Neglecting edge effects, what is the displacement current in milliamperes?

- $2000\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $0.002\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The displacement current is $I_s=C\dfrac{dV}{dt}$. Use $C=2\cdot10^{-9}$ farads. The result is $I_s=2\cdot10^{-3}$ A, or $2$ mA.
