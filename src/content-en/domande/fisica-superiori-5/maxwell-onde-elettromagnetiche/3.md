The potential difference across a parallel-plate capacitor of capacitance $C = 2.0\,\mathrm{nF}$ increases at a constant rate $\dfrac{d(\Delta V)}{dt} = 5.0 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{V/s}$. Neglecting edge effects, calculate the displacement current between the plates.

- $0.10\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

Plate charge is $Q=C\Delta V$. Its time derivative is the wire current, which equals the displacement current between the plates: $i_s=(2.0\cdot10^{-9})(5.0\cdot10^5)\,\mathrm A=10^{-3}\,\mathrm A$.
