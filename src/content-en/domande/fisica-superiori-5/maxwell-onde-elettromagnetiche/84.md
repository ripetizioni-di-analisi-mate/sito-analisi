A half-wave antenna has an active length of approximately $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$. A $1\,\mathrm m$ element sets the right scale for receiving $150\,\mathrm{MHz}$ in vacuum. What length is required when the same type of antenna is tuned to $50\,\mathrm{MHz}$?

- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

At fixed speed, wavelength varies inversely with frequency. Reducing frequency threefold triples both wavelength and resonant antenna length.
