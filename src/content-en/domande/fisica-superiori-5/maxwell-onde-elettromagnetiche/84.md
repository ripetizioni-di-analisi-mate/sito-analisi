Two waves in a vacuum have wavelengths $4$ m and $9$ m. Which statement is correct?

- The wave with the longer wavelength travels faster.
- The frequencies are equal.
- The wave of wavelength $4$ m has the higher frequency.
- The wave of wavelength $9$ m has the higher frequency.

## Explanation

Both waves travel at the same speed. Frequency is the ratio of speed to wavelength, so the wave with the shorter wavelength has the higher frequency.
