A medium is transparent for vacuum wavelengths between $0.4$ and $0.7\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. Which source lies in this window?

- A $10\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ emitter.
- A $532\,\mathrm{nm}$ laser.
- A $250\,\mathrm{nm}$ lamp.
- A $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ emitter.

## Explanation

The transmission window ranges from $400$ to $700\,\mathrm{nm}$. The wavelength $532\,\mathrm{nm}$ lies within it.
