A plane electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has average intensity $9$ W/m². Its electric field amplitude triples. What is its new intensity in watts per square meter?

- $1$
- $81$
- $27$
- $9$

## Explanation

The average intensity is proportional to the square of the electric field amplitude. Tripling the amplitude multiplies the intensity by nine. This gives $81$.
