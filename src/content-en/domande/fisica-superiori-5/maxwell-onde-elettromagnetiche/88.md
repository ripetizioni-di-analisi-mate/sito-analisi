A mirror reflects a pulse of energy $U$ normally back to the source, whereas a second ideal surface lets it pass without absorption or deflection and with unchanged energy in vacuum. What mechanical impulse acts on the second surface?

- Zero.
- $\dfrac{U}{2c}$
- $\dfrac{U}{c}$
- $\dfrac{2U}{c}$

## Explanation

The beam retains its energy and direction on traversing the second surface. Its momentum vector is unchanged, so the surface receives no net impulse.
