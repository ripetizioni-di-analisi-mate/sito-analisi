A beam of intensity $9$ W/m² strikes a surface of area $4$ m² at normal incidence for $5$ s. How much energy reaches the surface, in joules?

- $\dfrac{45}{4}$
- $180$
- $36$
- $45$

## Explanation

The incident power is the intensity multiplied by the area. The energy is that power multiplied by the duration of illumination. This gives $180$.
