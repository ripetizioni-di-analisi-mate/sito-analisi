A catalogue gives vacuum wavelengths $20\,\mathrm{nm}$ for A and $2\,\mathrm{nm}$ for B. Which beam has the shorter period and by what factor?

- B, by a factor of $100$.
- A, by a factor of $10$.
- The periods are equal.
- B, by a factor of $10$.

## Explanation

In vacuum $T=\dfrac{\lambda}{c}$. B's wavelength is one tenth of A's, so its period is also one tenth.
