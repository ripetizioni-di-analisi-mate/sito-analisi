An atlas arranges spectral bands by their typical vacuum wavelengths, from longest to shortest. Which sequence follows this convention?

- Long-wavelength radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays
- Long-wavelength radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, gamma rays, X-rays
- Gamma rays, X-rays, ultraviolet, visible light, infrared, microwaves, long-wavelength radio waves
- Long-wavelength radio waves, microwaves, visible light, infrared, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays

## Explanation

Frequency $f=\dfrac{c}{\lambda}$ increases from left to right. Microwaves are a part of the radio spectrum at shorter wavelengths than long radio waves. Visible light lies between infrared and ultraviolet. At the high-frequency end, X-rays and gamma rays can overlap in energy and are also distinguished by their origin.
