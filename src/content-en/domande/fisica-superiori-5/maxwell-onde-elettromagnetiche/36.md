An electron initially at rest encounters a wavefront. The electric field at its position reaches $600\,\mathrm{V/m}$ while its speed is still negligible. With $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$, what is the magnitude of the initial force?

- $6.8 \cdot 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.8 \cdot 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.6 \cdot 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.9 \cdot 10^{-16}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force on the electron is the **Lorentz force** $\vec{F} = -e\left( \vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B} \right)$.
Since the electron is at rest, $\vec{v} = \vec{0}$ and the magnetic term is zero, even though the magnetic field of the wave is nonzero.
Therefore the magnitude of the force is
$$
F = e E = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19} \cdot 600\,\mathrm{N} = 9.6 \cdot 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $1.9 \cdot 10^{-16}\,\mathrm{N}$ would be obtained by also adding $e\,c\,B$, which is the magnetic force on a charge moving with speed $c$.
