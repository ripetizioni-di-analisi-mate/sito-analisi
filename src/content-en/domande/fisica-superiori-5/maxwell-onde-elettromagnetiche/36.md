An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $9$ MHz. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its wavelength?

- $2700\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{100}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum, $\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}$. The frequency is $9\cdot10^6$ Hz. The wavelength is therefore $\lambda=\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{9\cdot10^6}=\dfrac{300}{9}$ m.
