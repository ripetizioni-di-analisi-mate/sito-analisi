A probe records $E_y=6\,\mathrm{V/m}$ and $E_z=8\,\mathrm{V/m}$ at the same instant in a single plane wave travelling along $+x$ in vacuum. What is the instantaneous magnitude of $B$ if $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $33\,\mathrm{nT}$
- $47\,\mathrm{nT}$
- $6.7\,\mathrm{nT}$
- $100\,\mathrm{nT}$

## Explanation

The electric components are perpendicular, giving $E=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10\,\mathrm{V/m}$. For a single plane wave $B=\dfrac{E}{c}\simeq3.3\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm T$.
