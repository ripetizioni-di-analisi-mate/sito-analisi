A monochromatic source illuminates two slits. The rays arrive in phase when their path difference is zero. What happens at a point with path difference $1.5\lambda$ and equal amplitudes?

- A zero-intensity minimum.
- The sum of two incoherent-ray intensities.
- A maximum four times the single-slit intensity.
- A maximum shifted only in time.

## Explanation

The path difference adds $3\pi$ in phase, equivalent to half a cycle. Equal-amplitude opposite fields cancel.
