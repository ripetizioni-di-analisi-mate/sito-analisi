A transmitter emits wave trains of duration $2\,\mu\mathrm s$ at $50\,\mathrm{MHz}$. How many oscillations does one train contain?

- $100$
- $10$
- $250$
- $25$

## Explanation

Frequency counts cycles per second. The number of cycles in the interval is $f\Delta t=50\cdot10^6\cdot2\cdot10^{-6}=100$.
