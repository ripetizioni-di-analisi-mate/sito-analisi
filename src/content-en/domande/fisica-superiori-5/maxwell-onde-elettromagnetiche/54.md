An ideal analyser is rotated in a beam. The transmitted intensity ranges from $2$ to $10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. The beam is an incoherent mixture of unpolarized and linearly polarized light. What was the intensity of the unpolarized component before the filter?

- $12\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

At the minimum the linear component is blocked. The two units transmitted equal half the incident unpolarized component, so that component has intensity four.
