Linearly polarized light of intensity $16$ watts per square meter passes through an ideal analyzer whose axis is at $60^\circ$ to the polarization direction. What is the transmitted intensity?

- $8\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

Malus's law gives $I=I_0\cos^2\theta$. Since $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$, the factor is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Thus the transmitted intensity is $4$ watts per square meter.
