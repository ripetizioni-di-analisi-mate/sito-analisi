An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $3$ MHz. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its wavelength?

- $900\,\mathrm{m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum, $\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}$. The frequency is $3\cdot10^6$ Hz. The wavelength is therefore $\lambda=\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{3\cdot10^6}=\dfrac{300}{3}$ m.
