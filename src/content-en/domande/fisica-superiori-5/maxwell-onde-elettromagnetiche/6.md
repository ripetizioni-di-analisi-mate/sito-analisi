A radio broadcast at $1.0\,\mathrm{MHz}$ propagates at $3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which pair gives its wavelength and period?

- $300\,\mathrm{m},\ 1.0\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m},\ 1.0\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m},\ 1.0\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m},\ 1.0\,\mu\mathrm s$

## Explanation

One megahertz means one million cycles per second, so each cycle lasts one microsecond. In that time the signal travels $cT=300\,\mathrm m$, one wavelength.
