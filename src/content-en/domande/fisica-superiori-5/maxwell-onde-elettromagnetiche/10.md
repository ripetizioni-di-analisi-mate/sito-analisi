A point source radiates $40\,\mathrm W$ uniformly in every direction. What root-mean-square electric field is measured at $2.5\,\mathrm m$ if absorption is negligible? Use $c\varepsilon_0=2.66\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{A/V}$.

- $0.51\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $28\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The power crosses a sphere of area $4\pi r^2$, giving an intensity of about $0.51\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. From $I=c\varepsilon_0 E_{\rm rms}^2$, take the square root to obtain the RMS field.
