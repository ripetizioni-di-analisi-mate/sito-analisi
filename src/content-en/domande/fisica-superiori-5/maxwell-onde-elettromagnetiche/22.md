Two straight fibres of length $300\,\mathrm m$ have indices $1.5$ and $1.6$. Pulses enter together. What is the delay of the second pulse if $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the quoted indices equal the group indices?

- $1.6\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $100\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $1\,\mu\mathrm s$
- $10\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

The transit time in each fibre is $\dfrac{nL}{c}$. Their difference is $\dfrac{(1.6-1.5)\,300}{c}=10^{-7}\,\mathrm s$.
