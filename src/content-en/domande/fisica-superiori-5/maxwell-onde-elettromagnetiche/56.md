An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $3$ GHz. What is its wavelength in centimeters? Use $c=3\cdot 10^8$ m/s.

- $10$
- $1$
- $90$
- $100$

## Explanation

Wavelength is speed divided by frequency. One gigahertz is one billion hertz, and one meter is one hundred centimeters. This gives $10$.
