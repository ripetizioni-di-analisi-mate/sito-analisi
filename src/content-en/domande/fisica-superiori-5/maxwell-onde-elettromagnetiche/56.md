A rotating polarizer transmits constant intensity. What can this measurement alone establish if the input may be circularly polarized or unpolarized?

- The beam must be unpolarized.
- Both states fit the measurement.
- The beam is linearly polarized.
- The beam must be circularly polarized.

## Explanation

The mean squared projection is identical along every axis for circular polarization and for unpolarized light. A single linear analyser cannot distinguish them.
