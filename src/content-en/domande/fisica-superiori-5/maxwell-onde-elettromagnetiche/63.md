A growing magnetic field creates an electric field tangent to concentric circles. A charge follows such a circle back to its starting point. Which property distinguishes this induced field from an electrostatic field?

- The work is always path independent.
- The work around the closed path can be nonzero.
- It acts only on moving charges.
- Its lines must start on positive charges.

## Explanation

Changing flux produces nonzero electric circulation. Multiplying the circulation by the charge gives the work around the closed path.
