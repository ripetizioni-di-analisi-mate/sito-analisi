At one point a wave has orthogonal electric components $E_y=E_0\cos\omega t$ and $E_z=E_0\sin\omega t$. What curve does the tip of the electric vector trace in a period?

- A parabola.
- A tilted line segment.
- A circle.
- A fixed point.

## Explanation

The components have equal amplitudes and a quarter-period phase difference. Since $E_y^2+E_z^2=E_0^2$, the magnitude is constant as the direction rotates.
