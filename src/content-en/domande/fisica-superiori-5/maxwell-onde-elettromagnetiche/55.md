An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $2$ GHz. What is its wavelength in centimeters? Use $c=3\cdot 10^8$ m/s.

- $150$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $15$
- $60$

## Explanation

Wavelength is speed divided by frequency. One gigahertz is one billion hertz, and one meter is one hundred centimeters. This gives $15$.
