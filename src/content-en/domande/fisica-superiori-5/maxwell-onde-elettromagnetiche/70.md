A point source radiates uniformly in all directions. At $2$ m the intensity is $20$ W/m². What is the intensity at $4$ m, in watts per square meter?

- $10$
- $5$
- $20$
- $80$

## Explanation

The same power spreads over the surface of a sphere. Doubling the radius quadruples the area. This gives $5$.
