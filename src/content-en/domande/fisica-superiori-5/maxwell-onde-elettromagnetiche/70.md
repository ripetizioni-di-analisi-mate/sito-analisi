Two radio carriers have frequencies $100$ and $101\,\mathrm{MHz}$. Which is passed by an ideal tuner with passband $99.8$ to $100.2\,\mathrm{MHz}$?

- Both carriers.
- Only the $100\,\mathrm{MHz}$ carrier.
- Only their mean frequency.
- Only the $101\,\mathrm{MHz}$ carrier.

## Explanation

A filter passes the frequency components in its specified interval. The first is inside; the second exceeds the upper limit.
