The magnetic flux through a stationary ring made of insulating material is changing. What exists around the ring even without free charge carriers?

- An electrostatic potential with a nonzero net change around the loop.
- An electric field with nonzero circulation.
- A source of magnetic charge.
- A necessarily nonzero conduction current.

## Explanation

Faraday’s law applies to a curve in space even without a conducting wire. Conduction current would require mobile charge carriers.
