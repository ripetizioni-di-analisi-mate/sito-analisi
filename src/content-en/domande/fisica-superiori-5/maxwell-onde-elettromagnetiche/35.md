Linearly polarized light of intensity $32$ watts per square meter passes through an ideal analyzer whose axis is at $60^\circ$ to the polarization direction. What is the transmitted intensity?

- $16\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $32\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

Malus's law gives $I=I_0\cos^2\theta$. Since $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$, the factor is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Thus the transmitted intensity is $8$ watts per square meter.
