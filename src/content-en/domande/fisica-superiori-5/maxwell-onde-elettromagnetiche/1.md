A uniform field perpendicular to a disk of radius $0.08\,\mathrm m$ increases at $0.25\,\mathrm{T/s}$. By symmetry the induced electric field is tangent to the rim. What is its magnitude in V/m?

- $0.25$
- $0.0016$
- $0.020$
- $0.010$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the circulation around the rim is $2\pi R E$. The flux changes at the rate $\pi R^2\,\dfrac{dB}{dt}$, so $E=\dfrac{R(\dfrac{dB}{dt})}{2}=0.010\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
