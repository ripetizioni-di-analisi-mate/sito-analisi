A wave described by $E=E_0\sin(kx-\omega t)$ has $k=1.57\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{rad/m}$. Which range contains its wavelength?

- $350$–$450\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $12$–$20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $50$–$80\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $2$–$4\,\mathrm{\mu m}$

## Explanation

One spatial cycle spans $2\pi$ radians. Hence $\lambda=\dfrac{2\pi}{k}\simeq4.0\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$, or $400\,\mathrm{nm}$.
