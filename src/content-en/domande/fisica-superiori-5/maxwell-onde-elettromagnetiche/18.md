A beam of intensity $5$ W/m² strikes a surface of area $2$ m² at normal incidence for $3$ s. How much energy reaches the surface, in joules?

- $10$
- $30$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}$

## Explanation

The incident power is the intensity multiplied by the area. The energy is that power multiplied by the duration of illumination. This gives $30$.
