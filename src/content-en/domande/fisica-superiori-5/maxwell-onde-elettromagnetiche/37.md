A plane wave in a vacuum has a magnetic field amplitude of $9$ nanoteslas. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its electric field amplitude?

- $2.7\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $27\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $270\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The amplitudes satisfy $E_0=cB_0$. One nanotesla equals $10^{-9}$ T. The result is therefore $E_0=3\cdot10^8\cdot9\cdot10^{-9}=2.7$ V/m.
