A plane wave travelling in vacuum has measured total mean energy density $8\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$. How much is due to the magnetic field alone?

- $2\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $16\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $8\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $4\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$

## Explanation

In a plane wave in vacuum the electric and magnetic contributions are equal. Each therefore accounts for half the total energy. The result is $4\,\mu\mathrm{J/m^3}$.
