An electromagnetic wave in a dielectric has phase $4x+8\cdot10^8t$ radians, with $x$ in metres and $t$ in seconds. At what velocity does a crest move along the $x$ axis?

- $-8\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Following one crest keeps its phase constant. Thus $4\Delta x+8\cdot10^8\Delta t=0$ gives $\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}=-2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The minus sign means motion towards decreasing $x$.
