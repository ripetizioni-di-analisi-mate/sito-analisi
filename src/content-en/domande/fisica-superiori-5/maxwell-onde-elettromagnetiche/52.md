A region of vacuum contains a uniform static magnetic field of $2$ T. What is the circulation of the electric field around a fixed closed path in the region?

- $2$ V.
- $-2$ V.
- Zero.
- $4$ V.

## Explanation

Faraday's law involves the time derivative of the magnetic flux. Since both the field and the surface are fixed, the derivative is zero.
