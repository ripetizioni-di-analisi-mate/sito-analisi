A straight receiving antenna is rotated until its terminal voltage peaks. A linearly polarized wave arrives perpendicular to the antenna. Which field direction is parallel to the antenna at the peak?

- The magnetic field.
- The propagation direction.
- The bisector of the electric and magnetic field directions.
- The electric field.

## Explanation

Free charges oscillate along the antenna in response to the electric field component along the wire. This component peaks when the field and antenna are parallel.
