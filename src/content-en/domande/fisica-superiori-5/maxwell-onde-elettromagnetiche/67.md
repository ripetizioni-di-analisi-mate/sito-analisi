A plane electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has average intensity $5$ W/m². Its electric field amplitude triples. What is its new intensity in watts per square meter?

- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $15$
- $5$
- $45$

## Explanation

The average intensity is proportional to the square of the electric field amplitude. Tripling the amplitude multiplies the intensity by nine. This gives $45$.
