A fixed flat loop encloses two equal regions. In one region $B$ increases upward at $2\,\mathrm{T/s}$, while in the other it increases downward at the same rate. What is the net emf around the outer boundary?

- Zero.
- Half the emf of one region.
- The emf induced by the left region alone.
- Twice the emf of one region.

## Explanation

With one common normal the two rates of flux change have opposite signs. Their sum vanishes, so the outer boundary has zero circulation.
