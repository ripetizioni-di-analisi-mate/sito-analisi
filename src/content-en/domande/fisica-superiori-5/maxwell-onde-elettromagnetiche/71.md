The amplitude of a radio carrier varies slowly with the sound being transmitted, while its carrier frequency stays fixed. Which measurement reproduces the audio information?

- The local speed of light.
- The carrier frequency alone.
- The time-dependent amplitude envelope.
- The average field over a carrier cycle, always close to zero.

## Explanation

In amplitude modulation the peaks follow the audio signal. Reconstructing the envelope recovers that slow variation.
