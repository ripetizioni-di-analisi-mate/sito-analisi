A point source radiates uniformly in all directions. At $3$ m the intensity is $28$ W/m². What is the intensity at $6$ m, in watts per square meter?

- $28$
- $14$
- $7$
- $112$

## Explanation

The same power spreads over the surface of a sphere. Doubling the radius quadruples the area. This gives $7$.
