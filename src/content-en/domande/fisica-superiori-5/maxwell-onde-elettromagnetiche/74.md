A beam strikes two surfaces at normal incidence. One absorbs the beam and the other reflects it perfectly. The pressure on the first is $7$ nPa. What is the pressure on the second in nanopascals?

- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $28$
- $14$
- $7$

## Explanation

Absorption transfers the incident momentum. At normal incidence, reflection reverses the momentum, doubling the momentum transfer. This gives $14$.
