A source emits rectangular $100\,\mathrm W$ pulses, each lasting $1\,\mathrm{ms}$, every $10\,\mathrm{ms}$. What is its average power over many periods?

- $1\,\mathrm W$
- $90\,\mathrm W$
- $10\,\mathrm W$
- $100\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The beam is on for one tenth of the time. Its average power is the on-pulse power multiplied by $\dfrac{1}{10}$.
