A parallel-plate capacitor in a vacuum, with plates of area $S = 0.050\,\mathrm{m^2}$, charges at a constant current $i = 0.30\,\mathrm{A}$. Neglecting edge effects, calculate the rate at which the electric field between the plates increases ($\varepsilon_0 = 8.85 \cdot 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\cdot m^2)}$).

- $6.8\cdot10^{10}\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$
- $3.4\cdot10^{10}\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$
- $6.8\cdot10^{11}\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$
- $1.7\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{V/(m\,s)}$

## Explanation

Circuit current equals the displacement current in the vacuum gap. For a uniform field and fixed area, $i=\varepsilon_0 S\,\dfrac{dE}{dt}$. Dividing the current by $\varepsilon_0 S$ gives the required rate.
