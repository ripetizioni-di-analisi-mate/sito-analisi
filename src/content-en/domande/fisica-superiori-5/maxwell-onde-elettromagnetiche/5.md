Between the plate planes of a charging circular capacitor of radius $R=3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, compare the magnitude of $B$ at distances $r_1=1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $r_2=6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis. The charging current is $2.0\,\mathrm A$ and fringe effects are negligible. Which pair, in microteslas and in the stated order, is correct? Use $\mu_0=4\pi\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}$.

- $(3.3,\ 13.3)$
- $(6.7,\ 6.7)$
- $(6.7,\ 0)$
- $(26.7,\ 6.7)$

## Explanation

The inner loop encloses a fraction $\dfrac{r_1^2}{R^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the displacement current, while the outer one encloses all of it. In $B(2\pi r)=\mu_0 i_{\rm enclosed}$ the second radius is four times the first, so the increase in enclosed current offsets the larger circumference.
