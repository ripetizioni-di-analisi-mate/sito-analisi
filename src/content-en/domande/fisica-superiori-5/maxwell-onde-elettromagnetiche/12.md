A plane wave in a vacuum has a magnetic field amplitude of $4$ nanoteslas. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its electric field amplitude?

- $12\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $120\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The amplitudes satisfy $E_0=cB_0$. One nanotesla equals $10^{-9}$ T. The result is therefore $E_0=3\cdot10^8\cdot4\cdot10^{-9}=1.2$ V/m.
