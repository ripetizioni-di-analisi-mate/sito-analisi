A beam of linearly polarized light with intensity $I_0 = 60\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ strikes a polarizing filter whose transmission axis makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the polarization direction. Calculate the transmitted light intensity.

- $15\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $45\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The transmitted field is the projection of the incident field onto the filter axis. Intensity follows its square: $I=60\cos^2(60^\circ)=60(\dfrac{1}{2})^2\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
