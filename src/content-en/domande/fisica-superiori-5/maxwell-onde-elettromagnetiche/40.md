A sensor maps magnetic flux through the faces of a cube. The bottom admits $5\,\mathrm{mWb}$, and $2\,\mathrm{mWb}$ leaves through the four side faces together. What reading is missing on the top face?

- $7.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ outward
- $3.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ outward
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ outward
- $3.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ inward

## Explanation

By **Gauss's law for magnetism** the outward magnetic flux through every closed surface is zero.
With the outward normal, the flux through the bottom face is $-5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ and the flux through the side faces is $+2.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
Therefore the flux through the top face is $\Phi = 5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb} - 2.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb} = 3.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$, **outward**.
