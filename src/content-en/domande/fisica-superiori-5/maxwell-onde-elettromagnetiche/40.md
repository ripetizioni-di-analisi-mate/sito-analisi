An antenna radiates the same power in all directions. At what multiple of the initial distance does the intensity fall to one ninth of its initial value?

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The intensity is inversely proportional to the square of the distance. To reduce it to one ninth, multiply the distance by the square root of nine. This gives $3$.
