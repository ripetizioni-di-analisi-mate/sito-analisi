An electromagnetic pulse of energy $0.9\,\mathrm J$ travels left in vacuum. What is its momentum component on an axis pointing right? Use $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $0\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $-2.7\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $-3\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $+3\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

The momentum magnitude is $\dfrac{U}{c}$. Propagation is along the negative axis, giving $p_x=-\dfrac{0.9}{3\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$.
