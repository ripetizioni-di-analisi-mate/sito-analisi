A parallel-plate capacitor has capacitance $9$ nF, and the voltage across it increases at $10^6$ V/s. Neglecting edge effects, what is the displacement current in milliamperes?

- $9000\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $9\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $0.009\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $18\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The displacement current is $I_s=C\dfrac{dV}{dt}$. Use $C=9\cdot10^{-9}$ farads. The result is $I_s=9\cdot10^{-3}$ A, or $9$ mA.
