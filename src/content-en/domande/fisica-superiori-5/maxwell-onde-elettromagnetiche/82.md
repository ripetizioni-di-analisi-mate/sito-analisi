A plane wavefront in vacuum reaches two synchronized receivers on the line of propagation. The arrival times differ by $0.20\,\mu\mathrm s$. How far apart are the receivers if $c=300\,\mathrm{m/\mu s}$?

- $1500\,\mathrm m$
- $60\,\mathrm m$
- $600\,\mathrm m$
- $30\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The delay is the arrival-time difference for the same front. The distance between the receivers is traversed once, giving $c\Delta t=60\,\mathrm m$.
