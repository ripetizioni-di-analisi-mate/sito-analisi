A $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$ panel receives $800\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ at normal incidence and converts $20\%$ of the incident power to electricity. What electrical power does it produce?

- $320\,\mathrm W$
- $1600\,\mathrm W$
- $640\,\mathrm W$
- $160\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The incident power is $IA=1600\,\mathrm W$. Multiplying by the efficiency $0.20$ gives $320\,\mathrm W$.
