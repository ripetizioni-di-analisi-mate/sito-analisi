A capacitor discharges through a weakly conducting dielectric. With cylindrical symmetry the magnetic field is zero even though a conduction current flows through the dielectric. What explains this?

- Displacement current is twice the conduction current in the same direction.
- Vacuum permeability is zero.
- The electric field remains constant.
- Displacement current is equal and opposite to conduction current.

## Explanation

During discharge the electric field decreases. The displacement term opposes the leakage current. When their magnitudes match, the net current in the Ampère–Maxwell law vanishes.
