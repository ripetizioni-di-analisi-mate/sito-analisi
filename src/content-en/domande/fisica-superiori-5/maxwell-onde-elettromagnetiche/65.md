The instantaneous electric field in a plane wave points along $+x$ and the magnetic field along $-y$. The electric field magnitude is $7$ V/m. In which direction does the wave propagate?

- Toward $-z$.
- Toward $+z$.
- Toward $-y$.
- Toward $+x$.

## Explanation

The Poynting vector points in the direction of the cross product of the electric field and the magnetic field. The cross product of $+x$ with $-y$ points along $-z$.
