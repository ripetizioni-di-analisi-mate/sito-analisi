On an oscilloscope a wave completes three cycles in $6\,\mathrm{ns}$. What is its angular frequency?

- $0.5\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\pi\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\pi\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6\pi\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

One cycle takes $2\,\mathrm{ns}$. The phase advances by $2\pi$ in that time, hence $\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{T}=\pi\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
