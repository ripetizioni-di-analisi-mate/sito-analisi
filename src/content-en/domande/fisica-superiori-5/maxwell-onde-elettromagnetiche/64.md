A flat disk rotates in a uniform constant electric field. When the disk's plane becomes parallel to the field lines, what is the electric flux?

- $-EA$
- $\dfrac{EA}{2}$
- Zero.
- $EA$

## Explanation

Flux uses the normal field component, $\Phi_E=EA\cos\theta$. Here the normal is perpendicular to the field, so the cosine vanishes.
