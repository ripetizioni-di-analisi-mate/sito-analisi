A radio source sends a short pulse towards a wall. The echo returns after $8\,\mu\mathrm s$. How far away is the wall if $c=300\,\mathrm{m/\mu s}$?

- $4800\,\mathrm m$
- $600\,\mathrm m$
- $2400\,\mathrm m$
- $1200\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The measured time includes the outward and return journeys. The distance is $\dfrac{c\Delta t}{2}=\dfrac{300\cdot8}{2}\,\mathrm m$.
