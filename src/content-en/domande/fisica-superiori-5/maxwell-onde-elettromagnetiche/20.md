Linearly polarized light of intensity $20$ watts per square meter passes through an ideal analyzer whose axis is at $60^\circ$ to the polarization direction. What is the transmitted intensity?

- $10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

Malus's law gives $I=I_0\cos^2\theta$. Since $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$, the factor is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Thus the transmitted intensity is $5$ watts per square meter.
