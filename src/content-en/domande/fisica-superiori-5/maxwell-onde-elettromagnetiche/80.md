A plane wave carries energy along $+x$. A small disk is held with its plane parallel to the $x$ axis, so its normal is perpendicular to the beam. What energy flux crosses that ideal disk?

- $\dfrac{IA}{2}$
- $2IA$
- $IA$
- Zero.

## Explanation

Only the component of the Poynting vector along the surface normal contributes. It vanishes here even though the wave carries energy through the surrounding space.
