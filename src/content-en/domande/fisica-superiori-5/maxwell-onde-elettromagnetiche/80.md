An unpolarized beam of intensity $28$ W/m² passes through two ideal polarizers whose axes make an angle of $45^\circ$. What intensity emerges, in watts per square meter?

- $7$
- $14$
- $28$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first polarizer halves the intensity of the unpolarized beam. The second multiplies this intensity by the square of the cosine of forty-five degrees, which is one half. This gives $7$.
