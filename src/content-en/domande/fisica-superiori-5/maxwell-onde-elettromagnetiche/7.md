A sinusoidal electromagnetic wave travels in vacuum, and the amplitude of its magnetic field is $B_0=4\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm T$. With $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the rms value of the electric field of the wave?

- $8.5\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $17\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

In the wave the amplitudes of the two fields are related by $E_0=cB_0$, so $E_0=3\cdot10^8\cdot4\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{V/m}=12\,\mathrm{V/m}$. The rms value of a sinusoidal quantity is the amplitude divided by $\sqrt{2}$. Therefore $E_{\text{rms}}=\dfrac{12}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{V/m}\approx8.5\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
