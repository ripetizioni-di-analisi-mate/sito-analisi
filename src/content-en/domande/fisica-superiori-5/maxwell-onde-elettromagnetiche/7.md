A meter records the peak magnetic field $B_0=4\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm T$ of a sinusoidal wave in vacuum. What rms electric field will a probe calibrated in V/m indicate? Use $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $8.5$
- $6$
- $17$
- $12$

## Explanation

The peak electric field is $cB_0=12\,\mathrm{V/m}$. The rms value of a sine wave is its peak divided by $\sqrt{2}$, giving about $8.5\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
