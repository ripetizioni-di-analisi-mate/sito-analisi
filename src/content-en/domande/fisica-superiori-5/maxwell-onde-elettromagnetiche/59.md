A charging current abruptly reverses without changing magnitude. Which quantity immediately changes sign between ideal plates in the quasistatic model?

- The plate separation.
- The charge already stored.
- The time derivative of the electric field.
- The capacitance.

## Explanation

Stored charge is continuous, but $\dfrac{dQ}{dt}$ reverses sign. Since $E=\dfrac{Q}{\varepsilon_0 A}$, $\dfrac{dE}{dt}$ reverses as well.
