An electromagnetic wave of wavelength $7$ m enters a transparent medium in which its speed is one third of its initial speed. What is the new wavelength, in meters?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$
- $21$
- $7$

## Explanation

The frequency stays the same at the interface. Wavelength is the ratio of speed to frequency, so it decreases in the same proportion as the speed. This gives $\dfrac{7}{3}$.
