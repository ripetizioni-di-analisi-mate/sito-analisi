The electric field of a linearly polarized beam oscillates vertically. Which orientation of an ideal polarizer's axis blocks the beam?

- Horizontal and perpendicular to the polarization.
- At thirty degrees to the vertical.
- Vertical and parallel to the polarization.
- At forty-five degrees to the vertical.

## Explanation

A polarizer transmits the electric field component along its axis. When the axis is horizontal, the projection of the vertical field onto it is zero.
