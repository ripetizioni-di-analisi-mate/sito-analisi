Two adjacent electric nodes in a standing wave are $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. Where is the first electric antinode measured from the first node towards the second?

- At $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- At the initial node.
- At $5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- At $10\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Adjacent nodes enclose half a wavelength. The antinode is halfway between them, a quarter wavelength from the first.
