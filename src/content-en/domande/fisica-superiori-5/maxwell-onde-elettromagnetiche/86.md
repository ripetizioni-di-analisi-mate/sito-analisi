A beam of intensity $7$ W/m² strikes a surface of area $3$ m² at normal incidence for $4$ s. How much energy reaches the surface, in joules?

- $21$
- $28$
- $84$
- $\dfrac{28}{3}$

## Explanation

The incident power is the intensity multiplied by the area. The energy is that power multiplied by the duration of illumination. This gives $84$.
