An electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has frequency $6$ MHz. With $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s, what is its wavelength?

- $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum, $\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}$. The frequency is $6\cdot10^6$ Hz. The wavelength is therefore $\lambda=\dfrac{3\cdot10^8}{6\cdot10^6}=\dfrac{300}{6}$ m.
