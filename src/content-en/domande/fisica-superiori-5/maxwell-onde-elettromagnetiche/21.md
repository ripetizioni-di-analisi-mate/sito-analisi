Two light pulses leave points $30\,\mathrm m$ apart simultaneously in vacuum and travel towards each other. When do they meet if $c=0.30\,\mathrm{m/ns}$?

- $100\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $25\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $200\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $50\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

In the source frame the separation closes at $2c$. Each pulse covers $15\,\mathrm m$, taking $\dfrac{15}{0.30}=50\,\mathrm{ns}$.
