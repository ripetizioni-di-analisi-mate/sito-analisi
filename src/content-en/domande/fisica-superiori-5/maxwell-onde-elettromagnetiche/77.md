A closed radiometer absorbs two equally powerful beams from opposite directions at normal incidence. Which combination describes the net effect on the radiometer?

- The force doubles and heating cancels.
- Both heating and force cancel.
- It heats up and the net force is zero.
- It heats and accelerates towards one source even with perfect symmetry.

## Explanation

The absorbed energies are scalars and add. The transferred momenta are equal and opposite vectors, so they cancel.
