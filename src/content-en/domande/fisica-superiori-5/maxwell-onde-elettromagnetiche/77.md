A linearly polarized beam of intensity $28$ W/m² passes through an ideal polarizer whose axis is at $60^\circ$ to the initial polarization. What is the transmitted intensity in watts per square meter?

- $21$
- $14$
- $0$
- $7$

## Explanation

By Malus's law, multiply the incident intensity by the square of the cosine of the angle. The cosine of sixty degrees is one half. This gives $7$.
