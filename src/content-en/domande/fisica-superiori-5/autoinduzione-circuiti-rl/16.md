A linear coil has a flux linkage of $5$ Wb when it carries $2$ A. What is its inductance?

- $10\,\mathrm{H}$
- $5\,\mathrm{H}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{H}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

The flux linkage of a linear inductor is $\Phi_c=LI$. Solving gives $L=\dfrac{\Phi_c}{I}$. Hence $L=\dfrac{5}{2}=2.5$ H.
