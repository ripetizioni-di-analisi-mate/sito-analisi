A circuit has an inductance of $21$ mH and a total resistance of $3$ Ω. What is its time constant in milliseconds?

- $7$
- $21$
- $63$
- $\dfrac{7}{1000}$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is the ratio of inductance to resistance. The prefix milli- in the inductance carries through to the result, giving $7$.
