A transient is considered settled when the initial gap falls below 1%. What is the first whole number of time constants that suffices? Use $e^{-4}\simeq0.018$ and $e^{-5}\simeq0.0067$.

- $4$
- $2$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The relative gap is $e^{-t/\tau}$. After four time constants it is still 1.8%, whereas after five it has fallen to 0.67%.
