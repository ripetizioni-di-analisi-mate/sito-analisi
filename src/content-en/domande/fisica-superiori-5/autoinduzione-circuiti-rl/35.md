A moving core increases inductance from 3 to 5 mH in 10 ms while a source maintains a constant 2 A. What is the mean emf magnitude due to motion?

- $0.2\,\mathrm V$
- $4\,\mathrm V$
- $0.4\,\mathrm V$
- $0\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Flux linkage is $LI$. At constant current it changes by $2\cdot0.002=0.004\,\mathrm{Wb}$, giving mean emf magnitude $0.004/0.010=0.4\,\mathrm V$.
