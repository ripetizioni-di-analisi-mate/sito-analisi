A 0.5 H inductor changes from $+2$ A to $-2$ A. What is its net magnetic-energy change between the endpoints, despite energy exchange during the transition?

- $-2\,\mathrm J$
- $4\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $+2\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Energy depends on $I^2$, so it has the same value at both endpoints. During reversal it falls to zero and then rises again.
