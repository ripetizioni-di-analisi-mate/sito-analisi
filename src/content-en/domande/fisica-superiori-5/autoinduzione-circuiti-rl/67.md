The steady-state current in an RL circuit is $5$ A. Starting from zero current, what is the current, in amperes, one time constant after the switch closes?

- $5 - \dfrac{5}{e^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{5}{e}$
- $5$
- $5 - \dfrac{5}{e}$

## Explanation

As the current rises, the fraction of its steady-state value that it reaches is $1-e^{-t/\tau}$. After one time constant the exponent is minus one, giving $5 - \dfrac{5}{e}$.
