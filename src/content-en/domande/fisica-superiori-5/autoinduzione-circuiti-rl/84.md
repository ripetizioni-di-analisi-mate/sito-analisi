A core with hysteresis completes a cycle in the $(H,B)$ plane. A measurement gives $\oint H\,dB=200\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$ per cycle. For a core of volume $5.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ operating at $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$, how much power is dissipated by hysteresis?

- $0.50\,\mathrm W$
- $0.010\,\mathrm W$
- $2.0\,\mathrm W$
- $10\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The loop area gives the energy dissipated per unit volume in one cycle. Multiplying by the volume gives $0.010\,\mathrm J$ per cycle. Fifty cycles per second therefore give $0.50\,\mathrm W$.
