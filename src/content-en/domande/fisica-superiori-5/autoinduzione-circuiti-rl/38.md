An inductor with $L=9$ H carries $2$ A. How much magnetic energy does it store?

- $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The stored energy is $U=\dfrac{1}{2}LI^2$. The square of the current is $4$. Hence $U=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot9\cdot4=18$ J.
