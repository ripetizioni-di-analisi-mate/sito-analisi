A long solenoid is doubled in length and given extra turns to keep turns per metre constant. With unchanged area and material, how does its inductance change?

- It quadruples
- It halves
- It doubles
- It is unchanged

## Explanation

Substituting $N=n\ell$ gives $L=\mu n^2A\ell$. At fixed turn density, inductance grows with length.
