An ideal RL discharge gives readings of 8 A and 4 A separated by 10 ms. What is expected another 20 ms after the second reading?

- $0\,\mathrm A$
- $0.5\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$
- $1\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The same reduction factor repeats over equal time intervals. Another 20 ms contains two halvings, $4\to2\to1\,\mathrm A$.
