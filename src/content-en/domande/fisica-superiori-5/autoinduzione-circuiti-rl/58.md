A circuit has an inductance of $10$ mH and a total resistance of $2$ Ω. What is its time constant in milliseconds?

- $20$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{1}{200}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is the ratio of inductance to resistance. The prefix milli- in the inductance carries through to the result, giving $5$.
