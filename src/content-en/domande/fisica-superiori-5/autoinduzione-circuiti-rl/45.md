An inductor receives $+2\,\mathrm V$ for $3\,\mathrm{ms}$ followed by $-3\,\mathrm V$ for $2\,\mathrm{ms}$. How are final and initial currents related?

- They are equal
- The final current is always zero
- The final current is double
- Their signs must be opposite

## Explanation

For an ideal constant inductor, current change is proportional to signed voltage area. Here $2\cdot3-3\cdot2=0$.
