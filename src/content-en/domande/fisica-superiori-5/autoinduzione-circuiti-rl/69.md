After a coil’s source is switched off, the initial current is $4\,\mathrm A$. A discharge path of resistance $R$ is replaced by one of resistance $2R$, with the same $L$ and initial current. Which pair describes the initial heating power and the characteristic discharge time?

- Half the power and twice the time
- Both double
- Twice the power and half the time
- Both stay the same

## Explanation

At the same initial current, $P(0)=Ri_0^2$ doubles. The time constant $\tau=\dfrac{L}{R}$ halves. The initial energy in the inductor is therefore dissipated more rapidly.
