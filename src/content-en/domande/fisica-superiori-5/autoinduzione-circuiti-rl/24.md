An RL circuit has $L=6$ H and $R=2$ ohms. What is its time constant?

- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is $\tau=\dfrac{L}{R}$. Divide the inductance by the total resistance. Hence $\tau=\dfrac{6}{2}=3$ s.
