An inductor with $L=5$ H carries $2$ A. How much magnetic energy does it store?

- $5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The stored energy is $U=\dfrac{1}{2}LI^2$. The square of the current is $4$. Hence $U=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot4=10$ J.
