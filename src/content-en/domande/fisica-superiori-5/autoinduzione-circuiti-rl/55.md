An ideal $5$ mH solenoid is replaced by one of the same length, cross-sectional area, and material, with three times as many turns. What is the new inductance in millihenries?

- $45$
- $15$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$
- $5$

## Explanation

The inductance of an ideal solenoid is proportional to the square of the number of turns. Tripling the number of turns increases the inductance by a factor of nine, giving $45$.
