An ideal $7$ mH solenoid is replaced by one of the same length, cross-sectional area, and material, with three times as many turns. What is the new inductance in millihenries?

- $63$
- $7$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$

## Explanation

The inductance of an ideal solenoid is proportional to the square of the number of turns. Tripling the number of turns increases the inductance by a factor of nine, giving $63$.
