The initial tangent to an RL current-rise graph meets its final-value horizontal line at 8 ms. What does this interval measure?

- The exact time to reach steady state
- The time constant
- The duration of half a discharge
- The oscillation period

## Explanation

Initial slope is $I_\infty/\tau$. Its tangent reaches height $I_\infty$ at time $\tau$, while the actual curve remains below the final level.
