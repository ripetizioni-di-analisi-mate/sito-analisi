Two distant ideal inductors of 2 mH and 5 mH are in series. When their common current grows at $1000\,\mathrm{A/s}$, what total voltage is required?

- $1.4\,\mathrm V$
- $7\,\mathrm V$
- $10\,\mathrm V$
- $3\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Individual voltages add in the absence of mutual coupling. The total is $(0.002+0.005)1000=7\,\mathrm V$.
