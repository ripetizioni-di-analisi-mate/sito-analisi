An inductor with $L=6$ H carries $2$ A. How much magnetic energy does it store?

- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The stored energy is $U=\dfrac{1}{2}LI^2$. The square of the current is $4$. Hence $U=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot4=12$ J.
