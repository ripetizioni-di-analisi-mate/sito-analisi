A series RL circuit is connected to a $10$ V DC source and has a total resistance of $2$ Ω. What is the steady-state current in amperes?

- $10$
- $20$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

In steady state, the current is constant. The voltage across the ideal inductor is zero, so the entire source voltage appears across the resistance. This gives $5$.
