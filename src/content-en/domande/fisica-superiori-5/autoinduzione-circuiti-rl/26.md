Two coupled coils are connected in series. When their windings are connected so that the fluxes they produce add, the total inductance measured is $0.50\,\mathrm{H}$; reversing the connection of one of the two coils, so that the fluxes subtract, one measures $0.30\,\mathrm{H}$. What is the mutual inductance of the two coils?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{H}$
- $0.050\,\mathrm{H}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{H}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

The coils in series carry the same current $i$. The flux linked with the first is $L_1 i \pm M i$ and that linked with the second is $L_2 i \pm M i$, with the positive sign if the fluxes add. The total flux is therefore $(L_1 + L_2 \pm 2M)\,i$, so the total inductance is $L_1 + L_2 + 2M$ in one connection and $L_1 + L_2 - 2M$ in the other. The difference between the two measurements is $4M$, hence
$$
M = \frac{0.50 - 0.30}{4}\,\mathrm{H} = 0.050\,\mathrm{H} .
$$
The value $0.10\,\mathrm{H}$ is obtained by forgetting that mutual induction acts on both coils.
