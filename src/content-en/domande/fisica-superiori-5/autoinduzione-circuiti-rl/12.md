Between the pole pieces of an electromagnet, a uniform magnetic field of $0.20\,\mathrm{T}$ occupies a volume of $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ in air ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$). How much magnetic energy is contained in the stated volume?

- $0.080\,\mathrm J$
- $1.60\,\mathrm J$
- $0.80\,\mathrm J$
- $8.0\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The field is uniform, so multiply energy density by the volume converted to cubic metres. $u_B = \dfrac{B^2}{2\mu_0} = 1.6 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$, $U = u_B V = 0.80\,\mathrm{J}$.
