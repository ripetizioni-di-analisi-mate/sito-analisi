The flux linkage of a coil with a core increases from $20$ to $30\,\mathrm{mWb}$ between $1$ and $2\,\mathrm A$, then from $30$ to $34\,\mathrm{mWb}$ between $2$ and $3\,\mathrm A$. What do the equal current intervals reveal?

- Incremental inductance decreases, as in a core approaching saturation
- Incremental inductance stays constant because the current steps are equal
- Flux reverses in the second interval
- Incremental inductance becomes negative in the second interval

## Explanation

Equal $1\,\mathrm A$ increases produce first $10$ and then only $4\,\mathrm{mWb}$ of additional flux linkage. The average slopes are therefore $10$ and $4\,\mathrm{mH}$, both positive but decreasing.
