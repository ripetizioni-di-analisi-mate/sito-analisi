An RL circuit has $L=8$ H and $R=2$ ohms. What is its time constant?

- $16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is $\tau=\dfrac{L}{R}$. Divide the inductance by the total resistance. Hence $\tau=\dfrac{8}{2}=4$ s.
