An initially energized ideal inductor has 5 J available. Changing only the resistor makes discharge faster. With the same initial current, how much total heat now reaches the resistor?

- $5\,\mathrm J$
- Necessarily less than $5\,\mathrm J$
- Zero
- Necessarily more than $5\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Resistance sets the rate of transfer. Ultimately all initial energy $LI_0^2/2$ is dissipated, regardless of discharge duration.
