An inductor of inductance $0.80\,\mathrm{H}$ must store an energy $0.90\,\mathrm{J}$. Calculate the required current.

- $2.25\,\mathrm A$
- $1.5\,\mathrm A$
- $0.75\,\mathrm A$
- $1.125\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

Magnetic energy in a linear inductor varies with the square of current. Solving for the required current magnitude therefore takes a square root. $I = \sqrt{\dfrac{2U}{L}} = 1.5\,\mathrm{A}$.
