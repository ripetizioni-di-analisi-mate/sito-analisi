An ideal $0.30\,\mathrm H$ inductor initially carries $2.0\,\mathrm A$ and discharges through $15\,\Omega$. When has half the field energy been lost? Use $\ln2\simeq0.69$.

- $3.5\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $20\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $13.8\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $6.9\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

Current decays as $e^{-t/\tau}$ with $\tau=L/R=20\,\mathrm{ms}$. Energy varies as current squared, so $e^{-2t/\tau}=1/2$.
