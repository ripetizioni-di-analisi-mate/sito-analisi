A coil with $250$ turns carries a current $I = 0.80\,\mathrm{A}$, and the magnetic flux through each turn is $2.4 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{Wb}$. Calculate the inductance of the coil.

- $7.5\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $0.030\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $75\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{mH}$

## Explanation

The given flux is for one turn. First add the fluxes of all 250 turns, then divide by the current in the linear coil. $L = \dfrac{N\,\Phi_1}{I} = 7.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{H} = 7.5\,\mathrm{mH}$.
