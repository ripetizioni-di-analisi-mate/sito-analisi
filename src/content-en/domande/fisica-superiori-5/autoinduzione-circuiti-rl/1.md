A linear coil has a flux linkage of $2$ Wb when it carries $2$ A. What is its inductance?

- $1\,\mathrm{H}$
- $4\,\mathrm{H}$
- $2\,\mathrm{H}$
- $0\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

The flux linkage of a linear inductor is $\Phi_c=LI$. Solving gives $L=\dfrac{\Phi_c}{I}$. Hence $L=\dfrac{2}{2}=1$ H.
