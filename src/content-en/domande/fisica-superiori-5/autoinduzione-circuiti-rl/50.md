An RL circuit initially carries $1\,\mathrm A$. After switching, its new steady value is $3\,\mathrm A$. What is the current at $t=\tau\ln2$?

- $3\,\mathrm A$
- $2.5\,\mathrm A$
- $1.5\,\mathrm A$
- $2\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The initial gap to the new steady current is $2\,\mathrm A$ and halves in the stated time. Thus current is $3-1=2\,\mathrm A$.
