An inductor of $3$ H has a self-induced emf of magnitude $21$ V. What is the magnitude of the rate of change of current, in amperes per second?

- $7$
- $63$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $21$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the emf is $L|di/dt|$. Dividing the measured value by the inductance gives $7$.
