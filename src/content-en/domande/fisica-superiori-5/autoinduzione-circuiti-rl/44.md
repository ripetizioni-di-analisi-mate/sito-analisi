An RL circuit is switched on with zero initial current. With the voltage held fixed, which change halves the initial rate of rise of the current while keeping its steady-state value unchanged?

- Halve the inductance.
- Halve the resistance.
- Double the resistance.
- Double the inductance.

## Explanation

The initial rate of rise depends on the ratio of voltage to inductance. The steady-state current depends on the ratio of voltage to resistance.
