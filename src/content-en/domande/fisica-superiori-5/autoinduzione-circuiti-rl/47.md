The current in an ideal $3$ H inductor rises at a constant rate from $7$ A to $11$ A in one second. What is the self-induced emf in volts, taking the direction of the current as positive?

- $12$
- $-4$
- $-12$
- $-21$

## Explanation

Since the interval is one second, the rate of change of current is numerically equal to the increase in current. The self-induced emf is this rate times the inductance, with the opposite sign, giving $-12$.
