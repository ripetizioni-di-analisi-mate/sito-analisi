An RL circuit is supplied at 10 V with $R=4\,\Omega$ and $L=2\,\mathrm H$. At the instant $i=2\,\mathrm A$, what is $di/dt$?

- $4\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{A/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{A/s}$

## Explanation

The resistor drops $Ri=8\,\mathrm V$. The remaining $2\,\mathrm V$ across the inductor give slope $2/2=1\,\mathrm{A/s}$.
