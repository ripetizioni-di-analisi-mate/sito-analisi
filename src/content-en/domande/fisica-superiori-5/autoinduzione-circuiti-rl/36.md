A linear coil has a flux linkage of $9$ Wb when it carries $2$ A. What is its inductance?

- $18\,\mathrm{H}$
- $9\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}\,\mathrm{H}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

The flux linkage of a linear inductor is $\Phi_c=LI$. Solving gives $L=\dfrac{\Phi_c}{I}$. Hence $L=\dfrac{9}{2}=4.5$ H.
