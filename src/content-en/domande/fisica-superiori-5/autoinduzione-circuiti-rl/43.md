A source maintains 3 V in the passive sign convention across a 0.6 H inductor for 0.2 s. Initial current is $-0.4\,\mathrm A$. What is the final current?

- $+1.4\,\mathrm A$
- $-1.4\,\mathrm A$
- $+1\,\mathrm A$
- $+0.6\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The change is $V\Delta t/L=3\cdot0.2/0.6=1\,\mathrm A$. Adding the initial current gives $+0.6\,\mathrm A$.
