An inductor with $L=10$ H carries $2$ A. How much magnetic energy does it store?

- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The stored energy is $U=\dfrac{1}{2}LI^2$. The square of the current is $4$. Hence $U=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot10\cdot4=20$ J.
