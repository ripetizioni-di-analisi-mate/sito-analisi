A linear coil has a flux linkage of $3$ Wb when it carries $2$ A. What is its inductance?

- $6\,\mathrm{H}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{H}$
- $3\,\mathrm{H}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{H}$

## Explanation

The flux linkage of a linear inductor is $\Phi_c=LI$. Solving gives $L=\dfrac{\Phi_c}{I}$. Hence $L=\dfrac{3}{2}=1.5$ H.
