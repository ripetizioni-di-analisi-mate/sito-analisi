In an RL circuit with $L = 0.25\,\mathrm{H}$ and $R = 5.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, the initial current is zero and a DC source is connected at time $t = 0$. Determine how long it takes for the current to reach two-thirds of its steady-state value.

- $55\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $33\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $17\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $100\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

When the current reaches two thirds of its final value, one third remains. The exponential term in the rise law must therefore equal one third. $t = \tau \ln 3 = 0.050 \cdot \ln 3\,\mathrm{s} = 0.055\,\mathrm{s}$.
