An ideal $9$ mH solenoid is replaced by one of the same length, cross-sectional area, and material, with three times as many turns. What is the new inductance in millihenries?

- $81$
- $1$
- $9$
- $27$

## Explanation

The inductance of an ideal solenoid is proportional to the square of the number of turns. Tripling the number of turns increases the inductance by a factor of nine, giving $81$.
