During coil discharge the plot of $\ln(i/i_0)$ versus time has slope $-25\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. What is the time constant?

- $4\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $250\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $40\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $25\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

Exponential decay gives $\ln(i/i_0)=-t/\tau$. The reciprocal slope magnitude is $1/25=0.040\,\mathrm s$.
