A 1 H inductor current rises from 0 to 1 A in 2 s, stays at 1 A for 1 s, then falls to zero in 2 s. During which entire interval is the exchanged power exactly zero?

- During the entire fall
- During the entire rise
- During all three intervals
- During the constant-current interval

## Explanation

On the flat section $di/dt=0$, hence voltage vanishes. Current is nonzero but its product with voltage is zero.
