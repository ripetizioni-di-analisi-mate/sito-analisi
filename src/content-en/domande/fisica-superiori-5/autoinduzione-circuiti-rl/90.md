An ideal $4$ H inductor carries a constant current of $9$ A. Which pair describes the voltage across the inductor and its stored magnetic energy?

- Positive voltage and positive energy.
- Zero voltage and zero energy.
- Zero voltage and positive energy.
- Positive voltage and zero energy.

## Explanation

The voltage is proportional to the derivative of the current, which is zero. The energy depends on the square of the current, which is positive.
