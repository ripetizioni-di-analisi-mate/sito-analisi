The energy density in the magnetic field of a linear solenoid is $7$ J/m³. The field triples while the permeability remains constant. What is the new energy density in joules per cubic meter?

- $63$
- $189$
- $21$
- $7$

## Explanation

At constant permeability, the energy density is proportional to the square of the field. The overall factor is nine, giving $63$.
