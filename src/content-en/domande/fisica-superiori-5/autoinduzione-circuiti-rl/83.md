Two coils have self-inductances of 4 mH and 9 mH. A report gives mutual inductance of 8 mH. What identifies the inconsistency for a passive linear system?

- The maximum is 13 mH and 8 exceeds it
- The self-inductances must be equal
- Mutual inductance must always vanish
- The largest possible magnitude of $M$ is 6 mH

## Explanation

The coupling coefficient cannot exceed one, so $\lvert M\rvert\le\sqrt{L_1L_2}$. Here $\sqrt{4\cdot9}=6\,\mathrm{mH}$.
