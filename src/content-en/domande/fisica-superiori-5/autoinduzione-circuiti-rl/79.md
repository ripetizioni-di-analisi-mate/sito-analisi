Two coils have perpendicular axes. The small secondary is in a uniform primary field parallel to its plane. What secondary emf results if primary current changes without changing field direction?

- Zero in the ideal model
- The maximum possible
- Twice the value for parallel axes
- An emf set solely by resistance

## Explanation

The primary field has zero normal component. Even when its magnitude changes, secondary flux linkage remains zero.
