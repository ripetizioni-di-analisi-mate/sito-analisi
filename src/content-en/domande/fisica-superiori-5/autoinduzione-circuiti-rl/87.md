A coil and resistor are supplied by a battery with known internal resistance. A student estimates $L$ from the measured time constant using only the external resistor. How is the estimate affected if all omitted resistances are positive?

- It must be negative
- It is too large
- It is too small
- It is always exact

## Explanation

The correct relation is $L=\tau R_{\rm total}$. A smaller resistance with the same measured $\tau$ underestimates inductance.
