Two coils have a mutual inductance of $4$ mH. The current in the first changes at $9000$ A/s. What is the magnitude of the emf induced in the second coil, in volts?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{9}{250}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is the mutual inductance times the rate of change of current. The factor from the prefix milli- cancels the factor of one thousand in the rate, giving $36$.
