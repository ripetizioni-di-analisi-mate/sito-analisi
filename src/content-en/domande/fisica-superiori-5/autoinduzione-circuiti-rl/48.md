The current in an ideal $4$ H inductor rises at a constant rate from $9$ A to $14$ A in one second. What is the self-induced emf in volts, taking the direction of the current as positive?

- $20$
- $-20$
- $-36$
- $-5$

## Explanation

Since the interval is one second, the rate of change of current is numerically equal to the increase in current. The self-induced emf is this rate times the inductance, with the opposite sign, giving $-20$.
