Current is still positive but decreasing. With voltage and current defined by the passive sign convention, what is the sign of ideal inductor power?

- It does not depend on the current change
- Always zero
- Negative
- Positive

## Explanation

Here $v=L\,di/dt<0$. With $i>0$, power $vi$ is negative and the inductor releases energy.
