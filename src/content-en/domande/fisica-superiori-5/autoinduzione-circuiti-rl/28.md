An inductor with $L=7$ H carries $2$ A. How much magnetic energy does it store?

- $7\,\mathrm{J}$
- $28\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $14\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The stored energy is $U=\dfrac{1}{2}LI^2$. The square of the current is $4$. Hence $U=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot7\cdot4=14$ J.
