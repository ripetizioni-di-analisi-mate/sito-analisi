An RL circuit at steady state carries $+I_0$. The battery polarity is reversed without changing resistance or voltage magnitude. When does current cross zero?

- $t=2\tau$
- $t=\tau\ln2$
- It never crosses zero
- $t=0$

## Explanation

The new limit is $-I_0$ and the initial gap from it is $2I_0$. Reaching zero requires halving the gap, which takes $\tau\ln2$.
