An inductor of $4$ H has a self-induced emf of magnitude $36$ V. What is the magnitude of the rate of change of current, in amperes per second?

- $9$
- $36$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $144$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the emf is $L|di/dt|$. Dividing the measured value by the inductance gives $9$.
