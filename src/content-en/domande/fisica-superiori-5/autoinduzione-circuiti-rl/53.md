A linear inductor has an inductance of $L=3$ H. What is its magnetic flux linkage at $7$ A, in webers?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $10$
- $21$

## Explanation

For a linear inductor, the flux linkage is proportional to the current. The proportionality constant is $L$, giving $21$.
