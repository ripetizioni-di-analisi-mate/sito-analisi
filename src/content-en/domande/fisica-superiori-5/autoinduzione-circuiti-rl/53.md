A coil and a resistive lamp are placed in series across a battery, initially with zero current. How does brightness evolve if thermal lag is negligible?

- It rises from zero with the power $Ri^2$
- It must alternate between dark and bright
- It remains zero at steady state
- It is greatest initially

## Explanation

The inductor slows current growth. Lamp power is proportional to current squared, so brightness increases gradually.
