One conductor is wound on a former so that its second half is wound opposite to its first half. Why may the self-inductance be very small despite the many turns?

- Current cannot pass through the turns
- Each half must have negative inductance
- Electrical resistance must be zero
- The fields of the halves largely cancel

## Explanation

The series current produces opposing fluxes in the two halves. The net linkage that changes with current is small.
