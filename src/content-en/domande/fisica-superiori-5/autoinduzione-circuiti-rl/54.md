A linear inductor has an inductance of $L=4$ H. What is its magnetic flux linkage at $9$ A, in webers?

- $13$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $36$

## Explanation

For a linear inductor, the flux linkage is proportional to the current. The proportionality constant is $L$, giving $36$.
