A student reads the rise time constant where the current reaches 37% of its final value. Which percentage should be used on the rise curve from zero?

- About $100\%$
- About $86\%$
- About $63\%$
- About $50\%$

## Explanation

At $t=\tau$ the remaining gap is $e^{-1}\simeq0.37$ of the initial gap. Current has thus reached about $1-0.37=0.63$ of its final value.
