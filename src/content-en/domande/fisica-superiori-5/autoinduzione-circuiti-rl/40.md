Two coils with identical turns, geometry and cores use wire of different resistivity. Which comparison holds in the low-frequency model?

- Same resistance, necessarily different inductance
- Same geometrical inductance, different resistance
- Neither coil stores energy
- Both quantities must double

## Explanation

Inductance measures linkage per ampere and depends on the magnetic geometry. At fixed wire geometry, resistance instead scales with resistivity.
