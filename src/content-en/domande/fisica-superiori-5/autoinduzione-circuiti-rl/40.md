The rising current in an RL circuit follows $I(t)=I_\infty(1-e^{-t/\tau})$, with $I_\infty=18$ A. What is its value at $t=\tau\ln2$?

- $18\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $9\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

At the specified time, $e^{-t/\tau}=e^{-\ln2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The current is therefore half its steady-state value, giving $I=9$ A.
