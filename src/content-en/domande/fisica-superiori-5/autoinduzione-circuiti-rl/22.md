Four circuits, each consisting of an ideal source, a resistor and an ideal inductor in series, are closed at the same instant. In which circuit does the current first reach $99\,\%$ of its steady-state value?

- $L = 0.40\,\mathrm{H}$, $R = 80\,\Omega$
- $L = 50\,\mathrm{mH}$, $R = 2.0\,\Omega$
- $L = 10\,\mathrm{mH}$, $R = 1.0\,\Omega$
- $L = 1.2\,\mathrm{H}$, $R = 100\,\Omega$

## Explanation

When the RL circuit is closed the current is $\dfrac{\mathcal{E}}{R} \left( 1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} \right)$, so the fraction of the steady-state value reached depends only on $\dfrac{t}{\tau}$. The $99\,\%$ is reached at $t = \tau \ln 100$, therefore the circuit with the smallest time constant $\tau = \dfrac{L}{R}$ arrives first. The time constants are $25\,\mathrm{ms}$, $10\,\mathrm{ms}$, $5.0\,\mathrm{ms}$ and $12\,\mathrm{ms}$. The fastest circuit is thus the one with $L = 0.40\,\mathrm{H}$ and $R = 80\,\Omega$, even though it has neither the smallest inductance nor the largest resistance.
