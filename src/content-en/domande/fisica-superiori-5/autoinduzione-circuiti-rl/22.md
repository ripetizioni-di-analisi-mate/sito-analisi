An inductor has $L=6$ H, and its current increases at $3$ A/s. What is the magnitude of the self-induced emf?

- $2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $18\,\mathrm{V}$
- $9\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The self-induced emf is $\mathcal E_L=-L\dfrac{dI}{dt}$. Its magnitude is the inductance times the magnitude of the rate of change of current. Hence $|\mathcal E_L|=18$ V.
