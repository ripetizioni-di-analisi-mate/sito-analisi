A long linear solenoid is filled first with air and then with a material of four times the permeability. Its flux linkage must stay the same. What current is needed after insertion?

- One quarter of the original
- Four times the original
- Twice the original
- The original current

## Explanation

The new inductance is four times greater. Keeping $LI$ constant requires reducing the current by a factor of four.
