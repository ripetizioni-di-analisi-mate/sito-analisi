A $0.25\,\mathrm H$ inductor discharges from $1.2\,\mathrm A$ through $8.0\,\Omega$. Which expression gives the current after $50\,\mathrm{ms}$?

- $1.2(1-e^{-1.6})\,\mathrm A$
- $1.2e^{-1.6}\,\mathrm A$
- $1.2e^{1.6}\,\mathrm A$
- $1.2e^{-0.625}\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The time constant is $L/R=31.25\,\mathrm{ms}$. In source-free decay the initial current is multiplied by $e^{-t/\tau}=e^{-50/31.25}$, leaving about $0.24\,\mathrm A$.
