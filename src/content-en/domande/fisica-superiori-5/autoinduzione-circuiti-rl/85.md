Two coils have a mutual inductance of $2$ mH. The current in the first changes at $5000$ A/s. What is the magnitude of the emf induced in the second coil, in volts?

- $5$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$

## Explanation

The magnitude is the mutual inductance times the rate of change of current. The factor from the prefix milli- cancels the factor of one thousand in the rate, giving $10$.
