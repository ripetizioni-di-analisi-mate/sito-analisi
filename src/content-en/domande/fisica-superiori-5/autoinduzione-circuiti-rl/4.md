An RL circuit has $L=2$ H and $R=2$ ohms. What is its time constant?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is $\tau=\dfrac{L}{R}$. Divide the inductance by the total resistance. Hence $\tau=\dfrac{2}{2}=1$ s.
