An air-core solenoid is to have length $25\,\mathrm{cm}$, turn radius $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, and inductance $2.0\,\mathrm{mH}$. Treat the solenoid as long compared with its radius ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$). Determine the number of turns.

- $282$
- $3160$
- $1126$
- $563$

## Explanation

For a long solenoid inductance grows as turns squared. Finding the required turn count therefore requires taking a square root. $N = \sqrt{\dfrac{L\,\ell}{\mu_0 \pi r^2}} \approx 563$.
