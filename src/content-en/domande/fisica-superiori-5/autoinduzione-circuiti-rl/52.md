Steady current before switching is 4 A. The new constant circuit conditions also give 4 A in the same direction at steady state. Does current undergo a transient?

- No, it is already at the new equilibrium
- Yes, a twofold overshoot is required
- Yes, it must always restart at zero
- Yes, it oscillates undamped

## Explanation

In $i=I_f+(I_i-I_f)e^{-t/\tau}$ the exponential coefficient is zero. Current remains 4 A.
