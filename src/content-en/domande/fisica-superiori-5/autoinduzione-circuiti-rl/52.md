A linear inductor has an inductance of $L=2$ H. What is its magnetic flux linkage at $5$ A, in webers?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $7$
- $10$

## Explanation

For a linear inductor, the flux linkage is proportional to the current. The proportionality constant is $L$, giving $10$.
