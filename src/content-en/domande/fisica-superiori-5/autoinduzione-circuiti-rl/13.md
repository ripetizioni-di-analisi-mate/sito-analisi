An inductor with $L=4$ H carries $2$ A. How much magnetic energy does it store?

- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The stored energy is $U=\dfrac{1}{2}LI^2$. The square of the current is $4$. Hence $U=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot4=8$ J.
