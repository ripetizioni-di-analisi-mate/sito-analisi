At the center of a long air-core solenoid with $1200$ turns per meter is a coaxial coil with $80$ turns of radius $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$). What is the mutual inductance between the windings?

- $3.8\,\mu\mathrm H$
- $38\,\mu\mathrm H$
- $76\,\mu\mathrm H$
- $380\,\mu\mathrm H$

## Explanation

Current in the solenoid produces field $B=\mu_0 n_1 I_1$. Flux per secondary turn is field times area, and the turn contributions add. $M = \mu_0 n_1 N_2 \pi r_2^2 = 3.8 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{H}$.
