A series RL circuit is connected to a $21$ V DC source and has a total resistance of $3$ Ω. What is the steady-state current in amperes?

- $0$
- $63$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

In steady state, the current is constant. The voltage across the ideal inductor is zero, so the entire source voltage appears across the resistance. This gives $7$.
