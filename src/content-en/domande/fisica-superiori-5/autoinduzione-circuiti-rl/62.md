A series RL circuit is switched on across a constant ideal voltage $V$. At what current does energy enter the inductor's field at the highest rate, rather than being dissipated by the resistor? You may use $P_L=i(V-Ri)$.

- $0$
- $V/R$
- $V/(4R)$
- $V/(2R)$

## Explanation

The parabola $Vi-Ri^2$ vanishes initially at $i=0$ and at steady state where $i=V/R$. Its vertex is halfway between, where both $v_L$ and $v_R$ equal $V/2$.
