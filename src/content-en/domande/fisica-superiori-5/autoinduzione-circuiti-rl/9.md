An RL circuit has $L=3$ H and $R=2$ ohms. What is its time constant?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is $\tau=\dfrac{L}{R}$. Divide the inductance by the total resistance. Hence $\tau=\dfrac{3}{2}=1.5$ s.
