An ideal coil connected in series with a resistor and an ideal source of emf $3.0\,\mathrm{V}$ reaches a steady-state current $0.60\,\mathrm{A}$. The measured time constant is $8.0\,\mathrm{ms}$. Which resistance and inductance pair fits both measurements?

- $(5\,\Omega,40\,\mathrm{mH})$
- $(5\,\Omega,8\,\mathrm{mH})$
- $(0.2\,\Omega,40\,\mathrm{mH})$
- $(5\,\Omega,400\,\mathrm{mH})$

## Explanation

The steady-state reading determines the resistance. The transient then identifies the inductance. $R = \dfrac{\mathcal{E}}{I} = 5.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, $L = R\,\tau = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{H}$.
