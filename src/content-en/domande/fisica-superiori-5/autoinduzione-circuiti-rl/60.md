During the rise of current in a series RL circuit a voltmeter reads 4 V across the resistor and 8 V across the inductor. What fraction of the final current already flows with the same DC source?

- $1/2$
- $1/3$
- $2/3$
- $1/4$

## Explanation

Source voltage is $4+8=12\,\mathrm V$. Present current is $4/R$ and final current is $12/R$, giving one third.
