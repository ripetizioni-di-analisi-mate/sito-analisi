A circuit has an inductance of $36$ mH and a total resistance of $4$ Ω. What is its time constant in milliseconds?

- $36$
- $9$
- $144$
- $\dfrac{9}{1000}$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is the ratio of inductance to resistance. The prefix milli- in the inductance carries through to the result, giving $9$.
