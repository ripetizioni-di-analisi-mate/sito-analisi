An inductor of $2$ H has a self-induced emf of magnitude $10$ V. What is the magnitude of the rate of change of current, in amperes per second?

- $20$
- $5$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the emf is $L|di/dt|$. Dividing the measured value by the inductance gives $5$.
