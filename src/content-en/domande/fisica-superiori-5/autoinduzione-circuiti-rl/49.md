An ideal inductor carries $i(t)=a t^2$, with $a>0$ and $t>0$. What is the shape of its passive voltage versus time?

- A rising straight line through the origin
- A negative horizontal line
- A decaying exponential
- A parabola identical to the current curve

## Explanation

Differentiation gives $di/dt=2at$. The voltage $2Lat$ grows linearly from zero.
