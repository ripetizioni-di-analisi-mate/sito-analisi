A $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ long air-core solenoid has 300 turns of radius $8.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. A student finds about $7.6\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm H$ by inserting the transverse dimension in $L=\mu_0N^2A/\ell$. If the error was using the diameter in place of the radius when finding the area, what is the corrected result?

- $3.8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm H$
- $1.9\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm H$
- $1.52\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm H$
- $3.04\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm H$

## Explanation

With $A=\pi r^2$, using the diameter in place of the radius quadruples the area. It also quadruples $L$ when all other data are unchanged, so divide the reported answer by four.
