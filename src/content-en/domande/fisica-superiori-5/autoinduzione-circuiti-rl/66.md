Discharge current has fallen to 20% of its initial value. What percentage of initial energy has been dissipated in a circuit containing only constant resistance and inductance?

- $80\%$
- $20\%$
- $4\%$
- $96\%$

## Explanation

Remaining energy is proportional to current squared, hence 4%. The remaining 96% has been dissipated in the resistance.
