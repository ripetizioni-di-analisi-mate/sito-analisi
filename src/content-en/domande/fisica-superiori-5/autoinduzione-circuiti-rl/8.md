After the source is disconnected, a coil forms a closed circuit of total resistance $25\,\mathrm{\Omega}$. The current falls to one-quarter of its initial value in $12\,\mathrm{ms}$. What is the coil inductance?

- $0.075\,\mathrm H$
- $0.44\,\mathrm H$
- $0.30\,\mathrm H$
- $0.22\,\mathrm H$

## Explanation

The current ratio determines the time constant without the initial current in amperes. Multiply by total resistance to obtain inductance. $\tau = \dfrac{12\,\mathrm{ms}}{\ln 4} = 8.7\,\mathrm{ms}$, $L = R\,\tau = 0.22\,\mathrm{H}$.
