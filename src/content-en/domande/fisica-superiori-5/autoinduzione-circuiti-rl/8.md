An inductor with $L=3$ H carries $2$ A. How much magnetic energy does it store?

- $3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The stored energy is $U=\dfrac{1}{2}LI^2$. The square of the current is $4$. Hence $U=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot3\cdot4=6$ J.
