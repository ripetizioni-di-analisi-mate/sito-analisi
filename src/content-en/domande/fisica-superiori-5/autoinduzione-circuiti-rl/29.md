An RL circuit has $L=7$ H and $R=2$ ohms. What is its time constant?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is $\tau=\dfrac{L}{R}$. Divide the inductance by the total resistance. Hence $\tau=\dfrac{7}{2}=3.5$ s.
