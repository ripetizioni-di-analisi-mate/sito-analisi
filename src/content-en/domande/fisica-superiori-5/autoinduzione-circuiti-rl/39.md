An RL circuit in steady state stores $18$ J in its inductor. How much energy remains, in joules, when the current falls to one-third of its steady-state value?

- $9$
- $6$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The magnetic energy is proportional to the square of the current. Reducing the current to one-third reduces the energy to one-ninth of its original value, giving $2$.
