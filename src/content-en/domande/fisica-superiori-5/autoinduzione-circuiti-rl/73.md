The current in an RL circuit falls from $10$ A to $5$ A. If $\tau=2$ ms, how long did this decrease take, in milliseconds?

- $\dfrac{2}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}$
- $2 \log{\left(2 \right)}$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The ratio of the final current to the initial current is one-half. Taking the logarithm in $e^{-t/\tau}=1/2$ and solving for the time gives $2 \log{\left(2 \right)}$.
