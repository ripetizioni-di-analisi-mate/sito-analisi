A relay needs $0.70\,\mathrm A$ to close. The coil current rises from zero as $i=1.0\,\mathrm A\,(1-e^{-t/\tau})$. The drive lasts exactly one time constant. Does it activate the relay? Use $e^{-1}\simeq0.37$.

- No, current rises to about $0.63\,\mathrm A$, below threshold
- No, because current stays zero throughout the first time constant
- Yes, because the eventual steady-state current is $1.0\,\mathrm A$
- Yes, because the transient peak always exceeds $1.0\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

Current rises throughout the drive, so the maximum available value is the one at its end. This gives $i(\tau)\simeq(1-0.37)\,\mathrm A=0.63\,\mathrm A$, below the closing threshold.
