The same flux density $B$ is maintained in a linear medium and in vacuum. If $\mu=4\mu_0$, what is the ratio of magnetic energy density in the medium to that in vacuum?

- $1/16$
- $1/4$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

At fixed $B$ use $u=B^2/(2\mu)$. The denominator is four times larger in the medium, giving one quarter of the energy density.
