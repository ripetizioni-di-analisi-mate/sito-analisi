The current in an RL circuit falls from $18$ A to $9$ A. If $\tau=4$ ms, how long did this decrease take, in milliseconds?

- $2$
- $4 \log{\left(2 \right)}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}$
- $8$

## Explanation

The ratio of the final current to the initial current is one-half. Taking the logarithm in $e^{-t/\tau}=1/2$ and solving for the time gives $4 \log{\left(2 \right)}$.
