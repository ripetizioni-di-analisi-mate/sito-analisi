A series RL circuit is switched on with zero initial current. The source voltage is $10$ V and the inductance is $L=2$ H. What is the initial rate of rise of the current, in amperes per second?

- $10$
- $5$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## Explanation

Just after the switch closes, the voltage drop across the resistance is zero because the current is still zero. The source voltage therefore equals $L\,di/dt$, giving $5$.
