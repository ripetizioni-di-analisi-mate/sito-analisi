Current in a $0.12\,\mathrm H$ coil falls uniformly from $2.0\,\mathrm A$ to zero in $40\,\mathrm{ms}$. What is the signed self-induced emf when the positive sense is that of the initial current?

- $+0.006\,\mathrm V$
- $0\,\mathrm V$
- $+6.0\,\mathrm V$
- $-6.0\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

The slope is $-2/0.040=-50\,\mathrm{A/s}$. The emf $-L\,di/dt$ acts along the initial current, opposing its decrease.
