An inductor has $L=2$ H, and its current increases at $3$ A/s. What is the magnitude of the self-induced emf?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The self-induced emf is $\mathcal E_L=-L\dfrac{dI}{dt}$. Its magnitude is the inductance times the magnitude of the rate of change of current. Hence $|\mathcal E_L|=6$ V.
