Two uncoupled ideal inductors are connected in parallel to a short voltage pulse. One is 2 mH and the other 6 mH. In which branch does current rise faster?

- In the first, with three times the slope
- In the second, with three times the slope
- With equal slopes in both
- In neither, because they are parallel

## Explanation

Both branches have the same voltage and $di/dt=v/L$. The smaller inductance has a current change three times as fast.
