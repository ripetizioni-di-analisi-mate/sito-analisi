An inductor of $3$ H carries $7$ A. How much energy does it store, in joules?

- $\dfrac{63}{2}$
- $\dfrac{147}{2}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}$
- $147$

## Explanation

The energy stored in an inductor is half the inductance times the square of the current. Substituting the values in SI units gives $\dfrac{147}{2}$.
