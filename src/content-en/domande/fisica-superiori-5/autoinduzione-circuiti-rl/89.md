After many time constants a real coil still draws power from a DC source. Where does that power go once the field has settled?

- It continually creates turns
- It becomes heat in the winding resistance
- It increases magnetic energy indefinitely
- It is all returned to the battery

## Explanation

With constant current the energy $LI^2/2$ stays fixed. Real wire still dissipates $I^2R$.
