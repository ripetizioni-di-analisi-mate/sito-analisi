An RL circuit has $L=5$ H and $R=2$ ohms. What is its time constant?

- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The time constant of an RL circuit is $\tau=\dfrac{L}{R}$. Divide the inductance by the total resistance. Hence $\tau=\dfrac{5}{2}=2.5$ s.
