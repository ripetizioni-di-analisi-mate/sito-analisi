Current in an inductor is ramped from 0 to 3 A. A second ramp takes it from 3 to 6 A in the same time. How do the energies absorbed by the field compare for constant $L$?

- The second is three times the first
- The second is fourfold
- The second is double
- They are equal

## Explanation

The difference of squared currents matters. For the two ramps the factors are $3^2-0=9$ and $6^2-3^2=27$.
