An RL circuit initially carries $9$ A when its source is removed and its resistive path is kept closed. What is the current after two time constants, in amperes?

- $4.5$
- $\dfrac{9}{e^{2}}$
- $9 - \dfrac{9}{e^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{9}{e}$

## Explanation

The decay factor after switch-off is $e^{-t/\tau}$. The ratio of elapsed time to the time constant is two, giving $\dfrac{9}{e^{2}}$.
