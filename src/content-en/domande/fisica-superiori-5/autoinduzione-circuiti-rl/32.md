An instrument displays inductance in $\mathrm{\Omega\,s}$. Which dimensional argument justifies that choice?

- From $P=vi$, $[L]=\mathrm{W/s}$
- From $v=Ri$, $[L]=\mathrm{\Omega/s}$
- From $q=Ci$, $[L]=\mathrm{A\,s}$
- From $v=L\,di/dt$, $[L]=\mathrm{V\,s/A}=\mathrm{\Omega\,s}$

## Explanation

The slope $di/dt$ has units of amperes per second. Dividing volts by that slope gives volt-seconds per ampere, or ohms multiplied by seconds. This unit is the henry.
