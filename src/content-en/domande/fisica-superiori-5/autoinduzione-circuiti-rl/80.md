An open secondary has 2 V induced emf during a primary current change. Why is the power transferred to an ideal voltmeter connected across the terminals zero?

- Primary current change must be zero
- There is no closed path for load current
- Mutual inductance must be zero
- Emf equals power

## Explanation

Induced voltage can exist across an open secondary. With no load current, voltage times current is zero.
