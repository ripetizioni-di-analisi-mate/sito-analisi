A digital drive periodically switches a coil between a high and a low voltage. An engineer replaces the drive with its mean voltage to estimate current. Which condition limits the error due to current ripple?

- The switching period is much shorter than the RL time constant
- The inductance is zero at any frequency
- The current reaches both extreme steady-state values every cycle
- The period is much longer than the RL time constant

## Explanation

RL current changes on the timescale $\tau=L/R$. If each phase lasts only a small fraction of $\tau$, current changes little during it and has only a small ripple around its mean.
