The current in an RL circuit falls from $14$ A to $7$ A. If $\tau=3$ ms, how long did this decrease take, in milliseconds?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{\log{\left(2 \right)}}$
- $3 \log{\left(2 \right)}$

## Explanation

The ratio of the final current to the initial current is one-half. Taking the logarithm in $e^{-t/\tau}=1/2$ and solving for the time gives $3 \log{\left(2 \right)}$.
