Two equal vacuum regions contain fields of magnitudes $B$ and $2B$. A single uniform field should store the same total energy in the same combined volume. What magnitude is needed?

- $\sqrt{5/2}\,B$
- $3B$
- $5B/4$
- $\sqrt5\,B$

## Explanation

Energy per volume is proportional to $B^2$. The equivalent field therefore satisfies $B_{\rm eq}^2=(B^2+4B^2)/2$.
