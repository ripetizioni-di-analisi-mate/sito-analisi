A coil is needed to carry $2\,\mathrm A$ of DC without saturating the core or exceeding its thermal limit. The data sheet lists a saturation current of $1.5\,\mathrm A$ and a thermal current rating of $3\,\mathrm A$. What follows?

- Both limits are met because only the higher rating matters.
- Both limits are exceeded.
- The thermal limit is met, but the inductance specified for small signals can drop.
- The thermal limit is exceeded, but the core certainly remains linear.

## Explanation

The two ratings describe different phenomena. At $2\,\mathrm A$ the magnetic saturation threshold is exceeded while the winding current remains within the thermal rating.
