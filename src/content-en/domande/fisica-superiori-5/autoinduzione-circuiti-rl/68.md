The steady-state current in an RL circuit is $7$ A. Starting from zero current, what is the current, in amperes, one time constant after the switch closes?

- $7 - \dfrac{7}{e^{2}}$
- $\dfrac{7}{e}$
- $7 - \dfrac{7}{e}$
- $7$

## Explanation

As the current rises, the fraction of its steady-state value that it reaches is $1-e^{-t/\tau}$. After one time constant the exponent is minus one, giving $7 - \dfrac{7}{e}$.
