The source is removed, leaving a closed resistive return path. Initial current is 3 A and $\tau=4\,\mathrm{ms}$. What total charge passes a section during the entire decay?

- $12\,\mathrm{mC}$
- $48\,\mathrm{mC}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{mC}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mC}$

## Explanation

Charge is the area under $I_0e^{-t/\tau}$ from zero to infinity. That area equals $I_0\tau=3\cdot0.004=0.012\,\mathrm C$.
