An ideal $4.5\,\mathrm V$ source, a $1.5\,\Omega$ resistor and a $30\,\mathrm{mH}$ inductor are connected in series with zero initial current. What current flows after $30\,\mathrm{ms}$? Use $e^{-1.5}\simeq0.22$.

- $0.66\,\mathrm A$
- $4.5\,\mathrm A$
- $3.0\,\mathrm A$
- $2.3\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The final current is $4.5/1.5=3\,\mathrm A$ and the time constant is $30\,\mathrm{mH}/1.5\,\Omega=20\,\mathrm{ms}$. After one and a half time constants the completed fraction of the rise is $1-e^{-1.5}$.
