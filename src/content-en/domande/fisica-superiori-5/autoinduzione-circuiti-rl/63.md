An extra resistor is inserted in series with an RL circuit already carrying steady DC. Immediately afterwards, which quantity changes instantaneously in the ideal model?

- The elementary charge of the carriers
- Magnetic energy by a finite jump
- Current itself by a finite jump
- The time derivative of current

## Explanation

Current is continuous, so $Li^2/2$ is continuous as well. The new resistance changes the resistive drop and thus the inductor voltage and current slope.
