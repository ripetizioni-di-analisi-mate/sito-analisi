A series RL circuit is connected to a $36$ V DC source and has a total resistance of $4$ Ω. What is the steady-state current in amperes?

- $9$
- $0$
- $36$
- $144$

## Explanation

In steady state, the current is constant. The voltage across the ideal inductor is zero, so the entire source voltage appears across the resistance. This gives $9$.
