A real coil is driven by a current ramp. At the measurement instant its terminal voltage is $5.0\,\mathrm V$, the ohmic drop in the same sense is $2.0\,\mathrm V$, and $di/dt=6.0\,\mathrm{A/s}$. What is its inductance?

- $0.83\,\mathrm H$
- $1.17\,\mathrm H$
- $0.50\,\mathrm H$
- $2.0\,\mathrm H$

## Explanation

In the passive convention $v=Ri+L\,di/dt$. Only $5-2=3\,\mathrm V$ are due to the change in current, giving $L=3/6\,\mathrm H$.
