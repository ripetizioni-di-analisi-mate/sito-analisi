The current in an ideal $2$ H inductor rises at a constant rate from $5$ A to $8$ A in one second. What is the self-induced emf in volts, taking the direction of the current as positive?

- $-3$
- $6$
- $-6$
- $-10$

## Explanation

Since the interval is one second, the rate of change of current is numerically equal to the increase in current. The self-induced emf is this rate times the inductance, with the opposite sign, giving $-6$.
