An inductive differential sensor has two opposing secondary windings. Each produces 10 mV with the core centred. After displacement, the voltages become 13 mV and 7 mV. What is the magnitude of the differential output?

- $6\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mV}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mV}$

## Explanation

The opposing connection subtracts the two signals. They cancel when centred, while the displacement gives magnitude $13-7=6\,\mathrm{mV}$.
