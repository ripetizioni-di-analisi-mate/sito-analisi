Two coils have a mutual inductance of $3$ mH. The current in the first changes at $7000$ A/s. What is the magnitude of the emf induced in the second coil, in volts?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{21}{1000}$
- $21$

## Explanation

The magnitude is the mutual inductance times the rate of change of current. The factor from the prefix milli- cancels the factor of one thousand in the rate, giving $21$.
