A winding links 7 mWb at zero current because of a fixed magnet. At 2 A its total flux linkage is 17 mWb. What is its linear inductance?

- $8.5\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mH}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{mH}$

## Explanation

Subtract the current-independent flux. The flux–current slope is $(17-7)/2=5\,\mathrm{mH}$.
