A circuit with a $20\,\mathrm V$ source, $10\,\Omega$ resistance and $0.15\,\mathrm H$ ideal inductance is in steady state. A switch removes the source and resistor and connects the inductor across $30\,\Omega$ without interrupting its current. What is the peak voltage magnitude across the new resistor?

- $0\,\mathrm V$
- $6.7\,\mathrm V$
- $60\,\mathrm V$
- $20\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

Current starts in the new branch at its previous value $20/10=2\,\mathrm A$. It then decays, so the peak is at switching: $30\cdot2=60\,\mathrm V$.
