Primary current is constant but a second coil moves closer to the primary. Can a secondary emf appear?

- No, constant current always precludes induction
- Only if secondary resistance vanishes
- Only by changing electron charge
- Yes, if mutual inductance changes

## Explanation

Primary-induced secondary linkage is $MI_1$. Even for fixed $I_1$, its derivative can be nonzero when geometry and therefore $M$ change.
