A coil with $N=10$, $I=3.0\,\mathrm A$ and area per turn $0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$ experiences a torque of magnitude $0.060\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ in a $0.40\,\mathrm T$ field. Which angles between the oriented normal and the field in the range $0^\circ$ to $180^\circ$ fit this reading?

- Only $30^\circ$
- $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$
- $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$
- $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$

## Explanation

The peak torque is $NIAB=0.12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The measured torque divided by this peak gives $\sin\theta=0.060/0.12=1/2$, shared by two supplementary angles.
