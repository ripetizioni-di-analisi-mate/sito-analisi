A freely rotating loop is at equilibrium with its normal antiparallel to a uniform field. What does a small angular displacement reveal?

- Torque carries it farther from its initial position
- The normal always returns to its initial position
- The field increases the loop's magnetic moment magnitude
- Torque remains zero at every nearby angle

## Explanation

The antiparallel configuration has maximum potential energy. A small rotation lowers the energy and the torque favors further rotation in the same direction.
