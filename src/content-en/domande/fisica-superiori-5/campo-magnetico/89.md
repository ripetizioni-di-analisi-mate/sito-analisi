A magnet and a current-carrying wire are attached to one isolated cart. They exert horizontal forces on each other, and external leads exert negligible horizontal force. How does this interaction affect the centre of mass of the complete cart?

- It always accelerates toward the wire
- It moves by the wire length
- It always accelerates toward the magnet
- It produces no horizontal acceleration of the centre of mass

## Explanation

For the complete system the two steady magnetic forces are internal and opposite. Centre-of-mass acceleration is set by the net external force.
