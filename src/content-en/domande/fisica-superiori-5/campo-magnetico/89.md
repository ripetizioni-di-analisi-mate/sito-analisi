Two particles have the same initial velocity perpendicular to a field of $3$ T. Their charges have opposite signs and equal magnitude $7$ µC. How do their initial forces compare?

- Equal magnitudes and opposite directions.
- Equal magnitudes and the same direction.
- One force is zero and the other has positive magnitude.
- Different magnitudes and the same direction.

## Explanation

For both particles, the force magnitude is the product of the same three magnitudes. Changing the sign of the charge reverses only the direction of the force.
