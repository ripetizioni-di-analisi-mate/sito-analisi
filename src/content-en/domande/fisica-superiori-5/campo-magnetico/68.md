A rectangular loop is reshaped into a square without stretching the wire. Current is unchanged. How does its magnetic moment magnitude compare with that of the original nonsquare rectangle?

- It stays the same
- It becomes zero
- It increases
- It decreases

## Explanation

For fixed perimeter a square has the largest area among rectangles. The magnetic moment $IA$ increases with enclosed area.
