Electrons in a metal also have random thermal velocities. Why does their mean drift velocity enter the total magnetic force on the wire?

- Random-velocity contributions cancel on average
- Charge depends on temperature
- All electrons have exactly the same velocity
- Drift velocity is always the largest velocity

## Explanation

Force is linear in velocity. When many carriers are summed, randomly directed thermal components average to zero, leaving the drift motion.
