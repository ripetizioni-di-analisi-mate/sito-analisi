A charge of magnitude $3$ µC enters a field of $4$ T at right angles with a speed of $7000$ m/s. What is the magnitude of the force in millinewtons?

- $84000\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $21\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $84\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\dfrac{21}{250}\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

Multiplying microcoulombs by thousands of meters per second introduces a factor of $10^{-3}$ in the force expressed in newtons. Since the vectors are perpendicular, the sine is one. This gives $84\,\mathrm{mN}$.
