A particle has kinetic energy $12$ J and experiences only the force exerted by a static magnetic field. How much work does the magnetic force do?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is perpendicular to the instantaneous velocity. Its dot product with the infinitesimal displacement is therefore zero. Thus the total work is zero and the kinetic energy remains $12$ J.
