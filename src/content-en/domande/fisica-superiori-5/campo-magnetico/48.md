A wire segment $90$ cm long carries $4$ A and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with a uniform field of $5$ T. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $9 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $900\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force has magnitude $ILB\sin\theta$. Express the length in meters and use the sine of thirty degrees, which is one half. This gives $9\,\mathrm{N}$.
