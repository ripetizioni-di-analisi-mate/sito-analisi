The current reverses at the same moment that the magnet is flipped. How does the force on a wire initially pushed upward change?

- It becomes parallel to the wire
- It continues to point upward
- It always vanishes
- It points downward

## Explanation

Both vector factors in $I\boldsymbol\ell\times\mathbf B$ reverse sign. The two sign changes cancel.
