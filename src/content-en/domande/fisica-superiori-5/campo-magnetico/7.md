An alpha particle of charge $2e$ moves perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.30\,\mathrm{T}$ and experiences a force of magnitude $4.8 \cdot 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{N}$. Calculate the particle's speed. Use $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$.

- $2.5\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.0\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

An alpha particle carries two elementary charges. Omitting that factor would double the speed inferred from the measured force. $v = \dfrac{F}{2 e B} = 5.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
