A straight wire is parallel to the field lines of a uniform magnetic field. The current doubles without changing direction. How does the total magnetic force on this segment change?

- It doubles and points along the wire.
- It doubles and points perpendicular to the wire.
- It remains zero.
- It reverses direction.

## Explanation

The force on the wire is proportional to the cross product of the length vector and the field. The vectors are parallel, so their cross product remains zero.
