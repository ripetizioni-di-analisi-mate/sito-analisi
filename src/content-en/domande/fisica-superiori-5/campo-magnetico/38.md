Iron filings on a card show arcs between two poles. Which information also requires an oriented compass?

- The positions of the poles on the card
- The visible shape of the arcs
- The distance between two drawn arcs
- The direction along each arc

## Explanation

Filings align with the field, but a filing does not distinguish the ends of its axis. The north end of a compass indicates the field direction.
