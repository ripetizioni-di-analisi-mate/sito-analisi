A square loop of side length $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ carrying a current $I = 2.5\,\mathrm{A}$ is in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.30\,\mathrm{T}$ parallel to its plane. Calculate the torque on the loop.

- $4.8\,\mathrm{mN\,m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mN\,m}$
- $0.48\,\mathrm{mN\,m}$

## Explanation

A field parallel to the plane is perpendicular to the magnetic moment. The area is the square of the side length in metres. $M = I S B = 4.8 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
