A wire $0.5$ m long carries a current of $5$ A and is perpendicular to a magnetic field of $0.2$ T. What is the magnitude of the force on the wire?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force on a wire is $F=I\ell B\sin\theta$. The wire is perpendicular to the field, so $\sin\theta=1$. This gives $F=5\cdot0.5\cdot0.2=0.5$ N.
