Opposite magnetic forces of $0.30\,\mathrm N$ act on a loop. Their lines of action are $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. What is the couple's torque magnitude?

- $0.024\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0.012\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0.006\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The couple's arm is the distance between the two lines of action. The torque is $Fd=0.30\cdot0.04=0.012\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
