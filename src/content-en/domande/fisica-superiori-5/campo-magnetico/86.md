A straight wire segment $7$ m long carries $3$ A along $+x$. The field has components $B_x=3$ T and $B_y=4$ T, with $B_z=0$. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $105\,\mathrm{N}$
- $147\,\mathrm{N}$
- $63\,\mathrm{N}$
- $84\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The field component along the wire contributes nothing to the cross product. Multiply the current by the length and the magnitude of the perpendicular component. This gives $84\,\mathrm{N}$.
