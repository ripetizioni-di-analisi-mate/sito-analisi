A wire segment $70$ cm long carries $3$ A and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with a uniform field of $4$ T. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $420\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{42}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{21}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{21 \sqrt{3}}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force has magnitude $ILB\sin\theta$. Express the length in meters and use the sine of thirty degrees, which is one half. This gives $\dfrac{21}{5}\,\mathrm{N}$.
