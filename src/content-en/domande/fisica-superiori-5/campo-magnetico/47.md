For a wire perpendicular to a field, the plot of magnetic force versus current has slope $0.06\,\mathrm{N/A}$. What does the slope represent?

- $B/\ell$
- $\ell/B$
- $BI$
- $B\ell$

## Explanation

Here $F=(B\ell)I$. The coefficient of $I$ in the graph is therefore the field times the immersed length.
