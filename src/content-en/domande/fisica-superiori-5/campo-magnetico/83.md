A magnetic needle free to rotate comes to rest in a uniform field of $3$ mT. In which direction does its north pole point at stable equilibrium?

- Opposite to the field.
- In the direction of the field.
- Perpendicular to the field.
- At 45 degrees to the field.

## Explanation

The magnetic moment of the needle points toward its north pole. At stable equilibrium, it points in the direction of the field.
