A loop can rotate only about an axis parallel to $\mathbf B$. How much work does magnetic torque do during an allowed rotation?

- Always positive
- $\mu B$ per radian in every position
- Zero
- Always negative

## Explanation

Torque $\boldsymbol\mu\times\mathbf B$ is perpendicular to $\mathbf B$ and hence to the allowed axis. Its component along the axis, which does work in the rotation, is zero.
