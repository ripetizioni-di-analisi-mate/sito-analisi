The magnetic moment of a loop has magnitude $5$ A·m² and points in the same direction as a field of $2$ T. What is the magnetic potential energy $U=-\boldsymbol\mu\cdot\mathbf B$?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $- \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the dot product, the cosine of the angle is one. The definition of potential energy includes a minus sign. This gives $-10\,\mathrm{J}$.
