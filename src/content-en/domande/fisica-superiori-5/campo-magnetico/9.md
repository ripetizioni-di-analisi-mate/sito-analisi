A coil has $10$ turns, each with an area of $0.02$ square meters, and carries a current of $3$ A. What is the magnitude of its magnetic moment?

- $0.6\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$

## Explanation

For a flat coil, the magnetic moment has magnitude $\mu=NIA$. Multiply the number of turns by the current and the area of a single turn. The result is $\mu=10\cdot3\cdot0.02=0.6$ A·m².
