A horizontal rod of length $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ and mass $8.0\,\mathrm{g}$ is in a uniform horizontal magnetic field $B = 0.20\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to the rod. Calculate the current required for the magnetic force to balance the rod's weight. Use $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $0.16\,\mathrm A$
- $6.4\,\mathrm A$
- $1.6\,\mathrm A$
- $0.39\,\mathrm A$

## Explanation

The rod can be supported if the current produces an upward magnetic force. Vertical equilibrium determines the required magnitude. $I = \dfrac{m g}{\ell B} \approx 1.6\,\mathrm{A}$.
