A particle moving at $7$ m/s enters a region where only a static magnetic field acts on it. What is its speed in meters per second after $3$ s if it remains in the region?

- $3$
- $0$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is always perpendicular to the velocity, so the power it delivers is zero. The kinetic energy remains constant, and so does the speed. This gives $7$.
