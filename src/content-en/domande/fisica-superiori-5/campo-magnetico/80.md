A moving-coil pointer settles at 18 divisions with a test current and at 2 divisions with no current. Assuming linear response, what reading is expected at twice the test current?

- 32 divisions
- 34 divisions
- 20 divisions
- 36 divisions

## Explanation

Current produces a deflection of $18-2=16$ divisions. Doubling the current adds 32 divisions to the zero reading, giving 34.
