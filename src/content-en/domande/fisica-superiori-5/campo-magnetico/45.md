A balance under a magnet registers a downward force of $3\,\mathrm{mN}$ when current flows in a nearby wire. What magnetic force does the magnet exert on the wire?

- $3\,\mathrm{mN}$ upward
- $3\,\mathrm{mN}$ downward
- Zero
- $6\,\mathrm{mN}$ upward

## Explanation

In this steady experiment the wire and magnet exert equal and opposite forces on one another. The wire is therefore pushed upward.
