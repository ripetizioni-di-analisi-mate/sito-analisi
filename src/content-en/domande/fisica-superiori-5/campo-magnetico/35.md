A particle of charge $2$ µC travels at $500$ m/s. The uniform magnetic field has magnitude $2$ T, and the force has magnitude $1$ mN. What is the sine of the angle between the field and the velocity?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The product of the three magnitudes is two millinewtons. Divide the force magnitude by this product to find the sine. This gives $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
