A charge of $2$ microcoulombs moves at $1000$ m/s perpendicular to a field of $B=0.5$ T. What is the magnitude of the magnetic force in millinewtons?

- $2\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $0.001\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the Lorentz force is $F=|q|vB$ when the velocity is perpendicular to the field. This gives $F=2\cdot10^{-6}\cdot1000\cdot0.5$ N. Converting to millinewtons gives $1$ mN.
