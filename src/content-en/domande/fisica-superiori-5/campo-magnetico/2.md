A $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ wire carries $6.0\,\mathrm A$ at $45^\circ$ to a $0.50\,\mathrm T$ field. Which effective length belongs in $F=IB\ell_{\perp}$ when calculating the force on the wire?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Project the wire length onto the plane perpendicular to the field. This gives $\ell_{\perp}=12\sin45^\circ\,\mathrm{cm}$ and hence $F\simeq0.25\,\mathrm N$.
