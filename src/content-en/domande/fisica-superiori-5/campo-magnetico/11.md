A wire $0.5$ m long carries a current of $4$ A and is perpendicular to a magnetic field of $0.2$ T. What is the magnitude of the force on the wire?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force on a wire is $F=I\ell B\sin\theta$. The wire is perpendicular to the field, so $\sin\theta=1$. This gives $F=4\cdot0.5\cdot0.2=0.4$ N.
