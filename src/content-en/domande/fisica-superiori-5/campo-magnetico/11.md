A 40-turn circular coil of radius $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries $0.60\,\mathrm A$. Its normal makes $45^\circ$ with a $0.25\,\mathrm T$ field. What is the magnetic moment of the coil, used when finding the torque?

- $1.7\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $4.2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $4.2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$

## Explanation

The coil has moment $\mu=NI\pi r^2$. The field and angle enter only in the subsequent torque calculation, $\mu B\sin45^\circ$.
