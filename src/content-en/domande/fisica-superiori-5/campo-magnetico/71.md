The magnetic moment of a loop has magnitude $7$ A·m² and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with a field of $4$ T. What is the magnitude of the torque?

- $14 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $28\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of a cross product is the product of the two magnitudes and the sine of the angle between them. The sine of thirty degrees is one half. This gives $14\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
