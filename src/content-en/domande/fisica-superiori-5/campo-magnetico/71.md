Two small coils of equal magnetic moment magnitude $m$ are fixed to one support with their moments perpendicular. What is the combined moment magnitude?

- $2m$
- $0$
- $\sqrt2\,m$
- $m$

## Explanation

Magnetic moments add as vectors. For equal perpendicular components the magnitude is $\sqrt{m^2+m^2}$.
