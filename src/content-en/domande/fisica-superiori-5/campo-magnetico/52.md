A horizontal rail supports a stationary conductor. Its horizontal magnetic force is $0.15\,\mathrm N$ and the maximum static friction is $0.20\,\mathrm N$. What is the actual friction magnitude?

- Zero
- $0.15\,\mathrm N$
- $0.20\,\mathrm N$
- $0.05\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The friction limit has not been reached, so the conductor remains stationary and friction exactly balances the applied force.
