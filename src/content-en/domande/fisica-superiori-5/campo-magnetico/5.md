A proton travels at $v = 6.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.50\,\mathrm{T}$. Calculate the magnitude of the Lorentz force on the proton. Use $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$.

- $1.3\cdot10^{-25}\,\mathrm N$
- $9.6\cdot10^{-14}\,\mathrm N$
- $4.8\cdot10^{-13}\,\mathrm N$
- $4.8\cdot10^{-14}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

A single proton carries one elementary charge. Perpendicular entry makes the angular factor equal to one. $F = e v B = 4.8 \cdot 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{N}$.
