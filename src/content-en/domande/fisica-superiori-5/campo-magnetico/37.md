Two compass needles are placed one centimetre apart to map Earth's field. Moving one turns the other. Which change would improve the measurement?

- Bring the needles closer
- Simply swap the point labels
- Clamp the needles together
- Move a single needle between the positions

## Explanation

Each needle aligns with the total field, which includes the other needle's field. A single compass removes that contribution.
