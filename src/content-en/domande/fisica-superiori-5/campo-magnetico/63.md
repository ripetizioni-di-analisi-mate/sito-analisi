A particle of charge $4$ µC travels at $9000$ m/s. The uniform field of $5$ T is antiparallel to its velocity. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $0\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $360\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $90\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $180\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The angle between the velocity and the field is 180 degrees. Its sine is zero, so the cross product vanishes. This gives $0\,\mathrm{mN}$.
