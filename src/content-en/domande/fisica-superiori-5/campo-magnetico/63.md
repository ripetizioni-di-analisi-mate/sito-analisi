A beam carries $10^{10}$ identical ions past a point each second. Each ion carries $2e$, with $e=1.6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$. What conventional current does the beam represent?

- $3.2\,\mathrm{nA}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{nA}$
- $32\,\mathrm{nA}$
- $0.32\,\mathrm{nA}$

## Explanation

Current is charge passing per unit time. The ion rate multiplied by $2e$ gives $3.2\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm A$.
