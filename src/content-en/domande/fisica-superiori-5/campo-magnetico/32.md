A bar magnet is cut perpendicular to its axis into three equal pieces. What is obtained?

- One piece with only a north pole, one with only a south pole and a central piece with no poles
- Three demagnetized pieces
- Two end pieces with one pole each and a central piece with two poles
- Three magnets, each with a north pole and a south pole

## Explanation

Magnetic field lines are closed, because there are no isolated magnetic charges from which they could start or on which they could end.

A piece with a single pole would be an isolated magnetic charge, so no piece can have a single pole. Each fragment keeps the magnetization of the material, therefore it produces a field whose lines leave one face and enter the opposite face. Consequently **three magnets** are obtained, each with a north pole and a south pole.
