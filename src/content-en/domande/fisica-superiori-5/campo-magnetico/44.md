A single-axis sensor reads zero. Rotating it by $90^\circ$ at the same point gives $4\,\mathrm{mT}$. What can be concluded about the field magnitude?

- It is exactly zero
- It is at most $2\,\mathrm{mT}$
- It is at least $4\,\mathrm{mT}$
- It is exactly $8\,\mathrm{mT}$

## Explanation

The reading is one component of the field. Its magnitude cannot exceed the vector magnitude, and a third component may be present.
