A wire above a compass runs along the needle when no current flows. Equal and opposite currents deflect the needle in opposite directions. Which contribution reverses?

- Earth's field
- The weight of the needle
- The wire-to-needle distance
- The field produced by the current

## Explanation

Earth's contribution stays fixed during the test. Reversing the current reverses the wire's field, changing the direction of their vector sum.
