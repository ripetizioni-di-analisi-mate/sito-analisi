An electron of mass $9.11\cdot10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$ enters a magnetic field at $30^\circ$. The magnetic force has magnitude $8.0\cdot10^{-16}\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnitude of the acceleration due to the field?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8.8\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8.8\cdot10^{15}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.4\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The given force already includes the $\sin30^\circ$ factor. Divide by mass. The acceleration is perpendicular to velocity and changes its direction.
