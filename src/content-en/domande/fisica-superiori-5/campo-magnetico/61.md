A particle of charge $2$ µC travels at $5000$ m/s. The uniform field of $3$ T is antiparallel to its velocity. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $15\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The angle between the velocity and the field is 180 degrees. Its sine is zero, so the cross product vanishes. This gives $0\,\mathrm{mN}$.
