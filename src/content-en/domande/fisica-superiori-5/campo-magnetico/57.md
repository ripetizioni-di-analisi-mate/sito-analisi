A detector tracks a particle entering a static field perpendicularly. Its kinetic energy drops as it also passes through a foil. Which mechanism explains the drop?

- The change in velocity direction alone
- Interactions with matter in the foil
- The choice of positive $x$
- Negative work by the magnetic field

## Explanation

Magnetic force is perpendicular to velocity and has zero power. Energy loss requires an exchange with the traversed material.
