A positive charge traveling at $9$ m/s along $+x$ enters a field of $4$ T pointing along $+z$. In which direction does the force initially point?

- Toward $+z$.
- Toward $+x$.
- Toward $+y$.
- Toward $-y$.

## Explanation

The force on a positive charge points in the direction of the cross product of its velocity and the field. The cross product of the unit vector along $+x$ with the unit vector along $+z$ points along $-y$.
