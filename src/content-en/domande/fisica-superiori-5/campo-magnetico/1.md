A straight cable of length $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a direct current $I = 1.2\,\mathrm{A}$. The cable is perpendicular to Earth's magnetic field. At this location the field has magnitude $B = 5.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$. Calculate the magnitude of the magnetic force on the cable.

- $0.048\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $0.48\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $480\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The cable is perpendicular to Earth's field, so its entire length contributes to the force. $F = I \ell B = 4.8 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N}$.
