A magnetic field points east. A proton enters the field traveling north. In which direction does the force initially point?

- East.
- Downward.
- Upward.
- North.

## Explanation

East, north, and up form a right-handed coordinate system. The cross product of the north unit vector with the east unit vector points downward, and the proton has positive charge.
