A small motor rotor starts from rest with moment of inertia $0.02\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. Its initial driving torque is $0.10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and its opposing torque is $0.04\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. What is its initial angular acceleration?

- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The net torque is $0.10-0.04=0.06\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Dividing by the moment of inertia gives $3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$.
