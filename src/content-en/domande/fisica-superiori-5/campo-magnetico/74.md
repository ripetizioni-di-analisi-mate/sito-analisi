The magnetic moment of a loop has magnitude $7$ A·m² and points in the same direction as a field of $3$ T. What is the magnetic potential energy $U=-\boldsymbol\mu\cdot\mathbf B$?

- $- \dfrac{3}{7}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $21\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-21\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the dot product, the cosine of the angle is one. The definition of potential energy includes a minus sign. This gives $-21\,\mathrm{J}$.
