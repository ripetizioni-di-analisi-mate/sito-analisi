A motor coil rotates uniformly and the commutator reverses the current every half-turn. Peak torque is $0.30\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Use $\langle\lvert\sin\theta\rvert\rangle=2/\pi$ over one turn. What mean torque does the motor produce?

- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0.47\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0.19\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Commutation makes the torques in the two halves of the turn have the same sign. At constant angular speed the time average equals the angular average, $0.30\cdot 2/\pi$ in newton metres.
