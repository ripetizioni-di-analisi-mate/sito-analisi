A constant magnetic force of $0.20\,\mathrm N$ on a wire lasts $40\,\mathrm{ms}$. What impulse does it deliver to the movable support holding the wire if the other forces balance?

- $8\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $0.008\,\mathrm{N\,s}$

## Explanation

Impulse is the area under the force–time graph. The rectangle has area $0.20\cdot0.040=0.008\,\mathrm{N\,s}$.
