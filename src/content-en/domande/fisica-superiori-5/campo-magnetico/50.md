A wire perpendicular to a field of $4$ T experiences a force of $84$ N on a segment $7$ m long. What current does it carry?

- $12\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $21\,\mathrm{A}$
- $84\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

For a wire perpendicular to the field, $F=ILB$. Divide the force by the product of the wire length and the field magnitude to find the current. This gives $3\,\mathrm{A}$.
