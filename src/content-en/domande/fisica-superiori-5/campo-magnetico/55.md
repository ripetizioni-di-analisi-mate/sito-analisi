A horizontal $12\,\mathrm g$ wire hangs from a support. An upward magnetic force is $0.04\,\mathrm N$. What total tension does the support carry in equilibrium? Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $0.12\,\mathrm N$
- $0.16\,\mathrm N$
- $0.04\,\mathrm N$
- $0.08\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The weight is $0.012\cdot10=0.12\,\mathrm N$. Tension and magnetic force support it together, so $T=0.12-0.04=0.08\,\mathrm N$.
