A coil has $10$ turns, each with an area of $0.02$ square meters, and carries a current of $7$ A. What is the magnitude of its magnetic moment?

- $1.4\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $0.14\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$

## Explanation

For a flat coil, the magnetic moment has magnitude $\mu=NIA$. Multiply the number of turns by the current and the area of a single turn. The result is $\mu=10\cdot7\cdot0.02=1.4$ A·m².
