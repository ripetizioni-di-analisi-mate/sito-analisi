A loop with a magnetic moment of $7$ A·m² is free to rotate in a uniform field of $3$ T. Which orientation gives stable equilibrium?

- The magnetic moment points in the same direction as the field.
- The magnetic moment makes an angle of 45 degrees with the field.
- The magnetic moment is perpendicular to the field.
- The magnetic moment points opposite to the field.

## Explanation

The torque vanishes when the vectors are parallel or antiparallel. The potential energy is lowest when the magnetic moment and the field point in the same direction.
