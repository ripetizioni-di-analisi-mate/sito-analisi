A galvanometer has equally spaced divisions. Which feature explains a deflection scale linear in current?

- A spring with no restoring torque
- A radial field and restoring torque proportional to angle
- Zero field throughout the coil
- Magnetic moment independent of current

## Explanation

In a radial field the driving torque is $NIAB$ at every position. It balances $k\theta$ at equilibrium, so $\theta$ is proportional to $I$.
