A horizontal compass points north in a $30\,\mu\mathrm T$ northward field. A $30\,\mu\mathrm T$ eastward field is added. Where does the needle settle?

- Southeast
- East
- North
- Northeast

## Explanation

The horizontal components are perpendicular and equal. Their resultant therefore makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with each and points northeast.
