A charge of $10$ microcoulombs moves at $1000$ m/s perpendicular to a field of $B=0.5$ T. What is the magnitude of the magnetic force in millinewtons?

- $10\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $0.005\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the Lorentz force is $F=|q|vB$ when the velocity is perpendicular to the field. This gives $F=10\cdot10^{-6}\cdot1000\cdot0.5$ N. Converting to millinewtons gives $5$ mN.
