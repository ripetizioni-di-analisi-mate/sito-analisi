A coil has $40$ identical turns of area $90$ cm² each and carries a current of $5$ A. What is its magnetic moment?

- $18\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $180\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{200}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$

## Explanation

Convert the area from square centimeters to square meters by dividing by ten thousand. The magnetic moment is the product of the number of turns, the current, and the area of one turn. This gives $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$.
