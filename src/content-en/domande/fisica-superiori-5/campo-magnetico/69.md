Two series windings on one frame have 12 turns in one sense and 8 in the other. Every turn has the same area. How many aligned turns would give the same net magnetic moment?

- $4$
- $20$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The two sections have opposite moments. Their sum is $(12-8)IA$, equal to the moment of four aligned turns.
