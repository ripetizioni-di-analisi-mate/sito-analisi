A clamp holds a rotor still under constant magnetic torque. Which quantity can be nonzero even though mechanical work on the rotor is zero?

- The reaction torque from the clamp
- Rotor rotational kinetic energy
- Angular displacement while clamped
- Rotor angular velocity

## Explanation

The clamp balances the magnetic torque. With zero angular displacement the work vanishes even though each torque is nonzero.
