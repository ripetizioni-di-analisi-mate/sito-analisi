A wire on a magnetic balance moves against a spring of stiffness $8\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The new equilibrium is $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the initial one. What is the constant magnetic force magnitude?

- $0.004\,\mathrm N$
- $1.6\,\mathrm N$
- $0.040\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the change in spring force balances the magnetic force. Thus $F=k\Delta x=8\cdot0.005=0.040\,\mathrm N$.
