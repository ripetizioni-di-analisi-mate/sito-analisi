A wire perpendicular to a field of $5$ T experiences a force of $180$ N on a segment $9$ m long. What current does it carry?

- $4\,\mathrm{A}$
- $36\,\mathrm{A}$
- $180\,\mathrm{A}$
- $20\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

For a wire perpendicular to the field, $F=ILB$. Divide the force by the product of the wire length and the field magnitude to find the current. This gives $4\,\mathrm{A}$.
