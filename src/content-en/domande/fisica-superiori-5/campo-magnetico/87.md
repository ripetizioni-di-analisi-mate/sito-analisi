A motor takes in $12\,\mathrm W$ and keeps a load rotating, delivering $9\,\mathrm W$ to it. What fraction of input power is dissipated in the motor?

- $75\%$
- $33\%$
- $100\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

At steady speed the power loss is $12-9=3\,\mathrm W$. Relative to input this is $3/12=0.25$.
