A straight wire segment $9$ m long carries $4$ A along $+x$. The field has components $B_x=4$ T and $B_y=5$ T, with $B_z=0$. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $144\,\mathrm{N}$
- $180\,\mathrm{N}$
- $324\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36 \sqrt{41}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The field component along the wire contributes nothing to the cross product. Multiply the current by the length and the magnitude of the perpendicular component. This gives $180\,\mathrm{N}$.
