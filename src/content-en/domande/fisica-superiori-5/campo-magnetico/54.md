Two segments carrying the same current are perpendicular to a uniform field. One experiences $3\,\mathrm N$ north and the other $4\,\mathrm N$ east. What is the magnitude of the total push on both segments?

- $1\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$
- $12\,\mathrm N$
- $7\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The forces are perpendicular. Their vector sum therefore has magnitude $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\,\mathrm N$.
