A wire $9$ m long carries $4$ A. What is the magnitude of the field perpendicular to the wire if the force is $180$ N?

- $180\,\mathrm{T}$
- $20\,\mathrm{T}$
- $5\,\mathrm{T}$
- $45\,\mathrm{T}$

## Explanation

The field is perpendicular to the wire, so the sine of the angle is one. Divide the force by the product of current and length to find the field magnitude. This gives $5\,\mathrm{T}$.
