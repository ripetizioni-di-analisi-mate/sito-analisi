A small loop carrying constant current has magnetic moment $0.20\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$ initially aligned with a uniform $0.50\,\mathrm T$ field. An operator slowly turns it until the moment and field make an angle of $60^\circ$. How much work does the operator do against the magnetic torque in the absence of friction?

- $-0.050\,\mathrm J$
- $+0.10\,\mathrm J$
- $+0.087\,\mathrm J$
- $+0.050\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Initially $U_i=-\mu B=-0.10\,\mathrm J$, while finally $U_f=-\mu B\cos60^\circ=-0.050\,\mathrm J$. For the slow rotation the external work supplies the increase $U_f-U_i$.
