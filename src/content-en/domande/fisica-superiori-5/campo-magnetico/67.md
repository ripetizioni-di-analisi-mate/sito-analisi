Near a magnet pole, a small loop aligned with the field is drawn toward the pole. Which assumption in the proof of zero net force on a closed loop fails?

- The field is uniform across the loop
- The current has a definite direction
- The loop has opposite sides
- The current follows a closed circuit

## Explanation

Near the pole, field strength can differ across the loop. Forces on opposite segments therefore need not cancel.
