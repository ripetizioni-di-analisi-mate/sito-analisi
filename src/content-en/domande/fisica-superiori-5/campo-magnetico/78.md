A meter movement is rated for $1\,\mathrm{mA}$ and has resistance $90\,\Omega$. A resistor is placed in parallel for a $10\,\mathrm{mA}$ total range. What resistance is required?

- $810\,\Omega$
- $10\,\Omega$
- $90\,\Omega$
- $9\,\Omega$

## Explanation

At full scale the movement has $0.09\,\mathrm V$ across it. The parallel branch carries $9\,\mathrm{mA}$ at this voltage, so $R=0.09/0.009=10\,\Omega$.
