A straight wire segment $5$ m long carries $2$ A along $+x$. The field has components $B_x=2$ T and $B_y=3$ T, with $B_z=0$. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10 \sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The field component along the wire contributes nothing to the cross product. Multiply the current by the length and the magnitude of the perpendicular component. This gives $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
