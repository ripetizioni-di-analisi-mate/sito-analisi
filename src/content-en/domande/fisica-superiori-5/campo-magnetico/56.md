A beam contains neutral atoms and positive ions entering a transverse magnetic field at the same speed. Neglect the magnetic moments of the atoms. Which track identifies the atoms?

- The track with greatest curvature
- The straight track
- The track half as long as the ion track
- The track that always returns to its starting point

## Explanation

The atoms have zero net charge, so $q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B=0$. The ions experience a transverse force.
