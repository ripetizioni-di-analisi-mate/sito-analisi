An electron moves along $+y$ at $7$ m/s in a field of $3$ T directed along $+z$. In which direction does the force initially point?

- Toward $+x$.
- Toward $-x$.
- Toward $-z$.
- Toward $+y$.

## Explanation

The cross product of the velocity and the field points along $+x$. Since the electron has negative charge, the force points in the opposite direction.
