Initially $\mathbf v$ is horizontal and the magnetic force is vertical. To first order over a short interval, which way does the change in momentum point?

- Always opposite the field
- Along the vertical force
- Nowhere because it is zero
- Along the initial velocity

## Explanation

Newton's second law gives $\Delta\mathbf p\simeq\mathbf F\Delta t$. Momentum changes direction even when its magnitude stays constant.
