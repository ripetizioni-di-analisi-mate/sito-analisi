A figure-eight loop has coplanar lobes of areas $3A$ and $A$. Current circulates oppositely in the lobes. What is the loop's magnetic moment magnitude?

- $2IA$
- $4IA$
- $0$
- $3IA$

## Explanation

The lobes have opposite oriented areas. The net moment magnitude is $I(3A-A)=2IA$.
