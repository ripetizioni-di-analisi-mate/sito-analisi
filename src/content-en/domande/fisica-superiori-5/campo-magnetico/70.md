The magnetic moment of a loop has magnitude $5$ A·m² and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with a field of $3$ T. What is the magnitude of the torque?

- $\dfrac{15 \sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of a cross product is the product of the two magnitudes and the sine of the angle between them. The sine of thirty degrees is one half. This gives $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
