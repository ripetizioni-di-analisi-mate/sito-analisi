A loop has a magnetic moment of magnitude $2\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}$, and its normal is perpendicular to a field of $B=0.2$ T. What is the magnitude of the torque?

- $2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The torque has magnitude $\tau=\mu B\sin\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the normal and the field. Here $\theta=90^\circ$. The result is therefore $\tau=2\cdot0.2=0.4$ N·m.
