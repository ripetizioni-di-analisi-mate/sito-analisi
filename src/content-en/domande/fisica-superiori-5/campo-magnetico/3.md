A wire segment of length $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ carrying a current $I = 2.0\,\mathrm{A}$ experiences a maximum force of $45\,\mathrm{mN}$ when it is perpendicular to the magnetic field. Determine the field magnitude.

- $1.5\,\mathrm T$
- $0.015\,\mathrm T$
- $0.15\,\mathrm T$
- $0.30\,\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The maximum identifies the perpendicular orientation. Convert millinewtons to newtons and centimetres to metres to use the wire as a field probe. $B = \dfrac{F_{\max}}{I \ell} = 0.15\,\mathrm{T}$.
