Two drawn field lines cross where a probe measures a nonzero field. What does the crossing establish?

- The drawing assigns two directions to one vector
- Two equal forces must act there
- The field must be vertical
- The probe measures flux through the entire page

## Explanation

At a point with nonzero field the line is tangent to a single vector. Two different tangents at that point make the drawing inconsistent.
