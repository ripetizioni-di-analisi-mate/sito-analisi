A charge of magnitude $2$ µC enters a field of $3$ T at right angles with a speed of $5000$ m/s. What is the magnitude of the force in millinewtons?

- $30\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\dfrac{3}{100}\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

Multiplying microcoulombs by thousands of meters per second introduces a factor of $10^{-3}$ in the force expressed in newtons. Since the vectors are perpendicular, the sine is one. This gives $30\,\mathrm{mN}$.
