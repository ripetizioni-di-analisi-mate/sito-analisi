An electrolyte conducts through positive ions moving right and negative ions moving left. In a field out of the page, how do their magnetic force directions compare?

- Both down the page
- Opposite directions in the plane
- Both out of the page
- Both up the page

## Explanation

For positive ions, right crossed with out points down. For negative ions both charge and velocity change sign, giving the same force direction.
