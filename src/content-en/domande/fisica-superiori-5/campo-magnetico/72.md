The magnetic moment of a loop has magnitude $9$ A·m² and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with a field of $5$ T. What is the magnitude of the torque?

- $45\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{45 \sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{45}{2}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of a cross product is the product of the two magnitudes and the sine of the angle between them. The sine of thirty degrees is one half. This gives $\dfrac{45}{2}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
