A motor delivers an instantaneous torque of $0.12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ at $50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What mechanical power is delivered at that instant if torque and rotation have the same sense?

- $6\,\mathrm W$
- $60\,\mathrm W$
- $0.0024\,\mathrm W$
- $12\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Rotational power is $P=M\omega$. Torque and angular velocity point the same way, so $P=0.12\cdot50=6\,\mathrm W$.
