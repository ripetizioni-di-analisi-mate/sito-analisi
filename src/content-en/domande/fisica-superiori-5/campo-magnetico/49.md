Two identical parallel branches share a total current of $6\,\mathrm A$. Only a $0.20\,\mathrm m$ segment of one branch is immersed perpendicular to a $0.50\,\mathrm T$ field. What is the force magnitude on that segment?

- $0.60\,\mathrm N$
- $0.15\,\mathrm N$
- $1.2\,\mathrm N$
- $0.30\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Equal branch resistances and voltages give $3\,\mathrm A$ in each branch. The immersed segment experiences $F=3\cdot0.20\cdot0.50=0.30\,\mathrm N$.
