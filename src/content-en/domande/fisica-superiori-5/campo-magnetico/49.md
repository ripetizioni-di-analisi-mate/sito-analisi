A wire perpendicular to a field of $3$ T experiences a force of $30$ N on a segment $5$ m long. What current does it carry?

- $10\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $6\,\mathrm{A}$
- $30\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

For a wire perpendicular to the field, $F=ILB$. Divide the force by the product of the wire length and the field magnitude to find the current. This gives $2\,\mathrm{A}$.
