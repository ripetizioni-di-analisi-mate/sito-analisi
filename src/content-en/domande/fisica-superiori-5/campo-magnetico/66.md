A charge of magnitude $4$ µC enters a field of $5$ T at right angles with a speed of $9000$ m/s. What is the magnitude of the force in millinewtons?

- $180\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $\dfrac{9}{50}\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $180000\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

Multiplying microcoulombs by thousands of meters per second introduces a factor of $10^{-3}$ in the force expressed in newtons. Since the vectors are perpendicular, the sine is one. This gives $180\,\mathrm{mN}$.
