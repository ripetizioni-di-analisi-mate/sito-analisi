A magnetic field turns a charge through a quarter-circle. If its momentum magnitude is $p$, what is the magnitude of the total change in momentum?

- $0$
- $p$
- $\sqrt{2}\,p$
- $2p$

## Explanation

Initial and final momenta have equal magnitude and are perpendicular. Their vector difference is the diagonal of a square of side $p$.
