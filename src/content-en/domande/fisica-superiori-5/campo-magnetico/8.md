A proton accelerated from rest through $2.0\,\mathrm{kV}$ enters a uniform magnetic field at $6.2\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to the field. Only the magnetic force acts until it leaves the field. What is its exit speed?

- $1.24\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.1\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.2\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The potential difference supplied kinetic energy before entry. In the magnetic region the force is perpendicular to each instantaneous displacement. It does zero work and kinetic energy is conserved.
