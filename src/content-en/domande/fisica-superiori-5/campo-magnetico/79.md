A $100\,\Omega$ meter movement reaches full scale at $1\,\mathrm{mA}$. What series resistance allows it to read up to $1\,\mathrm V$?

- $900\,\Omega$
- $1000\,\Omega$
- $10\,\Omega$
- $100\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Total resistance must be $1/0.001=1000\,\Omega$. The coil already contributes $100\,\Omega$, leaving $900\,\Omega$ in series.
