A particle moving at $5$ m/s enters a region where only a static magnetic field acts on it. What is its speed in meters per second after $2$ s if it remains in the region?

- $2$
- $10$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

The magnetic force is always perpendicular to the velocity, so the power it delivers is zero. The kinetic energy remains constant, and so does the speed. This gives $5$.
