A charge of $6$ microcoulombs moves at $1000$ m/s perpendicular to a field of $B=0.5$ T. What is the magnitude of the magnetic force in millinewtons?

- $6\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mN}$
- $0.003\,\mathrm{mN}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the Lorentz force is $F=|q|vB$ when the velocity is perpendicular to the field. This gives $F=6\cdot10^{-6}\cdot1000\cdot0.5$ N. Converting to millinewtons gives $3$ mN.
