Which pair of measurements can distinguish a uniform electric field from a purely magnetic field using a positive test charge?

- Measure the force for opposite velocities at the same point
- Only count the drawn field lines
- Measure the charge's weight twice
- Measure the container volume

## Explanation

Electric force $q\mathbf E$ keeps its direction. Magnetic force reverses with velocity if the transverse component is nonzero.
