A circular loop lies in the plane of the page and carries a current that flows clockwise as seen by someone looking at the page. What is the direction of its magnetic moment?

- In the plane of the page, toward the center of the loop
- Out of the page
- Into the page
- In the plane of the page, tangent to the loop

## Explanation

The magnetic moment is $\vec{\mu} = I A\,\hat{n}$, with the unit vector $\hat{n}$ perpendicular to the plane of the loop, so $\vec{\mu}$ is perpendicular to the page.

The direction of $\hat{n}$ is found with the right-hand rule, curling the fingers in the direction of the current. With the fingers turning clockwise as seen by the viewer, the thumb points away from the viewer. Therefore the magnetic moment points **into the page**.
