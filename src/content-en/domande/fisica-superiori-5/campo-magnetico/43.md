A magnetometer reads $+52\,\mu\mathrm T$ with a coil energized and $-28\,\mu\mathrm T$ after current reversal. What is the background field along the probe axis?

- $+12\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $+40\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $+24\,\mu\mathrm T$
- $-12\,\mu\mathrm T$

## Explanation

The readings are $B_0+b$ and $B_0-b$. Their mean cancels the coil field and yields $B_0=(52-28)/2=12\,\mu\mathrm T$.
