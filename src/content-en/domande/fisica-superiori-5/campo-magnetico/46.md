A straight wire crosses two successive regions. Over $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ the field is $+B$ and over $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ it is $-B$, always transverse. What length in the $+B$ field alone would give the same total force?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The forces point in opposite directions and are proportional to the immersed lengths. The net force equals that on $3-1=2\,\mathrm{cm}$ in the first field.
