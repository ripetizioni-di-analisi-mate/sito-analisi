A wire segment $50$ cm long carries $2$ A and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with a uniform field of $3$ T. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $\dfrac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force has magnitude $ILB\sin\theta$. Express the length in meters and use the sine of thirty degrees, which is one half. This gives $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$.
