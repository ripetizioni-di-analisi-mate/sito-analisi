At what nonzero speed is classical kinetic energy $90\%$ of the relativistic value? You may use $\dfrac{K_{\rm cl}}{K_{\rm rel}}=\dfrac{\gamma+1}{2\gamma^2}$ to compare them.

- $0.100c$
- $0.363c$
- $0.900c$
- $0.436c$

## Explanation

Set $\dfrac{\gamma+1}{2\gamma^2}=0.90$, giving $1.8\gamma^2-\gamma-1=0$. The physical root is $\gamma\simeq1.073$, hence $\dfrac{v}{c}=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{1}{\gamma^2}}\simeq0.363$.
