A particle has a total energy of $15$ MeV and $pc=9$ MeV. What is its rest energy?

- $12\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $6\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $24\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $144\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum relation is $E^2=(pc)^2+E_0^2$. Solving gives $E_0=\sqrt{E^2-(pc)^2}$. Hence $E_0=\sqrt{(15)^2-(9)^2}=12$ MeV.
