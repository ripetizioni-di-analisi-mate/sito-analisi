The sum of the masses of the free nucleons exceeds the mass of the nucleus by $3/100$ atomic mass units. What is the binding energy in MeV if $1\,\mathrm u\,c^2=930$ MeV?

- $\dfrac{279}{100}$
- $\dfrac{279}{10}$
- $31$
- $2790$

## Explanation

The binding energy is the mass defect multiplied by $c^2$. Using the conversion factor given for each atomic mass unit gives $\dfrac{279}{10}$.
