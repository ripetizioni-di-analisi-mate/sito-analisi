A free neutron moves through the lab. Which measurement, together with its total energy, permits its mass to be reconstructed from the invariant without measuring an electric field?

- The detector's model name.
- Its zero charge alone.
- Its momentum magnitude inferred from recoil balance.
- Its direction of travel alone.

## Explanation

Determining $mc^2=\sqrt{E^2-p^2c^2}$ requires the magnitude of $p$. In a fully reconstructed collision the other bodies' recoil permits that measurement even for a neutral particle.
