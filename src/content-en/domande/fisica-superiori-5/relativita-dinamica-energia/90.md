An isolated particle is first described in SI and then in units with $c=1$. A table writes $E^2=p^2+m^2$. How is the dimensionally explicit formula restored?

- $E^2=\dfrac{p^2}{c^2}+m^2c^2$
- $E^2=(p+mc)^2$
- $E^2=p^2c^4+m^2c^2$
- $E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4$

## Explanation

To obtain energies, multiply momentum by $c$ and mass by $c^2$. The invariant adds the squares of the two resulting quantities.
