Two objects of equal rest mass have Lorentz factors $4$ and $13$. What is the ratio of their total energies, second to first?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{13}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{13}$

## Explanation

Their rest energies are identical. This common factor cancels in the ratio of their total energies, leaving the ratio of the two Lorentz factors. This gives $\dfrac{13}{4}$.
