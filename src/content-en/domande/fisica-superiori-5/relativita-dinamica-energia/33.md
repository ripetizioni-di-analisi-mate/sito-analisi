A stationary cavity contains two oppositely directed light pulses of $0.45\,\mathrm J$ each. By how much does its mass exceed that of the empty cavity, with no other changes? Use $c^2=9\cdot10^{16}\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$.

- $10^{-14}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\cdot10^{-18}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $10^{-17}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The pulses have zero total momentum but increase internal energy by $0.90\,\mathrm J$. In the rest frame mass increases by $\dfrac{0.90}{c^2}$.
