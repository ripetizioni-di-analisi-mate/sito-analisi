A table of neutral atomic masses is used to calculate nuclear binding energy for atomic number $Z$. Which mass should be used for each of its $Z$ protons in the balance with neutrons, neglecting electronic binding?

- The sum of proton and positron masses minus all final electron masses twice.
- The neutral hydrogen atom mass.
- The bare proton mass with no electron correction.
- The free neutron mass.

## Explanation

Each hydrogen mass includes one electron. The $Z$ initial electrons correspond to the $Z$ electrons included in the final atomic mass and cancel in the balance.
