A particle has a rest energy of $9$ MeV and a total energy of $36$ MeV. What is its kinetic energy in MeV?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $45$
- $36$
- $27$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy is the amount by which the total energy exceeds the rest energy. Subtracting the rest energy from the total gives $27$.
