An electron hits a screen with **total** energy $1\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and is stopped. Its rest energy is $0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$. How much can it deposit as kinetic energy?

- $0.489\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

Stopping the electron leaves its rest energy in the final state. Its transferable kinetic energy is $1-0.511=0.489\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
