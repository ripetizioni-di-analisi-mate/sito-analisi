A particle and its antiparticle, each with rest energy $9$ MeV, annihilate at rest into two photons. What is the energy of each photon in MeV?

- $9$
- $18$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The initial momentum is zero. The two photons travel in opposite directions with momenta of equal magnitude, so they share the total energy equally, giving $9$.
