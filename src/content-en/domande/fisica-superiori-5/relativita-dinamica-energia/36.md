A body at rest emits two equal light pulses in opposite directions. Which combination of changes respects the conservation laws?

- The body must recoil towards one pulse.
- The emitted energy is zero because the momenta cancel.
- The body remains at rest and its mass decreases.
- The body stays at rest and its mass increases.

## Explanation

The pulses have opposite momenta and require no recoil. Their energies are both positive and must come from the body's internal energy.
