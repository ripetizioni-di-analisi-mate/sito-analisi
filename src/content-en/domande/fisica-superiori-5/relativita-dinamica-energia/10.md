The formation of a nucleus results in a mass defect of $3$ atomic mass units. Taking $1\,\mathrm u\,c^2=931$ MeV, what is the binding energy?

- $\dfrac{931}{3}\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $2793\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $\dfrac{3}{931}\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $934\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The binding energy is the energy equivalent of the mass defect, $E_b=\Delta m c^2$. Each atomic mass unit corresponds to $931$ MeV. The energy is therefore $2793$ MeV.
