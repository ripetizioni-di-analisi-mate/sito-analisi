A proton and an antiproton, both at rest and each with rest energy $938\,\mathrm{MeV}$, annihilate into two photons. What energy does each photon carry?

- $938\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $469\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1876\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

Initial momentum is zero. The photons therefore have equal and opposite momenta and, by $E=pc$, equal energies. They share the initial $1876\,\mathrm{MeV}$ equally.
