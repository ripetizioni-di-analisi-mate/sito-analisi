Two photons, each of energy $7$ MeV, travel in opposite directions. What is the magnitude of their total momentum in MeV/$c$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

The photons have momenta of equal magnitude because their energies are equal. Momentum adds as a vector, so the two opposite contributions cancel, giving $0$.
