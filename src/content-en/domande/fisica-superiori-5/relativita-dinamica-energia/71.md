An electron's total energy is to double from $4mc^2$ to $8mc^2$. What external work is required, neglecting losses?

- $3mc^2$
- $7mc^2$
- $8mc^2$
- $4mc^2$

## Explanation

Work is the change in kinetic energy. Rest mass is unchanged, so this is the difference between total energies $8mc^2-4mc^2$.
