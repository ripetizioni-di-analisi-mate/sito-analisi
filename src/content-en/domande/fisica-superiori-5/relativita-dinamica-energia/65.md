A particle has $pc=21$ MeV and a total energy of $35$ MeV. What is its speed in units of $c$?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

The ratio of $p=\gamma mv$ to $E=\gamma mc^2$ gives $v/c=pc/E$. The Lorentz factor cancels, giving $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
