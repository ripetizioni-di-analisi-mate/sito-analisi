Light passes near a massive star and is measured by local freely falling observers. Which pair of statements agrees with general relativity?

- Its local speed is $c$; its overall path can be deflected.
- Only light of nonzero rest mass is deflected.
- Its local speed must exceed $c$ near the star.
- Light must stop to bend.

## Explanation

Local measurements preserve light speed. Spacetime geometry connects those measurements over extended regions and permits deflected light paths.
