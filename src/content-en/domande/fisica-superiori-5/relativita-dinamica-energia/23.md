A constant force along the motion acts on one electron during equal lab-time intervals. Which increment stays the same in every interval?

- The velocity increment.
- A nonzero increase in rest mass.
- The momentum increment.
- The distance covered per interval.

## Explanation

Relativistic dynamics gives $F=\dfrac{dp}{dt}$, so $\Delta p=F\Delta t$. The relation between $p$ and $v$ is nonlinear, so the speed gain gets smaller at high speed.
