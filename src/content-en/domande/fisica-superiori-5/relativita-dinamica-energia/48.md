A particle has a rest energy of $9$ MeV and a Lorentz factor of $4$. What is its total energy in MeV?

- $36$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $9$
- $27$

## Explanation

Total energy is the product of the Lorentz factor and the rest energy. The rest energy is part of the total. The result is $36$.
