Two photons collide head-on with energy $0.40\,\mathrm{MeV}$ each. Can they create an electron–positron pair if $m_ec^2=0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$?

- Yes, because their momenta cancel.
- No, their total energy is below $1.022\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
- Yes, because there are two photons.
- No, each particle must be neutral.

## Explanation

Total momentum is zero, yet only $0.80\,\mathrm{MeV}$ is available in the centre-of-momentum frame. Even a pair at rest requires two rest energies of $0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
