A carbon-12 nucleus has total binding energy $92.2\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Which quantity permits comparison with nuclei containing different numbers of nucleons?

- $\dfrac{92.2}{12}\simeq7.68\,\mathrm{MeV}$ per nucleon.
- $92.2\cdot12\simeq1106\,\mathrm{MeV}$ per nucleon.
- $92.2\,\mathrm{MeV}$ per nucleon.
- $\dfrac{92.2}{6}\simeq15.4\,\mathrm{MeV}$ per nucleon.

## Explanation

Carbon-12 has twelve nucleons, including both protons and neutrons. Dividing by twelve gives the mean binding per constituent.
