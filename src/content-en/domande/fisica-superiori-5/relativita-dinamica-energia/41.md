Two identical bodies collide head-on at equal and opposite speeds and stick together. Each had kinetic energy $K$. What becomes of that contribution in the centre-of-momentum frame?

- It halves each original rest mass.
- It vanishes because the motions are opposite.
- It increases the final body's internal energy by $2K$ if no radiation escapes.
- It becomes final translational kinetic energy $2K$.

## Explanation

The total momentum is zero in this frame, so the composite stays at rest. The initial kinetic energy is retained in it as internal energy.
