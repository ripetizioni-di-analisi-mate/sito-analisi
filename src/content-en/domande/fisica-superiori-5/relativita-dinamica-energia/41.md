A particle has a rest energy of $1000$ MeV and travels at $0.6c$. What is its total energy?

- $1250\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $250\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $800\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

At $0.6c$ the Lorentz factor is $\dfrac{5}{4}$. The total energy is $E=\gamma E_0$, giving $E=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot1000=1250$ MeV.
