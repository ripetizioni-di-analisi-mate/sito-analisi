A spring in a box is compressed, and the final assembly is held at rest. With no losses, how does the system rest mass change when external work $W$ is supplied?

- It increases by $\dfrac{W}{c}$.
- It stays fixed because the box is at rest.
- It increases by $\dfrac{W}{c^2}$.
- It decreases by $\dfrac{W}{c^2}$.

## Explanation

The work is stored as internal elastic energy. Since total energy in the rest frame is $Mc^2$, the system's rest mass increases.
