Two objects of equal rest mass have Lorentz factors $2$ and $7$. What is the ratio of their total energies, second to first?

- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$

## Explanation

Their rest energies are identical. This common factor cancels in the ratio of their total energies, leaving the ratio of the two Lorentz factors. This gives $\dfrac{7}{2}$.
