A satellite is at a higher gravitational potential than the ground and also has considerable speed. Which two measurements determine the sign of the clock-rate difference in the weak-field limit?

- Only the mass of the individual clock.
- The gravitational potential difference and the difference of squared speeds in a common frame.
- Only the colours of the signals emitted on Earth.
- Only the sign of the satellite charge.

## Explanation

To first order the gravitational term is $\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{c^2}$ and the kinematic term is $-\dfrac{\Delta(v^2)}{2c^2}$. They can oppose each other, so both must be evaluated.
