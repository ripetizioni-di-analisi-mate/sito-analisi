A particle has a total energy of $35$ MeV and $pc=21$ MeV. What is its rest energy?

- $28\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $14\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $56\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $784\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum relation is $E^2=(pc)^2+E_0^2$. Solving gives $E_0=\sqrt{E^2-(pc)^2}$. Hence $E_0=\sqrt{(35)^2-(21)^2}=28$ MeV.
