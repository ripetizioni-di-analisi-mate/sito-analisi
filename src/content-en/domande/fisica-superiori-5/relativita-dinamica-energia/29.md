A proton and an electron have equal nonzero momentum. Which has greater kinetic energy?

- The electron.
- Neither has any kinetic energy.
- Their kinetic energies are always equal.
- The proton.

## Explanation

Writing $K=\dfrac{p^2c^2}{E+mc^2}$ gives the same numerator. At fixed $p$ the smaller mass gives a smaller denominator and hence larger $K$.
