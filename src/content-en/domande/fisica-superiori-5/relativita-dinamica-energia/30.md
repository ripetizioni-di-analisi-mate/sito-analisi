A proton and an alpha particle follow circles of equal radius in the same uniform magnetic field, with velocity perpendicular to the field. The alpha-particle charge is $2e$. How do their momentum magnitudes compare?

- $p_\alpha=\dfrac{p_p}{2}$
- $p_\alpha=4p_p$
- $p_\alpha=p_p$
- $p_\alpha=2p_p$

## Explanation

Track curvature measures the magnetic rigidity $\dfrac{p}{q}=Br$. At equal rigidity twice the charge implies twice the momentum $p$.
