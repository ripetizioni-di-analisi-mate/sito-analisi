In a hypothetical example, a nucleus with $A=8$ has mean binding $7\,\mathrm{MeV}$ per nucleon. It separates into two nuclei each with $A=4$ and mean binding $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$ per nucleon. Ignoring other products, what is the energy balance?

- It releases $1\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
- It requires $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
- No energy is exchanged.
- It releases $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$.

## Explanation

Binding rises from $8\cdot7=56$ to $2\cdot4\cdot8=64\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Greater final binding means smaller final rest energy by $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
