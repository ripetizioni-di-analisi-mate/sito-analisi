A resistor and battery are inside a perfectly isolated box. The battery discharges, heating the resistor. How does the rest mass of the complete box change?

- It vanishes when the battery is depleted.
- It increases by twice the mass equivalent of the heat.
- It stays the same.
- It decreases by the mass equivalent of all heat produced.

## Explanation

Chemical energy becomes internal energy but none leaves the isolated system. Total rest-frame energy and therefore the total mass stay constant.
