Two photons with energies $7$ MeV and $3$ MeV travel in opposite directions. What is the magnitude of their total momentum in MeV/$c$?

- $10$
- $21$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

For each photon the magnitude of the momentum is its energy divided by $c$. The momentum vectors point in opposite directions, so subtract their magnitudes. This gives $4$.
