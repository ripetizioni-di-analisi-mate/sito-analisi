In a sample each particle obeys $E=mc^2+K$. Two inertial observers moving relative to one another assign different $K$ values. Under what condition would both assign $K=0$ to the same massive particle?

- Neutral charge is sufficient.
- Setting their clocks to the same zero is sufficient.
- Changing the mass unit is sufficient.
- Both would have to be comoving with the particle, impossible if they move relative to one another.

## Explanation

For a massive particle $K=0$ implies $\gamma=1$ and therefore zero speed. Relatively moving frames cannot both assign it zero speed.
