A photon has an energy of $5$ MeV. What is the magnitude of its momentum in MeV/$c$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

For a massless particle, the energy–momentum relation reduces to $E=pc$. The numerical value of the momentum in MeV divided by $c$ equals that of the energy in MeV, giving $5$.
