For $p>0$, which line does the energy–momentum curve $E(p)$ of a massive particle approach when $pc\gg mc^2$?

- $E=\dfrac{p^2}{2m}$
- $E=2pc$
- $E=pc$
- $E=mc^2$

## Explanation

Write $E=pc\sqrt{1+(\dfrac{mc}{p})^2}$. At high momentum the added term under the root tends to zero and the curve approaches the line.
