A proton turns through $180^\circ$ in a lossless magnetic ring. It enters with $p_x=+4\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$. What is the net impulse along $x$?

- $+8\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$
- $-8\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$
- $-4\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$
- $0$

## Explanation

The magnitude of $p$ stays constant but its component changes from $+4$ to $-4$. The impulse is final minus initial momentum, $-8\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$.
