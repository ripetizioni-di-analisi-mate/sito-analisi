The sum of the masses of the free nucleons exceeds the mass of the nucleus by $4/100$ atomic mass units. What is the binding energy in MeV if $1\,\mathrm u\,c^2=930$ MeV?

- $3720$
- $\dfrac{93}{4}$
- $\dfrac{93}{25}$
- $\dfrac{186}{5}$

## Explanation

The binding energy is the mass defect multiplied by $c^2$. Using the conversion factor given for each atomic mass unit gives $\dfrac{186}{5}$.
