An atom absorbs radiation while a support takes up recoil. Why can a free stationary atom not simply absorb a single photon and remain stationary?

- Atoms cannot gain internal energy.
- The incident photon carries nonzero momentum.
- Every photon is electrically charged.
- The photon has no energy.

## Explanation

Before absorption the total momentum equals the photon's momentum. Without a support or another product the final atom has to carry it.
