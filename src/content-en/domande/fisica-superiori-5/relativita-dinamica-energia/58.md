An element has mass defect $0.020\,\mathrm u$. It is mistakenly transcribed as $20\,\mathrm u$. By what factor is the calculated binding energy wrong?

- $100$ too large.
- $1000$ too small.
- $1000$ too large.
- $10$ too large.

## Explanation

With fixed units the energy is proportional to mass defect. The ratio of the transcribed to the correct datum is $\dfrac{20}{0.020}=1000$.
