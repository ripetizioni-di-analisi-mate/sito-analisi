A particle has a rest energy of $15$ MeV and momentum $20$ MeV/$c$. What is its total energy in MeV?

- $25$
- $20$
- $5$
- $35$

## Explanation

The square of the total energy is the sum of the squares of the rest energy and $pc$. Taking the positive square root gives the total energy, $25$.
