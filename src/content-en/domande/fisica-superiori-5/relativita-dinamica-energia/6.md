The fuel in a power plant releases an energy of $3.0 \cdot 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$ per second. Calculate the decrease in fuel mass during one day of operation. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $2.9\,\mathrm{mg}$
- $33\,\mathrm{ng}$
- $2.9\,\mathrm g$
- $86\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

One day lasts $86400\,\mathrm s$. The emitted energy is therefore $P\Delta t=2.592\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm J$. Dividing by $c^2$ gives a mass decrease of $2.88\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
