A nuclide has four protons and five neutrons. Its nuclear mass is compared with nine free protons to estimate the binding. What correction is required?

- Ignore composition because all nucleons have exactly the same mass.
- Divide the nuclear mass by atomic number alone.
- Use four proton masses and five neutron masses.
- Use nine electron masses.

## Explanation

The mass-defect reference must contain the same free constituents. Proton and neutron masses are close but differ on the relevant energy scale.
