A beam with momentum $p$ receives external impulse $-\dfrac{p}{2}$ along its path. Which final quantity can be found without knowing particle mass?

- Momentum $\dfrac{p}{2}$.
- Kinetic energy $\dfrac{K}{2}$ in every regime.
- Speed $\dfrac{v}{2}$ in every regime.
- The halved rest mass.

## Explanation

Impulse is the change in momentum itself. Converting final momentum to speed or kinetic energy requires the relativistic relation involving mass.
