A particle has a total energy of $30$ MeV and $pc=18$ MeV. What is its rest energy?

- $12\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $48\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $576\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $24\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum relation is $E^2=(pc)^2+E_0^2$. Solving gives $E_0=\sqrt{E^2-(pc)^2}$. Hence $E_0=\sqrt{(30)^2-(18)^2}=24$ MeV.
