A mass defect of $0.02$ atomic mass units corresponds to $18.6$ MeV. What mass defect, in atomic mass units, corresponds to $46.5$ MeV?

- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}$

## Explanation

Binding energy is proportional to mass defect. The second energy is two and a half times the first, so the mass defect increases by the same factor, giving $\dfrac{1}{20}$.
