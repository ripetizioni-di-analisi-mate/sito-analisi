Before a reaction total energy is $20\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and total momentum is zero. The visible products have $15\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and total momentum magnitude $3\,\mathrm{MeV}/c$. If exactly one particle is missing, what is its rest energy?

- $2\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $4\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $5\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The missing particle carries $20-15=5\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and momentum opposite to the visible momentum. Its invariant gives $mc^2=\sqrt{5^2-3^2}=4\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
