Orbital and ground clocks have different fractional rates. For the satellite gravity contributes $+5\cdot10^{-10}$ and motion contributes $-1\cdot10^{-10}$ relative to the ground clock. What uncompensated net effect is observed to first order?

- The satellite gains $6\cdot10^{-10}$ seconds per second.
- The effects always cancel.
- The satellite gains $4\cdot10^{-10}$ seconds per second.
- The satellite loses $6\cdot10^{-10}$ seconds per second.

## Explanation

To first order the two signed corrections add. The gravitational term dominates and the result is positive.
