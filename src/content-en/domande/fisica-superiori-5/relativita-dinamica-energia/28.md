Two particles with charges $+e$ and $+2e$ start at rest and cross the same accelerating potential difference. Ignoring losses, what is the ratio of their final kinetic energies, second to first?

- $\sqrt2$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Electric work is $q\Delta V$ even at relativistic speeds. The ratio therefore depends on the charges and does not require the masses.
