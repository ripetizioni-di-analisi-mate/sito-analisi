A particle has a rest energy of $700$ MeV and a speed of $0.6c$. What is the product $pc$?

- $420\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $875\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $175\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $525\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The relativistic momentum is $p=\gamma mv$. Multiplying by $c$ gives $pc=\gamma\dfrac{v}{c}mc^2$. Hence $pc=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot700=525$ MeV.
