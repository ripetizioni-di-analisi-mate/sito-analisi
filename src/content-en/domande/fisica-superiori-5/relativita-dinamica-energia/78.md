Two beams form a system with $E_{\rm tot}=12\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $p_{\rm tot}c=3\,\mathrm{GeV}$ to the right. In which direction and at what speed does its centre of momentum move?

- Right at $4c$.
- It remains at rest.
- Left at $0.25c$.
- Right at $0.25c$.

## Explanation

For the whole system $\dfrac{v_{\rm CM}}{c}=\dfrac{p_{\rm tot}c}{E_{\rm tot}}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{12}$ and the direction is that of the total momentum.
