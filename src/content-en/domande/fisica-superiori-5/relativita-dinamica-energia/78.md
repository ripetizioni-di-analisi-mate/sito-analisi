Two photons, each with energy $9$ MeV, collide head-on. What is the total energy in the center-of-mass frame, in MeV?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $18$
- $9$

## Explanation

The opposing momenta cancel. The given frame is therefore already the center-of-mass frame, so adding the two photon energies gives $18$.
