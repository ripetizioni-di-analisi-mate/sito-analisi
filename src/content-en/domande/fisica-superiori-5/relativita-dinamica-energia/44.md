A neutral particle disappears in a chamber, leaving two charged tracks. The parent was at rest, but the two measured momentum vectors are not opposite. What explanation is compatible with conservation?

- The two products necessarily violate energy conservation.
- A neutral parent implies zero energies.
- Adding momentum magnitudes is sufficient.
- An unobserved product carries the missing momentum.

## Explanation

If the measurements are correct, the observed vectors have a nonzero sum. An additional product must carry the vector opposite to that sum.
