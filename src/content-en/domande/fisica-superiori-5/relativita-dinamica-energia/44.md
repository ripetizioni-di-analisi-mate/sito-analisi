A particle has a total energy of $50$ MeV and $pc=30$ MeV. What is its rest energy?

- $20\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum relation is $E^2=(pc)^2+E_0^2$. Solving gives $E_0=\sqrt{E^2-(pc)^2}$. Hence $E_0=\sqrt{(50)^2-(30)^2}=40$ MeV.
