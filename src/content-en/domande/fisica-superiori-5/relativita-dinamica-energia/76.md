Two photons, each with energy $5$ MeV, collide head-on. What is the total energy in the center-of-mass frame, in MeV?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $10$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

The opposing momenta cancel. The given frame is therefore already the center-of-mass frame, so adding the two photon energies gives $10$.
