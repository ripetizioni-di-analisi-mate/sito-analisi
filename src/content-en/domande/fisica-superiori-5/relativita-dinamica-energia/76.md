In the lab, a force of $2\,\mathrm N$ is measured on a particle travelling at $v=0.6c$ in that frame. The force points in the direction of motion. What instantaneous power does it transfer to the particle, with $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $1.8\cdot10^8\,\mathrm W$
- $1.2\,\mathrm W$
- $6.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm W$
- $3.6\cdot10^8\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The elementary work is $dW=\vec F\cdot d\vec x$. Per unit time this gives $P=\vec F\cdot\vec v=2\cdot0.6c$.
