A spreadsheet evaluates $K=E-mc^2$ at very low speed by subtracting nearly equal numbers. Which equivalent form is more stable under rounding?

- $K=\dfrac{p^2c^2}{E-mc^2}$
- $K=\dfrac{p^2c^2}{E+mc^2}$
- $K=E^2-m^2c^4$
- $K=\dfrac{E+mc^2}{p^2c^2}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the difference by its conjugate sum gives $(E-mc^2)(E+mc^2)=p^2c^2$. This avoids the numerically delicate subtraction.
