A photon has an energy of $9$ MeV. What is the magnitude of its momentum in MeV/$c$?

- $9$
- $18$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$

## Explanation

For a massless particle, the energy–momentum relation reduces to $E=pc$. The numerical value of the momentum in MeV divided by $c$ equals that of the energy in MeV, giving $9$.
