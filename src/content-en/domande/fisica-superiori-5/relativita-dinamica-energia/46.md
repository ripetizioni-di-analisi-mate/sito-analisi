Some particles transform into others in an isolated collision. Which statement describes conservation without confusing rest mass and energy?

- The sum of momentum magnitudes is always conserved.
- Total energy and momentum are conserved; the sum of individual rest masses can change.
- Every particle retains its identity.
- Kinetic energy alone is always conserved.

## Explanation

The invariant mass of the whole system follows from total energy and momentum. Energy can be distributed differently between the individual products' rest and kinetic energies.
