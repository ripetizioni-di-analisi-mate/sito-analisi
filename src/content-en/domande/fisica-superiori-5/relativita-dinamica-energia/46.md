A particle has a rest energy of $5$ MeV and a Lorentz factor of $2$. What is its total energy in MeV?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $-10$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

Total energy is the product of the Lorentz factor and the rest energy. The rest energy is part of the total. The result is $10$.
