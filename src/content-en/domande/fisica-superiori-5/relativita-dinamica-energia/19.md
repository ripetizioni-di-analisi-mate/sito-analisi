A particle has a total energy of $25$ MeV and $pc=15$ MeV. What is its rest energy?

- $10\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $400\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum relation is $E^2=(pc)^2+E_0^2$. Solving gives $E_0=\sqrt{E^2-(pc)^2}$. Hence $E_0=\sqrt{(25)^2-(15)^2}=20$ MeV.
