A particle of rest mass $m$ moves left. With the lab's positive axis pointing right, what are the signs of $(p_x,E)$?

- $(-,-)$
- $(+,-)$
- $(+,+)$
- $(-,+)$

## Explanation

Momentum follows velocity, whereas the total energy $\gamma mc^2$ of an ordinary particle is positive.
