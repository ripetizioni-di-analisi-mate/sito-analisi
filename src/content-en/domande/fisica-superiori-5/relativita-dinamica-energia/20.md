The formation of a nucleus results in a mass defect of $5$ atomic mass units. Taking $1\,\mathrm u\,c^2=931$ MeV, what is the binding energy?

- $186.2\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $\dfrac{5}{931}\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $936\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $4655\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The binding energy is the energy equivalent of the mass defect, $E_b=\Delta m c^2$. Each atomic mass unit corresponds to $931$ MeV. The energy is therefore $4655$ MeV.
