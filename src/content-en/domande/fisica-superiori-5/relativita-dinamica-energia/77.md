A longitudinal accelerator force obeys $F=\gamma^3ma$. With the same force and mass, what fraction of the low-speed acceleration remains at $\gamma=2$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

At low speed $a_0=\dfrac{F}{m}$. For the same force $\dfrac{a}{a_0}=\dfrac{1}{\gamma^3}=\dfrac{1}{8}$.
