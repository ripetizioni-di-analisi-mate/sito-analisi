Two stationary clocks at different heights in Earth's weak field are compared. Which sign of $\Delta\Phi=\Phi_{\rm alto}-\Phi_{\rm basso}$ explains the gain of the higher clock?

- $\Delta\Phi>0$
- $\Delta\Phi<0$
- Only the square of $\Delta\Phi$ matters.
- $\Delta\Phi=0$

## Explanation

Gravitational potential increases upwards. In the weak-field limit the fractional rate difference is $\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{c^2}$, positive for the higher clock.
