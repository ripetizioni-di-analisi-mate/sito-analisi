A particle has a rest energy of $21$ MeV and momentum $28$ MeV/$c$. What is its total energy in MeV?

- $35$
- $49$
- $28$
- $7$

## Explanation

The square of the total energy is the sum of the squares of the rest energy and $pc$. Taking the positive square root gives the total energy, $35$.
