A collision has initial totals $E_{\rm tot}=10\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $p_{\rm tot}c=6\,\mathrm{GeV}$ along one direction. What total energy is available in the centre-of-momentum frame?

- $4\,\mathrm{GeV}$
- $10\,\mathrm{GeV}$
- $16\,\mathrm{GeV}$
- $8\,\mathrm{GeV}$

## Explanation

Total momentum vanishes in the centre-of-momentum frame. The invariant therefore gives its energy as $\sqrt{10^2-6^2}=8\,\mathrm{GeV}$.
