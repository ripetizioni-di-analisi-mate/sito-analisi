A particle has a rest energy of $1000$ MeV and travels at $0.6c$. What is its relativistic kinetic energy?

- $1250\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $250\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $180\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=(\gamma-1)E_0$. At $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$ the factor $\gamma-1$ is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, giving $K=250$ MeV.
