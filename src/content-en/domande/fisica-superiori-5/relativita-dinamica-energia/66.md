The graph shows total energy in units of $mc^2$ versus momentum magnitude in units of $mc$. What physical energy corresponds to the vertical intercept for this massive particle?

::grafico{f="x<0?NaN:sqrt(1+x^2)" xmin=-0.30 xmax=3.7 ymin=-0.40 ymax=4.2 nomi="p/(mc),E/(mc²)" punti="0,1,pieno" legenda=0 titolo="Total energy versus momentum magnitude, in units set by the particle's rest mass."}

- $0$
- $mc^2$
- $mc$
- $m^2c^4$

## Explanation

On the vertical axis momentum is zero. The square root reduces to rest energy even though kinetic energy is zero.
