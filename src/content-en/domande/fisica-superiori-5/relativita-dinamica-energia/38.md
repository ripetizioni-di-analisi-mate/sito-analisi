A particle has a rest energy of $900$ MeV and a speed of $0.6c$. What is the product $pc$?

- $540\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $675\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1125\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $225\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The relativistic momentum is $p=\gamma mv$. Multiplying by $c$ gives $pc=\gamma\dfrac{v}{c}mc^2$. Hence $pc=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot900=675$ MeV.
