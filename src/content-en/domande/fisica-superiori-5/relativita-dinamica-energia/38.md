A particle at rest decays into two fragments with different rest masses. Without knowing the initial energy, what is certain?

- Their speeds are equal.
- Their total energies are equal.
- The fragment momentum magnitudes are equal.
- Their kinetic energies are equal.

## Explanation

In the parent rest frame the initial momentum is zero. With only two products, their momentum vectors must be opposite.
