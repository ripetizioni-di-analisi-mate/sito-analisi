A particle is measured to have $K=3mc^2$. Which speed is compatible with this measurement?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt3\,c}{2}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{15}\,c}{4}$
- $\dfrac{c}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3c}{4}$

## Explanation

The total energy is $4mc^2$, hence $\gamma=4$. Its definition gives $\dfrac{v}{c}=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{1}{16}}$.
