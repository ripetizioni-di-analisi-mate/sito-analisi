A particle has mass $9$ MeV/$c^2$, speed $3c/5$, and Lorentz factor $5/4$. What is the magnitude of its momentum in MeV/$c$?

- $\dfrac{27}{4}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{45}{4}$
- $\dfrac{27}{5}$

## Explanation

The relativistic momentum is $\gamma mv$. Multiply the numerical factors from the speed and the Lorentz factor. The units reduce to MeV divided by $c$, giving $\dfrac{27}{4}$.
