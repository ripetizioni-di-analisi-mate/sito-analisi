A star radiates $3.6\cdot10^{26}\,\mathrm{J}$ in one second. What quantity is obtained by dividing this value by $c^2$?

- The star's net momentum.
- The mass equivalent of the energy radiated during that second.
- The photon count.
- The entire mass of fuel converted into helium.

## Explanation

The division gives a mass equivalent. More fuel mass may take part because most of that mass remains in the reaction products.
