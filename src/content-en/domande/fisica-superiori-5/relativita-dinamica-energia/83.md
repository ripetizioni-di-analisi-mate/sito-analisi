Two photon beams have equal photon energies. To maximize the invariant mass of an individual two-photon encounter at fixed energies, how should they be oriented?

- Head-on in opposite directions.
- Necessarily at $90^\circ$.
- Direction is irrelevant.
- In the same direction.

## Explanation

Total energy is fixed. Invariant mass increases as the vector sum of the momenta decreases; equal-energy opposite photons minimize that sum to zero.
