A particle of rest mass $7$ MeV/$c^2$ is accelerated toward the speed of light. How does its total energy change as its speed approaches $c$?

- It approaches $14$ MeV.
- It approaches $7$ MeV.
- It approaches zero.
- It approaches $+\infty$.

## Explanation

The denominator of the Lorentz factor approaches zero from above. The rest energy is positive and constant, so its product with the Lorentz factor grows without bound.
