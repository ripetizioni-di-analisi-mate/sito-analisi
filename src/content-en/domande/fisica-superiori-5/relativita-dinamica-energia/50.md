A particle has a rest energy of $7$ MeV and a total energy of $21$ MeV. What is its kinetic energy in MeV?

- $28$
- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $14$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy is the amount by which the total energy exceeds the rest energy. Subtracting the rest energy from the total gives $14$.
