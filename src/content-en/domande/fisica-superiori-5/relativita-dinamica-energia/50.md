An electron and positron annihilate into two photons. The lab measures unequal photon energies. What follows about the initial centre of momentum?

- It was moving relative to the lab.
- The initial total energy was zero.
- It was at rest relative to the lab.
- The two particles had different rest masses.

## Explanation

In the centre-of-momentum frame two final photons must have equal and opposite momenta, hence equal energies. Unequal energies exclude that frame as the lab.
