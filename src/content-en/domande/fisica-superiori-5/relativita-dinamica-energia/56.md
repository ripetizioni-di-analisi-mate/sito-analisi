A particle has mass $7$ MeV/$c^2$, speed $3c/5$, and Lorentz factor $5/4$. What is the magnitude of its momentum in MeV/$c$?

- $7$
- $\dfrac{21}{5}$
- $\dfrac{35}{4}$
- $\dfrac{21}{4}$

## Explanation

The relativistic momentum is $\gamma mv$. Multiply the numerical factors from the speed and the Lorentz factor. The units reduce to MeV divided by $c$, giving $\dfrac{21}{4}$.
