Two reactions have $Q_A=+2\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $Q_B=-3\,\mathrm{MeV}$ under the convention $Q=(M_i-M_f)c^2$. Which is energetically possible from reactants at rest in the centre-of-momentum frame if other laws allow it?

- Only B.
- Neither, because $Q$ must vanish.
- Both without extra energy.
- Only A.

## Explanation

Positive $Q$ leaves energy available to the products. The negative value for B requires additional energy in the initial system.
