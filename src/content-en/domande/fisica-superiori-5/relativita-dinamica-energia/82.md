A parent at rest of rest energy $5\,\mathrm{MeV}$ decays to a particle of rest energy $3\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and a photon. Which equation simultaneously satisfies energy and momentum conservation, taking energies in MeV?

- $5=E_\gamma+3$
- $25=9+E_\gamma^2$
- $5=2E_\gamma+3$
- $5=E_\gamma+\sqrt{9+E_\gamma^2}$

## Explanation

The daughter has momentum opposite to the photon's, with $pc=E_\gamma$. Its energy is therefore $\sqrt{3^2+E_\gamma^2}$ and adds to the photon energy.
