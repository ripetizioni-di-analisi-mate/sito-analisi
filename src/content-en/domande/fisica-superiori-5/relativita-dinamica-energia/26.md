A table gives a free particle total energy $7\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $pc=9\,\mathrm{MeV}$. What is the correct diagnosis for an ordinary particle?

- It must be a photon.
- The data imply negative mass squared and are inconsistent.
- These data imply a speed below $c$.
- Its rest mass is $2\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$.

## Explanation

In $m^2c^4=7^2-9^2$ the right side is negative. The left side is nonnegative for ordinary matter and zero for a photon.
