A photon climbs from the ground to the roof of a tower. Identical stationary clocks measure frequency locally at emitter and receiver. What comparison is expected in Earth's weak field?

- A lower frequency is measured at the roof.
- The frequency changes sign.
- The roof frequency is zero.
- A higher frequency is measured at the roof.

## Explanation

The higher clock runs faster than the lower clock. Radiation from below is therefore received with a gravitational redshift.
