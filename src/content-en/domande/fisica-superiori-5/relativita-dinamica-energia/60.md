A particle has a rest energy of $27$ MeV and momentum $36$ MeV/$c$. What is its total energy in MeV?

- $9$
- $45$
- $63$
- $36$

## Explanation

The square of the total energy is the sum of the squares of the rest energy and $pc$. Taking the positive square root gives the total energy, $45$.
