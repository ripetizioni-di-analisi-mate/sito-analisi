A particle has a rest energy of $600$ MeV and travels at $0.6c$. What is its relativistic kinetic energy?

- $750\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $150\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $600\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $108\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=(\gamma-1)E_0$. At $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$ the factor $\gamma-1$ is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, giving $K=150$ MeV.
