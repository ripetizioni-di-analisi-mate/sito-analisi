A uniform magnetic field bends a charged beam perpendicular to the field without changing its speed. Which quantity changes even in the relativistic regime?

- The direction of momentum.
- Kinetic energy.
- Rest mass.
- Momentum magnitude.

## Explanation

The magnetic force is perpendicular to velocity and does no work. It rotates the momentum vector while $E$ and $\lvert\vec p\rvert$ stay constant.
