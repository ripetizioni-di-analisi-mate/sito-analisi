A particle of rest mass $5$ MeV/$c^2$ is accelerated toward the speed of light. How does its total energy change as its speed approaches $c$?

- It approaches $+\infty$.
- It approaches $5$ MeV.
- It approaches $10$ MeV.
- It approaches zero.

## Explanation

The denominator of the Lorentz factor approaches zero from above. The rest energy is positive and constant, so its product with the Lorentz factor grows without bound.
