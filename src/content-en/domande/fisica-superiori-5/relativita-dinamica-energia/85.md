A particle detector gives $K=3\,\mathrm{GeV}$ with uncertainty $0.1\,\mathrm{GeV}$. The rest mass uncertainty is negligible. What is the absolute uncertainty in total energy?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{GeV}$
- $3\,\mathrm{GeV}$
- $0$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{GeV}$

## Explanation

Adding the known value $mc^2$ to $K$ shifts the central value. The uncertainty interval retains its width.
