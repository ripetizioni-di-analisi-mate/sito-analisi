Inside a freely falling cabin two widely separated balls slowly approach because both fall toward Earth's centre. What does this observation reveal?

- Free fall eliminates gravity everywhere.
- A necessary electrostatic force between the balls.
- A tidal effect due to variation of the gravitational field.
- Loss of the balls' rest energies.

## Explanation

One freely falling frame removes the common acceleration locally. Across a finite region differences in field direction or strength remain; these are tidal effects.
