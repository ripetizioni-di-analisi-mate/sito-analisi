A particle has a rest energy of $700$ MeV and travels at $0.6c$. What is its relativistic kinetic energy?

- $875\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $700\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $175\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $126\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=(\gamma-1)E_0$. At $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$ the factor $\gamma-1$ is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, giving $K=175$ MeV.
