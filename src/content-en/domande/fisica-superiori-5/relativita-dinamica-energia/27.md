A detector measures $E=5\,\mathrm{GeV}$ for a photon. Which notation expresses its momentum in customary particle-physics units?

- $5c\,\mathrm{GeV}$
- $25\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$
- $5\,\mathrm{GeV}/c^2$
- $5\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$

## Explanation

For a photon $E=pc$. Dividing energy by $c$ gives momentum units; division by $c^2$ gives mass units.
