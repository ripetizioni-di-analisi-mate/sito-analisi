A calorimeter measures the kinetic energy of a proton at $0.50c$. Given $mc^2=938\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $\gamma\simeq1.155$, which result is expected?

- $145\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $469\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $117\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1083\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy available on stopping the proton is $K=(\gamma-1)mc^2$. Multiplying $0.155$ by $938\,\mathrm{MeV}$ gives about $145\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
