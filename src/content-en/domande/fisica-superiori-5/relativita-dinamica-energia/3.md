A particle has a rest energy of $200$ MeV and a speed of $0.6c$. What is the product $pc$?

- $120\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $250\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $150\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The relativistic momentum is $p=\gamma mv$. Multiplying by $c$ gives $pc=\gamma\dfrac{v}{c}mc^2$. Hence $pc=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot200=150$ MeV.
