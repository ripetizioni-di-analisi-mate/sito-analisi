A satellite-clock error of $1\,\mu\mathrm s$ biases a signal travel time. What distance error does this produce for a one-way measurement with $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $300\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $150\,\mathrm m$
- $300\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Distance is inferred as $ct$. One microsecond times $c$ gives $300\,\mathrm m$ for a one-way measurement.
