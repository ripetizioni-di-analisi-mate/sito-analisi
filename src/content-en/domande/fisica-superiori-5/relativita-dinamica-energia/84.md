A reaction's final particles are heavier than its initial particles. In the centre-of-momentum frame, which configuration minimizes final energy at the production threshold?

- All products are at rest relative to one another.
- All products travel at $c$.
- A photon of arbitrarily high energy is required.
- Two products have large opposite momenta.

## Explanation

For given final masses the energy sum is least when there is no relative kinetic energy. In the centre-of-momentum frame this means zero momenta for the massive products.
