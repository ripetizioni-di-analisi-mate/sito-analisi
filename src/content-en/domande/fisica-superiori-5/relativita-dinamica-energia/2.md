An electron travels at $0.980c$ and has rest mass $0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$. What is its total energy?

- $25.6\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $2.06\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $2.57\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The speed gives $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.980^2}}\simeq5.03$. To find total energy, multiply rest energy by that factor: $E=\gamma m_ec^2$.
