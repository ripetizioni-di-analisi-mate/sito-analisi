Two photons with energies $E_1$ and $E_2$ travel in the same direction. What is the invariant mass of the pair?

- $\dfrac{E_1+E_2}{c^2}$
- $\dfrac{E_1+E_2}{2c^2}$
- Zero.
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{E_1E_2}}{c^2}$

## Explanation

The momenta add in the same direction, so $p_{\rm tot}c=E_1+E_2$. In the invariant the squared energy and momentum terms cancel.
