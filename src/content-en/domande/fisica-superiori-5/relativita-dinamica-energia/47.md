A particle has a rest energy of $7$ MeV and a Lorentz factor of $3$. What is its total energy in MeV?

- $21$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

Total energy is the product of the Lorentz factor and the rest energy. The rest energy is part of the total. The result is $21$.
