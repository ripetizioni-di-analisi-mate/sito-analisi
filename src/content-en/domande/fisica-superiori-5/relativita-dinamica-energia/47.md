A generator creates an electron of charge $-e$ in an initially neutral region. If its antiparticle is the only other charged product, what charge and mass does the antiparticle have?

- $+e,\ m_e$
- $+e,\ 2m_e$
- $-e,\ m_e$
- $0,\ m_e$

## Explanation

The electron's antiparticle is the positron. It has equal rest mass and opposite charge, leaving the pair's total charge zero.
