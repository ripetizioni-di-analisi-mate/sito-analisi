An enclosed craft accelerates upwards for a short interval. A horizontally emitted ray appears to bend downward relative to the walls. Which idea connects this locally with gravity?

- The equivalence principle.
- Charge conservation.
- Angular momentum quantization.
- Wien's law.

## Explanation

During light transit the walls accelerate upwards relative to an inertial frame. Over a small enough region an accelerating frame mimics a uniform gravitational field.
