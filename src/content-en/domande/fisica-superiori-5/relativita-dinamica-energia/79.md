In another inertial frame energy transforms as $E'=\gamma(E-vp_x)$. For a photon moving along $+x$, an observer chasing it at $v=0.6c$ has $\gamma=1.25$. What energy is measured?

- $2E$
- $0.4E$
- $\dfrac{E}{2}$
- $1.25E$

## Explanation

For the photon $p_x=\dfrac{E}{c}$. Thus $\dfrac{E'}{E}=1.25(1-0.6)=0.5$, consistent with a redshift.
