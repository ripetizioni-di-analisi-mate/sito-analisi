Two objects of equal rest mass have Lorentz factors $3$ and $10$. What is the ratio of their total energies, second to first?

- $7$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

Their rest energies are identical. This common factor cancels in the ratio of their total energies, leaving the ratio of the two Lorentz factors. This gives $\dfrac{10}{3}$.
