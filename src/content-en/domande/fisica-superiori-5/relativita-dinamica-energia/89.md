A massive particle is measured to have $\dfrac{pc}{E}=0.99$. Its mass is reconstructed by subtracting the squares of nearly equal quantities. What explains the high sensitivity to small errors?

- The difference $E^2-p^2c^2$ is small compared with either term.
- Energy and momentum cannot be measured in the same frame.
- Rest mass depends on the detector.
- Light speed is close to zero.

## Explanation

The absolute errors of the two terms can be a substantial fraction of their difference. This amplifies the relative uncertainty of the reconstructed mass.
