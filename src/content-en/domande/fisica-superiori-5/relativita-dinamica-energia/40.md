A parent with rest energy $10\,\mathrm{MeV}$ decays at rest into two identical products of rest energy $3\,\mathrm{MeV}$ each. What is the momentum magnitude of each product?

- $5\,\mathrm{MeV}/c$
- $7\,\mathrm{MeV}/c$
- $4\,\mathrm{MeV}/c$
- $2\,\mathrm{MeV}/c$

## Explanation

By symmetry each product has total energy $5\,\mathrm{MeV}$. The energy–momentum relation gives $pc=\sqrt{5^2-3^2}\,\mathrm{MeV}=4\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
