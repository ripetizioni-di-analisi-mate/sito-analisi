Two photons with energies $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{MeV}$ travel at right angles. What is $c\lvert\vec p_{\rm tot}\rvert$?

- $1\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $5\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $7\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $12\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The contributions to $c\vec p$ are perpendicular. Pythagoras gives the sum's magnitude, $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$; the scalar energies would instead add directly.
