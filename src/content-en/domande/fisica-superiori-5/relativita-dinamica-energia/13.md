In the Sun's overall energy balance four hydrogen atoms produce helium and radiation. The initial total and final atomic masses differ by $0.0287\,\mathrm u$. With $1\,\mathrm u\,c^2=931.5\,\mathrm{MeV}$, how much energy does the process release?

- $107\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $26.7\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $0.0287\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $2.67\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The quoted mass loss already covers the full reaction. Multiplying by the energy equivalent of the mass unit gives $0.0287\cdot931.5\simeq26.7\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
