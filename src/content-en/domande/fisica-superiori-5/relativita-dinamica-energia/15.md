The formation of a nucleus results in a mass defect of $4$ atomic mass units. Taking $1\,\mathrm u\,c^2=931$ MeV, what is the binding energy?

- $232.75\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $\dfrac{4}{931}\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $3724\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $935\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The binding energy is the energy equivalent of the mass defect, $E_b=\Delta m c^2$. Each atomic mass unit corresponds to $931$ MeV. The energy is therefore $3724$ MeV.
