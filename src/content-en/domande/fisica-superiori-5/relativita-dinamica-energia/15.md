A particle has a total energy of $10$ MeV and $pc=6$ MeV. What is its rest energy?

- $64\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $4\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $16\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum relation is $E^2=(pc)^2+E_0^2$. Solving gives $E_0=\sqrt{E^2-(pc)^2}$. Hence $E_0=\sqrt{(10)^2-(6)^2}=8$ MeV.
