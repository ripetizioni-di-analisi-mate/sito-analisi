A low-speed model uses $\gamma\simeq1+\dfrac{\beta^2}{2}$. Which law follows by keeping the first nonzero term of $K=(\gamma-1)mc^2$?

- $K\simeq \dfrac{mv^2}{2}$
- $K\simeq mc^2$
- $K\simeq mv^2$
- $K\simeq mvc$

## Explanation

The constant term cancels in $\gamma-1$. What remains is $\dfrac{mc^2\beta^2}{2}=\dfrac{mv^2}{2}$.
