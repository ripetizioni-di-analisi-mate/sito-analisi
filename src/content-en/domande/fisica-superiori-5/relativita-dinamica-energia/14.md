A particle has a total energy of $20$ MeV and $pc=12$ MeV. What is its rest energy?

- $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $16\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $32\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $256\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum relation is $E^2=(pc)^2+E_0^2$. Solving gives $E_0=\sqrt{E^2-(pc)^2}$. Hence $E_0=\sqrt{(20)^2-(12)^2}=16$ MeV.
