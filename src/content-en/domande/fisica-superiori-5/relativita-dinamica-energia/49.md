A particle has a rest energy of $5$ MeV and a total energy of $10$ MeV. What is its kinetic energy in MeV?

- $15$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy is the amount by which the total energy exceeds the rest energy. Subtracting the rest energy from the total gives $5$.
