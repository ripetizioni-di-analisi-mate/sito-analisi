An electron gains kinetic energy in an accelerator. Which quantity has the same value when its measurements are described in another inertial frame?

- $p_x$
- $E$
- $K$
- $E^2-p^2c^2$

## Explanation

Energy and momentum depend on the frame. The stated combination equals $m^2c^4$, and the electron's rest mass is invariant.
