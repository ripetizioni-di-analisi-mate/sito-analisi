A beam of electrons with kinetic energy $2\,\mathrm{MeV}$ has current $1\,\mu\mathrm A$. What thermal power does it deposit when the electrons are stopped without annihilation?

- $2\,\mathrm{MW}$
- $2\,\mathrm W$
- $0.5\,\mathrm W$
- $2\,\mu\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Energy per unit charge is $2\,\mathrm{MV}$. Multiplying by the current gives $P=2\cdot10^6\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm W=2\,\mathrm W$.
