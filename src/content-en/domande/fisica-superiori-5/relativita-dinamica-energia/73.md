A particle and its antiparticle, each with rest energy $5$ MeV, annihilate at rest into two photons. What is the energy of each photon in MeV?

- $5$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The initial momentum is zero. The two photons travel in opposite directions with momenta of equal magnitude, so they share the total energy equally, giving $5$.
