A gamma-ray photon has energy $E = 1.00\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Calculate its momentum in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Use $1\,\mathrm{MeV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-13}\,\mathrm{J}$ and $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $4.80\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $5.33\cdot10^{-22}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $1.78\cdot10^{-30}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $5.33\cdot10^{-25}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

A photon has zero rest mass, so $E=pc$. Convert the energy to $1.60\cdot10^{-13}\,\mathrm J$ and divide by $3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
