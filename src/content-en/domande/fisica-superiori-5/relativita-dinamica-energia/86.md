An initially stationary system ejects a fragment eastwards. With no external impulse or radiation, which way does the momentum of everything left behind point?

- West, but necessarily smaller.
- East, with equal magnitude.
- It is zero because the remaining mass is larger.
- West, with magnitude equal to the fragment momentum.

## Explanation

Total momentum was zero and is conserved. The residual mass affects recoil speed, not the momentum required to balance the fragment.
