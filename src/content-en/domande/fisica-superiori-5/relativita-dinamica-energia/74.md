A particle and its antiparticle, each with rest energy $7$ MeV, annihilate at rest into two photons. What is the energy of each photon in MeV?

- $14$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$
- $0$

## Explanation

The initial momentum is zero. The two photons travel in opposite directions with momenta of equal magnitude, so they share the total energy equally, giving $7$.
