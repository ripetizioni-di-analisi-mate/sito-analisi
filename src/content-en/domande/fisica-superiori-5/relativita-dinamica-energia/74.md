A particle with $pc=12\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $mc^2=5\,\mathrm{MeV}$ is treated as ultrarelativistic by using $E\simeq pc$. By how much is its total energy underestimated?

- $5\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $7\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The exact energy is $\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=13\,\mathrm{MeV}$. The approximation gives $12\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and falls short by one MeV.
