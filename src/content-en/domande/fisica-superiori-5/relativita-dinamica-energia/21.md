A particle has a rest energy of $600$ MeV and travels at $0.6c$. What is its total energy?

- $750\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $600\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $150\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $480\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

At $0.6c$ the Lorentz factor is $\dfrac{5}{4}$. The total energy is $E=\gamma E_0$, giving $E=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot600=750$ MeV.
