Two particles of rest masses $m$ and $2m$ travel at the same velocity. How are their vectors from the origin related on a $(pc,E)$ plot?

- Both components of the second are twice those of the first.
- Only energy doubles.
- Both components are unchanged.
- Only momentum doubles.

## Explanation

At equal velocity the factors $\gamma$ and $\beta$ agree. Both $E=\gamma mc^2$ and $pc=\gamma mcv$ are proportional to rest mass.
