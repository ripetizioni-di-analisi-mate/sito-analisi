An electron starts from rest and gains energy through an accelerating potential of $1.00\,\mathrm{MV}$. Its rest energy is $0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$. What speed does it reach?

- $0.980c$
- $0.862c$
- $0.511c$
- $0.941c$

## Explanation

The energy gained is kinetic, $K=1.00\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Thus $\gamma=1+\dfrac{K}{m_ec^2}\simeq2.96$ and $\dfrac{v}{c}=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{1}{\gamma^2}}\simeq0.941$.
