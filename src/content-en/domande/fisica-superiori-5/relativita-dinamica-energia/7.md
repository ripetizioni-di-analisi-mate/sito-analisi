A particle has a rest energy of $300$ MeV and travels at $0.6c$. What is its relativistic kinetic energy?

- $375\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $300\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $75\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $54\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=(\gamma-1)E_0$. At $\gamma=\dfrac{5}{4}$ the factor $\gamma-1$ is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, giving $K=75$ MeV.
