A proton has momentum $500\,\mathrm{MeV}/c$ and rest energy $938\,\mathrm{MeV}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $500\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $438\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1063\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $125\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum invariant gives $E=\sqrt{500^2+938^2}\,\mathrm{MeV}\simeq1063\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Only the excess over the $938\,\mathrm{MeV}$ rest energy is kinetic energy.
