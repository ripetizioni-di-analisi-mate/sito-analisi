A neutron is removed from a nucleus with total binding energy $75\,\mathrm{MeV}$. The residual nucleus has binding energy $67\,\mathrm{MeV}$. What minimum energy is required, neglecting final recoil?

- $75\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $67\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $142\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The constituent nucleons are the same before and after. The required input is the binding lost, $75-67=8\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
