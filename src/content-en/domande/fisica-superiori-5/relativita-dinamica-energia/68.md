Two nearby points of the energy–momentum curve give $\Delta E=0.6\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $\Delta p=1\,\mathrm{MeV}/c$. Given that locally $\dfrac{dE}{dp}=v$, what velocity is estimated by the slope?

- $0.36c$
- $1.67c$
- $0.6c$
- $c$

## Explanation

For nearby points the finite-difference ratio approximates the derivative. Dividing the measured differences gives $\dfrac{0.6\,\mathrm{MeV}}{1\,\mathrm{MeV}/c}=0.6c$.
