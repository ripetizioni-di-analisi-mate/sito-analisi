A photon has an energy of $7$ MeV. What is the magnitude of its momentum in MeV/$c$?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $14$
- $7$

## Explanation

For a massless particle, the energy–momentum relation reduces to $E=pc$. The numerical value of the momentum in MeV divided by $c$ equals that of the energy in MeV, giving $7$.
