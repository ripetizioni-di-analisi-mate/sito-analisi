A particle has a total energy of $45$ MeV and $pc=27$ MeV. What is its rest energy?

- $18\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $72\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $36\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1296\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The energy–momentum relation is $E^2=(pc)^2+E_0^2$. Solving gives $E_0=\sqrt{E^2-(pc)^2}$. Hence $E_0=\sqrt{(45)^2-(27)^2}=36$ MeV.
