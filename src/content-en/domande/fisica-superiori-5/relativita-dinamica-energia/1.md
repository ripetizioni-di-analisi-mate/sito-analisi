A particle has a rest energy of $200$ MeV and travels at $0.6c$. What is its total energy?

- $250\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $200\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $160\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

At $0.6c$ the Lorentz factor is $\dfrac{5}{4}$. The total energy is $E=\gamma E_0$, giving $E=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot200=250$ MeV.
