A proton travels at $0.8c$. Which factor corrects the classical momentum prediction $mv$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

Momentum is $p=\gamma mv$. With $\beta=0.8$ the factor is $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.8^2}}=\dfrac{5}{3}$.
