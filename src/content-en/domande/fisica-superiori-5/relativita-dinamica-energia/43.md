A particle has a rest energy of $1000$ MeV and a speed of $0.6c$. What is the product $pc$?

- $600\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $1250\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $750\,\mathrm{MeV}$
- $250\,\mathrm{MeV}$

## Explanation

The relativistic momentum is $p=\gamma mv$. Multiplying by $c$ gives $pc=\gamma\dfrac{v}{c}mc^2$. Hence $pc=\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot1000=750$ MeV.
