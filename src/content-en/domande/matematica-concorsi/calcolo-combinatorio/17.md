A neighborhood’s streets form a grid. To go from intersection $A$ to intersection $B$ by a shortest route requires going $5$ blocks east and $3$ blocks north, in any order. How many shortest routes are there from $A$ to $B$?

- $15$
- $56$
- $336$
- $40\,320$

## Explanation

Each shortest route contains $8$ steps, $5$ east and $3$ north. Two routes differ if their northward steps occupy different positions in the sequence. The routes are the **permutations with repeated objects** of $8$ items of two kinds, $5$ identical items of one kind and $3$ identical items of the other, giving
$$
\frac{8!}{5! \cdot 3!} = \frac{8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 56 .
$$
The result $336 = \dfrac{8!}{5!}$ treats the northward steps as distinct. The result $15 = 5 \cdot 3$ multiplies the two numbers of blocks.
