In a single round-robin tournament, each team plays every other team once. A total of $28$ games are played. How many teams are in the tournament?

- $14$
- $8$
- $7$
- $9$

## Explanation

Each game corresponds to an unordered pair of teams, so with $n$ teams there are $\dbinom{n}{2} = \dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2}$ games. We seek $n$ such that $n \, (n - 1) = 56$ and can **test the options**. With $n = 8$ we get $8 \cdot 7 = 56$. With $n = 7$ there are $21$ games, and with $n = 9$ there are $36$. The result $14$ divides $28$ by $2$, but halving the number of games overlooks that every game involves two teams and every team plays each of the others.
