How many different sequences can be formed by rearranging all the letters of **ABBC**?

- $12$
- $8$
- $6$
- $24$

## Explanation

The four letters would give $4!$ orders if they were distinct. Since the two Bs are identical, each sequence was counted $2!$ times, giving $4!/2!=12$ as the result.
