A hospital department has $5$ doctors and $4$ nurses. A team of $4$ people with no distinct roles must include at least one doctor and at least one nurse. How many different teams can be formed?

- $126$
- $121$
- $120$
- $125$

## Explanation

There are $\dbinom{9}{4} = 126$ teams of $4$ people chosen from $9$. Exclude the $\dbinom{5}{4} = 5$ teams of doctors only and the $\dbinom{4}{4} = 1$ teams of nurses only. No team belongs to both excluded groups, so **counting with complements** gives $126 - 5 - 1 = 120$ eligible teams. The result $121$ overlooks the all-nurse team, and $125$ overlooks the all-doctor teams.
