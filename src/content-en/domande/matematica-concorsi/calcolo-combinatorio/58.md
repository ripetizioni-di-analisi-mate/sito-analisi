From a list of $7$ distinct topics, exactly $2$ are chosen for a meeting without deciding their presentation order. How many pairs can be chosen?

- $14$
- $42$
- $35$
- $21$

## Explanation

There would be $7\cdot6=42$ ordered choices. Each pair is counted in both orders, so there are $42/2=21$ pairs.
