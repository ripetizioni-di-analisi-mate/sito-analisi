A basketball coach must choose $5$ starters from $7$ players, with Paolo among the starters. The starters have no assigned positions. In how many ways can the coach choose them?

- $21$
- $35$
- $6$
- $15$

## Explanation

Since Paolo is already a starter, the coach chooses the other $4$ from the $6$ remaining players without regard to order. The choices are therefore the **combinations** $C_{6,4} = \dbinom{6}{4} = \dbinom{6}{2} = 15$, by the **symmetry** of binomial coefficients. The result $21 = \dbinom{7}{5}$ ignores the requirement about Paolo, and $6 = \dbinom{6}{5}$ counts teams without him.
