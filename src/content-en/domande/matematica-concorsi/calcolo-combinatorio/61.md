A form can be completed in one of $3$ paper formats, each available in $2$ languages, or in one digital format available in $5$ languages. One format and one language are chosen. How many versions are available?

- $17$
- $11$
- $8$
- $10$

## Explanation

There are $3\cdot2=6$ paper versions and $1\cdot5=5$ digital versions. Paper and digital versions are distinct, so add the results to get $11$.
