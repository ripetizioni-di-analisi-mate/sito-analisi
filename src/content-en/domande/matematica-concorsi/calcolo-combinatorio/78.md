Four numbered boxes are each filled with S or N. How many ways of filling the boxes contain at least one S and at least one N?

- $8$
- $14$
- $15$
- $12$

## Explanation

There are $2^4=16$ ways in total. By the **complement rule**, exclude the two constant sequences, all S or all N, leaving $14$ ways.
