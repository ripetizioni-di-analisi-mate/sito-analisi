A pizzeria offers $6$ different extra toppings. A customer may choose any number of them, including none, with each topping added at most once. How many different pizzas can be ordered?

- $63$
- $64$
- $720$
- $20$

## Explanation

For each of the $6$ toppings, either include it or leave it off, giving $2$ choices per topping. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $2^6 = 64$ pizzas. The same total comes from adding the combinations with $0, 1, 2, \dots, 6$ toppings.
$$
1 + 6 + 15 + 20 + 15 + 6 + 1 = 64 .
$$
The result $63$ excludes the pizza without extra toppings, which the question allows. The result $720 = 6!$ counts the order of the toppings, which does not matter on a pizza. The result $20 = \dbinom{6}{3}$ counts only pizzas with $3$ toppings.
