Eight friends split into two teams of $4$ players each for a small-sided soccer game. The teams have no names or colors, so only who plays with whom matters. In how many ways can the teams be formed?

- $70$
- $35$
- $1680$
- $840$

## Explanation

Choosing the $4$ players on one team gives $\dbinom{8}{4} = 70$ groups, and the other $4$ friends form the other team. Label the friends with letters from $A$ to $H$. Choosing $\{A, B, C, D\}$ or its complement $\{E, F, G, H\}$ gives the same division because the teams are indistinguishable. Each division has been counted $2$ times, so there are $\dfrac{70}{2} = 35$ divisions. The result $70$ would be correct for labeled teams, such as a red team and a blue team. The result $1680 = D_{8,4}$ also counts the order of the players.
