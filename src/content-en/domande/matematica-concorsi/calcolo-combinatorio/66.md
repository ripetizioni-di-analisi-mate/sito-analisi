Six cards form a row. Three show A, two show B, and one shows C. Cards with the same letter are indistinguishable. How many rows have C in one of the two middle positions?

- $30$
- $20$
- $60$
- $10$

## Explanation

The middle positions are the third and fourth. For each choice, fill the five remaining positions by choosing the two positions for the Bs in $\binom{5}{2}=10$ ways. This gives $2\cdot10=20$ rows.
