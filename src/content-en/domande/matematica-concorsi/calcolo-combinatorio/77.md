A sample of $4$ distinct cards must be chosen from $9$ cards. One particular card must be included and a different card must be excluded. How many samples are possible?

- $70$
- $35$
- $56$
- $21$

## Explanation

After including the required card and setting aside the excluded card, seven cards remain available. Since order does not matter, the last three can be selected in $\binom{7}{3}=35$ ways.
