A questionnaire has $5$ questions, and each one is answered «yes» or «no». Any question may also be left unanswered. In how many different ways can the questionnaire be handed in?

- $32$
- $125$
- $15$
- $243$

## Explanation

For each question there are three possibilities: «yes», «no» or no answer. The choices for the five questions are independent and the same possibility may be repeated, so we count the **ordered selections with repetition** of $3$ objects taken $5$ at a time. Therefore the questionnaire can be handed in in $3^5=243$ ways. The value $32$ is $2^5$ and ignores the unanswered question, the value $125$ is $5^3$ and swaps base and exponent, and the value $15$ multiplies the number of questions by the number of possibilities.
