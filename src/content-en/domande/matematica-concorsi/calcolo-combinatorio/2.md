A gym offers $3$ basic membership plans and $2$ full membership plans. A new member selects one plan and then one of $3$ payment methods (card, bank transfer, or cash), all available with every plan. In how many ways can someone sign up?

- $18$
- $8$
- $15$
- $6$

## Explanation

Selecting a plan is a single choice from two nonoverlapping groups, so by the **addition principle**, there are $3 + 2 = 5$ available plans. Each can be paired with one of $3$ payment methods. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $5 \cdot 3 = 15$ ways to sign up. The result $18 = 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 3$ treats the basic and full plans as two successive choices, although each member chooses only one plan.
