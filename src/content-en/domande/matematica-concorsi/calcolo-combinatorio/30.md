From an Italian deck of $40$ different cards, $2$ cards are dealt. The order in which they arrive does not matter. How many different pairs of cards can be dealt?

- $1560$
- $1600$
- $780$
- $80$

## Explanation

The two cards are different and their order does not matter, so count **combinations without repetition** of $2$ of the $40$ cards, with $2$ cards per hand.
$$
\binom{40}{2} = \frac{40 \cdot 39}{2} = 780 .
$$
The result $1560 = 40 \cdot 39$ counts every pair twice, once for each order in which the cards could arrive. The result $1600 = 40^2$ also allows the same card to be dealt twice.
