A group of four must be chosen from five technicians and four administrative staff members, all distinct. The group must include more technicians than administrative staff. How many groups are possible?

- $45$
- $50$
- $40$
- $105$

## Explanation

The allowed groups contain three technicians and one administrative staff member, or four technicians. The respective counts are $\binom{5}{3}\binom{4}{1}=40$ and $\binom{5}{4}=5$, giving $45$ in total.
