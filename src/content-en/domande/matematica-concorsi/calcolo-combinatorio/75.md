A box contains four distinct long screws and five distinct short screws. Two long screws and one short screw are drawn together. How many different sets of three can be obtained?

- $20$
- $30$
- $60$
- $36$

## Explanation

The two long screws can be chosen in $\binom{4}{2}=6$ ways. Each of the six pairs can be matched with any of five short screws, giving $30$ sets of three.
