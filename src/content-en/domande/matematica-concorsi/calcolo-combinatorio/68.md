How many five-digit codes can be formed by rearranging $0,0,1,1,2$ if a leading zero is allowed?

- $20$
- $60$
- $15$
- $30$

## Explanation

If the digits were distinguishable, the five positions would give $5!$ orders. Divide by the $2!$ permutations of the zeros and the $2!$ permutations of the ones to get $30$ codes.
