An employee must mail $4$ different letters using $3$ mailboxes. Each letter can go in any mailbox, and multiple letters may go in the same mailbox. In how many ways can the letters be distributed?

- $12$
- $64$
- $7$
- $81$

## Explanation

For each of the $4$ letters, choose one of $3$ mailboxes independently of the other letters. These are **ordered selections with repetition** from $3$ mailboxes, making $4$ choices for $4$ letters. The base is the number of mailboxes and the exponent is the number of letters, giving $3^4 = 81$. The result $64 = 4^3$ switches the base and the exponent. The result $12 = 3 \cdot 4$ multiplies the two given numbers.
