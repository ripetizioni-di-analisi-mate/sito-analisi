For a photograph, $4$ people chosen from $7$ friends stand in a row. How many different photographs are possible if changing the order of the same people gives a different photograph?

- $35$
- $2401$
- $840$
- $5040$

## Explanation

Order matters in the photograph and nobody appears twice. Count the **ordered selections without repetition** of $4$ of the $7$ friends for $4$ places in the photo.
$$
D_{7,4} = 7 \cdot 6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4 = 840 .
$$
The result $35 = C_{7,4}$ counts groups of four without regard to their order, $2401 = 7^4$ allows a person to appear more than once, and $5040 = 7!$ lines up all seven friends.
