Three colleagues, Dario, Ester, and Franco, must each take a day off during the same week. Each may choose from the $6$ days from Monday through Saturday, but no two may be absent on the same day. In how many ways can they choose their days off?

- $20$
- $216$
- $18$
- $120$

## Explanation

The people are distinct, so it matters who chooses which day. Their days must all differ, so repetition is not allowed. The count is the number of **ordered selections without repetition**, $D_{6,3} = 6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4 = 120$. The result $20 = C_{6,3}$ counts only the three days chosen without identifying who is off on each. The result $216 = 6^3$ allows two colleagues to choose the same day, and $18 = 6 \cdot 3$ multiplies the number of days by the number of colleagues.
