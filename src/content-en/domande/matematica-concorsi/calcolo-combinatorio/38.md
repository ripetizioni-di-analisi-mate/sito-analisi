An association with $5$ volunteers schedules overnight duty for $3$ consecutive nights, with one person on duty each night. A volunteer may serve more than one night. How many duty schedules can be formed?

- $60$
- $10$
- $125$
- $15$

## Explanation

The three nights are distinct, so order matters. A person may serve more than once, so repetition is allowed. Count **ordered selections with repetition** from $5$ volunteers, making $3$ choices for $3$ nights. There are $5^3 = 125$ schedules. The result $60 = D_{5,3}$ would apply if each volunteer could serve at most one night. The result $10 = C_{5,3}$ counts the ways to choose three distinct volunteers without assigning each one to a night.
