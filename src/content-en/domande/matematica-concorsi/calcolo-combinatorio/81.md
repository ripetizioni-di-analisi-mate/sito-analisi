From $8$ distinct technicians, three are chosen for a site visit. Anna and Luca must either both participate or both be left out. In how many ways can the group be formed?

- $26$
- $20$
- $56$
- $36$

## Explanation

If both participate, choose one of the other six technicians. If both are left out, choose three of the other six. The cases are disjoint, so the total is $6+\binom{6}{3}=26$.
