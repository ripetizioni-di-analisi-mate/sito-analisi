Tiles with the same letter are indistinguishable. How many distinct five-tile sequences can be made from two As, two Bs, and one C if the first tile must be A?

- $6$
- $12$
- $18$
- $24$

## Explanation

Fix one A in the first position. The remaining tiles are A, B, B, and C. They can be arranged in $4!/2!=12$ orders because the two Bs are indistinguishable.
