Strings of $4$ letters, which need not form words, are made from A, B, C, D, and E, with repetition allowed. How many strings contain at least one vowel, meaning at least one A or at least one E?

- $81$
- $544$
- $625$
- $216$

## Explanation

All the strings are **ordered selections with repetition** from $5$ letters, making $4$ choices for $4$ positions, giving $5^4 = 625$ strings. Strings without vowels use only the $3$ consonants B, C, and D, giving $3^4 = 81$ possibilities. By **counting with complements**, there are $625 - 81 = 544$ strings with at least one vowel. The result $81$ counts the complementary outcomes, and $625$ counts all strings. The result $216 = 4 \cdot 2 \cdot 3^3$ counts strings with *exactly* one vowel.
