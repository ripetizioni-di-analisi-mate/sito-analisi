An employee plans work for Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday. Each day can be spent on site or working remotely, but two consecutive days cannot both be remote. How many three-day schedules are possible?

- $8$
- $4$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

Without restrictions there would be $2^3 = 8$ schedules because each day offers $2$ choices. Let $S$ stand for on site and $R$ for remote. The schedules with consecutive remote days are $RRS$, $SRR$, and $RRR$, or $3$ in all. By **counting with complements**, there are $8 - 3 = 5$ valid schedules, namely $SSS$, $RSS$, $SRS$, $SSR$, and $RSR$. The result $4$ subtracts $2$ schedules for Monday–Tuesday and $2$ for Tuesday–Wednesday, removing $RRR$ twice.
