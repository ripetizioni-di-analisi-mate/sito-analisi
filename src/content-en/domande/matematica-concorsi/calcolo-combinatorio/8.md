An instructor must assign $4$ distinct topics to $4$ students, one topic per student, with no topic assigned twice. In how many ways can this be done?

- $24$
- $16$
- $256$
- $10$

## Explanation

The first student can be assigned any of $4$ topics, the second any of the $3$ left, the third any of the $2$ left, and the last the one remaining topic. The assignments are the **permutations** of the $4$ topics, giving $P_4 = 4! = 24$. The result $256 = 4^4$ allows more than one student to have the same topic. The result $10 = 4 + 3 + 2 + 1$ adds the numbers of choices instead of multiplying them.
