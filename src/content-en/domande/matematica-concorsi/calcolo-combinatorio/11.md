Three men and three women sit in a row of $6$ chairs, with men and women alternating. In how many ways can they sit?

- $36$
- $720$
- $20$
- $72$

## Explanation

The row can begin with a man or a woman, so there are $2$ seating patterns. For each pattern the three men can fill their three seats in $3! = 6$ ways and the three women can fill theirs in $3! = 6$ ways. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $2 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 72$ seatings. The result $36$ counts only the rows that begin with a man.
