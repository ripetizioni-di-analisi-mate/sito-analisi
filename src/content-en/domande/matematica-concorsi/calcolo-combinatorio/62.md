Two separate interviews must be booked. The first can take place in one of $4$ offices and the second in one of $6$. Three offices appear on both lists. How many ordered pairs of offices are possible if the interviews must take place in different offices?

- $21$
- $18$
- $20$
- $24$

## Explanation

Allowing the same office twice would give $4\cdot6=24$ pairs. The only pairs to exclude use the same office for both interviews, one pair for each of the three offices on both lists.
