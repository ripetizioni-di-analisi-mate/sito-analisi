In a signaling system, each signal has at least $1$ and at most $4$ symbols. Each symbol is a dot or a dash. How many different signals can be formed?

- $16$
- $30$
- $31$
- $8$

## Explanation

The sequences of a given length are **ordered selections with repetition** from $2$ symbols. There are $2^1 = 2$ one-symbol signals, $2^2 = 4$ two-symbol signals, $2^3 = 8$ three-symbol signals, and $2^4 = 16$ four-symbol signals. Signals of different lengths are distinct, so the **addition principle** gives $2 + 4 + 8 + 16 = 30$ signals. The result $16$ counts only four-symbol signals, and $31$ adds the empty sequence, which is excluded because every signal has at least one symbol.
