Six distinct employees must be divided into two groups of three. One group covers the morning and the other the afternoon. Ada and Luca must be in different groups. How many divisions are possible?

- $10$
- $12$
- $20$
- $6$

## Explanation

Choose whether Ada works in the morning or the afternoon, giving two choices. Choose her two teammates from the four employees other than Ada and Luca. This determines the other group, giving $2\binom{4}{2}=12$ divisions in all.
