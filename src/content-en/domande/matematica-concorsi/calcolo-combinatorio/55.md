From $6$ distinct employees, one is chosen for the morning shift and a different one for the evening shift. In how many ways can the shifts be assigned?

- $30$
- $36$
- $24$
- $15$

## Explanation

There are six choices for the morning shift and, once that person is chosen, five for the evening. The product is $6\cdot5=30$.
