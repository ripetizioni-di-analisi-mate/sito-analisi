On a grid, a path goes from $(0,0)$ to $(4,3)$ using only unit steps to the right or up. How many paths avoid the point $(2,1)$?

- $15$
- $18$
- $23$
- $17$

## Explanation

There are $\binom{7}{3}=35$ paths in total, found by choosing the positions of the three upward steps. Of these, $\binom{3}{1}\binom{4}{2}=18$ pass through $(2,1)$, leaving $17$ paths that avoid it.
