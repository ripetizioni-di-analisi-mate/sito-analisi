The digits $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, and $6$ are used to form three-digit numbers with distinct digits. How many of these numbers are even?

- $60$
- $120$
- $108$
- $20$

## Explanation

An even number ends in an even digit. The last digit can be $2$, $4$, or $6$, giving $3$ choices. There are then $5$ choices left for the first digit and $4$ for the second. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $3 \cdot 5 \cdot 4 = 60$ even numbers. The result $120 = D_{6,3}$ counts all three-digit numbers with distinct digits, both even and odd. The result $108 = 6 \cdot 6 \cdot 3$ allows repeated digits.
