To attend a meeting, choose one of $3$ morning time slots or one of $4$ afternoon slots. All slots are distinct and only one can be reserved. How many choices are there?

- $12$
- $14$
- $4$
- $7$

## Explanation

The two sets of time slots are disjoint. The **addition principle** gives $3+4=7$ choices.
