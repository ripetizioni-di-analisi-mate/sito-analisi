A shelf holds a row of $7$ different books, including a dictionary that must be at one of the two ends. In how many ways can the books be arranged?

- $720$
- $1440$
- $5040$
- $240$

## Explanation

The dictionary goes first or last, so its position can be chosen in $2$ ways. For each choice, the other $6$ books can fill the $6$ remaining positions in $6! = 720$ ways. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $2 \cdot 720 = 1440$ arrangements. The result $720$ considers only one end, and $5040 = 7!$ ignores the restriction on the dictionary.
