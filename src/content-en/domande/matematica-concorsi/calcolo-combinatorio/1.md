A dealership lets customers configure a car by choosing one of $6$ body colors, one of $3$ wheel designs, and whether to add a sunroof. How many different configurations are possible?

- $11$
- $36$
- $18$
- $20$

## Explanation

The configuration is chosen in three stages. There are $6$ ways to choose the color, $3$ for the wheels, and $2$ for the roof because the car can come with or without a sunroof. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $6 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 36$ configurations. The result $11$ is the sum $6 + 3 + 2$, which would count a single choice among all the options. The result $18$ overlooks the two roof options.
