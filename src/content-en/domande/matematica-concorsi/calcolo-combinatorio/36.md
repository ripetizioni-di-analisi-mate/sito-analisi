On a sheet of paper, $5$ parallel horizontal lines and $4$ parallel vertical lines intersect to form a grid. How many rectangles have all four sides on these lines?

- $60$
- $16$
- $20$
- $120$

## Explanation

A rectangle is determined by the two horizontal lines containing its horizontal sides and the two vertical lines containing its vertical sides. There are $\dbinom{5}{2} = 10$ ways to choose the horizontal lines and $\dbinom{4}{2} = 6$ ways to choose the vertical lines. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $10 \cdot 6 = 60$ rectangles. The result $16 = 10 + 6$ adds the two counts instead of multiplying, and $20 = 5 \cdot 4$ counts the intersection points.
