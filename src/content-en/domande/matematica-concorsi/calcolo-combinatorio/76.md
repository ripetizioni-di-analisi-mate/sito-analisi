From an archive of $6$ distinct case files, either one or two files are sent in a single shipment. How many different sets of files can be sent?

- $36$
- $30$
- $21$
- $15$

## Explanation

There are $6$ sets made of a single file and $\binom{6}{2}=15$ sets made of two files. The two cases are disjoint, so the possible sets are $6+15=21$.
