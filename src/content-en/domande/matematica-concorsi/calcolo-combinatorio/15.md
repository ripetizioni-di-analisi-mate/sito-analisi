How many anagrams of the Italian word SASSO, including arrangements that do not form words, begin with O?

- $20$
- $24$
- $12$
- $4$

## Explanation

Fix O in the first position, then arrange S, A, S, and S in the next $4$ positions. The S occurs $3$ times, so the formula for **permutations with repeated objects** gives $\dfrac{4!}{3!} = 4$ arrangements. Alternatively, A can occupy any of the $4$ positions and the Ss fill the others. The result $20 = \dfrac{5!}{3!}$ counts all anagrams of SASSO, without the restriction on the first letter.
