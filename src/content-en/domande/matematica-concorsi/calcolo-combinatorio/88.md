Four-letter sequences are formed from A, B, and C, with repetition allowed. How many contain all three letters?

- $48$
- $42$
- $24$
- $36$

## Explanation

There are $3^4=81$ sequences in total. For each of the three letters, $2^4=16$ sequences omit it. Each of the three constant sequences omits two letters, so add them back after subtracting them twice. This gives $81-3\cdot16+3=36$.
