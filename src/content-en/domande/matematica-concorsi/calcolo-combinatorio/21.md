How many four-digit numbers with distinct digits from $0$ through $9$ are divisible by $5$?

- $1008$
- $952$
- $504$
- $896$

## Explanation

A number is divisible by $5$ if it ends in $0$ or $5$. Count the two cases separately. If the last digit is $0$, the first three digits can be chosen from the remaining $9$ in $9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 = 504$ ways, with no restriction needed for the first digit. If the last digit is $5$, the first cannot be $0$ or $5$, giving $8$ choices. The two middle digits can be chosen in $8 \cdot 7$ ways, for a total of $8 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 = 448$. By the **addition principle**, there are $504 + 448 = 952$ numbers. The result $1008 = 2 \cdot 504$ overlooks that when the last digit is $5$, the first cannot be $0$.
