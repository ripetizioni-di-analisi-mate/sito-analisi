A number is a palindrome if it reads the same from left to right and from right to left, as $3443$ does. How many four-digit palindromes are there?

- $90$
- $100$
- $81$
- $900$

## Explanation

A four-digit palindrome has the form $abba$, since the fourth digit matches the first and the third matches the second. Only the first two digits need to be chosen. The first cannot be $0$, giving $9$ choices from $1$ to $9$. The second can be any of the $10$ digits. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $9 \cdot 10 = 90$ palindromes. The result $100 = 10^2$ includes sequences starting with $0$, and $81 = 9 \cdot 9$ requires the digits $a$ and $b$ to differ.
