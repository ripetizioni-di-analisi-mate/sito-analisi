A committee of $3$ people with no distinct roles is chosen from $8$ colleagues, including Anna and Bruno. Anna and Bruno cannot both serve, but exactly one of them or neither of them may serve. How many committees can be formed?

- $50$
- $6$
- $30$
- $56$

## Explanation

There are $\dbinom{8}{3} = 56$ committees of $3$ people chosen from $8$. A committee containing both Anna and Bruno is completed by choosing its third member from the other $6$ colleagues, giving $6$ choices. By **counting with complements**, there are $56 - 6 = 50$ eligible committees. The result $30 = 2 \cdot \dbinom{6}{2}$ counts only committees with exactly one of the two and omits the $\dbinom{6}{3} = 20$ with neither.
