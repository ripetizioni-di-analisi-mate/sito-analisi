Five distinct panels can be displayed in a row and viewed from either end. Two orders count as identical if one is the reverse of the other. How many different displays can be made?

- $48$
- $120$
- $24$
- $60$

## Explanation

With a fixed starting end, there are $5!=120$ permutations. Each display is counted twice because the panels are distinct and reversing them gives a different order. Dividing by two gives $60$.
