Seven cards labeled A, A, A, B, B, C, and D are arranged in a row. Cards with the same letter are indistinguishable. How many rows have exactly two cards between C and D?

- $40$
- $120$
- $80$
- $60$

## Explanation

The positions of C and D must differ by three, so there are four possible pairs of positions. For each pair, C and D can switch places, and the other letters can be arranged in $\dfrac{5!}{3!\,2!}=10$ ways. This gives $4\cdot2\cdot10=80$ rows.
