Three-digit numbers with distinct digits are formed using only $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, and $6$. How many even numbers are greater than $400$?

- $36$
- $30$
- $24$
- $28$

## Explanation

The hundreds digit must be $4$, $5$, or $6$. With $4$ or $6$ in the hundreds place, two even digits are available for the ones place. With $5$, three are available. Each choice of hundreds and ones digits leaves four choices for the tens digit, giving $(2+3+2)\cdot4=28$ numbers.
