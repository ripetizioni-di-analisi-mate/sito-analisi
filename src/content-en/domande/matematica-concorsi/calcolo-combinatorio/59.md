Five distinct documents are being considered for retention, and exactly two will be discarded. How many different sets of remaining documents can result?

- $10$
- $15$
- $5$
- $20$

## Explanation

Each set of three remaining documents corresponds to exactly one pair of discarded documents. There are $\dfrac{5\cdot4}{2}=10$ pairs.
