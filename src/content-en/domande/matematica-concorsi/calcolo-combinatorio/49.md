A user can access a service through $5$ physical locations or $2$ online portals. The user chooses a single channel, and all the channels are distinct. How many channels are available?

- $5$
- $7$
- $10$
- $3$

## Explanation

Each choice belongs to just one of the two categories. Adding $5$ locations and $2$ portals gives $7$ channels.
