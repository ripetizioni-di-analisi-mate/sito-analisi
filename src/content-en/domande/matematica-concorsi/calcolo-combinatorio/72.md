A three-digit code uses only $0$, $1$, $2$, and $3$, with repeated digits and a leading zero allowed. The third digit must equal the sum of the first two. How many codes are possible?

- $16$
- $10$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

When the first digit is $0$, $1$, $2$, or $3$, the second has $4$, $3$, $2$, or $1$ possible values, respectively, since the sum cannot exceed $3$. The third digit is determined, so the total is $4+3+2+1=10$.
