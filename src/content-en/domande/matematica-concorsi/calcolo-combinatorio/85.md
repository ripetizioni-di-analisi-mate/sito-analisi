How many different sequences can be made by arranging three indistinguishable A tiles and three indistinguishable B tiles in a row if no two A tiles may be consecutive?

- $2$
- $10$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The three Bs create four gaps, including the one before the first B and the one after the last. To keep the As separated, choose three different gaps from the four, giving $\binom{4}{3}=4$ sequences.
