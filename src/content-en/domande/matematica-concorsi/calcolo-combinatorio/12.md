Five friends, including Marco and Luisa, sit around a round table. Two seatings count as identical if one is a rotation of the other. In how many seatings are Marco and Luisa next to each other?

- $12$
- $24$
- $48$
- $6$

## Explanation

Treat Marco and Luisa as one block, giving $4$ items to seat around the table. For seatings at a **round table**, fix one item and arrange the other $3$, so the $4$ items can be seated in $3! = 6$ ways. Within the block, Marco and Luisa can sit in $2$ orders, with Marco on Luisa’s right or her left. There are therefore $6 \cdot 2 = 12$ seatings. The result $6$ overlooks the two orders within the block. The result $48 = 4! \cdot 2$ counts lineups with Marco and Luisa together as if the five friends sat in a straight row.
