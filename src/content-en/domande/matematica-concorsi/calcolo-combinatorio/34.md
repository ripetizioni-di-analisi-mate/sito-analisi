On a circle, $8$ distinct points are marked. How many triangles have all three vertices among these points?

- $336$
- $28$
- $56$
- $24$

## Explanation

Three distinct points on a circle are never collinear, so each choice of $3$ points determines a triangle. Changing the order of the vertices does not change the triangle. Thus count **combinations without repetition**.
$$
\binom{8}{3} = \frac{8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 56 .
$$
The result $336 = D_{8,3}$ counts each triangle $3! = 6$ times, once per ordering of its vertices. The result $28 = \dbinom{8}{2}$ counts the segments joining pairs of points.
