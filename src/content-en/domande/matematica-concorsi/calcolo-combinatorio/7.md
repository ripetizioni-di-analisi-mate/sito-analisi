Which of the following equalities is true?

- $\dfrac{6!}{3!} = 2!$
- $3! + 3! = 6!$
- $2 \cdot 4! = 8!$
- $\dfrac{9!}{7!} = 72$

## Explanation

Since $9! = 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7!$, the factor $7!$ cancels, leaving $\dfrac{9!}{7!} = 9 \cdot 8 = 72$. The other equalities treat the **factorial** as if it could be moved inside or outside an arithmetic operation. In fact, $\dfrac{6!}{3!} = 6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4 = 120$, while $2! = 2$. Likewise, $3! + 3! = 12$, while $6! = 720$, and $2 \cdot 4! = 48$, while $8! = 40\,320$.
