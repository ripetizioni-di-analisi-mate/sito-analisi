Five different employees occupy five numbered seats around a table. Ada must sit next to Luca in the seat immediately clockwise from his. How many seatings are possible?

- $6$
- $60$
- $30$
- $24$

## Explanation

There are five choices for Luca’s seat, and his seat determines Ada’s. The three remaining people can take the three open seats in $3!=6$ ways, giving $5\cdot6=30$ seatings.
