A flag has $3$ horizontal stripes, each painted in one of $5$ available colors. A color may repeat, provided adjacent stripes have different colors. How many different flags can be made?

- $80$
- $125$
- $60$
- $15$

## Explanation

The top stripe can be colored in $5$ ways. The middle stripe must differ from the top, leaving $4$ choices. The bottom stripe needs to differ only from the middle, so it again has $4$ choices, including the color of the top stripe. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $5 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 80$ flags. The result $60 = 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3$ requires all three colors to differ, although the outer stripes may match. The result $125 = 5^3$ ignores the restriction on adjacent stripes.
