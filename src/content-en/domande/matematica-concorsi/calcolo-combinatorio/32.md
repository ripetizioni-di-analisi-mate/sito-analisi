Which number equals the binomial coefficient $\dbinom{12}{9}$?

- $220$
- $1320$
- $36$
- $108$

## Explanation

By the **symmetry** of binomial coefficients, $\dbinom{12}{9} = \dbinom{12}{3}$. Choosing the $9$ objects to take is equivalent to choosing the $3$ to leave behind. Thus
$$
\binom{12}{9} = \binom{12}{3} = \frac{12 \cdot 11 \cdot 10}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = \frac{1320}{6} = 220 .
$$
The result $1320 = D_{12,3}$ omits division by $3! = 6$. The results $36 = 12 \cdot 3$ and $108 = 12 \cdot 9$ each multiply the two entries in one of the equivalent binomial coefficients.
