An itinerary must include three distinct stops chosen from five and placed in a specific order. If stops A and B are both included, they must be consecutive. How many itineraries are possible?

- $54$
- $60$
- $36$
- $48$

## Explanation

There are $5\cdot4\cdot3=60$ unrestricted itineraries. Exclude those with A and B at the two ends, in either order, and one of the other three stops in the middle. That leaves $60-2\cdot3=54$ itineraries.
