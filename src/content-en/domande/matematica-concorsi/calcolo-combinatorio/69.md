Three different people each lead one of three sessions. There are $7$ people to choose from, but Elena must lead the middle session. In how many ways can the three sessions be assigned?

- $15$
- $36$
- $30$
- $42$

## Explanation

With Elena fixed in the middle, six choices remain for the first session. Once that person is chosen, five choices remain for the third, giving $30$ assignments.
