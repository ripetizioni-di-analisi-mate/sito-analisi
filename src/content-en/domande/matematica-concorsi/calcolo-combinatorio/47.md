A label uses one of $4$ symbols, one of $3$ colors, and one of $2$ formats. Every combination is allowed. How many labels can be made?

- $24$
- $18$
- $12$
- $9$

## Explanation

Each symbol has three color choices, and each symbol-and-color choice has two formats. The **multiplication principle** gives $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ labels.
