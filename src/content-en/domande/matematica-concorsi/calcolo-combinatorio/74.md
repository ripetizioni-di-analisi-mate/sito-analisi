A three-member committee is drawn from five names. Each group is listed once for every possible chair drawn from that group. How many rows does the list have?

- $10$
- $30$
- $15$
- $60$

## Explanation

There are $\binom53=10$ groups. Each has three possible chairs, giving $10\cdot3=30$ rows.
