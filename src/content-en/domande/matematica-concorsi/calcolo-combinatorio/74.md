For an oral exam, $3$ distinct topics are chosen from $8$. The first topic chosen is designated as the introduction, while the other two are chosen without specifying their order. Which expression counts all possible choices?

- $8\cdot7\cdot6$
- $8\binom{7}{2}$
- $\binom{7}{2}$
- $\binom{8}{3}$

## Explanation

The introduction is chosen among all eight topics, so in $8$ ways. The other two are chosen among the seven remaining topics without regard to order, so the choices are the **combinations** $\binom{7}{2}=21$. By the **multiplication principle** the possible choices are $8\binom{7}{2}=168$. The expression $8\cdot7\cdot6$ also orders the last two topics and counts each choice twice, while $\binom{8}{3}$ does not distinguish the introduction from the other two topics.
