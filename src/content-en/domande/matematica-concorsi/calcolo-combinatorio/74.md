For an oral exam, $3$ distinct topics are chosen from $8$. One must be designated as the introduction, while the other two are chosen without specifying their order. Which expression counts all possible choices?

- $8\cdot7\cdot6$
- $8\binom{7}{2}$
- $\binom{7}{2}$
- $\binom{8}{3}$

## Explanation

The introduction can be chosen in eight ways. For each introduction, choose an unordered pair from the seven remaining topics. The product is $8\binom{7}{2}$.
