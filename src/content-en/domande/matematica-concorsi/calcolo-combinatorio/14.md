How many anagrams of the Italian word PAPPA, including arrangements that do not form words, have the two As separated?

- $10$
- $4$
- $6$
- $20$

## Explanation

PAPPA has $5$ letters, with $3$ occurrences of P and $2$ of A, so the formula for **permutations with repeated objects** gives $\dfrac{5!}{3! \cdot 2!} = 10$ anagrams. If the two As are adjacent, treat AA as one block. Together with the three Ps, it makes $4$ items, giving $\dfrac{4!}{3!} = 4$ anagrams. By **counting with complements**, there are $10 - 4 = 6$ anagrams with the As separated. The result $10$ includes the anagrams with adjacent As, and $4$ is precisely the number of those anagrams. The result $20 = \dfrac{5!}{3!}$ accounts only for the repeated P.
