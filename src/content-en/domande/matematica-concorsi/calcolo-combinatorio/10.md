A concert features $4$ different pieces by Mozart and $3$ different pieces by Verdi, played one after the other. All the pieces by each composer must be played consecutively. How many different programs are possible?

- $144$
- $576$
- $288$
- $5040$

## Explanation

Mozart’s pieces form one block and Verdi’s form another. The two blocks can be performed in $2! = 2$ orders, with either Mozart or Verdi first. Within the Mozart block, the $4$ pieces can be arranged in $4! = 24$ ways. Within the Verdi block, the $3$ pieces can be arranged in $3! = 6$ ways. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $2 \cdot 24 \cdot 6 = 288$ programs. The result $144 = 4! \cdot 3!$ ignores the order of the blocks. The result $576 = 4! \cdot 4!$ requires only Mozart’s pieces to be consecutive, and $5040 = 7!$ ignores the restriction entirely.
