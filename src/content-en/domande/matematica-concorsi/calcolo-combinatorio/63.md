A ticket allows departure from any of $3$ stations. The first station serves $2$ routes, the second $5$, and the third $4$. On each route a standard seat or a panoramic seat can be chosen. How many distinct bookings are possible?

- $22$
- $24$
- $11$
- $18$

## Explanation

There are $2+5+4=11$ choices of station and route. Each can be paired with either seat type, giving $11\cdot2=22$ bookings.
