A soccer pool entry covers $5$ games. For each game one of three symbols, $1$, $X$, or $2$, is chosen. How many entries contain exactly $2$ occurrences of $X$?

- $10$
- $243$
- $80$
- $32$

## Explanation

Choose the $2$ games marked $X$ from the $5$ games in $\dbinom{5}{2} = 10$ ways, because only the games chosen matter, not the order in which they are selected. Each of the other $3$ games is marked $1$ or $2$, so they can be completed in $2^3 = 8$ ways. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $10 \cdot 8 = 80$ entries. The result $10$ omits the choices for the remaining games, $243 = 3^5$ counts all entries, and $32 = 2^5$ counts entries with no $X$.
