Six distinct case files must be arranged in a row. File A must come before B and B before C, with or without other files in between. How many orders are possible?

- $180$
- $60$
- $120$
- $240$

## Explanation

The three files A, B, and C have $3!=6$ relative orders, each occurring in the same number of overall arrangements. One sixth of the $6!$ orders meet the requirement, giving $120$.
