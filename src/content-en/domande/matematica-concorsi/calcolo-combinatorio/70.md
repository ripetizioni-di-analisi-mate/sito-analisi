Six distinct people can serve as president, vice president, and secretary, with at most one office per person. Only Ada and Luca are available to serve as president. In how many ways can the three offices be assigned?

- $48$
- $60$
- $20$
- $40$

## Explanation

There are two choices for president. Five people remain for vice president and four for secretary, giving $2\cdot5\cdot4=40$ assignments.
