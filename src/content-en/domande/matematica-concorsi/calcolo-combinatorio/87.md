Five distinct people occupy five numbered chairs in a row. Ada cannot sit in the first chair, and Bruno cannot sit in the fifth. How many seatings meet both restrictions?

- $96$
- $84$
- $78$
- $72$

## Explanation

From the $5!=120$ seatings, subtract the $4!=24$ with Ada in the first chair and the $24$ with Bruno in the fifth. Add back the $3!=6$ seatings that violate both restrictions once. The total is $120-24-24+6=78$.
