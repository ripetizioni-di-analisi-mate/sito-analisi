Five-digit numbers are formed using the digits $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, and $5$ exactly once each. In how many of these numbers does $1$ come before $2$, without necessarily being next to it?

- $120$
- $60$
- $48$
- $24$

## Explanation

The five-digit numbers are the **permutations** $P_5 = 5! = 120$. In a number where $1$ precedes $2$, swapping $1$ and $2$ gives a number where $2$ precedes $1$, and vice versa. The two cases therefore have the same size, so the desired count is $\dfrac{120}{2} = 60$. The result $48 = 4! \cdot 2$ counts numbers with $1$ and $2$ next to each other. The result $24 = 4!$ counts those where the digits are adjacent and $1$ precedes $2$.
