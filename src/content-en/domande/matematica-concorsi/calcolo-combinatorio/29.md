A combination lock has $3$ dials, each showing a digit from $0$ to $9$. How many codes have matching first and last digits?

- $1000$
- $100$
- $900$
- $90$

## Explanation

The first digit can be chosen in $10$ ways and the middle in $10$ ways. The last digit is fixed because it must match the first. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $10 \cdot 10 = 100$ codes. The result $1000 = 10^3$ counts all lock codes, and $900 = 1000 - 100$ counts codes whose first and last digits differ. The result $90 = 10 \cdot 9$ also requires the middle digit to differ from the other two.
