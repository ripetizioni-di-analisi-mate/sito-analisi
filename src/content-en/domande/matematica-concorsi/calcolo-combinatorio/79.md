A code consists of five characters, each A, B, or C. How many codes contain C at least once?

- $211$
- $32$
- $242$
- $80$

## Explanation

There are $3^5=243$ codes in total. Codes without C use only A and B, giving $2^5=32$ such codes. The **complement rule** leaves $243-32=211$.
