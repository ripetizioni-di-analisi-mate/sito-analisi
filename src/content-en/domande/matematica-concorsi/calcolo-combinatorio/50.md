An office must put a stack of $4$ distinct case files in order. How many different linear orders are possible?

- $12$
- $16$
- $64$
- $24$

## Explanation

There are four choices for the first position. Three, two, and one remain for the subsequent positions, so there are $4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1=24$ orders.
