A manager chooses $3$ different people from $9$ to form a working group without assigning roles. Which expression counts the possible groups?

- $\dfrac{9!}{3!}$
- $\binom{9}{3}$
- $9^3$
- $9\cdot8\cdot7$

## Explanation

The set of three people chosen determines the group. Order does not matter, so use the **binomial coefficient** $\binom{9}{3}$.
