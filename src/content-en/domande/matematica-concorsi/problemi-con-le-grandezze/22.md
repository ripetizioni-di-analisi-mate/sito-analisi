A car travels at a constant speed and covers $42$ km in $28$ minutes. How many kilometers does it travel at the same speed in $1$ hour and $10$ minutes?

- $16.8$ km
- $90$ km
- $99$ km
- $105$ km

## Explanation

At constant speed, distance is directly proportional to time. One hour and $10$ minutes equals $70$ minutes, so the distance is $42 \cdot \dfrac{70}{28} = 42 \cdot 2.5 = 105$ km. The result $99$ km multiplies the speed of $90$ km/h by $1.1$ h, an incorrect reading of $1$ hour and $10$ minutes. The result $16.8$ km sets up the proportion in reverse, giving $42 \cdot \dfrac{28}{70}$.
