A container holds $250\ \text{g}$ of a solution that is $24\%$ sugar by mass. How much water must you add to make the solution $15\%$ sugar by mass?

- $100\ \text{g}$
- $60\ \text{g}$
- $150\ \text{g}$
- $400\ \text{g}$

## Explanation

During **dilution**, the mass of sugar stays at $250\cdot0.24=60\ \text{g}$. For this to be $15\%$ of the total, the final mass must be $60\div0.15=400\ \text{g}$, so you need $150\ \text{g}$ of water.
