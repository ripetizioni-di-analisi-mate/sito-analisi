You mix $300\ \text{g}$ of a solution that is $12\%$ salt by mass with $200\ \text{g}$ of a solution that is $4\%$ salt by mass. What is the resulting percentage by mass?

- $8\%$
- $9.6\%$
- $16\%$
- $8.8\%$

## Explanation

The two solutions supply $36\ \text{g}$ and $8\ \text{g}$ of salt, respectively. The **percentage by mass** of salt in the mixture is $44\div500=0.088$, or $8.8\%$.
