A worker tiles a floor in $8$ hours at a constant rate. What fraction of the floor has the worker tiled after $3$ hours?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

The worker's **hourly rate** is $\dfrac{1}{8}$, so the worker tiles one-eighth of the floor each hour. In $3$ hours the worker tiles $3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{8} = \dfrac{3}{8}$ of it. The result $\dfrac{5}{8}$ is the fraction still to be tiled, and $\dfrac{1}{8}$ is the fraction tiled in just one hour.
