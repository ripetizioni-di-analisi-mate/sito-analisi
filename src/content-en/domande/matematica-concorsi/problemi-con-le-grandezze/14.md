A flight departs at $22{:}50$ and lands at $6{:}15$ the next day. The times are given in the same time zone. How long is the flight?

- $8$ h $25$ min
- $16$ h $35$ min
- $8$ h $5$ min
- $7$ h $25$ min

## Explanation

From $22{:}50$ to midnight is $1$ h $10$ min, and from midnight to $6{:}15$ is $6$ h $15$ min. The flight therefore lasts $1\ \text{h}\ 10\ \text{min} + 6\ \text{h}\ 15\ \text{min} = 7\ \text{h}\ 25\ \text{min}$. The result $16$ h $35$ min is the difference between $22{:}50$ and $6{:}15$, which measures the time from $6{:}15$ to $22{:}50$ on the same day. The result $8$ h $5$ min comes from counting $1$ h $50$ min from $22{:}50$ to midnight.
