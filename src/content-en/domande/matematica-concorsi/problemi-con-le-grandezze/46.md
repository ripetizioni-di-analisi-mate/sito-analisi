A setup uses a cable measuring $2.4\ \text{m}$ and an extension cord measuring $85\ \text{cm}$. What is their combined length?

- $305\ \text{cm}$
- $248.5\ \text{cm}$
- $325\ \text{cm}$
- $245\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

Using the **unit conversion** $1\ \text{m}=100\ \text{cm}$, the cable measures $240\ \text{cm}$. Adding $85\ \text{cm}$ gives $325\ \text{cm}$.
