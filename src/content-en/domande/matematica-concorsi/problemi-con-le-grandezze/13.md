A plumber charges $32$ euros per hour, and the cost of a service call is proportional to its duration. How much does a service call lasting $2$ hours and $45$ minutes cost?

- $88$ euros
- $64$ euros
- $96$ euros
- $78.40$ euros

## Explanation

Forty-five minutes is $\dfrac{45}{60} = \dfrac{3}{4}$ of an hour, so the service call lasts $2.75$ h. The cost is therefore $32 \cdot 2.75 = 88$ euros. The result $78.40$ euros reads the duration as $2.45$ h, while $96$ euros corresponds to $3$ full hours.
