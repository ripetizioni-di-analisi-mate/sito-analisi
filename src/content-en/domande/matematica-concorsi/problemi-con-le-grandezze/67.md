A vehicle covers the first $36\ \text{km}$ at $48\ \text{km/h}$ and the next $56\ \text{km}$ at $84\ \text{km/h}$. How long does the trip take without stops?

- $1$ hour and $25$ minutes
- $1$ hour and $15$ minutes
- $1$ hour and $30$ minutes
- $1$ hour and $45$ minutes

## Explanation

The **travel time** on each leg is distance divided by speed. The first leg takes $\dfrac34$ of an hour and the second takes $\dfrac23$ of an hour, or $45$ and $40$ minutes, respectively. The total is $85$ minutes.
