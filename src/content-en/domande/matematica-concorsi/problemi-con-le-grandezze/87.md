A container holds $600\ \text{g}$ of a solution that is $20\%$ salt by mass. You remove $150\ \text{g}$ of the solution and replace it with water. After mixing thoroughly, you repeat the same operation. What is the final percentage of salt by mass?

- $12.5\%$
- $10\%$
- $15\%$
- $11.25\%$

## Explanation

After each **replacement**, $75\%$ of the salt previously present remains, and the total mass returns to $600\ \text{g}$. The final concentration is therefore $20\%\cdot0.75^2=11.25\%$.
