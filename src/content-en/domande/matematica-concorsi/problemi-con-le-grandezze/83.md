Two vehicles leave opposite ends of a route $182\ \text{km}$ long and travel toward each other. The first leaves at 8\:00 at a speed of $52\ \text{km/h}$, and the second leaves at 8\:30 at a speed of $78\ \text{km/h}$. At what time do they meet?

- 9\:30
- 9\:42
- 9\:54
- 9\:24

## Explanation

When the second vehicle leaves, the first has already covered $26\ \text{km}$ and the remaining distance is $156\ \text{km}$. Their **closing speed** is $130\ \text{km/h}$, so they meet after $1.2$ hours, or $1$ hour and $12$ minutes after 8\:30.
