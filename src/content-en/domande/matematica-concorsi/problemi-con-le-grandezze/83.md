The kilometre markers on a 90 km route start at the western terminus. A westbound bus starts at marker 72 and travels 25 km, but a nearby sign reads “43 km”. From which terminus is that distance measured?

- From the western one
- From the eastern one
- From either terminus
- From neither terminus

## Explanation

The bus is $72-25=47$ km from the western terminus. Its distance from the eastern terminus is $90-47=43$ km.
