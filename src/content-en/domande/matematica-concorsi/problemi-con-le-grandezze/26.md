Two runners start at the same time from the same point on a circular track $400$ m long. They run in the same direction at constant speeds of $5$ m/s and $3$ m/s. How long does it take the faster runner to lap the other, catching up after completing one extra lap?

- $2$ min
- $3$ min $20$ s
- $1$ min $20$ s
- $50$ s

## Explanation

The faster runner laps the other after covering $400$ m more than the other runner. Each second the faster runner gains $5 - 3 = 2$ m, so, as in a **catch-up problem**, this takes $\dfrac{400}{2} = 200$ seconds. Since $200 = 3 \cdot 60 + 20$, the time is $3$ min $20$ s. The result $2$ min treats $200$ seconds as if a minute contained $100$ seconds. The result $50$ s adds the speeds, which applies to two objects moving in opposite directions.
