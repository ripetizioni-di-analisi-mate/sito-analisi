A train $120$ m long travels at a constant speed of $72$ km/h and crosses a bridge $240$ m long. How much time elapses from the moment the front of the train reaches the bridge until the rear clears it?

- $6$ s
- $18$ s
- $5$ s
- $12$ s

## Explanation

To **convert between km/h and m/s** in the direction from km/h to m/s, divide by $3.6$. The train is traveling at $\dfrac{72}{3.6} = 20$ m/s. When the rear of the train clears the bridge, the front has traveled the length of the bridge plus the length of the train, or $240 + 120 = 360$ m. The time is therefore $\dfrac{360}{20} = 18$ s. The result $12$ s uses only the length of the bridge, and $5$ s divides $360$ by $72$ without converting the speed.
