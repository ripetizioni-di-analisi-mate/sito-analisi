A flat terrace with an area of $50\ \text{m}^2$ receives $12$ mm of rain, enough to form a layer of water $12$ mm deep over the terrace. All this water is collected in a tank. How many liters are collected?

- $600$ liters
- $60$ liters
- $0.6$ liters
- $6000$ liters

## Explanation

The water forms a rectangular prism with a base area of $50\ \text{m}^2$ and a height of $12$ mm. The calculation is simpler in decimeters because the numerical value of the volume in $\text{dm}^3$ then equals the capacity in liters. Since $1\ \text{m}^2 = 100\ \text{dm}^2$ and $12\ \text{mm} = 0.12$ dm, the volume is $5000 \cdot 0.12 = 600\ \text{dm}^3$, or $600$ liters. The result $0.6$ liters reads the volume $0.6\ \text{m}^3$ as if it were in liters, and $60$ liters comes from $1\ \text{m}^2 = 10\ \text{dm}^2$.
