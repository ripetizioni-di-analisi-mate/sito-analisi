A syrup is $64\%$ sugar by mass. How many grams of sugar are in $250$ g of syrup?

- $90$ g
- $64$ g
- $160$ g
- $16$ g

## Explanation

The **concentration** is the ratio of the mass of sugar to the mass of syrup, expressed as a percentage. The sugar therefore makes up $64\%$ of $250$ g, or $0.64 \cdot 250 = 160$ g. The result $90$ g is the mass of the rest of the syrup, $250 - 160$, while $16$ g uses $0.064$ instead of $0.64$.
