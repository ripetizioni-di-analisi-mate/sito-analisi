A printer warms up for 4 minutes and then prints at a constant rate. It produces 72 sheets in the first 10 minutes after switching on. How many sheets does it produce in the next 5 minutes if it stays on?

- $36$
- $40$
- $60$
- $72$

## Explanation

Printing occupies $10-4=6$ minutes, so the rate is 12 sheets per minute. The next five minutes produce $5\cdot12=60$ sheets.
