A machine packages $96$ parcels in $24$ minutes at a constant rate. How many parcels does it package in one hour?

- $288$
- $192$
- $240$
- $216$

## Explanation

The **production rate** is $96\div24=4$ parcels per minute. In $60$ minutes the machine packages $4\cdot60=240$ parcels.
