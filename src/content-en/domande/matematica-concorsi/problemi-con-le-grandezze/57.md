A printer takes $4$ minutes to warm up and then prints at a constant rate. In the first $10$ minutes after being switched on it produces $72$ sheets. How many sheets does it produce in the next $5$ minutes, without being switched off?

- $36$
- $12$
- $60$
- $90$

## Explanation

During the first $4$ minutes the printer does not print, so the $72$ sheets are produced in $10 - 4 = 6$ minutes. The **rate** is therefore $\dfrac{72}{6} = 12$ sheets per minute. In the next $5$ minutes the printer is already warm, so it produces $5 \cdot 12 = 60$ sheets. The value $36$ divides the $72$ sheets by all $10$ minutes, and the value $12$ subtracts the $4$ warm-up minutes again from the next $5$ minutes.
