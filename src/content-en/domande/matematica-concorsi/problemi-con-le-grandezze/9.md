A rectangular floor measures $4.5$ m by $3.6$ m. It is covered with square tiles with sides of $30$ cm, without cutting any of the tiles. How many tiles are needed?

- $54$
- $1800$
- $180$
- $18$

## Explanation

The floor has an area of $4.5 \cdot 3.6 = 16.2\ \text{m}^2$, and each tile has an area of $30 \cdot 30 = 900\ \text{cm}^2$. Since $1\ \text{m}^2 = 10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$, the floor measures $162\,000\ \text{cm}^2$, so it takes $\dfrac{162\,000}{900} = 180$ tiles. Counting the tiles along the two sides gives the same result. The counts are $\dfrac{450}{30} = 15$ and $\dfrac{360}{30} = 12$, and $15 \cdot 12 = 180$. The result $54$ divides the floor area in square meters, $16.2$, by the tile's side length in meters, $0.3$, instead of by its area.
