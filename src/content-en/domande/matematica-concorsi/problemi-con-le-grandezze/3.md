Which of the following lengths is greatest?

- $0.08$ km
- $750$ dm
- $8100$ cm
- $0.79$ hm

## Explanation

To compare the four lengths, express them all in meters. There are three steps from kilometers down to meters, so $0.08$ km $= 80$ m, and two steps from hectometers down to meters, so $0.79$ hm $= 79$ m. Moving up to meters takes one step from decimeters and two steps from centimeters, so $750$ dm $= 75$ m and $8100$ cm $= 81$ m. The greatest length is therefore $8100$ cm.
