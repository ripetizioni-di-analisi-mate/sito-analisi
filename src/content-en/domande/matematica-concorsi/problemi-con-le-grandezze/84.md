One printer completes a print run in $5$ hours and another can complete it alone in $8$ hours. They run together for $2$ hours, then only the second printer continues. How long does the second phase take?

- $2$ hours and $48$ minutes
- $2$ hours and $24$ minutes
- $3$ hours and $12$ minutes
- $1$ hour and $45$ minutes

## Explanation

The **fraction of the job** completed in the first phase is $2\left(\dfrac15+\dfrac18\right)=\dfrac{13}{20}$. The second printer must finish $\dfrac7{20}$ of the print run. It takes $\dfrac7{20}\cdot8=2.8$ hours, or $2$ hours and $48$ minutes.
