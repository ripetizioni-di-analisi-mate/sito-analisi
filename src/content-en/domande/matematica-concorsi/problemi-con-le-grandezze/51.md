A stopwatch shows $5\,490$ seconds. What is this duration in hours, minutes, and seconds?

- $1$ hour, $54$ minutes, and $9$ seconds
- $1$ hour, $49$ minutes, and $0$ seconds
- $1$ hour, $31$ minutes, and $30$ seconds
- $1$ hour, $29$ minutes, and $50$ seconds

## Explanation

One hour equals $3\,600$ seconds, leaving $1\,890$. In the **base-sixty system**, $1\,890=31\cdot60+30$, so the duration is $1$ hour, $31$ minutes, and $30$ seconds.
