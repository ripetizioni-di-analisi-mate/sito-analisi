At a factory, one machine packages $1200$ items in $5$ hours and a second machine packages $1200$ items in $3$ hours. Both run at constant rates. How many items do they package together in $1$ hour and $30$ minutes?

- $960$ items
- $832$ items
- $640$ items
- $225$ items

## Explanation

The first machine packages $\dfrac{1200}{5} = 240$ items per hour and the second packages $\dfrac{1200}{3} = 400$ items per hour, so together they package $640$ per hour. One hour and $30$ minutes equals $1.5$ h, giving $640 \cdot 1.5 = 960$ items. The result $832$ reads the duration as $1.3$ h, while $225$ adds the times as though the two machines together took $5 + 3 = 8$ hours to package $1200$ items.
