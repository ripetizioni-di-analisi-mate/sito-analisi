A canister holding $6\ \text{L}$ is used to fill bottles that hold $750\ \text{mL}$ each. How many whole bottles can be filled?

- $8$
- $10$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

To compare the **capacities**, express both in milliliters. The canister holds $6\,000\ \text{mL}$, and $6\,000\div750=8$.
