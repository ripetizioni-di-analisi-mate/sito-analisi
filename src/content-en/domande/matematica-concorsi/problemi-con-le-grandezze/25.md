At $8{:}00$, a train leaves station A for station B, $330$ km away, at a constant speed of $60$ km/h. At $8{:}30$, a second train leaves B for A on a parallel track at a constant speed of $90$ km/h. At what time do the two trains pass each other?

- $10{:}42$
- $10{:}00$
- $10{:}30$
- $10{:}12$

## Explanation

By $8{:}30$, the first train has traveled $60 \cdot 0.5 = 30$ km, leaving $330 - 30 = 300$ km between the trains. From then on, the distance decreases by $60 + 90 = 150$ km per hour, so the **time until they meet** is $\dfrac{300}{150} = 2$ hours. Count the $2$ hours from $8{:}30$, giving $10{:}30$. The results $10{:}12$ and $10{:}42$ use the full distance of $330$ km. This gives $2.2$ h, or $2$ h $12$ min, counted from $8{:}00$ or from $8{:}30$.
