A van maintains a speed of $54\ \text{km/h}$ for $20$ minutes. How far does it travel?

- $18\ \text{km}$
- $27\ \text{km}$
- $10.8\ \text{km}$
- $16.2\ \text{km}$

## Explanation

Twenty minutes is $\dfrac13$ of an hour. At **constant speed**, distance is speed times elapsed time, giving $54\cdot\dfrac13=18\ \text{km}$.
