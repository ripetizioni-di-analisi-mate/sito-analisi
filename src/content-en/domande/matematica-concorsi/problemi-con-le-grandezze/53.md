An odometer displays whole kilometres and advances when a kilometre is completed. Its readings at the start and end of a trip are 105 and 112. Which distance in kilometres is possible?

- $7.4$
- $6$
- $8$
- $8.2$

## Explanation

The actual readings lie in $[105,106)$ and $[112,113)$. Their difference is strictly between 6 and 8; for example, $112.6-105.2=7.4$.
