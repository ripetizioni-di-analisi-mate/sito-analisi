A faucet fills an empty tank in $6$ hours. After $2$ hours, a second faucet is opened, and the tank is full one hour later. Both faucets supply water at constant rates. How long would the second faucet take to fill the empty tank on its own?

- $1$ h $30$ min
- $4$ h
- $3$ h
- $2$ h

## Explanation

In the first $2$ hours, the first faucet fills $\dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ of the tank, so in the last hour the two faucets together fill the remaining $\dfrac{2}{3}$. The first faucet supplies $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the tank's capacity per hour, so the second supplies $\dfrac{2}{3} - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ in that same hour. The second faucet's **hourly rate** is therefore $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and it would fill the tank alone in $2$ hours. The result $1$ h $30$ min credits the second faucet alone with the $\dfrac{2}{3}$ supplied during the last hour.
