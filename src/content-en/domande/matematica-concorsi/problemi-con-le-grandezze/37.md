A $400$ g saltwater solution is $5\%$ salt by mass. You add $100$ g of salt, which dissolves completely. What is the new salt concentration?

- $25\%$
- $24\%$
- $30\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The initial solution contains $0.05 \cdot 400 = 20$ g of salt. After you add the salt, there are $20 + 100 = 120$ g of salt in $400 + 100 = 500$ g of solution, so the **concentration** is $\dfrac{120}{500} \cdot 100\% = 24\%$. The result $30\%$ divides $120$ g by $400$ g and ignores the increase in the mass of the solution. The result $25\%$ adds the initial $5\%$ to the $20\%$ that the added salt represents as a percentage of $500$ g.
