A currency exchange office converts $260$ euros at a rate of $1.20$ dollars per euro and deducts a flat fee of $10$ euros before the conversion. How many dollars does it pay out?

- $325$
- $302$
- $300$
- $312$

## Explanation

After the **fee**, $260-10=250$ euros remain to be converted. At the given rate, this gives $250\cdot1.20=300$ dollars.
