A measuring cylinder reads $420\,\mathrm{mL}$. After three identical cubes are completely immersed it reads $444\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is the edge length of each cube in centimetres?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The cubes displace $24\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ altogether, or 8 per cube. The edge length is $\sqrt[3]{8}=2$ centimetres.
