A crate has an internal volume of $18\,000\ \text{cm}^3$. How many cubic decimeters is this?

- $18\ \text{dm}^3$
- $1\,800\ \text{dm}^3$
- $1.8\ \text{dm}^3$
- $180\ \text{dm}^3$

## Explanation

Since $1\ \text{dm} = 10\ \text{cm}$, when **converting volumes** we have $1\ \text{dm}^3 = 10 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = 1\,000\ \text{cm}^3$. Hence $18\,000\ \text{cm}^3$ correspond to $\dfrac{18\,000}{1\,000} = 18\ \text{dm}^3$. The value $1\,800\ \text{dm}^3$ comes from the factor $10$ used for lengths, and the value $180\ \text{dm}^3$ from the factor $100$ used for areas.
