A crate has an internal volume of $18\,000\ \text{cm}^3$. How many cubic decimeters is this?

- $18\ \text{dm}^3$
- $1\,800\ \text{dm}^3$
- $1.8\ \text{dm}^3$
- $180\ \text{dm}^3$

## Explanation

When converting **units of volume**, $1\ \text{dm}^3=1\,000\ \text{cm}^3$ because $1\ \text{dm}=10\ \text{cm}$. Divide $18\,000$ by $1\,000$ to obtain $18\ \text{dm}^3$.
