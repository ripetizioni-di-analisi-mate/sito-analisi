A full tank empties through its drain in $3$ hours. A faucet fills the tank from empty in $5$ hours. Both operate at constant rates. If the tank starts full and the drain and faucet are opened together, how long does it take to empty?

- $2$ h
- $7$ h $30$ min
- $7$ h $50$ min
- $4$ h

## Explanation

Each hour the drain removes $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the tank's capacity and the faucet adds $\dfrac{1}{5}$. As with any **faucet and drain open together**, subtract the two rates. The tank loses $\dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{2}{15}$ of its volume each hour. It therefore empties in $\dfrac{15}{2} = 7.5$ h, or $7$ h $30$ min. The result $2$ h is the difference between the times, while $7$ h $50$ min reads $7.5$ h as $7$ hours and $50$ minutes.
