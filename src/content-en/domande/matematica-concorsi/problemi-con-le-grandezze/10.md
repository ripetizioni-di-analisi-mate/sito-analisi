A cube-shaped tank holds exactly $64$ liters when full. What is its inside edge length?

- $80$ cm
- $40$ cm
- $400$ cm
- $4$ cm

## Explanation

One liter is one cubic decimeter, so the inside volume of the cube is $64\ \text{dm}^3$. The edge length in decimeters is the number whose cube is $64$. That number is $4$, since $4 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 64$. The edge therefore measures $4$ dm, or $40$ cm. The result $4$ cm treats decimeters as centimeters, while $80$ cm takes the square root of $64$ instead of the cube root.
