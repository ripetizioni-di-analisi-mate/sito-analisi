An exam starts at $9{:}40$ and has three sections of $50$ minutes each, separated by two breaks of $15$ minutes each. At what time does the exam end?

- $12{:}40$
- $12{:}55$
- $12{:}10$
- $12{:}00$

## Explanation

The three sections total $3 \cdot 50 = 150$ minutes and the two breaks total $2 \cdot 15 = 30$ minutes, so the exam lasts $180$ minutes, or $3$ hours. Adding $3$ hours to $9{:}40$ gives $12{:}40$. The result $12{:}10$ ignores the breaks, and $12{:}55$ counts three breaks instead of two.
