A warehouse starts with $32$ parcels. A machine produces $18$ parcels per hour and an employee ships $10$ per hour, both at constant rates. After $3$ hours the machine stops, and shipping continues at the same rate. How much longer can the employee keep shipping parcels?

- $5$ hours and $36$ minutes
- $5$ hours and $6$ minutes
- $4$ hours and $24$ minutes
- $3$ hours and $12$ minutes

## Explanation

Over the first three hours, the **net change** is an increase of $(18-10)\cdot3=24$ parcels, bringing the inventory to $56$. At a rate of $10$ parcels per hour, this lasts $5.6$ hours, or $5$ hours and $36$ minutes.
