A cyclist reaches a speed of $18$ m/s in a sprint. What is this speed in km/h?

- $5$ km/h
- $6.48$ km/h
- $1080$ km/h
- $64.8$ km/h

## Explanation

To **convert between km/h and m/s** in the direction from m/s to km/h, multiply by $3.6$. Thus $18$ m/s equals $18 \cdot 3.6 = 64.8$ km/h. In fact, in one hour, or $3600$ seconds, the cyclist would travel $18 \cdot 3600 = 64\,800$ m, or $64.8$ km. The result $5$ km/h divides by $3.6$, which converts in the opposite direction. It can be ruled out because the numerical value of the cyclist's speed is always greater in km/h than in m/s.
