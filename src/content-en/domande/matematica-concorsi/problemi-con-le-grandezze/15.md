A training course for municipal employees consists of $12$ classes, each lasting $1$ hour and $45$ minutes. What is the total duration of the course?

- $12$ h $45$ min
- $21$ h
- $17$ h $24$ min
- $17$ h $40$ min

## Explanation

One class lasts $60 + 45 = 105$ minutes, so the $12$ classes last $12 \cdot 105 = 1260$ minutes. Since an hour contains $60$ minutes, $1260$ minutes equals $\dfrac{1260}{60} = 21$ hours. The result $17$ h $24$ min treats one class as $1.45$ h, since $12 \cdot 1.45 = 17.4$ h. The result $17$ h $40$ min converts $12 \cdot 45 = 540$ minutes to $5$ h $40$ min, as though an hour contained $100$ minutes.
