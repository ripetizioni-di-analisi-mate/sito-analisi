A tank contains $0.75\ \text{m}^3$ of diesel fuel. How many liters is this?

- $75$ liters
- $75\,000$ liters
- $750$ liters
- $7500$ liters

## Explanation

A cubic meter contains $1000$ cubic decimeters, and a cubic decimeter is one liter. The relationship between **liters and cubic decimeters** therefore gives $1\ \text{m}^3 = 1000$ liters. The tank holds $0.75 \cdot 1000 = 750$ liters. The result $75$ liters uses $100$ instead of $1000$, as though the conversion factor for each step were the same for volume as for area.
