A bottle of eye drops contains $0.45$ dl of liquid. How many milliliters is this?

- $4500$ ml
- $45$ ml
- $450$ ml
- $4.5$ ml

## Explanation

Moving from deciliters to milliliters takes two steps down the metric scale (deciliters, centiliters, milliliters), each with a factor of $10$. To **convert these units of capacity**, multiply by $100$, moving the decimal point two places to the right. Thus $0.45$ dl equals $45$ ml. The result $4.5$ ml comes from moving the decimal point only one place, as if deciliters and milliliters were one step apart.
