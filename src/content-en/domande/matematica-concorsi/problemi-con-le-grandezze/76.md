A liquid has a density of $0.84\ \text{kg/L}$. What volume in liters does $3\,360\ \text{g}$ of this liquid occupy?

- $3.36$
- $40$
- $2.82$
- $4$

## Explanation

The mass is $3.36\ \text{kg}$. From the formula for **density**, divide the mass by $0.84\ \text{kg/L}$ to obtain a volume of $4\ \text{L}$.
