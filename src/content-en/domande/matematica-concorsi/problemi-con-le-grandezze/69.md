A ferry departs every 18 minutes from 08:00. The walk from the station takes 7 minutes. If a train arrives at 08:46, how many minutes will its passengers wait at the pier for the first ferry they can catch?

- $7$
- $8$
- $11$
- $1$

## Explanation

The passengers reach the pier at 08:53. The surrounding departures are at 08:36 and 08:54, leaving a one-minute wait.
