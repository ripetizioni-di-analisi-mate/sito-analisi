A van has a head start of $18\ \text{km}$ on a car and travels at $72\ \text{km/h}$. The car travels in the same direction at $96\ \text{km/h}$. How many minutes does the car take to catch up?

- $30$
- $75$
- $60$
- $45$

## Explanation

Since the two vehicles travel in the same direction, the distance between them decreases each hour by $96 - 72 = 24$ km, that is by the **relative speed**. Closing the $18$ km head start therefore takes $\dfrac{18}{24} = \dfrac{3}{4}$ of an hour, that is $45$ minutes. The value $75$ comes from reading $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of an hour, that is $0.75$ hours, as $75$ minutes.
