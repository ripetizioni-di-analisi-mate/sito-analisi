A device draws a constant power of $800\ \text{W}$ for $45$ minutes. How much does it cost to run at a rate of $0.30$ euros per kilowatt-hour?

- $0.18$ euros
- $0.36$ euros
- $0.24$ euros
- $0.60$ euros

## Explanation

The **energy consumed** is the power of $0.8\ \text{kW}$ multiplied by the duration of $0.75$ hours, giving $0.6\ \text{kWh}$. The cost is $0.6\cdot0.30=0.18$ euros.
