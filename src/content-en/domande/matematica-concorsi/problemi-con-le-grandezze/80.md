A meter records $3\,\mathrm{kWh}$ over 5 hours. A $100\,\mathrm W$ lamp is on throughout; the only other load is a $1\,\mathrm{kW}$ appliance. How long was the appliance on?

- $2.5\,\mathrm h$
- $3\,\mathrm h$
- $0.5\,\mathrm h$
- $4.5\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

The lamp uses $0.1\cdot5=0.5$ kWh. The remaining $2.5$ kWh take $2.5$ hours at one kilowatt.
