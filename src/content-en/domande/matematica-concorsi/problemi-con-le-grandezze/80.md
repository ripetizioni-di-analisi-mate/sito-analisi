A device draws a constant power of $800\ \text{W}$ for $45$ minutes. How much does it cost to run at a rate of $0.30$ euros per kilowatt-hour?

- $0.18$ euros
- $0.36$ euros
- $0.24$ euros
- $0.60$ euros

## Explanation

The power of $800\ \text{W}$ is $0.8\ \text{kW}$ and the duration of $45$ minutes is $0.75$ hours. The **energy consumed** is the product of power and time, so it equals $0.8 \cdot 0.75 = 0.6\ \text{kWh}$. Therefore the cost is $0.6 \cdot 0.30 = 0.18$ euros. The value $0.24$ euros is the cost of a whole hour of operation instead of $45$ minutes.
