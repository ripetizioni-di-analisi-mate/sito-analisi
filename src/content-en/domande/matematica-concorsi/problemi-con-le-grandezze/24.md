Marco leaves home at $7{:}30$ and walks at a constant speed of $5$ km/h. At $7{:}48$, his sister sets out from home on her bicycle and follows him along the same road at a constant speed of $14$ km/h. At what time does she catch up with Marco?

- $8{:}04$
- $7{:}54$
- $7{:}40$
- $7{:}58$

## Explanation

Eighteen minutes equals $\dfrac{18}{60} = 0.3$ h, so by $7{:}48$ Marco has a head start of $5 \cdot 0.3 = 1.5$ km. His sister gains $14 - 5 = 9$ km per hour, so the **catch-up time** is $\dfrac{1.5}{9} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ h, or $10$ minutes. Count the $10$ minutes from the time his sister leaves, giving $7{:}58$. The result $8{:}04$ reads $\dfrac{1}{6}$ h, or $0.16\ldots$ h, as $16$ minutes. The result $7{:}40$ adds the $10$ minutes to Marco's departure time.
