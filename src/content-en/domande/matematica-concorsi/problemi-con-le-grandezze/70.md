A conveyor belt moves at $0.8\ \text{m/s}$. How many seconds pass from the time the front of a package $40\ \text{cm}$ long reaches the entrance of a scanner $2\ \text{m}$ long until the rear of the package clears the scanner?

- $2$
- $2.5$
- $3$
- $3.5$

## Explanation

To clear the scanner, the package must travel the **combined length** of the scanner and the package, which is $2.4\ \text{m}$. At $0.8\ \text{m/s}$, this takes $2.4\div0.8=3$ seconds.
