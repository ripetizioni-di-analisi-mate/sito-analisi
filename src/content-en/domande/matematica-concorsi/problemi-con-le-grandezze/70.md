Packages on a conveyor have their centres $0.8$ m apart. The belt runs at $0.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How many seconds elapse between the first and tenth package centres passing a mark?

- $20$
- $16$
- $18$
- $22$

## Explanation

Ten centres span nine gaps. Each gap takes 2 seconds, so the elapsed time is 18 seconds.
