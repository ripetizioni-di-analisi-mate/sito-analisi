On a conveyor belt the parcels are placed with their centres $0.8$ m apart, and the belt moves at $0.4$ m/s. How many seconds pass between the centre of the first parcel and the centre of the tenth parcel passing the same point?

- $20$
- $4.5$
- $18$
- $7.2$

## Explanation

From the first to the tenth parcel there are $9$ gaps between consecutive centres, so the belt must move $9 \cdot 0.8 = 7.2$ m. At $0.4$ m/s this displacement takes $\dfrac{7.2}{0.4} = 18$ s. The value $20$ counts $10$ gaps instead of $9$, and the value $7.2$ is the distance in metres read as a time.
