Two cyclists start at the same time from points $28\ \text{km}$ apart and ride toward each other at $18\ \text{km/h}$ and $24\ \text{km/h}$. How far from the slower cyclist's starting point do they meet?

- $18\ \text{km}$
- $12\ \text{km}$
- $16\ \text{km}$
- $14\ \text{km}$

## Explanation

Their **closing speed** is $18+24=42\ \text{km/h}$, so they meet after $\dfrac{28}{42}=\dfrac23$ of an hour. In that time, the slower cyclist covers $18\cdot\dfrac23=12\ \text{km}$.
