A boat, with its engine always at the same setting, travels $24$ km upstream on a river in $3$ hours and the same $24$ km downstream in $2$ hours. What is the speed of the current?

- $4$ km/h
- $2$ km/h
- $10$ km/h
- $12$ km/h

## Explanation

Going upstream the boat moves at $\dfrac{24}{3} = 8$ km/h, that is its speed in still water minus the speed of the current. Going downstream it moves at $\dfrac{24}{2} = 12$ km/h, that is its speed in still water plus the speed of the current. The difference $12 - 8 = 4$ km/h is therefore twice the speed of the current, so the current flows at $2$ km/h. The value $4$ km/h does not divide the difference by two, and the value $10$ km/h is the speed of the boat in still water.
