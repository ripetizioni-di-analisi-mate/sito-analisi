A track has consecutive sections of 400 and 600 m, originally covered in 80 and 120 s. A worker now reaches the end of the first section 10 s earlier while finishing at the original time. What is the new average speed on the second section?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{60}{13}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{60}{11}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The second section now takes $120+10=130$ seconds. Its average speed is $\dfrac{600}{130}=\dfrac{60}{13}$ metres per second.
