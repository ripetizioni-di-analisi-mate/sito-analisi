A container holds $20$ liters of a drink that is $30\%$ fruit juice by volume. You remove $5$ liters of the drink and replace it with $5$ liters of water. What percentage of the drink is now juice?

- $25\%$
- $7.5\%$
- $22.5\%$
- $24\%$

## Explanation

Removing $5$ liters leaves $15$ liters of a drink that is $30\%$ juice and contains $0.3 \cdot 15 = 4.5$ liters of juice. Adding water brings the volume back to $20$ liters, so the **concentration** is $\dfrac{4.5}{20} \cdot 100\% = 22.5\%$. The result $24\%$ adds the $5$ liters of water without first removing any of the drink, since $\dfrac{6}{25} = 24\%$. The result $25\%$ subtracts $5$ percentage points from $30\%$.
