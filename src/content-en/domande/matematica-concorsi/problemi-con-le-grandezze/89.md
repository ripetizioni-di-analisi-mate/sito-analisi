An office needs exactly $408$ dollars. Exchange counter A offers $1.20$ dollars per euro and charges $8$ euros in addition to the amount converted. Counter B offers $1.25$ dollars per euro and charges $18$ euros in the same way. How much can the office save by choosing the less expensive counter?

- $13.60$ euros
- $0.40$ euros
- $3.60$ euros
- $10$ euros

## Explanation

The **total cost** at A is $408\div1.20+8=348$ euros. At B it is $408\div1.25+18=344.40$ euros, so choosing B saves $3.60$ euros.
