Two faucets running together fill a tank in $4$ hours. The first faucet fills it alone in $6$ hours. Both supply water at constant rates. How many hours does the second faucet take to fill the tank on its own?

- $2$ hours
- $12$ hours
- $5$ hours
- $10$ hours

## Explanation

Together the two faucets fill $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the tank each hour, and the first fills $\dfrac{1}{6}$ on its own. Since the **hourly rates** add, the second faucet fills $\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{3}{12} - \dfrac{2}{12} = \dfrac{1}{12}$ of the tank each hour and takes $12$ hours on its own. The result $2$ hours is the difference between the times, $6 - 4$. It can be ruled out because one faucet alone takes longer than the two running together.
