Which of the following speeds is greatest?

- $75$ km/h
- $1150$ m/min
- $20$ m/s
- $1.3$ km/min

## Explanation

To compare the speeds, express them all in km/h. One minute is $\dfrac{1}{60}$ of an hour, so $1.3$ km/min equals $1.3 \cdot 60 = 78$ km/h, and $1150$ m/min, or $1.15$ km/min, equals $69$ km/h. To **convert between km/h and m/s** in the direction needed here, multiply by $3.6$, so $20$ m/s equals $72$ km/h. The greatest speed is therefore $1.3$ km/min.
