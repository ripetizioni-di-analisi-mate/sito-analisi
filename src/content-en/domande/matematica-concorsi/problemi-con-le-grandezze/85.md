An initially empty tank receives water at $18\ \text{L/min}$ until it contains $144\ \text{L}$. At that point a drain is opened at a rate of $6\ \text{L/min}$ and the inlet stays open. How much time elapses from the start until the tank contains $240\ \text{L}$?

- $20$ minutes
- $14$ minutes
- $16$ minutes
- $12$ minutes

## Explanation

The first phase takes $144\div18=8$ minutes. In the second phase the **net flow rate** is $12\ \text{L/min}$, and it takes another $96\div12=8$ minutes. The total is $16$ minutes.
