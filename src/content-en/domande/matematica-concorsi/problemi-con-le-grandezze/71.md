A tank contains $48\ \text{L}$. A pump supplies water at $14\ \text{L/min}$ and a valve drains water at $6\ \text{L/min}$. With both running continuously, how many minutes does it take for the tank to contain $160\ \text{L}$?

- $20$
- $8$
- $14$
- $12$

## Explanation

The **net flow rate** is $14-6=8\ \text{L/min}$. The volume must increase by $160-48=112\ \text{L}$, so it takes $112\div8=14$ minutes.
