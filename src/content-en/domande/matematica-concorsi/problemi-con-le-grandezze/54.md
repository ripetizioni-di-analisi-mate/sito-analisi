An employee walks $1\,500\ \text{m}$ in $18$ minutes at a constant pace. What is the employee's speed in kilometers per hour?

- $5\ \text{km/h}$
- $3\ \text{km/h}$
- $4.5\ \text{km/h}$
- $8.3\ \text{km/h}$

## Explanation

**Speed** is the ratio of distance to time. Converting the measurements gives $1.5\ \text{km}$ in $0.3$ hours, so the speed is $5\ \text{km/h}$.
