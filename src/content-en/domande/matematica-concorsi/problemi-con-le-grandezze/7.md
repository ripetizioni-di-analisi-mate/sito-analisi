A placemat has an area of $0.6\ \text{m}^2$. How many square centimeters is this?

- $60\ \text{cm}^2$
- $600\ \text{cm}^2$
- $6000\ \text{cm}^2$
- $60\,000\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Moving from square meters to square centimeters takes two steps. For areas, each step has a factor of $100$. To **convert the area**, multiply by $100 \cdot 100 = 10\,000$, moving the decimal point four places to the right to obtain $6000\ \text{cm}^2$. The result $60\ \text{cm}^2$ uses a factor of $10$ per step, as for lengths.
