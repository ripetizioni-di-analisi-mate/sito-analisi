A sample with a mass of $135\ \text{g}$ occupies $50\ \text{cm}^3$. What is its density?

- $0.37\ \text{g/cm}^3$
- $2.7\ \text{g/cm}^3$
- $6.75\ \text{g/cm}^3$
- $1.35\ \text{g/cm}^3$

## Explanation

**Density** is the ratio of mass to volume. Dividing $135$ by $50$ gives $2.7\ \text{g/cm}^3$.
