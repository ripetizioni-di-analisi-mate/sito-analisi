A service counter is open for $4$ hours and $20$ minutes, including a break of $35$ minutes. What is the greatest number of consecutive $18$-minute appointments it can schedule in the remaining time?

- $11$
- $14$
- $12$
- $13$

## Explanation

After the break, $260-35=225$ minutes remain. **Division with remainder** gives $225=12\cdot18+9$, so the counter can schedule $12$ full appointments.
