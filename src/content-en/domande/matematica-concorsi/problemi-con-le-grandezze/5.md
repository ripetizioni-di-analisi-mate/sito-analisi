A dose of syrup is $2.5$ ml, taken three times a day. For how many days will a $1.5$ dl bottle last?

- $200$ days
- $60$ days
- $2$ days
- $20$ days

## Explanation

Moving from deciliters to milliliters takes two steps down the scale, so the bottle contains $1.5 \cdot 100 = 150$ ml. The daily amount is $3 \cdot 2.5 = 7.5$ ml, so the bottle lasts $\dfrac{150}{7.5} = 20$ days. The result $60$ is the number of doses in the bottle. The results $2$ and $200$ come from taking one step too few or too many in the conversion.
