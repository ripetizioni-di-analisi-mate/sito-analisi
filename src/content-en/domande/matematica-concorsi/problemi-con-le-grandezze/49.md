What area in square meters is equivalent to $25\,000\ \text{cm}^2$?

- $2.5\ \text{m}^2$
- $250\ \text{m}^2$
- $25\ \text{m}^2$
- $0.25\ \text{m}^2$

## Explanation

When converting **units of area**, square the factor of $100$ between meters and centimeters. Divide $25\,000$ by $10\,000$ to obtain $2.5\ \text{m}^2$.
