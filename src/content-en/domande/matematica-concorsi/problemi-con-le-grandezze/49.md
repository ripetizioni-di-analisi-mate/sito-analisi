A scale drawing shows a plot as a rectangle measuring $2$ by $5$ cm. Its actual area is $1000\,\mathrm{m^2}$. How many actual metres correspond to one centimetre on the drawing?

- $10$
- $100$
- $20$
- $5$

## Explanation

Ten square centimetres represent one thousand square metres. Each square centimetre represents 100 square metres, so the linear factor is $\sqrt{100}=10$.
