A warehouse buys $18\ \text{m}$ of rope at $2.50$ euros per meter and pays $6$ euros for delivery. How much does it spend?

- $45$ euros
- $51$ euros
- $48$ euros
- $54$ euros

## Explanation

Multiply the **unit cost** by the length purchased to obtain $2.50\cdot18=45$ euros. With delivery, the total is $51$ euros.
