An air conditioner uses $1.2$ kWh of electricity per hour of operation. It runs for $6$ hours a day for $30$ days, and electricity costs $0.25$ euros per kWh. How much does it cost to run the air conditioner for those $30$ days?

- $864$ euros
- $1.80$ euros
- $54$ euros
- $216$ euros

## Explanation

The problem involves a **chain of ratios**. Apply one ratio at a time. The air conditioner uses $1.2 \cdot 6 = 7.2$ kWh in one day, so in $30$ days it uses $7.2 \cdot 30 = 216$ kWh. Each kWh costs $0.25$ euros, for a total cost of $216 \cdot 0.25 = 54$ euros. The result $1.80$ euros is the cost for just one day, while $864$ euros divides the kWh by the price instead of multiplying.
