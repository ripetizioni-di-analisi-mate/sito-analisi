A package containing $750\ \text{mL}$ costs $3.30$ euros and a package containing $1.2\ \text{L}$ costs $4.80$ euros. How much lower is the price per liter for the second package?

- $1.50$ euros
- $0.40$ euros
- $0.60$ euros
- $0.20$ euros

## Explanation

The first package costs $3.30\div0.75=4.40$ euros per liter and the second costs $4.80\div1.2=4$ euros per liter. The difference between the **unit prices** is $0.40$ euros per liter.
