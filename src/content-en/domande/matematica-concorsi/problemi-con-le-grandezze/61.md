A rope $13.4\ \text{m}$ long is cut into pieces of length $75\ \text{cm}$. How much rope remains after cutting as many pieces as possible, assuming no material is lost in the cuts?

- $60\ \text{cm}$
- $65\ \text{cm}$
- $15\ \text{cm}$
- $90\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The rope measures $1\,340\ \text{cm}$. **Division with remainder** gives $1\,340=17\cdot75+65$, leaving $65\ \text{cm}$.
