A plot of land measuring $2.4\ \text{ha}$ is divided into four equal lots. In each lot, $800\ \text{m}^2$ is set aside for access paths. How many square meters remain available for other uses in each lot?

- $5\,800$
- $6\,000$
- $21\,800$
- $5\,200$

## Explanation

Converting **units of area** gives $2.4\ \text{ha}$ as $24\,000\ \text{m}^2$. Each lot measures $24\,000\div4=6\,000\ \text{m}^2$, of which $6\,000-800=5\,200$ remains after subtracting the area of the paths.
