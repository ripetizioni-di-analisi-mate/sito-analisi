How many seconds are there in $2$ hours and $15$ minutes?

- $8100$ s
- $7740$ s
- $135$ s
- $7215$ s

## Explanation

Two hours and $15$ minutes equals $2 \cdot 60 + 15 = 135$ minutes. Each minute contains $60$ seconds, so the duration is $135 \cdot 60 = 8100$ seconds. The result $7740$ s reads the duration as $2.15$ h, since $2.15 \cdot 3600 = 7740$, whereas $15$ minutes equals $0.25$ h. The result $7215$ s converts only the hours to seconds and leaves the $15$ minutes unchanged.
