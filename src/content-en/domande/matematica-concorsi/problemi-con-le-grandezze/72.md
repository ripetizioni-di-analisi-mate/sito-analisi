Two scanners operate at the same time at constant rates. The first scans $180$ pages in $12$ minutes and the second scans $210$ pages in $14$ minutes. How many minutes do they take to scan $660$ pages together?

- $30$
- $22$
- $20$
- $44$

## Explanation

Each scanner scans $15$ pages per minute. Their **combined rate** is $30$ pages per minute, so the job takes $660\div30=22$ minutes.
