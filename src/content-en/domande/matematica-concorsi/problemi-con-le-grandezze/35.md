A store makes a bag of mixed nuts using $2$ kg of walnuts at $12$ euros per kilogram, $3$ kg of almonds at $9$ euros per kilogram, and $5$ kg of peanuts at $3$ euros per kilogram. What is the price per kilogram of the mixture?

- $8$ euros per kg
- $66$ euros per kg
- $6.60$ euros per kg
- $22$ euros per kg

## Explanation

The **price per unit of a mixture** is the total cost divided by the total quantity. The total cost is $2 \cdot 12 + 3 \cdot 9 + 5 \cdot 3 = 24 + 27 + 15 = 66$ euros for $10$ kg, so the mixture costs $6.60$ euros per kilogram. The result $8$ euros per kg is the arithmetic mean of the three prices and ignores the quantities. The result $22$ euros per kg divides the total cost by the number of products instead of by the number of kilograms. The mixture contains more of the least expensive ingredient, the peanuts, so its price is below the arithmetic mean.
