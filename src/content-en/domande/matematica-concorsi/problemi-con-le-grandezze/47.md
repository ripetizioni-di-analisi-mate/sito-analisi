An archive ships $8$ boxes with a mass of $375\ \text{g}$ each. What is their combined mass?

- $0.3\ \text{kg}$
- $3\ \text{kg}$
- $2.8\ \text{kg}$
- $30\ \text{kg}$

## Explanation

The boxes have a total mass of $8\cdot375=3\,000\ \text{g}$. Using the **unit conversion** $1\,000\ \text{g}=1\ \text{kg}$ gives $3\ \text{kg}$.
