A shipping box contains $24$ packages of coffee, each with a mass of $125$ g. The empty box has a mass of $2$ hg. What is the mass of the full box?

- $32$ kg
- $3.02$ kg
- $3$ kg
- $3.2$ kg

## Explanation

The packages together have a mass of $24 \cdot 125 = 3000$ g, or $3$ kg. A hectogram is $100$ g, so the empty box has a mass of $200$ g, or $0.2$ kg. The full box therefore has a mass of $3 + 0.2 = 3.2$ kg. The result $3.02$ kg treats $2$ hg as $20$ g, and $3$ kg is the mass of the coffee alone.
