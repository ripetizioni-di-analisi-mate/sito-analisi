A car travels at a constant speed of $96$ km/h. How many kilometers does it travel in $25$ minutes?

- $40$ km
- $24$ km
- $2400$ km
- $38.4$ km

## Explanation

Twenty-five minutes is $\dfrac{25}{60} = \dfrac{5}{12}$ of an hour. Distance equals speed times time, giving $s = 96 \cdot \dfrac{5}{12} = 40$ km. The result $24$ km reads $25$ minutes as $0.25$ h, and $2400$ km multiplies $96$ by $25$ without converting minutes to hours.
