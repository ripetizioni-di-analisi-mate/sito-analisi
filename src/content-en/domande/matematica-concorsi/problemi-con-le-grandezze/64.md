A courier must reach a service counter by 10\:15. The courier must first pick up an envelope, which takes $12$ minutes, then walk for $28$ minutes. What is the latest time the courier can start the pickup if the walk starts as soon as the pickup is complete?

- 9\:55
- 9\:47
- 10\:03
- 9\:35

## Explanation

The **total time** is $12+28=40$ minutes. Subtract $15$ minutes from 10\:15 to reach 10\:00, then subtract another $25$ minutes to reach 9\:35.
