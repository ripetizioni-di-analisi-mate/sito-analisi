A tourist exchanges $500$ euros for dollars at a rate of $1$ euro $= 1.12$ dollars, with no fees, and spends $448$ dollars during the trip. On returning, the tourist exchanges the remaining dollars for euros at the same rate, again with no fees. How many euros does the tourist receive?

- $52$ euros
- $125.44$ euros
- $112$ euros
- $100$ euros

## Explanation

With the given **exchange rate**, multiply by $1.12$ to convert euros to dollars, so the tourist receives $500 \cdot 1.12 = 560$ dollars. After the expenses, $560 - 448 = 112$ dollars remain. To convert dollars to euros, divide by $1.12$, giving $\dfrac{112}{1.12} = 100$ euros. The result $52$ euros subtracts dollars spent from the initial euros as if the currencies had the same value, and $125.44$ euros multiplies by $1.12$ instead of dividing.
