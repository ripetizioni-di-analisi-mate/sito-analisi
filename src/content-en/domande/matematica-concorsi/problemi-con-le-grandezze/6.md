A truck can carry a maximum load of $3.5$ t and already carries $15$ q of goods. What is the greatest number of $25$ kg bags of cement that can still be loaded?

- $60$
- $134$
- $80$
- $140$

## Explanation

A metric ton is $1000$ kg and a metric quintal is $100$ kg, so the maximum load is $3500$ kg and the goods already on board have a mass of $1500$ kg. This leaves $3500 - 1500 = 2000$ kg, enough for $\dfrac{2000}{25} = 80$ bags. The result $140$ ignores the existing load, while $134$ treats a metric quintal as $10$ kg.
