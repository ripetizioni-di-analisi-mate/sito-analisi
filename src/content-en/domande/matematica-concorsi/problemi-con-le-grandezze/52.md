How many hours and minutes is $1.35$ hours?

- $1$ hour and $18$ minutes
- $1$ hour and $21$ minutes
- $1$ hour and $35$ minutes
- $1$ hour and $25$ minutes

## Explanation

The decimal part is $0.35$ of an hour. In the **base-sixty system**, an hour equals $60$ minutes, so that part equals $0.35\cdot60=21$ minutes.
