Two cars leave at the same time from cities $210$ km apart and travel toward each other on the same road at constant speeds of $50$ km/h and $70$ km/h. How long does it take for the cars to be $30$ km apart for the first time?

- $2$ h
- $1$ h $45$ min
- $1$ h $50$ min
- $1$ h $30$ min

## Explanation

Each hour the distance between the cars decreases by $50 + 70 = 120$ km. The distance drops from $210$ km to $30$ km for the first time when it has decreased by $180$ km. This takes $\dfrac{180}{120} = 1.5$ h, or $1$ h $30$ min. The result $1$ h $45$ min gives the **time until the cars meet**, $\dfrac{210}{120} = 1.75$ h, while $2$ h is the time at which the cars are again $30$ km apart after passing each other. The result $1$ h $50$ min reads $1.5$ h as $1$ hour and $50$ minutes.
