In a $600$ m school relay, three students each run $200$ m. The first two take $50$ s and $25$ s. What is the maximum time, in seconds, the third may take for the average speed over the whole relay to be at least $6$ m/s?

- $\dfrac{100}{3}$
- $37.5$
- $50$
- $25$

## Explanation

For the **average speed** over $600$ m to be at least $6$ m/s, the total time must be at most $\dfrac{600}{6} = 100$ s. The first two students take $50 + 25 = 75$ s together, so the third has at most $100 - 75 = 25$ s left. The value $\dfrac{100}{3}$ is the time needed to run only the third $200$ m at $6$ m/s. With this time the overall average stays below $6$ m/s, because the total time becomes $75 + \dfrac{100}{3}$ s, which exceeds $100$ s. The value $37.5$ is instead the mean of the times of the first two students.
