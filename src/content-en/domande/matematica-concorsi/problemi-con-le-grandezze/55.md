A 600 m relay has three equal legs. The first two runners take 35 and 31 seconds. What is the longest time in seconds the third runner can take for the average speed to be at least $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $30$
- $36$
- $40$
- $34$

## Explanation

The total time can be at most 100 seconds, since $6\cdot100=600$. The first two legs use 66 seconds, leaving 34.
