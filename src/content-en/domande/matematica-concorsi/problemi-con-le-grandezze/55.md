A bicycle travels at $6\ \text{m/s}$. What is this speed in kilometers per hour?

- $16.7\ \text{km/h}$
- $60\ \text{km/h}$
- $10.8\ \text{km/h}$
- $21.6\ \text{km/h}$

## Explanation

To **convert the speed**, multiply by $3\,600$ to obtain meters per hour, then divide by $1\,000$ to obtain kilometers per hour. The result is $6\cdot3.6=21.6\ \text{km/h}$.
