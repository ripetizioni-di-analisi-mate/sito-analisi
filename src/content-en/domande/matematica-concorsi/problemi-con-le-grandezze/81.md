A box has inside dimensions of $48\ \text{cm}$ by $36\ \text{cm}$ by $30\ \text{cm}$. What is the greatest number of cubes with edge length $6\ \text{cm}$ that can fit inside, with the edges of each cube parallel to those of the box?

- $216$
- $288$
- $144$
- $240$

## Explanation

Along the three dimensions, $8$, $6$, and $5$ cubes fit, giving an arrangement of $240$ cubes. The ratio of the **volume** of the box to that of one cube is also $240$, so this is the maximum.
