A tank contains $30$ kg of salt water that is $4\%$ salt by mass. Some water evaporates, all the salt remains in the tank, and the concentration rises to $6\%$. How many kilograms of water evaporated?

- $10$ kg
- $45$ kg
- $20$ kg
- $15$ kg

## Explanation

The tank contains $0.04 \cdot 30 = 1.2$ kg of salt, and this stays the same after evaporation. Using the same relationship as for **dilution**, $c_1 \cdot m_1 = c_2 \cdot m_2$, gives a final solution mass of $\dfrac{1.2}{0.06} = 20$ kg. Therefore, $30 - 20 = 10$ kg of water evaporated. The result $20$ kg is the mass of the solution that remains, while $45$ kg comes from the proportion $4 : 6 = 30 : x$, which treats concentration and mass as directly proportional.
