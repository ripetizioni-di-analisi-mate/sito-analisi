A courier drives for $40$ minutes at $72\ \text{km/h}$ and then for $20$ minutes at $48\ \text{km/h}$, without stopping. What is the average speed for the trip?

- $66\ \text{km/h}$
- $56\ \text{km/h}$
- $64\ \text{km/h}$
- $60\ \text{km/h}$

## Explanation

The distances on the two legs are $72\cdot\dfrac23=48\ \text{km}$ and $48\cdot\dfrac13=16\ \text{km}$. The **average speed** is the total distance of $64\ \text{km}$ divided by the one hour the trip took.
