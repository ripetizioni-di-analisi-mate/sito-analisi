A team of $6$ employees files $180$ records in $5$ hours. In $4$ hours, how many records can a team of $10$ employees file if everyone files the same number per hour?

- $288$
- $300$
- $144$
- $240$

## Explanation

The first team works $6\cdot5=30$ **person-hours** and files $180\div30=6$ records per person-hour. The second team has $10\cdot4=40$ person-hours and files $240$ records.
