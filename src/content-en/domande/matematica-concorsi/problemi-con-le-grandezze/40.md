A merchant mixes $30$ kg of coffee bought for $8$ euros per kilogram with $20$ kg bought for $14$ euros per kilogram. The merchant sells the blend at a markup of $25\%$ on its cost. What is the selling price per kilogram?

- $13$ euros
- $13.75$ euros
- $10.40$ euros
- $10.65$ euros

## Explanation

The total cost is $30 \cdot 8 + 20 \cdot 14 = 240 + 280 = 520$ euros for $50$ kg, so the **price per unit of a mixture** gives a cost of $\dfrac{520}{50} = 10.40$ euros per kilogram. The $25\%$ markup multiplies that cost by $1.25$, giving a selling price of $10.40 \cdot 1.25 = 13$ euros per kilogram. The result $13.75$ euros applies the markup to the arithmetic mean of the two prices, $11$ euros. The result $10.40$ euros is the cost before markup. The result $10.65$ euros adds $0.25$ euros to the cost, treating a $25\%$ markup as $25$ cents.
