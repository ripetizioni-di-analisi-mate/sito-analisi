An empty container weighs $1.2\ \text{kg}$. When filled with $3\ \text{L}$ of a liquid, it weighs $3.6\ \text{kg}$. What is the density of the liquid?

- $0.4\ \text{kg/L}$
- $0.8\ \text{kg/L}$
- $1.2\ \text{kg/L}$
- $2.4\ \text{kg/L}$

## Explanation

Subtract the **tare mass** from the total mass to obtain $2.4\ \text{kg}$ of liquid. Dividing by $3\ \text{L}$ gives a density of $0.8\ \text{kg/L}$.
