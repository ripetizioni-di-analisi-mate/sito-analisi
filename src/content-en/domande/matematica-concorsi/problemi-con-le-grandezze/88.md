You combine $2$ liters of a liquid with a density of $0.8\ \text{kg/L}$ and $3$ liters of another liquid. If the volumes add and the mixture has a density of $1.04\ \text{kg/L}$, what is the density of the second liquid?

- $1.16\ \text{kg/L}$
- $1.3\ \text{kg/L}$
- $1.2\ \text{kg/L}$
- $1.04\ \text{kg/L}$

## Explanation

The **density** of the mixture gives a total mass of $5\cdot1.04=5.2\ \text{kg}$. The first liquid contributes $2\cdot0.8=1.6\ \text{kg}$, so the second has a mass of $3.6\ \text{kg}$ and a density of $3.6\div3=1.2\ \text{kg/L}$.
