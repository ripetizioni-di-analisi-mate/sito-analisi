A van travels $30\ \text{km}$ at $60\ \text{km/h}$, stops for $10$ minutes, then continues for another $30\ \text{km}$. What constant speed does it need on the second leg to achieve an overall average speed of $54\ \text{km/h}$, including the stop?

- $48\ \text{km/h}$
- $67.5\ \text{km/h}$
- $72\ \text{km/h}$
- $60\ \text{km/h}$

## Explanation

For an **average speed** of $54\ \text{km/h}$, the total time available is $\dfrac{60}{54}=\dfrac{10}{9}$ hours. The first leg and the stop take $\dfrac12+\dfrac16=\dfrac23$ of an hour, leaving $\dfrac49$ of an hour to cover $30\ \text{km}$. The required speed is $30\div\dfrac49=67.5\ \text{km/h}$.
