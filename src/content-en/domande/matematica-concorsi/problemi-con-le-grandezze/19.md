A bus travels $150$ km in $1$ hour and $30$ minutes, makes a $30$-minute stop, then travels another $90$ km in $1$ hour. What is its average speed over the entire trip, including the stop?

- $95$ km/h
- $100$ km/h
- $96$ km/h
- $80$ km/h

## Explanation

**Average speed** is total distance divided by total time. The total distance is $150 + 90 = 240$ km and the total time, including the stop, is $1.5 + 0.5 + 1 = 3$ h, so the average speed is $\dfrac{240}{3} = 80$ km/h. The result $96$ km/h ignores the stop, and $95$ km/h is the arithmetic mean of the speeds on the two legs, $100$ km/h and $90$ km/h.
