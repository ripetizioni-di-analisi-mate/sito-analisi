A sequence has first term 40 and satisfies $a_{n+1}=(a_n+20)/2$. After how many updates is the term first less than 2 units away from 20?

- $5$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Each update halves the distance from 20, which is initially 20. After three steps the distance is $2.5$, and after four it is $1.25$, the first distance less than 2.
