What is the sum of the first $30$ odd numbers, that is, $1 + 3 + 5 + \dots + 59$?

- $900$
- $930$
- $961$
- $465$

## Explanation

The **sum of the first odd numbers** is a square because the arithmetic sequence sum formula with first term $1$ and last term $2n - 1$ gives $\dfrac{(1 + 2n - 1) \cdot n}{2} = n^2$. For $n = 30$, the sum is therefore $30^2 = 900$. The value $930 = 30 \cdot 31$ is the sum of the first $30$ even numbers, and $961 = 31^2$ counts $31$ terms. The value $465$ is the sum of the natural numbers from $1$ through $30$.
