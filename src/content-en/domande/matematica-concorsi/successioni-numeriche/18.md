The sequence $1,2,6,15,31,56,\ldots$ grows by adding positive integer squares in order. Which value follows $56$?

- $92$
- $81$
- $112$
- $87$

## Explanation

The differences between consecutive terms are $1, 4, 9, 16, 25$, the **squares** of the numbers from $1$ to $5$. The next difference is therefore $6^2 = 36$, and the next term is $56 + 36 = 92$. The value $81$ repeats the last difference $25$, and $112$ doubles $56$, but doubling already fails between $2$ and $6$.
