A list contains the positive integers in increasing order after all perfect squares have been deleted. What is the position of $20$ in the new list, counting from $1$?

- $15$
- $17$
- $16$
- $20$

## Explanation

Between $1$ and $20$, the four deleted numbers are $1,4,9,16$. The number $20$ remains and is the last of the $20-4=16$ surviving terms up to that point.
