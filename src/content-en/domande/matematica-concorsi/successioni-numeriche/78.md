The positive multiples of 9 are listed in increasing order. How many terms lie strictly between 30 and 100?

- $7$
- $10$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

The first multiple in the interval is $9\cdot4=36$ and the last is $9\cdot11=99$. There are eight indices from 4 through 11, inclusive.
