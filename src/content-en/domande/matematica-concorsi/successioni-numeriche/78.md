For each $n\ge1$, term $u_n$ is the units digit of $3^n$. What is $u_{102}$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

The first units digits are $3,9,7,1$. Multiplying by $3$ again returns to $3$; the cycle repeats because each digit depends only on the previous one. Since $102=4\cdot25+2$, choose the second digit of the cycle, $9$.
