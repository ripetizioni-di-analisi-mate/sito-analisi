Deposits of $1,2,4,\ldots$ euros were planned over ten days, doubling each day. Exactly one deposit was missed and the others stayed as planned. The actual total is $991$ euros. On which day was the deposit missed?

- The fifth
- The seventh
- The tenth
- The sixth

## Explanation

The ten planned deposits sum to $2^{10}-1=1023$. The difference $1023-991=32$ is the missing amount. Day $n$ called for $2^{n-1}$ euros, and $32=2^5$, so the missing day is the sixth.
