In the sequence $2, 7, 14, 11, 16, 32, 29, \dots$, which number comes after $29$?

- $58$
- $26$
- $37$
- $34$

## Explanation

The sequence cycles through three **mixed operations**: add $5$, multiply by $2$, and subtract $3$. Indeed, $7 = 2 + 5$, $14 = 7 \cdot 2$, and $11 = 14 - 3$, and the same cycle gives $16 = 11 + 5$, $32 = 16 \cdot 2$, and $29 = 32 - 3$. The term $29$ is obtained by subtraction, so the next term is obtained by adding $5$ and equals $34$. The value $58$ multiplies $29$ by $2$ and $26$ subtracts $3$ again. Both skip the addition that follows subtraction in the cycle. The value $37 = 32 + 5$ applies the addition to $32$ instead of the last term.
