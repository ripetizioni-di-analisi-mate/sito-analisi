An arithmetic sequence has first term $12$ and common difference $-3$. What is the position of its first negative term?

- Fifth
- Fourth
- Seventh
- Sixth

## Explanation

The terms are $12, 9, 6, 3, 0, -3, \dots$ because each is the preceding one minus $3$. The fifth term is $a_5 = 12 + 4 \cdot (-3) = 0$, which is not negative, so the first negative term is $a_6 = -3$, in **sixth** position. The fifth position comes from treating zero as negative. The fourth position comes from the quotient $\dfrac{12}{3} = 4$, which counts the steps needed to reach $0$ rather than the positions.
