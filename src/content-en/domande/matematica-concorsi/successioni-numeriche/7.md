In the sequence $3, 5, 9, 15, 23, 35, 45$, all the terms follow a rule except for one that was written incorrectly. Which term is incorrect?

- $23$
- $45$
- $9$
- $35$

## Explanation

The differences between consecutive terms are $2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 10$. They start at $2$ and increase by $2$ at a time through the fourth difference. If all the **second differences** equal $2$, the correct first differences are $2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12$, so the sixth term is $23 + 10 = 33$. Placing $33$ in sixth position also makes the seventh term fit because $33 + 12 = 45$. The incorrect term is therefore $35$. The value $45$ looks out of place because it follows $35$, but the difference $45 - 33 = 12$ is exactly what the rule predicts.
