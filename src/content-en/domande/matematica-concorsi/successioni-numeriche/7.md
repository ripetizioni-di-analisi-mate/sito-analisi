One candidate proposes $a_n=n^2+1$ to explain $2,5,10,17,26,37$. Another proposes $b_n=n^2+1+(n-1)(n-2)\cdots(n-6)$. Which observation distinguishes the two rules, assuming exactly one is used?

- Comparing the sum of the first three terms
- Checking that the first term is even
- Rereading the sixth term
- Reading the seventh term

## Explanation

For $n=1,\ldots,6$ the added product has a zero factor. Every check confined to the first six terms therefore agrees. At $n=7$ the product equals $6!=720$, giving distinct predictions of $50$ and $770$.
