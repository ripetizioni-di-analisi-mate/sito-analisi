A service desk repeats the rota A, B, A, C without interruption. You observe $20$ consecutive turns without knowing where in the block the observation begins. How many turns are guaranteed to belong to A?

- $10$
- $8$
- It cannot be determined without knowing the first turn
- $5$

## Explanation

Any four consecutive turns, wherever they start, cover all four positions of the block once and therefore include two As. The twenty turns split into five groups of four, containing ten As in total.
