A sequence starts at 48. To obtain each new term, divide the preceding term by 2 and subtract 3. What is the fourth term?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

After 48 come 21 and then $21/2-3=15/2$. The fourth term is $15/4-3=3/4$.
