A hall has $18$ rows, the first with $15$ seats and each with $3$ more than the preceding one. Section A consists of odd rows and section B of even rows. By how many seats does B exceed A?

- $27$
- $3$
- $54$
- $15$

## Explanation

Pair each odd row with the even row immediately after it. This makes nine pairs, and each B row has three more seats than its paired A row. The total advantage is $9\cdot3=27$.
