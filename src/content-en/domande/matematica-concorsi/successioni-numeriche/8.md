In the sequence $2, 10, ?, 250, 1250$, which number replaces the question mark?

- $50$
- $130$
- $125$
- $20$

## Explanation

The ratio of the second term to the first is $5$, and $\dfrac{1250}{250} = 5$ as well, so each term is the preceding one multiplied by $5$. The missing term is therefore $10 \cdot 5 = 50$, and indeed $50 \cdot 5 = 250$. The value $130$ is the arithmetic mean of $10$ and $250$, which would give the missing term only if the differences were constant. With $130$, however, the differences would be $8, 120, 120, 1000$.
