The sum of the first 12 terms of a sequence is 153, and the sum of its first 7 terms is 63. What is the sum of the terms in positions 8 through 12, inclusive?

- $104$
- $84$
- $90$
- $75$

## Explanation

The sum through position 12 already includes the first seven terms. Subtracting 63 from 153 isolates the five requested terms, giving a total of 90.
