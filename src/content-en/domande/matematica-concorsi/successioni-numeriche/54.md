A geometric progression has a positive first term and a negative ratio. What is the sign of the product of its first $14$ terms?

- Negative
- Zero
- Positive
- It depends on the absolute value of the ratio

## Explanation

The signs alternate starting with positive. The even-position terms are negative, so seven of the first fourteen are negative. A product with an odd number of negative factors and all other factors positive is negative.
