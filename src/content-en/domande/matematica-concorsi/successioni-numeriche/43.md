In every column the bottom number is $ax+by$, where $x$ is the top number and $y$ the middle number. The coefficients $a,b$ are common to all columns.
$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
3 & 5 & 2 & 6 \\ \hline
4 & 1 & 6 & 3 \\ \hline
11 & 7 & 14 & ? \\ \hline
\end{array}
$$
Which number goes in place of the question mark?

- $9$
- $12$
- $18$
- $15$

## Explanation

In each complete column, the bottom number is the top number plus twice the middle number, since $3 + 2 \cdot 4 = 11$, $5 + 2 \cdot 1 = 7$, and $2 + 2 \cdot 6 = 14$. The missing number in the last column is therefore $6 + 2 \cdot 3 = 12$. The value $9$ is the sum $6 + 3$, which would give $7$ instead of $11$ in the first column. The value $15$ doubles the top number instead of the middle number.
