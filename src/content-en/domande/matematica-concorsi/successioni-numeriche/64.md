A checklist has 22 items on the first day and gains 6 items per day starting on the second. On which day does it first have at least 95 items?

- $12$
- $14$
- $15$
- $13$

## Explanation

On the thirteenth day, the total is $22+12\cdot6=94$. The next day it becomes 100, the first value to reach the threshold.
