An office starts with $18$ cases in progress. Each turn the new count is the smaller of $25$ and the previous count plus $3$. Which description satisfies this rule at every turn?

- It stops at $24$
- The count goes through $21,24,25$ and then stays at $25$
- It exceeds $25$ at the third update
- On reaching $25$ it restarts at $18$

## Explanation

The first two updates add $3$. From $24$ the candidate is $27$, but the smaller of $25$ and $27$ is $25$. Starting at $25$ the rule chooses $25$ again, so the value is stable.
