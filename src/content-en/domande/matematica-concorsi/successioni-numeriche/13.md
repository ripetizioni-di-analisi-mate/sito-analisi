In the sequence $1, 50, 4, 45, 9, 40, 16, 35, \dots$, which number comes after $35$?

- $30$
- $23$
- $25$
- $36$

## Explanation

The terms rise and fall, so the sequence is **interleaved**. The odd-position terms are $1, 4, 9, 16$, the squares of the numbers from $1$ to $4$, while the even-position terms are $50, 45, 40, 35$ and decrease by $5$. The next term is in ninth position, an odd position, so it continues the sequence of squares and equals $5^2 = 25$. The value $30$ continues the even-position sequence instead.
