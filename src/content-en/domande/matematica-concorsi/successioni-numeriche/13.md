In the 21-letter Italian alphabet, start from C and always move three positions forward, wrapping from Z to A. The series begins C, F, I, N. How many distinct letters are visited before the first return to C?

- $21$
- $18$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

After $k$ jumps the total distance is $3k$ positions. Returning to C requires a multiple of $21$, and the smallest positive $k$ allowing this is $7$. Thus seven distinct letters are visited, counting the initial C.
