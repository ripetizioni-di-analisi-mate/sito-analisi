The values $14,19,24,30,34,39$ were meant to form an arithmetic progression in that order. Exactly one value was copied incorrectly. Which correction restores the progression?

- Change $34$ to $35$
- Change $30$ to $29$
- Change $39$ to $40$
- Change $24$ to $25$

## Explanation

The first three values and the final two agree on a step of $5$. Around $30$ the steps are $6$ and $4$ instead. Changing it to $29$ makes both steps $5$, fixing both discrepancies.
