The terms $-9$, $-4$, and 1 are consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence. What is the common difference?

- $-5$
- $5$
- $4$
- $13$

## Explanation

Subtract the preceding term from the next to obtain the common difference. The increase from $-9$ to $-4$ is 5, as it is from $-4$ to 1.
