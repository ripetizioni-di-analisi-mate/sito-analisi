Three consecutive positive terms form both an arithmetic and a geometric progression. What property must they have?

- The second is twice the first
- The second is the sum of the other two
- All three are equal
- The first and third have product $1$

## Explanation

Write the arithmetic terms as $m-d,m,m+d$. The geometric property requires $m^2=(m-d)(m+d)=m^2-d^2$. Hence $d=0$ and the three terms coincide. A constant positive triple does satisfy both rules.
