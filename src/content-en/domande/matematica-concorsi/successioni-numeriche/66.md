A geometric sequence has a positive common ratio, a second term of 6, and a fifth term of 162. What is the common ratio?

- $27$
- $9$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

From the second term to the fifth, multiply three times by the common ratio $r$. From $r^3=162/6=27$ it follows that $r=3$.
