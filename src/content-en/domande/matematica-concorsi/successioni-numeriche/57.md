A sequence repeats the numbers 2, 7, 9 in order. What is the sum of the first five terms?

- $25$
- $18$
- $36$
- $27$

## Explanation

The first **block** contains $2$, $7$, and $9$, whose sum is $18$. To reach five terms, add the first two numbers of the next block, giving a total of $18+2+7=27$.
