In the sequence $3, 3, 6, 18, 72, \dots$, which number comes after $72$?

- $288$
- $144$
- $360$
- $432$

## Explanation

The **ratios** of consecutive terms are $1, 2, 3, 4$, so each term is the preceding one times a number that increases by $1$ at every step. The next term is therefore $72 \cdot 5 = 360$. The value $288$ repeats the factor $4$, and $432$ uses the factor $6$, skipping $5$.
