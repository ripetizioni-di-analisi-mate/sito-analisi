The sum of the even integers from $2$ through $80$ is $1640$. By how much does the sum decrease if each even integer is replaced by the odd integer immediately preceding it?

- $1$
- $160$
- $40$
- $80$

## Explanation

The even terms are $2k$ for $k=1,\ldots,40$. Replacing $2k$ by $2k-1$ subtracts one from each of forty terms. The decrease is $40$ regardless of the starting sum.
