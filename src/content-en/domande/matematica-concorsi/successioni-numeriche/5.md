Which number comes after $48$ in the sequence $3072, 768, 192, 48, \dots$?

- $24$
- $16$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

The **ratio** of each term to the preceding term is constant because $\dfrac{768}{3072} = \dfrac{192}{768} = \dfrac{48}{192} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. Each term is therefore the preceding one divided by $4$, and the next term is $\dfrac{48}{4} = 12$. The value $24$ comes from dividing by $2$, and the value $3$ is the term following $12$.
