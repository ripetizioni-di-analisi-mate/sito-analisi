Starting at $1$ the rule $a_{n+1}=3a_n+1$ generates $1,4,13,40,\ldots$. A report claims that $124$ is a term of the sequence. What is the correct first step in checking this claim by working back to a possible predecessor?

- Immediately conclude that $124$ belongs because it exceeds $40$
- Compute $\dfrac{124}{3}-1$ and round
- Compute $\dfrac{124-1}{3}=41$, then check whether $41$ belongs to the series
- Compute $3\cdot124+1$

## Explanation

To reverse a step, subtract $1$ before dividing by $3$. The candidate $41$ is an integer but does not occur: this increasing series goes directly from $40$ to $121$. Therefore $124$ cannot be a term either.
