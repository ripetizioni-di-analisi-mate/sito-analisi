For every positive integer $n$, the sum of the first $n$ terms of a sequence is $n(2n+3)$. What is the ninth term?

- $37$
- $36$
- $33$
- $39$

## Explanation

Subtract consecutive partial sums to obtain a single term. The sums through the ninth and eighth terms are 189 and 152, so the ninth term is 37.
