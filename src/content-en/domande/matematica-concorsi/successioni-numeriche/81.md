The sum of the first seven terms of an arithmetic sequence is 84. The first term is 3. What is the tenth?

- $33$
- $27$
- $24$
- $30$

## Explanation

The mean of the first seven terms equals the fourth term, so $a_4=84/7=12$. The common difference is $(12-3)/3=3$ and the tenth term is $3+9\cdot3=30$.
