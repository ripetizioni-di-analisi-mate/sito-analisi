What is the eighth term of the sequence $2, 1, 4, 8, 6, 27, 8, \dots$?

- $64$
- $10$
- $81$
- $54$

## Explanation

The terms in odd-numbered positions are $2, 4, 6, 8$ and those in even-numbered positions are $1, 8, 27$, so the sequence is **interleaved**. The even-position terms are the cubes $1^3, 2^3, 3^3$. The eighth position is even, so it contains the next cube, $4^3 = 64$. The value $10$ continues the even numbers in odd-numbered positions, and $81$ multiplies $27$ by $3$, a rule that does not fit the terms $1$ and $8$.
