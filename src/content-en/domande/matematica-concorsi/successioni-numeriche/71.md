Two procedures start from the same number $x$. At each turn A first doubles and then adds $4$, while B first adds $4$ and then doubles. After the first turn, which comparison holds for every $x$?

- The two results coincide
- A exceeds B by $4$
- B is always twice A
- B exceeds A by $4$

## Explanation

A returns $2x+4$, while B returns $2(x+4)=2x+8$. The addition made before doubling is doubled as well. B minus A equals $4$ for every input.
