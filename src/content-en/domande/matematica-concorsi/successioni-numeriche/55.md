A list writes $1$ once, $2$ twice, $3$ three times, and so on. What number is written in position sixteen?

- $6$
- $5$
- $7$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first five blocks occupy $1+2+3+4+5=15$ positions. The sixteenth position starts the next block, made up of six copies of $6$.
