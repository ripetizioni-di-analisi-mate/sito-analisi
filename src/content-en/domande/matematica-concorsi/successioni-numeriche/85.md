A sequence starts at 12 and cycles through the increments $+5$, $-3$, $+2$. What is the term in position 20, counting 12 as the first term?

- $36$
- $41$
- $40$
- $32$

## Explanation

Going from the first term to the twentieth takes 19 increments. Six complete cycles increase the value by 24, and the last increment is 5, giving 41.
