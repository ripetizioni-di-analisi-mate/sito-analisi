A principal of $5000$ euros is deposited at $10\%$ annual compound interest, meaning that at the end of each year the interest earned is added to the principal. After how many years does the balance first exceed $7000$ euros?

- $5$ years
- $4$ years
- $3$ years
- $6$ years

## Explanation

Each year the balance is multiplied by $1.1$, so the year-end balances form a **geometric sequence** with common ratio $1.1$, namely $5500$, $6050$, $6655$, and $7320.50$ euros. After $3$ years the balance is $6655$ euros, less than $7000$ euros, while after $4$ years it is $7320.50$ euros. The balance therefore first exceeds $7000$ euros after $4$ years. The answer of $5$ years comes from calculating interest only on the initial principal, giving $500$ euros per year. At that rate, after $4$ years the balance would be exactly $7000$ euros and would exceed it only after $5$ years.
