The term at position $n$ in a sequence is $a_n = 4n - 3$. What is the twenty-fifth term?

- $100$
- $97$
- $93$
- $101$

## Explanation

The **general term** gives the term at position $25$ by substituting $n = 25$, so $a_{25} = 4 \cdot 25 - 3 = 100 - 3 = 97$. The value $100$ is the product $4 \cdot 25$ without subtracting $3$. The value $93$ comes from using $n = 24$, so it is the term before the one requested.
