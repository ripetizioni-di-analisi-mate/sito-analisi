A register assigns the numbers $4,13,22,31,\ldots$, increasing by $9$ each time. Which number reported as already assigned reveals an error?

- $148$
- $225$
- $400$
- $94$

## Explanation

Every assigned number has the form $4+9k$ for a nonnegative integer $k$. Subtracting $4$ from the proposed values yields multiples of $9$ in the cases $90,144,396$, but $225-4=221$ is not a multiple of $9$. Thus $225$ cannot belong to the register.
