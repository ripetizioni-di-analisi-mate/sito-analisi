A ranking list grows by successive doubling: $7,14,28,56,\ldots$. If $d_n=a_{n+1}-a_n$ is the increase between positions, how is $d_n$ related to the starting value $a_n$?

- $d_n=2a_n$ for every $n$
- $d_n=a_\dfrac{n}{2}$ for every $n$
- $d_n=a_n$ for every $n$
- $d_n=7$ for every $n$

## Explanation

Since $a_{n+1}=2a_n$, the difference is $2a_n-a_n=a_n$. The first increase is $7$, but the next is $14$: the increase varies and always equals that step's starting value.
