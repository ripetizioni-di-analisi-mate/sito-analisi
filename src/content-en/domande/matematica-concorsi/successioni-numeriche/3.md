The first term of a sequence is $a_1 = 3$. Each subsequent term is obtained by multiplying the preceding term by $3$ and subtracting $4$ from the product, that is, $a_{n+1} = 3a_n - 4$. What is $a_4$?

- $11$
- $83$
- $29$
- $33$

## Explanation

The **recursive form** gives each term in terms of the preceding one, so calculate the terms one at a time. We have $a_2 = 3 \cdot 3 - 4 = 5$, then $a_3 = 3 \cdot 5 - 4 = 11$, and finally $a_4 = 3 \cdot 11 - 4 = 29$. The value $11$ is the third term. The value $83 = 3 \cdot 29 - 4$ is the fifth and comes from applying the rule one time too many. The value $33$ is three times $a_3$ without subtracting $4$.
