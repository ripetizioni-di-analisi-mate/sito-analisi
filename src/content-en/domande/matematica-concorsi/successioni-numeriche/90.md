One arithmetic sequence starts at 8 with common difference 6. Another starts at 14 with common difference 9. What is the second value common to both?

- $26$
- $38$
- $20$
- $32$

## Explanation

The first common value is 14. The next lies a common multiple of 6 and 9 further along. The least positive such multiple is 18, so the second common value is 32.
