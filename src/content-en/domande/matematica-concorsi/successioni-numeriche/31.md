A ball is dropped from a height of $16$ m and moves only vertically. After each bounce it rises to half the height from which it fell. How many meters has the ball traveled when it hits the ground for the fourth time?

- $30$ m
- $44$ m
- $60$ m
- $46$ m

## Explanation

The heights reached after the bounces form a geometric sequence with common ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Before the fourth contact with the ground, they are $8$, $4$, and $2$ m. The ball falls $16$ m, then rises and falls $8$ m, $4$ m, and $2$ m, so the total distance is
$$
16 + 2 \cdot (8 + 4 + 2) = 16 + 28 = 44 \text{ m} .
$$
The value $30$ m counts only the downward distances. The value $46$ m also includes the rise and fall of $1$ m that occur after the fourth contact.
