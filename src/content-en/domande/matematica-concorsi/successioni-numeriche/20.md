After the three starting values $1,1,2$, each term is the sum of the last three, giving $4,7,13$. What is the following term?

- $20$
- $26$
- $24$
- $21$

## Explanation

From the fourth term onward, each term is the sum of the three preceding ones, since $4 = 1 + 1 + 2$, $7 = 1 + 2 + 4$, and $13 = 2 + 4 + 7$. The next term is therefore $4 + 7 + 13 = 24$. The value $20$ adds only the last two terms, as in the **Fibonacci sequence**, but that rule already fails at the term $4$ because $1 + 2 = 3$. The value $21$ is the term after $13$ in the Fibonacci sequence $1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, \dots$.
