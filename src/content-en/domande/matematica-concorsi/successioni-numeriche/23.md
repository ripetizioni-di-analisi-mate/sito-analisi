An arithmetic sequence has first term $7$ and common difference $4$. What is the position of the term equal to $107$?

- $25$
- $27$
- $26$
- $24$

## Explanation

From the **general term**, $7 + (n - 1) \cdot 4 = 107$, so $(n - 1) \cdot 4 = 100$ and $n - 1 = 25$. The term $107$ is therefore in position $n = 26$. The value $25$ is the number of steps from the first term to $107$ and does not give the position. The value $27$ comes from rounding $\dfrac{107}{4}$ without subtracting the first term.
