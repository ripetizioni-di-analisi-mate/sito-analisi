A list of reference numbers reads $7,11,15,\ldots,107$ with constant step $4$. Between every consecutive pair a new number is inserted, equal to the mean of its endpoints. How many numbers, old and new, does the list now contain?

- $52$
- $26$
- $51$
- $50$

## Explanation

There are $\dfrac{107-7}{4}=25$ gaps between the endpoints, so the original list contains $26$ numbers. One number is inserted into each of the $25$ gaps. The new total is $26+25=51$, with the numbers now spaced by $2$.
