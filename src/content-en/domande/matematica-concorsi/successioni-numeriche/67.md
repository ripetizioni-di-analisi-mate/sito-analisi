A geometric sequence starts with 240, and each term is three quarters of the preceding one. What is the fourth term?

- $\dfrac{405}{4}$
- $180$
- $\dfrac{405}{8}$
- $135$

## Explanation

There are three steps from the first term to the fourth. The product $240(3/4)^3$ gives $405/4$.
