Only terms $2,5,8,11,\ldots$ of a geometric progression with ratio $q\ne0$ and nonzero first term are recorded. What is the ratio of the recorded sequence?

- $q^3$
- $q^2$
- $3q$
- $\dfrac{q}{3}$

## Explanation

Three original updates separate successive recorded terms. Each multiplies by $q$, so $a_{n+3}=q^3a_n$. The starting position changes the first recorded value but not the ratio.
