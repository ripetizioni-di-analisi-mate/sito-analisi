The positive odd numbers and the positive multiples of $3$ are merged into one increasing list, writing shared values only once. How many terms in the merged list are at most $12$?

- $8$
- $10$
- $7$
- $6$

## Explanation

Up to $12$ there are six odd numbers and four multiples of $3$. The numbers $3$ and $9$ were counted twice. Subtracting the two repeats gives $6+4-2=8$.
