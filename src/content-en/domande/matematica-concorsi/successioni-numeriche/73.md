The terms in odd-numbered positions are 3, 6, 12, 24, and so on, doubling each time. The terms in even-numbered positions are the positive multiples of 5 in increasing order. What is the ninth term?

- $48$
- $40$
- $96$
- $24$

## Explanation

Position 9 is the fifth odd-numbered position. The fifth value in the odd-position sequence is obtained by doubling four times, so it equals $3\cdot2^4=48$.
