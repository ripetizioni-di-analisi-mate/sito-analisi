A nonnegative integer sequence follows $a_{n+1}=|a_n-4|$. Knowing only that $a_2=3$, what are all possible values of $a_1$?

- Only $1$
- $-1$ and $7$
- Only $7$
- $1$ and $7$

## Explanation

The rule requires $|a_1-4|=3$. Its predecessor must be three units away from $4$, so it can be $4-3=1$ or $4+3=7$. Both are allowed and produce the observed value.
