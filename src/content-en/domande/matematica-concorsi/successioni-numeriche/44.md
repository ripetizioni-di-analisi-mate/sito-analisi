An archive's records are numbered consecutively from $100$ through $300$. Records whose number is a multiple of $7$ are checked. What is the greatest number of consecutive records that can go unchecked?

- $8$
- $6$
- $7$
- $28$

## Explanation

Every block of seven consecutive integers contains a multiple of $7$, so an unchecked run cannot reach seven records. A run of six actually occurs, for example from $106$ through $111$, between the checks at $105$ and $112$.
