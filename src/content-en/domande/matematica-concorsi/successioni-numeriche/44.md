How many multiples of $7$ lie between $100$ and $300$?

- $27$
- $28$
- $29$
- $42$

## Explanation

The smallest multiple of $7$ greater than $100$ is $105 = 7 \cdot 15$, and the greatest one less than $300$ is $294 = 7 \cdot 42$. The required multiples are therefore $7 \cdot 15, 7 \cdot 16, \dots, 7 \cdot 42$, and there are $42 - 15 + 1 = 28$ of them because both endpoints count. The value $27$ omits one endpoint. The value $42$ counts all multiples of $7$ up to $300$, including those below $100$.
