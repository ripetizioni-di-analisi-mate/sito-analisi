In the sequence $5, 6, 9, 18, 45, \dots$, which number comes after $45$?

- $126$
- $99$
- $135$
- $72$

## Explanation

The differences between consecutive terms are $1, 3, 9, 27$, each three times the preceding difference. The next difference is therefore $27 \cdot 3 = 81$, and the next term is $45 + 81 = 126$. The value $135$ is three times $45$, but tripling already fails between $5$ and $6$. The value $72$ repeats the last difference $27$ without multiplying it by $3$.
