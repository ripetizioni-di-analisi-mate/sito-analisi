To sum the integers from $1$ to $60$, a committee pairs the first with the last, the second with the penultimate, and so on. Which count uses every integer exactly once?

- $30$ pairs, each summing to $61$
- $30$ pairs, each summing to $60$
- $60$ pairs, each summing to $61$
- $29$ pairs summing to $61$ and one central term

## Explanation

The pair beginning with $k$ ends with $61-k$, so its sum is always $61$. There are an even number of integers and $\dfrac{60}{2}=30$ pairs. The last is $(30,31)$ and no term remains unpaired.
