For each positive integer $n$, let $a_n$ be its number of positive divisors, including $1$ and $n$ itself. What is the first index for which $a_n=3$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The integer $1$ has one divisor; $2$ and $3$ have two each. The divisors of $4$ are $1,2,4$, making $4$ the first index with three.
