Using the $21$-letter Italian alphabet, which letter comes next in the sequence B, D, G, I, N, …?

- P
- Q
- O
- R

## Explanation

In the Italian alphabet, the letters B, D, G, I, N occupy positions $2, 4, 7, 9, 12$, and the successive differences are $2, 3, 2, 3$. The differences alternate, so the difference $3$ is followed by $2$. The required letter is therefore in position $12 + 2 = 14$, occupied by P in the Italian alphabet. Q, at position $15$, repeats the difference $3$, and O is the letter immediately after N.
