In the geometric sequence $3, 6, 12, 24, \dots$, what position does the term $1536$ occupy?

- $9$
- $11$
- $10$
- $512$

## Explanation

The **general term** is $a_n = 3 \cdot 2^{\,n-1}$, and $1536 = 3 \cdot 512 = 3 \cdot 2^9$. Therefore $n - 1 = 9$, so the term $1536$ is in tenth position. The value $9$ is the exponent of $2$ rather than the position, and $512$ is the quotient $\dfrac{1536}{3}$.
