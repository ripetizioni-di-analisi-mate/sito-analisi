The list of squares of consecutive positive integers in decreasing order reads $121,100,?,64,49$. Which value was erased?

- $82$
- $80$
- $79$
- $81$

## Explanation

The known terms are the **perfect squares** $11^2$, $10^2$, $8^2$, and $7^2$, so the third position contains the square of $9$, or $81$. The differences confirm the rule. With $81$ in third position they are $-21, -19, -17, -15$, so the first increase is $2$ and every subsequent increase is also $2$. The value $82$ is the mean of $100$ and $64$, which would give the missing term only if the differences were constant.
