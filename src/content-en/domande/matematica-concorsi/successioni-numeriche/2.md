The terms $2,6,12,20,30,\ldots$ are products of the form $a_n=n(n+1)$ for $n\ge1$. Which argument proves that no odd number will ever occur in the list?

- Every integer $n$ is even
- The product of two positive integers is always even
- The first five products are even
- One of two consecutive integers is even

## Explanation

For every $n$, the two consecutive factors have opposite parity. One supplies a factor of $2$, so every product is even. Checking finitely many examples alone would not prove the property for all positions.
