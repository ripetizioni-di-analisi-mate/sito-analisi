In the Italian alphabet, which has $21$ letters (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, Z), the letters at positions $1$, $2$, $4$, $7$, $11$ are taken. The letters at the same positions in the English alphabet, which has $26$ letters, are then taken. Which is the first of the five positions where the two letters differ?

- Position $7$, with G in the Italian alphabet and I in the English one
- Position $4$, with D in the Italian alphabet and E in the English one
- None of the five positions
- Position $11$, with M in the Italian alphabet and K in the English one

## Explanation

The two alphabets share their first nine letters, from A to I, so positions $1$, $2$, $4$, $7$ give A, B, D, G in both. After I the English alphabet continues with J and K, while the Italian one continues with L and M. Therefore position $11$ holds M in the Italian alphabet and K in the English one, and it is the first position with different letters.
