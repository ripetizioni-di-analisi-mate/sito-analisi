Using the $21$-letter Italian alphabet, which letter comes next in the sequence A, B, D, G, M, …?

- Q
- S
- P
- R

## Explanation

The **positions of the letters** in the Italian alphabet are $1, 2, 4, 7, 11$, and the differences between consecutive positions are $1, 2, 3, 4$. The next difference is $5$, so the required letter is in position $11 + 5 = 16$ and is R. Q, at position $15$, repeats the difference $4$, and P is the sixteenth letter of the English alphabet.
