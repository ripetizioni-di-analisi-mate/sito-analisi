A code uses the Italian alphabet A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, Z. It selects the letters at positions $1,2,4,7,11$. A program mistakenly uses the English alphabet at those positions. Which of the five selected symbols is the first to change?

- The fourth symbol, from G to I
- The third symbol, from D to E
- None of the five
- The fifth symbol, from M to K

## Explanation

The alphabets agree through position nine, so positions $1,2,4,7$ give A, B, D, G in both. Position $11$ holds M in the Italian alphabet but K in the English alphabet, where J and K follow I.
