A table contains eight entries $a_1,\ldots,a_8$. A new entry of 6 is inserted at the top, shifting each old entry down one position. How does the new sum of odd-position entries compare with the old sum of even-position entries?

- It is 6 less than the previous sum
- It is equal
- It is twice as large
- It exceeds the previous sum by 6

## Explanation

The old positions $2,4,6,8$ become $3,5,7,9$. The new first term is the only extra contribution.
