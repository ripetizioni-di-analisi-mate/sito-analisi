In the following table, the numbers in every row follow the same rule.
$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
4 & 5 & 11 \\ \hline
3 & 6 & 9 \\ \hline
7 & 3 & 11 \\ \hline
6 & 4 & ? \\ \hline
\end{array}
$$
Which number goes in place of the question mark?

- $24$
- $10$
- $15$
- $14$

## Explanation

In each complete row, the third number is the product of the first two minus their sum, since $4 \cdot 5 - (4 + 5) = 11$, $3 \cdot 6 - (3 + 6) = 9$, and $7 \cdot 3 - (7 + 3) = 11$. The missing number in the last row is therefore $6 \cdot 4 - (6 + 4) = 14$. The value $24$ is the product alone. The value $15$ subtracts $9$ from the product, as in the first two rows, but this rule fails in the third row, where $21 - 9 = 12$.
