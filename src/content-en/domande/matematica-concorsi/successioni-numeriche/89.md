A sequence starts at 3 and alternates the increments $+4$ and $-1$. What is the sum of the first eight terms?

- $72$
- $80$
- $76$
- $68$

## Explanation

In pairs, the terms are $(3,7)$, $(6,10)$, $(9,13)$, and $(12,16)$. The pair sums of 10, 16, 22, and 28 give a total of 76.
