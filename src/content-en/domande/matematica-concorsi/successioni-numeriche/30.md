In a geometric sequence the second term is $6$ and the fifth term is $162$. What is the first term?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $2$
- $18$

## Explanation

There are $3$ steps from the second term to the fifth, so $162 = 6 \cdot q^3$ and $q^3 = 27$. The common ratio is therefore $q = 3$. Divide the second term by the common ratio to get the first term, $a_1 = \dfrac{6}{3} = 2$. The value $\dfrac{2}{3}$ uses $q = \dfrac{27}{3} = 9$, dividing the ratio $27$ by the number of steps as one would divide a difference in an arithmetic sequence. The value $3$ is the common ratio rather than the first term.
