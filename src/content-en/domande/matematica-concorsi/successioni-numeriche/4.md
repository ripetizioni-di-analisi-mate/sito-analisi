In the series $2,30,4,27,8,24,16,\ldots$ the odd-position terms double and the even-position terms decrease by $3$. Grouping the series into consecutive pairs, which is the first pair in which the odd-position term exceeds the even-position term?

- $(64,15)$
- $(24,16)$
- $(16,21)$
- $(32,18)$

## Explanation

The pairs are $(2,30),(4,27),(8,24),(16,21),(32,18)$. The first entry is smaller in the first four. It first becomes larger in the fifth; $(64,15)$ also satisfies the inequality but occurs later.
