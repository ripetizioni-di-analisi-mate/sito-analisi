In the sequence $81, 74, 67, 60, \dots$, which number comes immediately after $60$?

- $46$
- $54$
- $52$
- $53$

## Explanation

The **differences** between consecutive terms all equal $-7$, so each term is the previous one minus $7$. The number after $60$ is therefore $60 - 7 = 53$. The value $46$ is one term farther along and comes from subtracting $7$ twice. The value $54$ comes from subtracting $6$ instead of $7$.
