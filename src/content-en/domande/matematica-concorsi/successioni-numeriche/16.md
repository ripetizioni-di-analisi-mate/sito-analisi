A climbing team must gain $1000$ m in elevation. The ascent begins on the morning of the first day. Each day the team climbs $300$ m and each night it descends $200$ m to acclimatize. On which day does the team first reach $1000$ m above its starting elevation?

- On the eighth day
- On the tenth day
- On the fourth day
- On the seventh day

## Explanation

After each day and the following night, the team has gained $300 - 200 = 100$ m. At the start of day $k$, it is therefore $100\,(k - 1)$ m above its starting point. During day $k$ it climbs to $100\,(k - 1) + 300$ m, and this elevation reaches $1000$ m when $k - 1 \geq 7$, that is, for $k \geq 8$. The team therefore reaches its target **on the eighth day**, starting at $700$ m and climbing to $1000$ m. The tenth day comes from dividing $1000$ m by the net gain of $100$ m and overlooking the fact that the final climb takes place before the overnight descent.
