What is the sum $2 + 6 + 18 + \dots + 1458$, in which each term is three times the preceding one?

- $728$
- $2914$
- $2186$
- $4372$

## Explanation

The terms form a geometric sequence with $a_1 = 2$ and $q = 3$. The last term is $1458 = 2 \cdot 729 = 2 \cdot 3^6$, so there are $7$ terms. By the formula for the **sum of the terms of a geometric sequence**,
$$
S_7 = 2 \cdot \frac{3^7 - 1}{3 - 1} = 2 \cdot \frac{2186}{2} = 2186 .
$$
The value $728$ counts $6$ terms instead of $7$. The value $2914 = 2 \cdot 1458 - 2$ applies the rule “twice the last term minus the first,” which holds only for a common ratio of $2$.
