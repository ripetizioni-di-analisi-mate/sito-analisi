Pipes are stacked in horizontal rows in a warehouse. The bottom row holds $20$ pipes, each row holds one fewer than the row below it, and the top row holds $6$. How many pipes are there in all?

- $182$
- $195$
- $390$
- $210$

## Explanation

The rows contain $20, 19, \dots, 6$ pipes, so there are $20 - 6 + 1 = 15$ rows. The numbers of pipes form an arithmetic sequence, so by the formula for the **sum of the terms of an arithmetic sequence**, the total is $\dfrac{(20 + 6) \cdot 15}{2} = 195$. The value $182$ counts $14$ rows instead of $15$. The value $210$ is the sum of the numbers from $1$ through $20$ as though the stack continued all the way to a row containing a single pipe.
