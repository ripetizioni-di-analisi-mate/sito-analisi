What is the sum of the multiples of $4$ between $50$ and $150$?

- $2600$
- $2500$
- $5000$
- $2400$

## Explanation

The smallest multiple of $4$ greater than $50$ is $52$, and the greatest one less than $150$ is $148$. The multiples form an arithmetic sequence with common difference $4$ and $\dfrac{148 - 52}{4} + 1 = 25$ terms, so by the formula for the **sum of the terms of an arithmetic sequence**,
$$
S_{25} = \frac{(52 + 148) \cdot 25}{2} = 100 \cdot 25 = 2500 .
$$
The value $2400$ counts $24$ terms because it omits one of the endpoints, and $2600$ counts $26$ terms. The value $5000$ omits division by $2$.
