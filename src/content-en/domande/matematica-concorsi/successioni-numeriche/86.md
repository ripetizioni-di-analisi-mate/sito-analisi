A real geometric progression has $a_2=6$ and $a_4=54$. Which information is sufficient to choose between the two possible common ratios?

- The ratio $\dfrac{a_4}{a_2}$
- The sign of $a_4$
- The sign of $a_3$
- The absolute value of $a_3$

## Explanation

Two steps separate the positions, so $q^2=\dfrac{54}{6}=9$ and $q$ can be $3$ or $-3$. The term $a_3=6q$ is positive in the first case and negative in the second. Its absolute value is $18$ in both.
