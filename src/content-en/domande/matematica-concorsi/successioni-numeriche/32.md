The number of bacteria in a culture doubles every $20$ minutes. There are $300$ bacteria at 9\:00. How many are there at 11\:00?

- $9600$
- $19\,200$
- $38\,400$
- $3600$

## Explanation

From 9\:00 to 11\:00, $120$ minutes pass. This is $6$ intervals of $20$ minutes, and the number of bacteria doubles during each interval. The values therefore form a **geometric sequence** with common ratio $2$, and at 11\:00 there are $300 \cdot 2^6 = 300 \cdot 64 = 19\,200$ bacteria. The value $9600$ counts $5$ doublings instead of $6$. The value $3600$ multiplies $300$ by $2 \cdot 6 = 12$ instead of $2^6 = 64$.
