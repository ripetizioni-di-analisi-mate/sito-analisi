Which rule generates each term of the sequence $2, 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, \dots$ from the preceding one?

- Multiply by $2$ and subtract $1$
- Multiply by $2$ and add $1$
- Add a number that increases by $1$ at each step
- Add the two preceding terms

## Explanation

Multiplying by $2$ and subtracting $1$ gives $2 \cdot 2 - 1 = 3$, $3 \cdot 2 - 1 = 5$, $5 \cdot 2 - 1 = 9$, $9 \cdot 2 - 1 = 17$, and $17 \cdot 2 - 1 = 33$, so the rule fits every term. Multiplying by $2$ and adding $1$ would already give $5$ in second position. Adding the two preceding terms works for $5 = 2 + 3$ but fails for $9$ because $3 + 5 = 8$. The differences are $1, 2, 4, 8, 16$, so they double instead of increasing by $1$.
