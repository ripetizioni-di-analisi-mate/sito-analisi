A ranking system assigns scores $a_n=(-1)^n n$ for $n=1,2,\ldots$. What is the largest of the first $101$ scores?

- $-101$
- $99$
- $100$
- $101$

## Explanation

Odd-position terms are negative, so the maximum must be an even-position term. Those terms equal their indices and increase. The largest even index at most $101$ is $100$.
