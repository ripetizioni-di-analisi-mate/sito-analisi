The fourth term of an arithmetic sequence is 5 and its eighth term is $-7$. What is the first term?

- $8$
- $17$
- $14$
- $2$

## Explanation

From the fourth term to the eighth there are four steps, so the common difference is $(-7-5)/4=-3$. Going back three steps from the fourth term adds 9, giving 14.
