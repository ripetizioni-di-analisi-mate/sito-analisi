A magazine comes out every $9$ days. Its first issue appeared on January 3 of a non-leap year. On what date does the twentieth issue come out?

- July 2
- June 14
- June 22
- June 23

## Explanation

The publication dates, counted in days from the start of the year, form an arithmetic sequence with $a_1 = 3$ and $d = 9$. The twentieth issue therefore comes out on day $a_{20} = 3 + 19 \cdot 9 = 174$ of the year. January through May contain $31 + 28 + 31 + 30 + 31 = 151$ days in a non-leap year, so day $174$ is June $174 - 151 = 23$. July 2 corresponds to day $183 = 3 + 20 \cdot 9$, which uses $20$ intervals instead of $19$.
