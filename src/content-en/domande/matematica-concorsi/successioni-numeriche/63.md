A hall has 8 rows. The first has 9 seats, and each subsequent row has 3 more. How many seats are there in all?

- $168$
- $147$
- $144$
- $156$

## Explanation

The last row has $9+7\cdot3=30$ seats. Rows at equal distances from opposite ends have a combined 39 seats. There are four such pairs, giving 156 seats in all.
