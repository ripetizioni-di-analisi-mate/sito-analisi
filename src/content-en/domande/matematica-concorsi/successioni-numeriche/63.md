For $n=1,2,\ldots$ a sequence has term $a_n=\dfrac1n-\dfrac1{n+1}$. What is the sum of its first five terms?

- $1$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac56$

## Explanation

Writing out the sum gives $(1-\dfrac{1}{2})+(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3})+\cdots+(\dfrac{1}{5}-\dfrac{1}{6})$. Each internal fraction appears once with each sign. Only $1-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{5}{6}$ remains.
