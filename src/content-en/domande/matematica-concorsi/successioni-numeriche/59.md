A printer concatenates positive integers with no spaces, giving the digit sequence $12345678910111213\ldots$. What is the fifteenth digit, counting the first digit as position $1$?

- $1$
- $5$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

The first nine digits come from $1$ through $9$. Positions $10,11$ spell $10$, positions $12,13$ spell $11$, and positions $14,15$ spell $12$. The fifteenth digit is the final digit of $12$, namely $2$.
