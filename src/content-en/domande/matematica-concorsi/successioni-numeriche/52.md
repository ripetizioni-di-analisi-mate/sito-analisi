The terms in odd-numbered positions increase by 5 starting from 4, while those in even-numbered positions decrease by 2 starting from 20. What is the fifth term of the full sequence?

- $14$
- $16$
- $9$
- $18$

## Explanation

The odd-numbered positions through the requested term are 1, 3, and 5. Their values are 4, 9, and 14, respectively.
