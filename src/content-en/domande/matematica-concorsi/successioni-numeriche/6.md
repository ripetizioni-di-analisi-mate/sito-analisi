In which of the following sequences is the ratio of each term to the preceding one constant?

- $8, 12, 16, 20$
- $8, 16, 48, 192$
- $8, 12, 20, 36$
- $8, 12, 18, 27$

## Explanation

The **ratio** is constant when each term is the preceding term multiplied by the same number. In the sequence $8, 12, 18, 27$, we have $\dfrac{12}{8} = \dfrac{18}{12} = \dfrac{27}{18} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, so the ratio is constant. In the sequence $8, 12, 16, 20$, the differences are constant but the ratios $\dfrac{12}{8} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ and $\dfrac{16}{12} = \dfrac{4}{3}$ differ. In the sequence $8, 16, 48, 192$, the ratios are $2, 3, 4$, and in the sequence $8, 12, 20, 36$, the differences $4, 8, 16$ double.
