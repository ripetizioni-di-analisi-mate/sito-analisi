The sequence $3,4,7,11,18,29,\ldots$ continues by adding the two most recent terms. How many of its first $30$ terms are even?

- $9$
- $20$
- $15$
- $10$

## Explanation

The first parities are odd, even, odd, odd, even. The pair “odd, even” has returned, so the three-term pattern “odd, even, odd” now repeats. There are ten complete blocks in the first $30$ terms, each containing one even term.
