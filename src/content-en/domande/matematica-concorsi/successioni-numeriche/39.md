Using the $21$-letter Italian alphabet and starting again at A after Z, which letter comes next in the sequence Q, T, Z, C, …?

- E
- G
- F
- D

## Explanation

The letters Q, T, Z occupy positions $15, 18, 21$ in the Italian alphabet, so the sequence advances $3$ places at a time. After Z, start again at A. The next three places after Z are A, B, C, so C fits the rule. The next three after C are D, E, F, so the required letter is F. E comes from counting C itself as the first of the three places.
