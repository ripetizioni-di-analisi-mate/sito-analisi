A register uses the cyclic 21-letter Italian alphabet. Moving exactly three positions forward at each jump, it finishes on C. Exactly two jumps were made. What was the starting letter if Q, R, S, T, U, V, Z are the last seven?

- Q
- F
- T
- Z

## Explanation

Reverse the route by subtracting six positions from C, which is third. Add $21$ to position $3-6=-3$ to return to the cycle, giving position $18$, or T. The forward check is T, Z, C.
