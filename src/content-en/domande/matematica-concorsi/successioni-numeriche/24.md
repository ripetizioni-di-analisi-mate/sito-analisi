An office has $200$ reams of paper in stock. It uses $30$ on the first day and $4$ fewer each subsequent day than the day before. How many reams remain after $6$ days?

- $120$
- $80$
- $92$
- $20$

## Explanation

The numbers of reams used each day form an arithmetic sequence with $a_1 = 30$ and $d = -4$, so the office uses $a_6 = 30 - 5 \cdot 4 = 10$ reams on the sixth day. By the formula for the **sum of the terms of an arithmetic sequence**, in $6$ days the office uses $\dfrac{(30 + 10) \cdot 6}{2} = 120$ reams, leaving $200 - 120 = 80$. The value $120$ counts the reams used rather than those remaining. The value $92$ comes from $a_6 = 30 - 6 \cdot 4 = 6$, which counts one extra step.
