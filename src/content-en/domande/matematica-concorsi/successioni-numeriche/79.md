In round 1, line A has 24 people and line B has 45. In each subsequent round, line A gains 2 people and line B loses 5. In which round do the two lines have the same number of people?

- $7$
- $4$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The initial difference is 21, and it decreases by $2+5=7$ at each step. It takes three steps from round 1, so the lines are the same length in round 4.
