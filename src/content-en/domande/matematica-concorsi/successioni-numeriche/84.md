In an arithmetic sequence, the first three terms sum to 21 and the next three sum to 48. What is the first term?

- $4$
- $7$
- $1$
- $10$

## Explanation

The mean of each group of three equals its middle term, so $a_2=7$ and $a_5=16$. The common difference is $(16-7)/3=3$, so $a_1=7-3=4$.
