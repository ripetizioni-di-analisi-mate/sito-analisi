An index scans the 26-letter English alphabet, selecting positions equal to positive squares in increasing order. Which letter is selected after A, D, I, P?

- W
- X
- Y
- Z

## Explanation

In the English alphabet, A, D, I, P occupy positions $1, 4, 9, 16$, the **squares** of $1, 2, 3, 4$. The next position is therefore $5^2 = 25$, occupied by Y in the English alphabet. The differences between positions are $3, 5, 7$ and increase by $2$, so the next difference is $9$ and leads to the same position $16 + 9 = 25$. W, at position $23$, repeats the difference $7$. X, at position $24$, increases the difference by $1$ instead of $2$.
