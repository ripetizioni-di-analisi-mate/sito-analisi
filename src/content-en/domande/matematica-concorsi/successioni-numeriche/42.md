In every row of the table, the fourth number is obtained from the first three using the same rule.
$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
3 & 4 & 5 & 17 \\ \hline
2 & 6 & 3 & 16 \\ \hline
5 & 7 & 2 & 9 \\ \hline
4 & 8 & 3 & ? \\ \hline
\end{array}
$$
Which number replaces the question mark?

- $28$
- $20$
- $15$
- $96$

## Explanation

In each complete row, the fourth number is the product of the second and third minus the first, since $4 \cdot 5 - 3 = 17$, $6 \cdot 3 - 2 = 16$, and $7 \cdot 2 - 5 = 9$. The missing number in the last row is therefore $8 \cdot 3 - 4 = 20$. The value $28$ adds the first number instead of subtracting it, and $96$ is the product of all three numbers.
