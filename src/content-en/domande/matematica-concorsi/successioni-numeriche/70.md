A sequence starts with 2, 5, 10, and 17. The increments between terms are consecutive odd numbers in increasing order. What is the sixth term?

- $35$
- $26$
- $32$
- $37$

## Explanation

The increments used so far are 3, 5, and 7. The next two are 9 and 11, taking 17 to 26 and then to 37.
