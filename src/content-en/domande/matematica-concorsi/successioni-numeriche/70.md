Two sequences start with $a_1=5$ and $b_1=12$. At each position their increments agree: $a_{n+1}-a_n=b_{n+1}-b_n$, but that increment may vary from one position to the next. What is $b_{100}-a_{100}$?

- It cannot be determined
- $0$
- $700$
- $7$

## Explanation

Adding the same increment to both sequences preserves the difference $b_n-a_n$ at every step. It therefore keeps its initial value of $12-5=7$ even at the hundredth position.
