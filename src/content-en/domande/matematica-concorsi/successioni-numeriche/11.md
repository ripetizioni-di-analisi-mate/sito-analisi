A list has $a_1=5$ and $a_{n+1}=2a_n$. A printing error halves every term from the fourth onward. Between which consecutive positions does the doubling rule fail in the printed list?

- Between every pair from the fourth onward
- Only between the third and fourth
- Only between the fourth and fifth
- Between the second and third and between the third and fourth

## Explanation

The printed third term is $20$ and the fourth changes from $40$ to $20$, giving ratio $1$ at that step. Before the cutoff neither member of a pair is altered; after it both are halved and their ratio stays $2$.
