What is the seventh term of the sequence $-3, 6, -12, 24, \dots$?

- $192$
- $-192$
- $384$
- $96$

## Explanation

Each term is the preceding one multiplied by $-2$, so the sequence has **alternating signs**, with first term $-3$ and ratio $-2$. To obtain the seventh term, multiply $-3$ by $-2$ six times, giving $-3 \cdot (-2)^6 = -3 \cdot 64 = -192$. Its position confirms its sign because odd-position terms have the sign of the first term. The value $192$ ignores the sign, and $384 = -3 \cdot (-2)^7$ is the eighth term.
