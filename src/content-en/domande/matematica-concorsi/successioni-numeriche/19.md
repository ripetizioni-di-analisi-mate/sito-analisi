In the sequence $6, 24, 60, 120, 210, \dots$, which number comes after $210$?

- $336$
- $300$
- $343$
- $420$

## Explanation

Each term is the product of three consecutive natural numbers, since $6 = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3$, $24 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4$, $60 = 3 \cdot 4 \cdot 5$, $120 = 4 \cdot 5 \cdot 6$, and $210 = 5 \cdot 6 \cdot 7$. The next term is therefore $6 \cdot 7 \cdot 8 = 336$. The differences $18, 36, 60, 90$ give the same result. Their differences $18, 24, 30$ increase by $6$, so the next first difference is $90 + 36 = 126$ and $210 + 126 = 336$. The value $300$ repeats the last difference $90$, and $343$ is the cube of $7$.
