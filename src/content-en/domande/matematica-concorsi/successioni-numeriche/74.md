Which of the following sequences, defined for integers $n\ge1$, is a counterexample to the claim “every strictly increasing sequence of positive integers is arithmetic”?

- $a_n=n^2$
- $a_n=(-1)^n n$
- $a_n=3n+1$
- $a_n=7$

## Explanation

The squares are positive and strictly increasing, but their increments $(n+1)^2-n^2=2n+1$ vary with $n$. The sequence $3n+1$ is arithmetic, while the constant and alternating-sign sequences do not meet the strictly increasing assumption.
