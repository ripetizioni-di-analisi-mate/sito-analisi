The terms in odd-numbered positions start at 4 and increase by 4. The terms in even-numbered positions start at 25 and decrease by 2. What is the first odd-numbered position whose value exceeds the value in the even-numbered position immediately after it?

- $7$
- $11$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

At the fourth comparison, in positions 7 and 8, the values are 16 and 19. At the fifth, in positions 9 and 10, they are 20 and 17, and the odd-position value minus the even-position value increases by 6 with each comparison.
