A service-desk report records for day $n$ the total $s_n=b_n+b_{n+1}+b_{n+2}$ over three consecutive days. What determines the change $s_{n+1}-s_n$ when the reporting window advances?

- The sum of the two middle values
- Three times the change from the first day to the second
- The incoming value $b_{n+3}$ minus $b_{n+2}$
- The incoming value $b_{n+3}$ minus the outgoing value $b_n$

## Explanation

The new total is $b_{n+1}+b_{n+2}+b_{n+3}$. Subtracting the old one cancels the two days common to both windows. The incoming day remains with a positive sign and the outgoing day with a negative sign.
