The cubes of the positive natural numbers are listed in increasing order. What position does 216 occupy, counting from 1?

- $4$
- $6$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

In the **sequence of cubes**, the term in position $n$ equals $n^3$. Since $6^3=216$, the number is in sixth position.
