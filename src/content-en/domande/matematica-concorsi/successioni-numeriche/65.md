An arithmetic sequence starts with 5 and has common difference 4. What is the sum of the terms in positions 2, 4, 6, and 8?

- $68$
- $92$
- $84$
- $76$

## Explanation

The requested terms are 9, 17, 25, and 33. They can be grouped into two pairs that each sum to 42, giving 84.
