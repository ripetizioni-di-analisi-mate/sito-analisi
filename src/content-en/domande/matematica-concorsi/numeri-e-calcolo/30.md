What is $8.4651$ rounded to the nearest hundredth?

- $8.46$
- $8.47$
- $8.5$
- $8.465$

## Explanation

To round to the nearest hundredth, look at the thousandths digit, which in $8.4651$ is $5$. By the **rounding rule**, increase the hundredths digit by $1$, so the rounded number is $8.47$. The value $8.46$ truncates the number to hundredths, and $8.5$ rounds it to tenths.
