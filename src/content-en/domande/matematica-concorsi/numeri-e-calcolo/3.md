What is the value of the expression $-2^4 + (-2)^3 \cdot (-1)$?

- $24$
- $-24$
- $8$
- $-8$

## Explanation

In the expression $-2^4$ the exponent applies only to $2$, so $-2^4 = -16$. By contrast, $(-2)^3 = -8$ because the base is negative and the exponent is odd. The product $(-8) \cdot (-1)$ is positive by the **rule of signs** and equals $8$, so the expression equals $-16 + 8 = -8$. The result $24$ comes from calculating $16 + 8$, which treats $-2^4$ as though it were $(-2)^4$.
