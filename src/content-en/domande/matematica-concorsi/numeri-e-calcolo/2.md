Which of the following lists gives the numbers $-0.5$, $0.3$, $-2$, and $-1.5$ in increasing order?

- $-2$; $-1.5$; $-0.5$; $0.3$
- $-0.5$; $-1.5$; $-2$; $0.3$
- $0.3$; $-0.5$; $-1.5$; $-2$
- $-0.5$; $0.3$; $-1.5$; $-2$

## Explanation

In increasing order, the smallest number comes first. The three negative numbers are less than $0.3$, and of two negative numbers the one with the greater absolute value is smaller, so $-2 < -1.5 < -0.5$. The increasing order is therefore $-2$, $-1.5$, $-0.5$, $0.3$. The list $-0.5$, $-1.5$, $-2$, $0.3$ orders the negative numbers by absolute value as though their signs did not matter.
