What is the value of the expression $40 - 12 + 3 \cdot 2 - 1$?

- $21$
- $61$
- $33$
- $31$

## Explanation

By the **order of operations**, evaluate the product $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ first. Then perform the remaining additions and subtractions from left to right, giving $40 - 12 + 6 - 1 = 28 + 6 - 1 = 33$. The result $21$ comes from calculating $40 - (12 + 6) - 1$, performing the addition before the subtraction to its left. The result $61$ comes from performing all the operations in the order in which they are written, giving $(40 - 12 + 3) \cdot 2 - 1$.
