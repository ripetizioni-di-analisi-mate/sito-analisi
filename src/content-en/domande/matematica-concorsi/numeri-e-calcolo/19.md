What is the value of the expression $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{1}{6}$?

- $\dfrac{19}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The least common denominator is $\operatorname{mcm}(3, 4, 6) = 12$, so
$$
\frac{2}{3} + \frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{8}{12} + \frac{9}{12} - \frac{2}{12} = \frac{15}{12} = \frac{5}{4} .
$$
The result $\dfrac{19}{12}$ adds $\dfrac{2}{12}$ instead of subtracting it. The result $4$ comes from working separately with the numerators and denominators, giving $\dfrac{2 + 3 - 1}{3 + 4 - 6} = \dfrac{4}{1}$.
