What is the value of $\sqrt{0.0016}$?

- $0.4$
- $0.004$
- $0.04$
- $0.0008$

## Explanation

Write the radicand as a fraction, $0.0016 = \dfrac{16}{10\,000}$. By the **properties of roots**, the square root of a quotient is the quotient of the square roots, so $\sqrt{0.0016} = \dfrac{\sqrt{16}}{\sqrt{10\,000}} = \dfrac{4}{100} = 0.04$. Indeed, $0.04^2 = 0.0016$. The square of $0.4$ is $0.16$, and $0.0008$ is half the radicand.
