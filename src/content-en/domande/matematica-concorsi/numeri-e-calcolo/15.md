A rectangular floor measures $360$ cm by $280$ cm. It is to be covered with identical square tiles as large as possible without cutting any of them. How many tiles are needed?

- $40$
- $16$
- $2\,520$
- $63$

## Explanation

The side length of a tile must divide both dimensions exactly and be as large as possible, so it is the **greatest common divisor** of $360$ and $280$. Since $360 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$ and $280 = 2^3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$, the side length is $\operatorname{MCD}(360, 280) = 2^3 \cdot 5 = 40$ cm. The floor fits $\dfrac{360}{40} = 9$ tiles along one side and $\dfrac{280}{40} = 7$ along the other, so $9 \cdot 7 = 63$ tiles are needed. The result $16$ adds the counts along the two sides instead of multiplying them, and $40$ gives the side length in centimeters.
