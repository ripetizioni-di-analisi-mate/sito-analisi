For how many integer values of $n$ between $1$ and $11$, inclusive, does $\dfrac{n}{12}$ have a terminating decimal representation?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The denominator $12=2^2\cdot3$ has a factor of $3$ that must cancel to give a **terminating decimal**. Therefore $n$ must be a multiple of $3$, and the only such values in the interval are $3$, $6$, and $9$.
