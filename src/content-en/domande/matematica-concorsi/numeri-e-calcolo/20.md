What number, when multiplied by $\dfrac{2}{3}$, gives $\dfrac{8}{9}$?

- $\dfrac{16}{27}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{27}{16}$

## Explanation

The required number is $\dfrac{8}{9}$ divided by $\dfrac{2}{3}$. To divide, multiply by the **reciprocal** of the divisor, so the number is $\dfrac{8}{9} \cdot \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{24}{18} = \dfrac{4}{3}$. Indeed, $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{4}{3} = \dfrac{8}{9}$. The result $\dfrac{16}{27}$ is the product $\dfrac{8}{9} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3}$, obtained by multiplying instead of dividing.
