Which of the following numbers is the result of $123 \cdot 456$?

- $56\,088$
- $56\,188$
- $55\,988$
- $57\,088$

## Explanation

The four answers have the same order of magnitude and the same last digit, $8$, so use the **divisibility tests** to check them. The digits of $123$ add up to $6$ and those of $456$ add up to $15$, so both factors are multiples of $3$ and the product is a multiple of $9$. The digit sum of a multiple of $9$ is a multiple of $9$. Only $56\,088$ among the answers has this property, with a digit sum of $27$. The other answers have digit sums of $28$, $35$, and $28$. The full calculation indeed gives $123 \cdot 456 = 56\,088$.
