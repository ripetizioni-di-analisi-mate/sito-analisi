Which of the following numbers is greatest?

- $0.519$
- $0.5099$
- $0.52$
- $0.5\overline{1}$

## Explanation

All the numbers have integer part $0$ and tenths digit $5$, so compare the hundredths digits. The number $0.52$ has $2$ in the hundredths place, while $0.519$, $0.5099$, and $0.5\overline{1} = 0.5111\ldots$ have $1$ or $0$ there. The greatest is therefore $0.52$. The number $0.5099$ may appeal to someone who thinks more digits after the decimal point means a larger number, and $0.519$ to someone who compares $519$ and $52$ as integers.
