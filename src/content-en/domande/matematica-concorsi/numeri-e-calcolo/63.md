What is the only digit that can replace $x$ in $47x2$ to make the number divisible by $12$?

- $1$
- $5$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

**Divisibility by $12$** requires divisibility by both $3$ and $4$. The digit sum is $13+x$, giving the candidates $2,5,8$, but the last two digits form a number divisible by $4$ only in the case $52$.
