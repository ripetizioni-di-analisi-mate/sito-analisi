Which number is an integer but does not belong to the set of natural numbers?

- $12$
- $\sqrt{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The number $-8$ is a **negative integer**, so it is not natural. By contrast, $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is not an integer, and both $\sqrt{16}=4$ and $12$ are natural numbers.
