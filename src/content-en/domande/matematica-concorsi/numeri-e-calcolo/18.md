Which of the following fractions is equivalent to $\dfrac{18}{24}$?

- $\dfrac{21}{27}$
- $\dfrac{6}{12}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{15}{20}$

## Explanation

The greatest common divisor of $18$ and $24$ is $6$, so $\dfrac{18}{24} = \dfrac{3}{4}$. By the rule for **equivalent fractions**, $\dfrac{15}{20} = \dfrac{3 \cdot 5}{4 \cdot 5}$ also equals $\dfrac{3}{4}$, so the two fractions are equivalent. The fraction $\dfrac{21}{27}$ comes from adding $3$ to both the numerator and denominator and equals $\dfrac{7}{9}$, which differs from $\dfrac{3}{4}$. The fraction $\dfrac{6}{12}$ comes from subtracting $12$ from both terms and equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
