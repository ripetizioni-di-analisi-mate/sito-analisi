Which of the following numbers is closest to $-\dfrac{2}{5}$ on the number line?

- $-0.51$
- $0.38$
- $-0.45$
- $-0.34$

## Explanation

In decimal form $-\dfrac{2}{5}=-0.4$, and the **distance** between two numbers on the line is the absolute value of their difference. The distances from $-0.4$ are $|-0.45+0.4|=0.05$, $|-0.34+0.4|=0.06$, $|-0.51+0.4|=0.11$ and $|0.38+0.4|=0.78$. So the number closest to $-\dfrac{2}{5}$ is $-0.45$. The number $0.38$ has the absolute value closest to $0.4$, but it lies on the other side of zero, so its distance from $-0.4$ is the largest.
