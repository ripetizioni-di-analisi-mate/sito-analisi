Which of the following numbers is closest to $-\dfrac25$ on the number line?

- $-0.51$
- $0.38$
- $-0.45$
- $-0.34$

## Explanation

The reference value is $-0.4$, and the **distance** is the absolute value of the difference. In increasing order, the distances are $0.05$, $0.06$, $0.11$, and $0.78$, so the closest number is $-0.45$.
