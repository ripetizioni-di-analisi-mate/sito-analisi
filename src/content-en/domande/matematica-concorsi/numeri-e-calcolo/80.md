Dividing a positive integer by $17$ gives a quotient of $24$ and a remainder of $11$. What remainder do you get if you add $57$ to the integer and divide again by $17$?

- $16$
- $0$
- $11$
- $6$

## Explanation

The original integer is $17\cdot24+11$. After the addition, the part that determines the **remainder** is $11+57=68=17\cdot4$, so the new remainder is $0$.
