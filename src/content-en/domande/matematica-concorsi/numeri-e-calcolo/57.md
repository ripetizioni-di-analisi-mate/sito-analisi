What is the normalized scientific notation for $0.000\,072$?

- $7.2\cdot10^{-6}$
- $7.2\cdot10^{5}$
- $7.2\cdot10^{-4}$
- $7.2\cdot10^{-5}$

## Explanation

To obtain a coefficient between $1$ inclusive and $10$ exclusive, move the decimal point five places to the right. The **scientific notation** is therefore $7.2\cdot10^{-5}$.
