On a screening test with $30$ questions, each correct answer is worth $4$ points, each incorrect answer $-1$ point, and each unanswered question $0$ points. A candidate answers $7$ questions incorrectly and leaves $5$ unanswered. How many points does the candidate earn?

- $65$
- $72$
- $85$
- $79$

## Explanation

The number of correct answers is $30 - 7 - 5 = 18$. The score is therefore $18 \cdot 4 + 7 \cdot (-1) = 72 - 7 = 65$ points. The result $85$ counts the $5$ unanswered questions as correct as well, giving $23 \cdot 4 - 7$. The result $79$ adds the $7$ points for incorrect answers instead of subtracting them.
