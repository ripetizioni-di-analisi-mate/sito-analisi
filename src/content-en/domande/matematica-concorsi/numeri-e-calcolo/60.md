Without calculating the exact product, determine which interval contains $39\cdot 51$.

- Between $2\,050$ and $2\,100$
- Between $2\,000$ and $2\,050$
- Between $1\,950$ and $2\,000$
- Between $1\,900$ and $1\,950$

## Explanation

The product is greater than $39\cdot50=1\,950$. Using the **distributive property**, we add only $39$ units, so the result is also less than $2\,000$.
