Which of the following expressions has a negative value?

- $(-2)^3 \cdot (-3)^2 \cdot (-1)$
- $-(-2)^2 \cdot (-3)$
- $(-1)^7 - (-2)^3$
- $-4^2 + (-3)^2 - (-2)$

## Explanation

In the expression $-4^2$ the exponent applies only to $4$, so $-4^2 = -16$. Since $(-3)^2 = 9$ and $-(-2) = 2$, we obtain $-4^2 + (-3)^2 - (-2) = -16 + 9 + 2 = -5$, which is negative. The other expressions equal $(-2)^3 \cdot (-3)^2 \cdot (-1) = (-8) \cdot 9 \cdot (-1) = 72$, $-(-2)^2 \cdot (-3) = -4 \cdot (-3) = 12$, and $(-1)^7 - (-2)^3 = -1 + 8 = 7$ and are therefore positive. The expression $(-2)^3 \cdot (-3)^2 \cdot (-1)$ may appear to have three negative factors, but $(-3)^2 = 9$ is positive, leaving only two negative factors.
