For what value of $x$ is $\dfrac{x-\dfrac13}{\dfrac56}=\dfrac{7}{10}$ true?

- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac7{12}$
- $\dfrac{31}{30}$

## Explanation

Multiplying by the denominator gives $x-\dfrac13=\dfrac7{10}\cdot\dfrac56=\dfrac7{12}$. Finally, use the **common denominator** $12$ to add $\dfrac13$, obtaining $x=\dfrac{11}{12}$.
