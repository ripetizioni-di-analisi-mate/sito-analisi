Among the integers from $1$ to $240$ inclusive, how many are multiples of $6$ or $14$ but not both?

- $57$
- $42$
- $52$
- $47$

## Explanation

There are $40$ multiples of $6$, $17$ multiples of $14$, and $5$ multiples of their **least common multiple**, $42$. To exclude the numbers counted in both groups, subtract $5$ twice, obtaining $40+17-10=47$.
