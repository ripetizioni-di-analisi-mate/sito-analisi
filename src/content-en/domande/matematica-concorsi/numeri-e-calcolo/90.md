Two positive measurements were rounded to the nearest tenth, with $5$ rounded up, giving $2.4$ and $1.7$. Which value could be their exact sum?

- $4.18$
- $3.99$
- $4.25$
- $4.20$

## Explanation

The two **rounding intervals** are $[2.35;2.45)$ and $[1.65;1.75)$. Their sum is therefore at least $4.00$ and less than $4.20$. For example, $2.44+1.74=4.18$.
