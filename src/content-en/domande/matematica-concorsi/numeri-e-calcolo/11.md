How many prime numbers lie between $30$ and $50$?

- $5$
- $6$
- $4$
- $7$

## Explanation

Even numbers greater than $2$ are divisible by $2$, so we need only check the odd numbers from $31$ to $49$. Of these, $33 = 3 \cdot 11$, $35 = 5 \cdot 7$, $39 = 3 \cdot 13$, $45 = 5 \cdot 9$, and $49 = 7 \cdot 7$ are composite. None of the remaining numbers $31$, $37$, $41$, $43$, and $47$ is divisible by $2$, $3$, $5$, or $7$. Since $11^2 = 121$ exceeds $50$, they are **prime**. There are therefore $5$ prime numbers in the interval. The result $6$ also counts $49$, the square of $7$.
