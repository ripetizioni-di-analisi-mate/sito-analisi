A warehouse has $108$ blue pens, $180$ black pens, and $252$ red pens. What is the largest number of identical kits that can be made using all the pens?

- $12$
- $36$
- $72$
- $18$

## Explanation

The number of kits must divide all three quantities, so find the **greatest common divisor**. The greatest common divisor is $36$, and each kit contains $3$ blue pens, $5$ black pens, and $7$ red pens.
