What is the expression $2^{10} + 2^{10}$ equal to?

- $2^{20}$
- $2^{11}$
- $4^{20}$
- $2^{100}$

## Explanation

The sum of two equal terms is twice one of them, so $2^{10} + 2^{10} = 2 \cdot 2^{10}$. By the product rule for powers with the same base, $2 \cdot 2^{10} = 2^{1 + 10} = 2^{11}$. The result $2^{20}$ adds the exponents as though the operation were multiplication, and indeed $2^{20} = 2^{10} \cdot 2^{10}$. The result $4^{20}$ adds both the bases and the exponents.
