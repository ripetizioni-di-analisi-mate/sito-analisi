What is the value of $\sqrt{3^6 + 3^6 + 3^6}$?

- $27\sqrt{3}$
- $81$
- $729$
- $9\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

The sum of three equal terms is three times one of them, so $3^6 + 3^6 + 3^6 = 3 \cdot 3^6 = 3^7$. Since $3^7 = 3^6 \cdot 3$ and $\sqrt{3^6} = 3^3 = 27$, we have $\sqrt{3^7} = 27\sqrt{3}$. The result $81$ adds the square roots of the three terms, $27 + 27 + 27$, whereas the square root of a sum is not the sum of the square roots. The result $729$ writes the sum as $9^6$ by adding the bases, and $\sqrt{9^6} = 9^3 = 729$.
