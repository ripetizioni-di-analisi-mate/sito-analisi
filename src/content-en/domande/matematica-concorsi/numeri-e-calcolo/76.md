What is $\sqrt{\dfrac{49}{144}}+\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1}{8}}$ equal to?

- $\dfrac{13}{24}$
- $\dfrac{8}{12}$
- $\dfrac{13}{12}$
- $\dfrac{19}{12}$

## Explanation

The **root of a positive quotient** is the corresponding root of its numerator divided by that of its denominator. Then add $\dfrac7{12}$ and $\dfrac12=\dfrac6{12}$ to obtain $\dfrac{13}{12}$.
