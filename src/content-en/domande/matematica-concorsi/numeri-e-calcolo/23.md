An office has $45$ employees. Today $\dfrac{2}{9}$ of them are working remotely and all the others are in the office. How many are in the office?

- $10$
- $40$
- $35$
- $43$

## Explanation

To find $\dfrac{2}{9}$ of $45$, divide $45$ by $9$ and multiply the result by $2$. Thus $5 \cdot 2 = 10$ employees are working remotely. There are therefore $45 - 10 = 35$ employees in the office, or $\dfrac{7}{9}$ of the total. The result $10$ counts the employees working remotely. The result $40$ subtracts only one ninth of the total, or $5$ people.
