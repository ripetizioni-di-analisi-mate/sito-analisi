What is the value of the expression $\dfrac{1}{1 + \dfrac{1}{1 + \dfrac{1}{2}}}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Start with the innermost fraction. We have $1 + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, and the **reciprocal** of $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Thus the denominator of the main fraction is $1 + \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{5}{3}$, and the expression equals the reciprocal of $\dfrac{5}{3}$, namely $\dfrac{3}{5}$. The result $\dfrac{5}{3}$ stops at the denominator without taking its reciprocal. The result $\dfrac{2}{5}$ uses $\dfrac{3}{2}$ instead of its reciprocal, giving $\dfrac{1}{1 + \dfrac{3}{2}}$.
