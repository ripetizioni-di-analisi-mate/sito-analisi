What is $(6\cdot10^4)(5\cdot10^{-7})$ in normalized scientific notation?

- $3\cdot10^{-3}$
- $3\cdot10^{-4}$
- $3\cdot10^2$
- $3\cdot10^{-2}$

## Explanation

The product of the coefficients is $30$ and the sum of the exponents is $-3$. To obtain **normalized scientific notation**, rewrite the result as $30\cdot10^{-3}=3\cdot10^{-2}$.
