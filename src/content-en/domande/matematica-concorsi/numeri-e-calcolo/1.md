At 6\:00 a thermometer reads $-4$ °C, and at 14\:00 it reads $7$ °C. By how many degrees did the temperature rise between the two readings?

- $3$ °C
- $11$ °C
- $-3$ °C
- $-11$ °C

## Explanation

The increase is the final temperature minus the initial temperature, or $7 - (-4)$. The minus sign before $-4$ reverses the sign of the number, so $7 - (-4) = 7 + 4 = 11$. The temperature therefore rose by $11$ °C. The result $3$ °C comes from calculating $7 - 4$, which treats the initial temperature as positive.
