Which of the following numbers is divisible by $11$?

- $3\,512$
- $5\,593$
- $11\,111$
- $8\,371$

## Explanation

To use the **divisibility test for 11**, subtract the sum of the digits in even-numbered positions from the sum of those in odd-numbered positions. For $8\,371$, the difference is $(8 + 7) - (3 + 1) = 11$, a multiple of $11$, so $8\,371$ is divisible by $11$. For $3\,512$, the difference is $(3 + 1) - (5 + 2) = -3$, for $5\,593$ it is $(5 + 9) - (5 + 3) = 6$, and for $11\,111$ it is $(1 + 1 + 1) - (1 + 1) = 1$. None of these values is a multiple of $11$. The numbers $3\,512$ and $5\,593$ have digit sums of $11$ and $22$, respectively, and may appeal to someone who applies to $11$ the digit-sum test that works for $3$ and $9$.
