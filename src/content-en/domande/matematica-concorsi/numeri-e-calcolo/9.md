Which of the following numbers is divisible by $3$ but not by $9$?

- $6\,363$
- $2\,413$
- $4\,521$
- $7\,013$

## Explanation

By the **divisibility tests**, a number is divisible by $3$ if its digit sum is divisible by $3$, and by $9$ if its digit sum is divisible by $9$. The digit sums are $12$ for $4\,521$, $18$ for $6\,363$, $10$ for $2\,413$, and $11$ for $7\,013$. Only $12$ is a multiple of $3$ without being a multiple of $9$, so the required number is $4\,521$. The number $6\,363$ is divisible by both $3$ and $9$, while $2\,413$ and $7\,013$ end in $3$ and may appeal to someone who looks at the last digit instead of the digit sum.
