Which of the following numbers is irrational?

- $\sqrt{0.09}$
- $0.\overline{27}$
- $\sqrt{18}$
- $\sqrt[3]{-27}$

## Explanation

A number is rational if it can be written as a ratio of two integers. We have $\sqrt{0.09} = 0.3 = \dfrac{3}{10}$, $\sqrt[3]{-27} = -3$, and, by the rule for finding the **fractional equivalent** of a repeating decimal, $0.\overline{27} = \dfrac{27}{99} = \dfrac{3}{11}$, so these three numbers are rational. By contrast, $\sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$. Since $\sqrt{2}$ has infinitely many nonrepeating decimal digits, $\sqrt{18}$ is also **irrational**. The number $0.\overline{27}$ has infinitely many decimal digits, but they repeat, and every repeating decimal equals a fraction.
