Which inequality is true?

- $-\dfrac43<-1.4<-\dfrac54$
- $-1.4<-\dfrac54<-\dfrac43$
- $-\dfrac54<-\dfrac43<-1.4$
- $-1.4<-\dfrac43<-\dfrac54$

## Explanation

The corresponding positive values are $1.4$, $1.\overline3$, and $1.25$, in decreasing order. Reversing their signs reverses the **directions of the inequalities**, giving $-1.4<-\dfrac43<-\dfrac54$.
