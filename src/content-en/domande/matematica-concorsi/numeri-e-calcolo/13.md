What is the greatest common divisor of $84$ and $120$?

- $840$
- $12$
- $24$
- $6$

## Explanation

The prime factorizations are $84 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7$ and $120 = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$. The **greatest common divisor** is the product of the common factors, each raised to its smallest exponent, so $\operatorname{MCD}(84, 120) = 2^2 \cdot 3 = 12$. The value $24 = 2^3 \cdot 3$ uses the larger exponent for $2$, and $840$ is the least common multiple. Since the GCD cannot exceed the smaller of the two numbers, $840$ can be ruled out without calculation.
