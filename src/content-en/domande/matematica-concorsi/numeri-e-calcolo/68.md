A committee processes $\dfrac29$ of its cases on Monday and $\dfrac37$ of the remaining cases on Tuesday. What fraction of the original total remains after Tuesday?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac59$
- $\dfrac49$
- $\dfrac47$

## Explanation

After Monday, $\dfrac79$ of the total remains. After Tuesday, $\dfrac47$ of that **remaining quantity** is left, so the final fraction is $\dfrac79\cdot\dfrac47=\dfrac49$.
