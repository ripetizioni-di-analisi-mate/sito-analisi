For which consecutive integers $k$ and $k+1$ is their product negative?

- Only for $k=-1$
- Only for $k=0$
- For no pair
- For all $k<0$

## Explanation

Two consecutive integers of opposite signs would have to have zero strictly between them. Each pair straddling an endpoint at zero contains zero, so its product is zero.
