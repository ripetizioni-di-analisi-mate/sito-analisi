For which integers $k$ is the product $k\,(k+1)$ of two consecutive integers negative?

- Only for $k=-1$
- Only for $k=0$
- For no value of $k$
- For all values $k<0$

## Explanation

A product of two factors is negative only if the factors have opposite signs. Since $k+1>k$, the negative factor can only be $k$, so the conditions
$$
\begin{cases}
k<0 \\
k+1>0
\end{cases}
$$
must hold at the same time, that is $-1<k<0$. There is no integer between $-1$ and $0$, so the product is not negative for any value of $k$. For $k=-1$ and for $k=0$ one of the two factors is zero, so the product is $0$, which is not a negative number. For $k\le-2$ both factors are negative, and the product is positive.
