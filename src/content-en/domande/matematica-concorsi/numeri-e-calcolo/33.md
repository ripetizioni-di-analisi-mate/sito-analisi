What is the value of the expression $\dfrac{10^4 \cdot 10^{-6}}{10^{-3}}$?

- $10^{-5}$
- $10^{-2}$
- $10$
- $10^{7}$

## Explanation

By the **properties of powers**, add the exponents in the product and subtract them in the quotient, so the exponent of the result is $4 + (-6) - (-3) = 1$. The value of the expression is therefore $10^1 = 10$. The result $10^{-5}$ comes from calculating $4 - 6 - 3$, subtracting $3$ instead of $-3$. The result $10^{7}$ comes from calculating $4 + 6 - 3$, treating the exponent $-6$ as though it were $6$ and subtracting $3$ instead of $-3$. The result $10^{-2}$ is the numerator alone, without dividing by $10^{-3}$.
