After spending $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of its annual budget, an office still has $1\,500$ euros available. What is the annual budget?

- $2\,400$ euros
- $4\,000$ euros
- $937.50$ euros
- $562.50$ euros

## Explanation

After the expenditure, $1 - \dfrac{5}{8} = \dfrac{3}{8}$ of the budget remains, worth $1\,500$ euros. To **find the whole**, first find one eighth of the budget, $\dfrac{1\,500}{3} = 500$ euros, so the budget is $500 \cdot 8 = 4\,000$ euros. The result $2\,400$ euros assigns the $1\,500$ euros to the $\dfrac{5}{8}$ spent instead of the $\dfrac{3}{8}$ remaining. The result $937.50$ euros is the fraction $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of $1\,500$ euros, a direct calculation in place of the inverse one.
