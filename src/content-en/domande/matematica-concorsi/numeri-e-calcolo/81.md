How many positive even divisors does $540$ have?

- $12$
- $16$
- $24$
- $8$

## Explanation

Its **prime factorization** is $540=2^2\cdot3^3\cdot5$. For an even divisor, there are two choices for the exponent of $2$, four for that of $3$, and two for that of $5$, giving $2\cdot4\cdot2=16$ divisors.
