Which of the following numbers rounds to $4.3$ to the nearest tenth?

- $4.249$
- $4.35$
- $4.371$
- $4.25$

## Explanation

To round to the nearest tenth, look only at the hundredths digit. In $4.25$ that digit is $5$, so the tenths digit increases by $1$ and the rounded number is $4.3$. In $4.249$ the hundredths digit is $4$, so the number rounds to $4.2$ to the nearest tenth. The value $4.3$ for $4.249$ comes from rounding in two steps, first to $4.25$ and then to $4.3$, whereas **rounding** must be done in one step. The numbers $4.35$ and $4.371$ both round to $4.4$ to the nearest tenth and give $4.3$ only with truncation.
