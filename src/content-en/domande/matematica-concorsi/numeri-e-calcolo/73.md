What is the smallest natural number $n$ for which $2^n$ is a four-digit number?

- $9$
- $11$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

A natural number has four digits if it lies between $1\,000$ and $9\,999$. We have $2^9=512$, which has three digits, and $2^{10}=2\cdot512=1\,024$, which has four digits. Since the powers of $2$ increase as the exponent increases, every power with exponent less than $10$ is at most $512$. So the smallest exponent required is $n=10$. The value $11$ is the exponent of $2^{11}=2\,048$, which has four digits but is not the first power with this property.
