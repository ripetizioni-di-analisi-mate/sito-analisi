A positive fraction is in lowest terms, and the sum of its numerator and its denominator is $12$. How many fractions of this kind are less than $1$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

A positive fraction is less than $1$ if the numerator is less than the denominator, so the numerator is one of the numbers from $1$ to $5$. The possible fractions are therefore $\dfrac{1}{11}$, $\dfrac{2}{10}$, $\dfrac{3}{9}$, $\dfrac{4}{8}$ and $\dfrac{5}{7}$. In $\dfrac{2}{10}$, $\dfrac{3}{9}$ and $\dfrac{4}{8}$ the numerator and the denominator have a common divisor greater than $1$, so these fractions are not in lowest terms. Therefore the required fractions are only $\dfrac{1}{11}$ and $\dfrac{5}{7}$, so there are $2$ of them. The value $5$ counts all the fractions without checking whether they are reduced, and the value $6$ also includes $\dfrac{6}{6}$, which equals $1$.
