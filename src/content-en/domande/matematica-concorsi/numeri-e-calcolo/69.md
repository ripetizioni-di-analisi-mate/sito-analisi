A binder is $\dfrac47$ full. After $22$ more cards are added, it is $\dfrac56$ full. How many cards does it hold when full?

- $77$
- $132$
- $42$
- $84$

## Explanation

The **fraction added** is $\dfrac56-\dfrac47=\dfrac{11}{42}$. If $\dfrac{11}{42}$ corresponds to $22$ cards, each forty-second of the binder holds $2$ cards and the total is $84$.
