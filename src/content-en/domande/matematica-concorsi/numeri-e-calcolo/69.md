A positive fraction is in lowest terms. Its numerator and denominator add to 12. How many such fractions are less than 1?

- $3$
- $5$
- $6$
- $2$

## Explanation

The numerator ranges from 1 to 5. The pairs $(2,10),(3,9),(4,8)$ have common factors, whereas $(1,11)$ and $(5,7)$ are coprime.
