An archive contains $132$ files. If $\dfrac{5}{12}$ of them have already been checked, how many files have been checked?

- $11$
- $77$
- $60$
- $55$

## Explanation

To calculate a **fraction of a quantity**, divide by the denominator and multiply by the numerator. Here this gives $132\div12=11$ and then $11\cdot5=55$.
