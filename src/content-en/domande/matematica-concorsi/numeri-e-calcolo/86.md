Which fraction is the same distance from $\dfrac{7}{12}$ and $\dfrac{17}{20}$ on the number line?

- $\dfrac{43}{60}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{41}{60}$
- $\dfrac{47}{60}$

## Explanation

The **midpoint** is the average of the two endpoints. Writing them as $\dfrac{35}{60}$ and $\dfrac{51}{60}$ gives a mean numerator of $(35+51)\div2=43$, so the result is $\dfrac{43}{60}$.
