What is the value of the sum $0.\overline{3} + 0.1\overline{6}$?

- $\dfrac{49}{99}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{23}{50}$
- $\dfrac{23}{45}$

## Explanation

By the rule for the **fractional equivalent** of a repeating decimal, $0.\overline{3} = \dfrac{3}{9} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $0.1\overline{6} = \dfrac{16 - 1}{90} = \dfrac{15}{90} = \dfrac{1}{6}$. The sum is therefore $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{2}{6} + \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The result $\dfrac{23}{50}$ truncates the two numbers to $0.3$ and $0.16$, whose sum $0.46$ is only an approximation. The result $\dfrac{23}{45}$ writes $0.1\overline{6}$ as $\dfrac{16}{90}$ without subtracting the nonrepeating part $1$ in the numerator.
