What is the value of the sum $(-1)^1 + (-1)^2 + (-1)^3 + \cdots + (-1)^{25}$?

- $-1$
- $0$
- $1$
- $-25$

## Explanation

A power of $-1$ equals $1$ if the exponent is even and $-1$ if the exponent is odd. The first $24$ terms form $12$ pairs, $(-1)^1 + (-1)^2$, $(-1)^3 + (-1)^4$, …, $(-1)^{23} + (-1)^{24}$, each of which sums to $-1 + 1 = 0$. The last term $(-1)^{25} = -1$ remains, so the sum is $-1$. The result $0$ comes from pairing all the terms, although there are $25$ terms, an odd number.
