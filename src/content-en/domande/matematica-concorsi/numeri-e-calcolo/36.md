Which value comes last when the four numbers are arranged in increasing order?

- $3.2 \cdot 10^{-3}$
- $0.04$
- $5 \cdot 10^{-4}$
- $28 \cdot 10^{-4}$

## Explanation

In **scientific notation**, the four numbers are $4 \cdot 10^{-2}$, $3.2 \cdot 10^{-3}$, $5 \cdot 10^{-4}$, and $2.8 \cdot 10^{-3}$. For numbers written in the form $a \cdot 10^n$ with $1 \leq a < 10$, the one with the greater exponent $n$ is greater, so the greatest number is $0.04 = 4 \cdot 10^{-2}$. The other numbers in decimal form are $0.0032$, $0.0005$, and $0.0028$. The number $28 \cdot 10^{-4}$ may appeal to someone who compares the factors $28$, $5$, $4$, and $3.2$ without first writing the numbers in scientific notation.
