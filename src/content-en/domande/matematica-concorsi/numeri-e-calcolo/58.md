Between which two consecutive integers does $\sqrt{150}$ lie?

- $13$ and $14$
- $11$ and $12$
- $14$ and $15$
- $12$ and $13$

## Explanation

The **consecutive perfect squares** $12^2=144$ and $13^2=169$ lie on either side of $150$. Since the square root function is increasing, $\sqrt{150}$ lies between $12$ and $13$.
