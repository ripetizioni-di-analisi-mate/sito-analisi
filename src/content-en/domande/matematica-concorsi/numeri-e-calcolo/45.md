Which of the following fractions is closest to $0.3$?

- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$

## Explanation

Convert the familiar fractions mentally to obtain $\dfrac{1}{3} = 0.\overline{3}$, $\dfrac{1}{4} = 0.25$, and $\dfrac{3}{8} = 0.375$. Dividing $2$ by $7$ gives $\dfrac{2}{7} = 0.2857\ldots$. The distances from $0.3$ are therefore about $0.014$ for $\dfrac{2}{7}$, $0.033$ for $\dfrac{1}{3}$, $0.05$ for $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and $0.075$ for $\dfrac{3}{8}$. The closest fraction is therefore $\dfrac{2}{7}$. The fraction $\dfrac{1}{3}$ may appeal to someone who equates it with $0.3$, whereas $\dfrac{1}{3} = 0.333\ldots$ exceeds $0.3$ by $\dfrac{1}{30}$.
