What is the value of the expression $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} + 3^{-1} \cdot 3^{2}$?

- $\dfrac{25}{8}$
- $11$
- $\dfrac{23}{8}$
- $\dfrac{73}{9}$

## Explanation

To evaluate a power with a **negative exponent**, use the reciprocal of the base, so $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = 2^3 = 8$. The product $3^{-1} \cdot 3^2$ has exponent $-1 + 2 = 1$ and equals $3$. The expression therefore equals $8 + 3 = 11$. The result $\dfrac{25}{8}$ comes from writing $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$ without taking the reciprocal. The result $\dfrac{23}{8}$ treats the negative exponent as a minus sign, giving $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = -\dfrac{1}{8}$.
