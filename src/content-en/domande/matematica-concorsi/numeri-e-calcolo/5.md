What is the value of the expression $5 - 2 \cdot \{3 - [4 - (6 - 9)]\}$?

- $1$
- $-12$
- $-3$
- $13$

## Explanation

Evaluate the expression in parentheses first, then the one in square brackets, and finally the one in braces:
$$
\begin{aligned}
5 - 2 \cdot \{3 - [4 - (6 - 9)]\} &= 5 - 2 \cdot \{3 - [4 + 3]\} \\[6pt]
&= 5 - 2 \cdot (3 - 7) \\[6pt]
&= 5 - 2 \cdot (-4) = 5 + 8 = 13 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The product $-2 \cdot (-4)$ is positive by the **rule of signs**. The result $1$ comes from writing $4 - (-3) = 1$ instead of $4 + 3 = 7$. The result $-12$ comes from performing the subtraction $5 - 2$ before the multiplication.
