What is the value of the expression $\dfrac{(4 \cdot 10^{-3}) \cdot (6 \cdot 10^{8})}{8 \cdot 10^{2}}$?

- $3 \cdot 10^{3}$
- $3 \cdot 10^{7}$
- $3 \cdot 10^{9}$
- $3 \cdot 10^{5}$

## Explanation

Work separately with the numerical factors and the powers of $10$. The numerical factors give $\dfrac{4 \cdot 6}{8} = 3$, and by the **properties of powers**, the powers of $10$ give $10^{-3 + 8 - 2} = 10^{3}$. The expression therefore equals $3 \cdot 10^3$. The result $3 \cdot 10^{7}$ adds the exponent in the denominator instead of subtracting it, and $3 \cdot 10^{5}$ omits the division by $10^2$.
