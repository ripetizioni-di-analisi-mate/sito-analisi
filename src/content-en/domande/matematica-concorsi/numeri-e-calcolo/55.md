Which fraction lies strictly between $\dfrac{3}{8}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{16}$
- $\dfrac{7}{16}$

## Explanation

Rewrite both fractions with the **common denominator** $16$. Since $\dfrac38=\dfrac6{16}$ and $\dfrac12=\dfrac8{16}$, the required numerator is $7$.
