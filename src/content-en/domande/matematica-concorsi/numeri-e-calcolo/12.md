How many divisors does $200$ have, including $1$ and $200$?

- $6$
- $12$
- $10$
- $5$

## Explanation

The prime factorization is $200 = 2^3 \cdot 5^2$. To form a divisor, choose the exponent of $2$ from $0$, $1$, $2$, $3$ and the exponent of $5$ from $0$, $1$, $2$. By the rule for the **number of divisors**, there are therefore $(3 + 1) \cdot (2 + 1) = 12$ divisors. The result $6$ is the product $3 \cdot 2$ of the exponents without adding $1$ to each. The result $10$ excludes the divisors $1$ and $200$, which the question asks us to count.
