Which of the four powers has the greatest value?

- $5^8$
- $7^6$
- $2^{18}$
- $3^{12}$

## Explanation

The four **powers** can be rewritten as $(3^6)^2$, $(2^9)^2$, $(5^4)^2$, and $(7^3)^2$. The positive bases are $729$, $512$, $625$, and $343$, so the greatest square is $3^{12}$.
