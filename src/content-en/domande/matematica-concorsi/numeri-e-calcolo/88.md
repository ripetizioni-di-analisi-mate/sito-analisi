What is the units digit of $7^{40}+7^{41}$?

- $0$
- $4$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

Factoring out $7^{40}$ gives $7^{40}+7^{41}=7^{40}\cdot(1+7)=8\cdot7^{40}$. Since $7^4=2\,401$ ends in $1$, also $7^{40}=\left(7^4\right)^{10}$ ends in $1$. The **last digit** of a product depends only on the last digits of the factors, so $8\cdot7^{40}$ ends in $8\cdot1=8$. The value $6$ comes from shifting by one place the cycle of last digits $7$, $9$, $3$, $1$, that is from giving $7^{40}$ the last digit $7$ and $7^{41}$ the last digit $9$.
