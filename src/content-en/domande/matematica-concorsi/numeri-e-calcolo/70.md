Which fraction in lowest terms is equivalent to $1.2\overline7$?

- $\dfrac{23}{18}$
- $\dfrac{127}{99}$
- $\dfrac{127}{100}$
- $\dfrac{14}{11}$

## Explanation

Let $x=1.2\overline7$. The numbers $100x$ and $10x$ have the same **repeating part** after the decimal point. Subtracting them gives $90x=127-12=115$, so $x=\dfrac{23}{18}$.
