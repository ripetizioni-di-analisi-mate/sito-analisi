What is the smallest positive integer that leaves a remainder of $2$ when divided by $7$, leaves a remainder of $3$ when divided by $5$, and is a multiple of $4$?

- $268$
- $23$
- $58$
- $128$

## Explanation

The **remainders** for division by $7$ and $5$ identify the numbers $23,58,93,128,\ldots$, spaced $35$ apart. The first three are not multiples of $4$, whereas $128$ is divisible by $4$.
