Which of the following numbers gives $3.40$ when rounded to the nearest hundredth, with $5$ rounded up?

- $3.415$
- $3.395$
- $3.394$
- $3.405$

## Explanation

The values that round to $3.40$ range from $3.395$ inclusive to $3.405$ exclusive. The upper endpoint rounds to $3.41$, so only the proposed **lower endpoint** works.
