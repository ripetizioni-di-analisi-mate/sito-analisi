The two factors of the product $7.48\cdot3.51$ are rounded to the nearest integer, and then the rounded values are multiplied. Which estimate of the product is obtained?

- $21$
- $28$
- $24$
- $32$

## Explanation

To round to the nearest integer, only the tenths digit is considered. In $7.48$ the tenths digit is $4$, so the rounded number is $7$. In $3.51$ the tenths digit is $5$, so the rounded number is $4$. The estimate of the product is therefore $7\cdot4=28$, while the exact product is $26.2548$. The value $21$ comes from truncating both factors, and the value $32$ from rounding $7.48$ up as well.
