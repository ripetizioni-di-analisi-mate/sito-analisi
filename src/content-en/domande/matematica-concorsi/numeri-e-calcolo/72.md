A program first rounds $1.249$ to hundredths and then to tenths. What is the absolute difference from rounding directly to tenths? Round ties upwards.

- $0$
- $0.1$
- $0.01$
- $0.2$

## Explanation

The first step gives $1.25$, which rounds to $1.3$. Direct rounding gives $1.2$, since the hundredths digit is 4. The difference is one tenth.
