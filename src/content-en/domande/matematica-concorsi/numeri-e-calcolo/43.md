Which of the following numbers equals the product $47 \cdot 63 \cdot 29$?

- $85\,869$
- $85\,867$
- $85\,863$
- $85\,861$

## Explanation

The **last digit** of a product depends only on the last digits of its factors, which here are $7$, $3$, and $9$. Since $7 \cdot 3 = 21$ ends in $1$ and $1 \cdot 9 = 9$, the product ends in $9$. The only answer ending in $9$ is $85\,869$. The full calculation confirms this because $47 \cdot 63 = 2\,961$ and $2\,961 \cdot 29 = 85\,869$.
