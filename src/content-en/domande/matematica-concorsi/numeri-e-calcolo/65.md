What is the smallest positive common multiple of $18$, $24$, and $30$?

- $180$
- $360$
- $720$
- $120$

## Explanation

To find the **least common multiple**, take each prime factor with its largest exponent. The three numbers contribute $2^3$, $3^2$, and $5$, whose product is $360$.
