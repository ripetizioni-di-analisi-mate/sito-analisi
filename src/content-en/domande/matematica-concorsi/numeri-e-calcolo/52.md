What fraction results when $\dfrac{56}{98}$ is reduced to lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$

## Explanation

The **greatest common divisor** of $56$ and $98$ is $14$. Dividing the numerator and denominator by $14$ gives $\dfrac{4}{7}$.
