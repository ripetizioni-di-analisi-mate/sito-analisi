What remains after cancelling the intermediate factors in $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac45\cdots\dfrac9{10}$?

- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac45$

## Explanation

Each integer from 3 to 9 occurs once in the numerator and once in the denominator. The remaining fraction is two tenths.
