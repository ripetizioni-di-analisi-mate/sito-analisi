Three beacons flash at regular intervals. The first flashes every $8$ seconds, the second every $12$ seconds, and the third every $18$ seconds. At 21\:00 all three flash at the same instant. How many times do all three flash together again in the next $10$ minutes, excluding the flash at 21\:00?

- $9$
- $25$
- $16$
- $8$

## Explanation

The three beacons flash together at intervals of $\operatorname{mcm}(8, 12, 18)$ seconds. The factorizations are $8 = 2^3$, $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$, and $18 = 2 \cdot 3^2$, so the **least common multiple** is $2^3 \cdot 3^2 = 72$ seconds. Converting $10$ minutes gives $600$ seconds, and since $8 \cdot 72 = 576$ and $9 \cdot 72 = 648$, the multiples of $72$ between $1$ and $600$ are $8$. The beacons therefore flash together $8$ times. The result $9$ also counts the flash at 21\:00, which the question excludes, and $25$ uses only $\operatorname{mcm}(8, 12) = 24$.
