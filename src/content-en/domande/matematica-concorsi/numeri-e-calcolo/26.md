Of the applicants for a competitive examination, $\dfrac{3}{5}$ are women and the others are men. One quarter of the women and half of the men are over $40$ years old. What fraction of all applicants are over $40$?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Women over $40$ account for one quarter of the $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of applicants who are women, or $\dfrac{1}{4} \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{3}{20}$ of all applicants. Men make up $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of all applicants, so men over $40$ account for $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{4}{20}$ of all applicants. The required fraction is therefore $\dfrac{3}{20} + \dfrac{4}{20} = \dfrac{7}{20}$. The result $\dfrac{3}{4}$ adds $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$, fractions of two different groups. The result $\dfrac{3}{8}$ is the average of $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and would treat the groups as equal in size.
