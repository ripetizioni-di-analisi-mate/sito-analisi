What do you get by rounding $16.995$ to the nearest hundredth, rounding up when the next digit is $5$?

- $16.90$
- $16.99$
- $17.00$
- $17.01$

## Explanation

The **thousandths** digit is $5$, so increase the hundredths digit by one. Carrying through both $9$ digits gives $17.00$.
