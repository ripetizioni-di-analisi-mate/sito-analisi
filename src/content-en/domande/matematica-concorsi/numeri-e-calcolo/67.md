What is $\left(\dfrac58-\dfrac16\right)\div\dfrac{11}{12}$ equal to?

- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{11}{24}$
- $\dfrac{121}{288}$

## Explanation

The difference in parentheses equals $\dfrac{15-4}{24}=\dfrac{11}{24}$. To **divide fractions**, multiply by $\dfrac{12}{11}$, obtaining $\dfrac12$.
