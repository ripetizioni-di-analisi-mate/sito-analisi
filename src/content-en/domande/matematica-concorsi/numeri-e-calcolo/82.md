How many consecutive zeros appear at the end of the product $1\cdot2\cdot3\cdots35$?

- $7$
- $8$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

Each **trailing zero** requires a pair of factors $2$ and $5$, and there are more factors of $2$. There are $7$ multiples of $5$, and $25$ contributes one additional factor of $5$, giving a total of $8$ zeros.
