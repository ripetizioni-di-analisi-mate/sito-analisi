Two natural numbers have a greatest common divisor of $6$ and a least common multiple of $180$. One of the numbers is $36$. What is the other?

- $5$
- $216$
- $30$
- $1\,080$

## Explanation

For two numbers $a$ and $b$, the relationship $\operatorname{MCD}(a, b) \cdot \operatorname{mcm}(a, b) = a \cdot b$ holds. Thus $36 \cdot b = 6 \cdot 180 = 1\,080$, and the other number is $b = \dfrac{1\,080}{36} = 30$. Indeed, the factorizations $36 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2$ and $30 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$ give $\operatorname{MCD} = 2 \cdot 3 = 6$ and $\operatorname{mcm} = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 = 180$. The result $5$ is the quotient $\dfrac{180}{36}$, and the GCD of $36$ and $5$ is $1$.
