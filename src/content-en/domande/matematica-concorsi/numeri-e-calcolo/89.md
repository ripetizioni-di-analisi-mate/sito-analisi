What is the smallest positive integer by which $1\,512$ must be multiplied to obtain a perfect square?

- $42$
- $21$
- $14$
- $84$

## Explanation

The **prime factorization** gives $1\,512=2^3\cdot3^3\cdot7$. To make all the exponents even, add one factor of $2$, one of $3$, and one of $7$. Their product is $42$.
