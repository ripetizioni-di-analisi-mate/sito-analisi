Paolo has read $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of a book. If he read another $36$ pages, he would reach exactly the halfway point. How many pages does the book have?

- $180$
- $90$
- $72$
- $360$

## Explanation

The $36$ pages represent the difference between half the book and the $\dfrac{2}{5}$ already read, or $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{5}{10} - \dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{1}{10}$ of the book. Since one tenth of the book is $36$ pages, the book has $36 \cdot 10 = 360$ pages. The result $90$ assigns the $36$ pages to $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the book, and $180$ is half the book.
