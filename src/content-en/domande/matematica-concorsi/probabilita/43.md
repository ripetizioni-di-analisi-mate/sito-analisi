A fair coin is flipped $500$ times and lands heads $270$ times. Which statement is true?

- The relative frequency of heads is $0.54$ and the probability of heads is $0.54$
- The relative frequency of heads is $0.54$ and the probability of heads is $0.5$
- The relative frequency of heads is $0.27$ and the probability of heads is $0.5$
- Tails has a probability greater than $0.5$ on the next flip

## Explanation

The relative frequency of heads is $\dfrac{270}{500} = \dfrac{54}{100} = 0.54$. Since the coin is fair, the probability of heads is $\dfrac{1}{2} = 0.5$. The relative frequency is close to the probability, as the **empirical law of chance** predicts. The flips are independent, so the probability of tails on the next flip is still $0.5$. The value $0.27$ divides $270$ by $1000$ instead of dividing by the number of flips.
