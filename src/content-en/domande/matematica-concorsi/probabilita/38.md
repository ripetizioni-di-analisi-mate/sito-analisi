Six people, including Luca and Giulia, take seats at random in a row of $6$ chairs, with all arrangements equally likely. What is the probability that Luca and Giulia occupy the two end seats?

- $\dfrac{1}{30}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{36}$

## Explanation

The $6$ people can be seated in $P_6 = 6! = 720$ ways. Luca and Giulia can occupy the end seats in $2$ ways because either one can sit on the left. The other $4$ people can take the middle seats in $4! = 24$ ways. There are $2 \cdot 24 = 48$ favorable outcomes, giving $P = \dfrac{48}{720} = \dfrac{1}{15}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{30}$ counts only one order for the two people at the ends. The value $\dfrac{1}{36} = \dfrac{1}{6} \cdot \dfrac{1}{6}$ treats their seat choices as independent, although they cannot both occupy the same seat.
