An urn holds $4$ white balls and $1$ black ball that feel identical. One ball is drawn, returned to the urn, and then a second ball is drawn. What is the probability that the first is white and the second is black?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{8}{25}$
- $\dfrac{4}{25}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Replacing the ball leaves the contents of the urn unchanged, so the draws are independent. The first ball is white with probability $\dfrac{4}{5}$ and the second is black with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$. By the **multiplication rule**, $P = \dfrac{4}{5} \cdot \dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{4}{25}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{4}{5} \cdot \dfrac{1}{4}$ gives the result without replacement. The value $\dfrac{8}{25}$ also includes the reverse order, black followed by white, which the question excludes.
