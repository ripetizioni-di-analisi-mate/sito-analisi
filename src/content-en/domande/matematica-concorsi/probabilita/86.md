Five people, including Ada and Bruno, line up at random, with all orders equally likely. What is the probability that exactly one person stands between Ada and Bruno?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Their positions must differ by $2$, so they can be $(1,3)$, $(2,4)$, or $(3,5)$. All $\dbinom{5}{2}=10$ pairs of positions are equally likely, giving a probability of $\dfrac{3}{10}$.
