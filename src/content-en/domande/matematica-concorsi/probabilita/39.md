Three fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that all three numbers are different?

- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{54}$
- $\dfrac{25}{36}$
- $\dfrac{5}{9}$

## Explanation

There are $6^3 = 216$ possible ordered triples. For all three numbers to differ, there are $6$ choices for the first die, $5$ for the second, and $4$ for the third, so there are $D_{6,3} = 6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4 = 120$ favorable ordered selections. Thus $P = \dfrac{120}{216} = \dfrac{5}{9}$. The value $\dfrac{5}{54} = \dfrac{20}{216}$ ignores order in the favorable outcomes but counts it in the possible outcomes. The value $\dfrac{25}{36} = \dfrac{5}{6} \cdot \dfrac{5}{6}$ requires the third number to differ from the second but allows it to match the first.
