The probability that a request receives a response by the deadline is $72\%$. What is the probability that no response arrives by then?

- $38\%$
- $28\%$
- $72\%$
- $18\%$

## Explanation

The events are complementary, so their probabilities add to $100\%$. The required probability is $100\%-72\%=28\%$.
