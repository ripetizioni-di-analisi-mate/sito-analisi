A fair six-sided die is rolled twice. What is the probability that the second roll is higher than the first?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

There are $36$ equally likely ordered pairs, and in $6$ of them the numbers match. Of the remaining $30$ pairs, half have a higher second number and half a lower one, since reversing the order pairs each outcome in one group with an outcome in the other. There are $15$ favorable outcomes and $P = \dfrac{15}{36} = \dfrac{5}{12}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ ignores ties, while $\dfrac{7}{12}$ counts them as favorable. The value $\dfrac{1}{3}$ treats “higher,” “lower,” and “equal” as equally likely.
