Four fair coins are flipped independently. What is the probability of getting exactly one tail?

- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Choose which of the four coins lands tails. The other three must land heads. There are $4$ favorable outcomes among $2^4=16$ equally likely outcomes.
