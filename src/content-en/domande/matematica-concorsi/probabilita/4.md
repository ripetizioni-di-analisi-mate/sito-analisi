Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the product of their results is $12$?

- $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{21}$

## Explanation

The $6 \cdot 6 = 36$ ordered pairs are equally likely. The product is $12$ for the pairs $(2, 6)$, $(6, 2)$, $(3, 4)$, and $(4, 3)$ because $12 = 2 \cdot 6 = 3 \cdot 4$. The factorization $12 = 1 \cdot 12$ would require a face showing $12$. By the **classical definition of probability**, $P = \dfrac{4}{36} = \dfrac{1}{9}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{18}$ ignores order when counting favorable pairs, giving $2$ out of $36$. The value $\dfrac{2}{21}$ uses the $21$ unordered pairs as the possible outcomes, although they are not equally likely.
