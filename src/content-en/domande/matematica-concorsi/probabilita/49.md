A container holds $24$ slips that feel identical, numbered from $1$ to $24$. One slip is drawn at random. What is the probability that it shows a perfect square?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{24}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The perfect squares in the interval are $1$, $4$, $9$, and $16$. The slips are equally likely, so the probability is $\dfrac{4}{24}=\dfrac{1}{6}$.
