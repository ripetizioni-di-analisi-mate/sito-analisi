A box contains $3$ red tokens and $2$ green tokens that feel identical. Three tokens are drawn without replacement. Given that exactly two of the three are red, what is the probability that the green token was drawn last?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Given the counts of each color, the green token is equally likely to occupy any of the three positions. Only one is the last position, so the probability is $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
