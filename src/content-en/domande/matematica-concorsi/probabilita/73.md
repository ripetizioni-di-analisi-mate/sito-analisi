Two independent fair six-sided dice are rolled. Given that their sum is $8$, what is the probability that at least one die shows $6$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The ordered pairs with sum $8$ are $(2,6)$, $(3,5)$, $(4,4)$, $(5,3)$, and $(6,2)$. Two of these five contain a $6$, so the probability is $\dfrac{2}{5}$.
