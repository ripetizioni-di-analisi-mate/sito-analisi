An automated check detects an error with probability $70\%$. A second, independent check detects it with probability $40\%$. What is the probability that neither detects it?

- $42\%$
- $12\%$
- $18\%$
- $28\%$

## Explanation

The first check misses the error with probability $0.30$, and the second misses it with probability $0.60$. By independence, multiply the two values to get $0.18$.
