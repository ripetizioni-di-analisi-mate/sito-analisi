A well-shuffled Italian deck has $40$ cards, ten per suit, with one king in each suit. One card is drawn. Given that it is from the suit of coins or is a king, what is the probability that it is the king of coins?

- $\dfrac{1}{13}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

The condition covers the ten cards in the suit of coins and the three kings from the other suits, for a total of thirteen cards. Only one is the king of coins, giving $\dfrac{1}{13}$.
