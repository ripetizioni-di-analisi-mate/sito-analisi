In the morning session of a selection exam, $18$ out of $30$ candidates qualified. In the afternoon session, $12$ out of $20$ qualified. One of the qualified candidates from both sessions combined is chosen at random. What is the probability that this person attended the morning session?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$

## Explanation

The selection is made from the $18+12=30$ candidates who qualified. The $18$ morning candidates who qualified are the favorable outcomes, so the probability is $\dfrac{18}{30}=\dfrac{3}{5}$.
