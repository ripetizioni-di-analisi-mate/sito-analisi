In a sample of $90$ service visits, $42$ were online. In a second sample, $24$ out of $60$ were online. What is the relative frequency of online visits in the two samples combined?

- $\dfrac{13}{30}$
- $\dfrac{7}{15}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{11}{25}$

## Explanation

The combined samples contain $42+24=66$ online visits out of $90+60=150$ visits in total. The relative frequency is $\dfrac{66}{150}=\dfrac{11}{25}$.
