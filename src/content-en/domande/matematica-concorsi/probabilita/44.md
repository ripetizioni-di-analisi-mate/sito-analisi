Two events $A$ and $B$ have probabilities $P(A) = 0.6$ and $P(B) = 0.5$. Which value could be the probability $P(A \cap B)$ that both events occur?

- $0.55$
- $1.1$
- $0.3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The probability of both events occurring cannot exceed either $P(A)$ or $P(B)$. Thus $P(A \cap B) \leq 0.5$, which rules out $0.55$ and $1.1$. By the **general addition rule**, $P(A \cup B) = 0.6 + 0.5 - P(A \cap B) = 1.1 - P(A \cap B)$. A probability cannot exceed $1$, so $P(A \cap B) \geq 0.1$, ruling out $0$ as well. The value $0.3$ is possible, for example when $A$ and $B$ are independent, since $0.6 \cdot 0.5 = 0.3$.
