An anagram of the Italian word CANE is chosen at random. All arrangements of its $4$ letters in a row are equally likely. What is the probability that vowels and consonants alternate?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

The $4$ letters are distinct, so there are $P_4 = 4! = 24$ anagrams. Vowels and consonants alternate in two patterns. One is consonant, vowel, consonant, vowel. The other is vowel, consonant, vowel, consonant. In each pattern, the $2$ consonants can be arranged in $2! = 2$ ways and the $2$ vowels in another $2$ ways. This gives $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8$ favorable anagrams and a probability of $\dfrac{8}{24} = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{6}$ uses just one pattern, for example only the anagrams that begin with a consonant.
