In a class of $25$ students, $15$ have already passed the English exam. The teacher selects a student at random, with each student equally likely to be chosen. What is the probability that the selected student has yet to pass the exam?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

There are $25 - 15 = 10$ students who have not passed the exam. They are the favorable outcomes. All $25$ students are possible outcomes, so $P = \dfrac{10}{25} = \dfrac{2}{5}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{5}$ gives the probability of the complement, that the student has passed. The value $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is the ratio $10 : 15$ between the two groups. Its denominator is the number who passed rather than the total number of students.
