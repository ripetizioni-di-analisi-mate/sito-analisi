A fair six-sided die is rolled twice. Only the sum of the two results is recorded. How many distinct values can be recorded?

- $11$
- $36$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

The smallest sum is $2$ and the largest is $12$. Every integer between these endpoints is possible, so there are $12-2+1=11$ values.
