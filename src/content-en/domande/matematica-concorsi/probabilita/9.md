One card is drawn from a well-shuffled French-suited deck of $52$ cards. What is the probability that its rank is $2$, $4$, $6$, $8$, or $10$?

- $\dfrac{5}{13}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{13}$
- $\dfrac{5}{52}$

## Explanation

There are $5$ cards of the specified ranks in each suit, so the $4$ suits give $5 \cdot 4 = 20$ such cards in all. The $52$ cards are equally likely, so $P = \dfrac{20}{52} = \dfrac{5}{13}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ assumes that half the cards in a suit have an even number, whereas the specified ranks account for $5$ out of $13$. The value $\dfrac{4}{13}$ leaves out the $10$, and the value $\dfrac{5}{52}$ counts only one suit.
