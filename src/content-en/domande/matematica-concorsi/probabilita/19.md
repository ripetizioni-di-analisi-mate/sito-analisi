An apartment building has $40$ households. Of these, $22$ own a car, $15$ own a bicycle, and $8$ own both. One household is chosen at random. What is the probability that it owns neither a car nor a bicycle?

- $\dfrac{11}{40}$
- $\dfrac{3}{40}$
- $\dfrac{29}{40}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The $8$ households with both a car and a bicycle are counted among the $22$ car owners and the $15$ bicycle owners. By the **general addition rule**, $22 + 15 - 8 = 29$ households own at least one of the two. The event asked for is the complement of “owns at least one,” so there are $40 - 29 = 11$ favorable households and $P = \dfrac{11}{40}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{40}$ starts with $40$ and subtracts $22 + 15 = 37$ without correcting for households counted twice. The value $\dfrac{29}{40}$ gives the probability of the complement.
