One card is drawn from a well-shuffled French-suited deck of $52$ cards. Which of the following events is impossible?

- The card is the jack of spades
- The card is a red queen
- The card is the $10$ of clubs
- The card is a knight

## Explanation

Each suit in a French-suited deck has an ace, the numbers $2$ through $10$, a jack, a queen, and a king. There are no knights. The event “the card is a knight” has no favorable outcomes, so it is the **impossible event** and has probability $0$. The knight is a face card in the Italian $40$-card deck, which in turn has no $10$. The other events are possible. The jack of spades and the $10$ of clubs are in the French-suited deck, and there are two red queens, hearts and diamonds.
