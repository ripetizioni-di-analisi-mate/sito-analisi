One card is drawn from a well-shuffled Italian deck of $40$ cards. What is the probability that it is an ace or a face card (a jack, knight, or king)?

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{100}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

There are $4$ aces and $3 \cdot 4 = 12$ face cards. An ace is not a face card, so the two events are **mutually exclusive**. The **addition rule** gives
$$
P = \frac{4}{40} + \frac{12}{40} = \frac{16}{40} = \frac{2}{5} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{3}{10}$ gives the probability of drawing a face card. The value $\dfrac{3}{100}$ multiplies the probabilities $\dfrac{1}{10}$ and $\dfrac{3}{10}$, which would give the probability of two independent events occurring together.
