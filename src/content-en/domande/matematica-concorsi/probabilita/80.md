A call log shows that $12\%$ of callers hung up before their calls were answered. Using this frequency to estimate the probability, how many of $350$ calls would you expect to be abandoned?

- $47$
- $72$
- $42$
- $35$

## Explanation

The expected count is the relative frequency multiplied by the number of calls, giving $\dfrac{12}{100}\cdot350=42$.
