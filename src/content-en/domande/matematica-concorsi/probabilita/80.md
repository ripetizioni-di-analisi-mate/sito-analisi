In a series of $50$ trials, $2$ failures occurred. What is the smallest number of failure-free trials that must be added for the relative frequency of failures to drop to $2\%$?

- $25$
- $48$
- $50$
- $100$

## Explanation

The **relative frequency** of failures is the number of failures divided by the number of trials, and at first it is $\dfrac{2}{50}=4\%$. The added trials have no failures, so the number of failures stays $2$. For the frequency $\dfrac{2}{n}$ to equal $2\%$, that is $\dfrac{2}{100}$, the total number of trials must be $n=100$. Therefore $100-50=50$ trials must be added. The value $100$ is the total number of trials, and the value $25$ comes from halving the number of trials instead of doubling it.
