There are 2 failures in 50 trials. What is the smallest number of consecutive failure-free trials that must be added for the observed rate to fall to 2%?

- $25$
- $48$
- $50$
- $100$

## Explanation

The numerator stays at 2. Solving $\dfrac{2}{n}=0.02$ gives 100 trials altogether, or 50 more trials.
