A fair six-sided die is rolled. Which of the following events is certain?

- The number is less than $7$
- The number is less than $6$
- The number is even or prime
- The number is even or a multiple of $3$

## Explanation

An event is certain if it occurs for every outcome in the sample space $\Omega = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$. Every face of the die shows a number less than $7$, so this event equals $\Omega$ and is the **certain event**. The favorable outcomes for “even or prime” are $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, and $6$. This event is not certain because $1$ is neither even nor prime. Similarly, “even or a multiple of $3$” excludes $1$ and $5$, and “less than $6$” excludes $6$.
