One card is drawn from a well-shuffled French-suited deck of $52$ cards. What is the probability that it is a red face card, given that jacks, queens, and kings are the face cards and hearts and diamonds are the red suits?

- $\dfrac{1}{13}$
- $\dfrac{1}{26}$
- $\dfrac{3}{13}$
- $\dfrac{3}{26}$

## Explanation

Each red suit contains three face cards, giving $2\cdot3=6$ favorable cards. Dividing by the total gives $\dfrac{6}{52}=\dfrac{3}{26}$.
