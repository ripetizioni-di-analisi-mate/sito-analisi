Of $160$ case files reviewed, $24$ required additional information. What is the relative frequency of files requiring additional information, expressed as a percentage?

- $20\%$
- $12\%$
- $24\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

The relative frequency is $\dfrac{24}{160}=\dfrac{3}{20}$. Multiply the fraction by $100$ and append a percent sign to write the frequency as $15\%$.
