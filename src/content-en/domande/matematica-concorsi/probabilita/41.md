A bag contains $20$ tokens that feel identical, $7$ of them green. One token is drawn at random. What is the probability that it is green, expressed as a percentage?

- $35\%$
- $7\%$
- $3.5\%$
- $65\%$

## Explanation

The probability is $\dfrac{7}{20}$. Multiplying the numerator and denominator by $5$ gives $\dfrac{35}{100} = 0.35$. Multiply the decimal by $100$ and append a percent sign to express the probability as $35\%$. The value $7\%$ treats the green tokens as if there were $7$ out of $100$. The value $65\%$ gives the probability of drawing a token that is not green.
