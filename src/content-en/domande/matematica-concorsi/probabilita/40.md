A tournament has $8$ teams, including Polisportiva and Atletico. For the quarterfinals, they are drawn into $4$ pairs, with every pairing equally likely. What is the probability that Polisportiva and Atletico face each other in the quarterfinals?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{28}$

## Explanation

Polisportiva’s opponent is one of the other $7$ teams. By symmetry each is equally likely to be that opponent, so the probability that it is Atletico is $\dfrac{1}{7}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{28} = \dfrac{1}{C_{8,2}}$ gives the probability that a single pair selected from all $28$ possible pairs consists of those two teams. The draw actually forms $4$ pairs, and indeed $\dfrac{4}{28} = \dfrac{1}{7}$.
