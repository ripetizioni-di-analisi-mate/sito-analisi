Two fair six-sided dice are rolled and their results are known to match. What is the probability that their sum is greater than $7$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

Given that the numbers match, the possible outcomes are the $6$ pairs $(1, 1)$, $(2, 2)$, …, $(6, 6)$. Their sums are $2$, $4$, $6$, $8$, $10$, and $12$. The sums greater than $7$ are $8$, $10$, and $12$, so there are $3$ favorable outcomes and the **conditional probability** is $\dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{12} = \dfrac{3}{36}$ divides by all $36$ pairs, giving the probability that the numbers match and their sum exceeds $7$. The value $\dfrac{5}{12}$ gives the probability that the sum exceeds $7$ with no information about the two numbers.
