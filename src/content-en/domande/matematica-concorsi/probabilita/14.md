A fair coin is flipped $4$ times. What is the probability that heads and tails alternate, so that every flip after the first differs from the previous flip?

- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

There are $2^4 = 16$ equally likely sequences of four flips. Heads and tails alternate only in $TCTC$ and $CTCT$ (T for heads, C for tails). Once the first flip is fixed, this condition determines all the others. Thus $P = \dfrac{2}{16} = \dfrac{1}{8}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{16}$ counts only one of the two sequences, and $\dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{6}{16}$ gives the probability of two heads and two tails in any order.
