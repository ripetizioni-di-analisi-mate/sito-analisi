A selection exam had $80$ candidates divided into two sessions. The table shows the results.

| | passed | did not pass | total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| morning | $35$ | $15$ | $50$ |
| afternoon | $15$ | $15$ | $30$ |
| Total | $50$ | $30$ | $80$ |

One of the candidates who passed is chosen at random. What is the probability that this candidate took the exam in the afternoon?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

The candidate is chosen from the $50$ who passed, so these are the possible outcomes. Of them, $15$ took the exam in the afternoon. The **conditional probability** is therefore $P(\text{afternoon} \mid \text{passed}) = \dfrac{15}{50} = \dfrac{3}{10}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{15}{30}$ gives the probability that an afternoon candidate passed, reversing the roles of the two events. The value $\dfrac{3}{16} = \dfrac{15}{80}$ divides by all candidates. The value $\dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{30}{80}$ gives the probability that a candidate chosen from the full group took the exam in the afternoon.
