Two mutually exclusive events $A$ and $B$ have probabilities $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$. What is the probability that neither occurs?

- $\dfrac{11}{20}$
- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Because the events are mutually exclusive, their union has probability $\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{9}{20}$. By the **complement rule**, the probability that neither occurs is $\dfrac{11}{20}$.
