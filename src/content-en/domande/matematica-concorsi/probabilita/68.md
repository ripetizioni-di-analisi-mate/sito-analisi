Two events each have probability $\dfrac34$. What is the smallest possible probability that both occur?

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac9{16}$

## Explanation

The union has probability at most 1, so the intersection has probability at least $\dfrac34+\dfrac34-1=\dfrac12$. It attains that bound when the union covers every outcome.
