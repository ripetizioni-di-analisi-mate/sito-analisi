A six-sided die is loaded so that each of the faces from $1$ to $5$ comes up with probability $0.15$. What is the probability that face $6$ comes up?

- $0.25$
- $0.15$
- $0.75$
- $0.85$

## Explanation

The outcomes of the faces from $1$ to $5$ are mutually exclusive events, so by the **addition rule for mutually exclusive events** the probability that one of these faces comes up is $5\cdot0.15=0.75$. Face $6$ coming up is the complementary event, so by the **complement rule** its probability is $1-0.75=0.25$. The value $0.15$ gives the $6$ the same probability as the other faces, the value $0.75$ is the probability that the $6$ does not come up, and the value $0.85$ comes from subtracting from $1$ the probability of a single face.
