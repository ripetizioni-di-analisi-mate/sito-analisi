There are three independently operating traffic lights on the route from home to the office. The first is green with probability $0.6$, and the second and third are each green with probability $0.5$. What is the probability that all three are green?

- $0.15$
- $0.85$
- $0.3$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The three lights operate independently, so by the **multiplication rule**, the probability that all three are green is the product of their probabilities, $P = 0.6 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.5 = 0.15$. The value $0.3$ is the product for just the first two lights, and $0.25$ is the product for the last two. The value $0.85$ gives the probability of the complement, that at least one light is not green.
