A course offers $5$ administration modules and $3$ accounting modules. Two distinct modules are chosen at random, with all pairs equally likely. What is the probability that both are accounting modules?

- $\dfrac{9}{64}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{28}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}$

## Explanation

There are $\dbinom{3}{2}=3$ pairs of accounting modules and $\dbinom{8}{2}=28$ pairs in total. The required ratio is $\dfrac{3}{28}$.
