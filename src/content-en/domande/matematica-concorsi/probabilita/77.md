A bag contains $4$ green balls and $6$ yellow balls that feel identical. Two balls are drawn at the same time. What is the probability that they are different colors?

- $\dfrac{7}{15}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $\dfrac{12}{25}$

## Explanation

To get two different colors, choose one green and one yellow ball in $4\cdot6=24$ ways. There are $\dbinom{10}{2}=45$ possible pairs, so the probability is $\dfrac{8}{15}$.
