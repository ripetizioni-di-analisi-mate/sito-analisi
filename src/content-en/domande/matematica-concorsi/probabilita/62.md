A system selects an integer uniformly at random from $1$ to $30$. What is the probability that it is divisible by $3$ but not by $5$?

- $\dfrac{1}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

From $1$ to $30$ there are $10$ multiples of $3$, of which $15$ and $30$ are also multiples of $5$. This leaves $8$ favorable outcomes out of $30$, giving a probability of $\dfrac{4}{15}$.
