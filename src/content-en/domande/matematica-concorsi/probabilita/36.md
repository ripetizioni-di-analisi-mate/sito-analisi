A volunteer group has $7$ members, $4$ men and $3$ women. For the Sunday shift, $3$ volunteers are drawn at random, with all groups of $3$ equally likely to be chosen. What is the probability that at least one of those chosen is a woman?

- $\dfrac{4}{35}$
- $\dfrac{18}{35}$
- $\dfrac{31}{35}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

There are $C_{7,3} = 35$ possible groups. The complement of “at least one woman” is “men only,” with $C_{4,3} = 4$ favorable outcomes. By the **complement rule**, $P = 1 - \dfrac{4}{35} = \dfrac{31}{35}$. The value $\dfrac{4}{35}$ gives the probability of the complement. The value $\dfrac{18}{35}$ counts only the groups with *exactly* one woman, of which there are $C_{3,1} \cdot C_{4,2} = 3 \cdot 6 = 18$.
