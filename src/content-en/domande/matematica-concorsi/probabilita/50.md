A terminal randomly generates an integer from $1$ to $36$, with all integers equally likely. What is the probability that the number is divisible by $9$?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{36}$

## Explanation

The favorable values are $9$, $18$, $27$, and $36$. Dividing the four favorable outcomes by the $36$ possible outcomes gives $\dfrac{1}{9}$.
