Two independent trials succeed with probabilities $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$, respectively. What is the probability that exactly one succeeds?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Either the first succeeds and the second fails, or the reverse happens. These disjoint cases have probabilities $\dfrac{1}{12}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$, whose sum is $\dfrac{7}{12}$.
