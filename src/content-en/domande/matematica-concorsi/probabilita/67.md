Two checks are independent and each has probability $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of giving a favorable result. What is the probability that at least one gives a favorable result?

- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{4}{25}$
- $\dfrac{21}{25}$
- $\dfrac{16}{25}$

## Explanation

The complement is that both checks give unfavorable results. Its probability is $\dfrac{2}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}=\dfrac{4}{25}$, so the required probability is $\dfrac{21}{25}$.
