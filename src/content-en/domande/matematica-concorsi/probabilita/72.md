An urn contains $5$ red balls and $3$ blue balls that feel identical. Two balls are drawn without replacement. Given that the first is blue, what is the probability that the second is red?

- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

After a blue ball is drawn, seven balls remain, including all five red balls. The conditional probability is $\dfrac{5}{7}$.
