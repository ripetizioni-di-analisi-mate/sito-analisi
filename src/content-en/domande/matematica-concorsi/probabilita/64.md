From a French-suited deck of $52$ cards, the jacks, queens, and kings of the black suits (spades and clubs) are removed. The remaining deck is shuffled and one card is drawn. What is the probability of drawing a heart, given that each suit originally contained $13$ cards?

- $\dfrac{5}{23}$
- $\dfrac{13}{46}$
- $\dfrac{13}{49}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Three cards are removed from each of the two black suits, leaving $52-6=46$ cards. All thirteen hearts remain, so the probability is $\dfrac{13}{46}$.
