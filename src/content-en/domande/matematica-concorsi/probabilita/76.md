From $9$ people, a delegation of $3$ is selected at random, with all groups equally likely. What is the probability that a particular person is chosen?

- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$

## Explanation

There are $\dbinom{9}{3}$ possible groups and $\dbinom{8}{2}$ favorable groups, since one member is fixed. The ratio is $\dfrac{28}{84}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
