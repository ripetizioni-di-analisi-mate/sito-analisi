Urns A and B are equally likely to be chosen. Urn A contains $3$ red balls and $1$ white ball. Urn B contains $1$ red ball and $3$ white balls. The balls feel identical, and one is drawn from the chosen urn. Given that it is red, what is the probability that it came from A?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The two urns contain equal numbers of balls and are equally likely to be chosen, so the four red balls are equally likely outcomes. Three belong to A, so the conditional probability is $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
