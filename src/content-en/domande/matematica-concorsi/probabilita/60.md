An event has an observed relative frequency of $\dfrac{7}{40}$. What percentage does this correspond to?

- $28\%$
- $7\%$
- $14\%$
- $17.5\%$

## Explanation

Multiply the fraction by $100$ and append a percent sign to the result. The number before the percent sign comes from $\dfrac{7}{40}\cdot100=17.5$.
