Of $28$ questions in a question bank, $7$ cover administrative law and $5$ cover computer science. No question covers both subjects. If one question is drawn at random, what is the probability that it covers one of the two subjects?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{28}$

## Explanation

The questions on the two subjects form disjoint groups, so there are $7+5=12$ favorable questions. Dividing by the total gives $\dfrac{12}{28}=\dfrac{3}{7}$.
