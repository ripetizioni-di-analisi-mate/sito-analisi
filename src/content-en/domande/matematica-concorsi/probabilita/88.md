Three independent sensors monitor a machine. The first and second each detect a fault with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and the third detects it with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$. What is the probability that exactly two sensors detect the fault?

- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{16}$
- $\dfrac{5}{16}$
- $\dfrac{7}{16}$

## Explanation

If the third sensor misses the fault, both other sensors must detect it. This case has probability $\dfrac{1}{16}$. If the third sensor detects it, exactly one of the other two must detect it. This case has probability $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{6}{16}$. The sum is $\dfrac{7}{16}$.
