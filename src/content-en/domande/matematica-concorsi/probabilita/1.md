Three fair coins are flipped together, a $1$-euro coin, a $50$-cent coin, and a $20$-cent coin. An outcome records whether each coin shows heads or tails. How many outcomes are in the sample space?

- $4$
- $8$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

An outcome is an ordered triple, one result per coin. Each coin has $2$ possible results. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 2^3 = 8$ outcomes. The value $4$ counts only the possible numbers of heads, from $0$ to $3$. It groups together outcomes such as $TTC$ and $CTT$ (T for heads, C for tails), which are distinct because the coins are distinguishable. The value $6$ comes from adding $2 + 2 + 2$ instead of multiplying.
