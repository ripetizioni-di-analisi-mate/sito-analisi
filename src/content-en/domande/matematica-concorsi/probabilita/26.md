A resident sends the same request to three offices. They reply within a week with probabilities $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{3}$, and $\dfrac{1}{4}$, independently of one another. What is the probability that exactly one office replies within a week?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}$
- $\dfrac{11}{24}$

## Explanation

The probabilities that the offices do not reply within a week are $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$, and $\dfrac{3}{4}$. By the **multiplication rule**, the probability that only the first office replies is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{3}{4}$. The same method applies when only the second or only the third office replies. These three cases are mutually exclusive, so add their probabilities.
$$
P = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{6}{24} + \frac{3}{24} + \frac{2}{24} = \frac{11}{24} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{6}{24}$ gives the probability that no office replies, and $\dfrac{3}{4}$ gives the probability that *at least* one office replies.
