A fair eight-sided die is numbered from $1$ to $8$. What is the probability of rolling a divisor of $12$?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Of the numbers on the die, $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, and $6$ divide $12$. There are five favorable outcomes out of eight equally likely outcomes.
