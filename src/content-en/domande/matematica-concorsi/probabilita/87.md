One of the distinct anagrams of the Italian word MAMMA is chosen at random, with all anagrams equally likely. What is the probability that the two end letters are different?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The five letters give $\dfrac{5!}{3!\,2!}=10$ distinct anagrams. For the ends to differ, A and M can appear at the ends in either order, with three interior positions for the remaining A. There are $6$ favorable outcomes, so the probability is $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
