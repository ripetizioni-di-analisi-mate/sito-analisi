A fair coin is flipped $3$ times. What is the probability of getting more heads than tails?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

There are $2^3 = 8$ equally likely sequences of three flips. Heads outnumber tails if there are $2$ or $3$ heads, as in the sequences $TTC$, $TCT$, $CTT$, and $TTT$ (T for heads, C for tails). This gives $4$ favorable outcomes and $P = \dfrac{4}{8} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{8}$ counts only the sequences with exactly two heads and leaves out $TTT$. The value $\dfrac{7}{8}$ gives the probability of at least one head.
