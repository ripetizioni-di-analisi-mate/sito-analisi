A program generates a $4$-digit PIN by choosing each digit independently and at random from the $10$ digits $0$ through $9$. Digits can repeat. What is the probability that the PIN contains the digit $0$ at least once?

- $0.3439$
- $0.4$
- $0.6561$
- $0.0001$

## Explanation

The complement of “at least one $0$” is “no $0$.” Each digit differs from $0$ with probability $\dfrac{9}{10} = 0.9$. The digits are independent, so by the **multiplication rule**, the probability of no $0$ in the PIN is $0.9^4 = 0.6561$. By the **complement rule**, the probability sought is $1 - 0.6561 = 0.3439$. The value $0.4$ adds the four probabilities $0.1$. The events “the first digit is $0$” and “the second digit is $0$” can both occur, so the addition rule for mutually exclusive events does not apply.
