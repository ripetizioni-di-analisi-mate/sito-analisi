Two events $A$ and $B$ satisfy $P(A)=0.4$, $P(B)=0.35$, and $P(A\cap B)=0.15$. What is the probability that at least one occurs?

- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Adding the probabilities counts the shared outcomes twice. Subtracting the probability of the intersection gives $0.4+0.35-0.15=0.6$.
