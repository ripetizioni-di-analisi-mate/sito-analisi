For two events $A$ and $B$, it is known that $P(A)=\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $P(B)=\dfrac{2}{5}$. Which value is possible for the probability that at least one event occurs?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The union includes every outcome in $A$, so its probability is at least $\dfrac{3}{5}$. Equality is possible if $B$ is a subset of $A$.
