A board game uses two fair four-sided dice numbered from $1$ to $4$. What is the probability that their sum is $5$?

- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Each die has $4$ outcomes, giving $4 \cdot 4 = 16$ equally likely ordered pairs. The pairs with sum $5$ are $(1, 4)$, $(2, 3)$, $(3, 2)$, and $(4, 1)$, so $P = \dfrac{4}{16} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{7}$ treats the $7$ possible sums from $2$ to $8$ as equally likely, although different numbers of pairs produce them. The values $\dfrac{1}{8} = \dfrac{2}{16}$ and $\dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{2}{10}$ count pairs without regard to order. The first does this only for favorable outcomes, the second for all possible outcomes as well.
