Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that their sum is at least $4$?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac{9}{11}$

## Explanation

The complement of “sum of at least $4$” is “sum less than $4$,” so the sum must be $2$ or $3$. The pairs favorable to the complement are $(1, 1)$, $(1, 2)$, and $(2, 1)$, or $3$ out of $36$. By the **complement rule**, $P = 1 - \dfrac{3}{36} = \dfrac{33}{36} = \dfrac{11}{12}$. The value $\dfrac{5}{6}$ also removes the $3$ pairs with sum $4$, which are favorable. The value $\dfrac{9}{11}$ treats the $11$ sums from $2$ to $12$ as equally likely.
