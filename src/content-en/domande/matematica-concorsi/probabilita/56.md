Each document in an archive belongs to exactly one category. Of the documents, $25\%$ are in category A and $35\%$ are in category B. What is the probability that a randomly chosen document is in neither category?

- $60\%$
- $65\%$
- $10\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

The categories are disjoint, so A and B together make up $25\%+35\%=60\%$ of the total. The **complement rule** gives $40\%$.
