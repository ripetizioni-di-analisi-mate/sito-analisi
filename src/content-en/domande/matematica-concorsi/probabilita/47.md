A spinner has four sectors numbered from $1$ to $4$. It is spun twice and the ordered pair of numbers is recorded. How many outcomes are possible?

- $10$
- $8$
- $12$
- $16$

## Explanation

The first spin has four possible results, each of which can be paired with any of the four results of the second spin. By the **multiplication principle**, there are $4\cdot4=16$ pairs.
