A and B each roll a fair six-sided die. On a tie both roll again until one wins. What is the probability that A wins the game?

- $\dfrac5{12}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac7{12}$

## Explanation

The rules are symmetric. Among non-ties, exchanging the two rolls pairs every A win with a B win. The probability of tying forever is zero.
