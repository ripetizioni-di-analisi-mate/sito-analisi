Two independent fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the results differ by exactly $2$, regardless of which die shows the larger number?

- $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The unordered pairs are $(1,3)$, $(2,4)$, $(3,5)$, and $(4,6)$. Each pair can appear in two orders, so there are $8$ favorable outcomes out of $36$.
