Two fair, independent six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the results differ by exactly $2$, regardless of which die shows the larger value?

- $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The outcomes of the roll are the $6\cdot6=36$ ordered pairs of results, all equally likely. The pairs of numbers with difference $2$ are $1$ and $3$, $2$ and $4$, $3$ and $5$, $4$ and $6$. Each of them corresponds to two outcomes, depending on which die shows the larger number, so there are $8$ favorable outcomes. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{8}{36}=\dfrac{2}{9}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{9}$ counts each pair only once, and the value $\dfrac{5}{18}$ is the probability that the results differ by $1$.
