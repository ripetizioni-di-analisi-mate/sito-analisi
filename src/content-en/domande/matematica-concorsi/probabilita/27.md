In a practice soccer game, Elena and Paolo each take a penalty kick. Elena scores with probability $0.8$ and Paolo scores with probability $0.6$, independently. What is the probability that they have the same outcome, either both scoring or both missing?

- $0.56$
- $0.48$
- $0.44$
- $0.08$

## Explanation

By the **multiplication rule**, both score with probability $0.8 \cdot 0.6 = 0.48$ and both miss with probability $0.2 \cdot 0.4 = 0.08$. The cases are mutually exclusive, so add their probabilities to get $P = 0.48 + 0.08 = 0.56$. The value $0.48$ counts only the case where both score. The value $0.44$ gives the probability of the complement, that exactly one player scores.
