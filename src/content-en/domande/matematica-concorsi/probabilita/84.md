Two independent fair six-sided dice are rolled. Given that the product of their results is even, what is the probability that both results are even?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The product is even unless both results are odd. That leaves $36-9=27$ possible outcomes, of which $9$ have two even numbers, so the ratio is $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
