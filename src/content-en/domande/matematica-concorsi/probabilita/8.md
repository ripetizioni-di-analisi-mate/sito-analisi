One card is drawn from a well-shuffled Italian deck of $40$ cards. What is the probability that its rank is $7$?

- $\dfrac{1}{13}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{40}$
- $\dfrac{7}{40}$

## Explanation

An Italian deck has $4$ suits and just one $7$ per suit, giving $4$ favorable outcomes. The deck is well shuffled, so the $40$ cards are equally likely and $P = \dfrac{4}{40} = \dfrac{1}{10}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{13}$ gives the probability of drawing a specified rank from a French-suited deck of $52$ cards. The value $\dfrac{7}{40}$ counts the cards from the ace through the $7$ in one suit. The value $\dfrac{1}{40}$ gives the probability of drawing the $7$ of one specified suit.
