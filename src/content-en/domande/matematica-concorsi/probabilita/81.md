An urn contains $4$ white balls and $3$ black balls that feel identical. Two balls are drawn without replacement. Given that at least one is black, what is the probability that both are black?

- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

There are $\dbinom{7}{2}-\dbinom{4}{2}=15$ pairs with at least one black ball. There are $\dbinom{3}{2}=3$ pairs with two black balls, so the conditional probability is $\dfrac{3}{15}=\dfrac{1}{5}$.
