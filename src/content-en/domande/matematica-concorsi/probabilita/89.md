Of $60$ candidates, $28$ know English, $24$ know French, and $10$ know both languages. If one candidate is chosen at random, what is the probability that this person knows exactly one of the languages?

- $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

There are $28-10=18$ candidates who know only English and $24-10=14$ who know only French. The required probability is $\dfrac{18+14}{60}=\dfrac{8}{15}$.
