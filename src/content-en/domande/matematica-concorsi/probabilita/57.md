Two independent checks succeed with probabilities $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{2}{3}$. What is the probability that both succeed?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{7}{12}$

## Explanation

Because the checks are independent, the probability of two successes is the product of their individual probabilities, $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
