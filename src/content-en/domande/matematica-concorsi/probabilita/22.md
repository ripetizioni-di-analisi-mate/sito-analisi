A fair six-sided die is rolled and one card is drawn from a well-shuffled Italian deck of $40$ cards. What is the probability of rolling $6$ and drawing a card from the suit of cups?

- $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{240}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{24}$

## Explanation

Rolling the die and drawing the card are separate experiments, so the events are **independent**. The probability of rolling $6$ is $\dfrac{1}{6}$, and the probability of drawing a card from the suit of cups is $\dfrac{10}{40} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. By the **multiplication rule**, $P = \dfrac{1}{6} \cdot \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{24}$. The value $\dfrac{5}{12}$ adds the probabilities. The value $\dfrac{1}{240}$ uses $\dfrac{1}{40}$, the probability of one specific card, in place of the probability of a suit. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ accounts only for the card.
