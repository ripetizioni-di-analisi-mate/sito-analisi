A fair coin has been flipped $6$ times and has landed tails every time. What is the probability of heads on the seventh flip?

- $\dfrac{127}{128}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{128}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}$

## Explanation

The flips are **independent** because the conditions of each flip are the same regardless of previous results. The probability of heads on the seventh flip is still $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{128} = \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{7}$ is the probability, calculated before the first flip, of seven tails in a row. The value $\dfrac{127}{128}$ gives the probability of at least one head in seven flips, an event that concerns all seven flips together.
