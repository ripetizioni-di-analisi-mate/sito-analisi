Two different tokens are chosen at random without replacement from tokens numbered from $1$ to $6$. What is the probability that their sum is even?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The sum is even if both numbers are even or both are odd. There are $3$ pairs of each kind out of $15$ pairs in total, so the probability is $\dfrac{6}{15}=\dfrac{2}{5}$.
