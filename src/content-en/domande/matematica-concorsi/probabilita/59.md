In a first sample, 12 of 20 answers are correct. After another 10 answers, the overall rate is still 60%. How many correct answers are in the second sample?

- $3$
- $8$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

Sixty percent of 30 is 18 correct answers. Subtracting the original 12 leaves 6 in the second sample.
