Of $250$ participants, $110$ meet the qualifying threshold. Which fraction gives the relative frequency of participants who qualify?

- $\dfrac{14}{25}$
- $\dfrac{11}{50}$
- $\dfrac{11}{14}$
- $\dfrac{11}{25}$

## Explanation

The denominator is the total number of participants. The relative frequency is $\dfrac{110}{250}=\dfrac{11}{25}$.
