A fair six-sided die is rolled. Which of the following pairs consists of mutually exclusive events?

- “Roll a prime number” and “roll an even number”
- “Roll a multiple of $3$” and “roll a number less than $3$”
- “Roll an odd number” and “roll a number greater than $4$”
- “Roll a number less than $4$” and “roll a prime number”

## Explanation

Two events are **mutually exclusive** if they have no favorable outcomes in common. The multiples of $3$ on the die are $3$ and $6$. The numbers less than $3$ are $1$ and $2$. The two sets have no elements in common, so the events are mutually exclusive. All the other pairs overlap. The number $2$ is both prime and even, $5$ is odd and greater than $4$, and $2$ and $3$ are both prime and less than $4$.
