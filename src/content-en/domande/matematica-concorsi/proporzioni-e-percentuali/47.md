A form requires a photograph with a width-to-height ratio of $3:2$. A $960$ by $720$ pixel photograph is cropped using the full width. How many pixel rows must be removed in total?

- $40$
- $120$
- $80$
- $240$

## Explanation

The width of 960 represents three parts, so the required height is $2\cdot320=640$. The original photograph has $720-640=80$ extra rows.
