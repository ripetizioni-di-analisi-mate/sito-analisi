What is the ratio of $45\ \text{cm}$ to $1.2\ \text{m}$, in simplest form?

- $8:3$
- $15:4$
- $3:8$
- $3:80$

## Explanation

To find the **ratio**, express both lengths in the same unit, giving $1.2\ \text{m}=120\ \text{cm}$. Dividing both terms of $45:120$ by $15$ gives $3:8$.
