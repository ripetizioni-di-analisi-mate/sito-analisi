A form requires a photograph with a width-to-height ratio of $3:2$. A photo $960$ pixels wide and $720$ pixels high is cropped keeping its full width. How many rows of pixels must be removed in total?

- $40$
- $240$
- $80$
- $320$

## Explanation

Keeping the width of $960$ pixels, the height $h$ must satisfy the proportion $960 : h = 3 : 2$. By the **fundamental property of proportions**, $3h = 2 \cdot 960$, so $h = 640$ pixels. Therefore $720 - 640 = 80$ rows must be removed. The value $40$ is the number of rows removed from each edge when the crop is split between the top and bottom edges, and the value $240$ comes from taking $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the height instead of the width.
