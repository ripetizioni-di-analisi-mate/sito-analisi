In an archive, $30\%$ of the $900$ files are digital. The number of digital files increases by $20\%$ and the number of paper files decreases by $10\%$. What is the percentage change in the total number of files?

- It decreases by $1\%$.
- It decreases by $3\%$.
- It increases by $10\%$.
- It increases by $1\%$.

## Explanation

Calculate the **changes in the two groups** using their respective initial counts. Digital files increase by $270\cdot0.20=54$ and paper files decrease by $630\cdot0.10=63$. The total falls by $9$ out of $900$, or $1\%$.
