A printer prints $36$ pages in $4$ minutes. How many pages will it print in $12$ minutes at the same rate?

- $144$
- $108$
- $12$
- $432$

## Explanation

At a constant rate, the number of pages is **directly proportional** to the time. Since $12$ minutes is three times $4$ minutes, the number of pages is three times $36$, or $108$. The value $12$ treats the quantities as inversely proportional. It is less than $36$ even though the time increases.
