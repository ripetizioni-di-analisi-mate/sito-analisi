A box contains only red and blue balls. The red balls make up $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of the total. What is the ratio of red balls to blue balls?

- $5 : 3$
- $5 : 8$
- $3 : 5$
- $3 : 8$

## Explanation

The red balls account for $3$ parts out of $8$, leaving $8 - 3 = 5$ parts for the blue balls. The **ratio** of red to blue is therefore $3 : 5$. The ratio $3 : 8$ compares red balls with all the balls, while $5 : 3$ reverses the order and compares blue with red.
