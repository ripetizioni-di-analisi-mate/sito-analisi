What percentage of $1.5$ kg is $300$ g?

- $20\%$
- $5\%$
- $200\%$
- $2\%$

## Explanation

Express both quantities in the same unit. Since $1.5$ kg $= 1500$ g, the **percentage** is $\dfrac{300}{1500} \cdot 100\% = 20\%$. Incorrectly converting $1.5$ kg to $150$ g gives $200\%$. Converting it to $15\,000$ g instead gives $2\%$.
