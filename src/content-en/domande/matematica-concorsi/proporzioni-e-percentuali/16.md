A car uses an average of $6$ liters of fuel per $100$ km. At the same fuel consumption rate, how many kilometers can it travel on $27$ liters?

- $450$ km
- $45$ km
- $1620$ km
- $162$ km

## Explanation

Since fuel consumption is constant, the distance traveled is **directly proportional** to the number of liters. The proportion $6 : 100 = 27 : x$ gives $x = \dfrac{100 \cdot 27}{6} = 450$ km. Multiplying $27$ by $6$ instead of dividing gives $162$ km. The value $45$ km differs from the result by a factor of $10$.
