An amount of $840$ euros is divided among three groups in proportion to $4$, $6$, and $11$, respectively. How much larger is the largest share than the smallest?

- $240$ euros
- $440$ euros
- $280$ euros
- $160$ euros

## Explanation

The **sum of the weights** is $4+6+11=21$, so each part is worth $840\div21=40$ euros. The largest share exceeds the smallest by $11-4=7$ parts, or $280$ euros.
