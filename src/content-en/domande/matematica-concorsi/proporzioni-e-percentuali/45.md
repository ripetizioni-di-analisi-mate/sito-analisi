What is $37\%$ of $63$ plus $63\%$ of $37$?

- $23.31$
- $100$
- $4.662$
- $46.62$

## Explanation

The value of $p\%$ of $q$ equals $q\%$ of $p$ because both equal $\dfrac{p \cdot q}{100}$. By **switching the numbers**, we see that the two terms are equal, so the sum is $2 \cdot \dfrac{37 \cdot 63}{100}$. Since $37 \cdot 63 = 37 \cdot 60 + 37 \cdot 3 = 2220 + 111 = 2331$, the sum is $2 \cdot 23.31 = 46.62$. The value $23.31$ gives only one of the terms, while $100$ is the sum $37 + 63$.
