A train completes a trip in $3$ hours at an average speed of $80$ km/h. What average speed would it need to complete the same trip in $2$ hours and $24$ minutes?

- $100$ km/h
- $96$ km/h
- $120$ km/h
- $64$ km/h

## Explanation

The $24$ minutes equal $\dfrac{24}{60} = 0.4$ hours, so the new travel time is $2.4$ hours. The distance is $80 \cdot 3 = 240$ km, so the required speed is $\dfrac{240}{2.4} = 100$ km/h. The speed $96$ km/h assumes that because the time decreases by $20\%$, the speed must increase by the same $20\%$. Since speed and time are **inversely proportional**, the actual speed multiplier is $\dfrac{3}{2.4} = 1.25$.
