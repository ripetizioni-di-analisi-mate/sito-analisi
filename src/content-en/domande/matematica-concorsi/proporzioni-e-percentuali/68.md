Of the $480$ people registered, $35\%$ chose the morning session. Of those who chose the morning, $25\%$ have already confirmed. How many people registered for the morning session still need to confirm?

- $126$
- $120$
- $42$
- $168$

## Explanation

The number registered for the morning is $480\cdot0.35=168$. The **percentage still to confirm** is the complement of $25\%$, or $75\%$, so $168\cdot0.75=126$ people still need to confirm.
