Three departments A, B, and C have resource allocations satisfying $A:B=2:3$ and $B:C=4:5$. What is the ratio $A:C$ in simplest form?

- $15:8$
- $3:10$
- $2:5$
- $8:15$

## Explanation

Express the two **ratios** with the same value for B. This gives $A:B=8:12$ and $B:C=12:15$, so $A:C=8:15$.
