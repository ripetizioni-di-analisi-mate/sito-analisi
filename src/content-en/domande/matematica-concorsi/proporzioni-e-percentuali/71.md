Two items have the same final price of $144$ euros. The first was discounted by $20\%$ and the second by $40\%$. How much higher was the second item's original price?

- $28.80$ euros
- $60$ euros
- $36$ euros
- $48$ euros

## Explanation

To recover the **original prices**, divide the final price by the fraction left after each discount. The original prices were $144\div0.80=180$ euros and $144\div0.60=240$ euros, a difference of $60$ euros.
