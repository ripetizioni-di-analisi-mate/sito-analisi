Two departments have staffing levels in the ratio $5:3$. After $8$ workers transfer from the first department to the second, the ratio becomes $7:5$. How many workers are there in all?

- $96$
- $192$
- $120$
- $144$

## Explanation

The **total stays the same**, so compare the first department's shares before and after the transfer, $\dfrac58$ and $\dfrac7{12}$. The decrease is $\dfrac58-\dfrac7{12}=\dfrac1{24}$ of the total and corresponds to $8$ workers. The total is therefore $8\cdot24=192$.
