Store A discounts the list price by $25\%$ and charges $12$ euros for delivery. Store B discounts the same list price by $15\%$ and offers free delivery. At what list price are the final costs equal?

- $120$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $150$ euros
- $80$ euros

## Explanation

The **difference between the discounts** is $10\%$ of the list price. The final costs are equal when that difference offsets the $12$ euros for delivery, so the list price is $12\div0.10=120$ euros.
