The ratio of paper files to digital files is $4:7$. There are $45$ more digital files than paper files. How many files are there in all?

- $105$
- $120$
- $165$
- $150$

## Explanation

The **difference** of $45$ files corresponds to $7-4=3$ parts, so one part is worth $15$. There are $4+7=11$ parts in all, or $165$ files.
