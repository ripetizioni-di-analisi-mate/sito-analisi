A television costs $1198$ euros and is discounted by $24.8\%$. What is the discounted price, rounded to the nearest cent?

- $900.90$ euros
- $90.09$ euros
- $1495.10$ euros
- $297.10$ euros

## Explanation

The discount of $24.8\%$ is just under a quarter, so the discounted price is just over three-quarters of approximately $1200$ euros, or about $900$ euros. This **estimate** rules out $90.09$ euros, which is off by a factor of $10$. The amount $1495.10$ euros exceeds the original price and corresponds to an increase of $24.8\%$, while $297.10$ euros is the amount saved. The exact calculation $1198 \cdot 0.752 = 900.896$ confirms the result of $900.90$ euros.
