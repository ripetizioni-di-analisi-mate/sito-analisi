What positive number $x$ satisfies $4 : x = x : 25$?

- $100$
- $6.25$
- $10$
- $14.5$

## Explanation

By the **cross-product property**, the product of the means $x \cdot x$ equals the product of the extremes $4 \cdot 25 = 100$. Thus $x^2 = 100$, and since $x$ is positive, $x = 10$. The value $100$ gives $x^2$. The value $14.5$ is the arithmetic mean of $4$ and $25$ and does not satisfy the proportion.
