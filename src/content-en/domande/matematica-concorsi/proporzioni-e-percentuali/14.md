Six identical machines produce $900$ parts in $5$ hours. At the same rate, how many hours would $4$ of these machines take to produce $1080$ parts?

- $7.5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

Each machine makes $\dfrac{900}{6 \cdot 5} = 30$ parts per hour, so $4$ machines make $120$ parts per hour. Producing $1080$ parts therefore takes $\dfrac{1080}{120} = 9$ hours. The value $6$ accounts only for the increase in parts and $7.5$ only for the reduction in machines, while $4$ treats the number of machines as **directly proportional** to the time.
