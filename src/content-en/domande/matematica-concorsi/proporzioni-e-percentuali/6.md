In the proportion $(20 - x) : x = 3 : 2$, what is $x$?

- $\dfrac{40}{3}$
- $12$
- $8$
- $40$

## Explanation

The **cross-product property** gives $2\,(20 - x) = 3x$, or $40 - 2x = 3x$. Then $5x = 40$ and $x = 8$. To check, $20 - 8 = 12$ and $12 : 8 = 3 : 2$. The value $12$ gives $20 - x$, while $\dfrac{40}{3}$ replaces the entire expression $20 - x$ with the number $20$ alone.
