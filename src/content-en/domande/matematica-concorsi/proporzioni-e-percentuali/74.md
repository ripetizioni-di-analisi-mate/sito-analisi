An item is discounted by $30\%$ and then by another $10\%$ off the reduced price. What single discount gives the same result?

- $33\%$
- $36\%$
- $40\%$
- $37\%$

## Explanation

With two **successive discounts**, multiply the price first by $0.70$ and then by $0.90$. Their product is $0.63$, so the overall discount is $37\%$.
