Which pair of quantities is inversely proportional?

- The length of a trip and the time needed to complete it at a constant speed
- The number of equal installments used to repay a fixed sum and the amount of each installment
- The side length of a square and its area
- The side length of a square and its perimeter

## Explanation

Two quantities are **inversely proportional** if their product is constant. The number of installments times the amount of each installment always equals the fixed sum to be repaid, so these quantities are inversely proportional. The side length and perimeter of a square have a constant ratio and are directly proportional, as are distance and travel time at a constant speed. The area of a square equals the square of its side length, so side length and area increase together without being proportional.
