A receipt shows a total of 110 euros for two items with the same pre-tax price. One is exempt and the other is taxed at 20%. What is the pre-tax price of each item?

- 45 euros
- 55 euros
- 50 euros
- 60 euros

## Explanation

Writing $x$ for the common price gives $x+1.2x=110$. Thus $2.2x=110$ and $x=50$.
