A receipt shows a total of $110$ euros for two items that have the same price before tax. The first item is tax-exempt, the second is subject to a $20\%$ tax. What is the price of each item before tax?

- $55$ euros
- $45.83$ euros
- $50$ euros
- $66$ euros

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the common price before tax; the first item costs $x$ and the second, with the $20\%$ tax, costs $1.2x$. The total is therefore $x + 1.2x = 2.2x = 110$, so $x = \dfrac{110}{2.2} = 50$ euros. The value $55$ euros ignores the tax, the value $45.83$ euros applies the tax to both items and the value $66$ euros adds $20\%$ to half of the total.
