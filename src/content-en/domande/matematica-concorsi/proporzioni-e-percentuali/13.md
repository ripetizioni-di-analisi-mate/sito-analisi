Twelve workers, each working at the same constant rate, could complete a job in $18$ days. After $6$ days, $4$ workers transfer elsewhere, and the rest continue at the same rate. How many days does the job take in all?

- $24$
- $18$
- $33$
- $27$

## Explanation

The job requires $12 \cdot 18 = 216$ worker-days. In the first $6$ days, the workers complete $12 \cdot 6 = 72$ worker-days. This leaves $144$ worker-days for $8$ workers, so they need $\dfrac{144}{8} = 18$ more days. The job takes $6 + 18 = 24$ days in all. The value $18$ counts only the days after the transfer. Dividing the entire job among $8$ workers gives $27$ because it ignores the work already completed.
