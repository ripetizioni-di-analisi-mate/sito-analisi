After spending $65\%$ of his salary, Marco has $560$ euros left. What is his salary?

- $924$ euros
- $364$ euros
- $196$ euros
- $1600$ euros

## Explanation

The $560$ euros Marco has left represent $100\% - 65\% = 35\%$ of his salary. The proportion $560 : S = 35 : 100$ gives $S = \dfrac{560 \cdot 100}{35} = 1600$ euros. Increasing $560$ euros by $65\%$ gives $924$ euros, applying the percentage to the amount left instead of the salary.
