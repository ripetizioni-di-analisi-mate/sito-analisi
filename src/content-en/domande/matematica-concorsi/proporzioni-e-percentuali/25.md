At a company, $40\%$ of employees have a college degree. Of those, $25\%$ also have a master's degree. What percentage of all employees have a college degree and a master's degree?

- $65\%$
- $15\%$
- $10\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The figure $25\%$ applies only to employees with a college degree. To find the share among all employees, take $25\%$ of $40\%$. The result is $\dfrac{25}{100} \cdot 40\% = 10\%$, or one-quarter of $40\%$. Adding two percentages based on different totals gives $65\%$.
