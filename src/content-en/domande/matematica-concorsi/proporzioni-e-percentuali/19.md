A sum of money is divided in direct proportion to $2$, $3$, and $7$, and the largest share exceeds the smallest by $250$ euros. What is the total amount?

- $100$ euros
- $350$ euros
- $3000$ euros
- $600$ euros

## Explanation

The shares are $2k$, $3k$, and $7k$, where $k$ is the value of one part. The difference between the largest and smallest shares is $7k - 2k = 5k = 250$, so $k = 50$ euros. The total is therefore $(2 + 3 + 7) \cdot 50 = 600$ euros. The largest share is $350$ euros. Multiplying $250$ by $12$ gives $3000$ euros, as if $250$ euros were the value of just one part.
