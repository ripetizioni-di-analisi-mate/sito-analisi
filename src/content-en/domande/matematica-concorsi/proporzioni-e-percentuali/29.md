A store discounts a coat by $20\%$, saving the customer $34$ euros. How much does the customer pay?

- $27.20$ euros
- $136$ euros
- $170$ euros
- $204$ euros

## Explanation

The discount of $34$ euros is $20\%$ of the original price, so the original price is $\dfrac{34 \cdot 100}{20} = 170$ euros. The customer therefore pays $170 - 34 = 136$ euros, which also equals $170 \cdot 0.8$. The amount $170$ euros is the price before the discount. Applying the $20\%$ discount to the $34$ euros saved gives $27.20$ euros.
