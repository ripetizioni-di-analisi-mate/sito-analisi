A restaurant charges VAT at $10\%$. A bill totals $66$ euros including VAT. What is the taxable amount?

- $59.40$ euros
- $60$ euros
- $72.60$ euros
- $6$ euros

## Explanation

The price including VAT is the taxable amount times the multiplier $1.1$. To **remove VAT**, divide $66$ by $1.1$ to get $60$ euros. To check, $10\%$ of $60$ euros is $6$ euros, and $60 + 6 = 66$. Subtracting $10\%$ from the VAT-inclusive price of $66$ euros gives $59.40$ euros because it applies the tax rate to the wrong base.
