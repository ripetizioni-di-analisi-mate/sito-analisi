The price of a service increases by $20\%$, and a discount then applies to the increased price. The final price is $8\%$ higher than the original price. What was the percentage discount?

- $11.\overline{1}\%$
- $10\%$
- $12\%$
- $28\%$

## Explanation

For **successive percentage changes**, the final price is the initial price multiplied by $1.2$ and the discount factor $f$. It must equal the initial price times $1.08$. Thus $1.2\,f = 1.08$ and $f = \dfrac{1.08}{1.2} = 0.9$, so the discount is $10\%$. An initial price of $100$ euros rises to $120$ euros and then falls to $108$ euros. Subtracting the percentages gives $12\%$. Dividing the decrease of $12$ euros by the final price of $108$ euros instead of the increased price gives $11.\overline{1}\%$.
