What value of $x$ makes the proportion $14:35=x:20$ true?

- $8$
- $10$
- $5$
- $50$

## Explanation

By the **fundamental property of proportions** the product of the means equals the product of the extremes, so $35x = 14 \cdot 20 = 280$. Therefore $x = \dfrac{280}{35} = 8$. The same result follows by reducing the ratio $14:35$ to $2:5$, because $20$ is four times $5$ and four times $2$ is $8$. The value $50$ comes from swapping means and extremes, that is from solving $14x = 35 \cdot 20$.
