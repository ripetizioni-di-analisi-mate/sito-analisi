Two containers have the same shape. The ratio of each internal dimension of the first container to the corresponding dimension of the second is $1:5$. The first has a capacity of $320\ \text{mL}$. What is the capacity of the second?

- $8\ \text{L}$
- $1.6\ \text{L}$
- $16\ \text{L}$
- $40\ \text{L}$

## Explanation

From the first container to the second, the **linear scale factor** is $5$, so the volume factor is $5^3=125$. The second container has a capacity of $320\cdot125=40\,000\ \text{mL}$, or $40\ \text{L}$.
