A shop offers a $20\%$ discount, with a maximum saving of $12$ euros per item. On an item costing $90$ euros, what percentage of the price does the actual discount correspond to?

- $13.\overline{3}\%$
- $20\%$
- $12\%$
- $6.\overline{6}\%$

## Explanation

$20\%$ of $90$ euros is $18$ euros, which exceeds the maximum saving, so the actual discount is $12$ euros. As a percentage of the price the discount is $\dfrac{12}{90} \cdot 100\% = \dfrac{40}{3}\% = 13.\overline{3}\%$. The value $20\%$ ignores the $12$ euro cap, the value $12\%$ reads the $12$ euros as percentage points and the value $6.\overline{6}\%$ is the part of the discount not granted, $\dfrac{6}{90}$ of the price.
