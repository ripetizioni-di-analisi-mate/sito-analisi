A shop offers 20% off, capped at a saving of 12 euros. What percentage discount does a 90-euro item actually receive?

- $13\dfrac13\%$
- $20\%$
- $12\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

Twenty percent of 90 is 18, so the 12-euro cap applies. The saving is $\dfrac{12}{90}=\dfrac{2}{15}$ of the price.
