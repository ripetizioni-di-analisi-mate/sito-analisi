The price of a service rises by $25\%$ and then falls by $20\%$ of the increased price. How does the final price compare with the original price?

- It is the same.
- It is $5\%$ lower.
- It is $5\%$ higher.
- It is $2\%$ higher.

## Explanation

The **successive changes** have factors $1.25$ and $0.80$. Their product is $1$, so the final price equals the original price.
