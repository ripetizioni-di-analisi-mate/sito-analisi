A stock falls by $20\%$ on Monday, falls by another $20\%$ on Tuesday, and rises by $40\%$ on Wednesday. Each change is relative to the previous day's value. How does the price at Wednesday's close compare with the initial price?

- down by $16\%$
- unchanged
- up by $12\%$
- down by $10.4\%$

## Explanation

For **successive percentage changes**, multiply the factors $0.8$, $0.8$, and $1.4$. This gives $0.8 \cdot 0.8 = 0.64$ and $0.64 \cdot 1.4 = 0.896$, so the final price is $89.6\%$ of the original price, or $10.4\%$ lower. The answer “unchanged” adds the signed percentages. A decrease of $16\%$ treats the two declines as one drop of $40\%$.
