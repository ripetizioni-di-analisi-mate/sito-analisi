In a warehouse, $54$ containers account for $18\%$ of the total. How many containers are there in all?

- $324$
- $300$
- $270$
- $350$

## Explanation

If $54$ containers are $18\%$ of the total, **one percent** is $54\div18=3$ containers. Then $100\%$ corresponds to $300$ containers.
