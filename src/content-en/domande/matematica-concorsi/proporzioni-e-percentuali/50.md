The reading pairs $(x,y)$ are $(2,5),(4,10),(6,16)$. Which third value of $y$ would make the readings directly proportional?

- $15$
- $12$
- $18$
- $20$

## Explanation

The first two pairs fix the ratio $\dfrac yx=\dfrac52$. At $x=6$ the consistent value is $6\cdot\dfrac52=15$.
