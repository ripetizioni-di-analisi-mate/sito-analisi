On a map with scale $1:25\,000$, a straight trail measures $4\ \text{cm}$. How long is the actual trail?

- $0.1\ \text{km}$
- $1\ \text{km}$
- $10\ \text{km}$
- $2.5\ \text{km}$

## Explanation

The **scale** means that $1\ \text{cm}$ on the map represents $25\,000\ \text{cm}$ on the ground. The trail’s actual length is $100\,000\ \text{cm}$, or $1\ \text{km}$.
