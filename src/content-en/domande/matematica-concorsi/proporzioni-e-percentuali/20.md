Three teams divide a payment of $4400$ euros in direct proportion to the work they completed, measured in worker-days. The first team had $3$ workers for $4$ days, the second had $5$ workers for $2$ days, and the third had $2$ workers for $9$ days. How much does the third team receive?

- $1980$ euros
- $2640$ euros
- $880$ euros
- $1936$ euros

## Explanation

Each team's work equals its number of workers times the number of days, giving $12$, $10$, and $18$ worker-days. Divide the payment into $12 + 10 + 18 = 40$ parts worth $\dfrac{4400}{40} = 110$ euros each. The third team receives $18$ parts, or $1980$ euros. The amount $880$ euros accounts only for the number of workers, and $2640$ euros accounts only for the days. Adding workers and days instead of multiplying them gives $1936$ euros.
