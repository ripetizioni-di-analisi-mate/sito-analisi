In a class of $30$ students, $40\%$ are girls. No girls leave the class. How many boys must leave for girls to make up $60\%$ of the remaining students?

- $6$
- $12$
- $8$
- $10$

## Explanation

The girls make up $40\%$ of $30$, or $12$, and their number stays the same. If $12$ girls are to make up $60\%$ of the remaining students, the total must be $\dfrac{12 \cdot 100}{60} = 20$. The number of boys drops from $18$ to $8$, so $10$ boys must leave. The value $6$ applies the difference $60\% - 40\% = 20\%$ to the original total of $30$ students, while $8$ is the number of boys who remain.
