A fundraiser has reached $75\%$ of its target. The target is then raised by $20\%$, and meanwhile no new donations arrive. What percentage of the new target has already been raised?

- $55\%$
- $83.\overline{3}\%$
- $62.5\%$
- $90\%$

## Explanation

If the initial target is $100$ euros, $75$ euros have been raised and the new target is $100 \cdot 1.2 = 120$ euros. The amount raised is therefore $\dfrac{75}{120} = \dfrac{5}{8} = 0.625$ of the new target, that is $62.5\%$. The result does not depend on the initial target chosen, because both the amount raised and the target are proportional to it. The value $55\%$ subtracts $20$ points from $75\%$, the value $90\%$ increases $75\%$ by $20\%$ and the value $83.\overline{3}\%$ is the ratio of the old target to the new one.
