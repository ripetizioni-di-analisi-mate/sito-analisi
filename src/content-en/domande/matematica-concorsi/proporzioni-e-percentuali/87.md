A collection has reached 75% of its target. The target rises by 20% and no new donations arrive. What percentage of the new target has been collected?

- $55\%$
- $60\%$
- $62.5\%$
- $90\%$

## Explanation

On an original target of 100 units, 75 were collected. The new target is 120, giving a completion fraction of $\dfrac{75}{120}=0.625$.
