After an increase of $20\%$ and then a discount on the increased price, the final price is $4\%$ lower than the original price. What was the percentage discount?

- $24\%$
- $25\%$
- $20\%$
- $16\%$

## Explanation

The **overall multiplier** is $0.96$ and the multiplier for the initial increase is $1.20$. The discount multiplier is therefore $0.96\div1.20=0.80$, which corresponds to a discount of $20\%$.
