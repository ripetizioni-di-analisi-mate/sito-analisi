Multiplying a price by $0.85$ is equivalent to applying which discount?

- a discount of $0.85\%$
- a discount of $85\%$
- a discount of $8.5\%$
- a discount of $15\%$

## Explanation

A discount of $p\%$ multiplies the price by the **multiplier** $1 - \dfrac{p}{100}$. From $1 - \dfrac{p}{100} = 0.85$ we get $\dfrac{p}{100} = 0.15$, so $p = 15$. A discount of $85\%$ would leave $15\%$ of the price to pay, multiplying the price by $0.15$.
