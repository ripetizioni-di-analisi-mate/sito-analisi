Which set of pairs $(x,y)$ is consistent with inverse variation between $x$ and $y$?

- $(2,4)$, $(3,6)$, and $(6,12)$
- $(2,18)$, $(3,9)$, and $(6,6)$
- $(2,16)$, $(3,15)$, and $(6,12)$
- $(2,18)$, $(3,12)$, and $(6,6)$

## Explanation

In **inverse variation**, the product $xy$ stays constant. For the pairs $(2,18)$, $(3,12)$, and $(6,6)$, the product is always $36$. In the other sets it varies.
