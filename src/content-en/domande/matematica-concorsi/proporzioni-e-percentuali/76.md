After a value decreases by $60\%$, by what percentage must it increase to return to its original value?

- $250\%$
- $60\%$
- $100\%$
- $150\%$

## Explanation

After the decrease, $40\%$ of the initial value remains. Returning to the starting value requires adding $60$ parts for every $40$ remaining parts, so the **percentage increase** is $60\div40=150\%$.
