A supplier earns a commission of $6\%$ only on sales above $2\,000$ euros. If sales total $3\,250$ euros, how much is the commission?

- $120$ euros
- $87.50$ euros
- $195$ euros
- $75$ euros

## Explanation

The **commission base** is the amount above the threshold, $3\,250-2\,000=1\,250$ euros. Taking $6\%$ of that amount gives $1\,250\cdot0.06=75$ euros.
