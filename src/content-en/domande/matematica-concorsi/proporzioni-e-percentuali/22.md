An organization has $300$ employees, and $35\%$ of them work remotely. How many employees do not work remotely?

- $265$
- $200$
- $105$
- $195$

## Explanation

The employees who do not work remotely make up $100\% - 35\% = 65\%$ of the total. Then $65\%$ of $300$ is $\dfrac{65}{100} \cdot 300 = 195$. The value $105$ counts the remote workers. Subtracting $35$ from $300$ as if it were a head count gives $265$. Treating $35\%$ as one-third gives $200$.
