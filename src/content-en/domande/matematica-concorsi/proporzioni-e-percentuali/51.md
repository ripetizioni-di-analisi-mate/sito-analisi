Four identical machines complete an order in $9$ hours. How long would six identical machines take if all operate at the same rate?

- $13.5$ hours
- $6$ hours
- $4$ hours
- $7$ hours

## Explanation

For a fixed order, time is **inversely proportional** to the number of machines. The job requires $4\cdot9=36$ hours of work by one machine, or $6$ hours with six machines sharing the work.
