A job requires 30 person-hours. At most 4 people may work at a time, and each is paid only for the hours worked. What is the minimum number of two-hour shifts needed, allowing partly used shifts?

- $3$
- $4$
- $8$
- $15$

## Explanation

A shift provides at most $4\cdot2=8$ person-hours. Three shifts supply 24, and a fourth can cover the remaining 6.
