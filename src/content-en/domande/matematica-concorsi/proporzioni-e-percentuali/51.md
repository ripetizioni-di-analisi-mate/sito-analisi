Two gear wheels mesh with each other. The first has $36$ teeth and makes $60$ revolutions per minute; the second has $45$ teeth. How many revolutions per minute does the second wheel make?

- $75$
- $48$
- $0.8$
- $60$

## Explanation

When two wheels mesh, in the same time the same number of teeth of each wheel passes the point of contact. Hence the product of the number of teeth and the number of revolutions is the same for both wheels, and the two quantities are **inversely proportional**. On the first wheel $36 \cdot 60 = 2160$ teeth pass per minute, so the second makes $\dfrac{2160}{45} = 48$ revolutions per minute. The value $75$ comes from direct proportionality, and it is ruled out because the wheel with more teeth turns more slowly. The value $0.8$ is the ratio $\dfrac{36}{45}$ of the numbers of teeth, without multiplying by the $60$ revolutions.
