The interest rate on a mortgage changes from $4\%$ to $3\%$. Which statement is true?

- The rate decreased by $1$ percentage point, a relative decrease of $33.\overline{3}\%$
- The rate decreased by $25$ percentage points
- The rate decreased by $1$ percentage point, a relative decrease of $25\%$
- The rate decreased by $1$ percentage point, a relative decrease of $1\%$

## Explanation

The difference between the two rates is $4 - 3 = 1$, so the rate fell by $1$ **percentage point**. The **percentage change**, measured relative to the original rate, is $\dfrac{3 - 4}{4} \cdot 100\% = -25\%$, so the rate decreased by $25\%$. Dividing by the final rate $3\%$ instead of the initial rate gives $33.\overline{3}\%$.
