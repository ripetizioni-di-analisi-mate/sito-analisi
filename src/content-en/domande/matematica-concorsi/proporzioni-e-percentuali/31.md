A merchant buys an item for $75$ euros and resells it at a profit equal to $20\%$ of the selling price. What is the selling price?

- $60$ euros
- $95$ euros
- $93.75$ euros
- $90$ euros

## Explanation

Profit is $20\%$ of the selling price $V$, so the cost of $75$ euros is the remaining $80\%$ of $V$. Thus $0.8\,V = 75$ and $V = \dfrac{75}{0.8} = 93.75$ euros. The amount $90$ euros represents a profit of $20\%$ on the purchase cost, which uses a different base from the one in the question.
