A bonus of $2700$ euros is divided among three employees in direct proportion to their years of service, $3$, $5$, and $10$. How much does the employee with $5$ years of service receive?

- $900$ euros
- $450$ euros
- $1500$ euros
- $750$ euros

## Explanation

For **direct proportional division**, divide the bonus by the sum $3 + 5 + 10 = 18$. Each part is worth $\dfrac{2700}{18} = 150$ euros. The employee with $5$ years of service receives $5$ parts, or $750$ euros. The other two receive $450$ and $1500$ euros. The value $900$ euros is what each employee would receive if the bonus were split equally.
