A town's population falls from $1250$ to $1000$. By what percentage has it decreased?

- $0.2\%$
- $25\%$
- $20\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

To find the **percentage change**, divide the change by the initial value. The population fell by $250$ out of $1250$, giving a decrease of $\dfrac{250}{1250} \cdot 100\% = 20\%$. Dividing by the final value $1000$ gives $25\%$. The remaining population is $80\%$ of the initial population.
