An item costs $240$ euros. One offer takes $50$ euros off, and the other gives a discount of $18\%$. How much more do you save with the better offer?

- $43.20$ euros
- $8$ euros
- $6.80$ euros
- $13.20$ euros

## Explanation

The **percentage discount** saves $240\cdot0.18=43.20$ euros. The fixed discount saves $50-43.20=6.80$ euros more.
