An item has a taxable amount of $200$ euros. A discount of $10\%$ applies to that amount, followed by VAT at $22\%$ on the discounted price. What is the final price?

- $234$ euros
- $219.60$ euros
- $180$ euros
- $224$ euros

## Explanation

The discount and VAT are two **successive percentage changes**, so multiply the factors $0.9$ and $1.22$. The final price is $200 \cdot 0.9 \cdot 1.22 = 180 \cdot 1.22 = 219.60$ euros. Adding the signed percentages as if the price increased by $12\%$ gives $224$ euros. The discounted price before VAT is $180$ euros.
