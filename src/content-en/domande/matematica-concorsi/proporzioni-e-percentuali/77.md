An error rate falls by two percentage points. The relative drop equals one quarter of the initial rate. What is the new rate?

- $4\%$
- $6\%$
- $8\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

If two points are one quarter of the initial rate, the initial rate was $4\cdot2=8\%$. Subtracting the two points leaves 6%.
