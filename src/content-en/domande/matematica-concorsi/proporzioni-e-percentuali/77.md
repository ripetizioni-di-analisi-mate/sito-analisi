The unemployment rate of a province is the percentage of unemployed people in the labour force. In one year the rate falls from $8\%$ to $6\%$, while the labour force grows from $200\,000$ to $220\,000$ people. By how many people does the number of unemployed fall?

- $4000$
- $2800$
- $5600$
- $4400$

## Explanation

At the start the unemployed are $8\%$ of $200\,000$, that is $0.08 \cdot 200\,000 = 16\,000$. At the end they are $6\%$ of $220\,000$, that is $0.06 \cdot 220\,000 = 13\,200$. Therefore the number of unemployed falls by $16\,000 - 13\,200 = 2800$. The value $4000$ applies the fall of $2$ **percentage points** to the initial labour force, as if it had not changed, and the value $4400$ applies it to the final one. The value $5600$ comes from swapping the two bases, that is from computing $8\%$ of $220\,000$ and $6\%$ of $200\,000$.
