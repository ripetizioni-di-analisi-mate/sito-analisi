A rate rises from $12\%$ to $15\%$. Which pair gives the increase in percentage points followed by the relative percentage increase?

- $25$ points and $3\%$
- $3$ points and $25\%$
- $3$ points and $20\%$
- $12$ points and $25\%$

## Explanation

The difference is $15-12=3$ **percentage points**. For the relative increase, divide this difference by the initial rate to get $3\div12=25\%$.
