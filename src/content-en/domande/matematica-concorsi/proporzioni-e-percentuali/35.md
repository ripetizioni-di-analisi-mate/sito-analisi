An item costs $305$ euros including VAT at $22\%$. If the rate fell to $10\%$ and the taxable amount stayed the same, what would the new price including VAT be?

- $268.40$ euros
- $293$ euros
- $335.50$ euros
- $275$ euros

## Explanation

**Removing VAT** gives a taxable amount of $\dfrac{305}{1.22} = 250$ euros. With VAT at $10\%$, the price becomes $250 \cdot 1.1 = 275$ euros. Subtracting $12\%$ directly from $305$ euros gives $268.40$ euros. Applying VAT at $10\%$ to a price that already includes VAT at $22\%$ gives $335.50$ euros.
