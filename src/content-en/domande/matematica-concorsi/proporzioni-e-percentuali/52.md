An amount of $260$ euros is divided between two branches in proportion to $3$ and $2$. How much does the first branch receive?

- $173.33$ euros
- $130$ euros
- $104$ euros
- $156$ euros

## Explanation

In this **proportional division**, $260$ euros corresponds to $3+2=5$ parts worth $52$ euros each. The first branch receives $3$ parts, or $156$ euros.
