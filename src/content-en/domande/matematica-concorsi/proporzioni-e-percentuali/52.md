Seven places are allocated to two sites in proportion to 60 and 40 registrations. Take the integer parts of the quotas first, then assign spare places to the largest remainders. How many places go to the smaller site?

- $2$
- $4$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The exact quotas are $4.2$ and $2.8$. One place remains after the first six. It goes to the smaller site because $0.8>0.2$.
