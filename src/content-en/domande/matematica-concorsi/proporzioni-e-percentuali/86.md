A price quote applies a discount of $20\%$ to the pretax list price and then adds VAT at $22\%$ to the discounted price. The total payable is $292.80$ euros. What was the pretax list price?

- $285$ euros
- $366$ euros
- $300$ euros
- $240$ euros

## Explanation

The **multipliers** are $0.80$ for the discount and $1.22$ for VAT. Their product is $0.976$, so the pretax list price is $292.80\div0.976=300$ euros.
