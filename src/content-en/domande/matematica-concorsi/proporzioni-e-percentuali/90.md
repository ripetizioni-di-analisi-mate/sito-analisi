A store pays $84$ euros for an item. It sets a list price so that even after a discount of $20\%$, its profit is $30\%$ of the actual selling price. What is the list price?

- $136.50$ euros
- $150$ euros
- $147$ euros
- $120$ euros

## Explanation

With a **margin of $30\%$** on the actual selling price, the cost of $84$ euros represents $70\%$ of that price. The actual selling price is $84\div0.70=120$ euros and is $80\%$ of the list price. The list price is therefore $120\div0.80=150$ euros.
