A store buys an item for $64$ euros and sells it for $80$ euros. What is its profit as a percentage of the selling price?

- $20\%$
- $16\%$
- $25\%$
- $80\%$

## Explanation

The profit is $80-64=16$ euros. For the **profit margin on the selling price**, the base is $80$ euros, so $16\div80=20\%$.
