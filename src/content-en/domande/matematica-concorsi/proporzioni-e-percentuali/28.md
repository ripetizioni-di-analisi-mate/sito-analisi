If $30\%$ of a positive number $A$ equals $45\%$ of a positive number $B$, then $A$ is what percentage of $B$?

- $66.\overline{6}\%$
- $150\%$
- $15\%$
- $115\%$

## Explanation

The equation $\dfrac{30}{100}\,A = \dfrac{45}{100}\,B$ gives $A = \dfrac{45}{30}\,B = 1.5\,B$. Thus $A$ is $150\%$ of $B$. The value $66.\overline{6}\%$ gives the percentage that $B$ represents of $A$, reversing the ratio. Adding the difference between $45$ and $30$ to $100\%$ gives $115\%$.
