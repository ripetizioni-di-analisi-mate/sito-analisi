In the proportion $\dfrac{3}{4} : x = 6 : 10$, what is $x$?

- $\dfrac{9}{20}$
- $\dfrac{20}{9}$
- $80$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

The missing term $x$ is a mean. By the **cross-product property**, the product of the means $6x$ equals the product of the extremes $\dfrac{3}{4} \cdot 10 = \dfrac{15}{2}$, so
$$
x = \frac{15}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{4} .
$$
Multiplying $\dfrac{3}{4}$ by the mean $6$ and dividing by the extreme $10$ gives $\dfrac{9}{20}$, reversing the roles of means and extremes.
