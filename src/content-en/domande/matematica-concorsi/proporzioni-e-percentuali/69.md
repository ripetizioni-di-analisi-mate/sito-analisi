An office completes $70\%$ of $80$ cases in March and $90\%$ of $120$ cases in April. What percentage of the two months' cases does it complete overall?

- $80\%$
- $88\%$
- $84\%$
- $82\%$

## Explanation

The **overall percentage** uses the counts for both months. The office completes $56+108=164$ out of $80+120=200$ cases, or $82\%$.
