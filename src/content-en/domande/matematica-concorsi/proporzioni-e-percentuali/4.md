In an association, the ratio of men to women is $5 : 3$. Another $10$ women join and no new men join, bringing the ratio to $5 : 4$. How many men belong to the association?

- $80$
- $40$
- $30$
- $50$

## Explanation

Let the numbers of men and women initially be $5k$ and $3k$, respectively, for an unknown $k$. After the new members join, $5k : (3k + 10) = 5 : 4$ holds, and the **cross-product property** gives $20k = 15k + 50$. Then $k = 10$, so there are $50$ men. To check, the number of women rises from $30$ to $40$, and $50 : 40 = 5 : 4$. The value $30$ is the initial number of women and $40$ is the number after the new members join.
