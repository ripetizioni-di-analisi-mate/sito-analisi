In a parking lot, the ratio of cars to motorcycles is $7 : 2$, and there are $126$ vehicles in all. How many motorcycles are there?

- $36$
- $98$
- $28$
- $14$

## Explanation

By the **addition property of proportions**, the ratio of all vehicles to motorcycles is $7 + 2 = 9$ to $2$, so there are $\dfrac{126 \cdot 2}{9} = 28$ motorcycles. This leaves $126 - 28 = 98$ cars. Dividing $126$ by $7$ instead of $9$ gives $36$ because it compares motorcycles with cars alone instead of with all the vehicles.
