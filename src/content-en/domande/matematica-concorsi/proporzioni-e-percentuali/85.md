Of $360$ participants, $45\%$ come from outside the municipality. During the morning, $60$ participants leave, including $42$ from outside the municipality. What percentage of the remaining participants are from outside the municipality?

- $40\%$
- $33.33\%$
- $45\%$
- $55\%$

## Explanation

Initially, $360\cdot0.45=162$ participants are from outside the municipality. After the departures, $162-42=120$ remain, and the **new total** is $360-60=300$. The required share is $120\div300=40\%$.
