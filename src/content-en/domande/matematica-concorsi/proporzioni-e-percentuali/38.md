A monthly fee rises from $400$ to $484$ euros after two successive increases by the same percentage, each applied to the fee then in effect. What is the percentage of each increase?

- $10\%$
- $10.5\%$
- $21\%$
- $11\%$

## Explanation

If each increase multiplies the fee by a factor $f$, the fee after two increases is $400 \cdot f^2 = 484$. Then $f^2 = \dfrac{484}{400} = 1.21$ and $f = 1.1$, so each increase is $10\%$. Dividing the overall increase of $21\%$ by $2$ gives $10.5\%$, as if successive percentages could be added.
