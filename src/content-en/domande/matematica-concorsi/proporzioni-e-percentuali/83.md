Ten identical printers can complete an order in $18$ hours. After $6$ hours, four printers stop and the others continue at the same rate. How many hours does it take to complete the order in all?

- $30$
- $26$
- $36$
- $24$

## Explanation

The order requires $10\cdot18=180$ **machine-hours**, of which $60$ have already been completed. With six printers running, the remaining $120$ machine-hours take $20$ hours, for a total of $26$ hours.
