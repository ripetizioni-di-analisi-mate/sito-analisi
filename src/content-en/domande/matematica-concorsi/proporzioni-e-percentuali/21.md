Two grandchildren divide an inheritance of $9000$ euros in inverse proportion to their annual incomes of $20\,000$ and $30\,000$ euros. How much does the grandchild with the lower income receive?

- $6000$ euros
- $5400$ euros
- $4500$ euros
- $3600$ euros

## Explanation

The incomes are in the ratio $2$ to $3$. Dividing the inheritance **in inverse proportion** to $2$ and $3$ is equivalent to dividing it in direct proportion to the reciprocals $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{6}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{2}{6}$, or in the ratio $3$ to $2$. Divide the inheritance into $5$ parts worth $1800$ euros each. The grandchild with the lower income receives $3$ parts, or $5400$ euros. The amount $3600$ euros is this grandchild's share under direct proportional division, which gives more to the person with the higher income.
