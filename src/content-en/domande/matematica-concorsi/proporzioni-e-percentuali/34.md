A merchant buys an item for $48$ euros and chooses a list price. After a discount of $20\%$ off the list price, the customer must pay $30\%$ more than the purchase cost. What should the list price be?

- $74.88$ euros
- $72$ euros
- $78$ euros
- $62.40$ euros

## Explanation

The customer must pay $48 \cdot 1.3 = 62.40$ euros. This is the list price $L$ times the discount multiplier $0.8$, so $L = \dfrac{62.40}{0.8} = 78$ euros. Increasing $62.40$ euros by $20\%$ gives $74.88$ euros because it applies the discount rate to the discounted price instead of the list price. Adding the two percentages gives $72$ euros.
