In a selection process, $36$ out of $240$ applications are incomplete. What percentage of the applications are incomplete?

- $24\%$
- $18\%$
- $12\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

To find the **share of the total**, divide $36$ by $240$ to get $0.15$. Multiplying by $100\%$ gives $15\%$.
