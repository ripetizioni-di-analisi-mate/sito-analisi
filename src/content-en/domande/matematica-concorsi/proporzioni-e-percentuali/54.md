In a selection process, $36$ out of $240$ applications are incomplete. What percentage of the applications are incomplete?

- $24\%$
- $18\%$
- $12\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

The **percentage** of incomplete applications is the ratio of the part to the total, multiplied by $100\%$. The ratio is $\dfrac{36}{240} = \dfrac{3}{20} = 0.15$, so $15\%$ of the applications are incomplete. The result can be checked mentally, because $10\%$ of $240$ is $24$, $5\%$ is $12$ and $24 + 12 = 36$.
