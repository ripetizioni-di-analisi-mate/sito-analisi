A published percentage is 25%, rounded to the nearest whole percent. Out of 80 people, how many answered yes if the percentage refers to the yes votes?

- $19$
- $21$
- $25$
- $20$

## Explanation

One person out of 80 represents $1.25$ percentage points. Twenty yes votes give exactly 25%, whereas 19 and 21 give $23.75\%$ and $26.25\%$.
