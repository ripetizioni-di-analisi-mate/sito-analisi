Three groups share a payment. They have $4$, $6$, and $5$ workers, respectively, and take $2$, $3$, and $5$ days, respectively, to complete the activity. Each share is directly proportional to the number of workers and inversely proportional to the number of days. How much does the third group receive out of $1\,150$ euros?

- $230$ euros
- $300$ euros
- $460$ euros
- $250$ euros

## Explanation

The **weights for the shares** are the numbers of workers divided by the days taken, giving $2,2,1$. The third group receives one of the five parts, or $1\,150\div5=230$ euros.
