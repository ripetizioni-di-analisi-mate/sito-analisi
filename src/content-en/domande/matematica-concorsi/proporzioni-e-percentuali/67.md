An amount of $510$ euros is divided among three teams in inverse proportion to $3$, $4$, and $6$. How much does the team associated with $4$ receive?

- $136$ euros
- $170$ euros
- $204$ euros
- $255$ euros

## Explanation

The **reciprocal weights** $\dfrac13,\dfrac14,\dfrac16$ are proportional to $4,3,2$. The team's weight is $3$ out of $9$ parts, so it receives $510\cdot\dfrac39=170$ euros.
