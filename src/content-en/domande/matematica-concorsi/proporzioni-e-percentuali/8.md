The positive numbers $x$, $y$, and $z$ satisfy $x : y = 3 : 4$ and $y : z = 6 : 5$, and their sum is $186$. What is $z$?

- $77.5$
- $72$
- $60$
- $54$

## Explanation

To compare the ratios, give the term corresponding to $y$ the same value in both. It is $4$ in the first and $6$ in the second. The least common multiple $12$ gives $x : y = 9 : 12$ and $y : z = 12 : 10$, so $x$, $y$, and $z$ are in the ratio $9$ to $12$ to $10$. Divide the sum $186$ into $31$ equal parts of $6$ each. Then $z = 10 \cdot 6 = 60$, while $x = 54$ and $y = 72$. The value $77.5$ reads the ratios as $3 : 4 : 5$ without matching the terms for $y$.
