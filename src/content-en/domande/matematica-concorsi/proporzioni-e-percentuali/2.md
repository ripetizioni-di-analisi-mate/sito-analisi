On an apartment floor plan, a hallway $6$ m long is drawn as a line segment $12$ cm long. What is the scale of the plan?

- $1 : 500$
- $1 : 50$
- $2 : 1$
- $1 : 5$

## Explanation

The **scale** is the ratio of a length on the plan to the actual length, both measured in the same unit. Since $6$ m $= 600$ cm, the ratio is $12 : 600$. Dividing both terms by $12$ gives $1 : 50$. The scale $2 : 1$ compares $12$ and $6$ without converting meters to centimeters. The scales $1 : 5$ and $1 : 500$ come from conversions that are off by a factor of $10$.
