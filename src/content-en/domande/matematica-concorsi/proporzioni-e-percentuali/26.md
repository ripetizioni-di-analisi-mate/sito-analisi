Which fraction equals $37.5\%$?

- $\dfrac{15}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{80}$

## Explanation

By definition, $37.5\% = \dfrac{37.5}{100}$. Multiplying the numerator and denominator by $2$ gives $\dfrac{75}{200} = \dfrac{3}{8}$. The same result follows from observing that $12.5\% = \dfrac{1}{8}$ and that $37.5\%$ is three times $12.5\%$. The fraction $\dfrac{5}{8}$ equals $62.5\%$, the complementary percentage.
