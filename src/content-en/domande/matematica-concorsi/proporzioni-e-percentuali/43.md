What is $12.5\%$ of $4800$?

- $6000$
- $576$
- $600$
- $60$

## Explanation

The percentage $12.5\%$ equals the fraction $\dfrac{1}{8}$, so $12.5\%$ of $4800$ is $\dfrac{4800}{8} = 600$. The value $576$ is $12\%$ of $4800$, which leaves out $0.5\%$. The values $60$ and $6000$ differ from the correct result by a factor of $10$.
