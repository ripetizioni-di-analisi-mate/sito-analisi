A warehouse contains $18$ large boxes and $42$ small boxes. What is the ratio of large boxes to all boxes, in simplest form?

- $3:10$
- $3:7$
- $10:3$
- $7:10$

## Explanation

There are $18+42=60$ boxes in all. The required **ratio** is $18:60$, which reduces to $3:10$ when both terms are divided by $6$.
