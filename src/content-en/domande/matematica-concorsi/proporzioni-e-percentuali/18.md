Three brothers aged $10$, $15$, and $20$ divide $1300$ euros in inverse proportion to their ages. How much more does the youngest receive than the oldest?

- $300$ euros
- $200$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $600$ euros

## Explanation

For **inverse proportional division**, use the reciprocals $\dfrac{1}{10}$, $\dfrac{1}{15}$, and $\dfrac{1}{20}$. With denominator $60$, their numerators are $6$, $4$, and $3$. Divide the total into $13$ equal parts of $100$ euros each. The shares are $600$, $400$, and $300$ euros, so the youngest receives $600 - 300 = 300$ euros more than the oldest. The amount $600$ euros is the youngest brother's share, while $200$ euros is the difference between his share and that of the $15$-year-old brother.
