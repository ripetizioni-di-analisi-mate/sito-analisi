What is the ratio of $45$ minutes to $2$ hours?

- $\dfrac{45}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{9}{40}$

## Explanation

To compare two durations, express them in the same unit. Since $2$ hours is $120$ minutes, the **ratio** is $\dfrac{45}{120}$. Dividing the numerator and denominator by $15$ gives $\dfrac{3}{8}$. The value $\dfrac{9}{40}$ counts $200$ minutes in $2$ hours as if an hour had $100$ minutes. The value $\dfrac{45}{2}$ compares the two numbers without converting hours to minutes.
