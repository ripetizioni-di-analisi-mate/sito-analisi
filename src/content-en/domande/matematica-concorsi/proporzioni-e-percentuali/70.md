The number of places available falls from $320$ to $272$. What is the percentage decrease?

- $17.65\%$
- $18\%$
- $48\%$
- $15\%$

## Explanation

The decrease is $320-272=48$ places. The **percentage change** uses the initial number as its base, giving $48\div320=0.15$, or $15\%$.
