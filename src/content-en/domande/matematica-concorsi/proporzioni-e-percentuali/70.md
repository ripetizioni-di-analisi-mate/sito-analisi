The price index of a product is $100$ in 2020 and $150$ in 2024. The base is changed and the 2024 value is set equal to $100$. What is the 2020 index on the new base?

- $50$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $150$
- $\dfrac{200}{3}$

## Explanation

On the new base each value is divided by the 2024 value and multiplied by $100$. For 2020 this gives $\dfrac{100}{150} \cdot 100 = \dfrac{200}{3}$, about $66.7$. The value $50$ is obtained by subtracting the $50$ points of increase from $100$, but on the new base those $50$ points are worth $\dfrac{50}{150} \cdot 100 = \dfrac{100}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is the ratio of the two indices without multiplying by $100$.
