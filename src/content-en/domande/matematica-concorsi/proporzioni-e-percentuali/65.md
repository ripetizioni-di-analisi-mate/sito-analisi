Packing $240$ packages in $5$ hours takes $8$ workers. How many workers, each working at the same rate, are needed to pack $360$ packages in $6$ hours?

- $9$
- $15$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

Dividing $240$ by $8\cdot5$ gives a **productivity rate** of $6$ packages per worker per hour. In $6$ hours, each worker packs $36$ packages, so packing $360$ takes $10$ workers.
