A selection process receives $300$ applications through channels A and B. The acceptance rate is $40\%$ for channel A and $70\%$ for channel B. A total of $174$ applications are accepted. How many applications came through B?

- $210$
- $150$
- $180$
- $120$

## Explanation

If all applications had channel A's **acceptance rate**, $120$ would be accepted. Each application assigned to B instead of A increases the count by $0.30$. To obtain $174-120=54$ additional acceptances, $54\div0.30=180$ applications must come through B.
