At a school of $200$ students, the relative frequency of commuters is $0.3$. At a second school of $300$ students, the relative frequency of commuters is $0.5$. What is the relative frequency of commuters among all $500$ students at the two schools?

- $0.4$
- $0.42$
- $0.8$
- $0.38$

## Explanation

There are $200 \cdot 0.3 = 60$ commuters at the first school and $300 \cdot 0.5 = 150$ at the second, for a total of $210$. The overall **relative frequency** is therefore $\dfrac{210}{500} = 0.42$. The value $0.4$ is the mean of the two relative frequencies, which overlooks the difference in enrollment between the schools. The value $0.38$ switches the school enrollments, and $0.8$ adds the two frequencies.
