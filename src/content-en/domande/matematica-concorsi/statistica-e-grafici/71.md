In a histogram, the area of each rectangle equals the absolute frequency of its interval. What is the height of the rectangle for the interval from 10 inclusive to 30 exclusive if its frequency is 30?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $2$
- $20$
- $30$

## Explanation

The interval width is $30-10=20$. The height is the frequency density, found by dividing the area of 30 by the base of 20.
