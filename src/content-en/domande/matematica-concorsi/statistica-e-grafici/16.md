At a small office, the five monthly salaries are $1500$, $4400$, $1400$, $1700$, and $1500$ euros. By how many euros does the mean salary exceed the median?

- $600$ euros
- $700$ euros
- $1125$ euros
- $1400$ euros

## Explanation

In order, the salaries are $1400$, $1500$, $1500$, $1700$, and $4400$ euros, so the **median** is the third value, $1500$ euros. The mean is $\dfrac{10\,500}{5} = 2100$ euros and therefore exceeds the median by $600$ euros. The difference is due to the outlying salary of $4400$ euros, which affects the mean but leaves the median unchanged. The value $700$ euros uses the middle value of the unsorted list, $1400$, as the median. The value $1125$ euros divides the sum by $4$ instead of $5$, and $1400$ euros replaces the mean with the mean of just the two extreme values.
