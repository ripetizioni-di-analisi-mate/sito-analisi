The volunteers at an association have a mean age of $20$ years. A new volunteer aged $50$ joins, bringing the mean to $22$ years. How many volunteers were there before the new volunteer joined?

- $15$
- $28$
- $14$
- $30$

## Explanation

Let $n$ be the original number of volunteers. By the relationship between the **sum of the values and the mean**, the ages add up to $20n$ before and $22(n + 1)$ after the new volunteer joins. The difference between the sums is the new volunteer's age:
$$
20n + 50 = 22(n + 1) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2n = 28 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n = 14 .
$$
The value $15$ is the number of volunteers after the new one joins. The value $28$ omits division by the $2$-year increase in the mean, and $30$ is the difference between the new volunteer's age and the old mean.
