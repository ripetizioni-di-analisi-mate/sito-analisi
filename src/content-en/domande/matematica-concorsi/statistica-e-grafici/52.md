An office receives 18 applications by Monday, another 12 on Tuesday, and another 15 on Wednesday. What is the cumulative frequency through Tuesday?

- $30$
- $12$
- $27$
- $45$

## Explanation

The cumulative frequency through a given date counts all applications received up to that point. By the end of Tuesday the total is $18+12=30$.
