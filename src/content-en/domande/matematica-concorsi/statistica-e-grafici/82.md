In a course, 80 participants take the written test and 70 take the practical test. Of those who take both tests, 15 pass the written test and 25 do not. How many participants take exactly one test?

- $80$
- $40$
- $70$
- $110$

## Explanation

The intersection of the two groups contains $15+25=40$ participants. The sum $80+70$ counts them twice, so subtract $2\cdot40$ to count those who take exactly one test.
