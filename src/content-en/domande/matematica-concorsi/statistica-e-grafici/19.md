Which relationship between the mode, median, and arithmetic mean holds for $7$, $2$, $1$, $9$, $2$, $6$, $2$, and $3$?

- Mode $=$ median $<$ mean
- Median $<$ mode $<$ mean
- Mode $<$ mean $<$ median
- Mode $<$ median $<$ mean

## Explanation

In increasing order, the data are $1$, $2$, $2$, $2$, $3$, $6$, $7$, and $9$. The value $2$ occurs three times and each other value occurs once, so the **mode** is $2$. There are $8$ values, an even number, so the **median** is the mean of the fourth and fifth values, $\dfrac{2 + 3}{2} = 2.5$. The data sum to $32$, so the mean is $\dfrac{32}{8} = 4$, and $2 < 2.5 < 4$ holds. The choice with equal mode and median uses only the fourth observation as the median. The choice with the median below the mode uses the frequency $3$ as the mode instead of the data value $2$. The choice with the mean below the median finds the median in the unsorted list, $\dfrac{9 + 2}{2} = 5.5$.
