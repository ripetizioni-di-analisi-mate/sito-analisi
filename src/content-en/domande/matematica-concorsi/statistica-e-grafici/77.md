In a list, 12 appears 4 times, 15 appears 5 times, and 18 appears 3 times. One more 12 is added. Which statement about the mode is correct?

- The only mode is 15.
- The only mode is 12.
- The modes are 12 and 15.
- The modes are 15 and 18.

## Explanation

The frequency of 12 rises to 5. Both 12 and 15 have the highest frequency, so both are modes.
