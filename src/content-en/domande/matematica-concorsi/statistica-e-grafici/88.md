Office A handles 120 cases. In its pie chart, the sector for urgent cases measures $72^\circ$. Office B handles 80 cases, and its sector for urgent cases measures $126^\circ$. What percentage of the cases at the two offices combined are urgent?

- 55%
- 25%
- $27.5\%$
- 26%

## Explanation

The sectors correspond to 20% of 120 and 35% of 80, or 24 and 28 cases. Of the 200 cases overall, 52 are urgent, or 26%.
