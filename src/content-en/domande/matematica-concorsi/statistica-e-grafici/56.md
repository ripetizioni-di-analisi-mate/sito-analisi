An archive contains 32 signed paper records, 18 unsigned paper records, 46 signed digital records, and 14 unsigned digital records. How many signed records does it contain?

- $64$
- $50$
- $60$
- $78$

## Explanation

Both paper and digital records count toward the total number of signed records. The two frequencies add up to 78.
