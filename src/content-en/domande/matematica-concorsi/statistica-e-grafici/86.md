The overall mean score on a test taken by 50 participants is 72. Group A has a mean of 78 and group B a mean of 68. How many participants are in group A?

- $25$
- $15$
- $20$
- $30$

## Explanation

If everyone scored the mean for group B, the total would be $50\cdot68$. Each participant in A adds 10 points to this baseline on average. The required increase is $50(72-68)=200$, so group A has 20 participants.
