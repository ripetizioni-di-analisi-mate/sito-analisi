Start with the five values $12$, $15$, $18$, $21$, and $24$, then add the two observations $2$ and $48$. What are the arithmetic mean and median of the resulting seven values?

- Mean $18$ and median $18$
- Mean $20$ and median $21$
- Mean $20$ and median $18$
- Mean $28$ and median $18$

## Explanation

The five original values add up to $90$. Adding the two new values brings the sum to $90 + 2 + 48 = 140$, so the **mean** of the seven values is $\dfrac{140}{7} = 20$. In increasing order, they are $2$, $12$, $15$, $18$, $21$, $24$, and $48$, so the **median** is the fourth value, $18$. Adding one value below and one above the median leaves the median unchanged here. The mean changes because the two new values add up to $50$ instead of $2 \cdot 18 = 36$. The answer with mean $18$ assumes the two new values balance each other. The answer with median $21$ uses the fourth value in the unsorted list, and the answer with mean $28$ divides the sum by $5$ instead of $7$.
