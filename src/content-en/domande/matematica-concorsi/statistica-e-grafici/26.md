A data set has mean $15$ and median $12$. Every value is decreased by $20\%$. What are the new mean and median?

- Mean $12$ and median $12$
- Mean $12$ and median $9$
- Mean $12$ and median $9.6$
- Mean $3$ and median $2.4$

## Explanation

Decreasing a value by $20\%$ means multiplying it by $0.8$. By the rule for the **mean of transformed data**, the mean is also multiplied by $0.8$ and becomes $15 \cdot 0.8 = 12$. Since $0.8$ is positive, multiplication preserves the order of the data. The middle observations retain their positions, so the median is also multiplied by $0.8$ and becomes $12 \cdot 0.8 = 9.6$. The answer with median $9$ subtracts from both measures the $3$ points equal to $20\%$ of the mean. The answer with median $12$ leaves the median unchanged. The answer with mean $3$ calculates only $20\%$ of each measure.
