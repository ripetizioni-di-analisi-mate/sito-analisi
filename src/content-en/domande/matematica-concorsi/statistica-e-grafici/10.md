At a company, $50\%$ of the employees earn $1500$ euros per month, $30\%$ earn $1800$ euros, and the remaining $20\%$ earn $2400$ euros. What is the employees' mean monthly salary?

- $1900$ euros
- $2040$ euros
- $1770$ euros
- $1800$ euros

## Explanation

The percentages are the weights in the **weighted mean**, and they add up to $100\%$. The mean salary is therefore
$$
1500 \cdot 0.5 + 1800 \cdot 0.3 + 2400 \cdot 0.2 = 750 + 540 + 480 = 1770 \text{ euros}.
$$
Compared with the unweighted mean of the three salaries, the weighted mean is closer to $1500$ euros, the salary with the greatest weight. The value $1900$ euros is the unweighted mean of the three salaries and treats the three groups as if they had equal numbers of employees. The value $2040$ euros assigns the percentages to the salaries in reverse order, and $1800$ euros is the middle of the three salary levels.
