A correction subtracts 3 from every value in a dataset with a mean of 17 and a range of 12. What are the new mean and range?

- Mean 17 and range 9
- Mean 14 and range 12
- Mean 14 and range 9
- Mean 20 and range 12

## Explanation

Every value decreases by 3, so the mean also decreases by 3. The maximum and minimum decrease by the same amount, so their difference stays the same.
