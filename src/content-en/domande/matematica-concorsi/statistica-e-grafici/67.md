The share of cases completed by the deadline rises from 40% to 50%. What is the relative increase compared with the initial share?

- 10%
- 50%
- 20%
- 25%

## Explanation

The difference is 10 percentage points. Relative to the initial value, the difference is $10/40=0.25$, a relative increase of 25%.
