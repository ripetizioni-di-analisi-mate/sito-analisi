Each of the $50$ students enrolled in a language course was asked how many foreign languages they spoke. The table shows the cumulative frequencies of their responses.

| Foreign languages | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cumulative frequency | $8$ | $27$ | $41$ | $50$ |

How many students speak exactly $2$ foreign languages?

- $41$
- $23$
- $9$
- $14$

## Explanation

The **cumulative frequency** for the value $2$ counts the students who speak *at most* $2$ foreign languages, or $41$ students. The cumulative frequency for $1$ counts those who speak at most $1$, or $27$ students. The absolute frequency for $2$ is therefore the difference $41 - 27 = 14$. The value $41$ treats the cumulative frequency as an absolute frequency, $23 = 50 - 27$ counts the students speaking *at least* $2$ languages, and $9 = 50 - 41$ counts those speaking $3$.
