The first four values in an ordered list of eight integers are 4, 6, 9, and 11. The sixth value is 16 and the median is 13. What is the fifth value?

- $15$
- $13$
- $12$
- $14$

## Explanation

With eight values, the median is the mean of the fourth and fifth values. Their sum is 26, so the fifth value is $26-11=15$, which fits the ordering.
