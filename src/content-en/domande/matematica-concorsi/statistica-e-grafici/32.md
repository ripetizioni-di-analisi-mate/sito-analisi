A two-way table classifies $135$ museum visitors by residency and by whether they paid full or reduced admission. Some cells are illegible and have been left blank.

| | Full price | Reduced price | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residents | $30$ | | $80$ |
| Nonresidents | | $21$ | |
| Total | $64$ | | $135$ |

How many nonresidents paid full price?

- $34$
- $55$
- $43$
- $94$

## Explanation

Each total in a **two-way table** is the sum of the cells in its row or column. In the full-price column, the total is $64$ and $30$ visitors are residents, so $64 - 30 = 34$ nonresidents paid full price. The rows give the same result. There are $135 - 80 = 55$ nonresidents and $21$ paid reduced admission, leaving $55 - 21 = 34$ who paid full price. The value $55$ is the total number of nonresidents. The value $43 = 64 - 21$ subtracts a cell from a different column, and $94 = 64 + 30$ adds the residents' cell to the total instead of subtracting it.
