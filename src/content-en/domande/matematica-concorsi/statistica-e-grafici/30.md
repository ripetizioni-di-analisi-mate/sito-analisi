Which group of four observations has the smallest standard deviation?

- $19$, $19$, $19$, $23$
- $15$, $15$, $25$, $25$
- $18$, $19$, $21$, $22$
- $10$, $20$, $20$, $30$

## Explanation

All four groups have mean $20$, so compare their sums of squared deviations from $20$. For $18$, $19$, $21$, and $22$, the sum is $4 + 1 + 1 + 4 = 10$. For $19$, $19$, $19$, and $23$, it is $1 + 1 + 1 + 9 = 12$. For $15$, $15$, $25$, and $25$, it is $100$, and for $10$, $20$, $20$, and $30$, it is $200$. Each group has the same number of observations, so the group $18$, $19$, $21$, and $22$ has the smallest variance and hence the smallest **standard deviation**, with $\sigma^2 = \dfrac{10}{4} = 2.5$. The group $19$, $19$, $19$, and $23$ has the same range, $4$, so the range cannot distinguish the two groups.
