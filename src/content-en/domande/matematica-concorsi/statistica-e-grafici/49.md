Six users had wait times of 18, 7, 12, 3, 9, and 14 minutes. What is the median?

- $10.5$ minutes
- $9$ minutes
- $11$ minutes
- $12$ minutes

## Explanation

The wait times in ascending order are 3, 7, 9, 12, 14, and 18. The two middle values are 9 and 12, and their mean is $10.5$.
