At a company, the relative frequency of employees who work remotely is $0.25$, and $60$ employees work remotely. How many employees does the company have in total?

- $180$
- $75$
- $240$
- $85$

## Explanation

The **relative frequency** is the absolute frequency divided by the total number $N$ of employees, so $\dfrac{60}{N} = 0.25$. It follows that $N = \dfrac{60}{0.25} = 240$. The value $180$ is the number of employees who do not work remotely, namely $240 - 60$, while $75$ comes from increasing $60$ by $25\%$.
