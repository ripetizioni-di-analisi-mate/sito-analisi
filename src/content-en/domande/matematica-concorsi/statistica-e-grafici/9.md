In a survey, $250$ people each selected one means of transportation they use to get to work. The percentage frequencies are $44\%$ for driving, $28\%$ for taking the bus, and $12\%$ for walking. Everyone else rides a bicycle. How many people ride a bicycle?

- $16$
- $40$
- $70$
- $210$

## Explanation

Each person selects just one means of transportation, so the **percentage frequencies** sum to $100\%$, and the percentage frequency for cycling is $100\% - 44\% - 28\% - 12\% = 16\%$. The number of people who ride bicycles is therefore $16\%$ of $250$, or $250 \cdot 0.16 = 40$. The value $16$ treats the percentage as a head count. The value $70$ overlooks those who walk, and $210$ is the number who do not ride bicycles.
