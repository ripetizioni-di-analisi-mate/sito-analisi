Twelve measurements have a mean of 18. Two measurements, 9 and 21, are removed and three new measurements with a mean of 22 are added. What is the final mean?

- $21$
- $\dfrac{252}{13}$
- $\dfrac{39}{2}$
- $19$

## Explanation

The sum changes from 216 to $216-30+66=252$. The number of measurements changes from 12 to 13, so the final mean is $252/13$.
