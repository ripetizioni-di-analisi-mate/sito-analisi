In one month, a store sold $100$ packages of a product at $18$ euros each, $200$ packages at $24$ euros each, and $300$ packages at $30$ euros each. What was the mean selling price per package?

- $24$ euros
- $22$ euros
- $27$ euros
- $26$ euros

## Explanation

The mean price is the **weighted mean** of the three prices, with the numbers of packages sold as the weights:
$$
\frac{18 \cdot 100 + 24 \cdot 200 + 30 \cdot 300}{600} = \frac{15\,600}{600} = 26 \text{ euros}.
$$
The value $24$ euros is the unweighted mean of the three prices, and $22$ euros assigns the numbers of packages to the prices in reverse order. The value $27$ euros is the median of the $600$ selling prices, since in order of price the three-hundredth package costs $24$ euros and the next costs $30$ euros.
