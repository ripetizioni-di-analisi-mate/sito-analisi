Of the 35 documents in a file, 14 are signed. Five signed documents are added. What is the new relative frequency of signed documents, expressed as a percentage?

- $40\%$
- $54.3\%$
- $47.5\%$
- $45\%$

## Explanation

There are now $14+5=19$ signed documents out of $35+5=40$ documents. Their share is $19/40=0.475$, or $47.5\%$.
