On a competitive exam, the final score is the weighted mean of the scores on two written tests and one oral test. The oral test carries twice the weight of each written test. A candidate scores $5$ and $7$ on the written tests and $9$ on the oral test. What is the final score?

- $7.5$
- $7$
- $10$
- $8$

## Explanation

Assign a weight of $1$ to each written test and $2$ to the oral test. The weights therefore sum to $4$. The **weighted mean** is
$$
\frac{5 \cdot 1 + 7 \cdot 1 + 9 \cdot 2}{4} = \frac{30}{4} = 7.5 .
$$
The value $7$ is the arithmetic mean of the three scores, which gives all three tests the same weight. The value $10$ divides $30$ by the number of tests instead of the sum of the weights. The value $8$ replaces the two written scores with their mean $6$ and gives it a weight of $1$, although the two written tests together have weight $2$, equal to the oral test.
