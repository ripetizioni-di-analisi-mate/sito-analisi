A bar chart shows weekly sales of four products at a store. The table gives the underlying data.

| product | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| units sold | $120$ | $80$ | $150$ | $50$ |

By what percentage do sales of product C exceed those of product A?

- $20\%$
- $30\%$
- $125\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

Sales of C exceed sales of A by $150 - 120 = 30$ units. The base for the **percent change** is sales of A, the quantity used for comparison, so the answer is $\dfrac{30}{120} = 25\%$. The value $20\%$ uses sales of C as the base, giving $\dfrac{30}{150}$. The value $30\%$ treats the difference in units as a percentage, and $125\%$ treats the ratio $\dfrac{150}{120}$ as the percent increase.
