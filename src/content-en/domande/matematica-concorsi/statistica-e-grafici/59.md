The wait-time intervals are $0\le t<5$, $5\le t<10$, and $10\le t<15$ minutes. Which interval contains a wait of 10 minutes?

- $10\le t<15$
- $0\le t<5$
- None of the intervals
- $5\le t<10$

## Explanation

Each interval excludes its upper endpoint and includes its lower endpoint. Thus, 10 is the first value included in the third interval.
