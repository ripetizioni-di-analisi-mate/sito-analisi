Two lists each have variance zero. The first contains only 2s and the second only 8s, in equal numbers. What is the combined variance using the total number of observations as divisor?

- $0$
- $3$
- $18$
- $9$

## Explanation

The combined mean is 5. Every deviation is $-3$ or $+3$, so each squared deviation is 9.
