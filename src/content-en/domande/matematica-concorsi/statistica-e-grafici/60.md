One list contains $n$ values all equal to $2$ and a second list contains $n$ values all equal to $8$. The two lists are combined into a single list of $2n$ values. What is the variance of the combined list, computed by dividing by the number of data?

- $0$
- $3$
- $36$
- $9$

## Explanation

Half of the values of the combined list equal $2$ and half equal $8$, so its mean is $\dfrac{2 + 8}{2} = 5$. Every value is $3$ away from the mean, so every squared deviation equals $9$. The **variance** is the mean of the squared deviations, so it equals $9$. The value $0$ is the mean of the variances of the two lists, which are zero, the value $3$ is the standard deviation and the value $36$ is the square of the difference $8 - 2$.
