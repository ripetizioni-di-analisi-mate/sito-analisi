A set of times has a standard deviation of $2.5$ minutes. What is the standard deviation when all the times are expressed in seconds?

- $25$ seconds
- $150$ seconds
- $60$ seconds
- $9000$ seconds

## Explanation

Changing units multiplies every time and every deviation from the mean by 60. The standard deviation therefore becomes $2.5\cdot60=150$ seconds.
