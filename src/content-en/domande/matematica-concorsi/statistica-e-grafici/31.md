The deviations of five observations from their mean are $-3$, $1$, $x$, $4$, and $-1$. What is $x$?

- $1$
- $0$
- $9$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The **sum of the deviations** from the mean is always zero because the sum of the values equals their number times their mean. Thus $-3 + 1 + x + 4 - 1 = 0$, or $x + 1 = 0$, so $x = -1$. The value $1$ has the wrong sign, and $9$ is the sum of the absolute values of the known deviations.
