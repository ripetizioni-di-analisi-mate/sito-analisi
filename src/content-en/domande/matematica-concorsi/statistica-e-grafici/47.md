Of the 48 responses in a table, 17 are “yes” and 9 are “I don't know.” The rest are “no.” What is the absolute frequency of “no”?

- $31$
- $22$
- $26$
- $17$

## Explanation

The three categories account for all 48 responses. Subtract the 26 responses already accounted for to get 22.
