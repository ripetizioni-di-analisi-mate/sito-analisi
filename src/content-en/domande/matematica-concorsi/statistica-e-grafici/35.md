Among the candidates taking a competitive exam, $40\%$ have a college degree. Of the candidates with a degree, $30\%$ pass, as do $15\%$ of those without a degree. What percentage of all candidates pass?

- $22.5\%$
- $45\%$
- $12\%$
- $21\%$

## Explanation

Out of $100$ candidates, $40$ have a degree and $60$ do not. Among those with a degree, $30\%$ pass, or $12$ candidates. Among those without a degree, $15\%$ pass, or $9$ candidates. Thus $21$ out of $100$ candidates pass, or $21\%$. The result is the **weighted mean** of the two percentages with weights $40\%$ and $60\%$. The value $22.5\%$ is the simple mean of $30\%$ and $15\%$, $45\%$ is their sum, and $12\%$ counts only the candidates with degrees who pass.
