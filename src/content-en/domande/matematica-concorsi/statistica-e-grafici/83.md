In a histogram where area equals absolute frequency, the intervals $0\le x<5$, $5\le x<15$, and $15\le x<35$ have heights of 6, 4, and 2. What fraction of the observations fall in the last interval?

- $\dfrac{3}{11}$
- $\dfrac{4}{11}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

Multiplying width by height gives frequencies of 30, 40, and 40. The last interval therefore contains 40 of the 110 observations, a fraction of $4/11$.
