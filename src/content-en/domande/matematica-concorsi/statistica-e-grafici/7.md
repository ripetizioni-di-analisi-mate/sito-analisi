In a mountain town, the daily low temperatures on five days were $-4$ °C, $-3$ °C, $2$ °C, $0$ °C, and $0$ °C. What was the mean low temperature?

- $0$ °C
- $-1.25$ °C
- $1.8$ °C
- $-1$ °C

## Explanation

The temperatures add up to $-4 - 3 + 2 + 0 + 0 = -5$, and there are $5$ observations, so the **arithmetic mean** is $\dfrac{-5}{5} = -1$ °C. Both days with a temperature of $0$ °C count as observations. The value $-1.25$ °C comes from dividing by $4$. The value $1.8$ °C is the mean of the absolute values of the temperatures, and $0$ °C is the median.
