Five branches handled a mean of 24 requests each. Four of them handled 17, 31, 20, and 29 requests, respectively. How many did the fifth branch handle?

- $21$
- $27$
- $23$
- $19$

## Explanation

The mean gives a total of $5\cdot24=120$ requests. Subtract the 97 handled by the four branches to find that the fifth handled 23.
