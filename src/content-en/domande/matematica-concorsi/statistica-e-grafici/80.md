A percentage calculated for a group of 80 people is reported as 36%, rounded to the nearest percentage point. How many people does the reported percentage represent?

- $29$
- $36$
- $28$
- $30$

## Explanation

With 29 people, the percentage is $29/80=36.25\%$, which rounds to 36%. The counts immediately below and above 29 give 35% and $37.5\%$, both outside the rounding interval.
