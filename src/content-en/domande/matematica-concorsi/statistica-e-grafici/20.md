A student has taken $4$ exams with a mean grade of $24$. What grade is needed on the fifth exam for a mean of $25$ across all five exams?

- $29$
- $25$
- $26$
- $28$

## Explanation

By the relationship between the **sum of the values and the mean**, the four grades add up to $4 \cdot 24 = 96$, and the five grades must add up to $5 \cdot 25 = 125$. The fifth grade must therefore be $125 - 96 = 29$. The four grades are a total of $4$ points short of four grades of $25$, so the fifth grade must exceed $25$ by $4$ points. The value $28$ adds the $4$ points to the old mean $24$ instead of the new mean, and $25$ is the target mean. The value $26$ treats the old mean as a single observation, since $\dfrac{24 + 26}{2} = 25$.
