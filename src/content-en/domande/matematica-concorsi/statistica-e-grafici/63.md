The mean of ten recorded times was 35 minutes. Two times recorded as 28 and 44 were actually 33 and 36. What is the corrected mean?

- $34.2$ minutes
- $34.7$ minutes
- $34.4$ minutes
- $35.3$ minutes

## Explanation

The corrections add 5 minutes to the original sum and subtract 8. The sum changes from 350 to 347, so the new mean is $347/10=34.7$ minutes.
