An employee takes 12 minutes each to review 4 cases and 7 minutes each to review 6 more. What is the mean time per case?

- $10$
- $9$
- $9.5$
- $8$

## Explanation

The first four cases take 48 minutes and the other six take 42. The total is 90 minutes for 10 cases, or 9 minutes per case.
