The data are $3$, $5$, $5$, $6$, $8$, and $9$. One more observation, $8$, is added. Which statement about the resulting seven values is true?

- The only mode is $5$ and the median is $6$
- The modes are $5$ and $8$, and the median is $6$
- The modes are $5$ and $8$, and the median is $5.5$
- The only mode is $8$ and the median is $6$

## Explanation

With the new observation included, the seven values in order are $3$, $5$, $5$, $6$, $8$, $8$, and $9$. The values $5$ and $8$ each occur twice and no value occurs more often, so there are two **modes**, $5$ and $8$. With $7$ observations, the median is the fourth value, $6$. The value $5.5$ is the median of the original six observations. Answers with a single mode overlook that, after the addition, $5$ and $8$ share the highest frequency.
