The table shows delivery times for $80$ orders, grouped into $2$-day intervals. Each interval includes its lower endpoint and excludes its upper endpoint.

| days | $0$ to $2$ | $2$ to $4$ | $4$ to $6$ | $6$ to $8$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| orders | $30$ | $22$ | $18$ | $10$ |

Which interval contains the median delivery time?

- From $0$ to $2$ days
- From $2$ to $4$ days
- From $4$ to $6$ days
- From $6$ to $8$ days

## Explanation

There are $80$ orders, so the **median** is the mean of the 40th and 41st ordered values. The cumulative frequencies are $30$, $52$, $70$, and $80$, so the 31st through 52nd values fall in the interval from $2$ to $4$ days. Both the 40th and 41st values fall in this interval, so their mean, the median, also falls in the interval from $2$ to $4$ days. The interval from $0$ to $2$ days has the highest frequency, so it is the modal interval.
