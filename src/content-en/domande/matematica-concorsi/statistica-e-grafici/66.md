Five times, in ascending order, are 6, 9, 14, 18, and 25 minutes. Two times, 2 and 40 minutes, are added. What is the new median?

- $14$
- $18$
- $16$
- $9$

## Explanation

The new ordered list has one additional time at each end. The middle value is the fourth of the seven times and remains 14 minutes.
