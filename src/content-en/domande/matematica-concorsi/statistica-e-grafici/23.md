The mean of $20$ numbers is $15$. Ten of the numbers are increased by $3$, and the other ten stay the same. What is the new mean of the $20$ numbers?

- $18$
- $15.15$
- $15.5$
- $16.5$

## Explanation

By the relationship between the **sum of the values and the mean**, the $20$ numbers add up to $20 \cdot 15 = 300$. Each of the ten numbers is increased by $3$, so the sum increases by $10 \cdot 3 = 30$ and becomes $330$. The new mean is therefore $\dfrac{330}{20} = 16.5$. The value $18$ adds $3$ to the mean as if all the numbers had increased by $3$. The value $15.15$ adds $3$ to the sum only once, and $15.5$ adds $10$ to the sum instead of $30$.
