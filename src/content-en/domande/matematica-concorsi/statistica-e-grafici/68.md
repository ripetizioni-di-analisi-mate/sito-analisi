Of 40 internal candidates, 30 pass a test. Of 60 external candidates, 36 pass. Among the candidates who passed, what fraction are external?

- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{6}{11}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{11}$

## Explanation

The condition restricts the total to the $30+36=66$ candidates who passed. Of those, 36 are external, giving a fraction of $36/66=6/11$.
