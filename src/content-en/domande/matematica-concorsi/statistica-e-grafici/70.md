A table gives cumulative frequencies of 18, 47, and 65 for times strictly below 10, 20, and 30 minutes, respectively. How many times are at least 10 minutes and strictly below 20 minutes?

- $65$
- $47$
- $29$
- $18$

## Explanation

The frequency below 20 minutes includes the 18 times below 10 minutes. Subtract them to get $47-18=29$.
