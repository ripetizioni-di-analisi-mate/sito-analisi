At a company, the mean salary is $2100$ euros for men, $1900$ euros for women, and $1980$ euros for all employees. There are $30$ women. How many men are there?

- $20$
- $45$
- $50$
- $30$

## Explanation

Let $u$ be the number of men. By the relationship between the **sum of the values and the mean**, the total of the salaries is $2100u + 1900 \cdot 30$. It must equal the number of employees times the overall mean, or $1980(u + 30)$:
$$
2100u + 57\,000 = 1980u + 59\,400 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 120u = 2400 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad u = 20 .
$$
As a check, the overall mean is closer to the women's mean, so women form the larger group. The value $45$ reverses the ratio of the sizes of the two groups. The value $50$ is the total number of employees, and $30$ corresponds to two equal groups, whose overall mean would be $2000$ euros.
