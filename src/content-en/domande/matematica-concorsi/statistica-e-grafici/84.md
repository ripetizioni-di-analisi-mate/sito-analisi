Five values have a mean of 4, and the sum of their squares is 105. What is the variance when the divisor is the number of values?

- $16$
- $5$
- $4$
- $21$

## Explanation

The mean of the squares is $105/5=21$. The variance is the mean of the squares minus the square of the mean, giving $21-4^2=5$.
