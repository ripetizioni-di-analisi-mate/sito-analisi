The table shows the number of days each of the $40$ employees at an office was absent in one month.

| Days absent | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Employees | $12$ | $10$ | $8$ | $6$ | $4$ |

How many employees were absent for *at most* $2$ days?

- $8$
- $30$
- $18$
- $22$

## Explanation

Employees absent for at most $2$ days were absent for $0$, $1$, or $2$ days. Their number is therefore the **cumulative frequency** for the value $2$, or $12 + 10 + 8 = 30$. The value $8$ counts only the employees absent for exactly $2$ days. The value $22$ excludes those absent for $2$ days, and $18$ counts those absent for *at least* $2$ days.
