The table shows the ages of $40$ participants in a training course, grouped into $10$-year intervals. Each interval includes its lower endpoint and excludes its upper endpoint.

| age (years) | $20$ to $30$ | $30$ to $40$ | $40$ to $50$ | $50$ to $60$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| participants | $6$ | $14$ | $12$ | $8$ |

Using the midpoint of each interval to represent its ages, what is the approximate mean age of the participants?

- $40.5$ years
- $40$ years
- $35.5$ years
- $45.5$ years

## Explanation

The interval midpoints are $25$, $35$, $45$, and $55$ years. Weight each midpoint by its interval's frequency to find the **weighted mean**:
$$
\frac{25 \cdot 6 + 35 \cdot 14 + 45 \cdot 12 + 55 \cdot 8}{40} = \frac{1620}{40} = 40.5 \text{ years}.
$$
The value $40$ years is the mean of the four midpoints without weighting by frequency. The values $35.5$ and $45.5$ years use the lower and upper endpoints, respectively, in place of the midpoints.
