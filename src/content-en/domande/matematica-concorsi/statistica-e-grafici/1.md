The table shows the applications reviewed in one month by four offices of an agency.

| Office | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Applications reviewed | $80$ | $120$ | $50$ | $160$ |
| Applications rejected | $12$ | $15$ | $9$ | $20$ |

Which office has the highest relative frequency of rejected applications?

- Office C
- Office A
- Office B
- Office D

## Explanation

The **relative frequency** of rejected applications is the number rejected divided by the number reviewed by the same office. The ratios are $\dfrac{12}{80} = 0.15$ for A, $\dfrac{15}{120} = 0.125$ for B, $\dfrac{9}{50} = 0.18$ for C, and $\dfrac{20}{160} = 0.125$ for D, so office C has the highest relative frequency. Office D rejected the most applications, but it also reviewed more than any other office, so its relative frequency is among the lowest.
