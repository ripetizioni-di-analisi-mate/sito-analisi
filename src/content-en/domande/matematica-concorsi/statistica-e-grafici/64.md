A batch consists of 16 items with a mean mass of 3 kg and a second group with a mean mass of 5 kg. If the mean for the entire batch is 4 kg, how many items are in the second group?

- $8$
- $12$
- $16$
- $24$

## Explanation

On average, an item in the first group is one kilogram below the batch mean. An item in the second group is one kilogram above it on average, so the second group needs 16 items to balance the 16 in the first.
