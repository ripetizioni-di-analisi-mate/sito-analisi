Of the digital applications, 30% require additional information. Among paper applications, the share is 50%. Digital applications make up 60% of the total. What percentage of all applications require additional information?

- 38%
- 40%
- 48%
- 42%

## Explanation

The digital applications requiring additional information account for 30% of 60% of the total, or 18% of the total. The paper applications requiring additional information account for 50% of the remaining 40%, or 20% of the total. Together the shares add up to 38%.
