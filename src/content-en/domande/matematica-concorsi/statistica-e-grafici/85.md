A dataset has a mean of 12 and a variance of 9. Each value is transformed using the rule $y=20-2x$. What is the standard deviation of the transformed values?

- $3$
- $18$
- $9$
- $6$

## Explanation

Subtracting twice $x$ from a constant multiplies each deviation from the mean by $-2$. The standard deviation is multiplied by 2, increasing from $\sqrt{9}=3$ to 6.
