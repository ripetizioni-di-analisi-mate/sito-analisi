A pie chart has four sectors. Three of the sectors measure $72°$, $108°$, and $45°$. What percentage of the total does the fourth sector represent?

- $135\%$
- $62.5\%$
- $37.5\%$
- $13.5\%$

## Explanation

The sector angles in a **pie chart** add up to $360°$, so the fourth sector measures $360° - 72° - 108° - 45° = 135°$. Each percentage point corresponds to $3.6°$, so the fourth sector represents $\dfrac{135}{360} = 37.5\%$ of the total. The value $62.5\%$ is the percentage represented by the three known sectors. The value $135\%$ treats the angle as a percentage, and $13.5\%$ also introduces an error by a factor of $10$.
