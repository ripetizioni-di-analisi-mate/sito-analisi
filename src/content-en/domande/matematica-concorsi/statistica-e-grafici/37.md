A town spends $35\%$ of its budget on social services. In a pie chart of the budget, what is the angle of the social services sector?

- $35°$
- $126°$
- $12.6°$
- $234°$

## Explanation

In a **pie chart**, the full angle of $360°$ represents $100\%$, so each percentage point corresponds to $3.6°$. The sector angle is therefore $35 \cdot 3.6° = 126°$. The value $35°$ treats the percentage as an angle, $12.6°$ is off by a factor of $10$, and $234°$ is the angle for the rest of the budget.
