Of 120 people who registered, 70 chose an online test and 52 registered in the morning. Of those who registered in the morning, 34 chose the online test. How many people who registered later in the day chose the in-person test?

- $34$
- $18$
- $36$
- $32$

## Explanation

A total of $120-70=50$ people chose the in-person test. Of these, $52-34=18$ registered in the morning, leaving 32 who registered later.
