A car park constantly holds $10$ cars during the $6$ night hours and $90$ cars during the other $18$ hours of the day. What is the average number of cars present over the $24$ hours?

- $50$
- $70$
- $30$
- $90$

## Explanation

The average number of cars is the **weighted mean** of the two values, with weights equal to the hours in which they are observed. The weighted sum is $10 \cdot 6 + 90 \cdot 18 = 60 + 1620 = 1680$ and the sum of the weights is $24$, so the mean is $\dfrac{1680}{24} = 70$ cars. The value $50$ is the arithmetic mean of $10$ and $90$, which gives the same weight to night and day, and the value $30$ swaps the weights of the two time bands.
