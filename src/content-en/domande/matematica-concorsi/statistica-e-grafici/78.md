A score of 10 occurs 3 times, 20 occurs 5 times, and 40 occurs 2 times. What is the mean of the ten scores?

- $23$
- $21$
- $22$
- $20$

## Explanation

Multiply each score by its frequency to reconstruct the sum. This gives $3\cdot10+5\cdot20+2\cdot40=210$, which must be divided by 10.
