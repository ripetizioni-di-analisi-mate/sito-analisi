Three observations have mean 20. A clerk mistakenly uses weights $1,1,2$ and still obtains 20. What must the twice-counted observation be?

- $10$
- $20$
- $30$
- $40$

## Explanation

The original sum is 60. Double-counting produces four terms with sum 80. The extra term equals $80-60=20$.
