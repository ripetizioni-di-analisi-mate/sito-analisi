The table shows the $400$ people enrolled in a town's evening classes by sex and course taken.

| | English | Computer skills | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Men | $70$ | $90$ | $160$ |
| Women | $180$ | $60$ | $240$ |
| Total | $250$ | $150$ | $400$ |

What percentage of all students are women enrolled in the computer skills course?

- $15\%$
- $40\%$
- $25\%$
- $60\%$

## Explanation

The **reference total** is the number of students in both courses, $400$, and there are $60$ women enrolled in the computer skills course. The percentage is therefore $\dfrac{60}{400} = 15\%$. The value $40\%$ divides by total enrollment in the computer skills course and gives the percentage of women *among* the students in that course. The value $25\%$ divides by the total number of women and gives the percentage enrolled in computer skills *among* the women. The value $60\%$ treats the count in the cell as a percentage.
