Four measurements have a mean of 16. If a fifth measurement of 16 is added, what is the new mean?

- $17$
- $15$
- $20$
- $16$

## Explanation

The first four measurements add up to $4\cdot16$. The fifth measurement adds 16, bringing the sum to $5\cdot16$, so the mean stays at 16.
