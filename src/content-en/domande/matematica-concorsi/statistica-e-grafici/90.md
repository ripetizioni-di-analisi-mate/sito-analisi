A dataset consists of five distinct integers. The minimum is 4, the maximum is 16, the median is 10, and the second value in ascending order is 7. If the mean is $10.2$, what is the fourth value?

- $15$
- $13$
- $12$
- $14$

## Explanation

The median is the third value, so four known values are 4, 7, 10, and 16. The required total is 51 and the known values add up to 37, so the fourth value is 14.
