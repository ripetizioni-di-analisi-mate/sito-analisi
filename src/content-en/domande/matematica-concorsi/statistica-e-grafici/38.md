A pie chart shows the $720$ hours worked by an office in one month. The “service counter” sector measures $150°$ and the “records” sector measures $90°$. How many more hours were spent at the service counter than on records?

- $60$
- $432$
- $120$
- $300$

## Explanation

In the **pie chart**, the full angle of $360°$ represents $720$ hours, so each degree corresponds to $2$ hours. The two sectors differ by $150° - 90° = 60°$, so the office spent $60 \cdot 2 = 120$ more hours at the service counter than on records. The value $60$ treats the difference between the angles as a number of hours, $432$ treats $60°$ as $60\%$ of the hours, and $300$ is the number of hours spent at the service counter.
