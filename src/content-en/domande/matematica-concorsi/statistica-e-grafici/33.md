At a company with $200$ employees, $40\%$ are women. Of the women, $30\%$ work part-time, as do $20\%$ of the men. What percentage of the part-time employees are women?

- $30\%$
- $50\%$
- $12\%$
- $40\%$

## Explanation

There are $200 \cdot 0.4 = 80$ women and $120$ men. There are $80 \cdot 0.3 = 24$ women and $120 \cdot 0.2 = 24$ men working part-time, so there are $48$ part-time employees. The **reference total** is the number of part-time employees. Thus the percentage of women among the part-time employees is $\dfrac{24}{48} = 50\%$. The value $30\%$ gives the percentage working part-time *among* the women and answers the reverse question. The value $12\%$ divides the $24$ women working part-time by the grand total $200$, and $40\%$ gives the percentage of women among all employees.
