The mean salary at a company is $1800$ euros. Every salary is increased by $5\%$, and then each employee receives a bonus of $30$ euros. What is the new mean salary?

- $1920$ euros
- $1921.50$ euros
- $1890$ euros
- $1835$ euros

## Explanation

Each salary is first multiplied by $1.05$ and then increased by $30$ euros. By the rule for the **mean of transformed data**, the mean undergoes the same two operations in the same order. The new mean salary is therefore
$$
1800 \cdot 1.05 + 30 = 1890 + 30 = 1920 \text{ euros}.
$$
The value $1921.50$ euros reverses the two operations, $(1800 + 30) \cdot 1.05$, and $1890$ euros overlooks the bonus. The value $1835$ euros treats the $5\%$ raise as an increase of $5$ euros.
