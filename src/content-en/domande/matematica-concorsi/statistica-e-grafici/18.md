The eight numbers $3$, $5$, $6$, $x$, $11$, $13$, $15$, and $20$ are in increasing order, and their median is $9$. What is their arithmetic mean?

- $9$
- $7$
- $10$
- $10.25$

## Explanation

Eight is even, so the **median** is the mean of the fourth and fifth values, $\dfrac{x + 11}{2}$. The condition $\dfrac{x + 11}{2} = 9$ gives $x + 11 = 18$, so $x = 7$, which lies between $6$ and $11$ as required by the increasing order. The eight values sum to $3 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 11 + 13 + 15 + 20 = 80$, so the mean is $\dfrac{80}{8} = 10$. The value $10.25$ sets $x$ equal to the median $9$, while $7$ is the data value $x$.
