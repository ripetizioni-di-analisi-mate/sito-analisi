On a test, the scores 18, 21, 24, and 27 have frequencies of 3, 8, 6, and 5, respectively. What is the median?

- $23$
- $\dfrac{45}{2}$
- $24$
- $21$

## Explanation

There are 22 scores, so the median uses the 11th and 12th in ascending order. The 11th is 21 and the 12th is 24, giving a median of $22.5$.
