Four variables are recorded for all the employees of an agency. For which variable can the arithmetic mean *not* be calculated?

- Number of children
- Age in years
- Level of education
- Monthly salary

## Explanation

The arithmetic mean is the sum of the observations divided by their number, so it can be calculated only for numerical data. Level of education is a **qualitative variable** because its values are labels such as “high school diploma” or “college degree,” so its mean cannot be calculated. Number of children, age, and salary are quantitative variables, and each has a mean. For level of education, we can find the mode, the most frequent qualification.
