The table shows the number of rooms in each of the $20$ apartments in a building.

| Rooms | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apartments | $5$ | $5$ | $6$ | $4$ |

What is the median number of rooms?

- $3$
- $2$
- $2.45$
- $2.5$

## Explanation

There are $20$ apartments, an even number, so the **median** is the mean of the tenth and eleventh values in order. The cumulative frequencies are $5$, $10$, $16$, and $20$, so the tenth apartment has $2$ rooms and the eleventh has $3$. The median is therefore $\dfrac{2 + 3}{2} = 2.5$. The value $3$ is the mode, $2$ uses only the tenth value, and $2.45$ is the arithmetic mean $\dfrac{49}{20}$.
