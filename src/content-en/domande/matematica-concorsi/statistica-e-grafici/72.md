In a pie chart each angle is rounded to the nearest degree. Four categories are labelled $70^\circ,90^\circ,120^\circ,81^\circ$. What is the total discrepancy from a full turn?

- One degree too small
- No discrepancy
- Eleven degrees too large
- One degree too large

## Explanation

The angles add to $70+90+120+81=361$. A full turn is 360 degrees; rounding the four values separately can account for the discrepancy.
