A pie chart represents $240$ answers to a questionnaire. Sector A has three times the angle of sector B, and the two sectors together take up half of the circle. How many answers does sector A represent?

- $30$
- $120$
- $135$
- $90$

## Explanation

The two sectors together measure $180°$, and since A is three times B we have $B = \dfrac{180°}{4} = 45°$ and $A = 135°$. In a **pie chart** the full angle of $360°$ corresponds to $240$ answers, so sector A represents $240 \cdot \dfrac{135}{360} = 90$ answers. The value $135$ is the angle of the sector read as a number of answers, the value $30$ is the number of answers in sector B and the value $120$ gives half of the circle to sector A alone.
