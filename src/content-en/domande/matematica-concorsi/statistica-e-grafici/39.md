A pie chart shows the preferences of $200$ students. Each selected one sport from soccer, swimming, volleyball, and tennis. The soccer sector measures $144°$, swimming was chosen by $25\%$ of the students, and volleyball was chosen by $30$ students. How many students chose tennis?

- $65$
- $10$
- $70$
- $40$

## Explanation

In the **pie chart**, the $144°$ sector represents $\dfrac{144}{360} = \dfrac{2}{5}$ of the students, so $200 \cdot \dfrac{2}{5} = 80$ students chose soccer. Swimming was chosen by $200 \cdot 0.25 = 50$ students and volleyball by $30$, leaving $200 - 80 - 50 - 30 = 40$ who chose tennis. The value $65$ treats the $25\%$ who chose swimming as $25$ students, $10$ treats the $30$ students who chose volleyball as $30\%$, and $70$ overlooks volleyball.
