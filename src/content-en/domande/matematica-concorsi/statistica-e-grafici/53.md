The vertical axis of a bar chart runs from 0 to 120 with tick marks every 20 units. A bar ends exactly halfway between 60 and 80. What value does it represent?

- $80$
- $60$
- $70$
- $65$

## Explanation

The interval from 60 to 80 spans 20 units. Its midpoint is 10 units from either end, at 70.
