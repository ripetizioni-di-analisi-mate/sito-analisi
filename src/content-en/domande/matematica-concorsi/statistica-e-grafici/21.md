An office has $10$ employees with a mean age of $42$ years. An employee aged $63$ retires and is replaced by an employee aged $28$. What is the new mean age of the $10$ employees?

- $35$ years
- $38.5$ years
- $42$ years
- $45.5$ years

## Explanation

By the relationship between the **sum of the values and the mean**, the ages of the $10$ employees add up to $10 \cdot 42 = 420$ years. The replacement brings the sum to $420 - 63 + 28 = 385$ years, while the number of employees stays at $10$. The new mean age is therefore $\dfrac{385}{10} = 38.5$ years. The value $35$ years averages the old mean age and the new employee's age. The value $45.5$ years adds the change of $3.5$ years to the mean instead of subtracting it, and $42$ years overlooks the change in the sum.
