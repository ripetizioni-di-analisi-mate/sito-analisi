The table shows enrollment in four training courses in 2024 and 2025.

| course | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| enrollment in 2024 | $80$ | $120$ | $50$ | $200$ |
| enrollment in 2025 | $104$ | $150$ | $62$ | $236$ |

Which course had the greatest percent increase in enrollment from 2024 to 2025?

- Course A
- Course B
- Course C
- Course D

## Explanation

The percent increases are $\dfrac{24}{80} = 30\%$ for A, $\dfrac{30}{120} = 25\%$ for B, $\dfrac{12}{50} = 24\%$ for C, and $\dfrac{36}{200} = 18\%$ for D. Course A therefore has the greatest **percent increase**. Course D has the greatest absolute increase, $36$ participants. Since it already had $200$ participants in 2024, its percent increase is the smallest.
