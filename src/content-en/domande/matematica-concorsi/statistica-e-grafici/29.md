A data set has variance $4$. Every observation is multiplied by $3$ and then increased by $5$. What is the variance of the transformed data?

- $12$
- $36$
- $17$
- $41$

## Explanation

By the rule for the **variability of transformed data**, adding the same number to every observation leaves the deviations from the mean unchanged, so adding $5$ leaves the variance unchanged. Multiplying every observation by $3$ multiplies each deviation by $3$ and each squared deviation by $9$, so the variance is multiplied by $3^2 = 9$. The new variance is therefore $4 \cdot 9 = 36$. The value $12$ multiplies the variance by $3$, the factor for the standard deviation, while $17$ and $41$ also add $5$ to the variance.
