During one week in January, the lowest daily low temperature was $-4$ °C, and the range of daily low temperatures was $13$ °C. What was the highest daily low that week?

- $17$ °C
- $-17$ °C
- $-9$ °C
- $9$ °C

## Explanation

The **range** is the largest value minus the smallest, so the temperature $M$ must satisfy $M - (-4) = 13$, or $M + 4 = 13$. It follows that $M = 13 - 4 = 9$ °C. The value $17$ °C adds $13$ and $4$ without accounting for the sign of $-4$. The value $-17$ °C subtracts the range from the minimum instead of adding it, and $-9$ °C has the wrong sign.
