A monthly chart shows 110 requests in March, 145 in April, and 125 in May. By how many requests did the number increase from March to May?

- $25$
- $35$
- $20$
- $15$

## Explanation

The values for March and May are 110 and 125. The difference between the last and first values is $125-110=15$.
