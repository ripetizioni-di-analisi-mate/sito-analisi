Points $A=(-2,1)$, $B=(2,5)$, and $C=(5,k)$ lie on the same line. What is $k$?

- $6$
- $10$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

From $A$ to $B$, the horizontal and vertical coordinates both increase by four, so the slope is $1$. From $B$ to $C$, the horizontal coordinate increases by three, so the vertical coordinate also increases by three and $k=8$.
