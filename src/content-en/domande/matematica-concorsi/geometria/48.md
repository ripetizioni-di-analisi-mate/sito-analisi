A door forms an angle of $68^\circ$ with the wall. By how many degrees must the angle increase for the door to be perpendicular to the wall?

- $112$
- $12$
- $32$
- $22$

## Explanation

Perpendicular objects form an angle of $90^\circ$. The angle must increase by $90^\circ-68^\circ=22^\circ$.
