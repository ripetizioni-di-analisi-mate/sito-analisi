Each interior angle of a regular polygon exceeds the exterior angle at the same vertex by $120^\circ$. How many sides does the polygon have?

- $6$
- $3$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

At each vertex the interior angle $i$ and the exterior angle $e$ are supplementary, so both of the following conditions must hold:
$$
\begin{cases}
i + e = 180^\circ \\
i - e = 120^\circ
\end{cases}
$$
Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $2e = 60^\circ$, or $e = 30^\circ$. The **exterior angles** of a convex polygon add up to $360^\circ$. Since the exterior angles of a regular polygon are equal, it has $\dfrac{360}{30} = 12$ sides. The value $6$ uses $120^\circ$ as the interior angle, and $3$ uses $120^\circ$ as the exterior angle.
