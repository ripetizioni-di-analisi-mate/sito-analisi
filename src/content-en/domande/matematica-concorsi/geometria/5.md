The supplement of an acute angle is four times its complement. What is the measure of the angle?

- $30^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $150^\circ$
- $60^\circ$

## Explanation

Let $\alpha$ be the angle. Its supplement measures $180^\circ - \alpha$ and its complement measures $90^\circ - \alpha$. The condition gives the equation $180^\circ - \alpha = 4\,(90^\circ - \alpha)$, or $180^\circ - \alpha = 360^\circ - 4\alpha$, from which $3\alpha = 180^\circ$ and $\alpha = 60^\circ$ follow. Indeed, the supplement of $60^\circ$ is $120^\circ$, four times its complement $30^\circ$. The values $30^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ are the complement and the supplement themselves, so they answer a different question.
