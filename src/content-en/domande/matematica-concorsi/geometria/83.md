The legs of a right triangle measure $12$ cm and $35$ cm. What is the distance in centimeters from the vertex of the right angle to the hypotenuse?

- $\dfrac{37}{2}$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{420}{37}$
- $\dfrac{210}{37}$

## Explanation

By the **Pythagorean theorem**, the hypotenuse measures $\sqrt{12^2+35^2}=37$ cm. The area can be expressed as either $12\cdot35/2$ or $37h/2$, where $h$ is the required distance. Equating the expressions gives $h=\dfrac{420}{37}$ cm.
