A tank is shaped like a right circular cylinder. Its volume is $144\pi\ \mathrm{dm}^3$ and its base radius is $4$ dm. What is its height in decimeters?

- $12$
- $9$
- $36$
- $6$

## Explanation

The base area is $\pi\cdot4^2=16\pi\ \mathrm{dm}^2$. Dividing the volume by the base area gives $144\pi/(16\pi)=9$ dm.
