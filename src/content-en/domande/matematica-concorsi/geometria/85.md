Three concentric discs have radii $1,2,3$ centimetres. The inner ring lies between radii 1 and 2; the outer one between radii 2 and 3. What is the ratio of the outer ring's area to the inner ring's area?

- $3:2$
- $4:1$
- $5:3$
- $9:4$

## Explanation

The areas are $(9-4)\pi=5\pi$ and $(4-1)\pi=3\pi$. The factor $\pi$ cancels in their ratio.
