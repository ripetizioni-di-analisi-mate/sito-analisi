Two concentric circles have circumferences of $24\pi$ cm and $14\pi$ cm. What is the area of the ring between them, in square centimeters?

- $25\pi$
- $380\pi$
- $95\pi$
- $10\pi$

## Explanation

The circumference of a circle of radius $r$ is $2\pi r$, so the radii are found by dividing the circumferences by $2\pi$, and they are $12$ cm and $7$ cm. The area of the ring is the difference between the areas of the two disks, so it is $\pi\cdot12^2-\pi\cdot7^2=144\pi-49\pi=95\pi\ \mathrm{cm}^2$. The value $25\pi$ is $\pi\,(12-7)^2$ and comes from subtracting the radii before squaring them. The value $380\pi$ uses the diameters $24$ cm and $14$ cm instead of the radii, and the value $10\pi$ is the difference between the two circumferences.
