Two concentric circles have circumferences of $24\pi$ cm and $14\pi$ cm. What is the area of the ring between them in square centimeters?

- $25\pi$
- $190\pi$
- $95\pi$
- $70\pi$

## Explanation

Dividing the circumferences by $2\pi$ gives radii of $12$ cm and $7$ cm. The difference between the areas of the disks is $\pi(12^2-7^2)=95\pi\ \mathrm{cm}^2$.
