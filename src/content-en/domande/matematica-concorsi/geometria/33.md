The areas of two similar triangles are in the ratio $4 : 9$. The smaller has perimeter $24$ cm. What is the perimeter of the larger triangle?

- $54$ cm
- $81$ cm
- $16$ cm
- $36$ cm

## Explanation

The areas of similar figures have ratio $k^2$, so $k^2 = \dfrac{9}{4}$ and the scale factor from the smaller to the larger triangle is $k = \dfrac{3}{2}$. Perimeters are lengths, so their ratio is $k$ and the larger perimeter is $24 \cdot \dfrac{3}{2} = 36$ cm. The value $54$ cm uses the area ratio $\dfrac{9}{4}$ for the perimeter. The value $16$ cm uses the inverse ratio $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and gives the larger triangle a perimeter of less than $24$ cm.
